## What replaced Def_Compat_Mathlib430

- **What was used.** Of the file's 49 public declarations, only `Ideal.card_inertia_eq_ramificationIdxIn'` was used, by the proof of `card_lowerRamificationGroup_zero_eq_ramificationIdxIn`. It rested on two lemmas of the same file: `card_stabilizer_eq_card_inertia_mul_finrank'` and `ncard_primesOver_mul_card_inertia_mul_finrank'`.
- **The rest is dropped.** That is the `Representation`/`Rep` `…Finsupp` constructions, the `AddAut` group and its lemmas, the elliptic-sequence predicates and the `^+` notation. Nothing in the block or in `ref/tree` uses them.
- **The replacement.** The proof, now `card_lowerGroup_zero_eq_ramificationIdxIn` in `FixedSubringValuation.lean`, uses current Mathlib's `Ideal.card_inertia_eq_ramificationIdxIn`.
- **How the hypotheses differ.** The old lemma assumed Dedekind domains, `Module.IsTorsionFree`, `p ≠ ⊥`, and separability of `R ⧸ p → S ⧸ P`. Mathlib's assumes `[Module.Flat]` and `[Algebra.HasSeparableResidueFieldsAt R S p]`. `[Module.Flat]` comes from Mathlib's instance for a torsion-free module over a Dedekind domain.
- **What Mathlib lacks.** The step from separability of `A ⧸ m_A → B ⧸ m_B` to `HasSeparableResidueFieldsAt`, for a local Dedekind `B`. It is the new Library file `SeparableResidueField.lean`, `Langlands.Ramification.hasSeparableResidueFieldsAt_maximalIdeal_of_isSeparable`, proved from scratch in 40 lines: the only prime over `m_A ≠ ⊥` is `m_B`.

No Mathlib text is carried any more. `licenses/Anthropic-FLT/ATTRIBUTION.md` (in `ref/head`) names upstream's `Definitions/Def_Compat_Mathlib430.lean` as Mathlib text; nothing of that file remains in the port.
