## Concrete boundaries for reuse

**Haar scaling.** Imperial's `MeasureTheory.addEquivAddHaarChar_eq_ringHaarChar_det` gives a determinant formula for finite-dimensional continuous linear automorphisms. `MeasureTheory.ringHaarChar_padic` identifies the scalar character for Q_p. Our `Langlands.HarmonicAnalysis.PadicAdditiveHaarScalingStatement` quantifies over arbitrary p-adic fields and Haar measures. The bridge must establish the residue-normalized field character and the inverse factor in pushforward, then the determinant of X ↦ A X B⁻¹, including zero dimensions. The preliminary traversals found 26 and 20 local modules respectively, with no textual hole hits.

**Finite adeles.** Imperial's `IsDedekindDomain.FiniteAdeleRing.baseChangeAlgEquiv` and `baseChangeContinuousAlgEquiv` occur in `FLT/DedekindDomain/FiniteAdeleRing/BaseChange.lean`; Anthropic retains a derivative under `Definitions/Def_DedekindDomain_FiniteAdeleRing_BaseChange.lean`, documented in ATTRIBUTION.md. Explicit scalar-tower/fiberwise-action hypotheses need checking. Extension of scalars does not itself construct the field norm. The preliminary Imperial traversal found 37 local modules without textual hole hits.

**Reciprocity.** The inspected NYLL wrapper constructs a continuous surjective Kˣ → Gal(L/K) with field-norm kernel. It does not itself choose a normalized compatible system. `UnramifiedNormalization` and `GeneralTowerNaturality` are present and must be traced to the same map. The wrapper fields are Type0; the project requires explicit universes. Our uniformizer/geometric-Frobenius convention must be reconciled. The preliminary traversal reached 502 local modules, not a small standalone lemma.

**Local constants.** FML's `localConstant` is formed from exact conductor packages and a denominator-independent finite value, with an integral-realization theorem. Its value on the trivial additive character is zero only for totalization; the exported identity uses a nontrivial character. Comparing Delta with the project's epsilon factor requires sign, Haar, trace, conductor and spectral-normalization proofs. SML becomes a subsequent consumer only after this bridge.

## Current Mathlib overlap

The project's pinned Mathlib already contains `NumberTheory/LocalField/Basic.lean`, `RepresentationTheory/Homological/` Tate-cohomology infrastructure, `RingTheory/DedekindDomain/FiniteAdeleRing.lean`, and `NumberTheory/NumberField/AdeleRing.lean`/`InfiniteAdeleRing.lean`. The latter files provide genuine adele and idele objects and infinite-part local compactness. This observation does **not** assert that all finite-adele local compactness, reciprocity, norms or base change are already proved there. Search exact declarations before porting older definitions; use their authorship and proofs as provenance where needed.
