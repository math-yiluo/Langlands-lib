## Counterexamples and scope checks

For I005, the restricted-intersection condition alone is vacuous when `τ = ∅`. On a nonempty space, choose `x`; no finite subfamily of `∅` has a union that is a neighbourhood of `x`. Thus `IsQuasinet ∅` fails there. On the empty space it is vacuous too, so nonemptiness belongs in the counterexample. The missing condition is that the original family is a quasinet, not closure of the family under intersections.

For I008, smoothness requires local fixedness **at every point** of the section's domain. An empty domain has no point at which this condition can fail. If there exists a point in the domain with no neighbourhood stabilized by an open subgroup, then no section can satisfy the condition at that point. This correction changes neither the action assumptions nor the local fixedness definition.

For I010/DOC-01, take `n = 1`, `b = 0`, and the nonpivot coordinate. The polynomial is `T^0 + C(X₀) = C(1 + X₀)`. Over a normed field its constant coefficient is neither zero nor one, so it is not monic. The actual distinguishing-coordinate construction uses `separatingBase ≥ 2`; its application remains valid. Only the prose loses the positive-base restriction.

For I016, arbitrary integer torsion and arbitrary base change are distinct from the finite-étale conclusion for a positive natural number invertible in `Γ(S, ⊤)` on an abelian scheme. The exact theorem `torsionFiniteEtale` proves the latter statement; positivity is retained in the target. No multiplication-isogeny hypothesis was substituted for its conclusion. For I017, `GeometricFiberFinite` retains an algebraically closed coefficient field; connectedness and local Noetherianity occur in the Galois-fiber-functor and continuous-action-equivalence targets where applicable. The new finiteness proof does not eliminate the explicit `hfin` parameter from the original definitions or prove the other two targets.

For I024–I026, the category, coefficient and universe restrictions matter. `SmoothExt` uses one universe for `R`, `G` and representation carriers, with commutative `R` and a topological group. Its implemented derived Hom is not silently identified with the `Abelian.Ext` hypotheses in the general hyperext target. `SmoothAbelian` and the derived smooth-sheaf constructions retain `HasStableNeighbourhoods`. The comparison targets also require locally compact Hausdorff `X`. For I025/DOC-02, a recursive scan of project imports found 13 distinct project dependencies of `OperatorSpace.Statement` (14 modules including the entry module); none is `EquivariantDerivedSections`. This establishes the local import boundary, not an absence of the published instances.

For I027, the proof is an algebraic implication from the alternating rational-function identity, monicity, positive degree of `P`, purity and `q > 1`. It does not supply geometric trace identities, cohomological realizations or the full Kottwitz/Harris theorem. The corrected prose can cite the accepted exact final proof without asserting an unverified standalone acceptance history for `Weights.Algebraic`.

For I066, a chosen finite place `λ` of `L` above `ℓ` gives a field `L_λ`. Harris's notation instead uses the whole tensor-product algebra. The characteristic-zero companion quantifies over `[Field k] [CharZero k]` and therefore covers individual factors, not a formalized decomposition of representations or Ext over their product. Neither report had checked the original p.306; this audit did. The same field wording in Hyperext's source paragraph is included in C9 to keep the checked convention consistent without overlapping file ownership.

## Smooth Godement dependency and source analysis

The definition of `spacePoints` in `Cohomology/Flasque/Statement.lean` L24–26 indexes actual open-site points by every `x : X`. `Sheaf/Godement.lean` L30–39 takes the product of stalk/skyscraper functors and their adjunction units. `Sheaf/Equivariant/Godement/Smooth.lean` L31–68 composes ordinary all-point Godement with forgetting and smooth coinduction; L108–114 proves its coaugmentation monic. L117–124 uses `CokernelComplex.functor`. In `Cohomology/CokernelComplex.lean` L25–57, residuals are actual categorical cokernels, terms apply the endofunctor to residuals, and differentials are cokernel projections followed by coaugmentation. No comparison theorem is supplied as construction data.

`SmoothAbelian.lean` L117–141 supplies the abelian category and enough injectives. `SmoothSheafCompactSections.lean` proves smoothness of compact sections and gives the finite-limit instance at L163–171. `SmoothSheafCohomology.lean` L29–46 defines `compactDerived` as Mathlib's `rightDerived`, with the expected injective-resolution identification. `SmoothGodementCohomology.lean` L32–63 defines the compact-section complex, its homology and the degree-zero natural isomorphism. These implementations explain the types and direction of `comparisonMap` directly.

`Sheaf/Equivariant/SmoothPart.lean` L32–36 says every open neighbourhood of every point contains an open neighbourhood stabilized by some open subgroup. Its discrete-group and nonarchimedean-continuous-action instances at L38–67 have real hypotheses. It is not automatic for all actions: translation of the additive real group on the real line has no nonempty open set contained in a bounded interval that is stable under an open subgroup (the only open subgroup is the whole group).

`Sheaf/Godement/ExactnessStatement.lean` L62–67 is the Prop goal `CokernelResolutionStatement`; `Cohomology/AcyclicResolution/Statement.lean` L20–94 supplies lift, comparison, independence and naturality goals; `Cohomology/SmoothGodement/Statement.lean` L60–66 supplies smooth-term derived compact acyclicity. Reading these declarations, the full comparison pair and the accepted statement receipt P18 establishes what is claimed. A Library-wide reference check found no companion theorem naming these comparison goals, but that search is only corroborating status evidence and is not a mathematical proof. The target's Markdown accurately discloses these obligations and the private nature of its author's degree-zero test proof.

Harris 1999 p.304 constructs embeddings into smooth equivariant sheaves acyclic for compactly supported cohomology, states exactness of the construction, and iterates on cokernels in Lemma 2.2. Page 305 gives a finite truncation in Lemma 2.2 bis and the cohomology identification (2.3). Berkovich §1.5 p.10 constructs `C^0 = ν_*ν^*`, then iterates cokernels; Proposition 1.5.1 proves softness after pullback to `X`. Pages 11–12 define both invariant and smooth compact-section cohomology and give Corollary 1.5.2(i); its degree-zero argument uses fixation by an open subgroup, and higher degrees use softness. The ordinary-topology interpretation in F14 is explicitly derived; it does not identify these sites or claim Harris's torsion coefficient systems over an arbitrary ring.

The comparison through an augmentation-compatible map to an injective resolution is also supported by Grothendieck §2.4, Remark 3, p.148. Cartan–Eilenberg V.1.1a, p.78, gives existence and homotopy uniqueness of such lifts; V.§3, pp.82–83, explains the induced maps and independence/naturality for derived functors. These passages support the documented categorical argument, not a claim that the unproved Lean goals have been discharged.
