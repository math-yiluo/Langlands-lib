## Proposed counterexamples and suggested corrections

- P3-001: the counterexample was checked against Milne's definitions. The abelian-variety definition on p.3 imposes no positive dimension, and multiplication on the trivial group scheme over `Spec F_p` is the identity. The suggested correction (quote only the sufficient direction) was adopted. The Lean target is unaffected.
- P3-002: the example was checked independently. For the identity cover of `Spec ℚ` with identity trivializations of `O`, multiplication by `1` and by `2` are isomorphisms of `O`, but only the first is compatible with the trivializations. So `Nonempty (L ≅ L')` is strictly weaker than full faithfulness. The suggested wording was adopted.
- P3-003: the counterexample `Spec K[ε]` was checked against Stacks Lemma 31.15.9 (Tag 02OR): `ε` is a zero divisor, so `Z(ε)` is not an effective Cartier divisor, although `ε` detects every nonzero tangent vector at the closed point. The independent review of the first `PointTangentSeparation.md` candidate supplied a second counterexample, `Spec (K × K)` with `L = O`. There the sections `(0, 1)` and `(1, 0)` separate the two rational points, but every effective Cartier divisor is empty. Both counterexamples are now recorded in both files. The suggested correction (zero subscheme, regularity for the Cartier interpretation, no narrowing of the Lean predicates or of the projectivity target) was adopted.
- P3-019: the audit's claim that the published statement matches the `hL` binder was checked with a private `lean_check` (diagnostic `346d9d0c-401b-40e4-b544-5d567a90569c`). It compiled `relativeRigidifiedCube_of_field_local_zariski hF hL hZ` with `hL : LocalTwoAxisTrivialityStatement.{u}`. The audit's caveat (no proof of `hL` exists, so the cube theorem stays conditional) is correct.
- P3-035: a private `lean_check` (diagnostic `b338c51b-3594-40ac-8d79-05598114c80c`) checked that `universalBundleUniqueness pullbackCoherence` has the full quantified uniqueness type with no coherence hypothesis left. - P3-036: as recorded in the table, the audit's caveat "without declaring the separate universal-comparison target proved" is inaccurate, because that target is proved at the audited commit. The correction therefore cites both `smoothFamilyPullback` and `smoothFamilyUniversalComparison`.
- P3-025, P3-026, P3-027, P3-028, P3-029, P3-030, P3-031, P3-032, P3-033, P3-034, P3-037, P3-038, P3-039, P3-040, P3-041, P3-042: each suggested citation was checked against the full theorem type, its section variables and binders, and the target statement.
