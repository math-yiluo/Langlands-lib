## Published finite-subcoproduct characterization

At A.VIII.2, printed page 267 / PDF page 273, the finite-subsum
characterization is now proved by
`Langlands.Renard.smallProjectiveFiniteSupport` in
`Langlands.CategoryTheory.Renard.SmallProjectiveFiniteSupport`.
Its exact target is
`Langlands.Renard.SmallProjectiveFiniteSupportStatement` in the corresponding
`SmallProjectiveFiniteSupportStatements` module.

For `C : Type u`, `[Category.{v} C]`, `[Abelian C]` and
`[HasCoproducts.{w} C]`, it states, for every `P : C`,

```lean
HomSmallProjective.{w} P ↔
  HomProjective P ∧
    ∀ (I : Type w) (M : I → C) (φ : P ⟶ ∐ M),
      ∃ (s : Finset I) (g : P ⟶ ∐ (fun j : s => M j.val)),
        g ≫ Sigma.desc (fun j : s => Sigma.ι M j.val) = φ
```

The three universes are independent. The finite set may be empty, and the
factorization uses the actual canonical map into the full coproduct.
Projectivity remains on both sides. Here smallness is preservation of
`w`-indexed coproducts by the actual integer-module Hom functor.
No Grothendieck, AB5, generator or additional subobject-size hypothesis is
added.

The implemented forward proof forms the submodule generated by the
summand images in the Hom group. Colimit uniqueness makes its quotient
zero; induction then gives finite-subcoproduct factorization. The converse
reuses the published theorem
`Langlands.Renard.preservesCoproduct_of_finiteFactor` by name. Candidate
`ea9a9a00-0ef2-493f-991d-a2cbf2203a55` also made that theorem public in
`SmallProjectiveFiniteType`, retaining its statement and proof. This route
uses no `w`-indexed coproducts in the target `ModuleCat.{v} ℤ`.
The source gives no proof of this characterization on the consulted page;
the paired proof Markdown documents this implemented route.

## Published actual Hom-equivalence statement

The unnumbered theorem in A.VIII.2, printed page 267 / PDF page 273,
has a published proposition
`Langlands.Renard.SmallProgeneratorEquivalenceStatement`, in
`Langlands.CategoryTheory.Renard.SmallProgeneratorEquivalenceStatements`:

```lean
def SmallProgeneratorEquivalenceStatement (C : Type u) [Category.{v} C]
    [Abelian C] [HasCoproducts.{v} C] : Prop :=
  ∀ P : C, HomSmallProgenerator.{v} P →
    (preadditiveCoyonedaObj P : C ⥤ ModuleCat.{v} (End P)ᵐᵒᵖ).IsEquivalence
```

The hypothesis combines sequence exactness, coproduct preservation and
faithfulness of the actual integer-module Hom functor. The conclusion
concerns the specified endomorphism-module functor: its carrier at `X`
is `P ⟶ X`, its maps are postcomposition, and `op r • f = r ≫ f`.
Left modules over the opposite ring express unital right `End P`-modules.
No equivalence, fullness or essential surjectivity is assumed.

The object universe `u` stays independent of the Hom universe `v`.
Coproduct indices and target module carriers are in `Type v`.
Cocompleteness is read at this Hom size; in an abelian category,
`HasCoproducts.{v} C` gives all `v`-small colimits. The author qualifications
retain this size convention and the target-carrier restriction; there is
no claim about modules in larger universes.

## Published original-action Noetherian statements

`Langlands.Algebra.IntegerOriginalSubmoduleNoetherianStatements` publishes
`Langlands.Algebra.IntegerOriginalSubmoduleNoetherian.IntegerOriginalSubmoduleNoetherianStatement`.
Its three components are `ACCStatement`, `MaximalStatement` and
`ChainStatement`, in that order.

Each component internally binds arbitrary `A : Type uA`, `M : Type uM`,
`[NonUnitalRing A]`, `[AddCommGroup M]` and the original
`[NonUnitalModule A M]`, with independent universes. Write
`rho = NonUnitalModule.toIntLinearEndHom (A := A) (V := M)`.
The common left side is exactly

```lean
letI := CompatibleUnitization.module rho
IsNoetherian (Unitization ℤ A) M
```

The right sides, respectively, are well-founded strict reverse inclusion
on `NonUnitalSubmodule A M`; existence of a maximal member in every
nonempty subset of that poset; and eventual constancy of every increasing
natural-indexed sequence in it. These are actual additive subgroups stable
under the original action. The selected unitized action on the same
carrier is `(z, a) • m = z • m + a • m`. Zero actions and zero carriers are
included; no original unit law, nondegeneracy, nontriviality or finiteness
hypothesis is imposed.

This is an internal comparison motivated by the source conventions,
I.2.1 and A.I.2–3. It does not identify categorical subobjects with these
subgroups, choose Renard's finite-type convention, or establish a
nonunital Noetherian-algebra criterion. The public construction
`NonUnitalSubmodule.intUnitizedOrderIso` supplies the concrete submodule
comparison used by the separate proof.
