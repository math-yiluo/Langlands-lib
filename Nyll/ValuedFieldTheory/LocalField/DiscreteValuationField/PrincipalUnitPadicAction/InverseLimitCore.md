# Units of a complete DVR as an inverse limit of higher-unit quotients

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.LocalField.DiscreteValuationField.PrincipalUnitPadicAction.InverseLimitCore`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For a field `F` with a chosen complete discrete valuation (the library's `CompleteDVF`), with valuation ring O and higher principal unit groups U^n = 1 + 𝔪^n for n ≥ 1 and U^0 = O^*, the file identifies O^* with the inverse limit of the quotients O^*/U^(n+1), algebraically and topologically, and sets up the inverse limit of the quotients U¹/U^(n+1). These are the scaffolding for the ℤ_p-action on the first principal units built in later files.

## Main definitions and results

- `chosenPrincipalUnitPadicUniformizer F`: a uniformizer of O fixed by choice; `chosenPrincipalUnitPadicUniformizer_isUniformizer` and `..._irreducible` record that it is one. `higherUnitSubgroup_chosenPrincipalUnitPadicUniformizer`: the subgroup 1 + π^n O equals the intrinsic U^n.
- `Internal.higherUnitInverseLimitCarrier F`: the group of compatible families in the system O^*/U^(n+1) (`Internal.higherUnitQuotientTransition` are the reduction maps).
- `Internal.unitsEquivHigherUnitQuotientInverseLimit F`: a group isomorphism O^* ≃ lim O^*/U^(n+1); `..._apply` says it sends x to its class at every level.
- `Internal.unitsHomeomorphHigherUnitQuotientInverseLimit F`: the same map as a homeomorphism, when O^* carries the unit topology induced by the 𝔪-adic topology and each quotient the discrete topology. `Internal.uniformizerHigherUnitInverseLimitHomeomorphIntrinsic` is the comparison between the uniformizer-based and intrinsic presentations.
- `Internal.principalUnitQuotientCarrier F n`: the quotient U¹/U^(n+1), with transitions `Internal.principalUnitQuotientCarrierTransition` for m ≤ n, and `Internal.principalUnitInverseLimitCarrier F`, the group of compatible families lim U¹/U^(n+1), with evaluation maps, the compatibility law and an extensionality lemma.

## Proofs

The isomorphisms are obtained from the library's general statement that the units of a discrete valuation ring that is complete for the ideal generated by an irreducible element π are the inverse limit of the quotients by 1 + π^(n+1)O, applied with completeness of the valuation ring and transported along the equality 1 + π^n O = U^n.

## Points to note

- The module docstring describes the ℤ_p-action on U¹, but this file only builds the inverse-limit core: no ℤ_p-action is defined here, and no isomorphism U¹ ≃ lim U¹/U^(n+1) is stated.
- All levels are shifted by one: the n-th coordinate is the quotient by U^(n+1).
- The topology on O is given locally as the adic topology of the ideal generated by the chosen uniformizer, not as a global instance.
