# Adic and prodiscrete topologies on principal units

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.LocalField.DiscreteValuationField.PrincipalUnitPadicAction.AdicProdiscreteComparison`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `F` be a complete discretely valued field in the library's sense (`CompleteDVF`). Its first principal units `U^1` carry two natural topologies: the adic topology coming from the valuation ring, and the prodiscrete topology of the inverse limit of the quotients `U^1 / U^(n+1)`, each with the discrete topology. The library keeps them in two wrapper types, `AdicPrincipalUnits F` and `PrincipalUnitProdiscreteLimit F`, and this file identifies them.

- `adicPrincipalUnitsHomeomorphProdiscreteLimit F`: a homeomorphism `AdicPrincipalUnits F ≃ₜ PrincipalUnitProdiscreteLimit F`. By `adicPrincipalUnitsHomeomorphProdiscreteLimit_apply` it agrees with the additive equivalence `adicPrincipalUnitsAddEquivProdiscreteLimit F`, the canonical map from `U^1` to the inverse limit. `adicPrincipalUnitsContinuousAddEquivProdiscreteLimit` combines the two into an isomorphism of topological groups.
- Instances: `PrincipalUnitProdiscreteLimit F` and `AdicPrincipalUnits F` are Hausdorff.
- Internal homeomorphisms relate each wrapper type to its raw carrier, on which the topology is installed locally; `adicPrincipalUnitsHomeomorphProdiscreteLimit_to_addEquiv` records how the comparison acts on underlying elements.

The homeomorphism is obtained by composing these wrapper identifications with a homeomorphism between `U^1` and the inverse-limit carrier proved in an imported file.

Watch for:

- The topologies are not instances on `U^1` itself; they live on the wrapper types, and on raw carriers only through local `let` bindings inside declarations, including the definition of `adicPrincipalUnitsHomeomorphProdiscreteLimit`.
- The adic topology is defined from the ideal generated by a chosen uniformizer, `chosenPrincipalUnitPadicUniformizer F`.
- No finiteness of the residue field is assumed.
