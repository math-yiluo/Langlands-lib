# Principal units under the topology of a ℤᵐ⁰-valued valuation

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.LocalField.DiscreteValuationField.PrincipalUnitPadicAction.WithZeroValuationTopology`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `v` be a valuation on a field `K` with values in `WithZero (Multiplicative ℤ)`, complete and discrete, with finite residue field, and let `F = LocalField.ofWithZeroValuation v` be the resulting local field of the library. The file shows that the topology on the first principal units U¹ inherited from the valuation topology of `v` coincides with the adic topology used to build the ℤ_p-action on U¹, so that this action and addition are continuous for the inherited topology.

## Main results

- `adicPrincipalUnitsContinuousAddEquivUnderlyingOfWithZeroValuation v`: an isomorphism of topological additive groups between the wrapper `AdicPrincipalUnits F.toCompleteDVF` (U¹ with the adic topology of a chosen uniformizer) and U¹ in `Additive` form with the topology coming from `Valued.mk' v`; the underlying map is the identity on carriers. `..._map_smul` states that it commutes with the ℤ_p-action, p the residue characteristic.
- `principalUnitPadicContinuousSMulOfWithZeroValuation v`: the ℤ_p-action on `Additive U¹` is jointly continuous (`ContinuousSMul`) for the inherited topology.
- `principalUnitPadicContinuousAddOfWithZeroValuation v`: addition, that is multiplication of principal units, is continuous for the same topology.

## Proofs

The key step is that the valuation topology on the valuation ring of a rank-one discrete valuation is the adic topology of its maximal ideal (`rankOneDiscreteValuationSubring_isAdic`), and the maximal ideal is generated by the chosen uniformizer. Continuity is then transported along the isomorphism from the continuity results on `AdicPrincipalUnits`, proved in another file.

## Points to note

- The statements are restricted to value group ℤᵐ⁰, so the value-group universe of `F` is fixed to 0.
- The valued structure enters through a local `let` of `Valued.mk' v`, not a global instance; `ContinuousSMul` and `ContinuousAdd` are stated as theorems inside that context, not registered as instances.
