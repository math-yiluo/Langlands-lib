# First principal units as the inverse limit of U¹/U^(n+1)

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.LocalField.DiscreteValuationField.PrincipalUnitPadicAction.InverseLimitTopology`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For a field with a chosen complete discrete valuation (the library's `CompleteDVF`), valuation ring O and higher principal unit groups U^n, the file shows that the first principal units U¹ are, as a group and as a topological space, the inverse limit of the finite-level quotients U¹/U^(n+1). It restricts the identification O^* ≅ lim O^*/U^(n+1) established in an earlier file.

## Main definitions and results

- `Internal.principalUnitQuotientCarrierToFull F n`: the injective homomorphism U¹/U^(n+1) → O^*/U^(n+1) (`..._injective`), compatible with the transition maps (`..._transition`); at level 0 it is trivial (`..._zero_eq_one`).
- `Internal.principalUnitInverseLimitCarrierToFull`: the induced map lim U¹/U^(n+1) → lim O^*/U^(n+1).
- `Internal.principalUnitToInverseLimitCarrier`: U¹ → lim U¹/U^(n+1), sending x to its classes.
- `Internal.principalUnitMulEquivInverseLimitCarrier F`: a group isomorphism U¹ ≃ lim U¹/U^(n+1); `..._apply` gives its coordinates.
- `Internal.principalUnitHomeomorphInverseLimitCarrier F`: the same map as a homeomorphism, when O carries the adic topology of the ideal generated by the chosen uniformizer, U¹ the induced topology as a subgroup of O^*, and each quotient the discrete topology, so that the limit has the subspace topology of the product.
- `Internal.principalUnitAddEquivInverseLimitCarrier` and `Internal.principalUnitAddHomeomorphInverseLimitCarrier`: the additive forms (through `Additive`), with the same underlying map.

## Proofs

The inverse of U¹ → lim U¹/U^(n+1) pushes a family into lim O^*/U^(n+1), applies the inverse of the isomorphism with O^*, and checks that the resulting unit lies in U¹ because its level-0 class is trivial. Continuity in each direction is checked coordinatewise, using the homeomorphism for O^* and discreteness of the quotients.

## Points to note

- All levels are shifted by one: the n-th coordinate is U¹/U^(n+1).
- The topologies are introduced locally with `letI` inside the statements, not as global instances; in particular the topology on O is the adic topology defined from a uniformizer fixed by choice.
