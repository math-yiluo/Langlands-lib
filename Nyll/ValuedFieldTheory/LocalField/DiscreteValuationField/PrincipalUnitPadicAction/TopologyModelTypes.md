# Wrapper types fixing the topologies on principal units

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.LocalField.DiscreteValuationField.PrincipalUnitPadicAction.TopologyModelTypes`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For a field with a chosen complete discrete valuation (the library's `CompleteDVF`), three objects attached to the first principal units U¹ carry different topologies: U¹ itself with its adic topology, each quotient U¹/U^(n+1) with the discrete topology, and the inverse limit lim U¹/U^(n+1) with the product of the discrete topologies. The file gives each a separate one-field structure, written additively, whose topology is part of the type, so that instance search cannot confuse them.

## Main definitions

- `principalUnitAdicTopology F`: the topology on U¹ (in `Additive` form) induced by the adic topology of the ideal generated by a chosen uniformizer on the valuation ring. `AdicPrincipalUnits F` is the wrapper carrying it, with the additive group structure transported along `AdicPrincipalUnits.equiv`, a homeomorphism `AdicPrincipalUnits.homeomorph` to the underlying type with that topology, and `of`/`val`/`addEquiv`.
- `DiscretePrincipalUnitQuotient F n`: the quotient U¹/U^(n+1) with the discrete topology (`TopologicalSpace` and `DiscreteTopology` instances), an additive group structure, the additive equivalence `addEquiv` with the carrier, and simp lemmas for `of` and `val`.
- `principalUnitProdiscreteTopology F` and `PrincipalUnitProdiscreteLimit F`: the inverse limit of the quotients with the topology induced from the product of discrete spaces, its homeomorphism `PrincipalUnitProdiscreteLimit.homeomorph`, its additive equivalence, and `PrincipalUnitProdiscreteLimit.coordinate F n`, the evaluation at level n as an additive homomorphism to `DiscretePrincipalUnitQuotient F n`.

## Points to note

- These are the library's own wrapper types, not Mathlib constructions; every statement about them passes through `of`, `val` or the equivalences.
- Level n means the quotient by U^(n+1).
- No finiteness of the quotients is proved here; the docstrings say they are finite when `F` is a local field (finite residue field), and for a general `CompleteDVF` the limit is prodiscrete, not necessarily profinite.
- Nothing about these topologies is proved here beyond the homeomorphisms with the underlying types.
