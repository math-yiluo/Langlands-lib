# The field norm on unit groups and its cokernel

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.LocalField.DiscreteValuationField.FieldNorm`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For a field extension `L/K` this file studies the norm subgroup `N(Lˣ) ⊆ Kˣ`, the image of Mathlib's `Algebra.norm` on units (`normUnits K L`), and the quotient `Kˣ / N(Lˣ)`. Under hypotheses on integer valuations of the unit groups, it identifies this quotient with a cyclic group `ℤ/f`, `f` standing for the residue degree, and it treats the norm on principal-unit filtrations.

## Main definitions and results

- `fieldNormSubgroup K L`, the range of `normUnits K L`, and `fieldNormQuotientMap : Kˣ → Kˣ / N(Lˣ)`. Many lemmas restate membership and equality of classes as solvability of norm equations. `fieldNormUnitsQuotientKerEquivFieldNormSubgroup` is the first isomorphism theorem `Lˣ / ker N ≃* N(Lˣ)`.
- `fieldNormSubgroup_le_of_tower`: for a tower `K ⊆ E ⊆ L` with `L` free over `E`, `fieldNormSubgroup K L ≤ fieldNormSubgroup K E`.
- `fieldNormUnits_algebraMap`: `N(u) = u^r` for `u ∈ Kˣ`, where `r = Module.finrank K L` (zero for an infinite extension); hence every element of `Kˣ / N(Lˣ)` has `r`-th power `1`, and the quotient is trivial when `r = 1`.
- `valuedFieldNorm` packages the norm as a `ValuedNorm vK vL` once the valuation formula `vK(N x) = f · vL(x)` is supplied as the hypothesis `hformula`, for a natural number `f` (`residueDegree`).
- With `hformula` and a uniformizer of `vL`: the valuations of norms are exactly the multiples of `f` (`fieldNormSubgroup_valueImage_eq_residueDegree`).
- If moreover every valuation-zero unit of `K` is a norm (hypothesis `hzero`): `x ∈ N(Lˣ)` iff `f ∣ vK(x)`; with a uniformizer `ϖK` of `vK`, `fieldNormQuotientEquivZMod` gives `Kˣ / N(Lˣ) ≃* ℤ/f`, `x ↦ vK(x) mod f`, the quotient has cardinality `f` when `f ≠ 0`, and it is generated by the class of `ϖK`. The norm on units is surjective iff `f = 1` (`fieldNormUnits_surjective_iff_residueDegree_eq_one`).
- For subgroup filtrations `UK`, `UL` of the unit groups and a level function `targetLevel`, `fieldNormMapsFiltrationLevels` is the proposition that the norm sends level `n` of `L` into level `targetLevel n` of `K`. Under it, the norm restricts to the levels and descends to `fieldNormFiltrationQuotientMap` between the quotients of `Lˣ` and `Kˣ` by these levels. Its kernel is the image of the norm-preimage of the target level (`fieldNormFiltrationQuotientMap_ker_eq_preimageClass`), and first-isomorphism equivalences onto its range, and onto the target when it is surjective, are given.

## Points to watch

- `hformula` and `hzero` are hypotheses, not proved here.
- `f` is a free natural number tied to the extension only through `hformula`; it is not defined as the residue degree of `L/K`.
- The valuations `vK`, `vL` are the library's `MultiplicativeIntegerValuation` on unit groups, not Mathlib valuations, and the filtrations are abstract `AntitoneSubgroupFiltration`s.
- The file proves no instance of `fieldNormMapsFiltrationLevels`.
