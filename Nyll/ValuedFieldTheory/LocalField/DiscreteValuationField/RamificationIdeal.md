# The ramification identity 𝔪_K O_L = 𝔪_L^e

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.LocalField.DiscreteValuationField.RamificationIdeal`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `base` and `target` be fields K and L with chosen complete discrete valuations (the library's `CompleteDVF`), L an algebra over K, and the valuation of L extending that of K (`HasExtension`). Write `integerMap` for the map O_K → O_L and e for the library's ramification index `ramificationIndex base.toDVF target.toDVF`. The file proves that the extension of 𝔪_K to O_L is exactly 𝔪_L^e and draws consequences for powers of 𝔪_K and for uniformizers.

## Main results

- `target_maximalIdeal_pow_not_le_pow_succ`: 𝔪_L^n is never contained in 𝔪_L^(n+1) (the lemma takes a uniformizer π of L as input).
- `maximalIdeal_map_eq_target_maximalIdeal_pow_ramificationIndex`: the ideal of O_L generated by 𝔪_K is 𝔪_L^e.
- `maximalIdeal_pow_map_eq_target_maximalIdeal_pow_mul_ramificationIndex`: 𝔪_K^n generates 𝔪_L^(e·n); consequently elements of 𝔪_K^n map into 𝔪_L^(e·n) (`integerMap_mem_target_maximalIdeal_pow_mul_ramificationIndex`), and the image of 𝔪_K^n is not contained in 𝔪_L^(e·n+1).
- For a uniformizer ϖ of K: its image generates 𝔪_L^e (`span_base_uniformizer_image_eq_target_maximalIdeal_pow_ramificationIndex`), lies in 𝔪_L^e but not in 𝔪_L^(e+1), and the same holds for ϖ^n with exponent e·n (the two membership lemmas for ϖ^n also take a uniformizer π of L).
- `exists_unit_mul_target_uniformizer_pow_eq_base_uniformizer_image`: for uniformizers ϖ of K and π of L, the image of ϖ equals u·π^e for some unit u of O_L; `..._pow_mul_...` gives ϖ^n = u·π^(e·n).

## Proofs

Since O_L is a discrete valuation ring and the extended ideal is nonzero, it equals 𝔪_L^n for some n. This n is identified with e through `Ideal.ramificationIdx'_spec`, the characterizing property of the ideal-theoretic ramification index in terms of which `ramificationIndex` unfolds, using that 𝔪_L^n is not contained in 𝔪_L^(n+1).

## Points to note

- The module docstring says "finite extensions", but no finiteness or separability of L over K is assumed: the hypotheses are only the algebra structure and compatibility of the valuations.
- e is the library's ideal-theoretic ramification index for the chosen valuation rings; the file does not compare it with a value-group index.
