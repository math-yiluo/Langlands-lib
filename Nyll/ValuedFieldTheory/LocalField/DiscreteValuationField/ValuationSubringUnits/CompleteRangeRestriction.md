# Restricting a complete DVF's valuation to its range

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.LocalField.DiscreteValuationField.ValuationSubringUnits.CompleteRangeRestriction`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

A complete discretely valued field in this library (`CompleteDVF K`) carries a valuation into an abstract value group `F.ValueGroup` that may be larger than the set of values actually taken. The file replaces it by the same valuation with codomain its multiplicative range, and checks that the properties of the complete DVF pass to the new valuation.

## Definitions and results

- `CompleteDVF.mrangeRestrict F`: the valuation of `F` with values in `MonoidHom.mrange F.valuation`; coerced back to `F.ValueGroup` it equals `F.valuation` (`mrangeRestrict_apply`).
- `mrangeRestrict_residueField_finite`: if the residue field of `F` is finite, so is the residue field of the restricted valuation ring.
- `mrangeRestrict_isAdicComplete`: the restricted valuation ring is complete for the adic topology of its maximal ideal.
- `mrangeRestrict_units_isCyclic`: the unit group of the range is cyclic, generated by the image of the rank-one discrete generator of `F.valuation`.
- `mrangeRestrict_isNontrivial` and `mrangeRestrict_isRankOneDiscrete`: the restricted valuation is nontrivial and rank-one discrete.
- `mrangeRestrict_rankOne`: a `RankOne` structure on the restricted valuation, obtained from cyclicity of the units.

## Proofs

Finiteness and completeness are transported along the ring isomorphism between the original and the restricted valuation rings (`valuationSubringEquivMrangeRestrict` from the general range-restriction file), which carries maximal ideal to maximal ideal. Cyclicity uses that the value group of `F.valuation` is the subgroup generated by its discrete generator. The rank-one structure comes from `rankOneOfUnitsIsCyclic`.

## Watch for

- `mrangeRestrict_rankOne` is a definition, not an instance, and is built by choice, so it is not a canonical normalization of the absolute value.
