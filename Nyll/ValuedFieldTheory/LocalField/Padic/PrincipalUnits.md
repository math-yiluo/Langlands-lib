# Principal units of ℚ_p

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.LocalField.Padic.PrincipalUnits`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

The file compares two models of the p-adic integers used in the library, the valuation ring `𝒪[ℚ_[p]]` of the valuative relation and the valuation subring of the bundled complete discrete valuation field `padicCompleteDVF p`, and uses them to count the quotient `ℤ_pˣ / U^(k+1)` and to show, for odd `p`, that every unit in `U^(k+1)` is a `(p − 1)p^k`-th power of a unit in `U¹`. Here `U^n` is the group of units congruent to 1 modulo `p^n`.

## Main definitions

- `integerRingEquivPadicInt p : 𝒪[ℚ_[p]] ≃+* ℤ_[p]` and `integerRingEquivPadicDVRValuationSubring p`: the identifications of rings, both the identity on underlying elements of `ℚ_[p]`.
- `integerUnitsPrincipalQuotEquivPadicDVR p n`: the induced isomorphism between the two quotients of units by `n`-th principal units.
- `padicDVRQuotientEquivZMod p n`: the valuation subring modulo the `n`-th power of its maximal ideal is `ℤ/p^n`.
- `padicPrimeUnit p`, `padicPrimeInteger p`: `p` as a unit of `ℚ_[p]` and as an element of `𝒪[ℚ_[p]]`.
- `expLogMulEquivOfWithZeroValuation v hv n hlevel`: for a complete discretely valued field `K` of characteristic zero with finite residue field and surjective valuation `v` into `ℤᵐ⁰`, and `n > e/(p − 1)` (with `e` the absolute ramification index and `p` the residue characteristic), the exponential gives `(𝔪^n, +) ≃* U^n`. It repackages an equivalence built in an earlier file.

## Main results

- `principalUnits_map_eq_higherPrincipalUnitGroup`: the two definitions of `U^n` agree under the identification.
- `nat_card_integerUnitsPrincipalQuot_padic_succ`: `|ℤ_pˣ / U^(k+1)| = (p − 1)p^k`, via `(ℤ/p^(k+1))ˣ` and Euler's totient.
- `nat_card_fieldUnitsUniformizerPrincipalQuot_padic_succ`: the quotient of `ℚ_pˣ` by the subgroup generated by `p⁻¹` and `U^(k+1)` has the same order.
- `valuationMap_padicPrimeUnit`, `valuationMap_padicPrimeUnit_inv`: the normalized valuation of the library sends `p` to `−1` and `p⁻¹` to `1`.
- `padicDVR_residueCharacteristic`, `padicDVR_valuation_p`, `padicDVR_ramificationIndex_eq_one`: residue characteristic `p`, `v(p) = exp(−1)`, `e = 1`; the residue field is finite.
- `padicDVR_logExp_level_one_of_odd`, `…_succ_of_odd`: for `p ≠ 2`, every `n ≥ 1` satisfies `n > e/(p − 1)`.
- `expLogMulEquivOfWithZeroValuation_fieldVal`: the equivalence sends `a` to the exponential series at `a`.
- `padicInt_exists_degree_root_of_mem_maximalIdeal_pow_succ`: every `z ∈ p^(k+1)ℤ_p` is `(p − 1)p^k · b` with `b ∈ pℤ_p`, since `p − 1` is a unit.
- `padicDVR_higherPrincipalUnit_degree_is_power_odd`: for `p ≠ 2`, every `u ∈ U^(k+1)` is `r^((p − 1)p^k)` for some `r ∈ U¹`: write `u = exp(z)`, take `b` as above and `r = exp(b)`.

## Watch for

- The power result is proved only for odd `p`; for `p = 2` the level-one condition fails and nothing is stated.
- Sign convention: the valuation `valuationMap` gives `p` value `−1`, so the subgroup in the second count is generated by `p⁻¹`, the element of value `1`, together with `U^(k+1)`.
