# Arithmetic Frobenius of an unramified extension of local fields

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.LocalField.NonarchimedeanLocalField.UnramifiedFrobenius`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For an extension `L/K` of nonarchimedean local fields with compatible valuations, this file defines the arithmetic Frobenius `x ↦ x^q` of the residue extension `𝓀[L]/𝓀[K]`, where `q = #𝓀[K]`, and, when `L/K` is finite, Galois and unramified, lifts it to an element of `Gal(L/K)`. It follows that this Galois group is cyclic of order `[L:K]`, generated by Frobenius, and it constructs isomorphisms with `ℤ/[L:K]`.

## Residue Frobenius

- `residueExtensionArithmeticFrobeniusOfValuationExtension K L : 𝓀[L] ≃ₐ[𝓀[K]] 𝓀[L]`, Mathlib's finite-field Frobenius; `..._apply` says it is `x ↦ x^q`.
- `orderOf_residueExtensionArithmeticFrobeniusOfValuationExtension`: its order is `[𝓀[L] : 𝓀[K]]`; `..._generates`: its powers are all `𝓀[K]`-automorphisms of `𝓀[L]`. For `L/K` finite with `Module.Finite 𝒪[K] 𝒪[L]` and `IsUnramifiedValuedExtension K L`, its order is `[L:K]` (`orderOf_residueExtensionArithmeticFrobeniusOfUnramifiedValuation`).

## Frobenius in the Galois group

These assume `L/K` finite and Galois, `IsIntegralClosure 𝒪[L] 𝒪[K] L`, `Module.Finite 𝒪[K] 𝒪[L]`, and the library's class `IsUnramifiedValuedExtension K L`.

- `arithmeticFrobeniusOfUnramifiedValuation K L : Gal(L/K)`: the preimage of residue Frobenius under the isomorphism `galoisGroupEquivResidueAlgEquivOfUnramifiedValuationOfIsIntegralClosure` between `Gal(L/K)` and the residue automorphism group (imported). It acts on residues by `x ↦ x^q`.
- `arithmeticFrobeniusOfUnramifiedValuation_apply_primitiveRoot`: for a primitive `n`-th root of unity `ζ ∈ L` with `n` prime to `q`, Frobenius sends `ζ` to `ζ^q`.
- `orderOf_arithmeticFrobeniusOfUnramifiedValuation`, `arithmeticFrobeniusOfUnramifiedValuation_generates`, `isCyclic_galoisGroup_of_unramifiedValuation`, `galoisGroup_card_eq_finrank_of_unramifiedValuation`: Frobenius has order `[L:K]` and generates `Gal(L/K)`, which is cyclic of order `[L:K]`.
- `galoisGroupEquivZModOfUnramifiedValuationNormalized`: an isomorphism `Gal(L/K) ≃* ℤ/[L:K]` (multiplicative notation) sending Frobenius to `1`, and its `m`-th power to `m`. The unnormalized `galoisGroupEquivZModOfUnramifiedValuation` comes from cyclicity alone and does not fix where Frobenius goes.
- General helpers: `zmodAddEquivOfGenerator` and `zmodCyclicMulEquivOfGenerator` identify a cyclic group with a generator of order `n` with `ℤ/n`; `eq_of_simple_roots_of_residue_eq`: in a local ring, two roots of a polynomial with the same residue coincide if the derivative at one of them is a unit.

## Proofs

For the roots of unity, `ζ` is integral, `φ(ζ)` and `ζ^q` are roots of `X^n - 1` with the same residue, and the derivative at `ζ^q`, namely `n(ζ^q)^(n-1)`, is a unit because `n` is prime to the residue characteristic, so the two roots coincide. The group-theoretic statements are transported from the residue field through the imported isomorphism.

## Points to watch

- Unramifiedness is the library's class `IsUnramifiedValuedExtension`, not a Mathlib notion, and both `Module.Finite 𝒪[K] 𝒪[L]` and `IsIntegralClosure 𝒪[L] 𝒪[K] L` are hypotheses.
- Frobenius here is arithmetic (`x ↦ x^q`); only the normalized `ℤ/[L:K]` model is guaranteed to send it to `1`.
