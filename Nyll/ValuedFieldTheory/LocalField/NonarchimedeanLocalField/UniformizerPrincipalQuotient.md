# Kˣ modulo a uniformizer and Uⁿ

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.LocalField.NonarchimedeanLocalField.UniformizerPrincipalQuotient`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `K` be a nonarchimedean local field and `π ∈ Kˣ` an element of normalized valuation `1`. Every `x ∈ Kˣ` factors as `x = a · π^v(x)` with `a` a unit of `𝒪[K]` (the imported `uniformizerUnitFactor`). This file shows that `x ↦ a mod U^n` identifies `Kˣ/(⟨π⟩ · U^n)` with `𝒪[K]ˣ/U^n`.

## Main definitions and results

- `uniformizerUnitFactor_one`, `uniformizerUnitFactor_mul`, `uniformizerUnitFactor_integerUnit`: the unit factor of `1` is `1`, the unit factor is multiplicative, and it is the identity on units of `𝒪[K]`.
- `fieldUnitsToIntegerUnitsPrincipalQuotientHom K π hπ n : Kˣ →* 𝒪[K]ˣ/U^n`, taking the unit factor and reducing it modulo `U^n`.
- `fieldUnitsToIntegerUnitsPrincipalQuotientHom_surjective`: this homomorphism is onto.
- `fieldUnitsToIntegerUnitsPrincipalQuotientHom_ker`: its kernel is `uniformizerPrincipalSubgroup K π 1 n`, the subgroup generated by `π` and the principal units `U^n` in `Kˣ`.
- `uniformizerPrincipalQuotientEquivIntegerUnitsPrincipalQuotient`: the resulting isomorphism `Kˣ/(⟨π⟩ · U^n) ≃* 𝒪[K]ˣ/U^n`, by the first isomorphism theorem.

## Points to watch

- The isomorphism depends on the chosen `π`, although the module comment calls it canonical. The hypothesis is `valuationMap K π = 1`; in the library's sign convention this is satisfied by the inverse of a uniformizer.
- The statements hold for every `n`, including `n = 0`.
