# Open subgroups of Kˣ contain standard subgroups

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.LocalField.NonarchimedeanLocalField.StandardOpenSubgroups`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For a nonarchimedean local field `K`, every open subgroup of `Kˣ` of finite index contains a subgroup `⟨ϖ^d⟩ · U^n` built from an element of valuation one and a deep principal-unit group; the file says this is used in the existence theorem.

- `fieldPrincipalUnits K n`: the principal units `U^n`, as a subgroup of `Kˣ`.
- `uniformizerPrincipalSubgroup K ϖ d n`: the subgroup generated by `ϖ^d` and `U^n`.
- `exists_fieldPrincipalUnits_subset_of_mem_nhds`: every neighbourhood of `1` in `Kˣ` contains some `U^n` with `n ≥ 1`; `exists_fieldPrincipalUnits_le_of_isOpen` is the version for open subgroups.
- `exists_uniformizerPrincipalSubgroup_le_of_isOpen_finiteIndex`: if `H` is open of finite index, there are `ϖ` with `v ϖ = 1`, `d > 0` and `n ≥ 1` such that `⟨ϖ^d⟩ · U^n ≤ H`; one may take `d` to be the index of `H`.

Neighbourhoods of `1` are handled through neighbourhoods of `0` in `𝒪[K]`, which contain a power of the maximal ideal. In the library's sign convention `v ϖ = 1` holds for the inverse of a uniformizer.
