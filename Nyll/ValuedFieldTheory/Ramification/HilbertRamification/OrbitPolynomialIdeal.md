# Orbit polynomials and the quotient-depth identity

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.Ramification.HilbertRamification.OrbitPolynomialIdeal`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let L/K be a finite Galois extension with group G, with discrete valuations `base` on K and `target` on L (the library's `DVF` structures), `target` extending `base`, and valuation rings O_K and O_L. Let H be a subgroup of G and z an element with O_K[z] = O_L. For σ in G, the file shows that, when H is normal, the ideal of O_L generated by the product of στ(z) − z over τ ∈ H is the extension to O_L of the displacement ideal of the image of σ in Gal(L^H/K). This is the ideal-level identity behind the formula for ramification numbers of a quotient. Neither completeness nor a Henselian hypothesis is used.

## Definitions

- `subgroupOrbitPolynomialDVF huniq H z`: the product of X − τ(z) over τ ∈ H, a polynomial over O_L.
- `subgroupOrbitPolynomialCoeffFixedFieldDVF`: its n-th coefficient, as an element of the valuation ring of the fixed field L^H.
- `cosetGeneratorDisplacementProductDVF huniq H σ z`: the product of στ(z) − z over τ ∈ H, that is, over the coset σH.

## Results

- Over a domain, a polynomial vanishing on a finite set, or on an injective finite family, is divisible by the product of the corresponding linear factors. A polynomial with all coefficients in an ideal I takes values in I.
- `valuationSubringAutOfUniqueExtension_generator_injective`: if z generates O_L over O_K, then σ ↦ σ(z) is injective on G.
- The orbit polynomial is H-invariant and vanishes at z. Applying σ to its coefficients and evaluating at z gives (−1)^|H| times the coset product.
- `cosetGeneratorDisplacementProductDVF_dvd_fixed_displacement`: when z generates O_L, the coset product divides σ(a) − a for every a in the valuation ring of L^H.
- `cosetGeneratorDisplacementProductDVF_mem_map_fixedFieldDisplacementIdeal`: for H normal, the coset product lies in the extended displacement ideal. No generator hypothesis is needed here.
- `span_cosetGeneratorDisplacementProduct_eq_map_fixedFieldDisplacementIdeal`: for H normal and z a generator, the two ideals are equal.

## Proof

For one inclusion, write a = g(z) with g over O_K. Then g − a vanishes on the H-orbit of z, so the orbit polynomial divides it, and applying σ and evaluating at z gives the divisibility. For the other, σf − f has coefficients of the form σ(c) − c with c fixed by H, and its value at z is the coset product up to sign.

## Watch for

- The generator z is an explicit hypothesis `hz : Algebra.adjoin O_K {z} = ⊤`, and unique extension of the valuation is the explicit hypothesis `huniq : DVF.HasUniqueValuationExtension base target`.
- The fixed field carries the restriction of the valuation ring of L, not a normalized valuation of its own. The identity is stated before any normalization.
