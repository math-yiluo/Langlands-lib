# Ramification numbers and lower groups on a fixed field

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.Ramification.HilbertRamification.FixedFieldRamification`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `L/K` be a finite Galois extension of discretely valued fields in which the valuation of `K` extends uniquely to `L`, and let `H` be a normal subgroup of `Gal(L/K)` with fixed field `M = L^H`. The file works with the restricted valuation ring `O_M = O_L ∩ M` (a discrete valuation ring, from the companion fixed-field file) and defines, for `q ∈ Gal(M/K)`, the ramification number and the real lower ramification groups of `M/K` directly on this ring.

## Main definitions and results

- `fixedFieldDisplacementIdealDVF huniq H q`: the ideal of `O_M` generated by all `q(a) − a` with `a ∈ O_M`.
- `fixedFieldRamificationNumber huniq H q : ℕ∞`: the additive valuation of a generator of that (principal) ideal; it is `⊤` when the ideal is zero, as for `q = 1`. No monogenic generator of `O_M` over `O_K` is used.
- `fixedFieldRamificationIdealDVF H s`: the power `𝔪_M^(realRamificationExponent s)` of the maximal ideal of `O_M`.
- `fixedFieldLowerRamificationGroup huniq H s`: the subgroup of `Gal(M/K)` of those `q` with `q(a) − a ∈ 𝔪_M^(realRamificationExponent s)` for all `a ∈ O_M`; `mem_fixedFieldLowerRamificationGroup_iff` unfolds it.
- `mem_fixedFieldLowerRamificationGroup_iff_ramificationNumber`: `q` lies in the lower group of index `s` iff `realRamificationExponent s ≤ i(q)`.
- `mem_fixedFieldLowerRamificationGroup_nat_iff`: for a natural index `n`, `q ∈ G_n(M/K)` iff `n + 1 ≤ i(q)`.

Membership for all `a` is equivalent to the whole displacement ideal lying in the power of `𝔪_M`, which, for a principal ideal of a discrete valuation ring, is the stated inequality on the valuation of its generator.

## Points to watch

- The ramification number is normalized by the valuation of `M` and takes values in `ℕ∞`; with this convention `G_n` corresponds to `i(q) ≥ n + 1`.
- `H` must be normal and uniqueness of the valuation extension is the explicit hypothesis `huniq`.
- `fixedFieldRamificationNumber` evaluates Mathlib's chosen generator of the principal ideal; the file does not state a separate lemma on independence of the generator.
