# The decomposition field as L ∩ K_v; values and residue fields

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.Ramification.HilbertRamification.DecompositionFieldLocalization`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `L/K` be Galois (finite or infinite), `v` a nontrivial real absolute value on `K` with completion `K_v`, and `w` an extension of `v` to `L`. Inside the library's algebraic localization `L_w` (`localization vK w`, the field generated over `K_v` by the dense copy of `L`), the file identifies the decomposition field `Z_w` with `L ∩ K_v`. In the non-archimedean case it deduces that `w|Z_w` and `v` have the same values and isomorphic residue fields.

## Main results

- `decompositionField_toLocalizationAlgHom`: the `K`-embedding `L → L_w`; `decompositionField_localization_adjoin_range_eq_top`: its image generates `L_w` over `K_v`.
- `decompositionField_localization_normal`, `decompositionField_localization_separable`, `algebraicLocalization_isGalois`: `L_w/K_v` is normal, separable and Galois, without a finite-degree hypothesis.
- `decompositionField_decompositionField_eq_completionImage_comap`: `x ∈ L` lies in `Z_w` iff its image in `L_w` lies in the copy of `K_v`.
- `decompositionField_decompositionField_image_eq_intersection`: the image of `Z_w` in `L_w` is the intersection of the images of `L` and `K_v`.
- For non-archimedean `v`: `absoluteValueExtension_nonarchimedean_of_base` and companions transfer non-archimedeanness to `w`, to `w|Z_w` and to `K_v`; `decompositionField_completionAbsoluteValue_range_eq`: completion does not enlarge the range.
- `decompositionField_decompositionField_valueRange_eq`: `w|Z_w` and `v` have the same range in `ℝ`.
- `decompositionField_decompositionField_integerMap`: the local homomorphism from the valuation ring of `v` to that of `w|Z_w`; `decompositionField_decompositionField_residueMap_surjective` and `decompositionField_decompositionField_residueFieldEquiv`: the induced residue map is surjective, hence a ring isomorphism.

## Proofs

`L_w/K_v` is Galois because it is generated by images of elements of `L`, which are separable with split minimal polynomials. The description of `Z_w` uses the fixed-field criterion of infinite Galois theory with the isomorphism `decompositionGroupEquivAlgebraicLocalizationAut` between the decomposition group and `Aut(L_w/K_v)`, taken from another file. Value and residue statements follow by writing an element of `Z_w` as an element of `K_v` and approximating it by elements of `K`, using the strong triangle inequality.

## Points to watch

- `L_w` is the library's algebraic localization, not the full completion of `L`.
- "Same value group" is stated as equality of the ranges (as subsets of `ℝ`) of the two absolute values.
- Non-archimedean is the library's `NonarchimedeanAbsoluteValue`, defined by boundedness on the natural numbers.
- `w` is an exact extension of `v` (equal on `K`), and `v` must be nontrivial for the main comparison results.
