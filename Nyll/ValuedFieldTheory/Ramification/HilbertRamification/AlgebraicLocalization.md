# The decomposition group as the local Galois group

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.Ramification.HilbertRamification.AlgebraicLocalization`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `L / K` be a Galois extension, possibly infinite, `v` a nontrivial real absolute value on `K` and `w` an extension of `v` to `L`. The file proves that the decomposition group of `w` is isomorphic to the group of `K_v`-automorphisms of the algebraic localization `L K_v`. Here `K_v` is the completion of `K`, and `L K_v` is the subfield of the completion of `(L, w)` generated over `K_v` by the image of `L`.

## Main declarations

- `valuationPreservingCompletionRingEquiv` and `valuationPreservingLocalizationAlgEquiv`: a `K`-automorphism `σ` of `L` with `w(σx) = w(x)` for all `x` extends by continuity to the completion of `(L, w)`. The extension fixes `K_v` and maps `L K_v` onto itself, so it gives a `K_v`-automorphism of `L K_v`.
- `absoluteValueDecompositionGroup_preserves_absoluteValue`: for `v` nontrivial, an element `σ` of the decomposition group satisfies `w(σx) = w(x)`, an equality and not only an equivalence.
- `localizationAbsoluteValue_algEquiv`: every `K_v`-automorphism of `L K_v` preserves its absolute value.
- `localizationAlgEquivRestrict` and `localizationToDecompositionGroup`: a `K_v`-automorphism of `L K_v` maps the image of `L` to itself, by normality of `L / K`. Its restriction to `L` lies in the decomposition group.
- `decompositionGroupEquivAlgebraicLocalizationAut`: the group isomorphism `D_w ≃* Aut(L K_v / K_v)`, given by extension, with restriction as inverse. Its `simp` lemmas say how it acts on the image of `L`.

## Proofs

Invariance of the absolute value on `L K_v` comes from uniqueness of the extension of the absolute value of the complete field `K_v` to the algebraic extension `L K_v`; that uniqueness is imported. Restriction after extension is the identity by evaluation on each `x ∈ L` and injectivity of `L → L K_v`. Extension after restriction is the identity because a `K_v`-algebra map of `L K_v` is determined by its values on the image of `L`, which generates it (`IntermediateField.adjoin_algHom_ext`).

## Watch for

- `L K_v` is only the algebraic subfield of the completion of `(L, w)` generated by `L` over `K_v`; for an infinite extension it can be a proper subfield.
- The result is an isomorphism of abstract groups; no topology on either side is compared.
- `v` must be nontrivial and `w` must extend `v` exactly (`AbsoluteValueExtension`). The extension step needs neither normality nor the Galois hypothesis; the restriction step uses it.
- The decomposition group is the library's `absoluteValueDecompositionGroup`, defined in another file.
