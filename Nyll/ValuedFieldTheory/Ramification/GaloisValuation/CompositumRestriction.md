# Automorphisms of a compositum are determined by their restrictions

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.Ramification.GaloisValuation.CompositumRestriction`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

If a field is generated by two intermediate fields `E₁, E₂`, each normal over `K`, then a `K`-automorphism of it is determined by its restrictions to `E₁` and `E₂`.

## Main results

- `restrictNormalHom_prod_injective_of_sup_eq_top`: for intermediate fields `E₁, E₂` of `P / K`, normal over `K`, with `E₁ ⊔ E₂ = P`, the map `Aut_K(P) → Aut_K(E₁) × Aut_K(E₂)` is injective.
- `intermediateFieldRestrictNormalHom_prod_injective_of_sup_eq`: the same for `E₁, E₂ ≤ F` inside one ambient field `Ω`, when `E₁ ⊔ E₂ = F`, with restriction maps from `Aut_K(F)`.
- `subgroup_eq_of_prod_map_injective_of_left_maps_eq_bot`: if `(f₁, f₂) : G → G₁ × G₂` is injective, and two subgroups `A, B` both have trivial image under `f₁` and the same image under `f₂`, then `A = B`.

## Proofs

An automorphism restricting trivially to both fields fixes `E₁ ⊔ E₂`, whose fixing subgroup is the intersection of the two fixing subgroups. The relative version restricts `E₁, E₂` to subfields of `F` and reduces to the first result.

## Watch for

Only normality of `E₁` and `E₂` over `K` is assumed; the generated field need not be finite or Galois over `K`.
