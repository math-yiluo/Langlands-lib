# Algebraic preliminaries for Hensel's lemma in factorization form

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.Valuation.Henselian.Factorization.Basic`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

The file collects the algebraic steps that start the approximation proof of Hensel's lemma: lifting polynomials and Bézout relations from a residue ring, reading congruences coefficientwise, and dividing by a lift of a residual factor. No convergence argument appears here.

## Main results

- Lifts: along a surjective `φ : R →+* k`, every polynomial over `k` lifts with the same degree (`henselFactorization_exists_polynomial_lift_degree_eq`, `..._natDegree_eq`). If `ḡ`, `h̄` are coprime and `g₀`, `h₀` lift them, there are `a`, `b` with `a g₀ + b h₀` reducing to `1` (`henselFactorization_exists_bezout_lifts`).
- Over a local ring, a lift of `ḡ` with the same degree has unit leading coefficient when `ḡ` has nonzero leading coefficient (`henselFactorization_lift_leadingCoeff_isUnit_of_natDegree_eq`); multiplying by the inverse of a unit leading coefficient makes a polynomial monic without changing its degree.
- Division: over a nontrivial ring, by a monic polynomial, or by one with unit leading coefficient, `F = g q + p` with `deg p < deg g`; in particular by such a lift `g₀` (`henselFactorization_division_by_lifted_factor_degree_lt`).
- Coefficientwise congruences: `P` maps to `0` under `φ` exactly when all its coefficients lie in `ker φ`, with forms for `R ⧸ I` and for equality of reductions. If `f̄ = ḡ h̄` with lifts `g₀`, `h₀`, the coefficients of `f − g₀ h₀` lie in `ker φ` (in the maximal ideal for a local ring), and likewise for `a g₀ + b h₀ − 1`.
- Correction step: if `b fₙ = g₀ q + p`, then `g₀ (a fₙ + h₀ q) + h₀ p = (a g₀ + b h₀) fₙ` (`henselFactorization_correction_identity_after_division`). Hence the corrected expression is congruent to `fₙ` modulo `ker φ`, or modulo the maximal ideal, when `a g₀ + b h₀` reduces to `1`, and modulo `(π)` when `a g₀ + b h₀ − 1 = π · e`.
- Small lemmas: reduction of coefficients commutes with the expressions above, and a maximal ideal generated by `π` contains `π`.

## Caveats

The lifted factor `g₀` need not be monic: a unit leading coefficient suffices for the division step.
