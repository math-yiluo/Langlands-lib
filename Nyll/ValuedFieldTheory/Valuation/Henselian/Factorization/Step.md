# One step of Hensel's iteration

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.Valuation.Henselian.Factorization.Step`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

This file assembles one step of the coefficientwise proof of Hensel's lemma over a local ring `R` with maximal ideal 𝔪. From an approximate factorization f − g·h = π^n·f_n, with g, h congruent to initial lifts g0, h0, it produces corrections p, q such that f − (g + π^n·p)(h + π^n·q) = π^(n+1)·f_(n+1), while keeping the congruences with g0, h0 and bounds on the degrees (deg g ≤ m, deg h ≤ d − m).

## Two forms

Most results come in two versions.

- `..._of_mem_le`: π ∈ 𝔪 and 𝔪 ≤ (π), so π generates 𝔪; the Bezout relation is a·g0 + b·h0 ≡ 1 after reduction to the residue field, and congruences are taken modulo 𝔪.
- `..._of_mem_span`: only π ∈ 𝔪; the Bezout relation is given with an explicit error, a·g0 + b·h0 − 1 = π·e, and the congruences with g0, h0 are carried modulo (π) as well as modulo 𝔪.

## Main results

- `henselFactorization_update_preserves_reduction_of_mem` and `henselFactorization_update_preserves_span_singleton`: for n ≥ 1, adding π^n·p keeps g congruent to g0 modulo 𝔪 (when π ∈ 𝔪), resp. modulo (π).
- `henselFactorization_one_step_update_from_division_data_of_mem_le` and `_of_mem_span`: from division data b·f_n = g0·qdiv + p with degree bounds, the second correction is the part of degree at most d − m of a·f_n + h0·qdiv (`henselFactorization_lowPart`, defined in an earlier file), and the updated error is a multiple of π^(n+1). A variant `..._from_division_degree_lt_of_mem_le` takes the remainder bound deg p < deg g0.
- `henselFactorization_exists_one_step_update_of_mem_le` and `_of_mem_span`: existence of p, q, f_(n+1) from the current error alone, with deg p ≤ m and deg q ≤ d − m. Hypotheses: R has no zero divisors, π^n ≠ 0, n ≥ 1, deg f ≤ d, deg g ≤ m, deg h ≤ d − m, m ≤ d, g0 of degree m reducing to ḡ of degree m with nonzero leading coefficient, and h0 (or its reduction) of degree at most d − m.
- `henselFactorization_exists_one_step_update_with_degree_bounds_of_mem_span` and `_of_mem_le`: the same, adding deg(g + π^n·p) ≤ m and deg(h + π^n·q) ≤ d − m.

## Proof

Since deg g0 = deg ḡ, the leading coefficient of g0 is a unit, so b·f_n can be divided by g0. The truncation lemma gives the correction congruence for g0, h0, which passes to g, h; the power-update lemma bounds the new error.

## Points to watch

- The letter m is a degree; the maximal ideal is written 𝔪.
- The `_of_mem_le` forms need 𝔪 to be generated by π; the `_of_mem_span` forms replace this by the Bezout relation with an explicit factor π.
