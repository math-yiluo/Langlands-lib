# The compositum of `L` and the completed base inside the completion of `L`

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.Valuation.AbsoluteValue.AlgebraicLocalization`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `L/K` be a field extension and let `wL` be an absolute value on `L` that restricts exactly to `vK` on `K`. The file builds, inside the completion of `L` for `wL`, the subfield generated over the completion of `K` by the image of `L`. This is the compositum `L · K̂` inside `L̂`, which the library calls the algebraic localization of `L/K` at `wL`.

## Definitions and results

- `algebraicLocalization vK wL hw`: the intermediate field of `wL.Completion` over `vK.Completion` generated by the image of `L`. Here `hw : Extends vK wL`, and the algebra structure is `completionAlgebra` from the imported completion file.
- `toAlgebraicLocalization`: the ring map from `L` into it, with `toCompletion_mem_algebraicLocalization` and `toAlgebraicLocalization_apply` describing it.
- `algebraicLocalization_isAlgebraic`: if `L/K` is algebraic, the compositum is algebraic over `vK.Completion`. Each generator is integral over `K`, hence over the completed base.
- `algebraicLocalizationAbsoluteValue`: the absolute value induced from the completion of `L`. It extends the absolute value of `vK.Completion` (`algebraicLocalizationAbsoluteValue_extends`), and on the copy of `L` it equals `wL`.
- `toAlgebraicLocalization_algebraMap`: the two copies of `K`, through `L` and through the completed base, agree.

## Points to watch

- The "algebraic localization" is a compositum inside `L̂`, not the completion of `L`. The file does not show that it is complete, or that it equals `L̂` when `L/K` is finite.
- Extension means exact equality `wL ∘ algebraMap = vK`.
- Algebraicity is proved only when `L/K` is algebraic. No finiteness of degree is assumed.
