# Extensions of an absolute value to `K(α)` and factors over the completion

Module `Nyll.ValuedFieldTheory.Valuation.Completion.ExtensionFactorClassification`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `v` be a nontrivial real absolute value on `K`, `K_v` its completion, `K̄_v` Mathlib's algebraic closure of `K_v` and `v̄` the extension of `v` to `K̄_v`. For a simple extension `L = K[α]` and an irreducible `f ∈ K[X]` with `f(α) = 0`, the file proves that the absolute values on `L` extending `v` correspond bijectively to the distinct monic irreducible factors of `f` over `K_v`, and describes each completion `L_w` explicitly.

## Main definitions and results

- `CompletionExtensionFactorCompletionFactors v f`: the set of distinct normalized irreducible factors of `f` mapped to `K_v[X]`; it equals the corresponding set for the minimal polynomial of `α`.
- `completionExtensionFactor_extensionFactor v α w`: the minimal polynomial over `K_v` of the image of `α` in `L_w`; it is one of these factors.
- `completionExtensionFactor_extensionEquivFactors`: the map `w ↦` this factor is a bijection from extensions of `v` to factors.
- `completionExtensionFactor_completion_adjoin_eq_top` and `completionExtensionFactor_adjoinRootEquivCompletion`: `L_w` is generated over `K_v` by the image of `α`, hence `L_w ≅ K_v[X]/(g_w)` with `X ↦ α`.
- `completionExtensionFactor_completionEquivSimpleRoot`: if `w = v̄ ∘ τ` for an embedding `τ : L → K̄_v`, then `L_w ≅ K_v(τ α)` over `K_v`, extending `τ` on all of `L`.
- `completionExtensionFactor_classification`: the map to factors is bijective, and for each factor `g` and root `β` of `g` in `K̄_v` there is an embedding `τ` with `τ α = β`; the extension `w = v̄ ∘ τ` is the one corresponding to `g`, and `L_w ≅ K_v(β)` extends `τ`.

## Proof

Extensions correspond to `K_v`-conjugacy classes of `K`-embeddings `L → K̄_v` (the valuation-extension theorem of another file); embeddings of `K[α]` correspond to roots of the minimal polynomial in `K̄_v`, and `K_v`-conjugacy classes of roots to distinct irreducible factors over `K_v` (`simpleEmbeddingsEquivMappedMinpolyRoots`, `rootClassesEquivDistinctNormalizedFactors`). Generation of `L_w` by `α` uses the library result that the subfield of `L_w` generated by `L` over `K_v` is all of `L_w`.

## Points to watch

- No separability is assumed: for an inseparable `f`, each repeated factor counts once.
- `L = K[α]` is the hypothesis `Algebra.adjoin K {α} = ⊤`; finiteness of `L/K` follows from it.
- Extensions are absolute values restricting exactly to `v` (`AbsoluteValueExtension v L`), not equivalence classes.
