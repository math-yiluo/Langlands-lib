# Herbrand quotient of a lattice in a permutation representation

Module `Nyll.GaloisCohomology.Cyclic.Herbrand.Permutation.LatticeHerbrand`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let a finite cyclic group G, generated by σ, act on `ι → ℝ`, with ι a finite type, by permuting coordinates through `ρ : G →* Equiv.Perm ι`, and let L be a G-stable complete lattice. The file shows that the Herbrand quotient of L is defined and equals the product, over the G-orbits on ι, of the orders of the orbit stabilizers. It joins the permutation-stable sublattice built in the lattice file with the computation for permutation modules `ι → ℤ`.

## Main definitions and results

- `permutationMulAction ρ`, `intCoordinatePermutation σ`: the action of G on ι and the coordinate permutation of `ι → ℤ`. `basisEquivFun_symm_intCoordinatePermutation`: if G permutes a ℤ-basis, coordinates transform by the coordinate permutation.
- `permutationSublattice_stable`: the canonical sublattice is G-stable. `permutationSublatticeDistribMulAction` and `completePermutationLatticeDistribMulAction` are the actions on it and on L.
- `multiplicativeDistribMulAction`, `multiplicativeSubmoduleSubgroup`, `multiplicativeSubmoduleMulEquiv`, `multiplicativeSubmoduleQuotientFinite`: transport an additive action, a submodule and finiteness of its quotient to multiplicative notation.
- `permutationSublatticeMultiplicativeSubgroup` is the sublattice as a subgroup of `Multiplicative L`; it is stable and of finite index, and equivariantly isomorphic to the sublattice itself.
- `permutationSublatticeBasisMulEquivFunctions` and its `_equivariant` lemma: the distinguished basis identifies the sublattice G-equivariantly with `ι → ℤ`.
- `permutationSublatticeHerbrandH0Finite`, `permutationSublatticeHerbrandHMinusOneFinite`: the degree 0 and degree −1 Tate groups of the sublattice are finite.
- `permutationSublattice_herbrandQuotient_eq_stabilizerProduct`: its Herbrand quotient is the product over the orbits ω of the orders of `permutationOrbitStabilizer ω`, the stabilizer of the chosen point of ω defined in the permutation-module file (as a rational number).
- `completePermutationLattice_herbrandQuotient_eq_stabilizerProduct`: there is a proof `h` that the Herbrand quotient of L is defined, and with it the quotient equals the same product.

## Proof

Through the basis isomorphism the sublattice's Herbrand quotient is that of `ι → ℤ` with the permutation action, computed in the permutation-module file (`permutationFunction_herbrandQuotient_eq_stabilizerProduct`). Then a finite-index stable subgroup has the same Herbrand quotient as the ambient group (`herbrandQuotient_eq_of_finiteIndex_stableSubgroup`), and the ambient quotient is defined (`finiteIndexStableSubgroup_ambientHerbrandQuotientDefined`); both are imported results.

## Watch for

- Cyclicity is the explicit pair `σ` with `hgen : ∀ g, g ∈ Subgroup.zpowers σ`, and the Tate group of degree −1 depends on σ.
- The Herbrand quotient machinery is the library's own (`HerbrandH0`, `HerbrandHMinusOne`, `herbrandQuotient`, `HerbrandQuotientDefined`), stated for groups written multiplicatively; lattices enter as `Multiplicative L`.
- Actions are not instances: the statements fix them with `let` bindings to the reducible definitions above, so a user must supply the same actions.
- The final theorem is existential in the definedness proof.
