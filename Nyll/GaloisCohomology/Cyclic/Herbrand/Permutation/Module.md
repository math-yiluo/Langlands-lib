# Herbrand quotients of permutation modules

Module `Nyll.GaloisCohomology.Cyclic.Herbrand.Permutation.Module`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For a finite cyclic group G generated by σ, the file computes the Herbrand quotient of integral permutation modules: it is `|G|` for ℤ with trivial action, `|H|` for the module induced from the trivial ℤ of a subgroup H, and the product of the stabilizer orders over the orbits for the integer-valued functions on a finite G-set. It also shows that the Herbrand quotient does not change on passing to a stable subgroup of finite index.

## Main results

- Trivial module (`trivialIntMulDistribMulAction`): `trivialIntHerbrandH0EquivZMod` identifies the degree 0 Tate group with `ℤ/|G|`, the norm kernel is trivial, so degree −1 is trivial, and `trivialInt_herbrandQuotient_eq_card` gives quotient `|G|`. These hold for any finite group; only σ enters.
- `TransitivePermutationLattice H` is the library's induced module `InducedModule` from H of `Multiplicative ℤ` with a given H-action; the theorems take the trivial action. `transitivePermutationLattice_herbrandQuotient_eq_stabilizerCard`: its Herbrand quotient is `|H|`, with both Tate groups finite.
- `PermutationLatticeOrbitFamily H` is the product of the modules for a finite family of subgroups `H i`; `permutationLattice_herbrandQuotient_eq_stabilizerProduct` gives `∏ i, |H i|`.
- `permutationFunctionMulDistribMulAction` is the action `(g·f)(i) = f(g⁻¹ i)` on `ι → ℤ` for a G-set ι. `permutationFunctionOrbitEquiv` decomposes it G-equivariantly into induced modules over the orbits, using chosen representatives (`chosenPermutationOrbitRepresentative`, `chosenPermutationOrbitTransport`) and their stabilizers `permutationOrbitStabilizer ω`. For finite ι, `permutationFunction_herbrandQuotient_eq_stabilizerProduct` gives `∏ ω, |Stab ω|`, with finiteness of both Tate groups.
- `stableSubgroupMulDistribMulAction` and `stableQuotientMulDistribMulAction` are the actions on a stable subgroup B and on `A ⧸ B`. If A/B is finite and the Herbrand quotient of B is defined, then that of A is defined (`finiteIndexStableSubgroup_ambientHerbrandQuotientDefined`) and the two are equal (`herbrandQuotient_eq_of_finiteIndex_stableSubgroup`).

## Proof

The induced case moves to the stabilizer by Shapiro's lemma for the Tate groups (`inducedHerbrandH0EquivOfFiniteCyclic`, `inducedHerbrandHMinusOneEquivOfFiniteCyclic`, with a generator of H obtained from σ). Products use `herbrandQuotient_pi`. The finite-index statement uses multiplicativity in short exact sequences and the fact that a finite module has Herbrand quotient 1, both imported.

## Watch for

- The Herbrand quotient is the library's `herbrandQuotient σ`, the ratio `|Ĥ⁰| / |Ĥ⁻¹|` in ℚ, defined only with proofs that both groups are finite. Modules are written multiplicatively (`Multiplicative ℤ`, `CommGroup A`).
- Cyclicity is the explicit hypothesis `hgen : ∀ g, g ∈ Subgroup.zpowers σ`.
- Actions are reducible definitions fixed by `let` in the statements, not instances.
- The orbit decomposition depends on chosen orbit representatives (`Quotient.out`) and transport elements (`Classical.choose`); the stabilizers in the formula are those of `ω.out`.
