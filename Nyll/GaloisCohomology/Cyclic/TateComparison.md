# Tate cohomology of finite cyclic groups in degrees 0 and −1

Module `Nyll.GaloisCohomology.Cyclic.TateComparison`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

The file identifies Mathlib's Tate cohomology `tateCohomology A n` in degrees 0 and −1 with the homology of explicit short complexes, for a representation A over a commutative ring R of a finite group G, all in one universe.

- `zeroBoundaryComplexIso`, `isoZeroBoundary`: for any finite G, `Ĥ⁰(G, A)` is the homology of `A --N--> A --d₀₁--> C¹(G, A)`, with N the norm and `d₀₁` the first cochain differential.
- `isoFiniteCyclicZero`: for G commutative and generated by g, `Ĥ⁰(G, A)` is the homology of `A --N--> A --(ρ(g) − 1)--> A` (Mathlib's `Rep.FiniteCyclicGroup.normHomCompSub`).
- `isoFiniteCyclicNegOne`: likewise `Ĥ⁻¹(G, A)` is the homology of `A --(ρ(g) − 1)--> A --N--> A` (`Rep.FiniteCyclicGroup.subCompNormHom`).

The proofs identify the relevant piece of the Tate complex with chains and cochains; the kernels agree because the invariants are the kernel of `ρ(g) − 1`, the images because the coinvariant kernel is its range.

The cyclic statements take a commutative group structure on G and the hypothesis `∀ x, x ∈ Subgroup.zpowers g` explicitly.
