# The cyclic norm-kernel vanishing condition

Module `Nyll.GaloisCohomology.Cyclic.NormKernelVanishing`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

The file sets up the condition, used in an abstract construction of class field theory, that for every finite cyclic extension L | K the Tate group of degree −1 of `G(L|K)` with coefficients in `A_L` vanishes. Here a "field" is a closed subgroup of a group G with a topology and `A_L` is the L-fixed part of a representation A. The file also proves, for finite cyclic groups, that `H¹ ≅ Ĥ⁻¹` and what vanishing of `Ĥ⁻¹` means for elements.

## Main definitions and results

- `isCyclic_of_generator`: a group with an element g such that every element lies in `Subgroup.zpowers g` is cyclic.
- `extensionSubgroup K L hLK` is L as a subgroup of K (`L.subgroupOf K`), for closed subgroups L ≤ K.
- `extensionFixedRepresentation A K L hLK hnormal` restricts A to K, takes the L-invariants and lets `K/L` act (Mathlib's `Rep.quotientToInvariants`); it needs L normal in K.
- `IsContinuousDiscreteRepresentation A`: the action map `G × A → A` is continuous when A has the discrete topology.
- `SatisfiesCyclicNormKernelVanishing A`: for all closed L ≤ K with L normal in K, `K/L` finite and generated by some g, Mathlib's `tateCohomology` of `extensionFixedRepresentation A K L` in degree −1 is zero.
- `finiteCyclicH1IsoTateHMinusOne`: for a finite group G generated by g and any commutative ring R, `H¹(G, A) ≅ Ĥ⁻¹(G, A)`.
- `normKernel_le_sigmaMinusOneRange_of_tateHMinusOne_isZero`: if `Ĥ⁻¹(G, A)` is zero, every element of norm zero is `g·y − y` for some y.

## Proof

The isomorphism composes Mathlib's computation of odd-degree cohomology of a finite cyclic group (`Rep.FiniteCyclicGroup.groupCohomologyIsoOdd`) with `TateCohomology.isoFiniteCyclicNegOne` from the Tate comparison file. The element statement uses Mathlib's description of when a class in odd degree vanishes.

## Watch for

- `SatisfiesCyclicNormKernelVanishing` is a definition, a property of A, not a theorem and not an axiom. It does not include profiniteness of G or continuity of A, which the construction assumes separately.
- The predicate needs R, G and the carrier of A in one universe u; the integral version for independent universes is in the integral norm-kernel vanishing file.
- Modules here are additive ℤ-linear (or R-linear) left representations. The docstring notes that the construction elsewhere writes modules multiplicatively with a right action, and that passing between the two replaces the generator by its inverse without changing these quotients.
