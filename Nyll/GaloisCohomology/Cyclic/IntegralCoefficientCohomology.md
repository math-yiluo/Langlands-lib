# Integral group cohomology for a group in any universe

Module `Nyll.GaloisCohomology.Cyclic.IntegralCoefficientCohomology`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Mathlib's group cohomology of `Rep k G` needs the coefficient ring k in the universe of G. For a group G in `Type u` and an integral representation `A : Rep.{u} ℤ G`, the file transports A to a representation over `ULift.{u} ℤ`, with the same carrier, action and maps, applies the cohomology, homology and Tate cohomology functors there, and restricts scalars back to ℤ. It records the connecting maps, their naturality and the long-exact-sequence exactness, and shows that in universe 0 the result is the usual integral (co)homology.

## Main definitions and results

All live in the namespace `IntegralGroupCohomology`.

- `coefficientFunctor G : Rep.{u} ℤ G ⥤ Rep.{u} (ULift ℤ) G` is the functor of the library's `Rep.coefficientRingEquivalence` along `ULift.ringEquiv`; it is additive and preserves finite limits and colimits. `integerScalars` restricts scalars from `ULift ℤ` to ℤ. Lemmas say the action, maps, restriction along `q : H →* G`, the degree-one coboundary and (for finite G) the norm are unchanged.
- `cohomologyFunctor G n`, `homologyFunctor G n` and the objects `cohomology A n`, `homology A n`; `coefficient_shortExact` keeps short exact sequences short exact.
- `δ`, the connecting map `Hⁱ(X₃) → Hʲ(X₁)` for `i + 1 = j`, with `δ_naturality` and the exactness statements `cohomology_exact₁`, `cohomology_exact₂`, `cohomology_exact₃`.
- For finite G: `tateFunctor G n` and `tate A n` (n ∈ ℤ), the Tate connecting map `tateδ` and its naturality.
- `cyclicH1IsoTateMinusOne`: for G generated by g, `H¹(A) ≅ Ĥ⁻¹(A)`. `normKernel_of_tate_isZero`: if `Ĥ⁻¹(A)` is zero, every element of norm zero has the form `g·y − y`.
- For a group H in `Type` (universe 0): `cohomologyIsoOriginal`, `homologyIsoOriginal` and, for finite H, `tateIsoOriginal` identify the new objects with Mathlib's `groupCohomology A n`, `groupHomology A n` and `tateCohomology A n`; `δ_cohomologyIsoOriginal` and `tateδ_tateIsoOriginal` show the connecting maps agree, and `isZero_tate_iff_original` transfers vanishing.

## Proof

Everything is obtained by applying a functor to the corresponding statement over `ULift ℤ`. The comparisons in universe 0 use the coefficient-ring isomorphisms `groupCohomology.coefficientRingIso`, `groupHomology.coefficientRingIso` and `TateCohomology.coefficientRingIso`, with their compatibility with connecting maps, from the imported coefficient-ring files, and the unit of the restriction-of-scalars equivalence. The cyclic statements apply `CyclicCohomology.finiteCyclicH1IsoTateHMinusOne` and `normKernel_le_sigmaMinusOneRange_of_tateHMinusOne_isZero`.

## Watch for

- The objects are the library's own: `cohomology A n` is not Mathlib's `groupCohomology A n` except through the explicit isomorphisms, which exist only in universe 0.
- `cyclicH1IsoTateMinusOne` and `normKernel_of_tate_isZero` need G finite and the explicit generator hypothesis `∀ x, x ∈ Subgroup.zpowers g`.
- The cohomology objects are ℤ-modules in `ModuleCat.{u} ℤ`, that is, ℤ-modules whose carrier lies in universe u.
