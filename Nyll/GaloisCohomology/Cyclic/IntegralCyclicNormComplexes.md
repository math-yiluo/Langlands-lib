# Norm complexes of a cyclic group and integral Tate cohomology

Module `Nyll.GaloisCohomology.Cyclic.IntegralCyclicNormComplexes`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For a finite group G generated by g and an integral representation A, the degree 0 and degree −1 Tate groups are the homology of the two complexes `A --N--> A --(g−1)--> A` and `A --(g−1)--> A --N--> A`, where N is the norm. The file builds these complexes for G and A in independent universes and identifies their homology with the integral Tate cohomology `tate A n` of the universe-independent construction (namespace `IntegralGroupCohomology.Universe`).

## Main definitions and results

- `normHomCompSub A g` and `subCompNormHom A g` are the two short complexes of ℤ-modules, built from the norm `A.norm` and `A.ρ g − id`; four `rfl` lemmas give their maps.
- `nativeIntegerCarrierIso`: the ULift of A's carrier is, as a ℤ-module, the carrier of the lifted representation `(nativeFunctor G).obj A` with scalars restricted to ℤ; the map is the identity.
- For G commutative: `normHomCompSubLiftIso` and `subCompNormHomLiftIso` identify the lifted complexes with Mathlib's `Rep.FiniteCyclicGroup.normHomCompSub` and `subCompNormHom` of the lifted representation.
- For G generated by g: `normHomologyEquivTateZero` gives an additive isomorphism from the homology of the norm complex to `tate A 0`, and `subNormHomologyEquivTateMinusOne` from the reverse complex to `tate A (-1)`.
- `isZero_tateZero_iff_exact`, `isZero_tateMinusOne_iff_exact`: the Tate group of degree 0 (respectively −1) vanishes exactly when the corresponding complex is exact.
- In universe 0, for a finite commutative group, the two complexes are by definition Mathlib's (`normHomCompSub_eq_native`, `subCompNormHom_eq_native`).

## Proof

The homology isomorphisms compose: homology commutes with the ULift functor and with restriction of scalars, the complexes are isomorphic to Mathlib's finite-cyclic ones, and the imported `TateCohomology.isoFiniteCyclicZero` and `TateCohomology.isoFiniteCyclicNegOne` compute Tate cohomology of a finite cyclic group from those complexes. A commutative group structure on G is derived from cyclicity.

## Watch for

- `tate A n` is the library's construction through a universe lift and a change of coefficient ring, not Mathlib's `tateCohomology` directly; the identification with the latter is in the universe-independent cohomology file.
- Cyclicity is the explicit hypothesis `∀ x, x ∈ Subgroup.zpowers g`, and the isomorphisms depend on the chosen generator g.
- The isomorphisms are of additive groups (`≃+`), not of ℤ-modules in `ModuleCat`.
