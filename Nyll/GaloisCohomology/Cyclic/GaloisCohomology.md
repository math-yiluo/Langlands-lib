# H¹ and Tate cohomology in degree −1 for cyclic groups

Module `Nyll.GaloisCohomology.Cyclic.GaloisCohomology`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For a finite cyclic group, Mathlib computes odd group cohomology through a complex built from the generator and the norm; the library's `TateCohomology.isoFiniteCyclicNegOne` compares the homology of that complex with Tate cohomology in degree −1. This file assembles the isomorphism H¹(G, A) ≅ Ĥ⁻¹(G, A) and applies it to the units of a finite field extension with cyclic automorphism group.

- `cyclic_H1_iso_HminusOne`: for a finite group G generated by `g` and a representation A of G over a commutative ring, `groupCohomology A 1` is isomorphic to `tateCohomology A (-1)`; the isomorphism depends on `g`.
- `unitsH1IsoTateHminusOne`: the same for Gal(L/K) acting on Lˣ, for a finite extension L/K, not assumed Galois, whose automorphism group is generated by one element.
- `hilbert90_unitsTateHminusOne_isZero`: then Ĥ⁻¹(Gal(L/K), Lˣ) is zero, by Mathlib's Hilbert 90 transported along the isomorphism.

All objects are Mathlib's own; no separate low-degree model is introduced. The general comparison takes ring, group and representation in one universe; the field results take `K` and `L` in `Type` (universe 0).
