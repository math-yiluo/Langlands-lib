# The restricted Kummer pairing for a finite Galois extension

Module `Nyll.GaloisCohomology.Kummer.Concrete.RestrictedFinite`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `Δ ≤ Kˣ` contain `Kˣⁿ`, let `K` contain a primitive `n`-th root of unity, and let `L/K` be a Galois extension in which every element of `Δ` has an `n`-th root (`Δ` lies in the radical subgroup `finiteKummerRadicalSubgroup` of `L/K`). The file restricts the Kummer character of `L/K` to `Δ`, descends it to `Δ/Kˣⁿ`, transposes the pairing, and proves it nondegenerate on the side of `Δ/Kˣⁿ`, and on the Galois side when the roots of `Δ` generate `L`; for finite `L/K` generated by the roots of `Δ` this gives a perfect pairing and recovers `Δ` from `L`.

## Main definitions

- `restrictedNthPowersSubgroup n Δ` (the copy of `Kˣⁿ` in `Δ`) and `RestrictedRadicalQuotient n Δ`, the quotient `Δ/Kˣⁿ`, with projection, lift and induction principle.
- `restrictedSubgroupKummerCharacter`, `restrictedQuotientKummerCharacter`: the character `Δ → Hom(Gal(L/K), μ_n(L))` and its descent to `Δ/Kˣⁿ`.
- `restrictedKummerTranspose`: the transpose `Gal(L/K) → Hom(Δ/Kˣⁿ, μ_n(L))`.
- `restrictedKummerFixed`: a primitive `n`-th root of unity in `K` implies that Galois fixes `μ_n(L)`.

## Main results

All but `restrictedRadicalQuotient_pow_eq_one` need `L/K` Galois.

- `ker_restrictedSubgroupKummerCharacter`, `restrictedQuotientKummerCharacter_injective`: the kernel on `Δ` is exactly `Kˣⁿ`, so the descended character is injective.
- `restrictedKummerTranspose_fixes_root`: an automorphism with trivial transpose fixes every `n`-th root in `L` of every element of `Δ`.
- `restrictedKummerTranspose_injective_of_adjoin`: if these roots generate `L`, the transpose is injective.
- `restrictedRadicalQuotient_pow_eq_one`: `Δ/Kˣⁿ` has exponent dividing `n`.
- `restrictedQuotientKummerCharacter_surjective_of_adjoin`, `restrictedKummerCharacterMulEquivOfAdjoin`: for `L/K` finite and generated by the roots of `Δ`, `Δ/Kˣⁿ ≃* Hom(Gal(L/K), μ_n(L))`.
- `finiteKummerRadicalSubgroup_eq_of_adjoin`: under the same hypotheses the radical subgroup of `L/K` equals `Δ` (the finite inverse direction of the Kummer correspondence).

## Proofs

The kernel statement is inherited from the exact kernel theorem for the full radical subgroup (`ker_kummerCharacterWithoutSection_eq_ambientNthPowers`). For surjectivity, the two injective maps together with finite roots-of-unity duality (`finiteNthRootsCharacterDuality`) give `|Δ/Kˣⁿ| ≤ |Gal(L/K)| ≤ |Δ/Kˣⁿ|`; commutativity and exponent dividing `n` of the Galois group are deduced from injectivity of the transpose. For the radical subgroup, any radical has the same character as some element of `Δ`, and the two differ by an `n`-th power, which already lies in `Δ`.

## Points to watch

- The hypothesis `hDelta`, that every element of `Δ` has an `n`-th root in `L`, and the generation hypothesis `hgenerate` are carried explicitly.
- Surjectivity, the isomorphism and the recovery of `Δ` are proved only for finite `L/K`; the two injectivity statements hold for any Galois `L/K` (the second under `hgenerate`).
