# The field ℚ(μ∞) and its finite cyclotomic levels

Module `Nyll.GaloisCohomology.Kummer.Concrete.Cyclotomic.RationalCyclotomicField`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

The file constructs the field `ℚ(μ∞)` concretely, as an intermediate field of Mathlib's `SeparableClosure ℚ`, together with its finite levels `ℚ(ζ_n)`, using Mathlib's `IsCyclotomicExtension` API.

## Main definitions and results

- `rationalCyclotomicField`: the subfield of `SeparableClosure ℚ` generated over `ℚ` by all roots of unity of nonzero order. Instances: it is a cyclotomic extension for all orders, and abelian Galois over `ℚ`.
- `rationalCyclotomicLevel n` for `n : ℕ+`: the subfield of `ℚ(μ∞)` generated by the `n`-th roots of unity. Instances: it is a cyclotomic extension for `{n}`, Galois over `ℚ`, and a number field.
- `exists_separableClosureRatAutomorphism_pow_of_unit`: for `m ≠ 0` and `u ∈ (ℤ/m)ˣ`, some automorphism of the separable closure of `ℚ` sends every `m`-th root of unity `z` to `z^u`.
- `rationalCyclotomicLevel_mono`: `m ∣ n` implies `ℚ(ζ_m) ≤ ℚ(ζ_n)`.
- `iSup_rationalCyclotomicLevel`: the levels exhaust `ℚ(μ∞)`.

## Proof

The automorphism of the separable closure is obtained from Mathlib's isomorphism `Gal(ℚ(ζ_m)/ℚ) ≃ (ℤ/m)ˣ` and surjectivity of restriction from the separable closure to the normal subextension.

## Points to watch

Levels are indexed by positive naturals, to keep order `0` out of the directed system. The level `ℚ(ζ_n)` is an intermediate field of `ℚ(μ∞)`, not of the separable closure.
