# The product of the local unit decompositions

Module `Nyll.GaloisCohomology.Kummer.Concrete.Cyclotomic.ProfiniteUnitDecomposition.Local`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

One step in building the decomposition of `ℤ̂ˣ`.

- `localDecomposition`: the topological group isomorphism `∏_p (F_p × ℤ_p) ≃ₜ* ∏_p ℤ_pˣ`, the product over all primes of the library's local decompositions `padicUnitDecomposition p : F_p × ℤ_p ≃ₜ* ℤ_pˣ`, where `F_p` is the finite factor and `ℤ_p` is written multiplicatively.

The definition lives in the internal namespace `KummerTheory.ProfiniteUnitDecomposition.Internal`.
