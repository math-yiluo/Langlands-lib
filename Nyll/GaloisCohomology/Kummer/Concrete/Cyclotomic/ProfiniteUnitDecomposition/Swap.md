# Swapping the finite and free factors

Module `Nyll.GaloisCohomology.Kummer.Concrete.Cyclotomic.ProfiniteUnitDecomposition.Swap`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

- `freeFiniteSwap`: the topological group isomorphism `CyclotomicFinitePart × ℤ̂ ≃ₜ* ℤ̂ × CyclotomicFinitePart` exchanging the factors, with `ℤ̂` written multiplicatively and `CyclotomicFinitePart` the product over all primes of the finite factors of the `p`-adic unit groups. It is an instance of a general product-swap equivalence from the library.

The definition lives in the internal namespace `KummerTheory.ProfiniteUnitDecomposition.Internal`.
