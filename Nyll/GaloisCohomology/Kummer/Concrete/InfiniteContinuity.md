# Continuity of Kummer characters for infinite extensions

Module `Nyll.GaloisCohomology.Kummer.Concrete.InfiniteContinuity`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For a Galois extension `Ω/K`, possibly infinite, in which every `n`-th root of unity of `Ω` comes from `K` (`NthRootsOfUnityInBase`), the Kummer character `σ ↦ σ(β)/β` attached to an `n`-th root `β` of an element of `Δ = Kˣ ∩ (Ωˣ)ⁿ` is shown to be continuous for the Krull topology, with `μ_n(Ω)` discrete. The resulting continuous characters are assembled into an injective homomorphism on `Δ / (Δ ∩ Kˣⁿ)`.

## Main definitions and results

- `DiscreteNthRootsSubgroup Ω n`: a copy of the library's group `μ_n(Ω)` carrying the discrete topology.
- `infiniteKummerRootCharacter n hmu a`: the Kummer character of `a ∈ Δ`, computed with the root chosen in `chosenFiniteKummerRadicalDatum`, valued in `DiscreteNthRootsSubgroup`.
- `fixingSubgroup_adjoin_root_le_infiniteKummerRootCharacter_ker`: the subgroup fixing the finite Galois intermediate field generated by the chosen root lies in the kernel.
- `infiniteKummerRootCharacter_isOpen_ker` and `infiniteKummerRootCharacter_continuous`: hence the kernel is open and the character is continuous.
- `infiniteKummerContinuousCharacter`: `Δ → Hom_cont(Gal(Ω/K), μ_n(Ω))`, a group homomorphism.
- `infiniteKummerContinuousQuotientCharacter`: its descent to `Δ / (Δ ∩ Kˣⁿ)` (`RadicalQuotient`); after forgetting continuity it is the algebraic Kummer character (`infiniteKummerContinuousQuotientCharacter_toMonoidHom`).
- `infiniteKummerContinuousQuotientCharacter_injective`: the descended map is injective, by the algebraic kernel computation from the library's radical-quotient file.

## Points to watch

- Surjectivity onto continuous characters is not asserted here.
- The roots-of-unity hypothesis is the field-level `NthRootsOfUnityInBase`, not a primitive root in `K`.
