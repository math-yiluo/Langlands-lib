# The Kummer pairing for a radical extension K(ⁿ√Δ)

Module `Nyll.GaloisCohomology.Kummer.Concrete.KummerCorrespondenceFormula`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `K` be a field in which `n` is nonzero and which contains a primitive `n`-th root of unity, let `Ω` be a separable closure of `K`, and let `Δ` be a subgroup of `Kˣ` containing `Kˣⁿ`. The file shows that the canonical Kummer pairing identifies the Galois group of the radical extension `E = K(ⁿ√Δ) ⊆ Ω` with the full group of characters `Hom(Δ/Kˣⁿ, μ_n(E))`. The extension `E/K` is not assumed finite.

## Main results

- `kummerRadicalExtension_internalRoots_adjoin_eq_top`: the `n`-th roots of elements of `Δ`, taken inside `E`, generate `E` over `K`.
- `restrictedKummerTransposeMulEquivOfAdjoin`: for a finite Galois `L/K` in which every element of `Δ` has an `n`-th root and which is generated by those roots, `Gal(L/K) ≃* Hom(Δ/Kˣⁿ, μ_n(L))`. That the Galois group is commutative of exponent dividing `n` is derived from injectivity of the pairing.
- `exists_pairing_element_matching_finset`: an abstract lemma. Given a compact commutative topological group `G`, a commutative group `R` of exponent dividing `n`, and a pairing `R × G → μ_n(L)` (discrete) that is injective on the `R` side and continuous in `G`, every character of `R` is matched by a single `g ∈ G` on any finite subset of `R`.
- `kummerRadicalExtensionRestrictedTranspose`: the canonical map `Gal(E/K) → Hom(Δ/Kˣⁿ, μ_n(E))`, with `_injective` and `_surjective`.
- `kummerRadicalExtensionRestrictedTransposeMulEquiv`: the resulting group isomorphism (the displayed formula of the Kummer correspondence).
- `kummerRadicalExtension_finiteDimensional_of_finite_quotient`: if `Δ/Kˣⁿ` is finite, then `E/K` is finite.

Helper results: each evaluation `σ ↦ ⟨σ, q⟩` is continuous for the Krull topology and the discrete topology on `μ_n` (`restrictedKummerTranspose_evaluation_continuous`); the sets where it takes a prescribed value (`restrictedKummerEvaluationLocus`) are closed and compact.

## Proofs

Injectivity follows from the generation theorem. For surjectivity, finitely many coordinates factor through the finite quotient of the Galois group by the intersection of their open kernels; finite roots-of-unity duality solves that finite problem, and the finite intersection property in the compact Galois group yields one automorphism for all coordinates.

## Points to watch

- `Δ/Kˣⁿ` is the library's `RestrictedRadicalQuotient n Delta` and `Δ` is a `KummerSubgroup K n`; roots of unity are the library's `nthRootsSubgroup`, with the discrete topology through the synonym `DiscreteNthRootsSubgroup`.
- The characters take values in `μ_n(E)`, computed in `E`. The isomorphism is one of abstract groups; no topology is placed on the character group.
- Hypotheses of the results on `E` from the canonical map onwards: `n : ℕ+`, `(n : K) ≠ 0`, a primitive `n`-th root of unity in `K`, and `Ω` a separable closure of `K`. The generation theorem needs only `n : ℕ+` and a field extension `Ω` of `K`; the finite isomorphism and the abstract lemma need, of these, only the primitive root.
