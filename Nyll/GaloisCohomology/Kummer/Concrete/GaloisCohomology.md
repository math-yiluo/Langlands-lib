# Forms of Hilbert's Theorem 90 for Kummer theory

Module `Nyll.GaloisCohomology.Kummer.Concrete.GaloisCohomology`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

The file restates Mathlib's Hilbert 90 results in the shapes used by concrete Kummer theory. Throughout, `L/K` is a finite extension of fields.

## Noether's form

For a multiplicative 1-cocycle `f : Gal(L/K) → Lˣ` (`IsMulCocycle₁ f`):

- `noetherHilbert90_exists_mul`: there is `β ∈ Lˣ` with `σ(β) = f(σ) β` for all `σ`.
- `noetherHilbert90_exists_nonzero_div` and `noetherHilbert90_exists_nonzero_mul`: the same with `β ∈ L` nonzero, written `σ(β)/β = f(σ)` or `σ(β) = f(σ) β`.

## Cyclic form

For `L/K` Galois with `Gal(L/K)` generated by `g`, and `x` of norm `1`:

- `cyclicHilbert90_exists_div_of_norm_eq_one` and `cyclicHilbert90_exists_nonzero_div_of_norm_eq_one`: `x = y / g(y)` for some unit (resp. nonzero) `y`.
- `cyclicHilbert90_exists_gal_div_of_norm_eq_one`: `x = g(y) / y`, obtained by applying Mathlib's statement to `g⁻¹`.
- `cyclicHilbert90_exists_unit_div_of_norm_eq_one` and `cyclicHilbert90_exists_unit_mul_gal_of_norm_eq_one`: for `x ∈ Lˣ`, `x = y / g•y`, equivalently `x · g•y = y`.

## Integral form

- `cyclicHilbert90_exists_mul_galRestrict_of_norm_eq_one`: let `A` be a domain with fraction field `K` and `B` the integral closure of `A` in `L`, with `L/K` cyclic Galois generated by `g`. If `η ∈ B` has norm `1`, there is a nonzero `ε ∈ B` with `η · g(ε) = ε`, where `g` acts on `B` through `galRestrict`.

The proofs are appeals to Mathlib's `groupCohomology` Hilbert 90 lemmas; `noetherHilbert90_exists_nonzero_mul` and `cyclicHilbert90_exists_unit_mul_gal_of_norm_eq_one` rearrange the file's own division forms.

## Points to watch

The fields `K` and `L` are taken in `Type` (universe `0`), not in an arbitrary universe.
