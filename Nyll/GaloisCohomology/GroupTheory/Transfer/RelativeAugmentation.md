# Transfer and the relative augmentation quotient

Module `Nyll.GaloisCohomology.GroupTheory.Transfer.RelativeAugmentation`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For a subgroup `H` of a group `G`, the file builds the transfer `Gᵃᵇ → Hᵃᵇ` (for `H` of finite index) and the relative augmentation quotient `(I_H + I_G I_H) / I_G I_H` inside the integral group ring `ℤ[G]`, identifies that quotient with `Hᵃᵇ`, and proves that, through the identifications `Gᵃᵇ ≅ I_G / I_G²` and `Hᵃᵇ ≅ (I_H + I_G I_H) / I_G I_H`, the transfer becomes multiplication by the sum of a left transversal.

## Main definitions

- `transferToAbelianization`, `abelianizedTransfer`: Mathlib's `MonoidHom.transfer` into `Hᵃᵇ`, and its factorization through `Gᵃᵇ`; `abelianizedTransfer_of_eq_diff` and `abelianizedTransfer_of_eq_prod_transferComponent` give the transversal formula as a product over `G ⧸ H`.
- `subgroupRingMap`: the injective ring map `ℤ[H] → ℤ[G]`; `embeddedSubgroupIdeal` is the image of `I_H`.
- `mixedAugmentationProduct`: `I_G I_H`, the additive subgroup generated by the products `x y` with `x ∈ I_G`, `y` in the image of `I_H`.
- `relativeAugmentationNumerator` (`I_H + I_G I_H`) and `RelativeAugmentationQuotient` (the quotient by `I_G I_H`).
- `relativeDeltaClass h`: the class of `h − 1`; it is additive in `h` (`relativeDeltaClass_mul`).
- `augmentationTransfer`: the map `I_G / I_G² → (I_H + I_G I_H) / I_G I_H` obtained from the transfer by composing with the two augmentation isomorphisms (in multiplicative notation).
- `transversalNormElement T`: the sum in `ℤ[G]` of the representatives of a left transversal `T`.

## Main results

- `relativeDeltaAbelianizationEquiv`: `h ↦ class of h − 1` induces an isomorphism `Hᵃᵇ ≃* (I_H + I_G I_H) / I_G I_H`, for any subgroup `H` (no finite index needed).
- `augmentationTransfer_deltaAbelianization`: the transfer/augmentation square commutes.
- `augmentationTransfer_deltaClass_eq_deltaNorm` (the norm-element formula): the image of the class of `g − 1` is the class of `(g − 1) · ∑ρ`, for every left transversal `T`.
- `augmentationTransfer_apply_quotientMk` (the full formula): for every `x ∈ I_G`, the image of `x mod I_G²` is `x · ∑ρ mod I_G I_H`.

## Proofs

Injectivity uses the additive map `ℤ[G] → Hᵃᵇ` sending `g` to its `H`-component relative to a fixed transversal (`transversalLinearization`); it kills `I_G I_H` and so induces a left inverse on the relative augmentation quotient. The norm formula rests on the identity `(g − 1)·∑ρ = ∑_q ρ_q (t_q − 1)`, with `t_q` the transfer components. The augmentation ideal and `Gᵃᵇ ≅ I_G / I_G²` come from the library's `GroupTheory.Augmentation` file.

## Points to watch

- `I_G I_H` and the numerator are defined as additive subgroups of `ℤ[G]` (`AddSubgroup`), not as ideals.
- The quotients are additive groups, wrapped in `Multiplicative` to match the abelianizations.
- The transfer statements need `H.FiniteIndex`; the formulas hold for every choice of left transversal.
