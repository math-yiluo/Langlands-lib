# The class field axiom for cyclic extensions of local fields

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LocalClassFieldTheory.Finite.LocalReciprocity.FiniteExtensionClassFieldAxiom`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

This file states and proves the local class field axiom for a finite cyclic extension `L / K` of a nonarchimedean local field: the Tate group `Ĥ⁰(Gal(L / K), Lˣ)` has order `[L : K]` and `Ĥ⁻¹(Gal(L / K), Lˣ)` is trivial. No local-field structure on `L` is assumed: it is constructed inside the proof from the spectral norm.

## Main definitions and results

- `UnitsTateCardinalityData K L`: a structure recording, for the unit representation `Rep.ofAlgebraAutOnUnits K L`, that `tateCohomology` in degree `0` is finite with cardinality `Module.finrank K L`, and that in degree `-1` it has cardinality `1`. Bundling the finiteness proof keeps the `Nat.card` statement meaningful.
- `finiteExtensionUnits_tate_card_of_generator`: for `K` a nonarchimedean local field and `L / K` finite Galois with `Gal(L / K)` generated by an element `g` (hypothesis `hg`), this data exists.
- `finiteTowerUnits_tate_card_of_generator`: the same conclusion when `K` is only assumed finite over a nonarchimedean local field `k`; `K` receives its local-field structure from the spectral norm over `k`.

## Proof

`L` is given the spectral norm over `K`, making it a complete, locally compact, ultrametric normed field, hence a nonarchimedean local field whose valuation extends that of `K`. Integrality of `𝒪_L` over `𝒪_K` is proved directly: an element of norm at most `1` has a minimal polynomial with coefficients of norm at most `1` (`spectralValue_le_one_iff`). This yields `𝒪_L` as the integral closure of `𝒪_K` and its module-finiteness, after which the imported cyclic results `localFieldUnitsTateH0FiniteOfGenerator` and `localFieldUnits_tate_card_of_generator` give the cardinalities.

## Points to watch

- Only cyclic extensions are covered: the generator `g` and the hypothesis `hg` are part of the statement.
- The `-1` statement is a cardinality statement, `Nat.card = 1`.
- All fields are in universe `0`.
