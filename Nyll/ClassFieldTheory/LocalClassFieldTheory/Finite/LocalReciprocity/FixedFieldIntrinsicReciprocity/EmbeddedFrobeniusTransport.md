# Transport of Frobenius elements and Frobenius fixed fields between separable closures

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LocalClassFieldTheory.Finite.LocalReciprocity.FixedFieldIntrinsicReciprocity.EmbeddedFrobeniusTransport`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `K ⊆ F ⊆ E` with `K`, `F` nonarchimedean local fields, `F/K` finite separable with compatible valuations, and `E/F` finite Galois. Fix a `K`-embedding `j : E →ₐ[K] K^sep` and an `F`-isomorphism `e` from the separable closure of `F` to `K^sep`. Let `H₀ ⊇ J₀` be the subgroups of `Gal(K^sep/K)` fixing the images of `F` and `E`. Conjugation by `e` identifies `Gal(F^sep/F)` with `H₀` (`intrinsicBaseEquivAmbientEmbeddedField`). The file shows that this identification is a homeomorphism and carries over the Frobenius data of `localResidueDatum`: inertia quotients, normalized degrees, Frobenius elements, their restrictions to `Gal(E/F)`, the closed subgroups they generate, and their fixed fields.

## Topology and quotients

- `map_intrinsicExtensionInertia_eq_ambientFixedField`: the fixed-field version, for `F` the fixed field of a finite abstract field `H`. The isomorphism `intrinsicBaseEquivAmbientFixedField K H e` maps the intrinsic extension inertia onto the ambient one.
- `intrinsicBaseEquivAmbientFixedField_continuous` and `intrinsicBaseEquivAmbientEmbeddedField_continuous`: both identifications are continuous for the Krull topologies.
- `intrinsicBaseContinuousEquivAmbientEmbeddedField`: the identification as an isomorphism of topological groups. The inverse is continuous because the source is compact and the target Hausdorff.
- `intrinsicFrobeniusQuotientContinuousEquivAmbientEmbeddedField`: the induced topological isomorphism between the quotients by extension inertia, intrinsic over `F` and ambient for `J₀ ≤ H₀`. `..._normalizedDegree` says it preserves the extension normalized degree.

## Frobenius data

- `intrinsicFrobeniusElementToAmbientEmbeddedField`: sends a Frobenius element over `F` to a Frobenius element of the ambient datum. It is well defined because the normalized degree is preserved.
- `intrinsicExtensionRestriction_compatibility_ambientEmbeddedField` and `intrinsicFrobeniusRestriction_compatibility_ambientEmbeddedField`: restriction to `Gal(E/F)` commutes with the transport, through the two quotient isomorphisms with `Gal(E/F)`.
- `intrinsicFrobeniusClosure_iff_ambientEmbeddedField`: a class lies in the closed subgroup generated by `σ` if and only if its image lies in the closed subgroup generated by the transported `σ`.
- `intrinsicFrobeniusFixedSubgroup_iff_ambientEmbeddedField`: the same for the Frobenius fixed subgroups in the absolute Galois groups.
- `intrinsicFrobeniusFixedFieldEquivAmbientEmbeddedField`: restricting `e` gives an `F`-algebra isomorphism from the Frobenius fixed field of `σ` in `F^sep` to the relative fixed field in `K^sep` of the transported Frobenius subgroup. Here `F` acts on the latter through `j`. `..._apply_val` says it is `e` on elements.

## Proofs

The fixed-field isomorphism uses the previous equivalence, together with the description of the Frobenius fixed field's group as the Frobenius fixed subgroup (`extensionSubgroup_frobeniusFixedField`).

## Points to watch

- Inertia, normalized degrees, `FrobeniusElements`, Frobenius closures and fixed fields are the library's notions attached to its residue datum `localResidueDatum`, not Mathlib's Frobenius elements of primes.
- The local statements take the fields in `Type`; every result depends on the chosen `e`, and `j` enters only the statements about the embedded `E/F`.
