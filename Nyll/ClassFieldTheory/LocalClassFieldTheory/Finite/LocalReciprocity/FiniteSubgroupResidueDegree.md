# Residue degree of a finite-index fixed field

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LocalClassFieldTheory.Finite.LocalReciprocity.FiniteSubgroupResidueDegree`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `K` be a nonarchimedean local field, with the selected valuation subring of its separable closure, residue field `k` and selected residue field `Ω` (an algebraic closure of `k`). For a closed subgroup `H` of `Gal(K^sep/K)` with fixed field `E`, the file identifies the image of `H` under the residue action with the fixing group of the residue field of `E` inside `Ω`, and deduces, for `H` of finite index, that the residue degree attached to `H` by `localResidueDatum` is the degree of that residue field over `k`. The comparison takes place inside the common `Ω`, so `E` need not be normal over `K`.

## Main definitions and results

- `localAbstractFixedResidueIntermediateField K H`: the intermediate field of `Ω/k` generated by the image of the residue field of `E`.
- `localAbstractFixedDecompositionGroup_eq_top`: every automorphism over `E` preserves the selected valuation subring.
- `localAbstractFixedResidueAction_apply`: for `σ ∈ H`, the residue action over `K` and the residue action over `E` give the same automorphism of `Ω`.
- `localAbstractFixedResidueAction_map_eq_fixingSubgroup`: the image of `H` under `localSeparableResidueAlgAction K` is exactly the fixing subgroup of that intermediate field; the reverse inclusion uses surjectivity of the residue action over `E`.
- `localAbstractFixedResidueActionOverIntermediateField`: the residue action of `H` as automorphisms of `Ω` over the intermediate field; its `_apply` lemma says the underlying map is unchanged.
- `localAbstractFixedResidueIntermediateField_finiteDimensional`: if `H` has finite index (stated as finiteness of the quotient of `baseField` by `extensionSubgroup`), the intermediate field is finite-dimensional over `k`. The index of the image of `H` divides the index of `H` by surjectivity of the absolute residue action, and a closed subgroup of finite index is open.
- `localAbstractFixedResidueFiniteGaloisIntermediateField`: the same field packaged as a `FiniteGaloisIntermediateField` (Galois since the fields are finite).
- `localResidueDatum_residueDegree_eq_selectedResidueFinrank`: for a `FiniteAbstractField` `H`, the residue degree given by `localResidueDatum K` equals the `k`-dimension of the residue subfield.
- `localResidueDatum_normalizedDegree_eq_residueAbsoluteDegreeIn`: for `σ ∈ H`, the normalized degree of `σ` equals the absolute residue degree (in multiplicative `Ẑ`) of its residue action, computed over the residue subfield instead of over `k`. It is proved by multiplying both sides by the residue degree and cancelling, using the previous theorem.

## Points to watch

- `FiniteAbstractField`, the residue degree and the normalized degree are the library's own notions from its class formation framework (`localResidueDatum`), not Mathlib's; `abstractFixedField` is the library's name for the ordinary fixed field of the subgroup.
- The residue field of `E` is replaced by the subfield of `Ω` it generates, defined with `adjoin` rather than through a scalar tower.
- The theorems labelled "finite local reciprocity" in the docstrings are degree comparisons used on the way to reciprocity, not the reciprocity law itself.
