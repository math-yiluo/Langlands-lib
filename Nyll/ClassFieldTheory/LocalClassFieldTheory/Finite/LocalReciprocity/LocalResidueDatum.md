# The residue degree datum of a local field

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LocalClassFieldTheory.Finite.LocalReciprocity.LocalResidueDatum`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For a nonarchimedean local field `K`, this file constructs the degree map `Gal(SeparableClosure K / K) → ZHat` used by the abstract class formation: a Galois automorphism acts on the residue field of the separable closure, and that action is measured against Frobenius of the finite residue field of `K`. The choices this needs are made explicit.

## Main definitions and results

- `localAbsoluteValuationSubring`: a valuation ring of `AlgebraicClosure K` extending that of `K`, chosen by Chevalley's extension theorem (`Classical.choose`).
- `localSeparableValuationSubring`: its pullback to `SeparableClosure K`; `localSeparableValuationSubring_pullback` says it restricts to the valuation ring of `K`.
- `localSeparableValuationSubring_eq_of_hasExtension`: every valuation ring of `SeparableClosure K` extending that of `K` equals it, so the choice does not matter. Consequently `localSeparableDecompositionGroup_eq_top`: the decomposition group is the whole Galois group.
- `localBaseResidueEquivDecompositionResidue`: the residue field of `K` is isomorphic to the residue field of the decomposition field, compatibly with reduction (`localBaseResidueEquivDecompositionResidue_algebraMap`); hence the latter is finite.
- `localSelectedResidueIsAlgClosed` (instance): the residue field of `localSeparableValuationSubring K` is algebraically closed.
- `localSeparableResidueAlgAction`: the residue action as a continuous homomorphism for the Krull topologies; `localSeparableResidueAlgAction_surjective`: it is surjective.
- `localResidueDegree`: the composite of the residue action with the degree map `residueAbsoluteDegreeIn` of the finite residue field, a continuous homomorphism to `ZHatMul` (the profinite integers, written multiplicatively); `localResidueDegree_surjective`: it is surjective.
- `localResidueDatum`: the resulting `DegreeData` of the class formation.

## Proofs

Uniqueness of the extension is checked on the finite separable field generated by one element, where the extension of a complete discrete valuation is unique. Algebraic closedness: the residue extension up to `AlgebraicClosure K` is purely inseparable, while the residue field of the separable closure is perfect, so the two residue fields agree and the larger is algebraically closed. Continuity: an open subgroup fixing a finite residue extension is reached by adjoining to `K` one lift of each of its elements.

## Points to watch

`localSeparableValuationSubring` is defined through a choice; only the uniqueness theorem makes it canonical. `DegreeData` is the library's own notion.
