# Order reversal for finite abelian extensions in terms of field norms

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LocalClassFieldTheory.Finite.Existence.OrderReversal`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

This file passes from concrete finite abelian extensions `L / K` to the library's abstract finite abelian subextensions of the absolute Galois group of `K`, and transports the abstract order reversal for norm subgroups back to ordinary field norms. It also shows that the norm subgroup of a finite abelian extension of a local field contains a standard open subgroup.

## Main definitions and results

- `finiteAbelianAbstractExtensionOfEmbedding K L i`: for `L / K` finite and abelian Galois with a `K`-embedding `i : L →ₐ[K] SeparableClosure K`, the corresponding abstract finite abelian subextension. Commutativity is transported from `Gal(L / K)` through an isomorphism with the abstract quotient.
- `map_finiteAbelianAbstractExtension_normSubgroup_eq`: transported to `Kˣ`, the abstract norm subgroup of this object is the ordinary norm subgroup of `L` (in additive notation).
- `nonempty_algHom_of_normSubgroup_le`: let `K` be a nonarchimedean local field and `L`, `M` finite abelian Galois extensions. If `N(Mˣ) ≤ N(Lˣ)`, there is a `K`-algebra map `L → M`.
- `exists_uniformizerPrincipalSubgroup_le_normSubgroup`: for every `ϖ ∈ Kˣ` there are `d > 0` and `n ≥ 1` with `uniformizerPrincipalSubgroup K ϖ d n ≤ N(Lˣ)`; from the proof, this subgroup is generated by `ϖ ^ d` together with the principal units `U^(n)`.
- `exists_uniformizerPrincipalSubgroup_one_le_normSubgroup`: if `ϖ` is itself a norm, one may take `d = 1`.

## Proofs

For `nonempty_algHom_of_normSubgroup_le`, both fields are embedded in `SeparableClosure K`; the abstract order reversal `FiniteAbelianSubextension.le_iff_normSubgroup_le` turns the norm containment into an inclusion of fixing subgroups, hence of field ranges, and the embedding is the resulting composite. In the standard-subgroup lemma, `d` is the index of the norm subgroup, finite because `Kˣ / N(Lˣ)` is identified with the abelianization of `Gal(L / K)` (`abelianizationEquivNormQuotient`); `n` comes from openness of the norm subgroup (`localNormSubgroup_isOpen`).

## Points to watch

- `nonempty_algHom_of_normSubgroup_le` is one direction only: norm containment gives an embedding. The converse is not stated here.
- The docstrings speak of a prime element, but `ϖ` is an arbitrary unit of `K` in both standard-subgroup lemmas; no uniformizer hypothesis is made.
- All results except `finiteAbelianAbstractExtensionOfEmbedding` take `K`, `L`, `M : Type` in universe `0`.
