# Index of the canonical standard subgroup

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LocalClassFieldTheory.LubinTateApplication.StandardSubgroupIndex`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `K` be a nonarchimedean local field (Mathlib's `IsNonarchimedeanLocalField`) with residue field of cardinality `q`. The file computes the index in `Kˣ` of the subgroup generated by the canonical Lubin–Tate uniformizer and the principal units `U^(n+1)`: it is `(q − 1)·q^n`.

## Main definitions and results

- `standardLubinTateCanonicalBaseUniformizerUnit_eq_integerRingUniformizerFieldUnit`: the uniformizer of the packaged local field `standardLocalField K` used by the standard Lubin–Tate construction is the chosen integer-ring uniformizer of `K`, as a unit of `K`. Its inverse has normalized additive valuation `1` (`standardLubinTateCanonicalBaseUniformizerUnit_inv_valuationMap`).
- `standardLubinTateUnitParameterEquivIntegerUnitsPrincipalQuotient`: a group isomorphism between the standard finite Lubin–Tate unit parameters of level `n` and the quotient of the integer units of `K` by `U^(n+1)`.
- `standardLubinTateCanonicalUniformizerPrincipalSubgroup_index`: the subgroup generated by the canonical uniformizer and `U^(n+1)` has index `(q − 1)·q^n`.
- `subgroup_eq_of_le_of_index_eq_of_ne_zero`: in any group, if `H ≤ N`, the indices are equal and the index of `H` is nonzero, then `H = N`.

## Proof

The subgroup is unchanged when the uniformizer is replaced by its inverse, whose valuation is `1`. The quotient of `Kˣ` by it is then identified with the quotient of integer units by `U^(n+1)`, which is transported to the standard unit parameters, whose cardinality is known from the finite-parameter count.

## Points to watch

- The index statement concerns only the canonical uniformizer of `standardLocalField K`, not an arbitrary one.
- In `subgroup_eq_of_le_of_index_eq_of_ne_zero` the nonzero-index hypothesis is needed because Mathlib's `Subgroup.index` is `0` for infinite index.
