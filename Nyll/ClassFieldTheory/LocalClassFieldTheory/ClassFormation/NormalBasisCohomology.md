# Vanishing Herbrand groups of a deep normal-basis unit subgroup

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LocalClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.NormalBasisCohomology`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `L/K` be a finite Galois extension of nonarchimedean local fields whose Galois group is generated by `g`, with `𝒪_L` the integral closure of `𝒪_K` and finite over it.

- `exists_chosenNormalBasisPrincipalUnit_herbrand_subsingleton`: there is `c` such that for every `n ≥ c` and every subgroup `V` of `𝒪_Lˣ` whose underlying set is `chosenNormalBasisPrincipalUnitSet K L n`, both `HerbrandH0 Gal(L/K) V` and `HerbrandHMinusOne Gal(L/K) V g` are trivial.

The proof combines two results of the recursive-lifting file: at deep levels, fixed elements of `V` are norms of elements of `V` (`exists_chosenNormalBasisPrincipalUnit_fixed_is_tateNorm`), and elements of norm one have the form `g(b)/b` with `b` in `V` (`exists_chosenNormalBasisPrincipalUnit_normOne_is_sigmaMinusOne`). The two inclusions give the vanishing.

The docstring calls this the local class-field-axiom theorem; it is the vanishing step for `V` only, from which the statement on `Lˣ` is derived in another file. In Lean, `K` and `L` are taken in the same universe, the valuation of `L` extends that of `K` (`Valuation.HasExtension`), and `L` carries a `UniformSpace` structure with `IsUniformAddGroup`.
