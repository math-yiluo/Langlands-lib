# Low-degree Herbrand groups of local units as Tate groups

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LocalClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.LocalizedCompletionCohomology.HerbrandEquiv`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `ell/k` be a finite Galois extension, `v` a nontrivial absolute value on `k` with completion `K_v`, and `w` an extension of `v`, with algebraic localization `L_w = LocalizedCompletion v w`. The decomposition group `D_w` of `w` acts on `L_wˣ`. The file identifies the library's multiplicative Herbrand groups of this action with the Tate cohomology of `Aut(L_w/K_v)` acting on `L_wˣ` (the representation `Rep.ofAlgebraAutOnUnits`), and degree zero with the norm quotient.

## Definitions

- `localHerbrandH0EquivUnitsTateH0`: a group isomorphism between `HerbrandH0 D_w L_wˣ` and `tateCohomology` in degree 0, viewed multiplicatively.
- `localHerbrandHMinusOneEquivUnitsTateHminusOne`: if `Gal(ell/k)` is generated by `σ`, a bijection between `HerbrandHMinusOne D_w L_wˣ`, taken with respect to the generator `subgroupGeneratorOfGenerator` derived from `σ`, and `tateCohomology` in degree −1.
- `localHerbrandH0EquivNormQuotient`: a group isomorphism between `HerbrandH0 D_w L_wˣ` and `NormQuotient K_v L_w`, the quotient of `K_vˣ` by the norms from `L_wˣ`.

## Proofs

The acting group is first changed from `D_w` to `Aut(L_w/K_v)` along `decompositionGroupEquivAlgebraicLocalizationAut`, with `herbrandH0CompMulEquiv` and `herbrandHMinusOneCompMulEquiv`. In degree 0 the result is compared with Tate cohomology through the invariants-modulo-norms description (`herbrandH0MulEquivInvariantsNormQuotient`, `tateUnitsH0IsoInvariantsQuotient`), and then with the norm quotient by `H0TateUnitsIsoNormQuotient`. In degree −1 the comparison `herbrandHminusOneEquivUnitsTateHminusOne` uses the generator `localizedCompletionGaloisGenerator`.

## Points to note

- The degree −1 identification is an equivalence of types (`≃`), not a group isomorphism; it serves cardinality arguments.
- No local-field hypothesis on `K_v` is made: these identifications hold for any nontrivial `v`. Only the degree −1 statement needs `Gal(ell/k)` cyclic.
- All algebra, finiteness and Galois structures on `L_w` over `K_v` are fixed in the statements as `let`-bound instances.
- The fields `k` and `ell` are taken in `Type` (universe 0).
