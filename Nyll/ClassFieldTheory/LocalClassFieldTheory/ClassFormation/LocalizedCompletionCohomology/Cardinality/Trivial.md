# Elementwise triviality of the local degree −1 Herbrand group

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LocalClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.LocalizedCompletionCohomology.Cardinality.Trivial`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

The file restates the vanishing of the local degree −1 Herbrand group in elementwise form.

- `localHerbrandHMinusOne_eq_one`: let `ell/k` be finite Galois with Galois group generated by `σ`, `v` a nontrivial absolute value on `k` whose completion `K_v` is a nonarchimedean local field, and `w` an extension of `v`. Then every element of `HerbrandHMinusOne` of the decomposition group of `w` acting on `L_wˣ`, taken with respect to the generator `subgroupGeneratorOfGenerator` derived from `σ`, equals 1. Here `L_w` is `LocalizedCompletion v w`.

It follows from the cardinality statement `localHerbrandHMinusOne_card_eq_one`, together with finiteness from `localHerbrandHMinusOneFinite`: a group of cardinality one is a subsingleton.

The local-field structure of `K_v` enters as instance arguments (`ValuativeRel`, `IsNonarchimedeanLocalField`), not derived from `v`, and the whole group `Gal(ell/k)` must be cyclic. The fields `k` and `ell` are taken in `Type` (universe 0).
