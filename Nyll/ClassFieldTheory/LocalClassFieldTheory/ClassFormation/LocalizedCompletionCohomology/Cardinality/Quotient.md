# The Herbrand quotient of local units is the local degree

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LocalClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.LocalizedCompletionCohomology.Cardinality.Quotient`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

The file computes the Herbrand quotient of the units of a local field under the action of a decomposition group.

- `localUnits_herbrandQuotient_eq_localDegree`: let `ell/k` be finite Galois with Galois group generated by `σ`, `v` a nontrivial absolute value on `k` whose completion `K_v` is a nonarchimedean local field, and `w` an extension of `v`. Then the Herbrand quotient of the decomposition group of `w` acting on `L_wˣ`, with respect to the generator `subgroupGeneratorOfGenerator` derived from `σ`, equals `[L_w : K_v]` as a rational number. Here `L_w` is `LocalizedCompletion v w`.

The proof writes the quotient as `|H⁰| / |H⁻¹|` (`herbrandQuotient_eq_card_ratio`) and inserts the two local computations `localHerbrandH0_card_eq_localDegree` and `localHerbrandHMinusOne_card_eq_one`.

The local-field structure of `K_v` enters as instance arguments (`ValuativeRel`, `IsNonarchimedeanLocalField`), not derived from `v`, and the whole group `Gal(ell/k)` must be cyclic. `herbrandQuotient` is the library's own Herbrand quotient. The fields `k` and `ell` are taken in `Type` (universe 0).
