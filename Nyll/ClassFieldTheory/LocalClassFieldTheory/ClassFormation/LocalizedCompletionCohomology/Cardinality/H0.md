# Order of the local degree-zero Herbrand group

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LocalClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.LocalizedCompletionCohomology.Cardinality.H0`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

The file proves the degree-zero half of the local class-field axiom at one place, for the action of a decomposition group on local units.

- `localHerbrandH0_card_eq_localDegree`: let `ell/k` be finite Galois with Galois group generated by `σ`, `v` a nontrivial absolute value on `k` whose completion `K_v` is a nonarchimedean local field, and `w` an extension of `v`. Then `HerbrandH0` of the decomposition group of `w` acting on `L_wˣ` has `Nat.card` equal to `[L_w : K_v]`, where `L_w` is `LocalizedCompletion v w`.

The proof identifies this group with the Tate group `Ĥ⁰` of `Aut(L_w/K_v)` on `L_wˣ` (`localHerbrandH0EquivUnitsTateH0`) and applies `finiteExtensionUnits_tate_card_of_generator`, the cardinality computation for a cyclic extension of local fields, to the generator `localizedCompletionGaloisGenerator` obtained from `σ`.

The local-field structure of `K_v` enters as instance arguments (`ValuativeRel`, `IsNonarchimedeanLocalField`), not derived from `v`. The whole group `Gal(ell/k)` must be cyclic. The fields `k` and `ell` are taken in `Type` (universe 0).
