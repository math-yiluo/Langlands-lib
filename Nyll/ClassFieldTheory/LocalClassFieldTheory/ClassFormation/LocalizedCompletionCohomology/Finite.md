# Finiteness of the local Herbrand groups

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LocalClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.LocalizedCompletionCohomology.Finite`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `ell/k` be a finite Galois extension whose Galois group is generated by `σ`, `v` a nontrivial absolute value on `k` whose completion `K_v` is a nonarchimedean local field, and `w` an extension of `v` to `ell`, with local field `L_w = LocalizedCompletion v w`. The file shows that the low-degree Herbrand groups of the decomposition group of `w` acting on `L_wˣ` are finite.

## Results

- `localHerbrandH0Finite`: `HerbrandH0` of the decomposition group acting on `L_wˣ` is finite.
- `localHerbrandHMinusOneFinite`: `HerbrandHMinusOne` of the same action, taken with respect to the generator `subgroupGeneratorOfGenerator` derived from `σ`, is finite.

## Proofs

Both groups are identified with Tate groups of `Aut(L_w/K_v)` acting on `L_wˣ`, by `localHerbrandH0EquivUnitsTateH0` and `localHerbrandHMinusOneEquivUnitsTateHminusOne`. The local cardinality computation `finiteExtensionUnits_tate_card_of_generator`, applied to the generator `localizedCompletionGaloisGenerator`, gives finiteness of `Ĥ⁰` and shows that `Ĥ⁻¹` has one element.

## Points to note

The local-field structure of `K_v` enters as instance arguments (`ValuativeRel`, `IsNonarchimedeanLocalField`), not derived from `v`, and the whole group `Gal(ell/k)` must be cyclic. The algebra, finiteness and Galois structures on `L_w` over `K_v` are fixed in the statements as `let`-bound instances. The fields `k` and `ell` are taken in `Type` (universe 0).
