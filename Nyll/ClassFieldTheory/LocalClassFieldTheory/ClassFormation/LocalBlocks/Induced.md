# Local blocks of the idele group as induced modules

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LocalClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.LocalBlocks.Induced`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `L/K` be a finite Galois extension, `v` a nontrivial absolute value on `K` and `w` a chosen extension of `v` to `L`. The decomposition group of `w` acts on the units of the algebraic localization at `w`, and the block of local unit groups above `v` is the module induced from this decomposition group to `Gal(L/K)`. The file defines this block and identifies its low-degree Herbrand groups with those of the local units, for cyclic `Gal(L/K)`.

## Definitions and results

- `decompositionGroupLocalUnitsAction`: the action of `absoluteValueDecompositionGroup K w` on `(LocalizedCompletion v w)ˣ`, through the isomorphism `decompositionGroupEquivAlgebraicLocalizationAut` of the decomposition group with the automorphisms of `LocalizedCompletion v w` over the completion of `K`; `decompositionGroup_smul_localUnit_coe` evaluates it.
- `LocalPlaceBlock v hv w`: the induced module `InducedModule` from the decomposition group, a subgroup of the functions `Gal(L/K) → (L_w)ˣ`.
- `localPlaceBlockEquivProduct`: for `Gal(L/K)` generated by `σ`, a group isomorphism between the block and the functions from `Fin [G : D_w]` to `(L_w)ˣ`, that is, a product of as many copies of the local units as the index of the decomposition group.
- `localPlaceBlockHerbrandH0Equiv`, `localPlaceBlockHerbrandHMinusOneEquiv`: for `Gal(L/K)` generated by `σ`, isomorphisms between `HerbrandH0` (resp. `HerbrandHMinusOne` with respect to `σ`) of the block under `Gal(L/K)` and the same group of the local units under the decomposition group; in degree −1 the latter uses the generator `subgroupGeneratorOfGenerator` derived from `σ`.

## Proofs

Each comparison is a direct application of the general results on induced modules over finite cyclic groups: `inducedCoordinatesOfFiniteCyclic`, `inducedHerbrandH0EquivOfFiniteCyclic` and `inducedHerbrandHMinusOneEquivOfFiniteCyclic`.

## Points to note

- The field at `w` is `LocalizedCompletion v w`, the canonical algebraic localization, not the metric completion itself. The file's docstring states that for finite extensions the two agree, citing `absoluteValueExtension_finiteLocalization_eq_top`; that result is not proved here.
- The block is defined as an induced module, not literally as the product of the local factors at the places above `v`; in cyclic coordinates `localPlaceBlockEquivProduct` identifies it only with a product of `[G : D_w]` copies of `(LocalizedCompletion v w)ˣ`, as an isomorphism of groups without a Galois action.
- All comparisons require the whole group `Gal(L/K)` to be cyclic with a given generator.
- `HerbrandH0` and `HerbrandHMinusOne` are the library's own Herbrand groups for multiplicative actions.
