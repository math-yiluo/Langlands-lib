# Degree-zero Herbrand group of a finite family of local blocks

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LocalClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.LocalBlocks.Family.H0`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `L/K` be a finite Galois extension whose Galois group is cyclic with generator `σ`, and let `d : ι → LocalPlaceDatum K L` be a finite family of places, each a nontrivial absolute value on `K` with a chosen extension `w` to `L`. The file computes the degree-zero Herbrand group of the product `LocalBlockFamily d` of the corresponding local blocks.

- `localBlockFamilyHerbrandH0Equiv`: for `Fintype ι`, a multiplicative isomorphism from `HerbrandH0 Gal(L/K) (LocalBlockFamily d)` to the product over `i` of `HerbrandH0` of the decomposition group at `w_i` acting on the units of `LocalizedCompletion` at `w_i`.

It is the composite of `herbrandH0PiEquiv` (degree-zero Herbrand group of a product) with the one-block isomorphisms `localPlaceBlockHerbrandH0Equiv` of Shapiro type.

The result is an explicit isomorphism, not merely a cardinality statement. The actions on the local units, the blocks and the product are local instances fixed in the statement, and `HerbrandH0` is the library's own Herbrand group.
