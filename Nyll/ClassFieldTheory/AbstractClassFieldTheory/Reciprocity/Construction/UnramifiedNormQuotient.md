# The norm quotient of an unramified extension

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AbstractClassFieldTheory.Reciprocity.Construction.UnramifiedNormQuotient`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

This file works in the library's abstract class field theory, with a degree datum `D` and a valuation datum `v` on a coefficient representation `A`, written additively. For a finite unramified Galois extension `L/K` of degree `n`, the normalized valuation identifies the quotient of `A_K` by the norms from `L` with `ℤ/nℤ`. The file also defines the arithmetic Frobenius of `L/K`. In the Frobenius part `K` is a `FiniteResidueAbstractField D`; in the valuation part `K` is a `FiniteAbstractField G`, that is, finite over the base field.

## Frobenius

- `DegreeData.chosenUnramifiedFrobeniusLift`: an element of `D.FrobeniusElements K L hLK`, in the Galois group over `K` of the maximal unramified extension of `L`, of exponent 1 (`chosenUnramifiedFrobeniusLift_exponent`). It comes from an element of `G_K` of normalized degree 1, chosen with `Classical.choose`.
- `unramifiedFrobenius`: the arithmetic Frobenius `φ` of `L/K`, the restriction of this lift to `G_K / G_L`.
- `unramifiedFrobenius_generates`: if `L/K` is normal, finite and unramified, `φ` generates `G_K / G_L`. `unramifiedFrobenius_zmultiples_eq_top` is the additive form.

## The valuation isomorphism

- `unramifiedValuationHom`: the valuation at `K` reduced modulo `n`, as a map `A_K → ZMod n`.
- `finiteNormSubgroup_le_unramifiedValuationHom_ker`: for unramified `L/K`, norms lie in its kernel. The residue degree equals the degree, so `v_K(N b) = n · v_L(b)`.
- `unramifiedNormQuotientValuation`: the induced map on `FiniteNormQuotient`. It is surjective by `unramifiedNormQuotientValuation_surjective`.
- `unramifiedNormQuotientValuation_injective`: injective, under the unit-cohomology axiom.
- `unramifiedReciprocity_valuationEquiv`: the resulting isomorphism `FiniteNormQuotient A K L ≃+ ZMod n`.
- `primeClass_addOrderOf`: the class of a prime element `π` of `K` has additive order exactly `n`. This needs only unramifiedness.
- `primeClass_zmultiples_eq_top`: under the axiom, the class of `π` generates the norm quotient.

## Proof of injectivity

Suppose `v_K(a) = n·z`. Choose `b` in `L` with `v_L(b) = z`; then `a − N(b)` is a unit. The extension is cyclic, generated by a chosen generator of the quotient. Applied to it, the axiom gives `Ĥ⁰ = 0`, so this unit is the norm of a unit `ε` (`exists_unit_relativeNorm_eq_of_tateHZero_isZero`). Hence `a = N(b + ε)`.

## Watch for

- `hAxiom : SatisfiesUnramifiedUnitCohomology D v` is a hypothesis of injectivity, of the isomorphism and of `primeClass_zmultiples_eq_top`. It is assumed, not proved, and only its `Ĥ⁰` part is used here.
- `unramifiedReciprocity_valuationEquiv` is only the valuation part of the unramified case. It does not involve the Frobenius.
- "Unramified" is the library's structural notion `IsUnramified` relative to `D`. The degree `n` is that of `FiniteAbstractFieldExtension.ofInclusion L K hLK`.
