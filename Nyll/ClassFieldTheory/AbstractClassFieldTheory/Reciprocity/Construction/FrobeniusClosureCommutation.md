# Elements commuting with a Frobenius stabilize its fixed field

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AbstractClassFieldTheory.Reciprocity.Construction.FrobeniusClosureCommutation`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

A step in the abstract construction of the reciprocity map, for a profinite group `G` (compact, Hausdorff, totally disconnected) and a degree map `D`. Fix a finite residue abstract field `K`, a closed subgroup `L ≤ K` normal in `K`, and a Frobenius element `σ` in the quotient `Q` of `K` by the inertia subgroup `D.extensionInertiaWithin K L`. The Frobenius closure is the closed subgroup of `Q` generated by `σ`, and the Frobenius fixed field is the corresponding closed subgroup of `K`. The file shows that a class `q ∈ Q` commuting with `σ` normalizes this fixed field.

## Main results

- `DegreeData.frobeniusClosure_commutes_of_commutes_generator`: if `q σ = σ q`, then `q` commutes with every element of the Frobenius closure. The centralizer of `q` is closed in `Q` (Hausdorff, because the inertia subgroup is closed) and contains `σ`, hence contains the topological closure of `⟨σ⟩`.
- `DegreeData.conjugate_frobeniusFixedField_eq_of_commutes`: if `q σ = σ q` and `k` is the representative of `q` in `K` chosen by `Quotient.out`, conjugating the Frobenius fixed field by `k⁻¹` gives the fixed field back. Both inclusions are checked elementwise: the class of `k t k⁻¹` is `q t̄ q⁻¹ = t̄`, using the first result.

## Watch for

The conjugation statement is phrased for the specific representative `Quotient.out q`, and conjugates by its inverse; other representatives are not treated.
