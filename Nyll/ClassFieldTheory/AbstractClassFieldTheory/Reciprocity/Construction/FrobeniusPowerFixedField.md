# Fixed fields of powers of a degree-one Frobenius

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AbstractClassFieldTheory.Reciprocity.Construction.FrobeniusPowerFixedField`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For the universal norm-descent lemma of abstract class field theory one takes a Frobenius element `φ` of `G(L̃ | K) = G_K / I_L` of exponent one (normalized degree `1`) and the fixed fields `Σ` of `φⁿ` and `Σₘ` of `φⁿᵐ`, for `n, m > 0`. This file proves the properties of the tower `Σₘ | Σ` that the argument uses, for a finite residue abstract field `K`, a degree map `D` and `L ≤ K` with `G_L` normal in `G_K`.

## Main definitions and results

- `extensionInertia_commutes_of_mem_extensionSubgroup`: an element of `G_L` commutes, modulo `I_L`, with every class of normalized degree zero; this is `[G_L, I_K] ⊆ G_L ∩ I_K = I_L`.
- `quotientPower_card_commutes_degreeZero`: if `P` is a finite Galois intermediate field of `L̃ | K` containing `L`, and `n = |G(P | K)|`, then `qⁿ` commutes with every degree-zero `τ`, for every `q ∈ G(L̃ | K)`.
- `frobeniusPowerOfDegreeOne φ hφ n hn`: `φⁿ` as a Frobenius element, of exponent `n` (`frobeniusExponent_powerOfDegreeOne`).
- `frobeniusPowerFixedField_le_finiteField`: for `G` compact Hausdorff and `P` as above (Galois over `K`, no containment of `L` needed), the fixed field of `φⁿ` with `n = [P : K]` contains `P`.
- `frobeniusPowerFixedField_le`: `Σₘ ⊇ Σ`.
- `frobeniusPowerFixedField_normal` (for `G` Hausdorff): `Σₘ | Σ` is Galois, since the closed subgroup generated by `φⁿ` is commutative.
- `frobeniusPowerFixedField_isUnramified`: `Σₘ | Σ` is unramified, because both fields have inertia group `I_L`.
- `frobeniusPowerFixedField_finite`: `Σₘ | Σ` is finite when `L | K` is finite; `frobeniusPowerFixedField_quotientFinite` and `frobeniusPowerFixedField_quotientCard` give finiteness and `[Σₘ : Σ] = m` without that hypothesis, for `G` also totally disconnected.
- `frobeniusPowerFixedField_generator`: for `L | K` finite, there is `s ∈ G_Σ` representing `φⁿ`, of normalized degree `1` over `Σ`, whose class generates `Gal(Σₘ | Σ)`.

## Proofs

The degree `[Σₘ : Σ] = m` comes from unramifiedness, which reduces the index to the index of degree images, and the residue-degree formula `f_Σ = d_K(σ) · f_K`, which gives `n · f_K` and `nm · f_K`. The generator statement applies a lemma saying that a degree-one element generates the Galois group of an unramified extension. The containment `P ⊆ Σ` uses that `φⁿ` is trivial in `G(P | K)` and that the kernel of the continuous restriction map is closed.

## Watch for

- Every power construction requires `φ` of exponent exactly one.
- The topological hypotheses vary: Hausdorff for normality; compact Hausdorff for containment and finiteness; compact, Hausdorff and totally disconnected for unramifiedness, the degree and the generator.
- The generator statement says the restriction of `φⁿ` itself generates, and records the representative; it is the input to which the unit-cohomology hypothesis is later applied.
