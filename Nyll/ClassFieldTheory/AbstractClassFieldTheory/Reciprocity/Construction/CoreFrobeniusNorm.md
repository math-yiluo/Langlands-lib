# The Frobenius norm identity

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AbstractClassFieldTheory.Reciprocity.Construction.CoreFrobeniusNorm`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

This file proves the norm identity at the core of the abstract reciprocity construction. Let `K` be an abstract field of finite residue degree, `L | K` a finite Galois extension (a closed `G_L` normal of finite index in `G_K`), and `L̃`, `K̃` the maximal unramified extensions, represented by the inertia subgroups `I_L`, `I_K`. Let `φ, σ ∈ G(L̃|K)` be Frobenius elements with `d_K(φ) = 1` and `d_K(σ) = n`, and let `Σ` be the fixed field of `σ`. With `φ_n = 1 + φ + ⋯ + φ^(n−1)` acting on `A(L̃)`, the identity is

`N(Σ/K)(a) = N(L̃/K̃)(φ_n a) = φ_n(N(L̃/K̃) a)` for `a ∈ A(Σ)`.

## Main definitions and results

- `maximalUnramifiedExtension_finite`: if `L | K` is finite, so is `L̃ | K̃`.
- `inertiaQuotientDegreeKernelEquiv`: `G(L̃|K̃) = I_K / I_L` is identified with the kernel of `d_K` on `G(L̃|K)`.
- `frobeniusQuotientAction`: the action of `G(L̃|K)` on `A(L̃)`. `relativeNorm_frobeniusQuotientAction`: `N(L̃/K̃)` commutes with it.
- `frobeniusPowerSum`: the additive operator `φ_n`.
- `frobeniusFixedCosetClosureEquiv`: the cosets `G_K / G_Σ` correspond to the cosets of the closed subgroup `Γ` generated by `σ` in `G(L̃|K)`.
- `kernelPowerCosetEquiv`: for `d_K(φ) = 1`, the map `(τ, i) ↦ φ^i τ Γ` is a bijection from `ker d_K × {0, …, n−1}` onto `G(L̃|K) / Γ`. `frobeniusNormIdentityCosetEquiv` is the resulting bijection `G(L̃|K̃) × {0, …, n−1} ≃ G_K / G_Σ`.
- `frobeniusNormIdentity_norm_eq_powerSum_norm`: `N(Σ/K)(a) = φ_n(N(L̃/K̃) a)`.
- `frobeniusNormIdentity_norm_powerSum_eq_powerSum_norm`: `N(L̃/K̃) ∘ φ_n = φ_n ∘ N(L̃/K̃)`, for any class `φ` and any `n`.
- `frobeniusNormIdentities`: the two equalities together.

## Proofs

The coset bijection follows from `Γ ∩ ker d_K = 1`, a consequence of the procyclic degree isomorphism of the fixed-field file, and from a count: the index of `Γ` is `n · |ker d_K|`. The first identity reindexes the coset sum defining `N(Σ/K)` along this bijection. The second holds because `G_K` normalizes `I_K` and `I_L`, so conjugation permutes the cosets of `I_L` in `I_K`.

## Points to watch

- The main identities assume `G` a compact, Hausdorff, totally disconnected topological group, and the Frobenius exponent of `φ` equal to `1`. The commutation `N(L̃/K̃) ∘ φ_n = φ_n ∘ N(L̃/K̃)` needs none of this.
- Equalities are stated in the ambient module `A`, after the inclusions `A(Σ) → A(L̃)` and `A(K̃) → A(L̃)`; the norms land in different fixed submodules.
- The action of `G(L̃|K)` is defined by descent to the quotient, without chosen representatives; the explicit coset map `frobeniusNormIdentityCosetMap` uses chosen representatives (`Quotient.out`).
- `Σ` is the closed subgroup `frobeniusFixedField` from an imported file, and its finiteness over `K` comes from there.
