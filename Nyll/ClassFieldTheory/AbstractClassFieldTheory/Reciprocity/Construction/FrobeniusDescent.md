# Frobenius elements: fixed fields and joint injectivity

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AbstractClassFieldTheory.Reciprocity.Construction.FrobeniusDescent`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Group-theoretic facts about Frobenius lifts in the abstract construction of the reciprocity map. For a finite residue abstract field `K`, a closed subgroup `L ≤ K` with `G_L` normal in `G_K`, and the inertia subgroup `I_L` (`D.extensionInertiaWithin K L`), the quotient `G_K / I_L` plays the role of `G(L̃ | K)`, with `L̃` the maximal unramified extension of `L`. The file compares Frobenius fixed fields with other fields and shows that an element of `G(L̃ | K)` is determined by its restriction to `G(L | K)` together with its normalized degree.

## Main definitions and results

- `DegreeData.extensionImageInInertiaQuotient`: the image of `G_L` in `G_K / I_L`; `extensionImageInInertiaQuotient_isClosed` says it is closed when `G` is a compact Hausdorff topological group and `L | K` is finite, because `G_L` is then open in `G_K` and the image of an open subgroup is closed.
- `frobeniusFixedField_le_of_inertia_le_of_lift_mem`: if `M` is finite over `K`, `I_L ≤ G_M`, and some `s ∈ G_M` represents the Frobenius element `σ`, then the Frobenius fixed field of `σ` contains `M` (as subgroups, `G_Σ ≤ G_M`).
- `frobeniusFixedField_le_of_restriction_eq_one`: if `σ` restricts to `1` on `L`, its fixed field contains `L`, that is `G_Σ ≤ G_L`.
- `extensionRestriction_normalizedDegree_joint_injective`: the map `q ↦ (restriction of q to G(L | K), normalized degree of q)` on `G_K / I_L` is injective.
- `frobeniusRestriction_mul`: restriction of Frobenius elements is multiplicative.
- `frobenius_eq_of_restriction_eq_of_degree_eq`: two Frobenius elements with the same restriction and the same normalized degree are equal.

## Proofs

The two containments use minimality of a topological closure: the closed subgroup generated by `σ` lies in any closed subgroup containing `σ`, here the image of `G_M` or `G_L`; an element of `G_K` whose class lies in that image then lies in `G_M` (resp. `G_L`) because `I_L` does. Joint injectivity holds because `I_L` is the part of `G_L` on which the normalized degree is trivial.

## Watch for

The closedness and containment results need `G` compact and Hausdorff and a finiteness hypothesis on the relevant extension; joint injectivity and its corollary hold without topology.
