# Closed cyclic subgroups meeting a coset stabilizer

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AbstractClassFieldTheory.Reciprocity.Construction.TransferOrbitClosure`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `Q` be a topological group, `H` a closed subgroup of finite index, and `g ∈ Q`. The file computes the intersection of the closed subgroup generated by `g` with the stabilizer of a coset `x ∈ Q ⧸ H`, as needed when comparing transfer with norms.

- `closedSubgroupGenerated_pow_eq_inf_stabilizer`: let `m` be the minimal period of `x` under left multiplication by `g`. Then the closed subgroup generated by `g ^ m` is the intersection of the closed subgroup generated by `g` with the stabilizer of `x`.
- `mem_closedSubgroupGenerated_conjugate_iff`: `c` lies in the closed subgroup generated by `x` if and only if `q c q⁻¹` lies in the closed subgroup generated by `q x q⁻¹`.

Proof: the stabilizer, a conjugate of `H`, is closed of finite index, hence open. The minimal period gives `⟨g⟩ ∩ Stab(x) = ⟨g ^ m⟩`, and since the stabilizer is both open and closed, intersecting with it commutes with taking closures.

`closedSubgroupGenerated` is the library's construction: the topological closure of the abstract subgroup generated by a set.
