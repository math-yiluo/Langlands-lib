# Frobenius exponents, conjugates and action remainders

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AbstractClassFieldTheory.Reciprocity.Construction.MainMultiplicativity.FrobeniusActionRemainder`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Algebraic identities for Frobenius elements in the abstract class field theory of the library, prepared for the proof that the reciprocity map is multiplicative. The setting is a group `G` with a topology and with degree data `D` (a normalized degree map to `ℤ̂`), a field `K` given as a closed subgroup with finite-residue structure, and a closed subgroup `L ≤ K` whose extension `L/K` is normal. A Frobenius element `σ` of `L̃/K` is an element of `K` modulo the inertia of the extension together with a positive exponent `n(σ)` such that its normalized degree is `n(σ)` times the generator `1 ∈ ℤ̂`.

## Main definitions and results

- `frobeniusExponent_mul`: the exponent of `σ₁σ₂` is `n(σ₁) + n(σ₂)`.
- `frobeniusQuotientAction_fixedFieldInclusion`: for a `G`-module `A` (`Rep ℤ G`), `σ` acts trivially on the invariants of `A` under the fixed field of `σ`, viewed inside the invariants under the maximal unramified extension of `L`.
- `frobeniusActionRemainder φ σ := φ^(n(σ)) σ⁻¹`; by `frobeniusActionRemainder_mem_degreeKernel` it has degree zero when `n(φ) = 1`.
- `frobeniusActionConjugate φ σ m`: the Frobenius element `φᵐ σ φ⁻ᵐ`, which keeps the exponent of `σ` (`frobeniusExponent_actionConjugate`).
- `conjugate_frobeniusFixedField_actionConjugate`: the fixed field of `φᵐ σ φ⁻ᵐ` is the conjugate of the fixed field of `σ` by a representative of `φᵐ` in `K`.
- `frobeniusActionRemainder_mul`: the remainder of `σ₁σ₂` equals the remainder of `φ^(n(σ₁)) σ₂ φ^(-n(σ₁))` times the remainder of `σ₁`.
- `frobeniusActionRemainder_apply_fixedField`: on the elements fixed by the fixed field of `σ`, the remainder acts as `φ^(n(σ))`.

## Proofs

Exponent identities are checked after applying `n ↦ (1 ∈ ℤ̂)·n`, which is injective (`proCIntegerOne_pow_nat_injective`), and using that the normalized degree is a homomorphism. The fixed-field statement uses that conjugation carries the closed subgroup generated by `σ` to the one generated by its conjugate (`mem_closedSubgroupGenerated_conjugate_iff`).

## Watch for

- Representations in Lean act on the left, while the classical argument uses exponent (right-action) notation. Hence the remainder is `φⁿσ⁻¹` rather than `σφ⁻ⁿ`, and in the product formula the conjugation falls on the second factor and the order of the factors is reversed.
- The product of Frobenius elements and the Frobenius elements themselves are the library's own notions, not Mathlib's.
- The conjugation lemma depends on a chosen representative (`Quotient.out`) of `φ^m`.
- `frobeniusQuotientAction_fixedFieldInclusion`, `conjugate_frobeniusFixedField_actionConjugate` and `frobeniusActionRemainder_apply_fixedField` assume in addition that `G` is a topological group (`IsTopologicalGroup G`).
