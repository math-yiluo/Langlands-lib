# Orbit sets and double cosets

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AbstractClassFieldTheory.Reciprocity.Construction.DoubleCosetOrbitGeometry`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Group-theoretic bookkeeping for the transfer formula in the abstract construction of the reciprocity map. For subgroups `S`, `H` of a group `Q`, the orbits of `S` on the coset space `Q ⧸ H` are identified with the double cosets `S \ Q / H`; these index sets are then transported along inversion, along surjective equivariant maps, and along the passage from a cyclic subgroup to its topological closure. No class formation or field enters.

## Main definitions and results

- `orbitQuotientEquivDoubleCoset S H`: the equivalence between the orbit set of `S` acting on `Q ⧸ H` and `S \ Q / H`; its simp lemmas say that the orbit of the coset of `x` goes to the double coset of `x`.
- `doubleCosetInversionEquiv S H`: `x ↦ x⁻¹` induces `S \ Q / H ≃ H \ Q / S`.
- `orbitQuotientSwapEquiv S H`: the resulting equivalence between the `S`-orbits on `Q ⧸ H` and the `H`-orbits on `Q ⧸ S`, sending the orbit of `x` to that of `x⁻¹`; it reindexes the transfer decomposition by the norm decomposition.
- `orbitQuotientEquivOfSurjectiveEquivariant`: a surjective homomorphism `f : M →* N` and an equivalence `e : X ≃ Y` with `e (m • x) = f m • e x` induce an equivalence of orbit sets.
- `leftCosetEquivOfMulEquiv`: a group isomorphism `e : Q ≃* R` induces `Q ⧸ S ≃ R ⧸ e(S)`, without any normality assumption.
- `doubleCoset_closedCyclic_eq`: for a topological group `Q`, a closed subgroup `H` of finite index and `g : Q`, two elements lie in the same double coset `H x ⟨g⟩` for the abstract cyclic subgroup `⟨g⟩` (`Subgroup.zpowers g`) if and only if they lie in the same double coset for the closed subgroup generated by `g` (`closedSubgroupGenerated {g}`).
- `doubleCosetClosedCyclicEquiv` and `orbitQuotientClosedCyclicEquiv`: the resulting equivalences of double-coset sets and of the `H`-orbit sets on `Q ⧸ ⟨g⟩` and `Q ⧸ closure ⟨g⟩`, each with a simp lemma on representatives.

## Proofs

The comparison with the closure is a density argument: `H` is open, being closed of finite index, so for `c` in the closure of `⟨g⟩` the open set `{s | x c s⁻¹ x⁻¹ ∈ H}` contains `c` and therefore some power of `g`.

## Watch for

- The closure results need `H` closed and of finite index in a topological group; in the intended use `g` is a Frobenius element and the closed subgroup is its procyclic closure.
- Orbits are taken for the left action on left cosets, so the double cosets are `S x H` with the acting subgroup on the left.
