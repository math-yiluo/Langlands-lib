# Units in unramified cyclic extensions and the unit-cohomology axiom

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AbstractClassFieldTheory.Reciprocity.Construction.UnitCohomologyAxiom`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

This file works in the library's abstract class field theory. There is a group `G` with a topology, and fields are represented by closed subgroups. A degree datum `D : DegreeData G`, a coefficient representation `A : Rep ℤ G` (written additively) and a valuation datum `v : ValuationData D A` are fixed. For a finite normal extension `L/K` the file builds the representation of `G_K / G_L` on the units `U_L`. It then states the hypothesis that the Tate groups `Ĥ⁰` and `Ĥ⁻¹` of `U_L` vanish when `L/K` is unramified and cyclic.

## Definitions

- `FiniteCyclicSubextension K`: a closed subgroup inside `K.field`, normal in it, with finite quotient and a chosen generator of that quotient. It comes with forgetful maps `toFiniteAbstractExtension` and `toFiniteAbstractFieldExtension`, and with the predicates `IsUnramified` and `IsTotallyRamified` for `D`. `fixedRepresentation` gives the fixed coefficients as a representation of the quotient.
- `FiniteUnramifiedCyclicExtension D K`: such an extension, unramified for `D`.
- `ValuationData.unitRepresentation`: the units `U_L` as a representation of `G_K / G_L`, obtained from the action of `G_K` (`unitRepresentationOverK`) by descent. `unitInclusion` maps `U_K` into `U_L` when `L/K` is unramified.
- `SatisfiesUnramifiedUnitCohomology D v`: for every finite abstract field `K` and every finite unramified cyclic extension `L` of `K`, `Ĥ⁰` and `Ĥ⁻¹` of `U_L` are zero.

## Results

- `valuationAt_normalExtensionAction`: the normalized valuation at `L` is invariant under `G_K`. The proof uses transitivity of norms in a tower and the injectivity of multiplication by the residue degree on `ℤ̂`.
- `unitRepresentation_norm_coe`: on underlying elements of `A.V`, the representation norm on `U_L` is the relative norm from `L` to `K`.
- `exists_unit_relativeNorm_eq_of_tateHZero_isZero`: suppose `L/K` is normal and unramified and its group is generated by `g`. If `Ĥ⁰(U_L) = 0`, then every unit of `K` is the norm of a unit of `L`.
- `exists_unit_sigma_sub_eq_of_tateHMinusOne_isZero`: if `Ĥ⁻¹(U_L) = 0`, a unit of norm zero has the form `g·ε − ε`.
- In universe 0 only: `satisfiesUnramifiedUnitCohomology_iff_original` shows the predicate equivalent to its analogue for `tateCohomology`, and `exists_norm_eq_of_tateHZero_isZero` shows that if `tateCohomology M 0` is zero for a finite group generated by `g`, every `g`-fixed vector of `M` is a norm.

## Watch for

- `SatisfiesUnramifiedUnitCohomology` is a `Prop` used as a hypothesis by later files. It is not a theorem and not a Lean axiom, and no example satisfying it is given here. It quantifies only over cyclic extensions with a chosen generator, although its docstring speaks of Galois extensions.
- The Tate groups are `IntegralGroupCohomology.Universe.tate`, defined in an imported file. In universe 0 their vanishing is equivalent to that of `tateCohomology`.
- "Unramified" and "totally ramified" are the library's structural notions relative to `D`.
