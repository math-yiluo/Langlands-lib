# Independence of the reciprocity map from the prime element

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AbstractClassFieldTheory.Reciprocity.Construction.ReciprocityIndependence`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

In the abstract class field theory of the library, the reciprocity value of a Frobenius element `σ` of `L̃/K` is the class of `N(Σ/K)(π)` in `A_K / N(L̃/K) A_L̃`, where `Σ` is the fixed field of `σ` and `π` a prime element of `Σ`. The definition uses a chosen prime. This file proves that every prime element gives the same class, under the axiom on unit cohomology of unramified extensions. It first collects facts about finite intermediate fields (fields are closed subgroups, so inclusions are reversed).

## Main definitions and results

- `FiniteIntermediateField.normalCoreField`, `galoisRefinement`: for a finite intermediate field `M` of a normal extension `E/K`, the normal core of `G_M` in `G_K` gives a finite intermediate field containing `M` and normal over `K` (`galoisRefinement_normal`).
- `compositumWith M S`: the compositum `MS`, as the subgroup `G_M ∩ G_S`. For `S` an extension of `K`, it is finite over `S` (`compositumWith_finite`), finite over `K` when `S/K` is also finite (`compositumWith_finite_over_base`), and normal over `S` when `M/K` is normal (`compositumWith_normal`).
- `finite_extension_of_le`: if `P/K` is finite and `K ⊆ M ⊆ P`, then `P/M` is finite.
- `quotient_generator_of_unramified_degree_one`, `exists_quotient_generator_of_unramified`: for `K` with finite-residue structure (`FiniteResidueAbstractField D`), if `L/K` is finite, normal and unramified, `G(L/K)` is cyclic, generated by the image of any element of normalized degree 1.
- `reciprocityValueOfPrime_eq_reciprocityMap`: let `G` be profinite (compact, Hausdorff, totally disconnected), let `K` have finite absolute degree, and let `L/K` be finite and normal. If `hAxiom : v.SatisfiesUnramifiedUnitCohomology D` holds and `π` is any prime element of the fixed field of `σ`, then `reciprocityValueOfPrime σ π = reciprocityMap σ`.

## Proof

The difference `u = π − π₀` from the chosen prime `π₀` is a unit, and `N(Σ/K)(u)` has to lie in `N(M/K) A_M` for every finite intermediate field `M` of `L̃/K`. Let `R` be a Galois refinement of `M` and `P = RΣ`. Then `P/Σ` is finite, normal and unramified, because `Σ` has the inertia of `L`. It is therefore cyclic, and `hAxiom` applied to it says that the Tate cohomology of its units vanishes in degrees 0 and −1. Degree 0 gives a unit `ε` of `P` with `N(P/Σ)(ε) = u`. By transitivity of norms, `N(Σ/K)(u) = N(P/K)(ε) = N(M/K)(N(P/M)(ε))`.

## Watch for

The main theorem is conditional on `hAxiom`, an axiom of the abstract setting that the library carries as a hypothesis. It states that for every finite unramified cyclic extension, the Tate cohomology of the units vanishes in degrees 0 and −1. Only the degree-0 part is used here.
