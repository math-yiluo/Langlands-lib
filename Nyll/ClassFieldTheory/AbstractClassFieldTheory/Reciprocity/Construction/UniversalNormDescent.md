# Universal norm descent: lifting and correction

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AbstractClassFieldTheory.Reciprocity.Construction.UniversalNormDescent`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

This file contains the representation-theoretic step of the universal norm-descent argument in the abstract reciprocity construction. A relation of the form `φ·u − u = Σᵢ (τᵢ·uᵢ − uᵢ)` among `H`-fixed vectors is lifted through the norm of a finite cyclic normal subgroup `H`. The file also proves norm identities used alongside this step.

## Results

- `universalNormDescent_cyclic_lift_and_correction`: setting and hypotheses:
  - `R` is a group, `H` a finite normal subgroup generated by `g`, and `B` a `ℤ`-representation of `R`;
  - the Tate groups `Ĥ⁰` and `Ĥ⁻¹` of `B` restricted to `H` vanish;
  - `u` and the `uᵢ` are `H`-fixed and satisfy the relation above for some `φ` and `τᵢ` in `R`.

  Then there are `ū`, `ūᵢ` and `y` with `N_H(ū) = u` and `N_H(ūᵢ) = uᵢ`, and `g·y − y = φ·ū − ū − Σᵢ (τᵢ·ūᵢ − ūᵢ)`.
- `conjugateStableAction`: if conjugation by `s` maps the closed subgroup `F` to itself, conjugation induces an action on the fixed vectors `B^F`. On underlying vectors it is `ρ(s⁻¹)` (`conjugateStableAction_coe`).
- `relativeNorm_conjugateStableAction`: the relative norm from `E` to `F` commutes with this action when conjugation by `s` stabilizes both `E` and `F`.
- `rep_norm_eq_generatorPowerSum`: for a finite cyclic group of order `n` generated by `g`, the norm sends `x` to the sum of `gⁱ·x` over `0 ≤ i < n`. `rep_norm_eq_generatorIterateSum` writes the same sum with iterates of any map that agrees pointwise with the action of `g`.

## Proof

Vanishing of `Ĥ⁰` gives the lifts `ū` and `ūᵢ`. Because `H` is normal, the norm over `H` commutes with the action of `R` (a private lemma). The defect `φ·ū − ū − Σᵢ (τᵢ·ūᵢ − ūᵢ)` therefore has norm zero, and vanishing of `Ĥ⁻¹` writes it as `g·y − y`.

## Watch for

- The vanishing of `Ĥ⁰` and `Ĥ⁻¹` is assumed through the hypotheses `hzero0` and `hzeroMinusOne`. In the reciprocity construction this is supplied by the unit-cohomology axiom.
- The Tate groups are `IntegralGroupCohomology.Universe.tate`, defined in an imported file. The proof uses them only through two imported lemmas for a finite group generated by `g`: vanishing in degree 0 gives norm preimages of `g`-fixed vectors, and vanishing in degree −1 writes vectors of norm zero as `g·y − y`.
