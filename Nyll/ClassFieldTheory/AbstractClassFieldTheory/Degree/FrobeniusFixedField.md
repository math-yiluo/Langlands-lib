# The fixed field of a Frobenius lift

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AbstractClassFieldTheory.Degree.FrobeniusFixedField`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

This file proves, on the group side, the theorem on the fixed field of a Frobenius lift. Let `K` be an abstract field of finite residue degree, `L` a closed subgroup of `G_K` normal in it, and `σ` a Frobenius element of `G(L̃|K) = G_K / (G_L ∩ I_K)`, so `d_K(σ) = n` with `n > 0`. The fixed field `Σ` of `σ` is represented by the closed subgroup `Γ` of `G(L̃|K)` topologically generated by `σ`. The file shows that `Γ` corresponds to a finite extension of `K` of residue degree `n`, that the normalized degree `d_Σ` identifies `Γ` with `ℤ̂`, and that `σ` is the Frobenius of `Σ`.

## Main definitions and results

- `frobeniusExponent`: the `n > 0` with `d_K(σ) = n`; `frobeniusExponent_unique`.
- `extensionNormalizedDegreeContinuous`: `d_K` on `G(L̃|K)` as a continuous surjective homomorphism; `extensionDegreeKernelRestriction_injective`: its kernel embeds into `G(L|K)`.
- `frobeniusClosure` (`Γ`), `frobeniusInClosure`, `frobeniusInClosure_topologicallyGenerates`; `frobeniusClosureCommGroup` makes `Γ` commutative.
- `frobeniusClosureDegree_range`: `d_K(Γ) = n ℤ̂`; `frobeniusClosureDegree_range_index`: this image has index `n`, the residue degree of `Σ` over `K`.
- `fixedFieldNormalizedDegree`: `d_Σ = (1/n) d_K` on `Γ`; it sends `σ` to `1` and is surjective.
- `frobeniusFixedField_finiteIndex`: for `L | K` finite, `Γ` has finite index in `G(L̃|K)`.
- `frobeniusFixedField_normalizedDegree_injective`, `frobeniusFixedField_kernel`: `d_Σ` is injective, the group form of `Σ̃ = L̃`.
- `frobeniusClosure_index_le_extensionIndex_of_exponent_eq_one`: if `n = 1`, the index of `Γ` is at most `[G_K : G_L]`.
- `frobeniusFixedField_normalizedDegreeEquiv`: `Γ ≃ ℤ̂` as topological groups; `fixedFieldFrobenius` and `frobeniusFixedField_frobenius`: `σ` is the element with `d_Σ = 1`.

General helpers: `continuousQuotientLift`, `continuousMulEquivOfBijectiveCompactToT2` (a continuous bijective homomorphism from a compact group to a Hausdorff group is a topological isomorphism), `zHatPowNat`, `zHatReductionMul`.

## Proofs

Injectivity of `d_Σ` rests on a private lemma: a continuous homomorphism from a profinite abelian group topologically generated by `x` to `ℤ̂`, sending `x` to `1`, is injective; it compares the finite quotients by the closure of `x^m` with `ℤ/m`. Finite index follows from the index formula through `d_K`: the image part is `n`, the kernel part embeds into the finite `G(L|K)`.

## Points to watch

- `Σ` never appears as an object: `Γ` is a closed subgroup of the quotient `G(L̃|K)`, not of `G`, so it is not an abstract field in the sense of the fields file. "Residue degree `n`" and "`Σ̃ = L̃`" are expressed only through `d_K(Γ)` and the kernel of `d_Σ`.
- `G` is assumed a compact Hausdorff topological group; injectivity, the index bound, the isomorphism with `ℤ̂` and the Frobenius statement also assume it totally disconnected, so `G` is profinite. Finiteness results assume `G_L` of finite index in `G_K`.
- The index bound is proved only when `n = 1`.
