# Finite unit parameters of standard Lubin–Tate levels

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LubinTate.FiniteLevel.FiniteParameters`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `F` be a local field structure on `K` with valuation ring `O_F` and residue field of cardinality `q`. The units of `O_F` act on the Lubin–Tate torsion points through power series, and at the standard level `n` this action factors through `O_Fˣ / U^(n+1)`, where `U^(n+1)` is the higher principal unit group. The file defines this finite quotient, computes its order, and descends the action on the chosen primitive point to it.

## Main definitions and results

- `standardLubinTateUnitParameter F n`: the commutative group `O_Fˣ / U^(n+1)`, with quotient map `standardLubinTateUnitParameterClass`. It is finite, and two units have the same class iff their quotient lies in `U^(n+1)`.
- `standardLubinTateUnitParameterChosenRepresentative`: a representative chosen with `Classical.choose`.
- `standardLubinTateUnitParameter_natCard`: the group has `(q − 1)·q^n` elements.
- `standardLubinTateUnitParameterRoot F hπ n a`: for a uniformizer `π`, the element of the separable closure of `K` obtained by acting with a representative of `a` on the chosen primitive point. It does not depend on the representative (`standardLubinTateUnitParameterRoot_eq_action_of_class_eq`, `standardLubinTateUnitParameterRoot_class`), and it is a root of the primitive level polynomial over `K` (`standardLubinTateUnitParameterRoot_isRoot`).

## Proof

The order is computed from the subgroup `U^1 / U^(n+1)`, of order `q^n`, and the quotient `O_Fˣ / U^1`, isomorphic to the units of the residue field and of order `q − 1`. Independence of the representative rests on the result, from the file on the primitive action, that units congruent modulo `U^(n+1)` act equally on the primitive point.

## Points to watch

- Level `n` in the library's indexing is generated by a primitive point killed by `[π^(n+1)]` (called primitive level `n + 1` in the comments), so the quotient is by `U^(n+1)`, not `U^n`.
