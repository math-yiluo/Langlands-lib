# The primitive point is a uniformizer of the standard level field

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LubinTate.FiniteLevel.PrimitiveUniformizer`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let K be a local field with uniformizer π and residue field of q elements, and Lₙ = K(λₙ) the extension generated by the chosen root λₙ of the primitive division polynomial Qₙ. The file equips Lₙ with a complete discrete valuation whose valuation ring is the integral closure of the base valuation ring, proves that λₙ has normalized additive valuation 1, so it is a uniformizer and Lₙ/K is totally ramified, and shows that λₙ generates the whole valuation ring over the base valuation ring.

## Main definitions

- `standardLubinTateLevelPowerBasis`, `standardLubinTateLevelGenerator`: the power basis of Lₙ over K given by λₙ, and λₙ as an element of Lₙ.
- `standardLubinTateLevelCompleteDVF hπ n`: a complete discrete valuation on Lₙ, chosen by `Classical.choose` among those extending the base valuation whose valuation ring is the integral closure and which satisfy the fundamental identity degree = e·f (`standardLubinTateLevelCompleteDVFData_exists`).
- `standardLubinTateLevelLocalField`: the same structure as a `LocalField` (finite residue field).
- `standardLubinTatePrimitivePointInteger hπ n`: λₙ as an element of that valuation ring.

## Main results

- `standardLubinTateLevelCompleteDVF_hasExtension`, `_isIntegralClosure`, `_fundamentalIdentity`: the properties of the chosen valuation; the valuation ring is a finite module over the base valuation ring.
- `standardLubinTatePrimitivePoint_minpoly`: the minimal polynomial of λₙ over the base valuation ring is Qₙ.
- `standardLubinTatePrimitivePointInteger_adjoin_eq_top`: the valuation ring of Lₙ is generated by λₙ over the base valuation ring.
- `standardLubinTatePrimitivePointInteger_addVal`: λₙ has additive valuation 1.
- `standardLubinTateLevel_ramificationIndex_eq_degree`: the ramification index equals the degree (q − 1)·q^n.
- `standardLubinTateUniformizerInteger_map_addVal`, `standardLubinTateBaseUniformizerInteger_map_addVal`: any uniformizer of K, not only π, has valuation (q − 1)·q^n in Lₙ.
- `standardLubinTatePrimitivePointInteger_irreducible`, `standardLubinTatePrimitivePoint_isUniformizer`: λₙ is irreducible, hence a uniformizer.
- `standardLubinTatePrimitivePointInteger_iterate_succ_eq_zero`, `_iterate_ne_zero_of_le`: inside the valuation ring, f^[n+1](λₙ) = 0 and f^[r](λₙ) ≠ 0 for r ≤ n.

## Proofs

Write Qₙ = X^d + R with d = (q − 1)·q^n. All coefficients of R lie in the maximal ideal, so λₙ^d lies in the maximal ideal of Lₙ and λₙ does too. The constant term π of R has valuation e, while every other term of R(λₙ) has valuation at least e + 1; hence d·v(λₙ) = e. Since e ≤ d by the fundamental identity, v(λₙ) = 1 and e = d. Monogenicity of the valuation ring uses the discriminant of the power basis and Mathlib's lemma on Eisenstein generators.

## Watch for

- The valuation on Lₙ is a choice by `Classical.choose`, known only through the three properties above.
- The ramification statement is e = degree, not a separate statement that the residue degree is 1.
