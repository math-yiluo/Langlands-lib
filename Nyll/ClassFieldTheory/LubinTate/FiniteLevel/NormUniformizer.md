# Norm of a primitive Lubin–Tate point

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LubinTate.FiniteLevel.NormUniformizer`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `F` be a local field structure on `K`, `π` a uniformizer, and `L_n` the standard Lubin–Tate level `n`, generated by the chosen primitive point `λ`. The file shows that the norm from `L_n` to `K` of `−λ` is `π`.

- `standardLubinTatePrimitivePolynomialOverField_coeff_zero`: the primitive polynomial of level `n` over `K` has constant coefficient `π` (here `π` may be any element of the valuation ring).
- `standardLubinTate_norm_neg_levelGenerator`: `N_(L_n/K)(−λ) = π`.

The minimal polynomial of `λ` is the primitive polynomial, so the norm of `λ` is `(−1)^d` times its constant coefficient, `d = [L_n : K]`. Replacing `λ` by `−λ` contributes another `(−1)^d`, and the signs cancel.
