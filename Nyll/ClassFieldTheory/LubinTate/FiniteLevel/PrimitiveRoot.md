# Primitive roots and the standard Lubin–Tate level fields

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LubinTate.FiniteLevel.PrimitiveRoot`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let K be a local field with uniformizer π and residue field of q elements, and f(X) = X^q + π·X. The file carries the primitive division polynomial Qₙ = (f^[n])^(q−1) + π from the valuation ring to K, shows that it is irreducible and separable there, chooses a root in `SeparableClosure K`, and defines the standard level field as the extension of K generated by that root, of degree (q − 1)·q^n. The argument works in mixed and in equal characteristic.

## Main definitions

- `standardLubinTatePrimitivePolynomialOverField F π n`: Qₙ with coefficients mapped into K.
- `chosenStandardLubinTatePrimitiveRoot hπ n`: a root of Qₙ in `SeparableClosure K`, chosen by `Classical.choose`; its docstring calls it a primitive root of level n + 1.
- `standardLubinTateLevelField hπ n`: the intermediate field K(λ) of `SeparableClosure K` generated by that root.

## Main results

- `standardLubinTatePolynomialIterate_derivative_eval_zero`: (f^[n])′(0) = π^n.
- `standardLubinTatePrimitivePolynomialOverField_formula`, `_monic`, `_natDegree`: the expected formula, monicity, and degree (q − 1)·q^n.
- `standardLubinTatePrimitivePolynomialOverField_irreducible`: irreducibility over K.
- `residueFieldNatCard_sub_one_cast_ne_zero`: q − 1 is nonzero in K.
- `standardLubinTatePrimitivePolynomialOverField_derivative_ne_zero` and `_separable`: the derivative of Qₙ over K is nonzero, so Qₙ is separable.
- `exists_standardLubinTatePrimitivePolynomialOverField_root`, `chosenStandardLubinTatePrimitiveRoot_isRoot`, `_isIntegral`: existence of the root and its basic properties.
- `standardLubinTatePrimitivePolynomialOverField_eq_minpoly`: Qₙ over K is the minimal polynomial of the chosen root.
- `standardLubinTateLevelField_finiteDimensional`, `standardLubinTateLevelField_finrank`: the level field is finite over K, of degree (q − 1)·q^n.

## Proofs

Irreducibility over K comes from the Eisenstein irreducibility over the valuation ring by Gauss's lemma, using that the valuation ring is integrally closed with fraction field K. For separability, the derivative of Qₙ is (q − 1)·(f^[n])^(q−2)·(f^[n])′; q − 1 is nonzero in K because q is zero but q − 1 is not in the residue field, and (f^[n])′ is nonzero because its value at 0 is π^n ≠ 0. An irreducible polynomial with nonzero derivative is separable, so it has a root in the separable closure.

## Watch for

- The level field is an `IntermediateField` of `SeparableClosure K` generated by one chosen root; whether it is a splitting field of Qₙ, and Galois properties, are not proved in this file.
- The index is shifted: the field at index n is the one the docstrings call level n + 1, and its degree is (q − 1)·q^n.
