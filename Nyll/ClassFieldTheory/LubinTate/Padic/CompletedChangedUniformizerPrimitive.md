# The changed theta value generates the completed level

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LubinTate.Padic.CompletedChangedUniformizerPrimitive`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let A be the completed maximal unramified extension of ℚ_p, with valuation ring the completed unramified Witt ring, E the completed standard Lubin–Tate level at level n, and u a unit of ℤ_p. The primitive Lubin–Tate polynomial of level n for the changed uniformizer u·p stays Eisenstein after base change to the valuation ring of A. Hence the changed theta value θ in E has this polynomial as its minimal polynomial over A and generates E over A.

## Main definitions and results

- `padicChangedCompletedPrimitivePolynomialInteger`, `padicChangedCompletedPrimitivePolynomial`: the changed primitive polynomial, base-changed to the valuation ring of A and to A; the first maps to the second (`padicChangedCompletedPrimitivePolynomialInteger_map`). Both are monic of degree (p − 1)·p^n.
- `padicChangedCompletedUniformizer_isUniformizer`: the image of u·p is a uniformizer of A.
- `padicChangedCompletedPrimitivePolynomialInteger_isWeaklyEisensteinAt`, `..._isEisensteinAt`: the integral polynomial is Eisenstein at the maximal ideal; hence irreducible (`padicChangedCompletedPrimitivePolynomialInteger_irreducible`), and irreducible over A (`padicChangedCompletedPrimitivePolynomial_irreducible`).
- `padicChangedUniformizerThetaValue_isRoot_completedPrimitivePolynomial`, `..._isIntegral_completedBase`, `..._minpoly_completedBase`: θ is a root, is integral over A, and has this polynomial as minimal polynomial over A.
- `padicChangedUniformizerThetaValue_adjoin_completedBase_eq_top`: A(θ) = E.
- `padicChangedUniformizerThetaPowerBasis`: the power basis of E over A generated by θ (`..._gen`).

## Proofs

The weak Eisenstein property is the image of the Eisenstein property over ℤ_p; the constant term u·p is not in the square of the maximal ideal because it is a uniformizer of A, the unit u staying a unit. Irreducibility over A follows from Gauss's lemma for monic polynomials. A(θ) = E because the degree of θ over A equals [E : A], computed from the minimal polynomial of the standard primitive root, which has the same degree.
