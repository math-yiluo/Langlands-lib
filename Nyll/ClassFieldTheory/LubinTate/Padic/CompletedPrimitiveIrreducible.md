# Irreducibility of the completed primitive polynomial

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LubinTate.Padic.CompletedPrimitiveIrreducible`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let A be the completed maximal unramified extension of ℚ_p, with valuation ring W. The n-th standard primitive Lubin–Tate polynomial of ℚ_p for the uniformizer p stays Eisenstein after its coefficients are mapped from ℤ_p to W, hence stays irreducible over W and over A.

## Main results

- `padicCompletedUnramifiedIntegerMap_isUniformizer`: the image of p in W is a uniformizer of A.
- `padicCompletedPrimitivePolynomialInteger_natDegree`: the polynomial over W has degree (p − 1)·p^n.
- `padicCompletedPrimitivePolynomialInteger_isEisensteinAt`: it is Eisenstein at the maximal ideal of W.
- `padicCompletedPrimitivePolynomialInteger_irreducible`: it is irreducible over W.
- `padicCompletedPrimitivePolynomial_irreducible`: the base change to A is irreducible.

## Proofs

The maximal ideal of W is generated by the image of the maximal ideal of ℤ_p, so the image of p generates it and is a uniformizer. The non-leading coefficients lie in the maximal ideal by the weak Eisenstein property proved in the completed-level file; the constant term p is a uniformizer, hence not in the square of the maximal ideal. Mathlib's Eisenstein criterion gives irreducibility over W, and Gauss's lemma for monic polynomials transfers it to A.
