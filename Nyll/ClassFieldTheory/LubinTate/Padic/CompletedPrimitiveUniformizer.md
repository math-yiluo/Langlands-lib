# The completed p-adic primitive point is a uniformizer

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LubinTate.Padic.CompletedPrimitiveUniformizer`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For a prime `p` and a level `n`, the library builds a completed level field `padicCompletedLevelField p n` over the completed unramified coefficient field `padicCompletedUnramifiedField p`, generated by a chosen primitive point for the standard Lubin–Tate series with uniformizer `p`: a root of the primitive Lubin–Tate polynomial of degree d = (p − 1)·pⁿ. This file shows that this point has valuation one, so that the completed level is totally ramified of degree d, and does the same for the theta point attached to a changed uniformizer.

## Main results

- `padicCompletedPrimitiveRootInteger_addVal`: the normalized additive valuation (`IsDiscreteValuationRing.addVal`, with values in ℕ∞) of the chosen primitive point in the valuation ring of the completed level is 1.
- `padicCompletedLevel_ramificationIndex_eq_degree`: the ramification index of the completed level over the completed unramified field equals the degree, i.e. the extension is totally ramified.
- `padicCompletedPrimitiveRootInteger_irreducible` and `padicCompletedPrimitiveRoot_isUniformizer`: the point is irreducible in the valuation ring and is a uniformizer of the valuation of the completed level.
- `padicChangedUniformizerThetaValue_addVal` and `padicChangedUniformizerThetaValue_isUniformizer`: for a unit `u` of the valuation ring of ℚ_p, the theta point `padicChangedUniformizerThetaValue p u n` (a root of the primitive polynomial for the changed uniformizer built from `p` and `u`) also has valuation 1 and is a uniformizer of the same completed level. A private lemma also gives ramification index (p − 1)·pⁿ.

## Proof

The integral primitive polynomial `Q` is monic and Eisenstein, with constant term the image of the uniformizer. Writing `Q = X^d + R`, the value `R(root)` is the constant term (valuation `e`, the ramification index) plus `root` times an element of 𝔓^e, so it has valuation exactly `e`; hence d·v(root) = e. The degree d comes from the power basis whose generator has the completed primitive polynomial as minimal polynomial, and the fundamental identity d = e·f gives e ≤ d. With v(root) ≥ 1 this forces v(root) = 1 and e = d. For the theta point, membership in the maximal ideal comes from its evaluation property (some power lies in any neighbourhood of 0), and e = d is taken from the first part.

## Points to watch

- Ramification index, degree and the complete discretely valued fields `padicCompletedLevelCompleteDVF`, `padicCompletedUnramifiedCompleteDVF` are the library's own structures, not Mathlib's.
- The file installs private local instances (an ideal-adic structure, completeness and Hausdorffness) on the valuation ring of the completed level, used only in its own proofs.
