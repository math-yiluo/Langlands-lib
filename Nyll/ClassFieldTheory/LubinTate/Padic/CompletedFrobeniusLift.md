# Frobenius lifts on the completed p-adic Lubin–Tate levels

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LubinTate.Padic.CompletedFrobeniusLift`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let A be the completed maximal unramified extension of ℚ_p with arithmetic Frobenius φ, and E = A(λ) the completed standard Lubin–Tate level at level n, generated by a primitive root λ of the standard primitive polynomial. That polynomial has coefficients in ℚ_p, so φ fixes it, and φ extends to a field automorphism of E sending λ to any other root of it. The file constructs two families of such lifts: one indexed by a unit parameter a of level n, the other by a unit u of ℤ_p.

## Main definitions and results

- `padicCompletedLevelOriginalAlgebra`, `padicCompletedLevelFrobeniusAlgebra`: the usual A-algebra structure on E and the twisted one in which a ∈ A acts through φ(a); `..._algebraMap` records the twist.
- `padicCompletedPrimitivePolynomial_frobenius`: φ fixes the completed primitive polynomial.
- `padicCompletedUnitParameterRoot_aeval_minpoly_frobenius`: the unit-parameter root is a root of the minimal polynomial of λ for the twisted structure.
- `padicCompletedFrobeniusLiftAlgHom`, `padicCompletedFrobeniusLift`, `padicCompletedFrobeniusLiftEquiv`: for a unit parameter a, the φ-semilinear map E → E sending λ to the a-root, then as a ring map and, after `padicCompletedFrobeniusLift_surjective`, as a ring automorphism. Simp lemmas: it acts by φ on A and sends λ to the prescribed root.
- `padicCompletedUnitFrobeniusLiftAlgHom`, `padicCompletedUnitFrobeniusLift`, `padicCompletedUnitFrobeniusLiftEquiv`: the same construction for a unit u of ℤ_p, sending λ to the completed standard unit action of u on λ; `padicCompletedUnitFrobeniusLift_surjective` and the analogous simp lemmas.

## Proofs

Each lift is `PowerBasis.lift` along the power basis of λ, for the twisted codomain structure; it applies because φ fixes the minimal polynomial and the target point is a root. Injectivity is automatic for field maps. For surjectivity, the image contains A (through φ⁻¹) and the target root, and an imported result says that this root generates E over A.

## Watch for

- The lifts are ring automorphisms of E, not A-algebra maps: they are φ-semilinear, encoded by `AlgHom` with the twisted structure on the codomain.
- Only the action on A and on λ is specified here; that the lifts preserve the valuation ring of E is proved in another file.
