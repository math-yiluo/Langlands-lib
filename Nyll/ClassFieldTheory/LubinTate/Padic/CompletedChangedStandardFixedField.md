# Fixed field of the changed-uniformizer Artin candidate

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LubinTate.Padic.CompletedChangedStandardFixedField`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

In the finite compositum M of the standard multiplicative Lubin–Tate level and the changed-uniformizer field D inside the completed p-adic level, the file shows that the cyclic group generated by the Artin candidate τ (the restricted inverse completed Frobenius lift) has fixed field exactly D, so Gal(M/D) is generated by τ.

## Main results

- `padicCompletedChangedFieldInStandardChangedCompositum`: D as an intermediate field of M.
- `padicCompletedChangedUniformizerArtinCandidate_fixedField_eq_changed`: the fixed field of the powers of τ is D.
- `padicCompletedChangedUniformizerArtinCandidate_zpowers_eq_fixingSubgroup`: the powers of τ form the subgroup fixing D.
- `padicCompletedChangedUniformizerRelativeArtinCandidate_zpowers_eq_top`: viewed over D, τ generates the whole relative automorphism group.

## Proofs

An element fixed by τ is fixed by the Frobenius lift, hence lies in D, its fixed field; conversely τ is the identity on D. The second statement is the Galois correspondence of Mathlib for the finite Galois extension M of ℚ_p; the third follows by restricting scalars to ℚ_p.
