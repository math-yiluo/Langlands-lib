# The completed unramified coefficient field over ℚ_p

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LubinTate.Padic.CompletedUnramifiedField`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

The coefficient ring of the library's p-adic Lubin–Tate constructions is the ring of Witt vectors W = W(F̄_p) over an algebraic closure of 𝔽_p (`padicCompletedUnramifiedWittRing p`). Its fraction field plays the role of the completed maximal unramified extension of ℚ_p. This file equips Mathlib's fraction field of W with its discrete valuation, its completeness, a ℚ_p-algebra structure and a Frobenius automorphism over ℚ_p, all taken from Mathlib's Witt-vector theory.

## Main definitions and results

- `padicCompletedUnramifiedField p`: the fraction field of W (an abbreviation for Mathlib's `FractionRing`).
- `padicCompletedUnramifiedWittRing_maximalIdeal`: the maximal ideal of W is generated by `p`.
- `padicCompletedUnramifiedValuation`: the valuation of the DVR W on its fraction field, with values in ℤᵐ⁰, together with the induced `Valued` instance; `padicCompletedUnramifiedWittRingEquivValuationSubring` identifies W with its valuation ring.
- `padicCompletedUnramifiedValuation_isAdicComplete`: this valuation ring is complete for its maximal-ideal-adic topology; `padicCompletedUnramifiedCompleteDVF` packages the field as a complete discretely valued field.
- `padicCompletedUnramifiedFieldAlgebra` (instance): the ℚ_p-algebra structure extending the injective map ℤ_p → W (`padicIntToCompletedUnramifiedWittRing_injective`) to fraction fields.
- `padicCompletedUnramifiedIntegerMap`: the induced map from the valuation ring of ℚ_p to that of the field; it agrees with the algebra map, and the ideal generated by the image of the maximal ideal is the maximal ideal of the target (`padicCompletedUnramifiedIntegerMap_map_maximalIdeal`).
- `padicCompletedUnramifiedValuation_hasExtension` (also an instance): the valuation of the field extends the p-adic valuation.
- `padicCompletedUnramifiedFrobenius`: Mathlib's Witt-vector Frobenius extended to the fraction field, as a ℚ_p-algebra automorphism; it is the Witt Frobenius on W (`padicCompletedUnramifiedFrobenius_algebraMap_witt`), preserves the valuation (`padicCompletedUnramifiedFrobenius_valuation`) and is continuous (`padicCompletedUnramifiedFrobenius_continuous`).

## Proofs

Completeness is Mathlib's p-adic completeness of Witt vectors, carried across the ring isomorphism with the valuation ring. The extension of valuations comes from the fact that the integer map is a local homomorphism. That the Frobenius commutes with ℚ_p uses that it fixes the image of ℤ_p; that it preserves the valuation uses the factorization of a nonzero Witt vector as pᵐ times a unit.

## Points to watch

- The complete discretely valued field `CompleteDVF` and the valuation-ring map are the library's own structures. Completeness here is ideal-adic completeness of the valuation ring.
- The file stresses that no second Witt ring, p-adic field or Frobenius is introduced: these are Mathlib's, and the file adds the ℚ_p-algebra structure, the `CompleteDVF` package, the valuation-ring map and the valuation-extension instance.
