# Roots of the primitive polynomial in the completed level

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.LubinTate.Padic.CompletedPrimitiveAction`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let A be the completed maximal unramified extension of ℚ_p and E the completed level at level n, the splitting field over A of the n-th standard primitive Lubin–Tate polynomial Q (uniformizer p), with chosen root λ. The file embeds the finite standard level of ℚ_p into E, sending its generator to λ, and shows that the roots of Q in E are exactly the images of the unit parameters of level n. Consequently every root generates E over A, Q is the minimal polynomial of λ, and the unit action of a unit u of ℤ_p on λ is again a root.

## Main definitions and results

- `padicStandardLevelEmbedding p n`: the ℚ_p-embedding of the standard level into E with generator ↦ λ (`..._apply_gen`).
- `padicCompletedUnitParameterRoot p n a`: the image of the a-th unit-parameter root of the finite level; equal to λ for a = 1, injective in a, and a root of Q (`..._isRoot`).
- `padicCompletedStandardPrimitivePointUnitAction p n u`: the standard scalar endomorphism [u] evaluated at λ in the valuation ring of E. It is admissible for evaluation, equals λ for u = 1, is killed by the (n+1)-st iterate of the Lubin–Tate polynomial but not by the n-th, and is a root of Q (`..._isRoot`).
- `padicCompletedPrimitiveRootSet_natCard`, `padicStandardUnitParameter_natCard`: both the root set of Q in E and the unit parameters of level n have (p − 1)·p^n elements.
- `padicCompletedUnitParameterRootSet_bijective`: unit parameters correspond bijectively to roots of Q in E.
- `padicCompletedUnitParameterRoot_mem_adjoin`, `padicCompletedPrimitiveRootSet_subset_adjoin_parameter`: every root lies in the A-algebra generated by any given parameter root.
- `padicCompletedUnitParameterRoot_adjoin_eq_top`, `padicCompletedStandardPrimitivePointUnitAction_adjoin_eq_top`, `padicCompletedPrimitiveRoot_adjoin_eq_top`: each such root generates E over A.
- `padicCompletedPrimitiveRoot_minpoly`: the minimal polynomial of λ over A is Q.
- `padicCompletedPrimitivePowerBasis`, `padicCompletedUnitParameterPowerBasis`, `padicStandardLevelUnitParameterPowerBasis`: power bases generated by λ, by a parameter root, and, in the finite level, by a parameter root.

## Proofs

Parameter roots are distinct roots of Q, and counting shows they exhaust its roots, Q being separable and split in E. Each finite parameter root generates the finite level over ℚ_p, so every other root is a polynomial in it with ℚ_p-coefficients; since the roots generate the splitting field, so does each one. For [u]λ, the factorization of the (n+1)-st iterate as the n-th iterate times Q, together with the injectivity of [u] for a unit u, shows it is a root of Q. The minimal polynomial uses the irreducibility of Q over A from an imported file.

## Watch for

- λ is an arbitrary chosen root, fixed once in the file that defines the completed level.
