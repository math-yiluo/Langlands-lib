# The Herbrand quotient of the S-unit group

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AlgebraicNumberTheory.SUnit.Herbrand`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let L/K be an extension of number fields with cyclic group G = `L ≃ₐ[K] L` generated by σ, and S a G-stable finite set of finite places of L. This file computes the Herbrand quotient of the S-unit group of L. The answer is |G|⁻¹ times the product of the stabilizer orders of the G-orbits of logarithmic places, which are the infinite places of L and the places in S. For Galois L/K these orders are local degrees.

## From S-units to the logarithmic lattice

- `sUnitFullLogMulHom`: the logarithm as a surjective, G-equivariant homomorphism from the S-units onto `fullLogLattice S`. `sUnitFullLogMulHom_ker` says its kernel is the torsion subgroup.
- `rootsOfUnityEquivSUnitTorsion` and `sUnitTorsionFinite`: the torsion of the S-units is the group of roots of unity of L, and it is finite.
- `extendedFullLogLatticeEquivProdInt`: the imported extended lattice is G-equivariantly isomorphic to `fullLogLattice S` × ℤ, with trivial action on ℤ.

## Herbrand quotients

All three theorems assume that every element of G is a power of σ (hypothesis `hgen`). Each asserts that the Herbrand quotient for σ is defined and has the stated value in ℚ.

- `fullLogLattice_herbrandQuotient_eq_stabilizerProduct_div_card`: for the logarithmic lattice, h = (∏_ω |Stab ω|) / |G|.
- `sUnit_herbrandQuotient_eq_stabilizerProduct_div_card`: the same value for `SUnitGroup S`.
- `sUnit_herbrandQuotient_eq_localDegreeProduct_div_card`, assuming `IsGalois K L`: h = |G|⁻¹ ∏_ω d(ω), where d is `logPlaceLocalDegree` evaluated at a chosen representative `ω.out` of each orbit.

## Local degrees

- `finiteLogPlaceLocalDegree K L P` is `ramificationIdxIn` times `inertiaDegIn` of the prime p of K below P; for Galois L/K this is e(P/p)·f(P/p). `logPlaceLocalDegree` is this number at a finite place, and 1 or 2 at an infinite place according to whether it is unramified over K.
- `finitePlace_stabilizer_card_eq_localDegree` and `logPlace_stabilizer_card_eq_localDegree`: for Galois L/K, the stabilizer of a place has order equal to its local degree.

## Proof

For the logarithmic lattice Λ, the sequence Λ → Λ × ℤ → ℤ and h(ℤ) = |G| (`trivialInt_herbrandQuotient_eq_card`) give h(Λ)·|G| = h(Λ × ℤ). Λ × ℤ is the extended lattice, with the stabilizer product as quotient. The torsion is finite, with h = 1, so the value passes to the S-units.

## Points to watch

- S contains only finite places of L, and it must be G-stable. The infinite places of L are always included. The product runs over G-orbits of places of L, not over places of K.
- Cyclicity is the hypothesis `hgen`. The Galois hypothesis is used only in the local-degree form.
- `SUnitGroup S` (imported) abbreviates Mathlib's `Set.unit`.
- Herbrand quotients are stated as `∃ h : HerbrandQuotientDefined ..., herbrandQuotient ... = ...`. The value does not depend on the generator σ.
