# A degree-p subextension of a cyclic extension

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AlgebraicNumberTheory.Galois.CyclicPrimeDegreeSubextension`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For a finite cyclic Galois extension L/K of degree n > 1, this file fixes a prime p dividing n and constructs an intermediate field M that is cyclic Galois of degree p over K, with L/M cyclic Galois of degree n/p. It is meant as the induction step for arguments on cyclic extensions.

## Definitions

- `cyclicGaloisGenerator`: a chosen generator σ of Gal(L/K); `orderOf_cyclicGaloisGenerator` says its order is [L : K].
- `cyclicDegreePrime hdegree`: a chosen prime p dividing [L : K], given `hdegree : 1 < [L : K]` (`cyclicDegreePrime_prime`, `cyclicDegreePrime_dvd_finrank`).
- `cyclicPrimeDegreeSubgroup hdegree`: the subgroup generated by σ^p, of order [L : K]/p and index p (`cyclicPrimeDegreeSubgroup_card`, `cyclicPrimeDegreeSubgroup_index`); it is normal.
- `cyclicPrimeDegreeIntermediate hdegree`: its fixed field M.

## Results

- `cyclicPrimeDegreeIntermediate_finrank`: [M : K] = p.
- `cyclicPrimeDegreeIntermediate_top_finrank`: [L : M] = [L : K]/p.
- Instances: M/K and L/M are Galois, and both Gal(M/K) and Gal(L/M) are cyclic.

The degree of M comes from Gal(M/K) ≅ Gal(L/K)/⟨σ^p⟩, which is cyclic as a quotient of a cyclic group; Gal(L/M) is the cyclic subgroup itself.

## Points to watch

- The generator and the prime are made by choice, so M is one particular degree-p subfield; nothing here identifies it with a subfield given otherwise.
