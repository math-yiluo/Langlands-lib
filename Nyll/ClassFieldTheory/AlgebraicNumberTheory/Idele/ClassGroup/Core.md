# The ideal class group as a quotient of the idele group

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AlgebraicNumberTheory.Idele.ClassGroup.Core`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For a number field `K`, the file shows that the quotient of the idele group by the subgroup generated by the ideles integral at every finite place and the principal ideles is the ordinary ideal class group of `𝓞 K`.

## Main results

- `count_toPrincipalIdeal`: the exponent of a prime `v` in the principal fractional ideal of `x ∈ Kˣ` is `-log` of the `v`-adic valuation of `x` (the additive form of Mathlib's multiplicative valuation).
- `fractionalIdeal_principalIdele`: the library's map from ideles to fractional ideals sends the principal idele of `x` to the principal fractional ideal `(x)`; consequently `idealClass_principalIdele` says that principal ideles have trivial ideal class.
- `classGroup_mk_eq_one_iff`: the class of a fractional ideal is trivial if and only if the ideal is principal.
- `ordinaryIdealClassSubgroup`: the subgroup `integralAtFinitePlaces ⊔ principalSubgroup K`.
- `ordinaryIdealClassSubgroup_eq_ker`: this subgroup is the kernel of `idealClass`, the map from ideles to `ClassGroup (𝓞 K)`.
- `quotientIntegralSupPrincipalEquiv`: the group isomorphism `IdeleGroup K ⧸ (integralAtFinitePlaces ⊔ principalSubgroup K) ≃* ClassGroup (𝓞 K)`.

## Proofs

The kernel computation rests on the imported fact that the kernel of the fractional-ideal map is the subgroup of ideles integral at all finite places (`fractionalIdeal_ker`); the isomorphism then composes `QuotientGroup.congr` with the imported `quotientIdealClassKernelEquiv`.

## Points to watch

`integralAtFinitePlaces` imposes a unit condition only at the finite places; the archimedean components are unrestricted.
