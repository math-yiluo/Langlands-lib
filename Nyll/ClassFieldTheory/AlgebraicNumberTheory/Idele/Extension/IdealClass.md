# Extension of ideles, idele classes and ideal classes

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AlgebraicNumberTheory.Idele.Extension.IdealClass`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For a finite Galois extension `L/K` of number fields, the file defines the extension map `I_K → I_L` on ideles, passes it to idele classes, and compares it with extension of fractional ideals and ideal classes. The map is built by including `I_K` into the relative ideles of `𝔸_K ⊗_K L` and then applying the library's comparison with the ideles of `L`.

## Fractional ideals and ideal classes

- `FractionalIdealGroup.extension`: extension `I ↦ I·𝒪_L` of invertible fractional ideals, from Mathlib's `FractionalIdeal.extendedHom`.
- `count_extension_prime`, `count_extension_prime_ne`, `count_extension_factorization`, `count_extension`: the exponent of `I·𝒪_L` at a prime `W` is `e(W | w) · v_w(I)` for `w` the place below `W` (with `ramificationIdx'`); a prime extended from another place has exponent 0 at `W`.
- `classGroup_mk_extension_prime`, `classGroup_mk_extension`: extension of fractional ideals is compatible with `ClassGroup.extendedHom (𝓞 K) (𝓞 L)`.
- `ClassGroup.extendedHom_eq_one_iff_forall_ideal_map_isPrincipal`: for a torsion-free extension `A → B` of Dedekind domains, the extension map of class groups is trivial exactly when every ideal of `A` becomes principal in `B`.

## Ideles

- `IdeleGroup.extension : IdeleGroup K →* IdeleGroup L`. `extension_infiniteComponent` and `extension_finiteComponent`: the component at a place `W` of `L` is the image of the component at the place below under the canonical map of completions.
- `localOrder_finitePlaceAdicCompletionMap` and `extension_localOrder`: local orders are multiplied by the ramification index.
- `fractionalIdeal_extension`, `idealClass_extension`: the fractional ideal and ideal class of an extended idele are the extensions of those of the original idele. `extension_mem_ordinaryIdealClassSubgroup` follows: the subgroup generated by ideles integral at all finite places and principal ideles is preserved.
- `extension_principalIdele`: principal ideles go to principal ideles.
- `extension_self` and `extension_comp`: extension along `K = K` is the identity, and extension is functorial in a tower `K ⊆ M ⊆ L`.

## Idele classes

- `ideleClassExtension : IdeleClassGroup K →* IdeleClassGroup L`, the map induced on quotients, with `ideleClassExtension_mk`, `ideleClassExtension_self` and `ideleClassExtension_comp`.

## Proof

The component formulas unwind the relative-to-ordinary comparison place by place. The ideal-theoretic statements compare exponents prime by prime, using the factorization of a fractional ideal into prime powers.

## Points to watch

- The idele extension map is the library's own construction through the tensor-product model, not a map defined componentwise in Mathlib.
- Functoriality is stated only for towers in which `M/K`, `L/M` and `L/K` are all finite Galois. Most other results drop the Galois hypothesis.
