# Decomposing S-ideles, families of local tensor factors, and unramified unit blocks

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AlgebraicNumberTheory.Idele.Cohomology.Decomposition`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

The file collects three inputs for the cohomology of ideles in a Galois extension: a product decomposition of the `S`-ideles, the Galois-equivariant identification of families of local tensor factors with induced local blocks, and the vanishing of low-degree Tate cohomology for unramified induced blocks of local units.

## S-ideles as a product

For a number field `F` and a finite set `S` of finite places:

- `FiniteSPlaceFactors S` is the product of `(v.adicCompletion F)ˣ` over `v ∈ S` and of the integral local units over `v ∉ S`, and `IdeleSPlaceFactors S` adds the archimedean idele group `InfiniteIdeleGroup F`.
- `finiteIdeleOfSPlaceFactors` assembles such factors into a finite idele.
- `finiteSupportedAtEquivSPlaceFactors`: a group isomorphism from `FiniteIdeleGroup.supportedAt S` onto `FiniteSPlaceFactors S`; `ideleSupportedAtEquivSPlaceFactors`: a group isomorphism from the library's subgroup `IdeleGroup.supportedAt S` onto `IdeleSPlaceFactors S`, with simp lemmas for each component.

## Families of local tensor factors

For a finite Galois extension of fields `L/K` and a family `d : ι → LocalPlaceDatum K L` of local data (indexed by an arbitrary type):

- `LocalTensorFamily d`: the product of the unit groups of the local tensor algebras at the base places, with the componentwise Galois action `localTensorFamilyAction`; `localBlockFamilyAction` is the componentwise induced-module action on `LocalBlockFamily d`.
- `localTensorFamilyEquivLocalBlockFamily`: the componentwise isomorphism between them; `localTensorFamilyEquivLocalBlockFamily_smul` and its inverse form say it is equivariant for the full group `L ≃ₐ[K] L`.

## Unramified unit blocks

For a finite unramified Galois extension `ell/k` of nonarchimedean local fields:

- `unramifiedLocalIntegerUnitsHerbrand_subsingleton`: if `g` generates `Gal(ell/k)`, then `HerbrandH0` and `HerbrandHMinusOne` (for `g`) of the integer units `𝒪[ell]ˣ` are trivial; the `_frobenius` variant takes `g` to be the arithmetic Frobenius. They are read off the imported unramified local theorems `unramified_units_tateCohomology_and_norm_surjective_for_generator` and `unramified_units_tateCohomology_and_norm_surjective` respectively.
- `unramifiedInducedIntegerUnitsHerbrandH0Equiv`, `unramifiedInducedIntegerUnitsHerbrandHMinusOneEquiv`: for a finite group `G` generated by `σ` and a subgroup `H` with `e : H ≃* Gal(ell/k)`, Shapiro's lemma and change of group identify the cohomology of `G` with coefficients in the induced module of `𝒪[ell]ˣ` with that of `Gal(ell/k)` in `𝒪[ell]ˣ`. These two need only a finite field extension with `ValuativeRel ell` and a given action of `Gal(ell/k)` on `𝒪[ell]ˣ`.
- `unramifiedInducedIntegerUnitsHerbrand_subsingleton`: both groups vanish for the induced block.

## Points to watch

- `HerbrandH0` and `HerbrandHMinusOne` are the library's degree 0 and degree −1 groups for a cyclic group, the latter depending on a chosen generator; the results assume `G` cyclic, through the hypothesis that every element is a power of `σ`.
- The local fields `k` and `ell` live in `Type`, and the extension is unramified through the library's class `IsUnramifiedValuedExtension`. The vanishing theorems also assume `Valuation.HasExtension`, `IsIntegralClosure 𝒪[ell] 𝒪[k] ell` and `Module.Finite 𝒪[k] 𝒪[ell]` as instances.
