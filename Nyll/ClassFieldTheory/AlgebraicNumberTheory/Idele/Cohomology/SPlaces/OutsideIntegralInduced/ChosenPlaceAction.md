# The decomposition-group action at a chosen finite place

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AlgebraicNumberTheory.Idele.Cohomology.SPlaces.OutsideIntegralInduced.ChosenPlaceAction`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `L/K` be a finite Galois extension of number fields with group `G` and `w₀` a finite place of `K`. Above `w₀` the library fixes a place of `L` (`chosenFinitePlaceExtension`) and a local extension `E/k` (`ChosenFinitePlaceLocalizedCompletion` over `ChosenFinitePlaceBaseCompletion`). The file names the decomposition group, its actions on the units of `E`, and the induced `G`-modules used by the integral induced-block construction, and it records the local cohomological input in the unramified case.

## Main definitions

- `ChosenFinitePlaceDecompositionGroup w₀`: the decomposition group of the chosen place.
- `ChosenFinitePlaceLocalizedIntegerUnits w₀ = 𝒪[E]ˣ` and `ChosenFinitePlaceLocalizedFieldUnits w₀ = Eˣ`, with the decomposition-group actions `chosenFinitePlaceDecompositionGroupIntegerUnitsAction` and `chosenFinitePlaceDecompositionGroupLocalUnitsAction`.
- `ChosenFinitePlaceInducedIntegerUnits w₀`: the module induced from the decomposition group to `G` by `𝒪[E]ˣ`, with `G`-action `chosenFinitePlaceInducedIntegerUnitsAction`; `ChosenFinitePlaceLocalPlaceBlock w₀` is the local block at `w₀`, with the induced action `chosenFinitePlaceLocalPlaceBlockAction`.

## Main results

- `chosenFinitePlaceDecompositionGroupIntegerUnitsAction_smul_eq_pullback`: the action of the decomposition group on `𝒪[E]ˣ` is, by definition, the pullback of the Galois action of `Gal(E/k)` along the isomorphism `decompositionGroupEquivAlgebraicLocalizationAut`.
- `chosenFinitePlaceLocalizedIntegerUnits_unramifiedHerbrand_subsingleton`: if `g` generates `Gal(E/k)` and the chosen extension is unramified (`hunram : ChosenFinitePlaceIsUnramified w₀`), then `HerbrandH0` and `HerbrandHMinusOne` (for `g`) of `𝒪[E]ˣ` are trivial. It is the imported local theorem `unramifiedLocalIntegerUnitsHerbrand_subsingleton`, applied after turning `hunram` into the unramifiedness instance.

## Points to watch

- Unramifiedness is a hypothesis on the chosen place, and the local Galois group must be generated by the given `g`; the global group `G` is not assumed cyclic here.
- The actions are given names so that later files do not unfold the construction of the chosen localization.
- `K` and `L` are taken in `Type`.
