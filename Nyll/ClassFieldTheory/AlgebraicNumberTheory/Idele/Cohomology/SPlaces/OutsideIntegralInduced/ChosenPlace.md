# Integral induced blocks at an unramified chosen finite place

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AlgebraicNumberTheory.Idele.Cohomology.SPlaces.OutsideIntegralInduced.ChosenPlace`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `L/K` be a cyclic Galois extension of number fields with group `G`, generated by `σ`, and `w₀` a finite place of `K`. Above `w₀` a place of `L` is chosen (`chosenFinitePlaceExtension`), giving a local extension `E/k` (`ChosenFinitePlaceLocalizedCompletion` over `ChosenFinitePlaceBaseCompletion`) whose decomposition group `H` acts on the integer units `𝒪[E]ˣ`. The file shows that when this chosen extension is unramified, the integral unit subgroup of the local tensor factor at `w₀` has trivial low-degree Tate cohomology.

## Main results

- `relativeLocalTensorDecompositionIntegralUnitSubgroup_unramifiedHerbrandH0_subsingleton`: under the hypothesis `hunram : ChosenFinitePlaceIsUnramified w₀`, the degree-zero group `HerbrandH0 G` of `relativeLocalTensorDecompositionIntegralUnitSubgroup w₀` (with its `G`-action) is trivial.
- `relativeLocalTensorDecompositionIntegralUnitSubgroup_unramifiedHerbrandHMinusOne_subsingleton`: the same for the degree −1 group with respect to `σ`.

## Proofs

A private lemma first shows that `HerbrandH0 H 𝒪[E]ˣ` and `HerbrandHMinusOne H 𝒪[E]ˣ` (for the generator of `H` derived from `σ`) are trivial: the decomposition group is isomorphic to `Gal(E/k)` (`decompositionGroupEquivAlgebraicLocalizationAut`), its action on `𝒪[E]ˣ` is the pullback of the Galois action, and the imported unramified local result `chosenFinitePlaceLocalizedIntegerUnits_unramifiedHerbrand_subsingleton` applies to the image generator. The main results then use the imported `G`-equivariant isomorphism between the integral unit subgroup and the module induced from `H` to `G` by `𝒪[E]ˣ`, and Shapiro's lemma for finite cyclic groups (`inducedHerbrandH0EquivOfFiniteCyclic`, `inducedHerbrandHMinusOneEquivOfFiniteCyclic`).

## Points to watch

- Unramifiedness is a hypothesis, in the form of the library's predicate `ChosenFinitePlaceIsUnramified` on the chosen place above `w₀`.
- Cyclicity of `G` enters through the hypothesis `hσ` that every element is a power of `σ`.
- `K` and `L` are taken in `Type`.
