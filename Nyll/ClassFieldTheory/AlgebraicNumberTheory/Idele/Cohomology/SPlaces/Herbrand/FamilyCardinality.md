# Herbrand cardinalities of the unrestricted local-block family

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AlgebraicNumberTheory.Idele.Cohomology.SPlaces.Herbrand.FamilyCardinality`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `L/K` be a cyclic Galois extension of number fields with group `G`, generated by `σ`, and `S` a finite set of finite places of `K`. The family of local data `relativeUnrestrictedSPlaceDatum S` runs over all infinite places of `K` and the places in `S`; to each it attaches an induced local block, the module induced from the action of a decomposition group on the units of a local field. The file computes the orders of the degree 0 and degree −1 groups of the product of these blocks.

## Main results

- `relativeUnrestrictedLocalBlockFamilyHerbrandH0_card_eq_product`: the degree-zero group `HerbrandH0 G (LocalBlockFamily d)` has order `∏ relativeUnrestrictedSPlaceLocalDegree S i`, the product of the local degrees.
- `relativeUnrestrictedLocalBlockFamilyHerbrandHMinusOne_card_eq_one`: the degree −1 group, for the generator `σ`, has one element.

## Proofs

The imported equivalences `localBlockFamilyHerbrandH0Equiv` and `localBlockFamilyHerbrandHMinusOneEquiv` identify the cohomology of the family with the product over the places of the cohomology of each decomposition group, the generator in degree −1 being the one derived from `σ` (`subgroupGeneratorOfGenerator`). The order of a product is the product of the orders, and the local values (local degree in degree zero, one in degree −1) are imported from the local computation.

## Points to watch

- Both results assume that `G` is cyclic, through the hypothesis `hgen` that every element is a power of `σ`.
- `K` and `L` are taken in `Type`.
