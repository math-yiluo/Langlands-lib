# Finiteness for the unrestricted local-block family

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AlgebraicNumberTheory.Idele.Cohomology.SPlaces.Herbrand.FamilyFinite`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `L/K` be a Galois extension of number fields whose group `G` is generated by `σ`, and `S` a finite set of finite places of `K`. For the family of induced local blocks attached to all infinite places of `K` and the places in `S` (`relativeUnrestrictedSPlaceDatum S`), the file shows that the low-degree Tate groups of the product are finite.

- `relativeUnrestrictedLocalBlockFamilyHerbrandH0Finite`: the degree-zero group of `LocalBlockFamily d` is finite.
- `relativeUnrestrictedLocalBlockFamilyHerbrandHMinusOneFinite`: the degree −1 group, for the generator `σ`, is finite.

Both follow from the imported equivalences identifying the cohomology of the family with the product, over the places, of the cohomology of the decomposition groups acting on the multiplicative groups `(LocalizedCompletion …)ˣ` of the localized completions, together with the imported finiteness of each local term. The hypothesis `hgen` (every element of `G` is a power of `σ`) makes `G` cyclic; `K` and `L` are taken in `Type`.
