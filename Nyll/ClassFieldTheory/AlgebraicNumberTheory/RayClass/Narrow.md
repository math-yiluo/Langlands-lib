# The exact sequence of the narrow class group

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AlgebraicNumberTheory.RayClass.Narrow`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let K be a number field. This file defines the narrow class group of K as a quotient of the idele group. It then proves the exact sequence

1 → (units modulo totally positive units) → (signs at the infinite places) → Cl⁺(K) → Cl(K) → 1.

## Definitions

- `narrowDenominator`: the subgroup of the idele group generated by two subgroups. The first is the idele congruence subgroup of `Modulus.narrowOfFinite 0`, the modulus with zero finite part and every real place. The second is the principal ideles.
- `NarrowClassGroup K`: the idele group modulo `narrowDenominator`. `rayClassGroupNarrowZeroEquivNarrowClassGroup` identifies it with the ray class group of that modulus.
- `realSignGroup K`: the infinite idele group modulo `narrowInfiniteCongruenceSubgroup`, the positive congruence subgroup.
- `totallyPositiveUnitSubgroup`: the units of 𝓞 K whose image at the infinite places lies in that positive subgroup. `IntegralUnitSignGroup` is the quotient of the units by it.
- The maps: `integralUnitSignToRealSign` from unit sign classes to `realSignGroup K`, `signToNarrow` from `realSignGroup K` to `NarrowClassGroup K`, and `narrowToClassGroup` to Mathlib's `ClassGroup (𝓞 K)`, induced by `IdeleGroup.idealClass`. Helper maps such as `infiniteToIdele` build them.

## Results

- `finiteCongruenceSubgroup_zero`: for the zero finite modulus, the congruence subgroup of finite ideles is the subgroup of ideles that are units at every finite place.
- `toPrincipalIdeal_eq_one_iff_mem_integralUnits`: x ∈ Kˣ generates the unit fractional ideal if and only if x is a unit of 𝓞 K.
- `narrowClassGroup_exact_sequence`: the conjunction of four statements. `integralUnitSignToRealSign` is injective. Its range is the kernel of `signToNarrow`. The range of `signToNarrow` is the kernel of `narrowToClassGroup`. `narrowToClassGroup` is surjective. Each part is also a theorem of its own.

## Proof

Each map is induced by `QuotientGroup.lift`. Surjectivity comes from `IdeleGroup.idealClass_surjective`. At the narrow class group: an idele with trivial ideal class lies in `IdeleGroup.ordinaryIdealClassSubgroup`, so it is a principal idele times an idele u integral at the finite places. The finite part of u lies in `narrowDenominator`, so its infinite part is a sign class mapping to the given class. At the sign group: if an infinite idele equals c times the principal idele of x, with c in the congruence subgroup, then x generates the unit ideal, so x is a global unit.

## Points to watch

- `NarrowClassGroup` and `realSignGroup` are the library's own quotients of ideles. They are not quotients of ideal groups.
- `realSignGroup` is taken over all infinite places. The product of ℝˣ modulo the positive reals over real places appears only in a comment. The comment adds that complex places give a trivial factor, which the file does not prove.
- "Totally positive" means that the image lies in `narrowInfiniteCongruenceSubgroup`, which is defined in another file.
