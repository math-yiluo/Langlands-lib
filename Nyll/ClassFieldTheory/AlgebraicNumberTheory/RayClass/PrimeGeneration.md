# Homomorphisms on ideals prime to a modulus are fixed by primes

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.AlgebraicNumberTheory.RayClass.PrimeGeneration`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let K be a number field and m a modulus of K. The group of fractional ideals prime to m is generated by the prime ideals not dividing the finite part of m. This is the algebraic input for comparing ray reciprocity maps normalized by Frobenius elements.

## Main result

- `RayClass.primeToModulusIdeals_hom_ext`: for any monoid G, two monoid homomorphisms f, g from `RayClass.primeToModulusIdeals m` to G are equal if they agree on `RayClass.primeToModulusIdeal m v hv` for every height-one prime v of 𝓞 K outside the support of `m.finitePart`.

## Proof

An ideal I prime to m is the finite product of primes raised to the exponents of its count vector (`FractionalIdealGroup.countVector`, `FractionalIdealGroup.factorization`). These primes lie outside the support of m. The equalizer of f and g is a subgroup, so it contains I.

## Points to watch

The file does not state generation as a `Subgroup.closure` equality. It proves only this extensionality criterion for homomorphisms.
