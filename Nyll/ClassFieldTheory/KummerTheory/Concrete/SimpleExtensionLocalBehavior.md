# Local behaviour of simple Kummer extensions

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.KummerTheory.Concrete.SimpleExtensionLocalBehavior`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `K` be a number field and `L = K(β)` a finite Galois extension generated by an `n`-th root `β` of a unit `b ∈ Kˣ`. This file proves the local unit case of the Kummer ramification criterion: if `b` and `n` are units at a finite place `v`, then `L/K` is unramified at `v`. For the library's chosen simple Kummer extension `K(ⁿ√b)` it also shows that a finite place at which `b` is a local `n`-th power splits completely, and that at an infinite place where `b` is a local `n`-th power the local norm from `K_w ⊗[K] K(ⁿ√b)` is surjective.

## Main results

- `kummerGeneratedExtension_chosenFinitePlaceIsUnramified_of_valuation_eq_one`: for `L/K` finite Galois of number fields, `β ∈ Lˣ` with `β ^ n = b` and `K(β) = L`, and a finite place `v` with `v(b) = 1` and `v(n) = 1`, the extension is unramified at the chosen place above `v` (`ChosenFinitePlaceIsUnramified`).
- `chosenSimpleKummerExtension_chosenFinitePlaceIsUnramified_of_valuation_eq_one` and `chosenSimpleKummerExtension_isUnramifiedAt_at_all_finitePlacesAbove_of_valuation_eq_one`: the same for `chosenSimpleKummerExtension K n hnK b`, assuming `n ≠ 0` in `K` and a primitive `n`-th root of unity in `K`; the second gives `Algebra.IsUnramifiedAt` at every prime above `v`.
- `chosenSimpleKummerExtension_finitePlaceSplitsCompletely_of_mem_nthPowerSubgroup`: under the same two assumptions on `n` (`hnK`, `hmu`), if `b` is an `n`-th power in `(v.adicCompletion K)ˣ`, then `v` splits completely in `K(ⁿ√b)` (`FinitePlaceSplitsCompletely`).
- `chosenSimpleKummerExtension_infiniteTensorNormSubgroup_eq_top_of_mem_nthPowerSubgroup`: again under `hnK` and `hmu`, if `b` is an `n`-th power in the completion at an infinite place `w`, the norm subgroup `infiniteTensorNormSubgroup` of `K_w ⊗[K] K(ⁿ√b)` is all of `K_wˣ`.

## Proofs

For unramifiedness, the file equips the completion `K_v` and the library's local field `LocalizedCompletion` above it with the structure of nonarchimedean local fields, shows that the image of `β` is a unit generating the local extension, and applies the library's criterion `isUnramifiedValuedExtension_of_unit_kummer_generator`, a derivative argument for `X ^ n − b`. For splitting, the decomposition-field criterion of `FinitePlaceDecomposition` puts `β` in the fixed field of the decomposition group, so that group is trivial. At an infinite place the same argument makes the local degree one, so the local norm is the identity, and the tensor-product norm is transported to the library's local norm.

## Remarks

- `ChosenFinitePlaceIsUnramified` and `FinitePlaceSplitsCompletely` are the library's notions, phrased at one chosen extension of `v`; the third theorem passes to all primes above `v`.
- The unramifiedness hypothesis `v(n) = 1` excludes places above primes dividing `n`; the unramifiedness theorems claim nothing there, while the splitting theorem has no such hypothesis.
- The unramifiedness theorem for a generated extension does not ask for roots of unity in `K`; it asks that `L/K` be Galois.
