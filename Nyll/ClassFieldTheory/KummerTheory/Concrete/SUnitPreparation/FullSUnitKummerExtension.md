# The full S-unit Kummer extension

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.KummerTheory.Concrete.SUnitPreparation.FullSUnitKummerExtension`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `K` be a number field containing a primitive `n`-th root of unity, `S` a finite set of finite places and `Ω` a separable closure of `K`. This file builds `N = K(√[n]{Kˢ})`, the extension of `K` in `Ω` generated by the `n`-th roots of all `S`-units (together with `Kˣⁿ`), and shows that it is a finite Galois extension whose Galois group is `(ℤ/n)^s`, where `s = totalPlaceCard S` counts the places in `S` and the infinite places.

## Main definitions and results

- `fullSUnitKummerExtension n S`: the Kummer radical extension of the subgroup `fullSUnitKummerSubgroup n S` (generated by `S`-units and `n`-th powers).
- `exists_sUnitRoot_adjoin_eq_of_mem_fullSUnitKummerRootSet`: every defining root `β` may be rescaled by an element of `Kˣ` to a root `α` of an `S`-unit with `K(α) = K(β)`.
- `fullSUnitKummerExtension_isGalois`; `finite_fullSUnitKummerExtension_galois` and `fullSUnitKummerExtension_finiteDimensional` (assuming `n ≠ 0` in `K` and `μ_n ⊆ K`).
- `exists_finset_fullSUnitKummerRootSet_adjoin_eq` and `exists_finset_fullSUnitKummerExtensionRoots_adjoin_eq_top`: finitely many defining roots generate `N`, stated in `Ω` and inside `N`.
- `card_fullSUnitKummerExtension_galois`: `|Gal(N/K)| = n ^ s`.
- `fullSUnitKummerExtension_galois_pow_eq_one`: every element of `Gal(N/K)` has `n`-th power one.
- `chosenFullSUnitKummerExtensionGaloisEquivSUnitQuotient`: an isomorphism `Gal(N/K) ≃* Kˢ / (Kˢ)ⁿ`.
- `fullSUnitKummerExtensionGaloisCoordinates` and `fullSUnitKummerExtensionGaloisEquivPiZMod`: isomorphisms of `Gal(N/K)` with `ℤ/n × (ℤ/n)^{logRank S}` and with `Fin s → ℤ/n` (multiplicatively), using `totalPlaceCard_eq_sUnitLogRank_add_one`.
- `fullSUnitKummerExtension_galois_moduleFree`: `Gal(N/K)`, as a `ZMod n`-module, is free.

## Proofs

Finiteness and the cardinality come from the library's Kummer duality: `Gal(N/K)` is isomorphic to the characters of the restricted radical quotient with values in `μ_n`, and that quotient is finite of order `n ^ s` by results on `Kˢ / (Kˢ)ⁿ` in another file. The finite generating set comes from a primitive element of `N/K`, which lies in the field generated by finitely many roots.

## Remarks

- The isomorphisms with `Kˢ / (Kˢ)ⁿ` and with `(ℤ/n)^s` pass through a duality obtained by `Classical.choice`; they are not the canonical Kummer pairing.
- The `ZMod n`-module structure on `Additive Gal(N/K)` and the commutativity of the group are bound with `letI` in the statement of `fullSUnitKummerExtension_galois_moduleFree`.
