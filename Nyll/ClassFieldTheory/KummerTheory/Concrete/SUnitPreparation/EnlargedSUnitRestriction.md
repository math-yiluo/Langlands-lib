# Restriction from an enlarged S-unit Kummer extension

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.KummerTheory.Concrete.SUnitPreparation.EnlargedSUnitRestriction`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `K` be a number field containing a primitive `n`-th root of unity, `Ω` a separable closure of `K`, and `E ⊆ Ω` a finite abelian extension of `K` whose Galois group has exponent dividing `n`. After enlarging a finite set `S` of finite places to `S' = enlargeByFiniteKummerRadicalSupport n S`, the field `E` lies inside the full `S'`-unit Kummer extension `N = K(√[n]{K^{S'}})`. This file builds the restriction map `Gal(N/K) → Gal(E/K)`, identifies its kernel with `Gal(N/E)`, and studies the cyclic fixed fields `N^⟨σ⟩` for `σ` in that kernel. It is a step of the library's S-unit preparation argument.

## Main definitions and results

All statements assume `[FiniteDimensional K E] [IsGalois K E] [IsMulCommutative Gal(E/K)]`, a primitive `n`-th root of unity in `K`, and `σ ^ n = 1` for all `σ ∈ Gal(E/K)`.

- `le_fullSUnitKummerExtension_of_enlargedS`: `E ≤ N` as intermediate fields of `Ω`.
- `enlargedSUnitKummerAlgebra`, `enlargedSUnitKummerEmbeddedExtension`: the inclusion `E → N` as an algebra, and the image of `E` as an intermediate field of `N`.
- `enlargedSUnitKummerRestrictionHom`: restriction `Gal(N/K) →* Gal(E/K)`; it is surjective (`…_surjective`), its kernel is the fixing subgroup of the embedded copy of `E` (`…_ker_eq_fixingSubgroup`), and the fixed field of the kernel is that copy (`fixedField_enlargedSUnitKummerRestrictionHom_ker`).
- `enlargedSUnitKummerCyclicFixedField σ`: for `σ` in the kernel, the fixed field `N_σ` of the subgroup generated by `σ`. It contains `E`; `N/N_σ` is Galois and cyclic of degree `orderOf σ`; and `N_σ ≠ N` when `σ ≠ 1`.
- `orderOf_enlargedSUnitKummerRestrictionKernel_dvd`: `orderOf σ` divides `n`. If `n = p ^ v` with `p` prime, `orderOf σ = p ^ k` for some `k ≤ v`, with `k > 0` when `σ ≠ 1`.

## Proofs

The containment comes from the generation statement `kummerRadicalExtension_enlargedSUnitKummerSubgroup_eq` of the radical-support file and monotonicity of the Kummer radical extension. The remaining facts are the Galois correspondence for the finite Galois extension `N/K` (Mathlib's `restrictNormalHom`, `IsGalois.fixedField_fixingSubgroup`, `finrank_fixedField_eq_card`), together with `fullSUnitKummerExtension_galois_pow_eq_one`, which says that `Gal(N/K)` has exponent dividing `n`.

## Remarks

- The algebra `E → N` is not an instance; it is a reducible definition installed locally where needed.
- The enlarged set `S'` depends on `E` and is produced by a construction in another file, not chosen by the user.
