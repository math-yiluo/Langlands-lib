# The quadratic extension L(i)/K' at a ramified real place

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.GlobalClassFieldTheory.Reciprocity.ComplexificationArtin.RamifiedOverextension`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For a finite abelian extension `L/K` of number fields and an infinite place `v` of `K` whose chosen place above it in `L` is ramified (hypothesis `hRamified`), complex conjugation preserves the field `L(i) ⊆ ℂ` built in an earlier file. This file takes its fixed field `K'`, embeds `K` in `K'`, and shows that `L(i)/K'` is Galois with group `{1, c}`, `c` being complex conjugation, and that restriction to the rational fourth-root field is injective on this group.

## Main definitions and results

- `ramifiedInfinitePlaceRealFixedField v hRamified`: `K'`, the fixed field in `L(i)` of the subgroup generated by complex conjugation.
- `ramifiedInfinitePlaceOverfieldConjugation_fixes_base`: complex conjugation fixes the image of `K`; hence `ramifiedInfinitePlaceRealFixedFieldEmbedding`, an embedding `K → K'` compatible with `K → L(i)`. It defines the `K`-algebra structure on `K'`, with scalar towers `ℚ → K → K'` and `K → K' → L(i)` as instances.
- `K'` is a number field, finite over `K` (`ramifiedInfinitePlaceRealFixedField_finiteDimensional_over_base`) and abelian Galois over `K` (instance `ramifiedInfinitePlaceRealFixedField_isAbelianGalois_over_base`).
- `ramifiedInfinitePlaceOverextension_isGalois`, and the instance `ramifiedInfinitePlaceOverextension_isAbelianGalois`: `L(i)/K'` is Galois with cyclic, hence abelian, group.
- `ramifiedInfinitePlaceOverextensionConjugation`: the element `c` of `Gal(L(i)/K')`, acting as complex conjugation.
- `ramifiedInfinitePlaceOverextension_eq_one_or_conjugation`: every element of `Gal(L(i)/K')` is `1` or `c`.
- `ramifiedInfinitePlaceOverextensionCyclotomicRestriction`: restriction `Gal(L(i)/K') →* Gal(ℚ(μ₄)/ℚ)`; `c` restricts nontrivially (`ramifiedInfinitePlaceOverextensionCyclotomicRestriction_conjugation_ne_one`), so the restriction is injective (`ramifiedInfinitePlaceOverextensionCyclotomicRestriction_injective`).

## Proofs

`K` is fixed because the library's local Artin symbol of `-1` at the ramified place is an automorphism of `L/K` realizing complex conjugation for the chosen embedding. Galois-ness is Artin's theorem for the fixed field of a finite group, and the group is the image of the subgroup generated by `c`, with `c² = 1`. The image of a primitive fourth root of unity is not real, since its square is `-1`, so `c` moves it.

## Caveats

Everything depends on `hRamified` and on the place chosen by `chosenInfinitePlaceAbove`.
