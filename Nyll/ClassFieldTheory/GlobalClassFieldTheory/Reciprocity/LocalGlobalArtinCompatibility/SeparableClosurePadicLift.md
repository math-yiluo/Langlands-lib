# Decomposition-group lifts with positive p-adic cyclotomic degree

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.GlobalClassFieldTheory.Reciprocity.LocalGlobalArtinCompatibility.SeparableClosurePadicLift`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `L / K` be a finite abelian extension of number fields, `v` a finite place of `K`, and `p` a prime. The file builds, at the library's chosen extension of `v`, restriction maps between decomposition groups in `Gal(ℚˢᵉᵖ / K)`, `Gal(L / K)` and the cyclotomic compositum of `L`, and shows that every local decomposition automorphism of `L / K` lifts to the separable closure with `p`-adic cyclotomic degree a positive integer. It also sets up the reduction to a cyclic subextension.

## Cyclic reduction

- `automorphismCyclicFixedField σ`: the fixed field `M` of the subgroup generated by `σ ∈ Gal(L / K)`; `automorphismOverCyclicFixedField σ` is `σ` as an element of `Gal(L / M)`, which restricts to `σ` and generates `Gal(L / M)` (`..._generates`).
- `automorphismCyclicFixedPlace v σ`: the place of `M` under the chosen extension of `v`, lying above `v`; `automorphismCyclicFixedTopExtension` is the extension to `L` whose centre is the original chosen place.
- `automorphismOverCyclicFixedField_mem_chosenFinitePlaceDecompositionGroup` and `..._mem_chosenFinitePlaceArtin_range`: if `σ` lies in the decomposition group at the chosen extension of `v`, then `σ` over `M` lies in the decomposition group above the reduced place, hence in the range of the chosen local Artin map `chosenFinitePlaceArtinMonoidHom` over `M`.

## Restriction maps and the lift

- `finitePlaceSeparableClosureDecompositionRestriction v`: restriction from the decomposition group in `Gal(ℚˢᵉᵖ / K)` to that in `Gal(L / K)`; surjective. Its kernel is the image of `finitePlaceSeparableClosureTopDecompositionInclusion`, the decomposition group over `L`.
- `finitePlaceCyclotomicCompositumExtension v`: the valuation restricted to the cyclotomic compositum `numberFieldCyclotomicZHatCompositum L`, equivalent on `L` to the normalised one at the centre. The decomposition group over `L` surjects onto its decomposition group.
- `finitePlaceSeparableClosurePadicCyclotomicDegree v p`: the `p`-adic cyclotomic degree on the decomposition group in `Gal(ℚˢᵉᵖ / K)`.
- The degrees of the restriction kernel equal the range of the `p`-adic decomposition coordinate of the compositum, a closed (`..._isClosed`) and open (`..._isOpen`) subgroup of `ℤ_[p]`.
- `exists_positivePadicDegree_lift_of_surjective`: for groups, a surjection `D → A` and a degree `D → Multiplicative ℤ_[p]` under which the kernel has open image, every element of `A` lifts to some `τ` of degree `ofAdd 1 ^ n` with `n > 0`.
- `exists_finitePlaceSeparableClosureLift_with_positivePadicCyclotomicDegree`: the main result, this lemma applied to the maps above.

Openness comes from closedness plus finite index of the range, the latter from `numberFieldCyclotomicPadicDecompositionCoordinate_range_index_ne_zero_of_base_isEquiv`. The general lemma corrects any lift inside the kernel, by density of positive integers in `ℤ_[p]`.

## Remarks

- The finite-place statements refer to the library's chosen extensions (`chosenFinitePlaceExtension`) and its fixed embeddings of `K` and `L` into `ℚˢᵉᵖ`, bound locally inside the statements.
- The degree is written multiplicatively: "positive integral degree" means `ofAdd 1 ^ n` with `n : ℕ`, `n > 0`.
