# A p-adic cyclic auxiliary field for local–global Artin compatibility

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.GlobalClassFieldTheory.Reciprocity.LocalGlobalArtinCompatibility.FinitePadicCyclicData`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `L / K` be a finite abelian extension of number fields and `p` a prime. The library realises the absolute Galois group of `K` as a closed subgroup `numberFieldTowerBaseSubgroup K L` of `Gal(ℚˢᵉᵖ / ℚ)`, compatibly with an embedded copy of `L`. This file maps that group at once to the finite quotient `Gal(L / K)` and to `ℤ_[p]` by the `p`-adic cyclotomic degree, and uses the image of one element `τ` to cut out an auxiliary finite Galois extension of `K`.

## Main definitions and results

- `numberFieldTowerFiniteQuotientCoordinate`: the quotient map onto the finite quotient by the subgroup fixing `L`.
- `rationalSeparableClosurePadicCyclotomicDegree p`: the `p`-adic cyclotomic degree `Gal(ℚˢᵉᵖ / ℚ) → Multiplicative ℤ_[p]`, that is restriction to `rationalCyclotomicZHatField` followed by the coordinate `rationalCyclotomicPadicCoordinate p`; its kernel is the fixing subgroup of `rationalCyclotomicPadicField p` (`..._ker`).
- `numberFieldTowerFinitePadicCoordinate`, `numberFieldTowerFinitePadicImage`: the pair (finite coordinate, `p`-adic degree) and its image, a closed subgroup; the second projection on the image has finite kernel.
- `numberFieldTowerFinitePadicCyclicPreimage p τ`: the preimage of the closed `p`-adic cyclic subgroup (`padicCyclicClosure`) generated by the image of `τ`. It is normal, and open when the `p`-adic degree of `τ` is not `1`. `numberFieldTowerFinitePadicCyclicFixedSubgroup` is the same subgroup inside `Gal(ℚˢᵉᵖ / ℚ)`; it contains `τ`.
- `numberFieldTowerFinitePadicCyclicGaloisSubextension` and `numberFieldTowerFinitePadicAuxiliaryAbstractField` (degree of `τ` not `1`): this subgroup as a finite Galois subextension of `K`. `numberFieldTowerFinitePadicAuxiliaryCompositumSubextension`: the base change of `L / K` to it.

Assume now that the `p`-adic degree of `τ` is `ofAdd 1 ^ n` with `n > 0` and that its finite coordinate lies in the `p`-primary component of the finite quotient. Then:

- `numberFieldTowerFinitePadicCyclicImageDegree_injective`: the `p`-adic degree is injective on the closed cyclic image of `τ`;
- `..._inf_padicKernel_le_extensionSubgroup` and its ambient form `..._inf_absolutePadicKernel_le_topSubgroup`: the intersection of the cyclic preimage with the kernel of the `p`-adic degree lies in the subgroup fixing `L`;
- `numberFieldTowerFinitePadicAuxiliaryCompositumSubextension_isUnramified`: the compositum of `L` with the auxiliary field is unramified for `rationalCyclotomicDegreeData`.

Injectivity rests on `padicCyclicClosure_degree_injective_of_primePower_finiteCoordinate`. For unramifiedness, an inertia element has trivial `ℤ̂`-degree, hence trivial `p`-adic degree, and the kernel intersection places it in the subgroup fixing `L`.

## Remarks

- Fields are closed subgroups of `Gal(ℚˢᵉᵖ / ℚ)` ("abstract fields"), not Mathlib intermediate fields.
- "Unramified" is the abstract class-field-theory notion attached to the cyclotomic degree datum, tested through `isUnramified_iff_inertia_le`, where inertia means trivial cyclotomic degree; it is not ramification of primes.
- The Galois subextension needs the degree of `τ` to be nontrivial; the unramifiedness theorem needs the positivity and `p`-primary hypotheses above.
