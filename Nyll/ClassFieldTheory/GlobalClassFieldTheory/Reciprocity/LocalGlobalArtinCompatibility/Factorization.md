# Local-global compatibility of Artin maps at finite places

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.GlobalClassFieldTheory.Reciprocity.LocalGlobalArtinCompatibility.Factorization`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let L/K be a finite abelian extension of number fields and v a finite place of K. This file proves that the global norm-residue map C_K → Gal(L/K), restricted to the idele classes supported at v, equals the local Artin map at v. The proof factors the global map through the image of the local Artin map, which is the decomposition group, and checks the factor on each primary component with the cyclotomic auxiliary-field construction.

## Main results

- `globalNormResidueMonoidHom_comp_finitePlaceIdeleClass`: `globalNormResidueMonoidHom K L` composed with `IdeleGroup.finitePlaceIdeleClass v` (K_v^× → C_K) equals `chosenFinitePlaceArtinMonoidHom v`. The latter is the library's local Artin map at a chosen place of L above v.
- `finitePlaceGlobalNormResidueFactor v`: the homomorphism from the image of the local Artin map to Gal(L/K) through which the restricted global map factors (`..._comp_rangeRestrict`). `finitePlaceGlobalNormResidueFactor_eq_subtype` says it is the inclusion.
- Reductions: the main statement is equivalent to the factor being the inclusion (`globalNormResidueMonoidHom_comp_finitePlaceIdeleClass_iff`). That in turn may be checked on p-primary components (`..._eq_subtype_iff_primary`). `..._eq_subtype_on_primary` proves it there.
- `chosenFinitePlaceArtinMonoidHom_eq_one_iff_chosenLocalNorm` (simp): the local symbol is trivial exactly on the local norm subgroup.
- Local norms are killed by the global map (`chosenFinitePlaceLocalNormSubgroup_le_globalNormResidueKernel`), so the global value at a one-place class depends only on its local symbol.
- `globalNormResidueMonoidHom_finitePlaceIdeleClass_eq_of_primary_generator`: the compatibility at x when its local symbol is a p-primary element generating Gal(L/K).

## Proof

Take a p-primary element δ of the decomposition group. Let M be the fixed field of δ, so L/M is cyclic, generated by δ, and let W be the corresponding place of M over v. There δ is a p-primary generator. The auxiliary-field results of `FinitePadicAuxiliaryField` (`exists_finitePlacePrimaryCyclotomicAuxiliaryFixedField`, `numberFieldTowerFinitePadicAuxiliaryLocalGlobalRepresentative`) give an element y of M_W^× whose local and global symbols both equal δ. Its local norm z in K_v^× then has local and global symbols δ over K. For the local symbol this follows from norm-restriction compatibility of the local Artin maps. For the global symbol it follows from `globalNormResidueMonoidHomOfEmbedding_norm_restriction` and the norm of one-place idele classes. A homomorphism on a finite abelian group is fixed by its values on primary components, which ends the proof.

## Points to watch

- The local Artin map is the library's `chosenFinitePlaceArtinMonoidHom`, attached to a chosen place above v. Its kernel and image (local norms, decomposition group) come from imported files.
- The global map is the library's `globalNormResidueMonoidHom`, compared with its version through an embedding into `SeparableClosure ℚ` (`globalNormResidueMonoidHom_eq_ofEmbedding_standard`).
- The main theorem rests on the auxiliary-field construction of `FinitePadicAuxiliaryField`. This file adds the descent and the primary reduction.
- Three private steps are declared `opaque`, with proofs, so that the constructed field towers do not appear in later statements.
