# Localisations of rational cyclotomic fields at finite places

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.GlobalClassFieldTheory.Reciprocity.RationalCyclotomicLocalization`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For `m : ℕ+` and a finite place `v` of `ℚ`, the file sets up the localisation of the cyclotomic field `ℚ(ζ_m)` (`KummerTheory.rationalCyclotomicLevel m`) at the library's chosen place above `v`, and shows that it is an `m`-cyclotomic extension of the completion `ℚ_v`, generated by the image of a global primitive root.

## Main definitions and results

- `rationalCyclotomicLevelPrimitiveRoot m`: a primitive `m`-th root of unity of `ℚ(ζ_m)`, picked by choice; it is primitive of order `m` and generates `ℚ(ζ_m)` over `ℚ` (`..._adjoin_eq_top`).
- `rationalCyclotomicAdicAbsoluteValue v`: the adic absolute value of `ℚ` at `v`. `rationalCyclotomicChosenFinitePlaceExtension m v`: its extension to `ℚ(ζ_m)` given by `chosenFinitePlaceExtension`.
- `rationalCyclotomicLocalizedCompletion m v`: the library's `LocalizedCompletion` for this extension, which the file calls the algebraic localisation. Instances make it finite-dimensional and algebraic over the completion of `ℚ` at `v`; another instance makes the completion of `ℚ(ζ_m)` at the chosen extension an algebra over that completion.
- `rationalCyclotomicGlobalToLocalizedAlgHom m v`: the canonical global-to-local map from `ℚ(ζ_m)`, as a `ℚ`-algebra homomorphism. `rationalCyclotomicLocalizedPrimitiveRoot m v` is the image of the chosen root; it is primitive of order `m`, since the map is injective.
- `rationalCyclotomicLocalizedPrimitiveRoot_adjoin_eq_top` and `..._algebraAdjoin_eq_top`: this image generates the localisation over the completion, as a field and as an algebra.
- `rationalCyclotomicLevel_localizedCompletion_isCyclotomicExtension`: the localisation is an `m`-cyclotomic extension of the completion of `ℚ` at `v`.

Generation of the localisation comes from `localizedCompletion_adjoin_image_eq_top_of_adjoin_eq_top`: a generator of the global field maps to a generator of the localisation.

## Remarks

- The localisation is the library's own construction, a different type from the completion of `ℚ(ζ_m)` at the extension; both depend on the chosen extension.
- The primitive root is a choice, and the results about the localised root hold for that particular root.
