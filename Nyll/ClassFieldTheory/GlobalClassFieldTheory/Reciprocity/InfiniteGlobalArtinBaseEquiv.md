# Transport of the infinite global Artin map along field isomorphisms

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.GlobalClassFieldTheory.Reciprocity.InfiniteGlobalArtinBaseEquiv`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let K and M be number fields, with abelian Galois extensions Ω/K and Ψ/M, possibly infinite. This file shows that the global Artin map on idele classes, `infiniteGlobalIdeleClassArtinMonoidHom`, is natural for an isomorphism e : K ≃ M together with a compatible ring isomorphism c : Ω ≃ Ψ.

- `infiniteGlobalIdeleClassArtinMonoidHom_finiteProjection`: restricting the Artin symbol of an idele class to a finite Galois layer E gives the finite idele-class Artin map of E/K.
- `infiniteGlobalIdeleClassArtinMonoidHom_semilinear`: assume c extends e on K. Then conjugating the Artin symbol of a by c (`semilinearGaloisEquiv`) gives the Artin symbol over M of the transported class `ideleClassCongr e a`.

For the second result, take z in Ω and the finite Galois layer E generated by z. Its image F under c is finite abelian over M, and the finite statement `globalIdeleClassArtinMonoidHom_semilinear` for E and F gives equality at z.

The isomorphism e is a ℚ-algebra isomorphism, while c is only a ring isomorphism, tied to e by the hypothesis `hc`.
