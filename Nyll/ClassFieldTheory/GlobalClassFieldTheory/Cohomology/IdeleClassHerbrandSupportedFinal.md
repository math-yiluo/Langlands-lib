# The first inequality via supported ideles and Herbrand quotients

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.GlobalClassFieldTheory.Cohomology.IdeleClassHerbrandSupportedFinal`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `L / K` be a finite Galois extension of number fields whose Galois group `G` is cyclic, generated by `σ` (hypothesis `hgen`), and let `S` be a finite set of finite places of `K`. The file computes the Herbrand quotient of the idele class group `C_L` from the exact sequence `1 → (S-units of L) → I_L^S → C_L → 1`, where `I_L^S` is the group of relative ideles supported at `S` (units at the places above places outside `S`), and deduces the first inequality `[L : K] ≤ [C_K : N C_L]`.

## Main results

- `relativeOutsideSPlaceFactors_unramifiedHerbrandQuotient_eq_one`: if the chosen local extension is unramified at every place outside `S`, the product of the integral unit factors outside `S` has trivial `Ĥ⁰` and `Ĥ⁻¹` (`..._unramifiedHerbrand_subsingleton`), hence Herbrand quotient `1`.
- `relativeIdeleSupported_herbrandQuotient_eq_localDegreeProduct`: under the same hypothesis, `h(I_L^S)` is the product of the local degrees at the infinite places of `K` and the places of `S`.
- `sUnitToRelativeIdeleSupported`, `relativeIdeleSupportedToClass`: the two maps of the sequence, with equivariance, injectivity, exactness in the middle (`sUnit_supportedIdele_ideleClass_exact`) and surjectivity onto `C_L` (`relativeIdeleSupportedToClass_surjective`, assuming `hSP`: `I_L^S` and the principal ideles generate all relative ideles).
- `logPlaceOrbitEquivRelativeUnrestrictedSPlaceIndex`: Galois orbits of the infinite places of `L` and of the finite places above `S` correspond to the infinite places of `K` and the places of `S`; local degrees match (`relativeUnrestrictedSPlaceLocalDegree_product_eq_logPlaceOrbitProduct`).
- `ideleClass_herbrandQuotient_eq_card_of_sUnit_supported_values`: if `h(I_L^S) = q ≠ 0` and `h(S-units) = q / |G|`, then `h(C_L) = |G|`.
- `ideleClass_herbrandQuotient_eq_card_of_supported_local_calculation`: `h(C_L) = |G|`, assuming only `hSP` and unramifiedness outside `S`.
- `card_le_ideleClassNorm_index`, `finrank_le_ideleClassNorm_index`: unconditionally, `|G| ≤ [C_K : N C_L]` and `[L : K] ≤ [C_K : N C_L]`, the index of the range of the idele class norm.

## Proof

The Herbrand quotient is multiplicative on the sequence. The `S`-unit value `q / |G|` is imported (`sUnit_herbrandQuotient_eq_localDegreeProduct_div_card`), and orbit matching shows its `q` equals the supported-idele product. The norm index equals `|Ĥ⁰(G, C_L)|`, which is at least `h(C_L)` when that is a natural number. The unconditional forms take `S = ideleClassHerbrandSupport`, for which `hSP` and unramifiedness outside `S` are proved (`chosenFinitePlaceIsUnramified_of_notMem_ideleClassHerbrandSupport`).

## Points to watch

- The intermediate theorems carry hypotheses standing for other results: `hSP`, `hUnram`, and in the `_of_sUnit_supported_values` forms the two Herbrand-quotient values `hSupported`, `hUnit` and the finiteness Props `AboveSUnitHerbrandQuotientDefined`, `RelativeIdeleSupportedHerbrandQuotientDefined`. Among the norm-index bounds, only `card_le_ideleClassNorm_index` and `finrank_le_ideleClassNorm_index` are free of them.
- Herbrand quotients take values in `ℚ` and are stated together with an existential witness that `Ĥ⁰`, `Ĥ⁻¹` are finite.
- Cyclicity is a chosen generator `σ`, not an `IsCyclic` instance; "unramified" refers to the chosen completed local extension.
- `K` and `L` live in `Type`.
