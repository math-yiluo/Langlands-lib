# Local norm conditions for local-power ideles

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.GlobalClassFieldTheory.ClassFieldAxiom.IdelePowerLocalUnitNormContainment`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let L/K be a Galois extension of number fields and `h(S,T)` the ideles that are local n-th powers at the infinite places and at S, and local units outside `S ∪ T` (`idelePowerLocalUnitSubgroup`). The file shows that for Kummer-type extensions these ideles satisfy the finite-place local norm conditions, and computes the principal ideles in `h(S',T)` for a specially chosen pair `S', T`.

## Local norm conditions

- `finitePlaceLocalNormCondition S` and `allFinitePlaceLocalNormCondition`: ideles whose component at each v ∈ S (at each finite v) lies in `chosenFinitePlaceLocalNormSubgroup v`, the norms from the chosen localization of L/K at v.
- When `Gal(L/K) ≅ (ℤ/n)^r` (given as `eG`): `h(S,T)` meets the norm condition at S (`idelePowerLocalUnitSubgroup_le_finitePlaceLocalNormCondition`); at `S ∪ T` if every place of T splits completely (`..._le_unionLocalNormCondition`); and at every finite place if moreover every place outside `S ∪ T` is unramified (`..._le_allFinitePlaceLocalNormCondition`).
- `relativeIdeleNorm_range_le_allFinitePlaceLocalNormCondition`: norms of ideles of L satisfy all finite-place conditions.

## The principal part of h(S',T)

`principalIdelePowerLocalUnitSubgroup_eq_sUnitNthPowersInField_sUnitKummerPrimeSet`: let K contain a primitive n-th root of unity with `n = p^v` (p prime, v > 0), and let E be a finite abelian Galois extension of K inside a separable closure Ω with `Gal(E/K) ≅ (ℤ/n)^r`. Starting from a finite set S, put `S₀ = S ∪` (a sufficiently large set) `∪ chosenUnitFiniteSupport` (a chosen finite set outside which n is a unit), let `S'` be the Kummer-radical enlargement of `S₀` (`enlargeByFiniteKummerRadicalSupport`) and `T = sUnitKummerPrimeSet … S₀`. Then a unit of K has its principal idele in `h(S',T)` exactly when it is the n-th power of an `(S' ∪ T)`-unit.

## Proof

One inclusion is imported. For the other, such a unit b is an `(S' ∪ T)`-unit; let M be the cyclic Kummer extension generated by an n-th root of b. Places of `S'` split completely in M, and places outside `S' ∪ T` are unramified. Since ideles supported on `S'` and principal ideles generate `I_K`, and the map from `S'`-units to the product of `O_vˣ/(O_vˣ)ⁿ` over v ∈ T is surjective (imported), every idele class of K is a norm from M. The imported inequality `[M : K] ≤ [C_K : N C_M]` for cyclic extensions then forces `M = K`, so b is an n-th power.

## Points to watch

- The main theorem needs K, Ω in `Type` and is stated only for the particular sets `S'` and `T` built by the library from S and E, not for arbitrary sets.
- The local norm groups are the library's norms from a chosen place above v; splitting and unramifiedness are likewise the library's predicates on that chosen place.
