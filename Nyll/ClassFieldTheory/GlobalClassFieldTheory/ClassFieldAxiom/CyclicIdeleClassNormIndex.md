# Norm index of idèle classes in cyclic extensions

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.GlobalClassFieldTheory.ClassFieldAxiom.CyclicIdeleClassNormIndex`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

The file proves what its title calls the global class field axiom: for a cyclic extension `L/K` of number fields, the norm subgroup `N C_L` has index `[L : K]` in the idèle class group `C_K`. It derives a degree bound for abelian extensions, the low-degree Tate groups of `C_L`, and Hilbert 90 for `Lˣ` and for the principal idèles.

## Main results

- `ideleClassNorm_index_eq_finrank_cyclic`: for `L/K` Galois with cyclic Galois group, in any universes, the range of the library's `ideleClassNorm K L` has index `[L : K]` in `IdeleClassGroup K`. `relativeIdeleClassNorm_index_eq_finrank_cyclic` is the same for the relative-idèle presentation; `ideleClassNorm_index_le_prime_of_finrank_eq` gives `≤ p` in prime degree `p`.
- `ideleClassNormQuotient_finite_abelian` and `ideleClassNormQuotient_card_le_finrank_abelian`: for finite abelian `L/K`, `C_K / N C_L` is finite of order at most `[L : K]`.
- `ideleClass_tate_lowDegree_finite_card_eq_finrank_cyclic`: for a generator `σ` of `Gal(L/K)`, the Tate groups `Ĥ⁰` and `Ĥ⁻¹` of the relative idèle class group (Mathlib's `tateCohomology`) are finite of orders `[L : K]` and `1`. `ideleClass_tateHMinusOne_subsingleton_cyclic` is the form its docstring ties to the Hasse norm principle.
- `fieldUnitsHerbrandHMinusOne_subsingleton`, `principalIdelesHerbrandHMinusOne_subsingleton` and their cardinal forms: Hilbert 90, `H⁻¹(G, Lˣ) = 1` for `G` generated by a given `σ`, and the same for the principal relative idèles.
- Steps: `Ĥ⁰` is killed by `|G|` (`herbrandH0_pow_card_eq_one`); for a pushout `N = M ⊗_K L` with `M/K` and `L/K` Galois and `[M : K]` prime to `[L : K]`, the map `C_K / N C_L → C_M / N C_N` is injective (`actualPushoutNormQuotientMap_injective_of_coprime`); in a tower `K ⊆ M ⊆ L` with `M/K` Galois and finite right-hand quotients, `#(C_K / N C_L) ≤ #(C_M / N C_L) · #(C_K / N C_M)`.

## Proofs

The lower bound `[L : K] ≤ index` is imported (`finrank_le_ideleClassNorm_index`). The upper bound is by strong induction on the degree through an intermediate field of prime degree `p` over `K`. At the bottom, adjoining `p`-th roots of unity, of degree prime to `p`, makes the extension Kummer with norm index `p` by an imported calculation, and the injective pushout map transfers the bound. Arbitrary universes are reached from models in `Type` through `Shrink`. An imported Herbrand quotient `h(C_L) = [L : K]` and `#Ĥ⁰ = [L : K]` force `#Ĥ⁻¹ = 1`.

## Points to watch

- The low-degree cardinality and vanishing statements, Hilbert 90 included, are for fields in `Type` only.
- For abelian extensions only the upper bound `≤ [L : K]` is proved.
- The ordinary and the relative-idèle norm subgroups are identified by an imported lemma.
- Cyclicity is the instance `IsCyclic (L ≃ₐ[K] L)` in the two index equalities, but an explicit generator `σ` in the cohomological ones.
