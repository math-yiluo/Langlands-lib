# Coordinate places above the chosen base primes

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.GlobalClassFieldTheory.ClassFieldAxiom.SUnitKummerPrimeSelection.CoordinatePlaces`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

The setting is that of the S-unit Kummer prime selection: K a number field with a primitive n-th root of unity, `n = p^v` with p prime and v > 0, E/K finite abelian inside a separable closure Ω with `Gal(E/K) ≅ (ℤ/n)^r`, S a finite set of finite places with enlargement `S'`, N the extension generated by the n-th roots of the `S'`-units, and for each index i the cyclic subextension `N/N_i` of degree n. The file lifts the i-th chosen base prime `p_i` of K to a place of `N_i` and records that `p_i` is unramified in N.

## Definitions and results

- `exists_sUnitKummerCoordinatePlace`: there is a finite place of `N_i` above `p_i` whose decomposition group in `Gal(N/N_i)` is the whole group; this restates the candidacy of `p_i`.
- `sUnitKummerCoordinatePlace`: such a place, chosen by `Classical.choose`, with `sUnitKummerCoordinatePlace_below` (it lies above `p_i`) and `sUnitKummerCoordinatePlace_decompositionGroup` (its decomposition group is everything).
- `sUnitKummerChosenBasePlace_not_mem_ramified`: `p_i` is not among the places of K ramified in N, because the selection avoided them.
- `sUnitKummerChosenBasePlace_isUnramified`: the library's predicate `ChosenFinitePlaceIsUnramified` holds for N/K at `p_i`.

## Points to watch

- The comments call the coordinate place "completely decomposed", but the stated property is that its decomposition group is the whole of `Gal(N/N_i)`, so it does not split in N; this is not the usual "splits completely".
- The coordinate place is a noncanonical choice; K and Ω must lie in `Type`.
