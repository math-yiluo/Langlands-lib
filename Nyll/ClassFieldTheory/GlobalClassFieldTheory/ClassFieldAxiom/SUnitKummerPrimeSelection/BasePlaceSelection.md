# Choosing distinct base places for the S-unit Kummer prime set

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.GlobalClassFieldTheory.ClassFieldAxiom.SUnitKummerPrimeSelection.BasePlaceSelection`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

The setting is that of the Kummer-theoretic norm-index argument: a number field K with a primitive n-th root of unity, `n = p^v` a prime power, a finite abelian extension E/K inside a separable closure Ω with `Gal(E/K) ≅ (ℤ/n)^r`, and a finite set S of finite places. From the imported construction come the enlarged set `S'`, the extension N of K generated by the n-th roots of the `S'`-units, a number q (`sUnitKummerPrimeCount`), and for each i < q a subfield `N_i` with `N/N_i` cyclic of degree n. The file picks q pairwise distinct places of K, the i-th one lying below a suitable place of `N_i`.

## Definitions and results

- `sUnitKummerCoordinateBasePlaceCandidates … i`: the finite places of K lying below a place of `N_i` whose decomposition group in `Gal(N/N_i)` is the whole group.
- `sUnitKummerCoordinateBasePlaceCandidates_infinite`: each candidate set is infinite.
- `sUnitKummerAvoidedBasePlaces`: the finite set formed by `S'`, the places of K ramified in N, and `chosenUnitFiniteSupport` of n (a chosen finite set outside which n is a unit); it contains `S'`.
- `exists_sUnitKummerChosenBasePlaces`: there is an injective family `f : Fin q → places of K` with `f i` a candidate for i and outside the avoided set.
- `sUnitKummerChosenBasePlaces`: a family chosen by `Classical.choose`, with lemmas for injectivity, candidacy, avoidance, and `v(n) = 1` at each chosen place.

## Proof

Infinitude comes from the imported theorem that a cyclic extension of prime-power degree has infinitely many places whose decomposition group is the whole group, together with the finiteness of the places of `N_i` above a given place of K. Removing the finite avoided set leaves infinite sets; choosing q elements in each, the Hall condition holds trivially, and Mathlib's Hall marriage theorem gives an injective choice.

## Points to watch

- The comments call these places "completely decomposed" or "full-decomposition" places, but the condition is that the decomposition group is the whole Galois group, so the place of `N_i` has a single place above it in N. That is not the usual meaning of "splits completely".
- The family is a noncanonical choice; K and Ω must lie in `Type`.
