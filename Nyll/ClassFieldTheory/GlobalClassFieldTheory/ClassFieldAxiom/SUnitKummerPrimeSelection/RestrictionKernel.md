# Coordinate generators of the S-unit Kummer restriction kernel

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.GlobalClassFieldTheory.ClassFieldAxiom.SUnitKummerPrimeSelection.RestrictionKernel`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

Let `K` be a number field containing a primitive `n`-th root of unity, with `n = p^v` a prime power (`v > 0`), let `E/K` be a finite abelian Galois extension inside a separable closure `Ω`, given with an isomorphism `eG : Gal(E/K) ≃* (Fin r → ℤ/n)`, and let `S` be a finite set of finite places. Enlarging `S` by the support of the finite Kummer radical of `E` gives `S'`; let `N` be the full `S'`-unit Kummer extension of exponent `n`. The file turns a chosen isomorphism of the restriction kernel `Gal(N/E)` with `(ℤ/n)^(s − r)` into explicit generators and fixed fields.

- `sUnitKummerPrimeCount`: the number `s − r`, where `s = totalPlaceCard S'`.
- `sUnitKummerKernelGenerator i`: the preimage of the `i`-th standard basis vector under the chosen kernel isomorphism.
- `iSup_zpowers_sUnitKummerKernelGenerator_eq_top`: the cyclic subgroups generated by these elements together give the whole kernel.
- `orderOf_sUnitKummerKernelGenerator`: each generator has order exactly `n`.
- `sUnitKummerCoordinateFixedField i`: the fixed field in `N` of the cyclic group generated by the `i`-th generator; `sUnitKummerCoordinateFixedField_finrank` shows `N` has degree `n` over it.

## Points to watch

- `s − r` is truncated subtraction in `ℕ`.
- The generators and the fixed fields depend on the kernel isomorphism `chosenEnlargedSUnitKummerRestrictionKernelEquivPiZMod`, imported from elsewhere; the generators are not canonical.
