# Decomposition groups at the selected Kummer primes

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.GlobalClassFieldTheory.ClassFieldAxiom.SUnitKummerPrimeSelection.DecompositionFields`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

The setting is that of the S-unit Kummer prime selection: K a number field with a primitive n-th root of unity, `n = p^v` with p prime and v > 0, E/K finite abelian inside a separable closure Ω with `Gal(E/K) ≅ (ℤ/n)^r`, S a finite set of finite places with enlargement `S'`, N the extension generated by the n-th roots of the `S'`-units, `σ_i` the imported generators of the kernel of restriction `Gal(N/K) → Gal(E/K)`, and `p_i` the chosen base primes. The file computes the decomposition group of N/K at `p_i` and draws the splitting consequence for E.

## Main results

- `sUnitKummerKernelGenerator_mem_decompositionGroup`: `σ_i` lies in the decomposition group of N/K at `p_i`, because the coordinate place of `N_i` above `p_i` has full decomposition group in `Gal(N/N_i) = ⟨σ_i⟩`.
- `sUnitKummerChosenDecompositionGroup_eq_zpowers`: that decomposition group is exactly `⟨σ_i⟩`.
- `finitePlaceSplitsCompletely_of_mem_sUnitKummerPrimeSet`: every prime of the selected set `T = sUnitKummerPrimeSet … S` splits completely in E, since its decomposition group in N lies in the kernel of restriction to E.
- `sUnitKummerChosenDecompositionField_eq_coordinateFixedField`: the decomposition field of N/K at `p_i` is the coordinate fixed field `N_i`.

## Proof

Since `p_i` is unramified in N, its decomposition group D is cyclic. It contains `σ_i`, of order n, and `Gal(N/K)` has exponent n, so a cyclic D has at most n elements; hence `D = ⟨σ_i⟩`.

## Points to watch

- Decomposition groups are taken at the library's chosen place above `p_i`, and "splits completely" is the library's predicate `FinitePlaceSplitsCompletely`. K and Ω must lie in `Type`.
