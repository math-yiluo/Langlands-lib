# Supported principal ideles and the index of C_K(S,T)

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.GlobalClassFieldTheory.ClassFieldAxiom.IdeleClassPowerLocalUnitQuotient.SupportedPrincipalQuotient`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

For a number field K, finite sets S, T of finite places and `n ∈ ℕ+`, the file relates three quotients through the exact sequence

`1 → (I^{S∪T} ∩ Kˣ)/(h(S,T) ∩ Kˣ) → I^{S∪T}/h(S,T) → I^{S∪T}Kˣ/h(S,T)Kˣ → 1`,

where `I^{S∪T}` is the group of ideles supported on `S ∪ T` (`IdeleGroup.supportedAt`) and `h(S,T)` the subgroup of local powers and local units. The left term is rewritten through `(S ∪ T)`-units, and the right term is identified with `C_K / C_K(S,T)` when `I^{S∪T}` and Kˣ generate the idele group.

## Main definitions and results

- `supportedPrincipalIdeleSubgroup S T` and `sUnitEquivSupportedPrincipalIdeleSubgroup`: the principal ideles in `I^{S∪T}` are isomorphic to the `(S ∪ T)`-units via the diagonal map.
- `sUnitPrincipalIdelePowerSubgroup n S T`: the `(S ∪ T)`-units whose principal idele lies in `h(S,T)`; `sUnitPrincipalIdelePowerQuotientEquivSupportedPrincipalQuotient` and `card_supportedPrincipalQuotient_eq_sUnitPrincipalQuotient` identify the left term with the quotient of the `(S ∪ T)`-units by this subgroup.
- `supportedIdeleClassPowerDenominator`, `supportedIdeleToClassPowerQuotient` and its kernel: the map `I^{S∪T} → C_K / C_K(S,T)` has kernel generated by `h(S,T)` and the supported principal ideles; it is surjective under `hLarge` (`I^{S∪T} · Kˣ = I_K`), giving `supportedIdeleClassPowerQuotientEquiv` and `card_ideleClassPowerLocalUnitQuotient_eq_supported`.
- `card_supportedPrincipalQuotient_mul_card_supportedClassQuotient`: the product formula for the orders in the sequence (no largeness hypothesis).
- `card_supportedPrincipalQuotient_mul_card_ideleClassQuotient`, `card_sUnitPrincipalQuotient_mul_card_ideleClassQuotient`: under `hLarge`, `#(U_{S∪T} / (U_{S∪T} ∩ h(S,T))) · #(C_K / C_K(S,T)) = #(I^{S∪T} / h(S,T))`.

## Proof

The cardinal identity is the index relation `[P : H ∩ P] · [U : H ⊔ P] = [U : H]` for subgroups H, P of U.

## Points to watch

- The identities are between `Nat.card`s, which are 0 for infinite groups; finiteness is neither assumed nor proved here.
- `SUnitGroup` and the support condition refer to finite places only.
