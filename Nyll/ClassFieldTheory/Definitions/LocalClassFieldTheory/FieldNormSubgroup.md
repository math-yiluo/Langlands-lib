# The subgroup of field norms

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.Definitions.LocalClassFieldTheory.FieldNormSubgroup`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

The norm subgroup of a finite field extension.

- `fieldNormSubgroup K L`: for a finite-dimensional field extension `L/K`, the subgroup of `Kˣ` formed by the norms from `Lˣ`, defined as the range of `fieldNormHom K L`.
