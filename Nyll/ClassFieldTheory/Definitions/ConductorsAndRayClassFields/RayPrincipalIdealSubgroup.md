# Ray-principal ideals

Module `Nyll.ClassFieldTheory.Definitions.ConductorsAndRayClassFields.RayPrincipalIdealSubgroup`, copied unchanged from the Lean library NYLL. The origin of the file is given at the end of this page.

The group `P^m` of principal ideals generated by elements `x ≡ 1 mod* m`, for a ray modulus `m` of a number field `K`.

- `rayPrincipalIdealSubgroup m`: the subgroup of nonzero fractional ideals of `K` generated by the principal ideals `(x)` (Mathlib's `toPrincipalIdeal`) of elements `x ∈ Kˣ` satisfying `IsRayCongruent m x`.
- `rayPrincipalIdealSubgroupInPrimeTo m`: its preimage in the group `rayClassPrimeToIdeals m` of ideals prime to `m`, that is, its intersection with that group.

## Points to watch

The subgroup is defined as a subgroup closure, not as the image of a subgroup of `Kˣ`; the file proves no description of its elements, and does not show that ray-principal ideals are prime to `m`.
