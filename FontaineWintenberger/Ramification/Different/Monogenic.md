# The different of a monogenic extension

Module `FontaineWintenberger.Ramification.Different.Monogenic`, copied from the Lean library FontaineWintenberger. The origin of the file and the changes made in the copy are given at the end of this page.

When the ring `B` is generated over `A` by a single element `θ`, the different of `B/A` is generated by `f'(θ)` for `f` the minimal polynomial of `θ`. The file proves this, rewrites `f'(θ)` as a product over the nonidentity elements of a Galois group, and deduces that the exponent of the different is a sum of ramification orders.

## Results

- `adjoin_algebraMap_eq_top_of_integral_adjoin_eq_top`: if `Algebra.adjoin A {θ} = ⊤` in `B`, and `L` is the fraction field of `B`, finite over a field `K` containing the image of `A`, then the image of `θ` generates `L` as a `K`-algebra.
- `differentIdeal_eq_span_derivative_of_adjoin_eq_top`: for `A` an integrally closed domain with fraction field `K`, `B` a Dedekind domain that is the integral closure of `A` in a finite separable extension `L/K`, and `θ` with `Algebra.adjoin A {θ} = ⊤`, one has `differentIdeal A B = Ideal.span {aeval θ (minpoly A θ).derivative}`. It follows from Mathlib's identity relating conductor and different (`conductor_mul_differentIdeal`), the conductor being the unit ideal.
- `extensionValuationSubring_different_uniformizer`: for `K` a complete discretely valued field in the library's sense, `L/K` finite separable, and `w` a valuation on `L` extending that of `K`, with valuation ring `OL`: if the residue degree is `1`, then for every uniformizer `π` of `OL` the different of `OL` over `𝒪[K]` is generated by the derivative of the minimal polynomial of `π` at `π`.
- `maximalIdeal_emultiplicity_span`: in a discrete valuation ring `B`, the multiplicity of the maximal ideal in `Ideal.span {x}` equals `addVal B x`, also for `x = 0`.
- `aeval_derivative_minpoly_eq_prod_displacements`: if a finite group `G` acts on a domain `B` with `IsGaloisGroup G A B` and `θ` generates `B` over `A`, then `f'(θ) = ∏ (θ − g • θ)` over `g ≠ 1`.
- `differentIdeal_eq_span_prod_displacements`: with `B` moreover a discrete valuation ring, the different is generated by that product.
- `differentIdeal_order_eq_sum_ramificationOrder`: the multiplicity of the maximal ideal of `B` in `differentIdeal A B` equals the sum over `g ≠ 1` of `ramificationOrder (R := B) G g`.

The last step uses `ramificationOrder_eq_generator`, from an earlier file: the ramification order of `g` is the valuation of `g • θ − θ`.

## What to watch for

- Every result on the different except `extensionValuationSubring_different_uniformizer` assumes a generator `θ` with `Algebra.adjoin A {θ} = ⊤`; existence of such a generator is not proved here. That the fraction field is then generated by `θ` is proved, not assumed.
- `extensionValuationSubring_different_uniformizer` covers only residue degree `1`.
- `ramificationOrder` is the library's function with values in `ℕ∞`; the sum is over the whole group `G`.
