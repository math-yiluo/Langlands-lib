# Discriminant bounds for upper ramification of splitting fields

Module `FontaineWintenberger.Ramification.Infinite.SplittingField`, copied from the Lean library FontaineWintenberger. The origin of the file and the changes made in the copy are given at the end of this page.

Let `K` be a complete discretely valued field in the library's sense and `f` a monic polynomial over `𝒪[K]` whose image in `K[X]` is separable. The upper ramification groups of the splitting field of `f` are trivial beyond `(deg f)!` times the valuation of the discriminant of `f`, and the absolute upper ramification group beyond that bound fixes every root of `f`.

## Results

General bounds:

- `finrank_splittingField_le_factorial`, `finrank_le_factorial_of_isSplittingField`: a splitting field of a separable polynomial `f` over a field has degree at most `f.natDegree.factorial`. The Galois group acts faithfully on the roots.
- `herbrandFunction_le_self`: for a finite group acting on a discrete valuation ring, `herbrandFunction G t ≤ t` for all real `t`. `le_inverseHerbrandFunction`: `u ≤ inverseHerbrandFunction G u` for `u ≥ −1`.
- `spectral_ramificationIdx_le_finrank`: for `P/K` finite separable, the ramification index of `spectralIntegerRing K P` over `𝒪[K]` is at most `[P : K]`.
- `addVal_discr_ne_top`: the discriminant of `f` has finite valuation.

Main statements. Write `d` for the natural number `(addVal 𝒪[K] f.discr).toNat`.

- `upperRamificationGroup_fixes_integral_polynomial_root`: let `P/K` be finite separable with ramification index `e`. If `e · d ≤ b` and `g` lies in the upper ramification group of `P ≃ₐ[K] P` at `b`, then `g` fixes every root of `f` in `P`.
- `upperRamificationGroup_splittingField_eq_bot`: if `P` is a splitting field of `f` over `K` and `(deg f)! · d ≤ b`, then the upper ramification group of `P/K` at `b` is trivial.
- `absoluteUpperRamificationGroup_fixes_integral_polynomial_root`: if `(deg f)! · d ≤ b`, every element of `absoluteUpperRamificationGroup K b` fixes every root of `f` in `SeparableClosure K`.

## Proofs

If `g` moved a root `a`, then `(g a − a)²` would divide the discriminant of `f` in the integers of `P` (`Polynomial.sq_sub_dvd_discr`), so the valuation of `g a − a` is at most `e · d`. On the other hand `g` lies in the lower group at `⌈ψ(b)⌉`, and `ψ(b) ≥ b`, which forces the valuation of `g a − a` to exceed `b`. For the splitting field, `e ≤ [P : K] ≤ (deg f)!`, and an automorphism fixing all roots is the identity. The absolute statement restricts to the subfield generated by the roots.

## What to watch for

- The bound uses only `ψ(b) ≥ b` and `e ≤ (deg f)!`; it does not locate the last break.
- No assumption on the residue field is made in this file.
- In `upperRamificationGroup_splittingField_eq_bot`, finiteness and the Galois property of `P/K` are supplied inside the statement by `letI`.
