# The absolute Galois group and the separable closure

Module `FontaineWintenberger.Mathlib.FieldTheory.AbsoluteGaloisGroup`, copied from the Lean library FontaineWintenberger. The origin of the file and the changes made in the copy are given at the end of this page.

For any field `K`, Mathlib's `Field.absoluteGaloisGroup K` is the group of `K`-automorphisms of the algebraic closure `AlgebraicClosure K`, with the Krull topology. The file identifies it, as a topological group, with the Galois group of `SeparableClosure K` over `K`, and deduces that it is compact, also when `K` is imperfect.

## Main definitions and results

- `Field.absoluteGaloisGroup.toAlgEquiv`: an element of the group seen as an automorphism of the algebraic closure.
- `restrictSep : absoluteGaloisGroup K →* Gal(SeparableClosure K/K)`: restriction of automorphisms to the separable closure; `restrictSep_apply` says it commutes with the inclusion.
- `restrictSep_surjective`, `restrictSep_injective`, `continuous_restrictSep`, `isOpenMap_restrictSep`: restriction is a bijective, continuous, open homomorphism.
- `exists_fixingSubgroup_le_map`: for every finite subextension `E` of the algebraic closure there is a finite subextension `E′` of the separable closure such that every `τ` fixing `E′` is the restriction of some `σ` fixing `E`.
- `sepEquiv : absoluteGaloisGroup K ≃ₜ* Gal(SeparableClosure K/K)`: the resulting isomorphism of topological groups, with `sepEquiv_apply`.
- An instance: `absoluteGaloisGroup K` is a compact space.

## Proofs

Surjectivity and continuity are Mathlib's results on restriction to a normal subextension. Injectivity holds because the algebraic closure is purely inseparable over the separable closure, so a ring homomorphism out of it is determined by its restriction. For openness, let `q` be the exponential characteristic of `K`. Each generator `y` of a finite subextension `E` has a power `y^(q^n)` in the separable closure; `E′` is generated by these powers. An automorphism fixing `y^(q^n)` fixes `y`, because the iterated Frobenius is injective. This gives `exists_fixingSubgroup_le_map`, which says that the image of a basic neighbourhood of `1` contains a basic neighbourhood of `1`. Compactness is transported along `sepEquiv` from the Galois group of the separable closure, which is a Galois extension of `K`.

## Points to watch

- The declarations live in Mathlib's namespace `Field.absoluteGaloisGroup`, not in the library's own namespace.
- `SeparableClosure K` is Mathlib's separable closure of `K` inside `AlgebraicClosure K`.
- No hypothesis is made on `K`; the point of the file is the imperfect case, where the algebraic closure is not Galois over `K`.
