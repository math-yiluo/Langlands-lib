# APF indices of totally ramified finite extensions

Module `FontaineWintenberger.APF.Tower.TotallyRamified`, copied from the Lean library FontaineWintenberger. The origin of the file and the changes made in the copy are given at the end of this page.

Let `K` be a complete discretely valued field with perfect residue field. The file contains two index computations for finite extensions.

- `fixingSubgroup_sup_lowerRamificationGroup_zero_index_eq_residueDegree`: for a finite Galois extension `Ω/K` and an intermediate field `M`, the index in `Gal(Ω/K)` of the subgroup generated by `Gal(Ω/M)` and the lower ramification group of index `0` equals the residue degree of `M/K`, the degree over `𝓀[K]` of the residue field of `spectralIntegerRing K M`.
- `apfSubgroup_fieldRange_index_eq_apfIndex_of_ramificationIdx_eq_finrank`: for a finite separable `L/K` whose ramification index equals `[L : K]`, an embedding `j` of `L` into the separable closure and `u ≥ 0`, the index of `apfSubgroup K (j L) u` equals `apfIndex K L u`.

The second is proved inside the normal closure of `j(L)`: total ramification makes the residue degree `1`, so the index at parameter zero is `1`, and the full index factors as that index times the inertia-relative index.

Reference: Wintenberger, *Le corps des normes de certaines extensions infinies de corps locaux; applications*, §1.2.
