# The maximal unramified and tame parts of an APF extension

Module `FontaineWintenberger.APF.Tower.Tame`, copied from the Lean library FontaineWintenberger. The origin of the file and the changes made in the copy are given at the end of this page.

Let `K` satisfy `IsPerfectResidueLocalField K`, with residue characteristic `p = residueChar K`. For an APF extension `L/K`, the absolute inertia group and the absolute wild inertia group cut out two finite subfields of `L`: the maximal unramified part and the maximal tame part. The file defines them, proves their universal properties, and shows that beyond the tame part every finite step is totally ramified of `p`-power degree.

## Definitions

- `absoluteWildInertia K`: the topological closure of the subgroup of the absolute Galois group generated by the upper ramification groups `G_K^u`, `u > 0`. It is normal and closed.
- `apfTameSubgroup K L` and `apfTameStage K L`, for an intermediate field `L` of the separable closure: the subgroup `G_L ⊔ absoluteWildInertia K` and its fixed field.
- `apfMaximalUnramified K L`, `apfMaximalTame K L`, for an abstract separable APF extension: finite intermediate fields of `L` whose images under the embedding `IsSepClosed.lift` are `apfFiniteStage` at `0` and `apfTameStage`.
- `apfMaximalUnramifiedResidueEquiv`: the isomorphism from the residue field of `apfMaximalUnramified K L` to that of `integralClosure 𝒪[K] L`.

## Results

- `absoluteUpper_le_absoluteWildInertia`, `absoluteWildInertia_le_inertia`: `G_K^u ≤` wild inertia `≤ G_K^0` for `u > 0`.
- `apfTameSubgroup` is closed, and open when `L` is APF; so `apfTameStage K L` is finite over `K`, lies in `L`, and contains `apfFiniteStage K L 0`.
- `absoluteWildInertia_map`: wild inertia maps onto the lower ramification group of index `1` of every finite Galois subextension.
- `spectral_ramificationIdx_eq_one_iff_absoluteInertia_le`, `spectral_ramificationIdx_coprime_iff_absoluteWildInertia_le`: a finite subfield `M`, not necessarily normal, has ramification index `1` (resp. prime to `p`) iff `G_K^0` (resp. wild inertia) fixes it. Hence `le_apfFiniteStage_zero_iff_ramificationIdx_eq_one` and `le_apfTameStage_iff_ramificationIdx_coprime`.
- `finrank_eq_prime_pow_of_apfTameStage_le`: for `apfTameStage K L ≤ E ≤ F ≤ L` with `F` finite, `[F : E]` is a power of `p`.
- `le_apfMaximalUnramified_iff`, `isGreatest_apfMaximalUnramified`, `le_apfMaximalTame_iff`, `isGreatest_apfMaximalTame`: the two fields are the greatest finite intermediate fields with ramification index `1`, resp. prime to `p`.
- `residueDegree_eq_apfMaximalUnramified`, `spectralResidueInclusion_bijective_of_apfMaximalUnramified_le`: every finite `E` above the unramified part has its residue degree, and its residue field maps bijectively to that of `L`.
- `apfMaximalTame_wild_tail`: for finite `apfMaximalTame K L ≤ E ≤ F`, the residue map is surjective, `[F : E]` is a power of `p`, and the ramification index of `F/E` equals `[F : E]`.

## Proofs

Statements on non-normal fields are reduced to a finite Galois normal closure, where ramification indices are relative indices of lower ramification groups and the group of index `1` is a `p`-group.

## Points to watch

- `apfMaximalUnramified` and `apfMaximalTame` are obtained by choice with a fixed embedding; the `IsGreatest` statements determine them uniquely.
