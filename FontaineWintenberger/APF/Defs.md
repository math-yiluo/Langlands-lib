# Arithmetically profinite extensions: the definition

Module `FontaineWintenberger.APF.Defs`, copied from the Lean library FontaineWintenberger. The origin of the file and the changes made in the copy are given at the end of this page.

Let `K` be a complete discretely valued field (`NontriviallyNormedField K` with `IsCompleteDiscreteValuedField K`), `G_K` the Galois group of `SeparableClosure K` over `K` with the Krull topology, and `G_K^u` the absolute upper ramification groups `absoluteUpperRamificationGroup K u`, indexed by real `u`. The file defines when a separable extension `L/K` is arithmetically profinite (APF): `G_L · G_K^u` is open in `G_K` for every `u ≥ -1`.

## Definitions

- `apfSubgroup K L u`, for an intermediate field `L` of `SeparableClosure K`: the subgroup generated by the fixing subgroup of `L` and `G_K^u`. As `G_K^u` is normal, it is the set product (`coe_apfSubgroup`, `coe_apfSubgroup_upper_mul`).
- `IsAPFSubextension K L`: a class saying that `apfSubgroup K L u` is open for all `u ≥ -1`.
- `IsAPF K L`, for an abstract field extension `L/K` with `Algebra.IsSeparable K L`: the image of every `K`-embedding `L → SeparableClosure K` is an APF subextension.

## Results

- `apfSubgroup K L u` is compact and closed; it is open exactly when it has finite index (`isOpen_apfSubgroup_iff_finiteIndex`, `isAPFSubextension_iff_finiteIndex`). It is antitone in `u` on `[-1, ∞)` and equals `⊤` at `u = -1`.
- Conjugation by `σ ∈ G_K` carries `apfSubgroup K L u` to that of `σ(L)` and preserves its index (`apfSubgroup_map`, `apfSubgroup_map_index`, `isAPFSubextension_map_iff`).
- Embeddings into the separable closure exist (`nonempty_separableClosure_embeddings`) and any two are conjugate under `G_K` (`exists_aut_comp_separableClosure_embeddings`). Hence `IsAPF` can be tested on one embedding: `isAPF_iff_embedding`, `isAPF_iff_exists_embedding`, `isAPF_iff_subextension`, and the unfolded form `isAPF_iff_isOpen_apfSubgroup`.
- Finite separable extensions are APF: `isAPFSubextension_of_finite`, `isAPF_of_finite`.
- APF passes to subextensions: `IsAPFSubextension.of_le`, `IsAPF.of_algHom` (along any `K`-algebra map `M → L`), the instance `IsAPF.intermediateField`, and `isAPF_algEquiv`.

The proofs use compactness of the fixing subgroup and of `G_K^u`, normality of `G_K^u`, and the fact that a closed subgroup of finite index is open.

## What to watch for

- `IsAPF` is the library's predicate. It takes separability of `L/K` as an instance argument and makes no assumption of normality or finiteness; the quantifier over embeddings is shown to be harmless.
- Nothing in this file assumes a perfect residue field; the meaning of the condition depends on the library's definition of `absoluteUpperRamificationGroup`, which is made elsewhere.
- Instances pass between `IsAPF K E` and `IsAPFSubextension K E` for an intermediate field `E` of the separable closure.

## References

Wintenberger, *Le corps des normes de certaines extensions infinies de corps locaux; applications*, §1: 1.2.1 (definition), 1.2.2 (finite extensions), 1.2.3 (iii) (subextensions).
