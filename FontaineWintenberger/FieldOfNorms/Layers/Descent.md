# Finite descent in algebraic towers

Module `FontaineWintenberger.FieldOfNorms.Layers.Descent`, copied from the Lean library FontaineWintenberger. The origin of the file and the changes made in the copy are given at the end of this page.

Let `M/K` be an algebraic field extension and `N/M` a finite separable extension. The file shows that `N/M` descends to finite levels: there are a finite subextension `C` of `M/K` and a finite extension `D` of `C` inside `N`, linearly disjoint from `M` over `C`, with `M ⊗_C D ≅ N`, and such `D` can be taken to contain any given finite subextension of `N/K`. It also shows that the algebraic norm is compatible with such a tensor decomposition. These are the algebraic cofinality statements behind Wintenberger's paragraph 3.1.1; no valuation is involved.

## Main definitions

* `polynomialCoefficientField K q`: the finite subextension of `M/K` generated by the coefficients of a polynomial `q` over `M`.
* `adjoinTensorEquiv b hx hq`: for a tower `C ⊆ M ⊆ N`, a power basis `b` of `N/M` whose generator is integral over `C` and whose minimal polynomial over `M` has coefficients in `C`, the `M`-algebra isomorphism `M ⊗_C C(b.gen) ≃ N`, `m ⊗ d ↦ m · d` (`adjoinTensorEquiv_tmul`).

## Main results

* `exists_finiteIntermediateField_minpoly_lifts`, `exists_finiteIntermediateField_minpoly_descent`: the minimal polynomial over `M` of an element `x` integral over `M` descends to a monic polynomial of the same degree, vanishing at `x`, over a finite subextension `C` of `M/K`.
* `finrank_adjoin_eq_of_minpoly_lifts`, `linearDisjoint_adjoin_of_minpoly_lifts`: if `minpoly M x` has coefficients in `C` and `x` is integral over `C`, then `[C(x):C] = [M(x):M]` and `C(x)` is linearly disjoint from `M` over `C`.
* `adjoin_inf_fieldRange_eq_bot`: under the same hypotheses, `C(x) ∩ M = C` inside `N`.
* `exists_finiteIntermediateField_adjoin_linearDisjoint`, `..._containing`, `..._containing_layer`: existence of such a `C`, with `C(x)` moreover containing a prescribed finite set, or a prescribed finite subextension `D₀` of `N/K` (here `x` is the generator of `Field.powerBasisOfFiniteOfSeparable M N`).
* `exists_finiteIntermediateField_tensor_descent`: for `N/M` finite separable and `D₀` a finite subextension of `N/K`, there are `C` finite in `M/K` and `D` between `C` and `N`, finite over `K`, containing `D₀`, linearly disjoint from `M`, with `D ∩ M = C`, and an isomorphism `M ⊗_C D ≃ N` given by multiplication.
* `norm_tensor_includeRight`, `norm_eq_of_tensorEquiv`: for commutative rings, `B` finite free over `R` and an `A`-algebra isomorphism `e : A ⊗_R B ≃ S`, one has `Algebra.norm A (e (1 ⊗ b)) = algebraMap R A (Algebra.norm R b)`.
* `norm_adjoin_of_minpoly_lifts`: in the situation of `adjoinTensorEquiv`, `Algebra.norm M d = algebraMap C M (Algebra.norm C d)` for `d` in `C(b.gen)`.

## Proofs

Linear disjointness comes from the power basis of `C(x)`, which stays linearly independent over `M` because the degree of the minimal polynomial does not drop. The norm statement is base change of the determinant (`LinearMap.det_baseChange`).

## References
