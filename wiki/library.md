# Finding and reusing formalized results

Search the Library's Markdown first, and check the paired Lean files and version information before reusing a result. Use a result as a dependency only when it is in the Library.

Compilation alone does not show that a statement means what its source says: that needs a check against the original literature, the Lean code and the paired Markdown.

## Selected constructions and proofs

- [Finite Herbrand functions and upper ramification](../Langlands/LocalFactor/Ramification/Herbrand/Finite.md) — Actual real Herbrand functions phi and psi and finite upper ramification groups, with endpoint, normality and termination properties; arithmetic quotient and tower laws are not part of it.
- [Quasinet topology proof](../Langlands/Geometry/Analytic/AtlasTopology/Proof.md) — `Langlands.Analytic.AtlasTopology.quasinetTopology` proves the complete published `QuasinetTopologyStatement`; analytic representability and site constructions remain outside its scope.
