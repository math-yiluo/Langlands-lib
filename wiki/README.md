# Notes of the Library: index

This is the index of the notes in `notes/` and of the two other pages of
this directory, `wiki/`. Each entry gives the title of a note, a link and a
one-sentence description of its content. The notes record sources,
source-to-declaration maps, audits, routes of proofs, counterexamples and
the remaining proof obligations. A note is not a Lean result: the Library's
results are its Lean declarations.
