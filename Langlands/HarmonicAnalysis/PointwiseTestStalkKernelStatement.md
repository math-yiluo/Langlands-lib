## The statement

**`NondegenerateStalkKernelStatement`.** Let `X` be a t.d. space, `M` a nondegenerate
`𝒟(X)`-module, `x ∈ X` and `m ∈ M`. Then `m ∈ M(x)`, that is `f · m = 0` for some `f ∈ 𝒟(X)`
with `f(x) ≠ 0`, if and only if:

1. `χ_U · m = 0` for some compact open `U` containing `x`; and also if and only if
2. `m` lies in the additive subgroup of `M` generated by the elements `f · m'` with
   `f ∈ 𝒟(X)`, `m' ∈ M` and `f(x) = 0`.

## Formalization choices

- **The fibre.** Renard describes `ℳ_x` as `M` modulo `m ≡ m'`, defined by
  `χ_U · m = χ_U · m'` for some compact open neighbourhood `U` of `x`. Since `χ_U` acts additively,
  `m ≡ m'` iff `m − m'` satisfies condition 1. So condition 1 is "the class of `m` in `ℳ_x` is
  zero". The equivalence of `m ∈ M(x)` with condition 1 is the assertion `ℳ_x ≃ M/M(x)`,
  compatibly with the projections from `M`. It also shows that `M(x)` is an additive subgroup.
- **Condition 2.** This is the module-side form of the subspace `⟨f · φ, f(x) = 0⟩` of the
  published `StalkQuotientComparison`. It connects the module description with the sheaf
  description through the II.2.5 Theorem, `M ≅ ℳ_c(X)`. The additive subgroup generated equals the
  complex span, because `c · (f · m') = (c f) · m'` and `(c f)(x) = 0`.
- **No complex structure on `M`.** As in the published II.2.5 modules, only the action of `𝒟(X)`
  is used. Scalars enter through `𝒟(X)`.

## Why the statement is true

If `f(x) ≠ 0` and `f · m = 0`, take a compact open `U ∋ x` on which `f` is constant. Then
`(f(x)⁻¹ χ_U) f = χ_U`, so `χ_U · m = 0`. This is the source's argument with
`f = Σ α_j χ_{U_j}`. Conversely `χ_U` itself has `χ_U(x) = 1 ≠ 0`.

For condition 2: if `χ_U · m = 0`, choose a compact open `V` with `χ_V · m = m` (nondegeneracy).
Then `m = χ_V · m − χ_{V∩U} · m = χ_{V∖U} · m`, and `χ_{V∖U}(x) = 0`. In the other direction,
every generator `f · m'` with `f(x) = 0` is killed by `χ_U` for a compact open `U ∋ x` on which
`f` vanishes. The elements killed by some such `χ_U` form an additive subgroup, using
`χ_{U∩U'} = χ_U χ_{U'}`.
