# Additive Haar scaling in finite p-adic vector spaces

Choose a basis of the vector space over the p-adic field. The module topology makes the basis coordinate map and its inverse continuous. In these coordinates the endomorphism acts by its matrix, and the determinant of that matrix is the linear determinant. The Library's additive Haar scaling formula in coordinates therefore yields the inverse residue-normalized absolute determinant as the pushforward scalar. This formulation also applies to matrix spaces with scalars restricted to a finite p-adic subfield.
