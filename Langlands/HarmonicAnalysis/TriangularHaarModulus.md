# Haar modulus in triangular coordinates

Author: Worker 1179. Part of task 40747bb3 (finite volume of `G(F) \ G(𝔸_F)¹`), with
Worker 235.

This module computes the Haar modulus of a group automorphism in coordinates. The
coordinates must make left translations triangular, and the automorphism must act
separately on each coordinate. The results are general measure theory. They do not
mention algebraic groups or adeles.

The intended application is `PositiveTorusUnipotentModulusStatement`
(`Langlands/Global/AdelicSiegelMeasureReduction.lean`). There, `H` is the adelic points of
the standard unipotent radical, the coordinates are torus-weight coordinates, and `ψ` is
conjugation by a positive torus element. Each coordinate is then multiplied by an idele of
the form treated in `Langlands/Global/AdelicScalarHaarChar.md`. That application is not
part of this module.

## Conventions

For a locally compact topological group `G` with its Borel σ-algebra and a topological
automorphism `φ : G ≃ₜ* G`, Mathlib's `mulEquivHaarChar φ : ℝ≥0`
(`Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Measure/Haar/MulEquivHaarChar.lean`) is the factor by which `φ`
scales Haar measures. For every regular Haar measure `μ`, Mathlib's
`mulEquivHaarChar_smul_map` gives

`mulEquivHaarChar φ • φ_* μ = μ`,

so `μ (φ S) = mulEquivHaarChar φ * μ S` for Borel sets `S`. The additive version is
`addEquivAddHaarChar`.

## Declarations

### Characterization of the modulus

`mulEquivHaarChar_eq_of_smul_map`: let `G` be a locally compact topological group with a
Borel σ-algebra, let `μ` be a regular Haar measure on `G`, let `φ : G ≃ₜ* G`, and let
`c : ℝ≥0`. If `c • φ_* μ = μ`, then `mulEquivHaarChar φ = c`. In other words, one regular
Haar measure is enough to read off the modulus.

The additive version is `addEquivAddHaarChar_eq_of_smul_map`, generated by `to_additive`.
It concerns `addEquivAddHaarChar` for additive groups.

Proof. Mathlib's `mulEquivHaarChar_eq` writes `mulEquivHaarChar φ` as
`haarScalarFactor μ (φ_* μ)`. By `haarScalarFactor_smul`,
`haarScalarFactor (c • φ_* μ) (φ_* μ) = c • haarScalarFactor (φ_* μ) (φ_* μ) = c`.
Rewriting `c • φ_* μ = μ` turns the left side into `haarScalarFactor μ (φ_* μ)`.

### Triangular shears

`IsTriangularShear f`, for an additive commutative group `A`, `d : ℕ`, and a self-map `f`
of `Fin d → A`, says the following. For every `k` and all `v, w` that agree at every index
`l < k`,

`f v k - v k = f w k - w k`.

So `f v k = v k + c_k(v)`, where the correction `c_k` depends only on the coordinates
before `k`. Coordinate `0` is translated by a constant.

`measurePreserving_of_isTriangularShear`: let `A` have a σ-algebra with measurable
addition and subtraction (`MeasurableAdd₂`, `MeasurableSub₂`), and let `ν` be a σ-finite,
left-translation-invariant measure on `A`. Every measurable triangular shear of
`Fin d → A` preserves the product measure `Measure.pi (fun _ => ν)`.

Proof. This is a special case of the Library theorem `finiteTriangularTranslation`
(`Langlands/HarmonicAnalysis/TriangularTranslationProof.lean`), which proves
`FiniteTriangularTranslationStatement`. Take the index type `Fin d`, the rank `k ↦ k`, and
the translation term `v ↦ (k ↦ f v k - v k)`. This term is measurable because `f` and
subtraction are measurable. By the shear condition, its `k`-th entry depends only on the
coordinates of smaller rank. Since `(f v k - v k) + v k = f v k`, the translated map is `f`.

### Haar modulus of a coordinatewise automorphism

`mulEquivHaarChar_eq_prod_of_triangular` has the following hypotheses:

- `H` is a locally compact topological group with a Borel σ-algebra;
- `A` is a locally compact, second-countable topological additive group with a Borel
  σ-algebra, and `ν` is an additive Haar measure on `A`;
- `Φ : H ≃ₜ (Fin d → A)` is a homeomorphism such that, for every `g ∈ H`, the
  left-translation map `v ↦ Φ (g * Φ⁻¹ v)` is a triangular shear;
- `ψ : H ≃ₜ* H` is a topological group automorphism;
- for each `k`, `s k : A → A` is measurable and `r k : ℝ≥0` satisfies
  `r k • (s k)_* ν = ν`;
- `Φ (ψ h) k = s k (Φ h k)` for all `h` and `k`.

The conclusion is `mulEquivHaarChar ψ = ∏ k, r k`.

No algebraic condition is placed on the maps `s k`. The scaling identity `hr` is the only
requirement. When `d = 0`, the group is trivial and the product is `1`.

Proof.

1. Let `π = Measure.pi (fun _ => ν)` and `μ = Φ⁻¹_* π`. For `g ∈ H`, left translation by
   `g` satisfies `(g * ·) ∘ Φ⁻¹ = Φ⁻¹ ∘ S_g` with `S_g v = Φ (g * Φ⁻¹ v)`. The map `S_g` is
   continuous, hence measurable, and it is a triangular shear by hypothesis. By
   `measurePreserving_of_isTriangularShear` it preserves `π`, so `μ` is left invariant. The
   lemma applies because `A` is second countable: an additive Haar measure on `A` is then
   σ-finite, and addition and subtraction on `A` are measurable.
2. `μ` is finite on compact sets and positive on nonempty open sets, because `Φ⁻¹` is a
   homeomorphism. Hence `μ` is a Haar measure. `H` is second countable because `Φ` is an
   embedding into the second-countable space `Fin d → A`. Mathlib's
   `regular_of_isMulLeftInvariant`, applied to a compact set with nonempty interior, makes
   `μ` regular.
3. Since `ν ≠ 0`, every `r k` is nonzero, so `(s k)_* ν = (r k)⁻¹ • ν`. Mathlib's
   `Measure.pi_map_pi` and the Library's `pi_nnreal_smul`
   (`Langlands/HarmonicAnalysis/FiniteProductMeasureScaling.lean`) give
   `(v ↦ (k ↦ s k (v k)))_* π = (∏ k, r k)⁻¹ • π`.
4. The coordinate hypothesis gives `ψ ∘ Φ⁻¹ = Φ⁻¹ ∘ (v ↦ (k ↦ s k (v k)))`. Hence
   `(∏ k, r k) • ψ_* μ = μ`, and the characterization above gives the modulus.

## Provenance

This module is internal. The result is the coordinate form of a familiar fact: the modulus
of an automorphism of a unipotent group is the product of the absolute values of its
eigenvalues on the Lie algebra. The Lean route (a product measure in triangular
coordinates, measure preservation from the Library's triangular-translation theorem, and
coordinatewise scaling) is internal and follows no specific textbook proof.
