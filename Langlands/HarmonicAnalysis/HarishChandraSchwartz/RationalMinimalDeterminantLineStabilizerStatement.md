# The minimal adjoint determinant vector on the opposite radical

Let G be a connected reductive group over a field, S a maximal split torus,
and ν a generic cocharacter of S. Write U for the dynamic unipotent subgroup
of ν and U⁻ for the dynamic unipotent subgroup of its inverse. In any supplied
represented model of U and finite Lie coordinates, take the exterior vector
of the included basis of Lie U. The statement asks that every rational point
of U⁻ fixing that vector in the exterior power of the adjoint representation
be the identity. Equivalently, the orbit map of U⁻ on this vector is injective
on rational points.

This is a derived algebraic statement for the height estimate in Lemma II.3.4.
Waldspurger uses a representation whose first vector has the adjoint determinant
character and whose orbit map in the opposite radical is injective. The present
statement specifies the exterior adjoint representation in any characteristic
and quantifies over fields. The source treats a nonarchimedean local field and
uses the representation of II.2. The intended application is its minimal
parabolic case.
