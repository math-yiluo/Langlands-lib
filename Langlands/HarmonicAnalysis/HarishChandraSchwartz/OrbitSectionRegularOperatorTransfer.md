# A common lower section level for a regular compact operator

`HasRegularOrbitSectionTransfer` records a compact-picture formula for a family
of actual lower smooth sections indexed by sections of one upper level. For
each lower torus parameter, the lower compact vector is the value of the
specified regular operator on the original compact vector at its monomial
image in the upper torus.

`exists_regularOrbitSectionTransfer_finsetSup_bound` uses a compact-open
cofinality premise on the original inducing Levi, Laurent unitary coordinates,
a polynomial operator numerator and a denominator nonzero on the whole
unitary torus. The fixed original level has a finite-dimensional compact
fiber. Expanding on its basis yields a single finite output span, which is
fixed at one lower level. Faithfulness of compact restriction places every
lower section in that level. The inclusion of the level recovers each supplied
section. A lower coefficient functional evaluates the same finite formula
with smooth scalar multipliers. The monomial derivative bound controls each
ordered derivative by finitely many original seminorms, and finite unions
control any finite supremum of lower seminorms.

This is a derived step in the induction of Waldspurger VI.3.1. It estimates the
actual sections when the weighted compact formula has been obtained.
