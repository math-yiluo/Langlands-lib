# Power contraction of the minimal weight on the opposite radical

Let G be a connected reductive group over a nonarchimedean local field F and
let i be a maximal split torus with generic cocharacter ν. Let K be a compact
subgroup giving the minimal Iwasawa factorization, and let w be its actual
minimal Iwasawa weight. For a cocharacter ζ of that torus, write U⁻ for the
actual dynamic radical of its inverse. Given a unit t of normalized absolute
value less than one, set a = ζ(t) and θ = √|t|. The statement asks for a single
positive real C such that for every natural m and every x in U⁻,

```
w(a^(-m) x a^m)^(-1) ≤ C (1 + θ^m w(x)^(-1)).
```

This power estimate is a derived form of the contraction step in Waldspurger
II.4.1 (1). The source varies a in the weak chamber for a standard parabolic
and uses a bound in terms of its simple-root values. Here a is a fixed
cocharacter value, and ζ and the compact Iwasawa subgroup have the displayed
scope. Diagonalizing the action of ζ on the determinant representation gives
integral exponents. A coordinate changed by the opposite radical has strictly
smaller weight and contracts by at least |t| per step in vector norm. The
square-root factor θ corresponds to the half modulus in w.
