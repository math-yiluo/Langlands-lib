# Proof of the finite-product topology comparison for actual orbit sections

The theorem `orbitSectionFiniteProductTopologyStatement_holds` proves the
published aggregate `OrbitSectionFiniteProductTopologyStatement` (file
`OrbitSectionFiniteProductTopology.lean`). It covers all the generic
semidirect-parabolic data and the Levi compact-open cofinality hypothesis
`hcofinal`, and keeps the five independent universes `uG, uI, uV, uJ, uB` of
the statement. It assumes no topology comparison, no basis independence and no
`SmoothTorusDerivativePermutationStatement`.

The aggregate has three parts.

1. **Finiteness.** The witnesses `d H` come from the existing theorem
   `finiteDimensional_orbitSectionFixedCoefficients h K π hK hcofinal H.1 H.2`.
   The proof adds no new finiteness argument.
2. **Level comparison.** This is
   `orbitSectionLevelFiniteProductTopologyStatement_holds`. It holds for every
   open `H ≤ K` and every finite basis `b` of the fixed coefficients.
3. **Total comparison.** This is
   `orbitSectionTotalFiniteProductTopologyStatement_holds`, for the selected
   bases `orbitSectionFixedBasis H (d H)`.

## An abstract comparison of seminorm topologies

`topology_eq_induced_pi_of_seminorms` works on generic types, and every
topology in it is an explicit argument. This avoids clashing with the global
subtype topology already carried by `smoothFiniteTorusFunctions J`. The setup:

- `V` carries a topology `tV` with seminorm family `p` (`WithSeminorms`).
- `S` carries a topology `tS` with seminorm family `s`, where
  `s k x = ‖D k x‖` for linear maps `D k` into a normed space.
- `c : V →ₗ (B → S)` is a linear map with finitely many coordinates.

The lemma concludes `tV = induced c (product of tS)` from two conditions:

- (h₁) each coordinate seminorm `v ↦ s k (c v j)` is equal to some `p i`;
- (h₂) each `p i` has the form `v ↦ ‖∑ j, a j • D k (c v j)‖` for some `k`
  and some scalars `a`.

The proof has two directions:

- **`tV` is finer than the induced topology.** Each coordinate `v ↦ c v j` is
  continuous for `tV`, by `WithSeminorms.continuous_of_continuous_comp` and
  (h₁). So `c` is continuous into the product.
- **The induced topology is finer than `tV`.** Give `V` the induced topology.
  This makes `V` a topological additive group (`isTopologicalAddGroup_induced`).
  Each `D k` is continuous for `tS`, by the same criterion applied with the norm
  seminorm. So by (h₂) every `p i` is continuous for the induced topology.
  Applying `continuous_of_withSeminorms_explicit` to the identity map gives the
  inequality. That lemma is `WithSeminorms.continuous_of_continuous_comp` with
  both topologies explicit.

`withSeminorms_moduleFilterBasis_topology` states that a topology generated by
a seminorm family has that family. `smoothTorusFunctionTopology_withSeminorms`
is the special case for the independent scalar topology
`smoothTorusFunctionTopology J`, whose family `smoothTorusSeminorm J` uses every
ordered angle derivative list, including the empty one.

## Coefficients and coordinates

These lemmas work on the actual level `OrbitSectionLevel` with its published
coordinate map `orbitSectionLevelCoordinates` (built from
`orbitSectionLevelTensor`). Write `R q z` for the actual compact restriction of
the section `toSections q` at `z`.

- `orbitSectionLevelFixedRestriction_val` records that the coefficient of the
  fixed-valued restriction is `R q z`. This holds by `rfl`.
- `orbitSectionLevelCoordinates_eq_orbitSectionCoefficient`: let `ℓ` be a
  functional on the compact picture that extends `b.coord i`. Then the `i`-th
  coordinate of `q` equals the existing scalar coefficient
  `orbitSectionCoefficient ℓ (toSections q)`. The proof is pointwise in `z`. It
  combines the published `orbitSectionLevelCoordinates_apply`, the extension
  property, and the definitional value of the coefficient.
- `orbitSectionCoefficient_eq_sum_coordinates`: for any functional `ℓ`, the
  coefficient `orbitSectionCoefficient ℓ (toSections q)` equals
  `∑ i, ℓ (b i) • (coordinates of q) i`. The proof is pointwise. It expands the
  fixed-valued restriction in the basis (`Module.Basis.sum_repr`), applies `ℓ`,
  and identifies `b.repr x i = b.coord i x` with the `i`-th coordinate, again by
  `orbitSectionLevelCoordinates_apply`.

## Level comparison

Each coordinate functional `b.coord i` of the fixed subspace extends to a
functional `ℓ i` on the whole compact picture, by `LinearMap.exists_extend`
(over the field `ℂ`). The abstract lemma is then applied with:

- `V = OrbitSectionLevel ...` and its seminorm family
  `OrbitSectionLevel.seminormFamily` (`OrbitSectionLevel.withSeminorms`);
- `S = smoothFiniteTorusFunctions J` with `smoothTorusSeminorm J`;
- `D = smoothTorusDerivative J`, valued in `C(J → Circle, ℂ)`;
- `c = orbitSectionLevelCoordinates ... b`.

The two conditions are checked as follows.

- For (h₁), the seminorm `smoothTorusSeminorm J l` of the `j`-th coordinate is
  the level seminorm with index `(l, ℓ j)`. This follows from the
  coordinate/coefficient lemma, since both sides are the sup norm of
  `∂_l` applied to the same scalar function.
- For (h₂), the level seminorm with index `(l, ℓ₀)` is
  `‖smoothTorusDerivative J l (orbitSectionCoefficient ℓ₀ (toSections q))‖`
  (definitionally). The expansion lemma rewrites the coefficient as
  `∑ j, ℓ₀ (b j) • (coordinate j)`, and linearity of the derivative
  (`map_sum`, `map_smul`) puts it in the required form with `a j = ℓ₀ (b j)`.

So `OrbitSectionLevel.topologicalSpace` equals the topology induced by the
coordinates from the product of the scalar topologies. That product is
`smoothTorusProductTopology J B`, so the induced topology is
`orbitSectionFiniteProductLevelTopology`. The level keeps its actual subspace
type throughout. No parametrization by all coefficient-valued smooth functions
is used.

## Total comparison

`orbitSectionFiniteProductOriginalTop_eq` unfolds `OrbitSectionSpace.originalTop`
and `orbitSectionFiniteProductOriginalTop`. Both are suprema over
`H ≤ K` of the topologies coinduced by `OrbitSectionSpace.ofLevel`. They agree
term by term by the level comparison for the basis `orbitSectionFixedBasis H (d H)`.
`orbitSectionTotalFiniteProductTopologyStatement_holds` unfolds
`OrbitSectionSpace.topologicalSpace` and `orbitSectionFiniteProductTopology`.
Both are infima of the locally convex vector-space topologies coarser than the
respective final topology (`originalTop ≤ t`). They become identical after rewriting with the
previous equality.
