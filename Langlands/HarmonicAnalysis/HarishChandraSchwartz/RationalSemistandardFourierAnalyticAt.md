# Analytic inputs for semistandard Fourier evaluation at the source compact

`RationalSemistandard.FourierAnalyticAtStatement` asks for the analytic
input package of the pointwise Fourier comparison on every actual
semistandard parabolic. The compact is common to the Schwartz space,
the compact picture, and the three group Haar normalizations. The Levi
and radical each have mass one on their own compact trace. The Levi
height is the restriction of the ambient closed matrix height. The
conclusion supplies a Levi spherical weight for the input package.

The hypotheses specify the maximal split torus, generic chamber, special
compact properties, and a closed matrix realization. The representation
is irreducible admissible and square integrable modulo the displayed
central subgroup for the given quotient Haar on the Levi. The
quotient carries the Borel measurable structure of its quotient
topology. In the Plancherel application the central subgroup is the split
component. An inner
application uses the same statement with the outer Levi as ambient
group and its own compact trace.

The package in `RationalSemistandardFourierFamily` records the exact
input fields of the existing evaluation formula, including the
constant-term estimates, tempered bounds, and absolute integrability
of the double integral. At a chamber in which the parabolic is
standard, the intended weight is the Levi's spherical function at the
compact trace. The supporting estimates are those used in Waldspurger,
*La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques (d’après Harish-Chandra)*,
II.1.2, II.1.4, II.1.5, II.4.5 and III.1.2. I.1, printed p. 240, fixes
the Haar scale. VII.1.2, printed pp. 315–316, identifies the evaluated
Fourier tensor with the Fourier transform of the induced constant term.
