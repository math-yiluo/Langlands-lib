# Actual Weyl transport of intrinsic semistandard orbit representatives

A compact lift is a normalizer point in the given compact whose class lies
in the published actual relative-Weyl equality transporter. The condition
identifies the full conjugate Levi subgroup functor with the target Levi.
It gives literal inverse conjugation of their rational points and the
actual isomorphism of any two representing Levi models.

`conjugateCharacter` is restriction along the inverse of that isomorphism,
transported through the two inclusion homeomorphisms. Its evaluation is
pullback along the very same inverse conjugation used for the inducing
object. `conjugateRepresentative` uses the published pullback of irreducible
admissible objects, and membership follows from commutation of actual
classes with twisting. The target is the existing all-representative
unitary orbit of the conjugate base object.

For two lifts with the same left target-Weyl quotient class, the difference
`n' * n⁻¹` belongs to the literal target Levi. The proof uses the actual
quotient map from normalizer points and the published membership test for
`W^M'`. `changeLiftInducingIso` compares the conjugate objects by composing
the product pullback comparison with the canonical inner inducing
isomorphism. These constructions make the actual transports of Waldspurger
V.3 and VI.3 available on the intrinsic orbit representatives. Equality of
normalized standard tensors after these transports is a further target.
