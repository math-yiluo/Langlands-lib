# Parameter continuity on tails of actual constant-term rays

For each fixed Levi point and smooth section of the original unitary orbit,
`RationalOrbitConstantTermRayContinuityStatement` gives a natural-number
threshold past which the full constant term evaluated along the strict
component ray is continuous in the orbit variable. The premise is the
compact-open normal basis and its standard parabolic member clauses for
this parabolic. The result is derived from the uniform constant-term
asymptotics of Waldspurger I.4.3 and I.5.

The original orbit coefficients have a common compact open bi-invariance
level. Intersect it with its conjugate by the inverse of the given Levi
point to get a common level for the translated coefficients. Refine that
intersection to a member of the given basis. Each orbit coefficient is a
tensor coefficient of a literal unramified twist, by transport of the
coefficient map to that representative and the range characterization of
the tensor construction. The translated ray theorem applies at the
refined level with the dimension of the untwisted induced fixed space as a
common threshold. At each power past that threshold the full term is the
original continuous orbit coefficient divided by the nonzero standard
half-modulus of the evaluation point.
