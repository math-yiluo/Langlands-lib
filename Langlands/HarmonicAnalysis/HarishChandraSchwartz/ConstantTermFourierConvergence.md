# Absolute convergence of the Fourier pairing from constant-term estimates

`ConstantTermFourierDoubleIntegrabilityStatement` obtains the absolute
convergence of the actual double integral in VII.1.2 from the normalized
Iwasawa integration formula, the constant-term estimate, temperedness on
the Levi, and its square-weight integral.

Compactness and bi-invariance of a Schwartz function give a finite set of
double translates when the two translating elements lie in the compact
picture. Their seminorms admit a common finite bound. In Iwasawa coordinates
the normalized induction contraction is a Levi coefficient times the
positive square root of the parabolic modulus. The constant-term estimate
absorbs this modulus and the integral in the unipotent variable. The
Schwartz decay exponent can be chosen to cancel the tempered growth and
leave the integrable square weight on the Levi. The compact Haar probability
then integrates the uniform finite bound.

`rationalSemistandardFourierDoubleIntegrability` applies this argument to
the actual semistandard parabolic of a connected reductive group. Its
Iwasawa factorization comes from the supplied compact picture, and dynamic
coverage gives radical unimodularity. The normalized subgroup masses, the
factorization of the compact intersection, the constant-term estimate and
the two analytic Levi estimates remain explicit inputs.
