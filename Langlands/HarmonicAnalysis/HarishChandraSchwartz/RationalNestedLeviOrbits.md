# Actual inducing orbits for a nested semistandard parabolic

The `liftedOrbitClass` construction compares the actual unitary unramified
orbits of irreducible discrete series at the final Levi in the two stages of
the nested constant-term diagram. It applies the inverse final-Levi point
map to each representative. The pullback commutes with unitary unramified
twists because the character parameters use the two models' matching
algebraic norm characters. It also preserves square integrability for the
matched split-central Haar measures.

The representative comparison chooses, for each member of the inner
orbit, its actual inverse pullback in the single lifted ambient orbit.
The two consecutive inverse point transports cancel on the representation.
`finalLeviPointsEquiv_eq_leviEquiv` identifies the point map in this
construction with the one used in the Fourier evaluation.
The equality of formal degrees uses exact transport of the quotient Haar
and the Schur characterization in III.1(3). These supply the inducing
representation and formal-degree comparison of the nested Fourier
calculation of VIII.2.1.
