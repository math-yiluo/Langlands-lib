# Waldspurger source scalars on one semistandard index

`SourceConstants.gammaAtIndex` and `SourceConstants.cAtIndex` use `SemistandardSpectralDynamicCoverageStatement` to evaluate the published source constants at an actual semistandard triple. The compact open and its Haar normalization are fixed. The index-level definitions agree with their values in every cocharacter presentation of that triple.

For two presentations, the actual Levi detects the zero-root support and the actual parabolic detects the nonnegative-root support. Together these determine the positive-root support and the opposite radical. The two presentations therefore use the same rank-one cocharacter submodules, and the identity on the ambient points transports the source parabolic and opposite-radical intersections at each of those submodules. This proves equality of gamma, of the full rank-one gamma product, and of `c`.

This is a comparison of dynamic presentations for the scalar definitions in Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques (d’après Harish-Chandra)* (2003), I.1, printed pp. 240–241 (PDF pp. 6–7). The comparison where the parabolic changes at a common Levi is `SourceConstantParabolicIndependenceStatement`. [Original publication](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1474748003000082)
