# The proper Fourier kernel at source normalization

`SourceProperFourierKernelStatement` identifies the kernel of the actual
proper Fourier truncation with the intrinsic cuspidal Schwartz submodule.
The ambient group is connected reductive over a nonarchimedean local
field. It has the designated maximal split torus, generic cocharacter
and special vertex Iwasawa compact. The matrix representation is closed
and the ambient Haar is normalized at that compact. Every compact
picture uses the same compact and each split-central quotient Haar is
normalized at the image of the corresponding Levi trace. The quotient
measurable spaces are Borel. The square-integrable weight and tempered
inducing data are those of the Fourier family being compared.

The proposition takes Fourier injectivity on actual groups of strictly
smaller relative semisimple rank at the same source normalization. The
proper truncation can be viewed in the product of Fourier components.
This is the kernel comparison of Waldspurger VIII.2.1, printed pp.
321–322, using the evaluation of VII.1.2 and constant-term transitivity
of III.6.
