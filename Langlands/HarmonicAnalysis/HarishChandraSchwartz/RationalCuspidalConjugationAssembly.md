# Assembly of the actual intrinsic cuspidal conjugation bound

The theorem `rationalCuspidalConjugationBound_of_prerequisites` proves the
exact local conclusions of Waldspurger VIII.3.1 from the three global
inputs in `RationalCuspidalConjugationPrerequisites`. They are existence
of the joint special compact and basis data, the lattice Xi upper
estimate, and the standard constant-term estimate of II.4.5.

At positive relative semisimple rank the joint data give a compact with
the ambient and intrinsic Levi factorizations. The lattice estimate
gives weighted square integrability, hence convergence for all
conjugators. For each maximal simple-root sector, the corresponding
proper standard parabolic determines the dynamic split. Its actual
geometry gives the complement and cutoff inequalities at a common
compact open level of the two functions. Cuspidality gives convergence
and zero integral over the opposite radical. The standard and reversed
constant-term estimates use the same structural compact and equal
ambient and Levi Xi functions. Their radical Haar measures are transported
from the dynamic split. The intrinsic Levi Xi bound and the modulus
product identify the sector estimate with the ambient minimal
parabolic half-weight squared. Summing the finitely many sector
constants gives the full chamber estimate in the common norm kernel.

The separate rank-zero theorem supplies that case. The compact-change
equivalence transfers the convergence and the chamber estimate from the
joint witness to the arbitrary compact open Iwasawa subgroup of the
target, preserving the intrinsic kernel, matrix height, Haar measure
and pairing. The ambient structural subgroup quantified by the target
can be supplied independently of those choices.

This is a derived proof assembly for Waldspurger's 2003 Plancherel
article, VIII.3.1, printed pp. 323–326. Publication of the exact theorem
requires proofs of the three global inputs.
