# Fourier injectivity in relative semisimple rank zero

`RationalRankZeroFourierInjectivityStatement` restricts the actual local
zero-Fourier implication to connected reductive groups whose relative
root span has dimension zero. It retains every datum and premise of
`RationalLocalFourierInjectivityStatement`, including the weighted
square-integrability hypothesis for the supplied matrix height and the
ambient and split-central quotient Haar measures. The field and
representation-carrier universes are independent.

Waldspurger's proof of VIII.1.1 uses the zero-Fourier implication in
VIII.5 on printed p. 332. It detects a cuspidal Schwartz function using
its projections along the split center and the actions of discrete
series on the inverted function. In rank zero there are no proper
semistandard parabolics and the split-central quotient is compact.
This specialization states the injectivity assertion used at the
initial rank of the induction in VIII.1. The formulation concerns the
actual Fourier product, before a finite-support synthesis is formed.

J.-L. Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques
(d'après Harish-Chandra)*, 2003, VIII.1 and VIII.5, printed
pp. 320–321 and 332.
