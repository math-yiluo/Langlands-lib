# The tensor of the signed compact c-factors

`cCompactFamily_eq_tensor` identifies the actual `cCompactFamily` on its joint convergence domain with the tensor of the positive and negative `cFactorOnCompact` maps in the original scheme characters. The negative factor acts on the original smooth dual at the inverse character. Its convergence witness comes from `cFactorDualConvergence`. The source comparison is the inverse of `cCompactSourceChart`. The lower comparison is `cCompactTargetMap`, including the lower smooth-dual comparison and the inverse source gamma.

The proof starts with a pure tensor of two full normalized induced functions. `cMap_eq_tensor` evaluates the original c-map on their normalized product tensor. `cFamily_eq_transport` applies the lower coefficient transport, and `productInducedCoefficientTransport_tensor` moves it through the tensor comparison. `cCompactFamily_eq_composite` then restricts on the lower side and extends on the ambient side. The product and single-factor compact charts commute with these operations by the construction of `normalizedCompactProductChart`.

`cFactorPlus_on_lift` and `cFactorMinus_on_lift` identify the two lower compact outputs with the corresponding fixed compact factors. The source and lower tensor charts can then be evaluated at the trivial character by `normalizedCompactProductChart_eq`. `cCompactTargetMap_tmul` identifies the resulting lower value. Surjectivity of the normalized compact restriction maps gives the calculation on every pure tensor in the fixed source chart, and tensor extensionality proves equality of the linear maps.

The comparison implements the normalized product c-map of Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques, d’après Harish-Chandra*, V.1 and proof of V.1.1, printed pp. 288–289, with the fixed compact realizations of IV.1, printed pp. 276–277.
