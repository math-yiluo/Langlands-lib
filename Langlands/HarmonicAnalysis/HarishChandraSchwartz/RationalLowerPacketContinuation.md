# Whole-domain continuations at the compact of a lower Levi

`lowerDensityPackageOfContinuation` constructs the
actual density package on a standard output Levi in the weak constant-term
expansion. It takes the given standard inducing Levi, its conjugation into
the output Levi, the split-central quotient measure, and an irreducible
admissible square-integrable inducing representation. It uses the actual
standard-Levi Iwasawa products at the trace of the ambient compact.

The transported split center is the point image of a split component of
the dynamic Levi. Its closedness and the unimodularity of that Levi give
invariance of the transported Haar measure under inversion. Applying
`ActualOppositeFullDomainContinuationStatement` on the output Levi gives
both continuations and their respective nonempty open convergence
subregions. `lowerDensityFromOppositeContinuations` supplies the inverse
composition scalar and its regularity at every unitary unramified parameter.
The subgroup equality in the conclusion retains the literal compact trace
on the output Levi.

Proposition V.1.1 gives the unweighted weak constant-term expansion at
G-regular points. Section V.2 defines the density from the inverse
composition scalar and the squared rank-one gamma factors, and Lemma V.2.1
gives its regularity on the unitary orbit. The proof of VI.3.1 on printed
p. 304 combines V.1.1 and Corollary V.2.3 to obtain a density-weighted weak
constant-term identity. This construction supplies the lower-Levi density
for that identity. The whole-domain continuation statement is an explicit
premise.
