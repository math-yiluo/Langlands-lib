# Orbit-parameter continuity for dynamic induced coefficients

`continuous_inducedOrbitCoefficientMap` applies the published continuity
of `unitaryOrbitCoefficientMap` to the actual dynamic parabolic and its
compact picture. The dynamic Levi coordinates give Laurent character
evaluation and a continuous parameterization. The existing unimodularity
and modular character theorems supply the hypotheses for the coefficient.
The result concerns continuity in the orbit variable at each fixed group
point, with the given quotient measure and square-integrable representation.
This is a derived input for the lower coefficients in the weighted weak
expansion.
