# An auxiliary c-integral on fixed compact pictures

In Waldspurger (2003), V.1, printed pp. 288–289, one factor of the c-map translates the input by the inverse normalizer lift, applies the standard integral, then restricts to the lower Levi with the outer half-modulus normalization. `cFactorOnCompact` reads that actual factor in the ordinary fixed compact pictures at the original and lower representations. The complex parameter at the auxiliary Levi is the transport of the original scheme parameter from `factorParameterIso`.

`cFactorConvergenceRegion_eq` uses `domain_eq_top_iff_equiv` with `factorConjugateTwistEquiv` on the original pair quotient measure. `cFactorPairParameter` carries the point and full-domain witness to the precise domain of `DynamicCommonLeviCompact.compactFamily`. The two ambient compact factorizations follow from `isIwasawaPair_dynamicParabolic_of_minimal`, and the square uses the actual squared-modulus identity from `squaredModulus_of_centralSplitTorusCharacter`.

`cFactorOnCompact_eq` inserts `cFactorCompactTranslation` and `cFactorCompactRestriction` on either side of the actual compact integral. The middle square is `fullSourceIntertwiner_natural` for the coefficient equivalence and the original quotient and Haar measures. `Langlands.Plancherel.compactOperatorOfSquare` inserts the compact charts. `factorRestriction_eval` follows from `RealizedLeviStages.inductionInStagesIso_hom_eval` at the identity in the ambient group. Thus `cFactorCompactRestriction_eq` shows that the restriction is constant in the parameter when the lower compact points map into the ambient compact subgroup.

`normalizedCompactExtension_leftTranslate` gives the value identity behind the polynomial dependence of translation. Fix an Iwasawa factorization of the translating element as a parabolic element times a compact element. Multiplication on the right by any compact point keeps the parabolic factor. The normalized extension formula `unramifiedNormalizedInductionCompactEquiv_symm_mul` then gives one character evaluation at the Levi projection of that parabolic factor, for every compact evaluation and every input section.
