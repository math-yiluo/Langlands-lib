# The ordinary unitary Jacquet summand on the represented standard Levi

The ordinary normalized Jacquet action for the standard constant term and
the represented Jacquet functor use two quotient presentations. The
comparison identifies their unitary summands as representations of the
intrinsic points of the same Levi. This is an internal comparison used to
apply the represented tempered geometric lemma to the weak coefficient of
Waldspurger III.5.1 and V.1.1.

The comparison uses the exact point images of the represented Levi and
radical. `normalizedJacquetComponentCongrEquiv`, applied to the full
intrinsic Levi group, transports the normalized localized quotient along
these images. `standardNormalizedJacquetOrdinaryEquiv` identifies it
with the quotient carrying the same inverse half-modulus used in the
Casselman coefficient. The component maps agree under the standard
Levi-point isomorphism, so the equivalence restricts to the sums of
unitary weight spaces. A nonzero represented weight has open kernel,
and zero normalized real part then agrees with the norm-one condition.
Weights that are absent contribute the zero space.
