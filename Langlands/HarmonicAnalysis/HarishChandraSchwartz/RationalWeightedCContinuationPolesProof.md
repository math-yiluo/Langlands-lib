# Rational continuation and unitary poles of the weighted c-family

`weightedCContinuationPoles` proves `WeightedCContinuationPolesStatement` with independent local-field and coefficient-carrier universes. It uses the original Borel Haar quotient, the supplied density packages, and their common compact normalization. On the whole set \(\Omega\), the preimage of the interior of `cWeightedDomain`, it constructs one global polynomial operator numerator and a regular denominator nonzero throughout \(\Omega\). The second presentation has a unitary pole factorization and satisfies the global clearing identity with the first presentation.

For the positive factor, membership in the weighted interior implies the positive full convergence condition. For the negative factor, `cFactorDualConvergence` gives full convergence at the inverse character on the original smooth dual. Inversion preserves the openness and nonemptiness needed by `exists_cFactorOnCompactContinuationWithPoles`. The dual inherits irreducibility and admissibility by `SmoothRep.isIrreducible_contragredient_iff` and `SmoothRep.isAdmissible_contragredient_iff`, using `compactOpenCofinal_of_isClosed`. `CommonCompactNormalization.lower` identifies the lower compact subgroup with the preimage of the ambient compact by the represented inclusion. This verifies the compact membership condition of the signed continuations for both signs. The fixed Iwasawa factorizations in the statement and its lower density package supply the compact charts.

`cCompactFamily_eq_tensor` identifies the unweighted operator with the tensor of the two actual signed compact operators using `cCompactSourceChart` and `cCompactTargetMap`. The latter includes the inverse source gamma. The proof inverts the negative continuation, tensors the two continuations, restricts to \(\Omega\), and applies the fixed maps. It uses the corresponding operations on `RationalOperatorPolePresentation`. The exact fraction from `exists_weightedCScalarPolePresentation` is the scalar in `weightedCompactFamily`. Multiplying by that fraction uses `RationalOperatorContinuationData.regularRatio` and `RationalOperatorPolePresentation.regularRatio`, which give the weighted clearing and cross identities. When \(\Omega\) is empty, the constant denominator one and zero numerator satisfy the conditions, with `RationalUnitaryPoleFactorization.ofNonvanishing` for the second denominator.

The normalized c-factors and their tensor are those of Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques, d’après Harish-Chandra*, V.1, printed pp. 288–289. The compact realization and rationality use IV.1, and V.2.1 through V.2.3, printed pp. 292–294, specify the unitary pole and density calculations used in the continuation.
