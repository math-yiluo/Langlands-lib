# Constant-term estimates for the dynamic conjugation split

`RationalDynamicConstantTermEstimates` states the two normalized estimates
needed for the split integral in Waldspurger VIII.3.1, printed pp. 323–326.
It uses the actual forward and opposite radical Haar measures normalized
at the compact open scale of the split, the actual ambient Xi and height,
and a common Levi Xi weight.

The proof reads the forward and opposite radicals in their standard
presentations for the original and reversed chambers. The measure is
pushed along the inclusion-preserving isomorphism of the two equal
subgroups. The Haar character depends only on the conjugating element and
the underlying closed subgroup. For the opposite radical the reciprocal
character theorem gives the inverse of the common Levi's forward modulus.

The reversed basis and Cartan coverings use the original structural
compact. The inputs include the Iwasawa factorization and pointwise
equality of the Xi functions for each reversed presentation, and the
standard II.4.5 estimate. Applying that estimate in both presentations
and changing variables gives the required estimates on the original
dynamic subgroups and preserves the measure scale. The included
convolution-weight transport also preserves the normalized compact average
under equality of the actual Levi subgroups.
