# Discrete-series orbit finiteness in relative semisimple rank zero

`Langlands.HarishChandraSchwartz.RationalRankZeroDiscreteSeriesOrbitFinitenessStatement`
specializes Waldspurger's Theorem VIII.1.2 to connected reductive groups whose
actual relative-root span for a maximal split torus has dimension zero. The
relative-root span is taken over `ℚ`. The maximal split torus can have positive
dimension. The field and representation carrier universes are independent.

The conclusion is the published `RationalLocalDiscreteSeriesOrbitFinitenessStatement`.
It quantifies over each split component, each Haar measure on the associated
quotient of rational points, and each compact open subgroup `H`. The finite set
consists of unitary unramified twist orbits having a discrete-series member
with a nonzero `H`-fixed vector.

This restriction isolates the base case of the induction on semisimple rank
introduced in §VIII.1. It can be proved by finite evaluation on the compact
split-central quotient, followed by the counting argument of §VIII.5. The
unrestricted local assertion stays a separate target.

The source is J.-L. Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes
p-adiques (d'après Harish-Chandra)*, J. Inst. Math. Jussieu 2 (2003), 235–333.
See Theorem VIII.1.2 and the induction paragraph on printed p. 321
(PDF p. 87), and §VIII.5 on printed pp. 331–332 (PDF pp. 97–98).
[`waldspurger-2003-plancherel`](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1474748003000082)
