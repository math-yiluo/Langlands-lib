# From pointwise to global unitary regularity of the weighted c-family (Waldspurger V.2.3)

Author: Worker 986, task `f27f47c6-b5d0-4d3d-888a-4b73f31c1d94` (Task 7826, revision 1).

Lean module: `Langlands.HarmonicAnalysis.HarishChandraSchwartz.RationalWeakExpansionLocalRegularity`.
Namespaces: `Langlands.Plancherel.UnramifiedOperatorFamily` (generic part) and
`Langlands.HarishChandraSchwartz.WeakExpansion` (the weighted c-family).

## Purpose and status

The published target `WeightedCRegularityStatement` (module `RationalWeakExpansionTargets`,
Waldspurger Corollary V.2.3) asks, for every Weyl index `s`, for **one** rational extension
`WeightedCExtension` of `c̃_{P'|P}(s, ·)`. Such an extension has one regular denominator that
vanishes at no unitary character. Waldspurger's proof (printed p.294) fixes one point
`(ω, E) ∈ O` and shows that the function of the corollary is regular **at `ω`**.

This module proves that the pointwise statement implies the target:

- `WeightedCRegularityReductionStatement` is the implication
  `WeightedCLocalRegularityStatement → WeightedCRegularityStatement`, for the same data. It is proved
  in the module by `weightedCRegularityReduction`.
- The premise `WeightedCLocalRegularityStatement` is **not proved** here or anywhere in the
  Library. It contains the analytic content of Waldspurger's proof of V.2.3: IV.1 (12), Lemma V.2.2
  (the norm identity) and the minimality argument on `Q(λ) = ∏ ⟨λ, α̌⟩^{n(α)}`.
- So `WeightedCRegularityStatement` is **not proved** by this module. The module does not use or
  prove `WeightedWeakExpansionStatement`, `WeightedCDecompositionStatement` or
  `WeightedPacketIdentityStatement`.

## Generic part (namespace `Langlands.Plancherel.UnramifiedOperatorFamily`)

Throughout, `X = unramifiedCharacters ν` is the group of unramified characters of a group with
norm characters `ν`, `Im X = unitaryUnramifiedCharacters ν` is its unitary subgroup, and
`unramifiedRegularFunctions ν` is the algebra of regular functions on `X` (the algebra generated by
the evaluations).

- `IsPolynomialOperatorOn.finset_sum`: a finite sum of polynomial operator families
  (`IsPolynomialOperatorOn`) is polynomial. The proof is by induction on the finset, from
  `isPolynomialOperatorOn_const` and `IsPolynomialOperatorOn.add`.
- `unitaryInclusion : Im X → X` (the map `z ↦ ⟨z, z ∈ X⟩`) and `continuous_unitaryInclusion`.
- `unramifiedDagger_mul_self_unitary`: for a regular function `d` and a unitary `z`,
  `(d† d)(z) = |d(z)|²` (as a complex number). Here `d†(x) = conj(d(x†))`, where `x† = x̄⁻¹` is
  `unramifiedConjInv` (published `unramifiedDagger_apply`). The proof uses `z† = z` for unitary
  `z` (published `unramifiedConjInv_eq_self_of_mem_unitary`) and `|w|² = conj(w) w`.
- **Definition `IsRegularAtUnitary A z`.** `A : D₀ → (V →ₗ[ℂ] W)` is a family of linear maps on a
  set `D₀ ⊆ X`, and `z ∈ Im X`. The predicate says that there are a regular function `d` and a family
  `N : X → (V →ₗ[ℂ] W)` with:
  1. `N` is polynomial on all of `X` (`IsPolynomialOperatorOn Set.univ`);
  2. `N(x) = d(x) • A(x)` for every `x` in the interior of `D₀`;
  3. `d(z) ≠ 0`.

  This is the usual meaning of "the rational family `A` is regular at `z`": `A` has a presentation
  `N/d` whose denominator does not vanish at `z`.
- **Theorem `exists_unitaryRegular_of_local`.** Assume that `Im X` is compact and that
  `IsRegularAtUnitary A z` holds for every `z ∈ Im X`. Then there are one regular `d` and one
  polynomial `N` with `N(x) = d(x) • A(x)` on the interior of `D₀` and `d(z) ≠ 0` for **every**
  unitary `z`.

  Proof as implemented. Choose `(d_z, N_z)` for every `z`. The sets
  `U_z = {w ∈ Im X : d_z(w) ≠ 0}` are open: regular functions are continuous (published
  `continuous_unramifiedRegularFunction`) and `unitaryInclusion` is continuous. They cover `Im X`,
  since `z ∈ U_z`. Compactness gives a finite set `t` with `Im X = ⋃_{j ∈ t} U_j`. Put
  `d = Σ_{j∈t} d_j† d_j` and `N = Σ_{j∈t} d_j† • N_j`.
  - `N` is polynomial: each `d_j† • N_j` is polynomial (`IsPolynomialOperatorOn.regular_smul`), and
    finite sums are polynomial (`finset_sum`).
  - On the interior of `D₀`, `N(x) = Σ_j d_j†(x) d_j(x) • A(x) = d(x) • A(x)`.
  - At a unitary `w`, `d(w) = Σ_j |d_j(w)|²` by `unramifiedDagger_mul_self_unitary`. Some `j ∈ t`
    has `w ∈ U_j`, so the sum is a positive real number and `d(w) ≠ 0`.

## Specialization to the weighted c-family (namespace `Langlands.HarishChandraSchwartz.WeakExpansion`)

The data and notation are those of the published module `RationalWeakExpansionTargets` (see its
Markdown, sections "Data" and "Rational extensions and lower sections"): `M = M_I`, `ν_M`
(`leviRationalNormCharacters i ν I`), the domain `D₀ = cWeightedDomain …` of the weighted c-family,
and the weighted family `weightedCompactFamily …` (`c̃_{P'|P}(s, ·)` on the fixed compact
pictures).

- **Definition `WeightedCRegularAt … z`** (for `z ∈ Im X(M)`): `IsRegularAtUnitary` for
  `A = weightedCompactFamily …` on `D₀ = cWeightedDomain …` at `z`. In words, there are a regular
  `d` and a polynomial numerator `N` on `X(M)` with `N(x) = d(x) c̃(s, π ⊗ x)` on the compact
  pictures at every `x` in the interior of `D₀`, and `d(z) ≠ 0`. It is the same data as the
  fields `denominator`, `numerator`, `polynomial` and `clears` of the published
  `WeightedCExtension`, with nonvanishing at the one point `z` in place of the field
  `unitary_regular`.
- **Theorem `nonempty_weightedCExtension_of_local`.** If the interior of `D₀` is nonempty and
  `WeightedCRegularAt … z` holds for every unitary `z`, then
  `Nonempty (WeightedCExtension …)`. Proof: `Im X(M)` is compact, because it is homeomorphic to a
  compact torus by the published circle coordinates `leviRationalUnitaryCoordinates`. So
  `exists_unitaryRegular_of_local` applies. Its output, together with the nonempty interior, gives
  the fields of `WeightedCExtension`.
- **Definition `WeightedCLocalRegularityStatement`** (the premise, not proved). It has exactly the
  explicit arguments of `WeightedCRegularityStatement`: `hG hi hν I I' hζ hζ' hKc hPK sc hs π mu
  hRefl hCST PG R PM`. It also has the same three premises as the target, as arrows in its body:
  - square integrability of `π` modulo `C_M`;
  - properness of `I'`;
  - `IsSpecialVertexCompact ν hi K`.

  It then quantifies over `hN : SourceCompactNormalization …` and over every Weyl index `s`, and
  asserts two things:
  1. the interior of `cWeightedDomain … (nₛ s) …` is nonempty;
  2. `WeightedCRegularAt … (nₛ s) … (hN s) z` holds for every unitary `z`.
- **Definition `WeightedCRegularityReductionStatement`** (the recorded statement) and its proof
  **`weightedCRegularityReduction`**. Given the premise, fix the target's premises, `hN` and `s`,
  and apply `nonempty_weightedCExtension_of_local` to the two conjuncts.

## Relation to the source

Source: J.-L. Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques, d'après
Harish-Chandra* (2003), Corollary V.2.3 and its proof, printed p.294 (PDF page 60). The definition
of "rationnel et régulier sur `O`" is in V.1, printed p.288.

- The proof of V.2.3 begins "Fixons `(ω, E) ∈ O`". It uses IV.1 (12) to find
  `Q(λ) = ∏_α ⟨λ, α̌⟩^{n(α)}` such that `λ ↦ Q(λ) µ^G(ω ⊗ χ_λ) µ^{M'}(s(ω ⊗ χ_λ))^{-1}
  c_{P'|P}(s, ω ⊗ χ_λ)` "est régulière en `λ = 0`". It then shows, with Lemma V.2.2 and the
  minimality of the `n(α)`, that `Q = 1`, so that the function of the corollary is regular at `ω`.
  That conclusion at every `ω ∈ O`, for the actual family `c̃` on its convergence domain, is the
  content of `WeightedCLocalRegularityStatement`.
- The nonempty interior of `D₀`, the first conjunct of the premise, is part of the published
  `WeightedCExtension` (its field `interior_nonempty`), and the reduction needs it for that reason.
  In the source, the intertwining integrals from which the c-function is built converge on an open
  cone of parameters (IV.1). The premise asks for this nonemptiness for every `s`; it is not part of the source's
  regularity argument.
- The source does not write out the passage from "regular at every `ω ∈ O`" to one rational
  extension on an open set containing `O` (the definition of V.1, p.288). This module supplies it,
  with the derived argument above: compactness of `O ≅ Im X(M)` and the denominator
  `Σ_j d_j† d_j`, which equals `Σ_j |d_j|²` on `Im X(M)`. Its provenance is therefore **derived**.
