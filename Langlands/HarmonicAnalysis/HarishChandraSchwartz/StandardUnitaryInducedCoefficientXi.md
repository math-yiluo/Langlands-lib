# All-coefficient Xi bounds for the original unitary induced fibers

`HasStandardUnitaryInducedCoefficientXiBound` requires every smooth matrix
coefficient of every original normalized induced unitary unramified fiber
to be bounded by a positive multiple of the original ambient Xi. The
coefficient constant may depend on the chosen twist, vector and functional,
as required by `HasXiBoundedCoefficients`.

The original structural basis, source standard-Levi Cartan covering and
square integrability modulo the original central subgroup give the source
coefficient Xi bound at the compact subgroup obtained by projecting
`K ∩ P_I(F)` to the original literal Levi. Its compactness, openness and
Iwasawa factorization are derived, and no projection stability of `K` is
assumed. The displayed central quotient retains its Borel-compatible Haar
measure.

For an arbitrary smooth vector and functional in one fixed induced fiber,
take their actual coefficient tensor. The actual compact-induced tensor
equivalence realizes it in the original product-induced object. Its compact
picture image is an input to the arbitrary-source-compact coefficient bound.
The inverse compact-picture map returns that same product-induced element.
The actual tensor equivalence then gives precisely the original smooth matrix
coefficient. No extension to a coherent orbit section is asserted or needed.

The contextual source is Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les
groupes p-adiques, d'après Harish-Chandra* (2003), Corollary III.1.2,
printed p.264, and the proof of VI.2.2, printed pp.298–299, source
`waldspurger-2003-plancherel`. The projected-compact consumer is derived
project content. This supplies the coefficient input for the strict Jacquet
exponent argument and leaves the separately owned Xi upper estimate open.
