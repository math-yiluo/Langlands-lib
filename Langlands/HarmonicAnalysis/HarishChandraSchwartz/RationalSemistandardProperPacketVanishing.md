# Proper semistandard packet vanishing in VIII.2.3

`ProperPacketConvolutionFromFourierStatement` supplies the two raw
proper-packet zeros of `ProperPacketConvolutionVanishingStatement`. It keeps
the full semistandard comparison for the intrinsic cuspidal submodule, the
VII.1.2 inputs at each proper orbit, the VII.1.3 Fourier smoothness, and
the VI.2.2 product integrability for the two Schwartz tests explicit.

The comparison identifies intrinsic cuspidality with vanishing of every
normalized proper induced constant term. Evaluation at the identity of the
Levi removes the modulus and gives the unnormalized radical integral for
any two translates. Actual radical Haar is inversion invariant because the
radical is closed and each of its elements is contained in a compact open
subgroup. The two conjugate-inversion formulas preserve vanishing of the
translated integrals. Multiplication by the half-modulus restores the
normalized condition for both tests, so the comparison gives their intrinsic
cuspidality.

VII.1.2 gives zero of each proper Fourier component for the two tests. The
specialized VII.2(1) pairing then gives zero in both convolutions by
conjugate linearity in the first argument of the density-weighted form.
The zero functions are the input of the finite-sum reduction
`properSynthesisConvolution`. The ambient Haar is shared by Fourier,
convolution and pairing. The field and the complex smooth representation
carriers have independent universes.
