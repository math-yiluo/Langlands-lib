# The full-Levi Fourier tensor and the discrete-series Schwartz action

At `P = G`, the step in Waldspurger VIII.5 from `φ̂(ω,G) = 0` to
`ω(ˇφ) = 0` uses the actual Fourier operator formula of VII.1 and the
identification of induction from the whole group. The proposition
`RationalFullLeviFourierActionVanishingStatement` formulates this
implication at a semistandard index whose point parabolic and point
Levi are the whole group and whose point radical is trivial. It retains
the comparison of the two central subgroups under the actual full-Levi
point-group identification and allows any two Borel quotient Haar measures.

For each ambient discrete series class, the proof pulls back its
canonical representative to the literal Levi. The pushforward of the
Levi quotient measure is Haar, and Haar scaling preserves the
discrete-series predicate for the ambient group. This gives square
integrability for the specified Levi quotient measure. The VII.1 formula
identifies the tensor's endomorphism with the inducing formal degree
times the induced action of `ˇφ`. The degree is positive, so the induced
action vanishes. Evaluation at one, followed by the inverse pullback
comparison, gives an equivalence with the original representation.
Tempered coefficient bounds transfer through this equivalence by
precomposing smooth functionals. The naturality of the Schwartz action
then gives zero on the original representative.

The pointwise vanishing premise is the one obtained from vanishing of
the full-group orbit family by
`WavePacketPairing.fourierComponents_eq_zero_iff_forall_fourierAt_eq_zero`.
The carrier universe is independent of the field universe.
