# Finite level bounds for reparametrized scalar coefficients

`exists_orbitSectionLevel_monomialSeminorm_bound` takes an output scalar
family with a finite formula at a fixed original section level. Each summand
is a fixed smooth scalar times an original compact-picture coefficient of the
section, with the same integer monomial pullback for all summands. Each output
ordered derivative seminorm is bounded by a constant times a finite supremum
of original level seminorms, uniformly over all sections at that level.

The finite support consists of the input coefficient functionals in the formula
paired with all ordered derivative lists up to the output order. The scalar
product estimate on the source torus bounds each weighted summand. The triangle
inequality bounds their sum, and the mixed angular chain rule bounds its
monomial pullback. This is a derived step in transferring the original seminorms
to the lower sections of the weighted weak constant term.
