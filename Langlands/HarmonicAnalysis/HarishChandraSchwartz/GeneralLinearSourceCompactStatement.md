# The source compact at the standard general linear model

`GeneralLinearSourceCompactStatement` identifies the standard integral
points of \(\mathrm{GL}_N\) over a nonarchimedean local field as a compact
of the kind chosen in Waldspurger I.1. The torus is the diagonal split
torus of the actual group scheme. The subgroup is the image of the
integral points of the standard model in its rational-point group.

The conclusion is `IsSourceCompactAt`. It asserts compactness,
openness and maximality, and exhibits a special vertex in an apartment
for that torus with the normalized local-field order and the required
centralizer compatibility. The norm-one stabilizer of this vertex
equals the specified integral subgroup. The proposition ranges over
both characteristics and every natural matrix rank, including the
trivial group at rank zero.

Kaletha and Prasad identify the Chevalley point for a basis with the
norm that gives each basis vector value zero. Taking the coordinate
basis puts this point in the diagonal apartment and gives the standard
lattice \(\mathcal O_F^N\). Its stabilizer in the norm-one subgroup is
\(\mathrm{GL}_N(\mathcal O_F)\). This is the compact used for the
normalization of the Plancherel formula.
