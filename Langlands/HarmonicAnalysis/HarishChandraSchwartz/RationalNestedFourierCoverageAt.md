# The nested Fourier coverage at the two source compacts

`RationalSemistandardParabolicLift.SourceNestedFourierCoverageStatement`
asks for the nested Fourier match of VIII.2.1 on a connected reductive
group over a nonarchimedean local field. The split torus on the outer
Levi `M` is the factorization of the ambient maximal split torus through
the actual inclusion of `M`. Each group uses a supplied generic
cocharacter, special compact properties and minimal Iwasawa pair. The
compact on `M` is the preimage of the ambient compact. The group Haar
measures and the Borel split-central quotient Haar measures are
normalized on the respective compact traces.

The ambient Schwartz height is the matrix height of the supplied closed
embedding `ρ`. The Schwartz height on `M` uses the restriction of `ρ`
along the actual inclusion of `M`. For each inner semistandard parabolic
`Pᴹ = M′ Uᴹ` of `M` and each unitary unramified twist orbit of discrete
series of `M′`, the match gives an ambient orbit at the lift
`P′ = Pᴹ U`, where `U` is the radical of the outer parabolic. The
quantifier includes `Pᴹ = M`.

`RationalSemistandardNestedFourierCoverage` requires an ambient index
and orbit for every inner index and orbit, the literal Levi and radical
point maps, the two Fourier evaluation packages, the radical product
measure and modulus identities, the final Levi group Haar comparison,
and the identification of every inducing representation and its degree.
It also requires absolute convergence for all translated lifted radical
integrals of the ambient Schwartz functions. These are the inputs to
the existing nested Fourier vanishing implication.

Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques (d’après
Harish-Chandra)*, I.1, printed pp. 240 and 242, fixes the common compact
Haar normalization and the matrix-height convention. The comparison follows the III.6 diagram, printed
p. 272, and the two applications of VII.1.2 in the proof of VIII.2.1,
printed pp. 321–322.
