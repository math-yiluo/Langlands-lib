# The minimal parabolic modulus as a normalized adjoint determinant

The scalar identity identifies forward additive Haar scaling by a field unit with its
residue-normalized absolute value. The proof compares the normalized valuation with the
valuation on the field, constructs the uniformizing element from the order-one uniformizer,
and applies the scalar Haar pushforward formula to the inverse unit. Evaluating on the
compact open ring of integers identifies the two scaling factors since its additive Haar
measure is finite and positive.

For a generic cocharacter of a maximal split torus in a connected reductive group, the
empty-index standard parabolic and radical are the corresponding dynamic subgroups.
Factor a rational point of the minimal parabolic into a Levi point and a radical point.
The minimal Haar modulus and the eigenvalue on the exterior determinant line are both
trivial on the radical factor. Lift the Levi point to the affine centralizer model. The
Haar determinant formula for its conjugation on the radical, the scalar identity above,
and the restriction of the adjoint determinant character to the centralizer identify the
two factors on the Levi. This gives the residue-normalized absolute value of the
determinant on the radical Lie algebra for any of the represented models in the theorem.

This is the normalization step used with the minimal Iwasawa modulus in J.-L. Waldspurger,
*La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques (d'après Harish-Chandra)* (2003),
