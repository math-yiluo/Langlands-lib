# Finite-product topology of the actual section levels

This module supplies the independent finite-product level and inductive-limit
topologies for the actual smooth induced orbit sections in Waldspurger,
*La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques*, VI.1, printed pp.295–296,
and VI.3, printed p.301 (registered source
`waldspurger-2003-plancherel`, SHA256
`4b6a6162cd0b3863703fb2f65d1698f926caf813114917f2084c16e38f2e30f7`;
PDF pp.61–62 and 67). The basis-coordinate implementation and chosen-basis
presentation of the inductive limit are internal constructions. The topology
comparisons are unproved Prop-valued definitions.

Every declaration below is in `Langlands.HarishChandraSchwartz`. The generic
data are those of `OrbitSectionTopology`: a locally compact topological
nonarchimedean group, an actual semidirect decomposition `P = M U`, closed
`P` and `U`, an open compact subgroup `K`, continuous projection, a covering
`G = P K`, continuous valuation characters, a complex irreducible admissible
representation `π` of `M`, and a character group equivalence `e` with a
finite product of circles indexed by `J`. The target introduces no continuity
premise on the generic group equivalence `e`. The group, parameter, coefficient
carrier, torus and finite basis index universes are independent.

## Actual coordinates and level topology

Fix an open subgroup `H ≤ K` and a finite basis `b` of
`orbitSectionFixedCoefficients h K π H`. This is the actual `H × H` fixed
submodule of `ProductInducedCompactPicture h K π.obj`, as identified in
`OrbitSectionFixedTensor`.

`orbitSectionLevelCoordinates` composes the actual
`orbitSectionLevelTensor` with the algebraic linear equivalence
`smoothTensorCoordinates J b`. Its codomain is the finite product
`B → smoothFiniteTorusFunctions J`. Thus it uses the algebraic tensor
identification with finite coordinates, without asserting that every such
coordinate family comes from an actual section.

`orbitSectionLevelCoordinates_apply` proves that evaluation of coordinate
`i` at `z` is `b.coord i` of
`orbitSectionLevelFixedRestriction ... q z`. The latter's underlying
coefficient is exactly the original compact restriction. The proof uses
`smoothTensorCoordinates_apply` and the already proved
`orbitSectionLevelTensor_evaluation`.
`orbitSectionLevelCoordinates_injective` composes injectivity of the
coordinate equivalence with `orbitSectionLevelTensor_injective`.

`orbitSectionFiniteProductLevelTopology` is the topology induced by this
actual coordinate map from `smoothTorusProductTopology J B`. That product
uses the independently defined scalar topology generated by the supremum
norm of every ordered angle derivative, including order zero. It does not
use the existing topology of the section level. Equivalently, this is the
topology pulled back through the actual level tensor map from
`smoothTensorFiniteProductTopology J b`.

The full target
`Langlands.HarishChandraSchwartz.OrbitSectionLevelFiniteProductTopologyStatement`
compares `OrbitSectionLevel.topologicalSpace` with this independently
constructed topology, on the same underlying actual level. It is parameterized
by `H`, its inclusion in `K`, and any finite basis. It is unproved.
The independent scalar/tensor module supplies the separate target
`Langlands.HarishChandraSchwartz.SmoothTensorBasisIndependenceStatement`
for equality of the tensor topologies from different finite bases.

## Total locally convex inductive limit

Let `d` supply a finiteness witness for each actual fixed coefficient space,
indexed by `{H : OpenSubgroup G // H ≤ K}`.
`orbitSectionFixedBasis` selects `Module.finBasis` using each explicit
witness, with index `Fin (Module.finrank ℂ V_H)`.
This choice is used only to construct the total topology; the level comparison
still quantifies over every finite basis.

`orbitSectionFiniteProductOriginalTop` is the supremum of the topologies
coinduced by the existing `OrbitSectionSpace.ofLevel` maps from these
independent chosen-basis level topologies. Every actual smooth section belongs
to one such level by the imported `OrbitSectionSpace.exists_ofLevel`.
`orbitSectionFiniteProductTopology` is the infimum of all topologies `t`
coarser than that final topology for which addition and complex scalar
multiplication are continuous and the space is locally convex over `ℝ`.
In Lean's topology order, the coarseness condition is
`orbitSectionFiniteProductOriginalTop ... ≤ t`. This is exactly the
convention of `OrbitSectionSpace.originalTop` and
`OrbitSectionSpace.topologicalSpace`, with the new level topologies in place
of the original ones.

`orbitSectionFiniteProductTopology_locallyConvex` proves the three structure
properties by Mathlib's infimum lemmas for topological addition, continuous
scalar multiplication and local convexity. The new topologies are explicit
values rather than global instances; the established instances continue to
denote the original topologies.

The full target
`Langlands.HarishChandraSchwartz.OrbitSectionTotalFiniteProductTopologyStatement`
asserts equality of `OrbitSectionSpace.topologicalSpace` and this independently
constructed locally convex inductive-limit topology for the supplied family
`d`. It is unproved.

The full aggregate target
`Langlands.HarishChandraSchwartz.OrbitSectionFiniteProductTopologyStatement`
takes compact-open cofinality of the inducing Levi as a retained structural
hypothesis. It existentially concludes a family `d` of finite-dimensionality
witnesses, and concludes the level comparison for every open `H ≤ K`,
every finite basis index type (in the independently quantified universe
`uB`), and every basis. It also concludes the total comparison using `d`.
Finiteness and the comparisons are not premises. Finiteness itself is already
supplied by `finiteDimensional_orbitSectionFixedCoefficients`; only the
topology comparisons remain open.

## Source correspondence and scope

VI.1 imposes the actual self-twist covariance on the compact-picture family.
Both the source level and the total space here are the existing section types,
and the proved coordinate maps are injective. The level inherits a subspace
topology from the finite smooth tensor, rather than being replaced by all
smooth coefficient families. VI.3 supplies the finite-dimensional coefficient,
finite-product topology and total inductive-limit description implemented
above. No completed tensor product is used.

The scalar module documents the ordered-derivative convention for the usual
all-order smooth topology and exposes derivative permutation as the separate
optional `SmoothTorusDerivativePermutationStatement`. That target is not an
assumption of any section comparison. This module does not establish the
open comparisons, wave-packet Schwartz membership or synthesis continuity.
