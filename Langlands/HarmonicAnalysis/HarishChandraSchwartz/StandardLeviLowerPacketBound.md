## From continuity on a lower ambient group to a pointwise bound

`LowerChamberPacketContinuity` abbreviates the existing `StandardLeviPlancherelWavePacketContinuityStatement` at the actual lower ambient group `M' = M_{I'}`, with the chamber in which `wζ` is standard. Its inducing representation is `lowerChamberRepresentation`. It uses the transported split component and quotient Haar of `StandardLeviLowerSplitCenter`, the supplied lower density data read via `lowerChamberPackage`, and the compact subgroup of that package for the packets. The minimal Iwasawa hypothesis uses the re-chosen chamber of `M'`. The matrix representation in this abbreviation may be any matrix representation of `M'` on the stated nonempty finite index set.

`exists_lowerPacket_bound` proves a `LevelPacketBound` for the actual dynamic lower packet at every point of `M'(F)`, for any open `H` contained in the lower compact and any real order. It applies `exists_packet_bound_of_subgroupChange` to the synthesis map supplied by the continuity hypothesis. The standard and dynamic decompositions have the same subgroup points, the identity-on-points Levi equivalence pulls back the representation, and the kernels and torus coordinates satisfy the hypotheses of the transport. `map_lowerOrbitMeasure` and `standardLeviPlancherelWeight_lowerChamberOrbitTransport` match the actual orbit measures and weights. The input packet in the resulting estimate is `DynamicInducedOrbit.inducedOrbitWavePacket` on the level inclusion, not a new synthesis formula. The finite local heartbeat allowances cover elaboration of the dependent packet types (the two presentations have different reducible wrappers), not additional mathematical assumptions.

## The bound required in the weak-term expansion

`lowerDynamicPacketChamberEstimates_of_continuity` assembles the bounds for all Weyl indices using the supplied `SourceCompactNormalization` and the Iwasawa data for `K ∩ M'(F)`. For each index, the lower clause of that normalization identifies the subgroup of the supplied lower density package with the preimage of `K` along the inclusion of the Levi scheme. `isIwasawaPair_lowerCompact` provides the minimal Iwasawa decomposition for the chamber of `wζ`. It is used only to obtain the necessary Iwasawa proof and identify the weights; the density package is unchanged. The quotient for the specialized continuity hypothesis carries its Borel measurable structure and the transported Haar measure.

The proof evaluates the lower bound at the inverse image of a minimal Levi point under `leviRationalPointHomeomorph`. By `rationalXi_lowerCompact`, the lower spherical function there is precisely the `rationalLeviXi` in the consumer. It uses the **restriction** of the given ambient matrix representation along `Standard.leviHom`; `rationalHeight_restrict_leviHom` identifies its lower height at the inverse image with the ambient height at the original point. These are equalities, not comparisons up to a new constant. The same finite set of derivative seminorms and bound constant therefore work on the requested chamber. The proof does not need to use chamber membership, since the bound on the lower ambient group holds at every point.

## Provenance and scope

This is a derived step for Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques (d'après Harish-Chandra)*, J. Inst. Math. Jussieu 2 (2003), 235–333, Proposition VI.3.1 proof, printed p. 304 (PDF p. 70). The published induction argument uses the lower packet estimates there; the Lean theorem isolates the passage from the specialized lower continuity hypothesis to those estimates. It has independent field and representation-carrier universes and does not require the two simple-root label sets to be nested. Strict rank decrease, existence of normalized lower density packages, the weak constant-term identity, the other chamber estimates and the joint membership and continuity target at `G` are not proved in this module. In particular, the supplied lower continuity hypotheses are for use in an induction, not extra hypotheses of a proof of the source-normalized target.
