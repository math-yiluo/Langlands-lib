# The common final Levi in the nested Fourier diagram

For a semistandard parabolic \(P=MU\) and a parabolic \(P^M=M'U^M\)
of \(M\) with lift \(P'=P^MU\), the final Levi \(M'\) is the same subgroup
in the two constant terms of Waldspurger's VIII.2.1. The definition
`liftedLeviModel` represents the final ambient Levi using the inner
model with its inclusion composed through the outer Levi. The
`finalLeviPointsEquiv` induced by that model is the actual inclusion
on points. The isomorphism of this model with another ambient model
identifies their algebraic norm characters via `finalLevi_normCharacters`.

The literal split-component point images match under the same equivalence,
by independence of the chosen split component and model. This gives
`finalLeviQuotientEquiv` on the split-central quotients. When the outer
Levi compact is the trace of the ambient compact, the quotient equivalence
also identifies the source quotient Haar measures. Indeed the trace in
\(M'\) and its quotient image coincide on both sides, and both measures
give that image mass one. These are the measures for the square-integrable
coefficients and formal degrees of the final Levi.
