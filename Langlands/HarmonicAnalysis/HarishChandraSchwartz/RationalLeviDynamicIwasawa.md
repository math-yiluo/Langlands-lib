# Dynamic parabolic products at the literal Levi compact

The minimal-parabolic Iwasawa factorization at a subgroup `K` gives the
Iwasawa factorization for the parabolic of any cocharacter at that same
subgroup. The parabolic contains a rational minimal parabolic, which is a
rational conjugate of the chosen standard minimal parabolic. The proof
transfers the Iwasawa product by conjugation and enlarges the parabolic
factor using the actual inclusion on rational points. This argument uses
the published minimal-parabolic existence, containment and conjugacy
results over a field.

`StandardLeviDynamicIwasawaAt` gives this conclusion for each standard Levi
at the preimage of `K` under its inclusion into the ambient group. The
accompanying proof first transports the minimal product from the literal
Levi subgroup to the Levi scheme. The same torus and generic cocharacter
give the standard minimal parabolic there. Applying the dynamic
factorization in that Levi supplies both opposite parabolic products at the
literal compact required for the lower density packages in Waldspurger
VI.3.1. This is a derived consequence of the existing product and Levi
transport results.
