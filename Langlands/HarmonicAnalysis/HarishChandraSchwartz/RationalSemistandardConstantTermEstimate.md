# The constant-term estimate for the actual semistandard index

Waldspurger II.4.5, printed pp. 262–263, applies the normalized constant-term estimate to semistandard parabolics. The remark on p. 263 changes the minimal parabolic contained in the one under consideration. The estimate used here is the one with the positive half modulus and the integral over the actual radical.

`exists_standard_presentation` takes the published cocharacter realization of the entire intrinsic triple. An integral generic refinement leaves its cocharacter nonnegative on the positive roots of the new chamber. Its zero face presents the same parabolic, Levi and radical as a standard triple for that chamber. The conclusion is an equality of intrinsic indices.

`semistandardConstantTermEstimate` supplies the estimate under the exact standard II.4.5 proposition, the joint special-compact basis existence proposition, the displayed Levi Iwasawa factorization at the intersection with the ambient compact, and the closed matrix realization. The ambient Iwasawa factorization and Xi comparison hold at the same compact after changing the minimal parabolic. The standard proposition then gives the estimate on the literal subgroups with the original Haar measure. The Levi Xi is that of the selected standard presentation and the height is restricted from the ambient matrix model.
