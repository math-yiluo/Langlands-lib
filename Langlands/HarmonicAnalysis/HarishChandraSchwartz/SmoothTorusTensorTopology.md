# Scalar smooth and finite-product tensor topologies

All declarations below are in `Langlands.HarishChandraSchwartz`. This is an
internal construction for Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les
groupes p-adiques*, VI.1, printed pp.295–296, and VI.3, printed p.301
(registered source `waldspurger-2003-plancherel`, DOI
[10.1017/S1474748003000082](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1474748003000082)).
The original readable PDF is in the central literature collection; PDF pages
61–62 and 67 were read. VI.3 equips the scalar smooth functions on the compact
unitary character torus with their usual smooth topology and tensors them
with the finite-dimensional fixed compact-picture coefficient space.

For a finite coordinate type `J`, with decidable equality,
`smoothTorusDerivative J l` is the complex-linear composite of the published
`Langlands.HarmonicAnalysis.TorusFourier.torusPartialDerivListLinear l` and
`Langlands.HarmonicAnalysis.TorusFourier.smoothTorusToContinuousMap`.
`smoothTorusDerivative_apply` proves its pointwise formula as the ordered
angle derivative `torusPartialDerivList l f.val z`.
`smoothTorusSeminorm J l` is the continuous-function supremum norm of that
composite. `smoothTorusSeminorm_apply`,
`norm_torusPartialDerivList_le_smoothTorusSeminorm`, and
`smoothTorusSeminorm_le_iff` prove precisely the supremum-norm formula,
pointwise bound, and its converse for nonnegative bounds.

`smoothTorusFunctionTopology` is the topology generated by these seminorms
for all finite ordered coordinate lists, including the empty list. This is
the all-orders ordered-angle convention for the usual scalar smooth topology
on a finite product of circles: the directions are the standard invariant
angle directions and every order is included. It does not import or transport
any topology from section levels. No new global topology instance on the
established scalar function type is installed. Equality with a separately
constructed chart-based or multi-index topology is not asserted here.
In particular the full Prop-valued target
`Langlands.HarishChandraSchwartz.SmoothTorusDerivativePermutationStatement`
states equality of ordered derivatives for permuted lists. It is unproved
here and is not assumed by any principal section-topology target.

`smoothTorusProductTopology J B` is the ordinary product of this scalar
topology. For any complex vector space `V` and finite basis
`b : Module.Basis B ℂ V`, `smoothTensorCoordinates J b` is the linear
equivalence from the algebraic tensor
`smoothFiniteTorusFunctions J ⊗[ℂ] V` to `B → smoothFiniteTorusFunctions J`.
It uses Mathlib's `TensorProduct.equivFinsuppOfBasisRight` followed by
`Finsupp.linearEquivFunOnFinite`. The proved
`smoothTensorCoordinates_apply` is exactly the published
`Langlands.Plancherel.smoothTensorEvaluation_basisCoord`: evaluating coordinate
`i` at `z` gives `b.coord i (smoothTensorEvaluation J V t z)`.

`smoothTensorFiniteProductTopology J b` pulls back the ordinary scalar product
topology through this equivalence. Thus it is constructed from scalar
seminorms and coefficient coordinates independently of any section topology;
no completed tensor product is used. The full Prop-valued target
`Langlands.HarishChandraSchwartz.SmoothTensorBasisIndependenceStatement`
compares the resulting topologies on the common tensor space for any two
finite coefficient bases, with independently sized finite index types.
It is a statement, not a proved comparison.

The ordered-list and basis-coordinate formulations are internal choices
implementing the source's topology description; Waldspurger does not prescribe
these Lean constructions. The field of coefficients is complex; the torus,
coefficient carrier and basis index universes are independent.
