# Continuation of an auxiliary c-integral on fixed compact pictures

`exists_cFactorOnCompactContinuationWithPoles` treats an irreducible admissible smooth representation of the original standard Levi and a nonempty open subset of the scheme-character convergence region. It gives one continuation denominator nonzero at every point of that subset, and a second operator pole presentation whose denominator has a unitary factorization. The lower compact subgroup is required to map into the ambient compact subgroup under the represented inclusion. The ambient and lower normalized compact pictures use their respective Iwasawa factorizations.

`normalizedCompactTranslatedValue` evaluates the normalized compact extension along a fixed left translate of the ambient compact. By `normalizedCompactExtension_leftTranslate`, one Iwasawa decomposition of the inverse normalizer lift controls all these evaluations. The Laurent multiplier is the character value on its Levi component. `cFactorCompactTranslation_polynomial` transports that multiplier to the original scheme characters with `unramifiedRegularFunctionsEquiv` and uses `cFactorCompactTranslationValue_eval` to get polynomiality of the whole source map.

The image of the open subset under `rationalUnramifiedParameterHomeomorph` lies in the full convergence region by `cFactorConvergenceRegion_eq`. The conjugate inducing representation inherits irreducibility and admissibility, and the split component is carried by `factorParameterIso`. `pairContinuationWithPolesOfOpenSubset` supplies the continuation and pole presentation there. Their parameter pullbacks transport both global numerators and the unitary factorization back to the original scheme. The resulting family is the actual compact standard integral evaluated at `cFactorPairParameter`.

`cFactorOnCompact_eq` puts the polynomial translation before the standard integral and the lower restriction after it. `cFactorCompactRestriction_eq` makes the latter a fixed linear map under the compact inclusion. `RationalOperatorContinuationData.composePolynomial` and `RationalOperatorPolePresentation.composePolynomial` apply these maps to the two numerators while preserving their denominators and clearing identities.

The auxiliary integral and restriction are those of Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques, d’après Harish-Chandra*, V.1, printed pp. 288–289. The Laurent translation calculation uses the compact fixed realization and rationality of IV.1, printed pp. 276–277. The pole presentation uses the common-Levi continuation and gallery denominator for the same standard integral, as in IV.1 and V.2.1.
