# A closed matrix embedding adapted to the compact subgroup

`RationalCompactHeightAdaptationStatement` takes an affine group scheme
of locally finite type over a nonarchimedean local field, a supplied
closed matrix embedding of positive dimension and a compact subgroup
of the actual rational points. It asks for a rational general linear
point whose conjugation keeps the embedding closed and places the
given compact in the norm-at-most-one subgroup. This subgroup uses the
maximum of the norms of the entries of a matrix and its inverse. The
existing bi-invariance property of the height then applies at the
given compact.

Waldspurger (2003), I.1, printed p. 242, chooses a closed embedding
in which the fixed compact lands in the integral matrices and uses
the logarithm of this norm as the height. The statement expresses
the basis-change step at any prescribed compact. Its mathematical
input is a full valuation-ring lattice stable under the original
compact action. [DOI 10.1017/S1474748003000082](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1474748003000082).
