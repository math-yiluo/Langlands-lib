# Lower packet estimates for the original weighted weak integral

`LowerDynamicPacketChamberEstimates` is the lower-rank analytic input. For
each Weyl index, each fixed lower section level and every real order, the
actual transported lower packet is bounded by a finite supremum of lower
seminorms times the Levi Xi and the original ambient height factor. The
bounds are required at the images of the full closed minimal chamber. The
Levi Xi uses the literal Levi Iwasawa factorization at the supplied compact.
The packet uses the conjugated representation, transported quotient and
character measures and original lower density package from the weighted
packet identity.

The proof takes the pointwise weighted expansion and the weighted packet
identity, with their proper-parabolic, special compact, Weyl lift and common
source normalization premises. Continuity from the pointwise expansion gives
Bochner integrability of the weighted weak term on the original compact orbit
with its finite source-normalized measure.
For the norm bound, choose actual lower sections for each input at the fixed
upper level. The unitary values are independent of the weighted extension
chosen for each section. One extension for each Weyl index therefore allows
all its lower sections to use a common lower level and the finite derivative
control in terms of the original seminorms. Apply each lower estimate there,
then take the finite union of original seminorm supports and the sum of the
constants. The norm triangle inequality bounds the Weyl sum. The assumed
half-modulus Xi comparison transfers the bound to the original ambient Xi.

This is a derived induction step for Waldspurger VI.3.1, conditional on the
lower packet estimates and the weighted identities. It produces exactly the
original weighted weak integral estimate with the original measures, height
and finite level seminorms.
