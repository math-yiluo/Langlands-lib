# Literal Levi Iwasawa factors and compact Weyl lifts

`StandardLeviIwasawaFactorsAt` states that intersecting the minimal parabolic
and the given compact with any actual standard Levi gives an Iwasawa pair in
that Levi. When the compact intersection with the associated parabolic is the
product of its intersections with the Levi and unipotent radical, projecting
it onto the Levi gives the literal Levi intersection. Applying this to the
projected ambient minimal Iwasawa factorization proves the proposition.

The special compact input used by the weighted weak expansion supplies the
parabolic factorizations and Weyl representatives. Given the minimal ambient
Iwasawa factorization, it therefore supplies the literal Levi factors. For
each admissible Weyl index choose a normalizer lift in the compact. The
published invariance of the conjugated Levi under change of lift proves the
required admissibility for the chosen representative. The resulting system
satisfies the weighted expansion's compact-lift condition.

This is a derived structural step for the use of Waldspurger V.1.1 and VI.3.1.
It uses the supplied special vertex compact and ambient Iwasawa factorization.
