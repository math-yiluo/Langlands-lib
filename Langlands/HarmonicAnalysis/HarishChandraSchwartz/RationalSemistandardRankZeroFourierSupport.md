# Finite support of the actual Fourier family in relative rank zero

`RankZeroFourierFiniteSupportStatement` gives finite support of the
actual Fourier product for every Schwartz function in relative
semisimple rank zero, from the rank-zero VIII.1.2 statement. It keeps
the data and measures of the published Fourier product. The field
and inducing representation carrier have independent universes.

At the full-group endpoint the normalized induction is equivalent
to the ambient pullback of the inducing representation. The image
of a subgroup through the full Levi equivalence is its literal Levi
intersection. Therefore the inducing representation has a nonzero
fixed vector there exactly when the normalized induction has a
nonzero fixed vector at the ambient level.

The proper Levi rank theorem makes the parabolic of every rank-zero
semistandard index the whole group. Semistandard uniqueness then
identifies every index with the full standard one. The fixed-vector
equivalence supplies the normal-level comparison. The rank-zero
VIII.1.2 input gives orbit finiteness below the rank bound one and
hence for every semistandard Levi model of the rank-zero group.
The published VIII.1.3 deductions give finiteness of the actual
compact-open fixed index set and then finite support of the Fourier
product.
