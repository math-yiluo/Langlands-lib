# Specializing the VI.3.1 induction hypothesis to strict lower rank

`StandardLeviPlancherelWavePacketAtRankStatement r` quantifies the exact published `StandardLeviPlancherelWavePacketSourceNormalizedStatement` over all ambient groups and maximal split tori of relative root rank `r` over the fixed nonarchimedean local field. The chamber, inducing Levi, split component, valuation-image finite-index proof, irreducible representation, quotient Haar measure, opposite continuations, unitary regularity, separate density and packet compacts, and matrix representation are all quantified. The field and representation carrier have independent universes. The body is the published joint membership and continuity target, not a new packet or topology.

`LowerPacket.lowerDynamicPacketChamberEstimates_of_rankIH` applies that family to a proper output face `I'`, assuming the joint statement at every smaller rank. The same inherited torus `Standard.leviTorusHom i ν (rootSet i ν I')` serves in the lower ambient group for every Weyl index. `StandardLeviNested.relativeRootRank_leviTorusHom_rootSet_lt` proves strictness from the missing simple-root label; changing to the generic chamber `lowerChamber` for an index does not change this torus or its relative root rank.

The proof applies `lowerDynamicPacketChamberEstimates_of_continuity`. For each index it takes the transported split component, its finite-index proof, the lower representation and its square integrability for `lowerChamberQuotientMeasure`, and the continuation and regularity fields of `lowerChamberPackage`. It installs the normal and Borel quotient instances used by the existing lower packet consumer. The restricted ambient matrix representation and the lower Iwasawa pair supplied by that consumer are the height and packet-compact input of the specialized joint target. Its continuity conjunct is precisely `LowerChamberPacketContinuity` at these data. Thus the resulting `LowerDynamicPacketChamberEstimates` concern the same lower weighted packets, with the transported normalization and no change to the original orbit measures.

This is a derived Lean step for Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques (d’après Harish-Chandra)*, J. Inst. Math. Jussieu 2 (2003), VI.3.1, printed p. 304 (registered source `waldspurger-2003-plancherel`). The source uses induction on the semisimple rank for the packets on `M'` associated with `M' ∩ w·P`; the rank-indexed universal proposition and its specialization are internal to the formalization. The theorem retains the source compact normalization, the lower minimal Iwasawa data, the supplied density packages, and the original upper valuation-index premise. It does not construct these packages or prove the joint target at any rank.
