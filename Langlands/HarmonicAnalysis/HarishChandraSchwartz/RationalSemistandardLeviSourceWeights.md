# The constant-term weights of the actual outer Levi model

`LeviModel.literalXi` transports the spherical function of the actual
Levi scheme to its literal point subgroup. The point homeomorphism is
the one induced by the inclusion of the Levi in the ambient group.
`constantTermEstimate_literalXi` gives the normalized constant-term
estimate with this weight and the restriction of the original closed
matrix height, and supplies the corresponding translation bounds.
It takes Iwasawa pairs for the two compact open subgroups at generic
cocharacters and assumes that the compact on the Levi model is the
preimage of the ambient compact. The estimate uses the modulus of the
literal unipotent radical with its supplied Haar measure.

The proof presents the parabolic as standard in a generic chamber.
The intrinsic minimal parabolic in the Levi model is the preimage of
the ambient minimal parabolic for that chamber. This identifies both
Iwasawa pairs because the compact on the model is the ambient trace.
The modular characters agree on the corresponding minimal parabolics,
so the spherical functions agree by the compact integral formula.
Changing the minimal parabolic leaves the spherical functions at the
fixed compacts unchanged. The standard constant-term and translation
estimates therefore give the claimed bounds for the original
semistandard parabolic and its Levi model.

Waldspurger I.1, printed pp. 240 and 242, fixes the compact and the
matrix height. The estimates are those of II.1.2 and II.4.5, with the
semistandard version of II.4.5 on printed p. 263. III.6, printed p. 272,
uses these weights for the actual Schwartz constant term.
