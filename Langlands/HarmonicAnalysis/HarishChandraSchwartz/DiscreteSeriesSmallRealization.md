# Discrete series in independent carrier universes

`DiscreteSeriesSmallRealizationStatement` gives an equivalent
representation in any prescribed carrier universe for each discrete
series class of a separable topological group. The subgroup and Haar
measure in the square-integrability condition are the original ones.
The proof uses the published small realization of an irreducible smooth
representation over a separable group. That realization chooses a basis
in a countable smooth orbit and reindexes it in the prescribed universe.
Square integrability transfers through the resulting intertwiner and
includes admissibility of the new representative.

`discreteSeriesCentralCoefficientSpace_eq_of_separable` identifies the
two actual coefficient sums in the same space of complex-valued
functions. For each summand, the intertwiner preserves the central
character and identifies the smooth coefficient space on any two
equivalent carriers. This gives each inclusion of submodules.

The universe comparison is a formalization step for the algebraic sum
of discrete-series coefficients of one split-central character in
Waldspurger VIII.4.2. For the application to VIII.5, the rational
point group is separable by its published second-countable topology,
so the sum can be used at the universe of the Fourier family.
[Waldspurger (2003), VIII.4.2 and VIII.5, printed pp. 330–332](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1474748003000082)
