# Fourier injectivity by rank at the source compact and measures

`RationalLocalSourceFourierInjectivityStatement` states the zero case of
Fourier inversion for the actual Harish-Chandra Schwartz space at a
special vertex compact of the chosen maximal split torus. The matrix
height comes from a closed embedding. Ambient Haar has mass one on the
compact. All compact pictures use that compact, and each split-central
quotient Haar has mass one on the image of its Levi trace. The parabolic
modular normalization is the actual one, and the square-integrable
spherical weight and tempered coefficient inputs are displayed. These
are the Fourier data in Waldspurger I.1 and VII.1, printed pp. 238–242 and
313–317.

For a bound `n`, `RationalStrictlyLowerRankSourceFourierInjectivityStatement`
requires this injectivity on every actual connected reductive group with
relative semisimple rank strictly less than `n`. The inherited special
vertex compact and restricted closed embedding give the same conditions
on an actual proper Levi. This matches the use of the zero case of
VIII.1 (2) in VIII.2.1, printed pp. 321–322. The specializations of the
existing injectivity proposition and its rank-zero proof supply the
base at this normalization.
