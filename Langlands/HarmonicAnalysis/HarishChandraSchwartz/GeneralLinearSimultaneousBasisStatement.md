# Simultaneous standard basis at the general linear integral compact

`GeneralLinearSimultaneousBasisStatement` specializes Renard V.5.2(i)–(iv)
to the actual rational points of \(\mathrm{GL}_N\). It takes the diagonal
torus and any generic cocharacter of that torus, so the positive system
is determined by that cocharacter. The compact is the integral-point
subgroup of the standard \(\mathrm{GL}_{N,\mathcal O_F}\) model.
The field can have either characteristic and the statement includes
every natural matrix rank.

The conclusion is the existing `SimultaneousStandardBasisAt`
proposition. It supplies a common basis of compact open subgroups
normal in the integral-point subgroup and its inherited bases on
the actual standard Levis. Each standard parabolic has a compatible
split component. At every member of the basis the ordered
opposite–Levi–radical decomposition and the contraction and cofinality
clauses are those of `StandardParabolicBasisMember`.

The specialization uses the congruence tower of the standard model.
Diagonal cocharacters with integer weights lift by using the same
weights over \(\mathcal O_F\).
