# Finite projected Schwartz levels on a compact central quotient

Waldspurger VIII.4.1 includes finite dimensionality of the central
projections of a fixed compact open level of the cuspidal Schwartz space.
`CompactQuotientCentralProjectionFiniteStatement` proves the finite
dimensionality of the actual central integral span for any bi-invariant
Schwartz subspace when the central quotient is compact. The compact
quotient hypothesis applies to the relative semisimple rank-zero case
once compactness of the quotient by the actual split component is supplied.

The central integral preserves bi-invariance and makes every projected
function covariant for the chosen character. Both properties pass to the
span. The image of the open level subgroup in the central quotient is
open, and the compact central quotient has finitely many left cosets
modulo it. The proof evaluates functions on representatives of these
cosets. Character covariance and right invariance make the evaluation
map injective, giving finite dimensionality. The existing central
convergence results can identify the span with the raw integral image
when the corresponding integrability is available.

J.-L. Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques
(d'après Harish-Chandra)*, J. Inst. Math. Jussieu 2 (2003), 235–333,
VIII.4.1, printed p. 327 (PDF p. 93).
