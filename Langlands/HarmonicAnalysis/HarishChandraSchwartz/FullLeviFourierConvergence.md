# The full-group double integral in the Fourier regularity argument

`FullLeviDoubleIntegrabilityStatement` treats the product integral
against the ambient measure and the existing compact Haar probability
at the full-group parabolic. It uses the actual compact diagonal
contraction of an induced product tensor, an integrable ambient
function and an admissible unitarizable inducing representation.

The proof uses the normalized product induction equivalence to
reduce to pure tensors. Evaluation at one identifies the two
induced factors with their full-Levi representations. Invariance
of the smooth dual pairing cancels the common compact translation.
The resulting integrand is the ambient function times a bounded
smooth coefficient, independent of the compact variable, so the
product integral is absolutely convergent.

This proves the full-group case of the double integrability input
to the Fourier regularity proof. In relative rank zero the same
case applies at each semistandard parabolic once the Schwartz
function is shown to be integrable.

[Waldspurger (2003), VII.1.2–VII.1.3, printed pp. 315–317](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1474748003000082)
