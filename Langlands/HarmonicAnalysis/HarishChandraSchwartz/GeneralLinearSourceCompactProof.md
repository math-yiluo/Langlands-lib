# The special-vertex realization of the standard general linear compact

`generalLinearSourceCompact` proves `GeneralLinearSourceCompactStatement`
for the actual standard model and its diagonal torus.

The diagonal inclusion commutes with base change by the matrix point
equivalences for the general linear group and the coordinate equivalences
for the split torus. The integral diagonal is therefore maximal on every
fibre, and its generic inclusion is the specified diagonal torus. The
fibrewise root datum and the integral root parametrizations produce a
`SplitIntegralPinning`. Its subgroup is generated by the integral root
points and the torus points whose characters have order zero. Each
generator belongs to the model's integral points. In the converse
torus condition for the pinning, the orders of the relative roots and
the restricted ambient characters together determine the torus valuation,
so the torus point belongs to the generating subgroup.

The proof transports the actual valuation building to the diagonal
torus and uses the pinning there as a `SplitIntegralFrame`. A uniformizer
has order one and its square differs from one, so the frame yields a
special point whose norm-one stabilizer is the generated subgroup. The
normalized-order comparison carries the point and its stabilizer to the
local-field apartment. The transported apartment axioms make this point
a vertex, also in rank zero.

The supplied-model special-stabilizer comparison gives a point of the
original reduced building whose norm-one stabilizer is the group of
integral points. Under the conjugate-apartment comparison, the frame's
special point has norm-one stabilizer equal to the frame subgroup. Each
member of this subgroup is integral and therefore fixes the point from
the supplied-model comparison. The fixed-point uniqueness theorem for a
special point in the reduced building identifies the two points. Their
norm-one stabilizers are consequently equal. Compactness, openness and
maximality among compact subgroups follow from the integral-model
theorems.

`generalLinearSourceCompactGeometry` retains the transported apartment
axioms and bounded-open stabilizer property. Its projection
`generalLinearSourceCompact` uses the order compatibility contained
in those axioms.
