# Transport of coefficient levels and weighted lower wave packets

Module `Langlands.HarmonicAnalysis.HarishChandraSchwartz.StandardLeviLowerPacket`, namespace `Langlands.HarishChandraSchwartz.LowerPacket`.

This is a derived implementation for the induction step of Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques (d'après Harish-Chandra)*, J. Inst. Math. Jussieu 2 (2003), 235–333, §VI.3, proof of Proposition VI.3.1, printed p. 304 (PDF p. 70). The source applies the induction hypothesis to packets in `M'` for `M' ∩ w·P`; it does not state the change-of-presentation lemmas below. The Library writes these lower packets using the dynamic cocharacter and states its continuity target using a standard Levi. To apply that target to the lower packets, this module compares the section spaces, measures and weights of the two presentations. `StandardLeviLowerPacketBound` specializes the comparison and obtains the bounds.

## Coefficient levels and their seminorms

For two continuous families of norm characters on the same Levi with the same common kernel, `representativeChange` reindexes the representatives of the unitary orbit without changing their underlying representations. `sectionChange` is the resulting linear equivalence of the unrestricted section spaces. Coherence is preserved because the comparison uses the same representations and the same intertwining relations.

The two finite tori of unitary coordinates need not be given in the same basis. `HasEvaluationUnitaryCoordinates` asks that each coordinate of the first chart be evaluation at a fixed Levi point. If the second chart admits the existing `HasLaurentUnitaryCoordinates` description, `exists_monomial_of_eval` obtains the integer exponents for the map from second to first coordinates by evaluating the second chart at those points. Under that comparison, the new compact-picture family is the original family composed with the resulting `finiteTorusMonomial`. The existing `mem_finiteSmoothFamilies_comp_monomial` gives preservation of finite smooth families. The map `levelChange` restricts the reindexing to each fixed coefficient level: invariance is checked at the same open subgroup, representative by representative. `exists_levelChange_seminorm_bound` uses `exists_orbitSectionLevel_monomialSeminorm_bound` to control each new derivative seminorm on the reindexed sections by a finite supremum of old ones. This is a one-way bound, not an identification of the global orbit topologies.

## The integral and the estimate

`inducedOrbitWavePacket_levelChange` assumes an orbit homeomorphism matching the representatives' classes and pushing the given orbit measure forward to the other one, and matching the two weights pointwise. It changes variables by that homeomorphism in the actual Bochner integral, then compares the coefficients on each point of the orbit via a representative of that point. The coefficient at a representative is unchanged by reindexing. No normalization or integrability assertion is introduced by this equality of integrals.

`exists_subgroupChange` allows the two inducing decompositions to be given with different subgroup presentations in the same ambient group. The Levi homeomorphism is the identity on ambient points, and the parabolic and radical subgroups agree. This gives equal Levi subgroups; after substitution, the comparison reduces to the fixed-subgroup results above. It retains the assumptions that the representations match by pullback, the norm kernels match, and the orbit homeomorphism matches the classes, measures and weights.

`LevelPacketBound` expresses a pointwise bound by a finite supremum of the coefficient level's seminorms, times `Ξ(g) (1 + σ(g))^{-r}`, for a fixed real order `r`. `exists_packet_bound_of_subgroupChange` derives it for old-coordinate sections from a **supplied** continuous synthesis map in the second presentation. The proof composes that map with the continuous level inclusion, bounds its Schwartz seminorm by finitely many new level seminorms and transfers each of those seminorms to the old level. `exists_finset_sup_comp_le` combines the finitely many transfer bounds by union of the index sets and sum of the nonnegative constants. The packet equality identifies the pointwise values.

## Lower-Levi data used in the specialization

* `mem_commonNormKernel_lowerChamberLeviEquiv` compares the norm kernels after the parameter-scheme isomorphism; the underlying pointwise norms commute with restriction of rational characters along the isomorphism. `hasEvaluationUnitaryCoordinates_levi` verifies that the dynamic chart is evaluated at lifts of basis vectors of the valuation image. The standard chart has the Library's Laurent property.
* `lowerChamberPackage` reads the supplied `LowerDensityPackage` as the density data for the standard-chamber inducing representation of `M'`. This is the same package by definitional equality, not a construction of continuations, convergence domains or regularity witnesses.
* `standardLeviPlancherelWeight_lowerChamberOrbitTransport` identifies the standard and dynamic weights without a scalar. On a parameter character each side is the same package weight evaluated at its restriction. The two restrictions agree by evaluating them on the same points using `standardLeviCharacterRestrict_eq_leviCharacterRestrict`. The equality descends to the orbit by surjectivity of its character parametrization. The necessary character and orbit measure comparisons come from `StandardLeviLowerSplitCenter`.

All lower results keep the field and representation-carrier universes independent. The module does not assert existence of lower density packages, the lower continuity hypothesis, or any ambient Schwartz membership or continuity theorem.
