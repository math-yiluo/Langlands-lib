# Xi estimates on a literal semistandard Levi

`SemistandardLeviXiEstimatesStatement` gives the square-weight integrability and the pure Xi bound on the original smooth coefficients of a square-integrable representation on the literal Levi. It uses the Levi scheme's minimal-parabolic Iwasawa data, a supplied split component, the simultaneous standard basis and finite Cartan cover on the Levi scheme, and the restriction of a closed ambient matrix model. The Levi Haar measure and the Haar measure on the representation's central quotient are arbitrary in their given normalizations.

The proof pulls both measures to the Levi scheme using the same point isomorphism on the Levi and its induced quotient isomorphism. The restricted matrix model gives the same height under this identification. The published Cartan square-weight proof applies on the scheme and a measurable change of variables returns the estimate on the literal Levi. The actual smooth pullback preserves square integrability for the pulled-back quotient Haar measure. The published square-integrable coefficient bound then gives the pure Xi estimate, which transfers to the original coefficients along the Levi isomorphism.

These are literal-Levi applications of Waldspurger II.1.5 and Corollary III.1.2, printed pp. 252 and 264, used on the Levi in VII.1.3, printed pp. 316–317.
