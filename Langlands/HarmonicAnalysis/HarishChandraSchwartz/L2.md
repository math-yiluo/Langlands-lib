# Inversion and the `L²` inner product on the Harish-Chandra Schwartz space

Lean module: `Langlands.HarmonicAnalysis.HarishChandraSchwartz.L2`, namespace
`Langlands.HarishChandraSchwartz`. It builds on
`Langlands.HarmonicAnalysis.HarishChandraSchwartz.Basic` (the seminorms `ν_r`, the weights
`weight Ξ σ r`, the levels `C_H`, the space `C(G)`, its LF topology and `Space.mkCLM`) and
`Langlands.HarmonicAnalysis.HarishChandraSchwartz.Convolution` (`enorm_le_nu_mul`,
`continuous_of_mem_levelSubmodule`, `Level.continuous_of_nu_le`, `ConvolutionWeights`).
The `L²` space is Mathlib's `MeasureTheory.Lp ℂ 2 μ`.

## Source

J.-L. Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques, d'après
Harish-Chandra*, J. Inst. Math. Jussieu 2 (2003), 235–333, DOI 10.1017/S1474748003000082.

- VII.1 (printed p. 313, PDF p. 79): for a function `f` on `G`, `ˇf(g) = f(g⁻¹)`, and
  "On a `ˇf ∈ C(G)`" (stated without proof). In the same paragraph: if `X` is a space with
  a measure and `f₁, f₂` are functions on `X` with `f̄₁ f₂ ∈ L¹(X)`, then
  `(f₁, f₂)_X = ∫_X f̄₁(x) f₂(x) dx`.
- Lemma VII.1.1 (ii) (printed p. 314, PDF p. 80) and the proof of Proposition VII.2.2
  (printed p. 319, PDF p. 85) use `(f₁, f₂)_G` for functions on `G` with at least one
  argument in `C(G)`; the latter compares `(f_ψ', f_ψ)_G` with `(f_ψ, f_ψ')_G` for wave
  packets `f_ψ`, `f_ψ'`.
- Lemma II.1.4 (`Ξ(g⁻¹) = Ξ(g)`) and Lemma II.1.5 (there is `d ∈ ℕ` with
  `∫_G Ξ(g)² (1 + σ(g))^{-d} dg < ∞`), printed p. 252, PDF p. 18.

Waldspurger does not state the inclusion `C(G) ⊆ L²(G)`, its continuity, or the
continuity of `(f₁, f₂) ↦ (f₁, f₂)_G` as numbered results. They are derived here: the
pointwise bound `|f(g)| ≤ ν_r(f) Ξ(g) (1 + σ(g))^{-r}` (definition of `ν_r`, III.6)
multiplied for `f₁` and `f₂` and integrated against II.1.5 gives integrability of
`f̄₁ f₂`, which is exactly the condition under which VII.1 defines `(f₁, f₂)_G`. The
remaining statements are formal consequences of this estimate, of III.6 (1) (a linear map
on `C(G)` is continuous when its restriction to every level `C_K` is continuous) and of
Mathlib's `L²` theory.

## Scope: generic weights and explicit hypotheses

As in `Basic`, the weights `Ξ σ : G → ℝ` are arbitrary. Every property of the source
weights that a result uses appears as a hypothesis of that result:

- `hΞ : ∀ g, Ξ g⁻¹ = Ξ g` and `hσ : ∀ g, σ g⁻¹ = σ g` for everything about `ˇf`. For
  `σ = height τ` (module `Height`), `height_inv` proves `hσ`. For `Ξ = xi hPK δ` (module
  `Spherical`), `hΞ` is Lemma II.1.4. It is not proved in `Basic`, `Spherical`,
  `Convolution` or this module, and for Waldspurger's `Ξ` it remains an open proof
  obligation.
- `hw : L2Weights μ Ξ σ` for everything about `L²` and `(f₁, f₂)_G`. Its fields are
  `pos : ∀ g, 0 < Ξ g`, `nonneg : ∀ g, 0 ≤ σ g`, and
  `square : ∃ d : ℝ, ∫⁻ g, ENNReal.ofReal (Ξ g ^ 2 * (1 + σ g) ^ (-d)) ∂μ < ⊤`. The field
  `square` is Lemma II.1.5 with a real exponent instead of a natural number (a weaker
  requirement). `ConvolutionWeights.toL2Weights` derives `L2Weights` from the hypothesis
  structure of Lemma III.6.1. For the source weights, `pos` is `xi_pos` (`Spherical`),
  `nonneg` is `height_nonneg` (`Height`), and `square` (II.1.5) remains an open proof
  obligation.

The measure `μ` is any measure on `G` (it plays the role of the Haar measure `dg`); no
invariance is used in this module. Results about `C(G)` as a whole assume
`Nonempty (CompactOpenSubgroup G)`, so that `C(G)` is the union of the levels `C_K`
(`mem_schwartzSubmodule_iff` in `Basic`). Injectivity assumes `μ.IsOpenPosMeasure`
(every nonempty open set has positive measure), which holds for a Haar measure.

## Definitions

- `check f g = f g⁻¹`, the function `ˇf` of VII.1, for any function `f : G → ℂ` on a
  group.
- `Level.check hΞ hσ K : Level Ξ σ K →ₗ[ℂ] Level Ξ σ K`, the map `f ↦ ˇf` on the level
  `C_K`.
- `Space.checkLinear hΞ hσ` and `Space.check hΞ hσ : Space Ξ σ →L[ℂ] Space Ξ σ`, the map
  `f ↦ ˇf` on `C(G)`, as a linear map and as a continuous linear map.
- `pairing μ f₁ f₂ = ∫ g, conj (f₁ g) * f₂ g ∂μ`, the pairing `(f₁, f₂)_G` of VII.1, for
  any measure `μ` on a measurable space. It is a Bochner integral, so its value is `0`
  when the integrand is not integrable; VII.1 defines `(f₁, f₂)_X` only when
  `f̄₁ f₂ ∈ L¹(X)`. For `f₁, f₂ ∈ C(G)` the integrand is integrable
  (`Space.integrable_conj_mul`), so the two agree.
- `L2Weights μ Ξ σ`: the hypothesis structure above (a `Prop`).
- `L2Weights.exponent hw`: the exponent `d` chosen from `square`, and
  `L2Weights.const hw = ∫⁻ g, ENNReal.ofReal (Ξ g ^ 2 * (1 + σ g) ^ (-d)) ∂μ`.
- `Level.toL2 hw K : Level Ξ σ K →ₗ[ℂ] Lp ℂ 2 μ`, the inclusion `C_K → L²(G)`.
- `Space.toL2Linear hw` and `Space.toL2 hw : Space Ξ σ →L[ℂ] Lp ℂ 2 μ`, the inclusion
  `C(G) → L²(G)`, as a linear map and as a continuous linear map.

## Results

The hypotheses are listed per group of results.

For a group `G` (no topology):

- `check_apply`, `check_check` (`ˇˇf = f`), `check_add`, `check_smul`.
- `IsBiInvariant.check`: if `f` is `H`-bi-invariant, so is `ˇf`.
- `weight_inv` (`hΞ`, `hσ`): `weight Ξ σ r g⁻¹ = weight Ξ σ r g`.
- `nu_check` (`hΞ`, `hσ`): `ν_r(ˇf) = ν_r(f)` for every `r` and every `f`.
- `check_mem_levelSubmodule` (`hΞ`, `hσ`): `C_H` is stable under `f ↦ ˇf`.
- `check_mem_schwartzSubmodule` (`hΞ`, `hσ`, any topology on `G`): `ˇf ∈ C(G)` for
  `f ∈ C(G)`; this is the assertion "`ˇf ∈ C(G)`" of VII.1.

For a topological group `G`, with `hΞ` and `hσ`:

- `Level.coe_check`, `Level.continuous_check`: `f ↦ ˇf` is continuous on `C_K`.
- `Space.coe_check`, `Space.check_check`: `Space.check` is the function `ˇf`, and it is
  an involution.

Pointwise estimate (`Ξ > 0`, `σ ≥ 0`, no group structure used):

- `enorm_mul_enorm_le`:
  `|f₁(g)| |f₂(g)| ≤ ν_{r₁}(f₁) ν_{r₂}(f₂) Ξ(g)² (1 + σ(g))^{-(r₁ + r₂)}`.

For a measurable space `G`, any measure `μ` and `hw : L2Weights μ Ξ σ`:

- `pairing_apply`: the defining formula of `pairing`.
- `L2Weights.const_lt_top`: `const hw < ⊤`.
- `L2Weights.lintegral_enorm_mul_le` (`r₁ + r₂ = d`, `ν_{r₁}(f₁)` and `ν_{r₂}(f₂)`
  finite): `∫⁻ |f₁| |f₂| dμ ≤ ν_{r₁}(f₁) ν_{r₂}(f₂) const hw`.

If moreover `G` is a topological space whose open sets are measurable
(`OpensMeasurableSpace G`):

- `L2Weights.integrable_conj_mul` (`f₁`, `f₂` continuous, `r₁ + r₂ = d`, `ν_{r₁}(f₁)`
  and `ν_{r₂}(f₂)` finite): `f̄₁ f₂` is integrable.
- `L2Weights.memLp_two` (`f` continuous, `ν_{d/2}(f)` finite): `f ∈ L²(μ)`.
- `L2Weights.eLpNorm_le` (same hypotheses): `‖f‖_{L²} ≤ ν_{d/2}(f) (const hw)^{1/2}`.

For a topological group `G` with a Borel measurable structure, a compact subgroup `K`
and any measure `μ`:

- `ConvolutionWeights.toL2Weights`: `ConvolutionWeights μ K Ξ σ` implies
  `L2Weights μ Ξ σ`.

For a topological group `G` with `OpensMeasurableSpace G`, any measure `μ` and
`hw : L2Weights μ Ξ σ`:

- `Level.memLp_two`: every element of `C_K` lies in `L²(μ)`.
- `Level.continuous_toL2`: the inclusion `C_K → L²(G)` is continuous.

If moreover `G` has a compact open subgroup:

- `Space.continuous` (no measure hypotheses): every element of `C(G)` is continuous.
- `Space.memLp_two`: `C(G) ⊆ L²(μ)`.
- `Space.integrable_conj_mul`: for `f₁, f₂ ∈ C(G)`, `f̄₁ f₂ ∈ L¹(μ)`, so
  `(f₁, f₂)_G` is defined in the sense of VII.1.
- `Space.coeFn_toL2`: `Space.toL2 hw f` is almost everywhere equal to `f`.
- `Space.inner_toL2`: the `L²` inner product of `toL2 hw f₁` and `toL2 hw f₂` is
  `(f₁, f₂)_G`.
- `Space.pairing_swap`: `(f₂, f₁)_G = conj (f₁, f₂)_G`.
- `Space.pairing_self`: `(f, f)_G = ‖toL2 hw f‖²`.
- `Space.continuous_pairing`: `(f₁, f₂) ↦ (f₁, f₂)_G` is jointly continuous on
  `C(G) × C(G)`.

If moreover `μ.IsOpenPosMeasure`:

- `Space.toL2_injective`: `C(G) → L²(G)` is injective.
- `Space.pairing_self_eq_zero`: `(f, f)_G = 0` if and only if `f = 0`.

## Proof notes

1. `ˇf`. Reindexing the supremum defining `ν_r` by `g ↦ g⁻¹` (`Equiv.iSup_comp` with
   `Equiv.inv G`) and using `hΞ`, `hσ` gives `ν_r(ˇf) = ν_r(f)`. If `f` is
   `H`-bi-invariant, then `ˇf(h₁ g h₂) = f(h₂⁻¹ g⁻¹ h₁⁻¹) = f(g⁻¹)`, so `ˇf` is
   `H`-bi-invariant. Hence each `C_H` is stable, and so is `C(G)`, their span. On `C_K`
   the map preserves every seminorm, so it is continuous by `Level.continuous_of_nu_le`;
   `Space.mkCLM` (III.6 (1)) gives the continuous linear map on `C(G)`.
2. `L²` estimate. Multiplying the bounds `|f_i(g)| ≤ ν_{r_i}(f_i) Ξ(g) (1 + σ(g))^{-r_i}`
   gives `enorm_mul_enorm_le`. Integrating with `r₁ + r₂ = d` gives
   `lintegral_enorm_mul_le`. Continuous functions are strongly measurable when open sets
   are measurable, which gives integrability of `f̄₁ f₂`. With `f₁ = f₂ = f` and
   `r₁ = r₂ = d/2` this gives `f ∈ L²` and the bound on `‖f‖_{L²}`.
3. Inclusion. Elements of a level are continuous (they are bi-invariant under a compact
   open subgroup) and have all `ν_r` finite, so step 2 applies. On `C_K` the norm of the
   image is bounded by a constant times `ν_{d/2}`, so the inclusion is continuous
   (`WithSeminorms.continuous_of_isBounded`). `Space.mkCLM` gives `Space.toL2`.
4. Inner product. Mathlib's `L2.inner_def` writes the inner product as
   `∫ conj (f₁ g) * f₂ g dμ` for almost-everywhere representatives, which agree with
   `f₁`, `f₂` almost everywhere (`MemLp.coeFn_toLp`). Symmetry, `(f, f) = ‖f‖²` and joint
   continuity then follow from the inner product space structure of `L²` and the
   continuity of `Space.toL2`.
5. Injectivity. If `toL2 hw f₁ = toL2 hw f₂`, then `f₁ = f₂` almost everywhere. Both are
   continuous, and two continuous functions that agree almost everywhere for a measure
   positive on nonempty open sets are equal (`Continuous.ae_eq_iff_eq`).
