# The common standard compact for general linear wave packets

`GeneralLinearPacketCompactStatement` specializes the structural clauses
of `HasSpecialPacketCompact` to the standard integral points of
\(\mathrm{GL}_N\). The ambient group, torus and standard Levis are the
actual rational-point objects. The positive system comes from a
cocharacter of the diagonal torus with strictly decreasing coordinates.
The result ranges over every nonarchimedean local field and natural
matrix rank.

The open subgroup in the conclusion has underlying subgroup exactly
\(\mathrm{GL}_N(\mathcal O_F)\). It is compact and satisfies
`WeakExpansion.IsSpecialVertexCompact`, which supplies the apartment
axioms, the bounded-open stabilizers and the norm-one stabilizer
comparison at a special vertex. At this same subgroup the proposition
gives the ambient parabolic products, the simultaneous normal compact-open
basis and the finite Cartan coverings for the ambient group and its
standard Levis.

Waldspurger I.1 uses a single special maximal compact for Haar
normalizations and Iwasawa factorization. The covering clauses specialize
I.1(4) and Renard V.5.1, and the compact-open basis specializes Renard
V.5.2. The apartment clauses are the geometric realization used by the
weak expansion.
