# Analytic inputs to the actual rank-zero Fourier family

`RankZeroFourierBoundsStatement` gives the ambient square-weight estimate
and the induced coefficient estimate for the actual Fourier family of a
connected reductive group in relative semisimple rank zero. It uses a
maximal split torus, a generic cocharacter and a compact open Iwasawa
subgroup. The first conclusion concerns the actual `Ξ` and matrix height
`σ`, for a closed realization and any ambient Haar measure.
The second gives `HasTemperedCoefficients` for the actual normalized
induction of a smooth representation square integrable modulo the
specified central subgroup of the literal Levi. It uses the displayed
semistandard compact picture, modular normalization and inverse-invariant
quotient Haar measure. The inducing carrier universe is independent of
the field universe.

The actual relative root set is empty in rank zero, so the minimal
radical is trivial and `Ξ = 1`. The square-weight proof uses compactness
of the actual common norm kernel and the published estimate obtained
by summing over the rational character valuation lattice. This gives
the rank-zero case of the estimate of II.1.5.

For the induced estimate, square integrability gives an invariant
positive form by the published proof of III.1.3. The compact Haar
construction gives an invariant positive form on the actual normalized
induction. Its admissibility follows from the compact picture and
admissibility of the inducing representation. The bounded smooth
coefficient theorem then gives the required growth in III.2 with
exponent zero, since `Ξ = 1`. This gives the temperedness input used by
the Fourier transform in VII.1.
