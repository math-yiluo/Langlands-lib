# Uniform threshold for the actual induced orbit

`rationalOrbitConstantTermThreshold_of_minimalLevi` proves the published
`RationalOrbitConstantTermThresholdStatement` for every original parameter
and independent field and representation universes. For each smooth section
it gives one positive depth for the normalized constant-term identity
throughout the unitary orbit and the full closed minimal-Levi chamber.

The literal unitary unramified twists cover the orbit. The range theorem
for the original coefficient map represents each section value by a tensor
of the corresponding normalized induction. The orbit bi-invariance theorem
provides one compact open subgroup for all those coefficients.
At each level in the simultaneous basis, the induced fixed-vector space
of a twist is linearly equivalent to the fixed-vector space of the
untwisted induction. The fixed-vector dimension of the latter gives the
common bound.

The two faces of the simultaneous basis give the lattice Casselman identity
at the rank-dependent depth. The compact dominant reduction and the uniform
threshold theorem for coefficient families then give the identity on the
full chamber using the simultaneous basis's compatible component. Both
this component and the original component admit standard constant terms
of each tensor coefficient. Comparing each term with the same dynamic
constant term along powers of the standard cocharacter evaluated at the
uniformizer identifies the two functions. The final identity uses the
original chosen standard constant term.

The threshold is the application of the Casselman identity
(Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques
(d'après Harish-Chandra)*, Proposition I.4.3, printed pp. 245–246)
and constant terms (Proposition I.6.2) in Lemma VI.2.3
(printed pp. 299–301).
