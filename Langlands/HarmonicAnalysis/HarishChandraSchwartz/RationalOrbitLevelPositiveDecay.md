# Uniform level bounds for actual positive constant terms

`RationalOrbitLevelPositiveDecay` states the exponential estimate for all
original positive constant-term values at each fixed input level `H ≤ K`.
At a given positive-part region parameter, one exponent and a finite
supremum of original derivative seminorms control every section at the
level and every orbit parameter. The witness in the proof uses only
zero-order seminorms.

The theorem `rationalOrbitLevelPositiveDecay_of_perSection` takes the
per-section decay of Waldspurger VI.2.3 for the displayed nonnegative
weight. The actual compact positive-term map is complex-linear and agrees
with the original positive part on a section's compact restriction. A
finite collection of fixed-level test sections realizes all basis vectors
of the finite-dimensional fixed compact space at every parameter. The
common rate is the minimum of their finitely many positive rates, with
`1` included to handle the empty basis. The finite coordinate bound
extends estimates on the basis to every section and supplies the original
seminorm witnesses. Surjectivity of the unitary parameter map makes the
result hold on the entire orbit.
