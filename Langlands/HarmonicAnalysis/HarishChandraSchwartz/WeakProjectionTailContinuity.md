# Continuity of the weak projection from a tail of translates

For a family with uniform split annihilators at a component point, the
weak part is continuous at a fixed group point if the full coefficient
at every sufficiently deep translate of that point is continuous in the
family parameter. This derived statement uses the polynomial separation
argument from Waldspurger VI.2.3 (printed p. 300).

For the difference at two parameters, the products of the respective
monic annihilators kill the weak and positive differences. Multiplication
of the positive annihilator by a power of the variable preserves the
uniform root bound and forces the projection polynomial to use only the
tail of the translates. The uniform coefficient bound on the projection
polynomial then bounds the weak difference by a fixed finite sum of full
coefficient differences. Continuity of those samples proves continuity
of the weak part.
