# Fixed-level derivative bounds for the weighted lower sections

`HasWeightedCSectionLevelControl` states that the given family of actual
weighted lower sections indexed by an original section level has a single
lower level. Inclusion recovers each supplied section. Every finite supremum
of derivative seminorms on that lower level is bounded by a constant times
a finite supremum of original derivative seminorms.

The accompanying theorem takes the regular weighted c-extension and the
published `IsWeightedCSection` formula for each member of the family. The
original level lies within the original compact picture. The inverse Weyl
transport of the unitary character coordinates is an integer monomial.
Indeed each original coordinate evaluates at a point of the inducing Levi,
and evaluation at the conjugate point is Laurent in the lower coordinates.
The weighted section formula at the inverse-conjugate character is therefore
the regular compact operator formula after this monomial change. The generic
fixed-level transfer theorem applies using the finite original compact fiber,
the common nonvanishing denominator and the polynomial numerator. This
proves the lower-level seminorm bounds used in the rank induction of
Waldspurger VI.3.1, conditional on the actual weighted section formula.

The regular extension can be fixed for the whole original level. The public
identity theorem gives equal unitary values for any two such extensions, so
a weighted section specified using one extension satisfies the same formula
with the other.
