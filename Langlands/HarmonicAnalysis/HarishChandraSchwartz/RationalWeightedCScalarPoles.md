# The density ratio in the weighted compact c-family

`cWeightedScalarAt` is the scalar in `cWeightedCompactFamily`. Its domain is `cWeightedDomain`, where the two actual package densities are regular and the lower density has nonzero value. It takes the value of the ambient density times the inverse value of the lower density.

`exists_weightedCScalarPolePresentation` concerns the original standard Levi scheme and the preimage `Ω` of the weighted domain’s interior under `leviRationalUnramifiedCharacterHomeomorph`. It assumes square integrability modulo the rational split component on the Borel quotient with its given Haar measure, and nonemptiness of `Ω`. It gives a fraction `a/b` for the density ratio on `Ω`, with `b` nonzero throughout `Ω`, and a `RankOneScalarPolePresentation` of that fraction. The presentation includes a global cross identity and a factorization of its denominator into powers of unit-circle basis-coordinate roots and a remainder nonzero on the unitary characters.

The proof uses `weightedCScalarPresentation` for the fraction on the original domain and `unramifiedRegularFunctionsEquiv_symm_apply` to read it on the Levi scheme. To construct the alternative fraction, it uses `IsFractionRing.num_den_reduced` to choose a reduced denominator for the ambient density. If the density is regular at a parameter, that denominator divides every denominator of a presentation there and is therefore nonzero at the parameter. Its unitary nonvanishing follows from `plancherelDensity_unitaryRegular` applied to the ambient package’s regularity field.

For the lower density, `lowerRepresentation_isSquareIntegrable` transfers the original quotient data. The split component is transported by `isSplitComponent_lowerParameterSplitComponent`, and `lowerDensityCanonicalScalarNonzero` proves nonvanishing of the exact lower scalar. The proof applies `hasDenseRepresentedGalleryProduct_of_squareIntegrable` to the opposite endpoints, then `exists_denseContinuedCompositeRankOneProduct_of_gallery` and `hasDenseRankOneProduct_of_continuedComposite` to obtain the product for the lower package’s continued scalar. `RankOneScalarPolePresentation.ofDenseRankOneProduct` supplies its factored denominator. The density formula `plancherelDensity` identifies the reciprocal density with this scalar divided by the positive density constant. The latter is positive by `plancherelDensityConstant_pos` and `rankOneGammaProduct_ne_top_of_isSplitTorus`.

The proof evaluates these alternative fractions by clearing both sides against a fraction regular at the parameter. This yields a cleared identity even at zeros of the alternative denominator. It multiplies the two alternative fractions after transport by `standardLeviParameterIso` and `lowerParameterIso`. The factors transport by `RationalUnitaryPoleFactorization.parameterPullback`. The resulting fraction and the original fraction have equal cross products on `Ω`, so `rationalUnramifiedRegular_eq_zero_of_isOpen` gives the global cross identity.
