# Finite level seminorms for bounds on compact fibers

`exists_levelFamily_bound_of_fixedBasis` gives a bound on every section at a
fixed level from a uniform bound on a basis of its fixed compact coefficient
space. The bound applies on an arbitrary evaluation region. Extend each
basis coordinate to the ambient compact picture to obtain finitely many
original zero-order seminorms. The compact restriction takes its values in
the fixed submodule, so expanding it in the basis and taking the triangle
inequality gives the result with the same evaluation weight.
