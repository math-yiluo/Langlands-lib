# Compact change for the standard-Levi weight and weak integral estimate

`standardLeviDensityPackageWeight` evaluates the existing standard-Levi
Plancherel weight directly from the fields of a dynamic density package.
The character-to-orbit map is surjective, and at the image of every unitary
character the two weights are evaluated after the same standard-Levi
restriction. The dynamic compact-change identity therefore multiplies the
weight on this standard-Levi orbit by the same positive constant as on the
character space.

The weak bound transfer uses the inverse of that constant. Each original
weighted integrand is this constant times the integrand at the changed
density normalization. Bochner integrability is preserved, and pulling the
constant through the integral gives a bound with the same finite family of
input seminorms and a rescaled nonnegative bound constant. Both estimates
use the identical section level, weak constant term, half modulus, matrix
height, and character Haar measure.

This derived comparison applies with separate packet and continuation
compacts. It permits the weighted expansion on a common special compact in
the proof of Waldspurger VI.3.1, then transfers the weak bound to the density
specified by the original standard-Levi packet.
