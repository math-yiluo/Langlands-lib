# Parameter continuity of actual weak constant terms

`RationalOrbitWeakTermParameterContinuityStatement` gives continuity at
all orbit parameters of the actual weak constant term of each original
orbit section evaluated at a fixed lower-Levi point. It retains the
Borel structure on the quotient and the stated basis and source-Levi
Cartan covering data. This is a derived continuity step for the use
of Waldspurger V.1.1 on the entire orbit in VI.3.1, printed p. 304.

The strict element selected by the standard pairing data comes from
a strict point of the split component. The published uniform split
annihilator theorem at that point follows from the actual upper Xi
bound. The full constant-term ray result gives continuous samples on
the tail of the ray through the evaluation point. The bounded polynomial
projection onto the unitary generalized weights uses a finite sum of
those samples in the estimate for differences, giving continuity for
the original weak projection.
