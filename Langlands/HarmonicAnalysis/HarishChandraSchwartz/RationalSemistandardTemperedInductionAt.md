# Tempered induction at the prescribed semistandard compact

`RationalSemistandard.TemperedInductionAtStatement` concerns the normalized
induction from an actual semistandard parabolic of a connected reductive
group over a nonarchimedean local field. It fixes a maximal split torus,
a generic cocharacter, a special compact with the minimal Iwasawa product,
and a closed matrix representation. The inducing representation is
irreducible admissible and square-integrable modulo the supplied central
subgroup of the literal Levi, with Haar measure on the central quotient and
its Borel measurable structure. The conclusion bounds every smooth matrix
coefficient of the induced representation by the spherical function of the
prescribed compact times a power of one plus the prescribed matrix height.

Waldspurger III.1.2, printed p. 263, supplies the coefficient bound for the
inducing representation. III.2.3, printed pp. 266–267, gives the tempered
bound for normalized induction. I.1 specifies the height attached to
a matrix embedding. Changing the minimal parabolic at the same compact
preserves the spherical function defined in II.1, as used after II.4.5.
The equivalence of heights for closed matrix representations gives the
bound at the original embedding from a compact-adapted embedding.
