# Uniform constant-term thresholds on the full minimal Levi

`HasUniformMinimalLeviConstantTermThreshold` records the normalized
constant-term identity for a family at one positive depth on the full
closed chamber of the minimal Levi. The depth is measured on the simple
roots outside the specified standard parabolic face.

`hasUniformMinimalLeviConstantTermThreshold_of_fixedRanks` obtains the
threshold for a family of admissible representations with a common open
subgroup of bi-invariance. At each level of the basis, the fixed-vector
dimensions are bounded uniformly across the family. The theorem takes
compact dominant reduction, a standard cocharacter of the face `J`, and
one compact-open normal basis adapted to the specified compatible split
components for both `J` and the empty face. The two adaptations supply
the stable pairing for `J` and the compression factorization for the
minimal parabolic.

Compact dominant reduction expresses a chamber point as a dominant
uniformizer point with two factors in a single compact subset of the
minimal Levi. Continuity of conjugation and compactness give one basis
level of bi-invariance for every coefficient translated by these compact
factors.

The height map on this compact subset has finite image because it factors
through the discrete character-valuation image. A common upper bound for
the simple-root shifts of the two compact factors therefore translates
the geometric fixed-dimension depth into a threshold for the original
point. The tensor Casselman identity applies to the translated coefficient
at the uniformizer point. The left and right Levi translation laws for
standard constant terms and multiplicativity of the half modulus give
the equality at the original point.

This is a derived transport of the Casselman identity in Waldspurger,
*La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques (d'après Harish-Chandra)*,
Proposition I.4.3 (printed pp. 245–246), to the full chamber used in
Lemma VI.2.3 (printed pp. 299–301).
