# Source-normalized synthesis from the weak bounds at the common compact

`StandardLeviPacketWeakBoundsAt` collects the original weighted weak
estimates at an auxiliary packet compact, for every proper output face
and every compatible split component. Each estimate uses the original
continuation data, source-normalized orbit measure, matrix height and
finite families of input seminorms.

The proof chooses the supplied common special compact with its basis and
Cartan covers. Its parabolic products give the ambient minimal Iwasawa
factorization. The special compact properties give the literal minimal
Iwasawa factors in every standard Levi and the Weyl representatives.
The hypothesis on weak bounds is applied at this compact.

The published exact constant-term threshold and positive decay theorems
supply the other two estimates. A finite basis of the real cocharacter
space gives a positive-definite seminorm by pulling back the norm of the
coordinate space. The proper-region, chamber and whole-group bounds are
then supplied by the published weak reduction. The existing synthesis
theorem yields the original joint membership and continuity conclusion.

This derived step of Waldspurger VI.3.1 has two explicit inputs, the
existence of the common special compact and the original weighted weak
bounds there. It preserves the original packet compact and scalar
continuation compact as independent parameters.
