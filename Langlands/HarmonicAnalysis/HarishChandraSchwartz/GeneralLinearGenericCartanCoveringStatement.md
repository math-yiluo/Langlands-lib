# Cartan coverings for any generic diagonal cocharacter of the general linear group

For each generic cocharacter of the diagonal torus of
\(\mathrm{GL}_n(F)\), the standard integral group gives the ambient
Cartan covering. Its intersection with each standard Levi for that
cocharacter gives the Levi Cartan covering. Each covering uses the
common uniformizer and the chamber defined by the corresponding simple
roots.

The proposition uses the covering interfaces `FiniteCartanCovering`
and `StandardLeviCartanCovering`, also used by
`GeneralLinearCartanCoveringStatement` for the reference ordering of
diagonal weights. Here the generic cocharacter may give any ordering.
The field can have either characteristic, and the matrix size includes
zero and one.

The coverings are consequences of Renard's Cartan decomposition in
V.5.1(4) and its inheritance in V.5.1(5), using the uniformizer section
of V.3.23. See David Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs
p-adiques*, French edition dated 9 July 2026, printed pp. 117 and 122
(PDF pp. 123 and 128).
