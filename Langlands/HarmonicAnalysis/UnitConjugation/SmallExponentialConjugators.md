# Small analytic conjugators for products of exponentials

Let `F` be a characteristic-zero field with a nontrivial ultrametric norm and `A` a complete ultrametric normed `F`-algebra. `SmallExponentialConjugatorsStatement` gives two unit-valued families on a small closed pair ball in `A`. The exponential of the sum of the two conjugated inputs is the ordered product of the original exponentials. Each unit differs from one by at most a constant times the input norm. The inverse norms are bounded by `max 1 ‖1‖`, and the unit and inverse families are uniformly Lipschitz.

The positive scalar exponential radius bounds the inverse factorial coefficients by evaluating the multilinear terms on ones. A sufficiently small nonzero scalar then meets the factorial bound of the formal straightening theorem. Evaluate its unit series on the inputs divided by this scalar. The geometric estimate on the series with constant term removed gives the distance from one. Evaluation of the inverse series gives the inverse norm and its Lipschitz estimate. Evaluation of the formal exponential identity gives the asserted product formula, with the scalar on the exponent cancelling the rescaling of the inputs. The argument uses ultrametricity and works also in residue characteristic two. [Boyarchenko and Sabitova, *The orbit method for profinite groups and a p-adic analogue of Brown's theorem*, §§2.3–2.4](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0608126v1)
