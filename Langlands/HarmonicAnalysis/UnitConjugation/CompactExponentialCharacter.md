# Characters on small compact unit groups in exponential coordinates

`CompactExponentialCharacterStatement` assumes `ExponentialProductStraighteningStatement` for a proper ultrametric normed algebra `R`, finite dimensional over a complete, nontrivially normed ultrametric field of characteristic zero. There is a fixed factor `c ≥ 1` and a sufficiently small upper bound on the positive radius `r`. A finite sum of distinct locally constant additive characters on the ball of radius `r` is the character in exponential coordinates of a smooth finite-dimensional complex representation of the norm-unit subgroup. The sum must be invariant under conjugation by units of distance at most `c r` from `1`.

The finitely many additive characters are trivial on one sufficiently small additive congruence subgroup. They descend injectively to characters of the same finite quotient. `exponentialCongruenceQuotient` identifies this additive quotient with the corresponding unit quotient as a set. `additiveCongruencePairEquiv` descends the product change of variables. Its compatibility with exponentiation gives the required multiplication identity, and its two conjugation formulas preserve the character sum. Exponential equivariance also makes the sum a class function in the unit quotient. The finite character theorem constructs the representation there, and inflation gives smoothness since the kernel level is open.

The argument realizes invariant orbit sums through central idempotents. The use of a larger group for the invariance condition allows the same implication at residue characteristic two. [Boyarchenko and Sabitova, *The orbit method for profinite groups and a p-adic analogue of Brown's theorem*, Theorems 2.6 and 2.8 and Proposition 2.7(b)](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0608126v1)
