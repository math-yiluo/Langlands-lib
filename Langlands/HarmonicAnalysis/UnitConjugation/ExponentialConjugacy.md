# Exponential coordinates on the saturation of the convergence ball

Let \(A\) be a finite-dimensional complete normed algebra over a proper, nontrivially normed
characteristic-zero ultrametric field \(F\). `ExponentialConjugacyStatement` asserts that
the exponential is injective on the union of the algebra-automorphism images of its
convergence ball. In particular, if the exponentials of two elements of the ball are
conjugate by a unit, the same unit conjugates the original elements.

Compactness of the closed unit ball of \(F\) gives a finite cover by balls of radius \(1/2\).
Two distinct nonnegative integers belong to one ball of this cover, so their positive
difference \(p\) has norm less than one. Natural multiples of an element stay in the
exponential convergence ball. The identity for exponentiating commuting sums gives
\(\exp(nX)=\exp(X)^n\) there by induction. An equality \(e(\exp X)=\exp Y\) for an
algebra automorphism \(e\) therefore gives equality of the exponentials of \(p^m e(X)\)
and \(p^m Y\) for every \(m\). For large \(m\), both arguments are in a single injective
chart at zero. Cancelling the nonzero scalar \(p^m\) gives \(e(X)=Y\). Applying this
argument to the relative automorphism comparing two images proves the statement.

This supplies the injectivity needed to extend local exponential coordinates by
conjugation in the passage to the Lie algebra. See Clozel, *Characters of nonconnected,
reductive p-adic groups*, §3.4, p. 155.
