# An invariant clopen domain of exponential coordinates

`InvariantExponentialDomainStatement A` asserts that `x ↦ exp x − 1` is an open embedding on a conjugation-invariant clopen neighborhood of zero in the normed ring `A`. The theorem proves this for finite-dimensional normed algebras over a nonarchimedean local field of characteristic zero.

Take a sufficiently small nonzero scalar `a`. The domain consists of the `x` for which the characteristic coefficients of left multiplication by `a⁻¹ • x` are integral. This is an invariant clopen neighborhood by continuity of the lower coefficients and their invariance under conjugation. An adapted basis for each left multiplication operator puts its matrix entries in the integer ring. In these coordinates the operator norm of left multiplication by `x` is at most `‖a‖` by the ultrametric inequality for finite sums.

In each fixed basis, the left regular representation is a closed embedding. Its image intersects the small matrix ball in a domain on which `exp − 1` is an isometric self-bijection. These charts give continuity and openness on the whole coefficient domain. To prove injectivity, use one common reference basis. Every point of the domain has a matrix there which is an algebra-automorphism image of a matrix in the exponential convergence ball. Injectivity on the saturation of that ball gives injectivity in the original algebra.

This proves the clopen exponential chart assertion used in semisimple descent. [Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, §3.4, p. 155](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)
