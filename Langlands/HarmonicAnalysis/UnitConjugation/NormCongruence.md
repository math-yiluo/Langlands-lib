# Small congruence subgroups in an ultrametric normed algebra

For an ultrametric normed ring `R` and `0 ≤ r < 1`, `normUnitCongruence` consists of the units `u` with `‖u − 1‖ ≤ r`. Multiplication preserves the bound by expanding the product at `1`. The inverse bound follows from `y = −(x + xy)` for `x = u − 1` and `y = u⁻¹ − 1`, since `‖x‖ < 1`. The subgroup is open for positive `r`, compact when `R` is proper, and the positive-radius subgroups form a neighborhood basis at the identity when `R` is complete.

`NormUnitConjugationDisplacementStatement` bounds `‖u x u⁻¹ − x‖` by `max(1, ‖1‖) r ‖x‖`. The proof factors the difference as `((u − 1) x − x (u − 1)) u⁻¹` and applies the inverse bound and the ultrametric inequality. When the displacement coefficient is less than one, conjugation preserves the norm and every additive norm ball. These estimates apply to the ambient algebra and the fixed algebra in semisimple descent.

Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, Canadian Journal of Mathematics 39 (1987), §3.6, printed pp. 158–160, uses small compact open subgroups, the bounded displacement of their adjoint actions and preservation of norms. The norm-ball construction here gives these properties before choosing exponential coordinates.
