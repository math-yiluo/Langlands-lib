# Additive character orbits of compact unit groups

For a self-dual bicharacter `e` on the additive group of a topological ring, the frequency `z` defines `latticeAddChar he L z` on an additive subgroup `L` by `x ↦ e(x,z)`. Two such characters coincide exactly when their frequency difference lies in the annihilator of `L`. `latticeCharacterOrbit` is the set of characters associated to the conjugates of a frequency `z` under a subgroup `K` of the units.

`latticeCharacterOrbit_sum` proves that for compact `L` and `K` this orbit is a finite nonempty set of locally constant additive characters. Since `L` is compact, its annihilator is open. The image of `K` in the discrete quotient by this annihilator is compact, hence finite. If `K` preserves `L` and `e`, precomposition by conjugation permutes the finite set of characters, giving invariance of the sum.

`normUnitOrbitCharacter` applies this to a sufficiently small norm ball of radius `r` in a proper ultrametric normed algebra, finite dimensional over a complete, nontrivially normed ultrametric field of characteristic zero. Given exponential product straightening and a conjugation-invariant bicharacter, the orbit sum under the conjugating subgroup of radius `c r` is the character in exponential coordinates of a smooth finite-dimensional complex representation of the unit subgroup of radius `r`. The proof uses norm preservation for the larger group, then applies `compactExponentialCharacter` to the finite invariant sum. [Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, §3.5, Theorem 4](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3), [Boyarchenko and Sabitova, *The orbit method for profinite groups and a p-adic analogue of Brown's theorem*, §2.4](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0608126v1)
