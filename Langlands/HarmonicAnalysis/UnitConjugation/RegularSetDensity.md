# Density of the regular set over a nontrivially normed field

`RegularSetDensityStatement` says that `regularSet F A` is dense in a
finite-dimensional associative algebra over a nontrivially normed field, with
continuous addition and scalar multiplication on the algebra. The field and
algebra universes are independent. Harish-Chandra uses density of the regular
set in the proof of Corollary 2.3 of *Admissible invariant distributions on
reductive p-adic groups* (AMS, 1999), printed p. 15, when intersecting it with an
open image of semisimple conjugation.

For a point \(x\) in a nonempty open subset, choose any regular element \(y\).
The proof restricts the generic characteristic-polynomial coefficient at the
Lie algebra rank to the line \(x+t(y-x)\). In a basis, substitute the affine
coordinate polynomial of each coordinate into the multivariable coefficient.
Univariate evaluation at \(t\) commutes with this substitution, and the
resulting polynomial is nonzero at \(t=1\) because \(y\) is regular. Its root set
is finite. The continuous line map takes a neighborhood of \(0\) into the open
subset, and every such neighborhood in the field is infinite. Some parameter
therefore gives a regular point in the open subset.
