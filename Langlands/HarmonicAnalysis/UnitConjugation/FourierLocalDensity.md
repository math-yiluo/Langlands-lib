# Local density from Fourier agreement

Let `D` and `S` be distributions on a locally compact second-countable additive group with a self-dual bicharacter, compact open exhaustion, and additive Haar measure `μ`. The statement `FourierRestrictionDensityStatement` assumes that the Fourier transform of `D` agrees with `S` on Schwartz–Bruhat tests invariant under the annihilator of an additive subgroup `L`. If the Fourier transform of `S` is represented by a locally integrable function for `μ`, the statement gives a locally integrable function representing `D` on every test supported in `L`.

The proof `fourierRestrictionDensity` reflects a test `f` supported in `L` and Fourier transforms the reflection. This transform is invariant under the annihilator because the reflection is still supported in `L`. Applying the agreement hypothesis to that transform and using Fourier inversion gives `c D(f) = Ŝ(f ∘ (-id))`, where `c` is the positive inversion constant for `μ`. If `θ` represents `Ŝ`, the new function is `x ↦ c⁻¹ θ(-x)`. Negation preserves additive Haar measure and carries compact sets to compact sets, so the function is locally integrable.

The theorem `exists_density_on_subgroup_of_fourierAgreement` applies the argument to the additive group of a finite-dimensional semisimple algebra over a characteristic-zero nonarchimedean local field. For an invariant `S` supported in the closure of the conjugation saturation of a compact set, `LieAlgebraFourierRegularityStatement` is the premise supplying the density of `Ŝ`. The agreement hypothesis here is the conclusion needed from the Howe restriction argument. When `L` is a lattice, the test identity gives a density on an open neighborhood of zero.
