# Centralizers of idempotents

The module `Langlands.HarmonicAnalysis.UnitConjugation.IdempotentCentralizer` studies the
centralizer `M = Subalgebra.centralizer F {e}` of an idempotent `e` in an `F`-algebra `A`. With
`f = 1 - e`, `M = e A e ⊕ f A f`, and `A = M ⊕ N` with `N = e A f ⊕ f A e`. For a
finite-dimensional semisimple `A` over a characteristic-zero local field, `Mˣ` is the centralizer
in `Aˣ` of the split torus with points `{t e + s f : t, s ∈ Fˣ}`. Indeed, the torus contains
`1 + e`, and each element of `Mˣ` commutes with `e` and `f`. The group `Mˣ` is also the Levi
factor of `Aˣ ∩ (M ⊕ e A f)`, which becomes the block upper triangular parabolic after splitting
`A` over an algebraic closure. The module proves that `M` inherits semisimplicity, that it is
smaller than `A` when `e` is not central, and that regular elements of `A` lying in `M` are regular
in `M` (Harish-Chandra's `g'`, `regularSet`).

All declarations are in the namespace
`Langlands.HarmonicAnalysis.UnitConjugation.IdempotentCentralizer`.

## Semisimplicity

For a ring `A` and an idempotent `e` (`he : IsIdempotentElem e`):

- `bimoduleModule he : Module (A × A) A`: the module structure `(a, b) • x = a x e + b x (1 - e)`
  on the additive group of `A` (`bimodule_smul_def`); `bimodule_diag_smul_one`:
  `(x, x) • 1 = x`, so `A` is generated by `1`.
- `bimodule_end_apply`: every endomorphism `φ` of this module is right multiplication by
  `φ 1`; `bimodule_end_one_mem`: `φ 1` commutes with `e` (compare `φ e = (e, e) • φ 1` with
  `φ e = (1, 0) • φ 1`).
- `mem_centralizer_singleton_iff`: `x ∈ Subalgebra.centralizer F {e} ↔ e * x = x * e`.
- `rightMulEnd F he m`: right multiplication by `m ∈ M`, an endomorphism of the module
  (`rightMulEnd_apply`).
- `endRingEquiv F he`: the ring isomorphism `φ ↦ op (φ 1)` from the endomorphism ring of the
  module to the opposite ring `Mᵐᵒᵖ`, with inverse `m ↦ rightMulEnd F he m.unop`.
- `isSemisimpleRing_centralizer F he`: if `A` is a semisimple ring, so is `M`. The ring `A × A`
  is semisimple, so the module, generated by `1`, is semisimple and finitely generated; its
  endomorphism ring is semisimple (Mathlib's `IsSemisimpleRing.moduleEnd`), hence so are
  `Mᵐᵒᵖ` and `M` (`isSemisimpleRing_mulOpposite_iff`).

## Dimension

- `finrank_centralizer_lt F hc`: for finite-dimensional `A` and `e` failing to commute with some
  `a` (`hc : ∃ a, e * a ≠ a * e`), `finrank F M < finrank F A`, since `a ∉ M`
  (Mathlib's `Submodule.finrank_lt`).

## Regularity

For a field `F`, an `F`-algebra `A` and an idempotent `e`:

- `charpoly_eq_mul_of_isCompl`: for an endomorphism `φ` of a finite-dimensional vector space
  preserving two complementary subspaces `P` and `Q`, `charpoly φ` is the product of the
  characteristic polynomials of the restrictions (conjugate `φ` by
  `Submodule.prodEquivOfIsCompl` and use Mathlib's `LinearMap.charpoly_prodMap`).
- `diagonalProjection F e : A →ₗ[F] A`, `x ↦ e x e + (1 - e) x (1 - e)`
  (`diagonalProjection_apply`), and `offDiagonal F e`, its kernel `N` (`mem_offDiagonal_iff`).
  `idempotent_mul_rules` lists the products of `e` and `1 - e`;
  `diagonalProjection_of_commute`: the projection fixes the elements commuting with `e`;
  `commute_diagonalProjection`: its values commute with `e`; `diagonalProjection_idem`: it is
  idempotent; `diagonalProjection_mul_left`, `diagonalProjection_mul_right`: it commutes with
  left and right multiplication by elements commuting with `e`.
- `isCompl_centralizer_offDiagonal he`: `M` (as a subspace, `Subalgebra.toSubmodule`) and `N`
  are complementary.
- `adjoint_mem_centralizer`, `adjoint_mem_offDiagonal`: for `Y ∈ M`, `ad Y`
  (`adjointEndomorphism F Y`, `x ↦ Y x - x Y`) preserves `M` and `N`.
- `offDiagonalAdjoint F e : M →ₗ[F] Module.End F N`: `Y ↦ ad Y` restricted to `N`, a linear
  family of endomorphisms (`offDiagonalAdjoint_apply`).
- `charpoly_adjointEndomorphism_eq_mul he Y`: for `Y ∈ M`, `det(t - ad_A Y)` is the product of
  `det(t - ad_M Y)` (the adjoint endomorphism of the algebra `M`) and of the characteristic
  polynomial of `offDiagonalAdjoint F e Y`; `natTrailingDegree_charpoly_adjointEndomorphism`:
  the multiplicities of the root `0` add up.
- `offDiagonalAdjoint_injective`: `ad e` is injective on `N` (it is `1` on `e A f` and `-1` on
  `f A e`).
- `exists_regular_offDiagonal_invertible`: over an infinite field, for finite-dimensional `A`,
  some `Y ∈ M` is regular in `M` and has `0` as a root of multiplicity `0` of the characteristic
  polynomial of `offDiagonalAdjoint F e Y`. The coefficient of `t^{ℓ_M}` in the characteristic
  polynomial of `ad_M` of the generic element of `M` (Mathlib's `LinearMap.polyCharpoly`,
  `ℓ_M = LieAlgebra.rank F M`) is a nonzero polynomial (`LieAlgebra.polyCharpoly_coeff_rank_ne_zero`),
  and so is the constant coefficient of the characteristic polynomial of
  `offDiagonalAdjoint F e` of the generic element, since it does not vanish at `e`. Their
  product is nonzero, so it does not vanish at some point of `M` (`MvPolynomial.funext`).
- `mem_regularSet_centralizer he X hX`: over an infinite field, for finite-dimensional `A`, if
  `X ∈ M` is regular in `A`, it is regular in `M`. With `Y` as above and `p_A`, `p_M`, `p_N` the
  multiplicities of the root `0` of the three characteristic polynomials (so `p_A = p_M + p_N`),
  `ℓ_A ≤ p_A(Y) = p_M(Y) = ℓ_M`; and `ℓ_A = p_A(X) = p_M(X) + p_N(X) ≥ p_M(X) ≥ ℓ_M`, so
  `p_M(X) = ℓ_M` (`mem_regularSet_iff`, `lieAlgebraRank_le`).

Neither semisimplicity of `A` nor characteristic zero is used in the regularity results.
