# Exponential coordinates on finite congruence quotients

Let `R` be a proper ultrametric normed algebra over a nontrivially normed ultrametric field of characteristic zero. `ExponentialCongruenceQuotientStatement` states that on every sufficiently small positive-radius norm ball `B` the exponential induces a bijection from the additive congruence quotient of `B` to the multiplicative congruence quotient of its norm-unit subgroup `K`. The radius `r` of `B` satisfies `r < 1` and `max(1, ‖1‖) r < 1`. Both congruence relations use the same radius `s`, with `0 < s < 1`. The unit quotient is finite.

For the multiplicative relation, `unitCongruenceLevel_normal` uses norm preservation under conjugation by `K`. The quotient classes of `u` and `v` agree exactly when the distance between their algebra values is at most `s`, since right multiplication by a unit within distance less than one of `1` preserves the norm. The additive relation has the same distance criterion. `exponentialCongruenceQuotient` applies the isometry of the exponential minus one to identify the two relations, then descends the exponential bijection. Compactness of `K` and openness of the smaller subgroup give finiteness.

`additiveCongruencePairEquiv` also descends an isometric bijection of `B × B` to the square of the additive quotient. The product distance is the maximum of the two coordinate distances, so the pair relation is preserved in both directions. These descent statements supply the finite quotients for the product-fiber calculation in exponential coordinates.
