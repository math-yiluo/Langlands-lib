# Frequency matching on intersections of norm congruence subgroups

Let \(A,B\) be proper finite-dimensional ultrametric normed algebras
over a proper nontrivially normed ultrametric field of characteristic
zero. Use second-countable topologies, compact open exhaustions, and
Borel structures on the algebras and their units. Let \(i:A\to B\) be an
algebra homomorphism and \(p:B\to A\) a continuous additive homomorphism
adjoint to it for the given self-dual bicharacters. At the chosen small
norm radii, assume the image of the source ball under \(i\) lies in the
automorphism saturation of the target exponential convergence ball.

`normOrbitIntertwining` applies to two smooth finite-dimensional
representations with the given finite exponential character sums. Each
sum is over the characters obtained by conjugating a representative
frequency by a specified subgroup and restricting to the ball. A
nonzero intertwiner from the first type to the second on the actual
norm-unit subgroup intersection gives a target frequency \(x\) and a
source frequency \(y\) with \(p(x)=y\). Each frequency is in the orbit
of its representative modulo the annihilator of its own ball.

The exponential intersection chart transports Haar and inversion.
Choose one conjugated representative for each character in each sum.
Compact character orthogonality then gives a pair of representatives
whose projected difference belongs to the sum of the projected target
annihilator and the source annihilator. Subtract the target correction
from the target frequency and add the source correction to the source
frequency. The two corrected frequencies satisfy the projection equality.
This is the norm congruence form of the frequency matching in Clozel's
Lemma 9. [Clozel 1987, §3.6, printed p. 158](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)
