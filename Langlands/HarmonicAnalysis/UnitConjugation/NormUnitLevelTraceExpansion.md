# Finite compact character expansion of a congruence indicator

Let \(F\) be a complete, nontrivially normed characteristic-zero ultrametric
field and \(R\) a finite-dimensional proper ultrametric normed \(F\)-algebra.
Assume the stated second-countability, compact open exhaustion and Borel
measure structures, and a conjugation-invariant self-dual bicharacter on \(R\).
Under exponential product straightening, `normUnitLevelTraceExpansion` gives
a finite sum of smooth finite-dimensional characters on each sufficiently small
norm-unit subgroup \(K_r\) equal to a nonzero integer multiple of the indicator
of \(K_r\cap K_s\) for each \(0<s<1\).

Each character in the sum is an enlarged congruence group orbit sum in
exponential coordinates. Its representative frequency is in the annihilator
of the radius-\(s\) additive level. The larger group preserves both balls by
the small-conjugator norm estimate. The additive level indicator therefore
decomposes into its orbits, and the compact character construction realizes each
such sum. The exponential chart is onto \(K_r\), and the isometry of
\(\exp X-1\) gives the same radius-\(s\) membership condition in the two
coordinates.

This is the finite smoothing expansion for the compact interaction in Clozel,
*Characters of nonconnected, reductive p-adic groups*, §3.4, Lemma 5, p. 154
and §3.5, pp. 156–157.
