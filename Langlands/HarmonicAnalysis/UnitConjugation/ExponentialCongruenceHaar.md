# Exponential coordinates and Haar measure on small congruence subgroups

For a proper ultrametric normed algebra over a nontrivially normed ultrametric field of characteristic zero, with the Borel structures on the algebra and its units, `ExponentialCongruenceHaarStatement` gives an exponential homeomorphism from a sufficiently small additive norm ball onto the corresponding unit congruence subgroup. The pullback of additive Haar along the coordinate `u ↦ u − 1` is left Haar on that subgroup. The exponential preserves measure for the additive Haar on the ball and this Haar normalization on the unit subgroup.

The unit coordinate is an open embedding with image the ball, since an element of norm strictly below one can be added to the identity to obtain a unit. In the additive coordinate, left translation by `k` sends `x` to `x + (k − 1)x + (k − 1)`. The added term has Lipschitz constant at most the congruence radius. The Haar transport theorem for small ultrametric perturbations shows that left translation preserves the restricted additive measure. Finiteness on compact sets and positivity on open sets follow from the open embedding. Composing the small-ball exponential isometry with the inverse coordinate gives the asserted exponential and transports the measures.

Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, Canadian Journal of Mathematics 39 (1987), §3.6, proof of Lemma 9, printed p. 158, uses Haar preservation under exponentiation on small compact subgroups when comparing the character inner product with the additive Fourier pairing.
