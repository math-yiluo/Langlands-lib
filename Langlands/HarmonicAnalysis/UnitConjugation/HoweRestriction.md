# Invariant cutoffs in the Howe restriction argument

Let `A` be a topological ring and `L` a compact open additive subgroup. For a set `B` of coset centers, take the space of unit-conjugation-invariant distributions that vanish on the tests `1_(x+L)` for `x ∈ B`. Suppose its image under restriction to the lattice-invariant tests is finite-dimensional. Suppose there are invariant clopen sets `U n` that eventually contain each compact set. Each `U n` must be contained in the closure of the conjugation saturation of a compact set, and membership in `U n` must be constant on every coset with center in `B`.

`HoweRestrictionFromCutoffsStatement` proves that each distribution in this space agrees on all `L`-invariant tests with an invariant distribution supported in the closure of the conjugation saturation of one compact set. Multiply tests by the characteristic function of `U n` to obtain the cutoff distribution. Conjugation equivariance and constancy on the specified cosets preserve membership in the vanishing space. The cutoff distribution agrees eventually on each test, since the test has compact support. The evaluations on individual tests span a finite-dimensional subspace of the dual of the restriction space. A finite set of these evaluations suffices to choose one cutoff with equality on the entire space.

This is the cutoff step of Corollary 11.8 in the Howe restriction argument. The finite-dimensionality and geometric cutoff hypotheses enter explicitly. [Harish-Chandra, *Admissible invariant distributions on reductive p-adic groups*, §11.2, pp. 60–62]
