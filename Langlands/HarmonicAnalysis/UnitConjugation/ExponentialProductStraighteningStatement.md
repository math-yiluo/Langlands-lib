# Straightening products in small exponential coordinates

`ExponentialProductStraighteningStatement` concerns a complete ultrametric normed algebra `A` over a nontrivially normed ultrametric field `F` of characteristic zero. It asks for constants `c ≥ 1` and `ε > 0` with `c ε < 1`. For every positive radius `r ≤ ε` there is an isometric bijection `Φ` of the product of the closed ball `L` of radius `r` at zero with itself. If `Φ(x,y) = (x',y')`, then `exp(x' + y') = exp(x) exp(y)`. Each of `x'` and `y'` is conjugate to the corresponding original coordinate by a unit whose distance from `1` is at most `c r`.

The statement adapts the Campbell–Hausdorff change of variables for invariant convolution. The two conjugating Lie series have no constant term. A bound on the coefficients of each homogeneous term and a submultiplicative ultrametric norm therefore bound the two conjugators by a constant times `r`. The displacement of the pair map has terms of degree at least two, so shrinking the ball gives a strict contraction for the displacement and an isometric bijection for the pair map. The stated radius allows conjugators in a larger ball, including when halves occur in the Campbell–Hausdorff series at residue characteristic two. [Boyarchenko and Sabitova, *The orbit method for profinite groups and a p-adic analogue of Brown's theorem*, Lemma 2.3 and §§2.3–2.4](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0608126v1)
