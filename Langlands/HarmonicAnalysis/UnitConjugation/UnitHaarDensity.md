# Haar measure in additive unit coordinates

Let `A` be a second-countable locally compact Hausdorff topological ring with totally disconnected unit group. Assume the value map from units is an open embedding and use the Borel structures on both spaces. For additive Haar measure `μ`, `UnitHaarDensityStatement` gives a locally constant positive function on the unit group whose product with `μ.comap Units.val` is left Haar measure on the units. The value at `g` is the inverse of the modulus of the additive action of `g` by left multiplication on the additive group. This is a chart comparison for applying the submersion integration formula with group Haar measure in additive coordinates. Harish-Chandra, *A submersion principle and its applications*, §3, p. 97.

The proof chooses a compact open subgroup of the units. Left multiplication by a member preserves the subgroup image in the algebra, whose additive Haar mass is positive and finite. The additive modulus is therefore one on this subgroup and constant on its left cosets. A left translation by `g` scales the additive chart measure by the inverse modulus. At the same time, translating the weight to the point `g⁻¹x` multiplies it by the modulus of `g`, so the factors cancel. The weight is continuous and positive, which gives finiteness on compact sets and positivity on nonempty open sets for the weighted measure.
