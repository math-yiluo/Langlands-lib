# Isometric straightening of products in small exponential coordinates

`ExponentialStraighteningFromConjugatorsStatement` turns the quantitative estimates on two analytic unit conjugator families into the isometric straightening in `ExponentialProductStraighteningStatement`. Each conjugated coordinate is its input plus a displacement. The displacement is the commutator of the unit minus one with the input, multiplied by the inverse unit. Expanding the difference of two displacements and applying the ultrametric triangle inequality bounds its Lipschitz constant by a uniform constant times the ball radius.

Shrink the balls so the Lipschitz constant is less than one. The displacement vanishes at zero, so adding it preserves each ball. A difference of two outputs is the sum of the input difference and a term of strictly smaller norm, which proves isometry. To obtain a preimage of a point in the ball, subtract the displacement from that point and apply the contraction fixed point theorem on the complete ball. The pair ball in the maximum norm is isometric to the product of the two coordinate balls. The conjugator identities give the product exponential equation there. [Boyarchenko and Sabitova, *The orbit method for profinite groups and a p-adic analogue of Brown's theorem*, §§2.3–2.4](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0608126v1)
