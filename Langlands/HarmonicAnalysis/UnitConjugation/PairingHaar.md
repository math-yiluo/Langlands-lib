# Additive Haar preservation by conjugation

Let `A` be a finite-dimensional algebra over a nonarchimedean local field `F` with its module topology, topological ring structure and Borel σ-algebra. A nondegenerate invariant bilinear pairing gives measure preservation of unit conjugation for every additive Haar measure on `A`. The conclusion `PairingHaarPreservationStatement` is the measure invariance used with the pairing in Lie-algebra Fourier analysis. Harish-Chandra, *Admissible invariant distributions on reductive p-adic groups*, Introduction, printed pp. 1–2.

Conjugation `e` is the linear equivalence given by left multiplication by the unit and right multiplication by its inverse. The pairing identifies `A` with its dual so that the conjugate of `e` on the dual composed with the dual map of `e` is the identity. This follows by evaluating the maps against a vector and using invariance of the pairing. The determinant of a dual map equals the original determinant, and the same is true of conjugation by a linear equivalence. Thus `det(e)` has square one. The scalar Haar modulus is multiplicative and nonnegative, so its value at `det(e)` is one. The linear change of variables for additive Haar gives the measure-preserving identity.
