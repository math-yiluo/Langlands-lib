# Conjugation orbit expansion of a lattice level

Let \(A\) be a locally compact second-countable topological ring with compact
open exhaustion and a Borel measurable space. Let \(e\) be a self-dual
bicharacter, \(L\) a compact additive subgroup and \(N\) a compact open
additive subgroup. Suppose the group of units \(H\) preserves \(L\), \(N\)
and \(e\) under conjugation. Then `latticeLevelOrbitExpansion` writes the
indicator of \(N\) restricted to \(L\), multiplied by a nonzero natural
number, as a finite sum of the \(H\)-orbit character sums on \(L\).
Each representative frequency belongs to the annihilator of \(N\).

Conjugation acts on the finite group of restricted characters by precomposition
with inverse conjugation on \(L\). Invariance of the bicharacter identifies
this action with conjugation of the frequencies. Stability of \(N\) keeps the
frequencies in its annihilator. The finite character sum of the level then
splits into a sum over the orbits of this action.

This supplies the regrouping of the additive lattice indicator for the compact
smoothing in Clozel, *Characters of nonconnected, reductive p-adic groups*,
§3.4, Lemma 5, p. 154 and §3.5, pp. 156–157.
