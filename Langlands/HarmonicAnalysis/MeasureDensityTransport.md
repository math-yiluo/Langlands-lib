# Transport of densities by measurable maps

`map_withDensity_comp` pushes a measure with a pulled-back density through a measurable embedding. On the target, the pushforward has the prescribed density with respect to the pushforward of the original measure.

For a measurable equivalence `e`, `map_withDensity_equiv` transports any source density `w` to the target density `w ∘ e.symm`. Both lemmas are in the namespace `Langlands.RankinSelberg.GLDeterminantDensity`.
