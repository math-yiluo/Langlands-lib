# Fourier restriction to a subgroup and its annihilator

Use the Schwartz–Bruhat Fourier operator for a self-dual bicharacter and a fixed additive Haar measure on a locally compact second-countable abelian group with `CompactOpenExhaustion`. If the Fourier transforms of two distributions agree on every test invariant under translation by the annihilator of a subgroup `L`, `Distribution.eq_of_fourierAgreement_on_annihilator` proves that the original distributions agree on tests supported in `L`. On the distribution side the Fourier convention is precomposition with `bicharacterFourierSB`.

For a test supported in `L`, translating its frequency by an element of the annihilator leaves the Fourier integrand unchanged wherever the test is nonzero. The reflected test is still supported in `L`, so its transform is an invariant test on which the assumed agreement applies. Fourier inversion applied to this reflected test gives a common positive multiple of the original test. Cancelling that scalar proves the restriction identity. This is the Fourier step that turns a restriction to tests invariant by a dual lattice into agreement on the original lattice. [Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, §3.4, p. 155](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)
