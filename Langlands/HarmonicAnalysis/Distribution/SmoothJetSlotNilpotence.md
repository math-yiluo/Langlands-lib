# Nilpotent action on multilinear jet slots

A real continuous linear endomorphism whose `m`th power is zero defines an action on degree `k` multilinear derivatives. It acts by inserting the endomorphism in one slot at a time and summing. The resulting complex-linear endomorphism, multiplied by any complex scalar, has power `k*m + 1` equal to zero.

An individual insertion has `m`th power zero because repeated insertion applies the original powers in that slot. Insertions at different slots commute because the coordinate updates are independent. The binomial vanishing theorem for commuting nilpotents gives the uniform power bound by induction over the slots.
