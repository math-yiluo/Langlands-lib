# Leading full jet of a derivative along a vanishing field

If a smooth vector field vanishes at a point and all derivatives of a function below degree `k` vanish there, the degree `k` derivative of the field applied to the function is the sum of inserting the derivative of the field into each slot of the degree `k` derivative.

The proof uses the family obtained by composing the function with the affine perturbation `y ↦ y + tV(y)`. A cutoff equal to one near the point gives a common compact support for the family and its smooth divided differences. Evaluation of a spatial jet is continuous on that compact test space. The divided differences therefore justify differentiating the evaluated jet in time. The composition rule with vanishing lower derivatives gives a multilinear evaluation in the perturbed slots. Differentiating this multilinear evaluation gives the sum of slot insertions.
