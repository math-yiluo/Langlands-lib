# Extension of punctured eigen-distributions on compact supports

`NormalEigenDistributionExtensionStatement` concerns a family of distributions on an open product domain with its zero section removed. The family is holomorphic in one complex variable on each test. On a fixed compact set it has a common bound by finitely many test derivatives measured against a power of the normal norm. A smooth vector field and its scalar correction vanish on the zero section. The full derivative of the field is nilpotent there. Each punctured distribution is an eigenvector for testing against that corrected derivative, with the same nonzero eigenvalue.

The proof multiplies the field and the scalar correction by a compact cutoff that equals one on the fixed support. Fréchet differentiation and the continuous bilinear evaluation and multiplication maps then give a continuous endomorphism of the fixed-support test space. A single power of that endomorphism flattens every test through the required order. Taylor's inequality and the weighted normal cutoff bound control the derivatives of each punctured cutoff by the seminorm of the flattened test, uniformly in the cutoff scale.

The normalized limit of the punctured evaluations gives the extended value. Linearity follows from uniqueness of limits. Continuity of the field power allows its seminorm to be bounded by a single finite-order seminorm of the original test. Holomorphy follows from the uniform cutoff limit. When the test support avoids the zero section, the cutoff is eventually the identity on the differentiated test and the eigenvalue normalization recovers the original evaluation. The same formula agrees with any already defined eigen-distribution having the same punctured restriction and compact order at most the order of flattening. In that case the full-domain evaluation of the omitted cutoff tends to zero by its compact bound.

This is an internal local extension lemma for the Bruhat strata in the real Rankin–Selberg proof. Its estimate and convergence are on a single fixed compact test support.
