# Real linear coordinate changes on complex tests

A continuous real linear equivalence between normed spaces gives a continuous complex-linear pullback on their smooth compactly supported complex tests. Its value is ordinary composition, and its topological support lies in the inverse image of the original topological support.

Apply the existing continuous pullback on real tests to the real and imaginary parts. Recombine them into the complex pullback. The resulting pointwise formula proves complex linearity. The inverse image of the original topological support is closed and contains the support of the composed function, so it also contains its closure.
