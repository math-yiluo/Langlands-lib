# Recovering a local density from pushforward of tests

Let `X` and `Y` be locally compact Hausdorff totally disconnected spaces with their Borel measurable structures, with `Y` second countable. Suppose `μ` and `ν` are finite on compact sets and positive on nonempty open sets, with `ν` σ-finite. Let `p : X → Y` be continuous and let `P` be a linear test pushforward for these measures. `PushforwardLocalDensityStatement` states that, if `D ∘ P` is represented by a locally integrable function on an open neighborhood `U` of `x`, then `D` is represented on an open neighborhood of `p x` by a `ν`-integrable function. The two representations are tested on Schwartz–Bruhat functions supported in their respective neighborhoods.

Choose a compact clopen neighborhood `K` of `x` in `U`. On each sufficiently small compact clopen target set, the value of `P(1_K)` is constant. Testing on the characteristic function of that set shows that the constant is real and nonnegative. Since the compact clopen sets generate the Borel σ-algebra, `P(1_K)` is the density of the pushforward of `μ` restricted to `K`. Its value at `p x` is nonzero, since every open neighborhood of `p x` has preimage meeting the open set `K` in a set of positive `μ`-measure. Thus on some open neighborhood of `p x` the density is a fixed positive constant `a`. Pushing `1_K · (g ∘ p)` forward gives `a · g` for tests `g` supported in that neighborhood. Radon–Nikodym gives an integrable target density for the pushforward of the restricted source density, and division by `a` gives the desired representation of `D`.

This is the implication from local integrability of the descended distribution to local integrability of the original distribution in semisimple descent. [Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, §3.4, pp. 155–156](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)
