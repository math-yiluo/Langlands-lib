# Transpose invariance of real general linear distributions

On real matrix space, subtracting the transpose pullback from a conjugation-invariant distribution gives a conjugation-invariant transpose-odd distribution. The full-matrix spectral descent theorem gives vanishing of this difference, hence transpose invariance on the full matrix space.

For a test on the open general linear group, a compact-support determinant cutoff extends the distribution to the full space and preserves its conjugation invariance. The cutoff commutes with ordinary transpose. Full-space invariance therefore gives the exact open-group statement of Aizenbud and Gourevitch, arXiv:0803.3395v7, Corollary 7.6.3 (p. 22) for positive real matrix sizes.
