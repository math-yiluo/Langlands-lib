# Local vanishing from leading coefficients in a product chart

A full-space distribution can be expressed in a smooth open chart
and then restricted to an open product inside its source. The
resulting functional is continuous. Its support condition follows
by transporting each test to the original space. Restriction to a
smaller product agrees with restriction to that product directly.

If the coordinate distribution is zero on an open product, the
original distribution vanishes on the image of that product.
Pull back a test supported in the image through the forward chart.
Its support lies in the product, and pushing it through the inverse
chart gives the original test.

Suppose that in the larger product the distribution vanishes off
the zero section. In a smaller open product contained in a compact
subset of the larger product, the distribution has finite
transverse order. If at every such order its leading coefficient
is zero, the order descends through zero and the distribution
vanishes on the smaller product's chart image. This result is
internal and leaves the leading-coefficient vanishing to the
application.
