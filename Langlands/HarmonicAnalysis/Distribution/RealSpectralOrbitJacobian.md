# The orbit Jacobian at a fixed real spectral slice point

At a semisimple slice point \(A+z\) the chart derivative sends an orbit coordinate \(v\) to the commutator of \(Jv\) with \(A+z\). If the tangent image there is the original orbit tangent, this block becomes an endomorphism of the orbit coordinate space. It is injective because the full chart derivative is invertible. Finite dimension makes the block an isomorphism.

An ambient linear equivalence fixing the slice point and preserving the tangent induces a local chart transition. The chain rule intertwines the derivative of the transition with the ambient equivalence via the chart derivative. On orbit directions, the inverse of the orbit block transports the ambient tangent action back to the chart. This proves that the orbit block of the transition is conjugate to the restricted ambient action and has the same determinant.

For conjugation by a matrix unit centralizing \(A\), and for adjusted transpose from a unit conjugating \(A\) to its transpose, the tangent determinant has absolute value one. Both actions fix the semisimple slice, so their orbit Jacobians at its points with unchanged tangent image have absolute value one.

These internal consequences of the slice derivative and tangent determinant formulas supply the Jacobian at the center for the local orbit integral comparison.
