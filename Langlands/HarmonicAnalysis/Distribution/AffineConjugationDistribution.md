# Constant orbit derivatives in affine conjugation coordinates

For a unit affine frame `G(x) = 1 + J x`, differentiation along a constant coordinate direction `u` has generator `J u * G(x)⁻¹`. If the derivative of the base matrix in that direction is zero, the derivative of its conjugate is the commutator with this generator.

For a conjugation-invariant real matrix distribution, use a fixed real matrix basis to expand the smooth generator. Each basis commutator has trace zero and its distribution derivative vanishes. The chart normalization theorem shows that the normalized distribution annihilates the constant coordinate derivative. In stratum coordinates the base matrix depends only on the slice and normal coordinates, so the identity applies to the orbit directions.

These identities are auxiliary to the real matrix transpose argument in Aizenbud and Gourevitch, *Generalized Harish-Chandra descent and applications to Gelfand pairs*, §7.6.
