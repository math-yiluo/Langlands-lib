# Leading jets under smooth weights and fiber integration

A smooth scalar multiplier acts on the first potentially nonzero derivative of a smooth function by its value at the point. Apply the iterated derivative norm bound to the multiplier minus that value. Each term vanishes by the zeroth derivative of this difference or by a lower derivative of the function.

For a weighted pullback fixing the zero normal coordinate at the image point, combine that identity with the transverse chain rule. The leading derivative is pulled back by the normal component of the derivative and multiplied by the value of the weight.

`productFiberNormalJet` takes the transverse derivative after integrating an independent product coordinate. Its evaluation is the integral of the corresponding derivatives of the original slices. These identities apply to the leading coefficient after pulling back the Jacobian-normalized distribution tests and integrating the orbit variables.
