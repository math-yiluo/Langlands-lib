# Signed ambient covariance and normal determinants for real spectral charts

The adjusted transpose on all real matrices is ordinary transpose followed by conjugation by the inverse of a fixed real matrix unit. Its absolute determinant is one, using the determinant of conjugation and the absolute determinant of transpose. Pullback by adjusted transpose applies the transpose pullback to a conjugated test. A conjugation-invariant distribution odd under transpose therefore evaluates the adjusted-transpose pullback as the negative of its original value.

If a matrix unit centralizes the chart center, its conjugation preserves the orbit tangent and fixes the polynomial semisimple directions. If the unit sends the center to its transpose by conjugation, its adjusted transpose has the same two properties. The two actions have absolute determinant one on the orbit tangent. The normal determinant identity then gives unit absolute determinant for their induced actions on the normal quotient represented by the chart complement.

These internal lemmas combine the ambient test actions and determinant identities with the normal quotient construction for the real spectral chart.
