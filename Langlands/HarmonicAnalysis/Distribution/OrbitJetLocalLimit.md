# The limit of transported shrinking orbit averages

A smooth kernel on an open set has a smooth extension agreeing with it on any compact subset. The extension is the original kernel multiplied by a smooth cutoff equal to one on the compact subset and supported inside the open set. The continuous family built from the extension agrees pointwise with the original orbit average whenever the sampled points lie in the compact subset wherever both tests contribute.

For the transported leading-jet kernel, suppose the zero target orbit coordinate above each point of the slice test's support belongs to the chart target. Compactness supplies a common closed orbit ball above that support within the target. The shrinking orbit test then gives a continuously extended family of slice tests. At sufficiently small positive dilation the family is the weighted leading jet integrated on the source fiber, by Haar scaling and the orbit change of variables. Its value at zero is the transport kernel at the zero target orbit coordinate applied to the slice test. The zero value formula uses a normalized orbit test and a complete output space for the Bochner integral.

This internal limit allows the covariance identity for ordinary distribution tests to pass to the leading transverse coefficient.
