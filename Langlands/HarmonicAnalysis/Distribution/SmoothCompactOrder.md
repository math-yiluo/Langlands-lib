# Finite derivative bounds for smooth distributions

`smoothDistributionCompactOrder` chooses a finite derivative order for the restriction of a smooth distribution to tests supported in a fixed compact set in its open domain. The bound is a positive constant times the maximum of the fixed-support derivative seminorms through that order. The inclusion of the fixed-support test space is continuous, so the seminorm given by the norm of the restricted distribution is continuous. The topology generated by the derivative seminorms bounds it by a finite subfamily, all of whose orders are at most their maximum.

The accompanying flatness estimate concerns smooth functions with vanishing derivatives through order \(m\) at a point \(a\) and a uniform bound \(M\) on derivatives through order \(m+1\). If \(j+r\le m+1\), then the norm of the \(j\)-th derivative at \(b\) is at most \(M\lVert b-a\rVert^r\). Induction on \(r\) uses the mean value bound on the closed ball about \(a\) containing \(b\) and the norm identity between the derivative of an iterated derivative and the next iterated derivative.
