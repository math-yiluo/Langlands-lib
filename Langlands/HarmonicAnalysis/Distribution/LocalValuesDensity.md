# Local values of a locally integrable distribution density

Let the underlying space be Hausdorff, locally compact, totally disconnected and second countable, with its Borel measurable structure. Consider a nonempty family of measures and distributions with a common locally integrable density. Assume each measure is finite on compact sets and positive on nonempty open sets. Suppose at each point of an open set `R` the distributions have a common local value relative to their measures, in the sense of integration on tests in a neighborhood of that point.

`RegularValuesDensityStatement` gives a density for the same family that is locally constant at every point of `R`. The representative equals the prescribed local value on `R` and retains the original values on its complement. Uniqueness of local values for one measure gives local constancy on `R`. For each supplied measure, the original and modified densities have equal integrals on the compact clopen subsets of a sufficiently small compact clopen neighborhood of each point of `R`. Compact clopen sets generate the Borel σ-algebra. The equality extends to all measurable subsets of the neighborhood and implies almost-everywhere equality there. Second countability gives a countable cover of `R` by these neighborhoods, which proves almost-everywhere equality for each measure on the whole space.

This permits using the regular local values and local integrability as inputs to the same character representative. [Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, Theorem 1, p. 153](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)
