# Transport between smooth coordinate tests and full-space tests

A continuous linear equivalence gives a continuous pullback on
full-space smooth tests. The construction uses the corresponding
open partial homeomorphism with full source and target.

For an open chart with smooth inverse, a test on the chart source
defines an ambient test by inverse-chart pullback followed by
inclusion into the full test space. Its support lies in the image of
the original support under the chart. Evaluating a full-space
distribution on this ambient test gives a distribution in the
coordinates. The support inclusion transfers vanishing to
coordinate subsets whose images lie in the original vanishing set.

If a full-space distribution is covariant under a continuous linear
equivalence, then two coordinate tests inherit that covariance
provided their ambient representatives are related by this pullback.
It suffices to check the relation on the first chart and show that
the pulled-back support lies in its target. The representatives
then agree off the target by vanishing.

This internal construction lets local tests use the covariance of the
original distribution before taking leading transverse coefficients.
