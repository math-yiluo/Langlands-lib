# Pushforward of Schwartz–Bruhat tests

For a point map `p : X → Y` and measures `μ` on `X` and `ν` on `Y`, `IsTestPushforward μ ν p P` says that the complex-linear map `P` on test functions satisfies `∫ (P f) g dν = ∫ f (g ∘ p) dμ` for all source and target Schwartz–Bruhat tests. This is the characterization of the pushforward used in the submersion principle. Harish-Chandra, *A submersion principle and its applications*, §3, p. 97.

In semisimple descent, if `γ` is semisimple and `U_M` is the open subset of its connected centralizer on which the conjugation map `(x,m) ↦ xγmx⁻¹` is submersive, the pullback of a distribution on the image is its precomposition with the test pushforward. This is the product distribution used in the fiber integration step. [Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, §3.4, p. 154](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)

For Borel measures finite on compact sets, suppose `p` is continuous, the target is Hausdorff, locally compact and totally disconnected, and its measure is positive on nonempty open sets. The theorem `exists_testPushforward_of_compactClopen` constructs `P` if the image of `μ` restricted to every compact clopen subset of `X` is given by a compactly supported locally constant nonnegative density for `ν`. It decomposes each source test into the weighted indicators of its nonzero level sets. Each level set is compact clopen, and local constancy and compact support make the number of levels finite. The sum of the corresponding weighted densities gives a target test. Injectivity of the locally constant density embedding gives uniqueness against all target tests, which lets integral linearity give the linear map.

For a Hausdorff, locally compact, totally disconnected target and a target Borel measure finite on compact sets and positive on nonempty open sets, `IsTestPushforward.pullback` uses measure-preserving homeomorphisms of the source and target that intertwine `p`. Changing variables in the two integrals gives the equality of all test integrals. The same density injectivity gives equality of tests.

The theorem `exists_testPushforward_of_additiveSubmersion` takes finite-dimensional normed spaces `V` and `W` over a proper nontrivially normed field. The source `V` is ultrametric and the target `W` is locally compact and totally disconnected. Use additive Haar measures `μ` on `V` and `ν` on `W` with their Borel structures. The map is continuous on the open subset `U` of `V` and has a surjective strict derivative at each point of `U`. The measure on `U` is `μ.comap Subtype.val`. The inclusion sends a compact clopen subset of `U` to a compact clopen subset of `V`. For its characteristic function, extend the point map by zero outside that subset and apply `compactSubmersionPushforward` with a constant parameter. The inclusion transports the resulting measure identity back to `U`.
