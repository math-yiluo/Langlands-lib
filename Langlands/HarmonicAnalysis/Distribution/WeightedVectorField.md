# Infinitesimal covariance for weighted vector fields

`eval_weightedVectorFieldDerivativeTest` combines a finite family of
infinitesimal covariance laws on real tests with complex distribution
values. The field is a smooth weighted sum of the individual directions.
The scalar correction is the sum of the directional derivatives of their
weights. When the weighted real eigenvalue factors sum to one, the shifted
operator has the common complex eigenvalue.

For each summand the proof applies the original covariance law to the
weighted test. The product rule supplies the correction, and the finite
sum of the real eigenvalue multipliers acts as the identity on the test.
The companion trace formula freezes all weights at a point. In the
product rule each difference from the frozen derivative is a rank-one
operator whose trace is the corresponding directional derivative of the
weight. This internal formula is used in the real Bruhat charts.
