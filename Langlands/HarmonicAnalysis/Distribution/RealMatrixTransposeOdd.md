# The transpose-odd part of a real matrix distribution

The full-space test pullbacks by ordinary transpose and by general
linear conjugation agree with their pullbacks by continuous linear
equivalences. In particular, transpose acts continuously on the
space of smooth tests. For a distribution \(D\), the distribution
`transposeOddDistribution D` is the difference between \(D\) and
its transport by transpose.

Transposition is an involution, so the difference changes sign
under it. Transposition intertwines conjugation by \(g\) with
conjugation by \((g^{-1})^T\), so the difference inherits conjugation
invariance. The difference vanishes exactly when \(D\) is transpose
invariant.

This internal reduction expresses the matrix distribution problem
as vanishing of conjugation-invariant distributions that transform
by the sign of transpose.
