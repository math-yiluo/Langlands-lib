# Covariance of the leading coefficient after orbit integration

Assume a product distribution factors through an orbit integral and satisfies a local covariance law. For each test supported in the target of the chart action there is a weighted pullback supported in its source, with the prescribed scalar covariance. The chart action and its inverse preserve the zero-normal orbit fibers.

The proof takes a symmetric leading slice coefficient test and extends it to a full test using the factorial-normalized polynomial in the normal variable. The support of the slice test permits small orbit and normal windows in the chart target. Insert a shrinking normalized orbit test in this window. Lower transverse jets vanish for the resulting target test, its weighted pullback, and the integral of the pullback. The leading jet of the integrated pullback is the continuous family from the orbit change of variables.

The covariance identity on full tests therefore gives an identity for the slice coefficient along the family for sufficiently small positive dilations. Continuity passes the identity to zero, where the family equals the transported kernel at the zero target orbit coordinate. This internal argument supplies covariance without requiring the whole fixed product to lie inside a chart action's overlap.
