# Support and transverse order in Jacobian-normalized product charts

For a smooth chart between finite-dimensional real normed spaces, its normalized distribution on an open product in the source evaluates tests after multiplication by the reciprocal of the chart Jacobian. This multiplication preserves the inclusion of the test support in any prescribed set. Ambient vanishing on a set therefore implies vanishing of the normalized restriction on any set whose intersection with the product maps there.

Restricting further to a smaller product commutes with normalization because the pointwise multiplier is the same on both test spaces and test inclusion preserves the underlying functions. Multiplication by the Jacobian recovers each unnormalized evaluation. Consequently a zero normalized restriction gives a zero unnormalized restriction, to which the chart image vanishing theorem applies.

In a product containing the center, first choose a compact neighborhood of the center in the product. Then choose an open product around the center in the interior of that compact set. If the normalized distribution on the larger product vanishes off the zero section, its restriction to the smaller product has the transverse order given by the compact distribution order on the larger product. This uses the restriction identity and the finite transverse order theorem for distributions supported on the zero section.

These are internal consequences of smooth chart transport, the Jacobian normalization identity, and the local theorem on transverse jets. They provide support and order bounds for local coefficient arguments in product charts.
