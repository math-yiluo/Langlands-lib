# Smooth test pullback and distribution transport in open charts

An open partial homeomorphism between real normed spaces with a smooth
forward map pulls real tests back from the target to the source. The
pullback evaluates the original test on the chart image and extends by
zero off the source. For a test whose support lies in a compact subset
of the target, the inverse image of that compact set under the chart
corresponds to a compact subset of the source. The pullback is smooth
on the source and vanishes on a neighborhood of every point outside it.

On each fixed compact support, the chain rule estimates every output
derivative seminorm by finitely many input derivative seminorms.
The chart derivatives are bounded on the compact support transported
to the source. The fixed-support bounds and the universal property of
the test space give continuous linearity. If the inverse chart map is
also smooth, the two pullbacks are inverse and give a continuous
linear equivalence of test spaces.

A complex-valued distribution on the source pushes forward by
precomposition with test pullback. Restriction to a smaller open
subset uses the inclusion of its tests. These are internal constructions
for transporting supported distributions to local product charts.
