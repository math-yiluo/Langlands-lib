# Orbit charts on zero-normal fibers

An open partial homeomorphism of `((O × Z) × N)` induces an open partial homeomorphism on each orbit fiber at a fixed slice coordinate `z` when it and its inverse preserve the zero-normal fibers on their respective domains. The source and target are the preimages of the full chart source and target under the inclusion `t ↦ ((t, z), 0)`.

For a smooth chart, the fiber derivative is the orbit component of the full derivative applied to an orbit direction. If both chart directions are smooth, its absolute determinant is positive. In finite-dimensional orbit coordinates it is smooth jointly in the orbit and slice variables on the zero-normal source. The proof uses the derivative and inverse derivative of the fiber chart and the smoothness of the determinant away from its zero set after taking absolute values.

This internal construction supplies the Jacobian for the orbit integration in local transverse coefficient covariance.
