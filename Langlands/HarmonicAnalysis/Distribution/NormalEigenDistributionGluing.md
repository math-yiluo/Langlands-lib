# Holomorphic gluing of compact-support eigen-distribution extensions

`NormalEigenDistributionGluingStatement` extends a distribution family across a zero section on an open product domain. The parameter ranges over a preconnected open subset of the complex plane. Each punctured evaluation is holomorphic and every compact support admits a common weighted derivative estimate over that parameter set. A fixed corrected vector field vanishes on the zero section, with nilpotent field derivative there. The family satisfies its infinitesimal eigen-distribution equation with a common nonzero eigenvalue. An order-zero family defined across the section on a nonempty open subset of parameters supplies the seed and satisfies the same equation.

The fixed-support extension theorem produces one holomorphic family of continuous forms for every compact support. Each family agrees with the seed because its order-zero estimate implies the finite-order estimate used in the cutoff comparison. Evaluating a test with two different compact supports gives two holomorphic functions agreeing on the seed domain. The identity theorem makes them equal on the entire parameter domain. The universal property of the test-function topology then assembles the compact-support forms into continuous distributions with uniform order on each fixed support.

The fixed-support restriction property recovers the punctured evaluations. The identity theorem applied to the two sides of the eigen-distribution equation proves that equation across the section for every parameter in the domain. This internal lemma is a step in extending the real Jacquet distribution through the Bruhat filtration. It takes the weighted bounds and order-zero seed as input.
