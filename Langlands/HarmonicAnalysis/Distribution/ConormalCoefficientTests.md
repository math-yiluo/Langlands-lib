# Pure-power tests for normal coefficient functionals

Multiplying a real scalar test by a fixed coefficient vector is continuous postcomposition. For a fixed scalar test, the result depends continuously on the coefficient vector in finite dimension, by linearity into the test-function space. Pure covector powers are continuous in the covector, so the value of a continuous coefficient functional on a scalar test times such a power is also continuous.

A pointwise symmetric coefficient test expands as a fixed finite sum of pure-power tests. Its scalar coefficients are the postcomposition of the original test with the continuous coordinate functionals of the pure-power expansion. Therefore vanishing on all pure-power tests suffices for vanishing on every symmetric coefficient test.

On a real spectral stratum normal space, a coefficient functional satisfying stabilizer invariance and adjusted transpose oddness on pure-power tests vanishes on symmetric tests. For each fixed scalar test, the conormal closure comparison makes the continuous function of the covector invariant under adjusted transpose. The oddness equality then gives zero, since multiplication by two is injective in a real vector space. The finite test expansion completes the argument. These are internal lemmas for the leading normal jet comparison.
