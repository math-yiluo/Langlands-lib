# Vanishing of a transpose-odd distribution on a real spectral chart

Take an affine conjugation chart centered at a real matrix, with an orbit coordinate, a semisimple slice, and a normal coordinate. Suppose the chart has the local similarity recovery property, the tangent image is constant on the chosen semisimple slice, and the normalized distribution has bounded transverse order on a product inside the chart whose orbit open is a coordinate box.

The normalized distribution kills constant orbit derivatives, so it factors through integration in that box. Its leading coefficient after integration is invariant under ambient conjugation by the center's stabilizer and odd under adjusted transpose. The two actions fix the semisimple slice and carry similar matrices to similar matrices in both directions. The chart action therefore preserves the zero-normal fibers locally, and its orbit Jacobian is one at the fixed points. The leading coefficient covariance law acts by the corresponding normal block on pure covector-power tests.

Conormal invariance and expansion of symmetric forms by pure powers force the leading coefficient to vanish on every symmetric coefficient test. Since the top jet of a test is symmetric, descending the finite transverse order kills the integrated slice distribution. The original normalized product restriction vanishes by its factorization through that distribution.

This internal local vanishing result supplies the analytic step in the real matrix distribution transpose proof.
