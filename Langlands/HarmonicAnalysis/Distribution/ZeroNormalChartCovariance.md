# Local weighted covariance on zero-normal chart fibers

Restrict a chart transition to the part of a chosen open domain where the zero-normal fibers are locally preserved and whose image satisfies the corresponding condition for the inverse. A fixed slice point stays in the restricted transition if the forward and inverse preserve the fibers near that point and the point belongs to the chosen open domain.

For a chart transition induced by an ambient linear equivalence of unit absolute determinant, the normalized distribution inherits the signed weighted pullback law on this restriction. The proof applies the chart transition identity to a target test viewed in the restricted target and then includes the resulting source test in the original product. The evaluation of a normalized distribution agrees on copies of the same test in different open domains inside the chart.

If the orbit determinant has absolute value one at the fixed slice points and the normal derivative there is a fixed linear map, the leading orbit-integrated coefficient transforms by precomposing with that linear map in each multilinear argument. The full Jacobian at each fixed point is one by the ambient determinant identity and positivity of the chart Jacobian. The proof applies the zero-dilation covariance law to the restricted transition and evaluates its transport kernel at the fixed points in the support of the slice test.

This internal result transfers the ambient action to the leading coefficient used in real matrix spectral descent.
