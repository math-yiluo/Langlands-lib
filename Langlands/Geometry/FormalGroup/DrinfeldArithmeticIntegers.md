# Integer comparison for Drinfeld's unramified subfield

The valuation pulled back from the division algebra orders elements exactly as the spectral norm of the original embedded field. The identity on the field therefore identifies the two integer rings. Composing with the existing finite coefficient comparison gives an isomorphism of algebras over the original integers. The chosen subfield is finite and its coefficient algebra is étale, so its actual integer ring has the finite étaleness required in the special formal module definition.

Arithmetic Frobenius acts on the spectral residue field by the base residue cardinality power. The residue cardinality is unchanged by the comparison with the original base integers. The difference between the Frobenius image and the power lies in the maximal ideal, which gives the strict division valuation estimate. Together with the prescribed power and conjugation equations of the normalized division uniformizer, this proves the complete cyclic relation predicate of the chosen embedding.

These are the embedded integer ring and relations used in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 B, printed p. 110.
