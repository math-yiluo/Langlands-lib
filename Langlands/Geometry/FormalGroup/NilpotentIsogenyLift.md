# Lifting integral isogenies up to a scalar power

This internal argument supplies the integral numerators for the nilpotent rigidity proof. Over the quotient, choose an integral dual to the given isogeny. Both composites are the appropriate scalar-power endomorphism. Lift the isogeny and its dual as integral homomorphisms after possibly different scalar powers. The composites then reduce to the corresponding scalar endomorphisms with the sum of the three exponents. Integral rigidity identifies the lifted composites with those scalar endomorphisms. The scalar-composites criterion makes the lifted numerator an actual isogeny, including its finite projective kernel.

On a basic open the same argument applies to the surjective map of structure-sheaf section rings with nilpotent kernel. Transport the given numerator across the canonical isomorphisms of the coefficient square before lifting it. Conjugating back verifies the reduction equation for the actual open pullback.
