# Compatible sections of the schematic Tate point presheaf

On a scheme over the base, a section of the schematic Tate point presheaf is a compatible family of maps to the actual finite group schemes over the base. Compatibility is postcomposition by each transfer. Evaluation on the test scheme and passage to the underlying set preserve the limit. The comparison takes a section to its finite components, so it preserves addition and yields an additive equivalence with the group of compatible families.

Restriction along a map of tests precomposes each point by that map. A morphism of p-divisible groups postcomposes each point by its homomorphism on the corresponding finite group schemes. Two sections with the same finite components agree.

The comparison is derived from the scalar torsion levels in Rapoport and Zink, *Period Spaces for p-divisible Groups* (1996), §2, printed pp. 50–51 (PDF pp. 65–66). It identifies sections on arbitrary scheme tests. Harris's Tate module for level structures is an inverse limit on the rigid generic fibre. See his 1997 Appendix, printed p. 115 (PDF p. 41).
