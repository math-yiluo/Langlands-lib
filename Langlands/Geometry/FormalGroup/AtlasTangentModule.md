# The compatible tangent module of a formal atlas

For a finite-coordinate formal atlas over a commutative algebra, work on the actual principal localization of each chart in a test algebra. Differentiate the formal transition on every pairwise overlap to obtain a linear equivalence there. The formal identity and cocycle give the identity and cocycle of these linear equivalences. The two restrictions to each overlap are linear over the test algebra because the maps of localizations commute with its structural maps.

`tangentSubmodule` is the kernel of the overlap difference on the product of the coordinate tangent modules. Its elements are exactly the tuples whose restrictions agree through the differentiated transitions. This is the module of compatible tangent sections in the coordinates of the atlas.

This is a derived construction of the affine tangent module used for the tangent sheaf in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 B, printed p. 110 (PDF p. 4).
