# Localization of the overlap tangent module on a finite atlas

Let \(A\) be an affine test ring for a formal coordinate atlas, and let \(r\) be any element of \(A\). `BasicAtlas.tangentAwayMap` extends the compatible tangent vectors to \(A[1/r]\) and views the target as an \(A\)-module.

For an atlas with finitely many chart generators, this map exhibits the target as the localization of the compatible tangent module at the powers of \(r\). Localization commutes with the individual chart and overlap coefficient rings, and with the finite products of their coordinate modules. Coefficient naturality of the actual derivative transitions identifies the localized overlap difference with the overlap difference over \(A[1/r]\). Localization of kernels then gives the asserted module identification.

This is an affine localization result for the tangent construction in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 B, printed p. 110 (PDF p. 4).
