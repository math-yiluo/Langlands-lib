# Geometric ranks of the finite Tate levels

The native rank of each actual finite scheme agrees with the coordinate-algebra rank used in the height predicate. Where `p` is invertible on the base, the level schemes are finite étale. Thus on a test over an algebraically closed field the number of points at level `n` is `p ^ (n * r)` for a group of height `r`.

At level `n + 1`, the points killed by `p` are the image of level one. The transfer to level `n` followed by inclusion is multiplication by `p`, and that inclusion is injective on affine points. The actual short exact sequence then identifies the kernel with the image of level one. The cardinality criterion for finite abelian groups killed by a prime power gives a basis over the residue ring of level `n + 1`. The chosen equivalence is linear for the canonical residue action.

This derives the finite-level geometric rank from the actual scalar torsion exact sequences of Rapoport and Zink, *Period Spaces for p-divisible Groups* (1996), §2, printed pp. 50–51 (PDF pp. 65–66). Harris uses finite residue modules on the rigid generic fibre in his 1997 Appendix, printed p. 115 (PDF p. 41).
