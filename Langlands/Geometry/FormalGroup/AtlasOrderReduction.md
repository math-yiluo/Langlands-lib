# Quotient reduction with the full acting order

This is internal infrastructure specializing `AtlasOrderBaseChangeAlong`
and `AtlasOrderRationalBaseChangeAlong` to the actual quotient maps of
`ModuleReduction`. The underlying atlas is the `AtlasReduction`
construction. No additional original-literature assertion is made.

Let `O → R` be a homomorphism of commutative rings, `π ∈ R`, `A` an
object of `Nilp R π`, and `D` a ring with a central `O`-algebra structure.
The order `D` need not be commutative or a maximal order. For an actual
formal `O`-module atlas `X` over `A` with an order action `E`,
`OrderAction.reduction` extends the actual action along
`Nilp.quotientO O A`. Its underlying atlas is exactly `X.reduction`,
with the images of the original principal generators as its cover.
No residue-field or reducedness hypothesis is imposed on the quotient
`A/(π)`. Compared atlases may have different finite coordinate types;
their original covers agree. Tests have the coefficient-ring universe.

`reductionPointEquiv` compares the complete compatible families over every
quotient-algebra test with the original families restricted along the
quotient map. It is linear over all of `D`, is natural in every further
test map, and gives `reductionPointFunctorIso`, a natural isomorphism of
the actual order-module point functors. `reductionPointEquiv_eq` identifies
its underlying function with the ambient atlas reduction comparison.
This compares families on quotient-algebra tests, without identifying
the points over `A` with the points over `A/(π)`.

`Hom.reduction` reduces actual integral formal maps commuting with every
element of the order. It preserves identity and composition, and its
`reduction_val` law identifies its forgotten formal map with ambient
reduction. Its forward and reverse whole-family squares specialize the
existing supplied-target formal-map squares. `Iso.reduction` reduces
both integral maps with their two inverse equations. Its projection laws
and forward and reverse whole-family squares retain these actual maps.

For any field `O`-algebra `F`, `RationalHom.reduction` is an `F`-linear
map on the entire chartwise `D`-centralizer inside the global tensor
`F ⊗[O] BasicAtlas.Hom`. It agrees with ambient tensor reduction after
forgetting commutation, commutes with rationalization of integral
order-linear maps, and preserves rational identity and composition.
There is no assumption that the centralizer is generated by integral
order-linear maps.

If `O` is a domain and `F` its fraction field,
`QuasiIsogeny.reduction` reduces both order-linear rational maps, retaining
both inverse equations. It preserves identity, inverse, composition and
rationalization of integral isomorphisms. Its projection laws expose the
two reduced maps. `toAtlas_reduction` identifies the entire forgotten
ambient quasi-isogeny, including its inverse, with ambient reduction.
All proofs specialize the corresponding supplied-target extension laws
at the canonical quotient map.

This focused construction does not assert a reduction/base-change
comparison for varying Nilp algebras, invariance under arbitrary refined
comparisons, local rational gluing, height, specialness, framing, rigidity
or representability.
