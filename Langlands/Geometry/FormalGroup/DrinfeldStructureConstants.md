# The universal ring of structure constants

The variables are indexed by a nonnegative level and a cyclic degree.
The coefficient ring for the homogeneous basis in Drinfel'd's
Proposition 2.1 is the polynomial ring in these variables modulo the
relation that the product of the level-zero variables equals the
uniformizer. Evaluation into a test algebra is defined whenever the
chosen constants satisfy that relation. The universal constants
determine the evaluation map.

The uniformizer acts injectively on this quotient when it is a prime
element of the coefficient domain. Reduce an equation
\(\pi f=(\prod_i a_{0,i}-\pi)g\) modulo the uniformizer. The product of
the variables is nonzero over the residue domain, so the reduction of
\(g\) vanishes. Every coefficient of \(g\) is then divisible by
\(\pi\), and cancellation in the original polynomial ring puts \(f\)
in the defining ideal. This is the regularity property needed for the
universal case of the weighted-map argument.
[V. G. Drinfel'd, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions*, 1976, §2B, Propositions 2.1–2.2](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01077936)
