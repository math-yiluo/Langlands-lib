# Coefficient triangles for special formal atlas charts

On a test algebra in a given chart of a special formal order atlas, the chart law extends from the corresponding principal localization. For a map between such test algebras, uniqueness of the localization map identifies the direct coefficient homomorphism with the composite. The formal addition and all order-action series therefore agree after either extension. `chartTestCoefficientIso` is the resulting full-order isomorphism with identity coordinate series.

On a test map in a pair of charts, the coefficient triangles intertwine the two actual full-order transitions. The two forward composites agree by the atlas's coefficient naturality for the transition series, and the forward map determines an isomorphism. This is a derived coordinate compatibility for the special formal order modules used in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 A–B, printed pp. 109–110 (PDF pp. 3–4).
