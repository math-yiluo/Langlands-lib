# Local quasi-isogeny of the primal rigidifier

Rapoport–Zink, *Period Spaces for p-divisible Groups* (1996), §6.43 and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 308–310 (PDF pp. 320–322), restrict the polarized quasi-isogeny to the primal division summand in the comparison (6.17). Harris 1997, Appendix, printed p. 112 (PDF p. 38), gives the local quasi-isogeny and raw height conventions. Item (iii) on printed p. 113 (PDF p. 39) gives the order-linear, polarization-compatible rigidifier on the special fibre.

Given coordinates for the two division images on the reduced affine test, the original order-linearity gives the projector equation by evaluating it on `(0, 1)`. Apply restriction of local quasi-isogenies with the usual prime uniformizer of the p-adic integers. This supplies actual isogeny numerators on a Zariski cover with the original scalar denominators, as well as scalar divisibility of the two image laws. The resulting rational section is linear for the induced division-order actions. Its inverse section is the inverse of the previously constructed `primalFraming`, by equality of the forward sections.
