# Integral intertwiners for the standard Lubin–Tate polynomial

Over a local ring with finite residue field and principal maximal ideal generated by `π`, the defect of the standard intertwining equation vanishes after reducing the coefficients. The residue-cardinality power of a series is substitution by the same power on each variable. Each defect coefficient is therefore divisible by `π` and, from degree two on, by `π − π^degree` since the second factor `1 − π^(degree − 1)` is a unit.

`intertwiner` starts with the prescribed linear form and successively corrects one total degree. The corrections leave lower coefficients fixed and cancel the next defect degree by the coefficient calculation. Each coefficient stabilizes, and the resulting series satisfies the full equation. In a domain with nonzero `π`, the same coefficient calculation proves uniqueness by induction on the first degree of a possible difference.

This implements Lubin and Tate, *Formal Complex Multiplication in Local Fields* (1965), §1 Lemma 1, printed pp. 381–382, specialized to the same standard polynomial `πT + T^q` on both sides.
