# Actual frame compatibility on pulled stalks

This module proves that the actual inverse-image rational stalk comparison
preserves the rational frame and the O-lattice generated by its O-valued
vectors. All maps are the constructed sheafification, inverse-image and stalk
maps already used in `DeligneLatticeBaseChange`, `DeligneDeterminantLattice`
and `DeligneRationalStalkBaseChange`.

## Source and scope

The source motivation is V. G. Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions*
(1976), Section 2 A, printed pages 109–110 (PDF pages 3–4), especially the
rational frame at the page boundary and condition (c) in the opening paragraph
of page 110. The registered source is
`drinfeld-1976-coverings-p-adic-symmetric-regions`, with content SHA-256
`7dd09e1967f46c29195ec87a400e145a17374368fc3b9dd0a782b5b7bfc44e2f`.
Drinfeld specifies a rational degree-zero frame and compares determinants of
integral homogeneous components with exterior powers of integer Pi-translates
of the framed O-lattice. The present results are derived infrastructure for
transporting that condition under base change.

The hypotheses for this infrastructure are a commutative ring O, a field F
with an O-algebra structure, a positive natural number d, an element pi of O,
and an arbitrary continuous map f : X → Y. No nonzero image of pi is needed
for this unit. There is no assumption that f is flat or that the spaces are
schemes. The comparison and all its compatibility equations are constructed,
rather than supplied as additional input data.

The module proves transport of the unshifted frame lattice. It does not prove
compatibility of the rational Pi operator or its integer powers, determinant
normalization, injectivity of integral rationalization, full admissibility,
or representability. Those remain further obligations.

## Constant unit and rational component inclusions

`inverseImageConstantIso_unit` first identifies the global unit of the actual
constant sheaf under inverse image. For any O-module V, compose the
constant-sheaf adjunction on Y with the inverse-image/pushforward adjunction,
and compare it with the constant-sheaf adjunction on X. The existing
`inverseImageConstantIso` is the left-adjoint uniqueness comparison for exactly
these adjunctions. Their unit identity says that the composite

- the constant-presheaf sheafification unit at the top open of Y;
- the inverse-image adjunction unit for the constant sheaf;
- the constant-sheaf comparison at the top open of X

is the constant-presheaf sheafification unit at the top open of X.
The proof applies `Adjunction.homEquiv_leftAdjointUniq_hom_app` and unfolds
only its unit interpretation. This is a global-section identity, not an
assumed pointwise trivialization of a constant sheaf.

`rationalLatticeStalkPullbackIso_germ` computes the existing total rational
stalk comparison on each actual germ. It is the inverse-image unit on the
original open, followed by the total rational sheaf comparison on its
preimage, followed by the germ on that preimage. The proof expands the
constructed stalk comparison and uses the inverse-image germ formula and
naturality of germs for a sheaf morphism.

`LatticePiSheaf.rationalInclusion_pullback` proves that rationalization of each
actual homogeneous inclusion commutes with the total rational sheaf
comparison. It follows from naturality of `inverseImageRationalIso` and
functoriality of rationalization. The pulled inclusion is the inverse image
of the old inclusion followed by `inverseImageLatticeScalarIso`, exactly as
in the existing `LatticePiSheaf.inverseImage` definition.

## Actual frame and its image lattice

For a framed lattice M on Y,
`FramedLatticePiSheaf.frameToTotal_pullback` proves the sheaf square

`P_F(frameToTotal M) ≫ totalComparison = constantComparison ≫ frameToTotal(P M)`.

The proof uses the preceding rational-inclusion square. In the existing
definition of the pulled frame, the inverse of the constant comparison
precedes the inverse image of the original frame; the extra constant
comparison on the right cancels with that inverse. The result therefore
concerns the actual pulled frame, without choosing another one.

`frameStalkPullbackMap` is the original `frameStalkMap` at f(x), followed by
the actual F-linear total rational stalk comparison at x.
`frameStalkPullbackMap_eq` proves that this composite is the
`frameStalkMap` of the actual pulled framed lattice. The proof expands the
frame germ, uses `rationalLatticeStalkPullbackIso_germ`, and applies naturality
of the inverse-image adjunction unit to move the frame past that unit. The
sheaf square then replaces the comparison of framed sections by the pulled
framed section. Finally `inverseImageConstantIso_unit` identifies the
remaining constant unit with the one on X. Preimages of the top open are
identified with the top open.

`integralFrameStalkMap_pullback` evaluates this F-linear frame equality on
the vectors `(algebraMap O F (v j))`, for v in O^d. After restriction of the
comparison's scalars to O, it gives an equality of O-linear maps from O^d to
the pulled total rational stalk.

`frameStalkLattice_pullback` concludes that the image under this comparison
of the actual `frameStalkLattice` at f(x) is exactly the actual
`frameStalkLattice` of the pulled framed lattice at x. Both lattices are
ranges of the integral frame maps. The proof transports a range
representative v in each direction using the preceding linear-map equality.
It neither assumes nor proves injectivity of the integral frame map.
