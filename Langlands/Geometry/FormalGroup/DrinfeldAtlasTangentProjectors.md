# Split coefficient projectors on the compatible Drinfeld tangent module

A special formal order atlas on an affine nilpotent test algebra has the compatible tangent module formed by the linear overlap equations. The order action on this module extends the original scalar action, as checked on each chart tangent module. Fix the atlas's embedded degree-\(d\) unramified coefficient field, a residue embedding into \(\kappa\), and a residue coordinate \(\kappa \to B/\pi B\) for the test ring \(B\). Unique finite étale lifting of the cyclic residue characters splits the coefficient algebra after tensoring with \(B\).

`tangentCharacterAction` is the action of this split coefficient algebra through the full order representation. Its coordinate idempotents give pairwise orthogonal tangent projectors whose sum is the identity. The tuple of lifted characters of an embedded integer acts by its original order endomorphism. Given a division algebra unit \(\Pi\) with \(\Pi^d=\pi\), its integral uniformizer operator on the same module has \(d\)-th power equal to multiplication by the image of \(\pi\) in \(B\).

These are the affine module operations underlying the graded tangent datum in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 B, printed p. 110 (PDF p. 4).
