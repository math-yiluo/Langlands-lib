# Reduced split primal atlas and special frame

For a split-special polarized affine point, the chosen primal projector retractions extend along the actual principal-ideal quotient. The scalar algebra on the quotient agrees with the composite through the original test by the quotient map's commutation with scalar multiplication. The extended cover therefore presents the division image of the actual reduced polarized target.

The reduced image carries the central order law. `reducedPrimalImageIso` compares it by an actual integral order isomorphism with reduction of the constructed special primal atlas. The source frame comes from its chosen global division retraction. Its structural central tangent and geometric tangent characters give `primalSpecialFrame`. The central residue map is the canonical map from the residue coefficient ring to the reduced test ring.

These are the target reduction and source frame used in the split comparison of Rapoport–Zink, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310.
