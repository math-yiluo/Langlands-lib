# Coefficient and isometry transport of local torsion similitudes

The actual integral pairing equation is preserved by conjugation
with integral isometries at the source and target. The inverse
source isometry and the forward target isometry both have
multiplier one, so the composition law preserves the given
integral scalar.

For an open coefficient pullback, the structure-sheaf coefficient
map and the structural algebra maps form a commuting square.
The canonical comparisons along both paths are isometries.
Conjugating the coefficient extension of the numerator by these
comparisons gives the actual pulled-back numerator, with the
original multiplier.

For a local rational similitude, coefficient pullback uses the
inverse image of the witnessing open and extends its numerator.
Transport by isometries uses the same witnessing open with the
conjugated numerator. Both operations retain the denominator
exponent, the integral multiplier equation and the single rational
multiplier. The proofs use the actual section restriction,
coefficient, and composition formulas. These are internal
coherence laws for the rational polarization condition.
