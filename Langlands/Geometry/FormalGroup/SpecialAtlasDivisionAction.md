# Division units act on the special atlas comparison classes

Conjugating the actions in an integral atlas comparison keeps the same
formal isomorphism and refinements. Its order equations are the original
equations evaluated on the conjugated order element. The identity and
product laws for atlas twists follow from the corresponding coordinate
equations. Twisting by `g` and then `h` is the twist by `h * g`.

The whole rational atlas quasi-isogenies have these same identity and
product laws after the target order actions are identified. The forward
composite is the image of `(g⁻¹ h⁻¹ g) g⁻¹ = (h g)⁻¹` under the whole
atlas rational action. Inverse uniqueness compares the complete maps.

`divisionUnitAction` is a left action of `Dˣ` on the original comparison
classes of special atlases with their scalar-kernel height certificates.
The original comparisons descend the twists, and the atlas equations
give the group laws on the quotient. The action commutes with extension
along supplied coefficient maps. The original integral comparison makes
each integer-order unit act trivially on these target classes.

This is the action on the special target in Drinfel'd's formula in
*Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 A, printed p. 109.
The residue map and the Frobenius-corrected framing require further
constructions for the action on framed classes.
