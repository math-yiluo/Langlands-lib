# Integral order-linear maps with the central scalar law

For the split primal quasi-isogeny of Rapoport–Zink 1996, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310 (PDF pp. 321–322), a change to the central scalar ring uses the same underlying formal map. The central tangent condition holds over every coefficient algebra by fibre differentiation of the order action. Constructing the central scalar law after this coefficient extension gives the same chart law as extending the central law.

An order-linear integral map acts by the same coordinates on the same formal group points before and after this change. The natural kernel equivalence is the identity on points, and the original representing algebra and its finite projectivity give the new kernel model. The local lifting condition is unchanged on the underlying points. In particular integral isogeny numerators remain actual isogenies over the new scalar ring.
