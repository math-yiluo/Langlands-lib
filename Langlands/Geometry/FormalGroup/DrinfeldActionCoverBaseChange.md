# Extending the restriction of a global rational formal map

The source composite in Drinfel'd, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 A, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 3), is built on the fixed frame and restricted to the reduced target cover. `SpecialAtlas.restrictFrameHom` makes this restriction for a global rational formal map between any two coefficient extensions of the fixed frame. The comparison to the coefficient-extended global frame on the new cover has identity coordinate series on each chart. For an integral formal map, the chart formula therefore reduces the commuting square to composing the coefficient maps. Linearity extends the square to the whole rational formal Hom tensor.

The actual quotient-frame comparison has three parts. The first compares the base extension of the old global cover with the global cover of the extended frame. The second composes the two coefficient maps on the fixed frame. The third identifies the image cover with the cover of the new reduced target. Restriction preserves the given global coefficient square by compatibility with composition. The final cover identification preserves the restricted map by equality elimination on the covers. Pasting the three squares gives `quotientFrameBaseChangeIso_rational_natural` for the actual comparison on the whole atlas tensor. This applies to the source composite in the Drinfeld action using its global coefficient square.
