# Pullback in the category up to isogeny

Base change acts on each level by scheme-theoretic pullback. At level
\(n\), the natural \(\mathbb Z_p\)-action is multiplication by an integer
representative of the scalar modulo \(p^n\). Pullback of group scheme
maps is additive and preserves the identity, so it preserves this
scalar. `Hom.baseChangeLinear` gives the resulting linear pullback of
integral morphisms.

`RationalHom.baseChangeMap` extends that linear map to the actual
\(\mathbb Q_p\)-tensor. Its identity and composition laws make
`rationalBaseChangeFunctor` a functor. `QuasiIsogeny.baseChange` applies
it to both inverse maps of the original quasi-isogeny. The result
applies to passage to the special fibre used in the rigidifier, and
to the pullback of that fibre along a change of coefficients.

Conditions (i)–(iii) of C1 use the two groups pulled back to the
special fibre. See Harris (1997), Appendix, printed p. 113 (PDF p. 39).
Rapoport–Zink (1996), §2.11 and Definition 2.15, printed pp. 53–54
(PDF pp. 68–69), define the closed subscheme cut out by the ideal
generated by \(p\) and use the quasi-isogeny on that subscheme.
