# Scalar extension of the total Drinfeld tangent module and its operators

The tensor product of the total tangent module with a new test ring is identified with the tangent module in the transported coordinates. On a pure tensor this identification applies the coefficient homomorphism entrywise and scales by the first factor. Under that identification, scalar extension of each order operator and of each character projector gives the transported operator. Both equalities follow on pure tensors from the coefficient formulas for the actual derivative and projector.

This is the module comparison needed for coefficient pullback of the tangent part of the data in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 B and Proposition 2.4, printed pp. 110–111 (PDF pp. 4–5).
