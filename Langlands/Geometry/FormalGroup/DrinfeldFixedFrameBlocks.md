# Rational blocks of the actual fixed frame

The actual fixed special module is a finite power of the reduced Lubin–Tate line. Passing to rational Hom and composing with its integral inclusions and projections gives a matrix algebra. An integral diagonal formal map has diagonal blocks given by the rationalizations of its factor maps.

In these coordinates the chosen embedded integers act diagonally by the successive inverse arithmetic Frobenius twists of the coefficient action. The normalized division uniformizer acts on every factor by arithmetic Frobenius of the line. These are formulas for the order action on the fixed special frame constructed over the residue of the completed maximal unramified base. They use the arithmetic order presentation in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 A–B, printed pp. 109–110.
