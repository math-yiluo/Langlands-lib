# Homogeneous tangent lines

The projector onto a character summand splits its inclusion in the
coordinate tangent module. Its image is therefore finite projective.
After a scalar extension, the standard tensor equivalence for finite
tuples carries the extended projector to the new projector. Extending
the split inclusion and retraction identifies the tensor product of the
original image with the new image.

Over an algebraically closed test field, the special-module certificate
diagonalizes the coefficient action by distinct characters. Each of those
characters extends to the base-changed coefficient algebra. On the split
product each extension is evaluation at a coordinate. Distinctness and
the equal finite index sets identify the two indexings, so the image of
each projector is one of the one-dimensional eigenspaces. Evaluation at
the corresponding diagonal coordinate gives a linear equivalence with
the test field.

For a prime of a test algebra in the comparison functor's universe, the
algebraic closure of its residue field is a geometric test algebra. The
base-change equivalence identifies the extended component with the line
over that field. Pulling the zero prime of the field back to the original
prime gives stalk rank one.

These results realize the tangent lines of the character grading in
Drinfel'd's construction using the Library's actual affine test algebras.
[V. G. Drinfel'd, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions*, 1976, §2B, p. 110](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01077936)
