# Multiplication maps of actual scalar torsion schemes

At levels `m ≤ n` the actual inclusion and transfer have two composites. The
composite on each level is multiplication by `p^(n-m)` in its group scheme
endomorphisms, for a natural number `p` acting through the original scalar
ring. The proof evaluates on affine tests. Inclusion leaves the underlying
point unchanged and transfer multiplies it by this scalar power. Evaluation of
an integer multiple of the identity gives the same multiple on points.

For the prime scalar in a p-divisible group, the upper-level equality is the
factorization through the lower level in Tate, *p-Divisible Groups* (1967),
§2.1, equation (2) and the paragraph preceding it, printed p. 161 (PDF p. 2).
The equalities here use the actual group scheme maps of the formal module's
finite kernels.
