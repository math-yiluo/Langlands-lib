# Actual height-zero framed triples over the principal quotient

Drinfeld 1976, §2 A, printed p.109, specifies triples consisting of a residue
map, a special formal division-order module of height d², and a height-zero
quasi-isogeny from the extended fixed frame to the reduction of that module.
This module constructs actual atlas representatives of that shape from the
existing formal-module infrastructure. It keeps the framing direction and
the height-zero requirement.

Let K be a nonarchimedean local field with integer ring O and residue field k.
Let D be a finite-dimensional central division K-algebra of reduced degree d.
The existing special-module interface includes a supplied embedded unramified
subfield of full reduced degree and its geometric tangent-character
certificates. This module retains that interface; it does not yet specialize
the invariant of D to 1/d or construct the required subfield.

Fix an element π of O, an O-algebra R, and a supplied field κ with an
O-algebra structure. The fixed frame Φ is an actual global special formal
module over κ, already carrying its irreducibility and actual height-d²
certificate. Here κ, K, and the test coefficient algebras share a universe.
The embedded subfield and R may have other universes. The coordinate type is
Fin d; the principal covers may be infinite.

For A in `Nilp R (algebraMap O R π)`, the reduction is the actual quotient of
A by the ideal generated by its image of π. The quotient need not be reduced
or a field. `SpecialAtlas.quotientFrame Φ ψ Y` extends the actual fixed frame
along the O-algebra map ψ from κ to that quotient and restricts it to exactly
the specified principal cover of Y's reduction. It therefore has the same
cover as that reduction. `quotientFrame_height` extends the existing
scalar-kernel certificates and restricts them to these charts; it proves
height d² for this actual source.

`DrinfeldFramedAtlas Φ A` consists of that residue map ψ, an actual special
atlas Y over A with its own height-d² certificate, and a full-order
quasi-isogeny ρ from the constructed source to Y's actual reduction.
The stored ρ has actual rational formal forward and inverse maps, commuting
with every element of the division order, and both formal inverse equations.
Its forward map must satisfy
`BasicAtlas.RationalHom.HasConstantChartHeight π (Nat.card k) 0`.
Thus height zero means the existing local signed difference of actual
kernel-rank exponents on every chart after every coefficient-algebra test.
The local denominators and neighbourhoods may vary.

`source_height` and `reducedTarget_height` retain actual height-d² certificates
on both sides. Given nonvanishing of π in K, `localFraming` uses their actual
π-divisibility and the stored local rank relation to construct the existing
genuine local quasi-isogeny on each test chart. `localFraming_val` identifies
its section with the section of ρ's evaluated full-order rational map, and
`localFraming_height_zero` proves that this same section has the constant
local kernel-rank height function zero.

The construction uses `SpecialModule`, `SpecialAtlas`, `SpecialAtlasReduction`
and `AtlasRationalHeight`. The proofs use actual coefficient extension,
restriction of the fixed global module to the reduced cover, preservation of
the supplied scalar-kernel ranks, and the existing extraction of a local
quasi-isogeny from those ranks.

This is a restricted representative construction, not yet Drinfeld's functor
G. Identification of κ with the residue field of a strict henselization,
construction of a fixed frame, the invariant-1/d specialization, completeness
of the global-tensor rational atlas interface, framed comparison classes,
coefficient-base-change invariance of the height condition, varying-base
functoriality, group actions and representability remain separate obligations.
No representing or universal object is an input.
