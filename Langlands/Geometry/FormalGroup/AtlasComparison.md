# Formal coordinate comparison on a common principal cover

`BasicAtlas.Comparison` records actual formal coordinate isomorphisms between
two atlases after refinement to the same principal cover. Its constructed
`onPoints` and `pointFunctorIso` compare the original point modules and
functors by restriction, evaluation of the formal maps, and actual gluing.
The two atlases may have different covers and finite coordinate types.

## Provenance and scope

This is derived infrastructure for Drinfeld's convention of formal groups
glued from formal groups over localizations: Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic
symmetric regions* (1976), printed p.109, paragraph immediately before
section 2 (PDF page 3). The registered source is
`drinfeld-1976-coverings-p-adic-symmetric-regions`, SHA256
`7dd09e1967f46c29195ec87a400e145a17374368fc3b9dd0a782b5b7bfc44e2f`.
This module does not attribute its coordinate-comparison constructions to a
separate theorem in that paragraph.

O and R are arbitrary commutative rings with an O-algebra structure on R.
Coordinate types are finite types in `Type`. Test R-algebras lie in R's
universe v, and compatible-family modules lie in v+1. Covers may be infinite;
their generators span the unit ideal. The chart laws may vary.

A comparison supplies formal power-series maps with formal inverse,
coefficient-naturality and transition equations on a common refinement. It
supplies no abstract point equivalence. The comparison of full point
families is constructed from those formal data using refinement descent.
There is no assertion that any two atlases have such a comparison, or that
every natural point-functor isomorphism arises this way. This module does
not construct a quotient of presentations, comparison transitivity for
different refinement covers, a formal scheme, framing, heights, moduli or
representability.

## Same-cover formal isomorphisms

`BasicAtlas.Iso X Y h` consists of a formal atlas homomorphism in each
direction, in the sense of `AtlasMorphism`, and two equalities saying their
formal composites are the identity atlas maps. The cover equality h
identifies chart generators and transfers their membership proofs.

`Iso.onPoints` evaluates these maps on full compatible families. The two
formal inverse equations and the evaluation formulas for identity and
composition prove both inverse identities. The result is an O-linear
equivalence for the actual formal-law group operations and O-action.
`Iso.pointFunctorIso` uses coefficient naturality of the forward map to
package it as a natural isomorphism. `Iso.nilpPointFunctorIso` restricts that
isomorphism along the forgetful functor from actual algebras where the image
of π is nilpotent.

`Iso.refl`, `Iso.symm` and `Iso.trans` construct the corresponding formal
same-cover isomorphisms. The composite inverse equations follow by
associativity and the two input inverse equations. These operations are on
actual formal atlas maps, before evaluating any points.

## An actual common cover

`commonOpens X Y` is the set of products a*b with a in X.opens and b in
Y.opens. `commonOpens_covers` uses the equality between the product of the
two generated ideals and the ideal generated by these products. Since each
original ideal is the unit ideal, so is the product-cover ideal.

`exists_commonSources` and `commonSources` select a pair of factors for each
element of that set. `commonSources_mul` records their actual product. The
construction allows repeated products and makes no uniqueness or
injectivity assumption about the chosen factors.

In R[1/c], the chosen product is a unit. In a commutative ring both factors
of a unit product are units. `commonLeft_unit` and `commonRight_unit` apply
this fact after the structural ring map. `commonLeft` and `commonRight`
therefore give genuine `CoverRefinement` data for X and Y with exactly the
same new cover, rather than an assumed comparison of point carriers. Their
restricted laws and transitions are constructed by `AtlasRefinement`.

## Comparison across different covers

`Comparison X Y` has a refinement of each atlas, equality of their finer
covers, and a `BasicAtlas.Iso` between the two restricted atlases. Its
`onPoints A` first restricts an X family, applies that actual formal
coordinate isomorphism, and then glues back to a Y family by the inverse
refinement equivalence. Each step is an established O-linear equivalence.

`Comparison.pointFunctorIso` composes the corresponding natural
isomorphisms in the same order. `Comparison.nilpPointFunctorIso` restricts it
to actual Nilp algebras. `Comparison.ofCommon` uses the constructed product
cover for the two refinements; its remaining input is an actual formal
atlas isomorphism on that cover, with all inverse and compatibility laws.
