# The homogeneous projectors of a graded sheaf

Each homogeneous summand gives a projector on the total sheaf by composing its canonical projection with its inclusion. The sum of these projectors is the identity. An orthogonal splitting that identifies with the homogeneous summands computes the same projector, since the maps agree after precomposition with every homogeneous inclusion.

A total sheaf isomorphism that intertwines two homogeneous projectors restricts to an isomorphism of the corresponding components. The inverse total map intertwines the same projectors in reverse. Composing with the inclusions verifies the two inverse equations and identifies the component map as the restriction of the total map.

Structural pullback transports the projector by applying pullback to its projection and inclusion. The scalar relation can also be restated along an equality of global sections with the same sheaf maps.

These constructions apply to the component comparisons for the graded tangent sheaf in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 B and Proposition 2.4, printed pp. 110–111 (PDF pp. 4–5).
