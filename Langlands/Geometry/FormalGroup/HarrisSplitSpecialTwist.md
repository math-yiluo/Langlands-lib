# Coefficient twist of the split-special affine functor

Let \(W\) be the coefficient algebra over \(\mathcal O_F\), and let \(\sigma\)
be an \(\mathcal O_F\)-algebra automorphism of \(W\). On affine tests,
`splitSpecialTwistFrameNatIso` identifies the split-special functor for the
residue-twisted frame \(\bar\sigma^*Y\) with the split-special functor for
the original frame \(Y\) on tests whose structural map is precomposed
with \(\sigma\).

The identity on the underlying test ring intertwines the original and
twisted structural maps from \(\mathcal O_F\) because \(\sigma\) fixes
their image in \(W\). The inverse equivalence intertwines these maps in
the reverse direction. For either coefficient square, the structural
scalar action on the division tangent is therefore the required one
after extension. The geometric character decomposition extends by
pulling each geometric point back along the coefficient map. The chosen
unramified subfield of full reduced degree stays fixed throughout.

These verifications apply to representatives of the framed isomorphism
classes. They restrict both directions of the underlying polarized
coefficient equivalence to the split-special subtypes. The inverse and
naturality equations follow by equality of the underlying framed classes.

This is the coefficient comparison underlying Harris (1997), Appendix
(A.4), printed p. 114 (PDF p. 40), on the split-special locus of the
polarized affine functor. The split condition comes from the division
summand in Rapoport and Zink, *Period Spaces for \(p\)-divisible Groups*,
§6.43, printed pp. 308–309 (PDF pp. 320–321).
