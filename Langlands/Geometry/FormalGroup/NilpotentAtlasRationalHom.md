# Nilpotent rigidity for rational formal atlas maps

Drinfeld's *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §1 and §2A, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 3), allows formal modules glued from localizations and uses rational formal maps for the quasi-isogenies in the framing. This module establishes detection by reduction for the Library's formal atlas maps.

Given a surjective coefficient map with nilpotent kernel, the atlas base change chooses an original generator above each image generator. Their images span the whole smaller coefficient ring. The chosen original generators also span the whole ring, since a proper ideal lies in a maximal ideal and every maximal ideal contains the nilpotent kernel. Localizing the coefficient map at paired generators preserves surjectivity and nilpotence of the kernel.

Two integral atlas maps with equal reduction agree on the base-changed canonical localization tests. The coefficient comparisons preserve the coordinate series, and naturality identifies them with extensions of the original series on the selected principal subcover. Integral rigidity for scalar-divisible source laws and scalar-power torsion target laws detects equality there. Equality on this subcover detects the whole atlas map.

Flat extension of scalars preserves this injectivity on actual rational Hom. Transporting to the supplied target algebra and keeping the full order action gives detection of full-order rational maps, hence of quasi-isogenies by their forward maps.
