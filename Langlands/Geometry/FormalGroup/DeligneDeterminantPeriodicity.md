# Cyclic degree and Deligne's normalized determinant lattices

Drinfeld's *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2A, printed p. 110 (PDF p. 4), condition (c), states the determinant equality for every integer index. This module derives its equivalent form with one canonical index for each cyclic degree.

On the actual total rational stalk, the \(d\)-th power of the Pi automorphism is multiplication by \(\pi\). The presheaf relation passes to the stalk by the sheafification stalk isomorphism. In the original presheaf, scalar endomorphisms commute with germs, so the induced scalar map is multiplication by the same coefficient.

Advancing the integer index by \(d\) therefore multiplies the translated frame lattice by \(\pi\). Multilinearity makes its \(d\)-fold exterior lattice scale by \(\pi^d\). The change in the normalizing factor \(\pi^{-n}\) cancels this scale, giving period \(d\). Every integer differs from the least nonnegative representative of its degree by an integer multiple of \(d\), so the actual normalized determinant lattice equals its evaluation at that representative. Substituting this equality in condition (c) yields the equivalent test on \(i \in \mathbb Z/d\mathbb Z\), at every point of the same tangent Pi-zero locus.
