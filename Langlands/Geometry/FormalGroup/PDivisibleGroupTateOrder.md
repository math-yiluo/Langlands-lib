# Integral actions on schematic Tate points

A ring action on a p-divisible group acts on its schematic Tate sections through the additive Tate point functor. An order element acts at each finite component by postcomposition with its homomorphism of the actual finite group scheme. This gives the sections on any scheme test a left module structure over the acting ring. Restriction is linear, and an intertwining homomorphism of the original towers gives a linear morphism of the resulting presheaves.

For a subcanonical topology, the module presheaf is a sheaf because its underlying additive presheaf is the schematic Tate point sheaf. The construction applies to any ring acting on an actual p-divisible group, including the integral division order acting on a tower of special formal modules.

Harris, *Supercuspidal representations in the cohomology of Drinfel'd upper half spaces* (1997), Appendix, printed p. 115 (PDF p. 41), takes the Tate module of the universal object on the rigid generic fibre with its integral order action. The module here is the corresponding construction on scheme tests for an arbitrary given tower.
