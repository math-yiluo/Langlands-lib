# Integral units in Harris's rational-character kernel

In Harris, *Supercuspidal representations in the cohomology of Drinfel'd's symmetric space* (1997), Appendix (A.2), printed p. 114 (PDF p. 40), the group `C_p` is the common kernel of the absolute values of the rational characters. `splitRationalCharacterKernel` forms this intersection on the actual split similitude group. Each character is a group-scheme map into the multiplicative group and is evaluated on the rational point corresponding to a similitude. Its value lies in the integral scalar units exactly when its absolute value equals one.

The actual split rational point parametrization is continuous in the two unit coordinates. The finite-algebra and scalar unit maps land in the canonical rational-point topologies of their group schemes. The pairing operation for relative scheme points combines them into the product. With a continuous inclusion of local fields and the module topology on the division algebra, the continuous scalar action and finite dimensionality make the opposite-algebra basis coordinates continuous.

The two integral unit coordinates form a compact subgroup. The division-order factor is the continuous image of the maximal compact subgroup under passage to opposite units, and the multiplier factor is the maximal compact subgroup of the scalar units. Every rational character evaluation is continuous. Its image on the integral subgroup is therefore a compact subgroup of the scalar units and so consists of integral units. This proves the inclusion of the integral split subgroup in the common character kernel.

The containment holds for a finite-dimensional division algebra over the local field `F` with a specified finite basis of its opposite algebra over the scalar local field `k`. The proof uses the scalar tower and continuity of the inclusion `k → F`. These are the local topological conditions used for the containment. Identifying the reverse inclusion requires a character that detects the first valuation coordinate.
