# Dividing commuting endomorphisms of the Lubin–Tate line by the uniformizer

The coefficient action and arithmetic Frobenius are actual homomorphisms of the line over the original scalar order. Commuting with arithmetic Frobenius forces the tangent scalar to lie in the image of the original residue field. A lift to the original scalar order therefore gives a scalar endomorphism with the same derivative.

A commuting map with derivative zero kills the kernel of the scalar uniformizer by the degree bound from the linear Teichmüller action. The fppf factorization of formal homomorphisms gives division by the uniformizer, and cancellation of the locally surjective uniformizer shows that the factor still commutes with the coefficient action and Frobenius. This proves the scalar digit step on the line used for the rational endomorphisms of the frame in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 A–B, printed pp. 109–110.
