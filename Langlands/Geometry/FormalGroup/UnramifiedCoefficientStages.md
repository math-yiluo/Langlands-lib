# Finite étale stages of the unramified coefficients

`unramifiedIntegerStages F` consists of the actual integer subalgebras of
finite intermediate fields in the separable inertia fixed field of `F`.
A finite stage is étale over the norm-defined base integers. The maximal-ideal
and residue-field conditions give formal unramifiedness, and finite freeness
supplies flatness and finite presentation. The existing comparison of actual
integer rings transports formal étaleness to the original base integers.

The compositum contains any two stages, and the Library's unramified
exhaustion places every integer in a finite stage. Lifting along this directed
family proves that the actual uncompleted maximal unramified integer ring is
formally étale over the original integer ring.

This is the uncompleted coefficient step in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic
symmetric regions* (1976), §2A, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 3). The construction
uses the fields and their actual integer subalgebras inside the fixed field.
