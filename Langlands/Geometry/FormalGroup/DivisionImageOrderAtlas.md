# Division order on the formal image atlas

For the split order of Rapoport–Zink 1996, (6.17), printed p. 309 (PDF p. 321), the factor endomorphism `(0, b)` commutes with the projector `(0, 1)`. Restriction along the local formal retraction gives the order action in each chart. Over a test algebra the extended action is the restriction of the common ambient action, by the same coefficient identity used to compare the inclusions and projections.

The transition between two images of the projector is the restriction of the ambient identity. Composition of restrictions for maps commuting with the projector proves that the transition intertwines each division-order endomorphism. Thus the formal atlas of the image carries the actual induced division order action.
