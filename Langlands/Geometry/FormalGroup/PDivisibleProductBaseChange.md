# Pullback of the actual product tower

`baseChangeProdIso` identifies the pullback of \(G\times H\) with the
product of the pullbacks of \(G\) and \(H\). Its two projections are the
pullbacks of the original projections. Its inverse is the sum of the
maps through the two pulled-back factor inclusions.

The coordinate identities hold on the actual finite levels. Additive
pullback preserves them and the identity obtained by adding the two
coordinate endomorphisms. These equations prove the inverse identities.
The two projections also prove naturality for a pair of tower maps.

The comparison applies to the product in the split moduli problem of
Rapoport–Zink (1996), §6.43, printed p. 309 (PDF p. 321), and the
special-fibre pullback in Harris (1997), Appendix, printed p. 113
(PDF p. 39), condition (iii) of C1.
