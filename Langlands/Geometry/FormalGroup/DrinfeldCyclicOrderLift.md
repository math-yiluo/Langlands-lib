# Extending the cyclic generators

Suppose a coefficient homomorphism and an element of a ring satisfy the
power relation and the Frobenius commutation relation. The unique integral
cyclic expansion gives an additive map from the actual division order
by evaluating the coefficient homomorphism and the powers of the given
element. The power relation shows that the map evaluates monomials of
every degree, including degrees at least the reduced degree.

Moving coefficients past powers of the uniformizer iterates Frobenius.
Both rings satisfy the same monomial product formula, so bilinearity
makes the additive map a ring homomorphism. It has the specified values
on the generators and these values determine it on every integral
element. This realizes the universal property of the cyclic presentation
used by Drinfel'd. [V. G. Drinfel'd, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions*, 1976, §2B, p. 110](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01077936)
