# Central chart sections of the split primal tensor

On each test chart, extend the actual local quasi-isogeny and project it by the source and target division retractions. Centralize this projected section and transport it to the central atlas law by the integral chart comparisons. `centralFramingOnChart` gives the resulting section.

The section of the global central atlas tensor agrees with this section. An ambient integral numerator presenting a prime-power multiple of the rigidifier gives a numerator on every chart. The projected integral chart map and its central version use the same coordinates through the integral comparisons. Consequently both rational sections are given by this common central map divided by the same prime power.

Given zero prime-normalized height of the projected local sections, the scalar calibration \(p=u\varpi^e\), and the actual central source height, the local rank comparison gives zero uniformizer-normalized height on every chart. This is the height condition for the primal factor in Rapoport–Zink, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310.
