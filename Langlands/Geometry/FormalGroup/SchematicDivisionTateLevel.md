# Division-uniformizer classes of schematic Tate trivializations

On a scheme test, an integral trivialization is an order-linear isomorphism from the actual schematic Tate sections to the division order as a left regular module. The maximal compact subgroup is canonically the unit group of that order. A unit acts on the right by postcomposing the trivialization with right multiplication.

Choose an element of the ambient division unit group of norm less than one. At a positive index `N`, the level is the set of orbits under the published congruence subgroup at the radius of its `N`-th power, restricted to the maximal compact subgroup. For a division uniformizer this is the level `1 + Π^N O_D`. Translation descends to the published finite quotient. A finer level maps to a coarser level by retaining the representative trivialization, and this map commutes with the quotient transition on units.

Harris, *Supercuspidal representations in the cohomology of Drinfel'd upper half spaces* (1997), Appendix, printed pp. 115–116 (PDF pp. 41–42), directs the trivializations from the Tate module to the regular left order and describes the right actions on the rigid tower. This construction concerns the division-order coordinate on scheme tests for a given actual tower. The comparison with polarized similitude frames on the rigid generic fibre requires further geometric results.
