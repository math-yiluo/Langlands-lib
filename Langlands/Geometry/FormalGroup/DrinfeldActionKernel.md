# Trivial subgroups of Drinfeld's action

Drinfel'd, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 A, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 3), states that the division integer units and the opposite central pairs act trivially on the isomorphism classes of framed triples.

An integral division unit has zero signed exponent. Its matrix and Frobenius factors are identities. Its remaining target correction is the rationalization of the actual integral inverse-unit map. Coefficient extension of the integral order endomorphism agrees with the order endomorphism on the extended atlas by their complete chart series. Thus the transformed triple is the one equipped with the existing integral comparison from the original triple, and they define the same class.

On an opposite central pair the determinant order cancels the reduced-norm order. Central conjugation fixes the order action. The inverse matrix acts by the inverse scalar and the inverse division factor by the scalar. Their actions cancel in the whole rational framing tensor, so the representative is unchanged.

The two families lie in the kernel of the natural action. `actionQuotientHom` descends it to the quotient by the normal subgroup they generate. The construction has the same fixed-frame and matrix-coordinate inputs as the natural action.
