# Order-linear tensor recovery on affine bases

For a local quasi-isogeny on the full affine spectrum, `toOrderTensor` recovers the tensor and its inverse with the stipulated order actions. To check its order commutation equation, extend the two tensor composites to the rational Hom sheaf and use the equation there. Faithfulness on tensors proves the equality. Invertibility then gives commutation with the order for the inverse.

The same faithfulness compares the recovered tensors after affine coefficient pullback, conjugation by integral coordinate isomorphisms, and composition. For each equation, the two sides extend to the same rational section through the corresponding operation on the sheaf.

This supplies an affine comparison for the order-linear primal quasi-isogeny of Rapoport–Zink, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310.
