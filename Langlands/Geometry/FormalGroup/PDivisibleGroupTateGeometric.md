# Geometric finite-level quotients of schematic Tate points

On the spectrum of an algebraically closed field over the base, every transfer between finite levels is surjective on points. The tower's affine fppf exactness supplies a lift after a faithfully flat finitely presented extension. That extension is a nonzero finite-type algebra over the field. The Nullstellensatz gives a field-valued specialization, and restricting the lift along that specialization recovers the original point.

The countable inverse-limit projection is therefore surjective on a geometric test at every level. The first isomorphism theorem identifies the quotient by the kernel of projection with the actual points of the finite group scheme.

The proof uses the scalar torsion system of Rapoport and Zink, *Period Spaces for p-divisible Groups* (1996), §2, printed pp. 50–51 (PDF pp. 65–66). This geometric-point computation takes place on scheme tests. Harris, *Supercuspidal representations in the cohomology of Drinfel'd upper half spaces* (1997), Appendix, printed p. 115 (PDF p. 41), constructs his levels on the rigid generic fibre.
