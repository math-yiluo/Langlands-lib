# Order-linear local quasi-isogenies over the central field

The central rational section of an order-linear local quasi-isogeny is assembled from its actual integral isogeny numerators on the fixed-coordinate base. The original discrete valuation ring has the natural-number uniformizer `p`. Both central tangent actions are structural. Assume `p` and `ϖ` have nonzero images in the target coefficient field. If `p = u * ϖ^e` in the central ring, the presentation with denominator `p^n` can be given denominator `ϖ^(e*n)` by multiplying the integral map by the inverse unit power. That change preserves isogeny, since the unit scalar is an actual formal automorphism. With central `ϖ`-divisibility of the two laws, this proves the local quasi-isogeny condition over the central field.

The integral numerator commutes with the order after changing central scalars. Therefore each local central section commutes with the order, and the equalities glue on the actual rational Hom sheaf.

This construction applies to the split primal quasi-isogeny of Rapoport–Zink 1996, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310 (PDF pp. 321–322). Definition 2.8, printed p. 52 (PDF p. 67), supplies the original local isogeny numerator convention. The central height normalization used in the supporting modules is a kernel-rank calculation. Definition 2.6 and the height paragraph, printed p. 51 (PDF p. 66), use base `p` for the isogeny height. The comparison after Proposition 3.56, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 123), gives the residue-degree factor for heights of isogenies of the associated crystals.
