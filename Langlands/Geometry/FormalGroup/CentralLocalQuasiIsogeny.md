# Order-linear local quasi-isogenies over the central field

The central rational section of an order-linear local quasi-isogeny is assembled from its actual integral isogeny numerators. If `p = u * ϖ^e`, the numerator with denominator `p^n` can be presented with denominator `ϖ^(e*n)` by multiplying the integral map by the inverse unit power. That change preserves isogeny, since the unit scalar is an actual formal automorphism. With the central scalar divisibility of the two laws, this proves the full local quasi-isogeny condition over the central field.

The integral numerator commutes with the order after changing central scalars. Therefore each local central section commutes with the order, and the equalities glue on the actual rational Hom sheaf.

This construction applies to the split primal quasi-isogeny of Rapoport–Zink 1996, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310 (PDF pp. 321–322).
