# Drinfeld's fixed framed functor and its action

The functor uses the special formal module constructed over the residue field of the completed maximal unramified integers. On an original scalar `Nilp` algebra its values are classes of height-zero framed triples. The residue map varies with the triple. The matrix action uses the constructed identification of the frame's rational full-order endomorphisms with field matrices.

For a matrix and division pair, the residue map is composed with the inverse arithmetic Frobenius power specified by the sum of determinant and reduced-norm orders. The target order action is conjugated by the division component. The representative action uses the original framing precomposed with the inverse matrix, inverse division and signed relative Frobenius maps in the order implemented in `DrinfeldResidue.actionSourceHom`. The quotient action retains the natural transformations under every `Nilp` base change. Integral division units and anti-diagonal central pairs act trivially.

These are the height-zero functor and the action in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 A, printed p. 109. The special frame and matrix coordinates come from the arithmetic presentation of §2 B, printed p. 110.
