# Schematic Tate points of a p-divisible group

For a p-divisible group over an affine scheme, take the group of points of each of its finite group schemes on every scheme over the base. The maps in the inverse diagram are the tower's multiplication transfers evaluated on these tests. The schematic Tate point presheaf is the inverse limit of that diagram in additive group valued presheaves.

A morphism of p-divisible groups gives a map of the point diagrams through its actual homomorphisms at each level. The resulting Tate point functor is additive. Its projection to each finite level commutes with the tower transfers and with morphisms of p-divisible groups. Each finite point presheaf is a sheaf for a subcanonical topology, and the inverse limit remains a sheaf.

This is the scheme-test construction associated with the finite group levels in Rapoport and Zink, *Period Spaces for p-divisible Groups* (1996), §2, printed pp. 50–51 (PDF pp. 65–66). Harris, *Supercuspidal representations in the cohomology of Drinfel'd upper half spaces* (1997), Appendix, printed p. 115 (PDF p. 41), uses the Tate module on the rigid generic fibre of the universal object for level structures. The inverse-limit construction here concerns an arbitrary given schematic p-divisible group.
