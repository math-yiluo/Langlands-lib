# Cyclic relations on the division order and its tangent representation

Drinfeld's cyclic presentation uses a division uniformizer \(\Pi\) and an
arithmetic Frobenius \(\sigma\) on the degree-\(d\) unramified subfield. The
relations \(\Pi^d=\pi\) and \(\Pi a=\sigma(a)\Pi\) are supplied in the Library's
comparison statement. This development interprets them on the actual division
order and on the tangent module of a special formal module.

The reduced-norm valuation of \(\Pi^d\) is at most one, so the positivity of
\(d\) places \(\Pi\) in the order. The power relation then holds in the order
by injectivity of its inclusion into the division algebra. The subfield
Frobenius preserves the restricted valuation and fixes the base scalars. Its
restriction therefore acts by an algebra automorphism of the integer subfield,
where the commutation relation also holds integrally.

The actual fibre tangent representation is a ring homomorphism. Applied to the
commutation relation, it sends a vector of character \(\chi\) under the tangent
uniformizer to a vector of character \(\chi\circ\sigma^{-1}\). The power relation
on the same representation makes the \(d\)th power act by the image of \(\pi\)
in the tangent coefficient ring. Both statements apply to the identity ring map
as well as to maps to geometric fibres.

Source: Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2B, printed
p. 110 (PDF p. 4), the cyclic relations and the degree of the uniformizer action.
