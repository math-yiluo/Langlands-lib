# Block matrices of rational formal endomorphisms

`copiesMatrixEquiv` identifies the rational scalar-linear endomorphism algebra of a finite product of one-coordinate formal modules with a matrix algebra over the rational endomorphism algebra of one factor. Its entries are actual composites of rational inclusions, maps and projections. The inverse assembles the entries using the finite sum of those composites.

The relations between the projections and inclusions survive rationalization. They prove the inverse identities, and inserting their sum in the middle of a composite proves the matrix multiplication formula. This block calculation is used to identify the order-linear rational automorphism group of the special frame in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 A, printed p. 109.
