# The actual affine tangent line sheaf of a special formal order module

Let \(B\) be an affine \(\mathcal O_K\)-algebra on which \(\pi\) is nilpotent, and let \(Y\) be a special formal order module with a specified embedded unramified degree-\(d\) coefficient field \(L\). The construction takes a residue embedding \(\chi:k_L\hookrightarrow\kappa\) over \(k_K\) and the residue coordinate \(\psi:\kappa\to B/(\pi)\) over \(\mathcal O_K\). The residue characters are indexed by the inverse powers of arithmetic Frobenius before applying \(\chi\) and \(\psi\). Unique finite étale lifting across the nilpotent ideal \((\pi)\) gives the characters over \(B\). They split \(B\otimes_{\mathcal O_K}\mathcal O_L\) as a product of \(d\) copies of \(B\), whose coordinate idempotents act as the tangent projectors.

The construction also takes \(\Pi^d=\pi\) and \(\Pi a=\sigma(a)\Pi\), where \(\sigma\) acts on \(k_L\) by arithmetic Frobenius. The derivative of \(\Pi\) takes the component indexed by \(i\) to the component indexed by \(i+1\) in \(\mathbf Z/d\mathbf Z\). Its \(d\)-th power is multiplication by the structural scalar \(\pi\).

The associated sheaf carries the splitting and operator on \(\operatorname{Spec} B\). Its homogeneous subsheaves are the sheaves of the projector images. Each image is finite projective, and geometric specialness gives rank one at every prime after extending to an algebraic closure of the residue field. This yields the affine tangent line datum in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 A, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 3), and §2 B, printed p. 110 (PDF p. 4).
