# Actual local quasi-isogeny groupoid laws

`LocalQuasiIsogeny.comp` composes local quasi-isogenies of scalar-divisible formal module laws on an open of an affine spectrum. The forward section is `RationalSection.comp` of the two input sections. `LocalQuasiIsogenyComposition` restricts the numerator isogenies to the intersection of their witnessing opens and adds their scalar exponents. Here `Hom.finiteKernelCompositionStatement` supplies the finite-kernel composition premise on each open coefficient algebra.

`LocalQuasiIsogeny.toSectionIso` chooses the unique pair of inverse rational sections furnished by `localQuasiIsogenyInverse`. Its forward section is the original local quasi-isogeny. The reverse section satisfies the local scalar-isogeny condition, so `LocalQuasiIsogeny.inv` uses that section with the source and target divisibility hypotheses exchanged.

With `LocalQuasiIsogeny.idOn`, these operations satisfy the identity, associativity and two-sided inverse equations on each open. Inversion is involutive and reverses composition. The identity law for coefficient pullback comes from `LocalQuasiIsogenyCoefficientCoherence`. Restriction, coefficient pullback along a homomorphism of algebras, and conjugation by integral isomorphisms preserve identity, composition and inversion. Equality of local quasi-isogenies is equality of their sections. For the inversion laws, equality of the forward sections identifies the corresponding invertible sections, including their inverse sections.

This calculus is derived from the Library's local scalar-isogeny and rational section definitions. Rapoport and Zink, *Period Spaces for p-divisible Groups* (1996), Definition 2.8, printed p. 52, gives the p-divisible group notion behind the rationalization. The Lean constructions here use finite coordinate types, retain the nonvanishing of the scalar in the coefficient field and require the source and target scalar-divisibility fields carried by `LocalQuasiIsogeny`.
