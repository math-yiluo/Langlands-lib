# The height-zero Drinfeld triple of a split primal rigidifier

The actual primal residue frame and special target atlas have kernel-rank height certificates. When the residue coefficient quotient is a field and the constructed central rigidifier has chart height zero, `primalDrinfeldFramedAtlas` builds a height-zero Drinfeld triple from them. The residue map is induced by the given coefficient structure. The target is the constructed special primal atlas, and the framing is its actual central tensor quasi-isogeny from the extended fixed frame.

The zero-height hypothesis is the verified local kernel-rank relation on every chart test for this specific tensor. It is the height condition in the Drinfeld moduli problem, as used for the split primal factor by Rapoport–Zink, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310. The construction uses the square-height normalization of Drinfeld 1976, §2 A, printed p. 109.
