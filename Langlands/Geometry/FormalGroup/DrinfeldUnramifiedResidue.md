# Drinfeld's functor with the actual maximal unramified residue field

Drinfel'd, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 A, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 3), fixes a special formal frame over the residue of the strict henselization and a matrix identification of its full-order rational endomorphisms. `UnramifiedFrame` is the type of height-certified special frames over the residue of the actual completed maximal unramified integer ring. The completion map identifies this residue with the residue of the integer ring before completion.

For a chosen frame, `unramifiedFunctor` uses the original scalar integer ring for `Nilp`. An object of the functor is a comparison class of a residue map to the reduced test algebra, a height-certified special target, and a height-zero framing quasi-isogeny to the reduction of that target. The residue map remains part of the triple as the test algebra varies.

Given the chosen matrix coordinates, `unramifiedAction` specializes the existing natural action on those classes. The algebraic-closure instance on the actual residue field supplies the hypothesis for its arithmetic Frobenius. The finite scalar residue field supplies a prime and a positive exponent for its cardinality, and the published Frobenius theorems provide the rank and inverse calculations. The resulting action shifts the exact residue map by inverse arithmetic Frobenius and transforms the target by inverse conjugation of its order action. Its framing is the original signed Frobenius, inverse division and inverse matrix composite followed by the old framing.
