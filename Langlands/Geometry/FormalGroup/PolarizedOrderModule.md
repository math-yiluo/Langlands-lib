# Polarized formal modules with an order action

`PolarizedOrderModule` packages a finite-coordinate formal law, an
actual action of the specified order, and scalar divisibility. Its
principal polarization is the coherent antisymmetric self-duality of
the represented torsion kernels. The order involution acts as the
adjoint on the relative Cartier characters.

This implements the polarized formal object used in Harris,
*Supercuspidal representations in the cohomology of Drinfel'd upper
half spaces* (1997), Appendix, printed p. 113 (PDF p. 39),
condition (ii), and the integral polarization isomorphisms of
Rapoport–Zink, *Period Spaces for p-divisible Groups* (1996),
Definition 3.21(v), printed p. 82 (PDF p. 96).
The base and the order with involution are arbitrary. The formal law
has a single finite coordinate system over the affine base.

An isomorphism is an actual integral order isomorphism preserving
the pairings. The inverse preserves the pairings by evaluating the
original isometry on the inverse images. Identity, inversion and
composition yield an equivalence relation. Arbitrary coefficient
extension acts on the formal laws, their order actions and
polarizations, and on these isomorphisms.
