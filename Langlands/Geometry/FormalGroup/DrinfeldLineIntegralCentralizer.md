# The integral commutant of the reduced Lubin–Tate line

Let \(L/K\) be a degree-\(d\) unramified subfield embedded in the central division algebra \(D/K\) of reduced degree \(d\). Write \(\mathcal O_L\) for its integer ring. The reduced Lubin–Tate line \(H\) uses the chosen scalar uniformizer \(\pi\) of \(K\) and the chosen residue embedding \(k_L\hookrightarrow\kappa\). The scalar identification assumes \(\kappa/k_K\) is algebraic. The ambient ring is the ring of actual \(\mathcal O_K\)-linear formal endomorphisms of \(H\) over \(\kappa\). The commutant consists of endomorphisms that commute with the entire \(\mathcal O_L\)-action and with the arithmetic Frobenius endomorphism \(X\mapsto X^q\), where \(q=|k_K|\). The supplied automorphism of \(L/K\) induces this \(q\)-power on \(k_L\).

The scalar digit argument applies over a complete discrete valuation ring to a formal module whose points are killed individually by powers of its uniformizer. The chosen predicate on endomorphisms must admit a scalar digit and a new endomorphism satisfying the predicate after subtracting that digit and dividing by the uniformizer. Iteration yields compatible partial scalars and remainders. Completeness gives a scalar limit. Every point agrees with a sufficiently long truncation, so extensionality on all coefficient algebras identifies the limit with the given endomorphism. Here the predicate is precisely the two commutation conditions.

`drinfeldLineScalarEndEquiv` sends an original integer scalar to its endomorphism of \(H\). Injectivity writes a nonzero scalar as a unit times a uniformizer power, cancels the fppf locally surjective uniformizer power, and uses the derivative of the identity. The reduced line comes from Lubin and Tate, *Formal Complex Multiplication in Local Fields* (1965), §1, Theorem 1, printed p. 382 (PDF p. 4). This calculation supplies an integral step toward the frame endomorphisms in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 A–B, printed pp. 109–110 (PDF pp. 3–4).
