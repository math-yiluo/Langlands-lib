# Base change of the scalar-power torsion diagrams

Let \(M\) be a scalar-divisible formal module over \(R\), and let
\(R \to S\) be a map of coefficient algebras. Extending coefficients
commutes with the actual endomorphism \([\pi^n]\).
`IsPiDivisible.torsionPullbackIso` therefore identifies the represented
\(\pi^n\)-kernel of the extended law with the scheme pullback of the
original kernel. The comparison preserves the underlying formal points
on the global sections of every test scheme.

This point formula gives three naturality squares. Inclusion leaves
the underlying point unchanged. Scalar transition is evaluation of the
same scalar endomorphism after the coefficient change. An integral
formal homomorphism gives the same evaluation square on the two
kernels. Since their points determine maps between the represented
group schemes, these equations give the scheme naturality squares.
The first two assemble the comparisons into isomorphisms of the direct
and inverse torsion diagrams.

The proofs use arbitrary coefficient maps and actual finite kernel
schemes. This is internal functoriality of the formal scalar torsion
construction.
