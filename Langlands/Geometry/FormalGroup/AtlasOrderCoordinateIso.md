# Comparison of order atlases by coordinate series

On a common principal cover, suppose the formal addition, the order action and the transition maps agree as coordinate series on every chart test. The identity coordinates give integral isomorphisms of the formal laws. To check the scalar equations, use the image of each scalar in the order action. The matching order series also make each chart isomorphism order-linear.

The identity maps are natural under coefficient extension. The matching transitions give the atlas commutation equations. The inverse maps use the same identity coordinates with the comparisons reversed, so both atlas composites are the identities. This construction compares alternate local presentations of the primal summand in Rapoport–Zink, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310.
