# Integral similitudes of actual scalar torsion polarizations

`Hom.IsTorsionSimilitude` is the multiplier equation for an integral
formal homomorphism. On each scalar-power kernel and every relative
scheme test, it evaluates the target pairing on the two images.
The resulting point of the multiplicative group must equal the
source pairing with the stated scalar acting on its first argument.
All maps in this equation are induced by the actual integral formal
homomorphisms, including the scalar endomorphism.

The identity has multiplier one. Multipliers multiply under
composition, since an integral formal module map commutes with scalar
endomorphisms. The kernel pullback comparisons and preservation of the
evaluated unit give the same multiplier equation after arbitrary
coefficient extension.

This is the integral condition used to express the polarization
comparison in Harris's moduli problem after clearing denominators.
See Harris, *Supercuspidal representations in the cohomology of
Drinfel'd upper half spaces* (1997), Appendix, printed p. 113
(PDF p. 39), condition (iii).
