# Cyclically graded sheaves for Deligne's construction

This internal infrastructure implements sheaves with an actual distinguished
operator satisfying the quotient relation from Drinfeld (1976), §2 A,
printed pp.109–110 (PDF pp.3–4). It is preparation for the sheaf data in
Deligne's point description, rather than a construction of that entire
moduli functor.

`PiSheaf d R a` consists of a genuine sheaf of modules over the commutative
ring sheaf R, a linear sheaf endomorphism Π, and the equation Π^d=a on every
section module. Here a is an actual compatible family of sections of R.
For each object of the site, `representation` constructs a ring
homomorphism from the actual polynomial quotient `PiAlgebra d a` to the
linear endomorphisms of that section module. The proof factors polynomial
evaluation through the ideal generated by X^d−a; this ideal is in the
kernel by the defining equation. The distinguished generator acts by Π,
and coefficients act by their existing scalar multiplication. The coefficient
quotients form an actual ring-valued presheaf, with the restriction maps
constructed from the ring maps of R and the compatibility of a. Restriction
on module sections intertwines the full quotient action. If a is nilpotent
on an object, the corresponding linear operator is nilpotent as well.

Morphisms are actual linear sheaf maps commuting with Π. They form a
category with a faithful functor to sheaves of modules. Their compatibility
with every element of the quotient algebra is proved by polynomial
induction, including semilinear coefficient maps in the auxiliary lemma.

For nonzero d, `GradedPiSheaf` adds genuine homogeneous subsheaves indexed
by Z/dZ. Their section modules are required to form an internal direct sum,
and Π sends degree i into degree i+1. `componentSheaf` and `componentPi`
construct the homogeneous sheaves and the maps between them; their
composition with the inclusions is the original operator.

The site category, ring values and module values are in a common universe.
No sheaf condition for the newly constructed coefficient presheaf is asserted.

This module imposes no flatness, local rank, rational frame, fibre condition
or exterior-power condition, and constructs no Deligne points, change of
base functor, group action or representing object. Those conditions and
constructions remain separate obligations. The provenance is internal:
quotient evaluation and subsheaf restriction are elementary operations on
the existing concrete rings and sheaves, and no unproved moduli theorem
is supplied as input.
