# The atlas of a globally coordinated projector image

A global formal projector retraction restricts to every member of a prescribed principal cover. The resulting image atlas equals the ordinary cover atlas of the global image law. On a test algebra, the two extended retractions use the same global inclusion and projection, whose composite on the image is the identity. This gives the identity-coordinate transitions of the global-law atlas.

For the division projector of the split order, the order series on these charts are the coefficient extensions of the global division action. This applies to the fixed frame in the split comparison of Rapoport–Zink, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310.
