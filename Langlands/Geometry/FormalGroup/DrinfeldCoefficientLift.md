# Unramified coefficient maps for Drinfeld's residue coordinates

Drinfeld uses the degree-\(d\) unramified integer subring inside the division
order in §2B of *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), p. 110. The
residue coordinate of a framed point gives a map from the algebraic closure of
the residue field into \(B/(\pi)\). This construction lifts the resulting
characters of the embedded integer subring to \(B\).

The actual reduction ideal is nilpotent because its generator is nilpotent.
Formal smoothness provides a lift across that ideal and formal unramifiedness
gives uniqueness. The quotient square for an algebra map proves compatibility
with affine base change by that uniqueness.

The base map to the embedded integer ring is local. On a base scalar the
restricted division valuation is the positive reduced-degree power of the
base valuation, so it reflects units. The embedded residue field is finite
over the base residue field and embeds into the supplied algebraic closure.
One can choose a character by composing this embedding with reduction. The
lift construction applies to each specified character \( \chi \) and its
composite with the actual residue coordinate.

Source: Drinfeld, §2A–B, printed pp. 109–110 (PDF pp. 3–4).
