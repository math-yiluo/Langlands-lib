# Integral maps of formal projector-image atlases

This construction applies to the projector onto the primal formal division module of Rapoport–Zink, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310. Let an integral map of ambient formal laws intertwine their projectors. Choose image atlases on a common principal cover. On every chart test algebra, compose the source inclusion, the extended ambient map, and the target projection.

The resulting formal maps commute with coefficient extension by the naturality of each coefficient lift. They commute with the transitions because composing through an image retract inserts its projector, which acts as the identity on the image. The same identity proves that the atlas construction preserves the integral identity and composition.
