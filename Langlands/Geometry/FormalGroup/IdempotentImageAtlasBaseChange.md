# Coefficient extension of projector-image atlases

The actual image retractions used for the primal summand of Rapoport–Zink, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310, extend along coefficient maps. For each principal generator on the new base, choose the same original generator as in the coefficient extension of the atlas. Extend its retraction to the new localization. The commutative coefficient square identifies the two ambient laws by an integral coordinate isomorphism, so the new inclusion and projection retract the extended projector.

The image chart laws are those of the extended atlas. Their transition maps have the same coordinates as the extended transitions, since both are given by the extended source inclusion and target projection. `baseChange_atlas` identifies the two actual atlases.
