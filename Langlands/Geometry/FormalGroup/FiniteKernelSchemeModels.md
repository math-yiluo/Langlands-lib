# Canonical comparisons of represented finite kernel schemes

Each `FiniteKernelModel` represents the actual kernel of a formal-module homomorphism by a finite projective coordinate algebra. Finite projectivity gives finite presentation as a module, which gives finite presentation as an algebra. The spectrum of that algebra is therefore locally of finite presentation over the base. This applies also to each represented scalar torsion scheme in the inverse tower.

Two choices of model for the same kernel give canonically isomorphic commutative group schemes. The forward and reverse maps are both represented by the identity on actual kernel points. Their composites are the identity, and their evaluation on every affine point preserves its kernel element.

The comparisons compose across three models and commute with the scheme map of every natural linear map of actual kernel point functors. These are internal consequences of the Library's finite-kernel corepresentations and allow subsequent dual constructions to compare different choices of finite projective model.
