# Cyclic grading of the Drinfeld tangent representation

For a `Nilp` test algebra \(B\) with a residue coordinate, the lifted
unramified characters split the base change of the actual coefficient ring
into \(d\) copies of \(B\), where \(d\) is the reduced degree. The derivative
of the order action on \(B^d\) extends to the tensor product by letting
\(b \otimes a\) act as \(b\) times the derivative of \(a\). Transporting
this action along the splitting sends each coordinate idempotent to a
projector. The projectors are pairwise orthogonal and sum to the identity.

The image of a projector is its fixed submodule. On that image each
coefficient acts by its value at the selected character. Conversely, tensor
induction extends the character equation to the split algebra, so the
coordinate idempotent fixes every vector satisfying that equation. The
splitting and tensor action commute with affine base change, which gives
the corresponding equation for the projectors on tangent vectors.

Formal étale uniqueness identifies the successor of a lifted character
with its composite with inverse integral Frobenius. The order relation
\(\Pi a = \sigma(a)\Pi\) then shows that the derivative of \(\Pi\) maps the
image for \(i\) into the image for \(i+1\). This derives the tangent
grading and degree-one shift from the coefficient characters and cyclic
relation in Drinfel'd's construction. [V. G. Drinfel'd, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions*, 1976, §2B, p. 110](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01077936)
