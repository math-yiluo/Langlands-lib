# Rational sections from central scalar numerators

For a local presentation of an order-linear rigidifier as an isogeny numerator divided by a natural-number scalar power, use the same numerator with the central formal module laws. Divide it by the same power in the new coefficient field. This gives an actual rational Hom section on the presenting open.

Suppose two such presentations have equal original sections after restriction to an overlap. Cross multiplication gives equality of the two scaled integral maps there, by faithfulness of the original integral Hom. Central scalar change preserves the coordinates of both maps. A natural-number scalar power acts by the same coordinates before and after this change. Therefore the new numerators satisfy the same cross multiplication equation. The corresponding scalars are nonzero in the new field, so the new rational sections agree on the overlap.

This is a construction for the order-linear rigidification of the primal summand in Rapoport–Zink 1996, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310 (PDF pp. 321–322).
