# Pullback of order-linear quasi-isogenies

`OrderQuasiIsogeny.baseChange` pulls back the actual rational map and
its inverse. Applying the rational pullback to the intertwining equation
shows that it is linear for the pulled-back order actions.

`baseChangeIdOrderIso` and `baseChangeCompOrderIso` give the order-linear
comparisons for identity and successive pullback in the category up to
isogeny. Naturality of the integral comparisons for every order
endomorphism gives the equations before rationalization.

These constructions apply to the pullbacks of the order-linear rigidifier
in Harris (1997), Appendix, printed p. 113 (PDF p. 39), C1(iii).
The corresponding pullback of the rational morphism in a framed
deformation occurs in Rapoport–Zink (1996), Definition 2.15,
printed p. 54 (PDF p. 69).
