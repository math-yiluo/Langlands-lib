# Chart evaluation of the compatible formal tangent module

On a test algebra where a chart generator is a unit, the compatible tangent module is canonically the coordinate tangent module of that chart. The forward map restricts the tuple to the chosen localization, then evaluates along the localization's map back to the test algebra. The inverse extends the vector to each chart localization and applies the differentiated formal transition. Naturality for these transitions follows by mapping their linear series coefficients, and their cocycle verifies the overlap equations of the inverse.

To verify the identities, the factor map from the chosen localization composed with the structural localization map recovers the original vector. In the other direction, the overlap equation between that chart and any other chart can be evaluated in the other chart's canonical localization. Uniqueness of the localization maps identifies the resulting terms with the regenerated and original entries. This gives an equivalence of modules over the test algebra.

This local identification applies to the tangent module in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 B, printed p. 110 (PDF p. 4).
