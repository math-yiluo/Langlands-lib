# The arithmetic Frobenius operator of the reduced Lubin–Tate line

The arithmetic coefficient automorphism reduces to the residue-cardinality power. By coefficient covariance of the integral Lubin–Tate law, this power fixes the reduced addition series and acts on each reduced scalar series by that coefficient automorphism. Thus the arithmetic power of the variable is an actual endomorphism of the reduced formal group and satisfies the semilinear conjugation relation with the embedded integers.

The embedded residue field has cardinality `q^d`. The `d`-fold iterate of this endomorphism therefore equals the reduced scalar uniformizer. These are the relations needed to use the line in the cyclic action of Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 B, printed p. 110. The scalar polynomial follows from Lubin and Tate, *Formal Complex Multiplication in Local Fields* (1965), §1, Theorem 1, printed p. 382.
