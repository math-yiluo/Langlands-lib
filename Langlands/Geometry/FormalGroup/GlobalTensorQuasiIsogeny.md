# Affine tensor recovery for local quasi-isogenies

The actual tensor of integral Hom with the scalar fraction field maps to the rational Hom sheaf. For scalar-divisible coordinate formal modules over an affine base, a local quasi-isogeny on the full spectrum comes from a unique tensor quasi-isogeny.

Quasicompactness supplies a finite cover from the witnessing scalar-power presentations. Raise each numerator to the common denominator given by the largest exponent on that cover. The rational sections of these integral maps coincide on intersections, where scalar divisibility gives faithfulness of the integral-to-rational map. The integral numerators therefore glue over the full structure sections. The affine coefficient isomorphism recovers an integral map over the original ring, and dividing by the uniformizer power gives the tensor.

Faithfulness on tensors follows by clearing a uniformizer denominator of the difference and applying faithfulness on integral maps over the full structure sections. Recover the inverse tensor from the inverse local quasi-isogeny. Both inverse identities follow from their sheaf counterparts by faithfulness.

This is the affine comparison between local-coordinate maps and rationalized Hom for the formal modules of Drinfeld 1976, §1, printed pp. 108–109. It also applies to the central formal modules in Rapoport–Zink, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310.
