# Order-linearity of integral numerators

Two integral formal maps on an open are equal if they agree on neighbourhoods of all its points. Indeed, restriction maps each power-series coefficient through restriction of the actual structure sheaf. At any point of the open the equal restricted coefficients have equal values in the corresponding localization. This determines the coefficients as structure sections.

For a scalar-divisible source over a discrete valuation ring, rational equality of integral maps in the sheaf gives such local integral equality. Thus the map from integral homomorphisms to rational sections is injective on each open. If a scalar multiple of an order-linear rational section on an open is represented by an integral map, its order commutation equations become integral equations on that entire open, by injectivity. The construction equips the actual numerator with those equations.

This is a derived fact for the order-linear rigidifier on the split summand in Rapoport–Zink 1996, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310 (PDF pp. 321–322).
