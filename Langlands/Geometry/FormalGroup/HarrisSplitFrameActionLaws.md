# Group laws for the split affine frame action

`pointFrameAction` gives the action on actual `SplitPDivisibleAffinePoint`
data for a square-height frame and the ramification factorization of `p`
used to form its primal tower. A frame similitude consists of a rational
order automorphism of the primal frame and a nonzero `ℚ_p` multiplier.
The action pulls back the automorphism to the special fibre, inverts it
and precomposes the primal rigidifier. It multiplies the original scalar
by the inverse frame multiplier.

For the identity frame similitude, these operations fix the rigidifier
and scalar. For a product, functoriality of pullback and associativity of
composition give the left action law on the primal rigidifier. Inverse
multiplication in the scalar group gives the same law on the multiplier.
The target formal module is retained throughout.

This is the inverse-precomposition action in Harris (1997), Appendix,
printed p. 113 (PDF p. 39), for the polarized framed moduli problem.
Compare Rapoport–Zink (1996), Definition 3.22, printed p. 82 (PDF p. 96).
