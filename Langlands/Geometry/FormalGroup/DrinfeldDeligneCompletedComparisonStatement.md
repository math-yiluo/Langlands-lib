# Drinfeld's equivariant comparison over the completed unramified base

`DrinfeldDeligneCompletedComparisonStatement` gives the natural comparison in
Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2A, printed p. 109
(PDF p. 3). The fixed frame is a special formal module of height `d²` over
the actual unramified residue field. The statement uses the cyclic
uniformizer and Frobenius relations from §2B, printed p. 110 (PDF p. 4),
and the matrix coordinates of the fixed frame.

On each affine nilpotent test of the original integer ring, a Deligne point
of the completed base change has an admissible Deligne class and an actual
map from the completed unramified integers. The natural isomorphism must
recover that coefficient map by the unique lift of the framed class's
residue map. This coefficient identity also specifies the fibres over tests
of the completed integer ring.

The action equation tests every transformed framed representative. The
Drinfeld formula uses the inverse matrix action, conjugation on the order
action, and signed relative Frobenius. On the Deligne side the action uses
the projective class and the negative power of arithmetic Frobenius on the
actual completed coefficient map, with exponent determined by the
determinant and reduced-norm orders.
