# Polarized rational section isomorphisms

`RationalSection.IsOrderLinear` requires the actual rational
section to intertwine the sections of every order endomorphism.
This property survives composition, coefficient pullback and
integral order comparisons. The two inverse equations show that
the inverse section also intertwines the order maps.

`PolarizedOrderModule.RationalSimilitudeIso` is an actual invertible
order-linear section with a certified \(p\)-adic multiplier in the
forward direction and its reciprocal in the inverse direction.
The multiplier is an existential property. Equality is determined
by the forward section, which determines the inverse. Identity,
inversion and composition use the actual section operations and
the multiplier laws. Both equations survive coefficient pullback
and transport along integral polarized isometries. An integral
polarized isometry supplies both equations with multiplier one.

The two pairing certificates allow a change of polarized residue
frame by precomposing with the inverse. This is the operation used
for the action on \(C_1\) in Harris (1997), Appendix, printed
p. 113 (PDF p. 39). The construction works with specified
invertible sections and their two pairing certificates.
