# Coefficient base change of the Drinfeld tangent projectors

Tangent matrices are the linear coefficients of the actual order homomorphisms, so they transport entrywise under a map of affine test algebras. The natural splitting of the unramified coefficients gives the same compatibility on pure tensors for the entire split coefficient action. Additivity extends the compatibility to all tensors. The coordinate idempotents have the same entries after base change, which gives the projector square and the induced semilinear map on each image.

This is the coefficient base change of the tangent grading in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 B and Proposition 2.4, printed pp. 110–111 (PDF pp. 4–5). It constructs the tangent component maps on actual affine `Nilp` algebras.
