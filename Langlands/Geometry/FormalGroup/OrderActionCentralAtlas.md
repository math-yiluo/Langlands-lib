# Central scalars on a formal atlas

Suppose the central scalar ring in an order acts structurally on the tangent of each chart. Restrict the order action to this ring to construct the central formal module law on each chart. The underlying formal group laws stay the same. The localization maps have the same underlying ring maps for both scalar actions.

Each original transition is order-linear, so its forward and inverse maps commute with the new scalars. The identity, cocycle and coefficient equations follow by taking the same coordinates of the original equations. The resulting atlas again carries the order action. This construction applies the formal module condition of Drinfeld 1976, §1, printed pp. 108–109, to an atlas with its actual transitions.
