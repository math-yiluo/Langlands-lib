# Finite étale levels of a p-divisible group where the prime is invertible

For an affine group scheme killed by an integer that is a unit on the base, two points over a square-zero extension with the same reduction are equal. Their quotient belongs to the square-zero kernel. Its coordinate derivation is annihilated by the integer, so it vanishes. The infinitesimal uniqueness criterion gives formal unramifiedness of the affine coordinate algebra and of the original scheme.

In a p-divisible group the actual finite level `n` is killed by `p ^ n`. If `p` is a unit on the base, the level is formally unramified. The finite locally free hypotheses already in the tower give flatness and local finite presentation. Thus the original level scheme is a finite étale cover, with its original underlying scheme and structure morphism.

This derives finite étaleness of the scalar torsion levels of Rapoport and Zink, *Period Spaces for p-divisible Groups* (1996), §2, printed pp. 50–51 (PDF pp. 65–66), where the prime is invertible. Harris, *Supercuspidal representations in the cohomology of Drinfel'd upper half spaces* (1997), Appendix, printed p. 115 (PDF p. 41), uses finite Tate levels on the rigid generic fibre to make covers of the moduli space.
