# Relative Frobenius from the shifted frame

Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 A, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 3), uses the natural comparison from \((\psi\circ\mathrm{Fr}^{-n})_*\Phi\) to \(\psi_*\Phi\) in the group-action formula. This file constructs its integral forward map for \(n\geq 0\) over any target algebra in the frame universe. The frame field is algebraic over the scalar residue field and the integer-ring scalar action factors through that field. We use an exponential characteristic \(p\) and the equality \(q=p^f\), where \(q\) is the actual scalar residue cardinal.

`shiftedFrameFrobeniusHom` extends the original relative Frobenius along \(\psi\circ\mathrm{Fr}^{-n}\). Its target comparison identifies the successive coefficient extensions with extension along \(\psi\) by the identity formal series. Thus the resulting integral full-order map has exactly the coordinate series \(X_i^{q^n}\). It is the identity-coordinate comparison at zero, and composing two such maps adds their exponents after identifying the two shifted sources. The integral naturality proof extends the coordinate Frobenius square along the shifted coefficient map. Tensor induction then proves the same square in the whole rational Hom for every rational map of the underlying frames.

For a height-certified special frame, `shiftedFrameFrobeniusQuasiIsogeny` takes a proof of the separately stated whole rational invertibility of the original relative Frobenius. It extends both inverse maps and uses the same target comparison. Its whole forward tensor is exactly the rationalized integral map above. The whole quasi-isogenies inherit the zero and addition laws and commute with rational full-order endomorphisms of the frame.
