# The weighted map on the modified module

Assume Verschiebung is injective and the uniformizer action is injective
on the quotient by its image. If the weighted sum of a tuple vanishes,
its first coordinate lies in the image of Verschiebung. Subtracting the
adjacent relation determined by a preimage makes the first coordinate
zero. The weighted sum of the remaining coordinates then vanishes by
injectivity of Verschiebung. Induction on the length of the tuple
expresses the original tuple in the submodule of adjacent relations.
If \(\Pi^{n+1}\) is multiplication by a scalar, injectivity of that
scalar on the quotient suffices for the hypothesis on the uniformizer.

Under those hypotheses, a linear map into the weighted image lifts
through the weighted map. The lift evaluates the inverse of the linear
equivalence with the image. This implements the injective case of the
Frobenius lifting argument in Drinfel'd's construction.
[V. G. Drinfel'd, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions*, 1976, §2B, Proposition 2.2](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01077936)
