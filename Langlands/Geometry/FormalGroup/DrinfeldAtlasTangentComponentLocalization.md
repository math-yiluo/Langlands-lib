# Localization of the compatible Drinfeld tangent components

Over an affine nilpotent test algebra, the special atlas gives compatible tangent vectors with the split coefficient action defined by its embedded degree-\(d\) unramified field, its residue embedding into \(\kappa\) and the residue coordinate on the test algebra. Each actual homogeneous component is the image of the corresponding coordinate projector.

Evaluation on the universal localization of a chart exhibits its coordinate tangent module as the principal localization of the compatible tangent module. The evaluation intertwines the two projectors. Clearing denominators and applying the chart projector shows that evaluation of the corresponding images is also a localization. `SpecialAtlas.tangentComponentChartEquiv` is the resulting scalar extension isomorphism of the global component with the actual special chart component.

These are the localization identifications for the tangent grading in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 B, printed p. 110 (PDF p. 4).
