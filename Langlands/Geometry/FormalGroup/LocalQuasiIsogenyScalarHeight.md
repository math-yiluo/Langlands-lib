# Scalar height under a local quasi-isogeny

Drinfeld 1976, §1, printed pp. 108–109, defines height through the degree of a scalar kernel. This module compares the scalar height of two divisible formal modules related by a local quasi-isogeny, using the finite-rank convention for the scalar powers.

At a point of an open affine-base spectrum, take the germ of a structure section and map its localization to the residue field. The integral isogeny numerator from a witnessing neighbourhood extends to an actual isogeny over that field. Scalar powers commute with the numerator. Multiplicativity of degrees and positivity of the numerator degree give equality of the two field kernel ranks. Tensor base change reads the field rank as the rank of the original finite projective kernel at the corresponding prime. Therefore the scalar height of the source transfers to the target for a quasi-isogeny defined on the whole affine spectrum.

The same rank argument reflects a scalar height certificate along any coefficient map surjective on spectra, provided the original law is scalar-divisible.
