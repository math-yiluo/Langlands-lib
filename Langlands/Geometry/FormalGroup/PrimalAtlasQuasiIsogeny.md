# Central quasi-isogeny of the primal atlas

In the split comparison of Rapoport–Zink, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310, the primal division summands carry a quasi-isogeny over the central field. `restrictCentralQuasiIsogeny` constructs it on the actual image atlases of the two split formal module laws on a common principal cover.

Start with the invertible ambient tensor, and clear a power of the rational prime in each direction. Scalar divisibility makes integral formal Hom inject into tensor Hom, so the numerators commute with the split order and compose to scalar endomorphisms in both directions. Restrict those integral maps to the image atlases and use the structural tangent action to change the central scalar ring. These operations preserve the composite identities and the natural-number scalar maps. Dividing the two atlas numerators by their original prime powers in the central fraction field gives mutually inverse tensors in actual atlas Hom.

The forward atlas tensor has the same prime-power denominator as the chosen ambient numerator. The presentation lemma records this relation together with the actual restriction and change of scalars on the numerator, so a single global denominator works on all charts.
