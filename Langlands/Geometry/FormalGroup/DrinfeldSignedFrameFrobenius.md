# Signed relative Frobenius between actual Drinfeld frames

Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 A, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 3), uses the relative comparison \(\mathrm{Frob}^n\) from \((\psi\circ\mathrm{Fr}^{-n})_*\Phi\) to \(\psi_*\Phi\) in the action formula, with \(n\) an integer. This file takes the exact published frame Frobenius inverse proposition for every nonnegative exponent as a proof argument. It uses the actual residue-cardinal Frobenius and the fixed height-certified frame from the preceding constructions.

`signedFrameFrobenius` relates the two actual coefficient extensions of the frame along residue maps \(a,b\) when \(a=b\circ\mathrm{Fr}^{-n}\). For \(n\geq 0\) it is the positive relative map with an identity-coordinate source comparison. For \(n<0\) it is the inverse of the positive comparison with the two residue maps exchanged. On \(a=b\circ\mathrm{Fr}^{-n}\) for natural \(n\), `signedFrameFrobenius_natCast` identifies the whole result with the existing positive quasi-isogeny.

To prove composition of the signed comparisons, the proof precomposes both sides by a positive comparison with exponent large enough that each intermediate sum is nonnegative. It uses the integral coordinate power formula for the nonnegative compositions and then cancels the added comparison in the whole rational Hom. The zero exponent gives the identity, and reversing a comparison negates its exponent. Naturality for actual frame self quasi-isogenies follows from the whole rational naturality square at nonnegative exponents and inversion of that square at negative exponents.
