# Drinfeld’s formula on height-zero framed representatives

Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 A, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 3), gives the action formula on triples \((\psi,Y,\rho)\). It shifts the residue map by \(\mathrm{Fr}^{-n}\), twists the target by inverse conjugation by \(g_2\), and sends the framing to \(\rho g_1^{-1}g_2^{-1}\mathrm{Frob}^{n}\), where \(n=\operatorname{ord}_K(\det g_1)+\operatorname{ord}_K(\operatorname{Nrd}g_2)\). Harris, *Supercuspidal representations in the cohomology of Drinfel'd upper half spaces* (1997), Appendix, printed p. 112 (PDF p. 38), describes quasi-isogeny height as the difference of the two logarithmic kernel ranks.

`DrinfeldFramedAtlas.actionFramingOnCover_hasConstantChartHeight` adds the actual signed heights on each test of the original reduced cover. For a frame of dimension \(d\) and scalar height \(d^2\), signed Frobenius contributes \(nd\), the inverse matrix contributes \(-d\operatorname{ord}_K(\det g_1)\), the original framing contributes zero, and the reduced-target twist contributes \(-d\operatorname{ord}_K(\operatorname{Nrd}g_2)\). The exponent identity makes the sum zero. `actionFraming_hasConstantChartHeight` transports this height certificate to the shifted source and the reduction of the transformed target.

`DrinfeldFramedAtlas.act` assembles the shifted residue map, the twisted target, and this height-zero quasi-isogeny into one framed representative. It uses the fixed frame’s matrix coordinates and proofs of the published positive relative Frobenius rank and rational invertibility propositions for all powers used by the formula. The comparison-class and base change laws are separate obligations.
