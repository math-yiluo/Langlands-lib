# The unramified subfield and division uniformizer in Drinfeld's presentation

Take the Library's actual unramified stage of degree `d` in the separable closure. Scalar extension multiplies the local invariant by `d`, so the invariant of the extended division algebra vanishes. Its splitting chart gives an embedding of the stage by the rank-one division-module construction.

The arithmetic division uniformizer uses the chosen embedding and any scalar uniformizer. Its `d`-th power is exactly that scalar uniformizer and conjugation by it restricts to arithmetic Frobenius on the embedded field. The construction takes Drinfeld's invariant in the opposite-class convention verified by the cyclic chart calculation. The embedding and the unit give the two division-algebra relations of Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 B, printed p. 110. The comparison with the integer-ring residue formulas is a further step.
