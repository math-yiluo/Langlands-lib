# Weil pairing on schematic Tate points

The perfect pairings of an actual principally polarized p-divisible group evaluate on finite-level scheme points as global units. Every point at level `n` is killed by `p ^ n`. To see this on a general test scheme, use the annihilation result on the finite affine level itself and restrict its identity point. Bilinearity then makes the pairing value a `p ^ n`-th root of unity.

The adjoint law for the inclusions and transfers, together with the scalar formula for their composite, shows that taking the `p`-th power of the pairing at level `n + 1` gives the pairing at level `n`. Thus the finite values form an element of the existing integral Tate twist. The resulting pairing of schematic Tate sections is biadditive and alternating, with the original finite pairing as its coordinate formula. Restriction along a map of scheme tests commutes with the pairing and the associated map of Tate twists.

This is the scheme-test construction associated with the finite principal polarization. Harris uses the induced Tate polarization on the rigid generic fibre in *Supercuspidal representations in the cohomology of Drinfel'd upper half spaces* (1997), Appendix, printed p. 115 (PDF p. 41).
