# Central scalars on integral formal atlas maps

For atlases with actual division-order actions and a structural central action on each tangent space, `centralScalars` gives the same integral chart maps over the central scalar ring. Order commutation supplies commutation with every new scalar endomorphism. The coordinate equations for naturality and transitions continue to hold, and the resulting maps still commute with the whole division order.

This construction preserves identity and composition. A natural number gives the same scalar endomorphism under either scalar law, since both come from the action of the corresponding natural number in the order ring. These facts supply the change of scalars for restriction to the primal summand in Rapoport–Zink, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310.
