# Rectangular jets of formal modules over a field

This module supplies auxiliary results for the residue-cardinality rank
calculation in `RationalHeightFieldRank`. It uses the rectangular truncations
and kernel equations of `FiniteKernelComposition` and the monomial basis of
`PolynomialBoxQuotient`.

Over a field of characteristic \(p>0\), the jet of width \(p^b\) kills the
\(p^b\)-th power of each variable. Its augmentation ideal is generated by
the images of the variables and is nilpotent. A nilpotent element lies in
that ideal because its constant term vanishes. Its \(p^b\)-th power
vanishes by the Frobenius identity for sums. The image of the tautological
point under a formal homomorphism therefore defines a pullback of jets of
this same width. Checking on generators proves that these pullbacks respect
composition and restriction to smaller characteristic-power widths.

At widths above the uniform kernel bound, the quotient by the image of the
target augmentation ideal represents the actual kernel. Its dimension agrees
with that of any chosen finite kernel model by uniqueness of representing
algebras. Lifts of a basis of the quotient generate the source jet as a module
over the target jet by nilpotent Nakayama. Thus the source jet dimension is
bounded by the product of the kernel dimension and the target jet dimension.
The box dimensions grow as powers of the width with exponents equal to the
coordinate counts. The inequality for arbitrarily large widths gives
`card_le_of_finiteKernel`.
