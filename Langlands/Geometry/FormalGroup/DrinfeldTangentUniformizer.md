# The division uniformizer on the actual tangent components

The projector image for a lifted character equals the common coefficient eigenspace. A vector in that eigenspace has every other projector component zero, since the two lifted characters differ by a unit on a suitable coefficient. This uses the actual split tangent action on the affine test algebra.

The next character is the previous one precomposed by inverse arithmetic Frobenius. The relation holds on residue characters and lifts by uniqueness through the nilpotent quotient. The division uniformizer's arithmetic commutation law therefore moves each actual tangent summand to its successor. The reduced-degree power on the total tangent module is multiplication by the original scalar uniformizer. These are the tangent grading relations of Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 A–B, printed pp. 109–110 (PDF pp. 3–4).
