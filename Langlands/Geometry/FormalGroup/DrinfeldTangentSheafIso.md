# Actual comparison of affine Drinfeld tangent sheaves

An order-linear isomorphism between special formal modules on the same affine test algebra induces an isomorphism of their graded tangent sheaves. The total map is the associated sheaf of the derivative. Each component map is the associated sheaf of its restriction to the corresponding projector image, transported along the component identifications. The inclusions commute because the component map is a restriction of the derivative. The total map commutes with the uniformizer by differentiating order linearity.

This gives the chart comparison for the actual tangent part of Drinfeld's data, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 B, printed p. 110 (PDF p. 4).
