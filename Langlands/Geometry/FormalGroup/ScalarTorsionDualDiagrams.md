# Finite dual diagrams of the scalar torsion

The actual finite kernel group scheme of a scalar power has finite projective coordinates and is locally of finite presentation over the base. Its dual is the relative spectrum of the convolution-dual coordinate Hopf algebra. The result is finite, flat and locally of finite presentation at every level.

`torsionSchemeDualInclusions` takes the dual of each scalar multiplication transition to give a forward arrow. `torsionSchemeDualTower` takes the dual of each original inclusion to give a backward arrow. Both diagrams retain the same dual scheme at a given level. Their identity and composition equations come from the original diagrams and the contravariant dual map.

An actual integral map induces a map of dual levels in the opposite direction. The two verified natural transformations express compatibility with the forward and backward arrows. The level maps preserve identity and reverse composition. These statements construct the diagrams of finite group schemes, including the levels that can have an étale part. The assertion that the forward diagram satisfies the exactness conditions of a p-divisible group requires additional exactness results.

The application is the split product of a special formal module and its Cartier dual in Rapoport–Zink (1996), §6.43, printed p. 309 (PDF p. 321). The finite locally free duals are the coordinate constructions from Demazure and Gabriel (1970), Chapter II, §1, no. 2, 2.10, printed pp. 156–158 (PDF pp. 186–188).
