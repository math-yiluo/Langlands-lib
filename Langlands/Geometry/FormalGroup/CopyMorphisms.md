# Projections and inclusions of finite copies of a formal module

`copyProjection` and `copyInclusion` are actual scalar-linear formal maps. The projection followed after an inclusion is the identity on a matching coordinate and zero on the other coordinates. The projections jointly detect equality, so summing the inclusion–projection composites gives the identity of the whole product.

These identities give the block decomposition of formal endomorphisms used for the rational automorphism group of the fixed frame in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 A, printed p. 109.
