# Arithmetic Frobenius on completed unramified integers

An automorphism of the actual maximal unramified extension restricts to its
integer algebra. `completedUnramifiedAut` extends this integer automorphism
through the compatible quotient automorphisms at all powers of the maximal
ideal. Its restriction on the uncompleted integers is the original map. Its
reduction is the induced automorphism of the actual unramified residue field.

`completedUnramifiedFrobenius` uses the arithmetic Frobenius of the actual
separable inertia fixed field. Its reduction raises an element to the
cardinality of the base residue field. This gives the coefficient Frobenius
in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2A, printed
p. 109 (PDF p. 3), using the same cardinality convention. The construction
uses actual coefficient maps at each finite quotient of the completion.
