# Local quasi-isogenies on formal idempotent images

The split construction of Rapoport–Zink, *Period Spaces for p-divisible Groups* (1996), (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310 (PDF pp. 321–322), restricts a polarized rigidifier to the primal summand. This module provides the restriction for any two actual formal idempotent images over a coefficient base, using a discrete valuation ring, its fraction field and an irreducible uniformizer.

An ambient local quasi-isogeny that intertwines the projectors in rational Hom has integral isogeny numerators on an open cover. On each such open, multiply the rational intertwining equation by the same uniformizer power that gives the numerator. Faithfulness from a scalar-divisible source makes the numerator intertwine the integral projectors after refining the open. Its restriction to the two image laws is an actual isogeny, by the finite-kernel and fppf lifting retraction. The same uniformizer power presents the restricted rational map on the smaller open. Both image laws inherit scalar divisibility from their ambient laws.

The resulting local quasi-isogeny has the rational section obtained by composing with the image inclusion and projection. Its two-sided inverse agrees with the restriction of the ambient inverse by uniqueness.
