# Order-linear maps of division-image atlases

A homomorphism that commutes with the split order commutes with the division projector \((0,1)\). The restriction to the actual image atlases is therefore an integral atlas map. The action of an element \(b\) of the division order on each image is the restriction of the ambient action of \((0,b)\). The ambient order commutation and the composition formula for restriction imply the required commutation on every chart test. Restriction preserves the identity and composition of order-linear maps.

This verifies the integral chart map used when passing to the primal summand in Rapoport–Zink, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310.
