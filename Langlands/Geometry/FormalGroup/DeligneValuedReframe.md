# The DVR-normalized general linear action on actual Deligne classes

This module assigns to every linear automorphism a determinant-corrected
integer shift and constructs the resulting right general linear action on
the existing genuine admissible Deligne four-data isomorphism classes.
The correction is derived from the actual valuation of the coefficient DVR;
no preservation witness or action is supplied as an extra parameter.

The source motivation is V. G. Drinfel'd, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric
regions* (1976), Section 2 A, printed pp.109–110 (PDF pp.3–4). These pages
introduce the normalized valuation, the Deligne four sheaf data and their
conditions (a)–(c), and the natural projective action in the representability
theorem. The registered source is
`drinfeld-1976-coverings-p-adic-symmetric-regions`, SHA256
`7dd09e1967f46c29195ec87a400e145a17374368fc3b9dd0a782b5b7bfc44e2f`.
The valuation homomorphism, corrected-pair homomorphism and right action
below are derived infrastructure for those data. This module constructs the
full general linear action; proving scalar triviality and obtaining the
projective action still require further work.

## Hypotheses and the actual valuation

All carriers have a common universe `u`. The coefficient ring `O` is a
commutative domain with `IsDiscreteValuationRing O`, and the field `F` is an
`O`-algebra with `IsFractionRing O F`. A chosen element `pi : O` satisfies
`hpi : Irreducible pi`. No completeness, finite residue field or nilpotence
hypothesis is used. Arithmetic constructions allow any natural dimension
`d`; the actual four-data constructions assume `NeZero d` and work on any
scheme `S` with a structural map `phi : O →+* Γ(S, ⊤)`.

`coefficientValuation O F` is Mathlib's actual adic valuation on `F` at
`IsDiscreteValuationRing.maximalIdeal O`. Its value group is `WithZero
(Multiplicative Z)`, written `ℤᵐ⁰`. The group homomorphism
`coefficientOrder O F : Fˣ →* Multiplicative Z` sends a field unit `a` to

```text
ofAdd (-WithZero.log (coefficientValuation O F (a : F))).
```

A field unit has nonzero valuation. The valuation product law and
`WithZero.log_mul` therefore prove the order product law. The negative sign
converts Mathlib's multiplicative adic convention to the source convention
in which a uniformizer has additive order one.

`coefficientValuation_algebraMap_unit` proves that every integral unit has
valuation one by reducing to the integral valuation and exclusion from the
local maximal ideal. `coefficientOrder_algebraMap_unit` gives order zero
(the multiplicative identity) for its image in `Fˣ`.
`coefficientUniformizer_ne_zero` uses fraction-field injectivity and the
nonzero irreducible element. `coefficientValuation_uniformizer` identifies
the maximal ideal with the ideal generated by `pi` and applies the adic
valuation of that generator. Its value is `WithZero.exp (-1)`.
`coefficientOrder_uniformizer` then gives `ofAdd 1`.

## Deriving the correction from DVR arithmetic

`exists_coefficientOrder_correction hpi a` proves, for every `a : Fˣ`,

```text
∃ b : Oˣ,
  algebraMap O F pi ^ (-(coefficientOrder O F a).toAdd) * (a : F)
    = algebraMap O F (b : O).
```

The implemented proof uses Mathlib's
`IsDiscreteValuationRing.exists_units_eq_smul_zpow_of_irreducible` from
`Mathlib.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.Basic`: write the actual nonzero
fraction as `b * pi^n` with `b` an integral unit and `n : Z`. Equality of unit
values upgrades this to an equality in `Fˣ`. Applying the already constructed
order homomorphism shows that the order is exactly `n`, using order zero
for `b` and order one for `pi`. The negative and positive powers of the
nonzero uniformizer cancel, proving the displayed equation. Thus the
correction is obtained from DVR fraction-field arithmetic, without an
admissibility assumption.

`normalizedReframeHom hpi` is a group homomorphism

```text
GL(F^d) → shiftReframeSubgroup (coefficientUniformizer_ne_zero hpi)
g ↦ (g, (coefficientOrder O F (LinearEquiv.det g))⁻¹).
```

The target is the previously constructed arithmetic subgroup of
`GL(F^d) × Multiplicative Z`: a pair `(g,k)` belongs when
`pi^(k.toAdd) * det(g)` is the image of an integral unit. The correction
theorem applied to the actual determinant unit proves membership for every
`g`. Identity and product laws follow by subtype and product extensionality,
determinant multiplicativity, multiplicativity of `coefficientOrder`, and
commutativity of the integer group. `normalizedReframeHom_linear` identifies
the first projection with `g`; `normalizedReframeHom_shift` identifies the
integer shift as exactly minus the normalized determinant order.

## Actual admissibility and class action

The imported integer shift makes new degree `i` old degree `i+k`, including
the actual rational homogeneous Pi path in the shifted frame. The imported
reframe precomposes that frame by `g`. For the source's literal scalar
`pi^(-i)` after the top exterior power, their combined determinant factor is
`pi^k * det(g)`. This explains the negative correcting shift used here.

`SheafPreDatum.admissible_normalizedReframe_iff` applies the existing
corrected-pair preservation theorem to `normalizedReframeHom hpi g`. It
preserves all three existing conditions, including the determinant equality
for every integer on the corresponding tangent Pi-zero locus.
`SheafDatum.normalizedReframe` constructs the admissible representative by
the corrected-pair operation. Its `normalizedReframe_val` formula is

```text
(normalizedReframe hpi g D).val
  = D.val.shiftReframe (coefficientUniformizer_ne_zero hpi) g
      (-(coefficientOrder O F (LinearEquiv.det g)).toAdd).
```

`SheafDatum.IsoClass.normalizedReframe` pulls back the existing genuine
corrected-pair class operation through the homomorphism.
`normalizedReframe_mk` proves the representative formula, and
`toPreliminary_normalizedReframe` identifies its image after forgetting
admissibility. The quotient relation remains existence of an actual
isomorphism preserving all four data, including the rational frame and
`u`; the imported functor supplies its transport.

`normalizedReframe_one` and `normalizedReframe_mul` use the homomorphism
laws and the corrected-pair class laws:

```text
normalizedReframe hpi 1 q = q
normalizedReframe hpi h (normalizedReframe hpi g q)
  = normalizedReframe hpi (g*h) q.
```

Here `(g*h)(v) = g(h(v))`, so these are right-action laws. The two inverse
cancellation theorems use this product law and the group inverse laws.
`normalizedReframeEquiv` packages each operation as a bijection with inverse
`g⁻¹`. `normalizedReframeRightAction` encodes the resulting right action as
`MulAction GL(F^d)ᵐᵒᵖ` on the existing admissible classes, reversing the factors
of `unop` exactly as required by the displayed law.

## Remaining boundaries

Scalar triviality, descent to `PGL`, and independence from the chosen
uniformizer are not proved here. The action is on the current stalkwise
admissible class model; the independent exterior-subsheaf comparison,
arbitrary base change, its restriction to a complete `Nilp` functor,
comparison with formal-module moduli, and representability remain open.
No representing scheme or universal object is introduced or assumed.
