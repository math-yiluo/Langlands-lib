# Coefficient pullback on basic opens of a nilpotent thickening

These internal lemmas pass from the coefficient rings to the actual structure sheaf used in the nilpotent rigidity proof. For a surjective coefficient map, every basic open on the smaller spectrum is the inverse image of a basic open. Surjectivity on spectra lets us reflect inclusions of opens.

On a basic open the structure-sheaf section rings are away localizations. The compatibility of the section map with structural coefficients identifies that map with the map of localizations. The localized map is surjective, and its kernel is the extension of the original kernel. Ideal extension commutes with powers, so this kernel is nilpotent.

The integral Hom pullback applies the localized coefficient map and transports across the coefficient square. Equality of pullbacks gives equality of the mapped coordinates and hence equality of the original maps by integral rigidity. Tensoring with the flat scalar algebra preserves injectivity on the rational Hom presheaf.
