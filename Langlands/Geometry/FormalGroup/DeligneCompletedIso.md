# The completed-base and residue presentations of Deligne points

The actual reduction of a map from the completed maximal unramified integers is a bijection for each nilpotent test algebra. After restriction to the uncompleted integers, reduction is bijective by infinitesimal lifting along the nilpotent ideal generated by the uniformizer. The two unramified residue fields are canonically the same. Precomposition with the surjective residue map detects equality, so this bijectivity holds for the actual completed reduction.

The isomorphism `nilpDeligneCompletedIso` uses that equivalence on the base coordinate and the identity on Deligne classes. Its forward map is the previously constructed natural reduction, which intertwines the signed arithmetic Frobenius and the projective linear action. The resulting isomorphism supplies the coefficient comparison for Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 A, printed p. 109.
