# Transition maps of the finite division-uniformizer level groups

The finite integral unit quotients in `DivisionUniformizerLevels` form an inverse system on the positive integers. If `N ≤ M`, the norm radius at `M` is at most the radius at `N`, so the level subgroup at `M` is contained in the subgroup at `N`. The identity of the integral unit group descends to a surjective homomorphism between the quotients. On representatives it sends the class of a unit to the class of the same unit. Its kernel is the image in the finer quotient of the coarser level subgroup.

The identity and composition laws follow from the representative formula. Since each transition is a group homomorphism, right translation by a finer class descends to right translation by its coarser class.

Replacing the chosen division uniformizer by its `d`-th power identifies quotient `N` for the powered generator with quotient `d * N` for the original generator. These isomorphisms preserve representatives and commute with the transitions. In Harris, *Supercuspidal representations in the cohomology of Drinfel'd's symmetric space* (1997), §2, printed p. 88 (PDF p. 14), the levels are given using powers of the central maximal ideal. Appendix, printed p. 113 (PDF p. 39), chooses a division uniformizer with `d`-th power equal to a central uniformizer. This gives the two indexings of the same finite quotient system. Appendix, printed pp. 115–116 (PDF pp. 41–42), gives the level structures and the integral unit action for which these quotients supply the algebraic groups.

The constructions apply to positive indices in a finite-dimensional division algebra over a complete nonarchimedean local field with its module topology, given a unit of the ambient division algebra of absolute value less than one.
