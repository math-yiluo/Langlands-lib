# Gluing integral formal homomorphisms

Drinfeld 1976, §1, printed pp. 108–109, uses formal groups in local coordinates. To glue integral morphisms of the fixed coordinate laws, take a compatible family over an open cover. The compatibility of its maps gives compatibility of each coefficient as a section of the actual structure sheaf.

Glue those structure sections and use them as the coefficients of a power series for each target coordinate. Restriction recovers each original series. The zero constant coefficient, the formal addition equation and the scalar commutation equations hold after restriction to every member of the cover. Equality of structure sections is detected on the cover, so these identities hold for the glued coordinate map. The result is an integral formal homomorphism whose restrictions are the original maps.
