# Scalar algebra of whole rational formal atlas endomorphisms

Let `X` be a formal atlas over a commutative `O`-algebra and `F` a field
over `O`. Its whole rational endomorphism tensor `F ⊗[O] Hom X X rfl`
has the existing formal composition ring. Bilinearity of composition gives
its `F`-algebra structure, with scalar `a` acting by the endomorphism
`a • id X`.

`ofHomEnd` rationalizes each original integral atlas endomorphism as
`1 ⊗ f`. `onChartEnd` packages the existing evaluation on a test algebra
and chart as an algebra homomorphism to the whole coordinate endomorphism
tensor on that chart. `baseChangeEnd` gives the algebra homomorphism for
the existing coefficient extension of atlases. The multiplication and
identity fields come from preservation of actual composition and identity,
and the scalar fields follow from `F`-linearity.

This internal construction supplies the whole atlas algebra for the
division-unit term in the moduli action of Drinfel'd, *Coverings of p-adic
symmetric regions* (1976), §2 A, printed p. 109.
