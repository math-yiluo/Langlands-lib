# The degree character of rational formal endomorphisms over a field

The units of the actual rational formal endomorphism ring are
self quasi-isogenies by composition of their two inverse maps. The
positive rational degree defined by their finite kernel ranks gives
a multiplicative character to \(\mathbb Q^\times\). This is a derived
use of Harris's rank difference in the 1997 Appendix, printed p. 112
(PDF p. 38), for field-valued tensor quasi-isogenies with actual
uniformizer-power denominators.

An integral isomorphism has degree one and the scalar uniformizer has
degree \(q^H\) when the scalar height is \(H\). If a common
uniformizer power clears the denominators of all signed powers of
an actual endomorphism unit, then each of those powers has degree
at least the reciprocal of the scalar denominator's rank. Positivity
of the numerator rank gives this lower bound. Multiplicativity and
the Archimedean order on the rationals then force degree one.
