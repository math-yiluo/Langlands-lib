# Scalar extension of the actual Drinfeld tangent components

Each projector image is a finite projective module. The projection onto its image splits the inclusion, and both maps extend to the tensor product over any affine `Nilp` morphism. Under the standard coordinate-basis isomorphism for the extended tangent module, the extended projector is the projector for the transported character. The inclusion and retraction consequently give inverse linear isomorphisms between the scalar extension of the original component and the actual image on the new test ring.

This uses the character grading of Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 B, printed p. 110, and the coefficient base change in Proposition 2.4, printed pp. 110–111 (PDF pp. 4–5). It establishes scalar extension of the tangent summands at the module level.
