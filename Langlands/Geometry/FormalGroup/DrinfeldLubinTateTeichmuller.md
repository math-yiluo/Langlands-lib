# Linear Teichmüller action on the reduced Lubin–Tate line

An integer `a` with `a^q = a` acts on the standard Lubin–Tate law by `aX`. This series has the prescribed linear term and commutes with the standard polynomial, so integral uniqueness gives the formula. Hensel lifting for `X^q − X` supplies such a lift of every element of the embedded residue field.

An endomorphism commuting with those linear scalars has zero coefficient in every degree strictly between one and `q`. A nonzero coefficient in degree `n` would make the `(n − 1)`-st power of every nonzero residue scalar equal to one, which forces `q − 1` to divide `n − 1`. If the linear term vanishes, the series is therefore divisible by `X^q`, the uniformizer series on the reduced line.

The scalar construction uses Lubin and Tate, *Formal Complex Multiplication in Local Fields* (1965), §1, Lemma 1 and Theorem 1, printed pp. 381–382. The coefficient action is that of the degree-`d` unramified subfield in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 B, printed p. 110.
