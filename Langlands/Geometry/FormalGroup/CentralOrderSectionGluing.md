# Central scalar change of a rational order section

An actual local quasi-isogeny gives an integral isogeny numerator on a neighbourhood of each point. When the section is order-linear, the numerator commutes with the order on its entire presenting open by integral faithfulness. Use these numerators with the central formal module laws and divide by the original scalar power in the new coefficient field.

The local rational Hom sections agree on all overlaps by the cross multiplication comparison. The actual rational Hom sheaf glues them. Comparing on the intersections with any other presenting open proves that the glued section restricts to its centralized numerator there too. Consequently the restriction formula determines the glued section independently of the original choice of neighbourhoods and denominators.

This construction is used for the order-linear quasi-isogeny on the split primal factor in Rapoport–Zink 1996, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310 (PDF pp. 321–322).
