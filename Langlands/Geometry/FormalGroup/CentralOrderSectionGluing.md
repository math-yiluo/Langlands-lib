# Central scalar change of a rational order section

On an affine base with fixed finite coordinate sets, the original laws have scalars in a discrete valuation ring `O` with fraction field `F`. The natural number `p` maps to a uniformizer of `O` and to a nonzero element of the central coefficient field `K`. The central `C`-action on the tangent of each law is structural. The given local quasi-isogeny, with denominators that are powers of `p`, supplies an integral isogeny numerator on a neighbourhood of every point. Integral faithfulness and order-linearity of the original section make the numerator commute with the order on that open. Use the same numerator with the central formal module laws and divide by the corresponding power of `p` in `K`.

The local rational Hom sections agree on all overlaps by the cross multiplication comparison. The actual rational Hom sheaf glues them. Comparing on the intersections with any other presenting open proves that the glued section restricts to its centralized numerator there too. Consequently the restriction formula determines the glued section independently of the original choice of neighbourhoods and denominators.

This constructs the central rational Hom section of the forward primal rigidifier on a coordinate open. In the application of Rapoport–Zink 1996, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310 (PDF pp. 321–322), the original scalars are `ℤ_p` and `ℚ_p`, and the central scalars are the integers of `F_{𝔭_i}` and its fraction field. Definition 2.8, printed p. 52 (PDF p. 67), gives the original local `p`-power convention. Definition 3.57, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 123), gives the structural tangent condition.
