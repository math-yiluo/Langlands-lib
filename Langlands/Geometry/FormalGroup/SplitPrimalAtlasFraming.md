# The split primal rigidifier on the reduced atlas

Rapoport–Zink, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310, take the primal quasi-isogeny of the split polarized formal module. Here the source is the fixed special primal frame extended by the actual residue coefficient map and restricted to the reduced target cover. Its order action is compared with the localized division image of the original reduced frame. Both have the same addition and transition series, and the equality of order series follows from coefficient extension of the inclusion, order endomorphism and projection.

`primalAtlasFraming` recovers the ambient order-linear tensor from the actual polarized rigidifier on the reduced affine spectrum. It restricts the two integral numerators to the division-image atlases, changes central scalars, and divides by the same prime powers over the central fraction field, where the prime is assumed nonzero. It then transports the two tensor maps by the integral order comparisons for the source and target. Thus the result is an actual invertible order-equivariant formal atlas map from the extended residue frame to the reduction of the constructed special primal atlas.
