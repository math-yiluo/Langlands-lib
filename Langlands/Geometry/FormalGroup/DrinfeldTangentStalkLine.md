# Rank one of the actual Drinfeld tangent components at every stalk

For a prime of the affine test ring, the algebraic closure of its residue field is again a test algebra. Its zero ideal pulls back to the original prime. Scalar extension identifies the original projector image over that algebraic closure with the corresponding projector image of the transported module. Geometric specialness identifies the latter with a single copy of the field. The rank formula for base change therefore gives rank one at the original prime.

The projector image is finite projective, so its localization at each prime is free over the local ring. The rank calculation gives an isomorphism from that localization to the local ring. This derives the local rank-one property used for the homogeneous tangent lines in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 A–B, printed pp. 109–110 (PDF pp. 3–4).
