# Fixed elements under reduction

An equivariant surjection of modules induces an endomorphism on its
kernel. When that endomorphism is nilpotent, its difference from the
identity is invertible on the kernel. To lift a fixed element, take a
preimage and use this inverse to correct its defect in the kernel. Two
fixed lifts differ by a kernel element fixed by the nilpotent
endomorphism, so the lifts agree. The resulting linear equivalence
sends each fixed element to its image under the original map.

This is the linear algebra step from nilpotence on the reduction kernel
to the lifting of fixed elements in the modified Dieudonné module.
[V. G. Drinfel'd, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions*, 1976, §2B, Propositions 2.3–2.4](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01077936)
