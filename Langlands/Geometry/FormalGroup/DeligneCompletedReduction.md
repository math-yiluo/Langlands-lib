# Reducing the completed base coordinate of a Deligne point

Drinfel'd, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 A, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 3), describes framed triples with a residue map and uses the completed unramified integer base in the representability theorem. The maximal ideal of that base is generated by the image of the original uniformizer. Composing a completed-base map with reduction of the test algebra therefore factors through the residue field. The factorization gives a natural transformation from the actual completed-base Deligne point functor to the Deligne functor in residue coordinates. It leaves the original Deligne class as its first component.

The completed arithmetic Frobenius induces the cardinality-power Frobenius on the residue field. The group homomorphism on residue automorphisms gives the same compatibility for all signed powers. The two Deligne-side actions use the same normalized determinant and reduced-norm integer orders and the same projective matrix action. Reduction consequently intertwines both coordinates of the actions.
