# Maps from an adic coefficient ring to a nilpotent test algebra

For an `O`-algebra `C`, `Nilp.AdicCoefficients π C` is Mathlib's adic
completion in the principal ideal generated by the image of `π`.
`Nilp.adicCoefficientMapEquiv` identifies maps from the completion to a
`Nilp` test algebra with maps from `C` to that test algebra. The forward map
is restriction along `C` into its completion.

The proof uses any power of `π` that kills the test algebra. A coefficient
map factors through the quotient at this power, and the projection from the
completion to the quotient has kernel given by the same power in the
completion. Equality of the restricted maps therefore determines the entire
map. This also proves naturality of the inverse under maps of tests.

This comparison isolates the completion step for the completed unramified
base in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2A,
printed p. 109 (PDF p. 3). Its proof applies to any `O`-algebra and uses only
the nilpotence condition on the test algebra.
