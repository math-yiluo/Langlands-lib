# Closed subkernels of represented formal kernels

This internal construction represents the kernel of a formal-module
homomorphism g when that kernel is contained in the actual kernel of
another homomorphism h with a supplied finite projective representing
algebra C.

The universal h-kernel point is the point corresponding to the identity
map of C. Apply g to it, take its finitely many nilpotent point
coordinates, and quotient C by the ideal generated by those
coordinates. Naturality of the corepresenting equivalence and of actual
formal-map evaluation identifies factoring through this quotient with
being killed by g.

The constructed quotient therefore corepresents the actual point kernel
of g over every coefficient algebra. It is finite over the base because
it is a quotient of C. It is finitely presented as a module: C is finite
projective and hence finitely presented, and the coordinate ideal is
finitely generated both over C and, since C is finite, over the base.

No flatness or projectivity of the quotient is asserted here. In the
dual-isogeny application, take h to be a scalar power on the target of
the original isogeny. The composite equation ensures the required
kernel inclusion. A separate torsor argument must establish the
projectivity required for a finite locally free kernel model.

The coefficient ring O and the base O-algebra R are arbitrary
commutative rings with possibly different universes; all coordinate
types are finite. This construction is an internal precursor to
`LocalQuasiIsogenyInverseStatement`, with no external source quotation.
