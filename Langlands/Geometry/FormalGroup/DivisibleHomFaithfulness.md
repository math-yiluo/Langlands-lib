# Faithfulness of rationalization for divisible formal modules

The scalar-lifting property of a divisible formal module makes each power of its uniformizer injective on integral Hom out of that source. Scalar multiplication of a formal homomorphism is precomposition with the scalar on the source. Two maps that agree after this precomposition agree on every actual point by fppf local lifting and faithful flatness.

Over a discrete valuation ring, equality in the fraction-field tensor Hom yields equality after multiplication by a nonzero integral element. Write that element as a unit times a uniformizer power and cancel both factors. Equality of two integral maps in the rational Hom sheaf gives equality of their tensor representatives after restricting to a neighbourhood of each point. The preceding cancellation applies over the corresponding section algebras because scalar divisibility is preserved by coefficient extension.

These are derived facts for the split quasi-isogenies of Rapoport–Zink, *Period Spaces for p-divisible Groups* (1996), (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310 (PDF pp. 321–322).
