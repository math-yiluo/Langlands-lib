# The rational multiplier condition on scalar-divisible formal modules

Harris, *Supercuspidal representations in the cohomology of Drinfel'd
upper half spaces* (1997), Appendix, printed p. 113 (PDF p. 39),
requires the framing quasi-isogeny in condition (iii) to compare the
polarizations up to one constant in \(\mathbf Q_p^\times\).

`RationalSection.HasLocalTorsionSimilitude` formulates this condition
on the actual rational Hom sheaf of two scalar-divisible formal laws.
At each point it requires an open neighborhood and a genuine integral
isogeny representing \(p^m\rho\) there. It also requires an integral
scalar whose image in the multiplier field is \(p^{2m}c\), where
\(c\) is the same specified unit for the entire section. The doubled
exponent accounts for the two arguments of the polarization. On that
neighborhood the numerator satisfies the integral multiplier equation
on all actual scalar-power torsion kernels.
`HasPadicTorsionSimilitude` uses the \(p\)-adic integers and the \(p\)-adic
numbers as the integral multiplier ring and the multiplier field.

The same numerator witnesses give the local quasi-isogeny when the
scalar is nonzero in the rational Hom field. A global tensor
presentation satisfying the integral equation supplies these
witnesses on every open by coefficient extension. The identity
section has multiplier one, and witnesses for restrictions to an open
cover give witnesses on the covered open.
