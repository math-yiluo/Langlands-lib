# Central scalar actions on a formal group

An order action gives endomorphisms of the underlying commutative formal group by forgetting scalar-linearity. If a central scalar ring acts on the tangent through its structural map to the coefficient ring, restriction to that ring supplies a formal module law. Evaluating the tangent identity on each standard basis vector gives the required linear coefficients. Centrality lets the same order endomorphisms act linearly on the new formal module. They have the same fibre derivatives, and order-linear maps and isomorphisms keep the same coordinates.

This is a change-of-scalars construction for the natural tangent condition on formal modules in Drinfeld 1976, §1, printed pp. 108–109.
