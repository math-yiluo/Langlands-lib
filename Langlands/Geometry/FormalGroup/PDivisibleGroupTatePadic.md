# The p-adic integer action on schematic Tate sections

The additive group of points of the actual finite level `n` is killed by `p ^ n`, so it is canonically a module over `ℤ / p ^ n ℤ`. Reduce a p-adic integer through the standard residue map to act on that group. The transfer maps commute with these actions because an integer lifting the residue at a higher level also lifts the residue at every lower level.

The compatible families form a p-adic integer submodule of the product of the finite-level point groups. Transport this structure through the additive equivalence with schematic Tate sections. The projection formula recovers the residue action at each finite level. Restrictions are linear because the maps on the finite components are additive and preserve the residue actions. The underlying additive presheaf is the original schematic Tate point presheaf, so the module presheaf is a sheaf for each subcanonical topology.

Harris describes the Tate module as an inverse limit of modules over the prime-power residue rings in *Supercuspidal representations in the cohomology of Drinfel'd upper half spaces* (1997), Appendix, printed p. 115 (PDF p. 41). Here the action is constructed on sections over schemes of an arbitrary given schematic p-divisible group.
