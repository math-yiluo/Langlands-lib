# Pointed maps intertwining the standard Lubin–Tate polynomial

Over a commutative local ring \(A\) with finite residue field of size \(q\), `polynomialCoordinates` applies \(\pi X+X^q\) in each coordinate. `Intertwines` records the commuting square for a pointed map. It is equivalent to vanishing of each series defect and is preserved by composition and pairing.

When the maximal ideal is \((\pi)\), `liftLinear` realizes a prescribed tangent matrix by applying the integral degree recursion to each output. If \(A\) is a domain and \(\pi\ne 0\), two intertwiners with the same tangent map coincide. Under these same hypotheses, an endomorphism of \(A\) fixing \(\pi\) sends the lift of a matrix to the lift of its image.

This gives the form of the standard intertwiner construction used to deduce the formal law identities from their linear terms in Lubin and Tate, *Formal Complex Multiplication in Local Fields* (1965), §1, Lemma 1 and the proof of Theorem 1, printed pp. 381–382 (PDF pp. 3–4).
