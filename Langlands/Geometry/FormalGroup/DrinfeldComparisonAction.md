# Drinfeld's action on the classes of height-zero framed triples

Drinfel'd, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 A, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 3), gives an action of the matrix and division groups on the isomorphism classes of the triples defining \(G\). The formula precomposes the residue homomorphism by the negative Frobenius power, conjugates the target action by the inverse division unit and replaces the framing by the composite of the signed relative Frobenius, inverse division action, inverse matrix and original framing in application order. The fixed frame is over the Library's supplied algebraic residue extension. The construction takes the frame's matrix coordinates and proofs of the two published nonnegative relative Frobenius propositions for rank and invertibility.

`ComparisonClass.act` applies the representative transformation and takes its original comparison class. `Comparison.act` takes every specified framed comparison to a comparison of transformed representatives, so the quotient lift respects every comparison chain. Quotient induction carries the proved identity and ordered product of representatives to the classes. These operations form `ComparisonClass.groupAction` at each Nilp algebra. The residue projection has the stated Frobenius shift, and the target and its quotient reduction have the inverse-conjugated order action. Coefficient naturality is a further step for the action on \(G\) as a functor.
