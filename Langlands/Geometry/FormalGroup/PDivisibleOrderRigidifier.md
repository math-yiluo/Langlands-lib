# Order-linearity of the actual split rigidifier

`OrderQuasiIsogeny` consists of an actual quasi-isogeny and its
commutation equation with the integral order actions in rational Hom.
The equation also holds for the inverse, by composition with the two
inverse identities. Composition preserves the equation.

`splitOrderRigidifier` carries an order-linear primal quasi-isogeny to
an order-linear map of the actual split products. On the primal block,
commutation is the given equation. On the dual block, apply Cartier
duality to the inverse equation and then multiply by the common scalar
in \(\mathbb Q_p^\times\). The scalar commutes with the order action.
The product formula yields commutation with the full split order,
with the ordinary order on the primal block and its opposite on the dual.

Harris (1997), Appendix, printed p. 113 (PDF p. 39), condition (iii)
of C1, requires linearity for the split order. Rapoport–Zink (1996),
§6.43, printed p. 309 (PDF p. 321), identifies the two split factors
and the normalized rigidifier on them.
