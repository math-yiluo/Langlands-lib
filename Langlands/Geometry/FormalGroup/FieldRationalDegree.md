# Rational degrees from finite kernel ranks over a field

Harris 1997, Appendix, printed p. 112 (PDF p. 38), subtracts the logarithm
of the multiplication-by-\(p^n\) kernel rank from the logarithm of the
integral numerator kernel rank, with \(p\) the rational prime. The positive
rational degree here is a derived ratio of ranks before taking logarithms,
using powers of a uniformizer \(\pi\) of the DVR \(O\). The rational tensor
uses the fraction field \(F\) of \(O\). Its source and target are formal
\(O\)-modules over an \(O\)-algebra field \(k\), both `IsPiDivisible π`.
The rank of an integral isogeny over \(k\) is the dimension of the finite
algebra representing the actual kernel. It agrees with the pointwise rank
on the spectrum and is positive because the zero point gives an algebra map
to \(k\).

Cross multiplication of two scalar-denominator presentations gives equal
integral homomorphisms by injectivity of rationalization. Applying kernel
rank multiplicativity to each composite proves independence of presentation.
The same multiplicativity and commutation with scalar powers identify the
scalar kernel ranks on either side of an isogeny. Thus composition of
presentations gives multiplication of degrees.

The field-valued numerator construction applies this ratio to an invertible
tensor in Drinfeld's Hom up to isogeny (Drinfeld 1976, §2 A, printed p. 109,
PDF p. 3). The degree is multiplicative for actual compositions, agrees
with the integral kernel rank when the forward tensor comes from an
integral isogeny, and is one at the identity.
