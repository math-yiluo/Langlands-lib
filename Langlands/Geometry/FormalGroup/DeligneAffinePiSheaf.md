# The sheaf of a split cyclic operator on an affine scheme

The associated sheaf of a module on an affine scheme carries the module's actual operator. The tilde functor transports the operator's power relation, and multiplication by a coefficient becomes multiplication by the corresponding global section. The latter identity follows by the tilde and global-sections adjunction and the linearity of the map to global sections.

For a finite cyclic grading with split inclusions, the associated sheaves of the inclusions and retractions retain their orthogonality and resolve the identity on every open. Their ranges form the actual homogeneous subsheaves, and the intertwining equality for the step maps makes the operator degree one. Each range subsheaf is identified with the associated sheaf of the original module summand.

This affine construction is used for the tangent sheaf of a special formal module in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 B, printed p. 110 (PDF p. 4).
