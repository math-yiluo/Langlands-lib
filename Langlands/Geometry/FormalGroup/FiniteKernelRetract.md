# Finite kernels under formal retractions

Rapoport–Zink, *Period Spaces for p-divisible Groups* (1996), Definition 2.6 and the remark after Proposition 2.7, printed p. 51 (PDF p. 66), describe isogenies with finite locally free kernels and identify multiplication by \(p\) on a \(p\)-divisible group as an isogeny. The split polarized object described in §6.43, printed pp. 308–309 (PDF pp. 320–321), motivates applying the following construction to given formal retractions.

An actual retraction of kernel point functors induces an idempotent on the representing algebra of the ambient kernel. Its image algebra is a direct summand of the finite projective ambient algebra. Maps from this image algebra are naturally equivalent to the points of the retract kernel, using the two retraction equations and the universal point of the ambient kernel.

For an integral map intertwining two formal projectors, the inclusion and projection of the source induce a retraction between the kernels of the ambient and restricted maps. The represented kernel therefore stays finite locally free. Projecting a lift of the included target point provides the required affine fppf lift on the summand. Applied to scalar multiples of the identity, this proves scalar divisibility of the image. Scalar power torsion passes to the image by applying the projection to an ambient annihilation relation. The chosen scalar stays nilpotent on the base by the ambient scalar-divisibility hypothesis.
