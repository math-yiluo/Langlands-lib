# Existence of Drinfeld's special formal frame

Fix the local field \(K\), its integer ring and uniformizer, the
central division algebra \(D\) of reduced degree \(d\), and its actual
unramified subfield of degree \(d\). The statement assumes the cyclic
uniformizer and Frobenius relations for this presentation of \(D\).
Let \(\kappa\) be the residue field of the integer ring of the completed
maximal unramified extension of \(K\).

`DrinfeldSpecialFrameExistenceStatement` asserts the existence of an
actual special formal \(O_D\)-module \(\Phi\) over \(\kappa\). It has
\(d\) coordinates, the full set of distinct unramified tangent
characters on geometric fibres, and actual uniformizer-kernel height
\(d^2\). It also asserts the existence of an isomorphism of
\(K\)-algebras from \(\operatorname{Mat}_d(K)\) to the rational formal
endomorphisms of \(\Phi\) that commute with \(O_D\). The frame and
matrix coordinates are the two choices used in the stated action on
the framed functor.

See Drinfeld (1976), §2 A, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 3), the paragraph
fixing the frame and its endomorphism algebra, and §2 B, printed
p. 110 (PDF p. 4), for the cyclic relations.
