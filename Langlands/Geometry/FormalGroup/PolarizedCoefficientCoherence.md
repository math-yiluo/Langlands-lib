# Coherence for coefficient changes of actual polarizations

The canonical integral comparisons for identity and successive
coefficient extensions act by the identity on formal coordinates.
The represented torsion points admit the same coordinate
description on the relative global sections of every scheme test.
Under the comparison, both coefficient paths recover the same
point on the original kernel. The two structural maps of the
original test agree by functoriality of `Spec`.

For a principal polarization, the evaluated unit is unchanged by
each coefficient extension. Natural evaluation and the torsion
point comparison give equality of the units for the two paths.
Consequently the actual identity and composite coefficient
comparisons are integral isometries. A commuting square compares
each path with its composite and uses the equality of those
composites. The resulting isometry is the canonical
identity-coordinate square comparison.

Naturality of the underlying formal comparisons for order
endomorphisms upgrades these isometries to isomorphisms of
polarized order modules. The construction supplies the coefficient
coherence of the affine polarized objects and preserves their
existing polarizations.
