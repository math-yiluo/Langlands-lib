# The schematic Tate level torsor at a test

Suppose the actual schematic Tate sections on a test admit an integral order-linear trivialization. Evaluation at `1` identifies each automorphism of the left regular order module with right multiplication by a unique order unit. Thus postcomposition from the chosen trivialization parametrizes all trivializations by the integral units.

Passing to level classes gives a bijection with the published finite quotient of the integral unit group. Under this bijection the right action is right multiplication and forgetting a finer level is the published quotient homomorphism. In particular, the classes on a test with a chosen trivialization form a finite torsor.

This is the division-order calculation underlying the quotient action in Harris, *Supercuspidal representations in the cohomology of Drinfel'd upper half spaces* (1997), Appendix, printed pp. 115–116 (PDF pp. 41–42). The calculation is for a scheme test and depends on a trivialization on that test. Geometric local existence and the rigid-analytic covering require additional input.
