# The special formal atlas of the split primal image

Rapoport–Zink 1996, §6.43 and (6.17), printed pp. 308–309 (PDF pp. 320–321), impose the special condition on the primal factor of the split polarized object. Drinfeld 1976, §2 A, printed p. 109, uses the special formal division module in the unpolarized moduli problem.

Take all principal elements for which the actual division projector has coordinates indexed by the reduced degree. Every prime lies on one of these principal opens by the chart construction. The elements generate the unit ideal, since a maximal ideal containing them all would give a prime outside the cover. Choose a presentation on each chart and form the formal atlas using the projector retractions. The division order acts through the restricted endomorphisms and commutes with the transitions.

Each chart has the structural central tangent action and the distinct characters of the given unramified integer subfield. Change to the central formal module laws and retain the order action and transitions to get an actual `SpecialAtlas`. The input is the affine split-special target, including its tangent conditions. To obtain a finite-height special atlas one also needs the scalar-kernel rank certificate `HasHeightSquared`.
