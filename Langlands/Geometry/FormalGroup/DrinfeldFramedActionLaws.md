# Group laws on Drinfeld's height-zero framed representatives

Drinfel'd, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions*, §2 A, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 3), describes the action of \(\mathrm{GL}_d(K)\times D^\times\) on the height-zero triples. `DrinfeldFramedAtlas.groupAction` packages the identity and product laws for the representative transformation `DrinfeldFramedAtlas.act` of that formula. The action uses the chosen matrix coordinates and proofs of the published nonnegative relative Frobenius rank and invertibility propositions for the fixed frame.

The residue shifts compose by additivity of the determinant and reduced-norm orders. Successive twists of the target order are the inverse conjugation by the division-unit product. On the original reduced cover, the whole forward framing is the original map preceded by the fixed-frame comparison and followed by the reduced-target twist. The fixed-frame comparisons compose by `DrinfeldResidue.actionFrameQuasiIsogeny_mul`. The proof identifies the reduction of the intermediate target with the division twist of the original reduction. On that atlas the two target tensors compose by the existing inverse-conjugation product law. Associativity gives the product framing. At the identity the two comparisons are identities. The forward map determines its inverse, and the height certificates are propositions, so the component equalities yield equality of the representatives. The operation on comparison classes and its naturality under coefficient extension require further proofs.
