# Compatible families of actual scalar torsion points

`scalarTatePoints M π A` is the module of compatible families of points of the scalar-power kernels of a formal module on a coefficient algebra `A`. The condition at indices `m ≤ n` applies the actual transition by `π^(n-m)` to the point at `n` and obtains the point at `m`. The family module has the termwise formal group operation and scalar action.

The projection at each index is linear. The lift of any compatible family of linear maps into the kernels is the map given by their values at all indices. Its projections recover the original maps and determine it uniquely. This is the inverse limit property on each test algebra.

Coefficient morphisms apply the actual kernel maps to the individual coordinates. Naturality of the torsion transitions gives another compatible family, and the coefficient identities and composites hold at each index. An integral formal module homomorphism acts on the torsion points and preserves the transition equations by scalar linearity. The resulting maps are natural in `A`, linear in the formal homomorphism, and compatible with its identity and composition.

Rapoport and Zink, *Period Spaces for p-divisible Groups* (1996), §2, printed pp. 50–51 (PDF pp. 65–66), describe the scalar torsion system of a p-divisible group. Harris, *Supercuspidal representations in the cohomology of Drinfel'd's symmetric space* (1997), Appendix, printed p. 115 (PDF p. 41), uses the Tate module for level structures on the rigid generic fiber. This module supplies the inverse limit of actual scalar-kernel points on affine coefficient tests. The rigid generic-fiber construction and its level trivializations require further comparison.
