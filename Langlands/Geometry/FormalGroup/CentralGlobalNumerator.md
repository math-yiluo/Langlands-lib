# A global numerator for the centralized rational section

An integral order-linear global map \(f\) that presents \(p^n z\) computes the centralized section as the central integral map divided by \(p^n\). The comparison uses the actual local isogeny presentations of \(z\). On a presenting open, cross-multiply the two prime powers. Faithfulness from scalar divisibility makes the resulting equality an equality of integral maps there.

Changing the scalar law leaves the coordinate maps and the natural-number scalar endomorphisms in that equality unchanged. The two fractions therefore agree in the central rational sheaf on the presenting open, and equality on the cover proves the formula globally. The global numerator is required to represent the stated multiple of the section. The isogeny condition is used in the local presentations that define the centralized section.

This comparison identifies the two presentations of the primal quasi-isogeny in Rapoport–Zink, (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310.
