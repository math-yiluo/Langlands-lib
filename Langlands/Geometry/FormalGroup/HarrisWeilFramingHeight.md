# Raw height of the inverse Frobenius rigidifier

Harris, *Supercuspidal representations in the cohomology of Drinfel'd upper half spaces* (1997), Appendix, printed p. 112 (PDF p. 38), defines the raw height as the difference of two base-\(p\) logarithms of kernel ranks. In (A.4) and its displayed formula on printed p. 114 (PDF p. 40), the rigidifier is precomposed by inverse relative Frobenius over the structurally twisted test.

`splitSpecialWeilFrobeniusPoint_hasFramingHeight` applies the actual relative Frobenius kernel-rank premise to the full polarized frame. The forward map has rank \(p^{m\dim\Phi}\). The theorem identifies the base change of the forward section with this integral homomorphism, and uses the common scalar height of the frame and its coefficient twists to give the inverse section height \(-m\dim\Phi\). It then adds this signed contribution to the old rigidifier height. The twisted reduction square pulls back the resulting height function and the integral coordinate identifications preserve it.

The theorem concerns the whole polarized frame and assumes a positive scalar height and the exact relative Frobenius rank proposition. The division coordinate in (A.3) uses the division summand in the split pair of Rapoport–Zink (1996), (6.17) and Lemma 6.44, printed pp. 309–310 (PDF pp. 321–322).
