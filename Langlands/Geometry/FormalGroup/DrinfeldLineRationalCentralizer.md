# The rational commutant of the reduced Lubin–Tate line

Let \(L/K\) be the specified unramified subfield of degree \(d\) in the central division algebra of reduced degree \(d\). The scalar uniformizer \(\pi\) and the residue embedding \(k_L\hookrightarrow\kappa\) define the reduced Lubin–Tate line \(H\). The rational endomorphism algebra is \(K\otimes_{\mathcal O_K}\operatorname{End}_{\mathcal O_K}(H)\), with formal maps over \(\kappa\). Write \(f\) for the rationalized action of \(\mathcal O_L\) and \(t\) for the image of \(X\mapsto X^q\), where \(q=|k_K|\). The supplied automorphism of \(L/K\) induces the \(q\)-power on \(k_L\) and restricts to an automorphism \(\tau\) of \(\mathcal O_L\). The identities \(t f(a)=f(\tau(a))t\) and \(t^d=\pi\), with \(\pi\) acting by field scalars, hold in the rational endomorphism algebra. The latter makes \(t\) a unit.

When \(\kappa/k_K\) is algebraic, the commutant of \(f(\mathcal O_L)\) and \(t\) is canonically \(K\). A uniformizer power clears the denominator of a member of the rational commutant. Divisibility makes rationalization injective on integral homomorphisms, so the numerator satisfies the integral commutation equations. The integral calculation identifies it as an original integer scalar, and division by the same uniformizer power recovers the field scalar.

The scalar line comes from Lubin and Tate, *Formal Complex Multiplication in Local Fields* (1965), §1, Theorem 1, printed p. 382 (PDF p. 4). The commutant is a step in the frame automorphism calculation in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 A–B, printed pp. 109–110 (PDF pp. 3–4).
