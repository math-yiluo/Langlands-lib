# The rational commutant of the reduced Lubin–Tate line

Rationalization sends the actual scalar endomorphisms to field scalars and preserves the coefficient and Frobenius relations. The reduced-degree power of Frobenius is the uniformizer, so its image in rational Hom is a unit.

A uniformizer power clears the denominator of a rational endomorphism commuting with the coefficients and Frobenius. Cancellation on the divisible source makes rationalization injective on integral homomorphisms, so the numerator satisfies the integral commutation equations. The integral commutant calculation then identifies it as an integral scalar. Dividing by the same uniformizer power gives the original rational endomorphism as a field scalar. This is a step in the actual frame automorphism calculation in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 A–B, printed pp. 109–110.
