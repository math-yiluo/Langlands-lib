# Drinfeld's action on the framed Nilp functor

Drinfel'd, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 A, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 3), gives the transformation of the residue map, target and framing for the functor \(G\) on Nilp. The compatibility with coefficient extension follows from the actual maps used to construct those triples.

The shift of the residue homomorphism commutes with postcomposition, and extension of the target commutes with inverse conjugation of the order action. The two routes use the same underlying target atlas. The actual source comparison for base change is constructed over that atlas and its reduced cover, so the forward framing formula can use it on both routes. The source comparison is natural for the full signed composite by the global coefficient square and its restriction to the cover. Composing with the two inverse source comparisons gives the inverse comparison square. Associativity and preservation of rational composition by extension then give equality of the whole forward framing tensors. The forward tensor determines its rational inverse, so the equality holds for the height-certified representatives.

Quotient induction gives naturality for the comparison classes. For each group element the inverse element gives the inverse natural transformation. The unit and product laws of the original comparison-class action give the homomorphism `DrinfeldFramedAtlas.actionHom` to the natural automorphism group of `comparisonClassFunctor`. Evaluation at a Nilp algebra is exactly the existing action on its height-zero framed classes.

The construction takes a height-certified fixed frame over an algebraic extension of the scalar residue field, its chosen rational matrix coordinates and a prime-power expression for the residue cardinality. The nonnegative Frobenius kernel ranks and full-order rational inverses used by the action come from `ModuleLaw.relativeFrobeniusIsogeny` and `SpecialModule.relativeFrobeniusQuasiIsogeny`.
