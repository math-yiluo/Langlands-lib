# Actual tangent components for the lifted Drinfeld characters

Differentiation carries the action of the embedded integers to the tangent module over the test ring. Extending coefficients and using the split coefficient algebra transports this action to the finite product of the test ring. Its coordinate idempotents project onto the actual tangent summands. The projectors are orthogonal and sum to the identity, and the eigenvalue on each image is the corresponding lifted character. Applying all projectors and summing their images give the two inverse linear maps of the tangent decomposition.

This constructs the coefficient grading used in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 B, printed p. 110 (PDF p. 4), on each coordinate chart. Rank one of these summands under the geometric specialness condition and the degree-one effect of the division uniformizer are separate properties of this grading.
