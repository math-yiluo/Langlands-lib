# Joining specified special-atlas comparisons through quotient reduction

This internal coherence unit transports the existing actual formal comparison
join through equal scalar structures, supplied-target coefficient extension,
and the actual Nilp quotient. It derives its results from
`AtlasOrderComparisonBaseChangeJoin` and the published scalar-transport and
quotient constructions; it asserts no new literature theorem.

`reAlgebraComparisonJoinCover` transports the supplied equality between the
first comparison's right finer cover and the second comparison's left finer
cover. `reAlgebraComparison_join` shows that scalar transport retains the
specified join, including its actual middle source-change isomorphism. Both
proofs eliminate the equality of the two algebra structures; at reflexivity
they retain the original data.

For a supplied algebra map `f`, `baseChangeAlongComparisonJoinCover` first maps
the given equality to the image covers and then transports it to the supplied
target algebra structure. `baseChangeAlongComparison_join` is equality of
complete comparison records: extending the original join is joining the two
specified extended comparisons along this equality. Its proof uses the generic
whole-comparison coefficient-extension equation, followed by scalar transport.
It does not infer equality of formal maps from evaluation on points.

`reductionComparisonJoinCover` and `reductionComparison_join` specialize these
constructions to `Nilp.quotientO`, the actual map from the Nilp algebra to its
quotient by the ideal generated by the distinguished base element. In
particular, the result retains the same original comparisons and joins their
reductions by the prescribed middle source transition.

The parameters retain a nonarchimedean local field `K`, a finite-dimensional
central division algebra `D` over `K`, and a field `L` with its supplied
`K`-algebra embedding into `D`. Supplied coefficient rings are in universe `v`;
for the reduction specialization the Nilp object is explicitly in universes
`{v,u}`. Covers may be infinite, selected original sources may differ, and
coefficient maps may merge generators. Framing preservation is constructed in
`DrinfeldFramedComparisonJoin`; varying-base framed functoriality, group actions
and representability remain separate.
