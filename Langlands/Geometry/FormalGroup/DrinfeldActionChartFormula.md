# Drinfeld's framing formula in normalized chart coordinates

Drinfel'd, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions*, §2 A, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 3), gives the transformed framing as the original framing after signed relative Frobenius, the inverse division action and the inverse matrix. `DrinfeldResidue.actionSourceHom` composes those three actual source maps in that order. It uses the chosen matrix coordinates and invertibility of the nonnegative relative Frobenius maps on the fixed frame.

The whole forward tensor of the transformed representative is precomposition by the restriction of this global source map. The proof uses the compatibility of restriction with rational composition and with the division action. On each test of an original reduced chart, normalization to the coefficient-extended global frame cancels the two intermediate chart identifications. The resulting precomposition is the coefficient extension of `actionSourceHom`. The chart formula concerns the actual underlying rational maps, with the target order conjugated as in Drinfeld's formula. The transformed representative still uses the published Frobenius rank proposition for its height certificate.
