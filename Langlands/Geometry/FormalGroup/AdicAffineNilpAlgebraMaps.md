# Algebra maps for affine formal spectrum points

An affine formal spectrum point on a Nilp algebra is an algebra homomorphism to that test which annihilates a positive power of the ideal of definition. This is continuity for the ideal-adic topology on the source and the discrete topology on the test. Each such map factors through an ordinary power quotient, and precomposition with the quotient map determines the factor uniquely.

The maps from the quotient stages to the continuous algebra maps are natural in the tests. To verify the pointwise colimit, compare any two factorizations through a common thicker quotient given by the maximum of their two indices. The pointwise universal properties give a natural isomorphism with the original colimit functor, and its value on a point from an ordinary quotient is precomposition with the quotient map.

This is the affine algebra-map description of the colimit of quotient points in Rapoport and Zink, *Period Spaces for p-divisible Groups* (1996), §2.1, printed p. 49 (PDF p. 64).
