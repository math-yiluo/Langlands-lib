# Full-order naturality of successive rational coefficient comparisons

This is an internal construction from `AtlasOrderBaseChangeComposition`,
`AtlasOrderRationalBaseChange` and `AtlasRationalBaseChangeComposition`. It
rationalizes the existing actual full-order successive-extension isomorphism
and restricts the ambient naturality equations to the full-order centralizers.
It makes no additional original-literature assertion.

Let `O` be a commutative ring, `R` a commutative `O`-algebra, `D` an arbitrary
central `O`-algebra, and `F` a field with an `O`-algebra structure. The original
atlases may have different finite coordinate types and have equal principal
covers. Let `S` be a commutative `R`-algebra test and `T` a commutative
`S`-algebra test, both in the universe of `R`. All `O`-algebra structures in
these raw extensions are the composite structures. Full-order rational Hom is
the chartwise `D`-centralizer inside the global tensor
`F ⊗[O] BasicAtlas.Hom`; it is not defined by tensoring integral full-order Hom.

`Hom.baseComp_natural` states that extending an integral full-order map twice
and then applying the target's actual successive-to-direct comparison equals
applying the source's actual comparison and then extending the map directly
to `restrictTest S T`. `Hom.baseComp_inv_natural` gives the reverse square,
from direct to successive extension. These use the specified `baseCompHom`
and `baseCompInv`, with their original preimage-chart choices. Subtype
extensionality, the two comparison forgetful laws and the integral extension
forgetful law reduce both equations to ambient integral naturality.

`RationalHom.baseComp_natural` and `baseComp_inv_natural` give the same two
squares for every element of the full-order rational centralizer. The
comparison maps are the rationalizations of the actual integral maps.
Extensionality reduces the equations to equality of underlying global tensors.
The forgetful laws for composition, rationalization, both comparisons and
coefficient extension then give the ambient rational equations. This does not
require the full-order rational centralizer to be generated by integral
full-order maps, nor does it assume injectivity of chart evaluation.

For quasi-isogenies assume additionally that `O` is a domain and `F` is its
fraction field. `OrderAction.baseCompQuasiIsogeny E S T` is
`QuasiIsogeny.ofIso (E.baseCompIso S T)`. It retains the rationalizations of
both actual maps and their two inverse equations. The `_hom` and `_inv`
lemmas expose these maps. `baseCompQuasiIsogeny_toAtlas` identifies the entire
forgotten quasi-isogeny with `X.baseCompQuasiIsogeny S T`: equality of forward
maps suffices by uniqueness of the stored two-sided inverse.

`QuasiIsogeny.baseComp_natural` compares successive extension followed by the
target comparison with the source comparison followed by direct extension.
`baseComp_inv_natural` gives the reverse comparison square. Both whole
quasi-isogeny equalities follow by forward-map extensionality and the
corresponding rational naturality equation.

The construction concerns the specified actual two-extension comparison. It
does not assert strict equality of atlases, independence of arbitrary
comparison witnesses, three-extension or identity-unit coherence,
supplied-target scalar transport, local rational gluing, heights, framing,
polarization, descent, levels or representability.
