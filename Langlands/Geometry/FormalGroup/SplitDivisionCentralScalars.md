# Central scalars on the formal division image

The primal factor of the split order in Rapoport–Zink, *Period Spaces for p-divisible Groups* (1996), §6.43 and (6.17), printed pp. 308–309 (PDF pp. 320–321), is cut out by the formal idempotent `(0, 1)`. The scalar and character conditions for an actual coordinate presentation of this image follow from the derivative of its inclusion. The derivative identifies the coordinate tangent with the division summand of the original tangent and intertwines the order actions.

Use this equivalence to pull back the structural central scalar identity and form the formal module law over the central scalar ring. For a geometric fibre, compose the same tangent equivalence with its given character coordinates. This produces a decomposition of the tangent of the new formal module into the original distinct one-dimensional characters. The construction uses a formal image presentation and the split tangent conditions from the ambient law.
