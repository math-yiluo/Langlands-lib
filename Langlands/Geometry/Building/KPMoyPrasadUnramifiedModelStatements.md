# The models `𝒢_{x,r}` of KP §8.5 over the maximal unramified integers

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field of either characteristic, `K = inertiaFixedField F`
(`UnramifiedClosure F`) its maximal unramified extension in a separable closure, and
`𝒪 = fixedFieldIntegers F` the integers of `K`. The residue field of `F` is finite, hence
perfect, so the standing hypothesis of KP §§8.3–8.5 (as corrected on 1 November 2024) holds,
and the residue field of `𝒪` is an algebraic closure of it.

Each statement of this module quantifies over a group scheme `H` over `K`, affine over
`Spec K`, with building data `B : MaximalUnramified.BuildingData F H` (it records that `H` is
connected reductive, a maximal `K`-split torus `S`, its apartment and its centralizer
`T = Z_H(S)`) and a proof `hT : IsTorus B.centralizer.Z`. This is the setting of KP §8.5: the
group `G_K` there is quasi-split, so `T` is a torus. A point `x` of the apartment and a depth
`r : ℝ≥0` give `B.minimalCongruentMoyPrasad hT x r`, the group `G(K)_{x,r}` of KP Definition
7.3.3 at the constant concave function `r`, generated by the image of `T(K)_r` and the root
blocks `U_{a,x,r} U_{2a,x,r}`, where `T(K)_r` is the minimal congruent filtration of KP
Definition B.10.8 (`B.minimalCongruentTorusFiltration hT r`). For an apartment lift `L` of
`P : ParahoricBuildingData G` the instance `B = L.buildingData`, `hT = L.centralizer_isTorus`
and `x = L.e x'` is the group `G(K)_{x',r}` of KP §9.8.2.

A model of `H` over `𝒪` is an `IntegralModel 𝒪 K H`: an affine flat group scheme of finite
type over `𝒪` with a chosen identification of its generic fibre with `H`. Its integral points
`M.integralPoints` are the image of `M(𝒪)` in `H(K)`.

## Definition

`MaximalUnramified.BuildingData.minimalCongruentMoyPrasadPlus B hT x r` is
`⨆_{s > r} G(K)_{x,s}`, the group `G(K)_{x,r+}` with the strict-depth convention of KP §1.6.
When the family `s ↦ G(K)_{x,s}` decreases (for instance when the minimal congruent torus
filtration decreases, by `moyPrasadWith_antitone`) the supremum is the union.
`minimalCongruentMoyPrasad_le_minimalCongruentMoyPrasadPlus` records `G(K)_{x,s} ⊆ G(K)_{x,r+}`
for `r < s`.

## Statements

- `UnramifiedMoyPrasadModelStatement` is KP Theorem 8.5.2 for the constant concave function
  `f = r`. It asserts a smooth model `M` of `H` over `𝒪` with geometrically connected fibres
  and `M(𝒪) = G(K)_{x,r}`, together with the root-group and open-cell assertions of the theorem
  in the following form. There are a family `A o` of groups over `K` indexed by
  `o : Option (relativeRoots B.i)`, homomorphisms `incl o : A o ⟶ H`, models `N o` of `A o`
  over `𝒪` and homomorphisms of models `φ o : (N o).Hom M (incl o)` such that
  - `incl none` represents the centralizer functor of `S` (so `A none ≅ T`), and the image of
    `(N none)(𝒪)` in `H(K)` is that of `T(K)_r`: `N none` is the model `𝒯_{f(0)}` of KP 8.5.1;
  - for each relative root `a`, `incl (some a)` represents the root subgroup functor of `a`
    and the image of `(N (some a))(𝒪)` in `H(K)` is the block `U_{a,x,r} U_{2a,x,r}`
    (`B.apartment.filtrationBlock x (fun _ => r) a`): `N (some a)` is the model `𝒰_{a,x,f}`
    of KP 8.5.1 and Proposition 8.3.12;
  - every `N o` is smooth with geometrically connected fibres, and every `φ o` is a closed
    immersion ("the morphism `U_a → G` extends to a closed immersion `𝒰_{a,x,f} → 𝒢_{x,f}`",
    for every `a ∈ Φ̂`);
  - for every generic cocharacter `ν` of `S` there are a group scheme `V` over `𝒪` and a
    homomorphism `j : V ⟶ M.group` that is a closed immersion (`IsClosedSubgroup j`), with `V`
    smooth over `𝒪` and with unipotent generic fibre (base change along `𝒪 → K`) and
    unipotent special fibre (base change along the residue map `𝒪 → 𝔣`), such that for every
    `n` and every injective enumeration `a : Fin n → relativeRoots B.i` of the indivisible
    roots positive on `ν` there is an isomorphism `e` from the product of the models
    `N (some (a k))` onto `V` with `e ≫ j` equal to the multiplication morphism
    `RelativeRoot.orderedMultiplication` of the maps `φ (some (a k))`, taken in the order of
    `a`. Since `V` and `j` are chosen before the enumeration, every order of the factors gives
    the same closed subgroup scheme. This is the theorem's assertion that the product over
    `Φ^{+,nd}`, in any order, is one `𝒪`-smooth closed subgroup scheme of `𝒢_{x,f}` with
    unipotent fibres; the assertion for `Φ^{-,nd}` is the same clause at the generic
    cocharacter `-ν`, since the roots positive on `-ν` are those negative on `ν`;
  - for every generic cocharacter `ν` of `S` and all injective enumerations `aneg`, `apos` of
    the indivisible roots negative, respectively positive, on `ν`, the multiplication
    morphism, in the order of `TorusRootModelFamilyData.orderedIntegralIndex` (the roots
    `aneg`, then the torus, then the roots `apos`), from the product of the models to
    `M` is an open immersion. A generic cocharacter gives a system of positive roots, and every
    system of positive roots arises in this way, so this covers "any order of the factors".
- `UnramifiedMoyPrasadCongruenceStatement` is KP Proposition 8.5.16 at constant depth: for
  every smooth model `M` of `H` over `𝒪` with `M(𝒪) = G(K)_{x,r}` and every `n : ℕ+`,
  `M.congruenceSubgroupAt n`, the image of `ker(M(𝒪) → M(𝒪/𝔪ⁿ))`, is `G(K)_{x,r+n}`. This is
  the equality `𝒢_{x,f}(𝔪ⁿ) = 𝒢_{x,f+n}(𝒪)` of the proposition. The scheme-level identity
  `(𝒢_{x,f})_n = 𝒢_{x,f+n}` of the `n`-th congruence group scheme is not stated; by KP
  Corollary 2.10.11 it is equivalent to the stated equality of points.
- `UnramifiedMoyPrasadUnipotentFibreStatement` is KP Corollary 8.5.12(2) at constant depth:
  for `r > 0` the special fibre `M.specialFibre` of every such model is unipotent
  (`IsUnipotent`). The isomorphism of the open cell onto the special fibre is not stated.
- `UnramifiedMoyPrasadRadicalPreimageStatement` is KP Corollary 8.5.13 at the constant function
  `r`, in corrected form (see below). For every such model and every representative
  `i : U ⟶ M.specialFibre` of the unipotent radical of its special fibre (`IsUnipotentRadical`),
  the image in `H(K)` of the integral sections whose reduction lies in the image of `i` is
  `G(K)_{x,0+}` when `r = 0` and `G(K)_{x,r}` when `r > 0`.
- `UnramifiedMoyPrasadKernelStatement` is KP Proposition 8.5.18 for the constant functions
  `r ≤ s`: for smooth models `M`, `M'` of `H` over `𝒪` with `M(𝒪) = G(K)_{x,r}` and
  `M'(𝒪) = G(K)_{x,s}`, every homomorphism of models `ψ : M' → M` extending the identity of `H`
  (the homomorphism `ρ_{f,g}` of KP 8.5.17, which exists and is unique by KP Corollary
  2.10.10) has a smooth, connected and unipotent kernel on special fibres:
  `kernel ψ.specialFibreMap`. The product decomposition of the kernel in the proposition is
  not stated. The formulation follows `UnramifiedModelKernelStatement` (KP Proposition 8.4.15).

All model statements allow any smooth model with the given integral points. Over `𝒪`, whose
residue field is separably closed, such a model is unique up to unique isomorphism by KP
Corollary 2.10.11, so this is the model `𝒢_{x,r}` of the source.

## The correction to Corollary 8.5.13 at positive depth

KP Corollary 8.5.13 identifies the preimage of `R_u(Ḡ_{x,f})(𝔣)` with `G(K)_{x,f+}`, where
`f+` is the function of KP Definition 8.5.8: `f+(a) = f(a)+` if `f(a) + f(-a) = 0` and
`f+(a) = f(a)` otherwise. For the constant function `f = r` with `r > 0` every `a ∈ Φ̂`
(including `a = 0`) has `f(a) + f(-a) = 2r > 0`, so `f+ = f` and the preimage is all of
`G(K)_{x,r}`; this agrees with KP Corollary 8.5.12(2), by which the special fibre is then a
connected unipotent group, equal to its unipotent radical. Only for `r = 0` is `f+` the
constant function `0+`, and then `G(K)_{x,0+} = ⋃_{s>0} G(K)_{x,s}`.

Consequently the assertion "for `r ≥ 0` the preimage is `G(K)_{x,r+} = ⋃_{s>r} G(K)_{x,s}`" is
false at every positive depth `r` at which the filtration jumps. For example, let `H = G_m`
over `K` (split, `T = H`, no roots) and `r = 1`: `G(K)_{x,1} = 1 + 𝔪` and
`G(K)_{x,1+} = 1 + 𝔪²`. The model with integral points `1 + 𝔪` is the dilatation of the
standard model of `G_m` along the unit section of its special fibre; its special fibre is
`G_a`, which is its own unipotent radical, so the preimage is `1 + 𝔪 ≠ 1 + 𝔪²`. The statement
here therefore asserts the two cases `r = 0` and `r > 0` separately. Away from the jumps the
two groups can agree: for the same torus at `r = 1/2` both `G(K)_{x,1/2}` and
`⋃_{s>1/2} G(K)_{x,s}` are `1 + 𝔪`.
