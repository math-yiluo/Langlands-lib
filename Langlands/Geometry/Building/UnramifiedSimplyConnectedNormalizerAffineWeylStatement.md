# Simply connected normalizer image over the maximal unramified extension

`Langlands.Geometry.Building.MaximalUnramified.SimplyConnectedNormalizerAffineWeylStatement.{u}`
is a proposition definition. For every nonarchimedean local field `F : Type u`,
put `K = LocalGalois.Separable.inertiaFixedField F`. For every affine group
scheme `G` over `Spec K` with `IsSimplyConnected G` and every supplied
`C : MaximalUnramified.BuildingData F G`, it asserts

```text
C.apartment.normalizerAffineEquiv.range =
  AffineRootSystem.affineWeylGroup C.affineRoots.
```

The left side is the image of the existing action of the actual normalizer
`N_G(S)(K)`, where `S` is the maximal split torus supplied in `C`. The right
side is the subgroup of affine automorphisms generated by reflections in the
actual affine roots of `C`. The equality concerns the entire normalizer image.
It does not assert that the normalizer action is faithful.

`BuildingData F G` is the existing abbreviation for
`IntegerValuationBuildingData (MaximalUnramified.valuation F) G`. It supplies
connected reductivity, a maximal split torus, a full equipollence class of
valuations of the root datum, the actual centralizer and full omega
compatibility V 6. The valuation of `K` is the existing normalized
integer-valued valuation. `IsSimplyConnected G` includes semisimplicity.
The published `isSimplyConnectedFieldExtension` passes this property to an
algebraic closure, relating the Library's definition to the source's
geometric simple connectedness.
The group may be any simply connected `K`-group; no descent to `F` is required.
There is no premise asserting AS 5, a Tits system, a facet-fixing property,
or an identification with a canonical apartment. Rank zero is included.

## Source and comparison with the supplied apartment

The source is T. Kaletha and G. Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach*
(2023), Proposition 9.4.35, printed pp. 362–363 / PDF pp. 393–394.
For a semisimple simply connected group, the source identifies the affine
Weyl group with `N(k)/Z(k)_b`, the image of the normalizer on its apartment.
The preceding paragraph 9.4.34 describes the affine Weyl group using
reflections in affine root hyperplanes. Thus the source image and subgroup
correspond to the two sides of the Lean equality.

We specialize the source field `k` to `K`. The source setting is a discretely
valued Henselian field with perfect residue field (Chapter 4, printed p. 157 /
PDF p. 188). The normalized valuation of `K` is discrete; the extension of the
valuation from the complete local field `F` is Henselian and has residue field
an algebraic closure of the finite residue field of `F`. These field facts
and the comparison below are obligations of a proof, rather than extra
hypotheses of the proposition.

The source proposition is stated for the apartment constructed by unramified
descent. The Lean proposition quantifies over every supplied `C`, so the
following source comparisons are needed:

1. Theorem 9.6.5, printed pp. 366–367 / PDF pp. 397–398, identifies the
   points of the descended apartment with a full equipollence class of
   omega-compatible valuations of the root datum. This map is equivariant
   under the actual `V(S) ⋊ N(k)` action.
2. Proposition 6.2.6, printed p. 216 / PDF p. 247, says that any two
   omega-compatible valuations of the root datum of `(G,S)` are equipollent.
   Hence the full class supplied in `C` is the class obtained in Theorem
   9.6.5 for that same maximal split torus. Full centralizer V 6 is already
   included in `C`.
3. The first two paragraphs of 9.6.6, printed p. 367 / PDF p. 398, identify
   the affine apartments and their actual affine root groups, and therefore
   their affine root systems. The equivariance in Theorem 9.6.5 identifies
   the normalizer actions. Transporting Proposition 9.4.35 along this
   equivariant affine identification gives the stated equality. A proof
   must verify that the reflection-generated subgroups correspond.

The authors' *Corrections and additions*, version of 1 November 2024,
p. 33, expressly explains that the simply connected assertion of AS 5 for
a general group is proved in Proposition 9.4.35 under perfectness of the
residue field. In particular, the corrected Chapter 7 treats this assertion
as an additional input; the Chapter 7 Tits-system construction alone cannot
be used to establish it. Corrections pp. 39–41 contain no change to
Proposition 9.4.35, Theorem 9.6.5, or the apartment and root comparison in
9.6.6.

## Provenance and use

This is a derived specialization of the source normalizer-image theorem to
the actual maximal unramified field and arbitrary supplied full
omega-compatible data, using the cited apartment and root comparisons.
This module supplies no proof or apartment construction.

The equality is a general prerequisite for the chamber-stabilizer target:
it applies to every normalizer element, and also gives pointwise fixing of
any setwise stabilized apartment facet when combined with the existing
affine Weyl facet-fixing theorem and the affine-root-system theorem for the
supplied integer-valued data. The separate
`IntegerChamberStabilizerReduction` transports normalizer chamber fixing to
the action of all rational points on the reduced quotient. Those
applications do not establish the equality asserted here.
