# Chambers through a point form an orbit of a sharp-generated point fixer

This is an internal result, derived from existing Library results. All fields, schemes and
buildings have arbitrary universe `u`. It refines the published full-fixer transitivity
`IntegerValuationBuildingData.exists_fixer_smul_chamber_eq` (module `ChamberClosureOrbit`).
It is used for the transitivity clause of `UnramifiedTwistedResidueQuotientStatement`.

## Setting

Let `B : IntegerValuationBuildingData v G` be supplied KP apartment and centralizer data
for a field `k` with an integer-valued valuation `v`. Write `A` for its apartment, `B(G)`
for its reduced building and `G(k)^♯_a` for the sharp fixer of an apartment point `a`
(KP Definition 7.4.1). `G(k)_y` (`B.fixer y`) is the full fixer of `y ∈ B(G)`. A chamber
`Y` has a closure presentation through `y` (`B.HasChamberClosurePresentation Y y`) when
`Y = g · facet(x₀)` for a regular apartment point `x₀`, with an apartment point `a` in
the facet closure of `x₀` and `g · a = y`.

## Results

- `closure_pointReflections_le_map_sharpFixer p`: every product of reflections in
  affine roots vanishing at `p` is the apartment action of a normalizer point lying in
  `G(k)^♯_p`.
- `exists_sharpFixer_normalizer_fixing_facet`: two regular facets whose closures contain
  `p` are related by a normalizer point `m` with `m ∈ G(k)^♯_p` and `m · p = p`.
- `exists_conj_sharpFixer_smul_chamber_eq`: for chambers `Y₀`, `Y₁` with closure
  presentations through `y` there are `h ∈ G(k)`, an apartment point `a` and
  `s ∈ G(k)^♯_a` such that `h s h⁻¹` fixes `y` and maps `Y₀` onto `Y₁`.
- `exists_normal_fixer_smul_chamber_eq`: if `H ≤ G(k)` is normal and contains
  `G(k)^♯_a` for every apartment point `a`, then `H ∩ G(k)_y` acts transitively on the
  chambers with a closure presentation through `y`.
- `IntegerValuationNormalFixerChamberOrbitStatement` is the Prop-valued form of the
  previous item over every supplied integer-valued building. The theorem
  `integerValuationNormalFixerChamberOrbit` proves it. No hypothesis beyond the supplied
  data is used.
- `UnramifiedZeroDatum.exists_zeroPoints_fixer_smul_chamber_eq`: over the maximal
  unramified extension `K` of a nonarchimedean local field, `G(K)^0 ∩ G(K)_y` acts
  transitively on these chambers. Here `G(K)^0` is `E.zeroPoints`, the zero subgroup
  computed by a supplied `UnramifiedZeroDatum E`.

## Proof

1. The published `AffineRootSystem.exists_facetRel_closure_pointReflections` (module
   `AffineWeylPointStabilizer`) transports one regular facet through `p` into the other.
   It does so by an element of the subgroup generated by reflections in affine roots
   vanishing at `p`. The published `exists_sharpFixer_normalizer_eq_affineReflection`
   (module `IntegerValuationNormalizerSharpReduction`) lifts each such reflection to a
   normalizer point `p.1 u p.2` in `G(k)^♯_p`. There `u ∈ U_{a,p,0}` because the root
   vanishes at `p`, and the two opposite factors lie in `U_{-a,p,0}` by axiom V 5 of
   KP Definition 6.1.2. So the closure maps into the image of
   `N(k) ∩ G(k)^♯_p` under the apartment action. The lifted element fixes `p` by
   `apply_eq_of_mem_closure_pointReflections`, and it carries facets as the published
   proof of `exists_normalizer_fixing_facet` does.
2. Write `Y₀ = g₀ · facet(x₀)` with `g₀ · a₀ = y`, and `Y₁ = g₁ · facet(x₁)` with
   `g₁ · a₁ = y`. The building relation (`ApartmentQuotient.mk_eq_mk_iff`) applied to
   `(g₀⁻¹ g₁) · a₁ = a₀` gives `n ∈ N(k)` with `n · a₁ = a₀` and
   `s₀ := g₁⁻¹ g₀ n ∈ G(k)^♯_{a₁}`. Step 1 at `p = a₀`, applied to `x₀` and `n · x₁`,
   gives `m ∈ N(k) ∩ G(k)^♯_{a₀}`. The element `g = g₁ n⁻¹ m g₀⁻¹` is the element of the
   published full-fixer proof, so it fixes `y` and maps `Y₀` onto `Y₁` by the same
   computation. Moreover `g = g₁ (n⁻¹ m n · s₀⁻¹) g₁⁻¹`. Here
   `n⁻¹ m n ∈ G(k)^♯_{n⁻¹ · a₀} = G(k)^♯_{a₁}` by `KPApartmentData.sharpFixer_le_comap`,
   so `s := n⁻¹ m n · s₀⁻¹ ∈ G(k)^♯_{a₁}`.
3. For normal `H` containing every `G(k)^♯_a`, the conjugate `g₁ s g₁⁻¹` lies in `H`.
   For the unramified corollary, `E.zeroPoints` is normal (`normal_zeroPoints`, module
   `UnramifiedSplitBoundedPoints`). It contains every sharp fixer by
   `UnramifiedZeroDatum.sharpFixer_le_zeroPoints` (module `IntegerValuationSharpCoverImage`).

## Scope

The results concern only the supplied building data and chambers with an exact closure
presentation. No parahoric model, reductive quotient or Frobenius structure is
constructed here. The subgroup `H` enters only through normality and the containments
`G(k)^♯_a ≤ H`. The transporting element is a `G(k)`-conjugate of an element of a single
point sharp fixer.
