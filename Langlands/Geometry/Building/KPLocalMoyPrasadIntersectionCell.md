# Open-cell uniqueness for the groups `G(K)_{x,r}` over `K = F^ur`

Helper module for the proof of `LocalMoyPrasadGroupPropertiesStatement`, clauses 7 and 8
(KP Lemma 7.3.16 and separation of the filtration, at positive depth). Nothing here is a
new definition; every result is a theorem about the published objects
`BuildingData.moyPrasad`, `BuildingData.rootGroupSpan`, `BuildingData.rootFiltrationSpan`,
`BuildingData.centralizerTorusFiltration` and the field-independent `KPApartmentData`
filtrations.

Source: Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits theory: a new approach* (2023), Proposition 7.3.12(3),(5)
(the product decomposition `G_{x,f} = U^-_{x,f} · T_{f(0)} · U^+_{x,f}` with unique
factors) and Lemma 7.3.16 (`G_{x,f} ∩ T(K) = T(K)_{f(0)}`). KP prove uniqueness of the
factors from the big-cell isomorphism `U^- × T × U^+ → G`; the Lean proofs below use the
Library's algebraic open-cell injectivity instead.

## Positive root filtrations at constant depth (field-independent)

`KPApartmentData.range_orderedPositiveFiltrationBlockProduct_eq_iSup_of_reductive`.
For a connected reductive `G` with maximal split torus `i : S → G`, an apartment point `x`,
a depth `r ≥ 0`, a cocharacter `ν` and an enumeration `a : Fin n → X^*(S)` of the
indivisible `ν`-positive roots, the image of the ordered product of the constant-depth
blocks `U_{a_j,x,r} · U_{2a_j,x,r}` equals the subgroup generated by all `U_{b,x,r}`
with `b` a `ν`-positive root.

*Proof.* This is the Library theorem `range_orderedPositiveFiltrationBlockProduct_eq_iSup`
without its affine-root-system premise. Index `n · 2` slots by pairs `(j, ε)`, put
`a_j` in slot `(j,0)` and `2 a_j` in slot `(j,1)`, and give each slot the filtration
group if the label is a root and `⊥` otherwise. Every positive root is an indivisible
positive root or its double by the algebraic rank-one factorization
(`HasOppositeFactorPairs.exists_indivisiblePositiveRoot_eq_or_double`, fed by
`rootRankOneFactorization`). The commutator estimate
`filtration_commutator_positive_height` shows that commutators of slot groups lie in the
span of strictly higher slots, so `HeightCollection.range_orderedProduct_eq_iSup`
collects the ordered product onto the span. Regrouping the slots by
`range_orderedSubgroupPairsProduct` gives the block product.

`KPApartmentData.eq_one_of_forall_mem_iSup_positive_filtration`. An element lying in the
span of the positive root filtrations at every positive depth is `1`.

*Proof.* At each depth `r > 0` write the element as an ordered block product. The Library's
injectivity of the ordered product of the actual root groups
(`orderedRootSubgroupPoints_injective_of_statement` with the published
`dynamicRootOrderedProduct`) shows the coordinates do not depend on `r`. Each coordinate
therefore lies in every block `U_{a_j,x,r} U_{2a_j,x,r}` and is `1` by the block
separation lemma `eq_one_of_forall_mem_filtrationBlock`.

## Open-cell uniqueness over `K`

Fix building data `B` over `K = F^ur` with `Z = Z_G(S)` a torus (`hZ`).

- `rootGroupSpan_le_dynamicUnipotent`: if every root of `Ψ` pairs positively with `μ`,
  then `U_Ψ` lies in the dynamic unipotent group `U(μ)(K)`. Each root group lies there
  (`RelativeRoot.subgroup_le_dynamicUnipotent`).
- `rootFiltrationSpan_le_rootGroupSpan`, `rootFiltrationSpan_antitone`,
  `rootFiltrationSpan_eq_iSup_subtype`: elementary properties of the filtration span.
- `centralizer_map_injective`: `j : Z(K) → G(K)` is injective because `j` is a
  monomorphism.
- `eq_of_mul_eq_mul` (uniqueness in the open cell): if
  `u · j(z) · v = u' · j(z') · v'` with `u, u' ∈ U_{Φ⁺(-μ)}`, `z, z' ∈ Z(K)` and
  `v, v' ∈ U_{Φ⁺(μ)}`, then `u = u'`, `z = z'` and `v = v'`. This is the Library's
  `unipotentTripleProduct_injective` applied to `U(-μ) × Z_G(μ) × U(μ)`; `j(Z(K))` lies in
  the centralizer of `μ` by `map_mem_centralizerPoints`.
- `eq_one_of_forall_mem_rootFiltrationSpan`: an element of `U_{Φ⁺(μ),x,r}` for every
  `r > 0` is `1` (the field-independent lemma above).

## Clauses 7 and 8 at positive depth, from a product cover

The cover hypotheses below are inline premises of these helper lemmas. They are exactly
what the conclusion of `LocalMoyPrasadPositiveProductStatement` (the
positive-depth sign product cover of KP Lemma 7.3.11 and Proposition 7.3.12) supplies. The
Library proves that statement (`localMoyPrasadPositiveProduct`, module
`KPLocalMoyPrasadPositiveProductProof`), and the final proof of
`LocalMoyPrasadGroupPropertiesStatement` discharges them by that theorem, not by assuming them.

`comap_moyPrasad_eq_of_cover`. Let `r : ℝ≥0` and let `μ` be a cocharacter. Suppose every
`g ∈ G(K)_{x,r}` can be written `g = u · j(z) · v` with `u ∈ U_{Φ⁺(-μ),x,r}`,
`z ∈ Z(K)_r` and `v ∈ U_{Φ⁺(μ),x,r}`. Then `j⁻¹(G(K)_{x,r}) = Z(K)_r`.

*Proof.* `⊇` is `map_mem_moyPrasad`. For `⊆`, write `j(z) = u · j(z') · v`; comparing with
`1 · j(z) · 1` by `eq_of_mul_eq_mul` gives `z = z' ∈ Z(K)_r`.

`iInf_moyPrasad_eq_bot_of_cover`. If for one cocharacter `μ` the cover above holds at every
depth `r > 0`, then `⋂_r G(K)_{x,r} = {1}`.

*Proof.* For `g` in the intersection choose covers `g = u_r · j(z_r) · v_r` for each
`r > 0`. Uniqueness in the open cell gives `u_r = u_1`, `z_r = z_1`, `v_r = v_1`. Hence
`u_1` and `v_1` lie in the positive filtration spans at every positive depth and are `1`;
`z_1` lies in every `Z(K)_r` (for `r = 0` by antitonicity) and is `1` by the published
separation `iInf_centralizerTorusFiltration_eq_bot`. So `g = 1`.
