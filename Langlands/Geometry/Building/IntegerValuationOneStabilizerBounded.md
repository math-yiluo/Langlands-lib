# Boundedness of one-stabilizers for integer-valued building data

This module reduces the boundedness of every one-stabilizer `K_y = G(k)^1 ∩ G(k)_y`
of supplied `IntegerValuationBuildingData v G` to two inputs, over an arbitrary field `k`
with an integer-valued valuation `v` and an arbitrary valuative relation. The inputs are:

- the image `j(Z(k)^1)` in `G(k)` of the norm-one subgroup of the centralizer `Z = Z_G(S)`
  is algebraically bounded;
- every depth-zero filtration subgroup `U_{a,x,0}` (`C.filtration x a 0`) for a relative
  root `a` and a point `x` of the apartment is algebraically bounded.

No topology, completeness or local compactness is used, and the valuative relation need
not be related to `v`. This is the boundedness half of Kaletha--Prasad, *Bruhat--Tits
Theory: A New Approach* (`kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`), Theorem
4.2.15(1), printed p. 173 / PDF p. 204, which uses that `G(k)^1_x` is bounded (Axiom
4.1.2). The route follows the proof of KP Corollary 7.3.14, printed p. 251 / PDF p. 282:
write the fixer as a product of root-group pieces and a normalizer piece using
Proposition 7.3.12(1), printed p. 250 / PDF p. 281, and bound each factor. This module
replaces KP's appeal to the boundedness of `Z(k)^0` by the boundedness of `j(Z(k)^1)` and
the finiteness of the relative Weyl group. It is a derived organization of that argument.
The two inputs are hypotheses of the final theorem and are not proved here.

## Declarations

Throughout, `C : IntegerValuationBuildingData v G` with `[IsAffineHom G.X.hom]`.

- `sharpFixer_le_characterValuationKernel`: for every subset `Ω` of the apartment, the
  sharp fixer `C.sharpFixer Ω` lies in `G(k)^1 = characterValuationKernel v G`. The sharp
  fixer is a supremum of subgroups of relative root groups. Each root group lies in
  `G(k)^1` by the Library lemma `rootGroupPoints_le_characterValuationKernel`.
- `normOneNormalizerStabilizer x`: the subgroup `N(k)^1_x` of `G(k)`. It consists of the
  elements of `G(k)^1` that are images of points `n ∈ N(k)` of the normalizer of `S` with
  `n • x = x`. `mem_normOneNormalizerStabilizer` unfolds membership.
- `positiveZeroRootGroups x ν`: the subgroup of `G(k)` generated by the depth-zero root
  groups `C.apartment.chamberRootGroup x false b` at `x`, over the relative roots `b`
  positive on the cocharacter `ν`.
- `oneStabilizer_apartmentMap_subset`: for a generic cocharacter `mu`, the one-stabilizer
  of the image of an apartment point `x` in the reduced building is contained in
  `U_x(-mu) · U_x(mu) · N(k)^1_x`. Here `U_x(ν) = positiveZeroRootGroups x ν`.
- `isAlgebraicallyBounded_positiveZeroRootGroups`: if every `U_{a,x,0}` with
  `a ∈ relativeRoots C.i` is algebraically bounded, so is `positiveZeroRootGroups x ν`.
- `normOneNormalizerStabilizer_subset_iUnion`: there is a map
  `r : relativeWeylGroup C.i → G(k)` such that `N(k)^1_x` lies in the union of the
  translates `r w • j(Z(k)^1)`.
- `isAlgebraicallyBounded_normOneNormalizerStabilizer`: if `j(Z(k)^1)` is algebraically
  bounded, so is `N(k)^1_x` for every apartment point `x`.
- `isAlgebraicallyBounded_oneStabilizer` (main result): if `j(Z(k)^1)` is algebraically
  bounded (`hZ`) and every `U_{a,x,0}` is algebraically bounded (`hU`), then
  `C.oneStabilizer y` is algebraically bounded for every point `y` of the reduced
  building.

## Proofs

`oneStabilizer_apartmentMap_subset`. Let `g ∈ K_x`. By
`ApartmentQuotient.mem_stabilizer_apartment_iff`, `g = p * n` with `p` in the sharp fixer
of `{x}` and `n ∈ N(k)` fixing `x`. The first declaration puts `p` in `G(k)^1`, and hence
`n = p⁻¹ g` is in `G(k)^1` too. The generic-field Library form of KP Proposition 7.3.12(1),
`KPApartmentData.sharpFixer_subset_mul_sharpNormalizerStabilizer`, writes
`p = u * w * h`. Here `u ∈ U_x(-mu)`, `w ∈ U_x(mu)`, and `h` lies in the sharp fixer of
`{x}`, is in `N(k)` and fixes `x`. Again `h ∈ G(k)^1`, so `h n ∈ N(k)^1_x`, and
`g = u * w * (h n)`.

`isAlgebraicallyBounded_positiveZeroRootGroups`. The positive relative roots for `ν` form a
finite set, enumerated by `Fin m` (`Set.Finite.fin_param`). The Library lemmas
`iSup_chamberRootGroup_enum` and `commutator_chamberRootGroup_enum` (KP Lemma 7.3.11), with
`HeightCollection.range_orderedProduct_eq_iSup`, identify the generated subgroup with the
range of an ordered product of the root groups `U_{a_j,x,0}`. It is bounded by
`isAlgebraicallyBounded_range_orderedProduct_of_finiteType` from
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.ValuedBoundedProducts`. `G` is smooth since it is
connected reductive, hence locally of finite type.

`normOneNormalizerStabilizer_subset_iUnion`. For each class `w ∈ N(k)/Z(k)`, choose a
representative `m` that lies in `G(k)^1` and fixes `x`, if one exists. Otherwise take `1`.
If `n ∈ N(k)^1_x` has class `w`, then `m⁻¹ n` is in `Z(k)`, say `m⁻¹ n = j(z)`. It lies in
`G(k)^1` and fixes `x`. The Library lemma
`IntegerValuationBuildingData.mem_characterValuationKernel_of_smul_eq` then gives
`z ∈ Z(k)^1`. It applies the omega-compatibility axiom and uses the fact that relative
roots and restricted characters span. So `n = m · j(z)` lies in `m • j(Z(k)^1)`.

`isAlgebraicallyBounded_normOneNormalizerStabilizer`. The relative Weyl group is finite
(`finite_relativeWeylGroup_of_span`). A finite union of translates of a bounded set is
bounded (`isAlgebraicallyBounded_smul_set_of_finiteType`, `isAlgebraicallyBounded_iUnion`).

`isAlgebraicallyBounded_oneStabilizer`. Choose a generic cocharacter `mu`
(`exists_genericCocharacter`). Write `y = g • [x]` with `x` in the apartment
(`exists_smul_apartmentMap`). `K_[x]` is bounded: it lies in a product of three bounded
sets (`isAlgebraicallyBounded_mul_of_finiteType`). `K_y` lies in the conjugate
`{g} * K_[x] * {g⁻¹}`, because `G(k)^1` is normal (`characterValuationKernel_normal`) and
`g⁻¹ h g` fixes `[x]` when `h` fixes `y`. Conjugates of bounded sets are bounded
(`isAlgebraicallyBounded_conj_of_finiteType`).
