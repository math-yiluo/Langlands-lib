# The apartment embeds in the reduced building over any field

This module proves that the apartment of a maximal split torus embeds in the existing reduced
building. The result holds over every field and for every equipollence class of valuations
of the root datum. It needs no apartment axioms. As a consequence it proves the published
statement `Langlands.Geometry.Building.MaximalUnramifiedApartmentInjectiveStatement`, one of
the nine inputs of `UnramifiedBuildingDescentReductionStatement`.

Mathematically this is the injectivity part of KP Proposition 7.6.3 and Remark 7.6.5
(Kaletha–Prasad 2023): the map `A(S) → B(G)` is injective because an element of
`N(k) ∩ G(k)^♯_x` fixes `x`. The Lean proof follows the route of the existing local-field
lemma `ValuationBuildingData.normalizer_smul_eq_self_of_mem_sharpFixer`
(`PointParahoricNormalizer`). That lemma assumes the apartment axioms. Here the generic
point exchange of `GenericPointSharpExchange` replaces the exchange that uses them.

## Setting

Throughout, `k : Type u` is any field, `i : S.toGrp ⟶ G` a torus of a group scheme `G` over
`Spec k` with `Mono i.hom.hom.left` and `IsAffineHom G.X.hom`, `ω : kˣ → ℝ` any function, and
`D : KPApartmentData i ω`. The hypotheses `hG : IsConnectedReductive G` and
`hi : IsMaximalSplitTorus i` are explicit arguments.

## Declarations

### `KPApartmentData.normalizer_smul_eq_self_of_mem_sharpFixer`

For `hG`, `hi`, `x : D.Apartment` and `n : normalizerPoints i` with
`(n : rationalPoints G) ∈ D.sharpFixer {x}`, the conclusion is `n • x = x`.

Proof:

1. `G` is affine (`isAffine_of_isAffineHom`) and smooth (`hG.smooth`). The split torus `S`
   has a generic cocharacter `ν` (`IsSplitTorus.exists_genericCocharacter`).
2. `KPApartmentData.sharpFixer_subset_mul_sharpNormalizerStabilizer hG hi x ν` (generic
   point exchange, any field, no apartment axioms) writes `n = v * w * m`. Here `v` lies in
   the subgroup generated by the zero-depth root groups of the roots positive for `-ν`,
   `w` lies in the same subgroup for `ν`, and `m` lies in `D.sharpNormalizerStabilizer x`.
   By `KPApartmentData.mem_sharpNormalizerStabilizer`, `m` is in the normalizer `N(k)` and
   fixes `x`.
3. `KPApartmentData.positiveChamberRootGroups_le_sharpUnipotentPoints` puts `v` in the
   Dynamic unipotent group of `-ν` and `w` in that of `ν`. The opposite cocharacter is
   identified with `-ν` by `groupHom_neg_comp_eq_oppositeCocharacter`.
4. Put `q := n * m⁻¹` in `N(k)`; then `q = v * w`. `normalizer_eq_one_of_unipotent_product`
   (`BruhatOppositeCellReduction`; a normalizing point that is a product of opposite and
   original unipotent points is `1`) gives `q = 1`. Hence `n = m`, and `m • x = x`.

### `KPApartmentData.apartmentMap_injective`

For `hG` and `hi`, `D.apartmentMap : D.Apartment → D.ReducedBuilding` is injective. This is
`ApartmentQuotient.apartment_injective` for the sharp-fixer family of `D`. Its stabilizer
compatibility hypothesis is the previous lemma.

### `maximalUnramifiedApartmentInjective`

The proof of `MaximalUnramifiedApartmentInjectiveStatement`. For a nonarchimedean local field
`F`, a group `H` over `Spec (inertiaFixedField F)` and
`C : MaximalUnramified.BuildingData F H`, `C.apartmentMap` is
`C.apartment.apartmentMap`. Apply `KPApartmentData.apartmentMap_injective` with
`C.isConnectedReductive` and `C.isMaximalSplitTorus`. The quasi-split structure of `H` over
`K`, V 6, and the valuation normalization of `C` are not used.

## Scope

Both general lemmas are universe polymorphic. They hold in every characteristic. They do not
assume completeness, a discrete valuation, or apartment axioms. They use only connected
reductivity of `G` and maximality of the split torus.
