# Structural properties of the minimal congruent torus filtration

`minimalCongruentStructural` proves `MinimalCongruentStructuralStatements`
by pairing the six proofs at the same universe. Each assertion concerns
tori over `inertiaFixedField F`, with models over `fixedFieldIntegers F`.
The conjuncts are `minimalCongruentShallowModel`, `minimalCongruentFunctoriality`,
`minimalCongruentOrder`, `minimalCongruentConnectedModel`,
`minimalCongruentCongruence`, and `minimalCongruentWeaklyInduced`.

The assembled assertions correspond to Kaletha–Prasad,
*Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023), Definitions B.5.2–B.5.3
and B.10.2, Lemma B.10.9, Proposition B.10.10 and Corollary B.10.13.
