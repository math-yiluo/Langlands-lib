# Residue-characteristic powers in the minimal congruent torus filtration

## The statement

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field of either characteristic, let
`K = inertiaFixedField F` be its maximal unramified extension, let
`O = fixedFieldIntegers F` and let
`p = ringChar (IsLocalRing.ResidueField O)`, the residue characteristic.
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.MinimalCongruentResiduePowerStatement` asserts
the following for every `K`-torus `T` (with the binders `T`, `[IsAffine T.X.left]`
and `hT : IsTorus T.toGrp` of `TorusFiltration.StructuralStatements`) and all
real depths `r s : ℝ≥0` with

    0 < r,   r ≤ s,   s ≤ 2 * r,   s ≤ r + 1:

for every `g` in `T(K)_r = minimalCongruentPoints F T hT r`, the power `g ^ p`
lies in `T(K)_s = minimalCongruentPoints F T hT s`. Here `T(K)_r` is the
minimal congruent filtration of KP Definition B.10.8 as constructed in
`TorusFiltration.Points`, for an arbitrary torus.

The statement concerns only these rational-point subgroups. It has no
hypothesis about Lie lattices, integral models, special fibres or splitting
fields, and no comparison of `T(K)_r` with another filtration. The proposition
is universe-polymorphic in the universe of `F`. This file states the estimate
and does not prove it.

## Exponent of the quotient

`T(K)` is commutative, and `T(K)_s ≤ T(K)_r` when `r ≤ s` because the
filtration decreases (`minimalCongruentPoints_antitone`), so
`T(K)_r / T(K)_s` is an abelian group. The statement says that the `p`th power
map sends `T(K)_r` into `T(K)_s`, that is, every element of the quotient is
killed by `p`. Hence the exponent of `T(K)_r / T(K)_s` divides `p`: the quotient
is either trivial or an elementary abelian `p`-group, a vector space over the
field with `p` elements.

## Argument at depth one and higher

Let `r ≥ 1` and write `r = q + n` with `0 ≤ q < 1` and `n ≥ 1` an integer. Let
`M` be a smooth `O`-model of `T` with integral points `T(K)_q`: the connected
Néron model `connectedNeronModel F T hT` when `q = 0`, and a model given by
Lemma B.10.9 (`MinimalCongruentShallowModelStatement`, proved as
`minimalCongruentShallowModel`) when `q > 0`. By Definition B.10.8(3), in the
form `minimalCongruentPoints_add_pnat`, `T(K)_r` is the image
`M.congruenceSubgroupAt n` of the `n`th congruence kernel and `T(K)_{r+1}` is
`M.congruenceSubgroupAt (n + 1)`.

The residue characteristic lies in the maximal ideal of `O`
(`IntegralModel.residueChar_mem_maximalIdeal`). The congruence power lemma
`IntegralModel.pow_mem_congruenceSubgroupAt_succ` therefore sends `g` in level
`n` to `g ^ p` in level `n + 1`; this is the point form of the vector-group
statement of Proposition A.5.19(1). Thus `g ^ p ∈ T(K)_{r+1}`, and
`T(K)_{r+1} ≤ T(K)_s` because `s ≤ r + 1` and the filtration decreases. At these
depths only the bound `s ≤ r + 1` is used, and `s ≤ 2 r` holds automatically.

## Argument at shallow depth

Let `0 < r < 1`. By Definition B.10.8(2), in the form
`minimalCongruentShallowSubgroup`, `T(K)_r` is generated by two kinds of
elements. Since `T(K)` is commutative, `x ↦ x ^ p` is a homomorphism, and the
elements whose `p`th powers lie in `T(K)_s` form a subgroup. It suffices to
check the generators.

* The first congruence subgroup of the connected Néron model `T^0`, which is
  its level-one congruence subgroup (`congruenceSubgroupAt_one`). If `g`
  lies in it, the power lemma gives `g ^ p` in the second congruence subgroup,
  which is `T(K)_2` by `minimalCongruentPoints_pnat`. Since `s ≤ r + 1 < 2`,
  this lies in `T(K)_s`.
* The images `f(R(K)_r^std)` for every homomorphism `f : R → T` from an induced
  `K`-torus `R` (`inducedTorusStandardImages`). Write `R` as a product of tori
  `R_{E/K} G_m`. Normalize the valuation `v` so that `v(K^×) = ℤ` and extend it
  to a separable closure. At positive depth, `t ∈ R(K)_r^std` means
  `v(χ(t) − 1) ≥ r` for every geometric character `χ` of `R`
  (`mem_localTorusFiltration_of_induced`; for `R_{E/K} G_m` the values `χ(t)`
  are the conjugates of `t ∈ E^×`). Put `y = χ(t) − 1`. Then

      χ(t ^ p) − 1 = (1 + y) ^ p − 1 = p y + Σ_{k = 2}^{p} C(p, k) y ^ k.

  Because `p` lies in the maximal ideal, `v(p) ≥ 1`, so `v(p y) ≥ r + 1`; in
  equal characteristic `p y = 0`. Each term with `k ≥ 2` has valuation at least
  `k r ≥ 2 r`. Hence `v(χ(t ^ p) − 1) ≥ min (r + 1, 2 r) ≥ s` for every `χ`,
  that is, `t ^ p ∈ R(K)_s^std`. By Corollary B.10.13 for the induced torus `R`
  this group is `R(K)_s` of the minimal congruent filtration; in the Library
  this comparison is the induced case of
  `MinimalCongruentWeaklyInducedStatement`. Functoriality, Proposition B.10.10
  (`minimalCongruentFunctoriality`), then gives
  `f(t) ^ p = f(t ^ p) ∈ T(K)_s`. When `s < 1` the image `f(R(K)_s^std)` is
  already one of the generators of `T(K)_s`.

## The range of depths

Both bounds are needed. Take `F` of equal characteristic `2`, `t` a uniformizer
of `F`, `E = K(u)` with `u³ = t`, and `R = R_{E/K} G_m`. The point `1 + u` has
depth `1/3`, and its square `1 + u²` has depth exactly `2/3`, so it does not lie
in `R(K)_1` although `1 ≤ 1/3 + 1`. In this case `R(K)_{1/3} / R(K)_1` has an
element of order four, and the isomorphism of Proposition B.10.17 with the
residue-field vector space `t(k)_{1/3} / t(k)_1` fails for this torus. The
module `TorusFiltration.RadicalStatement` records the same example against the
printed vector-group assertion of Proposition B.10.12. For `r < 1` the binding
bound is `s ≤ 2 r`; for `r ≥ 1` it is `s ≤ r + 1`. The latter is also needed:
for `F = ℚ_p` and `T = G_m`, the point `1 + p²` has depth `2`, while
`(1 + p²) ^ p − 1 = p³ + C(p, 2) p⁴ + ⋯` has valuation exactly `3`, so
`(1 + p²) ^ p` does not lie in `T(K)_s` for `3 < s ≤ 4 = 2 · 2`.
