# Smooth connected models below depth one

`minimalCongruentShallowModel` proves `MinimalCongruentShallowModelStatement`.
It uses `minimalCongruentModel` at the given shallow depth and identifies its
integral points with `minimalCongruentShallowSubgroup` by
`minimalCongruentPoints_of_lt_one`. At positive shallow depths, the model
construction dilates the connected Néron model along the smooth closed
connected subgroup generated by the reductions of the induced-torus images.

This is Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023),
Lemma B.10.9, printed p. 690, PDF p. 721. The source is
