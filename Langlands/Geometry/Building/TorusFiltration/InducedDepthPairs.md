# First congruence of the induced coefficient models

Let \(O\) be the unramified valuation ring, \(C\) a finite product of
valuation rings of intermediate fields, and \(J_r\) the ideal of tuples of
additive valuation at least \(r\), normalized over the base field.
`InducedTorusShallow.depthModel` uses `WeilCongruence.integralModel` with
the pair algebra \(O \times_{C/J_r} C\). For any nonnegative \(r\) the power
\(\pi^{\lceil r\rceil}\) of the base uniformizer is nonzero in \(O\) and maps to
\(J_r\) in \(C\), as required for the generic-fibre comparison. The pair model
is smooth by `WeilCongruence.group_smooth`.

`WeilCongruence.reduction_eq_one_iff` tests reduction to the identity by
membership of the pair-unit difference in the extended maximal ideal.
The first coordinate of this difference is zero. Since the maximal ideal of
\(O\) is generated by \(\pi\), membership is equivalent to the second
coordinate lying in \(\pi J_r\). Division by the uniformizer in each field
identifies \(\pi J_r\) with \(J_{r+1}\), using the order-one normalization.

`WeilCongruence.mem_integralPoints_iff` identifies a generic point of
the model with an integral coefficient unit whose difference from one
lies in \(J_r\). The integral coefficient tuple embeds into the generic
coefficient algebra. A point with coefficient difference in \(J_{r+1}\)
therefore lifts to a section at depth \(r\), and the reduction test on that
section recovers the same tuple. This proves `depthModel_congruenceSubgroup`.
At depth zero any tuple of valuation-ring units satisfies the depth
condition. At positive depth `mem_geometricCharacterCongruence_iff`
gives the same inequalities on the generic coordinates. A positive
inequality on the difference from one makes each coordinate a
valuation-ring unit.

The model extends the coefficient construction of
`InducedTorusShallow.modelOfFactors` to the depths used in the induced-torus
filtration of Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023),
Proposition B.3.6(1), printed pp. 658–659, PDF pp. 689–690. The shift uses
their congruence-filtration convention in Definition B.5.3.
