# Unramified Galois stability, model descent and cohomology

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field and `T` an `F`-torus.
Let `K = inertiaFixedField F`, `O = fixedFieldIntegers F` and
`Γ = Gal(K/F)` with its Krull topology. The filtration subgroups
lie in the rational points of the actual base-changed `K`-torus.

`MinimalCongruentUnramifiedDescentStatement` asserts `Γ`-stability
of every subgroup. For each smooth `O`-model with those integral
points, it asserts descent to a smooth model over the integers
of `F`. The base change of the descended model is identified with
the original model by an isomorphism over the identity of the
generic torus. Its `F`-integral points equal the pullback of the
filtration subgroup by scalar extension. Given stability, the
descent conclusion follows from the Library's `unramifiedSmoothModelDescent`
and `integralPoints_eq_comap_baseChangeUnramified`.

`minimalCongruentGaloisModule` restricts actual coefficient
conjugation to a stable filtration subgroup. It gives the
discrete additive coefficient module over `ULift ℤ`.
The stabilizer of each point contains the open subgroup fixing
the field generated by its finitely many affine coordinates.
`MinimalCongruentCohomologyStatement` asserts that every
positive-degree continuous Galois cohomology group of this
module is trivial. The admissible depths are every positive
depth and also depth zero when `cdSup Γ ≤ 1`. The source assumes
completeness of the base field, which holds for `F`. The residue
field `f` is perfect, so the source's `dim(f) ≤ 1` is equivalent
to the cohomological dimension bound on `Gal(f^sep/f)`, which
identifies with `Γ`. The bound holds for the finite residue
field of a nonarchimedean local field.
