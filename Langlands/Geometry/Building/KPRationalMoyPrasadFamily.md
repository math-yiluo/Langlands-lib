# Centralizer filtrations from a rational Moy–Prasad family (KP §9.8)

Module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.KPRationalMoyPrasadFamily`.

## Purpose and provenance

KP §9.8 (Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach*, printed pp. 374–376,
PDF pp. 405–407) constructs the filtration of `Z(F)`, `Z = Z_G(S)`, as
`Z(F)_r = Z(K)_{x,r} ∩ Z(F)`, where `K` is the maximal unramified extension of `F`.
Lemma 9.8.1 proves that it is commutator friendly. Proposition 9.8.3(1), (2) and its proof
identify `G(F)_{x,f} = G(K)_{x,F} ∩ G(F)` and `U_a(F) ∩ G(K)_{x,F} = U_{a,x,f}`. Here
`U_{a,x,f}` is the block `U_{a,x,f(a)} · U_{2a,x,f(2a)}` of §9.8.2 (as in Definition
7.3.3(1)), not the bare root filtration `U_{a,x,f(a)}`. All of these
arguments use the `K`-level Moy–Prasad groups `G(K)_{y,r}` only through a few of their
properties, after intersecting with `G(F)`.

This module isolates that `F`-level part of the argument. The predicate
`IsRationalMoyPrasadFamily` is **derived** (an internal interface, not a definition from the
book): it lists the properties of the family `Q y r = G(K)_{y,r} ∩ G(F)` that the
argument uses. The module does **not** construct such a family and does not prove that
`G(K)_{y,r} ∩ G(F)` has these properties. That is the `K`-level input of Task 5647
(389efdd6-dfb3-478d-b92e-a38a722094e1), split into the definition task D1 (Task 5875,
079e96f3-eb4c-4477-8a40-a1acffc66e49) and the statement tasks S1–S3 of that task's
decomposition.

## The definition

`ParahoricBuildingData.IsRationalMoyPrasadFamily P Q` is a `Prop`-valued structure, for
`P : ParahoricBuildingData G` and a family
`Q : P.Apartment → NNReal → Subgroup (rationalPoints G)` of subgroups of `G(F)` indexed by
the points `y` of the apartment `A(S)` and by `r ≥ 0`. Write `j : Z(F) → G(F)` for
`rationalPoints.map P.centralizer.j`. Each field below gives the KP fact about
`G(K)_{y,r} ∩ G(F)` it records.

| Field | Lean content | KP source of the property for `G(K)_{y,r} ∩ G(F)` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `antitone` | `r ↦ Q y r` is antitone | the groups of Definition 7.3.3 decrease in `f` |
| `filtration_le` | `U_{b,y,s} ≤ Q y s` for every relative root `b` | §9.8.2 and Proposition 9.8.3(2): `G(F)_{y,s} = G(K)_{y,s} ∩ G(F)` is generated by the blocks `U_{a,y,s} U_{2a,y,s}` and `Z(F)_s`, and each `U_{b,y,s}` lies in a block (as its `a`-factor for `a = b`, or its `2a`-factor when `b = 2a`) |
| `commutator_le` | `⁅Q y r, Q y s⁆ ≤ Q y (r + s)` | commutator property of `G(K)_{y,•}`, used in the proof of Lemma 9.8.1 (via Lemma 7.1.2 and V 3 over `K`) |
| `conj_mem` | `n g n⁻¹ ∈ Q (n • y) r` for `g ∈ Q y r`, `n ∈ N(F)` | `N(F)`-equivariance of the construction of §7.3 |
| `map_mem_of_mul_mem` | for `t > 0`, `v' ∈ U_{-a}(F)`, `v ∈ U_a(F)`: `v' j(z) v ∈ Q y t → j(z) ∈ Q y t` | proof of Lemma 9.8.1: the `Z`-component of an element of `G(K)_{x,t}` lies in `G(K)_{x,t} ∩ Z(K)` |
| `comap_eq` | `j⁻¹(Q y r) = j⁻¹(Q y' r)` | Lemma 8.5.19: `G(K)_{y,r} ∩ Z(K) = Z(K)_{x,r}`, the same for every `y ∈ A(S)` |
| `comap_zero` | `j⁻¹(Q y 0) = P.centralizerZero` | first paragraph of the proof of Lemma 9.8.1: `Z(F)_0 = Z(F)^0` |
| `iInf_comap` | `⨅ r, j⁻¹(Q y r) = ⊥` | separation of the filtration `Z(K)_{x,r}` |
| `mem_filtrationBlock` | `g ∈ U_a(F)`, `g ∈ Q y t` ⇒ `g ∈ U_{a,y,t} U_{2a,y,t}` (`P.apartment.filtrationBlock` at the constant `t`) | proof of Proposition 9.8.3(1), with §9.8.2: `U_a(F) ∩ G(K)_{y,t} = U_{a,y,t} U_{2a,y,t}` (the block of §9.8.2 at the constant function `t`) |
| `isOpen` | every `j⁻¹(Q y r)` is open in `Z(F)` for the point topology | `Z(K)_{x,r}` is open, so `Z(F)_r` is open |

`mem_filtrationBlock` records one inclusion of KP's equality
`U_a(F) ∩ G(K)_{y,t} = U_{a,y,t} U_{2a,y,t}`: the inclusion of the left side in the right.
In KP §9.8.2 and Definition 7.3.3(1), `U_{a,y,f} = U_{a,y,f(a)} U_{2a,y,f(2a)}`. At the
constant function `t` this is the block `U_{a,y,t} U_{2a,y,t}`, which is the Library's
`P.apartment.filtrationBlock y (fun _ => t) a`. For a multipliable root `a` the block can be
strictly larger than the bare root filtration `U_{a,y,t}` (KP Remark 7.3.4). By V 4,
`y_a(u) = y_{2a}(u)/2` for `u ∈ U_{2a}(F)`, so an element of `U_{2a}(F)` with
`y_{2a}(u) = t > 0` lies in the block but not in `U_{a,y,t}`. The doubled-root factor
therefore cannot be dropped from this field. In
`map_mem_of_mul_mem` the depth is strictly positive, which is all the friendliness proofs
use.

## Construction

- `IsRationalMoyPrasadFamily.centralizerFiltration hQ x₀` is the
  `P.CentralizerFiltration` with `filtration r = j⁻¹(Q x₀ r)`, the analogue of
  `Z(F)_r = Z(K)_{x,r} ∩ Z(F)`. Its fields come from `comap_zero` (`filtration_zero`),
  `antitone` and `iInf_comap`.
- `mem_filtration_iff`: `z ∈ Z(F)_r ↔ j(z) ∈ Q y r` for **every** point `y` (by `comap_eq`).

## Results and their proofs

Two auxiliary results about the apartment, which do not mention `Q`:

- `exists_vadd_val_root hP x a c`: under `P.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms`, for a relative root
  `a` and `c ∈ ℝ` there is a point `y` with `y_a = x_a + c` and `y_{-a} = x_{-a} - c`.
  Proof: `KPApartmentData.exists_integralRootCoroot` gives `v ∈ V(S)` with `a(v) = 2`.
  Take `y = [(c/2) • v] +ᵥ x`. Then `KPApartmentData.val_mk_vadd` and
  `RootFunctionFamily.vadd_apply` give `y_b = x_b + b((c/2) • v)`, and
  `eval_neg_char` gives `(-a)((c/2) • v) = -c`.
- `exists_real_vals_of_add_eq`: if `r = x_a(u) + x_{-a}(u')` in `WithTop ℝ` with `r` real,
  both values are real numbers `p`, `q` with `p + q = r`. Moreover `-a` is a relative root,
  since otherwise `x_{-a} ≡ ⊤` (`eq_top_of_not_mem_roots`).

The filtration properties, in the namespace `IsRationalMoyPrasadFamily`, for `hQ` and a
base point `x₀`:

- `commutator_mem`: `u' ∈ Q y r`, `u ∈ Q y s` ⇒ `u'⁻¹ u u' u⁻¹ = ⁅u'⁻¹, u⁆ ∈ Q y (r + s)`,
  from `commutator_le`.
- `isCommutatorFriendly hP x₀` (KP Definition 7.2.1, the clause `IsCommutatorFriendly`).
  Let `p = x_a(u)`, `q = x_{-a}(u')` with `p + q = r > 0`, and let
  `u'⁻¹ u u' u⁻¹ = v' j(z) v`. Translate `x` by `c = -p` to a point `y` with
  `y_a(u) = 0` and `y_{-a}(u') = r`. Then `u ∈ U_{a,y,0} ≤ Q y 0` and
  `u' ∈ U_{-a,y,r} ≤ Q y r`, so `u'⁻¹ u u' u⁻¹ ∈ Q y r` by `commutator_mem`.
  `map_mem_of_mul_mem` gives `j(z) ∈ Q y r`, and `mem_filtration_iff` gives
  `z ∈ Z(F)_r`.
- `isDoubleRootCommutatorFriendly hP x₀`, the clause `IsDoubleRootCommutatorFriendly` of
  `KPMoyPrasadDoubleRootStatement`, with target `z ∈ Z(F)_{2r}`. There are two cases.
  - If `u ∈ U_{2a}(F)`: since `u ≠ 1` and `doubleRootGroupPoints` is `⊥` when `2a` is not a
    root, `2a` is a root and `u ∈ U_{2a}(F)`. Translate by `c = q` to `y` with
    `y_a(u) = r` and `y_{-a}(u') = 0`. Axiom V 4 (`IsRootDatumValuation.double`) gives
    `y_{2a}(u) = 2 y_a(u) = 2r`, so `u ∈ U_{2a,y,2r} ≤ Q y (2r)` and
    `u' ∈ U_{-a,y,0} ≤ Q y 0`. Hence `u'⁻¹ u u' u⁻¹ ∈ Q y (2r)`.
  - If `u' ∈ U_{-2a}(F)`: symmetrically `2(-a)` is a root. Translate by `c = -p` to `y`
    with `y_a(u) = 0` and `y_{-a}(u') = r`. V 4 for `-a` gives `y_{2(-a)}(u') = 2r`, so
    `u' ∈ Q y (2r)`, `u ∈ Q y 0` and `u'⁻¹ u u' u⁻¹ ∈ Q y (2r)`.

  In both cases `map_mem_of_mul_mem` at `t = 2r > 0` and `mem_filtration_iff` conclude.
- `conj_image_le` and `isNormalizerStable x₀`: for `n ∈ N(F)` and `z ∈ Z(F)_r`,
  `n j(z) n⁻¹` centralizes `S` (`conj_mem_centralizerPoints`,
  `map_mem_centralizerPoints`), so it equals `j(z')` for some `z'`
  (`torusCentralizer_carrier_base` and `P.centralizer.isRepresentedBy`). Then
  `j(z') ∈ Q (n • x₀) r` by `conj_mem`, so `z' ∈ Z(F)_r` by `mem_filtration_iff`. This
  gives `n j(Z(F)_r) n⁻¹ ≤ j(Z(F)_r)`; the same inclusion for `n⁻¹` gives equality.
- `isCommutatorFiltered x₀`: `j⁅z, w⁆ = ⁅j z, j w⁆` (`map_commutatorElement`) and
  `commutator_le` at `x₀`.
- `isRootCompatible x₀`: for `z ∈ Z(F)_r` and `u ∈ U_{a,x,s}`, `j(z) ∈ Q x r` and
  `u ∈ Q x s`, so `j(z) u j(z)⁻¹ u⁻¹ ∈ Q x (r + s)`. It lies in `U_a(F)` because
  `j(z)` normalizes `U_a(F)` with trivial Weyl action (`conj_mem_rootGroupPoints`,
  `weylAction_centralizerToNormalizer`). Then `mem_filtrationBlock` at `t = r + s`.
- `satisfiesFiltrationAxioms hP x₀` bundles the four clauses, and `isOpenFiltration x₀` is
  `isOpen` at `x₀`.

The main theorem:

```lean
theorem exists_doubleRootFriendly_filtration_of_rationalMoyPrasadFamily
    (hP : P.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms) {Q : P.Apartment → NNReal → Subgroup (rationalPoints G)}
    (hQ : P.IsRationalMoyPrasadFamily Q) :
    ∃ Zf : P.CentralizerFiltration, Zf.SatisfiesFiltrationAxioms ∧ Zf.IsOpenFiltration ∧
      Zf.IsDoubleRootCommutatorFriendly
```

It uses the base point `x₀ = Classical.arbitrary P.Apartment`. Its conclusion is the
conclusion of `CentralizerFiltrationDoubleRootExistenceStatement` for the given `P`. The
existence of a family `Q` with `P.IsRationalMoyPrasadFamily Q` is not proved here.
