# Unramified descent of the minimal congruent Moy–Prasad groups

`MinimalCongruentMoyPrasadDescentStatement` is KP Remark 13.2.3 and
the group comparison of Proposition 9.8.3(2), with the minimal
congruent torus filtration. It uses a nonarchimedean local field `F`
of either characteristic, `K = UnramifiedClosure F = inertiaFixedField F`,
an affine group scheme `G` and `P : ParahoricBuildingData G` satisfying
`P.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms`. The argument `h` supplies decreasingness
and separation for the constructed centralizer family, so the existing
`CentralizerFiltration.moyPrasad` can be applied.

The statement ranges over every special torus containing the
prescribed `F`-split torus and every `ApartmentLift` for it. At a point
`x` of `P.Apartment`, the lift supplies the upstairs valuation at `e(x)`.
For a finite real `r ≥ 0`, write `Q` for
`L.buildingData.minimalCongruentMoyPrasad L.centralizer_isTorus (L.e x) r`.
Its torus term is the minimal congruent subgroup of the centralizer of
the maximal `K`-split torus of `G_K`.

The two clauses assert

1. `Q` is invariant under each `rationalConjugate F K G σ`, for
   `σ : K ≃ₐ[F] K`.
2. The image under `rationalExtend F K G` of
   `P.minimalCongruentMoyPrasad h x r` equals `Q ⊓ fixed`.
   Here `fixed` pulls the Library's subgroup `GaloisPoint.fixedSubgroup`
   back along `baseChangeCoeffEquiv`. Its elements are precisely the
   points of the actual base change fixed by all coefficient
   automorphisms.

Thus the equality is \(P_{x,r} = (P^K_{x,r})^{\operatorname{Gal}(K/F)}\)
under the inclusion of rational points, with \(x\) represented over
\(K\) by its specified lift. Stability is stated so that the ambient
Galois action restricts to `Q`. The actual rational-point comparison
over a Galois extension identifies the invariant ambient points with
the image of extension. KP's group comparison then concerns the
subgroups generated by the two sets of roots and the chosen torus
terms. The proposition states the group assertion and leaves the
integral-model comparison of Proposition 9.8.3(2) to its own formulation.
