This proves the published proposition
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.ValuationBuildingData.SharpFixerBoundednessStatement`
(module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.SharpFixerBoundednessStatement`) exactly, at
arbitrary universe `u`. Let `F : Type u` be a nonarchimedean local field of either
characteristic, `G` a group scheme over `Spec F` with affine structure morphism, and
`B : ValuationBuildingData G` actual building data. If
`B.apartment.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms B.centralizer` holds (the corrected apartment
premise, including AS 5), then for every `x : B.Apartment` the subgroup
`B.sharpFixer {x}` of `G(F)`, generated by the actual root fixers, is algebraically
bounded: `IsAlgebraicallyBounded F G.X (B.sharpFixer {x} : Set (rationalPoints G))`,
which bounds every actual global regular function on `G`. The theorem has no
hypothesis besides the data `B`; the apartment premise is the antecedent of the
statement itself.

## How the structural inputs give the bound

This summarizes the published reduction that the term above applies; it is not
re-proved here.

- *Bounded central part.* `ActualCentralizerStructureStatement` makes the actual
  centralizer `Z = B.centralizer.Z` connected reductive. Its torus factor
  `B.centralizer.s` is central and a maximal split torus of `Z`
  (`centralizer_isCentral_maximalSplit`). With `CentralQuotientReductiveStatement` and
  `AnisotropicCompactnessStatement`, the published
  `BruhatTits.RationalPoints.isCompact_rationalCommonNormKernel_of_central_maximalSplit`
  makes `Z(F)^1 = rationalCommonNormKernel B.centralizer.Z` compact in the canonical
  topology of `Z(F)` (`isCompact_centralizerNormKernel`). Its image under the closed
  inclusion `j = B.centralizer.j` is therefore algebraically bounded in `G(F)`
  (`isAlgebraicallyBounded_image_centralizerNormKernel_of_isCompact`, module
  `SharpFixerBoundednessAssembly`).
- *The KP 7.3.12 cover.* `RootRankOneFactorizationStatement`, applied to the actual
  maximal split torus `B.i`, supplies opposite factor pairs, the elements `m(u)` of
  Kaletha–Prasad Proposition 2.11.17. With these,
  `sharpFixer_subset_mul_sharpNormalizerStabilizer` (module `PointSharpExchange`) gives
  `G(F)^♯_x ⊆ U⁻_x · U⁺_x · (G(F)^♯_x ⊓ N(F)_x)` for every generic cocharacter, under
  the apartment axioms. Here `U^±_x` are the subgroups generated by the zero-depth
  root groups of the roots positive or negative on that cocharacter.
- *Assembly.* `sharpFixerBoundedness_of_cover` (module `SharpFixerBoundednessAssembly`)
  picks a generic cocharacter. It bounds `U^±_x` as compact finite ordered
  chamber-block products, which keep their doubled-root factors. It bounds
  `G(F)^♯_x ⊓ N(F)_x` inside the norm-one point stabilizer `N(F)^1_x`, a finite union
  of translates of `j(Z(F)^1)` (module `NormalizerNormOneFixerBoundedness`). Products
  and subsets of bounded sets are bounded.

## Independence and scope

Every module imported here is a published Library module, and no premise asserting
sharp-fixer boundedness, bounded norm-one point stabilizers,
`HasBoundedOpenStabilizers`, or an Iwahori or Bruhat factorization is used. The full
Langlands import closure of this module was checked. No module in it mentions
`CentralizerParahoricIntersectionStatement`, and none imports a centralizer-parahoric
or chamber-Iwahori reduction or assembly module. Only the statement-only module
`ChamberSharpStatement`, which defines propositions, is imported, through
`ChamberFiltration`. The target Lean/Markdown pair and every pair it imports are
unchanged.

Only the singleton zero-depth case of the corollary is proved, not the full
Corollary 7.3.14 for arbitrary concave functions or bounded subsets `Ω`. Boundedness
does not identify the sharp fixer's pullback to `Z(F)` with `Z(F)^0`; with
`comap_sharpFixer_le_centralizerNormKernel` it gives membership in the actual
`Z(F)^1` only.

## Provenance

The theorem is the singleton sharp-subgroup consequence of Tasho Kaletha and Gopal
Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits theory: a new approach* (2023),
`kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`, SHA-256
`05ad9a284a2577b34d1a270228b74dfb28f520561ae7772d8f38dddb78938732`:
Corollary 7.3.14, printed pp.251–252 / PDF pp.282–283, applied to the generated
subgroup of Definition 7.4.1(2), printed p.255 / PDF p.286. The AS 5 premise comes
from the corrections of November 1, 2024, `kaletha-prasad-2024-corrections`, SHA-256
`23293f51e58d5ceecb91f834743f68058b98431797d2531142415d013fdf61ed`, PDF p.33.

KP derive the corollary from Proposition 7.3.12 (printed pp.250–251 /
PDF pp.281–282), from boundedness of the root groups, and from boundedness of
`N(k)^♯_x`, whose image in the affine Weyl group fixes `x`. The Library route follows
this outline with two published deviations. The normalizer factor is bounded through
the larger norm-one stabilizer `N(F)^1_x` and the finite relative Weyl group
`N(F)/Z(F)`. The central bound uses compactness of `Z(F)^1` from the anisotropic
compactness criterion, the locally compact consequence of KP Theorem 2.2.9 (printed
p.71 / PDF p.102), rather than `Z(k)_0`.
