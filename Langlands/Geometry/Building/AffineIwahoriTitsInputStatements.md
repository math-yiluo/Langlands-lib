# The remaining inputs of the actual affine Iwahori–Tits system

This module states five propositions about the actual data of
`P : ParahoricBuildingData G` under the apartment axioms. They are the
inputs of `AffineIwahoriTitsInputs` (module `AffineIwahoriTitsAssembly`) that
no published result supplies. Each proposition is stated and is **not**
proved here. `AffineIwahoriTitsInputStatement` is their conjunction.

The module then proves the two remaining inputs, and it derives the published
`AffineIwahoriTitsStatement` from the five statements, the published
`AffineRankOneTwoCellStatement` and the published
`IwahoriWeylExactSequenceStatement`. These three statements are unproved, so
`AffineIwahoriTitsStatement` remains unproved. Worker 1565 is the author.

## Notation

Throughout, `P : ParahoricBuildingData G` and the apartment is `A = P.Apartment`.

- `P_Ω = P.parahoric Ω`, the published `G(F)^0_Ω = Z(F)^0 · G(F)^♯_Ω`
  (KP Definition 7.4.1(4)), and `P_x = P.parahoricAt x = P.parahoric {x}`.
- `G(F)^0 = P.zeroSubgroup` (KP Definition 2.6.23(3)).
- `N(F) = normalizerPoints P.i`, the `F`-points of the normalizer of the
  split torus `S`, as a subgroup of `G(F)`.
- `Z(F)^0` is `P.centralizerZero` mapped into `G(F)` by
  `rationalPoints.map P.centralizer.j`. `P.normalizerZero` is the same
  subgroup viewed inside `N(F)`.
- `U_ψ = P.apartment.affineRootGroup α ψ`, the actual affine root group of the
  relative root `α` and the affine functional `ψ`.
- `U_a(F) = rootGroupPoints P.i α`, and
  `U_{a,x,0} = P.apartment.filtration x α 0` (KP Definition 6.1.2).
- `n • C` is the image of `C ⊆ A` under the apartment action of
  `n ∈ N(F)`.

Every statement quantifies over every nonarchimedean local field
`F : Type u`, every group scheme `G` over `Spec F` with `[IsAffineHom G.X.hom]`,
and every `P : ParahoricBuildingData G` with
`P.apartment.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms P.centralizer`. This is the hypothesis
of the published `AffineIwahoriTitsStatement`. The universe `u` is arbitrary.
Statements 1, 2, 3 and 5 also take a chamber `C`, i.e.
`AffineRootSystem.IsChamber P.affineRoots C`.

## The statements

KP's Theorem 7.5.1 builds the Tits system `(G, I, N, R)` for simply
connected `G`, with `I = G(k)^0_C`, `N = N(k)` and `Z = Z(k)^0`. KP then
transfer it to `G(k)^0`, with `N = N(k) ∩ G(k)^0`, by Lemma 7.5.2 and
Theorem 7.5.3. Here each step of the proof of Theorem 7.5.1 is stated
directly for `G(F)^0`, with `I = P_C`. The step is then a consequence of the
transferred Tits system of Theorem 7.5.3(1) or of Lemma 7.5.2. The
provenance of each statement is therefore *derived*.

1. **`ChamberParahoricNormalizerStatement`**: every `n : normalizerPoints P.i`
   with `n ∈ P_C` lies in `P.normalizerZero`.

   This is `I ∩ N = Z` of KP Lemma 7.5.2(1), which KP derive from
   Corollary 7.4.9 (`N^♯_x = Z^♯_x` for `x ∈ C`). The statement quantifies
   over all of `N(F)`, not only `N(F) ∩ G(F)^0`. These give the same
   statement, because `P_C ⊆ G(F)^0` (`P.parahoric_le_zeroSubgroup`, from
   `ApartmentRootCover`).

2. **`ChamberParahoricGenerationStatement`**:
   `G(F)^0 ≤ P_C ⊔ (N(F) ⊓ G(F)^0)`.

   This is the generation axiom of the Tits system `(G(k)^0, I, N, R)` of
   KP Theorem 7.5.3(1). In KP's proof of Theorem 7.5.1 the step is
   "`U_a(k) = ⋃_{t ∈ Z(k)} t U_{a,C,0} t⁻¹`, so the subgroup generated by `I`
   and `Z(k) ⊂ N` contains every `U_a(k)`". KP transfer it to `G(k)^0` in
   the proof of Theorem 7.5.3, by Lemma 1.4.12 and Lemma 7.5.2.

3. **`ChamberSimpleFactorizationStatement`**: let `ψ` be a chamber-simple
   affine root of `C` (`AffineRootSystem.simpleAffineRoots P.affineRoots C`),
   `α` a relative root with `ψ.linear = rootForm P.i α`, and `v` a coroot of
   `ψ` in `Ψ = P.affineRoots`. Write `r = AffineRootSystem.affineReflection ψ hv.1`,
   i.e. `z ↦ z - ψ(z) v`. Then every `b ∈ P_C` is a product `b = b' u` with
   `b' ∈ P_{r C}` and `u ∈ U_ψ`.

   This is KP (7.5.1), `s I ⊆ I s U_α`, multiplied on the left by `s⁻¹`:
   it gives `I ⊆ (s⁻¹ I s) U_α`. If `s ∈ N` realizes `r`, then
   `s⁻¹ I s = P_{r⁻¹ C} = P_{r C}`, as `r` is an involution. The Lean
   statement avoids the representative `s` and uses `P_{r C}` directly.

   KP's argument uses the decomposition
   `I = ∏_{β ∈ Ψ' - {α}} U_β · Z · U_α` (Corollaries 7.4.9 and 6.3.11).
   KP's corrections (PDF p. 35) replace `ψ` by `α ∈ Ψ'` in this paragraph.
   The library's chamber-simple roots are non-divisible
   (`simpleAffineRoots` requires `nonDivisibleRoots`). This matters when `ψ`
   has derivative `2a` for a root `a`, so that `U_ψ = U_{2a,ψ}`. Then
   `ψ/2 ∉ Ψ`, and the definition of `Ψ` gives
   `U_{a,ψ/2} ⊆ U_{a,ψ/2+} · U_{2a,ψ}`. Informally, the first factor lies in
   `P_{r C}`, because the next jump of `U_a` above `ψ/2` is positive on
   `r C`. So the statement also holds in this case.

4. **`RootGroupParahoricFiltrationStatement`**: for every relative root `α`
   and every `x ∈ A`, `U_a(F) ∩ P_x ≤ U_{a,x,0}`.

   KP's proof of Lemma 7.4.13(1) states "`U_a(k) ∩ P_z = U_{a,z,0}` by
   Proposition 7.3.12". The proof of Theorem 7.5.1 uses the same
   proposition in the form `U_a(k) ∩ s I s = U_{α+}`. Only the inclusion
   `⊆` is stated, since the assembly uses only this inclusion.

5. **`ChamberConjugateIntersectionStatement`**: let `g ∈ G(F)` lie in
   `P_{n C}` for every `n : normalizerPoints P.i` with `n ∈ G(F)^0`. Then `g`
   lies in `Z(F)^0`.

   This is the saturation equality `⋂_{n ∈ N} n I n⁻¹ = Z` with
   `N = N(F) ∩ G(F)^0`. It holds because the Tits system of Theorem 7.5.3(1)
   is saturated and `I ∩ N = Z` (Lemma 7.5.2(1)). KP's proof of
   Theorem 7.5.1 says "the argument used above to prove `I ∩ s I s ≠ I` can
   be iterated". Their corrections (PDF p. 35) replace `⋂_{n ∈ N} I` by
   `⋂_{n ∈ N} n I n⁻¹`. The Lean statement writes `n I n⁻¹` as `P_{n C}`.
   The two agree once `Z(F)^0` is normal in `N(F)` (published
   `map_parahoric_conj`), but the statement does not need that hypothesis.

`AffineIwahoriTitsInputStatement` is the conjunction, in this order, of
statements 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 at universe `u`. The library already bundles
statements in this way: `IwahoriWeylBijectionsStatement` is a conjunction.

## Proved results

- `ParahoricBuildingData.closure_chamberSimpleReflections_affineRoots`: under
  the apartment axioms, the chamber-simple reflections of a chamber generate
  `W(Ψ)` (KP Proposition 1.3.22(1)). The proof applies the published
  `AffineRootSystem.closure_chamberSimpleReflections_eq_top_of_wall_generation`
  to the published `affineChamberWallComparison` and
  `affineReflectionGalleryGeometry`. Both are used for
  `Ψ = P.affineRoots`, which is an affine root system by the published
  `isAffineRootSystem_affineRoots`.
- `ParahoricBuildingData.affineIwahoriTitsInputs_of_statements`: the five
  statements and `AffineRankOneTwoCellStatement` give
  `P.AffineIwahoriTitsInputs C` for every chamber `C`. Input 1 is the
  published `P.parahoric_le_zeroSubgroup`, input 4 is the theorem above,
  input 5 is the two-cell statement. The other five fields are the five
  statements.
- `ParahoricBuildingData.affineIwahoriTits_of_statements`: with
  `[P.normalizerZero.Normal]` and a comparison `e` satisfying
  `P.IsAffineWeylComparison e`, they give `P.AffineIwahoriTits C`, by the
  published `affineIwahoriTits_of_inputs`.
- `affineIwahoriTitsStatement_of_inputStatements`: the five statements,
  `AffineRankOneTwoCellStatement` (KP Lemma 7.1.5(3)) and
  `IwahoriWeylExactSequenceStatement` (KP Theorem 7.8.1(1)) imply
  `AffineIwahoriTitsStatement`. The exact-sequence statement supplies both
  normalities required by the target and the comparison `e`.
- `affineIwahoriTitsStatement_of_inputStatement`: the same implication from
  the conjunction `AffineIwahoriTitsInputStatement`.

The exact-sequence statement does not depend on a Tits system. The published
`iwahoriWeylExactSequenceStatement_of_prerequisites` reduces it to four
statements: KP Lemma 2.6.24, Fact 2.6.22, Corollary 2.6.28, and
`SimplyConnectedNormalizerAffineWeylStatement`. The last says that
`N(F) ∩ G(F)^♮` acts on the apartment with image `W(Ψ)`. None of the four
mentions a Tits system, so this use is not circular.
