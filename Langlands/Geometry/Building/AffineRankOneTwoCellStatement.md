# The filtered rank-one two-cell decomposition

`Langlands.Geometry.Building.AffineRankOneTwoCellStatement` states
Kaletha–Prasad Lemma 7.1.5(3) for the actual data of
`P : ParahoricBuildingData G`. It is the rank-one input that KP use in the
proof of Theorem 7.5.1 to build the affine Tits system. The proposition
is stated here and is not proved. Worker 1565 is the author.

## Definitions

Let `α : relativeRoots P.i` be a relative root and `ψ : P.Apartment →ᵃ[ℝ] ℝ`
an affine functional.

- `P.affineRankOneSubgroup α ψ` is the subgroup of `G(F)` generated by
  three subgroups, written as a supremum:
  - `U_ψ = P.apartment.affineRootGroup α ψ`;
  - `U_{-ψ} = P.apartment.affineRootGroup (-α) (-ψ)`, the actual affine root
    group of the character `-α` and the functional `-ψ`;
  - `Z(F)^0`, i.e. `P.centralizerZero` mapped into `G(F)` by
    `rationalPoints.map P.centralizer.j`.
- `P.affineRankOneBaseCell α ψ` is KP's
  `L₁ = U_{-ψ+} · Z(F)^0 · U_ψ`, a pointwise set product. Here
  `U_{-ψ+} = P.apartment.affineRootGroupPlus (-α) (-ψ)`.
- `P.affineRankOneReflectionCell α ψ m` is KP's
  `L₂ = U_ψ · {m} · Z(F)^0 · U_ψ` for a given `m ∈ G(F)`.

`U_ψ` is the published `affineRootGroup`. For `ψ` with derivative `a`, this
equals `U_{a,x,ψ(x)}` at every point `x` (`affineRootGroup_eq_filtration`).
This is KP's group `U_{a,x,j}` for `ψ(z) = a(z - x) + j`.

## The statement

`AffineRankOneTwoCellStatement.{u}` quantifies over every nonarchimedean
local field `F : Type u`, every group scheme `G` over `Spec F` with
`[IsAffineHom G.X.hom]`, and every `P : ParahoricBuildingData G`. Assume
`P.apartment.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms P.centralizer`. Let `α` be a relative
root and `ψ` an affine functional with `ψ.linear = rootForm P.i α`, and assume
`U_{ψ+} ≠ U_ψ` for the actual affine root groups of `α` and `ψ`. Then there is
`m : normalizerPoints P.i` such that

1. `m` lies in `P.affineRankOneSubgroup α ψ`;
2. for every `v` with `AffineRootSystem.IsCoroot P.affineRoots ψ v`, the action
   `P.apartment.normalizerAffineEquiv m` equals the reflection
   `AffineRootSystem.affineReflection ψ hv.1`, i.e. `z ↦ z - ψ(z) v`;
3. as a set, `P.affineRankOneSubgroup α ψ` equals
   `P.affineRankOneBaseCell α ψ ∪ P.affineRankOneReflectionCell α ψ m`.

## Formalization choices

- **KP's data.** KP fix `x ∈ A` and `j ∈ ℝ`, and use `U_{a,x,j}` and
  `U_{-a,x,-j}`. The statement instead takes the affine functional `ψ`; KP's
  proof uses `ψ(z) = a(z - x) + j`, and then `-ψ` has derivative `-a` and
  `U_{-ψ} = U_{-a,x,-j}`. KP's hypothesis `U_{a,x,j+} ⊊ U_{a,x,j}` is
  `U_{ψ+} ≠ U_ψ`, since `affineRootGroupPlus_le` gives the containment.
- **"m normalizes S".** This is the type `normalizerPoints P.i` of
  `F`-points of the normalizer.
- **"The reflection along ψ = 0".** An affine involution fixing the
  hyperplane `ψ = 0` pointwise has the form `z ↦ z - ψ(z) v` with
  `ψ.linear v = 2`, so the reflection must choose `v`. The statement uses the
  coroot vectors of `ψ` for the actual affine root system `Ψ = P.affineRoots`,
  in the sense of AR 2 (`IsCoroot`). Under the apartment axioms the clause is
  neither vacuous nor contradictory:
  - `ParahoricBuildingData.exists_isCoroot_affineRoots_of_mem` proves that a
    coroot vector exists. The jump puts `ψ` in `Ψ′`
    (`mem_extendedAffineRoots_of_ne`). By AS 4, either `ψ ∈ Ψ`, or `2ψ ∈ Ψ`
    and twice a coroot of `2ψ` is a coroot of `ψ`.
  - The published `IsAffineRootSystemWithRankZero.isCoroot_unique` holds for
    an arbitrary functional `ψ`, so the AS 3 axiom
    `isAffineRootSystem_affineRoots` makes the coroot vector unique.
- **Premise.** The only hypothesis on `P` is
  `SatisfiesApartmentAxioms`, the hypothesis of the published
  `AffineIwahoriTitsStatement`. No normality, root-reflection or
  decomposition axiom is assumed. KP's own proof uses Proposition 6.2.1 for
  `m` and Lemma 7.1.4 and Lemma 7.1.5(1) for the right stability of `L₁ ∪ L₂`.
  A proof here must derive these facts for the actual data.
- **Universe.** The statement is at an arbitrary universe `u`.

## Proved supporting results

The module also proves these elementary facts:

- each of `U_ψ`, `U_{-ψ}`, `U_{-ψ+}` and `Z(F)^0` lies in
  `P.affineRankOneSubgroup α ψ`;
- `1 ∈ L₁`, `L₁ ⊆ L`, and `L₂ ⊆ L` whenever `m ∈ L`;
- `affineRankOneSubgroup_eq_closure`: `L` is the subgroup closure of the union
  of the three generating subgroups;
- `affineRankOneSubgroup_subset_of_mul_mem`: a set containing `1` and closed
  under right multiplication by elements of `U_ψ`, `U_{-ψ}` and `Z(F)^0`
  contains `L`. The proof uses `Subgroup.closure_induction_right`;
- `coe_affineRankOneSubgroup_eq_of_mul_mem`: if `m ∈ L` and `L₁ ∪ L₂` is
  closed under right multiplication by the three generating subgroups, then
  `L = L₁ ∪ L₂`. This is the reduction in KP's proof; the closure property
  itself is not proved;
- `mem_extendedAffineRoots_of_ne` and `exists_isCoroot_affineRoots_of_mem`,
  described above.

KP reduce right stability to `U_ψ` and `m`, using `m U_ψ m⁻¹ = U_{-ψ}`. The
supporting lemma instead lists all three generating subgroups. That is the
general form, and it does not assume the conjugation identity.
