# Positive chamber products across a simple affine wall

The module works in arbitrary universe `u` over a field `k : Type u`, with
a commutative group scheme `S`, an affine group scheme `G`, a monomorphic
inclusion `i : S.toGrp ⟶ G`, a function `ω : kˣ → ℝ`, and the existing actual
apartment datum `D : KPApartmentData i ω`. All groups below are the existing
subgroups of `rationalPoints G`.

The hypotheses are:

- `S.toGrp` is a split torus and `relativeRoots i` is finite.
- Both `D.affineRoots` and `D.extendedAffineRoots` satisfy
  `IsAffineRootSystemWithRankZero`.
- The derivative roots of `D.extendedAffineRoots` are exactly
  `Set.range (rootForm i)`.
- AS4 holds: every extended affine root is an affine root or its double
  is an affine root.
- `ψ` belongs to the existing
  `simpleAffineRoots D.affineRoots C`, `α : relativeRoots i`,
  and `ψ.linear = rootForm i α`.
- A cocharacter `ν` has `0 < latticePairing S α ν`, a point `x` lies
  in `C`, and `v` is a coroot of `ψ` for `D.affineRoots`.

Simple-root membership includes the chamber hypothesis. Write `s` for
the actual affine reflection `affineReflection ψ hv.1`. The theorem
`positiveChamberRootGroups_subset_reflection_mul_wall` proves

`(⨆ b : positiveRelativeRoots i ν, D.chamberRootGroup x false b) ⊆`
`D.sharpFixer (s '' C) · D.affineRootGroup α ψ`,

as an inclusion of subsets of `rationalPoints G`. The selector `false`
means depth zero, so the left group is generated by the actual groups
`U_{b,x,0}` for all roots positive on `ν`. Genericity of `ν` is not needed
for this result, nor is a reducedness assumption.

The entry `positiveChamberWallProductInclusion` is the function from the
left subgroup to the subtype of the displayed product set that retains the
underlying rational point. It packages the theorem's inclusion; it does
not return a chosen pair of factors, assert their uniqueness, or assert
that the product set is a subgroup.

The proof first applies the existing
`exists_indivisiblePositiveRoot_eq_or_double` to the positive wall
derivative. This gives a positive indivisible root `a₀` such that
`α = a₀` or `α = 2a₀`. The published `rootForm_nsmul` calculation therefore
expresses `ψ.linear` as a real multiple of `rootForm i a₀`.

Finiteness supplies an enumeration of all positive indivisible roots
except `a₀`. Appending `a₀` by `Fin.lastCases` gives a complete enumeration.
The existing arbitrary-order collection theorem
`range_orderedIndivisiblePositiveRootGroupProduct_eq_iSup` writes any
element of the positive generated subgroup as the ordered product in
this enumeration. No new ordered-product hypothesis is assumed.

Facet constancy identifies each depth-zero point factor with the
corresponding chamber root fixer. Every prefix direction is a positive
indivisible root different from `a₀`. The root-containment module's
`rootFixer_eq_reflection_of_indivisible_ne` identifies its chamber fixer
with the fixer over `s(C)`, which lies in the reflected sharp fixer.
Subgroup closure puts the entire prefix product there.

The last factor lies in `rootFixer a₀ C`. The existing
`rootFixer_subset_reflection_mul_wall_of_eq_or_double` splits it as an
element of `rootFixer a₀ (s '' C)` followed by an element of
`affineRootGroup α ψ`, covering both the primitive and doubled derivative
cases. The first factor also lies in the reflected sharp fixer.
`List.ofFn_succ'` separates the ordered product into its prefix and last
factor, and associativity gives the desired inclusion. This also covers
the case of an empty prefix.

This is derived content from the imported chamber root-containment and
positive collection results; the latter's definitions and proofs are
already in the Library. No new external-source assertion, rank-one
factorization premise, or Dynamic ordered-product premise is introduced.
The preceding wall modules are bundled unchanged while their publication
is pending. The full generated-parahoric factorization and the unchanged
apartment-only `AffineIwahoriTitsStatement` remain open.
