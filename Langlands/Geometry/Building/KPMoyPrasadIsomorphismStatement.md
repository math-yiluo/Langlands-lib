# KP Theorem 13.5.1 (1): the Moy–Prasad isomorphism on supplied data

Author: Worker 3557.

This module belongs to the Kaletha–Prasad Bruhat–Tits program (task
`f33d727f-04b8-4fd2-9e9f-2f5c4eb2a703`). It states part (1) of KP Theorem
13.5.1, without the clause on unramified extensions, for the Library's
supplied building data. For `0 < r ≤ s` satisfying one of KP's three
alternatives, the statement asserts
`P_{x,r} / P_{x,s} ≅ 𝔤_{x,r} / 𝔤_{x,s}` as abstract abelian groups. It
assumes the matching isomorphisms for the centralizer `Z` and for each root
block, and the double-root commutator condition on the supplied filtration
of `Z(F)`. It is a partial result on supplied data, not KP's theorem for
KP's filtrations. The module has three auxiliary definitions and one
statement. It proves nothing.

## Setting

The setting is that of `Langlands.Geometry.Building.KPMoyPrasadFiltration`
and `Langlands.Geometry.Building.KPLieFiltration`. `F` is a nonarchimedean
local field with ring of integers `𝒪[F]`. `G` is a group scheme over
`Spec F` with `[IsAffineHom G.X.hom]`. `P : ParahoricBuildingData G` is the
Library's building data, with maximal split torus `P.i : S → G`, centralizer
`P.centralizer.j : Z → G` and apartment `P.Apartment`. The other supplied
objects are:

- `Zf : P.CentralizerFiltration`, the supplied filtration `Z(F)_r`
  (`Zf.filtration r`). Its Moy–Prasad subgroup `P_{x,r}` is
  `Zf.moyPrasad x r` (KP Definition 13.2.1).
  `Zf.IsDoubleRootCommutatorFriendly` (module
  `KPMoyPrasadDoubleRootStatement`) is the published sharpened commutator
  condition: when one of the root-group elements `u ∈ U_a(F)`,
  `u' ∈ U_{-a}(F)` lies in a double-root group and `r = x_a(u) + x_{-a}(u')`,
  the `Z`-component of `u'⁻¹ u u' u⁻¹` lies in `Z(F)_{2r}`.
- `L : P.LieFiltrationData`, the supplied lattices `z_r ⊆ Lie(Z)`
  (`L.centralizerLie r`) and `u_ψ` for affine roots `ψ`. From these,
  `L.lieFiltration x r` is `𝔤_{x,r}`, the `𝒪`-span of `z_r` and of the `u_ψ`
  with `ψ(x) ≥ r` (KP 13.3.3), and `L.rootLie x α r` is the root part
  `u_{α,x,r}`.

Lie algebras are `GenericLie 𝒪[F] H`: the Lie algebra of `H` over `F`,
viewed as an `𝒪[F]`-module.

## The definitions

### `HasMoyPrasadQuotientIso H₁ H₂ g₁ g₂`

Let `H₁ H₂ : Subgroup Γ` be subgroups of a group `Γ`, and let
`g₁ g₂ : AddSubgroup M` be additive subgroups of an abelian group `M`. The
proposition says that some monoid homomorphism

```
φ : H₁ →* Multiplicative (g₁ ⧸ g₂.addSubgroupOf g₁)
```

is surjective and has kernel `H₂.subgroupOf H₁`, that is, `H₂ ∩ H₁` viewed
inside `H₁`. When `H₂ ≤ H₁` and `g₂ ≤ g₁`, the first isomorphism theorem
makes this equivalent to an isomorphism of abstract groups
`H₁ / H₂ ≅ g₁ / g₂`. In particular `H₁ / H₂` is then abelian. This form needs
no `Normal` instance for `H₂`.

### `MoyPrasadAlternative F G r s`

For `r s : NNReal`, this is the disjunction of the three conditions of KP
§13.5:

1. `HasWeaklyInducedMaximallySplitTorus F G ∧ s ≤ 2 * r`. The first clause
   is the Library's (module `KPWeaklyInducedTorus`): some maximally split
   maximal torus of `G_K`, `K = F^ur`, is weakly induced (KP Definition
   B.6.2).
2. There are `r₀ : NNReal` and `n : ℕ` with `r₀ < 1`, `0 < n`,
   `r = r₀ + n` and `s ≤ r₀ + 2 * n`.
3. `s ≤ 2 * r ∧ 2 * r ≤ r + 1`.

KP's standing condition `0 < r ≤ s` is not part of this definition. The
statement assumes it separately.

### `LieFiltrationData.rootBlockLie L x α r`

For a relative root `α`, this is the `𝒪`-submodule

```
L.rootLie x α r ⊔ ⨆ (β : relativeRoots P.i) (_ : β.1 = 2 • α.1), L.rootLie x β r
```

of `𝔤`: it is `u_{α,x,r} + u_{2α,x,r}` when `2α` is a relative root, and
`u_{α,x,r}` otherwise. It is the Lie counterpart of the root block
`U_{α,x,r} U_{2α,x,r}`, the Library's
`P.apartment.filtrationBlock x (fun _ => r) α` (KP Definition 7.3.3(1) at
the constant function `r`).

### `MoyPrasadIsomorphismStatement`

For every `F`, `G`, `P`, `Zf` and `L` as above, assume

- `P.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms`, `Zf.SatisfiesFiltrationAxioms` and
  `Zf.IsDoubleRootCommutatorFriendly`;
- a point `x : P.Apartment` and `r s : NNReal` with `0 < r`, `r ≤ s` and
  `MoyPrasadAlternative F G r s`;
- **the hypothesis on `Z`**: `HasMoyPrasadQuotientIso (Zf.filtration r)
  (Zf.filtration s) (L.centralizerLie r).toAddSubgroup
  (L.centralizerLie s).toAddSubgroup`, i.e. `Z(F)_r / Z(F)_s ≅ z_r / z_s`;
- **the hypothesis on root blocks**: for every relative root `α` that is not
  of the form `2 • β` for a relative root `β`,
  `HasMoyPrasadQuotientIso` holds for the subgroups generated by the blocks
  `P.apartment.filtrationBlock x (fun _ => r) α` and
  `P.apartment.filtrationBlock x (fun _ => s) α`, and for
  `(L.rootBlockLie x α r).toAddSubgroup` and
  `(L.rootBlockLie x α s).toAddSubgroup`. That is,
  `U_{α,x,r} U_{2α,x,r} / U_{α,x,s} U_{2α,x,s} ≅
  (u_{α,x,r} + u_{2α,x,r}) / (u_{α,x,s} + u_{2α,x,s})`.

Then `HasMoyPrasadQuotientIso (Zf.moyPrasad x r) (Zf.moyPrasad x s)
(L.lieFiltration x r).toAddSubgroup (L.lieFiltration x s).toAddSubgroup`,
i.e. `P_{x,r} / P_{x,s} ≅ 𝔤_{x,r} / 𝔤_{x,s}`.

The blocks enter through `Subgroup.closure`. Under the apartment axioms each
block is already a subgroup (Library
`ParahoricBuildingData.filtrationBlockSubgroup`), so the
closure does not enlarge it. The closure only lets the statement avoid that
proof term.

## Derived formulation

**Supplied filtrations and the two hypotheses.** KP's theorem is about their
own filtrations: the minimal congruent filtration of tori (Definition
B.10.8) and the Lie algebras of the integral models of §8.5 and 13.3. The
Library has neither. `Zf` and `L` are supplied data, and nothing ties
`Z(F)_r` to `z_r` or the root groups to the `u_ψ`. For arbitrary supplied
data the isomorphism can fail, for instance when `z_r = 0` for all `r` while
`Z(F)_r / Z(F)_s` is nontrivial. The statement therefore assumes exactly the
component isomorphisms that KP's proof gets from those constructions: for
`Z` (from B.6.9 or B.10.17 over `K`) and for each root block (from C.5.1
and A.5.19). KP's proof then assembles them via Proposition 13.2.5(3),(4).
The hypotheses are posed directly over `F`. KP construct the components
over `K` and descend; the statement does not repeat that step.

**The double-root commutator premise.** KP's assembly uses that
`P_{x,r} / P_{x,s}` is abelian, which follows from
`[P_{x,r}, P_{x,r}] ⊆ P_{x,2r} ⊆ P_{x,s}` (Proposition 13.2.5(4)) once
`s ≤ 2r`. For supplied filtrations satisfying only
`SatisfiesFiltrationAxioms` this inclusion can fail, and so can the
statement without the premise. The ramified quasi-split `SU_3` over `ℚ_2`
with a small supplied filtration of `Z(F)` satisfies all the other
hypotheses at `r = 1/2`, `s = 1` (alternative (3)), yet has a commutator of
two elements of `P_{x,1/2}` outside `P_{x,1}`. This was found in review of
an earlier version of this module. The Library note
`notes/bruhat-tits/moy-prasad-supplied-filtration-counterexample.md` treats
the same group with a related filtration of `Z(F)`, contained in the one
used in review. It shows that `IsDoubleRootCommutatorFriendly` fails for
that filtration. The statement therefore assumes
`Zf.IsDoubleRootCommutatorFriendly`. Under it and the apartment and
filtration axioms, the Library proves
`[P_{x,r}, P_{x,s}] ⊆ P_{x,r+s}` (`moyPrasadDoubleRootCommutator`, module
`KPMoyPrasadDoubleRootCommutatorProof`).

**The alternatives enter only through `s ≤ 2r`.** Each of the three
alternatives implies `s ≤ 2r`: in (2), `s ≤ r₀ + 2n ≤ 2r₀ + 2n = 2r`. With
the components and the commutator premise assumed, the assembly needs only
this inequality. The statement keeps KP's three alternatives anyway. They
are part of KP's theorem, and they are the ranges where KP prove the
hypothesis on `Z` for the minimal congruent filtration: B.6.9 with B.10.13
for (1), B.10.16 for (2), and B.10.17 for (3). Under (2), KP's own proof
does not go through the components. It applies A.5.19 to `𝒢_{x,r₀}`
directly. A proof of this statement may use any route.

**How the hypotheses combine (not proved here).** Fix a positive system and
orders of the non-divisible roots of each sign. The intended argument has
four steps.

1. By `moyPrasadDoubleRootCommutator`, the Moy–Prasad filtration being
   decreasing, and `s ≤ 2r`, `[P_{x,r}, P_{x,r}] ⊆ P_{x,s}`. Hence
   `P_{x,s}` is normal in `P_{x,r}` and `A = P_{x,r} / P_{x,s}` is abelian.
2. `Z(F)_r` (through `Z → G`) and each block `U_{a,x,r} U_{2a,x,r}` are
   subgroups of `P_{x,r}`, so each maps homomorphically to `A`. Since `A` is abelian,
   multiplying the images in the fixed order is a homomorphism
   `Z(F)_r × ∏_a U_{a,x,r} U_{2a,x,r} → A`. It is surjective because the
   ordered product is onto `P_{x,r}` (KP 13.2.5(3); Library
   `MoyPrasadProductStatement`, proved in `KPMoyPrasadProductProof`).
3. Its kernel is `Z(F)_s × ∏_a U_{a,x,s} U_{2a,x,s}`. If an ordered product
   at depth `r` lies in `P_{x,s}`, it is also an ordered product at depth
   `s`. The components at depth `s` lie in those at depth `r`, and the
   ordered product at depth `r` is injective. So the two factorizations
   agree. Hence `A` is the product of the quotients `Z(F)_r / Z(F)_s` and
   `U_{a,x,r} U_{2a,x,r} / U_{a,x,s} U_{2a,x,s}`.
4. On the Lie side, `𝔤_{x,r}` is the sum of the image of `z_r` and of the
   blocks `u_{a,x,r} + u_{2a,x,r}`. These summands lie in the image of
   `Lie(Z)`, the weight-zero space of `S`, and in the sums `𝔤_a ⊕ 𝔤_{2a}`
   of weight spaces. For distinct non-divisible `a` these spaces form a
   direct sum. The map
   `Lie(Z) → Lie(G)` is injective. So `𝔤_{x,r} / 𝔤_{x,s}` is the direct
   sum of `z_r / z_s` and the block quotients. The hypotheses on `Z` and on
   the root blocks identify the factors, and the product of these
   isomorphisms is the conclusion.

Without the commutator premise, steps 2 and 3 still give a bijection of
sets, but step 1 fails and the map need not be a homomorphism. The
counterexample above shows that the conclusion can then fail.

**Non-divisible roots and blocks.** KP's products run over the
non-divisible roots `Φ_nd`. For such `a`, KP's factor `U_{a,x,r}` is the
block of Definition 7.3.3(1), which also contains `U_{2a,x,r}`. The
statement uses the Library's block `filtrationBlock` and its Lie
counterpart `rootBlockLie` for every relative root `α` with no `β` such
that `α = 2β`. When `2α` is not a root, the doubled factor is trivial on
both sides.

**`Z` rather than `T`.** Over `K`, `Z = T` is a maximal torus. Over `F`, the
centralizer `Z` of the maximal split torus need not be a torus. The
hypothesis on `Z` is stated for `Z(F)_r` and `z_r ⊆ Lie(Z)`.

## Not included

- The clause "compatible with unramified algebraic extensions of `k`" of
  part (1). It would need building data, filtrations and Lie lattices over
  the extension, with comparison maps. The Library compares Moy–Prasad
  groups with `K = F^ur`
  (`UnramifiedRationalMoyPrasadStatement`), but it has no counterpart for
  Lie lattices.
- Part (2), equivariance under `F`-rational automorphisms of `G`, and
  part (3), agreement of the two constructions. Part (3) speaks about
  particular isomorphisms built in KP's proofs. It cannot be stated without
  defining them.
- The minimal congruent filtration, the integral models `𝒢_{x,r}`,
  `𝒰_{a,x,r}`, and Propositions A.5.19, B.6.9, B.10.16, B.10.17 and C.5.1.
