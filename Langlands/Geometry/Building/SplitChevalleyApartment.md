# Normalizer-stable apartment classes of split reductive groups

Author: Worker 4783.

Lean module: `Langlands.Geometry.Building.SplitChevalleyApartment`.

This unit constructs, for every split maximal torus of every connected reductive
group over every field, an actual `KPApartmentData`: an equipollence class of
valuations of the root datum (axioms V 0–V 5), compatible with a prescribed
valuation on the split torus, and stable under the whole actual normalizer.
It is the split case of the apartment of Kaletha–Prasad, and an input for the
quasi-split apartment of task 8ffe0456.

## Main results

- **`nonempty_kpApartmentData_of_isSplitTorus`.** Let `k : Type u` be a field,
  `G` an affine group scheme over `Spec k` with `IsConnectedReductive G`, `T` a
  commutative group scheme with `IsSplitTorus T.toGrp`, and `j : T.toGrp ⟶ G` a
  monomorphism with `IsMaximalTorus j`. Let `omega : AddValuation k (WithTop ℝ)`
  have at least three values, and let `ω : kˣ → ℝ` agree with `omega` on units.
  Then `Nonempty (KPApartmentData j ω)`.
- **`KPApartmentData.ofSplitMaximalTorus`** chooses such data.
- **`ChevalleyRootFamily.apartmentData`.** For a Chevalley group `G : D.ChevGroup k`
  of the split existence construction, the translation orbit of the published
  Chevalley root family `ChevalleyRootFamily.family G omega` is normalizer-stable
  apartment data for the torus `G.t`.

No universe, characteristic, residue-field, completeness or rank restriction is
made. The valuation `omega` may be any additive valuation with at least three
values; in particular it need not be discrete or normalized.

## Generic lemmas

These hold for an arbitrary monomorphism `i : S.toGrp ⟶ G` into an affine group.

- `RootFunctionFamily.normalizer_smul_eq_vadd`: let `ψ` take the value `⊤` off
  the actual relative roots and off each actual root group. If a normalizer point
  `n` satisfies `ψ_{n⁻¹a}(n⁻¹ g n) = ψ_a(g) + a(w)` for every actual root `a`
  and every `g ∈ U_a(k)`, then `n • ψ = w +ᵥ ψ`. The proof uses the published
  `RelativeRoot.normalizer_conj_mem_iff` and `weylAction_mapsTo_relativeRoots`
  for the points and characters where both sides are `⊤`. It generalizes the
  published centralizer case `RootFunctionFamily.centralizer_smul_eq_vadd`.
- `RootFunctionFamily.shiftSubgroup i φ`: the normalizer points `n` with
  `n • φ = v +ᵥ φ` for some `v`. It is a subgroup because the normalizer action
  and the translation action fuse (`RootFunctionFamily.smul_vadd`, KP 6.1.8).
- `RootFunctionFamily.shiftSubgroup_eq_top`: if this subgroup contains the whole
  centralizer, if the kernel of the Weyl action is the centralizer, and if the
  actual Weyl image `weylImage i` lies in the image of the subgroup, then the
  subgroup is everything. For `n` choose `h` in the subgroup with the same Weyl
  action; then `h⁻¹ n` lies in the centralizer.
- `KPApartmentData.ofNormalizerShifts`: a compatible valuation all of whose
  normalizer transforms are translates gives `KPApartmentData`, with carrier its
  translation orbit.
- The published `Langlands.BruhatTits.Cartan.exists_eval_eq` supplies that on a
  split torus every additive real function of the characters is evaluation at a
  vector of `V(T)`.

## The Chevalley group

Let `G : D.ChevGroup k` be a Chevalley group for Chevalley data of a reduced
root datum `P` with finite index type and finite free weight lattice.

- `exists_weylPoint_inv_conj_rootPt` inverts the published conjugation formula
  `exists_weylPoint_conj_rootPt`: `n_i⁻¹ u_j(s) n_i = u_{s_i j}(ε s)` with a
  sign `ε`.
- `weylNormalizer_smul_family`: every simple Weyl point fixes the Chevalley
  root family. On a root group the formula above gives
  `omega(ε s) = omega(s)`; the generic lemma handles all other values.
- `centralizer_mem_shiftSubgroup`: a centralizer point is the image of a
  rational torus point `τ` (published `exists_eq_of_mem_centralizerPoints`).
  The published split-torus compatibility `isSplitTorusCompatible` shifts the
  value on the root group of `a` by `-ω(a(τ))`. This is additive in `a`, so
  `Langlands.BruhatTits.Cartan.exists_eval_eq` gives the translation vector.
- `exists_shift_actsByReflection`: every root `α` and its negative have
  reflection elements in the translation subgroup. This repeats the induction of
  the published `hasReflectionElement_all` (simple roots, conjugation by simple
  Weyl points, negation), keeping track of subgroup membership.
- `weylImage_le_map_shiftSubgroup`: by the published `weylGroup`
  (`WeylGroupStatement`) applied to `chevDatum k P`, the actual Weyl image is the
  image of the abstract Weyl group, which is generated by reflections. Each
  generator is the Weyl action of a reflection element of the previous step,
  by `chevDatum_reflection_charOf`.
- `exists_normalizer_smul_family_eq_vadd` combines these with the kernel
  equality of `WeylGroupStatement`. `apartmentData` then applies
  `KPApartmentData.ofNormalizerShifts` to the published
  `isRootDatumValuation` and `isSplitTorusCompatible`.

## Transport to an arbitrary split group

`nonempty_kpApartmentData_of_isSplitTorus` takes the root datum `P` of `j`
from the published `rootDatumExistence`, a base from `nonempty_base_int`,
Chevalley data from `nonempty_chevalleyLieData`, and a Chevalley group `Gc`
from `nonempty_chevGroup`. The torus isomorphism `t : T ≅ D(X^*(T))` is
`IsSplitTorus.isoOfCharacterEquiv` of the published `charEquiv`, so its
character map is `charEquiv`. The published `IsRootDatumOf.precomp_torusIso`
makes `P` a root datum of `t ≫ Gc.t`, and `IsMaximalTorus.precomp_torusIso`
keeps maximality. The published Isomorphism Theorem
`splitCommonTorusIsomorphism` then gives `ψ : G ≅ Gc.H` with
`j ≫ ψ = t ≫ Gc.t`. The published `KPApartmentData.transport` carries the
Chevalley apartment along `t⁻¹` and `ψ⁻¹`.

## Provenance

This is derived content. Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach*
(2023), source `kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`, §6.1(a), printed
pp. 196–204 (PDF pp. 227–235): Lemma 6.1.1 (the Chevalley collection is a
valuation of the root datum, compatible with `ω`), Fact 6.1.10 (`N_ad(k)` maps
Chevalley valuations to Chevalley valuations) and Lemmas 6.1.12–6.1.13 (torus
elements act by translations; all Chevalley valuations are equipollent), which
together give an `N(k)`-stable equipollence class (Definition 6.1.15). The
Lean proof does not compare pinnings. It shows directly that simple Weyl points
fix the family, that torus points translate it, and that these generate the
normalizer modulo the centralizer. The 2024 corrections (source
`kaletha-prasad-2024-corrections`, PDF p. 32) change the sign convention in
Lemma 6.1.12; no sign of that lemma enters this proof.

## Scope

The unit treats split maximal tori only. It does not construct Galois-stable
classes, quasi-split or general apartments, full centralizer compatibility for
non-split tori, V 7, or any building. All results are uniform in the universe
and the characteristic.
