# Root-filtration calculations for a concrete unramified apartment lift

This module defines the positive restriction fibre for a concrete unramified
apartment lift and proves the helper calculations for five fields of
`IsUnramifiedRootComparison`. It is derived from the published apartment-lift,
torus restriction and relative root-negation results. Worker 188 supplied the
initial calculations; Worker 510 prepared this helper unit for publication.

`ApartmentLift.filtration_eq_restrictedDepth` specializes the published
`rootFiltration_torusFieldRestrictedRootFunctionFamily` to the actual
special-torus factorization and the restriction identity of the lift. The upper
subgroup is generated by root filtrations at depths `m * r`, where `m` is the
positive integral restriction multiplier. Equality is asserted for every real
depth, without changing that downstairs depth. The proof extends the
factorization under base change, rewrites the supplied downstairs valuation by
`ApartmentLift.restrict`, and applies the published exact-preimage theorem.

The provenance is the published `RootValueTorusFieldRestriction` theorem and its
paired Markdown, applied to the data of `KPUnramifiedApartmentLift`. This is a
formal specialization, adding no claim about KP beyond those modules.

`rootFiber` selects the upper roots restricting to positive integral multiples
of the given lower character. `fieldFactorization` is the functorial image of
the supplied torus factorization. `root_mem_rootFiber` extends a lower root
point and uses the published
`RelativeRoot.subgroup_comp_torusHom_carrier_eq_iSup` to place it in the span of
this fibre. `filtration_mem_rootFiber` uses the exact-preimage identity; every
generator occurs at depth `m * s`, which is at least `s` when `m` is positive
and `s` is nonnegative. Filtration antitonicity then places it in the required
upper depth span. These results give two of the seven root-comparison fields
for this choice of root set.

`rootFiber_closed` proves closure under positive root combinations by adding
the positive integral restriction multipliers. `eval_rootFiber` uses the
published `eval_descentCocharacterMap` and
`eval_splitCocharacterBaseChange`: a root with restriction multiplier `m`
evaluates on a transported lower vector as `m` times the lower evaluation.
Consequently `rootFiber_pos` gives positivity on the entire fibre, and
`neg_rootFiber_subset_negativePairingRoots` puts the opposite fibre in the
negative half-space of the same vector.

`neg_mem_negativePairingRoots` applies `root_mem_rootFiber` to the opposite
lower root, whose root membership is supplied by the published
`relativeRootNegation`, then uses monotonicity of the generated subgroup.
`exists_root_positive_vector` follows from the published
`IsSplitTorus.exists_pairing_eq_pos` by embedding the resulting integral
cocharacter in its real scalar extension. Thus a positive lower vector exists,
and its transport supplies the `pos` and `neg_mem` fields for `rootFiber`.

Together these lemmas provide `closed`, `pos`, `root_mem`, `neg_mem`, and
`filtration_mem` of `IsUnramifiedRootComparison`. They do not prove its
`levi_mem` or `block_mem` fields, the complete root-comparison proposition,
or the existence of a comparison. In particular, the exact filtration-preimage
identity at scaled depths does not assert the required implication to the
downstairs doubled-root filtration block at the original depth.
