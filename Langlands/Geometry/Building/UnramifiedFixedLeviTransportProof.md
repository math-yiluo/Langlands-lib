# Levi transport of Galois-fixed points of the unramified apartment

The theorem `Langlands.Geometry.Building.unramifiedFixedLeviTransport.{u}`
proves exactly the published proposition
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.UnramifiedFixedLeviTransportStatement.{u}`.
The universe is arbitrary and all hypotheses are unchanged.

Write \(K=\operatorname{inertiaFixedField}(F)\) for the maximal unramified
extension of the nonarchimedean local field \(F\). For the statement's connected
reductive group \(G\), maximal \(F\)-split torus \(S\), containing torus \(T\)
with maximal split base change \(T_K\), and apartment data with full V6
compatibility, take \(n\in N_G(S)(F)\) and a coefficient-fixed apartment point
\(x\). The theorem gives \(h\in G(K)\) centralizing \(S_K\) and belonging to the
subgroup generated by `D.filtration x χ 0` over the relative roots with
`f_K ≫ χ = 0`. The product \(n_Kh\) normalizes \(T_K\), and its apartment action
on \(x\) is fixed by every coefficient automorphism of \(K/F\).

## Implemented proof

The Lean term applies
`unramifiedFixedLeviTransport_of_fixedApartment_and_leviSharp`, from
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.UnramifiedFixedLeviTransportReduction`, to two
published unconditional proofs at the same universe:

1. `unramifiedTorusUnitFixedApartment`, from
   `Langlands.Geometry.Building.UnramifiedTorusUnitFixedApartmentProof`;
2. `unramifiedLeviSharpApartmentTransport`, from
   `Langlands.Geometry.Building.UnramifiedLeviSharpApartmentTransportProof`.

The reduction first uses `unramifiedNormalizerFixedApartmentLift`. Full V6
gives fixation of the original chart point by the scalar-extended split-torus
unit kernel. Rational normalizer conjugacy preserves this property, and
coefficient semilinearity makes the normalizer translate coefficient-fixed.
The first input therefore supplies a coefficient-fixed apartment point \(y\)
whose chart is \(n_K\) applied to the chart of \(x\).

The second input supplies the required \(h\) from that chart membership.
Each depth-zero root group in its Levi subgroup lies in the sharp fixer of
\(x\). Consequently \(h\) fixes the chart of \(x\), so the chart of
\((n_Kh)\cdot x\) equals the chart of \(y\). The reduction's
`KPApartmentData.normalizer_mul_smul_eq_of_mem_sharpFixer` uses apartment
injectivity to identify the apartment points themselves. Substitution then
gives the required coefficient fixedness.

## Provenance

This module is a derived composition of the three published results named
above. The fixed-apartment input implements Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits
Theory: A New Approach* (2023), Proposition 9.3.13, with the split-translation
argument corrected in the authors' *Corrections and additions* of 1 November
2024, PDF pp.39–41; its paired Markdown records the source fingerprints and
the underlying published inputs.

The Levi input follows the algebraic proof in
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.LeviSharpTransport`, using an adapted
cocharacter and a sharp-fixer decomposition over the infinite field \(K\).
Its paired Markdown explains how this replaces the Levi apartment
transitivity route sketched in the target's Markdown. Both prerequisites
are discharged by the imported theorems.
