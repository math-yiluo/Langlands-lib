# Actual parahoric double cosets from a Tits system in `G(F)^0`

This internal adapter feeds the published conditional component constructions with a Tits
system stated in the form that Kaletha–Prasad Theorem 7.5.3 gives it. The ambient group is
`G(F)^0 = P.zeroSubgroup`, and the Weyl group is the actual affine part
`P.affinePart = (N(F) ∩ G(F)^0)/Z(F)^0` of the Iwahori–Weyl group. From such a system it
derives the fixed-data conclusions `P.IwahoriDoubleCosetBijection C` and
`P.ParahoricDoubleCosetBijection F₁ F₂` of Theorem 7.8.1(2),(3). It does not prove the
global statements `IwahoriDoubleCosetStatement` or `ParahoricDoubleCosetStatement`. No
Tits system, containment, surjectivity or facet comparison is proved here; each one is a
named hypothesis of the declarations below.

The objects are those of the existing `P : ParahoricBuildingData G` over a nonarchimedean
local field `F` in an arbitrary universe `u`. The normalizer is `normalizerPoints P.i`, the
full generated parahorics are `P.parahoric Ω`, and the Iwahori–Weyl quotient map is
`QuotientGroup.mk' P.normalizerZero`. The declarations also use `P.toComponents` and the
setwise affine facet stabilizers `P.affineFacetStabilizer Fᵢ`. Throughout,
`P.normalizerZero` and `P.zeroSubgroup` are assumed normal (instance arguments). These
normalities are conclusions of KP Theorem 7.8.1(1), recorded as
`IwahoriWeylExactSequence`, and are not proved here.

## Generic transport

`Langlands.Group.TitsSystem.isTitsSystem_kernelProjection_of_eq` is pure group theory.
Fix compatible homomorphisms `π : N →* W`, `q : G →* Γ` and `c : W →* Γ`, with
`c (π n) = q n`. Let `K` be a subgroup equal to `q.ker` and `W₀` a subgroup equal to
`c.ker`. Let `π₀ : N.subgroupOf K →* W₀` agree with `π` on underlying elements. If
`(B.subgroupOf K, N.subgroupOf K, π₀, S₀)` is a Tits system, then the published
`kernelProjection` for the same data is a Tits system over `q.ker`. Its generating set is
`c.ker.subtype ⁻¹' (W₀.subtype '' S₀)`, the same elements of `W` as `S₀`.

Proof: substitute both subgroup equalities. The compatibility hypothesis then shows that
`kernelProjection` equals `π₀`, by extensionality. The preimage of the image under an
injective subtype map is the original set.

## Actual data

The hypotheses below are shared by all of the actual-data declarations.

- `hI : P.parahoric C ≤ P.zeroSubgroup` is the containment of the full Iwahori `P_C` in
  `G(F)^0`.
- `π₀ : (normalizerPoints P.i).subgroupOf P.zeroSubgroup →* P.affinePart` is a projection,
  and `hπ₀` says that it agrees with `QuotientGroup.mk' P.normalizerZero` on the underlying
  normalizer element of every `n ∈ N(F) ∩ G(F)^0`. Any restriction of the actual quotient
  map satisfies this.
- `hTits` says that
  `((P.parahoric C).subgroupOf P.zeroSubgroup, (normalizerPoints P.i).subgroupOf P.zeroSubgroup, π₀, S)`
  is a `Langlands.Group.IsTitsSystem` for some `S : Set P.affinePart`. This is the shape of
  the `G(F)^0` branch of KP Theorem 7.5.3(1),(2).
- `hsurj` says that `P.toComponents` restricted to `P.chamberStabilizer C` is surjective.

`chamberStabilizer_toComponents_surjective_of_splitting` extracts `hsurj` from the KP 7.8.2
conclusion `P.IwahoriWeylChamberSplitting C`. Its bijectivity component is stated for the
same instances up to proof irrelevance.

`isTitsSystem_kernel_of_zeroSubgroup` applies the generic transport with
`q = QuotientGroup.mk' P.zeroSubgroup` and `c = P.toComponents`. The compatibility of `q`
and `c` is `toComponents_mk`. The two subgroup equalities are `QuotientGroup.ker_mk'` and
the published `toComponents_ker_eq_affinePart`. The result is exactly the kernel Tits-system
hypothesis of the published `iwahoriDoubleCosetEquivOfKernelTits`.
`parahoric_le_ker_mk'_zeroSubgroup` rewrites `hI` into its kernel form in the same way.

### Iwahori double cosets

`iwahoriDoubleCosetEquivOfZeroTits` is the published conditional equivalence
`P.IwahoriWeylGroup ≃ P_C \ G(F) / P_C`, applied to these transported inputs.
`iwahoriDoubleCosetEquivOfZeroTits_apply` states that it sends the class of every actual
`n : normalizerPoints P.i` to `P_C n P_C`. `iwahoriDoubleCosetBijection_of_zeroTits`
packages the equivalence and this formula as `P.IwahoriDoubleCosetBijection C`.

### Parahoric double cosets

Let `X, Y ⊆ S`, and let `F₁, F₂` be subsets of the apartment. The facet hypotheses are the
two parts of KP Proposition 7.5.5, one for each `Fᵢ`:

- `hV₁ : P.affineFacetStabilizer F₁ = (Subgroup.closure X).map P.affinePart.subtype`, and
  similarly `hV₂` with `Y`. These say that the existing setwise affine facet stabilizer, in
  the full `P.IwahoriWeylGroup`, is the image of the subgroup generated by `X`.
- `hP₁` says that the full generated parahoric, as a subset of `G(F)`, is the union of the
  Iwahori Bruhat cells indexed by the generated subgroup:
  `(P.parahoric F₁ : Set (rationalPoints G)) = standardParabolic (P.parahoric C) (QuotientGroup.mk' P.normalizerZero) (P.affinePart.subtype '' X)`.
  `hP₂` is the same with `Y`. The cells use the full normalizer and the actual quotient map.

`parahoricDoubleCosetEquivOfZeroTits` applies the published
`subgroupParabolicDoubleCosetEquiv` with:

- `K = P.zeroSubgroup` and `W₀ = P.affinePart`;
- the Iwahori equivalence above and its formula on all normalizer representatives;
- the actual Tits system `hTits`, the compatibility `hπ₀` and the containment `hI`;
- the normalizing representatives `exists_normalizing_zeroSubgroup_rep C hsurj`;
- surjectivity of `QuotientGroup.mk' P.normalizerZero`.

The representative input needs no rewriting, since it is stated with membership in
`P.zeroSubgroup`. The result is an equivalence

`W^aff_{F₁} \ W̃^0 / W^aff_{F₂} ≃ P_{F₁} \ G(F) / P_{F₂}`

between the double-coset quotients of the full `P.IwahoriWeylGroup` and of the full
rational-point group. `parahoricDoubleCosetEquivOfZeroTits_apply` states that it sends the
double coset of the class of every actual `n : normalizerPoints P.i` to `P_{F₁} n P_{F₂}`.
`parahoricDoubleCosetBijection_of_zeroTits` packages the equivalence, the normality
instance of `P.normalizerZero` and this formula as `P.ParahoricDoubleCosetBijection F₁ F₂`.

Task 4ace6b2b allows the parabolic comparison to be stated inside `G(F)^0`, so the module
also accepts that form:

- `hPKᵢ : P.parahoric Fᵢ ≤ P.zeroSubgroup`, and
- `((P.parahoric Fᵢ).subgroupOf P.zeroSubgroup : Set P.zeroSubgroup) = standardParabolic ((P.parahoric C).subgroupOf P.zeroSubgroup) π₀ X`
  (with `Y` for `F₂`).

`coe_parahoric_eq_standardParabolic_of_zeroSubgroup` converts this comparison into the
full-group comparison. It uses the published `coe_eq_standardParabolic_of_subgroup` with
the same Iwahori equivalence and Tits system. `parahoricDoubleCosetBijection_of_zeroTits_subgroupOf`
then gives `P.ParahoricDoubleCosetBijection F₁ F₂` from the subgroup-form comparisons.

## Provenance and scope

The provenance is derived. The Tits-system shape follows the `G(F)^0` branch of
Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023), Theorem 7.5.3(1),(2) and
Definition 7.5.4 (printed page 261, PDF page 292). In that branch the Weyl group is
identified with the affine Weyl group through the action of `N(F)`. The facet hypotheses are
the two assertions of Proposition 7.5.5 (PDF pages 292–293): `W^aff_F` is the stabilizer of
`F` in `W_aff`, and `G(F)^0_F = G(F)^0_C · W^aff_F · G(F)^0_C`. The conclusions are those of
Theorem 7.8.1(2),(3) (PDF pages 303–304). Chamber component surjectivity is part of §7.8.2
(PDF page 304). The imported component construction follows the corrected injectivity
argument of the authors' corrections of 1 November 2024, PDF page 35. The generic transport
lemma and the subgroup-to-full-group packaging are internal adapters.

To close the unchanged global targets, all of the following must still be derived from
`P.apartment.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms P.centralizer` and the chamber and common-chamber
face premises:

- the two normalities;
- the containment `hI`;
- an actual Tits system `hTits` with a compatible projection;
- the chamber component surjectivity;
- for both facets, the stabilizer equalities `hVᵢ` and the parabolic comparisons, with
  `X, Y` contained in the Tits generators `S`.

None of them is assumed by either global statement, and this module does not change those
statements. Task 4ace6b2b-7c6d-45a9-ae17-260afc919615 is to specify the geometric
chamber-simple reflections and these proof targets.
