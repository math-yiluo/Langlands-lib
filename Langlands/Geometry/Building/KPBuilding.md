# The reduced building, sharp fixers and compact stabilizers

This module belongs to the task on actual Kaletha–Prasad affine-root,
special-point, parahoric and compact-stabilizer objects. It continues the
module `KPApartment`. Given valid apartment data `D : KPApartmentData i ω`, it
defines the sharp fixer groups, the reduced building, fixers of points, special
points of the building, and over a local field the compact stabilizers
`K_x = G(F)^1 ∩ G(F)_x`.

## Setting

As in `KPApartment`, `i : S.toGrp ⟶ G` is a homomorphism of group schemes over
a field `k`, with `[Mono i.hom.hom.left]` and `[IsAffineHom G.X.hom]`. The
intended case is a maximal `k`-split torus of a connected reductive group.
`D : KPApartmentData i ω` is valid apartment data: an `N(k)`-stable,
`ω`-compatible equipollence class `A` of valuations of the root datum.
`D.Apartment` is its affine space of points.

- `G(k)` is the published `rationalPoints G`.
- `N(k)` and `Z(k)` are the published `normalizerPoints i` and
  `centralizerPoints i`.
- `U_a(k)` is `rootGroupPoints i a`, the rational points of the actual root
  subgroup functor.
- `n·a` is the published `weylAction i n a`.

## The conjugation law for the actual root groups

- `conj_mem_rootGroupPoints`: for `n ∈ N(k)` and `u ∈ U_a(k)`,
  `n u n⁻¹ ∈ U_{n·a}(k)`.
- `conj_rootGroupPoints`: `n U_a(k) n⁻¹ = U_{n·a}(k)`, as subgroups.
- `weylAction_inv_apply_apply` and `weylAction_apply_inv_apply` give
  `n⁻¹·(n·a) = a` and `n·(n⁻¹·a) = a`.

KP use this fact without comment, for example in §6.1.8 and Fact 6.3.3. The
Library did not have it, and it is needed for the covariance below. The proof
works directly with the published functor `RelativeRoot.subgroup`. Suppose `u`
has an orbit extension `e : T₀ × 𝔸¹ → G` through `a` with `e(0) = 1`, where
`T₀` is the base. Then `n e n⁻¹` (conjugation by the constant point `n`) is an
orbit extension of `n u n⁻¹` through `n·a = a ∘ σ⁻¹`, with value `1` at `0`.
Here `σ` is the automorphism of `S` through which `n` normalizes `i`, so that
`i(σ⁻¹(s)) = n⁻¹ i(s) n` (`IsNormalizingPoint` for `n⁻¹`). The identity
`i(s) n u n⁻¹ i(s)⁻¹ = n · i(σ⁻¹ s) u i(σ⁻¹ s)⁻¹ · n⁻¹` then reduces to group
algebra in the group of morphisms `T₀ × S → G`.

- `KPApartmentData.conj_mem_filtration`: `n U_{a,x,r} n⁻¹ ⊆ U_{n·a,nx,r}`. It
  combines the law above with the published formula
  `(nφ)_b(g) = φ_{n⁻¹·b}(n⁻¹ g n)` of KP 6.1.8.

## Sharp fixers (KP Definition 7.4.1)

Let `Ω : Set D.Apartment`.

- `D.rootFixer a Ω` is `U_{a,Ω,0} = ⋂_{x ∈ Ω} U_{a,x,0}` (Definition
  7.4.1(1)), intersected with `U_a(k)` (`mem_rootFixer`). KP take `Ω`
  nonempty. Then the extra intersection changes nothing, since every
  `U_{a,x,0}` lies in `U_a(k)`. For empty `Ω` it makes the group `U_a(k)`
  rather than all of `G(k)`. We also prove `rootFixer_singleton`
  (`U_{a,{x},0} = U_{a,x,0}`) and `rootFixer_anti`.
- `D.sharpFixer Ω` is `G(k)^♯_Ω`, the subgroup generated by the `U_{a,Ω,0}`
  for all relative roots `a ∈ Φ`, multipliable and divisible ones included
  (Definition 7.4.1(2)). It is written as the supremum over
  `α : relativeRoots i`. `sharpFixer_eq_closure` states it as the subgroup
  generated by the union. `rootFixer_le_sharpFixer` and `sharpFixer_anti` are
  also proved.
- `D.sharpNormalizer Ω = N(k) ⊓ G(k)^♯_Ω` and
  `D.sharpCentralizer Ω = Z(k) ⊓ G(k)^♯_Ω` are KP's `N^♯_Ω` and `Z^♯_Ω`
  (Definition 7.4.1(3)).
- `sharpFixer_le_comap`: `n G(k)^♯_Ω n⁻¹ ⊆ G(k)^♯_{nΩ}` for `n ∈ N(k)`, where
  `nΩ` is the pointwise image. Each generator `U_{a,Ω,0}` goes into
  `U_{n·a,nΩ,0}` by `conj_mem_filtration`, and `n·a ∈ Φ` by the published
  `weylAction_mapsTo_relativeRoots`.

Definition 7.4.1(4)–(5), `P_Ω = G(k)^0_Ω = Z(k)^0 · G(k)^♯_Ω` and
`N^0_Ω = N^♯_Ω · Z(k)^0`, are not defined. See the last section.

## The reduced building (KP (7.6.2))

- `D.sharpFixerFamily` is the family `x ↦ G(k)^♯_x = D.sharpFixer {x}` as a
  published `CovariantSubgroupFamily (normalizerPoints i) D.Apartment`. Its
  covariance `n G(k)^♯_x n⁻¹ ⊆ G(k)^♯_{nx}` is `sharpFixer_le_comap` for
  `Ω = {x}`.
- `D.ReducedBuilding` is the published `ApartmentQuotient` of this family. It
  is the quotient of `G(k) × A` by
  `(g, x) ∼ (h, y) ⇔ ∃ n ∈ N(k), nx = y and g⁻¹ h n ∈ G(k)^♯_x`. This is
  relation (7.6.2) of KP Remark 7.6.5.
- `MulAction (rationalPoints G) D.ReducedBuilding` is left multiplication on
  the first factor.
- `D.apartmentMap x` is the class of `(1, x)`. We prove:
  - `apartmentMap_smul`: it is `N(k)`-equivariant;
  - `exists_smul_apartmentMap`: every point is `g · apartmentMap x`;
  - `apartmentMap_eq_apartmentMap_iff`: `[x] = [y]` iff `nx = y` for some
    `n ∈ N(k) ∩ G(k)^♯_x`.

Provenance. KP construct the building `B(G)` in §7.5–7.6 from a Tits system in
`G(k)^0`. In Remark 7.6.5 they show that the quotient (7.6.2) is a geometric
realization of `B(G)`, compatible with the action of `G(k)`. The quotient is
also Bruhat–Tits' original definition of the building. We take the quotient
(7.6.2) as the definition, because it needs only the valuation data and the
actual groups `G(k)`, `N(k)`, `G(k)^♯_x`, and no `G(k)^0`. The Lean definition
applies to every `D`. That `D.ReducedBuilding` is a building with apartments
`g · apartmentMap(A)`, that `apartmentMap` is injective, and the comparison
with KP's Tits-system construction are theorems that need KP's Chapters 6–7.
They are not proved here.

This is the reduced building. The enlarged building
`B(G) × V(A_G)` of KP Definition 4.3.2 is not defined here.

## Fixers

- `D.reducedFixer y` is the full fixer `G(k)_y` of a point `y`, its stabilizer
  in `G(k)` (`mem_reducedFixer`). We prove:
  - `sharpFixer_le_reducedFixer`: `G(k)^♯_x ⊆ G(k)_{[x]}`;
  - `reducedFixer_apartmentMap`: `G(k)_{[x]}` is the subgroup generated by
    `G(k)^♯_x` and the stabilizer `N(k)_x` of `x` in `N(k)`. This is the
    published `ApartmentQuotient.stabilizer_apartment_eq_sup`. It matches the
    last group `G(k)_x = G(k)^♯_x · N(k)_x` of KP's chain (7.7.1).
  - `reducedFixer_smul`: `G(k)_{gy} = g G(k)_y g⁻¹`.
- `D.pointwiseFixer Y` is the pointwise fixer in `G(k)` of a subset `Y` of the
  building, Mathlib's `fixingSubgroup` (`mem_pointwiseFixer`). For a point,
  `pointwiseFixer_singleton` gives `D.pointwiseFixer {y} = D.reducedFixer y`.

## Special points of the building

`D.IsSpecialBuildingPoint y` holds if `y = g · apartmentMap x` for some
`g ∈ G(k)` and some `x ∈ A` that is special for the affine root system
`Ψ = D.affineRoots`. Here "special" is the published
`AffineRootSystem.IsSpecialPoint`, KP Definition 1.3.39(1): every affine-root
hyperplane is parallel to one through `x`.

KP call a point of `B(G)` special when it is special in an apartment
containing it; they use this, for example, in Theorem 5.3.4. The apartments are
the `g · A` (KP Corollary 7.6.9), so the Lean predicate says "special in some
apartment `g · A` containing `y`". KP show that the choice of apartment does
not matter (Proposition 7.6.4(2)); that is not proved here.

`isSpecialBuildingPoint_apartmentMap` and `IsSpecialBuildingPoint.smul` show
that the predicate holds on images of special points of `A` and is
`G(k)`-invariant. Extra special points (KP 1.3.39(2), the published
`IsExtraSpecialPoint`) form a different notion (KP 6.4.11–6.4.13) and are not
used here.

## Local fields: `G(F)^1` and `K_x`

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field, with the instances
`[ValuativeRel F] [TopologicalSpace F] [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F]`.

- `localFieldOrder F : Fˣ → ℝ` is the normalized order `ord`, cast to `ℝ`.
  It is the published `Plancherel.fieldOrderUnits`, which is positive on the
  maximal ideal and agrees with `FML.ord`. This is KP's `ω` with
  `ω(F^×) = ℤ` (Chapter 7, opening). With this `ω`, the published V 6 sign
  `φ_a(s⁻¹us) = φ_a(u) − ω(a(s))` is the corrected one.
- For `D : KPApartmentData i (localFieldOrder F)`, `D.compactStabilizer y` is
  `G(F)^1 ⊓ G(F)_y`, where `G(F)^1` is the published
  `Plancherel.rationalCommonNormKernel G`: the common kernel of the normalized
  absolute values of all rational characters. This is KP's `G(k)^1_x`
  (Axiom 4.1.2, Theorem 4.2.15), the stabilizer of `x` in `G(k)^1`, written
  `K_x` by the consumers. `mem_compactStabilizer`,
  `compactStabilizer_le_reducedFixer` and
  `compactStabilizer_le_rationalCommonNormKernel` are proved.

The Lean definitions keep `K_x`, the full fixer `G(F)_x` and `G(F)^♯_x` as
separate groups, and no equality between them is asserted. In general they are
different. `G(F)_x` contains the center of `G(F)`, which is not bounded when
`G` has a nontrivial split central torus. `K_x` can be larger than the
parahoric: in KP Example 4.2.16, for `PGL_2` and `x` the barycenter of a
chamber, `K_x` contains the Iwahori subgroup and an element exchanging the
two vertices.
