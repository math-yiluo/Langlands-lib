# Rational lifting from a stable chamber's neutral model

This derived unit implements the geometric-to-integral bridge in KP Corollary 11.7.5. It retains the general parahoric-model theorem, smooth-model descent, the chamber exact sequence and the actual Kottwitz map laws as hypotheses. It does not prove the unconditional corollary.

`rationalConjugate_mem_reductiveKottwitzOnBaseChange_ker` proves that equivariance of the actual Kottwitz map preserves its kernel on the rational-point carrier of the base-changed group. Its proof transports coefficient conjugation through the actual carrier equivalence and uses that every linear Galois automorphism sends zero to zero.

`rationalSubgroupSurjective_of_unramifiedModel` starts with a coefficient-stable subgroup P of G(K), surjectivity of the actual Kottwitz map on P, and a supplied smooth integral model N over the integers of K with connected fibres and integral-point image ker(κ_K) ∩ P. The kernel and P are stable, so the assumed smooth-model descent statement supplies a smooth model M over the integers of F and an actual generic-compatible isomorphism between its unramified base change and N. Connected fibres descend, giving a connected special fibre for M. The isomorphism and the published coordinate-integrality comparison identify M-integrality with membership in P and trivial Kottwitz value. Transporting P to coefficient points allows the proved `integralCosetLifting` to give rational surjectivity on the inverse image of P under actual scalar extension. Equivariance and identification of the actual rational restriction are explicit assumptions.

`StableChamberLiftingStatement` and its proof `stableChamberLifting` specialize this argument to a chamber Cch of supplied building data B, with an actual semilinear apartment action D and stability of Cch under every coefficient automorphism. The geometric exactness premises are surjectivity of κ_K on the setwise stabilizer P and the equality E.zeroPoints ∩ B.pointwiseFixer(Cch) = ker(κ_K) ∩ P for the supplied zero datum E. The published chamber-boundedness theorem realizes Cch as a translate of a nonempty bounded apartment subset. The assumed general parahoric-model theorem then supplies its smooth neutral model with connected fibres. Exactness identifies its integral-point image with the required kernel intersection. Semilinear compatibility proves coefficient stability of P. The previous model theorem gives surjectivity of the rational Kottwitz map on the rational setwise stabilizer.
