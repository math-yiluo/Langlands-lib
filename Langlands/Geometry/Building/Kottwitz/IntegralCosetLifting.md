# Integral-coset lifting for the actual rational Kottwitz map

This is a derived, conditional reduction of the lifting and quotient steps in KP Corollary 11.7.5, printed p. 434 (PDF p. 465). It uses the existing actual Kottwitz homomorphisms, coefficient action, invariant target and smooth integral models. It does not assert the geometric exactness or model-comparison hypotheses.

For a connected reductive group G over a nonarchimedean local field F, let K be its actual maximal unramified extension. The subgroup `rationalCoefficientPreimage F G H` consists of the rational points whose actual coefficient extensions belong to a given subgroup H of G(K).

`IntegralCosetLiftingStatement` assumes an affine finite-type generic group, a smooth integral model M with connected special fibre, a coefficient-Galois-stable subgroup H, and surjectivity of the actual Kottwitz map on H. It also assumes that a point is M-integral exactly when it belongs to H and has trivial Kottwitz value, that the actual Kottwitz map is Galois equivariant, and that its actual rational map satisfies the restriction identity. It concludes surjectivity of the rational Kottwitz map on `rationalCoefficientPreimage F G H`.

The proof `integralCosetLifting` lifts an invariant target value to x in H. Galois stability and equivariance show that every x⁻¹σ(x) belongs to H and has trivial Kottwitz value, hence is integral. The proved `unramifiedIntegralCosetDescent` gives a rational point g and an integral correction h with g_K = xh. The integral comparison puts h in H and in the Kottwitz kernel. Therefore g has the requested rational subgroup membership and target value. Injectivity of the invariant inclusion finishes the target comparison.

`rationalSubgroupQuotient_of_zeroKernel` is the separate elementary quotient step. Assuming the existing proposition `ZeroKernelStatement` and surjectivity of the rational map restricted to any subgroup P, it proves normality of the intersection of P with each datum's zero subgroup and constructs its quotient isomorphism with the invariant target. On every representative x the isomorphism takes [x] to the actual rational Kottwitz value of x. The proof identifies the intersection with the kernel of the restricted homomorphism and applies the group first isomorphism theorem.
