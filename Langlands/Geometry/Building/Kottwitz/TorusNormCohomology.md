# Norm surjectivity and norm kernels for split tori over dimension-one fields

Helper module for the proof of `TorusKottwitzExistenceStatement`
(Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach*, Proposition 11.1.1).
KP's proof of 11.1.1 uses two cohomological facts about the norm map
`N : T(L) → T(K)` for a finite Galois extension `L/K` that splits the torus `T`, where
`K` is the maximal unramified extension of `F`: the norm is onto, and its kernel is the
augmentation subgroup generated by the elements `σ(t)/t`. This module proves both facts
for any field `K` of dimension ≤ 1 (`Langlands.FieldTheory.HasDimensionLEOne`). It also
proves that relative norms compose in a tower. Everything here is derived internal
infrastructure; no new definitions of mathematical objects are introduced.

## Generic Tate-cohomology lemmas

For a representation `A` of a finite group `G`:

- `exists_norm_of_tateZero`: if `Ĥ⁰(G, A) = 0`, every invariant vector is a norm
  `A.ρ.norm b`. Proof: the class `Langlands.Tate.zeroClass` of the invariant vector is
  zero, so `zeroClass_eq_zero_iff` gives the preimage.
- `exists_d₁₀_of_tateMinusOne`: if `Ĥ⁻¹(G, A) = 0`, every vector of norm zero is the
  boundary `groupHomology.d₁₀ c` of a 1-chain. Proof: exactness of the Tate complex at
  degree `-1`, read through `groupHomology.chainsIso₀`/`chainsIso₁` and
  `groupHomology.comp_d₁₀_eq`.
- `invariant_eq_zero_of_tateMinusOne`: if `Ĥ⁻¹(G, A) = 0`, an additive map
  `φ : A →+ X` with `φ (g • a) = φ a` vanishes on every vector of norm zero. Proof: write
  the vector as `d₁₀ c` and use induction on the finitely supported chain `c`. On a single
  generator, `d₁₀ (single g m) = g⁻¹ • m - m`, which `φ` kills.

## The relative point representation

Let `k → K → L` be a scalar tower of fields and `T` a commutative group scheme over `k`.

- `relativeAutHom k K L : (L ≃ₐ[K] L) →* (L ≃ₐ[k] L)` restricts scalars.
- `relativePointRepresentation k K L T` is the Library's point representation
  `GaloisCocycle.pointRepresentation` of `Gal_k(L)` on `T(L)`, restricted along
  `relativeAutHom`. Its action is `relativeConjugate` (`relativePointRepresentation_apply`).
- `resUnitsIso` and `resTensorIso` identify the restriction of the unit representation
  and of a tensor product of representations; both are the identity on underlying modules.
  `resTensorIso` disables the instance `ULift.module` in a scoped section, as the Library
  does for the monoidal structure on `Rep`.

Assume now that `L/K` is finite Galois and `T_L` is split.

- `relativePointTensorIso`: the relative point representation is isomorphic to
  `L^× ⊗ X_*(T_L)`. This is the Library's `tensorPointIso` (Torus/PointsTensorGalois),
  restricted to `Gal(L/K)`.
- `relativePoint_tate_isZero`: if `K` has dimension ≤ 1, all Tate cohomology groups of
  the relative point representation vanish. Proof: `L^×` is cohomologically trivial
  (`HasDimensionLEOne.finite_units_cohomologicallyTrivial`). The cocharacter lattice is
  free over `ℤ` (`latticeRepresentation_free`), so the tensor product has vanishing Tate
  cohomology in every degree (`Langlands.Tate.FreeTensor.tensor_isZero`). Transport along
  the isomorphism above.

## Consequences for the relative norm (KP 11.1.1 inputs)

`relativeNorm k K L T : T(L) →* T(K)` is the Library's relative norm.
`relativePoint_norm_apply` says the representation norm of a point equals the image under
`relativeExtend` of its relative norm (`relativeExtend_norm`).

- `relativeNorm_surjective`: for `K` of dimension ≤ 1 and `T_L` split, the relative norm is
  surjective. Proof: the image of `y ∈ T(K)` in `T(L)` is invariant, so `Ĥ⁰ = 0` makes it a
  representation norm. Then `relativeExtend` is injective.
- `invariant_eq_zero_of_relativeNorm_eq_one`: under the same hypotheses, an additive map
  on `T(L)` that is invariant under every `relativeConjugate σ`, `σ ∈ Gal(L/K)`, vanishes on
  every point of relative norm `1`. Proof: such a point has representation norm zero; apply
  `invariant_eq_zero_of_tateMinusOne` with `Ĥ⁻¹ = 0`.

## Transitivity in a tower

For a tower `k → K → L → M` of fields:

- `relativeExtend_relativeExtend`: extending points from `K` to `L` and then to `M` is
  extending from `K` to `M`. The proof checks coordinates.
- `relativeExtend_relativeConjugate`: for `L/K` normal and `σ ∈ Aut_K(M)`, extension to `M`
  intertwines `σ.restrictNormal L` with `σ`.
- `relativeNorm_relativeNorm`: for `L/K`, `M/K` and `M/L` finite Galois,
  `N_{L/K} ∘ N_{M/L} = N_{M/K}` on `T(M)`. Proof: after `relativeExtend` to `M` (which is
  injective), both sides are products of conjugates. The double product over
  `τ ∈ Gal(L/K)` (lifted by `liftNormal`) and `σ ∈ Gal(M/L)` is reindexed to a product
  over `Gal(M/K)` by the Library's bijection `towerAutEquiv`.
