# Residual Galois equivariance of the torus Kottwitz map

`generalTorusKottwitzEquivariance : GeneralTorusKottwitzEquivarianceStatement.{u}` proves the
Library statement `Langlands.Geometry.Building.Kottwitz.GeneralTorusKottwitzEquivarianceStatement`
(`Kottwitz/ReductiveMapDescentReductionStatement`). This is the Galois-equivariance clause of
Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach*, Proposition 11.1.1 (printed p. 421, PDF
p. 452), at the coefficient-field generality the statement requires.

Notation: `K = UnramifiedClosure F` and `E = SeparableClosure F`. The coefficient field `k` maps
to `K` and `E` in a scalar tower `k → K → E`, with `K/k` normal and `E` a separable closure of
`k`. `T` is a torus over `k`, `σ ∈ Aut_k(K)`, and `κ_T = torusKottwitz F k T hT` is the selected
torus Kottwitz map. The goal is `κ_T(σ x) = σ · κ_T(x)` in `X_*(T)_I` for `x ∈ T(K)`. Here `σ x`
is the coefficient action `GaloisPoint.conjugate`, and `σ ·` is the residual action
`torusGammaRepresentation`.

## Route

KP prove equivariance from the norm square that characterizes `κ_T`: it is natural under Galois
conjugation. The Lean proof makes that naturality explicit. Since `σ` need not fix `F`, it also
proves that the valuation it uses is invariant.

1. **Lift `σ`.** `E/k` is normal, since it is a separable closure. So `σ` lifts to
   `ρ = σ.liftNormal E ∈ Aut_k(E)`, and `ρ.restrictNormal K = σ`
   (`AlgEquiv.restrict_liftNormal`).
2. **A `ρ`-stable splitting field** (`exists_stable_finiteGalois_splitting`). Start from a finite
   Galois splitting field `L₀` of `T` (`exists_finiteGalois_splitting`) and a primitive element
   `α` of `L₀/K`. Let `f` be the minimal polynomial of `α` over `k`, which is separable. Let `L`
   be the `K`-subfield of `E` generated by the roots of `f` in `E`. Then:
   - `L` is a splitting field over `K` of the separable polynomial `f`, so `L/K` is finite
     Galois;
   - `α ∈ L`, so `L₀ ≤ L` and `T` splits over `L`
     (`IsSplitTorus.baseChange_fieldTower_iff`);
   - `L` is stable under every `k`-automorphism of `E`, since such an automorphism permutes the
     roots of `f` and maps `K` to `K` (`adjoin_rootSet_stable`; it uses `K/k` normal).
   So `ρ` restricts to `τ = stableRestrict L ρ ∈ Aut_k(L)`, and
   `τ (algebraMap K L a) = algebraMap K L (σ a)`, by `AlgEquiv.liftNormal_commutes`.
3. **The norm commutes with conjugation** (`conjugate_relativeNorm`):
   `σ (N_{L/K} y) = N_{L/K} (τ y)` for `y ∈ T(L)`. Apply `relativeExtend` to both sides
   (`relativeExtend_injective`). The left side becomes `τ` applied to the product of the
   `Gal(L/K)`-conjugates of `y`, by `relativeExtend_conjugate` (TorusCocharacterTower) and
   `relativeExtend_norm`. Reindex that product by the bijection `g ↦ τ g τ⁻¹` of `Gal(L/K)`
   (`conjAut`, `conjAutEquiv`). This uses `τ · g = (τ g τ⁻¹) · τ` and `conjugate_mul`.
4. **The norm square value is equivariant** (`normLawValue_conjugate_of_valuation`): for
   `y ∈ T(L)`,
   `normLawValue L (τ y) = (ρ|_K) · normLawValue L y`.
   `normLawValue L` is the top-right composite of KP's square: the valuation-induced map
   `T(L) → X_*(T)` (`splitValuation`, with the normalized valuation of `L`) followed by
   extension to `E` and projection to the inertia coinvariants. Both sides are additive, so by
   `additive_ext_coefficientEvaluation` it suffices to check them on pure evaluations
   `w ⊗ ν ↦ ν(w)`, with `w ∈ Lˣ` and `ν ∈ X_*(T_L)`
   (`normLawValue_conjugate_tmul_of_valuation`). There:
   - `τ (ν(w)) = (τ ν)(τ w)` (`coefficientEvaluation_galois_tmul`);
   - `normLawValue L (ν(w)) = [ext(ord_L(w) • ν)]` (`normLawValue_tmul`);
   - `ord_L(τ w) = ord_L(w)` by the valuation hypothesis;
   - `ext(τ ν) = ρ · ext(ν)` (`extendGeometricCocharacters_galois`, TorusCocharacterTower);
   - `[ρ · μ] = (ρ|_K) · [μ]` in the inertia coinvariants
     (`inertiaGammaRepresentation_restrict`).
   The proof chains these equalities with `congrArg`/`Eq.trans`. A single `rw` chain timed out.
5. **Valuation invariance.** The hypothesis `ord_L ∘ τ = ord_L` is supplied by
   `normalizedIntermediateValuation_ringEquiv_of_comm` (ValuationAutomorphismInvariance, by
   Worker 5163), applied to the ring automorphisms `σ` of `K` and `τ` of `L`, which are
   compatible by step 2. That lemma needs `NormalizedIntermediateValuationStatement` and `L/K`
   finite Galois.
6. **Conclusion** (`generalTorusKottwitzEquivariance_of_existence`). Let `hex` be the existence
   statement. It shows that `κ_T` satisfies the norm square at `L`
   (`IsTorusKottwitzHom`, rewritten by `torusNormLawAt_iff`) and that `N_{L/K} : T(L) → T(K)`
   is onto. Write `x = N_{L/K} y`. Then
   `κ_T(σ x) = κ_T(N(τ y)) = normLawValue L (τ y) = (ρ|_K) · normLawValue L y = σ · κ_T(x)`,
   using steps 3 and 4 and `ρ|_K = σ`.

The final theorem applies `generalTorusKottwitzEquivariance_of_existence` to
`torusKottwitzExistence : TorusKottwitzExistenceStatement` (TorusMapProof) and
`normalizedIntermediateValuationStatement : NormalizedIntermediateValuationStatement`
(TorusNormValuation).

## Declarations

- `adjoin_rootSet_stable`, `stableRestrict`, `coe_stableRestrict`: the `K`-subfield of `E`
  generated by the roots of a polynomial over `k` is stable under `Aut_k(E)`, and the
  restriction of an automorphism to a stable intermediate field.
- `conjAut`, `conjAut_apply`, `conjAutEquiv`: conjugation of `Aut_K(L)` by a compatible pair
  `(σ, τ)`, as a bijection.
- `conjugate_relativeNorm`: step 3.
- `exists_stable_finiteGalois_splitting`: step 2.
- `normLawValue_conjugate_tmul_of_valuation`, `normLawValue_conjugate_of_valuation`: step 4.
  They take the valuation invariance as an explicit hypothesis `hv`.
- `generalTorusKottwitzEquivariance_of_existence`: steps 1–6, from the torus existence statement
  and `NormalizedIntermediateValuationStatement`.
- `generalTorusKottwitzEquivariance`: the target.

## Helper modules

Both helper modules are by Worker 5163 and are included in this candidate:

- `Langlands/FieldTheory/HenselianAutomorphismValuation`: ring automorphisms of a Henselian
  `ℤᵐ⁰`-valued field with algebraically closed residue field preserve the valuation up to
  equivalence. Equivalent restrictions to a Henselian base give equivalent real valuations on a
  finite Galois extension.
- `Langlands/Geometry/Building/Kottwitz/ValuationAutomorphismInvariance`: the normalized valuation
  of a finite Galois `L/K` is invariant under ring automorphisms of `L` compatible with ring
  automorphisms of `K`.

The proof also imports these published Library modules of the torus Kottwitz construction, all
under `Langlands/Geometry/Building/Kottwitz/`: TorusSplittingField, TorusCocharacterTower,
TorusKottwitzLaw, TorusKottwitzTransfer, TorusNormValuation and TorusMapProof.
