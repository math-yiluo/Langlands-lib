# Apartment axioms on actual valuation-building data

This internal derived unit compares two supplied `ValuationBuildingData` for
the same actual group over the same nonarchimedean local field. A supplied
isomorphism between their embedded maximal split tori must commute with
their embeddings in that group.

The topology lemmas use that square to identify the actual root subgroups
as subgroups of the same rational-point group, after reindexing characters.
The actual filtration sets also agree at every real level for the
transported family. Substitution therefore transfers the entire V7
predicate: the neighbourhood basis at one, openness in each root group and
algebraic boundedness in the ambient group. The topology is the unchanged
`rationalPointTopology F G.X`; no comparison of arbitrary supplied
topologies is assumed. A comparison of full valuation families at every
corresponding apartment point then transfers V7 for the whole class.

Compatibility of the actual normalizer actions identifies their entire
images under affine conjugation. The definition
`normalizerActionRangeEquivOfComparison` restricts that conjugation to a
multiplicative equivalence of these actual action-image subgroups; its
underlying map is exactly affine conjugation. When the apartment comparison has the
natural derivative on the quotient of real cocharacters, inverse dual
transport identifies precisely the sets of actual root forms. The proof
uses the existing actual relative-root equivalence and its evaluation
identity on quotient translation vectors.

The final theorem transfers every field of the unchanged
`SatisfiesApartmentAxioms` predicate. The target V6 witness is already part
of its supplied actual data. V7 uses the family comparison just described.
Full transported ordinary and extended affine-root equalities transfer
the two rank-zero-compatible root-system predicates and their complete
derivative sets. Linearity of functional transport transfers the
ordinary-or-double-root condition. The existing affine Weyl comparison,
the extended affine Weyl comparison and the normalizer-image identity
transfer both AS5 inclusions and both conditional equalities, retaining
their simply connected and quasi-split adjoint hypotheses.

The provenance is the existing actual cross-torus root, filtration,
translation and normalizer comparisons and the published definitions of
V7 and AS2–AS5. This is a derived transport argument, not a new translation
of a literature theorem. Both actual building data, the torus square,
the affine comparison, its natural derivative, its every-point family
comparison, the full ordinary and extended root comparisons, action
compatibility and the source axiom witness remain explicit inputs. The
result does not construct those inputs or prove the unconditional
`RootValuationBuildingConstructionStatement`. AS1 and AS6 are separate
predicates and are not conclusions of this module.
