# Restricted root functions under normalizer points through the Levi subgroup

Author: Worker 4356.

Lean module: `Langlands.Geometry.Building.RootValueTorusLeviTransport`.

Let `k` be a field, `f : S → T` a homomorphism of commutative group schemes over `k`,
`i : T → G` a monomorphism into an affine group `G`, and `j = f ≫ i` (in Lean `i'`, with
the hypothesis `hf : groupHom f ≫ i = i'`). For a valuation `φ` of the root datum of
`(G, T)`, the published `torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily hS hT hE hφ f` (module
`RootValueTorusRestriction`) is the restriction of `φ` from `T` to `S`. Its depth
subgroups are the published `torusRestrictedRootDepthSubgroup hφ f α r` (module
`RootDepthTorusRestriction`): the subgroup generated by the filtrations
`U_{χ, m r} = hφ.filtrationSubgroup χ (m r)` over roots `χ` of `T` and `m > 0` with
`f ≫ χ = m • α`.

The main theorem says how this restriction transforms under a normalizer point of `T` that
differs from a normalizer point of `S` by an element of the Levi subgroup `Z_G(S)` lying in
the subgroup generated by depth-zero root filtrations. This is the group-theoretic part of
the `N_G(S)(F)`-equivariance in Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach*,
Lemma 9.6.3 (there the Levi element comes from the parahoric subgroup of `Z_G(S)`). The
Lean statement and proof are original to this module. They use only the valuation axioms of
`φ` and need no building geometry.

## Weyl actions (section `Characters`)

- `torusHom_comp_weylAction_of_eq_mul`: let `w ∈ N_i(k)`, `n ∈ N_j(k)` and `h ∈ Z_j(k)`
  (`h ∈ centralizerPoints i'`) with `w = n h` in `G(k)`. Then for every character `χ` of
  `T`, `f ≫ weylAction i w χ = weylAction i' n (f ≫ χ)`.

  Proof. The normalizing automorphisms `σ_w` of `T` and `σ_n` of `S` satisfy
  `i ≫ int(w) = σ_w ≫ i` and `j ≫ int(n) = σ_n ≫ j` (`isNormalizingPoint_normalizingAut`).
  Since `int(n h) = int(h) ≫ int(n)` (`innerIso_mul_hom`) and `j ≫ int(h) = j`, this gives
  `f ≫ σ_w ≫ i = σ_n ≫ f ≫ i`. Cancelling the monomorphism `i` gives `f ≫ σ_w = σ_n ≫ f`,
  hence `σ_n⁻¹ ≫ f = f ≫ σ_w⁻¹`. The claim follows from `weylAction i w χ = σ_w⁻¹ ≫ χ`.
- `torusHom_comp_weylAction_inv_of_eq_mul`: the same identity for `w⁻¹` and `n⁻¹`.

## The Levi part normalizes restricted depth subgroups

From here on `G` is affine and `i` is a monomorphism.

- `torusRestriction_not_opposite`: let `S` be split, `α ≠ 0` a character of `S`, and `χ`,
  `θ` characters of `T` with `f ≫ χ = m • α` for some `m > 0` and `f ≫ θ = 0`. Then
  `1 ⊗ χ ≠ -(t • 1 ⊗ θ)` in `V*(T)` for every real `t > 0`.

  Proof. The perfect pairing of the split torus `S` gives a cocharacter `ν` of `S` with
  `⟨α, ν⟩ > 0`. Pairing both sides with `ν ≫ f` gives `m ⟨α, ν⟩ = -t · 0 = 0`
  (`latticePairing_comp_torusHom_of_restrict_eq_nsmul`), a contradiction.
- `filtrationSubgroup_le_normalizer_torusRestrictedRootDepthSubgroup`: for a root `θ` of
  `T` with `f ≫ θ = 0`, a nonzero character `α` of `S` and `r : ℝ`,
  `U_{θ,0} ≤ N(U^{res}_{α,r})`.

  Proof. Write `U^{res}_{α,r}` as the supremum over the fibre of triples `(χ, m)` above
  (`torusRestrictedRootDepthSubgroup_eq_iSup_fibre`). By the published
  `le_normalizer_iSup_of_commutator_le` (module `GeneratedCommutator`) it suffices that
  `[U_{θ,0}, U_{χ,m r}]` lies in `U^{res}_{α,r}` for each such `(χ, m)`. By the previous
  lemma `χ` is not on the opposite ray of `θ`, so V 3
  (`IsRootDatumValuation.filtrationSubgroup_commutator_le`, module
  `PositiveDepthCollection`) puts the commutator in the subgroup generated by the
  `U_{pθ + qχ, p·0 + q m r}` over roots `pθ + qχ` with `q > 0`. Since
  `f ≫ (pθ + qχ) = (q m) • α`, each of these is a member of the fibre, with depth
  `(q m) r`.
- `leviSharp_le_normalizer_torusRestrictedRootDepthSubgroup`: the subgroup generated by
  all `U_{θ,0}` with `θ` a root of `T` and `f ≫ θ = 0` normalizes every `U^{res}_{α,r}`
  with `α ≠ 0`.

## Conjugation by a normalizer point

- `torusRestrictedRootDepthSubgroup_congr`: the restricted depth subgroups depend only on
  the family, not on the proof that it is a valuation.
- `conj_mem_torusRestrictedRootDepthSubgroup_smul`: let `w ∈ N_i(k)`, with `φ` and `w • φ`
  valuations, and let `ρ` be a linear automorphism of the characters of `S` with
  `f ≫ weylAction i w χ = ρ (f ≫ χ)` for all `χ`. If `u ∈ U^{res,φ}_{β,r}`, then
  `w u w⁻¹ ∈ U^{res,w•φ}_{ρ β, r}`.

  Proof. It suffices to treat a generator `v ∈ U^φ_{χ, m r}` with `f ≫ χ = m • β`. Then
  `w v w⁻¹` lies in the root group of `weylAction i w χ` (`conj_mem_rootGroupPoints`), and
  `(w • φ)(weylAction i w χ)(w v w⁻¹) = φ χ v ≥ m r` by the formula
  `(w • φ) a g = φ (weylAction w⁻¹ a) (w⁻¹ g w)`. The root `weylAction i w χ` restricts to
  `m • ρ β`.

## Levi transport

- `mem_torusRestrictedRootDepthSubgroup_smul_iff`: let `S` be split, `w = n h` with
  `w ∈ N_i(k)`, `n ∈ N_j(k)`, `h ∈ Z_j(k)`, and `h` in the subgroup generated by the
  `U^φ_{θ,0}` with `θ` a root of `T` and `f ≫ θ = 0`. Suppose `w • φ` is also a valuation.
  Then for `α ≠ 0`, `r : ℝ` and `g ∈ G(k)`:
  `g ∈ U^{res, w•φ}_{α,r} ↔ n⁻¹ g n ∈ U^{res, φ}_{weylAction i' n⁻¹ α, r}`.

  Proof. Put `β = weylAction i' n⁻¹ α ≠ 0`; `h` normalizes `U^{res,φ}_{β,r}` by the
  previous section. Forward: conjugation by `w⁻¹`, with `ρ = weylAction i' n⁻¹`
  (compatible by `torusHom_comp_weylAction_inv_of_eq_mul`), carries `g` into
  `U^{res,φ}_{β,r}` as `w⁻¹ g w`. Conjugating by `h` gives
  `h (w⁻¹ g w) h⁻¹ = n⁻¹ g n`. Backward: conjugate `n⁻¹ g n` by `h⁻¹`, then by `w` with
  `ρ = weylAction i' n`, and use `w h⁻¹ n⁻¹ g n h w⁻¹ = g`.
- `torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily_smul_of_eq_mul`: assume moreover `G` smooth, `S` and
  `T` split, and `hE : HasOppositeFactorPairs i`. Then

  `torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily hS hT hE hwφ f = n • torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily hS hT hE hφ f`.

  Proof. By the published uniqueness `torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily_unique`, it
  suffices that `ψ = n • (restriction of φ)` takes the value `⊤` off the roots of `S` and
  off the root groups, and that its filtration at a root `α` and depth `r` is
  `U^{res, w•φ}_{α,r}`. Since `n` normalizes `j`, `weylAction i' n⁻¹` preserves the roots
  of `S` (`weylAction_mem_roots_iff`), and `g ↦ n⁻¹ g n` carries the root group of `α` onto
  that of `weylAction i' n⁻¹ α` (`conj_mem_rootGroupPoints`). So the first two conditions
  follow from the corresponding published properties of the restriction of `φ`. For the
  third, the filtration of `ψ` at `(α, r)` is the set of `g` with `n⁻¹ g n` in the
  filtration of the restriction of `φ` at `(weylAction i' n⁻¹ α, r)`. By the published
  `rootFiltration_torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily` that filtration is
  `U^{res,φ}_{weylAction i' n⁻¹ α, r}`, and the previous lemma identifies the result with
  `U^{res, w•φ}_{α,r}`.
