# Root filtration jumps pass from a root of a larger split torus to its restriction

Module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.TorusRestrictedRootJump`.

This module proves, over one field `k`, that a filtration jump of a root `χ` of `(G, T)` gives a
filtration jump of its restriction `α = f ≫ χ` for the torus-restricted family of root
functions on `(G, S)`. It contains no `def` and no new statement. It is a helper for
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.unramifiedZeroJumpDescent_of_galoisDescent` (module
`UnramifiedZeroJumpDescentAssembly`), where it is applied with `k = K = F^ur`.

The jump predicate is the published `IsRootFiltrationJump i φ a r` (module
`RootJumpLiftNonMultipliable`): `U_{a,φ,r} ⊈ U_{a,φ,r+} · U_{2a}(k)`, where
`U_{a,φ,r+}` is the set of points of `U_a(k)` with value `> r` and `U_{2a}(k)` is
`doubleRootGroupPoints i a`. This is KP's condition (Definition 6.3.4) for the affine
functional with derivative `a` and value `r` at the point to be an affine root.

## Results

### Two facts about ordered products in a monoid

- `list_ofFn_prod_mul_eq_prod_update`: if `y` commutes with every factor `X k`, then
  `(∏ₖ X k) * y` is the ordered product of `Function.update X l (X l * y)`, for any index `l`.
  The proof is by induction on the length; it moves `y` past the factors after position `l`.
- `list_ofFn_prod_update_one`: an ordered product whose factors are all `1` except the factor
  `u` at position `l` equals `u`.

### Two facts about split tori and restriction fibres

- `nsmul_injective_of_isSplitTorus`: for a nonzero character `α` of a split torus,
  `n • α = m • α` implies `n = m`. The proof pairs both sides with a cocharacter `ν` such that
  `⟨α, ν⟩ > 0` (`IsSplitTorus.exists_pairing_eq_pos`).
- `mem_semigroupIndivisibleRoots_of_restrict_eq`: if `χ` is a root of `(G, T)` with
  `f ≫ χ = α ≠ 0`, then `χ` is indivisible in the restriction fibre of `α`, that is
  `χ ∈ RelativeRoot.semigroupIndivisibleRoots j (characterSemigroupPreimage f (cyclicTail α 1))`.
  If `χ = m • b` with `f ≫ b = p • α`, then `(m p) • α = α`, so `m p = 1` and `m = 1`.

### Moving a doubled-root factor into ordered coordinates

`exists_rootCoordinates_mul_doubleRootGroupPoints`. Setting: `S`, `T` split tori,
`f : S ⟶ T`, `j : T ⟶ G`, opposite factor pairs `hE` for `j`, a nonzero character `α` of `S`
such that no `n • α` with `n ≥ 3` is a root of `groupHom f ≫ j`, an enumeration `a` of the
indivisible roots of the restriction fibre of `α`, and an index `l` with `a l = χ`,
`f ≫ χ = α`. Let `X k ∈ U_{a k}(k)` for all `k` and `w ∈ U_{2α}(k)` (the doubled root group
of the restricted torus). Then there are `X' k ∈ U_{a k}(k)` with
`∏ₖ X' k = (∏ₖ X k) · w` and `X' l = X l · d` for some `d ∈ U_{2χ}(k)`
(`doubleRootGroupPoints j χ`).

Proof.

- If `2α` is not a root, `U_{2α}(k)` is trivial and `X' = X`, `d = 1`.
- Otherwise, by `RelativeRoot.subgroup_comp_torusHom_carrier_eq_iSup`, `U_{2α}(k)` is
  generated by the groups `U_c(k)` for the roots `c` of `(G, T)` with `f ≫ c = q • (2α)`,
  `q ≥ 1`. The proof uses induction over this generated subgroup
  (`Subgroup.iSup_induction`). The cases `w = 1` and products `w₁ w₂` are immediate (the
  factors `d` multiply). For a generator `y ∈ U_c(k)`:
  - `y` lies in the restricted root group of `2α`
    (`RelativeRoot.subgroup_le_subgroup_comp_torusHom_of_nsmul`), and every `X k` lies in the
    restricted root group of `α`. By the published
    `RelativeRoot.double_subgroup_commute_of_noHigherRoots`, instantiated with the published
    proofs `semigroupRealization`, `semigroupCommutator` and `cyclicRootFunctorComparison`,
    `y` commutes with every `X k`. This is the only place where the hypothesis on `n • α`,
    `n ≥ 3`, is used.
  - `c` lies in the restriction fibre of `α` (as `f ≫ c = (2q) • α`), so by
    `RelativeRoot.exists_semigroupIndivisibleRoot_ray` it is `c = p • b` for an indivisible
    root `b = a kk` of the fibre. Hence `U_c(k) ≤ U_{a kk}(k)`
    (`RelativeRoot.subgroup_nsmul_le`).
  - Put `X' = Function.update X kk (X kk · y)`. Then `∏ X' = (∏ X) · y` by
    `list_ofFn_prod_mul_eq_prod_update`.
  - If `kk ≠ l`, then `X' l = X l` and `d = 1`. If `kk = l`, then `b = χ` and
    `p • α = f ≫ c = (2q) • α`, so `p = 2q ≥ 2`. Since `p • χ` is a root,
    `HasOppositeFactorPairs.eq_one_or_two_of_nsmul_mem` gives `p = 2`. So `c = 2χ` is a root,
    `y ∈ U_{2χ}(k)`, and `d = y`.

### Main theorem

`isRootFiltrationJump_torusRestricted_of_isRootFiltrationJump`. Let `S ⟶ T` be split tori
acting on `G` through `f` and `j`, with opposite factor pairs `hE` for `j`, and `φ` a
root-datum valuation for `(G, T)` (`hφ : IsRootDatumValuation j φ`). Let `ψ` be
`torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily hS hT hE hφ f`, the restriction of `φ` to `(G, S)`. Let
`α` be a root of `groupHom f ≫ j` such that no `n • α` with `n ≥ 3` is a root, and `χ` a
root of `j` with `f ≫ χ = α`. If `IsRootFiltrationJump j φ χ r`, then
`IsRootFiltrationJump (groupHom f ≫ j) ψ α r`. The value `r ∈ ℝ` is arbitrary.

Proof. Let `u ∈ U_{χ,φ,r}` with `u ∉ U_{χ,φ,r+} · U_{2χ}(k)`. Assume, for a contradiction,
that `U_{α,ψ,r} ⊆ U_{α,ψ,r+} · U_{2α}(k)`.

1. `u ∈ U_{α,ψ,r}`. The level-`r` filtration of `ψ` at `α` is the restricted depth subgroup
   `torusRestrictedRootDepthSubgroup hφ f α r` (`rootFiltration_torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily`),
   which contains `U_{χ,φ,1·r}` because `f ≫ χ = 1 • α`.
2. So `u = h · w` with `h ∈ U_α(k)`, `ψ α h > r`, and `w ∈ U_{2α}(k)`.
3. Choose the enumeration `a` of the indivisible roots of the fibre of `α`, with multipliers
   `m` (`f ≫ a k = m k • α`), from `exists_torusRestrictionRootDepth_enumeration`. Then
   `χ = a l` for some `l` (by `mem_semigroupIndivisibleRoots_of_restrict_eq`), and `m l = 1`.
4. Write `h` as an ordered product `∏ₖ Xs k` with `Xs k ∈ U_{a k}(k)`
   (`RelativeRoot.mem_subgroup_comp_torusHom_iff_existsUnique_rootPointsProduct`). Pick a
   real `s > r` with `ψ α h ≥ s`. By `le_torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily_iff`,
   `h ∈ torusRestrictedRootDepthSubgroup hφ f α s`, and by
   `mem_torusRestrictedRootDepthSubgroup_iff_coordinate_bounds` its `l`-th coordinate has
   `φ χ (Xs l) ≥ m l · s = s > r`.
5. By `exists_rootCoordinates_mul_doubleRootGroupPoints`, `u = h · w = ∏ₖ X' k` with
   `X' k ∈ U_{a k}(k)` and `X' l = Xs l · d`, `d ∈ U_{2χ}(k)`.
6. `u` also is the ordered product whose `l`-th factor is `u` and whose other factors are
   `1` (`list_ofFn_prod_update_one`; `u ∈ U_χ(k)`). The coordinates are unique (the same
   `existsUnique` theorem), so `X' l = u`.
7. Hence `u = Xs l · d` with `Xs l ∈ U_χ(k)`, `φ χ (Xs l) > r` and `d ∈ U_{2χ}(k)`, that is
   `u ∈ U_{χ,φ,r+} · U_{2χ}(k)`. This contradicts the choice of `u`.

## Source and provenance

This is an internal lemma, not a numbered result of Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A
New Approach* (2023). It is the single-field part of the converse direction of KP
Proposition 9.4.28 (printed pages 359–360): the affine roots of the larger torus that vanish at
a point restrict to affine roots of the smaller one. KP derive 9.4.28 differently, through
parahoric group schemes. The proof here uses only the ordered-coordinate description of the
restricted root groups and the centrality of the doubled root group.
