# Galois-fixed points of an unramified apartment under rational normalizer points

Author: Worker 4356.

Lean module: `Langlands.Geometry.Building.UnramifiedFixedLeviTransportStatement`.

This module states, without proof, the building-theoretic input behind clause 4 of the
published `UnramifiedValuationDescentStatement`, the `N_G(S)(F)`-equivariance of the
descended valuation (Kaletha–Prasad, Lemma 9.6.3 and Theorem 9.6.5). The companion module
`UnramifiedFixedNormalizerTransportReduction` proves that this statement implies clause 4.
It defines one `Prop` and contains no theorem.

## The statement (`UnramifiedFixedLeviTransportStatement`)

The binders and their instances are those of `UnramifiedValuationDescentStatement`, except
that `hE` is omitted. Thus:

- `F` is a nonarchimedean local field and `K = inertiaFixedField F` its maximal unramified
  extension;
- `G` is a connected reductive group over `Spec F` (`hG`), with affine and smooth instances
  from `hG`; `iS : S.toGrp ⟶ G` is a maximal split torus (`hiS`);
- `iT : T.toGrp ⟶ G` and `f : S ⟶ T` satisfy `CommGroupScheme.groupHom f ≫ iT = iS`, and
  `hiT` says that the scalar extension `iT_K` is a maximal split torus of `G_K`;
- `D : KPApartmentData iT_K ω` is an equipollence class of valuations of the root datum of
  `(G_K, T_K)`, where `ω` is the order of `MaximalUnramified.valuation F`, and `C` is a
  centralizer datum with full V 6 (`IntegerValuation.IsOmegaCompatible`).

Write `fixed x` for the condition that `x : D.Apartment` is fixed by
`RootFunctionFamily.galoisAction F K T G σ` for every `σ : K ≃ₐ[F] K`, as in the target.
Write `n_K = rationalExtend F K G n` for the image of `n ∈ G(F)` in `G(K)`.

The conclusion: for every `n : normalizerPoints iS` and every `x : D.Apartment` with
`fixed x`, there is `h ∈ G(K)` such that

1. `h ∈ centralizerPoints iS_K`: `h` centralizes `S_K`;
2. `h` lies in the subgroup generated by the filtration groups `D.filtration x χ 0`
   (KP's `U_{χ,x,0}`) over the roots `χ` of `T_K` with `f_K ≫ χ = 0`, that is, over the
   roots of the Levi subgroup `Z_G(S)_K` with respect to `T_K`;
3. `n_K * h` normalizes `T_K` (the proof `hw` of this is part of the conclusion), and the
   point `⟨n_K * h, hw⟩ • x` of `D.Apartment` is again `fixed`.

## Why it holds (Kaletha–Prasad)

The argument below is derived from KP and is not in KP in this form. Identify
`D.Apartment` with the apartment `A` of `T_K` in `B(G_K)`, as in the Markdown of
`UnramifiedValuationDescentStatement`. There `T` is a special `F`-torus containing `S`,
and `A` is the corresponding special `F`-apartment. Let `Γ = Aut(K/F)`.

1. **Proposition 9.3.13.** The point `n·x` lies in the apartment `A_{nTn⁻¹}` of the special
   torus `n T n⁻¹ ⊇ S`. It is `Γ`-fixed because `n` is `F`-rational and `x` is `Γ`-fixed.
   By Proposition 9.3.13 (used the same way in the proof of Proposition 9.3.16),
   `(A_{nTn⁻¹})^Γ = B(G_K)^{SΓ} = A^Γ`, so `n·x ∈ A`.
2. **Transitivity in the Levi subgroup.** By Proposition 9.3.11(2), `B(G_K)^S` is
   the union of the apartments of the maximal `K`-split tori containing `S_K`. For
   `M_K = Z_G(S)_K`, the precise decomposition in §9.7.4, equation (9.7.3), is
   `B(G_K)^S = B(M_K) × A(M_K,G_K)`. Here `A(M_K,G_K)` is an affine space with
   translation space `V(A_M/A_G)`, where `A_M` and `A_G` are the maximal `K`-split
   central tori of `M_K` and `G_K`. Each apartment in this union is the product of a
   reduced Levi apartment and this relative central factor. Choosing a point of the
   factor identifies the union with the enlarged building of `M_K/A_G` (§9.7.5).
   The full enlarged building of `M_K` instead has central translation space `V(A_M)`
   (Definition 4.3.2); the quotient by `A_G` is essential for general reductive `G_K`.
   Both `A` and `A_{nTn⁻¹}` contain `n·x`. Project to the reduced Levi building and apply
   Propositions 7.6.4(2) and 7.7.5(4) for `M_K`: the subgroup generated by
   `U_{χ, n·x, 0}` (Definition 7.4.1(2)), over the roots `χ` of `M_K` with respect to
   `n T_K n⁻¹`, acts transitively on the reduced Levi apartments containing the projected
   point and fixes that point. These Levi root groups lie in the derived subgroup of
   `M_K`, which acts trivially on `A(M_K,G_K)` by §9.7.4. Thus they fix the whole point
   `n·x` in `B(G_K)^S`. Some `m` in the generated subgroup carries `A_{nTn⁻¹}` onto `A`,
   so `m n T_K n⁻¹ m⁻¹ = T_K` and `m·(n·x) = n·x`.
   Compare Proposition 9.3.12 over `K` with `Ω = {n·x}`, in its corrected form, as used in
   the Corrections' addition before Proposition 9.3.13. It gives an element of
   `G(K)^0_{n·x}` that commutes with `S` and carries one of these apartments onto the
   other.
3. **The element `h`.** Put `h = n⁻¹ m n ∈ M(K)`. Then `h` centralizes `S_K`. Since `n`
   normalizes `S` and hence `M`, conjugation by `n⁻¹` carries the generators
   `U_{χ, n·x, 0}` for `n T_K n⁻¹` to the generators `U_{χ', x, 0}` for `T_K`, where `χ'`
   runs over the roots of `T_K` vanishing on `S_K`. This gives conditions 1 and 2. The
   element `n h = m n` normalizes `T_K`.
4. **Fixedness.** In `B(G_K)`, `(n h)·x = m·(n·x) = n·x`, which is `Γ`-fixed. The element
   `n h` lies in `N_{G_K}(T_K)(K)`, so `(n h)·x` lies in `A`. The map from `A` into
   `B(G_K)` is injective and `Γ`-equivariant, so `(n h)·x` is a `Γ`-fixed point of `A`.

The condition that `h` lies in the subgroup generated by the `U_{χ,x,0}` is what the
reduction uses. It makes `h` fix `x` in the reduced building of `G_K`, and it makes `h`
normalize the depth subgroups of the restricted valuation (V 3; module
`RootValueTorusLeviTransport`).

## Provenance and scope

The provenance is derived. KP work over Henselian discretely valued fields with perfect
residue field; here `F` is a nonarchimedean local field. KP state the facts above for the
building `B(G_K)`. The Lean statement uses the Library's `D.Apartment` and its Galois
action on root-function families. It drops the building points and keeps only what the
reduction to clause 4 needs: the element `h` and the fixedness of `(n_K h) • x`.

The statement is not proved here. Its proof needs the building of `G_K` over `K`, its
relative Levi decomposition `B(G_K)^S = B(M_K) × A(M_K,G_K)` with translation space
`V(A_M/A_G)` in the second factor, Proposition 9.3.13, which rests on Bruhat–Tits group
schemes through Proposition 9.3.11, and the injectivity of the apartment map. These
constructions and comparisons for `G_K` over `K` are not supplied by this module.
