# Compact open original Moy–Prasad subgroups

This internal derived unit constructs `CentralizerFiltration.moyPrasadOpenSubgroup` for a supplied original parahoric datum `P`, a supplied centralizer filtration `Zf`, an apartment point `x` and a finite nonnegative depth `r`. Its underlying subgroup is definitionally the original `Zf.moyPrasad x r`, with the canonical rational-point topology of `G(F)`. The input to the construction is V7 topology compatibility of the original apartment and openness of every supplied centralizer-depth subgroup in the canonical topology of the actual centralizer.

Openness is proved from the existing actual root-centralizer generation theorem. Rewrite the original sharp-depth subgroup as the supremum of the original root filtrations. V7 gives each filtration as an open subgroup of its actual root group, and the supplied open centralizer filtration provides the remaining open subgroup. The published root-centralizer generation theorem then makes their generated subgroup open in `G(F)`. No product surjectivity, separatedness, commutator-friendliness or covariance is used for this openness result.

Boundedness uses the supplied apartment axioms. The original depth group lies in the point parahoric, which lies in the original norm-one point fixer by topology compatibility. The published singleton sharp-fixer boundedness proof and actual centralizer norm-kernel boundedness give boundedness of that same fixer. Passing to the original depth subgroup proves `IsAlgebraicallyBounded`. This conclusion does not require openness of the supplied centralizer filtration or any of its four additional algebraic axioms.

With both the apartment axioms and the supplied centralizer openness, each original depth subgroup is also compact. Connected reductivity of the original datum supplies the canonical topological group structure. The already proved openness makes the subgroup closed; algebraic boundedness gives compact closure over the local field. Thus the same subgroup is a closed subset of its compact closure and is compact.

All statements work over arbitrary universe `u` and all nonarchimedean local fields, including depth zero. Compact openness asserts no depth-zero Iwahori factorization. No special point, valid assignment or alternate subgroup is introduced. The unit does not construct a centralizer filtration, prove separatedness or covariance of the original depth groups, or establish their ordered depth-product covering or the host simultaneous-basis target.
