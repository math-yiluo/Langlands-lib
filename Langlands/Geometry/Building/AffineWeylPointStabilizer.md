# Point stabilizers in the affine Weyl group

Module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.AffineWeylPointStabilizer`. Internal
geometric unit for a locally finite reflection arrangement
`Ψ : Set (A →ᵃ[ℝ] ℝ)` on a real affine space `A`
(`AffineRootSystem.IsLocallyFiniteReflectionArrangement Ψ`), in independent
universes `uV`, `uA`. No group-theoretic data is involved.

## Declarations

- `pointReflections Ψ p`: the set of reflections `affineReflection ψ hv.1` with
  `ψ ∈ Ψ`, `ψ p = 0` and `IsCoroot Ψ ψ v`.
- `pointReflections_subset_affineWeylGroup`,
  `closure_pointReflections_le_affineWeylGroup`: these reflections lie in
  `affineWeylGroup Ψ`. `apply_eq_of_mem_closure_pointReflections`: every
  element of the generated subgroup fixes `p`.
- `exists_facetRel_closure_pointReflections`: if `p` lies in the closures of the
  regular facets of `x` and `y`, some element `w` of the subgroup generated by
  `pointReflections Ψ p` has `facetRel Ψ (w x) y`. The proof is the induction
  of the published `exists_facetRel_fixing` (module `AffineWeylFacetFixing`) on
  the number of separating roots: each wall reflection used there is in a root
  that vanishes at `p` (`root_eq_zero_of_mem_closures`).
- `mem_closure_pointReflections_of_apply_eq`: **an element `w` of `W(Ψ)` with
  `w p = p` lies in the subgroup generated by `pointReflections Ψ p`.** Choose
  a regular `x` with `p` in its facet closure
  (`exists_regularPoint_closure`). The previous lemma gives `v` in the
  subgroup with `v x` in the facet of `w x`. Then `v⁻¹ w` stabilizes the
  chamber of `x`, so it is `1` by the published `chamber_stabilizer_eq_one`
  with `reflectionGalleryFreeness` and `affineReflectionGalleryGeometry`. This
  follows the proof of the published `eq_of_facetRel_apply`.
- `exists_generic_point`: for a finite non-empty `s : Finset A` there is a point
  `z` such that every `e : A ≃ᵃ[ℝ] A` fixing `s` pointwise fixes `z`, and every
  `ψ ∈ Ψ` with `ψ z = 0` vanishes on `s`. Induction on `s`: from `z` for `s`
  pass to `ε • (a -ᵥ z) +ᵥ z` for a small `ε > 0` given by the published
  `eventually_sign_smul_vadd`. This keeps the sign of every root not vanishing
  at `z`, and an affine combination of fixed points is fixed.

## Provenance

Internal. Together the two main results give the standard fact that the
pointwise fixer in `W(Ψ)` of a finite set is generated by the reflections that
fix the set (used in Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach*,
2023, Lemma 7.4.7, printed pp.256–257 / PDF pp.287–288, through the facet structure of
§1.3).
