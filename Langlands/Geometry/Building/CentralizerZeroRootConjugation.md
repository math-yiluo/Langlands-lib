`centralizerZeroAffineRootGroupAut` has the original affine root subgroup as
both domain and codomain; its value in the original rational group is exactly
`j(z) * g * j(z)⁻¹`.

More generally, a point of the actual centralizer's rational common norm
kernel fixes the apartment by the bundled omega compatibility. Its inverse
also fixes the apartment, so transport by its inverse preserves every affine
functional. Its Weyl action on every character is the identity. Applying the
imported weak and strict conjugation equalities therefore preserves each
`affineRootGroup a ψ` and `affineRootGroupPlus a ψ`.

The published inclusion of the supplied centralizer-zero subgroup in that
norm kernel specializes both equalities to centralizer zero. The corresponding

Restricting the ambient conjugation equivalence to the weak subgroup and
identifying its image with that same subgroup defines the automorphism.

The character and affine functional are arbitrary; no derivative or
root-membership condition is required. The scope is all supplied local-field
building data at arbitrary universe, with no additional apartment, topology,
cover-comparison or factor-pair-existence premise. This is an internal
deduction from the imported actual normalizer conjugation, centralizer
compatibility, Weyl action and centralizer-zero norm-kernel inclusion.
It supplies no invariance of centralizer zero under other normalizer points
and no two-cell decomposition.
