# Integral models for doubled-root depth subgroups of apartment lifts

Author: Worker 510.

The module defines `UnramifiedDoubledRootDepthModelStatement.{u}` as an unproved
proposition. It is an internal prerequisite for the depth-preserving two-factor
descent required by `UnramifiedComparisonRootFieldStatement`. It neither proves
that target nor changes its hypotheses.

The common data are a nonarchimedean local field `F : Type u`, an affine group
scheme `G/F`, supplied parahoric building data `P` satisfying the apartment
axioms, a comparison torus `t`, and a concrete apartment lift `L`. In particular,
the upper root valuation is the actual supplied valuation at `L.e y`, and its
Galois fixedness and torus-then-field restriction belong to `L`. The statement
retains every one of these supplied data.

For every lower apartment point `y`, every actual lower root `a` whose double
is also a root, every closed representative `q : U → G` of the actual subgroup
functor `U_{2a}`, and every nonnegative real depth `s`, the proposition asks for
an integral model

```
M : IntegralModel 𝒪[F] F U.
```

An `IntegralModel` is an affine flat finite-type group scheme over the integer
ring, with a specified isomorphism from its generic fibre to this very `U`.
The conclusion additionally requires smoothness and connectedness of the
special fibre over the residue field `𝓀[F]`. It does not require a reductive
special fibre or a model of the full ambient reductive group. Closed smooth
representatives of a nonzero root functor are already supplied by
`RelativeRoot.subgroup_has_smooth_closed_representative`; constructing such a
representative is distinct from constructing its depth model.

Write `K = UnramifiedClosure F`, `f_K` for the base change of `t.f`, and
`(2a)_K` for the base change of the lower doubled character. For every
`x ∈ U_K(K)`, the required equivalence is

```
M.HasIntegralCoordinates K (baseChangeCoeffEquiv F K U x)
  ↔ q_K(x) ∈ torusRestrictedRootDepthSubgroup φ f_K (2a)_K s,
```

where `φ` is the root-datum valuation at `L.e y`. The subgroup on the right is
generated by the upper root filtrations at depths `m*s` over all actual upper
roots restricting to `m*(2a)_K` for positive integers `m`. This is the published
multiplier-scaled depth subgroup, with its existing meaning unchanged.
`baseChangeCoeffEquiv` is the published multiplicative comparison between
rational points of the actual base change and `K`-coefficient points of `U`.
`HasIntegralCoordinates` tests integrality over `𝒪[F]` of every generic model
coordinate. The comparison covers all `K`-points, not only extensions of
`F`-rational points; no finite splitting extension is assumed.

The intended use is the central correction lattice at doubled depth: substituting
`s = 2r`, for `r ≥ 0`, provides a model to which the published
`unramifiedIntegralCosetDescent` can be applied once a Galois-stable correction
coset has been established. The present proposition does not assert that coset
stability or perform the correction. It does not assume either comparison field
or the unproved broader fixed-point model statements.

The provenance is internal and source-free: this is a focused model-existence
interface using `ApartmentLift`, `torusRestrictedRootDepthSubgroup`,
`IntegralModel`, and `HasIntegralCoordinates`. The published
`rootFieldDepthModelOfOrbit` constructs smooth connected models for supplied
additive root-field coordinates, but comparison with the arbitrary supplied
apartment valuation and with all `K`-points remains an obligation here.
No numbered assertion from the original literature is claimed to be
transcribed by this definition, and no proof of model existence is supplied.
