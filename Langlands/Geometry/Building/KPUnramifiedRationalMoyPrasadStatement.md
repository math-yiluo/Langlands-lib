# The rational Moy–Prasad family from the maximal unramified extension

Module: `Langlands.Geometry.Building.KPUnramifiedRationalMoyPrasadStatement`.

Declaration:
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.UnramifiedRationalMoyPrasadStatement`.

## Statement and scope

Let `F : Type u` be a nonarchimedean local field, with its `Field`, `ValuativeRel`,
`TopologicalSpace` and `IsNonarchimedeanLocalField` instances. Let `G` be a group scheme
over `F` with affine structure morphism, and let `P : ParahoricBuildingData G` satisfy
`P.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms`. The statement retains arbitrary universe `u` and arbitrary
characteristic. The connected reductive group and maximal split torus are part of `P`.

Put `K = UnramifiedClosure F = inertiaFixedField F` and
`G_K = GaloisPointComparison.baseChanged F K G`. There exist

- `B : MaximalUnramified.BuildingData F G_K`;
- `hZ : IsTorus B.centralizer.Z`, the torus binder used by the published upstairs
  Moy–Prasad definition;
- `e : P.Apartment → B.Apartment`;

such that

```lean
P.IsRationalMoyPrasadFamily
  (fun y r => (B.moyPrasad hZ (e y) r).comap (rationalExtend F K G))
```

holds. Here `r : ℝ≥0`, and `rationalExtend` is the actual extension homomorphism on
rational points of the base-changed group scheme. Its inverse image expresses
`Q y r = G(K)_{e(y),r} ∩ G(F)` on the existing carrier `rationalPoints G`.
The torus-centralizer condition describes the quasi-split setting over `K`.

The definition universally quantifies only `F`, its four instances, `G`, its affine
structure-morphism instance, `P` and the apartment-axiom premise. The building data `B`,
torus proof `hZ` and map `e` are existential witnesses in the conclusion. No filtration,
subgroup, upstairs building data, point map, tameness condition, residue-characteristic
condition, splitness condition, rank restriction or unproved proposition is supplied
as an additional premise.

The private affine-instance proof derives affineness of `G_K → Spec K` by base change
of the given affine morphism `G → Spec F`. It introduces no additional hypothesis.

## Published definitions used

The module imports `KPLocalMoyPrasadGroups` and uses exactly
`MaximalUnramified.BuildingData.moyPrasad B hZ x r`. This is the subgroup generated by
the image of `B.centralizerTorusFiltration hZ r` and
`B.apartment.sharpFiltration x (fun _ => (r : ℝ))`. The torus filtration is the published
`localTorusFiltration`, and all relative roots, including divisible roots, occur in the
sharp filtration. The binder `hZ` is enough: D1 derives the centralizer's commutative
group-scheme presentation from `IsTorus.isCommMonObj`. This statement introduces no
second definition of the upstairs groups or of their torus term.

The conclusion uses the published Prop-valued structure
`ParahoricBuildingData.IsRationalMoyPrasadFamily` from `KPRationalMoyPrasadFamily`.
Write `jF = rationalPoints.map P.centralizer.j`. Its ten fields, their source
provenance, and their roles in the published reduction are as follows.

then of its closed centralizer, then install the rational-point topology. Thus openness
is a statement about `Z(F)` with its specified topology.

The root-intersection field uses the block
`U_{a,y,t} U_{2a,y,t}` of Definition 7.3.3(1) and §9.8.2. It does not assert membership

factor is essential (Remark 7.3.4 and V 4). Correspondingly, when restricting bare
root filtrations, an upstairs root restricting to `2a` has depth `2r` for the bare
`a`-filtration at depth `r`; the printed uniform-depth reading of Lemma 9.6.3 is not
used here.

## Exact consumer and proof status

These are exactly the ten fields consumed by the published theorem
`ParahoricBuildingData.exists_doubleRootFriendly_filtration_of_rationalMoyPrasadFamily`.
Given the existential witnesses and the resulting `hQ`, that theorem, together with
the existing premise `P.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms`, yields

```lean
∃ Zf : P.CentralizerFiltration,
  Zf.SatisfiesFiltrationAxioms ∧ Zf.IsOpenFiltration ∧
    Zf.IsDoubleRootCommutatorFriendly
```

which is the conclusion of `CentralizerFiltrationDoubleRootExistenceStatement` for `P`.
It chooses a base apartment point `x₀` and sets the filtration to
`r ↦ (Q x₀ r).comap jF`; the independence field allows its use at any point. In the
double-root cases the proof uses `filtration_le` at depth `2r`, together with V 4 and
the positive-depth factorization field.

This module states the existence input as a Prop-valued `def`. It supplies no proof
of that proposition. Its formulation is derived from KP's construction and the
published ten-field reduction interface; the internal name “rational Moy–Prasad
family” is the Library interface's terminology.

The intended construction chooses a special torus as in Proposition 9.3.4 and
quasi-split data over `K`, then uses Construction 9.6.1, Theorem 9.6.5 and the
uniqueness of Proposition 6.2.6 to compare with `P`. No such choices are parameters
of the statement. For the Levi projection in the proof of Lemma 9.8.1, the correction
to (9.7.1) restricts the projection to `B(G)^Z → B(Z)`; points of the relevant
apartment lie in that domain.

The depth-zero comparison remains part of the asserted existence result. D1's
documented boundary between its norm-Iwahori zero term and the maximal-bounded
description is inherited; no theorem resolving that comparison is asserted in this
statement module.
