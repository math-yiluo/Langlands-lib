# Reduction of the split-centralizing transporter to two fixed points in one apartment

This module proves a conditional theorem and one helper lemma. It proves neither
proposition unconditionally.

## Results

- `KPApartmentData.iSup_filtration_le_reducedFixer`. Let `D : KPApartmentData i ω`
  be apartment data over any field \(k\), let \(x\) be an apartment point, and let
  \(p\) be any predicate on characters. The subgroup generated by the depth-zero
  filtration subgroups `D.filtration x χ 0`, over relative roots \(\chi\)
  satisfying \(p\), fixes the image `D.apartmentMap x` in the reduced building:
  it lies in `D.reducedFixer (D.apartmentMap x)`.
- `unramifiedFixedPointTransporter_of_fixedPairSpecialApartment`. This is the
  implication

  `UnramifiedFixedPairSpecialApartmentStatement.{u} → UnramifiedFixedPointTransporterStatement.{u}`.

So the transporter statement, KP Propositions 9.3.11(1) and 9.3.12 over \(K\),
follows from the two-point statement, KP 9.3.11(1) with torus conjugacy. The
9.3.12 part is replaced by the published Levi sharp transport.

## Proof of the helper lemma

The subgroup is bounded by `iSup_le`. For each relative root \(\chi\),
`D.rootFixer_singleton` rewrites `D.filtration x χ 0` as the root fixer of the
one-point set \(\{x\}\). `D.rootFixer_le_sharpFixer` puts it in the sharp fixer of
\(x\), and `D.sharpFixer_le_reducedFixer` puts the sharp fixer in the fixer of
`D.apartmentMap x`.

## Proof of the reduction

Assume the two-point statement and fix the data of the transporter statement:
\(F\), \(G\), `iS`, `f : S ⟶ T`, `iT`, `hiT`, `D`, `C`, the V 6 hypothesis, `hD`, a
coefficient-fixed apartment point \(x_0\), and a point \(y\) fixed by
`galoisQuotientAction` and by the units of \(S\). Write \([x]\) for
`D.apartmentMap x`.

1. **\([x_0]\) satisfies the two-point hypotheses.**
   - *Galois fixation.* The coefficient fixation of \(x_0\) gives
     `galoisAction σ x₀ = x₀` (by `KPApartmentData.Apartment.ext`). Then
     `KPApartmentData.galoisQuotientAction_apartmentMap` gives
     \(\sigma([x_0]) = [x_0]\).
   - *Fixation by the units of \(S\).* The published
     `isFixedByScalarTorusUnits_apartmentMap`, from
     `Langlands.Geometry.Building.TorusUnitApartmentFixation`, is applied to
     `groupHom f`, the V 6 hypothesis and \(x_0\). It shows that the units of
     \(S_K\), mapped into \(G_K\) through `f` and `iT`, fix \([x_0]\). The
     factorization `groupHom f ≫ iT = iS` turns this into
     `IsFixedByUnramifiedTorusUnits F iS [x₀]`.
2. **The two-point statement.** Apply it to \(y_1 = [x_0]\) and \(y_2 = y\). It
   gives \(g \in G_K(K)\) in `centralizerPoints` of \(i_{S,K}\), with
   \(g^{-1}\sigma(g)\) in `normalizerPoints` of \(i_{T,K}\) for every \(\sigma\).
   It also gives apartment points \(x_2\) and \(x_1\) with
   \(g \cdot [x_2] = [x_0]\) and \(g \cdot [x_1] = y\).
3. **Levi sharp transport.** Put \(n = g^{-1}\). It lies in the normalizer of
   \(i_{S,K}\) by `centralizerPoints_le_normalizerPoints`, and
   \(n \cdot [x_0] = [x_2]\) lies in the image of the apartment. The published
   `unramifiedLeviSharpApartmentTransport` (the proof of
   `UnramifiedLeviSharpApartmentTransportStatement`) applies to \(n\) and \(x_0\).
   It gives \(h \in G_K(K)\) such that:
   - \(h\) lies in `centralizerPoints` of \(i_{S,K}\);
   - \(h\) lies in the subgroup generated by the depth-zero filtration subgroups
     `D.filtration x₀ χ 0` over the relative roots \(\chi\) of \(T_K\) that vanish
     on \(S_K\);
   - \(m := g^{-1}h\) lies in `normalizerPoints` of \(i_{T,K}\).

   This \(h\) is the required element. The four conclusions follow.
4. **\(h\) commutes with \(S_K(K)\).** After `rationalPoints.map_apply`, this is
   `commute_of_mem_centralizerPoints`.
5. **\(h\) fixes \([x_0]\).** Apply the helper lemma at \(x_0\), with \(p\) the
   condition that \(\chi\) vanishes on \(S_K\). Then `D.mem_reducedFixer` gives
   \(h \cdot [x_0] = [x_0]\).
6. **The Galois differences of \(h\) normalize \(T_K\).** In the group
   \(G_K(K)\) one has the identity
   \(h^{-1}\sigma(h) = m^{-1}\,(g^{-1}\sigma(g))\,\sigma(m)\).
   It is proved by `map_mul`, `map_inv` for the group automorphism
   `rationalConjugate F K G σ`, followed by `group`. Here \(m^{-1}\) and
   \(g^{-1}\sigma(g)\) are in the normalizer. The published
   `rationalConjugate_mem_normalizerPoints` shows that \(\sigma(m)\) is in it
   too. Hence the product is in the normalizer.
7. **\(y\) lies in the \(h\)-translate of the apartment.** Take
   \(x = m^{-1} \cdot x_1\), using the action of the normalizer subgroup on
   `D.Apartment`. By `D.apartmentMap_smul` and `smul_smul`,
   \(h \cdot [x] = (h m^{-1}) \cdot [x_1]\). Since \(h m^{-1} = g\) (by `group`),
   this equals \(g \cdot [x_1] = y\).

## Implementation notes

The two-point statement and the Levi transport are called with explicit field
instances (`@hPair F iF iV iTop iL …`). This avoids slow repeated instance
synthesis on \(F\). The main theorem uses `set_option maxHeartbeats 4000000`, the
same finite bound as the Library's `UnramifiedTorusUnitCoveringReduction`. It also
uses `set_option synthInstance.maxHeartbeats 400000` for the normalizer action on
`D.Apartment` in step 7.
