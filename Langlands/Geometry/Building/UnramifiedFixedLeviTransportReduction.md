# Levi transport from a fixed-apartment lift and sharp apartment transport

Author: Worker 229.

This internal reduction uses the injectivity of the apartment chart of a maximal
split torus in a connected reductive group. The helper
`KPApartmentData.normalizer_mul_smul_eq_of_mem_sharpFixer` proves that if `g h`
normalizes the apartment torus, `h` is in the sharp fixer at `x`, and the chart of
`y` is `g` applied to the chart of `x`, then `(g h) • x = y`. Indeed the sharp
fixer acts trivially on the chart and injectivity lifts the resulting equality.

The Prop-valued `UnramifiedFixedLeviTransportReductionStatement` states that
`UnramifiedTorusUnitFixedApartmentStatement` together with
`UnramifiedLeviSharpApartmentTransportStatement` implies the unchanged
`UnramifiedFixedLeviTransportStatement`. The theorem
`unramifiedFixedLeviTransport_of_fixedApartment_and_leviSharp` proves this
conditional assertion; neither prerequisite is proved by this reduction.

The fixed-apartment lift gives a coefficient-fixed `y` whose chart is the
rational normalizer applied to the chart of `x`. Scalar extension puts that
normalizer in the normalizer of `S_K`. Apply Levi sharp transport to find `h`
centralizing `S_K`, in the depth-zero subgroup generated by the roots vanishing
on `S_K`, with `n_K h` normalizing `T_K`. Each generating root group is contained
in the sharp fixer at `x`; hence so is their supremum. The helper then identifies
`(n_K h) • x` with `y`. Connected reductivity after the algebraic extension
supplies the hypothesis needed for apartment injectivity. Substitution transfers
the coefficient fixedness of `y` to the required point.

This is a derived internal reduction from the imported statements and proved
sharp-fixer, scalar-extension and apartment-injectivity lemmas. The exact target
still requires independent proofs of both prerequisite propositions.
