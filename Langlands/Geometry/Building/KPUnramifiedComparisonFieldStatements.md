## Common shape of the four targets

At universe `u`, each target quantifies over

- every `F : Type u` with `Field F`, `ValuativeRel F`, `TopologicalSpace F` and
  `IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F` (any characteristic, no further restriction);
- every `G : GroupScheme (Spec F)` with `IsAffineHom G.X.hom`;
- every `P : ParahoricBuildingData G` with `P.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms`;
- every special torus `t : P.UnramifiedComparisonTorus` and every lift
  `L : t.ApartmentLift` (module `KPUnramifiedApartmentLift`).

So each target is asserted for *every* concrete lift, not for a chosen one. No target
asserts that a lift exists. The upstairs data are the lift's actual building data
`B = L.buildingData` over `K = UnramifiedClosure F`, and the map of apartments is `L.e`.
For each target, the conclusion is literally the type of the corresponding field of the
published `ParahoricBuildingData.IsUnramifiedComparison P B hZ e`, after substituting
`B := L.buildingData`, `hZ := L.centralizer_isTorus` and `e := L.e`.
The assembly theorem below confirms this: it supplies each field by direct specialization.

Write `ι = rationalExtend F K G : G(F) → G(K)` and
`j = rationalPoints.map P.centralizer.j : Z(F) → G(F)`, where `Z = Z_G(S)` is the
downstairs centralizer.

Notes on the targets.

- **Root.** The intended choice of `Ψ` is the set of relative roots of `(G_K, T_K)` that
  restrict to `a` or `2a`. The intended `ν` is a generic point of the image of the
  cocharacter space of `S` with `⟨a, ν⟩ > 0`. These choices are *not* clauses of the formal
  target, which only asserts that some `Ψ, ν` exist. `filtration_mem` uses the uniform depth
  `s` for all `b ∈ Ψ`, and `block_mem` concludes membership in the Library's block
  `U_{a,y,t} U_{2a,y,t}` (`P.apartment.filtrationBlock y (fun _ => t) a`). Both follow the
  published comparison structure.
- **Levi.** `B.pairingLeviSpan ν` is the subgroup `M_ν(K)` generated by `j_K(Z_K(K))` and
  the root groups `U_b(K)` with `⟨b, ν⟩ = 0`. `B.zeroPairingRoots ν` is the set of those
  `b`.
- **Zero.** The Moy–Prasad groups are formed with the lift's actual torus-centralizer proof
  `L.centralizer_isTorus`, proved unconditionally in `KPUnramifiedApartmentLift`. The
  published comparison Markdown keeps this as its own field, because the Library does not
  yet compare the zero term of the norm-Iwahori filtration of `Z(K)` (used by
  `KPLocalMoyPrasadGroups`) with `Z(K)^0`.
- **Open.** The topology is the exact rational-point topology
  `rationalPointTopology F P.centralizer.Z.X` on `Z(F)`. As in the field `isOpen`, it is
  introduced by local instances: `G` is affine because `G.X.hom` is affine, and `Z` is
  affine because the centralizer inclusion is a closed immersion. The target quantifies
  over every nonnegative depth `r` and uses `L.centralizer_isTorus`, like the zero target.

The 2024 corrections (`kaletha-prasad-2024-corrections`) have two entries on the cited

## The conditional theorem `unramifiedComparisonExistence_of_fields`

```lean
theorem unramifiedComparisonExistence_of_fields
    (hSpec : SpecialTorusExistenceStatement.{u})
    (hDesc : UnramifiedValuationDescentStatement.{u})
    (hRoot : UnramifiedComparisonRootFieldStatement.{u})
    (hLevi : UnramifiedComparisonLeviFieldStatement.{u})
    (hZero : UnramifiedComparisonZeroFieldStatement.{u})
    (hOpen : UnramifiedComparisonOpenFieldStatement.{u}) :
    UnramifiedComparisonExistenceStatement.{u}
```

Its conclusion is the unchanged published target `UnramifiedComparisonExistenceStatement`
(module `KPUnramifiedComparisonExistenceStatement`). It is **only a conditional
assembly**. Its six hypotheses are unproved proposition targets: the two published
constructor premises and the four targets of this module. It proves none of them and
gives no unconditional proof of the comparison existence.

Proof. Given `F`, `G`, `P` and `hP : P.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms`, the proved
`exists_unramifiedApartmentLift hSpec hDesc P` gives a special torus `t` and a lift `L`. The
witnesses are `L.buildingData`, `L.centralizer_isTorus` and `L.e`. The field
`apartmentMap_smul` is `L.normalizer`. The fields `rootComparison`, `levi`, `comap_zero` and
`isOpen` are `hRoot F G P hP t L`, `hLevi F G P hP t L`, `hZero F G P hP t L` and
`hOpen F G P hP t L`.
