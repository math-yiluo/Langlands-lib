# V 6 at Galois-fixed points from Levi transport

Authors: Worker 91 (Lean proof and Markdown); Worker 47 (rechecked against the published
Library and submitted).

Lean module: `Langlands.Geometry.Building.UnramifiedFixedCentralizerReduction`.

This module proves

```
unramifiedFixedCentralizerCompatibility_of_leviTransport :
  UnramifiedFixedLeviTransportStatement.{u} → UnramifiedFixedCentralizerCompatibilityStatement.{u}
```

Both statements are in the Library, in the modules `UnramifiedFixedLeviTransportStatement` and
`UnramifiedFixedLocusStatement`. Neither is proved here. With this implication, the reduction
`unramifiedValuationDescent_of_fixedLocus` of the published `UnramifiedValuationDescentStatement`
needs only the Levi transport statement, V 5 at the fixed points, and
`UnramifiedGaloisFixedApartmentStatement`.

## Packaged statement

The module also defines the internal proposition

```
def UnramifiedFixedCentralizerCompatibilityReductionStatement : Prop :=
  UnramifiedFixedLeviTransportStatement.{u} → UnramifiedFixedCentralizerCompatibilityStatement.{u}
```

and proves it as `unramifiedFixedCentralizerCompatibilityReduction`, which is
`unramifiedFixedCentralizerCompatibility_of_leviTransport`. The proposition only names the
implication, so that it can be submitted as a statement record together with its proof. It
does not assert either side.

## Provenance

The result is derived; it is not a statement of the literature. The source is Kaletha–Prasad,
*Bruhat–Tits theory: a new approach* (2023; registered source
`kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`). Axiom V 6 is the compatibility condition stated
after the list V 0–V 5 in Definition 6.1.2 (printed p. 199 / PDF p. 230):
`φ_a(z⁻¹ u z) = φ_a(u) + a(ν(z))` for `a ∈ Φ` and `z ∈ Z(k)`. The proof of Theorem 9.6.5
(printed pp. 366–367 / PDF pp. 397–398) gets V 6 for the descended valuation from
Proposition 9.3.16 (printed pp. 339–340 / PDF pp. 370–371): `z ∈ Z(k)` acts on
`A(S) = B(G_K)^Γ_S` by the translation `ν(z)`. For `n ∈ N(k)` it uses Lemma 9.4.15 (printed
p. 352 / PDF p. 383), `ψ_{n u n⁻¹} = n · ψ_u`. In this module the published bridge
`KPApartmentData.leviTransport_apartmentMap_torusFieldRestricted` plays that role.
The authors' corrections (version of 1 November 2024, registered source
`kaletha-prasad-2024-corrections`) change none of these four results.
KP's proof uses the building of `G_K`, which the Library does not have. This module replaces
it by an argument inside one apartment of `G_K`. The argument uses the Levi transport statement,
upstairs V 6 for the class `D`, and character theory of `Z = Z_G(S)`.

## `eval_eq_centralizerTranslation_of_leviTransport`

The data above are given, with `z ∈ Z(F)`, a point `x` of the apartment of `D`, and
`h ∈ G(K)` in the subgroup generated by the groups `D.filtration x χ 0`, over the roots `χ` of
`T_K` with `f_K ≫ χ = 0`. Let `w ∈ N_{T_K}(K)` with `w = (CS.j z)_K · h` and
`w • x = push v + x` for some `v ∈ V(S)`. Then every relative root `a` of `S` satisfies
`a(v) = a(ν(z))`.

Proof.

1. `exists_characterRestriction_eq_nsmul` (module `UnramifiedFixedCentralizerAux`) gives a
   rational character `χ` of `Z` and `m > 0` with `χ|_S = m • a`.
2. `T` centralizes `S` (`range_le_torusCentralizer_of_factor`), so `iT` factors as `τ ≫ CS.j`
   (`SubgroupFunctor.IsRepresentedBy.factorHom`), and `f ≫ τ = CS.s`. Set
   `ξ = χ|_T = torusCharacterRestrict τ χ`, so that `f ≫ ξ = m • a`. Set `β'` to be the base
   change of `ξ` to `T_K`. It is fixed by `Aut(K/F)` (`characterLattice.galoisRepresentation_baseChange`), and
   `f_K ≫ β' = m • a_K`.
3. `splittingFieldRootLift` gives a root `b` of `T_K` with `f_K ≫ b = a_K`. Its Galois orbit is
   finite (`finite_range_galoisRepresentation`). Let `β` be the sum `∑ O` of the finite set `O`
   of characters in that orbit. Then `β` lies in the integral span of the roots, is
   Galois-fixed, and satisfies `f_K ≫ β = |O| • a_K`.
4. The character `|O| • β' - m • β` is Galois-fixed and vanishes on `S_K`, hence is zero
   (`IsLargestSplitSubtorus.galoisInvariant_character_eq_zero`, with `unramifiedTorus_structure`).
   Since `β` kills the root annihilator of `T_K` (`eval_eq_zero_of_mem_span`), so does `β'`.
5. The base change `j_K` of `CS.j` represents the centralizer of `S_K`
   (`CentralizerDatum.isRepresentedBy_baseChange`). It is closed, and `Z_K` is smooth
   (`torusCentralizerSmooth`). `exists_map_eq_and_eval_eq_one_of_mem_iSup` gives `m_h ∈ Z(K)`
   with `j_K(m_h) = h` and `χ_K(m_h) = 1`. With `rationalExtend_map`,
   `w = j_K(z_K · m_h)`.
6. `weylAction_torusCharacterRestrict_of_comp` shows that `w⁻¹` fixes
   `β' = torusCharacterRestrict τ_K χ_K` (`characterLattice.baseChange_torusCharacterRestrict`).
7. `exists_pow_mem_centralizerPoints` gives `N > 0` with `w ^ N` centralizing `T_K`, so
   `w ^ N = C.j(s)` for some `s ∈ C.Z(K)`. Upstairs V 6
   (`IntegerValuation.IsCentralizerCompatible.smul_eq_vadd`) gives `w ^ N • x = ν_K(s) + x`.
8. `natCast_mul_eval_eq_of_pow_smul` gives `N · β'(push v) = β'(ν_K(s))`. The left side is
   `N · m · a(v)` (`eval_descentCocharacterMap`, `eval_splitCocharacterBaseChange`).
9. `C.Z` maps to `Z_K` by `κ`, with `κ ≫ j_K = C.j` and `C.s ≫ κ = τ_K`. Hence `β'` is the
   restriction along `C.s` of the character `κ^* χ_K` of `C.Z`, and upstairs
   (`IntegerValuation.eval_centralizerTranslation`)
   `β'(ν_K(s)) = -ord(χ_K(κ(s)))`. Moreover `κ(s) = (z_K · m_h)^N`, because `j_K` is a
   monomorphism. Since `χ_K(m_h) = 1` and `χ_K(z_K)` is the image of `χ(z)`
   (`rationalCharacterEval_extend`), this equals `-N · ord_F(χ(z))`
   (`MaximalUnramified.orderUnits_base_eq_fieldOrderUnits`).
10. Over `F`, `eval_centralizerTranslation` gives `m · a(ν(z)) = -ord_F(χ(z))`. Comparing with
    step 8 and cancelling `N · m > 0` gives `a(v) = a(ν(z))`.

## `unramifiedFixedCentralizerCompatibility_core` and the main theorem

The core theorem proves `IsCentralizerCompatible iS CS.j CS.s CS.bijective (φ x)` for fixed
explicit data and a fixed point `x`. Let `a` be a relative root, `z ∈ Z(F)` and `u ∈ U_a(F)`.
Apply the Levi transport statement to `n = CS.toNormalizer z` and `x`. It gives `h` and
`w = n_K h` with `y = w • x` fixed. The published bridge
`KPApartmentData.leviTransport_apartmentMap_torusFieldRestricted` gives `φ y = n • φ x`.
`unramified_exists_descent_vadd_of_galoisFixed` gives `v ∈ V(S)` with `y = push v + x`, and
`torusFieldRestricted_eq_vadd_of_eq_descent_vadd` turns this into `φ y = v + φ x`. Hence
`n • φ x = v + φ x`. The Weyl action of `n⁻¹` is trivial
(`weylAction_centralizerToNormalizer`), so evaluating at `(a, u)` gives
`φ_a(z⁻¹ u z) = φ_a(u) + a(v)`. The key lemma then replaces `a(v)` by `a(ν(z))`.

`unramifiedFixedCentralizerCompatibility_of_leviTransport` introduces the binders of
`UnramifiedFixedCentralizerCompatibilityStatement`, installs the affine, smooth and
monomorphism instances as that statement does, and applies the core theorem.
