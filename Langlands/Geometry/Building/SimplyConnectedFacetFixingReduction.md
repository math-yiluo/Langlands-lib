# Simply connected facet fixing from the translation-image equality

Author: Worker 4708.

Module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.SimplyConnectedFacetFixingReduction`.

## The statement

`Langlands.Geometry.Building.SimplyConnectedFacetFixingReductionStatement.{u}`
is the proposition

```text
SimplyConnectedReflectionTranslationStatement.{u} →
  SimplyConnectedFacetFixingStatement.{u}
```

Both propositions are published and unchanged.

- `SimplyConnectedReflectionTranslationStatement` (module
  `Geometry.Building.SimplyConnectedReflectionTranslationStatement`). For every
  nonarchimedean local field `F : Type u`, every `G` over `Spec F` with
  `[IsAffineHom G.X.hom]` and every `B : ValuationBuildingData G`,
  `IsSimplyConnected G` implies
  `B.reflectionTranslationLattice = B.centralizerTranslationLattice`.
  The right side is the image of the actual centralizer `Z(F)` under the
  translation map to the reduced translation space. The left side is the image
  of the actual centralizer points whose normalizer images lie in
  `B.reflectionNormalizer`. That is the subgroup generated by the actual
  opposite-pair reflection representatives.
- `SimplyConnectedFacetFixingStatement` (module
  `Geometry.Building.SimplyConnectedFacetFixingStatement`) is the target. With
  the same `F`, `G` and `B`, and `IsSimplyConnected G`, every
  `n : normalizerPoints B.i` that preserves a facet `Fa` of the apartment as a
  set (`AffineRootSystem.IsFacet B.affineRoots Fa`) fixes every point of `Fa`.

The theorem
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.simplyConnectedFacetFixingReduction` proves the
implication for every universe `u`. Its axioms are `propext`,
`Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. No apartment axiom, AS 5, V 7, Tits
system or source-comparison proposition is assumed.

## The proof

The published criterion
`ValuationBuildingData.normalizer_facet_fixing_of_translationLattice_eq`
(module `ReflectionTranslationLattice`) concludes pointwise fixing from three
premises on `B`: actual opposite pairs, `RelativeRoot.HasOppositeFactorPairs B.i`;
surjectivity of `B.reflectionRelativeWeylMap` onto the finite relative Weyl
group; and the lattice equality. This module discharges the first two for
every `B` from published proofs.

1. `ValuationBuildingData.hasOppositeFactorPairs`. The published theorem
   `rootRankOneFactorization : RootRankOneFactorizationStatement`, applied to
   `F`, `G`, `B.isConnectedReductive`, `B.S`, `B.i` and
   `B.isMaximalSplitTorus`, gives a rank-one factorization of each
   nonidentity actual root point. The existence part of the factorization is
   an opposite factor pair.
2. `ValuationBuildingData.hasRelativeRootSystem`. The published theorem
   `relativeRootSystem : RelativeRootSystemStatement`, applied to the same
   data, gives `HasRelativeRootSystem B.i`.
3. `ValuationBuildingData.reflectionRelativeWeylMap_surjective'` combines 1
   and 2 through the published `reflectionRelativeWeylMap_surjective`.
4. `ValuationBuildingData.normalizer_facet_fixing_of_translationLattice_eq'`
   applies the criterion with 1 and 3. Its only remaining premise is the
   lattice equality for `B`.

In steps 1 and 2 the two published statements fix their instances by
`hG.isAffineHom` and `hi.isSplitSubtorus.mono`. The data `B` carry
`[IsAffineHom G.X.hom]` and the instance `Mono B.i.hom.hom.left` from
`mono_of_isMaximalSplitTorus`. Both classes are propositions, so the instances
agree definitionally.

For `simplyConnectedFacetFixingReduction`, take the lattice equality `hL` and
the data of the target. Then `hL F G B hsc` is the lattice equality for `B`,
and step 4 gives the conclusion for the given facet and normalizer point.

## Two corollaries

- `simplyConnectedFacetFixing_of_rankOneCentralizerGeneration` derives the
  target from `SimplyConnectedRankOneCentralizerGenerationStatement`. The
  published `simplyConnectedReflectionTranslation_of_rankOneGeneration`, with
  `rootRankOneFactorization` as its first argument, turns rank-one generation
  into the lattice equality.
- `simplyConnectedFacetFixing_of_kneserTitsCentralizer` derives the target from
  `SimplyConnectedKneserTitsCentralizerStatement`. The published reduction
  `simplyConnectedRankOneCentralizerGeneration_of_kneserTits` turns it into
  rank-one generation. Its other input,
  `RootGeneratedCentralizerRankOneStatement`, is supplied by
  `rootGeneratedCentralizerRankOneReduction rootRankOneFactorization`.

So, once any of these three propositions is proved, the target follows by
applying the matching theorem to that proof. Their proofs are the subjects of
tasks `9d7aa6f8-7ca6-4c5a-9ab4-ee002a03b998` (lattice equality) and
`c291c661-7e71-4122-be6c-0204f3151088` (rank-one generation). The Kneser–Tits
centralizer statement is reduced in the Library, by
`simplyConnectedKneserTitsCentralizer_of_factors`, to its factor statements.

## Provenance

This is an internal reduction, proved in Lean. It adds no assertion beyond
its published input. The paired Markdown of
`SimplyConnectedFacetFixingStatement` records the source route of the target,
through Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023),
paragraph 9.3.26, and the comparisons of §§6.2, 9.4 and 9.6. The route here is
different. Under the lattice equality, the published criterion places every
normalizer point in the reflection subgroup joined with the apartment-action
kernel. The published facet theorem for that subgroup
(`normalizer_facet_fixing_of_reflections_sup_ker_of_oppositePairs`, module
`ActualAffineRootLocalFiniteness`) then gives pointwise fixing.

The `ValuationBuildingData.hasOppositeFactorPairs` helper is shared with
[SimplyConnectedNormalizerAffineWeylAssembly](SimplyConnectedNormalizerAffineWeylAssembly.md)
and imported from that module. The two independently accepted copies had the same
proposition; the combined Library keeps one declaration.
