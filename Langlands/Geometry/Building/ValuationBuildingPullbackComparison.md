# Apartment comparisons for the derived and central-isogeny pullbacks

Module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.ValuationBuildingPullbackComparison`.

The Library's two valuation pullbacks are packaged as apartment comparisons
(`KPApartmentData.Comparison`, module
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.ValuationBuildingQuotientMap`). A comparison of
an apartment class `D₁` of `(G₁, S₁)` with a class `D₂` of `(G₂, S₂)` along
`θ : G₁(k) →* G₂(k)` consists of an affine equivalence `e` of the apartments
together with three properties:

- `e` intertwines the normalizer actions through `θ`;
- `θ` maps each sharp fixer `G₁(k)^♯_x` onto `G₂(k)^♯_{e x}`;
- `e` transports the affine roots of `D₁` onto those of `D₂`.

The sharp fixer `G(k)^♯_x` is `KPApartmentData.sharpFixer {x}`: the subgroup
generated by the root filtration subgroups `U_{a,x,0}` for the relative
roots `a` (KP Definition 7.4.1).

## Derived pullback

Section `Derived` uses the hypotheses of the Library's derived pullback
(module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.DerivedApartment`). These are a derived
inclusion `d : D → G` (`hd : IsDerivedSubgroup d`), split tori `T`, `S`, `A`,
a maximal split torus `i : S → G`, a split torus `j : T → D` (a
monomorphism), maps `e : T → S` and `c : A → S` with `h : j ≫ d = e ≫ i`, a
flat surjective product `T × A → S`, and `c ≫ i` central. Let `P : KPApartmentData i ω` be a class for `(G, S)`, and write
`X := P.derivedPullback e c j i d hd h hT hS hA hc hi`.

- `map_filtration_derivedPullback y a r`: the image under `d` of the
  filtration subgroup of `X` at the point `derivedApartmentEquiv y`, for the
  root `e ≫ a`, is the filtration subgroup `P.filtration y a r`. One
  inclusion is `mem_filtration_derivedPullback_iff`. For the other, a
  point of `U_{a,y,r}` lifts through the root-group isomorphism
  `derivedRootGroupPointsEquiv`, and the lift lies in the source filtration
  by the same lemma.
- `map_sharpFixer_derivedPullback y`: the image under `d` of
  `X.sharpFixer {derivedApartmentEquiv y}` is `P.sharpFixer {y}`. Both sharp
  fixers are suprema over relative roots of single-point root fixers, which
  are filtration subgroups (`rootFixer_singleton`). The image of a supremum
  is the supremum of the images (`Subgroup.map_iSup`). The index sets are
  matched by the relative-root bijection
  `RelativeRoot.derivedRelativeRootEquiv`, `a ↦ e ≫ a`, through
  `Equiv.iSup_comp`. Each term is then the previous lemma at `r = 0`.
- `derivedComparison : Comparison X P (rationalPoints.map d)` has
  `e := (P.derivedApartmentAffineEquiv …).symm`, the inverse of the
  Library's affine point pullback. The normalizer map is
  `DerivedNormalizerMap.normalizerPointsHom`, whose value is
  `rationalPoints.map d n`. The equivariance comes from
  `derivedApartmentEquiv_smul`, the sharp fixers from
  `map_sharpFixer_derivedPullback`, and the roots from
  `transportedRoots_derivedPullback` with `AffineRootSystem.transportedRoots_symm`.
- `derivedComparison_e_symm_apply`: `e.symm` is `derivedApartmentEquiv`
  (by definition).

## Central-isogeny pullback

Section `CentralIsogeny` uses the hypotheses of the Library's central-isogeny
pullback (module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.CentralIsogenyApartment`). These
are a central isogeny `q : C → G` and split tori `i : S → C`, `j : T → G`
(monomorphisms). They are related by `t : S → T` with `i ≫ q = t ≫ j` and
`t` an isogeny. Let `D : KPApartmentData j ω`.

- `map_filtration_centralIsogenyPullback` and
  `map_sharpFixer_centralIsogenyPullback` are the same two statements for
  `D.centralIsogenyPullback q hq hS hT t h ht`, the map `q`, the point map
  `centralIsogenyApartmentEquiv` and the roots `t ≫ a`. Their proofs use
  `mem_filtration_centralIsogenyPullback_iff`,
  `centralIsogenyRootGroupPointsEquiv` and the relative-root bijection
  `centralIsogenyRootPullbackEquiv`.
- `centralIsogenyComparison :
  Comparison (D.centralIsogenyPullback …) D (rationalPoints.map q)` has `e`
  the inverse of `centralIsogenyApartmentAffineEquiv`. The normalizer map is
  `splitTorusIsogenyNormalizerMap`, with equivariance from
  `centralIsogenyApartmentEquiv_smul` and roots from
  `transportedRoots_centralIsogenyPullback`.
- `centralIsogenyComparison_e_symm_apply`: `e.symm` is
  `centralIsogenyApartmentEquiv`.

## Provenance

This is internal. It repackages the Library's derived and central-isogeny
pullbacks for the comparison of reduced buildings along `G_der → G` and
`G_sc → G_der` (Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach*,
§7.6 and (7.6.1), printed p.264). KP obtain those bijections from Tits
systems (Lemma 1.4.13). The Library instead compares the valuation quotients
(7.6.2) directly.
