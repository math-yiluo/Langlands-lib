# Existence of algebraic SU3 rank-one coordinates at multipliable roots: statement

Worker 163, for Task 6836 (8c04a3ed-3fa6-4680-a59f-856738cb578b).

`Langlands.Geometry.Building.AlgebraicSU3RootCoordinatesExistenceStatement.{u}`
is a proposition definition. This module does not prove it.

## The statement

The hypotheses are exactly those of
`AlgebraicRankOneCoordinatesExistenceStatement`:

- `k : Type u` is any field, including imperfect fields and fields of
  characteristic two;
- `G` is a group scheme over `Spec k` with `IsConnectedReductive G`. The `letI`
  line only supplies the affine structure-morphism instance that
  `relativeRoots`, `RelativeRoot.indivisibleRoots` and `normalizerPoints` use;
- `S` and `Z` are commutative group schemes over `Spec k`, with morphisms
  `i : S.toGrp ⟶ G`, `j : Z.toGrp ⟶ G` and `s : S.toGrp ⟶ Z.toGrp`;
- `i` is a maximal split torus (`IsMaximalSplitTorus i`);
- `Z.toGrp` is a torus (`IsTorus Z.toGrp`);
- `j` represents the whole subgroup functor `torusCentralizer i`;
- `s ≫ j = i`.

Let `b` be an indivisible relative root of `i` such that `2 • b` is also a
relative root, i.e. `b` is multipliable. The proposition asserts that there is a
record `C : AlgebraicSU3RootCoordinates i j s b` such that `C.rho` maps the
Library's standard SU3 Weyl element `SU3Root.weylElement` into
`normalizerPoints i`. Here `SU3Root.weylElement` is the Gram matrix
`[[0,0,1],[0,-1,0],[1,0,0]]` bundled with its proof of membership in the special
unitary group of `C.extension / C.base`.

`AlgebraicSU3RootCoordinates` is defined in `AlgebraicRankOneCoordinates`. It
contains:

- a finite separable field `base` over `k` and a separable quadratic extension
  `extension` of `base`;
- unitary root coordinates for `b` and for `-b` on that tower;
- a group homomorphism `rho` from the special unitary group to `G(k)`, agreeing
  with those coordinates on the upper and lower unipotent embeddings;
- a morphism `coroot` from the Weil restriction of `𝔾ₘ` along
  `extension / k` to `Z`, whose rational points are the diagonal elements
  `rho (SU3Root.diagonal t)`.

`normalizerPoints i` asks that conjugation induce an automorphism of the
split torus `S` as a scheme, not only of its rational points.

## Relation to the target of Task 6836

`AlgebraicRankOneCoordinatesExistenceStatement` asserts, for each indivisible
`b`, an SL2 clause (when `2 • b` is not a relative root) and an SU3 clause
(when `2 • b` is a relative root). This proposition is the SU3 clause
verbatim, with the same binders, record, Weyl element and normalizer
condition. Together with a proof of the SL2 clause, a proof of this proposition
proves the target by `constructor`. Task 6836 splits the clause off so that
it can be proved independently.

## Source

Tasho Kaletha and Gopal Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023),
source ID `kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`, SHA-256
`05ad9a284a2577b34d1a270228b74dfb28f520561ae7772d8f38dddb78938732`.
In the proof of Proposition 7.1.1 for quasi-split `G` (printed p. 239, PDF
p. 270):

> First, we may replace G by the group generated by T and the root groups for
> all rational multiples of a. Next we may replace G by G_sc. Now G is the
> restriction of scalars of either SL2 or SU3 and we may assume that G is SL2 or
> SU3.

When `2a` is a root, this is the SU3 case. The record makes explicit the
homomorphism from the special unitary group of the quadratic tower into `G(k)`
that this reduction implicitly uses, together with its compatibility with the
root coordinates and the torus.

## Provenance

Derived, and internal in its packaging. The source gives the reduction to
`R_{base/k} SU3` inside a proof, over a quasi-split group, with valuations in
view. This proposition keeps only the algebraic content, over an arbitrary
field, with the supplied centralizer presentation `(Z, j, s)`. It packages the
data as the Library's `AlgebraicSU3RootCoordinates` record and adds the Weyl
normalization. Like the parent target, it does not claim that the source
prints this exact proposition. The registered corrections
`kaletha-prasad-2024-corrections` (PDF p. 33, adding AS 5) concern the
building hypotheses of Chapter 7 and do not affect this valuation-free
statement.
