# The translation part of AS 5 from simple-root transitivity

This internal unit proves

`valuationBuildingQuasiSplitAdjointTranslation_of_simpleRootTransitivity :
  QuasiSplitAdjointSimpleRootTransitivityStatement.{u} →
    ValuationBuildingQuasiSplitAdjointTranslationStatement.{u}`.

So the translation part of AS 5 for quasi-split adjoint groups follows from the transitivity
of `Z(k)` on simple root groups. It uses no other unproved proposition. The axioms used are
`propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. The proof follows the translation part of
Kaletha–Prasad, Proposition 6.6.2 and Lemma 6.4.10 (printed pp.227–232, PDF pp.258–263).
The source computes the image of `ν` explicitly. The Lean proof instead realizes each
translation directly from the jumps of the root groups.

Throughout, `B` is a `ValuationBuildingData G` over a nonarchimedean local field `F`. Its
maximal split torus is `i : S → G`, `j : Z → G` represents the centralizer, `ν` is
`B.translation`, and `x_a` is the valuation of `U_a(F)` attached to a point `x` of the
apartment.

## Helper lemmas

- `centralizer_conj_mem_rootGroup`, `centralizer_conj_mem_doubleRootGroup` and their
  `inv` variants: conjugation by `j(z)`, `z ∈ Z(F)`, preserves `U_a(F)` and `U_{2a}(F)`.
  This is the Library's `conj_mem_rootGroupPoints` for the image of `z` in `N(F)`, whose Weyl
  action is trivial.
- `val_centralizer_conj`: axiom V 6 (`B.isOmegaCompatible`) at a point `x`:
  `x_a(j(z)⁻¹ u j(z)) = x_a(u) + a(ν(z))`.
- `IsRootJump x a r` (a definition): some `u ∈ U_a(F)` has `r ≤ x_a(u)`, and
  `u ≠ p · w` for all `p ∈ U_a(F)` with `r < x_a(p)` and all `w ∈ U_{2a}(F)`. That is, `r`
  is a jump of `U_a/U_{2a}` at `x`.
- `isRootJump_of_mem_affineRoots`: if `ψ ∈ Ψ` (`B.affineRoots`) has gradient
  `rootForm α`, then `ψ(x)` is a jump of `U_α` at `x`. The root `β` with
  `ψ ∈ Ψ` is equal to `α` by `rootForm_injective_of_split`. A point of `U_{ψ}` outside
  `U_{ψ+} · U_{2α}` is a witness, by `mem_affineRootGroup_iff` and
  `mem_affineRootGroupPlus_iff`. The Library's `affineRoots` follows KP Definition 6.3.4,
  which requires `U_ψ ⊈ U_{ψ+} · U_{2α}(F)`.
- `not_mem_doubleRootGroup_of_isRootJump_witness`: a witness of a jump is not in
  `U_{2a}(F)`. Otherwise it equals `1 · u` with `x_a(1) = ⊤ > r`.
- `exists_eval_translation_of_isRootJump`: assume the transitivity clause at `a`, and let
  `r, s` be jumps of `U_a` at `x`. Then some `z ∈ Z(F)` has `a(ν(z)) = r - s` and
  `b(ν(z)) = 0` for every other `b ∈ Δ`. Proof: take witnesses `u` for `r` and `u'` for
  `s`. Transitivity gives `z` with `j(z) u j(z)⁻¹ = u' w`, `w ∈ U_{2a}(F)`, and with `j(z)`
  centralizing `U_b(F)` for the other `b ∈ Δ`.
  - For `b ≠ a`, pick `y ∈ U_b(F)` with `x_b(y) ≠ ⊤`. Conjugation fixes `y`, so V 6 gives
    `x_b(y) = x_b(y) + b(ν(z))`, hence `b(ν(z)) = 0`.
  - Let `c = a(ν(z))`. By V 6, `x_a(u) = x_a(u' w) + c` and
    `x_a(j(z)⁻¹ u' j(z)) = x_a(u') + c`. If `r - c > s`, then `u' = (u' w) w⁻¹` with
    `x_a(u' w) > s`, contradicting the jump at `s`. If `s > r - c`, then
    `u = (j(z)⁻¹ u' j(z)) (j(z)⁻¹ w j(z))` with the first factor of value `> r` and the
    second in `U_{2a}(F)`, contradicting the jump at `r`. So `c = r - s`.

## The reduction

Let `v` be a translation of the apartment that preserves `Ψ`, and lift it to `w ∈ V(S)`.
Apply the transitivity proposition to `S`, `i`, `Z` and `j` to obtain `Δ`. Fix a point `x`
of the apartment.

1. For each `a ∈ Δ`: axiom V 7 (`B.isTopologyCompatible`) and
   `derivativeRoots_affineRoots_of_isTopologyCompatible` give `ψ ∈ Ψ` with gradient
   `rootForm a`. Since translation by `v` preserves `Ψ` (`mem_rootStabilizer`),
   `ψ ∘ (v + ·)` is in `Ψ`. It has the same gradient, and its value at `x` is
   `ψ(x) + a(w)`. Both values are jumps of `U_a` at `x`, so the previous lemma gives `z_a`
   with `a(ν(z_a)) = a(w)` and `b(ν(z_a)) = 0` for the other `b ∈ Δ`.
2. By induction on finite subsets `s ⊆ Δ`, some `z` has `b(ν(z)) = b(w)` for `b ∈ s` and
   `b(ν(z)) = 0` for `b ∈ Δ ∖ s`. The induction step multiplies by `z_a`. For `s = Δ`,
   `b(ν(z)) = b(w)` for every `b ∈ Δ`.
3. By induction on the additive closure, `b(ν(z)) = b(w)` for every `b` in the subgroup
   generated by `Δ`, which contains every relative root.
4. So `ν(z) - w` lies in the root annihilator (`mem_rootAnnihilator`), and the class of
   `ν(z)` in the translation space is `v`.

## Scope

The transitivity proposition remains an explicit premise. This unit does not prove it. It
also does not prove the first sentence of AS 5 or the apartment axioms.
