# Proof of the filtered rank-one two-cell decomposition (KP Lemma 7.1.5(3))

`affineRankOneTwoCell` proves the published proposition
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.AffineRankOneTwoCellStatement.{u}` exactly, at
arbitrary universe `u`. Fix a nonarchimedean local field `F`, a group scheme
`G` over `F` with affine structure map, and `P : ParahoricBuildingData G` whose
apartment satisfies `P.apartment.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms P.centralizer`. Let
`α` be a relative root and `ψ` an affine functional on the apartment with
derivative `α` and `U_{ψ+} ≠ U_ψ` for the actual affine root groups. The
theorem gives a normalizer point `m` in the subgroup `L` generated by `U_ψ`,
`U_{-ψ}` and the mapped `Z(F)^0`. This `m` acts on the apartment as
`AffineRootSystem.affineReflection ψ` for every `IsCoroot P.affineRoots` vector
of `ψ`, and `L = U_{-ψ+} Z(F)^0 U_ψ ∪ U_ψ m Z(F)^0 U_ψ` as sets. This is
Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023), Lemma 7.1.5(3)
(printed pp. 243–244), for the actual data of `P`.

## Implemented proof

The proof applies the published conditional theorem
`ParahoricBuildingData.exists_affineRankOneTwoCell_of_coverImage_factorization`
(module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.AffineRankOneTwoCellConditional`). Given the
apartment axioms, the root derivative condition and the jump, that theorem
yields exactly the existential conclusion of the target. It needs two further
inputs, both of which this module proves from published Library theorems.

1. **Actual rank-one factorization** (KP Proposition 2.11.17),
   `RelativeRoot.HasRankOneFactorization P.i`. It is the published theorem
   `rootRankOneFactorization : RootRankOneFactorizationStatement.{u}`,
   specialized to `F`, `G`, the connected reductivity `P.isConnectedReductive`
   and the maximal split torus `P.i` with `P.isMaximalSplitTorus`. These are
   fields of the building data.

2. **The cover-image comparison** from the proof of KP Corollary 2.6.28,
   `P.zeroDatum.CoverImageCentralizerNormKernelStatement`, i.e.
   `G(F)^♮ ∩ Z(F)^1 ⊆ Z(F)^0` for the actual zero datum of `P`. It is the
   published conditional theorem
   `ZeroSubgroupDatum.coverImageCentralizerNormKernel_of_inputs` (module
   `Langlands.Geometry.Building.KPCoverImageReduction`). Its five structural
   hypotheses are supplied as follows:
   - `CocenterQuotientExistenceStatement` by `cocenterQuotientExistence`.
   - `SplitTorusCentralizerCocenterInducedStatement` by
     `splitTorusCentralizerCocenterInduced_of_inputs`, applied to
     `dynamicCentralizerReductive`, `cocenterQuotientExistence`,
     `isSimplyConnectedFieldExtension`, `splitSimplyConnectedCorootSpan` and
     `SplitTorusCentralizerCocenterRootModelStatement`. The last input comes
     from `splitTorusCentralizerCocenterRootModel_of_structuralInputs`,
     applied to `rootDatumExistence`, `derivedSubgroupSemisimple`,
     `derivedMaximalTorusProductSurjectivity` and
     `derivedMaximalTorusIntersection`.
   - `InducedTorusIwahoriStatement` (KP Lemma 2.5.18) by `inducedTorusIwahori`.
   - `SplitTorusCentralizerCoverPreimageStatement` by

adds `affineRankOneTwoCell` as its only declaration.

## What the applied conditional theorem does

The imported conditional theorem follows the route of KP's proof. The witness
`m` is `affineRankOneReflectionAtJump`, the product `u′ u u″` for a point
`u ∈ U_ψ ∖ U_{ψ+}` and its opposite factor pair. The valuation axiom puts `u′`
and `u″` in `U_{-ψ}`, so `m ∈ L`. Under the apartment axioms `m` acts as the
reflection of `ψ`. The base cell `U_{-ψ+} Z(F)^0 U_ψ` is a subgroup
(`coe_affineRankOneBaseSubgroup`). This uses the centralizer-zero exchange of
KP Proposition 7.1.1(1), where the cover-image comparison puts the
`Z`-component in `Z(F)^0`. The point `m` normalizes the mapped `Z(F)^0`, and
the generic `FilteredRankOne.coe_sup_eq_twoCell` turns these facts into the
cell equality. That theorem and its imports carry these arguments; this module
only supplies their remaining inputs.

No hypothesis is added to the published statement. The theorem depends only
on the axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.
