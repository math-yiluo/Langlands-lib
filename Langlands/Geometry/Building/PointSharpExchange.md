# The sharp fixer of an arbitrary apartment point

This derived, internal unit proves, for `ValuationBuildingData` and a point
`x` of the apartment that need not lie in a chamber, the inclusion

`G(F)^♯_x ⊆ X_x(Φ_{-μ}) · X_x(Φ_μ) · H_x`

for every generic cocharacter `μ`. Here `X_x(P)` is the subgroup generated by
the root filtration groups `U_{b,x,0}` for `b ∈ P`, and `H_x` is the subgroup
of elements of `N(F)^♯_x = N(F) ∩ G(F)^♯_x` that fix `x`. This is the range
part of Kaletha–Prasad Proposition 7.3.12(1) for the zero concave function at
an arbitrary point, with `H_x` in place of the group `N′` of the book. It is a
step towards Kaletha–Prasad Corollary 7.3.14 for a single point
(`SharpFixerBoundednessStatement`): the remaining steps bound the two root
factors and `H_x`.

Two inputs are hypotheses and are not proved here:

- the apartment axioms `B.apartment.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms B.centralizer`
  (V 6 and V 7 of Kaletha–Prasad Definition 6.1.2, AS 2–AS 5 of Axiom 4.1.6);
  the proof uses only the AS 2 fields;
- opposite factor pairs, `RelativeRoot.HasRankOneFactorization B.i` (the
  conclusion of Kaletha–Prasad Proposition 2.11.17,
  `RootRankOneFactorizationStatement`).

AS 1 of Kaletha–Prasad Axiom 4.1.6 for the actual elements `m(u)`,
`B.HasRootReflections` (Kaletha–Prasad Proposition 6.2.1), is not a
hypothesis: it is supplied for every `B` by the Library theorem
`RootReflectionProof.hasRootReflections` (module
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.RootReflectionProof`, which also proves
`rootReflection : RootReflectionStatement`).

## Definitions

`KPApartmentData.sharpNormalizerStabilizer x` (written `H_x` below), for
apartment data `D : KPApartmentData i ω` and a point `x`, is the subgroup of
`G(k)` given by

`H_x = G(k)^♯_x ⊓ { n ∈ N(k) | n · x = x }`,

where `G(k)^♯_x` is `D.sharpFixer {x}` and the second subgroup is the image in
`G(k)` of the stabilizer of `x` under the action of `N(k) = normalizerPoints i`
on the apartment. So `H_x` is the set of elements of
`N(k)^♯_x = N(k) ∩ G(k)^♯_x` that fix `x`. By Kaletha–Prasad
Proposition 7.3.13 this is all of `N(k)^♯_x`. That equality is neither used nor
proved here: the subgroup is defined directly so that the third factor of the
exchange is visibly a subgroup of `N(k)^♯_x` fixing `x`.

`PointSharpExchangeStatement` is the Prop-valued entry definition: for every
nonarchimedean local field `F`, group scheme `G` over `F` with affine
structure morphism and building data `B : ValuationBuildingData G` that
satisfies the apartment axioms and has opposite factor pairs, for every
point `x : B.Apartment` and every cocharacter `mu` with
`IsGenericCocharacter B.i mu`,

`(B.sharpFixer {x} : Set G(F)) ⊆ X⁻ · X⁺ · H_x`,

where `X⁻ = ⨆ b : positiveRelativeRoots B.i (-mu), B.apartment.chamberRootGroup x false b`,
`X⁺ = ⨆ b : positiveRelativeRoots B.i mu, B.apartment.chamberRootGroup x false b`
(`chamberRootGroup x false b` is `U_{b,x,0} = B.filtration x b 0`) and
`H_x = B.apartment.sharpNormalizerStabilizer x`. The products are products of
subsets of `G(F)`.

## Statements

- `mem_sharpNormalizerStabilizer`: `g ∈ H_x` if and only if
  `g ∈ G(k)^♯_x`, `g ∈ N(k)` and `g · x = x`.
- `sharpNormalizerStabilizer_le_sharpNormalizer`:
  `H_x ≤ D.sharpNormalizer {x}`, that is `H_x ⊆ N(k)^♯_x`.
- `smul_eq_self_of_mem_centralizerPoints`: if `n ∈ N(k)` lies in
  `Z(k) = centralizerPoints i` and fixes one point `y` of the apartment, then it
  fixes every point `x`.
- `ValuationBuildingData.exists_rankOne_factorization_fixing`: assume opposite
  factor pairs. Let `x` be a point of the apartment, `c` and `-c`
  relative roots, `v ∈ U_c(F) ∖ {1}`, `v′ ∈ U_{-c}(F) ∖ {1}`,
  `x_c(v) = t`, `x_{-c}(v′) = t′` with `t + t′ > 0`. Then there are
  `x′ ∈ U_{-c}(F)`, `z ∈ Z(F)` and `y ∈ U_c(F)` with `v v′ = x′ z y`,
  `x_{-c}(x′) ≥ t′`, `x_c(y) ≥ t`, `z ∈ N(F)` and `z · x = x`. This is the
  rank-one part of Kaletha–Prasad Proposition 7.1.1(1), including the property
  that `z` fixes `x`.
- `ValuationBuildingData.filtration_neg_mul_filtration_subset_sharpNormalizerStabilizer`:
  assume opposite factor pairs. For every point `x` of the apartment and
  every relative root `a`,

  `U_{-a,x,0} · U_{a,x,0} ⊆ U_{a,x,0} · U_{-a,x,0} · H_x`

  as sets, where `U_{b,x,0}` is `B.filtration x b 0` and `H_x` is
  `B.apartment.sharpNormalizerStabilizer x`.
- `ValuationBuildingData.sharpFixer_subset_mul_sharpNormalizerStabilizer`:
  assume the apartment axioms and opposite factor pairs. For every point `x`
  and every generic cocharacter `mu`, `G(F)^♯_x ⊆ X⁻ · X⁺ · H_x` as in
  `PointSharpExchangeStatement`.
- `pointSharpExchange : PointSharpExchangeStatement`, at every universe `u`.

## Proof

*Centralizer points.* An element `n ∈ Z(k)` acts trivially on characters
(`normalizerAction_eq_one_of_mem_centralizerPoints`), so its action on the
translation space is the identity. By `smul_vadd`,
`n · x = n · ((x - y) + y) = (x - y) + n · y`, which equals `x` when
`n · y = y`.

*Factorization with `z` fixing `x`.* The witnesses are those of
`exists_rankOne_factorization` (from `ChamberRankOne`), reconstructed here so
that their form is available. Let `(p₁, p₂)` be an opposite pair of `v′` and
`m = p₁ v′ p₂`; let `w = v p₁⁻¹`, which has `x_c(w) = -t′` and is not `1`; let
`(q₁, q₂)` be an opposite pair of `w` and `n = q₁ w q₂`. Then `x′ = q₁⁻¹`,
`z = n m` and `y = m⁻¹ q₂⁻¹ m p₂⁻¹`. The facts `v v′ = x′ z y`,
`z ∈ Z(F)`, `x′ ∈ U_{-c}(F)`, `y ∈ U_c(F)` and the two depth bounds are proved
as in `ChamberRankOne` (`weylAction_mul_factorPairs_eq_one`,
`le_val_of_mul_eq`).

For `z · x = x`, write `m · x = w_m + x` (`exists_smul_val_eq_vadd`) and put
`y₀ = ½ w_m + x`. By `eval_translation_eq_of_factor`,
`⟨-c, w_m⟩ = -2t′`, so `y₀_{-c}(v′) = t′ + ½(-2t′) = 0` and
`y₀_c(w) = -t′ + ½(2t′) = 0`. Thus `y₀` lies on both hyperplanes `H_{v′}` and
`H_w`, and AS 1 (`RootReflectionProof.hasRootReflections B`) gives
`m · y₀ = y₀` and `n · y₀ = y₀`. Hence `z = n m` fixes
`y₀`, and since `z ∈ Z(F)` it fixes `x`.

*Rank-one exchange.* Let `v ∈ U_{-a,x,0}` and `v′ ∈ U_{a,x,0}`. The cases
`v = 1` or `v′ = 1` are immediate. Otherwise `t = x_{-a}(v) ≥ 0` and
`t′ = x_a(v′) ≥ 0`; `-a` is a relative root by
`neg_mem_relativeRoots_of_rankOne`.

- If `t > 0`, the factorization with `c = -a` gives `v v′ = x′ z y` with
  `x′ ∈ U_{a,x,0}`, `y ∈ U_{-a,x,0}` and `z ∈ Z(F)` fixing `x`. Write
  `v v′ = x′ (z y z⁻¹) z`. Since `z` fixes `x` and acts trivially on
  characters, `conj_mem_filtration` gives `z y z⁻¹ ∈ U_{-a,x,0}`. Since
  `z = x′⁻¹ (v v′) y⁻¹` is a product of elements of root filtration groups at
  `x`, `filtration_le_sharpFixer` gives `z ∈ G(F)^♯_x`. So `z ∈ H_x`.
- If `t = 0`, let `(q₁, q₂)` be an opposite pair of `v` and `n = q₁ v q₂`.
  By V 5 (`opposite`, `opposite_snd`), `x_a(q₁) = x_a(q₂) = 0`, so
  `q₁, q₂ ∈ U_{a,x,0}` and `n ∈ G(F)^♯_x`. Since `x_{-a}(v) = 0`, the point `x`
  lies on `H_v`, so AS 1 (`RootReflectionProof.hasRootReflections B`) gives
  `n · x = x`, and `n ∈ H_x`. The element `n`
  negates `a` (`IsOppositeFactorPair.weylAction_eq_neg`), so
  `conj_mem_filtration` gives `n (q₂⁻¹ v′) n⁻¹ ∈ U_{-a,x,0}`. The group
  identity `v v′ = q₁⁻¹ · (n (q₂⁻¹ v′) n⁻¹) · n` finishes the proof.

The finiteness of `Φ` and the spanning condition used by
`weylAction_mul_factorPairs_eq_one` are proved unconditionally from the data
(`IsSplitTorus.finite_relativeRoots`, `relativeRootsRestrictedCharactersSpan`).

*The whole sharp fixer.* `sharpFixer_subset_mul_sharpNormalizerStabilizer`
applies `KPApartmentData.sharpFixer_subset_mul_of_exchange` of
`ChamberExchange` (Worker 1536) to `D = B.apartment` and the subgroup
`H = H_x`. Its inputs are supplied as follows: `S` is a split torus
(`B.isMaximalSplitTorus`); `Φ` is finite (`IsSplitTorus.finite_relativeRoots`,
using the smoothness of `G` from `B.isConnectedReductive`); `Ψ′` is an affine
root system with derivative `Φ` (the AS 2 fields
`isAffineRootSystem_extendedAffineRoots` and
`derivativeRoots_extendedAffineRoots` of the apartment axioms); `Φ = -Φ`
(`neg_mem_relativeRoots_of_rankOne`); and the per-root exchange hypothesis is
the rank-one exchange above. `pointSharpExchange` unfolds the entry
definition and applies this theorem.

## Provenance

The factorization and its depth bounds are the construction in the proof of
Kaletha–Prasad Proposition 7.1.1(1) (printed pp. 237–239, PDF pp. 268–270), as
formalized by Worker 1536 in `ChamberRankOne`. In the book, the fact that `z`
fixes `x` comes from `z = m(w) m(v′)` being a product of two reflections
through the same hyperplane. Here the Lean proof instead finds an explicit
common point `y₀` of the two hyperplanes and uses that a centralizer point acts
by a translation.

The rank-one exchange is the rank-one input of the proof of Kaletha–Prasad
Proposition 7.3.12 (printed pp. 250–251, PDF pp. 281–282), which is stated
there for every point `x ∈ A`. In the book this input is Lemma 7.3.11(3)
(printed p. 249, PDF p. 280), `G(k)^(a)_{x,f} = U_{-a,x,f} · U_{a,x,f} · N^(a)_{x,f}`,
proved from Lemma 7.1.5(1) and (3) (printed p. 243, PDF p. 274), together
with the fact from Corollary 7.3.13 (printed p. 251, PDF p. 282) that
`N^(a)_{x,f}` fixes `x` when `f ≥ 0`. This unit proves only the consequence
used in the proof of Proposition 7.3.12, for `f = 0` and one root at a time,
with `H_x` in place of `N^(a)_{x,0}`. The case split `t > 0` / `t = 0` and the
choice of `H_x` are internal to this formalization.

The passage from the rank-one exchange to the whole sharp fixer is the
argument in the proof of Proposition 7.3.12 (printed p. 250, PDF p. 281): the
set `X⁻ · X⁺ · N′` does not depend on the positive system, is a subgroup and
contains every `U_{a,x,f}`, hence equals `G(k)^♯_{x,f}`. That argument is
formalized in `ChamberExchange`. Only the inclusion `⊆` with `f = 0` is used
here, so the Bruhat decomposition, which the book uses to identify the factors
with `U^±_{x,f}` and `N^♯_{x,f}`, is not needed. With `H_x` in place of the
book's `N′ = ⟨N^(a)_{x,f}⟩`, the third factor is by definition a subgroup of
`N(F)^♯_x` fixing `x`; in the book that property of `N^♯_{x,f}` is
Corollary 7.3.13 (printed p. 251, PDF p. 282), which itself rests on
Proposition 7.3.12(2) and thus on the Bruhat decomposition. The inclusion is
used for Corollary 7.3.14 (printed p. 251, PDF p. 282), whose proof bounds
the root groups and the image of `N^♯_x` in `W_aff`.
