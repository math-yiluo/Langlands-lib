# KP Proposition 13.2.5(3) on supplied data

This module defines one ordered multiplication map and states, as the Prop-valued
`def` `Langlands.Geometry.Building.MoyPrasadProductStatement`, that it is a bijection onto
the Moy–Prasad subgroup at positive depth. Nothing in the module proves the statement.
It extends the published module `KPMoyPrasadFiltration` and the four statements of
`KPMoyPrasadFiltrationStatement`, which leave part (3) of Proposition 13.2.5 unstated.

## The ordered product

`ParahoricBuildingData.CentralizerFiltration.orderedMoyPrasadProduct Zf x r aneg apos`
takes the following data:

- a supplied filtration `Zf : P.CentralizerFiltration` of `Z(F)`;
- an apartment point `x`;
- a depth `r : ℝ≥0`;
- two finite lists of characters, `aneg` and `apos`.

On a triple `(u, z, v)` it returns
`orderedFiltrationBlockProduct x (const r) aneg u * j(z) * orderedFiltrationBlockProduct x (const r) apos v`.
Here `u j` and `v j` are elements of the published blocks
`P.apartment.filtrationBlock x (fun _ => r) a = U_{a,x,r} U_{2a,x,r}`, the blocks of KP
Definition 7.3.3(1) for the constant concave function `r`. The second factor is trivial when
`2a` is not a root. `z` is an element of `Zf.filtration r = Z(F)_r` and `j` is the actual
centralizer inclusion. The products are taken in the listed orders
(`orderedFiltrationBlockProduct` multiplies with `List.ofFn`). The blocks are sets of group
elements, not pairs of root-group coordinates. The definition builds in no sign,
completeness or bijectivity condition.

## The statement

`MoyPrasadProductStatement` is universe polymorphic. It covers every nonarchimedean local
field `F : Type u`, in equal and mixed characteristic, and every `G` over `Spec F` with
`[IsAffineHom G.X.hom]`. It covers all `P : ParahoricBuildingData G` and
`Zf : P.CentralizerFiltration` with `P.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms` (V 7 and AS 2–AS 5; V 6 is
in the data) and `Zf.SatisfiesFiltrationAxioms`. These are the premises of the published
Moy–Prasad statements. For every apartment point `x` and every `r : ℝ≥0` with `0 < r`, it
fixes a positive system: a cocharacter `ν` with the published
`IsGenericCocharacter P.i ν`. It then takes injective lists `aneg` and `apos` whose ranges
are exactly the published `RelativeRoot.indivisiblePositiveRoots P.i (-ν)` and
`RelativeRoot.indivisiblePositiveRoots P.i ν`. For all of these, the statement asserts:

1. `Zf.orderedMoyPrasadProduct x r aneg apos` is injective;
2. its range is the set of elements of `Zf.moyPrasad x r = P_{x,r}`, the subgroup generated
   by `j(Z(F)_r)` and the blocks `U_{a,x,r} U_{2a,x,r}` (KP Definition 13.2.1).

The lists encode the source's "arbitrary fixed order", and `ν` its "any system of positive
roots". Positive systems of the relative root system are exactly those cut out by generic
cocharacters. The same encoding is used by the published chamber Iwahori statements
(`ChamberIwahoriStatement`).

### Root index

The printed Proposition 13.2.5(3) takes its products over all positive and negative roots.
Its cited Proposition 7.3.12(5) uses the indivisible roots. By V 4,
`U_{a,x,r} ∩ U_{2a}(k) = U_{2a,x,2r}`, so products over all roots with independent
coordinates for `a` and `2a` cannot be injective whenever that group is nontrivial. The
program's source ledger records this discrepancy in its Bundle E notes ("The printed
root-index discrepancy in Proposition 13.2.5"). The statement follows the indivisible-root
form of 7.3.12(5) with the `2a`-factors inside the blocks. This is a derived reading, not an
authors' erratum.

### Central factor

The middle factor of Proposition 7.3.12(5) is `Z♯_{x,r}`, and its target is the
root-generated group `G(k)♯_{x,r}`. Neither is the factor or the target of
Proposition 13.2.5(3), and `Z♯_{x,r}` is in general smaller than `Z(k)_r`. For `G = G_m` there
are no roots, so `G(k)♯_{x,r} = Z♯_{x,r} = {1}`, while `Z(k)_r = P_{x,r}` is a nontrivial
congruence subgroup (for example `1 + p Z_p` at `r = 1` over `Q_p`). The statement uses the
full filtration `Z(F)_r`, as Proposition 13.2.5(3) prints. The passage from 7.3.12(5) to this
form is derived as follows, for the constant function `r > 0`; KP do not write it out.

1. Parts (4) and (5) of Proposition 7.3.12 give
   `G(k)♯_{x,r} = X^- · Z♯_{x,r} · X^+`, where `X^±` is the ordered product of the blocks
   `U_{a,x,r}` over `a ∈ Φ^{±,nd}`.
2. The proof of Lemma 7.3.16 gives `Z♯_{x,r} ⊆ Z(k)_r`.
3. `Z(k)_r ⊆ Z(k)_0` lies in the kernel of `ν`, so by V 6 its elements normalize every
   `U_{a,x,r}`, hence `X^+`.
4. By Definition 7.3.3(3), `P_{x,r} = G(k)♯_{x,r} · Z(k)_r = X^- · Z♯_{x,r} · X^+ · Z(k)_r`.
   By steps 2 and 3 this equals `X^- · Z(k)_r · X^+`, which is surjectivity.
5. Injectivity: the multiplication `U^-(k) × Z(k) × U^+(k) → G(k)` is injective (the open
   cell, Proposition 2.11.3(2)). The ordered block products are injective by
   Proposition 7.3.12(3).

The central factor of the resulting decomposition is `Z(k) ∩ P_{x,r} = Z(k)_r`, the
full-group equality of Lemma 7.3.16. The statement does not identify `Z♯_{x,r}` with `Z(k)_r`.

## Provenance and scope

The provenance is derived: the indivisible-root blocks and the passage from the sharp
decomposition of Proposition 7.3.12(5) to the full filtration are explained above. The data
are supplied, as in the published Moy–Prasad statements: `P` satisfies the apartment axioms,
and `Zf` is a supplied filtration with the four explicit properties. Its existence is the
separate target `CentralizerFiltrationExistenceStatement`. In the Lean statement the
`Z`-factor is the image `j(Z(F)_r)` of the supplied filtration under the actual centralizer inclusion, and
`Zf.moyPrasad x r` is the subgroup generated by that image and the blocks. KP work over
Henselian discretely valued fields; `F` is specialized to nonarchimedean local fields.
Depth `0` is excluded, as in the source. Parts (5) and (6) of Proposition 13.2.5 (Iwahori
decomposition for a parabolic subgroup, intersection with a Levi subgroup) are not stated.
