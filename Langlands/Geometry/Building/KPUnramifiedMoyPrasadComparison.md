## Purpose

The published statement `UnramifiedRationalMoyPrasadStatement` (module
`KPUnramifiedRationalMoyPrasadStatement`) asserts that for building data
`P : ParahoricBuildingData G` over a nonarchimedean local field `F` with
`P.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms`, there are building data `B` of `G_K = baseChanged F K G` over
`K = UnramifiedClosure F`, a proof `hZ : IsTorus B.centralizer.Z` and a map
`e : P.Apartment → B.Apartment` such that

```lean
Q y r = (B.moyPrasad hZ (e y) r).comap (rationalExtend F K G)
```

(that is, `G(K)_{e y,r} ∩ G(F)`) is a rational Moy–Prasad family
(`ParahoricBuildingData.IsRationalMoyPrasadFamily`, ten fields).

KP derive these properties (§9.8, Lemma 9.8.1, Proposition 9.8.3) from two inputs:

1. the group properties of the groups `G(K)_{x,r}` over `K` (KP §7.3, Lemma 8.5.19,
   Proposition 13.2.5). These are the eight clauses of the published statement
   `MaximalUnramified.LocalMoyPrasadGroupPropertiesStatement` (module
   `KPLocalMoyPrasadGroupProperties`);
2. the comparison of the building data over `F` with those over `K`: Construction 9.6.1,
   Lemma 9.6.3, Theorem 9.6.5, and the identification of `A(S)` with the
   `Γ`-fixed points of the apartment of a special torus `T ⊇ S` (KP §9.6, §9.8.2).

This module states the second input as the Prop-valued structure
`P.IsUnramifiedComparison B hZ e`. It proves that the two inputs give the ten fields. The
proof is the theorem `isRationalMoyPrasadFamily_of_unramifiedComparison`. Its consequence
`unramifiedRationalMoyPrasad_of_unramifiedComparison` reduces
`UnramifiedRationalMoyPrasadStatement` to `LocalMoyPrasadGroupPropertiesStatement` and to
the existence of a comparison for every `P`. **Neither input is proved here:** both
appear only as hypotheses of the two theorems. The module asserts no new proposition and
introduces no axiom.

## Notation

Throughout, `F` is a nonarchimedean local field (instances `Field`, `ValuativeRel`,
`TopologicalSpace`, `IsNonarchimedeanLocalField`), `G` is a group scheme over `Spec F` with
affine structure morphism and `P : ParahoricBuildingData G`, with maximal split torus
`P.i : S → G` and centralizer `P.centralizer.j : Z → G`. The field is
`K = UnramifiedClosure F`. The data over `K` are
`B : MaximalUnramified.BuildingData F (baseChanged F K G)`, with maximal `K`-split torus
`B.S`, and `hZ : IsTorus B.centralizer.Z`. The map of apartments is
`e : P.Apartment → B.Apartment`. In the prose,

- `ι = rationalExtend F K G : G(F) → G(K)` is the extension map on rational points;
- `j = rationalPoints.map P.centralizer.j : Z(F) → G(F)`.

The `K`-side objects are those of `KPLocalMoyPrasadGroupProperties`:

- `B.rootGroupSpan Ψ`, the subgroup `U_Ψ(K)` generated by the `U_b(K)`, `b ∈ Ψ`;
- `B.rootFiltrationSpan x Ψ r`, generated by the `U_{b,x,r}`, `b ∈ Ψ`;
- `B.IsClosedRootSet Ψ`;
- `B.negativePairingRoots ν` and `B.zeroPairingRoots ν`, the relative roots `b` with
  `⟨b, ν⟩ < 0`, respectively `= 0`;
- `B.pairingLeviSpan ν = M_ν(K)`, generated by `j_K(Z_K(K))` and the `U_b(K)` with
  `⟨b, ν⟩ = 0`.

In the Lean proofs these maps are the local notations `ιK` and `jF`.

## The structure `IsUnramifiedRootComparison P B e a Ψ ν`

These are the comparison data at one relative root `a` of `(G, S)`. In the intended
instance, `Ψ` is the set of relative roots of `(G_K, T_K)` that restrict to `a` or `2a`, and
`ν` is a generic point of the image of the cocharacter space of `S`, with `⟨a, ν⟩ > 0` and
`⟨b, ν⟩ = 0` only for roots `b` with `b|_S = 0`. Here `T_K = B.S`. The structure does not fix
`Ψ` and `ν` by these descriptions; it only asserts the following properties of them.

| Field | Statement | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `closed` | `B.IsClosedRootSet Ψ`. | If `b, c` restrict to `a` or `2a` and `pb + qc` is a root, it restricts to a positive multiple of `a` that is a root, hence to `2a`; compare Remark 9.6.4 (printed p. 366, PDF p. 397). |
| `pos` | `0 < ⟨b, ν⟩` for `b ∈ Ψ`. | `⟨b, ν⟩ = ⟨b|_S, ν⟩ ∈ {⟨a,ν⟩, 2⟨a,ν⟩}`. |
| `root_mem` | `ι(U_a(F)) ⊆ U_Ψ(K)`. | `U_a(k) = G(k) ∩ ∏_b U_b(K)` over the non-divisible `b` restricting to `a` or `2a`, proof of Lemma 9.6.3 (printed p. 366, PDF p. 397). |
| `neg_mem` | `ι(U_{-a}(F)) ⊆ U_{-ν}(K)`, the span of the `U_b(K)` with `⟨b, ν⟩ < 0`. | The same description for `-a`; the roots restricting to `-a`, `-2a` pair negatively with `ν`. |
| `levi_mem` | `ι(j(Z(F))) ⊆ M_ν(K)`. | For generic `ν`, `M_ν` is `Z_K = Z_G(S)_K`. By the Bruhat decomposition of the reductive `K`-group `Z_K` relative to its maximal `K`-split torus `T_K`, its `K`-points are generated by `Z_G(T_K)(K)` and the root groups of `(Z_K, T_K)`, the roots vanishing on `S`. |
| `filtration_mem` | `ι(U_{a,y,s}) ⊆ ⟨U_{b,e y,s} ∣ b ∈ Ψ⟩` for `s ≥ 0`. | Lemma 9.6.3, inclusion `⊆` (printed p. 366, PDF p. 397), via the inclusion `A(S) ⊂ A(T_K)` of §9.8.2. Components at roots restricting to `2a` have depth `2s ≥ s`, so the uniform depth `s` is a valid (weaker) bound. |
| `block_mem` | If `g ∈ U_a(F)` and `ι g ∈ ⟨U_{b,e y,t} ∣ b ∈ Ψ⟩`, then `g ∈ U_{a,y,t} U_{2a,y,t}`, the Library's `P.apartment.filtrationBlock y (fun _ => t) a`. | Proposition 9.8.3(1) and its proof at the constant function `t` (printed pp. 375–376, PDF pp. 406–407): `U_a(k) ∩ ∏_b U_{b,x,F} = U_{a,x,f} = U_{a,x,t} U_{2a,x,t}` (§9.8.2 and Definition 7.3.3(1)). |

`block_mem` keeps the doubled-root factor: for a multipliable root `a`, an element of
`U_{2a}(F)` of `2a`-depth `t` has `a`-depth only `t/2` (V 4, Remark 7.3.4). This is the block
convention of the field `mem_filtrationBlock` of `IsRationalMoyPrasadFamily`.

## The structure `IsUnramifiedComparison P B hZ e`

| Field | Statement | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `apartmentMap_smul` | `B.apartmentMap (e (n • y)) = ι(n) • B.apartmentMap (e y)` in the reduced building of `G_K`, for `n ∈ N(F)` and `y ∈ A(S)`. | Equivariance of `x ↦ φ^x` under `V(S) ⋊ N(k)`, Theorem 9.6.5 (printed p. 366, PDF p. 397). In the Library this is the last clause of `UnramifiedValuationDescentStatement`: `D.apartmentMap y = rationalExtend n • D.apartmentMap x` together with `φ y = n • φ x`. The relation holds in the building, not in the apartment of `T_K`, because `N(F)` need not normalize `T_K`. |
| `rootComparison` | For each relative root `a`, some `Ψ, ν` satisfy `IsUnramifiedRootComparison P B e a Ψ ν`. | As above. |
| `levi` | Some `ν` satisfies `ι(j(Z(F))) ⊆ M_ν(K)` and `(e y).val b = (e y').val b` for all `y, y'` and all `b` with `⟨b, ν⟩ = 0`. | `ν` generic in the image of `V(S)`. Since `A(S) = A(T_K)^Γ` is an affine space over `V(S)` (§9.6, §9.8.2), `e y' - e y ∈ V(S)`, and `⟨b, v⟩ = ⟨b|_S, v⟩ = 0` for `v ∈ V(S)` and `b|_S = 0`. |
| `comap_zero` | `j⁻¹(ι⁻¹(G(K)_{e y,0})) = Z(F)^0` (`P.centralizerZero`) for every `y`. | First paragraph of the proof of Lemma 9.8.1 (printed p. 375, PDF p. 406), with Definition 2.6.23(2) and Corollary 9.7.3. Kept as its own field: comparing D1's zero term (the norm-Iwahori group of `Z(K)`) with `Z(K)^0` is not available in the Library. |
| `isOpen` | Each `j⁻¹(ι⁻¹(G(K)_{e y,r}))` is open in `Z(F)` for `rationalPointTopology`. | Openness of the descended groups, Proposition 9.8.3(2) with Corollary 7.3.14 (printed p. 251, PDF p. 282). Stated with exactly the instances of the field `isOpen` of `IsRationalMoyPrasadFamily`. |

`comap_zero` and `isOpen` restate the corresponding fields of `IsRationalMoyPrasadFamily`.
The `K`-side group properties do not imply them: they concern the `F`-structure of `Z`.
Collecting them here lets the existence of a comparison be stated and proved
independently of the `K`-side group theory.

## The theorem `isRationalMoyPrasadFamily_of_unramifiedComparison`

```lean
theorem isRationalMoyPrasadFamily_of_unramifiedComparison
    (hS2 : MaximalUnramified.LocalMoyPrasadGroupPropertiesStatement.{u})
    {B} {hZ} {e} (hC : P.IsUnramifiedComparison B hZ e) :
    P.IsRationalMoyPrasadFamily
      (fun y r => (B.moyPrasad hZ (e y) r).comap (rationalExtend F (UnramifiedClosure F) G))
```

The proof specializes `hS2` to `B` and `hZ`, obtaining the clauses
`LocalMoyPrasadEquivariance` (2), `LocalMoyPrasadCommutator` (3),
`LocalMoyPrasadRootIntersection` (4), `LocalMoyPrasadIwahoriFactorization` (5),
`LocalMoyPrasadLeviIntersection` (6) and `LocalMoyPrasadSeparated` (8). Clauses 1 and 7 are
not used. The ten fields are then obtained as follows. Each field except `antitone`,
`comap_zero` and `isOpen` is a separate lemma in the namespace `IsUnramifiedComparison`.

- `antitone`: `Subgroup.comap_mono` of D1's `moyPrasad_antitone`.
- `filtration_le` (lemma `filtration_le`): by `filtration_mem`, `ι g` lies in
  `⟨U_{c,e y,s} ∣ c ∈ Ψ⟩`. Since `Ψ` consists of relative roots (`closed`), each generator
  lies in `G(K)_{e y,s}` by D1's `filtration_le_moyPrasad`.
- `commutator_le` (lemma `commutator_le`): `ι⁅g₁, g₂⁆ = ⁅ι g₁, ι g₂⁆`
  (`map_commutatorElement`) and clause 3.
- `conj_mem` (lemma `conj_mem`): the first part of clause 2, applied to
  `g = ι(n)` and the points `e y`, `e (n • y)`, related by `apartmentMap_smul`, gives
  `ι(n) G(K)_{e y,r} ι(n)⁻¹ = G(K)_{e (n•y),r}`. Then `ι(n g n⁻¹) = ι(n) ι(g) ι(n)⁻¹`.
- `map_mem_of_mul_mem` (lemma `map_mem_of_mul_mem`): for `t > 0`, with `Ψ, ν` from
  `rootComparison a`, we have `ι v' ∈ U_{-ν}(K)` (`neg_mem`) and `ι(j z) ∈ M_ν(K)`
  (`levi_mem`). Also `ι v ∈ U_Ψ(K) ⊆ U_ν(K)`, by `root_mem` and `closed`/`pos`
  (`biSup_mono`). Clause 5 then puts the middle factor `ι(j z)` in `G(K)_{e y,t}`.
- `comap_eq` (lemma `mem_moyPrasad_of_mem`, both directions): take `ν` from `levi`.
  The valuation equality gives `U_{b,e y,r} = U_{b,e y',r}` for every `b` with
  `⟨b, ν⟩ = 0`, hence an inclusion of the spans `rootFiltrationSpan` (private lemma
  `rootFiltrationSpan_mono`). By clause 6, `M_ν(K) ∩ G(K)_{x,r}` is
  `j_K(Z(K)_r) · ⟨U_{b,x,r} ∣ ⟨b,ν⟩ = 0⟩` at both `x = e y` and `x = e y'`. So
  `ι(j z) ∈ M_ν(K) ∩ G(K)_{e y,r}` implies `ι(j z) ∈ G(K)_{e y',r}`.
- `comap_zero`: the field `comap_zero`.
- `iInf_comap` (lemma `iInf_comap`): if `ι(j z) ∈ G(K)_{e y,r}` for every `r`, then
  `ι(j z) = 1` by clause 8. So `z = 1`, because `ι` and `j` are injective (private lemmas
  `rationalExtend_injective'`, via `GaloisPoint.extend_injective`, and
  `centralizer_map_injective'`, via `cancel_mono`, as in the Library modules
  `RootValuationFieldRestriction` and `KPTorusCentralizerFiltration`).
- `mem_filtrationBlock` (lemma `mem_filtrationBlock`): for `g ∈ U_a(F)` with
  `ι g ∈ G(K)_{e y,t}`, we get `ι g ∈ U_Ψ(K) ∩ G(K)_{e y,t}` (`root_mem`), and clause 4
  (with `closed`, `pos`) gives `⟨U_{b,e y,t} ∣ b ∈ Ψ⟩`. Then `block_mem` applies.
- `isOpen`: the field `isOpen`.

## The theorem `unramifiedRationalMoyPrasad_of_unramifiedComparison`

```lean
theorem unramifiedRationalMoyPrasad_of_unramifiedComparison
    (hS2 : MaximalUnramified.LocalMoyPrasadGroupPropertiesStatement.{u})
    (hC : ∀ (F : Type u) [Field F] [ValuativeRel F] [TopologicalSpace F]
      [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F]
      (G : GroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of F))) [IsAffineHom G.X.hom]
      (P : ParahoricBuildingData G), P.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms →
        ∃ (B : MaximalUnramified.BuildingData F (baseChanged F (UnramifiedClosure F) G))
          (hZ : IsTorus B.centralizer.Z) (e : P.Apartment → B.Apartment),
          P.IsUnramifiedComparison B hZ e) :
    UnramifiedRationalMoyPrasadStatement.{u}
```

Given `F, G, P, hP`, it takes the witnesses `B, hZ, e` of `hC` and applies the previous
theorem. The hypothesis `hC` has the same binders and existential witnesses as
`UnramifiedRationalMoyPrasadStatement`; only the conclusion `IsRationalMoyPrasadFamily`
is replaced by `IsUnramifiedComparison`. A proof of `UnramifiedRationalMoyPrasadStatement`
can therefore be assembled from a proof of `LocalMoyPrasadGroupPropertiesStatement` and a
proof of `hC`. Neither proof exists in the Library at present.

## Intended construction of a comparison (not formalized here)

As in KP §9.8.2: choose a special `F`-torus `T ⊇ S` (Proposition 9.3.4; Library
`SpecialTorusExistenceStatement`), and take for `B` the building data of `G_K` relative to
`T_K` with its V 6 class (`quasiSplitIntegerApartmentExistence`).
`UnramifiedValuationDescentStatement` (KP Construction 9.6.1, Lemma 9.6.3,
Theorem 9.6.5) identifies the `Γ`-fixed points of that class with a class of valuations
of `(G, S)`. KP Proposition 6.2.6 (`OmegaCompatibleApartmentUniquenessProof`) identifies
this class with `P.apartment`. Take `e` to be the inverse of this identification, the
inclusion `A(S) ⊂ A(T_K)`. Then `apartmentMap_smul` is the normalizer clause of
`UnramifiedValuationDescentStatement`.

Some fields may need further Library input:

- the root-group, filtration and block clauses (Lemma 9.6.3, Proposition 9.8.3(1));
- the generation of `Z_G(S)(K)` by `Z_G(T_K)(K)` and root groups (`levi_mem`, `levi`);
- the depth-zero comparison `comap_zero`;
- openness `isOpen`.

This module does not assert any of these.

## Implementation notes

- The module sets `backward.isDefEq.respectTransparency false`, as D1's module
  `KPLocalMoyPrasadGroups` does.
- A private instance derives `IsAffineHom` for `baseChanged F K G → Spec K` by base change.
  It is the same argument as the private instance of `KPUnramifiedRationalMoyPrasadStatement`
  and adds no hypothesis.
- Monotonicity of `rootFiltrationSpan` is proved by unfolding it and applying `iSup₂_mono`.

## Provenance

The two structures are derived interfaces. KP do not name them; they list exactly the
properties of the `F`-to-`K` comparison that KP's proofs of Lemma 9.8.1 and
Proposition 9.8.3 use, separated from the `K`-side group theory. The tables above give the
KP statement or argument behind each field.
