# Global exchange at constant positive Moy–Prasad depth

This derived construction adapts the published `ChamberExchange` argument to the original root filtrations at one unchanged nonnegative depth. The group `depthRootSpan x r Ψ` is the supremum of those root-depth subgroups whose actual directions belong to `Ψ`. The set `depthExchangeSet` is the product of its negative and positive sign spans, followed by a supplied subgroup.

V3 shows that a separating root-depth subgroup normalizes the span of the roots positive on both sides of a single-ray crossing. The commutator depths are at least `r`, since the coefficients are positive integers and `r` is nonnegative. On the crossed ray, the published proportional-root lemma and a root of minimal positive evaluation identify the actual directions as that root and possibly its double. `depthRootSpan_eq_block_of_ray` therefore identifies this span with the entire original `filtrationBlockSubgroup`. Both factors retain their original depth. In particular, the doubled-root factor is not absorbed into an ordinary root filtration at the same depth.

The generic crossing argument uses an explicit local block-exchange inclusion. Common sign spans are collected by the proved normalizer containments. The finite real-vector crossing chain from `ChamberExchange` is then adapted without changing its hyperplane argument: a real vector avoiding finitely many hyperplanes gives distinct crossing parameters for nonproportional roots. Moving along this chain exchanges opposite signs. The subgroup of elements whose left multiplication and inverse left multiplication preserve the resulting exchange set contains every original root-depth generator. This proves `sharpFiltration_subset_depthExchangeSet` for the actual `sharpFiltration`, using its published equality with the supremum of the original root-depth subgroups.

For a supplied commutator-friendly centralizer filtration, `MoyPrasadBlockExchange.filtrationBlock_mul_opposite_subset` supplies the local exchange, including nonreduced systems. The actual centralizer-depth image normalizes each original root-depth subgroup and hence every selected span. Moving that image across the opposite block puts the local exchange in the form used by the generic crossing argument. Thus `sharpFiltration_subset_signs_mul_image` covers the original sharp subgroup by the negative span, positive span and actual centralizer-depth image.

Finally, `moyPrasad_eq_negative_image_positive` proves the exact set equality
`P_{x,r} = depthRootSpan x r Φ_{-mu} · j(Z(F)_r) · depthRootSpan x r Φ_mu`
for every positive `r` and generic cocharacter `mu`. Normalization first expresses the defining supremum of the actual centralizer-depth image and the original sharp subgroup as their set product. The preceding covering and normalization of both sign spans collect the central factors into the middle. The reverse inclusion follows from the original generators of `P_{x,r}`.

This module keeps the supplied `ParahoricBuildingData`, apartment point, root-depth subgroups and centralizer filtration. It assumes the supplied apartment axioms and commutator friendliness for the last two conclusions; the other centralizer-filtration axioms are not needed here. It neither constructs a centralizer filtration nor proves covariance or the host simultaneous normal-basis statement. Ordered block coordinates are assembled separately from this sign-span equality. The provenance is internal derivation from the published `ChamberExchange`, the original `KPRealDepthSubgroups` definitions and the accompanying complete local block exchange.
