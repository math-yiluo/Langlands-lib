# The quasi-split `SU_3`: no component and no length-zero data

Module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.SU3Regression.ComponentData`.

This module states, without proving it, `SU3ComponentDataStatement`: a regression example
for Kaletha–Prasad building data of the simply connected quasi-split group `SU_3` of a
separable quadratic extension `E/F`, the contrasting case to groups with nontrivial
length-zero data such as `GL_N`. For a simply connected group the component group
`G(F)/G(F)^0` and the length-zero part `Ω_C` of the Iwahori–Weyl group are trivial, and
parahoric subgroups are the full stabilizers of facets; the statement tests the Library's
`G(F)^0`, `G(F)^1`, depth-zero parahoric and Iwahori–Weyl objects on this group. It is a
closed proposition, universe-polymorphic in the universe `u` of `F`.

## The group and the data

The statement quantifies over every nonarchimedean local field `F` of either characteristic,
every field `E : Type u` with `Algebra F E`, `Algebra.IsQuadraticExtension F E` and
`Algebra.IsSeparable F E` (unramified or ramified, in every residue characteristic), every
diagonal Hermitian form `H : HermitianRoot.DiagonalForm (SU3Root.fieldInvolution F E) Unit`
with unit weight `w` and `hw : ∀ i, (w i : E) = H.weight i`, and every KP building datum
`D : KPBuildingDatum G` with `D.SatisfiesGroupAxioms`, where
`G = HermitianRoot.SchemeModel.groupScheme F E H w hw` is the special unitary group of the
Hermitian form `hyperbolic plane ⊕ ⟨w⟩` on `E³`. The Library proves that `G` is connected
reductive (`HermitianRoot.SchemeModel.isConnectedReductive`) and simply connected
(`HermitianRoot.SchemeModel.isSimplyConnected`). `P = D.base`.

## The statement

1. **`G(F)^0 = G(F)^1 = G(F)`.** `D.base.zeroSubgroup = ⊤`, where `zeroSubgroup` is
   `G(F)^0 = G(F)^♮ · Z(F)^0` of KP Definition 2.6.23(3) (as a join of subgroups), and
   `Plancherel.rationalCommonNormKernel G = ⊤`, where `rationalCommonNormKernel G` is
   `G(F)^1`, the common kernel of `|χ|` over the rational characters `χ` of `G`.
   `G` is semisimple, so it has no nontrivial rational character and `G(F)^1 = G(F)`; it is
   simply connected, so its derived subgroup is `G` and the simply connected cover is an
   isomorphism, whence `G(F)^♮ = G(F)`.
2. **Parahorics are stabilizers.** For every building facet
   `x : BuildingFacet D.base.toValuationBuildingData`, the parahoric `D.parahoric x` (the
   integral points of the neutral model of `x`, from `DepthZero.Definitions`) equals
   `D.base.pointwiseFixer x.val`, the subgroup of `G(F)` fixing `x` pointwise, and
   `MulAction.stabilizer (rationalPoints G) x.val`, the subgroup of `G(F)` preserving `x`
   (for the pointwise action of `G(F)` on subsets of the reduced building). By Axiom 4.1.20
   the parahoric is the pointwise fixer inside `G(F)^0`, which is all of `G(F)` here; a
   simply connected group acts on its building preserving types, so an element preserving a
   facet fixes it pointwise.
3. **No length-zero data.** `Z(F)^0` is normal in `N(F)` (`D.base.normalizerZero.Normal`,
   an existential over the instance, as in `ParahoricBuildingData.IwahoriWeylChamberSplitting`);
   the Iwahori–Weyl group `W̃^0 = N(F)/Z(F)^0` (`IwahoriWeylGroup`) equals its affine part
   `(N(F) ∩ G(F)^0)/Z(F)^0` (`D.base.affinePart = ⊤`); and for every chamber `C` of the
   apartment (`AffineRootSystem.IsChamber D.base.affineRoots C`) the stabilizer
   `W̃^0_C = D.base.chamberStabilizer C` is trivial. By KP 7.8.2 (`iwahoriWeylChamberSplitting`)
   `W̃^0_C ≅ G(F)/G(F)^0`, which is trivial by item 1, so `W̃^0 = W_aff ⋊ W̃^0_C = W_aff`.

The premise `D.SatisfiesGroupAxioms` is the standing hypothesis of the setting. Item 2 uses
the model axioms (Axiom 4.1.20, `neutral_points`) and the building axioms; item 3 uses the
apartment axioms of KP Chapter 7.

The affine Weyl group `AffineRootSystem.affineWeylGroup` and the Iwahori–Weyl group remain
distinct objects; the statement identifies `W̃^0` with its subgroup `affinePart`, not with the
group generated by affine reflections.
