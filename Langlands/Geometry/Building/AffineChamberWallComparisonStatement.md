# Chamber-simple reflections and separating wall reflections

`Langlands.Geometry.Building.AffineChamberWallComparisonStatement` is an
internal geometric prerequisite for using gallery generation in the actual
affine Iwahori–Tits theorem. It compares two existing definitions, without
changing either one. Worker 455 identified the need for this adapter and
suggested the image-equality interface; Worker 1565 supplied the statement
and supporting proofs.

The vector space `V : Type uV` and torsor `A : Type uA` have independent
universes. The instances are `AddCommGroup V`, `Module ℝ V` and
`AddTorsor V A`. For every set of affine
functionals `Ψ`, the hypotheses are exactly
`IsAffineRootSystemWithRankZero Ψ` and a regular point `x`, meaning
`∀ ψ ∈ Ψ, ψ x ≠ 0`. Thus derivative spanning, nonconstant roots, finite
derivatives, the coroot and integral-pairing axioms, and corrected AR 4
remain hypotheses. The statement includes nonreduced systems and the
empty rank-zero arrangement.

The conclusion is equality of sets of actual affine automorphisms:

- On the left is the image of
  `chamberSimpleReflections Ψ (facet Ψ x)` under the literal subgroup
  inclusion `(affineWeylGroup Ψ).subtype`. This simple set uses positive
  indivisible roots whose hyperplanes are affine spans of chamber faces.
- On the right is `wallReflections Ψ x`. An element `s` belongs precisely
  when it is an actual coroot reflection separating `x` from `s x`, and
  every actual coroot reflection separating those points equals `s`.
  Separation means strictly negative product of the defining root values.

The equality is a proof obligation. This file does not prove it or the
gallery-generation statement. In particular, equality of hyperplanes,
existence of the required face with full hyperplane span, positivity and
indivisibility all remain part of the comparison to be proved.

The supporting theorems are proved:

- A separating reflection lies in `affineWeylGroup Ψ`, directly from its
  root and coroot witnesses. Therefore every wall reflection also lies
  there.
- Every chamber-simple reflection has square one and differs from one.
  The square follows from the existing involution formula. For
  nonidentity, the implemented proof selects a point of the chamber,
  where its simple root is positive, and uses that reflection negates
  this value. These laws require no affine-root-system axioms.
- `closure_chamberSimpleReflections_eq_top_of_wall_generation` assumes
  the displayed image comparison for an arbitrary chamber subset `C`
  and point `x`, together with
  `Subgroup.closure (wallReflections Ψ x) = affineWeylGroup Ψ`.
  It concludes that the subgroup generated by the literal simple set
  is top in `affineWeylGroup Ψ`. The proof maps subgroup closure through
  the inclusion and lifts each Weyl element through the resulting
  equality. `Subgroup.closure` is algebraic generation.

This comparison is internally formulated from Kaletha–Prasad's geometric
conventions: Remark 1.3.16 describes chamber walls and faces; Definition
1.3.19 selects positive indivisible wall roots; Proposition 1.3.22(1)
states their reflection-generation consequence. The unique-separator
definition is the separate internal gallery interface, so the exact
comparison is not attributed as a literal statement of the book.
Once both the comparison and gallery generation have been proved, the
supporting transfer theorem supplies the literal simple-generation
axiom required by the unchanged affine Iwahori–Tits target. The actual
parahoric, normality, action, Bruhat and saturation obligations remain.
