# Invariance properties of torus-restricted root functions

Module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.TorusRestrictedRootInvariance`.

Setting. `k` is a field, `S` and `T` are split tori over `k`, `i : T ⟶ G` embeds `T` in a group
scheme `G`, `φ` is a valuation of the root datum of `(G, T)` (`IsRootDatumValuation i φ`), and
`f : S ⟶ T` is a homomorphism. `torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily hS hT hE hφ f` (module
`RootValueTorusRestriction`) is the family of root functions `ψ` of `(G, S)`, for the embedding
`groupHom f ≫ i`, whose level sets are the depth subgroups
`torusRestrictedRootDepthSubgroup hφ f α r` (module `RootDepthTorusRestriction`). For a root `α`
of `(G, S)`, a point `g ∈ U_α(k)` and a real `r`, `r ≤ ψ_α g` holds if and only if `g` lies in
that depth subgroup (`le_torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily_iff`). The depth subgroup is generated
by the filtration groups `U_{χ, φ, m r}` for the roots `χ` of `(G, T)` with `f ≫ χ = m • α`,
`m ≥ 1`.

The module proves three properties of `ψ` on a root group `U_α(k)`. They are hypotheses (H1),
(H2) and (H3) of the Galois descent of zero-value jumps used for
`UnramifiedZeroJumpDescentStatement`. The module contains no `def` and no new statement.

## Results

### Ultrametric inequality

`torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily_min_le_mul_inv`: for a root `α` of `(G, S)` and
`g, h ∈ U_α(k)`, `min (ψ_α g) (ψ_α h) ≤ ψ_α (g h⁻¹)`.

Proof. By `WithTop.forall_coe_le_iff_le`, it suffices to show `r ≤ ψ_α (g h⁻¹)` for every real
`r ≤ min (ψ_α g) (ψ_α h)`. Then `g` and `h` lie in the depth subgroup of level `r`, which is a
subgroup, so `g h⁻¹` lies in it too.

### Endomorphisms that preserve the depth subgroups

`torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily_apply_eq_of_depth_iff`: let `e` be a monoid endomorphism of
`G(k)` such that, for all `r` and `g`, `e g` lies in the depth subgroup of level `r` exactly when
`g` does. Then `ψ_α (e g) = ψ_α g` for every character `α` of `S` and every `g ∈ G(k)`.

Proof.

- If `α` is not a root, both values are `⊤`
  (`torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily_eq_top_of_not_mem_roots`).
- If `α` is a root, `U_α(k)` is the union of the depth subgroups
  (`mem_rootGroupPoints_iff_exists_torusRestrictedRootDepth`), so `e g ∈ U_α(k)` exactly when
  `g ∈ U_α(k)`. When `g ∉ U_α(k)`, both values are `⊤`
  (`torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily_eq_top_of_not_mem`). When `g ∈ U_α(k)`, both values have
  the same real lower bounds by `le_torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily_iff` and the hypothesis,
  so they are equal (`WithTop.eq_of_forall_coe_le_iff`).

### Conjugation by unit points of the torus

Additional data: a valuation `v` of `k` with values in `ℤₘ₀`, the centralizer `Z` of `T`
represented by `j : Z ⟶ G` (`(torusCentralizer i).IsRepresentedBy j`), `s : T ⟶ Z` with
`s ≫ j = i`, bijectivity `h` of `Cartan.restrictionEvaluation s`, and V6 for `φ` on `Z`,
`IntegerValuation.IsCentralizerCompatible v i j s h φ` (module `IntegerCentralizer`): for every
root `χ`, every `z ∈ Z(k)` and every `u ∈ U_χ(k)`,
`φ_χ (z⁻¹ u z) = φ_χ u + ⟨χ, ν(z)⟩`, where `ν(z) = centralizerTranslation v s h z`.

- `torusRestrictedRootDepthSubgroup_conj_mem_of_mem_characterValuationKernel`: if
  `z ∈ characterValuationKernel v Z` (every rational character of `Z` has valuation one at `z`),
  then `z⁻¹ g z` lies in the depth subgroup of level `r` whenever `g` does. Proof: `ν(z) = 1`
  (`IntegerValuation.centralizerTranslation_ker`). The image of the depth subgroup under
  conjugation is generated by the conjugates of its generating filtration groups
  (`Subgroup.map_iSup`). For `u ∈ U_{χ, φ, m r}`, `z⁻¹ u z ∈ U_χ(k)`, because `z` gives a
  normalizer point with trivial Weyl action (`conj_mem_rootGroupPoints`,
  `weylAction_centralizerToNormalizer`). Its value is `φ_χ u + 0 ≥ m r` by V6.
- `torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily_conj_of_mem_characterValuationKernel`: if
  `t ∈ characterValuationKernel v S`, let `p` be the image of `t` in `G(k)` under
  `groupHom f ≫ i`. Then `ψ_α (p g p⁻¹) = ψ_α g` for every character `α` and every `g`. Proof:
  the image `z` of `t` in `Z(k)` under `groupHom f ≫ s` lies in `characterValuationKernel v Z`
  (`IntegerValuation.map_mem_characterValuationKernel`), and so does the image of `t⁻¹`.
  Their images in `G(k)` under `j` are `p` and `p⁻¹`, because `s ≫ j = i`. By the previous
  item, conjugation by `p` and by `p⁻¹` both preserve each depth subgroup, so the hypothesis of
  `torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily_apply_eq_of_depth_iff` holds for `e = MulAut.conj p`.

### Galois invariance

Setting: a field extension `K/k`, commutative group schemes `S`, `T` and a group scheme `G`
over `k`, `i : T ⟶ G`, and a valuation `φ` of the root datum of the base change `(G_K, T_K)`.
The tori `S_K` and `T_K` are split (hypotheses `hS`, `hT` of the second item). `f_K` is the base change of
`f : S ⟶ T`, and `a_K = characterLattice.baseChange` of a character `a` of `S`.
`RootFunctionFamily.galoisAction k K T G σ` is the action of `σ ∈ Aut(K/k)` on root-function
families (module `RootFunctionGaloisAction`), and `rationalConjugate k K G σ` is coefficient
conjugation on `G(K)`.

- The imported `scaledRootDepth_galois_map_le` from `KPUnramifiedScaledRootDepth`:
  if `σ` fixes `φ`, then
  `σ(g)` lies in the depth subgroup of `a_K` at level `r` whenever `g` does. Proof: the image of
  the depth subgroup is generated by the images of its generating filtration groups. For a root
  `χ` of `(G_K, T_K)` with `f_K ≫ χ = m • a_K`, the Galois-transported character `σχ` is a root
  (`mem_roots_galois_iff`), and `f_K ≫ σχ = m • a_K`, because `a_K` comes from `k`
  (`characterLattice.galoisRepresentation_naturality`,
  `characterLattice.galoisRepresentation_baseChange`). By
  `rationalConjugate_mem_rootFiltration_iff`, `σ(U_{χ, φ, m r}) ⊆ U_{σχ, σφ, m r}`, and
  `σφ = φ`. The existing lemma already allows an arbitrary `f` and is reused here.
- `torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily_rationalConjugate`: if every `σ ∈ Aut(K/k)` fixes `φ`,
  then `ψ_{a_K} (σ g) = ψ_{a_K} g` for every `σ`, every character `a` of `S` and every
  `g ∈ G(K)`. Proof: apply the previous item to `σ` and to `σ⁻¹`, and use
  `rationalConjugate_mul` and `rationalConjugate_one` to get the hypothesis of
  `torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily_apply_eq_of_depth_iff` for `e = rationalConjugate k K G σ`.
