# The affine root system and affine Weyl group over the maximal unramified extension

This module states `MaximalUnramifiedAffineWeylStatement`, the part of Axiom 4.1.6 (AS 3 and
the first half of the first sentence of AS 5) that the Library needs for supplied building
data over the maximal unramified extension of a nonarchimedean local field. Nothing in this
module is proved.

## Statement

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field (`IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F`, at universe `u`) and
`K = LocalGalois.Separable.inertiaFixedField F` its maximal unramified extension inside the
separable closure, with its normalized integer-valued valuation
`MaximalUnramified.valuation F`. Let `H` be an affine group scheme over `Spec K`
(`IsAffineHom H.X.hom`) and let `C : MaximalUnramified.BuildingData F H` be supplied
building data: a connected reductive `H`, a maximal `K`-split torus `S`, an equipollence class
`A(S)` of valuations of the root datum of `(H, S)` (`C.apartment`), the actual centralizer
datum and axiom V 6. Write `Ψ = C.affineRoots` for the affine roots of KP Definition 6.3.4
(affine functionals `ψ` on the apartment `A(S)` with derivative a relative root `a` such that
`U_ψ ⊈ U_{ψ+} · U_{2a}(K)`), and `W(Ψ) = AffineRootSystem.affineWeylGroup Ψ` for the group of
affine automorphisms of `A(S)` generated by the reflections of `Ψ`.

The proposition asserts, for every such `F`, `H` and `C`:

1. `Ψ` is an affine root system in the sense of KP Definition 1.3.1 with the corrected
   axiom AR 4, in the Library form that admits rank zero
   (`AffineRootSystem.IsAffineRootSystemWithRankZero`);
2. `W(Ψ)` is contained in the image of the action map
   `C.apartment.normalizerAffineEquiv : N(K) → Aff(A(S))` of the `K`-points of the
   normalizer `N = N_H(S)`: every element of `W(Ψ)` is the action of some `n ∈ N(K)`.

## Source

Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits theory: a new approach* (2023), with the authors' corrections of
1 November 2024:

- Proposition 6.3.13 (printed p. 221, PDF p. 252): the sets `Ψ` and `Ψ′` are affine root
  systems in `A*` with derivative root system `Φ`, and the image of `N(k)/Z(k)_b` in `Aff(A)`
  contains `W(Ψ)`. Its proof realizes the reflections of `Ψ` by the elements `m(u)` of
  Proposition 6.2.1 (printed p. 212, PDF p. 243).
- Axiom 4.1.6, AS 3 and AS 5 (printed pp. 159–160, PDF pp. 190–191), which these two assertions
  instantiate; the overview on printed p. 166 (PDF p. 197) records that AS 3 is proved in
  Proposition 6.3.13.

Chapter 6 of the source works over a field with a discrete valuation and a valuation of the
root datum; here the field is `K` with its normalized valuation. The correction of the
second sentence of AS 5 (corrections, p. 33) concerns the simply connected case only and is
not part of this statement.

## Provenance

This is a derived statement: it restricts KP Proposition 6.3.13 to the two assertions
needed downstream and states them for the Library's supplied building data over `K`
(`IntegerValuationBuildingData` for `MaximalUnramified.valuation F`). The same assertions over
a nonarchimedean local field are fields of `KPApartmentData.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms`
(`isAffineRootSystem_affineRoots`, `affineWeylGroup_le_range`). The statement claims nothing
about the derivative root system, about `Ψ′`, or about the other half of AS 5.
