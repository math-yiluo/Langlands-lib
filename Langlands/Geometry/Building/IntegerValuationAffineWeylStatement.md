# Affine roots and Weyl reflections for integer-valuation building data

`Langlands.Geometry.Building.IntegerValuationAffineWeylStatement.{u}` is a
proposition over every field `k : Type u`, every explicit valuation
`v : Valuation k (WithZero (Multiplicative ℤ))`, every affine group scheme
`G` over `k`, and every supplied `B : IntegerValuationBuildingData v G`.
The sole additional premise is
`∃ t : kˣ, IntegerValuation.order v t = 1`.

The supplied data include connected reductivity of `G`, a maximal split torus
`B.i`, its root-datum valuation apartment, its actual centralizer with the
restriction-evaluation bijectivity, and full centralizer compatibility V6.
The statement quantifies over these data; it does not construct them.
There is no local-field, Henselian, completeness, local compactness,
residue-field, or topology hypothesis.

For `Ψ = B.affineRoots` on the reduced apartment `B.Apartment`, the conclusion
has exactly two conjuncts:

1. `AffineRootSystem.IsAffineRootSystemWithRankZero Ψ`;
2. `AffineRootSystem.affineWeylGroup Ψ ≤
   B.apartment.normalizerAffineEquiv.range`.

Here `Ψ` is the actual affine-root set of KP Definition 6.3.4, formed from
the root-group filtration jumps modulo the double-root group.
The second conjunct says that every element of the group generated by its
coroot reflections is realized by the existing affine action of the actual
normalizer of `B.i` on this same apartment.

## Order and rank conventions

The source-positive order is
`IntegerValuation.order v t = ((IntegerValuation.orderUnits v t).toAdd : ℝ)`,
where the integer order is `-WithZero.log (v (t : k))`.
Thus the premise supplies a unit of positive additive order one in the
valuation convention of the source. The rational-character valuation uses
the corrected positive formula `χ(ω(z)) = order v (χ(z))`; the apartment
translation has the negative sign `ν(z) = -ω(z)`.

`IsAffineRootSystemWithRankZero` uses derivative spanning in AR1:
the derivatives span the dual translation space, are finite, and each
affine root is nonconstant. AR2 supplies coroot reflections preserving
`Ψ`, AR3 gives integral pairings, and corrected AR4 requires infinite
derivative fibres with finite bounded subsets. This permits `Ψ = ∅` on a
point: the existing `isAffineRootSystemWithRankZero_empty_iff` identifies
that case with a subsingleton translation space. Its affine Weyl group
is then trivial. For nonempty `Ψ`, the existing
`IsAffineRootSystemWithRankZero.span_eq_top` recovers affine spanning.
This convention reconciles KP's printed affine-spanning AR1 with the
rank-zero case included immediately after Definition 1.3.1.

## Provenance and scope

This is an internal field-independent specialization of the
discrete-valuation arguments in Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory:
A New Approach* (2023), §§6.2–6.3, especially Proposition 6.3.13.
It is a statement target, not a proof or a verbatim transcription of that
proposition in its original setting. The supplied algebraic data and the
order-one unit isolate the hypotheses needed for the two stated conclusions.
The published local-field development
[ValuationAffineRootSystems](ValuationAffineRootSystems.md) supplies the
proof route to extend: actual root reflections, normalizer preservation,
discrete root-value differences from V6 and integer rational-character
values, and infinite fibres from a coroot evaluated at the supplied unit.
The successor proof must establish this specialization at arbitrary `u`.

The original-source correspondence was checked at:

- Definition 1.3.1 and Remark 1.3.2, printed pp.14–15, PDF pp.45–46:
  the affine-root axioms, rank convention, and reflection-generated Weyl group;
- Proposition 6.2.1, printed pp.212–213, PDF pp.243–244, and
  Proposition 6.2.4, printed p.215, PDF p.246:
  root reflections and the actual normalizer action;
- Definition 6.3.4, printed p.218, PDF p.249, Proposition 6.3.7,
  printed p.219, PDF p.250, Lemma 6.3.12, printed pp.220–221,
  PDF pp.251–252, and Proposition 6.3.13, printed p.221, PDF p.252:
  the affine-root set, discrete differences, normalizer preservation,
  and the source affine-root/Weyl conclusions.

The authors' *Corrections and additions*, version of 1 November 2024,
were checked at PDF p.2 (AR4 also requires infinite fibres), p.25
(the positive character-valuation sign), p.32 (the homomorphism in
Proposition 6.3.7 is `μ`; under V6 it equals `ν`), and p.33
(the Chapter 7 assumption of the remaining AS5 assertions).
In particular, the source proof of Proposition 6.3.13 invokes
Proposition 6.3.8, whose topological premise is explicit. The internal
target instead retains the route of the published local development
that avoids that premise. It asserts no derivative-root equality,
V7 compatibility, or further AS5 conclusions.
