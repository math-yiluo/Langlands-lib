# Finite derivative bases by deleting an affine node

For a supplied rank-zero-compatible affine root system, the predicate
`HasSimpleDerivativeBaseAfterDeletion C δ` asserts the existence of a Mathlib
base of the full rational derivative root pairing whose support is exactly
the image of the simple affine gradients other than `δ`. Both the translation
space and its affine torsor have independent universe parameters. The
definition uses the full derivative set, including multipliable roots.

This internal development normalizes the full coroot form by the squared
length of a prescribed full derivative root. Under equality of all derivative
lengths, the normalized Gram entries equal the actual root-coroot evaluations
and each derivative root has squared length two. Integrality of those
evaluations and symmetry show that the full integer root lattice is even
integral. Positive definiteness follows from full coroot spanning.

For a prescribed regular chamber and deleted simple affine root, assume the
remaining simple gradients are independent and generate that full integer
lattice. Their off-diagonal normalized pairings are nonpositive by the chamber
Cartan theorem. The short-vector coordinate theorem therefore proves that
every full derivative root has integer coefficients of one sign in this
family.

`hasSimpleDerivativeBaseAfterDeletion_of_equal_lengths` constructs the promised
base from these hypotheses. Real independence implies rational independence
in the rational root span. The integer coordinate sum remains an equality
in that subspace; nonnegative integer coefficients become natural
coefficients, so each root or its negative belongs to the additive monoid
generated by the proposed base. Equal full derivative lengths imply symmetry
of the entire root-coroot pairing, which implies reducedness. Mathlib's
`RootPairing.Base.mk'` then supplies both root and coroot independence and
the corresponding positive-or-negative span conditions. Its support is
precisely the proposed finite image.

`hasSimpleDerivativeBaseAfterDeletion_of_transitive_simpleRoots` supplies the
equal-length and deletion hypotheses when the system is irreducible, every
derivative has an indivisible affine lift, root-preserving affine pullback is
transitive on the chamber's simple roots, and the chamber Cartan matrix is
vertex-transitive. The two transitivity hypotheses remain explicit: the
matrix condition supplies the unit-coefficient gradient relation used for
deletion, while root-preserving pullback supplies equal lengths.

This is derived geometric and linear-algebra infrastructure, with explicit
regularity and other geometric hypotheses. It does not supply those
hypotheses for an actual algebraic group or assert an absolute Cartan
comparison. No additional source assertion is transcribed.
