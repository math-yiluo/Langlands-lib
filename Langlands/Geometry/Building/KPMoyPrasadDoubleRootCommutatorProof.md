# Proof: `[P_{x,r}, P_{x,s}] ⊆ P_{x,r+s}` with the double-root premise (KP Proposition 13.2.5(4))

The theorem `Langlands.Geometry.Building.moyPrasadDoubleRootCommutator` proves the published
proposition `MoyPrasadDoubleRootCommutatorStatement.{u}` (module
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.KPMoyPrasadDoubleRootStatement`) at an arbitrary universe `u`.

## Statement proved

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field and `G` a group scheme over `F` with
`IsAffineHom G.X.hom`. Let `P : ParahoricBuildingData G` and `Zf : P.CentralizerFiltration` be
supplied, with `P.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms`, `Zf.SatisfiesFiltrationAxioms` and
`Zf.IsDoubleRootCommutatorFriendly`. Then for every apartment point `x` and all
`r s : ℝ≥0`, `⁅Zf.moyPrasad x r, Zf.moyPrasad x s⁆ ≤ Zf.moyPrasad x (r + s)`. This is KP
Proposition 13.2.5(4) for supplied data, with the extra double-root premise.

## Implemented proof

The proof introduces the quantified data and hypotheses and applies one theorem:

- `Langlands.Geometry.Building.ParahoricBuildingData.CentralizerFiltration.commutator_moyPrasad_le`
  in the new helper module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.MoyPrasadCommutatorGenerators`,
  submitted with this proof. Its Markdown, `MoyPrasadCommutatorGenerators.md`, describes the
  proof in detail.

In outline, the helper module proves the following.

1. **Normality.** `P_x = j(Z(F)^0) ⊔ G(F)^♯_x` normalizes every `P_{x,t}`. For the factor
   `G(F)^♯_x` this is the published
   `sharpFixer_le_normalizer_moyPrasad_of_doubleRootCommutatorFriendly` (module
   `MoyPrasadDoubleRootConjugation`). For `j(Z(F)^0)` it follows from `IsCommutatorFiltered`
   and `image_le_normalizer_filtration` (module `MoyPrasadBlockExchange`).
2. **Depth zero.** If `r = 0` or `s = 0`, then `P_{x,0} = P_x` normalizes the other group, and
   the commutator inclusion is this normality.
3. **Reduction to generators.** For `r, s > 0` both groups are generated by `j(Z(F)_t)` and
   the root filtrations `U_{b,x,t}`. All these generators lie in `P_x`, which normalizes
   `P_{x,r+s}`. A general group lemma then reduces the claim to commutators of generators.
4. **Generator pairs.**
   - Two central generators: `IsCommutatorFiltered`.
   - A central and a root generator: `IsRootCompatible`.
   - Root generators of non-opposite directions: axiom V 3.
   - Opposite pairs `(U_{a,x,r}, U_{-a,x,s})`: commutator friendliness gives the central
     coordinate. The two root coordinates are obtained at translated apartment points from the
     published conjugation lemmas, using the uniqueness of the
     `U_{-a}(F) Z(F) U_a(F)` factorization.
   - Mixed pairs `(U_{a,x,r}, U_{-2a,x,s})` and the reversed pairs: the same argument, except
     that `IsDoubleRootCommutatorFriendly`, through the published
     `double_opposite_central_mem_of_doubleRootCommutatorFriendly`, gives the central
     coordinate in `Z(F)_{s+2r} ⊆ Z(F)_{r+s}`.

The hypothesis `Zf.SatisfiesFiltrationAxioms` is passed on whole. It is the conjunction of
`IsCommutatorFriendly`, `IsNormalizerStable`, `IsCommutatorFiltered` and `IsRootCompatible`;
the helper module uses all components except `IsNormalizerStable`.

## Scope

The building data and the filtration of `Z(F)` are supplied, exactly as in the statement;
this proof constructs neither. No hypothesis is added to the statement and no unproved
proposition is assumed. The source is Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits theory: a new approach*
(2023), Proposition 13.2.5(4) and its proof. The Lean route follows that proof. It differs in
one respect: the root coordinates for opposite pairs are read off at translated apartment
points instead of from explicit valuations.
