# Moy–Prasad filtration subgroups on supplied data

This module defines the Moy–Prasad filtration subgroups `P_{x,r}` of
Kaletha–Prasad Definition 13.2.1 for supplied building data
`P : ParahoricBuildingData G` together with a supplied filtration of `Z(F)`,
`Z = Z_G(S)`. It asserts no property of `P_{x,r}` beyond the elementary
comparisons listed below. The source properties of Proposition 13.2.5 are
stated separately in `KPMoyPrasadFiltrationStatement`.

## Setting

`F` is a nonarchimedean local field and `P : ParahoricBuildingData G` the
supplied building data of the Library: the maximal split torus `S`, its
apartment, the actual centralizer `Z` with `j : Z → G`, and
`P.centralizerZero = Z(F)^0`. `P.filtration x a r` is the actual root
filtration `U_{a,x,r}` and `P.apartment.filtrationBlock x f a` the block of
Definition 7.3.3(1).

## The supplied filtration of `Z(F)`

KP construct `Z(k)_r` in §9.8 from a functorial filtration of tori (Appendix
B), via the Moy–Prasad groups of `Z` over the maximal unramified extension.
That construction is not available in the Library. Here the filtration is
supplied data, and its properties are separate predicates.

- `CentralizerFiltration P` has a family `filtration : ℝ≥0 → Subgroup Z(F)`
  with `filtration 0 = Z(F)^0`, antitone and separated (`⨅ r, Z(F)_r = ⊥`).
  These are the first clauses of Definition 7.2.1. The index set is `ℝ≥0`, as
  in §7.2. All filtration subgroups lie in `Z(F)^0`, which is bounded, so no
  separate subgroup `Z(k)_b` is introduced.
- `image r` is the image of `Z(F)_r` in `G(F)` under `j`.
- `IsCommutatorFriendly` is the commutator clause of Definition 7.2.1. For a
  relative root `a`, a point `x` of the apartment, `u ∈ U_a(F)`, `u ≠ 1`,
  `u' ∈ U_{-a}(F)`, `u' ≠ 1` and `r = x_a(u) + x_{-a}(u') > 0`: whenever
  `u'⁻¹ u u' u⁻¹ = v' · j(z) · v` with `v' ∈ U_{-a}(F)` and `v ∈ U_a(F)`,
  `z ∈ Z(F)_r`. By Definition 7.1.3 `z` is the `Z`-component of the commutator.
  The predicate quantifies over every such factorization. The source shows it
  exists and is unique (Proposition 7.1.1), so this is the source condition.
- `IsNormalizerStable`: every `Z(F)_r` is stable under conjugation by `N(F)`.
- `IsCommutatorFiltered`: `[Z(F)_r, Z(F)_s] ⊆ Z(F)_{r+s}`.
- `IsRootCompatible`: for `z ∈ Z(F)_r`, `s ≥ 0` and `u ∈ U_{a,x,s}`,
  `z u z⁻¹ u⁻¹ ∈ U_{a,x,r+s} U_{2a,x,r+s}`.
- `SatisfiesFiltrationAxioms` is the conjunction of the four predicates above.
- `IsOpenFiltration`: every `Z(F)_r` is open in `Z(F)` for the point topology
  (`rationalPointTopology`; `Z` is affine as a closed subgroup of `G`).

Provenance of the last four predicates. They are derived, not stated as a
definition in the source, and are properties of the actual §9.8 filtration
used, sometimes tacitly, in the proof of Proposition 13.2.5. `Z(k)_r` is the
intersection with `Z(k)` of a Moy–Prasad group of `Z_K` at the unique point of
`B(Z)` (§9.8). Conjugation by `N(k)` is an automorphism of `Z`, which gives
normalizer stability. Proposition 13.2.5(4) for `Z_K` gives the commutator
filtration. KP's proof of 13.2.5(4) reduces to commutators of root-group
elements using (2) and (3). Root compatibility is the remaining commutator of
the central factor with root groups, written out explicitly here.

The block, rather than `U_{a,x,r+s}` alone, is necessary when `2a` is a
relative root. On `U_{2a}(F)` Axiom V 4 gives `x_a = x_{2a}/2`. An element of
depth `r` in a torus moves `U_{2a}` by the character `2a`, and the commutator
then gains only `r/2` in `x_a`. It does gain `r` in `x_{2a}`, which for
`s ≥ 0` puts it in `U_{2a,x,r+s}`.

## Moy–Prasad subgroups

- `moyPrasad x r = image r ⊔ sharpFiltration x (fun _ => r)`. This is the
  subgroup generated by `Z(F)_r` and the blocks `U_{a,x,r} U_{2a,x,r}`, for
  `r ≥ 0` and `x` in the apartment. It is the description in the paragraph
  after Definition 13.2.1, and Definition 7.3.3(3) at the constant concave
  function `r`. KP write `P_{x,f}` as the product `G(k)^♯_{x,f} · Z(k)_{f(0)}`.
  Under the source hypotheses it is a group, so it equals the generated
  subgroup used here.
- `image_le_moyPrasad` and `filtration_le_moyPrasad`: `Z(F)_r` and every
  `U_{a,x,r}` lie in `P_{x,r}`.
- `moyPrasad_zero`: `P_{x,0} = P_x`, the Library's point parahoric
  `P.parahoricAt x`, through `zeroCentralFiltration_zero` of
  `KPRealDepthSubgroups`.
- `moyPrasad_antitone`, `moyPrasad_le_parahoricAt`: the family decreases in
  `r` and lies in `P_x`.
- `moyPrasadPlus x r = ⨆_{s > r} P_{x,s}`, the strict-depth group
  `P_{x,r+}` of the convention of §1.6. It is contained in `P_{x,r}`
  (`moyPrasadPlus_le`). No identification of `P_{x,0+}` with the
  `0+` group of the integral models (`SpecialFibreQuotientData.positivePoints`)
  is made here.

## Points of the reduced building

`IsMoyPrasadAt y r Q` says `Q = g P_{x,r} g⁻¹` for some `g ∈ G(F)` and some
`x` in the apartment with `g · x = y` in the reduced building. It mirrors the
Library's `IsParahoricAt`. Every point has such a `Q`
(`exists_isMoyPrasadAt`), and the relation is covariant (`IsMoyPrasadAt.smul`).
Uniqueness of `Q` is KP Proposition 13.2.5(1), (2). It is the target
`MoyPrasadCovarianceStatement`, not a theorem here.

## Scope

Only real finite depths `r ≥ 0` are represented. The infinite depth term of
§1.6 is not a separate object. Its value `{1}` is the separatedness target in
`KPMoyPrasadFiltrationStatement`. Lie-algebra filtrations (§13.3), non-constant
concave functions with positive central depth, and the construction of an
actual filtration of `Z(F)` are not part of this module.
