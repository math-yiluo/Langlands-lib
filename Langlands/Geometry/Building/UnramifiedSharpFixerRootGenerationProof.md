# Sharp-fixer generation over the maximal unramified field: proof

Module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.UnramifiedSharpFixerRootGenerationProof`.

## Results

- `Langlands.Geometry.Building.unramifiedSharpFixerRootGeneration :
  UnramifiedSharpFixerRootGenerationStatement.{u}` proves the published
  proposition at every universe `u`. It is Kaletha–Prasad Lemma 7.4.4(3) over
  `K = Langlands.LocalGalois.Separable.inertiaFixedField F`. For every
  nonarchimedean local field `F : Type u`, every affine group scheme `H` over
  `Spec K`, every supplied `C : MaximalUnramified.BuildingData F H`, and every
  nonempty `Ω ⊆ A(S)`, the sharp fixer `C.sharpFixer Ω = G(K)^♯_Ω` equals the
  supremum of the affine root groups `U_ψ` over all relative roots `a` and all
  `ψ ∈ Ψ = C.affineRoots` with derivative `rootForm C.i a` and `ψ ≥ 0` on `Ω`.
  The statement and its hypotheses are unchanged.
- The derivative equality `∇Ψ = Φ` over `K` is proved inside this module by
  the private auxiliary theorem `derivativeRoots_affineRoots_eq_range_rootForm
  : UnramifiedAffineRootDerivativesStatement.{u}`. That is the exact
  proposition of the published statement module
  `UnramifiedAffineRootDerivativesStatement` (KP Proposition
  6.3.13, derivative part): for every supplied `C`,
  `AffineRootSystem.derivativeRoots C.affineRoots = Set.range (rootForm C.i)`,
  multipliable roots and their doubles included. It is private because the
  public theorem `unramifiedAffineRootDerivatives` for that statement is in the
  module `UnramifiedAffineRootDerivativesProof`. This module adds
  no public name for it.

Both theorems, the public one and the private one, use only the axioms
`propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

## Implemented proof

### Step 1: `∇Ψ = Φ` over `K`

Fix `F`, `H` and `C`. By definition, `MaximalUnramified.BuildingData F H` is
`IntegerValuationBuildingData (MaximalUnramified.valuation F) H`. So `C`
provides connected reductivity `C.isConnectedReductive`, the maximal split
torus `C.S` with inclusion `C.i` and proof `C.isMaximalSplitTorus`, the
apartment class `C.apartment`, the centralizer datum `C.centralizer`, and full
V 6 compatibility `C.isOmegaCompatible` for the normalized valuation of `K`.
`C.affineRoots` abbreviates `C.apartment.affineRoots`.

The published theorem `quasiSplitDerivativeAffineRoots` proves
`QuasiSplitDerivativeAffineRootsStatement`. That proposition covers every field
`k` with a surjective valuation `v : Valuation k ℤₘ₀` whose valuation ring is
henselian, every quasi-split affine `k`-group `G` with a maximal split torus
`i`, and every apartment `D` and centralizer datum with full V 6
compatibility. Its conclusion is `derivativeRoots D.affineRoots =
Set.range (rootForm i)`. The proof applies it with:

- `k = K` and `v = MaximalUnramified.valuation F`;
- surjectivity from `MaximalUnramified.valuation_surjective F`;
- henselianity from `MaximalUnramified.valuationSubring_isHenselian F`;
- quasi-splitness of `H` from `MaximalUnramified.isQuasiSplit F
  C.isConnectedReductive`. That published theorem states that every connected
  reductive group over `K` is quasi-split, as in KP Corollary 2.3.8;
- the torus, apartment, centralizer and V 6 datum of `C`.

The result is exactly `derivativeRoots C.affineRoots = Set.range (rootForm
C.i)`.

The route differs from KP. KP derive Proposition 6.3.13 from Proposition 6.3.8
and Lemma 6.3.9, and these use Axiom V 7 of Definition 6.1.2. The supplied data
over `K` contain V 0–V 6 but not V 7. The published quasi-split theorem avoids
V 7. It constructs a full-V 6 apartment for the same torus whose root
filtrations are algebraically bounded. Uniqueness of full-V 6 classes,
`IntegerValuation.IsOmegaCompatible.eq_of_isMaximalSplitTorus` (KP Proposition
6.2.6), identifies that apartment with the supplied `C.apartment`. Boundedness
then gives the derivative equality, including the multipliable case. This
module uses only the statement of that theorem.

### Step 2: generation

The published theorem `unramifiedSharpFixerRootGeneration_of_derivativeRoots`
(module `IntegerValuationNonnegativeRootGenerators`) derives
`UnramifiedSharpFixerRootGenerationStatement` from one hypothesis `hder`:
`derivativeRoots C.affineRoots = Set.range (rootForm C.i)` for every `F`, `H`
and supplied `C`. Its binders are `F` with its four instances, `H`,
`[IsAffineHom H.X.hom]` and `C`, in that order. `hder` is therefore definitionally
`UnramifiedAffineRootDerivativesStatement.{u}`, and the private theorem of
Step 1 is passed for it.

For context, that reduction proves `C.sharpFixer Ω` equal to the supremum
using the unit of order one from `MaximalUnramified.exists_order_eq_one F`.
For a non-multipliable root `a`, each `U_{a,Ω,0}` is generated by the groups
`U_ψ`. For a multipliable root `a`, it needs some `ψ₁ ∈ Ψ` with derivative
`a`, and `∇Ψ = Φ` supplies one. This is where KP use Corollary 6.3.10.
