# Sharp-fixer generation over the maximal unramified field

`Langlands.Geometry.Building.UnramifiedSharpFixerRootGenerationStatement.{u}`
is a Prop-valued statement of Kaletha–Prasad Lemma 7.4.4(3), specialized to
the maximal unramified extension of a nonarchimedean local field. This unit
defines the proposition; its proof is a separate obligation.

## Quantifiers and hypotheses

At every universe `u`, the proposition ranges over:

- `F : Type u` with `Field F`, `ValuativeRel F`, `TopologicalSpace F`, and
  `IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F`, in equal or mixed characteristic;
- every group scheme `H` over `Spec K`, where
  `K = Langlands.LocalGalois.Separable.inertiaFixedField F`, with
  `IsAffineHom H.X.hom`;
- every `C : MaximalUnramified.BuildingData F H`;
- every `Ω : Set C.Apartment`, subject to `Ω.Nonempty`.

Here `H` is an arbitrary affine group scheme over `K`; it need not be
presented as the base change of a group over `F`. An element `C` supplies
connected reductivity of `H`, a maximal split torus `C.S` with inclusion
`C.i`, an `N(K)`-stable equipollence class `C.apartment` of root-datum
valuations, a representing datum for `Z_H(C.S)`, and full compatibility
V6 with the normalized valuation on `K`. The valuations in that class
satisfy V0–V5; the underlying `KPApartmentData` also includes compatibility
on the split torus. The centralizer datum includes the
restriction-evaluation bijectivity used to define its valuation map.

The abbreviation `MaximalUnramified.BuildingData F H` is precisely
`IntegerValuationBuildingData (MaximalUnramified.valuation F) H`. Thus the
apartment, roots, filtrations and root groups all come from the same
supplied class and the actual constructed valuation on `K`.

The only additional premise is nonemptiness of `Ω`. There is no
boundedness condition. There is no local-field instance on `K` and no
added apartment-axiom predicate or assumed generation statement.

## Conclusion and definitions

The conclusion is the equality of subgroups of `rationalPoints H`:

```lean
C.sharpFixer Ω =
  ⨆ (a : relativeRoots C.i) (ψ : C.Apartment →ᵃ[ℝ] ℝ)
    (_ : ψ ∈ C.affineRoots) (_ : ψ.linear = rootForm C.i a)
    (_ : ∀ x ∈ Ω, 0 ≤ ψ x), C.affineRootGroup a ψ
```

`C.sharpFixer Ω` is the subgroup `H(K)^♯_Ω` generated by
`U_{a,Ω,0} = ⋂_{x∈Ω} U_{a,x,0}` for every relative root `a`, as in
Definition 7.4.1(1)–(2). The nested supremum is the subgroup generated by
the indicated subgroups, so it expresses the source's generation claim
directly.

The derivative condition identifies the linear part of `ψ` with the root
`a` on the reduced translation space `V(C.S)/V(C.S)^Φ`. For such a
functional, `C.affineRootGroup a ψ` is the actual subgroup `U_ψ` of
Definition 6.3.1: it is `U_{a,x,ψ(x)}` for any apartment point `x`.
The Library defines it by intersecting those equal subgroups over all
`x`. The final index condition means that `ψ(x) ≥ 0` for every `x ∈ Ω`.

`C.affineRoots` is exactly `Ψ` of Definition 6.3.4:
`ψ` has derivative `a ∈ Φ` and
`U_ψ ⊈ U_{ψ+} · U_{2a}(K)`. It is the smaller affine-root set, whereas
`Ψ′` only requires `U_{ψ+} ≠ U_ψ`. The statement uses the existing
`C.affineRoots` and the full actual groups `C.affineRootGroup a ψ`.

## Why every supplied datum fits the corrected source setting

KP Chapter 7 assumes a Henselian discretely valued field with normalized
value group `ℤ`, a connected reductive group, and compatible root-datum
apartments with the normalizer and conjugation actions of Axiom 4.1.4.
The authors' correction adds AS5 of Axiom 4.1.6 to that setting.

The field `K` here is the maximal unramified algebraic extension of the
complete discretely valued field `F` inside its chosen separable closure.
It is Henselian; unramifiedness preserves the normalized value group
`ℤ`. Its residue field is an algebraic closure of the finite residue field
of `F`, hence algebraically closed and perfect. The Library's
`MaximalUnramified.valuation F` realizes this normalization and is
surjective. Local compactness of `K` is not required by Chapter 7.

Connected reductivity of `H` and the maximal split torus come from `C`.
KP Corollary 2.3.8 applies over this Henselian discretely valued field
with algebraically closed residue field, so every such `H/K` is
quasi-split, including those not descended from `F`.

For a quasi-split group, Definition 6.1.27 gives the canonical compatible
class. Proposition 6.2.6 says that any two compatible root-datum
valuations are equipollent. Since `C` supplies full V6, its class is
therefore that canonical class. This applies to every supplied `C`.
The canonical class has the compatibility properties of §6.1, including
V7. Propositions 6.2.4–6.2.5 give the required normalizer action and
transport to conjugate maximal split tori, hence Axiom 4.1.4.
Propositions 6.3.13 and 6.6.2 supply the affine-root and Weyl assertions,
including AS5 in the quasi-split case, exactly as the Chapter 7
correction records. Thus the corrected source assumptions hold
mathematically for these supplied data without adding them as premises.
This source comparison supplies no Lean proof of those properties or
of the generation equality.

## Multipliable roots

The index `a : relativeRoots C.i` runs over all relative roots. When
`2a ∉ Φ`, the convention is `U_{2a}(K) = {1}` and `Ψ_a = Ψ′_a`.
When `a` is multipliable (`2a ∈ Φ`), these two fibers can differ.
The proof of Lemma 7.4.4(3) uses the groups for nonnegative roots in
both `Ψ_a` and `Ψ_{2a}` to generate the groups arising from
nonnegative functionals in `Ψ′_a`. The displayed supremum retains both
derivatives `a` and `2a`. It uses the full `U_ψ`, not its image in
`U_a(K)/U_{2a}(K)`, and requires no reduced-root-system hypothesis or
additional double-root premise.
