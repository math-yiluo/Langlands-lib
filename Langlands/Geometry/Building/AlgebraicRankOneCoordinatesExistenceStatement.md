# Existence of coherent algebraic opposite-root rank-one coordinates

`Langlands.Geometry.Building.AlgebraicRankOneCoordinatesExistenceStatement.{u}`
is an internal algebraic existence target. It asserts no valuation comparison.
Its base field `k : Type u` is arbitrary, including imperfect fields and
characteristic two.

Let `G` be a connected reductive group scheme over `k`. Supply commutative group
schemes `S,Z`, morphisms `i : S.toGrp → G`, `j : Z.toGrp → G`,
`s : S.toGrp → Z.toGrp`, and the following hypotheses:

- `i` is a maximal split torus of `G`;
- `Z.toGrp` is a torus;
- `j` represents the whole subgroup functor `torusCentralizer i`;
- `s ≫ j = i`.

The representation condition includes all scheme tests and refinements; a
centralizer on rational points alone does not suffice. No independent
quasi-splitness premise is supplied. A torus centralizer of a maximal split
torus in a connected reductive group is the algebraic quasi-split situation.
The target requires no additional affineness instance: connected reductivity
already includes affineness.

For every indivisible relative root `b` of this same `i`, it asserts both
conditional branches:

- If `2 • b` is not a relative root, there exists
  `C : AlgebraicSL2RootCoordinates i j s b` such that
  `C.rho SpecialLinear.BinaryUpper.weylMatrix ∈ normalizerPoints i`.
- If `2 • b` is a relative root, there exists
  `C : AlgebraicSU3RootCoordinates i j s b` such that the image under `C.rho`
  of the actual `SU3Root.weylElement`, bundled with its special-unitary
  membership proof, belongs to `normalizerPoints i`.

The coordinate records retain both signs on one finite separable field or
one finite separable quadratic tower, their full-centralizer characters, one
rank-one group homomorphism, and an actual induced-torus morphism with the
published diagonal point comparison. The SU3 record also retains each sign's
literal double-root identification. These are precisely the algebraic cores
defined in `AlgebraicRankOneCoordinates`; there is no injectivity requirement
on `rho`.

The SL2 Weyl matrix is `[[0,-1],[1,0]]`. The SU3 Weyl matrix is the Gram
matrix `[[0,0,1],[0,-1,0],[1,0,0]]` for the Library's Hermitian form.
`normalizerPoints i` means that conjugation induces an actual automorphism of
the supplied split torus, hence controls all its scheme points. The target
does not merely require normalization of its rational points. It does not
specify the induced lattice automorphism as an extra field.

No valuation, henselianity, apartment, common origin or depth identity occurs
in this proposition. In Task 6651 the intended specialization takes
`k = UnramifiedClosure F` and `Z = B.centralizerTorus hZ`; the building data
already supply connected reductivity, maximal splitness and the centralizer
representation. The remaining common-origin and normalized-depth comparison
is a separate obligation.

## Provenance

This proposition is an internal packaging and algebraic generalization of the
rank-one construction used by Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New
Approach* (2023), registered source
`kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`, SHA-256
`05ad9a284a2577b34d1a270228b74dfb28f520561ae7772d8f38dddb78938732`.
In the quasi-split proof of Proposition 7.1.1, printed p.239 (PDF p.270), the
authors pass to the group generated by the torus and the root groups for
rational multiples of a root, then to its simply connected cover, and then
to a restriction of scalars of SL2 or SU3. The present target packages the
algebraic coordinate maps, torus map and Weyl normalization needed before
any valuation comparison. The source does not print this exact proposition
or its bundled records. Extending that algebraic construction to the
displayed arbitrary-field hypotheses, including the supplied centralizer
presentation and both signs, is part of the proof obligation.

The registered corrections `kaletha-prasad-2024-corrections`, SHA-256
`23293f51e58d5ceecb91f834743f68058b98431797d2531142415d013fdf61ed`,
PDF p.33, add AS 5 to the Chapter 7 building hypotheses. This target contains
no building or affine-root assertion and imports no AS 5 assumption. Its
source motivation is the algebraic reduction, not the commutator estimates
or a claim that the Chapter 7 valuation hypotheses follow from reductivity.
