# Canonical centroids of original quotient facets

This internal construction uses supplied actual valuation-building data and its
full apartment axioms, including AS5. It chooses the canonical centroid of each
original quotient facet by averaging its entire finite set of actual incident
vertices in an original apartment presentation, then applying that original
chart. The averaging occurs on the original affine torsor.

The proof of chart independence uses the original quotient relation. At a
point shared by two charts, the existing normalizer comparison agrees on the
whole original closed facet. Root stabilizers commute with canonical affine
facet centroids, by full root transport and normalized affine averaging. These
facts identify the two quotient centroids. When presentations use different
points of the same original affine facet, equality of their affine centroids
finishes the comparison. No chart injectivity is used.

The quotient centroid belongs to the original open facet. The containing-facet
map is its left inverse, hence the centroid map is injective on original facets.
The construction commutes with the original rational-point action: translating
a presentation translates the same computed point.

These conclusions hold at every field universe and include rank zero. The
finite dimensional normed structures used on the translation space are the
existing root-coordinate metric structures. The result constructs neither
actual valuation data nor the apartment axioms, a Tits comparison or a
polysimplicial decomposition. Its provenance is the direct internal deduction
described above, rather than an additional assertion from the literature.
