# Wall invariance from depth-zero opposite factors and Levi transport

Author: Worker 169.

Lean module: `Langlands.Geometry.Building.UnramifiedFixedWallInvarianceReduction`.

This module defines the internal reduction statement
`UnramifiedFixedWallInvarianceReductionStatement` and proves it as
`unramifiedFixedWallInvariance_of_depthZero_of_leviTransport`. The statement says that two
statements together imply `UnramifiedFixedWallInvarianceStatement`: the depth-zero
statement `UnramifiedFixedDepthZeroOppositeStatement` (KP Proposition 9.4.9(2), restricted
form) and the published Levi transport statement `UnramifiedFixedLeviTransportStatement`.
`UnramifiedFixedOppositeReduction` then derives the published V 5 statement
`UnramifiedFixedOppositeStatement` from `UnramifiedFixedWallInvarianceStatement`.
The module also contains three general lemmas about `KPApartmentData` and three lemmas that
restate the hypotheses for fixed data with explicit binders.

## The reduction statement

```
def UnramifiedFixedWallInvarianceReductionStatement : Prop :=
  UnramifiedFixedDepthZeroOppositeStatement.{u} →
    UnramifiedFixedLeviTransportStatement.{u} →
      UnramifiedFixedWallInvarianceStatement.{u}
```

The three statements share the data of `UnramifiedFixedOppositeStatement`: a
nonarchimedean local field `F` with maximal unramified extension `K = inertiaFixedField F`,
a connected reductive `G` over `F` with maximal split torus `iS : S → G`, a torus
`iT : T → G` and `f : S ⟶ T` with `groupHom f ≫ iT = iS` such that `iT_K` is a maximal
split torus of `G_K`, opposite factor pairs `hE` for `iT_K`, an apartment class
`D : KPApartmentData iT_K ω` and a centralizer datum `C` with full V 6. A point
`z : D.Apartment` is fixed if every `σ ∈ Aut(K/F)` fixes it. `φ z` is the torus-then-field
restriction of `z`, a root-function family for `(G, S)`.

- `UnramifiedFixedDepthZeroOppositeStatement`: for fixed `z`, a root `a`,
  `g ∈ U_a(F) ∖ {1}` with `φ z a g = 0` and `g', g'' ∈ U_{-a}(F)` with
  `g' g g'' ∈ N(F)`, one has `0 ≤ φ z (-a) g'` and `0 ≤ φ z (-a) g''`.
- `UnramifiedFixedLeviTransportStatement`: for `n ∈ N(F)` and fixed `z` there is
  `h ∈ G(K)` that centralizes `S_K`, lies in the subgroup generated by the groups
  `D.filtration z χ 0` over the roots `χ` of `T_K` with `f_K ≫ χ = 0`, and makes
  `n_K h` normalize `T_K`. (It also says that `n_K h · z` is fixed; this part is not
  used.)
- `UnramifiedFixedWallInvarianceStatement`: under the hypotheses of the depth-zero statement,
  with `hm : g' g g'' ∈ N(F)`, one has `⟨g' g g'', hm⟩ • φ z = φ z`.

The reduction is internal to the Library. KP prove the last assertion of Proposition 9.4.9
from assertion (2) of the same proposition (printed p. 350 / PDF p. 381). The Lean route
replaces KP's building of `G_K` by the published apartment-class lemmas below.

## General lemmas

These hold for any field extension `K/k`, an affine smooth `G` over `k`, `iS : S → G` a
monomorphism, `iT : T → G` and `f : S ⟶ T` with `groupHom f ≫ iT = iS`, `iT_K` a
monomorphism, `S` and `T_K` split, opposite factor pairs `hE` for `iT_K` and
`D : KPApartmentData iT_K ω` for any `ω : Kˣ → ℝ`. Two private instances supply the affine
and smooth instances of `G_K` by pullback; a third private instance supplies the affine
instance of `G_K` for the local-field lemmas below.

- `KPApartmentData.rationalExtend_mem_sharpFixer_of_nonneg`: if `a` is a root of `S`,
  `g ∈ U_a(k)` and the torus-then-field restriction of `x` takes a value `≥ 0` at `g`,
  then the image `g_K` of `g` in `G(K)` lies in the sharp fixer `D.sharpFixer {x}`.

  Proof. `g` lies in the depth-`0` root filtration of the restricted family. By the
  published `rootFiltration_torusFieldRestrictedRootFunctionFamily` (module
  `RootValueTorusFieldRestriction`) this filtration consists of the `g` with
  `g_K ∈ torusRestrictedRootDepthSubgroup … a_K 0`. That subgroup is generated by groups
  `D.filtration x χ (m · 0) = D.filtration x χ 0` for roots `χ` of `T_K`. Each of these
  equals `D.rootFixer χ {x}` (`rootFixer_singleton`) and lies in `D.sharpFixer {x}`
  (`rootFixer_le_sharpFixer`).

- `KPApartmentData.iSup_filtration_zero_le_sharpFixer`: for every property `P` of roots
  of `T_K`, the subgroup generated by the groups `D.filtration x χ 0` over the roots `χ`
  with `P χ` lies in `D.sharpFixer {x}`. The proof is the last step of the previous one.

- `KPApartmentData.torusFieldRestricted_smul_eq_of_mem_sharpFixer`: assume moreover that
  `G_K` is connected reductive and `iT_K` is a maximal split torus. Let `n ∈ N_{iS}(k)` and
  `x : D.Apartment`. Let `h ∈ G(K)` centralize `S_K` and lie in the subgroup generated by
  the groups `D.filtration x χ 0` with `f_K ≫ χ = 0`, and let `w = n_K h` normalize `T_K`.
  If `n_K ∈ D.sharpFixer {x}`, then `n` fixes the torus-then-field restriction of `x`.

  Proof. By the previous lemma `h ∈ D.sharpFixer {x}`, so `w ∈ D.sharpFixer {x}`. The
  published `KPApartmentData.normalizer_smul_eq_self_of_mem_sharpFixer` (module
  `MaximalUnramifiedApartmentInjective`) gives `w • x = x`. The published
  `KPApartmentData.leviTransport_apartmentMap_torusFieldRestricted` (module
  `UnramifiedFixedNormalizerTransportReduction`) says that the restriction of `w • x` is
  `n •` (restriction of `x`). Hence `n •` (restriction of `x`) equals the restriction of
  `x`.

## Lemmas for fixed data

Three helper theorems keep elaboration within the heartbeat limit (the same pattern as
`UnramifiedValuationDescentReduction`):

- `unramifiedFixedDepthZeroOpposite_apply` restates
  `UnramifiedFixedDepthZeroOppositeStatement` with explicit binders and the instances
  supplied as instance arguments. Its proof is the application of the hypothesis.
- `unramifiedFixedLeviTransport_apply` does the same for
  `UnramifiedFixedLeviTransportStatement`, and drops the clause that the transported point
  is fixed.
- `unramifiedFixedWallInvariance_core`: for fixed data and `z : D.Apartment` (fixedness is
  not needed here), let `a` be a root, `g ∈ U_a(F)` with `φ z a g = 0`,
  `g', g'' ∈ U_{-a}(F)` with `0 ≤ φ z (-a) g'` and `0 ≤ φ z (-a) g''`, `n ∈ N(F)` with
  `n = g' g g''`, and `h` a Levi correction of `n` at `z` as above. Then `n • φ z = φ z`.

  Proof. `-a` is a root (`relativeRootNegation`). The three factors lie in
  `D.sharpFixer {z}` by `rationalExtend_mem_sharpFixer_of_nonneg` (for `g`, from
  `0 ≤ φ z a g = 0`). Since `rationalExtend` is multiplicative, `n_K` lies in
  `D.sharpFixer {z}`. `G_K` is connected reductive by
  `IsConnectedReductive.baseChange_of_algebraicExtension`. Then
  `torusFieldRestricted_smul_eq_of_mem_sharpFixer` gives `n • φ z = φ z`.

## Proof of the reduction

`unramifiedFixedWallInvariance_of_depthZero_of_leviTransport` introduces the data of
`UnramifiedFixedWallInvarianceStatement`, a fixed point `z`, a root `a`, `g`, `g'`, `g''`,
`hm` and `φ z a g = 0`. The depth-zero statement gives `0 ≤ φ z (-a) g'` and
`0 ≤ φ z (-a) g''`. Levi transport applied to `n = ⟨g' g g'', hm⟩` and `z` gives the Levi
correction `h`. `unramifiedFixedWallInvariance_core` with `n = g' g g''` (by `rfl`)
concludes.

The proofs of the explicit-binder lemmas and of the reduction use
`set_option maxHeartbeats 8000000`; the core lemma uses `1600000`.
