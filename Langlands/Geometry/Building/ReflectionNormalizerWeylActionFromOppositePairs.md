# The reflection-normalizer action from opposite-pair existence

For supplied `ValuationBuildingData B`, the construction
`ValuationBuildingData.reflectionWeylActionOfOppositePairs` is a homomorphism

```text
B.reflectionNormalizer →* affineWeylGroup B.affineRoots.
```

Its only extra premise is the existing existence predicate
`RelativeRoot.HasOppositeFactorPairs B.i`. The field `F` and actual group
scheme `G` have arbitrary universe `u`, and the usual field, valuation,
topology, nonarchimedean-local-field and affine-group instances are retained.
The domain is the existing subgroup generated by actual opposite-pair
reflection elements; its ambient group is `normalizerPoints B.i`.

The construction applies the actual apartment action
`B.apartment.normalizerAffineEquiv`. Its `_coe` and `_apply` lemmas state,
respectively, that its underlying affine equivalence is the supplied
normalizer action and its value at `x` is exactly `n.val • x`.

## Discharging AS4

The theorem `affineRoot_mem_or_two_smul_mem_of_oppositePairs` proves, for the
same supplied data,

```text
∀ ψ ∈ B.apartment.extendedAffineRoots,
  ψ ∈ B.affineRoots ∨ (2 : ℝ) • ψ ∈ B.affineRoots.
```

Smoothness follows from `B.isConnectedReductive.smooth`, and the torus is
split by `B.isMaximalSplitTorus.isSplitSubtorus.1`. The theorem then applies
`KPApartmentData.mem_or_two_smul_mem_affineRoots_of_oppositePairs` to
`B.apartment`. That imported result uses V4 and the integral-coroot bound
excluding quadruple character roots. The action is the previously constructed
`reflectionWeylActionOfAS4` supplied with this proved disjunction.

The action construction needs no local reflection-arrangement premise.

## Image and facet consequences

For the remaining results, assume
`AffineRootSystem.IsLocallyFiniteReflectionArrangement B.affineRoots`.
This is the existing five-condition real reflection-arrangement predicate:
the roots are nonconstant, their derivatives form a finite spanning set,
coroot reflections exist, and the hyperplanes are pointwise locally finite.

The theorems `reflectionNormalizer_map_eq_affineWeylGroup_of_oppositePairs`
and `reflectionWeylActionOfOppositePairs_surjective` show that the actual
reflection-generated image is the entire affine Weyl group. They specialize
the existing explicit-AS4 image and surjectivity results with the proved AS4
disjunction and the same opposite-pair-existence premise.

The theorem
`comap_affineWeylGroup_eq_reflectionNormalizer_sup_ker_of_oppositePairs`
identifies the full affine-Weyl preimage in `normalizerPoints B.i` with

```text
B.reflectionNormalizer ⊔ B.apartment.normalizerAffineEquiv.ker.
```

This is a preimage identity; it makes no claim that the joined subgroup is
the whole normalizer.

The theorem `reflectionNormalizer_facet_fixing_of_oppositePairs` then
shows that a reflection-normalizer element preserving a facet setwise fixes
every point of that facet. The final theorem
`normalizer_facet_fixing_of_mem_reflectionNormalizer_sup_ker` allows an
arbitrary actual normalizer element `n`, supplied with membership in the
joined subgroup above. The preimage identity puts its actual affine action
in the affine Weyl group, where `affineWeyl_facet_fixing` applies. In both
results `IsFacet` has its existing meaning of a full root-sign-equivalence
class, and setwise preservation is the equality of its image under the
actual point action with itself.

## Provenance and retained obligations

This is an internal adapter between the existing actual apartment data,
`AffineRootDoubling` and `ReflectionNormalizerWeylAction`. The only additional
argument beyond specialization is the final use of the preimage identity.
No new external source assertion is made.

Opposite-pair existence and local reflection-arrangement geometry remain
explicit premises. Full simply connected normalizer generation is still a
separate obligation: no theorem here places every normalizer element in
the joined subgroup. No AS5 or full apartment-axiom package is used, and
this unit does not prove `SimplyConnectedFacetFixingStatement`.
