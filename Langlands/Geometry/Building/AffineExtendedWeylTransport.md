# Extended affine Weyl groups under affine equivalences

This internal derived unit extends the existing `AffineWeylTransport` results
to the unchanged `extendedAffineWeylGroup` of `AffineWeylGroups`. Its inputs
are an arbitrary set of real affine functionals on an affine space and an
affine equivalence to another affine space. The translation spaces and
apartment carriers have independent universe parameters. No root-system
axioms or finite-dimensionality hypotheses are required.

`map_affineWeylGroup_linearHom` identifies the derivative Weyl group with the
image of the affine Weyl group under the derivative homomorphism. Both are
generated by the same reflections after taking derivatives, so the proof
maps subgroup closure and compares its generating sets.

Inverse precomposition transports root functionals and commutes with affine
conjugation. Cancelling the functional equivalence therefore proves
preservation and reflection of the complete root-stabilizer predicate.

To transport an extended affine Weyl element, the proof retains its root
stabilization and lifts its derivative to an affine Weyl element using the
image identity above. The existing affine Weyl transport theorem conjugates
that lift. Its derivative equals the derivative of the conjugated original
element, giving membership in the transported derivative Weyl group.
Applying this argument to the inverse affine equivalence proves equality of
the entire transported extended affine Weyl subgroup.

`extendedAffineWeylTransportEquiv` restricts affine conjugation to these
subgroups. Its inverse uses conjugation by the inverse affine equivalence;
the pointwise cancellation identities prove the two inverse laws.

The provenance is the existing definitions of affine reflections, root
stabilizers, derivative Weyl groups and extended affine Weyl groups, together
with `AffineWeylTransport` and inverse root transport from
`AffineRootSystemTransport`. This unit introduces no new literature
translation. It supplies an algebraic comparison needed for the AS 5
clauses of `SatisfiesApartmentAxioms`; actual building data, root comparisons
and witnesses of those clauses are separate obligations.
