# Actual simple lifts and normalizer invariance for integer-valued data

For the original `IntegerValuationBuildingData`, `affineRootReflectionLift`
chooses an actual normalizer point inducing the specified affine root reflection.
Its specification retains an actual relative root equal to the derivative and
states that the lift's Weyl action negates this relative root.

The existence proof expresses the affine root as the reflection functional of
a nonidentity root point. An actual opposite factor pair supplies the lift.
The published affine reflection formula and coroot uniqueness identify its
apartment action with the specified reflection; the integral coroot formula
gives the required Weyl negation. This construction is internal to the existing
integer-valued data and needs no extra apartment axioms.

With an order-one unit, the ordinary and extended affine-root systems, derivative
equality and AS4 are already proved for these data. They discharge all geometric
premises of the generic simple-lift cover lemma. Chamber-simple generation and
chamber transitivity then show that every actual normalizer point belongs to
the left stabilizer of `P_C N P_C`. This is normalizer invariance; full rational
point generation and the common-apartment conclusion are separate steps.
