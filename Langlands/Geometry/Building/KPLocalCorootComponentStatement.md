# KP Proposition 7.1.1 over `K = F^ur`: the `Z`-component of a rank-one commutator is a coroot image congruent to `1` at depth `r`

Lean module: `Langlands.Geometry.Building.KPLocalCorootComponentStatement`.
Declaration: `Langlands.Geometry.Building.KPLocalCorootComponentStatement : Prop`
(universe parameter `u`).

This is a statement only. Nothing here proves it.

## Setting and binders

The statement quantifies over:

- a nonarchimedean local field `F : Type u` (`Field`, `ValuativeRel`, `TopologicalSpace`,
  `IsNonarchimedeanLocalField`), in any universe and any characteristic;
- `K = UnramifiedClosure F`, the Library's maximal unramified extension of `F`;
- an affine group scheme `H` over `Spec K` (`[IsAffineHom H.X.hom]`) and building data
  `B : MaximalUnramified.BuildingData F H`, that is
  `IntegerValuationBuildingData (MaximalUnramified.valuation F) H`. This is a connected
  reductive group `G = H` over `K`, a maximal `K`-split torus `S` (`B.i`), the actual
  centralizer `Z = Z_G(S)` (`B.centralizer`), and the ω-compatible equipollence class of
  valuations of the root datum (`B.apartment`, KP Axiom V 6);
- `hZ : IsTorus B.centralizer.Z`. This is D1's binder convention (Task 5875, Library
  module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.KPLocalMoyPrasadGroups`): `Z` is a torus, and
  `B.centralizerTorus hZ` is `Z` viewed as a commutative group scheme. Its commutativity
  comes from `IsTorus.isCommMonObj`, so no structure is chosen. Over `K = F^ur` every
  connected reductive group is quasi-split, so `Z = T` is a maximal torus (KP §7.2:
  "When G is quasi-split, then Z = T is a maximal torus");
- a relative root `a : relativeRoots B.i`. Multipliable and divisible roots are included.

Notation: `Ω = SeparableClosure F` and `ω = algebraicExtensionAddValuation F Ω`. This is
the valuation that `localTorusFiltration` and `mem_localTorusFiltration_of_induced` use.

## Statement

For all such `F`, `H`, `B`, `hZ` and `a` there exist

- a commutative group scheme `T_a` over `Spec K` with `IsAffine T_a.X.left`,
  `IsTorus T_a.toGrp` and `IsInducedTorus T_a`, and
- a morphism `f : T_a ⟶ B.centralizerTorus hZ` of commutative group schemes,

such that the following holds for every `x : B.Apartment` and all `u u' : G(K)`
(`rationalPoints H`). Suppose that

- `u ∈ U_a(K)` (`B.rootGroup a`), `u ≠ 1`, `u' ∈ U_{-a}(K)` (`B.rootGroup (-a)`), `u' ≠ 1`;
- `r : ℝ≥0`, `0 < r` and `r = x.val a u + x.val (-a) u'` in `WithTop ℝ`. In KP's notation
  this is `r = φ_a(u) + φ_{-a}(u')` for the valuation `φ = x`;
- `v', v : G(K)`, `z : Z(K)` (`rationalPoints B.centralizer.Z`), `v' ∈ U_{-a}(K)`,
  `v ∈ U_a(K)` and `u'⁻¹ u u' u⁻¹ = v' · j(z) · v`, where `j = B.centralizer.j`.

Then there is `c ∈ T_a(K)` (`rationalPoints T_a.toGrp`) with

- `f(c) = z`, that is `rationalPoints.map (CommGroupScheme.groupHom f) c = z`. The point
  `f(c)` lies in `rationalPoints (B.centralizerTorus hZ).toGrp`, which is
  `rationalPoints B.centralizer.Z` by definition;
- for every character `χ` of `T_a` over `Ω`
  (`χ : rationalCharacters (baseChanged K Ω T_a.toGrp)`),
  `r ≤ ω(χ(c) - 1)`. Here `χ(c)` is `rationalCharacterEval χ (rationalExtend K Ω T_a.toGrp c)`.

The hypotheses on `x, u, u', r, v', v, z` copy the quantifier structure of
`ParahoricBuildingData.CentralizerFiltration.IsCommutatorFriendly` (module
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.KPMoyPrasadFiltration`), with `B` in place of `P`. Only the
conclusion differs: `IsCommutatorFriendly` concludes `z ∈ Zf.filtration r`. `T_a` and `f`
are chosen before `x`, `u` and `u'`, so they depend only on the data and on `a`.

The hypothesis quantifies over every factorization `u'⁻¹ u u' u⁻¹ = v' j(z) v`. Such a
factorization has a unique `z`, because `U_{-a}(K) ∩ Z(K) U_a(K) = {1}` (KP, proof of
Proposition 7.1.1(1)). This `z` is the `Z`-component of the commutator of `u'⁻¹` and `u`
in KP's Definition 7.1.3. KP show that the factorization exists when `r > 0`. The
statement does not assert existence: it only constrains `z` when a factorization is given.

## Provenance and mathematical content

KP do not state this formula as a separate result. It is extracted from the proof of
Proposition 7.1.1 for quasi-split `G` (printed pp. 239–240) and from the proof of
Lemma 7.2.3 (printed p. 246). That proof says: "one can argue as in that proof (using the
functoriality of the filtration) and reduce to computations in SL2 and SU3 ... the element
γ computed there is the Z-component of the commutator and has the desired property."
The statement records exactly this "desired property" of `γ`, in a form that does not
mention any filtration.

**Where the commutator is computed.** Let `G_a` be the simply connected cover of the
semisimple `K`-rank-one group generated by the root groups `U_{±a}` (and `U_{±2a}` when
`2a` is a root). KP's quasi-split proof replaces `G` by the group generated by `T` and the
root groups of all rational multiples of `a`, and then by its simply connected cover.
Since `G` is quasi-split over `K`, `G_a` is `R_{L/K} SL_2` or `R_{L/K} SU_3` for a finite
separable extension `L/K`. In the `SU_3` case, `SU_3` is the quasi-split special unitary
group of a separable quadratic extension `L'/L`.

**The torus `T_a` and the morphism `f`.** `T_a` is the diagonal maximal torus of `G_a`:

- `T_a = R_{L/K} G_m` for `G_a = R_{L/K} SL_2`, embedded by `γ ↦ diag(γ, γ⁻¹)`;
- `T_a = R_{L'/K} G_m` for `G_a = R_{L/K} SU_3`, embedded by
  `γ ↦ diag(γ, γ̄ γ⁻¹, γ̄⁻¹)`.

Both are induced tori, so `IsInducedTorus T_a` holds. `f` is the composite of this coroot
embedding `T_a → G_a` with the map `G_a → G`. Its image lies in the maximal torus `T = Z`.
It does not depend on `x`, `u` or `u'`.

**The element `c`.** `c` is the element `γ` of KP's computations. For the standard
Chevalley valuation (KP may replace `φ` by an equipollent valuation; this does not change
`r = φ_a(u) + φ_{-a}(u')`, because the translation parts cancel):

- `SL_2`. With `u = [[1, α], [0, 1]]` and `u' = [[1, 0], [α', 1]]`,
  `u u' = [[1, 0], [γ⁻¹α', 1]] · diag(γ, γ⁻¹) · [[1, γ⁻¹α], [0, 1]]` with `γ = 1 + αα'`.
  Here `φ_a(u) + φ_{-a}(u') = ω(α) + ω(α')`, so `ω(γ - 1) = ω(α) + ω(α') = r`.
- `SU_3`, `a` multipliable. With `u = u_a(α, β)` and `u' = u_{-a}(α', β')`,
  `u u' = x' · diag(γ, γ̄γ⁻¹, γ̄⁻¹) · x` with `γ = 1 + αα' + ββ'`. KP's root valuations
  are `φ_a(u_a(α, β)) = ½(ω(β) - μ)` and `φ_{-a}(u_{-a}(α', β')) = ½(ω(β') + μ)`
  (KP (3.2.3), (3.2.7)), so `r = ½(ω(β) + ω(β'))`. The relation `αᾱ = β + β̄` gives
  `2ω(α) = ω(β + β̄) ≥ ω(β)`, and likewise `2ω(α') ≥ ω(β')`. Hence
  `ω(αα') ≥ ½ω(β) + ½ω(β') = r` and `ω(ββ') = 2r ≥ r` (because `r > 0`). So
  `ω(γ - 1) ≥ r`. The same formula covers `u ∈ U_{2a}(K)` (then `α = 0`).
- `SU_3`, `a = 2b` divisible. Here `U_{±a} = U_{±2b}` and KP compute in the `SU_3`
  attached to `b`, with `u = u_b(0, β)` and `u' = u_{-b}(0, β')`, so `α = α' = 0` and
  `γ = 1 + ββ'`. KP's valuations are `φ_{2b}(u_b(0, β)) = ω(β) - μ` and
  `φ_{-2b}(u_{-b}(0, β')) = ω(β') + μ`, so `r = ω(β) + ω(β')` and `ω(γ - 1) = r`.
  Since `β̄ = -β` and `β̄' = -β'`, `γ` lies in `L` and `z = diag(γ, 1, γ⁻¹)`. This is the
  image of `γ` under the coroot of the group `R_{L/K} SL_2` generated by `U_{±2b}`. So
  one can take `T_a = R_{L/K} G_m` with that coroot, or the torus `R_{L'/K} G_m` and
  coroot of the `SU_3`. The statement only asserts that some `T_a` and `f` exist.

In every case `u'⁻¹ u u' u⁻¹ = (u'⁻¹ x') z (x u⁻¹)` with `z = f(γ)` (KP Definition 7.1.3),
so `c = γ` satisfies `f(c) = z`.

**Why every character satisfies the bound.** For `T_a = R_{E/K} G_m` (`E = L` or `L'`) the
characters of `T_a` over `Ω` form the free abelian group on the `K`-embeddings
`σ : E → Ω`, and `χ(c)` is a product of the `σ(γ)^{±1}`. The extension of the valuation of
`K` to `Ω` is unique, so `ω(σ(γ) - 1) = ω(σ(γ - 1)) = ω(γ - 1) ≥ r`. For `r > 0` the set
`{y ∈ Ω^× : ω(y - 1) ≥ r}` is a group, so `ω(χ(c) - 1) ≥ r` for every character `χ`.

## Use: clause 1 of S1

Clause 1 (commutator friendliness, KP Definition 7.2.1) of S1 (Task 5890, KP Lemma 7.2.3
over `K`) follows from this statement with `mem_localTorusFiltration_of_induced` and
`map_localTorusFiltration_le` (module
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.KPLocalTorusCharacterFiltration`). Given the data of
clause 1, obtain `T_a`, `f` and `c` from this statement. The congruence on `c` is the
right-hand side of `mem_localTorusFiltration_of_induced` (for the induced torus `T_a` and
`r > 0`), so `c ∈ localTorusFiltration F T_a hT_a r`. Then `map_localTorusFiltration_le`
for `f` gives `z = f(c) ∈ localTorusFiltration F (B.centralizerTorus hZ) hZ r`, which is
`B.centralizerTorusFiltration hZ r = Z(K)_r` in D1's notation. This derivation compiles
against the pinned environment. It is not part of this unit.
