# Sharp chamber conclusions from right-central core factorization

This supporting construction derives the existing `ChamberSharpConclusion`
from an explicit core inclusion. Its entry construction,
`positiveChamberSharpEquivOfCore`, is a multiplicative equivalence between the
subgroup generated by the positive depth-zero root groups and the actual
sharp-unipotent intersection. The equivalence preserves the underlying rational
point. The final theorem is `chamberSharpConclusion_of_core`.

All results use the actual `D : KPApartmentData i ω`, with `k : Type u` an
arbitrary field, actual group schemes `S` and `G`, a monomorphism `i`, and affine
`G`. No change is made to any imported definition, statement, or product map.
Put

```
X(mu) = ⨆ b : positiveRelativeRoots i mu, D.chamberRootGroup x false b
H     = D.sharpCentralizer {x}.
```

Here `X(mu)` is a supremum of actual subgroups of `rationalPoints G`.
The core premise is the set inclusion

```
(D.sharpFixer {x} : Set (rationalPoints G)) ⊆ X(-mu) * X(mu) * H.
```

The sets on the right are the subgroup carriers, and their product is
left associated. The first equivalence and range lemmas require the inclusion
for one cocharacter. The final theorem takes it for every generic `mu`, and
uses it at `ν` and `-ν`. Proving this premise from the apartment axioms remains
a separate part of the host proof.

The final theorem also assumes the existing
`RelativeRoot.DynamicRootOrderedProductStatement`, connected reductivity of
`G`, maximal splitness of `i`, the affine-root-system law for the extended roots,
the comparison of their derivatives with `Set.range (rootForm i)`, AS4, a chamber
`C`, `x ∈ C`, and genericity of `ν`. The Dynamic ordered product premise supplies
coordinate injectivity. The root-system premises supply the existing positive
collection theorem. AS4 and the chamber hypothesis supply the existing
filtration and fixer constancy results.

## Implemented proof

`positiveChamberRootGroups_le_sharpUnipotentPoints` places each positive
depth-zero root group in the corresponding Dynamic unipotent group by
`RelativeRoot.subgroup_le_dynamicUnipotent`. The singleton root-fixer identity
places it in `D.sharpFixer {x}`. Taking the subgroup supremum gives
`X(mu) ≤ D.sharpUnipotentPoints mu {x}` for any cocharacter.

`centralizer_conj_mem_dynamicUnipotent` observes that a point centralizing
the torus centralizes its cocharacter, lies in the Dynamic parabolic, and
therefore preserves the Dynamic unipotent group under conjugation.

For the reverse inclusion, take `g` in the sharp-unipotent intersection and
write `g = v * w * z` using the core inclusion. The first containment puts
`v` in the negative Dynamic group and `w` in the positive one. Since `z`
centralizes the torus, `z⁻¹ * w * z` is still positive Dynamic unipotent.
The equality

```
v * z * (z⁻¹ * w * z) = 1 * 1 * g
```

and `unipotentTripleProduct_injective` force `v = 1` and `z = 1`.
Consequently `g = w ∈ X(mu)`. This proves
`positiveChamberRootGroups_eq_sharpUnipotentPoints_of_core`.
`positiveChamberSharpEquivOfCore` is `MulEquiv.subgroupCongr` applied to
that equality. Its coercion lemma states that it changes no rational point.

`range_sharpUnipotentProduct_of_core` gives the exact range of the original
sharp three-factor map whenever `D.sharpFixer C = D.sharpFixer {x}`.
For surjectivity, rewrite the core factorization as
`v * z * (z⁻¹ * w * z)`. The conjugated last factor remains in the Dynamic
unipotent subgroup as above, and it remains in the sharp fixer because
`z` and `w` belong to that subgroup. Fixer equality transfers both unipotent
factors to the intersections over `C`. The other range inclusion follows
by closure of `D.sharpFixer C` under multiplication.

`range_orderedSharpChamberProduct_of_block_ranges` transfers the ranges of
the two original ordered block maps to the original three-factor map.
Forward inclusion packages each block product with its intersection-membership
proof; reverse inclusion chooses preimages in the supplied factor ranges.
The central coordinate and the factor order are preserved.

`chamberRootGroup_eq_zero_of_mem_chamber` uses the existing equality of the
depth-zero and depth-zero-plus filtrations at a chamber point. Hence, for either
Boolean depth, the existing positive collection theorem identifies the range
of the ordered actual root blocks with `X(mu)`. Combining this with the
intersection equality and fixer constancy gives the exact positive and
negative block ranges over `C`. The ordered maps retain their original
doubled-root blocks and all supplied orders.

Finally, `chamberSharpConclusion_of_core` assembles every clause of the
unchanged `ChamberSharpConclusion`: root-fixer constancy, equality of the two
chamber depths, the original block equalities, sharp normalizer equality,
injectivity and exact range of the sharp unipotent product, and the six
ordered injectivity/range clauses. The root comparisons come from
`chamberRootFiltrationConclusion`; normalizer equality follows from the exact
sharp product range by the published separation theorem. The existing
Dynamic ordered-product restrictions supply injectivity within each sign
and for the three-factor ordered map.

The result retains `D.sharpCentralizer {x}` throughout. It does not identify
that group with the full central factor `Z(k)^0`. The facet transport, proof
of the core premise, full central comparison, and final proof of
`ChamberIwahoriDecompositionStatement` remain host obligations.
