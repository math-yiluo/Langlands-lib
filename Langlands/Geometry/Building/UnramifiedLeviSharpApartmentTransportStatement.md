# Levi sharp transport from membership in an unramified apartment

Author: Worker 181.

Lean module: `Langlands.Geometry.Building.UnramifiedLeviSharpApartmentTransportStatement`.

This module states the geometric prerequisite for the proof of
`UnramifiedFixedLeviTransportStatement`. It defines one proposition and contains no proof.
The statement replaces the Galois-fixedness input by membership in the apartment chart of
an actual sharp-fixer quotient. It retains the maximal-unramified field and full V6
hypotheses of the consumer.

## Formal statement

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field and put `K = inertiaFixedField F`. Let `G` be a
connected reductive `F`-group and `iS : S.toGrp ⟶ G` a maximal `F`-split torus. Let
`iT : T.toGrp ⟶ G` and `f : S ⟶ T` satisfy `CommGroupScheme.groupHom f ≫ iT = iS`, with
`iT_K` a maximal `K`-split torus of `G_K`. Subscripts `K` mean the base-change functors
appearing literally in the Lean binders. In particular, `S_K` need not be a maximal
`K`-split torus.

Let `D` be `KPApartmentData iT_K` for the order of `MaximalUnramified.valuation F`, and
let `C` be a centralizer datum such that
`IntegerValuation.IsOmegaCompatible (MaximalUnramified.valuation F) D C` holds.
`D.ReducedBuilding` and `D.apartmentMap` are the sharp-fixer quotient and its apartment
chart from `KPBuilding`; no separate building datum is a hypothesis.

For every `n : normalizerPoints iS_K` and `x : D.Apartment`, assume

```
∃ y : D.Apartment, D.apartmentMap y = (n : G(K)) • D.apartmentMap x.
```

Here `G(K)` abbreviates the actual `rationalPoints G_K` type. The conclusion is an
`h ∈ G(K)` such that:

1. `h ∈ centralizerPoints iS_K`;
2. `h` belongs to the supremum of `D.filtration x χ 0` over all roots `χ` of `T_K` in
   `G_K` satisfying `f_K ≫ χ = 0`;
3. `(n : G(K)) * h ∈ normalizerPoints iT_K`.

The second condition uses the subgroup generated by the depth-zero root groups of the
Levi `M = Z_{G_K}(S_K)` at `x`, with the valuation restricted from `D`. It is the literal
iterated subgroup supremum in the Lean statement. There is no Galois-fixedness
condition and `n` can be any `K`-rational normalizer point of `S_K`.

## Derived source argument

The following explains the intended mathematical content; it is not an implemented
Lean proof. Identify the quotient chart with the apartment `A(T_K,G_K)` in the building
attached to the supplied valuation class, as in KP §7.6. The hypothesis gives a common
point `y = n·x` of the apartments `A` and `nA`.

Both `T_K` and `nT_Kn⁻¹` contain `S_K`, since `n` normalizes `S_K`. They are therefore
maximal split tori of the Levi `M`. The roots of `M` relative to `T_K` are exactly the
roots `χ` of `G_K` trivial on `S_K`. Restrict the valuation class to these roots as in
KP Lemma 6.7.1, which also preserves compatibility with the field valuation.

Use the relative Levi decomposition of KP §9.7.4, equation (9.7.3):

```
B(G_K)^M = B(M) × A(M,G_K).
```

Here the left side denotes the union of apartments of maximal split tori of `M`.
`A(M,G_K)` is an affine space with translation space `V(A_M/A_G)`, for the maximal
split central tori `A_M` and `A_G`. It is the relative central factor, as opposed to
`V(A_M)`, which occurs in the full enlarged building of `M`. Apartments on the left
are products of reduced Levi apartments with this whole relative central factor.
The subgroup `Mder(K)` acts trivially on that factor. Equivalently, after choosing a
point of it, KP §9.7.5 identifies the union with the enlarged building of `M/A_G`.

Project `y` to `B(M)` and apply KP Propositions 7.6.4(2) and 7.7.5(4) in `M` to the
two Levi apartments through that point. They supply an element `m` in the subgroup
generated by the depth-zero root groups at `y` for the starting apartment `nA`, carrying
that apartment to `A`. These root groups lie in `Mder(K)`. Thus `m` fixes both the
projected point and its relative central coordinate, so it fixes `y` in the ambient
building. The apartment transporter gives `m n T_K n⁻¹ m⁻¹ = T_K`.

Set `h = n⁻¹ m n`. Because `n` normalizes `S_K`, it normalizes `M`, and `h` centralizes
`S_K`. Conjugating the root-group generators back from `nA` at `n·x` to `A` at `x`
shows that `h` belongs to the supremum in condition 2. Finally `n h = m n` normalizes
`T_K`. This proves the stated conclusion in the source's building model.

## Provenance and proof obligations

The statement is derived from the cited results, rather than a verbatim proposition of
KP. Its source setting is the Henselian discretely valued field `K`, and full V6 for
the supplied valuation class is retained. No claim is made here for arbitrary fields
or valuations. The scalar-extension torus `S_K` is used only to define its centralizer
Levi; it is not asserted to be maximal split over `K`.

A proof must connect the Library's sharp-fixer quotient for `D` to the Levi restriction
and justify the apartment transporter and conjugation of its actual filtered root
groups. Ordinary ambient sharp-fixer transitivity supplies a correction using all
roots. It does not by itself establish condition 2, which restricts the correction
to the Levi roots. These are substantial remaining proof obligations; this module
does not assume their conclusions as axioms or assert a proof.

For the consumer, a scalar extension of an `F`-rational normalizer point is an allowed
`n`. A separate fixed-locus argument must establish chart membership. The resulting
Levi sharp correction fixes `D.apartmentMap x`; apartment-chart injectivity can then
transfer fixedness of the transported quotient point back to its apartment point.
This module states only the transporter, without that reduction.
