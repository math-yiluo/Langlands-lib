# KP Proposition 13.2.5(5),(6) on supplied data

This module defines three auxiliary objects and states parts (5) and (6) of Proposition 13.2.5
as Prop-valued `def`s: `Langlands.Geometry.Building.MoyPrasadIwahoriDecompositionStatement`,
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.MoyPrasadLeviIntersectionStatement` and their conjunction
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.MoyPrasadLeviStatement`. Nothing in the module proves them. It
extends the published modules `KPMoyPrasadFiltration`, `KPMoyPrasadFiltrationStatement`
(the opening sentence and parts (1), (2), (4)) and `KPMoyPrasadProductStatement` (part (3)),
which leave parts (5) and (6) unstated.

## Definitions

- `ParahoricBuildingData.CentralizerFiltration.leviMoyPrasad Zf j xM r`. Here `j : M ⟶ G` is
  a group homomorphism, `iM : P.S.toGrp ⟶ M` the same maximal split torus `S` inside `M`, and
  `DM : KPApartmentData iM (localFieldOrder F)` apartment data for `(M, S)` with a point `xM`.
  The definition is the subgroup of `M(F)` generated by the preimage under `j` of
  `Zf.image r = j_Z(Z(F)_r)` and by the published blocks `U^M_{a,xM,r} U^M_{2a,xM,r}` of
  `DM` (`DM.sharpFiltration xM (fun _ => r)`). It follows `Zf.moyPrasad x r`, which is
  `Zf.image r ⊔ P.apartment.sharpFiltration x (fun _ => r)`. When `M` is a Levi subgroup
  containing `S` and `j` is its inclusion, the centralizer of `S` in `M` is `Z`, so the first
  generator is `Z(F)_r` seen in `M(F)`, and the subgroup is `P^M_{xM,r}` (KP
  Definition 13.2.1 for `M`). The filtration of `Z(F)` is the same supplied `Zf` as for `G`.
- `standardComponentNonnegativePart i ν J q`, for a homomorphism `q : A.toGrp ⟶ S.toGrp` of
  tori. It is the image in `G(F)` under `q ≫ i` of the set of `a ∈ A(F)` such that
  `characterNorm F A (componentCharacterRestriction q α) a ≤ 1` for every
  `α ∈ positiveRelativeRoots i ν \ AddSubmonoid.closure J`. A positive relative root lies in
  the additive monoid generated by `J` exactly when it is a root of the standard Levi `M_J`.
  The excluded set is therefore the set of roots of `S` on `N_J`, and their restrictions to
  `A` are the set `R(A, N_J)` of KP Definition 13.2.4(2). For the normalized absolute value,
  `|α(a)| ≤ 1` is `ω(α(a)) ≥ 0`.
- `IsTopologicalIwahoriDecomposition K M N Nbar`: the published ordered multiplication
  `orderedIntersectionProduct K M N Nbar : (K ⊓ Nbar) × (K ⊓ M) × (K ⊓ N) → K` is a
  homeomorphism (`IsHomeomorph`). `G(F)` carries the published `rationalPointTopology`, and
  the intersections and `K` carry subspace topologies. Bijectivity alone is the published
  `HasIwahoriIntersectionDecomposition`.

## The statements

Both statements cover every nonarchimedean local field `F : Type u`, in equal and mixed
characteristic, and every `G` over `Spec F` with `[IsAffineHom G.X.hom]`. They cover all
`P : ParahoricBuildingData G` and `Zf : P.CentralizerFiltration` with
`P.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms` (V 7 and AS 2–AS 5; V 6 is in the data) and
`Zf.SatisfiesFiltrationAxioms`. These are the premises of the published Moy–Prasad
statements. Each statement takes an apartment point `x`, a depth `r : ℝ≥0` with `0 < r`, a
cocharacter `ν` with `IsGenericCocharacter P.i ν` (a positive system) and a set
`J ⊆ simpleRelativeRoots P.i ν`. The standard Levi `M_J`, its unipotent radical `N_J` and the
opposite radical `N_J⁻` are the published `standardLeviPoints P.i ν J`,
`standardUnipotentPoints P.i ν J` and `standardOppositeUnipotentPoints P.i ν J` in `G(F)`.

`MoyPrasadIwahoriDecompositionStatement` (part (5)) asserts two things:

1. `IsTopologicalIwahoriDecomposition (Zf.moyPrasad x r) M_J N_J N_J⁻`: the product
   `(P_{x,r} ∩ N_J⁻(F)) × (P_{x,r} ∩ M_J(F)) × (P_{x,r} ∩ N_J(F)) → P_{x,r}` is a
   homeomorphism.
2. Let `A` be a commutative group scheme and `q : A.toGrp ⟶ P.S.toGrp` a homomorphism with the
   published `IsCompatibleStandardSplitComponent P.i ν J A q`: `A` is the split component of a
   closed connected reductive representative of `M_J`, mapped into `S`. Then
   `WeakChamberContraction (Zf.moyPrasad x r) N_J N_J⁻ (standardComponentNonnegativePart P.i ν J q)`
   holds. For every `a` in that set, `a (P_{x,r} ∩ N_J(F)) a⁻¹ ⊆ P_{x,r} ∩ N_J(F)` and
   `a⁻¹ (P_{x,r} ∩ N_J⁻(F)) a ⊆ P_{x,r} ∩ N_J⁻(F)`.

`MoyPrasadLeviIntersectionStatement` (part (6)) uses the published closed Levi
`Standard.leviScheme P.i ν J` with inclusion `Standard.leviHom P.i ν J` and torus
`Standard.leviTorusHom P.i ν J`, with the instances supplied as in the published
`SpecialLeviInheritance`. For all apartment data `DM` of `(M_J, S)` and every point `xM` with
the published `IsLeviRestriction (Standard.leviHom P.i ν J) x xM`, it asserts
`(Zf.leviMoyPrasad (Standard.leviHom P.i ν J) xM r).map (rationalPoints.map (Standard.leviHom P.i ν J)) = Zf.moyPrasad x r ⊓ standardLeviPoints P.i ν J`.
That is, `P_{x,r} ∩ M_J(F)` is the image of `P^{M_J}_{xM,r}`. `IsLeviRestriction` says that
`xM_a(m) = x_a(j m)` for every relative root `a` of `(M_J, S)` and every `m ∈ M_J(F)`: `xM` is
the image `π_S(x)` of KP §6.7. The published `leviRestrictedApartmentData` and
`isLeviRestriction_leviApartmentProjection` (module
`Langlands.BruhatTits.SpecialCompact.LeviApartmentRestriction`) construct such `DM` and `xM`
for a closed representative of the centralizer of a cocharacter. So the premise is
satisfiable whenever `M_J` is presented that way, as it is by any standard cocharacter of `J`.

`MoyPrasadLeviStatement` is the conjunction of the two statements.

### Reading of Definition 13.2.4(2)

The printed first inclusion is `aN^+a^{-1} ⊂ N`. Since `a ∈ A_M(k) ⊂ M(k)` normalizes `N`,
the inclusion into `N(k)` holds for every `a` and needs no sign condition. The statement uses
the inclusion into `N^+ = K ∩ N(k)`. This is the analogue of the printed second inclusion
`a^{-1}N^-a ⊂ N^-`, and it is what the sign condition gives: conjugation by `a` raises the depth
of each root subgroup of `N` by `ω(α(a)) ≥ 0`. It is the condition `WeakChamberContraction`
of Renard's Iwahori decompositions in the Library. This is a derived reading, not an authors'
erratum.

### How the statements follow from part (3)

This derivation follows the proof of Proposition 13.2.5 and is not written out by KP.
Choose the positive system of `ν`; it contains `J`, a system of simple roots of `M_J`. Order
the indivisible negative roots with those of `M_J` last and the positive ones with those of
`M_J` first. Part (3) (`MoyPrasadProductStatement`) then gives
`P_{x,r} = X^-_N · X^-_M · Z(k)_r · X^+_M · X^+_N` with unique factors. Here `X^-_N` lies in
`N_J⁻(F)`, `X^+_N` in `N_J(F)` and `X^-_M · Z(k)_r · X^+_M` in `M_J(F)`. The multiplication
`N_J⁻ × M_J × N_J → G` is an open immersion. So an element of `P_{x,r} ∩ M_J(F)` has trivial
`N_J^±` factors, which gives the bijection of (5)(1) and the equality (6). The restriction
`IsLeviRestriction` identifies `U^{M}_{a,xM,r}` with `U_{a,x,r}` for the roots `a` of `M_J`.
The homeomorphism of (5)(1) is the restriction of the open immersion on rational points. For
(5)(2), conjugation by `t ∈ S(F)` maps `U_{a,x,r}` onto `U_{a,x,r+ω(a(t))}` (the
compatibility of the valuation with `ω`). Every root of `N_J` has `ω(a(t)) ≥ 0` for `t` in the
image of `standardComponentNonnegativePart`. By (5)(1), `P_{x,r} ∩ N_J(F) = X^+_N`.

## Provenance and scope

The provenance is derived, as explained above. The data are supplied, as in the published
Moy–Prasad statements. `P` satisfies the apartment axioms, and `Zf` is a supplied filtration
with the four explicit properties; its existence is the separate target
`CentralizerFiltrationExistenceStatement`. KP work over Henselian discretely valued fields;
`F` is specialized to nonarchimedean local fields.

- Parabolics. KP allow any parabolic `P = MN` with `x ∈ B(G)^M`. The statements take the
  parabolic subgroups containing the maximal split torus `S` of the data, as standard
  parabolics for all generic `ν` and all `J`. Every parabolic subgroup containing `S` is of
  this form. The apartment point `x` lies in `A(S)` and hence in `B(G)^{M_J}`.
- Split component. KP take `A_M` the maximal split central torus of `M`. Part (5)(2) is stated
  for every `A` and `q` with `IsCompatibleStandardSplitComponent`, and for all `a ∈ A(F)`
  satisfying the sign condition, not only for a lattice section.
- Depth. Only `r > 0` is stated, as in (5) and in the proof of (6). Part (6) at `r = 0`
  (Corollary 9.7.3) and the groups `P_{x,r+}` are not included.
- Compactness. KP's Definition 13.2.4 is for compact open `K`. Openness and boundedness of
  `P_{x,r}` are the published `MoyPrasadBoundedOpenStatement`; they are not repeated here.
- The image `x_M` is the apartment-level `π_S` of §6.7, encoded by `IsLeviRestriction` for
  arbitrary apartment data `DM` of `(M_J, S)`. The building `B(M)` and the bijection (9.7.1)
  are not formalized here.
