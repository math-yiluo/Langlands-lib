# Chambers through a twisted-fixed point stable under one topological generator

This module states `UnramifiedTwistedGeneratorChamberStatement`. It is the
residue-field core of `UnramifiedTwistedChamberThroughFixedPointStatement`, restricted to a
single topological generator of the Galois group. Nothing in this module is proved.

## Statement

The data and hypotheses are exactly those of
`UnramifiedTwistedChamberThroughFixedPointStatement`. Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field
(at universe `u`), `K = inertiaFixedField F` its maximal unramified extension inside the
separable closure, `Γ = Aut(K/F)` (`K ≃ₐ[F] K`) and `G_K = baseChanged F K G`. The proposition
quantifies over

- an `F`-group scheme `G` with `IsConnectedReductive G`;
- a commutative `F`-group scheme `T` with a homomorphism `iT : T → G` whose scalar extension
  `T_K → G_K` is a maximal `K`-split torus (`hiT`);
- an equipollence class `D` of valuations of the root datum of `(G_K, T_K)` for the order of
  the normalized valuation of `K`, a centralizer datum `C` of `T_K`, axiom V 6 for `D` and `C`
  (`h6`), and the stability of `D` under the coefficient action of `Γ` (`hD`);
- a map `c : Γ → G(K)` with `c (σ * τ) = c σ * σ(c τ)` for all `σ, τ`, where
  `σ(g) = rationalConjugate F K G σ g`, and a finite-dimensional intermediate field `E` of
  `K/F` with `c σ = 1` for every `σ` in the fixing subgroup of `E`.

Write `B = UnramifiedBuildingDescent.buildingData iT hGK hiT D C h6`, with `hGK` the connected
reductivity of `G_K` from `connectedReductiveFieldExtensionStatement`, and
`action σ = (UnramifiedBuildingDescent.semilinearData iT hGK hiT C h6 hD).action σ` for the
coefficient action of `σ` on the reduced building `B.ReducedBuilding`. The twisted action of
`σ` is `z ↦ c σ • action σ z`. Let `Ψ` be `B.affineRoots`, the affine roots on the apartment
`B.Apartment`.

The proposition asserts the following. Let `y` be a point of `B.ReducedBuilding` with
`c σ • action σ y = y` for every `σ ∈ Γ`. Then there is `φ ∈ Γ` such that:

1. (topological generator) for every finite-dimensional intermediate field `E` of `K/F` and
   every `σ ∈ Γ` there is an integer `n` with `σ⁻¹ * φ ^ n` in the fixing subgroup
   `E.fixingSubgroup`; that is, the powers of `φ` meet every coset of the fixing subgroup of
   every finite subextension;
2. there is a subset `Y` of `B.ReducedBuilding` with
   `(fun z => c φ • action φ z) '' Y = Y` (stability under the twisted action of `φ` alone),
   and there are `g ∈ G(K)` and apartment points `x₀`, `x` with
   - `ψ x₀ ≠ 0` for every `ψ ∈ Ψ`;
   - `Y = (fun z => g • B.apartmentMap z) '' AffineRootSystem.facet Ψ x₀`;
   - `x ∈ AffineRootSystem.facetClosure Ψ x₀`;
   - `g • B.apartmentMap x = y`.

The last clause is the same chamber presentation as in clause 3 of
`UnramifiedTwistedChamberThroughFixedPointStatement`: `Y` is the translate by `g` of the
apartment chamber of the generic point `x₀`, and `y` is the translate of a point of the closed
chamber. In particular `Y` is a chamber of `B` by the definition of
`IntegerValuationBuildingData.IsChamber`, so that clause is not repeated.

The generator `φ` is existentially quantified: the prover chooses it. A Frobenius element
(an automorphism of `K` inducing `x ↦ x^q` on the residue field, `q` the order of the residue
field of `F`) satisfies clause 1, because every finite subextension of `K/F` is cyclic with
Galois group generated by the restriction of Frobenius. No definition of Frobenius is needed in
the statement.

## Source

Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits theory: a new approach* (2023), with `k = F`, `K` its maximal
unramified extension, `f` the residue field of `k` and `f̄` the algebraic closure of `f`, the
residue field of `K`:

- Proposition 9.10.1, proof of (4) ⇒ (1) (printed p. 381, PDF p. 412). For a `k`-facet `F`,
  Axiom 4.1.22 (printed pp. 163–164, PDF pp. 194–195) gives a `Γ`-equivariant bijection
  between the `f̄`-Borel subgroups of the special fibre `G_F^0` and the `K`-chambers whose
  closure contains `F`. A Borel subgroup of `G_F^0` defined over `f` gives a `Γ`-invariant
  chamber.
- Proposition 9.10.4 (printed p. 382, PDF p. 413). As `f` is finite, `G_F^0` is quasi-split
  over `f` (Theorem 2.3.3(2), printed p. 79, PDF p. 110, which is Steinberg's theorem; over a
  finite field it is Lang's theorem).
- Proof of Theorem 10.6.1 (printed p. 397, PDF p. 428). For a cocycle `c`, the twist `_cG` has
  the same building over `K` with the twisted action `x ↦ c(γ) γ(x)`, and the preceding
  propositions are applied to `_cG`.

Over the finite field `f`, the Galois group `Gal(f̄/f) ≅ Γ` is topologically generated by the
Frobenius, and an `f̄`-Borel subgroup of `G_F^0` is defined over `f` exactly when it is stable
under the (twisted) Frobenius. Lang's theorem supplies such a Borel subgroup. The present
statement records this single-generator conclusion.

## Provenance

This is a derived statement, not a numbered result of the source. It is the conclusion of the
argument cited above for the twist `_cG` and the `k`-facet of the twisted-fixed point `y`,
restricted to stability under one topological generator `φ` of `Γ` instead of under all of
`Γ`. It is implied by `UnramifiedTwistedChamberThroughFixedPointStatement` together with the
existence of a Frobenius element of `Γ`: a chamber stable under the twisted action of every
`σ ∈ Γ` is in particular stable under that of `φ`. Conversely, stability under all of `Γ`
follows from stability under `φ` once one knows that the twisted action on a chamber is
locally constant in `σ` (it factors through the Galois group of a finite subextension), since
the powers of `φ` meet every coset; that reduction is proved separately. The statement keeps the
data, hypotheses and chamber presentation of `UnramifiedTwistedChamberThroughFixedPointStatement`
unchanged, uses the Library's supplied `Γ`-stable apartment class `D` and its coefficient action,
and mentions neither Borel subgroups nor special fibres. It does not assert that the data `D`,
`C` exist, nor that a twisted-fixed point exists.
