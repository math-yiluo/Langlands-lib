# Ordered positive root factors of apartment-point stabilizers, without apartment axioms

Module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.GenericOrderedFixerFactors`, namespace
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.KPApartmentData`.

The setting is arbitrary supplied apartment data `D : KPApartmentData i ω` over a
field `k : Type u`, with `i : S.toGrp ⟶ G` a monomorphism into an affine group
scheme. No apartment axiom (AS 1–AS 5, V 7) is assumed.

## Statements

- `exists_indivisiblePositiveRoot_eq_or_double_of_factorization`: if `G` is smooth, `S`
  is split and `i` has rank-one factorizations, every actual root `b` positive on
  a cocharacter `ν` equals an indivisible positive root `a` or `2 • a`.
- `range_orderedIndivisiblePositiveRootGroupProduct_eq_iSup_of_factorization`: under the
  same hypotheses, for every enumeration `a`, not necessarily injective, with range exactly
  the indivisible positive roots of `ν`, the ordered products of the depth-zero
  (or depth-`0+`) chamber root groups `U_{a r, x, 0}` in the given order are exactly
  the subgroup generated by all positive chamber root groups at `x`.
- `mem_filtration_of_orderedProduct_smul_apartmentMap_eq`: let `G` be connected
  reductive, `i` a maximal split torus, `ν` a generic cocharacter and `a` an
  injective enumeration of the indivisible positive roots of `ν`. If points
  `u r ∈ U_{a r}(k)` have ordered product fixing the image of the apartment point
  `x` in the reduced building, then every `u r` lies in `D.filtration x (a r) 0`.

## Implemented proof

The first theorem takes the positive-integral ray `b = m • a` given by the
published `RelativeRoot.exists_indivisiblePositiveRoot_ray` and applies the
published axiom-free ray property `hasRayProperty_of_rankOne`
(`GenericPointSharpExchange`) to `eval (m • a) = m • eval a`. The alternative
`a = 2 • b` is excluded by pairing with `ν`.

The second theorem is the published collection argument
`range_orderedIndivisiblePositiveRootGroupProduct_eq_iSup` of
`ChamberPositiveCollection`, copied with one change: its only use of the
apartment axioms (AS 2 and the derivative comparison, through
`exists_indivisiblePositiveRoot_eq_or_double`) is replaced by the first theorem.
The height collection, V 3 commutator bounds and V 4 doubled-root inclusions are
those of the published proof.

For the third theorem, each `u r` lies in the dynamic unipotent subgroup of `ν`
(`RelativeRoot.subgroup_le_dynamicUnipotent`), so their product does. The
published axiom-free `mem_positiveRootGroups_of_mem_unipotent_of_smul_apartmentMap_eq`
places the product in the subgroup generated by the positive depth-zero root
groups, which by the second theorem is an ordered product of factors
`v r ∈ U_{a r, x, 0}` in the same order. The published
`orderedRootSubgroupPoints_injective_of_statement`, applied with the proved
`RelativeRoot.dynamicRootOrderedProduct`, gives uniqueness of ordered factors in
the actual root groups, so `u r = v r`.

## Provenance

This is the depth-zero form of Kaletha–Prasad (2023), Lemma 7.3.11(2) and
Proposition 7.3.12(1),(3), printed pp. 249–251 / PDF pp. 280–282
(`kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`), restricted to the
consequence used for integral open cells: ordered factors of a point stabilizer
element of `U⁺(k)` are point stabilizers. The source works with apartment data
satisfying its axioms; the implementation shows that the depth-zero factor
statement needs none beyond the valuation axioms built into `KPApartmentData`.

The `_of_factorization` names distinguish these supplied-factorization
statements from the connected-reductive and maximal-split-torus statements in
[GenericApartmentFixer](GenericApartmentFixer.md). Their hypotheses are retained.
