# Exact local-field comparisons for integer-valued building data

Worker 27 constructs an equivalence between the existing `ValuationBuildingData G` over a nonarchimedean local field `F : Type u` and `IntegerValuationBuildingData (FML.normalizedMultiplicativeValuation F) G`. Both mixed and equal characteristic are allowed. `ofLocal` and `toLocal` retain the connected-reductive witness, actual maximal split torus and its inclusion, actual `CentralizerDatum`, and root-function equipollence class. The two round-trip laws give `localEquiv`. These are conversions of supplied data; they assert no existence of compatible apartments or bijectivity of restriction evaluation for every centralizer.

The prior modules prove the exact scalar and character comparisons: `IntegerValuation.orderUnits_normalized`, `order_normalized`, `rationalCharacterValuation_normalized`, `realCharacterValuation_normalized`, and `characterValuationKernel_normalized`; the centralizer comparisons are `centralizerValuation_normalized`, `centralizerTranslation_normalized` and `isCentralizerCompatible_normalized`. The present module uses these proved equalities, with no new comparison hypothesis. `KPApartmentData.isOmegaCompatible_normalized` explicitly identifies full V6 on the transported equipollence class with the existing local class predicate. Both bundle conversions discharge their V6 field by the family comparison and equality of the transported carriers.

`KPApartmentData.reindexOrder` transports existing data along any equality of field orders. Equality elimination proves its carrier and round-trip laws, its affine apartment equivalence, its equivariant reduced-building equivalence and their compatibility with the existing maps, filtrations and fixers. These constructions introduce no second quotient. The apartment equivalence preserves every root-function value and all translation vectors; the normalizer action is unchanged. The building equivalence commutes with the action of every actual rational point and intertwines the two apartment maps. Root filtrations and their positive-depth variants, root fixers, positive-depth root fixers, sharp fixers, full point fixers and pointwise fixers agree under the stated transports. For subsets, the transported subset is its image under the appropriate equivalence. The bundle round trips follow by reducing the records, cancelling the two equality transports and proof irrelevance.

For a local bundle `B`, these identifications are exposed as `ofLocalApartmentEquiv`, `ofLocalBuildingEquiv` and the `ofLocal*` laws. The actual root subgroup is literally unchanged. `ofLocal_translation` identifies the negative centralizer translations. Finally `ofLocal_oneStabilizer` proves equality of subgroups of the same `rationalPoints G`:

`(ofLocal B).oneStabilizer (ofLocalBuildingEquiv B y) = B.compactStabilizer y`.

Its proof rewrites the transported full fixer and the exact kernel comparison. Thus it recovers the existing subgroup `rationalCommonNormKernel G ∩ B.apartment.reducedFixer y`; the result is more specific than an abstract isomorphism of stabilizers. The existing name `compactStabilizer` supplies no new compactness theorem here.
