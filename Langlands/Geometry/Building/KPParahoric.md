# Parahoric and Iwahori subgroups

This module belongs to the task on actual Kaletha–Prasad affine-root,
special-point, parahoric and compact-stabilizer objects (objective item 3).
`ParahoricBuildingData G` adds to the building data `ValuationBuildingData G`
the data that define `Z(F)^0` and `G(F)^0` (module `KPZeroSubgroups`), for the
same maximal split torus `S` and the same centralizer `Z = Z_G(S)`. From these
it defines the groups `P_Ω = G(F)^0_Ω` of KP Definition 7.4.1, the parahoric
subgroup `P_F` of a facet, the Iwahori subgroup `P_C` of a chamber, the
parahoric and Iwahori subgroups of `G(F)`, and the parahoric subgroup of a
point of the reduced building.

`P_x`, `K_x = G(F)^1 ∩ G(F)_x` and the full fixer `G(F)_x` are three separate
definitions. `K_x` and `G(F)_x` are `compactStabilizer` and `fixer` of
`ValuationBuildingData`. Nothing here relates them beyond their definitions.

## Inputs

- The field `F` and the group `G` are those of `ValuationBuildingData`: a
  nonarchimedean local field `F` (`[Field F] [ValuativeRel F]
  [TopologicalSpace F] [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F]`) and
  `G : GroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of F))` with `[IsAffineHom G.X.hom]`.
  `G(F)` is `rationalPoints G`.
- The torus `i : S → G`, the apartment `A(S)`, the reduced building, the
  actual root groups and the fixers are those of `ValuationBuildingData G`.
  The centralizer `j : Z → G` is its field `centralizer`.
- `G(F)^0` and `Z(F)^0` come from `ZeroSubgroupDatum i j` of
  `KPZeroSubgroups`.
- `G(F)^1` is the published `Plancherel.rationalCommonNormKernel G`: the `g`
  with `|χ(g)| = 1` for every rational character `χ`.
- `N(F)` is the published `normalizerPoints i`.
- Facets and chambers of the apartment are the published
  `AffineRootSystem.IsFacet` and `AffineRootSystem.IsChamber` for the affine
  roots `P.affineRoots`. Both are classes of the facet relation of a point, so
  they are non-empty.

## Declarations

- `ParahoricBuildingData G` extends `ValuationBuildingData G` by one field,
  `zeroDatum : ZeroSubgroupDatum i centralizer.j`. Its data for `Z(F)^0` use
  the same `S` and the same closed subgroup `j : Z → G` as the building data.
- `map_conj_map_conj` and `map_conj_one`: conjugating a subgroup by `g` and
  then by `h` is conjugating by `h * g`, and conjugating by `1` does nothing.
  These lemmas support the conjugation laws below.
- `P.centralizerZero` is `Z(F)^0`, `P.simplyConnectedImage` is `G(F)^♮` and
  `P.zeroSubgroup` is `G(F)^0` (Definition 2.6.23), all abbreviations for
  the fields of `zeroDatum`. `P.normOneSubgroup` is `G(F)^1`, an abbreviation
  for `rationalCommonNormKernel G`.
- `P.parahoric Ω = j(Z(F)^0) ⊔ P.sharpFixer Ω` is `P_Ω = G(F)^0_Ω` for
  `Ω ⊆ A(S)` (Definition 7.4.1(4)). Here `P.sharpFixer Ω` is `G(F)^♯_Ω` of
  `ValuationBuildingData`, generated by the `U_{a,Ω,0}`. `P.parahoricAt x` is
  `P_x = P.parahoric {x}`. Laws: `map_centralizerZero_le_parahoric`,
  `sharpFixer_le_parahoric`, `rootFixer_le_parahoric`
  (`U_{a,Ω,0} ⊆ P_Ω`) and `parahoric_anti` (`Ω ⊆ Ω'` gives `P_{Ω'} ⊆ P_Ω`).
- `P.zeroNormalizer Ω = N(F) ⊓ P_Ω` is `N^0_Ω` (Definition 7.4.1(5), first
  equality).
- `P.IsParahoricSubgroup Q`: `Q = g P_F g⁻¹` for some `g ∈ G(F)` and some
  facet `F` of the apartment (Definition 7.4.5(1), (3)).
  `P.IsIwahoriSubgroup Q`: the same with a chamber (Definition 7.4.5(2),
  (4)). Laws: `isParahoricSubgroup_parahoric` and
  `isIwahoriSubgroup_parahoric` (`P_F` and `P_C` qualify),
  `IsParahoricSubgroup.conj` and `IsIwahoriSubgroup.conj` (stable under
  conjugation), and `IsIwahoriSubgroup.isParahoricSubgroup` (a chamber is a
  facet).
- `P.IsParahoricAt y Q`, for a point `y` of the reduced building:
  `Q = g P_x g⁻¹` for some `g ∈ G(F)` and some `x ∈ A(S)` with
  `y = g · [1, x]`. Laws: `isParahoricAt_apartmentMap` (`P_x` is a parahoric
  subgroup at `[1, x]`) and `IsParahoricAt.smul` (`h Q h⁻¹` is one at `h · y`).

## Conventions and derived choices

- Joins. KP's `Z(k)^0 · G(k)^♯_Ω` is a product of subgroups. Here it is the
  join `⊔`, the subgroup generated, as in the opening of Chapter 7. The
  product set is a subgroup, equal to the join, because `Z(k)^0` normalizes
  each `U_{a,Ω,0}`: `Z(k)^0 ⊆ Z(k)^1`, and `Z(k)^1` acts trivially on `A`.
  This is not proved here.
- Empty `Ω`. KP take `Ω` non-empty. The Lean definitions are total. For
  `Ω = ∅`, `U_{a,∅,0}` is `U_a(F)` (see `ValuationBuildingData`) and
  `P.parahoric ∅` carries no meaning. Facets and chambers are non-empty, so
  `IsParahoricSubgroup` and `IsIwahoriSubgroup` never use `Ω = ∅`.
- Parahoric subgroups of points of the building (`IsParahoricAt`). KP define
  `G(k)^0_Ω` for subsets of an apartment. By Proposition 7.6.4, for `Ω` inside
  a facet it is the stabilizer of `Ω` in `G(k)^0` and depends only on `Ω` as
  a subset of `B(G)`. Since `G(k)^0` is normal, the stabilizer of `g · x` in
  `G(k)^0` is `g P_x g⁻¹`. `IsParahoricAt y Q` uses this description as its
  definition. It is a predicate: that it determines `Q` from `y` (the
  independence of `g` and `x`) is part of Proposition 7.6.4 and is not
  proved.
- One apartment. As in `ValuationBuildingData`, there is one torus `S` and
  one apartment `A(S)`. Facets of other apartments are `G(F)`-translates, so
  conjugation by `G(F)` covers them in Definition 7.4.5(3), (4).

## What is not proved or defined

- Existence. `ParahoricBuildingData G` is a type of data, not a typeclass.
  Nothing says it is inhabited. That needs the existence of
  `ValuationBuildingData G` (see its Markdown) and of `ZeroSubgroupDatum`
  (see `KPZeroSubgroups`).
- That the join equals the product set, and the second equality
  `N^0_Ω = N^♯_Ω · Z(k)^0` of Definition 7.4.1(5).
- `Z(k) ∩ G(k)^0_Ω = Z(k)^0` (Lemma 7.4.3) and dependence only on the facet
  (Lemma 7.4.4).
- That `P_Ω ⊆ G(F)^0`, and Proposition 7.6.4: `P_x = G(F)^0 ∩ G(F)_x`,
  well-definedness of `IsParahoricAt`, and agreement with Definition 4.1.3.
- Relations among `P_x`, `K_x` and `G(F)_x`, such as `P_x ⊆ K_x ⊆ G(F)_x`.
  None is asserted.
- Openness and compactness of `P_Ω`. No topology on `G(F)` is used.
- `N^♯_Ω` and `Z^♯_Ω` (Definition 7.4.1(3)) are not restated under
  `ParahoricBuildingData`. They are `P.apartment.sharpNormalizer Ω` and
  `P.apartment.sharpCentralizer Ω` of the module `KPBuilding`.
- Not defined: the enlarged building.
