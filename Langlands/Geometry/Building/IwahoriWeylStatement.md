# Iwahori–Weyl group, Iwasawa decomposition and the corrected KP Theorem 7.8.1

Module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.IwahoriWeylStatement`.

This module states, without proving them, the following results of Kaletha–Prasad on the
actual building data `P : ParahoricBuildingData G` of a connected reductive group `G` over a
nonarchimedean local field `F`, with `N = N_G(S)`, `Z = Z_G(S)` and the Iwahori–Weyl group
`W̃^0 = N(F)/Z(F)^0` of the module `IwahoriWeyl`:

| --- | --- | --- |
| Theorem 7.8.1(1) | `IwahoriWeylExactSequence` | `IwahoriWeylExactSequenceStatement` |
| 7.8.2 | `IwahoriWeylChamberSplitting C` | `IwahoriWeylChamberSplittingStatement` |
| Theorem 7.8.1(2), corrected proof | `IwahoriDoubleCosetBijection C` | `IwahoriDoubleCosetStatement` |
| Theorem 7.8.1(3) | `ParahoricDoubleCosetBijection F₁ F₂` | `ParahoricDoubleCosetStatement` |
| Theorem 5.3.3 | `IwasawaDecomposition C ν` | `IwasawaDecompositionStatement` |
| Lemma 5.3.5, replaced | `UnipotentNormalizerCoset C ν` | `UnipotentNormalizerCosetStatement` |
| Theorem 7.8.1(4), added | `UnipotentIwahoriDoubleCosetBijection C ν` | `UnipotentIwahoriDoubleCosetStatement` |

`IwahoriWeylBijectionsStatement` is the conjunction of the seven global statements, each
with its own hypotheses; it adds nothing else. All fifteen declarations are `Prop`-valued
definitions. The global statements are closed propositions quantifying over all fields `F`,
groups `G` and building data `P`; they are the targets for later proofs.

## Objects

- `I = P_C` is `P.parahoric C` of `KPParahoric`, for a chamber `C` of the apartment
  (`AffineRootSystem.IsChamber P.affineRoots C`); `P_{F_i}` is `P.parahoric F_i`.
- `W̃^0 = P.IwahoriWeylGroup`, `W_aff = (N(F) ∩ G(F)^0)/Z(F)^0 = P.affinePart`,
  `W̃^0 → G(F)/G(F)^0 = P.toComponents`, `W̃^0_C = P.chamberStabilizer C` and
  `W^aff_F = P.affineFacetStabilizer F` are the definitions of `IwahoriWeyl`.
- `W(Ψ) = AffineRootSystem.affineWeylGroup P.affineRoots` is the group of affine
  automorphisms of the apartment generated by the reflections in the affine roots, KP's
  `W_aff` of §6.6.
- `U(F) = standardUnipotentPoints P.i ν ∅`, for a generic cocharacter `ν` of `S`
  (`IsGenericCocharacter P.i ν`: no relative root vanishes on `ν`). The cocharacter `ν`
  fixes the positive system `Φ^+ = {a | ⟨a, ν⟩ > 0}`; `standardParabolic P.i ν ∅` is the
  corresponding minimal parabolic subgroup `ZU` containing `S` of KP §5.3, and
  `standardUnipotent P.i ν ∅` is its unipotent radical, defined in the Library as the
  intersection of the dynamic unipotent subgroups `U(ζ)` over all cocharacters `ζ` positive on
  the simple roots of `Φ^+`. Every positive system of `Φ(S, G)` is of this form, so
  quantifying over generic `ν` covers every minimal parabolic subgroup containing `S`. (That
  this intersection equals `U(ν)` for the single cocharacter `ν` is true but is not proved in
  the Library; the statements use the published `standardUnipotentPoints` as the task
  prescribes.)
- `Z(F)^0` inside `G(F)` is `P.centralizerZero.map (rationalPoints.map P.centralizer.j)`;
  inside `N(F)` it is `P.normalizerZero`.

## Ambient hypotheses

All statements quantify over every nonarchimedean local field `F` (of either characteristic,
with the instances `Field`, `ValuativeRel`, `TopologicalSpace`, `IsNonarchimedeanLocalField`),
every affine `F`-group scheme `G` and every `P : ParahoricBuildingData G`. The data `P`
include connected reductivity of `G`, the chosen maximal split torus `S`, the normalized
`N(F)`-stable equipollence class of valuations of the root datum, V 6 on `Z(F)` and the
subgroups `Z(F)^0`, `G(F)^0`.

**Chapter 7 statements** (7.8.1(1)–(3), 7.8.2) assume only
`P.apartment.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms P.centralizer` (V 6, V 7, AS 2–AS 4 and both
sentences of AS 5). The corrections (PDF 33) state that Chapter 7 assumes AS 5 in addition
to AS 1–AS 4, which hold by §6.3. No perfectness of the residue field is assumed, matching
KP Chapter 7.

**Chapter 5 statements** (5.3.3, replaced 5.3.5, and 7.8.1(4), whose added proof uses 5.3.3
and 5.3.5) additionally assume the standing hypotheses of KP Chapter 5 (PDF 217): the
valuation ring is Henselian and the residue field is perfect, as the explicit hypotheses
`HenselianLocalRing 𝒪[F]` and `PerfectField 𝓀[F]`. Both hold for every nonarchimedean local
field (the valuation ring is complete; the residue field is finite, and Mathlib infers
`PerfectField 𝓀[F]` as an instance), so these hypotheses do not narrow the scope; they are
kept explicit because the task asks the Chapter 5 targets to expose the perfect-residue route.
KP prove the Chapter 5 results for a building satisfying the axioms of Chapter 4,
including the Iwahori decomposition of Axiom 4.1.16. KP 4.1.28 (PDF 197, printed 166) locates
the proofs of these axioms for the building constructed from a valuation of the root datum;
in particular Axiom 4.1.16 is Corollary 7.4.9. The Chapter 5 targets are therefore stated on
the same valuation building data, with the Chapter 7 apartment axioms; no further Chapter 4
axiom is assumed.

## The per-data conclusions

Equivalences are not mere bijections of types: each is required to send the class of every
`n ∈ N(F)` to the double coset of `n`, which is how "the inclusion `N → G` induces" is
encoded. Double coset spaces are Mathlib's `DoubleCoset.Quotient` with `DoubleCoset.mk`.

- `IwahoriWeylExactSequence`: there exist instances `P.normalizerZero.Normal` and
  `P.zeroSubgroup.Normal` (so that `W̃^0` and `G(F)/G(F)^0` are groups) such that
  1. there is a group isomorphism `e : W_aff ≃* W(Ψ)` with `e(n Z(F)^0)` equal, as an affine
     automorphism of the apartment, to the action `P.apartment.normalizerAffineEquiv n` of
     `n`, for every `n ∈ N(F) ∩ G(F)^0`;
  2. `ker(W̃^0 → G(F)/G(F)^0) = W_aff`; and
  3. `W̃^0 → G(F)/G(F)^0` is surjective.
  Together these are the exact sequence `1 → W_aff → W̃^0 → G(F)/G(F)^0 → 1`, with KP's
  identification of the kernel with the affine Weyl group through the action on `A`
  (proof of Theorem 7.8.1, via Theorem 7.5.3(2)). Clause 1 implies in particular that
  `Z(F)^0` acts trivially on `A`.
- `IwahoriWeylChamberSplitting C`: with the same normality instances, `W_aff` is normal in
  `W̃^0`, `W_aff` and `W̃^0_C` are complements (`Subgroup.IsComplement'`: every element of `W̃^0`
  is uniquely `w ρ` with `w ∈ W_aff`, `ρ ∈ W̃^0_C`, the decomposition used in the corrected
  proof of 7.8.1(2)), so `W̃^0 = W_aff ⋊ W̃^0_C`; and the restriction `W̃^0_C → G(F)/G(F)^0` is
  bijective.
- `IwahoriDoubleCosetBijection C`: an equivalence `W̃^0 ≃ I\G(F)/I` sending `n Z(F)^0` to
  `I n I` for every `n ∈ N(F)`. This is the full bijection of Theorem 7.8.1(2), including
  injectivity; it is not reduced to `I ∩ N(F) = Z(F)^0`, whose use in the printed injectivity
  proof the corrections replace by the argument through 7.8.2 and the Bruhat decomposition of
  the Iwahori–Tits system.
- `ParahoricDoubleCosetBijection F₁ F₂`: `Z(F)^0` is normal in `N(F)`, and there is an
  equivalence `W^aff_{F₁}\W̃^0/W^aff_{F₂} ≃ P_{F₁}\G(F)/P_{F₂}` sending the double coset of
  `n Z(F)^0` to `P_{F₁} n P_{F₂}`, for every `n ∈ N(F)`.
- `IwasawaDecomposition C ν`: every `g ∈ G(F)` is `u n p` with `u ∈ U(F)`, `n ∈ N(F)`,
  `p ∈ P_C`.
- `UnipotentNormalizerCoset C ν`: for all `n, n′ ∈ N(F)`, `n ∈ U(F) n′ P_C` (written
  `n = u n′ p` with `u ∈ U(F)`, `p ∈ P_C`) if and only if `n′ n⁻¹ ∈ Z(F)^0`.
- `UnipotentIwahoriDoubleCosetBijection C ν`: two equivalences, each sending `n Z(F)^0` to
  the double coset of `n`: `W̃^0 ≃ U(F)\G(F)/P_C`, and
  `W̃^0 ≃ (U(F)·Z(F)^0)\G(F)/P_C`, where `U(F)·Z(F)^0` is the literal pointwise product of
  the two subsets of `G(F)` (it is a subgroup because `Z` normalizes `U`, but the statement
  does not need this). The second equivalence uses `Quotient.mk''` because Mathlib's
  `DoubleCoset.mk` takes subgroups. These are the two descriptions
  `U(k)\G(k)/I = U(k)Z(k)^0\G(k)/I` of the corrected Theorem 7.8.1(4).

The normality of `Z(F)^0` in `N(F)` and of `G(F)^0` in `G(F)` is presupposed by KP but is not
yet proved in the Library; where a conclusion needs the group structure, these
normalities are existential conjuncts of the conclusion (an instance argument, so all
group operations in the remaining clauses use them).

## The global statements

- `IwahoriWeylExactSequenceStatement`: every `P` with `SatisfiesApartmentAxioms` satisfies
  `IwahoriWeylExactSequence`.
- `IwahoriWeylChamberSplittingStatement`, `IwahoriDoubleCosetStatement`: the same, for
  every chamber `C` of the apartment.
- `ParahoricDoubleCosetStatement`: the same, for every chamber `C` and all `F₁`, `F₂` with
  `AffineRootSystem.IsFaceOf P.affineRoots F_i C` (a facet contained in the closure of the
  facet `C`), i.e. any two facets in the closure of a common chamber.
- `IwasawaDecompositionStatement`, `UnipotentNormalizerCosetStatement`,
  `UnipotentIwahoriDoubleCosetStatement`: under `HenselianLocalRing 𝒪[F]`,
  `PerfectField 𝓀[F]` and `SatisfiesApartmentAxioms`, for every chamber `C` and every
  generic cocharacter `ν` of `S`.
- `IwahoriWeylBijectionsStatement`: the conjunction of the seven statements above, at the
  same universe level.

## Provenance of derived formulations

- KP's maps "induced by `N → G`" are encoded as equivalences prescribed on every
  representative in `N(F)`, as in the Library's `StandardParabolicPointsStatement`.
- KP's identification `ker = W_aff` is stated through the action on the apartment, the only
  identification KP use; the abstract `W(Ψ)` and the concrete subgroup
  `(N(F) ∩ G(F)^0)/Z(F)^0` are kept as separate objects.
- `W̃^0_C` and `W^aff_F` are defined as images of stabilizers in `N(F)`; see the module
  `IwahoriWeyl` for why this agrees with KP's definitions.
- The added 7.8.1(4) is stated with both of KP's double coset spaces; the corrections deduce
  the bijection from 5.3.3 and the replaced 5.3.5, which is why it is in the Chapter 5 scope.
