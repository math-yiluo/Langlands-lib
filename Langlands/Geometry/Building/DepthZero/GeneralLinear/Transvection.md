# Normal subgroups of `GL_n(F)` containing a transvection

Module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.DepthZero.GeneralLinear.Transvection`, namespace
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.GeneralLinearParahoric`. Pure linear algebra over a field `F`
and a finite index type `n`; it uses only Mathlib.

## Declarations

- `transvectionGL hij c`: the elementary transvection `1 + c E_{ij}` (`i ≠ j`) in `GL_n(F)`,
  the image of Mathlib's `Matrix.SpecialLinearGroup.transvection`; `coe_transvectionGL`,
  `transvectionGL_zero`.
- `diagGL d`: the invertible diagonal matrix with diagonal `d : n → Fˣ`; `coe_diagGL`,
  `coe_diagGL_inv`, `diagGL_mul`, `det_diagGL`.
- `diagGL_conj_transvectionGL`: `diag(d) (1 + c E_{ij}) diag(d)⁻¹ = 1 + d_i c d_j⁻¹ E_{ij}`.
- `permGL σ`: the permutation matrix `σ.permMatrix F` in `GL_n(F)`;
  `permMatrix_mul_single_mul` and `permGL_conj_transvectionGL`: conjugating `1 + c E_{ab}` by
  it gives `1 + c E_{σ⁻¹ a, σ⁻¹ b}`.
- `exists_perm_apply_eq_apply_eq`: for `i ≠ j` and `p ≠ q` some permutation sends `p, q` to
  `i, j` (a product of two swaps).
- `transvectionGL_mem_of_normal`: a normal subgroup `H` containing one transvection
  `1 + c E_{ij}` with `c ≠ 0` contains every transvection `1 + b E_{pq}`.
- `transvection_rotation_coe`: `(1 + E_{ij})(1 − E_{ji})(1 + E_{ij}) = 1 + E_{ij} − E_{ji} −
  E_{ii} − E_{jj}`.
- `diag2n_toGL_eq_commutator`: Mathlib's `diag2n hij a` (the diagonal matrix with `a` at `i`,
  `a⁻¹` at `j` and `1` elsewhere) equals `g w g⁻¹ w⁻¹` for `g = diag(a)` at `i` and the product
  `w` of three transvections above.
- `specialLinear_le_of_normal`: a normal subgroup of `GL_n(F)` containing one nontrivial
  elementary transvection contains the image of `SL_n(F)`.
- `eq_toGL_mul_diagGL`: every `g ∈ GL_n(F)` is `M · diag(det g, 1, …, 1)` with `M ∈ SL_n(F)`
  (the entry `det g` at a chosen index `i`).

## Proofs

`transvectionGL_mem_of_normal` first conjugates by `diag(b c⁻¹)` at `i` to change the
coefficient, then by a permutation matrix to move the position `(i, j)` to `(p, q)`.
`diag2n_toGL_eq_commutator` is checked entrywise after rewriting it as `g w = D w g`.
`specialLinear_le_of_normal` applies Mathlib's
`Matrix.SpecialLinearGroup.diagonal_transvection_induction'`: transvections lie in `H` by the
first lemma, the matrices `diag2n` lie in `H` as commutators of an element of `GL_n(F)` with
the product `w` of transvections (normality), and `H` is closed under products. The
decomposition `eq_toGL_mul_diagGL` takes `M = g · diag(det g, 1, …, 1)⁻¹`, whose determinant
is `1`.

This is the classical fact that `SL_n(F)` is generated by elementary transvections, in the form
needed for the comparisons of `G(F)^0` and `G(F)^1` in
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.DepthZero.GeneralLinear.ZeroSubgroup`.
