# Descent of the unramified valuation class and its apartment axioms

This module splits Kaletha–Prasad Theorem 9.6.5, in the form of the published
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.ValuationApartmentAxiomsExistenceStatement`, into two new
Prop-valued targets. It also proves that these, together with three published statements,
imply that form.

- `Langlands.Geometry.Building.UnramifiedValuationDescentStatement`: descent of the
  valuation of the root datum from the maximal unramified extension (KP Construction 9.6.1,
  Lemma 9.6.3 and Theorem 9.6.5).
- `Langlands.Geometry.Building.ValuationBuildingApartmentAxiomsStatement`: V 7 and
  AS 2–AS 5 for every compatible valuation class (KP §9.5, Theorem 9.6.5 and §9.6.6, with
  Proposition 6.2.6).
- `Langlands.Geometry.Building.UnramifiedValuationDescentPackageStatement`: their
  conjunction.
- `Langlands.Geometry.Building.ValuationApartmentAxiomsExistenceReductionStatement` and
  its proof `valuationApartmentAxiomsExistenceReduction`.

No statement of this module except the reduction is proved here. All statements are
universe polymorphic. They range over every `F : Type u` with `Field F`,
`ValuativeRel F`, `TopologicalSpace F` and `IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F`, in equal and
mixed characteristic, and impose no splitness, quasi-splitness, tameness, rank or
residue restriction.

Throughout, `K` is the Library's actual maximal unramified extension
`inertiaFixedField F`, definitionally `UnramifiedClosure F`, with its `F`-algebra
structure. `MaximalUnramified.valuation F` is its published normalized integer-valued
valuation. Scalar extension is the existing base change along `algebraBaseMap F K`.

## Descent of the valuation (`UnramifiedValuationDescentStatement`)

The binders are:

- a connected reductive group `G` over `Spec F` (`hG`). The affine and smooth instances
  of `G` come from `hG`; the affine instance of `G_K` is supplied by pullback;
- a maximal split torus `iS : S.toGrp ⟶ G` (`hiS`);
- a commutative group scheme `T` over `Spec F` with `iT : T.toGrp ⟶ G` and
  `f : S ⟶ T` such that `CommGroupScheme.groupHom f ≫ iT = iS`, so `S` lies in `T`;
- `hiT`: the scalar extension `iT_K` is a maximal split torus of `G_K`. Thus `T` is a
  special `F`-torus in the sense of KP §9.2.2;
- `hE`: actual opposite factor pairs for `iT_K` (`RelativeRoot.HasOppositeFactorPairs`).
  This is a Prop. The published `rootRankOneFactorization` (KP Proposition 2.11.17) proves
  it, so the binder only names a proof;
- `D : KPApartmentData iT_K ω`, an `N(K)`-stable equipollence class of valuations of the
  root datum of `(G_K, T_K)`, with `ω` the order of `MaximalUnramified.valuation F`;
- a centralizer datum `C` for `iT_K`, with full V 6 for `D` and `C`
  (`IntegerValuation.IsOmegaCompatible`).

The monomorphism instances needed by the apartment interfaces come from `hiS` and `hiT`
through `mono_of_isMaximalSplitTorus`.

Write `fixed x` for the condition that a point `x` of `D.Apartment` is fixed by
`RootFunctionFamily.galoisAction F K T G σ` for every `σ : K ≃ₐ[F] K`. Write `φ x` for
`torusFieldRestrictedRootFunctionFamily … (D.isRootDatumValuation_of_mem x.mem) f_K`,
where `f_K` is the scalar extension of `f`. The statement introduces both by `let`.
The conclusion asserts that there are `DS : KPApartmentData iS (localFieldOrder F)` and
`CS : CentralizerDatum iS` such that

1. `DS.IsOmegaCompatible CS`: full V 6 over `F`;
2. a root-function family `ψ` for `(G, S)` lies in `DS.carrier` exactly when `ψ = φ x`
   for some `x` with `fixed x`;
3. `φ` is injective on fixed points;
4. for every `n` in the actual normalizer `N(F)` of `iS` (`normalizerPoints iS`) and every
   fixed `x`, there is a fixed `y` with `φ y = n • φ x` and
   `D.apartmentMap y = rationalExtend F K G n • D.apartmentMap x`. The second equation is
   in the published reduced quotient `D.ReducedBuilding` of `G(K) × D.Apartment` (KP
   Remark 7.6.5), with its action of `G(K)`. The scalar extension of `n` is written with
   the base-change group that types `D`.

Clauses 3 and 4 give the `N(F)`-equivariant bijection between the `Aut(K/F)`-fixed part of
the upstairs apartment and the apartment of `DS`. This is what a comparison of quotients
(for example the published `UnramifiedQuotientLift` and the published
`UnramifiedBuildingDescentStatement`) needs. `V(S)`-equivariance follows from the orbit
structure of both classes.

`torusFieldRestrictedRootFunctionFamily` is the published restriction of an upstairs
valuation, first from the torus `T_K` to `S_K` and then from `K` to `F`. Its published
`rootFiltration_torusFieldRestrictedRootFunctionFamily` identifies each filtration
subgroup `U_{a,r}` of the restricted family with the preimage in `G(F)` of the subgroup
of `G(K)` generated by the upstairs filtrations `U_{b,x,m r}`. Here `b` runs over the roots
of `T_K` restricting to `m • a` with `m > 0`.

`KPApartmentData` is an `N(F)`-stable `V(S)`-orbit of valuations of the root datum
(V 0–V 5) that are `ω`-compatible on `S(F)` (KP Definitions 6.1.2, 6.1.7 and §6.1.8).
Together with V 6 the conclusion says the following. The restricted families of the
`Aut(K/F)`-fixed points of the upstairs class are exactly the members of an `N(F)`-stable
equipollence class of `ω`-compatible valuations of the root datum of `(G, S)`.

### Correspondence with the source

KP Construction 9.6.1 defines `ϕ^x_a(u) = ψ^a_u(x)` for `x` in the apartment
`A = A(S)` of the descended building, `a ∈ Φ(S, G)` and `u ∈ U_a(k)^*`. Remark 9.6.2
identifies a point of `A ⊂ A^Γ`, where `A` is the special `k`-apartment of `T_K`, with a
`Γ`-invariant valuation of the root datum of `(G_K, T_K)`. Lemma 9.6.3 expresses
`U_{a,x,r}` through the upstairs filtrations of the non-divisible roots `b` restricting to
`a` or `2a`.

The printed display of Lemma 9.6.3 uses the same depth `r` for every such `b`. Its proof
translates `x` by `−(r/2)a^∨` to reduce to `r = 0`, using Lemma 6.1.6. This translation
changes the depth of a root `b` restricting to `2a` by `2r`, not `r`. With a uniform `r`,
the display would also contradict V 4 of Definition 6.1.2, which says
`U_{a,x,r} ∩ U_{2a}(k) = U_{2a,x,2r}`. The published restriction therefore uses depth
`m r` for roots restricting to `m • a`, which is what the proof of Lemma 9.6.3 gives. The
corrections of 1 November 2024 do not mention Lemma 9.6.3. This is an independently derived
reading of the display, not an authors' erratum.

Theorem 9.6.5 states that `ϕ^x` is an `ω`-compatible valuation of the root datum, that
`x ↦ ϕ^x` is `V(S) ⋊ N(k)`-equivariant, and that its image is an equipollence class.
Clause 4 is this equivariance. The action of `n ∈ N(k)` on `A(S)` is the restriction of
its action on `B(G_K)`. By Proposition 9.3.13 it preserves
`A(S) = B(G_K)^{S, Γ} = A^Γ`. §9.6.6 states that `z ↦ ϕ^z` is an isomorphism of affine
spaces, which gives clause 3. The proof of Theorem 9.6.5 derives V 6 from
Proposition 9.3.16 (Axiom 4.1.4 for `A(S)`). By Proposition 9.3.13 and
UR 2 of Axiom 4.1.27, `A(S)` is the `Γ`-fixed part of the special apartment of any special
`k`-torus `T ⊇ S`; this is the set of fixed points in the Lean statement. The upstairs
class `D` is required to satisfy V 6. By KP Proposition 6.2.6, which the Library proves over
any field as `IsOmegaCompatible.eq_of_hasOppositeFactorPairs`, such a class is unique. It
is therefore the canonical class of the quasi-split group `G_K` used in §9.6.

## Apartment axioms (`ValuationBuildingApartmentAxiomsStatement`)

For every nonarchimedean local field `F`, every `G` over `Spec F` with
`[IsAffineHom G.X.hom]` and every `B : ValuationBuildingData G`, the statement asserts the
published `B.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms`. That is V 7 for `rationalPointTopology F G.X`,
AS 2–AS 4 and the three sentences of corrected AS 5. V 6 is a field of `B`.
`ValuationBuildingData` contains connected reductivity of `G`, a maximal split torus, an
`N(F)`-stable class of `localFieldOrder F`-compatible valuations of its root datum, the
actual centralizer and V 6.

By KP Proposition 6.2.6 (published `KPApartmentData.eq_of_isOmegaCompatible_of_hasOppositeFactorPairs`),
the class of `B` is the unique V 6 class for its torus and centralizer. Maximal split tori
are `G(F)`-conjugate (published `smoothMaximalSplitTorusConjugacy`). The statement is
therefore the assertion that the canonical class of Theorem 9.6.5 satisfies these axioms:

- §9.5 (printed pp. 363–364 / PDF pp. 394–395) proves Axiom 4.1.6 for the descended
  building: AS 2 and AS 3 in Proposition 9.4.19, AS 4 in Proposition 9.4.18, AS 5 in
  Proposition 9.4.35;
- §9.6.6 (printed p. 367 / PDF p. 398) identifies the affine root systems `Ψ` and `Ψ'` of
  Definition 6.3.4 for this class with those of 9.4.12;
- for V 7, Construction 9.6.1 identifies the filtration subgroups with the bounded affine
  root groups `U_ψ`. The remark after Definition 6.1.2 states that all valuations
  constructed in the book have V 6 and V 7.

Quantifying over every `B` instead of the constructed class is a derived formulation. It
relies on the uniqueness above and is recorded as such.

## The reduction

`ValuationApartmentAxiomsExistenceReductionStatement` says that the published
`UnramifiedQuasiSplitStatement` (KP Corollary 2.3.8 for `K`),
`QuasiSplitIntegerApartmentExistenceStatement` (KP §6.1),
`SpecialTorusExistenceStatement` (KP Proposition 9.3.4) and the two statements above
imply `ValuationApartmentAxiomsExistenceStatement`. The theorem
`valuationApartmentAxiomsExistenceReduction` proves it as follows.

1. The published `exists_maximalSplitTorus` gives a maximal split torus `iS` of `G`.
   `SpecialTorusExistenceStatement` gives a special torus `T` containing it.
2. The published `connectedReductiveFieldExtensionStatement` shows that `G_K` is connected
   reductive, so `UnramifiedQuasiSplitStatement` makes it quasi-split.
3. `QuasiSplitIntegerApartmentExistenceStatement`, applied to `K`, its surjective valuation
   `MaximalUnramified.valuation F` (`valuation_surjective`), its Henselian valuation ring
   (`valuationSubring_isHenselian`) and the maximal split torus `iT_K`, gives a class `D`
   and a centralizer datum `C` with V 6.
4. The published `rootRankOneFactorization`, with
   `hasRankOneFactorization_iff_hasOppositeFactorPairs`, gives opposite factor pairs for
   `iT_K`.
5. `UnramifiedValuationDescentStatement` gives `DS`, `CS` and V 6 over `F`. These form
   `B : ValuationBuildingData G`, and `ValuationBuildingApartmentAxiomsStatement` gives
   `B.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms`.

The proof uses a raised heartbeat limit for elaboration of the large instance-carrying
binders. It uses only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. Like the published
Galois-action modules, the module sets `backward.isDefEq.respectTransparency` to `false`.
This lets the rational points of `baseChanged F K G` act on the quotient of `D`, whose group
is written as a base change.

## Provenance and scope

The provenance is derived. KP work over Henselian discretely valued fields with perfect
residue field; here `F` is a nonarchimedean local field. KP construct the valuation from
the descended building `B(G_K)^Γ`. The Lean statement instead uses the published
torus-then-field restriction, which equals KP's `ϕ^x` by Lemma 9.6.3 read with the depths
its proof gives. The second statement is formulated for every compatible class by
uniqueness.

Neither target is proved here. Their proofs require the unramified-descent geometry of KP
§§9.3–9.4: the building `B(G_K)` of the quasi-split group over `K`, its `Γ`-action and
integral models. These remain open. This module constructs no special torus, valuation,
building, metric or integral model.
