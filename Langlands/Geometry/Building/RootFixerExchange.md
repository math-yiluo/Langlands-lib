# The sharp fixer of a finite set of apartment points

Module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.RootFixerExchange`. Derived, internal unit:
the range part of Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach*
(2023), Proposition 7.3.12(1) (printed p.250 / PDF p.281) for the concave
function of a finite set `Ω`, with the subgroup `H_Ω` below in place of the
book's `N′`. The published `PointSharpExchange` is the case `Ω = {x}`.

## Declarations

Namespace `KPApartmentData` (apartment data `D : KPApartmentData i ω`):

- `filtration_mk_vadd`: `U_{a, x + w, r} = U_{a, x, r - a(w)}` for every
  character `a` and `w ∈ V(S)` (from the published `rootFiltration_vadd'`).
- `val_mk_vadd_apply`: `(x + w).val a u = x.val a u + a(w)`.
- `exists_mk_vadd_eq`: every point is `x + w` for some `w ∈ V(S)`.
- `mem_filtration_of_val_le`: if `u′ ∈ U_a(k)` and `x.val a u ≤ x.val a u′` at
  one point `x`, then `u ∈ U_{a,y,r}` implies `u′ ∈ U_{a,y,r}` for every `y`.
- `sharpNormalizerFixer Ω` (`H_Ω`): `G(k)^♯_Ω ⊓` the image in `G(k)` of the
  elements of `N(k)` fixing every point of `Ω`; `mem_sharpNormalizerFixer`,
  `sharpNormalizerFixer_le_sharpFixer`.
- `rootFixer_hasCommutatorRelations`: for finite non-empty `Ω`, the family
  `b ↦ U_{b,Ω,0}` satisfies `RootGroupFamily.HasCommutatorRelations`. With a
  base point `x₀ ∈ Ω`, `U_{b,Ω,0} ⊆ U_{b,x₀,r_b}` for the largest depth
  `r_b = -b(y_b - x₀)`, `y_b ∈ Ω`. The published `filtration_commutator_le` at
  `x₀` with depths `r_c, r_b` lands in `U_{pc+qb, x₀, p r_c + q r_b}`, which lies
  in `U_{pc+qb,Ω,0}` since `-(pc+qb)(y - x₀) ≤ p r_c + q r_b` for `y ∈ Ω`.

Namespace `ValuationBuildingData` (building data `B` over a nonarchimedean
local field):

- `rootFixer_neg_mul_rootFixer_subset_sharpNormalizerFixer`: under opposite
  factor pairs (`RelativeRoot.HasRankOneFactorization B.i`), for non-empty `Ω`
  and a relative root `a`,
  `U_{-a,Ω,0} U_{a,Ω,0} ⊆ U_{a,Ω,0} U_{-a,Ω,0} H_Ω`. Let `v ∈ U_{-a,Ω,0}`,
  `v′ ∈ U_{a,Ω,0}` be non-trivial, with values `t, t′ ≥ 0` at a base point
  `x₀ ∈ Ω`. If `t + t′ > 0`, the published
  `exists_rankOne_factorization_fixing` at `x₀` gives `v v′ = x′ z y` with a
  centralizer point `z` fixing `x₀`, hence every point. The factors `x′, y` are
  at least as deep as `v′, v` at `x₀`, hence lie in the root fixers of `Ω`
  (`mem_filtration_of_val_le`). If `t + t′ = 0`, then `t = t′ = 0`, the value of
  `v` vanishes at every point of `Ω`, and `m(v) = q₁ v q₂` fixes every point of
  `Ω` by `RootReflectionProof.hasRootReflections`. This follows the published
  `filtration_neg_mul_filtration_subset_sharpNormalizerStabilizer`.
- `sharpFixer_subset_mul_sharpNormalizerFixer`: under the apartment axioms and
  opposite factor pairs, for finite non-empty `Ω` and every generic
  cocharacter `mu`,

  `G(F)^♯_Ω ⊆ X_Ω(Φ_{-mu}) · X_Ω(Φ_mu) · H_Ω`, with
  `X_Ω(P) = ⨆_{b ∈ P} U_{b,Ω,0}`.

  It applies `RootGroupFamily.iSup_subset_mul_of_exchange` (module
  `RootGroupFamilyExchange`) to `U b = U_{b,Ω,0}`. The double inclusion is the
  published `KPApartmentData.rootFixer_double_le`. The ray property is
  `eq_or_double_of_eval_eq_smul`.

## Statement

Namespace `Langlands.Geometry.Building`:

- `FiniteSharpFixerExchangeStatement.{u}` (a `Prop`): for every
  nonarchimedean local field `F`, every affine group scheme `G` over `F` (in
  universe `u`), all building data `B : ValuationBuildingData G` with
  `B.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms` (V 7 and AS 2–AS 5), every finite non-empty set
  `Ω` of points of `A(S)` and every generic cocharacter `mu` of `S`
  (`IsGenericCocharacter B.i mu`, i.e. `⟨b, mu⟩ ≠ 0` for every relative root
  `b`),

  `G(F)^♯_Ω ⊆ X_Ω(Φ_{-mu}) · X_Ω(Φ_mu) · H_Ω`.

  Here `G(F)^♯_Ω` is `B.apartment.sharpFixer Ω`, `X_Ω(Φ_{±mu})` is the
  subgroup generated by the root fixers `U_{b,Ω,0}` (`B.apartment.rootFixer b Ω`)
  for the relative roots `b` with `⟨b, ±mu⟩ > 0` (`positiveRelativeRoots`), and
  `H_Ω` is `B.apartment.sharpNormalizerFixer Ω` above. The products are
  pointwise products of subsets of `G(F)`. The statement assumes nothing beyond
  the apartment axioms.
- `finiteSharpFixerExchange : FiniteSharpFixerExchangeStatement.{u}`. Opposite
  factor pairs (`RelativeRoot.HasRankOneFactorization B.i`) hold for every
  maximal split torus of a connected reductive group by the published
  `rootRankOneFactorization` (module
  `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.RootRankOneFactorizationProof`), so
  `sharpFixer_subset_mul_sharpNormalizerFixer` applies.

KP state Proposition 7.3.12 for a point `x` and a concave function `f`. A
finite set `Ω` with a base point `x ∈ Ω` gives the concave function
`f_Ω(a) = max_{y ∈ Ω} (−a(y − x))`, whose groups `U_{a,x,f_Ω(a)}` are the
root fixers `U_{a,Ω,0}`. The statement is the inclusion `⊆` of part (1) for
`f_Ω`, with the positive system of `mu` and with `H_Ω` in place of KP's `N′`
(the subgroup generated by the `N^{(a)}_{x,f}`). It is meant for the formal
route to KP Lemma 7.7.4 for finite `Ω`, which is still unpublished. That
route's normalizer step also needs `SimplyConnectedNormalizerAffineWeylStatement`.
Neither this statement nor its proof uses it.
