# The actual common-chamber facet-parabolic comparison

`Langlands.Geometry.Building.facetParabolicComparison` proves
`FacetParabolicComparisonStatement.{u}` for arbitrary `u`, using the apartment
axioms, a chamber, and two faces of that chamber. `affineIwahoriTits` supplies the
two normality conclusions and a representative-compatible affine Weyl
comparison `e`. Both face comparisons use that same `e`, chamber parahoric, and
normalizer quotient projection.

For one face `Fa`, choose points representing `Fa` and the chamber `C`. The face
premise puts the first point in the closed chamber. The setwise stabilizer
calculation `coe_zeroStabilizerIn_facet` identifies the stabilizer of the image
of `Fa` in the reduced building with the union of Bruhat cells indexed by the
subgroup generated by the chamber-simple reflections whose roots vanish on
`Fa`. This calculation follows from the Tits axioms and the pointwise action of
affine Weyl elements on the facets they stabilize. Uniqueness of the Bruhat
index tests this stabilizer membership on each normalizer representative.
Equivariance and injectivity of the apartment map transfer the test to the
setwise stabilizer of `Fa` in the normalizer. Taking the image under the
quotient gives the required equality with `affineFacetStabilizer Fa` in the full
Iwahori–Weyl group.

The apartment axioms place the generated facet parahoric in `G(F)^0`.
Constancy of the generated parahorics along each facet and the depth-zero
filtration inequalities on the closed chamber put the chamber parahoric
inside the facet parahoric. Every element of the generated facet parahoric
fixes the image of `Fa` pointwise, so its normalizer elements have Bruhat
indices in the subgroup just computed. Conversely, each selected simple root
vanishes on `Fa`, so `exists_sharpFixer_normalizer_eq_affineReflection` lifts its
reflection to a normalizer element in the sharp subgroup of `Fa`, hence in the
generated parahoric. Compatibility of `e` identifies its quotient class with
the selected generator. Products and inverses lift the generated subgroup,
and `IsTitsSystem.coe_eq_standardParabolic` gives the cell-union equality in
`G(F)^0`. The existing lemmas `FacetParabolicComparisonWith.parahoric_image`
and `FacetParabolicComparisonWith.parahoric_eq_bruhat_cells` transport this
equality along the inclusion into the full rational-point group.
