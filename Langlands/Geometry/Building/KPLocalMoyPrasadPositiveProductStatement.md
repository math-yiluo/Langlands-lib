# Positive-depth sign product cover over the maximal unramified field

This module states
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.MaximalUnramified.LocalMoyPrasadPositiveProductStatement`,
at arbitrary universe `u`. It is the constant-positive-depth torus-filtration
variant of Kaletha–Prasad (KP), Lemma 7.3.11 and Proposition 7.3.12. The
proposition is conditional on the explicit commutator hypothesis below. This is
a statement only; no proof of it is supplied here.

## Data and the retained hypothesis

Let `F : Type u` have `Field F`, `ValuativeRel F`, `TopologicalSpace F` and
`IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F`. Put `K = UnramifiedClosure F`. Let `H` be a group
scheme over `Spec K` with `[IsAffineHom H.X.hom]`, let
`B : MaximalUnramified.BuildingData F H`, and let
`hZ : IsTorus B.centralizer.Z`.

The data `B` already specify a connected reductive group, a maximal split torus
`B.S` with inclusion `B.i`, a class of valuations of its actual root datum,
the actual centralizer `Z = B.centralizer.Z`, and compatibility with the
integer-valued field valuation. The hypothesis `hZ` makes this the quasi-split
case. The only added premise is the following formula, with the same
quantifiers as the first clause of the published
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.QuasiSplitLocalTorusFiltrationStatement`:

```lean
∀ (x : B.Apartment) (a : relativeRoots B.i) (u u' : rationalPoints H),
  u ∈ B.rootGroup a.1 → u ≠ 1 → u' ∈ B.rootGroup (-a.1) → u' ≠ 1 →
  ∀ r : ℝ≥0, 0 < r →
    (((r : ℝ) : WithTop ℝ) = x.val a.1 u + x.val (-a.1) u') →
  ∀ (v' v : rationalPoints H) (z : rationalPoints B.centralizer.Z),
    v' ∈ B.rootGroup (-a.1) → v ∈ B.rootGroup a.1 →
    u'⁻¹ * u * u' * u⁻¹ = v' * rationalPoints.map B.centralizer.j z * v →
    z ∈ B.centralizerTorusFiltration hZ r
```

Thus every torus component of the displayed opposite-root commutator has depth
at least the positive sum of the two root valuations. This is the commutator
clause of KP Definition 7.2.1. The statement does not assume the whole S1
proposition: neither its root-compatibility clause nor its normalizer-stability
clause is a premise. It also takes no premise asserting normality, a product
cover, the group-properties statement S2, apartment axioms or building axioms.
KP Lemma 7.2.3 motivates a separate proof of the commutator hypothesis for this
torus filtration; that proof is not part of this statement.

## The conclusion and its exact groups

For every `x : B.Apartment`, every `r : ℝ≥0` with `0 < r`, and every integral
cocharacter `mu : cocharacterLattice B.S` with
`IsGenericCocharacter B.i mu`, the conclusion is

```lean
(B.moyPrasad hZ x r : Set (rationalPoints H)) =
  (B.rootFiltrationSpan x (positiveRelativeRoots B.i (-mu)) r :
    Set (rationalPoints H)) *
  (B.centralizerTorusImage hZ r : Set (rationalPoints H)) *
  (B.rootFiltrationSpan x (positiveRelativeRoots B.i mu) r :
    Set (rationalPoints H))
```

The products are ordinary pointwise products of subsets of the actual group
`rationalPoints H`, associated as `(negative * torus) * positive`. Genericity
means that every actual relative root pairs nontrivially with `mu`.
`positiveRelativeRoots B.i mu` consists of the relative roots whose integral
character–cocharacter pairing with `mu` is positive. Replacing `mu` by `-mu`
gives the negative roots.

All three subgroup definitions and the left side are imported unchanged:

| Expression | Published meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `B.filtration x b r` | The actual root subgroup filtration `U_{b,x,r}` |
| `B.rootFiltrationSpan x Ψ r` | `⨆ b ∈ Ψ, B.filtration x b r`, the generated subgroup |
| `B.centralizerTorusFiltration hZ r` | The published `localTorusFiltration` of the torus `Z` |
| `B.centralizerTorusImage hZ r` | Its image under `rationalPoints.map B.centralizer.j` |
| `B.moyPrasad hZ x r` | The subgroup generated by that image and every relative-root filtration at depth `r` |

The last description is the published equality
`BuildingData.moyPrasad_eq_sup_iSup`. In particular, the left side is the full
generated group, not a group newly defined by the three-factor product.
`rootFiltrationSpan` comes from `KPLocalMoyPrasadGroupProperties`; positivity
and genericity are the actual lattice definitions of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.StandardParabolic` and
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.RelativeRoots`.

## Comparison with KP

For the constant concave function `f = r > 0`, KP Lemma 7.3.11 provides the
fixed-sign collection and rank-one exchange used in Proposition 7.3.12.
Proposition 7.3.12(1)–(5) first concerns the root-generated group
`G(K)♯_{x,r}` and its sharp torus intersection `Z♯_{x,r}`. Its positive and
negative coordinates are indexed by indivisible roots, with blocks
`U_{a,x,r} U_{2a,x,r}`. These blocks retain the separate depth-`r` doubled-root
factor when the relative root system is nonreduced. Collecting all such blocks
of either sign gives precisely the generated root-filtration span of that
sign. The present statement asserts only the sign-span cover and no ordered
coordinate bijection.

The middle factor here is the full `j(Z(K)_r)`, rather than `Z♯_{x,r}`.
The source-derived passage is the positive-depth argument in the proof of
Lemma 7.3.16: the sharp torus factor is generated by opposite-root
commutator components, and commutator friendliness places these in `Z(K)_r`.
At constant positive depth, each pair of block directions has valuation sum
at least `r` (in fact at least `2r` in the same root direction), and the torus
filtration is decreasing. The published torus filtration lies in the
character-valuation kernel; its image therefore acts on the apartment with
zero translation by the compatibility in `B`. Axiom V6 then normalizes the
root filtrations. Definition 7.3.3(3) adjoins the full `Z(K)_r`, giving the
displayed full-group cover with that factor in the middle. These paragraphs
explain the provenance of the target; they do not supply a Lean proof.

The authors' corrections, PDF p. 33, make AS5 an additional Chapter 7
hypothesis and state that it is known in the quasi-split case by Propositions
6.3.13 and 6.6.2; AS1–AS4 are established in §6.3. Here `B` has the
integer-valued root-datum geometry over the actual maximal unramified field,
and `hZ` is precisely the torus-centralizer condition for the quasi-split
scope. Thus the requisite geometry is a consequence to establish from these
data, not a new apartment-axiom premise of the target. In particular, this
does not infer AS5 from root-valuation axioms alone. The proof successor must
justify whatever geometric consequences its route uses. The field `K` has
infinite residue field and is not being treated as a nonarchimedean local
field; only `F` has that assumption.

The same correction adds the arbitrary-order assertion 7.3.12(6). The present
sign-span equality does not assert that stronger ordered bijection and does
not use it to replace or enlarge the target. No claim that the corrected
arbitrary-order assertion has been formalized is made here.
