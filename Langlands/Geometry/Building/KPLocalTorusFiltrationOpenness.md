# Openness of the actual local torus filtration

This internal derived result supplies the torus part of the openness argument
for the concrete unramified apartment lifts. It uses the existing
`localTorusFiltration` for an arbitrary actual torus over the maximal
unramified field `K = UnramifiedClosure F`, at every nonnegative real depth.
The topology is exactly `rationalPointTopology K T.toGrp.X`, with
`fixedFieldTopology F` on `K`.

The source motivation is Kaletha and Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New
Approach* (2023), Definition 2.5.13 for the norm Iwahori subgroup,
Corollary 7.3.14 and Proposition 9.8.3(2) for the surrounding openness
argument, read with the authors' corrections dated 1 November 2024.
These helper theorems are derived topology results for the Library's
specific torus-filtration construction; they do not assert the whole
building openness statement.

`isOpen_localTorusNormIwahori` adapts the implemented argument of
`UnramifiedZeroDatum.isOpen_torusIwahori` to an arbitrary torus and finite
Galois splitting field. The split torus's character-valuation kernel is
open by `isOpen_characterValuationKernel_of_isSplitTorus`. The inverse of
`GaloisNorm.rationalPointsEquiv` is continuous by
`AffineWeil.continuous_rationalBasePoints_of_continuousSMul`, using
`intermediateValuation_continuousSMul` for the exact field topologies.
Its inverse image is therefore open in the rational points of the Weil
restriction. The algebraic norm is smooth by
`GaloisNormSmoothnessProof.galoisNormSmoothness`. The published Henselian
smooth open-map theorem, through `isOpenMap_rationalPoint_of_smooth`,
makes its rational-point image open. `GaloisNorm.norm_rationalPoints`
identifies that image with the norm Iwahori subgroup used in the
filtration.

`isOpen_localGeometricCharacterCongruence` uses the spectral norm on the
fixed separable closure. The inclusion from `K` is continuous by
`fixedFieldTopology_eq_induced`. The character module of the base-changed
torus is finitely generated by `rationalCharacterLattice`, since a torus
is connected reductive. The theorem then applies
`isOpen_extendedCharacterCongruence_log` with the existing positive
`localLogScale F`; its logarithmic valuation is the
`algebraicExtensionAddValuation` used in the definition.

Finally, `isOpen_localTorusFiltration` chooses the existing finite Galois
splitting data. At zero depth it uses the open norm Iwahori subgroup. At
positive depth it intersects that subgroup with the open geometric
character congruence. No integral model or additional openness premise is
assumed.
