# Ordered actual root factors under field extension

This internal construction works over any extension k → K of fields in an
arbitrary common universe u. The original group scheme G is affine and smooth.
The supplied commutative group scheme S is a split torus, its map i into G is
monomorphic, and local opposite factor pairs for that original map are given.
Fix any injective enumeration of all its indivisible positive roots for a
cocharacter ν, in any order. No maximality, connected reductivity, finiteness,
separability or Galois assumption on the extension is added.

`orderedRootFiltrationProduct i ψ a d` is the set of actual rational points
that admit an ordered factorization in the supplied root filtrations, with
root list a and real depths d. It asserts no subgroup closure and makes no
injectivity assumption in its definition. Its ambient torus and group are the
original supplied ones.

`orderedRootFactors_fieldDescend` starts with an actual ordered factorization
of the scalar extension of an original rational point g. Each supplied factor
belongs to the root group for the pulled-back original root. It constructs an
original root-group point for every factor, proves that its actual rational
scalar extension equals the corresponding supplied factor, and proves that
their product in the original order is g. The torus upstairs is exactly the
pullback of S. It is not an enlarged maximal split torus.

For every root, the proof constructs a closed representative of the original
root subgroup from splitness and smoothness. Local opposite pairs give
injectivity of ordered multiplication on every test scheme. The actual field
extension is fpqc and hence an effective epimorphism. The subgroup base-change
equivalences turn the supplied factorization into a factorization over this
cover, while the rational-point pullback identity compares its product with
the original g. `orderedSubgroupFactors_descend` then descends each factor.
Injectivity of the base-change point equivalence recovers the asserted actual
rational extension equality.

`exists_orderedRootFiltrationFactors_fieldExtend_iff` applies this result at
arbitrary real depths to any upstairs root-function family ψ. It proves that
the extension of g has a supplied-order product of factors in those upstairs
filtrations if and only if g has such a product in the original filtrations
for the literal family `RootFunctionFamily.fieldRestrict ψ`. Factor equality
retains every filtration bound. In the forward direction original root-group
membership comes from factor descent and the bound comes from the definition
of field restriction. In the reverse direction actual scalar extension
preserves root-group membership and products, and the same definition gives
the bounds. No normalization or valuation axiom for ψ is needed. Both results
include an empty root enumeration.

`mem_orderedRootFiltrationProduct_fieldExtend_iff` restates that equivalence
as preservation and reflection of membership in the defined ordered-product
set. Its two sides retain the actual rational extension map, the pulled-back
root enumeration, the literal restricted family and every real depth.

The provenance is internal composition of the published actual root-subgroup
representability, local opposite-pair ordered-product injectivity, root-subgroup
base-change comparison, field-extension fpqc result and generic ordered-factor
descent. No new assertion from original literature is used. These results
concern an existing ordered factorization. They do not assert that the subgroup
generated by filtered roots admits such a factorization, do not discharge V3,
and do not construct the building comparison of the host task.
