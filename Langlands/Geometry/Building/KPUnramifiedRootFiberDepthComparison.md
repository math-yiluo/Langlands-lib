# Uniform root fibre depth preimages along a concrete apartment lift

This derived construction uses the positive restriction fibre and exact
multiplier-scaled filtration preimage from
`KPUnramifiedComparisonRootHelpers`, together with the scaled-versus-uniform
comparisons in `RootValueTorusFieldMultipliers` and `RootValueTorusUniformBlocks`.
It makes no additional claim attributed directly to original literature.

The context is an affine group scheme `G` over a nonarchimedean local field `F`,
supplied parahoric building data `P`, an unramified comparison torus, and a
concrete apartment lift `L`. The building data supply connected reductivity
and a maximal split torus, hence the smoothness, splitness and relative root
system used by the proof. The root `a` is an actual lower relative root.

`L.rootFiberDepthPreimage y a r` is the subgroup of lower rational points whose
scalar extensions lie in the subgroup generated by the upper positive
restriction fibre at the single uniform real depth `r`. It is the preimage of
`L.buildingData.rootFiltrationSpan (L.e y) (L.rootFiber a) r` under actual
rational-point extension.

At depth zero this subgroup equals `P.filtration y a 0` for every lower root,
including roots whose doubles are roots. The proof rewrites the downstairs
filtration using the exact multiplier-scaled preimage theorem. Scaling zero
by each positive integral restriction multiplier leaves zero, so the generic
zero-depth equality replaces the scaled subgroup by the uniform subgroup.
The remaining identification compares the same root-fibre suprema, expressed
with a subtype and with a membership index.

At a root `a` for which `2a` is absent, the corresponding filtration equality
holds at every real depth. The published relative root-system theorem for
connected reductive groups and maximal split tori supplies the root-system
predicate. The generic nonmultipliable multiplier theorem forces every
positive restriction multiplier to equal one, after which the same indexed
supremum comparison applies. The theorem
`filtration_eq_rootFiber_of_not_double` retains the explicit preimage expression.

The two block-membership corollaries apply respectively at zero depth for
every root and at any nonnegative depth for a nonmultipliable root. Each takes
a lower rational point whose extension lies in the upper fibre span. The
corresponding filtration equality places the point in the lower root
filtration; taking the identity in the doubled filtration gives the required
two-factor block. No separate root-group membership premise is needed.

These results do not provide positive-depth factor descent when `2a` is a root
and do not prove the full unramified root-comparison field.
