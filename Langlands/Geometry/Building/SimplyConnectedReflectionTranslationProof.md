# Simply connected realization of actual centralizer translations: proof

Lean module: `Langlands.Geometry.Building.SimplyConnectedReflectionTranslationProof`,
namespace `Langlands.Geometry.Building`.

## Statement

```
theorem Langlands.Geometry.Building.simplyConnectedReflectionTranslation :
    SimplyConnectedReflectionTranslationStatement.{u}
```

The type is exactly the published proposition
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.SimplyConnectedReflectionTranslationStatement.{u}`
of the module
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.SimplyConnectedReflectionTranslationStatement`, at
an arbitrary universe `u`, with no further hypothesis. Unfolded, it reads:

```
∀ (F : Type u) [Field F] [ValuativeRel F] [TopologicalSpace F]
  [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F]
  (G : GroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of F))) [IsAffineHom G.X.hom]
  (B : ValuationBuildingData G),
  IsSimplyConnected G →
    B.reflectionTranslationLattice = B.centralizerTranslationLattice
```

In words: for every nonarchimedean local field `F`, every affine group `G`
over `F` with valuation building data `B`, if `G` is simply connected, then the
image in the reduced translation space of the centralizer points whose
normalizer images lie in the subgroup generated by the opposite-pair
reflection representatives equals the image of the whole centralizer `Z(F)`.

## Proof

The Lean proof is the single application

```
simplyConnectedReflectionTranslation_of_rankOneGeneration rootRankOneFactorization
  simplyConnectedRankOneCentralizerGeneration
```

of the theorem `simplyConnectedReflectionTranslation_of_rankOneGeneration`
(module `Geometry.Building.SimplyConnectedReflectionTranslationReduction`),
which derives the statement from two hypotheses. Both are discharged by
theorems proved in the Library without hypotheses:

1. `rootRankOneFactorization : RootRankOneFactorizationStatement.{u}` (module
   `GroupScheme.Rational.RootRankOneFactorizationProof`): relative-root rank-one
   factorization, which gives the actual opposite factor pairs of `B`.
2. `simplyConnectedRankOneCentralizerGeneration :
   SimplyConnectedRankOneCentralizerGenerationStatement.{u}` (module
   `GroupScheme.SimplyConnected.RankOneCentralizerGenerationProof`): for simply
   connected `G` over a nonarchimedean local field, `Z(F)` lies in the subgroup
   generated by the groups `Z(F) ∩ ⟨U_a(F), U_{-a}(F)⟩` and the norm-one
   subgroup `Z(F)^1`.

Inside the reduction, each rank-one centralizer lies in the reflection
normalizer, and `Z(F)^1` acts trivially on the apartment; the details are on
the page of `Geometry.Building.SimplyConnectedReflectionTranslationReduction`.
The statement is not modified and no hypothesis is added.

## Literature

The statement's page records an internal provenance and cites no source: the
equality is the centralizer condition for generation of the normalizer by
reflections modulo the kernel of the apartment action. The rank-one generation
used in the proof is the Kneser–Tits theorem with the rank-one reduction of
Prasad–Raghunathan; its sources are on the page of
`GroupScheme.SimplyConnected.RankOneCentralizerGenerationProof`.
