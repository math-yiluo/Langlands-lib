# Reflection translations of simply connected groups from rank-one generation

This internal reduction works at arbitrary universe `u` on the actual
`ValuationBuildingData`. It proves the published proposition
`SimplyConnectedReflectionTranslationStatement` from two explicit premises,
which this module does not prove:

* `RootRankOneFactorizationStatement`, used only to obtain actual opposite
  factor pairs (`RelativeRoot.HasOppositeFactorPairs`);
* `SimplyConnectedRankOneCentralizerGenerationStatement`, the Kneser–Tits type
  statement that for simply connected `G` the image of `Z(F)` in `G(F)` lies in
  the subgroup generated by the groups `centralizerPoints i ⊓ ⟨U_a(F), U_{-a}(F)⟩`
  for relative roots `a`, together with the image of `Z(F)^1`.

All imported definitions and statements are unchanged.

## The definition

`ValuationBuildingData.rankOneCentralizerSubgroup B` is the subgroup of `G(F)`
generated by the union over `a ∈ relativeRoots B.i` of the subgroups
`centralizerPoints B.i ⊓ Subgroup.closure (B.rootGroup a ∪ B.rootGroup (-a))`.
It is the generated subgroup appearing on the right-hand side of the
rank-one generation statement, written with the actual root groups of `B`;
`B.rootGroup a` is by definition the carrier of `RelativeRoot.subgroup B.i a`
at the identity, so the premise applies to it without rewriting.

## The proofs

* `rankOneCentralizerSubgroup_le_reflectionNormalizer_map`: assuming
  opposite factor pairs, `rankOneCentralizerSubgroup B` is contained in the
  image of `B.reflectionNormalizer` under the inclusion of `normalizerPoints B.i`.
  By `Subgroup.closure_le` it suffices to treat each generator; for a generator
  in the rank-one centralizer of `a`, this is the imported rank-one theorem
  `rankOneCentralizer_le_reflectionNormalizer_map` from
  `RankOneReflectionGeneration`.
* `toNormalizer_mem_ker_of_mem_rationalCommonNormKernel`: for `z ∈ Z(F)^1`, the
  normalizer element `B.centralizer.toNormalizer z` lies in the kernel of the
  apartment action `B.apartment.normalizerAffineEquiv`. The affine equivalence
  is the identity pointwise by `B.isOmegaCompatible.smul_eq_self`.
* `rationalCommonNormKernel_map_le_ker_map`: the image of `Z(F)^1` in `G(F)` is
  contained in the image of that kernel under the inclusion of
  `normalizerPoints B.i`, by the previous lemma.
* `toNormalizer_mem_reflections_sup_ker_of_le`: assume opposite factor pairs and
  the containment `hgen` of the image of `Z(F)` in
  `rankOneCentralizerSubgroup B ⊔ (image of Z(F)^1)`. Then every
  `B.centralizer.toNormalizer z` lies in
  `B.reflectionNormalizer ⊔ B.apartment.normalizerAffineEquiv.ker`. Membership is
  transferred along the injective inclusion of `normalizerPoints B.i`
  (`Subgroup.mem_map_iff_mem`), the image of the join is the join of the images
  (`Subgroup.map_sup`), and the two previous containments are combined with
  `sup_le_sup`.
* `reflectionTranslationLattice_eq_of_le`: under the same two premises,
  `B.reflectionTranslationLattice = B.centralizerTranslationLattice`. One
  inclusion is the published
  `reflectionTranslationLattice_le_centralizerTranslationLattice`. For the other,
  a translation `v` of the centralizer lattice is the reduced translation of
  some `z ∈ Z(F)`, and the published equivalence
  `centralizer_mem_reflections_sup_ker_iff` turns the previous theorem into
  membership of `v` in the reflection translation lattice.
* `simplyConnectedReflectionTranslation_of_rankOneGeneration`: from
  `RootRankOneFactorizationStatement.{u}` and
  `SimplyConnectedRankOneCentralizerGenerationStatement.{u}` it proves
  `SimplyConnectedReflectionTranslationStatement.{u}`. Given `B` with `G` simply
  connected, the factorization statement, applied with the connected
  reductivity and maximal splitness recorded in `B`, gives for each nonidentity
  point of each actual root group a factorization whose existence part is an
  opposite factor pair; this is `RelativeRoot.HasOppositeFactorPairs B.i`. The
  rank-one generation statement, applied to `B.S`, `B.i`, `B.centralizer.Z`,
  `B.centralizer.j` and the representability in `B.centralizer`, gives `hgen`,
  and `reflectionTranslationLattice_eq_of_le` concludes.

## Provenance

Internal. Kaletha–Prasad obtain the equality of these lattices for simply
connected groups from axiom AS 5 (quasi-split case) and from the descent
argument of their Section 9.4, which uses facet fixing and is not available
here. This module replaces that route by the rank-one generation of `Z(F)`
(Kneser–Tits together with Prasad–Raghunathan, as recorded in the paired
Markdown of `RankOneCentralizerGenerationStatement`) and the elementary
rank-one Bruhat argument of `RankOneReflectionGeneration`. The remaining
argument is subgroup calculus with the published lattice and kernel lemmas.
This module proves neither premise; both appear explicitly in the Lean
theorem.
