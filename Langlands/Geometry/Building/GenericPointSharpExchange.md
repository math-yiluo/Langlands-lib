# Point exchange and unipotent stabilizers for apartment data over any field

Module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.GenericPointSharpExchange`.

The published `PointSharpExchange` and `RootGroupZeroFixer` prove the point
exchange and the unipotent-stabilizer lemma for building data over a
nonarchimedean local field that satisfy the apartment axioms. This derived unit
repeats them for arbitrary apartment data `D : KPApartmentData i ω` over any
field `k : Type u`, with no apartment axiom. Here `i : S.toGrp ⟶ G` is a
monomorphism into an affine group scheme `G`, and `ω : kˣ → ℝ` is arbitrary.
The main results assume that `G` is connected reductive (`hG`) and that `i`
is a maximal split torus (`hi`). Two inputs of the local proofs are replaced:

* AS 1 is proved for `D` directly. The published proof of
  `RootReflectionProof.hasRootReflections` only uses the root-function
  family, so it applies verbatim.
* The local proofs use the apartment axioms only through AS 2, to show that
  proportional roots with positive ratio are equal or differ by a factor two.
  That ray property is proved here from the coroots of opposite factor pairs.

All results are in the namespace `Langlands.Geometry.Building.KPApartmentData`.

## Declarations

* `smul_eq_self_of_val_eq_zero` (AS 1, Kaletha–Prasad Proposition 6.2.1).
  Assume `[Smooth G.X.hom]` and that `S` is a split torus. Let `a` be a
  relative root, `u ∈ U_a(k)` with `u ≠ 1`, and `p` an opposite factor pair of
  `u`. Then the normalizer element `⟨p.1 * u * p.2, _⟩` fixes every apartment
  point `x` with `x.val a u = 0`.
* `hasRayProperty_of_rankOne`: under `[Smooth G.X.hom]`, a split `S` and
  `RelativeRoot.HasRankOneFactorization i`, the published predicate
  `RootGroupFamily.HasRayProperty i` holds. That is, `eval d = t • eval c` with
  `t > 0` for relative roots `c, d` forces `d = c`, `d = 2 • c` or `c = 2 • d`.
* `exists_rankOne_factorization_fixing`: the factorization `v v′ = x′ z y` of
  `ChamberRankOne`, with a centralizer factor `z` that fixes `x`. Its
  statement is that of the published
  `ValuationBuildingData.exists_rankOne_factorization_fixing`, for `D` and
  under `hG`, `hi` and rank-one factorization.
* `filtration_neg_mul_filtration_subset_sharpNormalizerStabilizer`, the
  rank-one exchange at an arbitrary point:
  `U_{-a,x,0} U_{a,x,0} ⊆ U_{a,x,0} U_{-a,x,0} H_x`. Here
  `H_x = D.sharpNormalizerStabilizer x` is the published subgroup of elements
  of `N(k) ∩ G(k)^♯_x` that fix `x`.
* `sharpFixer_subset_mul_sharpNormalizerStabilizer`: under `hG` and `hi`, for
  every apartment point `x` and generic cocharacter `mu`,
  `G(k)^♯_x ⊆ X_x(Φ_{-mu}) X_x(Φ_mu) H_x`, with
  `X_x(P) = ⨆_{b ∈ P} D.chamberRootGroup x false b`.
* `mem_positiveRootGroups_of_mem_unipotent_of_smul_apartmentMap_eq`: under
  `hG` and `hi`, let `ν` be a generic cocharacter. If
  `g ∈ Dynamic.unipotent (groupHom ν ≫ i)` on rational points and `g` fixes
  `D.apartmentMap x`, then `g ∈ ⨆ b : positiveRelativeRoots i ν,
  D.chamberRootGroup x false b`.

## Implemented proof

`smul_eq_self_of_val_eq_zero` repeats the published proof of
`hasRootReflections`, with `B.apartment` replaced by `D`. Write
`m = p.1 u p.2`. Valuation axiom V 5, applied to `m` and `m⁻¹`, shows that `u`,
`p.1` and `p.2` have value zero at `x`. Then `m` lies in the subgroup
generated by the depth-zero filtrations of `a` and `-a`. The translation
vector `v` of `m` (with `m • x.val = v +ᵥ x.val`) satisfies `a(v) = 0`.
The published `RootReflectionProof.not_eval_neg`, applied to `m` and `m⁻¹`,
excludes roots `d` with `d(v) ≠ 0`. Hence `v` lies in the root annihilator,
and `m • x = x`.

`hasRayProperty_of_rankOne` follows the published
`eq_or_double_of_eval_eq_smul`, but takes its coroots from
`HasOppositeFactorPairs.rootCoroot`, which pairs each root with value `2`.
Evaluating `eval d = t • eval c` on the two coroots gives integers `z₁ = 2t`
and `z₂` with `t z₂ = 2`, so `z₁ z₂ = 4` with both positive. Hence
`t ∈ {1/2, 1, 2}`; the case `z₁ = 3` is impossible. The published
`eval_injective` turns this into the stated equality of characters.

The proofs of `exists_rankOne_factorization_fixing` and of the rank-one
exchange are the published ones, with three substitutions. AS 1 is
`smul_eq_self_of_val_eq_zero`. The spanning input is the published
`relativeRootsRestrictedCharactersSpan` over `k`. Rank-one factorization is
the published `rootRankOneFactorization`.

`sharpFixer_subset_mul_sharpNormalizerStabilizer` applies the published
`RootGroupFamily.iSup_subset_mul_of_exchange` to the family
`b ↦ D.rootFixer b {x}`. Its inputs are the published commutator relations
`rootFixer_hasCommutatorRelations`, finiteness of the relative roots,
`hasRayProperty_of_rankOne`, the published `neg_mem_relativeRoots_of_rankOne`
and `rootFixer_double_le`, and the rank-one exchange.
`rootFixer_singleton` identifies `D.rootFixer b {x}` with the depth-zero
filtration, hence with `D.chamberRootGroup x false b`. The sharp fixer is by
definition the supremum of these root fixers.

The unipotent-stabilizer lemma repeats the published proof of
`ParahoricBuildingData.mem_positiveRootGroups_of_mem_unipotent_of_smul_apartmentMap_eq`
with the new sharp exchange. The stabilizer of `D.apartmentMap x` is
`G(k)^♯_x N(k)_x` (`ApartmentQuotient.mem_stabilizer_apartment_iff`). The
exchange writes `g = v w n′` with `v ∈ X_x(Φ_ν)`, `w ∈ X_x(Φ_{-ν})` and
`n′ ∈ N(k)`. `normalizer_eq_one_of_unipotent_product` gives `n′ = 1`. Then
`Dynamic.eq_one_of_mem_opposite_unipotent_of_mem_parabolic` gives `w = 1`.

## Provenance and scope

This is a derived unit. It ports the published local-field arguments, which
follow Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023),
Proposition 6.2.1 (printed pp. 212–214 / PDF pp. 243–245) and the range part
of Proposition 7.3.12(1) (printed p. 250 / PDF p. 281), to apartment data
over any field. The local versions assume the apartment axioms. This unit
avoids them by deriving the ray property from opposite factor pairs. The
Lean statements are not quoted from the source. The unit proves no
apartment axiom, no common-apartment property and no statement about a
specific building.
