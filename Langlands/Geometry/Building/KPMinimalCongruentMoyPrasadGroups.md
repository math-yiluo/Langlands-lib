# Moy–Prasad groups with minimal congruent torus terms

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field of either characteristic and put
`K = UnramifiedClosure F = inertiaFixedField F`. Let `B` be building data
for an affine group `H` over `K`, and let `hT : IsTorus B.centralizer.Z`.
The centralizer of the maximal `K`-split torus is then a torus `T`. The
commutative presentation `B.centralizerTorus hT` has underlying group
scheme `B.centralizer.Z`.

`B.minimalCongruentTorusFiltration hT r` is
`minimalCongruentPoints F (B.centralizerTorus hT) hT r` for each finite
nonnegative real depth `r`. `B.minimalCongruentMoyPrasad hT x r` uses that
family in `B.moyPrasadWith`. It is the subgroup generated by the image
of `T(K)ᵐᶜ_r` and the root filtration blocks at the apartment point `x`.
The blocks include the divisible-root terms from KP Definition 7.3.3(1).
Taking `H = Z_K` and `x` the image of the point of the reduced building of
`Z` gives the upstairs group of KP §9.8.

`minimalCongruentTorusFiltration_zero` identifies the two torus
filtrations at zero using `minimalCongruentPoints_zero` and
`connectedNeronModel_integralPoints`. Rewriting the torus generator in
the common construction gives `minimalCongruentMoyPrasad_zero`.
