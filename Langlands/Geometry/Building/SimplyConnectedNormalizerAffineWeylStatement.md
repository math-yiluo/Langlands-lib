# The action of `N(F) ∩ G(F)^♮` on the apartment has image `W(Ψ)`

`Langlands.Geometry.Building.SimplyConnectedNormalizerAffineWeylStatement` is the image
part of Kaletha–Prasad, Theorem 7.5.3(2) (printed p.261/PDF p.292), for the Tits system
`(G^♮, I^♮, N^♮, R)` with `G^♮ = G(F)^♮` and `N^♮ = N(F) ∩ G^♮`.

It quantifies over a nonarchimedean local field `F`, a group scheme `G` over `F` with
affine structure morphism and actual building data `P : ParahoricBuildingData G`, and
assumes the apartment axioms `P.apartment.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms P.centralizer`
(V 6, V 7 and AS 2–AS 5, with the AS 5 of the corrected opening of KP Chapter 7), the
same hypothesis as `IwahoriWeylExactSequenceStatement`. It asserts that

`(P.simplyConnectedImage.subgroupOf (normalizerPoints P.i)).map
   P.apartment.normalizerAffineEquiv = AffineRootSystem.affineWeylGroup P.affineRoots`.

* `normalizerPoints P.i` is `N(F)`, `N = N_G(S)`, and `P.simplyConnectedImage` is
  `G(F)^♮`, the image of `G_sc(F) → G(F)` (KP Definition 2.6.20) for the simply
  connected cover in `P.zeroDatum`. The subgroup of `N(F)` on the left is
  `N^♮ = N(F) ∩ G(F)^♮`.
* `P.apartment.normalizerAffineEquiv : N(F) →* (A ≃ᵃ[ℝ] A)` is the actual action of
  `N(F)` on the valuations of the apartment `A = A(S)`.
* `AffineRootSystem.affineWeylGroup P.affineRoots` is `W(Ψ)`, the subgroup of affine
  automorphisms generated by the reflections along the affine roots `Ψ` of the apartment.

So every element of `N^♮` acts on `A` by an element of `W(Ψ)`, and every element of
`W(Ψ)` is the action of some element of `N^♮`. It does not assert that the kernel of the action on `N^♮` is `Z^♮`,
nor the Tits system axioms of Theorem 7.5.3(1).

## Provenance

KP Theorem 7.5.3(2) says that the action of `N_G(T)(k)` on `A` identifies the Weyl
groups of the Tits systems `(G, I, N, R)` and `(G^♮, I^♮, N^♮, R)` with the Weyl group
of the affine root system `Ψ`. For the second system this Weyl group is `N^♮/Z^♮`, and
the identification means that `N^♮` acts on `A` through exactly `W(Ψ)`; the formalized
clause is this image equality. The source proof transfers Theorem 7.5.1(2) for the
semisimple simply connected group `G_sc`, whose image statement is AS 5 for `G_sc` (KP
Proposition 6.6.2), along the map `G_sc(k) → G^♮`, whose kernel is central and contained
in `Z'(k)^0`.

The hypothesis is the apartment-axiom predicate of `G` itself. The AS 5 clause of that
predicate gives the image `W(Ψ)` of all of `N(F)` only when `G` is simply connected, so
the statement for general `G` uses the source's reduction to `G_sc`. The 2024
corrections (PDF p.33) record that AS 5 for semisimple simply connected groups, which this
reduction uses, is proved in KP Proposition 9.4.35 when the residue field is perfect; the
residue field of a nonarchimedean local field is finite, hence perfect. This file states
the proposition; it does not prove it.
