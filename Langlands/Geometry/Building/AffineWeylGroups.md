# Uniqueness of coroots, the derivative Weyl group and the extended affine Weyl group

Module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.AffineWeylGroups`, namespace
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.AffineRootSystem`, the same namespace as the module it extends.

This module complements `Langlands.Geometry.Building.AffineRootSystem` with three items
from Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023), §1.3:

- uniqueness of the coroot vector of AR 2 (Remark 1.3.2(2));
- the Weyl group `W(Φ)` of the derivative root system, realized on `V`;
- the extended affine Weyl group `W(Ψ)^ext` of Definition 1.3.71 (book PDF p. 75,
  printed p. 44).

The setting is the same: `[AddTorsor V A]` over a real vector space `V`, with
`Ψ : Set (A →ᵃ[ℝ] ℝ)`. The *Corrections and additions* of 1 November 2024 do not change
Remark 1.3.2 or Definition 1.3.71. No group, field or building is involved.

## Derivatives

- `exists_linear_eq χ`: every `χ ∈ V*` is the derivative of an affine functional on `A`.
  The proof picks a point `p ∈ A` and uses `x ↦ χ(x - p)`, via Mathlib's `AffineMap.mk'`.
  The point is used only in the proof; no origin enters any definition.
- `derivativeMap : (A →ᵃ[ℝ] ℝ) →ₗ[ℝ] Module.Dual ℝ V` is `ψ ↦ ψ̇` as a linear map. KP's
  `∇ : A* → V*` is its restriction to `Ψ` in AR 1. `derivativeRoots_eq_image` says
  `∇Ψ` is the image of `Ψ` under it.
- `span_derivativeRoots_eq_top`: if `Ψ` spans `A*`, as AR 1 requires, then `Φ = ∇Ψ`
  spans `V*`.

## Uniqueness of coroots

`IsAffineRootSystem.isCoroot_unique`: if `Ψ` is an affine root system and `v` and `w` both
satisfy `IsCoroot Ψ ψ` (AR 2 for the same `ψ`), then `v = w`. This is KP Remark 1.3.2(2).
KP derives it from Bourbaki, *Lie Groups and Lie Algebras* VI §1 no. 1, Lemma 1, applied to
the derivative reflection on `V*`. The Lean proof applies Mathlib's version of that lemma,
`Module.Dual.eq_of_preReflection_mapsTo`:

- take `M = V*`, the root `x = ψ̇`, the finite spanning set `Φ = ∇Ψ` (AR 1 and
  `span_derivativeRoots_eq_top`), and the two functionals `f = eval v`, `g = eval w` on
  `V*`;
- then `f(ψ̇) = ψ̇(v) = 2`, and the pre-reflection `χ ↦ χ - χ(v) ψ̇` maps `Φ` into `Φ`,
  because it sends `η̇` to the derivative of `η - η̇(v) ψ ∈ Ψ` (the AR 2 condition). The
  same holds for `w`;
- the lemma gives `eval v = eval w`, and `Module.eval_apply_injective` gives `v = w`.

Consequently, for an affine root system:

- the AR 3 field `coroot_integral` of `IsAffineRootSystem`, which quantifies over every
  AR 2 vector, refers to the unique `ψ̇∨`, as in KP;
- the generators of `affineWeylGroup Ψ` are exactly KP's reflections `r_ψ`, one for each
  `ψ ∈ Ψ`.

## The derivative Weyl group `W(Φ)`

`derivativeWeylGroup Ψ : Subgroup (V ≃ₗ[ℝ] V)` is the subgroup generated by the
reflections `Module.reflection hv.1 : w ↦ w - ψ̇(w) v` for `ψ ∈ Ψ` and `IsCoroot Ψ ψ v`.
`reflection_mem_derivativeWeylGroup` says each generator belongs to it.

These reflections are the derivatives of the affine reflections
(`affineReflection_linear`). KP Proposition 1.3.3(2) identifies `ψ̇∨` with the coroot of
`ψ̇` in the finite root system `Φ`, so this is the Weyl group `W(Φ)`, realized on `V` by the
contragredient of its action on `V*`. This identification is a derived reading: the module
does not prove that `Φ` is a root system or that `ψ̇∨` is its coroot.

## The extended affine Weyl group `W(Ψ)^ext`

`extendedAffineWeylGroup Ψ` is `rootStabilizer Ψ ⊓ (derivativeWeylGroup Ψ).comap
AffineEquiv.linearHom`. These are the affine automorphisms `e` of `A` such that pull-back
by `e` permutes `Ψ` and the derivative `e.linear` lies in `W(Φ)`
(`mem_extendedAffineWeylGroup`). This is KP Definition 1.3.71: "the group `W(Ψ)^ext`
consisting of those automorphisms of the affine space `A` that preserve `Ψ` and whose
derivative is an element of `W(Φ)`". "Preserve `Ψ`" is read as `Ψ ∘ e = Ψ`, which is the
`rootStabilizer` condition.

Laws:

- `extendedAffineWeylGroup_le_rootStabilizer`.
- `affineWeylGroup_le_extendedAffineWeylGroup`: `W(Ψ) ≤ W(Ψ)^ext`.
- `linear_mem_derivativeWeylGroup`: the derivative of every element of `W(Ψ)` lies in
  `W(Φ)`.

KP 1.3.72 (`W(Ψ)^ext = W(Ψ) ⋊ Ξ_C`) and Lemma 1.3.74 are not formalized.

These groups are what Axiom AS 5 of KP Axiom 4.1.6 (book PDF p. 191, printed p. 160)
refers to. Its first sentence, "the image of the action map `f : N(k) → Aff(A)` contains
`W(Ψ)` and is contained in `W(Ψ)^ext`", reads `affineWeylGroup Ψ ≤ f.range ∧ f.range ≤
extendedAffineWeylGroup Ψ` for `f : N(k) →* (A ≃ᵃ[ℝ] A)`. AS 5 also asks that the image
equal `W(Ψ)` when `G` is simply connected and `W(Ψ)^ext` when `G` is quasi-split and
adjoint; those clauses concern the reductive group. This module states no part of AS 5: the
actual action belongs to the apartment modules.
