# The models `𝒢_{x,r}`, `𝒰_ψ` and `𝒵_r` over the integers of `F`

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field of either characteristic, `K = inertiaFixedField F`
(`UnramifiedClosure F`) its maximal unramified extension, `G` an affine group over `F` and
`P : ParahoricBuildingData G` (it records that `G` is connected reductive, a maximal split
torus `S`, the apartment `A(S)` and the centralizer `Z = Z_G(S)`). An `UnramifiedComparisonTorus`
`t` is a special torus `T ⊇ S` with `T_K` maximal `K`-split, and an `ApartmentLift` `L` of `t`
gives building data `L.buildingData` of `G_K` and the embedding `L.e : A(S) → A(T_K)` of
Galois-fixed points. For `x ∈ A(S)` and `r : ℝ≥0`:

- `G(K)_{x,r}` is `L.buildingData.minimalCongruentMoyPrasad L.centralizer_isTorus (L.e x) r`
  (KP §9.8.2: the constant function `r` composed with restriction `Φ̂(T_K) → Φ̂(S)` is again
  the constant function `r`);
- `P_{x,r} = G(F)_{x,r}` is `P.minimalCongruentMoyPrasad h x r`, generated by
  `Z(F)_r = P.minimalCongruentCentralizerPoints r` and the root blocks `U_{a,x,r} U_{2a,x,r}`;
  it needs `h : P.MinimalCongruentCentralizerOrder`.

For a model `M : IntegralModel 𝒪[F] F G`, `M.integralPoints ⊆ G(F)` is the image of
`M(𝒪[F])` and `M.baseChangeUnramified.integralPoints ⊆ G(K)` is the image of the points of the
base change of `M` to the integers `fixedFieldIntegers F` of `K`. The statements below fix each
model by the latter group, as KP do: in KP `X(O)` always denotes points over the integers of
the maximal unramified extension (KP Definition 2.10.15 and Fact 2.10.16). The hypothesis
`P.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms` of the setting is assumed in the statements about `G`.

## Statements

- `MoyPrasadModelStatement` is KP §9.8.2 with Proposition 9.8.3(2) at the constant concave
  function `r`. For every special-torus lift `L`, there is a smooth model `𝒢_{x,r}` of `G` over
  `𝒪[F]` with geometrically connected fibres whose integral points over the integers of `K`
  are `G(K)_{x,r}` and whose integral points over `𝒪[F]` are `P_{x,r}`. By KP Corollary
  2.10.11, the first equality identifies the base change of `𝒢_{x,r}` with the model of `G_K`
  from KP Theorem 8.5.2; KP §9.8.2 obtains this descent by Fact 2.10.16. With
  `IntegralModel.integralPoints_eq_comap_baseChangeUnramified` the two equalities give
  `G(F)_{x,r} = G(K)_{x,r} ∩ G(F)`, the content of Proposition 9.8.3(2).
- `MoyPrasadModelCongruenceStatement` is KP Proposition 8.5.16 for these models: for every
  smooth model `M` of `G` over `𝒪[F]` whose integral points over the integers of `K` are
  `G(K)_{x,r}` and every `n : ℕ+`, `M.congruenceSubgroupAt n`, the image of
  `ker(M(𝒪[F]) → M(𝒪[F]/𝔪ⁿ))`, is `P_{x,r+n}`.
- `AffineRootGroupModelStatement` is KP 9.4.5 with Lemma 9.4.6. Let `ψ` be an affine root of `P`
  (`P.affineRoots`, KP Definition 6.3.4) whose derivative is the relative root `a`
  (`ψ.linear = rootForm P.i a`), and let `j : U ⟶ G` represent the root subgroup functor of
  `a`. There is a smooth model `𝒰_ψ` of `U` over `𝒪[F]` with geometrically connected fibres
  such that
  - for every lift `L` and every `z ∈ A(S)` on the vanishing hyperplane of `ψ`, the image of
    its integral points over the integers of `K` in `G(K)` is `U_a(K) ∩ G(K)^1_z`, where
    `U_a(K)` is the image of `U(K)` and `G(K)^1_z = L.buildingData.oneStabilizer` of the point
    `L.e z`; this is the group of integral points of the `a`-root group of the Bruhat–Tits
    model `𝒢^1_z` (KP 9.4.5), and quantifying over all such `z` contains Lemma 9.4.6(1);
  - the image of its integral points over `𝒪[F]` in `G(F)` is `U_ψ = P.affineRootGroup a ψ`
    (Lemma 9.4.6(2)).

  KP 9.4.5 constructs `𝒰_ψ` as the `a`-root group of `𝒢^1_z` with respect to the split torus
  `𝒮` and observes that its fibres are connected.
- `CentralizerModelStatement` is the model `𝒵_r` of KP 13.3.3. There is a smooth model `𝒵_r`
  of `Z` over `𝒪[F]` with geometrically connected fibres such that
  - its integral points over the integers of `K` are `Z(K)_{x₀,r}`, the group of KP §9.8
    computed at the unique point `x₀` of the building of `Z`, with the special-torus lift
    `P.centralizerUnramifiedApartmentLift` used in the definition of
    `minimalCongruentCentralizerPoints`;
  - its integral points over `𝒪[F]` are `Z(F)_r = P.minimalCongruentCentralizerPoints r`
    (this follows from the previous clause and the definition of `Z(F)_r`);
  - for every `x ∈ A(S)` and every lift `L` of `G`, the image of its integral points over the
    integers of `K` in `G(K)` is `Z(K) ∩ G(K)_{x,r}`.

  KP 13.3.3 defines `𝒵_r` as the fixed-point scheme of the action of the standard model `𝒮` of
  `S` on `𝒢_{x,r}` (Proposition 9.8.3); its integral points are
  `Z(K) ∩ G(K)_{x,r} = Z(K)_{x,r}` by Lemma 8.5.19, as in the proof of Proposition 9.8.3. The
  last clause records this description; by KP Corollary 2.10.10 it yields a homomorphism
  `𝒵_r → 𝒢_{x,r}` extending `Z → G`. The construction of the fixed-point scheme itself is not
  stated.

## Points over `𝒪[F]` do not determine the model

The models are characterized by their points over the integers of `K`, not by their points
over `𝒪[F]`. Over a finite residue field the latter can fail to determine a smooth model,
even one with connected fibres. Let `F = ℚ₂` (or `F = 𝔽₂((t))`) and `G = G_m`, with its
standard model `𝒳`, so `𝒳(𝒪[F]) = 𝒪[F]^×`. The special fibre `G_m` over `𝔽₂` has the single
rational point `1`. Let `𝒳'` be the dilatation of `𝒳` along the unit section of its special
fibre: a smooth affine model with connected fibres (its special fibre is `G_a`) and
`𝒳'(𝒪[F]) = 1 + 𝔪 = 𝒪[F]^×`. Thus `𝒳(𝒪[F]) = 𝒳'(𝒪[F])`, but `Lie(𝒳) = 𝒪[F]` while
`Lie(𝒳') = 𝔪`, and the first congruence subgroups are `1 + 𝔪` and `1 + 𝔪²`. Over the
integers of `K` the two models have different points, `𝒪^×` and `1 + 𝔪`. Here `𝒳` is the
model `𝒢_{x,0}` of `G = G_m` (`P_{x,0} = 𝒪[F]^×`), so the hypothesis of
`MoyPrasadModelCongruenceStatement` cannot be weakened to `M(𝒪[F]) = P_{x,r}`.
