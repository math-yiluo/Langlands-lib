# Axiom 4.1.8 under based group isomorphisms

Module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.FullValidity.DatumTransport.RootGroups`.

## Purpose and provenance

Internal infrastructure for `KPBuildingDatumTransportStatement`: clauses (2) and (3) of KP
Axiom 4.1.8 (with the corrected hypothesis that `Ω` is non-empty and bounded) for the source
datum give the same clauses for the transported datum. Clause (1) holds for all data in the
Library (`hasHalfApartmentFixers`), so it needs no transport. The source does not state this
transport.

## Contents

- `ValuationBuildingData.isBoundedSubset_image_basedApartmentEquiv_iff`: a subset `Ω` of `A(S)`
  is bounded exactly when its image under the apartment comparison is bounded. Affine
  functionals are pulled back and pushed forward along the comparison.
- `image_apartmentMap_basedTransport`: the building comparison carries the image of `Ω` in
  `B(G)` to the image of the transported subset in `B(H)`.
- `map_rootGroup_basedTransport`, `map_rootFixer_basedTransport`: `f` carries `U_a(F)` onto
  `U_{σ(a)}(F)` and `U_{a,Ω,0}` onto the corresponding fixer of the transported subset.
- `ParahoricBuildingData.map_zeroFixer_basedTransport`: `f` carries `G(F)^0_Ω` onto the
  corresponding group for `H` (the zero subgroups and the pointwise fixers correspond, and `f`
  is injective on points).
- `map_nonnegativeRootSubgroup_basedTransport`: `f` carries the group generated by `Z(F)^0` and
  the `U_ψ` with `ψ ∈ Ψ`, `ψ(Ω) ≥ 0` onto the corresponding group for `H`. Roots, affine roots,
  derivatives, non-negativity on `Ω` and the groups `U_ψ` all correspond.
- `exists_image_basedApartmentEquiv`: every subset of the target apartment is a transported
  subset.
- `HasZeroFixerGenerators.basedTransport` (Axiom 4.1.8 (2)) and
  `HasRootZeroFixers.basedTransport` (Axiom 4.1.8 (3)).
