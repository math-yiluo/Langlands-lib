# Covariance reduction

This derived construction assembles the local root-conjugation bounds and actual apartment quotient relation. It isolates one remaining input to full covariance and constructs the unique original subgroup at a building point conditional on that input. The mixed opposite-root bound is an explicit hypothesis; it is not proved by this module.

`conjugate_root_mem_moyPrasad_of_double_opposite_bound` classifies the remaining generator pairs. If V3's nonopposition condition holds, the accompanying nonopposite-root lemma applies. Otherwise the actual root-system proportionality theorem leaves three cases. Exactly opposite directions are covered by the accompanying original opposite-root conjugation coordinates. When the conjugating direction is itself twice the opposite input direction, V4 embeds its depth-zero filtration into the ordinary depth-zero filtration, reducing to the same proved case. The only case passed to the explicit `hmixed` hypothesis is conjugation of `U_{-2a,x,r}` by `U_{a,x,0}`, for positive `r` and actual roots `a` and `2a`.

`sharpFixer_le_normalizer_moyPrasad_of_root_conjugation` applies conjugation to the original defining generators. The root-compatible middle-image bound handles the original centralizer factor. The supplied generator bounds handle the original root-depth factors. Both suprema are taken in the original ambient rational-point group. Their conjugation containment gives normalization by every depth-zero root subgroup, and hence by the original sharp fixer.

`sharpFixer_le_normalizer_moyPrasad_of_double_opposite_bound` supplies these generator bounds from the classification and the explicit mixed hypothesis. At depth zero, the original Moy–Prasad subgroup is the original point parahoric; containment of the sharp fixer and subgroup closure give normalization directly. Thus no positive-depth estimate is used at depth zero.

`isMoyPrasadAt_unique_of_sharp_normalization` uses the original quotient defining the reduced building. Equality of two presentations `(g,x)` and `(h,x')` gives an actual normalizer point `n` with `n • x = x'` and `p = g⁻¹ h n` in the original sharp fixer at `x`. The accompanying normalizer map equality transports the original depth group from `x'` to `x`, while sharp normalization makes conjugation by `p` preserve that group. The identity `h = g p n⁻¹` then identifies the two original conjugate subgroups. No change of apartment quotient, subgroup or presentation predicate is made.

`moyPrasadAtUniqueOfSharpNormalization` chooses the published existence witness for `IsMoyPrasadAt` and adds this uniqueness certificate. `moyPrasadAtUniqueOfDoubleOppositeBound` specializes that construction to the supplied apartment and centralizer-filtration axioms together with the single explicit mixed bound. Its result is the original subgroup and its actual presentation and uniqueness certificates at the supplied point and depth.

All universes and depths are arbitrary. The construction is internal derivation from the original root-system classification, V4, the accompanying original conjugation bounds and normalizer covariance, and the published apartment quotient relation. It does not discharge `hmixed`, prove the unconditional `MoyPrasadCovarianceStatement`, construct an open centralizer filtration, or prove the host simultaneous normal-basis statement.
