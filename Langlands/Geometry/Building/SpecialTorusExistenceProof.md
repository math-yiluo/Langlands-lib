# Existence of special tori (KP Proposition 9.3.4, proof)

This module proves the Library proposition
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.SpecialTorusExistenceStatement.{u}` (Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits
Theory: A New Approach*, Proposition 9.3.4, printed p. 332) at arbitrary universe `u`, in equal
and mixed characteristic. The statement module
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.UnramifiedSpecialApartmentStatement` and its Markdown are
unchanged. The theorem has no premise: there is no extra hypothesis and no supplied building,
point, integral model or torus.

## Declaration

`Langlands.Geometry.Building.specialTorusExistence : SpecialTorusExistenceStatement.{u}`

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field and `K = inertiaFixedField F` its maximal unramified
extension. Let `G` be a connected reductive group scheme over `Spec F` and `iS : S ⟶ G` a
maximal split torus. Then there are a commutative group scheme `T` over `Spec F`, a
homomorphism `iT : T ⟶ G` and a homomorphism `f : S ⟶ T` of commutative group schemes, with
`f ≫ iT = iS` and `iT` a closed subgroup. The base change of `iT` along
`algebraBaseMap F K` is a maximal `K`-split torus of `G_K`.

## The implemented proof

The proof is one line, `specialTorusExistence_of_integralModel specialTorusIntegralModel`.
It combines two theorems.

1. `Langlands.Geometry.Building.specialTorusIntegralModel :
   SpecialTorusIntegralModelStatement.{u}`, a published Library theorem (module
   `Langlands.Geometry.Building.SpecialTorusIntegralModelProof`). For `F`, `G` and `iS` as
   above it gives a smooth integral model `M` of `G` over `𝒪[F]` with connected special fibre.
   `M` contains a closed split `𝒪[F]`-torus `𝒮` with generic fibre `iS`. Its base change to
   `O_K = fixedFieldIntegers F` contains a closed split `O_K`-torus `𝒜` whose generic fibre is
   a maximal `K`-split torus of `G_K`. This is the building part of the proof of KP 9.3.4.
2. `Langlands.Geometry.Building.specialTorusExistence_of_integralModel :
   SpecialTorusIntegralModelStatement.{u} → SpecialTorusExistenceStatement.{u}`, in the helper
   module `Langlands.Geometry.Building.SpecialTorusExistenceFromModel` of this contribution. Its
   Markdown describes the proof in full. In short, it does the following.
   * It takes the centralizer `Z` of `𝒮` in `M` and a maximal torus `T₀` of the special fibre
     of `M` that contains the special fibre of `𝒮`.
   * It lifts `T₀` to a closed relative torus `𝒯` of `Z`, using the published
     `HenselianTorusLifting.nonempty_liftData` (KP Proposition 8.2.1(1)).
   * `𝒯` splits over `O_K`, because `O_K` is Henselian with separably closed residue field.
   * Over `F`, the closed torus `T` is generated by `S` and the generic fibre of `𝒯`. These
     commute, since `𝒯` lies in the centralizer of `𝒮`.
   * `T_K` is a maximal `K`-split torus by a rank count. The rank of the lift is at least the
     rank of `𝒜`, by comparing both with `T₀` over the residue field of `O_K`. So `T_K` has at
     least the common rank of all maximal split tori of `G_K`.

The rank count replaces the apartment argument that KP use for maximality.

## Modules of this contribution

Besides this module and `SpecialTorusExistenceFromModel`, the contribution adds four helper
modules, each with its own Markdown:

* `Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.ResidueRankComparison`: ranks of closed split tori compared
  with a maximal torus of a residue fibre.
* `Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.StrictHenselianRelativeSplit`: relative tori over a Henselian
  local ring with separably closed residue field are split.
* `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.CommutingTorusImage`: the closed torus generated by two
  commuting tori that split over an extension field.
* `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.SplitRankMaximality`: a closed split subtorus whose rank is at
  least that of a maximal split torus is a maximal split torus.

## Restricted inputs

The proof does not use `UnramifiedSpecialApartmentStatement` or
`UnramifiedGaloisFixedApartmentStatement`. The statement module is imported only for the
definition of the target.
