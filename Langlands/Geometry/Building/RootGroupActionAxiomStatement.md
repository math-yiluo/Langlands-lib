# Axiom 4.1.8 (2) and (3) for the building of a valuation of the root datum

`Langlands.Geometry.Building.RootGroupActionAxiomStatement` is a Prop-valued
`def` at every universe `u`. It says that the existing building of supplied
valuation data satisfies parts (2) and (3) of Kaletha–Prasad Axiom 4.1.8,
under the hypotheses of KP Chapter 7 as corrected in 2024. The module supplies
no proof.

## Statement

The proposition quantifies over:

- every nonarchimedean local field `F`, of either characteristic;
- every group scheme `G` over `Spec F` with `[IsAffineHom G.X.hom]`;
- every `P : ParahoricBuildingData G`. These are the Library's building data:
  a maximal split torus `i : S → G`, the class `A(S)` of valuations of the
  root datum of `(G, S)` normalized by the valuation of `F`, the actual
  centralizer `Z = Z_G(S)` with axiom V 6 on `Z(F)`, and the data of KP
  Definition 2.6.23 for `Z(F)^0` and `G(F)^0`. Connected reductivity of `G`
  is a field of the data.

Its single premise is `P.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms`, which unfolds to
`P.apartment.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms P.centralizer`. Since V 6 is part of
the data, the premise asks for V 7 (the root filtrations are compatible with
the topology of `G(F)`, KP Definition 6.1.2) and AS 2–AS 5 of KP Axiom 4.1.6
for the class `A(S)`.

The conclusion is the conjunction `P.HasZeroFixerGenerators ∧
P.HasRootZeroFixers` of the Library's predicates from
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.ParahoricBuildingAxioms`. Both concern every
non-empty subset `Ω` of the apartment `A(S)` that is bounded in the sense of
`P.IsBoundedSubset Ω`: every affine functional on `A(S)` is bounded above on
`Ω`. Write `G(F)^0_Ω` for `P.zeroFixer Ω`, the elements of
`G(F)^0 = P.zeroSubgroup` that fix every point of the image of `Ω` in the
building `B(G) = P.toValuationBuildingData.ReducedBuilding`.

- `P.HasZeroFixerGenerators` (Axiom 4.1.8 (2)): `G(F)^0_Ω` equals
  `P.nonnegativeRootSubgroup Ω`. This is the subgroup of `G(F)` generated by
  the image of `Z(F)^0` under the centralizer inclusion and the groups
  `U_ψ = P.affineRootGroup a ψ` for the relative roots `a` and the affine roots
  `ψ ∈ Ψ = P.affineRoots` with derivative `a` that are non-negative on `Ω`.
- `P.HasRootZeroFixers` (Axiom 4.1.8 (3)): for every relative root `a`,
  `U_a(F) ∩ G(F)^0_Ω` equals `U_{a,Ω,0} = P.rootFixer a Ω`, the intersection
  over `x ∈ Ω` of the depth-zero filtration groups `U_{a,x,0}`. The published
  `HasRootZeroFixers.rootGroup_inf_zeroFixer_eq` rewrites this as KP's form
  `U_a(F) ∩ G(F)^0_Ω = U_ψ`, for `ψ ∈ Ψ′` the smallest element with
  derivative `a` that is non-negative on `Ω`.

Part (1) of Axiom 4.1.8 holds for all data and is already proved in the
Library (`ValuationBuildingData.hasHalfApartmentFixers`). Hence the
conclusion is equivalent to `D.SatisfiesRootGroupActionAxiom` for any
`D : KPBuildingDatum G` with `D.base = P`
(`KPBuildingDatum.satisfiesRootGroupActionAxiom_iff`).

## Derived scope and provenance

This is a derived formulation, not a literal transcription.

- **Field.** The statement covers nonarchimedean local fields, of mixed and
  equal characteristic. Their valuation rings are Henselian and their
  valuations discrete. Other fields allowed by KP Chapter 7 are not covered.
- **The building.** KP Chapter 7 builds `B(G)` from the Iwahori–Tits system
  of Theorem 7.5.3 (Definition 7.6.1). Remark 7.6.5, printed p.266 / PDF
  p.297, realizes it as the quotient (7.6.2) of `G(k) × A`. Here the building
  is the Library's quotient `P.toValuationBuildingData.ReducedBuilding`, with
  its existing action of `G(F)`, and the apartment is the image of `A(S)`.
- **Hypotheses.** The premise is the Library predicate
  `SatisfiesApartmentAxioms`. It contains AS 5, which the corrected Chapter 7
  assumes, and AS 2–AS 4, which KP prove in §6.3 for every valuation. It
  also contains V 7, which is not among the standing hypotheses of Chapter 7.
  The statement is therefore weaker than KP's by this extra premise. V 6 is
  part of the data `P`; the opening of Chapter 7 does not list it. The
  premise matches that of the published Chapter 7 statements
  `ParahoricBuildingData.FacetFixerConstancyStatement` and
  `AffineIwahoriTitsStatement`.
- **One apartment.** KP's Axiom 4.1.8 concerns every maximal split torus `S`
  and its apartment. The Library predicates concern the torus and apartment of
  the data `P`. The statement quantifies over all `P`, so it covers every
  maximal split torus for which data are supplied.
- **Bounded subsets.** Both parts are stated for non-empty bounded `Ω`, as in
  the corrections and in KP's definition of `G(k)^0_Ω` before Axiom 4.1.8.

No existence of the data, and no other axiom, is asserted. Together with the
published `RootValuationBuildingConstructionStatement`, whose conclusion
includes `P.SatisfiesApartmentAxioms`, this statement would supply Axiom 4.1.8
for the data of that construction. It is one of the certificate items of the
published `BruhatTitsAvailabilityStatement` (KP Theorem 9.2.7 with corrected
Definition 4.1.26).

## Expected proof route (not prescribed)

The route of KP is as follows.

1. At a point `x ∈ A(S)`: the stabilizer of the image of `x` in `B(G)` is
   `G(F)^♯_x · N(F)_x`, where `N(F)_x` is the stabilizer of `x` in
   `N(F)`. This is the quotient's stabilizer formula
   (`ApartmentQuotient.mem_stabilizer_apartment_iff`, used in
   `ValuationBuildingData.compactStabilizer_apartmentMap_eq_mul`). Since
   `G(F)^♯_x ⊆ G(F)^0`, the pointwise fixer of `x` in `G(F)^0` is
   `G(F)^♯_x · (N(F)_x ∩ G(F)^0)`. KP Lemma 7.4.7 and Theorem 7.5.3 (the map
   `N(F) ∩ G(F)^0 → W^aff` has kernel `Z(F)^0`) give
   `N(F)_x ∩ G(F)^0 ⊆ P_x`, so the fixer is `P_x`.
2. Lemma 7.7.4 for `* = 0`: `P_Ω = ⋂_{x ∈ Ω} P_x`. Its proof uses the
   decompositions of Proposition 7.3.12, the Bruhat decomposition and
   Proposition 2.11.3 (2).
3. Lemma 7.4.4 (3) identifies `P_Ω = P.parahoric Ω` with
   `P.nonnegativeRootSubgroup Ω`. The published
   `nonnegativeRootSubgroup_le_parahoric` gives one inclusion. For a
   multipliable root the other inclusion uses the groups `U_ψ` of the
   divisible root `2a`.
4. Part (3) follows from Lemma 7.7.3 and the identification of step 1.

Library material that may help: the Chapter 7 results on `P.parahoric`
(module `KPParahoric`), the published proofs `chamberParahoricNormalizer` of
`ChamberParahoricNormalizerStatement` (`P_C ∩ N(F) ⊆ Z(F)^0`),
`rootGroupParahoricFiltration` of `RootGroupParahoricFiltrationStatement`
(`U_a(F) ∩ P_x ⊆ U_{a,x,0}`), and `ParahoricBuildingData.facetFixerConstancy`
(KP Lemma 7.4.4 (1) on facets).
