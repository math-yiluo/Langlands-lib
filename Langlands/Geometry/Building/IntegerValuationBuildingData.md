# Building data for an explicit integer-valued valuation

Worker 27 defines `IntegerValuationBuildingData v G` for a field `k : Type u`, an actual valuation `v : Valuation k (WithZero (Multiplicative ℤ))` and an affine group scheme `G`. The structure supplies connected reductivity, a maximal split torus `i`, the existing `KPApartmentData i (IntegerValuation.order v)`, the existing actual `CentralizerDatum i`, and full centralizer V6 on that same equipollence class. Restriction-evaluation bijectivity is supplied within the centralizer datum. This is a type of data without a claim of inhabitance. No topology, Henselian property, value surjectivity or residue-field hypothesis is needed for this packaging.

The original setting is Kaletha–Prasad (2023), Chapter 7 opening, printed pp.235–236/PDF pp.266–267: a Henselian discretely valued field, a connected reductive group, and supplied compatible root-datum apartments. The authors' 1 November 2024 corrections, PDF p.33, additionally require AS5 for the chapter's construction theorems. This algebraic interface deliberately provides fewer conditions and proves none of those construction theorems. It reuses the already published quotient and its rational-point action. Its sources for positive character valuation, negative translation and full V6 are Notation 2.6.15, Axiom 4.1.4(A1), Definition 6.1.2(V6) and Proposition 6.2.4, with the corrected positive sign on corrections PDF p.25, as implemented in `IntegerCentralizer`.

`Apartment` and `ReducedBuilding` are aliases for the very apartment and quotient of the supplied `B.apartment`; `apartmentMap`, the normalizer and rational-point actions, actual root groups, filtrations and positive-depth filtrations, affine roots, root fixers and full fixers come from those same data. No second quotient is introduced. Facets, chambers, vertices and special points use the existing affine-root predicates and translated apartment maps, just as the published local bundle does.

`oneStabilizer y` is exactly `characterValuationKernel v G ⊓ B.apartment.reducedFixer y`. Its membership is the conjunction of membership in the character kernel and the equation `g • y = y`. V6 gives the negative centralizer translation action through the existing normalizer action. The actual image of the centralizer's character kernel lies in `oneStabilizer (apartmentMap x)`: restriction of characters proves kernel membership, and the proved trivial kernel action proves fixing. These proofs use the intrinsic laws in `IntegerCentralizer` and the existing apartment-map equivariance.

This module generalizes the supplied-data packaging of `ValuationBuildingData`, without changing its field-only algebraic objects. The separate local comparison module discharges compatibility and identifies these objects with that local wrapper. No compactness, parahoric equality, full building existence, AS5, V7, integral-model existence or descent is asserted.
