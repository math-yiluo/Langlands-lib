# Torus covariance and V0 for actual restricted scalar root functions

This internal prerequisite proves the exact split-torus covariance of the
canonical proper-torus filtration and of its constructed scalar family. With a
nontrivial additive unit character, it also proves V0. All points and products
belong to the original ambient group `rationalPoints G`.

Let `k : Type u` be an arbitrary field, let `S` and `T` be commutative group
schemes over `k`, and let `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G`. Write `j = groupHom f ≫ i` for a
homomorphism `f : S ⟶ T`. Let `ω : kˣ →* Multiplicative ℝ`, so
`b ↦ (ω b).toAdd` is the additive unit character used by split-torus
compatibility.

## Actual conjugation and shifted filtrations

`torusRestrictionConjugation f s` is the automorphism given by inverse
conjugation by the actual point `j(s)`: it sends `g` to `j(s)⁻¹ * g * j(s)`.
Its definition and inverse-cancellation theorem require only the field and the
specified group schemes and maps.

For an upper character `χ` with `f ≫ χ = m • α`, the evaluation theorem
`rationalCharacterEval_torusRestriction_eq_pow` identifies `χ(f(s))` with
`α(s)^m` for every natural `m`, including zero. The theorem
`unitCharacterValue_torusRestriction_eq_mul` consequently identifies their
real values by the multiplier `m`.

For the filtration results assume `G` is affine, `i` is monomorphic, and
`hφ : IsRootDatumValuation i φ` is an existing upper valuation. Retain upper
`IsSplitTorusCompatible i (fun b => (ω b).toAdd) φ`. Put

`H(α,r) = torusRestrictedRootDepthSubgroup hφ f α r`

and `c = (ω (α(s))).toAdd`, with character evaluation interpreted by the actual
rational-character/lattice equivalence. The subgroup containment theorem proves

`H(α,r) ≤ comap (torusRestrictionConjugation f s) H(α,r-c)`.

The membership theorem proves the exact equivalence

`torusRestrictionConjugation f s g ∈ H(α,r) ↔ g ∈ H(α,r+c)`.

These results hold for every character `α` and every real `r`. They need no
smoothness, splitness, opposite factor pairs, monomorphism of `f`, or lower
valuation. In particular they use no lower-root coordinate decomposition.

## Scalar compatibility and V0

For the scalar-family results additionally assume `G` is smooth, both tori are
split, and `hE : RelativeRoot.HasOppositeFactorPairs i`. Write

`ψ = torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily hS hT hE hφ f`.

`torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily_isSplitTorusCompatible` proves
`IsSplitTorusCompatible j (fun b => (ω b).toAdd) ψ`. Thus for each actual lower
root `α`, each `s ∈ S(k)` and each actual `g ∈ rootGroupPoints j α`,

`ψ α (j(s)⁻¹ * g * j(s)) = ψ α g + ↑(-c)`.

The right-hand side uses the existing addition on `WithTop ℝ`; the statement
also holds at the identity, whose value is top. No lower monomorphism or
maximality premise is added.

If `∃ b, (ω b).toAdd ≠ 0`, the theorem ending in `image_infinite` proves that
the image of `ψ α` on every actual lower root group is infinite. The theorem
ending in `three_le_encard` proves the V0 bound `3 ≤ encard` of that image.
Nontriviality is an explicit hypothesis of these two conclusions.

## Implemented proof and provenance

This is a source-free internal derivation from the actual Library interfaces.
Evaluation commutes with restriction and sends sums of rational characters to
products of their values. Induction gives the power formula; applying the
monoid homomorphism `ω` gives its real multiplier form.

The canonical subgroup is generated by upper factors at depth `m*r` for positive
restriction multipliers `m`. Upper torus stability keeps each generator in its
upper root group, and upper scalar compatibility changes its value by `-m*c`.
The elementary equality `m*(r-c) = m*r-m*c` places the conjugated generator in
the factor at shifted depth. Taking the subgroup supremum gives containment.
A general group lemma applies this containment at `s` and `s⁻¹`, uses
`c(s⁻¹) = -c(s)`, and cancels the conjugations to give the exact equivalence.
Abstracting this last step avoids expensive kernel unfolding of the scheme
objects and requires no increased heartbeat limit.

Actual lower root-group stability and the scalar family's exact finite level
sets turn the membership equivalence into equality of all real lower bounds.
For a value `v : WithTop ℝ`, splitting into top and real cases proves
`↑r ≤ v + ↑(-c) ↔ ↑(r+c) ≤ v`. The existing real-bound extensionality lemma then
gives scalar compatibility.

Finally `RootFunctionNontriviality` applies to the constructed compatibility and
the scalar family's exact top-value criterion. Its implemented argument chooses
an actual nonidentity root point, a positive-pairing split cocharacter, and a
unit of nonzero additive value. Integer powers of the corresponding torus point
produce pairwise distinct root values. Infinitude gives the V0 bound.

The lower normalizer/reflection, commutator, double-root and opposite valuation
laws remain open. An arbitrary proper subtorus need not itself supply a root
datum, so these hypotheses do not assert a full lower `IsRootDatumValuation`.
This unit supplies no initial valuation, metric building, supplied-model special
vertex, or unique-fixation comparison. The unchanged
`BuildingComparisonStatement` remains unproved.
