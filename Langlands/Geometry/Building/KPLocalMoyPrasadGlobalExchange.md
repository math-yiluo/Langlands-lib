# Global constant-depth exchange over the maximal unramified field

This internal helper adapts the published `MoyPrasadGlobalExchange` proof to
`MaximalUnramified.BuildingData F G`, without assuming apartment axioms or that
`UnramifiedClosure F` is a local field. It uses the integer-valued block
subgroups, whose order-one-unit witness is `exists_order_eq_one F`.

The subgroup `depthRootSpan` is generated by the original root filtrations.
The auxiliary set `depthExchangeSet` multiplies its negative span, positive
span and an arbitrary subgroup. During a crossing of a single root ray,
valuation commutator axiom V3 normalizes the common positive span. The
crossed directions form an ordinary/doubled root block by AS2 and the
derivative-root equality, supplied explicitly to the helper. A local
opposite-block exchange inclusion then preserves the auxiliary set.

A finite crossing chain between a real generic vector and its negative is
constructed using a second vector separating crossing parameters for
nonproportional roots. Equality across that chain makes the auxiliary set
stable under multiplication by every original root generator. Applying the
stabilizer subgroup to the identity gives
`sharpFiltration_subset_depthExchangeSet`.

These are derived algebraic helpers. Their mathematical route is the
constant-depth sign exchange of KP Lemma 7.3.11 and Proposition 7.3.12;
the finite real-vector chain is the implemented proof, inherited from
`MoyPrasadGlobalExchange`, rather than an additional geometric assumption.
