# Valuation axioms on actual field restriction, with V3 retained

Let k and K be fields in universe u with an arbitrary algebra inclusion. Let
S be an actual split torus, G a smooth affine group over k, and i a supplied
homomorphism whose underlying scheme map is a monomorphism. Write S_K, G_K
and i_K for their actual scheme pullbacks. Suppose psi is a root-datum
valuation for this same pulled-back torus i_K. This unit concerns the literal
fieldRestrict family, evaluated on the pulled-back character and the actual
rationalExtend of each original rational point.

The unit proves normalization and axioms V0, V1, V2, V4 and V5 for that family.
For V0, assume upstairs split-torus compatibility with a homomorphism omega
from K units to the additive real numbers, and that this homomorphism is
nontrivial on the image of k units. The split-torus compatibility formula
itself descends with omega restricted along the actual field-unit inclusion.
Neither V0 downstairs nor any other downstream valuation axiom except V3 is
supplied separately.

The selected constructor
IsRootDatumValuation.fieldRestrictValuationOfCommutator returns a bundled
root-datum valuation whose family is definitionally the existing fieldRestrict.
Its hcomm argument is exactly the existing V3 commutator condition on that
restricted family, including closure of the union of the prescribed positive
root filtrations. This condition is retained as an explicit premise. The unit
does not prove that membership in an upstairs generated subgroup descends to
membership in the subgroup generated by downstairs points.

The implementation uses the following published constructions and comparisons.
Actual root-support base change transfers root membership, and actual
root-group membership reflection gives normalization off the root group.
The rationalExtend map is injective on affine rational points, by the existing
coefficient comparison and coordinate-extension injectivity. This gives the
exact identity criterion. Each nonzero-root filtration is the literal preimage
of its upstairs filtration, so it retains the identity, multiplication and
inversion, proving V1.

The actual extended normalizer point realizes any reflection realized by the
original point. Character base change preserves the reflected character, and
the split real cocharacter equivalence is surjective and intertwines the
normalizer action. Thus the hyperplane is fixed on the entire upstairs real
space. This transfers V2 and its real reflection constant. Character pullback
preserves negation and natural multiples, and rationalExtend preserves products
and normalizer membership. These transfer V4 and V5 using the original root
points and opposite factors.

For V0, actual split-torus compatibility descends because rationalExtend
commutes with i and character evaluation extends the original field unit.
The descended identity criterion and the RootFunctionNontriviality constructor
then embed the integers into the actual downstairs root-value image. In
particular the image is infinite and satisfies the required bound of three.
The final constructor assembles these derived fields with precisely hcomm.

The provenance is internal composition of published actual base-change,
root-subgroup, character, normalizer and torus-conjugation constructions. No new
assertion from original literature is added. No finiteness, separability,
Galois or valuation-preservation hypothesis on K over k is imposed. The
upstairs valuation and compatibility remain inputs, the torus is the pullback
of S rather than a newly enlarged maximal split torus, and V3 remains open.
This constructs no apartment, metric, model comparison or special vertex and
does not prove BuildingComparisonStatement.
