# The centralizer filtration and its Moy–Prasad groups

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field of either characteristic and
`K = UnramifiedClosure F = inertiaFixedField F`. Let `P : ParahoricBuildingData G`
have maximal split torus `S` and centralizer `Z = Z_G(S)`. The construction
uses the actual centralizer supplied by `P`.

## Point of the centralizer building

`P.centralizerParahoricBuilding` constructs building data for `Z`.
The factor of `S → G` through `Z` is central and maximal split in `Z`.
All relative roots of `Z` for this split torus are therefore absent.
The root-free apartment constructor gives the singleton valuation class,
with representative `P.centralizerBuildingPoint`. The whole group `Z(F)`
normalizes `S`. It fixes the image of this point by the root-free fixer
formula. The image exhausts the reduced building since the building is
the union of rational translates of the apartment. This gives
`centralizerReducedBuilding_eq`, the point used in KP §9.8.

`P.centralizerUnramifiedApartmentLift` chooses a special torus of `Z`
and its actual apartment lift using `exists_unramifiedApartmentLift`,
`specialTorusExistence` and `unramifiedValuationDescent`. This gives the
lifted point and the normalized root valuations of `Z_K` there. The
centralizer of the upstairs maximal split torus is a torus by the
quasi-splitness over `K`, as encoded by `ApartmentLift.centralizer_isTorus`.

## Subgroups and packaging

- `minimalCongruentCentralizerPointsOfLift` applies the generator
  construction `BuildingData.minimalCongruentMoyPrasad` to `Z_K` at the
  lifted point and takes the inverse image under `rationalExtend F K Z`.
  This is `Z(K)_{x,r} ∩ Z(F)`, on `rationalPoints Z`. The canonical
  `P.minimalCongruentCentralizerPoints` uses the chosen lift. Both definitions
  give the subgroup at every finite nonnegative real depth without any
  premise about the resulting family.
- `minimalCongruentCentralizerPointsOfLift_zero` and
  `minimalCongruentCentralizerPoints_zero` identify the zero term with
  `P.centralizerZero`. The proof rewrites the upstairs minimal congruent
  zero term to the standard zero term, then applies the published
  `unramifiedComparisonZeroField` for the group `Z`. The split-torus
  centralizer in `Z` is represented by the identity on `Z`, so its extra
  inverse image is the identity. The zero datum for this centralizer was
  taken from `P.zeroDatum.centralizerDatum`, giving exactly the required
  `Z(F)^0`.
- `minimalCongruentCentralizerFiltration` puts this actual family in
  `P.CentralizerFiltration`. Its explicit proof argument
  `P.MinimalCongruentCentralizerOrder` asks that the family be decreasing
  and have trivial intersection. These two order properties are
  obligations on the constructed subgroups. The zero field uses the
  proved equality above.
- `P.minimalCongruentMoyPrasad h x r` and
  `P.minimalCongruentMoyPrasadPlus h x r` use `moyPrasad` and `moyPrasadPlus`
  of that `CentralizerFiltration`. The first is generated by `Z(F)_r` and
  the relative root blocks at the actual apartment point `x`. The second
  is the supremum over all strictly greater real depths, which form a
  directed family by antitonicity. This gives the union prescribed in
  KP §1.6. The groups are indexed by points, as in Remark 13.2.2.
