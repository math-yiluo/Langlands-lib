# Normalizer transport of Galois-fixed points from Levi transport

Author: Worker 4356.

Lean module: `Langlands.Geometry.Building.UnramifiedFixedNormalizerTransportReduction`.

This module proves that the statement `UnramifiedFixedLeviTransportStatement` (module of
the same name) implies clause 4 of the published `UnramifiedValuationDescentStatement`.
Clause 4 is the `N_G(S)(F)`-equivariance of KP Lemma 9.6.3 and Theorem 9.6.5. The
reduction itself is elementary: it uses only the axioms of valuations of root data and the
published restriction and field-descent results.

## The general transport lemma (section `General`)

Let `K/k` be any field extension, `G` affine and smooth over `k`, `iS : S → G` a
monomorphism, `iT : T → G`, and `f : S ⟶ T` with `groupHom f ≫ iT = iS`. Suppose `iT_K`
is a monomorphism, `S` is split, `T_K` is split, and `hE` gives opposite factor pairs for
`iT_K`. Let `D : KPApartmentData iT_K ω` for any `ω : Kˣ → ℝ`.
Three private instances supply the affine and smooth instances of `G_K` and the
monomorphism instance of `iS_K`, by pullback.

- `KPApartmentData.leviTransport_apartmentMap_torusFieldRestricted`: let
  `n ∈ N_{iS}(k)` and `x : D.Apartment`. Let `h ∈ G(K)` centralize `S_K` and lie in the
  subgroup generated by the groups `D.filtration x χ 0` over the roots `χ` of `T_K` with
  `f_K ≫ χ = 0`, and let `hw` say that `w = n_K * h` normalizes `T_K`. Then
  1. `D.apartmentMap (w • x) = n_K • D.apartmentMap x` in the reduced building of `G_K`;
  2. `torusFieldRestrictedRootFunctionFamily … (w • x) f_K =
     n • torusFieldRestrictedRootFunctionFamily … x f_K`.

  Proof. Each generator `D.filtration x χ 0` equals the root fixer `D.rootFixer χ {x}`
  (`rootFixer_singleton`). It lies in the sharp fixer `D.sharpFixer {x}`
  (`rootFixer_le_sharpFixer`), so `h ∈ D.sharpFixer {x}`.
  1. By `apartmentMap_smul`, `D.apartmentMap (w • x) = (n_K h) • D.apartmentMap x`. The
     sharp fixer of `x` lies in the reduced fixer (`sharpFixer_le_reducedFixer`), so `h`
     fixes `D.apartmentMap x`, and the result is `n_K • D.apartmentMap x`.
  2. The torus-then-field restriction is the field restriction of the torus restriction.
     The restriction of `f_K ≫ iT_K` is `iS_K`, by functoriality of base change. The
     theorem `torusRestrictedRootFunctionFamily_smul_of_eq_mul` (module
     `RootValueTorusLeviTransport`) applies over `K` with the normalizer point
     `normalizerPointsFieldExtend iS n` of `iS_K` (module
     `SplitTorusNormalizerBaseChange`), whose underlying point is `n_K`, and with `w`,
     `h` and `w = n_K h`. It says that the torus restriction of `w • x` is
     `n_K • (torus restriction of x)`. The published
     `RootFunctionFamily.fieldRestrict_normalizer` (module `RootFunctionFieldEquivariance`)
     says that field restriction carries `n_K • ψ` to `n • (field restriction of ψ)`.

## Clause 4 (section `Unramified`)

- `unramifiedFixedNormalizerTransport_of_leviTransport`: assume
  `hQ : UnramifiedFixedLeviTransportStatement.{u}`. Take the binders of
  `UnramifiedValuationDescentStatement`, with the instances
  `IsAffineHom G.X.hom`, `Smooth G.X.hom`, `Mono iS.hom.hom.left` and
  `Mono iT_K.hom.hom.left` as instance arguments. Then for every `n : normalizerPoints iS`
  and every `x : D.Apartment` fixed by `RootFunctionFamily.galoisAction F K T G σ` for
  all `σ : K ≃ₐ[F] K`, there is a fixed `y : D.Apartment` with
  `D.apartmentMap y = n_K • D.apartmentMap x` and `φ y = n • φ x`. Here `φ` is the
  torus-then-field restriction of the target, with the split-torus proofs
  `hiS.isSplitSubtorus.1` and `hiT.isSplitSubtorus.1`.

  Proof. `hQ` applied to the same data gives `h`, the membership conditions and `hw`, and
  the fixedness of `y = ⟨n_K h, hw⟩ • x`. The general lemma with `k = F` and
  `K = inertiaFixedField F` gives the two equations. The instance arguments are the
  instances the target creates by `letI`/`haveI`, so this theorem discharges clause 4 of
  the target. The elaboration needs `maxHeartbeats 1600000`.

## Provenance and scope

KP prove the equivariance from the action of `N(F)` on `B(G_K)^{SΓ}` (§9.3.14 and the proof
of Proposition 9.3.16). This module isolates the building-theoretic content in
`UnramifiedFixedLeviTransportStatement`. The rest of the argument is original to this
module. It is the group-theoretic Levi transport in `RootValueTorusLeviTransport`,
together with the facts that root fixers lie in the sharp fixer and the sharp fixer in the
reduced fixer.

Clause 4 is not proved unconditionally here. The theorem assumes
`UnramifiedFixedLeviTransportStatement`, which is not proved in the Library.
