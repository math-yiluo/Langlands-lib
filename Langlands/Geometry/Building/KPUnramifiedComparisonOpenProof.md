# Openness for concrete unramified apartment lifts

The theorem `Langlands.Geometry.Building.unramifiedComparisonOpenField.{u}`
proves exactly the published
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.UnramifiedComparisonOpenFieldStatement.{u}`.
It applies to every supplied lift, every point of the downstairs apartment,
and every nonnegative real depth, including zero. It has no additional
premise. The statement and all its imported pairs are unchanged.

For a supplied lift `L`, use its actual centralizer torus and its actual
upstairs Moy–Prasad subgroup. The theorem
`isOpen_localTorusFiltration` proves openness of the torus term in
`rationalPointTopology (UnramifiedClosure F)`, with `fixedFieldTopology F`
on the maximal unramified field. At depth zero this is the norm Iwahori:
the split-character valuation kernel is open, its transport to the Weil
restriction is open by continuity, and its norm image is open by smoothness
and the Henselian smooth open-map theorem. At positive depth, intersect
this subgroup with the geometric-character congruence, whose openness
follows from finite generation of the character lattice and the
logarithmic valuation congruence theorem.

Next apply
`MaximalUnramified.BuildingData.isOpen_moyPrasad_of_isOpen_torus`.
Its root-filtration input is unconditional over the actual maximal
unramified field: `isOpen_rootFiltration` combines character-kernel
openness with the integer-valued building-data openness theorem. That
proof uses opposite-cell coordinates and reflection identities to prove
local constancy away from the identity, then unbounded root values to
find an interior point at every finite depth. The published
`RelativeRoot.topologicalRootCentralizerGeneration`, together with
`moyPrasad_eq_sup_iSup`, gives openness of the subgroup generated by the
actual torus and root terms. This applies also at depth zero.

Finally apply
`ParahoricBuildingData.isOpen_unramifiedCentralizerComap` to the open
upstairs subgroup. Its continuous homomorphism is the actual composition
of `rationalPoints.map P.centralizer.j` and
`rationalExtend F (UnramifiedClosure F) G`. The centralizer inclusion is
continuous by `continuous_rationalPointMap`, and rational extension is
continuous by `unramifiedRationalExtensionContinuousHom`. The source
is exactly `rationalPointTopology F P.centralizer.Z.X`; the codomain
uses the fixed-field rational-point topology just used for upstairs
openness. Continuous preimage is therefore precisely the double comap
required by the target. Affineness of the centralizer is deduced from
its supplied closed immersion, and affineness after extension is inherited
by base change.

This is an internal assembly of the named Library constructions and
proved topology lemmas. It proves openness for every supplied lift and
requires no construction of a lift, integral model, or extra comparison
field.
