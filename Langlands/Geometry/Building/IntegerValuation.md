# Integer-valued valuation adapters on actual rational characters

Worker 27 constructs algebraic adapters for an arbitrary field `k : Type u` and an actual valuation `v : Valuation k (WithZero (Multiplicative ℤ))`. No topology, Henselian property, value-surjectivity or residue-field condition is assumed. The field and actual rational-character evaluations are unchanged.

The map `orderUnits v` restricts `v` to field units, uses the canonical `WithZero.unitsWithZeroEquiv`, and takes the inverse. Thus its additive value is `-WithZero.log (v a)`. This sign converts Mathlib's multiplicative valuation convention into the source-positive additive order. `order v` is its real cast. The integer `rationalCharacterValuation` composes the actual `rationalCharacterEvalHom` with this order; `realCharacterValuation` casts its coordinates into the real additive dual. Their evaluation and character-restriction formulas are proved directly, and both kernels equal the existing `characterValuationKernel v G`. The latter kernel is functorial under actual group-scheme homomorphisms.

The original source is Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023), Notation 2.6.15 and Lemma 2.6.16(1),(2), printed pp.102–103, PDF pp.133–134, source `kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`. The authors' 1 November 2024 corrections, PDF p.25, source `kaletha-prasad-2024-corrections`, replace the printed sign in (2.6.3). We implement the corrected positive formula `χ(ω_G(g)) = ω(χ(g))`. These formal algebraic composites need fewer hypotheses than the source's structural assertions about reductive groups. No claim about boundedness, building existence or centralizer restriction-evaluation bijectivity follows from this construction.

For every nonarchimedean local field `F`, `orderUnits_normalized` identifies this order with the existing `Plancherel.fieldOrderUnits F` by comparing their proved logarithm formulas. The integer character map consequently equals `Plancherel.rationalCharacterValuation`; the real character map equals `Plancherel.rationalNormLogarithm` using its proved integer-coordinate formula. The kernel equals `Plancherel.rationalCommonNormKernel`. These comparisons assume no compatibility premise and apply in arbitrary universe and either characteristic. The local comparison of real unit orders with `Building.localFieldOrder` belongs to the building adapter unit.
