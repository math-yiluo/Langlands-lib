# Integer-valued centralizer valuation and full V6 adapters

Worker 27 extends the integer-valuation character adapter over an arbitrary field `k : Type u`. The actual valuation `v : Valuation k (WithZero (Multiplicative ℤ))` needs no topology, Henselian property, surjectivity or residue-field hypothesis. The published field-only `CentralizerDatum i`, `Cartan.restrictionEvaluation` and `centralizerToNormalizer` are reused.

`centralizerValuation v s h` transports the real character valuation through the inverse of the actual restriction-evaluation linear equivalence. Its bijectivity `h` remains supplied data. The evaluation formula is the source-positive field order of the actual character value. Its kernel is exactly `characterValuationKernel v Z`. `centralizerTranslation` is its negative and has the same kernel. The projection of this vector into the existing `TranslationSpace i` is the apartment translation.

The source formulas are Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023), Notation 2.6.15 and Lemma 2.6.16, printed pp.102–103/PDF pp.133–134; Axiom 4.1.4(A1) and Remark 4.1.5, printed p.159/PDF p.190; Definition 6.1.2(V6), printed p.199/PDF p.230; and Propositions 6.2.3–6.2.4, printed pp.214–215/PDF pp.245–246. The authors' 1 November 2024 corrections, PDF p.25, correct the character valuation to the positive formula. The apartment translation retains the negative sign of A1. These algebraic adapters need fewer field hypotheses than the source's construction theorems.

`IsCentralizerCompatible` quantifies over all actual centralizer points and all elements of each actual root group, imposing `φ_a(j(z)⁻¹ u j(z)) = φ_a(u) + a(ν(z))`. `IsOmegaCompatible v D C` imposes it on the very equipollence class `D : KPApartmentData i (order v)`, with the actual centralizer `C`. V6 is preserved by translation, so it suffices to check any one member. The action proof uses the existing normalizer formula and trivial Weyl action of centralizer points; outside a root group the valuation is infinity on both sides. It follows that the existing apartment action is translation by the class of `ν(z)`. The character kernel therefore acts trivially and its image lies in every full fixer of an apartment point. These are intrinsic conditional laws, not existence proofs.

For the normalized valuation on a nonarchimedean local field, `order_normalized` identifies the exact real order with `Building.localFieldOrder`. The constructed centralizer valuation and translation equal `Cartan.centralizerValuation` and `Building.centralizerTranslation` by the previously proved equality of real character maps. The family V6 predicates are equivalent by that translation equality. No comparison is assumed as a premise. The bundle-level transported comparison is provided in the building-data unit.

No general restriction-evaluation bijectivity, compatible-apartment existence, AS5, V7, compactness, parahoric identification, integral-model existence or descent is proved here.
