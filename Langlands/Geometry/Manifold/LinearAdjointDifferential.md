# Differentiating the adjoint action through a linear representation

Let a smooth real Lie group act through a smooth monoid homomorphism into a normed real algebra. The derivative `LinearAdjoint.tangent` of the homomorphism at the identity carries the Lie bracket to the commutator. The proof tests the derivative against continuous scalar functionals. Differentiating twice along invariant vector fields gives the two orders of multiplication, and their difference is the derivative in the bracket direction by `groupDirectionalValue_commutator_of_vectorFieldCommutator`.

The chain rule sends tangent conjugation to conjugation of the realized tangent vector. A continuous linear left inverse therefore expresses the original tangent action by the product of the realized group element, the realized tangent, and the inverse group element. At the identity, the derivative of the realized inverse is the negative tangent, by differentiating the product with the original group element. The product rule gives a commutator, which the left inverse sends to the Lie bracket. These identities are the differential calculation underlying adjoint invariance of central enveloping elements.
