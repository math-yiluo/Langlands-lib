# Locally uniform limits of invariant derivatives

The theorem treats a family of smooth functions equipped with an index transition for differentiation in each invariant direction. It assumes locally uniform convergence of all members of the family. The coordinate derivative expansion and `invariantCoordinateDerivative` give locally uniform convergence of ordinary derivatives on every chart. Passing to the limit there and inducting on the differentiability order proves smoothness of the limit functions.

The proof recovers every limiting invariant derivative by pulling back through the chart at the identity and then translating to the desired point. The derivative of the inverse chart at its base point identifies the model direction with the original tangent direction. This is a derived smoothness criterion for limits in the uniform moderate growth topology.
