# Locally uniform limits of first invariant derivatives

`groupDirectionalValue_of_tendstoLocallyUniformly` identifies the limit
of the first invariant derivatives with the derivative of the smooth
limit. On an identity chart translated to the evaluation point, the
coordinate derivative is a finite sum of the first invariant derivatives
with smooth coefficients. The locally uniform derivative limit theorem
then gives the coordinate derivative of the limit. Evaluation at the
identity chart gives the actual invariant derivative.

This is a first-order variant of the internal
`SmoothGroupUniformLimit` argument. It allows the derivative of a family
of series to be compared on a common convergence region using bounds for
the first derivatives.
