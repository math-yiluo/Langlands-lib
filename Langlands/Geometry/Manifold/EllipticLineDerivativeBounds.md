# Compact bounds on the translated curves of elliptic solutions

The scalar interior elliptic estimate gives all derivatives in the Euclidean chart at the identity. The right-hand norm is bounded by a supremum on the image of its closed ball. For a point on a translated one-parameter curve, move the derivative to the identity by translating the solution. Conjugation by the point of the starting compact changes the curve used in the identity chart. A smooth cutoff extends the chart map near the identity, and the compact restriction theorem bounds each derivative of the resulting conjugated curves uniformly. The higher-order chain rule combines these constants with the elliptic bounds. All derivative orders use the same product of the compact parameter interval image, the starting compact and the elliptic ball image.
