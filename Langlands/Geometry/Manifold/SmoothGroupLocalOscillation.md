# Local oscillation from invariant derivatives

`SmoothGroupLocalOscillationStatement` chooses a compact set near identity and finitely
many invariant directions on a finite-dimensional real Lie group. A common bound for
the corresponding first derivatives on the compact set gives an arbitrarily small
oscillation from the identity on a sufficiently small open neighborhood, with the same
multiplicative size factor.

The proof takes a closed ball inside the identity chart. The invariant-derivative
expansion gives uniform bounds for the coordinate derivative there because its finitely
many coefficients are continuous. The mean-value inequality on the ball gives the
oscillation estimate. The neighborhood depends on the derivative bound and desired
oscillation factor, so it applies uniformly to families of smooth functions.
