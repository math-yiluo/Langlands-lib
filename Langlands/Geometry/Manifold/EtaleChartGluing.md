# Analytic transitions through a common refinement

This internal lemma supports gluing the analytic point charts of Conrad's Proposition 5.4. It concerns two open partial homeomorphisms from charted spaces to a topological space, agreeing on their sources with maps from the charted spaces. A common refinement supplies maps into both sources whose images agree in the topological space. If its first projection is a local analytic diffeomorphism and its second is analytic at the specified point, the change from the first branch to the second is analytic there.

The proof composes the second projection with the analytic local inverse of the first. Continuity keeps its values in the second branch's source. The commuting square and the inverse identity then identify this analytic composite with the topological branch transition on a neighborhood.
