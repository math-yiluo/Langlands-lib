# Statement: points of the affine analytification as multiplicative seminorms

`AffineAnalytificationSeminormStatement` compares the Library's
polynomial-locus affine analytification `Space K P` with Berkovich's
description of `Spec(A)^an` as a space of multiplicative seminorms. It states
that:

1. the map `x ↦ (a ↦ |a|_x)` from `Space K P` to the product space `A → ℝ` is a
   topological embedding;
2. the seminorms of points are exactly the multiplicative seminorms on `A`
   that extend the norm of `K`.

Here `|a|_x` is `pointSeminorm K P x a`. This is a statement only; its proof is
a separate successor.

Remark 3.4.2 identifies the analytification with a set, and Definition 1.5.1
gives the topology for affine space. For `Spec A`, the claim that the topology
of the glued space is the weakest one making every `|a|` continuous is the
corresponding description. It is part of this derived formulation for the
Library's model. Here it is expressed as an embedding into `A → ℝ`.

## Hypotheses

The field `K` is nontrivially normed, ultrametric and complete. `A` is any
commutative `K`-algebra with any finite polynomial presentation
`P : FinitePolynomialPresentation K A`. A finitely generated algebra has such
a presentation. No reducedness or nonemptiness hypothesis is imposed.

## The claims

- `IsEmbedding (fun (x : Space K P) (a : A) => pointSeminorm K P x a)`, where
  `A → ℝ` has the product topology. Mathlib's `IsEmbedding` means injective and
  inducing. So distinct points have distinct seminorms, and a subset of
  `Space K P` is open exactly when it is the preimage of an open subset of the
  product. In other words, the topology of `Space K P` is the weakest topology
  making every `x ↦ |a|_x` continuous.
- `range (pointSeminorm K P) = {ν : MulRingSeminorm A | ExtendsNorm K ν}`.
  `ExtendsNorm K ν` means `ν (algebraMap K A c) = ‖c‖` for every `c : K`.

`pointSeminorm` and `ExtendsNorm` come from `AffineAnalytificationSeminorm`.
On the `m`-th chart, `pointSeminorm` evaluates a spectrum point of the bounded
polynomial-locus algebra `algebra K P m` at the image of `a`.

## Why the claims are expected to hold

This is a truth check, not the proof.

- *Injectivity.* Two points lie in a common chart. The image of `A` is dense in
  the chart algebra, because polynomials are dense in the Tate algebra and the
  quotient map is continuous and surjective. A spectrum point is bounded by the
  norm, hence continuous. So two chart points that agree on `A` are equal.
- *Image.* Every point's seminorm extends the norm (proved in
  `AffineAnalytificationSeminorm`). Conversely, let `ν` extend the norm of `K`.
  Then `ν(n·1) ≤ 1` for every integer `n`, and the binomial estimate makes `ν`
  ultrametric. Choose `m` with `ν(xᵢ) ≤ radius K m` for the generators `xᵢ`
  of `P`; the radii are unbounded. On polynomials, `p ↦ ν(p(x))` is then
  bounded by the weighted Gauss norm. It therefore extends continuously to a
  multiplicative seminorm on the Tate algebra. This extension vanishes on the
  closed ideal generated by the relations, so it descends to a point of the
  `m`-th chart, bounded by the quotient norm.
- *Topology.* On each compact chart, the map to `A → ℝ` is a continuous
  injection into a Hausdorff space, hence an embedding. The open polydisc
  `openDisc K P m` is the preimage of the open set of functions `f` with
  `f(xᵢ) < radius K (m + 1)` for every `i`, since the `i`-th chart
  coordinate is the image of `xᵢ`. It lies in the image of the `(m+1)`-st
  chart, and these discs cover `Space K P`. So every open subset of
  `Space K P` is a union of preimages of open sets.

## Scope

The statement concerns only underlying sets and topologies. It says nothing
about the structure sheaf, the residue fields `ℋ(x)`, or the comparison with
`X(K')` over all non-Archimedean extension fields `K'` (the second half of
Remark 3.4.2). It covers only affine schemes given by a finite polynomial
presentation, not general schemes of locally finite type.
