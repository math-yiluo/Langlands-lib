# Open analytic sections from local equalities

Suppose `s` is a section of an analytic map `p`. At each point in the
image of `s`, assume there is an open domain on which `p ≫ s`
agrees with the identity. The section then identifies its source with
an open analytic domain.

Each of the open domains lies in the image, since its points are
fixed by `p ≫ s`. Their union is therefore the image. The proof uses
the domain-valued Hom presheaf to glue the equalities on these
neighbourhoods as equalities of full localized maps. This gives the
two inverse identities between the lift of `s` to the image domain
and the restriction of `p`.

The lemma is an internal form of source locality for the actual
localized open-immersion property.
