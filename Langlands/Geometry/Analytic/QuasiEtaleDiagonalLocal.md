# Quasi-étale diagonals from sections of étale morphisms

`LocalizedEtaleSectionsOpenStatement` asks that every section of
an étale morphism in the actual localized category be an analytic
open immersion. This is the section case of the graph argument in
Berkovich's *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces*
(1993, Proposition 3.3.7 and proof of Corollary 3.3.9, printed
pp. 63–64). A section is a pullback of the diagonal of the étale
morphism. For the quasi-étale diagonal argument, the statement
applies to the second projection of the self-pullback of an étale
envelope.

For a compact quasi-étale source, choose a finite compact domain
cover retaining the étale envelopes. On each source domain compare
its self-pullback with the self-pullback of its étale envelope.
The diagonal of the envelope is an open immersion under the
section hypothesis and étale base change. The diagonal of the
domain is its pullback. The square has the full universal property
because the domain inclusion is a monomorphism.

The source domain's self-pullback is the intersection of two
actual inverse-image domains in the original self-pullback.
Thus each compact source domain maps by the diagonal to an
analytic domain there. The compact-domain gluing lemma uses
the first projection as retraction and proves that the whole
diagonal is an analytic-domain embedding.
