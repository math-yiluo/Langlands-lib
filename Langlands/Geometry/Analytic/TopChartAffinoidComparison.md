# Actual bounded affinoid comparison for greatest-chart presentations

This module identifies full strong maps between actual greatest-chart atlas presentations with bounded homomorphisms between their greatest-chart algebras. It constructs an inverse on morphisms using actual restrictions and domain factorizations, a fully faithful functor to presented affinoid spaces, and a categorical equivalence in the universes supported by the canonical singleton atlas. It also compares the actual topological realization functors.

The coefficient field `K : Type u` has instances `NormedField K`, `IsUltrametricDist K` and `CompleteSpace K`. An object `A : TopChartPresentation.{u,v,w} K` is a supplied actual indexed affinoid atlas presentation with a chosen chart containing every chart. Its space has universe `v`, its chart index has universe `w`, and its algebras have universe `u`. The published net axiom proves that the greatest chart covers the entire space; `topSpectrumHomeomorph` identifies this space with the actual Banach spectrum of its greatest-chart algebra. Each `PresentedAffinoidAlgebra K` is a genuine finite positive-polyradius Tate algebra quotient by its displayed closed ideal, with its constructed norm and Banach structure. No reducedness, nonzero-algebra or nonempty-spectrum assumption is added.

These specify actual affinoid charts with bounded domain restrictions, and strong maps with compatible affinoid morphisms for every admissible chart pair. This module derives the greatest-chart comparison from the published `StrongAtlasComposition` and `AffinoidSpectrumAtlas` constructions. The comparison and categorical equivalence are derived infrastructure, not separately numbered assertions in the original source.

`affinoid A` is the categorical opposite of `A.presentation.atlas.algebra A.topChart`, an object of `PresentedAffinoidSpace K`. For a full strong map `f : A ⟶ B`, `topPullback f` is its actual bounded algebra pullback from the greatest target algebra to the greatest source algebra. The greatest target contains the image of each source chart, so this pullback is admissible. `topPullback_id` is the actual identity restriction. `topPullback_comp` uses the published exact intermediate-chart formula for strong composition and gives the contravariant equation `topPullback (f ≫ g) = (topPullback f).comp (topPullback g)`.

`topPullback_injective` proves equality of full strong maps from equality of these bounded maps. Greatest-chart coverage and the chart squares first identify the continuous maps. Source restriction naturality identifies every pullback to the greatest target as the source restriction composed with the greatest-chart pullback. The published algebraic injectivity of conversion to a chosen greatest-target map then recovers every admissible target pullback. The proof does not infer algebra-map equality from agreement on spectrum points, so it retains information on nonreduced algebras.

`topSpectrumHomeomorph_symm_chart` identifies the inverse greatest-chart coordinate of a point in any smaller chart with the spectrum comap of its actual restriction to the greatest chart. For a bounded map `φ` from the greatest target algebra to the greatest source algebra, `chosenOfPullback φ` has continuous map `B.topSpectrumHomeomorph ∘ comap φ ∘ A.topSpectrumHomeomorph.symm`. It selects the greatest target chart for every source chart `i` and uses `(A.presentation.atlas.restriction i A.topChart _).comp φ` as its bounded pullback. The coordinate identity proves the chart square; the atlas restriction composition law proves compatibility with source restrictions.

`ofTopPullback φ` applies the published `StrongHom.ofTopChart` construction to this chosen map, constructing all other admissible target pullbacks by the actual domain universal property. `ofTopPullback_pullback_top` recovers the source restriction followed by `φ` at every source chart and the greatest target. `ofTopPullback_comp_restriction` describes every admissible local pullback: composing it with the target restriction from the greatest target gives the source restriction followed by `φ`. Thus these local maps are actual bounded factorizations; no missing gluing or descent output is an input.

At the greatest source chart its restriction is the identity, giving `topPullback_ofTopPullback`. Injectivity then proves `ofTopPullback_topPullback`. `topPullbackEquiv` packages these explicit inverse operations as a bijection between full strong maps and actual bounded maps. `affinoidFunctor` takes the opposite of each bounded pullback; its identity and composition laws are the equations above. `affinoidFullyFaithful` uses `ofTopPullback` as its explicit inverse on morphisms and supplies the `Full` and `Faithful` instances. These constructions hold for all `v,w`. `topSpectrumHomeomorph_naturality` is the strong map's greatest-chart square, showing that its actual continuous map intertwines the two greatest-chart spectrum homeomorphisms.

`ofAffinoid` uses the published actual singleton atlas to realize every `PresentedAffinoidSpace K` as a `TopChartPresentation.{u,u,0} K`. Its unique index is retained even when the spectrum is empty. `ofAffinoidMap` is the published full strong map attached to the actual opposite bounded algebra map; `topPullback_ofAffinoidMap` reads exactly that original bounded map. Applying `topPullback_injective` proves the identity and composition laws for `singletonFunctor`. `singletonFunctor_spaceFunctor` is definitional equality with the original `affinoidSpectrumFunctor K`.

`singletonComparisonIso A : ofAffinoid A.affinoid ≅ A` lifts the identity isomorphism of the greatest affinoid through the explicit `affinoidFullyFaithful` structure. Its two maps are therefore the constructed `ofTopPullback` maps with bounded identity pullbacks; the generic fully faithful lifting proves both inverse laws. `topPullback_singletonComparisonIso_hom` and `topPullback_singletonComparisonIso_inv` record these actual bounded identities. `singletonComparison` proves their naturality by applying `topPullback_injective`, the contravariant composition formula and the bounded identity laws. `affinoidSingletonComparison` compares reading the greatest algebra after singleton realization with the identity functor on presented affinoids; its components are identity isomorphisms. `affinoidEquivalence` packages these functors and natural isomorphisms using Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.Equivalence.mk`, which refines the unit to satisfy its triangle identity.

The categorical equivalence is exactly `TopChartPresentation.{u,u,0} K ≌ PresentedAffinoidSpace K`. The singleton realization has space universe `u` and index universe zero; no equivalence with arbitrary `v,w` is asserted. Separately, `spaceComparison` identifies `affinoidFunctor.{u,u,w} ⋙ affinoidSpectrumFunctor K` with `spaceFunctor` through the actual greatest-chart spectrum homeomorphisms, with naturality given by `topSpectrumHomeomorph_naturality`. Here the space universe is `u` while the index universe remains arbitrary.

This unit constructs the actual affine comparison for supplied greatest-chart presentations. General extended atlas construction, finite gluing, composition without greatest intermediate and target charts, analytic localization, structure sheaves, étale and equivariant sites, and the Harris tower remain separate obligations.
