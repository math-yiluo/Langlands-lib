# The common structure map of two étale factors

For the canonical small product of two étale factors, the map to their common base is étale. At a product point, choose finite neighbourhoods of the two factor points lying in open Hausdorff patches. Over every recognized affinoid of their common base neighbourhood, both finite inverse images are supplied by the all-pieces hypotheses. Flattening the iterated recognitions produces the original index for the right factor and the common base.

The actual small pair chart is the entire inverse image of that base affinoid in the simultaneous open neighbourhood of the product point. Its bounded map to the common base is the composition of the right factor's full finite map and the finite base change on the right projection. That composition is finite and étale at the chosen point by finite tensor base change and composition of pointwise étale maps. These whole preimages give the all-pieces property directly over the common base.
