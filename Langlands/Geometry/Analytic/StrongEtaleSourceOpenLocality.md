# Source-open locality of canonical strong etaleness

This internal prerequisite proves locality on the source for the actual canonical strong etale property. The source-open etale locus consists of points in open subsets for which the original morphism restricted by the actual analytic domain inclusion is strongly etale. Every point of that locus is etale on all recognized pieces for the original morphism's canonical saturation extension. Consequently, strong etaleness on any open covering of the source gives strong etaleness of the original morphism.

The proof first checks that every actual analytic domain inclusion is a topological embedding and that each of its admissible original chart maps fully represents its literal image. These bounded maps are the ambient atlas restrictions. For an open domain the subtype inclusion is an open embedding. The generic domain-embedding source transport theorem therefore carries all-pieces etaleness of the restricted composite to the ambient point. Functoriality of the actual compatibility and saturation functors identifies that composite with the canonical saturation extension of the restriction, as equality of full strong maps.

The supporting transport theorem constructs recognized image affinoids, compares their complete bounded maps by bounded algebra equivalences, and carries finite neighborhoods and pointwise flatness and unramifiedness to their open images. It requires no passage from a finite family of pointwise conditions to all-pieces etaleness: the restricted morphisms already satisfy the all-pieces property.

No Hausdorffness, compactness, goodness of the ambient spaces, or equality of the coefficient-field and ambient-space universes is assumed. The chart-index universe is also independent. This construction uses the existing actual analytic domain and full-recognition definitions. It is derived from the recognized-image and source-transport arguments, and does not by itself prove unrestricted pointwise lifting in the etale site.
