# Pointed restrictions of finite analytic Galois coverings

A deck component is an open of the source preserved by a chosen subgroup, with disjoint translates by elements outside that subgroup. Restricting the covering to the component and its open image gives an analytic Galois covering with the subgroup as its deck group. The restricted relation is the open of the original self-pullback where both projections land in the component. Its universal property follows by factoring the original pullback lift through that open. Each graph over the restricted group composes with the relation inclusion to the original graph. The condition on translates forces every original graph meeting the restricted relation with source in the component to belong to the subgroup, giving coverage. The original graph disjointness descends along the same comparison.

For a finite deck group and Hausdorff source, distinct translates of a chosen point are separated by disjoint open neighborhoods. Intersecting the resulting finitely many conditions gives an open around the point disjoint from each translate by an element that moves the point. Choose a distinguished open with compact closure inside it, then intersect its translates by the point stabilizer. Pullback of analytic domains and finite intersection preserve the distinguished-open condition. The resulting open is stable under the stabilizer and still avoids the other translates. Its fibre over the image of the original point is a singleton by deck transitivity.

These are derived local properties of analytic deck graphs. They give pointed shrinking of the finite Galois covers used in Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces*, Proposition 1.5.1, step (1′), printed p. 11.
