# Monicity of analytic closed immersions

`closedImmersionAffinoidPreimage` chooses the recognized affinoid inverse
image of a recognized target domain for a closed strong map. The choice
retains the inverse image equality and the admissible epimorphism that
identifies the source domain algebra as a quotient.

Two points with the same image belong to the inverse image of a single
target chart. The point map on this inverse image is induced by a
surjective bounded algebra map. Equality of the two seminorms after
restriction along this quotient implies equality of the seminorms on
the entire quotient algebra, so the original points coincide.

For two strong maps whose composites with the closed map agree, choose
on each source chart a recognized target domain for the composite. Both
strong maps land in its recognized inverse image on that chart. The
bounded pullbacks to the source chart agree after precomposition by the
quotient map, hence agree by its surjectivity. Extensionality on the full
recognition target and cancellation along the recognition
quasi-isomorphism identify the strong maps.

A common refinement of two fractions representing equal composites
gives equality of their strong numerators after composition. Strong
cancellation removes the closed map from this equality and gives a
common refinement of the original fractions. Monicity transfers across
the arrow isomorphism in the definition of an analytic closed immersion.

The proof is an internal consequence of the published closed immersion
predicate, its full-recognition pullbacks, and the right fractions
description of the localized analytic category.
