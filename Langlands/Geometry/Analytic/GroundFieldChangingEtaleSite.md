# Étale test objects and translations in the varying-field analytic category

`varyingFieldEtale` recognizes actual analytic arrows isomorphic in the
varying-field category to étale arrows over a fixed field. The field
projection proves that the field arrow of each such test is an isomorphism.
This permits changing the presentations of the fields by isometries. An arrow over a
fixed field is tested by the usual étale property on its saturated
localized analytic presentation. The étale site over an object uses
these arrows as test objects and covering families jointly surjective
on analytic points.

Postcomposition by a varying-field isomorphism translates test objects.
The domains and maps between domains are the original ones, so the
jointly surjective covering condition is invariant under translation.
The actual twisted category of a group of automorphisms carries the
induced topology testing the arrows of identity twist. In particular
this applies to the absolute Galois group acting on completed scalar
extension by semilinear analytic automorphisms.

Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces*,
§§1.1–1.2, printed and PDF pp. 1–3, gives the varying-field analytic maps,
semilinear action and equivariant sites. The étale test objects and the
covering rule are in Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean
analytic spaces* (1993), §4.1, printed p. 82 (PDF p. 79). The retained PDFs have
