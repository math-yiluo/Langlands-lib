# Bounded comparisons of finite-chart section equalizers

This module constructs a bounded map between the actual Banach section equalizers of two finite original-chart families, using explicit subordinate covers of the target members and overlaps. Covers in both directions give inverse bounded maps. The construction uses full algebra descent and separation.

The source motivation is Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §1.2, printed pp.20–21 / PDF pp.17–18, registered as `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`. The paragraph after Lemma 1.2.12 invokes Tate acyclicity for independence of the Banach section algebra from a special covering. This is derived infrastructure under explicit geometric cover hypotheses. General intersection existence, special-cover admissibility, affinoid recognition and maximal-atlas extension remain separate obligations.

The coefficient field is complete, normed and ultrametric; the ambient-space and chart-index universes remain independent. Both finite families have represented literal pairwise overlaps. `ComparisonCover d e` contains a finite original-chart cover subordinate to `d` for each member of `e` and each represented overlap of `e`. It supplies no bounded comparison or inverse.

`member_subset_locus` and `locus_subset` prove containment of each target member and the target union in the source union. `memberRestrictions_compatible` uses naturality of the constructed subordinate-cover restriction to identify both target overlap restrictions. `comparison` is the bounded lift of those member maps into the actual equalizer of `e`. `projection_comp_comparison` records its component recovery.

`original_comp_projection_comparison` gives the full bounded restriction formula on any original chart contained in both a source and a target member. `comparison_eq` uses component extensionality and independence of subordinate covers to prove independence of all cover choices.

Given a reverse `ComparisonCover e d`, `locus_eq` proves equality of the literal unions. `comparison_comp_reverse` proves the inverse equation by projecting to each source member and applying full-domain algebra separation on that member's reverse subordinate cover. Original restriction recovery for the reverse descent map gives the local target-member formula; the common-original-chart equation for the forward comparison identifies it with the original source projection. Evaluating this full bounded-map equality on every section proves the component inverse law. `algEquiv` packages the two constructed bounded maps and both inverse laws as a `BoundedAlgEquiv`. The two final equations recover its forward and inverse bounded maps exactly.
