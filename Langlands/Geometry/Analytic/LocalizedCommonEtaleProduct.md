# Étale structure maps of products of localized étale morphisms

The complete common structure map of a limiting product of two localized étale morphisms over the same base is étale. For strong maps, identify the common map of the canonical small product with the structure map over the recognized base by naturality of saturation and the recognition isomorphisms. The all-pieces theorem makes that map étale.

For localized input maps, take right fractions with common target and use the canonical small product of their strong numerators. Étaleness passes through the isomorphisms given by the denominators. The universal property compares the constructed product with every supplied limiting product and transfers étaleness of the common map.
