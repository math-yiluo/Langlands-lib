# Naturality of the actual strong affine scalar-extension comparisons

The target compares the two induced *compatible strong maps* of the actual affine chart presentations, before saturation and localization. It is formulated over complete nontrivially normed ultrametric `K` and `L` with `NormedAlgebra K L`, for a homomorphism `φ : A →ₐ[K] B` and finite polynomial presentations `P` and `R` of its source and target algebras. The two fields and two algebras have independent universes.

`RespectsBaseChangeCoordinates` names the all-admissible bounded chart equation from `AffineAnalytificationGroundFieldComparisonStatement`: on a chart of the analytification of the algebraic base change, the pullback of the image of `1 ⊗ a` is `iota` of the original chart's image of `a`. The equation uses the actual map on each pair, with no chosen target index.

`AffineAnalytificationGroundFieldNaturalityStatement` says that any two comparison maps satisfying these equations make the strong square commute. One path extends `compatibleHom P R φ` via `compatibleMap K L` and then takes the comparison for `P`. The other takes the comparison for `R` and then applies the induced compatible strong map for `Algebra.TensorProduct.map (AlgHom.id L L) φ`. Both arrows run contravariantly from the extended atlas for `R` to the atlas of the algebraic base change of `P`. The conclusion is equality of complete compatible strong maps, not just their action on analytic points. This target does not assume the comparisons are quasi-isomorphisms; the separate strong affine comparison statement supplies such witnesses and their projection equations.

This is a derived presentation-level part of the naturality in Conrad–Temkin, *Non-archimedean analytification of algebraic spaces* (2009), Theorem 2.3.5, p. 13, registered as `conrad-temkin-2009-nonarchimedean-analytification`. It uses the atlas model for the analytification input in the proof of Conrad's Proposition 5.11(3), p. 19 of *Weil and Grothendieck approaches to adelic points* (2011), registered as `conrad-2011-weil-grothendieck-adelic-points`. The comparison with scalar extension of an already localized object, nonaffine naturality and transitivity remain separate.
