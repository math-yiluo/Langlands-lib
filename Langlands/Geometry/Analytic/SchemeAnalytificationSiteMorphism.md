# Analytification of étale sheaves

Let `K : Type u` be a complete nontrivially normed ultrametric field and `X` a separated scheme locally of finite type over `Spec K`. This is the ground-field specialization of the site construction in Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §4.1, p. 82. Analytification on the separated algebraic étale test objects uses `StrongEtaleSmallIndexComparisonStatement.{u}` for forward preservation of étaleness, as in Proposition 3.3.11, p. 64.

The separated algebraic étale objects have the topology generated by the families whose images generate covering sieves in the small algebraic étale site. Both the inclusion into that site and analytification into the ordinary analytic étale site are continuous. To prove continuity, every algebraic cospan is completed by its scheme pullback, whose source is separated and locally of finite type over `Spec K`. Analytification sends this to an analytic pullback. The center-preimage formula for analytic images in Proposition 2.6.8, pp. 53–54, sends jointly surjective families to jointly surjective families.

`analyticEtaleSheaf` restricts a sheaf on the small algebraic étale site and applies the inverse image given by left Kan extension and sheafification. The construction assumes the left Kan extensions and weak sheafification in the selected coefficient category. On the separated algebraic objects, the inverse image is left adjoint to precomposition along analytification. This follows the inverse-image convention of §4.1, p. 82. The compact-support comparison in §7.1, pp. 127–128, uses the analytified sheaf.
