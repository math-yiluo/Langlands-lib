# Continuity on localized pullbacks along a quasi-étale leg

For a mapped strong cospan with small fibre-product space,
`smallFibreProductComparisonIso` compares a supplied localized limiting
square with the canonical small strong product. The two comparison
equations preserve equivariance of the projections under conjugation
by this isomorphism. The small-product theorem gives continuity of the
conjugated action. Equivariant isomorphism transport gives continuity on
the supplied point.

`isContinuousAction_of_isPullback_of_quasiEtale` uses a strong
quasi-étale representative of the first leg and a right fraction for
the second leg over the same base. The recognition comparisons turn
this into a cospan whose glued product is small, by the
domain–étale chart smallness theorem. The proof transports the factor
actions along these isomorphisms and retains both projection equations
as equations of localized analytic arrows.

The theorem applies with an arbitrary topology on the group and an
arbitrary localized base. This is the quasi-étale-leg continuity
criterion used in constructing continuous lifts on compact tuple
pullbacks. The argument combines the Library's localized right
fractions, the quasi-étale product smallness construction and the
small strong product continuity theorem.
