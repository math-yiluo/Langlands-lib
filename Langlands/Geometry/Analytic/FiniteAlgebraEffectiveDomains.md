# Full parent domains from effective finite algebra descent

This internal adapter recovers the full represented component-domain
cover and exact overlap images of a supplied effective finite algebra
descent realization. It leaves existence of that realization to the
separate effective-descent theorem.

Postcomposition with a whole bounded algebra equivalence preserves a
full domain representation. Apply this to each original parent
base-change comparison equivalence. Its entire forward map is the
canonical pushout comparison, so the unchanged parent-to-component
bounded map represents exactly the inverse image of the original
base-domain locus. These domains cover the parent because the original
domains cover the original base spectrum.

For any further full base domain `E` contained in a component, its
actual relative domain in that component and bounded base change give
the entire local restriction. Full-domain composition represents its
parent inverse image. The exact structural bounded-map equation
identifies the locus. No spectral injectivity of the original finite
structural maps is assumed.

The matching-algebra equations identify the two entire parent overlap
composites. The full representation of the second overlap restriction
then proves that the original overlap spectrum image equals the literal
intersection of the two component images in the parent spectrum.
These are precisely the parent full-domain and overlap-image inputs
needed by the finite-chart parent recognition adapter after the
geometric chart algebra comparisons have been constructed.

All algebras and bounded maps remain the original ones. No global
analytic chart, parent spectral embedding or geometric recognition is
assumed. The coefficient field is any complete ultrametric normed field;
nonreduced and zero algebras and empty covers remain included. The
provenance is an internal deduction from actual full-domain pullbacks,
bounded comparison equivalences, matching equations and the full-domain
universal property. No external theorem is transcribed.
