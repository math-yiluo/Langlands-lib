# Scalar automorphisms on points of a base-changed scheme

Let a scheme over a field \(k\) be extended to a complete, nontrivially normed non-Archimedean field \(K\). Assume that the base change is locally of finite type over \(K\). A \(k\)-automorphism of \(K\) gives a semilinear automorphism of the base change. Each affine open from the original scheme pulls back to a stable affine open of the base change. Applying global sections to the affine Cartesian square identifies its coordinate ring with the tensor product of \(K\) and the original affine coordinate ring. Pullback by the automorphism acts on scalars by that automorphism and fixes all sections from the original affine open.

`baseChangePointMap` is the semilinear map on analytification points. `baseChangePointEvaluation` is jointly continuous in a parameter of isometric automorphisms and the analytic point, provided every scalar orbit is continuous. On each actual chart ring, `chart_sections_generate` expresses any section as a finite sum of products of scalars and pulled-back sections. Applying `continuous_pointSeminorm_sum` after varying those scalars proves continuity of each coordinate evaluation. The products of the parameter space with the pulled-back affine chart images form an open cover, so the evaluations give joint continuity on the whole analytification.

The construction uses the gluing and seminorm descriptions of Berkovich, *Spectral theory and analytic geometry over non-Archimedean fields* (1990), Theorem 3.4.1 and Remark 3.4.2, pp. 63–64. The action on points is the scalar action considered in Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on \(p\)-adic analytic spaces* (1996), §1.1.1(i), p. 2.
