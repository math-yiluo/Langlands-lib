# Localizing directed represented domains at an original chart

Let `K : Type u` be a complete ultrametric normed field, `X : Type v` a topological space, and `a : IndexedAffinoidAtlas.{u,v,w} K X` an actual affinoid atlas with pairwise directed original charts, witnessed by `hd`. This module works with its actual `DirectedDomain a hd` category: each object retains a full affinoid-domain representation in a specified original chart, and each arrow retains its entire bounded restriction over all common original upper charts.

`originalChart a hd i` is the whole full-domain representation over the original chart algebra `a.algebra i`, regarded as a directed-domain object. Its ambient locus is precisely the range of `a.chart i`. For each full domain over that algebra, `toOriginalChart` is its actual bounded structural map to this whole-chart object. It is not a choice of a different algebra or an assumed analytic-domain object.

For any represented domain `U` and actual inclusion `f : U ⟶ originalChart a hd i`, `represents_toOriginalChart` proves that the bounded map `f.restriction : a.algebra i → U.algebra` fully represents `a.chart i ⁻¹' U.locus`. Choose a common original upper chart `q` of `U.index` and `i`. Extend `U.domain` to `q` without changing its quotient algebra. The stored equation of `f` over `q` gives an actual full-domain arrow from that extension to the original relative chart domain for `i` in `q`. The published original-chart restriction theorem proves the full representation. The original chart square, exact extension image and injectivity of the original chart `q` identify its inverse-image locus with the stated preimage in chart `i`. This proves closedness, exact spectrum image and unique factorization against all positive-polyradius affinoid tests; it assumes no injectivity of the arbitrary full domain's structural spectrum map.

`restrictToOriginalChart` retains exactly `U.algebra`, uses exactly `f.restriction` as its bounded structural map, and uses that preimage as its locus. Its chart image is exactly `U.locus`, since `f` entails containment in the original chart. `sameChartOverFunctor` sends a fixed-chart full domain to its directed-domain object with the constructed structural inclusion. `restrictOriginalChartFunctor` is its inverse-direction functor on `Over (originalChart a hd i)`. An arrow's commuting triangle is the entire bounded base-map equation required for its restricted representations, so both functors retain every original bounded arrow.

The fixed-chart round trip is isomorphic through bounded identity maps on the unchanged quotient algebra. The directed-domain round trip has exactly the original ambient locus; `sameChartRestrictIso` constructs both arrows by the published locus-isomorphism construction. Full uniqueness over a common original upper identifies each complete bounded restriction with the identity on `U.algebra`. These identities prove the overcategory triangle, naturality and equivalence triangle. Thus `originalChartLocalizationEquivalence` constructs an equivalence between the full-domain category over `a.algebra i` and the overcategory of directed represented domains over that original chart. Forgetting the inclusion recovers `sameChartFunctor` exactly. The inverse functor preserves the actual affinoid and spectrum realization functors by definitional equality. Independent coefficient, ambient and index universes remain arbitrary.

The proof uses the additional pairwise-directed-original-chart hypothesis to supply one common upper; it does not implement the source's unrestricted finite-cover and Tate-acyclicity argument. The full affinoid-test meaning is Berkovich, *Non-archimedean analytic spaces (Trieste lectures)* (2009), registered as `berkovich-2009-trieste-analytic-spaces`, SHA-256 `8cfe4f6f35d5e717ac67c5c8f5ef9ff9185f460bbe5f9a10aeacd84cddeeb2ca`, Section 2.2, Definition 2.2.1, printed/PDF p.9. The construction reuses the published original-chart extension and localization theorems and the directed-domain category. It does not establish a new atlas net, arbitrary full-domain spectrum embedding, unrestricted saturated analytic atlas, unrestricted strong-map localization, a structure sheaf or an analytic etale site.
