# Finite étale refinements on affinoid charts

For a jointly surjective family of strong maps that are étale on all
recognized pieces, `StrongEtaleFiniteChartRefinementStatement` gives a
finite cover of each chart of the saturated target by smaller charts.
Each smaller chart, viewed in the full recognition atlas, receives a
finite Banach, algebraically finite étale map from an actual recognized
piece of one source. The associated map of bounded spectra is surjective.
The field, space, atlas-index and cover-index universes are independent.
The companion theorem proves the statement for complete ultrametric
normed fields.

At a chosen lift of a point of a target chart, shrink the finite open
source model within a prescribed neighborhood. The local-model theorem
makes each bounded map of this model finite étale. The image of the open
source model is open by étale openness. Choose a rational neighborhood
in the containing target chart inside this image, and realize it as a
chart of the saturated atlas. Restrict the finite model to that chart's
recognized subset. Its chosen source piece is the inverse image inside
the finite open source model, so every spectrum point of the target
piece has a lift. The chart embeddings and the actual bounded-map
commutation identify the point lifts with surjectivity on spectra.

For a jointly surjective family, choose one lift for each point of the
containing chart. The rational neighborhoods obtained from these lifts
cover its compact spectrum. A finite subcover gives the stated
refinement. This is an internal deduction from the Library's finite
models, étale openness, saturated chart realization and compactness.
