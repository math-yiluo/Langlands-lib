# Complete nonarchimedean ground fields

`CompleteUltrametricField` bundles a Mathlib normed field whose metric is
ultrametric and complete. Its arrows are norm-preserving ring
homomorphisms, so they are isometric field embeddings. Identity and
composition use the original ring homomorphisms.

An arrow gives the actual normed-algebra structure for extension of
scalars, with its given field embedding as the algebra map. For a
composable pair, these choices form a scalar tower with the composite
embedding as its total structure map. Conversely an existing normed
algebra structure on two such fields gives an arrow. The same category
includes trivially normed fields.

V. G. Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces*,
unfinished manuscript (1995–1996), §1.1, printed/PDF p. 1, defines the
ground fields and the isometric embeddings of the base category of
