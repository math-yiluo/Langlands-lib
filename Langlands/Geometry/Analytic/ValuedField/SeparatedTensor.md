# Separated tensor completion over a complete ultrametric field

Internal construction for full affinoid domain residue comparison. It extends the published nontrivial-norm tensor construction to trivially normed complete fields. Precisely, the coefficient field is any complete normed field, and the tensor factors are ultrametric normed commutative coefficient algebras; neither factor is required complete or to be a field. The three universes are independent, and the completed carrier lies in the maximum of the factor universes. In particular this includes every complete ultrametric coefficient field and its normed field extensions, including trivial norms. `toCompletion` embeds the algebraic tensor in a complete ultrametric normed commutative algebra and is contractive on pure tensors.

In the nontrivial case, positivity uses the existing `NonarchimedeanCompletedTensorProof.seminormFun_pos`. In the trivial case all scalar norms are at most one, so positive-radius open norm balls in a finite-dimensional subspace are scalar submodules. Choose a radius whose ball has minimal dimension. Every smaller positive-radius ball equals it, forcing it to contain no nonzero vector. Nonzero vectors therefore have a uniform positive norm lower bound. A scalar-valued linear functional on this subspace is bounded, since scalar norms are at most one.

The existing tensor representation reduction makes right factors independent without increasing the maximum norm product. Its left factors lie in the finite span of one fixed tensor representation. A dual functional detecting the tensor, bounded on that span by the preceding argument, gives a positive lower bound for every representation value. The projective tensor seminorm is consequently a norm. Completion then gives the asserted embedding and pure tensor norm bound, exactly as in the published nontrivial case. This is an internal extension of that implementation, rather than a new source transcription.
