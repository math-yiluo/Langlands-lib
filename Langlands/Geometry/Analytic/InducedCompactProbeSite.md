# Induction on equivariant quasi-étale sites

Fix a complete ultrametric normed field \(K\), a topological group \(G\), an
open subgroup \(H\), and a Hausdorff \(K\)-analytic space \(X\) with a
continuous \(H\)-action by \(K\)-analytic automorphisms.
`compactProbeSiteInductionEquivalence` is the equivalence between the
equivariant quasi-étale over-category of \(X\) and that of the induced
\(G\)-space \(X_{G/H}\). Its forward functor takes disjoint copies of an
equivariant object over the cosets in \(G/H\), acting on maps copy by copy.

The Hausdorff comparison identifies the ordinary compact-probe covering
test with the finite chart-neighbourhood test. After forgetting the actions,
the comparison applies at every object of each equivariant site. Induction
preserves and reflects families for the chart-neighbourhood test, so
`map_compactProbeSiteInduction_mem_iff` transports the generating families
of the two equivariant compact-probe topologies.

The instance `compactProbeSiteInduction_isDenseSubsite` gives preservation
and reflection of covering sieves. For the forward inclusion of topologies,
push forward the sieves generated by the covering families. For the inverse
inclusion, push such a sieve from the target site first along the inverse
equivalence and then along the forward equivalence. The result is the
pullback of the original sieve along the counit isomorphism. Pushing forward
along the forgetful functor commutes with pullback along an isomorphism
because composing a sieve factorization with the inverse isomorphism gives
a factorization over the new target. Stability under pullback in the
ordinary topology and reflection of the generating families then give
the inverse inclusion. Pullback along the unit identifies the original
sieve after the two pushforwards.

This realizes the quasi-étale part of Berkovich's induction example for
operators over \(K\). The compact-probe description uses the Hausdorff
comparison to interpret the covering test in the original sources.
