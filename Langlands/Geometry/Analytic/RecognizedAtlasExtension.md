# Actual strong extension to the recognized affinoid atlas

This module constructs the full strong map from an actual atlas presentation to its constructed recognition atlas. The underlying topological space and map are literally unchanged. Every admissible source-original/target-recognized chart pair has the constructed bounded full-domain restriction, including all information invisible on spectrum points.

`recognitionPresentation` packages the actual recognition atlas on the original space. `recognitionTarget` canonically lifts its literal-subset indices. The source uses the existing `saturationSource`, which in this notation is only the original atlas with canonically lifted indices; it is not an assumed saturated atlas. Both index types are thereby placed in the common `max v w` universe required by the current strong-map interface. The coefficient, ambient and original-index universes remain independent.

`toRecognition` uses the identity continuous ambient map. Every original source chart has a target chart given by its already proved original recognition. For an arbitrary admissible target chart, the pullback is its actual restriction to the unchanged original source algebra. The previously proved chart equation gives the strong-map square. Naturality of original restrictions in the source and the proved naturality of recognized restrictions in the target give the complete two-sided bounded restriction equation. These are equalities of entire bounded algebra maps.

`toRecognition_isQuasiIso` proves that this full strong map is a quasi-isomorphism. Its ambient map is the identity homeomorphism. Each admissible pullback is the previously constructed full domain of the recognized target with exactly the original source algebra and exact relative spectrum locus. No comparison, invertibility or full-domain output is supplied as an input.

This is derived infrastructure for the extension construction in Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), Proposition 1.2.13(ii), printed p.21 / PDF p.18. Registered original `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`; the page was read and visually checked. The current strong-map model interprets the extension through this constructed identity-space quasi-isomorphism. A raw inverse strong map, uniqueness of arbitrary analytic extension, recognition closure, equivalent-net invariance and idempotence are not asserted here.

The field is complete and ultrametric with arbitrary valuation; nonreduced or zero algebras and empty domains are allowed. The unproved special-intersection target is not imported. Applying this construction to the already constructed saturated presentation gives the same actual extension from the source full local-domain atlas to the full-net recognition atlas.
