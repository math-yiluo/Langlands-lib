# Faithfulness and composition of chart-family extraction

For each source chart, two strong morphisms into the same saturated affine
analytification admit a common original polynomial target chart. Their extracted
algebra maps can both be read using that chart. The images of the polynomial
generators in the original chart algebra are its affinoid coordinates. Equality
of the extracted algebra maps therefore makes the two bounded pullbacks equal by
the coordinate extensionality theorem.

Cancelling the chart algebra cast gives equality of the bounded pullbacks from
the saturated copy of each common target chart. These pullbacks give local
chart data realized by both morphisms. The uniqueness theorem for directed
atlas reconstruction then identifies the complete strong morphisms.

For postcomposition by the morphism induced by a coordinate-algebra map, take an
admissible chain of two original target charts. Saturated composition computes
the bounded pullback along that chain. The original-chart recovery theorem
transports the second pullback to the unsaturated polynomial charts. There the
coordinate-algebra formula for the induced morphism computes the composite as
precomposition by the given algebra map on every source chart.

This is an internal consequence of chart-family extraction, the coordinate
universal property of presented affinoid algebras, directed atlas
reconstruction and bounded pullback formulas for saturated morphisms.
