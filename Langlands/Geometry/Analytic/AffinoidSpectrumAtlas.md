# Singleton atlases on actual presented affinoid spectra

This module constructs the singleton affinoid atlas on every actual finite positive-polyradius closed-ideal quotient spectrum. It gives a fully faithful functor from the opposite category of actual bounded algebra maps into chosen atlas presentations, retaining exactly the original spectrum functor. It also constructs all-admissible-pair strong maps and proves a bijection between these strong maps and the original bounded algebra maps.

The coefficient field `K : Type u` is a complete ultrametric normed field. An object `A : PresentedAffinoidSpace K` is an object of the opposite of `PresentedAffinoidAlgebra K`. Its algebra `A.unop` is the actual quotient of a finite positive-polyradius Tate algebra by its displayed closed ideal, with its constructed quotient norm and Banach structure. Neither nonzero nor reducedness hypotheses are imposed. The atlas space universe is `u` and its index universe is zero.

The original source conventions are V. Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §1.2, Definition 1.2.3 and Remark 1.2.4(i), printed p. 16 / PDF p. 13, and Definition 1.2.7, printed p. 18 / PDF p. 15. Registered source: `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`. The first passage specifies an affinoid algebra and its spectrum homeomorphism on each compact net member, with bounded domain restrictions. The latter specifies strong maps at all admissible chart pairs. The singleton construction, fully faithful realization and inverse morphism formulas here are derived infrastructure using the published actual quotient algebras, spectra, atlas interface and greatest-target strong-map construction. They are not separately numbered source assertions.

`PresentedAffinoidSpace.singletonAtlas A` has index type `PUnit`, the constant algebra `A.unop`, and identity chart on `BanachSpectrum A.unop`. The chart is an embedding, and indexing by its range is injective because there is only one index. Hausdorffness of the actual Banach spectrum gives local Hausdorffness. Its chart family is the singleton whole-space family; `isNet_singleton_univ` proves the net condition, including the intersection condition. Every restriction is the actual bounded algebra identity. For every presented affinoid test algebra and every bounded map into that test algebra, the unique factorization through a restriction is that same map. Both uniqueness and restriction composition reduce to the identity equations. No affinoid-domain output or descent hypothesis is supplied.

`atlasPresentation A` pairs the actual spectrum from `affinoidSpectrumFunctor` with this constructed atlas. `atlasPresentation_top_contains` proves that its unique index is greatest. The unique index is retained when the spectrum is empty: no point or nonempty-spectrum hypothesis is needed to construct it. Thus this interface differs from the disjoint-family atlas, which omits empty components. It does not infer any algebra-map equality from emptiness or from equality on spectrum points.

For `f : A ⟶ B` in the opposite category, `atlasMap f` has continuous map the spectrum comap of the actual bounded map `f.unop : B.unop → A.unop`. It selects the unique target chart and uses exactly `f.unop` as every pullback; `atlasMap_pullback` records this equality. Chart compatibility is the defining comap equation, and restriction compatibility consists of identities. `fromAtlasMap` reads the bounded pullback at the unique source chart and takes its categorical opposite. `fromAtlasMap_atlasMap` is the identity on original morphisms. Conversely `atlasMap_fromAtlasMap` recovers the entire chosen map: the chart square identifies its continuous map, the unique index determines its index map, and its unique bounded pullback is unchanged. This uses retained bounded algebra data, rather than any separation principle for algebra maps on spectra.

`atlasHomEquiv` packages these inverse operations as a bijection on morphisms. `atlasFunctor` preserves identity and composition; the pullback and index equations are definitional, while the continuous-map equations are checked by extensionality of the actual spectrum seminorms. `atlasFullyFaithful` packages the explicit inverse on morphisms, and the `Full` and `Faithful` instances use the proved bijection. `atlasFunctor_spaceFunctor` is definitional equality with the original `affinoidSpectrumFunctor`, including its objects and maps.

`strongAtlasMap f` applies the published `StrongHom.ofTopChart` to `atlasMap f` at the unique greatest target. This constructs every admissible pullback by the actual domain factorization. `strongAtlasMap_pullback` proves that the pullback at every source index and the unique target is exactly `f.unop`, using `ofTopChart_pullback_selected`. `strongAtlasMap_fromChosen` starts with arbitrary full strong-map data, chooses the unique target chart, extracts its actual bounded algebra map, and reconstructs the entire strong map. It first applies the chosen-map recovery above, then the published `ofTopChart_toChosen` algebraic recovery theorem. Finally `strongAtlasHomEquiv` packages these inverse operations. Its left inverse reads the exact original bounded map; its right inverse is full strong-map recovery. This retains information on nonreduced algebras that the point map alone need not detect.

The only project import is the published `StrongAtlasMaps`; this construction does not depend on the separately staged strong-composition module. It supplies canonical actual affine input and full morphism comparisons. It does not construct extended atlases, general analytic morphism composition or localization, general domains, structure sheaves, étale sites or the Harris tower.
