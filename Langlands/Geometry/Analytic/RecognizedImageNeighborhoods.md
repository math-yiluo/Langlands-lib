# Pointed recognized neighborhoods with controlled target images

This internal construction supplies a finite family of recognized source
affinoids through a prescribed point. The family is contained in a given
open Hausdorff source patch and its union is an ambient neighborhood. Each
member's entire image lies in one chosen recognized target affinoid inside
a prescribed open base set. The field, ambient-space and atlas-index
universes are independent. No finite, flat, unramified or etale premise is
required of the supplied strong morphism.

Start with a finite recognized source neighborhood family inside the
intersection of the source patch and the inverse image of the open base
set. The existing rational-target pullback construction provides a finite
full-domain cover of each source member with entire images in recognized
base pieces. Recognize every one of these source domains through the
existing full-domain recognition functor. This gives a finite compact
refinement of the whole original neighborhood family.

Retain the refinement members containing the prescribed point. Their union
is still a neighborhood: every discarded compact member lies inside the
open Hausdorff patch, so its complement is a neighborhood of the point.
Intersect these finitely many complements with the original neighborhood
union. Every point of this intersection lies in a retained member. The
compactness and closedness argument takes place inside the given patch;
no global ambient Hausdorff hypothesis is used, and the argument includes
points on domain boundaries.

The module proves this compact finite-family refinement as a general
topological lemma. Its analytic construction records the finite recognized
family, point membership, source containment, chosen recognized targets,
whole-image containments and ambient neighborhood certificate. It retains
the already constructed original full-domain covers; it does not infer
algebra-map equality from spectrum data.

The provenance is internal: finite recognized neighborhoods of the full
recognition, actual rational target pullbacks and their finite full-domain
covers, full-domain recognition, and compactness inside an open Hausdorff
patch. This supplies target-controlled neighborhood pieces for the later
pointwise tensor base-change argument. It does not assert finiteness of a
product projection, existence of effective finite algebra descent or the
final etale base-change theorem.
