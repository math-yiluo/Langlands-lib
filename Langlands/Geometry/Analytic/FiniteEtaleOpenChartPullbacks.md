# Affinoid charts in finite étale open neighborhoods

A compact open cut of a recognized affinoid chart comes from a clopen
subset of its spectrum. The recognized domain of the clopen rational
chart realizes this cut. The restriction is finite étale, with Banach
finiteness over a nontrivially normed complete field.

For a chart in the image of an open restriction of a finite étale
neighborhood, start with the full finite étale preimage chart. The
cut is compact when it equals the inverse image of the target chart
in a larger recognized chart and the intermediate space is Hausdorff.
The restricted cover is finite étale and surjective on spectra.

The small chart of an exact domain preimage also gives the categorical
pullback square. Its lift is the unique factorization through the
small chart of any map whose image lies in the chart.

These are internal consequences of clopen chart localization, the
recognized finite étale windows, and the localized domain inclusions.
