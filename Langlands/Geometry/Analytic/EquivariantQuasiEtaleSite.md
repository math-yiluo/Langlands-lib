# The quasi-étale site `X(G)_qét` of a `G`-space

Author: Worker 21.

For a `G`-space `X`, Berkovich's equivariant quasi-étale site `X(G)_qét` has as
objects the quasi-étale `G`-morphisms `U(G) → X(G)` from `G`-spaces `U`. Its
coverings are the families whose underlying families are quasi-étale
coverings of `X`. This is the site on which Berkovich identifies equivariant
sheaves with sheaves (Theorem 1.4.2 of the manuscript). This module constructs
it from the Library's `G`-spaces (`ContinuousAnalyticAction`), the
equivariant étale site (`EquivariantEtaleSite`) and the quasi-étale site
(`QuasiEtaleSite`), with the functor forgetting the action, the inclusion of
the equivariant étale site, and restriction along continuous homomorphisms.

> For a G-space X, let Qét(X(G)) (resp. Ét(X(G))) denote the category of
> quasi-étale (resp. étale) morphisms U(G) → X(G). The quasi-étale (resp.
> étale) topology on X(G) is the Grothendieck topology on the category
> Qét(X(G)) (resp. Ét(X(G))) generated by the pretopology for which the set
> of coverings of (U(G) → X(G)) ∈ Qét(X(G)) (resp. Ét(X(G))) consists of the
> families {U_i(G) → U(G)}_{i∈I} such that {U_i → U}_{i∈I} is a covering in
> the quasi-étale (resp. étale) topology of X. We denote by X(G)_qét (resp.
> X(G)_ét) the site obtained in this way … There is a morphism of sites
> µ_G : X(G)_qét → X(G)_ét.

The quasi-étale coverings of `X` are those of Berkovich, *Vanishing cycles
for formal schemes* (1994), §3, printed p.544, as constructed in the
Library's `QuasiEtaleSite` (registered as
`berkovich-1994-vanishing-cycles-formal-schemes`, SHA256

## Derived choices

As in `EquivariantEtaleSite`: the objects of `Qét(X(G))` are morphisms of the
Library's category `GSpace` (morphisms of `𝒜n^op` with `ν` the identity of
`G`) whose underlying morphism is quasi-étale, and all `G`-morphisms over `X`
are morphisms. The topology is Mathlib's `Precoverage.toGrothendieck` of the
covering families, which is the topology generated by Berkovich's
pretopology. The coverings are defined literally as the families whose image
under the forgetful functor is a quasi-étale covering of `X`. The restriction
functor is the functor `U(G) ↦ U(H)` of the morphism `X(H) → X(G)` given by a
continuous homomorphism `H → G`, for which Berkovich's fibre product
`X ×_X U` is `U` (explained, not formalized).

## Definitions and results

All declarations are in `Langlands.Analytic.SaturatedAtlasPresentation`. The
field `K` is a complete ultrametric normed field (trivial valuation
allowed), `G` is a group with a topology, and the spaces use
`LocalizedCategory.{u, v, v}`.

- `GSpace.quasiEtale`: `G`-morphisms whose underlying morphism is
  quasi-étale (the Library's `quasiEtale`, through `GSpace.forget`);
  `quasiEtale_iff` records this. It respects isomorphisms and contains
  identities (Mathlib's instances for `MorphismProperty.inverseImage`).
  `GSpace.etale_le_quasiEtale`: étale `G`-morphisms are quasi-étale, from the
  Library's `etale_le_quasiEtale`.
- `EquivariantQuasiEtaleSite X` is Mathlib's `(GSpace.quasiEtale).Over ⊤ X`:
  Berkovich's `Qét(X(G))`.
- `EquivariantQuasiEtaleSite.toQuasiEtaleSite X` forgets the action:
  `U(G) ↦ U`, into the Library's `QuasiEtaleSite` of the underlying space.
  `toQuasiEtaleSite_obj_left`, `toQuasiEtaleSite_obj_hom` and
  `toQuasiEtaleSite_map_left` record its values.
- `EquivariantQuasiEtaleSite.ofEquivariantEtaleSite X` is the inclusion
  `Ét(X(G)) → Qét(X(G))` (Mathlib's `MorphismProperty.Over.changeProp`), the
  functor underlying Berkovich's morphism of sites `µ_G`.
- `equivariantQuasiEtalePrecoverage X` is the pullback along
  `toQuasiEtaleSite X` of the Library's `quasiEtalePrecoverage`.
  `mem_equivariantQuasiEtalePrecoverage_iff`: a family covers exactly when
  its image under `toQuasiEtaleSite` is a quasi-étale covering of `X`
  (Berkovich's definition).
- `equivariantQuasiEtaleTopology X` is the generated Grothendieck topology,
  the site `X(G)_qét`; `generate_mem_equivariantQuasiEtaleTopology` and
  `isSheaf_equivariantQuasiEtaleTopology_iff` as for the other sites.
- `EquivariantQuasiEtaleSite.res f`, for a continuous homomorphism
  `f : H →ₜ* G`, restricts the actions of the source and of every object and
  morphism (the Library's `GSpace.res`); `res_obj_left` records its values.
  `res_comp_toQuasiEtaleSite`: followed by forgetting the action, it is the
  forgetful functor of `X`. `map_res_mem_equivariantQuasiEtalePrecoverage_iff`:
  restriction preserves and reflects coverings.
