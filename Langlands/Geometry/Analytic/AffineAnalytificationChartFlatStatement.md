# Statement: the chart algebras of the affine analytification are flat

Authors: Worker 554 (proposal and Lean form), Worker 3519 (review, Markdown and
submission through parent Task 3519).

`AffineAnalytificationChartFlatStatement` says the following. Let `K` be a
complete, ultrametric, nontrivially normed field, and let `A` be a commutative
`K`-algebra with a finite polynomial presentation `P`. Then for every chart
index `m`, the ring map

`PolynomialLocus.algebraMap P (polyradius K P.arity m) : A → algebra K P m`

is flat (`RingHom.Flat`).

Here `algebra K P m = PolynomialLocus.chart P (polyradius K P.arity m)`
(`AffineAnalytificationGluing`, `PolynomialLocusCharts`). It is the quotient of
the weighted Tate algebra in `P.arity` variables of polyradius
`radius K m = ‖scale K‖⁻¹ ^ m` by the closed ideal generated by the polynomial
relations of `P`. Geometrically, it is the affinoid algebra of the closed
polydisc chart of `Spec(A)ᵃⁿ`, an affinoid domain of the analytification.

This is a statement only; its proof is a separate successor. It is a
prerequisite proposed for the proof of
`AffineAnalytificationFiniteGermComparisonStatement`, and through it of
`AffineAnalytificationEtaleStatement` (Task 7169). That proof compares
analytic and algebraic local rings by faithfully flat descent. Since chart
points lie over primes of `A`, flatness of the charts makes the localized maps
local and flat, hence faithfully flat.

## Provenance

The statement specializes the flatness of `𝒜_V` over `A` to the Library's
chart affinoid domains `V`, the closed polydisc loci of radius `radius K m`.
Expressing it for the actual map `PolynomialLocus.algebraMap` into the
Library's chart algebra is a derived choice. The radii lie in `|K^×|`, since
`scale K` is a nonzero element of `K`, so the charts are strictly affinoid.

## Hypotheses

- `K : Type u` is a `NontriviallyNormedField` with `IsUltrametricDist K` and
  `CompleteSpace K`.
- `A : Type v` is any commutative `K`-algebra with
  `P : FinitePolynomialPresentation K A`; zero algebras are allowed.
- `m : ℕ` is arbitrary.

## Why the claim is expected to hold

This is a truth check, not the proof. Write `T = (T₁, …, Tₙ)` with
`n = P.arity` and `r = radius K m`. The Tate algebra `K{r⁻¹T}` is Noetherian,
so every ideal in it is closed. Hence the chart algebra is
`K{r⁻¹T} / I·K{r⁻¹T} ≅ A ⊗_{K[T]} K{r⁻¹T}`, where `I` is the kernel of
`P.map`. Flatness over `A` therefore follows by base change from flatness of
`K[T] → K{r⁻¹T}`. That map is flat: at each maximal ideal of the Tate algebra,
the completions of the two local rings agree, and the map of local rings is
local.

## Scope

The statement asserts flatness, not faithful flatness. A single chart misses
the primes of `A` whose points lie outside the polydisc. It covers only the
Library's polydisc charts, not arbitrary affinoid domains, and says nothing
about local rings of the analytic space.
