# Recognition in the compatible atlas localization

This module proves that the constructed recognition extension is a morphism in the existing category of original compatible strong atlas maps and constructs its isomorphism in that category's localization at actual full affinoid-domain quasi-isomorphisms. Its source is the original atlas with canonical index lifting, and its target is the already constructed recognition atlas. The underlying space remains literally unchanged.

The substantive compatibility proof retains complete bounded algebra maps. Fix an original source chart and two recognized containing charts. Its recognized original chart lies inside both targets. The previously proved bounded naturality identities factor both pullbacks through that common chart. After any bounded map into any presented-affinoid test algebra, the existing common-subchart overlap theorem identifies the two factorizations through an arbitrary other common recognized subchart. This proves `originalRestriction_isMapChartChange`, including all coefficient tests. No equality of algebra maps is inferred solely from equality on spectrum points.

`toRecognition_hasCoherentPullbacks` transports this law through the canonical index lifting. The proved equivalence of coefficient coherence with explicit inverse-image local compatibility supplies `compatibleToRecognition`. The complete original all-admissible-pair bounded family is retained. This proof works for an arbitrary original atlas; it does not assume that the source contains all local domains.

`localizedRecognitionIso` applies the existing localization construction to this actual compatible map and its already proved full-domain quasi-isomorphism property. Its forward arrow is exactly the localization of `compatibleToRecognition`. Both its forward and inverse arrows induce the identity continuous map under the existing descended space functor. The inverse-space identity follows by applying that functor to the isomorphism identity and cancelling the identity forward map.
