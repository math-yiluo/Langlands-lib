# Analytic coefficient automorphisms of scalar-extended spaces

Let \(K\to L\) be an isometric embedding of complete nonarchimedean
fields, and let \(X\) be an analytic space over \(K\). Each isometric
automorphism \(\sigma\) of \(L\) fixing \(K\) determines an actual
semilinear analytic endomorphism of \(X_L\). It is the lift of the
projection \(X_L\to X\) with field component \(\sigma^{-1}\), using the
Cartesian universal property of this projection.
The analytic component is the tower comparison, transported along the
equality of the composite field embedding with \(K\to L\).

The lift of the identity is the identity. Composites are identified by
the same universal property, since they have the required field map
and preserve the projection. Applying the construction to
\(\sigma^{-1}\) gives the inverse. The resulting homomorphism acts
on the left on the scalar-extended analytic space.
