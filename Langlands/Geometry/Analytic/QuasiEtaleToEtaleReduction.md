# Reduction of the morphisms of sites `μ` and `μ_G` to geometric inputs

Author: Worker 1383.

This file proves the published `QuasiEtaleToEtaleStatement` from three
geometric statements about the localized analytic category. It does not prove
those three statements. The main theorem is
`quasiEtaleToEtale_of_inputs`. The mathematics is the standard argument behind
Berkovich's morphisms of sites `μ : X_qét → X_ét` and
`μ_G : X(G)_qét → X(G)_ét` (Berkovich 1996, §1.2). Berkovich states these
morphisms without giving a proof, so the categorical arguments below are
internal to this project.

## Inputs

All three inputs quantify over every complete ultrametric normed field
`K : Type u`. The space universe is `v`, and `G : Type z` is the group universe.

- `LocalizedEtalePullbackStatement.{u, v} K`. For every étale `f : A ⟶ Y`
  and every `g : B ⟶ Y` in `LocalizedCategory.{u, v, v}`, there is a pullback
  square `IsPullback p q f g` whose base-changed projection `q` is étale.
- `LocalizedEtaleStabilityStatement.{u, v} K`. Étale morphisms are stable
  under composition and have `HasOfPostcompProperty etale etale`: if `g`
  and `f ≫ g` are étale, so is `f`. The reduction uses only the second
  component; composition stability comes from the Library instance in
  `LocalizedEtaleComposition`.
- `EtalePullbackGSpaceStatement.{u, v, z} K`. Take a pullback square
  `p : P ⟶ A`, `q : P ⟶ B` of an étale `f : A ⟶ Y` and some `g : B ⟶ Y`,
  with `A`, `B`, `Y` Hausdorff. Let `G` act on `P`, `A` and `B`, continuously
  on `A` and `B`, with `p` and `q` equivariant. Then `P` is Hausdorff and its
  action is continuous.

## Generic flatness

`representablyFlat_changeProp` works in any category `T` and for any morphism
properties `P ≤ P'`. Here `P` contains the identities, is stable under
composition and satisfies `HasOfPostcompProperty P P`. Suppose also that every
`P`-morphism `f` has, along every `g`, a pullback square whose second
projection is in `P`. The theorem then shows that
`MorphismProperty.Over.changeProp X : P.Over ⊤ X ⥤ P'.Over ⊤ X` is
representably flat. It checks the three conditions for the category of
structured arrows `U ⟶ F(V)`, for each `U`:

- Nonempty: the identity of `X` is a `P`-object, and `U.hom` gives an arrow
  `U ⟶ F(X)`.
- Cones over pairs of objects: the base change of the first structure map along
  the second defines a `P`-object. The universal property gives the
  structured arrow, and the two projections give the legs.
- Equalizers of parallel arrows `f, g : A ⟶ B`: this uses the diagonal pattern
  of `geometricPointEqualization_of_etalePullbacks`. Form `D = B ×_X B`. The
  diagonal `d : B ⟶ D` is in `P` by right cancellation against the
  second projection. Take `c = (f, g) : A ⟶ D`, and pull `d` back along `c`
  to get `s : E ⟶ B` and `t : E ⟶ A`, with `t` in `P`. Composing with both
  projections of `D` gives `t ≫ f = s = t ≫ g`. The composites
  `U ⟶ B ⟶ D` through `d` and `U ⟶ A ⟶ D` through `c` agree by the
  uniqueness part of `D`'s pullback property. So the structured arrow
  `U ⟶ A` factors through `E`.

## Generic continuity

`Langlands.CategoryTheory.functorPushforward_pullback_le` shows that, for a representably flat `F`,
`(S.functorPushforward F).pullback (F.map f) ≤ (S.pullback f).functorPushforward F`.
Two structured arrows over a common target are joined by a cospan, using
`IsCofiltered.cospan`.

`coverPreserving_toGrothendieck` uses induction on `Precoverage.Saturate`
(the cases are generating covering, top, pullback and transitivity). It shows
that a representably flat functor is cover-preserving for
`J.toGrothendieck` and a topology `K` if the sieve generated by every
`J`-covering pushes forward to a `K`-covering sieve. The pullback case uses
the previous inclusion. `isContinuous_of_flat` combines this with Mathlib's
`compatiblePreservingOfFlat` and `Functor.isContinuous_of_coverPreserving`.

## Coverings

`exists_chartImages` starts with an étale localized morphism `φ : V ⟶ U` and a
point `y` of `V`. The net property of `V`'s chart family gives finitely many
charts through `y` whose union is a neighbourhood of `y`. Their images under
`φ` form a finite family, and its union is a neighbourhood of `φ y` because
étale morphisms are open (the published `etaleOpenMap`). Each member is the
image of a chart of `V`.

`isContinuous_toQuasiEtaleSite` uses the identity
`Sieve.generate_map_eq_functorPushforward`. It reduces to showing that the
image under `F` of an étale covering family is a quasi-étale covering family.
For each point `x` of the base, the étale covering supplies an arrow `f` of the
family and a point `y` with `x = f y`. The arrow `f` is étale by right
cancellation against the étale structure map of its target, so
`exists_chartImages` supplies the finite family of chart images required by
`mem_quasiEtalePrecoverage_iff`. The equivariant theorem
`isContinuous_ofEquivariantEtaleSite` is the same argument after passing
through `EquivariantQuasiEtaleSite.toQuasiEtaleSite` and the underlying
analytic morphism.

## Equivariant pullbacks

`exists_gSpace_pullback` constructs pullbacks in `GSpace K G`. Let `f` be an
étale `G`-morphism. Pull back its underlying morphism by the first input. The
published `CategoricalPullback.pullbackAction` defines an action of `G` on the
pullback that makes both projections equivariant (`pullbackAction_fst`,
`pullbackAction_snd`). The third input makes this pullback a `G`-space. The
square of `G`-morphisms is a pullback because the forgetful functor
`GSpace.forget = ObjectProperty.ι _ ⋙ Action.forget _ G` reflects limits:
`ι` is fully faithful, and Mathlib provides the reflection instance for
`Action.forget`.

## Conclusion

`representablyFlat_toQuasiEtaleSite` and
`representablyFlat_ofEquivariantEtaleSite` apply the generic flatness theorem.
For `G`-spaces, composition stability and right cancellation pass to
`GSpace.etale = etale.inverseImage GSpace.forget`. The continuity theorems
then follow from the generic continuity theorem.
`quasiEtaleToEtale_of_inputs` assembles the four results for every `K`, every
`X` and every group `G` with a topology. It concludes
`QuasiEtaleToEtaleStatement.{u, v, z}`. Its axioms are `propext`,
`Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.
