# Finite original-chart refinements on compact Hausdorff domains

Fix an actual indexed affinoid atlas `a` over a complete ultrametric normed
field `K`, on a topological space `X`. The field, point and chart-index
universes remain independent. The published `OriginalChartDomain a`
consists of subsets locally covered, in the finite relative neighborhood
sense, by contained original charts of `a`. These domains have constructed
subspace affinoid atlases and form a category under inclusion.
`quasinetTopology a` is the published Grothendieck topology on this
category, with the actual quasinet covering condition.

For a domain `S`, assume explicitly that `S.carrier` is compact as a subset
of `X` and that the subspace `S.carrier` is Hausdorff. There is no
Hausdorff assumption on all of `X`. For every covering sieve `R` on `S`,
`compactChartRefinement` constructs a finite set of original charts
contained in `S` whose inclusion arrows already belong to `R`. Their
generated sieve is covering and is contained in `R`. The construction
allows empty domains and empty finite families.

## Compactness and finite closed covers

`AtlasTopology.LocallyRefines.exists_finite_cover` is a topological
auxiliary result. If a family `υ` locally refines the singleton family
`{S}` and `S` is compact, there is a finite subfamily of `υ` with union
exactly `S`. At each point of `S`, the local refinement supplies finitely
many sets contained in `S`, all containing the point, whose union is a
relative neighborhood. Mathlib's
`IsCompact.elim_nhdsWithin_subcover'` selects finitely many of these
unions. The proof combines their finite witnessing families and proves
both inclusions of the resulting union equality. This extraction needs
no Hausdorff hypothesis.

`LocallyRefines.exists_finset_cover` retains indices of an arbitrary
supplied family `D : ι → Set X`. It chooses an index for each member of
the finite extracted family and takes the finite image of those indices.
Its conclusion is an actual `Finset ι` with
`(⋃ i ∈ I, D i) = S`. The index universe is independent.

`AtlasTopology.isQuasinet_of_finite_closed_cover` proves that a finite
family of closed subsets covering a space is a quasinet. For a point `x`,
keep the members containing `x`. The union of the remaining members is
closed and misses `x`. Its complement is an open neighborhood contained
in the union of the retained members. This produces the required finite
quasinet witness, including the requirement that every retained member
contains `x`.

## Original charts already in a sieve

`OriginalChartDomain.SieveChart R` is the subtype of actual original
charts contained in `S` whose inclusion arrow is a member of `R`.
`SieveChart.domain`, `SieveChart.inclusion` and `SieveChart.mem` expose
the corresponding domain, inclusion and sieve membership.
`sieveChartFamily R` is their family of actual underlying subsets.

`sieveChartFamily_locallyRefines` proves that this family locally refines
every domain belonging to `R`. For such a domain `V`, its defining domain
condition gives finite relative neighborhoods by original charts
contained in `V`. Each such chart is contained in `S`; downward closure
of `R` puts its inclusion in `R`. The same finite neighborhoods therefore
give the refinement. `IsCovering.sieveChartFamily` composes this
refinement with the covering condition of `R`. These two results do not
require compactness or Hausdorffness.

For `I : Finset (SieveChart R)`, `finiteChartSieve R I` is the sieve
generated by the selected chart inclusions. `finiteChartSieve_le` proves
it lies in `R` by downward closure.

`finiteChartSieve_mem_of_covers` assumes only Hausdorffness of `S` and
that the finite selected chart subsets have union `S`. Each chart subset
is compact: it is the continuous image of its actual affinoid spectrum.
Its inverse image in the subspace `S` is compact, because that inverse
image has the same image in `X`. Hausdorffness makes it closed in `S`.
The finite closed cover lemma makes these chart subsets a quasinet on
`S`, and the published relative-refinement characterization proves that
their generated sieve is covering. Thus finite point coverage is used
here with a proved closedness condition.

## Constructed finite refinements

`exists_finite_chart_cover` applies compactness to the locally refining
family of original charts already in the covering sieve. It obtains
finitely many chart indices with union exactly `S`, then applies the
finite-chart covering theorem. `compactChartRefinement` chooses a finite
set satisfying the resulting covering conclusion;
`compactChartRefinement_mem` proves its generated sieve covers `S`.
Together with `finiteChartSieve_le`, this is the asserted covering
refinement inside `R`.

`mem_quasinetTopology_iff_finite_chart_cover` characterizes covering
sieves on compact Hausdorff `S` by the existence of such a finite
original-chart covering sieve inside them. The forward implication is
the construction just described. The reverse implication is
monotonicity of the topology under inclusion of sieves.

## Source and provenance

The source is Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic
spaces* (1993), registered as `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA-256
`06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`.
Section 1.1, printed p.13 / PDF p.10, defines quasinets and explicitly
includes Hausdorffness in the source's compact-space convention.
The remarks following Lemma 1.1.1, printed p.14 / PDF p.11, discuss
quasinets of compact subsets in Hausdorff spaces. Definition 1.3.1,
printed p.23 / PDF p.20, gives the finite relative neighborhood condition
for analytic domains. The G-topology paragraph in section 1.3, printed
p.25 / PDF p.22, defines coverings as quasinet families of domains.

This module is a derived finiteness construction on the published
fixed-original-chart domain site. The proof uses the local domain and
quasinet conditions, Mathlib compactness, and compactness of the actual
affinoid charts. It is not presented as a separately numbered assertion
of the source. Its explicit subspace Hausdorff hypothesis is retained
throughout the finite-cover result. The module does not construct a
maximal atlas, identify the fixed-original-chart domains with all
analytic domains, or establish coherent, structural, étale or equivariant
sheaf results.
