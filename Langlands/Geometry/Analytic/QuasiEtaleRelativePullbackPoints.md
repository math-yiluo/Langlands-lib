# Point lifting over compact quasi-étale objects

For a cospan over a compact quasi-étale object, the proof takes the
product of the two sources over the ambient base and the square of the
intermediate object over that same base. The diagonal of the
intermediate object is an analytic-domain embedding. Its inverse image
in the first product maps into the given relative pullback by the
pullback universal property.

A compatible ordinary pair lifts to compatible field points over one
complete algebraically closed field. They lift to the product over the
ambient base by field-point preservation of quasi-étale pullbacks. Their
image in the square of the intermediate object is the diagonal field
point because its two projections determine it. The inverse-image
domain square lifts that field point to the diagonal cut. Its image in
the given relative pullback has the prescribed two ordinary projections.
This is an internal consequence of the compact diagonal and field-point
pullback theorems, with independent field and space universes.
