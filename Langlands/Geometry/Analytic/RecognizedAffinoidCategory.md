# Actual restrictions and full-net recognized affinoid subsets

This module constructs bounded restrictions and the actual algebra/spectrum diagrams on recognized literal subsets. An inclusion supplies no output algebra map: it determines the bounded map by conjugating the already constructed special-section restriction with the two constructed finite-presentation equivalences. The entire bounded maps satisfy identity and composition. Their spectrum maps commute with the embedded charts, and the canonical coordinates form a natural isomorphism from the literal-subspace inclusion diagram to the actual spectrum diagram. The natural isomorphism uses Mathlib's `TopCat.uliftFunctor` on both diagrams to place their independent ambient and algebra universes in one common universe.

The module then applies this construction to the existing `saturatedAtlas`. That atlas was constructed from actual local full-domain presentations and has exactly the original atlas's enlarged full local-domain family as its chart family. Consequently `FullSpecialSubset` uses finite special covers from the barred full domain net of Berkovich 1993, and `RecognizedAffinoidSubset` applies all three recognition conditions to that family. Every original local domain belongs by its actual singleton covering. Its unchanged algebra is bounded equivalent to the new finite presentation by the constructed local-domain comparison and original section-algebra recovery. The whole recognized family is compact Hausdorff memberwise and is a quasinet because it contains the enlarged full-domain net.

This is derived infrastructure for Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), the paragraph preceding Proposition 1.2.13 and the construction in part (ii), printed p.21 / PDF p.18. The registered source is `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`. The page was read and visually checked. The module reuses the already constructed enlarged atlas and full-domain closure instead of assuming such an atlas or replacing the full net by the smaller original-index family.

The coefficient field is complete and ultrametric, with arbitrary valuation, and the coefficient, ambient and original-index universes are independent. Zero algebras and empty domains remain allowed. No unproved special-intersection target is imported. This module does not yet prove that every inclusion of recognized subsets is a full affinoid-domain representation, prove the intersection quasinet condition for the recognized family, or package the full maximal atlas. Those remain construction obligations; the thin category here already retains the actual constructed bounded restriction on every arrow.
