# Ordinary ring relations from finite affinoid kernel pairs

The finite Banach tensor comparison identifies the bounded affinoid
pushout with the ordinary tensor product, keeping both factor maps.
For an analytic kernel pair whose source and two projections are
presented by small affinoid spectra, its universal property yields
bounded maps for the diagonal and interchange of factors. For the
composition of two composable pairs, take the bounded pushout of
the adjacent coefficient maps. Finiteness of the first of these
maps gives smallness and the ordinary ring pushout property.

A pair of ring maps is related if it comes from a ring map
out of the presented kernel pair algebra. The bounded diagonal
and interchange maps prove reflexivity and symmetry. To prove
transitivity, use the ordinary ring pushout to combine the two
witnesses and compose with the bounded map obtained from the
analytic kernel pair property. The test ring is any commutative
ring in the coefficient universe.

The two legs of the ring pushout generate the pushout algebra as a
ring. Their images still generate any surjective bounded quotient,
which includes the coefficient restriction to a clopen relation.
When the two projections are algebraically finite étale and the
cover factors a finite Banach étale map to a small affinoid base,
`finiteEtaleAffinoidQuotientOfLocalizedKernelPair` applies the
finite étale equalizer construction. It retains the factorization
of the base map, the finite Banach bounds and the ordinary ring
pushout for the relation.
