# Étale slices of compact analytic frames

Where a compact quasi-étale map restricts to an étale map over an open base, every finite nonempty fibre power restricts to an étale map over the same open base. The one-coordinate power is isomorphic to the original source. A power with one more coordinate is the pullback of that source and the preceding power. This follows from the two universal properties on analytic test arrows. Restrict this pullback to the base open and apply the base change and composition properties of étale maps.

The distinct-coordinate locus is relatively closed over the same open base. In the restricted self-pullback the diagonal is open because the original map is étale there. The two projection equations identify that diagonal with the open restriction of the original diagonal, and the factorization criterion for points identifies their ordinary images under restriction. The relatively closed distinct locus is the intersection of the inverse images of the complements of these open diagonal images.

This is a derived step of the ordered-frame proof of finite Galois refinement. The étale diagonal, base change, and germ context are those of Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), Proposition 3.3.7, Corollary 3.3.8 and Theorem 3.4.1, printed pp. 63 and 65.
