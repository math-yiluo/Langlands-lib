# Rational enlargement of an atlas with directed original charts

Let `K` be a complete ultrametric normed field, `X` a topological space, and `a : IndexedAffinoidAtlas K X` an actual indexed affinoid atlas. The coefficient, ambient and original index universes are independent. For each original index `i` and each actual finite positive-polyradius rational chart `d` over `a.algebra i`, `locus a i d` is the ambient image of its rational locus. `Index a` consists of these literal ambient subsets. Its chosen original index and rational presentation are used only to supply the actual chart algebra and spectrum map; repeated presentations of the same subset give one index. The output index universe is the ambient universe.

`chart_image_pullback` proves that pulling a rational presentation back along the bounded original restriction from `k` to `i` gives the intersection of the rational ambient locus with the original chart range of `k`. The proof uses the original chart square and injectivity of the containing original chart. `chart_image_inter_pullback` applies the explicit rational intersection presentation to two such pullbacks on a chart contained in both original charts.

`isNet_family` proves that the family of all these local rational loci is a net. This part requires no directedness. Whole rational charts retain every original chart range, so the original quasinet remains a quasinet. For two local rational loci in original charts `i` and `j`, the original net supplies finitely many original chart ranges in the original overlap, all containing the chosen point and with union a relative neighborhood. Cut each of those ranges by the two rational loci. The pullback and intersection identity supplies an actual local rational presentation for each cut. Their finite union is the intersection of the original relative neighborhood with the new overlap, hence is a relative neighborhood there. The contained-member characterization of a relative quasinet proves the intersection axiom.

For the algebraic construction assume `hd : a.HasDirectedCharts`: any two original chart ranges are contained in some original chart range. `Index.directedDomain` retains the chosen rational quotient as a full represented domain in its chosen original chart. `chart` composes its actual rational structural spectrum map with the original chart map. Its range is the literal index subset, and it is an embedding by the published rational spectrum embedding. Thus the chart ranges have no duplicates. No assumption about embeddings for arbitrary full-domain spectrum maps is used.

`inclusion` uses the published directed-domain construction to supply the actual bounded restriction whenever two ambient loci are contained. `inclusion_universal` proves full relative factorization for any arrow in that represented-domain category: extend the two domains, with their algebras unchanged, to one common original upper chart. The exact extension-image formula and injectivity of that original chart put the composite test map into the smaller extended domain. Its full representation supplies a factorization. Full representation uniqueness for the larger extended domain proves the entire bounded factorization equation, using the arrow's equation over the common upper chart. Full uniqueness for the smaller domain proves uniqueness of the factorization. Tests range over every finite positive-polyradius presented affinoid algebra. Equality is equality of whole bounded homomorphisms. The chart square follows from directed-domain naturality; identity and composition follow from the actual domain category, whose uniqueness was proved from the full universal properties.

`atlas a hd` packages the actual quotient algebras, embedded spectrum charts, proved net, bounded restrictions and full relative universal properties into an `IndexedAffinoidAtlas K X`. `atlas_chartFamily` identifies its net exactly. `atlas_ofOriginal_range` retains each original literal chart range; `atlas_ofChart_range` includes every rational locus in every original chart. `atlas_hasDirectedCharts` proves that the enlarged atlas is again directed: choose an original common upper chart and use its whole rational locus as an upper chart of the enlargement. No greatest original chart is required.

This is a derived rational-domain specialization of Berkovich's atlas extension construction, with an additional pairwise directedness hypothesis for the bounded algebra maps. The topological argument follows the finite overlap cuts in Berkovich, *Etale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), Section 1.2, Proposition 1.2.6, printed pp.17–18 / PDF pp.14–15; Definition 1.2.3 and Remark 1.2.4(i), printed p.16 / PDF p.13, give the atlas and restriction conventions. The general source construction includes every affinoid domain and uses finite coverings and Tate acyclicity for bounded restrictions. Here only local rational loci are added, and directedness permits full factorization over a common original upper chart. This does not assert unrestricted affinoid saturation, general finite gluing, a structure sheaf or an analytic etale site.

Berkovich, *Non-archimedean analytic spaces* (Trieste lectures, 2009), Section 2.2, Definition 2.2.1 and Exercise 2.2.2(ii)–(iii), printed/PDF p.9, give the exact closed-image universal property, rational domains and intersection conventions. The imported actual rational presentations and full domain representations implement these conventions. The new relative universal-property proof and the directedness statement are internal consequences of the published concrete constructions, rather than assumptions about the output atlas.
