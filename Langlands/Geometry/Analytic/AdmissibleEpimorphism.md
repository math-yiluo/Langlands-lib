# Admissible bounded epimorphisms and actual norm-controlled lifts

This module defines admissible bounded epimorphisms using the actual fiber quotient seminorm and proves an equivalent criterion in terms of uniformly controlled preimages. It proves admissibility for identity maps, composites, maps with an actual bounded section, all affinoid presentation quotient maps, and the constructed absolute affinoid coproduct diagonal.

The original definition is Berkovich, *Spectral Theory and Analytic Geometry over Non-Archimedean Fields* (1990), §1.1, printed p.11 / PDF p.25. The source defines the residue seminorm as the infimum of representative norms and calls a bounded homomorphism admissible when this quotient seminorm is equivalent to the image seminorm. The registered source is `provided-a03a0182668f6b92b01deacac09a9992b3edf55e23a0a2194554384ae44bd8d4`; the actual PDF's SHA256 is `9b543e7ffae8ec6febf154a60c4d11a288f7ecc3795c66f004ba3200a6824e79`. The project literature copy and the Central-Library original have identical bytes.

The geometric motivation is Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §1.2, printed p.20 / PDF p.17, special coverings and Lemma 1.2.12. Its registered source is `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`. This module supplies admissibility infrastructure; it does not establish the general special-cover intersection theorem or identify the affinoid coproduct with the projective completed tensor product.

The source definition is specialized to surjective bounded algebra homomorphisms between the project's actual normed commutative algebras. The zero target is permitted as a useful degenerate case. Coefficient, source, target and third-algebra universes are independent. The abstract criterion and composition results require neither completeness, ultrametric norms nor a nontrivial coefficient valuation.

`residueNorm f b` is the infimum of the real norms of all elements mapping to `b`. For a surjection these fibers are nonempty, and each is a coset of the kernel. This is the source's quotient seminorm expressed by representatives, without choosing a representative or a quotient algebra presentation. The seminorm comparison is used only under surjectivity, where every fiber is nonempty. `preimageNorms_bddBelow` bounds the set by zero, `preimageNorms_nonempty` records nonemptiness for each image element, and `residueNorm_le` bounds the infimum by any specified representative norm.

`norm_le_mul_residueNorm` uses the bounded-map constant to bound the target norm by a fixed positive multiple of the fiber infimum. It divides by the positive constant, takes the resulting lower bound through the infimum, and multiplies back. `IsAdmissibleEpi` includes surjectivity and positive constants bounding the two seminorms in both directions, exactly the norm-equivalence condition in the cited definition.

`isAdmissibleEpi_iff_normControlledLifts` proves the full criterion. A uniformly bounded preimage family bounds the residue norm from above, while the preceding bounded-map argument supplies the other comparison. Conversely, for each nonzero target element the residue-norm bound and the infimum property supply a representative of norm less than twice the bound. The zero target element uses the actual zero preimage. The factor two avoids an assumption that an infimum is attained.

`isAdmissibleEpi_of_bounded_section` applies the norm bound of an actual bounded right inverse. Identity admissibility follows by the identity preimages. `IsAdmissibleEpi.comp` lifts first through the second map and then the first, obtaining the product of their positive constants.

`presentationMap_isAdmissibleEpi` uses the actual quotient norm: a nonzero quotient element has a representative of norm less than twice its norm, and zero lifts to zero. This applies to every finite positive-polyradius affinoid presentation without coefficient completeness. `AffinoidCoproduct.diagonal_isAdmissibleEpi` uses the previously constructed constant-one lifting estimate of the split diagonal; completeness of the coefficient field is retained for that coproduct construction. Neither proof invokes an open mapping theorem or infers admissibility from bare surjectivity.
