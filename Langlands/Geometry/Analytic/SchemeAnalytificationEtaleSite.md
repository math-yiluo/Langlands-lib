# Analytification on separated algebraic étale objects

For a separated scheme $X$ locally of finite type over a complete nonarchimedean field $K$ with nontrivial norm, `SeparatedAlgebraicEtale K X` is the category of étale schemes over $X$ whose sources are separated over $K$. The functor `separatedAlgebraicEtaleForget` sends each object to the same étale morphism in Mathlib's small algebraic étale category. The separatedness restriction is used to construct localized analytification by gluing affine open charts.

`Localized.etale_map` implements the forward direction of Berkovich's étaleness comparison for separated schemes locally of finite type over $K$. It restricts to affine source charts subordinate to affine target charts, uses Mathlib's étaleness of the coordinate-ring map, and applies the hypothesis `StrongEtaleSmallIndexComparisonStatement.{u}` to the localized affine analytification. Under the same hypothesis, `separatedEtaleAnalytification` sends algebraic étale objects and arrows to their actual analytifications. `separatedEtaleAnalytification_cover` sends algebraic étale covering families with separated sources to covering families of the ordinary analytic étale site using the range of the analytic maps over each algebraic center. A comparison of sheaf inverse images requires further pullback results.
