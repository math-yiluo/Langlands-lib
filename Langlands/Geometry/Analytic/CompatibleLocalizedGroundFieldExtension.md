# Coherent scalar extension on localized compatible analytic atlases

Scalar extension along an isometric embedding of complete nonarchimedean
fields sends compatible strong quasi-isomorphisms to quasi-isomorphisms.
The functor therefore descends to the localization of compatible analytic
atlases. The localized identity comparison is induced by the actual
identity-extension projection, which is a quasi-isomorphism. The tower
isomorphism descends the chartwise bounded comparison of direct and
successive extensions.

The localization functor retains the objects. At each such object, applying
it to the unit and associativity equations on compatible atlases gives the
corresponding equations for the localized comparisons.

V. G. Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces*
(1993), §1.4, printed p. 30 (PDF p. 27), uses scalar extension to define
analytic arrows that change the ground field. Literature ID
