# The special-set full local-domain intersection target

`SpecialSetIntersectionStatement` is the unproved target for the special-subset intersection theorem needed in the maximal-atlas construction. It is a proposition definition, rather than an axiom or a theorem with an unfinished proof.

The source is Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), Lemma 1.2.12, printed p.20 / PDF p.17. The bar over the net in both its hypotheses and intersection conclusion is visible in the original page image. It refers to the full local affinoid-domain enlargement constructed in Proposition 1.2.6, printed pp.17–18 / PDF pp.14–15; the original indexed atlas itself need not contain every full local domain. The registered source is `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, PDF SHA256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`.

The completed tensor and admissibility meanings are those of Berkovich, *Spectral Theory and Analytic Geometry over Non-Archimedean Fields* (1990), §1.1, printed pp.11–12 / PDF pp.25–26. The registered source is `provided-a03a0182668f6b92b01deacac09a9992b3edf55e23a0a2194554384ae44bd8d4`, PDF SHA256 `9b543e7ffae8ec6febf154a60c4d11a288f7ecc3795c66f004ba3200a6824e79`.

The target quantifies over every complete ultrametric normed coefficient field, ambient topological space and actual indexed affinoid atlas, with independent coefficient, ambient-point and original-index universes. Given a literal special subset and two actual full local domains whose ambient loci lie in it, the conclusion constructs an actual full local domain representing their literal intersection. `LocalDomain` is a full affinoid-domain presentation in an original chart, so this is membership in the source's enlarged net. The statement does not require that the intersection already have an index in the original smaller net.

From the output domain's literal locus equality, the imported full-domain inclusion construction provides the two actual bounded restrictions from the unchanged input domain algebras to the intersection algebra. The genuine projective completed-tensor lift of these two maps must be an admissible epimorphism, meaning surjectivity and uniform equivalence of its actual fiber quotient seminorm with the target norm. This is a conclusion, not a supplied property of an arbitrary intersection object.

The original proof first refines compact cross intersections by finite original charts, constructs finite algebras on the corresponding product covering, and applies Kiehl's descent theorem. The current modules provide actual chart covers, tensor completions and presentations, bounded full-domain inclusions and the admissibility criterion. This statement retains the remaining intersection existence and tensor admissibility proof. It neither assumes a finite-module descent result nor claims to have proved Lemma 1.2.12 or the maximal-atlas construction.
