# The analytic comparison for extension of rational points

Let \(L/K\) be an isometric extension of complete nontrivially valued
ultrametric fields, with no restriction on degree. Let \(X\) be a
separated algebraic space locally of finite type over \(K\), with
an analytification \((Y,p)\). Assume
`SpaceAnalytificationGroundFieldStatement`.

`CompleteAnalyticExtensionPointEmbeddingStatement` gives an
analytification of \(X_L\) on the actual scalar extension \(Y_L\).
The analytic point associated to the extension of \(x\in X(K)\)
projects back to the analytic point of \(x\). This equality holds
with either ground-field presentation used to form the rational
point maps. The original algebraic map \(X(K)\to X_L(L)\) is a
closed embedding for the étale-chart topologies.

This is the complete nonarchimedean comparison used in the proof of
Proposition 5.11(3), last paragraph on p. 19, of Brian Conrad,
*Weil and Grothendieck approaches to adelic points*,
`conrad-2011-weil-grothendieck-adelic-points`, SHA-256
`fe4a9193aff4ef575665b40971f3da32f850a74d87b3f2d0fa28bba26b9cbabb`.
The inputs are the analytification of \(X\) and the ground field
comparison of Conrad–Temkin, Theorem 2.3.5.
