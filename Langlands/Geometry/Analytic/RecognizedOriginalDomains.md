# Original charts as full domains of recognized affinoid subsets

This module constructs a full affinoid-domain representation for every original chart contained in a recognized special subset. The representing algebra is the unchanged original chart algebra. Its structural map is the actual bounded restriction from the constructed section algebra, transported by the recognized finite presentation.

The actual spectrum map has the expected ambient chart equation. For a finite chart equalizer, choose the already constructed finite subordinate original cover of the contained original chart. On each subordinate chart, the proved full bounded restriction identity identifies spectrum comap with the canonical union point map. Surjectivity of that finite cover gives the identity on the entire original spectrum. Transporting it by the constructed finite presentation gives the recognized coordinate identity, spectrum injectivity, and the exact inverse-image locus.

The full-domain proof uses the witnessed covering-domain condition of recognition, rather than assuming every contained original chart is already a domain. Use the finite subordinate cover again, this time subordinate to that witnessed special cover. Its members are full domains of the original source chart. Each refined map from the recognized algebra equals the original restriction to one witnessed component followed by that component's actual map. The equality is proved for entire bounded maps by equalizer-comparison naturality. Composition of the two known full representations gives a full representation for each refined composite. The finite-source recognition construction then proves the full universal property of the original restriction, constructing bounded test factors by finite descent.

This is derived infrastructure for Berkovich 1993, Proposition 1.2.13(ii), printed p.21 / PDF p.18, the step treating an original-net member contained in a recognized affinoid subset. The registered original is `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`. The page was read and visually checked. The implemented route uses finite original subordinate charts and full-domain bounded descent; it does not assume the special-set intersection theorem invoked by the source's shorter route.

The coefficient field is complete and ultrametric, and its universe is independent of the ambient and original-index universes. Trivial valuations, nonreduced or zero algebras and empty domains remain allowed. The ambient space need not be Hausdorff. The constructed subordinate covers use Hausdorffness only on the actual special union. Full representation of arbitrary recognized inclusions and the maximal-atlas package remain subsequent constructions.
