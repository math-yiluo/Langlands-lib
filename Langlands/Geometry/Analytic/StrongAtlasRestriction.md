# Strong maps restricted to arbitrary admissible chart domains

This module works over a field `K` with `NormedField K`, `IsUltrametricDist K` and `CompleteSpace K`, and two actual `AffinoidAtlasPresentation` objects in the same coefficient, space and index universes. For any strong map `f`, source chart `i` and target chart `j` whose range contains the image of `i`, it constructs a strong map between the actual chart-domain subspace presentations. Every original chart contained in the two chosen domains remains available. It imports the published `StrongAtlasMaps` and `ChosenAtlasRestriction`.

This is derived infrastructure for the all-admissible-pair convention in Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §1.2, Definition 1.2.7, printed p. 18 / PDF p. 15 (registered source `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`). Definition 1.2.3 and Remark 1.2.4(i), printed p. 16 / PDF p. 13, supply the underlying atlas restrictions. The construction follows directly from these supplied data and the published actual chart-domain atlases; it is not a separately numbered assertion of the source.

`restrictBaseMap` restricts the original continuous map to the two actual chart subspaces. The outer admissibility hypothesis proves that it takes values in the target subspace. `restrictBaseMap_admissible_iff` proves that a pair of contained charts is admissible in these subspaces exactly when its original pair is admissible. The forward proof forgets subtype membership in a chart-range witness, and the reverse proof restores it by subtype extensionality.

`restrictChart` keeps precisely the bounded pullback `f.pullback c.val d.val` for every admissible pair of contained charts. The preceding equivalence supplies its original admissibility proof. Each source chart has the greatest chart of the target chart presentation as an available target, since its image is contained in the image of the outer source chart. The chart square descends by subtype extensionality. Reflecting source and target containments reduces the restriction square to the original strong-map square.

`restrictChart_apply_val` identifies the original point map after the target inclusion. `restrictChart_pullback` is equality of the original bounded algebra maps. `restrictChart_id` proves that restricting the strong identity to the same source and target chart gives the constructed strong identity on that chart presentation. Its proof uses strong-map extensionality; both underlying maps and all bounded pullbacks agree definitionally.

The remaining lemmas give precise compatibility with the chosen-map interface and nested domain inclusions. If the ambient target has a greatest chart `t`, `toChosen_restrictChart` identifies the restriction of `f.toChosen t ht` with the chosen map obtained from the strong restriction to `t`, selecting the greatest chart in that actual target-domain atlas. The maps, indices and bounded pullbacks are definitionally equal.

For nested source charts `i ⊆ i'`, with both images in a fixed target chart `j`, `chartMap_restrictChart_toChosen` proves that the source inclusion followed by the chosen restriction at `i'` is the chosen restriction at `i`. Every source index keeps the same original algebra and the same bounded pullback to `j`. Extensionality proves the equality. `restrictChart_source_naturality` then applies the published greatest-chart construction `ofTopChart` to that composite and proves equality with the whole strong restriction at `i`. It uses the chosen-map equality followed by `ofTopChart_toChosen`, so it recovers every admissible pullback.

For nested target charts `j ⊆ j'` containing the image of a fixed source chart, `restrictChart_toChosen_chartMap` first follows the chosen restriction at `j` by the target inclusion, and then applies `withTargetChart` to select the greatest chart of the `j'` presentation. It proves equality with the chosen restriction at `j'`. This coarsening is part of the statement: the category of chosen maps retains selected chart indices. After extensionality on base maps and indices, the bounded-map equality is precisely the original `pullback_comp_restriction` equation. `restrictChart_target_naturality` applies `ofTopChart` to this coarsened map and recovers the entire strong restriction at `j'`, again by `ofTopChart_toChosen`.

The domain choices may be any admissible pair, independently of a monotone chosen target function. These nested comparisons use the existing chosen-map composition and greatest-chart realization; they do not define a general composition operation on strong maps. Extended atlases, localization, general analytic domains, structure sheaves and étale sites remain separate construction obligations.
