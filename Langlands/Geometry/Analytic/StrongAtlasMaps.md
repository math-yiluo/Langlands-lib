# All-admissible-pair bounded atlas maps and greatest target charts

This module works over a complete ultrametric normed field `K`, with two actual `AffinoidAtlasPresentation` objects `A` and `B` in the same coefficient, space and index universes. It defines local strong-map data on these supplied atlases and constructs those data from every monotone chosen atlas map whose target has a greatest chart. It imports the published `AtlasRestriction` and, through it, `ChosenAtlasMorphisms`.

The source is V. Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §1.2, Definition 1.2.7, printed p. 18 / PDF p. 15 (registered source `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`). That definition requires a continuous map, a containing target chart for every source chart, and compatible affinoid morphisms for every admissible source/target chart pair. Definition 1.2.3 and Remark 1.2.4(i), printed p. 16 / PDF p. 13, supply the underlying atlas restrictions. The greatest-chart construction below is derived infrastructure using those conventions and the previously implemented domain universal property; it is not a separately asserted theorem of the source.

`StrongHom A B` contains a continuous `baseMap` and proves that each source chart has some target chart containing its image. For every pair `i,j` and proof that the image of source chart `i` lies in target chart `j`, its `pullback` is a bounded `K`-algebra homomorphism from the algebra of `j` to the algebra of `i`. `chart_comm` identifies the resulting map of actual Banach spectra with `baseMap` in chart coordinates. `restriction_comm` requires equality of bounded algebra maps whenever both source and target charts are enlarged. Thus compatibility retains algebraic information that an equality of spectrum-point maps alone would not detect. This record uses the project's supplied indexed atlases and presented affinoid algebras; it does not add extended atlases or construct the source's entire analytic-space category.

`StrongHom.ext` compares the continuous map and the dependent family of bounded pullbacks; the remaining fields are propositions. `StrongHom.id` constructs the identity using the actual atlas restriction for each admissible pair. The restriction composition law proves its compatibility square. `pullback_comp_restriction` specializes compatibility to a fixed source chart, with the source restriction identity removed.

Suppose `t` is a target index and `ht` proves that every target chart range is contained in its range. `StrongHom.image_chart_top` shows that each source-chart image lies in `t`, by combining `target_exists` with `ht`. `StrongHom.toChosen` then chooses `t` for every source index and retains precisely those pullbacks. Its chosen-target monotonicity is reflexive, and its restriction square follows from strong-map compatibility and the target restriction identity.

For an existing monotone chosen map `f : A ⟶ B`, `Hom.withTargetChart f t ht` constructs the corresponding chosen map with constant target index `t`. At source index `i`, its bounded pullback is `f.pullback i` composed with the target restriction from `t` to `f.indexMap i`. Its chart square uses the atlas restriction square and the original square for `f`. Associativity, the original compatibility for `f`, and composition of target restrictions prove its restriction square.

`StrongHom.ofTopChart f t ht` constructs the pullback for every admissible target `j` by `Hom.localPullback` applied to `f.withTargetChart t ht`. The hypotheses hold because `j` lies in `t` and the supplied admissibility proof places the source-chart image in `j`. This local pullback is obtained from the full domain factorization property for every presented affinoid test algebra, applied to the actual algebra of source chart `i`. Its chart square is `localPullback_chart`, and `localPullback_naturality` proves compatibility under both source and target restrictions. No family of missing local pullbacks or gluing output is assumed.

`ofTopChart_pullback_comp_restriction` gives the defining factorization equation through `t` as an equality of bounded algebra maps. `ofTopChart_pullback_selected` recovers exactly `f.pullback i` at the original selected target `f.indexMap i`, using uniqueness of this factorization. Conversely, `ofTopChart_toChosen` proves that applying the construction to `f.toChosen t ht` recovers an arbitrary strong map `f` in full. After extensionality, the proof uses `restriction_hom_ext` to compare each local pullback after composition with the restriction from `t`; the defining factorization equation and `pullback_comp_restriction` give that equality. This uniqueness argument uses algebraic domain factorization.

The last declarations supply actual targets for this construction. `chartTopIndex B j` is the original index `j`, viewed among all original charts contained in `j`. `chartTop_contains` proves that it is greatest in the actual subspace atlas of `B.chartPresentation j`. Consequently `Hom.toChartStrongHom` constructs every admissible local bounded pullback from any chosen map into that chart-domain presentation.

General composition of strong maps, extension to larger atlases and localization remain separate construction obligations. Berkovich's Proposition 1.2.8 and the following composition construction on printed p. 18 / PDF p. 15 use further affinoid domains and finite gluing; this module does not implement those steps. It also does not construct general analytic domains, structure sheaves, étale sites or the Harris tower.
