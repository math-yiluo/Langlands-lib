# Strong atlas composition through greatest charts

This module constructs composition of actual all-admissible-pair strong maps when the intermediate and final supplied indexed affinoid atlases have greatest charts. It proves all local bounded-map formulas, independence of the greatest-chart choices, identity and associativity. It then gives a category whose objects are actual atlas presentations with a greatest chart and whose morphisms are the full strong maps. Every actual chart-domain presentation supplies an object of this category.

The coefficient field `K` is a complete ultrametric normed field. All atlas presentations use the same coefficient, space and index universes. The only project import is the published `StrongAtlasMaps`, which includes the actual chart-domain atlas construction and the monotone chosen-map category. This module does not depend on the separately submitted `StrongAtlasRestriction`.

The source convention is V. Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §1.2, Definition 1.2.7 and the composition construction following Proposition 1.2.8, printed p. 18 / PDF p. 15, registered source `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`. Definition 1.2.7 requires bounded affinoid morphisms at every admissible chart pair, compatible with restrictions. The following composition construction treats arbitrary atlases by extending them and using finite affinoid gluing. The present greatest-chart construction is derived local infrastructure using the same strong-map convention. Its proof instead uses the published bounded domain factorization property; it does not prove the source's general extension or composition theorem.

`StrongHom.restriction_comp_pullback` specializes the supplied two-variable compatibility square to a fixed target chart. The resulting equality says that composing the larger source chart's pullback with the source restriction gives the smaller source chart's pullback. `toChosen_injective` proves that choosing the greatest target chart determines the entire strong map: applying `ofTopChart` to either chosen map recovers the original full strong map by `ofTopChart_toChosen`.

For `f : StrongHom A B`, `g : StrongHom B C`, and greatest indices `b` of `B` and `c` of `C`, `compOfTopCharts f g b hb c hc` first forms the actual chosen composite `f.toChosen b hb ≫ g.toChosen c hc`, then applies `ofTopChart` at `c`. Its underlying continuous map is exactly `g.baseMap.comp f.baseMap`, as recorded by `compOfTopCharts_baseMap`. Every further admissible target pullback is constructed by the actual atlas domain universal property, tested on the actual source-chart presented affinoid algebra. No unconstructed gluing map is supplied as an assumption.

`compOfTopCharts_toChosen` proves that the composite chosen at `c` is exactly the original chosen composite, including the selected indices and bounded maps. Hom extensionality reduces this to `ofTopChart_pullback_selected`. The stronger `compOfTopCharts_pullback` handles any source index `i`, intermediate index `j` and target index `k` for which `f` sends chart `i` into `j` and `g` sends all of chart `j` into `k`. At every such pair the composite pullback equals `f.pullback i j hf` composed with `g.pullback j k hg`. The statement also accepts an admissibility proof for the composite, which exists from those two image conditions; proof irrelevance makes its particular choice immaterial. The theorem does not require that every admissible composite pair admit such an intermediate chart. The definition constructs those remaining pullbacks through `c`.

The proof of this formula compares bounded algebra maps after precomposition with the restriction from `c` to `k`, using `restriction_hom_ext`. The defining factorization equation gives the product of the pullbacks through `b` and `c`. Target naturality for `f` and fixed-target source naturality for `g` replace `b` by `j`. Target naturality for `g` then gives the product through `k`. These are equalities of actual bounded algebra maps, rather than an inference from spectrum points.

`compOfTopCharts_eq` proves independence of both supplied greatest chart indices and their containment proofs. After applying `toChosen_injective` at the first target index, the preceding formula computes both sides using the same intermediate and target indices. `id_compOfTopCharts` proves the left identity when the source and target have greatest charts; its local equality is `restriction_comp_pullback`. `compOfTopCharts_id` proves the right identity using the greatest intermediate chart also as final chart, then removes its actual identity restriction. Independence permits changing these choices. `compOfTopCharts_assoc` proves full strong-map associativity with greatest charts in the three intervening presentations. Choosing the final greatest chart reduces it to associativity in the published chosen-map category, and injectivity recovers the entire family of admissible pullbacks.

`TopChartPresentation` contains an actual `AffinoidAtlasPresentation`, a greatest chart index and its containment proof. It introduces no new analytic-space axioms. `topChart_surjective` derives that this chart covers the actual space from the atlas net's covering theorem. `topSpectrumHomeomorph` therefore identifies that space with the actual Banach spectrum of its greatest-chart algebra, using the supplied chart embedding and the proved surjectivity. The category instance has `StrongHom` morphisms, the existing strong identity, and the constructed greatest-chart composition; the proved laws furnish its category laws. `baseMap_id` and `baseMap_comp` give the actual continuous maps of identity and composition. `spaceFunctor` retains these actual spaces and maps in `TopCat`. Finally, `ofChart A i` uses `A.chartPresentation i`, `A.chartTopIndex i` and `A.chartTop_contains i`; it retains every original chart contained in `i`, with its original algebra and restrictions.

General composition for atlases without greatest charts, extended atlases, localization, general analytic domains, structure sheaves, étale sites and the Harris tower remain separate obligations. This local category supplies a constructed strong-map category on actual greatest-chart presentations; it does not claim the full category of analytic spaces.
