# Preservation of étale and quasi-étale morphisms under scalar extension

`GroundFieldExtensionEtaleStatement` concerns any extension \(K\to L\) of
complete nonarchimedean normed fields. For a morphism \(f\) in the localized
\(K\)-analytic category, its image is the map of localized spaces constructed
by scalar extension of the affinoid chart algebras and their bounded
pullbacks. The proposition states that this map is étale if \(f\) is étale,
and quasi-étale if \(f\) is quasi-étale. The two fields may have independent
universes.

V. G. Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces*
(1993), Corollary 3.3.8, printed p. 63 (PDF p. 60), gives étale preservation
under extension of the ground field. Literature ID
`berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA-256
`06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`.

V. G. Berkovich, *Vanishing cycles for formal schemes* (1994), Lemma
3.1(i), printed p. 544 (PDF p. 6), gives the quasi-étale preservation
property. Literature ID `berkovich-1994-vanishing-cycles-formal-schemes`,
SHA-256 `f4f7bc00cc46a0a5f72a69d7bbb220bce1d3ed9fd465aeda629f37dfc054bed1`.
