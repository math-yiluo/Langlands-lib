# Compact inverse images of compatible pairs in localized pullbacks

`localizedProjectionPair` records the two underlying point
projections as a continuous map. The compact-preimage property
consists of Hausdorffness of the pullback space and compactness of the
inverse image of every compact set of factor pairs with coincident
base images.

The canonical small strong fibre product has the property by the
quotient chart-system theorem and its small-space homeomorphism. The
property transports through an isomorphism of the whole square. The
factor homeomorphisms carry the compact set to a compact set. The
base homeomorphism and the commutative square equations preserve
coincidence, and the pullback-point homeomorphism identifies the two
inverse images.

For a quasi-étale first leg, the proof chooses its strong
representative, a right fraction for the second leg, and the
recognition presentations. The domain–étale chart smallness theorem
constructs the small strong product, whose full universal property
identifies it with the supplied pullback. Hausdorffness of the
factors passes through the comparison isomorphisms. The proof
applies over an arbitrary localized analytic base.

The result combines the Library's categorical pullback and
quasi-étale smallness constructions with the compact-preimage
theorem on the glued product.
