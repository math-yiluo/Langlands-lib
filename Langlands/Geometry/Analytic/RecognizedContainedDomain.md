# Recognized affinoid subsets inside an original chart

This internal construction treats a recognized affinoid subset whose literal
locus is contained in one original chart. It makes that subset a full represented
domain of the original chart algebra, retaining the recognized algebra and an
actual bounded structural map.

The structural map is obtained by the existing bounded descent operation.
Its component maps are the original restrictions from the containing chart to
each original chart inside the recognized subset. The atlas composition law
proves their compatibility. The descent theorem gives equality of their entire
bounded composites with the canonical original restrictions.

The spectrum equation follows by writing a point in a member of the chosen
finite gluing cover. It proves injectivity and identifies the spectrum image
with the inverse image of the recognized locus in the containing chart.
Finally, the actual finite domain cover of the recognized algebra reduces the
full represented-domain property to the original atlas restrictions. Thus the
universal property is proved on all presented affinoid tests, without trying
to recover algebra maps from their action on points. No finiteness or
étaleness assertion is made.
