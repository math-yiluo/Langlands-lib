# Affinoid realizations of finite étale relation quotients

Let `A → B` be an algebraically finite étale bounded map of presented
affinoid algebras over a complete ultrametric nontrivially normed field.
Suppose two algebraically finite étale bounded maps `B ⇉ C` agree on
`A`, their images generate `C` as a ring, and they induce an
equivalence relation on ring maps out of `B` for every test ring.

The two `A`-algebra maps have an equalizer finite étale over `A` by
the published finite étale equalizer theorem. The equalizer inherits
its norm from `B` and is closed by continuity of the two maps. The
open mapping theorem gives uniform coefficient lifts, so the finite
Banach presentation theorem realizes that equalizer by a presented
affinoid algebra. Its map into `B` is injective, and its structural
map followed by this inclusion agrees with the original map.

The inclusion and the two relation maps satisfy the hypotheses of the
Library's invariant-ring quotient theorem for a finite locally free
equivalence relation. This gives finite faithful flatness and the
ordinary ring pushout square. Unramifiedness of the inclusion follows
from unramifiedness of `A → B`. Finiteness over the affinoid algebra
gives finite presentation, and hence the inclusion is algebraically
finite étale. Its Banach finiteness and injectivity give surjectivity
on bounded spectra by lying over.

This is a derived affinoid realization of the published algebraic
equalizer and invariant-ring results. The pushout is at the level of
ordinary rings. Recognition of its spectrum in an ambient analytic
space requires further geometric input.

The construction for a supplied finite Banach map over an arbitrary complete ultrametric normed field is given in [Finite Banach étale affinoid quotients](FiniteBanachEtaleAffinoidQuotient.md).
