# The quotient argument for scheme analytification gluing

This internal reduction concerns the published `SchemeAnalytification.Space` and its
actual affine charts. It is derived from their quotient construction, rather than
asserted as a separate result from the literature. It retains the explicit
`RestrictionGluingData K f` hypothesis: local restriction maps preserve identity
and composition, are open embeddings, and have range equal to the inverse image
of the smaller affine open under the local center map. This file does not prove
those local inputs or the separatedness criterion.

`CommonRefinement` means that two chart points are images of a single point in an
affine open contained in both charts. Reflexivity uses identity of local
restrictions. For transitivity, the two witnesses have equal centers in the middle
chart. An affine neighborhood of that center in the intersection of their charts
supports lifts of both witnesses, by the local range hypothesis. Injectivity of
the restriction map to the middle chart identifies those lifts, and compatibility
with composition gives a common refinement of the original endpoints.

Every generating gluing step has a common refinement. Induction on the equivalence
relation generated by the gluing steps gives `ι_eq_iff_common_refinement`. The
reverse direction is the published compatibility `ι_localMap`. Applying this
criterion to two points in one chart proves `injective_ι`.

The preimage in a chart `V` of the image of a set in a chart `U` is the union,
over common affine refinements `W`, of the image under restriction to `V` of
the preimage under restriction to `U`. Local open embeddings therefore show that
each `ι` is an open map. Continuity and the proved injectivity give
`isOpenEmbedding_ι`. The same equality criterion proves `range_ι_inter`, the
overlap formula in the published gluing statement.

When the scheme is affine, every chart maps into the top chart. The published
compatibility of `ι` with restriction proves `surjective_ι_top` without any
local hypotheses. Combining surjectivity with the conditional chart embedding
proves `isHomeomorph_ι_top`. All results hold in every universe and keep the
complete ultrametric nontrivially normed field and locally finite type hypotheses
of the published space.
