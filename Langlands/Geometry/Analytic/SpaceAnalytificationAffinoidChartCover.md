# Shared chart covers by affinoid-algebra points

Let `K` be a complete, ultrametric, nontrivially normed field. Given two
localized maps from the same source into analytifications of separated,
locally finite type algebraic spaces over `Spec K`, there is a saturated
presentation with localization isomorphic to the source and a quasinet
cover by its charts. On each small chart spectrum, the two given maps,
precomposed with the chart inclusion and the source isomorphism, are the
affinoid point comparisons of points valued in that chart algebra.

Normalize the target presentations and take strong representatives of
both arrows on a common quasi-isomorphism source. For each point of each
source chart, choose target charts containing the strong images. The
target chart neighborhood theorem gives smaller charts with the affinoid
point property. Continuity of the two bounded coefficient maps pulls
both neighborhoods back to the original source chart. A subchart in
their intersection has the required two point maps. These subcharts
locally refine the source chart family, which is a quasinet. Naturality
under the target isomorphisms gives the claimed identities.

This is an internal consequence of the affinoid point comparison,
localized strong representatives and chart neighborhood refinement.
