# Canonical section restrictions for Hausdorff finite unions

This module constructs bounded restrictions and canonical bounded algebra equivalences from literal inclusion or equality of finite unions of original charts. Each defining family retains the `FiniteChartGluingData` hypothesis that its own pairwise intersections are represented by original charts. Neither cross intersections between the two families nor subordinate covers are supplied. The containing literal union is Hausdorff; the ambient space need not be Hausdorff. Field, ambient-point and chart-index universes remain independent.

The source motivation is Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §1.1, printed pp.13–14 / PDF pp.10–11, net refinement and Lemma 1.1.2(ii), and §1.2, printed pp.20–21 / PDF pp.17–18, the cover-independent Banach section algebra and spectrum map after Lemma 1.2.12, followed by the recognition conditions preceding Proposition 1.2.13. The registered source is `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`.

This is derived infrastructure for Hausdorff literal unions. It does not assert unrestricted compact refinement in a non-Hausdorff containing union, general special-cover admissibility, affinoidness of the equalizer, or recognition of arbitrary finite unions. The source's compact Hausdorff special sets motivate the explicit separation hypothesis. The actual input algebras and original bounded restrictions are preserved.

`t2Space_locus_of_subset` transfers Hausdorffness along the topological embedding of a contained literal union. `comparisonCoverOfHausdorff` constructs finite subordinate original-chart covers for every target member and every target overlap. Each is contained in the source union by literal inclusion, using the overlap's containment in its left member. The previous compact-intersection construction therefore applies to each parent. All fields of the resulting `ComparisonCover` are geometric chart-cover data.

`restrictionTo` uses these constructed covers and bounded descent to construct the actual map between section equalizers. `projection_comp_restrictionTo` identifies its target coordinates with the constructed restrictions to the unchanged original algebras. `restrictionTo_eq_comparison` proves independence from every other geometric comparison-cover choice. `originalRestriction_index` recovers each defining projection and yields `restrictionTo_self`.

`originalRestriction_comp_restrictionTo` proves compatibility with restriction to any original chart contained in the target union. Its proof separates algebra elements on the target's subordinate original-chart cover. Full original-map recovery identifies the target restriction on each such chart; projection recovery and original-chart naturality identify the resulting source maps with the direct source restriction. No equality of algebra maps is inferred from spectrum points. Applying this identity to the target coordinates proves `restrictionTo_comp` for three nested unions. The identity and composition laws assume Hausdorffness where the two successive source restrictions are formed.

`comap_spectrumMap_restrictionTo` identifies the pullback along the constructed bounded restriction with the two canonical finite-union point maps and the literal subspace inclusion, at every point of the contained union. It applies the previously proved comparison formula to the constructed cover system.

`sectionAlgEquivOfLocusEq` transfers Hausdorffness across equality of literal unions, constructs covers in both directions, and obtains an actual bounded algebra equivalence from the proved inverse laws. Only the source union's Hausdorffness is an input. Its forward bounded map is `restrictionTo`. The spectrum homeomorphism is contravariant, from the target section spectrum to the source section spectrum. `sectionAlgEquivOfLocusEq_spectrumMap` and `spectrumMap_eq_transportOfLocusEq` identify it with the two canonical point maps over the same ambient points.

The final two equivalences transport injectivity and surjectivity of the canonical map across equality of Hausdorff literal unions. Surjectivity is onto the entire Banach spectrum, rather than just the already proved compact image. These properties remain recognition conditions; this module proves that their truth is independent of the chosen defining family.
