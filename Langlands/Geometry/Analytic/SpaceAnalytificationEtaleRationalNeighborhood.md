# Rational points near a rational point of an affine étale chart

Let `K` be a complete ultrametric nontrivially normed field and let `(Y, p)`
be an analytification of a separated algebraic space `X` over `Spec K`.
For a presentation `P₀` of `K`, `EtaleChartRationalNeighborhoods p P₀`
says that every algebraic `K`-point of a polynomially presented affine
étale test has an ordinary analytic open neighborhood `U` in the test.
If `z ∈ U` maps to `rationalPoint p P₀ y` for an algebraic `K`-point `y` of
`X`, then `z` is the analytic point of a `K`-algebra map from the test
algebra to `K`. The theorem `IsAnalytification.etaleChartRationalNeighborhoods`
proves this property for every `P₀` in arbitrary universe `u`.

The affine self-pullback of the chart is closed in the product by
separatedness. Its diagonal section is an open immersion because either
projection is étale. On coordinate rings the section is a split
surjection whose kernel is generated by an idempotent `e`. The subset of
the affine self-pullback analytification where `|e| = 1` is closed by
continuity of seminorm evaluation. Its joint image in the ordinary product
of the chart analytifications is closed by the affine closed-map theorem.

That image misses the repeated pair of the given rational chart point.
A normed-field realization of a seminorm above this pair restricts on
each factor to the ground-field evaluation, composed with the scalar
inclusion. This follows by evaluating the norm on the difference of an
element and its rational value. The two restrictions determine the map
on the whole self-pullback algebra because the tensor-product map is
surjective. The realization therefore factors through the diagonal and
sends `e` to zero. The neighborhood `U` is the inverse image of the
complement of the closed joint image under `z ↦ (z, z)`.

For a point `z` as in the conclusion, the analytic pullback over `y` has
an ordinary point above `z` and the identity point of `K`. The algebraic
pullback is affine finite étale over `K`. A field realization of the
seminorm on its algebra factors through a prime quotient, which is a
finite separable field over `K`. If this field has degree one, its
evaluation gives the required `K`-point of the chart. If it has higher
degree, two distinct embeddings in the complete algebraic closure give
the same seminorm by uniqueness of the extended norm. Their
restrictions to the chart are distinct because the fibre algebra is a
quotient of the chart algebra. The restrictions agree over `X` and
hence factor through the algebra of the self-pullback. The resulting
evaluation sends `e` to one because it is off the diagonal. Its
seminorm realizes a point with joint ordinary image `(z, z)`.
Membership in `U` excludes this case.

This is an internal consequence of the affine pullback and closed-map
constructions, ordinary analytic pullback point lifting, affine
seminorm realization and the finite étale algebra results used in the
proof.
