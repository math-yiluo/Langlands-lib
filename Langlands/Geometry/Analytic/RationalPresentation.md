# Proof of bounded extension for a rational presentation

Author: Worker 36.

`RationalPresentation.boundedExtension` proves the separate
`BoundedExtensionStatement`. The actual enlarged Tate quotient, bounded
base map, coordinate equations, algebraic unit-ideal condition and point
locus come from Definitions.

In the quotient, the coordinate equations make each f_j a multiple of
the image of g. The preimage of the principal ideal generated by that
image contains g and all f_j, hence contains one under the unit-ideal
condition. The principal ideal is the whole ring, proving g is a unit
in the quotient. For a point in the norm locus, if g had value zero,
the inequalities would put every f_j in the spectrum kernel. The unit
ideal would lie in this proper kernel, a contradiction. This proves
denominator nonvanishing on the locus. Forward spectrum inclusion
uses the same coordinate equations and the norm bound one for each
new Tate coordinate; this step needs no unit-ideal hypothesis.

For a bounded base map phi and a bounded-monomial tuple b solving
phi(g) b_j = phi(f_j), append b to the old coordinate images. The
finite comparison of power bounds with monomial bounds proves that
this combined tuple has bounded monomials. Convergent Tate evaluation
on the enlarged algebra agrees with phi on the old-variable inclusion,
by coordinate uniqueness. It therefore kills the old relations and,
using the supplied equations, the new ones. The relation-quotient
lift constructs `liftFromBase`. Surjectivity of the base projection
proves its restriction is phi; evaluation of a variable gives the
new-coordinate formula.

For uniqueness under the unit-ideal hypothesis, equal base restrictions
give equality on old coordinates. Apply the target maps to the new
coordinate equations and cancel the image of the already proved unit g
to obtain equality on new coordinates. The relation quotient's coordinate
uniqueness then identifies the maps. This argument does not require
completeness or ultrametricity of the target. The constructed lift and
uniqueness prove `existsUnique_extension`. The closing theorem assembles
all five clauses with exactly their separate hypotheses.

The Definitions and Statement Markdown explain the derived source scope.
This proof retains explicit bounded solutions and the algebraic unit-ideal
condition. It establishes no converse from absence of spectrum zeros,
no full analytic-domain factorization, and no structure sheaf or site.
