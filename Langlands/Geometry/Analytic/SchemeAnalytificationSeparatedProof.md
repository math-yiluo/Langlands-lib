# Proof: separatedness and Hausdorff scheme analytification

The theorem
`Langlands.Analytic.SchemeAnalytification.schemeAnalytificationSeparated`
proves the unchanged published
`SchemeAnalytificationSeparatedStatement.{u}` in every universe `u`.

Let `K : Type u` be a complete ultrametric nontrivially normed field, `X : Scheme.{u}`,
and `f : X ⟶ Spec (CommRingCat.of K)` a morphism locally of finite type. The theorem proves

`IsSeparated f ↔ T2Space (Space K f)`

for the published glued quotient space `Space K f`. It assumes neither quasi-compactness nor
affineness, reducedness, or nonemptiness of `X`.

## Dependencies

The module imports the unchanged target statement and
`SeparatedAnalytificationForward`, which in turn imports `SeparatedAnalytificationReverse`
and `SeparatedAnalytificationDensity`. These three helper modules are part of the same
contribution. Each has its own Markdown file that describes its proof. The helpers use
the published Library modules on affine analytification (seminorm comparison, centers,
morphisms of charts), the published gluing reduction `GluingReduction` and Hausdorff
reduction `SchemeAnalytificationHausdorffReduction`, and Mathlib. Neither the proof nor
the helpers use `separatedStatement_of_gluingStatement` or any proof of
`SchemeAnalytificationGluingStatement`.

## Proof

The theorem introduces the quantified data and proves the two implications separately.

* **Separated implies Hausdorff.** `SeparatedForward.t2Space_of_isSeparated K f`. Under
  separatedness, the intersection `U ∩ V` of two affine opens is affine, and its coordinate
  ring is generated by restrictions of sections from `U` and from `V`. It follows that each
  chart relation `{(x, y) | ι K f U x = ι K f V y}` is the range of the restriction pair map
  from the analytification of `U ∩ V`. This range is closed by a limit argument along an
  ultrafilter. Because the coordinate ring is generated by restrictions, the seminorms stay
  bounded along the ultrafilter. The published seminorm comparison then realizes the limit
  seminorm as a point. The charts are open maps, by the published `GluingReduction.isOpenMap_ι`
  applied to restriction gluing data. That gluing data is proved in the forward module from
  the published basic-open results. With open charts, the published
  `t2Space_iff_chartRelationClosed` gives Hausdorffness.
* **Hausdorff implies separated.** `SeparatedReverse.isSeparated_of_t2Space K f`. A scheme
  locally of finite type over a field is locally Noetherian, so `f` is quasi-separated, and
  Mathlib's valuative criterion reduces separatedness to uniqueness of lifts. Two lifts
  `Spec R ⟶ X` from a valuation ring land in affine opens `U` and `V`. Closedness of the
  chart relation, which follows from Hausdorffness, is combined with the density lemma of
  `SeparatedAnalytificationDensity`. This produces a pair of related points. Their common
  center forces both lifts into `U`. There they correspond to ring maps out of the sections
  of `U` that agree after composing with the injective map from `R` to its fraction field
  `F`, so the lifts are equal.

model `Space K f`. It makes no assertion about analytic separatedness or the analytic
structure.
