# Finite compact refinements for the compact-probe quasi-étale topology

`compactProbeQuasiEtaleCompactRefinements` proves
`CompactProbeQuasiEtaleCompactRefinementsStatement` for the quasi-étale site
over any localized analytic base \(X\). If \(S\) covers a compact Hausdorff
object \(U\), testing \(S\) at the identity of \(U\) gives a finite jointly
surjective family \(f_i \colon V_i \to U\) in \(S\) with compact Hausdorff
sources.

For any test arrow \(g \colon C \to U\) with compact Hausdorff source,
`compactQuasiEtalePullbacks` gives compact Hausdorff site objects
\(C \times_U V_i\) and pullback squares in the localized analytic
category. Each projection to \(C\) belongs to the pullback of the
generated sieve along \(g\), since its composite with \(g\) factors
through \(f_i\).

For a point \(x\) of \(C\), joint surjectivity gives \(i\) and a point
\(y\) of \(V_i\) with \(g(x)=f_i(y)\). The structure map \(U\to X\) and
the composite \(C\to U\to X\) are quasi-étale by the site object and
morphism conditions. The lemma
`exists_point_of_isPullback_over_compactQuasiEtale` lifts the pair
to a point of \(C \times_U V_i\) over \(x\). The projections are therefore
jointly surjective and pass the compact-probe test for the sieve
generated by the \(f_i\).

The field universe and the common space and atlas-index universe are
independent.
