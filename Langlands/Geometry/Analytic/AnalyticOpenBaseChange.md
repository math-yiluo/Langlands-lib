# Base change to an analytic open domain

Restrict an arrow over an analytic base to the inverse-image domains of an open subset. The pullback property of the inverse-image domain gives this restriction. It commutes with the inclusion into the original source and with the projection to the restricted base.

For any pullback over the base, the restricted projections again form a pullback. To lift a pair of test arrows, first lift their composites to the original factors and then factor that lift through the inverse-image domain of the original pullback. Projection identities and the monomorphism of the domain inclusion prove uniqueness. The pointwise image criterion follows because a preimage point of an arrow over the base lies over the same base point. Finally, when the two restricted base maps are étale, the composite of the restricted pullback to the open base is étale by stability under base change and composition.

These are derived consequences of the analytic fibre product and analytic open-domain base change. See Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), Proposition 1.4.1, printed pp. 29–30, and Corollary 3.3.8, printed p. 63.
