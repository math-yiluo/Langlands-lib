# Uniformity on analytic morphisms

`morphismUniformSpace Y X` gives the actual localized analytic morphisms
`Y ⟶ X` the uniformity generated by finite families of the Library's chart
tests `IsNear`. `morphismUniformity_hasBasis` identifies the entourages in
that basis. The empty family gives the whole relation. Union of test families
refines intersection of their entourages. Each entourage contains the diagonal,
is symmetric and contains its own composite. Reflexivity follows by
precomposing identity nearness with the morphism being tested. The other
properties use symmetry and transitivity of `IsNear` on localized morphisms.

Precomposition with any analytic morphism is uniformly continuous, by
`IsNear.precomp`. For postcomposition, compact inverse images of the chart
domains give finitely many tests controlling a composite. Taking the union of
these tests for a finite family proves uniform continuity under that compactness
condition. In particular, an analytic isomorphism has the required compact
inverse images because its underlying map is a homeomorphism.

`automorphismTopology X` is induced along the map from `Aut X` to `X ⟶ X`
that sends an isomorphism to its forward morphism. Finite test families centred
at an automorphism give a neighbourhood basis. In particular, the basis at the
identity consists of finite intersections of the `nearIdentity` subgroups.
Multiplication is continuous by combining precomposition with postcomposition
by a fixed isomorphism and applying transitivity. For inversion, start with
near automorphisms `b` and `a` in a family controlling postcomposition by
`a⁻¹`. Then `a⁻¹ ∘ b` is near the identity. Precomposing both morphisms with
`b⁻¹` gives nearness of `a⁻¹` and `b⁻¹` in the desired test. For a topological
group `G`, `isContinuousAction_iff_continuous` identifies the identity test
condition `IsContinuousAction ρ` with continuity of the action homomorphism
`ρ : G →* Aut X`.

This is the fixed-ground-field construction on the Library's saturated chart
presentations. The field is complete and ultrametric and may carry a trivial
valuation. The uniformity laws use the chart tests directly and require no
Hausdorff hypothesis on the underlying spaces. The source treats Hausdorff
analytic spaces and allows morphisms that change the ground field. The
chart-test presentation and its mathematical relation to the source's compact
domain tests are described in [ContinuousAnalyticAction](ContinuousAnalyticAction.md).
