# Analytic étale Galois coverings given by deck graphs

`AnalyticGaloisCover Γ p` records a surjective étale analytic map `p`, a group `Γ` acting by deck automorphisms, and an actual analytic self-pullback of `p`. The deck graphs have pairwise disjoint images that cover the space of the self-pullback. This is the graph form of the Galois covering condition. The construction accepts a group of any size and is used with finite groups in the Galois germ application.

Each graph is an open analytic immersion by applying the étale diagonal theorem to the pullback with its second projection reidentified by the inverse of the deck automorphism. Ordinary point lifting in the analytic pullback shows that the deck action is transitive on ordinary fibres. For two analytic arrows with the same composite to the base, their map to the self-pullback takes each point into one of the graph images. The inverse image of that open graph gives a neighborhood on which the arrows differ by the corresponding deck automorphism.

The definition is derived from the constant-group torsors called étale Galois coverings in Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §4.1, in the paragraph following Proposition 4.1.8 on p. 85. Its graph description is the analytic form of the product decomposition used by Milne, *Étale Cohomology* (1980), Chapter III, §2, Example 2.6, printed pp. 99–100.
