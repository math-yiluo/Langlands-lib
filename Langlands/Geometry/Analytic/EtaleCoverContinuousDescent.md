# Continuous map descent along localized étale coverings

Author: Worker 790.

`EtaleCoverContinuousDescentStatement` concerns a jointly surjective
family of localized étale maps to `S` and local analytic maps from the
covering objects to `T`. The local maps agree after precomposition with
every pair of localized arrows whose composites to `S` agree. Under
these hypotheses there is a unique continuous map from the underlying
space of `S` to that of `T` that restricts to the given point maps.

For two local points with the same image, the published pullback
existence theorem along étale maps supplies a categorical pullback.
The published matching-point result lifts the pair to that pullback.
The equality of the local analytic composites then identifies their
point values. Choosing a cover point above each point of `S` defines the
continuous map's point function with this restriction property.

The inverse image of an open set is the union of the images, under the
cover maps, of its local inverse images. The published openness of étale
maps and continuity of the local maps make this union open. Joint
surjectivity gives uniqueness.

## Provenance

This internal argument uses the published pullbacks along étale maps,
matching-point lifting and étale openness in the actual localization.
The field is complete and ultrametric, with trivial valuations allowed.
The field, space and chart index universes coincide as required by the
matching-point theorem. The cover index universe is independent. The
conclusion concerns the underlying continuous map. Descent of its
bounded coefficients remains a geometric obligation.
