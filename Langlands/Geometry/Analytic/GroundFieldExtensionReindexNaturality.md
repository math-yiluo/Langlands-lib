# Naturality of the scalar-extension reindexing homeomorphism

Reindex a compatible strong map at both endpoints by reading all of its original data at the corresponding original indices. The local compatibility equation on each inverse-image domain is then the equation for the original indices. This permits applying the actual scalar-extension functor to the reindexed map.

`reindexHomeomorph_compatibleMap` verifies that the two relabeling homeomorphisms commute with the two actual extended maps on the quotient point spaces. Represent the source point in one of the reindexed extended charts; choose an admissible target chart there and read it in the original target. The two formulas for the extended map use the same bounded pullback, so their representatives agree after relabeling. For every corresponding pair that is admissible on both sides, `compatibleMap_reindex_pullback` directly identifies the whole scalar-extended bounded pullbacks. These are equalities on the Library's quotient construction, for arbitrary complete ultrametric normed fields in the stated extension and independent chart-index universes. There is no assertion of localization or analytification in this adapter.
