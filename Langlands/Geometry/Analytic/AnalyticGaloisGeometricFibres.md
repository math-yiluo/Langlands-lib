# Geometric fibres detect analytic Galois coverings

Every ordinary point has a full point above it over a complete algebraically closed value extension. Choose a chart containing the point, pass from its completed residue field to a complete algebraic closure, and extend the resulting norm-preserving chart evaluation to the full point. The chosen value extension lies in the universe of the base field.

Suppose a surjective analytic étale map has an action over the base which is simply transitive on full geometric fibres for these value fields. Its deck graphs partition the ordinary points of its analytic self-pullback. To obtain coverage, lift an ordinary point of the relation to a full point. Its full projections have the same base value and hence are related by a deck element. Full point preservation of the étale pullback identifies the original full relation point with the graph of that element. For disjointness, any ordinary graph intersection admits a full point with lifts along both graphs. Each graph is open because it is a section of the étale first projection. Comparing the first projections identifies the two lifted full source points. Comparing the second projections and applying simple transitivity identifies the deck elements.

This criterion is a derived analytic form of the graph decomposition in Milne, *Étale Cohomology*, Chapter III, §2, Example 2.6, printed pp. 99–100. The preservation and openness arguments use étale pullbacks and the open diagonal in Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), Proposition 1.4.1 and Proposition 3.3.7, printed pp. 29–30 and 63.
