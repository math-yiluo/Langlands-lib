# From original chart data to a saturated strong morphism

The original polynomial atlas is reindexed into the universe of the saturated
source. A local target choice in this reindexing gives an original chart of the
saturated polynomial presentation. Compose each supplied bounded pullback with
the bounded identity from the saturated chart algebra to the original one.
The saturated restriction squares move these casts to the common upper
target chart. The given restriction equation then proves compatibility of the
transported pullbacks.

Directed atlas reconstruction assembles the transported data into a complete
strong morphism. To extract its coordinate-algebra maps, compute in the selected
original charts. The selected pullback formula gives the transported bounded
map, and its cast cancels the inverse cast from extraction. This recovers the
supplied coordinate-algebra map on each source chart.

This is an internal application of the original-chart restriction identities
under saturation and the reconstruction theorem for directed atlases.
