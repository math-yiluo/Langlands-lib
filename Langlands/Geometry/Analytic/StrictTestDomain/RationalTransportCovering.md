# Finite coverings in the rational transport category

Author: Worker 36.

Fix an ultrametric normed field K and a presented strict affinoid algebra P.
The imported `RationalTransport K P` has chosen unit-radius rational
presentations as objects. Its arrows retain a bounded algebra automorphism
label g and require the point action by g to send the source locus into the
target locus. This point action uses the inverse algebra map. Composition
of f followed by g has label `g.label * f.label`.

`FamilyCovers D f` requires every point x of the target locus to have the
form `(f i).label • y` for some index i and some point y of the source
locus `(D i).data.locus`. Thus the covering condition uses the actual
images of the arrows, including their labels. `IsFiniteCovering S` says
that S contains such a family with a finite index type in the scalar
universe. The index type may be empty. These predicates require neither
completeness nor an assumed covering topology.

For the topology construction K is complete. Given arrows `f : V → W`
and `g : U → W`, `refinement f g` is the actual rational presentation

```
V.data.inter ((f.label⁻¹ * g.label) • U.data).
```

The intersection uses the published all-products denominator/numerator
construction. `refinementLeft` maps it to V with label one.
`refinementRight` maps it to U with label `g.label⁻¹ * f.label`.
The published incidence equivalence for simultaneous translation of
rational data and spectrum points verifies the second map's image
condition. `refinement_comm` proves equality of the two composites to W
by cancelling labels; it does not assume the transport category is thin.
These definitions provide the square needed for the covering proof.
No categorical universal property for this square is claimed here.

`isFiniteCovering_top` uses the singleton identity family. For
`IsFiniteCovering.pullback`, refine every arrow of a chosen finite cover
by the displayed construction. The commutative square and downward
closure put the first projections in the pulled-back sieve. For a point
x of V, the original covering condition supplies a preimage y of
`f.label • x`. Applying the relative inverse action places x in one of
the refinement loci, so their identity-labelled projections cover V.

`IsFiniteCovering.transitive` chooses a finite covering family over each
member of the first cover. The dependent sum of those finite index types
is finite. Composing the two families gives arrows in the required sieve;
the group action law composes their point preimages. This argument needs
no completeness assumption. `finiteTopology` packages the three proved
axioms as a Mathlib `GrothendieckTopology` on `RationalTransport K P`.

`mem_finiteTopology` exposes the defining sieve predicate.
`ofArrows_mem_finiteTopology_iff` characterizes the sieve generated by a
finite arrow family: it covers if and only if that family's labelled
point images cover. For the forward direction, factor a covering-sieve
arrow through one of the generators and use the factor arrow's image
condition. The reverse direction takes the generating family itself.
`bot_mem_finiteTopology_iff` says that the empty sieve covers exactly the
objects with empty locus, using the empty index type for one implication.

`familyCovers_inclusion_iff` identifies covering by ordinary rational
inclusions with the published `RationalData.FamilyCovers`: every such
arrow has label one. `inclusion_coverPreserving` then proves that the
ordinary rational inclusion functor preserves the two finite covering
topologies, by pushing the witnessing finite family into the image sieve.
This is a covering-preservation statement; no continuity or equivalence
of sheaf categories is asserted.

This is a derived extension of Worker 36's published `RationalCovering`
to the published `RationalTransport` category. It uses the actual
`RationalLimits` intersections and `RationalAutomorphism` incidence
lemmas. The existing quotient-algebra presheaf is available by import,
but this module proves no sheaf condition or analytic descent. The site
is restricted to chosen unit-radius rational presentations over one fixed
base algebra. It keeps distinct labels, distinct presentations with equal
loci, empty loci, trivial scalar valuations and degenerate quotient
algebras. It supplies neither a topological-group action nor the full
analytic G-topology, etale site, quasi-etale site or their equivariant
versions. In particular, no claim about the full Harris/Berkovich
cohomological construction follows from this finite rational topology.

The original geometric source context is Berkovich, *Non-archimedean
analytic spaces*, Trieste lectures (2009), printed/PDF p. 9, Definition
2.2.1 and Exercise 2.2.2(ii)-(iii), and p. 10, the finite-affinoid-cover
discussion immediately before Fact 2.2.6. These passages give rational
domains, stability under intersection and finite covering families.
The labelled refinements and covering-sieve topology above are internal
categorical constructions from the published rational geometry, not a
claim that the source defines this particular presentation category.
