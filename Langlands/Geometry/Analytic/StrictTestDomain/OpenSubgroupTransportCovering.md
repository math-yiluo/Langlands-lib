# Finite rational covers with varying open subgroups

Worker 36 constructed the topology and comparisons. The retained-label
category and refinement pattern extend the published `RationalTransport`
construction, whose label-category pattern adapts Worker 13's `ActionOpens`;
both workers are credited.

Fix a complete ultrametric normed field `K`, a presented strict affinoid
algebra `P`, a topological group `G`, and its `RationalSpectralAction G P`.
The scalar and group universes are independent. The imported
`OpenSubgroupTransport` category has objects `(U,d)`, with `U` an actual open
subgroup and `d` a chosen unit-radius rational presentation whose locus is
preserved by `U`. Its quotient algebra is the constructed closed-ideal Tate
quotient. An arrow labelled `g : G` carries the subgroup inclusion
`gUg⁻¹ ≤ V` and maps the source locus into the target locus by `ρ(g)`.
The spectrum action uses the inverse algebra map. Composition of `f`
followed by `g` has label `g.label * f.label`.

## Covering families and refinement

`FamilyCovers D f` means that every point `x` of the target locus is
`ρ((f i).label) • y` for an index `i` and a point `y` of the corresponding
source locus. Thus it uses the actual images of the labelled arrows.
`IsFiniteCovering S` requires the sieve `S` to contain such a family with
a finite index type in the scalar universe. The index type may be empty.
Neither predicate assumes a topology or an analytic descent theorem.

Given `f : V → W` and `g : U → W`, put `h = f.label⁻¹ * g.label`.
`refinementSubgroup f g` is the open subgroup

```
V.subgroup ⊓ conjugateOpenSubgroup U.subgroup h.
```

`refinement f g` equips the actual rational presentation

```
V.domain.obj.inter (ρ(h) • U.domain.obj)
```

with this subgroup. Membership in the intersection subgroup gives
invariance of the first locus and, by conjugation, of the translated second
locus. The published invariance-under-intersection lemma proves invariance
of their intersection.

`refinementLeft` has label one and maps to `V`.
`refinementRight` has label `g.label⁻¹ * f.label` and maps to `U`.
The subgroup condition for the second arrow follows by conjugating the
displayed intersection subgroup back into `U.subgroup`. Its locus condition
uses the published incidence equivalence for translated rational
presentations and spectrum points. `refinement_comm` proves

```
refinementLeft f g ≫ f = refinementRight f g ≫ g
```

by equality and cancellation of the actual group labels. This supplies a
commutative refinement square; no categorical pullback universal property
is claimed.

`isFiniteCovering_top` uses the singleton identity family.
`IsFiniteCovering.pullback` applies the refinement to each member of a
chosen finite covering family. The commutative square and downward closure
put the left arrows in the pulled-back sieve. For a point `x` of `V`, the
original cover supplies a preimage of `ρ(f.label) • x`. Applying the
relative inverse action puts `x` in the corresponding intersection locus.
The identity-labelled left arrows therefore cover `V`.

`IsFiniteCovering.transitive` chooses a finite covering family over every
member of a first cover. The dependent sum of their finite index types is
finite. Composing the families gives arrows in the desired sieve; the
group action law composes their point preimages. `finiteTopology` packages
these proved axioms as a Mathlib `GrothendieckTopology` on the actual
varying-subgroup category. `mem_finiteTopology` exposes its defining sieve
predicate.

## Covering criteria and functors

`ofArrows_mem_finiteTopology_iff` says that the sieve generated by a finite
arrow family covers exactly when that family's labelled point images cover.
For the forward direction, factor each arrow of a witnessing cover through
one of the generators, and use the factor arrow's locus-image condition.
The reverse direction uses the generating family itself.
`bot_mem_finiteTopology_iff` says that the empty sieve covers exactly the
objects with empty rational locus; the reverse implication uses the empty
index type.

`familyCovers_forget_iff` identifies the family predicate with that of
`RationalTransport` after applying `toRationalTransport`. This forgetful
functor replaces `g` by `ρ(g)` and may identify labels. It preserves finite
covering sieves, as proved by `toRationalTransport_coverPreserving`.
Conversely, a finite cover in a pushed-forward sieve factors through
images of arrows of the original sieve. Mapping each preimage point by
that factor arrow recovers a finite covering family in the original
category. This proves `isFiniteCovering_of_forget_pushforward` and
`forget_pushforward_mem_iff`. The latter concerns the pushed-forward sieve;
it asserts no equivalence of sheaf categories.

For a fixed open subgroup `U`, `familyCovers_inclusion_iff` identifies
covering by the identity-labelled `inclusion U` functor with the published
`RationalData.FamilyCovers`. Accordingly, `inclusion_coverPreserving`
proves preservation from `InvariantRationalDomain.finiteRationalTopology`
to the new topology.

`familyCovers_iff_jointly_surjective` characterizes the family predicate
as joint surjectivity of the constructed continuous equivariant
quotient-spectrum maps `(f i).equivariantSpectrumMap`. The imported
rational representation theorem lifts each point of a source or target
locus to its actual quotient spectrum. The structural spectrum triangle
and injectivity of the target structural map then turn the labelled
point-image equality into equality of quotient-spectrum points, and
give the converse by applying the triangle. No quotient maps or spectral
surjectivity facts are assumed as additional fields.

For `V ≤ U`, `subgroupRestriction_familyCovers` proves that the single
arrow from the unchanged rational quotient with its restricted `V`-action
to the quotient with its `U`-action is a covering family. The label is one
and the point map is the identity. The generated-sieve criterion therefore
also makes its generated sieve a cover.

## Provenance and scope

This is a derived extension of the published `RationalTransportCovering`
to `OpenSubgroupTransport`. It uses the actual rational intersections,
translated loci, subgroup invariance, bounded quotient restrictions and
continuous equivariant spectrum maps already constructed in the Library.
The new step equips each refinement intersection with a common open
subgroup and proves the covering axioms and comparisons.

The geometric source context is Berkovich, *Non-Archimedean analytic
spaces*, Trieste lectures (2009), printed/PDF p. 9, Definition 2.2.1 and
Exercise 2.2.2(ii)–(iii), and p. 10, the finite-affinoid-cover discussion
before Fact 2.2.6. These passages give affinoid and rational domains,
intersection stability and finite covering families. The registered source
is `berkovich-2009-trieste-analytic-spaces`, SHA256
`8cfe4f6f35d5e717ac67c5c8f5ef9ff9185f460bbe5f9a10aeacd84cddeeb2ca`.

The operator convention comes from Berkovich's unfinished *Etale
equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces* (1995–1996), retained
`Equish_1996` manuscript, section 1.1, printed/PDF p. 3: operator morphisms
have continuous homomorphisms from the source group to the target group,
and an action element induces conjugation. The same page's section 1.2
defines the full equivariant etale and quasi-etale sites by the
corresponding underlying analytic covering families. The registered folder
is `provided-7bb3903652336bd799aad8853b8efd7ff3bcc0c7652745417f4745a14ac08e90`,
SHA256 `e046cadfb9dcec4b0cf6624f80d6316bf532416153b82023f5100bd1ab0726cd`.
Its member *Berkovich--Etale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces
(unfinished manuscript)--1996.pdf* has SHA256
`91567a75866a27cbe6690b6f075693df5afaa02524223d94d52ea37d77bb6637`.
These passages motivate the conventions; the finite topology on this
restricted varying-subgroup presentation category is an internal
construction, not a verbatim definition from either source.

The topology is restricted to chosen unit-radius rational presentations
over one fixed affinoid algebra, with the restricted rational spectral
action. It retains distinct original group labels, distinct presentations
with equal loci, empty loci, trivial scalar valuations and degenerate or
nonreduced quotients. No nonemptiness, faithful action or equality of the
scalar and group universes is required. Full analytic atlases,
arbitrary-radius domains, etale and quasi-etale morphisms and sites,
induced analytic spaces, sheafness of the existing algebra presheaves,
Tate acyclicity, cohomology and Harris towers remain obligations of the
broader construction.
