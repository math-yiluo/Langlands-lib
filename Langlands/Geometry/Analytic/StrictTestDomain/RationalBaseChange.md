# Rational base change and finite covering preservation

Author: Worker 36.

For an ultrametric normed field `K : Type u`, let P and Q be the existing
presented strict affinoid algebras, and let φ : P → Q be an actual bounded
K-algebra homomorphism. The underlying algebras are the constructed closed-ideal
quotients of finite strict Tate algebras. The categories `RationalData K P`
and `RationalData K Q` have chosen rational inputs as objects and inclusions
of their actual multiplicative-seminorm loci as morphisms. The construction
retains the chosen inputs rather than identifying objects with equal loci.

`pullbackFunctor φ` sends d to the existing `d.pullback φ`. Its denominator
and numerators are φ(g) and φ(f_j), and its locus is the inverse image of the
locus of d under the spectrum map M(Q) → M(P), x ↦ x ∘ φ. The absence of
common zeros is already proved in `RationalPullbackDefinitions`; it is not a
new assumption. For an inclusion h : d → e, `Inclusion.pullback h φ` applies
h's subset proof to the spectrum point x ∘ φ. This supplies the actual
inclusion of the inverse images. The identity and composition laws follow
from the proved subsingleton property of inclusion morphisms.

`pullbackFunctor_id` and `pullbackFunctor_comp` prove equalities of functors:
pullback by the identity is the identity functor, and pullback by ψ ∘ φ is
pullback by φ followed by pullback by ψ. On objects these use the existing
`pullback_id` and `pullback_comp`; on morphisms they use the same
subsingleton property. No equivalence or essential-surjectivity assertion
about these functors is made.

For the remaining algebra comparison, assume that K is complete. Write A_d
for `d.algebra`, α_d for `d.baseMap : P → A_d`, C_d for
`(d.pullback φ).algebra`, and β_d for its structural map from Q. The existing
`d.comparison φ : A_d → C_d` is the bounded map constructed by strict-test
rational representability, satisfying

    comparison_d ∘ α_d = β_d ∘ φ.

For h : d → e, write r_h : A_e → A_d for the actual bounded restriction and
r_hφ : C_e → C_d for the restriction after pullback. The theorem
`Inclusion.restriction_pullback_comp_comparison` proves

    r_hφ ∘ comparison_e = comparison_d ∘ r_h.

The proof applies the published `e.hom_ext`: bounded maps out of A_e are
equal if they agree after composition with α_e. The left side then becomes
r_hφ ∘ β_e ∘ φ = β_d ∘ φ by the comparison and restriction equations.
The right side becomes comparison_d ∘ α_d = β_d ∘ φ by the same equations.
Associativity is definitional for the bounded algebra homomorphisms. Thus
naturality uses the actual quotient-algebra maps, beyond thinness of the
inclusion category.

`pullbackComparison φ` packages these maps in the opposite algebra category
as a natural transformation

    pullbackFunctor φ ⋙ toAffinoidSpace K Q ⟶ toAffinoidSpace K P.

Its component at d is the affinoid-space map M(C_d) → M(A_d) opposite to
`d.comparison φ`. The naturality field is exactly the preceding bounded-map
equality with arrows reversed. The realization functors here are the existing
ones, built from the actual closed-ideal quotients; no output space or
comparison map is postulated.

`FamilyCovers.pullback` preserves point-covering families, even before imposing
finiteness: if a point x belongs to the pulled-back target locus, then x ∘ φ
belongs to the original target. Original coverage supplies an index i with
x ∘ φ in the i-th locus, equivalently x in its inverse image. There is no
surjectivity assumption on the spectrum map. For `IsFiniteCovering.functorPushforward`,
choose the finite family of inclusions supplied by a covering sieve S. Its
image has the same finite index type, lies in `S.functorPushforward
(pullbackFunctor φ)` by Mathlib's `Sieve.image_mem_functorPushforward`, and
covers by `FamilyCovers.pullback`. This is the sieve generated by the image
under the functor, not a claim that images alone are already a sieve.

`pullbackFunctor_coverPreserving` records precisely Mathlib's
`CategoryTheory.CoverPreserving` property for the existing
`finiteRationalTopology K P` and `finiteRationalTopology K Q`: every covering
sieve has covering functor pushforward. The inclusion construction, functor
laws, point-cover argument and `IsFiniteCovering` argument need only the
ultrametric normed field assumptions. The bounded comparison and the packaged
Grothendieck topologies use completeness of K. All finite cover index types
are in universe u, as in `RationalCovering`.

Empty finite covers, empty numerator tuples, empty rational loci, zero and
nonreduced quotient algebras, and trivial scalar valuations remain permitted.
No additional nonempty, nontrivial or spectral-injectivity assumptions are
introduced. All radii remain one. The contribution proves cover preservation
for the restricted finite rational topologies; it does not identify them with
the full analytic G-topology, prove continuity on categories of sheaves,
construct a structure sheaf or an etale site, or prove Tate acyclicity.

This is a derived construction based on Berkovich, *Non-archimedean analytic
spaces*, Trieste lectures (2009), Definition 2.1.11 on printed/PDF p. 8,
Definition 2.2.1 and Exercise 2.2.2(ii) on p. 9, the finite-affinoid-cover
paragraph preceding Fact 2.2.6 on p. 10, and the analytic G-topology paragraph
after Fact 3.2.4 on p. 15. The registered original is
`berkovich-2009-trieste-analytic-spaces`, SHA256
`8cfe4f6f35d5e717ac67c5c8f5ef9ff9185f460bbe5f9a10aeacd84cddeeb2ca`.
The source fixes the opposite algebra direction, rational inequalities and
covering context. Inverse-image functoriality, comparison naturality and
finite-cover preservation are derived from those constructions and the
published representation results; they are not separately quoted source
theorems. The source permits arbitrary positive radii and tests all affinoids,
whereas the representation and comparison used here are for unit-radius
rational data and presented strict affinoid tests.
