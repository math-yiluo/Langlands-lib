# Lifting finite rational covers to a common open subgroup

This derived local construction lifts finite families of actual rational
quotients to the invariant rational site after shrinking a prescribed open
subgroup. It uses the existing common open stabilizer, the actual bounded
restriction maps and the published finite covering topologies.

Let `K` be a complete ultrametric normed field, `P` an actual presented strict
affinoid algebra over `K`, and `ρ` a `RationalSpectralAction` of a topological
group `G`. The field and group have independent universes. Fix an open subgroup
`U`, a `U`-invariant rational target `d`, and a finite family of rational
presentations `D i` with inclusions `f i : D i ⟶ d.obj`. The family need not
initially be invariant under `U`.

## Actual family and sieve

The previously constructed `finiteFamilyOpenSubgroup U D` is the intersection
of `U` with all the open setwise stabilizers of the finitely many actual loci.
It is an open subgroup contained in `U` and in each locus stabilizer. It is the
largest open subgroup of `U` with this property. Its construction permits an empty
index type, in which case it equals `U`.

`finiteInvariantFamily` retains each `D i` with the proved invariance under this
common subgroup. `finiteInvariantFamilyArrow` retains `f i` as an arrow to the
subgroup restriction of `d`. Their underlying rational objects and inclusion
arrows are definitionally the originals, as the corresponding `_obj` and `_hom`
lemmas state. Thus the rational quotient algebras and bounded restrictions
are unchanged.

`finiteInvariantFamilySieve` is the sieve generated by these actual invariant
arrows. Its construction needs no covering hypothesis. The family, arrows,
sieve and exact forgetful pushforward comparison permit a finite index type in
an independent universe. `finiteInvariantFamilySieve_pushforward` identifies its
pushforward along the invariant forgetful functor with the original generated
sieve. The proof applies Mathlib's generated-family pushforward formula and
then the exact underlying object and arrow equalities.

## Covering and actual spectrum maps

The covering comparisons use an index type in the field universe, as do the
published finite rational topologies.
`finiteInvariantFamilySieve_mem_iff` identifies covering of the lifted sieve with
`RationalData.FamilyCovers d.obj D`, the original point-covering predicate.
`finiteInvariantFamilySieve_mem_iff_original` consequently compares covering of
the original and lifted generated sieves. Both statements follow from the
published generated-family covering criteria because all the actual loci are
unchanged.

`finiteInvariantFamily_jointly_surjective_iff` identifies the same point-covering
predicate with joint surjectivity of the constructed continuous equivariant maps
of actual quotient spectra. These maps are the published invariant spectrum
functor applied to the lifted inclusions. Its proof specializes the published
invariant spectrum covering comparison, whose construction uses the actual
rational representation and the injective structural spectrum map.

No nonemptiness is imposed on the finite family or its loci. In particular, an
empty family covers precisely an empty target locus, consistently with the
existing finite topology.

## Lifting an arbitrary finite covering sieve

`finiteCovering_iff_exists_invariantPullback` states the following equivalence
for an ordinary rational sieve `S` on the underlying presentation of `d`:
`S` is covering if and only if there is an open subgroup `V ≤ U` for which
the functor pullback of `S` along the `V`-invariant forgetful functor is
covering at the subgroup restriction of `d`.

For the forward implication the proof extracts a finite covering family already
contained in `S`. It uses that family's constructed
`finiteFamilyOpenSubgroup U D` as `V` and the actual family and arrows above as
the covering witness. Membership in the pulled-back sieve is exactly the
original membership in `S`. For the reverse implication the proof forgets the
invariance certificates of a finite covering family in the pullback. Its
underlying rational objects, arrows and point-covering property give a covering
witness for `S`. This is an existence theorem about a smaller open subgroup;
it makes no assertion that every ordinary cover lifts at the initially
prescribed subgroup.

## Provenance and scope

Berkovich's unfinished 1995–1996 manuscript, *Etale equivariant sheaves on p-adic
analytic spaces*, section 1.1, printed/PDF pp.2–3, discusses continuous actions,
open-subgroup constructions and morphisms with changes of acting group.
Section 1.2, printed/PDF p.3, defines equivariant etale and quasi-etale covering
families through their underlying geometric covers. These passages motivate the
finite local lifting construction; they do not state this restricted rational

The implementation is derived from the published
`InvariantSubgroupRestriction`, `InvariantRationalSite`, `RationalCovering` and
`InvariantRestrictionSite` interfaces and Mathlib's sieve functoriality. The
action is the existing rational spectral action, with its restricted topology.
The construction remains over a fixed field, using unit-radius rational
presentations. It does not construct analytic atlases, induced analytic spaces,
full etale or quasi-etale sites, a structure sheaf, Tate acyclicity, distinguished
analytic domains or cohomology.
