# Affinoid tests for algebraic-space analytifications

For a separated algebraic space, two maps from a presented affinoid spectrum
through affine test points agree precisely when they give the same ring-valued
point. The proof uses the finite polynomial model of the intersection of the
two algebraic tests and the pullback property of the analytification. On the
algebraic side, a lift through that intersection is a map of coordinate
algebras. On the analytic side, the localized affinoid Hom equivalence
identifies a lift through the analytified intersection with the same
coordinate map. Its composition law identifies the two projections.

This is an internal consequence of the affine test intersection and localized
affinoid Hom comparisons. For a locally finite type space, finite-stage
factorization supplies a polynomial test for each affinoid-algebra point.
The equality criterion makes the resulting analytic map independent of the
chosen factorization and proves injectivity. The two composition laws follow
by changing coefficients or applying a compatible morphism to a factorization.
