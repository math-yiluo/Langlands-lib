# Complete local-domain transport under atlas reindexing

This internal construction changes an atlas's index names and universe
through a supplied equivalence. The forward functor reads a local full
domain in the old atlas by applying the equivalence to its original
index. Its domain algebra, bounded structural map, ambient chart and
all entire bounded restrictions are literally unchanged.

The original whole coefficient-test chart-change condition survives
reindexing. Its uniqueness proves that canonical domain reexpression
is the complete original bounded map. These equations define the forward
functor and the explicit inverse on each Hom type. Every original index
has a preimage under the supplied equivalence, so this fully faithful
functor is essentially surjective and yields an actual category
equivalence between the local full-domain categories.

The chosen inverse object is compared with its original domain by the
equivalence's actual counit isomorphism. This constructs a bounded
algebra equivalence, the exact unchanged ambient-locus identity and the
actual spectrum chart formula. Naturality of that counit proves the
whole bounded coefficient identity on every original domain arrow.
No equality of algebra maps is inferred from their action on spectra.

The provenance is internal: actual atlas reindexing, coefficient-test
chart-change uniqueness, the full local-domain category, and the
full-faithful essentially-surjective equivalence construction. This
does not yet instantiate the product's small-atlas geometry or prove
global finite-affinoid recognition or etale base change.
