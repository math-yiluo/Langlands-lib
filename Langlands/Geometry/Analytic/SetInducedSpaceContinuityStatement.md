# Statement: the spaces `X_Σ` of discrete `G`-sets are `G`-spaces

Author: Worker 21.

This module states, without proof, that Berkovich's space `X_Σ` of a
`G`-space `X` and a discrete `G`-set `Σ` is a `G`-space. The space `X_Σ` and
its action are constructed in module `SetInducedSpace` (`setInduced X S`).
The module contains one Prop-valued definition,
`Langlands.Analytic.SaturatedAtlasPresentation.SetInducedSpace.ContinuityStatement`.

## The statement

`ContinuityStatement : Prop`. Let `K` be a complete ultrametric normed field
in universe `u`. Let `G : Type z` be a group with a topology making it a
topological group (`IsTopologicalGroup G`). Let
`X : Action (LocalizedCategory.{u, max z v, max z v}) G` be an analytic space
with an action of `G`, and let `S : Type z` be a set with an action of `G`
(`MulAction G S`). Assume:

1. every stabilizer `MulAction.stabilizer G s`, for `s : S`, is an open
   subset of `G`;
2. `X` satisfies `gSpaceProperty K G` (module `ContinuousAnalyticAction`).

Then `setInduced X S` satisfies `gSpaceProperty K G`.

`gSpaceProperty` is the Library's formalization of Berkovich's `G`-spaces
(1996 manuscript, §1.1, printed pp.1–2): the underlying topological space is
Hausdorff, and the action `G → G(X)` is continuous for Berkovich's topology on
the automorphism group, defined by the subgroups `G_ε(X)` for `ε ∈ E(X)`
(`IsContinuousAction`). With this statement, `X_S` is an object of
`GSpace K G`.

Hypothesis 1 says that `S` is a discrete `G`-set: the action
`G × S → S` is continuous for the discrete topology on `S`. It cannot be
dropped. Take `S = G` with left multiplication, `G` not discrete and `X`
nonempty. An element `g ≠ 1` moves the copy indexed by `1` to the copy indexed
by `g`. So only `g = 1` preserves a nonempty compact domain inside the copy
indexed by `1`, and the action on `X_G` is not continuous.

## Original source

Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces* (unfinished
manuscript, 1995–1996), registered as
`provided-7bb3903652336bd799aad8853b8efd7ff3bcc0c7652745417f4745a14ac08e90`,
§1.2, printed p.4: "For a G-set Σ, let X_Σ denote the disjoint union
∐_{σ∈Σ} X_σ (X_σ are copies of X) provided with the following action of G: an
element g ∈ G takes X_σ to X_{gσ} by the action of g on X. [...] The
correspondence Σ ↦ X_Σ(G) defines a morphism of sites
γ : X(G)_qét → P(G) (resp. X(G)_ét → P(G))". Here `P(G)` is "the category
of G-sets endowed with the Grothendieck topology generated by the pretopology
for which the sets of coverings consist of surjective families of G-maps". On
the same page its objects are the discrete `G`-sets: sheaves on `P(G)` are
represented by the `G`-sets `∪_N F(G/N)` over open subgroups `N`, and
`Γ_{X{G}}` takes values in "(discrete) G-sets".

Berkovich uses, without stating it separately, that `X_Σ(G)` is an object of
`G-An` for every such `Σ`, that is, `X_Σ` is a `G`-space. This module states
it for the Library's construction.

Derived choices: the universes are those of `SetInducedSpace`; the set `S`
lives in the universe `z` of `G`. Discreteness of `S` is expressed by open
stabilizers, which is equivalent to continuity of the action on `S` with the
discrete topology.

## Expected proof route

None of this is formalized here.

- Hausdorff: the underlying space of `X_S` is the topological sigma type of
  copies of the Hausdorff space of `X` (`sigmaPresentation_space`). Disjoint
  unions of Hausdorff spaces are Hausdorff.
- Continuity: a test triple `(U, f, t)` on `X_S` has a compact domain `U`, so
  `U` meets only finitely many copies `s_1, …, s_n`. On the open subgroup
  `⋂ stabilizer(s_i)`, every `g` maps the copy `s_i` to itself by the action of
  `g` on `X`. The continuity of the action on `X`, applied to the pieces
  `U ∩ copy s_i`, gives a neighbourhood of `1` on which the action is near the
  identity for `(U, f, t)`.
- For `S = G ⧸ N` with `N` open, this instance also follows from
  `InducedSpace.ContinuityStatement` for the restriction of `X` to `N`,
  through `SetInducedSpace.inducedIso`, once `gSpaceProperty` is known to be
  invariant under isomorphisms.

## Scope

This is a `lean.statement`: nothing is proved here.
