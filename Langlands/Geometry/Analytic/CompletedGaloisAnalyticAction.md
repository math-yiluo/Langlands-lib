# The absolute Galois action on the actual completed scalar extension

For a finite extension of `ℚ_p` with its complete ultrametric norm,
`completedFieldEmbedding` is the inclusion into the completion of its
spectral-normed algebraic closure. The space `completedScalarExtension`
is the actual extension of an analytic space in the varying-field
category. `completedGaloisAnalyticAction` uses the Cartesian lift of the
inverse coefficient automorphism, which gives the left action by
semilinear analytic automorphisms. The theorem
`completedGaloisAnalyticAction_comp_projection` identifies the composite
of each such automorphism with the projection to the original space.

`continuous_completedGaloisPointAction` obtains joint continuity on the
product of the Krull group and the point space from norm-continuity of
each coefficient orbit and the chart comparison for the analytic lifts.
Continuity on compact analytic domains and their sections uses the
corresponding section estimates for scalar extension.

The source is Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic
spaces*, §1.1.1(i), printed and PDF p. 2, with the analytic ground field
extension from *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces*
(1993), §1.4, printed p. 30. The supplied manuscript PDF has SHA-256
