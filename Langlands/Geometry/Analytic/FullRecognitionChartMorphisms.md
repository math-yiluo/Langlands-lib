# Small chart morphisms of full recognition maps

The localized small spectrum of a chart maps into the ambient space
as the canonical chart domain inclusion after an isomorphism. This
gives the literal range of the chart, injectivity on points, monicity
and factorization for maps into its range.

For an admissible pair of charts of an actual strong map, composition
with the small source chart inclusion is the spectrum map of the
actual bounded pullback followed by the target chart inclusion.
The same comparison applies to subspace domain inclusions. For a
full-recognition pair, use the corresponding chart of the admissible
source net. The inclusion of that net has the identity bounded map
on the chosen chart. Composing with the localized pair-system map is
then the map of the admissible net, whose pullback is the actual
full-recognition pullback. This also identifies the map on the
underlying space. Restriction between two recognized charts gives
the triangle of their chart inclusions.

The canonical comparison from the local-domain enlargement to
full recognition is a localized isomorphism. Its naturality identifies
the full-recognition map with the original localized map by conjugation.
This gives the comparison for étaleness and the composition rule.

This is an internal consequence of the localized chart-domain
isomorphisms and the actual admissible-net localization.
