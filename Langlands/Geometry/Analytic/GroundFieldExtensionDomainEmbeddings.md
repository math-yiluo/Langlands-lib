# Ground field extension of analytic domain embeddings

Let \(K \to L\) be an isometric extension of complete non-Archimedean normed fields. The bounded pullbacks of a compatible strong domain embedding represent the images of admissible source charts. The theorem `PresentedAffinoidAlgebra.GroundFieldExtension.domainPreservation` gives full representations of the inverse images of those loci. The images of the extended charts, and the image of the whole space, are therefore projection preimages of the original images.

The extended map is injective because its original map is injective and the extended points over any original chart share that chart. Each chart map is an embedding, using compactness and Hausdorffness on the affinoid spectra. To compare the topology of the image, project the image to the original domain and pull back its chart quasinet. The inverse of the extended map is continuous on each member by the chart embedding. The quasinet detects its continuity on the whole image.

`extendedChartDomainIso` identifies the scalar extension of a chart-domain object with the domain of the extended chart in the chosen saturated atlas. The extended inclusion is a domain embedding onto that range. Its strong factorization through the domain gives a quasi-isomorphism after refining the source, which yields the comparison in the localization. The comparison commutes with the two domain inclusions. Consequently the localized scalar-extension functor preserves `IsAffinoidDomainEmbedding`, the predicate that the arrow is isomorphic over the target to a chart-domain inclusion of the target's chosen saturated presentation.
