# Canonical spectrum maps under finite-chart cover comparison

This module proves that the actual bounded comparison of finite-chart section equalizers identifies their canonical finite-union spectrum maps. With subordinate covers in both directions, it transports continuity, injectivity and surjectivity between these canonical maps.

The original motivation is Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §1.2, printed pp.20–21 / PDF pp.17–18, registered as `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`. The paragraph after Lemma 1.2.12 defines the Banach section algebra independently of a special covering and its continuous spectrum map; the next paragraph adds affinoid-recognition conditions before Proposition 1.2.13 extends the atlas.

This is derived infrastructure using `FiniteChartCoverComparison` and `FiniteChartGluingSpectrum`. Each finite family consists of actual original atlas charts with represented pairwise overlaps. A directed `ComparisonCover d e` supplies finite original-chart covers subordinate to `d` for every member and represented overlap of `e`. The maps and equivalence are constructed from these geometric inputs by bounded descent and full algebra separation. General existence of these covers, special-cover admissibility, general continuity, and affinoid recognition remain separate obligations. The coefficient-field, ambient-space and chart-index universes remain independent; no Hausdorffness assumption is made on the ambient space or the literal unions.

`locusMap` is the actual inclusion of the target literal union into the source union, continuous for their inherited subspace topologies. `comap_spectrumMap_original` evaluates the full bounded comparison formula on an arbitrary point of any subordinate original chart. The component recovery equations of the canonical spectrum maps identify this with the corresponding source component.

`comap_spectrumMap` proves the equality on the whole target union. First the finite target chart family supplies a representative. Its subordinate cover supplies an original chart point. The stored original restriction-chart equation and embedding injectivity identify the comap of this point with the target representative. The same chart equation identifies its source representative with `locusMap` of that point. The local spectrum equation then gives the claimed global equality. Thus no continuity of the canonical pointwise map is used in this proof, and equality of algebra maps is never inferred from their spectrum points.

With a reverse cover system, `locusHomeomorph` is the identity on underlying ambient points along the proved equality of literal unions. `spectrumHomeomorph_spectrumMap` identifies the contravariant spectrum homeomorphism of the constructed bounded equivalence with both canonical pointwise maps. Its direction is from the target equalizer spectrum to the source equalizer spectrum.

`spectrumMap_eq_transport` expresses the target canonical map as the union homeomorphism, followed by the source canonical map, followed by the inverse spectrum homeomorphism. Composing continuous, injective or surjective functions proves the three `_of` results. Applying each implication to the reverse cover system proves its `_iff` result. These are preservation results: none asserts that a general finite union is already identified with the whole spectrum of its Banach equalizer.
