# Principal-open transport for affine analytification

This proof module supports the quasi-finite analytification theorem by treating
the source-local factorization in Zariski's Main Theorem. An injective
coordinate map from \(C\) to \(B\) that clears denominators by powers of an
element \(r\) gives an isomorphism on the localizations away from \(r\). The
localization universal property extends maps from \(C\) on which \(r\) is a
unit. Clearing denominators also proves uniqueness and gives injectivity of
the analytic map over the nonvanishing locus of \(r\).

For a local domain contained in that locus, the spectrum criterion makes
the image of \(r\) a unit in its algebra. The extended coordinate map from
\(B\) gives a bounded map from a sufficiently large \(B\)-chart. Its spectrum
image is the entire inverse image of the original domain. The local domain's
lifting property on \(C\), together with uniqueness of extension to \(B\),
proves the lifting property for this inverse image with the same algebra.

A recognized subset in the nonvanishing locus is compact and fits in an
original chart. Its model as a local domain retains its recognized algebra.
The comparison of the full-recognition pullback with the containing-chart
restrictions identifies that pullback with the bounded equivalence of the
two domain algebras. In particular, the pullback is finite.

The nonvanishing locus and its inverse image thus witness finiteness at
every source point over that locus. Saturated functoriality and composition
of maps finite at a point transfer finiteness of the preceding factor to
the composite.
