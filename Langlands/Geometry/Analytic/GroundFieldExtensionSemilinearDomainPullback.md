# Bounded pullbacks on full domains in a tower of ground fields

The full domain of an intermediate chart extends with its presented algebra and bounded structural map. Its locus is the inverse image under the spectrum projection. The tower comparison on an admissible pair of actual local domains uses `localDomainPullback` of the compatible strong map. On the original sections of the target domain, the resulting bounded pullback precomposes this map with the scalar embedding. Its scalar rule is the second field embedding and its ambient rule is the matching bounded chart tower map on the original scalar leg.

When the second embedding is a coefficient automorphism fixing the ground field, the ambient tower pullback is the usual bounded coefficient operator. If the automorphism lies in the subgroup of a finite ambient rational cover, the descended operator on the domain shows that the domain locus is preserved. This makes the local domain an admissible source for the tower comparison. The two bounded operators on its algebra have the same scalar and ambient rules, so the represented domain's bounded universal property proves their equality. The operator for a coefficient automorphism \(\sigma\) is the pullback of the analytic lift of \(\sigma^{-1}\) under the left action convention. The identification holds for the open-subgroup operators used in the section orbit and spectral estimates.

A bounded map between two represented domain algebras commutes with these semilinear operators when the ambient triangle comes from an extended bounded map and both domains admit the coefficient operator. Naturality on the ambient algebras gives equality after precomposition with the structural map. The scalar rule then gives equality on the entire source domain algebra.
