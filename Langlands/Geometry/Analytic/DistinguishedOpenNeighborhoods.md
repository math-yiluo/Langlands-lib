# Stable distinguished open neighborhoods

On a Hausdorff analytic space with a continuous analytic action, `hasStableDistinguishedOpenNeighborhoods` gives a distinguished open neighborhood of any point inside any prescribed open neighborhood. Its closure is compact and lies in the prescribed neighborhood. An open subgroup fixes the chosen open set setwise.

The proof chooses a compact neighborhood `C` which is a finite union of original affinoid charts inside the prescribed open. It takes a smaller open neighborhood `W` with compact closure in the interior of `C`. The compact set `C \ W` is covered by finitely many charts in `C` that miss the point. Let `B` be their union. Then `C \ B = W \ B` is open. Its complement is an analytic domain because it agrees locally with `B` on the interior of `C` and with an open domain off the closure of `W`. The intersection of the open stabilizers of the finitely many charts defining `C` and `B` fixes their difference.

This is a derived construction for the distinguished neighborhoods in Berkovich's *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces*, Equish_1996 manuscript, printed pp. 7 and 11. Page 7 describes distinguished opens and open subgroups preserving them in compact envelopes. Page 11 uses distinguished shrinking opens in the proof of Proposition 1.5.1, step (1′).
