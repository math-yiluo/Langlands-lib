# Finite covers subordinate to recognized open target pieces

This internal construction treats an actual strong map between local-domain
saturations and an arbitrary recognized source affinoid V whose whole image
lies in a prescribed open subset U of the target. It constructs a finite
full-domain cover of the actual V algebra, and for each member gives a
recognized target affinoid inside U together with the entire bounded map
and its actual ambient chart identity. No affinoid neighborhood of an
ambient analytic point is assumed. Field, space and old index universes
remain independent.

For one old source chart, choose an admissible target chart. At each source
spectrum point, continuity and openness of U give a rational neighborhood
of its image in that target chart. Extend the rational domain through the
original target local-domain representative and recognize the result.
It lies in U and in the chosen target chart. Its contained-domain
representation and the original bounded pullback give an actual full-domain
pullback in the source algebra. This is a neighborhood of the chosen source
point. The actual pushout identity and chart identities prove that its
entire bounded map has the required target image.

Compactness of the source spectrum gives a finite subcover from the
interiors of these domains. For arbitrary V, first use its canonical finite
old-chart domain cover. Apply the preceding construction on each old chart,
then extend each inner full domain through the outer component. Enumerating
the finite dependent family gives the final full-domain cover of V.
The algebras and bounded maps of the inner actual pushouts are retained.

This constructs local affine models needed to apply effective finite algebra
descent. It does not itself prove that a finite union of finite affinoid
preimages is affinoid, construct that descended algebra, recognize the whole
global pullback preimage, or prove global etale base change. The construction
is derived from rational neighborhoods, actual contained recognized domains,
full-domain pullbacks, finite recognized component covers and compactness;
no external theorem is transcribed.
