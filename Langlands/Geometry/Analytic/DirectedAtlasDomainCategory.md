# Actual domains represented in directed atlas charts

Let `K : Type u` be a complete ultrametric normed field, `X : Type v` a topological space, and `a : IndexedAffinoidAtlas.{u,v,w} K X` an actual affinoid atlas whose original charts are pairwise directed under containment. The proof `hd : a.HasDirectedCharts` is an explicit parameter. `DirectedDomain a hd` records an original chart index `i` and a full `Domain K (a.algebra i)`. Its algebra is the actual finite positive-polyradius quotient in that representation. Its ambient locus is `a.chart i '' U.domain.locus`. Objects retain their chosen chart and algebra presentation; equal loci do not make them definitionally equal.

The structural spectrum map followed by the original chart defines `ambientMap`. Its image is exactly `locus`, using the full domain representation's exact image. `spectrumToLocus` is a continuous surjection, and the ambient locus is compact. These results do not assume or establish that an arbitrary full domain's structural spectrum map is injective.

`Inclusion U V` stores the ambient containment, an actual bounded algebra map `V.algebra → U.algebra`, and its structural equation over every common original upper chart. For each such chart `q`, the equation is
`restriction.comp (V.domain.baseMap.comp (a.restriction V.index q hVq)) = U.domain.baseMap.comp (a.restriction U.index q hUq)`.
`Inclusion.ofSubset` constructs this data from any ambient containment using `directedDomainRestriction`. `Inclusion.restriction_eq` identifies any competing arrow with that actual constructed restriction by full representation uniqueness over a common upper chart. Thus the Hom type is a subsingleton because the full bounded maps are unique, rather than because agreement of spectrum points is taken to imply equality of algebra maps.

The category's identity is the actual bounded identity. Composition uses the actual composite bounded homomorphism. The published directed restriction composition law proves its equation over every common upper chart. Hom subsingletonness then proves the category laws. `nonempty_hom_iff_subset` gives the exact relationship with containment. `toAffinoidSpace` and `toTopologicalSpace` realize the category by its actual quotient algebras, bounded restrictions and multiplicative seminorm spectra. `locusFunctor` gives the ambient subspace diagram. `spectrumToLocusNat` is the natural comparison of the two diagrams: independent universes `u` and `v` are lifted to `max u v`, and its components are the continuous surjections described above. The actual bounded spectrum maps respect the ambient maps. `boundedPresheaf` and `banachPresheaf` retain the quotient algebras and bounded restrictions in the algebra direction; no sheaf property is claimed here.

For each original chart, `sameChartFunctor` embeds its existing full-domain category with exactly the original algebras and bounded arrows. `fullyFaithfulSameChartFunctor` recovers the original equation by using that chart itself as common upper and simplifying the atlas's identity restriction. The preimage retains the bounded map unchanged. The affinoid and spectrum realization functors agree exactly after this embedding.

`isoOfLocusEq` constructs both arrows between any two objects with the same ambient locus, allowing their original chart indices to differ. The converse `locus_eq_of_iso` uses the two containments. `boundedEquivOfIso` and `boundedEquivOfLocusEq` construct actual bounded algebra equivalences from the inverse restrictions, with both inverse identities proved by the category. The resulting spectra are homeomorphic over `X`. These are bounded equivalences; no isometric or contractive assertion is made.
