# Bounded universal extension of the actual completed tensor algebra

This module constructs the bounded algebra lift from the genuine projective completed tensor algebra into any complete ultrametric normed commutative target algebra. It proves recovery of both original maps and uniqueness on the entire completion. Coefficient, factor and target universes are independent.

This is derived infrastructure for Berkovich, *Spectral Theory and Analytic Geometry over Non-Archimedean Fields* (1990), §1.1, printed p.12 / PDF p.26, the completed tensor product defined by completing the algebraic tensor for the infimum of finite maximum factor-norm products. The completed carrier, its norm, scalar action and density were constructed previously from exactly this seminorm. The lift is now constructed from the two actual bounded algebra maps, rather than supplied as an interface field. No completed universal property is an input.

`algebraicLift` uses the ordinary algebraic tensor universal property. On a pure tensor its value is the product of the two given images. For any positive bounds on the given maps, `norm_algebraicLift_le` proves the product bound for the actual projective seminorm. For every finite pure-tensor representation, the ultrametric target bounds the norm of the sum by the maximum of the term norms. The two original bounds and submultiplicativity bound that maximum by the product constant times the representation's value. Taking the infimum over all representations gives the required projective bound. Consequently the algebraic map is continuous, without assuming completeness of either original factor.

`extensionRingHom` is Mathlib's actual ring extension to the Hausdorff completion. It agrees with the algebraic lift on the dense algebraic image. The same norm estimate passes to the completion because the inequality defines a closed set. The scalar equation on the dense image gives the coefficient-algebra equation. `lift` combines these proved facts into an actual bounded algebra homomorphism.

The two leg recovery theorems are identities of full bounded algebra maps. `hom_ext` proves uniqueness: the original leg identities determine the map on every algebraic tensor by the ordinary algebraic tensor extensionality theorem, and continuity and density determine it on the completion. Uniqueness itself requires neither ultrametric distance nor completeness of the target. No equality of algebra maps is inferred from spectrum points.

`boundedHomEquiv` gives the complete equivalence between bounded maps out of the completed tensor and arbitrary pairs of original bounded maps into the target. The target may be any complete ultrametric normed commutative coefficient algebra, including a zero algebra; it need not be affinoid or reduced. No nontrivial valuation, normalized target unit or original-factor completeness is assumed. The completed tensor is not claimed isometrically identified with any affinoid presentation here; constructing the bounded comparison with the explicit affinoid coproduct remains the next obligation.
