# Equivariant étale base change as a morphism of sites

`EquivariantEtaleBaseChangeMorphismStatement` concerns a morphism \(f:Y\to X\)
of Hausdorff analytic spaces with operators over a common complete
nonarchimedean normed field. The operator groups may differ. The pullback
functor first restricts an action along the continuous group homomorphism of
\(f\), then takes the analytic pullback along its underlying morphism. The
proposition asks for representable flatness and for continuity from the
equivariant étale topology on \(X\) to the equivariant étale topology on \(Y\).
These are the site morphism conditions for this functor.

unfinished manuscript (1995–1996), §1.2, printed/PDF p. 3, gives the
equivariant étale site morphism and its base-change formula. Literature ID
