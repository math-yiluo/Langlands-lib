# The quotient norm of the unit in a strict affinoid presentation

`Langlands.Analytic.PresentedStrictAffinoidAlgebra.quotient_norm_one_le`
proves `‖(1 : A)‖ ≤ 1` for every actual strict affinoid presentation over a
normed ultrametric field. It includes the zero quotient and does not require
completeness. The proof applies the quotient-map norm inequality to the unit
of the strict Tate algebra, whose norm is one.

This statement and proof are extracted unchanged from
[StrictTestDomain/RationalSectionContinuityProof](StrictTestDomain/RationalSectionContinuityProof.md).
Both that proof and the generalized spectral-power proof reuse this declaration.
No new mathematical assertion is introduced by the extraction.
