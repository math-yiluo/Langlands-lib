# Gluing the bounded polynomial loci

This unit constructs the topological glue of the actual closed-kernel
affinoid charts from `PolynomialLocusCharts`. The subsequent atlas unit equips
it with an affinoid atlas and passes to the existing localized category.

## Provenance

The construction is derived from Berkovich, *Spectral Theory and Analytic
Geometry over Non-Archimedean Fields* (1990), Definition 1.5.1 and Remark
1.5.2(i), printed p.19/PDF p.33, and the proof of Theorem 3.4.1, steps
(1)--(3), printed p.64/PDF p.78. These passages describe affine space through
its closed-polydisc covering and the passage to closed algebraic loci. The
specific quotient-gluing implementation here adapts the Library's
`AnalyticAffineSpace` construction to polynomial-kernel quotients; it is not a
separately numbered definition in the source. The registered source is
`provided-a03a0182668f6b92b01deacac09a9992b3edf55e23a0a2194554384ae44bd8d4`,
SHA256 `9b543e7ffae8ec6febf154a60c4d11a288f7ecc3795c66f004ba3200a6824e79`.

## Construction

In `Langlands.Analytic.AffineAnalytification`, let `K` be a complete,
nontrivially normed nonarchimedean field and let
`P : FinitePolynomialPresentation K A`. The algebra `A` may be in a different
universe from `K`, and needs only its commutative ring and `K`-algebra
structures and the specified finite polynomial surjection.

The radii are `AnalyticAffineSpace.radius K m = ‖scale K‖⁻¹ ^ m`, for a chosen
field element with norm strictly between zero and one. The imported radius
lemmas prove positivity and strict increase; `exists_radius_gt` proves
cofinality in the real numbers. This last fact needs only the nontrivially
normed field hypothesis.

`algebra K P m` is the actual polynomial-locus chart at the constant
polyradius of that radius. Its ideal is the closed ideal generated by the
polynomial kernel images, with no radical replacement. For `h : m ≤ k`,
`restriction K P h` is the actual bounded map from the larger-radius quotient
to the smaller one. `represents_restriction` uses the preceding chart unit's
proved full affinoid-domain representation, including every presented
affinoid test algebra. No domain-representation result is assumed as data.

`chartSystem K P` assembles these maps into the Library's
`AffinoidChartSystem`. Reflexivity and composition use equalities of the
actual bounded homomorphisms. Its overlap condition uses the smaller of two
natural indices. `Space K P` is that system's quotient of the disjoint union
of spectra with its quotient topology, and `chart K P m` is the canonical
map from the corresponding spectrum. `chart_pull` is its compatibility with
the actual restrictions; `exists_chart` proves that the charts cover.

## Intrinsic topological laws

The coordinate restriction identities show that the values of coordinates
agree across each gluing relation. Consequently `coordinateNorm K P i`
descends to the quotient and is continuous. `mem_range_chart_iff` identifies
the range of the `m`-th chart with the simultaneous inequalities
`coordinateNorm K P i z ≤ radius K m`. Its converse uses the full chart
restriction representation's exact spectrum image, rather than postulating
points of a smaller chart. `range_chart_mono` follows from the actual
restriction maps.

`openDisc K P m` imposes the strict coordinate inequalities at radius
`m + 1`. Finiteness of the variables makes it open; strict increase of radii
places the `m`-th chart inside it, while it lies inside the next chart.
`range_chart_mem_nhds` therefore supplies a chart neighborhood of every point.

The overlap of two charts is represented by the chart at their minimum
index. `hasFiniteOverlaps` exhibits this as a singleton finite overlap
family. The Library's gluing theorem then gives
`isClosedEmbedding_chart`. Each chart range is Hausdorff, and any two
points lie in a common open disc contained in a chart range. This proves
the `T2Space (Space K P)` instance.

These arguments impose no positive-arity, reducedness, nonzero-algebra or
nonempty-spectrum hypothesis. In particular the coordinate conditions can
be vacuous, and chart ranges need not be distinct. The construction keeps
each original quotient algebra and its actual maps; it makes no inference
of algebra equality from point-set equality.

## Scope

This pair provides cofinal bounded charts and their Hausdorff topological
glue. The subsequent construction units provide the affinoid atlas,
saturation/localization and genuine affine-scheme interface. General
independence of presentation, all-morphism functoriality, rational-point
and point-topology comparisons, the scheme analytification universal
property and arbitrary analytic field-extension comparisons remain
subsequent proposition and proof tasks. This pair does not establish that
final analytification bridge.
