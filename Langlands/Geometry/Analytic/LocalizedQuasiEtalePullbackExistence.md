# Pullback existence along localized quasi-étale morphisms

Every localized cospan with a quasi-étale first leg has a pullback
in the actual localized analytic category. The field universe and the
common space and atlas-index universe are independently variable.

The strong quasi-étale representative supplies a domain–étale chart
through each point. These charts bound the size of the recognized
glued product in the original space universe. The small saturated
product has the full localized universal property. Recognition
isomorphisms transport it to the original strong cospan.

For an arbitrary localized cospan, the quasi-étale condition supplies
an isomorphic arrow with a strong quasi-étale representative. Move
the second leg to that target and take its right fraction. The
strong pullback transports back along the two source isomorphisms
and the target isomorphism. `localizedQuasiEtalePullback` chooses a cone
with this full limit witness.

The provenance is internal. The proof combines the Library's localized
universal property for small glued products with the smallness proof
from the domain–étale charts of the first leg.
