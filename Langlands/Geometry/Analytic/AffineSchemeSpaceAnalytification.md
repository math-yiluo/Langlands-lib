# Comparison with a supplied analytification of an affine scheme

A surjective étale monomorphism gives a singleton covering presieve.
Its identity section with values in the source is compatible, since
the map is monic. Analytic Hom descent glues this section to a left
inverse, and the separatedness part of descent for maps to the target
gives the other inverse identity.

For an affine scheme represented as an algebraic space, every affine
test point comes from a coefficient algebra map. The naturality axiom
for a supplied analytification therefore factors every test map
through the map of the identity test. This map is surjective on points
by the coverage axiom and is étale by the étale map axiom. The affine
pullback axiom applied to two copies of the identity test gives
monicity. Hence the identity test identifies the supplied
analytification with the polynomial-locus model under analytic Hom
descent. Transporting the supplied point family across this
isomorphism gives precisely the canonical affine point family.

The construction is internal to the Library's affine test
analytification and the localized étale site. It assumes a supplied
analytification and the Hom sheaf property.
