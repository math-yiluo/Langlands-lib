# Diagonals of compact quasi-étale analytic spaces

The diagonal of a compact Hausdorff quasi-étale source is the inclusion of an analytic domain in its actual self-pullback. Each member of a finite actual domain cover of the source has a domain–étale factorization. Over that member, the diagonal is the inverse image of the open diagonal in the self-pullback of its étale envelope. The comparison square uses the monomorphism from the domain to the envelope and the universal properties of the two pullbacks.

The two inverse images of a source cover member define the corresponding domain in the full self-pullback. The local diagonal composed with this domain inclusion is the original diagonal restricted to the member. These local domain embeddings glue by compactness and the first projection's retraction equation.

This proof applies the direct open-diagonal theorem for étale maps, the property in Berkovich 1993, Proposition 3.3.7(c). The surrounding quasi-étale factorization is the one in Berkovich 1994, §3. The proof works for an arbitrary analytic base.
