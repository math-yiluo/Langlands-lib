# Open immersions of affine spectra give analytic open immersions

Let `K` be a complete, ultrametric, nontrivially normed field and let
`f : A →ₐ[K] B` be a map of commutative `K`-algebras with finite polynomial
presentations `P` and `Q`. The main theorem
`openImmersion_localizedHom_of_isOpenImmersion` shows: if
`Spec.map (CommRingCat.ofHom f.toRingHom)` is an open immersion of schemes,
then `localizedHom K P Q f` is an analytic open immersion (`openImmersion` of
`AnalyticImmersion`). The algebras lie in a common universe, independent of
that of `K`.

## Seminorms of chart points

In `OpenImmersionPreservation`:
- `saturated_chart_original` reads a point of a saturated original chart of
  `saturated K P` in the original polynomial chart, through the algebra cast
  `algebraCast (originalAlgebra P j).symm`. It uses the published
  `saturatedAtlas_original_chart_heq`.
- `chartPullbackAlgHom P F i j h` is, for a strong map
  `F : S ⟶ saturated K P`, a source chart `i` and an original target chart `j`
  containing the image of `i`, the composite of the coordinate map of `j`, the
  algebra cast and the bounded pullback of `F`. `chartPullbackAlgHom_apply`
  evaluates it.
- `pointSeminorm_baseMap_chart`: for a point `s` of the spectrum of the
  algebra of `i`, the multiplicative seminorm on `A` of the image point
  `F.baseMap (chart i s)` is `a ↦ s (chartPullbackAlgHom P F i j h a)`. The
  proof uses the chart compatibility `F.chart_comm` and the seminorm formula
  for original polynomial charts.
- `toAlgebraFamily_map_eq_chartPullbackAlgHom`: this algebra map is the
  published compatible algebra family `toAlgebraFamily P F` at the chart `i`,
  by `chartAlgHom_independent`.

## The algebraic lifting lemma

`exists_lift_of_center_mem` assumes that `A` and `B` lie in the universe of
`K`, that `Spec.map f` is an open immersion, and that
`φ : A →ₐ[K] R` is a map to a presented affinoid algebra such that the centers
(`center K P`) of all points of the image of the analytic map
`saturatedAffinoidHom K P R φ` lie in the image of `Spec B`. It produces
`ψ : B →ₐ[K] R` with `ψ ∘ f = φ`.

The proof works in the algebra `R'` of the top chart of the saturated small
spectrum of `R`, with the algebra map `φ'` read from that chart by
`chartPullbackAlgHom`. By `affinoidStrongAlgHom_saturatedAffinoidHom`, `φ'`
followed by the cast `R' = R` is `φ`. Let `W` be the open image of `Spec B`
and `I` the ideal of `R'` generated by the images of all `g ∈ A` whose basic
open set lies in `W`. If `I` were proper, the published
`exists_spectrum_kernel_of_proper_ideal` would give a spectrum point `s` of
`R'` annihilating `I`. The center of the corresponding analytic point lies in
`W`, hence in a basic open set `D(g) ⊆ W`, so `s (φ' g) ≠ 0` by
`center_mem_basicOpen` and `pointSeminorm_baseMap_chart`. This contradicts
`φ' g ∈ I`. Thus `I = R'`, and every prime of `R'` contracts into `W`, so the
image of `Spec φ'` lies in the image of `Spec f`. Mathlib's
`IsOpenImmersion.lift` lifts `Spec φ'` through `Spec f`; full faithfulness of
`Spec` turns the lift into a ring map `B → R'` with composite `φ'`, which is a
`K`-algebra map. Composing with the cast gives `ψ`.

## Proof of the main theorem

`openImmersion_localizedHom_of_isOpenImmersion_of_univ` treats algebras in the
universe of `K`. Write `F = localizedHom K P Q f`.
1. The ring map `f` is an epimorphism (`isOpenImmersion_specMap_iff`), so `F`
   is a monomorphism (`mono_localizedHom_of_epi`).
2. Let `U` be the preimage under the continuous center map of the open image
   of `Spec B`. It is open, and `F` lands in `U` by `center_baseMap`. So `F`
   factors through the open domain `D` of `U` (`domainLift`).
3. The domain `D` is the localization of the saturated presentation
   `(presentationOf Y).domain D`. Its charts form a quasinet. On the small
   spectrum of each chart, with algebra `R`, the composite with the domain
   inclusion is `localizedAffinoidHom K P R φ` for some `φ`
   (`localizedAffinoidHomEquiv`), and all its points lie in `D`. The lifting
   lemma factors `φ` through `f`. By `localizedAffinoidHom_comp` the analytic
   map of the factor lifts the chart through `F`.
4. Since `F` is monic, the published `exists_lift_of_chartCover` glues these
   local lifts to `G` from the domain to `Spec(B)ᵃⁿ` with `G ≫ F` the domain
   inclusion. Monicity of `F` and of the domain inclusion makes `G` inverse to
   the factorization of `F`. This is the isomorphism required by
   `openImmersion`.

`openImmersion_localizedHom_of_isOpenImmersion` treats arbitrary universes by
passing to the models of `AffineAnalytificationUniverseReduction`
(`isOpenImmersion_iff_model` and `openImmersion_iff_model`).
