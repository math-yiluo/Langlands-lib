# Affinoid point maps on chart neighborhoods

Precomposition of a small-spectrum map into a selected chart composes the
complete bounded coefficient maps. The proof computes both sides at the
whole-spectrum charts and applies the category's chart map comparison.

Let `K` be complete, ultrametric and nontrivially normed. Let `(Y, p)` be
an analytification of a separated, locally finite type algebraic space
`X` over `Spec K`, with `Y` the localization of a constructed saturated
atlas. Every point of a chart admits a subchart whose locus is a
neighborhood relative to that chart. For each presented affinoid
algebra `R`, any localized morphism from its small spectrum into `Y`
with image in the subchart equals the affinoid point comparison of some
`R`-valued point of `X`.

Choose an étale affine test over the point. In its
strong representative, a recognized finite étale piece surjects onto a
smaller target chart. Realize the complete map in the recognition atlas
and transport across the comparison isomorphisms. An affinoid map with
image in the smaller chart factors through its bounded coefficient map.
The finite chart lift then gives the required ring-valued point.

This is an internal consequence of the étale atlas axiom and the
finite étale descent of the affinoid point comparison.
