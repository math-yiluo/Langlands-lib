# Continuity detected by an equivariant analytic-domain embedding

`analyticActionOfAut` bundles a group homomorphism to the automorphisms
of a localized analytic space as a category-theoretic action. Its
automorphism homomorphism recovers the supplied one.

An equivariant analytic-domain embedding identifies its source with
an actual domain of the target. Equivariance, evaluated on points,
makes this domain invariant under the target action. Continuity of
the restricted action follows from analytic-domain restriction.
Cancelling the domain inclusion shows that the identification is
equivariant for the entire analytic maps, so isomorphism transport
gives continuity of the supplied source action.

The parameter group carries an arbitrary topology. The criterion is
derived from the Library's continuous invariant-domain action and
localized isomorphism transport, for use with analytic-domain cuts
of pullbacks.
