# Closed quotient pullbacks on affinoid covers

For a morphism into an affinoid test algebra, the analytic test has
a compatible lift through the original morphism exactly when the
bounded algebra map annihilates the ideal of the quotient pullback.
The proof uses the categorical pullback and the equivalence between
localized affinoid maps and bounded algebra maps.

For a finite full-domain cover, this criterion identifies the closed
extensions of the local ideals on the actual pairwise intersections.
Each inclusion is tested by the quotient by the other extended ideal.
Closed ideal effectiveness then identifies the local ideals with the
extensions of their contracted global ideal. The pullback of the
cover to the global quotient spectrum gives local arrows to the
source of the original morphism. Monicity makes them compatible and
chart gluing produces the global arrow.

For an arbitrary compatible analytic cone, first test it on affinoid
charts of its source. The inverse images of the finite cover in a
chart detect vanishing of the global ideal by separation of bounded
sections. The bounded quotient property gives the chart lift. The
quotient projection is monic because the two legs of its actual
self-pushout agree on the surjective quotient map. Chart gluing and
monicity establish the pullback universal property.

For a compatible analytified closed immersion, algebraic monicity
gives analytic monicity. A chart quasinet of an arbitrary affinoid
test realizes its map to the target by ring-valued points. The
transported charts give the finite full-domain cover and the
ring-valued points supply the local quotient models. This proves that
every affinoid test has an actual quotient pullback. The argument is
internal and combines affinoid point realization with closed ideal
effectiveness in the Library's analytic category.
