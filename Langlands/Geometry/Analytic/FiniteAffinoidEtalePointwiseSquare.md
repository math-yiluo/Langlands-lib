# Finite affinoid pointwise etale squares

This internal construction removes the supplied finite-pushout and ordinary tensor-comparison hypotheses from the existing small-affinoid pointwise square. Let `f : A → B` be a finite Banach bounded map between presented affinoid algebras, let `g : A → C` be any bounded map, and suppose the three ordinary spectra are small in an independently chosen universe `v`. At any compatible pair of ordinary points `x` of the small spectrum of `B` and `y` of the small spectrum of `C`, assume only that `f` is etale at the spectrum point corresponding to `x`. The construction gives an actual saturated analytic presentation `T`, strong maps `p : T → saturated B` and `q : T → saturated C`, equality of the complete composites over `saturated A`, and a point of `T` mapping to `x` and `y`. The second projection is strongly etale for its canonical saturation extension and etale in the actual localized category.

`finiteEtalePushout` is the existing actual open etale part of the finite affinoid pushout, instantiated with the published theorem `AffinoidPushout.finiteBanachPushout`. Its two projections are the existing restrictions of the original bounded pushout legs. The construction therefore retains the actual algebras, maps and commuting equation. The resulting open restriction is not asserted to be a finite morphism.

The published `AffinoidPushout.finiteTensorComparison` supplies the second previously conditional input. Applying the existing point-lifting theorem puts a point over the prescribed pair into this open etale part: finite-type pointwise flatness and unramifiedness base-change at the chosen point through the canonical ordinary tensor comparison. Applying the existing etaleness theorem for this actual open restriction proves that the second projection is localized etale. The established equivalence between localized etaleness of a strong map and canonical strong etaleness proves `finiteEtalePushoutSnd_strongEtale`. Packaging these maps and the point proves `exists_strongEtale_pointwiseSquare_of_finiteBanach`; the last theorem records the localized predicate used by the site.

The coefficient-field universe `u` and the small space and atlas-index universe `v` are independent. There is no nontrivial-valuation, strict-radius, ambient Hausdorffness, compactness or equal-universe premise. The finite Banach hypothesis, supplied spectrum smallness, and pointwise etaleness at the prescribed source point remain explicit. Etaleness of `f` at every source point is unnecessary.

This is an internal derived specialization of the published finite Banach pushout proof, finite tensor comparison proof and `SmallAffinoidEtalePointwiseSquare`. It provides an affinoid prerequisite. General geometric base change, localized cancellation, and the unrestricted `EtalePointwiseLiftingStatement` remain separate obligations.
