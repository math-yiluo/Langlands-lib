# Density of seminorms not vanishing at a given element

Module `Langlands.Geometry.Analytic.SeparatedAnalytificationDensity`, namespace
`Langlands.Analytic.SeparatedAnalytificationDensity`.

This is a helper module for the proof that a locally finite type scheme over a complete
ultrametric nontrivially normed field is separated if and only if its analytification is
Hausdorff (`SchemeAnalytificationSeparatedStatement`). The direction "Hausdorff implies
separated" uses the valuative criterion. Given a valuation ring `R` with two lifts
`Spec R → X`, the main lemma here supplies a multiplicative seminorm with the following
properties: it vanishes on the closed point, and it is a limit of seminorms that come from
the generic point. Nothing here is new mathematics. The statements are internal lemmas,
written for this proof. The route is a standard approximation argument: Noether
normalization, a rigid point over an algebraic closure, and a limit along an ultrafilter of
points of affine space. It is not taken from a particular source.

Topologies are pointwise convergence on `D → ℝ` (the product topology), as in the published
Berkovich spectrum `AffineAnalytificationSeminorm`.

## Main statements

* `exists_mulRingSeminorm_mem_closure` (**density lemma**). Let `K` be a complete ultrametric
  nontrivially normed field, `D` a domain of finite type over `K`, `I ≠ ⊤` an ideal of `D`
  and `g ≠ 0` in `D`. Then there is a multiplicative ring seminorm `η` on `D` with three
  properties: it extends the norm of `K` (`η (algebraMap K D c) = ‖c‖`), it vanishes on
  `I`, and its underlying function lies in the closure of the set of underlying functions of
  seminorms `ν` that extend the norm of `K` with `ν g ≠ 0`.
* `exists_mulRingSeminorm_mem_closure_of_isAlgClosed`. The same conclusion over an arbitrary
  normed field `K`, given an algebraically closed nontrivially normed field `Ω` with an
  algebra map `K → Ω` that preserves the norm.
* `exists_mulRingSeminorm_kernel_eq_of_finite`. The core case. `D` is a finite algebra with
  injective structure map over a polynomial ring `MvPolynomial (Fin s) K` (compatible with `K`), `𝔪` is a
  maximal ideal of `D`, and `χ : D →ₐ[K] Ω` has kernel `𝔪`. Then the limit seminorm `η` can
  be chosen with kernel exactly `𝔪`.

## Auxiliary statements

* `exists_ringHom_comp_eq_of_isIntegral`. Let `S` be an integral `R`-algebra and `Ω` an
  algebraically closed field. A ring homomorphism `χ : R → Ω` whose kernel contains the
  kernel of `R → S` extends to `S → Ω`. Proof: lying over gives a prime `Q` of `S` over
  `ker χ`, and `IsAlgClosed.lift` extends the induced embedding of `R ⧸ ker χ` to the
  algebraic extension `S ⧸ Q`.
* `exists_root_norm_pow_le`. A monic polynomial `q` of positive degree over an algebraically
  closed normed field has a root `β` with `‖β‖ ^ natDegree q ≤ ‖q.coeff 0‖`. Proof: take the
  root of least norm. The constant coefficient is, up to sign, the product of all the roots.
* `norm_le_of_isRoot_of_monic`. A root `x` of a monic polynomial `q` over a normed field
  satisfies `‖x‖ ≤ max 1 (∑_{i < deg q} ‖q.coeff i‖)` (Cauchy's bound).
* `exists_tendsto_of_bounded`. Let `u x` be a family of functions `D → ℝ` indexed by `x`. If
  for every `d` the values `u x d` lie eventually (along an ultrafilter) in a compact interval
  `[0, B d]`, then the family converges in the product topology. The proof uses compactness
  of intervals.
* `normComp ψ`. The multiplicative seminorm `d ↦ ‖ψ d‖` of a ring homomorphism `ψ` into a
  normed field.
* `seminormFunctions D`. The set of functions `D → ℝ` satisfying the axioms of a
  multiplicative ring seminorm. `isClosed_seminormFunctions` shows that it is closed in the
  product topology. `ofSeminormFunctions` turns a member back into a `MulRingSeminorm`.
* `kernel ν`. The ideal `{a | ν a = 0}`. `kernel_isPrime` shows that it is prime.
* `exists_ringHom_extend_root`. Let `P` be an integrally closed domain, `D` a domain that is an
  integral `P`-algebra with injective structure map (`FaithfulSMul`), `e ∈ D`, and `φ : P → Ω` a ring
  homomorphism to an algebraically closed normed field. Then `φ` extends to `ψ : D → Ω` with
  `‖ψ e‖ ^ deg m ≤ ‖φ (m.coeff 0)‖`, where `m` is the minimal polynomial of `e` over `P`.
  Proof: choose a root `β` of the image of `m` as in `exists_root_norm_pow_le`. Evaluation at
  `β` defines `P[X] → Ω`. Its kernel contains the kernel of `P[X] → D`, `X ↦ e`, because over
  an integrally closed domain that kernel is generated by `m`
  (`minpoly.isIntegrallyClosed_dvd`). Then apply `exists_ringHom_comp_eq_of_isIntegral` to the
  integral `P[X]`-algebra `D`.
* `exists_mem_eq_of_mem_of_under_eq`. Let `D` be a finite `P`-algebra and `𝔪` a maximal ideal
  of `D`. Then some `e ∈ 𝔪` lies in no other prime ideal of `D` over `𝔪 ∩ P`. Proof: finitely
  many primes lie over `𝔪 ∩ P` (`Algebra.QuasiFinite.finite_primesOver`), so prime avoidance
  (`Ideal.subset_union_prime`) applies.

## Proof of the core case

Write `P = MvPolynomial (Fin s) K`.

1. Let `t ∈ Ω^s` be the images under `χ` of the variables. Then `χ` restricted to `P` is
   evaluation at `t`.
2. Choose `e ∈ 𝔪` as in `exists_mem_eq_of_mem_of_under_eq`. Let `m` be its minimal polynomial
   over `P`. The constant coefficient `m.coeff 0` lies in `𝔪 ∩ P`, so it vanishes at `t`.
3. The ideal `(g)` contracts to a nonzero ideal of `P` (`Ideal.under_ne_bot_of_integral_mem`).
   So some nonzero `c_g ∈ P` can be written `c_g = h * g` in `D`.
4. For each point `x ∈ Ω^s`, `exists_ringHom_extend_root` gives `ψ x : D → Ω` that extends
   evaluation at `x` and satisfies `‖ψ x e‖ ^ deg m ≤ ‖m.coeff 0 (x)‖`. Each seminorm
   `normComp (ψ x)` extends the norm of `K`.
5. A nonzero polynomial cannot vanish on a product of infinite sets (`MvPolynomial.funext_set`).
   Balls in `Ω` are infinite (`infinite_of_mem_nhds`, since `Ω` is nontrivially normed). So `t`
   lies in the closure of `{x | c_g(x) ≠ 0}`. Fix an ultrafilter `𝒰` finer than the
   neighbourhood filter of `t` within that set.
6. For each `d ∈ D`, `ψ x d` is a root of the minimal polynomial of `d` with coefficients
   evaluated at `x`. Cauchy's bound and continuity of the coefficients in `x` bound
   `‖ψ x d‖` eventually along `𝒰`. By `exists_tendsto_of_bounded` the seminorms
   `normComp (ψ x)` converge to a function `η₀`. It lies in the closed set
   `seminormFunctions D`, so it is a multiplicative seminorm `η`.
7. On `P`, `η p = ‖p(t)‖`. Passing to the limit in step 4 gives
   `η e ^ deg m ≤ ‖m.coeff 0 (t)‖ = 0`, so `η e = 0`. Therefore `kernel η` is a prime of `D`.
   It contains `e`, and its contraction to `P` is the kernel of evaluation at `t`, which is
   `𝔪 ∩ P`. By the choice of `e`, `kernel η = 𝔪`.
8. Eventually along `𝒰`, `c_g(x) ≠ 0`. Hence `ψ x g ≠ 0`, since `ψ x c_g = ψ x h · ψ x g`.
   So `η` is a limit of seminorms in the target set, and it lies in the closure.

## Proof of the general statements

For `exists_mulRingSeminorm_mem_closure_of_isAlgClosed`, enlarge `I` to a maximal ideal `𝔪`.
By Zariski's lemma (`finite_of_finite_type_of_isJacobsonRing`), `D ⧸ 𝔪` is finite over `K`, so
`IsAlgClosed.lift` embeds it in `Ω`. This gives `χ` with kernel `𝔪`. Noether normalization
(`exists_finite_inj_algHom_of_fg`) makes `D` a finite, faithful algebra over some
`MvPolynomial (Fin s) K`. Then apply the core case.

For `exists_mulRingSeminorm_mem_closure`, take `Ω = AlgebraicClosure K` with the spectral norm
(`spectralNorm.nontriviallyNormedField`). It extends the norm of `K` (`spectralNorm_extends`).
This step is where completeness and the ultrametric inequality are used.
