# Finite full geometric fibres of étale maps

Let an analytic étale map and a full point of its target be given. If the ordinary fibre over the underlying point is compact, then the fibre of full points with the same values is finite.

For a finite recognized chart pair on a strong representative, a full source point over the target character determines an algebra homomorphism from the finite source algebra to the value field. The target character gives the algebra structure on the value field. Identical evaluations give identical norms and hence the same ordinary spectrum point. The extension theorem for chart characters and injectivity of full recognition then identify the full source points.

The algebra homomorphisms form a finite type. After scalar extension the finite algebra over the value field is Artinian. Distinct algebra homomorphisms to the value field have distinct kernels, since either homomorphism sends scalars to themselves. Their kernels therefore give an injection into the finite prime spectrum. Around each ordinary point of the fibre, local finiteness gives a recognized chart pair and an open neighborhood on which this argument applies. A finite subcover of the compact ordinary fibre gives finiteness for the whole geometric fibre. Arrow isomorphisms transport the proof to the localized analytic category.

This derived result uses the finite neighborhoods of Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), Lemma 3.1.2, printed pp. 54–55, and the completed-residue-field formulation of finite étale germs in Theorem 3.4.1, printed p. 65. It supplies the finite cardinal used in the ordered-frame construction of a Galois refinement.
