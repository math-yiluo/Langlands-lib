# Lifts on covers by actual small chart spectra

For a saturated analytic atlas, the small spectrum of a chart algebra
and the domain of the chart are isomorphic over the ambient space.
The comparison identifies the greatest-chart algebras and the complete
strong inclusion maps. The proofs work with a complete ultrametric
normed field.

For a family of charts whose loci form a quasinet cover, their small
spectrum inclusions detect equality of localized morphisms. For a pair
of jointly monic localized arrows, any two prescribed composites with
those arrows that admit a common lift on each chart spectrum admit a
common lift on the whole source. The chart comparison transfers the local
lifts to original domain objects. Joint monicity identifies the two
restrictions on each overlap. The domain gluing theorem gives the global
lift, and domain separation verifies the prescribed composites.

These are internal consequences of greatest-chart comparison and
gluing for the quotient Hom on original domains.
