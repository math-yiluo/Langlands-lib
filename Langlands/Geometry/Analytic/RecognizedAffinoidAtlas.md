# The actual affinoid atlas on recognized literal subsets

This module constructs the affinoid atlas on the existing recognized special subsets. Its actual algebras, charts, literal subset family, finite component covers, bounded restrictions, full domain representations and net are all constructed from the original atlas and the three recognition conditions.

The witnessed covering-domain condition gives a finite full-domain cover of each recognized source algebra. Full bounded comparison naturality identifies each component map with the canonical restriction to its unchanged original chart. The entire restriction between two nested recognized subsets commutes with every such original restriction; this follows from the corresponding special-section identity after cancelling the constructed bounded presentation equivalence.

The ambient chart square proves injectivity and the exact literal image of each restriction's spectrum map. Apply finite-source full-domain recognition to the source component cover. Its local composites are exactly the already proved full-domain restrictions from the recognized target to the original source components. This constructs the full universal property for every recognized inclusion, against all finite positive-radius presented affinoid tests. Equality of algebra maps uses full bounded descent and naturality, including information invisible on spectrum points.

`recognitionAtlas` packages the resulting actual `IndexedAffinoidAtlas`. The recognized subset is its literal index, so equal chart ranges give equal indices. The previously proved recognized-family net supplies the net axiom. Original local Hausdorffness is retained on the unchanged ambient space. The chart equations, full-test restrictions, identity and composition are the proved construction laws.

`originalRecognitionIndex` embeds every original chart range in the new family. `originalRecognitionAlgebraEquiv` constructs its full bounded algebra comparison, and `recognitionAtlas_original_chart` identifies the corresponding actual chart under the contravariant spectrum homeomorphism. `fullRecognitionAtlas` applies the construction to the already constructed enlarged full local-domain atlas. Its chart family is exactly the full recognized affinoid family built from source barred-net special covers, and it contains every original local-domain locus.

The source motivation is Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), Proposition 1.2.13(ii), printed p.21 / PDF p.18, the two-stage construction of affinoid-domain restrictions and the extension atlas. The implemented proof uses finite original subordinate covers and the already proved bounded finite-domain descent. It does not assume the unproved intersection target used by the source route.

The coefficient field is complete and ultrametric with arbitrary valuation. Coefficient, ambient and original-index universes are independent; the constructed indices are literal subsets in the ambient universe. Nonreduced or zero algebras and empty domains remain allowed. The construction does not assume an output atlas, output presentation, chart inverse or full inclusion property. Uniqueness as an analytic extension, full closure, invariance under equivalent underlying nets and idempotence of recognition remain further obligations; this module does not claim those maximality results.
