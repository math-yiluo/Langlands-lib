# Chartwise agreement over finite unramified pieces

A bounded agreement neighbourhood gives a smaller rational chart on which two full analytic maps agree. The rational domain is realized in the saturated source atlas. Its bounded restriction identifies the pullbacks of the common target chart, which determines the complete strong maps on that restriction.

For a finite unramified piece given in full recognition, both maps can be compared using its recognized chart. Composition of the bounded maps gives agreement over the base. Equality at a full field point gives vanishing of the seminorm of each coefficient difference. Applying the agreement neighbourhood and cancelling the quasi-isomorphism to full recognition gives equality with the original target.
