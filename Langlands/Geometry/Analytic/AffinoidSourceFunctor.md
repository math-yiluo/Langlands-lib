# Actual affinoids in the source quotient category

This module constructs a fully faithful functor from actual presented affinoid spaces to the constructed category of saturated atlas presentations with source-refinement quotient morphisms. It gives an inverse on every Hom type, reads the complete bounded algebra map from that inverse, and preserves the original spectrum functor.

The coefficient field `K : Type u` is a complete normed field with ultrametric distance. `PresentedAffinoidSpace K` is the opposite category of actual finite positive-polyradius Tate-algebra quotients by displayed closed ideals, equipped with their constructed quotient norms. Its morphisms retain the whole contravariant bounded algebra homomorphism. Singleton atlases have space universe `u` and index universe zero; their saturation and the source quotient category have space and index universes `u`. The result includes empty spectra, trivially valued fields and nonreduced algebras. None of nonemptiness, reducedness, nontrivial valuation or faithfulness of point maps on algebra homomorphisms is assumed.
