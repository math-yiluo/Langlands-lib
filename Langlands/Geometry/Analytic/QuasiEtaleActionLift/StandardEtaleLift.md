# Nearby lifts through a standard étale presentation

Author: Worker 786.

This internal lemma gives the algebraic step for lifts of nearby maps in
Berkovich, *Vanishing cycles for formal schemes* (1994), §7, Lemma 7.3
and the affinoid case of Key Lemma 7.2. A reference map out of a finite
Banach algebra factors through a standard étale algebra. The lemma gives
finitely many spectral tests on the coefficient map, uniformly over bounded
specializations of the reference algebra.

The standard étale presentation supplies a polynomial, a simple root, and
an inverted value of a second polynomial. After perturbing the coefficient
map, the nearby-root lemma gives a root with small spectral displacement.
Tests on the inverted polynomial make its displacement smaller than its
reference value at every spectrum point. The ultrametric inequality then
preserves its value at those points, so the affinoid units criterion makes it
invertible. The difference-of-inverses identity bounds the displacement of
its inverse.

The new root and unit define a map from the standard étale algebra. Its
restriction to the finite Banach algebra is bounded by the existing
finite Banach triangle lemma. Each requested output element has a
polynomial numerator and a power of the inverted value as denominator.
Finite spectral control of these expressions gives the output bounds.
