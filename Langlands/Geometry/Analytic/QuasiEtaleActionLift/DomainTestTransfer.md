# Transferring spectral tests to represented affinoid domains

Author: Worker 786.

This internal formulation gives the finite-test estimate for restriction to
an affinoid domain used in Berkovich, *Vanishing cycles for formal schemes*
(1994), §6, Proposition 6.1 and §7, Key Lemma 7.2. A structural map
has spectral test transfer if finite tests in the ambient algebra suffice
for a perturbation to factor through the domain algebra with prescribed
displacement on finitely many elements there.

The proof reduces the property for a represented domain to the property for
rational charts. Choose a finite rational cover of the domain and transfer
the requested tests on each member. A union of the ambient tests and the
minimum of their bounds gives a simultaneous estimate. Pull the rational
cover back along the reference map into the test affinoid. The rational
property applies on each member of this cover and shows that the new map
lands in the original domain. Its universal property supplies its bounded
factor and identifies the restrictions of this factor with the factors
already obtained on the rational cover. Each point of the test spectrum
lifts to some member of that cover, where the prescribed estimate holds.
