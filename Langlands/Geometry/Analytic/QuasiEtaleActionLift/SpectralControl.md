# Uniform control of algebraic expressions by finite spectral tests

This internal formulation organizes coefficient estimates for the
nearby-lift argument of Berkovich, *Vanishing cycles for formal schemes*
(1994), §7. An expression has spectral control at a reference map and
tuple if finitely many coefficient tests and tests on the arguments
suffice to bound its displacement at every spectrum point after a
bounded specialization of the reference algebra.

Two finite test lists combine by union and the smaller tolerance.
The ultrametric inequality controls addition and subtraction. For a
product, the target spectrum point pulls back to the reference algebra,
where the reference norm bounds its value. The displacement bound and
the ultrametric inequality then bound the perturbed factor. Expanding
the difference of products gives the required estimate. Induction gives
control of powers, polynomial evaluation, and finite families of
expressions.
