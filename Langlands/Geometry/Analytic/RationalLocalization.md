# Rational quotients with affinoid-algebra inputs: proof

Author: Worker 36 (Lean proof and original Markdown). Markdown revised by
Worker 247.

`RationalLocalization.localization` proves `LocalizationStatement` from
`RationalLocalization.Statement`. The input is an arbitrary
`PresentedStrictAffinoidAlgebra K` over a complete ultrametric normed
field `K`, an actual element `g` of that algebra and a finite tuple `f`
of its elements. Empty tuples, the zero quotient algebra and trivially
valued fields are allowed; no nontriviality instance is assumed. The
extension targets live in an independent universe `v`.

## Route

The construction is in `RationalLocalization.Definitions`. It replaces
`P` by its relation model `model K P`, built from the presentation's own
closed ideal, with the bounded equivalence `modelEquiv`. `seriesLift`
picks Tate-series representatives, and `algebra`, `baseMap` and
`fractionCoordinate` are the presentation-level rational quotient,
its base map composed with the inverse model equivalence, and its new
coordinates. Each main result below applies the corresponding
presentation-level theorem from the `RationalPresentation`,
`RationalSpectrum` or `RationalTransport` modules to these lifts and
rewrites with the projection equations `presentationMap_seriesLift`
and `modelEquiv_presentationMap_seriesLift`. The remaining helpers,
`generatesUnitIdeal_model`, `modelPoint_mem_locus`,
`modelTransport_seriesLift`, `spectrum_equiv_over_base` and
`existsUnique_extension`, are proved directly or from the other helpers
in this module. No result assumes its conclusion.

## Helper results

- `coordinate_equation` gives `baseMap(g) * Y_j = baseMap(f_j)` from
  `RationalPresentation.coordinate_equation`.
- `generatesUnitIdeal_model` transports the unit-ideal hypothesis,
  stated in `P`, to the relation model. The ideal generated by the
  images under the inverse model equivalence pulls back to an ideal
  containing the original generators, hence containing one.
- `isUnit_baseMap_denominator` derives invertibility of `baseMap(g)`
  from `RationalPresentation.isUnit_baseMap_denominator`.
- `comap_mem_locus` shows that every quotient spectrum point restricts
  into `locus`, the inequalities `x(f_j) ≤ x(g)` in the base spectrum.
- `modelPoint_mem_locus` sends a point of `locus` to the presentation
  locus through the model spectrum homeomorphism. `pointLift` then uses
  `RationalPresentation.pointLift`, which is constructed in the actual
  completed residue field of that point. `comap_pointLift` proves that
  the lift restricts to the original point.
- `range_comap_baseMap` combines these into equality of the spectrum
  image with `locus` under the unit-ideal hypothesis.
- `comap_baseMap_injective` transports
  `RationalPresentation.comap_baseMap_injective` through the model
  equivalence. `comap_baseMap_closedEmbedding` concludes, since the
  spectrum map is continuous from a compact space to a Hausdorff space.
- `modelTransport` composes the model equivalences of `P` and `Q` with
  a bounded equivalence `e : P ≃ Q`; `modelTransport_seriesLift`
  computes it on lifted elements. `equiv` applies
  `RationalPresentation.equiv` to it. The two presentations may have
  different arities, and no unit-ideal hypothesis is used.
  `equiv_comp_baseMap`, `equiv_fractionCoordinate` and
  `spectrum_equiv_over_base` give compatibility with the base maps, the
  new coordinates and the base spectrum homeomorphism. The last follows
  from `equiv_comp_baseMap` by evaluating spectrum points.
- `liftFromBase` applies `RationalPresentation.liftFromBase` to the
  composite of a bounded map `φ : P → B` with the model equivalence. It
  requires a tuple `b` with uniformly bounded monomials satisfying
  `φ(g) * b_j = φ(f_j)`; the target `B` is complete and ultrametric.
  `liftFromBase_comp_baseMap` and `liftFromBase_fractionCoordinate`
  give its base restriction and coordinate values.
- `hom_ext_base` proves, under the unit-ideal hypothesis, that bounded
  maps out of the quotient into any normed algebra are determined by
  their restriction to `P`. It uses `RationalPresentation.hom_ext_base`.
  `existsUnique_extension` combines it with `liftFromBase`.

## Closing theorem

`localization` introduces the field and inputs and proves the five
clauses. The first two are `coordinate_equation` and `comap_mem_locus`.
The third, under the unit-ideal hypothesis, uses
`isUnit_baseMap_denominator`, `range_comap_baseMap`,
`comap_baseMap_closedEmbedding` and `hom_ext_base`. The fourth chooses
`liftFromBase` with its two formulas. The fifth chooses `equiv` with
its three compatibility formulas. The bounded-monomial premise stays
explicit; it is not replaced by a spectral-image hypothesis, so the
full analytic-domain property remains a separate obligation.
