# Recognized images of affinoid pieces of étale charts

Work over a complete ultrametric nontrivially normed field `K`. Take
strong maps `q` and `f` between saturated presentations with `q` étale
on all pieces, and align open finite étale neighborhoods of `q` and
its composite with `f`. The intermediate space is Hausdorff and its
full recognition analytifies a separated algebraic space locally of
finite type over `K`. Assume that every map from a presented affinoid
spectrum into the full recognition of the source, followed by the
localized map of `q`, is induced by an affinoid ring-valued point
of that algebraic space.

Take a recognized chart contained in the composite's target
neighborhood. Its preimage in the composite's source neighborhood is
an actual affinoid pullback. The point associated to that piece gives
a finite étale equivalence relation and an affinoid quotient.

The quotient map has image equal to the intersection of the open
cover image and the inverse image of the target chart. This is an
analytic domain. Every chart of that domain has a finite étale
surjective cover by a compact open cut of the cover neighborhood.
Finite affinoid Hom descent and gluing identify the quotient with
the domain. The reverse recognition of the entire quotient chart
recognizes that domain inside the original intermediate atlas.

This is an internal consequence of the actual affinoid point comparison,
the finite étale relation quotient, the open-cut covers and invariance
of full recognition under iteration.
