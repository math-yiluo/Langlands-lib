# Common structure maps of quasi-étale products

For two morphisms with domain–étale factorizations, their full product over the common base is a domain in the product of the two étale envelopes. The étale product theorem supplies the common structure map of those envelopes. Intersecting the two inverse-image domains identifies the product of the original domains by its full universal property.

For maps with local domain–étale factorizations, pair the finite families of domains through the two projection points. Their inverse-image intersections form a finite domain neighbourhood and inherit the first construction. The existing local recognition theorem applies to a pair of quasi-étale maps with Hausdorff sources, so the product's common structure map is quasi-étale. The ambient base is arbitrary.
