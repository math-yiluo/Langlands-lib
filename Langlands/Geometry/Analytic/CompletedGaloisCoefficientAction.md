# The absolute Galois group on the completed algebraic closure

For a finite normed extension `F` of `ℚ_p`, the algebraic closure carries
its spectral norm and `Completed p F` is its metric completion. The
norms extend that of `F`. `galoisCoefficientAction` is the homomorphism
from the actual absolute Galois group into the isometric `F`-algebra
automorphisms used by analytic scalar extension. It uses the continuous
extension of each algebraic automorphism, with the original multiplication
and identity laws.

`continuous_galoisCoefficientOrbit` proves continuity for every vector
of the completed field, with the Krull topology on the group. An algebraic
approximation to the vector has an open stabilizer. Within a coset of that
stabilizer, two automorphisms agree on the approximation. Isometry bounds
the distance between the images of the original vector by twice the
approximation error.

The coefficient continuity is the input for Berkovich's canonical
analytic action on the scalar extension to the completed algebraic
closure. See Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic
spaces*, §1.1.1(i), printed and PDF p. 2. Retained PDF SHA-256
