# Full strong-map descent inside an actual original-chart domain

Author: Worker 36.

This derived construction fixes actual indexed affinoid atlas presentations
`A` and `B` over a complete ultrametric normed field, an actual
original-chart domain `S` of `A`, and an arbitrarily indexed family `D` of
ambient original-chart domains contained in `S`. From complete strong maps
on the domains `D k` agreeing on literal ambient intersections, it constructs
a complete strong map on the actual constructed presentation of `S`.
The sufficient coverage hypothesis is explicit: every whole original chart
contained in `S` lies in a family member. The construction recovers every
local map, including all its bounded algebra pullbacks, and proves uniqueness
and an equivalence with matching families. The same hypothesis also gives a
quasinet cover of `S` in the published domain topology.

The source conventions are Berkovich, *Etale cohomology for non-Archimedean
analytic spaces* (1993), section 1.1, printed pp.13–14 / PDF pp.10–11
(induced topology, quasinet and net), Definition 1.2.3 and Remark 1.2.4(i),
printed p.16 / PDF p.13 (actual atlases and bounded restrictions),
Definition 1.2.7, printed p.18 / PDF p.15 (full strong maps),
Definition 1.3.1, printed p.23 / PDF p.20 (contained-chart domain atlases),
and the G-topology paragraph in section 1.3, printed p.25 / PDF p.22
(coverings by quasinet families of domains). The registered source is
`berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA256
`06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`.
These passages were reread for this unit. The localized descent argument
is derived from the published full matching-family descent and natural
localization equivalence on fixed-original-chart domains. It is not
attributed to a separately numbered source theorem.

`OriginalDomainCoversChartsWithin S D` quantifies over the original chart
indices whose whole ranges lie in `S`, and asks that each range domain
`ofChart c.val` lie in some `D k`. Containment `hDS : ∀ k, D k ≤ S`
is a separate input. `OriginalDomainCoversChartsWithin.ofArrows_mem`
proves that the sieve generated by these inclusions covers `S` for
`quasinetTopology A.atlas`. The canonical family of all original charts
contained in `S` already covers. Every arrow in its generated sieve
factors through a chart, hence through a containing `D k`; monotonicity of
the topology then proves the result. The `isQuasinet` theorem applies the
published exact generated-sieve criterion to recover the full finite
relative-neighborhood condition on the subspace of `S`.
`toSubspace` proves the corresponding whole-chart coverage statement for
the constructed atlas on `S` and the nested domains
`S.subspaceRestrict ⟨D k, hDS k⟩`.

The matching data use the published
`OriginalDomainStrongMatchingFamily A B D`: every local value is an entire
`StrongHom` from the actual presentation of `D k` to `B`, and overlap
requires equality of the entire direct restrictions to `D k ⊓ D l`.
`StrongHom.toOriginalDomainStrongMatchingFamilyWithin` restricts an
actual full map on `S` to all ambient `D k`. Direct-restriction transitivity
proves its overlap equations.

The construction first treats a family `U` of domains in the constructed
atlas on `S`. Their ambient images are `S.subspaceExtend (U k)`.
`StrongHom.toNestedOriginalDomain_restrictFromDomain` identifies
transport of an ambient-image restriction with ordinary nested subspace
restriction. Full strong-map extensionality proves this identity:
the continuous maps and all bounded pullbacks agree by reduction.
`OriginalDomainStrongMatchingFamily.toNestedFamily` transports each
ambient-image local map by the published full localization equivalence.
For each nested intersection it restricts the ambient matching equation
to the smaller ambient image of that intersection and applies the proved
restriction naturality of the transport. This supplies equality of
complete nested local maps.

`glueIn` applies the published full matching-family descent on the actual
presentation of `S`. That descent constructs continuous maps using the
actual chart-range colimit and constructs every bounded pullback from
the given local data. `glueIn_restrictOriginalDomain` proves recovery of
each full ambient-image local map: injectivity of
`originalDomainStrongEquiv A B S (U k)` reduces it to nested local
recovery, using the restriction identity above. `glueIn_unique` transports
any proposed map's ambient-image restrictions to nested restrictions and
applies the same published descent uniqueness.

For arbitrary ambient domains `D k ≤ S`, the published domain localization
order isomorphism gives nested domains
`U k = S.subspaceRestrict ⟨D k, hDS k⟩`, with
`S.subspaceExtend (U k) = D k`. Private `glueTransport` and its recovery
and uniqueness lemmas eliminate the resulting equality of entire domain
families. Thus transport retains all the original matching data.
`glueWithin` uses these nested domains, the proved `toSubspace` coverage,
and `glueTransport` to construct the required map on `S`.
No global map or bounded pullback is among its inputs.

`glueWithin_restrictOriginalDomain` recovers every entire local map on
its literal ambient domain. `glueWithin_apply` extracts the point formula.
`glueWithin_pullback` gives equality of entire bounded algebra maps at
every contained source chart and admissible target chart, with separate
admissibility witnesses for the constructed and supplied maps. It follows
from full local recovery by `StrongHom.pullback_congr` and the direct
restriction admissibility equivalence. No equality of algebra maps is
inferred from equality of spectrum-point values.
`glueWithin_unique` proves uniqueness from these full local restrictions.
`glueWithin_toOriginalDomainStrongMatchingFamilyWithin` recovers the
whole matching-family record. Finally, `strongHomWithinEquiv` bundles
gluing and restriction; full local recovery proves one inverse law and
uniqueness proves the other.

The coefficient, point, chart-index and family-index universes `u`, `v`,
`w` and `z` remain independent. The target atlas is arbitrary; no
directedness, reducedness or explicit nonemptiness hypothesis is imposed.
Empty cases retain the literal coverage hypothesis. This result concerns
actual domains defined using contained charts of a fixed supplied atlas.
Whole-contained-chart coverage is stronger than arbitrary quasinet
coverage, so the result does not prove the general sheaf condition for
the strong-map presheaf. Atlas saturation and extension, arbitrary-cover
affinoid algebra descent, unrestricted strong composition, structure
sheaves, etale and equivariant sites and the Harris tower remain separate
obligations.
