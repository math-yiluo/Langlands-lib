# Ordinary points of analytic étale covering sieves

`Langlands.Analytic.SaturatedAtlasPresentation.etaleCoveringPoints` proves
`EtaleCoveringPointsStatement` for every complete ultrametric normed field
and every base in `LocalizedCategory.{u, v, v}`. For each point of an étale
object and each covering sieve on that object, it gives a sieve arrow whose
ordinary point map has the given point in its image.

The Lean proof fixes the base, the étale object, and a covering sieve. It
passes the published `etalePointwiseLifting` theorem to
`jointlySurjective_of_mem_etaleTopology_of_pointwiseLifting`. The former
supplies commuting squares in the étale site through compatible pairs of
ordinary points. The latter applies the generic covering criterion to the
Grothendieck topology generated by the jointly surjective precoverage,
yielding the required arrow and preimage point for the given sieve.

This proof derives the Library's formulation of the covering property in
Berkovich's *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces*, §4.1.
The coefficient-field universe `u` and the space and chart-index universe
`v` are independent throughout.
