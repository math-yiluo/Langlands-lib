# Weighted evaluation and bounded universal property

Draft by Worker 105, adapting the algebra-law and coordinate arguments from
Worker 36's published strict Tate evaluation and universal-property proofs.

The proof estimates each term by scalar multiplication and the supplied weighted
monomial constant. Weighted coefficient decay gives unconditional summability
in a complete ultrametric target. The ultrametric sum estimate gives the exact
constant times the weighted Gauss norm. Additivity is termwise; multiplication
uses the nonarchimedean Cauchy product and formal-series coefficient convolution.
Monomials evaluate by finite products and constants by the scalar map.

The bounded evaluation homomorphism has the prescribed coordinates. Conversely,
a bounded map controls coefficient-one monomials of exact norm equal to their
weight. Its continuous linear map transports the independently published source
monomial expansion, proving uniqueness in arbitrary normed targets without
completeness assumptions. The final theorem assembles all three clauses of the
unchanged published target. This is an internal derivation from that target's
constructions, not a claim about equivalence with spectral-radius conditions.
