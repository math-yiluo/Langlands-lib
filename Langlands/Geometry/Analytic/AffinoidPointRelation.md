# Affinoid relation of a ring-valued point over an affinoid base

Let `K` be a complete ultrametric nontrivially normed field. Take a
finite Banach map `A → B` of presented affinoid `K`-algebras and a
`B`-valued point `x` of a separated algebraic space over `K`. The
relation algebra is the closed quotient of the affinoid self-pushout
of `B` over `A` by the ideal of equality of the two changes of
coefficients of `x`.

The finite tensor comparison gives the ordinary ring pushout with
its two coefficient maps. Each map is finite by base change and
remains finite after the quotient. Their images generate the quotient
as a ring by the tensor-product induction and surjectivity of the
quotient map.

For every commutative algebra over the ground field, two coefficient
maps from `B` factor through the relation exactly when they agree on
`A` and induce equal changes of coefficients of `x`. The ordinary
pushout gives the required algebra map before taking the quotient.
The closed section equalizer gives precisely the condition that this
map annihilate the quotient ideal. The maps also define an equivalence
relation on ordinary ring maps to every test ring. A factorization
through the relation agrees on the ground field, which lets the proof
test both maps in the same algebra structure. Reflexivity, symmetry
and transitivity then follow from the two equality conditions.

This is an internal application of the Library's tensor comparison
and closed section equalizer.
