# Closed polydiscs of smaller radius as affinoid domains

Author: Worker 21.

This module shows that a closed polydisc of smaller polyradius is an affinoid
domain of a closed polydisc of larger polyradius, represented by the
coefficient-preserving restriction of power series. It is the gluing datum for
the analytic affine space as an increasing union of closed polydiscs.

All declarations lie in
`Langlands.Analytic.PresentedAffinoidAlgebra.Polydisc`. Let `K` be an
ultrametric normed field, `d` a natural number, and `s`, `t` positive
polyradii on `Fin d` with `t i ≤ s i` for every `i` (the hypothesis `hts`).
`polydisc K d s` is the existing zero-ideal presentation of the closed polydisc
`E(0, s)`; its algebra is the weighted Tate algebra `K{s⁻¹T}` with variables
`T 0, …, T (d-1)`, and `coordinate K (polydisc K d s) i` is the class of `T i`.

- Definition 2.2.1, printed p.27 / PDF p.41: a closed subset `V` of
  `X = 𝓜(𝒜)` is an affinoid domain if there is a bounded homomorphism
  `𝒜 → 𝒜_V` of affinoid algebras through which every bounded homomorphism
  `𝒜 → ℬ` whose spectrum image lies in `V` factors uniquely.
- Remark 2.2.2(i), same page: `X(p⁻¹f, qg⁻¹) = {x | |f_i(x)| ≤ p_i,
  |g_j(x)| ≥ q_j}` is an affinoid domain represented by
  `𝒜 → 𝒜{p⁻¹T, qS}/(T_i - f_i, g_j S_j - 1)`. The case `g = 1`, `q = 1` is
  written `X(p⁻¹f)`, and "Affinoid domains of the form `X(p⁻¹f)` ... are called
  Weierstrass ... domains in `X`".

The smaller polydisc `E(0, t) = {x ∈ E(0, s) | |T_i(x)| ≤ t_i}` is the
Weierstrass domain `E(0, s)(t⁻¹T)`. The identification of its representing
algebra with `K{t⁻¹T}` is the derived content of this module.

## Radius restriction

`restriction s t hts` is the bounded `K`-algebra map from `polydisc K d s` to
`polydisc K d t`. It is the quotient lift of the existing contractive map
`boundedRestrictRadius s t hts`, which keeps every coefficient of a series,
followed by the quotient map of `polydisc K d t`. The lift is well defined
because the ideal of `polydisc K d s` is zero.

- `restriction_presentationMap`: the class of a series goes to the class of the
  same series (by definition).
- `restriction_coordinate`: `T i` goes to `T i`.
- `restriction_self`: restriction to the same polyradius is the identity.
- `restriction_trans`: restriction from `s` to `q` is restriction from `t` to
  `q` after restriction from `s` to `t`.

The last two are proved with the existing extensionality principle
`hom_ext_coordinate` for maps out of a presented affinoid algebra.

## The Weierstrass chart

`weierstrassChart s t` is the existing rational chart over `polydisc K d s`
with `d` new variables, radii `t`, denominator `1` and numerators the variables
`T i`. The unit denominator generates the unit ideal. Its algebra is the existing
actual rational quotient: the weighted Tate algebra in the variables `T` (radii
`s`) and new variables `Z` (radii `t`), modulo the closure of the ideal generated by the
equations `1 · Z_i - T_i`. Its locus is by definition
`{x | x(T i) ≤ t i · x(1)}`, and `weierstrassChart_locus` rewrites it as
`{x | x(T i) ≤ t i for all i}`.

From here `K` is assumed complete.

- `fromChart s t hts` is the existing universal map out of the rational quotient
  (`RationalPresentation.liftFromBase`). It is `restriction s t hts` on the base
  polydisc and sends each new variable `Z_i` to `T i`. The required equations
  `1 · T i = T i` follow from `restriction_coordinate`, and the weighted
  monomial bound for the tuple `T` follows from `‖T i‖ ≤ t i` and `‖1‖ ≤ 1`.
  `fromChart_comp_baseMap` records that it extends `restriction s t hts`.
- `toChart s t` is the quotient lift of intrinsic bounded evaluation of
  `K{t⁻¹T}` at the tuple of fraction coordinates `Z_i`, whose weighted monomial
  bound follows from the existing fraction norm bound `‖Z_i‖ ≤ t i`.
  `toChart_coordinate` proves `T i ↦ Z_i`.
- `fromChart_toChart` and `toChart_fromChart` show the two maps are mutually
  inverse. The first is checked on the variables `T i`. The second uses the
  existing uniqueness principle `RationalPresentation.hom_ext_base` for maps
  out of the rational quotient, together with the existing denominator-one
  identity `Z_i = baseMap(T i)` (`RationalPresentation.weierstrass_fraction`).
- `chartEquiv s t hts` is the resulting bounded algebra equivalence between the
  Weierstrass chart algebra and `polydisc K d t`.

## Representation

`represents_restriction s t hts` proves

```
Represents K (restriction s t hts) {x | ∀ i, x (T i) ≤ t i}.
```

`Represents` is the existing form of Definition 2.2.1: the set is closed, it is
the spectrum image of the map, and every bounded map from `polydisc K d s` to a
presented affinoid algebra whose spectrum image lies in the set factors
uniquely through the map. The proof transports the existing representation
theorem of the Weierstrass chart (`RationalChart.represents`) along
`chartEquiv` with the existing `Represents.postcomp_equiv`, and identifies the
composite `chartEquiv ∘ baseMap` with `restriction s t hts` by
`fromChart_comp_baseMap`.

`range_comap_restriction` extracts the spectrum-image part: the image of
the spectrum of `polydisc K d t` in the spectrum of `polydisc K d s` is exactly
`{x | ∀ i, x (T i) ≤ t i}`.

## Scope

Nothing here assumes that the valuation is nontrivial or that the radii lie in
the value group. The module does not define the affine space itself; the
increasing union of polydiscs is built by gluing in a separate module.
