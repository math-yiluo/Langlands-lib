# Restricting chosen atlas maps to actual chart domains

For a complete nonarchimedean normed field `K`, this module starts with actual supplied indexed affinoid atlases and a morphism `f : A ⟶ B` of `AffinoidAtlasPresentation`. Such a morphism includes a continuous map, a monotone chosen target chart for each source chart, and actual bounded pullbacks satisfying the chart and restriction squares. The imported `chartPresentation` constructs the atlas on the actual subspace of a chart domain, retaining all original charts contained in that domain.

`Hom.restrictChart f i` constructs a morphism from `A.chartPresentation i` to `B.chartPresentation (f.indexMap i)`. Its continuous map is the restriction of `f.baseMap` to these subspaces. The chart square for `f` proves that this map takes values in the selected target domain. For every original chart `c` contained in `i`, monotonicity of `f.indexMap` proves that its selected target is contained in the selected target of `i`. The restricted index map therefore retains every source index in the constructed chart presentation; it requires no further containment hypothesis.

The algebra attached to a contained chart is the original presented affinoid algebra. The pullback at this chart is exactly `f.pullback c.val`, with its original boundedness and algebra structure. Containment reflection and preservation for the constructed chart atlases convert the original monotonicity and restriction compatibility into the required restricted compatibility. The chart square follows from the original chart square by subtype extensionality. No bounded map is reconstructed from its action on spectrum points.

The three computation lemmas identify the underlying point map, selected chart index, and bounded pullback. `restrictChart_chartInclusion` proves the equality of morphisms obtained by restricting and then including into the ambient target, or including into the ambient source and then applying `f`. `restrictChart_id` and `restrictChart_comp` prove compatibility with identity and composition, with composition passing through the selected intermediate chart. `chartMap_restrictChart` proves compatibility with inclusions of nested source chart domains and their selected target domains. These equalities use the existing morphism extensionality theorem on continuous maps, index functions, and dependent bounded pullbacks; the components are definitionally equal.

This is derived infrastructure for the existing category of monotone chosen atlas maps. Its conventions come from Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), Definition 1.2.3 and Remark 1.2.4(i), printed p.16 / PDF p.13: atlases have bounded algebra restrictions, whose direction is contravariant in chart inclusion. The present construction and categorical equalities follow directly from the supplied chosen-map data and the published chart-domain construction. They are not separately numbered assertions in that source. Definition 1.2.7, printed p.18 / PDF p.15, uses all admissible chart pairs for strong morphisms. This module neither supplies those additional pairs nor constructs extended atlases, strong-morphism localization, general analytic domains, a structure sheaf, or étale sites.
