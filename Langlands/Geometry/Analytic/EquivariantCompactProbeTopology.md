# Equivariant quasi-étale compact-probe coverings

`equivariantCompactProbeQuasiEtaleTopology X` is the Grothendieck topology
on the category of quasi-étale `G`-morphisms to a `G`-space `X` generated by
`equivariantCompactProbeQuasiEtalePrecoverage X`. A family belongs to this
precoverage when the sieve generated by its underlying analytic arrows
covers in `compactProbeQuasiEtaleTopology`. The construction uses the
ordinary topology as a precoverage of families generating covering sieves,
then takes its preimage along the action-forgetting functor. Restriction
along a continuous group homomorphism preserves and reflects these families
because it retains their underlying analytic arrows.

The chart-neighbourhood test on ordinary quasi-étale presieves is invariant
under generating the sieve. Indeed, each arrow of the generated sieve
factors through an arrow of the presieve. Map the compact source chart
through the first leg of that factorization. The resulting compact subset
lies in finitely many charts of the intermediate source, by the atlas net
and compactness. The images of these intermediate charts under the
presieve arrow cover the original chart image. Finite unions of these
chart images give the required neighbourhoods.

Given the proposition `CompactProbeQuasiEtaleHausdorffComparisonStatement`,
the compact-probe equivariant precoverage agrees with the chart-neighbourhood
precoverage `equivariantQuasiEtalePrecoverage`. The comparison applies at
every target object because `GSpace` requires a Hausdorff underlying space.
The invariance under sieve generation gives exactly the test on the
underlying family. Generating Grothendieck topologies then gives equality
with `equivariantQuasiEtaleTopology` under the same premise.
