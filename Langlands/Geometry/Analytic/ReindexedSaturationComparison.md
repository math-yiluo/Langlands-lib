# Comparing saturation with a relabelled original atlas

Let an equivalence relabel the original affinoid atlas indices in the universe of the ambient space. The existing reverse saturation comparison becomes a compatible strong map from the actual saturation to the relabelled original presentation. It keeps the identity map of the ambient space and retains the bounded restrictions between the same actual domains. Reindexing preserves its full quasi-isomorphism property.

The comparison is natural for complete compatible strong maps. On a pair of retained original charts, the reverse comparison has the bounded identity pullback after identifying the algebras. The saturated map has the original bounded pullback at that pair. Restricting the resulting equality from an original parent to any saturated source chart gives equality of the entire maps.

The quasi-isomorphism property uses the domain inclusions in Berkovich's criterion for strong maps, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), Definition 1.2.9, printed p. 19 (PDF p. 16). The comparison of indices and its naturality are derived from the actual bounded restrictions at corresponding domains.
