# Restriction to distinguished opens

`IsDistinguishedOpen` says that an open subset of an analytic space has analytic-domain complement. Two distinguished opens have distinguished intersection. `IsDistinguishedEtale.restrictToDistinguishedOpen` restricts the source of a distinguished étale morphism to the inverse image of a distinguished open in its base. The resulting morphism to the original base is distinguished étale.

The proof uses the same compact quasi-étale envelope as the original distinguished étale morphism. If the original source is represented by an open `O` of the envelope, the restricted source is represented by the intersection of `O` with the inverse image of the new base open. The inverse image theorem constructs the analytic domain complementary to that inverse image. The union of this closed analytic domain with the complement of `O` is again an analytic domain. The closed-union argument takes the union of the two finite chart refinements at a point. A closed member missing the point contributes the empty refinement. Transport of the inverse-image domain across the source isomorphism and the nested-domain comparison identify the restricted morphism.

The definition of distinguished open comes from Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces*, Equish_1996 manuscript, printed p. 7, in the paragraph preceding Definition 1.4.1. The restriction lemma implements the distinguished shrinking opens in the proof of Proposition 1.5.1, step (1′), printed p. 11.
