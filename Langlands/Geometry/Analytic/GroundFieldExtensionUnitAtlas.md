# Identity ground-field extension of an analytic atlas

The projection from the scalar-extended space along the identity of the
ground field is a homeomorphism. On every extended chart it is the
spectrum homeomorphism induced by the identity-extension algebra
equivalence. Each extended chart range is the whole inverse image of its
original range. This gives injectivity of the glued projection, and the
inverse is continuous on each original chart. The quasinet of original
charts gives its continuity on the whole space.

`unitCompatibleMap` makes the projection a compatible strong map of
atlases, with the identity-extension algebra inclusion on corresponding
charts. For a larger admissible target chart, its pullback is this
inclusion composed with the original bounded restriction. Naturality
of the inclusion gives the restriction law. The bounded comparison
preserves the affinoid-domain universal property, so the map is a full
quasi-isomorphism and becomes an isomorphism in the analytic localization.
The maps are natural on compatible presentations. On a matching pair
of charts the equality is the naturality of the affinoid algebra inclusion,
and the same identity of bounded pullbacks determines the equality of
compatible strong maps.
After saturation and localization, the projection components are
invertible. On saturated source presentations, `saturatedUnitIso`
compares identity extension followed by localization with the ordinary
localization functor.

V. G. Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces*
(1993), §1.4, printed p. 30 (PDF p. 27), defines extension of the ground
field and the associated projection by scalar extension on affinoid
charts. This is the comparison for the identity extension in that
construction. Literature ID `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA-256
