# Bounded universal property of Tate relation quotients

Author: Worker 36.

`PresentedStrictAffinoidAlgebra.RelationsUniversalPropertyStatement`
describes the actual quotient by the closure of the ideal generated by
a set of Tate series. The coefficient field and target algebra have
independent universes. It has three clauses, separating the completeness
assumptions needed for existence and uniqueness.

First, a tuple in a complete ultrametric normed K-algebra with one
positive bound on all its monomials, at which every supplied relation
evaluates to zero, admits a bounded algebra map out of the quotient.
The map agrees with convergent evaluation on every projected series and
has the specified coordinate values. This clause does not assume
completeness of K.

Second, over complete K, two bounded maps from the quotient to any
normed K-algebra that agree on all coordinates are equal. The target in
this clause need not be complete or ultrametric.

Third, when K and the ultrametric target are complete, a tuple is the
coordinate image of a unique bounded map exactly when its monomials
have a uniform positive norm bound and the supplied relations vanish.
This is an explicit condition on actual elements and sums, not a
predicate asserting the desired representing map as an input.

The finite variable count may be zero, the relation set may be empty,
and the relation ideal may be the unit ideal. No nonzero target or
normalized target unit norm is required. Quotient maps, coordinates,
bounded monomials and raw series evaluation are the actual definitions
imported from the separate definition modules.

This is a derived quotient form of the bounded Tate universal property.
Berkovich's *Non-archimedean analytic spaces* (Trieste 2009), Definitions
2.1.1 and 2.1.2 and the quotient-norm paragraph, p.7, provide the
presentation conventions. Exercise 2.1.5(i), pp.7–8, provides the
extension context. The source uses spectral inequalities in affinoid
targets; the present target instead retains an explicit uniform
monomial bound and allows general normed targets under the hypotheses
of each clause. Taking the closure of the generated relation ideal
does not assume the theorem that all Tate ideals are closed.
No spectrum-locus identification, analytic-domain representability,
structure sheaf or acyclicity is asserted.
