# Continuous actions on the small strong fibre product

Each original chart of the small fibre product gives a chart of its
saturated presentation. Its locus is the intersection of the two
projection preimages of the selected factor charts. This follows from
the chart range theorem on the quotient and the small-space
homeomorphism. Every saturated chart is a local domain of one of
these original charts.

The bounded pullbacks on the two original projections are the two
maps into the raw affinoid pushout. The proof preserves the entire
bounded maps using the original-chart saturation comparison and the
bounded identity for the comparison back to the corresponding
factor. Restricting a localized strong arrow to two chart domains
computes its original bounded pullback at those charts.

For `isContinuousAction_smallFibreProduct`, the zero-function tests
on the factors and projection equivariance give eventual
preservation of an original product chart. On that event the
restricted maps still commute with the two projections, by
cancellation of the respective domain inclusions. The bounded
pullbacks on the product therefore have small spectral displacement
on the images of both factor algebras. The affinoid pushout estimate
extends this to every section of the product chart. The analytic
chart restriction theorem then proves each near-identity test on a
subordinate chart.

The result is derived for the Library's actual small strong product
and the two supplied equivariant projections. The group carries an
arbitrary topology. The smallness premise is the one used to form
the product in the common space and index universe.
