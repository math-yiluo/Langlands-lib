# The underlying action of analytic coefficient automorphisms

The projection from a successive scalar extension to the first extension,
expressed on the direct extension, pulls back the original algebra
elements and sends coefficients along the second field embedding.
For an isometric automorphism of the extension field fixing the ground
field, the composite embedding is the original embedding. The pullback
then agrees with the bounded coefficient automorphism, since the two maps
agree on both tensor factors. The bounded comparison is
`towerProjectionPullback_eq_coefficientHom`. The resulting spectrum maps identify the
underlying analytic action with the chartwise coefficient action.

For a topological group acting by coefficient automorphisms, assume that
each scalar orbit is norm continuous. The chartwise coefficient action
is then jointly continuous on the product of the group and the extended
space. The pointwise identification transfers this continuity to the
underlying maps of the analytic coefficient lifts, for any object of the
compatible localization.
