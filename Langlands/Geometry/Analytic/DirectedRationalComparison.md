# Full strong comparison with every local rational locus

Let `K : Type u` be a complete ultrametric normed field, let `X : Type v` be a topological space, and let `a : IndexedAffinoidAtlas.{u,v,w} K X` be an actual affinoid atlas whose original chart ranges are pairwise directed under containment. `DirectedRationalAtlas.atlas a hd` is the constructed atlas of every literal ambient rational locus in every original chart. This module constructs full strong comparison maps between the original and rational atlases, proves both are quasi-isomorphisms in the published full affinoid-test sense, and proves that they are inverse arrows in the category of directed atlas presentations. The coefficient, ambient and original index universes are independent. Neither a greatest original chart nor nonempty original index type is required.

`originalPresentation` lifts the original index type to `ULift.{v} a.Index`, and `presentation` lifts the rational index type to `ULift.{w} (Index a)`. Both index types therefore live in `Type (max v w)`, as required by the existing category. Reindexing retains the actual space `X`, quotient algebras, spectrum charts and complete bounded restrictions. It does not identify the chosen algebra for a whole rational locus with the original algebra by definitional equality.

`originalInclusion` makes an original restriction into an arrow of the actual represented-domain category. Its bounded map is precisely `a.restriction`, and its equation over any common original upper chart follows from the original atlas composition axiom. `fromOriginalInclusion` and `toOriginalInclusion` use the actual bounded inclusions constructed by `DirectedDomain.Inclusion.ofSubset`. They apply to every containment between an original chart range and a rational chart range in the indicated direction. Their naturality equalities follow by comparing composites in this category and applying `Inclusion.restriction`. Thus the equalities concern the complete bounded algebra maps; they are not inferred merely from agreement on spectrum points.

The reusable lemma `inclusion_represents` proves that any actual directed-domain arrow `f : U ⟶ V` represents `V.ambientMap ⁻¹' U.locus` over `V.algebra`, when the containing ambient map is injective. Ambient naturality and `U.range_ambientMap` give one inclusion of the spectrum image. For the reverse inclusion, choose a spectrum point over the given ambient point using the exact range formula for `U`; injectivity of `V.ambientMap` identifies its image under `f.spectrumMap` with the given point of `V`. The range is closed because it is the continuous image of the compact source Banach spectrum in the Hausdorff target Banach spectrum. The universal clause is the previously constructed `inclusion_universal`: directedness gives common original upper charts, and full domain factorization and uniqueness construct the bounded map and its entire structural equation. This lemma requires injectivity only for the containing ambient map. It does not assume injectivity of an arbitrary full-domain structural spectrum map.

`fromOriginal` and `toOriginal` have the identity continuous map on `X`. Every admissible pullback is the corresponding actual mixed inclusion restriction. For the forward target-existence clause, use the whole rational locus of the original source chart; its range equals the original chart range. For the reverse clause, use the chosen original chart in which the rational source locus is represented. The chart squares are exactly ambient naturality of the constructed mixed inclusions, together with `originalChart_ambientMap`. The compatibility clauses are the entire bounded naturality equations just described.

`isQuasiIso_fromOriginal` applies `inclusion_represents` to each admissible original-to-rational pair, using the proved embedding of the containing rational chart. `isQuasiIso_toOriginal` applies `represents_toOriginalChart` to each admissible reverse pair, using full localization at the containing original chart. Both underlying maps are the identity homeomorphism. The local conclusions are `PresentedAffinoidAlgebra.Represents` for the actual comparison pullback and the exact inverse-image locus. This predicate includes closedness, exact spectrum image and unique factorization for every finite positive-polyradius presented-affinoid test algebra over `K`; it is not restricted to points or strictly affinoid tests.

`original_locus_eq` identifies the ambient locus of an original whole-domain object with that of the chosen rational object for the same whole chart. `originalAlgebraEquiv` applies the actual equal-locus bounded equivalence of the directed-domain category. Its forward bounded map is the reverse comparison restriction, and its inverse bounded map is the forward comparison restriction. The two `originalAlgebraEquiv_*_toBoundedAlgHom` lemmas prove these equalities on the whole homomorphisms by uniqueness of the corresponding category arrows. `fromOriginal_pullback_ofOriginal` and `toOriginal_pullback_ofOriginal` identify the actual strong pullbacks with those inverse and forward maps. This records the algebra comparison even when the choice of rational quotient differs from the original algebra.

The two mixed-composite lemmas compare actual composites in the represented-domain category. Original-to-rational-to-original restrictions equal the original atlas restrictions, and rational-to-original-to-rational restrictions equal the constructed rational restrictions. `originalDirectedPresentation` and `directedPresentation` use original directedness and the proved directedness of the rational atlas, respectively, after the index lifts. They retain the complete strong-map families of the existing directed-presentation category.

For `fromOriginal_comp_toOriginal`, take each original source chart, its whole rational locus as intermediate chart, and the same original chart as final chart. The formula `StrongHom.compOfDirected_pullback` identifies the constructed composite pullback with the mixed composite; the first mixed-composite lemma gives the original restriction. `StrongHom.ext_of_directed` then proves equality of the entire composite strong map with the identity. For `toOriginal_comp_fromOriginal`, take the chosen original chart containing each rational source locus and its whole rational locus as final chart. The second mixed-composite lemma gives the rational restriction on this chain, and the same directed extensionality proves the full identity. The extensionality argument uses directed upper charts and uniqueness for the actual restriction maps, so equality on these chosen chains proves equality on every admissible pair.

`directedIso` packages those explicit forward and reverse strong maps and the proved full inverse laws as an actual categorical isomorphism. Its `hom` is `fromOriginal`, its `inv` is `toOriginal`, and both underlying maps are exactly `ContinuousMap.id X`.
