# Pullbacks of separated-scheme analytifications

`localizedAnalytificationPullbackIsLimit` takes a pullback square of separated schemes locally of finite type over a complete nontrivially normed nonarchimedean field. It gives the limit structure on the cone with the actual analytified scheme projections.

Choose an affine target open and affine opens on both sides mapping into it. The intersection of the two inverse images in the algebraic pullback is affine. Taking spectra of the chart coordinate maps identifies this square with a pullback of affine tests, and the analytic affine tensor-product theorem preserves it. Naturality of the algebraic centers identifies its analytic pullback domain with the corresponding intersection of analytic inverse images. The open-domain gluing principle assembles these squares. The scheme charts use the inverse image and gluing of affine opens in Berkovich, *Spectral theory and analytic geometry over non-Archimedean fields* (1990), Theorem 3.4.1, steps (2)–(3), pp. 63–64. The analytic gluing follows the fiber-product construction in Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), Proposition 1.4.1, pp. 29–30.
