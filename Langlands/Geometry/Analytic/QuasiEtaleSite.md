# Quasi-étale morphisms and the quasi-étale site `Qét(X)`

Author: Worker 21.

Berkovich's quasi-étale morphisms are the morphisms that are étale after
locally identifying affinoid domains of the source with affinoid domains of
spaces étale over the target. They define the quasi-étale site, which is the
site of the Key Lemma and of Berkovich's equivariant sheaves. This module
defines quasi-étale morphisms in the Library's category of analytic spaces
`LocalizedCategory`, shows that étale morphisms are quasi-étale, and
constructs the category `Qét(X)`, its coverings and the generated topology.
It uses the Library's `EtaleSite` (étale morphisms) and
`AnalyticDomainSpaces` (affinoid domains and their embeddings).

## Original source

Berkovich, *Vanishing cycles for formal schemes*, Invent. Math. 115 (1994),
registered as `berkovich-1994-vanishing-cycles-formal-schemes`, SHA256
`f4f7bc00cc46a0a5f72a69d7bbb220bce1d3ed9fd465aeda629f37dfc054bed1`, §3.

- Printed p.543 / PDF p.5: "Let φ : Y → X be a morphism of k-analytic spaces.
  We say that φ is quasi-étale if for every point y ∈ Y there exist affinoid
  domains V_1, …, V_n ⊂ Y such that V_1 ∪ … ∪ V_n is a neighborhood of y and
  each V_i can be identified with an affinoid domain in a k-analytic space
  étale over X. For example, étale morphisms and the canonical embeddings of
  analytic domains in a k-analytic space are quasi-étale."
- Printed p.544 / PDF p.6: "We remark that if a morphism φ : Y → X is
  quasi-étale, then for every point y ∈ Y there exist affinoid domains
  V_1, …, V_n ⊂ Y such that V_1 ∪ … ∪ V_n is a neighborhood of y and each V_i
  can be identified over X with an affinoid domain in a paracompact k-analytic
  space separated and étale over X." Lemma 3.1(ii): "If Y and Z are
  quasi-étale over X, then any X-morphism Z → Y is quasi-étale."
- Printed p.544 / PDF p.6: "For a k-analytic space X, let Qét(X) denote the
  category of quasi-étale morphisms U → X. The quasi-étale topology on X is
  the Grothendieck topology on the category Qét(X) generated by the
  pretopology for which the set of coverings of (U → X) ∈ Qét(X) is formed
  by the families {U_i →^{f_i} U}_{i∈I} such that each point of U has a
  neighborhood of the form f_{i_1}(V_1) ∪ … ∪ f_{i_n}(V_n) for some affinoid
  domains V_1 ⊂ U_{i_1}, …, V_n ⊂ U_{i_n}. … We denote by X_qét the site
  obtained in this way (the quasi-étale site of X)".

## Derived choices

1. Affinoid domains are represented by charts of the saturated presentations,
   as in `AnalyticDomainSpaces`. In a Hausdorff space every affinoid domain
   is a finite union of affinoid domains contained in charts (Berkovich 1993,
   Lemma 1.1.2(ii) for the net of affinoid domains, Proposition 1.2.13), and
   these are charts of the saturated presentation. An affinoid domain `U`
   contained in an affinoid domain `V` identified with an affinoid domain `D`
   of `W` is identified with an affinoid domain of `D`, hence of `W`. So
   requiring the `V_i` to be charts gives the same morphisms.
2. "Identified with an affinoid domain in a space `W` étale over `X`" is read
   as an identification over `X`, as in the remark on p.544: there are an
   étale `w : W → X` and an affinoid domain embedding `j : V_i → W` (the
   Library's `IsAffinoidDomainEmbedding`) with `j ≫ w` equal to the canonical
   morphism of `V_i` followed by `φ`. `W` is existentially quantified, so
   using charts of the chosen presentation of `W` in `IsAffinoidDomainEmbedding`
   is no restriction (see `AnalyticDomainSpaces`).
3. As for étale morphisms, a morphism of `LocalizedCategory` is quasi-étale
   when it is isomorphic in the arrow category to the image of a quasi-étale
   strong map between constructed saturations. Every morphism is isomorphic
   to such an image (`EtaleSite`, provenance of `etale`).
4. As in `EtaleSite`, the topology is Mathlib's `Precoverage.toGrothendieck`
   of the covering families, which is the topology generated by Berkovich's
   pretopology; the pretopology axioms and fibre products are not needed.

## Definitions and results

All declarations are in `Langlands.Analytic.SaturatedAtlasPresentation`. The
field `K` is a complete ultrametric normed field (trivial valuation allowed),
and the category is `LocalizedCategory.{u, v, v}` as in `EtaleSite`.

- `IsQuasiEtaleAt g y`, for a strong map `g : ofSaturation A ⟶ ofSaturation B`
  and a point `y`: there is a finite family `σ` of subsets of the source with
  `⋃₀ σ` a neighbourhood of `y`, such that each member is the range of a
  chart `i`, and for that chart there are `W`, an étale `w : W ⟶ L(B)` and an
  affinoid domain embedding `j` from the affinoid domain of `i` to `W` with
  `j ≫ w = ν_i ≫ L(g)`. Here `L` is `localizationFunctor` and `ν_i` the
  canonical morphism `localizedDomainInclusion` of the chart's domain.
- `IsQuasiEtale g`: `IsQuasiEtaleAt g y` for every `y`.
- `isQuasiEtale_of_isEtaleAllPieces`: an étale strong map is quasi-étale.
  The charts through `y` given by the quasinet axiom of the source atlas form
  `σ`. For each chart take `W = L(ofSaturation A)`, `w = L(g)` (étale by
  `etale_localizationFunctor_map`) and `j = ν_i` (an affinoid domain
  embedding by `isAffinoidDomainEmbedding_localizedDomainInclusion`); the
  equation holds by definition.
- `quasiEtale : MorphismProperty LocalizedCategory`: arrows isomorphic in the
  arrow category to `L(g)` for a quasi-étale strong map `g` between constructed
  saturations. It respects isomorphisms (instance).
- `etale_le_quasiEtale`: étale morphisms are quasi-étale, transcribing
  "étale morphisms … are quasi-étale" (p.543). Hence `quasiEtale` contains
  identities (instance).
- `QuasiEtaleSite X` is Mathlib's `(quasiEtale).Over ⊤ X`: quasi-étale
  morphisms `U ⟶ X` with all morphisms over `X`. That such morphisms are
  quasi-étale (Lemma 3.1(ii)) is not formalized.
- `EtaleSite.toQuasiEtaleSite X : EtaleSite X ⥤ QuasiEtaleSite X` is the
  inclusion (Mathlib's `MorphismProperty.Over.changeProp`).
- `quasiEtalePrecoverage X`: a presieve `R` on `U` covers when for every point
  `x` of `U` there is a finite family `σ` of subsets with `⋃₀ σ` a
  neighbourhood of `x`, each member of the form `f(V)` for an arrow
  `f : V' ⟶ U` in `R` and a chart range `V` of `V'`. `f(V)` is the image under
  the underlying continuous map (`localizedSpaceFunctor`).
  `mem_quasiEtalePrecoverage_iff` restates this.
- `quasiEtaleTopology X` is the generated Grothendieck topology, the site
  `X_qét`. `generate_mem_quasiEtaleTopology` and
  `isSheaf_quasiEtaleTopology_iff` are as for the étale topology.

## Scope

Not done here: composition and base change of quasi-étale morphisms
(Lemma 3.1(i)); that morphisms in `Qét(X)` are quasi-étale (Lemma 3.1(ii));
that canonical embeddings of analytic domains are quasi-étale; the morphism
of sites `X_qét → X_ét`, whose underlying functor is
`EtaleSite.toQuasiEtaleSite`, and its continuity; the equivariant
quasi-étale site `X(G)_qét`; the Key Lemma.
