# The ordinary analytic étale pretopology

Morphisms between étale objects over the same analytic space are étale by right cancellation. The full analytic pullback of such a cospan has an étale projection, so its composite with the other structure map is étale. This places the pullback in the over-category. The analytic lifts obey the structure equation over the base, which gives the whole over-category limit. All pullback cones of that cospan compute analytic pullbacks on their underlying spaces.

For a jointly surjective family, take an underlying point of the pullback target and a point above its image in a covering object. The compatible-point lifting theorem for étale pullbacks gives a point in the corresponding pulled-back covering object. Thus the generating families are stable under base change. With identities and composition they form a pretopology, and its generated topology is `etaleTopology`.

The construction realizes the jointly surjective coverings of Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §4.1, printed p. 82 (PDF p. 79). Literature ID `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA-256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`.
