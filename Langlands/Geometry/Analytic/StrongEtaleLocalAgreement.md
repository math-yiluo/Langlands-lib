# Local equality of analytic maps over an unramified morphism

Two maps with a common composite through an unramified map and the same full field-point image agree on an open neighbourhood of that field point. The proof uses the unramifiedness hypothesis over all finite affinoid pieces.

Choose finitely many pointed affinoid subsets in the base which cover a neighbourhood of the image. For each base subset, both maps land locally in the same finite inverse-image piece. On source charts in that common piece, the bounded comparison gives smaller charts of equality. The resulting finite chart family covers an open neighbourhood in a Hausdorff subspace. Its traces form a closed quasinet, so equality on these domains detects equality of the full localized morphisms on the open neighbourhood.
