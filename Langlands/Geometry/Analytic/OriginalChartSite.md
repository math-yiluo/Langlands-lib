# The dense site of actual original-chart domains

Fix an actual indexed affinoid atlas `a` over a complete ultrametric normed
field `K`, on a topological space `X`. The field, point and chart-index
universes `u`, `v`, `w` are independent. The published category
`OriginalChartDomain a` has actual domain subsets as objects and subset
inclusions as arrows. Each domain satisfies the finite relative
neighborhood condition for original charts contained in that subset and
has its constructed subspace affinoid atlas.

`originalChartProperty a S` means that `S = ofChart i` for some supplied
chart index `i`, with the equality written in the opposite orientation in
Lean. `OriginalChart a` is its full subcategory, and
`originalChartInclusion a` is the fully faithful inclusion into the domain
category. Thus its arrows are exactly inclusions of chart ranges. Objects
are domains together with a proposition witnessing that they are chart
ranges; a witnessing chart index is not extra object data.
`originalChartObject i` supplies the object for each original index, and
`originalChartObject_obj` identifies its underlying domain.

The instance `originalChartInclusion_isCoverDense` proves cover density
for the already constructed domain topology `quasinetTopology a`.
For each domain `S`, the published `ofArrows_originalCharts_mem S`
gives a covering sieve generated by all original charts contained in `S`.
Any arrow of that generated sieve factors through one of those chart
domains. Its factorization therefore passes through the image of
`originalChartObject i`. The sieve of arrows factoring through chart
objects contains this covering sieve and is consequently covering.
This constructs the density witness from actual contained charts.

`originalChartTopology a` is the inclusion's induced Grothendieck
topology, and `originalChartInclusion_isDenseSubsite` supplies the dense
subsite instance from the proved cover density and the inclusion's full
faithfulness. `mem_originalChartTopology` gives its exact sieve criterion:
a chart sieve is covering if and only if its pushforward along the
inclusion is covering in the domain topology.

For an arbitrarily indexed family `f i : D i ⟶ S` of chart inclusions,
`originalChart_ofArrows_mem_iff_locallyRefines` proves that its generated
sieve is covering exactly when the family of literal subsets
`(D i).obj ⊆ X` locally refines the singleton consisting of `S.obj`.
Explicitly, at every point of the target chart there must be finitely
many family members all containing the point whose union is a
neighborhood relative to the target. The proof uses the preceding
pushforward criterion, Mathlib's identity between pushforward of a
generated family and the family of image arrows, and the published
domain-family covering characterization.
`originalChart_ofArrows_mem_iff_isQuasinet` expresses the same condition
as the quasinet property of the restricted family on the actual target
subspace. The family index universe is arbitrary and the index type may
be empty.

For a value category `C` with independent object and morphism universes
`t`, `s`, `originalChartSheafRestriction C` restricts genuine sheaves
from the domain site to the chart site. No limits in `C` are needed for
this restriction functor. The two displayed restriction formulas state
that its value at `S` is exactly the original sheaf's value at `S.obj`,
and its map on a chart inclusion is exactly the original sheaf's map
on the underlying domain inclusion.

`originalChartSheafEquivalence C` constructs an equivalence

```text
Sheaf (originalChartTopology a) C ≌ Sheaf (quasinetTopology a) C.
```

It retains the following precise hypothesis from Mathlib: for every
`S : (OriginalChartDomain a)ᵒᵖ`, the category `C` has limits of shape
`StructuredArrow S (originalChartInclusion a).op`. Under this
hypothesis, the proved dense subsite meets the assumptions of Mathlib's
`Functor.sheafInducedTopologyEquivOfIsCoverDense`, which constructs the
equivalence. `originalChartSheafEquivalence_inverse` identifies its entire
inverse functor with `originalChartSheafRestriction C`. This includes
both values and all restriction maps.

For an actual `AffinoidAtlasPresentation A`, `originalChartFunctor`
composes the chart inclusion with the published `originalDomainFunctor`.
It realizes each chart object by its constructed subspace presentation
and realizes each inclusion by its complete chosen bounded atlas arrow.
`originalChartFunctor_obj` identifies the presentation at an arbitrary
chart object, and `originalChartFunctor_obj_chart` identifies the one
at `originalChartObject i` with `A.chartPresentation i`.
`originalChartFunctor_map_pullback` states equality of the entire
bounded pullback with the original chart algebra's bounded identity.
These equalities are definitional; no equality of algebra maps is
inferred solely from a map on spectrum points.

The original source is Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean
analytic spaces* (1993), section 1.1, printed p.13 / PDF p.10, for
quasinets and induced subspaces; Definition 1.2.3 and Remark 1.2.4(i),
printed p.16 / PDF p.13, for actual affinoid atlas restrictions;
Definition 1.3.1, printed p.23 / PDF p.20, for analytic domains and their
contained-chart atlases; and the G-topology paragraph in section 1.3,
printed p.25 / PDF p.22, for coverings by quasinet families of domains.

`06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`.

The dense chart site and sheaf comparison are derived infrastructure
for the published fixed-original-chart domain construction, rather
than a separately numbered assertion in that source. Their route is
the actual contained-chart cover followed by Mathlib's full-subcategory
and dense-subsite machinery. The latter comes from
`Mathlib.CategoryTheory.ObjectProperty.FullSubcategory` and
`Mathlib.CategoryTheory.Sites.DenseSubsite.InducedTopology` at Mathlib
commit `5ed2965256430c3649e86755f9576b54eca72435`.
This construction concerns the inclusion categories of the supplied
original charts and their domains. It does not enlarge the atlas to all
affinoid domains, identify the original charts with a saturated affinoid
basis, construct a structure sheaf, prove any particular presheaf is a
sheaf, or construct the full analytic or étale site.
