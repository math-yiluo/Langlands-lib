# Closed ideals for equalities of affinoid-algebra points

For two points of a separated algebraic space with coefficients in a
presented affinoid algebra, the construction chooses the closed ideal
that imposes their equality. Every algebra map out of the affinoid
algebra annihilates this ideal exactly when the two changed-coefficient
points agree. The test algebra is any commutative algebra over the ground
field.

Pulling back the closed diagonal gives a represented section equalizer.
The closed immersion into the affine test is the spectrum of a
quotient, with the isomorphism transported to schemes over the ground
field. Factoring a spectrum test through this quotient is equivalent to
annihilating its ideal, by the algebra quotient universal property and
affine duality. Ideals of the presented affinoid algebra are closed over
the complete ultrametric nontrivially normed field.

This is an internal application of the closed diagonal and the
Library's section equalizer and affinoid algebra results.
