# Finite étale lifts on charts of an analytification

Let `K` be a complete, ultrametric, nontrivially normed field and let
`(Y, p)` be an analytification of a separated, locally finite type
algebraic space `X` over `Spec K`. Write `M(D)` for the small spectrum of
a presented affinoid algebra `D` in the localized category. Fix a map
`i : M(D) ⟶ Y`. Suppose a bounded map `f : D → U` is finite
Banach, algebraically finite étale, and surjective on bounded spectra.
In the localized category, assume that `M(f) ≫ i` factors through the map
of a finite polynomial test of `X`.

For every bounded `K`-algebra map `g : D → R` into a presented affinoid
algebra, there is an `R`-valued point of `X` whose affinoid point
comparison equals `M(g) ≫ i`. The finite Banach base change retains
étaleness by the ordinary tensor comparison. Every point of the new
base lifts by the point lifting property of the affinoid pushout. The
affine Hom equivalence expresses the composite upstairs by a coordinate
algebra map. Finite étale descent gives the required point downstairs
and identifies its analytic map with the original composite.

This is an internal consequence of finite base change, the affine
Hom equivalence and the descent of affinoid-algebra points.
