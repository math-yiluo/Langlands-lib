# The atlas on an actual chart domain

This module starts with a supplied `IndexedAffinoidAtlas` and constructs its atlas on the actual subspace that is the range of one of its charts. Its provenance is derived infrastructure for V. Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §1.2, Definition 1.2.3 and Remark 1.2.4(i), printed pp. 16–17 (registered source `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, PDF pp. 13–14), together with the net definitions in §1.1. It uses the already published indexed-atlas and chosen-map interfaces.

For an original chart `j`, `ContainedChart a j` consists of every original index whose chart range lies in the range of `j`. `containedChart` maps its original spectrum into that range by the same original chart map. The algebra is exactly the original algebra. `range_containedChart` identifies each new chart range with the inverse image of its original range. Containment is both preserved and reflected, so the original injectivity of chart domains descends to the subtype of indices.

`containedChartFamily_eq` identifies the new family with the entire contained-member family of the original net. The topology restriction module proves that this family is a net. The chart-domain subspace is Hausdorff because the original chart is a topological embedding of its actual Hausdorff Banach spectrum; the proof therefore supplies the required local Hausdorff neighborhoods.

`containedRestriction` is the original bounded algebra restriction, using reflected containment. Its chart square follows by subtype extensionality. `containedRestriction_universal` proves the full factorization property for every presented affinoid test algebra, including empty or nonstrict test spectra: a subspace chart-range witness gives an original chart-range witness by forgetting subtype membership, after which the original universal property applies unchanged. The restriction identity and composition laws are inherited as equalities of bounded algebra maps. These data construct `chartAtlas`.

`chartPresentation` bundles the actual subspace with this constructed atlas. `chartInclusion` gives its inclusion into the original presentation. For nested original chart domains, `chartMap` gives the actual subspace inclusion between their presentations. Both arrows keep the original algebras and have bounded identity pullbacks; containment compatibility is proved using reflection and preservation above. `chartMap_id`, `chartMap_comp`, and `chartMap_chartInclusion` prove the category laws and inclusion compatibility by extensionality of chosen atlas maps.

These are constructions from an actual supplied atlas. The arrows live in the published category of monotone chosen atlas maps. This module does not construct an extended atlas, a general analytic domain, or the localization that gives arbitrary analytic-space morphisms in the source.
