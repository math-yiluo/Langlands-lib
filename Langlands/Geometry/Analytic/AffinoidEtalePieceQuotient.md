# Quotients of affinoid pieces of étale charts

Let `K` be a complete ultrametric nontrivially normed field. Take an
étale localized analytic map `E → Y` to a supplied analytification of a
separated locally finite type algebraic space over `K`. Pull its
composite with `Y → Z` back along a monic affinoid test `M(A) → Z`.
Suppose this gives `M(B)` with projection to `M(A)` induced by a
bounded map `A → B` that is finite Banach and algebraically finite
étale. The composite `M(B) → E → Y` is the comparison map of a
`B`-valued point.

The affinoid point relation gives the actual kernel pair of
`M(B) → Y`. Its projections to `M(B)` are base changes of `E → Y`.
The proof constructs the lift to the original pullback using the leg to
`E` and the map to `M(A)`. Monicity of the inclusion into `E` gives
uniqueness in the kernel pair. Thus both relation projections are étale.

The cover of the finite étale affinoid quotient is finite Banach and
algebraically finite étale on coefficient algebras and surjective on
Banach spectra. The original point descends to the quotient's coefficient
algebra. The comparison of the descended point gives a map into `Y`
whose composite with the cover is the original point map. The ring
pushout makes the cover equalize the original kernel pair. The data
records this equality after every pair of test maps with a common image
in `Y`.

These are internal consequences of the actual analytic pullbacks,
étale base change, and the affinoid point relation and quotient.
