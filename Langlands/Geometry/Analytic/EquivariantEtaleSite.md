# The étale site `X(G)_ét` of a `G`-space

Author: Worker 21.

For a `G`-space `X`, Berkovich's equivariant étale site `X(G)_ét` has as
objects the étale `G`-morphisms `U(G) → X(G)` from `G`-spaces `U`. Its
coverings are the families whose underlying families are étale coverings of
`X`. This module constructs this site from the Library's `G`-spaces
(`ContinuousAnalyticAction`) and étale site (`EtaleSite`). It also
constructs the functor forgetting the action and the restriction along a
continuous homomorphism of groups, and shows that both preserve and reflect
coverings.

Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces*
(unfinished manuscript, 1995–1996), registered as
`provided-7bb3903652336bd799aad8853b8efd7ff3bcc0c7652745417f4745a14ac08e90`
(folder SHA256
`e046cadfb9dcec4b0cf6624f80d6316bf532416153b82023f5100bd1ab0726cd`; file
`Berkovich--Etale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces (unfinished manuscript)--1996.pdf`).

- §1.1, printed p.2: the category `𝒜n^op` has as objects the pairs `X(G)`
  of a topological group `G` and a `G`-space `X`. A morphism
  `φ : X′(G′) → X(G)` is "a pair consisting of a continuous homomorphism of
  topological groups ν_φ : G′ → G and a morphism of analytic spaces
  φ : Y → X compatible with the homomorphism ν_φ" (printed pp.2–3). The fibre
  over `G` is `G-𝒜n`.
- §1.2, printed p.3: "For a G-space X, let Qét(X(G)) (resp. Ét(X(G))) denote
  the category of quasi-étale (resp. étale) morphisms U(G) → X(G). The
  quasi-étale (resp. étale) topology on X(G) is the Grothendieck topology on
  the category Qét(X(G)) (resp. Ét(X(G))) generated by the pretopology for
  which the set of coverings of (U(G) → X(G)) ∈ Qét(X(G)) (resp. Ét(X(G)))
  consists of the families {U_i(G) → U(G)}_{i∈I} such that {U_i → U}_{i∈I} is
  a covering in the quasi-étale (resp. étale) topology of X. We denote by …
  X(G)_ét the site obtained in this way". The same paragraph states that any
  morphism `φ : X′(G′) → X(G)` gives morphisms of sites
  `X′(G′)_ét → X(G)_ét`.
- Example 1.1.3, printed p.3: the morphism `b : X → X(G)`, where
  `X = X({1})`.

The étale coverings of `X` are those of Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for
non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §4.1, printed p.82, as constructed
in the Library's `EtaleSite`: families `{f_i : U_i → U}` with
`U = ∪ f_i(U_i)`.

## Derived choices

- The objects of `Ét(X(G))` are the morphisms `U → X` of the Library's
  category of `G`-spaces (`GSpace`) whose underlying morphism is étale, that
  is, the morphisms `U(G) → X(G)` of `𝒜n^op` with `ν` the identity of `G`.
  Its morphisms are all `G`-morphisms over `X`.
- As in `EtaleSite`, the topology is Mathlib's `Precoverage.toGrothendieck`
  of the covering families. This is the topology generated by Berkovich's
  pretopology, and fibre products are not needed to define it.
- The functor forgetting the action is the functor `U(G) ↦ U` of the
  morphism `b : X → X(G)`; Berkovich's general description uses the fibre
  product `X′ ×_X U`, which here is `U`. Restriction along `f : H → G` is the
  functor `U(G) ↦ U(H)` of the morphism `X(H) → X(G)` with `ν = f` and the
  identity of `X`, for which `X ×_X U = U`. These identifications are
  explained here and not formalized, since fibre products are not yet in the
  Library.

## Definitions

All declarations are in the namespace
`Langlands.Analytic.SaturatedAtlasPresentation`. The field `K` is a complete
ultrametric normed field (trivial valuation allowed), `G` is a group with a
topology, and the spaces use `LocalizedCategory.{u, v, v}` as in
`EtaleSite`.

- `GSpace.forget : GSpace K G ⥤ LocalizedCategory` is the underlying analytic
  space and underlying morphism (full subcategory inclusion followed by
  Mathlib's `Action.forget`).
- `GSpace.etale`: a `G`-morphism is étale when its underlying morphism is
  étale in the sense of the Library's `etale`. `etale_iff` records this. It
  respects isomorphisms and contains identities (Mathlib's instances for
  `MorphismProperty.inverseImage`).
- `EquivariantEtaleSite X`, for a `G`-space `X`, is Mathlib's
  `(GSpace.etale).Over ⊤ X`: Berkovich's `Ét(X(G))`.
- `EquivariantEtaleSite.toEtaleSite X : EquivariantEtaleSite X ⥤ EtaleSite X`
  sends `U(G) → X(G)` to the underlying étale morphism `U → X`, and a
  `G`-morphism over `X` to its underlying morphism. It is built with
  Mathlib's `MorphismProperty.Comma.lift` from `Over.post GSpace.forget`. The
  lemmas `toEtaleSite_obj_left`, `toEtaleSite_obj_hom` and
  `toEtaleSite_map_left` record this.
- `EquivariantEtaleSite.points X` is `toEtaleSite X` followed by the points
  functor of `EtaleSite`; `points_obj` identifies the points of `U(G)` with
  those of the underlying space of `U`.

## Coverings and the topology

- `equivariantEtalePrecoverage X` is the pullback along `points X` of
  Mathlib's jointly surjective precoverage of types.
- `map_toEtaleSite_mem_etalePrecoverage_iff`: a family of `Ét(X(G))` is a
  covering exactly when its image under `toEtaleSite` is an étale covering of
  `Ét(X)`. This is Berkovich's definition of the coverings. The proof
  identifies the precoverage with the pullback of `etalePrecoverage` along
  `toEtaleSite` (`Precoverage.comap_comp`, as the point functors agree).
- `mem_equivariantEtalePrecoverage_iff` and
  `ofArrows_mem_equivariantEtalePrecoverage_iff`: a family covers `U` when
  every point of `U` is in the image of the point map of one of its members.
- The `HasIsos` and `IsStableUnderComposition` instances are inherited from
  the pulled-back precoverage.
- `equivariantEtaleTopology X` is the generated Grothendieck topology, the
  site `X(G)_ét`. `generate_mem_equivariantEtaleTopology`: the sieve
  generated by a covering is covering. `isSheaf_equivariantEtaleTopology_iff`
  is the sheaf condition for presheaves of types in terms of the pullbacks of
  the sieves generated by coverings (Mathlib's
  `Precoverage.isSheaf_toGrothendieck_iff`).

## Restriction along a continuous homomorphism

For a continuous homomorphism `f : H →ₜ* G`:

- `EquivariantEtaleSite.res f : EquivariantEtaleSite X ⥤
  EquivariantEtaleSite ((GSpace.res f).obj X)` restricts the actions of the
  source and of every object and morphism along `f`, using the Library's
  `GSpace.res`. The étale condition is unchanged because the underlying
  morphisms are unchanged. `res_obj_left` and `res_map_left` record this.
- `resPoints f` is the identity isomorphism between the point functors.
- `map_res_mem_equivariantEtalePrecoverage_iff`: restriction preserves and
  reflects coverings.
