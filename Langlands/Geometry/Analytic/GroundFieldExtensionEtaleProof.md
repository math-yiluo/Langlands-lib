# Étale and quasi-étale morphisms under ground field extension

`Langlands.Analytic.SaturatedAtlasPresentation.groundFieldExtensionEtale` proves `GroundFieldExtensionEtaleStatement` for the actual localized scalar-extension functor and the two analytic morphism predicates. The complete ultrametric normed fields and the analytic index universe are independent.

An all-pieces étale strong representative admits finite étale models over open neighbourhoods. The full-recognition presentation of the target and the admissible recognition subatlas of the source make a model's recognized affinoid pair into a literal pair of original charts. The strong recognition comparisons are quasi-isomorphisms and give an isomorphism of the corresponding localized arrows.

On an extended pair of those original charts the bounded pullback is the scalar extension of the original finite Banach étale map. The ordinary relative tensor comparison supplies the extended finite Banach and algebraic finite étale certificates. Saturation recovers the same bounded maps, and the original-chart comparison with full recognition conjugates those maps by bounded algebra equivalences. The extended finite preimage locus is the inverse image of the old preimage locus under the natural projections. The projection naturality equation identifies it with the required intersection of the extended source neighbourhood with the inverse image of the extended target chart.

A finite recognized target neighbourhood family pulls back to a neighbourhood family with the extended finite Banach witnesses. Target-local finiteness gives finiteness over the extended open pair, including on arbitrary recognized subdomains of the extended target. On the extended members of the finite family the algebraic finite étale certificate gives pointwise étaleness. The finite-to-all recognition theorem upgrades the pointwise tests to the all-pieces condition. The mapped recognition comparisons and the isomorphism of the given arrow with its strong representative give étaleness in the localization.

`GroundFieldExtensionQuasiEtaleReduction` gives the quasi-étale half by extending the local factorizations. Projection preimages transport the finite neighbourhood unions. The extended chart-domain comparison transports the affinoid domain embeddings and their factorization equations.
