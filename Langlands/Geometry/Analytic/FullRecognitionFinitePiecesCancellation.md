# Cancellation into a finite affinoid neighborhood family

This internal module assembles geometric cancellation without assuming that one finite affinoid family already gives the all-pieces condition.

`isFiniteAt_of_finiteOverRightCancellation` takes the separately stated common-base finite cancellation law. It shrinks a finite neighborhood of the composite into the inverse image of a finite source neighborhood of the second map, intersects their target neighborhoods, restricts both finite maps to that common base, and applies the law. Thus local finiteness of the first map follows from this explicit prerequisite.

`hasFinitePiecesAt_of_comp_of_isFiniteAt` is a generic property lemma. Its two pointwise properties must survive postcomposition by flat surjective restrictions of finite-type maps, and the first-map property must follow by cancellation in a finite-type algebra triangle. It assumes local finiteness of the first map, the second-map property over all pieces, and the composite property over all pieces. Its conclusion is a finite-pieces certificate for the first map.

The proof shrinks the first source neighborhood inside the old composite source, then shrinks the second source neighborhood inside the first target. It intersects final target neighborhoods and restricts the finite maps. A finite recognized family in the final target pulls back to a finite recognized family in the middle space. Its union contains an open source intersected with the inverse image of a neighborhood, so it is a neighborhood of the middle point. For each of these pieces, actual pair-system composition identifies the algebra triangle. The all-pieces restriction lemma transfers the old certificates to the new pieces, and the supplied pointwise cancellation law applies. The proof quantifies over every presentation of each recognized preimage, using its unique locus to compare it to the preimage supplied by finiteness.

The provenance is internal: the construction uses the published finite-neighborhood, recognized-neighborhood, pair-composition and clopen-restriction interfaces. No external assertion is newly formalized. The finite-to-all-pieces upgrade and the unconditional strong etale cancellation theorem remain separate proof obligations. All field, space and chart-index universes are independent, and no Hausdorffness, strictness or valuation hypothesis is added.
