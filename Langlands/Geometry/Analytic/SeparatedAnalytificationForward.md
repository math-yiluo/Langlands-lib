# Separatedness forces a Hausdorff analytification

Module `Langlands.Geometry.Analytic.SeparatedAnalytificationForward`, namespace
`Langlands.Analytic.SchemeAnalytification.SeparatedForward`.

This module proves the other direction of `SchemeAnalytificationSeparatedStatement`. Let `K`
be a complete ultrametric nontrivially normed field and `f : X ⟶ Spec K` a separated morphism
locally of finite type. Then the glued analytification `Space K f` is Hausdorff. This
direction is part of Berkovich, *Spectral Theory and Analytic Geometry over
Non-Archimedean Fields* (1990), Theorem 3.4.8(i), stated there for Berkovich's
analytification; here it concerns the Library's glued model `Space K f`. Berkovich's printed
proof uses Proposition 3.1.5 and Corollary 3.4.5. The argument implemented here is
different. It was written for this proof and not taken from a source. It checks the
published closedness criterion for the chart relations. For this it uses two facts. Under
separatedness, the intersection of two affine opens is affine, and its coordinate ring is
generated by restrictions of sections from the two opens. A limit argument along an
ultrafilter then uses the seminorm description of affine analytification.

Notation: `LocalSpace K f U` is the analytification of an affine open `U`, `ι K f U` its map
into the glued space, `localMap K f h` the restriction map of analytifications for an
inclusion `h : V ≤ U` of affine opens, `restriction f h` the corresponding restriction
`K`-algebra map of coordinate rings, `CoordinateRing (structureMap f U)` the `K`-algebra of
sections of `U`, and `pointSeminorm` the published embedding of a chart point as a
multiplicative seminorm on the coordinate ring.

## Main statements

* `t2Space_of_isSeparated K f [IsSeparated f] : T2Space (Space K f)`. **Separated implies
  Hausdorff.**
* `isClosed_chartRelation K f [IsSeparated f] U V`. For affine opens `U`, `V`, the set of pairs
  `(x, y)` in `LocalSpace K f U × LocalSpace K f V` with `ι K f U x = ι K f V y` is closed.
* `restrictionGluingData K f : GluingReduction.RestrictionGluingData K f`. The local inputs of
  the published gluing argument hold, with no separatedness hypothesis.

## Auxiliary statements

### Gluing (no separatedness hypothesis)

* `isOpenMap_localMap`. For affine opens `V ≤ U`, `localMap K f hVU` is an open map.
* `isOpenEmbedding_localMap`. For affine opens `V ≤ U`, `localMap K f hVU` is an open
  embedding.

### Algebra (assuming `IsSeparated f`)

* `isAffineOpen_inf f U V`. The intersection of two affine opens is affine.
* `inter f U V`. This intersection as an element of `X.affineOpens`; `inter_le_left` and
  `inter_le_right` are its inclusions into `U` and `V`.
* `surjective_of_isClosedImmersion`. A ring map `φ : R ⟶ S` such that `Spec.map φ` is a closed
  immersion is surjective. This holds without the separatedness hypothesis.
* `sup_range_restriction_eq_top f U V`. The `K`-subalgebra of the coordinate ring of
  `U ∩ V` generated by the ranges of the two restriction maps from `U` and from `V` is
  everything.

### Closedness (assuming `IsSeparated f` from `restrictionPair` on)

* `pointSeminorm_localMap`. The seminorm of `localMap K f h y` at a section `a` of `U` is the
  seminorm of `y` at `restriction f h a`. This holds without the separatedness hypothesis.
* `restrictionPair K f U V w`. The pair of restrictions of a point `w` of
  `LocalSpace K f (inter f U V)` to `U` and to `V`.
* `chartRelation_eq_range`. The chart relation of `U` and `V` equals the range of
  `restrictionPair K f U V`.

## Proofs of the gluing statements

`isOpenMap_localMap`: let `s` be open in `LocalSpace K f V`. Its image under
`localMap K f hVU` is the union, over sections `a` of `U` with basic open `D(a) ⊆ V`, of the
images of the preimages `(localMap K f ha)⁻¹ s` under the restriction map from `D(a)` to `U`.
For `⊆`: let `y ∈ s`. The center of `y` lies in `V` (published `center_ι_mem`), so it has a
basic open neighbourhood `D(a) ⊆ V` with `a` a section of `U`
(`IsAffineOpen.exists_basicOpen_le`). By the published
`MorphismExistence.range_localMap`, `y` is the restriction of a point `d` of the
analytification of `D(a)`. Then `localMap_localMap` (composition of restrictions) places the
image of `y` in the union. The inclusion `⊇` is the same composition rule. Each term of the
union is open by the published `MorphismExistence.isOpenEmbedding_localMap_basicOpen` and
continuity of `localMap K f ha`.

`isOpenEmbedding_localMap` combines continuity, the published injectivity
`MorphismExistence.localMap_injective`, and `isOpenMap_localMap`. `restrictionGluingData`
collects this with the published identity, composition and range lemmas
`MorphismExistence.localMap_self`, `localMap_localMap` and `range_localMap`.

## Proofs of the algebraic statements

`isAffineOpen_inf`: a separated morphism has affine diagonal. Mathlib's
`isAffineHom_diagonal_iff`, applied over the affine open `⊤` of `Spec K`, says that the
intersection of two affine opens is affine.

`surjective_of_isClosedImmersion`: a closed immersion of affine schemes is surjective on
global sections (`IsClosedImmersion.isAffine_surjective_of_isAffine`). Transport this along
the natural isomorphism `Scheme.ΓSpecIso`.

`sup_range_restriction_eq_top`: let `W = U ∩ V` and let `T` be the subalgebra generated by the
two ranges.

1. Let `rU` and `rV` be the restriction maps with codomain `T`, and `iT : T → Γ(W)` the
   inclusion. Then `rU ≫ iT` and `rV ≫ iT` are the restriction maps of global sections along
   the inclusions of `W` into `U` and `V`.
2. Both composites `Spec T → Spec Γ(U) ≅ U → X → Spec K` and the analogous one through `V` are
   `Spec` of the structure map `K → T`. This uses that the restriction maps are `K`-algebra
   maps and the lemma `SeparatedReverse.affineOpenMap_comp`. So they define
   `g : Spec T ⟶ U ×_K V` by `pullback.lift`.
3. `W` is the fibre product of the open immersions `U → X` and `V → X`
   (`IsOpenImmersion.isPullback`). Composing this identification with
   `pullback.mapDesc U.ι V.ι f`, the base change of the diagonal of `f`, gives
   `δ : W ⟶ U ×_K V`. It is a closed immersion because `f` is separated.
4. `W.isoSpec.inv ≫ δ = Spec.map iT ≫ g`, checked on both projections with step 1 and
   `Scheme.isoSpec_inv_naturality`. Hence `Spec.map iT ≫ g` is a closed immersion, so
   `Spec.map iT` is one (`IsClosedImmersion.of_comp`).
5. By `surjective_of_isClosedImmersion`, `iT` is surjective, so `T` is everything.

## Proof of the chart relation statement

`pointSeminorm_localMap` is the published `pointSeminorm_baseMap`.

`chartRelation_eq_range`: the published `GluingReduction.ι_eq_iff_common_refinement`, applied
to `restrictionGluingData`, says that `ι K f U x = ι K f V y` iff some affine open `W'`
contained in `U` and in `V` carries a point `z` that restricts to `x` and to `y`. Since
`W' ≤ U ∩ V`, the restriction of `z` to `U ∩ V` is a preimage of `(x, y)` under
`restrictionPair` (`localMap_localMap`). Conversely, both restrictions of a point of
`U ∩ V` have the same image in the glued space by `ι_localMap`.

## Proof of closedness (`isClosed_chartRelation`)

By `chartRelation_eq_range` it suffices to show that the range of `Q = restrictionPair` is
closed. `Q` is continuous, and injective because its first component is injective. Let
`ν w` denote the seminorm of a point `w` of `LocalSpace K f (U ∩ V)` on the coordinate ring of
`U ∩ V`.

1. Let `p` lie in the closure of the range. Then some ultrafilter `𝒰` on the product
   contains the range and converges to `p` (`mem_closure_iff_ultrafilter`). Pull it back
   along the injective `Q` to an ultrafilter `𝒱` on `LocalSpace K f (U ∩ V)`. Then `Q`
   tends to `p` along `𝒱`.
2. For a section `a` of `U`, by `pointSeminorm_localMap`, `ν w (restriction a)` is the
   seminorm of the first component of `Q w` at `a`. By continuity of evaluation
   (published `continuous_pointSeminorm_apply`) it tends along `𝒱` to the seminorm of `p.1`
   at `a`. So it is eventually bounded. The same holds for sections of `V`.
3. The elements `c` of the coordinate ring of `U ∩ V` such that `ν w c` is eventually
   bounded along `𝒱` form a `K`-subalgebra `S`. It is closed under products and sums by
   multiplicativity and the triangle inequality. It contains the scalars because restriction
   maps commute with the algebra maps. By step 2, `S` contains the ranges of both restriction
   maps. By `sup_range_restriction_eq_top`, `S` is everything. This is the only use of
   separatedness in this proof.
4. So every `ν w c` eventually lies in a bounded interval `[0, B]`. The helper
   `SeparatedAnalytificationDensity.exists_tendsto_of_bounded` gives a limit `η` of
   `w ↦ ν w` along `𝒱` in the product topology on functions. The set `seminormFunctions` of
   multiplicative seminorm functions is closed (`isClosed_seminormFunctions`), so `η` is a
   multiplicative seminorm. It extends the norm of `K`, since each `ν w` does and limits are
   unique.
5. The published `SeminormComparison.range_pointSeminorm` gives a point `w₀` of
   `LocalSpace K f (U ∩ V)` with seminorm `η`. The point seminorm map is an embedding
   (published `SeminormComparison.isEmbedding_pointSeminorm`), so `𝒱` converges to `w₀`.
6. By continuity, `Q` tends to `Q w₀` along `𝒱`. It also tends to `p`. The product of the two
   analytifications is Hausdorff, so `p = Q w₀` lies in the range.

## Proof of `t2Space_of_isSeparated`

The published `t2Space_iff_chartRelationClosed` reduces Hausdorffness of `Space K f` to
closedness of all chart relations, given that every chart `ι K f U` is an open map. That
premise is the published `GluingReduction.isOpenMap_ι`, applied to `restrictionGluingData`.
Closedness is `isClosed_chartRelation`.
