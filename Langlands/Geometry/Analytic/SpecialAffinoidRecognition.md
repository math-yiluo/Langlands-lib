# Constructed affinoid charts recognized from special-subset sections

This module implements the affinoid recognition step for the existing literal `SpecialSubset` objects. It uses the actual Banach compatible-tuple section algebra and its already constructed canonical continuous spectrum map. Intrinsic affinoidness constructs a finite positive-radius Tate closed-kernel presentation and a bounded equivalence to the unchanged section algebra. Bijectivity of the canonical map constructs a homeomorphism, using compactness and the Hausdorff target. Its inverse, followed by the original literal subspace inclusion, gives an actual embedded affinoid chart with exactly the original subset as its range.

The source is Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), printed p.21 / PDF p.18, the paragraph before Proposition 1.2.13. The registered source is `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`; the original page was read and visually checked. That paragraph retains three conditions: affinoidness of the section algebra, bijectivity of its canonical map, and full affinoid-domain representation by the actual maps from a finite special cover. `IsRecognized` retains all three. Its last condition is tested against every finite positive-polyradius presented affinoid, using the existing `Represents` universal property. The component maps are the actual bounded equalizer comparison and projections, transported by the constructed finite presentation; their ambient chart equation is proved.

The present `SpecialSubset` family and its witnesses use original indexed charts. The source's full maximal collection uses special covers from the enlarged full local-domain net. This module constructs the recognition objects on the existing original-chart family; it does not identify that preliminary collection with the full maximal net, prove its net axioms, or assert the unproved special-set intersection lemma.

From a proof of `IsRecognized`, the module constructs the actual presented algebra, bounded equivalence, embedded chart, and full represented component domains. The finite presentation criterion is the existing `IsAffinoidAlgebra`, corresponding to Berkovich 1990, Definition 2.1.1, printed p.21 / PDF p.35, over the unchanged complete ultrametric coefficient field. Coefficient, ambient and original-index universes remain independent. Trivial valuations and the project's degenerate empty-affinoid convention remain allowed.

`original_isRecognized` proves all three conditions for every original chart. The original-chart section-algebra equivalence first proves intrinsic affinoidness and bijectivity of the canonical map. `original_chart` identifies the constructed chart with the original chart composed with its actual bounded algebra comparison. The component-map equation is proved as an equality of full bounded maps, using finite full-domain descent and the comparison's identity law. Finally, `Represents.precomp_equiv` transports each original atlas restriction to the constructed presentation, proving its exact relative-domain universal property against all affinoid tests. Thus the covering-domain condition is proved rather than inferred from spectrum-point identities.

These are constructed recoveries of unchanged original data, with no arbitrary section equalizer assumed affinoid and no supplied inverse chart or output atlas. The intersection statement module is not imported.
