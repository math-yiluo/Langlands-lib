# Actual categorical comparison of rational-cut atlases

Let `K : Type u` be a complete ultrametric normed field, `X : Type v` a topological space, `a : IndexedAffinoidAtlas.{u,v,w} K X` an actual atlas, and `t` a given original index such that every original chart range is contained in `range (a.chart t)`. This module retains that greatest-chart hypothesis. It uses the full strong comparisons constructed in `RationalCutComparison` and the actual bounded-map composition of `DirectedAtlasComposition`.

`fromOriginalInclusion_comp_toOriginalInclusion` proves that a composable old-to-cut-to-old chain has precisely the original bounded restriction. `toOriginalInclusion_comp_fromOriginalInclusion` proves that a cut-to-old-to-cut chain has precisely the constructed cut restriction. Both compare actual inclusion arrows between full domains over `a.algebra t`. Full representation uniqueness makes each Hom subsingleton; applying the restriction projection gives equality of the entire bounded algebra maps.

`originalTopPresentation` equips the lifted original presentation with the greatest index `ULift.up t`. `presentation_top_contains` proves that every cut lies in the cut with original greatest locus: its range is exactly `range (a.chart t)`, which is the whole ambient space by the original net coverage. `topPresentation` equips the constructed cut presentation with that greatest index. `originalDirectedPresentation` and `directedPresentation` use the existing greatest-chart-to-directed construction. These presentations use actual supplied or constructed algebras, restrictions and points. The original ambient universe `v` and index universe `w` remain independent; the comparison uses index universe `max v w`.

`fromOriginal_comp_toOriginal` and `toOriginal_comp_fromOriginal` prove the two round trips for `StrongHom.compOfDirected`. In each case full strong-map extensionality reduces equality to a bounded pullback into the greatest target chart for each source chart. The greatest intermediate chart gives an admissible chain. The exact directed composition formula identifies the composite pullback with the two comparison restrictions, and the inclusion-composition lemmas identify that map with the appropriate identity strong map's atlas restriction. Extensionality then recovers every other admissible bounded pullback. Equality is never inferred solely from agreement of spectrum maps.

`fromOriginal_comp_toOriginal_top` and `toOriginal_comp_fromOriginal_top` transfer both identities to the existing greatest-chart composition using the published equality between that composition and directed composition. `topIso` bundles the two actual full strong maps and the two identities into an isomorphism of `TopChartPresentation`s. `directedIso` bundles precisely the same arrows into an isomorphism of `DirectedAtlasPresentation`s. The four arrow equations record their forward and inverse maps exactly. `directedIso_eq_mapIso_topIso` proves that the directed isomorphism is the image of the greatest-chart isomorphism under the existing fully faithful functor. Both underlying continuous maps are the identity on `X`, as proved for these same arrows in `RationalCutComparison`; the categorical identities also retain every bounded pullback.
