# Local agreement of maps to an unramified affinoid piece

Consider two bounded maps `g` and `h` from an affinoid coefficient
algebra with a finite-type map `f` from the base. The two composites
with `f` agree. At the chosen point `x` of their target spectrum,
each coefficient difference has seminorm zero, and `f` is unramified
at the image under `g`.

The unramified rigidity bound gives finitely many coefficient
differences and one coefficient of positive seminorm at `x`.
The strict inequality in that bound defines an open neighbourhood
of `x` by continuity of evaluation. For every affinoid restriction
with spectrum in this neighbourhood, the pulled-back coefficients
satisfy the same inequality everywhere. The rigidity theorem then
gives equality of the full restricted bounded maps.

This internal construction applies to the identity and a section
composed with its retraction on finite pieces of an étale map.
