# Comparison of surjective étale maps with a common kernel pair

Assume the étale Hom sheaf property. A surjective étale morphism
expresses its target as the coequalizer of its kernel pair.
Indeed, a morphism that equalizes the kernel pair projections
agrees on all pairs of factorizations through the cover, by the
pullback universal property. The singleton étale cover gluing
theorem then descends the morphism.

Apply this descent in both directions to two surjective étale
morphisms with a common source and common kernel pair.
The two descended maps are inverse by uniqueness on the
respective covers. `etaleQuotientIsoOfCommonKernelPair` constructs
the isomorphism together with compatibility over the common source.
The ground field is complete and ultrametric and the field and
space universes are independent.
