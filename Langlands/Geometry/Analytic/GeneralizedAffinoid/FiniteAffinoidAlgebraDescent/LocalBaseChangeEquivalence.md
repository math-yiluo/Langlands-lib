# Further base change of local algebras and pair transitions

Over a complete ultrametric normed coefficient field, the input is an actual
finite full-domain cover and its actual finite Banach local algebras. Further
base change of a local piece to a smaller full domain canonically identifies
with the direct local pushout to that domain. The comparison respects the
entire coefficient map and the already defined local restriction. This uses
the actual pushout pasting equivalence and composition of the original bounded
restrictions.

The bounded transition on a smaller full domain is the forward map of a
bounded equivalence. The construction transports the two entire legs along
the supplied pair transition, using that transition's bounded inverse. After
pasting on both sides, comparison on the two original pushout legs identifies
the resulting map with `PairTransitions.transition`. The proof applies to
arbitrary pair transitions preserving the original coefficient algebra.

This is an internal application of the Library's actual bounded pushout
universal property and pasting equivalence. Arbitrary positive polyradii and
trivial valuations are included.
