# The precise output of effective finite affinoid algebra descent

This module constructs the actual topological gluing and the canonical maps
needed to specify the output of effective descent. `EffectiveRealization` is a
type of witnesses, not an assertion that such a witness exists.

`PairTransitions.GluedSpectrum` is the quotient of the finite disjoint union of
the original local spectra by the equivalence relation generated by the two maps
from each actual overlap spectrum. The topology is the quotient topology. Each
overlap uses the original local pushout leg on one side and the supplied entire
bounded transition on the other. The canonical continuous map to the spectrum
of the complete matching algebra is constructed from its bounded projections.
No Hausdorff hypothesis on a separate ambient analytic space is present.

`MatchingPresentation` consists of an actual presented positive-polyradius
affinoid and an actual bounded algebra equivalence from it to the entire
matching Banach algebra, with bounded inverse. This record is exclusively output
data in the effectiveness statement. Its global bounded map is the transport
of the constructed matching-algebra map from the original base. Its local maps
are the matching projections through that same equivalence.

`comparison` is a constructed whole bounded map from the base change along
each original stored cover restriction to the original local algebra. The two
comparison lemmas recover the entire original global projection and the entire
original local structural map. `restrictedComparison` constructs the induced
map over any actual full subdomain. `OverlapCompatible` says that these
entire maps over each literal intersection agree through its supplied bounded
transition. None of these equalities is replaced by agreement of seminorms.

`EffectiveRealization` requires both original global structural maps, into
the complete matching algebra and into its presented realization, to satisfy
the existing `IsFiniteBanach` condition. This retains a finite coefficient map
with a uniform positive bound on lifts. It also requires actual bounded
base-change equivalences whose entire forward maps are the constructed
comparisons, as well as exact whole-map overlap compatibility.

Finally, it requires a homeomorphism from the actual glued spectrum to the
entire spectrum of the descended presented algebra, equal to the canonical
continuous map, and homeomorphisms from every original local spectrum to the
actual inverse image of its original base-domain locus. These too equal the
canonical maps through the original bounded projections. The local finite
maps to the original base domains need not be spectrally injective. This
injectivity is neither assumed nor claimed.

The definitions are internal explicit models for the effective finite-algebra
application of Kiehl's theorem in Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for
non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), printed p.15 / PDF p.12
(Kiehl's Theorem and Remark 1.2.1(ii)) and printed p.20 / PDF p.17
(Lemma 1.2.12). Registered source `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA256
`06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`.
The finite Banach convention is from Berkovich 1990, §§2.1.9–2.1.12,
printed pp.24–25 / PDF pp.38–39, registered source
`provided-a03a0182668f6b92b01deacac09a9992b3edf55e23a0a2194554384ae44bd8d4`.
The norm-controlled and explicit closed-quotient formulations make precise the
bounded algebra and topological conclusions required here. Their existence is
the separate effectiveness statement and is not proved by these definitions.

The helper proofs use the already proved matching equations, algebra equivalence
identities, the bounded pushout universal property, and quotient-topology
continuity. They do not establish affinoidness or effective descent.
