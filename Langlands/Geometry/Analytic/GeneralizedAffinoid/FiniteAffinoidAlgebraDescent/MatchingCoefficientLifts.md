# Norm-controlled coefficients for a locally generating matching tuple

Over a complete ultrametric normed coefficient field, fix a finite full-domain
cover of an actual presented affinoid algebra and finite Banach local
algebras with ordinary bounded pair transitions. A finite tuple of matching
sections is assumed to give module generators of every supplied local algebra
over its original cover algebra.

In the free square-zero extension of the base, the degree-one ideals on the
inverse-image cover encode the kernels of the original local coefficient
sums. Exact base change of the coefficient relations computes their closed
extensions. The overlap equivalences preserve the full coefficient algebra
and identify the images of the fixed tuple, so they identify the two relation
kernels. This proves compatibility on the actual literal intersections.
For a matching section, the local coefficients of its projections define
residues modulo these ideals. Their overlap differences belong to the common
extended kernel by the matching equation.

The bounded residue-gluing theorem lifts the local linear representatives to
an element of the global free square-zero algebra. Its degree-one coordinates
are the required global coefficients, since their local errors lie in the
coefficient kernels. The estimate combines the fixed local coefficient bounds,
the bounds of the linear inclusions, the gluing bound and the norm of the global
coordinate projection. This proves `IsFiniteBanach` for the actual structural
map into the matching algebra. It uses the supplied pair transitions and the
original norms and includes empty covers, arbitrary positive polyradii and
trivial valuations.

This is an internal consequence of actual finite tensor comparison, closed
ideal effectiveness and bounded scalar gluing.
