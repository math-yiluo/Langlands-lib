# Finite free square-zero affinoid algebras

This internal construction uses the trivial square-zero extension of a presented
coefficient algebra `A` by `Fin n → A`. Its norm is the maximum of the two
coordinate norms. The original ultrametric inequality bounds the sum of the
cross terms in multiplication. The additive identification with the product
supplies completeness, and the scalar inclusion has its original norm.

The unit and degree-one coordinate vectors give explicit coefficient lifts
with bound one. The finite Banach presentation theorem therefore gives a
presented affinoid algebra with a bounded equivalence to these coordinates.
The structural map of the presentation corresponds to the original scalar
inclusion. The construction follows from the explicit coordinate formulas and
the Library's finite Banach presentation theorem.
