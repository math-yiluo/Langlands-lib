# Full-domain covers in free square-zero coordinates

This construction works over a complete ultrametric normed coefficient
field, including trivial valuations. It uses the Library theorem that
postcomposition by a bounded equivalence preserves the represented locus
and its full factorization property.

The inverse image of each original domain over a finite free square-zero
extension is represented by the corresponding square-zero algebra over the
original domain algebra. The proof uses the actual pushout comparison and
preserves the entire structural map. The resulting cover has the original
indices and uses the original bounded restrictions on every coefficient.
The literal intersections in the resulting cover compare boundedly to the
representations over the original literal intersections. Equality of their
inverse-image loci gives these comparisons, and full-domain uniqueness
identifies both entire restriction maps.

This internal construction uses the established free square-zero pushout
formula and the Library's full-domain representation and comparison results.
It applies to positive polyradii and includes trivial valuations.
