# Matching sections on the full-domain intersection refinement

Over a complete ultrametric normed field, fix original finite Banach local
algebras, arbitrary pair transitions, a full domain `V`, and a finite
full-domain cover of `V`. The restricted pieces of a matching section over
`V` give a section of the matching algebra of the relative intersection
cover. The two maps to each relative overlap identify with the original
further restrictions after their pushout comparisons. The original matching
equation on the extended overlap proves the refined matching equation.

The restriction is an algebra map and preserves the whole structural map
from `V.algebra`, by the scalar legs of the pushouts and composition of the
original domain restrictions. It is injective. For each original piece,
the refined intersections cover its underlying coefficient domain. Pulling
that cover back to the original finite algebra yields the restrictions to
the refined pieces, up to the further-base-change equivalences. Separation
on this actual cover proves equality in the original piece.

This construction uses only the ordinary pair transitions and the Library's
full-domain localization, pushout and separation results. It applies to
arbitrary positive polyradii and includes trivial valuations.

If a fixed tuple of original matching families generates every restricted
piece over its intersection coefficient algebra, its images generate all
local algebras in the refined cover. The uniform matching coefficient
lifting theorem gives coefficients in `V.algebra` for the image of any
matching family. The equality of structural maps identifies the image
of the original coefficient sum. Injectivity of the restriction gives
the original generation assertion.
