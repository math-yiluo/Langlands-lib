# Base change of finite coefficient relations

For a bounded map of presented affinoid algebras over an ultrametric normed
field and a finite tuple in the target, `relations` is the kernel of the
coefficient sum over the source algebra. Suppose the field is complete,
the map is finite Banach, and the tuple generates the target module. Then
after any bounded base change between the supplied presented affinoid
algebras, the relations are the span of the coordinatewise restrictions
of the original relations. The result includes trivial valuations and
arbitrary positive polyradii.

The proof uses right exactness of the ordinary tensor product on the
surjective coefficient map and its kernel. A tensor product with the source's
finite free module is identified with the finite free module over the changed
base. On elementary tensors the changed coefficient sum agrees with the
original sum after the Library's finite Banach tensor comparison. Injectivity
of that comparison identifies the actual pushout coefficient relations with
the image furnished by right exactness. The finite free coordinates send this
image to the stated span. This is an internal application of the ordinary
linear-algebra right-exactness theorem and the Library's finite Banach pushout
comparison.
