# Finite local algebras on the intersections of two full-domain covers

Over a complete ultrametric normed field, let `c` be the original finite
full-domain cover and let `d` be a finite full-domain cover of a full domain
`V` in the same original spectrum. The literal intersections of the
members of `c` and `d` cover `V`. Their relative representations form a
whole-spectrum cover over `V.algebra` with the same intersection algebras.
The coefficient maps are the actual bounded restrictions into those algebras.

Each original local algebra is pushed out to its intersection. Finite
Banach base change proves finiteness for these new local algebras. A domain
contained in a relative cover member extends back to the original ambient
algebra with the same restriction. The comparison of further base change
with a direct original base change identifies both actual bounded legs.

The refined pair transitions are conjugates of the actual restricted
original pair equivalences by these bounded comparisons. The coefficient
leg formulas verify that they preserve the entire overlap coefficient
algebra. The construction uses arbitrary pair transitions, positive polyradii
and arbitrary finite full-domain covers, including empty covers where allowed.
This is derived from the full-domain localization and bounded pushout APIs.
