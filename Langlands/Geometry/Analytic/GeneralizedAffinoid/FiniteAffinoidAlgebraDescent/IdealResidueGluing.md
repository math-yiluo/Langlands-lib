# Norm-controlled gluing of closed ideal residue classes

Let `K` be a complete ultrametric normed field, `A` a presented
positive-polyradius affinoid algebra, and `c` a finite full-domain cover of
its whole spectrum. Suppose closed local ideals have equal closed
extensions on each literal intersection. A family of local representatives
is `ResidueCompatible` if its differences after the two actual intersection
restrictions belong to this common ideal.

The global contraction recovers the given closed local ideals by the
Library's ideal effectiveness theorem. Form the closed quotient by this
contraction and pull the cover back to it. The actual pushout kernel
formula shows that the local representatives give compatible elements of
these quotient pullbacks. The check on each literal intersection suffices
by factorization of every full-domain overlap test through its pullback.

Algebra gluing gives an element of the global quotient. Its norm is bounded
by the maximum of the norms of the original representatives times the
positive restriction bound of the quotient cover. Admissibility of the
quotient map lifts the element to the original algebra with a fixed
additional factor. The actual pushout kernel on each member gives the
prescribed residue there. The estimate is uniform over all compatible
families and includes empty covers and trivial valuations.

This is an internal application of the Library's closed-ideal effectiveness,
closed-quotient pushout, and quantitative full-domain algebra gluing results.
