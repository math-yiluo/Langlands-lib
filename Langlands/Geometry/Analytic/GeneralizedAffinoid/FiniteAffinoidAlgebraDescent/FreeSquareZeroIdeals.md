# Closed ideals in the linear part of the free square-zero algebra

Over a complete ultrametric normed field, a submodule of the finite free
coefficient module of a presented positive-polyradius affinoid algebra
defines a closed ideal of its presented square-zero algebra. Its elements
have constant term zero and linear coefficients in the submodule.
Multiplication acts on these linear coefficients through the scalar term
alone. The coefficient projections are continuous, and the Library proves
that finite free affinoid submodules are closed in the product norm.

Under a bounded change of coefficients, the closed extension of this ideal
is the ideal of the span of the coefficientwise images of the submodule. One
inclusion follows by the closed-ideal extension property. In the other
direction, linear elements commute with coefficient restriction and are closed
under the module sums and scalar multiples used to form the span. This is an
internal application of the coordinate formulas and the Library's closed
ideal and finite free submodule results.
