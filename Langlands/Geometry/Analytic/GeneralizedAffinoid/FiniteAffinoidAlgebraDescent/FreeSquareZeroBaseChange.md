# Bounded base change of finite free square-zero affinoid algebras

Let `K` be a complete ultrametric normed field, `A` and `B` presented
positive-polyradius `K`-affinoid algebras, and `n` a natural number. Any
bounded `K`-algebra map `A → B` induces a bounded map between the presented
free square-zero algebras of rank `n`. It applies the original map to the
constant and each degree-one coefficient and preserves the linear basis.
The finite Banach lifts of the source give the bound over the specified
coefficient leg. Trivial valuations are included.

The universal map from the square-zero algebra is given by a bounded scalar
map and a tuple whose pairwise products vanish. Its degree-one part is the
finite coefficient sum. Expansion in the unit and the degree-one basis gives
uniqueness. These formulas are internal consequences of the trivial
square-zero extension and the established finite Banach coefficient estimates.

Under the same hypotheses, the forward map of the actual affinoid pushout
along the scalar inclusion
uses the coordinate restriction and the inclusion of the new scalars. Its
inverse sends the new scalars along the original right leg and each linear
basis element along the original left leg. Products of those basis elements
vanish, so the universal map gives a bounded inverse. The two inverse
identities follow from the two pushout legs and from the scalar and basis
formulas. The resulting bounded equivalence preserves both entire legs.
