# Continuous actions for represented-domain spectral tests

## Meaning and assumptions

Let `K : Type u` be a complete ultrametric normed field and let `P` be an
actual `PresentedAffinoidAlgebra K`. Its underlying algebra is the previously
constructed closed quotient of a finite positive-polyradius Tate algebra. The
maps in its category are actual bounded `K`-algebra homomorphisms. No
nontrivial valuation, reducedness, nonzero algebra or nonempty spectrum is
required.

`Domain K P` is the existing full representation of a closed subset of the
bounded multiplicative spectrum, tested against all presented affinoid
algebras in the same universe. Its structural map and section algebra are
specified data. The imported domain representation constructs all stabilizer
section operators by full unique factorization.

The imported `domainSpectralTopology P` is generated by finite families of
positive spectral displacement tests on these represented domains. Each test
also requires setwise preservation of its actual locus. This file uses that
explicit topology locally; it installs no global topology on automorphisms.

## The constructions

`domainLocusOpenSubgroup` bundles the actual setwise stabilizer as an open
subgroup of the constructed automorphism topological space, using the
already proved zero-section test.

For any topological group `G : Type v` and monoid homomorphism
`ρ : G →* Aut P`, `continuous_domainSpectralTopology_iff` characterizes
continuity by the identity-neighborhood condition for the inverse image of
every finite test subgroup. Index types in `Type` suffice because they
already supply the complete topology basis. This does not restrict the
universe of the domain or its sections.

`DomainSpectralAction G P` consists precisely of such a homomorphism and its
continuity into this constructed topology. `ofFiniteTests` constructs it
from the proved criterion. `trivial` supplies the trivial continuous action,
and `precomp` pulls an action back along a continuous group homomorphism.
These constructions retain the specified bounded algebra automorphisms.

`testOpenSubgroup` is the inverse image of any finite test subgroup; its
finite index can lie in any universe. `locusOpenSubgroup` is the inverse
image of the actual setwise stabilizer of one represented locus. Both are
open subgroups of `G`, with the stated exact membership formulas.

`stabilizerHom` maps the latter subgroup into the ambient locus stabilizer.
`sectionAction` composes it with the previously constructed bounded section
action. Its forward map on the fixed section algebra satisfies

`sectionAction(g)(baseMap(a)) = baseMap(ρ(g)(a))`.

The section operator and its bounded inverse are constructed by the imported
full representation property, rather than supplied as an action output.

## Implemented verification and provenance

For the forward direction of the continuity criterion, continuity at one
pulls each finite test identity neighborhood back to an identity
neighborhood of `G`. For the reverse direction, the exact imported basis
criterion finds a finite test subgroup inside any target identity
neighborhood. Monotonicity gives convergence at one, and Mathlib's
`continuous_of_tendsto_nhds_one` upgrades it to continuity of the group
homomorphism. The topology's previously proved topological-group structure
is supplied only locally for this argument.

The trivial and pulled-back actions use continuity of constant maps and
composition. Test and locus subgroup openness is the inverse-image theorem
for open sets. The stabilizer homomorphism inherits the original group laws;
the section action and its base equation follow by composition with the
constructed ambient stabilizer action.

This is internal construction from `DomainSpectralTopology` and
`DomainAutomorphism`, adapting Worker 36's published
`StrictTestDomain/SpectralAction` pattern to full represented
positive-polyradius domains. No new original-source translation is claimed.
The imported tests and representations supply the mathematical inputs.

## Scope

Continuity into this explicit represented-domain topology is part of the
input action and can be checked by the proved finite-test criterion. This
unit does not identify that topology with the one using every compact
analytic domain, construct general analytic spaces or sites, assert norm
continuity of section orbits, or establish joint continuity of the spectrum
action. Those are separate obligations.
