# Coefficient automorphisms of scalar-extended affinoid algebras

Let \(K\) be a nonarchimedean normed field, \(L/K\) a complete normed
extension, and \(A\) a presented \(K\)-affinoid algebra. An isometric \(K\)-algebra
automorphism \(\sigma\) of \(L\) acts on the completed tensor product
\(A\widehat{\otimes}_K L\) by \(a\otimes c\mapsto a\otimes \sigma(c)\).
`coefficientHom` transports this bounded map to the actual presented
algebra \(A_L\). `coefficientAction` gives the action by \(K\)-algebra
automorphisms of \(A_L\), fixing the image of \(A\) and acting on the
image of \(L\) by \(\sigma\). The two tensor legs determine the group
laws and naturality for all bounded maps of presented affinoid algebras.
The equation \(\sigma(ca)=\sigma(c)\sigma(a)\) records its semilinearity over \(L\).

If a topological group acts on \(L\) by these automorphisms with
continuous element orbits, every element orbit on \(A_L\) is continuous
in the quotient norm. The maps on the completed tensor product have a
common norm bound of one. On algebraic tensors, continuity follows
from the formula on pure tensors and additivity. Density then extends
continuity to every element of the completion, and the bounded tensor
comparison carries it to \(A_L\).
