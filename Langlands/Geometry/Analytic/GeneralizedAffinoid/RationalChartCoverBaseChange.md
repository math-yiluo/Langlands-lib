# Continuous coefficient change on finite rational chart sites

Authors: Worker 56 and Worker 36. The sieve argument adapts the published
`StrictTestDomain.RationalBaseChange` proof by Worker 36 to actual bounded
restrictions between arbitrary positive-radius rational quotients.

Let K be an ultrametric normed field and let A and B be presented
positive-radius affinoid algebras. Fix an arbitrary bounded K-algebra
map `φ : A → B`. The existing `pullback φ d` retains the finite arity
and positive radii of a chart d and chooses scalar-series representatives
of the images of its denominator and numerator classes. Unit generation
is proved after mapping. The existing `pullback_locus` identifies its
actual base-spectrum locus with the inverse image of d's locus under
the continuous seminorm map induced by φ.

For any family of chosen charts `D i`, `FamilyCovers.pullback` uses
this exact inverse-image equality. A point of the pulled-back target
locus maps into the original target locus. Original coverage selects
an index i, and the same equality puts the point in the pulled-back
chart `D i`. This assertion has neither a finiteness assumption on the
family nor a completeness assumption on K.

Now assume K is complete. The published `pullbackRestriction` and
`pullbackFunctor` construct the actual bounded restriction arrows by
the rational-quotient coefficient pushout, with their equations over B.
Given a finite covering witness in a sieve S, pull back all its chart
objects and these actual arrows, retaining its finite index type.
Each image arrow lies in `S.functorPushforward (pullbackFunctor φ)`
by Mathlib's generated-image membership theorem. The preceding
point-coverage result proves that this finite family covers the target.
This is `IsFiniteCovering.functorPushforward`.

`pullbackFunctor_coverPreserving` packages that proved property as
Mathlib's `CoverPreserving` for the two actual finite rational
Grothendieck topologies. This uses the already constructed functor;
no arrow is postulated from a locus inclusion. Empty families and
empty spectra remain allowed, as do zero or nonreduced quotients,
trivial valuations and arbitrary positive radii.

The separate `RationalChartPullbackLimits` construction proves that the
coefficient functor preserves pullbacks, using actual representative
comparison maps and bounded intersection lifts. Together with covering
preservation, Mathlib's
`Functor.PreservesOneHypercovers.of_coverPreserving` proves
`pullbackFunctor_preservesOneHypercovers`. Every Grothendieck topology is
generated by one-hypercovers in the universe of the chart category, so
`Functor.isContinuous_of_preservesOneHypercovers` proves
`pullbackFunctor_isContinuous`. No continuity property is assumed.

For any category E, with independent object and morphism universes,
`coefficientSheafPushforward φ E` now constructs a functor from
E-valued sheaves on the finite rational chart site over B to E-valued
sheaves on the site over A. It applies Mathlib's proved continuous-site
construction to the actual coefficient-pullback functor. Its underlying
presheaf is `F ↦ (pullbackFunctor φ).op ⋙ F`; the displayed natural
isomorphism with the forgetful functor records this, and evaluation on
a chosen chart d is exactly the value of F on `pullback φ d`. Natural
transformations act by the same restriction. This is direct image in
the direction of the spectrum map from B to A; no sheafification,
completeness or limit hypothesis is imposed on E.

This construction concerns arbitrary existing sheaves on the finite
point-covering topology on chosen rational charts. It makes no identification with the full analytic
G-topology, and asserts no sheafness of the rational quotient
presheaf, full analytic-domain factorization, analytic atlas,
etale site or equivariant-site construction. It does construct the
specialized direct-image functor on these finite rational sites; it does
not assert that the quotient-algebra presheaf is a sheaf.
