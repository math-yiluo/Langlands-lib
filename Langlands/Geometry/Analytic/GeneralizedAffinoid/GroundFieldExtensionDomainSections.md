# Semilinear section descent on represented affinoid domains

Let a full affinoid domain in a scalar-extended affinoid spectrum
be equipped with a finite ambient rational cover. Use the common
subgroup of the coefficient isometries that act isometrically on the
rational quotients of every member and every pairwise intersection.

A section on the domain restricts to this cover, and the transformed
sections agree on each rational intersection since the operators
commute with the bounded restrictions. Any full-domain test mapping
to two members factors through their rational intersection. The
actual algebra gluing theorem therefore produces a section of the
original domain algebra. The finite cover inverse norm estimate and
the chart operator bounds give boundedness of this operator. Equality
on the cover proves the identity, multiplication law, and the exact
restriction equation on ambient base sections. A bounded semilinear map
out of the domain is determined by its composite with the ambient
algebra. Composing with the inverse section operator reduces this
uniqueness to the bounded universal property of the represented domain.

For a topological group with norm-continuous scalar orbits, the
intersection of the finitely many rational operator subgroups is
open. Continuity of the restricted sections and the inverse norm
estimate give norm continuity on the full domain algebra. Inverse
pullback acts jointly continuously on its spectrum and agrees with
the ambient coefficient action through the domain map. Near the
identity, each fixed section meets any positive spectral tolerance
uniformly on that domain.

The construction implements descent of operators for the compact-domain
tests of V. G. Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic
spaces*, unfinished manuscript (1995–1996), §1.1, printed/PDF pp. 1–2.
