# Ground field extension of presented affinoid algebras

This module constructs the ground field extension `A ↦ A_L` for the Library's
presented affinoid algebras, together with its structural map, its universal
property, its bounded functoriality and the projection of spectra. All
declarations live in the namespace
`Langlands.Analytic.PresentedAffinoidAlgebra.GroundFieldExtension`.

Throughout, `K` is an ultrametric normed field and `L` is an ultrametric normed
field with `NormedAlgebra K L`. The fields may live in different universes. The
module places no restriction on the degree or transcendence degree of `L/K`,
and it does not require either valuation to be nontrivial.

## The algebra `A_L`

`coefficientMap K L` is the structural map `K → L`, packaged as a bounded
`K`-algebra map. It is isometric (`norm_coefficientMap`), because a normed
algebra map from a normed field is isometric.

Let `A` be a `PresentedAffinoidAlgebra K`, with arity `n`, polyradius `r` and
closed presentation ideal `A.ideal` in the weighted Tate algebra over `K`.
`relations K L A` is the set of coefficientwise images of the elements of
`A.ideal` in the weighted Tate algebra over `L` with the same polyradius `r`.
The coefficient map used here is the Library's bounded
`mapCoefficientsAlgHom`.

`algebra K L A` is the presented `L`-affinoid algebra
`ofRelations L A.arity A.polyradius (relations K L A)`. Thus it has the same
arity and the same radii as `A` (`arity_algebra`, `polyradius_algebra`), and
its ideal is the closure of the ideal generated by the coefficient extension of
`A.ideal` (`ideal_algebra`, which holds by definition). It carries its quotient
norm. The instances `instNormedAlgebraBase` and `instIsScalarTower` regard it
as a normed `K`-algebra through `K → L`.

`overBase K L ψ` regards a bounded `L`-algebra map between `L`-algebras that
are also `K`-algebras through a scalar tower as a bounded `K`-algebra map with
the same function. `presentationMapOverBase` is the presentation map of `A_L`
regarded over `K` in this way.

## The structural map `ι : A → A_L`

`seriesMap` is coefficient extension followed by the presentation map of `A_L`.
It kills every element of `A.ideal` (`seriesMap_ideal`), because the image of
such an element is one of the generating relations of `A_L`. Hence it descends
through the closed quotient to the bounded `K`-algebra map `iota K L A`.

- `iota_presentationMap`: `ι` sends the class of a series over `K` to the class
  of its coefficient extension.
- `iota_coordinate`: `ι` sends the `i`-th presentation coordinate of `A` to the
  `i`-th presentation coordinate of `A_L`.
- `norm_iota_le`: `ι` does not increase quotient norms. The proof combines the
  bound on the presentation map with the bound on coefficient extension by an
  isometric coefficient map.

`scalarMap K L A` is the structural map `L → A_L`, regarded as a bounded
`K`-algebra map.

## Universal property

Let `E` be a normed commutative ring with a normed `L`-algebra structure, and
let `E` be a `K`-algebra with `IsScalarTower K L E`. Thus `E` is an `L`-algebra
regarded as a `K`-algebra through `K → L`; this is how the module expresses
"a `K`-algebra map from `A` to an `L`-algebra".

- `hom_ext`: two bounded `L`-algebra maps `A_L → E` with the same composite
  with `ι` are equal. They agree on the coordinates of `A_L` by
  `iota_coordinate`, and the Library's `hom_ext_coordinate` applies.

Assume moreover that `E` is complete and ultrametric, and that its
`K`-structure is a normed algebra structure.

- `evaluateHom_comp_mapCoefficients`: for a bounded `K`-algebra map
  `φ : A → E`, evaluating series over `L` at the values of `φ` on the
  coordinates of `A`, after coefficient extension, is `φ` composed with the
  presentation map of `A`. Both sides are bounded `K`-algebra maps out of the
  weighted Tate algebra over `K`. They agree on its variables, so the Library's
  uniqueness theorem for such maps applies.
- `evaluate_relations`: consequently this evaluation kills every relation of
  `A_L`.
- `lift K L A E φ`: the bounded `L`-algebra map `A_L → E` obtained from the
  Library's `evaluateRelations`. The bounded monomial condition is the Library's
  `hasBoundedMonomials_coordinate` for `φ`.
- `lift_coordinate`, `lift_comp_iota`, `lift_iota`: the lift sends each
  coordinate of `A_L` to the image under `φ` of the corresponding coordinate of
  `A`, and its composite with `ι` is `φ`.
- `lift_unique` and `existsUnique_lift`: the lift is the unique bounded
  `L`-algebra map whose composite with `ι` is `φ`.
- `liftEquiv`: these maps give a bijection between bounded `K`-algebra maps
  `A → E` and bounded `L`-algebra maps `A_L → E`.

The target `E` is an arbitrary complete ultrametric normed commutative
`L`-algebra, not only a presented `L`-affinoid algebra. The universal
property does not require `L` to be complete.

## Functoriality

From here on `L` is also complete. Then every presented `L`-affinoid algebra is
complete, so each `B_L` is an admissible target for `lift`.

- `map K L f`, for a bounded `K`-algebra map `f : A → B`, is the lift of
  `ι_B ∘ f`. It is the unique bounded `L`-algebra map `A_L → B_L` with
  `map_comp_iota`, that is, `f_L ∘ ι_A = ι_B ∘ f`.
- `map_iota` and `map_coordinate` evaluate `f_L` on `ι_A a` and on the
  coordinates of `A_L`.
- `map_id` and `map_comp` are the identity and composition laws, proved by
  `hom_ext`.
- `functor` packages the construction as a functor from the category of
  presented `K`-affinoid algebras to the category of presented `L`-affinoid
  algebras. Morphisms in both categories are the bounded algebra maps.

## Spectrum projection

`spectrumProjection K L A` is the continuous map from the Berkovich spectrum
of `A_L` to that of `A` that restricts a bounded multiplicative seminorm along
`ι`. It is the Library's `BanachSpectrum.comapContinuous` for `ι`, and
`spectrumProjection_apply` records its value. For complete `L`,
`spectrumProjection_naturality` states the following for every bounded map
`f : A → B` and every point `x` of the spectrum of `B_L`: pulling `x` back
along `f_L` and then projecting gives the same point of the spectrum of `A`
as projecting `x` to the spectrum of `B` and then pulling back along `f`. The
proof is `map_iota`.

## Scope

The construction allows zero algebras, nonreduced quotients, empty spectra and
arity zero. It assumes no strictness of the presentation and no nontriviality
of the valuations of `K` or `L`. Completeness of `K` is never assumed, so the
case of a complete `K` required by the task is included. Completeness of `L`
is used only for
functoriality and for the naturality of the spectrum projection. The
constructions of `A_L`, `ι`, `lift` and the spectrum projection do not need it.

This module does not identify `A_L` with the completed tensor product. That
comparison is in `GroundFieldExtensionTensor`. Affinoid domains and atlases
are not treated here.

## Provenance

The functor is Berkovich's ground field extension of affinoid algebras
(V. G. Berkovich, *Spectral theory and analytic geometry over non-Archimedean
fields*, AMS 1990, §2.3, printed p. 32). For an affinoid algebra `𝒜` over `k`
and a non-Archimedean field `K` over `k`, Berkovich defines the functor
`X ↦ X ⊗̂ K = 𝓜(𝒜 ⊗̂_k K)`, where `⊗̂` is the completed tensor product of §1.1,
printed p. 12.

The source defines `A_L` as the completed tensor product. Its description as
the quotient of the weighted Tate algebra over `L` by the closure of the
coefficient-extended ideal is a derived presentation that we chose because it
directly produces an object of `PresentedAffinoidAlgebra L`. The module
`GroundFieldExtensionTensor` justifies this choice: there `tensorEquiv` is a
bounded `K`-algebra equivalence between `A_L` and the Library's
`CompletedTensorAlgebra.Algebra K A L`, compatible with both legs. The universal
property, functoriality and spectrum projection are internal constructions
built on the Library's relation-quotient universal property
(`evaluateRelations`, `hom_ext_coordinate`) and bounded coefficient extension.
The same construction for the completed Laurent field appears in
`RelativeLaurentScalarExtension`.
