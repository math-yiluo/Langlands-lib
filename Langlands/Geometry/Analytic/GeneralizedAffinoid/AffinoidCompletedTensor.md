# Actual affinoid presentation of the completed tensor algebra

This module constructs a bounded algebra equivalence between the genuine projective completed tensor of two actual presented affinoid algebras and their constructed finite closed weighted Tate coproduct presentation. The input is only the two original presentations over a complete ultrametric normed field. No completed output presentation, comparison map, inverse, or recognition property is supplied.

The source motivation is Berkovich, *Spectral Theory and Analytic Geometry over Non-Archimedean Fields* (1990), §1.1, printed p.12 / PDF p.26, the nonarchimedean projective completed tensor convention; and *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §1.2, printed p.20 / PDF p.17, the completed tensor maps in the special-cover definition and Lemma 1.2.12. This is a derived comparison of two already constructed algebras by their proved bounded universal properties. The finite presentation comes from the scalar-presentation pushout. The genuine completed tensor comes from the completion of the algebraic tensor for the infimum of finite maximum factor-norm products. The comparison is bounded in both directions; an isometry between these particular quotient norms is not asserted.

`toCompletedTensor` applies the finite-presentation bounded coproduct lift to the two genuine completed-tensor legs. `fromCompletedTensor` applies the actual bounded completion extension to the two original presentation legs. Their four recovery equations identify the full bounded maps from each unchanged original algebra.

The first inverse law follows from the finite presentation's full two-leg extensionality. The second follows from completed-tensor extensionality, whose proof uses algebraic tensor generation and density. Both arguments compose the entire original leg equations. They preserve all algebra elements, including information invisible to spectrum points. `completedTensorEquiv` packages these two constructed bounded maps and the proved inverse laws.

`completedTensorSpectrumHomeomorph` is the contravariant spectrum homeomorphism of that actual equivalence, from the genuine completed tensor spectrum to the finite-presentation spectrum. This does not identify either spectrum with the Cartesian product of the underlying factor spectra.

`lift_comp_fromCompletedTensor` proves that every bounded coproduct lift into an arbitrary complete ultrametric normed commutative target, in an independent universe, is exactly the genuine completed-tensor lift after the constructed comparison. This gives full algebra-map compatibility for subsequent overlap maps. The target need not be affinoid or reduced. The construction permits zero factor algebras and uses no nontrivial-valuation hypothesis. General overlap-map admissibility, special-cover intersection and maximal-atlas recognition remain subsequent obligations.
