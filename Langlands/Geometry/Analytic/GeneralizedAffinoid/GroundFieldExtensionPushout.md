# Ground field extension of bounded affinoid pushouts

Given two bounded maps from a presented affinoid algebra \(A\) to
presented affinoid algebras \(B\) and \(C\), extend the bounded affinoid
pushout along an isometric embedding \(K\to L\) of complete
nonarchimedean fields. Its extension is boundedly isomorphic to the
affinoid pushout of the extended maps. The comparison intertwines both
quotient legs. The algebraic comparison only needs completeness of
the extension field.

To construct the comparison, restrict the two extended quotient legs
along the original scalar-extension inclusions and use the bounded
pushout over \(K\). Extend the resulting map by the scalar-extension
universal property. The inverse comes from the bounded pushout over
\(L\), applied to the scalar extensions of the original quotient legs.
The two universal properties identify the composites with the
identities.

This is the affinoid algebra comparison underlying commutation of the
chart fibre products with ground field extension. V. G. Berkovich,
*Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §1.4,
Proposition 1.4.1 through the construction of the ground field
extension, printed pp. 29–30 (PDF pp. 26–27).
