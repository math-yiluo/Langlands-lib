# Radius decrease as an actual Weierstrass quotient

Worker 56 derives an isometric algebra equivalence between the actual scalar
rational quotient at smaller radii and a relative Weierstrass quotient over
the quotient at the original radii. The construction uses the published
scalar radius map, intrinsic bounded evaluation and scalar-to-relative
comparison.

All declarations lie in
`Langlands.Analytic.PresentedAffinoidAlgebra.RationalPresentation.RadiusWeierstrass`.
Let `K` be an ultrametric normed field, `A` an actual presented affinoid
algebra, and `g,f j` scalar series representatives. Let `s,t` be positive
polyradii on the same finite variable block. Write

\[
B_s=\operatorname{algebra}(K,A,s,g,f),\qquad
i_s=\operatorname{baseMap}(K,A,s,g,f),\qquad
Y_{s,j}=\operatorname{fraction}(K,A,s,g,f,j).
\]

The coefficient and fraction equations are the existing actual quotient
identities `i_s(gbar) * Y_s,j = i_s(fbar_j)`.

## The relative Weierstrass algebra

`Algebra K A s t g f` is the existing intrinsic relative Weierstrass quotient

\[
W_{s,t}=B_s\{t^{-1}Z\}/\overline{(Z_j-Y_{s,j})_j}.
\]

More precisely, its carrier is
`RelativeRationalPresentation.WeierstrassAlgebra K B_s t Y_s`: the actual
weighted restricted-series algebra with coefficients in `B_s`, modulo the
closure of the ideal generated by the displayed equations. This definition
does not assume completeness, radius ordering or unit-ideal generation.

`base : B_s → W_s,t` is its existing bounded coefficient map.
`coordinate j` is the class of the newly adjoined variable `Z_j`.
`coordinate_eq_base` proves `Z_j = base(Y_s,j)` using the existing
denominator-one fraction equation. The coefficient map is contractive,
and `norm_coordinate_le` proves `‖Z_j‖ ≤ t_j`.

The private unit-norm bound uses contractivity of `base` at `1` and the
published inequality `‖1_B_s‖ ≤ 1`. The existing
`monomialValue_norm_le_weight` then supplies the weighted monomial bound
for the tuple `Z`. It includes the empty monomial and zero quotients.

## The two constructed bounded maps

The remaining algebra constructions assume `CompleteSpace K`. Completeness
provides the complete target quotients for intrinsic bounded evaluation
and the published scalar-to-relative comparison.

`fromSmaller : B_t → W_s,t` first applies the isometric scalar-to-relative
comparison at `t`. It then evaluates the intrinsic rational quotient over
`A` in `W_s,t`. The coefficient map is `base ∘ i_s`, a composite of
contractions; the new tuple is `Z`. Replacing `Z_j` by `base(Y_s,j)` and
applying `base` to the rational equation in `B_s` proves the evaluation
equations. Intrinsic bounded evaluation constructs the map.

`fromSmaller_baseMap` and `fromSmaller_fraction` give

\[
F(i_t(a))=\operatorname{base}(i_s(a)),\qquad F(Y_{t,j})=Z_j.
\]

`norm_fromSmaller_le` follows from contractive intrinsic evaluation
and the norm-preserving scalar-to-relative comparison. This map and
these assertions do not require `t ≤ s`.

Now suppose `hts : ∀ j, t j ≤ s j`. The published radius restriction is
the actual contractive map `R : B_s → B_t`. `toSmaller : W_s,t → B_t`
is intrinsic bounded evaluation with coefficient map `R` and tuple
`Y_t`. Its monomial bound follows from `‖Y_t,j‖ ≤ t_j` and `‖1_B_t‖ ≤ 1`.
The equation `R(1) * Y_t,j = R(Y_s,j)` follows from preservation of the
unit and the published fraction formula for radius restriction.

The proved formulas and bound are

\[
E(\operatorname{base}(b))=R(b),\qquad E(Z_j)=Y_{t,j},
\qquad \|E(w)\|\leq\|w\|.
\]

## Inverses, exact norms and spectra

`fromSmaller_comp_radiusMap` proves `F ∘ R = base` by scalar
coefficient-and-fraction extensionality. On fractions it uses
`Z_j = base(Y_s,j)`. Intrinsic coefficient-and-fraction extensionality
then proves `fromSmaller_toSmaller`: `F(E(w))=w` for every `w`.
Scalar extensionality at `t`, with the two evaluation formulas, proves
`toSmaller_fromSmaller`: `E(F(b))=b` for every `b`.

`equiv` packages these constructed mutually inverse bounded maps as

\[
W_{s,t}\simeq_{\mathrm{bounded},K}B_t.
\]

`equiv_base` identifies its composite with the Weierstrass coefficient
map as the original radius restriction; `equiv_coordinate` records its
coordinate values. Both maps contract, and they are inverses, so
`norm_equiv` and `norm_equiv_symm` prove equality of the actual quotient
norms in both directions. `isometry_equiv` follows from the forward norm
equality for an additive homomorphism.

`spectrumMap` is actual continuous precomposition along `base`.
`spectrumHomeomorph` is the contravariant spectrum homeomorphism induced
by `equiv`. Its evaluation is precomposition along `equiv`, as recorded
in `spectrumHomeomorph_apply`. `spectrumHomeomorph_naturality` proves
equality of two continuous maps from `M(B_t)` to `M(B_s)`: the Weierstrass
structural map after that homeomorphism equals the original continuous
radius spectrum pullback. The proof evaluates both seminorms and uses
`equiv_base`.

## Provenance and scope

The source context is Vladimir Berkovich, *Non-Archimedean analytic spaces*,
Trieste lectures (2009), registered as
`berkovich-2009-trieste-analytic-spaces`, PDF SHA256
`8cfe4f6f35d5e717ac67c5c8f5ef9ff9185f460bbe5f9a10aeacd84cddeeb2ca`.
Definition 2.1.6(i), printed/PDF page 8, defines relative weighted
restricted series. Exercise 2.2.2(i)-(ii), printed/PDF page 9, gives
the Weierstrass equations and common-denominator rational equations
at positive radii.

This quotient equivalence, its norm identities and its structural
spectrum square are internal derivations from the published concrete
algebras and evaluation constructions, with proofs described above.
They establish the chosen-presentation algebra identity for radius
decrease. No generation or proper-ideal hypothesis is imposed.
Empty blocks, arbitrary positive radii, trivial valuations and zero
quotients are permitted. The original radius map `B_s → B_t` is still
only asserted to be contractive: the inverse constructed here has domain
`B_t` and codomain `W_s,t`. The module does not establish the full
analytic-domain universal property, a structure sheaf, an atlas or
an analytic site.
