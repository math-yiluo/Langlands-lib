# Coordinate universal property of finite weighted relation quotients

Authors: Worker 105 and Worker 56. Worker 105 implements the proof and this explanation. Worker 56 supplied the descent and precomposition derivation in the task context. The proof imports the published weighted evaluation and closed relation quotient results; their existing authorship is retained in their own modules.

`Langlands.Analytic.PresentedAffinoidAlgebra.relationsUniversalProperty` proves the entire unchanged `RelationsUniversalPropertyStatement.{u, v}`. The coefficient field and target have independent universes. The field is ultrametric but need not be complete. The number of variables is any natural number, including zero, the radii are arbitrary positive real numbers, and the set of relations is arbitrary. The target may be the zero ring, its unit need not have norm one, and the relation ideal may be the unit ideal. There is no nontrivial valuation or value-group hypothesis.

First, three reusable lemmas apply to every `PresentedAffinoidAlgebra K`, whose carrier has the actual quotient norm. Precomposing a bounded map with `presentationMap` gives a bounded map from the weighted Tate algebra. `hasBoundedMonomials_coordinate` applies the published `hasBoundedMonomials_map_X` to this composite, since a presentation coordinate is definitionally the image of `X`. `map_presentation_eq_evaluate` applies the published `map_eq_evaluate` to the same composite. `hom_ext_coordinate` applies weighted Tate coordinate extensionality to the two composites and then uses surjectivity of the presentation map through `hom_ext_presentation`. These lemmas assume neither completeness nor ultrametricity of the target. In particular, boundedness of the coordinate monomials is a conclusion about every bounded map, not an additional hypothesis.

For a presentation `ofRelations K n r relations`, `evaluate_relation_coordinate` rewrites evaluation at the coordinate images of any bounded map as the value of that map on the projected series. Each supplied relation projects to zero by `presentationMap_relation`, so its evaluated value is zero. This necessity lemma also needs no target completeness or ultrametricity.

In a complete ultrametric target, `evaluateRelations` descends the published bounded weighted evaluation homomorphism `evaluateHom` by `descendRelations`. Its inputs are a tuple with `HasBoundedMonomials r B b` and actual vanishing of every supplied relation under `evaluate r K B b`. The imported descent uses continuity to annihilate the closure of the generated relation ideal. `evaluateRelations_presentationMap` gives agreement on every projected series, definitionally, and `evaluateRelations_coordinate` gives the prescribed coordinate values by the published `evaluate_X`.

`norm_evaluateRelations_le` preserves every supplied positive constant `C` satisfying the weighted monomial bound. The source bound comes from `evaluate_norm_le_bound`, and `norm_descendRelations_le` transfers it to the actual quotient norm without an additional factor. The constructed map is fixed before `C` is quantified; its construction uses boundedness of the tuple, while this estimate works with every admissible `C`, independently of a witness used to establish that boundedness.

`boundedSolution_iff_existsUnique` proves the solution criterion directly. A tuple satisfying the monomial and relation conditions yields `evaluateRelations`; coordinate extensionality proves uniqueness. Conversely, any map with the prescribed coordinates yields the monomial bound and relation vanishing by the preceding necessity lemmas. Function extensionality identifies its coordinate tuple with the prescribed tuple and transports these two properties. The converse uses only the existence part of unique existence. No equivalence of types or subtype of solutions is needed for this proof.

The final theorem assembles these results into all three conjunctions of the exact target: construction with projected-series and coordinate identities and the every-constant estimate; monomial necessity and uniqueness in arbitrary normed targets; and the equivalence with unique existence in complete ultrametric targets. Its whole type is the published proposition, with no additional hypotheses.

This is an internal derivation from `GeneralizedAffinoid.Relations` and `GeneralizedTateAlgebra.Evaluation`. The original-source motivation and its limits are recorded in the unchanged `RelationsStatement.md`: the explicit bounded-monomial criterion is a derived quotient form, not a claim of equivalence with a spectral-radius criterion or the relative-affinoid extension theorem. This module makes no additional assertion about spectra, analytic domains, structure sheaves, atlases, or the completion of the analytic-space parent task.
