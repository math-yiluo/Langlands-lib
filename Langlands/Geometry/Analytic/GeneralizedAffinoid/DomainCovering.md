# Finite coverings of all full represented affinoid domains

Author: Worker 36.

For a complete ultrametric normed field `K : Type u` and an actual
positive-polyradius presented affinoid algebra `A`, this module constructs
`finiteDomainTopology K A`, a Grothendieck topology on the existing category
`Domain K A` of all full represented domains over `A`. Objects retain their
actual quotient algebras, bounded structural maps and full affinoid-test
representation property. Arrows retain their actual bounded restrictions.

`FamilyCovers U D` means that every point of `U.locus` belongs to a member of
`D`. `IsFiniteCovering S` means that `S` contains a finite family of actual
arrows into its target whose loci cover. The finite index has universe `u`
and may be empty. A covering is defined by the original represented loci,
not by a supplied sheaf, cohomology object or abstract comparison.

## Rational refinement and the sieve axioms

`finiteRationalCoverData U` packages the published unconditional theorem
`Domain.finiteRationalCover` as a `FiniteRationalCover U`. It supplies rational
charts of the original ambient algebra, actual arrows from their domains
into `U`, and pointwise coverage. No rational cover is an additional input.

`IsFiniteCovering.rationalRefinement` applies that theorem to every member
of a finite covering family. A finite dependent sum indexes all resulting
rational charts. Their arrows are the composites of their actual bounded
restrictions with the original covering arrows; downward closure places
them in the original sieve. Two successive coverage statements prove that
this rational family covers the target. `isFiniteCovering_iff_rational`
therefore characterizes covering sieves exactly by such finite ambient
rational families.

The maximal sieve covers by the singleton identity family. For pullback
stability along `f : U ⟶ V`, first refine a covering of `V` by ambient rational
charts `D i`. Use the constructed domain `U.rationalInter (D i)` with its two
actual bounded projections. Its locus is `U.locus ∩ (D i).locus`, so these
domains cover `U`. Uniqueness of full-domain arrows identifies the composite
through `f` with the composite through the rational chart. Downward closure
then places the left projection in the pulled-back sieve. This proof uses
the published genuine rational quotient intersections; it does not assume
the existence of arbitrary intersections of two full domains.

For transitivity, choose a finite covering family in the first sieve and
a finite family in each specified pulled-back sieve. Their finite dependent
sum, with composite actual arrows, covers the target. These three proofs
are the fields of `finiteDomainTopology`.

## Concrete covers and empty loci

`ofArrows_mem_finiteDomainTopology_iff` states that the sieve generated by a
finite family of actual arrows covers exactly when that family's loci cover.
For the forward implication, a witness in the generated sieve factors
through an original member; its actual restriction implies locus containment.
The converse uses the original family itself.

`FiniteCover.ofArrows_mem_finiteDomainTopology` applies this criterion to
every existing concrete finite cover. `isFiniteCovering_iff_finiteCover`
gives the converse packaging: a covering sieve contains such a
`FiniteCover`, obtained by enumerating the finite witness with `Fin`.

The empty sieve covers exactly when the target locus is empty, equivalently
when its actual quotient spectrum is empty. The latter equivalence uses
the existing exact spectral image and surjective structural corestriction.
No structural injectivity theorem is needed in this module.

## Provenance and scope

This is an internal construction from the independently published
`DomainRationalCoverProof`, `DomainRationalNeighborhoodCover`,
`DomainRationalIntersection`, and concrete finite-cover API, using Mathlib's
definition of a Grothendieck topology. The sieve construction follows the
published finite rational chart topology, now with a proved rational
refinement step for every full domain. No private draft is reused, and no
additional assertion from the original literature is invoked.

All positive presentation and rational radii, trivial valuations,
nonreduced and zero quotients, and empty domains and covers remain allowed.
The construction is the finite point-covering site of represented domains
over one fixed affinoid. It does not yet construct an algebra sheaf on that
site, compare its sheaf category with the rational chart site, saturate an
analytic atlas, or construct etale or equivariant sites.
