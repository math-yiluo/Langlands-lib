# Strict base change of weighted quotient presentations

Author: Worker 972.

This module turns a quotient presentation with arbitrary positive radii over
an ultrametric field `K` into a strict presentation, with all radii equal to
one, over a larger ultrametric field `L`. It needs three things:

- an isometric field embedding `ι : K →+* L`;
- elements `c i : L` with `‖c i‖` equal to the `i`-th radius;
- an additive retraction `π : L →+ K` that is `K`-linear in the sense
  `π (ι a * x) = a * π x`, satisfies `π 1 = 1`, and is bounded,
  `‖π x‖ ≤ D * ‖x‖` with `D > 0`.

No completeness of `K` or `L` is assumed. The main result,
`PresentedAffinoidAlgebra.exists_strictBaseChange`, produces a strict
presentation `Q` over `L` and a ring homomorphism `ψ : P →+* Q` with
`‖ψ x‖ ≤ ‖x‖` and `‖x‖ ≤ D * ‖ψ x‖` for every `x` in `P`. In the theorem the
constant is stated existentially as some `C > 0`. The field `L` may live in
any universe.

## Series-level base change and descent

Write `c ^ d = ∏ i, c i ^ d i` for an exponent `d`. This is
`GeneralizedTateAlgebra.scale c d`. Its norm is the radius weight `r ^ d`
(`norm_scale`). In particular it is nonzero (`scale_ne_zero`). It is
multiplicative in `d` (`scale_add`).

`GeneralizedTateAlgebra.baseChange r ι hc hι` sends a weighted restricted
series `f = ∑ f_d X^d` over `K` to `∑ (c ^ d) ι(f_d) X^d`. In Lean it is
Mathlib's `MvPowerSeries.rescale c` composed with `MvPowerSeries.map ι`,
restricted to the radius-one Tate algebra over `L`. The restriction is valid
because `‖(c ^ d) ι(f_d)‖ = ‖f_d‖ r ^ d`
(`isRestricted_baseChange`). The map is a ring homomorphism, and it is an
isometry for the weighted and radius-one Gauss norms (`norm_baseChange`).

`GeneralizedTateAlgebra.descend r hc π hπD` goes back. It sends a radius-one
series `g = ∑ g_d X^d` over `L` to the series with coefficients
`π((c ^ d)⁻¹ g_d)`. It is an additive map with the following properties.

- Its coefficients satisfy `‖π((c ^ d)⁻¹ g_d)‖ r ^ d ≤ D ‖g_d‖`. So the image
  is weighted restricted (`isRestricted_descend`) and
  `‖descend g‖ ≤ D ‖g‖` (`norm_descend_le`). Hence `descend` is continuous
  (`continuous_descend`).
- It is linear over base-changed series:
  `descend (baseChange f * g) = f * descend g`
  (`descend_baseChange_mul`). The proof compares the coefficients of both
  products over the antidiagonal. It uses `c ^ (p + q) = c ^ p * c ^ q` and
  the `K`-linearity of `π`.
- Since `π 1 = 1`, it is a left inverse of base change:
  `descend (baseChange f) = f` (`descend_baseChange`).

`descend_mem_of_mem_closure_map` says the following. Let `I` be a closed
ideal of the weighted Tate algebra. Then `descend` sends every element of the
closure of `Ideal.map baseChange I` into `I`. The proof uses the auxiliary
ideal `T` of the radius-one Tate algebra. It consists of those `x` with
`descend (g * x) ∈ I` for every `g`. By the linearity above, `T` contains the
ideal generated by `baseChange I`. Taking `g = 1` puts that ideal inside the
preimage of `I` under `descend`. This preimage is closed by continuity, so it
contains the closure.

## The strict presentation and the comparison

`PresentedAffinoidAlgebra.strictBaseChange P ι hι hc` is the strict
presentation over `L` with the same number of variables. Its ideal is the
closure of the ideal generated by `baseChange` of `P.ideal`.
`strictBaseChangeHom` is the induced ring homomorphism of quotients. It is
built with `Ideal.Quotient.lift`, and it commutes with the presentation maps
by definition (`strictBaseChangeHom_presentationMap`).

- `norm_strictBaseChangeHom_le` says `‖ψ x‖ ≤ ‖x‖`. Choose a representative
  `f` of `x` with norm close to `‖x‖`. Then `ψ x` is the class of
  `baseChange f`, which has the same norm.
- `norm_le_mul_norm_strictBaseChangeHom` says `‖x‖ ≤ D ‖ψ x‖`. Write
  `x` as the class of `f` and choose a representative `g` of `ψ x` with
  `‖g‖ < ‖ψ x‖ + ε / D`. Then `g - baseChange f` lies in the ideal of the
  strict presentation. By the closure lemma, `descend g - f` lies in
  `P.ideal`. So `x` is the class of `descend g`, and
  `‖x‖ ≤ ‖descend g‖ ≤ D ‖g‖ < D ‖ψ x‖ + ε`.

`exists_strictBaseChange` packages these two facts with `Q = strictBaseChange`
and `C = D`.

## Scope and provenance

This module does not identify `Q` with the completed tensor product
`P ⊗̂_K L`, and it does not prove that `ψ` is injective or isometric. Only
the two norm inequalities above are proved. Choosing `L`, `c` and `π` is left
to the caller; Worker 1010's `RadiusRealization` module supplies them.

The idea of reducing a `K`-affinoid algebra to a strictly affinoid one by
extending the ground field comes from Berkovich, *Non-Archimedean analytic
spaces* (Trieste lectures, 2009). Definition 2.1.2 (PDF page 7) defines
`k`-affinoid and strictly `k`-affinoid algebras through admissible
epimorphisms from `k{r⁻¹T}`. Definition 2.1.12 (PDF page 9) uses a field
`k'` over `k` for which `A ⊗̂_k k'` is strictly `k'`-affinoid. These pages
were read directly. The construction here is an internal, elementary
replacement: an explicit rescaled coefficient map, the closure of the
generated ideal instead of a completed tensor product, and a bounded
retraction `π` that controls the norm. None of these lemmas is stated in the
source.
