# Proof of spectrum identification for weighted rational presentations

Authors: Worker 227, Worker 36 and Worker 56. Worker 227 implements the weighted proof and the weighted monomial estimate `GeneralizedTateAlgebra.hasBoundedMonomials_of_norm_le`. The density, point-lifting, injectivity and closed-embedding arguments adapt Worker 36's published strict proof `Langlands.Analytic.RationalPresentation.spectrumIdentification` in `RationalSpectrum`. Worker 56 formulated the weighted target and supplied the weighted proof route that this module follows. The weighted rational presentation, the bounded extension API (`liftFromBase`, `liftFromBase_comp_baseMap`, `denominator_ne_zero`), weighted monomial expansions and completed residue fields are imported by Lean name; their implementations are not copied.

`Langlands.Analytic.PresentedAffinoidAlgebra.RationalPresentation.spectrumIdentification` proves the entire unchanged `SpectrumIdentificationStatement.{u}`. No result in this module assumes completeness of the coefficient field `K`, a nontrivial valuation, a value-group condition on the radii or a nonzero quotient.

## Notation

All notation is imported unchanged from `GeneralizedAffinoid.RationalPresentation`. `A` is a `PresentedAffinoidAlgebra K` with polyradius `r`, `q` is its presentation map, and `s : PositivePolyradius (Fin m)` gives the new radii. The rational quotient `R = algebra K A s g f` is the quotient of the series algebra at `r.append s` by the closure of the ideal generated by the included old ideal and the equations `i(g) Y_j - i(f_j)`. Write `alpha = baseMap K A s g f`. The new quotient coordinate is `fraction j`, which satisfies `alpha(q g) * fraction j = alpha(q (f j))` (`fraction_equation`). The fraction subalgebra `D` is the imported `fractionSubalgebra K alpha (q g)`. Its elements are the `b` in `R` with `alpha(q g)^k * b = alpha(a)` for some natural number `k` and some `a` in `A`.

## Weighted monomial estimate

`GeneralizedTateAlgebra.hasBoundedMonomials_of_norm_le` takes an arbitrary index type `σ`, a positive polyradius `r` on `σ` and a tuple `b` in a normed commutative ring whose unit has norm one. If `‖b i‖ <= r i` for every `i`, then `b` has weighted bounded monomials with constant one. The proof unfolds the monomial value and the radius weight into finite products over the support of the exponent. The norm of the product is at most the product of the norms (`Finset.norm_prod_le`), and each factor satisfies `‖b i ^ n‖ <= ‖b i‖ ^ n <= r i ^ n`. Comparing the products factor by factor (`Finset.prod_le_prod₀`) gives the bound. The empty monomial is the unit, of norm one.

## Density without generation

`presentationMap_monomial_mem` shows that any subalgebra `C` of a presented algebra `T` that contains all quotient coordinates contains every projected coefficient monomial `q_T(monomial d c)`. The imported `GeneralizedTateAlgebra.map_monomial` writes this image as `algebraMap c` times the finite product of the coordinate images raised to the exponents. Each coordinate image is the coordinate itself, so this element lies in `C`.

`dense_fractionSubalgebra` takes `C` to be the topological closure of `D`. The coordinates of `R` are indexed by `Fin (A.arity + m)` and are split by `Fin.addCases`. An old coordinate equals `alpha(coordinate K A i)` by `baseMap_coordinate`. It therefore lies in `D` with exponent zero. A new coordinate is `fraction j`. It lies in `D` with exponent one and numerator `q (f j)`, by `fraction_equation`. Every element of `R` has the form `q_R(h)` by surjectivity of the presentation map. The imported `hasSum_monomials` expresses `h` as the unconditional sum of its coefficient monomials, without coefficient completeness. Mapping that sum through the continuous linear map underlying `q_R` gives a limit of finite sums of projected monomials. These sums lie in `C`, and `C` is closed, so `q_R(h)` lies in `C`. This clause uses neither the unit-ideal hypothesis nor invertibility of the denominator. It proves density of `D`, not density of the range of `alpha`.

## Point lifting and exact range

The remaining declarations assume `GeneratesUnitIdeal K A g f`. Let `x` be a point of `locus K A s g f`, let `H(x) = CompletedResidueField K x`, and let `rho = completedResidueMap K x`. The imported `norm_completedResidueMap` gives `‖rho a‖ = x a`. This complete ultrametric normed field is constructed from `x` itself.

- `residueFractions K A g f x j` is `rho(q (f j)) / rho(q g)` in `H(x)`.
- `residueDenominator_ne_zero` proves `rho(q g) ≠ 0`, because its norm `x(q g)` is nonzero by the imported `denominator_ne_zero`.
- `norm_residueFractions_le` proves `‖residueFractions K A g f x j‖ <= s j`. The norm of the quotient is `x(q (f j)) / x(q g)`, and the locus inequality `x(q (f j)) <= s j * x(q g)` is divided by the positive number `x(q g)`.
- `residueFractions_bounded` applies the weighted monomial estimate with polyradius `s` to obtain `HasBoundedMonomials s (H(x)) (residueFractions K A g f x)`.
- `residueFractions_equations` proves `rho(q g) * residueFractions K A g f x j = rho(q (f j))` by cancelling the nonzero denominator.

`pointLift` applies the imported `liftFromBase` to `rho` and this bounded fraction tuple; this gives a bounded algebra map from `R` to `H(x)`. The lifted point is the pullback (`comap`) of the field norm point `fieldPoint (H(x))` along that map. `spectrumMap_pointLift` proves that `spectrumMap K A s g f` sends it to `x`. The imported `liftFromBase_comp_baseMap` shows that the lift sends `alpha a` to `rho a`, whose norm is `x a`. `range_spectrumMap` combines this with the imported forward containment `spectrumMap_mem_locus` to prove that the range is exactly the locus.

## Injectivity and closed embedding

`spectrumMap_injective` considers two points `x` and `y` of the spectrum of `R` with the same image. Each point is continuous as a function on `R`, because it is the norm of its bounded completed residue map. The image point lies in the locus, so `denominator_ne_zero` gives `x(alpha(q g)) ≠ 0`. Now let `b` lie in `D`, with `alpha(q g)^k * b = alpha(a)`. Applying `x` and `y` to this equation, and using multiplicativity and the equality of the two points on the image of `alpha`, gives `x(alpha(q g))^k * x b = x(alpha(q g))^k * y b`. Cancelling the nonzero power gives `x b = y b`. The set where `x` and `y` agree is closed, so it contains the closure of `D`, which is all of `R` by density. Hence `x = y`.

`isClosedEmbedding_spectrumMap` uses the imported compactness of every `BanachSpectrum` and Hausdorffness of its target. These hold for normed commutative rings without completeness. A continuous injective map from a compact space to a Hausdorff space is a closed embedding. Finally, `spectrumIdentification` assembles the unconditional density clause with the two conditional clauses of exact range and closed embedding.

## Scope

No argument splits on the old or new variable counts, on whether the old ideal or the rational quotient is the unit ideal, or on whether the absolute value of `K` is trivial. Empty fraction tuples, zero quotients and trivially valued coefficient fields are therefore covered by the same proof. The only completeness used is that of the constructed field `H(x)`, which the extension API requires of its target.

This is an internal derivation from the published weighted presentation, extension and approximation results, together with the strict proof adaptation identified above. The source context and its limits are those recorded in the unchanged `RationalSpectrumStatement.md`: Berkovich, *Non-archimedean analytic spaces* (Trieste lectures, 2009), Exercise 2.2.2(ii), page 9, and Fact 2.2.3(i),(iv), page 10, registered as `berkovich-2009-trieste-analytic-spaces`. That source works over complete fields with relative restricted series and localizes at all functions nonvanishing on the domain. The density proved here is for the chosen-denominator subalgebra `D` of the specified presentation. The proof makes no relative-series comparison, no claim that absence of common spectral zeros implies unit-ideal generation, no presentation-independence or analytic-domain representability claim, and provides no structure sheaf or etale site.
