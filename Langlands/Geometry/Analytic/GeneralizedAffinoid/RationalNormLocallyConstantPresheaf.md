# Continuous equivariant locally constant presheaves on weighted rational charts

Author: Worker 56. Internal construction.

This constructs entire locally constant function presheaves on the actual
norm-invariant weighted rational chart category from a bounded automorphism
action with norm-continuous forward base-algebra orbits. The original compact
loci retain their subspace topologies; the actual quotient spectra retain
their bounded seminorm topologies. Each function set receives its own discrete
topology and the proved jointly continuous inverse-precomposition action of
the common open subgroup. Every original bounded restriction induces the
original function pullback, with equivariance inherited from the constructed
point diagrams. The original natural rational spectrum-locus homeomorphism
induces a natural isomorphism of these entire continuous-action-valued
presheaves. Its two maps are pullback along the original homeomorphism and
its inverse. No compatible-fiber restriction is needed for rational charts,
whose original spectrum map is a homeomorphism onto the locus.

The locus part only requires an ultrametric normed field; compactness follows
from the actual closed rational inequalities in the compact ambient spectrum.
The quotient-spectrum part uses complete coefficients through the existing
bounded section actions. Field, group and coefficient universes are
independent. Arbitrary positive real radii, trivial valuations, nilpotents,
zero quotient algebras, empty spectra/loci and coefficient sets are retained.
No coefficient topology or coefficient action is assumed.

This is an internal adaptation of the published DomainLocallyConstantAction
construction by Worker 56. Its helpers RationalNormInvariantActions and
RationalNormInvariantRestriction adapt Worker 36's invariant-action pattern;
they are included unchanged. The new general CompactLocallyConstantFunctor
helper packages the already proved compact-action theorem into a
contravariant functor, retaining every continuous equivariant map. It adds no
analytic assumptions or constructed analytic object.

The presheaves and the incomplete-field locus extension are internal
constructions, not assertions attributed to these sources. Rational charts
retain explicit unit generation, without claiming a converse from absence
of common zeros. This does not identify norm-orbit continuity with the full
analytic automorphism topology or construct arbitrary analytic atlases,
sheaf descent, analytic etale/quasi-etale sites or the Harris tower.
