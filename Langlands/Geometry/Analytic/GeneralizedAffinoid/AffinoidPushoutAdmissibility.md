# Admissible completed-tensor quotients onto actual affinoid pushouts

Worker 1685 constructs the canonical map from the genuine nonarchimedean projective completed tensor of two presented affinoid algebras onto the actual affinoid pushout of two bounded maps with a common source, and proves that it is an admissible epimorphism. The construction permits arbitrary positive polyradii, trivial coefficient valuations, nonreduced and zero algebras. The coefficient field is complete, normed and ultrametric.

This is internal derived infrastructure for the closed-immersion steps of Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), Lemma 1.2.12, printed p.20 / PDF p.17, registered source `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`. Its tensor and quotient-seminorm conventions are inherited unchanged from the imported actual constructions. The admissibility meaning is Berkovich, *Spectral Theory and Analytic Geometry over Non-Archimedean Fields* (1990), §1.1, printed pp.11–12 / PDF pp.25–26, registered source `provided-a03a0182668f6b92b01deacac09a9992b3edf55e23a0a2194554384ae44bd8d4`, SHA256 `9b543e7ffae8ec6febf154a60c4d11a288f7ecc3795c66f004ba3200a6824e79`.

`IsAdmissibleEpi.of_comp` proves that a bounded factor of an admissible composite is admissible. The uniform lift criterion gives representatives for the composite; applying the preceding bounded map preserves a uniform positive lift bound for the remaining factor.

`AffinoidPushout.coproductMap` is the bounded absolute-coproduct lift of the unchanged two pushout quotient legs. `coproductMap_comp_presentationMap` proves that its composite with the actual coproduct presentation is the actual target presentation map. Both finite presentations use the same appended weighted Tate variables; the proof checks every variable in each block using the entire original leg equations. Thus admissibility of the target presentation and the factor lemma prove `coproductMap_isAdmissibleEpi`.

`tensorMap` is the genuine completed-tensor lift of the two original quotient legs. `coproductMap_comp_fromCompletedTensor` identifies it with the constructed finite-presentation map after the actual bounded tensor comparison. The comparison has its constructed bounded inverse, hence is admissible by the bounded-section criterion. Composing the two admissible maps proves `tensorMap_isAdmissibleEpi`. In particular, the proof establishes both surjectivity and positive two-sided comparison with the actual infimum-over-preimages quotient seminorm, without an open mapping theorem or a nontrivial-valuation assumption.

This quotient result does not by itself construct intersections between full local domains in unrelated original charts of an atlas. The finite closed-immersion descent step in the full special-set theorem remains a separate obligation.
