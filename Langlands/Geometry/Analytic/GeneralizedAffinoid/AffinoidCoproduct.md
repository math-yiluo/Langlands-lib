# Actual scalar presentation and bounded affinoid coproducts

This module constructs a scalar presentation and the bounded algebra coproduct of two actual finite positive-polyradius affinoid presentations. It uses the existing appended-presentation bounded pushout. The original quotient carriers and their norms are preserved; the output is an actual finite closed quotient, rather than a supplied algebra with an assumed universal property.

This is derived infrastructure motivated by Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §1.2, printed p.20 / PDF p.17, the definition of special coverings and Lemma 1.2.12. That passage requires the completed tensor map to an overlap to be an admissible epimorphism. The registered source is `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`.

The present contribution proves the bounded algebra coproduct property and a norm-controlled split diagonal. It does not identify the quotient norm isometrically with a projective tensor norm, construct the completion of an algebraic tensor product, or assert specialness or admissibility of arbitrary overlap maps. Those source comparisons remain separate. It also makes no assertion that its spectrum is the Cartesian product of the two underlying topological spectra.

`initialAlgebra` is the zero-variable weighted Tate relation quotient with no relations. `initial_hom_ext` proves uniqueness of every bounded map from this presentation by coordinate extensionality, since there are no coordinates. This uniqueness requires neither target completeness nor an ultrametric target. `initialMap` evaluates the empty variable tuple in any complete ultrametric normed target algebra, in an independent universe. Its monomial bound uses the norm of the target unit plus one and therefore allows the zero algebra and a unit whose norm has not been normalized.

`scalarInclusion` is the original scalar algebra map into this quotient, with its norm bound proved from the normed-algebra scalar formula. The evaluation map recovers every scalar. Uniqueness of maps out of the zero-variable presentation supplies the other inverse equation. `initialAlgebraEquiv` is consequently an actual bounded algebra equivalence with the unchanged coefficient field. Completeness of the coefficient field is assumed for this equivalence and the coproduct constructions.

`algebra` is the previously constructed finite appended weighted Tate quotient pushed out over this explicit scalar presentation. `leftMap` and `rightMap` are its constructed bounded legs, and their norms are at most the norms of the original elements. Every pair of bounded algebra maps out of the two original algebras agrees over the scalar presentation by its proved uniqueness theorem. `lift` therefore applies the existing bounded pushout lift without requiring any compatibility input from the caller.

`hom_ext`, the two full bounded-map recovery equations, and `boundedHomEquiv` prove the complete bounded algebra coproduct property. The target can be any complete ultrametric normed commutative algebra over the coefficient field, in an independent universe. It need not be affinoid, reduced, nonzero, or endowed with a nontrivial valuation. `lift_mul` records the exact product formula on elements from the two original legs.

`diagonal` takes both original maps to be the identity. Both composites with the legs are the identity as full bounded algebra maps. The first leg is an explicit bounded section, and `diagonal_preimage` constructs a preimage of every original algebra element with no larger norm. Thus the diagonal's surjectivity and this norm estimate are proved without an open mapping theorem or any nontrivial-valuation hypothesis. General overlap maps and the special-cover intersection theorem remain unproved by this module.
