# Closed ideals on a finite full-domain cover

This module supplies internal definitions and elementary properties for a closed-ideal specialization of Kiehl descent. The source motivation is Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), Kiehl's Theorem and Remark 1.2.1(ii), printed p.15 / PDF p.12. Berkovich states finite-module effectiveness on a finite affinoid covering, identifies the descended module with its section equalizer, and explicitly includes general affinoids and trivially valued coefficient fields by reduction. Lemma 1.2.12, printed p.20 / PDF p.17, uses this theorem for compatible local quotient algebras on a product cover. Registered source: `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`.

These definitions and elementary proofs are derived infrastructure, rather than a transcription or proof of Kiehl's theorem. In particular, this module does not assert that every compatible family is effective.

For an actual bounded algebra map `f`, `closedIdealMap f I` is the topological closure of the ideal generated by the image of `I`. The topology is that of the stored norm on the actual target ring. The module proves that this ideal is closed, contains the image of `I`, is monotone, and commutes with composition. It also proves its adjunction with contraction into a closed ideal. Composition follows by applying the adjunction twice; continuity of a bounded map makes the inverse image of a closed ideal closed.

For a concrete finite full-domain cover `c : FiniteCover U`, `idealContraction c I` is the intersection of the inverse images of the local ideals under the actual restrictions from `U.algebra`. Its membership condition is exactly that every restriction belongs to the corresponding ideal. It is closed when each local ideal is closed, since it is an intersection of closed inverse images. Its closed extension to each cover member is contained in the supplied local ideal. This follows directly from the adjunction, without any compatibility assumption. The contraction is the largest global ideal with this containment property.

`IdealCompatible c I` states equality of the two closed extensions on each actual `Domain.inter` overlap. The maps are the original bounded legs of the constructed affinoid pushout. The module proves this compatibility for closed extensions of any global ideal, using composition and uniqueness of full-domain inclusions.

The cover and every domain retain their actual positive-polyradius quotient presentations. There is no assumption of a nontrivial coefficient valuation, nonempty spectrum, nonzero quotient, reduced algebra, strict radius, or positive cover size. The source's effectiveness step is a separate proposition and proof obligation.
