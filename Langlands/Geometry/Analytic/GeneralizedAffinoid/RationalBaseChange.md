# Bounded coefficient base change of weighted rational quotients

Author: Worker 56.

This module constructs the bounded pushout for an arbitrary bounded change of
coefficient algebra with matched rational presentation classes. Under source
unit-ideal generation, it also constructs the topological pullback of the
actual quotient spectra. Both results use the existing scalar rational
quotients and the actual bounded coefficient transport.

The namespace is
`Langlands.Analytic.PresentedAffinoidAlgebra.RationalPresentation`;
the base-change constructions are in its `BaseChange` namespace.
Let `K : Type u` be an ultrametric normed field and let `A,B` be presented
affinoid `K`-algebras. Their presentation arities, positive polyradii and
closed ideals may differ. Supply a common finite new-variable block
`Fin m`, its positive polyradius `s`, source representatives `g,f` and
target representatives `g',f'`. Write `q_A,q_B` for the actual coefficient
presentation maps. The bounded coefficient homomorphism `φ : A → B`
satisfies precisely

```text
hg : φ(q_A(g)) = q_B(g'),
hf : φ(q_A(f(j))) = q_B(f'(j)) for every j.
```

Thus equality is required only for the actual coefficient classes. An inverse
of `φ`, coefficientwise scalar representatives of `φ`, contractivity and
unit generation are not part of these matching hypotheses.
Write `Q_A = algebra K A s g f` and `Q_B = algebra K B s g' f'`.
They are the existing enlarged weighted-series quotients by the closed ideals
generated by the old relations and the equations `g T_j - f_j`, respectively
`g' T_j - f'_j`. Write `ι_A,ι_B` for their `baseMap`s and `t_A(j),t_B(j)`
for their actual fraction coordinates. When `K` is complete, the published
`presentationTransport` supplies `τ : Q_A → Q_B`, with

```text
τ(ι_A(a)) = ι_B(φ(a)),    τ(t_A(j)) = t_B(j).
```

The resulting square of bounded maps commutes by these coefficient formulas.

`hasBoundedMonomials_map_fraction` proves that every bounded map
`α : Q_A → E`, for an arbitrary normed commutative `K`-algebra `E : Type v`,
sends the fraction tuple to a tuple with the weighted monomial bound at `s`.
The proof commutes each finite monomial through `α`, applies a positive
boundedness constant for `α`, then uses the actual quotient fraction norm
bounds. The published monomial estimate uses `‖1‖ ≤ 1`, so the empty monomial
and a zero quotient need no exceptional assumptions. The universes `u,v`
are independent.

`BaseChange.generatesUnitIdeal` proves that source unit-ideal generation
implies target unit-ideal generation. The proof puts the target generators
in an ideal `J`, shows that the source generated ideal lies in the comap
of `J` along `φ`, and maps membership of `1` to conclude `J = ⊤`.
`BaseChange.locus_eq_preimage` proves, without generation, the exact equality

```text
locus_B = (BanachSpectrum.comap φ) ⁻¹' locus_A.
```

Here each locus uses the corresponding actual coefficient classes and the
inequalities `x(q(f(j))) ≤ s(j) * x(q(g))`. Matching classes turn the
target inequalities into the pulled-back source inequalities. Neither of
these two facts needs completeness of `K`.

For the bounded pushout, supply a complete ultrametric normed commutative
`K`-algebra `E : Type v`, bounded maps `α : Q_A → E`, `β : B → E`, and

```text
hcompat : α.comp ι_A = β.comp φ.
```

`BaseChange.fraction_equation` transfers the actual source quotient equations
to the target coefficient classes:

```text
β(q_B(g')) * α(t_A(j)) = β(q_B(f'(j))).
```

Compatibility identifies the two base restrictions; multiplicativity of `α`
and the source fraction equation give the result. `pushoutLift` is the
published `liftFromBase` for `Q_B`, evaluated at the tuple `α(t_A(j))`.
The monomial bound and the transferred equations supply its required inputs.
Thus it is an actual bounded algebra map obtained by descended convergent
weighted evaluation. This construction and its base and fraction formulas
need completeness and ultrametricity of `E`, but not completeness of `K`.

For the remaining algebra comparisons assume `K` complete, as required by
`τ`. `pushoutLift_comp_baseMap`, `pushoutLift_fraction` and
`pushoutLift_comp_transport` prove

```text
pushoutLift.comp ι_B = β,
pushoutLift(t_B(j)) = α(t_A(j)),
pushoutLift.comp τ = α.
```

The last equation uses the published source base-and-fraction extensionality
and the two displayed formulas for `τ`. `BaseChange.hom_ext` proves that
the two legs `τ,ι_B` determine a bounded map out of `Q_B` into any normed
commutative `K`-algebra, without completeness or ultrametricity of that
receiving algebra. The coefficient values follow from the `ι_B` leg, and
the fraction values from the `τ` leg. Finally,
`existsUnique_pushoutLift` proves the precise bounded universal property

```text
∃! θ : BoundedAlgHom K Q_B E,
  θ.comp τ = α ∧ θ.comp ι_B = β.
```

No unit-generation hypothesis occurs in this result. The module gives this
explicit universal property; it does not construct a category of all the
receiving normed algebras or a bundled colimit in such a category.

For spectra, assume `hunit : GeneratesUnitIdeal K A g f`. Target generation
is the proved `generatesUnitIdeal hg hf hunit`, rather than an additional
input. Let `c : M(B) → M(A)` be the actual continuous seminorm pullback
along `φ`, let `a : M(Q_A) → M(A)` and `b : M(Q_B) → M(B)` be the
structural maps, and let `t : M(Q_B) → M(Q_A)` be the actual
`presentationSpectrumMap` induced by `τ`.

For every topological space `X : Type v`, `spectrumLift` takes continuous
maps `p : X → M(B)` and `q : X → M(Q_A)` satisfying
`c(p(x)) = a(q(x))` for every `x`. The exact locus inverse-image equation
and the published spectrum-image membership show `p(x) ∈ locus_B`.
The lift is the actual inverse of the published target `locusHomeomorph`
applied to this subspace-valued map. Its continuity follows from subspace
continuity and continuity of the inverse homeomorphism. The construction
itself and its projection `spectrumMap_spectrumLift` do not need
completeness of `K`.

With `K` complete, `presentationSpectrumMap_spectrumLift` proves the other
projection by applying source spectrum injectivity, the published transport
naturality equation `a ∘ t = c ∘ b`, and the first projection.
`spectrumLift_unique` uses target spectrum injectivity to show that the
projection to `M(B)` already determines the lift. `spectrumCone` bundles
the actual maps `b,t` as a `TopCat` pullback cone over `c,a`.
`spectrumConeIsLimit` constructs its `IsLimit` from the continuous lift,
both projection identities and uniqueness. Hence the explicit spectrum
square is a topological pullback, with no supplied limit witness.

The original context is Berkovich, *Non-Archimedean analytic spaces*,
Trieste lectures (2009), registered as
`berkovich-2009-trieste-analytic-spaces`, PDF SHA256
`8cfe4f6f35d5e717ac67c5c8f5ef9ff9185f460bbe5f9a10aeacd84cddeeb2ca`.
Definition 2.1.11, printed/PDF p.8, specifies bounded homomorphisms and the
dual direction of affinoid spaces. Exercise 2.2.2(ii), p.9, gives the
positive-radius rational quotient and inequalities, with a no-common-zero
hypothesis for its domain conclusion. Fact 2.2.3(i), p.10, identifies the
spectrum of a representing domain with that domain. These original pages
were read directly; no source-specific errata file is supplied.

The bounded pushout and the explicit `TopCat` pullback here are internal
derivations from the published weighted evaluation, rational extension,
coefficient transport and proved rational spectrum homeomorphism. They are
not separately numbered results in the cited passages. Unit-ideal generation
is the precise algebraic hypothesis used for the spectrum identification;
no equivalence with the source's no-common-zero wording is asserted here.
The implemented proof uses the quotient fraction equations and monomial
bounds for the algebra lift, and the proved inequality-locus identification
for the continuous lift. It assumes neither a pushout nor a pullback as
input.

The new-variable block may be empty, ideals need not be proper, and zero
quotients, empty loci, trivial valuations and arbitrary positive radii are
allowed. No full rational-domain representation theorem, analytic structure
sheaf, atlas or etale site is constructed by this module. Those remain
separate obligations of the broader analytic-space task.
