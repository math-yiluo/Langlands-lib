# Bounded maps between scalar weighted rational presentations

This module constructs bounded maps between actual scalar weighted rational
quotients from bounded maps of their actual coefficient algebras. The
coefficient algebras may have different presentation arities, positive
polyradii and closed ideals. The finite new coordinate block and its positive
polyradius `s` are shared.

The namespace is
`Langlands.Analytic.PresentedAffinoidAlgebra.RationalPresentation`.
For the scalar construction let `K` be a complete ultrametric normed field.
Supply presented coefficient algebras `A,B`, scalar denominator/numerator
representatives `g,f` over `A` and `g',f'` over `B`, and a bounded
`K`-algebra homomorphism `φ : A → B`. The hypotheses are exactly

```text
φ(q_A(g)) = q_B(g'),
φ(q_A(f(j))) = q_B(f'(j)) for every j.
```

No inverse of `φ` is required. In particular the input is an actual bounded
map between closed-ideal quotient algebras, and the rational quotient map is
constructed from it.

Write `Q_A = algebra K A s g f` and `Q_B = algebra K B s g' f'`.
They are the published scalar quotients of the enlarged weighted-series
algebras by the closed ideal generated by the old coefficient relations
and the rational equations. Write `R_A,R_B` for the corresponding intrinsic
relative-series quotients using `q_A(g),q_A(f(j))` and
`q_B(g'),q_B(f'(j))`. The published `relativeEquiv` gives actual
isometric bounded equivalences `α_A : Q_A ≃ R_A` and `α_B : Q_B ≃ R_B`.

The private `matchedTransport` starts with the published intrinsic
coefficient transport and identifies its mapped target data with the
supplied target data by equality induction. The intrinsic transport itself
is the actual descended coefficientwise weighted-series map, with rational
relation compatibility proved in the imported module. Equality induction
also proves its coefficient, fraction, projection and unchanged
nonnegative-bound formulas. It introduces no assumed quotient map or
extension property.

`presentationTransport` is the composite
`α_B.symm ∘ matchedTransport ∘ α_A`, implemented as a composition of
bounded algebra homomorphisms. Its coefficient and fraction formulas are

```text
presentationTransport(baseMap_A(a)) = baseMap_B(φ(a)),
presentationTransport(fraction_A(j)) = fraction_B(j).
```

The proofs apply the injectivity of `α_B`, cancel its inverse, and use the
published coefficient and fraction formulas and the equality-induced
intrinsic formulas.

`relativeEquiv_presentationTransport_presentationMap` computes every
source scalar representative `h` in the intrinsic target:

```text
α_B(presentationTransport(q_Q_A(h))) =
  quotientMap_B(mapCoefficientsAlgHom s φ
    (Adjoin.relativeSeriesEvaluation K A s h)).
```

The proof rewrites by the scalar-to-relative representative formula and the
intrinsic coefficientwise projection formula. This formula does not choose
scalar representatives over `B` for the mapped coefficients.

`eq_presentationTransport` proves uniqueness among all bounded
`K`-algebra maps `Q_A → Q_B` with the displayed coefficient and fraction
values. Its proof uses the published scalar `hom_ext`; no uniqueness
assumption is an input to the construction.

For every real `c ≥ 0`, `norm_presentationTransport_le` proves

```text
(∀ a : A, ‖φ(a)‖ ≤ c * ‖a‖)
  → ∀ x : Q_A, ‖presentationTransport(x)‖ ≤ c * ‖x‖.
```

The bound retains the same constant, including `c = 0`. Its proof moves
to the intrinsic target using exact norm preservation of `α_B`, applies
the proved intrinsic transport bound, and uses exact norm preservation of
`α_A`. The contractive specialization is also given. These are estimates
for the actual quotient norms.

`presentationTransport_id` and `presentationTransport_comp` prove
equalities of bounded maps. Identity coefficient transport is the identity
on the scalar quotient. For composable `φ : A → B` and `ψ : B → C`
with matching rational data at each step, the map for `ψ.comp φ` equals
the composite of their scalar quotient maps. The proof supplies the
composite matching equalities by congruence and transitivity and applies
`hom_ext` to coefficients and fractions.

`presentationTransport_eq_representativeMap` proves that the identity
coefficient-map specialization with two choices of equal scalar classes
agrees with the previously published `representativeMap`. The proof again
uses `hom_ext` and the two families of formulas.

`presentationSpectrumMap` is the genuine continuous contravariant
pullback from `BanachSpectrum Q_B` to `BanachSpectrum Q_A`. Its
evaluation formula is `x(presentationTransport(a))`. The imported
spectrum topology is the topology of evaluation of multiplicative
seminorms. `presentationSpectrumMap_naturality` is equality of the two
continuous maps to `BanachSpectrum A`: first quotient pullback and then
the source structural map, or first the target structural map and then
coefficient pullback along `φ`. Evaluation reduces the proof to the
coefficient formula.

`presentationLocusMap` restricts actual coefficient-spectrum pullback
to the scalar rational inequality loci. The matching denominator and
numerator equalities turn the target inequalities into the required source
inequalities. Continuity is inherited from the coefficient-spectrum map
through the subspace topologies. Its evaluation formula is `x(φ(a))`.
Identity and contravariant composition are proved as equalities of
continuous maps by evaluation and extensionality. These locus
declarations omit completeness of `K`; the scalar quotient construction
uses completeness.

The original context is Berkovich, *Non-Archimedean analytic spaces*,
Trieste lectures (2009), registered as
`berkovich-2009-trieste-analytic-spaces`, retained PDF SHA256
`8cfe4f6f35d5e717ac67c5c8f5ef9ff9185f460bbe5f9a10aeacd84cddeeb2ca`.
Printed/PDF pages 8–9 were read directly and visually checked.
Definition 2.1.6(i) supplies relative weighted restricted series;
Definition 2.1.11 specifies bounded homomorphisms and the dual direction
of spectra; Exercise 2.2.2(ii) supplies the rational equations and
inequalities. The maps and laws implemented here are internal
derivations from the published actual quotient constructions, rather
than a numbered source assertion. The source's rational-domain
conclusion has an additional no-common-zero hypothesis and is outside
this module's claims.

The new block may be empty and the coefficient ideals need not be proper.
Zero quotients, trivial valuations and arbitrary positive radii are
allowed. No unit-ideal or nonemptiness hypothesis is used. No isometry,
injectivity, surjectivity, inverse map, denominator invertibility,
rational-domain representability, atlas, sheaf or analytic site is
asserted for a general bounded coefficient map. This is one local
morphism construction in the larger analytic-space task.
