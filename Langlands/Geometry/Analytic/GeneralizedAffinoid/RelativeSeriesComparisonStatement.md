# Isometric comparison with relative weighted restricted series: target

Author: Worker 56.

`Langlands.Analytic.PresentedAffinoidAlgebra.Adjoin.RelativeSeriesComparisonStatement`
is a proposition definition specifying three comparison obligations for the
published actual forward map. This module does not prove the proposition.

## Objects and hypotheses

Let `K` be a complete ultrametric normed field and let
`A : PresentedAffinoidAlgebra K`. Write `n = A.arity`,
`r = A.polyradius`, and `I = A.ideal`. The carrier of `A` is the actual
quotient `K{r⁻¹T}/I`, equipped with its quotient norm; `I` is closed.
For `m : ℕ` and an arbitrary positive real polyradius
`s : PositivePolyradius (Fin m)`, put

\[
 D=K\{r^{-1}T,s^{-1}Y\},\qquad
 J=\overline{\langle i(I)\rangle},\qquad
 B=D/J,\qquad C=A\{s^{-1}Y\},
\]

where `i = GeneralizedTateAlgebra.appendLeft r s K`.
The ideal `J` is exactly `(Adjoin.algebra K A s ∅).ideal`:
`Adjoin.relations K A s ∅` consists of the included old ideal, and
`ofRelations` takes the topological closure of its generated ideal.
The algebra `B` is exactly `Adjoin.algebra K A s ∅`, with its quotient
norm. The algebra `C` is exactly `GeneralizedTateAlgebra s A`, with
weighted coefficient norm

\[
 \left\|\sum_\nu a_\nu Y^\nu\right\|
   =\sup_\nu\left(\|a_\nu\|\prod_j s_j^{\nu_j}\right).
\]

Both objects and all maps below are imported from the published construction.
`relativeSeriesEvaluation K A s`, denoted by `E`, evaluates scalar series
in `D` by sending the old variables to constant series of their quotient
coordinates in `A` and the new variables to the variables of `C`.
`relativeSeriesMap K A s`, denoted by `Φ`, is its actual descent to `B`.
For the quotient map `q_B`, the established representative formula is
`Φ(q_B(f)) = E(f)`. The existing map is a bounded `K`-algebra homomorphism
and is contractive. Its restriction to the base algebra gives constant
series, and it sends each new quotient coordinate to the corresponding
variable of `C`.

The proposition quantifies over every `K`, `A`, `m` and `s` with precisely
the hypotheses above. It imposes no nontrivial-valuation assumption, no
value-group condition on the radii, and no properness condition on `I`.
Both `n` and `m` may be zero. In particular, the zero quotient `I = ⊤` is
included. It assumes no inverse, bijectivity, isometry, coefficient-lifting
theorem or comparison equivalence.

## The three asserted clauses

After the common quantifiers, the Lean proposition conjoins:

1. `Function.Surjective Φ`: every element of the actual relative-series
   algebra `C` has a preimage in the actual enlarged quotient `B`.
2. `Isometry Φ`: the map preserves the distances induced by the stated
   quotient and weighted coefficient norms. Since `Φ` is additive, this
   also expresses exact norm preservation, `‖Φ(b)‖ = ‖b‖`. This is stronger
   than the contraction already established.
3. For every `f : D`, `E(f) = 0` if and only if `f ∈ J`. This is a
   pointwise description of the prequotient evaluation kernel as exactly
   the closed ideal generated by the included old ideal.

The first two clauses express an isometric algebraic comparison using the
already constructed forward map, rather than an existential choice of an
unspecified equivalence. Isometry supplies injectivity. The third clause is
included as a useful explicit kernel formulation; it follows from
injectivity, the representative formula and the quotient's zero criterion
once those ingredients are proved or supplied. No inverse map is defined
in this statement module.

## Derived provenance and proof boundary

The source context is Vladimir Berkovich, *Non-Archimedean analytic spaces*
(Trieste lectures, 2009), Definition 2.1.6(i), printed/PDF page 8.
It defines finite-variable relative weighted restricted series over an
affinoid algebra by weighted coefficient decay and the maximum weighted
coefficient norm. For finitely many variables, the imported cofinite decay
condition agrees with decay as total degree tends to infinity, and the
supremum above agrees with that norm. The passage was read directly.

The displayed isometric quotient comparison and its exact kernel are
derived targets for the project's specific quotient norm and specific
evaluation map. They are not claimed to be a theorem stated in that
numbered definition. The imported forward-map module already explains its
construction from weighted evaluation and closed-relation descent.

A proposed proof can first identify scalar series in the two variable
blocks with weighted series in the new variables whose coefficients are
old-variable series, preserving the weighted norm. Lifting the quotient
coefficients with any fixed multiplicative norm excess greater than one
suggests a route to surjectivity and comparison of the quotient norms.
Coefficients which vanish in `A` lie in `I`; convergence of their
new-variable polynomial truncations suggests the reverse inclusion in
the closed evaluation kernel. These are proof obligations, not hypotheses
of the proposition or results established by this module. A proof may
instead follow another route.

This target fixes the displayed presentation of `A`. It does not formulate
independence from an arbitrary change of base presentation, additional
equation quotients, analytic atlases, analytic-domain representability,
structure sheaves or etale sites. These remain separate constructions.
