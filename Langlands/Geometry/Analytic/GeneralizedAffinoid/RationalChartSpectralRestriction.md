# Spectral restriction to an actual rational domain

Author: Worker 56.

This module constructs a continuous local action on the original quotient algebra
of a rational chart. The group topologies are the existing
`DomainAutomorphism.domainSpectralTopology`, generated by finite tests on actual
represented domains and quotient sections. A locus stabilizer has the subspace
topology from its ambient automorphism group. Continuity into this particular
local automorphism topology is the new conclusion.

The field is a complete ultrametric normed field. The presented affinoid algebra
and rational chart use arbitrary positive polyradii and the existing explicit
unit-ideal generation condition for the rational presentation. No nontrivial
valuation, reducedness, nonzero quotient or inhabited spectrum is required.

## Actual domain and action comparison

For `d : RationalChart K A`, `rationalSectionAction d` is the existing full
bounded section-action homomorphism `stabilizerSectionAction d.toDomain`, with
its target written explicitly as `Aut d.algebra`. This is an alias of the
constructed map, with no change of algebra or operator. The original domain
representation property constructs these automorphisms and their bounded
inverses.

Given `U : Domain K d.algebra`, the imported `d.extendDomain U` has exactly
`U.algebra` as its section algebra and the composite structural bounded map
`U.baseMap.comp d.baseMap`. Its locus is `d.spectrumMap '' U.locus` in the
original ambient spectrum.

`mem_locusStabilizer_extendDomain_iff` proves, for each
`g : locusStabilizer d.toDomain`,

```text
rationalSectionAction d g preserves U.locus
  iff
(g : Aut A) preserves (d.extendDomain U).locus.
```

The proof first compares the images of `U.locus` under the two spectrum actions.
The already proved equivariance of the actual rational structural spectrum map
identifies those images. Its proved injectivity then cancels the outer image in
the reverse implication. Thus this argument uses injectivity of the rational
chart spectrum map, without assuming injectivity for arbitrary represented
domains.

`sectionAction_extendDomain` compares the two resulting bounded automorphisms
on the unchanged section algebra. After taking their forward bounded maps, the
proof applies `boundedHom_ext U`. The action/structural-map identity for `U`
and the imported full restriction/action square for
`d.extendDomainInclusion U` identify their composites with `U.baseMap`.
Full-map uniqueness proves equality of the forward maps; categorical
isomorphism extensionality gives equality of the automorphisms, including their
inverses. This is an equality of the constructed bounded algebra maps, beyond
an equality of their spectrum operators.

## Exact section-test pullback

For any actual section `b : U.algebra`, real radius `r` and proof `hr : 0 ≤ r`,
`domainSpectralSubgroup_comap_sectionAction` proves

```text
(domainSpectralSubgroup U b r hr).comap (rationalSectionAction d)
  =
(domainSpectralSubgroup (d.extendDomain U) b r hr).comap
  (locusStabilizer d.toDomain).subtype.
```

Both sides are subgroups of the original outer rational locus stabilizer.
Unfolded test membership requires setwise stabilization and the uniform bound
`x (sigma(b) - b) ≤ r` at every point of the actual section algebra's Banach
spectrum. The locus comparison supplies precisely the corresponding
stabilization proof. Evaluating `sectionAction_extendDomain` at `b` and
subtracting `b` identifies the displacement elements themselves. Substitution
then transports every spectral inequality in both directions. The proof keeps
the same section, same radius and same bounded automorphisms.

`extendSpectralTest d t` bundles the extended represented domain with exactly
`t.element`, `t.radius` and `t.radius_pos`. For `ι : Type v`, independent of the
field universe `u`, and `[Finite ι]`,
`finiteDomainSpectralSubgroup_comap_sectionAction` identifies the comap of the
entire local family with the comap of the ambient family
`fun i => extendSpectralTest d (tests i)`. Comap distributes over the defining
infimum, and each factor is the single-test equality above. The identity
includes the empty finite family.

## Continuity and a bundled local action

`continuous_rationalSectionAction` proves continuity from the actual rational
locus stabilizer, with its ambient subspace topology, into
`domainSpectralTopology d.algebra`. The imported
`continuous_domainSpectralTopology_iff` reduces continuity to finite-test
identity neighborhoods. The exact family comap identity expresses each
preimage as the preimage of an ambient finite-test subgroup under the subtype
inclusion. That ambient subgroup is open by the existing finite-test topology
theorem, so its preimage is open and contains the identity. This supplies the
required neighborhood. The criterion uses small finite index types, while the
exact family identity above allows independent index universes.

`spectralStabilizerAction d` packages this homomorphism and continuity proof as
an existing `DomainSpectralAction`. It introduces no new action record or
competing automorphism topology.

Now let `G : Type v` be a topological group and let
`rho : DomainSpectralAction G A` be a given continuous ambient action.
`continuous_actionStabilizerHom` proves continuity of the existing
`rho.stabilizerHom d.toDomain`. Its underlying map is the ambient action
composed with the open stabilizer's subtype inclusion; the result lands in the
actual automorphism locus stabilizer. Continuity into that subtype follows from
continuity of the underlying ambient map. The open subgroup is a topological
group by its induced topology and original subtype homomorphism.

The main construction `restrictSpectralAction d rho` composes this continuous
homomorphism with `spectralStabilizerAction d`. Its group is exactly
`rho.locusOpenSubgroup d.toDomain` and its algebra is exactly `d.algebra`.
`restrictSpectralAction_toMonoidHom` proves definitional equality with the
previously constructed full homomorphism `rho.sectionAction d.toDomain`.
`restrictSpectralAction_baseMap` retains the original forward base-section law:

```text
localAction(g)(d.baseMap(a)) = d.baseMap(rho(g)(a)).
```

The group and field universes remain independent. The existing local spectral
continuity theorem concerns displacement and spectrum evaluation; the present
unit additionally proves continuity into the represented-domain automorphism
topology on the rational quotient itself.

## Scope

The exact single-test identity permits zero radius; the topology and bundled
tests use positive radii. Empty loci, empty spectra, zero quotients and empty
finite test families are included. The argument uses actual quotient sections
and makes no assumption equating quotient and spectral norms or making spectra
faithful on all algebra elements.

The local algebra is an actual rational chart algebra. No corresponding
continuity theorem for an arbitrary represented domain is asserted here.
General compact-domain cofinality, comparison with the full analytic
automorphism topology, analytic structure sheafness, general analytic atlases,
etale/quasi-etale sites, compact/distinguished lifting and the Harris tower
remain separate obligations of the broader project.
