# Gauss points of an actual relative Laurent annulus

This internal supporting construction gives an actual spectrum point over each
base point of a presented affinoid algebra. It also proves surjectivity of the
restriction map. It does not yet prove continuity of the constructed section.

Let `K : Type u` be an ultrametric normed field, let `A` be an actual
`PresentedAffinoidAlgebra K`, and let `t` be a positive real number. The
construction of the quotient and its coefficient maps does not require
completeness of `K`. Completeness is assumed for evaluation of the enlarged scalar
series and for the section and surjectivity results below. No radius independence,
nontrivial valuation, strict presentation, or properness of the ideal of `A` is
assumed.

The namespace is `Langlands.Analytic.RelativeLaurentAnnulus`.

`relation` is the equation `X_new,0 * X_new,1 - 1` in the scalar weighted Tate
algebra whose old polyradius is extended by the two radii `(t, t⁻¹)`.
`algebra K A t` uses the existing `Adjoin.algebra`: it is the actual quotient by
the closed ideal generated by the image of the old ideal and this new relation.
`baseMap` is the existing bounded structural map descending inclusion of the old
variables. Thus both the carrier and the quotient norm are the existing concrete
ones, rather than an abstract replacement.

For `x : BanachSpectrum A`, let `H(x)` be
`BanachSpectrum.CompletedResidueField K x`. The type `Fiber K A t x` is the
existing `CompletedGaussLaurent H(x) t`. Its normed ring, completeness and
ultrametric structure are those of that Laurent completion. The `K`-algebra
structure is explicitly given by the composite
`K → H(x) → CompletedGaussLaurent H(x) t`. The scalar norm estimate follows from
the isometric constant inclusion and the normed field algebra map.

`coefficientMap x` composes the actual completed-residue-field evaluation
`A → H(x)` with constant Laurent inclusion. The lemma `norm_coefficientMap`
proves the exact formula `‖coefficientMap x a‖ = x a`; its proof uses the norm
of completion inclusion and the published residue-field norm formula.
In particular the map is contractive.

`fiberTuple` consists of the Laurent variable and its inverse. Their published
coordinate norm bounds give the weighted monomial estimate for this tuple.
`coefficientEvaluation` evaluates relative weighted series over `A` using
`evaluateCoefficientsHom`, the contractive coefficient map and that monomial
estimate. Its values on constants and the two variables are proved, as is its
contractivity.

Assume now that `K` is complete. `seriesEvaluation` first applies the published
`Adjoin.relativeSeriesEvaluation`, which sends the old coordinates to their
quotient coefficients and the new coordinates to relative variables. It then
applies `coefficientEvaluation`. On the old variable block its value is the
coefficient map applied to the original presentation map. On the new variables
it has the values of `fiberTuple`. These formulas show that it kills both the
old ideal and the new inverse relation. Contractivity holds by composition.

`evaluation x` descends this map with `descendRelations`. Continuity annihilates
the closed generated ideal, as guaranteed by that published descent operation.
The formula `evaluation_baseMap` identifies its restriction to `A` with
`coefficientMap x`. The quotient norm estimate in `norm_descendRelations_le`
proves `norm_evaluation_le`, so the descended map is contractive for the actual
quotient norm.

`fiberPoint x` is the norm-valued point `CompletedGaussLaurent.gaussPoint`.
All-positive-radius multiplicativity is essential here: `t` need not be
independent over `H(x)`. Pullback along `evaluation x` defines `sectionPoint x`.
The implemented formula is

```text
sectionPoint x b = ‖evaluation x b‖.
```

The coefficient norm formula then proves
`sectionPoint x (baseMap a) = x a`.

`restriction` is the continuous spectrum pullback along `baseMap`.
`restriction_sectionPoint` proves the section identity by spectrum
extensionality and the preceding formula. `restriction_surjective` supplies
`sectionPoint x` as an explicit preimage of every `x`.

The construction is internal: its provenance is the composition of the published
actual residue maps, Laurent completion, relative series evaluation and closed
quotient descent. It asserts no separately cited numbered result. The Lean proof
follows those constructions directly. This module establishes a set-theoretic
section, not a continuous section. It does not establish scalar base change of
affinoid domains, strictification, finite rational pullback decomposition, or the
host finite ambient rational covering statement.
