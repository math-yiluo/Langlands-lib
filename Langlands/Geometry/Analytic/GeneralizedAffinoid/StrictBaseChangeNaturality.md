# Variable inclusions and coefficient descent

Author: Worker 101.

This internal construction proves naturality of the published explicit strict
base change and its bounded additive retraction for inclusions of variables.
It uses an isometric field homomorphism from K to L and elements realizing the
two positive polyradii whose entries agree on the included variables.
The fields have independent universes and need not be complete.

`strictRename` preserves coefficients and includes the variables along an
embedding. It is a bounded algebra map. `baseChange_rename` and
`descend_strictRename` are identities of actual restricted series: on exponents
supported on the embedded variables, the coefficient and its scaling product
agree; off that support both coefficients vanish.

For two actual affinoid presentations and an inclusion sending the first
ideal into the second, `renameHom` descends the original variable inclusion.
`strictBaseChangeRenameHom` descends the corresponding strict variable
inclusion. Its kernel contains the ideal generated by the base-changed old
relations, and continuity annihilates its closure. The representative formulas
then prove both the scalar-change square and the additive-retraction square.

This module is derived from the Library's `StrictBaseChange`,
`StrictBaseChangeRetraction` and weighted `Embedding` constructions, with no
new external mathematical assertion. It does not assert a general functor on
all bounded homomorphisms, flatness, or rational descent. Empty variable sets,
unit ideals and zero quotient rings are included.
