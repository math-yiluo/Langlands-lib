# Finite Banach algebras under ground field extension

Let `K → L` be an isometric extension of ultrametric normed fields with `L` complete. For a bounded map `f : A → B` of presented `K`-affinoid algebras, `relativeTensor` is the ordinary tensor product `A_L ⊗_A B`. The inclusion maps use the structural map `A → A_L` and the given `f`. The comparison `relativeTensorComparison` induces `A_L → B_L` on the left factor and the structural map `B → B_L` on the right factor.

When `f` is finite Banach, `relativeTensorEquiv` is the canonical ring equivalence defined by that comparison. A finite tuple of generators for `B` gives a surjective coefficient map from a finite free `A_L`-module to the ordinary tensor product. Its kernel is closed by `isClosed_submodule_pi` over the presented affinoid `A_L`. The quotient norm is complete and ultrametric. A common positive bound on coefficients for the products of the generators rescales it to a submultiplicative norm. The original bounded coefficient lifts give a bound on `B → A_L ⊗_A B`, and the finite coefficient sum bounds the comparison to `B_L`. The universal property of scalar extension then constructs the inverse. The two composites are checked on the tensor factors and the original `B` inside `B_L`.

`map_isFiniteBanach` uses the bounded inverse and quotient representatives to give a uniform norm bound for coefficient lifts in `B_L`, using the images of the original generators. This includes algebras of rank zero and trivially valued fields. `map_algebraicallyFiniteEtale` also preserves algebraic finite étaleness when the original finite Banach map has that property. The comparison intertwines the `A_L`-algebra structures, so algebraic étaleness follows from ordinary tensor base change.

The construction is the finite Banach coefficient-quotient argument applied over the extended base algebra. It supplies the affine algebra input for ground field extension of finite étale charts in Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces*, §1.4, printed p. 30 (PDF p. 27), and Corollary 3.3.8, printed p. 63 (PDF p. 60), *Publications mathématiques de l'IHÉS* 78 (1993), pp. 5–161.
