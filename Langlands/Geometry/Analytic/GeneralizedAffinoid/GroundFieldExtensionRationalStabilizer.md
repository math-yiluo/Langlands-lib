# Domain stabilizers under semilinear coefficient actions

An isometric coefficient automorphism acts on a scalar-extended affinoid
spectrum by inverse pullback on multiplicative seminorms. If a topological
group acts by coefficient automorphisms with norm-continuous scalar
orbits, the setwise stabilizer of every weighted rational locus is open.

On a rational locus the denominator has a positive lower bound. A
smaller displacement of its value preserves that value by the
ultrametric inequality. For each numerator, its displacement is bounded
using the denominator bound multiplied by the prescribed radius.
Continuity of the coefficient orbits gives these finitely many bounds for
both a group element and its inverse near the identity. The resulting
setwise stabilizer contains an identity neighborhood. A finite rational
cover gives the same result for every represented affinoid domain of the
extended algebra.

The stabilizers give the locus-preservation part of the analytic
continuity tests in V. G. Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic
analytic spaces*, unfinished manuscript (1995–1996), §1.1, printed/PDF
