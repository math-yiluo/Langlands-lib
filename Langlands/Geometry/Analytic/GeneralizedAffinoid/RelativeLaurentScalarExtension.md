# The actual relative Laurent annulus over its completed Laurent field

This internal construction identifies the actual relative Laurent annulus with
an actual weighted affinoid presentation over the completed Laurent field. All
objects remain at the original universe `u`.

Let `K` be a complete ultrametric normed field, let `A` be any presented affinoid
`K`-algebra, and let `t > 0`. The actual relative annulus `algebra K A t` is the
existing enlarged weighted Tate quotient, with the inverse equation imposed on
the two new coordinates.

## Completed Laurent constants

`norm_coordinateUnit_zpow_le` bounds every integer power of the actual annulus
unit by `t` to that power. For nonnegative exponents the proof uses
submultiplicativity and the bound on the first new coordinate; for negative
exponents it uses the second coordinate and its reciprocal-radius bound.
`completedScalarMap` then applies the existing completed Laurent evaluation to
obtain a contractive bounded `K`-algebra map from `CompletedGaussLaurent K t` to
the annulus. Its variable and inverse-variable values are the two actual new
coordinates. `transport_completedScalarMap` proves naturality under every
bounded map of the original affinoid algebras, by the completed Laurent
homomorphism uniqueness theorem. These constructions require only positivity of
`t`, without the independence condition below.

## An independent radius as a scalar field

The namespace `ScalarExtension` assumes

`Fact (∀ (a : K) (n : ℤ), n ≠ 0 → ‖a‖ ≠ t ^ n)`.

`independentNormedField` installs the existing field structure on the same
completed Laurent ring under exactly this condition. `annulusNormedAlgebra`
uses the actual completed scalar map as its scalar action. Contractivity and
submultiplicativity prove the normed-algebra estimate. `annulusScalarTower`
retains the original `K` action through the completed field.

`presentation K A t` has the same number of original coordinates and the same
original polyradius as `A`. Its ideal is the closed ideal generated by the
coefficientwise image of every element of `A.ideal` in the weighted Tate algebra
over the completed Laurent field. In particular the construction uses a genuine
closed quotient, with its quotient norm. The map `base` is obtained from the
bounded coefficient series map by annihilation of the original closed ideal.
It sends each original coordinate to the corresponding quotient coordinate.

## The two bounded comparison maps

`scalarInclusion` is the contractive structural scalar map from the completed
Laurent field to the extended quotient. Its variable and inverse variable have the required
annulus coordinate bounds and satisfy the inverse equation.
`toPresentation` therefore evaluates the actual annulus at `base` and this
inverse tuple, using the existing bounded annulus universal construction.
It sends the old coefficient algebra by `base` and the two new coordinates to
the specified completed scalars.

In the opposite direction, `annulusEvaluation` evaluates the weighted series
over the completed Laurent field at the original coordinates of `A` mapped to
the annulus. The bounded original structural map gives the required bounded
monomials, so no contraction of that structural map is assumed. After
restriction to `K`, the evaluation composed with coefficient extension equals
the original presentation map followed by the annulus structural map: bounded
weighted Tate maps are determined by coordinates. Hence it annihilates every
extended relation. `fromPresentationOverExtension` descends through the closed
relation ideal; `fromPresentation` retains its bounded `K`-algebra map.

The forward comparison preserves all completed Laurent scalars because the two
bounded maps on the completion agree on its variable. This gives the actual
completed-field-linear map `toPresentationOverExtension`.

`fromPresentation_toPresentation` checks the first composite on the old
coefficient algebra and both new annulus coordinates. The existing annulus
homomorphism uniqueness theorem makes it the identity.
`toPresentation_fromPresentation` checks the other composite on completed
scalar constants and the original coordinates, then uses uniqueness for bounded
relative series maps and surjectivity of the quotient presentation.

`boundedEquivOverBase` packages these inverse bounded maps over `K`.
`boundedEquiv` packages the same maps as an actual bounded algebra equivalence
over `CompletedGaussLaurent K t`. Neither equivalence asserts preservation of the
quotient norm, only the proved boundedness in both directions.

## Scope and provenance

The presentation permits arbitrary original positive real radii, arbitrary
closed ideals, zero and nonreduced quotients, and an empty coordinate tuple.
There is no assumption that `K` is nontrivially valued. The field comparison
requires precisely the displayed independent-radius condition. No strictness of
the extended presentation is asserted.

The provenance is internal: the construction composes the existing actual
completed Laurent evaluation, bounded weighted coefficient extension, closed
relation descent and uniqueness on constants and coordinates. It does not
supply a representation for a domain after changing its coefficient field, a
strict presentation, or an unconditional finite rational cover. The host
`Domain.FiniteRationalCoverStatement` remains a separate unfinished obligation.
