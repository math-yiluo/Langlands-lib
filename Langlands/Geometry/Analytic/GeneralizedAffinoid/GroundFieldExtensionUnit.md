# Identity extension of affinoid algebras

For a presented affinoid algebra \(A\) over a complete nonarchimedean
normed field \(K\), `unitEquiv` identifies \(A\) with the actual
ground-field extension \(A_K\) along the identity of \(K\).
Its forward map is the scalar-extension inclusion, and its inverse
is the bounded lift of the identity of \(A\). The composite on \(A\)
is the identity by the universal property. The other composite fixes
each presentation coordinate. Equality on those coordinates determines
a bounded homomorphism out of \(A_K\).

The induced spectrum homeomorphism is the original scalar-extension
projection. The inclusion formula for extended bounded maps gives
the natural isomorphism `unitNatIso` on presented affinoid algebras.

V. G. Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces*
(1993), §1.4, printed p. 30 (PDF p. 27), defines extension of the ground
field by the completed tensor product on affinoid charts. The identity
comparison uses its universal property in the presented-algebra model.
