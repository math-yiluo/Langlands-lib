# Closed finite free submodules over general affinoids

Worker 271 extends the existing strict scalar-extension descent argument from
ideals to submodules of finite free modules. The result
`PresentedAffinoidAlgebra.isClosed_submodule_pi` says that every
`A`-submodule of `Fin m → A`, for a presented positive-radius affinoid `A` over
any complete ultrametric normed field `K`, is closed in its original maximum
product norm. The rank may be zero, the affinoid may be the zero quotient,
and the original field may be trivially valued.

## Descent submodule

Fix the existing strict scalar extension `A → B` supplied by a norm-preserving
field extension and radius realizations. Its bounded additive retraction
`ρ : B → A` is `A`-linear over the embedding and retracts embedded elements
when the coefficient retraction sends `1` to `1`.

`strictBaseChangeDescentSubmodule S` consists of vectors `v : Fin m → B`
for which `(ρ (b * v j)) j` belongs to `S` for every `b : B`. Addition follows
from additivity of `ρ`, and closure under `B`-scalars follows by associativity.
For a coordinatewise embedded vector `v` from `S`, this retracted tuple is
`ρ b • v`, so it belongs to `S`. Therefore the `B`-span of the embedded `S`
is contained in this descent submodule.

Taking `b = 1` and using the retraction identity proves
`preimage_span_strictBaseChange`: the inverse image of that generated
submodule under the coordinatewise embedding is precisely `S`. The opposite
inclusion uses the generators of the span. No flatness assumption is needed.

## Closedness

Choose a finite radius realization for the presentation's radii together with
one extra radius equal to `2`. This gives a complete nontrivially normed
extension field even if `K` is trivially valued. The resulting strict
scalar-extension algebra is Noetherian and Banach. Apply the existing theorem
`isClosed_submodule_pi_of_noetherian_banach` to the submodule generated by the
embedded `S`. The coordinatewise embedding is continuous, using the existing
norm bound for `strictBaseChangeHom`. Pull back the closed generated submodule
and apply the contraction identity to obtain closedness of the original `S`.

## Provenance and scope

This is an internal derived construction. It follows the implemented ideal
descent in `GeneralizedAffinoid/Noetherian` and the bounded linear retraction
in `GeneralizedAffinoid/StrictBaseChangeRetraction`, using the existing
`FiniteRadiusRealization` and `ClosedSubmodulesBanach` theorems. No new claim
from external literature is used. This supplies closed coefficient kernels
for subsequent finite tensor comparison; it does not prove étale base change
or unrestricted pointwise lifting.
