# Norm of a coefficient automorphism

The coefficient automorphism of a scalar-extended affinoid presentation
preserves its quotient norm. To bound its operator norm, compose the
comparison into the completed tensor product with the bounded tensor
lift of the two legs. The original algebra leg is contractive, and
the coefficient leg is the composition of an isometric field map with
the contractive scalar inclusion. The tensor lift is contractive.
The comparison into the completed tensor is contractive by the weighted
quotient norm. Applying the resulting contraction to the inverse gives
the opposite inequality.

The construction uses the completed tensor description of scalar
extension in V. G. Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean
analytic spaces* (1993), §1.4, printed p. 30 (PDF p. 27). Literature ID
