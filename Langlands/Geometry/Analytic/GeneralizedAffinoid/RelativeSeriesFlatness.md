# Flatness of relative series and of one-variable rational quotients

This module supplies the algebraic input for the flatness of affinoid-domain
restrictions: relative weighted series over a presented affinoid algebra `A`
are flat over `A`, and so is the closed quotient of one-variable relative series
by a single rational equation `g X - f₀` whose coefficients generate the unit
ideal. The final theorem transports the second statement to the scalar rational
presentations used by the Library's rational charts.

The route is internal to this project. It replaces the usual argument through
strictly affinoid algebras (Berkovich, *Spectral theory and analytic geometry
over non-Archimedean fields*, Prop. 2.2.4, which relies on Bosch–Güntzer–Remmert
7.3.2 and a descent from `K_r`) by purely algebraic arguments plus one bounded
lifting lemma, so that no strictness or nontrivial valuation is needed on `K`.

## Declarations

All declarations are in namespace `Langlands.Analytic.PresentedAffinoidAlgebra`,
over a complete ultrametric normed field `K`.

- `exists_bounded_span_coefficients A n`: for a finite family `n : Fin q → Aᵏ`
  there is `C ≥ 0` such that every `v` in the `A`-span of the family is
  `∑ t, a t • n t` with `‖a t‖ ≤ C ‖v‖`.
- `exists_series_span_lift A s n x`: if, for each multi-index `d`, the vector of
  `d`-th coefficients of the relative series `x i` lies in the span of the
  family `n`, then `x i = ∑ t, n t i • y t` for relative series `y t`.
- `flat_generalizedTateAlgebra A s`: `Module.Flat A (GeneralizedTateAlgebra s A)`
  for every positive polyradius `s` on any index type.
- `eq_zero_of_rationalRecurrence`: in a Noetherian ring, if `a g + b f = 1`,
  `f c₀ = 0` and `g cₖ = f cₖ₊₁` for all `k`, then every `cₖ` vanishes.
- `isClosed_ideal_generalizedTateAlgebra A s J`: every ideal of a
  finite-variable relative series algebra is closed.
- `exists_series_ideal_lift A s f x`: a relative series whose coefficients all
  lie in the ideal generated by `f` is `∑ t, f t • z t`.
- `flat_quotient_rationalEquation A s g f₀ hgen`: if `(g, f₀) = A`, then the
  quotient of one-variable relative series by the principal ideal of
  `g X - f₀` is flat over `A`.
- `flat_relativeRationalAlgebra A s g f hgen`: the intrinsic relative rational
  quotient `RelativeRationalPresentation.Algebra K A s g f` with one fraction
  coordinate is flat over `A`.
- `flat_rationalPresentation_baseMap A s g f hgen`: the structural ring map of
  the scalar rational presentation `RationalPresentation.algebra K A s g f`
  with one fraction coordinate is flat.

## Proofs

**Bounded coefficients.** The Library realises `A` inside a strict base change
`A_L` over a nontrivially valued field `L` (`FiniteRadiusRealization`) with a
bounded coefficient retraction `ρ : A_L → A` that is `A`-linear and splits the
inclusion `φ`. Over `A_L` the span `N` of the transported family is closed in
`A_Lᵏ` (`isClosed_submodule_pi_of_noetherian_banach`), so the surjection
`A_L^q → N` given by the family is open by the Banach open mapping theorem
(`ContinuousLinearMap.exists_preimage_norm_le`). A preimage of `φ v` with
controlled norm, pushed back by `ρ`, gives the coefficients.

**Series lifting and flatness of relative series.** Applying the bounded
coefficients lemma degree by degree yields coefficient families `a d t` with
`‖a d t‖ ≤ C ∑ᵢ ‖coeff_d(x i)‖`; hence each `d ↦ a d t` is again weighted
restricted and defines a relative series `y t`, and comparing coefficients gives
`x i = ∑ t, n t i • y t`. Flatness follows from the equational criterion
(`Module.Flat.of_forall_isTrivialRelation`): for a relation `∑ fᵢ xᵢ = 0`, the
coefficient vectors lie in the kernel of `f : Aˡ → A`, which is finitely
generated since `A` is Noetherian; the lift writes `x` through generators of the
kernel.

**The recurrence lemma.** The submodule `M` spanned by all `cₖ` satisfies
`M ⊆ f M`, because `cₖ = f (a cₖ₊₁ + b cₖ)`. By Nakayama
(`Submodule.exists_sub_one_mem_and_smul_eq_zero_of_fg_of_le_smul`) there is `r`
with `r ≡ 1 mod f` killing `M`; so `f cₖ = 0` forces `cₖ = 0`, and induction
along the recurrence finishes.

**Closed ideals.** An ideal of `GeneralizedTateAlgebra s A` is the preimage of
its comap in the Library's presented affinoid `Adjoin.algebra K A s ∅` under the
bounded inverse of the relative series comparison; ideals of a presented
affinoid algebra are closed (`isClosed_ideal`).

**Flatness of the rational quotient.** Write `h = g X - f₀` and use the
equational criterion. Lift a relation `∑ fᵢ x̄ᵢ = 0` in the quotient to
`∑ fᵢ xᵢ = q h`. With `I = (f₁, …, f_l)`, all coefficients of `q h` lie in `I`.
Comparing coefficients of `q h = g X q - f₀ q` in degrees `0` and `k + 1` shows
that the classes `cₖ` of the coefficients of `q` in `A/I` satisfy the recurrence
of the previous lemma, so they vanish: all coefficients of `q` lie in `I`.
The ideal lift writes `q = ∑ fᵢ zᵢ`, so `∑ fᵢ (xᵢ - zᵢ h) = 0` in the flat module
of relative series; its trivialisation, projected to the quotient, trivialises
the original relation.

**Rational quotients.** For one fraction coordinate the generated ideal of the
rational equations is the principal ideal of `g X - f 0`, which is already
closed, so the intrinsic quotient is exactly the quotient treated above, and its
coefficient map is the algebra map. The scalar presentation's structural map is
the intrinsic one followed by the inverse of the Library comparison
`RationalPresentation.relativeEquiv` (`relativeEquiv_symm_baseMap`), and flatness
is preserved by composing with a bijective ring map.
