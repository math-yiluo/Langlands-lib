# Closed-equation quotients of relative weighted restricted series

Author: Worker 228. The ultrametric proof adapts the published argument for
`PresentedAffinoidAlgebra` in `GeneralizedAffinoid/Definitions` by Worker 56
and Worker 36. The construction follows the pattern of Worker 56's scalar
`PresentedAffinoidAlgebra.ofRelations` and `descendRelations` in
`GeneralizedAffinoid/Relations`. Bounded quotient descent is imported from
Worker 36's `BoundedQuotient`. Relative series, their `A`-algebra and normed
`K`-algebra structures and the bounded inclusion `constantAlgHom` are imported
from Worker 56's `GeneralizedTateAlgebra/RelativeAlgebra`.

## Data and hypotheses

Let `K` be a normed field with an ultrametric norm, and let
`A : PresentedAffinoidAlgebra K` be an actual weighted affinoid presentation.
Let `m` be a natural number and `s : PositivePolyradius (Fin m)` a family of
strictly positive real radii. Let `E` be any set of elements of
`GeneralizedTateAlgebra s A`, the published relative weighted restricted
series over `A` with the weighted Gauss norm `‖f‖ = sup_ν ‖a_ν‖ s^ν`.

All declarations are in the namespace
`Langlands.Analytic.PresentedAffinoidAlgebra.RelativeRelations`.

## The quotient and its structures

`ideal K A s E` is the topological closure `(Ideal.span E).closure` of the
ideal generated by `E`. It is closed by `isClosed_closure`
(`ideal_closed` and an `IsClosed` instance). No theorem that ideals are
automatically closed is used.

`QuotientAlgebra K A s E` is defined as Mathlib's actual ring quotient
`GeneralizedTateAlgebra s A ⧸ ideal K A s E`. Its instances are transported
unchanged from that quotient by `inferInstanceAs`:

- `NormedCommRing`: Mathlib's quotient norm by a closed ideal,
  `‖x‖ = inf {‖f‖ : f ↦ x}`;
- `NormedAlgebra K` (hence `Algebra K`): the quotient of the normed
  `K`-algebra structure from `RelativeAlgebra`;
- `Algebra A` and `IsScalarTower K A`: the quotient of the inherited
  coefficient algebra structure and scalar tower `K → A → series`.

`IsUltrametricDist` is proved directly. Given quotient elements `x` and `y`
and `ε > 0`, `Ideal.Quotient.norm_mk_lt` chooses representatives `a` and `b`
with `‖a‖ < ‖x‖ + ε` and `‖b‖ < ‖y‖ + ε`. Then

\[
\|x+y\| \le \|a+b\| \le \max(\|a\|,\|b\|) \le \max(\|x\|,\|y\|) + \varepsilon,
\]

using the contraction of the quotient map and ultrametricity of relative
series. Letting `ε` tend to zero gives the ultrametric inequality.

Only `instCompleteSpace` assumes `[CompleteSpace K]`. Then `A` is complete by
the published `PresentedAffinoidAlgebra.instCompleteSpace`, relative series
over `A` are complete by the published
`GeneralizedTateAlgebra.instCompleteSpace`, and Mathlib's
`Submodule.Quotient.completeSpace`, applied to the ideal as a submodule,
gives completeness of the quotient. No other declaration assumes completeness of `K`.

## Quotient map, coefficient map and new coordinates

`quotientMap K A s E` is the published `BoundedAlgHom.quotientMap` for the
closed ideal: the bounded `K`-algebra map `f ↦ Ideal.Quotient.mk _ f`
(`quotientMap_apply`). The following facts are proved:

- `quotientMap_surjective`: it is surjective (`Ideal.Quotient.mk_surjective`);
- `quotientMap_eq_zero_iff` and `ker_quotientMap`: `f` maps to zero exactly
  when `f ∈ (Ideal.span E).closure`, and the kernel equals this displayed
  ideal (`Ideal.Quotient.eq_zero_iff_mem`, `Ideal.Quotient.mkₐ_ker`);
- `equation_mem` and `quotientMap_equation`: each `f ∈ E` lies in the span,
  hence in its closure, so its image is zero;
- `norm_quotientMap_le`: `‖quotientMap f‖ ≤ ‖f‖` (`Ideal.Quotient.norm_mk_le`);
- `exists_representative_norm_lt`: for each quotient element `a` and each
  `ε > 0` there is `f` with `quotientMap f = a` and `‖f‖ < ‖a‖ + ε`
  (`Ideal.Quotient.norm_mk_lt`). This expresses that the norm is the
  quotient norm.

`baseMap K A s E` is the composite of the isometric bounded constant-series
inclusion `constantAlgHom s K A : A → GeneralizedTateAlgebra s A` with the
quotient map. `baseMap_eq_algebraMap` shows, definitionally, that it is the
algebra map of the quotient's `A`-algebra structure. `norm_baseMap_le` proves
`‖baseMap a‖ ≤ ‖a‖` from contraction and `norm_constantAlgHom`. The base map
is not claimed to be injective or isometric; for general `E` it is neither.

`newCoordinate K A s E j` is the image of the series variable `X s A j`.
`norm_newCoordinate_le` proves `‖newCoordinate j‖ ≤ s j`. The published
`norm_X` gives `‖X s A j‖ = ‖(1 : A)‖ * s j`, and the published
`PresentedAffinoidAlgebra.norm_one_le` gives `‖(1 : A)‖ ≤ 1`; contraction
then gives the bound. The unit of `A` may have norm zero when `A` is the zero
quotient.

## Bounded descent

Let `B` be a normed commutative `K`-algebra in an independent universe. It need
not be complete or ultrametric. Let
`φ : BoundedAlgHom K (GeneralizedTateAlgebra s A) B` satisfy `φ f = 0` for
every `f ∈ E`.

- `annihilates_ideal`: `φ` vanishes on `(Ideal.span E).closure`. The
  kernel of `φ` is an ideal containing `E`, so it contains the span
  (`Ideal.span_le`). The published `BoundedAlgHom.annihilates_closure` then
  uses continuity of `φ` to extend vanishing to the closure.
- `descend K A s E B φ hE` is the published `BoundedAlgHom.liftQuotient` for
  the closed ideal and this vanishing.
- `descend_quotientMap` (definitional) and `descend_eq_of_quotientMap_eq` give
  the representative formula: if `quotientMap f = a`, then
  `descend φ a = φ f`. `descend_comp_quotientMap` states this as an equality
  of bounded maps. `descend_baseMap` and `descend_newCoordinate` record the
  values on coefficients and new coordinates.
- `hom_ext` uses the published `BoundedAlgHom.quotient_hom_ext`, based on
  surjectivity. Two bounded maps out of the quotient agree if they agree after
  the quotient map. `eq_descend` gives uniqueness: any bounded `ψ` with
  `ψ.comp quotientMap = φ` equals the descent.
- `norm_descend_le`: if `C > 0` and `‖φ f‖ ≤ C * ‖f‖` for all `f`, then
  `‖descend φ a‖ ≤ C * ‖a‖` for the actual quotient norm. This is the
  published `BoundedAlgHom.quotient_norm_bound`, proved with representatives
  of arbitrarily small norm excess.
- `homEquiv` is an equivalence between `BoundedAlgHom K (QuotientAlgebra K A s E) B`
  and the subtype of bounded relative series maps annihilating `E`. Its
  forward map is composition with `quotientMap`, which annihilates `E` by
  `quotientMap_equation`. Its inverse is `descend`. The left inverse law
  follows from `hom_ext`, and the right inverse law is pointwise definitional.
  `homEquiv_apply_coe` and `homEquiv_symm_apply` record both directions.

The descent starts from a given bounded map on relative series; it does not
construct one from coefficient and coordinate images.

## Scope

The constructions allow `m = 0`, presentations of `A` with no variables,
arbitrary positive real radii without value-group conditions, trivial
valuations on `K`, arbitrary `E` (including `E` generating the unit ideal),
the zero quotient and the zero target. The construction, norm estimates and
descent do not assume completeness of `K`.
This module defines no new `PresentedAffinoidAlgebra K` structure. It also
makes no claim about comparison with `Adjoin.algebra K A s L` for scalar
enlarged-series equations, presentation independence, coefficient lifting,
analytic domains, structure sheaves or étale sites.

## Source conventions and derived content

The original series convention is Berkovich, *Non-Archimedean analytic
spaces*, Trieste lectures (2009), Definition 2.1.6, printed/PDF page 8, read
directly. Part (i) defines `A{r₁⁻¹T₁, …, rₙ⁻¹Tₙ}` for a `k`-affinoid algebra
`A` as the power series `∑ a_ν T^ν` over `A` with `‖a_ν‖ r^ν → 0`, with the
norm `max_ν ‖a_ν‖ r^ν`. Part (ii) defines an `A`-affinoid algebra as a
commutative Banach `A`-algebra admitting an admissible epimorphism from such
a series algebra. The relative series here are the published
`GeneralizedTateAlgebra s A` with this weighted norm. Decay is along the
cofinite filter of exponents, which agrees with decay as `|ν| → ∞` in finitely
many variables.

The quotient construction is internal derived infrastructure. It takes the
closure of the ideal generated by an arbitrary set of equations and puts on
the quotient the actual quotient norm. It proves the normed-algebra
structures, ultrametricity, the norm estimates and the bounded descent from
Mathlib's quotient-norm API and the published `BoundedQuotient` results. These
are not quoted theorems of the source. The source works over complete fields
and with admissible epimorphisms up to equivalent Banach norms. Here the
quotient norm is fixed, the normed-algebra part allows incomplete `K`, and
no claim is made that the quotient is `A`-affinoid or `k`-affinoid in the
sense of part (ii) or its exercise.
