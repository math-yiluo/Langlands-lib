# Semilinear operators on weighted rational section algebras

Consider a weighted rational chart of a scalar-extended affinoid
presentation. On the subgroup where a coefficient automorphism and its
inverse admit contractive lifts to the rational quotient, these lifts
are unique. Their composites are the lifts of the composites on the
base. The inverse lift gives an inverse on the quotient, so these
operators are isometries for the actual quotient norm.
They restrict on scalars to the given coefficient automorphisms.
Bounded restrictions between rational domains commute with these
operators on their common subgroup by the same uniqueness.

For a topological group acting with norm-continuous scalar orbits,
this subgroup pulls back to an open subgroup. The image of the
denominator is a unit near the identity. The new fraction images
are then continuous at the identity. A sufficiently small displacement
keeps each fraction within its prescribed radius by the ultrametric
inequality. The bounded rational evaluation therefore supplies a
contractive lift. Applying the same argument to inverse parameters
shows openness.

The base sections and fraction coordinates have continuous norm orbits
on this open subgroup. Finite polynomials in these coordinates therefore
have continuous norm orbits. Their images are dense in the quotient,
and the common isometry bound gives continuity for every section.

These are local section operators used in the analytic continuity tests
of V. G. Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces*,
unfinished manuscript (1995–1996), §1.1, printed/PDF pp. 1–2.
