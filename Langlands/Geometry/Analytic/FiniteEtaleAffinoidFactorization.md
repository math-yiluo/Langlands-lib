# Affinoid models of intermediate finite étale maps

Work over a complete ultrametric normed field, with independent field and
space universes, and assume the étale Hom sheaf property. Consider a finite
Banach finite étale map of small affinoid spectra that factors through an
étale surjection onto a Hausdorff space.

The kernel pair of the surjection is compact Hausdorff and maps by an
étale monomorphism to the affinoid kernel pair of the composite. The image
is clopen, so the idempotent chart realizes the comparison. Its bounded
restriction is finite Banach and algebraically finite étale. The original
affinoid kernel pair has two finite Banach finite étale projections,
related by interchanging the factors. Composing them with the chart
restriction gives two coefficient maps that generate the relation algebra.

The ordinary ring relation yields the finite étale equalizer quotient.
Its cover induces the same kernel pair in the localized category and is
étale and surjective. Étale Hom descent identifies its target with the
intermediate space. Uniqueness on the common cover checks compatibility
with the original affinoid base. The model records this isomorphism and
the finite Banach and algebraic finite étale data over the base.
