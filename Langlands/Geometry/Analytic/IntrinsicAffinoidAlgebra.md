# Intrinsic affinoidness and constructed closed-kernel presentations

This module expresses affinoidness by the original admissible Tate-quotient criterion and constructs an actual finite closed weighted Tate presentation from it. The output presentation and bounded inverse are constructed; they are not fields of a supplied recognition record.

The source is Berkovich, *Spectral Theory and Analytic Geometry over Non-Archimedean Fields* (1990), Definition 2.1.1, printed p.21 / PDF p.35: a commutative Banach algebra over the specified nonarchimedean field is affinoid over that field when there exists an admissible epimorphism from a finite positive-radius Tate algebra. The meaning of admissibility is §1.1, printed p.11 / PDF p.25, equivalence of the quotient seminorm with the image seminorm. The registered source is `provided-a03a0182668f6b92b01deacac09a9992b3edf55e23a0a2194554384ae44bd8d4`, PDF SHA256 `9b543e7ffae8ec6febf154a60c4d11a288f7ecc3795c66f004ba3200a6824e79`.

`IsAffinoidAlgebra K E` is the source criterion over the fixed coefficient field `K`; it does not vary the coefficient field. The field is complete, normed and ultrametric, and the target is a complete ultrametric normed commutative coefficient algebra. Coefficient and target universes are independent. As elsewhere in the project, zero algebras are allowed as degenerate empty affinoids; normalization of the target's unit norm and a nontrivial coefficient valuation are not assumed.

The generic prerequisite `BoundedAlgHom.kernelQuotientEquiv` applies to any admissible bounded epimorphism of normed commutative coefficient algebras, without completeness or ultrametric hypotheses. The kernel is closed by continuity. Mathlib's algebraic first isomorphism theorem provides the actual algebra equivalence from the kernel quotient. The forward norm bound follows from the original map's bound by the already proved quotient-bound theorem. For the inverse, the admissibility lifting criterion supplies a preimage of every target element with a fixed norm bound. Its quotient class is the inverse image, and the quotient norm is at most the representative norm. Thus both directions are bounded without an open mapping theorem.

`kernelQuotientEquiv_mk` and `kernelQuotientEquiv_comp_quotientMap` recover the original map on every element and as an entire bounded algebra map. `BoundedAlgEquiv.isAdmissibleEpi` uses the actual bounded inverse as a section, supplying the needed admissibility without a bare-surjectivity inference.

For an intrinsic affinoid witness, `arity`, `polyradius` and `quotientMap` choose the finite admissible Tate quotient already asserted by the source criterion. `presentation` uses that map's kernel as its ideal and the proved kernel closedness as its closed-ideal proof. `presentationEquiv` constructs the actual bounded equivalence with the unchanged target algebra by the generic kernel-quotient result. `presentationEquiv_presentationMap` proves recovery of the entire original Tate quotient on each series.

`of_boundedEquiv` proves that intrinsic affinoidness is preserved by every bounded algebra equivalence between the displayed Banach algebras, by composing the original Tate quotient and the proved admissible equivalence. Finally, every existing `PresentedAffinoidAlgebra` satisfies the intrinsic criterion by its actual presentation map and its previously proved admissibility.

These are the source affinoid criterion and derived closed-kernel constructions. They do not prove that an arbitrary special-subset section equalizer is affinoid, assert finite-union recognition, assume the unproved special-set intersection theorem, or construct a maximal atlas from assumed output presentations.
