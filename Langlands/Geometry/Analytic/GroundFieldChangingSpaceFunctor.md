# The underlying space of an analytic arrow with varying fields

The chart projections glue to the continuous projection from a
scalar-extended analytic space to its original space. The projection is
natural for localized analytic maps. The projection along the identity
embedding is the underlying map of the analytic unit comparison. For
successive extensions, their projections compose to the direct projection
after the tower comparison, by the corresponding equation on each chart.

The underlying continuous map of an arrow \(Y\to X_L\) over \(L\) is its
underlying map followed by the projection \(X_L\to X\). The unit equation
gives preservation of identities. Naturality of the projections and the
tower equation give preservation of composition.

V. G. Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces*
(1993), §1.4, printed p. 30 (PDF p. 27), constructs the continuous
projection of scalar extension and uses the corresponding analytic maps
for changes of ground field. Literature ID `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`,
