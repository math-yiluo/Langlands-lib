# Equivariant quasi-étale base change as a morphism of compact-probe sites

`EquivariantQuasiEtaleBaseChangeMorphismStatement` concerns a morphism \(f:Y\to
X\) of Hausdorff analytic spaces with operators over a common complete
nonarchimedean normed field. Its pullback functor restricts an action along the
continuous group homomorphism of \(f\) and then takes the analytic pullback
with the diagonal action. The proposition asks for representable flatness and
continuity of this functor for the equivariant compact-probe quasi-étale
topologies on \(X\) and \(Y\). The ordinary Hausdorff covering comparison
relates the compact-probe generators to the chart-neighbourhood test used to
express the quasi-étale coverings.
