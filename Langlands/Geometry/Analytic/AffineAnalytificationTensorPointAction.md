# Scalar automorphisms on affine base change points

Let \(k\) be a field, \(K\) a complete nontrivially normed nonarchimedean extension of \(k\), and \(A\) a commutative \(k\)-algebra. Fix a finite polynomial presentation over \(K\) of \(K\otimes_k A\). A \(k\)-automorphism of \(K\) acts on the first tensor factor. If the automorphism preserves the norm, it induces the continuous semilinear point map of the affine analytification by precomposing each multiplicative seminorm with this tensor ring map.

`tensorCoefficientPointEvaluation` gives joint continuity in a parameter of isometric automorphisms and the affine point, assuming every scalar orbit is norm-continuous. For each fixed element of the tensor ring, choose a finite sum of simple tensors. Each coefficient then varies continuously and the elements from the original algebra stay fixed. The finite-coefficient seminorm evaluation proves joint continuity through the defining evaluation topology.

The construction uses the affine seminorm description in Berkovich, *Spectral theory and analytic geometry over non-Archimedean fields* (1990), Remark 3.4.2, pp. 63–64, and the semilinear convention in *Étale equivariant sheaves on \(p\)-adic analytic spaces* (1996), §1.1.1(i), p. 2. Joint continuity follows from the evaluation argument above.
