# Finiteness from bounded monic equations on presentation coordinates

This internal lemma gives a finite-module criterion for an actual bounded
map of presented affinoid algebras. It uses the published contractive
polynomial division lemma and expansion of restricted series into weighted
monomials.

Use a scalar of each coordinate radius to normalize the coordinates. Reduce
every power of a normalized coordinate by its monic polynomial. The remainders
have coefficients in the unit ball and exponents below the degree of the
corresponding polynomial. Multiplying these expressions gives a uniformly
bounded choice of coefficients in a fixed finite tuple of monomials. After
restoring the scalar weights, the coefficients of a restricted series give
summable coefficient tuples in the complete base algebra. The bounded
coefficient map carries their sum to the desired quotient element.
