# Full field points of quasi-étale pullbacks

The field-point functor preserves the pullback defining an inverse-image
analytic domain. A compatible pair determines a point of the literal
inverse-image subset, where the full field point restricts. Injectivity
along the target domain inclusion gives the required second projection.

For a map with a domain–étale factorization, first pull back the étale
envelope. The Library's full field-point theorem and the domain case give
preservation by pasting. The result transfers to any isomorphic
pullback cone.

A quasi-étale map has a chart with a domain–étale factorization through
the ordinary point of each first-factor field point. Restrict to this
chart and apply the factored case. Any other lift restricts to the same
chart by the inverse-image square and hence agrees with the constructed
lift. This is a derived consequence of the published étale theorem and
the Library's domain and quasi-étale interfaces.
