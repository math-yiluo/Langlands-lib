# Retained scalar-extended charts in the ambient index universe

Raise the original chart index type of the scalar-extended presentation by the canonical universe-lift equivalence. The relabelled presentation has the same ambient space, chart spectra and bounded restrictions. Its complete maps retain the corresponding entire bounded pullbacks. Admissibility is equivalent to admissibility of the original pair of charts by the scalar-extension projection.

The retained maps compose. Their continuous maps are the scalar-extended maps of the original composite. On a composable chart chain, the bounded pullbacks agree by the composition law before relabelling. The proof uses complete strong-map extensionality.
