# Full strong comparison with the rational-cut atlas

Let `K : Type u` be a complete ultrametric normed field, let `X : Type v` be a topological space, and let `a : IndexedAffinoidAtlas.{u,v,w} K X` be an actual supplied affinoid atlas. Fix an original index `t` and a proof `ht` that every original chart range is contained in the range of `a.chart t`. This module constructs full strong maps in both directions between that atlas and `RationalCutAtlas.atlas a t ht`, and proves that both are quasi-isomorphisms. Both continuous maps are the identity on `X`. The greatest-chart hypothesis is retained throughout.

The rational-cut atlas has literal chart loci of the form
`range (a.chart i) ∩ a.chart t '' d.locus`, where `d` is a positive-radius rational presentation over the greatest-chart algebra. Its algebras are the actual rational pullback quotients constructed in `RationalCutAtlas`. This module uses their proved full domain representations; it assumes no comparison map or new representation property.

The reusable lemma `PresentedAffinoidAlgebra.Domain.Inclusion.represents_of_injective` applies to an actual inclusion `f : U ⟶ V` of full domains over one presented affinoid algebra. If `V.spectrumMap` is injective, then `f.restriction` represents the relative locus `V.spectrumMap ⁻¹' U.locus` against every presented affinoid test, with arbitrary finite positive polyradius. The proof uses the equality `f.restriction.comp V.baseMap = U.baseMap` and the existing theorem `Represents.of_comp_of_injective`. Thus it descends the entire bounded-map factorization and uniqueness property, together with closedness and the precise spectrum image.

All local maps are constructed over the common base `a.algebra t`. `originalInclusion` packages an old atlas restriction as an actual inclusion between the corresponding original restriction domains over that base. `fromOriginalInclusion` maps an old chart domain to a containing cut domain; `toOriginalInclusion` maps a cut domain to a containing old chart domain. In both cases ambient containment implies common-base locus containment, because the cut domain locus is the preimage of its literal subset of `X` under `a.chart t`. `Domain.inclusionOfSubset` then constructs the bounded restriction from the containing domain's algebra to the contained domain's algebra using the full universal property.

`fromOriginalInclusion_naturality` and `toOriginalInclusion_naturality` prove compatibility in both chart variables. Each side is the restriction of a composite arrow between the same full domains over `a.algebra t`. Those arrows are equal by full representation uniqueness, through the proved subsingleton instance for `Domain.Inclusion`. This establishes equality of entire bounded algebra maps, including algebraic information that their spectrum maps alone might not detect.

The original index universe `w` and the ambient universe `v` may differ. `originalPresentation` reindexes the original atlas by `ULift.{v} a.Index`, while `presentation` reindexes the cut atlas by `ULift.{w} (Index a t)`. Both are presentations with index universe `max v w`. The published reindexing construction preserves the actual points, algebras, charts and restrictions; these universe lifts impose no equality of the original universes.

`fromOriginal` and `toOriginal` are `StrongHom`s between those presentations. Every admissible pair uses the corresponding common-base inclusion restriction. The chart equations follow from its equality over the base and the original atlas's restriction equation. Compatibility is the naturality proved above. For `fromOriginal`, an original chart can use the cut with the same locus as its target. For `toOriginal`, every cut can use the original greatest chart, whose surjectivity was proved in `RationalCutAtlas`. The base-map lemmas record that both continuous maps are exactly the identity.

`fromOriginalInclusion_original` and `toOriginalInclusion_original` identify the two comparison restrictions at a retained original locus with the inverse and forward maps, respectively, of `originalAlgebraEquiv`. `fromOriginal_pullback_original` and `toOriginal_pullback_original` give the same identities for the full strong maps. The proofs compare bounded maps over the common base using `Domain.boundedHom_eq_of_over_base`; the equivalence itself was constructed from the equal-locus full representations. `originalAlgebraEquiv_naturality` proves
`r_cut(i,j) ∘ e_j = e_i ∘ r_old(i,j)`
for every old containment, where `e_i` is the forward bounded equivalence and composition is composition of bounded algebra maps. Both sides agree after composition with the structural restriction from `a.algebra t`, by the base-map equations and old restriction composition; full uniqueness then identifies them.

`isQuasiIso_fromOriginal` and `isQuasiIso_toOriginal` prove the existing `StrongHom.IsQuasiIso` predicate, rather than only the homeomorphism condition. The ambient identity is a homeomorphism. For each admissible pair, `represents_of_injective` proves the full relative representation. The cut structural spectrum map is embedded by the rational-cut construction, and the old restriction spectrum map is embedded by the original chart equation. In the reverse direction the original restriction equation also identifies the common-base preimage with the exact chart-image locus required by `IsQuasiIso`. Both results quantify over every admissible chart pair and every presented affinoid test.

The source supplies the meanings of the atlas, strong-map and quasi-isomorphism conditions. The module does not prove unrestricted atlas saturation, extend arbitrary strong maps to saturated atlases, or construct unrestricted strong-map composition. It does not require those results, general Tate gluing, or any assumed output algebra maps.
