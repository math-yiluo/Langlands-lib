# Scalar extension of Hausdorff analytic spaces

An extended affinoid chart range is the inverse image of its original chart
range under the scalar-extension projection. If the original space is
Hausdorff, the original chart range is compact and closed. Each extended chart
range is therefore closed, and its intersection with any other extended chart
range is compact. The compact-intersection criterion for the extended atlas
then gives Hausdorffness.

`localizedHausdorffFunctor` restricts the localized scalar-extension functor
to the full subcategories of spaces with Hausdorff underlying topology.

V. G. Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces*
(1993), Lemma 1.1.1(ii), printed pp. 13–14 (PDF pp. 10–11), gives the
criterion for a quasinet of compact Hausdorff subsets. Section 1.4,
printed p. 30 (PDF p. 27), constructs the ground-field extension and its
projection. Literature ID `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA-256
