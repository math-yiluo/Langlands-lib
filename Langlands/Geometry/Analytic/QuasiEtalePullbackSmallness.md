# Smallness from domain–étale charts

`small_recognition_fibreProductSpace_of_domainEtaleCharts` bounds
the space of the recognized glued pullback in the original space
universe. It assumes that the first map has a domain–étale chart
through every point of its source.

For a point of the glued product, choose such a chart at its first
projection and realize its factorization on a common strong source.
The étale envelope supplies a finite Banach chart pair with the
second factor after full recognition. Choose a chart of the common
source that maps into the chosen envelope chart and into a chart
of the recognized first factor. Its images in both charts are full
domains, by composition of the actual bounded domain representations.
The original chart of the first factor realizing its image has a
bounded algebra equivalence with the common source chart. The
strong factorization gives equality of the two actual bounded maps
to that algebra. Transport along the bounded equivalence expresses
the first leg of the pair pushout as restriction to a domain of the
finite Banach envelope.

The spectrum of the restricted pushout is small by the finite Banach
bound and affinoid domain embedding. Changing the common base chart
retains smallness by the bounded pushout equivalence. The small
pair spectra cover the actual glued space and give a surjection
from their small dependent sum.

The provenance is internal. The argument uses the published
recognition and finite Banach constructions and the full bounded
domain representations of the strong refinement.
