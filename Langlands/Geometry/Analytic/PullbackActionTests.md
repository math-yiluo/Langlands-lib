# Invariance of pullback chart patches

An equivariant arrow to a space with a continuous analytic action pulls
each target chart back to a subset preserved by all group elements
sufficiently near the identity. The near-identity test with function zero
and bound one supplies preimage equality on the target. Equivariance
identifies the source action's preimage of the pulled-back subset with
the pullback of the target action's preimage.

For two such projections, intersection preserves preimage equality on
the intersection of the two identity neighbourhoods. This internal
lemma supplies the preservation of chart patches used in pullback
action tests. It applies to equivariant arrows in the localized category.
