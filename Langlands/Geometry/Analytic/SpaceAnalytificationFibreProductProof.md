# Analytification preserves fibre products of algebraic spaces

This proves the published `SpaceAnalytificationFibreProductStatement` in
every universe. Work over the given complete, ultrametric, nontrivially
normed field. The algebraic pullback is separated because the diagonal
of a presheaf pullback inherits the closed diagonal property of the
factors. The fibre product of the given locally finite type atlases is
locally of finite type, by the closed diagonal of the base space.
These properties transfer to the specified algebraic pullback.

The four compatibility identities give commutativity after precomposition
by each étale affine test of the pullback. Joint surjectivity of those
tests and étale uniqueness give commutativity of the analytic square.

For joint monicity, take two maps into the analytification of the
algebraic pullback with the same projections. A common chart cover on
an isomorphic source expresses both restrictions as affinoid-algebra
points. Compatibility and injectivity identify their projected points.
The algebraic pullback identifies the points, and chart separation
identifies the two maps.

For a cone over the analytic square, take a common chart cover for its
two legs. On each chart, the two ring points have equal images in the
algebraic base by the injective affinoid point comparison. The algebraic
pullback gives a ring point on that chart, whose analytic map lifts both
legs. The two lifts on an overlap agree by joint monicity. Original
domain gluing supplies a global lift on the chosen source, which
transports along its isomorphism to the cone source. These lifts and
joint monicity give the categorical pullback.
