# Subordinate original-chart covers inside Hausdorff finite unions

This module constructs finite subordinate original-chart covers and the resulting bounded restrictions from a finite-chart section equalizer. An original parent chart need only be contained in the literal union, and that union must be Hausdorff. No chart representing each cross intersection is required.

The source motivation is Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §1.1, printed pp.13–14 / PDF pp.10–11, especially the net definition and Lemma 1.1.2(ii), and §1.2, printed pp.20–21 / PDF pp.17–18, especially the paragraph after Lemma 1.2.12 and Proposition 1.2.13(ii). The registered source is `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`.

This is a derived construction with an explicit Hausdorff hypothesis on the containing finite union. It does not assert the unrestricted ambient-space version of Lemma 1.1.2(ii). The source's special sets are compact with its convention that compact spaces are Hausdorff. General specialness, intersection representation, affinoid recognition and maximal-atlas extension remain separate obligations. Neither global Hausdorffness of the ambient space nor reducedness or a nontrivial valuation is assumed. Coefficient, point and chart-index universes remain independent.

`subordinateCoverOfCompactIntersections` first handles the more general input of compact cross intersections. For each member of the defining finite family, the original net supplies a finite cover of its intersection with the parent by original charts contained in both. This uses `compactIntersectionChartCover` and the proved compact-subcover theorem for relative quasinets. The dependent disjoint union of these finite families is reindexed by a finite ordinal. Every point of the parent lies in a selected member, and hence in one chart of that member's intersection cover. The resulting `SubordinateChartCover` contains only chart indices, assignments, containments and coverage.

`isCompact_inter_member` proves the required compactness when the containing union is Hausdorff. Both original chart ranges are compact. Regarding them as subsets of the union preserves compactness because they are contained in that subspace. Their intersection is compact in the Hausdorff subspace, and its image under the continuous subtype inclusion is precisely the original ambient intersection. This argument does not extract a finite subcover from an arbitrary compact cover.

`subordinateCoverOfHausdorff` applies the first construction using these compact intersections. `originalRestriction` then applies the established full-domain bounded descent to this constructed finite cover, yielding an actual bounded homomorphism from the section equalizer to the unchanged original parent algebra. No output homomorphism or affinoid presentation is an input.

`original_comp_originalRestriction` records full bounded-map recovery on any original chart contained in both the parent and a defining member. `originalRestriction_naturality` proves compatibility with original-chart inclusions. Both reuse the full-map recovery and naturality of the previously constructed subordinate-cover descent; they do not infer algebra-map equality from spectrum points.
