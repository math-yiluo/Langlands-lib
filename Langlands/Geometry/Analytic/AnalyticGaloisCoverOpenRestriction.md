# Open restriction of analytic Galois coverings

An analytic Galois covering restricts over any open of its base. The source is the inverse image open domain, and its deck group acts by restricting each original automorphism to that domain. The open restriction of the original self-pullback, taken over the first projection, is the new self-pullback by pasting the two pullback squares and cancelling the base-change square. The second projection lands in the restricted source because the original projections have the same composite to the base.

Each new deck graph composed with the relation inclusion agrees with the original graph restricted to the new source, by the pullback property. This identity carries disjointness to the new graphs. To prove graph coverage, lift a point of the restricted relation to an original graph, then use the first projection to see that its chosen preimage lies in the restricted source.

This is an analytic open-restriction consequence of the graph presentation. It implements the shrinking of finite Galois covers used in Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces*, proof of Proposition 1.5.1, step (1′), printed p. 11.
