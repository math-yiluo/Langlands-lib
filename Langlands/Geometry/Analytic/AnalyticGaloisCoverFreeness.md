# Freeness of analytic Galois deck actions

For an analytic Galois covering over a complete ultrametric normed field, a deck element that fixes a full point with values in any normed extension is the identity. A deck element that fixes an analytic arrow by postcomposition is also the identity if the source of the arrow has a point.

An isometric enlargement of the value field acts on full points by postcomposition on each chart evaluation. This commutes with the pushforward along strong maps because the enlargement leaves the underlying point unchanged and composes with the chart pullbacks. The natural transformation extends through the localization. To compare the two deck graphs of a fixed full point, enlarge its value field to a complete algebraically closed one. Preservation of the self-pullback by the full point functor over that field identifies the two graph points because their two projections agree. Their common underlying point belongs to both graph images, so disjointness forces the deck element to be the identity.

For a fixed analytic arrow, the two graph composites already agree by the analytic pullback property. Evaluating that equality at a source point again gives a point in the intersection of the two graph images.

This is a derived consequence of the analytic deck-graph presentation, applied to the finite Galois coverings in step (1′) of the proof of Proposition 1.5.1 in Berkovich's *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces*, printed p. 11. The graph construction is the analytic counterpart of Milne's *Étale Cohomology*, Chapter III, §2, Example 2.6, pp. 99–100.
