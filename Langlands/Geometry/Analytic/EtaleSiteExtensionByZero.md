# Exact extension by zero along an analytic étale map

Let `p : Y ⟶ X` be étale between localized analytic spaces over a complete ultrametric normed field. On the ordinary étale sites, restriction along `p` is precomposition by the functor sending `V ⟶ Y` to `V ⟶ Y ⟶ X`. The functor `EtaleSite.extensionByZero` from module sheaves over `Y` to module sheaves over `X` sheafifies the presheaf left Kan extension along the opposite of this functor. The module and ring universes agree. The site over `Y` is essentially small in that universe, the site over `X` is locally small there, and module sheafification is available on the site over `X`.

For an étale test `U ⟶ X`, the comma category for evaluating the Kan extension admits a final discrete family indexed by arrows from `U` to the test `Y ⟶ X`. Each lift equips the underlying space of `U` with an étale map to `Y`. An arrow from `U` to a postcomposed test factors through the lift indexed by its composite to `Y`. The original arrow determines both the lift and the factor's underlying map. The evaluation colimit is therefore a coproduct of modules. Module coproducts and sheafification preserve finite limits, so extension by zero preserves finite limits. The left adjunction supplies preservation of colimits. Its right adjoint, ordinary étale restriction, preserves injective module sheaves.

The adjunction is the one described for étale maps in Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §5.4, Remark 5.4.2(ii), p. 109. The Lean construction obtains the left adjoint directly on the site by the Kan extension formula and proves exactness using the discrete family of lifts.
