# Full strong composition on actual chart domains

This module constructs full strong inclusions between nested actual chart domains and proves their identity, composition and source/target naturality laws. It also proves that the restriction of an ambient greatest-chart strong composite is the categorical composite of the two admissible chart restrictions. The coefficient field `K` has `NormedField K`, `IsUltrametricDist K` and `CompleteSpace K`. The ambient `AffinoidAtlasPresentation` objects have the same coefficient universe `u`, space universe `v` and chart-index universe `w`. The imports are the published `StrongAtlasRestriction` and `StrongAtlasComposition`.

The source is V. Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §1.2, Definition 1.2.3 and Remark 1.2.4(i), printed p. 16 / PDF p. 13, and Definition 1.2.7 and the composition construction following Proposition 1.2.8, printed p. 18 / PDF p. 15. The registered source is `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`. The first passage supplies the actual bounded restrictions and their transitivity. The second requires a compatible affinoid morphism for every admissible chart pair. The present constructions and equations are derived infrastructure for those conventions; they are not separately numbered results in the source.

For a full strong map `f`, `StrongHom.onCharts f i j h` regards the published `restrictChart f i j h` as a morphism from `TopChartPresentation.ofChart A i` to `TopChartPresentation.ofChart B j`. Here `h` says that the image of the whole source chart lies in the target chart. Each domain presentation retains every original chart contained in the selected outer chart, with its original algebra and actual bounded restrictions. Its selected outer chart is greatest automatically. Thus this local morphism requires no greatest chart in either ambient atlas.

`StrongHom.compOfTopCharts_pullback_top` specializes the published composition formula to the supplied greatest intermediate and target indices. It supplies all three admissibility proofs using `image_chart_top`. At any original source index, the bounded pullback of the full composite to the greatest target is exactly the product of the two bounded pullbacks through the greatest intermediate chart.

`StrongHom.onCharts_compOfTopCharts` treats ambient strong maps `f : A → B` and `g : B → C` with greatest charts `b` in `B` and `c` in `C`. For any indices `i`, `j`, `k` with `f(chart i) ⊆ chart j` and `g(chart j) ⊆ chart k`, restriction of `f.compOfTopCharts g b hb c hc` from `i` to `k` equals `f.onCharts i j hf ≫ g.onCharts j k hg`. The statement takes an explicit admissibility proof for the composite restriction as well. Its proof chooses the greatest chart of the actual target-domain presentation and uses `toChosen_injective`. Base maps and chosen indices agree definitionally. For each contained source chart, the ambient composition formula and the preceding greatest-target formula compute both bounded pullbacks as the same product through `j` and `k`. This proves equality of the entire strong maps, including every admissible pullback.

`chartStrongMap A i j hij` restricts the actual ambient strong identity to chart domains with `chart i ⊆ chart j`. It is a morphism in the constructed greatest-chart category of those actual domain presentations. `chartStrongMap_apply_val` records that its underlying map sends a point of the source subspace to the same ambient point in the target subspace.

`chartStrongMap_pullback` identifies every admissible local pullback of this inclusion with the original bounded restriction `A.atlas.restriction a.val b.val`, for contained source and target indices `a` and `b`. The inclusion of their original ranges is recovered by `restrictBaseMap_admissible_iff` and the ambient identity map. The equality is definitional, up to proof irrelevance for the containment witnesses. In particular, the construction retains algebraic information that cannot in general be recovered from spectrum points alone.

`chartStrongMap_id` proves that the inclusion from a chart to itself is the actual categorical identity, for any supplied reflexive containment witness, by the published `restrictChart_id`. `chartStrongMap_comp` proves that the inclusions for `i ⊆ j ⊆ k` compose to the inclusion for `i ⊆ k`, with an explicit witness for each containment. Applying `toChosen_injective` at the greatest chart of the `k` domain reduces the proof to bounded maps. The greatest-target composite formula and the ambient atlas equation `restriction_comp` give the required equality, with the latter read in the reverse direction.

The final two laws require no greatest chart of an ambient atlas. `StrongHom.chartStrongMap_onCharts` says that, for `i ⊆ i'` and a fixed target chart `j` containing both images, the source inclusion followed by the strong restriction at `i'` equals the strong restriction at `i`. Its bounded equality is the supplied source restriction compatibility `restriction_comp_pullback`. `StrongHom.onCharts_chartStrongMap` says that, for `j ⊆ j'` both containing the image of source chart `i`, the strong restriction to `j` followed by the target inclusion equals the strong restriction to `j'`. Its bounded equality is `pullback_comp_restriction` read in reverse. Both proofs use greatest-target detection on the actual target-domain presentation and the greatest-target composite formula.

The ambient composition comparison retains the greatest intermediate and target hypotheses of the published composition construction. The source's general composition construction extends atlases and glues affinoid morphisms over finite covers. General extended atlases, composition for ambient atlases without greatest charts, localization to analytic spaces, structure sheaves, étale sites and the Harris tower remain separate obligations.
