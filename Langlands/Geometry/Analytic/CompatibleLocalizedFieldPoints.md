# Compatible field points above a pair of ordinary points

Two ordinary points with equal images under strong maps lie in source
charts over a common base chart. Their spectrum points have the same
restriction to that chart. Affinoid point amalgamation gives two bounded
characters agreeing over the base, and the proof composes them with the
isometric embedding into the complete algebraic closure of their common
field.

Each character extends to a full field point by the Library's domain
residue comparison. Their images have the same ordinary point and the same
character on the common base chart, so the images agree as full field
points. Right fractions transport the ordinary points to the numerator
sources and the resulting field points back along the denominators. The
argument is derived from the common-chart, point amalgamation, and field
point extension theorems. It uses independent field and space universes.
