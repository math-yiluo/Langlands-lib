# Polynomial-locus affinoid charts and actual radius restrictions

This is the first construction unit for finite-type affine analytification. It
constructs the closed bounded polynomial loci and their affinoid-domain maps.
It does not yet glue them or identify the resulting space with a scheme
analytification functor.

## Provenance

The packaging here is derived from Berkovich, *Spectral Theory and Analytic
Geometry over Non-Archimedean Fields* (1990), Definition 1.5.1 and Remark
1.5.2(i), printed p.19/PDF p.33, together with the proof of Theorem 3.4.1,
steps (1)--(3), printed p.64/PDF p.78. The former describes affine space by
polynomial seminorms and its closed-polydisc covering. The latter constructs
analytic affine space from polydiscs, handles closed subschemes by their ideal,
and then obtains finite-type affine schemes. The exact closed-kernel quotient
and explicit finite-presentation interface below are internal implementation
choices, rather than a separately numbered definition in that source.

## Definitions and hypotheses

`Langlands.Analytic.FinitePolynomialPresentation K A` consists of a natural
number `arity`, an actual algebra map from `MvPolynomial (Fin arity) K` to `A`,
and its surjectivity proof. Here both rings are commutative and `A` is a
`K`-algebra. `ofFiniteType` chooses such data using Mathlib's proved finite-type
characterization. A caller can instead supply any such presentation.

In `Langlands.Analytic.PolynomialLocus`, fix this presentation `P`, a
nonarchimedean normed field `K`, and any positive polyradius `r`. No norm on
`A` is required. `relations P r` is the image of the entire polynomial kernel
under the actual weighted Tate polynomial map. `chart P r` is
`PresentedAffinoidAlgebra.ofRelations` for this relation set: its ideal is
exactly the closure of the ideal generated by these images. The carrier and
norm are the Library's actual closed quotient, not an algebra specified through
its spectrum points.

`polynomialMap P r` is polynomial evaluation followed by this quotient map.
`polynomialMap_relation` proves annihilation of every polynomial kernel
element. `algebraMap P r` descends the polynomial map along the given
surjection onto `A`. `algebraMap_map` and `algebraMap_comp` give the exact
factorization. The name `algebraMap` denotes this explicit `K`-algebra
homomorphism; no additional algebra instance on the chart is installed.

For coordinatewise `t ≤ s`, `restriction P s t hts` is the actual bounded
coefficient restriction descended through the closed relation ideal.
`restriction_presentationMap`, `restriction_coordinate`,
`restriction_polynomial`, and `restriction_algebraMap` record its values on
series, coordinates, polynomials, and elements of `A`. `restriction_self` and
`restriction_trans` prove the identity and composition laws for these actual
bounded homomorphisms.

## Full domain representation

Assume additionally that `K` is complete. `weierstrassChart P s t` is the
Library rational chart on the larger quotient with denominator one,
numerators the coordinate series, and new radii `t`. Its locus is exactly the
coordinate inequalities `x(T_i) ≤ t_i`.

`fromChart` extends the actual radius restriction and sends the new fraction
coordinates to the smaller quotient's coordinates. `toChart` evaluates the
smaller Tate algebra at the fraction coordinates. The identity
`fraction_polynomial` proves that this evaluation agrees on every polynomial
with the larger quotient's polynomial map followed by the rational base map;
therefore it annihilates every required relation. The construction uses
bounded evaluation and closed-ideal descent. The two inverse laws use
coordinate extensionality and rational base-map extensionality. They form the
actual bounded algebra equivalence `chartEquiv`, over the larger chart.

`represents_restriction` transports the existing full rational-chart
representation along this explicit equivalence. Its `Represents` predicate
tests every finite positive-polyradius presented affinoid algebra, rather
than only field-valued points or reduced tests. `range_comap_restriction`
extracts the corresponding spectrum-image equality. No domain representation
or comparison theorem is supplied as input data.

All arities, including zero, and all commutative finite-type algebras,
including the zero algebra and nonreduced algebras, are permitted. Positivity
is required only of the specified radii; nontriviality of the field norm is
not needed in this chart unit. No algebra is identified merely because its
spectrum has the same underlying set as another spectrum. The maps and
inverse identities above are algebra identities, retaining nilpotents in the
closed quotients.

## Remaining scope

Cofinal radii, gluing, an affinoid atlas, saturation and localization, and the
interface for genuine affine schemes belong to the subsequent construction
units. General independence of algebraic presentation, functoriality for all
affine scheme morphisms, rational-point and point-topology comparisons, the
scheme analytification universal property, and arbitrary analytic
field-extension comparisons are subsequent proposition and proof tasks.
Nothing here proves that final analytification bridge.
