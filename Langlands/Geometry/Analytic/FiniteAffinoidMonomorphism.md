# Finite Banach monomorphisms of affinoid spectra

Let `f : A → B` be a bounded homomorphism of presented affinoid algebras
over a complete ultrametric normed field, with an explicit finite Banach
witness. If the corresponding morphism of small spectra is a monomorphism
in the localized analytic category, its two maps out of the affinoid
self-pullback agree. The finite Banach tensor comparison identifies this
agreement with equality of the two algebraic factor maps to `B ⊗[A] B`.
This is the algebra epimorphism criterion, which gives surjectivity for the
finite map `f`.

When `f` is also flat and surjective on bounded spectra, faithful flatness
gives injectivity. The algebraic inverse is bounded by applying the
finite Banach triangle estimate to `f` and the identity of `A`. The two
inverse identities of bounded maps give
`SmallAffinoidSpectrum.isoOfFiniteBanachMonoOfFlatOfSpectrumSurjective`,
whose forward morphism is the localized spectrum map of `f`. This applies
in particular to a surjective finite étale monomorphism with a finite Banach
witness. The field and small spectrum universes are independent.
