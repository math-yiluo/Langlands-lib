# The category of analytic spaces with varying ground fields

An object consists of a complete nonarchimedean normed field \(K\) and a
localized analytic space \(X\) over \(K\). An arrow from \((L,Y)\) to
\((K,X)\) consists of an isometric field embedding \(K\to L\) and an analytic
map \(Y\to X_L\) over \(L\). Composition extends the second analytic map
along the source embedding and then uses the tower comparison to identify
the successive extension with the direct extension.

The unit and tower comparisons give the scalar-extension pseudofunctor.
The contravariant Grothendieck construction gives the category and its
fibration over the opposite category of complete fields. The Cartesian
arrow above an embedding is the projection from the scalar-extended
space. The fibre over a field is equivalent to the localized saturated
analytic category by the actual saturation equivalence.

V. G. Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces*
(1993), §1.4, printed p. 30 (PDF p. 27), constructs ground-field extension
and uses it to define arrows between analytic spaces over varying ground
fields. Literature ID `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA-256
