# Coherence of scalar extension on compatible analytic atlases

The unit projection and tower comparisons preserve the indices and pull back
each presentation coordinate to its counterpart on the matching chart.
Bounded algebra maps on affinoid presentations are determined by these
coordinates. The chart equations then identify the underlying maps at every
point. Equality of the bounded pullbacks on matching charts identifies the
compatible strong maps by chart change.

The coherence identities express the compatibility of the projections and
the comparison between direct and successive extension in Berkovich's
ground-field construction. See V. G. Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for
non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §1.4, printed p. 30 (PDF p. 27),
