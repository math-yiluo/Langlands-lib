# Restricting a compact quasi-étale lift to a distinguished étale object

A distinguished étale object is isomorphic over its base to an open analytic
domain of a compact quasi-étale object. The open has an analytic-domain
complement. Given a lift on the compact object, its open subgroup can be
shrunk so the action preserves the complement and hence the open.

The complement is compact. Its contained charts form a quasinet, so finitely
many of those charts cover it. Their open stabilizers intersect in an open
subgroup. This gives invariance of the analytic complement, and invariance
under inverse elements gives invariance of the open domain. Restrict the
continuous action to the invariant open domain and transport the lift along
the distinguished object's source isomorphism.

This internal result uses the Library's compact quasinet subcover,
analytic chart stabilizers and continuity of invariant-domain restrictions.
It reduces existence of distinguished étale lifts to existence on compact
quasi-étale objects.
