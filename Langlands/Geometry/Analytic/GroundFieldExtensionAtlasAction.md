# The coefficient action on a scalar-extended analytic atlas

For \(L/K\) an extension of complete nonarchimedean normed fields, the
scalar-extended chart system has the same indices as the original atlas.
An isometric \(K\)-automorphism of \(L\) acts on a chart spectrum by
precomposing a bounded multiplicative seminorm with the inverse
semilinear automorphism of its extended chart algebra. The bounded
coefficient maps commute with every extended restriction map.
Consequently the action respects the quotient that glues the spectra.

`coefficientMulAction` gives the resulting point action for a group
homomorphism into the isometric coefficient automorphisms. It acts
over the natural projection to the original space and preserves each
extended chart range. Its pullback on a chart is the given semilinear
automorphism, determined by its actions on the two tensor factors.

Pointwise norm-continuity of the action on \(L\) gives joint continuity
of the action on the extended space. The induced action on each chart
spectrum is jointly continuous by continuity of its norm-valued
evaluations. The rectangles formed by the operator group and each
extended chart range form a quasinet, so continuity on these rectangles
gives continuity on the whole product.
