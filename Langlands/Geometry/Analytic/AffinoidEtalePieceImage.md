# Affinoid quotient isomorphisms from finite chart covers

Work over a complete ultrametric nontrivially normed field `K` with
supplied `AffinoidEtalePieceQuotient` data for `a : M(B) → Y`. The map
from the quotient to `Y` is monic. Pull the quotient's finite étale
surjective cover back along each of two test maps. The two successive
pullbacks are surjective and étale. On them the original pair lifts to
the piece. Equality in `Y` gives equality in the quotient by its
kernel-pair equation. Separation along the two étale covers gives equality
on the original test.

Suppose the quotient map factors through a monomorphism `i : T → Y`
from the localization of a saturated atlas. For each small chart
`b : M(A) → T`, assume there is a bounded map `g : A → C` that is
finite Banach and algebraically finite étale and induces a surjection
on Banach spectra. Assume there is also a lift `r : M(C) → M(B)` with
`r ≫ a = M(g) ≫ b ≫ i`. The two maps from the self-pullback of this
cover agree after mapping to the quotient, by the kernel-pair equation.
Finite affinoid Hom descent gives a lift of the chart to the quotient.
The preceding monicity gives uniqueness and gluing. The resulting arrow
is inverse to the factorization of the quotient map.

This internal argument uses actual localized pullbacks, point lifting,
separation along étale covers, and finite affinoid Hom descent. It
requires the finite cover diagrams for the charts of the proposed
image.
