# Restriction morphisms of equivariant sites from fibre products

This module proves `RestrictionSiteMorphismStatement` **conditionally**: from
the two fibre-product statements `EquivariantQuasiEtalePullbacksStatement` and
`EquivariantEtalePullbacksStatement` (module
`EquivariantSitePullbacksStatement`). Both premises are stated in that module
and are **not proved** here or elsewhere in the Library at the time of
writing. The final theorem is therefore not an unconditional proof of
`RestrictionSiteMorphismStatement`; it reduces that statement to the two
premises.

## Main result

```
theorem restrictionSiteMorphism_of_equivariantPullbacks
    (hq : EquivariantQuasiEtalePullbacksStatement.{u, v, z})
    (he : EquivariantEtalePullbacksStatement.{u, v, z}) :
    RestrictionSiteMorphismStatement.{u, v, z, z'}
```

in namespace `Langlands.Analytic.SaturatedAtlasPresentation`. For every
complete ultrametric normed field `K`, every continuous homomorphism
`f : H →ₜ* G` of groups with topologies and every `G`-space `X`, it gives:
`EquivariantQuasiEtaleSite.res f : Qét(X(G)) ⥤ Qét(X(H))` is representably
flat and continuous for the quasi-étale topologies, and
`EquivariantEtaleSite.res f : Ét(X(G)) ⥤ Ét(X(H))` is representably flat and
continuous for the étale topologies. The premises are used only for the
group `G` (universe `z`); the group `H` may live in another universe `z'`.

## Declarations

### A general cover-preservation lemma (namespace `CategoryTheory`)

`coverPreserving_toGrothendieck_of_preservesPullbacks`. Let `F : C ⥤ D` be
a functor, where `C` has pullbacks and `F` preserves limits of shape
`WalkingCospan`. Let `J` be a precoverage on `C` and `K` a Grothendieck
topology on `D`. If for every covering presieve `R ∈ J U` the sieve generated
by the image presieve `R.map F` is a `K`-covering sieve of `F.obj U`, then `F`
is cover-preserving from `J.toGrothendieck` to `K`.

Proof: by Mathlib's description of `J.toGrothendieck` through
`Precoverage.Saturate` (`Precoverage.mem_toGrothendieck_iff`), induction on
the saturation.

- Generating coverings: the pushforward of `Sieve.generate R` is
  `Sieve.generate (R.map F)` (`Sieve.generate_map_eq_functorPushforward`),
  which covers by assumption.
- The maximal sieve: its pushforward is the maximal sieve.
- Pullback of a sieve `S` along `f : Y ⟶ X`: the pushforward of
  `S.pullback f` contains the pullback along `F.map f` of the pushforward of
  `S`. An element of the latter factors through `F.map a` with `a` in `S`;
  since `F` maps the pullback square of `a` and `f` to a pullback square, it
  factors through `F.map (pullback.snd a f)`, and `pullback.snd a f` lies in
  `S.pullback f`. Pullback-stability and the superset property of `K`
  conclude.
- Transitivity: from the transitivity axiom of `K` and the pullback-stability
  of `K`, using that the pushforward of a sieve is generated by images.

This is where the preservation of pullbacks enters. It is used instead of
Mathlib's `isContinuous_toGrothendieck_of_pullbacksPreservedBy`, which asks
for coverings stable under base change, something the Library does not prove
for the equivariant precoverages.

### Lemmas on equivariant spaces over a base (namespace `GSpace`)

Throughout, `Γ` is a group with a topology, `P` a morphism property of
`GSpace.{u, v, v} K Γ`, `Y` a `Γ`-space, and `P.Over ⊤ Y` the category of
`Γ`-spaces over `Y` whose structure morphism satisfies `P`, with all
`Γ`-morphisms over `Y` (the sites `EquivariantQuasiEtaleSite X` and
`EquivariantEtaleSite X` are of this form). `forget` is the Library's
functor `GSpace.forget` to the category of analytic spaces
`LocalizedCategory.{u, v, v}`.

- `forget_map_injective`: `forget` is faithful (a restatement of
  `forget.map_injective`).
- `homOfForget φ hφ`: the `Γ`-morphism with underlying morphism `φ`, given
  that `φ` commutes with the actions of every `g : Γ`;
  `forget_map_homOfForget` records that its underlying morphism is `φ`.
- `forget_map_comm`: the underlying morphism of a `Γ`-morphism commutes
  with the actions.
- `isLimitPullbackConeOfForget w h`: constructs the `IsLimit` data for
  `PullbackCone.mk p₁ p₂ w` in `P.Over ⊤ Y`, where `w : p₁ ≫ f₁ = p₂ ≫ f₂`
  and `h` says the underlying analytic square is a pullback. For each cone
  `s`, take the analytic lift `l` from `h`. It commutes with the actions
  because `s.pt`'s action composed with `l` and `l` composed with the
  vertex's action have the same two projections, using equivariance of
  `p₁`, `p₂`, `s.fst` and `s.snd`. The equation over `Y` follows from the
  equation for `p₁` and the first factorization identity. The two
  factorization identities and uniqueness are checked after `forget` by
  faithfulness and the universal property of the analytic pullback.
- `isPullback_of_isPullback_forget`: a square in `P.Over ⊤ Y` whose
  underlying analytic square is a pullback is a pullback in `P.Over ⊤ Y`.
  Faithfulness gives `w` from the underlying commutativity equation, so
  `isLimitPullbackConeOfForget w h` gives the universal property.
- `isTerminalOfIsIsoHom`: an object `T` of `P.Over ⊤ Y` whose structure
  morphism is an isomorphism is terminal: the unique morphism from `U` is
  `U.hom ≫ inv T.hom`.
- `hasPullbacks_of_isPullback_forget`: if every cospan in `P.Over ⊤ Y` is
  completed by a square whose underlying square of analytic spaces is a
  pullback, then `P.Over ⊤ Y` has pullbacks (by
  `isPullback_of_isPullback_forget`).
- `representablyFlat_and_isContinuous_of_isPullback_forget`: let
  `P` contain identities and let `F : P.Over ⊤ Y ⥤ P'.Over ⊤ Y'` be a
  functor to the corresponding category for a group `Γ'`. Assume
  1. every cospan in `P.Over ⊤ Y` is completed by a square whose underlying
     square is a pullback of analytic spaces;
  2. `F` maps squares whose underlying square is a pullback to squares whose
     underlying square is a pullback;
  3. `F` maps objects with invertible structure morphism to objects with
     invertible structure morphism;
  4. `F` maps every covering presieve of a precoverage `J` on
     `P.Over ⊤ Y` to a covering presieve of a precoverage `J'` on
     `P'.Over ⊤ Y'`.

  Then `F` is representably flat and continuous from `J.toGrothendieck` to
  `J'.toGrothendieck`.

  Proof. The object `(Y, 𝟙 Y)` is terminal (`isTerminalOfIsIsoHom`), and its
  image is terminal by 3, so `F` preserves terminal objects. By 1 the
  category has pullbacks; for each cospan, the square of 1 is a pullback by
  `isPullback_of_isPullback_forget`, and its image under `F` is a pullback
  by 2 and the same lemma, so `F` preserves limits of shape
  `WalkingCospan`. Mathlib's `preservesFiniteLimits_of_preservesTerminal_and_pullbacks`
  and `hasFiniteLimits_of_hasTerminal_and_pullbacks` then show that `F`
  preserves finite limits out of a finitely complete category, and
  `flat_of_preservesFiniteLimits` gives representable flatness. For a
  representably flat functor, continuity is equivalent to cover
  preservation (`Functor.isContinuous_iff_coverPreserving`), which follows
  from `coverPreserving_toGrothendieck_of_preservesPullbacks` and 4, since a
  covering presieve of `J'` generates a covering sieve of
  `J'.toGrothendieck` (`Precoverage.generate_mem_toGrothendieck`).

### The reduction

`restrictionSiteMorphism_of_equivariantPullbacks` applies
`representablyFlat_and_isContinuous_of_isPullback_forget` twice, with
`F = EquivariantQuasiEtaleSite.res f` (resp. `EquivariantEtaleSite.res f`),
`J` and `J'` the precoverages `equivariantQuasiEtalePrecoverage` (resp.
`equivariantEtalePrecoverage`) of `X` and of `(GSpace.res f).obj X`. The
Library's topologies `equivariantQuasiEtaleTopology` and
`equivariantEtaleTopology` are by definition `toGrothendieck` of these
precoverages. The four hypotheses are discharged as follows:

1. by the premise `hq K G X` (resp. `he K G X`);
2. restriction does not change underlying analytic spaces or morphisms:
   `forget.map ((res f).map φ).left` is definitionally `forget.map φ.left`,
   so the hypothesis is passed on unchanged;
3. the structure morphism of `(res f).obj U` is `(GSpace.res f).map U.hom`,
   an isomorphism when `U.hom` is (functors preserve isomorphisms);
4. by the Library's
   `EquivariantQuasiEtaleSite.map_res_mem_equivariantQuasiEtalePrecoverage_iff`
   (resp. `EquivariantEtaleSite.map_res_mem_equivariantEtalePrecoverage_iff`).

## Original source and provenance

Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces* (unfinished
manuscript, 1995–1996), registered as
`provided-7bb3903652336bd799aad8853b8efd7ff3bcc0c7652745417f4745a14ac08e90`,
§1.2, printed p.3, asserts that a morphism `X′(G′) → X(G)` gives morphisms of
sites "in the evident way" and gives no proof. The proof route here is
internal: fibre products in `Qét(X(G))` and `Ét(X(G))` computed on
underlying analytic spaces (the premises), the terminal object `X(G)` itself,
preservation of both by restriction because restriction keeps the
underlying analytic spaces, and Mathlib's criteria for representable
flatness and continuity. No other source is used.

## Scope

- The theorem is conditional on `EquivariantQuasiEtalePullbacksStatement`
  and `EquivariantEtalePullbacksStatement`; neither is proved. Do not cite
  this module as a proof of `RestrictionSiteMorphismStatement` until both
  premises are proved and accepted.
- The `GSpace` lemmas are general (any `P`, `Y`, `F`) and are proved
  unconditionally, as is the cover-preservation lemma.
