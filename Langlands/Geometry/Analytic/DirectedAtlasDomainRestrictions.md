# Bounded restrictions between full domains in directed original charts

Let `K : Type u` be a complete ultrametric normed field, `X : Type v` a topological space, and `a : IndexedAffinoidAtlas.{u,v,w} K X` an actual affinoid atlas. This module assumes `hd : a.HasDirectedCharts`: every two original chart ranges lie in a common original chart range. No greatest chart is chosen. The coefficient, ambient and original index universes remain independent.

For old indices `i,j`, let `U : Domain K (a.algebra i)` and `V : Domain K (a.algebra j)` be full represented domains, and suppose their ambient subsets satisfy `a.chart i '' U.locus ⊆ a.chart j '' V.locus`. Both domain algebras and structural maps are supplied by those actual domain representations. All their factorization tests range over arbitrary finite positive-polyradius presented affinoid algebras.

`extendDomain_locus_subset_of_chart_image_subset` places both domains in any common original upper chart `q` using the published `extendDomain`. The published ambient image equations and injectivity of the original chart `a.chart q` turn the ambient containment into containment of these two full domains over `a.algebra q`. `domainRestrictionToUpper` applies the full-domain factorization property to construct the bounded algebra map `V.algebra → U.algebra`. `domainRestrictionToUpper_comp_baseMap` proves its equation over `a.algebra q`:

`f.comp (V.baseMap.comp (a.restriction j q hjq)) = U.baseMap.comp (a.restriction i q hiq)`.

`extendDomain_baseMap_comp_restriction` proves compatibility of these structural maps with a further original upper chart by the original atlas's restriction-composition equation.

`directedDomainRestriction_existsUnique` constructs one entire bounded map satisfying this equation for every common original upper chart. It first constructs the map in one upper chart `q₀`. Given another `q`, directedness supplies a further original chart containing both. The original restriction-composition law transports the equation to that chart; the original relative domain's full factorization uniqueness cancels restriction from the further chart to `q`. Uniqueness among all candidate bounded maps follows from full uniqueness for the extended domain representations over `q₀`. Thus equality is proved for bounded algebra homomorphisms, with no inference from agreement of maps on spectrum points.

`directedDomainRestriction` selects this proved unique map, and `directedDomainRestriction_comp_baseMap` records its equation over every common upper. `eq_directedDomainRestriction_of_over_base` proves that a single such equation already determines the entire map. `domainRestrictionToUpper_eq_directedDomainRestriction` proves that every original common upper produces precisely the selected restriction.

`directedDomainRestriction_chart` proves compatibility with the actual ambient maps on all spectrum points of `U.algebra`, by evaluating the bounded base equation in a common upper and applying both original chart squares. `directedDomainRestriction_sameChart` recovers every supplied full-domain inclusion in a single original chart. `directedDomainRestriction_id` gives the bounded identity. `directedDomainRestriction_comp` proves the composition law for three represented domains in three independently chosen original charts. It chooses a common original upper for all three, uses the two bounded base equations and associativity, and applies full uniqueness over that upper.

This is a derived construction toward the extended-atlas restrictions of Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), Lemma 1.2.5 and Proposition 1.2.6, printed pp.17–18 / PDF pp.14–15, registered as `berkovich-1993-etale-cohomology`, SHA-256 `06b42aca1c8f237e607d2cf1f15a41fdc9718324a4dc9fd5bce66574254bb91c`. The full-domain convention is Berkovich, *Non-archimedean analytic spaces* (Trieste lectures, 2009), Definition 2.2.1, printed/PDF p.9, registered as `berkovich-2009-trieste-analytic-spaces`, SHA-256 `8cfe4f6f35d5e717ac67c5c8f5ef9ff9185f460bbe5f9a10aeacd84cddeeb2ca`.

The extra directed-original-chart hypothesis supplies a common base for full uniqueness. The general argument in the 1993 source instead uses intersections, finite coverings and Tate acyclicity, and is broader. This module constructs the bounded restrictions and their laws in the directed case. It does not yet construct the whole enlarged net or atlas, prove that every such relative spectrum map is an embedding, or establish the complete extended-atlas theorem.
