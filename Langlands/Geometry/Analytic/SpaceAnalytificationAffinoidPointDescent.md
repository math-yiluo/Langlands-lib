# Descent of affinoid-algebra points in analytifications

Let a bounded map of presented affinoid algebras be finite Banach,
algebraically finite étale, and surjective on bounded spectra. The finite
tensor comparison identifies its affinoid self-pushout with the ordinary
algebraic tensor product, with the actual maps from both factors. Flatness
and surjectivity on bounded spectra make the induced map of schemes
faithfully flat. Its étaleness supplies local finite presentation.

If an affinoid-algebra point of a separated locally finite type algebraic
space gives matching analytic maps on the two projections, naturality and
injectivity of the comparison give matching algebraic points. The fppf
sheaf property descends them to a point on the base. In particular, an
analytic map belongs to the image of the comparison if its restriction to
this cover belongs to the image. Equality with the map of the descended
point follows from uniqueness along a surjective étale analytic map.

This is an internal deduction from the finite tensor comparison, the
algebraic space's fppf sheaf property and the affinoid point comparison.
