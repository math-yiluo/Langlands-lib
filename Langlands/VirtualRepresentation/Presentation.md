# Integral presentations and projections of generated sublattices

This construction uses the concrete integral coefficient modules in `Integral`.
For an actual family `family : J → IntegralLattice I`, `Presentation family`
is the quotient of finite integral combinations on `J` by the kernel of
evaluation in `IntegralLattice I`. `presentationClass` is the quotient map.
`presentationEquiv` identifies this quotient with the integral span of the
family. Its construction composes Mathlib's proved first isomorphism theorem
with the equality between the range of finite linear combination and the
span of the generator family. No assertion that this span is the whole
ambient lattice is required.

`descend` takes values in any integral module on the same generators and a
proof that the evaluation kernel of `family` is annihilated by those values.
It constructs the induced map on the generated span by quotient lifting.
`spanEquivOfRelations` takes two actual families with the same generator
index and a proof that their evaluation kernels are equal. It composes their
quotient presentations to construct a linear equivalence between the two
spans. These are conditional constructions on explicit proof inputs, not
assertions that the inputs hold for Henniart's global families. Neither
isometry nor relation compatibility is a structure field or a project axiom.

For two actual families indexed by `J`, `evaluatePairs` evaluates formal
combinations in their paired span from `Integral.generatedPairs`. `pairLeft`
and `pairRight` project this span to the two individual spans. Membership
of each projection is proved by integral-span induction: a paired generator
projects to its generator, and zero, addition and integer scalar multiplication
preserve membership. Their injectivity is not assumed or supplied.

All index types and the target module have independent universes. The scalar
ring is exactly the integers; no topology, finite index set, representation
decomposition, or local/global comparison is needed for these constructions.

## Source correspondence

Henniart, *Une preuve simple des conjectures de Langlands pour GL(n) sur un
corps p-adique* (2000), section 2.5, pp.445-446, defines the two subgroups
generated by images of associated global pairs. Section 2.8, pp.447-448,
obtains equality of integral relations from a comparison of pairings and
then concludes that the induced correspondence on the generated subgroups
is well-defined and bijective. The retained original PDF pages 7-10 were read.

Source ID: `henniart-2000-simple-proof`.
PDF SHA256:
`be283c3e8187f1eed365537c72379c3e06e7791756954c2ac783c6beea2979de`.

This module extracts the elementary presentation construction from that
argument. It proves the quotient and projection obligations using actual
Mathlib objects. It neither constructs the global families nor proves the
equality of their relation kernels, which is precisely the substantive
theorem obligation retained by the companion statement branch.
