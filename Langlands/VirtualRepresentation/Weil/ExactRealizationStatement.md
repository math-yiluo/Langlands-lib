# Exact K0 realization and unique irreducible expansion

Author: Worker 34.

This pair states the finite-length comparison between the published
irreducible lattice and the exact Grothendieck group. It does not prove that
comparison. The only theorems proved here are the logical equivalence of its
bijectivity and unique-expansion formulations.

## Generic statements

In namespace `Langlands.VirtualRepresentation.Weil`, for every
`G : Type uG`, `[Group G]` and `[TopologicalSpace G]`, with an independent
representation-carrier universe `uV`:

- `ExactRealizationBijectiveStatement G` is precisely
  `Function.Bijective (realizeExact G)`.
- `ExactExpansionUniqueStatement G` is precisely
  `∀ x : ContinuousExactGroup G, ∃! v : IrreducibleLattice G,
  ExactExpansion G x v`.

These are `Prop`-valued definitions, with the displayed group and topology
parameters as their entire outer telescope. There is no additional
topological-group, semisimplicity, finite-image or decomposition hypothesis.
The universe parameters on both statements are `uG` and `uV`, independently.

The source is the existing finite-support integer module on genuine continuous
irreducible isomorphism classes across positive ranks, from `Weil.Generic`.
The target is the existing `ExactGrothendieckGroup (fun A => A.IsContinuous)`,
from `Weil.ExactRealization` and `VirtualRepresentation.Exact`. Its relations
are generated by actual short exact sequences whose three terms are continuous
finite-dimensional complex representations. The imported continuity predicate
uses the usual complex module topology on endomorphisms.

`ExactExpansion G x v` already means `realizeExact G v = x`. Accordingly, these
statements include zero and negative virtual classes, and exact classes of
nonsemisimple actual representations. There is no positivity constraint on the
integer coefficients. Neither definition selects an inverse or assigns
multiplicities to an arbitrary exact class. Equality here is equality in the
exact quotient; it does not assert that an original representation is
isomorphic to its semisimplification. No equality with split K0 is asserted.

## Actual local Weil specialization

In namespace `Langlands.VirtualRepresentation.LocalWeil`, the declarations
`ExactRealizationBijectiveStatement` and `ExactExpansionUniqueStatement`
have outer parameters `{p : ℕ}`, `[Fact p.Prime]` and

The local predicates are definitionally the generic predicates at this group.

## Internal finite-length rationale and source provenance

The statement is an internal derived comparison of the published constructions,
not a transcription of a numbered theorem in either source below.
`Weil.Representation.Basic` supplies actual finite-dimensional vector spaces
and invertible linear intertwiners; `Predicates` supplies continuity and
irreducibility. `Weil.Generic` supplies the irreducible lattice;
`VirtualRepresentation.Exact` supplies the exact relation quotient; and
`Weil.ExactRealization` supplies its canonical forward map. The latter's
generator, linearity and degree formulas alone do not establish bijectivity.

The mathematical rationale for the future proof is Jordan–Hölder finite
length. Strict chains of invariant subspaces are bounded by the finite vector
space dimension. Thus composition factors have positive dimension and are
algebraically irreducible. They remain continuous: choose an ordinary complex
linear complement to an invariant subspace, without requiring that complement
to be invariant. Restriction and quotient actions are obtained by composing
the original endomorphisms with fixed inclusions, projections and linear
sections. These operations on finite-dimensional complex endomorphism spaces
are continuous for the module topology used in `IsContinuous`. Their
composition with a continuous map out of `G` is continuous for any topology on
`G`; continuity of multiplication or inversion on `G` is not needed.
Subspaces and quotients of a carrier in `Type uV` stay in that universe.

A composition series would express an actual exact class as the sum of its
irreducible factors, using the defining short exact relations. Jordan–Hölder
uniqueness would make factor multiplicities independent of the series;
concatenating series along a short exact sequence would make them exact
additive. These facts would yield existence and uniqueness of the integral
expansion after passage to the quotient. This paragraph explains the intended
finite-length justification and the chosen hypotheses; none of those
representation-theoretic steps is implemented or asserted as a theorem here.

scope here is an explicit internal generalization justified by the preceding
linear continuity argument. Neither citation is being represented as a proof
of this Lean statement or its generalized scope.

## Implemented logical equivalence

`Weil.exactRealizationBijective_iff_uniqueExpansion` unfolds the expansion
relation to the graph of the forward map. Surjectivity supplies a preimage and
injectivity proves its uniqueness. Conversely, unique preimages supply
surjectivity; two elements with the same image equal the unique preimage of
that image, giving injectivity. The local theorem of the same name specializes
this equivalence definitionally. The argument neither proves either side
unconditionally nor constructs a global inverse.

The substantive bijectivity proof remains a separate task. Tensor
decomposition, inertia cancellation, break comparison, epsilon factors and
stability are outside this pair.
