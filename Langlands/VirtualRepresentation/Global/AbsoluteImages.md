# Integral images of witnessed absolute global pairs

This derived construction forms Henniart's two generated images over the projective limit
`W_F = S.WeilGroup` of a supplied transitive system `S` of Weil maps, and compares them with
the published relative images of the finite Galois layers. As in the relative module
`Langlands.VirtualRepresentation.Global.RelativeImages`, the index retains every local
witness. Every hypothesis of the comparison theorems is explicit. No generator, local map or
system of Weil maps is constructed here.

## Provenance and inputs

Henniart, *Une preuve simple des conjectures de Langlands pour GL(n) sur un corps p-adique*,
Invent. Math. 139 (2000), sections 2.4–2.5, printed pages 445–446 (PDF pages 7–8). The
registered source is `henniart-2000-simple-proof`, SHA256
`be283c3e8187f1eed365537c72379c3e06e7791756954c2ac783c6beea2979de`. There `G(F)` is the
set of pairs `(σ, Π)` with `σ` a continuous irreducible unitary representation of `W_F`, `Π` a
cuspidal unitary automorphic representation of `GL_n(A_F)` with `n = dim σ`, and `σ` and `Π`
associated. At a fixed finite place `v₀`, `φ_G(σ, Π)` is the class of `σ_{v₀}` in
`R_G(F_{v₀})`. If `Π_{v₀} = π₁ × ⋯ × π_r` with unitary supercuspidal `π_i` (Theorem 2.4),
`φ_A(σ, Π)` is the sum of the classes of the `π_i` in `R_A(F_{v₀})`. `R_G(F, v₀)` and
`R_A(F, v₀)` are the subgroups generated by the images of `φ_G` and `φ_A`.

The restriction `σ ↦ σ_v` is the one of Tate, *Number theoretic background*, Proc. Sympos.
Pure Math. 33, Part II (1979). The registered source is
`tate-1979-number-theoretic-background`, SHA256
`c890894fe19783890cc4def2c5bf92a18d71619acf8ba7005aefce4984fe70d6`, with PDF page = printed
page + 9. In (2.1) (printed p.9), a representation of `W_F` factors through some `W_{E/F}`,
and the restriction is induced by a map `θ_v : W_{F_v} → W_F` of Proposition (1.6.1)
(printed p.8). Tate shows that the restricted class does not depend on the choice of `θ_v`.
The published predicate `S.IsLocalWeilMap v θ` formalizes such maps.

This module follows the relative module and differs from the source in the same way:

- The global unitarity of `Π` is not asserted. An actual unitary supercuspidal support of the
  local component at `v` is supplied as a witness. Henniart's Theorem 2.4, which provides
  that support, is not used or proved.
- Association is the published absolute weak association `S.IsWeaklyAssociated`. It asks for
  continuity, the rank equality, and, at cofinitely many finite places, unramified matching of
  Euler polynomials along **every** local-to-global Weil map at that place.
- The class of `σ_v` is the exact class of the full restriction along one local-to-global Weil
  map `θ`. That map is part of the index, so all maps satisfying `S.IsLocalWeilMap v θ`
  contribute. The independence of the choice of `θ` is not used.
- The Weil element is an effective element of the unitary irreducible lattice whose image in
  the exact Grothendieck group is that full class, as an explicit witness.

Let `F` and `Ω` be fields in universe `u`, with `F` a number field and `Ω` an `F`-algebra.
Fix a fundamental-class family `c`, a restriction datum `h`, a transitive system
`S : WeilMapSystem c F Ω h` and a finite place `v` of `F`. The Weil representation carriers
have universe `uW` and the presentation factors have universe `uP`. The automorphic support
data use universe `u`, the universe of the actual form-space carrier. The generator structure
and the lattices use the existing compactness instance of the adele quotient of `F`, as in the
relative module.

## Full local classes

`localExactClass S v A hA θ` is the class in the continuous exact Grothendieck group of
`W_{F_v}` of `S.localComponent A θ`, the restriction of a continuous representation `A` of
`W_F` along `θ`, on the original carrier. `localExactClass_degree` gives its degree as the rank
of `A`.

The section `Conjugation` contains three auxiliary results:

- `restrictConjEquiv`: if `φ g = x⁻¹ * ψ g * x` for all `g`, then `A(x)` is a representation
  equivalence from the restriction of `A` along `φ` to the restriction along `ψ`. Its inverse
  is `A(x⁻¹)`.
- `localExactClass_eq_of_equiv`: equivalent representations of `W_F` have equal full local
  classes along the same `θ`. It restricts the equivalence along `θ`.
- `localExactClass_inflate`: the full local class of the inflation `S.inflate K B` along `θ`
  equals the relative class `RelativeGlobal.localExactClass F K c v B hB ι`, whenever
  `S.toLevel K (θ g) = x⁻¹ * ι g * x` for all `g`. It applies `restrictConjEquiv` to `B`
  with `φ` the composite of `θ` with `S.levelHom K`.

## Witnessed generators and degree laws

`WitnessedGenerator S v` retains:

- a rank index `rankIndex`, for the rank `rankIndex + 1`;
- an actual representation `weil` of `W_F`, with irreducibility and unitarity;
- an actual cuspidal operator realization of rank index `rankIndex`, a spectral presentation
  of it, and the weak association `S.IsWeaklyAssociated weil presentation.spectralSystem`;
- a local-to-global map `localMap` with `S.IsLocalWeilMap v localMap`;
- local irreducibility and admissibility of the presentation's local component at `v`;
- an effective Weil element of the unitary lattice of `W_{F_v}`, with an exact expansion equal
  to `localExactClass` of `weil` along `localMap`;
- an automorphic element of the unitary supercuspidal lattice of `F_v`, with actual
  `IsUnitaryLatticeSupport` of the local component placed in the p-adic category by
  `padicObject`.

`associatedClass` recovers the published `S.IsWeaklyAssociatedClass` with the cuspidal
isomorphism class. `weil_degree` applies degree to the exact expansion and uses
`localExactClass_degree` and the rank equality of the association. `automorphic_degree` uses
the support's degree law, so `degrees_eq` identifies the two degrees.
`automorphic_effective` gives effectivity of the support.

## Generated images

`pairedLattice S v` is the integral span of the pairs of **all** witnessed absolute
generators. `weilImage S v` and `automorphicImage S v` are the two integral generated spans.
They specialize the published `generatedPairs` and `generated`. The generator membership
lemmas, `degrees_eq_of_mem`, the projections `weilProjection` and `automorphicProjection` and
their surjectivity onto the generated spans are proved exactly as in the relative module:
integral span induction with the cases generator, zero, sum and integer multiple.

## Comparison of generators

Relative to absolute. `WitnessedGenerator.ofRelative` turns a relative generator `d` at a
finite Galois layer `K` into an absolute one with the same Weil and automorphic elements.
Its inputs are:

1. `hK`: surjectivity of `S.toLevel K`;
2. `hθ`: existence of local-to-global Weil maps at cofinitely many finite places;
3. a map `θ` with `S.IsLocalWeilMap v θ` and an `x` with `S.toLevel K (θ g) =
   x⁻¹ * d.localMap g * x`.

The Weil representation is `S.inflate K d.weil`. Irreducibility uses the published
`isIrreducible_inflate` with `hK`, unitarity uses `isUnitary_inflate`, and association uses
`isWeaklyAssociated_inflate` with `hθ`. The expansion is transported by
`localExactClass_inflate`.

`exists_ofRelative` produces `θ` and `x` from a hypothesis `hlift`. `hlift` asks that every
local-to-relative Weil map `ι` at a place above `v` lift to a local-to-global one, i.e.
`S.toLevel K (θ g) = x * ι g * x⁻¹`. This is the published `LocalWeilMapLiftStatement`
specialized to `S`, `v` and `K`. The proof applies it to the generator's own local map and
uses `x⁻¹`.

Absolute to relative. `WitnessedGenerator.exists_relative` produces, for an absolute
generator `d`, a layer `K` and a relative generator with the same elements. Its explicit
hypotheses are:

1. `hfact`: every continuous representation of `W_F` is equivalent to the inflation of a
   continuous representation of some `W_{K/F}`. This is the published
   `WeilRepresentationFactorizationStatement` specialized to `S` (Tate (2.1)).
2. `hsurj`: every `S.toLevel K` is surjective.
3. `hlift`: the lifting hypothesis above, at every finite place and every layer.
4. `hrel`: for every layer `K`, at cofinitely many finite places `v'` of `F`, every place of
   `K` above `v'` has a reciprocity candidate and a local-to-relative Weil map.

Hypothesis 4 is needed because the relative unramified matching requires a local map at every
place above `v'`. The absolute association supplies maps only into `W_F`, and no transport
between the places above `v'` is used.

The proof takes `B` and an equivalence `e : d.weil ≃ S.inflate K B` from `hfact`, and a place
`w`, a candidate, `x` and `ι` with `S.toLevel K (d.localMap g) = x⁻¹ * ι g * x` from
`IsLocalWeilMap.exists_level`. Then:

- Irreducibility of `B`: irreducibility transfers along `e`, and the published order
  isomorphism `WeilMapSystem.subrepresentationCompOrderIso` for the surjective `S.levelHom K`
  identifies subrepresentations of the inflation with those of `B`.
- Unitarity of `B`: `FiniteRepresentation.isUnitary_of_equiv` transfers an invariant form to
  the inflation. Surjectivity of `S.toLevel K` makes it invariant under `W_{K/F}`.
- Association: continuity is `hB`, and the rank follows from `e`. On the intersection of the
  cofinite set of the absolute association with that of `hrel K`, the absolute matching is
  transported to `S.inflate K B` by `IsUnramifiedMatchAt.of_equiv`. Existence of local maps at
  each `w'` comes from `hrel`. For every local-to-relative map `ι'` at `w'`, `hlift` gives `θ'`
  and `y` with `S.toLevel K (θ' g) = y * ι' g * y⁻¹`. Triviality on inertia and the Euler
  polynomial transfer through this conjugation, the latter by
  `WeilMapSystem.eulerPolynomial_representation_conj`.
- Expansion: the absolute expansion, `localExactClass_eq_of_equiv` for `e` and
  `localExactClass_inflate` for `x` give the relative expansion along `ι`.

## Comparison of lattices

For a layer `K`, `relative_pairedLattice_le`, `relative_weilImage_le` and
`relative_automorphicImage_le` show that the relative lattice at `K` lies in the absolute
one, under the inputs `hK`, `hθ` and `hlift` (at `v` and `K`) of `exists_ofRelative`. Each
proof checks generators with `Submodule.span_le`.

`iSup_relative_pairedLattice_le_of_statements` and its Weil and automorphic versions show that
the join over all finite Galois layers of the relative lattices lies in the absolute one. Their
hypotheses are `RelativeWeilGroup.IsArithmeticClassFamily c` and the published
`WeilLevelSurjectiveStatement`, `LocalWeilMapExistenceStatement` and
`LocalWeilMapLiftStatement`. These statements are hypotheses here and are not proved.

`pairedLattice_le_iSup_relative`, `weilImage_le_iSup_relative` and
`automorphicImage_le_iSup_relative` give the converse inclusions under the hypotheses
`hfact`, `hsurj`, `hlift` and `hrel` of `exists_relative`. Adding `hθ`, the theorems
`pairedLattice_eq_iSup_relative`, `weilImage_eq_iSup_relative` and
`automorphicImage_eq_iSup_relative` identify each absolute lattice with the join of the
relative ones.

`pairedLattice_eq_iSup_relative_of_statements` and its two companions derive the same
equalities from the following hypotheses:

- `IsArithmeticClassFamily c`;
- `WeilLevelSurjectiveStatement`, `LocalWeilMapExistenceStatement` and
  `LocalWeilMapLiftStatement`;
- `WeilRepresentationFactorizationStatement` at the carrier universe `uW`;
- the place-wise existence hypothesis `hrel`.

## Scope

All hypotheses of the comparison theorems are explicit. This module proves none of the
following:

- the existence statements for the absolute Weil group (`AbsoluteWeilGroupStatement`);
- the factorization statement;
- the existence of local-to-relative Weil maps used in `hrel`;
- Henniart's Theorem 2.4.

No generator, unitary exact expansion or unitary supercuspidal support is asserted to exist.
As in the relative module, the following remain separate obligations:

- global automorphic unitarity;
- existence and uniqueness of the local support and of the expansion;
- independence of the lattice elements from the chosen witnesses;
- isometry and injectivity of the projections;
- Henniart's isomorphism `R_G(F, v₀) ≅ R_A(F, v₀)` (Theorem 2.5).

The tensor product on `R_G(F_{v₀})` is not built here.
