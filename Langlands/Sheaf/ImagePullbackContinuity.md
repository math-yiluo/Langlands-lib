# Continuity of functors whose images of some squares are pullbacks

Author: Worker 26. Builds only on Mathlib:

- `CategoryTheory.Limits.IsPullback` with `IsPullback.lift`, `IsPullback.lift_fst`,
  `IsPullback.lift_snd`, `IsPullback.of_hasPullback` and `Functor.map_isPullback`.
- `Precoverage`, `Precoverage.comap`, `Precoverage.toGrothendieck` (the Grothendieck topology
  generated by a precoverage, whose covering sieves are given by the inductive predicate
  `Precoverage.Saturate`), and `Precoverage.generate_mem_toGrothendieck`.
- `CompatiblePreserving`, `CoverPreserving`, `Sieve.functorPushforward_top`,
  `Sieve.generate_le_iff`, and `Functor.isContinuous_of_coverPreserving` (a functor that is
  compatible-preserving and cover-preserving is continuous).

All declarations are in the namespace `Langlands.Sheaf`.

## Purpose

Let `F : C ⥤ D` be a functor, and let `J` and `K` be precoverages on `C` and `D` such that
`F` sends `J`-coverings to `K`-coverings. Mathlib's criterion for continuity of `F` for the
generated topologies is that `F` is compatible-preserving and cover-preserving. A sufficient
condition for both is that every cospan of `C` has a commutative square over it whose image
under `F` is a pullback in `D`. This module defines that condition and proves the
consequences. Neither `C` nor `D` needs to have pullbacks.

The intended application is the forgetful functor `Qét(X(G)) ⥤ Qét(X)` of a `G`-space `X`
(see `Langlands.Sheaf.Equivariant.AnalyticInvariantSheaf`). There continuity says that
Berkovich's morphism `b : X → X(G)` is a morphism of sites.

## Setting

`C : Type u₁` with `[Category.{v₁} C]` and `D : Type u₂` with `[Category.{v₂} D]`.

## Definition

- `HasImagePullbacks F : Prop`, for `F : C ⥤ D`: for all objects `U V W` of `C` and morphisms
  `f : V ⟶ U`, `g : W ⟶ U`, there are an object `P` and morphisms `p₁ : P ⟶ V`, `p₂ : P ⟶ W`
  with `p₁ ≫ f = p₂ ≫ g` such that
  `IsPullback (F.map p₁) (F.map p₂) (F.map f) (F.map g)`.

## Results

- `HasImagePullbacks.of_hasPullbacks F`: if `C` has pullbacks and `F` preserves limits of
  shape `WalkingCospan`, then `HasImagePullbacks F`. The squares are the pullback squares of
  `C`. This lemma only confirms that the definition can be satisfied. It is not used in the
  application, where `C` is not known to have pullbacks.

Let `hF : HasImagePullbacks F`.

- `hF.compatiblePreserving K`, for any Grothendieck topology `K` on `D`:
  `CompatiblePreserving.{w} K F`. Proof: let `f₁ : Z ⟶ F.obj Y₁` and `f₂ : Z ⟶ F.obj Y₂` satisfy
  `f₁ ≫ F.map g₁ = f₂ ≫ F.map g₂`, where `g₁` and `g₂` are morphisms of the family. Take the
  square `p₁, p₂` over `g₁, g₂` from `hF`. Then `f₁` and `f₂` factor through `F.obj P` by a
  common morphism `m` (`IsPullback.lift`), so `f₁ = m ≫ F.map p₁` and `f₂ = m ≫ F.map p₂`.
  The restrictions of the compatible family along `p₁` and `p₂` agree because
  `p₁ ≫ g₁ = p₂ ≫ g₂`. Restricting further along `m` gives the claim.
- `hF.coverPreserving J K hJK`, for precoverages `J` on `C` and `K` on `D` with
  `hJK : J ≤ K.comap F`:
  `CoverPreserving J.toGrothendieck K.toGrothendieck F`. Proof by induction on
  `Precoverage.Saturate`, which generates the covering sieves of `J.toGrothendieck`.
  - Generating case: the sieve generated by a `J`-covering `R`. Its pushforward contains the
    sieve generated by the image presieve `R.map F`, which is a `K`-covering by `hJK`.
  - Maximal sieve: its pushforward is the maximal sieve (`Sieve.functorPushforward_top`).
  - Pullback of `S` along `f : V ⟶ U`: the pullback of the pushforward of `S` along `F.map f`
    is a covering sieve by induction and stability under pullback. It is contained in the
    pushforward of the pulled-back sieve. An arrow `h` with `h ≫ F.map f = k ≫ F.map s`, for
    `s` in `S`, factors through the image of the square `p₁, p₂` over `f, s` given by `hF`. Its
    leg `p₁` lies in the pulled-back sieve, because `p₁ ≫ f = p₂ ≫ s` lies in `S`.
  - Transitivity: by the transitivity axiom of `K.toGrothendieck`, using that pushforwards of
    composites are composites of pushforwards.
- `hF.isContinuous J K hJK`:
  `F.IsContinuous J.toGrothendieck K.toGrothendieck`, by
  `Functor.isContinuous_of_coverPreserving` from the two previous results. Precomposition with
  `F` then sends `K.toGrothendieck`-sheaves to `J.toGrothendieck`-sheaves.

## Provenance

Derived. Mathlib's `Functor.isContinuous_of_coverPreserving` is the continuity criterion for
functors between sites. The condition `HasImagePullbacks` and the two lemmas are ours. They
are a variant of a classical fact. The Stacks Project (Definition 7.13.1, tag 00WV) calls a
functor `u` between sites continuous if it sends coverings `{V_i → V}` to coverings and
`u(T ×_V V_i) → u(T) ×_{u(V)} u(V_i)` is an isomorphism. Lemma 7.13.2 (tag 00WW) shows that
precomposition with such a functor sends sheaves to sheaves. Here no fibre products are
required in `C`, and only those fibre products in `D` that are images of squares of `C` are
used. In the application, `Qét(X)` has the fibre products of Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for
non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), Proposition 1.4.1. It is not known in the Library
whether `Qét(X(G))` has fibre products. Berkovich's 1996 manuscript (§1.2, printed p.3) says
that the morphism `ϕ : X'(G') → X(G)` gives a morphism of sites "in the evident way", without
proof. The exact source passages are quoted in `AnalyticInvariantSheaf.md`.
