# Stalks of tensor products of lisse sheaves

Module `Langlands.Sheaf.LisseTensorStalk`. Let `Z` be a scheme and `s : Spec K ⟶ Z` a point with
values in a field. Write `Φ = Γ(Spec K, s^* -)` for weil-conjectures's stalk functor
`AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.stalkFunctor l s` on sheaves of `ℚ̄_ℓ`-modules, with values in modules
over `ULift ℚ̄_ℓ` (`AlgebraicGeometry.WeilCoefficients`). Its lax monoidal structure combines
monoidal pullback with lax monoidal global sections. For `ℓ`-adic sheaves `G` and `H` (modules over
the constant sheaf `ℚ̄_ℓ`), `G ⊗_{ℚ̄_ℓ} H` is weil-conjectures's relative tensor product
`(CategoryTheory.Mod.tensorLeft H).obj G`, a quotient of the tensor product of the underlying
sheaves by `CategoryTheory.Mod.tensorπ G H`.

## The comparison

- `AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.tensorStalkMap s G H : Φ G ⊗ Φ H ⟶ Φ (G ⊗_{ℚ̄_ℓ} H)` is the tensor
  comparison `μ` of `Φ` followed by `Φ (tensorπ G H)`.
- `tensorStalkMap_naturality` and `tensorStalkMap_naturality_right` give naturality in both
  sheaves, using `Mod.tensorLeft` in the first variable and `Mod.tensorLeftMap` in the second.
- `evaluationPairing_unit` expresses the comparison as the evaluation pairing (weil-conjectures's
  `CategoryTheory.Mod.evaluationPairing`) of the stalk of the unit
  `G ⟶ 𝓗om(H, G ⊗_{ℚ̄_ℓ} H)` of the adjunction `- ⊗_{ℚ̄_ℓ} H ⊣ 𝓗om(H, -)` with the stalk of `H`.
  This is the triangle identity for the adjunction.

## Isomorphism for lisse sheaves

- `isIso_tensorStalkMap_unit` gives an isomorphism for `H = ℚ̄_ℓ`. Followed by
  `G ⊗_{ℚ̄_ℓ} ℚ̄_ℓ ≅ G` (`Mod.tensorLeftRegularHom`) it is the action of the stalk of `ℚ̄_ℓ` on the
  stalk of `G`, by `Mod.tensorπ_tensorLeftRegularHom_app` and the compatibility of `Φ` with the
  braiding (`μ_comp_map_braiding_upperStar_comp_Γ`). The stalk of `ℚ̄_ℓ` is `ℚ̄_ℓ`, generated by the
  unit section (`unitSection`, an isomorphism by `isIso_unitSection`), which acts as the
  identity (`stalkFunctor_act_unitSection`). So the composite with `Φ G ◁ unitSection` is the
  right unitor.
- `isIso_tensorStalkMap_of_iso` and `isIso_tensorStalkMap_biproduct` extend the isomorphism to an
  isomorphic sheaf and to a finite direct sum `⨁ H i` once it holds on every summand. The inverse
  on a direct sum is assembled from the
  projections and inclusions, using naturality in `H` and the additivity of `Mod.tensorLeftMap`.
- For `f : V ⟶ Z` and `t : Spec K ⟶ V`, `isIso_tensorStalkMap_comp_iff` gives that the comparison
  at `t ≫ f` for `G`, `H` is an isomorphism exactly when the comparison at `t` for `f^* G`, `f^* H`
  is one. The stalk functor at `t ≫ f` is the stalk functor at `t` after `f^*`, through a
  monoidal natural isomorphism, and `f^*` commutes with the relative tensor product compatibly
  with the quotient maps (`adicUpperStarTensorLeftIso`,
  `Mod.map_tensorπ_comp_tensorLeftCompMapModComapIso_hom`).
- `isIso_tensorStalkMap` gives the isomorphism for every `G` when `K` is algebraically closed and
  `H` is lisse. Write `s = 𝟙 ≫ s` and pull back along `s`. On `Spec K` the lisse
  sheaf `s^* H` is a finite direct sum of copies of `ℚ̄_ℓ`
  (`IsAdicLisseSheaf.exists_iso_biproduct_of_isAlgClosed`), and the three previous facts apply.

## Lisseness

- `tensorLeftFlip P` is the additive functor `M ↦ P ⊗_{ℚ̄_ℓ} M`.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.IsAdicLisseSheaf.tensorLeft` proves lisseness of the tensor product
  of two lisse sheaves. Take a pro-étale cover trivialising `H`. On a member `g`,
  `g^*(G ⊗ H) ≅ g^* G ⊗ g^* H ≅ g^* G ⊗ ℚ̄_ℓⁿ ≅ (g^* G)ⁿ`. This uses `adicUpperStarTensorLeftIso`,
  `tensorLeftFlip` preserving biproducts and `Mod.tensorLeftRegularIso`. That sheaf is lisse
  (`IsAdicLisseSheaf.biproduct_const`), and lisseness is local (`IsAdicLisseSheaf.of_cover`).

## Monodromy

- `AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.LisseMonodromy.mono_tensorStalkMap` gives compatibility with
  transport for a monodromy `M` of lisse sheaves on `Y` and a path `τ : fiber y ≅ fiber z`
  between geometric points. For lisse `G`, `H` and `G ⊗_{ℚ̄_ℓ} H`, the transport `M.mono τ`
  carries `tensorStalkMap y G H (g ⊗ h)` to `tensorStalkMap z G H (τ g ⊗ τ h)`.
  The internal hom of lisse sheaves is lisse
  (`IsAdicLisseSheaf.ihom`), so the unit `G ⟶ 𝓗om(H, G ⊗ H)` is a morphism of lisse sheaves.
  Transport is natural in lisse sheaves and preserves evaluation pairings (the field
  `mono_evaluationPairing` of `LisseMonodromy`), and the comparison is an evaluation pairing by
  `evaluationPairing_unit`.

## Scalars

The stalks of the lisse monodromy (`LisseMonodromy.StalkSpace`) are the underlying `ℚ̄_ℓ`-vector
spaces of modules over `ULift ℚ̄_ℓ` (`WeilCoefficients.toModuleCat`).
`AlgebraicGeometry.WeilCoefficients.tensorRestrict M N` is the canonical `ℚ̄_ℓ`-linear map
`M ⊗_{ℚ̄_ℓ} N → M ⊗_{ULift ℚ̄_ℓ} N`. In `tensorRestrict_bijective`, its inverse is lifted from
the balanced map `(m, n) ↦ m ⊗ n`. `ofToModuleCat` and `toToModuleCat`
are the identity maps between the carriers of the two module structures.

All declarations depend only on `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.
