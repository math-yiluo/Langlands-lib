Renard uses complex coefficients on a totally disconnected space. Here the ring can be noncommutative and the space has any supplied topology; that extension is an internal generalization because local constancy and pointwise ring operations need neither commutativity nor topological separation properties. The original specialization is `ringPresheaf X ℂ` on Renard's space X.
