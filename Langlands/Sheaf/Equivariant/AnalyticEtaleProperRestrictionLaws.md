# Laws of proper restriction of analytic étale sections

Étale restriction is precomposition with the functor on test objects. The maps of over-categories give canonical comparisons for identity and composition. At the identity test, a global section is restricted by the unique map to the terminal test object. Consequently the identity and composite comparisons commute with these restrictions.

`etaleCompactSectionsRestriction_id` and `etaleCompactSectionsRestriction_comp` give the corresponding identities on compactly supported sections. Here the étale maps are proper on the underlying Hausdorff analytic spaces and the chosen geometric point fields have surjective underlying point projections. Both identities follow from ordinary restriction by the inclusion of compactly supported sections. Different fields in a common universe may be used at the three spaces in the composition law.

This is a consequence of postcomposition on the ordinary analytic étale site and the compact-support construction of Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §§4.1 and 5.1.
