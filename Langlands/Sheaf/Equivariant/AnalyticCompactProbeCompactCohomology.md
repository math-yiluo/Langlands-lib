# Compactly supported cohomology for the compact-probe quasi-étale topology

The field-valued points are the actual points `QuasiEtaleSite.compactProbeGeometricPoint L` of the compact-probe site. They carry the topology induced by projection to the analytic space $|X|$. When $|X|$ is Hausdorff and the projection is surjective, `compactProbe_isCompactlySupported_iff` identifies compact support with compactness of the closure in $|X|$ of the image of the nonzero germ locus. The resulting ordinary compactly supported section functor includes into the global sections functor. If $|X|$ is compact and the projection surjective, the inclusion is an isomorphism.

Translations by the action of $G$ identify the appropriate fibres of the field-valued points and act by homeomorphisms on their index space. The functor `compactProbeQuasiEtaleGSheafCompactSections` uses these identifications to take the smooth part of the representation on compactly supported sections. The degreewise functors `compactProbeQuasiEtaleCompactCohomology` and `compactProbeQuasiEtaleGSheafCompactCohomology` derive compactly supported sections in module sheaves and compact-probe $G$-sheaves respectively. The equivariant functor uses finite equivariant refinements, supplied by `compactProbe_hasFiniteEquivariantRefinements` for Hausdorff analytic spaces with a continuous action. The two compactly supported complexes realize these functors as the nonnegative cohomology of the corresponding bounded below derived objects. `compactProbeQuasiEtaleCompactComparison` compares smooth compactly supported cohomology with ordinary compactly supported cohomology after restriction along untwisted arrows.

The coefficient ring is commutative in universe `max u (v + 1) uL`. For the equivariant derived functors the group is small in that universe. A complete algebraically closed point field surjective on ordinary points exists by `commonAlgebraicallyClosedPointField`.

Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces* (1996), §1.5, pp. 11–12, defines compact support using the germ support and derives the smooth compactly supported sections. These constructions apply the same operations on the compact-probe quasi-étale site with the stated field-valued point family. The Lean comparison is a morphism. The isomorphism assertion of Corollary 1.5.2 is a further theorem and its source is on the étale site.
