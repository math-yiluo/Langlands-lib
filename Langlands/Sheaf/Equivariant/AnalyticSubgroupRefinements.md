# Finite equivariant refinements for an open subgroup

This is an internal consequence of the published twisted analytic categories,
action lifts, and finite equivariant refinement condition. Let a topological
group `G` act through `ρ` on an analytic space `X`, and let `H` be an open
subgroup. Fix a morphism property `P` respecting isomorphisms, a
translation-invariant Grothendieck topology `J` on `P.Over ⊤ X`, and a
translation-invariant predicate `Adm` on its objects. If the lift system for
`G` has finite equivariant refinements, the lift system for the restricted
action of `H` also has them.

`LocalActionSystem.FiniteEquivariantRefinement` is a data structure for a
general local action system. For an action `a` on `U` and a sieve `S` on `U`,
it stores a natural number, that many objects, open acting subgroups, local
actions, and arrows to `U`. Its proof fields state that each action is
admissible, each arrow belongs to `S` and is locally equivariant with `a`,
and the arrows generate a covering sieve.

The analytic construction uses three helpers.

- `untwist_mem_of_functorPushforward` starts with a sieve on the plain
  over-category, extends it to the twisted category, and shows that untwisting
  an arrow in this extension gives an arrow in the original sieve. A
  factorization through an original sieve arrow can itself be untwisted; sieve
  downward closure then gives the result.
- `covering_untwist_family` shows that untwisting every arrow of a covering
  family gives a covering family in the plain topology. A plain arrow in the
  original covering sieve factors through one of the twisted arrows.
  Composing the first factor with the corresponding relabelling has twist
  one, so it is the image of a plain arrow. This gives containment in the
  sieve generated by the untwisted family.
- `isLocallyEquivariant_untwist_subgroup` treats a lift on a target for `H`
  and a lift on a source for `G`, with both local actions possibly restricted
  to smaller open subgroups. The target action is viewed as a `G` action by
  mapping its acting subgroup into `G`. Suppose a twisted arrow `f` is
  locally equivariant for these actions. Set `t` to its twist. Taking the
  entire source acting subgroup in the local equivariance condition gives
  an open target subgroup and an intertwining element `k` for each of its
  elements `h`. Comparing twists forces `k = t⁻¹ h t`. Intersecting the
  inverse image of that open target subgroup in `H` with the original
  target acting subgroup gives a common open subgroup. The translated
  source lift restricts to `H` on this subgroup, and the underlying-arrow
  equality is exactly ordinary intertwining for the untwisted arrow.
  `isLocallyEquivariant_of_intertwines` gives the required local
  equivariance on the `H` site.

`nonempty_subgroupFiniteEquivariantRefinement` constructs the refinement
data. It views the given admissible `H` action as a `G` action using
`liftOfSubgroup`. It pulls the given sieve back to the plain category and
extends that sieve to the `G` twisted category; this extension covers because
it contains the original plain covering sieve. A finite equivariant
`G` refinement is then available by hypothesis. For each of its arrows,
the construction translates its source by the arrow's twist, translates
its action lift, restricts the translated lift to `H`, and uses the
untwisted arrow. Translation invariance of `Adm` proves admissibility.
The three helpers prove membership, local equivariance, and coverage.

`subgroupFiniteEquivariantRefinement` chooses this finite refinement data
from the proved nonempty type. `hasFiniteEquivariantRefinements_subgroup`
forgets the package to obtain the published refinement class for `H`.

`hasFiniteEquivariantRefinements_compactLiftSystem_subgroup` specializes to
the quasi-étale topology and `Adm V := IsCompactSpace V.left`.
Translation leaves the underlying analytic space unchanged, so this
admissibility condition is translation invariant. The result needs only
the full-group refinement hypothesis, an open subgroup, and the
topological-group structure. It has no group-smallness or coefficient-ring
hypothesis and assumes neither compactness of `X` nor continuity of `ρ`.

The provenance is internal: the construction uses the definitions of
`HasFiniteEquivariantRefinements`, `liftSystem`, `ActionLift.translate`,
`liftOfSubgroup`, `liftToSubgroup`, and the twisted-site translation and
sieve operations. It supplies a subgroup refinement prerequisite for the
quasi-étale cohomology comparison; it does not prove injective acyclicity
or the global comparison theorem.
