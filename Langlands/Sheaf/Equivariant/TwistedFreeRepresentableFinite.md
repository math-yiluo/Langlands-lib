# Finite sums of discrete lifted free representables

This internal construction uses supplied whole-group lifts with explicit
eventual equivariance. The site has ordinary and twisted module sheafification,
translation invariance, finite equivariant refinements, and the pointwise right
Kan extensions required for the ordinary restriction comparison.

`discreteFreeSheafSum` is the actual finite coproduct of the supplied discrete
free representable sheaves. Since ordinary restriction preserves finite
colimits, `discreteFreeSheafSumRestrictionIso` identifies its restriction with
the finite coproduct of the actual ordinary free representable sheaves.
The injection formula specifies this comparison on every summand.

An index map and equivariant ordinary arrows induce
`discreteFreeSheafSumMap`. Its restriction square identifies it with the
ordinary coproduct map generated by those same ordinary arrows. The proof
checks the square on coproduct injections, using the individual restriction
squares from `TwistedFreeRepresentableMaps`.

`discreteFreeSheafSumLift` lifts a finite family of fixed sections to a morphism
in the discrete sheaf category. Its restriction theorem identifies the
resulting ordinary morphism with the coproduct of the ordinary represented
sections, using the individual fixed-section restriction theorem.

The provenance is the universal property of finite coproducts and the existing
individual lifted-representable constructions. All actions, eventual
equivariance and arrow equivariance remain inputs. No exact three-term model,
analytic existence or global comparison is asserted.
