For a `G`-space `X`, inverse image along `b : X → X(G)` induces an
equivalence from sheaves on `X(G)_qét` to quasi-étale `G`-sheaves on `X`.
Steps A.1–A.3 compute `b^*E` on a compact quasi-étale `U → X` using the
filtered colimit over induced spaces `U_{G/N}` for open subgroups `N`
of a group lifting the action to `U`. Each section consequently has an
open stabilizer. Steps A.4–A.5 give the two isomorphisms for the
inverse functor `(b_*-)^G`, which takes `F` to the sheaf with value
`F(V)^G` at the equivariant object `V(G)`.

## Lean statement

In `Langlands.Sheaf.Equivariant.Analytic`, the proposition
`CompactProbeQuasiEtaleGSheafToposComparisonStatement X R hX` asserts that the
specified adjunction induces the equivalence, in two steps.

1. `CompactProbeQuasiEtaleInverseImageDiscrete X R` says that the published inverse
   image `compactProbeQuasiEtaleInverseImage` takes each module sheaf to a
   `CompactProbeQuasiEtaleGSheaf`. Sections over every compact quasi-étale test object
   are discrete for the local actions in `compactLiftSystem`.
2. With that property, `compactProbeQuasiEtaleDiscreteInverseImage` factors `b^*`
   through the full subcategory. The adjunction
   `compactProbeQuasiEtaleDiscreteInverseImageAdjunction` restricts the published
   adjunction with invariant sheaves to that subcategory. The proposition
   requires its unit and counit to be isomorphisms, giving the canonical
   maps `E → (b_*b^*E)^G` and `b^*((b_*F)^G) → F`.

The analytic spaces are over a fixed complete ultrametric field
`K : Type u`. `X : GSpace.{u,v,v} K G` includes Hausdorffness and
continuity of the analytic action by automorphisms over `K`. The source
also permits automorphisms acting on the base field. The equivariant topology is generated by families that cover after
forgetting the action, tested against compact Hausdorff probes. The
twisted topology tests untwisted arrows by the same covering rule.
`compactProbeQuasiEtaleHausdorffComparison` gives the chart-neighbourhood
criterion on the Hausdorff underlying equivariant objects. The compact
probe formulation supplies the finite refinement test on the objects
used in A.2. The
assertion concerns sheaves of modules over the single commutative ring
`R : Type (max u (v + 1) z uL)`, with `G : Type z`. The module universe
accommodates the comma-category colimits defining inverse image. Taking
an integer coefficient ring gives the abelian-sheaf case. The general
set-valued equivalence is another part of the manuscript.

The explicit hypothesis `hX : HasImagePullbacks
(EquivariantQuasiEtaleSite.toQuasiEtaleSite X)` supplies image pullbacks
used for continuity of the comparison functors. Each cospan in the
equivariant quasi-étale site has a square whose image is a pullback in
the ordinary quasi-étale site. The proposition also assumes
`OpenQuotientsSmall.{v,z}` as in the étale case. This requires each
`G ⧸ N` for `N` open to be `v`-small so the coset-indexed induced spaces
used in A.1–A.5 fit in the geometric site. For compact `G` the coset
sets are finite. The hypotheses include no compactness condition on
`G` or on `X`.

The categorical restriction to discrete sheaves is implemented in the
module. The inverse image's discreteness and the two isomorphism clauses
are the conclusion of the proposition. The induced-object and filtered-colimit arguments above explain the claim
for the compact-probe comparison.
