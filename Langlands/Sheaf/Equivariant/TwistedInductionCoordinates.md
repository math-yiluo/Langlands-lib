# Product coordinates for twisted induction

This internal categorical construction computes the actual right Kan extension used by `TwistedOver.induction`. It derives its indexing family from `TwistedOver.translate` and `TwistedOver.relabelInv`; no analytic, support or cohomological vanishing hypothesis is used.

For a twisted object U, the coordinate indexed by g is the ordinary object translated by g, with its inverse relabelling arrow to U. The corresponding arrow in the opposite category is an object of the structured-arrow category defining pointwise right Kan extension. Any such object d receives a unique arrow from the coordinate indexed by the inverse of the twist of its underlying arrow. The ordinary factor has the same underlying map as d, and its structure-map identity follows by cancelling the action of the twist. Equality of the twists forces the coordinate index; equality of underlying arrows gives uniqueness.

Consequently the discrete coordinate family is initial: every costructured-arrow category is nonempty, and any two objects are joined by the unique map over equality of their indices. This is a categorical prerequisite for the compact-acyclicity proof and by itself makes no vanishing claim.

For any coefficient category A with pointwise right Kan extensions along the opposite inclusion, `objects ρ F U g` is exactly F evaluated on the ordinary translate gU. `projection` applies the actual right Kan extension to the inverse relabelling and then the actual Kan counit. `fanIsLimit` proves these projections are a limiting fan: whisker the pointwise Kan limit cone by the initial family, identify its discrete diagram with the translated objects, and transport the limit along the identity-component diagram isomorphism.

When A has products indexed by G, `productIso` identifies the actual Kan value with the categorical product of these objects. The two projection identities specify both directions of this isomorphism. `sheafProductIso` is the same isomorphism for the published `induction` functor, whose underlying presheaf is the actual right Kan extension; its proof is the presheaf comparison itself.

The underlying category's object and hom universes, the group universe, and A's object and hom universes remain independent. The morphism property must respect isomorphisms, and the sheaf comparison additionally uses a translation-invariant Grothendieck topology. The pointwise Kan existence hypothesis is explicit for every A-valued ordinary presheaf. Product existence is only needed for the chosen categorical product comparison, not for the limiting-fan construction. These are generic categorical definitions and derived proofs, based on Mathlib's structured arrows, initial functors, limits and right Kan extensions and the existing twisted-site constructions; they make no analytic or cohomological assertion.
