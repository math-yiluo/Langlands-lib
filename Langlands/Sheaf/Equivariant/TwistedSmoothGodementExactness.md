# Exactness of the locally discrete Godement functor on a twisted site

Let $P$ be a morphism property respecting isomorphisms, let a group act on $X$, and give `P.Over ⊤ X` a translation-invariant topology $J$. For a fixed commutative ring $R$ in universe `w`, use the category `ModuleCat.{w} R` and a family indexed by `I : Type w` with values in `J.Point.{w}`. Assume pointwise right Kan extensions of ordinary module presheaves along `(ι P ρ).op` and the admissible refinements for the local action system on the twisted site. The locally discrete Godement functor is the composite of the locally discrete inclusion, restriction, ordinary degree-zero Godement, twisted induction, and the locally discrete coreflector.

The proof uses module sheafification on both sites for preservation of epimorphisms. The point fibers of the underlying ordinary sheaf preserve epimorphisms. The natural isomorphism `restrictedSmoothGodementPointProductIso` identifies the ordinary restriction of the resulting Godement term with the actual restricted smooth point product of these fibers. Exactness of that product gives preservation of epimorphisms after restriction. Faithfulness of the inclusion and restriction detects epimorphisms. For left exactness, the twisted sheafification gives the needed structures on locally discrete sheaves, and `LocallySmall.{w} (P.Over ⊤ X)` ensures the point fibers preserve finite limits. The inclusion and restriction preserve finite limits, and the point product, induction, and coreflection are right adjoints. The composite is additive and preserves homology and finite colimits. On the ordinary analytic étale site these instances apply once the same size and local-action requirements are supplied.

The source is Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces*, §1.5, construction (a) and property (ii), p. 10. Harris, *Galois properties of cohomological automorphic forms on GL(n)* (1999), §2, p. 304, uses the smooth equivariant resolution and states exactness in Fact 3.
