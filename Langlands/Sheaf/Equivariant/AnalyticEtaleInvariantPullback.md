# Equivariant pullback along an invariant analytic étale map

Composition with an étale map of analytic bases gives a continuous functor on their ordinary étale sites. To prove preservation of pullback squares, a morphism between étale test objects is étale: its graph is a section of the étale pullback projection, hence an open immersion. The underlying localized pullbacks therefore exist and give the needed squares. Postcomposition preserves jointly surjective families because their underlying maps are unchanged.

For a group acting on the source and an invariant map to the target, every twisted arrow is an ordinary arrow over the target. `invariantEtaleSheafPullback` evaluates the given sheaf on these composed structure maps. The sheaf condition follows from continuity on untwisted arrows. For a discrete group, `invariantEtaleGSheafPullback` has locally discrete values if the distinguished lifted objects cover. Restriction to untwisted arrows is naturally ordinary étale pullback. This supplies a coefficient pullback to analytic normal-level actions. The site and locally discrete sheaf conventions are from Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces* (1996), §§1.2 and 1.4, and the ordinary étale covering convention is from *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §4.1.
