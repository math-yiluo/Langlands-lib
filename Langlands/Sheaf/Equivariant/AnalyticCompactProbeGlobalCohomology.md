# Derived global sections for the compact-probe quasi-étale topology

For the ordinary compact-probe site, global sections of an $R$-module sheaf are its value at the identity of the analytic space $X$. For a compact-probe $G$-sheaf, global sections carry a representation by restriction along inverse elements of $G$. The equivariant functor takes its smooth part. `compactProbeQuasiEtaleGlobalInclusion` is the inclusion of these smooth vectors into the ordinary sections of the restriction along untwisted arrows.

`compactProbe_hasFiniteEquivariantRefinements` supplies the refinement hypothesis used to construct injective resolutions among the $G$-sheaves. For a Hausdorff $X$ and a continuous action, the finite compact refinement theorem supplies compact Hausdorff members covering the given compact object. Each member admits an analytic lift, and lift compatibility gives the intertwining of each arrow over an open subgroup. After restriction of the twisted sieve to untwisted arrows, the covering claim follows from the ordinary compact-probe refinement. This argument uses `compactProbeQuasiEtaleCompactRefinements`, `quasiEtaleActionLift`, and `quasiEtaleLiftCompatibility`.

The ordinary and twisted sites are essentially small in the coefficient universe `max u (v + 1) uL`, with $G$ small in that universe for the twisted site. Mathlib's sheafification and Grothendieck abelian category theorems supply enough injectives for module sheaves. The compact-lift discreteness category then inherits enough injectives using the finite equivariant refinements. The degreewise functors `compactProbeQuasiEtaleCohomology` and `compactProbeQuasiEtaleGSheafCohomology` are right derived sections. The two zero-degree isomorphisms follow from left exactness. `compactProbeQuasiEtaleGlobalComparison` derives the inclusion through the exact restriction and the exact forgetful functor from smooth representations.

The bounded below complexes `compactProbeQuasiEtaleGlobalComplex` and `compactProbeQuasiEtaleGSheafGlobalComplex` retain the derived objects in modules and smooth representations respectively. The homology isomorphisms identify their cohomology in nonnegative degrees with the corresponding degreewise functors. The coefficient ring is commutative. The constructions allow an arbitrary complete nonarchimedean ground field, with the compact-probe topology fixed throughout.

Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces* (1996), §1.2 describes quasi-étale coverings by forgetting the action, §1.4 defines the discrete section condition, and Theorem 1.6.1(ii), p. 13, describes the global cohomology comparison for compact spaces. The Lean constructions instantiate the ordinary and smooth global sections with the compact-probe rule and construct the comparison morphism. The isomorphism assertion of Theorem 1.6.1(ii) is a further theorem.
