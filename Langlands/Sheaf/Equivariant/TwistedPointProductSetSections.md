# Compact lifts for actual locally discrete induced point products

This internal categorical and support construction composes the existing point-product set sections with the actual twisted right Kan induction, locally discrete coreflector, inclusion, and ordinary-site restriction. It is derived from `PointProduct.setSection`, `InductionCoordinates.smoothSheafInductionUnderlyingMap`, and `PointSupport.sheafCompactSections_map_surjective_of_setSection`. It makes no external analytic attribution.

`smoothPointProductFunctor` is the composite of `PointProduct.productFunctor`, `TwistedOver.induction`, and `LocalActionSystem.smoothCoreflector`. `restrictedSmoothPointProductFunctor` then applies the existing smooth-sheaf inclusion and `TwistedOver.restriction`. Thus its objects and linear maps are the actual functor objects and maps, rather than a new coordinate model.

For a map of independent module coefficients that is surjective at every index, `restrictedSmoothPointProductSetSection` first uses a chosen zero-preserving right inverse of each coefficient map in the product's actual section coordinates. The existing point-product construction makes this a natural transformation of underlying set presheaves. The underlying-map induction construction transports this natural transformation through the actual Kan extension and then the actual locally discrete submodule; precomposition with the ordinary-site inclusion supplies the displayed restricted natural transformation. No additivity of this chosen section is asserted.

`restrictedSmoothPointProductSetSection_zero` applies zero preservation through induction and the locally discrete part. `restrictedSmoothPointProduct_map_setSection` first proves the raw point-product right-inverse identity, transports it through induction and the locally discrete part, and uses the explicit linear-map comparison to identify the resulting forward map with the map of the actual composite functor. Its order says that applying the chosen lift followed by the original linear map returns the input section. `restrictedSmoothPointProduct_map_app_surjective` consequently gives surjectivity on every ordinary section module.

For any topological family of support points on the ordinary site, `restrictedSmoothPointProduct_compactSections_map_surjective` applies the existing support transport theorem to this natural zero-preserving section. That theorem detects a zero germ by a local zero restriction in the filtered fiber diagram, uses naturality and zero preservation to preserve that local witness, and concludes that the lifted section's germ support is contained in the original support. The same compact containing set therefore witnesses compact support. These are the actual `PointSupport.sheafCompactSections` modules. The support-point index need not be the coefficient-product index and has no compatibility assumption with it.

The category object and morphism universes, group universe, and coefficient universe are independent. The ring, module carriers, product index, and point fiber universe share the coefficient universe. The hypotheses explicitly include the pointwise right Kan extensions, translation invariance of the ordinary topology, and admissible refinements for the supplied local action system. The compact-support conclusion additionally assumes local smallness in the coefficient universe and a topology on the support-point index. No separation or analytic assumption is needed for this lift construction.

This unit proves section lifting and compact-section lifting. It contains no derived-functor identification or higher-cohomology vanishing result. In particular, exactness on coefficient families does not by itself give acyclicity of these restricted smooth sheaves for right-derived compact sections; acyclicity of the required resolution terms remains a separate obligation for the host task.
