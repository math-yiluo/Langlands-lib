# Actual compact cohomology across ordinary universe lift

This internal module lifts the proved degree-zero compact-section isomorphism
through the actual homeomorphism sheaf equivalence to all right-derived
compact-section functors.

The equivalence is additive, exact and preserves injective objects. It
therefore carries an injective resolution of the original ordinary sheaf to
an injective resolution of its lifted sheaf. The published resolution
comparison, with the identity functor on coefficient modules and the proved
compact-section isomorphism, gives
`underlyingTopologyLiftCompactCohomologyIso`.

The final vanishing-transfer theorem uses that actual isomorphism. No
c-soft acyclicity assertion or analytic higher-direct-image vanishing is
assumed to construct the comparison.
