# Invariants and constant coefficients on the compact-probe site

The space lies in the localized analytic category over a complete ultrametric normed field and carries an action by analytic automorphisms. On its compact-probe quasi-étale site, `compactProbeQuasiEtaleGSheafInvariantSections` takes the invariants in smooth global sections of a module sheaf. The right derived functors of this composite and its bounded-below derived complex are formed in the compact-lift discrete sheaf category. The edge map to the invariants in equivariant cohomology and the degree-zero isomorphism specialize the general exact inclusion construction. The coefficient ring is commutative in universe `max u (v + 1) uL`. Derived invariant cohomology uses a topological group small in that universe and the compact-probe finite equivariant refinement condition. The complex uses a chosen derived category of modules.

`compactProbeQuasiEtaleRestrictionCohomologyRep` equips ordinary compact-probe global cohomology of the restriction with the actual group action. This uses the derived global sections representation on the twisted site and the natural identification of its underlying modules with ordinary derived sections. The representation and this identification require group smallness in the same coefficient universe.

The constant compact-probe `G`-sheaf is the sheafification of the constant module presheaf with arrows of twist `g` acting by `g⁻¹`. The constant functor on smooth representations is left adjoint to smooth sections and preserves monomorphisms. Under the finite equivariant refinements this gives preservation of injectives by smooth sections. The coefficient constructions and their role in derived invariant cohomology follow Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces* (1996), §1.2, pp. 3–5, and §1.4, pp. 7–9, applied to the compact-probe covering rule.
