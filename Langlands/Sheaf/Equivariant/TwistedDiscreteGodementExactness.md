# Exactness of the discrete Godement functor on a twisted site

Let $P$ be a morphism property respecting isomorphisms. Let a group act on $X$, and let $J$ be a translation-invariant topology on the ordinary site `P.Over ⊤ X`. Fix a commutative ring $R$ in universe `w`, use `ModuleCat.{w} R`, and take a family indexed by `I : Type w` with values in `J.Point.{w}`. Assume finite equivariant refinements for the local action system on the twisted site. Induction requires pointwise right Kan extensions of ordinary module presheaves along `(ι P ρ).op`. The degree-zero discrete Godement functor restricts to the ordinary site, takes the product of skyscrapers of the point fibers, induces back to the twisted site, and takes the discrete part. `discreteGodementPointProductIso` exhibits its factorization through the independent point coefficients.

A pointwise coefficient epimorphism has a natural section on the underlying sets of the ordinary product of skyscrapers. Coordinatewise right Kan induction preserves that section and its naturality for twisted arrows. The section thus preserves every local fixedness condition defining the discrete part. This proves surjectivity on every section of the discrete point product. With module sheafification on both the ordinary and twisted sites, the site fibers and restriction preserve epimorphisms, giving preservation of epimorphisms by the Godement functor itself. For left exactness, `LocallySmall.{w} (P.Over ⊤ X)` ensures the site fibers preserve finite limits. The products, restriction, induction, and discrete coreflector also preserve finite limits. Together these facts give preservation of homology and finite colimits.

This is the twisted-site version of exactness for degree-zero equivariant Godement in Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces*, §1.5, construction (a) and property (ii) on p. 10. Harris uses the smooth equivariant resolution in *Galois properties of cohomological automorphic forms on GL(n)* (1999), §2, p. 304. Joint faithfulness of the given point fibers is a separate condition for the Godement coaugmentation to be monic.
