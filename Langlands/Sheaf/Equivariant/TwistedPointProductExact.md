# Exactness of actual restricted locally discrete point products

This internal categorical prerequisite concerns the existing point product, twisted-site right Kan induction, locally discrete coreflector, inclusion, and ordinary-site restriction. It does not assert compact cohomology vanishing.

Fix a category `C`, an isomorphism-respecting morphism property `P`, an object `X`, a topological group `G` acting by `ρ`, a translation-invariant topology `J` on `P.Over ⊤ X`, and a local action system `L` with admissible refinements on the twisted site. The group universe is independent of the coefficient universe. The coefficient ring `R`, module carriers, point index `I`, and the supplied site points have universe `w`. The construction explicitly retains pointwise right Kan extension existence for all ordinary module presheaves.

`coefficientRestrictedSmoothPointProductFunctor` presents independent coefficient modules as functors on `Discrete I`. It first uses the inverse of `piEquivalenceFunctorDiscrete` to recover the family, then applies the existing `restrictedSmoothPointProductFunctor`. Consequently its values and maps are those of the actual point product followed by induction, the locally discrete coreflector, inclusion, and restriction.

With sheafification available on both sites, this functor preserves finite limits. The discrete-diagram equivalence, point product, induction and coreflector preserve limits as equivalences or right adjoints. The existing locally discrete inclusion and ordinary restriction preserve finite limits. The coreflector's right adjoint instance is supplied locally from `smoothAdjunction`. Additivity follows from preservation of binary products between the abelian categories.

For an epimorphism of coefficient diagrams, each component is a surjective module map. `coefficientRestrictedSmoothPointProductFunctor_map_app_surjective` applies the previously constructed natural set section through the actual restricted locally discrete point product. Thus every ordinary section lifts. The resulting componentwise epimorphism of presheaves is an epimorphism, and faithfulness of the sheaf inclusion reflects that epimorphism. Together with finite-limit preservation this proves `coefficientRestrictedSmoothPointProductFunctor_preservesHomology`, using Mathlib's exactness criterion of preserving epimorphisms and kernels.

For an arbitrary topological support-point family `supportPoints`, whose index may be different from `I`, and a coefficient-universe locally small ordinary site, the composite with the actual `PointSupport.sheafCompactSections` also preserves epimorphisms. Its proof uses `restrictedSmoothPointProduct_compactSections_map_surjective`: the natural zero-preserving set section transports local zero-germ witnesses, so its lift has support contained in the original support and retains a compact bound. The compact-sections functor preserves finite limits. Applying the same criterion gives `coefficientRestrictedSmoothPointProductFunctor_compactSections_preservesHomology` for every ordinary object `U`.

These exactness conclusions concern variation of the independent coefficients. They do not show that injective coefficient families map to injective ordinary sheaves, or establish vanishing of the actual right-derived compact-sections functors on these sheaves. In particular the analytic Godement compact-acyclicity target requires a separate acyclicity argument. No compact or global comparison theorem, preservation of injectives by restriction, or unproved Library proposition is used.

The provenance is internal: the new diagram presentation and instances are derived from the existing adjunctions, exact inclusion and restriction, actual compact-sections left exactness, and the natural section-lifting constructions. There is no external source assertion attached to this module.
