# Twisted centralizers of rational points of `Res_{L/K} GL_n` with regular elliptic norm (statement)

`Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearTwistedEllipticCentralizerStatement.{u}` is a
proposition definition.

Let `L/K` be a cyclic extension of number fields with generator `σ`, and `W = Res_{L/K} GL_n`
with the twist `θ` attached to `σ`. The geometric terms in the simple twisted trace formula
pair the volume of a rational centralizer quotient with a twisted orbital integral. This
proposition states finite volume and integrability for rational points whose norm is regular
elliptic at a finite place of `L`, with the measure conditions of
`GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement`.

## Statement

For all number fields `K` and `L` (in universe `u`), every `σ` and `l` with
`IsCyclicAlgebra σ l`, every `n`, every finite place `w` of `L`, and every `r ∈ Γ` such that
the component of `Φ(N(r))` at `w` is regular elliptic, write `C` for the `θ`-centralizer of `r`
in `W(𝔸_K)¹`. Then both of the following hold.

1. For every `MeasurableSpace` and `BorelSpace` instance on `(Γ ∩ C) \ C`, every right-invariant
   `μC` on `C` finite on compact sets and positive on nonempty open sets, and every `νΓC`
   satisfying `IsCompatible ((rationalNormOneSubgroup K Wₙ).subgroupOf C) μC Measure.count νΓC`,
   the total mass `νΓC Set.univ` is finite (`≠ ⊤`).
2. For every `MeasurableSpace` and `BorelSpace` instance on `C \ W(𝔸_K)¹`, every `μC` with the
   same three properties, and every `νC` satisfying `IsCompatible C Measure.haar μC νC`, the
   twisted orbit function `C x ↦ φ(x⁻¹ r θ(x))` is `νC`-integrable for every continuous,
   compactly supported `φ : W(𝔸_K)¹ → ℂ`. Here `Measure.haar` is on `W(𝔸_K)¹`.

The two assertions quantify over their measures independently. A choice of `μC`, `νC` and
`νΓC` satisfying both compatibilities therefore gives both conclusions, as required for each
class in `GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement`. The split form elaborates within the default heartbeat limit. Existence
of compatible measures is a separate proposition.

## Mathematical proof sketch

Through `Φ`, `r` comes from a matrix `δ ∈ GL_n(L)`. In the rational coordinates the twist
applies `σ` to the entries. The tensor and rational-section compatibilities are
`AdelicGaloisUnit.galoisTwist_tensorAdelicEquiv` and `AdelicGaloisUnit.galoisTwist_rationalSection`.
Thus `Φ(N(r))` is the adelic image of `Nδ = δ σ(δ) ⋯ σ^{l-1}(δ)`.

1. The identity `σ(Nδ) = δ⁻¹ (Nδ) δ` follows from `σ^l = 1` (`IsCyclicAlgebra.pow_eq_one`).
   The characteristic polynomial `p` of `Nδ` therefore has coefficients in `K`. Since
   `p` is irreducible and separable over `L_w`, it is irreducible and separable over `L` and
   over `K`, and `E = K[X]/(p)` is a field of degree `n` over `K` (so `n ≥ 1`). The companion
   matrix `γ ∈ GL_n(K)` of `p` is conjugate to `Nδ` in `GL_n(L)`, since both are regular
   semisimple with characteristic polynomial `p`.
2. Let `G_δθ` be the algebraic `θ`-centralizer of `δ` in `W`, a closed
   subgroup scheme over `K`. Over `K̄`, `W = GL_n^l` with `θ` permuting the factors cyclically.
   A point `x = (x_1, …, x_l)` of `G_δθ` is determined by `x_1`, which commutes with an
   element conjugate to `Nδ`, so `x ↦ x_1` identifies `G_δθ` over `K̄` with the centralizer of
   `Nδ`. By Arthur–Clozel Chapter 1, Lemma 1.1, `G_δθ` is an inner form over `K` of the
   centralizer `G_γ = Res_{E/K} 𝔾_m` of `γ`. Inner automorphisms of a torus are trivial, so
   the comparison gives `G_δθ ≅ Res_{E/K} 𝔾_m` over `K`.
3. The `K`-rational characters of `W` are the powers of `N_{L/K} ∘ det`, whose idelic norm is
   `|det x|_{𝔸_L}`. On `G_δθ`, the character `N_{L/K} ∘ det` is `det(x_1)^l` over `K̄`, since
   the other components are conjugates of `x_1`. Under the torus identification it is
   `N_{E/K}^l`. Its idelic norm has the same kernel as the norm on `𝔸_E^×`, so
   `C ≅ 𝔸_E¹` with the topology induced by the closed immersion `G_δθ ↪ W`. The matching
   rational subgroup is `Γ ∩ C ≅ E^×` by the product formula.
4. The quotient `E^× \ 𝔸_E¹` is compact. In the Library,
   `hasCompactNormOneRationalCover_of_isTorus` and `HasCompactNormOneRationalCover.compactSpace`
   give compactness of `T(K) \ T(𝔸_K)¹` for a torus `T`. Regularity in `IsCompatible` then gives
   `νΓC Set.univ < ⊤`.
5. Let `O` be the `Θ`-conjugacy class of `δ` under `W(𝔸_K)`. Since `Θ^l = 1`,
   `N(x⁻¹ δ Θ(x)) = x⁻¹ N(δ) x`, so every point of `O` has norm with characteristic
   polynomial `p` in every local component. Conversely, over an algebraic closure of `K_v`,
   cyclic permutation of the `l` factors identifies twisted conjugacy with ordinary conjugacy
   of the ordered product in the first factor. A norm with characteristic polynomial `p`
   gives that geometric conjugacy class because `p` is separable. The twisted conjugator
   descends to `K_v` by `H¹(K_v, G_δθ) = H¹(K_v, Res_{E/K} 𝔾_m) = 0` (Shapiro and Hilbert 90).
   To assemble these local conjugators adelically, take a finite place `v` where `L/K` is
   unramified, both points are integral, and `p` is integral with unit discriminant. After
   an étale extension of the valuation ring that splits the factors and `p`, the same
   product calculation identifies the transporter with the scheme of conjugators of two
   matrices with distinct eigenvalues. Choosing bases in the rank-one eigenspaces exhibits
   it locally as a torsor under a split torus. The transporter is therefore smooth, and
   Lang's theorem for its residue-field torus followed by Hensel lifting gives an integral
   conjugator. These conditions hold at almost all `v`.
   Thus `O` is exactly the closed intersection of the local characteristic-polynomial
   fibres. The positive scalar subgroup `A⁺` is central and fixed pointwise by `Θ`.
   Its determinant norms cover the positive reals, giving `W(𝔸_K) = A⁺ · W(𝔸_K)¹`, so
   `O` is also the orbit under `W(𝔸_K)¹`.
6. The norm-one group is second-countable and locally compact, hence σ-compact, and `O`
   is a closed subspace of it. The open mapping theorem for this transitive action makes the orbit map
   `C \ W(𝔸_K)¹ → O` a homeomorphism. The twisted orbit function is continuous with support
   in the compact preimage of `tsupport φ ∩ O`. Regularity of `νC` gives integrability.

## Source and what is derived

- Getz–Hahn, *An introduction to automorphic representations* (2024), registered
  `getz-hahn-2024-automorphic-representations`. On printed p. 487, (18.31) and (18.32)
  define the twisted centralizer and orbital integral, and `θ`-ellipticity means that
  the volume of `A^θ_G C^θ_γ(F) \ C^θ_γ(𝔸_F)` is finite. Corollary 18.5.1 (printed p. 488)
  sums `τ(C^θ_γ) TO_γ(f)` over `θ`-elliptic, `θ`-semisimple classes. It specializes Theorem
  18.2.5 (printed p. 479), which requires closed orbits. The norm-one model uses the
  splitting in §2.6, (2.19)–(2.22) and Lemma 2.6.2 (printed pp. 68–69). For `W`, `A_W`
  is the positive scalar subgroup, so `A_W^θ = A_W`.
- Arthur–Clozel, *Simple algebras, base change, and the advanced theory of the trace formula*
  (1989), registered `arthur-clozel-1989-base-change`. Chapter 1, §1 (printed p. 3)
  introduces the ordered norm. The proof of Lemma 1.1 (printed pp. 3–4) gives the
  inner-form comparison with the centralizer of a rational representative of the norm.

This is derived content. It combines the two per-class analytic requirements of the
Getz–Hahn formula with the centralizer comparison of Arthur–Clozel and the regular
ellipticity test at a finite place. The result is expressed using the Library's norm-one
group, compatible measures, and continuous compactly supported test functions.

## Relation to other statements

- `Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement` uses
  the same `θ`, `Φ`, `N`, local regular ellipticity condition and measure hypotheses. To
  obtain its per-class conclusions, take `r = r j` and `φ = fOne`, using continuity and
  compact support of `fOne` from the conclusion of
  `Langlands.TraceFormula.PositiveQuotientTraceComparisonStatement`.
- `Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearEllipticCentralizerStatement` is the
  untwisted analogue for `GL_n` over a number field.
