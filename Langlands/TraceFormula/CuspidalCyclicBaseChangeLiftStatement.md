# Cuspidal cyclic base change with two split supercuspidal places

`Langlands.TraceFormula.CuspidalCyclicBaseChangeLiftStatement` states existence of a
cuspidal base change of a representation of `GL_{r+1}(𝔸_K)` to `GL_{r+1}(𝔸_L)`, with
local comparisons at every finite place. The module defines three maps and the
proposition `Langlands.TraceFormula.CuspidalCyclicBaseChangeLiftStatement.{u, uV, uW}`.

## Notation and transport

For a number field `K`, `𝓞 K` is its ring of integers. A finite place `v` is a height-one
prime of `𝓞 K`, and `K_v` denotes `v.adicCompletion K`. The type `FinitePlacesAbove L v`
consists of finite places of `L` equipped with evidence that they lie over `v`. Write `L_w`
for `w.1.adicCompletion L`. Mathlib's `w.1.asIdeal.ramificationIdx (𝓞 K)` and
`w.1.asIdeal.inertiaDeg (𝓞 K)` are the ramification index and residue degree of `w` over `v`.

- `tensorPlaceAboveHom v w : K_v ⊗[K] L →+* L_w` evaluates `finiteTensorMap v` at `w`.
  On a pure tensor `a ⊗ x`, it multiplies the images of `a : K_v` and `x : L` in `L_w`.
- `tensorPlaceAboveRepresentation v w ρ` pulls back a complex representation `ρ` of
  `GL_n(L_w)` along `Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.map (tensorPlaceAboveHom v w)`.
- `completionPlaceAboveRepresentation v w ρ` pulls it back along the group map induced
  by `FiniteExtension.completionMap v w.1 : K_v →+* L_w`.

The finite-place part of `Langlands.Global.AdelicFunctoriality.completedTensorComparison`
gives bijectivity of `finiteTensorMap v`. With a single `w` above `v`, evaluation identifies
`K_v ⊗[K] L` with `L_w`. At a completely split place, each completed embedding `K_v → L_w`
is an isomorphism. These are the two comparisons used to put the upper component on the
group over the relevant lower completion or tensor algebra.

A `CuspidalOperatorRealization K r` is an irreducible realization in the Library's
cuspidal form space. A `CuspidalSpectralPresentation S` gives a nonzero finite slice of
`S`, with its restricted tensor factors and spherical Hecke spectra. Its `localComponent v`
is the embedded realization of the factor at `v`. `LocalRealization.IsIrreducible` and
`LocalRealization.IsAdmissible` refer to that representation, with the completion topology
used for admissibility. `LocalRealization.IsSupercuspidal` asks for these properties and
supercuspidality of the canonical local class.
`Langlands.ClassFieldTheory.Global.RelativeWeilGroup.HasUnitaryCentralCharacter` asks for
a continuous idele class character of absolute value one acting on the realization.

The finite set `D.spectralSystem.exceptional` is `D.exceptional`. At `v` outside it,
`D.spectralSystem.eigenvalues v hv` is the unordered tuple of normalized Satake parameters.
`Eigenvalues.power` raises each entry to a given natural power, with its multiplicity.
`SpectralSystem.IsArithmeticWeakBaseChangeOf` expresses the residue-degree power
comparison, with simultaneous definedness and unramifiedness at all places over a
cofinite set of base places.

## Hypotheses and conclusion

The proposition ranges over number fields `K` and `L` in the same universe `u`, with
`[Algebra K L]` and compact additive adele quotients
`[CompactSpace (AdeleQuotient K)]` and `[CompactSpace (AdeleQuotient L)]` as used by the
cuspidal realizations. It takes `σ : L ≃ₐ[K] L` and a prime `l : ℕ` with
`IsCyclicAlgebra σ l`. For fields this gives a cyclic Galois extension of degree `l`
generated by `σ`. At every infinite place `x` of `K` it requires
`IsCyclicShiftSplit K L x.Completion σ l`. This predicate identifies the scalar
extension to the completion with a product of `l` copies of the completion, with
`id ⊗ σ` acting by cyclic shift.

For every `r : ℕ`, take `S : CuspidalOperatorRealization K r` with unitary central character
and `D : CuspidalSpectralPresentation.{u, uV} S` with
`(D.localComponent v).IsIrreducible ∧ (D.localComponent v).IsAdmissible` at every finite `v`.
Take two distinct finite places `v₁`, `v₂` satisfying
`IsCyclicShiftSplit K L (vᵢ.adicCompletion K) σ l` and
`(D.localComponent vᵢ).IsSupercuspidal` for each `i`.

There exist `T : CuspidalOperatorRealization L r` and
`P : CuspidalSpectralPresentation.{u, uW} T` with all six properties.

1. `T` has unitary central character.
2. `P.spectralSystem.IsArithmeticWeakBaseChangeOf D.spectralSystem` holds.
3. For every finite `v` outside `D.spectralSystem.exceptional` with ramification index
   one at every `w` above `v`, there is `hw : ∀ w : FinitePlacesAbove L v,
   w.1 ∉ P.spectralSystem.exceptional`. For each such `w` the equality is
   `P.spectralSystem.eigenvalues w.1 (hw w) =
   (D.spectralSystem.eigenvalues v hv).power (w.1.asIdeal.inertiaDeg (𝓞 K))`,
   where `hv` witnesses that the lower spectrum is defined.
4. For every split finite `v` in the sense of `IsCyclicShiftSplit` and every `w` above it,
   `completionPlaceAboveRepresentation v w (P.localComponent w.1).representation`
   and `(D.localComponent v).representation` are isomorphic (`Nonempty` of
   `Representation.Equiv`).
5. For every finite `v` and `w : FinitePlacesAbove L v` with `∀ w', w' = w`, put
   `E_v = K_v ⊗[K] L` with its module topology over `K_v` and
   `σv = Algebra.TensorProduct.congr AlgEquiv.refl σ`. The local field data on `K_v`
   come from `CompletionPadic.topologicalData v`. Write `πv` for
   `(D.localComponent v).representation` and `PiV` for
   `tensorPlaceAboveRepresentation v w (P.localComponent w.1).representation`.
   There exist `hπ : Langlands.IsAdmissible πv`, `hP : Langlands.IsAdmissible PiV`,
   `A : SigmaIntertwiner σv (r + 1) PiV` and `c : ℂ` such that
   `IsSigmaLift σv l πv hπ PiV hP A c`.
6. At every finite place `w` of `L`,
   `(P.localComponent w).IsIrreducible ∧ (P.localComponent w).IsAdmissible`.

The intertwiner `A` is an isomorphism from `PiV` to its twist by `σv`.
`IsSigmaLift` requires `c ≠ 0` and the identity
`tr(PiV(φ) ∘ A) = c · tr(πv(f))` for all Haar measures and concordant
Schwartz–Bruhat functions `(φ, f)`. The associated centralizer measures in `AreConcordant`
give the same scaling on both sides of the identity. Primality implies that a finite
place either splits completely or has a single place above it, so properties 4 and 5
apply at every finite place.

The local hypothesis on `D` controls the factors of the supplied finite slice.
`CuspidalSpectralPresentation` permits the whole finite image, where the archimedean
factor has trivial finite-adelic action. An infinite-dimensional multiplicity space can
therefore be absorbed into the tensor factor at a place in the exceptional set. The
enlarged factor can have infinite-dimensional fixed spaces, whereas property 5 requires
admissibility of the given component at a place with one place above it.

## Trace comparison

1. For `r ≥ 1`, use the simple trace formulas at a unitary central character. Take a
   matrix coefficient of the contragredient of `π_{v₁}` on the lower side, with the
   corresponding supercuspidal coefficients in each split factor on the upper side.
   The coefficients can be scaled so their convolution gives the lower test function.
   At `v₂` use a strongly regular elliptic test with nonzero trace on the supercuspidal
   `π_{v₂}` and choose its split transfer with the product of the supports in the
   required elliptic set. At the other split places use cyclic convolution, at good
   unramified places use spherical Hecke transfer, and at the remaining finite places
   use concordant pairs. At the remaining places one can choose pairs with nonzero
   lower trace. At a nonsplit finite place, for example, an averaging idempotent on a
   sufficiently small compact open subgroup has nonzero trace and admits matching near
   the identity. Split archimedean matching uses convolution.

2. The trace comparison gives `l · ∑_Π tr(Π(φ) I_σ) = ∑_{π′} tr(π′(f))`. In the
   Library, the comparison and separation are expressed by
   `GeneralLinearSimpleTraceFormulaStatement`, `GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement`,
   `GeneralLinearBaseChangeTraceComparisonStatement` and `BoundedCharacterSeparationStatement`.
   Vary the matching spherical factors to isolate the transferred Hecke eigencharacter.
   `Langlands.Automorphic.Unramified.CyclicCuspidalFiberStatement` describes the surviving
   lower cuspidal classes as determinant twists of `π` by characters trivial on the norm.
   Their traces coincide on matching functions by Weyl integration, since the orbital
   integrals vanish off norms. Their sum is nonzero for the chosen tests. On the upper
   side, strong multiplicity one leaves a single σ-stable cuspidal representation with
   the selected spectrum.

3. Fix a finite place and vary its local tests while retaining nonzero trace factors
   elsewhere. Separation away from that place selects the same cuspidal classes.
   The local trace factorization uses the actual components of the presentations.
   The Library interfaces are `GroupSchemeCuspidalLocalTraceFactorizationStatement` and
   `GeneralLinearCuspidalLocalComponentTraceStatement`. At a split place the cyclic
   tensor trace reduces to the trace on one factor against the convolution, and
   character independence gives the local isomorphism. At `v₁` and `v₂`, put the
   supercuspidal coefficient in turn at the place whose component is being identified.
   Its nonzero trace selects the required component there. At a place with one `w`,
   taking the ratio of the fixed nonzero factors gives the σ-lift identity with a
   nonzero scalar as in `IsSigmaLift`.

4. Spherical transfer at each finite `v` outside the lower exceptional set and
   unramified in `L` gives the power by the residue degree. These places can all be
   tested with spherical functions in the separation. In rank at least two the two
   auxiliary places already lie in the lower exceptional set because a supercuspidal
   component has no spherical vector. For the upper presentation, use the irreducible
   admissible finite factors of the lift and choose spherical reference vectors at all
   the places retained in property 3. The restricted tensor product theorem then
   supplies the presentation with these places outside both `tensorExceptional` and
   `exceptional`. This yields the pointwise Satake assertion and hence the cofinite
   one. For `r = 0`, pull the unitary idele class character back along the idele class
   norm. The local norm restricts to the identity on each split factor and gives the
   rank-one twisted character identity at a single place above `v`. At an unramified
   place the norm of an upper uniformizer is a lower uniformizer to the residue degree
   times a lower unit.

The proposition extracts the global existence step of Arthur–Clozel §6.3 for an arbitrary
supplied cuspidal realization with the two auxiliary components. It expresses the local
identities simultaneously, using Henniart–Lemaire's σ-lift relation with an existential scalar.
The formulation of the finite slices, the choice of the upper exceptional set and the
transports of their local components are specific to the Library.
