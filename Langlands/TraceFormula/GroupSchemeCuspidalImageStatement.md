# Cuspidal image of convolution on `L²([G])`

`Langlands.TraceFormula.GroupSchemeCuspidalImageStatement` is a proposition about
the image of the Haar-integrated right action on the Library's automorphic `L²`
space.

## Parameters and notation

- `F : Type u` is a field with a `NumberField` instance. `G` is a group scheme over
  `Spec F` with affine underlying scheme (`IsAffine G.X.left`) and structure
  morphism locally of finite type (`LocallyOfFiniteType G.X.hom`). The hypothesis
  `hG : IsConnectedReductive G` makes `G` connected reductive. The hypothesis
  `hPD : ParabolicDynamicStatement.{u}` identifies the closed parabolic subgroups
  of connected reductive groups over fields with the dynamic subgroups `P(χ)` of
  field-defined cocharacters. The Library uses `hPD` and `hG` in the construction of
  the rational parabolic radicals.
- `T` is a commutative group scheme over `Spec F` and `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G` is central
  (`hc : IsCentral i`). For a type of indices `I : Type u` with `[Fintype I]`,
  `e : T.toGrp ≅ splitTorus (Spec (CommRingCat.of F)) I` identifies `T` with the
  split torus indexed by `I`. The character restriction `characterRestrict i` is
  injective (`hi`) and has finite-index range. The Haar measure of the norm-one
  subgroup `G(𝔸_F)¹ = normOneSubgroup F G` is assumed right invariant. These are
  the parameters of `GroupSchemeFullAEL2CuspidalTraceClassStatement`.
- `AdelicGroupPoints F G` is `G(𝔸_F)`. The quotient `[G]` is
  `PositiveRationalQuotient F G T i`, the left quotient of `G(𝔸_F)` by the subgroup
  generated by `G(F)` and the image under `i` of the positive real points of `T`.
  Its measure `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure F G T i` is the pushforward of
  the Haar quotient measure on `G(F) \ G(𝔸_F)¹`. The space `L²([G])` consists of
  complex `Lp` classes of exponent `2` for that measure.
- `ProperRationalParabolic G` ranges over the proper rational parabolic subgroup
  functors of `G`. For such a `P` with unipotent radical `N_P`, write `dn_P` for
  `properParabolicHaarMeasure F G hPD hG P` on `N_P(𝔸_F)`. The associated measure
  `properParabolicQuotientMeasure F G hPD hG P` on `N_P(F) \ N_P(𝔸_F)` has mass one.
- `fullAEL2CuspidalSubmodule F G hPD hG T i hi I e hc` is the subspace of `L²([G])`
  in which, for every `P : ProperRationalParabolic G`, the constant term converges
  absolutely and vanishes for Haar-almost every `g ∈ G(𝔸_F)¹`. This term integrates
  `n ↦ φ([n g])` on `N_P(F) \ N_P(𝔸_F)` using `properParabolicQuotientMeasure`.
  The condition depends only on the `L²` class.

## Statement

For all these parameters let `μG = Measure.haar` on `G(𝔸_F)`. Take a continuous
compactly supported function `f : C_c(G(𝔸_F), ℂ)` and a proof
`hf : Integrable f μG`. Assume the following vanishing identity holds for every
`P : ProperRationalParabolic G` and all `x, y ∈ G(𝔸_F)`.

`∫_{N_P(𝔸_F)} f(x n y) dn_P = 0`.

The operator `haarQuotientL2IntegratedAction F G T i hi I e hc μG f hf` is the
bounded operator `R(f)` given by the `L²`-valued Bochner integral
`R(f)φ = ∫ f(a) • R(a)φ dμG(a)`, where `R(a)` is right translation by `a`.
The proposition concludes that `R(f)` maps all of `L²([G])` into the full
almost-everywhere cuspidal subspace. In Lean the conclusion is

```lean
∀ φ : Lp ℂ 2 (positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure F G T i),
  haarQuotientL2IntegratedAction F G T i hi I e hc μG f hf φ ∈
    fullAEL2CuspidalSubmodule F G hPD hG T i hi I e hc
```

If `G` has no proper rational parabolic subgroup, both the radical-integral
hypothesis and the cuspidality condition are vacuous.

## Source and derivation

Getz and Hahn take a smooth test function `f = f_v ⊗ f^v` on
`A_G \ G(𝔸_F)`, compactly supported on that quotient, with `f_v` supercuspidal
at a finite place `v`. Their Definition 16.4.1 states the vanishing condition on
two-sided translates of the local unipotent radicals. In the proof of
Lemma 16.4.2 they unfold the constant term of `R(f)φ` for
`φ ∈ L²(A_G G(F) \ G(𝔸_F))` and use local supercuspidality to cancel the inner
integral over the adelic radical. [Getz–Hahn, *An Introduction to Automorphic Representations* (2024), Definition 16.4.1 and Lemma 16.4.2 with its proof, printed p. 430 (PDF p. 438)](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-41153-3)

This derived statement takes the adelic vanishing used in the unfolding as its
hypothesis on an arbitrary continuous compactly supported function on `G(𝔸_F)`.
It uses the Library's quotient by `G(F)` and the positive real points of `T`
and expresses cuspidality by the almost-everywhere condition above. The
registered source is `getz-hahn-2024-automorphic-representations`.
