# Comparison of the simple trace formula for `GL_n` with its twisted form (statement)

The module defines the proposition
`Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearBaseChangeTraceComparisonStatement.{u}`.

It states the global trace identity of cyclic base change for `GL_n`: if the global orbital
integrals of a test function `φ` on `Res_{L/K} GL_n` match those of a test function `f` on
`GL_n` over `K` at norms, up to a constant `c`, and those of `f` vanish off norms, then
`tr (R(φ) ∘ I_θ) = c · tr R(f)`. Both functions satisfy the hypotheses of the simple trace
formulas `GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement` (twisted) and
`GeneralLinearSimpleTraceFormulaStatement` (untwisted).

## Statement

Fix `K`, `L`, `σ`, `l` with `IsCyclicAlgebra σ l`, `n : ℕ`, and a common
`ParabolicDynamicStatement`. For each group, assume `IsConnectedReductive`, a central morphism
`iW : TW → Wₙ` or `iG : TG → GLₙ` from a commutative group scheme over `K`, and an
isomorphism of that group scheme with `splitTorus` on a type in universe `u` with a
`Fintype` instance. The character restriction `characterRestrict i` for the relevant
morphism is injective with finite-index image. The norm-one group's `Measure.haar` is
right-invariant.
On the `W` side the central morphism also satisfies
`iW ≫ (unitGaloisAut K L GLₙ σ).hom = iW`.

Let `φ₁, φ₂` be continuous compactly supported complex functions on `W(𝔸_K)`. Their cuspidal
integrated operators `R_cusp(φ₁)` and `R_cusp(φ₂)` are Hilbert–Schmidt for every integrability
witness for the respective function, using `Measure.haar` on `W(𝔸_K)`. The integral of `φ₂`
against `properParabolicHaarMeasure` along every two-sided translate of the unipotent
radical of every proper rational parabolic vanishes. Form `φ = φ₁ * φ₂` with that ambient Haar
measure by `φ(g) = ∫ a, φ₁(a) φ₂(a⁻¹ g)`. At the chosen finite place `w` of `L`, the
projection of `Φ(N(g))` is regular elliptic for every `g ∈ tsupport φ`. These are the
hypotheses of `GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement`.

On `G(𝔸_K)`, let `f₁, f₂` be continuous compactly supported complex functions with the
corresponding Hilbert–Schmidt conditions for every integrability witness and the same
cuspidality condition on `f₂` for `GLₙ`. Form `f = f₁ * f₂` with `Measure.haar` on `G(𝔸_K)`
and the same convolution convention. At the chosen finite place `v` of `K`, the projection of
`adelicHomeomorph K n g` is regular elliptic for every `g ∈ tsupport f`. These are the
hypotheses of `GeneralLinearSimpleTraceFormulaStatement`.

For `x` in the respective norm-one subgroup, let
`φ¹(x) = ∫_{A⁺_W} φ(a x) da` and `f¹(x) = ∫_{A⁺_G} f(a x) da`, using `positivePointMap` for
the image of `a` and `normalizedPositiveHaarMeasure` for `da`. For any `c ∈ ℂ`, assume both
of the following conditions.

1. **Matching at norms.** Take any `r ∈ rationalNormOneSubgroup K Wₙ`,
   `q ∈ rationalNormOneSubgroup K GLₙ` and `y ∈ GL_n(L)` with
   `Φ(N(r)) · rational(y) = rational(y) · ΦG(q)`, where `rational(y) = AdelicGL.rational L n y`.
   Require that `Φ(N(r))` be regular elliptic at `w` or that `q` be regular elliptic at `v`.
   For every group isomorphism `e` from `C_r = twistedCentralizer θ r` in `W(𝔸_K)¹` onto
   `C_q = twistedCentralizer (MonoidHom.id _) q` in `G(𝔸_K)¹` with
   `ΦG(e(x)) = rational(y)⁻¹ · Φ(x) · rational(y)` for all `x ∈ C_r`, take any
   right-invariant `μr` on `C_r` that is finite on compacts and positive on nonempty open
   sets, and any right-invariant `μq` on `C_q` with `μq = e_* μr`. For every pair of
   quotient measures `νr`, `νq` on the respective left coset spaces equipped with Borel
   measurable structures, compatible via `IsCompatible` with the respective norm-one Haar
   measures and with `μr`, `μq`, require
   `twistedOrbitalIntegral θ r νr φ¹ = c · orbitalIntegral q νq f¹`.
2. **Vanishing off norms.** Take any `q ∈ rationalNormOneSubgroup K GLₙ` regular elliptic
   at `v` such that `Φ(N(r)) · rational(y) ≠ rational(y) · ΦG(q)` for all
   `r ∈ rationalNormOneSubgroup K Wₙ` and `y ∈ GL_n(L)`. For every right-invariant measure
   `μq` on `C_q` that is finite on compacts and positive on nonempty open sets, and every
   measure `νq` on the Borel left coset space compatible with the norm-one Haar measure
   and `μq`, require `orbitalIntegral q νq f¹ = 0`.

There exist witnesses `hΘ` that `Θ` preserves `positiveRationalSubgroup K Wₙ TW iW`,
`hΘμ` that the inverse induced automorphism on `[W]` preserves
`positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure`, and `hφ`, `hf` that `φ` and `f` are integrable
against their ambient Haar measures. The operators `R(φ)` on `L²([W])` and `R(f)` on
`L²([G])` defined by `haarQuotientL2IntegratedAction` with these witnesses are trace class,
and `operatorTrace` gives

`tr (R(φ) ∘ I_θ) = c · tr R(f)`.

## Proof sketch

1. `GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement` supplies `hΘ`, `hΘμ`, integrability and trace class on the twisted side, and
   gives `tr (R(φ) ∘ I_θ) = ∑_j vol(C_{r_j}(K) \ C_{r_j}) · TO_{r_j}(φ¹)`. Its finite family
   of rational representatives `r_j` has distinct `θ`-conjugacy classes with regular elliptic
   norms at `w` and covers every rational class whose adelic twisted orbit meets the nonzero
   locus of `φ¹`. `GeneralLinearSimpleTraceFormulaStatement` supplies integrability and trace class on the untwisted side,
   and gives `tr R(f) = ∑_k vol(C_{q_k}(K) \ C_{q_k}) · O_{q_k}(f¹)` for the corresponding
   finite family of ordinary classes regular elliptic at `v`. The volumes use quotient
   measures compatible with the centralizer Haar measures and counting measures on the
   rational centralizers. The orbital integrals use quotient measures compatible with the
   ambient norm-one Haar measures and the same centralizer measures.
2. Through `Φ`, `W(K) = GL_n(L)` and `Θ` acts entrywise by `σ` (`GeneralLinearWeilAdelicTwistStatement`). For `δ ∈ GL_n(L)`,
   `N(δ)` is `GL_n(L)`-conjugate to some `ι(q)` with `q ∈ GL_n(K)`, `q` is determined up to
   `GL_n(K)`-conjugacy, and `δ` is determined up to `σ`-conjugacy by the `GL_n(L)`-conjugacy
   class of `N(δ)`. These are the assertions of `CyclicNormLemmaStatement` in the Library
   (Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, Lemma 1.1, p. 3 and Henniart–Lemaire, I.2.2, pp. 11–12). Thus
   `δ ↦ q` is a bijection from `σ`-classes in `GL_n(L)` onto the classes of norms in `GL_n(K)`.
3. For a pair associated by `Φ(N(r)) y = y ΦG(q)`, use `y` also for its diagonal adelic image.
   Regular ellipticity at either chosen place implies that `q` is regular semisimple, since
   the characteristic polynomial of the norm agrees with that of `q` after extension to `L`.
   If `x` is in the `Θ`-centralizer of `r` in `W(𝔸_K)`, then `x` commutes with `N(r)` because
   `Θ^l = 1`. Thus `z = y⁻¹ Φ(x) y` commutes with `ΦG(q)`. With `u = y⁻¹ Φ(r) σ(y)`, which
   commutes with `ΦG(q)`, one gets `σ(z) = u⁻¹ z u = z`, since the centralizer of a regular
   semisimple element is commutative. Hence `z` descends to `GL_n(𝔸_K)`. Conversely every
   such `z` in the centralizer comes from such an `x` by the inverse conjugation. This
   identifies the adelic centralizers by a homeomorphism. The identification restricts to
   the norm-one groups because the absolute value of the adelic determinant on the `W` side
   is the `l`th power of that of the descended matrix on the `G` side. The resulting `e`
   maps `C_r(K)` onto `C_q(K)` because `y` is rational. With `μq = e_* μr`, the transported
   measures on the rational centralizer quotients give equal volumes.
4. Use the norm bijection to pair the classes represented in the two finite sums. For a
   pair represented in both sums, hypothesis 1 matches the terms after multiplying the
   untwisted term by `c`. If a twisted representative has its norm class outside the
   untwisted sum, the corresponding ordinary orbit misses the nonzero locus of `f¹` by the
   covering property in step 1. Hypothesis 1 then gives `TO_{r_j}(φ¹) = c · 0`.
   If an untwisted representative has a norm preimage outside the twisted sum, the same
   covering property gives `c · O_{q_k}(f¹) = 0`. In both cases the represented side supplies
   the regular ellipticity disjunct in hypothesis 1. Hypothesis 2 removes non-norm classes
   from the untwisted sum. This gives the trace identity for arbitrary `c`, including `0`.

This is derived content. The proposition isolates the global comparison over number fields
for the Library's group schemes `Res_{L/K} GL_n` and `GL_n`. It uses norm-one subgroups and
`A⁺` averages and takes matching at global norms and vanishing off global norms as hypotheses.
The scalar `c` is the factor supplied by the matching hypothesis for the chosen functions and
measure normalizations.
