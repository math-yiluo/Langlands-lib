# Rational centralizer measure for a prescribed orbital normalization

`ExtendTwistedGeometricMeasuresStatement` concerns a second-countable,
locally compact Hausdorff group with its Borel measurable structure,
a continuous twisting endomorphism, and a discrete rational subgroup.
The chosen point has a commutative twisted centralizer. Its measure is
right invariant, finite on compact sets and positive on open sets. The
orbital quotient with its Borel structure has a compatible measure for
the supplied ambient and centralizer measures.

The statement extends these measures to a
`TwistedGeometricMeasures` package with the centralizer and orbital
quotient measures prescribed. Its rational-centralizer quotient has
the Borel structure, and its compatibility uses counting on the
rational intersection. Commutativity gives left invariance of the
centralizer measure. The centralizer is closed by continuity of the
twist, and the rational intersection is discrete and closed in it. The
quotient measure theorem applied with counting gives the extension.
A separate lemma shows that a finite compatible rational covolume is
nonzero as a complex scalar, using the nonzero quotient measure.

This internal construction supplies the two compatible measures in
the geometric terms used in Arthur–Clozel 1989, Chapter 1, equations
(4.4) and (4.5).
