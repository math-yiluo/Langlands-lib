# Trace replacement by compact self-adjoint smoothing

`CompactOperatorTraceReplacementStatement` compares two complex Hilbert
spaces with symmetric trace-class operators \(T_1,T_2\). The given
identities compare \(\operatorname{tr}(A_{1,i}T_1^m)\) and
\(\operatorname{tr}(A_{2,i}T_2^m)\) for each test \(i\) and each positive
integer \(m\). If equality of the unweighted compressed traces for all
tests on a nonzero eigenspace implies equality of the compressed traces
for the replacement operators \(B_1,B_2\), then the ordinary operator
traces of \(B_1 T_1\) and \(B_2 T_2\) agree.

Trace class implies compactness, so the spectral theorem gives an
orthogonal Hilbert sum of eigenspaces with finite dimension at every
nonzero eigenvalue. The trace of a bounded operator times a positive
power of \(T_j\) is the absolutely convergent sum of its compressed
traces multiplied by the corresponding powers of the eigenvalues.
One power is absorbed in the coefficients, and the remaining powers
yield the polynomial moments of a summable family on a compact real
spectral interval. Bounded character separation for polynomial
evaluation isolates each eigenvalue. Cancelling the nonzero eigenvalue
gives the unweighted equality needed by the replacement hypothesis.
The proof then sums the replacement traces with one power of \(T_j\).

The replacement implication is supplied. The result gives an operator
form of the separation and resummation in Arthur–Clozel (1989), Chapter
1, § 4, formulas (4.6)–(4.7) and (4.11), printed pp. 44–46
(PDF pp. 58–60). A trace formula application supplies the smoothing
operators and the identities associated with their powers.
