# Transporting the weighted orbital term between rational representatives

`TwistedGeometricConjugationStatement` fixes a topological group with
its Borel structure, an endomorphism, a subgroup of rational
conjugators, and a left-invariant ambient measure. For an element and
its twisted conjugate under the rational subgroup, the statement
transports both quotient measures used in the geometric trace term.
It takes compatible measures for the ambient centralizer quotient
and for the quotient of the centralizer by its rational intersection.
The latter compatibility uses counting measure on the intersection.

The Library's twisted conjugation theorem transports the orbital
integral and its compatible measure. Conjugating the centralizer
also carries its rational intersection onto the new rational
intersection, since the conjugator belongs to the rational subgroup.
The restricted isomorphism preserves counting measure. Transporting
the second Weil formula gives compatibility and preserves its total
volume. The transported centralizer measure remains right invariant,
finite on compacts and positive on open sets.

This internal result allows a representative in the simple trace
formula to be replaced by a specified rational representative without
changing either factor of the weighted orbital term. It applies to
the ordinary case by taking the identity endomorphism.
