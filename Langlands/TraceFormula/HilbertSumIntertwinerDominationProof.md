# Extension of a dominated map from a Hilbert sum

`Langlands.TraceFormula.hilbertSumIntertwinerDomination` extends the dominated
map to the closure of the range of the Hilbert-sum component. Joint invariance
and uniqueness on the dense range make the extension equivariant. Composing
with the orthogonal projection gives a bounded intertwiner on the Hilbert sum.
Its composite with each summand inclusion is zero by hypothesis. The sum of
the single-coordinate vectors converges to each Hilbert-sum vector, so the
intertwiner vanishes. Its restriction to the original image is the target map.
