# Integral families of `GL_n` and `Res_{L/K} GL_n` on adelic points (statement)

The module `Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearIntegralFamilyTopologyStatement` defines one
proposition and proves nothing:

- `Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearIntegralFamilyTopologyStatement.{u}`.

Let `L/K` be a finite extension of number fields, `G = GL_n` over `K` and
`W = Res_{L/K} GL_n`. The proposition has two parts. First, every regular function of `W` is
integral on the whole subgroup `GL_n(O_{E_v})` of `W(K_v) = GL_n(K_v ⊗_K L)` at almost every
finite place `v` of `K`. Second, for every finite set `T` of places of `K`, the adelic points of
`W` whose components outside `T` lie in `GL_n(O_{E_v})`, and the adelic points of `G` whose
components outside `T` lie in `GL_n(O_v)`, form open subsets of `W(𝔸_K)` and `G(𝔸_K)`.

This is derived content; see "Sources and derivation" below.

## Statement

For all number fields `K`, `L : Type u` with `[Algebra K L] [FiniteDimensional K L]` and every
`n : ℕ`, the conjunction of:

1. `IsUniformlyIntegral K Wₙ (localIntegralFamily K L n)`. Unfolded: for every regular function
   `a` of `Wₙ`, for all but finitely many finite places `v` of `K`, every
   `k ∈ localIntegralSubgroup K L n v` satisfies
   `Wₙ.affinePointEvaluation (placeCompletion K (.inr v)) k a ∈ v.adicCompletionIntegers K`.
2. For every `T : Finset (Place K)`, both of the following sets are open:
   - `{x : AdelicGroupPoints K Wₙ | ∀ i : Place K, i ∉ T → atPlace K Wₙ i x ∈ localIntegralFamily K L n i}`
     in `W(𝔸_K)`;
   - `{x : AdelicGroupPoints K GLₙ | ∀ i : Place K, i ∉ T → atPlace K GLₙ i x ∈ glIntegralFamily K n i}`
     in `G(𝔸_K)`.

There is no hypothesis beyond the number fields, the finite extension and `n`. `T` is an
arbitrary finite set of places; it need not contain the infinite places, since the two
families are the whole group there. The analogue of part 1 for `glIntegralFamily` is the
theorem `Langlands.TraceFormula.isUniformlyIntegral_glIntegralFamily` of
`Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearBaseChangeLocal`; the proposition does not repeat it.

## Sources and derivation

Getz–Hahn, *An Introduction to Automorphic Representations* (2024), source ID
`getz-hahn-2024-automorphic-representations`, §2.3 (printed pp. 55–57). Let `H` be an affine
algebraic group over a global field `F` (the source calls it `G`) with a faithful
representation `H → GL_N`, and set `C_v = H(F_v) ∩ GL_N(O_{F_v})` at the finite places `v`
(written `K_v` in the source, (2.10)). Proposition 2.3.1 (printed p. 57) says that `H(𝔸_F)` is,
as a topological group, the restricted direct product of the `H(F_v)` with respect to the
`C_v`. The restricted direct product and its topology are defined on printed pp. 56–57: its
basic open sets are `U × ∏_{v ∉ S} C_v` for finite sets `S` of places containing the infinite
places and open `U ⊆ ∏_{v ∈ S} H(F_v)`. The October 2026 errata of the book (source ID
`getz-hahn-2024-errata-2026-10`) list no correction to Chapter 2.

The proposition is not stated in the source; it is derived from this description for the two
integral families of the Library, with `F = K` and `H` equal to `GL_n` or `W`. For `GL_n` with
the standard representation, `C_v` is `GL_n(O_v)`, the subgroup `glIntegralSubgroup K n v`. For
`W = Res_{L/K} GL_n`, choose a `K`-basis `b_1, …, b_d` of `L` in `𝓞_L`, and let
`GL_n(R ⊗_K L)` act on `(R ⊗_K L)^n ≅ R^{nd}` through this basis; this is a faithful
representation `W → GL_{nd}`. At every finite place `v` at which the `1 ⊗ b_j` form an
`O_v`-basis of `O_{E_v}` (all but finitely many, see below), `C_v` is `GL_n(O_{E_v})`, the
subgroup `localIntegralSubgroup K L n v`. Part 1 is the integrality of the regular functions on
these subgroups that underlies the description: a regular function of `H` is a polynomial in
the matrix entries of the representation and the inverse of its determinant, with
coefficients in `F` that are integral at almost every place, so it is integral on all of `C_v`
at almost every `v`. Part 2 says that `∏_{i ∈ T} H(K_i) × ∏_{i ∉ T} U_i` is open, where `U_i`
is the subgroup of the Library's family at `i`; this is a basic open set of the restricted
direct product, since the `U_i` are open and agree with the `C_v` at almost all places.

## Why the statement is true (not formalized)

The module does not prove the statement. One argument, in the Library's terms:

1. Uniform integrality for `W`. Identify `W(R)` with `GL_n(R ⊗_K L)` for `K`-algebras `R`.
   Write `g ∈ GL_n(R ⊗_K L)` as `g = Σ_j g_j ⊗ b_j` with `g_j ∈ M_n(R)`, and likewise
   `g⁻¹ = Σ_j h_j ⊗ b_j`. The regular functions of `W` are the polynomials over `K` in the
   entries of the `g_j` divided by powers of `N(det g)`, the norm from `R ⊗_K L` to `R`. Since
   `N(det g)⁻¹ = N(det g⁻¹)` is a polynomial in the entries of the `h_j`, they form the
   `K`-algebra generated by the entries of the `g_j` and of the `h_j`. So a regular function
   `a` is a polynomial with coefficients in `K` in these finitely many generators. Let `M` be
   the `𝓞_K`-span of the `b_j`; it has finite index in `𝓞_L`, so `c 𝓞_L ⊆ M` for some integer
   `c ≠ 0`. At every finite place `v` not dividing `c`, the `O_v`-span of the `1 ⊗ b_j` is the
   `O_v`-span of `1 ⊗ 𝓞_L`, which is `O_{E_v}` (the module
   `Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearBaseChangeLocal` uses this description of
   `localIntegers` in its proof that `O_{E_v}` is compact and open), and the `1 ⊗ b_j` are
   linearly independent over `K_v`. At such `v`, the generators take values in `O_v` on every
   element of `GL_n(O_{E_v})`. If moreover the finitely many coefficients of the polynomial
   lie in `O_v`, then `a(k) ∈ O_v` for every `k ∈ GL_n(O_{E_v})`. Both conditions hold at
   almost every `v`.
2. Openness. Let `H` be `W` or `GL_n`, with `U_v` the subgroup of its family at a finite
   place `v`. Use the generators of part 1 for `W`, and the entries of `g` and of `g⁻¹` for
   `GL_n`. Let `S` be the finite set of finite places dividing `c` for `W`, and the empty set
   for `GL_n`. At every finite place `v ∉ S`, `U_v` is exactly the set of points at which all
   generators take values in `O_v`. For `x ∈ H(𝔸_K)` the value of a regular function `a` at
   `x` is an adele whose `v`-component is `a(atPlace K H v x)`
   (`AdelicGroupPoints.atPlace_evaluation`), and it depends continuously on `x`. The set of
   adeles whose components at the finite places outside the finite set `T ∪ S` are integral
   is open in `𝔸_K`. So the condition "`atPlace K H v x ∈ U_v` for every finite place `v`
   outside `T ∪ S`" is open: it says that the finitely many generators take values in this
   open set. At each of the finitely many places `v ∈ S` outside `T`, the condition
   `atPlace K H v x ∈ U_v` is open because `U_v` is open (`isOpen_localIntegralSubgroup`) and
   `atPlace` is continuous. At the infinite places there is no condition. The set of part 2 is
   the intersection of these finitely many open conditions.

## Use

Restricted tensor products of local test functions are described by `IsAdelicFactorization`
with the families `localIntegralFamily K L n` and `glIntegralFamily K n`: such a function is
`x ↦ ∏_{i ∈ T} φ_i(x_i)` on the set of part 2 and zero off it. That set is a subgroup (an
intersection of preimages of subgroups under the homomorphisms `atPlace`), so by part 2 it is
an open subgroup, hence also closed, and the function is continuous when its local factors at
the places of `T` are. Part 1 gives the compactness of the product of the integral subgroups.
Let `T` contain the infinite places, and replace the subgroups at the places of `T` by the
trivial subgroup (`IsUniformlyIntegral.of_eventuallyEq`). Then
`IsUniformlyIntegral.compactProductLift` maps the product of the subgroups `GL_n(O_{E_v})`,
`v ∉ T`, continuously into `W(𝔸_K)`. That product is compact
(`isCompact_localIntegralSubgroup`), and so is its image. The test functions `φ` on `W(𝔸_K)`
and `f` on `GL_n(𝔸_K)` of `Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearBaseChangeGlobalMatchingStatement`
are of this form, given by `IsAdelicFactorization` with these two families.
