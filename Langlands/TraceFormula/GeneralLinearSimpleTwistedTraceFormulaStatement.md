# The simple twisted trace formula for `Res_{L/K} GL_n` and a cyclic extension (statement)

`Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement.{u}` is a
proposition definition for the simple twisted trace formula used in cyclic base change of
`GL_n`. It states the formula in the Library's group-scheme automorphic `L²` space for
`W = Res_{L/K} GL_n`. It is the twisted counterpart of
`Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearSimpleTraceFormulaStatement`.

  reduced `K`-algebra of dimension `l`, that `σ` has order `l`, and that the elements of `L`
  fixed by `σ` are those of `K`. For fields this says that `L/K` is cyclic of degree `l` with

## Statement

The proposition quantifies over `K`, `L`, `σ`, `l` with `IsCyclicAlgebra σ l`, `n : ℕ` and the
group-scheme parameters and twist condition above. Let `μG = Measure.haar` on `W(𝔸_K)`.
For all `f₁, f₂ : C_c(W(𝔸_K), ℂ)`, the assumptions are as follows.

1. `R_cusp(f₁)` and `R_cusp(f₂)` are Hilbert–Schmidt, for every integrability proof (the
   operators do not depend on the proof).
2. For every proper rational parabolic `P` of `W` and all `x, y ∈ W(𝔸_K)`,
   `∫_{N_P(𝔸_K)} f₂(x m y) d(properParabolicHaarMeasure P)(m) = 0`.

Let `f(g) = ∫ f₁(a) f₂(a⁻¹ g) dμG(a)` be the Haar convolution `f₁ * f₂`. For a finite place
`w` of `L`, the support hypothesis is the following.

3. For every `g` in the closed support `tsupport f`, the component of `Φ(N(g))` at `w` is
   regular elliptic in `GL_n(L_w)`.

Define `fOne : W(𝔸_K)¹ → ℂ` by `fOne(x) = ∫_{A⁺} f(a x) da`, using `positivePointMap`
and the normalized Haar measure above. For every `w` satisfying hypothesis 3, the
conclusion supplies the following proofs.

- `hΘ` says `Θ x ∈ H ↔ x ∈ H` for every `x`, where `H` is the subgroup generated by
  `A⁺` and `W(K)`.
- `hΘμ` says that the inverse of the induced quotient homeomorphism preserves `ν`.
  Together `hΘ` and `hΘμ` give the operator `I_θ = twistOperator H Θ hΘ ν hΘμ`.
- `h : Integrable f μG` defines `R(f)`, and `R(f)` is trace class on `L²([W])`.

There exist `m : ℕ` and `r : Fin m → Γ` representing distinct `θ`-twisted classes under `Γ`.
Every `Φ(N(r j))` is regular elliptic at `w`. Each `q ∈ Γ` with
`fOne(x⁻¹ q θ(x)) ≠ 0` for some `x ∈ W(𝔸_K)¹` is `θ`-conjugate under `Γ` to some `r j`.

For each `j`, write `C_j` for the `θ`-centralizer of `r j` in `W(𝔸_K)¹`. The conclusion
applies to every choice of Borel measurable structures on `C_j \ W(𝔸_K)¹` and
`(Γ ∩ C_j) \ C_j` and of measures as follows.

- `μC j` is a right-invariant measure on `C_j`, finite on compacts and positive on
  nonempty open sets.
- `νC j` is a measure on `C_j \ W(𝔸_K)¹` compatible with Haar measure on `W(𝔸_K)¹`
  and `μC j`.
- `νΓC j` is a measure on `(Γ ∩ C_j) \ C_j` compatible with `μC j` and counting measure
  on `Γ ∩ C_j` (the subgroup `(rationalNormOneSubgroup K Wₙ).subgroupOf C_j` of `C_j`).

For every such family of measures the volumes `νΓC j Set.univ` are finite, the twisted
orbit functions of `fOne` are `νC j`-integrable, and the trace identity is

```text
operatorTrace (R(f) ∘ I_θ)
  = ∑ j, (νΓC j Set.univ).toReal · twistedOrbitalIntegral θ (r j) (νC j) fOne.
```

The real volume in each summand is cast to `ℂ`.

If `n = 0`, the characteristic polynomial of the unique element of `GL_0(L_w)` is the unit
polynomial `1`. Hypothesis 3 therefore forces `tsupport f` to be empty and `f = 0`.
The twist is the identity on the trivial group, `R(f)` is the zero operator, and the
family of representatives is empty.

## Getz–Hahn correspondence

Getz and Hahn, *An Introduction to Automorphic Representations* (2024), §18.5 and
Corollary 18.5.1 (printed pp. 486–488), apply Theorem 18.2.5 (printed p. 479) to the
subgroup `{(g, θ(g))}` with trivial character. Their test function is smooth and compactly
supported on `A_G \ G(𝔸)`. They require its right convolution operator to have cuspidal image
and the rational classes reaching its support under twisted conjugation to be `θ`-elliptic
and `θ`-semisimple. Literature ID `getz-hahn-2024-automorphic-representations`.

- For `G = W`, the image of `A⁺` is the positive split-center subgroup `A_G` in the source
  convention of §2.6, (2.19)–(2.22) (printed pp. 68–69). For `n > 0` the split center of `W`
  is the scalar torus. A supplied central split torus with injective finite-index character
  restriction has the same positive image. The identification
  `A_G \ W(𝔸_K) ≃ W(𝔸_K)¹` makes the averaged function `fOne` the counterpart of the source's
  test function. The twist fixes the image of `i` pointwise, hence `A_G^θ = A_G`.
- Let `C^θ_γ` denote the source's twisted centralizer of a representative `γ = r j` from
  (18.31). In the splitting `W(𝔸_K) = A⁺ × W(𝔸_K)¹`, a point `a x` centralizes `γ` under
  twisted conjugation exactly when `x` does, since `A⁺` is central and fixed. Thus `C_j`
  identifies with `A_G \ C^θ_γ(𝔸_K)` and `Γ ∩ C_j` identifies with `C^θ_γ(K)`. With matching
  Haar measures, the volume `νΓC j Set.univ` is the source's `τ(C^θ_γ)` and integration over
  `C_j \ W(𝔸_K)¹` is (18.32).
- The inverse pullback defining `I_θ` is (18.33). In the source convolution kernel on
  the norm-one model, the substitution `y = θ(xg)` turns
  `fOne(g) φ(θ⁻¹(xg))` into `fOne(x⁻¹ θ(y)) φ(y)`. Periodizing over `Γ` puts the twist on
  the second kernel variable, giving `∑_{q ∈ Γ} fOne(x⁻¹ q θ(y))` by reindexing with
  `θ(Γ) = Γ`. The diagonal has the argument `x⁻¹ q θ(x)` used on the geometric side.
- Under the source's cuspidal-image hypothesis the ambient trace of `R(f) ∘ I_θ` is the
  trace on the cuspidal subspace. In (18.34)–(18.36), the spectral side writes this trace
  as the sum over `θ`-stable cuspidal isotypic components, with multiplicities included
  in their twisted traces. The Lean formula uses the single operator trace on `L²([W])`.

## Arthur–Clozel correspondence

Arthur and Clozel, *Simple Algebras, Base Change, and the Advanced Theory of the Trace
Formula* (1989), Chapter 1, Lemma 2.5 (printed p. 14), give the twisted Deligne–Kazhdan trace
formula for a cyclic extension `E/F`, using smooth test functions with a central character
and split-place hypotheses. Their geometric sum is over rational `σ`-conjugacy classes
with regular elliptic norm. Literature ID `arthur-clozel-1989-base-change`.

Here `E/F = L/K` and Weil restriction gives `W(K) = GL_n(L)`. The ordered product `N` is
Arthur–Clozel's norm for the coefficient action of `σ`. Lemma 1.1 (printed p. 3) associates
to the rational norm product a conjugacy class in `GL_n(K)` and identifies `σ`-conjugacy
of rational points with equality of those norm classes. Regular ellipticity of the norm at
`w` forces the characteristic polynomial of the descended class to be irreducible over `K`,
since any factorization over `K` would persist over `L_w`.

In condition (4′), the finite place `v₂` of `K` splits completely in `L`. Number the factors
over `v₂` in the cyclic order induced by `σ`, starting at `w`. The `w`-component of `Φ(N(g))`
is then `g₁ ⋯ g_l`. Condition (4′) requires the products of the local component supports to
have regular elliptic image in `PGL_n(K_{v₂})`, giving the `GL_n` norm-ellipticity condition
on those products. Hypothesis 3 directly tests the closed support of `f` at a single
finite place `w` of `L` and uses the characteristic polynomial in `GL_n(L_w)`. It applies
also at places above a nonsplit place of `K`.

## Derived form

As in `GeneralLinearSimpleTraceFormulaStatement`, the Lean test function is a convolution of two continuous compactly
supported functions on the full adelic group. The Hilbert–Schmidt properties of their
cuspidal operators are premises. Getz–Hahn's Theorem 9.1.2 (printed p. 241) proves trace class
of the cuspidal operator for a smooth compactly supported function on the central quotient.
The Lean premise corresponding to cuspidal image is the vanishing of the radical integrals
of the second factor on all two-sided translates.

The geometric side uses a finite family covering every rational class with a nonzero
value of `fOne` somewhere on its twisted orbit. It states finiteness of the centralizer
quotient volumes and integrability of the orbit functions in the conclusion, along with the
identity for all compatible measures. The inverse-pullback data `hΘ` and `hΘμ` are also
supplied in the conclusion.
