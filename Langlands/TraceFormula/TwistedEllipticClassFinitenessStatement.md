# Finiteness of rational σ-elliptic classes meeting a compact adelic set

`Langlands.TraceFormula.TwistedEllipticClassFinitenessStatement.{u,v} : Prop` states
finiteness without proving it. Let `K : Type u` and `L : Type v` be number fields
with `[Algebra K L]`, and let `σ : L ≃ₐ[K] L` satisfy `IsCyclicAlgebra σ l`. In this
field setting the condition says that `L/K` is cyclic of degree `l`: the subgroup
generated by `σ` has order `l = [L:K]`, and its fixed field is the image of `K`.

For any `n : ℕ` and compact `Ω ⊆ AdelicGL L n`, a rational `δ : RationalGL L n` meets
`Ω` if there is `x : AdelicGL L n` such that

```lean
x⁻¹ * AdelicGL.rational L n δ * adelicEquiv n (σ : L ≃+* L) x ∈ Ω
```

Here `adelicEquiv` acts on each matrix entry by the induced automorphism of the adeles
of `L`, transporting the coefficients along the permutation of places induced by `σ`.
On rational matrices, `entrywiseAut σ n` applies `σ` to each entry.

The statement gives `R : Finset (RationalGL L n)` with both properties:

- Every `r ∈ R` satisfies `IsSigmaElliptic σ l r` and meets `Ω`.
- For every rational `δ` with those two properties, there are `r ∈ R` and
  `y : RationalGL L n` with `y⁻¹ * δ * entrywiseAut σ n y = r`.

Representatives need not be unique within their twisted classes. The Library's
`IsSigmaElliptic σ l δ` means that `cyclicNorm σ l δ`, the ordered product
`δ σ(δ) ⋯ σ^{l-1}(δ)`, is conjugate over `L` to the base change of a matrix over `K`
with separable irreducible characteristic polynomial. That polynomial need not remain
irreducible over `L`. There is no ellipticity condition over a completion of `L` or
any other local hypothesis on `Ω`.

## Sources and scope

This is a derived finiteness statement for the geometric side of the simple twisted
trace formula. In Getz–Hahn, *An Introduction to Automorphic Representations* (2024),
Lemma 18.2.6 (printed p. 479) gives finiteness of relatively semisimple geometric
classes meeting a compact set modulo the split centre, in the setting of the general
simple relative trace formula of Theorem 18.2.5. Section 18.5, (18.30)–(18.32) and
Corollary 18.5.1 (pp. 486–488), give the twisted action, centralizer, orbital integral
and simple twisted trace formula for a finite-order automorphism. The statement here
isolates the compact-set finiteness step for the cyclic `GL_n` case over number fields;
it is not the trace identity, a statement on the quotient by the split centre, or a
local support criterion for global σ-ellipticity.

Henniart–Lemaire, *Changement de base et induction automorphe pour GL_n en
caractéristique non nulle* (2011), I.2.2 (pp. 11–12), give the norm lemma and norm
association. Section I.2.3 (pp. 13–14) specifies regular ellipticity over the base
field, and II.6.4 (pp. 120–121) indexes the twisted geometric terms by classes whose
norms are regular semisimple elliptic over the base field. Only the norm and indexing
conventions are taken from their positive-characteristic setting.

## Proof idea, not formalized here

The ordered adelic norm of a twisted conjugate of `δ` is an ordinary adelic conjugate
of the rational norm of `δ`, since `σ ^ l = 1`. The characteristic coefficients of
these norms thus belong to the intersection of a compact image of `Ω` with the
closed discrete diagonal `L^{n+1}` in `𝔸_L^{n+1}`. This gives finitely many
characteristic polynomials. For each polynomial, the invariant-factor theorem
(`matrixCharpolyFiniteConjugacy` in the Library) gives finitely many rational
conjugacy classes of norm matrices over `L`. Henniart–Lemaire's norm lemma I.2.2(2)
identifies rational twisted classes with conjugate norms, so one can choose a
representative of each occurring class from the σ-elliptic elements meeting `Ω`.
