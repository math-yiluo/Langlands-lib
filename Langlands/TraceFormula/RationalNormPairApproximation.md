# Simultaneous approximation by an associated rational norm pair

`RationalNormPairApproximationStatement` concerns a cyclic extension
of number fields \(L/K\) with the chosen generator and a finite
set of finite places of \(K\). At each chosen place the upper
matrix is in \(\mathrm{GL}_n(K_v\otimes_K L)\). Its supplied
associated ordinary matrix in \(\mathrm{GL}_n(K_v)\) is regular
semisimple. Given neighborhoods of both matrices at each place,
one associated rational pair in
\(\mathrm{GL}_n(L)\times\mathrm{GL}_n(K)\) has both localizations
in all the prescribed neighborhoods.

The proof uses the continuous choice of associated regular
representatives and the openness of the \(\sigma\)-regular locus.
Near each upper matrix the ordinary representative therefore lies
in the interior of its prescribed neighborhood. Weak approximation
in the actual local tensor groups gives an upper rational matrix.
The cyclic norm lemma supplies one associated ordinary rational
matrix. At each chosen place its localization is associated
with the same upper localization, so the two ordinary matrices
are conjugate by local norm uniqueness. A second weak approximation
chooses one ordinary rational conjugator close to the local ones.
Conjugation is continuous and preserves association with the
upper rational point.

This internal result implements the simultaneous approximation of
regular pairs in Henniart–Lemaire 2011, I.6.8.
