# Concordant tensor tests at a split infinite place

`infiniteSplitConcordance` proves `InfiniteSplitConcordanceStatement`.
Let \(L/K\) be a finite extension of number fields with a chosen
\(K\)-automorphism. At an infinite place \(w\) of \(K\), assume a
splitting of the completed tensor algebra \(K_w\otimes_K L\) into
\(m+1\) copies of \(K_w\) in which the automorphism acts by cyclic shift.
Fix a base Haar measure and continuous compactly supported factor
functions on the base matrix group in the chosen rank.

The theorem chooses the cyclic splitting and uses its matrix group
coordinates to give the actual upper group product Haar measure.
The associated tensor in these coordinates and the ordered
convolution on the base group are continuous, compactly supported
and concordant for the local Galois action. The measurable structures
are the Borel structures on the actual matrix groups.

The completed tensor algebra has its module topology, so the chosen
algebra equivalence and its inverse are continuous. The proof uses the
split comparison with product Haar and pulls it back through that
coefficient equivalence. The ambient Haar measure on the tensor
algebra pushes forward to the measure used in the split comparison,
and the coefficient transport preserves the association of centralizer
measures and compatibility of their quotients.

This is the local orbital comparison at infinite split places in the
proof of Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, Theorem 4.5, printed pp. 43–44.
The argument works with continuous compactly supported local factors.
