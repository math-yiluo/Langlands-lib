# Rational σ-ellipticity from σ_v-ellipticity at a finite place of the base

`Langlands.TraceFormula.WeilGeneralLinearRationalSigmaEllipticityStatement.{u} : Prop`
is a proposition definition; this module states it and does not prove it. It says that a
rational point of `W = Res_{L/K} GL_n` whose component at one finite place `v` of `K` is
σ_v-elliptic corresponds to a matrix of `GL_n(L)` that is σ-elliptic over `K`.

## Setting

- `K L : Type u` are number fields with `[Algebra K L]` and `[FiniteDimensional K L]`,
  `σ : L ≃ₐ[K] L` and `l : ℕ`, and `IsCyclicAlgebra σ l` holds
  (`Langlands.BaseChange.IsCyclicAlgebra`). For the field `L` this says that `L/K` is
  cyclic of degree `l = [L : K]` with Galois group generated by `σ`. The degree `l` and
  the extension are otherwise arbitrary.
- For `n : ℕ`, `GLₙ` is `GroupScheme.GeneralLinear.groupScheme (Fin n) (CommRingCat.of K)`
  and `Wₙ` is `weilGroup K L GLₙ`, the Weil restriction to `K` of the base change of
  `GLₙ` to `L`. The local `IsAffine` and `Smooth` instances are those of
  `GeneralLinearWeilAdelicTwistStatement`.
- `Φ : AdelicGroupPoints K Wₙ → AdelicGL L n` is, term for term, the composite of
  `GeneralLinearWeilAdelicTwistStatement`: `AdelicGaloisUnit.weilAdelicEquiv K L GLₙ`, then
  `AdelicGroupPoints.map` of the base-change isomorphism
  `(GroupScheme.GeneralLinear.baseChangeIso (Fin n) (Spec.algebraMap K L)).hom`, then
  `GeneralLinearGroupPoints.adelicHomeomorph L n`. It identifies `W(𝔸_K)` with
  `GL_n(𝔸_L)`.
- `v : Langlands.Global.FinitePlace K` is a finite place of `K` (a height-one prime of
  its ring of integers), used as the place `.inr v`. Its completion is
  `K_v = placeCompletion K (.inr v)`. The field structure on `K_v` is
  `placeCompletionField K (.inr v)`, made available by
  `open scoped Langlands.TraceFormula.PlaceCompletion`.
- `E_v = K_v ⊗_K L` is `LocalBaseChangeAlgebra K L (.inr v)`, and
  `σ_v = 1 ⊗ σ` is `localGalois K L σ (.inr v)`. In general `E_v` is not a field: it is
  isomorphic to the product of the completions of `L` at the places above `v`, and
  `σ_v` permutes these factors. By `isCyclicAlgebra_localGalois`, `E_v` is a cyclic `K_v`-algebra of
  degree `l` with automorphism `σ_v`.
- `localPointsEquiv K L n (.inr v) : W(K_v) ≃* GL_n(E_v)` is the Library's identification
  of the local points, and `AdelicGroupPoints.atPlace K Wₙ (.inr v)` takes the component
  of an adelic point at `v`.

## Statement

For all such data and every `r : AdelicGroupPoints K Wₙ`, assume

1. `r ∈ AdelicGroupPoints.rationalSubgroup K Wₙ`, the image of `W(K)` in `W(𝔸_K)`;
2. `IsSigmaElliptic (localGalois K L σ (.inr v)) l
   (localPointsEquiv K L n (.inr v) (AdelicGroupPoints.atPlace K Wₙ (.inr v) r))`.

Then there is `δ : RationalGL L n` with `Φ r = AdelicGL.rational L n δ` and
`IsSigmaElliptic σ l δ`.

Since `AdelicGL.rational` is injective (`AdelicGL.rational_injective`), the equation
`Φ r = AdelicGL.rational L n δ` determines `δ`: the conclusion is σ-ellipticity of the matrix of `GL_n(L)`
that corresponds to `r` under `Φ`.

## The two ellipticity predicates

Both hypothesis and conclusion use `Langlands.BaseChange.IsSigmaElliptic`, over the
base fields `K_v` and `K` respectively. `IsSigmaElliptic σ l δ` holds when the ordered
norm `cyclicNorm σ l δ = δ δ^σ ⋯ δ^{σ^{l-1}}` is conjugate, in `GL_n` of the upper
algebra (`E_v` or `L`), to the image of a matrix `γ` over the base field whose
characteristic polynomial is separable and irreducible **over the base field**
(`IsRegularElliptic γ`). Thus:

- the hypothesis does not ask for `E_v` to be a field, nor for any matrix to be
  elliptic over a completion of `L`;
- the conclusion does not ask for the characteristic polynomial of `γ` to remain
  irreducible over `L`.

For `n = 0` the characteristic polynomial is `1`, which is not irreducible, so the
hypothesis never holds. The proposition has no analytic content: it involves no test
function, support condition, measure or integrability hypothesis, and it is not a trace
identity.

## Sources

The proposition is derived from the algebraic norm results and the elliptic indexing
convention of Henniart–Lemaire, *Changement de base et induction automorphe pour GL_n
en caractéristique non nulle*, Mém. SMF 124 (2011), registered source
`provided-a0a5445aba86b871bcd83f08813de3234fddb463ff9bb3c9dfd60d2d2fcdae61` (PDF page =
printed page + 8). Their algebraic results in I.2 hold over any field. Their global
argument in II.6 is over global fields of positive characteristic. Here the fields are
number fields, and from II.6 only the indexing convention is used.

- I.2.1–I.2.2 (printed pp. 10–12): cyclic algebras `E = E₁ × ⋯ × E_r` over an arbitrary
  field `F`, the norm `N(δ) = δ δ^σ ⋯ δ^{σ^{l-1}}` and the Lemme of I.2.2. Its part (1)
  says that `N(δ)` is conjugate in `G(E)` to an element of `G(F)`, and that all such
  elements are conjugate in `G(F)`. These two halves are (1a) and (1b) of the Library's
  `Langlands.BaseChange.CyclicNormLemmaStatement`, proved as
  `Langlands.BaseChange.cyclicNormLemma`. The Définition of associated elements follows
  the Lemme.
- I.2.3 (printed pp. 13–14): regular and elliptic elements of `G(F)` (characteristic
  polynomial a product of distinct irreducible factors, respectively irreducible over
  `F`) and the set `G(F)''` of regular semisimple elliptic elements. II.2.2 (printed
  p. 63) calls `δ ∈ G(E)` σ-elliptic when the orbit `N(O_σ(δ))` is elliptic. In
  characteristic zero an irreducible polynomial is separable, so this is the Library's
  `IsSigmaElliptic`.
- II.6.1 (printed p. 118): for a cyclic extension `E/F` of global fields and a place `v`
  of `F`, `E_v = E ⊗ F_v` is a cyclic `F_v`-algebra, the product of the completions of
  `E` above `v`.
- II.6.4 (printed pp. 120–121): the simple twisted trace formula. Part of condition
  `c^σ_{v₁}(φ)` is that the test function at one place `v₁` of `F` vanishes outside the
  σ-elliptic elements of `G(E_{v₁})`. The geometric side then runs over the rational
  σ-conjugacy classes whose norm lies in `G(F)''`: regular semisimple elliptic over the
  **global base field** `F`. The present proposition is the algebraic step joining
  these two conditions: a rational element that is σ_v-elliptic at one finite place of
  the base is σ-elliptic over the global base field.

## Proof idea, not formalized here

1. The rational clause of `GeneralLinearWeilAdelicTwistStatement`, proved as
   `Langlands.TraceFormula.generalLinearWeilAdelicTwist`, gives `δ` with
   `Φ r = AdelicGL.rational L n δ`.
2. The component `localPointsEquiv K L n (.inr v) (atPlace … r)` is the image of `δ`
   under the entrywise coefficient map `ι_v : L → E_v`, `b ↦ 1 ⊗ b`. This follows from
   the naturality of the Weil-restriction and general-linear point comparisons; this
   module does not prove it. Since `ι_v ∘ σ = σ_v ∘ ι_v`, the norm of `ι_v(δ)` for
   `σ_v` is `ι_v` of the norm of `δ` for `σ`.
3. By (1a) over `K`, `δ` is associated with some `γ ∈ GL_n(K)`. Applying `ι_v` to the
   association equation shows that the image `γ_v` of `γ` in `GL_n(K_v)` is associated
   with `ι_v(δ)`.
4. The hypothesis gives a regular elliptic `γ'` over `K_v` associated with `ι_v(δ)`. By
   (1b) for the cyclic algebra `E_v/K_v`, `γ_v` and `γ'` are conjugate in `GL_n(K_v)`,
   so the characteristic polynomial of `γ_v` is separable and irreducible over `K_v`.
5. That polynomial is the image of the characteristic polynomial of `γ`. Separability
   descends along `K → K_v`, and so does irreducibility of a monic polynomial. Hence
   `γ` is regular elliptic over `K`, and `γ` witnesses `IsSigmaElliptic σ l δ`.
