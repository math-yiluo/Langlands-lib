# Local factorization of traces on an irreducible cuspidal summand

`Langlands.TraceFormula.GroupSchemeCuspidalLocalTraceFactorizationStatement.{u}` defines a
proposition about one irreducible closed invariant subspace `P` of the group-scheme cuspidal
`L²` space. For a function split at a finite place `v`, the trace of the integrated action on
`P`, when trace class, is a product `tr ρ_v(f_v) · C(f^v)`. For fixed preceding data the same
irreducible admissible representation `ρ_v` of `G(F_v)` serves both the ordinary and twisted
parts. In the twisted part `I_Θ` preserves `P`, and the local factor is
`tr (ρ_v(f_v) ∘ A_v)` for a bijective intertwining operator `A_v` from `ρ_v` to `ρ_v ∘ θ_v`.

## Setting and notation from the Library

The group data are those of the Library's
`Langlands.Automorphic.GroupSchemeFunctions.GroupSchemeFullAEL2CuspidalDecompositionStatement`.
Take a number field `F : Type u` and an affine group scheme `G` over `Spec F` locally of finite
type, with `hPD : ParabolicDynamicStatement.{u}` and `hG : IsConnectedReductive G`. Take a
commutative group scheme `T` over `Spec F` with `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G` and `hc : IsCentral i`. The
character restriction `characterRestrict i` is injective by `hi` and its range has finite
index. For `I : Type u` with `[Fintype I]`, take an isomorphism `e` of `T.toGrp` with the split
torus indexed by `I`. The Haar measure on `normOneSubgroup F G` is right invariant.

- Write `G(𝔸_F)` for `AdelicGroupPoints F G`. The subgroup `positiveRationalSubgroup F G T i`
  is generated by the image of `G(F)` and the image under `i` of the intrinsic positive real
  points of `T`. The left quotient `[G]` has the measure
  `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure F G T i`, written `μ_Q`.
- `fullAEL2CuspidalSubmodule F G hPD hG T i hi I e hc`, written `S`, is the closed cuspidal
  subspace of `L²([G], μ_Q)`. It is complete by `fullAEL2Cuspidal_completeSpace`.
  `fullAEL2CuspidalBoundedRightAction F G hPD hG T i hi I e hc a`, written `π_c(a)`, is right
  translation by `a ∈ G(𝔸_F)` on `S`.
- For a measure `ν` on `G(𝔸_F)` and `hf : Integrable f ν`,
  `fullAEL2CuspidalIntegratedAction F G hPD hG T i hi I e hc ν f hf`, written `R_S(f)`, is the
  integrated action on `S`. Its value at `x` is the Bochner integral `∫ f(a) • π_c(a)x dν(a)`.
  `haarQuotientL2IntegratedAction F G T i hi I e hc ν f hf`, written `R(f)`, integrates the
  right action on all of `L²([G], μ_Q)`.
- Let `Θ` be a topological group automorphism of `G(𝔸_F)` and let
  `hΘ : ∀ x, Θ x ∈ positiveRationalSubgroup F G T i ↔ x ∈ positiveRationalSubgroup F G T i`.
  Then `quotientAutomorphism (positiveRationalSubgroup F G T i) Θ hΘ` is the induced
  homeomorphism of `[G]`. Given `hΘμ` asserting that its inverse preserves `μ_Q`,
  `twistOperator (positiveRationalSubgroup F G T i) Θ hΘ μ_Q hΘμ`, written `I_Θ`, sends
  `φ` to `φ ∘ Θ⁻¹` on the quotient. This is the pullback convention of Getz–Hahn (18.33).
- The variable `v : Langlands.Global.FinitePlace F` is a finite place and `.inr v` is the
  corresponding element of `Place F = InfinitePlace F ⊕ FinitePlace F`. Write `G(F_v)` for
  `PlaceGroupPoints F G (.inr v)`, with the Library's topology on points over the completion
  `F_v`. The map `atPlace F G (.inr v)` sends `g` to its local component `g_v`, and
  `ofPlace F G (.inr v)` inserts a local point at `v` and the identity at all other places.
- `SchwartzBruhat G(F_v)` is the space of locally constant compactly supported functions.
  `IsAdmissible ρ` says that every vector is fixed by an open subgroup and the fixed vectors
  of each open subgroup form a finite-dimensional space. `ρ.IsIrreducible` is Mathlib's
  algebraic irreducibility of a representation. For a Haar measure `μ_v` on `G(F_v)` and
  `hρ : IsAdmissible ρ`, `Langlands.Character.distribution μ_v ρ hρ f_v` is the trace of the
  finite-rank operator `ρ(f_v) = ∫ f_v(y) ρ(y) dμ_v(y)`. The twisted distribution
  `Langlands.BaseChange.twistedDistribution μ_v ρ hρ A f_v` is the trace of `ρ(f_v) ∘ A`.
- `IsTraceClass` is the Library's summability predicate for the real diagonal of the positive
  left modulus on a Hilbert basis. `operatorTrace` sums the complex diagonal of the operator
  in a chosen Hilbert basis. For a complete subspace `P` of a Hilbert space `E`, the
  compression of an operator `X : E →L[ℂ] E` to `P` is `P_P X ι_P`. Here `P_P` and `ι_P` are
  Mathlib's `P.orthogonalProjectionOnto` and `P.subtypeL`, and the composition is
  `P.orthogonalProjectionOnto ∘L X ∘L P.subtypeL : P →L[ℂ] P`.

The statement takes `[MeasurableSpace G(F_v)] [BorelSpace G(F_v)] [LocallyCompactSpace G(F_v)]`
and `[TotallyDisconnectedSpace G(F_v)]` as instance binders. The usual topology and Borel
σ-algebra on points of an affine group scheme of finite type over a `p`-adic field give these
structures.

## Statement

For all group data as above, every finite place `v` with the four instances, every Haar
measure `μ_v` on `G(F_v)` and every Haar measure `ν` on `G(𝔸_F)`, take `P : Submodule ℂ S` with
`[CompleteSpace P]` and `P ≠ ⊥`. Assume that `P` is invariant under every `π_c(a)` and every
closed subspace `Q ≤ P` invariant under all `π_c(a)` is `⊥` or `P`. Then there are `V : Type u`
with `AddCommGroup V` and `Module ℂ V`, a representation `ρ : Representation ℂ G(F_v) V`, and
`hρ : IsAdmissible ρ`. This `ρ` satisfies `ρ.IsIrreducible` and both parts below.

1. There is a function `C : (G(𝔸_F) → ℂ) → ℂ` with the following property. Let
   `f_v : SchwartzBruhat G(F_v)` and `f^v : G(𝔸_F) → ℂ` satisfy
   `f^v(g · ofPlace x) = f^v(g)` for all `g ∈ G(𝔸_F)` and `x ∈ G(F_v)`. Let
   `f(g) = f_v(g_v) · f^v(g)` and suppose `hf : Integrable f ν`. If the compression
   `P_P R_S(f) ι_P` satisfies `IsTraceClass`, then
   `operatorTrace (P_P R_S(f) ι_P) = Character.distribution μ_v ρ hρ f_v · C(f^v)`.
2. For every `Θ`, `hΘ`, `hΘμ` as above and every topological group automorphism `θ_v` of
   `G(F_v)`, assume `(Θ g)_v = θ_v(g_v)` for all `g ∈ G(𝔸_F)` and
   `Θ(ofPlace x) = ofPlace(θ_v x)` for all `x ∈ G(F_v)`. Suppose that for every `x ∈ P`
   there is `y ∈ P` with `y = I_Θ x` in `L²`. Then there are a bijective complex-linear
   map `A_v : V → V` with `A_v(ρ(y) w) = ρ(θ_v y)(A_v w)` for all `y ∈ G(F_v)` and `w ∈ V`,
   and a function `C : (G(𝔸_F) → ℂ) → ℂ` with the following property. For `f_v`, `f^v`, `f` and
   `hf` as in part 1, let `B = P_S (R(f) ∘ I_Θ) ι_S : S →L[ℂ] S` be the compression to `S`.
   If `P_P B ι_P` satisfies `IsTraceClass`, then
   `operatorTrace (P_P B ι_P) = BaseChange.twistedDistribution μ_v ρ hρ A_v f_v · C(f^v)`.

Each part chooses a single function `C` before quantifying over `f_v` and `f^v`. Its choice may
depend on all preceding data, including the two Haar measures, `P` and `ρ`, and in the twisted
part `Θ`, `θ_v` and `A_v`. Trace class is a hypothesis on the corresponding compression.

## Mathematical argument

Write `H = ker(atPlace F G (.inr v))` and `ι_v = ofPlace F G (.inr v)`. The map
`(x, h) ↦ ι_v(x)h` gives the topological group isomorphism `G(F_v) × H ≃ G(𝔸_F)`. The two
subgroups commute because their components commute at each place, and the inverse sends `g` to
`(g_v, ι_v(g_v)⁻¹g)`. The projection and insertion are continuous. For Haar measure `ν^v`
on `H`, Haar uniqueness makes `ν` a positive scalar `c` times the transported product
`μ_v × ν^v`. In these coordinates `f^v` is pulled back from its restriction to `H` by the right
invariance hypothesis. Write `f^v|_H` for that restriction.

The right-invariant measure `μ_Q` and continuous orbit maps give a strongly continuous unitary
representation on `S`. Its restriction to `P` is irreducible by the closed-subspace condition.
The group `G(F_v)` is of type I by Getz–Hahn, Theorem 3.10.2, so Folland's Theorem 7.17 identifies
this representation with `π_v ⊗̂ π^v` for irreducible unitary factors. By Getz–Hahn, Proposition
5.3.8 and Theorem 5.3.9, the smooth space `V` of `π_v` carries an irreducible admissible
representation `ρ`. Every open subgroup contains a compact open subgroup, so finite
dimensionality of compact-open fixed vectors also gives the all-open-subgroups convention.

Since `P` is closed and invariant, the Bochner integrals defining `R_S(f)` and `R(f)` preserve
it. The compression `P_P R_S(f) ι_P` is thus the restriction of `R_S(f)`. For nonzero `f_v`,
its nonzero set is open with positive Haar measure. Fubini supplies an integrable slice of
`f` at a point of this set. Dividing the slice by the nonzero value of `f_v` gives integrability
of `f^v|_H`, even though `f^v` is quantified as an arbitrary function. Bochner Fubini on
elementary tensors then gives the compressed action `c · π_v(f_v) ⊗ π^v(f^v|_H)`.

The local image of `π_v(f_v)` is fixed by a compact open subgroup of left invariance of `f_v`
and is finite dimensional. On `V` the Bochner integral and the algebraic action give the
same finite sum over compact open cosets. The trace can be computed on the finite-dimensional
space of fixed vectors, so it equals `Character.distribution μ_v ρ hρ f_v`. Suppose a nonzero
bounded `X` has `X ⊗ Y` trace class. Precompose by `w ↦ u ⊗ w` and postcompose by the first-factor
matrix coefficient at a vector `z`, chosen so that `⟨z, Xu⟩ = 1`. The two-sided ideal law makes
the resulting operator `Y` trace class. The product basis then computes
`tr (X ⊗ Y) = tr X · tr Y` for finite-rank `X` and trace-class `Y`. Take `C(f^v)` to be
`c · tr π^v(f^v|_H)` when the restriction is integrable and its action is trace class, and
zero otherwise. If `f_v = 0` the global and local operators vanish. If `f_v ≠ 0` with
`π_v(f_v) = 0`, the tensor expression also gives zero on both sides.

The compatibility equations for `Θ` imply `Θ = θ_v × Θ^v` in the product coordinates. The
pullback identity `R(a) I_Θ = I_Θ R(Θ⁻¹ a)` makes `I_Θ(P)` invariant under right translation.
This is an isometric image of the nonzero complete `P`, so it is closed, and the assumed
inclusion and irreducibility give `I_Θ(P) = P`. The restriction is a unitary intertwiner
from `π_v ⊗̂ π^v` to `(π_v ∘ θ_v) ⊗̂ (π^v ∘ Θ^v)`. Uniqueness of the factors up to
equivalence and Schur's lemma express it as `U_v ⊗ U^v` for unitary intertwiners, with any
unit scalar absorbed into `U^v`. The map `U_v` and its inverse preserve smooth vectors
because `θ_v` carries compact open subgroups to compact open subgroups. The restriction
`A_v = U_v|_V` gives the required bijection and intertwining equation.

Both `I_Θ` and `R(f)` preserve `P ⊆ S`, so the twisted compression is the restriction of
`R(f) ∘ I_Θ` to `P`. For nonzero `f_v` this is
`c · (π_v(f_v) U_v) ⊗ (π^v(f^v|_H) U^v)`. The local operator has the same finite-rank image
as in the untwisted part and its restriction to `V` computes
`Langlands.BaseChange.twistedDistribution μ_v ρ hρ A_v f_v`. Apply the same trace-class
and trace-product argument with `C(f^v) = c · tr (π^v(f^v|_H) U^v)` when the restriction is
integrable and the away operator is trace class, and zero otherwise. A zero local operator
again gives zero on both sides.

## Provenance

This proposition is derived from the representation and intertwining operator factorizations
used in automorphic trace comparisons. Its formulation splits off one place of the actual
cuspidal `L²` action, packages the remaining trace and any Haar scaling into `C`, and computes
the trace conditional on trace class. The twist is a quotient-compatible topological
automorphism with the specified local action. Its local intertwiner may be rescaled by a nonzero
scalar, with the inverse scalar absorbed into `C`.

- Henniart–Lemaire, *Changement de base et induction automorphe pour GL_n en caractéristique
  non nulle* (2011), Proposition of II.6.5 (printed pp. 121–122, PDF pp. 129–130). For a
  `σ`-stable cuspidal `Π` the global operator `I_σ(Π)` is the tensor product of Whittaker-normalized
  local operators `I_σ(Π_v)`. That factorization enters the trace comparison II.6.6 (1)
  (printed p. 123, PDF p. 131).
- Getz–Hahn, *An Introduction to Automorphic Representations* (2024), Theorem 3.10.2
  (printed p. 97, PDF p. 113) gives type I for the points of affine algebraic groups over
  characteristic-zero local fields. Proposition 5.3.8 and Theorem 5.3.9 (printed p. 141,
  PDF p. 156) relate unitary irreducibility to smooth irreducibility and give admissibility
  for the smooth vectors of an irreducible unitary representation of a nonarchimedean
  reductive group. Flath's Theorem 5.7.1 (printed p. 152, PDF p. 167) and (11.14) (printed
  p. 300, PDF p. 311) give the broader restricted tensor product context. Equation (18.33)
  (printed p. 487, PDF p. 494) gives the pullback convention for the twist.
- Folland, *A Course in Abstract Harmonic Analysis*, 2nd ed. (2015), Theorem 7.17
  (printed p. 237, PDF p. 250). The unitary dual of a product with a type I factor is the
  product of the unitary duals via the outer tensor product.
