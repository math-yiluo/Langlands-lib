# Spectral expansion of traces on the cuspidal space

`Langlands.TraceFormula.GroupSchemeTwistedCuspidalSpectralExpansionStatement.{u}` states the
ordinary and twisted trace expansions of an integrated right action with cuspidal image in
the Library's group-scheme model.

## Group data and operators

The group data are those of
`Langlands.Automorphic.GroupSchemeFunctions.GroupSchemeFullAEL2CuspidalDecompositionStatement`.
Take a number field `F : Type u` and an affine group scheme `G` over `Spec F` that is locally
of finite type. Assume `hPD : ParabolicDynamicStatement.{u}` and `hG : IsConnectedReductive G`.
Take a commutative group scheme `T` over `Spec F` and a morphism `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G` with
`hc : IsCentral i`, `hi : Function.Injective (characterRestrict i)`, and finite-index range of
`characterRestrict i`. Take a finite index type `I : Type u` and an isomorphism
`e : T.toGrp ≅ splitTorus (Spec (CommRingCat.of F)) I`. Assume Haar measure on
`normOneSubgroup F G` is right invariant.

- Let `Γ = positiveRationalSubgroup F G T i`. It is generated by the rational points and the
  image of the positive real subgroup of `T`. Write `[G]` for the left quotient of
  `AdelicGroupPoints F G` by `Γ` and `μ_Q` for `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure F G T i`. The
  ambient Hilbert space is `H = Lp ℂ 2 μ_Q`.
- Let `S = fullAEL2CuspidalSubmodule F G hPD hG T i hi I e hc`. It consists of classes in
  `H` whose periods along unipotent radicals of proper rational parabolics converge and
  vanish almost everywhere, with respect to Haar measure on the norm-one subgroup. The
  Library supplies its completeness through `fullAEL2Cuspidal_completeSpace`.
- Write `π_c(a)` for `fullAEL2CuspidalBoundedRightAction F G hPD hG T i hi I e hc a`, the
  right translation by `a` on `S`. For any measure `ν` on `AdelicGroupPoints F G` and any
  `f : AdelicGroupPoints F G → ℂ` with `hf : Integrable f ν`, let `R(f)` be
  `haarQuotientL2IntegratedAction F G T i hi I e hc ν f hf` on `H` and let `R_S(f)` be
  `fullAEL2CuspidalIntegratedAction F G hPD hG T i hi I e hc ν f hf` on `S`. They act by
  taking the Bochner integral of the weighted orbit of each vector under the respective
  right action.
- For the twisted part take a topological group automorphism `Θ` of `AdelicGroupPoints F G`
  with `hΘ : ∀ x, Θ x ∈ Γ ↔ x ∈ Γ`. Then `quotientAutomorphism Γ Θ hΘ` is the induced
  homeomorphism of `[G]`. Assume `hΘμ : MeasurePreserving (quotientAutomorphism Γ Θ hΘ).symm
  μ_Q μ_Q`. The bounded map `I_Θ = twistOperator Γ Θ hΘ μ_Q hΘμ` acts by pullback under that
  inverse, so its action on an `L²` class is represented almost everywhere by
  `φ ↦ φ ∘ Θ⁻¹` on the quotient.
- Write `tr` for `operatorTrace`, the Library's diagonal sum in a chosen Hilbert basis. On
  the domain of `IsTraceClass`, that sum is basis-independent.

For a complete subspace `P` of a Hilbert space `E`, write `p_P` for Mathlib's
`P.orthogonalProjectionOnto : E →L[ℂ] P` and `ι_P` for `P.subtypeL : P →L[ℂ] E`. The
compression of `X : E →L[ℂ] E` to `P` is `p_P ∘L X ∘L ι_P`. A decomposition of `S` is
specified by `A : Set (Submodule ℂ S)` with `[∀ P : A, CompleteSpace (P : Submodule ℂ S)]`
and

```lean
IsHilbertSum ℂ (fun P : A => (P : Submodule ℂ S))
  (fun P => (P : Submodule ℂ S).subtypeₗᵢ)
```

Here `subtypeₗᵢ` is the isometric inclusion. The inclusions are pairwise orthogonal and induce
a surjective isometry from the external Hilbert sum to `S`.

## Proposition

For every group datum and every `ν`, `f`, and `hf` above, suppose `IsTraceClass R(f)` and
`R(f) φ ∈ S` for every `φ : H`. Both expansions hold.

1. **Ordinary trace.** `R_S(f)` is trace class and `tr R_S(f) = tr R(f)`. For every
   Hilbert sum decomposition `A` as above, each `c_P = p_P ∘L R_S(f) ∘L ι_P` is trace
   class. The family `fun P : A => ‖tr c_P‖` is summable, and `fun P : A => tr c_P` has
   sum `tr R(f)` in Mathlib's sense of `HasSum`.
2. **Twisted trace.** For every `Θ`, `hΘ`, and `hΘμ` above, let
   `B = p_S ∘L (R(f) ∘L I_Θ) ∘L ι_S`. Then `B` is trace class and
   `tr B = tr (R(f) ∘L I_Θ)`. Take any Hilbert sum decomposition `A` as above in which
   every `P : A` is invariant under every `π_c(a)`. For any `τ : A → A`, assume that for
   every `P : A` and `x : S` with `x ∈ P`, there is a `y : S` with `y ∈ τ P` and
   `(y : H) = I_Θ (x : H)`. Then each `c_P = p_P ∘L B ∘L ι_P` is trace class, and
   `fun P : A => ‖tr c_P‖` is summable over all of `A`. The family of traces on the subtype
   `{P : A // τ P = P}`, obtained by taking the trace of the compression to `P.1`, has sum
   `tr (R(f) ∘L I_Θ)` in the sense of `HasSum`.

## Mathematical argument

`fullAEL2CuspidalIntegratedAction_val` identifies `R_S(f)` with `p_S R(f) ι_S`. More
generally, if a trace-class operator `X` on `H` has range contained in `S`, the ideal property
gives trace class for `p_S X ι_S`. Put `Q = ι_S p_S`, the ambient orthogonal projection onto
`S`. Rectangular cyclicity gives `tr (p_S X ι_S) = tr (X Q)`. By cyclicity this equals
`tr (Q X) = tr X`, since `Q` acts as the identity on the range of `X`.

For any trace-class `Y` on `S`, the ideal property gives trace class for each compression
to `P : A`. Hilbert bases of the summands combine into a Hilbert basis of `S`. The diagonal
entries of `Y` in this basis are absolutely summable by `operatorTraceBasis`. Group them
by summand. The diagonal entries in each group are those of the corresponding compression,
and the sum of their absolute values bounds the norm of its trace. This yields both
summability of the norms of the traces and `HasSum` with sum `tr Y`. Use `Y = R_S(f)` for
the first expansion.

The ideal property also gives trace class for `R(f) ∘L I_Θ`, whose range lies in `S`.
Compressing to `S` therefore preserves the trace as above. For each invariant summand
`P : A`, the Bochner integral giving `R_S(f)` preserves `P`, because each weighted orbit
of a vector in `P` lies in that closed subspace. The hypothesis on `τ` thus implies
`B x = R_S(f) y ∈ τ P` for `x ∈ P` and `y` as in the proposition. If `τ P ≠ P`, orthogonality
makes `p_P B ι_P` zero. Applying the Hilbert sum argument to `Y = B` and restricting the
sum to fixed points gives the second expansion.

## Sources and scope

The statement is derived content. It expresses both spectral expansions as Hilbert-space
identities for the actual Library operators, for any measure and integrable weight meeting
the trace-class and range hypotheses. The decomposition is supplied. In the twisted
expansion, the supplied map `τ` records containment of the pullback images of summands.

- Getz–Hahn, *An Introduction to Automorphic Representations* (2024), Theorem 18.2.5
  (printed p. 479) gives the simple relative trace formula. In the ordinary diagonal case,
  Corollary 18.4.1 and its proof (printed pp. 485–486) identify the spectral terms as
  `m(π) tr π(f)`, the traces on the cuspidal isotypic components. Equations (18.33)–(18.36)
  and Corollary 18.5.1 (printed pp. 487–488) use the inverse pullback and sum the twisted
  traces over the isotypic components stable under the twist. The present statement uses
  compressions indexed by the supplied Hilbert sum. For the stable isotypic components in
  Getz–Hahn's setting, the trace of a compression includes the multiplicity of the
  representation. [Book](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-41153-3)
- Henniart–Lemaire, *Changement de base et induction automorphe pour GL_n en caractéristique
  non nulle* (2011), II.6.3–II.6.5 (printed pp. 119–122) expand the ordinary and twisted
  traces on their cuspidal spaces with prescribed central characters. Their comparison
  equality (1) in II.6.6 (printed p. 123) is
  `d ∑_Π tr (Π(φ) ∘ A_Π) = ∑_π tr π(f)`, with `Π` ranging over the `σ`-stable cuspidal
  representations and `A_Π` induced by the global pullback on the fixed summand. The
  corresponding compression here is the operator on a summand fixed by `τ`, using the
  quotient pullback defined by `I_Θ`. Henniart–Lemaire write their pullback as
  `ξ(g^σ)`, so the operator conventions agree when the induced inverse of `Θ` acts as
  `g ↦ g^σ`. Their factor `d` belongs to the trace comparison between the two groups.
