# Integrated smooth tests for archimedean character separation

The mixed archimedean coordinates transport compact smooth kernels between
`InfiniteAdelicGL F n` and the real manifold of units. `leftTranslate` is pullback
along left multiplication by the inverse of a group element. `correlation` has
value `∫ a, conj (f a) * f (a * g) dμ(a)` at `g`. It is smooth and compactly
supported by the compact smooth convolution theorem applied to the pushforward
of the measure under inversion.

The integrated operators are linear in the test and commute with bounded
intertwiners. Left invariance identifies the integrated translate with left
multiplication by the group action. In the correlation identity, the
measure-preserving shear `(a, g) ↦ (a, a * g)` controls product integrability.
Fubini and left invariance rewrite the integral as the average of adjoints
applied to the integrated operator. The result is `A(f)† A(f)`.

A positive normalized smooth weight in an identity neighborhood integrates any
fixed nonzero vector to within half its norm. This gives a test with nonzero
integrated image on that vector.
