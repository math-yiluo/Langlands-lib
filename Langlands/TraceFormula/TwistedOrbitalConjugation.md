# Conjugation on twisted orbital integrals

The constructions use the left quotient convention of `Langlands.TraceFormula.TwistedOrbital` and the compatible measures of `Langlands.QuotientIntegration.IsCompatible`. They supply the change of representative needed to apply parabolic descent to the base-change orbital integrals of Henniart–Lemaire, I.2.4–I.2.5.

For a twist by an endomorphism \(\theta\), the stabilizer of \(x^{-1}\delta\theta(x)\) is identified with the stabilizer of \(\delta\) by conjugation by \(x^{-1}\). On orbit parameters the corresponding homeomorphism is left translation by \(x^{-1}\). The proof transports the stabilizer measure by the group isomorphism and the quotient measure by that homeomorphism. It verifies the five fields of quotient compatibility, using left invariance of the ambient measure in the Weil formula. Subgroup averaging and the orbital integral itself are compared by change of variables along measurable equivalences.
