# Irreducible cuspidal `L²` summands of `Res_{L/K} GL_n` and cuspidal realizations over `L` (statements)

Module `Langlands.TraceFormula.WeilGeneralLinearCuspidalSummandRealizationStatement`, namespace
`Langlands.TraceFormula`. It defines one auxiliary proposition and states two propositions. It
contains no proof:

- `Langlands.TraceFormula.IsWeilFormClass`, a proposition relating an `L²` class on the
  positive-central rational quotient of `Res_{L/K} GL_{r+1}` to a continuous function on
  `GL_{r+1}(𝔸_L)`;
- `Langlands.TraceFormula.WeilGeneralLinearCuspidalSummandRealizationStatement.{u}` (from `L²`
  summands to realizations);
- `Langlands.TraceFormula.WeilGeneralLinearCuspidalRealizationSummandStatement.{u}` (from
  realizations to `L²` summands).

The module is the analogue, for the Weil restriction `W = Res_{L/K} GL_{r+1}` of a finite
extension `L/K` of number fields, of
`Langlands.Automorphic.GroupSchemeFunctions.GeneralLinearCuspidalSummandRealizationStatement` and
`GeneralLinearCuspidalRealizationSummandStatement` (module
`Langlands.Automorphic.GeneralLinearCuspidalSummandRealizationStatement`), which treat
`GL_{r+1}` over one number field. It links two Library models:

- the `L²` model in which the Library's twisted trace formula for `W` is stated
  (`Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement`): the full
  almost-everywhere cuspidal subspace of `L²` of the positive-central rational quotient of
  `W(𝔸_K)`;
- the model of cuspidal automorphic representations of `GL_{r+1}(𝔸_L)` by forms,
  `Langlands.Automorphic.CuspidalOperatorRealization L r`, on which the Library states local
  conditions at the places of `L`.

The two local instances supply `IsAffine` and `Smooth` for the general linear group scheme over
`K` from the published `GroupScheme.GeneralLinear.isAffine_groupScheme` and
`GroupScheme.GeneralLinear.smoothStatement`, as in the other Weil-restriction modules (for example
`GeneralLinearWeilAdelicTwistStatement`). They are not exported.

## Setting and notation from the Library

- `K L : Type u` are number fields with `[Algebra K L]` and `[FiniteDimensional K L]`. The adele
  quotient `AdeleQuotient L` is assumed compact (an instance argument that
  `CuspidalOperatorRealization L r` requires; the adele quotient of a number field is compact).
  `r : ℕ`.
- `W` is `weilGroup K L (GroupScheme.GeneralLinear.groupScheme (Fin (r + 1)) (CommRingCat.of K))`,
  the Library's Weil restriction to `K` of the base change to `L` of `GL_{r+1}` (written `Wᵣ` in
  the Lean file). `AdelicGroupPoints K W` is `W(𝔸_K)` with its adelic topology.
- `hPD : ParabolicDynamicStatement` is the standing structural hypothesis of the Library's
  cuspidal `L²` spaces. `hG : IsConnectedReductive W` is a binder, as in the Library's twisted
  trace formula statements for `W` (for example `GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement`).
- `T` is a commutative group scheme over `K` with a central morphism `i : T → W` (`IsCentral i`),
  an isomorphism `e` of `T` with a split torus on a finite type `I`, and injective character
  restriction `characterRestrict i` with finite-index range (an instance argument). Haar measure on
  the norm-one subgroup of `W(𝔸_K)` is right invariant (an instance argument). These are the
  hypotheses of `GeneralLinearCuspidalSummandRealizationStatement`, here for `W`.
- `Q := PositiveRationalQuotient K W T i` is the quotient of `W(𝔸_K)` by the subgroup
  `positiveRationalSubgroup K W T i` generated by `W(K)` and the positive real points of `T`, and
  `μQ := positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure K W T i` is its quotient Haar measure.
  `LeftCosetSpace.mk (positiveRationalSubgroup K W T i) g` is the image of `g` in `Q`.
- `S := fullAEL2CuspidalSubmodule K W hPD hG T i hi I e hc` is the closed subspace of
  `Lp ℂ 2 μQ` of classes whose constant terms along every proper parabolic subgroup vanish almost
  everywhere, and `πc a := fullAEL2CuspidalBoundedRightAction K W hPD hG T i hi I e hc a` is right
  translation by `a ∈ W(𝔸_K)` on `S`.
- `Langlands.Automorphic.ContinuousRationalFunctions L (r + 1)` is the space of continuous
  functions on `GL_{r+1}(𝔸_L)` (matrix model, `AdelicGL L (r + 1)`), and
  `ContinuousRationalFunctions.evaluate L (r + 1) f x` evaluates `f` at `x`.
- `R : Langlands.Automorphic.CuspidalOperatorRealization L r` is an irreducible cuspidal subspace
  of automorphic forms on `GL_{r+1}(𝔸_L)` (matrix model), stable under the finite adelic group,
  the standard maximal compact subgroup at infinity and the enveloping algebra. `R.Space` is its
  carrier and `R.realization.toContinuous` the injective linear map sending a vector to its
  continuous function.

### The comparison map `Φ`

`Φ : W(𝔸_K) → AdelicGL L (r + 1)` is the composite used in
`GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement`, `GeneralLinearTwistedEllipticCentralizerStatement`
and `GeneralLinearWeilAdelicTwistStatement`:

1. `AdelicGaloisUnit.weilAdelicEquiv K L GLᵣ₊₁` takes a point of `W(𝔸_K)` to an `𝔸_L`-point of
   `AffineWeil.unitBaseChange K L GLᵣ₊₁`;
2. `AdelicGroupPoints.map L` of
   `(GroupScheme.GeneralLinear.baseChangeIso (Fin (r + 1)) (Spec.algebraMap K L)).hom` takes it
   to an `𝔸_L`-point of the general linear group scheme over `L`;
3. `GeneralLinearGroupPoints.adelicHomeomorph L (r + 1)` identifies that point with an invertible
   matrix over `𝔸_L`.

`GeneralLinearWeilAdelicTwistStatement` states that `Φ` underlies an isomorphism of
topological groups `W(𝔸_K) ≃ₜ* GL_{r+1}(𝔸_L)`, that it carries the Galois automorphism
`adelicAut K (unitGaloisAut K L GLᵣ₊₁ σ)` to the entrywise action of `σ` on adelic matrices, and
that `g ∈ W(K)` if and only if `Φ g ∈ GL_{r+1}(L)`. The present module does not assume that
statement; it only uses the map. The map is written out in both places where it is needed.

## `IsWeilFormClass K L r T i φ f`

For `φ : Lp ℂ 2 μQ` and `f : ContinuousRationalFunctions L (r + 1)`,
`IsWeilFormClass K L r T i φ f` holds when there is a continuous function `h : C(Q, ℂ)` with

- `h (LeftCosetSpace.mk (positiveRationalSubgroup K W T i) g) = evaluate f (Φ g)` for every
  `g ∈ W(𝔸_K)`, and
- `φ = h` `μQ`-almost everywhere.

So `f ∘ Φ` is invariant on the left under `W(K)` and the positive real points of `T`, it
descends to the continuous function `h` on `Q`, and `φ` is the `L²` class of `h`. The definition
is that of `IsGeneralLinearFormClass`, with the identification of
`continuousGeneralLinearEquiv` replaced by composition with `Φ`.

## The statements

### `WeilGeneralLinearCuspidalSummandRealizationStatement` (from `L²` summands to realizations)

For all data as above, let `P` be a submodule of `S` that is nonzero, closed, invariant under
`πc a` for every `a ∈ W(𝔸_K)`, and minimal (every closed invariant submodule `P' ≤ P` is `⊥` or
`P`). Then there are `R : CuspidalOperatorRealization L r` and a linear map
`Ψ : R.Space →ₗ[ℂ] S` such that

- for every `f : R.Space`, `IsWeilFormClass K L r T i (Ψ f) (R.realization.toContinuous f)`:
  `Ψ f` is the `L²` class of the function on `Q` induced by the form `f`; and
- the topological closure of the range of `Ψ` is `P`.

The first condition determines `Ψ`. The second gives `Ψ f ∈ P` for every `f` and density of the
range in `P`.

### `WeilGeneralLinearCuspidalRealizationSummandStatement` (from realizations to `L²` summands)

For all data as above, let `R : CuspidalOperatorRealization L r` be such that for every
`f : R.Space` there is `h : C(Q, ℂ)` with `h (LeftCosetSpace.mk _ g) = evaluate
(R.realization.toContinuous f) (Φ g)` for every `g`. Then there are a submodule `P` of `S` that is
nonzero, closed, invariant and minimal as above, and a linear map `Ψ : R.Space →ₗ[ℂ] S` with
`IsWeilFormClass K L r T i (Ψ f) (R.realization.toContinuous f)` for every `f`, such that the
closure of the range of `Ψ` is `P`.

The descent hypothesis is needed for the same reason as in
`GeneralLinearCuspidalRealizationSummandStatement`: the forms of `R` are
functions on `GL_{r+1}(L) \ GL_{r+1}(𝔸_L)`, and they define functions on `Q` only if they are
invariant under `Φ` of the positive real points of `T`.

## Why the statements are true

1. **Transport of the quotient.** By `GeneralLinearWeilAdelicTwistStatement`, `Φ` is an isomorphism of topological groups
   with `Φ(W(K)) = GL_{r+1}(L)`. Hence it induces a homeomorphism of `Q` onto the quotient
   `Q_L` of `GL_{r+1}(𝔸_L)` by the subgroup generated by `GL_{r+1}(L)` and the central subgroup
   `A := Φ(positive real points of T)`, carrying `μQ` to a Haar quotient measure on `Q_L`.
   Right translation by `a` on `Q` corresponds to right translation by `Φ a` on `Q_L`.
2. **Transport of cuspidality.** The parabolic subgroups of `W` over `K` are the Weil
   restrictions of the parabolic subgroups of `GL_{r+1}` over `L`, with
   `N_W(K) \ N_W(𝔸_K) = N(L) \ N(𝔸_L)` for the unipotent radicals, and the quotient Haar measures
   correspond. So the constant terms along `K`-parabolics of `W` are the constant terms along
   `L`-parabolics of `GL_{r+1}`, and `S` corresponds to the cuspidal subspace of `L²(Q_L)`.
3. **Getz–Hahn 9.7.3 over `L`.** For `GL_{r+1}` over `L`, the irreducible subrepresentations of
   the cuspidal subspace of `L²` and the cuspidal automorphic representations correspond: the
   `K`-finite vectors of an irreducible summand form an irreducible space of cusp forms, and an
   irreducible space of cusp forms invariant under the central subgroup lies in, and is dense in,
   an irreducible summand. The proof of Getz–Hahn's Theorem 9.7.3 applies with `A_G` replaced by
   the central subgroup `A`: cusp forms invariant under `A` have a unitary central character
   on `A \ Z(𝔸_L)`, which is compact modulo `Z(L)`. Cusp forms are rapidly decreasing modulo
   the centre (Getz–Hahn Theorem 9.7.1), hence square integrable on `Q_L`. Composing with `Φ`
   gives both statements, as for `GL_{r+1}` over one number field
   (`GeneralLinearCuspidalSummandRealizationStatement` and its converse).

## Provenance

This is derived content, formulated for this project: the content of
`GeneralLinearCuspidalSummandRealizationStatement` and
`GeneralLinearCuspidalRealizationSummandStatement` for `GL_{r+1}` over `L`, transported to the
Weil restriction by the identification of `GeneralLinearWeilAdelicTwistStatement`.

- J. R. Getz and H. Hahn, *An Introduction to Automorphic Representations*, Graduate Texts in
  Mathematics 300, Springer, 2024 (registered literature source
  `getz-hahn-2024-automorphic-representations`):
  - §1.4, printed p. 12: the Weil restriction of scalars `Res_{k'/k} X'(R) := X'(R ⊗_k k')`;
  - §13.4, printed p. 350: `Res_{E/F} GL_n(𝔸_F) := GL_n(𝔸_E)`, the identification used to
    compare automorphic representations of the two groups;
  - Theorem 9.7.1, printed p. 264 (rapid decrease of cusp forms) and Theorem 9.7.3, printed
    p. 265, with its proof on pp. 265–267 (cuspidal `L²` summands and cuspidal automorphic
    representations), as cited in the Markdown of `GeneralLinearCuspidalSummandRealizationStatement`.
- The Library statements `GeneralLinearCuspidalSummandRealizationStatement` and
  `GeneralLinearCuspidalRealizationSummandStatement`, whose form is followed, and
  `GeneralLinearWeilAdelicTwistStatement`, which identifies `W(𝔸_K)` with
  `GL_{r+1}(𝔸_L)`.

Differences from the sources, all of which keep the content: the summand is a closed invariant
submodule of the Library's full almost-everywhere cuspidal subspace of `W`, not an abstract
irreducible representation; the cuspidal automorphic representation is the Library's
`CuspidalOperatorRealization` over `L`; the correspondence is stated through the map `Ψ` and the
predicate `IsWeilFormClass` instead of an equality of isomorphism classes; and the central
subgroup is the positive real points of the given torus `T` instead of `A_G`.

## Use

In local cyclic base change for `GL_n`, these statements serve to pass between the cuspidal
spectrum of `W` in its twisted trace formula and cuspidal automorphic representations of `GL_{r+1}(𝔸_L)`, on which the Library
states local conditions (for example supercuspidality of a local component in
`Langlands.Automorphic.Unramified.CyclicWeakBaseChangeSupercuspidalStatement`). The first
statement turns a summand of the spectral side into a realization over `L`; the second turns a
given cuspidal representation over `L` (for example in the restricted base change statement
with a second supercuspidal place) into a summand to which the trace formula applies. The local
factor of such a summand is identified in
`Langlands.TraceFormula.WeilGeneralLinearCuspidalLocalTwistedTraceStatement`.
