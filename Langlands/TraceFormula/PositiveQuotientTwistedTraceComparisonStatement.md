# Twisted comparison of traces on the positive and norm-one quotients

`Langlands.TraceFormula.PositiveQuotientTwistedTraceComparisonStatement.{u}` is a
proposition definition for comparing the integrated right actions on
`L²(A⁺ G(F) \ G(𝔸_F))` and `L²(G(F) \ G(𝔸_F)¹)` after twisting.

## Data and measures

Take a number field `F : Type u` and a group scheme `G` over `Spec F` whose underlying
scheme is affine and whose structure morphism is locally of finite type. Write
`G(𝔸_F)` for `AdelicGroupPoints F G` and `G(𝔸_F)¹` for `normOneSubgroup F G`, the
intersection of the kernels of absolute values of rational characters. Assume
`Measure.haar` on the norm-one group is right invariant.

Take a commutative group scheme `T` over `Spec F` and a morphism `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G`.
Use a finite index type `I : Type u` with a `Fintype` instance, a splitting
`e : T.toGrp ≅ splitTorus (Spec (CommRingCat.of F)) I`, and centrality
`hc : IsCentral i`. The rational-character restriction `characterRestrict i` is
injective by `hi` and has finite-index range. The group `intrinsicSubgroup F T`
of positive real points maps into `G(𝔸_F)` by `positivePointMap F G T i`. Write
`A⁺` for its image. The map `(a, x) ↦ positivePointMap F G T i a * x` sends
`normalizedPositiveHaarMeasure F G T i hi I e hc` times norm-one `Measure.haar`
to ambient `Measure.haar`, by `map_normalizedPositiveHaarMeasure_prod`.

- `positiveRationalSubgroup F G T i` is `A⁺ G(F)`, the subgroup generated by the
  positive image and the rational points. Its left coset space carries
  `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure F G T i`.
- `rationalNormOneSubgroup F G` is the image of `G(F)` in `G(𝔸_F)¹`.
  `NormOneRationalQuotient F G` is its left coset space and
  `normOneRationalQuotientHaarMeasure F G` is its quotient measure, compatible
  with norm-one Haar and counting measure on the rational subgroup.
- The inclusion of norm-one cosets into positive cosets is the homeomorphism
  `positiveQuotientHomeomorph`. The positive quotient measure is the pushforward
  of the norm-one quotient measure by this inclusion.

## Statement

Let `τ : Aut G` have finite order (`hτ : IsOfFinOrder τ`) and fix the torus
pointwise (`i ≫ τ.hom = i`). The induced continuous automorphism of `G(𝔸_F)` is
`Θ = adelicAut F τ`, and `θ = normOneAut F τ` is its restriction to `G(𝔸_F)¹`.
The proposition concludes that there are proofs

- `hΘ` of `Θ(x) ∈ A⁺ G(F) ↔ x ∈ A⁺ G(F)` for every adelic point `x`.
- `hΘμ` that the inverse of `quotientAutomorphism (A⁺ G(F)) Θ hΘ` preserves the
  positive quotient measure.

These two conclusions define the positive-quotient twist for the automorphism,
independently of the choice of test function. Indeed, functoriality on points
and `i ≫ τ.hom = i` give pointwise fixation of `A⁺`, and `τ` carries rational
points onto rational points. On cosets, the inclusion intertwines the maps
induced by `θ` and `Θ`. The norm-one inverse quotient map preserves its measure
by `measurePreserving_rationalQuotientAut_symm F τ hτ`, which transfers to the
positive quotient by the pushforward formula.

`quotientAutomorphism` sends a coset `Γ x` to `Γ Θ(x)` for an automorphism
preserving `Γ`. The operator `twistOperator` is pullback along the inverse
quotient map on `L²`. For `I_Θ` on the positive quotient, it uses `hΘ`, the
positive quotient measure and `hΘμ`. For `I_θ` on the norm-one quotient, it uses
`normOneAut_mem_rationalNormOneSubgroup_iff F τ`, the norm-one quotient measure
and `measurePreserving_rationalQuotientAut_symm F τ hτ`.

For every `f : C_c(G(𝔸_F), ℂ)`, put
`fOne(x) = ∫_{A⁺} f(a x) da` on `G(𝔸_F)¹`, using `positivePointMap` and the
normalized positive Haar measure above. Quantify over integrability proofs
`h` for `f` under ambient `Measure.haar` and `hOne` for `fOne` under norm-one
`Measure.haar`. Quantify over representations `π` of the norm-one group,
as monoid homomorphisms to the bounded complex-linear endomorphisms of its
quotient `L²` space. The representation hypotheses specialize those in
`AbstractSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement` (Task 7420) to this group and measure.

- `hπnorm` says each `π(g)` preserves the norm.
- `hπmeas` says each orbit `g ↦ π(g) φ` is almost everywhere strongly measurable
  for norm-one Haar.
- For every `g` and `φ`, the vector `π(g) φ` agrees with
  `q ↦ φ (rightTranslate (rationalNormOneSubgroup F G) g q)` almost everywhere
  for the norm-one quotient measure.

Write `R(f)` for `haarQuotientL2IntegratedAction F G T i hi I e hc Measure.haar f h`
and `π(f¹)` for
`Langlands.Analysis.integratedIsometricAction Measure.haar π hπnorm hπmeas fOne hOne`.
Both operators integrate the weighted action on each vector. The proposition
asserts the conjunction

```text
(IsTraceClass (R(f) ∘ I_Θ) ↔ IsTraceClass (π(f¹) ∘ I_θ))
∧ (IsTraceClass (R(f) ∘ I_Θ) →
    operatorTrace (R(f) ∘ I_Θ) = operatorTrace (π(f¹) ∘ I_θ))
```

## Unitary comparison

Pullback along the coset inclusion gives a unitary from the positive quotient
space to the norm-one quotient space by `positiveQuotientMeasurableEquiv` and
`measurePreserving_positiveQuotient`. It conjugates `I_Θ` to `I_θ` by the
equivariance on cosets. On the positive quotient, right translation by a
positive central point acts as the identity. After the Haar product
decomposition and integration in the positive factor, the almost everywhere
right translation formula for `π` identifies the transported `R(f)` with
`π(f¹)`. Thus `R(f) ∘ I_Θ` is unitarily equivalent to `π(f¹) ∘ I_θ`.

Unitary conjugation preserves the diagonal of the positive left modulus in
transported Hilbert bases, giving the trace-class equivalence. When the
positive quotient composite is trace class, `operatorTraceBasis` identifies
each `operatorTrace` with the same diagonal sum in corresponding bases.
