# The local trace factor of a cuspidal summand of `GL_n` is the Library's local component (statement)

Module `Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearCuspidalLocalComponentTraceStatement` contains one
definition and one proposition definition, and no proof:

- `Langlands.TraceFormula.localRealizationPlaceRepresentation`, a representation of the points of
  the group scheme `GL_{r+1}` over a completion `F_v`;
- `Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearCuspidalLocalComponentTraceStatement.{u, uV}`.

The statement is the untwisted part of
`Langlands.TraceFormula.GroupSchemeCuspidalLocalTraceFactorizationStatement` (Task 7701) for the
group `GL_{r+1}`, with the local factor identified. Task 7701 asserts that the traces on an
irreducible cuspidal summand `P` split off the character of *some* irreducible admissible
representation `ρ_v` of `GL_{r+1}(F_v)`. Here `ρ_v` is the Library's local component at `v` of a
cuspidal operator realization `R` attached to `P`. That is the object on which the Library states
local conditions, for example supercuspidality in the cyclic base change statements of
`Langlands.Automorphic.Unramified.CyclicWeakBaseChangeSupercuspidalStatement`. The link between
`P` and `R` is the one of
`Langlands.Automorphic.GroupSchemeFunctions.GeneralLinearCuspidalSummandRealizationStatement`
(module `Langlands.Automorphic.GeneralLinearCuspidalSummandRealizationStatement`).

The module also declares five `local instance`s of `AddCommGroup`. They provide the additive
group structure on the Library's form spaces `Langlands.Automorphic.RationalInvariantFunctions`,
`ContinuousRationalFunctions`, `FiniteSmoothRationalFunctions`, `AutomorphicForms` and on the
carrier of a `LocalRealization`, as the Library's own modules do (for example
`Langlands.Automorphic.PresentedLocalCategory`). They are not exported.

## Setting and notation from the Library

Group data. As in `GeneralLinearCuspidalSummandRealizationStatement`: a number field `F` whose
adele quotient `AdeleQuotient F` is compact (a Library instance hypothesis, needed by the
cuspidal constant terms), `r : ℕ`, the group scheme
`GLₙ := GroupScheme.GeneralLinear.groupScheme (Fin (r + 1)) (CommRingCat.of F)`, the hypothesis
`hPD : ParabolicDynamicStatement`, a commutative group scheme `T` with a central map
`i : T → GLₙ` whose character restriction is injective with finite-index range, an isomorphism
`e` of `T` with a split torus indexed by a finite type `I`, `hc : IsCentral i`, and right
invariance of Haar measure on the norm-one subgroup. The module supplies the instances
`IsAffine` and `Smooth` for `GLₙ` from the Library lemmas
`GroupScheme.GeneralLinear.isAffine_groupScheme` and `GroupScheme.GeneralLinear.smoothStatement`.
With `hG := GroupScheme.isConnectedReductive_generalLinear F (Fin (r + 1))`:

- `S := fullAEL2CuspidalSubmodule F GLₙ hPD hG T i hi I e hc` is the closed cuspidal subspace of
  `L²` of the positive rational quotient `[GLₙ]` (the quotient of `GLₙ(𝔸_F)` by the subgroup
  generated by `GLₙ(F)` and the positive real points of `T`), for its Haar quotient measure.
- `πc a := fullAEL2CuspidalBoundedRightAction F GLₙ hPD hG T i hi I e hc a` is right translation
  by `a ∈ GLₙ(𝔸_F)` on `S`, and, for a measure `ν` on `GLₙ(𝔸_F)` and `hf : Integrable f ν`,
  `fullAEL2CuspidalIntegratedAction F GLₙ hPD hG T i hi I e hc ν f hf` (written `R_S(f)`) is
  the integrated action `∫ f(a) πc(a) dν(a)` on `S`.
- `IsTraceClass` and `operatorTrace` are the Library's trace-class predicate and trace. For a
  complete subspace `P` of `S`, `P_P X ι_P` denotes the compression
  `P.orthogonalProjectionOnto ∘L X ∘L P.subtypeL`.

Places and local groups. `v : Langlands.Global.FinitePlace F` is a finite place and `.inr v` the
corresponding element of `Place F`. `PlaceGroupPoints F GLₙ (.inr v)` (written `GLₙ(F_v)`) is
the topological group of points of the group scheme over the completion `F_v`;
`atPlace F GLₙ (.inr v)` is the projection `g ↦ g_v` and `ofPlace F GLₙ (.inr v)` the insertion
of a local point at `v`. Exactly as Task 7701 does, the statement takes
`[MeasurableSpace GLₙ(F_v)] [BorelSpace GLₙ(F_v)] [LocallyCompactSpace GLₙ(F_v)]` and
`[TotallyDisconnectedSpace GLₙ(F_v)]` as instance binders. (The Library also has a Borel
σ-algebra and, for affine group schemes of finite type, local compactness on these points, in
`Langlands.Global.AdelicLocalCharacterNorm`; inside the statement the binders are the instances
used, and `BorelSpace` makes the bound σ-algebra the Borel one.)
`SchwartzBruhat GLₙ(F_v)` is the space of locally constant compactly supported
functions, and for a Haar measure `μv`, an admissible representation `ρ` and
`hρ : Langlands.IsAdmissible ρ`, `Character.distribution μv ρ hρ fv` is the trace of the
finite-rank operator `ρ(fv) = ∫ fv(y) ρ(y) dμv(y)` (module
`Langlands.Representation.Character.Basic`).

Matrix model. The Library's automorphic forms for `GL_{r+1}` live in a matrix model:
`FiniteLocalGL F (r + 1) v` is `GL_{r+1}` of the completion `FiniteCompletion F v`, with its
completion topology.
`Langlands.TraceFormula.localGLHomeomorph F (r + 1) (.inr v) : GLₙ(F_v) ≃ₜ* GL (Fin (r + 1)) F_v`
(module `Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearBaseChangeLocal`, Task 7684) is the Library's
isomorphism of topological groups between the group-scheme model and the matrix model, and
`continuousGeneralLinearEquiv` is the corresponding identification of continuous functions on
the adelic groups.

Realizations.

- `R : Langlands.Automorphic.CuspidalOperatorRealization F r` is an irreducible cuspidal
  subspace of actual automorphic functions on `GL_{r+1}(𝔸_F)` (matrix model), stable under the
  finite adelic group, the standard maximal compact subgroup at infinity and the enveloping
  algebra. `R.Space` is its carrier and `R.realization.toContinuous` the injective linear map
  sending a vector to its continuous function.
- `IsGeneralLinearFormClass F r T i φ f` (from the module of
  `GeneralLinearCuspidalSummandRealizationStatement`) says that the `L²` class `φ` on `[GLₙ]` is
  the class of the matrix-model continuous function `f`: some continuous `h` on `[GLₙ]` pulls
  back, through `continuousGeneralLinearEquiv`, to `f`, and `φ = h` almost everywhere.
- `D : Langlands.Automorphic.CuspidalSpectralPresentation.{u, uV} R` is a spectral presentation
  of `R` (module `Langlands.Automorphic.CuspidalSpectralPresentation`): a finite tensor
  presentation of a nonzero finite-adelic subspace of `R` with unramified spectral data. Its
  local component `D.localComponent v : LocalRealization F (r + 1) v` is a nonzero subspace of
  actual automorphic forms in the image of `R`, stable under right translation by
  `FiniteLocalGL F (r + 1) v` (`CuspidalSpectralPresentation.localComponent_ne_bot`, and
  `CuspidalSpectralPresentation.localComponent_le_full` in module
  `Langlands.Automorphic.AssociatedPresentationComparison`). Its representation
  `(D.localComponent v).representation` is that right translation. `IsIrreducible` is Mathlib's
  irreducibility of this representation, and `IsAdmissible` is `Langlands.IsAdmissible` for the
  completion topology (module `Langlands.Automorphic.PresentedLocalCategory`). For irreducible
  local components the class does not depend on the presentation
  (`CuspidalSpectralPresentation.localComponent_isoClass_eq`, same module as
  `localComponent_le_full`).

## The definition `localRealizationPlaceRepresentation`

For a finite place `v` and `A : LocalRealization F (r + 1) v`,

`localRealizationPlaceRepresentation A : Representation ℂ GLₙ(F_v) A.Space`

is `A.representation` composed with the group isomorphism underlying
`localGLHomeomorph F (r + 1) (.inr v)`. So `y ∈ GLₙ(F_v)` acts on a form in `A` by right
translation by the matrix `localGLHomeomorph y` at the place `v`. The definition only transports
the local action from the matrix model to the group-scheme model in which Task 7701 is stated.

## Statement

For all data as above, every finite place `v` with the instance binders on `GLₙ(F_v)`, every Haar
measure `μv` on `GLₙ(F_v)` and every Haar measure `ν` on `GLₙ(𝔸_F)`, assume:

1. `P` is a subspace of `S` with `[CompleteSpace P]` and `P ≠ ⊥`, invariant under every `πc a`,
   such that every closed subspace `Q ≤ P` invariant under every `πc a` is `⊥` or `P` (these
   are the hypotheses on `P` in Task 7701);
2. `R` is a cuspidal operator realization and `Φ : R.Space →ₗ[ℂ] S` is a linear map with
   `IsGeneralLinearFormClass F r T i (Φ f) (R.realization.toContinuous f)` for every
   `f : R.Space`, and the topological closure of the range of `Φ` is `P` (this link is the
   conclusion of `GeneralLinearCuspidalSummandRealizationStatement` and of its converse
   `GeneralLinearCuspidalRealizationSummandStatement`; here it is a hypothesis, and neither
   statement is used);
3. `D` is a spectral presentation of `R` with `(D.localComponent v).IsIrreducible` and
   `(D.localComponent v).IsAdmissible`.

Let `ρ := localRealizationPlaceRepresentation (D.localComponent v)`. Then there are
`hρ : Langlands.IsAdmissible ρ` (for the Library topology on `GLₙ(F_v)`) and a function
`C : (GLₙ(𝔸_F) → ℂ) → ℂ` with the following property. Let `fv ∈ SchwartzBruhat GLₙ(F_v)`
and let `fw : GLₙ(𝔸_F) → ℂ` satisfy `fw (g * ofPlace x) = fw g` for all `g ∈ GLₙ(𝔸_F)` and
`x ∈ GLₙ(F_v)`. Put `f(g) = fv(g_v) · fw(g)` and suppose `hf : Integrable f ν`. If the
compression `P_P R_S(f) ι_P` is trace class, then

`operatorTrace (P_P R_S(f) ι_P) = Character.distribution μv ρ hρ fv · C(fw)`.

The function `C` may depend on everything chosen before it (including `ν`, `P`, `R`, `Φ` and
`D`), but not on `fv` or `fw`: one `C` serves for all test-function pairs, and `ρ` is the
transported local component itself, not a representation chosen in the conclusion. The
statement asserts nothing about the trace class property; it computes
the trace when the property holds. It has no twisted part (see "Scope").

## Why the statement is true

1. **Admissibility of `ρ`.** `localGLHomeomorph` is an isomorphism of topological groups onto
   `GL (Fin (r + 1)) (placeCompletion F (.inr v))`. The topology of
   `placeCompletion F (.inr v)` is by definition that of `v.adicCompletion F`, which is
   `FiniteCompletion F v` (module `Langlands.Global.AdelicGroupLocalPoints`), and
   `FiniteLocalGL F (r + 1) v` is `Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup (Fin (r + 1)) (FiniteCompletion F v)`
   with Mathlib's topology on it: this is the topology used by `LocalRealization.IsAdmissible`.
   Open subgroups correspond, and so do their fixed vectors. So smoothness and
   finite-dimensionality of fixed spaces transfer from `D.localComponent v` to `ρ`.
2. **Factorization of `P`.** As in the argument for Task 7701: `GLₙ(𝔸_F)` is the product
   `GLₙ(F_v) × GLₙ(𝔸_F^v)` and `GLₙ(F_v)` is of type I, so the irreducible unitary
   representation on `P` is a Hilbert tensor product `π_v ⊗̂ π^v` of irreducible unitary
   representations (Folland, Theorem 7.17, printed p. 237), with `GLₙ(F_v)` acting on the first
   factor only. The smooth vectors of `π_v` form an irreducible admissible representation
   `π_v^∞`, and the argument of Task 7701 gives
   `operatorTrace (P_P R_S(f) ι_P) = tr π_v^∞(fv) · C(fw)`.
3. **The local component is `π_v^∞`.** By `localComponent_le_full`, every vector of
   `D.localComponent v` is the function of a unique vector of `R` (`toContinuous` is injective),
   and hypothesis 2 sends that vector by `Φ` to its `L²` class in `P`. The resulting linear map
   `j : D.localComponent v → P` is injective (continuous functions that agree almost everywhere
   for a Haar measure agree). The identification `continuousGeneralLinearEquiv` is compatible
   with the local embeddings, so it carries right translation at `v` in the matrix model to right
   translation at `v` in the group-scheme model. Hence `j` intertwines `ρ(y)` with
   `πc (ofPlace F GLₙ (.inr v) y)` for `y ∈ GLₙ(F_v)`. Pick a nonzero vector `x` of `ρ` and a
   continuous linear functional `λ` on `π^v` with `(id ⊗ λ)(j x) ≠ 0`. The map
   `id ⊗ λ : π_v ⊗̂ π^v → π_v` is `GLₙ(F_v)`-equivariant, so `(id ⊗ λ) ∘ j` is a nonzero
   `GLₙ(F_v)`-map from `ρ` to `π_v`, with image in the smooth vectors `π_v^∞` (the vectors of
   `ρ` are smooth). Both are irreducible, so this map is an isomorphism `ρ ≅ π_v^∞`. This is
   the analytic form of Flath's observation (Theorem 1, p. 180) that the restriction of
   `W_1 ⊗ W_2` to `G_1` is `W_1`-isotypic. Compare, in the Library's matrix model,
   `CuspidalSpectralPresentation.localComponent_isotypicPart_eq_top` (module
   `Langlands.Automorphic.AssociatedPresentationSpectrum`): the local restriction of `R` is
   isotypic of the type of `D.localComponent v`.
4. **Conclusion.** Isomorphic admissible representations have the same distribution character,
   so `tr π_v^∞(fv) = Character.distribution μv ρ hρ fv`, and step 2 gives the claim with the
   same `C`.

## Use and provenance

This is derived content, formulated for this project. In the literature the identification is
part of the factorization of a cuspidal automorphic representation:

- J. R. Getz and H. Hahn, *An introduction to automorphic representations*, GTM 300 (2024):
  Theorem 9.7.3 (printed p. 265; the `K`-finite vectors of a discrete cuspidal automorphic
  representation in `L²_cusp([G])` form a cuspidal automorphic representation, and this gives a
  bijection of isomorphism classes; the Library states its `GL_n` case as
  `GeneralLinearCuspidalSummandRealizationStatement`); Theorem 5.7.1 (Flath, printed p. 152;
  every irreducible admissible representation of `G(𝔸_F^∞)` is factorizable, with local factors
  unique up to isomorphism); and the factorization (11.14) (printed p. 300) of the smooth vectors
  of a globally generic cuspidal representation realized on a closed subspace of
  `L²_cusp([G])` as `V_{∞,sm} ⊗ (⊗'_{v∤∞} V_v)`, obtained there from Theorem 5.7.1 and
  Theorem 6.6.3.
- D. Flath, *Decomposition of representations into tensor products*, Proc. Sympos. Pure Math.
  33, part 1 (1979): Theorem 1 (printed pp. 179–180), an irreducible admissible representation
  of `G_1 × G_2` is `W_1 ⊗ W_2`, and its restriction to `G_i` is `W_i`-isotypic, which
  determines the class of `W_i`; Theorem 3 (printed p. 181), every admissible irreducible
  representation of `G(𝔸)` is factorizable, with factors unique up to equivalence.
- G. B. Folland, *A course in abstract harmonic analysis*, 2nd ed. (2015): Theorem 7.17
  (printed p. 237), irreducible unitary representations of `G_1 × G_2` with `G_1` of type I are
  tensor products `π_1 ⊗ π_2`.

No erratum in the October 2026 Getz–Hahn errata concerns Theorem 9.7.3, Theorem 5.7.1 or
(11.14).

Differences from the sources, all of which keep the content: one place `v` is split off, so the
remaining factor is the arbitrary functional `C`. The local component is the Library's
`CuspidalSpectralPresentation.localComponent`, transported by `localGLHomeomorph`, and its
irreducibility and admissibility are hypotheses because the Library states them as hypotheses
on local components (`LocalRealization.IsIrreducible`, `LocalRealization.IsAdmissible`). The
trace class property is a hypothesis.

Intended use. Task 7340 uses the statement in the global assembly of the local base change
identities. The traces on the cuspidal summands `P` from the twisted spectral expansion
(Task 7698) and the local trace factorization (Task 7701) become characters of the Library's
local components. These are the objects that carry the local hypotheses (for example
`LocalRealization.IsSupercuspidal`, module `Langlands.Automorphic.SupercuspidalComponent`) of the
Library's cyclic base change statements.

## Scope

Only the untwisted part of Task 7701 is stated. The twisted part needs, in addition, the
identification of the twisting operator `I_Θ` for the Weil restriction of `GL_n` with the
matrix-model Galois action on forms. That identification belongs to a separate statement.
