# Compact smooth adelic matrix tests

Clozel–Rajan, *Solvable base change*, arXiv:1806.02513v1, §4, PDF pp. 10–11, uses a test in `C_c^∞(GL_n(A_E))` and takes the discrete trace on the quotient by the diagonally embedded positive archimedean scalars. This module implements the finite-sum tensor test space used for that comparison. It applies to every number field; when `E` is totally complex its archimedean component uses the same actual product of completions, now all complex.

`compactSmoothAdelicGLTestSpace` is the complex linear span of the pointwise products of an `ArchimedeanGL.SmoothCompactKernel` and a `CompactFiniteGLTest`, pulled back along the actual infinite and finite projections. Its elements are functions, with equality of functions, not formal expressions or choices of tensor presentation. For a single tensor it is continuous and its support is contained in the pullback of the product of the two compact supports under `AdelicGL.split`. These properties extend to the span. There are no cuspidal, elliptic-support or supercuspidal-place requirements on these tests.

`CompactSmoothAdelicGLTest.centralAverage` integrates the representative test `f(a(x) g)` over the real logarithm `x` of the positive scalar, with a supplied measure. For a translation-invariant measure it is unchanged by multiplying `g` by a positive scalar: `expUnit (x+y) = expUnit x * expUnit y` and translation invariance give the identity. This is the trivial-character instance of the split-centre averaging convention of `cyclicCentralAverage` (Arthur–Clozel, *Simple algebras, base change, and the advanced theory of the trace formula*, Ch. II, (17.7)–(17.8)). It does not choose local transfer factors, identify matched pairs or assert an equality of trace distributions.
