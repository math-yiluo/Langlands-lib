# Rank zero in supercuspidal globalization for `GL_n`

The rank-zero branch uses the one-dimensional subspace of the actual cuspidal
Hilbert space generated by the class of the constant function one. The matrix
and group-scheme adelic comparisons make the adelic group trivial. The strict
block-weight criterion rules out proper rational parabolics. The quotient is a
singleton of finite positive Haar mass, so the constant class belongs to the
cuspidal space and is nonzero. Its span is complete, irreducible and fixed by
every local integral subgroup.

Each prescribed representation is irreducible on a trivial group. The span of
any nonzero local vector is invariant, hence is the whole local space. A
linear equivalence to the constant line gives the embedding with the trivial
determinant twist. The local Schwartz–Bruhat action is scalar multiplication
by the value of the test at the identity times the local Haar mass. The global
compression is scalar multiplication by the product of the two test values at
the identity times the adelic Haar mass. The quotient of the two masses,
together with the trace of the identity on the constant line, gives a single
functional for all the test pairs.
