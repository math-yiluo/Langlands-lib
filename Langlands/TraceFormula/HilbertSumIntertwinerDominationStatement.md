# Norm domination and intertwiners from a Hilbert sum

`Langlands.TraceFormula.HilbertSumIntertwinerDominationStatement` concerns a linear
image of a test space in `lp H 2 × E`. Its first component is in the Hilbert sum of
spaces `H i` with isometric group actions. Its second component is in the Hilbert
space `E` with a bounded linear action of the same group. The image is invariant
for the two actions taken together. If the first component dominates the norm of
the second up to a fixed factor, and each summand has only the zero bounded
intertwiner to `E`, the second component vanishes.

This is the linear-map form of the norm domination step in the remark inside the
proof of Jacquet–Langlands, Lemma 16.1.1. Their maps send a test function to its
integrated action on specified vectors. The joint invariance condition records
that translating a test function realizes the action on both components.
Their irreducibility and inequivalence hypotheses supply the vanishing of the
bounded intertwiners from the summands via Schur's lemma.
