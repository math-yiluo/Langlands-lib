# Closed twisted orbits and centralizer quotients

The twisted orbit map is a continuous bijection from the actual left quotient by the twisted centralizer to the orbit. Inverting the group coordinate turns twisted conjugation into a continuous transitive left action on that orbit. Closedness makes the orbit locally compact and Hausdorff. The open mapping theorem for continuous transitive actions of sigma-compact groups shows that the orbit map on the group is open, and openness descends through the quotient projection. The quotient map to the orbit is therefore a homeomorphism. Its composition with the orbit inclusion is the required closed embedding.

This is an internal topological lemma for the twisted orbital functions used in Henniart–Lemaire, I.2.4. It applies to any continuous group endomorphism and a closed twisted orbit.
