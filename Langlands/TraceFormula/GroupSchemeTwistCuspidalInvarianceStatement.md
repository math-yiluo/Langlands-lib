# Twisting by an algebraic automorphism preserves the cuspidal subspace

`Langlands.TraceFormula.GroupSchemeTwistCuspidalInvarianceStatement` is the proposition
that the Library's twisting operator preserves its full almost-everywhere cuspidal
subspace of `L²([G])`.

## Group and quotient data

- `F : Type u` is a number field, and `G : GroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of F))` is
  a group scheme over `F`. The instances `IsAffine G.X.left` and
  `LocallyOfFiniteType G.X.hom` specify affineness and local finite type over `F`.
  The hypothesis `hG : IsConnectedReductive G` makes `G` connected reductive.
- `hPD : ParabolicDynamicStatement.{u}` is the characterization of parabolic
  subgroups of connected reductive groups over fields by the dynamic parabolics
  attached to rational cocharacters. The Library takes it as input to the
  construction of the unipotent radicals used in the cuspidality condition.
- `T` is a commutative group scheme over `F`. The isomorphism
  `e : T.toGrp ≅ splitTorus (Spec (CommRingCat.of F)) I` presents it as a split torus
  with coordinates indexed by `I : Type u` with `[Fintype I]`. The map
  `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G` has central image by `hc : IsCentral i`. Restriction of
  algebraic characters from `G` to `T` along `i` is injective by
  `hi : Function.Injective (characterRestrict i)`, and the instance
  `[(characterRestrict i).range.FiniteIndex]` says its image has finite index.

The adelic group `G(𝔸_F)` is `AdelicGroupPoints F G`. Write
`Γ = positiveRationalSubgroup F G T i` for the subgroup generated by the principal
rational points `G(F)` and the image of the positive real points of `T`. The actual
left quotient `[G] = Γ \ G(𝔸_F)` is `PositiveRationalQuotient F G T i`. Its measure
`μ_Q = positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure F G T i` is the pushforward of the
quotient Haar measure on `G(F) \ G(𝔸_F)^1` under the map induced by inclusion of
the norm-one subgroup `G(𝔸_F)^1`.

Set `S = fullAEL2CuspidalSubmodule F G hPD hG T i hi I e hc`, a complex subspace of
`L²([G], μ_Q)`. For each proper rational parabolic `P` of `G` with unipotent radical
`N_P`, a class in `S` has an integrable period integrand `n ↦ φ([ng])` on
`N_P(F) \ N_P(𝔸_F)` for Haar-almost every `g ∈ G(𝔸_F)^1`. Here `[ng]` denotes
the coset in `[G]`, and integration uses the invariant probability measure
`ν_P = properParabolicQuotientMeasure F G hPD hG P`. The period, or constant term
along `P`, also vanishes for Haar-almost every `g`. This is the condition
`fullAEL2CuspidalLocus` in `Langlands.Automorphic.GroupSchemeParabolicL2Period`.

## Twisting and the assertion

- `α : G ≅ G` is an automorphism of group schemes over `F` of arbitrary order.
  `Θ : AdelicGroupPoints F G ≃ₜ* AdelicGroupPoints F G` is a supplied topological
  group automorphism. The hypothesis `∀ x, Θ x = AdelicGroupPoints.map F G G α.hom x`
  identifies its underlying map with the continuous adelic-point map of `α`.
- The hypothesis `hΘ : ∀ x, Θ x ∈ Γ ↔ x ∈ Γ` says that `Θ` preserves `Γ` in both
  directions. The induced homeomorphism `θ̄ = quotientAutomorphism Γ Θ hΘ`
  sends `[g]` to `[Θ g]`.
- The hypothesis `hΘμ : MeasurePreserving θ̄.symm μ_Q μ_Q` says that the inverse
  of that homeomorphism preserves the quotient measure. Both `hΘ` and `hΘμ` are
  explicit inputs to the proposition and to the Library's `twistOperator`.

For every choice of these data and every `φ ∈ S`, the proposition asserts that
`I_Θ φ ∈ S`, where `I_Θ = twistOperator Γ Θ hΘ μ_Q hΘμ`. On `L²` classes,
`I_Θ` is pullback along the inverse quotient homeomorphism `θ̄.symm`.

## Provenance and application

Getz–Hahn introduce `I_θ` by pullback along the inverse in display (18.33) and
assert preservation of the cuspidal subspace without giving a proof. Their
§18.5 assumes `θ` has finite order. This proposition is derived content. It
allows every order and uses the Library's full almost-everywhere cuspidal
subspace and its explicit hypotheses for pullback. [Getz–Hahn, *An Introduction
to Automorphic Representations* (2024), §18.5, printed pp. 486–487, display
(18.33) on p. 487](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-41153-3).

In Task 7340, `GroupSchemeTwistedCuspidalSpectralExpansionStatement` (Task 7698) uses a map
`τ : A → A` on the irreducible closed invariant summands `A` of `S`, satisfying
`I_Θ(P) ⊆ τ(P)`. This proposition gives the first step, `I_Θ(S) ⊆ S`.
Since `I_Θ` is an isometry by `norm_twistOperator_apply` and intertwines the right translations by
`I_Θ ∘ R(g) = R(Θ g) ∘ I_Θ`, it carries each nonzero irreducible closed
invariant summand `P` to a nonzero irreducible closed invariant subspace of
`S`. For `GL_n`, clause 2 of `UnitarySchurHilbertSumStatement` (Task 7729)
identifies `I_Θ(P)` up to an intertwining isometric equivalence with a summand
`P'` in `A`. The Library's `GeneralLinearCuspidalMultiplicityOneStatement`
then identifies the two subspaces, giving `τ(P) = I_Θ(P)`.
[Getz–Hahn, Theorem 11.4.3, printed p. 303](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-41153-3).

For a cyclic extension `L/K`, the group on the twisted side is `Res_{L/K} GL_n` and the
scheme automorphism comes from a generator of `Gal(L/K)`. In the adelic
realization `GL_n(𝔸_L)`, the generator acts on the coefficients. This is the
use of the twisted trace formula in the base-change comparison.
[Getz–Hahn, Corollary 18.5.1, printed p. 488, and §19.4, printed pp. 516–517](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-41153-3).

## Proof sketch

1. Put `β = Θ⁻¹`, the adelic-point map of `α⁻¹` by functoriality. Use the
   strongly measurable representative of `φ` to define `ψ([g]) = φ([β(g)])`.
   The pullback formula `twistOperator_coeFn` identifies `ψ` almost everywhere
   with the representative of `I_Θ φ`.
2. For a proper rational parabolic `P`, its image `P' = α⁻¹(P)` is again a
   proper rational parabolic. The canonical unipotent radical is carried to
   that of `P'` by transport of a represented radical and
   `rationalParabolicRadical_eq`. Thus `β` sends `N_P(𝔸_F)` and `N_P(F)` onto
   `N_{P'}(𝔸_F)` and `N_{P'}(F)`. The induced homeomorphism of their compact
   quotients intertwines the actions of the radicals and sends `ν_P` to
   `ν_{P'}` by uniqueness of the invariant probability measure.
3. The maps of `α` and `α⁻¹` both preserve norm-one membership in the forward
   direction by `map_normOneSubgroup_le`. Thus `β` restricts to a topological
   group automorphism of `G(𝔸_F)^1`, so pushforward scales its Haar measure
   by a positive constant and preserves null sets. The equality
   `ψ([ng]) = φ([β(n) β(g)])` and the change of variables on the radical
   quotient give integrability and vanishing for `ψ` along `P` at almost
   every `g`, using those for `φ` along `P'` at `β(g)`.
4. This holds for each `P`. The Library's
   `mem_aeParabolicCuspidalFunctionSubmodule_congr` transfers the condition
   from the chosen pullback representative to the `L²` class `I_Θ φ`.
