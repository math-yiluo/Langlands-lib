# The simple twisted trace formula with rational σ-elliptic support (statement)

`Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearRationalEllipticTwistedTraceFormulaStatement.{u}`
is a proposition definition for the simple twisted trace formula of
`W = Res_{L/K} GL_n` and a cyclic extension `L/K` of number fields. It is the
support-general form of `Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement.{u}`:
the operators, measures, twisted orbital integrals and the trace identity are the same,
and only the support hypothesis and the corresponding clause on the representatives
differ. Here the hypothesis tests the rational twisted classes that actually contribute
to the geometric side: each must be **σ-elliptic over the base field `K`**, in the sense
of the Library's `Langlands.BaseChange.IsSigmaElliptic`. The file states the formula
and does not prove it.

## Rational σ-ellipticity

For `δ ∈ GL_n(L)` (`RationalGL L n`), `IsSigmaElliptic σ l δ` says that there are
`γ ∈ GL_n(K)` and `g ∈ GL_n(L)` with `g⁻¹ N(δ) g = γ`, where
`N(δ) = cyclicNorm σ l δ = δ σ(δ) ⋯ σ^{l-1}(δ)`, and that `γ` is regular elliptic
(`JacquetLanglands.IsRegularElliptic`): its characteristic polynomial is separable and
irreducible **over `K`**. This is the association of norms and the ellipticity of
Henniart–Lemaire (2011), I.1.2, I.2.2 and I.2.3, and of Arthur–Clozel (1989), Chapter 1,
§1. The polynomial need not stay irreducible over `L` or over a completion of `L`.
Since `σ ^ l = 1`, σ-ellipticity is invariant under twisted conjugation
`δ ↦ y⁻¹ δ σ(y)` by `y ∈ GL_n(L)` (Library `IsNormAssociated.twistedConj`). Since `Φ`
carries `θ` to the entrywise action of `σ`, rational σ-ellipticity of a point of `Γ` is a
property of its rational `θ`-twisted class.

Ellipticity over the base field is weaker than ellipticity of the norm over a completion of
`L`. For a quadratic extension of fields `E/F` with nontrivial automorphism `σ` and
`α ∈ Eˣ \ Fˣ`, take `δ = [[0, α], [1, 0]] ∈ GL_2(E)`. Then
`N(δ) = δ σ(δ) = diag(α, σ(α))`, whose characteristic polynomial
`(X - α)(X - σ(α))` lies in `F[X]`, is separable and is irreducible over `F`. So `N(δ)` is
conjugate over `E` to the companion matrix of that polynomial, and `δ` is σ-elliptic,
although `N(δ)` is split over `E`. With `F = K_v` and `E = L_w` for a finite place `v` of
`K` that does not split in a quadratic `L`, this is a local σ-elliptic point whose norm is
not elliptic over `L_w`.

## Statement

The proposition quantifies over `K`, `L`, `σ`, `l` with `IsCyclicAlgebra σ l`, `n : ℕ`, the
group-scheme data above and the fixed-torus hypothesis `i ≫ τ.asIso.hom = i`. For all
`f₁, f₂ : C_c(G, ℂ)` the hypotheses are the following.

1. For every integrability witness, `R_cusp(f₁)` and `R_cusp(f₂)` are Hilbert–Schmidt.
2. For every proper rational parabolic `P` of `W` (`ProperRationalParabolic Wₙ`) and all
   `x, y ∈ G`, `∫ f₂(x m y) dm = 0`, where `dm` is `properParabolicHaarMeasure K Wₙ hPD hG P`
   on the adelic points of the unipotent radical.

Let `f(g) = ∫ f₁(a) f₂(a⁻¹ g) dμG(a)` and `fOne(x) = ∫_{A⁺} f(positivePointMap(a) · x) da`
for `x ∈ G¹`. A point `q ∈ G¹` **contributes** (`contributes q`) when
`fOne(x⁻¹ q θ(x)) ≠ 0` for some `x ∈ G¹`. It is **rationally σ-elliptic**
(`rationalElliptic q`) when there is `δ : RationalGL L n` with
`Φ(q) = AdelicGL.rational L n δ` and `IsSigmaElliptic σ l δ`. The support hypothesis is

3. every `q ∈ Γ` that contributes is rationally σ-elliptic.

The conclusion supplies:

- `hΘ : ∀ x, Θ x ∈ H ↔ x ∈ H`, and `hΘμ`, saying that the inverse of
  `quotientAutomorphism H Θ hΘ` preserves `ν`; these define `I_Θ`;
- `h : Integrable f μG`, with `R(f)` trace class on `L²(H \ G, ν)`;
- `m : ℕ` and `r : Fin m → Γ` such that the `r j` lie in pairwise distinct
  `θ`-twisted `Γ`-classes, each `r j` contributes and is rationally σ-elliptic, and every
  contributing `q ∈ Γ` is `θ`-twisted conjugate under `Γ` to some `r j`.

For each `j` let `C_j = twistedCentralizer θ (r j) ⊆ G¹`. For every choice of Borel
measurable structures on `C_j \ G¹` and on `(Γ ∩ C_j) \ C_j` (the latter written with
`(rationalNormOneSubgroup K Wₙ).subgroupOf C_j`), of right-invariant measures `μC j` on
`C_j` that are finite on compacts and positive on nonempty open sets, and of measures
`νC j` on `C_j \ G¹` compatible with Haar measure on `G¹` and `μC j`, and `νΓC j` on
`(Γ ∩ C_j) \ C_j` compatible with `μC j` and counting measure: every `νΓC j Set.univ` is
finite, every `twistedOrbitFunction θ (r j) fOne` is `νC j`-integrable, and

```text
operatorTrace (R(f) ∘ I_Θ)
  = ∑ j, (νΓC j Set.univ).toReal · twistedOrbitalIntegral θ (r j) (νC j) fOne.
```

The real volume in each summand is cast to `ℂ`.

A class that does not contribute has orbital integral zero, so the sum runs over all
classes with a nonzero term. Requiring the representatives to contribute, rather than
only to cover, lets the specialization below recover the extension-place clause of
`GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement`.

If `n = 0`, no element of `GL_0(K)` is regular elliptic (its characteristic polynomial
is the unit `1`), so hypothesis 3 says that no point of `Γ` contributes. Here `G` is
trivial, and so is `A⁺`, because the character restriction of `i` has finite-index image.
Then `fOne(1)` is `f(1)` times the finite positive mass of the Haar measure `da`, so
`f = 0`, `R(f) = 0`, and the empty family (`m = 0`) gives the conclusion.

## Scope compared with `GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement`

`GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement` fixes a finite place `w` of `L` and
assumes that for every `g` in the closed support of `f`, the `w`-component of
`Φ(N(g))` is regular elliptic in `GL_n(L_w)`, where `N(g) = Θ⁰(g) Θ¹(g) ⋯ Θ^{l-1}(g)`.
Its representatives satisfy the same condition at `w`. This statement removes `w` and its
hypothesis and assumes hypothesis 3 instead. Both scopes are cases of the single formula
here, by two support arguments that this file does not contain.

- **A finite place `w` of `L`.** If `q ∈ Γ` contributes, then `f(a x⁻¹ q θ(x)) ≠ 0` for
  some `a ∈ A⁺` and `x ∈ G¹`, so `g = a x⁻¹ q θ(x)` lies in the closed support of `f`.
  The image of `A⁺` is central and fixed by `Θ`, and `Θ^l` is the identity, so
  `N(g) = a^l x⁻¹ N(q) x`. The points of `A⁺` have component `1` at every finite place,
  so the `w`-component of `Φ(N(g))` is conjugate in `GL_n(L_w)` to that of `Φ(N(q))`,
  and it is regular elliptic. The Library theorem
  `exists_sigmaElliptic_rational_matrix_of_isRegularElliptic_localNorm` (module
  `Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearTwistedEllipticCentralizerReduction`) turns this
  into a rational σ-elliptic `δ` with `Φ(q) = AdelicGL.rational L n δ`: a factorization
  over `K` of the characteristic polynomial of the norm would persist over `L_w`. So
  hypothesis 3 holds. Because each `r j` contributes, the same computation shows that the
  `w`-component of `Φ(N(r j))` is regular elliptic, which is the clause on the
  representatives in `GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement`. The remaining
  conclusions are identical.
- **A finite place `v` of `K`.** At `v`, the component of `G` is
  `GL_n(K_v ⊗_K L)`, on which `Θ` acts through `σ` on the cyclic `K_v`-algebra
  `K_v ⊗_K L`. Suppose that every `g ∈ G` with `f(g) ≠ 0` has `σ`-elliptic component at
  `v` in this local sense. A closed-support condition, that every point of the closed
  support of `f` has `σ`-elliptic component at `v`, implies this pointwise condition. For a
  factorizable `f` with `v`-factor `f_v`, the pointwise condition holds when `f_v`
  satisfies `Langlands.BaseChange.IsSigmaEllipticSupported`, which asks that every point
  at which `f_v` is nonzero be σ-elliptic, because `f(g) ≠ 0` forces `f_v(g_v) ≠ 0`.
  Let `q` contribute, with `Φ(q)` the image of `δ ∈ GL_n(L)`. As in the first case,
  `f(a x⁻¹ q θ(x)) ≠ 0` for some `a ∈ A⁺` and `x ∈ G¹`. The `v`-component of `a` is `1`,
  so the `v`-component of `a x⁻¹ q θ(x)` is a local twisted conjugate of `δ`, and `δ` is
  σ-elliptic in `GL_n(K_v ⊗_K L)`. The characteristic polynomial `p` of `N(δ)` has
  coefficients in `K`, because `σ(N(δ)) = δ⁻¹ N(δ) δ`, and the local norm of `δ`, the
  image of `N(δ)`, has characteristic polynomial the image of `p`. The local condition
  makes `p` separable and irreducible over `K_v`, hence over `K`, and `N(δ)` is then
  conjugate over `L` to the companion matrix of `p` in `GL_n(K)`. So hypothesis 3 holds.
  No irreducibility over a completion `L_w` is required, so this scope covers places `v`
  that do not split in `L`, as in the example above.

## Sources

- Getz–Hahn, *An Introduction to Automorphic Representations* (2024), §18.5,
  (18.30)–(18.36) and Corollary 18.5.1, printed pp. 486–488; Theorem 18.2.5 and
  Lemma 18.2.6, p. 479; orbits (17.3)–(17.4) and Definition 17.2.8 in §17.2, pp. 436–438
  (`getz-hahn-2024-automorphic-representations`). Corollary 18.5.1 takes a test function
  on `A_G \ G(𝔸_F)`, assumes that `R(f)` has cuspidal image and that every element of
  `G(F)` that is `θ`-conjugate under `G(𝔸_F)` to the support of `f` is `θ`-elliptic
  (finite `τ(C^θ_γ)`) and `θ`-semisimple, and sums over the `θ`-elliptic and
  `θ`-semisimple classes. In §18.5, `θ`-semisimple means that the orbit of `γ` under
  `θ`-conjugation by the algebraic group is closed, as for the orbits of §17.2 and
  Definition 17.2.8.

  In the Library the input is the convolution `f` on `G`. The twist fixes `A⁺`, and the
  normalized integral `fOne(x) = ∫_{A⁺} f(a x) da` is a function on `G¹`, which represents
  a function on the quotient under `A⁺ \ G ≃ G¹`. It plays the role of Getz–Hahn's test
  function on `A_G \ G(𝔸_F)`. The kernel of `R(f) ∘ I_Θ` on the norm-one model has
  diagonal `∑_{q ∈ Γ} fOne(x⁻¹ q θ(x))`, to which only contributing `q` add.

  For a rationally σ-elliptic class, Getz–Hahn's `θ`-semisimplicity follows from an
  algebraic argument, given here on paper. Let `K̄` be an algebraic closure of `K`, fix an
  embedding `ι₀ : L → K̄` and put `ι_j = ι₀ ∘ σ^j`, so that the `ι_j` for `0 ≤ j < l` are
  the embeddings of `L`. Through `L ⊗_K K̄ ≅ K̄^l`, `a ⊗ b ↦ (ι_j(a) b)_j`, the
  `K̄`-points of `W` become `∏_j GL_n(K̄)`, and `δ ∈ GL_n(L)` has components
  `δ_j = ι_j(δ)`. The twist acts through `σ ⊗ 1`, so it shifts the factors:
  `θ(x)_j = x_{j+1}`, with indices mod `l`, and `(x⁻¹ δ θ(x))_j = x_j⁻¹ δ_j x_{j+1}`. The
  product `P(g) = g_0 g_1 ⋯ g_{l-1}` is a morphism `∏_j GL_n → GL_n` with
  `P(δ) = ι₀(N(δ))`. The twisted orbit of `δ` is the inverse image under `P` of the
  conjugacy class of `P(δ)`. The product of `x⁻¹ δ θ(x)` telescopes to
  `x_0⁻¹ P(δ) x_0`. Conversely, if `P(g) = y⁻¹ P(δ) y`, take `x_0 = y` and
  `x_{j+1} = δ_j⁻¹ x_j g_j` for `0 ≤ j ≤ l - 2`. Then `x⁻¹ δ θ(x)` agrees with `g` in the
  first `l - 1` factors, and in the last factor because both products equal
  `y⁻¹ P(δ) y`. For rationally σ-elliptic `δ`, `N(δ)` is conjugate to a matrix in `GL_n(K)`
  with separable characteristic polynomial. A matrix over `K̄` with this characteristic
  polynomial has `n` distinct eigenvalues and is diagonalizable, so the conjugacy class of
  `P(δ)` in `GL_n(K̄)` is the Zariski-closed set of all matrices with that characteristic
  polynomial. Hence the twisted orbit of `δ` is closed.

  The Library supplies the corresponding analytic facts in the norm-one model. For a
  rationally σ-elliptic point `q` of `Γ` with twisted centralizer `C`, the quotient
  `(Γ ∩ C) \ C` is compact (`generalLinearWeilTwistedCompactQuotient`). This is the
  norm-one counterpart of the finite volume in `θ`-ellipticity. The analytic twisted orbit
  `{x⁻¹ q θ(x) : x ∈ G¹}` is a closed subset of `G¹` (`generalLinearWeilTwistedClosedOrbit`).
  Together these give, for compatible measures, the finite volume of `(Γ ∩ C) \ C` and the
  integrability of the orbit function of every continuous compactly supported function on
  `G¹` (`generalLinearWeilTwistedAnalytic_of_compact_closed`). The cuspidal-image hypothesis
  corresponds to hypotheses 1 and 2, as in `GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement`.

## Derived form

As in `GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement`, the test function is the
convolution of two continuous compactly supported functions on `G`, the Hilbert–Schmidt
properties of their cuspidal operators are premises, and the vanishing of the radical
integrals of `f₂` replaces the cuspidal-image hypothesis. Finiteness of the volumes and
integrability of the orbit functions are conclusions, stated for every compatible choice
of measures, and the data `hΘ`, `hΘμ` and `h` defining the operators are supplied in the
conclusion.

The file contains no proof. Library results relevant to one are
`groupSchemeFullL2TraceClass` (trace class of `R(f)` under hypotheses 1 and 2),
`positiveQuotientTwistedTraceComparison` (the comparison of `R(f) ∘ I_Θ` on `H \ G` with
the averaged operator on `Γ \ G¹`), `abstractSimpleTwistedTraceFormula` (the abstract
simple twisted trace formula for a support compact modulo `Γ`), `twistedEllipticClassFiniteness`
(finitely many rational σ-elliptic twisted classes meet a compact set of `GL_n(𝔸_L)`),
and `generalLinearWeilTwistedAnalytic_of_compact_closed`, which, with
`generalLinearWeilTwistedCompactQuotient` and `generalLinearWeilTwistedClosedOrbit`,
gives the finite volumes and the integrability of the orbit functions of continuous
compactly supported functions for a rationally σ-elliptic representative.
