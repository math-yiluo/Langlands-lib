# The central-character restriction on the twisted cuspidal rank

**Source.** Laurent Clozel and C. S. Rajan, *Solvable base change*, arXiv:1806.02513v1, `clozel-rajan-2018-solvable-base-change`, PDF p. 2, remark immediately after Theorem 1 and equation (1.1); also §7.3, PDF p. 26, first paragraph. The p. 2 remark says that the theorem is immediate when the extension degree does not divide the rank, by restricting the central character to the base idele classes.

`Langlands.TraceFormula.CyclicTwistedCuspidalRankDivisibilityStatement` states that restriction argument as a separate proposition on actual cuspidal classes. It uses the same finite-order determinant twist, arithmetic pullback and norm-kernel predicate as `CyclicPrimeTwistedCuspidalObstructionStatement`. The rank of the positive-rank carrier at index `r` is `r + 1`. Primality is not used for this argument: the supplied generator condition makes the Galois group cyclic, and the conclusion is divisibility by its order `Module.finrank F E`. In particular this statement supplies the nondivisible case for both field scopes of the prime-cyclic comparison.

The proposed proof uses the unique central character of the realized cusp. Galois pullback does not change that character on the image of `C_F`, while a twist by `χ` multiplies it on that image by the `(r + 1)`-st power of the restriction of `χ`, because the determinant of a scalar matrix of size `r + 1` is that power of the scalar. The restriction has order `[E:F]` by the cyclic norm-kernel character theorem. Thus its `(r + 1)`-st power can be trivial only if the degree divides `r + 1`. No unitarity, local transfer, trace formula or lower-rank obstruction is assumed. This file states only the divisibility target; it neither proves it nor addresses the divisible-rank case.
