# Trace class on the full automorphic L² space

`Langlands.TraceFormula.GroupSchemeFullL2TraceClassStatement.{u}` is a proposition definition
for connected reductive group schemes over number fields.

## Setting

- `F : Type u` is a field with a `NumberField F` instance. The group scheme `G` over `Spec F`
  has affine underlying scheme and a locally finite type structure morphism.
  `hG : IsConnectedReductive G` asserts connected reductivity. The argument
  `hPD : ParabolicDynamicStatement.{u}` identifies rational parabolics with the dynamic
  parabolics of cocharacters and is used by the Library's radical construction.
- The central morphism `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G` has centrality witness `hc`. The commutative group
  scheme `T` is split via `e : T.toGrp ≅ splitTorus (Spec (CommRingCat.of F)) I`, where
  `I : Type u` has a `Fintype I` instance. The rational-character restriction
  `characterRestrict i` is injective by `hi` and has finite-index range. The Haar measure on
  `normOneSubgroup F G`, the subgroup `G(𝔸_F)¹`, is assumed right invariant.

These are the parameters of the Library's `GroupSchemeFullAEL2CuspidalTraceClassStatement`.
`AdelicGroupPoints F G` is `G(𝔸_F)`, with left Haar measure `μG := Measure.haar`.
Write `[G]` for `PositiveRationalQuotient F G T i`, the left quotient by the subgroup
generated by `G(F)` and the image of the positive real points of `T`.
The measure `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure F G T i` pushes the norm-one quotient
Haar measure on `G(F) \ G(𝔸_F)¹` forward to `[G]`.

Write `S` for the closed `fullAEL2CuspidalSubmodule F G hPD hG T i hi I e hc` of `L²([G])`.
For every proper rational parabolic, its classes have convergent and vanishing constant terms
over the mass-one radical quotient for Haar-almost every point of `G(𝔸_F)¹`.
For `hf : Integrable f μG`,
`haarQuotientL2IntegratedAction F G T i hi I e hc μG f hf` is `R(f)` on `L²([G])`, given by
the Bochner integral `φ ↦ ∫ f(a) R(a)φ dμG(a)` of right translations.
`fullAEL2CuspidalIntegratedAction F G hPD hG T i hi I e hc μG f hf` is the corresponding
operator `R_cusp(f)` on `S`.

`ProperRationalParabolic G` indexes the proper rational parabolic subgroup functors. For
such a parabolic `P` with unipotent radical `N_P`, write `dn_P` for
`properParabolicHaarMeasure F G hPD hG P`. It is Haar measure on `N_P(𝔸_F)` normalized
so that the induced measure on `N_P(F) \ N_P(𝔸_F)` has mass one.

## Hypotheses and conclusion

For any `f₁ f₂ : C_c(G(𝔸_F), ℂ)`, the proposition assumes the following.

- For every `h₁ : Integrable f₁ μG`, `R_cusp(f₁)` satisfies `IsHilbertSchmidt`.
- For every `h₂ : Integrable f₂ μG`, `R_cusp(f₂)` satisfies `IsHilbertSchmidt`.
- For every `P : ProperRationalParabolic G` and all `x, y ∈ G(𝔸_F)`, the integral
  `∫_{N_P(𝔸_F)} f₂(x n y) dn_P(n)` is zero. Here `n` is viewed in the ambient adelic group.

The two Hilbert–Schmidt hypotheses specialize the Library's
`GroupSchemeFullAEL2CuspidalTraceClassStatement` to `f₁` and `f₂`. They are assumptions
supplied for those two functions. The predicate `Langlands.Analysis.OperatorIdeals.IsHilbertSchmidt`
requires summability of the squared norms of the images of some Hilbert basis.

The conclusion gives an integrability witness for the left Haar convolution
`(f₁ * f₂)(g) = ∫ f₁(a) f₂(a⁻¹ g) dμG(a)` with respect to `μG`, and asserts
`IsTraceClass` of the full-quotient integrated action at that weight and witness.
In words, `R(f₁ * f₂)` is trace class on all of `L²([G])`. The Library's
`Langlands.Analysis.OperatorIdeals.IsTraceClass` uses summability of the real diagonal
of the positive left modulus `√(A A*)` on some Hilbert basis.

If `G` has no proper rational parabolic subgroup, the radical-integral condition is vacuous
and `S` is all of `L²([G])`. The two Hilbert–Schmidt assumptions then concern the full
operators `R(f₁)` and `R(f₂)`.

## Source and derivation

The source is Getz–Hahn, *An Introduction to Automorphic Representations* (2024),
registered as `getz-hahn-2024-automorphic-representations`.

- Theorem 9.1.2 (printed p. 241) asserts trace class of `R_cusp(f)` for smooth compactly
  supported `f` on `A_G \ G(𝔸_F)`. In the number-field proof, Lemma 9.3.3
  (printed pp. 247–248) uses Hilbert–Schmidt operators for the smooth convolution factors.
  [Chapter 9, The Cuspidal Spectrum](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-41153-3_9)
- Lemma 16.4.2 (printed p. 430) obtains cuspidal image for a smooth test function with a
  supercuspidal factor at a finite place. The unfolding in its proof uses integrals over
  two-sided translates of adelic unipotent radicals.
  [Chapter 16, Spectral Sides of Trace Formulae](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-41153-3_16)
- Theorem 18.2.5 (printed p. 479) takes cuspidal image as a hypothesis. Section 18.5
  (printed p. 487) uses Theorem 9.1.2 for the cuspidal restriction of the twisted operator,
  and Corollary 18.5.1 (printed p. 488) takes cuspidal image as a hypothesis of the simple
  twisted trace formula.
  [Chapter 18, Simple Trace Formulae](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-41153-3_18)

The proposition is derived content. It uses the global vanishing integrals from the
unfolding argument directly as a condition on `f₂`. The intended argument uses that
condition to obtain cuspidal image for `R(f₂)` and applies the product rule for two
Hilbert–Schmidt operators to factors through `S`. The formal statement assumes the two
Hilbert–Schmidt properties for the compactly supported continuous convolution factors.
Getz–Hahn use the quotient `A_G G(F) \ G(𝔸_F)` and smooth weights on `A_G \ G(𝔸_F)`.
The formal statement uses the Library's quotient formed with the supplied `T` and
compactly supported continuous weights on `G(𝔸_F)`.
