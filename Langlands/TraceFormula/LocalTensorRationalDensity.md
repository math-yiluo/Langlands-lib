# Weak approximation in the local tensor groups of a Weil restriction

`LocalTensorRationalDensityStatement` concerns an extension \(L/K\)
of number fields and a finite set of finite places of \(K\). The
diagonal map from \(\mathrm{GL}_n(L)\) into the product of the
matrix groups \(\mathrm{GL}_n(K_v\otimes_K L)\) has dense range.
The local groups use the actual completed tensor algebras with
their module topologies. The diagonal is induced by the upper
rational coefficient maps \(b\mapsto 1\otimes b\).

For each base place, the completed tensor map identifies its algebra
with the product of \(L_w\) over the places \(w\) above it. The map
and its inverse are continuous linear maps over \(K_v\) for the
module topologies. Applying the product comparison for invertible
matrices and regrouping the factors identifies the full product of
local tensor groups with the matrix groups at all upper places above
the chosen base places. An upper place has a unique restriction
to \(K\), so each occurs once. On rational matrices the comparison
is entrywise completion, and the Library's rational matrix weak
approximation theorem gives the asserted density.

This internal step supplies simultaneous rational approximation
at the distinguished places in Henniart–Lemaire 2011, I.6.8.
