# Finite-place factor of the weighted geometric trace term

`NormOneFinitePlaceGeometricTermStatement` uses the actual norm-one
group of an affine group scheme locally of finite type over a number
field. The supplied central split torus satisfies the character
restriction hypotheses for the norm-one decomposition. The ambient
norm-one Haar is right invariant and the automorphism fixes the
torus morphism. The chosen rational norm-one point has commutative
twisted centralizer.

At the distinguished finite place, the local group and orbital
quotient have their Borel measurable structures. The local ambient
Haar and compatible centralizer quotient measures are supplied. The
local centralizer measure is right invariant, finite on compact sets
and positive on open sets.

The theorem chooses an exterior quotient measure and a compatible
geometric measure package at the selected rational representative.
The exterior measure is nonzero, regular and right invariant, and
the rational centralizer measure uses counting on its discrete
rational intersection. The exterior and the two global quotients
have their Borel measurable structures.

For each exterior test invariant under right insertion of the
distinguished finite-place group, the twisted orbital integral of the
normalized positive average of a tensor is the product of its local
and exterior orbital integrals. The weighted geometric term is the
local orbital integral multiplied by the rational covolume and the
exterior orbital integral. The measures serve for every choice of
test factors. These are total Bochner orbital integrals, and the
weighted identity uses the `toReal` of the covolume.

The proof factors the norm-one orbital integral with the supplied
local normalization, then extends the global orbital measure to the
compatible rational quotient measure. This is an internal step in
the distinguished-place comparison of Arthur–Clozel 1989,
Chapter 1, §4.
