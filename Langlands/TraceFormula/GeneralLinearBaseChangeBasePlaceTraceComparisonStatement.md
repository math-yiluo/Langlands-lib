# Comparison of the simple trace formula for `GL_n` with its twisted form: the base-place form (statement)

The module defines the proposition
`Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearBaseChangeBasePlaceTraceComparisonStatement.{u}`.
It states the proposition and does not prove it.

It states the global trace identity of cyclic base change for `GL_n` when both test functions
are elliptic at the same finite place `v` of the base field `K`. Let `φ` be a test function on
`Res_{L/K} GL_n` whose closed support consists of points with σ_v-elliptic component at `v`,
and `f` a test function on `GL_n` over `K` whose closed support consists of points with
regular elliptic component at `v`. Suppose that the global orbital integrals of `φ` and `f` match
up to a constant `c` at the pairs related by the norm for which the `GL_n` point is regular elliptic at
`v`, and that those of `f` vanish at the points regular elliptic at `v` that are not norms. Then
`tr (R(φ) ∘ I_θ) = c · tr R(f)`.

It is the form of the Library's `GeneralLinearBaseChangeTraceComparisonStatement` in which the
condition on the twisted test function sits at a place of `K` instead of a place of `L`. Every
binder, local definition, hypothesis and conclusion of that statement is kept, except for the
three changes listed in "Comparison with `GeneralLinearBaseChangeTraceComparisonStatement`".

## Notation from the Library

The notation is that of `GeneralLinearBaseChangeTraceComparisonStatement`, whose Markdown page
describes each construction; the main points are repeated here.

  `σ : L ≃ₐ[K] L` and `l : ℕ`. The predicate `IsCyclicAlgebra σ l` (module
  `Langlands.BaseChange.Norm`) says, for fields, that `L/K` is cyclic of degree `l` with Galois
  group generated by `σ`.
- `GLₙ` is a local notation for `GroupScheme.GeneralLinear.groupScheme (Fin n)
  (CommRingCat.of K)`, with local `IsAffine` and `Smooth` instances from

  `twistOperator … Θ …` is the operator `I_θ` on `L²([W])`.

  GL_n(𝔸_K) → GL_n(𝔸_L)` is the entrywise inclusion of adeles, and
  `ΦG(g) = incl (adelicHomeomorph K n g)` for `g ∈ G(𝔸_K)`. `AdelicGL.rational L n` is the
  diagonal embedding `GL_n(L) → GL_n(𝔸_L)`.

  is the component in `GL_n(K_v)`. `IsRegularElliptic`

  component of `adelicHomeomorph K n q` at `v` is regular elliptic.

  `IsCompatible C μ μC ν` (`Langlands.QuotientIntegration`) says that `ν` is the quotient

- `fullAEL2CuspidalIntegratedAction`, `haarQuotientL2IntegratedAction`, `IsHilbertSchmidt`,
  `IsTraceClass`, `operatorTrace`, `properParabolicHaarMeasure`, `positivePointMap` and

  `GeneralLinearSimpleTraceFormulaStatement` and `GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement`.

  `IsRegularElliptic`). No irreducibility over a completion of `L` is required.

## Statement

Fix `K`, `L`, `σ`, `l` with `IsCyclicAlgebra σ l`, `n : ℕ`, and a common
`ParabolicDynamicStatement`. For each group, assume `IsConnectedReductive`, a central morphism
`iW : TW → Wₙ` or `iG : TG → GLₙ` from a commutative group scheme over `K`, and an isomorphism of
that group scheme with `splitTorus` on a type in universe `u` with a `Fintype` instance. The
character restriction of each central morphism is injective with finite-index image, and the
`Measure.haar` of each norm-one group is right-invariant. On the `W` side, also
`iW ≫ (unitGaloisAut K L GLₙ σ).asIso.hom = iW`.

Let `φ₁, φ₂` be continuous compactly supported complex functions on `W(𝔸_K)` such that
`R_cusp(φ₁)` and `R_cusp(φ₂)` are Hilbert–Schmidt for every integrability witness (with
`Measure.haar` on `W(𝔸_K)`), and such that the integral of `φ₂` against
`properParabolicHaarMeasure` along every two-sided translate of the unipotent radical of every
proper rational parabolic vanishes. Put `φ(g) = ∫ φ₁(a) φ₂(a⁻¹ g) da`.

Let `v : Langlands.Global.FinitePlace K`, and assume

- **σ_v-ellipticity of `φ` at `v`:** for every `g ∈ tsupport φ`,
  `IsSigmaElliptic (localGalois K L σ (.inr v)) l
    (localPointsEquiv K L n (.inr v) (AdelicGroupPoints.atPlace K Wₙ (.inr v) g))`.

Let `f₁, f₂` be continuous compactly supported complex functions on `G(𝔸_K)` with the
corresponding Hilbert–Schmidt conditions and the same cuspidality condition on `f₂` for `GLₙ`,
and put `f(g) = ∫ f₁(a) f₂(a⁻¹ g) da` with `Measure.haar` on `G(𝔸_K)`. Assume

- **regular ellipticity of `f` at `v`:** for every `g ∈ tsupport f`, the component of
  `adelicHomeomorph K n g` at the same place `v` is regular elliptic.

For `x` in the respective norm-one subgroup, let `φ¹(x) = ∫_{A⁺_W} φ(a x) da` and
`f¹(x) = ∫_{A⁺_G} f(a x) da` (`positivePointMap` and `normalizedPositiveHaarMeasure`). For any
`c ∈ ℂ`, assume:

1. **Matching at norms regular elliptic at `v`.** Take any
   `r ∈ rationalNormOneSubgroup K Wₙ`, `q ∈ rationalNormOneSubgroup K GLₙ` and `y ∈ GL_n(L)`
   with `Φ(N(r)) · rational(y) = rational(y) · ΦG(q)`, where
   `rational(y) = AdelicGL.rational L n y`, and with `q` regular elliptic at `v`. For every group
   isomorphism `e` from `C_r = twistedCentralizer θ r` onto
   `C_q = twistedCentralizer (MonoidHom.id _) q` with
   `ΦG(e(x)) = rational(y)⁻¹ · Φ(x) · rational(y)` for all `x ∈ C_r`, every right-invariant `μr`
   on `C_r` that is finite on compacts and positive on nonempty open sets, every right-invariant
   `μq` on `C_q` with `μq = e_* μr`, and all quotient measures `νr`, `νq` on the Borel left coset
   spaces compatible with the norm-one Haar measures and with `μr`, `μq`:
   `twistedOrbitalIntegral θ r νr φ¹ = c · orbitalIntegral q νq f¹`.
2. **Vanishing off norms.** For every `q ∈ rationalNormOneSubgroup K GLₙ` regular elliptic at
   `v` such that `Φ(N(r)) · rational(y) ≠ rational(y) · ΦG(q)` for all
   `r ∈ rationalNormOneSubgroup K Wₙ` and `y ∈ GL_n(L)`, every right-invariant measure `μq` on
   `C_q` that is finite on compacts and positive on nonempty open sets, and every compatible
   quotient measure `νq`: `orbitalIntegral q νq f¹ = 0`.

Conclusion: there exist `hΘ` (`Θ` preserves `positiveRationalSubgroup K Wₙ TW iW`), `hΘμ` (the
inverse of the induced automorphism of `[W]` preserves `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure`),
and integrability witnesses `hφ`, `hf`, such that `R(φ)` on `L²([W])` and `R(f)` on `L²([G])`
(`haarQuotientL2IntegratedAction`) are trace class and

`tr (R(φ) ∘ I_θ) = c · tr R(f)`.

For `n = 0` neither ellipticity condition can hold (the characteristic polynomial is `1`), so
the two support hypotheses force `φ = 0` and `f = 0`.

## Comparison with `GeneralLinearBaseChangeTraceComparisonStatement`

The Lean term differs from that of `GeneralLinearBaseChangeTraceComparisonStatement` exactly as
follows.

1. The finite place `w` of `L` and the hypothesis that `Φ(N(g))` is regular elliptic at `w` for
   `g ∈ tsupport φ` are removed. The finite place `v` of `K` is bound at their position, and the
   new hypothesis is the σ_v-ellipticity of `φ` at `v` stated above, the local condition of
   `WeilGeneralLinearRationalSigmaEllipticityStatement` written with the same Library terms. The
   hypothesis on `tsupport f` uses this `v`, which is no longer bound a second time.
2. In hypothesis 1 the premise "`Φ(N(r))` is regular elliptic at `w`, or `q` is regular elliptic
   at `v`" becomes "`q` is regular elliptic at `v`", written as in the existing statement.
3. Hypothesis 2, all other data and all conclusions are unchanged.

The two statements have the same data and conclusions and differ only in these hypotheses. The
support condition at `v` does not give the one at a place `w` of `L` above `v`: for a quadratic
extension of fields `E/F` with nontrivial automorphism `σ` and `α ∈ Eˣ \ Fˣ`, the matrix
`δ = [[0, α], [1, 0]] ∈ GL_2(E)` has norm `δ σ(δ) = diag(α, σ(α))`. That norm is split over `E`,
while it is conjugate to the matrix of multiplication by `α` on the `F`-vector space `E`, which
is regular elliptic over `F`. With `F = K_v` and `E = L_w` for a place `v` that does not split
in a quadratic `L`, this is a σ_v-elliptic point whose norm is not regular elliptic at `w`. (The
same example is in the Markdown of `GeneralLinearRationalEllipticTwistedTraceFormulaStatement`.)
So the support condition at `v` can hold at points where the norm condition at the place `w`
above `v` fails.

## Why the two changed hypotheses suffice

Hypothesis 1 of `GeneralLinearBaseChangeTraceComparisonStatement` carries a disjunction because
its proof pairs a twisted class represented in the twisted geometric side with an untwisted
class that need not be represented in the untwisted side, and the twisted side then supplies
regular ellipticity of the norm at `w`. In the present statement the twisted side supplies
regular ellipticity at `v` of the associated `GL_n` point, as follows.

- *Contributing twisted classes have σ_v-elliptic component at `v`.* Let `r` be a rational
  norm-one point of `W` contributing to the twisted side, that is `φ¹(x⁻¹ r θ(x)) ≠ 0` for some
  `x ∈ W(𝔸_K)¹`. Then `g = a x⁻¹ r Θ(x)` lies in `tsupport φ` for some `a ∈ A⁺_W`. The points
  of `A⁺_W` have component `1` at every finite place, and the component at `v` of `Θ` is the
  entrywise action of `σ_v` (`localPointsEquiv_atPlace_adelicAut`). So the component of `g` at
  `v` is a σ_v-twisted conjugate of that of `r`. Since `σ_v ^ l = 1`, σ_v-ellipticity is
  invariant under twisted conjugation (`IsNormAssociated.twistedConj`), and the component of `r`
  at `v` is σ_v-elliptic.
- *Such a point is rationally σ-elliptic.* By `WeilGeneralLinearRationalSigmaEllipticityStatement`,
  `Φ(r) = rational(δ)` for some `δ ∈ GL_n(L)` that is σ-elliptic over `K`. This is the support
  hypothesis of `GeneralLinearRationalEllipticTwistedTraceFormulaStatement`.
- *Every `GL_n` point associated with its norm is regular elliptic at `v`.* Let `q` be a rational
  point of `G` and `y ∈ GL_n(L)` with `Φ(N(r)) · rational(y) = rational(y) · ΦG(q)`, and let
  `γ ∈ GL_n(K)` be the matrix of `q`. Since `Φ` carries `Θ` to the entrywise action of `σ`
  (`GeneralLinearWeilAdelicTwistStatement`), `Φ(N(r))` is the image of the cyclic norm
  `N(δ) = δ σ(δ) ⋯ σ^{l-1}(δ)`, so `y⁻¹ N(δ) y = γ`: `δ` and `γ` are associated over `K`. The
  component of `r` at `v` is the image `δ_v` of `δ` under `L → E_v`, and applying this map
  to the association shows that the image `γ_v` of `γ` in `GL_n(K_v)` is associated with `δ_v`.
  The σ_v-ellipticity of `δ_v` gives a regular elliptic `γ' ∈ GL_n(K_v)` associated with `δ_v`.
  By part (1b) of `CyclicNormLemmaStatement` (proved as `cyclicNormLemma`) for the cyclic
  `K_v`-algebra `E_v`, `γ_v` and `γ'` are conjugate in `GL_n(K_v)`. So `γ_v` is regular elliptic,
  that is, `q` is regular elliptic at `v`. These are steps 2–4 of the proof idea in the Markdown of
  `WeilGeneralLinearRationalSigmaEllipticityStatement`.

Hence for every pair `(r, q)` that the proof meets, `q` is regular elliptic at `v`, whether it
comes from a represented twisted class or from a represented untwisted class (the
representatives of `GeneralLinearSimpleTraceFormulaStatement` are regular elliptic at `v`), and
hypothesis 1 in its single-condition form applies. Hypothesis 2 is used only at untwisted
representatives, which are regular elliptic at `v`.

## Relation with `GeneralLinearBaseChangeGlobalMatchingStatement`

`GeneralLinearBaseChangeGlobalMatchingStatement` gives, for factorizable test functions that are
concordant at a finite set of places and units elsewhere, a real `c > 0` and two conclusions
for every finite place `w` of `L` and every finite place `v` of `K`. Take `TW = TG = T₀`,
`IW = IG = I`, `eW = eG = e₀` and the constant `(c : ℂ)`, and consider test functions that
satisfy the hypotheses of both statements.

- Its first conclusion, for any finite place `w` of `L`, implies hypothesis 1 of the present
  statement: if `q` is regular elliptic at `v`, the right disjunct of its premise
  "`Φ(N(r))` is regular elliptic at `w`, or `q` is regular elliptic at `v`" holds. A number
  field has finite places, so such a `w` exists.
- Its second conclusion is hypothesis 2 of the present statement verbatim.

## Proof sketch (not formalized here)

The pairing of terms is that of the proof sketch of
`GeneralLinearBaseChangeTraceComparisonStatement`; only the source of the ellipticity at `v`
changes.

1. **Twisted side.** Apply `GeneralLinearRationalEllipticTwistedTraceFormulaStatement` to
   `TW, iW, hiW, IW, eW, hcW`, the fixed-torus hypothesis and `φ₁, φ₂`. Its convolution and
   average are `φ` and `φ¹`. Its support hypothesis (every contributing rational point is
   rationally σ-elliptic) follows from the σ_v-ellipticity of `φ` at `v` and
   `WeilGeneralLinearRationalSigmaEllipticityStatement`, as in the first two items of the
   previous section. It supplies `hΘ`, `hΘμ`, `hφ`, trace class of `R(φ)`, and
   `tr (R(φ) ∘ I_θ) = ∑_j vol((Γ ∩ C_{r_j}) \ C_{r_j}) · TO_{r_j}(φ¹)` for representatives
   `r_j` of pairwise distinct rational twisted classes, each contributing, covering all
   contributing classes, for every compatible choice of measures.
2. **Untwisted side.** `GeneralLinearSimpleTraceFormulaStatement` (proved as
   `generalLinearSimpleTraceFormula`), applied to `TG, iG, hiG, IG, eG, hcG`, `f₁, f₂` and the
   place `v`, supplies `hf`, trace class of `R(f)` and
   `tr R(f) = ∑_k vol((Γ ∩ C_{q_k}) \ C_{q_k}) · O_{q_k}(f¹)` for representatives `q_k` of
   pairwise distinct rational classes, each regular elliptic at `v`, covering all contributing
   classes.
3. **Norms.** Through `Φ`, `W(K) = GL_n(L)` and `Θ` acts entrywise by `σ`
   (`GeneralLinearWeilAdelicTwistStatement`, proved as `generalLinearWeilAdelicTwist`). By
   `cyclicNormLemma` over `K`, the norm `N(δ)` of `δ ∈ GL_n(L)` is `GL_n(L)`-conjugate to some
   `γ ∈ GL_n(K)`, `γ` is unique up to `GL_n(K)`-conjugacy, and `δ` is determined up to
   σ-conjugacy by the `GL_n(L)`-class of `N(δ)`. So the norm is a bijection from σ-classes in
   `GL_n(L)` onto the classes of norms in `GL_n(K)`. For the twisted representatives the
   associated `GL_n` points are regular elliptic at `v` (previous section).
4. **Centralizers and measures.** For a pair with `Φ(N(r)) y = y ΦG(q)` and `q` regular elliptic
   at `v`, `q` is regular semisimple, and the construction of step 3 of the proof sketch of
   `GeneralLinearBaseChangeTraceComparisonStatement` gives the isomorphism `e` of the norm-one
   centralizers with `ΦG(e(x)) = y⁻¹ Φ(x) y`, which maps the rational centralizers onto each
   other, and `μq = e_* μr` gives equal volumes.
5. **Pairing.** For a pair of representatives `r_j`, `q_k` related by the norm, hypothesis 1
   gives `TO_{r_j}(φ¹) = c · O_{q_k}(f¹)`. If the norm class of `r_j` is not represented in the
   untwisted sum, choose an associated `q`; it is regular elliptic at `v`, it does not contribute
   by the covering property of step 2, so its orbital integral vanishes and hypothesis 1 gives
   `TO_{r_j}(φ¹) = c · 0`. If `q_k` is a norm of a rational `r` whose class is not represented in
   the twisted sum, then `r` does not contribute by the covering property of step 1, and
   hypothesis 1, which applies because `q_k` is regular elliptic at `v`, gives
   `0 = c · O_{q_k}(f¹)`. If `q_k` is not a norm, hypothesis 2 gives `O_{q_k}(f¹) = 0`. Summing
   gives `tr (R(φ) ∘ I_θ) = c · tr R(f)` for every `c`, including `0`.

Library's group schemes `Res_{L/K} GL_n` and `GL_n`, with norm-one subgroups and `A⁺` averages,
matching at global norms and vanishing off global norms taken as hypotheses, and the

`GeneralLinearBaseChangeTraceComparisonStatement`. The scalar `c` is the factor supplied by the
matching hypothesis for the chosen functions and measure normalizations.
