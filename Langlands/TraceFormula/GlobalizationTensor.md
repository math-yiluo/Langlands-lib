# Compact adelic tensors with prescribed finite factors

`GlobalizationTensor.test` forms a compactly supported continuous function on the actual
adelic matrix group from an archimedean test, a Schwartz–Bruhat function at each prescribed
finite place, and the indicator of the integral subgroup at all remaining finite places.
The splitting at finitely many places expresses the support bound as a compact product.

Factoring out one prescribed local test leaves a complementary function invariant under
inserting a matrix at that place. Inserting a locally integral matrix at any remaining
finite place preserves the whole tensor. The coordinate comparisons identify localization
and insertion of actual group-scheme points with the corresponding matrix operations.
These are the tensor and invariance inputs for adelic convolution with a prescribed
supercuspidal coefficient.
