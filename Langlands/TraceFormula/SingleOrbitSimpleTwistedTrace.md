# The twisted simple trace formula on an isolated rational orbit

`GeneralLinearSingleOrbitTwistedTraceStatement` assumes the Library's
`GeneralLinearSimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStatement` and
`GeneralLinearWeilAdelicTwistStatement`. It uses the cyclic extension,
the Weil restriction of the general linear group, and the central
split torus fixed by the twist from the simple trace formula. It
retains the character restriction hypotheses and right invariance of
the norm-one Haar. The two convolution factors satisfy the
Hilbert–Schmidt and parabolic vanishing conditions of that formula.
The transported norms of the closed convolution support are regular
elliptic at a supplied finite place of the upper number field.

The chosen rational norm-one representative comes with compatible
measures for the orbital quotient and for the quotient of its
adelic centralizer by the rational intersection. The latter uses
counting measure on the rational intersection. Assume every rational
twisted orbit on which the normalized positive-torus average is
nonzero somewhere is rationally twisted-conjugate to the chosen
representative.

The conclusion gives the subgroup and measure preservation required
by the twist operator on the positive rational quotient. It gives
integrability of the convolution and trace class of the integrated
operator. The trace of its composition with the twist operator equals
the chosen covolume times the twisted orbital integral. The chosen
covolume is finite whenever that orbital integral is nonzero.

The proof applies the twisted simple trace formula. The Galois
automorphism homomorphism equates the two ordered power expressions
for the norm. Regularity and the Weil comparison then give
commutative centralizers for the representatives in the finite
expansion. The proof constructs compatible measures for those
representatives, instantiates the geometric expansion using the Borel
quotient structures, and uses rational twisted conjugation to
evaluate the sum with the supplied measures at the chosen class.

This internal reduction uses the Library's form of Getz–Hahn 2024,
Corollary 18.5.1, in the orbital isolation of Arthur–Clozel 1989,
Chapter 1, §4.
