# Local components of `Res_{L/K} GL_n` at a place of a number field

Module `Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearBaseChangeLocal`. Let `K` be a number field
and `L / K` a finite extension. Let `W = Res_{L/K} GL_n` be the Library's Weil restriction
(`weilGroup K L GLₙ` of `Langlands.TraceFormula.AdelicGroupAutomorphism`). At a place `i` of
`K`, with completion `K_i` (the Library's `placeCompletion K i`), this module identifies the
local points `W(K_i)` with `GL_n(E_i)`, where `E_i = K_i ⊗_K L`. It transports the Galois
twist, the unit `GL_n → W` and the adelic norm to the place `i`. It also defines the integral
subgroups at the finite places and the restricted tensor product condition on adelic
functions. These are the local objects of the comparison of the twisted trace formula for
`W` with the trace formula for `GL_n` (cyclic base change).

## Sources

Arthur–Clozel, *Simple algebras, base change, and the advanced theory of the trace formula*
(1989), registered `arthur-clozel-1989-base-change`, Ch. 1 §1, printed pp. 3–4 (PDF pp. 17–18).
There `G = GL(n)`, `E / F` is cyclic with Galois group generated by `σ`, the norm of
`x ∈ G(E)` is `Nx = x x^σ ⋯ x^{σ^{l-1}}`, and for a global field `F` the local norm at a place
`v` is defined on `G(E ⊗_F F_v)`, with the Galois group acting through the factor `E`.

Henniart–Lemaire, *Changement de base et induction automorphe pour GL(n) en caractéristique
non nulle* (2011), registered
`provided-a0a5445aba86b871bcd83f08813de3234fddb463ff9bb3c9dfd60d2d2fcdae61`,
§I.2.1, printed pp. 10–11, describes cyclic algebras as products of fields, with fixed scalars
and the same ordered matrix norm. The Library's `IsCyclicAlgebra` encodes the cyclic algebra
conditions using reducedness, dimension, the order of the automorphism and its fixed scalars.

The local point comparisons and the integral subgroup results are derived using the Library's
Weil restriction, point topologies and integral matrix groups. `IsAdelicFactorization` expresses
the function identity determined by a finite set of local functions and the subgroup indicators
at the other places.

## Conventions

- The local algebra is `E_i = K_i ⊗_K L`, with `K_i` on the left. The Library's local points
  of the Weil restriction (`AffineWeil.unitGroundPointsEquiv`) use `L ⊗_K K_i`.
  The isomorphism `localSwap` exchanges the factors.
- `σ_i = id ⊗ σ` acts on `E_i`; on matrices it acts entrywise (Library `Langlands.BaseChange.entrywiseAut`).
- The integral families are the whole group at the infinite places.

## Field structure on the completions

- `Langlands.Global.placeCompletion.isField K i` proves that every completion `K_i` is a field.
- `placeCompletionField K i : Field (placeCompletion K i)` is built from that fact
  (`IsField.toField`), so its ring structure is the ring structure of `placeCompletion K i`.
  `open scoped Langlands.TraceFormula.PlaceCompletion` activates `PlaceCompletion.field`.
  The scope is needed because at a finite place `v` the inverse from `placeCompletionField`
  differs definitionally from the inverse of `v.adicCompletion K`.

## The local algebra and its automorphism

- `LocalBaseChangeAlgebra K L i` is the type `K_i ⊗_K L`. Its instances are the commutative
  ring, the `K_i`-algebra and `K`-algebra structures with the scalar tower `K → K_i → E_i`,
  and finiteness over `K_i` when `L / K` is finite. Its topology is the module topology over
  `K_i` (`IsModuleTopology`), and it is a topological ring when `L / K` is finite.
  The instances `LocalBaseChangeAlgebra.t2Space` and `LocalBaseChangeAlgebra.locallyCompactSpace`
  follow from the coefficient homeomorphism for a basis of `L` over `K` extended to `K_i`.
  The coefficient space is a finite product of copies of the Hausdorff locally compact
  completion `K_i`.
- `localGalois K L σ i : E_i ≃ₐ[K_i] E_i` is `σ_i = id ⊗ σ` for `σ : L ≃ₐ[K] L`
  (`localGalois_tmul`: `σ_i (x ⊗ y) = x ⊗ σ y`).
- `localGaloisHom K L i : (L ≃ₐ[K] L) →* (E_i ≃ₐ[K_i] E_i)`, `σ ↦ σ_i`, is a group
  homomorphism with this action given by `localGaloisHom_apply`.
  The lemma `localGalois_pow` proves `(σ^k)_i = σ_i^k`.
- `localGaloisHom_injective` proves that `σ ↦ σ_i` is injective using the embedding `1 ⊗ L`.
- `isCyclicAlgebra_localGalois` sends `IsCyclicAlgebra σ l` over `K` to
  `IsCyclicAlgebra σ_i l` over `K_i` using the scoped field instance. For the field `L` the
  hypothesis expresses that `L / K` is cyclic of degree `l` with generator `σ`.
  The proof computes the rank by base change, gets reducedness from separability of `L / K`
  (formally unramified), the order of `σ_i` from injectivity, and the fixed points from
  flatness of `K_i` over `K` applied to the exact sequence `K → L → L`, `y ↦ σ y - y`.

## Local points

Assume `L / K` finite.

- `localPointsEquiv K L n i : W(K_i) ≃* GL_n(E_i)` composes the Library's identification
  `W(K_i) ≃ GL_n(L ⊗_K K_i)` (`AffineWeil.unitGroundPointsEquiv` followed by
  `GeneralLinear.affinePointsEquiv`) with `localSwap` applied entrywise.
- `localPointsHomeomorph K L n i : W(K_i) ≃ₜ* GL_n(E_i)` uses the Library's
  regular-function evaluation topology on `W(K_i)` and the matrix-units topology over `E_i`.
  Its underlying group isomorphism is `localPointsEquiv` by `localPointsHomeomorph_toMulEquiv`.
  For continuity of the Weil restriction comparison, evaluation at each section is given by
  evaluating a universal tensor. Tensor induction reduces continuity to finite sums of
  continuous coefficient evaluations and the continuous maps `t ↦ b ⊗ t` for `b ∈ L`.
  On `L ⊗_K K_i` the proof uses the topology induced by `localSwap`, under which
  `b ⊗ t` is transported to `t • (1 ⊗ b)`. The remaining maps are the Library's continuous
  base-change and matrix comparisons followed by the entrywise swap.
  For inverse continuity, the source is sigma compact by local compactness and second
  countability. The target is Hausdorff and locally compact because the matrix units
  embed as the closed subset of pairs of mutually inverse matrices. It is therefore a
  Baire space, and the topological group open mapping theorem gives inverse continuity.
- `localGLEquiv K n i : GL_n(K_i)` (points of the group scheme) `≃* GL_n(K_i)` (matrices),
  and `localGLHomeomorph K n i`, the same map as a topological group isomorphism (Library
  `GeneralLinear.affinePointsHomeomorph`). The lemma `localGLHomeomorph_toMulEquiv` identifies
  its underlying group isomorphism.
- By `localSwap_coefficientConjugate` and `localSwap_one_tmul`, `localSwap` carries `σ ⊗ 1` on
  `L ⊗_K K_i` to `σ_i`, and `1 ⊗ x` to `algebraMap K_i E_i x`.
- `localPointsEquiv_twist` identifies the Galois twist `unitGaloisAut K L GLₙ σ` of `W` on
  `W(K_i) = GL_n(E_i)` with `entrywiseAut σ_i`.
- By `localPointsEquiv_unit`, the unit `AffineWeil.unit K L GLₙ : GL_n → W` acts on local points
  as the base change `GL_n(K_i) → GL_n(E_i)` (Library `Langlands.BaseChange.baseChange`). The same map is
  packaged as `localUnitHom K L n i : GL_n(K_i) →* W(K_i)` with
  `localPointsEquiv_localUnitHom`.
- `localPointsEquiv_atPlace_adelicAut` identifies the component at `i` of the adelic twist
  `adelicAut K (unitGaloisAut K L GLₙ σ)` with `entrywiseAut σ_i` of the component at `i`.
- `localPointsEquiv_atPlace_norm` identifies the component at `i` of the ordered adelic norm
  `x · θ(x) ⋯ θ^{l-1}(x)` with `cyclicNorm σ_i l` (Library `Langlands.BaseChange.cyclicNorm`)
  of the component at `i`. The proof uses that both the place projection and the matrix
  comparison preserve the product, and that lifting `σ` commutes with powers.
- By `localPointsEquiv_atPlace_unit`, the component at `i` of the adelic unit
  `GL_n(𝔸_K) → W(𝔸_K)` is the base change of the component at `i`.

## Integral subgroups

At a finite place `v` of `K`, with integers `O_v` (`v.adicCompletionIntegers K`):

- `localIntegers K L v` is the subring `O_{E_v}` of `E_v` generated by `O_v ⊗ 1` and `1 ⊗ 𝓞_L`.
- `isOpen_localIntegers` and `isCompact_localIntegers`: `O_{E_v}` is open and compact in
  the module topology on `E_v`. The image of a `ℤ`-basis of `𝓞_L` spans that subring over
  `O_v` because the image of `𝓞_L` is closed under multiplication. The same basis is a
  `ℚ`-basis of `L` and its image spans `E_v` over `K_v` by extension of scalars. The
  `K_v`-linear map taking coefficients to their linear combination is therefore a
  continuous open surjection. It sends the finite product of copies of `O_v` onto
  `O_{E_v}`, giving openness and compactness.
- `localIntegralSubgroup K L n v` is the subgroup of `W(K_v)` corresponding to
  `GL_n(O_{E_v})` under `localPointsEquiv`. The Library's `GeneralLinearGroup.integralSubgroup`
  requires the matrix entries and the inverse matrix entries to lie in `O_{E_v}`.
  `isOpen_localIntegralSubgroup` and `isCompact_localIntegralSubgroup` transport openness and
  compactness of these integral matrices through `localPointsHomeomorph`.
- `localIntegralFamily K L n i` gives `localIntegralSubgroup` at finite places and `⊤` at
  infinite places.
- `localGalois_mem_localIntegers` proves that `σ_v` preserves `O_{E_v}` by checking its
  action on the two sets of subring generators.
- `twist_mem_localIntegralFamily` proves that the twist preserves each subgroup of
  `localIntegralFamily`.
- `glIntegralSubgroup K n v` is the subgroup of `GL_n(K_v)` corresponding to `GL_n(O_v)`
  under `localGLEquiv`. `isOpen_glIntegralSubgroup` and `isCompact_glIntegralSubgroup` prove it
  is open and compact via `localGLHomeomorph`.
- `glIntegralFamily K n i` gives `glIntegralSubgroup` at finite places and `⊤` at infinite
  places. `isUniformlyIntegral_glIntegralFamily` proves that for each regular function,
  at almost all finite places it takes integral values on all of `GL_n(O_v)`. The proof
  uses the matrix integrality condition from the standard representation and applies
  `LinearIntegralFamily.eventually_uniform_integral`.
- By `localUnitHom_mem_localIntegralFamily`, the unit maps each subgroup of `glIntegralFamily`
  into the corresponding subgroup of `localIntegralFamily`.

## Restricted tensor products of functions

For a group scheme `G` over `K`, a finite set `T` of places, local functions
`φloc i : G(K_i) → ℂ` and local subgroups `U i`, the predicate `IsAdelicFactorization T φloc U φ`
says that for every `x ∈ G(𝔸_K)`, `φ(x) = ∏_{i ∈ T} φloc i (x_i)` if `x_i ∈ U i` for every
`i ∉ T`, and `φ(x) = 0` otherwise.
