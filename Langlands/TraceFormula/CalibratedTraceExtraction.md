# Local orbital equality from calibrated trace identities

Let \(G\) and \(H\) be topological groups with their Borel measurable
structures. The statement uses fixed ordinary and twisted geometric measure
packages, each with associated orbital and rational quotient measures. For
each of two pairs of tests, its trace is the weighted term of that same
rational orbit. Its orbital integral factors as the specified local orbital
value times an exterior value that is fixed as the local test varies.

For the first pair the two local values agree and are nonzero. The ordinary
exterior value is nonzero, and a nonzero ordinary orbital integral for
this pair implies finiteness of its rational covolume. Suppose that the
twisted trace equals the ordinary trace times the same nonzero factor for
both pairs. `CalibratedTraceExtractionStatement` gives equality of the
two local values for the second pair.

The first trace identity identifies the two covolume-weighted exterior
values up to the comparison factor. The ordinary weighted exterior value
is nonzero by finiteness and compatibility of its rational quotient
measure. Substitution in the second trace identity and cancellation give
the result. The finite-place factorization and the isolated simple trace
formula supply the geometric hypotheses in the application.

This is an internal reduction for the single-orbit extraction in
Arthur–Clozel (1989), Chapter 1, proof of Theorem 4.5, pp. 43–46, and
Henniart–Lemaire (2011), I.6.8, pp. 49–50. The proof here takes the common
weight ratio from a concordant regular test pair with nonzero orbital value.
