# Centralizers of rational `GL_n` elements that are regular elliptic at a finite place (statement)

Module `Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearEllipticCentralizerStatement` contains one
proposition definition and no proof:

- `Langlands.TraceFormula.GeneralLinearEllipticCentralizerStatement.{u}`.

On the geometric side of the simple trace formula for `GL_n`, each rational conjugacy class
`γ` that is regular elliptic at a finite place contributes
`vol(Γ_γ \ C_γ) · ∫_{C_γ \ G(𝔸_F)¹} f¹(x⁻¹ γ x) dx`. This statement supplies the two analytic
facts these terms need: the volume is finite, and the orbit function of a compactly supported
continuous function is integrable. It is stated for all measures satisfying the compatibility
conditions used in `GeneralLinearSimpleTraceFormulaStatement` (Task 7585).

## Statement

For every number field `F` (in universe `u`), every `n`, every finite place `v`, and every
`r ∈ Γ` whose component at `v` is regular elliptic, write `C` for the centralizer of `r` in
`GL_n(𝔸_F)¹`. For all Borel measurable structures on `C \ GL_n(𝔸_F)¹` and on `(Γ ∩ C) \ C`,
every right-invariant measure `μC` on `C` that is finite on compact sets and positive on open
sets, and all measures `νC` on `C \ GL_n(𝔸_F)¹` and `νΓC` on `(Γ ∩ C) \ C` such that

- `νC` is compatible with `Measure.haar` on `GL_n(𝔸_F)¹` and `μC`, and
- `νΓC` is compatible with `μC` and counting measure on `Γ ∩ C`,

the following hold:

1. `νΓC(univ) ≠ ⊤`: the quotient `(Γ ∩ C) \ C` has finite volume;
2. for every continuous, compactly supported `φ : GL_n(𝔸_F)¹ → ℂ`, the orbit function
   `C x ↦ φ(x⁻¹ r x)` is `νC`-integrable.

If no such measures exist the statement says nothing about that `r`. It does not assert that
compatible measures exist.

## Why it is true (for the reviewer)

Let `p` be the characteristic polynomial of `r`, as a matrix in `GL_n(F)`. Since `p` is
irreducible and separable over `F_v`, it is irreducible and separable over `F`, and
`E = F[X]/(p)` is a field of degree `n` over `F` (so `n ≥ 1`).

1. Centralizer. The centralizer of `r` in `M_n(R)`, for any `F`-algebra `R`, is
   `F[r] ⊗_F R ≅ E ⊗_F R`, since `r` is regular. So the centralizer of `r` in `GL_n(𝔸_F)` is
   the image of `T(𝔸_F) = (E ⊗_F 𝔸_F)^× = 𝔸_E^×`, where `T = Res_{E/F} 𝔾_m` embeds in `GL_n` as
   a closed subgroup. Its rational characters are generated by `N_{E/F}`, and
   `det = N_{E/F}` on `T`, so `C = T(𝔸_F)¹ = 𝔸_E¹` and `Γ ∩ C = T(F) = E^×`.
2. Finite volume. `E^× \ 𝔸_E¹` is compact (Fujisaki's lemma; in the Library,
   `hasCompactNormOneRationalCover_of_isTorus` and `HasCompactNormOneRationalCover.compactSpace`
   give compactness of `T(F) \ T(𝔸_F)¹` for every torus `T`). The topology of `C` as a subspace of
   `GL_n(𝔸_F)¹` is the adelic topology of `T(𝔸_F)¹`, since `T` is closed in `GL_n`. A regular
   measure (part of `IsCompatible`) is finite on compact sets, so `νΓC(univ) < ∞`.
3. Closed orbit. Let `O` be the `GL_n(𝔸_F)`-conjugacy class of `r`. It equals the set of
   `g ∈ GL_n(𝔸_F)` with characteristic polynomial `p` at every place: at each place `w`, the
   matrices with the separable characteristic polynomial `p` form one `GL_n(F_w)`-class; at
   almost all `w`, `p` has `w`-integral coefficients and unit discriminant, so `O_w[X]/(p)` is
   étale over `O_w` and every `g_w ∈ GL_n(O_w)` with characteristic polynomial `p` is
   `GL_n(O_w)`-conjugate to the companion matrix (the module `O_w^n` is locally free of rank one
   over the étale algebra, hence free). So `O` is closed in `GL_n(𝔸_F)`, being cut out by
   continuous conditions. Since `A⁺` is central and `GL_n(𝔸_F) = A⁺ · GL_n(𝔸_F)¹`, `O` is also the
   `GL_n(𝔸_F)¹`-class of `r`, and is closed in `GL_n(𝔸_F)¹`.
4. Integrability. `GL_n(𝔸_F)¹` is a second-countable locally compact group acting transitively
   on the locally compact space `O`, so the orbit map `C \ GL_n(𝔸_F)¹ → O`, `C x ↦ x⁻¹ r x`, is a
   homeomorphism (open mapping theorem for σ-compact groups). Hence the orbit function of `φ`
   is continuous with support in the preimage of the compact set `supp φ ∩ O`, which is
   compact. `νC` is regular, so finite on compact sets, and the orbit function is integrable.

## Source and what is derived

Derived content. Getz–Hahn, *An introduction to automorphic representations* (2024),
registered `getz-hahn-2024-automorphic-representations`, §18.4 (printed p. 485), call
`γ ∈ G(F)` elliptic when `A_G C_γ(F) \ C_γ(𝔸_F)` has finite volume, and Corollary 18.4.1 there
sums `τ(C_γ) O_γ(f)` over elliptic semisimple classes, with `O_γ(f)` the orbital integral
(18.26) over `C_γ(𝔸_F) \ G(𝔸_F)`. Theorem 18.2.5 (printed p. 479), of which Corollary 18.4.1
is a case, defines `τ(H_γ)` in (18.14) as the volume of `A H_γ(F) \ H_γ(𝔸_F)` and assumes that
the relevant orbits are closed. For `GL_n` and rational elements regular elliptic at a finite
place, this statement asserts the corresponding two properties (finite volume, and
integrability of the orbit function that defines the orbital integral) in the measure
conventions of the Library: `G(𝔸_F)¹` in place of `A_G \ G(𝔸_F)`, the centralizer `C` in
`G(𝔸_F)¹` in place of `A_G \ C_γ(𝔸_F)`, and `(Γ ∩ C) \ C` in place of
`A_G C_γ(F) \ C_γ(𝔸_F)`. It is not stated in this form in the source; in particular the source
takes ellipticity as a hypothesis, while here finiteness of the volume is a conclusion from
regular ellipticity at a finite place.
