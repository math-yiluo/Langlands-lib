# Fixed-type finite expansions of the discrete trace sum

Arthur and Clozel, *Simple Algebras, Base Change, and the Advanced Theory of
the Trace Formula* (1989), II §9, (9.2)–(9.3), printed p. 132, expands the
discrete sum into characters. III.4.2, printed pp. 203–204, fixes the compact
types and the finite ramification level so the expansion is uniform as
the good-place spherical tests vary.

`HasFixedTraceTypes` fixes a compact open subgroup of the finite adeles
and two finite-dimensional continuous representations of the actual
infinite compact. The left and right translates of the test belong to
the respective spaces generated by intertwining images of these types.
The test is bi-invariant under the norm-one image of the finite level.

`FixedTypeTraceSummands` gives finite families of mutually orthogonal
irreducible closed subrepresentations of the actual induced Hilbert
slices. The existence statement requires each finite family to work
uniformly at the fixed types and level. The integrated action vanishes
on their common orthogonal complement. The trace of each summand equals
the sum of the actual compressed traces on this family, with the
intertwiner and the twist in their original order. Multiplying by the
Weyl weights gives the corresponding expansion of the full distribution.
