# Finite-level norm preservation and approximation

The theorem `Langlands.LocalClassField.UnramifiedAlgebra.proof.{u,v}` proves
exactly `Langlands.LocalClassField.UnramifiedAlgebra.Statement.{u,v}` from
`Langlands.LocalClassField.UnramifiedAlgebraStatement`. The two ring universes
remain independent. The imported statement and its paired Markdown are
unchanged.

For a finite free local algebra `S` over a DVR `R`, with the structural
hypotheses in that statement and
`Ideal.map (algebraMap R S) (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal R) =
IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal S`, the proof establishes both conclusions:

- The actual determinant norm `Algebra.norm R` preserves units congruent to
  one modulo every natural power of the maximal ideal.
- A unit of `R` at level `n` admits a unit of `S` at that same level whose norm
  differs from it by the specified multiplicative correction at level `n + 1`.

Both conclusions include `n = 0`. No domain or DVR hypothesis on `S`, Galois
hypothesis, completeness hypothesis, or norm/trace-surjectivity assumption is
added. The result concerns these finite congruence levels; it does not assert
full norm surjectivity.

## Implemented proof

Write `p` for the maximal ideal of `R` and `q` for its image in `S`.
All auxiliary lemmas are private to this proof module.

`norm_quotient_mk` proves that the determinant norm commutes with reduction
from `S/R` to `(S/q)/(R/p)`. Choose a finite free basis of `S` over `R` and
its quotient basis `IsLocalRing.basisQuotient`. Their multiplication matrices have
entries related by `Ideal.Quotient.mk p`; this follows from the quotient
basis coordinate formula. Rewriting both norms with
`Algebra.norm_eq_matrix_det` and applying `RingHom.map_det` gives the claimed
compatibility. This argument requires no domain hypothesis on `S`.

`trace_residue_lift` uses the given equality `q = maximalIdeal S` to obtain
the field structure on `S/q`. Its finiteness is transported from the actual
residue field of `S` along `Ideal.quotEquivOfEq`. The resulting finite field
extension of `R/p` has surjective trace by `Algebra.trace_surjective`; the
finite-dimensionality and separability instances are consequences of these
field structures and finiteness. Lifting a trace preimage to `S` and using
`Algebra.trace_quotient_mk` yields, for each `b : R`, an `a : S` such that
`Algebra.trace R S a - b` belongs to `p`.

`norm_residue_lift` uses the same quotient fields and
`FiniteField.unitsMap_norm_surjective`. It lifts a residue unit with prescribed
norm to an element of `S`. The lift is a unit because its residue is nonzero,
using `IsLocalRing.residue_ne_zero_iff_isUnit`. The determinant comparison
above then gives, for a given unit `y` of `R`, a unit `x` of `S` with
`y - Algebra.norm R x` in `p`.

`correction_mem` converts additive norm congruences into the target
multiplicative correction. For units `y` and `v` and any ideal `I`, it uses

\[
 yv^{-1}-1=(y-v)v^{-1}.
\]

Thus membership of `y-v` in `I` implies membership of `yv⁻¹-1`. In the
applications, `v` is exactly `Units.map (Algebra.norm R) x`.

The final theorem chooses an irreducible element `π` in the DVR `R`.
`Irreducible.maximalIdeal_eq` gives `p = (π)`, and the mapped-ideal hypothesis
gives `maximalIdeal S = (algebraMap R S π)`. Powers of these principal ideals
are generated by the corresponding powers of `π`.

`norm_preserves` writes a given unit at level `n` as `1 + πⁿ • a`.
`Algebra.norm_one_add_smul` supplies an `r : R` satisfying

\[
 N(1+\pi^n a)=1+\operatorname{Tr}(a)\pi^n+r(\pi^n)^2.
\]

The difference from one is divisible by `πⁿ`, with quotient
`Tr(a) + r * πⁿ`. This proves preservation at every natural `n`, including
zero, directly.

`norm_approximates` first handles `n = 0` by `norm_residue_lift` and
`correction_mem`; the input ideal power is the whole ring. For `n > 0`, it
writes `y - 1 = πⁿ b` and chooses `a` by `trace_residue_lift`, so that
`Tr(a) - b` lies in `p`. The element `1 + πⁿ • a` is a unit because its
residue is one, and its difference from one belongs to the required power of
the maximal ideal of `S`. With the same norm expansion,

\[
 y-N(1+\pi^n a)
   =-(\operatorname{Tr}(a)-b)\pi^n-r(\pi^n)^2.
\]

The first term belongs to `p^(n+1)` by multiplication of ideal memberships.
The second belongs to `p^(2n)`, which is contained in `p^(n+1)` because
`n + 1 ≤ n + n`. Closure under negation and subtraction proves the additive
congruence, and `correction_mem` supplies exactly the unit-group expression
in the published target. The final theorem combines these two proofs.

## Provenance and source scope

This is a derived finite free local-algebra proof of the published statement.
Its source motivation is Jean-Pierre Serre, *Local Fields*, Marvin Jay
Greenberg translation, GTM 67, Springer (1979), Chapter V, §2, Propositions
1–3, the subsequent finite-residue-field remark, and the non-Galois exercise,
printed pp.81–82 (PDF pp.88–89). Those original pages were read for this proof.
The registered source is `serre-1979-local-fields`, at
`/home/taskhub/central-library/Serre--Local Fields--1979.pdf`, SHA256
`109b461d42915da70771c70c4b551a363fc1f33412331ae2671c5c85be433112`.

Serre identifies the maps on unit congruence quotients with the residue norm
at level zero and the residue trace at positive levels. His initial proof
uses conjugates in an unramified local-field extension. The present Lean
proof implements the finite-free matrix argument described above, which
justifies the more general ring formulation. It does not use the completeness
argument in Serre's Proposition 3, nor any local class field theory result.
Worker 26 contributed the task formulation and suggested the quotient-matrix,
finite-residue, and first-order norm/trace route.

The implementation uses Mathlib commit
`5ed2965256430c3649e86755f9576b54eca72435` and Lean 4.34.0. The principal
external ingredients are in `RingTheory/NormTrace.lean`,
`RingTheory/Trace/Quotient.lean`, `RingTheory/Trace/Basic.lean`,
`FieldTheory/Finite/GaloisField.lean`,
`RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/Basic.lean`, and
`RingTheory/LocalRing/ResidueField/Basic.lean`.
