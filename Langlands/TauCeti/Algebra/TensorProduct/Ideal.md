## Contents

Tensor ideals in `A ⊗[R] B`.

- `Ideal.leftTensorIdeal R B I` (generated by `includeLeft '' I`) and
  `Ideal.rightTensorIdeal R A J`, with membership, `le_iff`, monotonicity, `iSup`
  and `sup` lemmas.
- `Ideal.ker_tensorProduct_map_id_quotient`, `Ideal.ker_tensorProduct_map_quotient_id`:
  the kernel of tensoring a quotient map is the tensor ideal.
- `AlgHom.map_tensor_leftTensorIdeal`, `AlgHom.map_tensor_rightTensorIdeal`, and
  `AlgHom.ker_tensorProduct_map_eq_leftTensorIdeal_sup_rightTensorIdeal` for two
  surjective algebra maps.
