# Completeness of finite integral closures over complete DVFs

This module defines exactly two closed proposition targets corresponding to
two theorems of Naganori Yamaguchi's NYLL ClassFieldTheory at commit
`fbd320cd8b1b06487c58227e92eb135794267709`, both in
[`Nyll/ValuedFieldTheory/Valuation/DiscreteValuationField/FiniteIntegralClosure.lean`, lines 61–115](https://github.com/n-yamaguchi-0729/ClassFieldTheory/blob/fbd320cd8b1b06487c58227e92eb135794267709/Nyll/ValuedFieldTheory/Valuation/DiscreteValuationField/FiniteIntegralClosure.lean#L61-L115):

- M245.003, adic completeness of the integral closure, lines 61–91;
- M245.004, the Henselian property of the integral closure, lines 93–115.

These definitions name propositions; they contain no proofs of the
propositions. Publishing them proves neither assertion. A subsequent proof
must establish each published target separately.

## Independent universes

`K : Type u` is the base field, `v` is the universe of the value group
`base.ValueGroup` of `base : CompleteDVF.{u, v} K`, and `L : Type w` is the
extension field. The source binds the same three independent universes
(lines 23, 30 and the binder `base : CompleteDVF.{u, v} K` at lines 64 and
101). Neither target identifies any two of them or restricts to `Type 0`.
The valuation subring `A` lies in `Type u`; the integral closure `B` and the
ideal-adic data on it lie in `Type w`.

## Quantifiers and mathematical meaning

Both targets universally quantify, in order:
`(K : Type u) (L : Type w) [Field K] [Field L] [Algebra K L]
[FiniteDimensional K L] (base : CompleteDVF.{u, v} K)
[Algebra.IsSeparable K L]`. Then they introduce two local abbreviations:

- `A := base.valuation.valuationSubring`, the valuation subring of `K` for the
  valuation stored in `base`, used as a type through its coercion;
- `B := integralClosure A L`, the subalgebra of `L` consisting of the
  elements integral over `A`.

The first target concludes
`IsAdicComplete ((IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal A).map (algebraMap A B)) B`. The
second target concludes
`HenselianRing B ((IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal A).map (algebraMap A B))`.

In words: let `K` be a field with a complete discrete valuation, `L` a finite
separable extension of `K`, `A` the valuation ring of `K` and `B` the integral
closure of `A` in `L`. Let `I` be the ideal of `B` generated by the image of
the maximal ideal of `A`. The first target asserts that `B` is `I`-adically
complete. The second asserts that `B` is Henselian along `I`.

The ideal is Mathlib's `Ideal.map` of the maximal ideal of `A` along the
algebra map `A → B`, that is, the ideal of `B` generated by its image. It is
not the maximal ideal of `B`: neither target assumes or asserts that `B` is
local. `IsAdicComplete I B` is Mathlib's algebraic class from
`Mathlib/RingTheory/AdicCompletion/Basic.lean` (line 56), with `B` as a
module over itself: the submodules `I ^ n • ⊤` intersect in zero, and every
sequence that is Cauchy for this filtration converges for it. It takes no
topology argument. `HenselianRing B I` is Mathlib's class from
`Mathlib/RingTheory/Henselian.lean` (line 94): `I` lies in the Jacobson
radical of `B`, and for every monic polynomial `f` over `B` and every
`a₀ : B` with `f(a₀) ∈ I` and `f'(a₀)` a unit modulo `I`, there is a root `a`
of `f` with `a - a₀ ∈ I`.

`CompleteDVF.{u, v} K` is the public structure of
`Langlands.NYLL.ValuedFieldTheory.Valuation.DiscreteValuationField.Complete.Definitions`.
It stores a value group, its `LinearOrderedCommGroupWithZero` structure, a
valuation `K → ValueGroup`, and a proof that this valuation is rank-one
discrete and that its valuation subring is complete for the adic topology of
its maximal ideal. That stored completeness is part of the supplied `base`;
it is not a separate premise of either target.

No further hypothesis is added. In particular neither target assumes
`Module.Finite A B`, adic completeness or Henselianity of `B`, `IsLocalRing B`,
an `IsIntegralClosure` instance for some other ring, a chosen extension
valuation on `L`, `Valuation.HasExtension`, residue-field finiteness, a
topology, or an extension record.

## Direct unfolding of the source projections

The source states both theorems with the NYLL projections
`base.valuationSubring` and `base.maximalIdeal`. In the published
`Complete.Projections` module, `base.valuationSubring` unfolds, through the
`CompleteDVF → HenselianDVF → DVF` abbreviations, to the coerced type of
`base.valuation.valuationSubring`, and `base.maximalIdeal` unfolds to
`IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal` of that type. These projections are
abbreviations and structure projections, so the unfolding is definitional.
The targets therefore write `A` and
`IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal A` directly and do not import the projections. The
source conclusions

```
IsAdicComplete
  (base.maximalIdeal.map
    (algebraMap base.valuationSubring (integralClosure base.valuationSubring L)))
  (integralClosure base.valuationSubring L)

HenselianRing (integralClosure base.valuationSubring L)
  (base.maximalIdeal.map
    (algebraMap base.valuationSubring (integralClosure base.valuationSubring L)))
```

are, after this unfolding, the two conclusions above. A private compile
check, not part of this module, confirmed each target `Iff.rfl`-equal to its
source form written with the public projections.

The `let` binders for `A` and `B` only abbreviate. When the body is printed,
Lean displays some of them as `have`, because the rest of the body does not
depend on their definitional values. The proposition is the same either way.

## Actual scalar maps

No algebra structure is supplied or assumed beyond `[Algebra K L]`; every
other structure is Mathlib's canonical instance.

- `A` is a commutative local ring through `ValuationSubring`'s instances
  (`Mathlib/RingTheory/Valuation/ValuationSubring.lean`, `isLocalRing` at
  line 148; `Valuation.valuationSubring` at line 440).
- The algebra `A → L` is `Algebra.ofSubsemiring`
  (`Mathlib/Algebra/Algebra/Basic.lean`, line 101). Its map is
  `(algebraMap K L).comp` the inclusion of `A` into `K`, so `a ∈ A` maps to
  `algebraMap K L a`.
- `B = integralClosure A L` (`Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/Algebra/Basic.lean`,
  line 216) is a `Subalgebra A L`. Its ring structure is
  `Subalgebra.toCommRing`, and the algebra `A → B` is `Subalgebra.algebra`
  (`Mathlib/Algebra/Algebra/Subalgebra/Basic.lean`, line 336), whose map
  sends `a` to `algebraMap K L a`, viewed as an element of `B`.

So `algebraMap A B` is the composite `A → K → L` corestricted to `B`, as in
the source.

## Reuse

The current Library and the pinned Mathlib were searched by the original and
adapted names and for completeness or Henselianity of an integral closure
along a mapped ideal. Neither contains these statements. Results that a
later proof may use are Mathlib's `IsIntegralClosure.finite`,
`IsAdicComplete.map_algebraMap_iff` and the instance
`IsAdicComplete.henselianRing`, together with the published independent-universe
`Langlands.NYLL.ValuationTheory.DiscreteValuationField.isAdicComplete_of_moduleFinite`.
These are tools for later proofs, not proofs of these targets.

For provenance only: the original M245.003 proof obtains Noetherianity,
fraction-ring and integral-closedness instances for the base valuation ring,
derives `Module.Finite` from `IsIntegralClosure.finite`, applies NYLL's
finite-module completeness theorem for the base maximal ideal and transports
completeness to the mapped ideal. The original M245.004 proof infers
`HenselianRing` from M245.003. No original proof is included or claimed as an
implemented proof by this module.

## Scope

This unit contains only these two statements. It does not state or prove
M245.001/.002 (injectivity and faithfulness of the scalar action),
M245.005 onward, localness of `B`, the discrete valuation ring property of
`B`, existence or uniqueness of an extension valuation, local-field
packaging, residue actions, inertia or reciprocity. It adds no global
instance and no alias of the `CompleteDVF` projections.
