## Data, universes and scalar maps

The target quantifies, in order, over
`(K : Type u) (L : Type w) [Field K] [Field L] [Algebra K L]
[FiniteDimensional K L] (base : CompleteDVF.{u, v} K) [Algebra.IsSeparable K L]`
and concludes

```
∃ n : ℕ,
  (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal base.valuation.valuationSubring).map
      (algebraMap base.valuation.valuationSubring
        (integralClosure base.valuation.valuationSubring L)) =
    CanonicalIntegralClosure.maximalIdeal (K := K) (L := L) base ^ n
```

The proof introduces exactly these parameters. The `K`-algebra structure on
`L` and the complete discrete valuation `base` are arbitrary supplied data. The
theorem covers every finite separable extension, including the trivial
extension `L = K`. The value group stored in `base` lies in `Type v`. None of
the three universes is identified with another.

Write `A := base.valuation.valuationSubring`, the actual valuation subring of
`K` in `Type u`; `m := IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal A`, its maximal ideal for
Mathlib's valuation-subring local-ring instance; `B := integralClosure A L`,
the actual Mathlib subalgebra of `L` in `Type w`; and
`M := CanonicalIntegralClosure.maximalIdeal (K := K) (L := L) base`, the
published M245.007 ideal, which by definition is `IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal B`
for the local-ring witness given by the published M245.006 theorem. The scalar
map `algebraMap A B` is the canonical one: the inclusion `A → K` followed by
`algebraMap K L`, corestricted to the integral closure (`Subalgebra.algebra`).
`m.map (algebraMap A B)` is the ideal of `B` generated by the image of `m`.
The power `M ^ n` is Mathlib's ideal power (the `Submodule` power), and the
exponent `n = 0`, giving `M ^ 0 = ⊤`, is not excluded by the target.

## Implemented proof

1. Introduce `K`, `L`, the instances, `FiniteDimensional K L`, `base` and
   separability.
2. Localness. Install the accepted M245.006 theorem as a local instance:
   `let : IsLocalRing B :=
   integralClosure_isLocalRing_of_finite_separable.{u, v, w} K L base`.
   It is a `let` inside the proof, so no global instance is added.
3. The published M245.007 definition `M` is, by its definition,
   `IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal B` computed with this same M245.006 witness. The
   proof uses `change` to restate the goal as
   `∃ n : ℕ, m.map (algebraMap A B) = IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal B ^ n`; this
   unfolds the definition only to identify its local-ring witness and does not
   build a replacement ideal.
4. DVR. The published M245.011 theorem
   `integralClosure_isDiscreteValuationRing_of_finite_separable.{u, v, w} K L base`
   gives `IsDiscreteValuationRing B`, held as a local instance (`have`). Its
   `IsDomain B` argument is Mathlib's instance
   `instIsDomainSubtypeMemSubalgebraIntegralClosure`, found by instance
   search, as in the published M245.011 target.
5. Nonvanishing. The published M245.009 theorem
   `integralClosure_base_maximal_map_ne_bot_of_finite_separable.{u, v, w} K L base`
   gives `hI : m.map (algebraMap A B) ≠ ⊥`. Its target does not assume finite
   dimensionality, so it applies here directly.
6. A uniformizer. Mathlib's `IsDiscreteValuationRing.exists_irreducible B`
   (`Mathlib/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/Basic.lean`, line 105) gives an
   irreducible `ϖ : B`.
7. Classification of nonzero ideals. Mathlib's
   `IsDiscreteValuationRing.ideal_eq_span_pow_irreducible hI hϖ` (same file,
   line 364) gives `n : ℕ` with
   `m.map (algebraMap A B) = Ideal.span {ϖ ^ n}`.
8. Conclusion with this `n`. The proof rewrites the left side by step 7, then
   by `Ideal.span_singleton_pow` from right to left
   (`Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Operations.lean`, line 420:
   `span {s} ^ n = span {s ^ n}`, whose two-sidedness instance holds in the
   commutative ring `B`) to `Ideal.span {ϖ} ^ n`, and then by
   `Irreducible.maximalIdeal_eq` from right to left
   (`Mathlib/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/Basic.lean`, line 98:
   `maximalIdeal B = Ideal.span {ϖ}`) to `IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal B ^ n`.
   In that Mathlib lemma the maximal ideal is taken for the local-ring part of
   the DVR proof of step 4, while the goal uses the M245.006 witness of step 2.
   Both are proofs of the proposition `IsLocalRing B`, so the two ideals are
   definitionally equal by proof irrelevance, and `rw` closes the goal by
   `rfl`.

`fbd320cd8b1b06487c58227e92eb135794267709`, file

The proof keeps the source route: localness (M245.006) as a local instance,
`change` to `IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal B ^ n`, the DVR theorem (M245.011),

modifications are:

- The closed-target signature, the explicit independent universes `u, v, w`,
  and the source abbreviations `base.valuationSubring` and `base.maximalIdeal`
  expanded to the actual valuation subring `base.valuation.valuationSubring`

- The published theorems are applied to `K`, `L` and `base` explicitly, with
  explicit universes, because their statements are closed propositions.
- The source installs `IsDomain B` with `infer_instance` as a local instance;
  here instance search finds it directly, so that step is omitted.
- The source holds the DVR proof by `let`; here it is a `have`, which is
  equivalent for a proposition.
- The uniformizer is named `ϖ` instead of `pi`.
- The NYLL upstream imports are replaced by the published target, the
  published M245.011 and M245.009 proof modules and a focused Mathlib import.
