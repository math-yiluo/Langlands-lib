`Langlands.NYLL.ValuedFieldTheory.Valuation.DiscreteValuationField.FiniteIntegralClosure.IdealImageProofs`.

## Data, universes and scalar maps

Both targets quantify over `(K : Type u) (L : Type w) [Field K] [Field L]
[Algebra K L]`, a chosen complete discrete valuation
`(base : CompleteDVF.{u, v} K)` and `[Algebra.IsSeparable K L]`. They differ in
the finite-dimensional hypothesis:

- the M245.008 target also has `[FiniteDimensional K L]`, placed between
  `[Algebra K L]` and `base`, as in the source's ambient variables;
- the M245.009 target has no `FiniteDimensional K L` and no other finiteness
  hypothesis, as in the source's `omit [FiniteDimensional K L] in` clause. Its
  proof therefore proves the target for every separable extension, despite the
  historical `finite_separable` name.

Both proofs introduce exactly these parameters. The universes are not
identified with one another.

Write `A := base.valuation.valuationSubring`, the actual valuation subring of
`K` in `Type u`; `m := IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal A`, its maximal ideal for
Mathlib's valuation-subring local-ring instance; and
`B := integralClosure A L`, the actual Mathlib subalgebra of `L`, in `Type w`.
The scalar maps are the canonical ones. The algebra map `A → L` is the
inclusion `A → K` followed by `algebraMap K L`, and `algebraMap A B` is its
corestriction to the integral closure (`Subalgebra.algebra`). Both conclusions
concern `m.map (algebraMap A B)`, the ideal of `B` generated by the image of
`m`.

The separability hypothesis `[Algebra.IsSeparable K L]` is part of both
targets and is introduced by both proofs. Only M245.008 uses it, and only to
apply the M245.006 localness theorem and to state the M245.007 ideal. The
M245.009 proof does not use separability.

## Private auxiliary facts

Two small facts are private theorems of this module.

1. `algebraMap_valuationSubring_integralClosure_injective.{u, v, w} K L base`:
   the map `algebraMap A B` is injective. If two elements of `A` have equal
   images in `B`, coercing to `L` gives equal images under the composite
   `A → K → L`. The field map `algebraMap K L` is injective
   (`RingHom.injective`, since `K` is a field and `L` is nontrivial), so the
   underlying elements of `K` agree, and `Subtype.ext` gives equality in `A`.
   No finiteness or separability hypothesis is involved.
2. `valuationSubring_maximalIdeal_ne_bot base`: `m ≠ ⊥`. The structure
   `CompleteDVF` makes `base.valuation` complete and rank-one discrete
   (`Valuation.IsCompleteDiscrete`, whose `IsRankOneDiscrete` field is an
   instance). From this, Mathlib's instances in
   `RingTheory.Valuation.Discrete.Basic` give a cyclic value group and a
   nontrivial valuation, and the instance in `RingTheory.Valuation.Basic`
   turns the latter into a nontrivial value group. Then
   `Valuation.valuationSubring_not_isField base.valuation` shows that `A` is
   not a field, and `Ring.ne_bot_of_isMaximal_of_not_isField` applied to the
   maximal ideal `m` gives `m ≠ ⊥`.

## Implemented proof of M245.008

1. Introduce `K`, `L`, the instances, `FiniteDimensional K L`, `base` and
   separability.
2. Install the accepted M245.006 theorem as a local instance:
   `let : IsLocalRing B :=
   integralClosure_isLocalRing_of_finite_separable.{u, v, w} K L base`.
   It is a `let` inside the proof, so no global instance is added.
3. The published M245.007 definition
   `CanonicalIntegralClosure.maximalIdeal (K := K) (L := L) base` is, by its
   definition, `IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal B` computed with this same M245.006
   witness. The proof uses `change` to restate the goal as
   `m.map (algebraMap A B) ≤ IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal B`; this unfolds the
   definition only to identify its local-ring witness and does not build a
   replacement ideal.
4. `algebraMap A B` is a local homomorphism. Mathlib's instance
   `integralClosure.AlgebraIsIntegral` gives `Algebra.IsIntegral A B`, which
   `algebraMap_isIntegral_iff` turns into `(algebraMap A B).IsIntegral`. With
   the private injectivity fact, `RingHom.IsIntegral.isLocalHom` gives
   `IsLocalHom (algebraMap A B)`.
5. `IsLocalRing.map_maximalIdeal_le (algebraMap A B)` concludes:
   a local homomorphism maps the maximal ideal into the maximal ideal.

## Implemented proof of M245.009

1. Introduce `K`, `L`, the instances, `base` and separability; there is no
   finite-dimensional hypothesis. Assume `hbot : m.map (algebraMap A B) = ⊥`.
2. `Ideal.map_eq_bot_iff_of_injective`, applied to the private injectivity
   fact, turns `hbot` into `m = ⊥`.
3. This contradicts the private fact `m ≠ ⊥`.

The proof uses neither M245.006 localness nor M245.007, and no finiteness of
`L` over `K`.
