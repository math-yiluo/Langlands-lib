# Relative norm of a maximal ideal in a separable extension: statement target

`Langlands.NYLL.LocalClassFieldTheory.relNorm_eq_pow_inertiaDeg_of_isSeparableStatement`
concerns a finite, torsion-free extension of Dedekind domains `R → S` with
`Frac S / Frac R` separable, and maximal ideals `P` of `S` and `p` of `R` with
`P` lying over `p`. The proposition says that the relative ideal norm of `P` is
`p` raised to the inertia degree of `P` over `R`. In Lean:

```lean
attribute [local instance] FractionRing.liftAlgebra

def relNorm_eq_pow_inertiaDeg_of_isSeparableStatement
    (R : Type u) (S : Type v)
    [CommRing R] [IsDedekindDomain R]
    [CommRing S] [IsDedekindDomain S]
    [Algebra R S] [Module.Finite R S] [Module.IsTorsionFree R S]
    [Algebra.IsSeparable (FractionRing R) (FractionRing S)]
    (P : Ideal S) (p : Ideal R) [P.LiesOver p]
    [P.IsMaximal] [p.IsMaximal] : Prop :=
  Ideal.relNorm R P = p ^ P.inertiaDeg R
```

This is the statement of source unit M162.012. Defining the proposition does not
prove it.

## Parameters and meaning

The declaration has universe parameters `u` and `v`, as in the source
(`R : Type u`, `S : Type v`). The parameters are:

| Binder | Kind | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `R`, `S` | explicit, `Type u`, `Type v` | Base and extension rings |
| `[CommRing R]`, `[IsDedekindDomain R]`, `[CommRing S]`, `[IsDedekindDomain S]` | instance-implicit | Both rings are Dedekind domains |
| `[Algebra R S]` | instance-implicit | The extension `R → S` |
| `[Module.Finite R S]` | instance-implicit | `S` is a finitely generated `R`-module |
| `[Module.IsTorsionFree R S]` | instance-implicit | `S` is torsion-free over `R`; in particular `R → S` is injective |
| `[Algebra.IsSeparable (FractionRing R) (FractionRing S)]` | instance-implicit | `Frac S / Frac R` is separable |
| `P : Ideal S`, `p : Ideal R` | explicit | The two ideals |
| `[P.LiesOver p]` | instance-implicit | `P` lies over `p`, that is, `p = P ∩ R` (`Ideal.LiesOver`) |
| `[P.IsMaximal]`, `[p.IsMaximal]` | instance-implicit | Both ideals are maximal |

The algebra structure `Frac R → Frac S` in the separability hypothesis is not a
hypothesis. It is Mathlib's `FractionRing.liftAlgebra R (FractionRing S)`
(`Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/FractionRing.lean`, line 769), the unique
extension of `algebraMap R S` to fraction fields. As in the source, the file
registers `FractionRing.liftAlgebra` as a local instance. Mathlib's own
`Ideal.relNorm` file uses the same attribute
(`Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Norm/RelNorm.lean`, line 50).

The two sides of the equation are ideals of `R`:

- `Ideal.relNorm R : Ideal S →*₀ Ideal R`
  (`Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Norm/RelNorm.lean`, line 270) is the relative ideal
  norm. It sends `I` to the ideal of `R` generated by the integral norms
  `Algebra.intNorm R S x` of the elements `x ∈ I`.
- `P.inertiaDeg R` (`Ideal.inertiaDeg`,
  `Mathlib/RingTheory/RamificationInertia/Inertia.lean`, line 44) is the degree
  of the residue field of `P` over the residue field of its contraction to `R`.
  For maximal `P` lying over maximal `p` this is the residue degree
  `[S/P : R/p]`.

So the proposition asserts `N_{S/R}(P) = p ^ f(P | p)` for a maximal ideal `P`
of `S` over a maximal ideal `p` of `R`, in a finite torsion-free extension of
Dedekind domains whose fraction-field extension is separable. Mathlib's
`Ideal.relNorm_eq_pow_of_isMaximal` gives this when `Frac R` is perfect; the
source docstring calls the present statement the separable replacement.
