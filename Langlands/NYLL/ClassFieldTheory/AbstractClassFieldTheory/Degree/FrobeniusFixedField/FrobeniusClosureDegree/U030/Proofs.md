## Implemented proof

The proof follows the source. After `show` unfolds the target, it installs compactness of `G_K` as a local instance and names the Frobenius closure `Γ`, its generator `x`, the degree map `f`, the exponent `n` and the closed subgroup `R = n ℤ̂`. The source then uses M014.027 `frobeniusInClosure_topologicallyGenerates` (lines 525–553), whose proof is not in the Library; the module proves the same fact as the local `hgen0` by that theorem's source proof: `Γ` is the closed subgroup generated by the one-point family `fun _ => σ.1`, which this family topologically generates by M007.005. With `hfx : f x = (ofAdd 1)^n` from M014.029, the inclusion of the range of `f` in `R` holds because `R` is closed (M009.006) and contains `f x`, and every element of `Γ` lies in the closed subgroup generated by `x`, whose image lies in the closed subgroup generated by `f x` (M007.007). For the reverse inclusion, an element `y` of `R` has additive coordinate `zHatMulNat n z` for some `z`, so it is the image of `z` under `zHatPowNat n` (M014.006). As `z` lies in the closed subgroup generated by `ofAdd 1` (M008.024, restated as the local fact `hone` because its published type is a Statement proposition), M007.007 puts its image in the closed subgroup generated by `zHatPowNat n (ofAdd 1)`, which equals `(ofAdd 1)^n` by the local equation `hpow`. That closed subgroup lies in the range of `f`, which contains `f x = (ofAdd 1)^n` and is closed as the compact image of the compact group `Γ` in the Hausdorff group `ℤ̂`.
