## Implemented proof

The imported order on finite abelian subextensions reverses inclusion of their
representing closed subgroups. The imported compositum has representing subgroup
`L₁.field ⊓ L₂.field`. Thus `change` unfolds the target, order and compositum
to the subgroup inclusion
`(L₁.field.toSubgroup ⊓ L₂.field.toSubgroup) ≤ L₁.field.toSubgroup`.
Mathlib's `inf_le_left` proves this inclusion. Under the reversed order, this
says that the left subextension is contained in the compositum.
