# NYLL M296.019: the right subextension lies below its compositum

Worker 662, 14 November 2026.

The theorem
`Langlands.NYLL.ClassFormation.FiniteAbelianSubextension.le_compositum_right`
proves the published
`Langlands.NYLL.ClassFormation.FiniteAbelianSubextension.le_compositum_rightStatement`
with the complete source telescope:

```lean
{G : Type u} [Group G] [TopologicalSpace G] {K : ClosedSubgroup G}
(L₁ L₂ : FiniteAbelianSubextension K)
```

Its type is the target applied to `L₁ L₂`, namely `L₂ ≤ L₁.compositum L₂`.
There is no additional assumption on the topology or group. The target and all
its dependencies are imported from the published `OrderStatements` module.

## Implemented proof

Finite abelian subextensions are ordered by reverse inclusion of their closed
subgroups. The closed subgroup of the compositum is the intersection of the
two input subgroups. The Lean `change` command unfolds these definitions and
the target to

```lean
(L₁.field.toSubgroup ⊓ L₂.field.toSubgroup) ≤ L₂.field.toSubgroup
```

The existing lattice theorem `inf_le_right` proves this inclusion. Thus the
right factor is below the compositum in the field-inclusion order.
