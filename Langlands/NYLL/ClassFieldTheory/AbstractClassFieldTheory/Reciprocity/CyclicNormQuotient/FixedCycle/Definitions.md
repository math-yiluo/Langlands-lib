## Meaning and construction

For a finite quotient `K/L` generated by a specified element `g`, this is the
actual additive equivalence from the ambient fixed additive subgroup `A_K` to
the cycle module of `Rep.FiniteCyclicGroup.normHomCompSub M g`, where
`M = extensionFixedRepresentation A K L hLK hnormal`. The cycle module is the
kernel of `ρ(g) - 1` on `A_L`.

The forward map includes a `K`-fixed coefficient into `A_L`, uses the inverse
of the published extension-fixed representation equivalence, and supplies the
cycle certificate using quotient induction. The inverse map uses the generator
hypothesis to pass from `g`-fixedness to fixedness by every quotient element,
then the published M073.004 action comparison and M024.002 representative-action
comparison to obtain ambient `K`-fixedness. The original subtype inverse and
additivity proofs are preserved. These are proof terms of the data construction;
no theorem target of this task is proved here.

The complete telescope retains implicit `G : IntegralRepGroupType` with group
and topology instances, explicit `A : Rep ℤ G`, `K L`, `hLK`, explicit `hnormal`
and `hfinite`, `g`, and `hg`, in source order. The acting group and coefficient
carrier are universe zero, as in the source. The result type retains the local
normality, finiteness, Fintype, cyclicity and commutative-group instances and the
local definitions `M` and `T`. No stronger hypothesis or global instance is added.
