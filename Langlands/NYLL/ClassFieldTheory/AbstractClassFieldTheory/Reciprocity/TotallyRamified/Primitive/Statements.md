## The statement

The target has the source telescope, in the source order:

```lean
{G : IntegralRepGroupType} [Group G] [TopologicalSpace G] {A : Rep ℤ G}
(hcf : SatisfiesClassFieldAxiom A)
(E : FiniteAbstractFieldExtension G)
(hnormal : (extensionSubgroup E.base.field E.field.field E.below).Normal)
(g : E.base.field.toSubgroup ⧸
  extensionSubgroup E.base.field E.field.field E.below)
(hg : ∀ q, q ∈ Subgroup.zpowers g)
(u w : ambientFixedAddSubgroup A E.field.field)
(hnorm : relativeNorm A E.base.field E.field.field E.below w =
  relativeNorm A E.base.field E.field.field E.below u)
```

The section variable `{D : DegreeData G}` occurs neither in the source
statement nor in the target. So, as for the source theorem, it does not enter
the telescope. A focused `#check` confirms the telescope above.

The proposition is the source conclusion, verbatim:

```lean
let M := extensionFixedRepresentation A E.base.field E.field.field
  E.below hnormal
∃ a : M.V,
  M.ρ g a - a =
    (extensionFixedRepresentationEquiv A E.base.field E.field.field
      E.below hnormal).symm (w - u)
```

Here `E` is a finite extension `L / K` of fields that are finite over the
distinguished base, with `K = E.base` and `L = E.field`. The hypothesis
`hnormal` says `G_L` is normal in `G_K`, so `G(L/K) = G_K / G_L` is a group,
and `hg` says it is cyclic, generated by `g`. The elements `u` and `w` lie in
the fixed module `A_L`, and `hnorm` says `N_{L/K} w = N_{L/K} u`. In words:
under the class-field axiom there is an `a` in the descended representation
`M` of `G(L/K)` on `A_L` with `g · a - a` equal to `w - u`, carried into the
carrier of `M` by the published equivalence between that carrier and `A_L`.

The quotient's group structure, used by `Subgroup.zpowers g` and `M.ρ g`,
comes from the class-valued binder `hnormal`, as in the source. The finiteness
instance that `relativeNorm` needs is the published instance
`Langlands.NYLL.PortInternal.M010.d139 E` (M010.139), the source's anonymous
instance on `FiniteAbstractFieldExtension`. The conclusion does not mention
`hcf`, `hg` or `hnorm`. They are genuine hypotheses of the target, as in the
source, and the source proof uses all three.
