```lean
(D.frobeniusFixedField K L hLK σ).toSubgroup ≤ L.toSubgroup
```

Mathematically: let `G_K = K.field` and `G_L = L` with `G_L ≤ G_K` normal of
finite index, let `I_L = G_L ∩ ker d` be the relative inertia subgroup
`D.extensionInertiaWithin K.field L hLK` of `G_K`, and let `σ` be a Frobenius
element of `G_K / I_L`. Let `Γ = closure ⟨σ⟩ ≤ G_K / I_L` be the closed
subgroup generated by `σ` (`D.frobeniusClosure K L hLK σ`) and `G_Σ ≤ G` the
image in `G` of its inverse image in `G_K`
(`D.frobeniusFixedField K L hLK σ`). If `σ` maps to `1` in `G_K / G_L`
(`D.frobeniusRestriction`), then `G_Σ ≤ G_L`.

## Implemented proof

The module has one private lemma and the target theorem.

**Private lemma `extensionImageInInertiaQuotient_isClosed_aux`.** Under the
binders of the target up to `hLfinite`, the image
`J = D.extensionImageInInertiaQuotient K.field L hLK` of `G_L ∩ G_K` in
`G_K / I_L` is closed. This is the source theorem M017.002
`extensionImageInInertiaQuotient_isClosed`, whose proof was not published at
the workspace base; it is proved here by its source proof under a private
name, so no second copy of M017.002 is published. The proof: the subgroup
`E = L.toSubgroup.subgroupOf K.field.toSubgroup` is closed in `G_K`, being
the preimage of the closed set `L` under the continuous inclusion
(`L.isClosed'.preimage continuous_subtype_val`, after a `rfl` rewriting of
its carrier); it has finite index by `Subgroup.finiteIndex_of_finite_quotient`
applied to `hLfinite`, so it is open by
`Subgroup.isOpen_of_isClosed_of_finiteIndex`. Its image `J` under the
quotient map is open because the quotient map is open
(`QuotientGroup.isOpenMap_coe`), and an open subgroup of the topological
group `G_K / I_L` is closed (`Subgroup.isClosed_of_isOpen`). The source proof
begins with the local instance `D.extensionInertiaWithin_isClosed K L hLK`
(M014.022, then unpublished); no later step uses it, so it is omitted.

**Target theorem.** After `show` of the unfolded conclusion, with
`H = I_L`, `E` as above, `Q = G_K / H` and `J` as above:

1. `σ.1 ∈ J`. Let `k = Quotient.out σ.1`, so `QuotientGroup.mk k = σ.1`
   (`Quotient.out_eq'`). The class of `k` in `G_K / E` is
   `D.extensionRestriction K.field L hLK σ.1`, by `change` (the restriction is
   `QuotientGroup.map` along the identity), which is `1` by `hσ`. Hence
   `k ∈ E` by `QuotientGroup.eq` and `simpa`, and `⟨k, hkE, hkq⟩` witnesses
   `σ.1 ∈ J`.
2. `Γ ≤ J`. By definition `Γ` is the topological closure of
   `Subgroup.closure (Set.range fun _ : Unit => σ.1)` (a `change`). By
   `Subgroup.topologicalClosure_minimal` it suffices that this closure lies in
   `J`, which follows from step 1 and `Subgroup.closure_le`, and that `J` is
   closed, which is the private lemma.
3. Let `g ∈ G_Σ`, so `g = k` for some `k ∈ G_K` whose class lies in `Γ`,
   hence in `J` by step 2: there is `e ∈ E` with `mk e = mk k`. Then
   `e⁻¹ * k ∈ H` by `QuotientGroup.eq`, so `e⁻¹ * k ∈ E` (the first component
   of the infimum `H = E ⊓ fieldInertiaWithin`), hence `k = e * (e⁻¹ * k) ∈ E`
   (`E.mul_mem` and `simpa [mul_assoc]`), that is `(k : G) ∈ L`.

The dependencies are the published definitions `frobeniusFixedField`,
`frobeniusFixedSubgroupWithin`, `frobeniusClosure`, `FrobeniusElements`,
`frobeniusRestriction`, `extensionRestriction`, `extensionInertiaWithin` and
`extensionImageInInertiaQuotient`, and the Mathlib lemmas named above. No
other auxiliary declaration, instance or simp attribute is added.
