## Target and mathematical meaning

The theorem has the statement telescope, in the source order: the section
variables `{G : Type*} [Group G] [TopologicalSpace G]`, then explicit
`(D : DegreeData G)`, the instance `[IsTopologicalGroup G]`, explicit
`(K : FiniteResidueAbstractField D) (L : ClosedSubgroup G)
(hLK : L.toSubgroup ≤ K.field.toSubgroup)`, the instance binder

explicit `(σ : D.FrobeniusElements K L hLK)`. Its type is

which unfolds to

```lean
(D.frobeniusFixedSubgroupWithin K L hLK σ).map
    (D.normalizedDegree K).toMonoidHom =
  (D.frobeniusClosureDegree K L hLK σ).toMonoidHom.range
```

Mathematically: let `I_L` be the relative inertia subgroup
`D.extensionInertiaWithin K.field L hLK` of `G_K`, let
`Γ = closure ⟨σ⟩ ≤ G_K / I_L` be the closed cyclic subgroup generated by the
class `σ.1` (`D.frobeniusClosure K L hLK σ`), and let
`G_Σ ≤ G_K` be its inverse image under `G_K → G_K / I_L`
(`D.frobeniusFixedSubgroupWithin K L hLK σ`). Then the image of `G_Σ` under
the normalized degree `d_K : G_K → Ẑ` (written multiplicatively as
`ZHatMul`) equals the image of `Γ` under the factorized normalized degree
`G_K / I_L → Ẑ` restricted to `Γ` (`D.frobeniusClosureDegree K L hLK σ`).

## Implemented proof

The proof is the source tactic proof with one replacement. After `ext z`,
both inclusions are proved.

- If `z = d_K(k)` with `k ∈ G_Σ`, then by definition of the inverse image the
  class `QuotientGroup.mk k` lies in `Γ`, and `⟨QuotientGroup.mk k, hk⟩` is a
  preimage of `z` under `frobeniusClosureDegree`. The equation holds by
  `rfl`: `frobeniusClosureDegree` is the continuous factorized degree
  `extensionNormalizedDegreeContinuous` composed with the subgroup inclusion
  `PortInternal.M014.d003`, and the factorized degree
  `extensionNormalizedDegree` is `QuotientGroup.lift` of `d_K`, which
  evaluates definitionally on a class `QuotientGroup.mk k` to `d_K(k)`.
- If `z = frobeniusClosureDegree a` with `a ∈ Γ`, choose `k` with
  `QuotientGroup.mk' I_L k = a.1` by `QuotientGroup.mk'_surjective`. Then
  `k ∈ G_Σ` (its class is `a.1 ∈ Γ`, transported along that equation), and
  `d_K(k) = extensionNormalizedDegree (QuotientGroup.mk' I_L k) =
  extensionNormalizedDegree a.1` by a two-step `calc`.

The first `calc` step is the source theorem M012.006
`extensionNormalizedDegree_mk` read backwards. Its proof was not yet
published at the workspace base, so the source application
`(D.extensionNormalizedDegree_mk K L hLK k).symm` is replaced by `rfl`, which
is the source proof of M012.006 (source
`Nyll/ClassFieldTheory/AbstractClassFieldTheory/Degree/FrobeniusLift.lean`,
lines 97–104); no copy of that theorem is introduced. The second step is
`congrArg` along the choice equation.

The dependencies are the published definitions `frobeniusFixedSubgroupWithin`
(M016.001), `frobeniusClosure`, `frobeniusClosureDegree`,
`extensionNormalizedDegreeContinuous`, `extensionNormalizedDegree`,
`extensionInertiaWithin` and `normalizedDegree`, all imported through the
statement module, and Mathlib's `QuotientGroup.mk'_surjective`. No auxiliary
declaration, instance or simp attribute is added.
