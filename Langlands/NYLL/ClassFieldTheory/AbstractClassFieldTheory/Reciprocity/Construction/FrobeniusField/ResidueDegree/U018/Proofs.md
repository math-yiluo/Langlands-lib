## Target and mathematical meaning

The theorem has the statement telescope, in the source order: the section
variables `{G : Type*} [Group G] [TopologicalSpace G]`, then explicit
`(D : DegreeData G)`, the instances
`[IsTopologicalGroup G] [CompactSpace G] [T2Space G]`, explicit
`(K : FiniteResidueAbstractField D) (L : ClosedSubgroup G)
(hLK : L.toSubgroup ≤ K.field.toSubgroup)`, the instance binder
`[hLnormal : (L.toSubgroup.subgroupOf K.field.toSubgroup).Normal]` and
explicit `(σ : D.FrobeniusElements K L hLK)`. Its type is
`D.frobeniusFixedField_relativeResidueQuotientFiniteStatement K L hLK σ`,
which unfolds to

```lean
Finite
  (↥(K.field.toSubgroup.map D.degree.toMonoidHom) ⧸
    ((D.frobeniusFixedField K L hLK σ).toSubgroup.map
        D.degree.toMonoidHom).subgroupOf
      (K.field.toSubgroup.map D.degree.toMonoidHom))
```

Mathematically: let `d : G → Ẑ` be the degree of `D` (written
multiplicatively as `ZHatMul`), `G_K` the closed subgroup of the base field
`K`, and `G_Σ` the closed subgroup of the field `Σ` fixed by the Frobenius
lift `σ` (`D.frobeniusFixedField K L hLK σ`: the inverse image in `G_K` of
the closed cyclic subgroup `Γ = closure ⟨σ⟩` of `G_K / I_L`, where `I_L` is
the relative inertia subgroup). Then `d(G_Σ)` has finite index in `d(G_K)`:
the quotient of `d(G_K)` by `d(G_Σ)`, viewed as a subgroup of `d(G_K)`, is
finite. The proof shows that this index is the Frobenius exponent
`n = d_K(σ)`, which is positive.

## Implemented proof

The main theorem is the source tactic proof: by
`Subgroup.index_ne_zero_iff_finite` it suffices that the index of the
`subgroupOf` is nonzero; `change` rewrites it as the relative index
`relIndex` of `d(G_Σ)` in `d(G_K)`; this relative index is rewritten to
`n` by the private lemma `frobeniusFixedField_mappedRelIndex_aux`, and
`n ≠ 0` follows from `frobeniusExponent_pos_aux`.

The source theorems used along the way had no published proofs at the
workspace base `72480b70c88adff84a438bd7029d64ee3977469c`, so each is proved
here as a private theorem with the source name and an `_aux` suffix (to
avoid clashing with the published names when those proofs appear), with its
source statement (namespace `Langlands.NYLL.ClassFormation.DegreeData`,
`extensionSubgroup K.field L hLK` expanded to
`L.toSubgroup.subgroupOf K.field.toSubgroup`) and its source proof, with the
changes listed.

1. `frobeniusExponent_pos_aux` (source M014.013 `frobeniusExponent_pos`):
   `0 < D.frobeniusExponent K L hLK σ`, proved by the source term
   `(Exists.choose_spec σ.2).1`, since `frobeniusExponent` is the chosen
   witness `Exists.choose σ.2` of the Frobenius-element condition.
2. `frobeniusClosureDegree_range_aux` (source M014.030
   `frobeniusClosureDegree_range`): the range of
   `D.frobeniusClosureDegree K L hLK σ : Γ →ₜ* ZHatMul` equals
   `AddSubgroup.toSubgroup (zHatMulNat n).toAddMonoidHom.range`, that is
   `n Ẑ`. The proof follows the adaptation of the source proof by Worker 5264
   in the shared draft of Task 7047 (revision 1, module
   `Langlands.NYLL.ClassFieldTheory.AbstractClassFieldTheory.Degree.FrobeniusFixedField.FrobeniusClosureDegree.U030.Proofs`).
   - `Γ` is compact, and is topologically generated by the image `x` of `σ`.
     This generation fact is source M014.027
     `frobeniusInClosure_topologicallyGenerates`; it is proved inline as the
     local fact `hgen0` by its source proof from the published
     `closedSubgroupGeneratedMap_topologicallyGenerates`.
   - The degree of `x` is `(ofAdd 1)^n` (local fact `hfx`). The source applies
     M014.029 `frobeniusClosureDegree_generator`; `hfx` is its source proof
     (`change` to the factorized degree of `x.1`, `rfl` for `x.1 = σ.1`), whose
     last step, source M014.014 `extensionNormalizedDegree_frobenius_eq_pow`,
     is its source proof `(Exists.choose_spec σ.2).2`.
   - Range `⊆ n Ẑ`: `n Ẑ` is closed (published `isClosed_zHatMulNat_range`)
     and contains `f x`, so it contains the closed subgroup generated by
     `f x`, which contains `f(Γ)` by the published
     `map_mem_closedSubgroupGenerated_singleton`, since `x` generates `Γ`.
   - `n Ẑ ⊆` range: `ofAdd 1` topologically generates `Ẑ` (published
     `zHatOne_topologicallyGenerates`, restated as the local fact `hone`
     because its type is a Statement proposition), so multiplication by `n`
     maps every `z` into the closed subgroup generated by `(ofAdd 1)^n`. That
     subgroup lies in the range of `f`, which is closed as the compact image
     of `Γ` in the Hausdorff `Ẑ`. The source step `simpa using hmap`, which
     uses the source simp lemma `zHatPowNat_one`, is replaced by `rw` with
     the local equation `hpow`, proved by the source proof of
     `zHatPowNat_one`. The source's unused local fact `hn : 0 < n` is
     omitted.
3. `frobeniusFixedSubgroupWithin_normalizedDegree_image_aux` (source M016.015
   `frobeniusFixedSubgroupWithin_normalizedDegree_image`): the image of the
   subgroup `D.frobeniusFixedSubgroupWithin K L hLK σ ≤ G_K` under the
   normalized degree `d_K` equals the range of `frobeniusClosureDegree`. Its
   proof is the source proof, in which the application of M012.006
   `extensionNormalizedDegree_mk` is replaced by `rfl`, the source proof of
   M012.006. It is the same proof as Worker 754's accepted, not yet published
   candidate `26da87d5-8eb5-4af1-833a-1a2595c583b8` for M016.015; it is
   repeated here as a private lemma so that this module depends only on the
   published Library.
4. `frobeniusFixedField_mappedRelIndex_aux` (source-private M016.016
   `frobeniusFixedField_mappedRelIndex`): the relative index of `d(G_Σ)` in
   `d(G_K)` is `n`. Proof: by the published
   `residueDegree_nsmul_normalizedDegree`, the restricted degree on `G_K` is
   `f_K · d_K`, i.e. the normalized degree followed by the injective
   (published `zHatMulNat_injective`) map `scale` of multiplication by the
   residue degree `f_K`. Both images are rewritten as images of subgroups of
   `G_K` (`hSigmaImage`, `hKimage`), the injective `scale` is cancelled by
   `Subgroup.relIndex_map_map_of_injective`, the image of `G_Σ` under `d_K` is
   replaced by the range of `frobeniusClosureDegree` (lemma 3), the image of
   `⊤` is `⊤` by the published `normalizedDegree_surjective`, and
   `Subgroup.relIndex_top_right`, lemma 2 and `AddSubgroup.index_toSubgroup`
   reduce the goal to the index of `n Ẑ`, which is `n` by the published
   `zHatMulNat_range_index` with `0 < n` from lemma 1. Changes from the
   source: the step `S.map dK = range` is the term of lemma 3 in place of
   `simpa [S, dK] using` M016.015, and `zHatMulNat_range_index`, whose type
   is a Statement proposition, is restated as the local fact `hindex` before
   the final `rw`.

The two M014 lemmas use the section variable `{G : Type*}` of this file in
place of the source's `universe u` and `{G : Type u}`.
