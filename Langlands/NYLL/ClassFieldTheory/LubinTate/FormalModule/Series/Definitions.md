## Parameters and meaning

The declaration retains independent universes `u v`, an implicit
`K : Type u`, the instance `[Field K]`, an explicit
`F : Langlands.NYLL.LocalFieldTheory.DiscreteValuationField.LocalField.{u,v} K`,
and an explicit `π : F.valuationSubring`. The chosen `LocalField` is the
shared extension of the published `CompleteDVF` by residue-field finiteness.
Its valuation subring, residue field and residue map are the existing
accessors of that same package.

The source name `LubinTate.LubinTateSeries` becomes
`Langlands.NYLL.LubinTate.LubinTateSeries`. Its four source fields, with
the same names and types, are:

| Field | Mathematical content |
| --- | --- |
| `toPowerSeries` | A one-variable formal power series over `F.valuationSubring`. |
| `constantCoeff_eq_zero` | Its constant coefficient is zero. |
| `coeff_one_eq_uniformizer` | Its coefficient of degree one is `π`. |
| `map_residue_eq_frobenius` | Applying `PowerSeries.map F.residueMap` gives `PowerSeries.X ^ Nat.card F.residueField`. |

Although the second law's source name says `uniformizer`, there is no
uniformizer hypothesis on `π`. The exponent is exactly the natural
cardinality of the chosen residue field. The simp attributes are retained
on all three laws. The usual constructor, projections and recursors are
generated by the structure command; no coercion or new global instance
is introduced.
