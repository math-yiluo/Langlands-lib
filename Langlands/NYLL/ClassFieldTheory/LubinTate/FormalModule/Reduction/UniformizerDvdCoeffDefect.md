## Target and mathematical meaning

The theorem lives in namespace `Langlands.NYLL.LubinTate.SameUniformizer`
and has the target's exact telescope: implicit `K : Type u` with
`[Field K]`, implicit `F : LocalField.{u, v} K`, implicit
`π : F.valuationSubring`, implicit `σ : Type w` with `[Fintype σ]`, the
explicit hypothesis `hπ : F.toCompleteDVF.valuation.IsUniformizer (π : K)`,
the explicit `e ebar : LubinTateSeries F π`, implicit
`H : MvPowerSeries σ F.valuationSubring` and `L : σ → F.valuationSubring`,
the explicit `hH : HasLinearTerm H L` and the explicit exponent
`d : σ →₀ ℕ`. Its type is
`uniformizer_dvd_coeff_defectStatement hπ e ebar hH d`, which unfolds to

```lean
π ∣ MvPowerSeries.coeff d (defect e ebar H)
```

with divisibility in the valuation subring. Mathematically: by M349.005 the
defect `e(H) - H(ebar(X_1), …)` reduces to zero, so each of its coefficients
lies in the kernel of the residue map, i.e. in the maximal ideal; when `π` is
a uniformizer, this ideal is generated by `π` (M337.002). The uniformizer
hypothesis `hπ` is used only in this last step.

## Implemented proof

### Private M349.005 copy

The private theorem `map_defect_eq_zero_for_dvd e ebar hH` has the same
binders as M349.005 and states its unfolded conclusion
`MvPowerSeries.map F.residueMap (defect e ebar H) = 0` directly. Its proof is
the source proof of M349.005 (lines 116–119):
`rw [defect, map_sub, map_subst_lubinTateSeries e hH,
map_subst_inVariables ebar H, mvPowerSeries_expand_natCard]` followed by
`exact sub_self _`. With `q = Nat.card F.residueField`, the rewrites unfold
the published `defect` into `e(H) - H(ebar(X_1), …)`, distribute the
reduction over the difference, reduce the left term to `Hbar ^ q` (M349.003),
reduce the right term to the expansion `Hbar(X_1 ^ q, …)` (M349.004, with
`[Finite σ]` inferred from `[Fintype σ]`), and identify that expansion with
`Hbar ^ q` over the finite residue field (M349.001); `sub_self` closes
`Hbar ^ q - Hbar ^ q = 0`. The three rewritten theorems have reducible
`abbrev` targets, so `rw` sees their equations. The copy is private, so
this module does not publish a second theorem named `map_defect_eq_zero`.

### Main theorem

1. `show _ ∣ _` unfolds the reducible target to the displayed divisibility.
2. `apply uniformizer_dvd_of_residueMap_eq_zero hπ` uses the published
   M337.002 theorem, whose `def` target
   `uniformizer_dvd_of_residueMap_eq_zeroStatement hπ ha` unfolds to `π ∣ a`;
   the goal becomes
   `F.residueMap (MvPowerSeries.coeff d (defect e ebar H)) = 0`.
3. `hcoeff` applies `congrArg (MvPowerSeries.coeff d)` to
   `map_defect_eq_zero_for_dvd e ebar hH`, giving
   `coeff d (map F.residueMap (defect e ebar H)) = coeff d 0`.
4. `simpa using hcoeff` closes the goal. In the development environment
   `simpa?` reports the rewrite set `MvPowerSeries.coeff_map`,
   `MvPowerSeries.coeff_zero`, `IsLocalRing.residue_eq_zero_iff`,
   `IsLocalRing.mem_maximalIdeal` and `mem_nonunits_iff`: the coefficient of
   the reduced series is the residue of the coefficient, the coefficient of
   `0` is `0`, and both the hypothesis and the goal are normalized to the same
   statement that the coefficient is not a unit.

Dependencies: the published definition `defect`, the published theorems
`mvPowerSeries_expand_natCard`, `map_subst_lubinTateSeries`,
`map_subst_inVariables` and `uniformizer_dvd_of_residueMap_eq_zero`, Mathlib's
`map_sub` and `sub_self`, and the Mathlib simp lemmas listed above. The only
auxiliary declaration is the private `map_defect_eq_zero_for_dvd`; no
instance or simp attribute is added.
