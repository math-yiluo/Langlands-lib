no proof of either proposition is included.

The first proposition says that an element `z` of a finite separable extension
`L / K'`, integral over a discrete valuation ring `R` with fraction field `K'`,
belongs to `Algebra.adjoin R {B.gen}` when the integral power-basis generator's
minimal polynomial is Eisenstein at the ideal generated by the given
irreducible `pi : R`.

Its complete telescope is the source's: implicit independently universe
polymorphic types `R K' L : Type*`; `CommRing R`, `IsDomain R`,
`IsDiscreteValuationRing R`, `Field K'`, `Field L`; algebras `R → K'`,
`K' → L`, `R → L`; their `IsScalarTower`; `IsFractionRing R K'`;
`FiniteDimensional K' L` and `Algebra.IsSeparable K' L`. The explicit arguments
are `B`, `pi`, `hpi : Irreducible pi`, `hBint : IsIntegral R B.gen` and the
Eisenstein proof `hei`; implicit `{z : L}` and explicit
`hzint : IsIntegral R z` follow. The Prop body is exactly
`z ∈ Algebra.adjoin R ({B.gen} : Set L)`.

The second proposition adds a separate type `A : Type*`, `CommRing A`,
algebras `R → A` and `A → L`, the tower `R → A → L` and
`IsIntegralClosure A R L`. All source instances appear in their original
order. Its explicit arguments are `B`, `pi`, `hpi`, `a : A`,
`ha : algebraMap A L a = B.gen`, the injectivity premise `hmap_injective`,
`hBint` and `hei`. Its body is exactly
`Algebra.adjoin R ({a} : Set A) = ⊤`: the lifted primitive element generates
the actual integral closure as an `R`-algebra. It retains every premise of
the source statement, including the explicit injectivity premise.
