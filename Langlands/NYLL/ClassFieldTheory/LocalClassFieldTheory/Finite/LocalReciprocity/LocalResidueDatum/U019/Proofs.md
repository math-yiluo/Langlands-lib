For every nonarchimedean local field `K : Type` (with its `Field`,
`ValuativeRel`, `TopologicalSpace` and `IsNonarchimedeanLocalField`

```lean

  (Ω ≃ₐ[K] Ω) →* (λ ≃ₐ[κ] λ)
```

is continuous when both groups carry Mathlib's Krull topologies. The statement

## The implemented proof

Write `ρ` for the homomorphism above. After introducing `K` and its instances
from the closed target, the proof switches to classical logic and sets up the
following local facts.

1. **The base residue field is finite (inlined M249.014).** The residue field
   `(localCompleteDVF K).residueField` of the canonical complete discrete
   valuation of `K` is, by `change`, Mathlib's residue field `𝓀[K]` of the
   valuation integers. It is finite by Mathlib's instance for a
   nonarchimedean local field. `Finite.of_equiv` along the published ring
   equivalence `localBaseResidueEquivDecompositionResidue K` (M249.012)
   gives `Finite κ`. This is a local `have`, so it is an instance only inside
   this proof.
2. **Chosen lifts (inlined M249.017 and M249.018).** A local function
   `lift : λ → A` sends `x` to `Classical.choose` of
   `IsLocalRing.residue_surjective x`, and the local fact `lift_residue`
   (`IsLocalRing.residue A (lift x) = x`) is the matching
   `Classical.choose_spec`.
3. **Reduction to the identity.** `continuous_of_continuousAt_one` reduces
   continuity of the homomorphism `ρ` to continuity at `1`. After rewriting
   `ρ 1 = 1` and unfolding `Filter.Tendsto`, it remains to show that for every
   neighbourhood `s` of `1` in `λ ≃ₐ[κ] λ`, the preimage `ρ ⁻¹' s` is a
   neighbourhood of `1` in `Ω ≃ₐ[K] Ω`.
4. **A finite residue subextension.** By `krullTopology_mem_nhds_one_iff`
   for `κ` and `λ`, there is an intermediate field `E` of `λ / κ`, finite
   dimensional over `κ`, whose fixing subgroup lies in `s`. Since `κ` is
   finite (step 1), `Module.finite_of_finite` shows `E` is finite, and
   `Fintype.ofFinite` enumerates it.
5. **A finite extension of `K` generated by lifts.** Let `lifts` be the
   finite set of the images in `Ω` of `lift x` for `x ∈ E`, and let
   `F = K⟮lifts⟯` (`IntermediateField.adjoin`). Every element of `Ω` is
   integral over `K` (`Algebra.IsIntegral.isIntegral`), so
   `IntermediateField.finiteDimensional_adjoin` shows `F` is finite
   dimensional over `K`.
6. **The preimage is a neighbourhood.** By `krullTopology_mem_nhds_one_iff`
   for `K` and `Ω`, it suffices that every `σ` fixing `F` lies in
   `ρ ⁻¹' s`; since the fixing subgroup of `E` lies in `s`, it suffices that
   `ρ σ` fixes each `x ∈ E` (`IntermediateField.mem_fixingSubgroup_iff`).
7. **The action on one element.** With `y = ⟨x, hx⟩ : E` and
   `a = lift y : A`, the element `a` lies in `lifts` (`Finset.mem_image`),
   hence in `F` (`IntermediateField.subset_adjoin`), so `σ a = a`. The goal
   `ρ σ y = y` is rewritten backwards with `lift_residue y` to
   `ρ σ (residue a) = residue a`. By the definitions of
   `residueAlgActionOfEqTop` (M244.003) and the published residue action
   (a composite with Mathlib's `Ideal.Quotient.stabilizerHom`), the left side
   is definitionally `IsLocalRing.residue A (toDecompositionGroupOfEqTop K A hA σ • a)`;
   a `change` states this. `congr 1` and `Subtype.ext` reduce the equation to
   `σ a = a` in `Ω`, which is the fixing fact above.
