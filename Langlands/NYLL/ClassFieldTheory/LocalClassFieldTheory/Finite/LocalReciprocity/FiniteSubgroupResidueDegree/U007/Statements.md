# The image of a closed subgroup under the local residue action: target

This module states NYLL source unit M250.007 as a closed proposition. It
exports one Prop-valued `def` in namespace
`Langlands.NYLL.LocalClassFieldTheory`:

- `localAbstractFixedResidueAction_map_eq_fixingSubgroupStatement`
  (M250.007), for the source theorem
  `localAbstractFixedResidueAction_map_eq_fixingSubgroup`.

Nothing is proved here. A later proof unit will prove it under the adapted
source name
`Langlands.NYLL.LocalClassFieldTheory.localAbstractFixedResidueAction_map_eq_fixingSubgroup`.

Mathematically, let `K` be a nonarchimedean local field, let `K^sep` be its
separable closure, and let `B` be the valuation ring
`localSeparableValuationSubring K` of `K^sep` chosen in M249.006. Let `λ` be
the residue field of `B` and let `κ_K` be the residue field attached to `B`
over `K` (`decompositionResidueField K B`), so that `λ` is a field
extension of `κ_K`. Every automorphism of `K^sep` over `K` maps `B` onto
itself, so the absolute Galois group acts on `λ` by automorphisms over
`κ_K`. This is the local residue action `ρ_K` of M249.020.

Let `H` be a closed subgroup of `Gal(K^sep/K)`, let `E = (K^sep)^H` be its
fixed field and let `κ_E` be the residue field attached to `B` over `E`
(`decompositionResidueField E B`), which also maps to `λ`. Let
`F ⊆ λ` be the intermediate field over `κ_K` generated by the image of
`κ_E` (M250.001). The statement says

`ρ_K(H) = Gal(λ/F)`,

that is, the image of `H` under `ρ_K` is exactly the subgroup of
automorphisms of `λ` over `κ_K` that fix `F` pointwise. The source docstring
says the same thing: the image of `H` under the local residue action is
exactly the subgroup fixing the residue subfield of its fixed field.

## The proposition

```lean
def localAbstractFixedResidueAction_map_eq_fixingSubgroupStatement : Prop :=
  ∀ (K : Type) [Field K] [ValuativeRel K] [TopologicalSpace K]
    [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField K]
    (H : ClosedSubgroup (Gal(SeparableClosure K/K))),
    H.toSubgroup.map (localSeparableResidueAlgAction K).toMonoidHom =
      (localAbstractFixedResidueIntermediateField K H).fixingSubgroup
```

The definition has no universe parameters: `K` lives in `Type`, as in the
source. The binders, in order, are the source section variables (lines
33–34) followed by the theorem's own explicit binder `H` (line 129).

| Binder | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `K : Type` | the base field |
| `[Field K] [ValuativeRel K] [TopologicalSpace K] [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField K]` | `K` is a nonarchimedean local field (Mathlib's class) |
| `H : ClosedSubgroup (Gal(SeparableClosure K/K))` | a closed subgroup of the absolute Galois group `Gal(SeparableClosure K/K)` for the Krull topology (Mathlib's `ClosedSubgroup`) |

### The two sides

Both sides are subgroups of the group of automorphisms of
`selectedResidueField (localSeparableValuationSubring K)` (the field `λ`)
over `decompositionResidueField K (localSeparableValuationSubring K)` (the
field `κ_K`).

- Left side. `localSeparableResidueAlgAction K` is a continuous monoid
  homomorphism from `Gal(SeparableClosure K/K)` to these automorphisms, and
  `.toMonoidHom` is its underlying monoid homomorphism.
  `H.toSubgroup.map f` is Mathlib's image `Subgroup.map` of the subgroup
  underlying `H` under `f`.
- Right side. `localAbstractFixedResidueIntermediateField K H` is the
  intermediate field `F` of `λ` over `κ_K` defined in M250.001 as
  `IntermediateField.adjoin κ_K (Set.range (algebraMap κ_E λ))`, where `κ_E`
  is `decompositionResidueField E B` for
  `E = abstractFixedField K (SeparableClosure K) H`. Mathlib's
  `IntermediateField.fixingSubgroup F` is the subgroup of automorphisms of
  `λ` over `κ_K` that fix every element of `F`.

So the equation is an equality of subgroups: an automorphism `τ` of `λ`
over `κ_K` is the residue action of some element of `H` if and only if `τ`
fixes `F` pointwise.

A private probe compiled against this module replays the source proof
(lines 132–210) after `intro K _ _ _ _ H`. In the probe the two source
results the proof cites that are not yet proved in the Library are
hypotheses: M250.006 (the published target
`localAbstractFixedResidueAction_applyStatement`) and M244.004 (the
published target `residueAlgActionOfEqTop_surjectiveStatement` at universes
`0, 0`). The proof shows each inclusion. For `⊆`, every `ρ_K(σ)` with
`σ ∈ H` fixes the image of `κ_E`, because by M250.006 it equals the residue
action of `σ` over `E`, which is linear over `κ_E`. So it fixes the field
`F` generated by that image. For `⊇`, an automorphism fixing `F` is linear
over `κ_E`, so by M244.004 it is the residue action over `E` of some element
of `Gal(K^sep/E)`. That element corresponds to an element of `H`, and
M250.006 gives the equality. This shows that the target is the intended
proposition and that its proof needs, besides published declarations, only
the proofs of M250.006 and M244.004. The probe is development evidence only;
the proof is a separate unit and is not part of this module.
