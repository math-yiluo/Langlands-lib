# Continuity of the fixed-field intrinsic-to-ambient base equivalence

This module proves the published closed proposition
`Langlands.NYLL.LocalClassFieldTheory.intrinsicBaseEquivAmbientFixedField_continuousStatement`
(source row M281.002) by the theorem
`Langlands.NYLL.LocalClassFieldTheory.intrinsicBaseEquivAmbientFixedField_continuous`.
The theorem's type is exactly that proposition, with no parameters of its own.

Its imports are
`Langlands.NYLL.ClassFieldTheory.LocalClassFieldTheory.Finite.LocalReciprocity.FixedFieldIntrinsicReciprocity.EmbeddedFrobeniusTransport.U002.Statements`
(the statement) and
`Langlands.NYLL.ClassFieldTheory.AlgebraicNumberTheory.SeparableClosureEmbedding.U001.Proofs`
(the published theorem
`Langlands.NYLL.AlgebraicNumberTheory.continuous_algEquiv_autCongr`, source row

## Target

For a field `K : Type`, a finite abstract field
`H : Langlands.NYLL.ClassFormation.FiniteAbstractField Gal(SeparableClosure K / K)`,
its fixed field `F := abstractFixedField K (SeparableClosure K) H.field` and an
`F`-algebra equivalence `e : SeparableClosure F ≃ₐ[F] SeparableClosure K`
(with the standard `F`-algebra structure on `SeparableClosure F`), the
statement asserts

```lean
Continuous (intrinsicBaseEquivAmbientFixedField K H e)
```

The domain `(intrinsicAbstractBase F).toSubgroup` and the codomain
`H.field.toSubgroup` carry the subspace topologies of the Krull topologies on
`Gal(SeparableClosure F / F)` and `Gal(SeparableClosure K / K)`.

## Implemented proof

```lean
  intro K _ H F e
  rw [Topology.IsInducing.subtypeVal.continuous_iff]
  refine Continuous.congr
    ((continuous_restrictScalarsHom K F (SeparableClosure K)).comp
      ((AlgebraicNumberTheory.continuous_algEquiv_autCongr e).comp
        continuous_subtype_val)) ?_
  intro tau
  rfl
```

1. The codomain is a subtype with the induced topology, so
   `Topology.IsInducing.subtypeVal.continuous_iff` reduces the goal to
   continuity of `Subtype.val ∘ intrinsicBaseEquivAmbientFixedField K H e`
   as a map into `Gal(SeparableClosure K / K)`.
2. By definition, `intrinsicBaseEquivAmbientFixedField K H e` sends `tau` to
   `(abstractSubgroupEquivGaloisGroup K (SeparableClosure K) H.field).symm
   (AlgEquiv.autCongr e tau.1)`, whose underlying automorphism is
   `(AlgEquiv.autCongr e tau.1).restrictScalars K`. So the map agrees
   pointwise, by `rfl`, with the composite of `Subtype.val`,
   `AlgEquiv.autCongr e` and `AlgEquiv.restrictScalarsHom K` for the tower
   `K → F → SeparableClosure K`.
3. The three factors are continuous: `continuous_subtype_val`; the published
   `Langlands.NYLL.AlgebraicNumberTheory.continuous_algEquiv_autCongr e`; and
   the private helper below with `P := F`, `L := SeparableClosure K` (the
   tower instance is Mathlib's for an intermediate field, and
   `SeparableClosure K` is algebraic over `F`). `Continuous.congr` transfers
   continuity along the pointwise equality.

No hypothesis, global instance or public auxiliary declaration is added.

## Private helper lemma

```lean
private theorem continuous_restrictScalarsHom
    (K P L : Type) [Field K] [Field P] [Field L]
    [Algebra K P] [Algebra P L] [Algebra K L]
    [IsScalarTower K P L] [Algebra.IsAlgebraic P L] :
    Continuous (AlgEquiv.restrictScalarsHom K : Gal(L/P) →* Gal(L/K))
```

For a scalar tower `K → P → L` with `L` algebraic over `P`, restricting
scalars from `Gal(L / P)` to `Gal(L / K)` is continuous for the Krull
topologies. The helper reuses the
private theorem `continuous_restrictScalars` in
`Langlands/Galois/AbsoluteTopologyProof.lean` (lines 14–34, namespace
`Langlands.GaloisRepresentation`), which cannot be used from another file.
The helper copies that proof verbatim; the only changes are the name and the
universe (`Type` instead of `Type u`, `Type v`, `Type w`). The proof: a
group homomorphism is continuous if it is continuous at `1`; a neighbourhood
of `1` in `Gal(L / K)` contains the fixing subgroup of some
finite-dimensional intermediate field `E` of `L / K`; `E` is generated over
`K` by a finite set `s` (`essFiniteType_iff`); the intermediate field of
`L / P` generated by `s` is finite-dimensional because `L` is algebraic over
`P`, so its fixing subgroup is a neighbourhood of `1` in `Gal(L / P)`; and an
automorphism fixing it fixes `s`, hence (after restricting scalars) fixes
`E = K(s)` (`forall_mem_adjoin_smul_eq_self_iff`).

The helper is private to this module and states only Mathlib notions.

## Relation to the original proof

The original proof (lines 133–149) has the same structure: it reduces to the
underlying automorphisms with `continuous_induced_rng`, rewrites the map as
`τ ↦ (AlgEquiv.autCongr e τ.1).restrictScalars K`, and composes the source
lemmas `Field.absoluteGaloisGroup.ofIntermediateFieldInExtension_continuous`
(source row M235.035) and `Field.absoluteGaloisGroup.algEquiv_autCongr_continuous`
(source row M235.010). Neither is in the Library. The implemented proof uses
the published `continuous_algEquiv_autCongr` for the second and the private
helper for the first. The source proof also installs
`FiniteDimensional K F` from the unproved `abstractFixedField_finiteDimensional`
(M099.005); the implemented proof does not need it.
