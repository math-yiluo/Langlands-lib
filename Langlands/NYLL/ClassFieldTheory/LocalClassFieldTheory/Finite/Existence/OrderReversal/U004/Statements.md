# Standard subgroups inside finite abelian norm subgroups: proposition target

This module defines the closed proposition
`Langlands.NYLL.LocalClassFieldTheory.exists_uniformizerPrincipalSubgroup_le_normSubgroupStatement`.
With `open LocalFieldTheory` inside `namespace Langlands.NYLL.LocalClassFieldTheory`,
its exact body is

```lean
∀ (K L : Type) [Field K] [Field L] [Algebra K L]
  [FiniteDimensional K L] [IsAbelianGalois K L]
  [ValuativeRel K] [TopologicalSpace K]
  [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField K]
  (ϖ : Kˣ),
  ∃ d n : Nat, 0 < d ∧ 1 ≤ n ∧
    LocalFieldTheory.uniformizerPrincipalSubgroup K ϖ d n ≤
      localNormSubgroup K L
```

It says: let `L/K` be a finite abelian Galois extension of fields, where `K`
carries a valuation relation and a topology making it a nonarchimedean local
field, and let `ϖ` be any unit of `K`. Then there are natural numbers `d ≥ 1`
and `n ≥ 1` such that the standard subgroup generated by `ϖ ^ d` and the
`n`-th principal units of `K` lies in the image of the norm map `Lˣ → Kˣ`.

The objects are the actual published ones:

- `Langlands.NYLL.LocalFieldTheory.uniformizerPrincipalSubgroup K ϖ d n` is
  `Subgroup.zpowers (ϖ ^ d) ⊔ fieldPrincipalUnits K n`, the subgroup of `Kˣ`
  generated by the integer powers of `ϖ ^ d` and the image in `Kˣ` of the
  `n`-th principal-unit subgroup (published unit M289.002).
- `Langlands.NYLL.LocalFieldTheory.localNormSubgroup K L` is the range of the
  norm homomorphism on unit groups, `N_{L/K}(Lˣ) ≤ Kˣ` (published unit M093.001).
- `IsAbelianGalois`, `FiniteDimensional`, `ValuativeRel` and
  `IsNonarchimedeanLocalField` are the Mathlib classes of those names.

Both fields are in `Type`, that is universe zero, as in the source; the
declaration has no universe parameters. No hypothesis is imposed on `ϖ`: it is
not required to be a uniformizer, and the target is stated for every unit
exactly as in the source. No valuation, topology or local field structure is
required on `L`. This is a proposition definition; no proof is asserted.
