```lean
∀ (K : Type) [Field K] [ValuativeRel K] [TopologicalSpace K]
  [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField K]
  (H : Subgroup Kˣ)
  (ϖ : Kˣ) (d n : ℕ)
  (hϖ : valuationMap K (Additive.ofMul ϖ) = 1)
  (hstandard : LocalFieldTheory.uniformizerPrincipalSubgroup K ϖ d n ≤ H)
  (U T : ClassFormation.FiniteAbelianSubextension
    (intrinsicAbstractBase K))
  (hUle :
    (U.normSubgroup (intrinsicAbsoluteUnits K)).map
        (baseUnitsEquivGaloisAmbientFixed K (SeparableClosure K)).symm.toAddMonoidHom ≤
      (unramifiedNormSubgroup K d).toAddSubgroup)
  (hTle :
    (T.normSubgroup (intrinsicAbsoluteUnits K)).map
        (baseUnitsEquivGaloisAmbientFixed K (SeparableClosure K)).symm.toAddMonoidHom ≤
      (LocalFieldTheory.uniformizerPrincipalSubgroup K ϖ 1 n).toAddSubgroup),
  (U.compositum T).normSubgroup (intrinsicAbsoluteUnits K) ≤
    H.toAddSubgroup.map
      (baseUnitsEquivGaloisAmbientFixed K (SeparableClosure K)).toAddMonoidHom
```

Here `K` is a nonarchimedean local field, `H` is a subgroup of its
multiplicative units, and `ϖ` is a unit of normalized valuation one. The
hypothesis `hstandard` says that `H` contains the standard subgroup generated
by `ϖ ^ d` and the level-`n` principal units. The two finite abelian
subextensions `U` and `T` have norm subgroups whose images in the additive
type tag of `Kˣ` lie respectively in the unramified degree-`d` subgroup and
the standard subgroup generated by `ϖ` and the level-`n` principal units.
The requested conclusion places the norm subgroup of their actual finite
abelian compositum in the image of `H` in the fixed coefficient group.

The map direction is part of the statement. Writing
`e = baseUnitsEquivGaloisAmbientFixed K (SeparableClosure K)`, the hypotheses
use `e.symm.toAddMonoidHom` to map fixed coefficients back to `Additive Kˣ`,
whereas the conclusion uses `e.toAddMonoidHom` to map `H.toAddSubgroup` into
fixed coefficients.

`K : Type` means universe zero. The source file's earlier `universe u` is
unused by M403.003, so this target introduces no universe parameter.
All four instance binders, every explicit parameter, and all four named
hypotheses retain their source order and binder kinds. Both `d` and `n` range
over all natural numbers, including zero; there is no positivity premise.
