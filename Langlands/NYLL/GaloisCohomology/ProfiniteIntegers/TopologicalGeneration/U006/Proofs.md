# Continuous homomorphisms preserve closed generated membership

`Langlands.NYLL.ClassFormation.map_mem_closedSubgroupGenerated_image.{u, v}`
proves exactly the published closed proposition
`Langlands.NYLL.ClassFormation.map_mem_closedSubgroupGenerated_imageStatement.{u, v}`.
The theorem has no external term parameters. Its two universe parameters
`u` and `v` stay independent. The proposition contains all twelve binders:

```lean
∀ {G : Type u} [Group G] [TopologicalSpace G] [IsTopologicalGroup G]
  {H : Type v} [Group H] [TopologicalSpace H] [IsTopologicalGroup H]
  (φ : G →ₜ* H) {X : Set G} {y : G}
  (hy : y ∈ (closedSubgroupGenerated (G := G) X : Subgroup G)),
  φ y ∈ (closedSubgroupGenerated (G := H) (φ '' X) : Subgroup H)
```

Here `closedSubgroupGenerated X` is the published NYLL closed subgroup whose
underlying subgroup is `(Subgroup.closure X).topologicalClosure`. That is
the topological closure of the abstract subgroup generated by `X`. The
homomorphism `φ` is a bundled continuous monoid homomorphism. The premise
`hy` is ordinary membership in that actual subgroup. The conclusion is
membership of `φ y` in the corresponding subgroup of `H` for `φ '' X`.

## Implemented proof

The sole direct import is the published
`Langlands.NYLL.GaloisCohomology.ProfiniteIntegers.TopologicalGeneration.Statements`
module. Through it, the published `Definitions` module supplies
`closedSubgroupGenerated`. The proof first introduces the twelve binders
in order: `G`, its three instances, `H`, its three instances, `φ`, `X`,
`y` and `hy`. It then runs the original source proof unchanged.

1. Let `K : Subgroup G` be the preimage (`Subgroup.comap`) of the subgroup
   underlying `closedSubgroupGenerated (φ '' X)` along `φ`, viewed as a
   monoid homomorphism `G →* H`.
2. `hX : X ⊆ K`. For `x ∈ X`, the image `φ x` lies in `φ '' X` through the
   witness `⟨x, hx, rfl⟩`. Hence `Subgroup.subset_closure` puts it in
   `Subgroup.closure (φ '' X)`, and `Subgroup.le_topologicalClosure` puts
   it in that subgroup's topological closure. Membership in a `comap` is
   definitionally membership of the image, so this proves `x ∈ K`.
3. `hKclosed : IsClosed (K : Set G)`. A `change` rewrites the carrier of
   `K` as the preimage set `{x | φ x ∈ closedSubgroupGenerated (φ '' X)}`.
   The `isClosed'` field of the `ClosedSubgroup` structure says that this
   target carrier is closed. `IsClosed.preimage` with `φ.continuous` then
   gives a closed preimage.
4. `Subgroup.closure_le` turns `hX` into `Subgroup.closure X ≤ K`. Mathlib's
   `Subgroup.topologicalClosure_minimal` with `hKclosed` gives
   `(Subgroup.closure X).topologicalClosure ≤ K`. This order relation is
   applied to `hy`, which gives `y ∈ K`. By the definition of `comap`, that
   membership is `φ y ∈ closedSubgroupGenerated (φ '' X)`, which is the
   goal.

The Mathlib declarations used are `Subgroup.comap`,
`Subgroup.subset_closure`, `Subgroup.le_topologicalClosure`,
`Subgroup.closure_le`, `Subgroup.topologicalClosure_minimal`,
`ClosedSubgroup.isClosed'`, `IsClosed.preimage` and
`ContinuousMonoidHom.continuous`. No other M007 theorem is used.
No new definition, instance, simp lemma, axiom or helper module is introduced.
