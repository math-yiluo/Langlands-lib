# The canonical family topologically generates its closed generated subgroup

This module proves the independently published closed proposition
`Langlands.NYLL.ClassFormation.closedSubgroupGeneratedMap_topologicallyGeneratesStatement.{u, v}`
by the theorem
`Langlands.NYLL.ClassFormation.closedSubgroupGeneratedMap_topologicallyGenerates`.
The theorem's unapplied type is exactly that proposition, with no external
term or instance parameters. The universes `u` and `v` are arbitrary and
independent.

Its sole direct import is
`Langlands.NYLL.GaloisCohomology.ProfiniteIntegers.TopologicalGeneration.Statements`.
That published Lean/Markdown pair and its imported
`TopologicalGeneration.Definitions` pair remain unchanged. The proof uses
their existing definitions and Mathlib lemmas only.

## Target and mathematical meaning

Unfolding the published assertion gives

```lean
∀ {G : Type u} [Group G] [TopologicalSpace G] [IsTopologicalGroup G]
  {A : Type v} (φ : A → G),
  TopologicallyGenerates
    (G := (closedSubgroupGenerated (G := G) (Set.range φ) : Subgroup G))
    (Set.range (closedSubgroupGeneratedMap (G := G) φ))
```

The six internal binders keep their source order and kinds: implicit
`{G : Type u}`, the three instances `[Group G]`, `[TopologicalSpace G]`,
`[IsTopologicalGroup G]`, implicit `{A : Type v}` and explicit
`(φ : A → G)`. No structure is assumed on `A`.

The published definitions are used unchanged. `TopologicallyGenerates X`
means `(Subgroup.closure X).topologicalClosure = ⊤`.
`closedSubgroupGenerated X` is the `ClosedSubgroup` whose underlying subgroup
is `(Subgroup.closure X).topologicalClosure`. `closedSubgroupGeneratedMap φ`
sends `a` to `φ a`, viewed as an element of that subgroup. The group
`K = closedSubgroupGenerated (Set.range φ)` carries Mathlib's subtype group
structure and subspace topology.

Mathematically: write `K` for the closure of the abstract subgroup generated
by the values of `φ`, and `L` for the abstract subgroup of `K` generated by
the same values viewed in `K`. The claim is that `L` is dense in `K`. The
inclusion `K → G` maps `L` onto the abstract subgroup of `G` generated by
the values of `φ`. A point of a subspace lies in the closure of a subset
exactly when its image lies in the ambient closure of the image subset.
Every point of `K` lies in the ambient closure of that generated subgroup,
by the definition of `K`, hence in the closure of `L` inside `K`.

## Implemented proof

After `intro G _ _ _ A φ`, which exposes the six binders, the proof is the
original proof body with a single line replaced:

1. Local abbreviations `K`, `φK` and `L` name the closed generated subgroup,
   the canonical family into it, and the abstract subgroup of `K` generated
   by the range of `φK`.
2. `hmap` proves `Subgroup.map K.subtype L = Subgroup.closure (Set.range φ)`
   by `le_antisymm`. For `≤`, `Subgroup.map_le_iff_le_comap` and
   `Subgroup.closure_le` reduce to the generators `φK a`, whose images `φ a`
   lie in `Subgroup.closure (Set.range φ)` by `Subgroup.subset_closure`.
   For `≥`, `Subgroup.closure_le` reduces to the generators `φ a`, each the
   image of `φK a ∈ L`.
3. `himage` identifies the image of `L` under `Subtype.val` with the
   carrier of `Subgroup.map K.subtype L`, by elementwise extensionality.
4. The goal `TopologicallyGenerates (Set.range φK)` is rewritten with
   `TopologicallyGenerates`, `SetLike.ext'_iff`,
   `Subgroup.topologicalClosure_coe` and `Subgroup.coe_top` to
   `closure (L : Set K) = Set.univ`, and `ext y` splits it into two
   directions. The forward direction is `simp only [mem_univ]`.
5. For the reverse direction, `change` exposes `y ∈ closure (L : Set K)`,
   Mathlib's `closure_subtype` turns it into
   `(y : G) ∈ closure (Subtype.val '' (L : Set K))`, and rewriting with
   `himage` and then `hmap` gives
   `(y : G) ∈ closure (Subgroup.closure (Set.range φ) : Set G)`. A final
   `change` restates this as membership in the carrier of
   `(Subgroup.closure (Set.range φ)).topologicalClosure`, which is the
   membership proof `y.2` carried by `y : K`.

The only change from the source proof is in step 4. The source rewrites
with its theorem `topologicallyGenerates_iff_dense` (M007.002) and then
`dense_iff_closure_eq`. The proof of M007.002 is not in the Library at the
workspace base `8ff9abe94fbb8d61ec05e4a00fc92f7cf1534b96`; only its target
`topologicallyGenerates_iff_denseStatement` is. The source proof of M007.002
(lines 40–41) is the single rewrite `TopologicallyGenerates,
SetLike.ext'_iff, Subgroup.topologicalClosure_coe, Subgroup.coe_top,
dense_iff_closure_eq`. Composing it with the following
`dense_iff_closure_eq` rewrite produces the same goal
`closure (L : Set K) = Set.univ` as the four rewrites used here, which are
that proof without its last step. No auxiliary declaration, simp attribute,
instance or new hypothesis is introduced.
