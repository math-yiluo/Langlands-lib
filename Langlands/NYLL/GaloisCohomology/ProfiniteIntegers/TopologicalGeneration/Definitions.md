## Constructor laws and reused mathematics

`Subgroup.closure X` is Mathlib's abstract subgroup generated by `X`.
Mathlib's `Subgroup.topologicalClosure` then takes the topological closure
of that subgroup, with the subgroup laws already implemented by Mathlib.
The implementation uses these existing constructions directly.

The `closedSubgroupGenerated` constructor fills `ClosedSubgroup.toSubgroup`
with that topological closure. It fills the sole additional certificate,
`isClosed'`, with `Subgroup.isClosed_topologicalClosure _`. Thus closedness
is certified for the actual carrier set, and the group operations and laws
come from the supplied underlying subgroup.

For each `a : A`, the map's value is the subtype pair with first component
`φ a`. The witness `⟨a, rfl⟩` first proves
`φ a ∈ Set.range φ`. `Subgroup.subset_closure` places that value in the
abstract subgroup generated by the range; `Subgroup.le_topologicalClosure`
then places it in its topological closure. This is exactly the membership
certificate required by the codomain. Consequently the ambient value of
`closedSubgroupGeneratedMap φ a` reduces to `φ a` by definition.

The reuse comparison was made against the actual definitions and certificates
in Mathlib's `Topology/Algebra/Group/Subgroup.lean` and
`Topology/Algebra/Group/ClosedSubgroup.lean`. This module reuses their
`Subgroup.topologicalClosure`, `ClosedSubgroup` and membership/closedness
certificates, and Mathlib's `Subgroup.closure`; it creates no replacement
subgroup structure or global instance. The existing Library construction
`Langlands.LocalClassField.ContinuousProfinite.imageClosure` is specialized to
the diagonal homomorphism into the product of open finite quotients. Its
particular ambient group and subgroup do not supply this generic set-indexed
API.
