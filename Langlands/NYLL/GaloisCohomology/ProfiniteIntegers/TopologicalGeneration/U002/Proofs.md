# Topological generation as density

This module proves the theorem
`Langlands.NYLL.ClassFormation.topologicallyGenerates_iff_dense`.
Its complete unapplied type is the already published closed proposition
`Langlands.NYLL.ClassFormation.topologicallyGenerates_iff_denseStatement.{u}`.
This is the proof phase of M007.002 in the
[NYLL partition](../../../../../../notes/reuse/nyll-public-port/m001-m024.md#m007).

## Closed target and binders

The target internally quantifies over exactly this telescope and conclusion:

```lean
∀ {G : Type u} [Group G] [TopologicalSpace G] [IsTopologicalGroup G]
  {X : Set G},
  TopologicallyGenerates (G := G) X ↔
    Dense ((Subgroup.closure X : Subgroup G) : Set G)
```

All five term binders are inside the closed proposition, in this order:

| Binders | Kind | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `G : Type u` | implicit | The ambient group |
| `[Group G]`, `[TopologicalSpace G]`, `[IsTopologicalGroup G]` | instance-implicit | Its group structure, topology and the continuity of multiplication and inversion |
| `X : Set G` | implicit | The arbitrary generating set |

The conclusion is an equivalence. Its left side is the published predicate
`TopologicallyGenerates X`, which by its definition in the imported
`Definitions` module is the equation
`(Subgroup.closure X).topologicalClosure = ⊤` of subgroups of `G`. Its right
side states that the carrier of the abstract subgroup `Subgroup.closure X`
generated by `X` is dense in `G`. The single universe parameter `u` is
arbitrary and is not restricted to zero. The theorem has no external term
parameters and adds no hypothesis: no compactness, profiniteness,
Hausdorff, commutativity or finiteness assumption on `G` or `X`.

The sole direct import is the published
`Langlands.NYLL.GaloisCohomology.ProfiniteIntegers.TopologicalGeneration.Statements`
module, which imports the published `Definitions` module and through it the
two original Mathlib imports `Mathlib.Topology.Algebra.ClopenNhdofOne` and
`Mathlib.Topology.Algebra.ContinuousMonoidHom`. No other M007 proof is
used.

## Implemented proof

The proof introduces the target's five binders, naming the three instances
anonymously, then applies the original rewrite chain of source lines 40–41
without changing its steps:

```lean
intro G _ _ _ X
rw [TopologicallyGenerates, SetLike.ext'_iff, Subgroup.topologicalClosure_coe,
  Subgroup.coe_top, dense_iff_closure_eq]
```

The rewrites act as follows, all with Mathlib lemmas of the approved
environment:

1. `TopologicallyGenerates` unfolds the published predicate through its
   equation lemma, so the left side becomes
   `(Subgroup.closure X).topologicalClosure = ⊤`.
2. `SetLike.ext'_iff` (`Mathlib/Data/SetLike/Basic.lean`, line 168) turns this
   equality of subgroups into the equality of their carriers,
   `((Subgroup.closure X).topologicalClosure : Set G) = ((⊤ : Subgroup G) : Set G)`.
3. `Subgroup.topologicalClosure_coe`
   (`Mathlib/Topology/Algebra/Group/Subgroup.lean`, lines 46–48) replaces the
   carrier of the topological closure by the topological closure
   `closure ((Subgroup.closure X : Subgroup G) : Set G)` of the carrier.
4. `Subgroup.coe_top` (`Mathlib/Algebra/Group/Subgroup/Lattice.lean`,
   lines 147–148) replaces the carrier of `⊤` by `Set.univ`.
5. `dense_iff_closure_eq` (`Mathlib/Topology/Closure.lean`, line 385) rewrites
   the right side `Dense s` to `closure s = Set.univ` for the same carrier `s`.

After the last rewrite both sides of the equivalence are the same
proposition `closure ((Subgroup.closure X : Subgroup G) : Set G) = Set.univ`,
and `rw` closes the goal by reflexivity. The instance
`[IsTopologicalGroup G]` is used only to form `Subgroup.topologicalClosure`,
which needs continuous multiplication and inversion; the equivalence itself
is pure point-set topology of carriers.

The published statement already depends on `propext`, `Classical.choice`
and `Quot.sound` through its imported definitions and Mathlib; the proof
adds no further axiom.
