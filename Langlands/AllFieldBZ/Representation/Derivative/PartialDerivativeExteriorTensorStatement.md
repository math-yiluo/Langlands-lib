# Partial derivatives of exterior tensors over local fields

The partial derivative of a smooth exterior tensor on
\(\mathrm{GL}_a(F)\times\mathrm{GL}_b(F)\) is naturally the
tensor of the left block with the top derivative of the right
block, with the resulting action carried by the left block.
The comparison concerns the actual normalized functors for
every nonarchimedean local field and includes the rank-zero
conventions.

This is Zelevinsky (1980), Proposition 3.7(a), printed p. 182.
The functor formulation records naturality in the two block
representations. [Zelevinsky (1980)](https://www.numdam.org/item/ASENS_1980_4_13_2_165_0/)
