# Equal-block Jacquet modules of the two segment endpoints

`Langlands.AllFieldBZ.Classification.SegmentFullJacquetStatement` applies
over any nonarchimedean local field with the specified topology and
valuation. For a segment of length \(m\) and cuspidal rank \(r\), take an
actual representative \(A\) of its beginning. Form the increasing
normalized product of \(A,\nu A,\ldots,\nu^{m-1} A\), where
\(\nu=|\det|\) uses the residue-normalized absolute value.

For an irreducible subrepresentation of this product, the normalized
Jacquet module along the standard shape of \(m\) blocks of size \(r\) is
equivalent to the external tensor in increasing order. For an
irreducible quotient of the same product, it is equivalent to the
external tensor in decreasing order. Both conclusions are actual
equivalences of representations of the ordered block Levi. The two
hypotheses are witnessed by an injective intertwiner into the product
and a surjective intertwiner out of it respectively.
