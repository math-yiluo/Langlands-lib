# The initial and terminal pieces of a segment

Cutting the increasing list of a segment after its first positive number
of entries gives an initial segment with unchanged base and a terminal
segment beginning at the member at the cut. The cut lies before the end.
The degrees of the two segments therefore sum to the degree of the
original segment, defining the ordered two-block shape for the Jacquet
module. The lengths of the pieces are the cut position and the original
length minus that position. This is the two-block case of the contiguous
segment decomposition in Zelevinsky's Proposition 3.4, printed p. 181.
