# Existence of the local-field special segment head

`Langlands.AllFieldBZ.Classification.SegmentHeadExistenceStatement`
says that each segment over a nonarchimedean local field has an actual
irreducible smooth head in its increasing normalized induction. The
predicate `IsSegmentHead` requires a quotient map whose kernel equals
the kernel of every irreducible quotient map from that induction.

This is the increasing-order quotient description of the special
representation \(\langle\Delta\rangle^t\) in Zelevinsky,
*Induced representations of reductive p-adic groups II. On irreducible
representations of GL(n)* (1980), §9.1, printed p. 197
(`zelevinsky-1980-induced-ii`).
