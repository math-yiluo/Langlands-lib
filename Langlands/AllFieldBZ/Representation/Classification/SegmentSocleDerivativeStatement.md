# Derivatives of a segment socle over local fields

For an actual segment socle with cuspidal rank \(r\), all positive
Bernstein–Zelevinsky derivatives of orders other than \(r\) vanish.
The order-\(r\) derivative is a scalar line for a singleton.
For a segment of at least two members it is the actual socle of
the segment obtained by deleting the last member. That relation
uses the actual derivative representation after transport to the
degree of the truncated segment.

This is Zelevinsky (1980), Theorem 3.5, printed p. 182, with the
derivative conventions in Proposition 3.7.
[Zelevinsky (1980)](https://www.numdam.org/item/ASENS_1980_4_13_2_165_0/)
