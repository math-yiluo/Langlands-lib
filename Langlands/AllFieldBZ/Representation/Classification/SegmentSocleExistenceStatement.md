# Existence of the local-field segment socle

`Langlands.AllFieldBZ.Classification.SegmentSocleExistenceStatement`
says that each segment over a nonarchimedean local field has an actual
irreducible smooth socle in its increasing normalized induction. The
predicate `IsSegmentSocle` includes an embedding whose image equals the
image of every irreducible embedding into that induction.

This is the increasing-order submodule description of
\(\langle\Delta\rangle\) in Zelevinsky, *Induced representations of reductive
p-adic groups II. On irreducible representations of GL(n)* (1980), §3.1,
printed pp. 180–181 (`zelevinsky-1980-induced-ii`).
