# The socle of an ordered product of distinct supercuspidal factors

`Langlands.AllFieldBZ.Classification.RegularCuspidalProductSocleStatement`
quantifies over a nonarchimedean local field, a positive rank \(n\), an ordered
block shape of \(n\), and a smooth irreducible supercuspidal representation in
each block. The distinctness condition concerns the cuspidal data, which include
both their ranks and their actual isomorphism classes.

The product is normalized smooth parabolic induction of the external tensor
from the standard upper block parabolic. Its irreducible socle is witnessed
by an actual injective intertwiner. Every irreducible embedding into the
product has the same image subspace. This is the unique-submodule assertion
in Zelevinsky, *Induced representations of reductive p-adic groups II. On
irreducible representations of GL(n)* (1980), Proposition 2.10, printed p. 180
(`zelevinsky-1980-induced-ii`). Both orders of the segment products in §§3.1
and 9.1 are instances for successive distinct determinant twists.
