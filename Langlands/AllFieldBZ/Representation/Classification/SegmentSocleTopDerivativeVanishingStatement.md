# Top derivative vanishing for a segment socle

Let \(\Delta\) be a segment of at least two members over a
nonarchimedean local field. The top Bernstein–Zelevinsky derivative
of the actual irreducible socle of its increasing normalized
induction has zero carrier. The derivative uses the supplied
unitary additive character and the exact rank of the segment.

This is the vanishing in Zelevinsky (1980), Lemma 3.9, printed
pp. 182–183. The statement uses the actual normalized derivative
functor. [Zelevinsky (1980)](https://www.numdam.org/item/ASENS_1980_4_13_2_165_0/)
