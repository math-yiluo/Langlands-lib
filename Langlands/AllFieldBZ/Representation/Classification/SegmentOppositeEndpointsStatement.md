# The decreasing-order descriptions of the segment constituents

`Langlands.AllFieldBZ.Classification.SegmentOppositeEndpointsStatement`
concerns every segment \(\Delta=[\rho,\nu^{m-1}\rho]\) over a nonarchimedean
local field and every actual supercuspidal representative \(A\) of its
beginning. It compares the increasing-order defining relations with the
actual normalized induction in the order
\((\nu^{m-1}A,\ldots,\nu A,A)\).

The increasing-order irreducible socle is characterized as the
irreducible head of the decreasing product, with equality of the kernels
of all irreducible quotient maps. The increasing-order special head is
the irreducible socle of the decreasing product, with equality of the
images of all irreducible embeddings. Both statements are equivalences
for a supplied smooth representation.

These are the two descriptions in Zelevinsky, *Induced representations
of reductive p-adic groups II. On irreducible representations of GL(n)*
(1980), §§3.1 and 9.1, printed pp. 180–181 and 197
(`zelevinsky-1980-induced-ii`).
