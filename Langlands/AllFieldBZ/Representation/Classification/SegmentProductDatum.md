# Ordered products of segment socles over local fields

A multisegment is a finite multiset of segments of irreducible
supercuspidal classes, with total degree given by the sum of their
degrees. Two segments are linked if their union is a segment and
neither contains the other. A linked first segment precedes a
second when the second starts at a positive integral determinant
twist of the first.

A product datum gives an ordering and an actual smooth block
representation of the degree of each segment. Its induced object is
the normalized induction from the upper block parabolic. The
subrepresentation relation requires each block to be the actual
socle of its segment induction. The Zelevinsky socle relation also
requires the order in which no earlier segment precedes a later
one and the unique irreducible subobject of the induced object.

These are the definitions in Zelevinsky (1980), §4.1, printed
p. 184, and §§6.1 and 6.5, printed pp. 188–189. They use
the segment convention of §3.1, printed pp. 180–181.
[Zelevinsky (1980)](https://www.numdam.org/item/ASENS_1980_4_13_2_165_0/)
