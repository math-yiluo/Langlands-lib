# Length and Jacquet factors of an adjacent supercuspidal product

`Langlands.AllFieldBZ.Classification.AdjacentSupercuspidalProductStructureStatement`
uses a specified nonarchimedean local field and its topological data.
Let \(A,B\) be actual supercuspidal representations of \(\mathrm{GL}_r(F)\)
with \(r>0\), and suppose \(B\simeq |\det| A\) or \(A\simeq |\det| B\).
Here \(|\cdot|\) is the residue-normalized absolute value.

The normalized upper parabolic product \(A\times B\) has composition
length two and exactly one nonzero proper invariant subspace. Its
subrepresentation and quotient are irreducible. Their actual normalized
\((r,r)\) Jacquet modules are equivalent to \(A\otimes B\) and
\(B\otimes A\), respectively, as smooth representations of the two
ordered Levi factors.
