# Reducibility of adjacent supercuspidal induction over a local field

`Langlands.AllFieldBZ.Classification.AdjacentSupercuspidalProductReducibilityStatement`
concerns actual smooth irreducible supercuspidal representations \(A,B\) of
\(\operatorname{GL}_r(F)\) for \(r>0\) over any nonarchimedean local field \(F\).
Write \(\nu(g)=|\det g|_F\) with the residue-normalized absolute value.
If \(B\simeq \nu A\) or \(A\simeq \nu B\), the actual normalized
induction \(A\times B\) from the upper block parabolic of shape \((r,r)\)
is reducible. The hypothesis compares the isomorphism classes of the
actual objects. The order of the factors is \(A\) followed by \(B\).

This is the reducibility assertion in Zelevinsky,
*Induced representations of reductive p-adic groups II. On irreducible
representations of GL(n)* (1980), Proposition 1.11(b), printed p. 175
(`zelevinsky-1980-induced-ii`). The statement concerns reducibility,
which is the part of that proposition used to detect the adjacent
order in the full cuspidal Jacquet filtration of a segment.
