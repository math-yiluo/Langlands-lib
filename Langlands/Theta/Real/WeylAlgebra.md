# The Weyl algebra and the `L²` control of Schwartz seminorms

**Outside GKT.** This file belongs to the archimedean theory, which Gan–Kudla–Takeda leave out
(preface, p. iv).
There is no counterpart over a nonarchimedean field. The
declarations are in the name space `Langlands.Theta.Real` (`RealMetaplecticGroup` and its name
space are in `Langlands.Theta`).

The module `Langlands.Theta.Real.WeylAlgebra` defines the Weyl algebra of operators on `𝓢(ℝˡ, ℂ)`
and proves that Schwartz seminorms, hence continuous linear functionals, are controlled by `L²`
norms of images under it.

## Content

`weylAlgebra l` is the subalgebra generated by the operators `schrodingerDeriv w`; it contains
all iterated directional derivatives and the weights `weylAlgebra.weight l = 1 + ‖x‖²` (as an
operator) and `weylAlgebra.fourierWeight l = 1 - Δ / 4π²`, which the inverse Fourier transform
exchanges. Since `‖𝓕⁻¹ g‖_∞ ≤ ‖g‖_{L¹}` and `‖g‖_{L¹} ≤ C ‖(1 + ‖x‖²)^N g‖_{L²}` for `N > dim / 2`
(Hölder), every `sup |f|` is bounded by an `L²` norm of an element of the Weyl algebra applied to
`f`; applying this to `(1 + ‖x‖²)^k` times the derivatives of `f` gives
`exists_seminorm_le_sum_norm_toLp`. A continuous linear functional is bounded by finitely many
seminorms; by Hahn–Banach it factors through `f ↦ ((A i f) ∈ L²)ᵢ` for finitely many `A i` in the
Weyl algebra (`exists_factor_through_toLp`). This is how weak statements on Schwartz space are
reduced to statements in `L²` in `Theta/Real/Intertwiner` and `Theta/Real/Metaplectic/Smooth`.
