# Cyclic local degree coverage and conditional invariant descent

Let `E/F` be a Galois extension of number fields in a common arbitrary
universe `u`, with cyclic Galois group. This module supplies the algebraic
reduction from the explicitly assumed
`Langlands.Global.CyclicPrimePowerLocalDegreeStatement.{u,u}` to local degree
coverage for `E/F`, and uses that coverage in a conditional idele-class
invariant construction. It does not prove the assumed arithmetic statement.

For a natural number `n`, `cyclicIntermediateFieldOfDegree F E n` is the
fixed field of the subgroup generated by `g ^ n`, where `g` is a chosen
generator of the finite cyclic Galois group. The definition is available
for every `n`. The module installs number-field, Galois-over-`F`, and cyclic
Galois-group instances for this intermediate field. The Galois assertion
uses normality of every subgroup of the commutative Galois group; the
cyclic assertion uses surjectivity of restriction of automorphisms.

For positive `n` dividing `[E:F]`,
`finrank_cyclicIntermediateFieldOfDegree` proves that the intermediate
field has degree `n` over `F`. The chosen generator has order `[E:F]`, so
the subgroup generated by `g ^ n` has order `[E:F] / n`. The fixed-field
index theorem gives that same degree for `E` over the intermediate field.
Degree multiplicativity and cancellation by the positive quotient then
give the claimed degree over `F`. This is the subgroup argument already
used in the published `CyclotomicAuxiliary.exists_cyclic_extension`,
extracted here for an arbitrary supplied cyclic Galois extension; no
cyclotomic construction is performed by this module.

The theorem `primePowerLocalDegreeCoverage_of_cyclic` takes the prime-power
arithmetic statement as an explicit argument and an arbitrary family `wf`
of chosen finite places above the places of `F`. Given a prime power `p ^ k`
with `k > 0` dividing `[E:F]`, it applies that statement to the intermediate
field of degree `p ^ k`, using the empty set of excluded places. It obtains
a finite place with local degree `p ^ k` in that intermediate extension.
A prime above it in `E` exists by the published lying-over result. Completed
degrees divide upward in the tower, so `p ^ k` divides the local degree in
`E`. Finally, the Galois equality of ramification indices and inertia
degrees above a fixed base prime, together with `localDegree_eq`, identifies
that degree with the degree at the supplied choice `wf v`. Thus the
conclusion is exactly `PrimePowerLocalDegreeCoverage wf`, for arbitrary
choices. The places may depend on the prime power, and no single place of
full degree for an arbitrary cyclic extension is asserted.

The definition `cyclicIdeleClassInvariantEquiv` additionally takes the local
invariant family `I` with `RelativeImages`, finite and infinite choices
`wf` and `wi`, reciprocity for every field-unit degree-two cohomology class,
an explicit `Finite` instance on the actual idele-class degree-two
cohomology group, and its cardinality bound by `[E:F]`. It returns an
additive equivalence from `relativeIdeleClassCohomology F E 2` to
`denominatorSubgroup (Module.finrank F E)`. It is the published conditional
prime-power descent construction applied to the coverage just proved.
The evaluation theorem identifies its value on the quotient of an idele
cohomology class with the denominator-valued total idele invariant.

The provenance is internal: the proof combines the fixed-field cardinality
argument, restriction of automorphisms, actual local degree tower and
Galois equal-degree results, and the conditional prime-power descent API.
The general prime-power arithmetic statement, reciprocity, cohomology
finiteness and the order bound remain explicit hypotheses. The module does
not construct an unconditional global invariant family or prove the four
laws of `GlobalInvariantStatement`.
