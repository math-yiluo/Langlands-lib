# Unramified cyclotomic completions and the Frobenius of the Artin symbol (proof)

This module proves the Library proposition
`Langlands.ClassField.Global.UnramifiedCyclotomic.UnramifiedCyclotomicFrobenius` from
`Langlands.ClassField.Global.UnramifiedCyclotomicStatement`, as the theorem

```lean
theorem unramifiedCyclotomicFrobenius : UnramifiedCyclotomicFrobenius.{u}
```

It is the unramified input to theorem `relative_cyclicClass` in
`Langlands.ClassField.Global.LocalSymbolIdentity`, which is used in the proof of global
class field theory (task fb66219f). The statement module is unchanged.

All declarations are in the namespace `Langlands.ClassField.Global.UnramifiedCyclotomic`.
The only new definitions are `towerField` (with its three instances), `integersEquiv` and
`stabilizerRoot`. Everything else is a theorem.

## Mathematical content

Let `F_v` have residue field `𝔽_q`, `q = p^f`, and let `N` be prime to `p`. Let
`ζ_N = ζ^{M/N}`, `U = F(ζ_N)` and `U_w` its completion.

1. `U_w / F_v` is unramified. Its Galois group is cyclic of order the order of `q` modulo
   `N`, and is generated by the Frobenius `ζ_N ↦ ζ_N^q`. This is Neukirch, *Algebraic
   Number Theory*, Chapter II §7, Proposition (7.12), printed p.158 (PDF p.173). The proof
   below does not use the minimal polynomial argument given there. Distinct `N`-th roots of
   unity stay distinct modulo the maximal ideal, so an automorphism acting trivially on the
   residue field fixes `ζ_N`. Since `Gal(U_w/F_v)` acts faithfully on `ζ_N`, the residue map
   on `Gal(U_w/F_v)` is injective.
2. Let `π ∈ F_vˣ` have order `1`. The Artin symbol of `π` acts on roots of unity of order
   prime to `p` through `p^{v_p(N_{F_v/ℚ_p} π)} = p^f = q`. Artin–Tate, *Class Field
   Theory*, Chapter VII §2, proof of Theorem 5, printed p.41 (PDF p.50), uses the same
   fact: the explicit symbol of an element of ordinal `1` acts as the Frobenius on every
   unramified field.
3. An element of `Gal(U/F)` is determined by its action on `ζ_N`, so the Frobenius and the
   restricted Artin symbol agree.

The provenance is derived. The steps through the Library's `PadicField` coordinate
predicate, the spectral integers and the Artin symbol of `LocalSymbolIdentity` are
internal.

## Sections and declarations

### `RootsOfUnity`

- `eq_of_pow_eq_one_of_residue_eq`: in a local domain where `N` is a unit, two `N`-th roots
  of unity with the same residue are equal. If `α ≠ β`, then
  `∑ αⁱ β^{N-1-i} = 0`, while its residue is `N · ᾱ^{N-1} ≠ 0`.

### `LocalCoordinate`

Here `F : PadicField p` and `L` is a finite extension of `F.Carrier`. The section repeats the
local-instance block of the Library module `UnramifiedArithmeticProof`: the canonical
normed field on `F.Carrier` and its spectral norm.

- `mem_extensionIntegers_of_pow_eq_one`, `isUnit_natCast_extensionIntegers`: roots of
  unity are spectral integers. If `N ≠ 0` in the base residue field, `N` is a unit in the
  spectral integers of `L`.
- `apply_eq_of_residueHom_eq_one`: if `σ` acts on the residue field as `x ↦ xⁿ`, then
  `σ α = αⁿ` for every `N`-th root of unity `α` with `N` prime to `p`. This uses
  `integerAut` and `eq_of_pow_eq_one_of_residue_eq`.
- `residueHom_injective_of_root` (for `L / F.Carrier` Galois): if `Gal(L/F.Carrier)` acts
  faithfully on such an `α`, the Library's `residueHom` is injective.
- `towerField F L : PadicField p`, with carrier `L` and `ℚ_p`-algebra structure through
  `F.Carrier`, together with the instances `towerField.instAlgebra`,
  `towerField.instFiniteDimensional` and `towerField.instIsScalarTower`. This lets
  `extensionResidue_finite` reuse the Library's `ArithmeticCoordinate.extensionResidueFinite`.
- `exists_frobenius_generator`: if the residue map is injective, the preimage `φ` of the
  residue Frobenius (Mathlib's `FiniteField.frobeniusAlgEquivOfAlgebraic`) generates
  `Gal(L/F.Carrier)` and acts on the residue field by `x ↦ x^{|k_F|}`.
- `exists_coordinate_of_generator`: Mathlib's `zmodMulEquivOfGenerator` turns `φ` into a
  coordinate `χ : Gal(L/F.Carrier) ≃* Multiplicative (ZMod e)` with `χ⁻¹(1) = φ`, where
  `e = |Gal(L/F.Carrier)|`. This coordinate satisfies `IsArithmeticUnramifiedCoordinate`.
- `integersEquiv O hO`: a valuation subring `O` whose members are exactly the elements of
  canonical norm `≤ 1` is identified with `baseIntegers F.Carrier`.
- `exists_frobenius_coordinate`: let `O` be as above, `N ≠ 0` in the residue field of `O`,
  and `α` an `N`-th root of unity on which `Gal(L/F.Carrier)` acts faithfully. Then some
  `φ` generates the group, `φ α = α^{|O/𝔪|}`, and for every `e ≠ 0` with
  `|Gal(L/F.Carrier)| = e` there is an arithmetic unramified coordinate `χ` onto `ZMod e`
  with `χ⁻¹(1) = φ`.
- `card_residueField_eq_pow`, `natCast_residueField_eq_zero`,
  `coprime_of_natCast_ne_zero`: the residue field of such an `O` has `p^f` elements and
  characteristic `p`. Any `N ≠ 0` in it is prime to `p`. Here `f` is the residue degree of
  `F.canonicalTopologicalData`.

### `Stabilizer`

- `pow_eq_pow_iff_natCast_eq`: for a primitive `N`-th root `ξ`, `ξ^a = ξ^b` iff
  `a ≡ b` in `ZMod N`.
- `stabilizerRoot ζ k : stabilizerField ζ k` is the element `ζ^k`, and
  `algebraMap_stabilizerRoot` states that its image in `L` is `ζ^k`.
- `isPrimitiveRoot_stabilizerRoot`: for `N ∣ M`, `ζ^{M/N}` is a primitive `N`-th root of
  unity in `stabilizerField ζ (M/N)`.
- `restrictNormal_stabilizerRoot`: `σ|_{F(ζ^k)}` sends `ζ^k` to `(ζ^k)^{a}`, where `a` is the
  value of `hζ.autToPow F σ`.
- `eq_one_of_apply_stabilizerRoot`, `eq_of_apply_stabilizerRoot_eq`,
  `autToPow_stabilizerRoot_injective`: when `rootStabilizer k` is normal, `Gal(F(ζ^k)/F)`
  acts faithfully on `ζ^k`. These use `AlgEquiv.restrictNormalHom_surjective` and the
  Galois correspondence (`restrictNormalHom_ker`, `fixingSubgroup_fixedField`). Hence the
  cyclotomic character of `F(ζ^k)` is injective.

### `NormValuation`

- `valuation_localNorm`: if `π ∈ F_vˣ` has order `1`, the `p`-adic valuation of its local
  norm to `ℚ_p` is the residue degree `f` of `padicField v`. It combines the Library's
  `residueDegree_mul_ord_relative_norm` over `ℚ_p` with
  `indices_eq_one_of_degree_one` and `ord_algebraMap`. `Algebra.norm_eq_of_ringEquiv`
  identifies `FiniteExtension.localNorm`, transported by `padicEquiv`, with the algebra norm
  of `padicAlgebra v`.

### `Artin`

- `castHom_autToPow_artinSymbol`: let `N ∣ M` and `N ∣ M / p^{v_p(M)}`. Then for `π` of
  order `1`, the cyclotomic character of `artinSymbol hζ hinj v π` reduced modulo `N` is
  `p^f`. The proof unfolds the Library's `autToPow_artinSymbol`, `normSymbolAt_apply`,
  `localSymbol_apply` and `castHom_crtUnits_snd`, then applies `valuation_localNorm`.

### `Main`

`unramifiedCyclotomicFrobenius`. Given the data of the statement, put
`U = stabilizerField ζ (M/N)`, `w = stabilizerPlace ζ (M/N) u`,
`ζ_N = stabilizerRoot ζ (M/N)` and `α` the image of `ζ_N` in `U_w`.

1. `U_w / F_v` is Galois (Library `isGalois_adicCompletion`). It acts faithfully on `α`:
   `decompositionHom_injective`, `eq_one_of_apply_stabilizerRoot` and
   `algebraMap_decompositionHom`.
2. `(N : 𝓞 F) ∉ v` gives `N ≠ 0` in the residue field of `v.adicCompletionIntegers F`
   (Library `adicResidueEquiv`). That residue field also has `q = |𝓞 F / v|` elements.
3. `exists_frobenius_coordinate` is applied with `O = v.adicCompletionIntegers F`; the
   condition on `O` is the Library's `mem_adicCompletionIntegers_iff`. It gives a
   generator `φ` with `φ α = α^q` and, for `e = |Gal(U_w/F_v)|`, a coordinate `χ_U` with
   `χ_U⁻¹(1) = φ`.
4. Put `ψ = autToPow ∘ decompositionHom : Gal(U_w/F_v) → (ZMod N)ˣ`. It is injective and
   `ψ φ = q`, so `orderOf (q mod N) = orderOf φ = |Gal(U_w/F_v)| = e`. This uses
   `orderOf_injective` and `orderOf_eq_card_of_forall_mem_zpowers`.
5. For `π` of order `1`, both `decompositionHom φ` and `(artinSymbol hζ hinj v π)|_U` send
   `ζ_N` to `ζ_N^q`. For the Artin symbol this follows from `castHom_autToPow_artinSymbol`
   and `card_residueField_eq_pow`. The divisibility `N ∣ M / p^{v_p(M)}` holds because `N`
   is prime to `p` (`Nat.ordProj_mul_ordCompl_eq_self`). Then
   `eq_of_apply_stabilizerRoot_eq` concludes.
