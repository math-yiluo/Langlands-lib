# Finiteness of the residual idele-class quotient

The theorem `Langlands.ClassField.Global.integralIdeleClassQuotientFinite`
proves `IntegralIdeleClassQuotientFinite.{u}` for every number field in
arbitrary universe `u`. It uses the actual double quotient `C_E/C∞` and
requires no extension or Galois hypothesis.

The private homomorphism `ideleFractionalIdeal` composes the published
idele divisor homomorphism with the prime-product equivalence. Equality
of all prime multiplicities, using `principalIdeleDivisorFormula`,
identifies its value on a principal idele with `toPrincipalIdeal`.
The divisor kernel criterion shows its kernel consists of exactly the
integral ideles.

The map to fractional ideals modulo principal ideals kills principal
ideles, so `QuotientGroup.lift` defines `ideleClassIdealClass` on the
actual idele class group. Its kernel is exactly `integralIdeleClasses E`.
For the nontrivial inclusion, an idele with trivial ideal class has
fractional ideal generated by a field unit `a`. Dividing by the principal
idele of `a` gives an integral idele representing the same idele class.
The reverse inclusion follows directly from the fractional-ideal kernel
criterion and the definition of the image subgroup.

A second quotient lift therefore defines an injective map from `C_E/C∞`
to fractional ideals modulo principal ideals; injectivity is
`QuotientGroup.injective_lift_iff`. Mathlib's `ClassGroup.equiv E`
identifies the target with `ClassGroup (𝓞 E)`, which is finite by the
number-field class-number theorem. `Finite.of_injective` completes the
proof. Surjectivity or an isomorphism from `C_E/C∞` is not needed.

This implements the finiteness assertion derived from Artin–Tate,
*Class Field Theory* (2009), V §3, printed p.13/PDF22, by taking all
infinite places. Registered source `artin-tate-2009-class-field-theory`,
SHA256 `af7ba934904a3c10012e6fd326cea493ffb6200d885d97583277bb45f935c3dd`.
No Herbrand formula is proved here.
