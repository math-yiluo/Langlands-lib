# Unramified cyclotomic completions and the Frobenius of the Artin symbol

This module states the unramified input of the local identity proved in
`Langlands.ClassField.Global.LocalSymbolIdentity` (reciprocity proof, task fb66219f).
Theorem `relative_cyclicClass` there takes as hypotheses an unramified subfield `U` of a
cyclotomic extension, an arithmetic coordinate of its completion, and the action of the
local Artin symbol on `U`. The proposition `UnramifiedCyclotomicFrobenius` below asserts
that these data exist for `U = F(ζ^{M/N})` whenever `N` is prime to the residue
characteristic. This module states it and proves nothing about it.

All declarations are in the namespace `Langlands.ClassField.Global.UnramifiedCyclotomic`.

## Definitions

- `rootStabilizer k : Subgroup (L ≃ₐ[F] L)` is the subgroup of automorphisms fixing `ζ ^ k`.
  `ζ` is an implicit argument.
- `stabilizerField ζ k := IntermediateField.fixedField (rootStabilizer k)`. When `ζ`
  generates `L / F`, this is `F(ζ ^ k)`.
- `stabilizerPlace ζ k u` is the place of `stabilizerField ζ k` below the place `u` of `L`.
  It is the Library's `fixedFieldPlace`.

## The statement

`UnramifiedCyclotomicFrobenius : Prop` (universe `u`) quantifies over the following data:

- number fields `F, L : Type u` with `L / F` Galois;
- `M ≠ 0` and a primitive `M`-th root of unity `ζ ∈ L` such that
  `autToPow F : Gal(L/F) → (ℤ/M)ˣ` is injective;
- a finite place `v` of `F` and a place `u` of `L` above it;
- `N ≠ 0` with `N ∣ M` and `(N : 𝓞 F) ∉ v`, i.e. `N` prime to the residue characteristic;
- a proof that `H = rootStabilizer (M / N)` is normal, so that `U = stabilizerField ζ (M/N)`
  is Galois over `F`.

For these data it asserts the existence of:

1. `e ≠ 0` with `e = orderOf (q mod N)`, where `q = |𝓞 F / v|`;
2. a group isomorphism `χ_U : Gal(U_w/F_v) ≃* Multiplicative (ZMod e)`, where `U_w` is the
   completion of `U` at `stabilizerPlace ζ (M/N) u`, such that
   `IsArithmeticUnramifiedCoordinate (padicField v) U_w χ_U` holds. This is the Library's
   condition: the residue map on `Gal(U_w/F_v)` is injective (`U_w / F_v` is unramified),
   and `χ_U⁻¹(1)` acts on the residue field as `x ↦ x^q`;
3. for every `π ∈ F_vˣ` with `ord π = 1` (the canonical order of `padicField v`):
   `decompositionHom v (stabilizerPlace ζ (M/N) u) (χ_U⁻¹(1)) = (artinSymbol hζ hinj v π)|_U`.
   Here `artinSymbol` is the local Artin symbol of `LocalSymbolIdentity`.

## Mathematical content and provenance

1. Since `N` is prime to `p`, `U_w = F_v(ζ_N)` with `ζ_N = ζ^{M/N}` is unramified over `F_v`.
   Its residue field is `k_v(ζ̄_N)`, of degree the order of `q` modulo `N`. Its Galois group
   is cyclic, generated by the arithmetic Frobenius, which gives `χ_U`.
2. Let `π` have order `1`. The cyclotomic symbol of `N_{F_v/ℚ_p}(π)` is
   `p^{v_p(N π)} = p^f = q` modulo the prime-to-`p` part of `M`, hence modulo `N`. So the
   Artin symbol sends `ζ_N` to `ζ_N^q`.
3. The Frobenius also sends `ζ_N` to `ζ_N^q`, because distinct `N`-th roots of unity stay
   distinct modulo the maximal ideal. Elements of `Gal(U/F)` are determined by their action
   on `ζ_N`.

See Neukirch, *Algebraic Number Theory*, Chapter II §7, Proposition (7.12), printed p.158
(PDF p.173): for `(n, p) = 1`, `K(ζ_n) / K` is unramified of degree the order `f` of `q`
modulo `n`, and its Galois group is generated by `ζ ↦ ζ^q`. See also Artin–Tate, Chapter VII §2, the proof of Theorem 5
(printed p.41, PDF p.50): an element of ordinal `1` acts as the Frobenius on unramified
fields. Provenance is derived. The formulation through the Library's `PadicField`
coordinate predicate and the Artin symbol of `LocalSymbolIdentity` is internal.
