# The global fundamental classes from the global invariant maps

This module `Langlands.ClassField.Global.FundamentalClassReduction` reduces the published
`Langlands.ClassField.Global.GlobalFundamentalClassStatement` to two published `Prop`s:

- `FundamentalClass.IdeleClassH1Vanishing` (module
  `Langlands.ClassField.Global.FundamentalClassStatement`: `H¹(Gal(E/F), C_E) = 0`);
- `GlobalInvariantStatement` (module `Langlands.ClassField.Global.InvariantStatement`:
  for every local family `I` satisfying `RelativeClassFormation` and
  `ArithmeticNormalization` there are global invariant maps
  `inv_{E/F} : H²(Gal(E/F), C_E) → ℚ/ℤ` with `IsGlobalInvariant I inv`).

Neither input is proved here; both are explicit hypotheses. The other arithmetic inputs are
`Langlands.Global.exists_galois_ramified` (module
`Langlands.Global.Arithmetic.CyclotomicAuxiliary`, proved there) and the local-degree
lemmas of `Langlands.Global.AdelicFunctoriality.LocalDegree` (see below). Everything is
for number fields in one universe `u`, as in the target statement.

## Main results

- `fundamentalClass_exists_of (hinv : GlobalInvariantStatement.{u}) I hI`: there is a
  `c : ClassFamily.{u}` with `Generates c ∧ Restriction c ∧ Inflation c ∧
  LocalCompatibility I c`.
- `fundamentalClass_unique_of (hinv : GlobalInvariantStatement.{u}) I hI hc hc'`: two
  families `c, c'` that both satisfy `Inflation` and `LocalCompatibility I` are equal.
- `globalFundamentalClassStatement_of (hH1 : IdeleClassH1Vanishing.{u})
  (hinv : GlobalInvariantStatement.{u}) : GlobalFundamentalClassStatement.{u}`: the first
  conjunct is `hH1`; the unique existence combines the two previous theorems.

The hypotheses `hI` are the target's `∀ p [Fact p.Prime], (I p).RelativeClassFormation ∧
(I p).ArithmeticNormalization`. These theorems use only the axioms `propext`,
`Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. Once proofs of the two inputs are published, the
unconditional target is `globalFundamentalClassStatement_of` applied to them.

The theorems for a fixed `inv` with `IsGlobalInvariant I inv` are
`exists_of_isGlobalInvariant` and `unique_of_isGlobalInvariant` (namespace
`FundamentalClass`); the three theorems above obtain such an `inv` from `hinv I hI`.

## Arithmetic of `ℚ/ℤ`

`RationalModIntegers` is the published `ℚ ⧸ zmultiples 1`, with `rationalClass` and
`reciprocalClass n = rationalClass (1/n)`. The lemmas `nsmul_reciprocalClass_mul`
(`k • (1/(k m)) = 1/m` for `k ≠ 0`), `nsmul_reciprocalClass_self`,
`addOrderOf_reciprocalClass` (`1/n` has order `n`) and `mem_zmultiples_reciprocalClass`
(`denominatorSubgroup n` is generated by `1/n`) are proved directly from the quotient.

## Local degrees

The facts about local degrees and Galois completions come from the module
`Langlands.Global.AdelicFunctoriality.LocalDegree`:
- `localDegree_eq`: `[E_w : F_v] = e(w|v) f(w|v)`.
- `localDegree_dvd`: for Galois `E / F`, `[E_w : F_v]` divides `[E : F]`.
- `localDegree_dvd_localDegree`: for `F ⊆ K ⊆ L` and `w | w' | v`, `[K_{w'} : F_v]` divides
  `[L_w : F_v]`.
- `isGalois_adicCompletion`: if `E / F` is Galois, so is `E_w / F_v`.
- `exists_place_above`: every finite place lies under a finite place of a finite
  extension.

This module adds one lemma of its own:
- `exists_galois_tower`: two Galois extensions `E, K` of `F` lie in a common Galois
  extension `L` in the same universe, namely the compositum of their images in the
  algebraic closure of `E` (Mathlib's `IntermediateField.normal_sup`; separability holds in
  characteristic zero).

## Existence

From `IsGlobalInvariant I inv`, `invariantClass` chooses `u_{E/F}` with
`inv_{E/F} u_{E/F} = 1/[E : F]` (it exists since the range of `inv_{E/F}` is
`denominatorSubgroup [E : F]`).

- `Generates`: `inv` is injective, so `u_{E/F}` has the order of `1/[E:F]`, namely `[E : F]`,
  and every class is an integer multiple of `u_{E/F}`.
- `Restriction`: by the "if" direction of `InvariantRestriction`, since
  `[F' : F] • 1/[E : F] = 1/[E : F']` (`[E : F] = [F' : F] [E : F']`).
- `Inflation`: by the "if" direction of `InvariantInflation` with `m = [L : E]`, since
  `[L : E] • 1/[L : F] = 1/[E : F]`.
- `LocalCompatibility`: by the "if" direction of `InvariantLocal`, since
  `([E : F]/[E_w : F_v]) • 1/[E : F] = 1/[E_w : F_v]` (`localDegree_dvd`), which is the
  invariant of a local fundamental class.

This is `exists_of_isGlobalInvariant`; `invariantClass` is the definition named for this
module.

## Uniqueness

`unique_of_isGlobalInvariant` uses only `Inflation` and `LocalCompatibility` of the two
families `c, c'`. Fix `E / F` of degree `n`. Take a Galois `K / F` with a finite place
`w₀ | v₀` whose ramification index `e(w₀|v₀)` is divisible by `n`
(`exists_galois_ramified`), and a Galois `L ⊇ E, K` (`exists_galois_tower`),
`N = [L : F]`. Choose representatives `x, x'` of `c F E, c' F E` and `y, y'` of `c F L,
c' F L`.

- By `Inflation` and the "only if" direction of `InvariantInflation` (with `m = [L : E]`),
  `[L : E] • inv[y] = inv[x]` and `[L : E] • inv[y'] = inv[x']`.
- At every finite place `w | v` of `L / F` there is a local fundamental class `z`
  (`RelativeImages` of `I`; `E_w / F_v` is Galois by `isGalois_adicCompletion`). By
  `LocalCompatibility` and the "only if" direction of `InvariantLocal`,
  `(N/[L_w : F_v]) • inv[y] = 1/[L_w : F_v] = (N/[L_w : F_v]) • inv[y']`
  (`nsmul_inv_eq_of_localCompatibility`). So `δ = inv[y] - inv[y']` is killed by
  `N/[L_w : F_v]`; with `e` the order of `δ` (which divides `N`), every `[L_w : F_v]`
  divides `N/e`.
- Take a finite place `w` of `L` above `w₀` (`exists_place_above`). Then `n` divides
  `e(w₀|v₀)`, which divides `e(w₀|v₀) f(w₀|v₀) = [K_{w₀} : F_{v₀}]` (`localDegree_eq`),
  which divides `[L_w : F_{v₀}]` (`localDegree_dvd_localDegree`), which divides `N/e`. So
  `n` divides `N/e`. Since `N = n [L : E]`, `e` divides `[L : E]`, so `[L : E] • δ = 0`.
- Hence `inv[x] = inv[x']`, and `c F E = c' F E` by injectivity.

This is the argument in the Markdown of `FundamentalClassStatement`, except that Artin–Tate
use a cyclic `K / F` of degree `n` and the fact that its local degrees have least common
multiple `n`. Here a single place of `K` with local degree divisible by `n` suffices, and
cyclicity is not needed.

## Provenance

Derived; the mathematics is that of the sources cited by `FundamentalClassStatement`
(Artin–Tate VII §3, Lemmas 1–6 and Theorem 7, printed pp.41–50, and the uniqueness
argument there; `artin-tate-2009-class-field-theory`), with the formalization-specific
local-degree and compositum lemmas above. The replacement of the cyclic extension by a
ramified cyclotomic one in the uniqueness step is internal to this formalization.
