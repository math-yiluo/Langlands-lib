# Norm factorizations from compatible torsion primitives

Take a finite separable extension `B/F` embedded over `F` in a finite
Galois extension `L/F`. For two cochains on `Gal(L/F)` with values in
`(B ⊗[F] L)ˣ` of `n`-th power one, assume their degree-two differentials
agree. Assume the second cochain has determinant norm one. If the norm
of the first is the scalar Kummer cocycle of a radical `z` with
`z^n = s` for a base unit `s`, the statement asks for a factorization
of `s` as a norm from `B` times an `n`-th power in `F`.

In characteristic zero the quotient of the two cochains is a crossed
cocycle and tensor-unit Hilbert 90 expresses it as the coboundary of
a unit `x`. The unit `x^n` is fixed by all coefficient automorphisms.
Fixed-tensor descent expresses it as `y ⊗ 1` for a unit `y` of `B`.
Taking norms of the quotient shows that `z / N(x)` is a fixed scalar
unit, so it comes from a unit `q` of `F`. Taking `n`-th powers in
`z = N(x) q` gives `s = N(y) q^n` after reflecting the equality through
the field embedding. This is an internal comparison used to recover
the local norm condition in the adjoint norm-kernel case.
