# Properties of morphisms local on the target in the fppf topology

Authors: Worker 1001.

Internal infrastructure for the proof of
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.EtaleEquivalenceRelationAlgebraicSpaceQuotientStatement`.
In the proof of Lemma 65.10.3 (Tag [02WV](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02WV),
retained as `stacks-etale-quotient-effective-fppf-descent-2026-11-01`) the Stacks Project
checks that a morphism is étale after base change to the members of an fppf covering. It
cites Remark 65.4.3, which says that such properties are local on the base in the fppf
topology. Mathlib has the descent of a property along a single fppf morphism
(`MorphismProperty.DescendsAlong`), but has no locality statement for the fppf precoverage
or for fppf covering sieves. This module derives both. The declarations are in the namespace
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.FppfLocal`.

Throughout, `P : MorphismProperty Scheme.{u}` satisfies three instance hypotheses:
`IsZariskiLocalAtTarget P`, `P.IsStableUnderBaseChange`, and
`P.DescendsAlong (@Surjective ⊓ @Flat ⊓ @LocallyOfFinitePresentation)`. For example, `@Etale`
satisfies all three. Mathlib proves fpqc descent for it in
`Mathlib.AlgebraicGeometry.Morphisms.LocalFlatDescent`, and an instance in
`Mathlib.AlgebraicGeometry.Morphisms.FlatDescent` turns fpqc descent of a Zariski-local
property into fppf descent.

## Results

- `isLocalAtTarget_fppfPrecoverage P : P.IsLocalAtTarget Scheme.fppfPrecoverage`. A morphism
  `f : X ⟶ Y` has `P` if and only if all its base changes along the members of an fppf
  covering family of `Y` do. One direction is stability under base change. For the other,
  the proof uses Mathlib's `IsLocalAtTarget.mk_of_small` and the single morphism
  `g = Sigma.desc 𝒰.f` from the disjoint union of the cover. `g` is flat and locally of
  finite presentation because these properties are Zariski local on the source
  (`IsZariskiLocalAtSource.sigmaDesc`). It is surjective because the family is jointly
  surjective. By `MorphismProperty.of_pullback_snd_of_descendsAlong` it suffices that the
  base change of `f` along `g` has `P`. This is checked on the open cover of the disjoint
  union by its summands (`Scheme.sigmaOpenCover`, `IsZariskiLocalAtTarget.of_openCover`).
  Over the summand `i` that base change is, by pasting pullbacks, isomorphic to the base
  change of `f` along `𝒰.f i`.
- `of_fppfTopology_sieve P p J hJ h`: let `p : V ⟶ T` be a morphism of schemes and `J` a
  covering sieve of `T` for `Scheme.fppfTopology`. Suppose that for every `f : Y ⟶ T` in
  `J` there exist a scheme `W` and morphisms `fst : W ⟶ V`, `snd : W ⟶ Y` with
  `IsPullback fst snd p f` and `P snd`. Then `P p`. A covering sieve of the topology
  generated by the fppf precoverage contains an fppf covering family
  (`Precoverage.mem_toGrothendieck_iff_of_isStableUnderComposition`). The previous result
  applies to that family, and the given pullback is identified with the chosen one
  (`IsPullback.isoPullback_hom_snd`, `cancel_left_of_respectsIso`).
