# Restricting neighbourhood sections to a fibre

This internal interface defines the canonical restriction of an actual
module-sheaf section over `f ⁻¹ᵁ V` to the scheme-theoretic residue-field
fibre at `s ∈ V`. It uses the unit of the actual module-sheaf
pullback–pushforward adjunction, followed by restriction to the whole
fibre. It does not replace the fibre by its reduction or by a geometric
fibre.

The containment lemma follows from the range description of the actual
fibre inclusion. The compatibility lemma is naturality of the adjunction
unit and composition of sheaf restriction maps. These are derived
implementation facts for the proper-fibre ampleness spreading proof;
they assert no section-lifting or ampleness theorem.
