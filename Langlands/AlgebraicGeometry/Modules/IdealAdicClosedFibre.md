# Ideal quotients as pushforwards from the closed subscheme

Let `f : X → Spec R` be a morphism of schemes and `I` an ideal of `R`.
Suppose `i : Z → X` and `g : Z → Spec (R/I)` form a pullback square with `f`
and `Spec (R → R/I)`. The hypothesis `hpb` is an actual `IsPullback` square.
For every quasi-coherent module sheaf `M` on `X`, this module shows that the
ideal quotient `M/I M` of `IdealAdicQuotients` is isomorphic to `i_* i^* M`.
The isomorphism is the canonical map induced by the pullback-pushforward unit.

## Declarations

- `quotientRingHom I` is the quotient map `R → R/I` in `CommRingCat`.
- `structureRingHom_eq_zero_of_isPullback`: the structure-ring map of
  `i ≫ f` sends every element of `I` to zero. Its proof factors this map
  through `R/I` using the pullback square.
- `isClosedImmersion_of_isPullback`: `i` is a closed immersion, as a base
  change of the closed immersion `Spec (R/I) → Spec R`.
- `ker_app_le_map_scalar`: over an affine open `U` of `X`, the kernel of the
  ring map `Γ(X, U) → Γ(Z, i⁻¹U)` is contained in the ideal generated by the
  image of `I`. The proof applies the universal property of the pullback to
  `Spec (Γ(X, U)/I Γ(X, U)) → X`. That morphism factors through `Z`, and so
  every element of the kernel vanishes in `Γ(X, U)/I Γ(X, U)`.
- `mem_ker_smul_top_of_one_tmul_eq_zero`: for a surjective ring map
  `φ : B → C` and a `B`-module `N`, if `1 ⊗ m` is zero in `C ⊗_B N`, then
  `m ∈ (ker φ) N`. The proof uses right exactness of the tensor product.
- `sectionPullback_surjective_of_isPullback`: over an affine open `U`, every
  section of `i^* M` over `i⁻¹U` is the pullback of a section of `M` over
  `U`. The proof uses the affine tensor description of pullback sections
  from `PullbackAffineSections` and the surjectivity of the closed-immersion
  ring map.
- `mem_map_scalar_smul_top_of_sectionPullback_eq_zero`: over an affine open
  `U`, a section of `M` with zero pullback lies in `I Γ(X, U) · Γ(M, U)`.
  This combines the affine tensor description, the tensor lemma above and the
  kernel bound.
- `app_eq_zero_of_mem_map_scalar_smul_top`: a morphism `ψ : M → N` whose
  composite with the inclusion `I M → M` is zero kills every section in
  `I Γ(X, U) · Γ(M, U)`, for every open `U`. This uses the generator criterion
  of `IdealAdicQuotientDescent`.
- `app_eq_zero_of_sectionPullback_eq_zero`: such a morphism `ψ` kills every
  section of `M`, over any open, whose pullback to `Z` vanishes. The proof
  restricts the section to affine opens and uses the two preceding lemmas.
- `epi_unit_of_isPullback`: the unit `M → i_* i^* M` is an epimorphism. It
  is locally surjective on sections by the affine surjectivity lemma.
- `kernel_ι_unit_projection`: the kernel of this unit maps to zero in
  `M/I M`.
- `isIso_pullbackUnitDesc_of_isPullback`: the map `pullbackUnitDesc` from
  `IdealAdicPullbackUnit`, with the annihilation hypothesis above, is an
  isomorphism. Because the unit is an epimorphism in an abelian category, it
  is the cokernel of its kernel. The quotient projection then descends through
  it, and that map is the inverse.
- `closedFibreIso hpb M : quotient f I M ≅ i_* i^* M` is that isomorphism.
  `projection_closedFibreIso_hom` states that the quotient projection followed
  by it is the pullback-pushforward unit.

## Closed fibre over a local base

In section `ClosedPoint`, `R` is a local ring with maximal ideal `𝔪`.

- `toClosedPointResidueField R` is the composite of the inverse of
  `stalkClosedPointIso R` with the residue map. This is a ring map from `R`
  to the residue field at the closed point of `Spec R`.
  `toClosedPointResidueField_surjective` and `ker_toClosedPointResidueField`
  state that it is surjective with kernel `𝔪`.
- `residueFieldClosedPointIso R : R/𝔪 ≅ κ(closedPoint)` is the induced ring
  isomorphism. `quotientRingHom_residueFieldClosedPointIso_hom` states that
  the quotient map followed by it is `toClosedPointResidueField R`.
- `fromSpecResidueField_closedPoint` writes the canonical morphism
  `Spec κ(closedPoint) → Spec R` as `Spec` of this isomorphism followed by
  `Spec (R → R/𝔪)`.
- `isPullback_fiberι_closedPoint f` states that the fibre inclusion
  `f.fiberι (closedPoint R)` and the map from the fibre to `Spec (R/𝔪)` form a
  pullback square with `f` and `Spec (R → R/𝔪)`. Here that map is
  `f.fiberToSpecResidueField` followed by `Spec` of the residue-field
  isomorphism. Hence `closedFibreIso` applies to the actual closed fibre of
  `f` with `I = 𝔪`.

## Provenance and scope

These are internal constructions and proofs from Mathlib's scheme-module
pullback, pushforward and adjunction, and from the Library modules
`IdealAdicQuotients`, `IdealAdicQuotientDescent`, `IdealAdicPullbackUnit`,
`PullbackAffineSections`, `OpenCohomologyVanishing` and
`ClosedImmersionHigherDirectImageVanishingProof`. They give the standard
identification of `M/I M` with the pushforward of the restriction of `M` to
the closed subscheme cut out by `I`. The proof of Stacks Project Lemma
30.21.3 (tag 02OF), the source of the host task, uses this identification when
it says that the fibre `X_y` is the closed subscheme cut out by `𝔪 O_X`, with
`F_y = F/𝔪 F`. The module proves it under the hypotheses displayed above: the
closed subscheme is given as a base change of `Spec (R/I) → Spec R` for an
arbitrary ideal `I`, and `M` is quasi-coherent. No flatness, properness,
coherence or noetherian hypothesis is used. The module does not identify
other subquotients such as `𝔪ⁿ M/𝔪ⁿ⁺¹ M` with modules on the fibre.
