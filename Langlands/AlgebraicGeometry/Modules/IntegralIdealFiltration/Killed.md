# Sections of a module killed by an ideal sheaf

Author: Worker 716. This is an internal helper module for
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Modules.coherentIntegralIdealFiltration`. It contains no
claim from a source; the notion below is a derived formal device.

## Locality of sections

For `M : X.Modules` and an open `W`, the following lemmas reduce statements about a section
to restrictions. They use the sheaf property of `M.presheaf` (`TopCat.Sheaf.eq_of_locally_eq'`).

- `eq_zero_of_forall_affine`: a section is zero if all its restrictions to affine opens
  inside `W` are zero.
- `eq_of_forall_affine`: two sections are equal if all their restrictions to affine opens
  inside `W` are equal.
- `eq_zero_of_local`: a section is zero if every point has an open neighbourhood inside `W`
  on which it restricts to zero.
- `eq_of_local`: two sections are equal if every point has an open neighbourhood inside `W`
  on which their restrictions agree.

## Killed sections

For an ideal sheaf `J : X.IdealSheafData` and `t : Γ(Q, W)`, `Killed J t` means that for
every affine open `V ≤ W` and every `c ∈ J.ideal V`, `c • t|_V = 0`. So `t` is a section of
the subsheaf of sections annihilated by `J`, expressed on affine opens, where Mathlib's
`IdealSheafData` has its ideals.

- `Killed.res`, `killed_zero`, `Killed.add`, `Killed.sub`, `Killed.smul`: closure under
  restriction, zero, sums, differences and scalar multiples.
- `killed_of_affine`: on an affine open `W`, `J.ideal W • t = 0` already implies `Killed J t`.
  For affine `V ≤ W`, Mathlib's `IdealSheafData.map_ideal` identifies `J.ideal V` with the
  ideal generated by the restriction of `J.ideal W`. The scalars annihilating `t|_V` form an
  ideal, the kernel of `LinearMap.toSpanSingleton`, which contains that image.
- `Killed.affine`: the converse on an affine open.
- `killed_of_cover`: being killed is local. For affine `V` and `c ∈ J.ideal V`, the section
  `c • t|_V` vanishes near every point. Near a point, a smaller affine open lies in a
  neighbourhood where `t` is killed, and the restriction of `c` lies in its ideal
  (`IdealSheafData.ideal_le_comap_ideal`).
- `Killed.eq_zero_of_ideal_eq_top`: a killed section vanishes if `J.ideal V = ⊤` for all
  affine `V ≤ W`, because `1` kills it on every affine open.
