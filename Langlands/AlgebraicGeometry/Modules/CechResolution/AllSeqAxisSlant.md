# Degree-two normalization and slant of axis pullbacks

This internal derived unit proves that the actual shuffle slant associated to any actual first-factor H1 dual kills every degree-two class pulled back from either axis. It uses normalized ordered Cech representatives, so it does not assume that degree-two classes are generated by cups.

## Additive normalization

Let `P` be any presheaf of abelian groups on a topological space, and let `U` be any family of opens. In this section there is no finiteness, affineness, covering, or field hypothesis. For an ordered two-cocycle `e`, `twoCocycle_face_left` and `twoCocycle_face_right` prove

\[
 \operatorname{face}(e,\sigma,(0,0,1))
   =\operatorname{face}(e,\sigma,(0,0,0)),\qquad
 \operatorname{face}(e,\sigma,(0,1,1))
   =\operatorname{face}(e,\sigma,(1,1,1)).
\]

Here `sigma` has two entries, and each face is restricted to their intersection. The proofs evaluate `de = 0` on the four-entry faces `(0,0,0,1)` and `(0,1,1,1)`. The alternating sum cancels its repeated terms.

Define the one-cochain `twoCorrection e` by

\[
 h(\sigma)=\operatorname{face}(e,\sigma,(0,0,0)),
\]

and define `normalizeTwo e = e - d h`. The formula `face_twoCorrection` follows from composition of restrictions. The identity `d d = 0` proves `normalizeTwo_closed` whenever `e` is closed. Direct evaluation of `d h` on the two repeated-index faces, followed by the preceding cocycle identities, proves

\[
 \operatorname{face}(\operatorname{normalizeTwo}(e),\sigma,(0,0,1))=0,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{face}(\operatorname{normalizeTwo}(e),\sigma,(0,1,1))=0.
\]

## Raw shuffle vanishing

Work over a commutative ring `R` with an actual pullback square `P = X ×[Spec R] Y`. The first-factor index type is finite. The section algebra structures on `X` and `Y` agree with their structure maps. Two-entry intersections of `U` and all nonempty finite intersections of `V` are affine. The functional `l` is any `R`-linear map from all first-factor one-cochains to `R`; no boundary-vanishing condition is used in this section.

`ev_pull_left_zero` and `ev_pull_right_zero` express the following elementary implication: a zero face of an axis cochain gives zero shuffle evaluation of its pullback. The proof commutes section restriction with scheme pullback via `res_appLE` and `appLE_res`, and then applies additivity of `slant` to the zero family.

In degree two there are two shuffle terms. On the first factor their step functions are `(0,1,1)` and `(0,0,1)`. On the second factor their degeneracies are `(0,0,1)` and `(0,1,1)`. Thus both terms vanish individually for a normalized two-cocycle. This proves `L_pull_left_normalizeTwo` and `L_pull_right_normalizeTwo` for the raw shuffle operator `L`.

## Actual cohomology

For this section `K` is an arbitrary field. First `normalizeTwoZ` packages normalization as a cocycle in the kernel of the scalar-linear differential. On a separated scheme with a finite affine cover, `canonicalClass_normalizeTwoZ` proves that normalization preserves its actual degree-two structure-sheaf cohomology class. The proof rewrites classes using the existing linear extension `canonicalClassExtension`, expands the subtraction, and uses `canonicalClassExtension_dCochain` to kill the subtracted boundary.

For the two axis theorems, the full hypotheses are supplied explicitly: `X`, `Y`, and `P` are objects over `Spec K`; their structure morphisms are separated; `p1` and `p2` form an actual pullback square; the section algebra structures on all three schemes agree with the structure maps; `U` and `V` are finite affine open covers, with two-entry intersections of `U` and all nonempty finite intersections of `V` affine; and their product family is an affine open cover of `P`.

For every actual linear functional `ell : H1(X,O_X) → K`, the conclusions are

\[
 \operatorname{slantByH1Dual}_{\ell}(p_1^*x)=0
 \quad (x\in H^2(X,\mathcal O_X)),\qquad
 \operatorname{slantByH1Dual}_{\ell}(p_2^*y)=0
 \quad (y\in H^2(Y,\mathcal O_Y)).
\]

These are `slantByH1Dual_pull_left` and `slantByH1Dual_pull_right`, with values in actual `H1(Y,O_Y)`. The pullbacks are precisely `unitCohomologyPullback`, which includes the unit-module comparison. The proof chooses an ordered Cech cocycle representative, replaces it by its normalized representative without changing its class, uses the actual pullback and slant cocycle formulas, and applies the raw vanishing theorem. The chosen cochain extension used in `slantByH1Dual` is the existing one in `AllSeqSlantDual`.
