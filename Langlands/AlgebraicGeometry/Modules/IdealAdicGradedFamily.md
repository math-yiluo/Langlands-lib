# The graded family of ideal-adic layers and uniform vanishing of its twists

This derived module applies the published action-family devissage
(`ActionFamily.exists_uniform_vanishing`) to the ideal-adic layers
`adicLayer f J M n = JⁿM / Jⁿ⁺¹M` of a module sheaf `M`, where
`f : X ⟶ Spec R` is any morphism and `J` is a finitely generated ideal of
`R`. The output is one twisting bound that kills the higher cohomology of
every layer. It is conditional on Serre vanishing for each single coherent
module annihilated by `J`, stated as the hypothesis `hserre`. That hypothesis
is not proved here.

## Multiplication between ideal multiples

- `smulEnd_mul`: `smulEnd f (a * b) M = smulEnd f b M ≫ smulEnd f a M`, where
  `smulEnd` (module `IdealAdicScalarAction`) multiplies sections by the image
  of a base scalar.
- `mulMultiple f a h M : multiple f I M ⟶ multiple f K M` for
  `h : ∀ b ∈ I, a * b ∈ K`. The composite `IM ⟶ M ⟶ M` of the inclusion with
  `smulEnd f a M` vanishes after the projection `M ⟶ M/KM`. By the published
  `inclusion_comp_eq_zero_iff` it suffices to check this on
  `scalar f U b • m` with `b ∈ I`. There it is `smulEnd f (a * b)` followed
  by the projection, which vanishes by `smulEnd_projection`. The map is the
  resulting factorization through the monomorphism `KM ⟶ M`
  (`Abelian.monoLift`). `mulMultiple_inclusion` records the factorization.
- `multipleMap_mulMultiple`, `mulMultiple_comm` and `smulEnd_multiple` are
  checked after composing with the monomorphism inclusion into `M`.
  `multipleMap_mulMultiple`: these maps commute with the maps induced by
  inclusions of ideals. `mulMultiple_comm`: multiplications by two scalars
  commute, by `smulEnd_naturality`. `smulEnd_multiple`: for `K ≤ I`, the
  scalar `a` acting on `IM` equals `mulMultiple` into `KM` followed by
  `multipleMap`.
- `mul_mem_pow_succ`: for `a ∈ J`, `a * b ∈ Jⁿ⁺¹` whenever `b ∈ Jⁿ`.

## The family

`layerMul f ha M n : adicLayer f J M n ⟶ adicLayer f J M (n + 1)` holds for
`a ∈ J`. It is the map of cokernels (`cokernel.map`) induced by the
multiplications `JⁿM ⟶ Jⁿ⁺¹M` and `Jⁿ⁺¹M ⟶ Jⁿ⁺²M`, and `π_layerMul` is its
defining square.

`gradedFamily f J g hg M : ActionFamily X r` is defined for
`g : Fin r → R` with all `g j ∈ J`. It has the layers as modules and the maps
`layerMul` for the `g j` as actions. Commutation of the actions is checked
after the cokernel projection, using `mulMultiple_comm`.

## The class of annihilated coherent modules

`AnnihilatedCoherent f J G` means three things: `G` is quasi-coherent, has
finitely generated sections over every affine open, and satisfies
`smulEnd f a G = 0` for every `a ∈ J`.

- `annihilatedCoherent_kernel` (`X` locally noetherian) and
  `annihilatedCoherent_cokernel`: the class is closed under kernels and
  cokernels of arbitrary morphisms between its members. Quasi-coherence and
  finite affine sections come from the published closure lemmas. Vanishing of
  the scalars follows from `smulEnd_naturality` by cancelling the kernel
  monomorphism or the cokernel epimorphism.
- `annihilatedCoherent_adicLayer` (`X` locally noetherian, `J` finitely
  generated, `M` quasi-coherent with finite affine sections): every layer
  `JⁿM / Jⁿ⁺¹M` lies in the class. The multiples are quasi-coherent
  (published `isQuasicoherent_multiple`, since every power of `J` is finitely
  generated). The multiple `JⁿM` has finite affine sections as a submodule of
  `M`. On the cokernel, `smulEnd f a` with `a ∈ J` vanishes. After the
  projection it equals `smulEnd f a` on `JⁿM` followed by the projection. By
  `smulEnd_multiple` this factors through `multipleMap` from `Jⁿ⁺¹M`, which
  the projection kills.

## Generation

`isGeneratedOn_gradedFamily` assumes that `J` is generated by the `g j`
(`hJ : J ≤ Ideal.span (Set.range g)`), that `M` is quasi-coherent, and that
`U` is a family of affine opens. Then the family is generated on `U` from
degree `0`: on each `U l`, every section of layer `n + 1` is a sum of images
of sections of layer `n` under the actions. The proof uses the following
steps.

1. A section `s` of `Jⁿ⁺¹M / Jⁿ⁺²M` over the affine `V = U l` lifts to a
   section `t` of `Jⁿ⁺¹M`, because the projection is an epimorphism of
   quasi-coherent modules.
2. Let `W` be the span of the images of the actions. Let `S` be the set of
   sections of `M` over `V` that are images of sections of `Jⁿ⁺¹M` whose
   projections lie in `W`. Every `scalar c • m` with `c ∈ Jⁿ⁺¹ = J · Jⁿ` lies
   in `S`. By induction on products (`Submodule.mul_induction_on`) and on the
   span of the `g j` (`Submodule.span_induction`), it suffices to treat
   `c = g j * b` with `b ∈ Jⁿ`. In that case `scalar b • m` is the image of a
   section `y` of `JⁿM` (published `mem_range_inclusion_app_iff`). The image
   of `y` under multiplication by `g j` maps to `scalar c • m` in `M`. Its
   projection is the action `g j` applied to the projection of `y`, by
   `π_layerMul`.
3. The image of `t` in `M` lies in the sectionwise span of these generators,
   by `mem_range_inclusion_app_iff` for `Jⁿ⁺¹`. So it lies in `S`, and
   injectivity of the inclusion on sections (`inclusion_app_injective`)
   identifies the witness with `t`. Hence `s ∈ W`.

## Uniform vanishing

`exists_uniform_vanishing_adicLayer` has the following hypotheses: `X` is
noetherian, `J` is finitely generated, and `M` is quasi-coherent with finite
affine sections. Each `T d` is a functor preserving zero morphisms and short
exact sequences. The hypothesis `hserre` says that every
`AnnihilatedCoherent f J` module `G` has a bound `d₀` such that
`H^{p+1}(X, T d G)` (relative cohomology over `f`) vanishes for all `d ≥ d₀`
and all `p`. Then one bound `d₀` gives vanishing of `H^{p+1}(X, T d (JⁿM/Jⁿ⁺¹M))`
for all `d ≥ d₀`, all `n` and all `p`. The proof chooses generators
`g : Fin r → R` of `J` (`Submodule.fg_iff_exists_fin_generating_family`) and
a finite affine cover (`IntegralIdealFiltration.exists_finite_affine_cover`).
It then applies `ActionFamily.exists_uniform_vanishing` to `gradedFamily`
with the class `AnnihilatedCoherent f J`.

`exists_uniform_vanishing_adicLayer_tensorPow` is the case `M = O_X` and
`T d = tensorBy (tensorPow N d)` for a locally free rank-one `N`. Exactness is
the published `shortExact_tensorBy`, and the structure sheaf has finite
affine sections. Its conclusion is stated for the layers of the powers
themselves: one `d₀` gives vanishing of `H^{p+1}(X, JⁿN^d / Jⁿ⁺¹N^d)` for all
`d ≥ d₀` and all `n`, `p`. The transfer uses
`layerTwistIso : (JⁿO_X / Jⁿ⁺¹O_X) ⊗ N^d ≅ JⁿN^d / Jⁿ⁺¹N^d` from
`IdealAdicScalarAction`. The hypothesis `hserre` is unchanged, for the functors
`tensorBy (tensorPow N d)`.

## Provenance

Stacks Project Lemma 30.21.3 (registered snapshot
`stacks-noetherian-fibre-section-lifting-2026-11-03`, `02OF.txt`) treats the
graded module `⊕ₙ H^p(X, mⁿL^d / mⁿ⁺¹L^d)` over `Gr_m(A)`. It realizes the
graded pieces as one coherent sheaf on `X ×_A Spec Gr_m(A)` and applies Serre
vanishing there. This module replaces that realization with a devissage on
`X` itself. The multiplications by generators of `J` make the layers a family
with commuting actions. The published action-family theorem then reduces
uniform vanishing to Serre vanishing for single coherent modules annihilated
by `J`. Those modules are supplied separately through `hserre`.
