# Ideal multiples and adic quotients of actual scheme modules

For a morphism `f : X → Spec R`, an ideal `I` of the base ring, and an actual
structure-sheaf module `M`, this module constructs the local ideal multiple
and its quotient in `X.Modules`. No flatness, properness, coherence or
noetherian hypothesis is needed for these constructions.

The source convention is the opening paragraphs of the Stacks Project,
Section 30.20, tag [02O7](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02O7),
retained as `02O7.html` and `02O7.txt` in the registered source
`stacks-formal-functions-stable-images-2026-11-04`. There `I M` is the
subsheaf locally generated by products of base-ideal elements and local
sections of `M`.

`scalar f U` is the structure map from `R` to global source functions
followed by restriction to `U`. `scalar_naturality` proves compatibility
with restriction. `multiplePresubmodule` takes the span, over the ring of
functions on each open, of `scalar f U a • m` with `a ∈ I`. The same
naturality identity and semilinearity prove restriction stability.
`multiple` is its module sheafification, and `inclusion` is the sheafified
inclusion followed by the canonical sheafification counit isomorphism.
Thus the construction takes local spans and then allows their local gluing.
The inclusion is a monomorphism because module sheafification preserves
finite limits.

`quotient` is the categorical cokernel of this inclusion, and `projection`
is its canonical projection. For `I ≤ J`, `multipleMap` and `quotientMap`
are the induced maps; `multipleMap_inclusion` and
`projection_quotientMap` prove their canonical compatibility.
The projections are epimorphisms. Cancellation by a projection proves
the identity and composition laws `quotientMap_refl` and `quotientMap_comp`.

`adicQuotient f I M n` uses `I ^ n`; `adicProjection` and `adicTransition`
are the canonical projection and the transition for `n ≤ k`. In
particular, degree zero uses `I ^ 0 = ⊤`. These are derived implementations
of the source notation, using Mathlib's actual module presheaves,
sheafification and categorical cokernels. This unit makes no cohomology
stabilization or lifting claim.
