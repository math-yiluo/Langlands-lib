# Singleton forms of the relative derived comparison

This internal supporting unit transports the derived sections/Hom comparison from an object placed in degree zero in the bounded below derived category to its actual singleton complex. It uses only the existing comparison and canonical localization isomorphisms; it does not prove the final arbitrary-morphism cohomology pullback statement.

For an additive functor `U : C ⥤ A`, `singleComplexDerivedIso U M` identifies the localization of the termwise image of the singleton complex of `M` with the derived singleton of `U(M)`. It is the localization of `U.mapCochainComplexSingleFunctor` followed by the inverse of `DerivedCategory.singleFunctorIsoCompQ`.

When `U` is exact, `singleMapDerivedIso_singlePlusQIso` identifies the composite of `singleMapDerivedIso.inv`, the image of `DerivedPlus.singlePlusQIso.hom`, the bounded below localization comparison and the exact-functor factorization with `singleComplexDerivedIso.inv`. The proof expands the singleton localization isomorphism, cancels its inverse components and uses the exact-functor singleton factorization identity.

`comparisonOnComplex_singlePlus` works in the generic setting of `DerivedSectionsHomComparison`: `C` has enough injectives, `F : C ⥤ D` is additive, `G : D ⥤ AddCommGrpCat` is additive and preserves homology, `U` is exact, and `e` identifies `F ⋙ G` with Hom from a specified `Z` after `U`. If the derived comparison at the singleton sends `y` to `a` followed by the shifted inverse of `singleMapDerivedIso`, the comparison on the actual singleton complex sends the image of `y` along `singlePlusQIso.hom` to `a` followed by the shifted inverse of `singleComplexDerivedIso`. This holds in every integer degree. The proof applies naturality of the derived comparison and the preceding coherence identity.

For a scheme `X` over `Spec R`, `relativeSingleDerivedSectionsClass M n x` is the actual relative additive comparison `relativeHAddEquiv X.hom M n x`, transported by the inverse of `DerivedPlus.homologyIso` and lifted to the universe used by derived Hom. Here `x` is an Ext-defined sheaf cohomology class of the underlying abelian sheaf of the actual module `M`, and `n` is a natural number. `relativeComparisonOnComplex_singlePlus` evaluates the comparison on the actual singleton at this class: it is `x.hom` followed by the shifted inverse of `singleComplexDerivedIso (toAbSheaf X.left) M`. This is a direct specialization of the generic transport lemma using `relativeDerivedHomComparison_single`. No condition on a scheme morphism and no exactness of module pullback or pushforward is introduced.
