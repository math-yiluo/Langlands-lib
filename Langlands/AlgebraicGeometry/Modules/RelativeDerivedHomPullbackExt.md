# Singleton pullback and the Ext-defined sheaf map

This internal comparison unit identifies the inverse-image/counit action on the localized Hom of an actual module singleton complex with `AbelianSheafCohomologyPullback.pushforwardMap` at the identity coefficient arrow. It supports the arbitrary-morphism relative cohomology comparison; it does not itself prove the final statement.

For a commutative ring `R`, schemes `X` and `Y` over `Spec R`, an arbitrary morphism `f : Y ⟶ X`, and a module `N` on `Y`, `relativePushforwardSingleDerivedIso` identifies the localization of the underlying abelian-sheaf complex of `f_* (N[0])` with the derived singleton of the underlying abelian sheaf of `f_* N`. It uses the termwise singleton isomorphism for the additive composite `pushforward ⋙ toAbSheaf`, followed by the canonical singleton localization isomorphism. This construction requires no exactness of module pushforward.

`relativePushforwardSingleDerivedIso_inv` identifies its inverse with the scalar-forgetting singleton inverse followed by the localized map of `DerivedPlus.singlePlusMapIso`. The proof expands the canonical isomorphisms and applies compatibility of the termwise singleton isomorphism with composition of additive functors.

`relativePullbackCounit_single` computes the actual termwise abelian-sheaf pullback counit on the singleton complex. After the singleton identifications, it is the singleton of the sheaf counit followed by the scalar-forgetting singleton inverse. A map from a singleton complex is determined by its degree-zero component; that component equality reduces to functoriality on identities. `relativePullbackCounit_single_derived` transports this equality through localization and the exact abelian-sheaf inverse-image functor. Its proof applies localization to the chain identity, uses naturality of the derived-functor factorization, and cancels the canonical inverse-image singleton isomorphism.

For every natural degree `n` and every class `x` in the sheaf cohomology of the underlying abelian sheaf of `f_* N`, `relativeComplexHomPullback_single` evaluates `relativeComplexHomPullback` on `x.hom` followed by the shifted inverse of `relativePushforwardSingleDerivedIso`. Its value is the derived morphism of `pushforwardMap f.base (𝟙 _) n x` followed by the shifted scalar-forgetting singleton inverse on `Y`. The proof expands Ext composition and exact-functor action on derived morphisms, applies the preceding derived counit identity, and uses that the sheaf adjoint of the identity is the counit. The inverse-image functor here is the actual exact functor on abelian sheaves, already used to define `pushforwardMap`; there is no assumption that module inverse image or direct image is exact.

All constructions and identities are derived internally from the imported comparison maps and Mathlib's singleton, localization, shifted-Hom and Ext interfaces. No external literature claim is introduced.
