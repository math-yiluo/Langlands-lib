# Fibre-dense opens at small primes in smooth DVR schemes

`genericOpen` is the complement of the closed point of the spectrum of a DVR.
Smoothness over the DVR gives a regular local ring at each point, hence a
domain and a unique factorization monoid by `regularLocalFactorial`.

`smallPrime_mem` puts the images of the zero prime and primes generated by
prime elements of each stalk in any fibre-dense open containing the generic
fibre. Flatness lifts generization to the generic point of the base, which
shows that the zero prime of the stalk lies over that generic point. Below
a principal prime in a factorial domain the only strict generization is
the zero prime. If the principal prime lies over the closed point, a
same-fibre generization of it in the open must therefore equal it.

This is the codimension-one observation used in Bosch, Lütkebohmert and
Raynaud, *Néron Models* (1990), §5.1 Theorem 5, printed p. 114 (PDF p. 121).
