# Flat base change of the kernel of a quasi-compact morphism

Helper module of the proof of
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.FlatProperEquivalenceRelationQuotientReductionStatement` (entry
module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.FlatProperEquivalenceRelationQuotientReductionProof`).
It contains no `sorry` and no new axioms.

## Main result

`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.KernelFlatBaseChange.ker_comap_of_flat`: let `f : X ⟶ Y` be
quasi-compact and `p : Y' ⟶ Y` flat. Then

```lean
f.ker.comap p = (pullback.snd f p).ker
```

The kernel `f.ker` is Mathlib's kernel ideal sheaf, the ideal sheaf of the scheme-theoretic
image of `f`. So the scheme-theoretic image of the base change `X ×_Y Y' ⟶ Y'` is the base change
of the scheme-theoretic image of `f`. This is Stacks Project, Tag 081I (Lemma 29.26.16, "Taking
scheme theoretic images commutes with flat base change in the quasi-compact case").
`ker_comap_of_flat_of_isPullback` is the same statement for any pullback square.

## Route

1. **Algebra** (`ker_algebraMap_eq_map_ker_of_flat`, `ker_eq_map_ker_of_isPushout_of_flat`):
   for a pushout square of commutative rings `R → B`, `R → C`, `B → P = B ⊗_R C` with `R → B`
   flat, the kernel of `B → P` is the ideal generated by the image of the kernel of `R → C`. The
   proof tensors the exact sequence `0 → ker → R → C` with the flat `B`
   (`Module.Flat.lTensor_exact`).
2. **Affine case** (`ker_comap_of_flat_of_isAffine`): `X`, `Y`, `Y'` affine. The kernel of a
   morphism of affine schemes is the kernel of the map on global sections. The pullback of an
   ideal sheaf along a morphism of affine schemes is the extended ideal (FLT's
   `ComapLocal.ideal_comap_top`). The global sections of the pullback form a pushout (FLT's
   `isPushout_appTop_of_isPullback`).
3. **Affine base** (`ker_comp_eq_of_factors`, `ker_comap_of_isPullback_of_isOpenImmersion`,
   `ker_eq_of_isPullback`, `ker_comap_of_flat_of_isAffine_base`): `Y`, `Y'` affine and `f`
   arbitrary quasi-compact. Replace `X` by the finite disjoint union of the affine opens of a
   finite subcover. This does not change the kernel, because the kernel is the infimum of the
   kernels over an open cover (Mathlib's `Hom.iInf_ker_openCover_map_comp`).
4. **General case** (`eq_of_comap_eq`, `comap_ι_of_le`, `ker_comap_of_flat`): two ideal sheaves
   are equal if their pullbacks to the members of an open cover agree. Cover `Y'` by affine
   opens `V ⊆ p⁻¹(U)` with `U ⊆ Y` affine. On each such `V`, both sides restrict to the ideal of
   step 3, by compatibility of kernels with restriction to opens of the target.

## Use

`FlatEquivalenceRelation.Quotient` uses `ker_comap_of_flat` along the flat universal family
`D'' ⟶ H''` to show that the graph `Z ⟶ D''` is the base change of the quotient `Q ⟶ H''`. This
replaces the fpqc descent of closed subschemes in Nitsure's proof (Lemma 6.7(2)).
