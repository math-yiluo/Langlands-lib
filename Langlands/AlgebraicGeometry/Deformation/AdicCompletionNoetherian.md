# Noetherianness of actual adic completions

This internal algebra prerequisite constructs finite-power-series evaluation
homomorphisms onto Mathlib's actual `AdicCompletion I R`, and supplies its
noetherian-ring instance. Its assumptions are a commutative
noetherian ring `R : Type u` and any ideal `I` of `R`. The universe `u` is
arbitrary. No localness, properness of the ideal, residue-field section or
coefficient field is required. The zero ideal and the unit ideal are included.

The data construction `AdicCompletionNoetherian.evaluation` takes any finite
family `g : Fin n → R` spanning `I` and evaluates its variables at their
canonical images in the completion. `evaluation_C` and `evaluation_X` prove
the constant and variable formulas. `evaluation_surjective` proves that this
actual homomorphism is surjective for every such family. Noetherianness
reuses the published theorem
`AdicCompletion.isNoetherianRing_of_isNoetherianRing`.
The named instance `AdicCompletionNoetherian.instIsNoetherianRing` exposes it to
typeclass inference when this module is imported. These declarations live in
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Deformation`.

## Implemented proof

Choose a finite generating family `g : Fin n → R` of `I`. Write
`S = AdicCompletion I R` and let `J` be the image ideal of `I` under the
canonical ring map `R → S`. Install the `J`-adic topology on `S`. Mathlib's
`AdicCompletion.isAdicComplete_self` gives algebraic completeness and
separatedness for this ideal; `IsAdic.isAdicComplete_iff` gives the complete
Hausdorff topological structures needed for power-series evaluation. Give the
coefficient ring `R` its discrete topology.

Every canonical image of a generator `g i` lies in `J`, so it is topologically
nilpotent in the `J`-adic topology. The other convergence requirement for
power-series evaluation holds because the variable set is finite. Thus
`MvPowerSeries.eval₂Hom` defines a ring homomorphism

`f : MvPowerSeries (Fin n) R →+* S`

which sends constants to their canonical images and variables to the images of
the corresponding generators. If `M` is the ideal generated by the variables,
`Ideal.map_span`, the variable-evaluation identity and the generating-family
equation prove `M.map f = J`.

The composite of `f` with the quotient map `S → S/J` is surjective. For
`s : S`, choose `r : R` whose residue in `R/I` is
`AdicCompletion.evalOneₐ I s`. Evaluation sends the constant series `C r` to
the canonical image of `r`. Its difference from `s` lies in the kernel of
`evalOneₐ`; `AdicCompletion.ker_evalOneₐ_eq_map` identifies this kernel with
`J`. The class of `s` is therefore the image of the class of `C r`.

The variable-ideal-adic power-series ring is precomplete, and `S` is separated
for the image ideal `M.map f = J`.
`surjective_of_mk_map_comp_surjective` now proves that `f` itself is
surjective. The noetherian-ring instance then uses the existing
[adic-completion noetherianness theorem](../../AnthropicFLT/Theorems/Thm_AdicCompletion_isNoetherianRing_of_isNoetherianRing.md).
That theorem establishes the same arbitrary-universe conclusion on the
actual completion, so no duplicate noetherianness proof is introduced here.

## Provenance and use

This is an internal derivation from the pinned Mathlib implementation of adic
completion, finite-variable power series, their topological evaluation, and
the published Anthropic FLT noetherianness theorem linked above. Its port
attribution and license are recorded with that theorem. This reuse adds no
mathematical assumption. The proof uses the actual completion ring throughout; it does not
replace that ring by an existential quotient presentation.

The instance supplies one algebraic property needed when completing the local
polynomial source of Artinian tests. This pair constructs no augmentation,
formal Picard family, deformation hull, algebraization, or smooth scheme
atlas. Those geometric constructions remain separate obligations of the
Picard atlas argument.
