# Lifting the multiplication of the reduction

Let the family be smooth and proper over a local Artinian base and suppose
its section induces the unit of the group law on an elementary quotient.
Use a geometrically connected residue group on the target fibre and fixed
finite bases for the affine difference coordinates.

The product of an affine cover of the family with itself gives an affine
cover of the square. Write `e` for the section. The two axes pair the identity
with the constant section induced by `e` in either order. Their preimages of
the product charts are affine, because an open of the local Artinian base is
empty or the whole base. On each axis the identity map is an actual lift of
the restriction of the reduced multiplication, so the affine lifting
cocycle restricts to a coboundary.

The residue-fibre product cover is the product of the two induced covers.
The two-axis Čech lemma, applied to each scalar coordinate, gives the
primitives needed to correct and glue the local lifts. A constant correction
at the pair of unit sections prescribes the value there. This is the
multiplication version of the lifting argument of GIT Proposition 6.15.
