# Pointed elementary lifts

Consider the elementary quotient of a local Artinian ring, a group law on
the residue fibre of the target, and fixed finite bases of the elementary
ideal and the identity cotangent space over the residue field. On a smooth,
separated, quasi-compact source over the Artinian base, the residue
difference coordinates of two global lifts with the same reduction glue to
global functions on the residue fibre. If all such functions are constants,
then pullback to a section detects each difference coordinate. In that case
two lifts with the same reduction that agree at the section coincide.

For a smooth proper source, the normalization lemma prescribes a value at a
section by comparing the original value and the desired value on the affine
base. If their reductions agree, this gives a tuple of residue-field
constants. Correct the lift on every chart of the source by this same tuple.
The corrected lifts glue, since the constants agree on overlaps. At the
section the correction is exactly the prescribed difference.

These are the degree-zero parts of the torsor calculation in SGA 1, Exposé III,
Corollary 5.2, used for the operations and pointed identities in the elementary
Artinian case of GIT Proposition 6.15.
