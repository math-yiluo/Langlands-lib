# Chosen reductions of fibre products

A fibre product of two chosen reductions over the quotient base presents the
reduction of the fibre product over the original base. Its map to the
original product comes from the two projection squares. The universal
property follows by lifting to each factor and pairing the lifts.

Reduction in these presentations preserves projections, pairings and the
map to the terminal object. On opens, the preimage of a product chart is the
product of the preimage charts. These compatibilities implement the product
and axis diagrams of GIT Proposition 6.15.
