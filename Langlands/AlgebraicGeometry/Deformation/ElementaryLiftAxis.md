# Restricting the elementary lifting cocycle

Consider the affine lifting cocycle for a smooth separated source `Y` over a
local ring `R` with maximal ideal `m`. Let `I` be a proper ideal of `R`
with `mI = 0`. The inclusion `I ⊆ m` then gives `I² = 0`. The coefficients
use a group law on the target residue fibre and fixed finite bases of the
identity cotangent space and `I` over the residue field.

Let `Y' → Y` be a morphism over `Spec R` with `Y'` smooth. Suppose the
preimages of the cover charts and their pairwise intersections are affine,
and that the pulled-back prescribed map on the quotient base change of
`Y'` has a lift on `Y'`.

The primitive on each preimage chart is the difference from the actual lift
to the pulled-back local lift. The three-lift identity identifies its Čech
boundary with the pullback of the original cocycle. This is the restriction
of the lifting torsor of SGA 1, Exposé III, Corollary 5.2, in the affine
coordinates for GIT Proposition 6.15.
