# Ordinary pullback on the degree-one Hodge maps

For an actual morphism over the base with compatible affine covers,
`closedFormsPullback` pulls back the chart forms and commutes with the
Čech restriction maps and exterior derivative. In the degree-one
pullback formula, the section maps on an overlap agree at both
vertices. The correction is therefore zero. The map on closed-form
classes is the class of the pulled-back form. Projection to structure
cohomology takes the class of the ordinary pullback of the function
cocycle. These formulas follow from the affine corrected pullback
formula in Lan, *Arithmetic compactifications of PEL-type Shimura
varieties*, 14 March 2021 revision, Proposition 2.1.6.4.
