# Uniform point bounds for étale algebras

This is a derived algebra helper for the bounded fibre degree used in Stacks
Project Lemma 68.8.4, Tag 07S9, and the tuple construction of Lemma 68.8.3,
Tag 0BBN. The registered source is
`stacks-stratification-2026-11-02`, in `07S9.html`, statement and proof, and
`0BBN.html`, the first paragraph of the proof. The boundedness in this module
is proved using finite standard étale covers and polynomial root counts in
the pinned Mathlib. It is not assumed from those passages; no quotient,
stratification, or Noetherian approximation theorem is asserted here.

`standardEtaleRootMap` sends an algebra homomorphism from an algebra with a
standard étale presentation to the image of its distinguished generator,
regarded as a root of the presentation's monic polynomial. Its injectivity is
the generator extensionality theorem for a standard étale presentation.
`standardEtale_hom_finite_card_le` consequently proves finiteness and bounds
the number of homomorphisms by that polynomial's natural degree. The target
may be any integral domain, in any universe, with an algebra structure over
the coefficient ring.

`exists_finite_standardEtale_cover` proves that an étale algebra has a finite
family of elements generating the unit ideal whose principal localizations
are standard étale. The local structure theorem gives such an element outside
each prime ideal. If the ideal generated by all such elements were proper, a
maximal ideal containing it would contradict this fact. Finite generation of
the unit ideal then extracts the required finite family.

`StandardEtaleCover R S` records a finite set of elements of `S`, the fact that
they generate the unit ideal, and a standard étale presentation of each
principal localization. `StandardEtaleCover.ofEtale` chooses this data using
the preceding finite cover theorem and the presentation supplied by the
standard étale property. `StandardEtaleCover.bound` is the sum, over that
finite set, of the natural degrees of the defining monic polynomials.

`StandardEtaleCover.hom_finite_card_le` proves that, for any field `K` with
an `R`-algebra structure, the type `S →ₐ[R] K` is finite and its natural
cardinality is at most this sum. Every homomorphism sends at least one member
of the cover to a nonzero element, since the members generate the unit ideal.
That element is a unit in the field, so the homomorphism extends to its
principal localization. Thus precomposition gives a surjection from the
disjoint union of the localized homomorphism types onto `S →ₐ[R] K`.
The standard presentation root counts make the source finite and bound its
cardinality by the sum of degrees, which bounds the target as well.

`uniformBound R S` is this sum for a chosen cover of an étale `R`-algebra `S`.
`hom_finite_card_le` specializes the cover theorem to give
`Finite (S →ₐ[R] K)` and `Nat.card (S →ₐ[R] K) ≤ uniformBound R S` for every
field `K` and every `R`-algebra structure on it. The rings and field lie in
arbitrary independent universes `u`, `v`, and `w`. The bound depends only on
the algebra `R → S`, independently of `K` and its algebra structure. No
algebraic closedness, Noetherian hypothesis, or injectivity of a coefficient
map is required.

The implemented algebra proof is internal to this development. Its ingredients
are Mathlib's local structure theorem
`Algebra.IsEtaleAt.exists_isStandardEtale` (annotated there with Stacks Tag
00UE), `StandardEtalePresentation.hom_ext`, the localization lifting
property, and the degree bound `Polynomial.ncard_rootSet_le`. The role of
07S9 is to identify the boundedness ingredient needed by the subsequent
geometric tuple construction; this module provides a direct algebra proof
of the field-valued version.
