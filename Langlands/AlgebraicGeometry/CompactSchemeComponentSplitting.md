# Common finite fields for affine covers and compact component splitting

This derived module constructs a common splitting field for the actual maximal components of a compact locally finite-type scheme over a field. The field and scheme share an arbitrary universe `u`; an open cover may have an independently sized index type in universe `v`. Every component property uses `ComponentsGeometricallyIrreducible`, with the vanishing-ideal reduced closed scheme structure on each actual maximal irreducible component.

`isPullback_fieldBaseChangeTower` proves that the outer square of successive scalar extensions along a field tower `K → E → L` is cartesian. It pastes the two defining pullback squares and uses the scalar-tower equality for the algebra maps. `fieldBaseChangeTowerIso` therefore identifies the successive categorical scalar extensions with the direct extension along `K → L`. The coherence theorem `fieldBaseChangeTowerIso_hom_snd` identifies their structure morphisms to `Spec L`.

`affineCoverComponentSplittingField K f U` is the supremum in `SeparableClosure K` of the chosen fields `affineSchemeComponentSplittingField K (U.f i ≫ f)` for the affine charts of `U`. Each chart field lies in this supremum. When the index type is finite, the supremum is finite dimensional over `K`, by Mathlib's finite-compositum theorem for intermediate fields. It is separable over `K` because it is an intermediate field of the separable closure. These constructions require only that `f` is locally of finite type and each chart is affine.

`componentsGeometricallyIrreducible_affineCoverComponentSplittingField` proves that the categorical scalar extension of `X` to this common field has geometrically irreducible actual reduced maximal components. For each chart, the inclusion of its chosen splitting field into the common field defines an algebra structure and compatible scalar tower. The chart after its first scalar extension is affine and locally of finite type over a field. Thus it is locally Noetherian and compact, hence Noetherian, so it has finitely many irreducible components. The imported `ComponentsGeometricallyIrreducible.baseChange` then transfers its component property to the further field extension. The tower isomorphism gives the corresponding direct scalar extension, with the required equality over the common field. Finally `ComponentsGeometricallyIrreducible.of_openCover_baseChange` assembles these chart conclusions. This component-property theorem works even for an infinite affine cover; only finiteness of the constructed field requires a finite cover. No preservation of reducedness under a field extension is assumed.

For compact `X`, `compactSchemeComponentSplittingField K f` uses the canonical finite subcover of `X.affineCover`. Its specification theorems give finiteness, separability, and the actual component property. `exists_finite_separable_components_of_compactSpace` packages these into the existence of a field `L` in the same universe, its field and `K`-algebra structures, finiteness and separability over `K`, and geometric irreducibility of every actual reduced maximal component of `X ×[Spec K] Spec L`. It assumes compactness and local finite type, with no additional finite-component, separation, reducedness, perfectness or characteristic premise.

The provenance is internal: this module combines the affine splitting construction, further-field-extension stability for actual components, open-cover assembly, categorical pullback pasting, and Mathlib's finite intermediate-field supremum and Noetherianity results. The compact theorem is an intermediate result; the unrestricted finite separable component-splitting statement is obtained separately through the generic-point compact-open reduction.
