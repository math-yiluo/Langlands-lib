# Relative differential sheaves on schemes

For every `f : X ⟶ S` between arbitrary `Scheme.{u}`, the definition
`Langlands.Scheme.relativeDifferentials f` is an object of Mathlib's actual
category `X.Modules`. It is the module sheafification of the constructed
`Langlands.PresheafDifferentials.relativeDifferentials f.c`, using the
inverse-image functor `Opens.map f.base` on opens.

On an open `U` of `X`, the input presheaf is the Kähler differential module
of `O_X(U)` relative to the subring generated by restrictions to `U` of
images of local sections of `O_S`. Its restriction maps come from the
restriction maps of `O_X`. Thus the construction uses sections on all
opens and the sheafification functor, rather than only global sections.

`relativeDifferentialsDerivation f` postcomposes the constructed presheaf
derivation with the unit of module sheafification. It is a relative
derivation into the resulting sheaf: additive on sections, compatible with
restriction, satisfying Leibniz, and vanishing on pulled-back base sections.
The equivalence with the inverse-image-sheaf vanishing condition is explained
in `RelativeDerivations.md`.

These definitions take no assumed cotangent object or universal property
as input. Universality is the separate proposition
`RelativeDifferentialsUniversalStatement` and is proved in
`RelativeDifferentialsUniversal`.

## Provenance

The original is the Stacks Project, Section 17.28 (tag 08RL), Definitions
17.28.1, 17.28.3 and 17.28.10, Lemmas 17.28.2 and 17.28.4, and Section 29.33
(tag 01UM), Definition 29.33.1 and Lemma 29.33.2. The implemented construction
uses the presheaf of subrings generated by restricted base sections, followed
by module sheafification. The presheaf derivation equivalence identifies this
construction with the derivation functor of the original definition; it does
not require constructing an inverse-image ring sheaf separately.
