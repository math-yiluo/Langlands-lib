# Noetherian constructibility of section-selected components

`SectionComponent.NoetherianComponentConstructibilityStatement` says that the actual selected-component union of every quasi-compact locally finite-type morphism with an actual section over a Noetherian scheme is constructible. It quantifies over arbitrary schemes in universe `u`; it assumes no flatness, reducedness, separatedness or affineness.

`noetherianComponentConstructibility_of_genericConstancy` proves this assertion conditionally on the existing `SchemeFiberGeometry.NoetherianGeometricallyConnectedFiberGenericConstancy` interface. The generic-constancy premise is not proved here, and this unit does not assert unconditional constructibility or the host openness theorem.

The general topological lemma `isConstructible_of_noetherian_target_slices` uses well-founded induction on closed subsets of a Noetherian target. For any function `f : A → B` and subset `S` of a topological source `A`, assume that over a nonempty open part of every irreducible closed target subset, the intersection with `S` upstairs is constructible. Removing that open part leaves a smaller closed target subset. Reducible closed subsets split into two smaller closed subsets. The resulting finite unions prove constructibility of `S`. This lemma requires neither continuity of `f` nor Noetherianity of `A`.

For each irreducible closed target subset `Z`, the scheme proof changes base to its reduced induced scheme. That base is Noetherian and irreducible, so its generic point exists. `exists_open_componentClosure_eq_componentSet` identifies the pulled-back selected-component union over an open neighbourhood with the selected generic component's closure. On the Noetherian pulled-back total space, this closed/open intersection is constructible. Its image under the actual closed immersion into the original total space is constructible by the scheme image theorem. Arbitrary base change of the selected-component union and the Cartesian image/preimage identity identify that image with the slice required by the topological induction.

The construction is internal, using the imported actual-fibre comparisons, the generic-closure theorem, constructible finite-type images, and elementary Noetherian induction. It implements the induction part of EGA IV3 Proposition 9.7.12, printed p.83 (retained PDF zero-based page81), while leaving geometric-connectedness generic constancy explicit. The original EGA IV4 List III errata, printed pp.352–353 (retained PDF zero-based pages350–351), passes from §8 corrections to §10 and makes no correction to 9.7.12.
