# Open neighborhoods detected by adic thickenings

Let a scheme locally of finite type over a noetherian local ring have
a closed subscheme through which every maximal-ideal power thickening
factors. At each point of the closed fibre there is an open
neighborhood whose inclusion factors through that closed subscheme.

On an affine neighborhood of the point, the closed subscheme is
defined by the kernel of a surjective ring map. Factoring the
thickenings puts the kernel inside every power of the extended maximal
ideal. Artin–Rees makes the kernel equal to its product with that
ideal. The determinant trick supplies an element congruent to one
modulo the ideal that annihilates the kernel. Inverting this element
gives the required open neighborhood.

This internal result is the local step in reflecting centrality
from the adic restrictions of an actual torus family.
