# Good points of a relation with an identity section

Author: Worker 303. The predicate was agreed with Worker 101 as the interface
between two steps of the proof of
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.SmoothEquivalenceRelationEtaleSliceStatement`
(task `3c6168ac-543c-4dc2-a8a6-81647cb26eb6`): slicing at a good point, and
existence of a good point in every orbit.

The module imports only `Mathlib` and contains one definition, in namespace
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.SmoothRelationGoodPoint`.

## Definition

Let `U R : Scheme.{u}`, `t s : R ⟶ U`, `e : U ⟶ R` and `x : U`. Write `p = e x`,
`𝒪_{R,p}` for the stalk `R.presheaf.stalk p` and `𝔪_p` for its maximal ideal.
Write `𝔪_{s(p)}` and `𝔪_{t(p)}` for the maximal ideals of the stalks of `U` at
`s p` and at `t p`. The stalk maps are
`s^# = (s.stalkMap p).hom : 𝒪_{U,s(p)} → 𝒪_{R,p}` and
`t^# = (t.stalkMap p).hom : 𝒪_{U,t(p)} → 𝒪_{R,p}`.

`IsGoodPoint t s e x` is the equality of ideals of `𝒪_{R,p}`

```
𝔪_p = s^#(𝔪_{s(p)}) 𝒪_{R,p} + t^#(𝔪_{t(p)}) 𝒪_{R,p}.
```

Here `Ideal.map f I` is the ideal generated by the image `f(I)`, and `⊔` is the
sum of ideals. In words: the maximal ideal of the local ring of `R` at `e x` is
generated by the pullbacks along `s` and along `t` of functions on `U` that vanish
at the images of `e x`.

The definition assumes nothing about `t`, `s` and `e`. In the intended use,
`(t, s)` is an equivalence relation with smooth projections and `e` is its
identity section, so `e ≫ t = 𝟙 U` and `e ≫ s = 𝟙 U`. Then `s (e x)` and
`t (e x)` both equal `x`. This holds only propositionally, not definitionally,
so the definition names the stalks of `U` at `s (e x)` and at `t (e x)`.

Both stalk maps are local ring homomorphisms (Mathlib's `IsLocalHom` instance
for `Scheme.Hom.stalkMap`), so the right-hand side is always contained in `𝔪_p`.
The predicate therefore says that the quotient
`𝒪_{R,p} / (s^#(𝔪_{s(p)}) + t^#(𝔪_{t(p)}))` is the residue field `κ(p)`.

## Meaning in the intended use

The following explains the role of the predicate. None of it is proved in this
module.

Assume `e ≫ t = 𝟙 U` and `e ≫ s = 𝟙 U`, and let `O_x = s⁻¹(x)` be the scheme
theoretic fibre of `s` over `x`, the orbit of `x`. The point `p` lies on `O_x`,
with residue field `κ(p) = κ(x)`, and the local ring of `O_x` at `p` is
`𝒪_{R,p} / s^#(𝔪_x) 𝒪_{R,p}`. So `IsGoodPoint t s e x` says that the maximal
ideal of this local ring is generated by the pullbacks along `t` of the functions
in `𝔪_x`. That is, the orbit morphism `t|_{O_x} : O_x → U` is unramified at its
rational point `p` in the sense of local rings.

In affine charts `x ∈ Spec A ⊆ U` and `p ∈ Spec B ⊆ R` with `s^# = σ : A → B` and
`t^# = θ : A → B`, the relative cotangent space at `p` satisfies
`Ω_{B/A,σ} ⊗ κ(p) ≅ 𝔪_p / (𝔪_p² + σ(𝔪_x) B_p)`, with `dθ(g)` corresponding to the
class of `θ(g) − σ(g)`. So when `s` is smooth, the predicate says that the
differentials `dθ(g)` for `g ∈ 𝔪_x` span `Ω_{B/A,σ} ⊗ κ(p)`. This is the input of
the slicing step: finitely many such `g` with independent differentials cut out
of `R` a closed subscheme that is etale over `U` along `s` near `p`.

## Provenance

This is an internal notion of the proof of
`SmoothEquivalenceRelationEtaleSliceStatement`, not a definition from the
literature. It replaces the dimension hypotheses of the Cohen–Macaulay slicing of
groupoids in the Stacks Project (Section 04LV, Lemma 0461) by a condition at one
point that suits smooth relations. The slicing it feeds is the smooth slicing of
Stacks Lemmas 057C and 057G (a smooth morphism cut by functions with independent
differentials is etale at the point). Stating the condition by stalk ideals
rather than by the orbit scheme `O_x` keeps it intrinsic to `R` and avoids
building fibres.
