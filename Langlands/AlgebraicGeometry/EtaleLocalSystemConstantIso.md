# Maps between constant abelian presheaves

This helper for the isotriviality spreading argument in Conrad,
*Reductive group schemes*, proof of Corollary B.3.6, uses the Library's
concrete constant big étale presheaf. A natural transformation between
constant models acts pointwise through its values on constant sections at
the base. Naturality on spectra of residue fields proves the pointwise
formula. The inverse of an isomorphism gives the inverse additive map at
each point. Finite generation of the source expresses each fibre of the
resulting family of equivalences as a finite intersection of open sets.

Conversely, a locally constant family of additive equivalences acts on
sections by evaluating the equivalence at the image of each point of the
test scheme. The family of inverse equivalences supplies the inverse
isomorphism. These constructions recover the original isomorphism and
family under the finite generation hypothesis.
