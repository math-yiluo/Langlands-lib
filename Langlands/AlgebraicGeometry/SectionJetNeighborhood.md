# Uniform finite-jet detection near a smooth section

Let a smooth morphism over a quasi-compact base have a quasi-compact
section, and let an ideal sheaf on its source be finitely generated
on every affine chart. `exists_order_of_vanishing_near_section` proves
that some order satisfies `DetectsVanishingNearSection`. On every test
scheme, containment of the extended ideal in the extended section
ideal power makes the ideal vanish on a neighborhood of each point
of the changed section.

Near each base point the proof takes affine opens in the source and
base on which the section restricts to a section of a smooth affine
chart. A finite cover of the base by these opens supplies a maximum of
the detection orders of the affine charts. At a point of an arbitrary
test scheme, the proof restricts the test to an affine open over one of
these charts. The affine detection theorem gives a neighborhood of
the restricted section that maps by an open immersion into the full
pullback. Its image gives the required neighborhood with the same
vanishing. The module also gives the finite-generation condition for
the kernel ideal of a locally finitely presented closed immersion.

This internal lemma derives its detection criterion from the
Library's affine scheme result and functoriality of ideal sheaves.
