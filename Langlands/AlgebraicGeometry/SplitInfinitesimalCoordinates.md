# Coordinates of split infinitesimal points at a rational section

For a rational point of a smooth scheme over a field, choose an affine
neighbourhood with coordinate ring `A` and point augmentation `A → k`.
A point over `D ⋉ M` with constant term the given rational point factors
uniquely through this open because the split infinitesimal thickening has
surjective reduction on spectra. Its representative is a ring map from `A`
whose first component is the rational point over `D`.

The second component of the representative is a `k`-derivation into `M`,
where `A` acts through the point augmentation. The first component and this
derivation recover the representative. The universal property of Kähler
differentials and scalar extension along `A → k` identify the derivation
with a `k`-linear map out of the finite-dimensional cotangent space.

A change of coefficient test postcomposes these linear maps by the actual
coefficient morphism. The compatibility is checked on the differentials of
chart functions, where it is the formula for the second component of the
map between split extensions. The constant point has zero coordinates.

This construction is the affine split coefficient calculation of SGA 1,
Exposé III, Proposition 5.1. The registered source is
