# Scheme pushouts along closed immersions and integral maps

It supplies a scheme `P`, actual cocone maps `a` and `b`, the pushout and pullback properties in schemes, integrality of `a`, and the closed-immersion property of `b`.

The proof uses the compatible affine atlas constructed in `IntegralSchemePushoutAtlas`. The global pullback descent lemma applies these local cartesian squares on the compatible affine cover.
