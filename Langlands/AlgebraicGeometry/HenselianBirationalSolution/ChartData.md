# Overlaps and transition maps

`GlueHyp` supplies strictness and the integrally closed domain stalks needed
by the graph-projection argument. The first open immersion from the graph
closure identifies it with the open `chartOverlap τ σ`. Composing that
identification's inverse with the second projection defines `chartTrans τ σ`.
Its image is `chartOverlap σ τ` by symmetry of the graph. The graph of
`chartTrans τ σ` is the original closed image, and `chartTrans` restricts
to `trans` on the original overlap. For `τ = σ` the graph is the diagonal
and the transition is the identity.

These data extend the two-chart construction in BLR, *Néron Models*, §5.3,
p. 122, to changes between any two translates in the chart family.
