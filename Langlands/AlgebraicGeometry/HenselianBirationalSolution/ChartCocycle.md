# The transition cocycle

The open `cocycleGeneric` consists of points in the domain of the first
translation whose image lies in the ranges of the other two. Its density
follows from the densities of the three translations. There the transition
maps are the literal compositions `τⱼ⁻¹ ∘ τᵢ`, so the cocycle is an equality
coming from the defining lifts through open immersions.

On the common extended overlap of charts 1–2 and 1–3, the pair of candidate
coordinates in charts 2 and 3 lies in their closed graph, since it does on
`cocycleGeneric`. Factoring the first coordinate through the 2–3 overlap
gives a map whose transition equals the second coordinate by dense agreement
from a reduced scheme into the separated `Y` over `S`. This is the cocycle
needed to use the translation construction of BLR, *Néron Models*, §5.3,
p. 122, for the whole chart family.
