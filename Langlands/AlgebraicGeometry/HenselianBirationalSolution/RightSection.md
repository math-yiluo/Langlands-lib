# Right translation by a section

`rightDom` is the open of `Y` where the original law is defined on
`(x,c)` for a section `c`, and `rightMap` is the resulting map `x ↦ xc`.
Its open immersion property comes from pulling back the universal right
translation along the fixed second coordinate. Strictness gives fibrewise
density of the domain by the same slice. The point lemmas identify
`rightMap` with the partial product on scheme-valued points.

This is the right-hand version of the partial translations in BLR,
*Néron Models*, §5.2 Definition 1, p. 115, and is used for the
multiplication pieces in `MulBar`.
