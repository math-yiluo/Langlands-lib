# The graph of a change of chart

For two translations `τ` and `σ`, the open `overlap τ σ` is the part of the
domain of `τ` mapping into the image of `σ`. The map `trans τ σ` lifts that
composite through `σ` and includes the result into `Y`. The graph in `Y ×_S Y`
is an immersion by separatedness. When `Y` is Noetherian the graph is
quasi-compact, so its scheme-theoretic image gives the schematic closure and
the original graph maps into it by a dense open immersion. The lemma
`isReduced_image` derives reducedness of this image from reducedness of the
source, using injectivity of sections along the map to the image.

Interchanging the two factors takes the original graph to the graph for the
reversed translations. This gives the symmetry of the closed graph ranges.
For a translation and the identity, this is the translation graph of BLR,
*Néron Models*, §5.3, p. 122.
