# Agreement along the schematic closure

Write `Γ` for the closure of the graph of `σ⁻¹ ∘ τ`. On the open `omega` of
`Y ×_S Γ` where both expressions are defined, `lam_eq_rho` proves
`τ(xw) = σ(yw)`. The extra coordinate `w` is common to the two products. Over
the original graph one has `τ(x) = σ(y)`. The open `gOpen` also requires the
product of this common value with `w` to be defined, so compatibility of the
translations with right multiplication gives the equality there.

The conditions defining `gOpen` are fibrewise dense by strictness. Smoothness
makes the projection to `Γ` open, so the part over the original graph is dense.
The source of the two morphisms is reduced by smoothness over the reduced
graph closure, and separatedness of `Y ⟶ S` extends the equality to `omega`.
This adapts the calculation and density argument of BLR, *Néron Models*,
§5.3 Lemmas 1–2, p. 120, to graphs of changes of chart.
