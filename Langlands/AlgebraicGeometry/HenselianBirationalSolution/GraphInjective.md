# The graph projections are open immersions

For two lifts to the graph closure with the same first coordinate, the
`omega` conditions for both lifts pull back to fibrewise dense opens in the
family of possible right factors. Surjectivity supplies a point in each fibre
of that family, and fibrewise density gives a common right factor after a
base change. The two `lam_eq_rho` equations and cancellation give equality of
the second coordinates after that base change. Morphisms from residue fields
and the existence of points in their fibre product turn this calculation into
injectivity on underlying points. The other projection uses the same equality
with the roles of the two translations exchanged.

Injectivity gives local quasi-finiteness. The graph is reduced and the two
projections are open immersions on its original dense open part. The result
`isOpenImmersion_of_birational` therefore applies to both projections.
This is the graph-projection argument of BLR, *Néron Models*, §5.3 Lemma 3
and the translation construction, pp. 121–122.
