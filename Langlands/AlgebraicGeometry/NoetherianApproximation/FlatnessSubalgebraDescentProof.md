# Finite-stage relative flatness descent

`isFlatOverSubalgebraDescent` proves `IsFlatOverSubalgebraDescentStatement` in every universe. Let A₀ be noetherian, let A be an arbitrary A₀-algebra, let X₀ → Spec A₀ be quasi-compact and locally of finite type, and let M₀ be a finitely presented actual module sheaf on X₀. If its ordinary pullback to a Cartesian base change over Spec A is relatively flat, then any finite subset of A is contained in a finitely generated A₀-subalgebra T such that the ordinary pullback of M₀ to every Cartesian realization over Spec T is relatively flat. There is no flatness assumption on the ambient scheme or on M₀ over A₀, and the algebra map need not be injective.

The proof is an internal reduction to algebraic module flatness. Ordinary pullback on an open immersion is compared with restriction by the canonical natural isomorphism. Local finite type on an affine chart implies finite type of its coordinate ring over the base ring. For a Cartesian square of affine schemes, the canonical scalar sections isomorphism identifies relative sections of the pulled-back sheaf with a tensor product; the affine relative-flatness criterion then detects flatness on that tensor product.

`isFlatOver_pullback_iff_forall_flat_tensor_sections` extends this criterion to any affine open cover. It pulls the cover back to the Cartesian realization. Pasting the chart square with the given Cartesian square shows that each pulled-back chart is affine and is the required scalar extension of its original chart. The composition and congruence isomorphisms for ordinary module pullback compare restriction of the global pullback with pullback of the original chart module. The source open-cover criterion for relative flatness then gives the equivalence with flatness of all the tensor-extended chart section modules.

For the descent theorem, quasi-compactness over the affine base makes X₀ compact, so its standard affine cover admits a finite subcover. Pullback preserves finite presentation, and finite presentation gives a finite module of sections over each affine coordinate ring. The input flatness and the cover criterion give flatness after tensor extension to A for every member of this finite family. `Langlands.Algebra.exists_fg_subalgebra_forall_flat_module` supplies one finitely generated T containing the prescribed finite set at which all these section modules are flat. Applying the same cover criterion to an arbitrary Cartesian realization over Spec T proves the universally quantified conclusion. Equalities between the algebra structures induced by the algebra maps and the existing algebra structures ensure that these are the ordinary tensor actions.

This proves the relative-flatness descent interface only. The construction of the proper noetherian scheme model and the descent of the finitely presented sheaves are separate steps in the host model-existence theorem.
