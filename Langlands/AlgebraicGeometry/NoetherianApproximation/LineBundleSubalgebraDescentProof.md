# Proof of descent of line bundles to finitely generated subalgebras

`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.NoetherianApproximation.lineBundleSubalgebraDescent` proves
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.NoetherianApproximation.LineBundleSubalgebraDescentStatement.{u}`
for an arbitrary universe `u`. See `LineBundleDescentStatements.md` for the statement and its
Stacks Project sources.

This module contains no ported FLT code. It applies the ported FLT theorem
`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.exists_isInvertible_nonempty_pullback_iso_of_isInvertible_of_isDirectLimit`
(module
`Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_Scheme_Modules_exists_isInvertible_nonempty_pullback_iso_of_isInvertible_of_isDirectLimit`,
Anthropic FLT Lean source at commit `6e837e75355538c7f80bab5b956861e86c4eacc2`, Apache-2.0).
That theorem works for an arbitrary directed system of rings `G` with direct limit `R`
(FLT's predicate `IsDirectLimit`), a stage `i`, and a quasi-compact, quasi-separated
`fX : X ⟶ Spec (G i)`. Let `𝓛` be a module on the chosen base change
`pullback fX (Spec.map (g i))` of `X` to `Spec R` that satisfies FLT's
`Scheme.Modules.IsInvertible`. The theorem gives `j ≥ i` and an FLT-invertible module `𝓛j` on the
chosen base change `pullback fX (Spec.map (φ i j hij))` of `X` to `Spec (G j)`. For every
`cX` between these two base changes that is compatible with both projections, it gives
`(pullback cX).obj 𝓛j ≅ 𝓛`.

This module specialises the system to finitely generated subalgebras. It converts between the
Library's `IsLocallyFreeRankOne` and FLT's `IsInvertible`. It also moves between the chosen
fibre products of the FLT theorem and the data of the statement: an arbitrary pullback square
over `Spec A`, and Mathlib's chosen base change of `X₀` to `Spec T`.

## Specialisation of the FLT theorem

`exists_fg_isInvertible_nonempty_pullback_iso` is the FLT theorem with `ι := FGIndex A₀ A`,
`G := FGStage`, `R := A`, `φ := fgTransition` and `g := fgToLimit`. These system arguments are
passed by name (the remaining arguments `fgIsDirectLimit T fY L hL` are positional), as in the
companion proof `LineBundleIsoSubalgebraDescentProof`, where positional system arguments made the
elaboration hit the heartbeat limit.

## The main proof

Fix the data of the statement: `f₀ : X₀ ⟶ Spec A₀`, the pullback square `hpb` with sides
`a : X ⟶ X₀` and `f : X ⟶ Spec A`, the module `M` on `X` with `IsLocallyFreeRankOne M`, and
`s : Finset A`. Let `i` be the index `Algebra.adjoin A₀ s` (finitely generated by
`Subalgebra.fg_adjoin_finset`), and write `ι₀ := Spec.map (algebraMap A₀ (FGStage i))`. Let
`Y := pullback f₀ ι₀` be the chosen base change of `X₀` to `Spec i`. Its second projection
`pullback.snd f₀ ι₀` is quasi-compact and quasi-separated by Mathlib's base-change instances.
Let `P := pullback (pullback.snd f₀ ι₀) (Spec.map (fgToLimit i))` be the chosen base change of `Y`
to `Spec A`.

1. **A retraction `e : P ⟶ X` with a section `e'`.** The pullback square `hpb` gives
   `e := hpb.lift (P.fst ≫ Y.fst) P.snd`, so `e ≫ a = P.fst ≫ Y.fst` and `e ≫ f = P.snd`. Its
   condition `(P.fst ≫ Y.fst) ≫ f₀ = P.snd ≫ Spec.map (algebraMap A₀ A)` follows from the two
   `pullback.condition` equations and `spec_map_fgToLimit_comp`. In the other direction,
   `e' := pullback.lift (pullback.lift a (f ≫ Spec.map (fgToLimit i)) _) f _`. The inner lift
   needs `a ≫ f₀ = (f ≫ Spec.map (fgToLimit i)) ≫ ι₀`, which is `hpb.w` with
   `spec_map_fgToLimit_comp`; the outer condition is `pullback.lift_snd`. Then `e' ≫ e = 𝟙 X` by
   `hpb.hom_ext`: composed with `a` both sides give `a` (by `pullback.lift_fst`), and composed
   with `f` both give `f`. Both morphisms are kept as existential witnesses with their defining
   equations, so later rewriting does not unfold them.
2. **Descent to a stage `j`.** The module `(pullback e).obj M` on `P` is FLT-invertible, by
   `Picard.isInvertible_of_isLocallyFreeRankOne` and `Scheme.Modules.IsInvertible.pullback`. Applying
   `exists_fg_isInvertible_nonempty_pullback_iso` at `i` to `pullback.snd f₀ ι₀` gives `j ≥ i`,
   an FLT-invertible module `Lj` on `Q := pullback (pullback.snd f₀ ι₀) (Spec.map (fgTransition i j hij))`,
   and `hj`: for every compatible `cX : P ⟶ Q`, `(pullback cX).obj Lj ≅ (pullback e).obj M`.
3. **From `Q` to the chosen base change of `X₀` to `Spec j`.** Write
   `Z := pullback f₀ (Spec.map (algebraMap A₀ (FGStage j)))`. Define
   `u := pullback.lift (pullback.lift Z.fst (Z.snd ≫ Spec.map (fgTransition i j hij)) _) Z.snd _`.
   The inner lift needs `Z.fst ≫ f₀ = (Z.snd ≫ Spec.map (fgTransition i j hij)) ≫ ι₀`, which is
   `pullback.condition` with `spec_map_fgTransition_comp`; the outer condition is
   `pullback.lift_snd`.
4. **Choice of `T` and `M₀`.** Take `T := j.1`. It is finitely generated, and it contains `s`
   because `s ⊆ Algebra.adjoin A₀ s = i ≤ j` (`Algebra.subset_adjoin`). Take
   `M₀ := (pullback u).obj Lj` on `Z`. It is locally free of rank one by
   `Scheme.Modules.IsInvertible.pullback` and `isLocallyFreeRankOne_of_isInvertible`.
5. **The comparison along every `c`.** Let `c : X ⟶ Z` with `c ≫ Z.fst = a` and
   `c ≫ Z.snd = f ≫ Spec.map (algebraMap T A)`. Apply `hj` to `cX := e ≫ c ≫ u`.
   - `cX ≫ Q.fst = P.fst` by `pullback.hom_ext` on `Y`. Composed with `Y.fst`, the left side is
     `e ≫ c ≫ Z.fst = e ≫ a = P.fst ≫ Y.fst`. Composed with `Y.snd`, the left side is
     `e ≫ c ≫ Z.snd ≫ Spec.map (fgTransition i j hij)`. This becomes
     `P.snd ≫ Spec.map (fgToLimit j) ≫ Spec.map (fgTransition i j hij) = P.snd ≫ Spec.map (fgToLimit i)`
     by the hypothesis on `c`, `e ≫ f = P.snd`, `algebraMap_fgStage` and
     `spec_map_fgToLimit_fgTransition`. That equals `P.fst ≫ Y.snd` by `pullback.condition`.
   - `cX ≫ Q.snd = P.snd ≫ Spec.map (fgToLimit j)`, since `u ≫ Q.snd = Z.snd`, by the
     hypothesis on `c`, `e ≫ f = P.snd` and `algebraMap_fgStage`.

   So `hj` gives `(pullback (e ≫ (c ≫ u))).obj Lj ≅ (pullback e).obj M`.
   `nonempty_iso_pullback_of_section` with `e' ≫ e = 𝟙 X` turns this into
   `(pullback (c ≫ u)).obj Lj ≅ M`. Composing with `Scheme.Modules.pullbackComp c u` at `Lj`
   gives `(pullback c).obj M₀ ≅ M`.

The proof uses the pullback property of the square `hpb` (for `e` and for `e' ≫ e = 𝟙 X`). It uses
no property of `c` beyond the two equations of the statement.
