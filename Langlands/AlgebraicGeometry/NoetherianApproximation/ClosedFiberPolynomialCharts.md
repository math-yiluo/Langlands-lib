# Polynomial charts from a closed fibre

`integerPolynomialChart` proves exactly `IntegerPolynomialChartStatement`
at arbitrary universe. For a characteristic-zero finite type integer domain
and a maximal ideal, it constructs an injective map from a polynomial ring
over the integers that is quasi-finite at that ideal.

The contracted ideal in the integers is maximal: its residue field is finite
over the integers by the Jacobson form of Zariski's lemma. Noether normalization
of the closed fibre over this integer residue field gives a finite injective
polynomial map. Choose lifts of its variables. The resulting map from the
integer polynomial ring has kernel in the ideal generated by the integer
prime, and the principal-kernel helper makes it injective. The finite closed
fibre and the quotient quasi-finiteness helper give quasi-finiteness.

This is an internal derivation from Mathlib's field Noether normalization,
Krull intersection and quasi-finiteness results. The draft does not yet
produce a finite normalization or a completed proof of the task's target.

The auxiliary proof that the integers are a Jacobson ring treats nonzero
prime ideals using the principal ideal domain theorem. For the zero ideal,
the Jacobson criterion would make `z*z+1` a unit for every element `z` of
its Jacobson radical. The only integer units are `1` and `-1`, forcing
`z=0`. This supplies the Jacobson hypothesis without an extra assumption.
