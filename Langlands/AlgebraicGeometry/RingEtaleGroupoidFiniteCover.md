# Finite surjective covers at finitely generated levels

Author: Worker 266.

## Setting

`t₀ : B₀ →+* C₀` is a ring étale groupoid (`RingEtaleGroupoid`). `B` and `B'`
are `B₀`-algebras with multiplicative coactions `θ`, `θ'` (Library
`GroupoidCoaction`, `GroupoidCoaction.IsMultiplicative`). `ψ : B →ₐ[B₀] B'` is
equivariant: `θ' (ψ b) = (C₀ ⊗ ψ) (θ b)`. In the proof of NL, `ψ` is
`f'.appTop` for the finite surjective cover `f' : E'.U ⟶ E.U`.

## Declarations

In the namespace `RingEtaleGroupoid`:

* `ker_le_nilradical_of_comap_surjective`: if `PrimeSpectrum.comap ψ` is
  surjective, every element of the kernel of `ψ` lies in every prime ideal,
  hence is nilpotent.
* `comap_surjective_of_isIntegral`: an integral ring map whose kernel consists
  of nilpotents induces a surjection of prime spectra. The kernel lies in every
  prime, so Mathlib's lying-over theorem
  `Ideal.exists_ideal_over_prime_of_isIntegral` applies.
* `map_map_apply` and `lTensor_comp_mem_range`: tensor identities.
  `lTensor_comp_mem_range` says that the tensor image of a map that factors
  through a subalgebra `S` lies in the tensor image of `S`.
* `subalgebraCoaction_map`: let `φ : D →ₐ[B₀] D'` be the restriction of `ψ` to
  stable subalgebras `D ⊆ B` and `D' ⊆ B'`. Then `φ` is equivariant for the
  restricted coactions `RingEtaleGroupoid.subalgebraCoaction` (module
  `RingEtaleGroupoidStableSubalgebra`). The proof checks this after the
  injective map `C₀ ⊗ D' → C₀ ⊗ B'`.
* `exists_finite_level`: let `B₀` be Noetherian, `ψ` finite, and the kernel of
  `ψ` nil. For every finite `s ⊆ B'` there are stable subalgebras `D ⊆ B` and
  `D' ⊆ B'` and a restriction `φ : D →ₐ[B₀] D'` of `ψ` such that `D` is finitely
  generated, `s ⊆ D'`, `φ` is finite, and `PrimeSpectrum.comap φ` is surjective.
  Construction:
  1. `F ⊆ B'` is a finitely generated stable subalgebra containing `s`
     (`exists_fg_stableSubalgebra`), generated by a finite set `Y`.
  2. Each `y ∈ Y` is integral over `B`, being a root of a monic `P_y ∈ B[X]`.
     `D ⊆ B` is a finitely generated stable subalgebra containing the
     coefficients of all `P_y`.
  3. `D' = adjoin (ψ(D) ∪ F)`. It is stable by `isStableSubalgebra_adjoin`:
     the coaction of `ψ d` comes from `C₀ ⊗ D` by equivariance, and that of an
     element of `F` from `C₀ ⊗ F`. `D'` is generated over `B₀` by `ψ` of
     generators of `D` together with `Y`, so it is of finite type over `D`.
     The `P_y` lift to monic polynomials over `D` (`Polynomial.lifts`), so `Y`
     is integral over `D`. Hence `D'` is integral over `D`, by comparing it with
     the integral closure of `D` in `D'`.
  4. Integral and of finite type gives finite (`Algebra.IsIntegral.finite`). The
     kernel of `φ` is nil, because the kernel of `ψ` is nil and `D → B` is
     injective. So `comap_surjective_of_isIntegral` gives surjectivity.

## Provenance and scope

This is step 3 of the ring-level route in the task's `context.md` for the proof
of Stacks Project Lemma 70.15.1 (Tag 07VS). There, `D` contains the
coefficients of monic equations over `B` of generators of a finite stable
submodule, `D' = D[F]`, and the kernel of `D → D'` lies in the nil kernel of
`B → B'`. One difference: here `F` is a finitely generated stable subalgebra
chosen to contain a prescribed finite set `s`, the set needed for the affine
quotient at the level (`RingEtaleGroupoidQuotientLevel`). It need not contain
`B`-module generators of `B'`, because the proof of NL uses a single level and
no union `⋃ D' = B'`.
