# Actual relative Hom colimit and its canonical comparison

This internal construction exposes the set colimit and natural comparison in
Brian Conrad, *Weil and Grothendieck approaches to adelic points* (December 31,
2011), printed pp. 5–6, Theorem 3.4(1). It uses the actual relative morphisms
constructed in `SchemeLimitModelMaps`. Bijectivity and the assertion that the
base-change cocone is a colimit require separate descent proofs.

Fix a small category `I`, a diagram `D : I ⥤ Scheme`, a compatible family
`tᵢ : Dᵢ → S`, and two arbitrary scheme morphisms `a : X → S` and `b : Y → S`.
All objects and morphisms share an arbitrary universe `u`. There are no
filteredness, affineness, separation, finite type or finite presentation
hypotheses in this file.

`homFunctor D t a b : Iᵒᵖ ⥤ Type u` has value `LevelHom D t a b i` at `op i`.
These are actual morphisms `Dᵢ ×ₛ X → Dᵢ ×ₛ Y` over `Dᵢ`. On the opposite
of an arrow `r : i → j`, the functor uses the existing actual base-change map
`restrict r`, from morphisms at `j` to morphisms at `i`. Its functor laws are
the previously proved restriction identity and composition laws.

`HomColimit D t a b` is Mathlib's `Functor.ColimitType` of this functor:
the quotient of the disjoint union of the level morphism sets by the equivalence
relation generated by the functor's transition identifications. Thus the
carrier is an actual type colimit. It is not a supplied type with an assumed
comparison. `homColimitι i` sends a level morphism to its class.
`homColimitι_restrict` proves that restriction preserves that class;
`homColimitι_jointly_surjective` gives a representative for each class. Both
are intrinsic quotient laws, without any descent claim.

For any supplied cone `c` of `D`, map `p : c.pt → S`, and equations
`c.πᵢ ≫ tᵢ = p`, `homCoconeTypes` has point `LimitHom a b p` and legs
`toLimit i`. Its naturality is the existing `toLimit_restrict` identity.
`homCocone` is the corresponding categorical cocone of types, obtained using
Mathlib's `Functor.coconeTypesEquiv`. The lemma `homCocone_ι_app` identifies
each categorical leg with actual base change. These constructions need no
witness that `c` is a limit.

`homColimitMap` is the canonical map from `HomColimit` to `LimitHom`, defined
by `Functor.descColimitType` on that cocone. `homColimitMap_ι` proves that it
sends every level representative to its actual base change over `c.pt`.
Consequently the definition realizes the natural Hom comparison itself.

The provenance is internal: this file packages already constructed relative
morphisms and their intrinsic coherence into Mathlib's quotient and cocone
interfaces. Conrad's directed ring situation is obtained by taking the
opposite diagram of spectra over the initial model base. This file does not
yet construct that spectral specialization or prove injectivity, surjectivity,
bijectivity, or the colimit property of `homCocone`. Initial model existence,
property descent, arithmetic realization and topology also remain separate.
