# Specializing section-selected components over a DVR

This internal unit constructs an open neighbourhood of a clopen in a closed residue fibre and proves the two-section DVR step used in the proof of EGA IV, third part (1966), Proposition 15.6.4, printed pp.237–238 (retained PDF pages236–237). Corollary 15.6.5, printed pp.238–239 (retained PDF pages237–238), supplies the downstream finite-presentation motivation. The unit proves the DVR step for flat morphisms locally of finite presentation. It does not yet realize general Noetherian generizations by DVRs or prove the arbitrary-base openness proposition.

For an actual morphism `p : X ⟶ Y`, a closed point `y`, and a clopen `C` of the actual residue fibre `p.fiber y`, `fiberClopenNeighbourhood p y hy C` is the open subscheme

\[
U=X\setminus p.\mathrm{fiberι}(y)\bigl((p.\mathrm{fiber}(y))\setminus C\bigr).
\]

The fibre inclusion is an embedding with closed range `p ⁻¹' {y}`, hence a closed embedding. The image of the closed complement of `C` is therefore closed. `fiberι_mem_fiberClopenNeighbourhood` proves the exact trace identity: a fibre point maps into `U` precisely when it belongs to `C`. These constructions require no Noetherian or finite-type assumptions.

`openFiberMap` is the actual morphism on residue fibres induced by a source restriction. Its composite with the original fibre inclusion is the restricted fibre inclusion followed by the source map; it is an open immersion whenever that source map is. `range_openFiberMap_neighbourhood` shows that the special fibre of `U` maps into `C`. Factoring this open immersion through the open subscheme `C.toOpens` proves `isReduced_fiber_fiberClopenNeighbourhood`: reducedness of that actual open subscheme makes the actual special fibre of `U` reduced. `fiberClopenInclusion` constructs the morphism from `C.toOpens` into `U`, with its explicit factorization identity.

`range_section_subset_fiberClopenNeighbourhood` proves that any actual section whose fibre point lies in `C` factors through `U`. Indeed, a point of the section lying in the removed part must lie over `y`; the section identity then identifies it with the section point in that fibre, which belongs to `C`.

The principal theorem `section_generic_mem_componentSet_of_clopen` takes an arbitrary universe `u`, a domain DVR `R : Type u`, a scheme `X : Scheme.{u}`, a flat morphism `p : X ⟶ Spec R` locally of finite presentation, and two actual sections `e` and `v`. It assumes a connected reduced clopen `C` in the actual closed residue fibre containing both section fibre points. It concludes that `v` at the zero prime belongs to `componentSet p e he`, the original union of residue-fibre connected components selected by `e`. No quasi-compactness, quasi-separatedness, separatedness, or reducedness of the other special-fibre components is assumed.

The implemented proof first factors both sections through `U`. The connected image of `C` in `U` contains their two closed points, so those points belong to one connected component of `U`. The DVR base is connected, and the image of each entire section is connected. Consequently both sections factor through the same connected component `W` of `U`; `range_map_subset_connectedComponentOpen` supplies the general connected-image argument. Local finite presentation over the Noetherian DVR spectrum makes the source locally Noetherian, so this component is an actual open subscheme. Its actual closed fibre is reduced because it is an open subscheme of the reduced closed fibre of `U`.

The morphism `W ⟶ Spec R` remains flat and locally of finite presentation. The imported theorem `FlatReducedDvrConnectedness.preconnectedSpace_genericFiber_of_reduced_specialFiber` makes its actual generic residue fibre preconnected. The two section points in that fibre therefore lie in the same connected component. Finally `image_componentSet_subset` maps their membership along the actual inclusion `W ⟶ X`. This argument implements the neighbourhood-removal and connected-component reduction in the cited proof of Proposition 15.6.4, using the separately proved flat-DVR connectedness theorem.

`fiberComponentClopen` bundles the selected component of any locally finite-type residue fibre as a clopen; its underlying open is `fiberComponentOpen`, and it is connected. The corollary `section_generic_mem_componentSet_of_closed` specializes the principal theorem to this component. Its only reducedness premise is `IsReduced (fiberComponentOpen p e he (IsLocalRing.closedPoint R))`; its incidence premise is `v(closed) ∈ componentSet p e he`, and its conclusion is the same membership at the generic point. Geometric reducedness is unnecessary for this already-fixed DVR family. It will be needed when deriving this reducedness premise after a general base change.
