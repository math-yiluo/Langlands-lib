# Valued points and generization in selected fibre components

This internal unit constructs a section from an actual scheme-valued point and proves the DVR-valued-point step for the unchanged `SectionComponent.componentSet`. It does not yet assert general generization stability or relative openness. Its universe `u` is arbitrary.

For actual morphisms `f : X ⟶ Y` and `a : T ⟶ X`, `valuedPointSection f a` is the actual pullback lift of `a` and the identity of `T` into `X ×[Y] T`, with base map `a ≫ f`. The two projection identities are `valuedPointSection_fst` and `valuedPointSection_snd`. Thus this valued point becomes a second section of the base-changed family.

`isReduced_fiberComponentOpen_of_isPullback` considers an actual Cartesian square, locally finite type original and new structure morphisms, and two commuting actual sections. Geometric reducedness of the original selected open component in the residue fibre at the image of `y'` implies ordinary scheme reducedness of the new selected open component at `y'`. The proof pastes the Cartesian residue-fibre square with the square restricting to the original component. The definition of geometric reducedness gives reducedness of this actual pullback along the residue-field extension. The isomorphism `fiberComponentBaseChangeIso` then transfers reducedness to the new selected component. Neither ambient reducedness nor reducedness of unselected components is assumed.

`dvr_generic_mem_componentSet` assumes a flat morphism locally of finite presentation, an actual section, a domain DVR `R : Type u`, and an actual map `a : Spec R ⟶ X`. Its reducedness premise is geometric reducedness of the indexed selected component at `f (a (IsLocalRing.closedPoint R))`. If `a` maps the closed point into `componentSet`, then it maps the zero prime into `componentSet`. The proof base changes the family along `a ≫ f`, takes `valuedPointSection f a` as the second section, and transports the closed-point membership by the established exact preimage identity for `componentSet`. The preceding reducedness lemma supplies reducedness of the selected special-fibre component. The two-section theorem `section_generic_mem_componentSet_of_closed` gives generic membership, which is transported back by the same preimage identity and the first projection identity. No quasi-compactness, separation, or Noetherian hypothesis on `X` or `Y` is imposed in this DVR-valued-point result.

The provenance is internal: the pullback construction, Cartesian fibre square, geometric reducedness, and the previously established two-section DVR theorem give this comparison directly. The motivation is EGA IV3, Proposition 15.6.4, printed pp.237–238 (retained PDF pages236–237), especially the second-section reduction on p.238. Corollary 15.6.5, printed pp.238–239 (retained PDF pages237–238), motivates the remaining finite-presentation openness task. Realizing general Noetherian specializations by DVR-valued points and the full arbitrary-base openness theorem remain separate obligations.
