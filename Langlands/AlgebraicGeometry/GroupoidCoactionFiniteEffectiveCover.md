# Finite effective covers of ring étale equivalence relations and their quotients

Module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.GroupoidCoactionFiniteEffectiveCover`, namespace
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.GroupoidCoaction`. It imports `GroupoidCoactionGenericEvaluation`,
`GroupoidCoactionPushout` and `RingEtaleEquivalenceRelationQuotient` from the Library, and the
Mathlib files on going-up (`Ideal.Over`), faithfully flat ring maps and quotients of tensor
products.

This module is a ring-level ingredient for the comodule proof of the Noetherian case of
finite-surjective affine descent (Stacks, Cohomology of Algebraic Spaces, Lemma 69.17.3,
Tag 07VP). It was written for the Key proof task 74e16b76 (Worker 148), whose Noetherian
induction over invariant ideals needs the cover data to pass to closed invariant subspaces, and
in the end for this task (Tag 07VS). Source: the registered folder
`stacks-noetherian-space-affine-descent-2026-11-01`, page `07VP.html`.

## Setting

All rings are commutative and live in one universe `u`. `t : B →+* C` (with `[Algebra B C]`, the
source map) is the ring form of an affine étale equivalence relation `R ⇉ U`, `U = Spec B`,
`R = Spec C`. A covering relation `td : D →+* P` stands for `Y ×_X U ⇉`, where `Y → X` is the
finite surjective map from an affine scheme `Y`.

## Declarations

- `structure FiniteEffectiveCover (t : B →+* C)` (the selected declaration). Fields:
  - the rings `D` and `P`, with instance fields `CommRing D`, `CommRing P`, `Algebra B D`,
    `Algebra D P`, `Algebra B P` and `IsScalarTower B D P`;
  - `td : D →+* P` and `relation : RingEtaleEquivalenceRelation td`;
  - `e : P ≃ₐ[D] D ⊗[B] C` and `cartesian : (cartesianBaseMap e).comp t = td.comp (algebraMap B D)`.
    Together they say that the covering relation is source-cartesian over `t`;
  - the instance field `Module.FaithfullyFlat (invariantRing td) D`;
  - `ε : P ≃ₐ[D] D ⊗[invariantRing td] D` and
    `ε_target : ∀ d, ε (td d) = 1 ⊗ₜ d`. Together they say that the covering relation is the
    tensor square of `D` over its invariant ring, so it is effective with quotient
    `Spec (invariantRing td)` (the role of the affine `Y`);
  - the instance field `Module.Finite B D`;
  - `comap_surjective : Function.Surjective (PrimeSpectrum.comap (algebraMap B D))`.

  These fields are exactly the cover hypotheses of the Library theorem
  `GroupoidCoaction.exists_genericEvaluation`. The eight instance-implicit fields are registered
  as instances.
- `FiniteEffectiveCover.exists_genericEvaluation`: `exists_genericEvaluation` restated for a
  cover `X`. It is a direct application with the fields of `X`.
- Ideals attached to an ideal `I` of `B`: `coverIdeal I = I D` (`abbrev`, the image of `I` under
  `algebraMap B D`) and `relationIdeal I = (I D) P` (`abbrev`, the image of `I D` under the
  source map `algebraMap D P`).
- `relationIdeal_eq_map_td`: for an invariant ideal (`IsInvariantIdeal t I`, i.e.
  `I.map t = I.map (algebraMap B C)`), `I P` is also the image of `I D` under `td`. Hence
  `quotientTd hI : D ⧸ I D →+* P ⧸ I P` (the map induced by `td`, `quotientTd_mk`) is defined,
  and `quotient_relation` shows that it is a ring étale equivalence relation (Library lemma
  `RingEtaleEquivalenceRelation.quotient`).
- Invariants. `quotientInvariantMap hI : invariantRing td →+* invariantRing (quotientTd hI)` is
  the reduction of invariants (`quotientInvariantMap_coe`).
  - `quotientInvariantMap_surjective`: every invariant of the quotient relation lifts to an
    invariant of `td`.
  - `invariantKernel hI` is its kernel (`abbrev`). By `mem_invariantKernel`, it is
    `invariantRing td ∩ I D`.
  - `coverIdeal_eq_map_invariantKernel`: `I D` is generated by its invariant elements.
- Faithful flatness.
  - `quotientInvariantEquiv hI`: the ring isomorphism
    `invariantRing td ⧸ invariantKernel hI ≃+* invariantRing (quotientTd hI)`, with
    `quotientInvariantEquiv_symm_apply`.
  - `quotientBaseChangeEquiv hI`: the ring isomorphism
    `(invariantRing td ⧸ invariantKernel hI) ⊗[invariantRing td] D ≃+* D ⧸ I D`, with
    `quotientBaseChangeEquiv_one_tmul`.
  - `algebraMap_quotient_eq_comp`: the algebra map of `D ⧸ I D` over the new invariant ring is
    the composite of these isomorphisms with the base-change algebra map.
  - `faithfullyFlat_algebraMap_quotient_tensor`: the base-change algebra map is faithfully flat.
  - `quotient_faithfullyFlat`: `D ⧸ I D` is faithfully flat over `invariantRing (quotientTd hI)`.
- Tensor square. `quotientTensorMap`, `quotientSquareInv`, `ε_symm_tmul` and
  `quotient_relationTensorHom_bijective` show that the Library map `relationTensorHom` of the
  quotient relation is bijective. `quotientε hI` is the inverse algebra isomorphism
  `P ⧸ I P ≃ₐ[D ⧸ I D] (D ⧸ I D) ⊗[invariantRing (quotientTd hI)] (D ⧸ I D)`, and
  `quotientε_target` gives its value on `quotientTd hI d`.

  `quotientCartesianInv` and the two composition lemmas build
  `quotientEquiv I : P ⧸ I P ≃ₐ[D ⧸ I D] (D ⧸ I D) ⊗[B ⧸ I] (C ⧸ I C)`. Here `C ⧸ I C` is the

  `quotient_cartesian` is the compatibility with the target maps, where the quotient relation of

- `quotient_finite`: `D ⧸ I D` is finite over `B ⧸ I`.
- `quotient_comap_surjective`: `Spec (D ⧸ I D) → Spec (B ⧸ I)` is surjective.
- `quotientAlgebraBP I`: the `B ⧸ I`-algebra structure on `P ⧸ I P` through `D ⧸ I D` (`abbrev`).
- `def FiniteEffectiveCover.quotient (hI : IsInvariantIdeal t I) :
  FiniteEffectiveCover (RingEtaleEquivalenceRelation.quotientTarget I hI.symm)`: the cover of
  the quotient relation `B ⧸ I → C ⧸ I C`, with covering rings `D ⧸ I D` and `P ⧸ I P` and the
  data above.

## Proofs

- Lifting invariants (`quotientInvariantMap_surjective`). Let `x ∈ D` reduce to an invariant
  of the quotient relation. Then `td x - x` lies in `(I D) P`, which is the image of `I D` under
  `td`. Apply `ε` and then `D ⊗[A] D → D ⊗[A] (D ⧸ I D)` (`A = invariantRing td`). Elements of
  `td (I D)` go to `0`, so `1 ⊗ x̄ = x ⊗ 1`. Map further to `D ⊗[A] ((D ⧸ I D) ⧸ image of A)`.
  This gives `1 ⊗ [x̄] = 0`, and faithful flatness of `D` over `A`
  (`Module.FaithfullyFlat.one_tmul_eq_zero_iff`) gives `[x̄] = 0`. So `x̄` is the image of an
  invariant `a ∈ A`.
- Descent of `I D` (`coverIdeal_eq_map_invariantKernel`). Let `x ∈ I D`. Then `td x ∈ (I D) P`.
  Apply `ε` and `D ⊗[A] D → (D ⧸ I D) ⊗[A] D`; this gives `1 ⊗ x = 0` in `(D ⧸ I D) ⊗[A] D`.
  The sequence `A ∩ I D → A → D ⧸ I D` is exact, and tensoring with the flat `A`-module `D` keeps
  it exact (`Module.Flat.rTensor_exact`). So `1 ⊗ x` comes from `(A ∩ I D) ⊗[A] D`, whose image
  in `D` lies in `(A ∩ I D) D`.
- Faithful flatness. By the two previous facts, `D ⧸ I D = D ⧸ (A ∩ I D) D` is the base change
  `(A ⧸ (A ∩ I D)) ⊗[A] D`, using Mathlib's `Algebra.TensorProduct.quotIdealMapEquivQuotTensor`,
  and `A ⧸ (A ∩ I D)` is the new invariant ring. Faithful flatness is stable under base change
  (Mathlib instance) and under composition with ring isomorphisms
  (`RingHom.FaithfullyFlat.stableUnderComposition`, `RingHom.FaithfullyFlat.of_bijective`).
- Tensor square. The map `D ⊗[A] D → D' ⊗[A'] D'` (`D' = D ⧸ I D`, `A'` the new invariant
  ring) is defined with `tensorRingLift`. Composed with `ε`, it kills `(I D) P` and descends to
  `P ⧸ I P`. It is inverse to `relationTensorHom` of the quotient relation; this is checked on
  pure tensors with `ε.symm (d ⊗ d') = d · td d'`.
- Cartesian square. The proof is the same with `e`. The map `D ⊗[B] C → D' ⊗[B ⧸ I] C'`
  descends to `P ⧸ I P`. The inverse `D' ⊗[B ⧸ I] C' → P ⧸ I P` is `tensorRingLift` of the
  source map and of the reduction of `cartesianBaseMap e`; this uses
  `I C ≤ (I P).comap (cartesianBaseMap e)` (`map_le_comap_cartesianBaseMap`). The
  target compatibility is the reduction of `X.cartesian`.
- Finiteness is `Module.Finite.of_restrictScalars_finite`. For surjectivity on spectra, a
  prime `p'` of `B ⧸ I` pulls back to a prime of `B`, which is the contraction of a prime `Q` of
  `D`. Then `I D ≤ Q`, and the image of `Q` in `D ⧸ I D` is a prime over `p'`.

## Provenance and relation to the literature

Provenance: derived (internal ring-level packaging). The structure `FiniteEffectiveCover`
collects the cover hypotheses of `exists_genericEvaluation`. They are the ring form of the
situation of Tag 07VP: a finite surjective `f : Y → X` with `Y` affine, pulled back to an affine
étale chart `U → X`. The constructor `quotient` is the ring form of the passage to a closed
subspace used in that proof. For a reduced closed subspace `Z ⊆ X`, the proof of Tag 07VP takes
`Z' → Z` as in Lemma 69.17.1: it is again finite surjective, and `Z'` is affine "as a closed
subscheme of an affine scheme". Here `Z` corresponds to an invariant ideal `I` of the chart ring
`B`. The pulled-back cover of `Z` has rings `D ⧸ I D` and `P ⧸ I P`, and its quotient is
`Spec (A ⧸ (A ∩ I D))`, the ring form of `Z' = Y ×_X Z`.

Stacks restricts to reduced irreducible `Z`. Here `I` is an arbitrary invariant ideal, because the
Key task runs a Noetherian induction over all invariant ideals of the chart ring. Stacks does not
spell out the faithful flatness of `D ⧸ I D` over the new invariant ring or the identification
of that ring with `A ⧸ (A ∩ I D)`. The proofs above supply them by faithfully flat descent. The
choice of fields and the ring-level constructions are internal to this formalization and follow
the interface of the Key task.
