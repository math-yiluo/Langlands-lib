# Relative differentials for a general pullback functor of ring presheaves

Module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.PresheafRelativeDifferentials`, namespace
`Langlands.PresheafDifferentials`.

Mathlib (`Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Differentials/Presheaf.lean`)
defines, for a functor `F : C ⥤ D`, presheaves of commutative rings
`S : Cᵒᵖ ⥤ CommRingCat.{u}` and `R : Dᵒᵖ ⥤ CommRingCat.{u}` and a morphism
`φ : S ⟶ F.op ⋙ R`, the type `M.Derivation φ` of `φ`-derivations into a presheaf
of `R`-modules `M`, and the structure `Derivation.Universal`. It constructs a
universal derivation (`DifferentialsConstruction.relativeDifferentials'`,
`derivation'`, `isUniversal'`) only when `F` is the identity functor; the general
case is a TODO there. This module supplies the general construction of the
presheaf of relative differentials and of its derivation. The universal property
is the separate proposition
`Langlands.PresheafDifferentials.DerivationUniversalStatement`
(module `PresheafRelativeDifferentialsStatement`), proved in a separate module.

## Setting and universes

Throughout, `C : Type u₁` with `[Category.{v₁} C]` and `D : Type u₂` with
`[Category.{v₂} D]` are arbitrary categories; no smallness is assumed. Hence the
construction covers in particular small categories `C`, `D` at universe `u`.
The ring presheaves take values in `CommRingCat.{u}`. A φ-derivation in Mathlib is
a family of additive maps `d : R.obj Y →+ M.obj Y`, for all `Y : Dᵒᵖ`, that
satisfies the Leibniz rule `d (a * b) = a • d b + b • d a` (`d_mul`), commutes
with restriction, `d (R.map f x) = M.map f (d x)` (`d_map`), and vanishes on the
image of `S`, `d (φ.app X a) = 0` for all `X : Cᵒᵖ` and `a : S.obj X` (`d_app`).

## Declarations

- `generators φ Y : Set (R.obj Y)` for `Y : Dᵒᵖ` is the set of all elements
  `R.map f (φ.app X a)`, where `X : Cᵒᵖ`, `f : F.op.obj X ⟶ Y` (that is,
  `f : op (F.obj X.unop) ⟶ Y`) and `a : S.obj X`.
- `pullbackSubring φ Y : Subring (R.obj Y)` is `Subring.closure (generators φ Y)`,
  the subring generated by these elements.
- `map_mem_pullbackSubring`: for `g : Y ⟶ Y'` in `Dᵒᵖ`, `R.map g` maps
  `pullbackSubring φ Y` into `pullbackSubring φ Y'` (a generator
  `R.map f (φ.app X a)` goes to the generator `R.map (f ≫ g) (φ.app X a)`).
- `pullbackRing φ : Dᵒᵖ ⥤ CommRingCat.{u}` sends `Y` to the commutative ring
  `pullbackSubring φ Y` and `g` to the restriction of `R.map g`
  (`RingHom.restrict`). The `@[simps]` attribute generates its `obj`/`map`
  lemmas.
- `pullbackRingι φ : pullbackRing φ ⟶ R` is the inclusion, whose component at
  `Y` is `(pullbackSubring φ Y).subtype` (with `@[simps]` lemmas).
- `derivation_eq_zero_of_mem`: for any presheaf of `R`-modules `M` (module
  universe `v` arbitrary) and any `d : M.Derivation φ`, `d.d b = 0` for every
  `b ∈ pullbackSubring φ Y`. Proof by closure induction: on a generator,
  `d (R.map f (φ.app X a)) = M.map f (d (φ.app X a)) = M.map f 0 = 0`; the
  zero, one, sum, negative and product cases use additivity, `d 1 = 0` and the
  Leibniz rule.
- `toPullbackDerivation d : M.Derivation' (pullbackRingι φ)` regards a
  φ-derivation `d` as a derivation relative to `pullbackRingι φ` (Mathlib's
  `Derivation'` is `Derivation` with `F` the identity). The maps `d.d` are
  unchanged; the vanishing condition is `derivation_eq_zero_of_mem`.
- `ofPullbackDerivation d : M.Derivation φ` is the converse: a derivation
  relative to `pullbackRingι φ` with the same maps `d.d` is a φ-derivation,
  because `φ.app X a = R.map (𝟙 _) (φ.app X a)` is a generator of
  `pullbackSubring φ (F.op.obj X)`.
- `derivationEquiv : M.Derivation φ ≃ M.Derivation' (pullbackRingι φ)` packages
  the two conversions; both composites are the identity by `rfl`.
- `toPullbackDerivation_d`, `ofPullbackDerivation_d` (simp lemmas): the
  conversions do not change `d.d b`.
- `toPullbackDerivation_postcomp`, `ofPullbackDerivation_postcomp`: the
  conversions commute with Mathlib's `Derivation.postcomp` along a morphism
  `α : M ⟶ N` of presheaves of `R`-modules.
- `relativeDifferentials φ : PresheafOfModulesOfCommRing.{u} R` is defined as
  `DifferentialsConstruction.relativeDifferentials' (pullbackRingι φ)`. Its
  module over `Y` is `CommRingCat.KaehlerDifferential ((pullbackRingι φ).app Y)`,
  the Kähler differentials `Ω[R.obj Y ⁄ pullbackSubring φ Y]` of `R.obj Y`
  relative to the inclusion of the subring. Its restriction maps are Mathlib's
  functorial maps of Kähler differentials induced by `R.map g`.
- `derivation φ : (relativeDifferentials φ).Derivation φ` is
  `ofPullbackDerivation (DifferentialsConstruction.derivation' (pullbackRingι φ))`.
- `derivation_d`: `(derivation φ).d b = CommRingCat.KaehlerDifferential.d b`
  for `b : R.obj Y`, i.e. on each `Y` it is the universal Kähler differential
  `b ↦ d b` (proved by `rfl`).

The construction uses no `HasDifferentials φ` hypothesis and no chosen universal
object: `relativeDifferentials φ` and `derivation φ` are explicit. The two
`noncomputable example`s at the end check that the construction applies to every
morphism of schemes `f : X ⟶ S` in `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.{u}`, with
`F := TopologicalSpace.Opens.map f.base`, `S := S.presheaf`, `R := X.presheaf` and
`φ := f.c`; they give
`relativeDifferentials (F := Opens.map f.base) f.c :
PresheafOfModulesOfCommRing.{u} X.presheaf` and its derivation
`derivation _ : (relativeDifferentials _ f.c).Derivation (F := Opens.map f.base) f.c`.
In that case `generators f.c U`, for an open `U` of `X`, consists of the
restrictions to `U` of the sections `f.c.app V a` with `V` open in `S`,
`U ≤ f⁻¹ V` and `a ∈ 𝒪_S(V)`.

## Provenance

This is internal infrastructure derived from the definition of relative
derivations; the literature does not state a theorem for general functors of
categories. The original source is the Stacks Project, Section 17.28 (tag 08RL),
registered as `stacks-relative-differentials-internal-hom-2026-10-21`
(*Stacks Project - Relative differentials and internal Hom (HTML snapshot
2026-10-21)*, file `08RL.html`, content SHA256
`9c075370ba97d8876785c5486425c6c661765e747452c026583f4dc51f1e1133`).

- Definition 17.28.1 defines, for a homomorphism of sheaves of rings
  `φ : 𝒪₁ → 𝒪₂` on a space, a φ-derivation into an `𝒪₂`-module `ℱ` as an
  additive map `D : 𝒪₂ → ℱ` that annihilates the image of `𝒪₁` and satisfies the
  Leibniz rule. Mathlib's `Derivation φ` is the presheaf version, with the
  vanishing condition stated through `φ : S ⟶ F.op ⋙ R`.
- Definition 17.28.10 defines, for a morphism of ringed spaces `f : X → S`, an
  `S`-derivation as an `f^♯`-derivation for `f^♯ : f⁻¹𝒪_S → 𝒪_X`, and `Ω_{X/S}`
  as the corresponding module of differentials.
- Lemma 17.28.2 and Definition 17.28.3 state that φ-derivations are represented
  by `Ω_{𝒪₂/𝒪₁}` with universal derivation `d`. Lemma 17.28.4 states that
  `Ω_{𝒪₂/𝒪₁}` is the sheaf associated to the presheaf
  `U ↦ Ω_{𝒪₂(U)/𝒪₁(U)}`, which motivates the sectionwise Kähler differentials
  used here.

The reduction to the identity-functor case is the one proposed in the header of
Mathlib's file: identify φ-derivations with derivations relative to a ring
presheaf on `D` mapping to `R`, observing that only the vanishing conditions
differ. Instead of the left Kan extension of `S` along `F.op`, this module uses
the subrings `pullbackSubring φ Y` of `R.obj Y`. Informally (this is not
formalized here), `pullbackSubring φ Y` is the image in `R.obj Y` of the colimit
over `(X, f : F.op.obj X ⟶ Y)` that computes the left Kan extension at `Y`,
because such a colimit of commutative rings is generated by the images of its
coprojections. For `F = Opens.map f.base` it is the image of the presheaf inverse
image `(f⁻¹𝒪_S)(U) = colim_{f(U) ⊆ V} 𝒪_S(V)` in `𝒪_X(U)`. The Lean
construction does not depend on these remarks: the only fact it uses is
`derivation_eq_zero_of_mem`, proved above.

This module does not sheafify, does not compare with Stacks' sheaf `Ω_{X/S}`
(which uses the sheaf inverse image), and does not construct functorial maps,
base change, or quasicoherence.
