# Comparing lifts with split infinitesimal points

Fix a square-zero ideal `J` of the test ring `C` and a surjection `C → D`
through which the scalar action on `J` factors. Two maps `g, h : Spec C → X`
with the same restriction to `Spec(C/J)` glue to a map from `Spec(C ⋉ J)`.
Its restriction to `Spec(D ⋉ J)` is `splitPoint g h`, with constant term
the restriction of the first lift `g` to `Spec D`. The other pushout
presentation of `Spec(C ⋉ J)` recovers `h` from `g` and that split point.
The same presentation constructs a lift from any split point with that
constant term.

Suppose the map `C → D` sends every element of `J` to zero. Then three lifts
with the same restriction to `Spec(C/J)` have the same restriction to
`Spec D`. In the split triple test the first and second faces give the two
successive differences, and the total face gives their sum. This is the
additive relation of the affine derivation description of SGA 1,
Exposé III, Proposition 5.1, printed pp. 59–60 (PDF pp. 75–76), in
`sga1-arxiv-math-0206203v2`, with SHA-256
`8e64218d356456c534eebf996940f0f957e43b54f1a080241debe12cbaf60d3c`.
