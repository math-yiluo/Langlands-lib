# Relative normalization of affine schemes

For a ring map `D → G`, `affineNormalizationIso` identifies the
relative normalization of `Spec D` in `Spec G` with the spectrum
of the integral closure of `D` in `G`. The compatibility lemma
identifies the map to `Spec D` with the one induced by the algebra
map into the integral closure.

The construction takes global sections of the relative normalization
on the affine open `⊤`. Naturality of the global sections isomorphisms
of spectra gives a compatible square of ring isomorphisms. The
integral closure comparison for such a square transports the
integral closure of the global rings to the integral closure of `D`
in `G`. The normalization is affine, so taking spectra recovers the
scheme isomorphism. Its compatibility with the structure map and
the affine finiteness criterion give the equivalence between finiteness
of the morphism and module finiteness of the integral closure.

This derived construction relates the affine integral closure in the
target to the scheme normalization used in Conrad's appendix to
Prasad–Yu, *On quasi-reductive group schemes*, author preprint,
Theorem A.2 and Remark A.1 (p. 28). The registered source is
