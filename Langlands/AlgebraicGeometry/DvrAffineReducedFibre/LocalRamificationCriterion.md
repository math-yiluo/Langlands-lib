# Testing a base uniformizer in local normalizations

Worker 594.

`localTensorNormalization` takes an algebra map \(B \to G\), a base
extension \(R \to S\), and a prime \(p\) of \(B\).
It is the integral closure of \(B_p \otimes_B (S \otimes_R B)\) in
\(B_p \otimes_B (S \otimes_R G)\).

For an injective local, integral, flat map of discrete valuation rings,
`baseUniformizerAtGenericPoints_of_localTests` transports the uniformizer
test from these local normalizations to the normalized base change.
The theorem assumes that \(B\) has integrally closed domain localizations
and that \(G\) is flat over \(B\). A prime minimal over the new uniformizer
is also minimal over the original uniformizer, since that uniformizer
maps to a unit times a power of the new one. Going down then shows that its
contraction is minimal over the original uniformizer in \(B\).

At this contracted prime, the corresponding prime of
`localTensorNormalization` is maximal by integrality. The comparison of
iterated localizations identifies the two local rings and transports the
ideal generated by the new uniformizer.

This is a derived local comparison for the generic-point criterion in
Conrad's appendix to Prasad–Yu, *On quasi-reductive group schemes*,
author preprint, Remark A.7 on printed and PDF p. 30. The registered source
is `prasad-yu-2006-quasi-reductive-author-preprint`, SHA-256
`138d931a21fa522d97449c77acf429918826b1ef649be940d4721d616a1fc73b`.
