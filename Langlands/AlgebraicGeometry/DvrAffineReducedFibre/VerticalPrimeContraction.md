# Contraction of vertical primes under an integral map

`primeTensorLocalization` extends a prime ideal to the tensor
product with a localized base when the prime is disjoint from
the denominators. This tensor product is the localization at
the corresponding scalar denominators. Its extended prime
contracts to the original prime by localization of ideals.

For an integral algebra contained in an ambient algebra flat
over the base, assume the base has normal domain localizations
at all primes. To lift a generalization below a given prime,
localize at the contraction of that prime. Flatness of localization
preserves the embedding into the ambient ring, and the localized
ambient ring is flat over the localized base. A nonzero scalar
of the localized base therefore acts as a non-zero-divisor on
the localized integral algebra. The integral going-down criterion
for a normal domain applies and contraction gives the desired
prime before localization.

For an algebra satisfying going down, the contraction of a prime
minimal over an extended ideal is minimal over the original ideal.
A smaller prime containing the original ideal would lift to a
smaller prime containing the extended ideal. This applies in
particular to the uniformizer ideals at the generic points of
normalized special fibres in Conrad's appendix to Prasad–Yu,
*On quasi-reductive group schemes*, author preprint, Remark A.7
on printed and PDF p. 30. The registered source is
`prasad-yu-2006-quasi-reductive-author-preprint`, SHA-256
