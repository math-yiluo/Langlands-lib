# Finite field extensions of the complete DVR base

Worker 594. The proof uses the finite separable extension result
from Worker 519.

`basisAdjoinFractionEquiv` identifies the fraction field of the
algebra generated by a field-extension basis over a domain with
the extension field. The image of the fraction-field map contains
the basis and the fraction field of the base domain, so it contains
the span of the basis.

For a finite extension of the fraction field of a complete DVR
with perfect residue field, choose a basis of integral elements.
The algebra it generates over the DVR is finite and has the given
fraction field. Finite normalization of this finite-type domain
and transitivity of integrality give finiteness of the integral
closure of the DVR in the field extension.

The integral closure in the maximal separable subextension is
local by the finite separable extension result. Each element of
the full integral closure has a power lying in the image of that
local ring, by pure inseparability over the separable subextension.
Maximal ideals contract to the same maximal ideal there. Primality
and the power property then identify any two maximal ideals of
the full integral closure. A finite local normal domain integral
over the DVR is again a DVR. Completeness for the extended base
maximal ideal follows from module finiteness, and that ideal is a
positive power of the new maximal ideal. The residue extension is
finite and is trivial if the base residue field is algebraically
closed.

This is the complete perfect-residue case of the change of base
in Conrad's appendix to Prasad–Yu, *On quasi-reductive group
schemes*, author preprint, Remark A.1 on printed and PDF p. 28.
