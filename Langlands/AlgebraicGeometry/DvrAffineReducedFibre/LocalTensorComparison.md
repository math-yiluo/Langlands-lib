# Comparing tensor presentations of a local normalization

Worker 594.

`tensorScalarExchangeEquiv` exchanges the order of extending scalars
from \(R\) to \(S\) and from an \(R\)-algebra \(B\) to a \(B\)-algebra
\(Q\). It sends \(q \otimes (s \otimes x)\) to
\(s \otimes (q \otimes x)\) by tensor symmetries and associativity.

Applied to the normalization at an original prime \(p\), this identifies
the normalization after localizing the tensor extension of \(B\) with
the integral closure of \(S \otimes_R B_p\) in
\(S \otimes_R (B_p \otimes_B G)\), where \(G\) is the ambient algebra.
The source and ambient comparisons commute with their algebra maps on
pure tensors, so monic equations transport in both directions.

The comparison preserves the map from \(S\). It therefore identifies
the two tests that a scalar from \(S\) generates the maximal ideal in
every maximal localization. The proof transports each localized
principal ideal and the corresponding maximal ideal through the
isomorphism of local rings.

This is a derived tensor comparison for local calculations at the
generic points of the normalized fibre in Conrad's appendix to
Prasad–Yu, *On quasi-reductive group schemes*, author preprint,
Remark A.7 on printed and PDF p. 30. The registered source is
`prasad-yu-2006-quasi-reductive-author-preprint`, SHA-256
