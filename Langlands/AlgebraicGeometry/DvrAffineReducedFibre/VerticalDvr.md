# Discrete valuation rings at the generic points of a principal fibre

Worker 519.

In a Noetherian ring, a prime minimal over a regular principal ideal has
height at most one by Krull's principal ideal theorem. If its local ring
is a normal domain, the regular generator has nonzero image in the
maximal ideal. The local ring is therefore a Dedekind domain with a
nonzero maximal ideal, hence a DVR.

For a nonzero nonunit in a DVR, the principal quotient is reduced if and
only if the generator is a uniformizer. If the quotient is reduced, the
principal ideal is radical. The factorization into a unit times a
uniformizer power puts the uniformizer itself in this radical ideal.
The ideal is then the maximal ideal, whose quotient is a field.

For the affine reduced fibre argument these lemmas identify the local
rings at generic points of the special fibre and let one test reducedness
there by the uniformizer condition. In Conrad's appendix to Prasad and Yu,
*On quasi-reductive group schemes*, author preprint, A.7 uses generic
reducedness and A.10 uses the DVR property of the local ring at a generic
point of the special fibre (p. 33).
