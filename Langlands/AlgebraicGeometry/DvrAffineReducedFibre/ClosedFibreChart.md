# Polynomial charts near the closed fibre of a DVR

Worker 594.

`ClosedFibrePolynomialChart` records an injective polynomial algebra
map over the base and quasi-finiteness of the map at the specified
prime. The proof constructs the chart for a finite-type domain over
a DVR when the structure map is injective and the prime contains the
image of a uniformizer.

Noether normalization gives a finite injective polynomial chart on
the closed fibre. Lifting the images of the variables gives a
polynomial map over the DVR. Its kernel is contained in the ideal
generated by the uniformizer in the polynomial ring. The image
of the uniformizer is nonzero, so divisibility by every power
of the uniformizer and the intersection theorem give injectivity.
The finite closed-fibre polynomial map gives quasi-finiteness at
the chosen prime by the finite-quotient criterion.

The proof generalizes the published closed-fibre chart argument over
the integers in `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.NoetherianApproximation.ClosedFiberPolynomialCharts`.
It is an auxiliary step toward the finiteness assertion in Conrad's
appendix to Prasad–Yu, *On quasi-reductive group schemes*, author preprint,
Theorem A.6 (p. 30). The registered source is
`prasad-yu-2006-quasi-reductive-author-preprint`, SHA-256
`138d931a21fa522d97449c77acf429918826b1ef649be940d4721d616a1fc73b`.
