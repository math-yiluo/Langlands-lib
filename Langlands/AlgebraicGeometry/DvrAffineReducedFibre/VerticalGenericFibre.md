# Generic fibres and integral extensions at vertical primes

Worker 594.

For an injective local map of DVRs, the image of a base
uniformizer is a unit times a positive power of a target
uniformizer. Every nonzero element of the target divides the
image of a power of the base uniformizer. Localization at the
nonzero scalars from the base is therefore a fraction field of
the target. `genericFibreFractionRingEquiv` compares the generic
fibre tensor with the target fraction ring by uniqueness of
localization.

The going-down lemma starts with a prime in an integral algebra
over an integrally closed domain. When each nonzero base scalar
acts as a non-zero-divisor, the map into the quotient by a minimal
prime below the given prime is injective. The quotient is a domain,
so the usual integral going-down theorem applies. Taking inverse
images lifts going down to the original algebra. In particular,
a prime minimal over the image of a principal ideal contracts to
a prime minimal over that principal ideal.

These are derived steps for studying the DVR localizations at
special fibre generic points of the normalizations in Conrad's
appendix to Prasad–Yu, *On quasi-reductive group schemes*, author
preprint, Remark A.7 on printed and PDF p. 30. The registered
source is `prasad-yu-2006-quasi-reductive-author-preprint`, SHA-256
`138d931a21fa522d97449c77acf429918826b1ef649be940d4721d616a1fc73b`.
