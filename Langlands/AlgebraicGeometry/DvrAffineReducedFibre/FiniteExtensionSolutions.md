# One finite extension for finitely many normalized residue fibres

`finiteExtensionNormalizedResidue` is the residue tensor of the
normalization in the generic fibre after a finite fraction-field
extension of a complete DVR with algebraically closed residue field.
The new base is the integral closure of the DVR in the extension field.

An embedding of two finite extension fields induces an injective local
map between the two integral closures. This map is flat over the
intermediate DVR and induces an isomorphism on residue fields, since
both residue fields agree with that of the original DVR. Reducedness
therefore persists when changing the base along this map. Tensor
cancellation identifies the iterated base change with the direct one.

For a finite family, assume that every member has some finite extension
giving a reduced normalized residue fibre. The compositum of the chosen
extensions in an algebraic closure is finite and lies in the same
universe as the original field. Reducedness persists to this compositum
for every member of the family.

This is a derived step for combining local computations at the generic
points of the normalized fibre in Conrad's appendix to Prasad–Yu,
*On quasi-reductive group schemes*, author preprint, Remark A.7 on
printed and PDF p. 30. The registered source is
`prasad-yu-2006-quasi-reductive-author-preprint`, SHA-256
