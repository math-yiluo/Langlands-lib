# Normalization in a tensor of fraction fields at a vertical point

`verticalGenericFibreRingEquiv` compares the generic fibre of
\(S \otimes_R Q\) with
\(\operatorname{Frac}(Q) \otimes_{\operatorname{Frac}(R)} E\).
Here \(R \to S\) and \(R \to Q\) are injective local maps of DVRs,
and \(E\) is a fraction field of \(S\) with the compatible fraction-field
action. The comparison identifies the generic fibre with the base
change of the old generic fibre. The old generic fibre is
\(\operatorname{Frac}(Q)\), so the tensor can be taken over
\(\operatorname{Frac}(R)\).

When \(S\) is finite over \(R\), \(S \otimes_R Q\) is integral over
\(Q\). Integrality over either ring in the generic fibre is then
equivalent. The generic-fibre isomorphism respects the \(Q\)-algebra
structure and carries the integral closure to the integral closure
of \(Q\) in the tensor of fraction fields. It carries the map from
\(S\) to the map through \(E\) on the right tensor factor.

This is a derived comparison for calculations at the generic points
of the normalized fibre in Conrad's appendix to Prasad–Yu, *On
quasi-reductive group schemes*, author preprint, Remark A.7 on printed
and PDF p. 30. The registered source is
`prasad-yu-2006-quasi-reductive-author-preprint`, SHA-256
`138d931a21fa522d97449c77acf429918826b1ef649be940d4721d616a1fc73b`.
