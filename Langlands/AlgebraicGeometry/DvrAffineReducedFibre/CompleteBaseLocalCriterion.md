# Local uniformizer tests for the affine reduced fibre

`localTensorNormalizationBaseMap` sends the extended base into the
normalization of a localized tensor product. It composes the scalar
map of the global tensor normalization with the map that localizes
the original scalar algebra. `LocalUniformizerTest` says that the
resulting scalar generates the maximal ideal at each maximal
localization of this localized normalization.

For an injective local, integral, flat extension of DVRs, the
complete-base criterion assumes that the new base is complete with
perfect residue field. It also assumes a flat finite-type algebra
with smooth generic fibre over the original DVR. Its input is the
local uniformizer test for the new uniformizer at each generic point
of the normalized special fibre over the original DVR.

The proof uses flatness of the old generic fibre over its normalization
because the generic fibre localizes that normalization. Normality of
the old prime localizations gives the hypotheses of the going-down
argument. The algebra equivalence between the tensor normalization and
the normalization in the new generic fibre identifies their prime
localizations and the ideals generated by the new uniformizer. The
complete-base uniformizer criterion then gives finite normalization and
reduced tensors with all fields of the statement's universe.

This is a derived combination of the generic-point criterion in
Conrad's appendix to Prasad–Yu, *On quasi-reductive group schemes*,
author preprint, Remark A.7, with the finiteness result of Theorem A.6.
