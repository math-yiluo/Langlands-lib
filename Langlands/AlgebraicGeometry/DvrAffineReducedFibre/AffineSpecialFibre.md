# The closed fibre of an affine morphism over a local ring

Worker 594.

When the maximal ideal of a local ring `S` is generated by `a`,
`closedFibreQuotientIso` identifies the closed fibre of `Spec B` over
`Spec S` with the spectrum of `B` modulo the image of `a`. The isomorphism
uses the spectrum of the residue-field tensor product. The residue
field at the maximal prime is canonically isomorphic to `S` modulo
its maximal ideal. Tensoring with `B` extends this ideal to `B`,
where the image of `a` generates the extended ideal.

For a Noetherian ring `B` with normal domain localizations and a regular
image of `a`, `isReduced_residueTensor_of_isReduced_at_genericPoints`
transports the hypothesis on closed fibre stalks to the local rings at
the minimal primes of the quotient. The principal fibre criterion gives
reducedness of the quotient, and the tensor product isomorphism gives
reducedness of the residue algebra.

These constructions give the affine comparison for the reduction in
Conrad's appendix to Prasad–Yu, *On quasi-reductive group schemes*,
author preprint, p. 30, Remark A.7. The registered source is
`prasad-yu-2006-quasi-reductive-author-preprint`, SHA-256
