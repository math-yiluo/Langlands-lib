# Good points in affine charts

Author: Worker 303. This module translates the good-point condition
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.SmoothRelationGoodPoint.IsGoodPoint`, stated with
stalks of schemes, into commutative algebra on affine charts. It is the
pointwise input that Worker 101's slicing step needs in the proof of
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.SmoothEquivalenceRelationEtaleSliceStatement`
(task `3c6168ac-543c-4dc2-a8a6-81647cb26eb6`). Its main theorem concludes the
cotangent spanning hypothesis of that step exactly as Worker 101 stated it.

The module imports `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.SmoothRelationGoodPoint` (the
predicate) and `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.SmoothRelationGoodPointCotangent`
(the commutative algebra criterion `cotangentSpan_eq_top_of_maximalIdeal_eq`).
All declarations are in namespace
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.SmoothRelationGoodPoint`.

## Setting

`U R : Scheme.{u}`, `t s : R ⟶ U`, `e : U ⟶ R` and `x : U`. Most results assume
that `e` is a common section, `e ≫ t = 𝟙 U` and `e ≫ s = 𝟙 U`. Charts are an
open `V ⊆ U` and an open `W ⊆ R` with `e x ∈ W`, `W ≤ s ⁻¹ᵁ V` and
`W ≤ t ⁻¹ᵁ V`. Write `A = Γ(U, V)`, `B = Γ(R, W)`, `p = e x` and `𝒪_{R,p}` for
the stalk. `f.appLE V W h : Γ(U, V) ⟶ Γ(R, W)` is the ring map induced by a
morphism `f` with `W ≤ f ⁻¹ᵁ V`.

## Definition

- `chartMap f hW x hx : Γ(Y, V) →+* X.presheaf.stalk x`, for `f : X ⟶ Y`,
  `hW : W ≤ f ⁻¹ᵁ V` and `x ∈ W`: the composite of `f.appLE V W hW` with the
  germ map at `x`. `chartMap_apply` unfolds it.

## Results

Transport along a ring isomorphism `φ : B₁ ≃+* B₂` of local rings (section
`Transport`):

- `comap_maximalIdeal_ringEquiv`, `map_maximalIdeal_ringEquiv_symm`: `φ`
  pulls back the maximal ideal to the maximal ideal, and `φ⁻¹` maps it onto the
  maximal ideal.
- `isRationalPoint_of_ringEquiv`: if `B₁` and `B₂` are `A`-algebras and `φ`
  commutes with the algebra maps, then `IsRationalPoint A B₂` implies
  `IsRationalPoint A B₁`.
- `maximalIdeal_eq_of_ringEquiv`: under the same compatibility, for
  `θ₁ : A →+* B₁`, the equality `𝔪 = σ(𝔭) B + θ(𝔭) B` in `B₂` for `θ = φ ∘ θ₁`
  implies the same equality in `B₁` for `θ₁`. Here `σ` is the algebra map and
  `𝔭` the pullback of the maximal ideal along it.

Charts of stalk maps (section `Chart`, for `f : X ⟶ Y` and `W ≤ f ⁻¹ᵁ V`):

- The existing `FiberStalkFlatness.germ_stalkMap_eq_appLE_germ` from
  [FiberStalkFlatness](FiberStalkFlatness.md), together with `stalkMap_comp_germ`: the germ at `f x`
  followed by `f.stalkMap x` equals `chartMap f hW x hx`.
- `map_comap_germ`: if `V` is affine and `y ∈ V`, every ideal `J` of the stalk
  `𝒪_{Y,y}` is generated by the image of its pullback to `Γ(Y, V)`.
- `map_maximalIdeal_stalkMap`: if `V` is affine, the ideal generated by
  `f^#(𝔪_{f x})` in `𝒪_{X,x}` equals the ideal generated by the image under
  `chartMap` of the pullback of `𝔪_x` along `chartMap`.

Good points in charts (section `GoodPoint`):

- `apply_apply_of_comp_eq_id`, `mem_of_comp_eq_id`: if `e ≫ f = 𝟙 U`, then
  `f (e x) = x`, and `e x ∈ W ≤ f ⁻¹ᵁ V` gives `x ∈ V`.
- `isGoodPoint_iff_chartMap`: for affine `V`, `IsGoodPoint t s e x` holds iff
  `𝔪_p = c_s(c_s⁻¹ 𝔪_p) 𝒪_{R,p} + c_t(c_t⁻¹ 𝔪_p) 𝒪_{R,p}`, where `c_s` and `c_t`
  are the chart maps of `s` and `t` at `p`.
- `appLE_germ_of_eq_id`, `appLE_germ_stalkMap_of_comp_eq_id`,
  `stalkMap_chartMap_of_comp_eq_id`: if `e ≫ f = 𝟙 U` and `x ∈ V`, then
  `e^#` sends `chartMap f hW (e x) hx a` to the germ of `a` at `x`.
- `comap_maximalIdeal_chartMap_of_comp_eq_id`: under the same hypotheses, the
  pullback of `𝔪_p` along the chart map of `f` is the pullback of `𝔪_x` along
  the germ map `Γ(U, V) → 𝒪_{U,x}`. It does not depend on `f`.
- `isRationalPoint_chartMap`: if `e ≫ f = 𝟙 U` and `V` is affine, every
  `b : 𝒪_{R,p}` satisfies `c a' ∉ 𝔪_p` and `c a' * b - c a ∈ 𝔪_p` for some
  `a a' : Γ(U, V)`, where `c` is the chart map of `f`. This is `IsRationalPoint`
  for the algebra structure given by `c`, written out.

The cotangent span (section `Residue`):

- `comap_appLE_eq_comap_appLE`: if `e` is a section of `t` and of `s` and `W` is
  affine, let `q` be the prime of `Γ(R, W)` at `e x`
  (`(hW.primeIdealOf ⟨e x, hpW⟩).asIdeal`). Then `q.comap t^# = q.comap s^#`,
  where `t^# = (t.appLE V W hWt).hom` and `s^# = (s.appLE V W hWs).hom`.
- `residueSpan_eq_top_of_isGoodPoint` (main theorem): assume `e` is a section of
  `t` and of `s`, `V` and `W` are affine, `Γ(R, W)` is a `Γ(U, V)`-algebra with
  `algebraMap = s^#`, and `q` is the prime of `Γ(R, W)` at `e x`. If
  `IsGoodPoint t s e x`, then the elements
  `1 ⊗ D(t^# g)` (precisely `1 ⊗ₜ KaehlerDifferential.D Γ(U, V) B_q (algebraMap B B_q (t^# g))`),
  for `g ∈ q.comap (algebraMap Γ(U, V) Γ(R, W))`, span
  `κ(q) ⊗[B_q] Ω[B_q⁄Γ(U, V)]` over `κ(q)`. Here `B_q = Localization.AtPrime q`
  and `κ(q) = ResidueField B_q`. The span is indexed by the image set
  `t^# '' (q.comap σ)`, as in Worker 101's hypothesis.

## Proofs

`map_comap_germ` is `IsLocalization.map_under`, because the stalk of an affine
open at `y` is the localization of its sections at the prime of `y`
(`IsAffineOpen.isLocalization_stalk`). With `stalkMap_comp_germ` this gives
`map_maximalIdeal_stalkMap`: the pullback of `𝔪_x` along `chartMap` equals the
pullback of `𝔪_{f x}` along the germ, since `f^#` is local. Rewriting both terms
of `IsGoodPoint` with it gives `isGoodPoint_iff_chartMap`.

For a section `e` of `f`, the composite `Γ(U, V) → Γ(R, W) → 𝒪_{R,p} → 𝒪_{U,x}`
is `(e ≫ f).appLE` followed by a germ, which is the germ of `Γ(U, V)` at `x`
because `e ≫ f = 𝟙`. Since `e^#` is local, pulling back `𝔪_x` gives
`comap_maximalIdeal_chartMap_of_comp_eq_id`. For `isRationalPoint_chartMap`,
write `e^# b` as a fraction `germ a / germ a'` in the localization `𝒪_{U,x}`;
then `germ a'` is a unit, so `c a' ∉ 𝔪_p`, and `e^#` maps `c a' * b - c a` to
`0`, so it lies in `𝔪_p`.

`comap_appLE_eq_comap_appLE`: the prime `q` is the pullback of `𝔪_p` along the
germ at `p` (`IsLocalization.AtPrime.under_maximalIdeal`). So both sides are
pullbacks of `𝔪_p` along chart maps, and both equal the pullback of `𝔪_x` by the
previous paragraph.

Main theorem. Rewrite `IsGoodPoint` with `isGoodPoint_iff_chartMap`, then replace
the `t`-pullback by the `s`-pullback (`comap_maximalIdeal_chartMap_of_comp_eq_id`
for both sections). Let `φ : B_q ≃ₐ[B] 𝒪_{R,p}` be the isomorphism of
localizations (`IsLocalization.algEquiv`), and give `𝒪_{R,p}` the
`Γ(U, V)`-algebra structure of the chart map of `s`. With
`θ₁ = algebraMap B B_q ∘ t^#`, `φ ∘ θ₁` is the chart map of `t`. Transport the
ideal equality and `isRationalPoint_chartMap` along `φ`
(`maximalIdeal_eq_of_ringEquiv`, `isRationalPoint_of_ringEquiv`) and apply
`cotangentSpan_eq_top_of_maximalIdeal_eq` in `B_q` with `θ₁`. Finally, the
pullback of `𝔪_{B_q}` to `Γ(U, V)` is `q.comap σ`
(`Localization.AtPrime.under_maximalIdeal`), so `cotangentSpan θ₁` is the span
in the statement, after reindexing from `q.comap σ` to its image under `t^#`.

## Provenance

The module is internal to the direct proof of
`SmoothEquivalenceRelationEtaleSliceStatement` in task
`3c6168ac-543c-4dc2-a8a6-81647cb26eb6`. That proof slices the smooth morphism
`s : R → U` at `e x` by functions whose differentials span the relative cotangent
space. This is the method of Stacks Project, More on Morphisms, Lemmas 057C
(slicing a smooth morphism by one function with nonzero differential at the
point) and 057G (étale slices of smooth morphisms). The module itself adds no new
mathematics. It uses the description of stalks of affine opens as localizations,
and it transports the local criterion of `SmoothRelationGoodPointCotangent` to
the chart in which Worker 101's commutative algebra slicing lemma is stated. The
form of the main theorem's conclusion was agreed with Worker 101: `θ = t^#` and
`p = q.comap σ`, and `p ≤ q.comap θ` follows from `comap_appLE_eq_comap_appLE`.

The surrounding groupoid slicing construction is the route of Stacks Project,
Lemma 04LV, used for Theorem 04S6 in the étale-quotient argument of 0461.
