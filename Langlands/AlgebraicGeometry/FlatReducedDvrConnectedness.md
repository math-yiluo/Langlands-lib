# Connectedness of the generic fibre with reduced special fibre

This internal supporting module proves the flat case of the DVR connectedness principle motivating EGA IV3, Lemma 15.5.6 (printed pp.234–235, retained PDF pages233–234). The source assumes an open morphism locally of finite type with connected total space and reduced special fibre. Here flatness and local finite presentation give universal openness, and the implemented proof uses the regular-divisor clopen extension from `ReducedPrincipalDivisorConnectedness`. It does not use the printed punctured-spectrum/depth proof.

The entry construction `globalSectionAlgebra p` is the actual `R`-algebra structure on `Γ(X, ⊤)` for a morphism `p : X ⟶ Spec R`: its coefficient map is the composite of the inverse of `Scheme.ΓSpecIso` with `p.appTop`. For affine `X`, `globalSectionAlgebra_specMap` identifies its spectrum map with `X.isoSpec.inv ≫ p`. Consequently `flat_globalSectionAlgebra` derives flatness of this actual section algebra from flatness of `p`.

The main conclusions lie in namespace `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.FlatReducedDvrConnectedness`. For every universe `u`, a domain `R : Type u` that is a discrete valuation ring, an arbitrary scheme `X : Scheme.{u}`, and an actual morphism `p : X ⟶ Spec R` that is flat and locally of finite presentation:

- `preconnectedSpace_genericFiber_of_reduced_specialFiber` assumes `PreconnectedSpace X` and reducedness of the actual residue fibre `p.fiber (IsLocalRing.closedPoint R)`, and concludes `PreconnectedSpace` of the actual fibre at the zero prime.
- `connectedSpace_genericFiber_of_reduced_specialFiber` replaces the total-space assumption by `ConnectedSpace X` and concludes `ConnectedSpace` of the same generic fibre.

No affineness, quasi-compactness, quasi-separatedness, separatedness, or nonempty special-fibre assumption is imposed. Preconnectedness includes the empty space; the connected-space corollary supplies generic-fibre nonemptiness separately.

The algebraic and geometric bridges are proved explicitly. Reducedness restricts along an open immersion on either leg of a pullback. At a maximal ideal `I`, the bijective coefficient map `R/I → I.ResidueField`, together with `Scheme.Spec.residueFieldIso`, identifies the quotient base change with the actual residue fibre. The proof uses this identification to transfer reducedness to the quotient base change. On an affine source, the spectrum/pullback tensor-product equivalence and `Algebra.TensorProduct.quotIdealMapEquivTensorQuot` then give reducedness of the quotient of the actual global-section algebra by `I` extended along its coefficient map.

For a regular base scalar `r`, module flatness makes its image in each affine section algebra a nonzerodivisor. Reducedness of the quotient base change makes the principal ideal generated by this image radical. If `D(r)` is dense in the base and `p` is universally open, its inverse image is dense. Applying the imported clopen-globalization theorem on all affine opens proves `preconnectedSpace_basicOpen_of_flat_reduced_quotient`, with the actual pulled-back scalar basic open as its conclusion.

For the DVR application, the two points of `Spec R` are the zero prime and the maximal ideal. An irreducible element `π` generates the maximal ideal and has `D(π)` equal to the singleton generic point; this open is dense because `R` is a domain. The preceding theorem proves preconnectedness of its inverse image. The canonical `p.fiberHomeo` identifies that inverse image with the actual generic residue fibre, giving `preconnectedSpace_genericFiber_of_reduced_quotient`. Existence of `π` and the maximal-ideal quotient bridge give the actual-special-fibre theorem. Finally, openness of `p` pulls back the dense generic singleton to a dense subset of nonempty `X`; this subset is nonempty, so its fibre homeomorphism yields the connected-space corollary.

The provenance is internal: the section-algebra construction, quotient/residue-fibre comparison, flatness and tensor-quotient bridges, and elementary DVR argument are assembled from the pinned Mathlib interfaces and the imported internal clopen-extension proof. EGA IV3, Proposition 15.6.4 and Corollary 15.6.5 (printed pp.237–239, retained PDF pages236–238), motivate the downstream use. Generization of section-selected components and the full `SectionComponent.FlatFinitePresentationOpennessStatement` remain further obligations.
