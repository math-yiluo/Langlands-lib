# Realizing Noetherian specializations by DVR-valued points

`DvrSpecialization X η s` packages an actual ring `R : CommRingCat`, domain and discrete valuation ring instances, and an actual morphism `Spec R ⟶ X`. Its two identity fields say that the zero prime maps to `η` and the maximal ideal, represented by `IsLocalRing.closedPoint R`, maps to `s`. The ring, scheme, and points all remain in arbitrary universe `u`. The DVR convention includes the nonfield condition.

`nonempty_dvrSpecialization_spec` constructs this object for distinct prime ideals `η ≤ s` of a Noetherian ring `C`. Form the domain `D = C/η`, the nonzero prime `P` induced by `s`, and the local domain `B = D_P`. The prime is nonzero because its contraction is `s`, while contraction of zero is `η`; equality would contradict the distinctness assumption. Injectivity of the localization map and the contraction identity for its maximal ideal show that `B` is not a field. The published theorem `IsLocalRing.exists_valuationSubring_isDiscreteValuationRing_dominates` provides a DVR `V` inside the fraction field of `D` containing `B` and dominating it. Restrict the actual composite `C → D → B → Frac(D)` to `V`. The kernel of this map is exactly `η`, since localization and the fraction-field inclusion are injective. Domination identifies the contraction of the maximal ideal of `V` with `s`. Applying `Spec.map` proves precisely the two endpoint identities in `DvrSpecialization`.

`nonempty_dvrSpecialization` proves that every strict specialization `η ⤳ s` in a locally Noetherian scheme has such an actual DVR-valued point. Choose an affine open containing `s`; specialization implies that it also contains `η`. The affine open immersion induces the original specialization on the two lifted spectrum points. Its coordinate ring is Noetherian by local Noetherianity. Apply the spectrum theorem and compose its morphism with this actual affine open immersion. The two projection computations give the prescribed endpoint images. Neither a dimension-one condition on the local scheme nor an adjacency condition on the two points is imposed; the chosen DVR need not be a finite type algebra over the coordinate ring.

The provenance is internal. This unit combines the existing published Noetherian local-domain DVR domination theorem with quotient, localization, prime-contraction, and affine-open APIs. The quotient/localization pattern is also used in `NoetherianApproximation.GeometricallyConnectedSubalgebraDescentProof.exists_ring`; the present construction prescribes both actual endpoint images for arbitrary comparable primes instead of choosing a minimal prime and a field extension. The local-domain domination theorem is imported from the existing Anthropic FLT port, whose paired Markdown documents its source and licence. No new copy or modification of that port is made here.

The application motivating this construction is EGA IV3, Proposition 15.6.4, printed pp.237–238 (retained PDF pages236–237): its proof reduces Noetherian generization questions to a DVR-valued point and two sections. Corollary 15.6.5, printed pp.238–239 (retained PDF pages237–238), motivates the finite-presentation openness task. The scheme realization here is an internal supporting theorem, not a transcription of EGA's entire reduction proof. This unit does not prove selected-component generization stability or the full arbitrary-base openness statement; it supplies the actual DVR morphism needed to apply the separately established DVR-valued-point component theorem.
