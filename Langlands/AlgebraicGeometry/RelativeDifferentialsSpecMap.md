# Algebraic-to-sheaf differential comparison on spectra

For bundled commutative rings `R` and `A` and an `Algebra R A` structure, this construction uses the ring map `CommRingCat.ofHom (algebraMap R A)`. It constructs `relativeDifferentialsSpecMap` from the tilde sheaf of `KaehlerDifferential R A` to the existing `relativeDifferentials` of the induced morphism of spectra. An arbitrary ring homomorphism can supply the algebra structure via `RingHom.toAlgebra`. Invertibility of this map remains a separate obligation.

`RelativeDerivation.specToAlgebra` evaluates a sheaf derivation on global sections using `Scheme.ΓSpecIso A`. Naturality of this isomorphism and vanishing on pulled-back base sections give base linearity. Applying the algebraic universal property to the existing universal sheaf derivation gives `relativeDifferentialsSpecLinearMap`; the inverse of Mathlib's tilde/global-sections adjunction gives `relativeDifferentialsSpecMap`.

`relativeDifferentialsSpecMap_adjoint_D` identifies the adjoint's value on every algebraic differential `D R A a` with the actual sheaf differential of the global section corresponding to `a`. `relativeDifferentialsSpecMap_app_toOpen_D` proves the corresponding formula on every open `W` of `Spec A`, using `tilde.toOpen` and restriction of that global function. Its proof uses naturality of `tilde.toOpen`, the adjunction counit formula, and restriction compatibility of the actual universal derivation. `relativeDifferentialsSpecMap_unique` shows that the adjoint values on these algebraic differentials uniquely determine the map. It uses injectivity of the adjunction equivalence and uniqueness in the algebraic universal property of Kähler differentials.

The provenance is the canonical comparison implicit in Stacks Project, Morphisms §29.33, Lemmas 29.33.4–5 (tag 01UM), in the registered snapshot `stacks-relative-differentials-internal-hom-2026-10-21`. The construction is derived from those comparisons using the project's existing universal derivation and Mathlib's tilde adjunction. Only the forward global-sections operation, induced comparison, and its defining compatibility and uniqueness properties are supplied here. Bijectivity in Lemma 29.33.4 and invertibility of the comparison are separate obligations. No affine nonemptiness, smoothness, finiteness, or reducedness assumption is used. The original sheafification definition and universal derivation remain unchanged.
