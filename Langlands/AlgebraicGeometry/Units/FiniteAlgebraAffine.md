# Finite-free unit coordinates on algebras

This internal development derives the algebra-point equivalence from the published
finite-algebra determinant and universal-unit constructions. A homomorphism from
the candidate localization sends the algebraic universal unit to a unit of the
tensor algebra. In the other direction, the base-changed basis gives the
coefficients of a unit. The determinant of multiplication is a unit, so its
coefficient homomorphism extends to the away-localization. Basis uniqueness and
localization uniqueness prove the inverse laws. The proof retains zero algebras
and rank zero and does not use a reducedness assumption.

The candidate's structure morphism is smooth: its ring is an away-localization
of a finite polynomial ring, and these two smooth algebra maps compose.

This module concerns algebra points. The comparison with the actual pullback
unit on arbitrary schemes is a further step of the representability proof.
