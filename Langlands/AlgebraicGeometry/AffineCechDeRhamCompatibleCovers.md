# Finite affine covers compatible with a reduced morphism

A square-zero reduction is a closed immersion surjective on points and
therefore induces a homeomorphism. Given a finite or infinite affine
cover of a compact source scheme, choose an affine neighborhood of
each point inside a source chart and inside the open corresponding to
the inverse image of a target chart on the reduction. A finite
subcover gives `ReducedChartRefinement`. Its source assignment records
the affine refinement. Its target assignment states the preimage
inclusions for the actual morphism on the reduction.

This constructs the compatible affine covers used in the local
pullback formula in Lan, *Arithmetic compactifications of PEL-type
Shimura varieties*, 14 March 2021 revision, Proposition 2.1.6.4.
