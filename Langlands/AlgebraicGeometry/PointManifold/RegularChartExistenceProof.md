# Existence of regular charts on actual smooth affine points

`regularChartExistence` proves the exact Library proposition `regularChartExistenceStatement k X d` for every `RCLike` field `k : Type`, affine scheme `X` over `Spec k`, and natural dimension `d`. The only smoothness hypothesis is the algebraic property `SmoothOfRelativeDimension d X.hom`. The conclusion is a `RegularAtlas` on the actual `RingPoint k X k` carrier with its existing evaluation topology.

The proof combines the pointwise standard-smooth chart theorem `exists_regularChart_of_standardSmooth` with `regularChartExistence_of_standardSmooth`, which supplies the algebraic principal-neighborhood and covering argument. The former comes from a finite submersive algebra presentation: split the variables into the complement of its injective dependent-variable map and its relation indices, identify the complement with `Fin d` by the presentation dimension, and reindex the presentation. Its evaluated dependent Jacobian is the transpose of the presentation Jacobian matrix after evaluation; the determinant is the value of the presentation Jacobian unit and is nonzero.

The polynomial coordinate map consists of the free coordinates together with the relation values. Its derivative has trivial kernel, by the nonsingular dependent Jacobian, and is bijective by equality of the finite dimensions. The analytic inverse function theorem gives an ambient chart, which `zeroFibreChart` restricts to the polynomial solution space. `presentationPointHomeomorph` identifies that solution space with actual algebra-map points by quotient lifting and polynomial representative evaluation. The published affine scheme point homeomorphism then transports the chart to actual relative scheme points. The forward coordinates are generator evaluations with denominator one. Evaluation of each coordinate-algebra element along the inverse equals evaluation of its polynomial representative; the inverse chart and polynomial evaluation are smooth over `k`, and restriction of scalars proves the required real smoothness.

For a general algebraically smooth affine scheme, `standardSmoothPrincipalNeighborhood` provides a principal open with standard-smooth actual coordinate algebra at the closed-point image of any actual point. The actual point factors through this open. Its pointwise chart transfers to the original actual point space by `RegularChart.ofBasicOpen`: localization surjectivity clears each numerator and denominator to global regular functions, and the principal-open function is nonzero at the included points. The partial homeomorphism transfers along the published open embedding. Evaluation of any original global function along its inverse is evaluation of its restriction along the input inverse, so the real smoothness requirement transfers as well. Choosing the transferred chart at every point gives the covering atlas.

There is no positive-dimension or inhabitedness assumption. The finite index splitting, inverse function theorem and pointwise choice include zero dimension, empty relation indices and empty actual point spaces. In particular the theorem specializes to the exact real and complex propositions in the target module.

This proof is an internal composition of the proved analytic, algebraic-presentation and actual-point compatibility constructions. It proves the Library target without changing its definition or the definitions it imports.
