# Multiplication of a fibrewise dense open in a smooth group scheme

The module proves the smoothness and surjectivity used in Bosch, Lütkebohmert and Raynaud, *Néron
Models* (1990), §5.1 Lemma 4, printed p. 113 (PDF p. 120). The source ID is
`bosch-lutkebohmert-raynaud-1990-neron-models` and the file SHA-256 is
`16d0701b4192ae9db91d083c1df69c0492b00a994aee7bcb0abca365d859d529`.

The automorphisms `(x,y) ↦ (x,xy)` and `(x,y) ↦ (xy,y)` identify multiplication with a projection
whose other factor is the group over the base. This gives smoothness and allows the two inverse
images of a fibrewise dense open to be tested in the fibres of multiplication. Their intersection
is fibrewise dense there and equals the image of the product of the open with itself. The unit
gives a section of multiplication, so each fibre meets this image. The module also describes the
open image of a product of open immersions and proves its fibrewise density when both ambient
schemes are flat and the factors are fibrewise dense.
