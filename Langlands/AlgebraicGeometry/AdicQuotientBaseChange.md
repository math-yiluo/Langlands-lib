# Base change of spectra of adic quotients

For a ring map $A \to B$ and an ideal $I$ of $A$, `quotientRingMap` gives the map on quotients by the $(n+1)$st powers of $I$ and its extension to $B$. The induced maps of spectra commute with the structure maps and the transitions between consecutive levels. `isPullback_quotientSpecMap` identifies each resulting square as a pullback. The declarations retain the namespace `Langlands.GroupScheme.HomScheme.Adic`.
