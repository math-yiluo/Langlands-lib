# The closure of a section-selected generic component

`SectionComponent.componentClosure f e he y` is the actual closure in `X` of the image of the connected component selected by `e` in the residue fibre at `y`. It is a closed subset of `X`. The theorem `preimage_componentClosure` proves that its trace on the original fibre is exactly that selected component, with no finiteness hypotheses.

The main supporting theorem, `exists_open_componentClosure_eq_componentSet`, assumes the existing `SchemeFiberGeometry.NoetherianGeometricallyConnectedFiberGenericConstancy` proposition. For a quasi-compact locally finite-type morphism over any Noetherian scheme, an actual section and a point generic for the entire base, it gives an open neighbourhood `W` of that point such that the actual selected-component union and the selected generic component's closure agree over `W`. All schemes and the assertion lie in arbitrary universe `u`. Neither flatness, reducedness nor separation is required.

The proof takes closures of the selected generic component and its complementary clopen. The published finite closed-cover and incidence theorems spread their partition. The selected open component is also a closed immersion, so the published equal-support comparison shows that the selected closure's original fibre is geometrically connected. The explicit generic-constancy premise gives connected nearby fibres of that closure. Continuity of the section supplies a further open neighbourhood where it avoids the complementary closure. The selected closure's fibre is then a connected clopen containing the section point; maximality and the clopen characterization identify it with the full selected component. Passing from actual fibre points back to total-space points proves the stated equality.

This is an internal construction from the imported actual-fibre and closed-support comparisons. Its route implements the generic-fibre step in EGA IV3 Proposition 9.7.12, printed p.83 (retained PDF zero-based page81), whose proof spreads the selected generic clopen piece and uses geometric-connectedness spreading.
