# The ring form of an étale equivalence relation with affine relation scheme

Authors: Worker 239, Worker 712.

This module states that an étale equivalence relation `E` whose schemes `E.U`
and `E.R` are affine, and whose map `j = (t, s) : E.R ⟶ E.U × E.U` is a closed
immersion, satisfies the ring-level conditions `RingEtaleEquivalenceRelation`
on its rings of global sections. It uses the Library structure
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.EtaleEquivalenceRelation` and the definition
`RingEtaleEquivalenceRelation` of
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.EtaleEquivalenceRelationComodule` unchanged,
together with the Mathlib class `AlgebraicGeometry.IsClosedImmersion` from
`Mathlib.AlgebraicGeometry.Morphisms.ClosedImmersion`. The module states the
proposition and supplies no proof.

## Formula and quantifiers

The declaration is
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.EtaleEquivalenceRelationRingFormStatement.{u}`.
Its defining formula is

```lean
def EtaleEquivalenceRelationRingFormStatement : Prop :=
  ∀ E : EtaleEquivalenceRelation.{u}, IsAffine E.U → IsAffine E.R →
    IsClosedImmersion (prod.lift E.t E.s) →
      letI : Algebra Γ(E.U, ⊤) Γ(E.R, ⊤) := E.s.appTop.hom.toAlgebra
      RingEtaleEquivalenceRelation E.t.appTop.hom
```

The universe `u` is arbitrary. The quantifier ranges over every étale
equivalence relation `E` with schemes in `Scheme.{u}`, absolute over
`Spec ℤ`.

- `IsAffine E.U` and `IsAffine E.R` are Mathlib's absolute affineness of the
  two schemes.
- `prod.lift E.t E.s : E.R ⟶ E.U ⨯ E.U` is `j = (t, s)` into the categorical
  product of schemes, the fibre product over `Spec ℤ`. `IsClosedImmersion` is
  Mathlib's class of closed immersions.
- In the conclusion, `B = Γ(E.U, ⊤)` and `C = Γ(E.R, ⊤)` are the rings of
  global sections. The local instance makes `C` a `B`-algebra through
  `E.s.appTop`, the ring map of the source map `s`. The target map enters as
  the ring map `E.t.appTop.hom : B →+* C`. These are the same choices as in
  `EtaleEquivalenceRelation.ComodulesAcyclic` of the comodule module, so the
  conclusion is the ring form that `ComodulesAcyclic E` refers to.

`RingEtaleEquivalenceRelation t` (described in the Markdown of
`EtaleEquivalenceRelationComodule`) has six fields: `C` is generated by
`t(B)` as a `B`-algebra; reflexivity, symmetry and transitivity on ring
points `C →+* T` for every commutative ring `T : Type u`; and the source and
target ring maps are étale (`RingHom.Etale`).

## Original source and correspondence

The statement is derived: it is not stated in this form in the literature.
It is the translation of the scheme-level structure into rings, and every
field follows from a standard fact about affine schemes.

- `stacks-etale-equivalence-relations-2026-11-01` (directory SHA-256
  `df1ecbfa63c5e31cf736d9ea79dd262976c3098fd9b36f2dd32668d44bf75420`):
  `022P.html` (Definition 39.3.1: `j = (t, s)` is an equivalence relation
  when it is a monomorphism whose `T`-points form an equivalence relation for
  every scheme `T`) and `02WS.html` (Definition 65.9.2: étale equivalence
  relations). The Library structure `EtaleEquivalenceRelation` records these
  conditions on `T`-valued points.
- `stacks-groupoid-schemes-chapter-pdf-2026-07-14` (Groupoid Schemes chapter,
  file SHA-256
  `4506a39201063afc270cbb68c9c0c1d3c90937c03f98cfee074605b402e7a30f`):
  Section 39.23 (Tag 03BE), the convention of writing an affine groupoid
  `U = Spec A`, `R = Spec B` through ring maps `s, t : A → B`.

The correspondence is as follows. Let `B = Γ(E.U, ⊤)` and `C = Γ(E.R, ⊤)`.

- For any scheme `X` and ring `A`, morphisms `X ⟶ Spec A` correspond to ring
  maps `A ⟶ Γ(X, ⊤)` (the Γ-Spec adjunction, Mathlib
  `AlgebraicGeometry.ΓSpec.adjunction`). As `E.R ≅ Spec C` is affine, a
  morphism `Spec T ⟶ E.R` is a ring map `φ : C →+* T`, and composing with
  `E.t` or `E.s` corresponds to `φ ∘ t` or `φ ∘ s` on `B`. With `T` ranging
  over rings in `Type u`, the fields `refl`, `symm` and `trans` of the Library
  structure at the affine test schemes `Spec T` give the fields `refl`,
  `symm` and `trans` of `RingEtaleEquivalenceRelation`.
- `E.U ⨯ E.U` is affine with ring `B ⊗_ℤ B`: the product is the fibre
  product over the terminal scheme `Spec ℤ`, and Mathlib
  `AlgebraicGeometry.pullbackSpecIso` identifies a fibre product of affine
  schemes over an affine scheme with `Spec` of the tensor product. A morphism of affine schemes is a
  closed immersion if and only if its ring map is surjective (Mathlib
  `IsClosedImmersion.isAffine_surjective_of_isAffine` and
  `IsClosedImmersion.of_surjective_of_isAffine`). The ring map of `j` is
  `b ⊗ b' ↦ t b · s b'`, whose image is the `B`-subalgebra of `C` generated
  by `t(B)`. So `j` is a closed immersion exactly when
  `Algebra.adjoin B (Set.range t) = ⊤`.
- `@Etale` has the ring hom property `RingHom.Etale` (Mathlib instance
  `HasRingHomProperty @Etale RingHom.Etale` in
  `Mathlib.AlgebraicGeometry.Morphisms.Etale`). For affine source and target,
  `HasRingHomProperty.iff_of_isAffine` says that `E.s` and `E.t` are étale
  if and only if `E.s.appTop.hom` and `E.t.appTop.hom` are étale ring maps.
  This gives `etale_algebraMap` and `etale_t`.

The monomorphism condition of the Library structure is not used; for the
ring form it follows from `adjoin_range_eq_top`.

## Scope and role

This statement belongs to the planned proof of
`NoetherianEtaleEquivalenceRelationAffineDescentStatement` (the presentation
form of Stacks Lemma 69.17.3, Tag 07VP), where modules on the quotient are
handled as comodules over the ring form. The proofs of the acyclicity
statement `EtaleEquivalenceRelationComodulesAcyclicStatement` and of the
Serre criterion `EtaleEquivalenceRelationSerreCriterionStatement` work with
`RingEtaleEquivalenceRelation`, and this statement supplies it from the
scheme-level data. The proof is a separate unit.
