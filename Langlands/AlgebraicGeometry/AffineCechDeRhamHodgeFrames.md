# Čech coordinates from a finite frame of the differential sheaf

A trivialization of the relative differential sheaf gives a section basis
on each open by `globalSectionBasis`. `oneFormsSectionsEquiv` transfers
this basis to the Kähler differential module of each affine section
ring. The restrictions send each frame vector to its corresponding
frame vector by naturality of the section comparison. The increasing
wedges give the corresponding bases of the exterior squares.

In these bases the semilinear restriction acts on coordinates by the
ring restriction. Applying this coordinate identity to each coface
shows that the one-form and two-form coordinates intertwine the signed
Čech differential with the differential on the function cochains.
