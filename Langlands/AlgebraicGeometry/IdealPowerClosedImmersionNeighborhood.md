# Open neighborhoods detected by ideal-sheaf powers

On a locally noetherian scheme `X`, let `i : Z ⟶ X` be a closed immersion and let `I` be a quasi-coherent ideal sheaf. If the kernel ideal of `i` lies in every positive power of `I`, then every point of the support of `I` has an open neighborhood that factors through `i`.

On an affine chart, Artin–Rees and the determinant trick give one element of the form `1 - r` with `r` in the chart ideal of `I`, annihilating the entire ideal of `Z`. The selected point is in the support of `I`, so `1 - r` does not vanish there. The ideal of `Z` is zero on the corresponding basic open. This is a scheme-theoretic factorization, obtained from the inclusion of ideal sheaf kernels.

The statement is an internal form of the noetherian intersection argument needed to pass from all identity neighborhoods to a whole smooth connected subgroup. It uses the same affine algebra argument as `AdicClosedImmersionNeighborhood`, with the powers taken on the scheme itself.
