# Relative forms across a change of coefficients

For a commutative square of rings with horizontal maps `R → T` and
`A → B` and vertical algebra maps, `mapOneAcross` sends a relative
one-form over `R` to a relative one-form over `T`. It is semilinear
along `A → B` and sends `d a` to the differential of the image of `a`.
The construction uses the universal Kähler derivation in the square.

`mapTwoAcross` uses the second exterior power and takes wedges to wedges.
Compatibility with the differential follows on `a d b` and extends by
additivity. Naturality under compatible restrictions is proved on the
generating differentials and wedge generators, then extends by linearity.
