# Localizations that are isomorphisms, pinched rings and finite type subrings

Module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.AbsoluteNoetherianApproximation.AwayBijective`, namespace
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.AbsoluteNoetherianApproximation`.

This module provides the ring-theoretic inputs of Stacks, Limits, Lemma 32.5.3 (Tag 07RN), the
step of absolute Noetherian approximation that extends a finite type model of a quasi-compact
open of a scheme to a finite type model of the whole scheme by pinching. Sources: the registered
folder `stacks-noetherian-approximation-rings-2026-11-02` (pages `01Z7.html`, `01Z8.html`,
`01Z9.html`).

## Definitions

- `pinchedRing s t` for ring maps `s : B →+* R` and `t : R' →+* R`: the subring of `B × R'` of
  pairs `(b, r)` with `s b = t r`. This is the fibre product ring `B' = B ×_R R'` of Tag 01Z8
  ("cartesian diagram of rings, i.e. `B' = B ×_R R'`"). `mem_pinchedRing` unfolds membership.
- `pinchedRing.fst s t` and `pinchedRing.snd s t`: the two projections `B ×_R R' →+* B` and
  `B ×_R R' →+* R'`, with `pinchedRing.comp_fst_eq : s ∘ fst = t ∘ snd` (the square commutes)
  and the simp lemmas `fst_apply`, `snd_apply`.

## Results

- `bijective_awayMap_pinchedRing_snd` (Tag 01Z8 and the ring part of Tag 01Z9). Let
  `h = (g, f)` be an element of `B ×_R R'`, so `s g = t f`. If the localization of `s`,
  `B_g → R_{s g}` (Mathlib's `Localization.awayMap s g`), is bijective, then the localization of
  the second projection, `(B ×_R R')_h → R'_f`, is bijective. Tag 01Z8 states that the localized
  square is again cartesian; Tag 01Z9 uses exactly the consequence formalized here, that
  `(B')_{h} ≅ (R')_{f}` when `B_g ≅ R_{s(g)}`.
- `exists_finiteType_subalgebra_bijective_awayMap` (the ring part of Tag 01Z7). Let `R` be a
  commutative ring, `h_l ∈ R` finitely many elements such that each localization `R_{h_l}` is a
  finite type `ℤ`-algebra, and `T ⊆ R` a finite set. Then there is a finite type
  `ℤ`-subalgebra `A ⊆ R` containing `T` and all `h_l` such that every map `A_{h_l} → R_{h_l}`
  induced by the inclusion is bijective. In Tag 01Z7 the `D(h_l)` form an affine cover of the
  quasi-affine scheme `W`, and the "finitely many additional elements" of its proof are `T`.
- `isIso_SpecMap_restrict_iSup_basicOpen` (derived; the scheme form of the conclusions of Tags
  01Z7 and 01Z9). For a ring map `φ : A ⟶ R` in `CommRingCat` and elements `h_l ∈ A` (any
  index type) such that each `A_{h_l} → R_{φ h_l}` is bijective, the restriction of
  `Spec φ : Spec R ⟶ Spec A` over the open `⋃ D(h_l)` of `Spec A` is an isomorphism. The two Stacks
  lemmas phrase their conclusion as an open immersion into `Spec A` of the corresponding open of
  `Spec R`; this module states only the isomorphism of the restriction.

## Proofs

- `bijective_awayMap_pinchedRing_snd`: Mathlib's `Localization.awayMap_injective_iff` and
  `awayMap_surjective_iff` reduce bijectivity of a localization map to element statements.
  Injectivity: if `(b, r)` maps to `0` in `R'`, then `r = 0`, so `s b = t r = 0`; by injectivity
  of `B_g → R_{s g}` a power `g^n` kills `b`, hence `h^n` kills `(b, 0)`. Surjectivity: for
  `a ∈ R'`, surjectivity of `B_g → R_{s g}` gives `b` and `m` with `s b = s(g)^m t(a)`; then
  `(b, f^m a)` lies in the fibre product (as `s g = t f`) and maps to `f^m a`. This is the
  element-level form of the exactness argument of Tag 01Z8.
- `exists_finiteType_subalgebra_bijective_awayMap`: as in the proof of Tag 01Z7, choose finite
  `ℤ`-algebra generators of each `R_{h_l}`, write each generator as `y / h_l^n` with `y ∈ R`,
  and let `A` be generated by `T`, the `h_l` and these numerators `y`. Then `A` is a finite type
  `ℤ`-algebra (`Subalgebra.fg_iff_finiteType`). Injectivity of `A_{h_l} → R_{h_l}` holds since
  `A → R` is injective. For surjectivity, the range of `A_{h_l} → R_{h_l}` is a subring
  containing every chosen generator `y / h_l^n`, so it contains the `ℤ`-subalgebra they
  generate, which is all of `R_{h_l}` (`Algebra.adjoin_induction`).
- `isIso_SpecMap_restrict_iSup_basicOpen`: being an isomorphism is local on the target
  (`IsZariskiLocalAtTarget.of_iSup_eq_top` for `MorphismProperty.isomorphisms`). Over the
  open `⋃ D(h_l)` the opens `D(h_l)` cover, and the restriction of `Spec φ` over `D(h_l)` is
  identified (`morphismRestrictRestrict`, `morphismRestrictEq`, Mathlib's
  `SpecMapRestrictBasicOpenIso`) with `Spec` of the localization `A_{h_l} → R_{φ h_l}`, which is
  an isomorphism since this map is bijective.
