# Connectedness across a reduced regular principal divisor

This is an internal supporting argument for the flat specialization step associated with EGA IV, third part, Lemma 15.5.6 (printed pp. 234–235), and the component-openness results 15.6.4–15.6.5 (printed pp. 237–239). The proof globalizes the denominator argument in `RadicalLocalizationIdempotents`; it is not an implementation of EGA's punctured-spectrum argument. No Noetherian hypothesis is used. The module does not yet prove the DVR specialization theorem or section-component openness.

For an arbitrary scheme `X` and a global section `t`, the main theorem `preconnectedSpace_basicOpen_of_locally_radical` proves that the actual open subspace `X.basicOpen t` is preconnected under the following explicit assumptions:

- The underlying space of `X` is preconnected.
- The basic open of `t` is dense in `X`.
- For every affine open `U`, the restriction `U.ι.appTop t` is a nonzerodivisor in the actual global section ring of `U`.
- For every such `U`, the principal ideal generated by that restriction is radical.

The conclusion is preconnectedness; it does not assert nonemptiness. Both empty schemes and empty opens are therefore covered. All schemes and rings have arbitrary universe size.

The supporting results implement the proof in these steps:

1. `closure_inter_dense_of_isClopen` states that the closure of the trace of a clopen on a dense subset recovers that clopen. One inclusion uses closedness; the other uses openness and density.
2. `isClopen_closure_of_local_extensions` glues local clopen extensions of a subset across a dense subset of any topological space. On each open neighborhood, the preimage of the global closure is the closure of the local trace, hence the local clopen. Its open image provides a neighborhood inside the global closure, which is consequently both closed and open.
3. `exists_clopen_basicOpen_of_radical` extends a clopen subset of the actual basic open in a prime spectrum when the element is regular and its principal ideal is radical. The localization map has exactly that basic open as its range. The preceding algebraic module extends the clopen pulled back to the localization spectrum, and surjectivity onto the basic open identifies its trace.
4. `exists_clopen_affine_basicOpen_of_radical` transfers this result to an affine scheme through its canonical spectrum homeomorphism, restricted to the corresponding basic opens.
5. `exists_clopen_basicOpen_of_locally_radical` gives the global clopen extension with exactly the density, regularity, and radicality assumptions above, without assuming preconnectedness. The continuous map from each affine chart's principal open to the original principal open pulls back the given clopen. The affine extension provides the required local clopen; taking the closure of the given clopen's ambient image glues these extensions. Because the original principal open is open, restricting that closure recovers the original closed subset.
6. Finally, `preconnectedSpace_basicOpen_of_locally_radical` applies the clopen characterization of preconnectedness: the ambient clopen extension is empty or the whole space, so its trace has the same alternative.

The regularity and radicality assumptions remain visible in the theorem. Deriving them from flatness over a DVR and reducedness of the special fibre is separate work. This supporting result changes none of the published section-component definitions or the target openness statement.

The definition `clopenExtensionOfLocallyRadical` sends a bundled clopen of the principal open to the closure of its ambient image, bundled as a clopen of `X`. The preceding existence theorem shows this closure is clopen: any extension has the same trace, and density recovers it as that closure. The theorem `clopenExtensionOfLocallyRadical_trace` proves that restricting this actual construction recovers the input clopen. The construction retains exactly the same explicit density, local regularity, and local radicality assumptions.
