# Generators of a smooth centre, from the residue field to the base

- `tensorKaehlerMapBase R S L : L ⊗_A Ω_{A/R} → L ⊗_B Ω_{B/S}` for a commutative square of rings
  `R → S`, `A → B`, with `tensorKaehlerMapBase_tmul_D`.
- `exists_mul_mem_span_of_residue`: let `R` be a discrete valuation ring with uniformizer `ϖ` and
  residue field `k`, `B` a formally smooth `k`-algebra, regarded as an `R`-algebra through
  `R → k → B`, `P = R[X₁, …, X_N]`, and `β₀ : P → B` a surjective homomorphism of `R`-algebras.
  Let `K` be a field such that `P → K` factors through `B`. If finitely many elements
  `yᵢ ∈ ker β₀` have differentials spanning those of all elements of `ker β₀` in `K ⊗_P Ω_{P/R}`,
  then every `j ∈ ker β₀` has a multiple `s j`, with `s` of non-zero image in `K`, in the ideal
  generated by `ϖ` and the `yᵢ`. Since `β₀` is `R`-linear and `R` acts on `B` through `k`, `β₀`
  factors through the reduction `R[X] → k[X]` of coefficients. The proof applies
  `DilatationCentre.exists_mul_mem_span_of_formallySmooth` to the induced surjection
  `k[X] → B` and lifts back using that the kernel of the reduction `R[X] → k[X]` is generated by
  `ϖ` (`MvPolynomial.ker_map`).
