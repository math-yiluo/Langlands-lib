# Local generators of a smooth centre

`exists_mul_mem_span_of_formallySmooth`: let `P` be a formally smooth noetherian algebra over a
field `k` with finite free differentials, `J ⊆ P` an ideal with `P ⧸ J` formally smooth over `k`,
and `K` a field which is a `P`-algebra killing `J`. If finitely many elements `yᵢ ∈ J` have
differentials `1 ⊗ dyᵢ` spanning the differentials of all elements of `J` in `K ⊗_P Ω_{P/k}`,
then every `j ∈ J` has a multiple `s j` in the ideal generated by the `yᵢ`, with `s` of non-zero
image in `K`.

The proof localizes `P` at the kernel `𝔭` of `P → K`, giving a local ring `P'` with
`J' = J P'`. Mathlib's Jacobian criterion
`Algebra.FormallySmooth.iff_injective_cotangentComplexBaseChange`, applied to the local, formally
smooth quotient `P' ⧸ J'` (a localization of `P ⧸ J`, by `JacobianPoint.isLocalization_quotient`),
shows that `K ⊗_{P'} J' → K ⊗_{P'} Ω_{P'/k}` is injective. Hence the classes of the `yᵢ` span
`K ⊗_{P'} J'`. Nakayama's lemma, through faithful flatness of `K` over the residue field of `P'`,
gives that the `yᵢ` generate the finite `P'`-module `J'`, which is the claim.

This is the standard fact that a smooth closed subscheme of a smooth scheme is cut out locally by
equations with independent differentials, used in Bosch–Lütkebohmert–Raynaud, *Néron Models*,
§3.3, proof of Proposition 5 (printed p. 69).
