# The defect of smoothness drops under dilatation of a good centre

The module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.DvrSmoothening.DilatationDefectStatement` contains one
proposition, `DilatationDefectStatement`, and proves nothing.

## The proposition

`DilatationDefectStatement` quantifies over a universe `u`, a discrete valuation ring `R` with
an irreducible element `ϖ`, an `R`-algebra `A` of finite type, an ideal `J` of `A` and a set
`S` of ideals of `A`. Its hypothesis on `A` is

0. `Algebra.Smooth R (Localization.Away (algebraMap R A ϖ))`: the generic fibre `A[1/ϖ]` of
   `Spec A` is smooth (over `R`, equivalently over the fraction field of `R`, of which `A[1/ϖ]`
   is an algebra).

Its hypotheses on the centre `Y_k = V(J)` are

1. `J = sInf S`, where every `p ∈ S` is `ψ⁻¹(𝔪_{R''})` for a point `ψ : A →ₐ[R] R''` with values
   in a discrete valuation ring `R''` in universe `u` with a local homomorphism `R → R''`
   satisfying `IsRamificationIndexOne R R''` (BLR's property (N));
2. `k ⊗_R (A ⧸ J)` is smooth over the residue field `k` of `R` (`Y_k` is smooth over `k`);
3. `(A ⧸ J) ⊗_A Ω_{A/R}` is a free `A ⧸ J`-module (`Ω_{X/R}|_{Y_k}` is free).

For every discrete valuation ring `R'` in universe `u` with a local homomorphism `R → R'`
satisfying `IsRamificationIndexOne R R'`, every `φ : A →ₐ[R] R'` with `J ⊆ φ⁻¹(𝔪_{R'})` (the
point specializes into `Y_k`) and every `φ'` from the dilatation algebra
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Dilatation.blowupAlgebra ϖ J` (the subalgebra `A[J/ϖ]` of `A[1/ϖ]`
generated by the fractions `f/ϖ`, `f ∈ J`) with `φ' ∘ blowupMap ϖ J = φ` (the lift of the point to
the dilatation), it asserts: if `pointDefect φ ≠ 0` then `pointDefect φ' < pointDefect φ`.

## Source

Bosch, Lütkebohmert and Raynaud, *Néron Models* (1990), literature ID
`bosch-lutkebohmert-raynaud-1990-neron-models` (SHA-256
`16d0701b4192ae9db91d083c1df69c0492b00a994aee7bcb0abca365d859d529`; printed page `p` is PDF
page `p + 7`):

- §3.3, Proposition 5, printed p. 68, with its proof on printed pp. 68–71: let `(X, Y_k)` satisfy
  property (N), let `U_k ⊆ Y_k` be open, smooth over `k`, with `Ω¹_{X/R}|_{U_k}` locally free,
  and let `X'_k → X` be the dilatation of `Y_k`; then `δ(a') ≤ max(0, δ(a) − 1)` for every
  `a ∈ X(R^{sh})` specializing into a point of `U_k`, where `a'` is the lift of `a`.
- §3.2, Proposition 1, printed p. 63: over `Spec A` the dilatation of `Y_k = V(J)` is
  `Spec A[J/π]`, and a point with values in a flat `R`-algebra whose special fibre factors
  through `Y_k` lifts uniquely to it.
- §3.6, Proposition 3, printed p. 79: for an `R'`-valued point `a` with `R'` of ramification
  index 1 over `R` whose generic fibre `a_K` factors through the smooth locus of `X_K`, and for
  `Y_k` the schematic closure of the specialization `a_k`, the same estimate
  `δ(a') ≤ max(0, δ(a) − 1)` holds ("Literally the same proof as the one of 3.3/5 works"). The
  proof only uses that `a_k` lies in the smooth locus of `Y_k`.

Throughout §3.3 the generic fibre `X_K` is assumed smooth (standing hypothesis, printed p. 64);
the proof of 3.3/5 uses it on printed pp. 70–71 to read the size `n + r − d` of the relevant
minors of the Jacobian matrix from the dimension `d` of `X_K` at `a_K`. Hypothesis 0 is that
standing hypothesis for `X = Spec A`.

The proposition is the affine case with `U_k = Y_k` (hypotheses 2 and 3 hold on all of `Y_k`);
property (N) is replaced by hypothesis 1, which is how the proof of 3.3/5 uses it: an element of
`A` that vanishes modulo `π` at all those points vanishes on `Y_k`. Unlike §3.6, Proposition 3,
the centre may contain the specializations of several points; they are all points of
ramification index one, and the point `φ` only has to specialize into `Y_k`, which is smooth
over `k` by hypothesis 2. For points of positive defect the conclusion is the inequality
`δ(a') ≤ δ(a) − 1`.
