# The defect drops under the dilatation of a good centre

`dilatationDefectStatement` proves the Library proposition
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.DvrSmoothening.DilatationDefectStatement`.

```lean
theorem dilatationDefectStatement : DilatationDefectStatement.{u}
```

## The dilatation algebra

For `A' = blowupAlgebra ϖ J`, the `A`-subalgebra of `A[1/ϖ]` generated by the `j/ϖ`, `j ∈ J`
(`Langlands.GroupScheme.Dilatation`):

- `blowupAlgebra_exists_div`: every `j ∈ J` is divisible by `ϖ` in `A'` (`mul_dividedElement`);
- `blowupAlgebra_adjoin_eq_top`: `A'` is generated over `A` by such quotients;
- `blowupAlgebra_eq_zero_of_mul`: `A'` has no `ϖ`-torsion;
- `blowupAlgebra_exists_pow_mul_eq_zero`: the kernel of `A → A'` is `ϖ`-power torsion.

## The defect drop

`pointDefect_lt_of_dilatation` proves the inequality for any `A`-algebra `A'` with these four
properties: for `A` of finite type over `R` and a good centre `J` as in
`DilatationDefectStatement`, every point `φ' : A' → R'` of ramification index one lifting a point
`φ` of `A` with non-zero defect whose closed point lies on the centre has smaller defect than `φ`.
Choose a presentation `P = R[X₁, …, X_N] → A` and the elements `z, w` of
`DilatationRelations.exists_dilatation_relations`. Write `u ⊆ A` for the prime of the closed point
of `φ` (the inverse image of the maximal ideal of `R'`) and `𝔭 ⊆ P` for its inverse image in `P`.

- *Localizing.* Let `A'_u` be the localization of `A'` at the image of `A ∖ u`. The point `φ'`
  extends to `A'_u`, and this does not change its defect
  (`DefectTransport.pointDefect_comp_formallyEtale`).
- *Presenting.* Let `P₂` be the localization of `R[X₁, …, X_N, Y₁, …, Y_n]` at the image of
  `P ∖ 𝔭` under the inclusion of the original variables `Xᵢ`. Choose `tⱼ ∈ A'` with `ϖ tⱼ = zⱼ`,
  and map `P₂ → A'_u` by sending `Xᵢ` to the image of `Xᵢ` in `A` and `Yⱼ` to `tⱼ`. This map is
  surjective. Every element of `A'`, multiplied by the image of an element of `A ∖ u`, is the image
  of a polynomial in the `Xᵢ` and `Yⱼ`. This is shown by induction over the generation of `A'` by
  the quotients `j/ϖ`, `j ∈ J`. For a generator, the third property of
  `exists_dilatation_relations` writes a unit-valued multiple `e j` as `ϖ a + ∑ bⱼ zⱼ`, and
  `ϖ`-torsion-freeness of `A'` gives `e · (j/ϖ) = a + ∑ bⱼ tⱼ`.
- *Differentials.* The differentials of `P₂` have the basis `dXᵢ, dYⱼ` (Mathlib's
  `KaehlerDifferential.tensorKaehlerEquivOfFormallyEtale` and `mvPolynomialBasis`).
- *The remaining hypotheses* of `DilatationPresentation.torsionLength_lt_of_dilatationPresentation`
  hold, with `A₂ = A'_u` and the elements `Xⱼ := Yⱼ` of `P₂`. The independence of the
  differentials of the `zⱼ, wₗ` and the condition `e f ∈ (ϖ, z)² + (w)` on relations are the first
  two properties of `exists_dilatation_relations`. Each `zⱼ − ϖ Yⱼ` vanishes in `A'_u` since
  `ϖ tⱼ = zⱼ`, and the relations of `A` vanish in `A'_u`. `A'_u` has no `ϖ`-torsion because `A'`
  has none. An element of `P` vanishing in `A'_u` is, after
  multiplication by an element of `P ∖ 𝔭`, killed in `A` by a power of `ϖ` (the kernel property
  of `A'`). For the generation hypothesis, write an element of `P₂` as `q / p₀` with
  `p₀ ∈ P ∖ 𝔭`, and show by induction on the polynomial `q` that `ϖ^v q` lies in
  `P + (zⱼ − ϖ Yⱼ)`, using `ϖ Yⱼ ≡ zⱼ`.

Since the defect of `φ` is non-zero, the torsion of `R' ⊗_A Ω_{A/R}` is non-zero, and the theorem
gives the strict inequality.

The hypothesis that `A[1/ϖ]` is smooth over `R` is not used by this proof.

The theorem depends only on the standard axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

Source: Bosch–Lütkebohmert–Raynaud, *Néron Models*, §3.3, Proposition 5 and its proof (printed
pp. 68–71), for points of ramification index one as in §3.6 (printed pp. 78–79). The implemented
proof follows BLR's route (ideal of `X` in the square of the ideal of the centre, comparison of the
differentials before and after dilatation), with polynomial presentations instead of a smooth
ambient scheme and a lattice comparison instead of Jacobian minors.
