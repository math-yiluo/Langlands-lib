# Locally closed loci from closed representatives over base opens

Let a property of maps to a scheme be preserved by precomposition and
local on the source for open covers. Suppose that every point in a test
satisfying the property maps into a base open on which the property is
represented by a closed immersion. The union of these base opens carries
a closed immersion representing the property on every test of the
original scheme.

The subopens of the union that lie in a chart form a basis. Each carries
a closed representative by pullback from its chart. The relative closed
locus gluing theorem identifies the overlaps by their common all-test
property. Every satisfying map has image in the union and then factors
through the glued locus. This is the gluing step used with the local
closed charts in SGA 3, Exposé XV, Lemma 5.4.
