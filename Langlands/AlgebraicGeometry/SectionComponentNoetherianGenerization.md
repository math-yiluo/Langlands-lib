# Generizations in a selected fibre-component union

This internal unit proves pointwise generization stability for the unchanged actual subset `SectionComponent.componentSet` and constructs its continuous inclusion on the generizations of a selected point. The universe `u` is arbitrary.

`geometricallyReduced_comp_of_isOpenImmersion` proves that an actual open immersion `j : U ⟶ F` followed by a geometrically reduced morphism `p : F ⟶ S` is geometrically reduced. Given an arbitrary field-valued pullback of their composite, the induced map to the pullback of `p` is a base change of `j`, by cancellation of the lower Cartesian square. It is therefore an open immersion. The target is reduced by geometric reducedness of `p`, and reducedness restricts to this open source. This provides the restriction needed for the selected open component of an indexed residue fibre.

`mem_componentSet_of_specializes` assumes a locally Noetherian source `X`, a flat morphism `f : X ⟶ Y` locally of finite presentation, an actual section `e`, and a point `x` in `componentSet f e he`. Its only reducedness premise concerns the actual selected open component of the residue fibre at `f x`, as a morphism to `Spec κ(f x)`. It concludes that every point `η` with `η ⤳ x` also belongs to the same relative union. When the points agree the conclusion is the given membership. Otherwise `nonempty_dvrSpecialization` realizes this strict specialization by an actual map `a : Spec R ⟶ X` from a domain DVR, with the zero prime mapping to `η` and the closed point mapping to `x`. Rewriting the closed endpoint supplies exactly the indexed geometric-reducedness premise and closed membership required by `dvr_generic_mem_componentSet`. Its generic membership, rewritten by the generic endpoint identity, gives the conclusion.

`mem_componentSet_of_specializes_of_geometricallyReduced_fiber` accepts geometric reducedness of the whole actual residue fibre at `f x`. The preceding restriction lemma supplies geometric reducedness of its selected open component, and the pointwise theorem applies. Neither theorem assumes quasi-compactness or quasi-separatedness of `f`, a Noetherian base, separation, ambient reducedness, or geometric reducedness at any other base point.

`componentGenerizationMap f e he x hx` is an actual continuous map from the subtype of all generizations of `x` into the subtype `componentSet f e he`, under the selected-component reducedness premise of the first membership theorem. Its value at `η` is the same underlying point `η`; the theorem just proved supplies membership in the target subtype. Continuity follows by restricting the continuous subtype inclusion into `X` and lifting it to the target subtype. `componentGenerizationMap_coe` records that its composite with inclusion into `X` is the original inclusion. It is a topological map of subspaces and does not assert a scheme structure or relative openness for the target subset.

The provenance is internal. The membership proof composes the separately established DVR realization theorem with the actual DVR-valued-point membership theorem; the continuous map packages their set inclusion without altering the points. The proof is motivated by the DVR reduction in EGA IV3, Proposition 15.6.4, printed pp.237–238 (retained PDF pages236–237). Corollary 15.6.5, printed pp.238–239 (retained PDF pages237–238), motivates the remaining finite-presentation openness task. This unit supplies the pointwise generization input to the Noetherian constructibility argument. Constructibility, interior membership, descent to an arbitrary base, and the exact `FlatFinitePresentationOpennessStatement` are separate obligations.
