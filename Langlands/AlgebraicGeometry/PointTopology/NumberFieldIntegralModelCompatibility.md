# Original number-field integral-model field and integer Weil compatibility

This derived local compatibility unit supports the integral-model passage in Conrad, *Weil and Grothendieck approaches to adelic points* (December 31, 2011), Example 4.2, printed p.10. Let K → L be the original map of number fields in any common universe, v any original finite prime of O_K, and Y an actual locally finite type scheme over O_L with a specified immersion into finite projective space over O_L, commuting with its structure map. Write W for the actual `affineChartWeilRestriction O_K O_L Y`.

`numberFieldIntegralModelFieldPointHomeomorph` gives the homeomorphism from W(K_v) to the product of Y(L_q) over every original upper prime q above v. Here W and Y retain their original bases O_K and O_L when evaluated at the fields. The source and factor scheme point topologies are constructed independently. The helper `IntegralModelFieldTensor.tensorEquiv` supplies the canonical K_v tensor_(O_K) O_L decomposition. The actual complete nontrivially valued lower field and finite-dimensional upper extensions supply the original finite-module topologies. The earlier integer-topology helper proves that O_L is finite projective over O_K. The generic projective-immersion completed-tensor Weil comparison then applies. The `_counit` formula proves that each component is the original model's relative Weil counit along its actual factor test spectrum.

`numberFieldIntegralModelFieldPointHomeomorph_integralToField` proves the square with the independently constructed integral homeomorphism commutes. Its lower vertical map is extension along the original inclusion O_v → K_v; its upper maps are extension along every original inclusion O_q → L_q. The coefficient square commutes by the definition of the actual completed-integer map. The proof applies the generic original-counit coefficient naturality theorem, retaining the same actual schemes and original embeddings throughout.

`numberFieldIntegralModelFieldPointHomeomorph_integral_iff` states the resulting exact integrality criterion: a K_v-point of W comes from an O_v-point if and only if each of its L_q-components comes from an O_q-point of Y. For the forward implication the commuting square supplies all components. For the reverse implication choose integral component lifts, use the inverse integral homeomorphism to construct the lower integral point, and use injectivity of the field homeomorphism to identify its extension with the given field point. This criterion uses the actual images of point maps and does not assume injectivity of coefficient-change point maps.

Every upper prime occurs. No tensor equivalence, module topology, finite-dimensionality or coefficient compatibility is supplied as an unproved hypothesis. This local unit does not spread Y from a generic-fiber scheme, compare W's generic fiber with an independently constructed field Weil scheme, supply one common model outside finitely many places, or assert the global adelic homeomorphism. Those are separate remaining steps.
