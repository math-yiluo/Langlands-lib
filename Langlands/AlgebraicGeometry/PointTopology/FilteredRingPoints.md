# Actual relative scheme points along algebra diagrams

This is the fixed-model construction underlying Brian Conrad, *Weil and
Grothendieck approaches to adelic points* (31 December 2011), printed page 6,
Remark 3.5 and (3.5.1), using the directed-ring context of Theorem 3.4 on
printed pages 5–6. The registered source is
`conrad-2011-weil-grothendieck-adelic-points`, SHA-256
`fe4a9193aff4ef575665b40971f3da32f850a74d87b3f2d0fa28bba26b9cbabb`.
The source pages were read for this construction; no registered errata file
was present at the workspace's Library base.

The construction is derived categorical infrastructure. It is stated for
an arbitrary commutative base ring `R`, arbitrary `R`-scheme `X`, arbitrary
small category `J`, and diagram `F : J ⥤ CommAlgCat R`. The base ring, algebra
carriers and index category lie in one arbitrary universe. Neither the index
category nor the cocone is required to be filtered or a colimit for the point
comparison to be defined. No topology, finiteness, separatedness or
affineness of `X` is imposed.

`algSpec_obj_eq_ringTest` and `algSpec_map_eq_ringTestMap` check definitional
agreement of Mathlib's relative spectrum functor `algSpec` with the existing
actual test schemes `ringTest` and their contravariant maps `ringTestMap`.
`ringPointFunctor` is the composite of its opposite-valued form with the
Yoneda functor of `X`. Thus its value at an algebra `A` is exactly
`RingPoint R X A`, the morphisms `Spec A ⟶ X` over `Spec R`. Its action on an
algebra map is exactly `RingPoint.changeRing`, precomposition with the actual
spectrum map. `ringPointFunctorMap` is postcomposition with a morphism of
relative schemes, with its component formula checked definitionally.

For an algebra cocone `c`, `ringTestCone` applies `algSpec` to the opposite
cone. Its legs are the actual spectra of the cocone algebra maps. Given an
actual algebra-colimit witness `hc : IsColimit c`, `ringTestConeIsLimit`
uses Mathlib's proved limit-preservation instance for `algSpec` and `hc.op`
to obtain the actual limit of these relative spectra. No point-colimit
assertion is used to construct this witness.

`ringPointCocone` applies the point functor to `c`. `RingPointColimit` reuses
Mathlib's usual type-colimit quotient of the disjoint union of the stage point
sets by the relation generated by transitions. `ringPointColimitι` is its
stage map; `ringPointColimitι_changeRing` is the standard type-colimit
transition law. The universal descent map `ringPointColimitMap` sends this
quotient to the actual `c.pt`-valued points. Its stage formula is precisely
`p.changeRing (c.ι.app j).hom`; forgetting the base equation yields
`Spec.map (c.ι.app j) ≫ p.left` with the underlying ring homomorphism made
explicit in Lean. These formulas follow definitionally from the reused
constructions; their coherence is the functor and type-colimit coherence.

This file does not assert that the comparison is injective or surjective.
Those conclusions require filteredness and suitable finiteness of the target
morphism and belong to separate statements and proofs. Specializing to the
actual arithmetic rings, comparing points of varying base-changed models,
proving model existence and independence, and constructing the adelic topology
remain separate obligations. In particular this fixed-model construction by
itself does not establish all of (3.5.1), Theorem 3.4, or Remark 3.5.
