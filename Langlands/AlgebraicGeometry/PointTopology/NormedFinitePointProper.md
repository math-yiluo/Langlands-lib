# Finite maps on affine points over a proper normed field

This internal lemma supplies the properness step in complex analytification of finite étale affine-space algebras. It uses the evaluation topology on algebra maps and works over any proper normed field. For an integral element, specialize a monic relation at a base point and apply the Cauchy root bound. Over a compact set of base points the finitely many coefficient evaluations are continuous and bounded, so the same bound holds uniformly for the integral element. Choose finitely many algebra generators for the source and use their evaluations to embed its point space as a closed subspace of a finite product of the coefficient field. The inverse image of a compact set is closed and lies in a product of compact balls. The target point space is locally compact, so continuity and compact inverse images give a proper map.
