# Zariski density of real open sets in unirational groups

The underlying scheme points of a nonempty real open subset of a unirational
affine group over `ℝ` are Zariski dense. In affine space, a nonempty real open
set contains a product of open intervals in coordinates. A polynomial vanishing
on the box vanishes identically because every interval is infinite. The basic
opens of the polynomial spectrum then give Zariski density.

For the group, restrict a dominant affine-space parametrization to a nonempty
affine open of its domain. The restriction is dominant and has a real point.
Translate the parametrization so that this point lands in the chosen real open.
Its preimage is nonempty and open, and the affine-space argument applies through
the open immersion of the domain. The dominant translated morphism carries that
Zariski-dense set of points into the chosen open of the group.

This is an internal density comparison for the real approximation proof.
