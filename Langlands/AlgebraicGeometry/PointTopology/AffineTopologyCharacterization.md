# Characterization of the affine evaluation topology

Author: Worker 105.

This proof combines the coordinate-algebra existence and uniqueness
proofs for the four original affine-topology axioms derived from
Conrad, *Weil and Grothendieck approaches to adelic points*
(December 31, 2011), Proposition 2.1, printed pages 2–3.
Registered source `conrad-2011-weil-grothendieck-adelic-points`, SHA256
`fe4a9193aff4ef575665b40971f3da32f850a74d87b3f2d0fa28bba26b9cbabb`.
An assignment satisfying the axioms equals evaluation by uniqueness.
Conversely, substituting an assignment equal to evaluation reduces its
axioms to the evaluation existence theorem.

This proves `AffineTopologyCharacterizationProperty` with the hypotheses
of that statement, including independent coefficient and value-ring universes.
Finite polynomial-space compatibility is derived from binary pushouts and the
line normalization; no finite-coproduct axiom, Hausdorffness or finite
presentation is assumed. It is still an intermediate coordinate-algebra
formulation; the bridge to actual affine schemes is a separate result.
