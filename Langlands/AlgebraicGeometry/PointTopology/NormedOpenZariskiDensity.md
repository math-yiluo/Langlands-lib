# Zariski density of nonempty analytic opens

Over a nontrivially normed field every neighborhood of a point is infinite. A
multivariate polynomial that vanishes on a nonempty analytic open in affine
space vanishes on a product of infinite subsets, so it is zero.

For a unirational affine group, restrict a dominant parametrization to a
nonempty affine open in its source. Translate the map so that a rational point
of the source lands in the prescribed analytic open. Its inverse image now
contains a nonempty analytic open whose underlying points are Zariski dense.
Dominance gives density in the group. This is the argument of
`LiftedRealOpenZariskiDensity` with the infinite-neighborhood criterion in place
of real intervals.
