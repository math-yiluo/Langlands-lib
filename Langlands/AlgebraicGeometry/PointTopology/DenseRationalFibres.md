# Equality of morphisms detected on rational fibres

`hom_ext_of_denseRationalFibres` proves `DenseRationalFibresStatement`. A dense
family of rational points of a reduced scheme over a field detects equality of
maps from the product with an affine parameter scheme to a separated target
over that field. The parameter can carry nilpotents. This is an internal
base-change form of the Library's equality test on a dense rational family,
used for the finite scheme of translation periods.

On an affine chart the coordinate algebra of the product is generated by the
coordinates of both factors. In its tensor description, expand in a field
basis of the parameter algebra. Restriction to a rational fibre evaluates the
coefficients of this expansion. A section whose evaluations on the dense
family vanish has all coefficients zero, by reducedness of the second factor.
Consequently the closed equalizer of the two maps has zero defining ideal
on the chart. Affine charts cover the second factor.
