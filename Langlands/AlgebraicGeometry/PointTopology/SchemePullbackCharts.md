keeps the actual relative scheme-point carrier and arbitrary common universe.

universal property. For affine factors and base the actual scheme pullback is

the compatible-pair subspaces. The published arbitrary-algebra-pushout

affine comparison. Naturality identifies its map with the two actual cone legs.

obligation; the relative pullback cone itself is defined for arbitrary schemes.
