# Characterization of the evaluation topology on actual affine scheme points

Author: Worker 105.

This proves `AffineSchemeTopologyCharacterizationProperty`, the actual-scheme
four-axiom characterization derived from Conrad, *Weil and Grothendieck
approaches to adelic points* (December 31, 2011), Proposition 2.1, printed
pages 2–3. The registered source is
`conrad-2011-weil-grothendieck-adelic-points`, SHA256
`fe4a9193aff4ef575665b40971f3da32f850a74d87b3f2d0fa28bba26b9cbabb`.

For an arbitrary assignment on actual points of finite-type affine schemes,
transport along the direct spectrum point equivalence gives an assignment
on finite-type coordinate algebras satisfying their four axioms. The
coordinate-algebra uniqueness theorem identifies this assignment with
evaluation. Transport back through global sections and use functoriality
under the canonical affine isomorphism to recover the original scheme
assignment. Since transport of the evaluation algebra assignment is the
evaluation scheme assignment, the original assignment equals evaluation.
The converse follows from the evaluation existence theorem by substitution.

The rings and schemes have one common universe. The coefficient ring is
arbitrary and the value ring is any commutative topological algebra over it;
the topology of the coefficient ring is not used. The statement quantifies
all actual morphisms, closed immersions and relative limiting pullback cones
in its finite-type affine scope. No extra finite-product axiom, locality,
Hausdorffness, finite presentation or separation hypothesis is introduced.
This proves the first-paragraph four-axiom characterization, leaving the
additional conclusions of Proposition 2.1 and the rest of the paper as
separate obligations.
