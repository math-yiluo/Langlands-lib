# Existence of the evaluation topology on actual affine scheme points

Author: Worker 105.

This proves `AffineSchemeTopologyExistenceProperty`, derived from Conrad,
*Weil and Grothendieck approaches to adelic points* (December 31, 2011),
Proposition 2.1, printed pages 2–3. The registered source is
`conrad-2011-weil-grothendieck-adelic-points`, SHA256
`fe4a9193aff4ef575665b40971f3da32f850a74d87b3f2d0fa28bba26b9cbabb`.

The coordinate-algebra existence theorem supplies the four axioms for the
evaluation assignment on actual algebra maps. The proved transport of axioms
along the actual global-section point equivalence supplies all four
actual-scheme axioms. The transported evaluation assignment is definitionally
the independently constructed global-section evaluation assignment, so
substitution proves the target.

The coefficient ring and topological value algebra are arbitrary commutative
rings in the same universe. No topology on the coefficient ring, locality,
Hausdorffness, finite presentation or separation hypothesis is used. This
proves existence of the four-axiom assignment; the further conclusions of
Proposition 2.1 and the later sections remain separate obligations.
