# Stable subalgebras of algebras with a multiplicative coaction

Author: Worker 266. `subalgebraSemilinearMap` follows the Library
`GroupoidCoaction.submoduleSemilinearMap` (module `GroupoidCoactionSubmodule`,
Worker 148), with the inclusion of a subalgebra in place of that of a submodule.

## Setting

`t₀ : B₀ →+* C₀` is a ring étale groupoid (`RingEtaleGroupoid`), `D` is a
`B₀`-algebra and `θ : GroupoidCoaction t₀ D` is a Library coaction.

## Declarations

* `GroupoidCoaction.IsStableSubalgebra θ S`, for a `B₀`-subalgebra `S ⊆ D`:
  for every `x ∈ S`, `θ x` lies in the image of `C₀ ⊗[B₀] S → C₀ ⊗[B₀] D`.
  This is the stability condition of step 1 of the task's `context.md`. It is
  the Library predicate `IsStableSubmodule` with the inclusion `S.val` of the
  subalgebra in place of the inclusion of a submodule. The Library predicate is
  not reused, because it would give a coaction on the submodule
  `Subalgebra.toSubmodule S` rather than on the ring `S`.
* `RingEtaleGroupoid.lTensor_eq_map`: `C₀ ⊗ ψ` as a linear map is the algebra map
  `Algebra.TensorProduct.map id ψ`.
* `GroupoidCoaction.subalgebraSemilinearMap` (with `_val`) and
  `RingEtaleGroupoid.subalgebraCoaction` (with `subalgebraCoaction_val`): the
  restricted coaction on a stable `S`. The inclusion `C₀ ⊗[B₀] S → C₀ ⊗[B₀] D`
  is injective (`lTensor_val_injective`) because `C₀` is flat over `B₀`. The
  restriction is defined through this injection. It is a `GroupoidCoaction` by
  `RingEtaleGroupoid.coactionOfInjective` (module `RingEtaleGroupoid`).
  `subalgebraCoaction_isMultiplicative`: it is multiplicative when `θ` is,
  because the inclusion is an injective ring map.
* `GroupoidCoaction.mem_range_lTensor_of_le`: the tensor image of a submodule
  `N ≤ S` lies in that of `S`.
* `GroupoidCoaction.isStableSubalgebra_adjoin`: if `θ` is multiplicative and
  `θ x` lies in the tensor image of `adjoin X` for every `x ∈ X`, then
  `adjoin X` is stable. Let `M` be the subring that is the image of
  `C₀ ⊗[B₀] adjoin X` in `C₀ ⊗[B₀] D`. The elements of `D` whose coaction lies
  in `M` form a subalgebra, since `θ` is a ring map and `θ (b • 1) = t₀ b ⊗ 1`.
  This subalgebra contains `X`, hence `adjoin X`. This is step 1 of
  `context.md`: the subalgebra generated by a stable submodule is stable
  because `θ` is multiplicative.
* `RingEtaleGroupoid.exists_fg_stableSubalgebra`: over a Noetherian `B₀`, every
  finite subset of `D` lies in a finitely generated stable subalgebra. Each
  element lies in a finite stable submodule (`exists_finite_stableSubmodule`,
  module `RingEtaleGroupoid`). The subalgebra generated by finite generating
  sets of these submodules is finitely generated and stable by
  `isStableSubalgebra_adjoin`.

## Provenance and scope

The exhaustion of `Γ(E.U)` by finitely generated stable subalgebras is step 1
of the ring-level route in the task's `context.md`, which cites Stacks Project
Lemmas 39.15.1, 39.15.3 and 39.15.4 (Tags 07TR, 07TT and 07TU). The finite stable
submodules come from `exists_finite_stableSubmodule`, the ring form of the
exhaustion in Lemma 39.15.4 for affine groupoids. Passing to generated
subalgebras uses only that the coaction is multiplicative. The module proves
the finite-subset statement used in the proof of NL. It does not set up the
directed system of all finitely generated stable subalgebras.
