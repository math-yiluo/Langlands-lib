# Nonzero invariants for Noetherian étale equivalence relations

Authors: Worker 101, Worker 239.

This internal proposition isolates the faithfulness step required by the
proof task for `EtaleEquivalenceRelationSerreCriterionStatement`. It states
that if a ring étale equivalence relation has Noetherian object ring and
every coaction is acyclic, then every nonzero coaction has a nonzero
invariant element. This module states the proposition and supplies no proof.

The declaration is
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.GroupoidCoactionNonzeroInvariantStatement.{u}`.
It quantifies over commutative rings `B` and `C` in `Type u`, a `B`-algebra
structure on `C` representing the source ring map, and a separate target
ring map `t : B →+* C`. It assumes the unchanged published predicate
`RingEtaleEquivalenceRelation t`: the two ring maps are étale, `C` is
generated by the target image as a source `B`-algebra, and the ring-valued
points satisfy reflexivity, symmetry, and transitivity. These are the ring
conditions for the affine étale equivalence relation with closed relation
immersion used by the host Serre-criterion task.

The second hypothesis is `IsNoetherianRing B`. The third is the unchanged
all-module acyclicity condition, written explicitly: for every `B`-module
`K : Type u` and every `GroupoidCoaction t K`, the predicate `IsAcyclic`
holds. In that predicate, invariant elements lift along every supplied
equivariant short exact sequence whose first term is `K`. The conclusion
quantifies over every `B`-module `M : Type u` with `Nontrivial M` and every
coaction `θ` on it. It asserts the existence of an element `m ≠ 0` with
`θ.coaction m = 1 ⊗ m`, using the published additive subgroup `θ.invariants`.
All rings and modules use the same arbitrary universe, matching the
restriction in `EtaleEquivalenceRelation.ComodulesAcyclic`.

The provenance is internal. This is precisely the nonzero-invariant
intermediate assertion in step 3 of the host task's proposed ring proof,
designed by Worker 239. It is not attributed to a verbatim theorem in the
literature and is not a new definition of acyclicity or of a coaction.
Its intended role is to show that a coaction with zero invariant module is
zero, after exactness and the unit calculation have been established.
The statement assumes no affine quotient or affine descent conclusion and
does not assert that any quotient has yet been constructed.
