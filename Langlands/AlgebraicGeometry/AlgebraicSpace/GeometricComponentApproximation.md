# Noetherian models and geometric component constructibility

This is an internal construction of the approximation bridge for the
geometric fibre component constructibility problem. The final reduction
retains the explicit premise `SpaceLimitModelExistenceQuasiSeparated`.
This unit proves neither that premise nor the unconditional host statement.
Its inputs other than this premise are the actual component-transport and
Noetherian constructibility results already constructed in this development,
and the existing limit of spectra of an exhaustive subalgebra system.

`GeometricComponentNoetherianModel B X` records actual mathematical data:
a Noetherian scheme base, a morphism from `Spec B` to that base, a
quasi-separated algebraic space over it with a locally finitely presented
quasi-compact etale surjective atlas, and an isomorphism between its base
change and the original space `X`. The comparison is an isomorphism in the
category of algebraic spaces, not merely an equality of component counts.
The base and rings inhabit the same universe as the original space.

`schemeGeometricFibreComponentCount_iso` shows invariance of the actual
geometric count under a whole-presheaf isomorphism of spaces over a fixed
scheme base. It takes the product isomorphism with the identity on the
canonical geometric point, applies `Points.isoHomeomorph` with the explicit
point topologies, transports connected components with
`GeometricComponentTransport.componentsEquiv`, and takes `ENat.card_congr`.
No finiteness or nonemptiness premise is needed for this isomorphism step.

`isConstructible_geometricFibreComponentCount_of_noetherianModel` applies
the Noetherian constructibility theorem to the recorded atlas and model.
The isomorphism comparison and arbitrary-base-change count equality show
that the original count at every point equals the model count at its image
in the Noetherian base. Consequently the original exact `n`-count locus is
the inverse image of the model's `n`-count locus. Scheme morphisms preserve
constructibility under inverse image, so this proves the original locus
constructible for every natural number `n` and arbitrary commutative `B`,
provided this actual model is supplied.

The namespace `GeometricComponentApproximation` constructs a specific base
system for every `B`. It uses the canonical algebra structure of `B` over
`ULift ℤ`, keeping both rings in the scheme universe. `subalgebras B` is
the monotone family indexed by finite subsets `s` of `B`, whose stage is
the integer subalgebra `Algebra.adjoin (ULift ℤ) s`. Every element belongs
to the stage generated by its singleton, proving exhaustion. Every stage
is finitely generated as a subalgebra, hence of finite type over the
Noetherian lifted integer ring, so `stage_noetherian` proves it is a
Noetherian ring.

`diagram B` is the inverse diagram of the spectra of these actual rings.
`cone B` has point exactly `Spec B`, with the inclusion-induced morphisms
as its legs. `isLimit B` applies the existing
`P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_SubalgebraStages_nonempty_isLimit_cone.LimBC.nonempty_isLimit_specCone`
to the exhaustive family. That Library result obtains the limit by applying
`Spec` to the directed ring-colimit presentation. The opposite of the
directed finite-subset order is a small cofiltered category in the same
universe, and every stage scheme is affine.

`GeometricComponentApproximation.model` takes an explicit proof `hlim` of
`SpaceLimitModelExistenceQuasiSeparated`, together with a quasi-separated
space `X` satisfying `FinitePresentationOverRing B X`. It applies `hlim`
to the actual diagram and limit cone just constructed. The resulting
finite-stage space, atlas, quasi-separatedness and comparison isomorphism
form a `GeometricComponentNoetherianModel`; the stage base is Noetherian
by `stage_noetherian`. Classical choice extracts this data from the
existential conclusion. It introduces no new assumption beyond the stated
`hlim` and the original space hypotheses.

`geometricFibreComponentsConstructible_of_limitModels` has conclusion exactly
`GeometricFibreComponentsConstructibleStatement`, conditional on `hlim`.
For each original ring and space it constructs the model and applies the
preceding constructibility theorem. The original target's count expression
agrees definitionally with the actual count used here. The host statement
and its import closure are unchanged. The separate model-existence premise
must be proved and published before this reduction can produce the
unconditional proof requested by the host task. No connected-fibre descent
or conditional version of the desired component constructibility is used
to establish a model.
