# Descent of invariant scheme charts along quotients by finite free actions

Internal infrastructure for the proof of `RationalPointEtaleLiftStatement` (Knutson,
*Algebraic Spaces*, II.6.4; Stacks Project, Tag 0BBP). In the Galois-descent argument a
finite group acts freely on an affine scheme `A` with an étale map `t` to the algebraic
space. The map `t` is invariant under the action, and it must descend to the affine
quotient `M = A/G`. This module proves that descent and the étaleness of the descended map.
These are standard descent facts for fppf sheaves, not a specific statement from the
literature.

Throughout, `S` is a scheme and names are in the namespace
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.AlgebraicSpace`. The finite free actions and their affine
quotients are the Library's `FiniteFreeSchemeAction` and
`FiniteFreeSchemeAction.AffineQuotient`.

## Statements

- `sheaf_ext_sigmaOver`: let `X` be a presheaf on schemes over `S` that is an fppf sheaf, and
  let `V : ι → Over S` be a family. Two sections of `X` over the coproduct `sigmaOver V`
  that agree on every component `sigmaOverι V i` are equal.
- `exists_desc_of_fppf_cover`: let `X` be an fppf sheaf and let `q : A ⟶ M` be a morphism in
  `Over S` whose underlying scheme morphism is flat, locally of finite presentation and
  surjective. Let `t : h_A ⟶ X` be such that `t` takes the same value on any two `T`-points
  `w₁, w₂` of `A` with `w₁ ≫ q = w₂ ≫ q`. Then `t = h(q) ≫ t'` for some `t' : h_M ⟶ X`.
- `isEtale_of_finiteEtale_comp`: let `X` be a presheaf with representable diagonal and let
  `q : A ⟶ M` be a morphism in `Over S` whose underlying scheme morphism is finite, étale and
  surjective. If `h(q) ≫ t` is étale (`IsEtale`) for `t : h_M ⟶ X`, then `t` is étale.

In the namespace `FiniteActionChart`, let `G` be a group, `A : Over S`, `α` a
`FiniteFreeSchemeAction G A.left` acting over `S` (`hα : (α.act g).hom ≫ A.hom = A.hom`),
and `Q` an affine quotient of `α`:

- `actOver α hα g : A ⟶ A` is the action of `g` as a morphism over `S`.
- `quotientOver α hα Q : Over S` is the quotient scheme `Q.scheme` with the structure
  morphism `Q.desc A.hom hα`. `quotientMapOver α hα Q : A ⟶ quotientOver α hα Q` is the
  quotient map `Q.map` over `S`. The instances record that the quotient is affine and that
  the quotient map is finite, étale and surjective.
- `map_actOver_uliftYonedaEquiv`: if `h(actOver g) ≫ t = t` for all `g`, the element of
  `X(A)` classifying `t` is invariant under every `actOver g`.
- `app_eq_of_map_eq`: let `X` be an fppf sheaf and `t : h_A ⟶ X` invariant under the action.
  Then `t` takes the same value on two `T`-points `w₁, w₂` of `A` with
  `w₁.left ≫ Q.map = w₂.left ≫ Q.map`.
- `exists_desc`: let `X` be an fppf sheaf and `t : h_A ⟶ X` invariant under the action. Then
  `t = h(quotientMapOver α hα Q) ≫ t'` for some `t' : h_{quotientOver α hα Q} ⟶ X`.

## Proofs

- `sheaf_ext_sigmaOver`: the component inclusions of a coproduct of schemes are open
  immersions that cover it, so they form a Zariski cover. Zariski covers are fppf covers
  (`Scheme.zariskiPrecoverage_le_fppfPrecoverage`), so the sieve generated by the inclusions
  is an fppf covering sieve. The sheaf is separated for it.
- `exists_desc_of_fppf_cover`: the induced map `q'` of represented fppf sheaves is locally
  surjective (`isLocallySurjective_schemeYoneda_map`). It is therefore an epimorphism, and a
  regular, hence effective, epimorphism in the category of fppf sheaves. The hypothesis on
  `t` says that the sheaf map defined by `t` coequalizes every pair of maps coequalized by
  `q'`. `EffectiveEpi.desc` gives the descended map, and `EffectiveEpi.fac` gives the
  factorization.
- `isEtale_of_finiteEtale_comp`: `t` is relatively representable because `X` has
  representable diagonal. Given a base change `p : W ⟶ T` of `t` along a scheme point
  `T ⟶ M`, paste it with the base change of `q` along `T ⟶ M`. The composite of the
  base-changed `q` with `p` is a base change of the étale `h(q) ≫ t`, hence étale. The
  base-changed `q` is finite, étale and surjective, so `p` is étale by the Library lemma
  `etale_of_finiteEtale_surjective_comp`.
- `app_eq_of_map_eq`: by `Q.isPullback`, the pair `(w₁, w₂)` factors through the relation
  scheme `∐_{g ∈ G} A` of the action, by a morphism `r'` over `S`. Then
  `w₁ = r' ≫ [actOver g]_g` and `w₂ = r' ≫ [𝟙]_g`. The sections of `X` over `∐_g A`
  obtained by pulling back the class of `t` along these two maps agree on each component,
  by invariance of `t` (`map_actOver_uliftYonedaEquiv`). Hence they are equal by
  `sheaf_ext_sigmaOver`, and pulling back along `r'` gives the claim.
- `exists_desc`: apply `exists_desc_of_fppf_cover` to the quotient map, which is finite
  étale surjective and hence flat and locally of finite presentation, with
  `app_eq_of_map_eq` as the hypothesis.
