# Affine neighborhoods of an atlas over a scheme monomorphism

This internal construction localizes the source and target of the atlas composite without imposing separatedness or affineness on the original schemes. All constructions are over the original arbitrary base scheme and preserve the scheme universe `u`.

`AffineAtlasNeighborhood F a f x` records an affine scheme `T` mapping by an open immersion to the original target scheme `Y`, an actual fppf sheaf `G` with a cartesian square `G = F × h_T`, the open projection `G → F`, and a monomorphism `G → h_T`. It also records an affine scheme `D`, an open immersion `D → W` with a point above `x`, and an etale chart `h_D → G`. The chart composites are the recorded scheme maps to `W` and `T`; the latter is locally of finite type and locally quasi-finite. The record makes no claim that this single chart covers `G`.

`nonempty_affineAtlasNeighborhood` assumes an etale chart `a`, a monomorphism `f` into the functor of points of `Y`, and a locally finite type scheme composite `w : W → Y` satisfying `a ≫ f = h_w`. For each point `x` of `W` it constructs this record. The proof chooses an affine open of `Y` above `w(x)`, forms `R = W ×_Y T`, lifts `x` and the chosen point of `T` to a point of `R`, and chooses an affine open neighborhood of that point in `R`. It forms the actual sheaf pullback `G` and constructs its chart by the cartesian universal property. Etaleness follows by base change and source restriction; openness of `G → F` is base change of `T → Y`; monicity is base change of `f`; and local finite type of `D → T` is base change followed by an open restriction. The published atlas kernel-pair argument in `MonomorphismAtlasGeometry` gives local quasi-finiteness. The two chosen point lifts give the point above the original `x`.

`AffineAtlasNeighborhood.nonempty_etaleAffineImageChart` applies the finite affine chart construction to the localized chart. Its source is affine, so its morphism to `T` is separated. This supplies the separatedness needed by `EtaleAffineImageCharts` without requiring it of the original composite `W → Y`.

The provenance is internal: this combines scheme affine covers, scheme and sheaf pullbacks, `MonomorphismAtlasGeometry`, and `EtaleAffineImageCharts`. It supplies local input for etale image descent; it does not assume or prove that descent theorem or the final monomorphism schemehood proposition.
