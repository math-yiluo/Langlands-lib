# Diagonal properties of products and pullbacks

These internal categorical lemmas derive diagonal closure under binary products,
monomorphisms and fibre products for any multiplicative morphism property stable
under base change. They apply both to scheme representability and to relative
quasi-compactness of algebraic-space diagonals. A binary-product diagonal is
identified with the relative diagonal over the terminal object. Relative diagonal
properties are multiplicative and stable under base change by Mathlib. A fibre
product embeds as a monomorphism into the product, and the relative diagonal of
a monomorphism is an isomorphism. This is a categorical implementation of the
diagonal argument in Stacks Project, Algebraic Spaces, Lemmas 7.1–7.2,
Tags 02X0 and 04T9 (version ed88ff78, printed pp.9–10).
