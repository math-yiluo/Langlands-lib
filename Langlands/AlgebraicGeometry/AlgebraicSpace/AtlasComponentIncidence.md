# Atlas kernel-pair incidence and connected components

This internal construction connects the Library's common-test-scheme relation
`SameImage₂` to its field-valued quotient `Points`, and applies the published
topological quotient-component construction to an atlas. It is a supporting
unit for the geometric component-count argument.

For arbitrary charts `a : h_U → F` and `b : h_V → F` into any presheaf,
`chartPointMap_eq_iff_sameImage₂` identifies equality of their quotient points
with `SameImage₂ a b`. A generating common-field relation supplies a common
test spectrum. The existing reflexivity, symmetry, and transitivity of
`SameImage₂` then handle the equivalence closure used by `Quot`. Canonical
residue-field representatives and the existing compatibility with scheme maps
give the assertion for ordinary points of the two schemes.

When `b` is representable, `chartPointMap_eq_iff_exists_pullback` says that a
pair of chart points has the same image exactly when some point of the actual
representing pullback lies over both. This uses the Library's existing
represented-pullback lifting results for `SameImage₂`.

`atlasComponentIncidence a ha` relates two connected components of `U` when
the two projections of some point of the represented kernel pair of `a`
belong to those components. The equality characterization proves that this
is exactly `Langlands.Topology.componentFibreRelation (chartPointMap a)`.

For a surjective étale chart whose connected-component space is discrete,
`atlasConnectedComponentsEquiv` identifies the connected components of
`Points F`, with `pointTopology F`, with the quotient of the atlas components
by the equivalence relation generated by this incidence. The proof composes
the equality of incidence relations with
`Langlands.Topology.quotientConnectedComponentsEquiv` and the published atlas
quotient-map theorem. No finiteness assumption is used. Geometric spreading,
constancy on an open set, and constructibility remain separate obligations.
