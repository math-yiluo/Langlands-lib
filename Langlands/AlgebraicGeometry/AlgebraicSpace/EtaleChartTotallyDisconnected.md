# Separated spaces over non-archimedean fields: totally disconnected rational points

Author: Worker 105; edited by Worker 1516.

This proof module proves the remaining separated-space property of Proposition 5.4 of
Brian Conrad, *Weil and Grothendieck approaches to adelic points* (December 31, 2011),
printed p.15 (source `conrad-2011-weil-grothendieck-adelic-points`, SHA-256
`fe4a9193aff4ef575665b40971f3da32f850a74d87b3f2d0fa28bba26b9cbabb`): "If `X` is separated
then the topology on `X(k)` is ... totally disconnected ... when `k` is non-archimedean."
Conrad says this is "clear via the scheme case". When `k` is not locally compact, a local
argument is not enough: a Hausdorff space that is locally totally disconnected need not be
totally disconnected. The proof here produces clopen neighbourhoods. It builds on
`EtaleChartSeparated`, `EtaleBoundedImageClosed` and `AffineBoundedPoints`.

## Preliminaries

- `exists_affine_chart_pointMap_eq`: every rational point of a quasi-separated space
  locally of finite type lies in the image of an affine chart (Knutson II.6.4, as in
  `exists_chart_pointMap_eq`, retaining affineness).
- `isLocallySeparated_of_isSeparated`: separated spaces are locally separated, because
  closed immersions are immersions.
- `isAffine_tensorObj_left` (instance): the product `U ⊗ V` over `Spec K` of two affine
  schemes is affine.
- `isAffine_pairPullback`: if `X` is separated, the overlap `U ×_X V` of two affine
  schemes over `X` is affine. Its pairing into the affine scheme `U ⊗ V` is a closed
  immersion (`isClosedImmersion_pairPullbackLift`).
- `mem_closure_pairPullback`: under the same hypothesis, the global sections of
  `U ×_X V` are generated as a ring by the pullbacks of the global sections of `U` and
  `V`. The closed immersion into `U ⊗ V` is surjective on global sections (Mathlib's
  `Scheme.Hom.app_surjective`). The global sections of `U ⊗ V` form a pushout
  (`isPushout_appTop_of_isPullback`), generated by the images of its legs
  (`mem_closure_of_isPushout`).

## Clopen images of bounded balls

- `isClosed_preimage_image_of_bounded`: let `K` be a henselian ultrametric valued field
  and `X` separated. Let `c`, `d` be affine charts, and `B ⊆ U_c(K)` closed with every
  global function bounded on it. Then `d⁻¹(c(B))` is closed in `U_d(K)`. This set is
  the image under the étale projection `U_c ×_X U_d → U_d` of the preimage of `B`
  (`preimage_image_schemePointsMap`). That preimage is closed and locally bounded over
  `U_d`: by `mem_closure_pairPullback`, a global function of the overlap is generated by
  functions from `U_c`, bounded over `B`, and from `U_d`, bounded on a bounded clopen
  neighbourhood of each point (`exists_isClopen_bounded`). So
  `isClosed_image_of_locallyBounded` applies.
- `totallyDisconnectedSpace_etaleChartTopology` (**totally disconnected**): over a henselian
  valued field with ultrametric norm, if `X` is quasi-separated, locally of finite type and
  separated, then `X(K)` is totally disconnected. Given `x` in an open set `O`, choose an
  affine chart `c` with `c(u) = x`, and a bounded clopen ball `B ⊆ c⁻¹(O)` around `u`
  (`exists_isClopen_bounded`). Then `c(B)` is open (chart maps are open) and contains `x`.
  It is also closed. A point `z` outside it lies in the image of an affine chart `d`, and
  the image under `d` of the open complement of `d⁻¹(c(B))` is an open neighbourhood of
  `z` that avoids `c(B)`. Hausdorffness (`t2Space_etaleChartTopology`) together with these
  clopen sets gives a totally separated space (Mathlib's
  `totallySeparatedSpace_iff_exists_isClopen`), which is totally disconnected.
