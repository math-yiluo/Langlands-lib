# Gluing representations over an open cover of the base

This is an internal categorical construction from the published
`LocalRepresentationChart` and `SliceOpenGluing` modules and Mathlib's covering
sieve API. It supplies no properness, flatness, or finite-presentation input.

Fix a scheme `B` in universe `u`, a presheaf `F` on the entire slice `Over B`
with values in `Type (u + 1)`, and an open cover `𝒰` indexed in `Type u`.
For each index `i`, assume an object `H i : Over (𝒰.X i)` and a natural
isomorphism from its lifted represented presheaf to the restriction of `F`
along `Over.map (𝒰.f i)`. These natural isomorphisms concern all test schemes
and all arrows in the slice.

`LocalRepresentation.baseCoverSieve` is the sieve on the terminal slice
object `Over.mk (𝟙 B)` generated by the members of `𝒰`, viewed over `B`.
`baseCoverSieve_mem` proves that it covers for the slice fppf topology. Under
the slice sieve equivalence it becomes the original open covering sieve;
Mathlib's inclusion of the Zariski precoverage into the fppf precoverage gives
the required membership.

`charts_locallySurjective` proves that the coproduct of the maps
`LocalRepresentation.chart (𝒰.f i) (H i) (e i)` is locally surjective for
that topology. For a section `x` over an arbitrary object `Z : Over B`, pull
back `baseCoverSieve` along the terminal map from `Z`. An arrow `k : W ⟶ Z`
in this covering sieve factors through a member of `𝒰`; this supplies a map
`α : W ⟶ Over.mk (𝒰.f i)`. Componentwise surjectivity of the already proved
`chartToProduct` lifts the pair consisting of `F.map k.op x` and `α` to an
actual map from `W` to the representative `H i`, viewed over `B`. The first
projection of that equality gives a preimage under the chart; the coproduct
inclusion gives a preimage under the whole chart family. Thus the image sieve
contains a covering sieve.

`exists_representation` additionally assumes explicitly that `F` is an fppf
sheaf. Each chart is a representable open immersion by
`LocalRepresentation.chart_isOpenImmersion`. Applying the published
`SliceOpenGluing.exists_representation` with the local-surjectivity instance
then produces an actual object `V : Over B` and a natural isomorphism from
its lifted represented presheaf to `F`. The sheaf premise and the existence
of the local representatives remain assumptions. This module does not yet
transfer morphism properties from the local representatives and does not
prove the proper coherent-family Hom representability statement.
