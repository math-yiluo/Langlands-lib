# Noetherian models of separated finitely presented algebraic spaces

This internal unit supplies the Noetherian approximation step of the
finite-presentation refinement of the weak Chow cover. That refinement
proves `ProperSchemeCoverStatement`, and the route is the one described in
the task context. It writes an affine scheme S as a cofiltered limit of
Noetherian affine schemes. It then applies the published model-existence
theorem `spaceLimitModelExistence` (`SpaceLimitModelExistenceProof`) to this
limit.

## The ring diagram

All declarations except the last are in the namespace `NoetherianModel`.
Let R be a commutative ring with decidable equality.

- `stage R s`, for a finite subset s of R, is the subring of R generated by
  s, written as the `ℤ`-subalgebra `Algebra.adjoin ℤ s`. `stage_mono` says
  that it is monotone in s.
- Each `stage R s` is a Noetherian ring. It is a finitely generated
  `ℤ`-algebra, and Mathlib's `Algebra.FiniteType.isNoetherianRing` (the
  Hilbert basis theorem) applies because `ℤ` is Noetherian.
- `ringDiagram R : Finset R ⥤ CommRingCat` sends s to `stage R s` and an
  inclusion of finite sets to the inclusion of subrings. Its index category
  is the directed poset of finite subsets.
- `ringCocone R` is the cocone with point R whose legs are the subring
  inclusions.
- `ringCoconeIsColimit R` proves that this cocone is a colimit. The forgetful
  functor to types reflects this colimit because it reflects isomorphisms.
  The filtered-colimit criterion for types then has two conditions. Every
  x ∈ R lies in `stage R {x}`. If elements of two stages i and j have the same
  image in R, their images in the common stage i ∪ j are equal, because the
  inclusions are injective.

## The affine base as a limit

Let S be an affine scheme, and fix decidable equality on Γ(S, ⊤).

- `specDiagram S : (Finset Γ(S, ⊤))ᵒᵖ ⥤ Scheme` is `Spec` applied to the
  opposite of `ringDiagram Γ(S, ⊤)`. All its objects are affine.
- `specCone S` is the image under `Spec` of the opposite ring cocone. Its
  point is changed from Spec Γ(S, ⊤) to S by the inverse of the canonical
  isomorphism `S.isoSpec`.
- `specConeIsLimit S` proves that this cone is a limit. `Scheme.Spec`
  preserves limits, so it takes the opposite of the colimit cocone to a limit
  cone, and `Cone.extendIso` carries the limit property along the change of
  point.

## Noetherian models

`exists_noetherian_model` assumes that S is affine and that X is an algebraic
space over S which is separated (`IsSeparated X`). It also assumes that X has
an atlas whose structure morphism is locally of finite presentation and
quasi-compact. It produces a Noetherian ring R, a morphism g : S ⟶ Spec R,
and an algebraic space X₀ over Spec R. X₀ is separated, has such an atlas, and
`baseChange g X₀` is isomorphic to X.

The proof applies `spaceLimitModelExistence` to `specDiagram S`, which is
cofiltered because its index is the opposite of a directed poset, together
with `specCone S` and `specConeIsLimit S`. It takes R to be the stage given by
that theorem and g to be the corresponding leg of `specCone S`.

## Provenance

This unit is internal. The colimit of finitely generated subrings and the
Hilbert basis theorem are standard. The model existence is the published
theorem cited above. Nothing here uses properness or
`SpaceLimitPropertyDescentStatement`.
