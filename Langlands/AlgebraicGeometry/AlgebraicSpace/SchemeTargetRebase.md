# A space over its represented target scheme

This internal supporting construction views an actual algebraic-space morphism
to the space of a scheme V as an actual algebraic space over V. It is built as
the pullback of the base-changed morphism along the diagonal section of the
base-changed represented target. No representability of the original morphism
is assumed.

The auxiliary fibre over the original base is isomorphic to the original
source, because its represented test map is an isomorphism. The existing
fibre-restriction atlas construction transfers an atlas of that source to
the new base, retaining exactly the same underlying atlas scheme. The new
structure map followed by V's structure map equals the original structure map.
The common atlas therefore supplies an actual surjective etale chart of the
constructed algebraic space.

Quasi-separatedness follows from its preservation by scheme base change and
actual fibre products. Over an affine original base and an affine V locally
of finite type over it, a locally finitely presented quasi-compact source
atlas remains such an atlas over V. Local finite presentation follows by
cancelling the locally finite type structure map of V. Compactness of the
unchanged atlas scheme gives quasi-compactness over affine V.

All content is derived from published Library constructions, their actual
pullback and atlas identities, and the existing scheme cancellation lemma.
It supplies structural finiteness data and does not establish eventual
connectedness or the full eight-property limit-descent theorem.
