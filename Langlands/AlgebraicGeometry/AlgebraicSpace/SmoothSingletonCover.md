# Smooth surjections generate big étale covering sieves

This internal lemma is derived from the published theorem
`smoothSurjectiveEtaleLocalSections` and the published singleton-cover
criterion `generate_singleton_mem_etaleTopology`.

For any morphism `f : W ⟶ T` of schemes over an arbitrary base in universe
`u`, smoothness and surjectivity imply that the sieve generated by `f` is
covering for the big étale slice topology. The statement imposes neither
an affineness nor a quasi-compactness hypothesis.

The proof chooses a surjective étale morphism `e : V ⟶ T` with a lift
`s : V ⟶ W` satisfying `s ≫ f = e`, supplies their structure morphisms to
the base, and observes that every arrow in the sieve generated by `e`
also belongs to the sieve generated by `f`. A sieve containing a covering
sieve is covering. The result concerns generated sieves; it makes no
étaleness assertion about `f` itself.
