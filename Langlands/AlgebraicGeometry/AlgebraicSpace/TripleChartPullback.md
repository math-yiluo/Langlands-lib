# Two charts over one target chart

This internal categorical construction expresses the canonical map
`(D ×_Z U) ×_D (D ×_Z V) → U ×_Z V` as the base change of `D → Z`.
It uses four supplied cartesian squares and their universal properties,
without assuming that the ambient category has all limits. The map and its
projection identities are explicit. The final cartesian square is proved
by successively lifting into the two chart pullbacks and their pullback.

The result is derived categorical infrastructure for the actual
finite-presentation atlas of an algebraic-space fibre product. It introduces
no geometric assumptions or external mathematical input.
