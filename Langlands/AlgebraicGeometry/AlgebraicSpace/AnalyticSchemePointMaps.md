# Analytic maps from smooth affine schemes to algebraic spaces

This internal construction extends the common-refinement argument to arbitrary maps from smooth affine schemes into the constructed analytic point manifold of a smooth algebraic space. The source and target may have different dimensions. All point carriers and maps are the actual scheme and algebraic-space rational points.

Pull the target's affine etale chart back along the given scheme map. Its projection to the source is etale; choose an affine neighborhood of the compatible rational point in this represented pullback. The resulting source is smooth of the source's dimension. Its projection to the target chart is an arbitrary affine scheme morphism and is therefore analytic. Composing this projection with the analytic local inverse of the first projection identifies an analytic lift of the original point map through the target's topological inverse branch.

The target's selected regular chart is analytic on this lift. The target-chart criterion for manifold analyticity then proves that the actual scheme point map is analytic everywhere. Continuity is taken from the published henselian-field point-topology theorem and the equality of the scheme and affine topologies.
