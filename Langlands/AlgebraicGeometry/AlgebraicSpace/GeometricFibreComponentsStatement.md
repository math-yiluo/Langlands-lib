# Constructibility of the number of geometric connected components

Author: Worker 105.

`GeometricFibreComponentsConstructibleStatement` states Lemma 5.1 of Brian Conrad,
*Weil and Grothendieck approaches to adelic points* (December 31, 2011), printed p.13.
The registered source is `conrad-2011-weil-grothendieck-adelic-points`, SHA-256
`fe4a9193aff4ef575665b40971f3da32f850a74d87b3f2d0fa28bba26b9cbabb`. The module only
states the lemma and contains no proof.

Conrad's Lemma 5.1 (printed p.13) reads: "If `X0 → Spec(B0)` is a finitely presented
algebraic space over a ring `B0` and `n ∈ Z` is an integer then the locus in
`Spec(B0)` where the geometric fiber has `n` connected components is constructible."
Section 5 works throughout with quasi-separated algebraic spaces (printed p.13: "We will
work with quasi-separated algebraic spaces (as is the case throughout [Kn])").

## Objects

Throughout, `B : Type u` is a commutative ring and `X : AlgebraicSpace (Spec B)` is an
actual algebraic space over `Spec B` in the sense of
`Langlands/AlgebraicGeometry/AlgebraicSpace/Basic.lean`: an fppf sheaf on schemes over
`Spec B` with representable diagonal and a surjective étale map from a scheme.

- `IsQuasiSeparated X` (from `RingPoints.lean`) says that the diagonal
  `X ⟶ X × X` over `Spec B` is representable by quasi-compact morphisms of schemes.
- `FinitePresentationOverRing B X` (from `SpaceWeilRestriction.lean`) says that `X` has
  an étale surjective atlas by an actual scheme that is locally of finite presentation
  and quasi-compact over `Spec B`. Together with `IsQuasiSeparated X`, this is
  finite presentation of `X → Spec B` in the sense of the Stacks Project (Morphisms of
  Algebraic Spaces, Definition 67.28.1, Tag 03XP: locally of finite presentation,
  quasi-compact and quasi-separated), as explained in `SpaceWeilRestriction.md`.
- For a point `p` of `Spec B`, `(Spec B).residueField p` is Mathlib's residue field
  `κ(p)` and `(Spec B).fromSpecResidueField p : Spec κ(p) ⟶ Spec B` is the canonical
  morphism. `AlgebraicClosure κ(p)` is Mathlib's algebraic closure `Ω` of `κ(p)`. The
  geometric point over `p` is the composite
  `Spec Ω ⟶ Spec κ(p) ⟶ Spec B`, where the first map is induced by the inclusion
  `κ(p) → Ω`.
- The geometric fibre over `p` is the product
  `X.presheaf ⨯ h_{Spec Ω}` in the category of presheaves on schemes over `Spec B`,
  where `h_{Spec Ω}` is the presheaf represented by `Spec Ω` over `Spec B`. The terminal
  presheaf is the one represented by `Spec B` over itself, so this product is the
  fibre product `X ×_{Spec B} Spec Ω` of presheaves.
- `Points F` and `pointTopology F` (from `SpacePoints.lean`) are the points `|F|` of a
  presheaf `F` (field-valued sections up to agreement over a common field extension,
  Stacks, Properties of Algebraic Spaces, Tag 03BU) and the final topology for the maps
  `|U| → |F|` given by étale maps `h_U ⟶ F` from schemes (Tag 03BX).
- `ConnectedComponents` is Mathlib's space of connected components of a topological
  space, and `ENat.card` is its cardinality in `ℕ∞` (equal to `⊤` for an infinite set).
- `Topology.IsConstructible` is Mathlib's constructibility: membership in the Boolean
  subalgebra of subsets generated by the retrocompact open subsets. Since retrocompact
  open subsets are closed under finite unions and intersections, these are the finite
  unions of sets `U ∩ Vᶜ` with `U` and `V` retrocompact open, the constructible subsets
  of EGA for the quasi-compact and quasi-separated space `Spec B`. This description is
  not proved here.

## The statement

`GeometricFibreComponentsConstructibleStatement` says: for every commutative ring
`B : Type u`, every algebraic space `X` over `Spec B` with `IsQuasiSeparated X` and
`FinitePresentationOverRing B X`, and every natural number `n`, the set of points `p`
of `Spec B` such that the points of the geometric fibre `X ×_{Spec B} Spec Ω` over `p`,
with their point topology, have exactly `n` connected components
(`ENat.card (ConnectedComponents _) = n`) is constructible in `Spec B`.

## Relation to the source and derived content

Conrad's "finitely presented algebraic space" is read with his quasi-separatedness
convention, so the statement assumes `IsQuasiSeparated X` in addition to
`FinitePresentationOverRing B X`.

Conrad's "geometric fiber" at a point of `Spec(B0)` is the base change of `X0` to an
algebraic closure of the residue field, and its number of connected components is that
of its underlying topological space. The statement uses Mathlib's `AlgebraicClosure`
for the algebraic closure and the point space `|X ×_{Spec B} Spec Ω|` with the
topology of `SpacePoints.lean`. Conrad's lemma is about all integers `n`; for a negative
integer the locus is empty, which is constructible, so the statement quantifies over
natural numbers. The geometric fibre is formed as a fibre product of presheaves; that
this presheaf is an algebraic space (Stacks, Algebraic Spaces, Lemma 65.7.1, Tag 02X0) is not used
by the statement, because points and their topology are defined for every presheaf.
Also derived here: the name of the statement.

## Not stated here

Conrad's proof (printed p.13) reduces to a Noetherian base by descending finitely
presented algebraic spaces through a limit, then by Noetherian induction to comparing the
geometric generic fibre over a domain with the geometric fibres over a dense open, and
argues as in [EGA, IV₃, 9.7.7] using étale scheme covers. These steps are proof
material. The scheme analogue of the lemma and the extension of Theorem 3.4 to algebraic
spaces described before the lemma are not stated here.
