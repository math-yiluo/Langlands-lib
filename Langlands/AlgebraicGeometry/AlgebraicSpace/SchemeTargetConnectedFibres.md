# Connected fibres after rebasing to a represented target

This internal supporting unit compares an actual morphism to the space
represented by a scheme V with the structural morphism of its actual rebase
over V. It asserts equivalence of the published geometrically connected
nonempty fibre predicates, with no finiteness or separation assumption.

Terminality identifies the second projection of the actual rebase pullback
with its structural morphism. Pasting pullback squares identifies each
structural fibre with a fibre of the base-changed original morphism. The
published fibre-restriction homeomorphism then compares its prescribed point
topology with that of the original-base fibre.

Conversely, Yoneda full faithfulness recovers an actual scheme map from every
original represented field test. The forgetful Yoneda naturality identity
computes the test obtained from the canonical rebase section. Its underlying
field scheme is unchanged. The Over-category structure equation gives an
actual isomorphism, identity on that scheme, from the recovered original-base
test to the given test. The induced map of actual fibre pullbacks is an
isomorphism. These point homeomorphisms transport nonempty connectedness in
both directions.

The provenance is the actual rebase construction, its pullback square,
terminality, Yoneda full faithfulness, and the previously derived
fibre-restriction topology comparisons. No external descent assertion is
used. This comparison supplies one bridge for the connected-fibre clause and
does not prove eventual connectedness along limits or the full eight-property
descent statement.
