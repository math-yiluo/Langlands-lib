# Closed rational loci over extensions of complete normed fields: proof

Author: Worker 5045. This module proves
`CompleteExtensionPointClosedRangeStatement` as the theorem
`CompleteExtensionClosedRange.completeExtensionPointClosedRange`. Its declarations
are in the namespace
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.AlgebraicSpace.CompleteExtensionClosedRange`. The
statement is a derived internal prerequisite, not a numbered result from the
literature (see its own Markdown). The proof below is internal. It combines
published Library results with standard Galois theory and the elementary fact
that a finite-dimensional subspace over a complete field is closed. The
statement keeps all of its hypotheses: `L/K` is an arbitrary extension of
complete nontrivially normed fields, and `X` is a separated, quasi-separated
algebraic space locally of finite type over `K`.

## Route

1. Enlarge `L` to `M = AlgebraicClosure L`, which is separably closed and
   carries the unique norm extending that of `L`. Closedness over `M` implies
   closedness over `L` (`isClosed_range_of_tower`).
2. Over a separably closed henselian `M`, take a point `z` in the closure of
   the rational locus. Choose an affine étale chart over `K` through `z` and a
   neighbourhood on which the chart is injective. Chart lifts of rational
   points near `z` have coordinates in `S = separableClosure K M`. The new
   *Galois trapping* lemma puts the coordinates of the lift of `z` into one
   finite Galois extension `E/K` inside `M`.
3. Over the finite Galois extension `E`, the Library's closedness result and
   the embedding `X_E(E) → X_M(M)` show that `z` is rational
   (`mem_range_of_intermediateField`).

## Section `Trapping`

Here `K, M` are nontrivially normed fields, `M` is a normed `K`-algebra, and
`A` is a commutative `K`-algebra. Points are `AlgPoint K A M` with the product
(coordinate) topology.

- `isClosed_intermediateField`: for complete `K`, a finite-dimensional
  intermediate field `E` of `M/K` is closed in `M`. This is Mathlib's
  `Submodule.closed_of_finiteDimensional` applied to the underlying
  `K`-subspace.
- `isClosed_setOf_forall_mem`: the points whose coordinates all lie in such
  an `E` form a closed set. It is an intersection of preimages under the
  continuous coordinate evaluations.
- `exists_finiteGalois_of_mem_closure` (Galois trapping). Assume `K` is
  complete and henselian and `M` is separably closed. Put
  `S = separableClosure K M`, which is Galois over `K`. Let `V` be open and
  `T` a set of `S`-points. Assume that whenever `η ∈ T` and a
  `K`-automorphism `σ` of `S` both have their images `η` and `σ η` in `V`,
  then `σ η = η`. Then every point `p ∈ V` in the closure of the image of `T`
  has all coordinates in one finite-dimensional Galois intermediate field `E`
  of `M/K`.

  Proof as implemented. First choose a basic neighbourhood of `p` inside `V`,
  given by finitely many coordinates `I` and radii `ε a`. Next pick `w ∈ T`
  whose `I`-coordinates are within `ε a / 2` of those of `p`. Let `E₀` be the
  finite Galois extension of `K` in `S` generated by the `I`-coordinates of
  `w` (`FiniteGaloisIntermediateField.adjoin`), and let `E` be its image in
  `M`. Let `B` be the ball of radius `ε a / 2` around `w` in the
  `I`-coordinates. It contains `p` and lies inside `V`. Take `η ∈ T` in `B`
  and `σ` fixing `E₀`. Then `σ η` also lies in `B`, because `σ` is an
  isometry (`FiniteExtensionEmbedding.isometry_aut`, from henselianity of
  `K`) and fixes the `I`-coordinates of `w`. The hypothesis then gives
  `σ η = η`. So every coordinate of `η` is fixed by the fixing subgroup of
  `E₀` and lies in `E₀` (`InfiniteGalois.fixedField_fixingSubgroup`). Hence
  `p` is in the closure of the points with coordinates in `E`, and that set is
  closed by the previous lemma.

## Section `SepClosed`

Here `K, M` are nontrivially normed fields, `M` is a normed `K`-algebra, `K` is
complete, and `X` is an algebraic space over `Spec K`.

- `mem_range_of_intermediateField`. Assume `M` is henselian, `X` is separated,
  quasi-separated and locally of finite type, and `E` is a finite Galois
  intermediate field of `M/K`. Let `z ∈ X_M(M)` lie in the closure of the
  rational locus, and suppose that under `baseChangeRingPointsEquiv`, `z` is
  the coefficient change of some `y ∈ X(E)` along `E → M`. Then `z` is
  rational. In the proof, `E` gets the subspace norm and the normed
  `E`-algebra structure on `M` given by multiplication. `E` is henselian
  (`FiniteExtensionEmbedding.isHenselianValuedField_extension`), and `K` is
  henselian by `PointTopology.completeHenselianValuedField`. The map
  `f : X_E(E) → X_M(M)` is coefficient change composed with the tower
  isomorphism `towerIso K E M X`. It is a topological embedding: the published
  `isEmbedding_henselianExtensionPointMap` for the base change of `X` to `E`,
  followed by the homeomorphism induced by an isomorphism of algebraic spaces.
  By `fieldExtensionPointMap_tower`, `f` maps the rational locus over `E` onto
  the rational locus over `M`. The point `f⁻¹(z)` therefore lies in the closure
  of the rational locus over `E`, which is closed by
  `ExtensionClosedRange.isClosed_range_of_finite_galois`.
- `isClosed_range_of_isSepClosed`. If moreover `M` is separably closed and
  henselian, the rational locus in `X_M(M)` is closed. Let `z` be in its
  closure. `FieldPointEtaleLift.exists_affine_etale_point` gives an affine
  étale chart `U → X` over `K` and `v ∈ U(M)` mapping to `z`. The base-changed
  chart map `q = ExtensionClosedRange.chartExtensionMap` is open, and
  `exists_isOpen_injOn_schemePointsMap` gives an open `V ∋ v` on which `q` is
  injective. The relevant hypothesis is that `X` is separated, hence locally
  separated after base change. Pass to affine coordinates `AlgPoint K A M` with
  `affineSchemePointEquiv`, a homeomorphism. Let `T` be the set of `S`-points
  of `U` that map to `K`-rational points of `X`, viewed as `S`-points.
  - Hypothesis of the trapping lemma: for `η ∈ T` and an automorphism `σ` of
    `S`, the points `η` and `σ η`, viewed in `U(M)`, have the same image under
    `q`, namely the rational point. If both lie in `V`, injectivity gives
    `σ η = η`.
  - Closure: every neighbourhood of `v` inside `V` maps under `q` to an open
    neighbourhood of `z`, which contains a rational point. Its chart preimage
    is defined over `S`, by
    `ExtensionCompletionReduction.exists_chart_lift_to_subfield` applied with
    `separableClosure S M = ⊥`, and lies over a `K`-rational point after
    coefficient change to `S`. This uses injectivity of coefficient change
    for separated spaces, `RelativeIntegralPoints.changeRing_injective`. Hence
    `v` is in the closure of the image of `T`.

  The trapping lemma gives a finite Galois `E` containing all coordinates of
  `v`. Then `v` comes from an `E`-point of `U` (`AlgPoint.range_mapCoefficients`),
  whose image in `X(E)` satisfies the hypothesis of
  `mem_range_of_intermediateField`.

## Section `Tower` and the final theorem

- `isClosed_range_of_tower`: for a tower `K → L → M` of nontrivially normed
  fields with compatible normed-algebra structures and a separated `X`,
  closedness of the rational locus in `X_M(M)` implies closedness in
  `X_L(L)`. The map `g : X_L(L) → X_M(M)` is coefficient change followed by
  `towerIso K L M X`. It is continuous by `continuous_fieldExtensionPointMap`
  and `isHomeomorph_ringPointsMap_of_iso`, and injective by
  `RelativeIntegralPoints.changeRing_injective` for the separated base change.
  By `fieldExtensionPointMap_tower`, the rational locus over `L` is exactly
  `g⁻¹` of the rational locus over `M`.
- `completeExtensionPointClosedRange : CompleteExtensionPointClosedRangeStatement.{u}`.
  `L` is henselian by `PointTopology.completeHenselianValuedField`. Its unique
  extending absolute value on `M = AlgebraicClosure L` makes `M` a
  nontrivially normed field, a normed `L`-algebra, and a normed `K`-algebra
  through the native scalar tower `K → L → M`. `M` is henselian by
  `FiniteExtensionEmbedding.isHenselianValuedField_extension` and separably
  closed because it is algebraically closed. The result follows from
  `isClosed_range_of_isSepClosed` for `M` and `isClosed_range_of_tower`.
