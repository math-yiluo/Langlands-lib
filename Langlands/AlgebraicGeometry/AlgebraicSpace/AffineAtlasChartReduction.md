# Reduction of schemehood to images of affine etale charts

This internal reduction isolates the remaining etale image representability step from affine localization, point coverage, and final gluing. It works over an arbitrary scheme base and at every scheme universe `u`.

`EtaleAffineImageChart.HasRepresentedImage C` means that an actual scheme over the original base represents the fppf sheafified range of `C.chart ≫ C.toSpace`. This is the image in the sheaf before the etale base change, rather than the affine open image inside the sheaf after that base change. The definition is a predicate, not an assertion that the image is represented.

`AffineAtlasNeighborhood.pointLift_of_representedImage` starts with an affine neighborhood `N` of an original atlas point and an affine-image chart `C` of its localized chart. Assuming the image predicate for `C`, it constructs an open scheme chart of the original sheaf and a scheme point lifting the original atlas point into that chart. The projection of the etale sheaf pullback is etale by base change, so the sheafified range of the composite chart is open. The assumed representer, followed by this open inclusion and the open map from the localized sheaf to the original sheaf, is the desired open scheme chart. Full faithfulness of scheme Yoneda turns the canonical image factorization into an actual map from `C.source` to its representer. The two chart equations and the two stored point equations give the required compatibility and point lift.

`hasPointLiftedOpenCharts_of_affine_etale_images` assumes an etale chart of an fppf sheaf, a monomorphism into a scheme, and a locally finite type scheme composite. Its additional, explicit hypothesis supplies `HasRepresentedImage C` for the finite affine chart data in every affine localization. It chooses that data at each atlas point and applies the preceding construction. `exists_representation_of_affine_etale_images` adds surjectivity of the original atlas and uses the point-lift coverage and open-chart gluing theorem to obtain an actual scheme representer.

The provenance is internal, using `AffineAtlasNeighborhoods`, `EtaleAffineImageCharts`, `OpenSubfunctorImages`, relative open-immersion composition, and `PointLiftedOpenCharts`. These theorems are conditional reductions. The etale image representability hypothesis remains to be proved separately; this module does not establish the unconditional monomorphism schemehood statement.
