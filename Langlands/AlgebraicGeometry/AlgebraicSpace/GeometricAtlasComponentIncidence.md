# Finite atlas incidence for actual geometric fibres

This internal construction specializes the published atlas kernel-pair incidence
description to the actual geometric fibres in the component-count target. Its
inputs are `AtlasComponentIncidence` and `GeometricFibreComponentGeometry`; it
uses no constructibility or geometric spreading assertion.

`geometricAtlasObject B U p` is the product, in schemes over `Spec B`, of the
chart `U` with `Spec` of the algebraic closure of the residue field of `p`,
using the canonical geometric point from `SchemeFiberGeometry`. If the chart
morphism is quasi-compact and locally of finite presentation, its geometric
fibre is Noetherian: the second projection of the actual scheme pullback is
quasi-compact and locally of finite type over a Noetherian field spectrum.
The canonical isomorphism from the product in the over category transports
this topology. Open connected components in a Noetherian space give a
discrete component space, and compactness gives finitely many components.
No Noetherian assumption on `B` is needed.

`geometricAtlasComponentIncidence B a ha p` is the existing represented
kernel-pair incidence relation of the geometric base change of the étale
chart `a`. Its vertices are the actual connected components of the geometric
chart fibre, and an edge is witnessed by an actual point of the represented
kernel pair. The quotient generated by this relation is finite under the
chart finiteness hypotheses above.

If the chart is also geometrically surjective,
`geometricAtlasConnectedComponentsEquiv` identifies this finite quotient
with the connected components of the geometric presheaf fibre, using precisely
`pointTopology` from the target. It applies `atlasConnectedComponentsEquiv`
to the base-changed atlas and supplies discreteness from the Noetherian argument.

`geometricFibreComponentCount_eq_card_incidence` computes the unchanged target
count as the `ENat.card` of this finite quotient. Finally,
`geometricFibreComponentCount_eq_of_incidenceEquiv` proves equality of the
counts at two base points when an equivalence of the chart component sets
preserves and reflects incidence. It transports the relation with `Quot.congr`
and takes cardinalities. Producing such equivalences on a dense open,
proving constructibility, and descending through approximation remain
separate obligations.
