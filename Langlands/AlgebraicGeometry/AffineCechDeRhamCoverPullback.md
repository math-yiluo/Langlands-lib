# Pullback on compatible affine Čech covers

A morphism over the affine base and a compatible index map give an algebra
homomorphism on each tuple intersection. Pullback of functions is the
actual map on sections. For one-forms and two-forms it is the induced map
of Kähler differentials or of their second exterior powers. Each map is
linear over the base.

The two ways around a coface restriction square are the same algebra
homomorphism by functoriality of section restriction. Their maps on
functions and one-forms therefore commute with the signed Čech
restriction sums. Naturality of the universal derivation and the
exterior differential gives the corresponding differential identities.
These constructions are the pullbacks of the Čech model in Lan,
*Arithmetic compactifications of PEL-type Shimura varieties*, revision
of 14 March 2021, §2.1.6. The retained source is
