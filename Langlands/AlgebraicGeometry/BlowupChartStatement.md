# Blow-ups with a chart at a section of the centre

The module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.BlowupChartStatement` contains one
proposition, `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.BlowupChartStatement`, and proves nothing.

## The proposition

`BlowupChartStatement` quantifies over a universe `u`, a locally noetherian scheme
`X : Scheme.{u}`, a global section `t : Γ(X, ⊤)` and an ideal sheaf `I : X.IdealSheafData`
(Mathlib's data of an ideal `I.ideal U ⊆ Γ(X, U)` for every affine open `U`, compatible with
basic opens). Its hypothesis is that the restriction of `t` to every affine open `U` lies in
`I.ideal U`, that is, `t` is a section of the ideal sheaf. It asserts a scheme `X'` and a
morphism `g : X' ⟶ X` such that

1. `g` is proper (`IsProper g`);
2. `g` is an isomorphism over the open complement of the support of `I`: the restriction
   `g ∣_ I.support.compl : g⁻¹(X ∖ supp I) ⟶ X ∖ supp I` is an isomorphism;
3. for every affine open `U` of `X`, writing `A = Γ(X, U)`, `t_U` for the restriction of `t`
   to `U` and `J = I.ideal U`, there is an open immersion
   `j : Spec A[J/t_U] ⟶ X'` with `j ≫ g = Spec(A → A[J/t_U]) ≫ U.fromSpec`. Here
   `A[J/t_U]` is `Langlands.GroupScheme.Dilatation.coordinateAlgebra t_U J`, the
   `A`-subalgebra of the localization `A[1/t_U]` generated by the fractions `f/t_U` with
   `f ∈ J`, and `U.fromSpec : Spec A ⟶ X` is the canonical open immersion of the affine open.

The proposition does not say that `X'` is unique, nor that it is the relative Proj of the
Rees algebra; any `X'` with the three properties is allowed.

## Sources

The Stacks Project, Sections 10.70 and 31.33 (literature ID
`stacks-blowing-up-10-70-31-33-2026-11-26`, folder SHA-256
`8545f3197157f1573eb8635145e6a048787b47734a21467723ee36321762d91a`; files `052P.html` and
`01OF.html`):

- Definition 10.70.1 (tag 052Q): the Rees algebra `⊕ I^n` and, for `a ∈ I`, the affine
  blowup algebra `R[I/a]`, whose elements are the fractions `x/a^n` with `x ∈ I^n`, two
  fractions being equal when a power of `a` kills their difference. Mapping `x/a^n` to the
  same fraction in `R[1/a]` identifies `R[I/a]` with the `R`-subalgebra of `R[1/a]`
  generated by the fractions `f/a` with `f ∈ I`, which is the Library's `coordinateAlgebra`.
- Definition 31.33.1: the blowing up of `X` in a quasi-coherent ideal sheaf `𝓘` is the
  relative Proj of `⊕_{n ≥ 0} 𝓘^n`.
- Lemma 31.33.2 (tag 0804): over an affine open `U = Spec(A)` with ideal `I`, the inverse
  image of `U` in the blowing up is `Proj(⊕ I^d)`, and it is covered by the affine opens
  `D₊(a^{(1)}) = Spec(A[I/a])` for `a ∈ I`. This gives clause 3 with `a = t_U`.
- Lemma 31.33.4 (tag 02OS): the blowing up is an isomorphism over the complement of the
  centre. This is clause 2; the closed subscheme defined by `I` has underlying set the
  support of `I`.
- Lemma 31.33.13 (tag 02NS): if `𝓘` is of finite type, the blowing up is projective. On a
  locally noetherian scheme every quasi-coherent ideal sheaf is of finite type, and over each
  affine open `Spec(A)` the blowing up is the Proj of a graded `A`-algebra generated by
  finitely many elements of degree one, which is proper over `Spec(A)`; properness is local
  on the target. This is clause 1.

Bosch, Lütkebohmert and Raynaud, *Néron Models* (1990), literature ID
`bosch-lutkebohmert-raynaud-1990-neron-models` (SHA-256
`16d0701b4192ae9db91d083c1df69c0492b00a994aee7bcb0abca365d859d529`), §3.2, printed
pp. 62–63 (PDF pp. 69–70), describe the same construction for a closed subscheme `Y_k` of the
special fibre of a scheme `X` over a discrete valuation ring with uniformizer `π`: the
blowing up is locally the closed subscheme of a projective space over `Spec(A)` with affine
charts given by the algebras `A[𝔍/g_i]`, and the chart where `π` generates `𝔍 · O_{X'}` is
the dilatation `X'_k`. With `t` the image of `π` and `I` the ideal sheaf of `Y_k`, clause 3
is this dilatation chart.

## Use

The proposition supplies the proper morphisms of the smoothening process over a discrete
valuation ring (BLR §3.1, Theorem 3 and §3.4): each step blows up a reduced closed subscheme of
the special fibre, the morphism is an isomorphism away from it and in particular on generic
fibres, and points over extensions of ramification index one which specialize into the centre
lift into the dilatation chart.
