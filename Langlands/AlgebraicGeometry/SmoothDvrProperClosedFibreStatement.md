# Properness from the closed fibre over a discrete valuation ring

`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.SmoothDvrProperClosedFibreStatement` concerns
a smooth, separated, quasi-compact morphism to the spectrum of a discrete
valuation ring. It assumes geometrically connected fibres and properness of
the actual pullback to the residue field. The conclusion is properness of
the given morphism.

## Provenance

This is the smooth DVR case of Grothendieck and Dieudonné, *Éléments de
géométrie algébrique IV*, third part (1966), Corollary 15.7.10, printed
p. 247. Smoothness and quasi-compactness give finite type. The
geometrically connected fibres give surjectivity, and a smooth surjective
morphism is universally open and universally submersive. An open
neighbourhood of the closed point in a local spectrum is the entire base.
