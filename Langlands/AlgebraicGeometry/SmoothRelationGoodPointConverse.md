# Good points from cotangent spanning in an affine chart

This internal module supplies the converse chart bridge in the good-point
existence route designed by Worker 303. Worker 101 implemented this bridge
using the imported chart comparisons, cotangent criterion, finite-generation
argument and simple-integer-equation lemmas. The construction makes no claim
that the required spanning coordinates or simple equations exist on an orbit.

Let `t,s : R → U` be morphisms of schemes, and let `e : U → R` be a common
section: `e ≫ t = 𝟙 U` and `e ≫ s = 𝟙 U`. Fix a point `x`, affine opens
`V` of `U` and `W` of `R`, with `e x ∈ W` and
`W ≤ s ⁻¹ᵁ V ∩ t ⁻¹ᵁ V`. Put `A = Γ(U,V)`, `B = Γ(R,W)` and let `q` be
the prime of `B` corresponding to `e x`. The given `A`-algebra structure
on `B` must be the pullback along `s`; this compatibility is the explicit
hypothesis `hσ`. Write `T = B_q` and let `θ : A → T` be the pullback along
`t` followed by localization. The residue cotangent space in this module
is `κ(T) ⊗_T Ω[T/A]`, relative to the `s` algebra structure.

The lemma `isRationalPoint_localizationAtPrime` proves `IsRationalPoint A T`.
The imported chart lemma supplies rationality of the actual stalk of `R`
at `e x`, using the section of `s` and fraction representatives in the
affine base chart. The canonical equivalence between `B_q` and that stalk
commutes with the `A` maps. Transporting rationality along this equivalence
gives the stated result. No finite type assumption is needed for this step.

The theorem `isGoodPoint_of_cotangentSpan_eq_top` assumes
`LocallyOfFiniteType s` and `cotangentSpan θ = ⊤` and concludes the intrinsic
`IsGoodPoint t s e x`. The imported `cotangentSpan` is generated by
`1 ⊗ dθ(a)` for elements `a` of the prime contracted along the `s` map.
The section identities show that the contractions along `s` and `t` agree.
Local finite type makes the affine chart algebra `B` finite type over `A`;
its localization `T` is therefore essentially finite type. The proved
finite-generation result and rationality supply all hypotheses of
`maximalIdeal_eq_of_cotangentSpan_eq_top_of_essFiniteType`. That criterion
identifies the maximal ideal of `T` with the sum of the extensions of
the common contracted prime along the two maps. Transport this equality
to the actual stalk, use equality of the two chart contractions, and apply
`isGoodPoint_iff_chartMap` to obtain goodness.

The corollary `isGoodPoint_of_residueSpan_eq_top` uses the image-indexed
spanning condition appearing in differential slicing: the generators are
`1 ⊗ d(b)` with `b` in the image under the chart pullback of `t` of
`q.comap (algebraMap A B)`. Contracting the maximal ideal of `B_q` to `B`
gives `q`; reindexing the image identifies this span with `cotangentSpan θ`.
Together with the imported forward implication, this proves
`isGoodPoint_iff_residueSpan_eq_top` under the same local finite type
hypothesis.

For `isGoodPoint_of_simpleIntegerRoots`, instead supply a family
`g : ι → A` whose `t`-pullback differentials span the residue cotangent
space. For each index, assume an integer polynomial `P` such that
`P(g i)` lies in `q.comap (t.appLE V W hWt).hom` and `P'(g i)` lies outside
that ideal. Localization transfers these conditions to the maximal ideal
of `T`. The imported integer chain rule and cancellation of the nonzero
derivative residues show that these equations supply the full
`cotangentSpan θ`. The preceding converse then gives goodness. The
universe of the index type is independent of the scheme universe, and no
finiteness assumption on the index type is imposed.

`SimpleIntegerGoodPointData t s e x hWt q g` records actual integer
polynomials for these coordinates, equation membership and derivative
nonmembership in the indicated chart prime, and a proof of the intrinsic
good-point condition. The definition `simpleIntegerGoodPointData` chooses
the polynomials from the supplied coordinatewise existence proofs and
applies `isGoodPoint_of_simpleIntegerRoots` for its goodness field. It
requires all the chart, section, local finite type and spanning hypotheses
above. It neither chooses the coordinates nor establishes their hypotheses.

Only local finite type of `s` is required. Smoothness, the monomorphism
condition on the relation and the other equivalence-relation axioms are
not assumptions of these conditional bridge results. The full existence
of a good point in every orbit, the construction of a slice and the exact
global slicing proposition remain separate results. This module is
internally derived from the included proved helpers and the pinned Mathlib
localization, finite type and affine-chart interfaces; it has no additional
literature source claim.
