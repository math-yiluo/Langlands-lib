# Finite separable splitting of actual irreducible components

`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.SchemeFiberGeometry.finiteSeparableComponentSplitting` proves exactly the published proposition `FiniteSeparableComponentSplitting.{u}` from `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteSeparableComponentSplittingStatement`, for an arbitrary common universe `u`. The published statement and its import closure remain unchanged.

For an arbitrary field `K` and an actual locally finite-type map `X → Spec K` with finitely many actual maximal irreducible components, the conclusion constructs one actual finite separable field extension `L/K`. Every actual maximal component of the categorical scalar extension `X ×[Spec K] Spec L`, equipped with its vanishing-ideal reduced closed subscheme structure, is geometrically irreducible over `L` through the second projection. No compactness, separation, reducedness, global Noetherianity, perfectness, characteristic, or nonemptiness assumption is added to this conclusion or its hypotheses.

The implemented proof applies `finiteSeparableComponentSplitting_of_compactSpace`. This established reduction chooses a compact open subscheme containing all generic points, using the given finite component set. Local finite type over a field makes that compact open Noetherian, so its component set is finite. After any field extension, the flat projection sends generic points to generic points, and dense-open geometric irreducibility of actual reduced components transfers the compact-open conclusion to all of the original scalar extension. The reduction retains the full noncompact scope of the target.

The compact premise is discharged by `exists_finite_separable_components_of_compactSpace K g`, which needs only compactness and local finite type. Its proof chooses a finite affine subcover and the supremum, inside the separable closure of `K`, of the finitely many affine splitting fields. This supremum is finite dimensional and separable over `K`. Each affine chart's actual component property persists under its further extension to that common field; the field-tower pullback comparison and the open-cover theorem then assemble the chart conclusions. The affine splitting fields are constructed from finite coefficient models of component ideals over the separable closure and geometrically irreducible closed covers, rather than assumed as input.

Thus the proof packages the compact construction into the unrestricted Stacks 054R proposition. It concerns the actual maximal components and their actual reduced closed structures throughout. It does not assert the prescribed finite-etale cover or the Stacks 0551 scheme assembly, which remain separate consumer obligations.
