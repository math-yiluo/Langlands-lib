# Geometric reducedness of reductions after a finite field extension

Lean module: `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.PotentialGeometricReduction`
(namespace `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.PotentialGeometricReduction`).

## Purpose and provenance

This is an internal helper module for the proof of
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.DvrPotentialGenericReductionStatement`
(`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.DvrPotentialGenericReductionProof`). It does not
prove that target. It proves the field-level input in the affine case:

> for a finite-type algebra `A` over a field `K` there is a finite extension `L / K`
> such that the canonical reduced closed subscheme of `Spec (L ⊗[K] A)` is
> geometrically reduced over `L`

(`exists_good_affine`). It also proves the general lemmas that move this property
between schemes: invariance, base change along fields, iterated base change, and
descent along flat surjective geometrically reduced covers. The quasi-compact case and
the DVR step are in a separate proof module.

The route follows the first sentence of the proof of Lemma A.5 in Brian Conrad's
appendix to Gopal Prasad and Jiu-Kang Yu, *On quasi-reductive group schemes*
(author preprint, p. 29): "Since `(X_K̄)_red` is generically smooth, and the nilradical
is locally generated by finitely many elements, by chasing `K`-coefficients we may
find a finite extension `K'/K` such that `(X'_red) ⊗_{K'} K̄` is reduced." Here
"generically smooth" is used in the form of `geometricallyReduced_of_perfectField`
below. The nilradical generators are handled in `exists_isReduced_algebraicClosure`.
Registered source: `prasad-yu-2006-quasi-reductive-author-preprint`, SHA-256
`138d931a21fa522d97449c77acf429918826b1ef649be940d4721d616a1fc73b`.

The module sets `backward.isDefEq.respectTransparency false`, as several Library
modules it imports do.

## Declarations

### Perfect fields

- `geometricallyReduced_of_perfectField {F} [Field F] [PerfectField F] {Y} [IsReduced Y]
  (f : Y ⟶ Spec F) [QuasiCompact f] [LocallyOfFiniteType f] : GeometricallyReduced f`.
  A reduced quasi-compact, locally finite-type scheme over a perfect field is
  geometrically reduced.

  Proof. Over a noetherian base, finite type gives finite presentation
  (`NoetherianValuativeCriterion.locallyOfFinitePresentation_of_isNoetherianRing`).
  Mathlib's `Scheme.Hom.dense_smoothLocus_of_perfectField` then says that the smooth
  locus `U` of `f` is dense. `Y` is noetherian, so the open immersion `U.ι` is
  quasi-compact and dominant. Since `Y` is reduced, it is schematically dominant
  (`IsSchemeTheoreticallyDominant.of_isDominant`). The
  composite `U.ι ≫ f` is smooth, so it is geometrically reduced by the published
  `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.smoothGeometricallyReduced`. The published
  `Langlands.SchemeImageGeometry.geometricallyReduced_of_schematicallyDominant` then
  transfers geometric reducedness from `U.ι ≫ f` to `f`.

### The canonical reduction and the predicate `Good`

- `red Y` and `redι Y : red Y ⟶ Y` (abbreviations): the closed subscheme cut out by
  `Scheme.IdealSheafData.vanishingIdeal (⊤ : Closeds Y)` and its inclusion. This is the
  same construction as `DvrPotentialGenericReduction.reducedOver` in the target
  statement: for `X : Over S`, `(reducedOver X).hom` is by definition
  `redι X.left ≫ X.hom`.
- Instances: `red Y` is reduced
  (`ReducedSubscheme.isReduced_vanishingIdeal_subscheme`) and `redι Y` is surjective
  (`ReducedSubscheme.range_subschemeι_vanishingIdeal`).
- `exists_isIso_lift_red i`: a surjective closed immersion `i : Z ⟶ Y` with `Z` reduced
  factors as `e ≫ redι Y` with `e` an isomorphism. The lift `e` comes from
  `ReducedSubscheme.exists_lift_vanishingIdeal`. It is a closed immersion (since
  `e ≫ redι Y = i` is one) and surjective (since `redι Y` is injective and `i` is
  surjective), hence an isomorphism (`isIso_of_isClosedImmersion_of_surjective`).
- `geometricallyReduced_comp_iff_red i f`: for such `i` and any `f : Y ⟶ S`,
  `GeometricallyReduced (i ≫ f) ↔ GeometricallyReduced (redι Y ≫ f)`. This follows
  from the previous lemma, because geometric reducedness respects isomorphisms.
- `Good f` (definition, a `Prop`) for `f : Y ⟶ S`: there are a scheme `Z` and a closed
  immersion `i : Z ⟶ Y` that is surjective, with `Z` reduced and
  `GeometricallyReduced (i ≫ f)`. It is an existential version of "the reduction of `Y`
  is geometrically reduced over `S`". It is convenient because it does not refer to a
  fixed construction of the reduction.
- `good_iff f : Good f ↔ GeometricallyReduced (redι Y ≫ f)`. Use
  `geometricallyReduced_comp_iff_red` in one direction and the witness `redι Y` in the
  other.
- `Good.iso_comp`: if `Good f` and `e : Y' ⟶ Y` is an isomorphism, then `Good (e ≫ f)`.
  The witness is `i ≫ inv e`.
- `Good.pullback_snd`: if `Good f` with `f : Y ⟶ S` and `g : Spec M ⟶ S` for a field `M`,
  then `Good (pullback.snd f g)`. If `i : Z ⟶ Y` is a witness, then
  `pullback.snd i (pullback.fst f g)` is a witness. Its source is isomorphic to
  `pullback (i ≫ f) g` (`pullbackRightPullbackFstIso`). That scheme is reduced because
  `i ≫ f` is geometrically reduced (`pullback_of_geometrically`). Geometric reducedness
  is stable under base change.
- `good_pullback_snd_comp_iff f g g'`: for `f : X ⟶ Z`, `g : Y ⟶ Z`, `g' : W ⟶ Y`,
  `Good (pullback.snd f (g' ≫ g)) ↔ Good (pullback.snd (pullback.snd f g) g')`. Both
  directions use `Good.iso_comp` with `pullbackLeftPullbackSndIso`.
- `Good.of_comp p f`: if `p : P ⟶ Y` is flat, surjective and geometrically reduced,
  `Y` is locally noetherian, and `Good (p ≫ f)`, then `Good f`. After `good_iff`, pull
  `redι Y` back along `p`. The scheme `red Y` is reduced, and it is locally noetherian
  because `redι Y` is of finite type over the locally noetherian `Y`. Since `p` is flat
  and geometrically reduced, Mathlib's instance (proved via
  `GeometricallyReduced.isReduced_of_flat_of_isLocallyNoetherian`) makes the pullback
  reduced. Its projection to `P` is a surjective closed immersion, so by
  `geometricallyReduced_comp_iff_red` the composite
  `pullback.snd (redι Y) p ≫ p ≫ f` is geometrically reduced. By `pullback.condition`
  this is `pullback.fst (redι Y) p ≫ redι Y ≫ f`, and `pullback.fst` is flat and
  surjective. The published
  `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.geometricallyReduced_of_surjective_flat_comp` then gives
  `GeometricallyReduced (redι Y ≫ f)`.
- `good_of_perfectField f`: over a perfect field every quasi-compact, locally
  finite-type `f` is good. Apply `geometricallyReduced_of_perfectField` to `redι Y ≫ f`.
- `geometricallyReduced_of_isReduced_pullback`: let `f : Z ⟶ Spec L` be quasi-compact
  and locally of finite type over a field `L`, and let `M` be a perfect field over `L`.
  If the base change of `Z` along `Spec M ⟶ Spec L` is reduced, then `f` is
  geometrically reduced. By `geometricallyReduced_of_perfectField`, the base change is
  geometrically reduced over `M`. `Spec M ⟶ Spec L` is surjective, so the published
  `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.geometricallyReduced_of_surjective_baseChange` descends
  this to `f`.

### Algebra: chasing coefficients of nilradical generators

Throughout, `K` is a field, `A` a commutative `K`-algebra and `K̄ = AlgebraicClosure K`.

- `isReduced_tensor_quotient_nilradical`: for a field `L`, an `L`-algebra `E` and an
  `L`-algebra `B`, suppose the nilradical of `E ⊗[L] B` is contained in the ideal
  generated by the image of the nilradical of `B` under `b ↦ 1 ⊗ b`. Then
  `E ⊗[L] (B ⧸ nilradical B)` is reduced. The kernel of the surjection
  `E ⊗[L] B → E ⊗[L] (B ⧸ nilradical B)` is exactly that extended ideal
  (`Algebra.TensorProduct.lTensor_ker`). That ideal consists of nilpotents, so by
  hypothesis it equals the nilradical.
- `psi K A L` (abbreviation) for an intermediate field `L` of `K̄ / K`: the algebra map
  `L ⊗[K] A → K̄ ⊗[K] A` induced by the inclusion `L → K̄`.
- `exists_finite_mem_range_psi K A c`: every `c ∈ K̄ ⊗[K] A` lies in the range of
  `psi K A L'` for every `L'` containing some finite-dimensional intermediate field `L`.
  The proof is by induction on tensors: for `x ⊗ a` take `L = K(x)`, which is finite
  because `x` is algebraic. For sums take the compositum.
- `exists_finite_subset_range_psi K A s`: the same for a finite set `s`, with one
  finite-dimensional `L`. The proof is by induction on `s`, using the compositum and the
  compatibility of `psi` with inclusions of intermediate fields.
- `psi' K A L` (abbreviation): the comparison map `L ⊗[K] A → K̄ ⊗[K] A` for a field `L`
  given as a type with `K ⊆ L ⊆ K̄` (scalar tower). `psi'_eq_psi` identifies it with
  `psi` for intermediate fields. `psi'_injective` holds because `A` is flat over the
  field `K` (`Module.Flat.rTensor_preserves_injective_linearMap`).
- `isReduced_of_nilradical_generators K A L s hs hsL`: let `s` generate the nilradical
  of `K̄ ⊗[K] A`, with every element of `s` in the range of `psi' K A L`. Then
  `K̄ ⊗[L] ((L ⊗[K] A) ⧸ nilradical (L ⊗[K] A))` is reduced. Apply
  `isReduced_tensor_quotient_nilradical` with `E = K̄`, `B = L ⊗[K] A`. The isomorphism
  `e : K̄ ⊗[L] (L ⊗[K] A) ≃ K̄ ⊗[K] A` (`Algebra.TensorProduct.cancelBaseChange`)
  restricts to `psi' K A L` on `1 ⊗ B`. If `g = psi' b` is a generator, then `g` is
  nilpotent. Since `psi'` is injective, `b` is nilpotent, so `e⁻¹ g` lies in the
  extended nilradical of `B`. Hence `e⁻¹` maps the whole nilradical of `K̄ ⊗[K] A` into
  that ideal, which is the hypothesis needed.
- `exists_isReduced_algebraicClosure K A` for `[Algebra.FiniteType K A]`: there are a
  field `L` with a `K`-algebra structure, finite-dimensional over `K`, and a perfect
  field `M` with an `L`-algebra structure, such that
  `M ⊗[L] ((L ⊗[K] A) ⧸ nilradical (L ⊗[K] A))` is reduced. The proof takes `M = K̄`.
  `K̄ ⊗[K] A` is a finitely generated `K̄`-algebra, hence noetherian, so its nilradical
  has a finite generating set `s`. Then `exists_finite_subset_range_psi` gives `L` and
  `isReduced_of_nilradical_generators` concludes. The conclusion is phrased with an
  abstract field `L` (an existential over types and instances), because
  quotient-ring instances on `↥L ⊗[K] A` for an intermediate field `L` were hard for
  instance synthesis.

### The affine case

- `good_spec h`: let `B` be a finite-type algebra over a field `L`, and `M` a perfect
  field over `L`. If `M ⊗[L] (B ⧸ nilradical B)` is reduced, then
  `Good (Spec.map (algebraMap L B))`. The witness is the closed immersion
  `Spec (B ⧸ nilradical B) ⟶ Spec B`. It is surjective because the nilradical lies in
  every prime (`PrimeSpectrum.comap_quotientMk_bijective_of_le_nilradical`), and its
  source is reduced. Its composite with the structure map is
  `Spec.map (algebraMap L (B ⧸ nilradical B))`, which is locally of finite type. Its
  base change to `Spec M` is `Spec ((B ⧸ nilradical B) ⊗[L] M)` (`pullbackSpecIso`),
  which is reduced by the hypothesis and commutativity of `⊗`.
  `geometricallyReduced_of_isReduced_pullback` concludes.
- `exists_good_affine K A` for `[Algebra.FiniteType K A]`: there is a field `L`,
  finite-dimensional over `K`, such that
  `Good (pullback.snd (Spec.map (algebraMap K A)) (Spec.map (algebraMap K L)))`, i.e.
  the base change of `Spec A` to `Spec L` is good over `Spec L`. Take `L` from
  `exists_isReduced_algebraicClosure`. Then `good_spec` applies to `B = L ⊗[K] A`,
  which is of finite type over `L`. `pullbackSpecIso` identifies the base change with
  `Spec (L ⊗[K] A) ⟶ Spec L`, and `Good.iso_comp` transports the property.

`red` and `redι` use the canonical scheme and inclusion in `GroupScheme/ReducedSubgroup`.
