# Global functions and the special fibre of a smooth DVR scheme

This module concerns an arbitrary actual smooth scheme `Y` over a discrete
valuation ring `R`. It imposes no affineness, quasi-compactness, separatedness,
properness, connectedness or henselianity hypothesis.

`scalar Y r` is the pullback of a base element as a global function.
For an irreducible element `π` of `R`, its basic open is the complement of the
actual special fibre. A function on an affine open that vanishes at all points
of the special fibre is divisible by the restricted `π`. This follows from
reducedness of the smooth residue algebra and the theorem
`SmoothDVRUniformizerDivision.dvd_of_forall_mem_prime`. The local quotients of
a global function glue: multiplication by `π` is injective on all affine
opens by flatness, and `Smoothening.exists_eq_mul_of_affine` applies.

`specialFiber_appTop_eq_zero_iff` identifies zero restriction under the actual
fibre inclusion with pointwise vanishing on that fibre. The converse uses
reducedness of the smooth special fibre: a function with empty basic open is
zero. `ker_specialFiber_appTop` then proves that the kernel of the actual
restriction map is exactly the ideal generated by the pulled-back uniformizer.

`specialFiberSectionMap Y hπ` is the resulting ring map
`Γ(Y, O_Y)/(π) → Γ(Y_s, O_{Y_s})`, where `Y_s` is the actual scheme-theoretic
fibre at the closed point of `Spec R`. Its composition with the quotient map
is precisely restriction along the actual fibre inclusion
(`specialFiberSectionMap_mk`). `specialFiberSectionMap_injective` proves
injectivity from the kernel computation. The codomain is the actual global
function ring, and no surjectivity or base-change isomorphism is asserted.
