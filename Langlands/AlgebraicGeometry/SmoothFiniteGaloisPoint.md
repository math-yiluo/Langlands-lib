# Points of smooth schemes over finite Galois extensions

Worker 1296.

This helper module shows that a nonempty smooth affine scheme over a field
`K` has a point over a finite Galois extension of `K`. This is a standard fact
(smooth schemes over a field have points over finite separable extensions). It
is internal infrastructure for the proof of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.RegularConjugateFiniteGaloisStatement`. The field `K`
is arbitrary in universe `u`: it may be imperfect or finite.

## Algebraic form

`exists_finiteGalois_algHom_of_smooth K R`: let `R` be a nontrivial commutative
ring with `Algebra.Smooth K R`. Then there are a field `L` in universe `u` with
a `K`-algebra structure, finite-dimensional and Galois over `K`, and a
`K`-algebra homomorphism `R →ₐ[K] L`.

Proof.

1. Let `Ω = SeparableClosure K`. The ring `Ω ⊗[K] R` is nontrivial: it maps
   to `Ω ⊗[K] (R/m)` for a maximal ideal `m`, a tensor product of two field
   extensions of `K`, which is nontrivial.
2. `Ω ⊗[K] R` is smooth over `Ω`, hence geometrically reduced over `Ω`
   (published `PointTopology.isGeometricallyReduced_of_smooth`).
3. The published theorem `separablyClosedRationalPoint` gives an
   `Ω`-algebra homomorphism `Ω ⊗[K] R → Ω`. Restricting to `R` gives
   `φ : R →ₐ[K] Ω`.
4. `R` is of finite type over `K`. Let `t` be a finite generating set and `F`
   the subfield of `Ω` generated over `K` by `φ(t)`. Its generators are
   separable algebraic, so `F` is finite over `K`.
5. Let `N` be the normal closure of `F` in `Ω`. It is finite and normal over
   `K`, and separable since `Ω` is, hence Galois. The image of `φ` is the
   subalgebra generated by `φ(t)`, which lies in `F ≤ N`. So `φ` corestricts
   to `R →ₐ[K] N`.

## Geometric form

`exists_finiteGalois_point_of_smooth K f`: let `X` be a nonempty affine scheme
and `f : X ⟶ Spec K` smooth. Then there are a finite Galois extension `L` of
`K` as above and `σ : Spec L ⟶ X` with
`σ ≫ f = Spec.map (algebraMap K L)`.

Proof. Write `X ≅ Spec Γ(X, ⊤)` (`X.isoSpec`). The composite with `f` is
`Spec.map φ` for a ring map `φ : K → Γ(X, ⊤)`, which is smooth by the affine
characterization of smoothness. The ring `Γ(X, ⊤)` is nontrivial since `X` is
nonempty. The algebraic form gives `a : Γ(X, ⊤) →ₐ[K] L`, and
`σ = Spec.map a ≫ X.isoSpec.inv`. The compatibility with the structure
morphisms is `a.commutes`.
