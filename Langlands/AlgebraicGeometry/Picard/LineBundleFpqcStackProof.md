# Full fpqc stack descent for actual line bundles: proof

Module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.LineBundleFpqcStackProof`.

## Main result

`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.lineBundleFpqcStack : LineBundleFpqcStackStatement.{u}`
proves the Library proposition `LineBundleFpqcStackStatement` (module
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.LineBundleFpqcStackStatement`) at every universe `u`.
The first conjunct is the instance `PicardGroupoid.isStack_fpqc`, proved here. The second
conjunct is the published `PicardGroupoid.relative_isStack_fpqc f` (module
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.RelativeStack`). That lemma deduces the relative stack
property for every `f : X ⟶ S` from the global instance by stack transport along
`relativeSpace f`.

The proof does not follow the source route of the statement (descent of quasi-coherent
sheaves, Stacks Tag 023T). It reduces to two base cases for which the Library already has
module-level descent results: open covers, and single faithfully flat morphisms. The base
cases are in [`LineBundleStackBase`](LineBundleStackBase.md) and are applied through the
realization `PicardGroupoid.moduleRealization` of
[`LineBundleStackDictionary`](LineBundleStackDictionary.md).

## Base cases for `PicardGroupoid.pseudofunctor`

`isPrestackFor_openCover`, `essSurj_openCover`, `isPrestackFor_singleton` and
`essSurj_singleton` specialize the corresponding results of `LineBundleStackBase` to
`PicardGroupoid.pseudofunctor`. The open-cover results are for `X.OpenCover.{u}`. The
singleton results are for quasi-compact flat surjections (morphisms) and for affine flat
surjections onto an affine scheme (objects).

## Morphism descent (`isPrestack_fpqc`)

- `isPrestack_zariski`: the line-bundle pseudofunctor is a prestack for the Zariski topology.
  The Zariski precoverage is `Small.{u}` and stable under base change, so
  `StackComposite.isPrestack_of_small` reduces to morphism descent along open covers indexed
  in universe `u`, which is `isPrestackFor_openCover`.
- `isSheaf_fpqcTopology_over` (site reduction): a presheaf of types on `Over S` is an fpqc
  sheaf as soon as it is a Zariski sheaf and satisfies the sheaf condition along every lift
  to `Over S` of a flat surjection of affine schemes `Spec R' ⟶ Spec R`. The proof is the
  fpqc analogue of the Library's `OverFppfSheaf.isSheaf_fppfTopology_over`. It transfers the
  sheaf condition between `Over S` and schemes with the Library's
  `isSheaf_over_of_isSheaf_ext`, `isSheaf_ext_of_isSheaf_over` and
  `isSheafFor_singleton_ext`, and uses Mathlib's characterization
  `isSheaf_type_propQCTopology_iff` of fpqc sheaves.
- `isPrestack_fpqc`: for line bundles `M`, `N` on `S`, the presheaf `presheafHom M N` is an
  fpqc sheaf on `Over S`. The Zariski condition is `isPrestack_zariski`. The singleton
  condition follows from `isPrestackFor_singleton` (a morphism of affine schemes is
  quasi-compact) through Mathlib's `IsPrestackFor.isSheafFor'`.

## Effective descent (`isStack_fpqc`)

By Mathlib's `IsStack.of_precoverage`, it suffices to prove `isStackFor_fpqc`: effective
descent along every presieve `R` of `Scheme.fpqcPrecoverage S`.

1. By `mem_propQCPrecoverage_iff_exists_quasiCompactCover`, `R` is the presieve of a
   quasi-compact flat cover `𝒰`. It is replaced by Mathlib's `Cover.qculift 𝒰`, whose members
   are members of `𝒰` and whose index type lies in universe `u`.
2. `exists_refinement_affine`: for every affine scheme `A` mapping to `S` by `r`, the pulled
   back cover `𝒰.pullback₂ r` has a finite refinement by spectra (Mathlib's
   `QuasiCompactCover.exists_hom`). Let `W = ∐ₖ Spec Rₖ`. Then `w = Sigma.desc : W ⟶ A` is
   an affine flat surjection between affine schemes, and the coprojections form the open
   cover `sigmaOpenCover` of `W`. `StackComposite.essSurj_toDescentData_comp` applies to
   `essSurj_singleton w` and `essSurj_openCover`. It gives effective descent along the
   composite family `Spec Rₖ ⟶ W ⟶ A`. This family generates an fpqc covering sieve, and each
   member factors through a member of `𝒰` after composing with `r`.
3. `isStackFor_fpqc`: apply step 2 to every member `r` of `S.affineCover`, then apply
   `essSurj_toDescentData_comp` once more with `essSurj_openCover S.affineCover`. This gives
   effective descent along a family `d` of composites into `S`. Its sieve covers by
   transitivity of the fpqc topology. Together with `isPrestack_fpqc`,
   `isStackFor_of_essSurj` makes `d` a stack family. Every member of `d` factors through a
   member of `𝒰`, so Mathlib's `IsStackFor.of_le'` (which uses the prestack property) gives
   effective descent along the sieve generated by `R`. This is equivalent to effective
   descent along `R` (`IsStackFor_generate_iff`).
