# The zero ideal of a section in local frames, and its pullback

Helper module of the proof of `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.ProperFlatIntegralLinearSystemStatement` (entry module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.ProperFlatIntegralLinearSystemProof`). It contains no `sorry` and no new axioms.

## Content

Namespace `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.SectionZeroIdeal`. Frames are the FLT port's
`Scheme.Modules.IsFrameOn` (a section `t` over `U` such that `a ↦ a • t` is bijective over every
smaller open).

- `frameEquiv ht`: the coordinate in a frame, a linear equivalence `L(U) ≃ Γ(X, U)`;
  `frameEquiv_smul`.
- `zeroIdeal_ideal_of_frame`: on an affine open with a frame, the zero ideal is generated by the
  coordinate of `σ`.
- `exists_affine_frame`: every point has an affine neighbourhood carrying a frame (FLT
  `exists_isFrameOn_of_pullback_iso_unit`).
- `zeroIdeal_zero` (the zero section has zero ideal `⊥`), `zeroIdeal_smul_unit` (unit multiples),
  `zeroIdeal_iso` (transport along isomorphisms of line bundles).
- `pullbackSection φ L σ` and `zeroIdeal_comap`: the zero ideal commutes with pullback along any
  morphism `φ` (FLT `IsFrameOn.pullbackLocalSection` and `IdealSheafData.ideal_comap_of_le`).
