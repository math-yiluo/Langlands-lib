Provenance: internal. The notions are those of S. L. Kleiman, *The Picard
scheme* (`kleiman-2005-picard-scheme-arxiv-math0504020v1`), 3.1 and
Definition 3.3, p.22: an effective divisor is a closed subscheme with
invertible ideal, and a relative effective divisor is moreover flat over the
base. In Lean these are the Library predicates `IsEffectiveCartierIdeal I`
(the kernel module of `I.subschemeι` is locally free of rank one) and
`IsRelativeEffectiveCartierIdeal f I` (moreover `I.subschemeι ≫ f` is flat),
from `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.RelativeEffectiveDivisor`. The last
theorem is Kleiman's Lemma 3.4, (iii) ⟹ (i) (p.23), in affine form.

## Helper lemmas

Let `I : X.IdealSheafData` be an ideal sheaf on a scheme `X`.

- `flat_res`: restriction maps `Γ(X, U) → Γ(X, W)` between affine opens
  `W ≤ U` are flat.
- `subschemeι_app_res_eq_zero`: if `s ∈ I.ideal U` (`U` affine), the
  restriction of `s` to any open `W ≤ U` is killed by `I.subschemeι.app W`.
- `sRes s V`: the restriction of `s : Γ(X, U)` to the image in `X` of an open
  `V` of the scheme `U`.
- `mulFun I s hs V`: multiplication by `sRes s V`, from `Γ(X, U.ι ''ᵁ V)` to
  the kernel of `I.subschemeι.app (U.ι ''ᵁ V)`; `mulFun_val` computes it.
- `mulEquation I s hs`: these maps form a morphism of sheaves of modules from
  the structure sheaf of `U` to the restriction to `U` of the kernel module
  `kernelModule I.subschemeι`.
- `bijective_mulEquation`: if `I.ideal U = (s)` and `s` is regular in
  `Γ(X, U)`, then `mulEquation` is bijective on sections over every affine
  open `V` of `U`. Injectivity: `s` stays regular on `V`, since restriction is
  flat. Surjectivity: by `I.map_ideal`, the ideal on `V` is generated by the
  restriction of `s`, and the kernel of `I.subschemeι.app` on an affine open
  is `I.ideal` (`ker_subschemeι_app`).

## Main results

- `isEffectiveCartierIdeal_of_local`: if every point `x` has an affine open
  neighbourhood `U` and `s : Γ(X, U)` with `I.ideal U = (s)` and `s` regular,
  then `I` is effective Cartier. Proof: `mulEquation` is bijective on affine
  opens, which form a basis, so it is an isomorphism
  (`ProperFormalCoherentExistenceOfFLT.isIso_of_bijective_of_basis`). Its
  inverse shows that the kernel module is free of rank one on `U`.
- `flat_subscheme_of_local`: let `f : X ⟶ S`. If every point has affine
  neighbourhoods `U` of `X` and `V` of `S` with `U ≤ f⁻¹ V` such that
  `Γ(S, V) → Γ(X, U) ⧸ I.ideal U` is flat, then `I.subschemeι ≫ f` is flat.
  Proof: flatness is checked on affine charts
  (`HasRingHomProperty.iff_exists_appLE`), and the closed subscheme over `U`
  has ring `Γ(X, U) ⧸ I.ideal U` (`subschemeObjIso`).
- `isRelativeEffectiveCartierIdeal_of_local`: combines the two: local regular
  generators `s` with `Γ(S, V) → Γ(X, U) ⧸ (s)` flat give a relative effective
  Cartier ideal.
- `isRelativeEffectiveCartierIdeal_of_fibreRegular` (Kleiman, Lemma 3.4,
  (iii) ⟹ (i)): let `f : X ⟶ S` be flat with `X` locally noetherian. If every
  point has affine neighbourhoods `U ≤ f⁻¹ V` and a generator `s` of
  `I.ideal U` that is regular in the fibre local ring
  `R_P ⧸ (P ∩ Γ(S, V)) R_P` at every maximal ideal `P` of `R = Γ(X, U)`, then
  `I` is a relative effective Cartier ideal for `f`. Proof:
  `FibrewiseRegular.regular_and_flat_quotient` (module
  `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.FibrewiseRegularFlat`) and the previous
  theorem.
