# Positive finite-support Euler characteristics and nonempty ample cuts

The first two lemmas combine the published finite-closed-support vanishing
and zero-detection theorem with the actual Euler cutoff identity. The
degree-zero derived comparison identifies zeroth cohomology with actual
global sections. Since its vector space is finite dimensional by hypothesis,
positive dimension is equivalent to the coefficient sheaf being nonzero.

The third lemma argues that a proper affine scheme over a field is finite.
The resulting locally quasi-finite morphism to the Artinian base makes the
scheme locally Artinian. Its topology is discrete, so its topological
Krull dimension is at most zero, contrary to the positive-dimension
hypothesis.

An affine open covering the whole scheme would make the scheme affine.
The fourth lemma uses this observation to find a point outside any affine
open in a nonaffine scheme. The final theorem applies it to the section
provided by the published ampleness predicate. It proves exactly
`EulerPositivityPreparationStatement`, without a construction of a zero
scheme or an assertion about Cartier-divisor dimension.
