Its conclusion uses the existing `IntegralDiagonalPositive` predicate and the fixed actual rational trace form. The conclusion quantifies over arbitrary universe `u`, algebraically closed characteristic-zero fields `k : Type u`, all Rosati construction prerequisites, and every `HasRationalInverse` instance. The theorem `integralDiagonalPositive_of_ampleMumfordPresentation` proves this reduction.

In dimension zero every integral endomorphism is zero, so the conclusion is vacuous. In positive dimension the mixed positivity statement makes the pullback mixed Euler coefficient strictly positive. The trace formula identifies twice that coefficient with the diagonal trace times the top pure Euler coefficient. Ampleness makes the latter coefficient strictly positive, so the trace is strictly positive as well.
