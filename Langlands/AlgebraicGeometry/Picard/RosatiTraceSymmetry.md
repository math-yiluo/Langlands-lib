# Symmetry of the fixed actual Rosati trace form

`Langlands.AbelianScheme.Rosati.TraceFormSymmetryStatement` states symmetry of the already defined rational trace form for every actual geometric polarization over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field `k : Type u`. It retains arbitrary universe `u`, all existing Rosati construction prerequisites, the actual dual data and additivity witness, and every `HasRationalInverse` instance. `traceFormSymmetry` proves this statement; `traceForm_symm` gives its pointwise conclusion.

The prime-level theorem `primeEndomorphismTrace_rosati_swap` works over an algebraically closed field of any characteristic different from the chosen prime. The actual polarization Weil pairing is alternating and nondegenerate, by the published geometric results. A scalar coordinate on its rational Tate twist turns it into a nondegenerate alternating scalar bilinear form. The defining Rosati operation is adjoint for this form. Alternation makes the adjointness equation reversible: if the operator of `b` has adjoint the operator of `b` dagger, the latter has adjoint the former. Consequently the operators of `a * b` dagger and `b * a` dagger are adjoint. The published equality of traces of nondegenerate adjoint operators gives the prime trace identity, including Mathlib's totalized infinite-dimensional trace case.

For the fixed rational trace, the proof distinguishes the two branches of its existing definition. When a rational lift of the actual 2-adic trace exists, injectivity of the scalar map and the prime identity prove symmetry. In the zero fallback branch both values are zero. No existence or rationality of a trace lift is claimed or assumed.

This is an internal derivation from published actual Weil adjunction, alternation, nondegeneracy and trace lemmas. It proves symmetry only. It does not prove strict positivity, the geometric trace comparison or the full algebra anti-involution laws. In particular, the argument does not require a separate proof that the actual Rosati operation is involutive on rational endomorphisms.
