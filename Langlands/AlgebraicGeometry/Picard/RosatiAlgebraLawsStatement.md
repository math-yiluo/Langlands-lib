# Algebra laws of the actual rational Rosati operation

`Langlands.AbelianScheme.Rosati.AlgebraLawsStatement` is the unproved assertion that the actual Rosati conjugation attached to a geometric polarization is additive, rational-linear, reverses products, fixes rational scalar endomorphisms, and squares to the identity. It concerns all actual abelian schemes over algebraically closed fields of characteristic zero in universe `u`.

The source is Milne, *Abelian Varieties*, January 2, 2022 author revision, §17 p.35, the formulas immediately after the definition of the Rosati involution and the subsequent argument for involutivity using the Weil pairing. Source ID: `milne-abelian-varieties-1986-author-2022`. The full registered errata snapshot was read; its corrections are incorporated in this revision. The source has wider field scope; this target uses the geometric characteristic-zero scope needed here.

All operations are those of the definition module: the existing rational dual `dualHom0`, the rationalization of the actual polarization, and its actual inverse in the rational category. The product on `End0 A` is composition with the right factor applied first, so the source formula becomes `involution (a * b) = involution b * involution a`. Fixing `algebraMap ℚ (End0 A) q` expresses the source's assertion about rational scalars, including the identity at `q = 1`. Rational linearity is made explicit in addition to additivity.

The existing tensor/Picard prerequisites, the actual dual witnesses and pointwise dual additivity are explicit in `P`, `W` and `hadd`. `W.IsPolarization P lam` is the unchanged Library predicate. The quantified `HasRationalInverse` instance is precisely the prerequisite needed to form the already defined operation; the separate `RationalInverseStatement` asserts its existence from that same polarization premise without extra assumptions. No dual anti-involution laws, biduality, positivity, trace comparison or finite-dimensionality are supplied as premises. Additivity and rational linearity already follow from the bundled linear map, while the other clauses remain proof obligations.
