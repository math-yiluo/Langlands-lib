# Universal elements from representability on noetherian affine tests

Helper module for `noetherianAffineTestsPicardExtension`, the proof of
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.NoetherianAffineTestsPicardExtensionStatement`
(a step towards
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.AmpleNormalFiniteTypeAbelianPicardSheafAFSchemeStatement`).
It contains the part of the extension from noetherian affine tests to all tests
that works for an arbitrary fppf sheaf. The Picard-specific part is in
`NoetherianAffineTestsPicardExtension`.

Throughout, `S` is a scheme, `F : FppfSheaf S` is a type-valued fppf sheaf on the
whole slice `Over S`, and `P : Over S`. A *noetherian affine test* is an object
`T : Over S` whose underlying scheme `T.left` is affine and noetherian (Mathlib's
`IsAffine` and `IsNoetherian`). The input is a natural isomorphism

`α : ι.op ⋙ yoneda.obj P ⋙ uliftFunctor ≅ ι.op ⋙ F.obj`,

where `ι = ObjectProperty.ι (fun T ↦ IsAffine T.left ∧ IsNoetherian T.left)` is the
inclusion of the full subcategory of noetherian affine tests. Thus `α` identifies
`Hom_S(T, P)` with `F(T)` naturally in noetherian affine tests `T`. This is the
form of the representability conclusion in the statement
`NoetherianIntegralFibrePicardNoetherianTestsStatement` of Task 5705 (accepted in
review, not yet in the Library; written there with the abbreviation
`noetherianAffineOverι`). This module does not import that statement.

## Declarations

- `eq_of_isOpenCover`: two sections of `F` over `T` that agree on the members of an
  open cover of `T.left` (viewed as tests via `ProperSchemeCover.openOver`) are
  equal. Proof: the sieve generated by the open cover is an fppf covering
  (`ProperSchemeCover.openOver_covering`), and a sheaf is separated for it.
- `isOpenCover_affineOpens`, `exists_affineOpen_le`, `isOpenCover_all_affineOpens`:
  the affine opens with a property `Q` cover a scheme when every point lies in
  one of them; every point has an affine neighbourhood inside any open
  neighbourhood (`Scheme.isBasis_affineOpens`); all affine opens cover.
- Instances: the open subscheme `openOver T W` of an affine open `W` is affine;
  it is noetherian when `T.left` is locally noetherian (an affine scheme is
  quasi-compact); every open of a locally noetherian scheme is locally noetherian.
- `aff α T g` and `affInv α T x`: the components of `α.hom` and `α.inv` at a
  noetherian affine test `T`, with the `ULift` removed. `aff_comp` is the
  naturality of `α.hom` for a morphism `b : T' ⟶ T` of noetherian affine tests:
  `aff α T' (b ≫ g) = F.map b.op (aff α T g)`. `aff_affInv`, `affInv_aff` and
  `aff_injective` record that `aff α T` is a bijection with inverse `affInv α T`.
- `chartSection α W` (assuming `P.left` locally noetherian): for an affine open
  `W` of `P.left`, the section `aff α (openOver P W) (openOverι P W)` of `F` over
  the open subscheme `W`, which is a noetherian affine test.
- `chartSection_compatible`: the chart sections of two affine opens `W`, `W'`
  agree on `W ⊓ W'`. Proof: by `eq_of_isOpenCover` it suffices to compare them on
  each affine open `V` of `W ⊓ W'`. Such `V` is a noetherian affine test, and by
  `aff_comp` both restrictions equal `aff α V (V ↪ P)`, since the composites
  `V ↪ W ⊓ W' ↪ W ↪ P` and `V ↪ W ⊓ W' ↪ W' ↪ P` are both the inclusion of `V`
  (`openOverHomOfLE_ι`).
- `exists_universal`: there is a section `ξ ∈ F(P)` restricting to
  `chartSection α W` on every affine open `W` of `P.left`. Proof: glue the
  compatible chart sections by the fppf sheaf property for the open cover of
  `P.left` by all its affine opens (`ProperSchemeCover.exists_glue`).
- `map_universal`: for such `ξ`, every noetherian affine test `T` and every
  `g : T ⟶ P`, `F.map g.op ξ = aff α T g`. Proof: cover `T.left` by the affine
  opens `V` with `g(V) ⊆ W` for some affine open `W` of `P.left`. For such `V`, `g`
  restricted to `V` factors as `c ≫ (W ↪ P)` with `c : V ⟶ W`
  (`Scheme.Hom.resLE`). Then `ξ|_V = F.map c.op (chartSection α W)
  = aff α V (c ≫ (W ↪ P)) = aff α V (g|_V) = (aff α T g)|_V` by `aff_comp`
  (used twice). Since `T` is noetherian, `V` is a noetherian affine test.
  Conclude by `eq_of_isOpenCover`.
- `exists_universal_map`: the combination of the two previous results. There is
  `ξ ∈ F(P)` with `F.map g.op ξ = aff α T g` for every noetherian affine test `T`
  and every `g : T ⟶ P`.
- `representableByOfBijectiveAffine F P ξ h`: if for every `T : Over S` with
  affine `T.left` the map `g ↦ F.map g.op ξ` from `Hom_S(T, P)` to `F(T)` is
  bijective, then `F.obj.RepresentableBy P`, with `homEquiv g = F.map g.op ξ`
  (simp lemma `representableByOfBijectiveAffine_homEquiv`). Proof: `ξ` defines,
  by `uliftYonedaEquiv`, a morphism `Ψ` of fppf sheaves from the (ulifted)
  functor of points of `P`, which is an fppf sheaf (`AlgebraicSpace.schemeSheaf`),
  to `F`. `Ψ` is bijective on affine tests, so it is an isomorphism by the Library
  lemma `LineHom.isIso_of_bijective_affine`. Mathlib's
  `Functor.RepresentableBy.equivUliftYonedaIso` turns it into a representation.

## Provenance

This is standard sheaf-theoretic glueing, internal to this development. It
supplies the Zariski-gluing step that the Markdown of
`NoetherianIntegralFibrePicardNoetherianTestsStatement` leaves as a separate
obligation. The step from affine tests to all tests is the Library lemma
`LineHom.isIso_of_bijective_affine`.
