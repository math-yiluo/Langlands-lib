# Positive multiplier of an actual rational polarized self-isogeny

`Langlands.AbelianScheme.Rosati.MultiplierPositivityStatement` is an unproved internal consequence of actual Rosati positivity. It asserts that, over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field, a rational self-isogeny `e` of an actually geometrically polarized abelian scheme has `0 < (q : ℚ)` whenever its actual rational dual pullback multiplies that polarization by `q : ℚˣ` and `id0 A ≠ 0`.

The equation is the existing `IsRationalDualPullbackMultiple`, specialized to the same object at source and target, the same actual constructed dual and pointwise additivity, and the same rationalized integral polarization. It says that the actual rational dual of `e.hom`, after the polarization, after `e.hom`, equals `q` times the polarization. It does not replace that equation with preservation of a supplied pairing. `e` is an isomorphism in the existing rational category; its maps are actual rational Hom elements. These are the objects and equation used by `PolarizedWeilLevel.Multiple`. `homToHom0 lam` and that consumer's `Rationalization.ofHom lam` are the same rationalization.

The provenance is derived, not a separate quoted theorem of Milne. The source ingredients are Milne, *Abelian Varieties*, January 2, 2022 author revision, §17 p.35 (the actual conjugation, rational inverse and algebra laws) and Theorem 17.3, pp.35–36 (positive-definite trace form), with geometric trace comparison from §12 p.23, Proposition 12.9. Source ID: `milne-abelian-varieties-1986-author-2022`. These original pages and the complete registered errata snapshot were read; the PDF incorporates all listed corrections.

The intended consequence follows by composing the pullback equation with the rational inverse polarization, giving `f† * f = q * 1` for `f = e.hom`. Cyclicity of the actual geometric trace identifies the trace of its left side with `Tr(f * f†)`. Since `f` is invertible and the identity is nonzero, `f` is nonzero. Positivity gives `Tr(f * f†) > 0` and `Tr(1) > 0`, so `q * Tr(1) > 0` forces `q > 0`. This explains the mathematical implication to be proved; this statement file does not implement that proof. No positivity, trace-comparison, finite-dimensionality or rational-inverse premise is added to the proposition.

The nonzero-identity premise is necessary. On the zero abelian variety the rational category has zero identity and the scalar equation holds for every rational unit, including negative ones. For positive dimension the geometric Tate rank gives a nonzero identity, via trace `2g`. For the existing Harris–Taylor action, the proved `id0_ne_zero_of_faithful` helper applies directly to `actionObj.action.toRingHom` and `faithful`, since the acting algebra is nontrivial. Thus this premise is derived from the consumer's existing faithful action; it does not impose a new assumption on that consumer. Its statement and import closure are left unchanged.
