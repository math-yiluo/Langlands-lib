# The Mumford Rosati trace formula in mixed Euler coefficients

`Langlands.AbelianScheme.Rosati.MumfordMixedEulerTraceStatement` is a geometric comparison proof target over every algebraically closed characteristic-zero field `k : Type u`. It retains the existing Rosati construction prerequisites `P`, actual abelian scheme `A`, dual witnesses `W`, additivity witness `hadd`, homomorphism `lam` and every instance asserting that the rationalization of `lam` is invertible. It assumes a fixed positive relative dimension `g`.

The ample Mumford presentation is explicit. The statement quantifies an actual line bundle `L` on `A`, the base line bundle `B` with its evaluation isomorphism, the inverse bundle `M` with its evaluation isomorphism for the normalized family, and requires `L.IsAmple`. It requires equality of the actual underlying map of `lam` with `mumfordSchemeMapOfLineBundle P.preservation A L B evaluationB M evaluation P.square W.hasDual`. Thus this statement concerns a direct ample Mumford presentation over `k`. It does not assert that the relative predicate `W.IsPolarization P lam` supplies such a presentation over `k`.

For every actual integral endomorphism `f`, the conclusion is

`traceForm(lam, f, f) * pureTopCoefficient(L, g) = 2 * mixedCoefficient(L, f*L, g)`.

Both occurrences of `f` in the form use the existing rationalization map `homToHom0`. The form is the existing fixed rational trace of `f * involution(f)`. The pure coefficient is the coefficient at `g` of `lineBundleEulerPolynomial A.X.hom L g`; the mixed coefficient is the coefficient at `g - 1` of the canonical actual coherent Euler difference polynomial for `f*L` and `L`. The pullback is the actual `LineBundle.pullback (hom f)` functor. The multiplied form of the equality avoids dividing by the pure coefficient. Its strict positivity for ample `L` is available separately from `lineBundleEulerPolynomial_topCoeff_pos`.

This is a derived Euler-polynomial reformulation of Milne, *Abelian Varieties*, author revision January 2, 2022, Theorem 17.3, printed and one-based PDF pp.35–36, together with Theorem 13.3(b), p.25 (registered source `milne-abelian-varieties-1986-author-2022`). If `L = O(D)`, Riemann–Roch gives the pure coefficient `D^g/g!` and the mixed coefficient `(D^(g-1) · f*D)/(g-1)!`. Substituting these in Milne's formula `Tr(f f†) = 2g (D^(g-1) · f*D)/D^g` gives the displayed factor `2`. The geometric comparison must identify Milne's trace with the Library's fixed rational lift of actual prime Tate trace, as in Proposition 12.9, p.23. No trace-comparison, finite-dimensionality or positivity premise is supplied by this statement. The registered errata snapshot says its corrections are incorporated in the cited author revision.

This file defines the proposition and proves nothing. In addition to proving this formula, a full proof of the existing `Rosati.PositivityStatement` must obtain a Mumford presentation after the extensions allowed by its relative polarization predicate, justify the required transport, establish mixed coefficient positivity for a nonzero endomorphism and cover dimension zero. None of those obligations is removed here.
