# Line-bundle sections cutting out the prime divisor of a prime germ (statement)

This module states the proposition
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.PrimeGermDivisorSectionStatement`. It supplies a
statement target, not a proof; the proof is the theorem
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.primeGermDivisorSection` in the sibling module
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.PrimeGermDivisorSectionProof`.

## Statement

For every universe `u` and every scheme `X : Scheme.{u}` that is integral (`IsIntegral X`)
and locally Noetherian (`IsLocallyNoetherian X`) and all of whose stalks
`X.presheaf.stalk x` are unique factorization monoids, and for every point `e` of `X` and
every prime element `π` of the stalk at `e`, there are a line bundle `L : LineBundle X` and
a global section `s : GlobalSections L.obj` such that:

1. `e ∉ trivializationOpen L.obj s`: the point `e` is not in the generation open of `s`
   (the union of the opens on which `s` trivializes `L`);
2. the generation open `trivializationOpen L.obj s` is nonempty;
3. for every open `U ∋ e` and every function `f : Γ(X, U)` whose germ at `e` is divisible
   by `π`, the basic open `X.basicOpen f` is contained in the generation open of `s`;
4. there are an open `V ∋ e` and a function `a : Γ(X, V)` whose germ at `e` is `Associated`
   to `π` (equal to `π` up to a unit of the stalk), such that for every scheme `T` and every
   morphism `g : T ⟶ X` with `⊤ ≤ g ⁻¹ᵁ V` (that is, `g` factors through `V`),
   `VanishesAlong L g s ↔ g.appLE V ⊤ hg a = 0`: the section `s` pulls back to zero along
   `g` exactly when the function `a` pulls back to zero on `T`.

No separatedness, finite type, base field, smoothness or dimension assumption is made. The
published `LineBundle`, `GlobalSections`, `trivializationOpen` and `VanishesAlong` are used
unchanged.

## Source and correspondence

The statement is the section form of the invertible sheaf of a prime divisor and its
canonical section, as in the Stacks Project (registered source
`stacks-divisors-locally-factorial-2026-10-31`, HTML snapshot of 2026-10-31):

* Lemma 31.16.7 (tag 0AGA): if `X` is locally Noetherian and `D ⊂ X` is an integral closed
  subscheme of codimension 1 such that the local ring `𝒪_{X,x}` is a UFD at every point
  `x ∈ D`, then `D` is an effective Cartier divisor. The reason is that the prime ideal of
  the generic point of `D` in `𝒪_{X,x}` has height 1, hence is principal in the UFD
  `𝒪_{X,x}`.
* Definition 31.15.1 (tag 01WX): for an effective Cartier divisor `D` with ideal sheaf
  `𝓘_D`, the invertible sheaf `𝒪_X(D)` is the dual of `𝓘_D`, with canonical section `1_D`.
* The proof of Lemma 31.28.7 (tag 0BE9) notes that the canonical section `1_D` vanishes
  exactly along `D`.

Correspondence. The prime element `π` generates a prime ideal `(π)` of `𝒪_{X,e}` of
height one; it corresponds to a point `ζ` of `X` specializing to `e`, and `D` is the closure
of `ζ`, a prime divisor through `e`. The line bundle `L` and the section `s` play the roles
of `𝒪_X(D)` and `1_D`. In the intended construction the generation open of `s` is the
complement of `D`; the Lean records only the consequences listed above: `e ∈ D` (clause 1),
`D ≠ X` (clause 2), and `D ⊆ V(f)` whenever `π` divides the germ `f_e` (clause 3), together
with a local equation `a` of `D` near `e` whose germ at `e` is associated to `π`
(clause 4). This is a derived formulation, not a literal transcription of the Stacks
statements. No divisor, Weil or Cartier divisor group, or comparison with them is defined or
proved.

This is the step of Milne's proof of Theorem 7.1 (J. S. Milne, *Abelian Varieties*,
corrected author version of January 2, 2022, p. 11; registered source
`milne-abelian-varieties-1986-author-2022`) that passes from prime divisors `Zᵢ` through `0`
to their line bundles `L(Zᵢ)`.

## The vanishing clause

On `V` the line bundle `L` has a frame in which `s` has coefficient `a` (in the intended
construction, `𝒪_X(D)|_V` is generated by `1/a` and `1_D = a · (1/a)`). Hence `s` vanishes
along a morphism into `V` exactly when `a` pulls back to zero. In particular this applies to
rational points `Spec K ⟶ X` and to `K[ε]`-points (tangent vectors) whose image lies in `V`.

## Why the hypotheses are needed

* Integrality gives the generic point and the point `ζ`, makes germs of nonzero local
  equations nonzero, and lets local equations whose germs agree up to units glue.
* Local Noetherianity makes the ideal of `D` coherent and lets a principal generator of the
  stalk ideal at a point spread to a generator on a neighbourhood; it is also needed for the
  height-one argument.
* Factoriality is required at every point of `D`, not only at `e`, so that `D` is Cartier
  everywhere and `𝒪_X(D)` is a line bundle on all of `X`.

If `X` is zero-dimensional, its stalks are fields, which have no prime elements, so the
statement is vacuous.

## Use

The statement is used in the proof of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.AlgebraicallyClosedSectionSeedExistenceStatement` (proof task
ff2d671a-f569-4f74-bab5-c409b9407294), where the published theorem
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.smoothAlgClosedFactorialStalks` supplies factorial stalks for
schemes smooth over an algebraically closed field: a rational point `x ≠ e` is separated by
a prime factor of the germ at `e` of a function vanishing at `e` but not at `x`, and a
nonzero tangent vector at `e` by a prime factor not killed by it.

The Lean text is, unchanged, the required statement text of statement task
ccde8b6e-eab2-4800-89b1-cb3b09bfdaee, which was published from the seed-existence proof task
but never received an executor; it is therefore submitted here together with its proof.
