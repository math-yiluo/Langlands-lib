# Nonzero section cokernels for integral ample geometry

The monomorphism lemma proves injectivity on sections over each open
`U`. When `U` is nonempty, irreducibility makes its intersection with the
section's nonempty generation open nonempty. The section morphism is
bijective on that intersection. Restriction of structure-sheaf
functions to a nonempty subopen of an integral scheme is injective,
which gives the required injectivity on `U`. On the empty open the
structure-sheaf section ring is a subsingleton.

A zero cokernel would make this monomorphism an epimorphism, hence an
isomorphism in the abelian sheaf category. This is equivalent to
generation on the whole scheme. The converse follows from the
vanishing of the cokernel of an isomorphism.

Restriction to the generation open preserves the cokernel and makes
the section morphism an isomorphism, so the restricted cokernel
vanishes. The canonical restriction/stalk isomorphism then proves
vanishing of each stalk in that open. This gives the support
containment.

For a line bundle with a nonempty generation open on an integral
scheme, the stronger support equality follows as well. The exact
abelian stalk functor preserves the monomorphism and its cokernel. A
zero cokernel stalk makes the stalk section map both monic and epic,
hence an isomorphism. The published stalk-frame openness theorem for
line bundles then places the point in the generation open. Thus its
complement is precisely the actual support.

The last theorem combines these facts with the nonempty-complement
assertion of `EulerPositivityPreparationStatement`. The point outside
the affine generation open excludes a zero cokernel. The resulting
short complex is the standard cokernel sequence, which is exact, with
the proved monomorphism and the canonical epimorphism to the cokernel.
