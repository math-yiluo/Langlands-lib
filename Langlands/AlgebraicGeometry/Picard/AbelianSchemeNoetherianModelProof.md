# Proof: noetherian models from abelian-scheme and line-bundle descent

`Langlands.AbelianScheme.abelianSchemeNoetherianModelReduction` proves
`Langlands.AbelianScheme.AbelianSchemeNoetherianModelReductionStatement.{u}`:

```lean
Langlands.AbelianScheme.SubalgebraDescentStatement.{u} →
  LineBundleSubalgebraDescentStatement.{u} →
    LineBundleIsoSubalgebraDescentStatement.{u} →
      AbelianSchemeNoetherianModelStatement.{u}
```

See `AbelianSchemeNoetherianModelReductionStatement.md` for the four propositions. The work is
done by `Langlands.AbelianScheme.NoetherianModelProof.noetherianModel_of`, which takes the
three hypotheses as arguments. The module sets
`backward.isDefEq.respectTransparency false` (and its `.types` variant), as
`Langlands/AlgebraicGeometry/Picard/FamilyBaseChange.lean` does. The underlying scheme of a base
change `(AbelianScheme.baseChange b).obj B` is the chosen pullback `pullback B.X.hom b` only up
to unfolding, and rewriting needs that unfolding.

## Stages

Fix `R : CommRingCat.{u}` and `A : AbelianScheme (Spec R)`. A `ModelStage A` consists of:
- a subalgebra `T : Subalgebra ℤ R` with `T.FG`;
- an abelian scheme `B` over `Spec (CommRingCat.of ↥T)`;
- a morphism `a : A.X.left ⟶ B.X.left` with
  `IsPullback a A.X.hom B.X.hom (Spec.map (CommRingCat.ofHom (algebraMap ↥T R)))`;
- the unit compatibility `unitSection A ≫ a = Spec.map (…) ≫ unitSection B`.

These are the data that `AbelianSchemeNoetherianModelStatement` asks for, apart from the line
bundles. The noetherian ring at the end is `CommRingCat.of ↥T`: by `isNoetherianRing_of_fg`, a
finitely generated `ℤ`-algebra is noetherian (`Algebra.FiniteType.isNoetherianRing`).

`ModelStage.step S T' hT'` enlarges a stage along a finitely generated `↥S.T`-subalgebra `T'` of
`R`. The new ring is `T'.restrictScalars ℤ`, which is finitely generated by
`fg_restrictScalars` (transitivity of finite type). The new abelian scheme is the base change of
`S.B` along `Spec T' ⟶ Spec T`. The new map `a'` is `ModelStage.stepMap`, the lift of `S.a` and
`A.X.hom ≫ Spec.map (T' → R)` into the chosen pullback. Its compatibility uses `specMap_comp`:
`Spec.map (T → R)` equals `Spec.map (T' → R) ≫ Spec.map (T → T')`. The pullback square for `a'`
comes from `IsPullback.of_right`, since the outer square is the old one and the right square is
the chosen pullback. The unit compatibility is checked on both pullback projections. On the first
it uses `unitSection_baseChange_fst` and the old compatibility. On the second it uses
`unitSection_comp` twice.

`stage₀` builds the first stage from the first hypothesis. That hypothesis gives `R₀`, an abelian
scheme `A₀` and an isomorphism `e : A ≅ (AbelianScheme.baseChange (Spec.map (R₀ → R))).obj A₀`.
The map is `a := AbelianScheme.hom e.hom ≫ pullback.fst _ _`. Its pullback square pastes the
square of the isomorphism `AbelianScheme.hom e.hom` over `𝟙`, built with
`IsPullback.of_horiz_isIso` and `Over.w`, with the chosen pullback square. The unit compatibility
follows from `unitSection_hom` (homomorphisms preserve the unit) and
`unitSection_baseChange_fst`.

## Line bundles on stages

For a stage `S`, a module `M` on `A.X.left` and a module `N` on `S.B.X.left`, two predicates are
used:
- `Carried S M N` says that `N` is `IsLocallyFreeRankOne` and
  `(Scheme.Modules.pullback S.a).obj N ≅ M`;
- `Trivialized S N` says that `structureModule ≅ (Scheme.Modules.pullback (unitSection S.B)).obj N`.

Both are preserved by `step` when `N` is replaced by its pullback along the projection
`ModelStage.proj` (`Carried.step`, `Trivialized.step`). The isomorphisms are composites of the
module-pullback composition isomorphisms (`pullbackCompEq`, built from Mathlib
`Scheme.Modules.pullbackComp` and `pullbackCongr`), using `stepMap_fst` and
`unitSection_baseChange_fst`. The structure sheaf is moved along base maps by
`Langlands.Scheme.Modules.pullbackUnitIso`.

`exists_step_carried` applies the line-bundle descent hypothesis to the pullback square of a
stage. Its `X₀ ⟶ Spec T` is `S.B.X.hom`, which is quasi-compact and quasi-separated because `S.B`
is proper. The hypothesis gives `T'` and a rank-one `N` on the chosen pullback, which is the
underlying scheme of `(S.step T' hT').B`. Evaluating its comparison clause at `c := S.stepMap T'`
gives `Carried (S.step T' hT') M N`.

`exists_step_trivialized` starts from `Carried S M.obj.carrier N`, where `M` is rigidified along
`unitSection A`. It applies the isomorphism-descent hypothesis with `X₀ := Spec T`,
`f₀ := 𝟙`, the modules `ε^*N` (`ε` the unit section of `S.B`) and `structureModule`, and the
trivial square `IsPullback b (𝟙 _) (𝟙 _) b` for `b := Spec.map (T → R)`. The required
isomorphism `b^*ε^*N ≅ b^*𝒪` is the chain
`b^*ε^*N ≅ (b ≫ ε)^*N = (unitSection A ≫ S.a)^*N ≅ (unitSection A)^*(S.a^*N) ≅ (unitSection A)^*M ≅ 𝒪 ≅ b^*𝒪`.
It uses the unit compatibility of the stage, the carried isomorphism and the rigidification of
`M`. The hypothesis then gives `T'`. It is applied to the trivial square for
`g := Spec.map (T → T')` and yields `g^*ε^*N ≅ g^*𝒪`. With `pullbackUnitIso` and
`unitSection_baseChange_fst`, this becomes `Trivialized (S.step T' hT') (proj^*N)`. `Carried.step`
gives the carried part.

## Assembly

`noetherianModel_of` runs the stages in order:
1. `S₀ := stage₀` from the abelian-scheme descent;
2. `exists_step_carried` for `M₁` (stage `S₁`), then for `M₂` (stage `S₂`), carrying the bundle
   of `M₁` to `S₂`;
3. `exists_step_trivialized` for the bundle of `M₁` (stage `S₃`), carrying the bundle of `M₂`;
4. `exists_step_trivialized` for the bundle of `M₂` (stage `S₄`), carrying both the bundle of
   `M₁` and its trivialization to `S₄`.

At `S₄` each bundle `Nᵢ` is rank one, with `S₄.a^*Nᵢ ≅ Mᵢ` and a trivialization `αᵢ`. The
rigidified bundles are `RigidifiedLineBundle.ofLineBundle ⟨Nᵢ, _⟩ αᵢ`. The required isomorphisms
of underlying line bundles are `ObjectProperty.isoMk _` of the carried isomorphisms. The output
is `R₀ := CommRingCat.of ↥S₄.T` with `φ := CommRingCat.ofHom (algebraMap ↥S₄.T R)`, together with
`S₄.B`, `S₄.a`, `S₄.isPullback` and `S₄.unit`.
