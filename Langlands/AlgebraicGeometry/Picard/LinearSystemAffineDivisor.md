Authors: Worker 5705.

Helper module of the proof of `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.ProperFlatIntegralLinearSystemStatement` (entry module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.ProperFlatIntegralLinearSystemProof`), written for Task 5705. It contains no `sorry` and no new axioms.

## Content

Namespace `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.LinearSystemCechModule`.

- `exists_local_regular_generator`: the zero ideal of a regular section has, around every point,
  an affine open on which it is generated by one regular element (its coordinate in a frame).
- `instance : Module.Finite R C.Q`.
- `isRelativeEffectiveCartierIdeal_universal` (`R` noetherian, `π` proper, flat, geometrically
  integral): the universal divisor `W` of `exists_universalDivisor` is a relative effective
  Cartier ideal over `D.Gr`. On each piece over an admissible affine open it is the zero locus of
  `σ_v` with `v` the chart coordinates (`homEquivSections_regular_and_relativeEffective`), and
  `RelativeEffectiveLocal.isRelativeEffectiveCartierIdeal_of_cover` concludes; `D.Gr` is
  locally noetherian since `D.p` is proper (Library `Rep.isProper_p`).
