# The two-axis class presheaf is an fpqc sheaf

Author: Worker 384.

The module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.TwoAxisClassFpqcSheaf` is the
support module of the proofs of Murre's conditions (F1a) and (F1b) for the
two-axis class presheaf. Those proofs are in the modules
`TwoAxisClassZariskiSheafProof` and `TwoAxisClassFaithfullyFlatSheafProof`.
Its main result is

```lean
theorem Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.TwoAxisRigidifiedLineBundle.classPresheaf_isSheaf_fpqc
    {S : Scheme.{u}} (A : Langlands.AbelianScheme S) :
    Presieve.IsSheaf (Scheme.fpqcTopology.over S) (classPresheaf A)
```

For every abelian scheme `A` over every scheme `S`, the presheaf
`TwoAxisRigidifiedLineBundle.classPresheaf A` on `Over S` is a sheaf for the
fpqc topology of Mathlib, restricted to `Over S`. Mathlib's
`Scheme.fpqcTopology` is generated by the precoverage
`fpqcPrecoverage = propQCPrecoverage @Flat`: jointly surjective families of
flat morphisms that are quasi-compact covers.
Its value at a test object `T` is the set of isomorphism classes of line bundles
on the self-product of the base change `abelian A T`, with compatible
trivializations along the two unit axes. No noetherian hypothesis is used.

The module also proves two general lemmas, for every presheaf
`F : (Over S)ᵒᵖ ⥤ Type _` that is a sheaf for `Scheme.fpqcTopology.over S`:

- `UnramifiedFunctor.zariskiSheaf_of_isSheaf_fpqc`: `F` satisfies Murre's
  condition `UnramifiedFunctor.ZariskiSheaf` (F1a).
- `UnramifiedFunctor.faithfullyFlatSheaf_of_isSheaf_fpqc`: `F` satisfies Murre's
  condition `UnramifiedFunctor.FaithfullyFlatSheaf` (F1b).

## Strategy

The class presheaf `F = classPresheaf A` is embedded into the published
one-section rigidified class presheaf

```
G = RigidifiedLineBundle.classPresheaf pullbackCoherence (fP A) (eP A) (eP_comp A)
```

of the self-product `P = A ×_S A` with its unit section `(e, e)`. The image is
the set of classes whose pullbacks along the two axes `(id, e)` and `(e, id)`
are trivial in the published rigidified class presheaf

```
H = RigidifiedLineBundle.classPresheaf pullbackCoherence A.X.hom (unitSection A) (unitSection_comp A)
```

of `A`. Both `G` and `H` are fpqc sheaves by the published descent results for
rigidified classes. A presheaf with such an embedding into a sheaf `G`, cut out
by two maps into a separated presheaf `H`, is again a sheaf. All comparisons are
made between isomorphism classes only. No isomorphism is chosen coherently.

## Contents

### Site reduction for the fpqc topology

`OverFppfSheaf.isSheaf_fpqcTopology_over` is the fpqc version of the published
site reduction `OverFppfSheaf.isSheaf_fppfTopology_over`, with the same
hypotheses. A presheaf `G` of types on `Over S` is an fpqc sheaf if

- it is a sheaf for `Scheme.zariskiTopology.over S`, and
- for every flat surjective `Spec.map φ : Spec R' ⟶ Spec R` and every
  `s : Spec R ⟶ S`, it satisfies the sheaf condition for the singleton presieve
  of `Over.homMk (Spec.map φ) : Over.mk (Spec.map φ ≫ s) ⟶ Over.mk s`.

The proof reuses the steps of the published fppf proof:

- the published transfer `isSheaf_over_of_isSheaf_ext` to the extension
  `ext G` on all schemes;
- Mathlib's characterization `isSheaf_type_propQCTopology_iff`, which applies
  because `Scheme.fpqcTopology_eq_propQCTopology`;
- `isSheaf_ext_of_isSheaf_over` for the Zariski part;
- `isSheafFor_singleton_ext` for the affine part, where `yoneda.obj S` satisfies
  the singleton condition because `Spec.map φ` is a regular epimorphism
  (`isRegularEpi_of_flat_of_surjective_of_isAffine`).

The published fppf proof gets fppf from the same fpqc argument, so only the
final comparison of topologies is dropped.

### One-section rigidified classes are fpqc sheaves

`RigidifiedLineBundle.classPresheaf_isSheaf_fpqc f ε section_eq hO` says this.
Let `f : X ⟶ S` have a section `ε`. Suppose every base change
`pullback.fst T.hom f` induces isomorphisms on the sections of the structure
sheaf over every open set (hypothesis `hO`). Then the rigidified class presheaf
`classPresheaf pullbackCoherence f ε section_eq` is an fpqc sheaf on `Over S`.
The proof applies the site reduction above. It takes the Zariski condition from
`classPresheaf_isSheaf_zariski_of_unique` and the affine condition from
`classPresheaf_isSheafFor_affine`, using the published descent theorems
`LineBundleIsoDescent.lineBundleIsoDescent` and `lineBundleDescentEffective`.
Uniqueness of relative isomorphisms comes from `relative_iso_subsingleton`.

### Transfer lemma

`TwoAxisClassFpqc.isSheafFor_of_injective` is a statement about presheaves of
types `F G H` on any category and a presieve `P` on an object `X`. Suppose we
are given:

- componentwise maps `Φ T : F(T) → G(T)` that are natural and injective;
- natural maps `Ψ₁ Ψ₂ : G → H`;
- a family `z T ∈ H(T)` compatible with restriction;
- for every `x`, `Ψᵢ (Φ x) = z`;
- every `g` with `Ψ₁ g = z` and `Ψ₂ g = z` lies in the image of `Φ`.

If `G` is a sheaf for `P` and `H` is separated for `P`, then `F` is a sheaf for
`P`. Proof: the images of a compatible family of `F` amalgamate in `G`. The
amalgamation satisfies `Ψᵢ g = z` because `H` is separated, so it comes from
`F`. Uniqueness follows from injectivity and uniqueness in `G`.

### Classes of underlying line bundles

All comparisons pass through the published group of isomorphism classes of line
bundles, `LineBundle.IsoClasses pullbackTensorComparisonIsIso Z`, with the
pullback homomorphisms `LineBundle.pullbackHom` (written `pb` in the source).

- `carrierClass f ε hε T` sends a one-section rigidified class to the class of
  its underlying line bundle on `(relativeSpace f).obj T`. It is injective
  (`carrierClass_injective`, by the published
  `RigidifiedLineBundle.nonempty_iso_of_forget_iso`) and compatible with
  restriction (`carrierClass_map`). The trivial rigidified bundle has class `1`
  (`carrierClass_trivial`). A class `m` is in the image exactly when its
  pullback along the relative section is `1` (`exists_carrierClass_eq`).
- `twoAxisCarrierClass B` does the same for two-axis-rigidified classes on the
  self-product of an abelian scheme `B`. It is injective
  (`twoAxisCarrierClass_injective`, by the published
  `TwoAxisRigidifiedLineBundle.nonempty_iso_of_carrierIso`). Pullbacks along
  `mumfordSection B` and `twoAxisSecond B` are `1`
  (`twoAxisCarrierClass_first`, `twoAxisCarrierClass_second`). Conversely, every
  class with both axis pullbacks `1` is in the image
  (`exists_twoAxisCarrierClass_eq`, by the published
  `nonempty_twoAxisRigidification`).

### The self-product as a family over `S`

- `fP A : (mumfordProduct A).left ⟶ S` is the structure morphism of `A ×_S A`.
  `eP A = unitSection A ≫ mumfordSection A` is its unit section, and
  `eP_comp A : eP A ≫ fP A = 𝟙 S`.
- `fP A` is smooth, proper and geometrically connected, as the composite of the
  first projection with `A.X.hom`.
- So `relativeProjection_fP_app_isIso A` holds, by the published theorem
  `smoothProperConnectedGlobalFunctions`. It is the hypothesis `hO` for
  `(fP A, eP A)`. The same argument for `A.X.hom` gives
  `relativeProjection_app_isIso'`.

### Scheme comparisons

- `productIso A T` is an isomorphism from the self-product of the base change
  `abelian A T` to `(relativeSpace (fP A)).obj T = T ×_S (A ×_S A)`. It is the
  published `mumfordProductBaseChangeIso` composed with `pullbackSymmetry`.
- Its interaction with the projections, the relative unit section
  (`section_productIso`, `section_productIso_inv`) and the restriction maps
  `productMap A h` (`productMap_productIso`, `productIso_inv_productMap`) is
  checked componentwise.
- For a section `s` of the first projection of `A ×_S A` over `A`
  (`hs : s ≫ fP A = A.X.hom`), `axis A s hs T` is the induced morphism
  `T ×_S A ⟶ T ×_S (A ×_S A)`. It is natural in `T` (`axis_naturality`).
  When `unitSection A ≫ s = eP A`, it carries relative sections to relative
  sections (`section_axis`).
- For the two axes `s = mumfordSection A` and `s = twoAxisSecond A`,
  `mumfordSection_productIso` and `twoAxisSecond_productIso` identify the axis
  `abelian A T ⟶` (self-product of `abelian A T`), followed by `productIso`,
  with `pullbackSymmetry` followed by `axis`. `axis_productIso_inv` rewrites
  this through the inverse.

### The comparison maps

- `axisPullback A s hs hs' T : G(T) → H(T)` is pullback of rigidified classes
  along `axis A s hs T`. It acts on underlying classes by `pb (axis A s hs T)`
  (`carrierClass_axisPullback`) and is natural (`axisPullback_map`).
- `unitClass A T ∈ H(T)` is the class of the trivial rigidified bundle. It is
  stable under restriction (`unitClass_map`).
- `toProduct A T : F(T) → G(T)` sends a two-axis class `c` to the unique
  one-section class whose underlying class is
  `pb (productIso A T).inv (twoAxisCarrierClass (abelian A T) c)`. This class
  exists by `exists_carrierClass_eq`: its pullback along the unit section is the
  pullback of `twoAxisCarrierClass c` along `unitSection ≫ mumfordSection`,
  which is `1` (`toProduct_aux`). The class is chosen with `Classical.choose`,
  and `carrierClass_toProduct` records its defining property. It is natural
  (`toProduct_map`, using `twoAxisCarrierClass_map` and
  `productIso_inv_productMap`) and injective (`toProduct_injective`).
- `axisPullback_toProduct` gives `Ψᵢ (toProduct c) = unitClass`.
  `axisPullback_eq_unitClass` and `exists_toProduct_eq` prove the image
  condition. If `g ∈ G(T)` has both axis pullbacks trivial, then
  `pb (productIso A T).hom (carrierClass g)` has both two-axis pullbacks `1`. So
  it is the class of a two-axis class `c`, and then `toProduct c = g`.

### Main theorem

`TwoAxisRigidifiedLineBundle.classPresheaf_isSheaf_fpqc A` applies the transfer
lemma to every covering sieve of `Scheme.fpqcTopology.over S`, with:

- `Φ = toProduct A`;
- `Ψ₁ = axisPullback` along `mumfordSection A`, and `Ψ₂ = axisPullback` along
  `twoAxisSecond A`;
- `z = unitClass A`;
- `G` and `H` from `RigidifiedLineBundle.classPresheaf_isSheaf_fpqc`, for
  `(fP A, eP A)` and for `(A.X.hom, unitSection A)`.

### Murre's conditions (F1a) and (F1b) from the fpqc sheaf property

- `zariskiSheaf_of_isSheaf_fpqc`: for an open cover `𝒰` of `T.left`, the
  presieve in `ZariskiSheaf` generates a sieve. Under `Sieve.overEquiv` that
  sieve becomes `Sieve.ofArrows 𝒰.X 𝒰.f`. This sieve lies in the Zariski
  topology (`Scheme.Cover.mem_grothendieckTopology`) and hence in the fpqc
  topology (`Scheme.zariskiTopology_le_fpqcTopology`). Then
  `Presieve.isSheafFor_iff_generate` applies.
- `faithfullyFlatSheaf_of_isSheaf_fpqc`: take `g : T' ⟶ T` with `g.left` flat,
  surjective and quasi-compact. The singleton sieve on `g` corresponds under
  `Sieve.overEquiv` to the singleton sieve on `g.left`. That sieve is generated
  by a member of the fpqc precoverage
  (`Scheme.Hom.singleton_mem_fpqcPrecoverage`), so it lies in the fpqc
  topology (`Precoverage.generate_mem_toGrothendieck`).

## Provenance

This module is internal support for the proofs of
`NoetherianTwoAxisClassZariskiSheafStatement` and
`NoetherianTwoAxisClassFaithfullyFlatSheafStatement`. Murre's conditions and
their source are documented in `TwoAxisClassMurreConditionsStatement.md` and
`MurreCriterionStatement.md`. The argument follows a standard route:

- the two-axis classes form a subfunctor of rigidified classes on `A ×_S A`,
  cut out by triviality along the two axes;
- rigidified classes satisfy fpqc descent because line bundles and their
  isomorphisms descend along fpqc coverings and rigidified objects have no
  nontrivial automorphisms.

The second point is the published descent development for rigidified classes.
The comparisons and the transfer lemma are derived here.
