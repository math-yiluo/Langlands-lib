# The triviality locus over a Noetherian base is cut out by an immersion

Author: Worker 766.

This module is `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.TrivialLocusNoetherian`. It
imports `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.TrivialLocusOpen` (and through it
`TrivialLocusComparison`, `FieldBaseChangeSections` and `TrivialLocusStrata`)
and the Library module
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.ClassEqualizerReduction`
(`HasImmersionLocus`, `fieldPicardClassEqualizerImmersion_of_noetherian`).

## Purpose and provenance

This module completes the proof of the Library statement
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.FieldPicardClassEqualizerImmersionStatement`,
which is part of the diagonal branch of
`FieldPicardSheafAlgebraicSpaceStatement`. That statement says that, for a
proper scheme `X` over a field `k`, the locus where the fppf Picard classes of
two line bundles on `T ×_k X` agree is represented by an immersion into `T`.
`ClassEqualizerReduction` reduced it to Noetherian affine bases. This module
proves that case. No literature theorem is claimed. The proof is internal. It
stratifies the base by the column splits of a two-term complex computing
sections, and on each stratum it uses the openness argument of
`TrivialLocusOpen`.

## Conventions

As in `TrivialLocusStrata`. `X : Over (Spec k)` for a field `k`. `specOver k A`
is the spectrum of a `k`-algebra `A` over `Spec k`, and `specMap R A` is
`Spec.map (CommRingCat.ofHom (algebraMap R A))`.

## Declarations

These declarations are in the namespace `RelativePicard`.

- `HasImmersionLocus.of_eq_comp`: if `ψ ≫ φ' = φ` and the property holds for
  the pullback of `N` along `(relativeSpace f).map φ'`, it holds for the
  pullback along `(relativeSpace f).map φ`.
- `HasImmersionLocus.of_factor`: let `φ : V ⟶ T` be a morphism of `Over S`
  whose underlying morphism is an immersion. Suppose every member of the
  triviality locus of `N` factors through `φ.left`, and the property holds for
  the pullback of `N` to `V`. Then it holds for `N`. The immersion is the
  composite with `φ.left` (`CutsOut.comp`, `trivialLocus_pullback`).
- `HasImmersionLocus.of_iso`: the property depends only on the isomorphism
  class of the bundle.
- `stratumHom S : specOver k (Stratum S) ⟶ specOver k B` and
  `awayHom t : specOver k (Localization.Away t) ⟶ specOver k B` are the
  morphisms over `Spec k` given by `specMap`.
- `sectionsFree_stratum`: for `ε : SectionsEquivs X N G` and a column split
  `S` of `G.d`, the pullback of `N` to the stratum satisfies
  `SectionsFree X _ (Fintype.card S.α)`. For a `Stratum S`-algebra `A`, the
  scalar `S.t` is a unit in `A` and the Schur complement vanishes in `A`, so the
  kernel of `G.d ⊗ A` is free on the remaining columns
  (`ColumnSplit.kerBaseChangeEquivFun`). The sections of the base change of the
  pulled-back bundle are identified with those for `N` over `B` by
  `sectionsEquivOfIsPullback`: two pullback squares of the same cospan, pasted
  with `relativeSpace_isPullback`.
- `hasImmersionLocus_of_isNoetherianRing`: let `X.hom` be proper, `B` a
  Noetherian `k`-algebra and `N` a line bundle on `Spec B ×_k X`. Then
  `HasImmersionLocus X.hom (specOver k B) N` holds. The proof runs as follows.
  1. Take `a` with `SectionsRank X a` and the FLT two-term complex `G` of `N`
     over `B`.
  2. For a column split `S` with `a` remaining columns, the property holds over
     `Localization.Away S.t`. There every member of the triviality locus factors
     through the closed immersion of the stratum
     (`factorSieve_stratum_of_mem_trivialLocus`), and on the stratum the
     property holds by `sectionsFree_stratum` and
     `hasImmersionLocus_of_sectionsFree`. `HasImmersionLocus.of_factor` combines
     the two.
  3. Let `W` be the union of the open images of `Spec (Localization.Away S.t)`
     over these splits. Every member of the triviality locus factors through
     `W` (`exists_columnSplit_of_mem_trivialLocus` and Mathlib's
     `PrimeSpectrum.localization_away_comap_range`).
  4. Over `W` the property holds by `HasImmersionLocus.of_cover`. The cover is
     the sieve generated by the open immersions of the localizations into `W`,
     and on its generators the property comes from step 2.
  5. `HasImmersionLocus.of_factor` along the open immersion `W.ι` concludes.
- `fieldPicardClassEqualizerImmersion :
  FieldPicardClassEqualizerImmersionStatement.{u}`. This is the Library's
  `fieldPicardClassEqualizerImmersion_of_noetherian` applied to
  `hasImmersionLocus_of_isNoetherianRing`.
