# Geometric Picard zero under ground base change: proof

This module proves `Langlands.AbelianScheme.PicardZeroFamilyBaseChangeStatement.{u}` by comparing the actual group schemes and line bundles over the same parameter scheme. It is internal derived infrastructure for the published statement; it asserts no additional literature identification of the geometric predicate.

The proof first transports translation invariance through a group-scheme isomorphism in both directions. Preservation uses `isTranslationInvariant_pullback`. Reflection pulls back along the inverse and uses pullback composition and the identity pullback isomorphism to recover the original line bundle. Applying this argument to the base change of the group isomorphism at every algebraically closed test field proves transport of `IsGeometricallyPicardZero`. Naturality of the fibre projections identifies the two orders of restricting and pulling back the line bundle.

`familyBaseChangeGroupIso` is the inverse of the published group-scheme composition comparison for `b` and `T.hom`. Its underlying map commutes with the relative-space symmetries and `familyBaseChangeSpaceMap`: the parameter projection follows from its being a morphism over `T.left`, and the projection to `A` follows from `baseChangeCompIso_inv_fst`. The line-bundle pullback composition isomorphisms then give `familyBaseChangeLineBundleIso`, comparing the pullback of `familyLineBundle A L` with the underlying bundle of the actual rigidified `familyBaseChange` functor.

The theorem `picardZeroFamilyBaseChange` composes the geometric transport equivalence with this line-bundle isomorphism. Both implications hold for arbitrary schemes in one universe with exactly the hypotheses of the published statement. The rigidification is retained by the existing functor, while the geometric predicate depends on the underlying line bundle. No dual-scheme existence, universal classification, parameter compatibility, or field-existence theorem is used or proved here.
