# Murre's condition (F7) for the two-axis class presheaf

Worker 387 wrote this module for Task 6297. It proves the published proposition
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.NoetherianTwoAxisClassOneDimensionalLiftingStatement` by
the theorem

```
theorem noetherianTwoAxisClassOneDimensionalLifting :
    NoetherianTwoAxisClassOneDimensionalLiftingStatement.{u}
```

in namespace `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard`, at arbitrary universe `u`.

## What is proved

Let `A` be an abelian scheme over a scheme `S`. The proof shows that the published presheaf
`TwoAxisRigidifiedLineBundle.classPresheaf A` satisfies `UnramifiedFunctor.OneDimensionalLifting`,
Murre's condition (F7). Its value at a test object `T : Over S` is the set of isomorphism
classes of line bundles on the self-product `A_T ×_T A_T`, with compatible trivializations
along both unit axes. The statement:

- Let `Λ` be a complete local noetherian ring of Krull dimension one, with a single associated
  prime, and let `t` be the generic point of `Spec Λ`.
- Let `I` be a nilpotent ideal with `N · I = 0`, where `N` is the nilradical, and
  `f : Spec Λ ⟶ S`.
- Let `ξ'` be a class over `Spec (Λ/I)`. Suppose its restriction to `Spec (Λ_t/IΛ_t)` is the
  restriction of a class `η` over `Spec Λ_t` (`UnramifiedFunctor.LiftsTo`).
- Then `ξ'` is the restriction of a class over `Spec Λ`.

The general theorem is `TwoAxisRigidifiedLineBundle.classPresheaf_oneDimensionalLifting A`.
It holds over every base `S`. The required theorem specializes it to `S = Spec R` with `R`
noetherian; the noetherian hypothesis on `R` is not used.

Not all hypotheses of (F7) are used. Completeness and `dim Λ = 1` are never used. The
noetherian hypothesis on `Λ`, the single associated prime and the generic point give only
one fact: `Λ → Λ_t` is injective. From `N · I = 0` and nilpotency only `I² = 0` is used, and
`I ≠ ⊤` follows from nilpotency.

## Proof outline

Write `Λ' = Λ_t` and `IΛ'` for the extension of `I` to `Λ'`. The module
`GenericThickeningPullback` supplies the rings.

1. **First gluing.** Let `R₁ = Λ' ×_{Λ'/IΛ'} Λ/I` (`GenericThickening.fibre Λ' I`). This is
   a ring pullback, and `Λ' → Λ'/IΛ'` is surjective with square-zero kernel. The hypothesis
   says `η` and `ξ'` agree over `Λ'/IΛ'`. By `classes_squareGluing` (module
   `TwoAxisClassSquareGluing`) there is a class `z₁` over `R₁` whose restriction to `Λ/I`
   (along the second projection) is `ξ'`.
2. **The thickening.** Let `J ⊆ R₁` be the ideal generated by the image of `I`. Since
   `I² = 0`, it equals the image of `I` and has square zero. Since `Λ → Λ'` is injective,
   `Λ = R₁ ×_{R₁/J} Λ/I` is a ring pullback, and `R₁ → R₁/J` is surjective with square-zero
   kernel.
3. **Agreement over `R₁/J`.** There are two classes over `R₁/J`: the restriction of `z₁`,
   and the restriction of `ξ'` along `secMap : Λ/I → R₁/J`. The second projection induces a
   retraction `R₁/J → Λ/I` of `secMap`, and both classes restrict along it to `ξ'`. `R₁/J` is
   local, so the retraction is a surjection from a local ring onto a nontrivial ring. By
   Worker 386's nilpotent-injectivity result (F5), restriction along it is injective. Hence
   the two classes agree.
4. **Second gluing.** `classes_squareGluing` for the square of step 2 gives a class over `Λ`
   whose restriction to `Λ/I` is `ξ'`.

## Declarations

### Functoriality (section `Functoriality`)

- `classesPullback_comp` and `classesPullback_id`: class restriction is functorial. These
  are `map_comp_apply` and `map_id_apply` for the presheaf `classPresheaf A`.
- `classesPullback_injective_of_isIso`: restriction along an isomorphism of test objects is
  injective, because the presheaf sends it to a bijection.
- `algHomOver_comp` and `algHomOver_id`: `algHomOver f` sends composition of `Λ`-algebra maps
  to composition of test morphisms (in the opposite order) and the identity to the identity.
  Both are checked on the underlying morphisms with `Spec.map_comp` and `Spec.map_id`.
- Instance `algHomOver_isIso`: for an algebra isomorphism `e`, `algHomOver f e` is an
  isomorphism with inverse `algHomOver f e.symm`.

### Transfer of local injectivity (section `Transfer`)

`classesPullback_algHomOver_injective A f π hπ`: let `D` be a local `Λ`-algebra, `E` a
nontrivial `Λ`-algebra and `π : D →ₐ[Λ] E` surjective. Then
`classesPullback A (algHomOver f π)` is injective.

Proof. `K = ker π` is proper since `E` is nontrivial. The test morphism `algHomOver f π` is
written as a composite of three morphisms (`hfac`):

- `algHomOver f` of the isomorphism `D ⧸ K ≃ₐ[Λ] E` (`Ideal.quotientKerAlgEquivOfSurjective`);
- an isomorphism `w` of test objects, with identity underlying morphism, from
  `algebraOver f (D ⧸ K)` to `Over.mk (Spec (D ⧸ K) → Spec D → S)`;
- `compTestHom` of `Spec.map` of the quotient map `D → D ⧸ K`.

Restriction along the last morphism is injective. This is the F5 lemma
`classesPullback_injective_local A D K _ (algebraOver f D).hom` from
`TwoAxisClassNilpotentInjectiveProof`, which needs `D` local and `K ≠ ⊤`. Restriction along
the other two is injective by `classesPullback_injective_of_isIso`. `classesPullback_comp`
splits the restriction along the composite.

### The gluing argument (section `GenericLifting`)

`exists_lift_of_generic A Λ' hItop hII hinj f ξ' η hη` takes the following data:

- a local ring `Λ`, an ideal `I` with `I ≠ ⊤` and `I * I = ⊥`, and `f : Spec Λ ⟶ S`;
- a `Λ`-algebra `Λ'` such that `hinj : ∀ x ∈ I, algebraMap Λ Λ' x = 0 → x = 0`;
- classes `ξ'` over `algebraOver f (Λ ⧸ I)` and `η` over `algebraOver f Λ'`, with `hη`: the
  restriction of `η` along `Ideal.Quotient.mkₐ Λ IΛ'` equals the restriction of `ξ'` along
  `GenericThickening.quotMap Λ' I`.

It concludes that some class `ξ` over `Over.mk f` satisfies
`classesPullback A (algebraOverBase f (Λ ⧸ I)) ξ = ξ'`. Proof, following the outline:

1. `classes_squareGluing` with `p = fibreSnd`, `q = fibreFst`, `a = quotMap Λ' I`,
   `b = Ideal.Quotient.mkₐ Λ IΛ'`, the square `fibre_isPullback`, and
   `isNilpotent_ker_mkₐ_map`. It gives `z₁` over `fibre Λ' I` with restriction `ξ'` along
   `fibreSnd`.
2. `hagree`: the restrictions of `z₁` along `thickMk Λ' I` and of `ξ'` along `secMap Λ' I`
   agree. Apply `classesPullback_algHomOver_injective` to `retraction Λ' I`, using
   `retraction_surjective` and `isLocalRing_quotient_thick`. Then rewrite with
   `classesPullback_comp`, `algHomOver_comp`, `retraction_comp_mkₐ`,
   `retraction_comp_secMap`, `algHomOver_id` and `classesPullback_id`. Both sides become `ξ'`,
   by step 1.
3. `classes_squareGluing` with `p = Ideal.Quotient.mkₐ Λ I`,
   `q = Algebra.ofId Λ (fibre Λ' I)`, `a = secMap Λ' I`, `b = thickMk Λ' I`, the square
   `thick_isPullback Λ' hII hinj` and `isNilpotent_ker_mkₐ_thick`. It gives `z` over
   `algebraOver f Λ` with restriction `ξ'` along `algHomOver f (Ideal.Quotient.mkₐ Λ I)`.
4. Let `u : Over.mk f ⟶ algebraOver f Λ` have identity underlying morphism; this uses
   `algebraMap Λ Λ = id`. Then `algebraOverBase f (Λ ⧸ I) ≫ u =
   algHomOver f (Ideal.Quotient.mkₐ Λ I)` (`hu`), so `ξ = classesPullback A u z` works by
   `classesPullback_comp`.

`classPresheaf_oneDimensionalLifting A : UnramifiedFunctor.OneDimensionalLifting (classPresheaf A)`.
Proof. Unpack the hypotheses and the witness `η` of `LiftsTo`. `I ≠ ⊤` holds because `I` is
nilpotent and `⊤ ^ n = ⊤ ≠ ⊥`. Apply `exists_lift_of_generic` with
`Λ' = Localization.AtPrime t.asIdeal`. The inputs are `I * I = ⊥`, from
`GenericThickening.mul_self_eq_bot`, and injectivity of `Λ → Λ_t`, from
`GenericThickening.algebraMap_atPrime_injective` (single associated prime and generic `t`).
The hypothesis `hη` is the `LiftsTo` equation, since `restrictQuotient` is restriction along
`quotMap`.

`noetherianTwoAxisClassOneDimensionalLifting` (namespace `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard`)
applies `classPresheaf_oneDimensionalLifting` to each noetherian `R` and abelian scheme `A`
over `Spec R`.

## Source

Condition (F7) is Murre's, from J. P. Murre, *Representation of unramified functors.
Applications (according to unpublished results of A. Grothendieck)*, Séminaire Bourbaki exp.
294. It is stated on printed pp. 245–246 (PDF pp. 4–5), and the source is registered as
`murre-1965-bourbaki-294-unramified-functors`. The Lean form is the published
`UnramifiedFunctor.OneDimensionalLifting`. Murre proves (F7) for the Picard-type functor on
printed p. 257 (PDF p. 16). He uses the obstruction to lifting in `H²` with coefficients in
`I`, a parameter `u` of `Λ`, and the long exact sequence of `0 → I → I → I/uI → 0`. The proof
here takes a different route. It glues classes formally along the two ring pullback squares
of the outline, and uses (F5) for the agreement step. It needs no cohomology, no
completeness and no dimension hypothesis.

Class pullback functoriality reuses `classesPullback_comp` from [TwoAxisClassProductBaseChange](TwoAxisClassProductBaseChange.md).
