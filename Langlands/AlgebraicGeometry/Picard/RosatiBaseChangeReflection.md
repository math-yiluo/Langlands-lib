# Reflection of the rational involution equation by surjective base change

Rational base change is faithful for an abelian scheme and its actual dual.
After base change, postcompose both sides of the involution equation with
the canonical dual comparison, which is an isomorphism. The naturality
square for the contravariant dual rewrites the resulting equality as the
involution equation on the changed abelian scheme. Thus existence of the
two lifted maps suffices for checking their compatibility on the reduction.
This is an internal consequence of rational dual naturality and the
faithfulness of rational base change.
