# Positivity of the mixed Euler coefficient for a nonzero pullback

The theorem `Langlands.AbelianScheme.nonzeroEndomorphismMixedEulerPositive` has exactly the existing full-universe type `Langlands.AbelianScheme.NonzeroEndomorphismMixedEulerPositiveStatement`. It proves strict positivity of the actual mixed Euler coefficient for the pullback of an ample line bundle by a nonzero integral endomorphism in positive relative dimension. It adds no trace or Rosati premise.

The implemented argument first proves a stronger arbitrary-field version in dimension `d + 1`. The actual pullback power-section construction gives a section of a positive tensor power of the pulled-back bundle, with both a frame point and a nonframe point. The section-cokernel positivity criterion implies that this tensor-power bundle has strictly positive mixed coefficient against the original ample bundle. Actual tensor-power scaling identifies that coefficient with the positive integer exponent times the pullback mixed coefficient. Positivity of the latter follows. Field abelian-scheme geometry supplies integrality, Noetherianity, separation, and the exact topological dimension bound required by scaling. Properness and geometric connectedness of the structure morphism, required by the section criterion, are fields of the abelian-scheme structure.

The final theorem writes the positive relative dimension as a successor and specializes this arbitrary-field argument to the algebraically closed characteristic-zero scope of the existing statement. This is an internal composite proof using actual line bundles, sections, and coherent Euler characteristics. It proves no trace formula and does not establish full Rosati positivity on its own.
