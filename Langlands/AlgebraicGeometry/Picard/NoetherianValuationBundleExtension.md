# Actual bundle extension over a Noetherian valuation domain

Worker 513 authored this internal combination of the published field and DVR
extension constructions. It covers every Noetherian valuation domain and
every actual generic self-product line bundle, without an axis-triviality
premise.

Fix an arbitrary universe `u`, a commutative valuation domain `R : Type u`
with `IsNoetherianRing R`, any specified fraction field `K : Type u` with its
given `Field K`, `Algebra R K` and `IsFractionRing R K` instances, and every
Library abelian scheme `A / Spec R`. Let `D` be an actual line bundle on the
self-product of the actual base change of `A` to `Spec K`.

`Langlands.AbelianScheme.noetherianValuationRingSelfProductExtension R K A D`
returns a dependent pair. Its first component is an actual line bundle `E`
on the total self-product. Its second component is an actual isomorphism
of module sheaves

```text
((LineBundle.pullback q).obj E).obj ≅ D.obj,
```

where
`q = mumfordProductBaseChangeMap A (Spec.map (CommRingCat.ofHom (algebraMap R K)))`.
This is exactly the canonical product map used in the published
`ValuationRingTwoAxisExtensionStatement`. The specified fraction field and
algebra structure are retained. Classical choice selects the actual pair
from the existence proofs in the two cases.

The construction splits on `IsField R`. In that case it reuses the published
`selfProductLineBundleExtension_of_isField` from `FractionFieldBundleExtension`.
That theorem extends by inverse pullback along the isomorphic chosen-field
product map.

In the other case a valuation domain is a local Bezout domain. Every ideal
is finitely generated by Noetherianity, so Mathlib's
`IsPrincipalIdealRing.of_isNoetherianRing_of_isBezout` makes it a principal
ideal ring. The negation of `IsField R` gives a nonzero maximal ideal by
`IsLocalRing.isField_iff_maximalIdeal_eq`. These are precisely Mathlib's
`IsDiscreteValuationRing R` conditions. The published unconditional
`discreteValuationRingSelfProductLineBundleExtension` then gives the actual
bundle and isomorphism for the same `K`, `A`, `D` and `q`.

The theorem
`selfProductLineBundleExtension_of_noetherianValuationRing` forgets the chosen
pair to assert existence of `E` and `Nonempty` of the actual restriction
isomorphism.

The provenance is internal: this module combines the two published
extension results with Mathlib's valuation, Bezout, principal-ideal and local
ring interfaces. It introduces no new source assertion or axiom. No
ampleness, projectivity, characteristic, henselianity or completeness
assumption is added. No correspondence representer or comparison is
constructed, and no axis rigidifications are extended.

Noetherianity is a required hypothesis of this construction. Consequently
this module does not prove extension over arbitrary valuation domains or
the host's exact `ValuationRingTwoAxisExtensionStatement`.
