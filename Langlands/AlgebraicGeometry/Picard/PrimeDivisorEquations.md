# Local equations for the closure of a prime germ

This helper module develops the local geometric input for the proof of `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.PrimeGermDivisorSectionStatement` (theorem `primeGermDivisorSection_of_gluing` in `PrimeGermDivisorSectionProof`). Its declarations are in the namespace `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.PrimeDivisor`. It constructs local equations of the closure of a point and proves their compatibility; it does not construct a line bundle. The lemmas are internal consequences of the height-one prime and localization arguments in [Stacks Project, Lemma 31.16.7, tag 0AGA](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0AGA), as used before forming the line bundle in [Definition 31.15.1, tag 01WX](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01WX). The registered source is `stacks-divisors-locally-factorial-2026-10-31`.

For an arbitrary scheme, `closureIdeal` is the reduced ideal data of the closure of a point. On an affine open containing that point, the ideal is its corresponding prime ideal; on an affine open not containing the point, it is the unit ideal. This follows by pulling the singleton closure through the affine open immersion and taking its vanishing ideal. The affine prime ideal has height equal to the coheight of the scheme point. The image of any ideal data in a stalk is independent of the affine neighborhood, by restricting both neighborhoods to a third one contained in their intersection. At a specialization of the original point, the closure ideal has a prime stalk image. Its generator has basic open equal to the complement of the point closure within its chart.

For an integral locally Noetherian scheme, a prime germ at a point determines a coheight-one point specializing to that point. The proof uses the canonical map from the spectrum of the stalk, localization invariance of prime-ideal height, and the principal ideal height theorem in the Noetherian stalk. Membership of this point in a basic open is equivalent to nondivisibility of the germ of the defining function by the original prime.

For a coheight-one point on an integral scheme, a factorial stalk at a specialization makes the localized closure ideal principal: its prime image has height one. A generator can be chosen as the germ of a nonzero element of the original affine ideal. On a locally Noetherian scheme with factorial stalks everywhere, finite generation and `exists_principal_neighborhood` spread this generator to an affine basic neighborhood. Away from the point closure, the equation is `1`. This gives a nonzero affine equation near every point.

Any two equations of the same ideal data have associated germs on overlaps because they generate the same principal ideal in the integral stalk. At a specialization, the germ of a nonzero closure equation is prime. At the original base point, the basic-open divisibility criterion forces the original prime germ to divide this prime equation germ, so the two are associated. The file uses only native Mathlib and the paired principal-localization helper module; no Cartier divisor group or comparison with one is defined.
