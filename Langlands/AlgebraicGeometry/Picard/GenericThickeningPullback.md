# Ring pullbacks gluing a generic lift with a square-zero thickening

This module is commutative-algebra support for the proof of
Murre's condition (F7) in `TwoAxisClassOneDimensionalLiftingProof`. It contains no geometry.
Everything lives in namespace `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.GenericThickening`, at an
arbitrary universe `u`.

## Setting and summary

Let `Λ` be a commutative ring, `I` an ideal of `Λ`, and `Λ'` a `Λ`-algebra. In the
application `Λ'` is the localization of `Λ` at its generic point. The module builds two
pullback squares in `CommRingCat`.

1. The fibre product `R₁ = Λ' ×_{Λ'/IΛ'} Λ/I` (`fibre Λ' I`) is the pullback of
   `Λ/I → Λ'/IΛ' ← Λ'`.
2. Let `J` be the ideal of `R₁` generated by the image of `I` (`thick Λ' I`). Assume `I² = 0`
   and that `Λ → Λ'` is injective on `I`. Then `Λ` is the pullback of `Λ/I → R₁/J ← R₁`.

In both squares the leg into the corner is a quotient map whose kernel has square zero when
`I² = 0`. Moreover `R₁/J` retracts onto `Λ/I`, and `R₁/J` is local when `Λ` is local and
`I ≠ ⊤`. Two facts about the hypotheses of (F7) are also proved here:

- a nilpotent ideal killed by the nilradical has square zero;
- a noetherian ring with a single associated prime embeds in its localization at the
  generic point.

## Declarations

### A criterion for ring pullbacks

`isPullback_of_forall p q a b hw hinj hsurj` takes `Λ`-algebra maps `p : R → A'`,
`q : R → B'`, `a : A' → C` and `b : B' → C` with `a ∘ p = b ∘ q` pointwise (`hw`). It
proves that the square of `CommRingCat.ofHom`s is an `IsPullback` provided the map
`R → A' ×_C B'` to the set-theoretic fibre product is injective (`hinj`) and surjective
(`hsurj`). The proof uses that the forgetful functor `CommRingCat ⥤ Type` reflects
isomorphisms and preserves limits, so it reflects this limit (`reflectsLimit_of_reflectsIsomorphisms`).
`IsPullback.of_map` then reduces to the pullback in types, which is `Types.isPullback_iff`.

### The hypotheses of (F7)

- `mul_self_eq_bot`: if `I` is nilpotent and `nilradical Λ * I = ⊥`, then `I * I = ⊥`.
  A nilpotent ideal lies in the nilradical, so `I * I ≤ nilradical Λ * I = ⊥`.
- `algebraMap_atPrime_injective`: let `Λ` be noetherian with
  `associatedPrimes Λ Λ = {p}`, and let `t` be a generic point of `Spec Λ`
  (`IsGenericPoint t Set.univ`). Then `Λ → Λ_t` is injective. Proof:
  1. `t.asIdeal` is a minimal prime of `Module.annihilator Λ Λ = ⊥`. Minimality is
     `PrimeSpectrum.le_iff_mem_closure` with the closure of `{t}` equal to everything.
  2. Minimal primes of the annihilator are associated primes
     (`Module.associatedPrimes.minimalPrimes_annihilator_subset_associatedPrimes`). So
     `t.asIdeal = p`.
  3. If `x ↦ 0` in `Λ_t`, then `s * x = 0` for some `s ∉ t.asIdeal`
     (`IsLocalization.map_eq_zero_iff`). If `x ≠ 0`, then `s` is a zero divisor, so `s` lies
     in an associated prime (`biUnion_associatedPrimes_eq_zero_divisors`), hence in `p`. That
     is a contradiction.

### The fibre product `R₁`

- `quotMap Λ' I : Λ ⧸ I →ₐ[Λ] Λ' ⧸ IΛ'`, where `IΛ'` is `I.map (algebraMap Λ Λ')`. It is
  the map induced by the structure map.
- `fibreSubalgebra Λ' I` is the equalizer subalgebra of `Λ' × Λ/I` of the two maps to
  `Λ'/IΛ'`.
- `fibre Λ' I` is a type synonym (a `def`) of this subalgebra. It carries exactly one
  `CommRing` and one `Algebra Λ` instance, both copied from the subalgebra. The synonym
  prevents clashing ring structures on its quotients, which arise if one takes quotients of
  the subalgebra directly.
- `fibreVal` (the inclusion into `Λ' × Λ/I`), `fibreMk x y h` (the element with components
  `x` and `y`), `fibreFst` and `fibreSnd` (the two projections, as `Λ`-algebra maps).
- `fibre_spec` (the components agree in `Λ'/IΛ'`), `fibre_ext` (equality is checked on the
  components), `fibreFst_algebraMap` and `fibreSnd_algebraMap` (the structure map has
  components `algebraMap Λ Λ' x` and the class of `x`).
- `fibre_isPullback Λ' I`: the square with sides `fibreSnd`, `fibreFst`, `quotMap Λ' I` and
  `Ideal.Quotient.mkₐ Λ IΛ'` is a pullback, by `isPullback_of_forall`.
- `ker_mkₐ J`: the kernel of `Ideal.Quotient.mkₐ Λ J` is `J`.
- `isNilpotent_ker_mkₐ_map`: if `I * I = ⊥`, the kernel `IΛ'` of `Λ' → Λ'/IΛ'` squares to
  zero, since `IΛ' * IΛ' = (I * I)Λ'`.

### The thickening ideal `J`

- `thick Λ' I` is `I.map (algebraMap Λ (fibre Λ' I))`, and `thickMk Λ' I` is the quotient map
  `fibre Λ' I → fibre Λ' I ⧸ thick Λ' I`.
- `exists_of_mem_thick`: if `I * I = ⊥`, every element of `thick Λ' I` is the image of an
  element of `I`. In other words, the image of `I` is already an ideal. Proof by induction on
  the span. The key case is a multiple `r * x` with `x ∈ I`. Choose `b ∈ Λ` with the class of
  `b` equal to `fibreSnd r`. Then `fibreFst r - b ∈ IΛ'`, so
  `(fibreFst r - b) * x = 0` in `Λ'`, because `IΛ' · IΛ' = 0`. Hence `r * x` is the image
  of `b * x`. This is checked on both components.
- `thick_mul_self` and `isNilpotent_ker_mkₐ_thick`: if `I * I = ⊥`, then `J * J = ⊥`, so the
  kernel of `thickMk` is nilpotent.
- `secMap Λ' I : Λ ⧸ I →ₐ[Λ] fibre Λ' I ⧸ thick Λ' I` is induced by the structure map.
- `thick_isPullback Λ' hII hinj`: assume `I * I = ⊥` and
  `hinj : ∀ x ∈ I, algebraMap Λ Λ' x = 0 → x = 0`. Then the square with sides
  `Ideal.Quotient.mkₐ Λ I`, `Algebra.ofId Λ (fibre Λ' I)`, `secMap Λ' I` and `thickMk Λ' I`
  is a pullback. By `isPullback_of_forall`:
  - Injectivity. If `r` and `s` agree in `Λ/I` and in `R₁`, then `r - s ∈ I` and
    `r - s ↦ 0` in `Λ'`. So `r = s` by `hinj`.
  - Surjectivity. Take `c = [b] ∈ Λ/I` and `z ∈ R₁` with the same image in `R₁/J`. Then
    `z - b ∈ J`, so `z - b` is the image of some `x ∈ I` (`exists_of_mem_thick`). Hence
    `b + x` maps to `c` and to `z`.

### The retraction and locality

- `retraction Λ' I : fibre Λ' I ⧸ thick Λ' I →ₐ[Λ] Λ ⧸ I` is induced by `fibreSnd`, which
  kills `J`. `retraction_mk` and `retraction_comp_mkₐ` say it is `fibreSnd` on
  representatives. `retraction_comp_secMap` says `retraction ∘ secMap = id`, and
  `retraction_surjective` follows.
- `mul_self_eq_zero_of_fibreSnd`: if `I * I = ⊥` and `fibreSnd r = 0`, then `r * r = 0`.
  Indeed `fibreFst r ∈ IΛ'`, which squares to zero.
- `isLocalRing_quotient_thick`: if `Λ` is local, `I ≠ ⊤` and `I * I = ⊥`, then
  `fibre Λ' I ⧸ thick Λ' I` is local. `Λ/I` is local, and the retraction is a local ring
  homomorphism, so `RingHom.domain_isLocalRing` applies. Locality of the retraction: suppose
  `retraction a` is a unit. Choose `v` with `retraction a * retraction v = 1` (surjectivity),
  and a representative `w` of `a * v - 1`. Then `fibreSnd w = 0`, so `w² = 0` and `a * v - 1`
  is nilpotent. Therefore `a * v` is a unit, and so is `a`.

## Dependencies and provenance

Only Mathlib is imported. The constructions are standard commutative algebra, chosen for the
gluing proof of (F7) in `TwoAxisClassOneDimensionalLiftingProof.md`; they are not taken from
a specific source. Murre's own argument for (F7) is different (see there).
