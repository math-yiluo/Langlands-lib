# Universal line bundles over an affine base cover

This internal construction restricts a supplied representing scheme along an
arbitrary base morphism using the slice map–pullback adjunction. Its coherent
universal line bundle is the actual pullback of the original universal bundle.
The canonical isomorphism to any other supplied local representer identifies
these actual line bundles.

For a compact, quasi-separated scheme over an affine base, ampleness of a fixed
line bundle follows from ampleness on the pullbacks of a base open cover.
The proof identifies each pulled-back scheme with the inverse image of the
corresponding open range and applies the existing affine-base neighbourhood
ampleness theorem. Finally, for a proper supplied global representer, ample
universal bundles on supplied local representers therefore give ampleness of
the same coherent universal bundle on the global representer.

The construction and proofs derive from the existing representing bijections,
cartesian coherent-bundle maps, invariance of ampleness under isomorphism and
the affine-base ampleness theorem. No representer, positive bundle or effective
descent theorem is asserted to exist without the stated inputs. Scheme and
presheaf-value universes are independent.
