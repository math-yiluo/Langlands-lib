# Exact topological dimension of a smooth line-bundle section cut

A local frame turns the restricted section map into an endomorphism of
the local structure module. Its value at one is the scalar coefficient.
The published residue/stalk criterion and the equality between the
line-bundle stalk-frame set and the whole generation open identify the
basic open of that coefficient with the pullback of the generation open.

For a compact irreducible locally Noetherian scheme with global dimension
at most `g` and dimension `g` at every closed-point stalk, assume that both
the section's generation open and its complement are nonempty. A point in
the generation open makes the closed complement proper. Extending chains
of irreducible closed subsets by the whole scheme bounds the complement's
dimension plus one by `g`.

Choose a closed point in the complement and a local frame neighborhood.
The coefficient is a nonunit in the local ring. The Noetherian local
principal quotient inequality bounds the stalk dimension by the quotient
dimension plus one. No regularity hypothesis is needed for this direction.
The quotient spectrum embeds in the local vanishing locus, which embeds
in the global vanishing locus. Stalk dimension is preserved by the open
immersion. These comparisons supply the reverse inequality.

Finally published smooth geometric integrality gives irreducibility for
the actual smooth geometrically connected field scheme. Properness gives
compactness. Smoothness gives local Noetherianness, the global dimension
bound, and the closed-point stalk dimensions. The specialization retains
both hypotheses: a point in the generation open and a point outside it.
Applying the preceding argument proves
`LineBundleSectionVanishingDimensionStatement` in arbitrary universe `u`:
the topological Krull dimension of the generation-open complement, plus
one, equals `g`.
