# Reducing the class equalizer immersion to Noetherian affine bases

Author: Worker 766.

This module is `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.ClassEqualizerReduction`. It
imports the Library modules
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.ClassEqualizerLocus` (`CutsOut`,
`RelativePicard.isoLocus`, `representsEqualizer_of_cutsOut`),
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.FieldPicardClassEqualizerImmersionStatement`,
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Descent.FppfImmersionLocusDescent`
(`fppfImmersionLocusDescent`),
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.NoetherianApproximation.LineBundleSubalgebraDescentProof`
(`NoetherianApproximation.lineBundleSubalgebraDescent`) and
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.LineBundleIsoLemmas` (`pullbackCompEqIso`).

## Purpose and provenance

This is an internal helper for the diagonal branch of the proof of
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.FieldPicardSheafAlgebraicSpaceStatement`.
It reduces the Library statement `FieldPicardClassEqualizerImmersionStatement`
to the case of a line bundle `N` on `Spec B ×_k X` with `B` a Noetherian
`k`-algebra. In that case one has to cut out, by an immersion into `Spec B`,
the locus where `N` becomes trivial fppf-locally. No literature theorem is
claimed. The reductions are the usual ones. Being cut out by an immersion is
stable under base change and is fppf-local on the base: the descent step is the
Library theorem `fppfImmersionLocusDescent`, after Stacks Project, Descent,
Lemma 35.24.1, tag 02YM. A line bundle over an arbitrary `k`-algebra comes from
a finitely generated subalgebra, which is the Library theorem
`lineBundleSubalgebraDescent`. The Noetherian case is not proved here.

## Conventions

- `S X : Scheme`, `f : X ⟶ S`, `T : Over S`, and `N` is a line bundle on
  `(relativeSpace f).obj T = T ×_S X`.
- For `g : V ⟶ T.left`, the test object over `S` is `Over.mk (g ≫ T.hom)`, and
  `Over.homMk g` is the corresponding morphism to `T`.
- The existing `AlgebraicSpace.specOver k A`, imported from
  `AlgebraicSpace.SpecFieldPoints`, for a field `k` and a `k`-algebra `A`, is
  `Over.mk (Spec.map (CommRingCat.ofHom (algebraMap k A)))`, the spectrum of
  `A` as an object over `Spec k`.

## The fppf triviality locus

All declarations are in the namespace `RelativePicard`.

- `trivialLocus f T N : Sieve T.left` is the fppf closure
  `Scheme.fppfTopology.close (isoLocus f T N (LineBundle.trivial _))`. By
  `ClassEqualizerLocus`, a morphism lies in it exactly when the fppf Picard
  class of the pullback of `N` is the class of the trivial bundle.
- `trivialLocus_isClosed`: the sieve is closed for the fppf topology.
- `trivialLocus_congr`: it depends only on the isomorphism class of `N`.
- `trivialLocus_pullback`: for `φ : V ⟶ T` in `Over S`, the pullback of
  `trivialLocus f T N` along `φ.left` is the triviality locus of the pullback
  of `N` along `(relativeSpace f).map φ`. The proof uses Mathlib's
  `GrothendieckTopology.pullback_close`, `isoLocus_pullback`, and
  `LineBundle.pullbackTrivialIso`.

## Immersions cutting out the locus

- `HasImmersionLocus f T N` means that there are a scheme `Z` and an immersion
  `ι : Z ⟶ T.left` with `CutsOut ι (trivialLocus f T N)`.
- `HasImmersionLocus.of_pullback`: if the property holds for `N₀` over `T`,
  `φ : V ⟶ T` is a morphism of `Over S`, and the pullback of `N₀` along
  `(relativeSpace f).map φ` is isomorphic to `N`, then it holds for `N` over
  `V`. The new immersion is the base change `pullback.snd ι φ.left`.
- `HasImmersionLocus.of_retraction`: if `φ : T ⟶ V` and `ψ : V ⟶ T` satisfy
  `φ ≫ ψ = 𝟙 T`, then the property for the pullback of `N` to `V` gives it
  for `N` over `T`.
- `HasImmersionLocus.of_cover`: if `R` is an fppf covering sieve of `T.left`
  and the property holds for the pullback of `N` over `Over.mk (g ≫ T.hom)`
  for every `g` in `R`, then it holds for `N`. This applies
  `fppfImmersionLocusDescent` to the fppf-closed sieve `trivialLocus f T N`.
- `HasImmersionLocus.of_isAffine`: if the property holds whenever `T.left` is
  affine, then it holds for all `T`. The covering sieve is generated by the
  affine open cover `T.left.affineCover`. It is an fppf covering because open
  immersions are flat and locally of finite presentation
  (`Precoverage.generate_mem_toGrothendieck`,
  `Scheme.ofArrows_mem_precoverage_iff`).

## Spreading out over a field

Let `k` be a field and `X : Over (Spec k)`.

- `HasImmersionLocus.of_isAffine_of_algebra`: if the property holds over
  `specOver k A` for every `k`-algebra `A`, then it holds over every `T` with
  `T.left` affine. Here `A = Γ(T.left, ⊤)`, with the `k`-algebra structure
  `Spec.preimage (T.left.isoSpec.inv ≫ T.hom)`. The isomorphism
  `T.left.isoSpec` gives morphisms of `Over (Spec k)` in both directions, and
  `of_retraction` applies.
- `HasImmersionLocus.of_noetherian`: assume `X.hom` is quasi-compact and
  quasi-separated, and that the property holds over `specOver k B` for every
  Noetherian `k`-algebra `B`. Then it holds over `specOver k A` for every
  `k`-algebra `A`. The proof applies `lineBundleSubalgebraDescent` to the
  pullback square of `Spec A ×_k X`. It gives a finitely generated
  `k`-subalgebra `B` of `A`, which is Noetherian by
  `Algebra.FiniteType.isNoetherianRing`, and a line bundle on
  `X ×_k Spec B` whose pullback is isomorphic to `N`. The line bundle is moved
  to `Spec B ×_k X` with `pullbackSymmetry`, and `of_pullback` applies along
  `Spec A ⟶ Spec B`.

## The reduction theorem

- `fieldPicardClassEqualizerImmersion_of_noetherian`: suppose that for every
  field `k`, every proper `X` over `Spec k`, every Noetherian `k`-algebra `B`
  and every line bundle `N` on `Spec B ×_k X`, the property
  `HasImmersionLocus X.hom (specOver k B) N` holds. Then
  `FieldPicardClassEqualizerImmersionStatement.{u}` holds. For `T` over
  `Spec k` and line bundles `L` and `M`, the hypothesis is applied to
  `N = L ⊗ M⁻¹`. Here `L ⊗ M⁻¹` is
  `LineBundle.tensor Langlands.Scheme.Modules.pullbackTensorComparisonIsIso L
  M.inverse`. The chain `of_isAffine`, `of_isAffine_of_algebra` and
  `of_noetherian` produces an immersion `ι` cutting out its triviality locus.
  By `isoLocus_eq_tensor_inverse` this locus is the fppf closure of the
  isomorphism locus of `L` and `M`, so `representsEqualizer_of_cutsOut` gives
  the required morphism `Over.homMk ι : Over.mk (ι ≫ T.hom) ⟶ T`. Its
  underlying morphism is `ι`, which is an immersion.
