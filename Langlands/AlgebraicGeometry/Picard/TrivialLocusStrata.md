# Field points of the triviality locus and column splits

Author: Worker 766.

This module is `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.TrivialLocusStrata`. It
imports `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.FieldBaseChangeSections`, the
Library module `Langlands.LinearAlgebra.KernelColumnSplit` (`ColumnSplit`,
`schurMatrix`, `schurMatrix_map_eq_zero`, `exists_columnSplit`) and
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.NoetherianLineBundleSeesawProof`
(`isInvertible_of_isLocallyFreeRankOne`, used by the importing modules).

## Purpose and provenance

This is an internal helper for the proof of the Library statement
`FieldPicardClassEqualizerImmersionStatement`. Let `N` be a line bundle on
`Spec B ×_k X` and `E` its fppf triviality locus, a sieve on `Spec B`. The
module proves the first stratification step. Every point in the image of a
member of `E` lies in the basic open set of the scalar of a column split, with
`a` remaining columns, of a two-term complex computing the sections of `N`.
On that basic open set, the members of `E` factor through the closed subscheme
where the Schur complement of the split vanishes. No literature theorem is
claimed. The argument is the usual flattening-stratification argument for the
kernel of a matrix, written with column splits and Schur complements instead
of Fitting ideals, which Mathlib does not provide.

## Conventions

- `X : Over (Spec k)` for a field `k`. `trivialLocus`, `isoLocus`, `baseMap`,
  `SectionsRank` and `specOver` are as in `ClassEqualizerReduction` and
  `FieldBaseChangeSections`.
- `specMap R A` is `Spec.map (CommRingCat.ofHom (algebraMap R A))`.

## Field points and column splits

These declarations are in the namespace `RelativePicard`.

- `SectionsEquivs X N G` is the type of families, indexed by the `R`-algebras
  `A`, of `A`-linear equivalences between the global sections of the base change
  of `N` along `specMap R A` and the kernel `G.H0 A` of the base-changed complex
  `G`. This is the shape of the output of the FLT port's theorem
  `exists_twoTermComplex_sectionsEquiv_forall_baseChange` applied to
  `baseMap X R` and `N`.
- `exists_field_of_mem_trivialLocus`: if `g : T' ⟶ Spec R` lies in the
  triviality locus and `x` is a point of `T'`, there are a field `K` and an
  `R`-algebra structure on `K` with `RingHom.ker (algebraMap R K)` equal to the
  prime `g x`, such that `specMap R K` lies in the isomorphism locus of `N` and
  the trivial bundle. The proof takes an fppf cover inside the covering sieve
  (`Scheme.mem_grothendieckTopology_iff`), a point `x'` of the cover over `x`,
  and its residue field `κ(x')`. The composite `Spec κ(x') ⟶ Spec R` is
  `Spec.map` of a ring map, by full faithfulness of `Spec`.
- `exists_columnSplit_of_mem_trivialLocus`: assume `SectionsRank X a` and
  `ε : SectionsEquivs X N G`. For `g` in the triviality locus and a point `x`,
  there is a column split `S` of the matrix of `G.d` with `a` remaining columns
  whose scalar `S.t` is not in the prime `g x`. With the field `K` from the
  previous lemma, `ε K` and `SectionsRank` show that the kernel of `G.d ⊗ K`
  has dimension `a`. Then `exists_columnSplit` applies.

## Sieves of morphisms factoring through a monomorphism

These declarations are in the namespace `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard`.

- `factorSieve ι` is the sieve of morphisms factoring through `ι : Z ⟶ Y`, and
  `cutsOut_factorSieve` records that `ι` cuts it out.
- `factorSieve_isClosed`: for a monomorphism `ι`, this sieve is closed for the
  fppf topology. Local lifts glue because the fppf topology is subcanonical
  (Library `FppfClosedLocusDescent.exists_lift_of_covering`).
- `close_le_of_forall_spec`: let `F` be fppf-closed and `G` any sieve. If every
  member of `G` whose source is the spectrum of a ring lies in `F`, then the
  fppf closure of `G` lies in `F`. Members with arbitrary source are covered by
  an affine open cover (`Scheme.affineOpenCover`).

## Strata of column splits

- `stratumIdeal S` is the ideal of the localization `Localization.Away S.t`
  generated by the images of the entries of `S.schurMatrix`. `Stratum S` is the
  quotient ring. Its spectrum is the locus where `S.t` is invertible and the
  Schur complement vanishes.
- `isImmersion_specMap_stratum`: `specMap B (Stratum S)` is an immersion. It is
  a closed immersion followed by the open immersion of a localization.
- `factorSieve_stratum_of_mem_trivialLocus`: assume `SectionsRank X a`,
  `ε : SectionsEquivs X N G`, and a column split `S` of `G.d` with `a`
  remaining columns. If `g` lies in the triviality locus and factors through
  `Spec (Localization.Away S.t)`, then `g` factors through `Spec (Stratum S)`.
  By `factorSieve_isClosed` and `close_le_of_forall_spec`, it is enough to
  treat members `Spec A ⟶ Spec B` of the isomorphism locus that factor through
  the localization. For these, `SectionsRank` and `ε A` show that the kernel of
  `G.d ⊗ A` is free with one generator for each remaining column, and `S.t` is a
  unit in `A`. So `schurMatrix_map_eq_zero` shows that the ring map from the
  localization to `A` kills `stratumIdeal S`, and it factors through
  `Stratum S`.
