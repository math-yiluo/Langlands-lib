# Global units and actual line-bundle automorphisms

This internal derived construction combines the published unit action with the
published scalar classification of automorphisms. For every actual line bundle
`L` on any scheme `X`, `unitAutEquiv L` identifies the global-unit group of `X`
with the actual group of automorphisms of `L`. Its forward map is the previously
constructed multiplication by the unit, and its bijectivity follows from unique
scalar classification. It needs no field, properness or nonemptiness assumption.

The pullback formulas retain the ordinary map on global functions, in both
directions of the equivalence. Transport through an arbitrary isomorphism of
line bundles leaves the scalar unchanged. This module asserts no geometric
representability or smoothness theorem.
