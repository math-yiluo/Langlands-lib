# Actual rational Rosati conjugation

This construction implements Milne, *Abelian Varieties*, January 2, 2022 author revision, §17 p.35: the Rosati operation associated with a polarization is the composite of the polarization, the actual dual endomorphism, and the rational inverse polarization, in that order. The corresponding source ID is `milne-abelian-varieties-1986-author-2022`; the registered errata snapshot was read in full and incorporates all listed corrections in this PDF.

`Rosati.Prerequisites` contains precisely the seven existing tensor/pullback premises and the square and universal-family base-change premises used by the existing dual and polarization definitions. `DualData A` stores the same actual dual representability, geometry, inverse Poincare bundle and evaluation as the Library. Its `Additivity` is the existing pointwise self-pair additivity prerequisite. `dual`, `dual0` and `IsPolarization` reuse `dualAbelianSchemeFromEvaluation`, `dualHom0` and the existing actual `IsPolarization`; no supplied linear dual operation, bilinear form, finiteness or positivity is substituted for them.

The construction is meaningful over any base scheme in the same universe. `HasRationalInverse` states that the actual rationalization of the particular integral homomorphism is an isomorphism. With that explicit prerequisite, `polarizationInverse` is its category-theoretic inverse in the existing rational category. The two inverse equations follow from the actual inverse laws. Unconditional rational invertibility of geometric polarizations is a separate statement target, rather than a new field of `IsPolarization`.

`involution P W hadd lam` is a rational-linear map on the existing `End0 A`. Its defining formula is `comp0 (comp0 (homToHom0 lam) (dual0 a)) (polarizationInverse ...)`, i.e. lambda inverse after alpha dual after lambda. Additivity and rational linearity follow directly from the three linear maps. The operation is defined before asserting its anti-multiplicativity or involutivity, which are separate targets on actual polarizations.

Over a field, `traceForm` is the rational bilinear form using the fixed `geometricEndomorphismTrace` of the companion module. Its value is `Tr(a * involution b)`; the Library's product is composition, with the right factor applied first. The trace is constrained by the actual Tate action, not chosen to ensure positivity. Symmetry and positive-definiteness are separate theorem targets. This file does not prove Milne's Theorem 17.3 and does not assert biduality or unconditional existence of the construction witnesses.
