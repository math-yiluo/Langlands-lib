# Linear systems and their universal divisors over opens of the base

Helper module of the proof of `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.ProperFlatIntegralLinearSystemStatement` (entry module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.ProperFlatIntegralLinearSystemProof`). It contains no `sorry` and no new axioms.

## Content

Namespace `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.LinearSystemGluing`. Let `g : Y ⟶ S` and `m` a Picard class on `Y`. `mapX g h w` is
`pullback.map g t' g t (𝟙) h (𝟙)`, the map `Y ×_S T' ⟶ Y ×_S T` (the Library's
`PicardHilbertPiecesReduction.mapX`).

- `exists_local_regular_generator_of_isEffectiveCartierIdeal`: an effective Cartier ideal is
  locally generated by one regular element (a frame of its kernel module).
- `LS g m t E`: `E` is a relative effective Cartier ideal on `Y ×_S T` over `T` with
  `[O(E)] = [m_T] + [n]` for some class `n` on `T` (Kleiman's `LinSys`, Definition 3.12, in terms
  of classes).
- `LS.comap`: the linear system is stable under pullback along `T' ⟶ T` (Library
  `relativeEffectiveDivisorBaseChange` through `divBC`).
- `mapX_id`.
- `IsUniversalOn g m V l W` for an open `V` of `S`, `l : P ⟶ S` and `W` on `Y ×_S P`: `l` lands
  in `V`, `W` is in the linear system, every divisor of the linear system over a locally
  noetherian test landing in `V` is a pullback of `W` along some point, and two points over the same
  locally noetherian test with equal pullbacks of `W` are equal.
- `IsUniversalOn.of_isPullback`: universality passes to the base change over a smaller open.
- `isIso_of_isUniversalOn`: a map between two universal families over the same open (with locally
  noetherian sources) that matches the divisors is an isomorphism.

## Source

Kleiman, *The Picard scheme*, Definition 3.12 (p.25) and the representability of Theorem 3.13, on
the locally noetherian test category of p.15.
