# Morphisms of fppf sheaves that are bijective on affine tests

Helper module for the proof of
`Picard.ProperLineBundleHomRepresentabilityStatement`.

For a scheme `B`, `affineCoverSieve T` is the sieve on a test `T : Over B`
generated by the standard affine open cover `T.left.affineCover` (each member
viewed in `Over B` by composing with `T.hom`). `affineCoverSieve_mem` shows it
is an fppf covering sieve (a Zariski cover is an fppf cover, via
`Scheme.zariskiPrecoverage_le_fppfPrecoverage`), and
`isAffine_of_mem_affineCoverSieve` shows every arrow of it factors through a
test with affine underlying scheme.

`isIso_of_bijective_affine` is the standard fact that a morphism `Ψ : F ⟶ G`
of type-valued fppf sheaves on the full slice `Over B` is an isomorphism as
soon as `Ψ.app (op T)` is bijective for every test `T` whose underlying scheme
is affine.

Proof as implemented: on an arbitrary test, injectivity follows from
separatedness of `F` on `affineCoverSieve`, since two sections with the same
image agree after restriction to each affine member. With injectivity known
everywhere, the image sieve `Presheaf.imageSieve Ψ s` of a section `s` of `G`
contains `affineCoverSieve`, hence is covering; the chosen local preimages
(`Presheaf.localPreimage`) form a compatible family by injectivity, and their
amalgamation in `F` maps to `s` by separatedness of `G`. This follows the
proof of Mathlib's private lemma behind `Sheaf.isLocallyBijective_iff_isIso`.
Bijectivity on every object then gives `IsIso` by
`NatTrans.isIso_iff_isIso_app` and `isIso_iff_bijective`.
