# Extension of an actual invertible ideal across an open immersion

`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.OpenImmersionInvertibleIdealExtensionStatement.{u}`
is the invertible-ideal form of Stacks Project Lemma 31.29.2, Tag
[0BD8](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BD8).
This module defines its proposition, with no proof or new axiom.

For a locally Noetherian scheme `X`, any open immersion `j : Y ⟶ X`,
and factorial structure-sheaf stalks at all points outside `Set.range j`,
let `D` be a line bundle on `Y` with a monomorphism

```text
s : D.obj ⟶ SheafOfModules.unit Y.ringCatSheaf.
```

The statement asks for a line bundle `E` on `X`, a monomorphism
`t : E.obj ⟶ SheafOfModules.unit X.ringCatSheaf`, and an actual isomorphism
`e : ((LineBundle.pullback j).obj E).obj ≅ D.obj` such that

```text
j*(t) ≫ pullbackUnitIso(j).hom = e.hom ≫ s.
```

Consequently the extension is compatible with the given ideal embedding,
not just with the isomorphism class of its underlying line bundle.
`LineBundle.pullback` uses the usual pullback of module sheaves, and
`pullbackUnitIso` is the published comparison with the structure module.

To compare with the source, identify `Y` with its open range `U` in `X`.
The image of `s` is an invertible ideal subsheaf, since `s` is monic and
its source is locally free of rank one. Locally this is a principal ideal
generated by a non-zero-divisor, and hence the ideal of an effective
Cartier divisor on `U`. The source extends that divisor to `X`;
its invertible ideal gives `E` and `t`. Restriction identifies the ideal
subsheaves, giving `e` and the commuting equation above. The orientation
of `e` is chosen from the restricting extension to the original bundle.

The assumptions retain the source's full scope. Factoriality is required
only on the complement; `Y` need not be factorial, integral, reduced,
connected or quasi-compact. Neither `X` nor `Y` is assumed separated or
projective. No uniqueness of extension is asserted. The input embedding
is a genuine additional datum for this prerequisite; the separate
`QuasiAffineLineBundleIdealEmbeddingStatement` supplies it on Noetherian
quasi-affine schemes. These two inputs are intended for the local step of
the general `OpenImmersionLineBundleExtensionStatement`.
