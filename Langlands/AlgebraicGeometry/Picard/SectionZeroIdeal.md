# The zero ideal sheaf of a section of a line bundle

Helper module of the proof of `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.ProperFlatIntegralLinearSystemStatement` (entry module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.ProperFlatIntegralLinearSystemProof`). It contains no `sorry` and no new axioms.

## Content

Namespace `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.SectionZeroIdeal`. Let `L` be a line bundle (Library
`LineBundle`) on a scheme `X` and `σ` a global section.

- `map_colon_eq`: colon ideals `(σ) : P` of a finite module localize at powers of an element.
- `colon_span_eq_of_equiv`: for a free module of rank one, `(σ) : P` is generated by the coordinate
  of `σ`.
- `res L σ U`: the restriction of `σ` to `U`; `resBasicOpen`: restriction to a basic open as a
  semilinear map.
- `zeroIdeal L σ : X.IdealSheafData`: on an affine open `U` its ideal is the colon ideal
  `(σ|_U) : L(U)`, i.e. the functions `a` with `a • L(U) ⊆ Γ(X, U) • σ|_U`; compatibility with
  basic opens is `map_colon_eq`.
- `zeroIdeal_ideal_of_equiv`: on an affine open where `L` is trivialized by
  `e : L(U) ≃ Γ(X, U)`, the ideal is generated by `e(σ|_U)`.

## Source

The zero scheme of a section of an invertible sheaf, whose ideal is locally generated by the local
equation of the section (Kleiman, *The Picard scheme*, p.25, proof of Theorem 3.13: the divisor
`D` of a section of `L ⊗ N`). The colon description is a choice-free way to define the ideal
sheaf globally.
