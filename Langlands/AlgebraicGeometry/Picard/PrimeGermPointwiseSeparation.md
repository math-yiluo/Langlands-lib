# Point and tangent separation from prime-germ divisor sections

This helper module, written by Worker 399 for the seed-existence proof, develops the pointwise part of J. S. Milne, *Abelian Varieties* (corrected author version January 2, 2022), p.11, proof of Theorem 7.1, registered as `milne-abelian-varieties-1986-author-2022`. The retained author errata, registered as `milne-abelian-varieties-1986-errata-2026-10-25`, have no correction to Sections 6 or 7. Its main theorem takes the proposition `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.PrimeGermDivisorSectionStatement` as an explicit hypothesis `hS1`; that proposition is proved separately as `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.primeGermDivisorSection` (module `PrimeGermDivisorSectionProof`), and the final assembly `Langlands.AbelianScheme.AlgebraicallyClosedSectionSeedExistenceProof` supplies that proof. This module neither proves that input nor asserts seed existence.

The main theorem, `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.separation_of_primeGermDivisorSection`, assumes that proposition, an algebraically closed field `K` in an arbitrary universe, and a commutative group scheme `G` whose structure map is smooth, proper and geometrically connected. At any rational point `z`, it proves the following two conclusions:

- Each rational point different from `z` has a line bundle and a global section vanishing along `z` and not along that point, with a rational nonvanishing witness.
- Each nonzero tangent vector at `z` has a line bundle and a global section vanishing along `z` and not along that tangent vector, again with a rational nonvanishing witness.

These are precisely the pointwise hypotheses intended for finite seed extraction. The theorem does not extract a finite family. It imposes no characteristic or positive-dimension assumption.

The wrapper obtains irreducibility from the published group-scheme theorem `SeparatingLineBundle.irreducibleSpace_of_smooth_of_isProper`, reducedness from `SmoothReducedProof.isReduced_of_smooth_over_field`, and hence integrality. Local finite type gives local Noetherianity, and the published theorem `smoothAlgClosedFactorialStalks` gives factorial stalks. The commutative group structure supplies a common affine neighbourhood for two closed points through the published `algebraicallyClosedCommonAffine` theorem. The named `SeparatingLineBundle` namespace contributes only its rational-point and irreducibility lemmas; no separating-line-bundle or projectivity result is used.

The internal lemma `PrimeGermSeparation.separate_point` works on an integral locally Noetherian scheme with factorial stalks, locally of finite type over `K`, given a common affine neighbourhood of the two rational images. Their images differ by the injectivity of rational points over an algebraically closed field. A basic open inside the complement of the first point contains the second. Its defining function is nonzero, has a nonzero germ by integrality, and that germ is a nonunit. A prime factor of the germ supplies, through the hypothesis `hS1`, a section whose generation open excludes the first point and contains this basic open. The generation-open vanishing criterion proves the two claims, and the second point itself is the nonvanishing witness.

For tangent vectors, `stalkMapAt`, `spec_stalkMapAt` and `stalkMapAt_germ` transport the local-ring map of a local-ring-valued point to a specified underlying image point, reconstruct its morphism, and identify its values on germs with pullbacks of functions. `tangentStalkAlgHom` packages that map as a `K`-algebra homomorphism from the stalk to `K[ε]`. If this homomorphism equalled the constant lift of the rational augmentation, reconstruction would force the tangent vector to be constant. Subtracting the scalar augmentation of a distinguished element therefore gives a stalk element with augmentation zero and nonzero dual-number image. It is a nonzero nonunit.

The internal lemma `PrimeGermSeparation.separate_tangent` chooses a prime factor of this element. Its image under the tangent map is nonzero, since the original element is a multiple of it. The hypothesis `hS1` supplies a section and a local equation whose germ is associated to that prime. Associatedness preserves nonzero image. The germ-pullback identity and the vanishing equivalence of `hS1` show that the section does not vanish along the tangent. It vanishes along the rational point because that image is outside the generation open. The nonempty generation open has a rational point by `SeparatingLineBundle.exists_rationalPoint_mem_of_isOpen`, giving the additional witness.

These are derived implementation lemmas, rather than a literal transcription of Milne's hyperplane argument. The point proof uses a basic open and prime factor of a germ; the tangent proof uses the augmented stalk map and a prime factor detected by that map. The construction of a global divisor section is entirely in the hypothesis `hS1`. In dimension zero, field stalks have no prime elements, and the point and nonzero-tangent inputs to the relevant separation clauses are absent; no artificial positive-dimension assumption has been added.
