# Mixed Euler coefficients from actual section cokernels

This internal module relates the canonical mixed Euler coefficient to the actual cokernel of a section of its coefficient line bundle. It works in arbitrary universe `u` over an arbitrary field, using proper morphisms to that field's spectrum. The section and line bundles are actual sheaves and morphisms. No Rosati form, trace comparison or supplied positivity form occurs.

`MixedEulerCoefficientDetectsSectionSupport f L N s d` says that the mixed coefficient at dimension `d + 1` is nonnegative and is strictly positive exactly when the cokernel of `sectionHom N.obj s` fails the strict support bound at `d`. This is a predicate, rather than an assertion that every section satisfies this criterion.

For an integral scheme, a section of `N` whose generation open is nonempty gives a short exact sequence from the structure module to `N` and then its cokernel. Additivity of the actual coherent Euler polynomials identifies the difference defining `mixedLineBundleEulerPolynomial` with the cokernel polynomial, at every common truncation. This first identity has no dimension bound. If the cokernel has support dimension strictly less than `d + 1`, on a Noetherian separated scheme, reconstruction identifies its truncations at `d + 1` and `d`. The mixed coefficient is therefore its coefficient at `d` in truncation `d`.

When `L` is ample, the published ample coherent Euler coefficient reduction, applied to the published finite integral-ideal filtration theorem, gives the nonnegative coefficient and its exact criterion for positivity. Rewriting by the preceding identity proves `mixedEulerCoefficientDetectsSectionSupport`. The section's generation open must be nonempty, the scheme integral, Noetherian and separated, and the cokernel must satisfy the stated support bound. The coefficient line bundle `N` is not required to be ample.

The final theorem `mixedLineBundleEulerCoefficient_pos_of_section` assumes smooth relative dimension `d + 1`, properness and geometric connectedness, an ample twisting bundle `L`, and a section of `N` whose generation open and complement are both nonempty. Smooth geometric integrality gives integrality of the scheme. The published line-bundle section dimension theorem makes the vanishing set have dimension `d`; the published equality with cokernel support gives the same dimension for the actual cokernel. This establishes its support bound at `d + 1` and failure at `d`. The preceding criterion then yields strict positivity. All geometric and section hypotheses are retained; this theorem also applies when `d = 0`.

The module derives these results entirely from the Library's actual section exact sequences, Euler polynomial additivity and reconstruction, ample coefficient criterion, coherent filtration theorem and section support dimension theorem. It introduces no new literature assertion. To use the result for `N = f*L` in the Rosati program, one must still construct the needed pullback section, or a section of a positive power together with a coefficient-scaling argument. The result alone does not prove `NonzeroEndomorphismMixedEulerPositiveStatement` or `Rosati.PositivityStatement`.
