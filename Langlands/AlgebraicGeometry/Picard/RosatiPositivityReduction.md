# Algebraic reductions for the actual Rosati trace form

This internal helper develops the algebraic part of Task 6879 without changing its geometric positivity target or its import closure. All endomorphisms, conjugations and traces are the existing actual constructions. No theorem here proves `Rosati.PositivityStatement`.

`primeEndomorphismTrace_mul_comm` applies Mathlib's intrinsic trace cyclicity to the actual rational prime Tate algebra action. The action preserves products. This trace identity holds without assuming finite dimension. `geometricEndomorphismTrace_mul_comm` then splits the definition of the fixed rational trace: if a rational lift exists, injectivity of the rational inclusion in the 2-adic field transfers cyclicity; in the zero fallback case both values vanish. This argument does not establish existence of the rational lift, nonvanishing of the trace or any positivity.

`IntegralDiagonalPositive A P W hadd lam` is the proposition that the fixed trace form has strictly positive diagonal on every nonzero actual integral endomorphism, after applying the existing rationalization map `homToHom0`. Its parameters retain the original dual data, additivity proof and rational-inverse instance. The definition supplies no proof of geometric positivity.

`traceForm_smul_smul` records bilinearity with two rational scalars. `traceForm_pos_of_integral` uses the published `Rationalization.exists_integral_multiple`: write the rational endomorphism multiplied by a nonzero integer as the rationalization of an integral endomorphism. A nonzero rational endomorphism has a nonzero integral representative. The two diagonal scalars contribute the square of that nonzero integer, which is strictly positive. Thus `IntegralDiagonalPositive` implies the rational version. The hypothesis is a retained obligation of this reduction, rather than a new premise added to the public target.

`traceForm_symm_of_trace_invariant` applies trace invariance under the existing conjugation, reversal of products, and involutivity to `a * involution b`. `positivity_at_of_integral` assembles symmetry, nonnegative diagonal (splitting off zero), and the strict actual-trace clause. Trace invariance, the two conjugation laws and integral strict positivity are explicit premises of this conditional helper. Their geometric proofs remain necessary before this can yield the unconditional target. Neither finite dimension nor positive dimension is assumed by these reductions.

The provenance is internal linear algebra and localization, using the Library's actual definitions and published denominator-clearing theorem. The motivating geometric theorem is Milne, *Abelian Varieties*, January 2, 2022 revision, §17 Theorem 17.3, pp.35–36 (registered source `milne-abelian-varieties-1986-author-2022`), but no geometric intersection or trace comparison result is used or asserted in these proofs.
