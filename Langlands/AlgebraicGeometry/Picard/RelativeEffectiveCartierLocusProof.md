## What is proved

Let `B` be locally noetherian, `q : Y ⟶ B` proper and flat, and
`Z : Y.IdealSheafData` with `Z.subschemeι ≫ q` flat. Then there is an open
`U : B.Opens` such that for every pullback square
`IsPullback gY qT q g` (with `g : T ⟶ B`, `qT : YT ⟶ T`, `gY : YT ⟶ Y`), the
ideal `Z.comap gY` is a relative effective Cartier ideal for `qT` if and only
if `Set.range g.base ⊆ U`.

## Source and route

S. L. Kleiman, *The Picard scheme*
(`kleiman-2005-picard-scheme-arxiv-math0504020v1`), Lemma 3.4 (p.22, proof
pp.22–23), the base-change paragraph after Definition 3.6 (p.23) and the proof
of Theorem 3.7 (p.24). Kleiman sets `V` = the points where the subscheme is an
effective divisor and `U = H − q(W − V)`. The Lean proof defines `U` the same
way. The direction "lands in `U` ⟹ relative Cartier" is Kleiman's
restriction to `q⁻¹ U` followed by base change. For the converse, Kleiman
passes to fibres over residue fields and applies Lemma 3.4 on `Y`. The Lean
proof avoids fibres: it works directly with the stalk of `YT` at a point
`y'` over `y ∈ Y`. That stalk is flat over the affine chart of `YT`, which is
the pushout `Γ(Y, W) ⊗ Γ(T, V_T)` over `Γ(B, V_B)`. The Tor and Nakayama
arguments of Kleiman's Lemma 3.4, (ii) ⟹ (iii) and (iii) ⟹ (i), are run with
this stalk in place of the residue field (module `CartierLocusDescent`).
Only the effective Cartier property of `Z.comap gY` is used in this
direction, not its flatness over `T`.

## Local equations (section `Local`)

- `HasLocalEquation I x`: there are an affine open `W ∋ x` and
  `s : Γ(X, W)` with `I.ideal W = (s)` and `s` regular in `Γ(X, W)`.
- `hasLocalEquation_of_isEffectiveCartierIdeal`: an effective Cartier ideal
  has a local equation at every point. Proof: the kernel module
  `K = kernelModule I.subschemeι` has a frame `e` on an open `O ∋ x`
  (`exists_isFrameOn_of_isLocallyFreeRankOne`). On an affine `W ∋ x` inside
  `O`, the sections of `K` are `ker (I.subschemeι.app W) = I.ideal W`
  (`ker_subschemeι_app`), and `r ↦ r • e|W` is bijective. With `s` the value
  of `e|W`, surjectivity gives `I.ideal W = (s)` and injectivity gives
  regularity.
- `isSMulRegular_map_of_flat`: regular elements stay regular along flat ring
  maps.
- `hasLocalEquation_comap`: if `f : X' ⟶ X` is flat and `I` has a local
  equation at `f x`, then `I.comap f` has one at `x`. Proof: on an affine
  `W' ∋ x` inside `f⁻¹ W`, `(I.comap f).ideal W'` is the extension of
  `I.ideal W` (`ideal_comap_of_le`), generated by the image of `s`, which is
  regular since `f.appLE` is flat.
- `isOpen_setOf_hasLocalEquation`: the points with a local equation form an
  open set.

## Stalks (section `Stalk`)

For `f : X ⟶ Y`, `x : X` and an open `W ∋ f x`:

- `stalkHom f x W h : Γ(Y, W) → 𝒪_{X,x}` is the germ at `f x` followed by
  the stalk map of `f`. `stalkHom_eq_comp_appLE` writes it as `f.appLE`
  followed by a germ of `X`.
- `isUnit_stalkHom_iff`: `stalkHom f x W h a` is a unit iff
  `f x ∈ Y.basicOpen a` (the stalk map is local).
- `flat_germ`: for an affine `V ∋ x`, the germ `Γ(X, V) → 𝒪_{X,x}` is flat
  (a localization, `IsAffineOpen.isLocalization_stalk`).
- `map_stalkHom_eq`: if `(I.comap f).ideal W' = (s')` on an affine `W' ∋ x`,
  then for every affine `W ∋ f x` the extension of `I.ideal W` along
  `stalkHom f x W h` is generated by the germ of `s'` at `x`. Proof: compare
  both on an affine `U₃ ∋ x` inside `W' ⊓ f⁻¹ W` via `ideal_comap_of_le` and
  `map_ideal`.

## The chart lemma (section `Chart`)

Fix a pullback square `sq : IsPullback gY qT q g`, with `Y` locally noetherian,
`q` flat and `Z.subschemeι ≫ q` flat.

- `flat_quotient`: for affine opens `V_B` of `B` and `W ≤ q⁻¹ V_B` of `Y`,
  the map `Γ(B, V_B) → Γ(Y, W) ⧸ Z.ideal W` is flat. Proof: it is the
  `appLE` map of the flat morphism `Z.subschemeι ≫ q`, up to the isomorphism
  `subschemeObjIso`.
- `chart`: let `V_B` and `V_T ≤ g⁻¹ V_B` be affine, `y'` a point of `YT` with
  `qT y' ∈ V_T`, and `W ≤ q⁻¹ V_B` an affine open containing `gY y'`. Write
  `R = Γ(Y, W)` and `φ = stalkHom gY y' W`. Then:
  1. if `f ∈ Z.ideal W` and `(Z.ideal W).map φ = (φ f)`, there is `a : R`
     with `φ a` a unit and `a * x ∈ R f` for every `x ∈ Z.ideal W`;
  2. if `Z.ideal W = (f)` and `φ f` is regular in the stalk, there is
     `a : R` with `φ a` a unit and `a * r = 0` whenever `r * f = 0`.

  Proof. Let `U_Y = gY⁻¹ W ⊓ qT⁻¹ V_T`. It is affine, and Mathlib's
  `isIso_pushoutSection_of_isAffineOpen` shows that the four `appLE` maps form
  a pushout of rings, so `Algebra.IsPushout Γ(B, V_B) R Γ(T, V_T) Γ(YT, U_Y)`.
  The stalk of `YT` at `y'` is local and flat over `Γ(YT, U_Y)`
  (`flat_germ`). The ring `R` is noetherian, flat over `Γ(B, V_B)` (since `q`
  is flat), and `R ⧸ Z.ideal W` is flat over `Γ(B, V_B)` (`flat_quotient`).
  Now 1 is `CartierLocusDescent.exists_mul_eq_of_map_eq_span` and 2 is
  `CartierLocusDescent.exists_mul_eq_zero_of_isSMulRegular`.

- `hasLocalEquation_of_comap` (descent, Kleiman, proof of Theorem 3.7): if
  `Z.comap gY` has a local equation at `y'`, then `Z` has one at `gY y'`.

  Proof. Let `(s')` be the local equation at `y'` and `t₀` its germ, which is
  regular in the stalk (`flat_germ`). Choose affine `V_B ∋ g (qT y')`,
  `V_T ∋ qT y'` inside `g⁻¹ V_B`, and `W ∋ gY y'` inside `q⁻¹ V_B`.
  - Step A. By `map_stalkHom_eq`, `(Z.ideal W).map φ = (t₀)`. By
    `CartierLocusDescent.exists_mem_map_eq_span`, `(Z.ideal W).map φ = (φ f)`
    for some `f ∈ Z.ideal W`. Part 1 of `chart` gives `a` with `φ a` a unit
    and `a · Z.ideal W ⊆ R f`.
  - Step B. On the affine basic open `D(a) ∋ gY y'` (`isUnit_stalkHom_iff`),
    the ideal is generated by the image `f₁` of `f`
    (`map_ideal_basicOpen` and `CartierLocusDescent.map_eq_span_of_mul_eq`).
  - Step C. Apply `chart` again with `W = D(a)`. By `map_stalkHom_eq`, the
    image of `f₁` in the stalk generates `(t₀)`, so it is regular
    (`CartierLocusDescent.isSMulRegular_of_mem_span`). Part 2 gives `a₂` with
    `φ a₂` a unit and `a₂ · ann(f₁) = 0`.
  - Step D. On the affine basic open `D(a₂) ⊆ D(a)`, which contains `gY y'`,
    the ideal is generated by the image of `f₁` (`map_ideal_basicOpen`), and
    this image is regular (`CartierLocusDescent.isSMulRegular_of_mul_eq_zero`).

## The open set (section `Main`)

Let `q : Y ⟶ B` be universally closed.

- `cartierLocus q Z`: the complement of the image under `q` of the closed set
  of points of `Y` without a local equation. It is open because `q` is a
  closed map. This is Kleiman's `U = H − q(W − V)`.
- `hasLocalEquation_of_mem_cartierLocus`: if `q y ∈ cartierLocus q Z`, then
  `Z` has a local equation at `y`.
- `mem_cartierLocus`: if `Z` has a local equation at every point over `b`,
  then `b ∈ cartierLocus q Z`.
- `isRelativeEffectiveCartierIdeal_restrict` (with `Z.subschemeι ≫ q` flat):
  over `U = cartierLocus q Z`, the restriction `Z.comap (q⁻¹ U).ι` is a
  relative effective Cartier ideal for `q ∣_ U`. Cartier: every point of
  `q⁻¹ U` has a local equation (`hasLocalEquation_comap` along the open
  immersion), and `isEffectiveCartierIdeal_of_local` applies. Flatness: the
  closed subscheme of the restriction is the pullback of the closed subscheme
  of `Z` along `q ∣_ U → q`; pasting `isPullback_comap` with
  `isPullback_morphismRestrict` shows that its map to `U` is a base change of
  the flat `Z.subschemeι ≫ q`.

## The main theorem

`relativeEffectiveCartierLocus`: `Y` is locally noetherian
(`LocallyOfFiniteType.isLocallyNoetherian q`). Take `U = cartierLocus q Z`.
Let `sq : IsPullback gY qT q g`.

- (⟹) Let `Z.comap gY` be relative effective Cartier and `t : T`. For every
  `y` over `g t` there is `y' : YT` with `gY y' = y`
  (`Scheme.Pullback.exists_preimage_pullback`, transported along
  `sq.isoPullback`). `Z.comap gY` has a local
  equation at `y'` (`hasLocalEquation_of_isEffectiveCartierIdeal`), so `Z`
  has one at `y` (`hasLocalEquation_of_comap`). Hence
  `g t ∈ cartierLocus q Z` (`mem_cartierLocus`).
- (⟸) If `g` lands in `U`, it factors as `g' ≫ U.ι`
  (`IsOpenImmersion.lift`). The square `sq` is then the composite of a
  pullback square over `g'` with the restriction square
  `isPullback_morphismRestrict q U` (`IsPullback.of_right'`). Base change of
  relative effective Cartier ideals along that square
  (`bc_comap relativeEffectiveDivisorBaseChange`) applied to
  `isRelativeEffectiveCartierIdeal_restrict` shows that
  `(Z.comap (q⁻¹ U).ι).comap (lift)` is relative effective Cartier for `qT`.
  By `comap_comp` and `lift ≫ (q⁻¹ U).ι = gY`, this ideal is `Z.comap gY`.
