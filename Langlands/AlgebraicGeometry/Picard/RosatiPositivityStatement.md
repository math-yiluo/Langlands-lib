# Positivity of the actual geometric Rosati trace form

`Langlands.AbelianScheme.Rosati.PositivityStatement` states, without a proof, that the already defined rational bilinear form `traceForm P W hadd lam` of an actual geometric polarization is symmetric, is nonnegative on every diagonal value, and has strictly positive diagonal value at every nonzero rational endomorphism. The strict clause is written explicitly as `0 < geometricEndomorphismTrace A (a * involution P W hadd lam a)`. The proved defining formula `traceForm_apply` identifies this with the diagonal value, so these clauses state positive-definiteness on the existing `End0 A`.

The source is Milne, *Abelian Varieties*, January 2, 2022 author revision, §17 Theorem 17.3, pp.35–36. Source ID: `milne-abelian-varieties-1986-author-2022`. The original definition, theorem and proof pages and the complete registered errata snapshot were read; all listed corrections are incorporated in this PDF. The theorem concerns the actual geometric endomorphism trace, with Rosati conjugation defined by the polarization and its dual. The source proof uses the ample divisor intersection formula and its Tate-module comparison. This target asserts the qualitative symmetric positive-definite conclusion in the required algebraically closed characteristic-zero scope; it does not state the quantitative intersection formula.

The form and trace are fixed before this assertion. `involution` is lambda inverse after the actual rational dual endomorphism after lambda. `geometricEndomorphismTrace` is the unique rational lift, when it exists, of the actual 2-adic Tate trace. The separate unconditional `GeometricEndomorphismTraceComparisonStatement`, corresponding to Milne §12 p.23 and Proposition 12.9, identifies it with the source's geometric characteristic-polynomial trace and with every prime Tate trace, and asserts finite-dimensionality of those actual Tate modules. That comparison is a separate proof obligation, not a premise of positivity. The trace has no polarization parameter or choice of positive form.

`P`, `W` and `hadd` keep exactly the Library's existing construction prerequisites explicit. The sole geometric premise is the unchanged actual `W.IsPolarization P lam`. The `HasRationalInverse` instance is needed to form the defined operation and is asserted to exist by the companion unconditional `RationalInverseStatement`. No finite-dimensionality, trace-comparison, Rosati-law or positivity hypothesis is inserted. Positive dimension is not needed for the positive-definite form itself: for the zero abelian variety its nonzero-vector clause is vacuous. The later multiplier consequence separately retains a nonzero-identity premise.
