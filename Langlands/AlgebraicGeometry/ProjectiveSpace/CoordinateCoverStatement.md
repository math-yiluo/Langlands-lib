# The finite affine coordinate cover of polynomial projective space

`ProjectiveSpaceCoordinateCoverStatement` is a universe-polymorphic proposition, with level `u`. It is a statement target only: this module proves none of its clauses.

## Exact statement

For every commutative ring `R : Type u` and every `n : ℕ`, the proposition asserts the conjunction of three properties of the actual polynomial-Proj scheme `ProjectiveSpace R n = Proj (projectiveSpaceGrading R n)` from `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.ProjectiveSpace`:

1. `∀ i : Fin (n + 1), IsAffineOpen (projectiveSpaceCoordinateOpen R n i)`: each coordinate open `D₊(X i)`, which is Mathlib's Proj basic open of the degree-one variable `MvPolynomial.X i`, is an affine open of `ProjectiveSpace R n`;
2. `(⨆ i : Fin (n + 1), projectiveSpaceCoordinateOpen R n i) = ⊤`: these `n + 1` opens have supremum the whole space in the lattice `(ProjectiveSpace R n).Opens`, so they cover it;
3. `CompactSpace (ProjectiveSpace R n)`: the underlying topological space of the scheme is compact, that is, quasi-compact in the sense of the Stacks Project.

The definitions `ProjectiveSpace`, `projectiveSpaceGrading` and `projectiveSpaceCoordinateOpen` are imported unchanged. There is no hypothesis on `R` or `n`. In particular the statement covers the zero ring, where `ProjectiveSpace R n` has empty underlying space, and `n = 0`, where there is a single coordinate open `D₊(X 0)`. No Noetherian, field, finite-type, positive-dimension, line-bundle or ampleness premise occurs, and no cover, affineness or quasi-compactness fact is assumed.

## Source correspondence

The source for the coordinate cover is the registered original `stacks-projective-space-twists-morphisms-2026-10-26`, file `01ND.html` (Stacks Section 27.13, tag 01ND). Lemma 27.13.3 (tag 01NG) states that projective `n`-space over `ℤ` is covered by the `n + 1` standard opens `D₊(T_i)`, each isomorphic to affine `n`-space. Its proof rests on the equality of the irrelevant ideal with `(T_0, …, T_n)`. Definition 27.13.2 (tag 01NF) defines projective space over a ring by base change from `ℤ`, and the paragraph after it identifies this with the relative Proj of the polynomial algebra. The Library's `ProjectiveSpace R n` is that polynomial Proj over the ring `R` directly. A formal comparison with the base-change presentation remains outside the Library, as its own companion records.

For an arbitrary ring `R`, clauses 1 and 2 are therefore the polynomial specialization of two general facts about Proj in the registered original `stacks-proj-graded-ring-27-8-2026-10-27`, file `01M3.html` (Stacks Section 27.8, tag 01M3). By Lemma 27.8.7 (tag 01MB), every standard open `D₊(f)` with `f` homogeneous of positive degree is affine. By Lemma 27.8.9 (tag 01MD), finitely many homogeneous elements of positive degree whose ideal has radical containing the irrelevant ideal give standard opens covering Proj. For `R[T_0, …, T_n]` with every variable of degree one, the irrelevant ideal is generated by the variables. This is the equality used in the proof of Lemma 27.13.3, and it holds over any commutative ring `R`.

Lemma 27.13.3 also asserts that each `D₊(T_i)` is isomorphic to affine `n`-space. This statement records only that each `D₊(X i)` is an affine open. It asserts no chart isomorphism with affine space, nor any description of the ring of sections of `D₊(X i)`.

The retained comment threads of both pages were read. Comments #10914, #10932 and #11106 on Section 27.13 concern notation and cross-references in the paragraph between Definition 27.13.2 and Lemma 27.13.3; the Stacks Project addressed them on 19 January 2026. None changes the statement of Lemma 27.13.3. The Section 27.8 comments do not concern Lemmas 27.8.7 or 27.8.9.

## Derived compactness

Clause 3 is derived content, not a separate assertion of Lemma 27.13.3. It is the elementary consequence of clauses 1 and 2: an affine open is quasi-compact, and a finite union of quasi-compact opens that equals the whole space makes the space compact. The source uses the same consequence. The proof of Lemma 27.13.4 (tag 01NH) in `01ND.html` says that quasi-compactness of `P^n_ℤ → Spec ℤ` follows from Lemma 27.13.3, and Lemma 27.8.9 concludes quasi-compactness of Proj from such finitely many generating elements. Mathlib's `CompactSpace` imposes no Hausdorff condition, so it matches Stacks quasi-compactness.

## Role and remaining obligations

This statement is a prerequisite for the route that uses the actual twisting sheaf `O(1)` on polynomial projective space toward ample-line-bundle existence. It is not a statement about twisting sheaves, generation by sections or ampleness, and it asserts no ample-existence result. The proof of the whole proposition is the obligation of a separate exact-target proof task. That task is expected to reuse Mathlib's Proj API rather than rebuild Proj. Relevant declarations are `Proj.isAffineOpen_basicOpen` for degree-one basic opens and `Proj.iSup_basicOpen_eq_top`, whose hypothesis is that the irrelevant ideal lies in the span of the chosen elements.
