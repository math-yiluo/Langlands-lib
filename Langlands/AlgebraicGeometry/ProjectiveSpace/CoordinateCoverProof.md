# Proof of the finite affine coordinate cover of polynomial projective space

`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.projectiveSpaceCoordinateCover` proves exactly the published
proposition `ProjectiveSpaceCoordinateCoverStatement.{u}` from
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.ProjectiveSpace.CoordinateCoverStatement`:

```lean
theorem projectiveSpaceCoordinateCover : ProjectiveSpaceCoordinateCoverStatement.{u}
```

The proposition says that for every commutative ring `R : Type u` and every `n : ℕ`:

1. each coordinate open `projectiveSpaceCoordinateOpen R n i`, Mathlib's
   `Proj.basicOpen` of the variable `MvPolynomial.X i`, is an affine open of
   `ProjectiveSpace R n`;
2. the supremum of these `n + 1` opens in the lattice of opens is `⊤`;
3. the underlying topological space of `ProjectiveSpace R n` is compact.

The proof adds no hypotheses on `R` or `n`, so it covers the zero ring and `n = 0`. The
statement pair, `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.ProjectiveSpace` and every other imported
declaration are unchanged. The module only adds `Mathlib.RingTheory.MvPolynomial.Ideal`
as an import. It declares no instances and no new model of projective space. All results
are about the Library's `ProjectiveSpace R n`, which is `Proj (projectiveSpaceGrading R n)`
for the total-degree grading of `MvPolynomial (Fin (n + 1)) R`.

## Implemented proof

The module proves three auxiliary lemmas and then assembles them.

- `isAffineOpen_projectiveSpaceCoordinateOpen R n i` proves conjunct 1. It applies
  Mathlib's `Proj.isAffineOpen_basicOpen`, which states that `Proj.basicOpen 𝒜 f` is affine
  when `f ∈ 𝒜 m` with `0 < m`. Here `f = X i`, `m = 1`, and the degree hypothesis is the
  Library lemma `projectiveSpace_X_mem R n i : X i ∈ projectiveSpaceGrading R n 1`, with
  `Nat.one_pos` for `0 < 1`.
- `projectiveSpace_irrelevant_le_span_X R n` is the polynomial-specific step. It shows that
  the irrelevant ideal of `projectiveSpaceGrading R n` lies in `Ideal.span (Set.range X)`,
  the ideal generated by the variables. Membership of `p` in the irrelevant ideal unfolds,
  through `HomogeneousIdeal.mem_iff` and `HomogeneousIdeal.mem_irrelevant_iff`, to
  `GradedRing.proj _ 0 p = 0`. Mathlib's `MvPolynomial.decomposition.decompose'_apply`
  identifies this degree-zero projection with `MvPolynomial.homogeneousComponent 0 p`. By
  `MvPolynomial.homogeneousComponent_zero` that component is `C (p.coeff 0)`, so
  `MvPolynomial.C_eq_zero` gives `p.coeff 0 = 0`. Mathlib's
  `MvPolynomial.mem_pow_idealOfVars_iff' 1 p` says that `p ∈ idealOfVars _ R ^ 1` exactly
  when `p.coeff x = 0` for every exponent `x` of total degree `< 1`. By
  `Finsupp.degree_eq_zero_iff`, the only such `x` is `0`, and that case is the coefficient
  just computed. Finally `simpa` rewrites `idealOfVars _ R ^ 1` to `idealOfVars _ R`,
  which Mathlib defines (as an `abbrev`) to be `Ideal.span (Set.range X)`.
- `iSup_projectiveSpaceCoordinateOpen R n` proves conjunct 2. It applies Mathlib's
  `Proj.iSup_basicOpen_eq_top`: if the irrelevant ideal lies in the span of a family
  `f : ι → A`, then `⨆ i, Proj.basicOpen 𝒜 (f i) = ⊤`. The family is `X` on
  `Fin (n + 1)`, and the hypothesis is the previous lemma.

The theorem `projectiveSpaceCoordinateCover` introduces `R`, its `CommRing` instance and
`n`, and supplies the first two conjuncts from these lemmas. For the third conjunct it
rewrites `CompactSpace` as compactness of `Set.univ` (`isCompact_univ_iff`). Taking
underlying sets in the equality of conjunct 2, with `TopologicalSpace.Opens.coe_iSup` and
`TopologicalSpace.Opens.coe_top`, turns `Set.univ` into the union of the `n + 1` coordinate
opens. That finite union is compact by `isCompact_iUnion`, since each coordinate open is
compact as an affine open (`IsAffineOpen.isCompact`).

## Provenance

The statement's sources are Stacks Lemma 27.13.3 (tag 01NG) and Lemmas 27.8.7 (tag 01MB)
and 27.8.9 (tag 01MD), as recorded in the statement's Markdown. The Lean proof follows the
same route. The general Proj facts, affineness of standard opens and the cover criterion
from the irrelevant ideal, are Mathlib's existing `Proj` API. The only polynomial-specific
input is that the irrelevant ideal is contained in `(X_0, …, X_n)`, the inclusion used in
the proof of Lemma 27.13.3. Compactness of the finite union of affine opens is the
consequence also drawn in Lemma 27.8.9 and in the proof of Lemma 27.13.4.
