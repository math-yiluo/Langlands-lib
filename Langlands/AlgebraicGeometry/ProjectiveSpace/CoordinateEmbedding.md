# Coordinate embeddings of projective spaces

Helper module of the proof of
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.FlatProperEquivalenceRelationQuotientReductionStatement` (entry
module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.FlatProperEquivalenceRelationQuotientReductionProof`).
It contains no `sorry` and no new axioms.

## Content

Namespace `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.ProjectiveSpace`.

**Generic part.** Let `f : 𝒜 →+*ᵍ ℬ` be a graded ring homomorphism of `ℕ`-graded rings that is
surjective in every degree (`hsurj`).

- `irrelevant_le_map_of_surjective`: the irrelevant ideal of `ℬ` is contained in the ideal
  generated by the image of the irrelevant ideal of `𝒜`. This is the hypothesis of Mathlib's
  `Proj.map f`.
- `away_map_surjective`: for homogeneous `s` of positive degree, `f` is surjective on the
  degree-zero localizations `A[s⁻¹]₀ → B[f(s)⁻¹]₀`.
- `isPullback_awayι_map`: over the chart `Spec A[s⁻¹]₀ ⟶ Proj 𝒜`, the preimage under
  `Proj.map f` is the chart `Spec B[f(s)⁻¹]₀`, and the square of the two chart inclusions with
  `Proj.map f` and `Spec` of the localized map is a pullback (Mathlib's
  `Proj.map_preimage_basicOpen` and `IsOpenImmersion.isPullback`).
- `isClosedImmersion_map_of_surjective`: `Proj.map f` is a closed immersion. Being a closed
  immersion is local on the target; on the charts `D₊(s)` it is `Spec` of a surjective ring map.

**Coordinate embeddings.** Let `e : Fin (a + 1) → Fin (N + 1)` be injective.

- `coordinateImage R e k` is `X j` when `k = e j`, and `0` when `k` is not in the range of `e`.
  `coordinateRestriction R e : R[X₀, …, X_N] →ₐ[R] R[X₀, …, X_a]` is the substitution
  `X k ↦ coordinateImage R e k`. `gradedCoordinateRestriction R e` is the same map, viewed as a
  graded ring homomorphism.
- `coordinateRestriction_rename`, `exists_preimage`: renaming variables along `e` is a section
  of the substitution, so it is surjective in every degree.
- `coordinateEmbedding R he : ProjectiveSpace R a ⟶ ProjectiveSpace R N` is
  `Proj.map (gradedCoordinateRestriction R e)`. The instance
  `isClosedImmersion_coordinateEmbedding` says it is a closed immersion.
  `coordinateEmbedding_toSpec` says it is a morphism over `Spec R`.
- `coordinateEmbedding_preimage`, `coordinateEmbedding_preimage_apply`,
  `coordinateEmbedding_preimage_eq_bot`: the preimage of the coordinate open `D₊(X k)` is
  `D₊(coordinateImage R e k)`. So `D₊(X (e j))` pulls back to `D₊(X j)`, and `D₊(X k)` pulls
  back to the empty open when `k` is not in the range of `e`.

In coordinates, the coordinate embedding sends `[x₀ : … : x_a]` to the point whose `e j`-th
coordinate is `x_j` and whose other coordinates are `0`.

## Use

`HQuasiProjectiveDisjointUnion` embeds finitely many projective spaces into one, using pairwise
disjoint blocks of coordinates.
