# Bounded homogeneous equations on local-ring tests of a family

`polynomialIsoSpec B m` identifies the polynomial Proj model of projective
space over a commutative ring `B` with the relative pullback model over
`Spec B`. The comparison is compatible with both projections. Its inverse
uses the published Cartesian square for coefficient extension from `ULift ℤ`.
For any `B`-algebra `C`, `polynomialIsoSpec_natural` identifies polynomial
coefficient extension with relative base change along `Spec C ⟶ Spec B`.
The two projections verify this equality: the base projection uses the
commutative coefficient-extension square and the integral projection uses
composition of graded polynomial maps along the scalar tower.

## Uniform bound

Let `T` be a noetherian scheme, and let `W` be an ideal sheaf on relative
projective `m`-space over `T`. The theorem
`exists_uniformBoundedHomogeneousEquations` supplies a single natural number
`D` for every map `x : Spec A ⟶ T` from a commutative local ring `A` in the
same universe. In the polynomial model over `A`, the pulled-back ideal on
each standard chart is generated by the dehomogenizations of homogeneous
forms of degrees at most `D` vanishing on the entire pulled-back closed
subscheme, in the sense of `HasBoundedHomogeneousEquations`.

The proof chooses a finite affine subcover of `T` and applies
`exists_boundedHomogeneousEquations` to the ideal on each affine chart of the
**base**, using its noetherian coordinate ring. Take the maximum of those
cutoffs. An affine open containing the image of the closed point of `Spec A`
contains the whole image: every point of `Spec A` specializes to its closed
point, and opens are stable under generization. Hence `x` factors through
one of the affine opens. In affine coordinates this factorization is a
ring homomorphism into `A`. The polynomial/relative naturality formula and
`HasBoundedHomogeneousEquations.comap_map` give the cutoff on this test;
monotonicity increases it to the common maximum.

This is a derived consequence of the affine bounded-equations theorem and
projective base change. The bound is for an individual noetherian family;
there is no common bound asserted across unrelated families, nor a generation
assertion for a saturated homogeneous ideal in the polynomial ring itself.
