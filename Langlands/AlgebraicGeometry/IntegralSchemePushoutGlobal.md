# Global scheme pushout from compatible affine models

This internal construction takes arbitrary schemes `X`, `Y`, `Z` in a common universe and actual maps `i : Z ⟶ X`, `j : Z ⟶ Y`. The structure `IntegralSchemePushout.CompatibleAffinePushoutAtlas i j` consists of an index type in the same universe, compatible open pairs `leftOpen t`, `rightOpen t`, coverage of both schemes, affine scheme models `model t`, two maps from the open subschemes to each model, and witnesses that each model square is a categorical pushout in locally ringed spaces. Compatibility means equality of the inverse-image opens under `i` and `j`. The two restricted maps are the maps defined in `IntegralSchemePushoutCompatibleCharts`.

From these data `space` is the canonical pushout of the underlying locally ringed spaces. `spaceChart` compares each affine model with this pushout. The compatible-open comparison theorem makes these comparisons open immersions and identifies the inverse images of their ranges in `X` and `Y` with the prescribed opens. The colimit cocone is jointly surjective on points after forgetting to sheafed spaces. Together with coverage of both inputs, this proves `spaceChart_covers`.

The definition `scheme` equips `space` with a scheme structure: each affine model is isomorphic to the spectrum of its global sections, and its open comparison supplies an affine neighborhood of every point. The definitions `left` and `right` are the scheme morphisms induced by the locally ringed cocone legs. Full faithfulness of the forgetful functor reflects the locally ringed pushout and proves `isPushout_global` in the category of all schemes.

The scheme morphisms `chart t` are open immersions, commute with the cocone legs as recorded in `inl_chart` and `inr_chart`, and cover the resulting scheme via `openCover`. The lemmas `chart_preimage_left` and `chart_preimage_right` identify their inverse-image opens. Mathlib's criterion for squares along open immersions then proves `isPullback_left` and `isPullback_right`: the two given open subschemes are precisely the pullbacks of each model chart.

The provenance is internal: this construction applies the previously derived compatible-open comparison and Mathlib's locally ringed colimits, local-affineness construction and fully faithful forgetful functor. It introduces no additional literature assertion. It assumes the covering affine models; it does not construct them from the closed-immersion and integral-map hypotheses, descend those morphism properties, or prove the final global pullback square. Those remain obligations of the exact `IntegralSchemePushoutStatement` proof.
