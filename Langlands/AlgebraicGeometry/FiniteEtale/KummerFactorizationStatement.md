# Kummer factorization of finite ℓ-group quotients over a strictly Henselian regular local ring

The module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteEtale.KummerFactorizationStatement`
defines the Kummer algebras `R[1/t][X]/(X^m - t)` and their structure
morphisms. It states that over a strictly Henselian regular local ring every
finite `ℓ`-group quotient of the finite étale fundamental group of
`Spec R[1/t]` becomes trivial on some Kummer cover of `ℓ`-power level. The
module states the proposition and does not prove it.

## Definitions

- `KummerAlgebra t m`, for a commutative ring `R`, `t : R` and `m : ℕ`, is
  `AdjoinRoot (X ^ m - C (algebraMap R (Localization.Away t) t))`. This is the
  ring `R[1/t][X]/(X^m - t)`, built from Mathlib's localization
  `Localization.Away t` and Mathlib's `AdjoinRoot`.
- `kummerMorphism t m : Spec (KummerAlgebra t m) ⟶ Spec (Localization.Away t)`
  is `Spec.map` of the structure map
  `algebraMap (Localization.Away t) (KummerAlgebra t m)`.

## Statement

`StrictlyHenselianKummerFactorizationStatement` has universe parameters `u`
(rings, schemes and the geometric point) and `v` (the finite group). It
quantifies over the following data:

- a domain `R` that is a regular local ring (`IsRegularLocalRing R`, Mathlib's
  notion: Noetherian local with maximal ideal generated by `dim R`
  elements), Henselian (`HenselianLocalRing R`) and has a separably closed
  residue field (`IsSepClosed (ResidueField R)`). Such an `R` is a strictly
  Henselian regular local domain. The domain hypothesis is part of the
  wording of the result; it also follows from regularity;
- an element `t` with `t ∈ maximalIdeal R` and `t ∉ maximalIdeal R ^ 2`, that
  is, a regular parameter: `t` is part of a regular system of parameters, so
  `R/(t)` is again regular and `D = V(t)` is a regular divisor of `Spec R`;
- a natural number `ℓ` with `Fact ℓ.Prime` and `IsUnit (ℓ : R)`, that is, a
  prime different from the residue characteristic;
- an algebraically closed field `Ω` and a geometric point
  `x : Spec Ω ⟶ Spec (Localization.Away t)` of `U = Spec R[1/t]`;
- a group `H` with `Finite H`, the topology of `H` discrete
  (`DiscreteTopology H`), and `IsPGroup ℓ H`;
- a group homomorphism `ρ : FundamentalGroup x (geometricFiberFinite x) →* H`
  with `Continuous ρ`. Here `FundamentalGroup x _` is the Library's finite
  étale fundamental group `π₁(U, x)`, the automorphism group of the fibre
  functor at `x` with its profinite topology, and `geometricFiberFinite x` is
  the Library's proof that the geometric fibres at `x` are finite.

The conclusion is that there exist `n : ℕ` and a geometric point
`y : Spec Ω ⟶ Spec (KummerAlgebra t (ℓ ^ n))` with
`hy : y ≫ kummerMorphism t (ℓ ^ n) = x` such that

`ρ.comp (fundamentalGroupMap (kummerMorphism t (ℓ ^ n)) y x hy
(geometricFiberFinite y) (geometricFiberFinite x)) = 1`.

In words: on the Kummer cover `U_n = Spec (R[1/t][X]/(X^(ℓ^n) - t)) → U`, at a
geometric point `y` lying over `x`, the composite of the induced homomorphism
`π₁(U_n, y) → π₁(U, x)` (the Library's `fundamentalGroupMap`) with `ρ` is the
trivial homomorphism. So `ρ` kills the image of `π₁(U_n, y)`. No property of
`ρ` other than continuity is assumed.

### The case of a discrete valuation ring

A strictly Henselian discrete valuation ring `R` with a uniformizer `t` is
included: `R` is a regular local domain of dimension one, `t` lies in the
maximal ideal but not in its square, and `Localization.Away t` is the fraction
field `K` of `R`. The Kummer covers are then
`Spec (K[X]/(X^(ℓ^n) - t)) → Spec K`, and the statement says that every
continuous homomorphism from `π₁(Spec K, x)` to a finite discrete `ℓ`-group
kills the image of `π₁` of some field `K(t^(1/ℓ^n))`.

## Relation to the sources

SGA 7 I (*Groupes de monodromie en géométrie algébrique*, directed by
A. Grothendieck, Lecture Notes in Mathematics 288, 1972), Exposé I
(*Résumé des premiers exposés de A. Grothendieck*, written by P. Deligne),
registered as `deligne-1972-sga7-i-expose-i-monodromie`, page numbers of the
exposé:

- 0.3 (pp. I-3 and I-4) recalls, for a Henselian trait with residue
  characteristic exponent `p`, the inertia group `I`, its largest pro-`p`
  subgroup `P`, and the canonical isomorphism
  `I/P ≅ lim_n μ_n(k(s̄)) = Ẑ(1)(k(s̄))`. The map `I/P → μ_n(k̄)` is read off
  from the action on an element `u` of valuation `1/n`:
  `σ(u) ≡ φ_n(σ)·u` modulo elements of higher valuation.
- 0.4 (p. I-4) takes `S` strictly local regular, `D` a regular divisor in `S`,
  `p'` the characteristic exponent of the generic point of `D`, and `η̄` a
  generic geometric point. By Abhyankar's lemma, `π₁(S - D, η̄)` has the
  dévissage (0.4.1): `π₁(S - D, η̄) ⊃ I ⊃ P`,
  `π₁(S - D, η̄)/I ≅ Gal(s̄/s)`, `I/P ≅ Ẑ(1)(k(s̄))`, and `P` has no quotient
  of order prime to `p'`.

For `S = Spec R` strictly local, `Gal(s̄/s)` is trivial and `I` is all of
`π₁(S - D)`. Since `ℓ` is invertible in `R`, it differs from `p'`. The image
`ρ(P)` is an `ℓ`-group quotient of `P`, hence trivial. So `ρ` factors through
`I/P ≅ Ẑ(1)`, and its image, a finite `ℓ`-group, is a quotient of `μ_(ℓ^n)`
for some `n`. The finite quotient `π₁(S - D) → μ_(ℓ^n)` is the Galois group of
the cover obtained by adjoining an `ℓ^n`-th root of the equation `t` of `D`,
as in the description of `I/P` through roots of the uniformizer in 0.3.

The Stacks Project, *Fundamental Groups of Schemes*, Section 58.31 (Tag 0BSE),
registered as `stacks-tame-ramification-58-31-0BSE-2026-11-27`, gives the
Kummer form explicitly:

- Example 58.31.4 (Tag 0EYF): for a Noetherian ring `A`, a nonzerodivisor
  `f` with `A/fA` reduced and an integer `e ≥ 1` invertible in `A`, the
  morphism `Spec (A_f[x]/(x^e - f)) → Spec A_f` is finite étale and tamely
  ramified in codimension one.
- Lemma 58.31.5 (Tag 0EYG, Abhyankar's lemma for a regular divisor): if `D`
  is an effective Cartier divisor that is a regular scheme and `Y → X - D` is
  finite étale and tamely ramified in codimension one, then étale locally `Y`
  is a finite disjoint union of such standard morphisms. Its proof treats the
  case of a strictly Henselian local ring `A` directly; there
  `Y = Spec B` with `B ≅ ∏ A_f[x]/(x^(e_i) - f)`.

A connected Galois cover of `U` with group a finite `ℓ`-group is tamely
ramified along `D`, since its local degrees are powers of `ℓ` and `ℓ` is
invertible on `D`. By the lemma it is a Kummer cover of some level `ℓ^n`, and
the kernel of `ρ` contains the image of `π₁(U_n, y)`. The Lean proposition
records this conclusion with the Library's fundamental groups at actual
geometric points.
