# Specialization of split finite étale affine-space algebras

The internal theorem `exists_algEquiv_pi_of_split_extension` descends the
splitting of a finite étale polynomial algebra across a coefficient-field
extension of an algebraically closed field. It is used to transfer the
complex splitting theorem to a model over a smaller algebraically closed
field.

An origin-fibre point extends to the larger field and then to a section
because the larger algebra is split. The images of finitely many algebra
generators under that section have coefficients in a finitely generated
subalgebra of the larger field. The image of the polynomial ring over this
subalgebra contains the full image of the section, so the section factors
through it. A maximal ideal of the coefficient subalgebra gives a finite
residue field extension by Zariski's lemma. Algebraic closedness supplies
an evaluation of the residue field back into the smaller field. Applying
it coefficientwise gives a section over the smaller field and preserves
its prescribed origin-fibre value. The geometric fibre criterion then
gives the splitting.
