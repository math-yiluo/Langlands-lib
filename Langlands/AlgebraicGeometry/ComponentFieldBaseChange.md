# Actual components under further field base change

This derived supporting unit proves `ComponentsGeometricallyIrreducible.baseChange`: if an actual scheme `X` has finitely many maximal irreducible components and their actual vanishing-ideal reduced closed subschemes are geometrically irreducible over `S`, then every actual reduced maximal component of `X ×[S] Spec L` is geometrically irreducible over `L`. The scheme and field share an arbitrary universe. The morphism `Spec L → S` is arbitrary; in particular it can be any further field extension of a field base. No finite-type, compactness, separation, reducedness, or Noetherianity hypothesis is needed beyond the explicit finiteness of the components.

`componentBaseChangeInclusion f e C` constructs the actual pullback of the vanishing-ideal closed inclusion of the maximal component `C` along `X ×[S] T → X`. It is a closed immersion. Its range is the inverse image of `C`, by the scheme pullback range formula and the range of the vanishing-ideal inclusion. The union of these ranges is the whole pullback because the maximal irreducible components cover `X`. These constructions and support identities allow arbitrary `T` and do not require the component set to be finite.

`geometricallyIrreducible_componentBaseChangeInclusion` proves geometric irreducibility of this pulled-back closed scheme over `T` from the original component hypothesis. Pullback pasting identifies its map to `T` with the base change of the original component map to `S`; geometric irreducibility is stable under that base change and source isomorphisms. This step concerns the actual pulled-back scheme, which can fail to be reduced after an inseparable field extension.

For a field-valued base change and a finite component set, the imported finite geometrically irreducible closed-cover theorem applies to these actual closed immersions. It identifies every maximal component of the pullback with a member of the finite closed cover by irreducibility and maximality. Equal-support geometric comparison then gives geometric irreducibility of the maximal component with its own vanishing-ideal reduced closed structure. Thus the proof does not assume that reducedness is preserved by scalar extension or that the original and final reduced component schemes are isomorphic.

The provenance is internal: the construction uses Mathlib's actual categorical pullbacks, their range and pasting formulas, stability of geometric irreducibility, and the imported finite closed-cover theorem. It provides the stability step needed when combining finitely many affine splitting fields into one common finite field. It does not itself construct a splitting extension or prove the unrestricted finite separable component-splitting statement.
