# Affine-valued extension in factorial local rings

`IsGenericOrPrimeElement` describes the zero prime and primes generated by a
prime element. `exists_extension` extends a morphism to an affine scheme from
an open of the spectrum of a factorial domain containing these points. A
section on the open embeds in the function field. Write the section as a
reduced fraction. If the denominator has a prime factor `r`, represent the
same germ in the localization at `(r)` with a denominator outside `(r)`.
Cross multiplication forces `r` to divide the reduced numerator, a
contradiction. This proves that restriction of global functions to the open
is bijective. The affine target turns that bijection into an extension of
the morphism.

`contains_primes_of_localMap` gives a way to detect whether those points lie
in an open of the target spectrum of a local ring homomorphism. A prime
element of the target spectrum's coordinate ring maps to a nonunit. Its
image is either zero or has a prime factor in the factorial domain of the
source spectrum. The zero prime or the prime generated by that factor maps
to a specialization of the target prime in question. The open then contains
the target prime too.

The affine-valued extension is the factorial case of Bosch, Lütkebohmert and
Raynaud, *Néron Models* (1990), §4.4 Lemma 2, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 116).
The local detection lemma implements the required denominator argument for
restriction along a morphism in the diagonal step of §4.4 Theorem 1, printed
pp. 109–110 (PDF pp. 116–117). The literature ID is
`bosch-lutkebohmert-raynaud-1990-neron-models`, and the retained file SHA-256 is
`16d0701b4192ae9db91d083c1df69c0492b00a994aee7bcb0abca365d859d529`.
