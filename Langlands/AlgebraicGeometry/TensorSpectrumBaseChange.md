# Scalar extension of tensor spectra and closed supports

This is an internal derived helper for component splitting. Fix fields `K`, `E`, and `L` in an arbitrary common universe, a compatible field tower `K → E → L`, and a commutative `K`-algebra `A` in that universe. No finiteness, algebraicity, or separability hypothesis on the tower is used.

`fieldTensorMap K E L A` is the actual `K`-algebra homomorphism `E ⊗[K] A → L ⊗[K] A` extending the field coefficient and acting identically on `A`. `isPullback_fieldTensorMap` proves that its contravariant spectrum map, together with the structure maps to `Spec E` and `Spec L`, forms a categorical pullback square. The proof uses the tensor-product pushout squares for `E` and `L` over `K`; it checks the composite maps and cancels the lower pullback square from the composite square.

`preimage_zeroLocus_fieldTensorMap` identifies the inverse image of `V(J)` under that spectrum map with `V(J.map fieldTensorMap)`. It follows from Mathlib's zero-locus inverse-image formula and the fact that ideal extension is the ideal generated by the image. The general helper `range_spec_quotient` identifies the range of the actual quotient spectrum map `Spec(R/J) → Spec R` with `V(J)`, using surjectivity of the quotient homomorphism and its kernel.

`geometricallyIrreducible_quotient_of_isSepClosed_baseChange` additionally assumes `IsSepClosed L` and irreducibility, including nonemptiness, of the zero locus of the extended ideal in `Spec(L ⊗[K] A)`. Its conclusion is geometric irreducibility of the actual composite `Spec((E ⊗[K] A)/J) → Spec(E ⊗[K] A) → Spec E`. In particular, the theorem does not assume that `J` is prime or that the quotient is reduced.

For that proof, base-change the quotient closed immersion to `Spec(L ⊗[K] A)`. Its range is the assumed irreducible zero locus by the preceding formulas, so the source is irreducible by the closed immersion's homeomorphism onto its range. The field criterion from `GeometricIrreducibilityFieldCriteria` makes its composite to the separably closed field spectrum geometrically irreducible. Pasting with the tensor-spectrum pullback identifies this scheme with the scalar extension of the original quotient over `Spec E`. Geometric irreducibility is transported across this isomorphism and then descends along the surjective map `Spec L → Spec E`, using the surjective-base-change theorem from the same imported helper.

The provenance is internal: the proofs combine Mathlib's tensor pushout, pullback pasting, prime-spectrum, and closed-immersion APIs with the two imported field criteria. This unit constructs no finite extension and proves no finite-separable splitting assertion by itself; constructing finitely many descended ideals with these hypotheses remains a downstream obligation.
