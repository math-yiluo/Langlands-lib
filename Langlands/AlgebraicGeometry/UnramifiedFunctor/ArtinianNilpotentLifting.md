# Nilpotent lifting on Artinian and complete local tests

For a presheaf on the slice over a base scheme, Artinian nilpotent
bijectivity requires bijective restriction along every affine test map
induced by a surjection with nilpotent kernel from a local Artinian ring.
If the presheaf also satisfies Murre's condition on adic limits, it
lifts sections along nilpotent quotients of complete noetherian local
rings.

The proof compares the maximal-ideal adic towers of the original
ring and of its nilpotent quotient. The quotient is again complete,
and each comparison of positive levels is a surjection of local
Artinian rings with nilpotent kernel. The zero-level comparison is an
isomorphism of the two zero rings. The unique lifts are compatible
by the squares of quotient maps. Adic effectivity supplies the lift
on the original ring and adic injectivity identifies its restriction
with the original section on the quotient.

This internal reduction supplies the one-dimensional lifting
condition in the separated étale form of Murre's criterion.
