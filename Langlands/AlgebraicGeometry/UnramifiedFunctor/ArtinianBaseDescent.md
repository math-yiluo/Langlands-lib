# Step 1 of Murre's induction: the descent problem for finite extensions

Module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.UnramifiedFunctor.ArtinianBaseDescent`, a supporting module of
`murreArtinianBase` (module `MurreArtinianBase`). Namespace
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.UnramifiedFunctor.ArtinianBase`. Notation as in `ArtinianBaseTools`.

The main result, `descend_finite`, solves the following descent problem when `A` is finite over
`C`. Let `C ⊆ A` be local artinian rings and `π : C → ℓ` the residue map. If an element
`v ∈ F(Spec A)` lies over `η ∈ F(Spec ℓ)`, then `η` lifts to `F(Spec C)`. The residue field
of `A` may be a nontrivial (finite) extension of `ℓ`. Chains of (F4′)-steps
(`ArtinianBaseChain`) cannot change the residue field. They are combined here with a faithfully
flat base change `C → P` and (F1b). This module is internal. It is the part of Murre's
remark that (F4) and (F4′) are equivalent "by using (F1b) and (F5)" (Bourbaki exp. 294, PDF
p. 6) that is needed here.

## Lemmas

* `range_le_of_normal`: let `K / ℓ` be algebraic, `N / ℓ` normal, and `σ : K → N` an
  `ℓ`-embedding. Given maps `α : K → κ` and `β : N → κ` to a field that agree on `ℓ`, the image
  of `α` lies in the image of `β`. Each `α x` is a root of the minimal polynomial of `x`, which
  splits in `N`.
* `lift_quotient_localization`: let `P` be local artinian, `T` a finite artinian `P`-algebra,
  and `vT ∈ F(Spec T)` over `η'`. Let `𝔪` be a maximal ideal of `T` whose local ring `T_𝔪` has
  residue field reached from `P`, and let `k` be the kernel of `P → T_𝔪`. Then `η'` has an
  element over it on `P/k`. Indeed `P/k ⊆ T_𝔪` is a finite extension of local artinian rings
  with the same residue field, and `descend_local` applies.
* `lift_of_iInf`: if `η'` has elements over it on `P/kᵢ` for a nonempty finite family of ideals
  with `⋂ kᵢ = 0`, then it lifts to `P`. The proof iterates `inter_closed` over a `Finset`.
* `descend_flat`: let `C → P` be finite and faithfully flat with `P` local artinian, and let the
  residue maps `π : C → ℓ`, `πP : P → N` be compatible. If `wP ∈ F(Spec P)` restricts to the
  image of `η` in `F(Spec N)`, then `η` lifts to `C`.
  - The two pullbacks of `wP` to `P ⊗_C P` agree at every field point. Each such point
    factors through `N` on both sides, and the two resulting maps `N → K` agree on `ℓ`.
  - `P ⊗_C P` is artinian, so the two pullbacks are equal (`eq_of_fieldAgree`).
  - `flatDescent` gives `w₀ ∈ F(Spec C)`, and `fieldInj` along `ℓ → N` gives `res π w₀ = η`.

## `descend_finite`

Let `C → A` be an injective finite map of local artinian rings, `π : C → ℓ` a residue map, and
`v ∈ F(Spec A)` over `η`. Then there is `w ∈ F(Spec C)` with `res π w = η`.

1. The residue field `K` of `A` is finite over `ℓ`. Embed it in the normal closure `N` of `K`
   in an algebraic closure of `ℓ`; `N` is finite and normal over `ℓ`.
2. `flatExt` gives a local artinian `P`, finite free over `C`, with residue field `N`. Since
   `C → P` is a local homomorphism, `P` is faithfully flat over `C`.
3. Put `T = P ⊗_C A`. It is finite over `P`, hence artinian. `P → T` is injective by flatness
   of `P`. The restriction `vT` of `v` lies over the image `η'` of `η` in `F(Spec N)`.
4. For every maximal ideal `𝔪` of `T`, the residue field of `T_𝔪` is reached from `P`.
   - It is generated by the images of `P` and `A`.
   - The image of `A` lies in that of `N` by `range_le_of_normal`.
5. `lift_quotient_localization` lifts `η'` to `P/kₘ` for every `𝔪`. The `kₘ` intersect to `0`
   because `P → T` is injective and an element of `T` vanishing in all localizations at
   maximal ideals is zero. `lift_of_iInf` then lifts `η'` to `P`.
6. `descend_flat` descends the lift to `C`.
