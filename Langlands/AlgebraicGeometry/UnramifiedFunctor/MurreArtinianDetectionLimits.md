# Spreading and affine limits for Murre's Lemma 1

These auxiliary lemmas implement the two reductions in Murre's proof sketch
for Lemma 1, from local rings to neighbourhoods and from finite-type algebras
to arbitrary algebras. The source is J. P. Murre, *Representation of
unramified functors. Applications*, Séminaire Bourbaki exp. 294 (1964–65),
printed pp. 247–248, PDF pp. 6–7 (registered as
`murre-1965-bourbaki-294-unramified-functors`).

`ArtinianDetection.exists_open_agreement` uses the germ-injectivity spreading
lemma for scheme morphisms. `ArtinianDetection.exists_open_factorization`
first spreads the scheme morphism from the stalk. The factorization gives
equality of two sections on the stalk of the resulting neighbourhood.
`ChartGluing.exists_opens_eq_of_stalkPt` shrinks the neighbourhood until the
sections agree, giving the factorization on an open of the original source.

The affine lemmas use the filtered diagram of subalgebras generated by finite
subsets of the test algebra. In `ArtinianDetection.affineInjective`, the
spectra of these subalgebras form a cofiltered diagram of affine schemes with
affine transition maps. Its limit is the spectrum of the test algebra.
Local finite presentation spreads both maps to stages. Condition (F2) then
gives a common later stage where their images under the comparison are equal.
Injectivity there yields equality of the two original maps. For
`ArtinianDetection.affineSurjective`, (F2) realizes a section at a finite
stage. A preimage there restricts to the desired preimage on the original
affine.
