# Shrinking a chart: monomorphism

Module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.UnramifiedFunctor.MurreChartsMono`, namespace
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.UnramifiedFunctor.Charts`, in the setting of
`MurreChartsShrink`. It implements the last paragraph of Murre's proof of Proposition 2
(Bourbaki exp. 294, printed p.253): with `η₁, η₂` the two pullbacks of `ξ` to `U ×_S U`, the
kernel of `(η₁, η₂)` is open (Lemma 4), it agrees with the diagonal over `s`, hence over a
neighbourhood of `s` (EGA IV 8.8.2), so `ξ` is a monomorphism after shrinking. Here EGA IV
8.8.2 is replaced by the elementary `exists_mem_basicOpen_disjoint` of `MurreChartsAlgebra`,
and Lemma 4 by `isOpen_agreeAt` of `MurreChartsEqualizer`.

## Contents

Let `a ∈ M` with `A[1/a]` formally unramified over `R`, `C = A[1/a] ⊗_R A[1/a]`, and
`ξ₁, ξ₂ ∈ F(Spec C)` the pullbacks of `resA ξ a` along the two inclusions.

* `mem_range_comap_lmul`: a prime `c` of `C` at which `ξ₁, ξ₂` agree (`AgreeAt`) and whose
  two projections are disjoint from `M` lies in the image of
  `Spec A[1/a] ⟶ Spec C` (the diagonal, `Spec` of the multiplication). Proof: the two maps
  `ψ₁, ψ₂ : A[1/a] → κ(c)` invert `M`, so factor through `B` as `χ₁, χ₂`; the agreement says
  `F(χ₁)(ξ_B) = F(χ₂)(ξ_B)`, i.e. (by `elemEquiv`) the two composites `Spec κ(c) ⟶ Spec B ⟶ Y`
  coincide; `j` is a monomorphism, so `χ₁ = χ₂` and `ψ₁ = ψ₂`; then `C → κ(c)` factors through
  the multiplication, and `c` is the image of the corresponding point.
* `exists_mono` ((F1a), (F1b), (F2), local representability, `R` noetherian, `A` of finite
  type): some `n ∈ M` makes `resA ξ b` a monomorphism for every `b` divisible by `a n`. Proof:
  the diagonal `Δ ⊆ Spec C` is closed (`Spec` of a surjection) and the agreement locus is open
  (`isOpen_agreeAt`), so `Eq ∩ Δᶜ` is open in the noetherian `Spec C` and, by the previous
  lemma, contains no prime disjoint from the submonoid generated by `M ⊗ 1` and `1 ⊗ M`.
  `exists_mem_basicOpen_disjoint` gives `m₁, m₂ ∈ M` with `D((m₁ ⊗ 1)(1 ⊗ m₂)) ∩ Eq ⊆ Δ`; take
  `n = m₁ m₂`. For `h₁, h₂ : T ⟶ Spec A[1/b]` with `F(h₁)(resA ξ b) = F(h₂)(resA ξ b)`, compose
  with `Spec A[1/b] ⟶ Spec A[1/a]`. For each point `x` of `T` the image `c` of `x` in `Spec C`
  (via `pullbackSpecIso`) lies in `D((m₁ ⊗ 1)(1 ⊗ m₂))` and in `Eq` (agreement at the field
  point `Spec κ(x)`, `agreeAt_of_map_eq`), hence in `Δ`. So the pair maps every point into the
  image of the diagonal of `Spec A[1/a] ⟶ Spec R`, which is an open immersion
  (`diagonal_SpecMap`, `Algebra.FormallyUnramified.isOpenImmersion_SpecMap_lmul`), and
  `eq_of_forall_mem_range_diagonal` gives `h₁ = h₂`.
