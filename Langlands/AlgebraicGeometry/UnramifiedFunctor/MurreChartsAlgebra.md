# Commutative algebra for Murre's charts

Module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.UnramifiedFunctor.MurreChartsAlgebra`, namespace
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.UnramifiedFunctor.Charts`. Helper results of commutative algebra
for the chart construction in `MurreCharts`. They replace the references to EGA used in
Murre's proof of Proposition 2 (Bourbaki exp. 294, printed pp.252–253) for spreading out
from a local base and for shrinking a chart to a neighbourhood of a point.

## Contents

* `exists_subalgebra_isLocalization`: let `p` be a prime of `R`, `O` a localization of `R` at
  `p` (`IsLocalization.AtPrime O p`) and `B` an `O`-algebra of finite type which is also an
  `R`-algebra compatibly. Then there is an `R`-subalgebra `A ⊆ B` of finite type over `R` such
  that `B` is the localization of `A` at the image of `R \ p`. Proof: take `A` generated over
  `R` by a finite set generating `B` over `O`. For every `b ∈ B` some `r ∈ R \ p` has
  `r b ∈ A` (induction on `Algebra.adjoin`; for `b ∈ O` this is the localization property of
  `O`), which gives surjectivity in the definition of `IsLocalization`; elements of `R \ p` are
  units in `B` because they are units in `O`; injectivity is trivial since `A ⊆ B`.
* `exists_mem_basicOpen_subset`: if an open subset `O` of `Spec A` contains every prime
  disjoint from a submonoid `M`, then `D(m) ⊆ O` for some `m ∈ M`. Proof: write
  `Oᶜ = V(I)`; if `I` were disjoint from `M`, a prime containing `I` and disjoint from `M`
  (`Ideal.exists_le_prime_disjoint`) would lie outside `O`; so some `m ∈ M ∩ I`, and then
  `D(m) ∩ V(I) = ∅`.
* `exists_mem_basicOpen_disjoint`: for `C` noetherian, if an open subset `O` of `Spec C`
  contains no prime disjoint from a submonoid `N`, then `D(n) ∩ O = ∅` for some `n ∈ N`.
  Proof: `O` is quasi-compact, hence `O = ⋃ D(cᵢ)` for finitely many `cᵢ`
  (`PrimeSpectrum.isCompact_isOpen_iff`). For each `c = cᵢ` some `n ∈ N` and `k` satisfy
  `n cᵏ = 0`: otherwise the submonoid generated by `N` and `c` misses `0`, and a prime
  disjoint from it lies in `D(c) ⊆ O` and is disjoint from `N`. Then `D(n) ∩ D(c) = ∅`, and the
  product of these `n` works.
* `mem_unramifiedLocus`: if `B = M⁻¹A` is formally unramified over `R`, every prime `q` of `A`
  disjoint from `M` lies in Mathlib's `Algebra.unramifiedLocus R A` (the primes `q` with
  `A_q` formally unramified over `R`): `A_q` is a localization of `B`
  (`IsLocalization.isLocalization_of_submonoid_le`), hence formally unramified over `B`, hence
  over `R`.
* `exists_formallyUnramified_away`: if moreover `A` is of finite type over `R`, then
  `A[1/m]` is formally unramified over `R` for some `m ∈ M`: the unramified locus is open
  (`Algebra.isOpen_unramifiedLocus`), so `exists_mem_basicOpen_subset` and
  `Algebra.basicOpen_subset_unramifiedLocus_iff` apply.
