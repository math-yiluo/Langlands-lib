# Commutative algebra for Murre's Lemma 2, case (b)

Module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.UnramifiedFunctor.MurreGenericLiftAlgebra`, namespace
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.UnramifiedFunctor.GenericLift`. It imports only Mathlib and
supports the proof of `EtaleLocus.GenericNoLargerLiftStatement` (see
`MurreGenericNoLargerLiftProof.md`). All results use only the standard axioms.

## Unramified algebras (section `Unramified`)

`R → S` is a ring map with `S` essentially of finite type and formally unramified over `R`.

- `map_under_eq_maximalIdeal`: for a prime `q` of `S`, the ideal generated in `S_q` by the
  prime `q ∩ R` of `R` is the maximal ideal of `S_q`. This is Mathlib's
  `Algebra.FormallyUnramified.map_maximalIdeal` (Stacks 00UW) applied to `R_{q∩R} → S_q`,
  set up as in Mathlib's proof of `Ideal.ramificationIdx_eq_one`.
- `isNilpotent_algebraMap_of_mem`: if the prime `P ∩ R` under a prime `P` of `S` lies in the
  nilradical of `R`, then every `x ∈ P` has nilpotent image in `S_P`. Its image lies in the
  maximal ideal, which is generated by images of nilpotent elements of `R`.
- `mem_minimalPrimes_of_under_le_nilradical`: under the same hypothesis, `P` is a minimal
  prime of `S`. Proof: for a prime `P' ≤ P` and `x ∈ P`, `x^n` maps to `0` in `S_P`, so
  `s x^n = 0` for some `s ∉ P`. Then `s ∉ P'`, hence `x ∈ P'`.

In Murre's case (b) the point lies over the generic point of an irreducible component of the
base. These lemmas show that the corresponding prime of the étale algebra is a minimal prime.

## Minimal primes (section `Minimal`)

`S` is a commutative ring.

- `exists_mem_other_minimalPrimes`: if `S` is noetherian and `P` is a minimal prime, some
  `h ∉ P` lies in every other minimal prime. Minimal primes are finitely many
  (`minimalPrimes.finite_of_isNoetherianRing`). Prime avoidance (`Ideal.IsPrime.inf_le'`)
  shows that their intersection is not contained in `P`.
- `le_of_notMem_of_mem_other`: for such `h`, every prime `Q` with `h ∉ Q` contains `P`,
  since `Q` contains some minimal prime and the only one not containing `h` is `P`.
- `nilradical_away_eq`: if `h ∉ P` and every prime not containing `h` contains the prime
  `P`, then `nilradical (S[1/h]) = P S[1/h]`. So `Spec S[1/h]` is irreducible with generic
  point `P`. Proof: `P S[1/h]` is prime, and it lies in every prime `J` of `S[1/h]` because
  `J = (J ∩ S) S[1/h]` and `h ∉ J ∩ S`.

## Quotients by nil ideals (section `Quotient`)

For an ideal `K ≤ nilradical T`:

- `comap_nilradical_quotient`: the preimage in `T` of the nilradical of `T/K` is the
  nilradical of `T`;
- `nilradical_quotient_eq_map`: the nilradical of `T/K` is the image of the nilradical of
  `T`;
- `isPrime_nilradical_quotient`: if the nilradical of `T` is prime, so is that of `T/K`.

The provenance of these facts is internal: they are standard commutative algebra, and the
proofs are this project's.
