# Murre's separated étale representability criterion

Author: Worker 668.

Over a locally noetherian base, a small-valued presheaf with the Zariski
and singleton fpqc sheaf conditions, filtered-colimit preservation,
adic-limit bijectivity, valuative injectivity and bijective restriction on local
Artinian nilpotent thickenings is represented by a separated étale
scheme. The conclusion supplies a representation on the full slice.

Artinian bijectivity gives the lifting and injectivity conditions for
the Library's unramified criterion. Lifting over complete local rings
follows by comparing the adic towers. At a generic localization of a
noetherian ring with an irreducible spectrum, the localization is
Artinian. A section there lifts over the entire localization, so the
absence of a larger lift forces the localized ideal to vanish. Its
finite generators vanish after inverting a common element outside the
generic prime. This gives the generic nonlifting condition.

The resulting representative is locally of finite type and formally
unramified. Artinian lifting transfers through its natural representing
equivalence, making it smooth. A smooth formally unramified morphism
is étale.

This is the sufficiency direction of the separated étale criterion used
in SGA 3, Exposé XV, Lemma 8.10, in the Library's small-valued and affine
test conventions. It is derived from the published Lean unramified
criterion. [Gille–Polo recomposition, PDF pp. 61–62](https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~patrick.polo/SGA3/Expo15.pdf)
