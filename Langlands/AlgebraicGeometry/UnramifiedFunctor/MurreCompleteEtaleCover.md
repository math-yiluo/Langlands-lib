# A finite étale local cover of a finite unramified local algebra

Module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.UnramifiedFunctor.MurreCompleteEtaleCover`, namespace
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.UnramifiedFunctor.CompleteLocal`. This module supports the proof of
Step 2a of Murre's induction (`MurreCompleteLocalStepStatement`, Bourbaki exp. 294, Case I,
Step 2a). The Library's analysis of étale loci (`EtaleLocus.*`) works with closed subschemes of
étale schemes, `Spec (L/I)` with `L` étale over `R`. This module writes each closed-fibre chart
ring `B_y` in that form. The proof is pure commutative algebra and depends on no Library result
about `F`.

Let `R` be a local ring.

* `adjoinRoot_isMaximal`: let `f` be a polynomial whose reduction modulo `m` is irreducible.
  Then `m · R[X]/(f)` is a maximal ideal: the quotient is `k[X]/(f̄)`, where `k = R/m`
  (`AdjoinRoot.quotEquivQuotMap`, `PrincipalIdealRing.isMaximal_of_irreducible`).
* `adjoinRoot_isLocalRing`: if moreover `f` is monic, `R[X]/(f)` is local with this maximal
  ideal. Being finite over `R`, every maximal ideal of `R[X]/(f)` lies over `m`
  (`Ideal.IsMaximal.under`), so it contains `m · R[X]/(f)`.
* `residue_eval_map`: the residue of `p(b)` is the evaluation of the reduction of `p` at the
  residue of `b`.

## `exists_etaleCover`

Let `B` be a local `R`-algebra with local structure map, which is finite, formally unramified and
henselian for its maximal ideal. Then there are a local `R`-algebra `L`, finite and étale over
`R`, and a surjective `R`-algebra map `π : L → B`.

Proof. The residue field extension `κ(B)/κ(R)` is finite and separable (Mathlib's instances for
formally unramified local algebras essentially of finite type). By the primitive element theorem
it is generated by one element `θ₀`. Lift the minimal polynomial of `θ₀` to a monic `f ∈ R[X]`
of the same degree (`lifts_and_degree_eq_and_monic`). Its reduction is irreducible, so
`L = R[X]/(f)` is local (`adjoinRoot_isLocalRing`) and finite over `R`.

* Étale: `f'` is a unit modulo `f`, because `f'` is nonzero modulo `m` by separability. This
  gives an `Algebra.IsStandardEtale` structure (a `StandardEtalePair` with `g = 1`; the
  localization at `g` is `L` itself by `IsLocalization.atUnits`), and standard étale algebras are
  étale.
* The map: `B` is henselian and `f(θ₀) = 0` with `f'(θ₀) ≠ 0` in `κ(B)`. So Hensel's lemma
  (`HenselianRing.is_henselian`) gives a root `θ` of `f` in `B` lifting `θ₀`, and
  `π = AdjoinRoot.liftAlgHom f θ`.
* Surjectivity: every `b ∈ B` is congruent modulo `m_B = m B`
  (`Algebra.FormallyUnramified.map_maximalIdeal`) to a polynomial in `θ`, since `κ(B)` is
  generated by `θ₀`. So `B = range π + m B`, and Nakayama's lemma for the finite `R`-module `B`
  (`Submodule.le_of_le_smul_of_le_jacobson_bot`) gives `B = range π`.
