# Simple integer equations in a residue cotangent space

This internal auxiliary module implements the polynomial chain-rule step in
the orbit-good-point construction. Let `B` be a local `A`-algebra through a map
`σ`, and let `θ : A →+* B` be a second ring map. The differentials are relative
to `σ`. The index type and both rings may have independent universe levels.

The lemma `differential_map_integerPolynomial` transports evaluation of an
integer polynomial through `θ` and applies the usual derivation chain rule,
after restricting the relative derivation to the integers. It proves
`dθ(P(g)) = θ(P'(g)) • dθ(g)`.

If the residue classes of `dθ(g_i)` span the residue cotangent space and each
`θ(P_i'(g_i))` lies outside the maximal ideal of `B`, their residues are
nonzero scalars. Multiplication by those scalars preserves spanning. This
proves `span_simpleIntegerEquations_eq_top`, without a finiteness assumption
on the coordinates.

For `cotangentSpan_eq_top_of_simpleIntegerRoots`, assume additionally that
`σ` and `θ` have the same contraction of the maximal ideal and that each
`θ(P_i(g_i))` lies in that maximal ideal. Then `P_i(g_i)` lies in the common
contracted prime, so these replacement differentials occur among the
generators of the previously defined `cotangentSpan θ`. Their span is the
whole residue cotangent space.

The corollary `maximalIdeal_eq_of_simpleIntegerRoots` applies the imported
cotangent converse. It explicitly retains its rationality hypothesis and the
existence of a finitely generated ideal contained in the maximal ideal that
generates it modulo the image of the common contracted prime under `σ`.
Under those hypotheses, the maximal ideal equals the sum of the ideals
generated by the images of that prime under `σ` and `θ`.

The structure `SimpleIntegerCotangentData θ g` records actual integer
polynomials, membership of their equations in the common contracted prime,
nonvanishing of the derivatives in the residue field, and the spanning
conclusion for the replacement equations. The definition
`simpleIntegerCotangentData` chooses the polynomials from the supplied
existence proofs and applies the proved spanning lemma. It requires equal
contractions, the original spanning condition, and a simple integer equation
for every coordinate; it needs neither rationality nor finite generation.

The module proves only this algebraic step. It does not assert that every
orbit has such coordinates or prove the geometric good-point existence
theorem. The construction is internally derived from the pinned Mathlib
polynomial evaluation and derivation identities and the imported, proved
cotangent-space results. Worker 303 supplied the good-point proof route and
the cotangent-space interface; Worker 101 implemented this equation
replacement and chosen-data construction.
