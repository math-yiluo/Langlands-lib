# Integrality from connectedness and domain stalks

The theorem
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.connectedLocallyNoetherianDomainStalksIntegral.{u}`
proves the exact published proposition
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.ConnectedLocallyNoetherianDomainStalksIntegral.{u}`.
The statement module is imported unchanged. For every universe `u` and every
Mathlib scheme `X : Scheme.{u}`, the only hypotheses are local Noetherianity,
connectedness, and domain stalks at every point. Connectedness includes
nonemptiness.

## Implemented proof

The proof uses three private helpers in this module.

`exists_domain_away` is an algebraic neighborhood argument. Let `R` be a
Noetherian commutative ring, let `p` be a prime ideal, and suppose `R_p` is a
domain. Write `g : R →+* R_p` for the localization map. Its kernel has a finite
generating family `a_i`. For each generator the localization criterion for
zero supplies an element `s_i` outside `p` with `s_i * a_i = 0`. The product
`f = ∏ i, s_i` lies outside `p` and kills each generator. Thus every generator
maps to zero in `R_f`, and the ideal generated by them, `ker g`, is contained
in the kernel of `R → R_f`.

Mathlib's `Localization.awayMap_injective_iff` now shows that the induced map
`R_f → (R_p)_(g f)` is injective: any element of `ker g` is killed by a power
of `f`, by the localization criterion just established. The element `g f`
is a unit and hence nonzero in the domain `R_p`, so its localization is also
a domain. An injective ring map into that domain makes `R_f` a domain.
This argument also handles an empty kernel generating family, with product
equal to one.

`exists_irreducible_open` passes to schemes using
`AlgebraicGeometry.reduce_to_affine_nbhd`. On `Spec R`, local Noetherianity
makes `R` Noetherian, and `Spec.stalkIso` transports the domain hypothesis at
`p` to `Localization.AtPrime p.asIdeal`. The preceding helper supplies `f`.
The spectrum of the domain `R_f` is irreducible; its continuous image under
the localization comap is precisely the basic open `D(f)`, by
`PrimeSpectrum.localization_away_comap_range`. This is an irreducible open
neighborhood of `p`. For an open immersion, local Noetherianity and the
domain stalk hypothesis transfer to the source, using the stalk
isomorphism. The image of the source neighborhood is open and irreducible,
and contains the original point.

`isOpen_irreducible_component` supplies the global step for any topological
space having these neighborhoods. Let `C` be an irreducible component and
`x ∈ C`. Choose an irreducible open neighborhood `U` of `x`, and an
irreducible component `D` containing `U`. Since `C ∩ U` is nonempty and open
in `C`, it is dense in `C`, as expressed by
`subset_closure_inter_of_isPreirreducible_of_isOpen`. The component `D` is
closed and contains `C ∩ U`, so it contains `C`. Maximality of the component
`C` gives `D ⊆ C`, and consequently `U ⊆ C`. These neighborhoods prove
that `C` is open.

The main theorem chooses a point using connectedness and considers its
irreducible component. This component is nonempty, closed, and open, so
connectedness makes it the entire space. Hence `X` is irreducible. Every
domain stalk is reduced, and `isReduced_of_isReduced_stalk` then makes `X`
reduced. Finally,
`isIntegral_of_irreducibleSpace_of_isReduced` proves integrality.

Local Noetherianity is used only to obtain Noetherian coordinate rings on
affine neighborhoods. The argument uses no finite global affine cover or
global quasi-compactness, separation, finite type, base field, regularity,
normality, or prior integrality hypothesis.

## Provenance and authorship

This is an internal derivation of the published statement, with a finite
localization-kernel proof replacing its suggested minimal-prime argument.
The Stacks Project results
[Tag 0357](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0357),
[Tag 033N](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/033N), and
[Tag 033O](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/033O) motivate the component
argument. They concern normal schemes and do not state this exact weaker
domain-stalk formulation. Their retained originals and corrected finiteness
comments were read under literature ID
`stacks-smooth-connected-integral-2026-10-24`, source SHA256
`5374d7e59f547f5114ea9217b3fdfc3c6d0b3f9cf7ab3ed0f35da7163ba7f794`;
no corresponding project errata note was found. The local algebra argument
above is the separate justification for the weaker hypothesis. In
particular, integrally closedness is never assumed.

Worker 447 implemented the proof and its local algebra and topology
helpers. Worker 416 contributed the exact interface and motivating
component reduction. No proof from the parent smooth geometric integrality
task, or from its separate smooth-stalk prerequisite, is used.
