# Open charts through an fppf base change cover the descended presheaf

Module `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Descent.LocallyQuasiFiniteDescent.Covering`, namespace
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.LocallyQuasiFiniteDescent`. This is a helper for the proof of
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.FppfSeparatedLocallyQuasiFiniteDescentStatement` (Stacks Tag 02W8 in
sheaf form). It covers the step where the descended presheaf is shown to be glued from open
scheme charts. It is internal: Stacks has no separate tag for it. There the step is the remark
that the charts built over an fppf cover of `T` jointly cover the sheaf.

## Statements

Notation: `h` is `schemeYoneda S`, i.e. the functor of points with values lifted to
`Type (u + 1)`, and all topologies are `fppfTopology S`.

- `imageSieve_mem_of_charts a b hcov`. Let `a : h X ⟶ G` and let `b k : h (Y k) ⟶ G` (`k : K`)
  be charts. Suppose every point `ξ` of `X` lies in a Zariski open `U ⊆ X` with a morphism
  `z : U ⟶ Y k` over `S` such that `h z ≫ b k` equals the restriction of `a` to `U`. Then the
  image sieve of `Sigma.desc b` at the universal section `uliftYonedaEquiv a` is an fppf covering
  sieve of `X`.
- `isLocallySurjective_sigmaDesc_of_charts p g hg x' a' hpb b hcov`. Let `p : G ⟶ h T` be a map of
  presheaves, and let `g i : V i ⟶ T` (`i : I`) generate a covering sieve of `T`. For each `i`,
  suppose `a' i : h (X' i) ⟶ G` and `x' i : X' i ⟶ V i` form a pullback square of `p` along
  `h (g i)`. Suppose also that the charts `b k` satisfy the hypothesis of the previous item for
  every `a' i`. Then `Sigma.desc b : ∐ₖ h (Y k) ⟶ G` is fppf locally surjective.

The signature is the one Worker 1072 asked for, for use in their final step of the 02W8 proof.

## Proof

First lemma: choose, for every point `ξ`, a chart index `k ξ`, an open `U ξ` and a factorization
`z ξ`. The inclusions `U ξ ⟶ X` form a Zariski cover (`Scheme.Cover.mkOfCovers`). Its sieve is in
the fppf topology on `Over S`, by `GrothendieckTopology.mem_over_iff`,
`Sieve.overEquiv_ofArrows` and `Scheme.zariskiPrecoverage_le_fppfPrecoverage`. Each inclusion lies
in the image sieve. Restricting the universal section of `a` to `U ξ` gives `a` applied to the
inclusion, which equals `b (k ξ)` applied to `z ξ`, and that is the value of `Sigma.desc b` at the
`k ξ`-th coprojection of `z ξ`. This uses `uliftYonedaEquiv_comp`,
`uliftYonedaEquiv_uliftYoneda_map` and `Sigma.ι_comp_desc`.

Second lemma: take a section `y ∈ G(Z)` and write `t : Z ⟶ T` for `p(y)`. By transitivity of the
topology, it suffices to check the image sieve of `y` on the pullback along `t` of the sieve
generated by the `g i`. That pullback is a covering sieve. Let `f : Z' ⟶ Z` be in it, so that
`f ≫ t = c ≫ g i` (`Sieve.mem_ofArrows_iff`). Then `p(G.map f y)` is `h (g i)` applied to `c`. The
pullback square, evaluated at `Z'` (`IsPullback.app`, `Types.exists_of_isPullback`), gives
`w : Z' ⟶ X' i` with `a' i (w) = G.map f y`. Hence the pulled-back image sieve is the pullback
along `w` of the image sieve at the universal section of `a' i`
(`Presheaf.pullback_imageSieve`). That sieve covers by the first lemma.
