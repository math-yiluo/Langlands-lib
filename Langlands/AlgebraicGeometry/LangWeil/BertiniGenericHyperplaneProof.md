# Proof of Bertini's theorem for the generic hyperplane section

Author: Worker 205, with Worker 99 (module `BertiniHyperplaneDimension`).

This module proves the published statement
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.LangWeil.BertiniGenericHyperplaneStatement`, unchanged, as the
theorem

`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.LangWeil.BertiniGenericHyperplane.bertiniGenericHyperplaneStatement`.

The statement, its meaning and its relation to the Stacks Project and Jouanolou are documented
in the statement's paired Markdown. The proof uses no hypothesis beyond the statement's, and
it covers finite base fields: it reduces to an algebraically closed field, not to the field
`k(t)` of the Stacks proof.

## Supporting modules

- `BertiniHyperplaneSeparable`: field Bertini for the generic linear form `ξ = ∑ gᵢ xᵢ` over an
  algebraically closed field.
- `BertiniHyperplaneKernel`: the kernel of `𝓛 ⊗[k] D → Frac(D)(x)`, `a_* ↦ -ξ`, `aᵢ ↦ xᵢ`,
  is generated by `h`; lemmas on prime radicals of principal ideals.
- `BertiniHyperplaneCore`: over an algebraically closed `k`, for a domain `D` of finite type
  with two algebraically independent entries of `g`, the radical of `(1 ⊗ h)` in
  `Ω ⊗[𝓛] (𝓛 ⊗[k] D)` is prime for every algebraic field extension `Ω` of `𝓛`.
- `BertiniHyperplaneDimension` (Worker 99): dimension and generator bookkeeping for the
  reduction `D = (k̄ ⊗[k] A) ⧸ nil`, where `k̄` is the algebraic closure of `k`, and extraction
  of two algebraically independent generators.

## Declarations of this module

Section `Reduction`. Let `K` be a field over `k`, `D` a `K`-algebra, `π : K ⊗[k] A →ₐ[K] D`,
and fields `S` (over `K`) and `Ω` (over `k`, `K` and `S`, with scalar towers `k → K → Ω` and
`K → S → Ω`).

- `reductionMap : Ω ⊗[k] A → Ω ⊗[S] (S ⊗[K] D)` is
  `Ω ⊗[k] A ≅ Ω ⊗[K] (K ⊗[k] A) → Ω ⊗[K] D ≅ Ω ⊗[S] (S ⊗[K] D)`. It uses Mathlib's
  `Algebra.TensorProduct.cancelBaseChange` twice and `id ⊗ π` in the middle.
  `reductionMap_tmul` computes it on pure tensors.
- `reductionMap_surjective`: it is surjective if `π` is.
- `ker_reductionMap_le`: if `π` is surjective with kernel in the nilradical, the kernel of
  `reductionMap` lies in the nilradical. The kernel of `id ⊗ π` is generated by the image of
  `ker π` (Mathlib's `Algebra.TensorProduct.lTensor_ker`), and that image consists of nilpotent
  elements.

Section `Descent`. Let `𝓛 = k(a)` and `𝓛' = K(a)` be the rational function fields in the
variables `a_*, a_0, …, a_{n-1}`. Let `Ω` be a field that is an algebra over both, compatibly
with `k → K`, and in which the variables `a_o` of `𝓛` and `𝓛'` have the same image
(`hcompat`).

- `reductionMap_hyperplaneEq`: `reductionMap` with `S = 𝓛'` sends the image of
  `h = a_* ⊗ 1 + ∑ aᵢ ⊗ gᵢ` in `Ω ⊗[k] A` to `1 ⊗ h'`. Here `h'` is the hyperplane equation
  over `K` for the family `π(1 ⊗ gᵢ)` in `D`.
- `radical_span_fieldTensorMap_hyperplaneEq_isPrime`: let `K` be algebraically closed, `D` a
  domain of finite type over `K`, and `Ω` algebraic over `𝓛'`. Let `π` be surjective with
  kernel in the nilradical, and let two of the `π(1 ⊗ gᵢ)` be algebraically independent over
  `K` in `Frac D`. Then the radical of the ideal of `Ω ⊗[k] A` generated by the image of `h`
  is prime. `BertiniHyperplaneCore` gives this for `1 ⊗ h'`. It descends along the surjection
  `reductionMap`, whose kernel is nil, by `radical_span_singleton_isPrime_of_surjective`.
- `geometricallyIrreducible_of_reduction`: under the same hypotheses and with `Ω` separably
  closed, `Spec((𝓛 ⊗[k] A) ⧸ (h)) → Spec 𝓛` is geometrically irreducible. The prime radical
  makes the zero locus of the extended ideal irreducible (`PrimeSpectrum.isIrreducible_zeroLocus_iff`).
  The Library criterion
  `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.TensorSpectrumBaseChange.geometricallyIrreducible_quotient_of_isSepClosed_baseChange`
  then gives geometric irreducibility of the composite `Spec((𝓛 ⊗ A)/(h)) → Spec(𝓛 ⊗ A) → Spec 𝓛`.
  It is rewritten into the target's morphism with `Spec.map_comp`.

Section `Main`. `fracMap : k(a) →ₐ[k] K(a)` is the map of rational function fields induced by
`k → K`, with `fracMap_algebraMap_X` sending each variable to the same variable.

## Proof of the theorem

Let `k`, `A`, `n`, `g` satisfy the hypotheses, and let `k̄ = AlgebraicClosure k`.

1. Geometric irreducibility of `Spec A` gives that the nilradical of `k̄ ⊗[k] A` is prime
   (Library `SchemeFiberGeometry.geometricallyIrreducible_specMap_iff`). Hence
   `D = (k̄ ⊗[k] A) ⧸ nil` is a domain, and `π` is the quotient map, which is surjective with
   kernel the nilradical.
2. The images of `g` generate `D` over `k̄` (`adjoin_range_baseChange_reduction`). Hence `D`
   is of finite type over `k̄`, and `2 ≤ dim A ≤ dim D` (`ringKrullDim_le_baseChange_reduction`).
3. `exists_algebraicIndependent_pair_of_two_le_ringKrullDim` gives `j, l` with the images of
   `g j, g l` algebraically independent over `k̄` in `Frac D`.
4. Let `Ω` be the algebraic closure of `𝓛' = k̄(a)`. It is algebraic over `𝓛'` and separably
   closed. Make `Ω` an `𝓛`-algebra through `fracMap` followed by `𝓛' → Ω`; then `hcompat`
   holds by `fracMap_algebraMap_X`.
5. `geometricallyIrreducible_of_reduction` gives the target.

## Provenance

Derived. The overall route follows the Stacks Project, Section 37.32 (Tag 0G4C), proof of
Lemma 37.32.3 (Tag 0G4F): reduce to an integral variety, show that the function field of the
universal divisor is geometrically irreducible over the parameter field, and descend. The
implementation differs in two ways. It passes to `k̄` and the reduced ring `D` before working
with function fields, which also handles finite `k`. It replaces Lemma 37.32.2 by the shift
argument of `BertiniHyperplaneSeparable`, which uses `2 ≤ dim A` through two algebraically
independent generators.
