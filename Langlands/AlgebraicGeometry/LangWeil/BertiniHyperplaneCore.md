# Primality of the generic hyperplane section over an algebraically closed field

Author: Worker 205.

This internal helper module is the third step of the proof of
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.LangWeil.BertiniGenericHyperplaneStatement`. It proves Bertini's
theorem for the generic hyperplane section of an integral affine variety over an algebraically
closed field, in ring-theoretic form. Notation is that of `BertiniHyperplaneKernel`: `𝓛`,
`hyperplaneEq k g = h`, `ℱ = Frac(D)(x)`, `ξ`, `paramEval` and `tensorEval`.

## Main result

`radical_span_one_tmul_hyperplaneEq_isPrime`: let `k` be algebraically closed, `D` a domain
of finite type over `k`, and `g : Fin N → D` with `![g j, g l]` algebraically independent
over `k` in `Frac D`. For every field `Ω` algebraic over `𝓛`, the radical of the ideal
generated by `1 ⊗ h` in `Ω ⊗[𝓛] (𝓛 ⊗[k] D)` is prime.

The family `g` need not generate `D`.

## Supporting declarations

- `paramEval_fieldRange`: the image of `paramEval : 𝓛 → ℱ` is the intermediate field
  `k(x, ξ)`. Both inclusions are checked on generators, and `paramEval(a_*) = -ξ`.
- `mem_fieldRange_of_isSeparable`: every element of `ℱ` separable over this image lies in it.
  This is `mem_adjoin_of_isSeparable` (module `BertiniHyperplaneSeparable`), using that
  `Frac D` is finitely generated over `k` (Library helper
  `P2MW.S_Ideal_exists_mem_and_mem_and_radical_span_singleton_isPrime.P2mIrredHyp.fg_top_fractionRing`).
  The index `j` shows that `N` is a successor.
- `forall_isSeparable_mem_range`: for any `𝓛`-algebra structure on `ℱ` whose algebra map is
  `paramEval`, `𝓛` is separably closed in `ℱ`. Separability is transported along the field
  isomorphism of `𝓛` with the image of `paramEval` (Library helper `isSeparable_transport` of
  the same `P2mIrredHyp` namespace).
- `lift_apply_eq_tensorEval`: with such an algebra structure, the `𝓛`-algebra map
  `φ : 𝓛 ⊗[k] D → ℱ` (Mathlib's `Algebra.TensorProduct.lift`) agrees with `tensorEval`.
- `mem_span_one_tmul_of_map_eq_zero`: for every `𝓛`-algebra `Ω`, the kernel of
  `id ⊗ φ : Ω ⊗[𝓛] (𝓛 ⊗[k] D) → Ω ⊗[𝓛] ℱ` lies in the ideal generated by `1 ⊗ h`. By
  `tensorEval_eq_zero_iff` (module `BertiniHyperplaneKernel`), multiplication by `h` followed
  by `φ` is an exact sequence of `𝓛`-modules. Since `Ω` is flat over the field `𝓛`, the
  sequence stays exact after `Ω ⊗[𝓛] -` (Mathlib's `Module.Flat.lTensor_exact`). The image
  of `id ⊗ (h · -)` lies in the ideal of `1 ⊗ h`, by induction over tensors.

## Proof of the main result

`paramEval` is injective on `𝒫` by `paramEval₀_injective`. Make `ℱ` an `𝓛`-algebra through
`paramEval`; this is compatible with the `k`-algebra structures. Let
`Φ : Ω ⊗[𝓛] (𝓛 ⊗[k] D) → ℱ ⊗[𝓛] Ω` be `id ⊗ φ` followed by the swap of factors.

- `Φ(1 ⊗ h) = 0`, because `φ(h) = tensorEval(h) = 0`.
- If `Φ s = 0`, then `s` lies in the ideal of `1 ⊗ h` by `mem_span_one_tmul_of_map_eq_zero`.
- The nilradical of `ℱ ⊗[𝓛] Ω` is prime by the Library lemma
  `Algebra.TensorProduct.nilradical_isPrime_of_isAlgebraic_of_forall_isSeparable_mem_range`
  (ported from the Anthropic FLT formalisation). Its hypotheses are that `Ω / 𝓛` is
  algebraic and that `𝓛` is separably closed in `ℱ` (`forall_isSeparable_mem_range`).

`radical_span_singleton_isPrime_of_ker` (module `BertiniHyperplaneKernel`) concludes.

## Provenance

Internal, following the Stacks Project, Section 37.32 (Tag 0G4C), proof of Lemma 37.32.3
(Tag 0G4F). Its last step shows that the function field of the universal divisor is
geometrically irreducible over the function field of the parameter space. Here that extension
is `ℱ` over the image `k(x, ξ)` of `𝓛`, by `BertiniHyperplaneKernel`. Geometric irreducibility of
`ℱ` over `𝓛` is obtained as separable closedness of `𝓛` in `ℱ` (Lemma 37.32.1, through
`BertiniHyperplaneSeparable`). The Library's tensor-product lemma then gives a prime
nilradical after algebraic base change.
