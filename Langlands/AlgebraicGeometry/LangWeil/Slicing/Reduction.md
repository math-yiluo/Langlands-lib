# Reduction of the uniform Lang–Weil estimate to affine families over integral bases

Author: Worker 196.

This module defines the ring-level interface `AffineFamilyLangWeil e` and proves
`familyLangWeil_of_affine`. Given the uniform point-count upper bound
(`FamilyPointUpperBoundStatement`), the interface in dimension `e + 1` implies the scheme-level
uniform Lang–Weil estimate `FamilyLangWeilInDimension (e + 1)`. The hyperplane-slicing proof of
`FamilyLangWeilSlicingStatement` uses it with `e = d + 1`. All types are in universe `u`.

## Provenance

Provenance: internal. The module adds no theorem from the literature. It reduces the
scheme-level formulation of `FamilyLangWeilInDimension` (see
`Langlands/AlgebraicGeometry/LangWeil/FamilyLangWeilStatement.md`) to affine families over
integral bases, where the slicing argument of Lang and Weil (registered source
`lang-weil-1954-number-of-points`) is carried out in `Slicing/Core.lean`. The reduction uses
standard tools: finite affine covers of the base and of the total space, Noetherian induction on
the base, and generic nonemptiness of fibres (Chevalley's theorem). The formulations are specific
to this proof.

## The interface

`AffineFamilyLangWeil e` says: let `R` be a domain of finite type over `ℤ`, `A` an `R`-algebra
of finite type and `r₀ ≠ 0` in `R`, such that `K ⊗[R] A` has prime nilradical and Krull
dimension `e` for every field `K` over `R` in which `r₀` does not vanish. Then there are `r ≠ 0`
in `R` and `C > 0` with

`|#(A →ₐ[R] κ) - (#κ)^e| ≤ C (#κ)^(e - 1/2)`

for every finite field `κ` with an `R`-algebra structure in which `r` does not vanish. The
algebra maps `A →ₐ[R] κ` are the `κ`-points of `Spec A` over the `κ`-point of `Spec R` given by
`algebraMap R κ`. So this is the Lang–Weil estimate for a geometrically irreducible affine
family of dimension `e` over the dense open subset `D(r)` of an integral base.

## Main result

`familyLangWeil_of_affine hUB e hA`: assume `FamilyPointUpperBoundStatement` and
`AffineFamilyLangWeil (e + 1)`. Then `FamilyLangWeilInDimension (e + 1)` holds. Let
`f : X ⟶ S` be locally of finite type, quasi-compact and geometrically irreducible, with fibres
of pure dimension `e + 1`, over a quasi-compact `S` locally of finite type over `ℤ`. Then there
is `C > 0` with `|#{x : Spec κ ⟶ X // x ≫ f = t} - (#κ)^(e+1)| ≤ C (#κ)^(e + 1 - 1/2)` for
every finite field `κ` and every `t : Spec κ ⟶ S`.

## Proof

The proof has six steps, one per section of the module.

1. **Ring lemmas** (section `RingLevel`). Fix a Noetherian ring `R₀`, a prime `𝔭` of `R₀` and
   an `R₀`-algebra `B` of finite type. "For all `K` near `𝔭`" below means: for all fields `K`
   over `R₀` in which `𝔭` vanishes and a fixed `r ∉ 𝔭` does not.
   - `finite_algHom_of_finiteType`: there are finitely many algebra maps from `B` to a finite
     ring. They are determined by the images of finitely many generators.
   - `exists_ne_zero_isNilpotent_of_forall_comap_ne`: over a domain `R`, if no prime of `A` lies
     over `0`, some `c ≠ 0` in `R` is nilpotent in `A`. Proof: a prime avoiding the image of the
     nonzero elements of `R` (`Ideal.exists_le_prime_disjoint`) would lie over `0`.
   - `finiteType_int_of_surjective`: a quotient of a ring of finite type over `ℤ` is of finite
     type over `ℤ`.
   - `quotientAlgebra 𝔭 K h` is the `R₀ ⧸ 𝔭`-algebra structure on a ring `K` over `R₀` in which
     `𝔭` vanishes; `quotientAlgebra_isScalarTower` is the scalar tower `R₀ → R₀ ⧸ 𝔭 → K`.
   - `exists_not_mem_forall_nontrivial`: if a prime `P` of `B` lies over `𝔭`, then there is
     `r ∉ 𝔭` with `K ⊗[R₀] B ≠ 0` for all `K` near `𝔭`. Proof: `B ⧸ P` gives a prime of
     `(R₀ ⧸ 𝔭) ⊗[R₀] B` lying over `0`. Worker 5132's `exists_ne_zero_forall_nontrivial_tensor`
     (`Slicing/GenericImage.lean`, from Chevalley's theorem) applies over the domain `R₀ ⧸ 𝔭`,
     and `Algebra.TensorProduct.cancelBaseChange` identifies `K ⊗[R₀ ⧸ 𝔭] ((R₀ ⧸ 𝔭) ⊗[R₀] B)`
     with `K ⊗[R₀] B`.
   - `exists_not_mem_forall_subsingleton`: if no prime of `B` lies over `𝔭`, then there is
     `r ∉ 𝔭` with `K ⊗[R₀] B = 0` for all `K` near `𝔭`. Proof: by the second lemma some
     `c ∉ 𝔭` is nilpotent in `(R₀ ⧸ 𝔭) ⊗[R₀] B`. Its image in `K ⊗[R₀] B` is then a nilpotent
     unit, so the ring is zero.
   - `exists_not_mem_forall_not_isNilpotent`: if a prime `P` of `B` lies over `𝔭` and `a ∉ P`,
     then there is `r ∉ 𝔭` such that `1 ⊗ a` is not nilpotent in `K ⊗[R₀] B` for all `K` near
     `𝔭`. Proof: apply `exists_not_mem_forall_nontrivial` to the localization `B[1/a]` and the
     prime generated by `P`. In the nonzero ring `K ⊗[R₀] B[1/a]` the element `1 ⊗ a` is a unit.
   - `ringKrullDim_tensor_quotient_le`: if `K ⊗[R₀] B` has prime nilradical and dimension
     `e + 1` and `1 ⊗ a` is not nilpotent, then `K ⊗[R₀] (B ⧸ (a))` has dimension at most `e`.
     Proof: Worker 5132's `ringKrullDim_quotient_succ_le_of_not_le_nilradical`
     (`Slicing/Dimension.lean`), and `K ⊗[R₀] (B ⧸ (a))` is a quotient of
     `(K ⊗[R₀] B) ⧸ (1 ⊗ a)`.
   - `subsingleton_tensor_quotient`: if `K ⊗[R₀] B = 0` then `K ⊗[R₀] (B ⧸ I) = 0`.
   - `natCard_algHom_eq_zero_le`: the algebra maps `B → κ` killing `a` are at most as many as
     the algebra maps `B ⧸ (a) → κ`. They factor through the quotient.

2. **Fibres of affine charts** (section `ChartFibre`). The Library's `AffineChartPoints` module
   gives, for an affine open `S₀` of `S`, the point `testMap hS₀ K : Spec K ⟶ S` of an algebra
   `K` over `Γ(S₀)`, and affine opens `c : Chart S₀ (Over.mk f)` of `X` lying over `S₀`, with
   rings `c.Ring = Γ(X, c.U)`.
   - `chartPullbackMap` is the open immersion from the base change of `Spec c.Ring ⟶ Spec Γ(S₀)`
     to `Spec K` into `pullback f (testMap hS₀ K)`, built with `pullback.map` from the open
     immersions `c.fromSpec` and `hS₀.fromSpec`.
   - `irreducibleSpace_pullback_testMap` and `hasPureDimension_pullback_testMap`: the base
     change of `f` to `Spec K` is irreducible (geometric irreducibility), and it has pure
     dimension `E` when all fibres of `f` do. For the second, it is homeomorphic to the fibre of
     its projection over the single point of `Spec K`; the Library's
     `hasPureDimension_locallyFiniteType_fiberBaseChange_iff` relates that fibre to the fibre of
     `f`.
   - `chart_isPrime_and_dim`: if `K ⊗[Γ(S₀)] c.Ring ≠ 0`, it has prime nilradical and Krull
     dimension `E`. Proof: its spectrum is a nonempty open subscheme of the irreducible base
     change of `f`, hence irreducible. This gives the prime nilradical by the Library's
     `irreducibleSpace_pullbackSpec_iff_nilradical_isPrime`. Pure dimension passes to open
     subschemes (`componentOpenDimensionInvariant_locallyOfFiniteType`), and
     `hasPureDimension_spec_iff` turns it into ring dimension. The nilradical is the unique
     minimal prime.

3. **Noetherian induction on the base** (section `Induction`).
   `exists_forall_of_forall_isPrime`: let `P κ C` be a property of finite fields `κ` over a
   Noetherian ring `R₀` and constants `C`, monotone in `C`. Suppose every prime `𝔭` has `r ∉ 𝔭`
   and `C` such that `P κ C` holds for all `κ` near `𝔭`. Then one `C` works for all `κ`. Proof:
   Noetherian induction (`IsNoetherian.induction`) on ideals `𝔞` for the claim "one `C` works
   for all `κ` in which `𝔞` vanishes". For `𝔞 = ⊤` there are no such `κ`. For prime `𝔞` the
   fields with `r = 0` are handled by `𝔞 + (r)`. For non-prime `𝔞 ≠ ⊤` take `xy ∈ 𝔞` with
   `x, y ∉ 𝔞`: then `𝔞 + (x)` or `𝔞 + (y)` vanishes in `κ`. Take the maximum of the constants.

4. **Counting through charts** (section `Counting`).
   - `pointsEquiv`: the `κ`-points of `X` over `testMap hS₀ κ` are the morphisms
     `testObj hS₀ κ ⟶ Over.mk f` over `S`.
   - `fromSpec_mem_basicOpen_iff` and `pt_mem_basicOpen_iff`: the point given by
     `ψ : c.Ring →ₐ κ` lies in the basic open `D(a)` iff `ψ a ≠ 0`.
   - `natCard_bounds`: let finitely many charts `c j` cover `f⁻¹(S₀)`, and let elements
     `a j ∈ Γ(c j)` satisfy `D(a j) ⊆ c j₀` for a fixed `j₀`. Then the number of `κ`-points of
     `X` over `testMap hS₀ κ` is at least `#((c j₀).Ring →ₐ κ)` and at most this plus the sum
     over `j` of `#{ψ : (c j).Ring →ₐ κ // ψ (a j) = 0}`. Proof: a point lies in some chart
     (`Chart.exists_pt`). If it is not in `c j₀`, it is not in any `D(a j)`. Chart points are
     injective (`Chart.pt_injective`).
   - `chart_apply` and `chart_comap_eq`: a point of a chart given by a prime `y` lies over the
     point of `S₀` given by the prime of `Γ(S₀)` below `y`.

5. **The estimate near a prime** (section `PrimeCase`).
   - `exists_basicOpen_le_forall`: let `x₀` be a point of the chart `c₀` over the point `𝔭` of
     `S₀`. Every chart `c` has some `a ∈ Γ(c)` with `D(a) ⊆ c₀` and some `r ∉ 𝔭` such that for
     all `K` near `𝔭`, either `K ⊗ c.Ring = 0` or `1 ⊗ a` is not nilpotent. Proof: if some
     point of `c` lies over `𝔭`, then `c` and `c₀` both meet the fibre over `𝔭`. This fibre is
     irreducible (geometric irreducibility), so they share a point `w` of the fibre. Choose a
     basic open `D(a) ∋ w` of `c` inside `c ∩ c₀` (`IsAffineOpen.exists_basicOpen_le`) and apply
     `exists_not_mem_forall_not_isNilpotent` to the prime of `w`. Otherwise no prime of `c.Ring`
     lies over `𝔭`; take `a = 0` and apply `exists_not_mem_forall_subsingleton`.
   - `prime_case`: assume `FamilyPointUpperBoundStatement` and `AffineFamilyLangWeil (e + 1)`,
     with `Γ(S₀)` of finite type over `ℤ`, finitely many charts of finite type covering
     `f⁻¹(S₀)`, and fibres of `f` of pure dimension `e + 1`. Then every prime `𝔭` of `Γ(S₀)`
     has `r ∉ 𝔭` and `C` with
     `|#{κ-points of X over testMap hS₀ κ} - (#κ)^(e+1)| ≤ C (#κ)^(e + 1 - 1/2)` for all finite
     fields `κ` near `𝔭`. Proof: `f` is surjective, so some `x₀` over `𝔭` lies in a chart
     `c j₀`. Get `a j` and `r j` from `exists_basicOpen_le_forall`.
     - Main term: apply `AffineFamilyLangWeil (e + 1)` over the domain `Γ(S₀) ⧸ 𝔭` (of finite
       type over `ℤ`) to `(Γ(S₀) ⧸ 𝔭) ⊗ (c j₀).Ring`, with `r₀` from
       `exists_not_mem_forall_nontrivial`. Its fibre hypothesis comes from
       `chart_isPrime_and_dim` through `cancelBaseChange`. This gives `r₂` and `C₀`, and
       `Algebra.TensorProduct.liftEquivRight` identifies the counted algebra maps with
       `(c j₀).Ring →ₐ[Γ(S₀)] κ`.
     - Error terms: apply Worker 5132's `upperBound_ring` (`Slicing/Bridges.lean`, the ring
       form of `FamilyPointUpperBoundStatement`) to `(c j).Ring ⧸ (a j)` over `Γ(S₀)` in
       dimension `e`. The dimension hypothesis holds at every residue field `κ(p)` of a prime
       `p` with `p = ker (Γ(S₀) → κ)`: either the tensor product vanishes, or
       `chart_isPrime_and_dim` and `ringKrullDim_tensor_quotient_le` bound it by `e`.
     - Combine with `natCard_bounds`, using `(#κ)^e ≤ (#κ)^(e + 1 - 1/2)`. The final `r` is
       `r₂ · ∏ r j ∉ 𝔭`, and the constant is `C₀ + ∑ C' j`.

6. **Global step** (section `Global`).
   - `finiteType_int_of_isAffineOpen`: if `S` is locally of finite type over `ℤ`, then
     `Γ(S₀)` is of finite type over `ℤ` for every affine open `S₀`. Proof:
     `HasRingHomProperty.Spec_iff` applied to `hS₀.fromSpec` composed with the map to
     `Spec (ULift ℤ)`.
   - `affine_open_case`: one constant works for all `κ`-points of `S` factoring through a fixed
     affine open `S₀`. Proof: `Γ(S₀)` is Noetherian. The preimage `f⁻¹(S₀)` is quasi-compact,
     so finitely many affine opens cover it (`isCompact_iff_finite_and_eq_biUnion_affineOpens`).
     Their rings are of finite type over `Γ(S₀)` (`HasRingHomProperty.appLE`). Then
     `exists_forall_of_forall_isPrime` applies to `prime_case`. The constant is made positive by
     `max C 1`.
   - `familyLangWeil_of_affine`: cover the quasi-compact `S` by finitely many affine opens.
     A `κ`-point `t` of `S` has a single point in its image, so it factors through the open
     immersion of one of them (`IsOpenImmersion.lift`). Every morphism `Spec κ ⟶ Spec Γ(S₀)` is
     `Spec` of a ring map (`Spec.map_surjective`), so `t = testMap hS₀ κ` for an algebra
     structure on `κ`. The constant is the sum of the constants of `affine_open_case` over the
     cover, plus `1`.
