# Geometric irreducibility of generic hyperplane sections

Author: Worker 5132.

This helper module of the hyperplane-slicing step `FamilyLangWeilSlicingStatement` spreads
Bertini's theorem out from the generic point of the parameter space of hyperplanes.

## The result

Let `R` be a domain and `A` an `R`-algebra generated by `g : Fin n → A`. Assume:

- `K ⊗[R] A` has prime nilradical for every field `K` into which `R` injects (the generic fibre
  is geometrically irreducible);
- `2 ≤ dim (Frac R ⊗[R] A)`.

Write `𝒫 = R[a_*, a_0, …, a_{n-1}]` (`MvPolynomial (Option (Fin n)) R`). Then
`exists_ne_zero_forall_nilradical_slice_isPrime` gives a nonzero `c ∈ 𝒫` with the following
property. For every field `K` over `R` and every `β : Option (Fin n) → K` with `c(β) ≠ 0`, the
hyperplane section `Slice R g β = (K ⊗[R] A) ⧸ (β_* + ∑ᵢ βᵢ gᵢ)` has prime nilradical.

## Provenance

Provenance: internal. Bertini's theorem enters through the Library hypothesis
`BertiniGenericHyperplaneStatement` (Stacks Project, Lemma 37.32.3, Tag 0G4F; see that
statement's Markdown). Spreading out geometric irreducibility from the generic point is Stacks
Project, Lemma 37.27.5 (Tag 0559), available in the Library in the ring form
`Langlands.Algebra.GenericIrreducibilityReduction.spreading_of_domain_algebras`. This is the
"Bertini plus spreading" step of the proof route in the Markdown of
`FamilyLangWeilSlicingStatement`.

## Declarations

- `isFractionRing_mvPolynomial R σ`: `Frac (Frac(R)[σ])` is a fraction field of `R[σ]`. Proof:
  Mathlib's `IsLocalization.localization_localization_isLocalization_of_has_all_units`, applied
  to the localization `R[σ] → Frac(R)[σ]` at the nonzero constants.
- `nilradical_algebraicClosure_slice_isPrime g hB hg hgi hdim`: let `𝓛 = Frac (Frac(R)[a])`.
  The universal hyperplane section `Slice R g X` over `𝒫`, base changed to the algebraic
  closure of `𝓛`, has prime nilradical. Proof: apply `hB` to the `Frac R`-algebra
  `Frac R ⊗[R] A` with generators `1 ⊗ gᵢ`. Geometric irreducibility of
  `Spec (Frac R ⊗[R] A) → Spec (Frac R)` follows from `hgi` through the Library's
  `geometricallyIrreducible_specMap_iff` and `cancelBaseChange`. Bertini then says the generic
  hyperplane section over `𝓛` is geometrically irreducible. A chain of `AlgEquiv`s from
  `Hyperplane.lean` (`sliceBaseChange`, `sliceCancelBaseChange`, `sliceEquivOfEq`) identifies
  its base change to the algebraic closure of `𝓛` with that of the universal section over `𝒫`.
- `exists_ne_zero_forall_nilradical_slice_isPrime g hB hg hgi hdim`: the result above. Proof:
  `spreading_of_domain_algebras` (with the Library's
  `GenericIrreducibilitySpreading.exists_ne_zero_tensor_nilradical_isPrime`) applies to the
  `𝒫`-algebra `Slice R g X` with fraction field `𝓛` of `𝒫`. It gives `c ≠ 0` such that
  `K ⊗[𝒫] Slice R g X` has prime nilradical for every field `K` over `𝒫` in which `c` does not
  vanish. For `β` with `c(β) ≠ 0`, take `K` with the `𝒫`-algebra structure `aeval β`, and
  identify `K ⊗[𝒫] Slice R g X` with `Slice R g β` by `sliceBaseChange`.
