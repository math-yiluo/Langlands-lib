# Affine étale groupoids in ring form without the closed immersion condition

Author: Worker 266. The coaction constructions repeat, under a weaker
hypothesis, constructions of the Library modules `GroupoidCoactionUniversal`
and `GroupoidCoactionExact` (Worker 101), `GroupoidCoactionCore`, and
`GroupoidCoactionSubmodule` (Worker 148).

## Purpose

The Library structure `RingEtaleEquivalenceRelation t` (module
`EtaleEquivalenceRelationComodule`) is the ring form of an affine étale
equivalence relation `Spec C ⇉ Spec B` in which `j = (t, s)` is a closed
immersion. Its field `adjoin_range_eq_top` says that `C` is generated by
`t(B)` as a `B`-algebra. The proof of NL also needs groupoids for which `j`
is only known to be a monomorphism, such as an affine first level `E₀`.

`RingEtaleGroupoid t` has the fields `refl`, `symm`, `trans`,
`etale_algebraMap` and `etale_t` of `RingEtaleEquivalenceRelation t`,
verbatim. The field `adjoin_range_eq_top` is replaced by `hom_ext`: two ring
maps `C →+* T` that agree after composition with `t` and with
`algebraMap B C` are equal. This is the ring form of "`j` is a
monomorphism", and it is the statement of the Library lemma
`RingEtaleEquivalenceRelation.hom_ext`. So
`RingEtaleEquivalenceRelation.toRingEtaleGroupoid` shows that every ring
étale equivalence relation is a ring étale groupoid. The name "groupoid"
refers to these groupoid laws. The structure is internal to this proof and
does not come from a definition in the literature.

## Declarations

In the namespace `RingEtaleGroupoid`:

* `flat`: the source algebra `C` is flat over `B`, since `algebraMap B C` is
  étale.
* `identity`: the identity point `C →ₐ[B] B` chosen by `refl` at the
  identity of `B`, with `identity_comp_t`.
* `composableComposite`: the composite of the two universal composable
  points of the Library ring `composableRing t`, chosen by `trans`, with
  `composableComposite_comp_t`. `composable_flat` shows that
  `composableRing t` is flat over `B`.
* `identity_factor` and `composableComposite_factor`: every identity point,
  and every composite of a composable pair, factors through the universal
  ones. They are proved by `hom_ext`.
* `coactionOfUniversal`: a `t`-semilinear additive map `θ : M →+ C ⊗[B] M`
  is a Library `GroupoidCoaction t M` as soon as the counit identity holds at
  `identity` and the cocycle identity holds at `composableComposite`. This
  repeats `GroupoidCoaction.ofUniversal` of `GroupoidCoactionUniversal`, with
  the same proof from the factorisation lemmas.
* `coactionOfInjective`: transport of a coaction along an injective
  equivariant linear map. It repeats `GroupoidCoaction.ofInjective` of
  `GroupoidCoactionExact`, using the flatness of `B → C` and of
  `B → composableRing t` to cancel the injective map.
* `core_le`, `pairComposite` (with `pairComposite_comp_t`), `core_stable`,
  `coreSemilinearMap` (with `coreSemilinearMap_subtype`), `coreCoaction`,
  `core_subtype_isHom` and `finite_core`: the core construction of
  `GroupoidCoactionCore` for a submodule `H ⊆ M`. They use the Library
  definitions `core`, `coreMap`, `PairRing`, `pairTensorMap` and their
  lemmas, which do not depend on the relation hypothesis. The core lies in
  `H`, is stable, inherits a coaction, and is finite over a Noetherian `B`
  when `H` is.
* `exists_finite_stableSubmodule`: over a Noetherian `B`, every element of a
  module with a coaction lies in a finite submodule that is stable in the
  sense of the Library predicate `GroupoidCoaction.IsStableSubmodule`. For
  affine groupoids this is the ring form of the exhaustion in Stacks Project
  Lemma 39.15.4 (Tag 07TU): a module on the groupoid is the union of coherent
  submodules on the groupoid. As in `exists_finite_subcoaction` of
  `GroupoidCoactionCore`, `H` is a finite submodule containing the
  coefficients of `θ m`, and the submodule is the core of `H`. The core plays
  the role of the module `F ∩ s_* t^* H` in the proof of Lemma 39.15.4. Its
  finiteness follows from `core_le` and the Noetherian hypothesis, in place of
  Lemma 39.15.3 (Tag 07TT).
* `submoduleCoaction` with `submoduleCoaction_subtype`: the coaction
  restricted to a stable submodule, built from the Library
  `submoduleSemilinearMap` of `GroupoidCoactionSubmodule` and
  `coactionOfInjective`.

## Provenance

In the Library modules listed above, `RingEtaleEquivalenceRelation` is used
only through `hom_ext`, flatness of the source map, and the points chosen by
`refl` and `trans`. Each proof here follows the corresponding Library proof
line by line, with `RingEtaleEquivalenceRelation` replaced by
`RingEtaleGroupoid`. The mathematics is the exhaustion of Stacks Project
Section 39.15 (Lemmas 39.15.1, 39.15.3 and 39.15.4, Tags 07TR, 07TT and 07TU)
in ring form, as in `GroupoidCoactionCore`.

## Scope

The module proves no statement about schemes. `RingEtaleGroupoidScheme`
attaches an étale equivalence relation to a ring étale groupoid and proves
the converse for affine étale equivalence relations.
