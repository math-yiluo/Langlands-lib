# An algebraic differential slicing criterion

`SmoothAlgebraDifferentialSliceStatement` is an internal algebraic ingredient for
the proof of `SmoothEquivalenceRelationEtaleSliceStatement`. It is derived from
Mathlib's definitions of smoothness, etaleness, and the cotangent complex of a
surjective algebra map; it is not a transcription of a separate literature
statement.

An `Algebra.Extension R S` supplies a surjective map from an algebra `P.Ring` to
`S`. Assume the source is smooth over `R`, its kernel is generated by finitely
many elements `f i`, and the vectors `1 ⊗ d(f i)` form a basis of
`S ⊗[P.Ring] Ω[P.Ring/R]`. The proposition asserts that `S` is etale over `R`.
The equations need only form a basis after tensoring with the quotient; no
freeness of the differentials on the whole source is assumed.

The intended application is a transverse closed slice in a smooth affine chart.
The proposition itself does not choose such a chart or equations, and makes no
claim of existence of a scheme slice. It is a statement awaiting a separate
proof.
