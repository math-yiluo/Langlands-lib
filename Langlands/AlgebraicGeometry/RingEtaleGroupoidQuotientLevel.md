# A Čech presentation of the relation ring at a finitely generated level

Author: Worker 266.

## Statement

`RingEtaleGroupoid.exists_bijective_level` has the following hypotheses:

* `t₀ : B₀ →+* C₀` is a ring étale groupoid with `C₀` of finite type over `B₀`;
* `θ'` is a multiplicative coaction (Library `GroupoidCoaction`) on a
  `B₀`-algebra `B'`;
* `A₁ → B₁` is flat, `B₁` is of finite type over `ℤ`, and `φ : B₁ →+* B'` is a
  ring map; `B'` is an `A₁`-algebra through `φ ∘ algebraMap A₁ B₁`;
* `L : B₁ ⊗[A₁] B' →+* C₀ ⊗[B₀] B'` is bijective and
  `L (b ⊗ d) = θ' (φ b) * (1 ⊗ d)`.

Then there is a finite set `s ⊆ B'` with the following property. Let `D' ⊆ B'`
be a stable subalgebra (`GroupoidCoaction.IsStableSubalgebra`) containing `s`.
Then `φ` factors as `φ' : B₁ →+* D'`, and there is a bijective ring map
`F : B₁ ⊗[A₁] D' →+* C₀ ⊗[B₀] D'` with `F (b ⊗ d) = θ_{D'} (φ' b) * (1 ⊗ d)`,
where `θ_{D'}` is the restricted coaction `RingEtaleGroupoid.subalgebraCoaction`.

In the proof of NL, `B'` is the coordinate ring of the cover `E'`. `L` comes from
an affine quotient of `E'` (with `A₁ → B₁` étale and faithfully flat) and the
identification `Γ(E'.R) ≅ C₀ ⊗[B₀] B'`. The conclusion is the input of the
criterion `EtaleEquivalenceRelation.hasAffineQuotient_of_bijective` (module
`EtaleEquivalenceRelationKernelPair`) for the level relation of `D'`.

## Proof

The set `s` consists of two parts:

* the images under `φ` of finitely many ring generators of `B₁`. For `D' ⊇ s`,
  `φ⁻¹(D')` is a subring containing the generators, so `φ(B₁) ⊆ D'`;
* `B₀`-algebra generators `c` of `C₀` give preimages `w_c` of `c ⊗ 1` under
  `L`. `TensorProduct.exists_finite_submodule_right_of_setFinite` gives a
  finite `A₁`-submodule `N ⊆ B'` with all `w_c` in the image of
  `B₁ ⊗[A₁] N`. `s` contains generators of `N`, so `N ⊆ D'`, since `D'` is
  closed under the action of `A₁` through `φ(B₁) ⊆ D'`.

The image of `A₁` is invariant: `θ' (φ a) = 1 ⊗ φ a`. This follows from the
formula for `L` and `a ⊗ 1 = 1 ⊗ a` in `B₁ ⊗[A₁] B'`. The invariance also holds
in `C₀ ⊗[B₀] D'`, because `C₀ ⊗[B₀] D' → C₀ ⊗[B₀] B'` is injective (`C₀` is
flat over `B₀`). Hence `F` is defined as the ring map `b ⊗ d ↦ θ_{D'} (φ' b) *
(1 ⊗ d)` (`RingEtaleGroupoid.tensorLift`). After the injection into
`C₀ ⊗[B₀] B'`, `F` becomes `L` composed with `B₁ ⊗[A₁] D' → B₁ ⊗[A₁] B'`. That
map is injective because `B₁` is flat over `A₁`, so `F` is injective. The image
of `F` is a subring containing every `1 ⊗ d`. It also contains `c ⊗ 1` for each
generator `c`: the image of `w_c` in `B₁ ⊗[A₁] D'` maps to `c ⊗ 1`. These
elements contain `b ⊗ 1` for every `b` in the subalgebra generated by the `c`,
which is `C₀`. So the image of `F` contains all pure tensors, and `F` is
surjective.

## Provenance and scope

Step 4 of the ring-level route in the task's `context.md` (Stacks Project
Lemma 70.5.10, Tag 07SQ, in ring form) needs the comparison map
`B''_D ⊗[A'_D] D' → C₀ ⊗[B₀] D'` to be an isomorphism at a level. `context.md`
obtains this by a colimit argument over finitely presented algebras. This
module proves the needed special case directly. The ring `A₁` stays fixed,
in place of the invariants `A'_D` of the levels, and finitely many elements of
`D'` are chosen by hand. Flatness of `B₁` gives injectivity, and generators of
`C₀` give surjectivity. The argument is internal to this proof.
