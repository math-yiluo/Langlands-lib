# Standard factors of determinant twists of spherical representations

`SphericalStandardTwistFactorsStatement` starts with an irreducible Langlands quotient of an ordered normalized unramified principal series over a finite extension of ℚₚ with its canonical topology. The input includes a representative equivalent to its determinant twist by a continuous quasicharacter and a representative of the trivial character of GL₁. At a supplied nontrivial unitary additive character and a self-dual Haar measure, the statement evaluates a polynomial and an integer exponent given by the existing Langlands pair factor certificates for these representatives. The polynomial is the product of the Tate polynomials of the twisted inducing characters, and the exponent is the sum of their Tate exponents. The common twisting character may be ramified.

A common character multiplies the norms of the uniformizer values by the same scalar, preserving the order of the Langlands data. The twist equivalence for normalized principal series carries the surjection onto the original quotient to a surjection onto its twist. The rank-one and Langlands pair factor theorems then give the product and sum. This is the standard-factor case of the multiplicativity used at auxiliary places in Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, Lemma 6.13.
