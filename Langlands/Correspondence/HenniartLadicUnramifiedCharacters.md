# Unramified ℓ-adic Galois characters and their Frobenius values

`Langlands.Henniart.LocalNumerical.ladicUnramifiedCharacterEquiv`
identifies continuous unramified \(\ell\)-adic Galois characters with
values in finite extensions of \(\mathbb Q_\ell\) with the units of
norm one in \(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\). The map is evaluation at a
geometric Frobenius lift.

For existence, choose the finite extension generated by the given
scalar. Its norm-one units form a compact totally disconnected group.
The powers of the scalar extend from the integers to the profinite
integers and then pull back along the residue degree of the absolute
Galois group. This also gives the finite coefficient extension of the
constructed character.

The restriction of each of these characters to the Weil group is the
geometric degree power of its value at Frobenius. Density of the
projection to the absolute Galois group gives uniqueness. The image of
a continuous character of a compact group has bounded norms, so
application to powers of an element and its inverse forces norm one.
[ℓ-adic classes and twists](HenniartLadicClasses.md)

The construction derives the unramified character interface used in
the \(\ell\)-adic comparison in Henniart's numerical correspondence.
