Let \(r=n/f\) and \(J=\lfloor j/f\rfloor\). Lean counts the coefficient tuples
in `GeneratingTuples` over the degree-\(f\) extension of the base field \(k\)
of size \(q\). These tuples have indices \(0,\ldots,J\), a nonzero constant
coefficient, and coefficients that generate the extension over the base field.

## Count by support

`assembleTuple` inserts the coefficients of a polar part with parameters
\((r/d,m)\) at the indices \(d i\), for \(d\mid r\) and \(m\le J/d\).
It fills the other indices with zeros, which preserves the generated field.
The gcd of the positive support indices scales by \(d\), so the polar part's
coprimality condition makes \(d\) recoverable as the gcd of \(r\) and the
positive support. The last nonzero index is \(dm\). For the inverse, Lean
computes these two invariants and restricts to the indices \(d i\) up to
that last index. A tuple supported only at zero has \(d=r\) and \(m=0\),
using the published convention that the gcd of the empty positive support
is zero. `card_generatingTuples_eq_sum` takes the cardinality of this
bijection to obtain a sum of coefficient-tuple counts.

`polar_stabilizer_eq_bot` shows that an automorphism fixing the tuple fixes
the field generated by its entries, hence is the identity.
`card_polarParts` uses `MulAction.selfEquivOrbitsQuotientProd` to express
each tuple count as the order of the Galois group times the orbit count.
The group has order \(f\). The map \(m\mapsto fm\) is a bijection from the
range \(m\le J/d\) to the indices \(s\) satisfying \(f\mid s\) and \(ds\le j\).
The latter inequality also implies \(s\le j\).
For \(d\mid n/f\), one has \(f\mid n/d\) and \((n/d)/f=(n/f)/d\), so the
published `polarPartCount` at \((q,n/d,f,s)\) is the orbit count in this
summand.

## Count by generated subfield

Over a degree-\(t\) extension, a nonzero constant coefficient and \(J\)
arbitrary coefficients give \((q^t-1)q^{tJ}\) tuples. Lean partitions these
tuples by the intermediate field generated over the base by their
coefficients. `subfieldDegreeEquiv` indexes the intermediate fields by the
divisors of \(t\). A \(k\)-embedding between intermediate fields of the same
degree exists by `FiniteField.nonempty_algHom_of_finrank_dvd`. Normality makes its image
in the ambient field equal to the source subfield, and equal degrees
then give equality of the two subfields. For each divisor of \(t\),
an embedding of Mathlib's chosen extension of that degree supplies an
intermediate field of the required degree.

An algebra equivalence transports generating tuples, so the total count
is the divisor sum of the generating-tuple counts by degree.
`card_generatingTuples_eq_moebius` casts that divisor-sum relation to
\(\mathbb Z\), applies `ArithmeticFunction.sum_eq_iff_sum_mul_moebius_eq`,
and reindexes the divisor pairs as \((f/i,i)\). At degree \(f\), the result is
\(\sum_{i\mid f}\mu(f/i)(q^i-1)q^{iJ}\). The theorem identifies the two counts
after computing the cardinality of `polarBaseField q` from the prime-power
hypothesis.
