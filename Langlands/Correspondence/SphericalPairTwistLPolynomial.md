# Pair L-polynomials of determinant twists of spherical representations

`SphericalPairTwistLPolynomialStatement` concerns the local Langlands pair L-polynomial for two spherical representations after a determinant twist of the first member by a continuous quasicharacter. If the twisting character is trivial on integral units, the conclusion substitutes its uniformizer value times the polynomial variable into the pair Euler polynomial of the original two Satake spectra. If the character is ramified, the conclusion gives the constant polynomial one. The representations, twist relation, additive character, and self-dual measure are those of the local pair-factor interface.

`sphericalPairTwistLPolynomial` chooses ordered unramified rank-one inducing characters for both spherical representations. The twist carries the first quotient to the Langlands datum obtained by twisting each of its inducing characters. The square-integrable data formula expresses the pair L-polynomial as the product of the rank-one pair polynomials. Each is the Tate polynomial of the product of the two inducing characters and the twisting character \(\omega\). The unramified inducing characters are trivial on integral units, so they leave the ramification of this product unchanged. In the unramified case the substitution \(X \mapsto \omega(\varpi) X\) commutes with the finite product, where \(\varpi\) is the uniformizer.

The product formula comes from Jacquet, Piatetski-Shapiro and Shalika, *Rankin–Selberg convolutions* (1983), Theorem (9.5), pp. 462–463. The rank-one formula is in Tate, *Number theoretic background* (1979), §(3.1.3), p. 13. This computation supplies the L-factor part for spherical pairs at auxiliary places. Henniart and Herb, *Automorphic induction for GL(n) (over local non-archimedean fields)* (1995), §8.16, p. 183, use highly ramified twists to compare general local pair gamma factors around equations (16)–(19).
