# Strict target trait and residue action

Use the rational end ring `R = endRing k` with uniformizer `t`, fraction field
`K' = endField k` and chosen separable closure `Ω_s = endGenericClosure k`.
The maximal unramified field `K^nr = endUnramifiedField k` is the subextension
generated by the geometric constants. Its valuation ring `R^nr = endStrictRing k`
is defined by integral closure. The construction uses the same uniformizer
and underlying ring at each of the rational Fourier ends. Its spectrum is
the pointed strict henselization `S^nr` in SGA 7 XIII, (2.1.3)–(2.1.4), p. 100.
The middle base of the Cartesian diagram in (1.3.2.1), p. 94, is `S̄`, the
spectrum of the normalization in `Ω_s` defined in (0.2.5), p. 86. The geometric
points in that diagram factor through `S^nr` by the map `S̄ → S^nr` induced by
the inclusion of integral closures. Laumon (1987), (2.1.1), p. 151, describes
the pointed strict henselization by extension of constants. The local charts
at the corresponding ends appear in (2.4.1)–(2.4.2.1), p. 162.

## Constructions

`strictUniformizer` is the image of `t` in `R^nr`. The map `strictResidue` is the
quotient by `strictMaximalIdeal = (t)`. It extends the end trait's reduction
via `strictResidueBaseHom`, as recorded by `strictResidue_comp_endStrictRingHom`.
The map `strictConstantsHom` includes the geometric constants in `R^nr`.
The constants lie in `K^nr` by the definition of that subfield, and their monic
polynomials over `k` give monic polynomials over `R`. Composing with reduction
gives `strictConstantsResidue`.

`strictTargetTrait` uses `NearbyCyclesModel.ofTrait` on `strictGenericHom`, the
inclusion `R^nr → Ω_s`, and `strictResidue`. Its total space is `Spec R^nr`.
Its generic and special maps are the spectra of those two ring maps. Every
`σ ∈ Gal(Ω_s/K')` preserves the geometric constants, hence `K^nr`. The map
`unramifiedGalois` is that restriction. It preserves integrality over `R`, giving
`strictRingGalois`, and fixes the generator of `(t)`, giving `strictResidueGalois`
by reduction. The commuting maps on the generic and special rings give
`strictTargetTraitAut` via `NearbyCyclesModel.ofTraitHom`. The geometric generic
point retains the action on `Ω_s` even for an element that acts trivially on
`R^nr` and its residue. See SGA 7 XIII, (0.2.5), p. 86, and (1.3.2.2), p. 94.

## `StrictTargetTraitGaloisStatement`

- For finite `k`, `R^nr` is a strictly henselian DVR with maximal ideal `(t)`
  and fraction field `K^nr`. The equality with the subring generated by `R`
  and the geometric constants expresses the directed union of the constant
  stages. Every expression uses finitely many constants, contained in one
  finite extension of `k` in the chosen geometric constants. Reduction of the
  geometric constants is bijective, identifying the special point with `Spec k̄`.
  See SGA 7 XIII, (0.2.5), p. 86, and (2.1.3), p. 100.
- Restriction to `R^nr` and to its residue respects multiplication in
  `Gal(Ω_s/K')`. Both kernels are the fixing subgroup of `K^nr`, which is
  `Gal(Ω_s/K^nr)`. Every residue-ring endomorphism fixing `k` lifts to
  `Gal(Ω_s/K')`. This is the inertia sequence of SGA 7 XIII, (0.2.5), p. 86,
  on the chosen model.
- Under `completionRestriction`, an isomorphism with the algebraic closure
  of the completed residue field intertwines the residue actions. For a
  completed Weil element of degree `n`, the induced map on the strict residue
  is geometric Frobenius to the power `n`. The equality uses nonnegative
  exponents on both sides. These clauses specialize the residue and degree
  comparisons in `Completion` through `strictConstantsResidue`. They use the
  inertia and residue convention of SGA 7 XIII, (0.2.5), p. 86, and the
  henselian trait convention of Laumon, (2.1.1), p. 151.
