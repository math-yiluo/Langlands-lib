# The absolute Galois group of a henselian end and of its completion

`Langlands.Henniart.Laumon.henselianCompletionGalois` proves
`HenselianCompletionGaloisStatement`. For every field `k`, the homomorphism
`completionRestriction k` is a homeomorphism for the Krull topologies.
[Statement and canonical restriction](Completion.md)

Write `F = LaurentSeries k`, `K = endField k`, `L = SeparableClosure F` and
`N = endGenericClosure k`. Then `K` is the relative separable closure of `k(t)` in `F`, so
`separableClosure K F = ⊥` by `separableClosure.separableClosure_eq_bot`. The dense inclusion
`k(t) → F` from `LaurentSeries.coe_range_dense` factors through `K`, making `K → F` dense as well.

The rank-one norm from `Langlands.LocalField.Laurent.valuationRankOne` induces the existing
Laurent-series uniformity. In this norm `F` is complete and ultrametric. The dense-subfield
generation theorem identifies the field generated by `N` over `F` with `separableClosure F L`,
which is all of `L`. [Separable generation over a dense subfield](../../FieldTheory/DenseSeparableGeneration.md)

The restriction theorem for scalar towers now applies to the given `N ⊆ L`. It gives
surjectivity from relative separable closedness, injectivity from generation of `L`, and
the homeomorphism from continuity and compactness. The restriction in that theorem is
`completionRestriction k`. [Restriction along a scalar tower](../../Galois/RestrictAlongTower.md)

Katz uses the general comparison with completion in (1.4.8), pp. 81–82, for the union of
Laurent-series fields over finite Galois constant extensions. This proof establishes the
comparison for the end field defined as the relative separable closure of `k(t)`.
See also Serre, *Local Fields*, Chapter II, §3, Theorem 1 and Corollary 4, pp. 30–31, for the
finite-extension and Galois comparison with completion.
