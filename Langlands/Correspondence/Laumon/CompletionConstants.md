# Constants of the henselian end field and the residue field of `k((t))`

The module `Langlands.Correspondence.Laumon.CompletionConstants` proves clauses 1–3 of
`Langlands.Henniart.Laumon.HenselianCompletionRamificationStatement`
(`Langlands.Correspondence.Laumon.Completion`): clauses 2 and 3 for every finite field \(k\),
clause 1 under the hypothesis that `completionRestriction k` is surjective. Write
\(X=\)`LaurentSeries k` (with the Library's `Langlands.LocalField.Laurent.valuativeRel` and
local-field structure), \(\Omega_X=\)`SeparableClosure X`, \(\Omega=\)`endGenericClosure k`,
\(K=\)`endField k`, \(q=\#k\), \(\mathbb A=\)`closureIntegers X Ω_X` (the integers of
`closureValuation X Ω_X`) and \(r=\)`completionRestriction k`.

## Restriction

- `completionRestriction_apply`: \(r(\sigma)\) acts on \(\Omega\subset\Omega_X\) as \(\sigma\)
  (`AlgEquiv.restrictNormalHom_apply`).

## The constants are the Teichmüller representatives (section `Constants`)

The constants `endConstants k` are the algebraic closure of \(k\) in \(\Omega\).

- `card_cast_eq_zero` gives \(q=0\) in \(\Omega_X\). For \(d>0\),
  `card_pow_sub_one_cast` gives \(q^d-1=-1\) in \(\Omega_X\).
- `endConstants_pow_eq_one` shows that a nonzero constant is a root of unity of order dividing
  \(q^d-1\) for some \(d>0\) (the Library's `Separable.exists_pow_card_pow_sub_one_eq_one`).
- `mem_endGenericClosure_of_pow_eq_one`, `constantOfPowEqOne`: a root of unity in
  \(\Omega_X\) of order \(N\) with \(N\ne 0\) in \(\Omega_X\) is separable over \(K\)
  (its minimal polynomial divides the separable \(X^N-1\)), hence lies in \(\Omega\), and is a
  constant.
- `completionRestriction_mem_endConstants`, `restrictedConstant`: \(r(\sigma)\) maps constants
  to constants.
- `constant_mem_closureIntegers`: constants are integral (`Separable.mem_integers_of_pow_eq_one`).
- `constantsResidue`: reduction \(\rho\) of the constants into the residue field of
  \(\mathbb A\), a ring homomorphism from a field, hence injective
  (`constantsResidue_injective`).
- `constantsResidue_surjective`: every nonzero residue class \(c\) satisfies
  \(c^{q_X^d-1}=1\) for some \(d>0\), \(q_X\) the residue cardinality of \(X\); the Library's
  `Separable.exists_teich` gives a primitive root of unity \(\zeta\in\mathbb A\) of that order,
  whose reduction is primitive (`Separable.isPrimitiveRoot_residue`), so \(c\) is the
  reduction of a power of \(\zeta\), which is a constant by `constantOfPowEqOne`
  (\(q_X^d-1\) is a unit of \(\mathbb A\), `Separable.teichOrder_isUnit`).
- `constantsResidue_map`: \(\rho(r(\sigma)a)\) is the Library's residue action
  `residueHom X Ω_X σ` applied to \(\rho(a)\) (`residueHom_apply`).
- `constantsResidueEquiv`, `constantsClosureEquiv`: \(\rho\) as a ring isomorphism, and its
  composite with the Library's `residueClosureEquiv X Ω_X` onto
  `AlgebraicClosure (localResidueField X)`.
- `constantsClosureEquiv_map`: this isomorphism \(\varphi\) intertwines \(r(\sigma)\) with
  `absoluteResidueHom X Ω_X σ`, which is `residueHom X Ω_X σ` transported through
  `residueClosureEquiv` (`residueTransport`, `semilinearAutCongr`).

These are the identifications described in the statement's Markdown: reduction maps the
constants isomorphically onto the residue field of \(\Omega_X\). Each nonzero residue class
has a Teichmüller representative of order prime to \(p\).

## Clauses 1 and 2

- `mem_absoluteInertia_iff`: \(\sigma\) lies in the Library's
  `absoluteInertia X Ω_X` (the kernel of `residueHom`) exactly when \(r(\sigma)\) fixes every
  constant: one direction is the injectivity of \(\rho\) with `constantsResidue_map`, the other
  the surjectivity of \(\rho\) with `mem_inertia_iff`.
- `mem_fixingSubgroup_endUnramifiedField_iff`: an automorphism of \(\Omega\) over \(K\) fixes
  `endUnramifiedField k`, the field generated over \(K\) by the constants, exactly when it fixes
  every constant. If it fixes every constant, the fixed field of the subgroup generated by the
  automorphism contains the constants and their adjunction to \(K\).
- `absoluteInertia_map_completionRestriction` (**clause 1**): if \(r\) is surjective, the image
  of `absoluteInertia X Ω_X` under \(r\) is `(endUnramifiedField k).fixingSubgroup`.
- `exists_constants_residue_equiv` (**clause 2**): the existential of clause 2, witnessed by
  `constantsClosureEquiv k`.

## Clause 3 (sections `Frobenius` and `Degree`)

- `geometricFrobenius_zpow_apply_pow`: on the algebraic closure of a finite field \(F\) with
  \(q_F\) elements, \(g=\)`geometricFrobenius F ^ n` satisfies
  \(g(y)^{q_F^{n^+}}=y^{q_F^{n^-}}\), with \(n^+=\)`n.toNat` and \(n^-=\)`(-n).toNat`; it uses
  that arithmetic Frobenius is \(y\mapsto y^{q_F}\) (`arithmetic_apply`).
- `absoluteResidueHom_localProjection`: for \(w\) in the Library's local Weil group
  `localWeilGroup X Ω_X` (the pullback of `localResidueDegree X Ω_X` along `frobenius`),
  `absoluteResidueHom` of `localProjection X Ω_X w` is `geometricFrobenius` to the power
  `localDegreeInt X Ω_X w`; this is the pullback condition and
  `profiniteEquivalence_symm_frobenius`, as `localResidueDegree` is `toProfiniteIntegers`
  composed with the residue map.
- `completionRestriction_localProjection_pow` (**clause 3**): with \(n=\)`localDegreeInt X Ω_X w`
  and \(\sigma=\)`localProjection X Ω_X w`, every constant \(a\) satisfies
  \(r(\sigma)(a)^{q^{n^+}}=a^{q^{n^-}}\). Apply \(\varphi\), use `constantsClosureEquiv_map`, the
  two previous lemmas and the residue cardinality `LocalField.Laurent.residueCardinality_eq`
  (\(q_X=q\)), and the injectivity of \(\varphi\).
