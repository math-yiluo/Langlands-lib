# Density of the henselian elements in the finite levels of `k((t))`

The module `Langlands.Correspondence.Laumon.CompletionDensity` shows that the henselian
elements of a finite Galois level of `LaurentSeries k` are dense in it, assuming that
restriction `completionRestriction k` is a homeomorphism (the content of
`HenselianCompletionGaloisStatement`, `Langlands.Correspondence.Laumon.Completion`). Notation:
\(X=\)`LaurentSeries k` for a finite field \(k\), \(\Omega_X=\)`SeparableClosure X`,
\(K=\)`endField k`, \(\Omega=\)`endGenericClosure k`, \(r=\)`completionRestriction k`, and
\(v=\)`closureValuation X Ω_X`, the Library's valuation of \(\Omega_X\) extending that of \(X\).

## Approximation in \(\mathbb R_{\ge 0}\)

- `mul_lt_of_lt_div_add_one`: \(d<\gamma/(a+1)\) implies \(ad<\gamma\);
  `div_add_one_pos`: \(\gamma/(a+1)>0\) for \(\gamma>0\).

## The end field is dense in \(X\)

- `exists_endField_closureValuation_lt`: for \(a\in X\) and \(\gamma>0\) there is \(c\in K\)
  with \(v(a-c)<\gamma\). Rational functions are dense in Laurent series
  (`LaurentSeries.coe_range_dense`), the Library's normed structure `localNormedField X` has the
  topology of \(X\), \(v\) restricts on \(X\) to that norm (`spectralNorm_extends`), and
  \(k(t)\subset K\).

## The henselian elements of a finite level

- `henselianLevel L` is \(L\cap\Omega\), as a subfield of \(\Omega_X\), for a finite Galois
  intermediate field \(L\) of \(\Omega_X/X\); `mem_henselianLevel` unfolds it.
- `le_adjoin_henselianLevel`: if \(r\) is a homeomorphism, \(L\) is contained in the field
  generated over \(X\) by \(L\cap\Omega\). By infinite Galois theory
  (`InfiniteGalois.fixedField_fixingSubgroup`) it suffices that every \(\sigma\) fixing
  \(L\cap\Omega\) fixes \(L\). The image \(H=r(\mathrm{Gal}(\Omega_X/L))\) is closed, as \(r\)
  is a closed map, so \(H\) is the fixing group of its fixed field
  (`InfiniteGalois.fixingSubgroup_fixedField`); that fixed field consists of elements of
  \(\Omega\) fixed by \(\mathrm{Gal}(\Omega_X/L)\), hence lies in \(L\cap\Omega\). So
  \(r(\sigma)\in H\), \(r(\sigma)=r(\sigma')\) with \(\sigma'\) fixing \(L\), and \(\sigma=\sigma'\)
  by injectivity.
- `exists_henselianLevel_closureValuation_lt` (**density**): if \(r\) is a homeomorphism, for
  every \(x\in L\) and \(\gamma>0\) there is \(b\in L\cap\Omega\) with \(v(x-b)<\gamma\). The
  elements approximable in this sense form an \(X\)-submodule of \(\Omega_X\): for
  \(a\in X\) approximate \(x\) by \(b\) and then \(a\) by \(c\in K\), and use
  \(ax-cb=a(x-b)+(a-c)b\) with \(cb\in L\cap\Omega\). It contains \(L\cap\Omega\), hence its
  \(X\)-span, which is the algebra, and also the field, generated by \(L\cap\Omega\) over \(X\)
  (`IntermediateField.adjoin_toSubalgebra_of_isAlgebraic`, `Algebra.adjoin_eq_span`), which
  contains \(L\).

In the terms of Serre, *Local Fields*, II §3, Theorem 1 and its Corollary 1 (pp. 30–31), the
finite level \(L\) is the completion of \(L\cap\Omega\) and the composite of \(X\) and
\(L\cap\Omega\): \(L=X\cdot(L\cap\Omega)\), and \(L\cap\Omega\) is dense in \(L\). Every declaration of the module depends only on `propext`,
`Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.
