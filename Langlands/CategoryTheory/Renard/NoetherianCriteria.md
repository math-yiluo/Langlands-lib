## Implemented proof

`noetherianMathlibComparison` unwraps Mathlib's `ObjectProperty.Is` wrapper around `WellFoundedGT (Subobject X)`. `WellFounded.wellFoundedGT_iff_exists_maximal` identifies this with the existence of a maximal member in every nonempty subset. In the actual partial order, its maximality conclusion `P ≤ Q → Q ≤ P` is equivalent by antisymmetry to the source-facing `P ≤ Q → Q = P`. This proves both directions of the internal comparison without assuming a Noetherianity instance.

`noetherianSequenceStabilization` uses that comparison and `CategoryTheory.isNoetherianObject_iff_monotone_chain_condition`. Mathlib's theorem quantifies over order homomorphisms from naturals into the actual subobject order. The proof passes between an order homomorphism and its underlying monotone function, preserving every value and index. Its conclusion is one index `n` such that `f n = f m` for every `m ≥ n`.

`noetherianChainMaximality` restricts the source subset condition to nonempty chains in the forward direction. Conversely, the range of every monotone natural sequence is a nonempty chain, by `Monotone.isChain_range`. A maximal member of that range equals some `f n`; monotonicity places every later `f m` above it, so maximality forces `f m = f n`. Symmetry gives the required orientation of equality. The already proved sequence criterion then yields source Noetherianity. Thus the chain criterion is derived without a union or completeness assumption.

`noetherianCriteria` combines the Mathlib comparison, chain criterion and sequence criterion in precisely the target's right-associated order. Each component is also available as a separately named theorem in `Langlands.Renard`.
