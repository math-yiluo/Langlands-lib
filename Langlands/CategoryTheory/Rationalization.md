# Rationalization of a preadditive category

This is a derived algebraic construction for any preadditive category C, with independent object and morphism universes. `Langlands.Rationalization C` contains exactly a wrapped object of C. Its morphisms `Rationalization.Hom X Y` are the actual tensor product Q tensor_Z Hom_C(X,Y), and `homTensorComm` is the integral linear tensor-commutation equivalence to Hom_C(X,Y) tensor_Z Q. Its pure-tensor formula is explicit.

`comp` is `TensorProduct.map₂` applied to rational multiplication and the integral bilinear composition map of C. Thus (q tensor f) followed by (r tensor g) is qr tensor (f followed by g). The identity is 1 tensor id. Tensor induction proves the two identity laws and associativity, reducing the pure-tensor case to rational associativity and associativity in C. These proofs construct the category instance. The resulting category is preadditive and Q-linear: additivity comes from the tensor construction, and additive maps between Q-vector spaces commute with rational scaling. `compLinear` bundles the same operation as a Q-bilinear map.

`ofHom` is the canonical integral linear map f ↦ 1 tensor f. `inclusion` is the corresponding additive functor from C to its rationalization. `End X` is the actual categorical endomorphism ring of the object `of X`, with its canonical Q-algebra structure supplied by Mathlib for a Q-linear category. Multiplication is reverse categorical composition: f*g means g followed by f. `ofEnd` is the canonical unital ring map. `endTensor` lets pure tensors retain their endomorphism type during elaboration; `end_mul_tmul` gives (q tensor f)*(r tensor g) = qr tensor (f*g). `algebraMap_eq` identifies q in this algebra with q tensor id.

`postAction X Y` is the Q-algebra homomorphism from End(Y) to the actual Q-linear endomorphisms of Hom(X,Y), obtained by postcomposition. `preAction X Y a` is precomposition by a in End(X). It sends the unit to the identity and reverses multiplication: preAction(a*a')(f) = preAction(a')(preAction(a)(f)). The two actions commute by category associativity. The formulas `postAction_ofEnd` and `preAction_ofEnd` recover integral composition under the canonical maps. Each action is constructed from composition, with all laws proved; no desired conclusions are input data.

Provenance is internal and derived from Mathlib's tensor universal property, rational linearity of additive maps, and the categorical endomorphism algebra. The generic module requires no geometry. Its intended abelian-scheme application supplies the rational endomorphism algebra used in Harris–Taylor, *The Geometry and Cohomology of Some Simple Shimura Varieties* (2001), III.1, printed pages 89–91 (PDF 97–99), source `harris-taylor-2001-geometry-cohomology-simple-shimura`, SHA256 `5be28164b61d6f6cc28dd9e6b990d669504f3470860b230ebbc97e3da10af56b`. The original pages were inspected: page 89 uses an embedding into End^0(A), and page 91 uses End^0(A) and equivalence through an isogeny. They motivate the application and are not asserted to prove these generic algebraic construction laws. No corresponding project errata file was present when inspected.

This module does not claim that rational morphisms are scheme morphisms, that inclusion is faithful, or that geometric isogenies are invertible. The actual abelian-scheme specialization requires the independently published abelian-scheme category. In particular, this rationalization is not identified here with localization at all geometric isogenies over arbitrary bases.
