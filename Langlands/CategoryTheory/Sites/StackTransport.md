# Stack transport under pullback-preserving reindexing

Let `G : C ⥤ D` preserve pullbacks and let
`F : LocallyDiscrete Dᵒᵖ ⥤ᵖ Cat` be an arbitrary category-valued
pseudofunctor. Write `reindex G F` for `G.op.toPseudofunctor.comp F`.
For an indexed family `f i : X i ⟶ S`, the construction
`toDescentDataIso` identifies

`(reindex G F).toDescentData f ⋙ (descentDataEquivalence G F sq sq₃).functor`

with `F.toDescentData (fun i ↦ G.map (f i))`. As in `StackPullback`,
`sq` and `sq₃` are chosen pair and triple pullbacks. This comparison uses
the actual canonical global-to-descent functors, including their composition
isomorphisms.

The lemma `isStack_reindex` then proves that if `C` has pullbacks,
`J` and `K` are Grothendieck topologies on `C` and `D`,
`CoverPreserving J K G` holds, and `F.IsStack K` holds, then
`(reindex G F).IsStack J` holds. It places no groupoid hypothesis on
the fibres and no extra limit hypothesis on `D`. The object and morphism
universes of both base categories and the fibres are independent.

The proof begins with `ofObj_hom_reindex`, identifying the transition maps
in the descent datum of a global object via `mapComp'_reindex`.
In the chosen-pullback descriptions, the natural isomorphism
`toDescentDataPrimeIso` has identity component morphisms. Its compatibility
is exactly the transition-map identity; naturality follows from the identity
laws. Composing with the equivalence from chosen-pullback descent data back
to unrestricted descent data and its counit gives `toDescentDataIso`.
Equivalence of the mapped canonical descent functor therefore implies
equivalence of the original one, by cancellation of the descent-category
equivalence.

The construction `chosenPullback` chooses the usual categorical pullback.
`chosenPullback₃` takes the pullback of the two middle projections of any
three chosen pair pullbacks. Equality of their composites to the middle
object and then to the common base gives the lift to the first–third
pair pullback. Thus `HasPullbacks C` supplies all the choices needed for
the comparison.

For each covering sieve `R` on `C`, cover preservation makes its image
sieve cover in `D`. This image sieve is generated by the images of the
arrows of `R`, so the stack property of `F` makes the mapped canonical
descent functor an equivalence. The comparison just proved transfers this
equivalence to `reindex G F`. Mathlib's `IsStack.of_isStackFor` then
supplies the full stack property, including the prestack condition.

This is internal categorical infrastructure derived from `StackPullback`
and the pinned Mathlib definitions and lemmas in
`CategoryTheory/Sites/Descent/IsStack.lean`,
`CategoryTheory/Sites/CoverPreserving.lean`, and
`CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/ChosenPullback.lean`.
It is not a translation of a separate literature assertion. In the
line-bundle fpqc stack task it transports a global stack property to a
relative pseudofunctor once its base-change functor meets the two
preservation hypotheses. It does not itself establish global fpqc descent
for line bundles.
