# Eigenvalues of central norm-kernel points have spectral norm one

Author: Worker 1532. Uses `SpectralBlock` by Worker 1045.

Namespace: `Langlands.BruhatTits.RationalPoints.CentralNormKernel`. Internal helper for the
proof of `CentralNormKernelCompactnessStatement`. Norms on a nonarchimedean local field `F` are
those of the published `LocalGalois.localNormedField F`, whose topology is the given one, and
which is ultrametric and complete (`localNormedField_ultrametric`, `localNormedField_complete`).

- `centralBlockCharacter ρ hz P hP` (any field `F`): for a central rational point `z` of `M` and
  a polynomial `P` with `π = P(ρ(z))` idempotent, the rational character
  `g ↦ det (ρ(g) π + (1 - π))`. It is the published `blockCharacter` of `π`; `π` lies in the
  algebra generated by `ρ(z)` (`aeval_mem_adjoin_singleton`), hence in the central algebra, so
  `commute_scalarMap_of_mem` supplies the commutation hypothesis.
- `rationalCharacterEval_centralBlockCharacter`: its value at a rational point `x` is
  `det (ρ(x) π + (1 - π))` (via `comp_blockCharacter`, `blockDetPoints_val` and
  `ΓSpecIso_det_blockMatrix`).
- `norm_eq_one_of_fieldNormUnits_eq_one`: a unit `a` with `Plancherel.fieldNormUnits F a = 1`
  has `‖a‖ = 1`. By `Plancherel.fieldNormUnits_ker` it lies in `FML.unitGroup F`, so its
  valuation is `1`, and `localNormedField_le` compares norms with valuations.
- `spectralNorm_eq_one_of_mem_normKernel`: if `z` is a scheme-centre point
  (`GroupScheme.schemeCenterPoints M`) in `Plancherel.rationalCommonNormKernel M`, then every root
  `c` in `AlgebraicClosure F` of the characteristic polynomial of `ρ(z)` has
  `spectralNorm F (AlgebraicClosure F) c = 1`. Worker 1045's `exists_blockProjector_spectralNorm`
  gives `P` with `π = P(ρ(z))` idempotent and `‖det (ρ(z) π + (1 - π))‖ = spectralNorm c ^ b`
  with `b ≥ 1`. The left side is the norm of the value at `z` of `centralBlockCharacter`, which is
  `1` because `z` is in the norm kernel (`Plancherel.mem_rationalCommonNormKernel`). A
  nonnegative real whose positive power is `1` is `1`.
