# Open normal subgroups contain dynamic unipotent points

Author: Worker 1374.

This internal helper is derived from the published actual-point contraction theorem
`Langlands.BruhatTits.DynamicUnipotentContainment.exists_conj_mem` in
`Langlands.BruhatTits.Unipotent.CompactOpenContainmentProof`. Its provenance is
that theorem and its paired Markdown; it introduces no additional external
structural assertion.

`dynamicUnipotentGeneratedSubgroup G` is the supremum, in the subgroup lattice
of the actual rational point group, of `unipotentPoints χ` over all rational
cocharacters `χ`. Thus it is precisely the subgroup generated by those actual
subgroups. Its definition requires only a field and a group scheme. No
identification with the algebraic derived subgroup is part of the definition.

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field in either characteristic, and let `G`
be an affine group scheme over `F`. The topology on its rational points is
`rationalPointTopology F G.X`. For any rational cocharacter `χ`,
`unipotentPoints_le_openNormal` proves that every open normal subgroup of the
ambient point group contains the rational points of the actual dynamic
unipotent subgroup `U_G(χ)`.

For a point `x` of that subgroup, apply the compact contraction theorem to the
singleton `{x}` and the given open subgroup. It supplies a cocharacter value
whose conjugation carries `x` into that subgroup. Normality conjugates back,
placing `x` itself in the subgroup. The proof does not require finite type,
smoothness, or reductivity of `G`.

`unipotentPoints_le_openKernel` applies this result to the open kernel of a
group homomorphism. The codomain is an arbitrary group in an independent
universe; neither commutativity nor torsion-freeness is required.

The two theorems prefixed `dynamicUnipotentGeneratedSubgroup_le_` extend the
individual-radical inclusions to the defined supremum by its universal property.

This helper does not identify algebraic derived rational points with abstract
commutators or with dynamic unipotent generators. The comparison covering all
actual algebraic derived points remains a separate obligation in the parent
smooth-real annihilation task.
