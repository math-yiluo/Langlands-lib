Its assumptions are the original apartment axioms, all four original algebraic filtration axioms, `IsDoubleRootCommutatorFriendly`, openness of the centralizer filtration, and compactness of that fixer. A generic cocharacter is supplied for the simultaneous standard-parabolic conclusion. The field is any nonarchimedean local field in an arbitrary universe, and all point topologies are canonical.

The provenance is composition of the named published constructions and the accompanying double-root conjugation specialization. This module proves no existence theorem for a centralizer filtration, no unconditional covariance statement under the weaker original premises, and no full `AvailableSimultaneousStandardBasisStatement`.
