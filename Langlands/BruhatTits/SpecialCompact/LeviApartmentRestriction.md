# The prescribed Levi apartment as a restricted valuation class

This construction implements the valuation-class part of Kaletha–Prasad Lemma 6.7.1(1)–(3), at the same prescribed torus. The map `leviApartmentProjection` restricts every ambient valuation point to the actual represented Levi along its original point inclusion. It is surjective and satisfies the published `IsLeviRestriction` predicate.

The inputs are local: a connected reductive ambient group with a prescribed maximal split torus, a closed representative of a cocharacter centralizer at the same torus, the factorization of that torus through the representative, and ambient `KPApartmentData` compatible with the chosen field valuation. The representative is affine of finite type. The construction uses no `KPBuildingAssignment.IsValid`, existence of available building data or assumed special-compact/basis proposition. The explicit ambient smoothness and connectedness instances agree with the connected-reductivity hypothesis.

The new carrier is literally the image of the original valuation class under `leviRestrictedRootFunctionFamily`. The companion module proves every V0–V5 valuation axiom, compatibility with the same field valuation, translation equivariance and normalizer equivariance. Translation equivariance identifies this image with a full equipollence orbit. Normalizer equivariance and the ambient class's stability provide the exact stability required by `KPApartmentData`. No independently chosen torus or assignment datum for the Levi is used.

The source is Kaletha and Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits theory: a new approach* (2023), registered `kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`, SHA256 `05ad9a284a2577b34d1a270228b74dfb28f520561ae7772d8f38dddb78938732`, §6.7 and Lemma 6.7.1, printed page 233/PDF264. The 1 November 2024 corrections, registered `kaletha-prasad-2024-corrections`, SHA256 `23293f51e58d5ceecb91f834743f68058b98431797d2531142415d013fdf61ed`, were consulted alongside the book. The actual implementation uses the Library's normalized total root-function families: off the Levi roots the value is infinity, whereas on Levi roots it is the literal ambient evaluation.

This unit does not yet prove the affine quotient-space identification of Lemma 6.7.1(4), preservation of special vertices in §6.7.2, full centralizer omega compatibility, or the special-compact-preimage equality. Its `KPApartmentData` retains the split-torus compatibility built into that structure; the stronger `IsOmegaCompatible` predicate involves additional centralizer data and remains separate. It also proves no simultaneous normal basis or parabolic intersection factorization.
