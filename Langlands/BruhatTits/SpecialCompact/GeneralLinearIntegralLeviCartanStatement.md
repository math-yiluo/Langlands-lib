# Dominant Cartan representatives in every inherited integral Levi of `GL_N`

`GeneralLinearIntegralLeviCartanStatement` carries the Cartan clause
of Renard V.5.1 to the actual standard Levis of `GL_N`. For every
nonarchimedean local field and every generic diagonal cocharacter,
the subgroup `KJ` is the preimage of the ambient standard integral
points under the closed inclusion of the standard Levi of index `J`.
The torus `iJ` is the original diagonal torus factored through that
Levi, and the original cocharacter specifies its positive roots.
[Standard Levi properties](Properties.md)

The double cosets of `KJ` are indexed by the image of the cocharacters
with nonnegative pairings with those positive roots, evaluated at the
same order-one uniformizer of the field. The specified bijection sends
each such point to its own double coset in the Levi. The centralizer
of the diagonal torus in the Levi is the diagonal torus itself, so the
character valuation has the full cocharacter lattice as its image.
The finite residual transversal can be chosen to be \(\{1\}\) in every
Levi, as in the ambient `GL_N`.
[Uniformizer and chamber](../../Representation/Plancherel/RenardUniformizerSection.md)
