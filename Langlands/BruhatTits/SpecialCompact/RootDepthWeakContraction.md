# Weak-chamber preservation of original root-depth generators

This internal derived unit works on the original `ValuationBuildingData G` over an arbitrary nonarchimedean local field `F : Type u`. Its group is connected reductive, its torus is the supplied maximal split torus, and the apartment point is arbitrary. No specialness, topology compatibility, compactness or open-subgroup hypothesis is used. The assertions concern algebraic subgroups of the original `G(F)` and their actual ambient conjugation.

The generic root-filtration lemma uses a supplied `KPApartmentData i (localFieldOrder F)` and an actual relative root. Split-torus compatibility applied to the inverse torus point identifies the valuation after conjugation by that point as the original valuation plus its root-character order. If that order is nonnegative, every original real-depth subgroup is preserved. Taking subgroup closure of the union gives the same preservation for `rootDepthGeneratedSubgroup` whenever every selected root has nonnegative character order. No product or collection theorem is used in this closure argument. The order of a character on the intrinsic common-uniformizer section equals the integer pairing by the published section evaluation theorem.

`standardRadicalDepth B x nu J r` is precisely the subgroup generated by the original depth-`r` root filtrations for positive roots outside the additive monoid generated by `J`. All selected roots remain present, including doubled roots. The real depth is retained without rescaling. The subgroup lies in the actual standard unipotent radical: each standard cocharacter of the face pairs positively with these roots, and the published relative-root inclusion puts every generator in its dynamic unipotent subgroup. For a supplied `ParahoricBuildingData P` and centralizer filtration `Zf`, at nonnegative depth it also lies in the literal original `Zf.moyPrasad x r`, because every generator does. Neither inclusion asserts equality of an intersection.

For a compatible standard split component and a face contained in the simple roots, the published `exists_eq_uniformizerPowers` presents each weak-chamber point as the original torus uniformizer point of a cocharacter nonnegative on every simple root. Positive-root induction makes the pairing nonnegative on every positive root, hence on every selected root. The generic preservation result proves `map_standardRadicalDepth_conj_le` at each real depth. Inversion sends the same weak chamber into the reversed chamber; the proof uses the published reversal of restricted characters and the negative-character/inverse-point norm identity.

The final theorem assumes that the two literal intersections of a supplied subgroup `K` with the actual standard radical and opposite radical equal these two original generated depth subgroups. It also assumes genericity of `nu` for reversal of the compatible component. Applying the preservation theorem to the original and reversed chambers yields precisely `WeakChamberContraction` for `K` and the specified component. The intersection equalities are substantive explicit hypotheses, not consequences proved in this unit.

Provenance is the published actual root-filtration definitions and split-torus compatibility, the character-order/section identity, positive-root induction, the compatible-component weak-point theorem, and chamber reversal. This unit adds no external literature assertion. It constructs neither a centralizer filtration nor a common normal Iwahori family, proves no ordered Iwahori factorization or Moy–Prasad radical-intersection identity, and does not prove the exact available-data target or geometric Levi inheritance.
