# Weyl representatives and parabolic factorizations in the inherited integral Levi

`GeneralLinearIntegralLeviParabolicsStatement` specializes two of the
inheritance clauses of Renard V.5.1(5) to the standard integral model of
`GL_N` over any nonarchimedean local field. Each actual standard Levi
uses the preimage `KJ` of the ambient integral points. The image of `KJ`
under the closed Levi inclusion is the literal intersection of the
ambient subgroup with the Levi.

The first conclusion represents each class in the relative Weyl group
of the factored diagonal torus by a point in `KJ`. The second conclusion
factors the intersection of `KJ` with each standard parabolic of the Levi
as the product of its intersections with that parabolic's standard Levi
and unipotent radical, in that order. The factored torus and the original
generic cocharacter select these standard subgroups.
