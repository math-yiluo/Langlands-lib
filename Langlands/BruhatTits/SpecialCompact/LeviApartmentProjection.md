# The affine projection to the prescribed Levi apartment

`leviApartmentAffineProjection` equips the existing `leviApartmentProjection` with its actual linear part. It maps the original ambient apartment to the previously constructed apartment of the prescribed cocharacter Levi, at the same split torus and along the original inclusion. It is surjective on the actual apartment carriers.

The hypotheses are exactly those of the underlying local restriction construction: an arbitrary universe-`u` field, a connected reductive ambient group with a prescribed maximal split torus, a closed representative of a cocharacter centralizer at that same torus, the factorization of the torus through the representative, and ambient `KPApartmentData` compatible with the specified field valuation. The representative is affine of finite type. No valid building assignment, full building metric, special vertex or special-compact conclusion is assumed.

The published translation spaces are `V(S) / rootAnnihilator i` and `V(S) / rootAnnihilator iM`. Because every actual Levi root is an ambient root, `rootAnnihilator_le_levi` gives inclusion of their annihilators. `leviTranslationProjection` is the quotient map induced by identity on the same full real cocharacter space. `leviTranslationProjection_mk` and `leviTranslationProjection_surjective` give its literal formula and surjectivity. `ker_leviTranslationProjection` identifies its kernel with the image of the Levi annihilator in the ambient translation quotient. `rootForm_comp_leviTranslationProjection` identifies each Levi root form with its actual ambient root form after pullback.

The point-restriction construction already intertwines translations by every vector in `V(S)`. `leviApartmentProjection_mk_vadd` expresses that fact for the two actual quotient classes of the same vector. Quotient induction then constructs `leviApartmentAffineProjection`, with precisely `leviTranslationProjection` as its linear part. Its point-surjectivity is inherited from `leviApartmentProjection_surjective`. Finally, `leviApartmentProjection_eq_iff` states that two ambient points have equal projections exactly when their vector difference lies in the explicit kernel above; the proof uses the affine map's vector-subtraction identity and the kernel equality.

This is a derived quotient-space construction toward KP Lemma 6.7.1(4), *Bruhat–Tits theory: a new approach* (2023), printed page233/PDF264, registered `kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`, SHA256 `05ad9a284a2577b34d1a270228b74dfb28f520561ae7772d8f38dddb78938732`. The proof uses the existing quotient definitions and the concrete earlier valuation-class restriction. It does not yet identify the kernel with `V(A)` for the particular algebraic torus A of the source, or construct that source's specific quotient affine isomorphism. It also proves no affine-root pullback theorem, preservation of special vertices, full centralizer omega compatibility, compact-preimage equality, parabolic factorization or simultaneous normal basis.
