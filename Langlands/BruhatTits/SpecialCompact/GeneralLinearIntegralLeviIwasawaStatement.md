# Iwasawa decomposition at the inherited integral subgroup of each standard Levi

`GeneralLinearIntegralLeviIwasawaStatement` gives the maximal compactness,
openness and Iwasawa products of Renard V.5.1(5) for the standard integral
subgroup of `GL_N` over any nonarchimedean local field. It uses the actual
standard Levi group scheme for each subset of the simple roots of a generic
diagonal cocharacter. Its subgroup `KJ` is the preimage of the ambient
integral points along the closed Levi inclusion, so it maps to the literal
intersection of the ambient compact with the Levi.
[Compactness predicate](Properties.md)

The minimal standard parabolic in the Levi uses the factor of the original
diagonal torus and the original cocharacter. The standard-family comparison
identifies this parabolic with the intersection of the ambient minimal
standard parabolic and the Levi. The proposition asserts both orders of
the product of this parabolic with `KJ` on the Levi's rational points.
[Standard families in the Levi](../../GroupScheme/Rational/StandardLeviStandardFamiliesProof.md)

p-adiques*, French edition dated 9 July 2026, Theorem V.5.1(5), printed
p. 122 / PDF p. 128, that asserts maximal compactness and carries part (1)
