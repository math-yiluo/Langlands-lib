# Power bases for every strict component-chamber point

This internal derived unit is at arbitrary universe `u`. Its algebraic setup is an arbitrary field `F`, an affine group scheme `G` over `Spec F`, commutative group schemes `S` and `A`, maps `i : S → G` and `q : A → S`, a cocharacter `ν` of `S` and a set `J` of characters of `S`. It extends the fixed-cocharacter power argument to every point of the actual character-defined strict chamber, while retaining all specified maps, subgroups and literal radical intersections.

`componentCharacterRestriction_pairing` is associativity of the actual character and cocharacter composites over that arbitrary field. The separate `pairing_eq_zero` result assumes connected reductivity of `G`, `J ⊆ simpleRelativeRoots i ν` and `IsCompatibleStandardSplitComponent i ν J A q`. It applies the published standard-Levi central-image theorem and `dynamicLie`: every component cocharacter annihilates the selected roots because the component is central in the represented standard Levi.

The strict-point existence theorem and `standardCocharacterOfStrictPoint` additionally assume that `F` is a nonarchimedean local field, with its valuative relation and topology. They retain connected reductivity of `G`, the face-subset condition and the compatible component. Every point of `C_A` is the common-uniformizer evaluation of an actual component cocharacter. For a strict point, `characterNorm_latticeSection` identifies each outside-face character norm with the negative integral pairing power of the residue cardinality. Since that cardinality is greater than one, the strict inequality makes each such pairing positive. The vanishing selected pairings and positive outside pairings make the composite cocharacter standard for the original face. Evaluation and postcomposition show that its common-uniformizer value is exactly the original point.

`standardCocharacterOfStrictPoint` selects this actual standard cocharacter together with its standardness and point-identification proofs. No alternate point or compact subgroup is selected.

The final theorem `strictChamber_power_basis_and_exhaustion` is over that nonarchimedean local field, for connected reductive `G`, a maximal split torus `i`, generic `ν`, `J ⊆ simpleRelativeRoots i ν`, the specified compatible component and any compact open subgroup `K`. Its ambient topology is exactly `rationalPointTopology F G.X`; the standard and opposite radicals have the inherited topologies. The proof uses the published `standardCocharacter` and `standardSubgroups_reversedChamber` to identify the two original standard radicals with the dynamic radicals of the chosen cocharacter and its inverse. The published convergence of powers of the canonical uniformizer, combined with `dynamic_power_basis_and_exhaustion`, proves both `StrictChamberExhaustion` and `StrictChamberNeighborhoodBases` for all actual strict-chamber points. No chamber invariance or normality assumption on `K` is required for these two clauses.

This unit proves the two power clauses only. It does not construct the simultaneous normal Iwahori basis, prove weak-chamber preservation, establish estimates uniform over an epsilon chamber, or prove the full available-data target.
