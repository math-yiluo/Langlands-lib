# The literal integral intersection in a standard Levi of `GL_N`

`GeneralLinearStandardLeviIntegralPointsStatement` identifies the subgroup
used in Renard V.5.1(5) in matrix coordinates. It uses the actual standard
Levi points `L` and the fixed standard integral-point image `K` of `GL_N`.
Any generic cocharacter of the diagonal torus supplies the ambient positive
system. For each choice of a standard cocharacter for the Levi index, the
fibres of its integer weight function partition the coordinate indices into
blocks.
[Standard cocharacters](../../GroupScheme/Rational/StandardCocharacterStatement.md)

The rational-point matrix equivalence sends `L` to the subgroup of
invertible matrices with zero entries between distinct blocks. It sends
the literal intersection `K ⊓ L` to the image of the same block diagonal
subgroup over the valuation ring. The comparison is algebraic and applies
to every field with the Library's valuative relation. In the local-field
case it identifies the compact subgroup inherited from the ambient
standard model.
[Standard integral points](../../GroupScheme/IntegralModel/GeneralLinearStandardPoints.md)

This is the matrix comparison for the inherited subgroup in David Renard,
*Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, French edition dated
9 July 2026, Theorem V.5.1(5), printed p. 122 / PDF p. 128. The registered
source ID is `renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, SHA-256
