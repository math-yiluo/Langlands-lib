# Compact core at the same special vertex

This module proves the bounded-open maximality, compact-open maximality and actual relative Weyl representative clauses at the literal geometric subgroup of a supplied valid KP datum. It does not yet prove standard-parabolic intersection factorization, geometric Levi restriction or the simultaneous basis.

The concrete reflection and compact-orbit arguments are adapted from Worker 102 and Worker 3557's published module `Langlands.BruhatTits.Iwasawa.AvailableProof`. Both are coauthors of this reused development. The reflection argument reuses the public `WeylRepresentatives.special_parahoric_normalizer_surjective` theorem. The compact-orbit helper remains private and is implemented here with the same actual building carriers and canonical rational-point topology.

The reflection argument uses the published relative root system, relative Tits system and rank-one factorization proofs, plus the same datum's AS5 and zero-fixer generators. A special point supplies a parallel affine wall through the point for each finite simple root. Actual reflection elements in the point parahoric map to the finite simple reflections; generation gives representatives for all Weyl elements. The published `parahoricAt_le_compactStabilizer` puts these representatives in the exact geometric subgroup.

For compactness, Axiom 4.1.2 gives openness and algebraic boundedness. `IsAlgebraicallyBounded.isCompact_closure` and closedness of open subgroups yield compactness. `metricRealization`, `completeNPCGeodesic` and `boundedSetStabilizerFixedPoint` produce a common fixed point for any compact overgroup. `eq_of_compactStabilizer_fixed_of_special_buildingPoint` identifies it with the original point. `compactSubgroup_le_rationalCommonNormKernel` then proves maximality of the same stabilizer. The additional bounded maximality lemma applies compact maximality to the compact closure of any algebraically bounded overgroup.

This is the fixed-point and reflection route of KP Theorem 4.2.15(1) and the Weyl-representative observation in the proof of Theorem 5.3.4. The final target remains `AvailableSimultaneousStandardBasisStatement.{u}` with no added hypotheses. No existence of a valid assignment is asserted.

`availableNormalBasisCore` applies the internal compact normal basis construction to this exact stabilizer. Canonical rational points are totally disconnected by `rationalPoint_totallyDisconnectedSpace_of_localField`; each actual standard Levi is closed by its published closed representative. The internal intersection theorem therefore supplies the inherited basis for all cocharacters and all standard index sets, without a separate Levi choice. This topological basis has not yet been proved compatible with Iwahori decomposition or contraction.
