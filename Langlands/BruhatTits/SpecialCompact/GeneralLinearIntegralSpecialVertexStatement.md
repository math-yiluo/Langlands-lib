# The standard integral compact in a valid diagonal building assignment

`GeneralLinearIntegralSpecialVertexStatement` asserts that for every
nonarchimedean local field \(F\) and every \(N\), a valid Bruhat–Tits
building assignment realizes the chosen subgroup
\(K_0=\mathrm{GL}_N(\mathcal O_F)\) at a special vertex in the apartment of
the actual diagonal torus. The two torus equalities identify the source of
the torus inclusion and then the inclusion itself. At the same vertex the
special compact subgroup, the intersection of the point fixer with the
common rational character norm kernel, equals the integral points of the
standard model.

The field may have either characteristic. The vertex is independent of the
positive system chosen by a generic diagonal cocharacter. At that vertex
`availableSimultaneousStandardBasis`,
`Iwasawa.availableIwasawa` and `validSpecialCompactRenardCartanComparison`
apply to the actual diagonal parabolics and their standard Levis.

This is the concrete compatibility used to specialize Renard,
*Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, French edition of 9 July
2026, V.5.1–V.5.2 (printed pp. 122–123, PDF pp. 128–129), to the standard
integral compact. Validity uses the corrected axioms of Kaletha and Prasad,
*Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach*, Definition 4.1.26. Proposition 15.1.31(1)
(printed p. 523, PDF p. 554) identifies the lattice stabilizer, and
Proposition 6.2.6 (printed p. 216, PDF p. 247) identifies its apartment with
the one in the valid assignment at the diagonal torus.
