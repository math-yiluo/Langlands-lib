# Grouping original Moy–Prasad root coordinates by a standard parabolic

This internal construction groups the original root filtration blocks by their membership in a standard Levi or one of its radicals. It keeps the supplied parahoric datum `P`, centralizer filtration `Zf`, apartment point `x`, cocharacter `nu`, selected simple-root set `J` and nonnegative depth `r`. The field is a nonarchimedean local field in an arbitrary universe. Both factors of every doubled-root block are retained.

The finite enumeration helper lists any selected part of a finite set before its complement, with no repetitions. The two finite-product helpers put an ordered product in a subgroup when all its coordinates belong there, and split a product at a specified index without permuting any factor.

A block whose root pairs to zero with every standard cocharacter lies in the literal standard Levi, by the published root subgroup centralizer inclusion. This applies to roots in the additive monoid generated by `J`; negating standard cocharacters gives the same original Levi for roots in the monoid generated by `-J`. A block at a positive root outside the corresponding monoid lies in the original generated radical depth group. Positivity of a standard cocharacter also excludes the doubled root from that monoid. Each block lies in the same original Moy–Prasad group by the defining supremum. Every centralizer-filtration factor maps into that group and the literal standard Levi.

`parabolicMoyPrasadCoordinates` takes four prescribed lists of roots: opposite-radical roots, negative Levi roots, positive Levi roots and radical roots. Its domain consists of the original root-block coordinates for the concatenated negative and positive lists, with a factor in `Zf.filtration r` between them. Its output is an element of the original generated opposite-radical depth subgroup, the literal intersection `Zf.moyPrasad x r ⊓ standardLeviPoints P.i nu J`, and the original generated radical depth subgroup. It multiplies each radical list in order, and multiplies the two Levi lists around the original centralizer factor. The membership hypotheses on the four lists and a supplied standard cocharacter ensure that all these values have the stated types. The product compatibility theorem proves that this regrouping preserves the actual product in `G(F)`, using only the ordered finite-product splitting and associativity.

`moyPrasadRadicalDepthProduct_surjective_of_ordered_cover` assumes covering of the original Moy–Prasad group for every complete injective ordering of the indivisible roots of each sign, using the original constant-depth blocks and centralizer factor. Finiteness of the actual roots supplies an ordering with the opposite-radical roots first on the negative side and the radical roots last on the positive side. The remaining roots lie in their respective Levi monoids. The coordinate map and its product compatibility then prove surjectivity of the original `moyPrasadRadicalDepthProduct`. Its middle factor is exactly the literal Moy–Prasad/Levi intersection.

These definitions and theorems are derived from the published filtration and standard subgroup definitions, root finiteness, and elementary finite products. Ordered-root covering remains an explicit hypothesis; it is not proved here. The theorem does not assert covering at depth zero, even though the conditional construction is defined at every nonnegative depth. No centralizer filtration, Moy–Prasad separatedness or covariance, compact parabolic factorization, geometric Levi inheritance, or proof of the host `AvailableSimultaneousStandardBasisStatement` is constructed by this unit.
