# Literal radical intersections under contracting powers

This internal derived construction concerns the power clauses in the published simultaneous-basis predicates. `conjugatePowerIntersection K U m n` is the subgroup obtained by applying the actual conjugation by `m^n` to the literal intersection `K ∩ U`; its underlying set is exactly the published `ambientConjugate`. It selects no substitute for the supplied subgroup.

For a topological group, an open subgroup `K`, a subgroup `U` normalized by all positive and negative powers of `m`, and pointwise contraction of `U`, `iUnion_expanding_conjugate_intersection` proves the literal expanding-union identity. Each point eventually enters `K`, and conjugating back puts it in the displayed union. Normalization supplies the reverse inclusion.

If `K` is also compact, `U` is closed, and the powers contract compact subsets of `U` uniformly into every ambient identity neighborhood, `conjugatePowerNeighborhoodBasis_of_uniform_contraction` proves the precise intrinsic `ConjugatePowerNeighborhoodBasis`. Each contracting image contains the preimage of the open subgroup under inverse conjugation, hence is an identity neighborhood in `U`. Uniform contraction of the compact intersection `K ∩ U` makes those images cofinal in all intrinsic identity neighborhoods, using the inherited subgroup topology. No monotonicity or invariance of `K` under the contracting element is assumed.

The dynamic specialization uses the actual rational-point topology of an affine group over a topological field. `cocharPoint_pow_conj_mem` and its inverse-power counterpart are purely algebraic: they use the published normalization of the dynamic radical by its dynamic parabolic. The Library's `eventually_cocharPoint_pow_conj_mem_ambient`, in `Langlands.BruhatTits.Unipotent.CocharacterContraction`, supplies compact-uniform contraction for any field unit whose powers tend to zero. Applied to a singleton it supplies the pointwise convergence used for exhaustion. The neighborhood-basis result additionally assumes that the field is Hausdorff and that the actual rational-point topology makes the point group a topological group; the Hausdorff assumption supplies closedness of the dynamic radical.

`dynamic_power_basis_and_exhaustion` applies these arguments to a cocharacter and its inverse. It proves the published `StrictChamberExhaustion` and `StrictChamberNeighborhoodBases` predicates for the singleton containing that actual cocharacter point, in the two opposite radical directions, for every compact open ambient subgroup. This unit does not identify all strict character-chamber elements with such cocharacter points, prove uniform estimates over an entire epsilon chamber, construct a simultaneous normal Iwahori basis, or prove the full available-data target.
