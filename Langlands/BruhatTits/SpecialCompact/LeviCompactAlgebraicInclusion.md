# Levi compact inclusion from root-character triviality

This internal construction uses the published triviality of rational characters on dynamic unipotent points. A generic cocharacter has nonzero pairing with every actual relative root; choosing its sign puts any one root subgroup in a dynamic unipotent group. Every rational character is therefore one on that root subgroup, and the entire root subgroup lies in the actual rational norm kernel. Taking the subgroup generated by the original root fixers gives the same assertion for the original sharp fixer.

For a supplied ambient and Levi restriction pair with full omega compatibility and the stated actual centralizer comparison, the sharp fixer of the Levi is consequently norm one. The published normalizer fixed-point comparison then puts the remaining Levi normalizer factor in the original ambient fixer. This proves inclusion of the same Levi norm-one fixer in the literal preimage of the ambient norm-one fixer, without assuming the Levi valuation-topology axiom.

`leviSpecialCompactInclusionOfCompatible` is the actual group-scheme inclusion restricted to these two original subgroups. Its ambient value is unchanged. The results use published character, root and restriction definitions and the existing normalizer comparison, with no claim of compactness, maximality, equality of the two subgroups, full geometric inheritance or the host simultaneous-basis statement.
