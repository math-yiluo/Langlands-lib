# A prescribed Moy–Prasad normal basis

This internal derived unit retains the actual depth subgroups of a supplied `ParahoricBuildingData G` and supplied `CentralizerFiltration` on a nonarchimedean local field `F`, at an arbitrary universe `u`. All rational-point topology assertions use `rationalPointTopology F G.X`. The datum supplies connected reductivity and its prescribed maximal split torus. The construction changes neither this datum nor the chosen apartment point `x` nor the norm-one point fixer `K0 = P.apartment.specialCompactSubgroup x`. Specialness of `x` is not needed for these conditional conclusions.

The generic theorem `compactOpenNormalBasis_of_antitone` concerns any topological group, an arbitrary nonempty semilattice of indices, and a specified antitone family `K r` of open subgroups contained and normal in a supplied compact subgroup `K0`. It assumes that the subgroup infimum is bottom. Open subgroups are closed, so containment in `K0` makes every `K r` compact. Antitonicity supplies a directed decreasing family of closed compact sets. Its intersection is the identity singleton by the separatedness hypothesis. Mathlib's compact-intersection neighborhood theorem then puts some original `K r` in every identity neighborhood. The range of this same family is therefore a `CompactOpenNormalBasis`. No Hausdorffness, countability or replacement of the members is required in this generic argument.

`moyPrasadFamily P Zf x` is exactly the range of the original subgroups `Zf.moyPrasad x r` over all `r : NNReal`, including zero. `moyPrasad_map_conj_eq_of_fixes` assumes uniqueness of the published `IsMoyPrasadAt` presentations at this one building point, at every depth. A presentation at `x` conjugated by an element fixing its image is another presentation at the same point. The uniqueness hypothesis identifies its subgroup with the original one. `moyPrasad_normal_in_specialCompactSubgroup` applies that equality to each element of the original norm-one point fixer, proving normality of each literal depth subgroup viewed inside that fixer. These algebraic arguments require no compactness, topology compatibility, openness, or filtration axioms beyond the supplied data and the explicit uniqueness hypothesis.

`compactOpenNormalBasis_moyPrasadFamily` additionally assumes V7 topology compatibility for `P`, compactness of the original `K0`, openness of every actual depth subgroup, and separatedness of the full depth filtration. The published comparison `moyPrasad_le_parahoricAt`, followed by `parahoricAt_le_compactStabilizer`, puts every depth subgroup in that same `K0`. The published antitonicity and the preceding normality theorem meet the generic theorem's remaining hypotheses. Connected reductivity supplies smoothness, so the canonical rational-point topology is a topological group. This proves compactness and cofinality of the original depth subgroups rather than taking either as an extra premise about those subgroups.

`compactOpenNormalBasis_moyPrasadFamily_of_statements` specializes the preceding result using the three explicit hypotheses `MoyPrasadSeparatedStatement`, `MoyPrasadBoundedOpenStatement`, and `MoyPrasadCovarianceStatement`. It also assumes the datum's `SatisfiesApartmentAxioms`, the supplied centralizer filtration's `SatisfiesFiltrationAxioms` and `IsOpenFiltration`, and compactness of the same geometric `K0`. Only the openness conclusion of bounded openness is used: compactness comes instead from the inclusion into the original compact stabilizer. The full-group commutator target is unnecessary for this bridge.

The provenance is the published actual Moy–Prasad definitions and elementary depth comparisons, the published parahoric/fixer comparison, and Mathlib compactness. This unit proves none of the three supplied proposition targets, constructs no centralizer filtration, and does not identify arbitrary model congruence subgroups with depth subgroups. Ordered Iwahori factorization and weak-chamber preservation for these depth groups remain separate obligations. In particular it does not prove `AvailableSimultaneousStandardBasisStatement` or any geometric Levi-inheritance assertion.
