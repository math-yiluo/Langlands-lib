# Recovering the `GL_N` Cartan decomposition from the general Cartan theorem: proof

Module `Langlands.BruhatTits.Cartan.GeneralLinearRecoveryProof`.

`Langlands.BruhatTits.Cartan.generalLinearCartanRecovery` proves
`GeneralLinearCartanRecoveryStatement.{u}` at an arbitrary universe `u`
([statement](GeneralLinearRecoveryStatement.md)):

```
Geometry.Building.GeneralLinearVertexParahoricStatement.{u} →
  SpecialCompact.GeneralLinearIntegralCartanStatement.{u}
```

The proof follows the route recorded in the statement's Markdown. It uses the
premise, the general Cartan theorem for one datum
(`parahoricCartanConclusions_of_satisfiesGroupAxioms`, corrected KP Theorem
5.2.1) and the existence of data (`Geometry.Building.groupAxiomsExistence`).
The supporting lemmas are in the namespace
`Langlands.BruhatTits.Cartan.GeneralLinearRecovery`. The matrix lemmas they use
are in [`GeneralLinearRecoveryLattice`](GeneralLinearRecoveryLattice.md).

## Notation

Fix a nonarchimedean local field `F`, `N : ℕ`, `G = GL_N` over `F`
(`GeneralLinear.groupScheme (Fin N)`), and
`K = GL_N(𝒪[F])`, the integral points of `GeneralLinear.standardIntegralModel`.
Write `T` for the diagonal torus `GeneralLinear.diagonalTorus` on the coordinate
split torus `𝔻 = splitTorus.commGroupScheme (Spec F) (ULift (Fin N))`. The matrix
of a point of `G` is given by `GeneralLinear.rationalPointsEquiv`. Fix a generic
cocharacter `ν` of `𝔻`.

## Matrices of points

- `mem_K_iff`: a point lies in `K` exactly when its matrix `g` satisfies
  `IsIntegral`, i.e. `g` and `g⁻¹` have integral entries. This combines
  `GeneralLinear.mem_standardIntegralModel_integralPoints_iff` with
  `mem_range_map_iff`.
- `rationalPointsEquiv_diagonalTorus`: the matrix of a point `τ` of `𝔻` under `T`
  is the diagonal matrix of the coordinates of `τ`
  (`GeneralLinear.pointsEquiv_diagonalTorus`).
- `columnSupported_of_mem_rootGroupPoints`: let `α` be a relative root of `T`.
  By `GeneralLinear.relativeRoots_diagonalTorus`, `α = e_a - e_b` with `a ≠ b`.
  Take the cocharacter with weights `e_a`, which pairs with `α` to `1`. Then
  `RelativeRoot.subgroup_le_dynamicUnipotent` puts the root group points `U_α(F)`
  in the dynamic unipotent subgroup of the composite of that cocharacter with
  `T`. This composite is the diagonal cocharacter of weight `e_a`
  (`GeneralLinear.cocharacterLatticeEquiv_symm_comp_diagonalTorus`), and the
  rational points of its dynamic unipotent subgroup have block-unipotent
  matrices (`GeneralLinear.rationalPointsEquiv_mem_blockUnipotentSubgroup`). So every
  `u ∈ U_α(F)` has a matrix of the form `1 + e_a wᵀ`, which is `ColumnSupported`.
- `isDiag_of_mem_torusCentralizer`: the centralizer of `T` lies in the dynamic
  centralizer of a cocharacter with distinct weights
  (`torusCentralizer_le_dynamicCentralizer`). That centralizer is a block Levi
  subgroup (`GeneralLinear.centralizer_diagonalCocharacter`), hence diagonal
  (`GeneralLinear.mem_blockLeviSubgroup_iff_isDiag_of_injective`).
- `exists_map_s_eq` and `map_j_map_s`: for a centralizer datum
  `C : CentralizerDatum T`, every point `z` of `Z = C.Z` is `s(τ)` for a point
  `τ` of `𝔻`, and then `j(z) = T(τ)`. Indeed `j(z)` is diagonal, and `j` is a
  monomorphism (`C.isRepresentedBy`).

## Proof of the theorem

1. **A datum based at the diagonal torus.** `groupAxiomsExistence` gives
   `D : KPBuildingDatum G` with `D.SatisfiesGroupAxioms`.
   `maximalSplitTorusConjugacy` gives `g ∈ G(F)` and an isomorphism `e` from the
   torus of `D` to `𝔻` with `i_D ≫ Int(g) = e ≫ T`. The transported datum
   `E = D.basedTransport σ (G.innerIso g) he` has torus `𝔻` and inclusion `T` by
   definition. This is the construction behind
   `kpBuildingDatumTransportStatement`. `SatisfiesGroupAxioms.basedTransport`
   gives `E.SatisfiesGroupAxioms`.
2. **A special vertex and its facet.** The affine roots of `E` form an affine
   root system (`isAffineRootSystem_affineRoots` of the apartment axioms). So
   they have a special point `x` (`exists_isSpecialPoint`), and `x` is a vertex
   (`AffineRootSystem.isVertex_of_isSpecialPoint`). Let `𝓕` be the facet
   `{[x]}` of the reduced building (`isFacet_image`). Its point is a vertex
   (`isVertex_apartmentMap`).
3. **Two parahorics** (`parahoric_facet_eq_parahoricAt`). By Axiom 4.1.20
   (`neutral_points`), `E.parahoric 𝓕` is the subgroup of `G(F)^0` fixing `[x]`.
   It lies in `P_x = E.base.parahoricAt x` by Axiom 4.1.8 (2)
   (`HasZeroFixerGenerators.zeroFixer_le_parahoric`, for the nonempty bounded
   set `{x}`). Conversely `P_x` lies in it by `parahoric_le_zeroFixer`.
4. **The premise.** The first clause of the premise, for `E` and the vertex
   facet `𝓕`, gives `g₀` with `P_x = g₀ K g₀⁻¹`.
5. **A diagonal conjugating element** (`exists_diagonal_parahoric`). By
   definition, `P_x` is generated by `j(Z(F)^0)` and the groups `U_{α,x,0} ⊆ U_α(F)`.
   By the lemmas above, these generators and their inverses have
   `ColumnSupported` matrices (`le_closure_columnSupported`). Apply
   `exists_diagonal_conj_bound` to the matrix of `g₀`. It gives a point `t = T(τ)`
   of the diagonal torus with every generator in `t K t⁻¹`, so
   `P_x ⊆ t K t⁻¹`. Then `g₀ K g₀⁻¹ ⊆ t K t⁻¹` says that `k K k⁻¹ ⊆ K` for the
   matrix `k` of `t⁻¹ g₀`. Now `isIntegral_inv_mul_mul_of_conj` gives the reverse
   inclusion, so `P_x = t K t⁻¹`.
6. **The general Cartan theorem and its transport** (`bijective_of_cartan`).
   `parahoricCartanConclusions_of_satisfiesGroupAxioms E hE ν hν x` gives:
   (a) `G(F) = P_x · j(D) · P_x` for the dominant set `D ⊆ Z(F)` of `ν`;
   (b) for `z, z' ∈ D`, `P_x j(z) P_x = P_x j(z') P_x` exactly when
   `z' z⁻¹ ∈ Z(F)^0`.
   Since `P_x = t K t⁻¹`, the map `g ↦ t⁻¹ g t` sends `P_x` onto `K`, and its
   inverse `k ↦ t k t⁻¹` sends `K` onto `P_x`. Both maps fix every diagonal
   point, because `t` is diagonal.
   - For `a = T(c(π)) ∈ C0Plus`, `a = j(s(c(π)))`. The normalized valuation of
     `s(c(π))` is `1 ⊗ c` (`centralizerValuation_map_s`, from
     `torusValuationRestriction_eq_split`, and
     `SplitTorusValuation.valuation_cocharacterPoints_uniformizer`). So
     `s(c(π)) ∈ D` exactly when `c` is nonnegative on the positive roots
     (`map_s_mem_dominant_iff`).
   - *Injectivity.* Suppose `K a K = K b K` with `a, b ∈ C0Plus`, say
     `b = k a l` with `k, l ∈ K`. Then `b = (t k t⁻¹) a (t l t⁻¹)` with
     `t k t⁻¹, t l t⁻¹ ∈ P_x`, so `P_x a P_x = P_x b P_x`. Then (b) puts the quotient of the two
     dominant points in `Z(F)^0`, which lies in the norm-one kernel
     (`centralizerZero_le_rationalCommonNormKernel`). So their valuations agree
     (`centralizerValuation_eq_iff`). Their cocharacters agree because
     `RealTorusSpace.latticeEmbedding_injective`, hence `a = b`.
   - *Surjectivity.* For `g ∈ G(F)`, (a) writes `t g t⁻¹ = p₁ j(z) p₂`. Here
     `j(z) = T(τ')`, and `T(τ') = a · T(τ₀)`, where `a = T(c(π))` and `c` is the
     valuation of `τ'`. Since `z` is dominant, `a ∈ C0Plus`. The point `τ₀` has unit
     coordinates (`SplitTorusValuation.mem_valuation_ker`), so `T(τ₀) ∈ K`
     (`map_mem_K_of_coords`). Applying `h ↦ t⁻¹ h t` to
     `t g t⁻¹ = p₁ j(z) p₂` gives `g = (t⁻¹ p₁ t) a (T(τ₀) t⁻¹ p₂ t)`, where
     `t⁻¹ p₁ t` and `t⁻¹ p₂ t` lie in `K`. Hence `g ∈ K a K`.

## Use of the premise and dependencies

The premise is used once, in step 4. The general theorem enters in step 6
through `parahoricCartanConclusions_of_satisfiesGroupAxioms`; its bijectivity
clause is not needed. The datum comes from `groupAxiomsExistence`.

The module does not import
`Langlands.BruhatTits.SpecialCompact.GeneralLinearIntegralCartanProof` or
`Langlands.Group.GL.CartanProof`. A scan of all constants on which
`generalLinearCartanRecovery` depends, transitively, found none declared in
either module. The only axioms are `propext`, `Classical.choice` and
`Quot.sound`.

Step 5 is step 2 of the route in the statement's Markdown, in matrix form. The
units produced by `exists_diagonal_conj_bound` describe the intersections of the
lattice `g₀ 𝒪^N` with the coordinate lines, the eigenlines of `T`. The lattice is
the direct sum of these intersections. This is derived from the root-group
generators of `P_x`, not from `T(𝒪) ⊆ P_x` alone: when the residue field has two
elements, the integral points of the diagonal torus also fix vertices outside
the apartment of `T`.

## Scope

The theorem proves exactly the implication of the statement, with no premise
beyond `GeneralLinearVertexParahoricStatement`. It does not prove that premise.
