# The KP order correspondence for all incident building facets

This focused definition unit expresses Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits theory: a new approach* (2023), Axiom 4.1.22(5), printed p.164 / PDF p.195, with the models and special-fibre maps of the preamble and parts (2)–(3), printed pp.163–164 / PDF pp.194–195. The November 2024 corrections, PDF p.27, retain Axiom 4.1.22 in the corrected ten-axiom Definition 4.1.26. This unit supplies no existence or structural truth assertion.

`BuildingFacet B` is the subtype of all subsets of the existing `B.ReducedBuilding` satisfying the existing `B.IsFacet`. Its elements are facets throughout the building, including every translated apartment. `FacetPrecedes B F F'` means that the two actual facets have presentations in one translated apartment whose apartment facets satisfy `AffineRootSystem.IsFaceOf B.affineRoots Fa Fa'`. The direction is that `F` is contained in the affine-root closure of `F'`, as in the source's `F ≺ F'`. The relation includes equality. `IncidentFacets B F` contains every such whole-building facet `F'`; restricting to one fixed apartment would omit parabolics not containing its specified torus. The intrinsic `facetPrecedes_refl` uses the apartment presentation of a facet and the published inclusion of a facet in its affine-root closure.

The chart and face constructions are derived packaging of the existing actual quotient realization and KP Remark 1.3.16's facet incidence. The face closure is the explicit affine-root sign formula in `AffineRootSystem`, also recalled in the November 2024 corrections to Proposition 1.3.35, PDF p.4. Identification of these chart-defined facets and their incidence with the polysimplicial building belongs to the surrounding full building clauses. No arbitrary facet order or unrelated abstract point set is introduced.

`FacetModelData B R` supplies an actual `RelativeComponentModelData` for each whole-building facet and an actual `RelativeComponentModelHom` from the model of `F'` to that of `F` for every `F ≺ F'`. That morphism already has identity generic fibres and a commuting square for the relative identity-component inclusions. These supplied data are a facet-indexed view of the full model family; the host interface must identify them with its models for nonempty bounded apartment subsets, using actual compatible model isomorphisms where presentations differ.

For a fixed facet `F` and supplied quotient data `Q` of its neutral special fibre, `incidentParabolicImage` sends `F'` to the all-test subgroup functor of the actual closed schematic image inclusion in `Q.quotient`. The image is constructed from the actual source-to-target neutral special-fibre map followed by `Q.projection`. Its comparisons use every residue-field test scheme, rather than only rational points or abstract presentations of subgroup schemes.

`HasFacetParabolicOrderBijection` requires this map to be a `Set.BijOn` from all incident building facets onto exactly the existing `IsParabolicFunctor` predicate: every image is parabolic, the map is injective, and every parabolic subgroup over the residue field is attained. The predicate additionally requires that `F₁ ≺ F₂` gives inclusion of the image of `F₂` in the image of `F₁`, which is the source's order by opposite inclusion. The existing parabolic predicate means representability as the all-test range of an actual smooth closed parabolic subgroup. The four intrinsic accessors merely extract parabolicity, injectivity, surjectivity and the order implication from these formulas.

The surrounding validity interface supplies the full building axioms, the models' bounded-subset comparisons, validity of `Q` as the maximal reductive quotient, and the remaining model/facet clauses. This focused order correspondence keeps the neutral special fibre distinct from its reductive quotient and does not prove any of those conditions or complete corrected Definition 4.1.26 validity.
