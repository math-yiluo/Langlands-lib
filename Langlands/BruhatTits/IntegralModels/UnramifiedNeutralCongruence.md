# Strict root congruence and incident reduction preimages

This internal module concerns the actual neutral component of supplied
unramified full fixer models. It derives strict root bounds, congruence
containment in incident neutral models, and an exact equivalence with the
reduction preimage of the actual special-fibre image. All results use an
arbitrary common universe.

Let `C` be maximal-unramified building data over a nonarchimedean local
field `F`, with affine generic group `G`. Let `M` be relative-component
model data over the integers of the inertia-fixed field. Its full model
is assumed to have integral-point subgroup
`characterValuationKernel ⊓ C.pointwiseFixer (C.apartmentMap '' Ω)`.
Thus the neutral model, integral torus and root subgroups are the actual
ones supplied or constructed from these data.

`unramifiedNeutralRootCentralizerCongruencePoints_eq` specializes the
intrinsic congruence-generation theorem to this neutral model. For every
prescribed positive integer `n`, its congruence subgroup equals the subgroup
generated by its intersections with all actual generic relative root groups
and the whole generic torus centralizer. Smoothness and geometric
connectedness come from the relative-component data; connected reductivity
and generic maximal splitness come from `C`.

For the remaining root bounds, assume `Ω` is a nonempty subset of one
affine facet `Fa`. Given actual reductive quotient data `Q` for `M`,
`unramifiedNeutralPositivePoints_inf_rootGroupPoints_le_rootFixerPlus`
proves that a root point in `Q.positivePoints` has strict depth on `Ω`.
Here `positivePoints` is the generic image of the full reduction preimage
of the supplied unipotent radical.

The proof takes a point `x ∈ Ω`. A root point integral in the neutral model
has nonnegative valuation there. If it fails the strict-depth condition,
facet constancy makes its valuation zero at `x`. The published rank-one
factorization supplies two opposite-root factors. The two valuation
opposite identities make both factor valuations zero at `x`; facet
constancy then puts both in the neutral integral-point subgroup. The
published integral opposite-necessity theorem excludes radical reduction
of the original root point, giving the contradiction. This uses only the
necessity direction of integral opposite factors.

The general lemma
`SpecialFibreQuotientData.congruenceSubgroup_le_positivePoints` observes
that identity reduction belongs to every supplied radical. Actual
reductive-quotient existence over the algebraically closed residue field
therefore yields
`unramifiedNeutralCongruence_inf_rootGroupPoints_le_rootFixerPlus`,
without supplied quotient data. Combining these root bounds with the
level-one generation formula gives
`unramifiedNeutralCongruence_le_positiveRootCentralizerGroup`: the entire
original residue congruence subgroup lies in the subgroup generated by the
full actual neutral centralizer and all strict root fixers. The centralizer
term is controlled by the exact integral-point image formula for the
constructed closed centralizer model.

Now let `Ca` be a facet having `Fa` as a face, let `Ω' ⊆ Ca`, and let `M'`
be another relative-component model with the corresponding full fixer
formula for `Ω'`. The published incident generated-group bound puts the
whole target congruence subgroup in `M'.neutral.integralPoints`.
This is
`unramifiedNeutralIncidentCongruence_le_neutral_of_facet`.
There is no nonemptiness condition on `Ω'`.

For a full component-model comparison `φ : RelativeComponentModelHom M' M`,
`unramifiedNeutralIncident_hasParabolicReductionPreimage_of_facet`
proves the exact section-range equality expressed by
`φ.HasParabolicReductionPreimage`. The entry definition
`unramifiedNeutralIncidentFacetReductionPreimageEquiv` is a multiplicative
equivalence from the original source neutral sections to the full inverse
image of `φ.specialFibreImagePoints` under the original target reduction
map. Its application theorem says that the underlying target section is
exactly `rationalPoints.map φ.neutral.hom x`. The construction uses the
proved congruence containment, Henselianity of the fixed-field integers,
and surjectivity of the actual image projection on points over the
algebraically closed residue field. It needs no radical-preimage generation
premise.

The provenance is internal: these are consequences of the published
rank-one, integral opposite-necessity, facet-constancy, congruence-generation,
centralizer-image and special-fibre-image recovery results. This module does
not assert geometric parabolicity of the special-fibre image. Generation
of the whole radical preimage, existence of all full fixer models, and the
original unconditional `UnramifiedFixedDepthZeroOppositeStatement` are not
proved here; the last is proved as `unramifiedFixedDepthZeroOpposite` in
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.UnramifiedFixedDepthZeroOppositeProof`.

The generic `SpecialFibreQuotientData.congruenceSubgroup_le_positivePoints` is reused from `DepthZeroCompactOpen`, preserving its existing name and general hypotheses.
