# The coefficient algebra of congruence pairs

For a coefficient algebra \(C\) over a ring \(A\) and an ideal \(J\) of \(C\),
`WeilCongruence.pairs` is the algebra of pairs \((a,c)\in A\times C\) with
\(c-a\in J\). The first projection has the section given by the algebra map of
\(A\). The unit pairs above one are precisely the units of \(C\) congruent to
one modulo \(J\). If \(A\) is a principal ideal domain and \(C\) is finite
free, the pair algebra is a finite free submodule of \(A\times C\). A
nonzero scalar whose image belongs to \(J\) clears the congruence condition
after passage to the fraction field. `genericPairsEquiv` gives the resulting
product identification, with the specified comparison for the second factor.

This algebra is a coordinate construction for the multiplicative congruence
model in the proof of Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach*
(2023), Proposition B.3.6(1), printed p. 659 (one-based PDF p. 690). For the
ideal \(J=(q)\) generated by a nonzerodivisor, the unit pairs above one can
be written as \((1,1+qx)\). Their product in the parameter \(x\) is
\(x+y+qxy\). The pair algebra lets the restriction use the full ideal
without a choice of generator.
