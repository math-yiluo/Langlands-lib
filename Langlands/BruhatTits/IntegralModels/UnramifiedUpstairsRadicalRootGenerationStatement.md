# Full radical preimages over maximal-unramified integers

`UnramifiedUpstairsRadicalRootGenerationStatement.{u}` is derived from
Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023), Corollary
8.4.12. It makes the common positive torus factor explicit and identifies
the model by its integral points. The combined formulation does not
appear verbatim in the source. It is a proposition definition; no proof is
asserted. The registered source is `kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`,
SHA-256 `05ad9a284a2577b34d1a270228b74dfb28f520561ae7772d8f38dddb78938732`.
Page locators below are printed page / PDF page.

## The statement

Let F be any nonarchimedean local field in universe u, K = `inertiaFixedField F`
its actual maximal unramified extension, and O = `fixedFieldIntegers F`.
The group G is any affine group over K with a supplied maximal-unramified
building datum `C : MaximalUnramified.BuildingData F G`. The datum supplies
connected reductivity, the maximal split torus S, the apartment class and
its root valuations, and the actual centralizer inclusion j : Z_G(S) → G.
E is any `UnramifiedZeroDatum F G`; `E.zeroPoints` is G(K)^0 of KP
Definition 2.6.23(1). No descent of G, or of its models, to F is assumed.

The proposition asserts that there is one subgroup H of G(K) such that:

- H lies in the image of `rationalPoints.map C.centralizer.j`;
- H lies in `E.zeroPoints ⊓ C.pointwiseFixer (Set.range C.apartmentMap)`,
  that is, in G(K)^0 and in the pointwise fixer of the whole apartment image;
- for every nonempty Ω ⊆ `C.Apartment` contained in one affine-root facet
  `AffineRootSystem.facet C.affineRoots x`, every smooth
  `N : IntegralModel O K G` with

  `N.integralPoints = E.zeroPoints ⊓ C.pointwiseFixer (C.apartmentMap '' Ω)`,

  and every `i : U ⟶ N.specialFibre` with `IsUnipotentRadical i`,

  `((rationalPoints.map i).range.comap N.reduction).map N.integralPointMap =
    H ⊔ ⨆ a : relativeRoots C.i, C.apartment.rootFixerPlus a Ω`.

H is chosen before Ω, N, U and i, so one subgroup serves every facet
subset and every model. The left side uses the actual maps of N: the
residue-field points of U are pushed into the special fibre, pulled back
along N's reduction map, and the resulting O-points are mapped into G(K)
by N's generic integral-point map. The right side is the subgroup
generated by H and the Library positive root fixers
`rootFixerPlus a Ω = U_a(K) ⊓ ⋂_{x ∈ Ω} U_{a,x,0+}`, over all relative
roots a (divisible ones included).

`IsUnipotentRadical` is the Library's greatest smooth connected normal
unipotent closed-subgroup predicate over the residue field of O. The
statement does not require N's special fibre to be connected: the
characterization and smoothness force N to be the connected-fibre KP model
(see below), so the source's radical applies. Affineness, flatness and
finite type are fields of `IntegralModel`; smoothness is an explicit
premise. The formula is asserted for every representative i of the radical.

## The source witness H and its bounds

In the source, the torus factor of the preimage is T(K)_{0+} for the
maximal torus T = Z_G(S) (G is quasi-split over K by Corollary 2.3.8, 81 /
112). The filtration of T(K) is the one chosen in §7.2 (245 / 276); §8.5.1
(310 / 341) fixes it to be the minimal congruent filtration of Definition
B.10.8 (690 / 721). The witness is therefore

H = j(T(K)^{mc}_{0+}).

Its bounds hold as follows.

- It lies in the centralizer image by construction.
- Lemma B.10.11 (690–691 / 721–722) gives T(K)^{mc}_{0+} = T(K)^0 ∩ T(K)^{std}_{0+},
  so T(K)^{mc}_{0+} ⊆ T(K)^0. As T = Z_G(S) is a maximally split maximal
  torus, Definition 2.6.23(1) and the independence of the torus in Lemma
  2.6.24 (104–105 / 135–136) put j(T(K)^0) in G(K)^0, for every supplied E.
- Lemma 2.5.14 (90 / 121) puts T(K)^0 in the maximal bounded subgroup T(K)^1.
  Lemma 6.1.26(3) (209 / 240) and, for reductive G, Lemma 6.1.29 (210 / 241)
  show that T(K)^1 acts trivially on the apartment. In the Library,
  `IntegerValuationBuildingData.map_mem_oneStabilizer` records that j(Z(K)^1)
  fixes every point of the apartment image. Hence H fixes the whole apartment
  pointwise.

The full-apartment fixer bound is what a consumer needs when comparing the
radical preimages of incident facets. H is not an arbitrary filtration
subgroup chosen from `CentralizerDatum`. The proposition only asserts that
some H with these properties exists.

## Identification of N with the KP connected model

Corollary 8.4.12 (306 / 337) concerns the Bruhat–Tits model G^0_Ω of a
nonempty bounded Ω. Theorem 8.3.13 (299 / 330), with the corrected end of
its proof, gives G^0_Ω(O) = G(K)^0_Ω, the group generated by T(K)^0 and
the U_{a,Ω,0} (Definition 7.4.1, 255 / 286). Proposition 7.6.4(1) (265 / 296),
for any subset of a facet, and Proposition 7.7.5(1) (269 / 300), for
nonempty bounded subsets, identify G(K)^0_Ω with the pointwise stabilizer of
Ω in G(K)^0. Proposition 6.2.6 (216 / 247) makes the supplied ω-compatible
apartment class the source's apartment. Lemma 2.6.24 makes G(K)^0
independent of E's torus. So the right side of N's characterization is the
O-point group of the KP connected model. Corollary 2.10.11 (124 / 155)
says that smooth affine O-models are determined by their O-points.
Therefore any smooth N satisfying the characterization is isomorphic to the
KP model, compatibly with the generic identification. In particular, N's
special fibre is connected.

## Single-facet domain and the two positive-root conventions

KP works with nonempty bounded subsets. Here Ω is any nonempty subset of
one facet, possibly the whole facet, and no facet is assumed bounded. The
reduction to a point uses the following.

- By Definition 6.1.2 (199 / 230), Lemma 6.3.20 and 6.3.21 (222–223 / 253–254),
  the groups U_{a,x,0} and U_{a,x,0+} are constant as x ranges over a facet.
- The discussion before Lemma 7.4.12 (258 / 289) identifies the facet groups
  P_{F,0} and P_{F,0+} with G(K)_{x,0} and G(K)_{x,0+} for any x ∈ F.
- Proposition 7.6.4(1) shows that the pointwise stabilizer of Ω in G(K)^0
  is that of the facet, hence that of any point x ∈ Ω.

Thus for any x ∈ Ω both sides of the formula agree with their values for
the bounded singleton {x}.

The source and the Library define positive root groups differently. KP 8.4.3
(303 / 334) sets U_{a,Ω,0+} = ⋃_{x∈Ω} U_{a,x,0+} ∩ U_{a,Ω,0}, a union.
Library `rootFixerPlus a Ω` intersects all U_{a,x,0+}. For a general
bounded Ω these differ. On a nonempty subset of one facet both equal the
constant U_{a,x,0+}. This is why the proposition is restricted to the
single-facet domain and makes no assertion for other subsets.

## How the right side matches the source

KP does not separately define G(K)_{Ω,0+} for general Ω. For a point x,
Definition 7.3.3 (247 / 278) with the constant concave function 0+ defines
G(K)_{x,0+} as the subgroup generated by T(K)_{0+} and all U_{a,x,0+}. The
proof of Corollary 8.4.12 assembles the preimage from the following.

- Theorem 8.4.10(1) (305 / 336) decomposes the special-fibre radical as a
  product of the positive root pieces U^+_{a,Ω,0} and the torus piece T^+.
- Corollary 8.4.5(2) (304 / 335) says the preimage of U^+_{a,Ω,0}(f) is
  U_{a,Ω,0+}.
- Lemma B.10.11 and Proposition B.10.12 (690–691 / 721–722) say the
  preimage of T^+ is T(K)^{mc}_{0+}.
- Proposition 7.3.12 (250 / 281) gives the product decomposition of the
  resulting group.

The preimage is a subgroup containing every factor and contained in the
subgroup those factors generate. So it equals the subgroup generated by
T(K)^{mc}_{0+} and all U_{a,Ω,0+}. That is the right side here, with H as
the torus factor. The formula takes the subgroup generated by all relative
roots. It needs no root ordering or indivisible-root index. Including a
divisible root 2a adds nothing: axiom V 4 of Definition 6.1.2 gives
φ_{2a} = 2φ_a on U_{2a}(K), so U_{2a,x,0+} ⊆ U_{a,x,0+}.

## Corrections

The registered corrections `kaletha-prasad-2024-corrections`, SHA-256
`23293f51e58d5ceecb91f834743f68058b98431797d2531142415d013fdf61ed`, make
these changes on PDF pp.37–38:

- Sections 8.3–8.4 assume a perfect residue field. Local fields satisfy
  this, and the residue field of O is then an algebraic closure.
- In 8.3.6, T^0 is the connected Néron model of the centralizer T.
- The last two sentences of the proof of Theorem 8.3.13 are replaced. The
  new argument shows that G(K)^0_Ω, generated by the U_{a,Ω,0} and T(K)^0,
  equals G^0_Ω(O).
- In 8.4.2, U_{a,Ω,0} is defined as the a-root group of the special fibre.

On PDF p.50 the corrections clarify that R in Definition B.10.8(2) is an
induced K-torus, and add B.10.8(4) for the base-field filtration. Only the
upstairs K-filtration is used here.

## Relation to the downstairs statement

This is an upstairs statement about every smooth O-model N of G over the
maximal unramified integers and its actual special-fibre radical. The
published `UnramifiedRadicalRootGenerationStatement` instead quantifies over
groups over F and relative-component model data D over 𝒪[F]. It describes
only the F-rational points `Q.positivePoints` reducing into the radical of a
reductive quotient Q, as the pullback along scalar extension of a
g-conjugated generated subgroup. That rational-point pullback does not
determine the full preimage of the radical in G(K), so it does not imply
the assertion here.

This proposition assumes none of the following: a lower building, descent
to F, model existence, facet incidence, a model morphism, parabolicity, a
pair of radical preimages, a reductive quotient, assumed generation or
radical containment, or a final integral-model axiom.
