# Radical preimages and incident images of actual neutral models

This internal application concerns maximal-unramified building data `C` for an
affine group over the inertia-fixed field of a nonarchimedean local field `F`,
at arbitrary universe `u`. Supply a relative-component model `M` whose full
integral points are the character-valuation kernel intersected with the
pointwise fixer of `Ω`. Assume `Ω` is a nonempty subset of a facet `Fa`, and
supply valid reductive special-fibre quotient data `Q` for its actual neutral
component. The results assert no existence of building or full-model data.

`unramifiedNeutralPositivePoints_le_positiveRootCentralizerGroup` bounds the
**whole** actual radical preimage `Q.positivePoints` by the group generated by
the actual neutral-centralizer integral points and all strict-depth root
fixers on `Ω`. It removes the generation premise retained by
`UnramifiedNeutralIncidentRadicalImage`. The upper bound includes the entire
neutral centralizer; an equality with the radical preimage is not claimed.

The proof identifies the radical preimage with the generic image of the
kernel of reduction followed by `Q.projection`. The valid quotient supplies a
smooth connected affine target, a smooth surjective projection and a connected
unipotent full scheme kernel. The actual neutral model is smooth with
geometrically connected fibres. The compatible integral split maximal torus
therefore permits application of the whole quotient-kernel root-and-centralizer
generation theorem. Its root bounds are the already proved strict-depth
bounds for actual radical-preimage intersections on a facet subset. Its
centralizer bound follows from neutral integrality and the exact integral-point
image of the actual neutral centralizer. Thus no root-generation or
radical-preimage-generation assumption is retained.

For an incident facet `Ca` with `Fa` a face, supply a second full-fixer model
`M'` for `Ω' ⊆ Ca`. The whole upper bound is integral in its actual neutral
component, so `unramifiedNeutralIncidentPositivePoints_le_neutral` contains the
whole target radical preimage there. For a supplied generic-identity neutral
model morphism `ψ : M'.neutral.Hom M.neutral (𝟙 G)`, the definition
`unramifiedNeutralIncidentFacetRadicalImageFactor` constructs an actual map from
`Q.radical` into the schematic image of `ψ.specialFibreMap`. Its composite with
the image inclusion is exactly `Q.radicalInclusion`; the resulting containment
holds on every residue-scheme test. If `Ω'` is also nonempty,
`unramifiedNeutralIncidentImage_isParabolic_of_facet` proves geometric
parabolicity of that actual neutral comparison image.

The image factor uses the existing Henselian integral-image recovery theorem
over the actual fixed-field integer ring and its algebraically closed residue
field. Parabolicity uses the existing incident dynamic-parabolic image theorem
once the radical containment is supplied. The provenance is this composition
of proved interfaces; the module does not construct the full models or prove
the host depth-zero opposite-factor statement.
