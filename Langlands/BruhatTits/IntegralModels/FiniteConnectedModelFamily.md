# Applying connected generation to a finite family of integral models

This is internal, derived content. It packages a finite family of actual
integral models and applies a supplied proof of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.ConnectedGenerationStatement.{u}` using the transport
identities in `GeneratorImages`. It does not prove that proposition.

Let `R` be a commutative domain with fraction field `K`, in arbitrary universe
`u`, and let `N` be an actual integral model of the generic group `G`.
`FiniteConnectedModelFamily N` consists of:

- a natural number `size` and generic groups indexed by `Fin size`;
- an actual integral model of each generic group, with smooth structural
  morphism and geometrically connected fibres;
- a homomorphism from each generic group to `G`;
- an actual compatible integral-model map into `N` extending that specified
  generic homomorphism.

Affineness, flatness, and local finite type are already fields of
`IntegralModel`. Geometric connectedness concerns every actual fibre, not
only the special fibre or the integral sections. The family may be empty.
Its existence and the existence of its model maps are explicit supplied data.

`FiniteConnectedModelFamily.integralImages` is the subgroup supremum of the
images of these models' integral-point subgroups under their specified generic
homomorphisms. It uses `IntegralModel.generatedIntegralImages`.
`integralImages_le_integralPoints` applies the naturality of each compatible
map and takes the subgroup supremum. Thus the generated subgroup lies inside
`N.integralPoints`.

For `integralImages_eq_neutral`, additionally assume that `R` is a Henselian
discrete valuation ring with algebraically closed residue field. Supply
`D : RelativeComponentModelData R K G` and a family mapping into `D.full`.
The theorem assumes a proof of `ConnectedGenerationStatement.{u}`, finite
relative index of the generated subgroup in `D.full.integralPoints`, and
containment of `D.full.congruenceSubgroupAt m` for some positive integer `m`.
This congruence subgroup is the generic image of the kernel of actual
reduction modulo the `m`th power of the maximal ideal.

The implemented proof first uses generic-evaluation injectivity and the
compatible model maps, through the two `GeneratorImages` equivalences, to
transport finite relative index and congruence containment to the subgroup
generated by the integral-section images. All the source groups are affine,
smooth and geometrically connected by the supplied models. Applying the
assumed connected-generation theorem to `D.full.group` and the supplied
relative-component inclusion therefore identifies that section subgroup with
`D.neutralSections`. Finally,
`D.iSup_integralPoints_eq_neutral_of_sections` transports this equality to
`A.integralImages = D.neutral.integralPoints`.

No index in all of `G(K)` is assumed. No topology is substituted for the
actual congruence kernel. This unit does not construct torus or root models,
extend generic homomorphisms, establish connected generation, or prove that
the generated subgroup equals the intersection of a building fixer with the
zero subgroup. Those remain separate obligations of the unramified
parahoric-model proof.
