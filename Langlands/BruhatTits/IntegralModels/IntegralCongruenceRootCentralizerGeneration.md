# Prescribed congruence levels and actual generic root intersections

This is internal derived content. It transports
`RelativeRoot.congruenceKernelAt_le_of_exponentRoots_and_dynamicCentralizer`
from coordinate split tori to the original generic group and its specified
torus. The transport follows the published
`IntegralRootCentralizerGeneration` construction and retains the actual
integral model and generic identification.

Let R be a local domain with fraction field K, let G be an affine group
scheme over K, and let N be an integral model of G. Given a torus map
`i : S.toGrp ⟶ G`, `N.rootCentralizerCongruencePoints i n` is the join of
the supremum of `N.congruenceSubgroupAt n ⊓ rootGroupPoints i a` over every
actual relative root a and the intersection of the same congruence subgroup
with the whole generic torus centralizer. The elementary bound
`rootCentralizerCongruencePoints_le` puts this generated subgroup inside
the original congruence subgroup.

For the reverse inclusion, assume N is smooth with geometrically connected
fibres, G is connected reductive, i is a maximal split torus, and a
compatible `IntegralSplitTorusData N i` is supplied. This data includes the
actual integral split torus, its inclusion in N, its generic isomorphism to
S, and the commuting square. Then
`IntegralSplitTorusData.rootCentralizerCongruencePoints_eq` proves equality
with `N.congruenceSubgroupAt n` for every specified positive integer n.
The ring need not be Henselian and its residue field need not be closed.

The proof chooses split coordinates for the supplied integral torus. The
commuting generic square transports maximality to the coordinate generic
torus, so the relative root system follows from the reductivity of G.
A generic cocharacter whose dynamic centralizer is the entire torus
centralizer is written in exponent coordinates and lifted integrally.
The subgroup generated by the prescribed generic root and centralizer
intersections is pulled back to the integral section group.

For a section in an integral root functor and in the prescribed congruence
kernel, base restriction preserves root-functor membership. The published
exponent-root and torus-isomorphism transport lemmas put its generic point
in the original generic root group, and its kernel membership puts that
point in the original prescribed congruence subgroup. The dynamic
centralizer is handled by the analogous base-change and torus-centralizer
transport. The coordinate prescribed-level generation theorem now puts
the whole integral congruence kernel in the pulled-back subgroup. Mapping
the original sections generically proves the desired reverse inclusion.

`congruenceSubgroupAt_le_of_roots_and_centralizer` expresses the resulting
universal property: bounds on all root intersections and on the whole
torus-centralizer intersection imply the same bound on the entire
prescribed congruence subgroup. At level one,
`congruenceSubgroup_eq_roots_sup_centralizer` states the formula directly
for the original special-fibre reduction kernel, using the canonical
published level-one comparison.

The roots in this module are the actual generic relative roots of i.
No identification of their congruence intersections with strict-depth
building root fixers is asserted. Existence of an integral model or of the
supplied integral torus, generation of a full radical reduction preimage,
and the host opposite-factor theorem remain outside these results.
