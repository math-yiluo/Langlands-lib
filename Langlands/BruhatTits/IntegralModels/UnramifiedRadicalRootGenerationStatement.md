# Positive radical preimages from one torus factor and positive root groups (KP 8.4.12, 9.5)

`UnramifiedRadicalRootGenerationStatement.{u}` is a statement, not a proof. It gives a
group-theoretic description of the subgroup `G(F)^{0+}_Ω` cut out by the actual unipotent
radical of the special fibre of a descended parahoric-type model. The description is the
group generated by one fixed subgroup of the centralizer and the positive root groups
`U_{a,Ω,0+}`, conjugated by an element of `G(K)` and pulled back to `G(F)`. It is a derived
consequence of Kaletha–Prasad. The combination stated here, on the Library's existing data,
does not appear verbatim in the source.

## Source assertion

KP §8.4 works over `K`, the maximal unramified extension of `k`, and assumes that the
residue field `f` of `k` is perfect, so that the residue field of `K` is an algebraic closure
`f̄`. The group `G_K` is quasi-split (Corollary 2.3.8). Fix a maximal `K`-split torus `S` with
apartment `A`, and let `T = Z_G(S)`, a maximal torus (8.3.6). Let `Ω ⊂ A` be nonempty and
bounded, and let `𝒢^0_Ω` be the smooth affine `O`-model of Theorem 8.3.13, with
`𝒢^0_Ω(O) = G(K)^0_Ω` and connected special fibre. Corollary 8.4.12 says that the preimage of
`R_u(𝒢^0_Ω)(f̄)` under reduction `G(K)^0_Ω → 𝒢^0_Ω(f̄)` is `G(K)_{Ω,0+}`. Its proof combines
Theorem 8.4.10, Corollary 8.4.5(2), Lemma B.10.11 and Proposition 7.3.12:

- the root factors of the radical are the reductions of the groups `U_{a,Ω,0+}` of 8.4.3;
- the torus factor `T^+ = R_u(𝒯^0)` is the reduction of `T(K)^mc_{0+}`, for the minimal
  congruent filtration of Appendix B;
- the product decomposition reassembles these factors.

In §9.5 the same subgroup over the base field is `G(k)^{0+}_F = G(K)^{0+}_F ∩ G(k)`. This holds
because `f` is perfect, so the unipotent radical of the geometric special fibre is defined
over `f`.

## Lean statement

Write `K = inertiaFixedField F` and `G_K = baseChanged F K G`. The proposition quantifies over:

- a universe `u`, a nonarchimedean local field `F : Type u`, and an affine group scheme `G`
  over `Spec F`;
- any `C : MaximalUnramified.BuildingData F G_K`. This is supplied KP data for `G_K`
  (connected reductive): a maximal split torus `C.i : S → G_K`, an ω-compatible apartment
  class, and the actual centralizer datum `C.centralizer` of `Z = Z_{G_K}(S)`;
- any `E : UnramifiedZeroDatum F G_K`. Its `E.zeroPoints` is `G(K)^0 = G(K)^♮ · T_E(K)^0`
  (Definition 2.6.23(1)).

It asserts that there is `H : Subgroup G(K)` with `H ≤ (rationalPoints.map C.centralizer.j).range`
such that the following holds for all of the data below:

- every `Ωa : Set C.Apartment` that is nonempty and contained in one facet
  `AffineRootSystem.facet C.affineRoots x` of the affine roots `Ψ`;
- every `g ∈ G(K)`;
- every `D : RelativeComponentModelData ↥𝒪[F] F G` with `D.neutral.IsSmooth` and
  `D.neutral.baseChangeUnramified.integralPoints = E.zeroPoints ⊓ C.pointwiseFixer (g · Ωa)`,
  where `g · Ωa` is the image `(fun x => g • C.apartmentMap x) '' Ωa` in the reduced
  building;
- every `Q : SpecialFibreQuotientData D` with `Q.IsReductiveQuotient`.

For these data,

```
Q.positivePoints =
  ((H ⊔ ⨆ a : relativeRoots C.i, C.rootFixerPlus a Ωa).map
    (MulAut.conj g).toMonoidHom).comap (rationalExtend F K G)
```

Here `Q.positivePoints` consists of the points of `G(F)` given by integral sections of
`D.neutral` over `𝒪[F]` whose reduction lies in the actual residue-field points of the
specified radical of `Q`. `Q.IsReductiveQuotient` says that this radical is the greatest
smooth connected normal unipotent closed subgroup, that `Q` is its represented fppf quotient,
and that `Q` is connected reductive. The group `C.rootFixerPlus a Ωa` is
`U_a(K) ⊓ ⨅_{x∈Ωa} U_{a,x,0+}`. The map `rationalExtend` sends `G(F)` to `G(K)`.

The order of quantifiers matters: `H` is chosen before the subset, the conjugating element,
the model and the quotient, so a single subgroup serves all of them.

## Why the source gives the conclusion

**Setting.** For a local field `F`, the residue field is finite and therefore perfect. The
corrected hypothesis of §§8.3–8.4 therefore holds, and `G_K` is quasi-split
(Corollary 2.3.8). The datum `C` is arbitrary, but its apartment class is ω-compatible
(`C.isOmegaCompatible`). By Proposition 6.2.6 it is therefore KP's canonical class for
`S`, so the filtrations `U_{a,x,r}` of `C` are those of KP. The centralizer `Z` of `C.i` is
the maximal torus `T` (8.3.6). Since every datum `E` computes the same normal subgroup
`G(K)^0` (Lemma 2.6.24), quantifying over all `E` adds nothing.

**The common torus factor.** The witness is `H = j(T(K)^mc_{0+})`, the image under
`C.centralizer.j` of the `0+` subgroup of the minimal congruent filtration of `T`
(Definition B.10.8, Proposition B.10.10). It depends only on `T`, hence only on `C`, and lies
in the image of the centralizer. KP Remark 7.3.5 stresses that the torus part of `G(k)_{x,f}`
depends on the chosen filtration of `Z(k)`. An arbitrary commutator-friendly filtration
(Definition 7.2.1, Lemma 7.2.3) need not give the radical preimage. The canonical one does,
because Lemma B.10.11 and Proposition B.10.12 identify `T(K)^mc_{0+}` with the preimage of
`R_u(𝒯^0)(f̄)`. The Lean keeps only the existence of `H`; it does not name `T(K)^mc_{0+}`.

**Identifying the model.** Let `Ω = Ωa`. Facets of the reduced apartment are bounded, so `Ω`
is nonempty and bounded. By Propositions 7.6.4(1) and 7.7.5(1), together with the
description of the reduced building as `G(K) × A` modulo (7.6.2) (Remark 7.6.5), the group
`G(K)^0 ∩ Fix(g · Ω)` equals `g G(K)^0_Ω g⁻¹`. The base change of `D.neutral` to the integers
of `K` is smooth and affine (an `IntegralModel` is affine, flat and of finite type), and has
these integral points. Corollary 2.10.11 therefore identifies it, compatibly with generic
fibres, with the `g`-conjugate of `𝒢^0_Ω` from Theorem 8.3.13. The special fibres and their
unipotent radicals correspond under this identification. The redundant hypothesis
`D.neutral.IsSmooth` duplicates the structure field `D.neutral_smooth`. It is kept for parity
with the published `UnramifiedFacetPositiveRadicalStatement` and the intended consumer.

**The radical preimage over `K`.** Corollary 8.4.12 identifies the preimage of the radical
in `G(K)^0_Ω` with `G(K)_{Ω,0+}`. Its proof assembles this group from three factors:

- `T(K)^mc_{0+}`, by Lemma B.10.11;
- the groups `U_{a,Ω,0+}` of 8.4.3, by Theorem 8.4.10(1) and Corollary 8.4.5(2);
- the product decomposition of Proposition 7.3.12, where `f(0) > 0` gives `N^♯ = Z^♯`.

Fix `x ∈ Ω` and let `F_x` be its facet. By Lemma 6.3.20 and 6.3.21, `U_{a,Ω,0+} = U_{a,x,0+}`,
so this group is KP's `P_{F_x,0+} = G(K)_{x,0+}` (printed p. 258) for the constant concave
function `0+`. By Definition 7.3.3 it is `G(K)^♯_{x,0+} · T(K)^mc_{0+}`, the subgroup
generated by `T(K)^mc_{0+}` and the groups `U_{a,x,0+} · U_{2a,x,0+}` for `a ∈ Φ`. In Lean this subgroup is the join
`H ⊔ ⨆ a, C.rootFixerPlus a Ω`, by the comparison in the next paragraph. Taking the
supremum over all relative roots, including divisible ones, matches the factors
`U_{2a,x,0+}` in Definition 7.3.3(1). Conjugating by `g` gives the preimage for the model
characterized by `g · Ω`.

**KP's positive root groups versus `rootFixerPlus`.** KP 8.4.3 defines, for a general
nonempty bounded `Ω`,
`U_{a,Ω,0+} = (⋃_{x∈Ω} U_{a,x,0+}) ∩ U_{a,Ω,0}`.
The Library's `rootFixerPlus a Ω` is the intersection `U_a(K) ∩ ⋂_{x∈Ω} U_{a,x,0+}`. On a
nonempty subset of one facet, `U_{a,x,0}` and `U_{a,x,0+}` do not depend on `x`
(Lemma 6.3.20 and 6.3.21). Both definitions therefore equal `U_{a,x,0+}` for any `x ∈ Ω`, and
the formula of Corollary 8.4.12 is exactly the Lean conclusion.

The two conventions differ on general bounded sets. Take `Ω = {v, y}`, where an affine root
`ψ` with derivative `a` vanishes at `v` and is positive at `y`. An element
`u ∈ U_ψ \ U_{ψ+}` has `v_a(u) = 0 < y_a(u)`. It lies in KP's union group but not in
`rootFixerPlus a Ω`. The statement makes no claim on such sets. It also excludes the empty
set, where `rootFixerPlus` is all of `U_a(K)` and the pointwise fixer is all of `G(K)`.

**Descent to `F`.** The finite residue field `f` of `F` is perfect, and the residue field of
`K` is `f̄`. The radical of `Q` is the unipotent radical over `f`, and its base change to `f̄`
is the unipotent radical of the geometric special fibre (§9.5). An integral section of
`D.neutral` over `𝒪[F]` therefore reduces into the radical over `f` exactly when its image
over the integers of `K` reduces into the radical over `f̄`. Integral points of the affine
model over `𝒪[F]` are exactly its points over the integers of `K` that lie in `G(F)`. These
two facts give
`G(F)^{0+} = {γ ∈ G(F) : γ_K ∈ g · G(K)_{Ω,0+} · g⁻¹}`, which is the asserted
`comap (rationalExtend F K G)`. This is the identity `G(k)^{0+}_F = G(K)^{0+}_F ∩ G(k)` of
§9.5.

## Scope

- `C`, `E`, `g`, every characterized model `D` and every reductive quotient `Q` are
  arbitrary. The full model `D.full` is unconstrained.
- The model is characterized by its points over the integers of the maximal unramified
  extension, never only by its `𝒪[F]`-points.
- Nothing is assumed about facet incidence, pairs of models, model morphisms, model
  existence, availability of full models, `IntegralModelAxiomsStatement`, `FacetAxiomClauses`,
  or any inclusion of positive radicals. If no model satisfies the hypotheses, the statement
  is vacuous for that subset.
- The statement does not use Theorem 8.4.19, so the faulty index set in 8.4.19(1) plays no
  role.

## Corrections

Corrections PDF pp. 37–38 make the following changes:

- add the perfect-residue-field hypothesis to §§8.3–8.4, with `G_K` quasi-split by
  Corollary 2.3.8;
- replace the second paragraph of 8.3.6 (`T = Z_G(S)` is a maximal `K`-torus, and `S → 𝒯^0`
  is a closed immersion);
- replace the end of the proof of Theorem 8.3.13 (its conclusion `G(K)^0_Ω = 𝒢^0_Ω(O)` is
  unchanged);
- define the root groups of `𝒢^0_Ω` in 8.4.2;
- fix the proof of Lemma 8.4.4 (`k` → `K`);
- insert the map `ρ_{Ω,Ω'}` before 8.4.15.

Pages 33–34 add Proposition 7.3.12(6), and page 50 amends B.10.8(2) (induced `K`-torus),
B.10.8(4) and Remark B.10.15. None of these changes the statements of 8.4.3, Corollaries 8.4.5
and 8.4.12, Theorem 8.4.10 or Lemma B.10.11, or the text of §9.5 used above. Local fields
satisfy the added hypothesis.
