# Actual special-fibre quotients and parabolic images for KP models

This focused interface supplies actual group schemes and expanded predicates for parts of Kaletha–Prasad Axiom 4.1.22. The source is *Bruhat–Tits theory: a new approach* (2023), printed pp.163–164, PDF pp.194–195. The authors' 1 November 2024 corrections, PDF p.27, retain 4.1.22 in the corrected ten-axiom Definition 4.1.26. No radical, reductive quotient, model or building existence assertion is proved here.

All data are over the actual residue field of a local domain `R`, with fraction field `K`, in arbitrary universe `u`. They use the published `RelativeComponentModelData`, `RelativeComponentModelHom`, and `IntegralTorusData` on the same original generic group. This interface specializes to valuation rings of nonarchimedean local fields in either characteristic. Smooth connected total spaces, connected fibres, neutral special fibres and reductive quotients retain their distinct meanings.

`SpecialFibreQuotientData D` supplies a radical candidate, its map into `D.neutral.specialFibre`, an actual affine quotient candidate and its projection. Affineness is supplied to support actual schematic images and subsequent root interfaces. `IsReductiveQuotient` separately asserts that the inclusion is the represented ground-field unipotent radical, that the projection is the represented fppf quotient by that same all-test subgroup, and that the quotient is connected reductive. The imported radical predicate asserts closedness, smoothness, connectedness, unipotence, normality and greatest containment among all such ground-field subgroups. The imported fppf predicate asserts flatness, surjectivity and local finite presentation, with the actual scheme kernel equal to the specified subgroup functor. It does not assert surjectivity on every test's rational points. These predicates characterize the quotient `G_F` used in Axiom 4.1.22(3),(5),(6); they do not prove the requisite algebraic existence or perfect-field reductivity results.

For a supplied morphism `φ` with source corresponding to `F'` and target corresponding to `F`, the declarations map to the original clauses as follows:

| Original clause | Elaborated Lean declaration and formula |
| --- | --- |
| 4.1.22(2), PDF195 | `specialFibreImage` and `specialFibreImageInclusion` are the actual schematic image of the neutral special-fibre map and its inclusion. `HasParabolicSpecialFibreImage` asserts `IsParabolicSubgroup` of this inclusion into the actual neutral special fibre. |
| 4.1.22(3), first paragraph, PDF195 | `targetTorusInclusion` composes the source integral torus's actual residue inclusion with the neutral residue map; `quotientTorusInclusion` then composes with the actual quotient projection. `HasMaximalSplitTori` asserts maximal splitness of this same torus in the source and target neutral special fibres and in the quotient. Each imported maximal-split predicate includes splitness and closed immersion, which expresses an isomorphism onto the claimed torus image. The host also retains `SatisfiesTorusClause` on the original integral torus. |
| 4.1.22(3), second paragraph, and (5), PDF195 | `reductiveImage` and `reductiveImageInclusion` are the schematic image of the same neutral map followed by the quotient projection. `HasParabolicReductiveImage` asserts the parabolicity used by those clauses. It does not yet state the Lie-weight sign formula or the facet order bijection. |
| 4.1.22(4), PDF195 | `specialFibreImagePoints` is the rational-point image of the closed inclusion of the image group scheme. `HasParabolicReductionPreimage` equates its preimage under the actual target reduction map with the image of the actual source integral-section map. Thus `G^0_{F'}(R)` is compared inside `G^0_F(R)`, without identifying unrelated section types. |
| 4.1.22(7), PDF195 | `radicalPoints`, `positiveSections` and `positivePoints` form the radical's rational-point subgroup, its actual reduction preimage and its image in the original group `G(K)`. `SatisfiesPositiveRadicalClause` requires surjectivity of the two actual reduction maps and the inclusion of target positive points in source positive points. This is precisely the direction `G(K)^{0+}_F ≤ G(K)^{0+}_{F'}`. |

`mem_positivePoints` unfolds subgroup preimage and image membership to the witnessing integral section. `positivePoints_le_integralPoints` retains that same section as a witness for membership in the neutral integral-point image. These are intrinsic consequences of the definitions, with no structural axiom proved.

The host task must quantify over the actual comparable bounded subsets and facets in the actual building, assert the quotient predicates on the specified data and supply the integral-torus conditions. The Lie-weight sign formula of (3), the order bijection of (5), and the exact derivative-root datum of (6) remain separate work. The existing preamble and (1) predicates remain in `ModelMorphism`. This unit is not the complete corrected Definition 4.1.26 validity predicate.
