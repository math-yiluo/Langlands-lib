# The torus Iwahori contribution to unramified neutral integral points

This internal construction uses a supplied full one-fixer model and its relative
identity component, together with an unramified zero datum `E` whose maximal
torus inclusion `E.i` represents the whole centralizer of the building datum's
maximal split torus. The original generic map `E.i` is retained throughout.
All declarations allow arbitrary universe parameter and arbitrary apartment
subset, without boundedness or nonemptiness assumptions.

`compatibleNeutralCentralizerModel` is the actual smooth integral centralizer
inside the neutral model, using `E.T` as its prescribed generic representative.
Given `UnramifiedTorusIwahoriModelStatement` and
`UnramifiedTorusConnectedBoundStatement`, its integral-point image is exactly
`E.torusIwahori`. These are explicit premises; this module does not prove either
torus statement.

For the reverse inclusion, the norm-defined Iwahori subgroup first has unit
values under every rational character for the prescribed fixed-field
valuation. Equivalence with the normalized integer valuation preserves this
condition. The canonical isomorphism between two representatives of the
whole-torus centralizer then transports the points into the building datum's
centralizer. V6 shows that their translations are zero, so they fix every
apartment point. Character restriction also puts their ambient images in the
ambient character kernel. Thus these images belong to the full model's
integral points.

A connected smooth integral model supplied by the torus-model premise maps
into the full model by the published `IntegralModel.smoothHomExtension`.
Geometric connectedness factors this actual model homomorphism through the
relative identity component. Consequently all the torus Iwahori points lie in
the neutral model. The constructed integral centralizer's closed compatible
map characterizes its integral points as the inverse image of the neutral
integral points, giving the desired reverse inclusion. The connected bound
supplies the forward inclusion.

Finally `compatibleTorusIwahori_sup_sharpFixer_eq_neutral` substitutes this
comparison into the actual root-centralizer generation theorem. The definition
`compatibleTorusIwahoriSharpFixerEquiv` identifies the subgroup generated by the
specified torus Iwahori image and all apartment root fixers with the neutral
integral-point subgroup. Its accompanying coercion theorem states that the
equivalence preserves ambient rational points.

This is derived supporting content for KP Theorem 8.3.13. It neither constructs
the full one-fixer model nor identifies the generated subgroup with the whole
intersection of `E.zeroPoints` and the pointwise fixer.
