# Actual unramified facet-image order

This internal composition proves order reflection for actual special-fibre and reductive-quotient images of supplied models characterized by full downstairs facets. All results are at arbitrary universe `u`. The provenance is the published full-facet restriction and zero-fixer recovery theorems, the preceding conditional full-facet zero-fixer equality, actual special-fibre image recovery, and the proved parabolic correspondence through a unipotent quotient. No new original-literature assertion is made.

The downstairs data `B` are valuation building data over a nonarchimedean local field `F`. The upstairs data `C` are maximal-unramified building data for an affine group scheme `H` over `UnramifiedClosure F`, with a supplied zero datum `E`. The homomorphism `ρ`, point map `e`, and affine apartment map `a` satisfy downstairs apartment axioms and common point apartments, equivariance, apartment compatibility, and exact pullback of upstairs affine facets through points of the apartment image. The upstairs group need not be specified as a base change, and the point map need not be assumed injective or surjective.

`UnramifiedZeroDatum.descendedFacet_precedes_iff_zeroFixer_le` proves that `Fc ≺ Fd` is equivalent to the zero fixer of `e '' Fd.val` being contained in that of `e '' Fc.val`. The no-inversions proposition is an explicit premise. Each downstairs facet image is a nonempty subset of its assigned full upstairs facet. Under no inversions, the zero fixer of this subset equals that of the whole upstairs facet. Forward incidence transfers upstairs by `facetRestrictionEmbedding_facetPrecedes_iff` and then reverses full-facet pointwise-fixer inclusion. Conversely, inclusion of the two actual zero fixers reflects upstairs incidence by `facetPrecedes_of_buildingZeroFixer_le`; exact facet restriction reflects that incidence downstairs.

The model results use a Henselian local domain `R` with fraction field `UnramifiedClosure F` and algebraically closed residue field. Two supplied relative component source models map compatibly to one target by generic-identity homomorphisms `φ` and `ψ`. Their neutral integral-point subgroups equal the actual zero fixers of the respective full downstairs facet images. Each source subgroup is separately required to contain the target neutral congruence subgroup.

Under these hypotheses, `unramifiedSpecialFibreImage_le_iff_descendedFacetPrecedes` identifies inclusion of the actual neutral special-fibre schematic-image subgroup functors with reverse incidence of the two full downstairs facets. The image inclusion is on every residue-scheme test. The proof first uses `specialFibreImage_le_iff_integralPoints_le` to recover integral-point inclusion, rewrites by the characterizations, and applies the zero-fixer incidence equivalence.

For a supplied reductive special-fibre quotient `Q`, assume additionally that both actual special-fibre images are parabolic. Then `unramifiedReductiveImage_le_iff_descendedFacetPrecedes` proves the same equivalence for the actual schematic images in `Q.quotient`. The theorem `parabolicReductiveImage_and_comap` makes the quotient images parabolic and identifies their full inverse images with the original special-fibre images. The order isomorphism `Q.parabolicComapOrderIso` therefore reflects and preserves their inclusion. `HasDescendedFacetImageOrder` packages the two conditional equivalences, and `hasDescendedFacetImageOrder` proves this proposition under the geometric hypotheses.

This module does not prove no inversions, the congruence containments, or parabolicity of the supplied images. It does not construct source models, descend images or parabolics from the maximal-unramified residue field, prove surjectivity onto parabolics, or assert the host descended order bijection.
