# A common centralizer radical subgroup

This internal supporting construction chooses one subgroup `H` before every
apartment subset, characterized model and represented unipotent radical. Its
conclusion is conditional on the existing torus-centralizer radical-intersection
statement. Under that premise, the intersection of each full upstairs radical
preimage with the image of the generic centralizer is exactly `H`.

If a characterized smooth zero-fixer model on a nonempty single-facet apartment
subset exists, choose one and take the image in the original generic group of
the intrinsic centralizer radical preimage. Intrinsic radical-preimage
independence shows that the choice of model, subset and represented radical
does not affect the subgroup. The conditional ambient comparison identifies
it with the centralizer part of any supplied ambient radical preimage. If
there is no characterized model, choose the trivial subgroup and the universal
model clause is vacuous.

Every element of `H` is integral in the chosen centralizer model, so its ambient
image lies in the original zero subgroup and fixes some apartment point.
Centralizer points act by translation, hence fix the whole apartment once they
fix one point. This proves both bounds on `H` from the original upstairs target.
The construction does not prove generation by positive root fixers. The
radical-intersection premise must be discharged before it can complete that
target without added hypotheses.

The Prop-valued statement packages this conditional result as an implication
from `TorusCentralizerUnipotentRadicalStatement.{u}`. Its accompanying proof
establishes that implication at arbitrary universe `u`. The premise remains
explicit and is not added to the original upstairs root-generation statement.
