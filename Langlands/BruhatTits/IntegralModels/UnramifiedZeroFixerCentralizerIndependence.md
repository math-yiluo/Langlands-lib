# Independence of integral centralizer models

This internal supporting result compares the actual centralizer models constructed
inside any two smooth maximal-unramified zero-fixer models characterized by
nonempty apartment subsets. The subsets need not belong to the same facet.

The integral points of each centralizer are the inverse image of its ambient
model's integral points under the original centralizer inclusion. A centralizer
point acts on the apartment by translation, so fixing any point forces it to fix
the entire apartment. Therefore the inverse images of the two pointwise fixers
coincide, and the centralizer integral-point subgroups coincide.

The strictly Henselian smooth homomorphism extension theorem then extends the
generic identity in each direction. Generic rigidity makes the two extensions
inverse. The resulting isomorphism retains the specified generic identification
with the original centralizer. This is a derived consequence of the published
integral-centralizer construction, apartment translation formula, smooth
extension theorem and generic rigidity. It does not assert radical-preimage
independence or the full root-generation theorem.
