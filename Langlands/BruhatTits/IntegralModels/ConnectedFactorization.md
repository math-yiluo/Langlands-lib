# Connected-source factorization and neutral integral-point images

This is an internal consequence of the Library's relative identity-component
criterion and its proved geometric connectedness helper. It supplies the
factorization step used in the corrected proof of Kaletha–Prasad Theorem
8.3.13, but does not construct the torus or root models, prove their
connectedness, or prove the equality of neutral integral points and the KP
zero subgroup.

## Group-scheme factorization

All group schemes have the same arbitrary base scheme `S`. The source group
`H` has geometrically connected fibres, expressed by
`GeometricallyConnected H.X.hom`; no smoothness or affineness assumption is
needed for this step. A supplied open representative `i : N ⟶ G` of the
relative identity component satisfies `IsRelativeIdentityComponent G i`.

`inRelativeIdentityComponent_hom f` applies the published
`inRelativeIdentityComponent_of_geometricallyConnected` to the actual
homomorphism `f : H ⟶ G`. Its unit compatibility is exactly
`IsMonHom.one_hom`. The fibre-image criterion therefore holds on all of `H`.
`range_hom_subset_range` translates this to containment in the topological
range of `i`, using the proved equality between that range and the relative
identity set.

`factor f hi` uses the Library's open-immersion lift to construct an actual
group-scheme homomorphism `H ⟶ N`. `factor_comp` proves its composite with
`i` is `f`. `existsUnique_factor` proves uniqueness by cancellation through
the open immersion.

## Integral-point images

Let `D : RelativeComponentModelData R K G`, where `R` is a domain and `K`
is its fraction field. Let `M` be an integral model of a possibly different
generic group `H`, with geometrically connected fibres. Given
`φ : M.Hom D.full f`, `map_mem_neutralPoints` says that the generic
homomorphism `f` sends every element of `M.integralPoints` into
`D.neutralPoints`. The proof chooses its integral section, applies the
connected-source criterion to `φ.hom`, and restricts it along that section.
The generic-point equality is the model morphism's published naturality
lemma.

`map_integralPoints_le_neutral` restates this as a subgroup-image inclusion
in `D.neutral.integralPoints`, using `D.neutralPoints_eq`.
`iSup_map_integralPoints_le_neutral` combines these inclusions for any
indexed family of compatible models. The supremum is the subgroup
generated by the union of the images. The indexing universe is independent
of the universe of the rings and schemes.

These statements supply an inclusion only. The reverse inclusion in KP
8.3.13 additionally needs the connected-generator and finite-index
argument of KP Lemma A.4.26.
