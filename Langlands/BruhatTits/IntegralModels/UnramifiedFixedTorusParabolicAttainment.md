This internal supporting unit proves maximality of the chosen residue apartment
torus and realizes every supplied parabolic containing its image in the
reductive quotient. Realization remains conditional on
`UnramifiedRootPositiveReductionStatement`. All declarations have arbitrary
universe `u`.

local field F. Supply relative component model data D whose neutral model is
characterized by the zero fixer at an apartment point x. The chosen closed
integral apartment torus has generic fibre identified with C's maximal split
torus. The integer ring is Henselian and its residue field is algebraically
closed. The published specialization theorem therefore makes this same
torus's special-fibre inclusion a maximal torus in the actual neutral special
fibre. For every supplied reductive quotient Q, its kernel is the represented
smooth connected unipotent radical. Preservation of maximal tori under such
quotients over a perfect field makes the same residue torus maximal in Q.

Now supply an actual parabolic inclusion i:P→Q.quotient and a factorization of
that fixed torus through i. The published fixed-torus parabolic classification
provides a cocharacter of this very torus whose dynamic parabolic equals the
range of i on every scheme test. The prescribed-cocharacter quotient-image
equality theorem selects a positive incident ray parameter. Every supplied

then has precisely the range of i as its schematic quotient image.

conclusion, and `hasFixedTorusIncidentQuotientAttainment` proves it. The positive

The result supplies neither those source models nor their comparisons. It
requires the specified parabolic to contain the fixed residue torus; passing
