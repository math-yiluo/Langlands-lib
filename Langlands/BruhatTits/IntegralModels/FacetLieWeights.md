The former also uses the specified generic-fibre isomorphism to the original maximal split torus. For a split integral torus over a local domain with fraction field, the published `IsSplitTorus.characterLattice_baseChange_bijective` applies both to the fraction field and to the residue field. `genericCharacterEquiv`, `specialCharacterEquiv`, and `characterSpecialization` package these bijective maps as additive equivalences. These helpers are derived constructions implementing the source's identification of the generic and special character lattices; they introduce no additional chosen lattice map. `characterSpecialization_genericCharacter` cancels the generic-character equivalence and proves that both resulting characters come from the same integral character.

The Lie algebra is the published dual-number identity kernel with its scalar structure; `weightSpace` tests eigenvector identities after every coefficient extension. For the closed parabolic inclusion containing the indicated torus, its injective differential identifies this image with its Lie algebra and its torus action with the ambient adjoint action. Thus this formula expresses the source's weights in the Lie algebra of the parabolic, without a separately supplied representation or weight list.
