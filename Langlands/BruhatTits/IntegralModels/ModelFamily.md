# The actual indexed family of KP integral models

This supplied-data interface packages Axiom 4.1.20 and the general preamble and part (1) of Axiom 4.1.22 of Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits theory: a new approach* (2023), printed pp.163–164 / PDF pp.194–195. The November 2024 corrections, PDF p.27, include both axioms in corrected Definition 4.1.26. This unit does not assert that the supplied data exist or satisfy these predicates.

The apartment closure is ordinary topological closure for the initial topology of all real affine functionals. This is the intrinsic real affine topology of the finite-dimensional apartment, without a chosen origin or norm. In particular, it is not the union of closures of facets meeting a subset in Definition 4.1.14. The source's notation `Ω ≺ Ω′` means `Ω ⊆ closure Ω′` (Notation 1.3.34, printed p.26 / PDF p.57, recalled on printed p.158 / PDF p.189). The intrinsic continuity proof uses the defining infimum of induced topologies. The bounded-closure proof applies continuity to put the image of a closure in the closure of the image, whose upper bound is unchanged; nonemptiness follows from inclusion in the closure.

`IsNonemptyBoundedApartmentSubset` uses a nonempty bounded subset of the existing apartment and its image under an actual translated chart `B.translatedApartmentMap g`. Boundedness is the published condition that every real affine functional is bounded above on the subset. `BuildingModelIndex` consists of actual subsets of the existing reduced building satisfying this condition, together with all actual building facets. Facets are included explicitly so that the common interface can be defined before establishing their boundedness from the surrounding affine-root-system axioms. This introduces no model on an arbitrary unrelated set. Every index is nonempty, as proved from its presentation. Equal actual subsets have equal indices by subtype extensionality, including two different apartment presentations of the same subset.

`ApartmentSubsetPrecedes` uses presentations in one actual translated chart and ordinary apartment closure. `ModelMorphismRequired` admits these ordered pairs and the incident facets of the already defined `FacetPrecedes` interface. The latter is a second presentation of the source's facet arrows, retained before the surrounding building validity identifies the affine-root facet-closure formula with topological closure. These are precisely the general and facet cases for which the source requires comparison morphisms. They do not impose a new arbitrary ordering. All these arrows belong to the same family and have the source-to-target direction `Ω′ → Ω`.

`BuildingModelFamily` supplies one `RelativeComponentModelData` for each such actual subset and actual identity-generic model morphisms for all required arrows. Its torus for every chart presentation has generic torus `S` with conjugated inclusion `i ≫ Int(g)`, so it represents the maximal split torus for the apartment `g A(S)` rather than retaining the unmodified inclusion. The `facetModels` adapter restricts this same family to the whole-building `FacetModelData`; it introduces no separate facet models or morphisms. The index and adapter constructions are internal packaging of the source's common family, with provenance in the existing quotient-building charts and facet interface.

`SatisfiesModelAxioms` is a Prop-valued definition of `ModelAxiomClauses`, whose named fields expose each assertion. It first requires that the supplied `ParahoricBuildingData` has exactly the building datum used throughout. The full model's integral-point image equals the intersection of the actual norm-one subgroup with the actual pointwise fixer. The neutral model's image equals the corresponding intersection with the same zero subgroup used in the building data. These intersections do not replace a pointwise fixer by a generated parahoric. The neutral model is the actual relative identity component, and both models are smooth affine models with compatible generic fibres, by `RelativeComponentModelData`. Only the neutral model is required to have connected fibres; connectedness of the full model's total space does not assert connectedness of its special fibre (Remark 4.1.19).

The four remaining fields state all numbered clauses of Axiom 4.1.20. Facet constancy and ordinary closure invariance require actual model isomorphisms whose generic fibres are the identity, instead of equality between chosen scheme presentations. Every apartment presentation has the closed integral split torus and its maximal-split special fibre before taking any reductive quotient. For a semisimple simply connected group the actual component inclusion is an isomorphism; `IsSimplyConnected` already includes semisimplicity.

`SatisfiesGeneralMorphismAxioms` asserts the entire general special-fibre preamble and part (1) of Axiom 4.1.22 for every supplied required arrow: preservation of every torus, including nonsplit tori, and a smooth connected unipotent scheme-theoretic kernel. The actual full and neutral morphisms already have the identity generic fibre and commute with component inclusions; their integral-point naturality recovers the source inclusions. The definitions of these properties remain those of `ModelMorphism`.

This focused unit does not include the facet-only parts (2)–(7), the other eight axiom groups of corrected Definition 4.1.26, or the comparison of the chart-defined polysimplicial structure with the full building. Those are surrounding assembly obligations. The arbitrary local integral domain and fraction field parameters specialize to the local field's valuation ring; that specialization and all structural existence proofs remain separate.
