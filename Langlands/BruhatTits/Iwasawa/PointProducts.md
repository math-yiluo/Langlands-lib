# Product propositions on actual parabolic point carriers

Author: Worker 1265.

This module defines the conclusion bodies used to state Iwasawa decomposition
for the actual rational points of algebraic groups. It constructs no building,
parahoric or compact subgroup, and proves no decomposition. Its subgroup
parameter `K` will be instantiated with the separately constructed special
parahoric `P_x` and the shared special compact `K0` in the geometric statement
targets. A proposition parameterized by `K` is not the general Iwasawa theorem
or the required building-existence assertion.

## Exact carriers and products

The common parameters are an arbitrary universe `u`, a field `F : Type u`,
and `G : Langlands.GroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of F))`. The actual point
group is the published `Langlands.GroupScheme.rationalPoints G`, definitionally
the morphisms `Over.mk (𝟙 (Spec F)) ⟶ G.X`, with the group-object law.

`PointProductEqualities P K` is the conjunction

\[
 G(F)=P K\quad\text{and}\quad G(F)=K P.
\]

Each equality is an equality of sets in this exact point group: `G(F)` is
`Set.univ` and multiplication is the pointwise set product. It is not the
subgroup generated by the two factors, their join in the subgroup lattice, or
an assertion about density or closure.

The six family predicates retain the actual carriers:

| Predicate | Subgroup in the first product factor |
| --- | --- |
| `AlgebraicParabolicProducts K` | `(rationalPoints.map j).range` for every actual algebraic inclusion `j : P ⟶ G` satisfying the existing `IsParabolicSubgroup j` |
| `DynamicParabolicProducts K` | Evaluation of `Dynamic.parabolic χ` at the identity test scheme, for every rational cocharacter `χ : Gm ⟶ G` |
| `StandardParabolicProducts i ν K` | The existing `standardParabolicPoints i ν J`, for every `J ⊆ simpleRelativeRoots i ν` |
| `AlgebraicConjugateParabolicProducts K` | The range of the actual point map of `j ≫ (G.innerIso g).hom`, for every such algebraic parabolic and every `g ∈ G(F)` |
| `DynamicConjugateParabolicProducts K` | Evaluation of `Dynamic.parabolic (χ ≫ (G.innerIso g).hom)` |
| `StandardConjugateParabolicProducts i ν K` | Evaluation of `(standardParabolic i ν J).comap (G.innerIso g).inv` |

Each row asserts both product orders. The last three use algebraic conjugation
by `g`, hence the intended subgroup is `g P(F) g⁻¹`. The inverse in the last
row matters: pullback through `Int(g)` itself would instead give `g⁻¹ P(F) g`.
The equalities identifying these literal carriers with images under the point
homomorphism `x ↦ g x g⁻¹` are separate intrinsic adapter laws; they are not
assumed as certificates or proved here.

The standard predicates also take an explicit commutative group scheme `T`,
a homomorphism `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G`, a cocharacter `ν`, and
`[IsAffineHom G.X.hom]`, exactly as required by the existing standard-parabolic
construction. `ParabolicPointProductFamily i ν K` conjoins the six predicates
with one fixed `K`, before any quantification over `J`, `χ`, `j` or `g`.
The empty index and full simple-root index are included, as are empty
relative-root sets, trivial cocharacters and the improper parabolic. The
standard constructions remain their published intersection definitions; this
module adds no classification or representability claim for them.

These definitions need neither reductivity nor a valuation or topology.
The final geometric statements must impose their own actual connected
reductive, maximal split torus, minimal parabolic, field and valid-building-data
hypotheses. No `K` is described here as geometric, compact, open or maximal.

## Source and derived provenance

The propositions are internal derived formulations of the two product orders
in Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023), Theorem 5.3.4,
printed pp.190–191 / PDF pp.221–222. Registered source:
`kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`, SHA-256
`05ad9a284a2577b34d1a270228b74dfb28f520561ae7772d8f38dddb78938732`.
Chapter 5's hypotheses are a discretely valued field with Henselian valuation
ring and perfect residue field, a connected reductive group, and its actual
building; see printed p.186 / PDF p.217. The theorem applies to every special
vertex `x` and every algebraic parabolic, with `K = P_x` its parahoric.
The generic predicates in this module do not assert the theorem without
those hypotheses.

The mandatory *Corrections and additions*, 1 November 2024, registered as
`kaletha-prasad-2024-corrections`, SHA-256

They leave the statement of Theorem 5.3.4 unchanged. Corrected 5.3.3 and

Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, 9 July 2026 edition,

## Topology and remaining geometric targets

For the local-field application the field parameters will be
`[Field F] [ValuativeRel F] [TopologicalSpace F]`
`[IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F]`, covering both characteristics. Affineness
comes from the explicit connected-reductive hypothesis, and the topology on
this point group is the named canonical
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.PointTopology.rationalPointTopology F G.X`;
subgroups have the induced topology. The product equalities themselves
do not depend on a topology, and this module installs none.

The geometric targets still must quantify over the actual valid corrected
building data and every special reduced-building vertex, use its actual
`P_x` and the sibling's same `K0 = G(F)^1 ∩ Stab(x)`, and retain `P_x ≤ K0`
without identifying either group with the full reduced fixer. A separate
general-group target must explicitly conclude existence of those data and
a special vertex. No such existence, Iwasawa decomposition, flag transitivity,
or quotient compactness is supplied as a hypothesis by this module.
Proofs of the product conclusions and the deductions about `G/P` remain
separate obligations.
