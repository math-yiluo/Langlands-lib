# Iwasawa decomposition on available corrected Bruhat–Tits data

Author: Worker 1265.

`Langlands.BruhatTits.Iwasawa.AvailableIwasawaStatement` is a Prop-valued
statement target for KP Theorem 5.3.4 and the consequence for the same special
maximal compact subgroup. It proves neither assertion and asserts no existence
of building data. Its sole building-validity premise is the full published
`KPBuildingAssignment.IsAvailableFor`, the formalization of the corrected KP
Definition 4.1.26.

## Parameters and full validity

For an arbitrary universe `u`, the statement quantifies over every
`F : Type u` with
`[Field F] [ValuativeRel F] [TopologicalSpace F]`
`[IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F]`, every actual group scheme `G` over
`Spec F`, and `hG : IsConnectedReductive G`. Both equal and mixed
characteristic are included. There is no splitness, quasi-splitness, tameness,
large residue-characteristic or positive split-rank restriction.
The affine instance is specifically `hG.isAffineHom`.

For every `𝔅 : KPBuildingAssignment.{u}`, assume
`𝔅.IsAvailableFor F G hG`, and set `B := (𝔅.datum F G hG).base`.
Here `B : ParahoricBuildingData G` contains the actual chosen maximal split
torus `B.i : B.S.toGrp ⟶ G`, its apartment of root-datum valuations, its
reduced building, the centralizer of that same torus, and the zero-subgroup
data defining its parahorics. The statement then quantifies over every
`ν : cocharacterLattice B.S` satisfying `IsGenericCocharacter B.i ν`.
This fixes the positive system and the standard minimal parabolic
`P_∅` used for the standard families. No extra torus is chosen independently
of the valid data.

`IsAvailableFor` contains all ten corrected axioms: 4.1.1, 4.1.2, 4.1.4,
4.1.6, 4.1.8, 4.1.9, 4.1.16, 4.1.17, 4.1.20 and 4.1.22. In particular,
it retains the comparisons between groups in 4.1.1 and the valued-field
isomorphism comparisons in 4.1.17. Neither the one-group predicate
`KPBuildingDatum.SatisfiesGroupAxioms` nor the partial apartment/building
axioms are used as substitutes. This target does not require the stronger
global assertion `𝔅.IsValid`.

## Two forms of the same geometric conclusion

The conclusion is a conjunction of an explicit apartment form and a form on
the whole reduced building.

For every `x : B.Apartment` with `B.apartment.IsSpecialApartmentVertex x`,
write

\[
 P_x=B.\mathrm{parahoricAt}(x),\qquad
 K_0=B.\mathrm{apartment}.\mathrm{specialCompactSubgroup}(x).
\]

The special-vertex predicate is the conjunction of special-point and vertex
predicates for the affine roots of this reduced apartment. The parahoric is
the existing subgroup generated by the image of `Z(F)^0` and
`G(F)^♯_x`, namely the published `P_x = Z(F)^0 · G(F)^♯_x` construction.
The compact subgroup is literally
`G(F)^1 ∩ Stab(B.apartmentMap x)`. The conclusion states:

1. both product orders for `P_x`, in every actual-parabolic family below;
2. `P_x ≤ K_0`;
3. both product orders for this same `K_0`, in all those families;
4. `IsCompactOpenMaximal F K_0`.

For every `y : B.ReducedBuilding` with `B.IsSpecialPoint y` and
`B.IsVertex y`, and every `Q` with `B.IsParahoricAt y Q`, the second clause
states the same four assertions with `Q` and `B.compactStabilizer y`.
`IsParahoricAt` is the published actual geometric relation: there are
`g ∈ G(F)` and `x ∈ B.Apartment` with
`y = g • B.apartmentMap x` and
`Q = (B.parahoricAt x).map (MulAut.conj g).toMonoidHom`.
It is not a condition that `Q` satisfy a product decomposition. This clause
therefore explicitly covers special vertices throughout the reduced building,
including their actual conjugate parahorics. It does not assume independence
of a chosen chart as an extra certificate. At an apartment point,
`B.compactStabilizer (B.apartmentMap x)` is definitionally the `K_0` above.

The inclusion is part of each conclusion, rather than a premise. Its
apartment form is the independently stated
`Langlands.BruhatTits.SpecialCompact.ParahoricLeCompactStabilizer`;
this module does not assume that proposition as a proof.
Enlarging a factor from `P_x` to `K_0` gives the asserted compact-subgroup
product consequence. Neither subgroup is replaced by the full reduced
fixer, and the statement does not identify `P_x` with `K_0`.

## Actual parabolics and both literal products

Each occurrence of `ParabolicPointProductFamily B.i ν K` expands to the six
published predicates from `Iwasawa.PointProducts`, with that one fixed `K`:

| Family | Actual subgroup of `G(F)` |
| --- | --- |
| algebraic | `(rationalPoints.map j).range`, for every `j : P ⟶ G` with `IsParabolicSubgroup j` |
| dynamic | `(Dynamic.parabolic χ).carrier` at the identity test scheme, for every rational cocharacter `χ` |
| standard | `standardParabolicPoints B.i ν J`, for every `J ⊆ simpleRelativeRoots B.i ν` |
| algebraic conjugate | range of the point map of `j ≫ (G.innerIso g).hom` |
| dynamic conjugate | points of `Dynamic.parabolic (χ ≫ (G.innerIso g).hom)` |
| standard conjugate | points of `(standardParabolic B.i ν J).comap (G.innerIso g).inv` |

For each row with subgroup `H`, both assertions are literal set equalities
in the actual group `rationalPoints G`:

\[
 \mathrm{Set.univ}=H K,\qquad \mathrm{Set.univ}=K H.
\]

Multiplication is pointwise set multiplication. In particular, the
parahoric product conclusion is not a subgroup join. All indices, including
the empty and full simple-root sets, are included, as are the improper
parabolic and root-rank-zero cases.

The imported `Rational.ParabolicPointConjugation` supplies the published
intrinsic adapters:
`rationalPoints_range_comp_innerIso`,
`Dynamic.rationalPoints_parabolic_conjugate`, and
`standardParabolicPoints_comap_innerIso_inv`.
They identify the last three carriers with `g H g⁻¹` on actual rational
points. The inverse in the standard pullback is essential:
pullback through `(G.innerIso g).hom` instead has the `g⁻¹ H g` orientation.
No conjugation law is added as an assumption.

## Canonical topology and Renard S19(1)

The topology used by `IsCompactOpenMaximal F K` is precisely
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.PointTopology.rationalPointTopology F G.X`.
That predicate installs it locally on the actual point carrier, using the
affineness coming from `hG`. It says that `K` is compact and open, and
`Maximal (fun L : Subgroup (rationalPoints G) => IsCompact (L : Set _)) K`.
Thus maximality compares all compact overgroups, rather than only the
subgroups attached to building points. Subgroups have the induced topology.
The set-product assertions themselves use no topology.

For Renard V.5.1(1), consumer row S19(1) of
`notes/renard/chapter-v-structural-inputs.md`, select any special apartment
vertex supplied by the separate existence target. Keep its single
`K_0 = B.apartment.specialCompactSubgroup x`, and take `J = ∅` in the
standard family. This gives both
`G(F) = K_0 P_∅(F)` and `G(F) = P_∅(F) K_0`, with that same open maximal
compact subgroup. For the sibling special-compact construction this is
exactly its geometric `K_0`, when its torus and apartment are identified
with those of the available data. No new subgroup is chosen after the
parabolic. The general-group statement still must conclude existence of
such available data and the special vertex, while retaining those same-data
identifications. This conditional statement alone does not discharge that
obligation.
