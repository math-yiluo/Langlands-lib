# The prime support and five-part bound for E₆ root-datum automorphisms

`TypeE6AutomorphismOrderStatement` applies to an integral root datum with finite root index, a simple-root numbering by `CartanMatrix.E 6`, and coroot span equal to the entire cocharacter lattice. Its root-datum automorphism group is finite and has order dividing \(2^{13}3^8\cdot 5\). In particular, only 2, 3 and 5 can divide the order, and 5 occurs with multiplicity at most one.

In the simple coroot basis, the automorphisms act faithfully by integral matrices. The basis identifies the vectors annihilated modulo three by every root pairing with the kernel of the reduced Cartan matrix. This is the line generated by the reduction of \((1,0,2,0,1,2)\). The verification uses the finite Cartan calculation for the kernel and the integral expansion of an arbitrary root in the simple roots. The pairing comparison for a root-datum automorphism shows that the line is invariant. `finiteIntegralRankSixGroupOrderWithLine` then bounds the order.

Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, Proposition 6.21, printed pp. 375–377, use these prime divisibility properties via the E₆ Weyl group order and the diagram action. The congruence proof here derives the sufficient divisibility bound directly from the integral datum.
