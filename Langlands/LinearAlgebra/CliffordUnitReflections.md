# Clifford products of two unit-vector reflections

Over a commutative ring, `unitReflection` is the involutive linear map that sends `x` to `x - B(v,x) v`, where `Q(v) = 1` and `B` is the polar form. On a finite free module the rank-one determinant identity gives determinant minus one.

`unitReflectionPair` combines the product of two Clifford vectors of quadratic value one with the corresponding product of reflections. The Clifford product is even, has reversion norm one, and intertwines the vector action. Its action has determinant one. Consequently the pair belongs to the Clifford spin lift group on the given coefficient ring.

This is the unit-valued case of the Clifford reflection construction underlying the spinor norm in Kneser, *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups*, §5.11, p. 99. The ring-level formulation allows the reflection vectors to depend on an affine coordinate parameter.
