# A permutation basis after restriction in type E₆

`TypeE6PermutationSubgroupStatement` concerns an integral root datum with a finite root index, a base numbered by the E₆ Cartan matrix, and a cocharacter lattice generated by the coroots. For any action of a finite group by automorphisms of this datum, it gives a subgroup of index dividing \(2^{13}3^8\) and an integral basis of the weight lattice permuted by that subgroup.

`typeE6PermutationSubgroup` uses the order-five Weyl element from `CartanE6FiveCycle`. The perfect root-datum pairing transports the dual of its cocharacter basis to the weight lattice, where the element acts by the inverse permutation. The order bound from `CartanE6AutomorphismOrder` shows that the generated cyclic group is Sylow. A Sylow subgroup at five in the image of the supplied action lies in a conjugate of that cyclic group. The proof takes its preimage in the original finite group and transports the weight basis by the conjugating automorphism. The index in the image divides \(2^{13}3^8\cdot 5\) and is coprime to five, so the preimage has the claimed index.

This is an integral lattice argument for the prime-five step of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, Proposition 6.21, printed pp. 375–377 (PDF pp. 385–387). The proposition eliminates five using a type D₅ subsystem. Here the permutation basis prepares the use of Hilbert 90 for a torus induced from a finite étale algebra.
