# Simple subquotients of a tensor product with a multiplicity space

`Langlands.TensorProductSubquotientStatement` lets the source operators on `V ⊗ M` act on `V` with the identity on `M`. Suppose an equivariant map from an invariant subspace of `V ⊗ M` has an image containing a nonzero irreducible invariant subspace `J`. The proposition gives an invariant subspace of `V` and an equivariant map whose image is exactly `J`. The source and target operators may be any indexed families of linear endomorphisms over a field.

`Langlands.exists_subquotient_of_tensorProduct` chooses a basis of `M` and writes tensors in its coordinates with `TensorProduct.equivFinsuppOfBasisRight`. The coordinate maps intertwine each source operator with its action on `V`. The proof chooses a vector with the fewest nonzero coordinates among vectors whose image is nonzero in `J`. Restrict the domain to vectors supported on those coordinates whose images lie in `J`. This subspace is invariant. If evaluation at a chosen coordinate of the support vanishes on a vector in the restricted domain, that vector has smaller support, so its image in `J` vanishes. The first isomorphism theorem factors the original map through the range of coordinate evaluation. The factored map has invariant range containing the nonzero image of the chosen vector. Irreducibility makes the range equal to `J`.

This is the algebraic extraction used in Task 7041. The finite support argument is a variant of the finite-stage step in R. P. Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Lemma 1, printed p. 203, PDF p. 198 of the registered Corvallis Part I (`langlands-1979-notion-automorphic-representation`). The source uses a restricted tensor product over the places. The space `M` collects the multiplicity away from one place.
