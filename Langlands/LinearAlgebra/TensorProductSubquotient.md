# Simple subquotients of a tensor product with a multiplicity space

`Langlands.TensorProductSubquotientStatement` lets the source operators on `V ⊗ M` act on `V` with the identity on `M`. Suppose an equivariant map from an invariant subspace of `V ⊗ M` has an image containing a nonzero irreducible invariant subspace `J`. The proposition gives an invariant subspace of `V` and an equivariant map whose image is exactly `J`. The source and target operators may be any indexed families of linear endomorphisms over a field.

`Langlands.TensorProductNonzeroSubquotientStatement` requires a nonzero invariant `J` and gives an equivariant map whose image is a nonzero subspace of `J`. `Langlands.exists_nonzero_subquotient_of_tensorProduct` chooses a basis of `M` and writes tensors in its coordinates with `TensorProduct.equivFinsuppOfBasisRight`. The coordinate maps intertwine each source operator with its action on `V`. The proof chooses a vector with the fewest nonzero coordinates among vectors whose image is nonzero in `J`. Restrict the domain to vectors supported on those coordinates whose images lie in `J`. This subspace is invariant. If evaluation at a chosen coordinate of the support vanishes on a vector in the restricted domain, that vector has smaller support, so its image in `J` vanishes. The first isomorphism theorem factors the original map through the range of coordinate evaluation. The factored map has invariant range containing the nonzero image of the chosen vector.

`Langlands.exists_subquotient_of_tensorProduct` applies this construction to an irreducible `J`. The invariant range is nonzero and contained in `J`, so it equals `J`.

This is the algebraic extraction used for `CuspidalSupportEveryPlaceStatement`, through `Langlands.Global.Factors.CuspidalSupportArchimedeanTensor`. The finite support argument is a variant of the finite-stage step in R. P. Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Lemma 1, printed p. 203, PDF p. 198 of the registered Corvallis Part I (`langlands-1979-notion-automorphic-representation`). The source uses a restricted tensor product over the places. The space `M` collects the multiplicity away from one place.
