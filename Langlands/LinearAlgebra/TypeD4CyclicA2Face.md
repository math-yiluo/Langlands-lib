# A simple base adapted to the cyclic A2 face

For two D4 roots with mutual pairing minus one, the chosen cocharacter vanishes precisely on the corresponding six coroots. A large multiple of this cocharacter plus the sum of the two coroots is regular and preserves the sign away from the zero face. On the zero face its positive coroots are the original two and their sum. The first two have value one and are indecomposable among positive coroots. Their sum is decomposable. The base defined by the regular perturbation therefore has the desired pair as its simple zero roots.

This is the based root datum calculation for realizing the two cyclic A2 subgroups as derived Levi subgroups in the trialitarian argument of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed pp. 372–373.
