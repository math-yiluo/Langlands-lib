# The split D4 root signs for three leaf parabolics

In the coordinate model with roots `±eᵢ ± eⱼ` for distinct coordinates, the three cocharacters are twice the vector and half-spin weights `e₁`, `(-e₁-e₂-e₃+e₄)/2` and `(-e₁+e₂+e₃-e₄)/2`. Their sum is zero. The cocharacters have even coordinate sum. The common zero roots are the six roots of the subsystem generated by `e₂-e₃` and `e₃+e₄`, in one-based vector coordinates. Every other root has negative pairing with exactly one of the three cocharacters. Lean checks the signs by finite enumeration.

The calculation gives a configuration for the intersection of three parabolics from the distinct leaf types used in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed p. 372. The sign condition can be used to show that simultaneous conjugation has surjective differential on the product of the three parabolic quotients at this configuration.

The simple-coroot coefficient vectors sum to zero. The explicit words in simple reflections take the cocharacters to dominant cocharacters with a positive value at a single leaf. The difference of the first two cocharacters has the common zero roots of the triple and retains the simple vertices numbered 1 and 3.
