# The spinor norm from even Clifford reflection products

For a quadratic form over a field, `reflectionWordValue` multiplies the nonzero quadratic values of the vectors in a reflection expression. Assume the space is finite dimensional, the polar form is nondegenerate, and `2` is invertible. Two even words inducing the same isometry have value products in the same square class. For an even word, that class is trivial exactly when its isometry is induced by conjugation by a Clifford unit fixed by the grading with reversion norm one.

The ratio of two even Clifford units inducing the same action on the vectors is scalar by the even centralizer calculation. Reversion squares the scalar change in the value product. If the value product is a square, divide the word by a square root to get the norm-one lift.

This is the field-level Clifford calculation for the spinor norm discussed by Kneser, *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups* (1969), §5.11, p. 99.
