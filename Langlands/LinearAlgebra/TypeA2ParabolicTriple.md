# Three integral line cocharacters in type A₂

For an integral root datum with a pair of roots with off-diagonal
Cartan pairings `-1`, let `a` and `b` be their coroots. If each coroot
belongs to the list `±a`, `±b`, `±(a+b)`, the three cocharacters
`2a+b`, `-a+b`, and `-a-2b` have sum zero. Their pairing table
shows that every root is negative on exactly one cocharacter and
that the first difference pairs nontrivially with every root.
Composing the two simple coreflections gives the Weyl elements
relating the three cocharacters.

`coweightEquiv_eq_self_of_a2_of_cube_eq_one` treats a based
automorphism whose cube is the identity. The coroot span has
rank two, and the base gives a two-element simple support, so
the automorphism fixes each simple index. It consequently fixes
the coroot span pointwise. This formulation handles an ambient
lattice with additional central cocharacters and is used in
the three-parabolic descent inside the trialitarian Levi.
