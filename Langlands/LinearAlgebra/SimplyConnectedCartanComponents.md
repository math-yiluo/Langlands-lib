# Cartan components and coroot support in simply connected root data

Worker 634.

This internal root-datum helper defines the finite quotient `Component b` of
the simple indices of a base `b` by the equivalence relation generated by
nonzero Cartan entries. Distinct components have zero Cartan pairing.

When the coroots span the full integral coweight lattice, the simple coroots
form an integral basis. `CorootSupported b hspan c y` says that the coordinates
of `y` in that basis vanish outside component `c`. Every coroot of a finite
reduced root datum has a unique such component, defining `rootComponent` on
all root indices and extending the quotient map on simple indices.

The proof follows the published base induction by simple reflections. Simple
coroots have the asserted support; negation preserves it. Reflecting in a
simple index in the same component preserves support by the coroot reflection
formula. A simple root in another component annihilates a supported coweight,
as seen by expanding in the integral simple-coroot basis, so its reflection
fixes that coroot. A nonzero coweight cannot have support in two disjoint
components, which proves uniqueness.

This module is internally derived from the Library's integral simple-coroot
basis construction and Mathlib's root-datum base induction and reflection
identities. It prepares complementary tori for the affine simple-factor
construction motivated by Conrad, *Reductive group schemes* (2014),
Proposition 6.4.4, pp.232–233. It does not construct a product root datum or a
group-scheme factorization.
