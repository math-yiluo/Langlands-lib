# Rank-six hyperbolicity over a number field

`NumberFieldRankSixHyperbolicityStatement` assumes a nondegenerate symmetric
bilinear form in dimension six over a number field. Its Gram determinant is the
negative of a square in a supplied basis. At every completion the extended
form has two independent orthogonal isotropic vectors. The associated quadratic
form is isometric over the number field to three hyperbolic planes.

Extending the basis maps the Gram determinant to the corresponding scalar in the
completion and preserves nondegeneracy. The rank-six field criterion makes each
extended quadratic form hyperbolic. The standard form of three hyperbolic planes
commutes up to isometry with scalar extension by the tensor-product comparisons
for products and one-dimensional factors. Transport both forms to a common finite
coordinate space and apply the published number-field quadratic isometry theorem.

This gives the global maximal-index step in Springer, appendix to M. Kneser,
*Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups* (1969), Theorem 5.3.3(i),
printed p. 109 (PDF p. 115).
