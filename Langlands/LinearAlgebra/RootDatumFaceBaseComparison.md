# Independence of integral face heights from the adapted base

This derived unit compares two actual sign-refining coweight-face data
`F F' : RootDatumCoweightFace.Face P cv` of a finite reduced root datum.
The reflection subgroup generated by roots vanishing on `cv` is assumed finite,
as holds when the ambient Weyl group is finite.

`mem_baseOf_iff_of_signs` compares indecomposable positive-root sets under a
root-datum automorphism matching two sign systems. Pulling an additive root
decomposition back through the lattice automorphism proves the forward
implication; its inverse proves the reverse.

`nonempty_faceAlignment` aligns the regular refinements on the zero roots
using the finite reflection-orbit sign-alignment theorem. The chosen
reflection element fixes `cv`. Away from the zero roots, both refinements
already have the signs of `cv`, so all root signs match. The indecomposable
positive-root comparison and the two actual support equations then identify
the bases. `FaceAlignment` records this zero-root reflection element and its
support equivalence; `faceAlignment` chooses the proved alignment.

`faceHeight_eq` proves exact equality of the two integral face-height linear
forms on the entire root span. On the simple-root basis of `F`, composing the
height for `F'` with the aligning reflection element gives the height for `F`:
the element maps simple roots to simple roots and preserves their zero status.
The existing zero-root-reflection invariance of the face height for `F'` then
removes that composition. The equality involves no arbitrary multiplier.

Provenance is the existing finite reflection-orbit sign alignment, the actual
sign-refining face data, integral simple-root basis and zero-root-reflection
invariance of face height. The proof generalizes the existing based-correction
sign argument to two regular refinements. It asserts a root-datum statement;
comparisons of actual split group charts and descent are subsequent work.
