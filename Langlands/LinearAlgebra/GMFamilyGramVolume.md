# Gram volumes of finite vector families

Reindexing a family preserves the determinant of its Gram matrix. The matrix is positive semidefinite and is nonsingular exactly when the vectors are independent, which identifies positivity of `gramVolume`.

In orthonormal coordinates the Gram matrix is the transpose of the coordinate matrix times the coordinate matrix. Taking its determinant and square root gives the absolute coordinate determinant. Replacing a single basis vector multiplies this determinant in absolute value by the absolute value of its coordinate at the replaced index. This is the volume ratio used for basis exchanges in an affine slice.
