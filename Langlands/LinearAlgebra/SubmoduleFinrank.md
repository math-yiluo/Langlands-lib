# Finite sums of independent subspace dimensions

`Langlands.Plancherel.finrank_biSup_eq_sum` computes the dimension of a finite supremum of an independent family of subspaces as the sum of their dimensions, over any division ring and in a finite-dimensional ambient module.

The declaration and its induction proof are moved unchanged from [ParabolicRootCone](../Representation/Plancherel/ParabolicRootCone.md). Its original public name is retained. [SignedWeightCount](../GroupScheme/Torus/SignedWeightCount.md) now uses this same result without importing the representation-theoretic application.
