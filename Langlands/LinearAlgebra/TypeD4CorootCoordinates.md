# Integral coroot coordinates in type D4

The D4 numbering identifies the integral simple coroot basis with the standard signed coordinate differences and final coordinate sum. `d4CorootCoordinates_dotProduct_coroot` starts with the Cartan pairing on simple coroots and uses induction under simple reflections to extend the dot product formula to every root. Each coroot consequently has squared norm two and is a vector with two signed coordinates. This is the coordinate model of the simply laced type D root system used to compute the signs of the three leaf parabolics.

The coordinate map transports the three leaf cocharacters to a numbered based root datum. Their sum is zero, the difference of the first two has the common zero roots, and each cocharacter is Weyl-conjugate to one that pairs positively at just its corresponding leaf.
