# Rigidity of based full-root type-B automorphisms

A based automorphism of a root datum with numbered type-B Cartan matrix
fixes every index in the simple support. If the integral roots span the
full character lattice, the entire root-datum automorphism is the
identity, including its full character and cocharacter maps.

The induced permutation of the simple support preserves every Cartan
entry. The published rigidity of a type-B numbering therefore fixes
that permutation. Simple roots span the integral root lattice, so their
fixed values determine the character map when that span is the whole
lattice. Injectivity of the character-map representation of root-datum
automorphisms determines the complete automorphism.

This is an internal consequence of integral Cartan numbering rigidity
and the root-datum base axioms. It applies for rank at least two and
makes no field or group-scheme assumption or new external translation.
