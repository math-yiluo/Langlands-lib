# Extending intertwiners from the range of an endomorphism

Let \(f\) and \(g\) be endomorphisms of finite-dimensional spaces over a field, with equal ambient dimensions. An isomorphism \(e\) of their ranges intertwining the restricted endomorphisms extends to an isomorphism of the full spaces that intertwines \(f\) and \(g\).

To construct the extension, project onto \(\operatorname{im} f\), apply \(e\), and use a linear right inverse of \(g\) onto its range to correct the resulting map. The correction vanishes on \(\operatorname{im} f\) and gives a linear extension \(F_0\) satisfying \(gF_0=ef\). Choose a complement of \(\ker f\cap\operatorname{im} f\) inside \(\ker f\), and the corresponding complement for \(g\). The complements have the same dimension by rank-nullity and the isomorphism of the kernels of the restrictions. Adjust \(F_0\) along the first complement to map it isomorphically onto the second using a projection that kills \(\operatorname{im} f\). The resulting map is injective on \(\ker f\) and still satisfies \(gF=ef\), so it is injective everywhere. Equal ambient dimensions make it an isomorphism.

For a nilpotent endomorphism, the restriction to its range is nilpotent and has lower-dimensional domain whenever the original domain has positive dimension. The \(k\)-th power of this restriction has the rank of the \((k+1)\)-st ambient power. Thus if two nilpotent endomorphisms have the same ranks of powers from degree zero through the dimension of the first space, their restrictions meet the same hypothesis in smaller dimension. Induction gives a range intertwiner, which extends by the preceding construction. The degree-zero equality supplies equality of ambient dimensions. This is an internal proof for comparing the nilpotent restrictions to matched generalized eigenspaces.
