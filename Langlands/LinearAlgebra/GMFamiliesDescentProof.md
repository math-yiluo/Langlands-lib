# Same-field Levi descent for `(G, M)`-families

`Langlands.GMFamily.descentFormula` proves `Langlands.GMFamily.DescentFormulaStatement`. The finite exceptional set consists of the nonzero projections of the original roots to the coarse Levi space. Away from their annihilating hyperplanes the complex parameter evaluates nontrivially on every nonzero projected fine normal.

The proof expresses both sides as a sum over fine chambers. Each term is the fine family value at the extended parameter multiplied by the weighted sum of feasible vertices of the affine chamber slice. A basis at such a vertex is the projection of the vanishing simple-root basis of a transverse Levi flat. `GMFamilySliceVertices` identifies the section sign set and compares the weight with the original Gram coefficient times the reciprocal relative denominator. The relative chambers over the Levi flat are exactly the fine chambers incident to this slice point.

`GMFamilySliceCoarse` evaluates the weighted sum for each fine chamber. If the fine chamber contains a coarse chamber in its closure, the coarse simple roots give its unique projected basis. The basis is feasible precisely for the fine lift chosen by the displacement. Its weight is the inverse coarse denominator. If there is no coarse chamber, `AffineVertexWallCancellation` proves that the sum is zero. The cancellation uses the finite gallery of the inequalities in offset space. A group of changes with a common wall support sums to the parameter applied to the linear dependence among its normals. `GMFamilyInducedValues` identifies the fine value on the selected lift with the value of the induced family.
