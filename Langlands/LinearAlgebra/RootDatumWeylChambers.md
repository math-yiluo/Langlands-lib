# Integral chamber alignment by a specified reflection subgroup

For an integral root datum `P : RootDatum J M N` and a set `S` of root
indices, `reflectionSubgroup P S` is the subgroup of `P.Aut` generated by
the reflections indexed by `S`. It abbreviates the existing
`Langlands.RootDatum.reflectionSubgroup` from `Torus/LeviWeylLattice`. The ambient additive groups and index type
have independent universes. This construction and the results below are
internal consequences of Mathlib's root-pairing and canonical-form APIs;
they are not transcriptions of an external source.

The subgroup lies in `P.weylGroup`, so finiteness of that Weyl group implies
finiteness of the subgroup. Independently of finiteness, `fixes_annihilator`
proves that every element fixes each weight pairing to zero with all coroots
indexed by `S`. The inclusion and fixation proofs reuse
`Langlands.RootDatum.reflectionSubgroup_le_weylGroup` and
`Langlands.RootDatum.reflectionSubgroup_fixed`. The latter contains the
generated subgroup in the weight's stabilizer using the reflection formula.

Assume `Fintype J` and that `reflectionSubgroup P S` is finite.
For any coweights `f` and `g`, `exists_nonneg` gives a member `w` such that

```text
0 ≤ P.root' i f * P.root' i (w.val.coweightEquiv g)
```

for every `i ∈ S`. If both coweights are regular, meaning that every root
evaluates nontrivially on them, `exists_same_signs` strengthens this to
equivalence of strictly positive evaluation for every `i ∈ S`.

The proof maximizes the canonical coroot form
`P.CorootForm f (w.val.coweightEquiv g)` on the finite subgroup. Comparing
the maximum with the value after a generating reflection gives a nonnegative
product with the canonical pairing against that coroot. Applying the root
pairing to the canonical copolarization identity gives

```text
2 * P.CorootForm f (P.coroot i)
  = P.CorootForm (P.coroot i) (P.coroot i) * P.root' i f.
```

The diagonal coroot norm is a sum of integer squares with a positive term
from the self-pairing `2`. Its positivity gives the required nonnegative
product of root evaluations. Root-pairing automorphisms preserve regularity,
so in the regular case this product is strictly positive and both
evaluations have the same sign.

Neither reducedness nor spanning of either ambient group is assumed.
These results align signs only on the specified generating indices. They
do not assert that the subgroup preserves `S`, preserves signs outside
`S`, or restores a chosen root base. Those compatibility inputs and the
actual Levi geometry remain obligations for the cocenter root-model task.
