# Integral saturation of closed root sets

This internal helper uses a finite crystallographic root pairing over the rational
numbers. Reducedness is not assumed. `IsAdditivelyClosedRootSet` means that `X`
consists of roots and contains the sum of two of its members whenever that sum is
a root.

The module proves that a root in the additive submonoid generated by `X` already
belongs to `X`. The submonoid includes zero, but roots are nonzero. The assertion
concerns integral nonnegative combinations, not rational or real cones.

The implemented argument represents a submonoid member by a multiset. If its sum
is the root `β`, applying `β`'s coroot gives a sum equal to 2. Some summand `α`
therefore has positive pairing with that coroot. If `α = β`, membership is already
known. Otherwise Mathlib's crystallographic root-subtraction lemma gives
`α - β` as a root, hence also `β - α`. Strong induction after erasing `α` shows
that `β - α` belongs to `X`, and additive closedness gives `β`.

The provenance is internal: the definition is the additive root-set condition
used in the project's closed-root-subset interfaces, expressed for an abstract
root pairing. The proof is derived from Mathlib's
`RootPairing.root_sub_root_mem_of_pairingIn_pos`, closure under negation, the
root-coroot pairing identity, and the finite-sum description of additive
submonoid closure.
