# A star-fixed A₂ face in D₄

In the numbered D₄ base the center is vertex `1`. Write its simple root as \(\alpha_1\). The reflection in \(\alpha_1+\alpha_2+\alpha_3\) fixes \(\alpha_1\) and sends \(\alpha_0\) to \(\alpha_0+\alpha_1+\alpha_2+\alpha_3\). The first two roots of the reflected base therefore have Cartan matrix A₂. Every automorphism preserving the given base fixes its center and permutes all four simple roots, so it fixes the two reflected roots individually.

The calculation constructs \(\alpha_1+\alpha_2+\alpha_3\) by successive simple reflections and computes its coroot by the dual reflections. The lemma `indexEquiv_eq_of_root'_eq_zero` then extends fixation of the simple zero roots of any dominant cocharacter to all its zero roots, using their integral span.

This realizes the two invariant roots used by Platonov and Rapinchuk in *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Theorem 6.26, printed p. 354, diagram (6.37). [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
