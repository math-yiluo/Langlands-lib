# Based correction fixing a coweight face

This internal construction concerns an integral root datum `P : RootDatum J M N`
on its original perfectly paired additive groups. It uses no spanning or
reducedness hypothesis. The main correction results assume `Fintype J` and
finiteness of the subgroup generated by reflections at the roots vanishing
on a supplied coweight `ν : N`. These are explicit premises.

Write `S = {i | P.root' i ν = 0}` and let `W_S` be the reflection subgroup
defined in `RootDatumWeylChambers`. A regular coweight `f : N` satisfies
`P.root' i f ≠ 0` for every root index. It refines the signs of `ν` when, for
every index outside `S`, its root evaluation is positive exactly when that
of `ν` is positive. The construction takes such an `f` as input.

## Evaluation and positivity

`fixes_coweight` proves that every element of `W_S` fixes `ν` under its
contravariant coweight equivalence. Each generating coreflection fixes `ν`
by the reflection formula, and subgroup closure induction handles the
identity, products and inverses. `root_evaluation_eq` proves that an
automorphism fixing `ν` preserves every root evaluation on `ν`.

For a root-datum automorphism `δ` fixing `ν`,
`exists_positive_correction` produces `w : W_S` such that `θ = w * δ`
preserves positivity on every root relative to `f`, and preserves every
root evaluation on `ν`. On `S`, apply the integral finite-subgroup
chamber-alignment theorem to `δ.coweightEquiv.symm f` and `f`, then evaluate
at `δ.indexEquiv i`. Regularity is preserved by root-index permutations.
Outside `S`, the fixed-coweight evaluation identity and the refinement
premise give the positivity equivalence directly. The product convention
is the ordinary covariant convention on weights and the reverse
composition convention on coweights.

## The simple permutation and the two lattices

`mem_baseOf_iff` says that an automorphism preserving all positive roots
permutes their indecomposable positive indices. Pull a proposed positive
root decomposition back by its inverse weight equivalence and apply
indecomposability. Apply the same argument to the inverse automorphism
for the converse. This lemma needs no finiteness hypothesis.

`exists_based_correction` additionally takes a genuine base `B` whose
support is `baseOf P.root (P.flip.toLinearMap f)`. It restricts
`θ.indexEquiv` to a permutation `e` of `B.support`. This permutation
preserves the subset of simple roots vanishing on `ν`. The same `w`
fixes every weight annihilating every coroot indexed by `S`, pointwise.
This last conclusion follows from `RootDatumWeylChambers.fixes_annihilator`
and holds simultaneously on the entire annihilator.

The theorem also records, for all weights `x` and coweights `y`,

```
⟨w.weightEquiv (δ.weightEquiv x),
  w.coweightEquiv.symm (δ.coweightEquiv.symm y)⟩ = ⟨x, y⟩.
```

For every simple index `i`, the second displayed composite sends
`P.coroot i` to `P.coroot (e i)`. These identities follow directly from
the root-pairing automorphism axioms, with the inverse coweight maps
required by their contravariant convention. `Correction` packages the
reflection-subgroup member, simple permutation and all these properties;
`correction` chooses one such package by classical choice.

This is an internal algebraic deduction from Mathlib root-pairing
identities and the imported finite-subgroup alignment theorem, rather
than a transcription of a literature statement. It constructs no
algebraic group, splitting field, actual root datum or Galois action.
Existence of a compatible regular refinement and base, and the comparison
of the zero-root coroot span with an actual Levi, are separate inputs.
It asserts no group law for the choice as `δ` varies.
