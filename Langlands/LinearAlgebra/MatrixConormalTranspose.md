# Adjusted transpose on a matrix conormal

This internal lemma concerns the conormal to the sum of the conjugation tangent at a real matrix \(C\) and a subspace \(Z\) of matrices. The trace form identifies the conormal with the matrices that commute with \(C\) and are trace-orthogonal to \(Z\).

Assume that \(Z\) is trace-nondegenerate, lies in the centralizer and is fixed pointwise by the units of that centralizer and by \(A\mapsto q^{-1}A^{\mathsf T}q\), where \(qCq^{-1}=C^{\mathsf T}\). The trace projection onto \(Z^\perp\) preserves the centralizer and commutes with both actions. Projecting the centralizer conjugacy pairs and taking their limit gives the conormal result. For each conormal matrix \(A\), the pair \((A,q^{-1}A^{\mathsf T}q)\) is in the closure of centralizer conjugacy pairs whose first entries lie in the conormal.

The hypotheses concerning the actions hold whenever \(Z\) is contained in the algebra generated by \(C\). Conjugation and transposition carry polynomial evaluation at \(C\) to polynomial evaluation at the transformed \(C\).
