# Cones of an obtuse basis for a supplied positive form

This internal development supplies linear algebra for Renard V.3.18. It
constructs the Euclidean core of a symmetric positive definite form and the
nonnegative coordinate cones of a finite basis. Coordinate cones agree with
pointed hulls, and coordinate support characterizes the corresponding span.

For an obtuse basis, the negative-coordinate part of a vector proves that
nonnegative root pairings at all its negative coordinates force every coordinate
to be nonnegative. In particular, an orthogonal lift of a cone modulo selected
basis directions remains in the full nonnegative basis cone. This argument is
an internal positive-form consequence, with no classification input.

The nearest-point inequality is obtained by expanding the positive quadratic
form. Existence of a complementary chamber/root-cone decomposition uses the
published proof of Renard V.5.5 for a finite basis and its inner-product dual.
