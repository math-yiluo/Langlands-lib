# Simple-root coordinates of Levi chamber faces

The projected ambient simple-root basis gives a basis of the noncentral part of a Levi chamber. The positive restricted roots are additively generated by this basis. These facts describe the chamber closure by simple-root inequalities and identify the normal space of a face with the span of its vanishing simple roots.

`vanishingSimpleRoots` selects the normals of a chamber that evaluate to zero at a point of its closure. The roots of the relative Levi chamber are the fine positive roots vanishing at that point. In a decomposition of a vanishing positive root, both summands are positive and have nonnegative evaluation, so both must also vanish. Thus the indecomposable roots of the relative chamber are exactly the vanishing simple roots. The two chamber-sign lemmas split a fine chamber into face signs and relative signs and reassemble it by a positive perturbation of the face point.

This is the Euclidean description of the face and restriction constructions in Arthur, *An introduction to the trace formula*, §5, printed pp. 25–26, and §17, printed pp. 96–97.
