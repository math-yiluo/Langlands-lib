# Transpose invariance on the real spectral normal dual

The normal covectors are identified with the annihilator of the orbit tangent and polynomial semisimple directions using the trace form. For a continuous function on these covectors, invariance under precomposition by all normal stabilizer actions gives equality of the resulting ambient functions on all centralizer conjugacy pairs in the trace conormal. The ambient function is evaluated by restricting a matrix's trace functional to the normal complement. Under stabilizer precomposition the trace representative transforms by inverse conjugation, so the hypothesis gives equality for every pair.

The equality set is closed, since the functions are continuous into a Hausdorff space. The adjusted transpose pair is in the closure of those conjugacy pairs. Adjusted transpose also preserves the orbit tangent and fixes the semisimple directions, and its inverse preserves the trace conormal by trace isometry. Applying the closure equality to the inverse trace representative gives the claimed invariance under precomposition by adjusted transpose.

This internal result transports the centralizer orbit-closure lemma into the local coordinates used to compare leading distribution jets.
