# Local actions on the real spectral stratum chart

An ambient continuous linear equivalence acts on the overlap of a chart with its inverse image. The local action and its inverse are smooth on their open domains when the chart and inverse are smooth. A semisimple slice point fixed by the ambient map gives a fixed point of the local action.

An ambient linear map preserving similarity and fixing the semisimple slice preserves the nearby zero-normal plane in its chart action. A point of this plane is represented as an invertible conjugate of a semisimple slice point by the affine chart formula. The image under the ambient action is similar to the same slice point. The exact recovery property for nearby similar pairs gives its original semisimple coordinate and a zero normal coordinate. Continuity allows this comparison near each slice point where the chart and the nearby-pair criterion both apply.

The file proves the needed similarity preservation for inner conjugation and adjusted transpose by constructing the intertwiners using matrix units. Transposing a conjugation uses the transpose of the inverse unit because transpose reverses multiplication. These internal local action identities are used in the distribution covariance argument.
