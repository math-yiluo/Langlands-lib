# Differentiation along the real spectral stratum chart

At a semisimple slice point with orbit and normal coordinates zero, the derivative of the affine conjugation chart sends an orbit displacement to its commutator with the shifted base matrix. It embeds the semisimple and normal displacements by their linear inclusions. This follows by differentiating the two products and matrix inversion at the identity.

When the shifted orbit tangent agrees with the original one, composing the chart derivative with the normal projection gives coordinate projection onto the normal factor. The chain rule for the chart and its smooth inverse then identifies the normal component of the inverse derivative with that ambient normal projection. For an ambient linear map fixing the slice point, the normal derivative in local chart coordinates is its compression by normal inclusion and projection. If the map preserves the sum of the orbit tangent and semisimple directions, the compression represents the induced normal quotient map.

These internal identities relate the algebraic normal action to the normal derivatives in the distribution comparison.
