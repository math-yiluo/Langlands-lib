# The zero face of a based root datum as a based root datum

Author: Worker 146 (Task 5619).

This module is pure linear algebra on Mathlib root data over `ℤ`
(`RootDatum ι M N`, a `RootPairing ι ℤ M N`). It supplies the combinatorial
part of the proof that the index of the semisimple anisotropic kernel is the
subdiagram on `Δ₀`
([`TitsIndexAnisotropicKernelIndexProof`](../GroupScheme/Reductive/TitsIndexAnisotropicKernelIndexProof.md)).
Everything is in the namespace `Langlands.LinearAlgebra.RootDatumZeroFace`.

## Provenance

All results are internal and derived. They are standard facts about root
systems: positive roots are the nonnegative integral combinations of the simple
roots; the roots killed by a coweight that is nonnegative on a base form a root
subsystem with base the simple roots killed by it; and Weyl groups and based
automorphisms behave accordingly. The Lean proofs are self-contained, using
Mathlib's `RootPairing.Base`, the canonical root form, and the Library lemmas
named below. No external statement is assumed.

## Positivity and the simple-root monoid

Here `P : RootDatum ι M N` and `b : P.Base`.

- `exists_nat_of_mem_closure`: an element of the additive monoid generated by
  the simple roots is `∑ j ∈ b.support, f j • P.root j` for some `f : ι → ℕ`.
- `isPos_iff_mem_closure` (`ι` finite is not needed): `b.IsPos i` holds exactly
  when `P.root i` lies in `AddSubmonoid.closure (P.root '' b.support)`. One
  direction rewrites the root as a natural combination and applies Mathlib's
  `RootPairing.Base.height_eq_sum`. For the other, a positive root that is the
  negative of a natural combination would have height at most zero.
- `neg_mem_closure_iff_not_isPos`: `-P.root i` lies in that monoid exactly when
  `i` is not positive. This uses the index negation `P.indexNeg` and
  `RootPairing.Base.IsPos.neg_iff_not`.
- `isPos_indexEquiv_of_mapsTo` and `isPos_indexEquiv_iff_of_based`: an
  automorphism `θ : P.Aut` with `a ∈ b.support ↔ θ.indexEquiv a ∈ b.support`
  for all `a` preserves positivity. Its weight map carries the simple-root monoid
  into itself, and the inverse automorphism gives the converse.
- `based_of_isPos`: conversely, an automorphism that preserves positivity
  preserves `b.support`. This is the Library lemma
  `RootPairing.Base.mem_support_equiv_iff_of_isPos`
  ([`Reductive/RootDatumGaloisSimpleRoots`](../GroupScheme/Reductive/RootDatumGaloisSimpleRoots.md))
  applied to `θ.indexEquiv` and `θ.weightEquiv`.
- `root'_reflectionPerm_of_root'_eq_zero`: if `P.root' j ν = 0`, the reflection
  in `j` preserves every value `P.root' a ν`.
- `mem_closure_zero_of_mem_closure_nonneg`: if an integral functional `g` is
  nonnegative on the generators `v i` (`i ∈ s`) of an additive monoid, every
  element `y` of that monoid with `g y = 0` lies in the monoid generated by the
  `v i` with `i ∈ s` and `g (v i) = 0`. This is the monoid version of the
  Library lemma `mem_span_zero_of_mem_closure_nonneg`
  ([`LeviCorootSpan`](LeviCorootSpan.md)), with the same induction.

## The structure `FaceDatum P b ν Q`

Fix `P : RootDatum ι M N` with base `b`, a coweight `ν : N`, and a second root
datum `Q : RootDatum κ M' N'` with `ι`, `κ` finite. `FaceDatum P b ν Q` has the
fields

- `sign`: for every root `a` with `P.root' a ν ≠ 0`, `b.IsPos a` holds exactly
  when `0 < P.root' a ν`;
- `weightMap : M →ₗ[ℤ] M'` and `coweightMap : N' →ₗ[ℤ] N`, with
  `coweightMap` injective, and the adjunction
  `Q.toLinearMap (weightMap x) y = P.toLinearMap x (coweightMap y)`;
- `indexEquiv : κ ≃ {a : ι // P.root' a ν = 0}`;
- `root_eq : Q.root k = weightMap (P.root (indexEquiv k))` and
  `coroot_eq : coweightMap (Q.coroot k) = P.coroot (indexEquiv k)`.

So `Q` realizes the roots of `P` that vanish on `ν`. In the application, `Q`
is a root datum of the derived subgroup `D` over `k_s`, `weightMap` is
restriction of characters to `T ∩ D` and `coweightMap` is pushforward of
cocharacters into `T`. For a structure `F : FaceDatum P b ν Q`:

- `root'_simple_nonneg`: simple roots of `P` have `0 ≤ P.root' s ν`, by
  `sign`, since simple roots are positive.
- `pairing_eq`: `Q.pairing k l = P.pairing (indexEquiv k) (indexEquiv l)`, from
  `root_eq`, the adjunction and `coroot_eq`.
- `reflectionPerm_eq`: `P.reflectionPerm (indexEquiv k) (indexEquiv l)` is
  `indexEquiv (Q.reflectionPerm k l)`. Both sides have the same coroot, by
  `coroot_eq` and `pairing_eq`; coroots of `P` are injective.
- `exists_weyl_transport`: for every `w'` in the Weyl group of `Q` there is `w`
  in the Weyl group of `P` with `w.indexEquiv (indexEquiv k) =
  indexEquiv (w'.indexEquiv k)` for all `k`, and `P.root' (w.indexEquiv a) ν =
  P.root' a ν` for all `a`. The proof is induction on the Weyl group
  (`RootPairing.weylGroup.induction`): the reflection in `k` goes to the
  reflection in `indexEquiv k`.

### The base of `Q`

`F.baseSupport` is the finite set of `k` with `indexEquiv k ∈ b.support`, and
`F.base : Q.Base` has this support (`mem_base_support`). Its four axioms are
proved as follows.

- Linear independence of the simple roots (`linearIndepOn_root`). Let
  `∑ c k • Q.root k = 0` over the support and put
  `x = ∑ c k • P.root (indexEquiv k)`. Then `weightMap x = 0`, so `x` pairs to
  zero with the coroots `P.coroot (indexEquiv l)` of the support. The Library
  identity `two_mul_rootForm_root`
  ([`SplitExistence/IntegralBase`](../GroupScheme/SplitExistence/IntegralBase.md))
  then kills the canonical root form of `x` against these roots, so the root
  form of `x` with itself is zero. The Library lemma `eq_zero_of_mem_rootSpan`
  gives `x = 0`, and linear independence of the simple roots of `P` gives
  `c = 0`.
- Linear independence of the simple coroots (`linearIndepOn_coroot`): their
  images under the injective `coweightMap` are simple coroots of `P`.
- Every root of `Q` or its negative is a natural combination of simple roots of
  `Q`. Write `±P.root (indexEquiv k)` in the simple-root monoid of `P`. The
  functional `⟨·, ν⟩` is nonnegative on simple roots and zero on this root, so
  `mem_closure_zero_of_mem_closure_nonneg` keeps only simple roots with zero
  `ν`-value. These are of the form `P.root (indexEquiv l)` with `l` in the
  support, and `weightMap` carries them to `Q.root l` (`weightMap_mem_closure`).
- The same for coroots (`coroot_mem_closure_aux`). The functional
  `y ↦ ⟨P.flip.Polarization y, ν⟩` on `N` satisfies
  `2 · g(P.coroot s) = B(P.coroot s, P.coroot s) · P.root' s ν` with
  `B(P.coroot s, P.coroot s) ≥ 4` (Library `two_mul_dual_polarization_root` and
  `four_le_rootForm_root` for `P.flip`). So it is nonnegative on simple coroots
  and vanishes exactly on the coroots of roots with zero `ν`-value. After
  discarding the other simple coroots, injectivity of `coweightMap` pulls the
  combination back to `Q`.

`isPos_iff`: a root `k` is positive for `F.base` exactly when
`indexEquiv k` is positive for `b`. Both directions use
`isPos_iff_mem_closure`, `neg_mem_closure_iff_not_isPos` and
`weightMap_mem_closure`.

### Transport of based corrections

`exists_based_correction` takes automorphisms `δ : P.Aut` and `δ' : Q.Aut` such
that `δ` preserves all values `P.root' a ν` and
`δ.indexEquiv (indexEquiv k) = indexEquiv (δ'.indexEquiv k)` for all `k`. It
also takes `w'` in the Weyl group of `Q` such that `w' * δ'` preserves
`F.base.support`. It produces `w` in the Weyl group of `P` such that `w * δ`
preserves `b.support` and
`(w * δ).indexEquiv (indexEquiv k) = indexEquiv ((w' * δ').indexEquiv k)` for
all `k`.

The element `w` is the transport of `w'` from `exists_weyl_transport`, so
`w * δ` preserves `ν`-values and is compatible with `indexEquiv`. It preserves
positivity of every root `a`. If `P.root' a ν ≠ 0`, this is the field `sign`.
Otherwise `a = indexEquiv k`, and `isPos_iff` with
`isPos_indexEquiv_iff_of_based` for `w' * δ'` and `F.base` gives it.
`based_of_isPos` then shows that `w * δ` preserves `b.support`.
