# Weights of the paired simple normals

`disjSum_is_basis_iff` identifies a basis of the paired inequalities with two
subsets of simple roots whose flats have nonzero `dCoeff`. The proof compares
the span and independence of the combined normals with the direct sum of the
two individual normal spaces.

`normalizedWeight_disjSum` expresses its normalized affine weight as
`dCoeff` times the product of the two `rootWeight` expressions. The numerator
identity is `gramVolume_sum_eq_measureFactor`. A disjoint-sum reindexing
separates the denominator into the two products of imaginary evaluations.
