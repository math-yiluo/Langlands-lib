# Root count bounds for types D₅ and F₄

`CartanD5F4RootCountsStatement` bounds the number of roots in a finite reduced integral root datum with a D₅ base by forty. It also shows that four independent roots with F₄ simple pairings in a finite integral root datum generate at least forty-eight distinct roots.

For D₅, a signed list of twenty coefficient vectors contains the simple roots and is closed under the simple reflections. Induction by reflections puts every root in this list. For F₄, each of twenty-four positive coefficient vectors is either simple or obtained by reflection from an earlier one. Their positives and negatives are pairwise distinct, and linear independence of the four starting roots preserves distinctness. The reflection and distinctness tests are finite calculations with the numbered Cartan matrices.

The bounds apply to the dimension comparison for parabolic intersections in the E₆ splitting argument in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Theorem 6.26, printed p. 355. [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
