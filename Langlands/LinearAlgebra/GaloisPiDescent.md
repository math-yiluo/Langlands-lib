# Scalar extension and Galois descent in finite coordinate spaces

This internal derived module transports the published finitely supported
Galois submodule descent theorem to finite coordinate vector spaces. It also
provides the original-vector inverse image and the extension-field span of a
subspace, proving that these operations undo one another when the extended
subspace is invariant under every ground-field automorphism of a Galois
extension. There is no finiteness assumption on the field extension.

The coefficient extension map is linear over the original field. Reflection
of original-vector membership holds over any field extension: the quotient
by the original subspace, extended as a tensor product, kills the extended
span. Faithful flatness of a field extension then reflects the vanishing of
an original quotient class. The finite-product tensor comparison identifies
coefficientwise extension with the tensor `1 ⊗ v`.

An extended subspace is invariant under all coefficient automorphisms by
span induction. In the other direction, the finite-function/finitely-supported
linear equivalence carries the subspace to the Library's existing infinite
Galois descent theorem. Transporting its spanning conclusion back gives
recovery from original vectors. These are compatibility and descent helpers
for actual representation spaces; no group action or semisimplicity theorem
is asserted here.
