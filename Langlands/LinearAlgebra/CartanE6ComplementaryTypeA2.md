# A complementary A₂ face in E₆

`CartanE6ComplementaryTypeA2Statement` computes a reflected simple face in a datum with the Cartan matrix of E₆. The numbering is the chain \(0,2,3,4,5\), with vertex \(1\) attached to \(3\). The vertices \(0,2,4,5\) form two long-arm chains of type A₂.

The reflecting root has simple coefficients \((1,1,2,2,2,1)\). Its root and coroot with those coefficients are obtained by successive simple reflections. The reflected vertices \(1,3\) form a simple A₂ face. The coroot combination with coefficients \((-1,0,-3,0,-3,-1)\) pairs to zero on that face and to one on the other reflected simple roots. Both roots of the face are orthogonal to the four original long-arm simple coroots. The sum of the two reflected simple coroots, and the sum with the second coroot doubled, have the original fundamental coweight pairings at \(1\) and \(3\), respectively.

This calculation realizes the complementary type A₂ in the centralizer argument for outer E₆ in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Theorem 6.26, printed p. 356. [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
