# Positive roots and Levi flats

`simpleRoots_independent` proves linear independence for the restricted simple roots of each regular chamber of a Levi flat. The positivity convention and the model of Levi subspaces are those of [GMFamilies](GMFamilies.md).

The root-set axioms give symmetry and imply that the difference of distinct roots with positive inner product is a root. For the latter fact, if both positive Cartan integers were at least two, the squared norm of the difference would be nonpositive. One Cartan integer is therefore one, so reflection and symmetry give the difference. At an ambient regular point, a positive difference decomposes one of the two roots. This gives pairwise nonpositive inner products between distinct positive indecomposables. Mathlib's linear independence lemma for vectors in a common open half-space then applies. The additive closure of the positive roots is generated by these indecomposables.

A small displacement from a regular point of the Levi flat gives an ambient regular point and preserves every strict sign. The roots vanishing on the flat span the same space as the positive simple roots that vanish there. Orthogonal projection has this span as its kernel. The surviving simple roots project to an independent family. The image of a simple root that survives is indecomposable by coefficient comparison in this family. Conversely, for a projected indecomposable, choose a positive preimage of minimum height at the ambient regular point. Any decomposition of this preimage either survives as a decomposition of its image or gives a smaller preimage of the same vector.

The finite sign-perturbation lemma and the zero-sign description of the Levi roots also apply to the face constructions. These are derived facts about the Euclidean root model used in Arthur's §17. The main reference is [Arthur's product splitting formula](GMFamiliesSplittingProof.md).
