# A determinant obstruction to type A₅ inside type D₅

`CartanD5ExcludesA5Statement` rules out five roots with type A₅ pairing matrix in an integral based root pairing of type D₅. For any based integral root pairing, expand a list of roots in the simple roots and a list of coroots in the simple coroots. At full rank the restricted pairing matrix is a product of the two integral coefficient matrices and the ambient Cartan matrix. Its determinant is therefore a multiple of the ambient Cartan determinant. Here the ambient determinant is four and the proposed restricted determinant is six.

This is the integral root-pairing version of the A₅ exclusion used in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Theorem 6.26, printed p. 355. [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
