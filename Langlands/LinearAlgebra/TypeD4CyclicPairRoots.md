# Two cyclic A2 pairs in a D4 coroot lattice

For a cubic action with zero coroot norms, the pairing of each coroot with its first translate is minus one. The other translate is the negative of their sum. The coordinate calculation finds a numbered simple coroot outside the corresponding six roots that pairs nontrivially with the original pair. Its cyclic pair has cross pairings with the first pair between minus one and one. Pairing invariance and the zero norm of the second pair give the zero sum of the three cross entries. The determinant calculation in `TypeD4CyclicPairGram` shows that the four coroots form an integral basis.

This supplies the root calculation used for the two rank-two subgroups in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed pp. 372–373.
