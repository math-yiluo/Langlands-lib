# Exact numberings for classical leaf extensions

This intermediate module supplies entry-preserving numberings for the
infinite extension families. It also proves invariance of the exact Cartan
presentation predicates under an entry-preserving equivalence.

Reversing the old A-chain fixes the newly appended vertex and reflects its
attachment index. Attaching a single edge at the last A-vertex gives the
next A-matrix; either ordered double edge gives B or C. Attaching a single
edge at the penultimate A-vertex gives D. At the first vertex of B, C or D,
the cyclic numbering that moves the new vertex to zero gives the next matrix
of the same family.

All identities compare the full ordered integral matrices, by cases on old
vertices and the appended vertex followed by integer index arithmetic.
They are internal computations from Mathlib's definitions.
