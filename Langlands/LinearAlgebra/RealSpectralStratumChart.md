# Smooth local coordinates for a fixed real spectral rank label

For a real square matrix \(A\), let \(T\) be the infinitesimal conjugation image and let \(Z\) be the real polynomial algebra generated by its semisimple part. The trace form pairs \(T\) to zero against \(Z\), since \(Z\) commutes with \(A\). Its restriction to \(Z\) is nondegenerate, so \(T\) and \(Z\) are disjoint.

For every \(z\in Z\), the centralizer of \(A\) lies in the centralizer of \(A+z\), since \(z\) is polynomial in \(A\). Rank-nullity implies that the tangent rank cannot increase. For sufficiently small \(z\), lower semicontinuity of rank gives equality, so both centralizers coincide. The trace form identifies the centralizer with the orthogonal complement of the tangent image. Taking its orthogonal complement gives equality of the tangent images as well. The centralizer units of \(A\) and the transpose adjusted by a conjugator from \(A\) to \(A^{\mathsf T}\) fix every \(A+z\).

Choose a complement \(W\) of \(T\) containing \(Z\), and a complement \(N\) of \(Z\) inside \(W\). In the coordinates \(((t,z),y)\) of \((T\times Z)\times N\), the chart is

\[
((t,z),y)\longmapsto (1+Jt)(A+z+y)(1+Jt)^{-1},
\]

where \(J\) lifts the infinitesimal conjugation map onto \(T\). The chart and its inverse are smooth on their respective open domains. For a sufficiently nearby pair consisting of \(A+z\) and a similar matrix \(B\), the rank comparison for the intertwining equation and the local slice theorem show that the inverse chart of \(B\) has semisimple coordinate \(z\) and zero normal coordinate.

Every sufficiently nearby matrix with the same spectral rank label as \(A\) is similar to \(A+z\) for a sufficiently small \(z\in Z\). It is therefore contained in the zero normal coordinate plane in this chart. This internal construction provides local coordinates for distributions supported on the fixed-label locus.
