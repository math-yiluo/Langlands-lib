# Arithmetic comparison after the integral cyclotomic twist

This lemma combines the pure Euler-polynomial comparison with the actual
integral inverse cyclotomic twist. At a good place of residue cardinality
\(q\), the arithmetic comparison substitutes the complex pure radius
\(q^{(m-n)/2}\) into the polynomial of weight \(n\). When \(m-n=2k\), this
radius is \(q^k\). The inverse cyclotomic character to the power \(k\)
gives the same substitution on actual Euler polynomials over the
coefficient completion.

The arithmetic equality holds after mapping both coefficient
completions to the common field. To prove it, map further to the complex
field by the supplied embedding, use compatibility on the original
coefficient number fields, and apply injectivity. All identities apply
outside the finite set required by purity, weak association and the two
residue characteristics.
