# Odd-real-degree reduction for Harris's cuspidal existence theorem

`Langlands.GaloisRepresentation.HarrisOddNormalizedExistenceStatement` is the odd-real-degree counterpart of `HarrisEvenNormalizedExistenceStatement`. Both have independent `uE`, `uL`, `uV`, and `uW` universes. The input is the CM compositum `E = E₀ K₀` of Harris (1998), Theorem 1.2, with positive rank index `r`, a regular algebraic conjugate self-dual cuspidal class `c` of rank `r+1`, and a square-integrable component at `v` whose rational prime splits in `K₀`. The odd statement requires that `[E₀ : ℚ]` be odd. For each chosen embedding `e : E →+* ℂ`, it gives one number field `L`, an embedding `ι : L →+* ℂ`, and a lift `j : E →+* L` of `e`. It gives a `HarrisNormalizedSystem` of actual continuous semisimple representations at all coefficient places of `L`, with regular `HarrisLabelledWeights` at the label induced by `j` and with multiplicity one.

`HarrisOddNormalizedExistenceReductionStatement` is the conditional reduction to the even result. Clozel, §5.1, before Corollary 5.4.2, pp. 132–133, uses the totally real quadratic degree change. Harris–Taylor, Theorem VII.1.9, pp. 229–231, gives the automorphic auxiliary step, the Frobenius argument on the composita, and the patching back. Sørensen, §1, Lemma 1, gives the abstract patching theorem. The following route includes coefficient descent and a common exceptional set for the stronger family asserted here. [Sørensen's manuscript](https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~csorense/patch.pdf)

## Automorphic lifts and common polynomials

`HarrisParityAuxiliaryFieldsStatement` supplies quadratic CM extensions `N/E` split at the distinguished place `v`, with a totally real factor `N₀` of doubled degree and with the image of the same imaginary quadratic `K₀`. `ConjugateSelfDualUnitaryCentralCharacterStatement` supplies the unitary central character of a representative of `c`. The stated inputs `CyclicPrimeIsobaricBaseChangeTCStatement`, `QuadraticWeakBaseChangeSplitSquareIntegrableStatement`, `CyclicWeakBaseChangeComplexPlaceTCStatement`, and `CuspidalComplexParameterBridgeTCStatement` give the hypotheses of `HarrisParityBaseChangeStatement`. Apply it to obtain a regular algebraic conjugate self-dual cuspidal weak lift on `N` with a square-integrable component at an actual place `w` over `v`. The residue characteristic of `w` agrees with that of `v`. Splitting in the mapped imaginary quadratic field follows by transporting the two primes of `K₀` along the field isomorphism onto its image. The even theorem applies, with an embedding of `N` into `ℂ` extending `e`, and supplies an upper normalized system with its specified coefficient label.

Fix a spectral system of `c` from one weak-transfer comparison. Spectral systems of the same cuspidal class have the same Euler polynomials on a cofinite set, by spherical spectral uniqueness and transport along the class equivalence. On a cofinite set for each `N`, the weak-transfer relation gives the upper unitary Satake eigenvalues by raising the base eigenvalues to the residue degree `f(w/v)`. Since the upper residue cardinality is the corresponding power of the base cardinality, the arithmetic normalization `q^(r/2)` gives the same powering rule for the monic normalized polynomials.

Use the three-field cover from `HarrisParityAuxiliaryFieldsStatement`. Remove its finite exceptional set and the finite failures of unramifiedness, matching or comparison for those three fields, projected to `E`, together with the base spectral exception set. Every remaining place splits in one of the three. Its normalized characteristic polynomial is an upper polynomial at a place of residue degree one. Take `L` to be the compositum inside `ℂ` of the three upper coefficient images and `e(E)`, transported to universe `uL` as needed. All these base polynomials lie in `L` and are pure of weight `r` because each is the image of a pure upper polynomial. This gives a finite exceptional set independent of the coefficient place. The finitely many discarded base places can be assigned any monic degree-`r+1` placeholder when a total polynomial family is needed.

## Patching and labelled coefficient descent

For each finite place `λ` of `L`, take the spectral algebraic closure of its completion. Extend the completion embedding of `L` to the algebraic numbers inside `ℂ`, with values in this closure. Every upper coefficient field `L_N` maps to the same closure through its complex image. The induced place over the residue characteristic of `λ` gives an actual completion of `L_N` embedded in the closure, with the finite-extension topology. Scalar extension of the semisimple even representations to this field preserves continuity, rank, semisimplicity, Frobenius polynomials and unramifiedness. Comparing through the common algebraic-number embedding gives exactly the root-power hypothesis of `QuadraticFrobeniusPatchingStatement`, with its own cofinite set for each `N`. The arithmetic supply gives quadratic abundance. Apply `QuadraticPatchingStatement` through the Frobenius reduction. At each base place outside the common finite set and away from the residue characteristic of `λ`, one of the three splits and gives the prescribed unramified polynomial on the patch.

`AbsoluteGeometricFrobeniusDensityStatement` and trace continuity give rational trace on the whole absolute Galois group. The image of `L_λ` in its spectral algebraic closure is closed since `L_λ` is complete, and the trace belongs to this image on the good geometric Frobenius conjugacy classes. `FiniteCoefficientModelStatement` then gives a finite matrix model over `L_λ`. Its finite coefficient field can contain the image of the completion of one chosen upper coefficient field. The matrices give an actual continuous representation there through the inherited topology, and scalar extension gives the patch. Semisimplicity descends to the finite coefficient field.

Choose the upper comparison at an embedding of `N` extending `e`, with the even label induced by its embedding into `L_N`. Its composite into the chosen finite coefficient extension extends the specified label of `E`. The two restrictions to the absolute Galois group of that finite coefficient field agree after scalar extension, using the patched restriction isomorphism to `N`, functoriality of the restriction maps, and independence of closure embeddings. The labelled Hodge–Tate decomposition is preserved under this finite extension of the base and coefficients, by the scalar and Galois-equivariant comparison of the completed algebraic closures. It has the same pairwise distinct twists as the chosen even representation. The arithmetic supply also permits choosing `N` split at the original place selected by the label if the comparison is made through local completions.

Apply `RegularHodgeTateCoefficientDescentStatement` with that label and the rational trace to obtain an actual `L_λ` model and regular labelled Hodge–Tate weights. The extension of scalars is faithful, so rank, semisimplicity, triviality on inertia and Frobenius polynomial equality hold for the model. Doing this for all `λ` with the same finite set and the same polynomials gives the collection in the odd statement.

The unchanged Harris cuspidal target uses the resulting normalized collection as a single pure summand in this branch. Its general conclusion retains the finite sum of pure families and the original weak association predicate.
