# Coefficient identifications for p-adic Galois representations

These internal constructions give the coefficient choices used to compare
compatible families over different number fields. The algebraic closure
of \(\mathbf Q_p\) and the complex field each have cardinality continuum.
The lower bound for \(\mathbf Q_p\) follows from completeness and its
nontrivial norm. The upper bound comes from choosing a convergent
rational sequence for each point and using uniqueness of its limit.
Passing to the algebraic closure preserves this infinite cardinality.
The classification of algebraically closed fields gives an algebraic
identification. To make it respect embeddings of a number field, use
transcendence bases over that countable field and extend the resulting
isomorphism of the generated algebras to their algebraic closures.

For a number field embedded in the algebraic closure of \(\mathbf Q_p\),
the images of a rational basis generate a finite extension of
\(\mathbf Q_p\). The original number field is dense in this local
extension because it contains the rational numbers and its image
generates the extension. The comparison theorem for dense embeddings
identifies this extension with the completion at the prime cut out by
the norm. That prime contains \(p\), so its residue characteristic is
\(p\). The resulting map from the completion into the algebraic closure
is continuous and extends the original embedding.

For a fixed coefficient place, its completion is already a finite
extension of \(\mathbf Q_p\) for the prime below it. Choose a
\(\mathbf Q_p\)-algebra embedding into the algebraic closure. This map
is continuous because the completion has the module topology over
\(\mathbf Q_p\).
