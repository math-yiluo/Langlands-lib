# Krull density of geometric Frobenius conjugacy classes

`Langlands.GaloisRepresentation.absoluteGeometricFrobeniusDensity` proves the published `AbsoluteGeometricFrobeniusDensityStatement` in the full field universe `uE`, for every finite excluded set `S`.

The proof uses monotonicity of density. The Library theorem `dense_conjugates_globalGeometricFrobenius_degreeOne S` gives a dense subset using just places outside `S` of prime residue cardinality and just the chosen `geometricFrobeniusLift v` at each such place. For each element of this subset, use the same place and conjugating element and take that chosen lift as the witness. Its geometric Frobenius property is `geometricFrobeniusLift_spec v`. The element being conjugated is definitionally the image under the completion restriction map in the target, by `globalGeometricFrobenius`. Reversing the source equality gives the target's equality; the prime-cardinality condition is not needed for the inclusion.

The finite Chebotarev and local-to-global comparison steps are carried by the imported density theorem. It applies the Library's degree-one refinement of Chebotarev to the inverse of the desired finite-quotient element. At the prime induced by the chosen closure embedding, the inverse of the local geometric lift restricts to arithmetic Frobenius. Transitivity on the primes above the place and uniqueness of arithmetic Frobenius at the unramified prime give the comparison up to conjugacy. The conjugating element lifts to the absolute group, so the geometric lift gives a point in the desired Krull coset. The finite Chebotarev source is Neukirch, *Algebraic Number Theory*, Chapter VII, §13, Theorem 13.4, pp. 545–546.
