# Rank-one inputs for the cuspidal case of Harris's Theorem 1.2

`HarrisRankOneHeckeSpectrumStatement` concerns a regular algebraic,
conjugate self-dual cuspidal class of `GL(1)` over a CM field. It asks for
an actual unitary algebraic Hecke character and a spherical spectral system
attached to the given class. Outside a finite set the character is
unramified and the spectral Euler polynomial is `1 − χ_v(ϖ_v) X` for every
uniformizer.

The determinant identifies `GL(1)` with the multiplicative group. The
infinity type is integral because the half-rank shift in Clozel's
Definition 1.8 vanishes at rank one. Under the determinant identification,
conjugate self-duality gives `χ ∘ c = χ⁻¹`. CM conjugation fixes the positive
real scalar ideles, on which a continuous character satisfying this identity
is trivial. The Library proves `HeckeCharacter.isUnitary_of_positiveRealIdeles`;
the transport of the infinitesimal character and the identification of the
actual spherical presentation remain obligations of this statement.

`AlgebraicUnitaryHeckeCharacterRealizationStatement` starts with an actual
algebraic unitary Hecke character of a CM field. It asks for a single number
field `L` with an embedding into `ℂ`, a finite set `S` of field places, one
value `a_v ∈ Lˣ` for each place, and continuous absolute Galois characters
over the actual completions of `L` at every coefficient place. At a field
place outside `S` the complex image of `a_v` is the unramified Hecke character
value on a uniformizer. At any coefficient place of different residue
characteristic the Galois character is unramified and its value on a
geometric Frobenius lift is the completion image of `a_v`.

The weight-zero condition is on `X − a_v` over `L`, so it tests all complex
embeddings. For this part of the proof, the unitary algebraic infinity type
has exponents of opposite sign in each complex conjugate pair. A positive
power of a prime ideal outside the conductor is generated by an element
in the ray of the conductor. The corresponding power of the character
value is therefore a product of ratios of conjugate embeddings of that
element. CM conjugation commutes with the embeddings of the CM field into
`ℂ`, giving absolute value one at every complex embedding. Finitely many
ray-class representatives and the embeddings of the field suffice to
bound the field of values.

The character realization is the rank-one specialization of Serre's
idelic construction. One removes the complex infinity factor and uses
its algebraic counterpart to correct at the places above the coefficient
characteristic. This leaves the same unramified values outside the
conductor and the coefficient characteristic. Descent of the corrected
character uses reciprocity on finite quotients with the uniformizer
comparison. The formulation takes the inverse of the arithmetic
Frobenius convention when needed and follows the Library's
`IsLambdaAdicRealization` convention throughout.

These are independent statement definitions. In the Harris reduction, the
second supplies rank-one representations on the completion lines and the
first identifies their normalized Euler factors with the given class.
