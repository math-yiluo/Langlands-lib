# Continuous characters from geometric Frobenius Euler polynomials

This module passes from equality of unramified Euler polynomials to comparison of actual local restrictions in Task 6846. Linear trace is continuous from the canonical module topology of the endomorphism space. It is the negative of the linear coefficient of the Library's reciprocal characteristic polynomial, including in rank zero. The trace of a tensor-extended operator is the original trace mapped into the extension.

The density theorem and conjugacy invariance extend equality of two continuous class functions on degree-one geometric Frobenius values outside a finite set to the entire absolute Galois group. For representations with distinct coefficient fields, the proof first maps their Euler polynomials to a common Hausdorff field along continuous embeddings. Equality of the linear coefficients gives equality of the mapped traces on those Frobenius elements. The extended equality restricts along the actual completion embedding and becomes equality of the trace characters of the two tensor-extended local actions. When the common field is algebraically closed, ranks agree and the first extended local action is irreducible, the one-sided character comparison supplies an invertible linear intertwiner of the actual restrictions.
