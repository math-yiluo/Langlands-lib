# The automorphic lift in the Harris parity argument

`Langlands.GaloisRepresentation.HarrisParityBaseChangeStatement` isolates the automorphic step in passing to an auxiliary CM field. Clozel, §5.1, p. 132, chooses the auxiliary field to split at the discrete-series place. In the proof of Harris–Taylor, Theorem VII.1.9, p. 229, the same split-place argument gives a cuspidal lift, and strong multiplicity one and the complex-place comparison give conjugate self-duality and the regular algebraic infinitesimal character.

Fix a quadratic Galois extension `N/E` of CM number fields with the actual adelic quotients. Start with an actual cuspidal rank-`r+1` realization `S` having unitary central character, regular algebraic infinitesimal character, conjugate self-duality, and a square-integrable component at a finite place `v` that splits completely in `N`. The conclusion is a cuspidal realization on `N`, weakly associated to `S` by arithmetic base change, retaining regular algebraicity and conjugate self-duality and having a square-integrable component at every place above `v`.

The proposition assumes four published or independently stated inputs. `CyclicPrimeIsobaricBaseChangeTCStatement` gives the unitary weak isobaric lift and its uniqueness. `QuadraticWeakBaseChangeSplitSquareIntegrableStatement` identifies its singleton cuspidal datum at the given split place, using a spectral presentation from the square-integrability hypothesis. The comparison in `CyclicWeakBaseChangeComplexPlaceTCStatement` transports the complex-place parameters. `CuspidalComplexParameterBridgeTCStatement` identifies those parameters with the actual infinitesimal characters and characterizes regular algebraicity. Complex-place transport either retains or exchanges the two embeddings of a complex completion. Clozel regular algebraicity is preserved in either case.

For conjugate self-duality, the CM involution on `N` restricts to the one on `E`. The cuspidal contragredient exists in the actual Library realization, and CM transport preserves cuspidality and unitarity. The unramified description of duality in `ContragredientSphericalSpectrum` inverts the unordered Satake spectrum. Both inversion and reindexing by the CM involutions commute with taking the residue-degree powers. Thus the conjugate contragredient of the upper singleton is another unitary weak lift of the lower datum. Uniqueness in the first premise identifies the two and gives conjugate self-duality. This argument uses the unramified polynomial comparison and the actual full-operator pairing.

The source unitary central character is supplied separately by the conjugate-self-dual central-character statement `Langlands.Global.Harris.ConjugateSelfDualUnitaryCentralCharacterStatement` (`Langlands.Global.Arithmetic.HarrisAuxiliaryConjugatePairStatement`). `CuspidalOperatorRealization.isUnitarizable_of_hasUnitaryCentralCharacter` gives the unitarity required on the singleton. The arithmetic properties of the auxiliary field, including its real degree and the original imaginary quadratic factor, enter when applying the even branch.
