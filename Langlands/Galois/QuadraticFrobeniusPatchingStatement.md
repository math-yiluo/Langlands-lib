# Quadratic patching from geometric Frobenius polynomials

`Langlands.GaloisRepresentation.QuadraticFrobeniusPatchingStatement` isolates the Frobenius argument in Harris–Taylor, Theorem VII.1.9, printed pp. 230–231. The proof there compares the representations on the auxiliary quadratic fields and their composita by Chebotarev before patching, then recovers the matching at places that split in an auxiliary field. Here that argument takes the abstract `QuadraticPatchingStatement` of Sørensen, §1, Lemma 1, as a premise. [Sørensen's manuscript](https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~csorense/patch.pdf)

The common algebraically closed coefficient field `C`, the quadratic fields in one algebraic closure of the number field `F`, their abundance away from `S`, and the actual continuous semisimple rank-`n` representations are as in `QuadraticPatchingStatement`. Supply monic degree-`n` polynomials `P v` over `C`. For each auxiliary field `N`, at every place `w` above each of a cofinite set of base places `v`, the representation is unramified and has geometric Frobenius characteristic polynomial obtained by raising each root of `P v` to the residue degree of `w/v`. Each auxiliary has its own cofinite set. `frobeniusRootPower` forms this polynomial over a splitting field and retains root multiplicity. The statement takes reverses to use `HasEulerPolynomialAt`, which includes both unramifiedness and an actual geometric Frobenius lift.

The conclusion supplies a rank-`n` continuous semisimple representation of the absolute Galois group of `F` whose restriction to every auxiliary is isomorphic to its prescribed representation. It also recovers `HasEulerPolynomialAt` with the reverse of `P v` at any completely split place where an auxiliary has this polynomial at an actual prime above `v`.

A proof first uses the functoriality of geometric Frobenius under restriction, together with multiplicativity of residue degrees in towers. Two restrictions to a compositum then have identical characteristic polynomials at all places above a cofinite set of base places. Galois conjugation over `F` preserves the residue degrees and the same formula. The proofs of the two isomorphisms use semisimplicity under finite-index restriction, density of the conjugacy classes of actual geometric Frobenius lifts, and Brauer–Nesbitt. The finite prime-realization input is already available as `Langlands.Global.chebotarevPrimeRealizationStatement`. Its use on the absolute Galois group still requires comparing the images of the Library's local Frobenius lifts with the finite global Frobenius elements. At a completely split place, the completion map is an isomorphism and carries geometric Frobenius and inertia through the local restriction, which gives the asserted recovery.
