# Density of actual geometric Frobenius conjugates

The global geometric Frobenius at a finite place is the image of the Library's chosen geometric lift under the same map of absolute Galois groups used for local restriction. The theorem shows that conjugates of these elements at places of prime residue cardinality outside a finite set are dense in the Krull topology.

For a finite Galois subextension of the absolute algebraic closure, the chosen extension of the completion embedding sends its global integers into the spectral valuation ring of the algebraic closure of the local completion. The kernel of their reduction is a prime above the given place. The inverse local lift acts on this reduction by the residue cardinality power, so its restricted global action is an arithmetic Frobenius at the induced prime. For an unramified prime supplied by finite Chebotarev, transitivity on primes and uniqueness of arithmetic Frobenius there give the desired conjugacy. The conjugating automorphism extends to the full algebraic closure. Every nonempty open set contains a coset of the fixing subgroup of a finite normal subextension, so the finite degree-one realization meets every such open set.
