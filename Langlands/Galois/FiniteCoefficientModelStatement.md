# Finite coefficient fields for semisimple representations with rational trace

`Langlands.GaloisRepresentation.FiniteCoefficientModelStatement` supplies an algebraic step between the patched algebraic-closure-valued representation of Harris–Taylor, Theorem VII.1.9, pp. 230–231, and a descent criterion over a finite extension of a specified coefficient completion. It is a derived statement about the algebra of representation matrices.

Fix an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field `C` algebraic over `K`. Let `A` be an actual continuous finite-dimensional representation of the absolute Galois group of `E` with coefficients in `C`. Assume `A` is semisimple and the trace of every group element is in the image of `K`. For any prescribed finite intermediate extension `Λ₀` of `K` in `C`, there is a finite intermediate extension `Λ` containing `Λ₀` and a basis of `A` in which every matrix entry of every element lies in `Λ`.

For the proof, the `C`-span of the image matrices is a faithful semisimple algebra of endomorphisms. It is a product of full matrix algebras and its trace pairing in the given representation is nondegenerate. On each simple block that trace pairing is weighted by the positive multiplicity with which the block acts, which is nonzero in characteristic zero. Choose a basis of this span from matrices `A(g_i)`. The Gram matrix of the trace pairing has entries `tr(A(g_i g_j))` in `K`. It is invertible, so solving the trace equations for any `A(g)` expresses it as a `K`-linear combination of the finitely many `A(g_i)`. After choosing a carrier basis, their entries generate a finite extension of `K` in `C`. Its compositum with `Λ₀` contains all matrices.

In the parity reduction `C` is the algebraic closure of the actual coefficient completion with the spectral topology. The finite matrix model there gives a continuous representation over `Λ` by the inherited topology, which is the finite-dimensional coefficient topology. This statement supplies the finite extension and matrix entries. Descent to the original completion and the labelled Hodge–Tate comparison are supplied by the labelled criterion and the split restriction.
