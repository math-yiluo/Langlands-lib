# Rational primes and residue characteristics at finite places

For a finite place `v` of a number field `K`, `residueCharacteristic_eq_of_natCast_mem` identifies its residue characteristic with any rational prime `p` whose image belongs to the prime ideal of `v`. The quotient ring has prime characteristic dividing `p`.

For each nonzero natural number `p`, `finite_setOf_residueCharacteristic_eq` shows that only finitely many places have residue characteristic `p`. Their prime ideals all contain the image of `p` in the ring of integers.

For a place `w` above `v` in an extension of number fields, `residueCharacteristic_eq_of_liesOver` identifies the two residue characteristics through the map of residue fields.
