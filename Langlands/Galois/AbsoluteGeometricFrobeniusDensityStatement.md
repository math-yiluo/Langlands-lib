# Density of geometric Frobenius conjugacy classes

`Langlands.GaloisRepresentation.AbsoluteGeometricFrobeniusDensityStatement` expresses the absolute Galois group consequence of Chebotarev used by Harris–Taylor, Theorem VII.1.9, p. 230, and Sørensen, §1, proof of Lemma 1, pp. 2 and 5. The finite-extension theorem is Neukirch, *Algebraic Number Theory*, Chapter VII, §13, Theorem 13.4, pp. 545–546.

Fix a number field `E` and a finite set `S` of its finite places. Take every actual geometric Frobenius lift in the absolute Galois group of the completion at every `v ∉ S`, map it to the absolute Galois group of `E` by the Library's completion restriction map, and take all global conjugates. The resulting set is dense in the Krull topology. The definition of a geometric lift says that its inverse acts on residues by the cardinality power.

To derive this from the published `chebotarevPrimeRealizationStatement`, test each open coset in a finite Galois quotient. Realize the inverse of the desired quotient element as an arithmetic Frobenius at an unramified prime over a place outside `S`. Choose a compatible embedding of the algebraic closure of `E` into the algebraic closure of the completion inducing that prime. The local geometric Frobenius lift then projects to the required inverse of the arithmetic element in the finite quotient. Changing from the selected embedding to `Field.absoluteGaloisGroup.map` changes the image by conjugacy. This local-to-global comparison, together with the finite Galois basis of the Krull topology, is the proof obligation. It allows one to extend character identities and closed trace-value conditions from the good Frobenius elements to the whole group.
