# The normed completion of the actual maximal unramified extension

Let $F$ be a nonarchimedean local field with its given valuation and topology. `inertiaFixedField F` is the field fixed by inertia inside the chosen separable closure. Its real absolute value is the real-valued `fixedFieldValuation`, which is the spectral extension of `localNormedField F`. `CompletedField F` is its metric completion. It is complete, ultrametric and nontrivially normed, and its algebra embedding of $F$ preserves this norm.

An $F$-automorphism of the inertia fixed field preserves the spectral norm. `completedFieldAut` extends it and its inverse using Mathlib's completion of continuous ring maps. The extension fixes $F$ and agrees with the original action on the dense subfield. `completedFieldGaloisAction` proves identity and multiplication by checking the continuous extensions there. `completedFieldArithmeticFrobenius` extends the same arithmetic Frobenius lift whose residue action is the cardinality power map in `exists_fixedField_arithmeticFrobenius`.

Harris, *Galois properties of cohomological automorphic forms on GL(n)* (1999), §4, p. 309, (4.3), uses the maximal unramified base on the Shimura side. Harris, *The local Langlands conjecture for GL(n) over a p-adic field, n < p* (1998), Appendix, p. 208, immediately before (A.9), describes the maximal unramified field and a Frobenius action on it. The normed completion here provides a complete ground field for analytic spaces while retaining its actual algebraic dense subfield and the extended arithmetic Frobenius. The Weil descent of an analytic space over that field is a separate construction.
