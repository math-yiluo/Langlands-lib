# The completed valuation ring of the maximal unramified extension

`UnramifiedCompletion.Integers F` is the completion of the valuation ring of the separable inertia fixed field at its maximal ideal. [Unramified completion](SeparableUnramifiedCompletion.md) and [Completed unramified coefficients](../../Geometry/FormalGroup/CompletedUnramifiedCoefficients.md) use this ring for their respective coefficient maps.
