# Arithmetic Frobenius of the completed maximal unramified integer ring

Drinfel'd, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 A, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 3), uses Frobenius on the unramified base in the statement of the action. The arithmetic convention on the residue field is the power by the cardinality of the original finite residue field.

A local ring automorphism preserves the maximal ideal and every power. Its automorphisms of the quotient rings commute with the transition maps, as one verifies on residue classes of original elements. The resulting compatible automorphisms extend to an automorphism of the adic completion, whose action on the image of the original ring is the given automorphism.

The Library's arithmetic Frobenius on the actual inertia fixed field restricts to the original unramified integers. Its completed extension still fixes the base integer ring. Every class in the completed residue field is the image of a class from before completion, so the original residue power formula gives the same formula for the completed automorphism.
