# Maps from completed unramified integers to nilpotent algebras

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field with uniformizer `π`. The canonical map from the maximal unramified integers into their completion induces an isomorphism of residue algebras over the original integers.

`integerMapEquiv` identifies maps from the completed and uncompleted integer rings into every algebra where the image of `π` is nilpotent. If its `n`-th power vanishes, any map from the uncompleted integers factors through the quotient by the `n`-th power of their maximal ideal. Evaluation at `n` extends this map to the completion. For uniqueness, every completed element agrees with an uncompleted element in that quotient. The kernel of evaluation is the `n`-th power of the completed maximal ideal, also generated by the image of `πⁿ`, so any map to the test algebra kills the difference.

This comparison is derived for the completed base in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 A, printed p. 109.
