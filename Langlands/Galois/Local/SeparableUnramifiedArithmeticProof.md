# Proof of the arithmetic of the actual separable inertia fixed field

Worker 972. The final theorem also uses two published helper modules:
`Langlands.Galois.Local.SeparableValueArithmetic` by Worker 706, and
`Langlands.Galois.Local.SeparableHenselian` by Worker 706 and Worker 972.

## Target

`separableUnramifiedArithmetic : SeparableUnramifiedArithmeticStatement.{u}`
proves the closed statement of
`Langlands.Galois.Local.SeparableUnramifiedArithmeticStatement`. That
statement is reused unchanged. For every `F : Type u` with
`[Field F] [ValuativeRel F] [TopologicalSpace F] [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F]`,
it proves all five conjuncts:

1. `FixedFieldResidueIsAlgClosure F` is `fixedFieldResidueIsAlgClosure F`
   (this module).
2. `FixedFieldValuesFromBase F` is `fixedFieldValuesFromBase F` (this module).
3. `FixedFieldIntrinsicDiscrete F` is
   `fixedFieldIntrinsicDiscrete_of_valuesFromBase F` applied to conjunct 2
   (`SeparableValueArithmetic`).
4. `FixedFieldIntegersIsDVR F` is
   `fixedFieldIntegersIsDVR_of_valuesFromBase F` applied to conjunct 2
   (`SeparableValueArithmetic`).
5. `FixedFieldIntegersIsHenselian F` is `fixedFieldIntegersIsHenselian F`
   (`SeparableHenselian`).

There are no additional hypotheses. There is no characteristic or
perfectness assumption on `F`, and the theorem has the single universe
parameter `u`.

## Notation and instances

All declarations are in `Langlands.LocalGalois.Separable`. Write
`Ω = SeparableClosure F`, `U = inertiaFixedField F`,
`𝔸 = extensionIntegers F Ω`, `𝔹 = fixedFieldIntegers F`,
`κ = ResidueField (baseIntegers F)` and `q = Nat.card κ`. The section
`Arithmetic` installs the published choices `localNormedField F`,
`localNormedField_ultrametric F`, `localNormedField_complete F` and
`localResidue_finite F` as the named local instances `arithNormedField`,
`arithUltrametric`, `arithComplete` and `arithResidueFinite`. These are the
same four choices that the statement unit uses. The names keep the
generated instance names distinct from those of the imported helper modules.

## Implemented proof

### Generic lemmas

- `eq_of_pow_eq_one_of_sub_mem`: in a local commutative ring where `k` is a
  unit, two `k`-th roots of unity `a`, `b` with `a - b` in the maximal ideal
  are equal. The proof applies Mathlib's uniqueness of simple roots,
  `IsLocalRing.eq_of_eval_eq_zero_of_not_isUnit_sub`, to `X ^ k - 1`. The
  derivative `k X ^ (k - 1)` is a unit at `a`.
- `exists_pow_card_pow_sub_one_eq_one`: let `z ≠ 0` be integral over a finite
  field `k`. Then `z ^ (#k ^ d - 1) = 1` for some `d > 0`. Here `k⟮z⟯` is a
  finite field with `#k ^ d` elements, `d = [k⟮z⟯ : k]`.
- `mem_maximalIdeal_iff_lt` and `isUnit_iff_eq_one` are the valuation tests
  for the maximal ideal and the units of `v.valuationSubring`, where `v` is
  an `ℝ≥0`-valued valuation.

### Teichmüller roots of unity

- `teichOrder_isUnit`, `teichOrder_ne_zero`: for `M > 0`, the integer
  `q ^ M - 1` is a unit in `𝔸`. Its residue is `-1`, because `q = 0` in the
  finite field `κ`, and hence in `ResidueField 𝔸`.
- `mem_integers_of_pow_eq_one`: a root of unity in `Ω` has valuation at most
  one.
- `exists_teich`: `Ω` is separably closed and `q ^ M - 1` is nonzero in `Ω`.
  Mathlib's `HasEnoughRootsOfUnity` therefore gives a primitive root of unity
  `ζ ∈ 𝔸` of order `q ^ M - 1`.
- `isPrimitiveRoot_residue`: its residue is again primitive of that order,
  by `eq_of_pow_eq_one_of_sub_mem`.
- `mem_inertiaFixedField_of_pow_eq_one`: let `x ∈ 𝔸` with
  `x ^ (q ^ M - 1) = 1`, and let `σ ∈ separableInertia F`. Then
  `integerAut F Ω σ x` is a root of unity of the same order with the same
  residue (`mem_separableInertia_iff`). So it equals `x`, and `x ∈ U`.

### The inertia fixed field is generated by Teichmüller roots

`teichSet F` is the set of `x ∈ Ω` with `x ^ (q ^ M - 1) = 1` for some `M > 0`.

- `mem_separableInertia_of_forall_teichSet`: an automorphism `σ` of `Ω/F` that
  fixes `teichSet F` lies in `separableInertia F`. Every nonzero residue `z`
  of `𝔸` is integral over `κ`. So `z ^ (q ^ d - 1) = 1` for some `d > 0`, and
  `z` is a power of the residue of a primitive Teichmüller root `ζ`. Since
  `σ` fixes `ζ`, the residue automorphism `residueHom` fixes `z`.
- `inertiaFixedField_le_adjoin`: `U ≤ F⟮teichSet F⟯`. By the infinite Galois
  correspondence (`InfiniteGalois.fixedField_fixingSubgroup`), it suffices
  that the fixing subgroup of `F⟮teichSet F⟯` lies in the inertia group.
  That is the previous item. Only this inclusion is proved, and only it is
  used.
- `exists_common_teich`, `mem_adjoin_of_pow_eq_one`,
  `exists_teich_of_mem_inertiaFixedField`: each `x ∈ U` lies in `F⟮ζ⟯` for one
  primitive root `ζ ∈ 𝔸` of order `q ^ M - 1`, `M > 0`. The element `x` lies
  in the field generated by finitely many elements of `teichSet F`. They have
  a common order `q ^ M - 1`, because `q ^ a - 1` divides `q ^ (a b) - 1`.
  Each of them is then a power of `ζ`.

### Degree comparison and values

- `natDegree_minpoly_le`: for such `ζ`, the degree of `minpoly F ζ` is at most
  the degree of `minpoly κ ζ̄`, where `ζ̄` is the residue of `ζ`. The extension
  `Ω/F` is normal and separable, so `minpoly F ζ` has exactly its degree many
  roots in `Ω`. Each root is `σ ζ` for some `σ` in the Galois group
  (`minpoly.exists_algEquiv_of_root'`). Its residue is the image of `ζ̄` under
  the `κ`-algebra map `residueHom F Ω σ`, hence a root of `minpoly κ ζ̄` in
  `ResidueField 𝔸`. These residues are pairwise distinct, because the roots
  `σ ζ` are roots of unity of order `q ^ M - 1`
  (`eq_of_pow_eq_one_of_sub_mem`). Counting roots gives the inequality.
- `exists_sepClosureValuation_aeval_eq`: let `f ∈ F[X]` have degree less than
  that of `minpoly F ζ`. Then `sepClosureValuation F (f(ζ)) = ‖a‖₊`, where `a`
  is a coefficient of `f` of largest norm. Dividing by `a` gives a
  polynomial `g₀` over `baseIntegers F` with a coefficient equal to one. Its
  reduction `ḡ₀` is nonzero of degree less than that of `minpoly κ ζ̄`, by the
  previous item, so `ḡ₀(ζ̄) ≠ 0`. Therefore `g₀(ζ)` is a unit of `𝔸` and has
  valuation one. The spectral valuation restricts to the base norm
  (`spectralNorm_extends`).
- `fixedFieldValuesFromBase`: each `x ∈ U` lies in some `F⟮ζ⟯`. Its power basis
  writes `x = f(ζ)` with `deg f < deg minpoly F ζ`. Since
  `fixedFieldValuation F x = sepClosureValuation F x`
  (`fixedFieldValuation_apply`), the previous item gives the base value. The
  case `x = 0` is included, with `a = 0`.

### Residue field

- `exists_residue_pow_eq_one`: every nonzero residue of `𝔹` satisfies
  `r ^ (q ^ d - 1) = 1` for some `d > 0`. Its image under the injective
  `fixedFieldResidueInclusion F` in `ResidueField 𝔸` is integral over `κ`.
- `exists_isPrimitiveRoot_residue`: `ResidueField 𝔹` contains a primitive root
  of unity of order `q ^ M - 1` for every `M > 0`. It is the residue of a
  Teichmüller root, which lies in `U` and has valuation at most one.
  Primitivity is checked after `fixedFieldResidueInclusion F`, using
  `fixedFieldResidueInclusion_residue`.
- `fixedFieldResidueIsAlgClosure`: install the statement's algebra
  `(residueToFixedField F).toAlgebra`. Algebraicity holds because every
  nonzero residue is a root of unity. For algebraic closedness, use
  `IsAlgClosed.of_exists_root`. Let `p` be monic irreducible over
  `ResidueField 𝔹`. The root of `p` in `AdjoinRoot p` is integral over
  `𝓀[F]` by transitivity. If this root is nonzero, it is a root of unity of
  order `#𝓀[F] ^ d - 1 = q ^ d - 1`; the cardinalities agree through
  `localResidueEquiv F`. So it is a power of the image of the primitive root
  from the previous item. That power comes from `ResidueField 𝔹`, and
  injectivity of `AdjoinRoot.of p` makes it a root of `p` there.

## Sources and derived correspondence

The route is derived rather than transcribed. The literature obtains the
maximal unramified extension as a direct limit of finite unramified
extensions. It identifies it with the inertia fixed field through finite
Galois theory. It then reads off values, residue field and Henselianity from
complete finite stages. The present proof works directly with the actual
infinite field `U = inertiaFixedField F` inside `Ω`, and needs no
completeness of any extension field.

Jean-Pierre Serre, *Local Fields*, GTM 67, Springer, 1979, registered source
`serre-1979-local-fields`, `/home/taskhub/central-library/Serre--Local Fields--1979.pdf`,
SHA-256 `109b461d42915da70771c70c4b551a363fc1f33412331ae2671c5c85be433112`:

- Chapter III §5, Theorems 2–3 and Corollary 1, printed pp. 54–55/PDF
  pp. 61–62. Finite unramified extensions correspond to finite separable
  residue extensions, and the maximal unramified extension has residue field
  the separable closure of the residue field. Over the finite residue field
  `κ`, this closure is an algebraic closure, which is conjunct 1. Theorem 3
  lifts residue roots uniquely. `natDegree_minpoly_le` uses the same
  reduction of conjugates, as an inequality for Teichmüller roots, which
  have distinct residues.
- Chapter II §2, Proposition 3 and Corollaries, printed pp. 28–29/PDF
  pp. 36–37. In an unramified finite extension every value is a base value.
  `exists_sepClosureValuation_aeval_eq` proves this directly on `F⟮ζ⟯`, by
  reducing a normalized polynomial in `ζ` modulo the maximal ideal.
- Chapter II §4, Proposition 7, printed p. 34/PDF p. 42. Its uniqueness part
  uses `f(X) = (X - x) g(X)` with `g(x')` invertible, the same argument as
  `IsLocalRing.eq_of_eval_eq_zero_of_not_isUnit_sub`. Here it is used only
  for the polynomials `X ^ k - 1` with `k` a unit. The existence part, which
  needs completeness, is not used.

Read with Serre, *Corrections to J-P. Serre's books* (2019), registered
source `serre-book-corrections-2019`, PDF pp. 2–3; no correction affects
these results. The setting of the maximal unramified extension inside a
fixed separable closure follows Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New
Approach* (2023), §2.5(c), printed p. 88/PDF p. 119, registered source
`kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`, as recorded in the statement
unit.
