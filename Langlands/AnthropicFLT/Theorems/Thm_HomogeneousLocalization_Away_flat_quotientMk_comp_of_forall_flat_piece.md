## Content

Statement. `HomogeneousLocalization.Away.flat_quotientMk_comp_of_forall_flat_piece`: let `I ⊆ A` be a homogeneous ideal such that
the graded pieces of `A / I` are flat over `S`, and `f ∈ 𝒜 d`. Then the quotient of `A_(f)` by the ideal generated by
the fractions with numerator in `I` is flat over `S`.

The proof term applies the companion solution theorem `P2MW.S_HomogeneousLocalization_Away_flat_quotientMk_comp_of_forall_flat_piece.solution`.
