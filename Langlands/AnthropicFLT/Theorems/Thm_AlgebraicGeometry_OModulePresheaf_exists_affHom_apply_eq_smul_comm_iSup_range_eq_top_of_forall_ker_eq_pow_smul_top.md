## Content

Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.OModulePresheaf.exists_affHom_apply_eq_smul_comm_iSup_range_eq_top_of_forall_ker_eq_pow_smul_top`:
let `A` be a commutative ring, `I = (g_1, …, g_s)` an ideal (`Ideal.span (Set.range g) = I`), `π : V ⟶ Spec A`, and
`F : ℕ → OModulePresheaf π` with maps `φ k : AffHom (F (k+1)) (F k)` surjective on affine opens with kernel
`I^(k+1) • ⊤`. Let `j k : AffHom (K k) (F (k+1))` be injective on affine opens with image the kernel of `φ k` (so
`K k ≅ I^(k+1) F_{k+1}`). Then there are `θ m k : AffHom (K k) (K (k+1))` for `m : Fin s` such that
`j (k+1) (θ m k x) = g m • y` whenever `φ (k+1) y = j k x` (multiplication by `g m`), the `θ m` commute pairwise,
and for every `k` and affine `U` the ranges of the `θ m k` on `U` together span `K (k+1)(U)`.
(The kernels `I^(k+1) F_{k+1}` form a module over `A[Y_1, …, Y_s]` generated in level `0`, the graded-module
structure of the Rees construction.)

The proof term applies the companion solution theorem
`P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_OModulePresheaf_exists_affHom_apply_eq_smul_comm_iSup_range_eq_top_of_forall_ker_eq_pow_smul_top.solution`.
