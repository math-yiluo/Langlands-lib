## Content

Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.OModulePresheaf.exists_isCoherent_forall_ker_eq_pow_smul_top_of_surjective_of_range_eq_ker_of_isProper_of_isAdicComplete`:
let `A` be a noetherian, `I`-adically complete commutative ring, `q : P ⟶ Spec A` proper, and let `F`, `E` be coherent
quasi-coherent adic systems (transition maps surjective on affines with kernels `I^(k+1) • ⊤`) with compatible
surjections `ε k : F k → E k`. Suppose that `E` is algebraized by a coherent quasi-coherent `GE`
(`ψE k : GE → E k` surjective with kernels `I^(k+1) • ⊤`, compatible) and that a coherent quasi-coherent `GK` maps
compatibly to `F` by `lam k` with image `ker ε k` on affines and with `ker (lam (k+c)) ⊆ I^(k+1) GK` for some `c`
depending on the affine. Then `F` is algebraizable: there are a coherent quasi-coherent `G` and compatible
`ψ k : G → F k`, surjective on affine opens with kernels `I^(k+1) • ⊤`. (The dévissage step of Grothendieck's
existence theorem: algebraizability passes to extensions.)
