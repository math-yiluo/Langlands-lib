## Content

Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.OModulePresheaf.exists_forall_sub_mem_pow_smul_of_forall_res_sub_res_mem_pow_smul_preimage_of_isProper`:
let `A` be a noetherian commutative ring with an ideal `I`, `q : P ⟶ Spec A` proper, `p : V' ⟶ P` proper, `G` a
coherent quasi-coherent FLT presheaf over `p ≫ q`, `K'` an ordered affine cover of `V'`, `W` an affine open of
`P` and `n : ℕ`. Then there is `c` such that for every family `t i ∈ G(K'.U i ∩ p⁻¹(W))` whose differences on the
pairwise overlaps lie in `I^(n+c) • ⊤`, there is a family `a` on the same charts that agrees on the overlaps and
satisfies `t i - a i ∈ I^n • ⊤` for all `i`. (Approximately compatible families are close to compatible ones.)

The proof term applies the companion solution theorem
`P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_OModulePresheaf_exists_forall_sub_mem_pow_smul_of_forall_res_sub_res_mem_pow_smul_preimage_of_isProper.solution`.
