## Content

Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.HilbertFunctor.mem_of_forall_exists_X_pow_mul_mem_of_forall_mem_iff_app_awayToSection_eq_zero`:
let `I` be an ideal of `k[X₀, …, Xₙ]` (a field `k`) whose forms of each degree are exactly those
vanishing on a closed subscheme `Z_k` in all charts. Then `I` is saturated: a form `F` with
`Xᵢ^N F ∈ I` for every `i` (some `N`) lies in `I`.

The proof term applies the companion solution theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_HilbertFunctor_mem_of_forall_exists_X_pow_mul_mem_of_forall_mem_iff_app_awayToSection_eq_zero.solution`.
