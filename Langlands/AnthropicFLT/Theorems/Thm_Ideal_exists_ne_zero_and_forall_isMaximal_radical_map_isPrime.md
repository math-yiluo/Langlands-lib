## Content

Statement. `Ideal.exists_ne_zero_and_forall_isMaximal_radical_map_isPrime` (generic irreducibility of fibres): let `R ⊆ C` be
finitely generated domains over a field `k`, with `C` of finite type over `R`, such that the subfield generated by `R`
is separably closed in `Frac C`. There is `r ≠ 0` in `R` such that for every maximal `n ⊆ R` with `r ∉ n`, either
`nC = C` or `√(nC)` is prime.

The proof term applies the companion solution theorem `P2MW.S_Ideal_exists_ne_zero_and_forall_isMaximal_radical_map_isPrime.solution`.
