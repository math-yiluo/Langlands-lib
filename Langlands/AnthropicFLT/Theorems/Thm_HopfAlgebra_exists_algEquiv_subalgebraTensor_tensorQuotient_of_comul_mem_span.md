## Content

Statement. `HopfAlgebra.exists_algEquiv_subalgebraTensor_tensorQuotient_of_comul_mem_span K hΔ`: let `H` be a
commutative Hopf algebra over a commutative ring `k` (in universes `v` and `u`) and `K` a `k`-subalgebra of `H` such
that `Δ x` lies in the `k`-span of `{a ⊗ b | a ∈ K, b ∈ K}` for every `x ∈ K` (hypothesis `hΔ`). Write
`J = Ideal.span {x | x ∈ K ∧ ε x = 0}` for the ideal of `H` generated by the augmentation ideal of `K`. Then there is
an `H`-algebra isomorphism `β : H ⊗[K] H ≃ₐ[H] H ⊗[k] (H ⧸ J)` such that
`β (h₁ ⊗ₜ h₂) = (h₁ ⊗ₜ 1) * (id ⊗ mkₐ J) (Δ h₂)` for all `h₁, h₂ : H`.

Geometrically: if `G = Spec H` and `N = Spec (H ⧸ J)`, the map `G × N → G ×_{Spec K} G`, `(g, n) ↦ (g, g n)`, is an
isomorphism.

The proof term applies the companion solution theorem `P2MW.S_HopfAlgebra_exists_algEquiv_subalgebraTensor_tensorQuotient_of_comul_mem_span.solution`.
