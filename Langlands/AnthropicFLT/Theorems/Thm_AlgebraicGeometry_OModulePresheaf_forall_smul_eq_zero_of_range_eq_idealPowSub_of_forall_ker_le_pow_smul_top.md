## Content

Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.OModulePresheaf.forall_smul_eq_zero_of_range_eq_idealPowSub_of_forall_ker_le_pow_smul_top`:
let `A` be a commutative ring, `I` an ideal, `q : P ⟶ Spec A`, and `S`, `K` adic systems of FLT presheaves over `q`
(transition maps `φ`, `κ` surjective on affines with kernels `I^(k+1) • ⊤`) with compatible maps `j k : K k → S k`.
Let `𝓙, 𝓙₁, 𝓙₂` be ideal sheaves on `P` and `s, t ∈ ℕ` with `𝓙₂^t 𝓙₁ ⊆ 𝓙^s` on affines, such that `𝓙^s` kills every
`S k`, the image of `j k` is `𝓙₁ • S k` (Library `idealPowSub`), and `ker (j (k+c)) ⊆ I^(k+1) K (k+c)` for some `c`
depending on the affine. Then `𝓙₂^t` kills every `K k` on affine opens.

The proof term applies the companion solution theorem
`P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_OModulePresheaf_forall_smul_eq_zero_of_range_eq_idealPowSub_of_forall_ker_le_pow_smul_top.solution`.
