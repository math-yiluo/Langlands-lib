# Quotient by the augmentation ideal of a stable subalgebra — theorem interface

For a commutative Hopf algebra H over a field k and a subalgebra K' stable under comultiplication and antipode, quotient H by the ideal generated by elements of K' with zero counit. The theorem supplies a surjective bialgebra map to a Hopf algebra with exactly that kernel. It records counit and antipode compatibility, and preservation of both stability properties for the image of any other stable subalgebra K.

The theorem `HopfAlgebra.exists_hopfAlgebra_surjective_ker_eq_span_of_comul_mem_span_of_antipode_mem` applies the companion `P2MW.S_HopfAlgebra_exists_hopfAlgebra_surjective_ker_eq_span_of_comul_mem_span_of_antipode_mem.solution` with the displayed arguments. Its proof is implemented in [the solution module](../P2M/Sol/S_HopfAlgebra_exists_hopfAlgebra_surjective_ker_eq_span_of_comul_mem_span_of_antipode_mem.md):

In the quotient every element of K' equals its counit scalar, since their difference is an augmentation generator. Span induction and the counit identities show that comultiplication of the generated ideal vanishes after quotienting both tensor factors. The counit kills the ideal and the antipode preserves it. Apply the general Hopf-ideal quotient theorem. Convolution uniqueness proves antipode compatibility for the quotient bialgebra map. Transporting comultiplication through that map and applying span induction proves stability of mapped subalgebras.
