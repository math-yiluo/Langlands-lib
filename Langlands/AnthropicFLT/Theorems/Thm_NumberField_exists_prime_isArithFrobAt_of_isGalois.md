# Every automorphism of a Galois extension of number fields is a Frobenius

`NumberField.exists_prime_isArithFrobAt_of_isGalois` (the root of this port): let L/K be a Galois extension of number fields, with K : Type u and L : Type v, let σ ∈ Gal(L/K), and let S be a finite set of naturals. Then there are a prime p ∉ S and a maximal ideal P of the ring of integers of L with p ∈ P and `IsArithFrobAt ℤ σ P`. The conclusion is the absolute arithmetic Frobenius over ℤ and contains no unramifiedness clause. The adapter `Langlands/Global/Arithmetic/ChebotarevPrimeRealizationProof.lean` derives the relative, unramified form used in the Library. The proof applies the solution module.
