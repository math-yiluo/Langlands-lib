## Content

Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.OModulePresheaf.exists_forall_exists_H0_tensor_twist_span_eq_top`: let `A` be a
commutative ring, `ι : P ⟶ ℙ^r_A` a closed immersion into `Proj A[X_0, …, X_r]`, `q = ι ≫ ProjSpace.π A r` the
structure morphism, and `F` a coherent quasi-coherent FLT presheaf over `q`. Then there is `d₁` such that for every
`d ≥ d₁` there are finitely many Čech `0`-cocycles `σ l` of `F ⊗ O(d)` (`F.tensor (ProjSpace.twist q ι d)`) on the
standard cover `ProjSpace.stdCoverPullback ι` whose components span the sections over every chart of the cover.
(Serre's theorem A: `F(d)` is generated by global sections for `d ≫ 0`.)

The proof term applies the companion solution theorem
`P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_OModulePresheaf_exists_forall_exists_H0_tensor_twist_span_eq_top.solution`.
