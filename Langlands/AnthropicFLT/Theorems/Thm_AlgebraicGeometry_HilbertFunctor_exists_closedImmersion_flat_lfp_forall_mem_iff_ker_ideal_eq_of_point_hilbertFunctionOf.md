## Content

Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.HilbertFunctor.exists_closedImmersion_flat_lfp_forall_mem_iff_ker_ideal_eq_of_point_hilbertFunctionOf`:
let `P ∈ ℚ[X]` be the Hilbert polynomial of some homogeneous ideal over some field (universe `uz`).
There is `D₀` such that for every `m ≥ D₀` there is an assignment `Φ` sending each point `p` of the
Hilbert functor of `ℙⁿ` over a ring `A` with Hilbert function `hilbertFunctionOf n P m` to a closed
subscheme `Φ A p` of `ℙⁿ_A`, with: (1) `Φ A p` is a closed immersion, flat and locally of finite
presentation over `Spec A`; (2) `Φ A` is injective on points up to isomorphism over `ℙⁿ_A`;
(3) compatibility with base change along `A → B` when `q.I` is the extension of `p.I` (a pullback
square over `ℙⁿ_B → ℙⁿ_A`); (4) every closed subscheme of `ℙⁿ_A`, flat and locally of finite
presentation, all of whose geometric fibres over algebraically closed fields arise from points, arises
from a point of `A`; (5) for `d ≥ m`, a form `F` of degree `d` lies in `p.I` iff all its
dehomogenizations vanish on `Φ A p`; (6) the ideal of `Φ A p` on each chart `D₊(Xᵢ)` is generated by
the dehomogenizations of the forms of `p.I`.

The proof term applies the companion solution theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_HilbertFunctor_exists_closedImmersion_flat_lfp_forall_mem_iff_ker_ideal_eq_of_point_hilbertFunctionOf.solution`.
