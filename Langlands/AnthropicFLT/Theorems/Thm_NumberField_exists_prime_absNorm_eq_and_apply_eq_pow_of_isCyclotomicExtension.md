# Primes of prime norm in a prescribed cyclotomic class

`NumberField.exists_prime_absNorm_eq_and_apply_eq_pow_of_isCyclotomicExtension`: let L/K be cyclotomic for {m}, ζ a primitive m-th root of unity in L, τ ∈ Gal(L/K), and S a finite set of naturals. Then there are a prime p ∉ S and a maximal ideal v of the ring of integers of K with N(v) = p and τ(ζ) = ζ^p. The proof applies the solution module.
