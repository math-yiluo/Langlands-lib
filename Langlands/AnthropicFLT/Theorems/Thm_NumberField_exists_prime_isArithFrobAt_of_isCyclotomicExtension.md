# Realization of automorphisms of cyclotomic extensions as Frobenius elements

`NumberField.exists_prime_isArithFrobAt_of_isCyclotomicExtension`: let L/K be a Galois extension of number fields that is cyclotomic for {m}, τ ∈ Gal(L/K), and S a finite set of naturals. Then there are a prime p ∉ S and a maximal ideal P of the ring of integers of L with p ∈ P and `IsArithFrobAt ℤ τ P`, that is, τ(x) ≡ x^#(ℤ/(P ∩ ℤ)) modulo P for every algebraic integer x of L. The proof applies the solution module.
