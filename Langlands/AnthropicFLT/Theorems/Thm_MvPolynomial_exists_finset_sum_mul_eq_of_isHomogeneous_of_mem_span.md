## Content

Statement. `MvPolynomial.exists_finset_sum_mul_eq_of_isHomogeneous_of_mem_span`: if a homogeneous polynomial `F` of degree `d`
lies in the ideal generated by a set `J₀` of homogeneous polynomials, then `F = Σ c_G G` over finitely many `G ∈ J₀`
of degrees `e_G ≤ d`, with `c_G` homogeneous of degree `d - e_G`.

The proof term applies the companion solution theorem `P2MW.S_MvPolynomial_exists_finset_sum_mul_eq_of_isHomogeneous_of_mem_span.solution`.
