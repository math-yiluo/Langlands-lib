## Content

Regularity ascends along flat local homomorphisms with regular closed fibre.

- `quotSpanSingletonEquivQuotSMulTop`: `R ⧸ (x) ≃ QuotSMulTop x R`.
- `exists_isWeaklyRegular_ofList_eq_maximalIdeal` and `exists_isRegular_ofList_eq_maximalIdeal`:
  the maximal ideal of a regular local ring is generated by a regular sequence of length
  `spanFinrank (maximalIdeal R)`.
- `isRegularLocalRing_of_flat_of_isRegularLocalRing_quotient`: if `R → S` is a flat local
  homomorphism from a regular local ring to a noetherian local ring and the closed fibre
  `S ⧸ 𝔪_R S` is regular, then `S` is regular (lift the regular sequence generating `𝔪_R`, which
  stays regular on `S` by flatness, and apply the regular-sequence ascent).
- `flatLocalAscent_forall` restates this; `gate_flat_ascent_runs_at_identity` (identity on
  `ℤ_[p]`) and `not_forall_ascent_dropping_flat` (the flatness hypothesis is needed) are checks.
