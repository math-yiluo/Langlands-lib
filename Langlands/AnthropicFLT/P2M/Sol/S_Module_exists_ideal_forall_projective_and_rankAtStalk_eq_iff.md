## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_Module_exists_ideal_forall_projective_and_rankAtStalk_eq_iff.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_Module_exists_ideal_forall_projective_and_rankAtStalk_eq_iff`. Statement. `Module.exists_ideal_forall_projective_and_rankAtStalk_eq_iff`: let `M` be a finite `A`-module all of whose fibres
have dimension `≤ r`. There is an ideal `𝔞 ⊆ A` such that, for every `A`-algebra `B`, `B ⊗ M` is projective of constant
rank `r` iff `𝔞` maps to zero in `B`.

Proof. Sections `Modules`, `Core`: if `M` is a quotient of `Aʳ`, then `B ⊗ M` is projective of rank `r` iff the
relation module (syzygies `syz`) maps to zero in `Bʳ`, i.e. iff the ideal generated by the coordinates of the
relations maps to zero (`core_equiv`, `core_vanish`). Sections `Chart`, `LocalGen`: as the fibres have dimension `≤ r`,
by Nakayama Zariski-locally on `Spec A` the module `M` is generated by `r` elements (`pres`, `exists_chart`), giving
local ideals (`chartIdeal`). Sections `Compat`, `Glue`: these local ideals agree on overlaps (`compat`) and glue to `𝔞`
(`main`).
