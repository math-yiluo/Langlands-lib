## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_HopfAlgebra_free_subalgebra_of_isNilpotent_ker_counit.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_HopfAlgebra_free_subalgebra_of_isNilpotent_ker_counit`.

Statement. Let `H` be a commutative Hopf algebra over a field `k` and `K` a `k`-subalgebra of `H` that is closed
under the antipode and whose comultiplication lands in the `k`-span of `{a ⊗ b | a, b ∈ K}`. If the augmentation
ideal `ker (ε|_K)` of `K` is nilpotent, then `H` is a free `K`-module. This is the infinitesimal case of the
faithful flatness of `H` over such a Hopf subalgebra.

Proof (namespace `S17Nil`). Let `J` (`JH`) be the ideal of `H` generated by `augK = ker (ε|_K)`; it is nilpotent
(`JH_pow_eq_bot`). Choose a `k`-basis `b` of `H ⧸ J` and lifts `e i ∈ H`, and let `Λ : (ι →₀ K) → H` be the
`K`-linear combination of the `e i`.

- Surjectivity (`range_Λ_eq_top`): the span of the `e i` together with `augK • H` is all of `H`, and a nilpotent
  Nakayama argument (`eq_top_of_le_sup_smul`) removes `augK • H`.
- Injectivity (`Λ_injective`): base change along `K → H` gives the `H`-linear map
  `Λ' : (ι →₀ H) → H ⊗[K] H`, `m ↦ Σ m i • (1 ⊗ e i)`, which is surjective (`Λ'_surjective`). By the torsor
  isomorphism `HopfAlgebra.exists_algEquiv_subalgebraTensor_tensorQuotient_of_comul_mem_span`,
  `H ⊗[K] H ≅ H ⊗[k] (H ⧸ J)` is a free, hence projective, `H`-module, so `Λ'` splits and its kernel `M` is a
  direct summand. The map `θ : H ⊗[K] H → (H ⧸ J) ⊗[k] (H ⧸ J)` shows that every coordinate of an element of `M` lies
  in `J` (`apply_mem_JH_of_Λ'_eq_zero`), so `M ≤ J • M`, hence `M ≤ J ^ n • M = 0` (`ker_Λ'_eq_bot`). Injectivity of
  `Λ` follows by applying this to the image of a kernel element of `Λ`.

`solution` concludes that `Λ` is a linear equivalence, so `H` is free over `K`.
