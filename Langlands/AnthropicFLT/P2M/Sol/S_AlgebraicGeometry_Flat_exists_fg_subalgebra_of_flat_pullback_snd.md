## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_Flat_exists_fg_subalgebra_of_flat_pullback_snd.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_Flat_exists_fg_subalgebra_of_flat_pullback_snd`. Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.Flat.exists_fg_subalgebra_of_flat_pullback_snd`: let `f : X ⟶ Spec A₀` be
quasi-compact and locally of finite presentation, and `A` an `A₀`-algebra such that the base change
of `f` to `Spec A` is flat. Then every finite subset `s` of `A` is contained in a finitely generated
`A₀`-subalgebra `T ⊆ A` such that the base change of `f` to `Spec T` is already flat.

Proof (namespace `FlatDesc21`). `flat_pullback_snd_Spec_iff`: for affine schemes, flatness of
`Spec (R ⊗ B) ⟶ Spec R` is flatness of `R ⊗[A₀] B` over `R`; `flat_tensor_of_le`: flatness passes from
`T' ⊗ B` over `T'` to `T ⊗ B` over `T` for `T' ≤ T` (base change). Cover the quasi-compact `X` by
finitely many affine opens `Spec Bᵢ`, each of finite presentation over `A₀`. Flatness over `A` gives
`A ⊗[A₀] Bᵢ` flat over `A`; the ported theorem `Module.Flat.exists_fg_subalgebra_flat_tensorProduct`
gives finitely generated `Tᵢ ⊇ s` with `Tᵢ ⊗[A₀] Bᵢ` flat over `Tᵢ`. Let `T` be generated by `s` and
the generators of all `Tᵢ`; then each chart of the base change to `Spec T` is flat, and flatness is
local on the source (`IsZariskiLocalAtSource.of_openCover`).
