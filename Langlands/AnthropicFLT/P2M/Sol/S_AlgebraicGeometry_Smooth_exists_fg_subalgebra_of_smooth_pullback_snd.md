## Content

Proof (namespace `SmoothDescGC7`). Affine case (`exists_fg_subalgebra_smooth_tensorProduct`): for a
finitely presented `A₀`-algebra `B₀` with `A ⊗[A₀] B₀` smooth over `A`, Mathlib's
`Algebra.Smooth.exists_subalgebra_fg` gives a finitely generated `A₀`-subalgebra `A₁` of `A`, a smooth
finitely presented `A₁`-algebra `B₁` and an isomorphism `A ⊗[A₁] B₁ ≅ A ⊗[A₀] B₀`. The finitely generated
subalgebras `T ⊇ A₁ ∪ s` (`Stage A₁ s`, directed, with `A` as direct limit, `Stage.isDirectLimit`) are a
direct system with limit `A`, so the isomorphism theorem
`Algebra.exists_algEquiv_tensorProduct_map_eq_of_finitePresentation_of_isDirectLimit` descends the
isomorphism to some `T`, and `T ⊗[A₀] B₀ ≅ T ⊗[A₁] B₁` is smooth over `T`. Global case (`solution`): `X` is
quasi-compact; take a finite affine open cover `Spec Bᵢ` of `X`. Each `Bᵢ` is finitely presented over
`A₀`, and `A ⊗[A₀] Bᵢ` is smooth over `A` (`smooth_pullback_snd_Spec_iff`, base change of the cover). Apply
the affine case to each `Bᵢ`, take `T` generated by `s` and generators of all the resulting subalgebras
(smoothness passes to larger subalgebras, `smooth_tensor_of_le`), and conclude because smoothness is local
on the source (`IsZariskiLocalAtSource.of_openCover` for the pulled-back cover).
