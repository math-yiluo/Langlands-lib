## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_HomogeneousLocalization_Away_flat_quotientMk_comp_of_forall_flat_piece.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_HomogeneousLocalization_Away_flat_quotientMk_comp_of_forall_flat_piece`. Statement. `HomogeneousLocalization.Away.flat_quotientMk_comp_of_forall_flat_piece`: let `I ⊆ A` be a homogeneous ideal such that
the graded pieces of `A / I` are flat over `S`, and `f ∈ 𝒜 d`. Then the quotient of `A_(f)` by the ideal generated by
the fractions with numerator in `I` is flat over `S`.

Proof. Section `Grading`: `A / I` is graded by the images `𝓑 k` of the pieces, which are the flat quotients
`𝒜 k / (𝒜 k ∩ I)` (`flat_piece`). Section `Localise`: the natural map from `A_(f)` to `(A/I)_(f̄)` is surjective with
kernel the given ideal (`Φ_surjective`, `ker_Φ`), so the quotient is isomorphic to `(A/I)_(f̄)`, which is flat by the
ported `HomogeneousLocalization.Away.flat_fromZeroRingHom_comp_algebraMap_of_forall_flat` (`flat_of_eq`).
