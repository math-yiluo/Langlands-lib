## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_MvPolynomial_mem_span_of_linear_mul_mem_of_forall_relation_modulo_mem_span.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_MvPolynomial_mem_span_of_linear_mul_mem_of_forall_relation_modulo_mem_span`. Statement. `MvPolynomial.mem_span_of_linear_mul_mem_of_forall_relation_modulo_mem_span`: let `F₁, …, F_g` be forms of degree `m`
and `ℓ` a linear form that is a nonzerodivisor modulo `(F)` on degree-`m` forms, such that every relation
`Σ cᵢ Fᵢ ∈ (ℓ)` is generated by such relations of degree `≤ 1`. Then `ℓ` is a nonzerodivisor modulo `(F)` on forms of all
degrees `k ≥ m`.

Proof. Induction on `k` (`main`). If `ℓ f = Σ cᵢ Fᵢ` with `f` of degree `k + 1`, then `(cᵢ)` is a relation modulo `(ℓ)`;
by hypothesis it is a combination of relations of degree `≤ 1`, which by the degree-`m` case lift to expressions
divisible by `ℓ` (`exists_hom_coeffs`, `exists_hom_coeffs_span`, `sum_mul_mem_span`); cancelling `ℓ` (`eq_zero_of_linear_mul`,
`isHomogeneous_of_linear_mul`) writes `f` in `(F)`.
