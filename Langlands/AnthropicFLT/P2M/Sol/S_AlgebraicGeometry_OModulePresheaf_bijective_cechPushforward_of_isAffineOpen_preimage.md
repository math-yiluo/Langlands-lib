## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_OModulePresheaf_bijective_cechPushforward_of_isAffineOpen_preimage.solution`
proves the statement of the companion theorem in
`Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_OModulePresheaf_bijective_cechPushforward_of_isAffineOpen_preimage`.

Proof (namespace `AlgebraicGeometry.OModulePresheaf.Rebase` inside the solution namespace). The open
subscheme `pW := p⁻¹ W` is a scheme over `Spec Γ(P, W)` through `qW := (p ∣_ W) ≫ W.isoSpec` (proper if `p`
is, `isProper_qW`; separated if `p` is, `CechAffine.isSeparated_qW`). Section `Datum`: `GW` is `G` restricted
to `pW`, as an FLT presheaf of modules over `qW` (sections `G (ι '' O)`, restriction `resW`, scalar actions
`modSec`, `modB`, `modA`), and it is coherent and quasi-coherent when `G` is (`isCoherent_GW`,
`isQuasicoherent_GW`). `KW` is the ordered affine cover of `pW` by the traces of the members of `K'`
(`image_KW_U`). Section `Powers` relates `I • ⊤` in products and along algebra maps
(`cochain_mem_pow_smul_top_iff`). Section `Bridge0`: `e0` identifies `0`-cochains of `GW` on `KW` with
families of sections of `G` over the charts `K'.U j ⊓ p⁻¹ W` (`e0inv`, `e0_e0inv`, `e0_surjective`,
linearity lemmas), and `0`-cocycles with compatible families (`d0_apply`, `d0_eq_zero_iff`,
`mem_H0_iff`).

`solution`: `pW` is affine (`hW`). Injectivity: restrict `x` and `y` to the whole of `pW`; the constant
cochains they define are cocycles with the same components (hypothesis), so the uniqueness part of the
ported `d_zero_eq_zero_iff_existsUnique_of_isQuasicoherent` (for `GW` on the affine scheme `pW`) gives
`x = y`. Surjectivity: a section `c` of the Čech pushforward over `W` is a compatible family, i.e. a
`0`-cocycle `e0inv c`, and the existence part of the same theorem produces a global section of `GW`
restricting to it.
