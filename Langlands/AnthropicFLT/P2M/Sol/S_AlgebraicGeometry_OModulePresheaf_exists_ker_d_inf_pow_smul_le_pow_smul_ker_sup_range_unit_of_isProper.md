The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_OModulePresheaf_exists_ker_d_inf_pow_smul_le_pow_smul_ker_sup_range_unit_of_isProper.solution`

`Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_OModulePresheaf_exists_ker_d_inf_pow_smul_le_pow_smul_ker_sup_range_unit_of_isProper`.

Proof (namespace `SolFFSuccSepUnit`): an Artin–Rees statement for Čech cocycles of the structure sheaf, via the
Rees algebra `R = ⊕_k I^k t^k ⊆ A[t]` and finiteness of the Čech cohomology of a coherent sheaf on the proper
`R`-scheme `P' = P ×_A Spec R`.

- Setup: `Rr I` is the Rees algebra (Mathlib `reesAlgebra`, noetherian; `Rr.val`, `Rr.coeff_mem`, `Rr.mk`,
  `Rr.incl`), with `P' = P ×_{Spec A} Spec R` (`sA`, `fst'`, `π'`, `isPullback_P'`) and
  `P₁ = P' ×_{Spec R} Spec A[t] = P ×_A Spec A[t]` (`sX`, `g`, `snd₁`, `p₁`, `sX_sA`, `isPullback_P₁`); `g` and
  `fst'` are affine. On `P₁`, sections over the preimage of an affine `U` are `Γ(P, U)[t]` (`alg`, `alg_res`, `T`,
  `θ`, `θ_C`, `θ_X`, `appLE_top_top`, `ΓSpecIso_inv_C`, `θ_map`, `θ_res`, `θ_bijective`; generation
  `closure_eq_top`; scalars from `R`: `algR`, `algR₁`, `algR₁_eq_θ`, `g_appLE_algR`, `algR_res`, `g_appLE_res`;
  helpers `preimage_iInf`, `appLE_congr_hom`).
- `F'` is the image of `O_{P'} → g_* O_{P₁}` (`φg`, `φg_app`; `F'val`, `F'_exists`, `mem_F'_iff`, `F'_res_val`,
  `F'_smul_val`, `F'_rsmul_val`, `F'_add_val`, `F'_zero_val`, `F'_ext`): over `U` it is the subring
  `⊕_k I^k Γ(P, U) t^k`, the Rees module of `O_P`. It is coherent and quasi-coherent (`isCoherent_F'`,
  `res_eq_zero_iff_of_eq`, `isQuasicoherent_F'`). The cover `K` pulls back to an ordered affine cover `K'` of `P'`
  (`K'`, `K'_inter`, `hW'`, `hW`, `e_s`, `e'_s`).
- Cochains: `ΦC` identifies sections of `P₁` over the cover with polynomials (`ΦC_θ`, `θ_ΦC`, `ΦC_res`,
  `ΦC_algR₁`); `coeffC j` takes the `t^j`-coefficient of an `F'`-cochain, giving an `O_P`-cochain with values in
  `I^j` that commutes with `d` (`coeffC_apply`, `coeffC_add`, `coeffC_zero`, `coeffC_sub`, `F'valHom`,
  `F'valHom_apply`, `coeffC_d`, `coeffC_smul`, `coeffC_mem`). Conversely, a cochain `b` with values in `I^k`
  defines the `F'`-cochain `zC = b t^k` (`Sθ`, `mem_range_of_mem_Sθ`, `C_mem_Sθ`, `map_mem_Sθ`, `monomial_mem_Sθ`,
  `zC`, `F'val_zC`, `coeffC_zC`, `F'val_d_zC`), a cocycle if `b` is (`d_zC_eq_zero`).
- `Main`: `Zs`/`Bs` are the `F'`-cocycles and coboundaries in degree `i+1` and `Hs = Zs / Bs` their cohomology,
  finite over `R` by the ported theorem `cechFinite_of_isProper` (`P'` is proper over `Spec R`), hence noetherian.
  `N m` is the submodule of `Hs` generated by the classes `zZ k z = z t^k` of cocycles `z` with values in `I^k`,
  `k ≤ m` (`gensZ`, `N_mono`); the increasing chain stabilizes at some `c₀`. `Tgt m = I^m ker d + range d`
  (`Tgt_anti`); `Good c₀ x` says that every coefficient `coeffC j x` lies in `Tgt (j − c₀)`; it is closed under
  sums, negatives, coboundaries and `R`-combinations of generators (`good_add`, `good_neg`, `good_of_mem_Bs`,
  `good_of_mem_span`, `good_of_mkQ_mem`, using `mem_map_of_mem_smul_top`). `main`: a cocycle
  `z ∈ ker d ∩ I^(n + c₀) ⊤` gives `zZ (n + c₀) z ∈ N (n + c₀) = N c₀`, so it is `Good`, and its `t^(n+c₀)`
  coefficient `z` lies in `Tgt n`. `solution` is `SolFFSuccSepUnit.main`.
