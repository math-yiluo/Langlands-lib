## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_MvPolynomial_exists_finset_sum_mul_eq_of_isHomogeneous_of_mem_span.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_MvPolynomial_exists_finset_sum_mul_eq_of_isHomogeneous_of_mem_span`. Statement. `MvPolynomial.exists_finset_sum_mul_eq_of_isHomogeneous_of_mem_span`: if a homogeneous polynomial `F` of degree `d`
lies in the ideal generated by a set `J₀` of homogeneous polynomials, then `F = Σ c_G G` over finitely many `G ∈ J₀`
of degrees `e_G ≤ d`, with `c_G` homogeneous of degree `d - e_G`.

Proof. Write `F = Σ c_G G` and replace each `c_G` by its homogeneous component of degree `d - e_G` (zero if
`e_G > d`), taking the degree-`d` component of both sides (local `coe_decompose`,
`homogeneousComponent_mul_of_mem_right`).
