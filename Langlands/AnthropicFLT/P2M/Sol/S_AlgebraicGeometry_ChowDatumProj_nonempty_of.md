# From a Chow datum in a product of projective spaces to one in a single projective space (proof)

Module `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.P2M.Sol.S_AlgebraicGeometry_ChowDatumProj_nonempty_of`. Its declarations live
in the namespace `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_ChowDatumProj_nonempty_of`.

## Statement

`solution`: for a commutative ring `R` and a morphism `π : V ⟶ Spec R`, if `ChowDatum π` is nonempty then
`ChowDatumProj π` is nonempty. A Chow datum embeds `V'` into a product of projective spaces; a projective
Chow datum embeds it into a single projective space (`Def_AlgebraicGeometry_ProjSpace`). The theorem module
`Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_ChowDatumProj_nonempty_of` states this as
`AlgebraicGeometry.ChowDatumProj.nonempty_of` and proves it from `solution`.

## Proof outline

The proof composes the embedding with iterated Segre embeddings (namespace
`…AlgebraicGeometry.ProjSpace.Segre`).

1. **Segre charts** (section `ChartHom`). Put `N = (n+1)(p+1) − 1`, and let `idx` identify
   `Fin (n+1) × Fin (p+1)` with `Fin (N+1)` (`bigN`, `bigN_succ`). On the chart
   `D₊(Xᵢ) ×_R D₊(Y_j)` of `ℙⁿ ×_R ℙᵖ`, with coordinate ring `Tij = 𝒜ˣ_(Xᵢ) ⊗_R 𝒜ʸ_(Y_j)`, the ring map
   `segreChart : 𝒜ᶻ_(Z_{ij}) → Tij` sends `Z_{kl}/Z_{ij}` to `(X_k/X_i) ⊗ (Y_l/Y_j)` (`preSegre`,
   `preSegre_apply`, `preSegre_unit`, `preSegre_mulone`, `segreLift`, `segreChart_ratio`,
   `segreChart_algebraMap`). It is surjective (`segreChart_surjective`), because both factors are
   generated by the ratios (`ProjSpace.adjoin_range_ratio`).
2. **Gluing the Segre morphism** (section `ProductCover`). `prodCover` is the open cover of
   `ℙⁿ ×_R ℙᵖ` by the products of standard charts, `chartIso` identifies each piece with
   `Spec (𝒜ˣ_(Xᵢ) ⊗ 𝒜ʸ_(Y_j))`, and `chartMap` maps it to `ℙᴺ` through `Spec segreChart` and the chart
   `D₊(Z_{ij})`. Each `chartMap` lies over `Spec R` (`chartMap_over`). The chart maps agree on overlaps
   (`chartMap_compat`, using the transition formulas for ratios `awayMap_ratio_transition` and the
   auxiliary lemmas `prodCover_fst`, `prodCover_snd`, `chartIso_incL`, `chartIso_incR`, `isLocElem_ratio`,
   `range_SpecMap_awayMap`, `opensRange_prodCover_f`, `overlap_fst_condition`, `overlap_snd_condition`).
   They glue to `segre2 : ℙⁿ ×_R ℙᵖ ⟶ ℙᴺ` over `Spec R` (`segre2_over`). The preimage of each chart
   `D₊(Z_{ij})` is the product chart (`segre2_preimage`), and on it `segre2` is `Spec` of a surjection.
   So `segre2` is a closed immersion (`segre2_closedImm`), since closed immersions are local on the
   target.
3. **Iteration** (section `Recursive`). A `SegreDatum R e` is a closed immersion
   `prodOver R e ⟶ ℙ^{Nd}_R` over `Spec R`. For the empty product (`Spec R`) it is the section
   `Spec R ⟶ ℙ⁰_R` (`eval0`, `eval0_irrelevant`, `eval0_grad0`, `segreAux0_section`, `segreAux0`). For
   `m + 1` factors, apply the datum of the last `m` factors on the second factor and then `segre2`
   (`segreAuxSucc`). Closed immersions are stable under base change and composition. `segreAux` builds the
   datum by recursion.
4. **Conversion** (section `Converter`). `ChowDatum.toChowDatumProj` (private) composes the closed
   immersion of a Chow datum with `segreAux`. `ChowDatum.nonempty_chowDatumProj_of` and `solution`
   conclude.
