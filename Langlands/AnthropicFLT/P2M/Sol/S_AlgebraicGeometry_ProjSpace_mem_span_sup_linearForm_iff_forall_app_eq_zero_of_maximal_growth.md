## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_ProjSpace_mem_span_sup_linearForm_iff_forall_app_eq_zero_of_maximal_growth.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_ProjSpace_mem_span_sup_linearForm_iff_forall_app_eq_zero_of_maximal_growth`. Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.ProjSpace.mem_span_sup_linearForm_iff_forall_app_eq_zero_of_maximal_growth`: let `J`
over a field be generated in degree `m ≥ 1`, `Z_k ⊆ ℙⁿ_k` with forms of degree `≥ m` those of `J`, and
`ℓ = ∑ aⱼ Xⱼ` a linear form such that the ideal `J'` generated by the degree-`m` forms of `J + (ℓ)` has
maximal growth from `m` to `m + 1`. Then for the hyperplane section `Z'` (ideal `ker ιk ⊔ (ℓ)`), the forms of
degree `≥ m` of `J'` are exactly those vanishing on `Z'`.

Proof. The ideal sheaf of `Z'` on each chart is generated by dehomogenizations of the forms of degree
`≥ m` of `J + (ℓ)` (ported `ProjSpace.exists_idealSheafData_ideal_basicOpen_eq_span`,
`ProjSpace.eq_ker_of_ideal_basicOpen_eq_span_of_isClosedImmersion`), so a form vanishes on `Z'` iff some
`Xᵢ^N F` lies in `J + (ℓ)` for each chart (ported `ProjSpace.awayToSection_mk_mem_span_iff_exists_X_pow_mul_mem`,
with the finite-sum description `MvPolynomial.exists_finset_sum_mul_eq_of_isHomogeneous_of_mem_span`).
Maximal growth makes `J'` saturated in degrees `≥ m` (ported
`MvPolynomial.mem_of_forall_exists_X_pow_mul_mem_of_finrank_piece_succ_eq_macaulayPow`), so `F ∈ J'`.
