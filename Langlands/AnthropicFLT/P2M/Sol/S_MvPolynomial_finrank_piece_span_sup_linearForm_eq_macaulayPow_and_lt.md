## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_MvPolynomial_finrank_piece_span_sup_linearForm_eq_macaulayPow_and_lt.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_MvPolynomial_finrank_piece_span_sup_linearForm_eq_macaulayPow_and_lt`. Statement. `MvPolynomial.finrank_piece_span_sup_linearForm_eq_macaulayPow_and_lt`: let `J` be generated in degree `m ≥ 1` and
`ℓ = Σ aᵢ xᵢ` such that `J + (ℓ)` has maximal growth in degree `m` and `h_{J+(ℓ)}(m)^⟨m⟩ + h_J(m) = h_J(m)^⟨m⟩`. Let `J'`
be generated by the degree-`m` forms of `J + (ℓ)`. Then `J'` has maximal growth in degree `m`, and
`h_{J'}(m) < h_J(m)` when `h_J(m) > 0`.

Proof. `J'` agrees with `J + (ℓ)` in degree `m` and is contained in it in degree `m + 1`, so its growth is at least that of
`J + (ℓ)`, which is maximal; Macaulay's bound gives equality. For the strict inequality: if `h_{J'}(m) = h_J(m) > 0`, the
hypothesis `hsum` would give `h_J(m)^⟨m⟩ + h_J(m) = h_J(m)^⟨m⟩`, impossible; and `h_{J'}(m) ≤ h_J(m)` since `J ⊆ J'`
(using the ported strict monotonicity `Nat.macaulayPow_lt_macaulayPow_of_lt` to compare the Macaulay powers).
