## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_OModulePresheaf_forall_smul_eq_zero_and_exists_eq_smul_of_le_of_flat_of_bijective.solution`
proves the statement of the companion theorem in
`Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_OModulePresheaf_forall_smul_eq_zero_and_exists_eq_smul_of_le_of_flat_of_bijective`.

Proof (namespace `W7Aux`: `smul_eq_zero_of_mem_map` and `exists_eq_smul_of_mem_map` pass the two properties
from an ideal `I` to its extension `I B`; `resEquiv` is restriction along an equality of opens). In
`solution`: the charts of `K'` over `W₀` and their pairwise intersections are affine, and so are their
preimages in `Y₀`. The two pullback squares give a morphism `π : Y ⟶ Y₀` with `π ≫ t₀ = t` that is a pullback
of `Spec R → Spec R₀` (`hPB`, via `IsPullback.of_right`). The ported theorem
`exists_algEquiv_sections_preimage_tensor_of_isPullback_of_isAffineOpen` identifies the sections of `Y` over
`π⁻¹ V` with `Γ(Y₀, V) ⊗[R₀] R` for affine `V`; with `hεβ₀`, `hεβ` and the ported
`IsBaseChange.exists_linearEquiv_tensor_of_algEquiv_tensor_of_isBaseChange` this gives `R`-linear isomorphisms
`Φ : R ⊗[R₀] G₀ (V) ≃ G (π⁻¹ V)` compatible with `ε₀`, `ε` and `e` (`keyΦ`) and with restriction (`natΦ`).
The preimages under `π` of the chart preimages in `Y₀` are the chart preimages in `Y` (`hUeq`, `hUeq₂`).

Let `u₀ : L₀ → ∏ G₀ (t₀⁻¹ chart j)` and `d₀` (differences of restrictions to pairwise intersections) be the
Čech maps over `W₀`, and `u`, `d` those over `W`. Through `eL := e` and the isomorphisms `Φ` on products, `u`
and `d` are the base changes of `u₀` and `d₀` (`hu`, `hd`). With `J := (vanishing ideal over W₀)^N R₀`, `hk₀`
and `hc₀` give the two properties of `u₀`, `d₀` for `J`, and the ported
`LinearMap.forall_smul_eq_zero_and_forall_exists_eq_smul_of_ker_of_equiv_baseChange_of_flat` (flatness of
`R₀ → R`) transfers them to `u`, `d` for `J R`. Finally, every element of `(vanishing ideal over W)^N` maps
into `J R` (`hJ`: the vanishing ideal over `W` is generated by the restriction of the one over `W₀`,
`IdealSheafData.map_ideal'`).
