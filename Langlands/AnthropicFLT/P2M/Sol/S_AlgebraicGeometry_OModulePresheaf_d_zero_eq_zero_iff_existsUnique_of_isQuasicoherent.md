## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_OModulePresheaf_d_zero_eq_zero_iff_existsUnique_of_isQuasicoherent.solution`
proves the statement of the companion theorem in
`Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_OModulePresheaf_d_zero_eq_zero_iff_existsUnique_of_isQuasicoherent`:
for `V` affine and `F` quasi-coherent, a Čech `0`-cochain on an ordered affine cover is a cocycle if and
only if it is the family of restrictions of a unique global section.

Proof (namespace `…AlgebraicGeometry.OModulePresheaf.AffineAcyclicF0`). The module works with covers
`K : Scheme.OrderedAffineCoverOf W` of an affine open `W`, with the alternating Čech cochains `cochainOf`,
the differential `dOf` and the augmentation `augOf : F(W) → cochainOf 0`. The section `AltImg` handles
alternating indices as finite sets of cover indices. If one member of the cover is all of `W`, the explicit
contracting homotopy `htpy` (and `hAug` in degree `0`) shows that the augmented complex is exact
(`wholeSpace_exact`, `wholeSpace_exact₀`). In general, restrict the cover to a basic open `D(f)` contained
in a member of `K` (`restrictBasic`, `resBasic`); there that member is all of `D(f)`, so the restricted
complex is exact. Quasi-coherence of `F` identifies the sections over `D(f)` and its intersections with
localisations at `f` (`away_surj`, `away_ker`), so every cocycle becomes a coboundary after multiplying by
a power of `f` (`smul_pow_mem_range`, `smul_pow_mem_rangeAug`). Such `f` generate the unit ideal
(`exists_refinement`), so the partition-of-unity lemma gives exactness on `W` itself (`affine_acyclic` in
positive degrees, `affine_acyclic₀` in degree `0`) and injectivity of the augmentation (`augOf_injective`).
`solution` applies `affine_acyclic₀` and `augOf_injective` to the cover `K` of `⊤`.
