Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.HilbertFunctor.exists_closedImmersion_flat_lfp_forall_mem_iff_ker_ideal_eq_of_point_hilbertFunctionOf`:
let `P ∈ ℚ[X]` be the Hilbert polynomial of some homogeneous ideal over some field (universe `uz`).
There is `D₀` such that for every `m ≥ D₀` there is an assignment `Φ` sending each point `p` of the
Hilbert functor of `ℙⁿ` over a ring `A` with Hilbert function `hilbertFunctionOf n P m` to a closed
subscheme `Φ A p` of `ℙⁿ_A`, with: (1) `Φ A p` is a closed immersion, flat and locally of finite
presentation over `Spec A`; (2) `Φ A` is injective on points up to isomorphism over `ℙⁿ_A`;
(3) compatibility with base change along `A → B` when `q.I` is the extension of `p.I` (a pullback
square over `ℙⁿ_B → ℙⁿ_A`); (4) every closed subscheme of `ℙⁿ_A`, flat and locally of finite
presentation, all of whose geometric fibres over algebraically closed fields arise from points, arises
from a point of `A`; (5) for `d ≥ m`, a form `F` of degree `d` lies in `p.I` iff all its
dehomogenizations vanish on `Φ A p`; (6) the ideal of `Φ A p` on each chart `D₊(Xᵢ)` is generated by
the dehomogenizations of the forms of `p.I`.

Proof (namespace `HilbComparisonAssembly`). `sheafOf n A I`: the ideal sheaf on `ℙⁿ_A` with chart
ideals generated by dehomogenizations of `I` (from the ported
`ProjSpace.exists_idealSheafData_ideal_basicOpen_eq_span`), `Phi` its closed subscheme. Auxiliary
facts on degrees of elements of spans of forms (`degree_ge_of_mem_span`, `eq_zero_of_mem_span_of_lt`,
`le_of_forall_isHomogeneous`). Main proof: choose `D₀` as the maximum of the bounds from the ported
theorems `HilbertFunctor.exists_forall_mem_of_forall_X_pow_mul_mem_hilbertFunctionOf` (saturation),
`HilbertFunctor.exists_point_I_eq_span_of_isClosedImmersion_of_flat_of_locallyOfFinitePresentation`
(points from flat closed subschemes), `MvPolynomial.exists_forall_finrank_piece_succ_le_eval_and_exists_eq_eval`
(Gotzmann-type persistence) and the degree where `P` agrees with the Hilbert function. By
`Point.I_eq_span_of_forall_finrank_piece_succ_le` the ideals of points are generated in degree `m`;
flatness is `Point.flat_comp_of_ideal_basicOpen_eq_span`, local finite presentation
`ProjSpace.locallyOfFinitePresentation_comp_of_fg_of_ideal_basicOpen_eq_span` (the degree-`m`
part of `p.I` is finitely generated, a direct summand of a finite free module). The remaining clauses
follow from the chart description of the ideal sheaf
(`ProjSpace.awayToSection_mk_mem_span_iff_exists_X_pow_mul_mem`) and the ported base-change and
injectivity results for these ideal sheaves.
