## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_HilbertFunctor_Point_I_eq_span_of_forall_finrank_piece_succ_le.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_HilbertFunctor_Point_I_eq_span_of_forall_finrank_piece_succ_le`. Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.HilbertFunctor.Point.I_eq_span_of_forall_finrank_piece_succ_le`: let
`h : ℕ → ℕ` satisfy `h d = C(n + d, n)` for `d < m`, and suppose that for every `e ≥ m`, every field
`K` (universe `uz`) and every ideal `J` of `K[X₀, …, Xₙ]` generated by forms of degree `e` with
`dim piece J e = h e`, one has `dim piece J (e + 1) ≤ h (e + 1)` (a Gotzmann-type persistence bound).
Then for every ring `A` and every point `P` of the Hilbert functor with Hilbert function `h`
(`Point A n h`), the ideal `P.I` is generated by its forms of degree `m`.

Proof (namespace `HilbGenH2c`). Module facts: a surjection onto a projective module splits
(`exists_equiv_prod_ker`); a finite module with all fibre dimensions `0` is zero
(`subsingleton_of_finrank_fiber_eq_zero`); homogeneous pieces of degree `d` are finite free of rank
`C(n + d, n)` (`finite_homogeneousSubmodule`, `free_homogeneousSubmodule`, `natCard_degree_eq`,
`finrank_homogeneousSubmodule`); spans of forms are homogeneous ideals (`span_homogeneous`).
Section `Step`: for `J' = (forms of degree e in P.I)`, `piece J' e ≅ piece P.I e`, and the natural
surjection `π : piece J' (e + 1) → piece P.I (e + 1)` is injective (`ker_π_eq_bot`): by the
hypothesis its source has fibre dimensions at most `h (e + 1)` (`finrank_fiber_Q₁_le`), the target is
projective of constant rank `h (e + 1)`, so the kernel (a direct summand) has zero fibres. Hence
every form of degree `e + 1` in `P.I` lies in `J'` (`mem_J'_of_mem`). Section `Low`: `P.I` has no
nonzero forms of degree `d < m` (their pieces already have full rank). The solution concludes by
induction on the degree from `m`, decomposing elements of `P.I` into homogeneous components.
