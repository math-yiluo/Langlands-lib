## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_Ideal_exists_ne_zero_and_forall_isMaximal_radical_map_isPrime.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_Ideal_exists_ne_zero_and_forall_isMaximal_radical_map_isPrime`. Statement. `Ideal.exists_ne_zero_and_forall_isMaximal_radical_map_isPrime` (generic irreducibility of fibres): let `R ⊆ C` be
finitely generated domains over a field `k`, with `C` of finite type over `R`, such that the subfield generated by `R`
is separably closed in `Frac C`. There is `r ≠ 0` in `R` such that for every maximal `n ⊆ R` with `r ∉ n`, either
`nC = C` or `√(nC)` is prime.

Proof. By the ported `Algebra.exists_monic_irreducible_map_algebraicClosure_hypersurfaceModel_of_forall_isSeparable`,
after inverting an element `Frac C` is the function field of a hypersurface over `R[z₁, …, z_d]` defined by a
polynomial that stays irreducible over an algebraic closure (section `OptionEquiv` for the variable bookkeeping).
The ported `MvPolynomial.exists_ne_zero_and_forall_irreducible_map_of_irreducible_map_algebraicClosure` shows that its
specializations at maximal ideals outside some `V(r)` stay irreducible. Section `Main`: for such `n`, the fibre
`C/nC` is generically the irreducible hypersurface; dimension counting (ported
`Ideal.height_add_ringKrullDim_quotient_eq_ringKrullDim_of_finiteType`,
`Ideal.natCast_le_ringKrullDim_quotient_of_mem_minimalPrimes_map_of_algebraicIndependent`) shows every minimal prime
of `nC` has full dimension and hence is the unique one, so `√(nC)` is prime (`radical_eq_comap_nilradical`,
`comap_mem_minimalPrimes`, `nilradical_isPrime_of_radicial`).
