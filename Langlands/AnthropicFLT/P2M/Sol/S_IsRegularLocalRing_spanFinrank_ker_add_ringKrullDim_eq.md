## Content

Proof (namespace `RSMU`). Section `CotImage`: the image `cotImage I` of an ideal `I ⊆ 𝔪` in the
cotangent space `𝔪/𝔪²` and lifts of its elements. Section `Surjection`: for `f` surjective with
kernel `I`, the cotangent space of `B` is the quotient of that of `A` by the image of `I`. Section
`Main`: put `a = dim A` and `b = dim B` (for regular rings these are the generator counts of the
maximal ideals). The image `W` of `I` has dimension `s` with `s + b = a`. Lifting a basis of `W` to
elements of `I` gives an ideal `J ⊆ I` with at most `s` generators. The local ring `A ⧸ J` has
maximal ideal generated by `b` elements and height `≥ a - s = b` (Krull's bound
`ringKrullDim_le_ringKrullDim_quotient_add_spanFinrank`), so it is regular of dimension `b`, hence a
domain. The induced surjection `A ⧸ J → B` is injective: a nonzero kernel element `y` would give
`dim ((A ⧸ J) ⧸ (y)) < b ≤ dim B`. So `I = J` has at most `s = a - b` generators, and Krull's
bound gives the reverse inequality.
