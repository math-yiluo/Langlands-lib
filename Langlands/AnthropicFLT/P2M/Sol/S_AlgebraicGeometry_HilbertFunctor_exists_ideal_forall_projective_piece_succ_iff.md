## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_HilbertFunctor_exists_ideal_forall_projective_piece_succ_iff.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_HilbertFunctor_exists_ideal_forall_projective_piece_succ_iff`. Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.HilbertFunctor.exists_ideal_forall_projective_piece_succ_iff`: suppose that over
every field `K`, ideals generated by forms of degree `m` with `dim piece J m = q` satisfy
`dim piece J (m + 1) ≤ r`. Let `I` be an ideal of `A[X₀, …, Xₙ]` generated by forms of degree `m` with
`piece I m` projective of constant rank `q`. Then there is an ideal `𝔞` of `A` such that for every
`A`-algebra `B`, the piece in degree `m + 1` of the extended ideal is projective of constant rank `r`
over `B` iff `A → B` kills `𝔞` (the locus is a closed subscheme of `Spec A`).

Proof (namespace `HilbClosedH2b`). The pieces are finite (`finite_homogeneousSubmodule`,
`finite_piece`) and spans of forms are homogeneous (`span_homogeneous`). Base change of pieces (ported
`HilbertFunctor.exists_linearEquiv_baseChange_piece_map`) and the hypothesis over residue fields give
`dim κ(𝔭) ⊗ piece I (m + 1) ≤ r` at every prime. The ported flattening-type result for finite modules
with fibre rank at most `r` gives the ideal `𝔞`; base change of pieces along `A → B` transports
projectivity and rank (`Module.rankAtStalk_eq_of_equiv`).
