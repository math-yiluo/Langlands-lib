## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_ClassFunction_exists_one_eq_sum_zsmul_induced_one_of_hyperelementary.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in
`Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_ClassFunction_exists_one_eq_sum_zsmul_induced_one_of_hyperelementary`.
It is `SolomonProof.main` with the hyperelementary predicate unfolded.

Proof (namespace `…SolomonProof`, for `G : Type` finite):

- `IsHyper H` is the hyperelementary predicate of the statement; `IsHyper.of_le_conj` shows that `S` is hyperelementary whenever `a⁻¹ * S * a ≤ K` for a hyperelementary `K`.
- `pc H g` is the integer number of cosets in `G ⧸ H` fixed by `g` (the permutation character). `card_mul_pc` and `induced_one_eq_pc` identify it with `ClassFunction.induced H 1`; `pc_top` gives `pc ⊤ = 1`.
- `pc_mul_pc_eq_sum` writes `pc H * pc K` as the sum, over the `G`-orbits on `G ⧸ H × G ⧸ K`, of `pc` of the stabilizer of an orbit representative. Each such stabilizer conjugates into `K` (`conj_mem_of_mem_stabilizer`), so `pc_mul_pc_mem_span` puts the product in the span of `pc S` for subgroups `S` conjugating into `K`. Hence `P`, the `ℤ`-span of all `pc H`, is a subring `Psub` of `G → ℤ` containing `1`, and `I`, the `ℤ`-span of the `pc H` with `H` hyperelementary, is an ideal of it (`mul_mem_I`, using `IsHyper.of_le_conj`).
- Arithmetic part (section `Arith`, prime `q`): `exists_isHyper_not_dvd` finds, for every `x : G`, a hyperelementary `H` with `q ∤ pc H x`. Here `C` is the cyclic group generated by the part `x ^ (q ^ a)` of `x` of order prime to `q`, and `H` is generated by `C` and a Sylow `q`-subgroup of the normalizer of `C` (which contains `x`).
- `one_mem_I`: if `1 ∉ I`, then `I` lies in a maximal ideal `𝔪` of the finite `ℤ`-algebra `Psub`. Its residue field has prime characteristic `p` (characteristic `0` is impossible since the contraction of `𝔪` to `ℤ` is maximal). By going up, `𝔪` lies under a maximal ideal `𝔐` of `G → ℤ`, and `𝔐` is `{f | p ∣ f x₀}` for some `x₀ : G`. Then `exists_isHyper_not_dvd p x₀` gives `pc H ∈ I ⊆ 𝔪 ⊆ 𝔐` with `p ∤ pc H x₀`, a contradiction.
- `main` writes `1 ∈ I` as an integer combination indexed by the finite set of hyperelementary subgroups and converts to the `ℚ`-valued equation via `induced_one_eq_pc`.
