## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_ProjSpace_awayToSection_mk_mem_span_iff_exists_X_pow_mul_mem.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_ProjSpace_awayToSection_mk_mem_span_iff_exists_X_pow_mul_mem`. Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.ProjSpace.awayToSection_mk_mem_span_iff_exists_X_pow_mul_mem`: for an ideal `I` of
`A[X₀, …, Xₙ]` and a form `F` of degree `d`, the dehomogenization `F / Xᵢ^d` lies in the ideal of the
chart `D₊(Xᵢ)` generated by the dehomogenizations of the forms of `I` iff `Xᵢ^N F ∈ I` for some `N`.

Proof (namespace `K6SOL`). Section `ChartIdeal`: `frac`, `comp`, `fcHom` (dehomogenization of degree-`d`
components) and their additivity/multiplicativity. Section `Sat`: the set `satAway I i` of elements
`P / Xᵢ^D` with `Xᵢ^N P ∈ I` for some `N` is an ideal of the chart ring (`InSat`, `satAway`), containing the
generators; hence it contains the generated ideal, giving `Xᵢ^N F ∈ I` (`exists_X_pow_mul_mem_of_inSat`,
`awayMk_eq_frac`). Conversely `F / Xᵢ^d = (Xᵢ^N F) / Xᵢ^{N + d}` (`frac_xpow_mul`) is a generator.
