## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_MvPolynomial_exists_forall_sum_C_mul_X_mul_mem_imp_of_forall_exists_X_pow_mul_mem.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_MvPolynomial_exists_forall_sum_C_mul_X_mul_mem_imp_of_forall_exists_X_pow_mul_mem`. Statement. `MvPolynomial.exists_forall_sum_C_mul_X_mul_mem_imp_of_forall_exists_X_pow_mul_mem`: a saturated homogeneous ideal `I`
of `K[x₀, …, x_n]` over an infinite field (if `xᵢᴺ F ∈ I` for all `i` then `F ∈ I`) has a linear nonzerodivisor:
there is `ℓ = Σ aᵢ xᵢ` such that `ℓ F ∈ I` implies `F ∈ I` for every form `F`.

Proof. For `m` large, the ideal `I_m` generated by the degree-`m` part of `I` has maximal growth in degree `m`
(Hilbert polynomial and Macaulay's bound: ported `MvPolynomial.finrank_piece_succ_le_macaulayPow`,
`Nat.exists_forall_eq_macaulayPow_of_forall_le_macaulayPow`, and persistence
`MvPolynomial.forall_finrank_piece_succ_eq_macaulayPow_of_finrank_piece_succ_eq_macaulayPow`), and agrees with `I` in
degrees `≥ m` (`Ipart`, `Ipart_eq_of_le_of_finrank_eq`). The ported
`MvPolynomial.exists_forall_eval_ne_zero_mem_of_mul_mem_of_finrank_piece_succ_eq_macaulayPow` gives a nonzerodivisor
`ℓ` in degrees `≥ m`, chosen using that `K` is infinite (`exists_eval_ne_zero`). For a form `F` of lower degree with
`ℓ F ∈ I`, multiplying by powers of the variables reaches degree `≥ m`, so `xᵢᴺ F ∈ I` for all `i` and saturation
gives `F ∈ I` (`main`).
