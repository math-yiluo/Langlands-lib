## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_OModulePresheaf_exists_H0_inf_pow_smul_le_pow_smul_H0_unit_of_isProper.solution`
proves the statement of the companion theorem in
`Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_OModulePresheaf_exists_H0_inf_pow_smul_le_pow_smul_H0_unit_of_isProper`;
it is `SolFF0SepUnit.main`.

Proof: Artin–Rees via the Rees algebra (namespace `SolFF0SepUnit`). Section `Setup`: `Rr I` is the Rees
algebra `⊕ I^k X^k ⊆ A[X]` (noetherian when `A` is; `Rr.coeff_mem`). `P' := P ×_{Spec A} Spec (Rr I)` with
projections `fst'`, `π'`, and `P₁ := P' ×_{Spec (Rr I)} Spec A[X]`, which is `P ×_{Spec A} Spec A[X]`
(`isPullback_P₁`) with `g : P₁ ⟶ P'`, `p₁ : P₁ ⟶ P`. Over an affine open `U` of `P`, the sections of `P₁`
over the preimage are `Γ(P, U)[X]` (`θ`, `θ_bijective`), and the sections of `P'` are generated by those of
`P` and of `Rr I` (`closure_eq_top`). `φg : 𝒪_{P'} → g_* 𝒪_{P₁}` is the pullback map, and `F'` is its image,
an FLT presheaf of modules over `π'`: on the preimage of `U` it is the Rees ring `⊕ I^k Γ(P, U) X^k`. It is
coherent and quasi-coherent (`isCoherent_F'`, `isQuasicoherent_F'`). `K'` is the cover of `P'` pulled
back from `K`.

Section `Cochains`: `ΦC` identifies sections of `P₁` over the cover pieces with polynomials over
`Γ(P, K.inter s)`; `coeffC i j` takes the `j`-th coefficient of a Čech cochain of `F'`, commutes with the
differential (`coeffC_d`), and lies in `I^j` (`coeffC_mem`); `coeffC_smul` computes it for an `Rr I`-multiple.
`zC i k b` is the cochain `b X^k` of `F'` for a cochain `b` of `𝒪_P` with entries in `I^k`
(`coeffC_zC`, `d_zC_eq_zero`).

Section `Main`: `Z0` (the `0`-cocycles of `F'` on `K'`) is a finite `Rr I`-module by the ported finiteness
theorem `OModulePresheaf.cechFinite_of_isProper` applied to the proper morphism `π'`, so it is noetherian.
`N m` is the submodule generated by the cocycles `zZ k z = z X^k` with `k ≤ m` (`gens`, `N_mono`).
`good_of_mem`: every element of `N c₀` has `j`-th coefficient in `I^(j - c₀) • H⁰`. `main`: the chain `N`
stabilises at some `c₀`; a cocycle `z` with entries in `I^(n + c₀)` gives `z X^(n+c₀) ∈ N (n+c₀) = N c₀`, and
`good_of_mem` with `j = n + c₀` gives `z ∈ I^n • H⁰`.
