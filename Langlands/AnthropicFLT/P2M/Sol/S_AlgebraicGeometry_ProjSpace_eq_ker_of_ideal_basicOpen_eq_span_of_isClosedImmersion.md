## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_ProjSpace_eq_ker_of_ideal_basicOpen_eq_span_of_isClosedImmersion.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_ProjSpace_eq_ker_of_ideal_basicOpen_eq_span_of_isClosedImmersion`. Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.ProjSpace.eq_ker_of_ideal_basicOpen_eq_span_of_isClosedImmersion`: for a closed
immersion `ι : Z ⟶ ℙⁿ_A` and `m ∈ ℕ`, the ideal sheaf whose chart ideals are generated by the
dehomogenizations of the ideal generated by the forms of degree `≥ m` vanishing on `Z` is the kernel
ideal sheaf of `ι`.

Proof (namespace `K5Aux`: dehomogenization `frac` with its additivity, multiplicativity and sum
lemmas `val_sum`, `mk_sum`). By chart-wise extensionality it suffices to compare the ideals on each
`D₊(Xᵢ)`. The generators vanish on `Z`, so the generated ideal lies in the kernel. Conversely, every
element of the kernel on the chart is `H / Xᵢ^d` with `H` vanishing on the chart; multiplying by a power
of `Xᵢ` makes `Xᵢ^N H` of degree `≥ m` and vanishing on all charts, hence a generator, and the ported
`ProjSpace.awayToSection_mk_mem_span_iff_exists_X_pow_mul_mem` concludes.
