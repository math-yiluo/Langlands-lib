## Content

Proof (namespace `SolKS`): an Artin–Rees argument on the Rees algebra, using finiteness of Čech
cohomology of a coherent module presheaf on a proper scheme over a noetherian ring.

- Setup. `Rr I` is the Rees algebra `A[It] ⊆ A[X]` (noetherian since `A` is). Base change `q` to
  `P' = P ×_A Spec (Rr I)` (proper over `Spec (Rr I)`, projection `π'`) and further to
  `P₁ = P' ×_{Rr I} Spec A[X] = P ×_A Spec A[X]` (map `g : P₁ ⟶ P'`, affine). Over an affine open
  `U` of `P`, `Γ(P₁, …) ≅ Γ(P, U)[X]` (`θ`, `θ_bijective`, `ΦC`). `F'` is the image of the
  structure presheaf of `P'` in the push-forward of the structure presheaf of `P₁` along `g`
  (`φg`, `OModulePresheaf.im`); over the cover's affine opens its sections are polynomials over
  `Γ(P, U)` whose `X^j`-coefficient lies in `I^j Γ(P, U)` (`coeffC_mem`, via the Rees algebra of
  `I Γ(P, U)`), and every monomial `x X^k` with `x ∈ I^k Γ(P, U)` is such a section
  (`monomial_mem_Sθ`). `F'` is coherent and quasi-coherent (`isCoherent_F'`,
  `isQuasicoherent_F'`); `K'` is the pulled-back ordered affine cover of `P'`.
- Cochains. `coeffC i j` extracts the `X^j`-coefficient of an `F'`-cochain, giving a cochain of
  the structure presheaf of `P` with entries in `I^j` (`coeffC_mem`), compatible with `d`, sums and
  the `Rr I`-action; `zC i k b` is the `F'`-cochain `b·X^k` for a cochain `b` with entries in `I^k`,
  a cocycle when `b` is a coboundary (`d_zC_eq_zero`).
- Main. `M = Z1 / B1` is the first Čech cohomology of `F'` for `K'`; it is a finite, hence
  noetherian, `Rr I`-module by the C1 theorem `OModulePresheaf.cechFinite_of_isProper`. `N m` is the
  submodule generated by the classes `cls k b` (`k ≤ m`) of `b·X^k` for coboundaries `b = d w`
  with entries in `I^k`. The increasing chain `N m` stabilizes at some `c₀`; every element of
  `N c₀` is `Good` (represented by a cocycle whose `X^j`-coefficient lies in `d (IC (j - c₀))`,
  `good_of_mem`). For `w` with `d w` in `I^{n+c₀}`, comparing coefficients of `X^{n+c₀}` in
  `zZ (n+c₀) (d w) = z + d w'` with `z` good gives `w₁ ∈ IC n` with `d w₁ = (d w) - d(coeffC w')`,
  and `w₁ + coeffC 0 (n+c₀) w'` is the required cochain with entries in `I^n` (`main`). `solution`
  applies `main`.
