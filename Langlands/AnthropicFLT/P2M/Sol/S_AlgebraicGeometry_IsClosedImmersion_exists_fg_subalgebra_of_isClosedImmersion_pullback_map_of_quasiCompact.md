## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_IsClosedImmersion_exists_fg_subalgebra_of_isClosedImmersion_pullback_map_of_quasiCompact.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_IsClosedImmersion_exists_fg_subalgebra_of_isClosedImmersion_pullback_map_of_quasiCompact`. Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.IsClosedImmersion.exists_fg_subalgebra_of_isClosedImmersion_pullback_map_of_quasiCompact`:
let `m : W ⟶ V` be quasi-compact, quasi-separated and locally of finite type, and `v : V ⟶ Spec A₀`
quasi-compact. If for an `A₀`-algebra `A` the base change of `m` to `Spec A` is a closed immersion,
then every finite subset of `A` lies in a finitely generated `A₀`-subalgebra `T` such that the base
change of `m` to `Spec T` is already a closed immersion.

Proof (namespace `ClosedImmQc18`). `isPullback_fst_of_comm`, `isPullback_chart`: pullback squares
relating the base changes of `m` and of its restrictions to an open cover of `V`; `fg_finset_sup`:
finite suprema of finitely generated subalgebras are finitely generated. Cover the quasi-compact `V`
by finitely many affines `Vⱼ`; over each chart the base change of `m` to `A` is a closed immersion, and
the affine case (the ported theorem for the restriction `mⱼ` of `m` over an affine) gives
finitely generated `Tⱼ ⊇ s`. Let `T` be generated by all `Tⱼ` and `s`; over each chart the base change
to `T` is a base change of the one to `Tⱼ` (a pullback square), hence a closed immersion, and being a
closed immersion is local on the target (`IsZariskiLocalAtTarget.of_openCover`).
