## Content

Proof. Write `T = adjoin A₀ s` and `T' = adjoin T t`; by Mathlib's `Algebra.adjoin_union_eq_adjoin_adjoin`,
`T'.restrictScalars A₀ = adjoin A₀ (s ∪ t)`, which is finitely generated and contains `adjoin A₀ s`.
