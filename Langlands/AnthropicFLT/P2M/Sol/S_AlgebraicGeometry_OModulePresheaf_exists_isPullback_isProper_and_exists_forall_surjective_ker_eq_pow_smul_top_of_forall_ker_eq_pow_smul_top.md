The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_OModulePresheaf_exists_isPullback_isProper_and_exists_forall_surjective_ker_eq_pow_smul_top_of_forall_ker_eq_pow_smul_top.solution`
proves the statement of the companion theorem in
`Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_OModulePresheaf_exists_isPullback_isProper_and_exists_forall_surjective_ker_eq_pow_smul_top_of_forall_ker_eq_pow_smul_top`.

Proof. The module-local namespace `AlgebraicGeometry.OModulePresheaf` (inside the solution namespace)
defines, for `s : Y ⟶ Spec R` and an `R`-module `M`, the constant tensor presheaf
`tensorDatum s M` with sections `Γ(Y, V) ⊗[R] M` (sections typed as `SecR s V`, an `R`-algebra through `s`;
restriction `resAlg`), the map `tensorUnit s M V : m ↦ 1 ⊗ m` (`res_tensorUnit`), and proves that it is
coherent for `M` finite (`isCoherent_tensorDatum`) and quasi-coherent (`isQuasicoherent_tensorDatum`, from
`isLocalization_basicOpen`).

Namespace `K73W2A`, pure algebra: for an `Algebra.IsPushout B C R D`, a surjection `pr : L → M` of
`B`-modules and an isomorphism `β : C ⊗[B] M ≃ N`, `theta : D ⊗[R] L → N` is `β ∘ (C ⊗ pr)` composed with
the cancellation isomorphism `frameEquiv : D ⊗[R] L ≃ C ⊗[B] L`. It is `C`-linear (`theta_smul`),
sends `1 ⊗ x` to `β (1 ⊗ pr x)` (`theta_one_tmul`), is surjective (`theta_surjective`), has kernel
`(K R) • ⊤` when `ker pr = K • ⊤` (`theta_eq_zero_iff`), and is natural in `C`, `D` (`theta_naturality`) and
in `M` (`theta_level`).

Namespace `W2`, geometry: for `p : V' ⟶ P` and an affine open `W` of `P` with an algebra `Γ(P, W) → R`,
`Y` is the pullback of `qW : p ⁻¹ᵁ W ⟶ Spec Γ(P, W)` along `Spec R → Spec Γ(P, W)`, with projections
`sY : Y ⟶ Spec R` (proper when `p` is: `isProper_sY`) and `tY : Y ⟶ V'`. `isPullback_tY_sY` identifies
it as the pullback of `p` along `Spec R → W → P`; preimages of affine opens `V ≤ p ⁻¹ᵁ W` are affine
(`isAffineOpen_tY_preimage`), and their rings of sections form a pushout square
(`isPushout_sections`, `algebra_isPushout`).

`solution` (with `p = g ≫ i`): take `Y`, `sY`, `tY` from `W2`, `G := tensorDatum sY L`,
`ε := tensorUnit`, and `θ n V h := theta` for the pushout of rings over `V`, with `pr n` and the
isomorphism `β` chosen from `hβ`. The listed properties are `isCoherent_tensorDatum`,
`isQuasicoherent_tensorDatum`, `res_tensorUnit`, the identity isomorphism on affine opens, and
`theta_smul`, `theta_naturality` (with `hηV`), `theta_level` (with `hηφ`), `theta_one_tmul`,
`theta_surjective`, `theta_eq_zero_iff` (with `hprk`).
