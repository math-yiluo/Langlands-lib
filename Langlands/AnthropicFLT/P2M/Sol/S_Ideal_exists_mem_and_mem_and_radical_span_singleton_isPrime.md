## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_Ideal_exists_mem_and_mem_and_radical_span_singleton_isPrime.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_Ideal_exists_mem_and_mem_and_radical_span_singleton_isPrime`. Statement. `Ideal.exists_mem_and_mem_and_radical_span_singleton_isPrime` (an irreducible hypersurface through two points): let
`A` be a finitely generated domain of dimension `≥ 2` over an algebraically closed field `k` and `m₀ ≠ m₁` maximal
ideals. There is `f ≠ 0` in `m₀ ∩ m₁` such that `√(f)` is prime.

Proof (Bertini-type argument). Choose a Noether normalization `k[x₁, …, x_n] ⊆ A` with `n ≥ 2` adapted to the two
points (`exists_noether_two_le`, `two_le_height_of_mem_of_mem`). Consider the pencil of functions
`f = ρ(x) ` vanishing at both points, given by linear combinations with parameters (`rhoVec`, `rho`,
`transcendental_add_mul`, `algebraicIndependent_ratio`). Section `Fields`: by the ported field-theoretic Bertini
theorem `IntermediateField.mem_adjoin_sum_mul_of_isSeparable_of_algebraicIndependent`, for the generic member the
function field of `A` is separably closed over the field generated by the generic member, so the ported
`Ideal.exists_ne_zero_and_forall_isMaximal_radical_map_isPrime` gives a dense open set of parameters for which
`√(f)` is prime (`exists_ne_zero_forall_fibre`, `radical_span_isPrime_of_away`). Section `AvoidMinimalPrimes`: as
`k` is infinite, a parameter in this open set with `f ≠ 0` exists (`irredHyp`).
