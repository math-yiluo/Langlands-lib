## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_MvPolynomial_finrank_piece_eq_of_maximal_growth.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_MvPolynomial_finrank_piece_eq_of_maximal_growth`. Statement. `MvPolynomial.finrank_piece_eq_of_maximal_growth` (Gotzmann persistence for a numerical function): let `h : ℕ → ℕ`
satisfy, for `e ≥ m`, the bound "every ideal generated in degree `e` with `h_J(e) = h(e)` has `h_J(e + 1) ≤ h(e + 1)`"
and the realizability "some ideal generated in degree `e` has `h_J(e) = h(e)` and `h_J(e + 1) = h(e + 1)`". If `J` is
generated in degree `m` with `h_J(m) = h(m)` and `h_J(m + 1) = h(m + 1)`, then `h_J(e) = h(e)` for all `e ≥ m`.

Proof. Section `Growth`: the hypotheses force `h(e + 1) = h(e)^⟨e⟩` for `e ≥ m` (Macaulay's bound, ported
`MvPolynomial.finrank_piece_succ_le_macaulayPow`, against the lex-segment ideal of the ported
`MvPolynomial.exists_span_monomial_finrank_piece_eq_and_finrank_piece_succ_eq_macaulayPow`; `h_succ_eq`,
`two_values`). Sections `Regen`, `Forms`: the ideal generated by the degree-`e` part of `J` (`formsIdeal`) agrees with
`J` in degrees `≥ e` (`Ik_formsIdeal_eq`). Then induction on `e` with the ported persistence step
`MvPolynomial.finrank_piece_add_two_eq_macaulayPow_of_finrank_piece_succ_eq_macaulayPow` (`main`); sections `Full`,
`Positive`, `Zero` treat the cases `h(e)` maximal, positive and zero (`h_eq_full`, `h_eq_zero`,
`persistence_zero`).
