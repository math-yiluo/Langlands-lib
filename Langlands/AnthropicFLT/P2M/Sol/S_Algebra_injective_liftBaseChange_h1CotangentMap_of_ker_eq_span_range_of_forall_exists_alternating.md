## Content

The theorem `solution` proves the statement of
`Algebra.injective_liftBaseChange_h1CotangentMap_of_ker_eq_span_range_of_forall_exists_alternating`
(companion module `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_Algebra_injective_liftBaseChange_h1CotangentMap_of_ker_eq_span_range_of_forall_exists_alternating`).

Proof (namespace `P2mKcKoszulSyz`).
- Section `Alt`: alternating matrices (`IsAlt`), `sum_sum_smul_eq_zero_of_isAlt`, lifting alternating matrices
  along surjections (`exists_isAlt_lift`), and the Koszul syzygy condition `HasKoszulSyzygies`.
- Section `KerSq`: `ker_inf_sq_le_ker_mul`: for a surjection `φ : A → B` and `r` with Koszul syzygies,
  `ker φ ⊓ (φ⁻¹(r))² ≤ ker φ · φ⁻¹(r)`.
- Section `Comp`: for generators `Q` of `T` over `S` and `P` of `S` over `R`, with composite generators
  `Q.comp P`, the auxiliary maps `nu`, `theta` and `alpha : T ⊗[S] Cot(P) → Cot(Q.comp P)` on the cotangent
  spaces of the presentations. If the kernel of `Q` is generated by `rs` with Koszul syzygies, then
  `map_ker_inf_sq_le` (from `ker_inf_sq_le_ker_mul`) gives `alpha_injective`, and with flatness of `T` over `S`,
  `h1_liftBaseChange_injective` gives injectivity of `T ⊗[S] H1(P) → H1(Q.comp P)`.
- Section `Main`: `main` takes `Q = Generators.ofSurjective x hx` and `P = Generators.self R S`, and transfers
  injectivity to `Algebra.H1Cotangent` through the comparison isomorphism `Extension.H1Cotangent.equiv` between
  `Q.comp P` and the canonical presentation of `T` over `R`. `solution` converts the hypothesis on relations to
  `HasKoszulSyzygies`.
