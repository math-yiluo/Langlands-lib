## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_HilbertFunctor_exists_point_I_eq_of_projective_piece_succ.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_HilbertFunctor_exists_point_I_eq_of_projective_piece_succ`. Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.HilbertFunctor.exists_point_I_eq_of_projective_piece_succ` (Gotzmann's
persistence over a ring): let `h` satisfy `h d = C(n + d, n)` for `d < m`, the persistence bound of
`Point.I_eq_span_of_forall_finrank_piece_succ_le` for `e ≥ m`, and suppose for every `e ≥ m` some
ideal over some field generated in degree `e` attains `h e` and `h (e + 1)`. Let `I ⊆ A[X₀, …, Xₙ]` be
generated by forms of degree `m` with `piece I m` and `piece I (m + 1)` projective of constant ranks
`h m` and `h (m + 1)`. Then `I` is the ideal of a point of the Hilbert functor with Hilbert function
`h`.

Proof (namespace `GotzH2a`). Section `Pieces`: finiteness and freeness of homogeneous pieces and
their ranks, homogeneous components of products, split surjections onto projectives. Section
`SpanF`: for `I` generated by finitely many forms `F₁, …, F_g` of degree `m` (obtained from a finite
generating set of the kernel of `piece`-projection, which is a direct summand), the degree-`(m + d)`
part of `I` is the image of the presentation map `presMap` (`range_presMap`), so every piece is
finitely presented (`finitePresentation_piece`) and pieces below `m` are full
(`comap_eq_bot_of_lt`, `finrank_piece_of_lt`). Section `AtPrime`: the fibre dimensions of the pieces
are the ranks at stalks (`finrank_piece_residueField`), and over the local rings the pieces are free
(`free_piece_atPrime`, from Gotzmann persistence over the residue fields). Then each piece is
projective (local freeness of a finitely presented module), and its rank at every prime is `h d` by
the ported `MvPolynomial.finrank_piece_eq_of_maximal_growth` over the residue field.
