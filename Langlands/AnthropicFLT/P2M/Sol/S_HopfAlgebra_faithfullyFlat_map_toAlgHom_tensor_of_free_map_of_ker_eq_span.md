# A faithfully flat tensor square from a free quotient

Suppose K' and K are stable subalgebras of a commutative Hopf algebra H over k, with compatible K'-algebra structure on K and H flat over K'. Let π: H → Hb be a surjective algebra map whose kernel is the ideal generated by the augmentation ideal of K'. If Hb is free over the image K.map π, then the map K tensor_{K'} H → H tensor_{K'} H is faithfully flat. The theorem requires only comultiplication stability for these two subalgebras.

`S17Torsor` explicitly constructs the algebra isomorphism H tensor_{K'} H ≃ H tensor_k (H/J), where J is the stated augmentation-generated ideal. Its forward map uses comultiplication, and its inverse uses the antipode; two Sweedler identities prove that they are inverse.

`S17Sq` identifies H tensor_{K'} K with H tensor_k image(K in H/J). Flatness over K' makes the first tensor inclusion injective; comultiplication stability and the antipode formula identify its range. The quotient H/J is nonzero because its counit factors to k. Freeness over the image therefore gives faithful flatness, preserved by tensor extension and these isomorphisms. Finally the first isomorphism theorem identifies H/J with the supplied Hb and transports its free module structure. Tensor flips put the result in the order used by the statement.
