## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_MvPolynomial_relation_mem_span_of_forall_isHomogeneous_mem_span.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_MvPolynomial_relation_mem_span_of_forall_isHomogeneous_mem_span`. Statement. `MvPolynomial.relation_mem_span_of_forall_isHomogeneous_mem_span`: if the forms `F₁, …, F_g` of degree `m` span all forms
of degree `m`, then every homogeneous relation `Σ cᵢ Fᵢ = 0` is generated by relations of degree `≤ 1`.

Proof. Write each coefficient `cᵢ` (of degree `d ≥ 1`) as `Σ_j c_{ij} x_j` (`exists_eq_sum_X_mul`) and each `x_j Fᵢ` as a
combination of the `F`s (`exists_eq_sum_mul_of_span`), which gives linear relations; the remaining relation
`Σ_j (Σ_i c_{ij} Fᵢ) x_j = 0` is handled by the ported Koszul lemma
`MvPolynomial.exists_skew_eq_sum_mul_X_of_sum_mul_X_eq_zero` and induction on `d` (`sum_sum_eq_zero_of_alternating`,
`main`).
