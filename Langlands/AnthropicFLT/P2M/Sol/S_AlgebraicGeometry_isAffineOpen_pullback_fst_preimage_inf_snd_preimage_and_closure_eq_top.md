## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_isAffineOpen_pullback_fst_preimage_inf_snd_preimage_and_closure_eq_top.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_isAffineOpen_pullback_fst_preimage_inf_snd_preimage_and_closure_eq_top`. Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.isAffineOpen_pullback_fst_preimage_inf_snd_preimage_and_closure_eq_top`: for affine `S` and affine
opens `U ⊆ X`, `V ⊆ Y`, the open `W = pr₁⁻¹ U ∩ pr₂⁻¹ V` of `X ×_S Y` is affine, and its ring of sections is generated
as a ring by the restrictions of the pulled-back sections of `U` and of `V`.

Proof. Affineness is the ported `isAffineOpen_pullback_fst_preimage_inf_snd_preimage`. `W` is identified with
`U ×_S V` (local `fromSpec_appLE_eq`, `appLE_congrHom`), whose global sections are generated by the two factors by
the ported `closure_range_pullback_fst_appTop_union_range_snd_appTop_eq_top`; generation is transported along the
ring isomorphism (local `closure_union_range_eq_top_of_injective`).
