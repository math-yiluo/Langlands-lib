## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_ProjSpace_locallyOfFinitePresentation_comp_of_fg_of_ideal_basicOpen_eq_span.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_ProjSpace_locallyOfFinitePresentation_comp_of_fg_of_ideal_basicOpen_eq_span`. Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.ProjSpace.locallyOfFinitePresentation_comp_of_fg_of_ideal_basicOpen_eq_span`: for a
finitely generated homogeneous ideal `I` of `A[X₀, …, Xₙ]` and the ideal sheaf `𝓘` on `ℙⁿ_A` with chart
ideals generated by its dehomogenizations, the closed subscheme of `𝓘` is locally of finite presentation
over `Spec A`.

Proof. Local finite presentation is checked on the charts. The chart ring `A[X]_{(Xᵢ)}` is a finitely
presented `A`-algebra (a polynomial ring in the ratios, via the ported
`ProjSpace.exists_algHom_away_apply_ratio_eq`), and the chart ideal of `𝓘` is generated by the
dehomogenizations of the finitely many homogeneous components of generators of `I`; a quotient of a
finitely presented algebra by a finitely generated ideal is finitely presented.
