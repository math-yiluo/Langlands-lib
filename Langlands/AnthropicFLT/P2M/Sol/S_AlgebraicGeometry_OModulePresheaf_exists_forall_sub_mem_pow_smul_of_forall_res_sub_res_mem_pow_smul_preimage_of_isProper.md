## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_OModulePresheaf_exists_forall_sub_mem_pow_smul_of_forall_res_sub_res_mem_pow_smul_preimage_of_isProper.solution`
proves the statement of the companion theorem in
`Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_OModulePresheaf_exists_forall_sub_mem_pow_smul_of_forall_res_sub_res_mem_pow_smul_preimage_of_isProper`.

Proof. Namespace `AlgebraicGeometry.OModulePresheaf.Rebase` (inside the solution namespace) is the same
rebasing over an affine open `W` as in the solution of
`exists_forall_eq_sum_smul_of_forall_mem_pow_smul_preimage_of_isProper` (proper `qW : p⁻¹(W) ⟶ Spec Γ(P, W)`,
presheaf `GW`, cover `KW`, the identification `e0`/`e0inv` of `0`-cochains with chart families, and the
sections `Powers` and `Deg1`). In `Deg1`, `d0_apply_eq_neg_res` and `d0_mem_pow_smul_top_of_forall` show that the
Čech differential of `e0inv t` lies in `I^m • ⊤` when all overlap differences of `t` do.

`solution`: over the noetherian ring `Γ(P, W)` with the extended ideal `J`, the ported theorem
`exists_d_eq_zero_sub_mem_pow_of_d_mem_pow_of_isProper` (for a coherent sheaf under a proper morphism, a
`0`-cochain whose differential lies in `J^(n+c) • ⊤` is congruent modulo `J^n • ⊤` to a cocycle) gives `c`. For a
family `t` with overlap differences in `I^(n+c) • ⊤`, the cochain `x = e0inv t` has differential in
`J^(n+c) • ⊤`, so there is a cocycle `a` with `x - a ∈ J^n • ⊤`; then `e0 a` agrees on the overlaps
(`d0_eq_zero_iff`) and `t - e0 a = e0 (x - a)` has components in `I^n • ⊤` (`e0_mem_pow_smul_top`).
