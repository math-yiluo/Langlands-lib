## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_ProjSpace_exists_idealSheafData_ideal_basicOpen_eq_span.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_ProjSpace_exists_idealSheafData_ideal_basicOpen_eq_span`. Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.ProjSpace.exists_idealSheafData_ideal_basicOpen_eq_span`: for every ideal `I` of
`A[X₀, …, Xₙ]` there is an ideal sheaf `𝓘` on `ℙⁿ_A` whose ideal on each standard chart `D₊(Xᵢ)` is
generated by the dehomogenizations `F / Xᵢ^d` of the forms `F ∈ I` (the closed subscheme `V₊(I)`).

Proof (namespace `AlgebraicGeometry.ProjSpace.K0`). On the chart ring `A[X]_{(f)}` of a linear form `f`
let `chartIdeal` be generated by the dehomogenizations of forms of `I` (`gens`). On an overlap
`D₊(fg)` the two chart ideals generate the same ideal (`map_chartIdeal_le`, `map_chartIdeal_eq`, using
the unit `overlapUnit`), so the ideal sheaves `chartSheaf` on the affine charts agree on overlaps
(`chartSheaf_comap_specMap`, `compat`). They glue to an ideal sheaf on `ℙⁿ_A` (`main`, using
`bijective_appLE` and `ideal_eq_of_map_appLE` to compare with the sections of `Proj`).
