## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_ProjSpace_exists_linearForm_section_maximal_growth.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_ProjSpace_exists_linearForm_section_maximal_growth`. Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.ProjSpace.exists_linearForm_section_maximal_growth`: let `k` be an infinite field,
`m ≥ 1`, `J` an ideal generated by forms of degree `m` with maximal growth
`dim piece J (m + 1) = macaulayPow m (dim piece J m)`, and `ιk : Z_k ⟶ ℙⁿ_k` a closed immersion whose
forms of degree `≥ m` vanishing on it are those of `J`. Then there is a linear form
`ℓ = ∑ aⱼ Xⱼ` acting injectively on all charts of `Z_k` such that, for the hyperplane section
`ι' : Z' ⟶ ℙⁿ_k` with ideal `ker ιk ⊔ (ℓ)`, the ideal `J'` generated by the degree-`m` forms of `J + (ℓ)`
again has maximal growth, its forms of degree `≥ m` are exactly those vanishing on `Z'`, and
`dim piece J' m < dim piece J m` when the latter is positive.

Proof. The ported Gotzmann-type results give the linear form: by
`MvPolynomial.exists_forall_eval_ne_zero_mem_of_mul_mem_of_finrank_piece_succ_eq_macaulayPow` and
`MvPolynomial.exists_forall_eval_ne_zero_mem_of_mul_mem_and_finrank_piece_sup_eq_macaulayPow` (over an
infinite field a nonzero form `G` with `G(a) ≠ 0` exists), `ℓ` is a non-zero-divisor on `J` in degrees
`≥ m`; hence on the charts of `Z_k` (ported
`ProjSpace.app_awayToSection_linearForm_mul_eq_zero_imp_of_forall_mul_mem_imp`). The growth and the
dimension drop of `J'` are `MvPolynomial.finrank_piece_span_sup_linearForm_eq_macaulayPow_and_lt`, and
the description of the forms vanishing on `Z'` is
`ProjSpace.mem_span_sup_linearForm_iff_forall_app_eq_zero_of_maximal_growth`.
