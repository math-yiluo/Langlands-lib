Proof (namespace `AlgebraicGeometry.OModulePresheaf.C2Head` inside the solution namespace, in three blocks).
Write `GP` for the Čech pushforward of `G'`; it is coherent and quasi-coherent by the ported theorems
`isCoherent_cechPushforward_of_isProper` and `isQuasicoherent_cechPushforward_of_isSeparated`.

Truncations (section `Trunc`). `smulHom H a : H^m → H` is `x ↦ ∑ a l • x l`, with range `span(a) • ⊤`
(`range_smulHom_app`); since `A` is noetherian, `I^(k+1)` is generated by finitely many `gen I k`. `trunc I H k`
is the cokernel presheaf of `smulHom H (gen I k)` (Library construction `coker`), i.e. `H / I^(k+1) H`, with
projection `truncProj` (surjective, kernel `I^(k+1) • ⊤`) and transition maps `truncTrans` (surjective, kernel
`I^(k+1) • ⊤`); it is coherent and quasi-coherent with `H` (`isCoherent_coker`, `isQuasicoherent_coker`,
`isQuasicoherent_pow`). The system is `Ps k := trunc I GP k` with `π := truncTrans`, `ψP := truncProj`.

The maps `ν` (section `Nu`). The Čech pushforward of `ψ' k` kills `I^(k+1) GP`, so it descends to
`nu k : trunc I GP k → cechPushforward (F' k)` (`nu_comp_truncProj`), compatible with `φ'` (`nu_compat`). The
shift bound `nu_shift` comes from the ported theorem
`exists_forall_eq_sum_smul_of_forall_mem_pow_smul_preimage_of_isProper`: a Čech section killed by
`ψ' (k+c)` has components in `I^(k+1+c) • ⊤`, hence is a sum of `I^(k+1)`-multiples of Čech sections.

The maps `u` (sections `Threads`, `Adapted`, `U`, `Defu`, `Algebra`, `Props`). Every element of `F k (U)` lies in
a thread of the `φ` (`exists_isThread`), unique modulo `I^(k+1)` at level `k` (`IsThread.sub_mem`). A Čech
section `a` of `GP` is adapted to a level-`L` value `z` if `ψ' L` maps `a` to `v L z`. For every thread `s` and
level `L` an adapted section exists (`exists_adapted`): the chart components of `v L (s L)` are lifted through
the surjective `ψ' L` and corrected into a compatible family with the ported theorem
`exists_forall_sub_mem_pow_smul_of_forall_res_sub_res_mem_pow_smul_preimage_of_isProper`. `Good U k L` says that
sections killed by `ψ' L` lie in `I^(k+1) • ⊤`; it holds for `L = k + c` (`good_exists`, again from
`exists_forall_eq_sum_smul_of_forall_mem_pow_smul_preimage_of_isProper`). For `y ∈ F k (U)`, `uFun U k y` is the
class in `trunc I GP k` of a section adapted at the good level `lvl U k` to a thread through `y`; it does not
depend on the choices (`wit_unique`, `wit_compare`, `uFun_eq`), so it is additive, linear over `A` and
`Γ(P, U)`, compatible with restriction and with the transition maps (`uFun_add` … `uFun_compat`), and satisfies
`nu k (uFun y) = v k y` (`uFun_pin`). This defines `uHom k : AffHom (F k) (Ps k)` with `uHom_compat` and
`uHom_pin`. `solution` assembles all of this.
