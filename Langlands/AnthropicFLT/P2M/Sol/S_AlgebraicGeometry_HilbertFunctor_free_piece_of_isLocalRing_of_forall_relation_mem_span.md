## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_HilbertFunctor_free_piece_of_isLocalRing_of_forall_relation_mem_span.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_HilbertFunctor_free_piece_of_isLocalRing_of_forall_relation_mem_span`. Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.HilbertFunctor.free_piece_of_isLocalRing_of_forall_relation_mem_span`: let `A` be a
local ring and `F₁, …, F_g` forms of degree `m` in `A[X₀, …, Xₙ]` such that the pieces of `(F)` in
degrees `m` and `m + 1` are projective, and over the residue field every relation among the reductions
of the `Fᵢ` is generated by relations of degree at most `1`. Then every piece of `(F)` is free.

Proof (namespace `GotzLocalLift`). Sections `Pieces` and `SpanF`: graded calculus for ideals generated
by forms of degree `m` (homogeneous components of `∑ aᵢ Fᵢ`, `exists_eq_sum_of_mem`, vanishing below
`m`). Section `Local`: `InM p` (all coefficients in the maximal ideal, i.e. `p` reduces to `0`), with
closure properties; `Lifts F d`: every combination `∑ aᵢ Fᵢ` with coefficients of degree `d` lying
in `𝔪` can be rewritten with coefficients in `𝔪`. Projectivity in degree `m + d` gives `Lifts F d`
(`lifts_of_projective`, for `d = 0, 1`); the hypothesis on residue-field relations propagates lifting
to all `d` (`lifts_of_forall_relation`, by induction using that relations are generated in degrees
`≤ 1`). With lifting, the presentation `presMap` of the degree-`(m + d)` part has a kernel compatible
with the residue field, so the piece is free (`free_piece_add`); below `m` the pieces are the free
modules of all forms (`free_piece_of_lt`).
