## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_GoodReductionJacobian_RelativeGroupLaw_exists_isOpenImmersion_irreducibleSpace_range_eq_connectedComponent_finiteIndex.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_GoodReductionJacobian_RelativeGroupLaw_exists_isOpenImmersion_irreducibleSpace_range_eq_connectedComponent_finiteIndex`. Statement. `GoodReductionJacobian.RelativeGroupLaw.exists_isOpenImmersion_irreducibleSpace_range_eq_connectedComponent_finiteIndex`:
the same statement over an algebraically closed field `k`, without the geometric clauses: there is an open and
closed irreducible subgroup scheme `G₀` equal to the connected component of the unit, commutative if `G` is, with
finitely many cosets of `G₀(k)` covering `G(k)`.

Proof. By the ported `RelativeGroupLaw.eq_of_mem_irreducibleComponents_of_apply_closedPoint_mem` there is a unique
irreducible component `idComp` through the unit. Since `G` is noetherian it has finitely many irreducible
components (`finite_irreducibleComponents`); different components through `k`-points are translates and do not
meet `idComp` (`disjoint_idComp_of_ne`, `exists_clpt_mem_of_isClosed`), so `idComp` is clopen
(`compl_idComp_eq`, `isClopen_idComp`) and equals the connected component of the unit. Section `Mul`: with reduced
structures (ported `Scheme.IdealSheafData.isReduced_subscheme_vanishingIdeal_and_le_ker`) and geometric
integrality (ported `geometricallyIntegral_of_isAlgClosed`), the image of `idComp × idComp` under multiplication is
irreducible and contains the unit, hence lies in `idComp`; similarly for the inverse. Section `Law` restricts the group
law to the open subscheme `G₀` (`law₀`, `hom_ι₀`, `isCommutative_law₀`), and section `FiniteIndex` bounds the
cosets by the finitely many components.
