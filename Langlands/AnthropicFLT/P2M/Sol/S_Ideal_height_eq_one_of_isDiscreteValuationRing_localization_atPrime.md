## Content

Proof. The discrete valuation ring `R_p` is not a field and is a principal ideal ring, so it has Krull
dimension `1` (`IsPrincipalIdealRing.ringKrullDim_eq_one`), and the dimension of `R_p` is the
height of `p` (`IsLocalization.AtPrime.ringKrullDim_eq_height`).
