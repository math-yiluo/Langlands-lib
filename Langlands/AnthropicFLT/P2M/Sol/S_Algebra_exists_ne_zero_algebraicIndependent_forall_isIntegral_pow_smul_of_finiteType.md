## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_Algebra_exists_ne_zero_algebraicIndependent_forall_isIntegral_pow_smul_of_finiteType.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_Algebra_exists_ne_zero_algebraicIndependent_forall_isIntegral_pow_smul_of_finiteType`. Statement. `Algebra.exists_ne_zero_algebraicIndependent_forall_isIntegral_pow_smul_of_finiteType` (generic
Noether normalization): let `R → C` be an injective map of domains with `C` of finite type over `R`.
Then there are `r ∈ R`, `r ≠ 0`, and elements `z₁, …, z_d ∈ C` algebraically independent over `R`
such that for every `c ∈ C` some `rⁿ c` is integral over `R[z₁, …, z_d]`.

Proof (namespace `P2mGenericNoether`, theorem `main`). With `K = Frac R` and `L = Frac C`, the
`K`-subalgebra of `L` generated by generators `s` of `C` is finite and injective over a polynomial ring
`K[Y₁, …, Y_d]` (Noether normalization, `exists_finite_inj_algHom_of_fg`). Clearing denominators
(`exists_mul_eq_of_mem_adjoin`) the images of the `Yᵢ` become `zᵢ ∈ C` after multiplying by nonzero
`ρᵢ ∈ R`; the rescaling automorphism `scale` of `K[Y]` (`scale_comp_scale_inv`,
`scale_injective`) shows that the `zᵢ` are algebraically independent. Each `c ∈ C` satisfies a monic
equation over the image of `K[Y]`; clearing the denominators of its coefficients and scaling the
roots (`scaleRoots`) gives `τ_c ≠ 0` with `τ_c c` integral over `R[z]`. Take `r = ∏_{c ∈ s} τ_c`; the
elements `rⁿ c` are then integral for all `c`, since integral elements form a subalgebra.
