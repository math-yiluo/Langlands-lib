# The integral step of the finiteness theorem, via Chow’s lemma and the Leray spectral sequence (proof)

## Statement

`solution`: let `R` be a noetherian ring, `πZ : Z ⟶ Spec R` proper with `Z` integral, and `K` an ordered
affine cover of `Z`. Assume the induction hypothesis `ih`: every coherent, quasi-coherent presheaf of modules
over `πZ` supported in a closed subset `Y' < ⊤` has `CechFinite K`. Then the structure sheaf
`OModulePresheaf.unit πZ` has `CechFinite K`. The theorem module
`Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_OModulePresheaf_cechFinite_unit_of_isIntegral_of_ih` states this as
`AlgebraicGeometry.OModulePresheaf.cechFinite_unit_of_isIntegral_of_ih` and proves it from `solution`.

## Proof outline

The proof is in the namespace `…AlgebraicGeometry.OModulePresheaf.Leray`.

1. **Chow's lemma.** `ChowDatum.nonempty` and `ChowDatumProj.nonempty_of` (from the corresponding theorem
   modules) give a Chow datum `D : ChowDatumProj πZ`: a proper `D.p : D.V' ⟶ Z` with `D.V'` closed in a
   projective space `ℙ^{D.Nd}_R`, and a dense open `D.U` over which `D.p` is an isomorphism. Its complement is
   a proper closed subset (`chowCompl_lt_top`, using density).
2. **`D.p_* 𝒪` has finite cohomology** (`cechFinite_relHPresheaf_zero_chow`). Let `K'` be the pullback to
   `D.V'` of the standard cover of projective space, and consider the Čech–Leray bicomplex
   `LerayDblCpx D.p πZ K K'` with its convergence data from `DoubleComplex.boundedSpectralSequence`. - The
   total cohomology is the Čech cohomology of `𝒪_{D.V'}` (`OModulePresheaf.Leray.nonempty_HTot_equiv`), which
   is finitely generated by `OModulePresheaf.cechFinite_unit_of_isClosedImmersion_proj` (`D.V'` is a closed
   subscheme of projective space). - The `E₂` terms are the Čech cohomology for `K` of the relative cohomology
   presheaves (`OModulePresheaf.Leray.nonempty_E2I_equiv`). For `b ≥ 1` these presheaves are coherent,
   quasi-coherent and supported off `D.U` (`isCoherent_relHPresheaf_chow`,
   `isQuasicoherent_relHPresheaf_chow`, `supportedIn_relHPresheaf_chow`), so by `ih` the rows `b ≥ 1` of `E₂`
   are finitely generated. - `DoubleComplex.Convergence.finite_E2_q0` then gives finiteness of the bottom row
   `E₂ a 0` (trivially beyond the bound), which is the Čech cohomology of `relHPresheaf … 0`.
3. **Back to `𝒪_Z`** (`cechFinite_unit_of_isIntegral_of_ih'`). `OModulePresheaf.Leray.exists_chowSES` gives a
   short exact sequence `0 → 𝒪_Z → relHPresheaf … 0 → Q → 0` with `Q` coherent, quasi-coherent and supported
   off `D.U`, so `Q` has finite cohomology by `ih`. `OModulePresheaf.cechFinite_of_affSES_left` concludes
   finiteness for `𝒪_Z` from the middle and right terms.
