## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_HilbertFunctor_exists_point_I_eq_span_of_forall_surjective_of_forall_projective.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_HilbertFunctor_exists_point_I_eq_span_of_forall_surjective_of_forall_projective`. Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.HilbertFunctor.exists_point_I_eq_span_of_forall_surjective_of_forall_projective`:
let `h d = C(n + d, n)` for `d < m`, and let `Θ_d : (forms of degree d over A) → N_d` be `A`-linear
maps, surjective for `d ≥ m`, onto finite projective modules of constant rank `h d`, such that
`Θ_d F = 0` implies `Θ_{e+d}(G F) = 0` for every form `G` of degree `e`. Then the ideal generated by
the forms `F` of degree `d ≥ m` with `Θ_d F = 0` is the ideal of a point of the Hilbert functor with
Hilbert function `h`.

Proof (namespace `K4DSOL`). Let `V` be the set of such forms and `I = (V)`, a homogeneous ideal. Lemma
`exists_finset_sum_mul_eq`: a form of degree `d` in the span of a set of forms is a finite sum
`∑ c_G G` with homogeneous coefficients of complementary degrees. Using it and the multiplicativity
hypothesis, a form of degree `d ≥ m` lies in `I` iff `Θ_d F = 0`, and `I` has no nonzero forms of
degree `< m`. Hence `piece I d ≅ N_d` for `d ≥ m` (surjectivity of `Θ_d`) and `piece I d` is the full
degree-`d` part, free of rank `C(n + d, n)`, for `d < m` (`finrank_homogeneousSubmodule`). These give
the finiteness, projectivity and rank fields of the point.
