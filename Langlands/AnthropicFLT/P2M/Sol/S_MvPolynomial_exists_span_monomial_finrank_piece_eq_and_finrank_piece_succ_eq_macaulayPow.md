## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_MvPolynomial_exists_span_monomial_finrank_piece_eq_and_finrank_piece_succ_eq_macaulayPow.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_MvPolynomial_exists_span_monomial_finrank_piece_eq_and_finrank_piece_succ_eq_macaulayPow`. Statement. `MvPolynomial.exists_span_monomial_finrank_piece_eq_and_finrank_piece_succ_eq_macaulayPow` (lex-segment ideals): for
`d ≥ 1` and `a ≤ C(n + d, n)`, there is an ideal of `K[x₀, …, x_n]` generated by monomials of degree `d` with
`h_J(d) = a` and `h_J(d + 1) = a^⟨d⟩`.

Proof. Let `B` be the set of `a` monomials of degree `d` that are last in the lexicographic order, built
recursively from the Macaulay representation of `a` (`top`, `rem`; `BW`, `shift`, `MonW`); let `J` be generated by the
other degree-`d` monomials. The degree-`(d + 1)` monomials outside `J` are those all of whose divisions lie in `B`
(`Bplus`, `Bplus_union_shift`), and counting them (`card_Bplus_BW`, `card_MonW`) gives `a^⟨d⟩`; dimensions of pieces
of monomial ideals are counts of monomials (`finrank_piece_eq_card`, `exists_ideal`).
