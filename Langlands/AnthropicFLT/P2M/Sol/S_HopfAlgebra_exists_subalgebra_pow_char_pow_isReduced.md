## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_HopfAlgebra_exists_subalgebra_pow_char_pow_isReduced.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_HopfAlgebra_exists_subalgebra_pow_char_pow_isReduced`.

Statement. Let `k` be a perfect field of characteristic `p > 0`, `H` a commutative Hopf algebra over `k` and `K` a
finitely generated `k`-subalgebra of `H` that is closed under the antipode and whose comultiplication lands in the
`k`-span of `{a ⊗ b | a, b ∈ K}`. Then for some `r`, the set `K' = {x ^ p ^ r | x ∈ K}` is a finitely generated,
reduced `k`-subalgebra of `K` with the same two closure properties.

Proof (a single theorem). `K` is noetherian, so its nilradical is nilpotent, say of exponent `m`; choose `r` with
`m ≤ p ^ r`. The map `x ↦ x ^ p ^ r` is additive on `H` and on `H ⊗[k] H` (characteristic `p`), multiplicative, and
`k` is closed under `p ^ r`-th roots (perfectness), so `K'` is a subalgebra; it is generated by the `p ^ r`-th powers
of generators of `K`. If `y = x ^ p ^ r ∈ K'` is nilpotent then `x` is nilpotent in `K`, so `x ^ m = 0` and `y = 0`.
Comultiplication and antipode are ring maps, so they commute with `p ^ r`-th powers, and the `p ^ r`-th power of an
element of the span of `{a ⊗ b | a, b ∈ K}` lies in the span of `{a' ⊗ b' | a', b' ∈ K'}`.
