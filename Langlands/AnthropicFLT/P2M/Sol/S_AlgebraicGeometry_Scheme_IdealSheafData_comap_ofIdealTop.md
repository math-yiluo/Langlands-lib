## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_Scheme_IdealSheafData_comap_ofIdealTop.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_Scheme_IdealSheafData_comap_ofIdealTop`. Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IdealSheafData.comap_ofIdealTop`: for an ideal `I` of the global sections of
`Y` and `f : X ⟶ Y`, the pullback along `f` of the ideal sheaf generated by `I` is the ideal sheaf
generated by the image of `I` in `Γ(X, ⊤)`.

Proof. Ideal sheaves are compared on affine opens; on an affine open `V` of `X` mapping into an affine
open of `Y`, both ideals are generated by the images of `I` (ported
`Scheme.IdealSheafData.ideal_comap_of_le`, which computes the pullback ideal on affine opens).
