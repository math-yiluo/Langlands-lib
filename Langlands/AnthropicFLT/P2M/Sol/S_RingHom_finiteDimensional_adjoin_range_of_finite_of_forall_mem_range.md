## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_RingHom_finiteDimensional_adjoin_range_of_finite_of_forall_mem_range.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_RingHom_finiteDimensional_adjoin_range_of_finite_of_forall_mem_range`. Statement. `RingHom.finiteDimensional_adjoin_range_of_finite_of_forall_mem_range`: let `S` be a finite `R`-algebra and
`χ' : S → K` a ring map to a field extension of `E` such that the images of `R` lie in `E`. Then the subfield of `K`
generated over `E` by the image of `χ'` is finite-dimensional over `E`.

Proof. Every element of `S` is integral over `R` (finite algebra), so its image is integral over `E` (local
`isIntegral_apply`); the image of `S` is generated by the images of finitely many module generators, and a field
generated by finitely many algebraic elements is finite-dimensional.
