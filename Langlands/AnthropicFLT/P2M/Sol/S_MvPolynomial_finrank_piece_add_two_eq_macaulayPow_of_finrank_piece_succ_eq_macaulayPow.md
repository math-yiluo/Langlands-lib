## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_MvPolynomial_finrank_piece_add_two_eq_macaulayPow_of_finrank_piece_succ_eq_macaulayPow.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_MvPolynomial_finrank_piece_add_two_eq_macaulayPow_of_finrank_piece_succ_eq_macaulayPow`. Statement. `MvPolynomial.finrank_piece_add_two_eq_macaulayPow_of_finrank_piece_succ_eq_macaulayPow` (Gotzmann persistence, one
step): if `J` is generated by forms of degree `m` and has maximal growth `h_J(m + 1) = h_J(m)^⟨m⟩`, then
`h_J(m + 2) = h_J(m + 1)^⟨m + 1⟩`.

Proof. Section `BaseChange`: dimensions of pieces are unchanged by field extension (ported
`HilbertFunctor.exists_linearEquiv_baseChange_piece_map`), so `K` may be assumed infinite. Section `Regen`: replace `J`
in degree `m + 1` by the ideal generated by its degree-`(m + 1)` part (`regen`, `Ik_regen_eq`). Section `Core`: induction
on the number of variables. A generic linear form `ℓ` is a nonzerodivisor modulo `J` in degrees `≥ m` (ported
`MvPolynomial.exists_forall_eval_ne_zero_mem_of_mul_mem_of_finrank_piece_succ_eq_macaulayPow`), so
`h_J(k + 1) = h_J(k) + h_{J+(ℓ)}(k + 1)`; `J + (ℓ)` lives in one variable fewer and has maximal growth by the ported
`MvPolynomial.exists_forall_eval_ne_zero_macaulayPow_finrank_piece_sup_add_le` with Macaulay's bound (ported
`MvPolynomial.finrank_piece_succ_le_macaulayPow`); the induction hypothesis and Macaulay arithmetic
(`macaulayPow_macaulayPow`, `low_macaulayPow`, `lt_of_macaulayPow_add_le`) give the claim.
