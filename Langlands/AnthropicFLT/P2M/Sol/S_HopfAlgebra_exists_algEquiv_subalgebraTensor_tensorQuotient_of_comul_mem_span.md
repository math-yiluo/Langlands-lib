## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_HopfAlgebra_exists_algEquiv_subalgebraTensor_tensorQuotient_of_comul_mem_span.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_HopfAlgebra_exists_algEquiv_subalgebraTensor_tensorQuotient_of_comul_mem_span`.

Statement. Let `H` be a commutative Hopf algebra over a commutative ring `k` and `K` a `k`-subalgebra of `H` whose
comultiplication lands in the `k`-span of the pure tensors `a ⊗ b` with `a, b ∈ K`. Let `J` be the ideal of `H`
generated by the elements of `K` with counit `0`. Then there is an `H`-algebra isomorphism
`β : H ⊗[K] H ≃ₐ[H] H ⊗[k] (H ⧸ J)` with `β (h₁ ⊗ h₂) = (h₁ ⊗ 1) * (id ⊗ π) (Δ h₂)`, where `π : H → H ⧸ J` is the
quotient map. Geometrically, for `G = Spec H` and `N = Spec (H ⧸ J)` this is the torsor isomorphism
`G × N ≅ G ×_{Spec K} G`.

Proof (namespace `S17Torsor`). `θ = id ⊗ π : H ⊗[k] H → H ⊗[k] (H ⧸ J)` sends `Δ a` to `a ⊗ 1` for `a ∈ K`
(`θ_comul_of_mem`, from the span hypothesis and the counit axiom), so `h ↦ θ (Δ h)` is a `K`-algebra map `g` and
`β` is the `H`-algebra map out of `H ⊗[K] H` given by the algebra map on the left factor and `g` on the right. The
inverse `δ` is `x ⊗ π c ↦ x • ψ₀ c`, with `ψ₀ c = Σ S(c₁) ⊗ c₂` taken in `H ⊗[K] H` (`ψ₀`, `mapK`, `Sₐ`); `ψ₀`
vanishes on the generators of `J` (`ψ₀_eq_zero_of_mem`), so it factors through `H ⧸ J` (`ψ`). The two Sweedler
identities `sweedler₁` and `sweedler₂` (coassociativity together with the antipode axioms) give
`δ ∘ β = id` (`δ_comp_β`) and `β ∘ δ = id` (`β_comp_δ`), and `βEquiv` is the resulting equivalence.
