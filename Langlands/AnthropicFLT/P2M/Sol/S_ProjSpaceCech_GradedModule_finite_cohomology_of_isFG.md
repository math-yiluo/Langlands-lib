# Finitely generated graded modules have finitely generated Čech cohomology (proof)

Module `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.P2M.Sol.S_ProjSpaceCech_GradedModule_finite_cohomology_of_isFG`. Its
declarations live in the namespace `P2MW.S_ProjSpaceCech_GradedModule_finite_cohomology_of_isFG`.

## Statement

`solution`: let `R` be a noetherian ring and `D : ProjSpaceCech.GradedModule R n` a graded module over
`S = R[X₀, …, Xₙ]` that has a presentation (`ProjSpaceCech.GradedModule.IsFG D`,
`Def_AlgebraicGeometry_ProjSpaceCechGradedModule`). Then for every `i` the graded Čech cohomology
`ProjSpaceCech.GradedModule.H D i` is a finitely generated `R`-module. The theorem module
`Theorems.Thm_ProjSpaceCech_GradedModule_finite_cohomology_of_isFG` states this as
`ProjSpaceCech.GradedModule.finite_cohomology_of_isFG` and proves it from `solution`.

## Proof outline

The proof is `GradedModule.finite_cohomology_of_isFG'` in the namespace `…ProjSpaceCech`. It proves the claim
for all finitely generated `D'` at once, by decreasing induction on `i` (`Nat.strong_decreasing_induction`).

1. **Base.** For `i > n` the cohomology is trivial
   (`ProjSpaceCech.GradedModule.subsingleton_cohomology_of_lt`), hence finitely generated.
2. **Step.** Assume the claim in all degrees above `i`, and let `σ` be a presentation of `D'`, with free part
   `σ.F = ⊕ₖ S(σ.d₀ k)` and kernel `σ.ker`. `H^i(σ.F)` is finitely generated by
   `ProjSpaceCech.GradedModule.finite_cohomology_pi_FD`. The kernel is again finitely generated by
   `ProjSpaceCech.GradedModule.Presentation.ker_isFG` (Hilbert's basis theorem), so `H^{i+1}(σ.ker)` is
   finitely generated by the induction hypothesis. `ProjSpaceCech.GradedModule.Presentation.finite_H_of_ses`
   (the long exact sequence of `0 → σ.ker → σ.F → D' → 0`) then shows `H^i(D')` is finitely generated.
