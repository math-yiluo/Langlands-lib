## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_ProjSpace_forall_subsingleton_HSucc_twist_and_forall_H0_exists_of_maximal_growth.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_ProjSpace_forall_subsingleton_HSucc_twist_and_forall_H0_exists_of_maximal_growth`. Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.ProjSpace.forall_subsingleton_HSucc_twist_and_forall_H0_exists_of_maximal_growth`
(Gotzmann regularity over an infinite field): let `J` be generated by forms of degree `m ≥ 1` with
maximal growth from `m` to `m + 1`, and `Z_k ⊆ ℙⁿ_k` a closed subscheme whose forms of degree `≥ m` are
those of `J`. Then for all `d ≥ m` the higher Čech cohomology of `𝒪_{Z_k}(d)` vanishes and every `H⁰`
class of `𝒪_{Z_k}(d)` is given by a form of degree `d`.

Proof, by induction on `dim piece J m` (the case `0` is the ported
`ProjSpace.forall_subsingleton_HSucc_twist_and_forall_H0_exists_of_finrank_piece_eq_zero`). Otherwise the
ported `ProjSpace.exists_linearForm_section_maximal_growth` gives a general linear form `ℓ` and a
hyperplane section `Z'` (ideal sheaf from `ProjSpace.exists_idealSheafData_ideal_basicOpen_eq_span`)
whose ideal `J'` has maximal growth and smaller dimension, so the induction hypothesis applies to `Z'`.
The hyperplane sequence `0 → 𝒪_Z(d) → 𝒪_Z(d + 1) → 𝒪_{Z'}(d + 1) → 0` (ported
`ProjSpace.exists_affSES_twist_succ_of_forall_mul_eq_zero_imp`) and the long exact sequence (ported
`OModulePresheaf.AffSES.injective_inc_HSuccMap_of_forall_subsingleton_HSucc_of_surjective_proj_H0Map`,
`stepA`) show that `Hⁱ⁺¹(𝒪_Z(d)) → Hⁱ⁺¹(𝒪_Z(d + 1))` is injective for `d ≥ m`; by Serre vanishing (ported
`ProjSpace.exists_forall_subsingleton_HSucc_twist`) the higher cohomology vanishes for `d ≥ m`. The
statement on `H⁰` then follows from the ported
`ProjSpace.forall_H0_twist_exists_of_forall_subsingleton_HSucc_of_maximal_growth` (`exists_H0_of_form`,
`subsingleton_of_tower`, `res_top_mem_H0`).
