## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_Algebra_exists_monic_irreducible_map_algebraicClosure_hypersurfaceModel_of_forall_isSeparable.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_Algebra_exists_monic_irreducible_map_algebraicClosure_hypersurfaceModel_of_forall_isSeparable`. Statement. `Algebra.exists_monic_irreducible_map_algebraicClosure_hypersurfaceModel_of_forall_isSeparable`: let
`k` be a field, `R` a domain of finite type over `k`, and `C` a domain of finite type over `R` with
`R → C` injective, such that the subfield of `Frac C` generated by `k` and `R` is separably closed in
`Frac C`. Then there are `r ∈ R`, `r ≠ 0`, elements `z₁, …, z_d, w ∈ C`, a monic polynomial
`F ∈ R[Y₁, …, Y_d][T]` and `g ∈ R[Y₁, …, Y_d]`, `g ≠ 0`, such that: the `zᵢ` are algebraically
independent over `R`; `F(z, w) = 0`; `F` stays irreducible over the algebraic closure of `Frac R`;
for every `c ∈ C` some power of `g(z)ᵐ c` lies in `R[z, w]`; and for every `c ∈ C` some `rⁿ c` is
integral over `R[z]`. (A generic hypersurface model of `Spec C` over `Spec R`.)

Proof (namespace `P2mHypModel`, theorem `main`). Auxiliary lemmas: separability of `t^{qⁿ}` for
large `n` (`exists_isSeparable_pow`, `isSeparable_pow_add`); the fraction field of `R` is separably
closed in `Frac C` (`coe_adjoin_range_eq_range`, `forall_isSeparable_mem_range`); clearing
denominators of polynomials (`exists_ne_zero_map_eq_C_mul`). Main argument: the ported generic
Noether normalization `Algebra.exists_ne_zero_algebraicIndependent_forall_isIntegral_pow_smul_of_finiteType`
gives `r ≠ 0` and algebraically independent `z` with `rⁿ c` integral over `R[z]`. Over
`M = Frac (K[z])` (`K = Frac R`), `Frac C` is finite; the `q^e`-th powers of generators of `C` are
separable, and a primitive element `α` of the field they generate is written, after clearing
denominators, as `w₀ ∈ C`; scaling the minimal polynomial of `w₀` (`scaleRoots`) gives a monic `F` over
`R[Y]` with root `w = ρ w₀`. `F` is irreducible over the algebraic closure of `K` by the ported
theorem `Polynomial.irreducible_map_map_algebraicClosure_of_separable_of_forall_isSeparable_mem_range`.
A discriminant argument for the power basis of `M(w)` gives `g` with the stated membership.
