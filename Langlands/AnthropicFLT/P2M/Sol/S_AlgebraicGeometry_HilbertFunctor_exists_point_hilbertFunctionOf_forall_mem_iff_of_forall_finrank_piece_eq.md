## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_HilbertFunctor_exists_point_hilbertFunctionOf_forall_mem_iff_of_forall_finrank_piece_eq.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_HilbertFunctor_exists_point_hilbertFunctionOf_forall_mem_iff_of_forall_finrank_piece_eq`. Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.HilbertFunctor.exists_point_hilbertFunctionOf_forall_mem_iff_of_forall_finrank_piece_eq`:
over a field `k`, if a homogeneous ideal `I` satisfies `dim piece I d = P(d)` for all `d ≥ m`, there
is a point `q` with Hilbert function `hilbertFunctionOf n P m` whose forms of degree `≥ m` are those
of `I`.

Proof (namespace `P2mWs30PointPackaging`). Let `J` be the ideal generated by the forms of degree
`≥ m` in `I`: it is homogeneous, has the same forms as `I` in degrees `≥ m`, and has no nonzero forms
of degree `< m` (`homogeneousComponent_mul_of_lt`). So `dim piece J d` is `P(d)` for `d ≥ m` and
`C(n + d, n)` for `d < m` (`finrank_homogeneousSubmodule`), which is `hilbertFunctionOf n P m d`; over
a field the pieces are finite and projective, so `J` is a point.
