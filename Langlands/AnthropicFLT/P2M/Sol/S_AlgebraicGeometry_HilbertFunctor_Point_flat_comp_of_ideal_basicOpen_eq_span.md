## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_AlgebraicGeometry_HilbertFunctor_Point_flat_comp_of_ideal_basicOpen_eq_span.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_AlgebraicGeometry_HilbertFunctor_Point_flat_comp_of_ideal_basicOpen_eq_span`. Statement. `AlgebraicGeometry.HilbertFunctor.Point.flat_comp_of_ideal_basicOpen_eq_span`: let `p` be a point of
the Hilbert functor over `A` (`Point A n h`) and `𝓘` an ideal sheaf on `ℙⁿ_A` whose ideal on each
standard chart `D₊(Xᵢ)` is generated by the dehomogenizations `F / Xᵢ^d` of the forms `F ∈ p.I` of
degree `d`. Then the closed subscheme of `𝓘` is flat over `Spec A`.

Proof (namespace `K1ASOL`). The charts `D₊(Xᵢ)` cover `ℙⁿ_A` (`iSup_basicOpen_X`), so flatness is
checked on their preimages in the subscheme (`IsZariskiLocalAtSource.of_iSup_eq_top`). On the chart
`i`, the subscheme is `Spec` of the degree-zero localization `A[X]_{(Xᵢ)}` modulo the ideal generated
by the dehomogenized elements of `p.I` (identified through `Proj.basicOpenIsoAway`; `fromSpec_eq`,
`algebraMap_away_eq`). Its flatness over `A` is the ported theorem
`HomogeneousLocalization.Away.flat_quotientMk_comp_of_forall_flat_piece`, applicable because the
pieces of `p.I` are projective.
