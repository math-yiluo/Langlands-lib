## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_MvPolynomial_relation_mem_span_of_linear_of_forall_relation_modulo_mem_span.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_MvPolynomial_relation_mem_span_of_linear_of_forall_relation_modulo_mem_span`. Statement. `MvPolynomial.relation_mem_span_of_linear_of_forall_relation_modulo_mem_span`: under the hypotheses of
`mem_span_of_linear_mul_mem_of_forall_relation_modulo_mem_span` (a linear nonzerodivisor `ℓ` in degree `m` and relations
modulo `(ℓ)` generated in degree `≤ 1`), every homogeneous relation `Σ cᵢ Fᵢ = 0` is generated by relations of degree
`≤ 1`.

Proof. Induction on the degree of the relation (`main`). A relation is in particular a relation modulo `(ℓ)`, hence a
combination of degree-`≤ 1` relations modulo `(ℓ)`; their actual values are multiples of `ℓ` lying in `(F)`, so by the
nonzerodivisor property (as in `mem_span_of_linear_mul_mem_of_forall_relation_modulo_mem_span`, local copies) they can be
corrected by `ℓ` times relations of lower degree (`ev`, `ev_eq_zero_of_mem_span`), to which the induction hypothesis
applies.
