## Content

The theorem `P2MW.S_Module_Projective_exists_ideal_fg_forall_tmul_eq_zero_iff_map_eq_bot.solution` proves the statement of the companion theorem in `Langlands.AnthropicFLT.Theorems.Thm_Module_Projective_exists_ideal_fg_forall_tmul_eq_zero_iff_map_eq_bot`. Statement. `Module.Projective.exists_ideal_fg_forall_tmul_eq_zero_iff_map_eq_bot`: for a finite projective `B`-module `M` and
`x ∈ M`, there is a finitely generated ideal `J ⊆ B` such that `1 ⊗ x = 0` in `A ⊗_B M` iff `J` maps to zero in `A`.

Proof. `M` is a direct summand of some `Bⁿ`; let `J` be generated by the coordinates of `x` there. Base change
preserves the splitting, so `1 ⊗ x = 0` iff all coordinates vanish in `A`.
