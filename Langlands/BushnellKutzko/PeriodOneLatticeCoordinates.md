# Integral matrix coordinates for a period-one lattice chain

The period relation identifies the zeroth graded piece with the zeroth lattice modulo the maximal ideal. The quotient is residue-field base change, so lifts of a residue basis form a basis of the finite free lattice. Extending scalars gives a basis of the ambient vector space. All lattices in the chain are scalar translates of the zeroth lattice. Consequently the units preserving the chain are precisely the automorphisms with integral forward and inverse matrices in the extended basis. Scalar automorphisms give scalar matrices.

The fraction-field valuation associated with the maximal ideal of a discrete valuation ring lets these coordinates be used over the integers of the base field and over the integers of a finite field extension. This is the lattice and matrix realization for the period-one specialization of Bushnell–Kutzko (1993), §§5.4–5.5.
