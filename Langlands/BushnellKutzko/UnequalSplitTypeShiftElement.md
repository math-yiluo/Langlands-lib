# The corrected shift for a split type

`SplitTypeShiftStatement` concerns a simple stratum of index zero with a chosen defining sequence, a Bushnell–Kutzko additive character, and a simple character `θ` at index `m − 1`. The depth `m` is below the stratum depth and the simple critical depth. The character on `H = H^m` is `ϑ = θ ψ_c`, where `c ∈ P^(−m)` has zero off-diagonal blocks for the supplied complementary orthogonal idempotents. The first idempotent lies in the integral centralizer, and the second is integral and commutes with `β`. The first-block factors `ζ` and `ζ′` are mutually inverse on that block, at depths `1` and `−1`, and `ζ` commutes with `β`. A tame corestriction for the field generated by `β` kills the difference `ζ c₁₁ ζ′ − c₁₁`.

`exists_shift_element` constructs `z = ζ + e₂` and `z⁻¹ = ζ′ + e₂`. By tame corestriction exactness and the stability of the centralizer–adjoint product lattice under corner projection, the difference in the first block gives a correction unit `g₀` supported there. The scalar case uses injectivity of the corestriction and takes `g₀ = 1`. The corrected shift `y = g₀ z` sends `M_{j−1}` into `M_j` by conjugation. Since `g₀` normalizes `H`, `y` and `z` have the same inverse-conjugate subgroup of `H`. On that subgroup, `ϑ(y κ y⁻¹) = θ(z κ z⁻¹) ψ_c(κ)`. On its intersection with `H`, covariance of the simple character under `z` gives `ϑ(y κ y⁻¹) = ϑ(κ)`.

This uses adjoint exactness and simple character covariance to implement the shift of Bushnell and Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), (8.2.8)–(8.2.10) and (8.2.16), printed pp. 278–282. The two units give the filtration shift and the character comparison in the form used by the vector transfers.
