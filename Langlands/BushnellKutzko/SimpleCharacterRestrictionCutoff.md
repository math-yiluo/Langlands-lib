# Restricting a simple character below the cutoff

Source: Bushnell–Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), (3.2.1), printed p. 98 (PDF p. 110), and (3.2.3)–(3.2.5), printed pp. 99–100 (PDF pp. 111–112). Literature ID `bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln`, SHA-256 `811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`.

A character factors through the relative determinant image when it kills the determinant kernel on the pullback of its domain. The proof uses the kernel criterion for lifting a homomorphism through the surjection onto the determinant image. This also gives stability under restriction, multiplication, and inversion.

For the forward map of (3.2.5), a terminal simple character restricts by retaining its trace agreement and determinant factorization. In the nonterminal case, every index at or below `⌊r/2⌋` uses the same deeper test subgroup in (3.2.3). The same witness therefore supplies the twist agreement after restriction. Normalization of the restricted character follows from normalization of the original character and the supplied preservation of the new domain. These results give the forward membership calculation used for restriction. Surjectivity and the fibre description use the separate subgroup-product argument.
