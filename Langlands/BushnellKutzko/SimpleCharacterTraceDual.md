# The dual of a simple-character layer

`SimpleStratumLattice.Sequence.traceDual_simpleHLayer` proves the dual formula of BK93 (3.1.19), printed pp. 96–97. At the cutoff, the terminal formula follows from the ambient filtration duality and a commutator depth bound. At the successor step the H and J layers agree at their cutoffs. The perturbation changes the adjoint image only in the complementary ambient step. The adjoint kills the relative part of the successor J layer, so the successor's dual formula applies. The scalar terminal has zero adjoint and an ambient filtration layer.

The cutoff formula extends upward using duality for an intersection. Below the cutoff it uses the dual of the relative step and a mixed adjoint lift at the complementary depth. Finiteness for the intersection duality comes from the bounds by ambient filtration steps and their duals.
