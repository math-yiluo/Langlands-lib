# Containment of a simple type

For a compact open subgroup \(J\) and its smooth representation \(\lambda\),
`ContainsType π λ` means that there is a nonzero \(J\)-equivariant complex
linear map \(\lambda \to \pi|_J\). This is containment as used in the
introduction to Chapter 6 and in Theorem (6.2.1). For an irreducible
\(\lambda\) such a map identifies it with an irreducible subrepresentation.
`ContainsRepresentation π i ρ` uses the same condition on restriction
along a homomorphism `i`, so it also applies to the inclusion of \(J\)
in \(E^\times J\) in (6.1.2) and (6.2.2). The two smooth representations
can have carriers in independent universes.

C. J. Bushnell and P. C. Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact
open subgroups* (1993), printed pp. 199, 200, 202–203, especially
(6.1.2), (6.2.1) and (6.2.2).
