# Complete refinement of the chain over the parameter field

To obtain the minimal relative order used by Bushnell–Kutzko (1993),
(5.1.12) and (5.2.3), first view the lattices as a chain over the
parameter field. Refine that relative chain using the complete chain
of an ordered splitting basis. Its period is the dimension over the
parameter field, it contains each original lattice, and its zeroth
lattice is the original one.

The spectral integer ring and the integral closure give the same
lattice subsets. Transfer the refined chain along their isomorphism
and lift it to the base field. Its period multiplies by the
ramification index, and it is still normalized by the units of the
parameter field. The depth-zero and depth-one subgroup bounds follow
from the preservation of the original lattice subsets in the
refinement.
