# The subgroup properties of a simple stratum (U2)

Source: C. J. Bushnell and P. C. Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), (3.1.14)–(3.1.15), printed p. 95 (PDF p. 107).

`SimpleStratumGroupPropertiesStatement` is the conjunction below for every simple `[𝔄,n,0,β]` and defining sequence starting at `β`. The field and vector space have independent universes. Natural indices give all the nonnegative levels.

- The carriers of `H^m` and `J^m` are exactly the literal intersections from (3.1.14). This records that the intersections are already groups, so algebraic generation in their bundled construction does not enlarge them.
- If `F[β]≠F`, put `r=−k₀(β,𝔄)`, `a=⌊r/2⌋+1`, `b=⌊(r+1)/2⌋`. Then `H^m=U^m(𝔅_β)H^a` for `0≤m≤a`, and `J^m=U^m(𝔅_β)J^b` for `0≤m≤b`. These are pointwise products, not subgroup joins.
- `𝔎(𝔅_β)` normalizes `H^m` and `J^m` for every `m`. At every level `H^m≤J^m`, and `H^{m+1}` lies in `J=J^0` and is normalized by it, expressing normality in `J`.
- For `k,l≥1`, the subgroup generated by the commutators of `J^k` with `J^l` is contained in `H^{k+l}`.

Only the product formulas are confined to the nonscalar case. This is a proposition definition; it does not prove the claims.
