# Jacquet refinement by a shift and transfer

Let `M_j` be a decreasing family of subgroups indexed by the integers, covering a subgroup `U`. Let `P` be the finite-dimensional `ϑ`-isotypic subspace for a subgroup `K` in a complex representation `ρ`. Suppose `P` meets the complement of the Jacquet relations of `M_j` for some `j`. In `not_le_relations_of_shift_transfer` a unit `z` conjugates `M_{j−1}` into `M_j` for each `j`. A vector from `P` outside the relations of `M_j` then gives, by applying `ρ(z⁻¹)`, a vector outside the relations of `M_{j−1}` with the transported `ϑ` action on `z⁻¹ K z`. The supplied transfer gives a vector in `P` outside those same relations. The finite-dimensional refinement lemma concludes that `P` meets the complement of the relations of `U`.

This abstracts the shift and return to the original isotypic subspace in Bushnell and Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), (8.2.14)–(8.2.28), printed pp. 281–289.
