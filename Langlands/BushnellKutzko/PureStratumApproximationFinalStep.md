# The final step of the proof of BK93 (2.4.1)(i)

Source: Bushnell–Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), `bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln`, proof of (2.4.1)(i), printed pp. 70–71 (PDF pp. 82–83). There the derived stratum `[𝔅_m,r_m,r_m−1,s(b_m)]` of the chosen approximation is fundamental, not scalar fundamental, and nonsplit by (2.3.12). (2.3.4) over `E = F[γ_m]` gives a simple stratum `[𝔅',r',r'−1,α]`, and (2.2.8) gives a simple refinement `γ` whose field `F[γ]` has the ramification index and residue degree of `F[γ_m, α]`. The field `F[γ]` need not equal `F[γ_m, α]`, but `[F[γ]:F] = [F[γ_m, α]:F] > [F[γ_m]:F]`, as on printed p. 71.

This module proves that step in the form `improvement_or_increase`. It uses the Library proposition `NonsplitRefinementPremise` ((2.3.4)) for fields in the universe of `E` as a hypothesis. It does not assume that the derived stratum is not scalar fundamental. Instead it splits on whether `α` is a scalar of `E`. This is a derived formulation, not a numbered assertion of the source.

## Statement

`improvement_or_increase hRef β S s D hD hfund hns`: let `D` be the derived stratum of `β − S.b` for a simple `S` (`Stratum.IsDerivedStratum`), fundamental and not split. Then one of the following holds:

1. some simple stratum `T` has `β ∈ T.coset`, `T.r · e(S) < S.r · e(T)`, and the ramification index and residue degree of `F[S.b]`;
2. there are simple strata `T`, `U` with `U.chain = T.chain`, `U.n = T.n`, `U.r = T.r − 1` and `β` in both cosets, such that
   - `k₀(U.b) = −T.r`,
   - `[F[S.b]:F] < [F[U.b]:F]`,
   - the ramification index and residue degree of `F[T.b]` divide those of `F[U.b]`.

The second alternative is the conclusion of the final step in the source.

## Proof

- `sub_le_of_strictMono` and `sandwiched_of_lattice_mem`: if every lattice of `𝓛` is a lattice of `𝓜`, then every lattice of `𝓜` lies between two consecutive lattices of `𝓛`. The index map `σ` from `𝓛` to `𝓜` is strictly increasing, hence unbounded in both directions. For `M_t`, take the greatest `q` with `σ(q) ≤ t`.
- `exists_simple_of_not_isSplit` gives `C = [𝓜_E,ρ,ρ−1,c]` over `E`. Let `𝓜 = liftChain C.chain`. Its lattices contain those of `S.chain`, so the previous item gives compatibility. The periods give `ρ e(S) = S.r e(𝓜)` from `D.n e(C) = C.n e(D)`, using `liftChain_period` for both chains. The coset inclusion gives `s(β − S.b) ∈ C.coset` and maps `E`-linear elements of `𝔓^{1−r}(S.chain)` into `𝔓^{1−ρ}(𝓜)`. `exists_refinement_of_coset` then gives `T` and `U` on `𝓜`; `F[U.b]` has the ramification index and residue degree of the algebra `F[S.b, c_F]` generated by `S.b` and the restriction `c_F` of `c` to `F`.
- If `c` is a scalar of `E`, `F[S.b, c_F] = F[S.b]`, and `U` gives the first alternative, since `(ρ − 1) e(S) < S.r e(𝓜)`.
- Otherwise `c_F ∉ F[S.b]`, and `E[c] ≠ E`. `derived_mem_criticalIndexSet_of_mem` and `derived_exists_isGreatest_criticalIndexSet` give `k₀(c) = −ρ`, from `c ∈ 𝔔^{−ρ}` and simplicity of `C`. Also `k₀(S.b, 𝓜) < −ρ` (`criticalExponent_lt_of_normalized`), so `k₀(U.b) = max(k₀(S.b,𝓜), k₀(c)) = −ρ = −T.r`. The algebra `E₁ = F[S.b, c_F]` is a field, because the ramification index of `F[U.b]` equals its constructed index and is positive (`subfieldRamificationIndex_pos'`). So `[F[U.b]:F] = e(E₁) f(E₁) = [E₁:F]` (`subfieldRamificationIndex_mul_residueDegree`). This is larger than `[F[S.b]:F]` because `F[S.b] ⊊ E₁`. The divisibilities follow from the inclusion `F[S.b] → E₁` (`subfieldInvariantsEmbedding`), since `F[T.b]` has the invariants of `F[S.b]`.
