# Intertwining at or above the cutoff

`simpleCharacterIntertwining_high_of_successor` proves the formula at finite critical depth `r` for character indices `m ≥ ⌊r/2⌋`. The domain `H^{m+1}` thus has group index at least the H cutoff `⌊r/2⌋ + 1`. In the terminal case all character values are given by the element character. The resulting covariance on every overlap identifies self-intertwining with formal intertwining of the dual lattice coset.

For a nonterminal parameter, restriction to `H^{r+1}` kills the perturbation. The resulting character of the successor at index `r` is simple by forward restriction. Induction gives its intertwining set and the centralizer intertwining of the successor character at the original index. This supplies covariance on the restricted character's entire intertwining set. The twist transports that covariance back to the preceding parameter. Both the original intertwining set and the formal set lie inside the restricted set. The latter inclusion follows by enlarging the ordinary stratum coset to the congruent successor coset and using cutoff J normalization on both sides.

This is the high-depth part of BK93 (3.3.2), including (3.3.5), (3.3.8) and the covariance calculation (3.3.9), printed pp. 105–110.
