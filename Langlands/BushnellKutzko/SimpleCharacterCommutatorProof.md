# Commutator identities along a defining sequence

`simpleCharacterCommutatorAt` proves the mixed and J calculations by induction on the length of the defining sequence. The terminal stratum has critical exponent equal to the negative level in the finite case. At a successor step the common high layers provide the relevant H and J groups. The adjoint lattice of the successor splits into a relative lattice and a mixed term at the successor critical exponent. Forward restriction puts the successor character at the depth needed for the inductive calculation.

`simpleCharacterMixedJCommutator` specializes to the J version, with the subgroup properties supplied by the lattice and subgroup theorems. The depth hypotheses and commutator convention are those of BK93 (3.2.6) and (3.2.8), printed pp. 100–103.
