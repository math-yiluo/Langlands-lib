# Relative unit double cosets

C. J. Bushnell and P. C. Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open
subgroups*, Annals of Mathematics Studies 129 (1993), Theorem (1.6.1), printed
p. 47 (PDF p. 59), and the relative filtration in (1.2.4), printed p. 25
(PDF p. 37). The retained source `bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln`
has SHA-256 `811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`.

`RelativeUnitDoubleCosetStatement` specializes (1.6.1) to the generated field
`E = F[β]` of an endomorphism `β`. Its units stabilize the given lattice chain
and thus normalize the associated hereditary order `𝔄`. The centralizer
`B_β` of `β` is the centralizer of this field. For `m ≥ 1`, the relative units
`U^m(𝔅)` of `𝔅 = 𝔄 ∩ B_β` are represented in the shared ambient group by
`relativeUnitFiltration L β m = B_β^× ∩ U^m(𝔄)`. The displayed equality is
between the intersection of the ambient double coset with `B_β^×` and the
relative double coset, for each `y ∈ B_β^×`. The field and vector space have
independent universes.
