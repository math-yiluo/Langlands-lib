# Extending an invariant representation across a subgroup of coprime index

This is the abstract extension step used in Bushnell–Kutzko (1993),
(5.2.4), printed pp. 167–169 (PDF pp. 179–181). There the normal
subgroup is `J_M^1` in `J_M`, the finite-index subgroup is a Sylow
pro-`p` subgroup containing `J_M^1`, and the degree and the order of
the determinant of the representation of `J_M^1` are powers of `p`.
The index is prime to `p`. The retained source
`bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln` has SHA-256
`811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`.

`CoprimeRepresentationExtensionStatement` considers a normal subgroup
`N` of `G` and a finite-index subgroup `P` containing `N`. The
finite-dimensional irreducible complex representation `η` of `N` is
assumed isomorphic to each of its `G`-conjugates and has a supplied
extension to `P` on the same space. If the determinant character of
`η` has exponent dividing `a` and the index of `P` is coprime to
`dim(η) * a`, then a representation of `G` on that space restricts
to `η`. The group and the representation space have independent universes.
