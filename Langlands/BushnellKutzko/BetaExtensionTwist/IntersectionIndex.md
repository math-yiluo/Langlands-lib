# The level-one index on the common domain

The lower bound proved here corresponds to the index equality in
Bushnell–Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups*
(1993), Lemma (5.1.10), printed pp. 161–162. The retained source
`bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln` has SHA-256
`811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`.

For a simple stratum at level zero with a chosen defining sequence, write
`H` and `J` for its level-one additive layers and `H'` and `J'` for their
inverse conjugates by a centralizing unit. Assume `H ≤ J`, that the
original relative index is finite, and that the additive character has
the Bushnell–Kutzko normalization. The common index of `H ⊓ H'` in
`J ⊓ J'` is at least the index of `H` in `J`.

The trace of a commutator times an endomorphism followed by the character
gives a pairing between the sum quotient and the intersection quotient.
The level-one adjoint duality identifies the commutator images for each
layer. A projection preserving both radical steps lets preimages from
`J` and `J'` with the same commutator be adjusted to agree, by subtracting
their centralizer components. This identifies the common commutator image
with the part of the dual of `H ⊔ H'` in the adjoint range. The trace
functional obtained by composing with the projection supplies the other
part, which annihilates both radical steps. The character normalization
and stability under integral scalar multiplication turn character
annihilation into maximal-ideal trace values. Double trace duality then
gives injectivity from the sum quotient into the character group of the
intersection quotient. The sum and intersection indices multiply to the
square of the original index, so the intersection index gives the bound.

Given the simple-stratum lattice and group properties, `g ↦ g − 1`
identifies these additive indices with the group indices at level one.
The group comparison assumes the finite original group index, which is
nonzero in the natural-valued index convention.
