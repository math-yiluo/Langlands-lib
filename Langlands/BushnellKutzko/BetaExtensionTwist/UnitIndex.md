# Positive-depth unit indices

Bushnell–Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups*
(1993), Lemma (5.1.10), printed pp. 161–162, compares two conjugate
intersections by passing to additive lattices. The retained source
`bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln` has SHA-256
`811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`.

For a unit group with `g - 1` ranging over an additive subgroup `P`,
reduction modulo `Q ≤ P` is a homomorphism if products of pairs of elements
of `P` belong to `Q`. The construction uses the identity
`gh - 1 = (g - 1) + (h - 1) + (g - 1)(h - 1)`. The kernel consists of
units whose difference from one belongs to `Q`. Surjectivity identifies
the group index with the cardinality of the additive quotient.

For the level-one simple-stratum groups, the group carrier formulas
identify `g - 1` with the additive layers, and conjugation commutes with
taking that difference. The product bound of (3.1.13)(ii), printed p. 94,
places the product defect in the `H¹` layer on both sides of the
intersection. This gives the equality of relative indices on the common
intertwining domain, using the supplied subgroup and lattice properties.
