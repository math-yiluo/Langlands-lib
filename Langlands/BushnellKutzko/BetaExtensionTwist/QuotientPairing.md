# Finite quotient pairings and relative indices

The arithmetic is used in the lattice calculation of Bushnell–Kutzko,
*The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), Lemmas
(5.1.5) and (5.1.10), printed pp. 159 and 161–162. The retained source
`bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln` has SHA-256
`811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`.

For additive subgroups `H ≤ J` and `H' ≤ J'`, the sum map from `J × J'`
has image `J + J'` and kernel the antidiagonal image of `J ∩ J'`. The
subgroup `H × H'` maps to `H + H'`, and its pullback along the antidiagonal
is `H ∩ H'`. The index formula for the sum map and index multiplication
therefore express the product of the original indices as the product
of the intersection and sum indices.

A pairing with values in complex additive characters descends through
specified subgroups on which it is trivial in each argument. If the
left radical is exactly the left subgroup, the induced map into the
character group of the right quotient is injective. For a finite right
quotient, its character group has the same cardinality. The resulting
inequality compares the left and right subgroup indices.
