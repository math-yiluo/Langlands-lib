# Scalar simple-character intertwining and normalizer

`simpleCharacterIntertwining_of_scalar` proves both formulas of
`SimpleCharacterIntertwiningStatement` for scalar `β`. The critical
exponent is bottom, so the first step of a defining sequence would
require an impossible finite critical exponent. The empty tail and
scalar centralizer give `H^{m+1}=U^{m+1}` by unfolding the lattice and
carrier. Every ambient unit intertwines the character by its relative
determinant factorization. The normalizer is thus the normalizer of
`U^{m+1}`, which is the chain stabilizer. These are the two formulas with
the singleton outer factor.

The scalar case is identified in the proof of BK93 (3.3.2), printed p. 105
(PDF p. 117), with the chain normalizer in §1.1, printed pp. 21–22, and
(3.3.17), printed p. 112. The source is
`bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln`, SHA-256
`811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`.
