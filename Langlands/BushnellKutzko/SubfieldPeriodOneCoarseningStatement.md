# Coarsening to an order of relative period one

Bushnell–Kutzko (1993) choose an overorder of the relative hereditary
order in (5.1.12), printed p. 162 (PDF p. 174), and take it maximal in
(5.2.3), printed p. 167 (PDF p. 179). Proposition (1.2.4), printed
p. 25 (PDF p. 37), identifies the relative order and its filtration
using the chain over the parameter field. The retained source
`bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln` has SHA-256
`811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`.

Let \(L\) be a lattice chain in an \(F\)-vector space \(V\) with a
compatible \(E\)-action, where \(F\) is a nonarchimedean local field and
\(E^\times\) normalizes \(L\). There is another \(E^\times\)-normalized
chain \(M\) with the same zeroth lattice and base-field period \(e(E/F)\).
The unit group of \(L\) is contained in the unit group of \(M\), and
the depth-one unit group of \(M\) is contained in that of \(L\). The
lattices of \(M\), with the extension integer scalars, form a chain
of period one over \(E\). The base field, parameter field, and vector
space have independent universes.
