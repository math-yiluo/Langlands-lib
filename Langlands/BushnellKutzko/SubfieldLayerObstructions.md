# Endomorphism families from a single shift orbit

A field unit of positive shift transports an endomorphism of layer zero
to every layer in its orbit. The family is zero off the orbit. The
product rule for transports makes this family intertwine the selected
unit. Because field-unit translations respect extension residue scalars,
any scalar commuting with the initial map commutes throughout the orbit.

For a proper subalgebra of the finite extension residue field, choose a
nonzero vector in layer zero and a scalar outside that subalgebra. The
subalgebra is a field. Projection onto the line spanned by the vector
over that field commutes with its scalars. It fails to commute with the
chosen outside scalar, which sends the vector outside the line. The
transported family retains this failure on layer zero.

This construction, together with the layer indicator, gives the
obstructions used in the proof of Bushnell–Kutzko (1993), (1.4.15),
printed p. 41.
