# Valuation, normalization and invariants under the field-line diagonal

Source context: Bushnell–Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), `bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln`, the reduction to a maximal generated subfield in the opening proof of (2.4.1)(i), printed p. 66 (PDF p. 78). This is a derived comparison for the repeated diagonal in that reduction, using the Library's chain and block models.

`DiagonalStratumTransportStatement` assumes that the base field `F` is a nonarchimedean local field. `[TopologicalSpace F]` and `[IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F]` are explicit binders of the definition, as in the theorem `diagonalStratumTransport`. The exact-depth step and the field-invariant comparison use that hypothesis. It also assumes a nonempty relative splitting basis with scaled lattice coordinates of common positive scale `d`. The valuation of the repeated diagonal `Δb` is `d` times the line valuation of `b`, preserving the value top for zero. If the algebra `F[b]` has normalizing units for the line chain, `F[Δb]` has normalizing units for the ambient chain and the ramification indices and residue degrees agree. The critical-exponent comparison is in `PureStratumApproximationScaling`; no simple approximant is constructed here.

For valuation, the rounded diagonal filtration formula at depths `d*k` and `d*k+1` reads off line depths `k` and `k+1`. The exact finite depth therefore dilates by `d`, and the zero case follows from the definition. For normalization, a stabilizer unit and its inverse lie at opposite filtration depths. Apply the diagonal to get the corresponding two ambient inclusions, now at scaled depths. Together an inclusion and the inverse inclusion give equality of shifted lattices. This applies to each nonzero element of the generated algebra because that algebra is the image of the line algebra under the diagonal. Finally the injective diagonal is an `F`-algebra isomorphism onto that generated image. The field invariants agree by `subfieldInvariants_of_algEquiv`.
