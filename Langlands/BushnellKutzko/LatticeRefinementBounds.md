# Unit-group bounds for refinement of a lattice chain

If every lattice of \(L\) occurs in \(M\), the lattice order identifies
the indices of \(L\) with a strictly increasing sequence of indices
in \(M\). An endomorphism preserving every lattice of \(M\) therefore
preserves the lattices of \(L\). This is the inclusion of orders under
chain refinement in Bushnell–Kutzko (1993), (1.1.2), printed p. 20.

The index map between chains grows by at least one at each step.
At each position of \(M\), select the last retained index at or before
that position. A map lowering \(L\) maps the upper retained lattice
to the lower retained lattice. This maps the lattice at the selected
position of \(M\) into its successor. The depth-zero and depth-one
unit-group bounds follow from these two filtration inclusions.
