# The indicator family for multiples of a layer index

The indicator family acts as identity on the layers whose indices are
multiples of an integer and as zero on the other layers. A translation
intertwines the family if its shift is a multiple of that integer. In
the other direction the layer at index zero is nonzero, so comparison
with its translated layer forces the translated index to be a multiple.

This family gives an obstruction when the two shifts in the minimality
criterion have a common divisor, in the proof of Bushnell–Kutzko
(1993), (1.4.15), printed p. 41.
