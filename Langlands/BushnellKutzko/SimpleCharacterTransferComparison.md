# Comparing the relative restrictions of simple characters

The source is Bushnell and Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), `bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln`, SHA-256 `811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`, (3.6.9) and the uniqueness argument on printed pp. 130–132 (PDF pp. 142–144).

A unit commutes with `β` precisely when the unit and its inverse restrict from `F[β]`-linear maps. For characters factoring through the relative determinant, equal determinants give equal character values on their relative domains. At equal normalized depths, every unit in the second relative group has the same determinant as a unit in the common relative group. If two characters on the second domain both agree with a fixed character on the first domain, evaluating this common lift shows that their relative restrictions coincide.

For a terminal target domain, the definition also prescribes the character as the common trace character on the higher ambient subgroup. The relative and ambient subgroups generate that domain, so two possible transfers must coincide. This gives the at-most-one assertion for a terminal defining sequence on the second chain, including when the first defining sequence is nonterminal.
