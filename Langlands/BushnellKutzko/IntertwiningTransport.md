# Transport of self-intertwining

The pointwise conjugation equation for a nonzero linear map is equivalent to self-intertwining in the sense of `RepresentationIntertwining`. A representation equivalence transports the linear map by conjugation, and an injective homomorphism inducing an isomorphism of the subgroups identifies the two domains of the equation. Multiplication on the left or right by a subgroup element transports the equation using that element's action on the carrier. A central element has the identity as an intertwiner on a nonzero carrier.
