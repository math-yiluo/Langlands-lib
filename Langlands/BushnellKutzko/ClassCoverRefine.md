# Covering simple-character classes: scalar perturbations of simple refinements

This module proves `RefinementSimpleAt` and reduces `SimpleCharacterClassCoverStatement` to the
fibre-character input `SimpleCharacterFibreCharacterStatement`.

Scalar lemmas, over any field `K`: `scalarEnd c` is multiplication by `c`; adding `scalarEnd c`
changes neither the generated algebra (`elementAlgebra_add_algebraMap`), nor the adjoint map,
nor the critical exponent (`criticalExponent_add_algebraMap`), so
`Stratum.isSimple_of_add_algebraMap` keeps simplicity when the valuation is unchanged.
`isSimple_of_algebraMap`: a scalar stratum over an intermediate field `E`, on a chain with the
lattices of an `E^×`-normalized chain, is simple when its valuation is exact.
`LatticeChain.eq_of_lattice_coe`: two indexed chains are equal when the underlying sets of
their lattices agree at every index (a shift of indexing gives a different chain).

Perturbations (Bushnell and Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups*
(1993); *Simple types in GL(N): computing conjugacy classes* (1994), (1.8)):

* `exists_adjoint_approx` (BK93 (1.4.10), the last exact sequence of `adjointExactness`): let
  `E = F[γ]` be a field generated by an element `γ` that is not a scalar, with `E^×` normalizing
  the chain, let `s` be a tame corestriction relative to `E/F`, and assume the strict bound
  `k₀(γ,𝔄) < -(k + 1)` on the critical exponent. If `b ∈ 𝔓^{-(k+1)}` and `s(b) ∈ 𝔓^{-k}`, then
  `b ≡ a_γ(x) (mod 𝔓^{-k})` with `x ∈ 𝔓`. A simple stratum `[𝔄,n,k+1,γ]` with `γ` not a scalar
  supplies these hypotheses. Without the bound the conclusion can fail.
* `mem_endFiltration_of_tameCorestriction_scalar`: if the field `E` acts through scalars of `F`
  (as `F[γ]` does for a scalar `γ`) and `E^×` normalizes the chain, then a tame corestriction
  relative to `E/F` detects the filtration: `s(y) ∈ 𝔓^j` implies `y ∈ 𝔓^j`.
* `exists_conj_refinement`: for finite-dimensional `V`, let `[𝔄,n,k+1,γ]` be a simple stratum
  on `L`, `b ∈ 𝔓^{-(k+1)}` and `x ∈ 𝔓` with
  `b ≡ γx - xγ (mod 𝔓^{-k})`. The conjugate of `[𝔄,n,k,γ]` by `1 + x` (a simple stratum by
  `SimpleCharacterConjugationStatement`, used with `ψ_F` trivial on `𝔭_F` and nontrivial on
  `𝔬_F`) is a simple stratum `[𝔄,n,k,β']` on `L` with `β' ≡ γ + b (mod 𝔓^{-k})`.
* `exists_derived_approx` (BK93 (2.4.1)(ii)(c) and (iii)): for finite-dimensional `V`, a simple
  refinement `[𝔄,n,k,β]` on `L` of a simple stratum `[𝔄,n,k+1,γ]` on `L` with `F[γ]` a field, and
  every tame corestriction relative to `F[γ]/F`, the tame corestriction of `β - γ` is congruent
  modulo `𝔓^{-k}` to a scalar of `F[γ]` (`simpleStratumCorestrictionCongruence`, BK93
  (2.4.1)(ii)(c), when `[𝔄,n,k+1,β]` is simple) or to the element of a simple stratum over `F[γ]`
  at index `k` on a chain with the same lattices as `L` (`criticalDerivedStratum`, BK93
  (2.4.1)(iii), when `k + 1` is the critical depth of `β`).
* `refinementSimpleAt` proves `RefinementSimpleAt L n k` for finite-dimensional `V`, assuming
  `SimpleCharacterConjugationStatement` and an additive character `ψ_F` as above (the
  proposition `RefinementSimpleAt` itself does not involve `ψ_F`). Let `[𝔄,n,k+1,γ]` be simple,
  `[𝔄,n,k,β]` a simple refinement of it on `L`, and `e ∈ 𝔓^{-(k+1)}` with
  `CorestrictionScalarMod L γ e k`. Put `b = (β - γ) + e`, which lies in `𝔓^{-(k+1)}`. Either
  `b ∈ 𝔓^{-k}` (take `γ`), or the derived
  stratum of `b` is null (`exists_adjoint_approx` and `exists_conj_refinement`, or a
  contradiction for scalar `γ`), or it is equivalent to the simple stratum obtained by adding
  the scalar of `e` to the approximation of `β - γ`, and `refinement` (BK93 (2.2.8)) gives the
  required simple stratum.

`simpleCharacterClassCover_of_fibreCharacter` proves `SimpleCharacterClassCoverStatement` from
`PureStratumSimpleApproximationStatement`, `SimpleCharacterConjugationStatement`,
`SimpleCharacterChoiceStatement`, `SimpleStratumGroupPropertiesStatement`,
`SimpleCharacterRestrictionStatement`, `SimpleCharacterCoherenceStatement`,
`SimpleCharacterTranslationStatement`, `SimpleCharacterCovarianceStatement` and
`SimpleCharacterFibreCharacterStatement`; it does not prove these hypotheses.
