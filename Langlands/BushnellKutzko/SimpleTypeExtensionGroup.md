# The subgroup \(E^\times J\)

For exhibited simple type data `d`, `d.fieldUnitsHom` maps the units
of its parameter field into the matrix group. It takes units in the
inclusion of \(E\) into \(\operatorname{End}_F(F^n)\) and applies
`Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.toLin.symm`. The subgroup
`d.extensionSubgroup` is generated by the image of \(E^\times\) and the
type subgroup \(J\). The inclusion of \(J\) proves openness because
\(J\) is open. At level zero the field acts by scalars and the type
subgroup is the image of \(U(\mathfrak A)\) in matrix coordinates, giving
\(F^\times U(\mathfrak A)\).

C. J. Bushnell and P. C. Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact
open subgroups* (1993), (6.1.2) and (6.2.2), printed pp. 200 and 203.
The scalar convention for type (b) is in the paragraph following
(5.5.10), printed p. 185.
