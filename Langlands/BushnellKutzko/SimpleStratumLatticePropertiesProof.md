# Lattice properties of a simple stratum

`Langlands.BushnellKutzko.simpleStratumLatticeProperties` proves `Langlands.BushnellKutzko.SimpleStratumLatticePropertiesStatement`, with the field and ambient vector space in independent universes. The source is C. J. Bushnell and P. C. Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups*, Annals of Mathematics Studies 129, Princeton University Press, 1993, (3.1.9)–(3.1.13), printed pp. 91–94 (PDF pp. 103–106). The registered source is `bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln`.

The induction in `SimpleStratumLatticeInduction` gives both lattice bounds, multiplication closure, adjoint bimodule stability, fractional commutation, and comparison of the layers. The bounds between `𝔓^n` and `𝔓^0` give finite generation over `𝔬_F` and spanning over `F` by clearing denominators. The integral centralizer supplies the identity, so multiplication closure gives the two subalgebras. Intersecting with a nonnegative filtration step gives the two-sided ideals. The comparison of the two cutoffs gives `ℌ ⊆ 𝔍` and the corresponding layer inclusions.

Above each cutoff the induction identifies the layers with those of any critical approximation. Commutation with the fractional centralizer steps gives conjugation invariance by applying the steps of opposite depths to a relative normalizer element and its inverse. The two cross-product bounds are intersected with the filtration at the sum of the exponents. For positive H-layers, the integral centralizer acts on both sides, and the remaining part of `𝔍` belongs to the first J-layer. The positive J-layer product bound supplies the required H-lattice containment, and multiplication in the ambient filtration preserves the layer depth.

For equivalent simple data the critical exponents agree. At finite critical depth `r` and comparison depth `t`, the induction gives equality for `k ≥ 0` with `k ≥ t+C(r)−r`. The two versions of that bound are `t+1−⌊(r+1)/2⌋` for H and `t−⌊r/2⌋` for J. This includes negative comparison depths. In the scalar case both filtrations are the full nonnegative filtration and comparison uses only `k ≥ 0`. Taking the same parameter at depth zero and using the two cutoffs at `k = 0` proves independence of the defining sequence.
