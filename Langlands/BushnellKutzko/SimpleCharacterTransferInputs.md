# Subgroup, restriction and choice inputs to simple-character transfer

The source is Bushnell and Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), `bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln`, SHA-256 `811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`, (3.1.14)–(3.1.15), (3.3.18), and (3.3.20), printed pp. 95 and 112–114 (PDF pp. 107 and 124–126).

The given subgroup, restriction and choice statements apply at stratum index zero. Lowering a nonnegative simple index to zero preserves the defining-sequence tail, which leaves the groups and character sets used here unchanged. The subgroup product below the cutoff gives the generating join by group closure. At and above the cutoff the high domain is the whole domain and contains the relative units.

At the high index of a critical approximation, the critical-twist bijection has an inverse. Apply this inverse and multiply by the nonzero common twist to get the character in the direction used by transfer. The choice comparison supplies untwisted transport between defining sequences. After identifying equal subgroups, an untwisted transported character is the original character by extensionality, and the transfer assertion is preserved.
