# A simple type in an irreducible supercuspidal representation is maximal

`SupercuspidalSimpleTypeMaximalityStatement` takes a simple type
\((J,\lambda)\) with exhibiting data in \(\mathrm{GL}_n(F)\), where \(F\)
is a nonarchimedean local field and \(n>0\). If an irreducible
supercuspidal smooth representation \(\pi\) contains \(\lambda\) on
restriction to \(J\), then the centralizer-order period
\(e(\mathfrak B_\beta\mid\mathfrak o_E)\) is one. At level zero the
conclusion is \(e(\mathfrak A\mid\mathfrak o_F)=1\). The data are
universally quantified, so the conclusion applies to every choice
exhibiting the type. The field and the two carriers have independent
universe parameters.

C. J. Bushnell and P. C. Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact
open subgroups* (1993), (6.2.1), printed p. 202, with the period-one
criterion explained on p. 203 and before (6.2.3) on p. 204.
