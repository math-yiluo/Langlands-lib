# Duality and the adjoint annihilator of a simple stratum

Fix a nonarchimedean local field `F`, a simple stratum `[𝔄,n,0,β]` in `End_F(V)`, and a defining sequence whose first element is `β`. The base additive character `ψ` is trivial on `𝔭_F` and nontrivial on `𝒪_F`, as in Bushnell–Kutzko (1993), printed pp. 19–20. Write `𝔓^a` for the filtration step of integer degree `a` and `ℌ^k`, `𝔍^k` for the layers attached to the supplied sequence at natural degree `k`. The integer depth `d` is `−k₀(β,𝔄)` when finite and `n` when `β` is scalar. The two cutoffs are `j = ⌊(d+1)/2⌋` and `h = ⌊d/2⌋+1`.

Write `a_β(x) = βx−xβ`. The character dual `L*` consists of the elements `z` for which `ψ(tr(zy)) = 1` for every `y ∈ L`. For `k ≤ j`, `SimpleStratum.traceDualLattice_simpleJLayer` proves `(𝔍^k)* = a_β(ℌ^h) + 𝔓^(1−k)`, the part of (3.1.21) at or below the `J` cutoff. `SimpleStratum.mem_simpleHLayer_iff_adjoint_annihilates` gives the corresponding adjoint-annihilator description for `x ∈ 𝔍^k` in that range, as in (3.1.22). Both formulas are on printed p. 97 (PDF p. 109). The registered source `bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln` has SHA-256 `811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`.

At the `J` cutoff the terminal `J` layer is `𝔓^j`. In a nonterminal step the layers at the cutoffs agree with those for the next term. Its parameter differs from `β` by an element of `𝔓^(−d)`, so the two adjoint images on `ℌ^h` agree modulo `𝔓^(h−d) = 𝔓^(1−j)`. This transfers the dual formula at the cutoff from the shorter sequence.

Below the cutoff, `𝔍^k` is the sum of the centralizer lattice at degree `k` and `𝔍^j`. The relative dual writes an element of `(𝔍^k)*` as `p+v` with `p ∈ 𝔓^(1−k)` and `v` in the range of `a_β`. The cutoff dual gives another decomposition `a_β(x)+w` with `x ∈ ℌ^h` and `w ∈ 𝔓^(1−j)`. Since `k ≤ j`, the residual adjoint image `v−a_β(x) = w−p` lies in `𝔓^(1−j)`. The mixed lifting theorem lifts it into `ℌ^h` using `h−d = 1−j`. The filtration summand in the resulting formula is `𝔓^(1−k)`.

For the annihilator calculation, suppose `x ∈ 𝔍^k` and `a_β(x) ∈ (𝔍^k)*`. After subtracting an adjoint image from `ℌ^h`, lift the remaining image in `𝔓^(1−k)` at depth `d+1−k`, which is at least `h`. Subtracting both lifts from `x` leaves a centralizer element at degree `k`, so `x ∈ ℌ^k`. Conversely the centralizer summand of `ℌ^k` has zero adjoint image, and the adjoint image of `ℌ^h` lies in `(𝔍^k)*` by the dual formula.

`adjointCharacterPair ψ b x y` is the trace character evaluated on `a_b(x)y`, or equivalently on `b(xy−yx)`. Its values multiply under addition in either argument, and swapping the arguments inverts the value. `SimpleStratum.mem_simpleHLayer_iff_adjointCharacterPair` proves for each natural `k` that an element `x ∈ 𝔍^k` lies in `ℌ^k` exactly when `adjointCharacterPair ψ β x y = 1` for every `y ∈ 𝔍^k`. Above the `J` cutoff, the difference of the adjoint products for successive parameters lies in `𝔓¹` because both arguments are at depth `k` and `2k > d`. Its trace lies in `𝔭_F`, where `ψ` is trivial. The test thus transfers to the shorter sequence. In the terminal case above the cutoff both layers equal `𝔓^k`, and the adjoint product for `β ∈ 𝔓^(−n)` lies in `𝔓¹` since `2k > n`. This is the additive version of the high-level step of (3.4.1), printed pp. 115–116 (PDF pp. 127–128).
