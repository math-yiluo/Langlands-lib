# Deep unit subgroups and simple characters

Successive positive-depth unit quotients are finite. The map given by reduction of `g - 1` takes values in an endomorphism filtration quotient which is a finite-dimensional vector space over the residue field. Multiplication of subgroup indices gives finiteness for every positive depth relative to depth one.

At a sufficiently deep step the simple character is trivial. In the terminal clause, the defining trace lands in the maximal ideal by the trace dual of the endomorphism filtration. The recursive clauses use the same bound for the trace twist and the induction hypothesis for the character of the shorter tail. The needed deep unit subgroup lies in the character's domain because each lattice in the recursion contains a sufficiently deep filtration step.

These are consequences of the definitions associated to Bushnell–Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), (1.1.4)–(1.1.6), printed p. 22 (PDF p. 34), and (3.1.7), (3.2.1), (3.2.3), printed pp. 90 and 98–99 (PDF pp. 102 and 110–111).
