# Induction along the tail of a defining sequence

The induction along a defining sequence in Bushnell–Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), (2.4.2), printed pp. 65–66 (PDF pp. 77–78), and §3.1, printed p. 90 (PDF p. 102), uses the same hereditary order and level after each approximation. The registered source `bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln` has SHA-256 `811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`.

`Stratum.DefiningSequenceChoice.exists_suffix` implements the induction on the supplied finite sequence. The new first stratum has the next element, the original chain and level, and depth zero. The common order identifies the endomorphism filtration and the stabilizer, so this replacement preserves purity and the critical inequality. The new sequence uses this stratum as its head and retains each following stratum verbatim. The depth of the discarded critical approximation is positive, and the remaining depths are strictly increasing. The equality of tails records that the recursion computes on the very same elements. `terminal_critical` gives the terminal alternative directly from the final condition on the defining sequence.
