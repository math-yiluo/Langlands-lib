# Coherence of simple character sets (U7)

Source: C. J. Bushnell and P. C. Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), Theorem (3.5.8), printed p. 120 (PDF p. 132).

`SimpleCharacterCoherenceStatement` takes two simple strata at the same level `n` and nonnegative depth `m` in the same hereditary order of `End_F(V)`. It quantifies over the base additive character with the specified conductor and over the two defining sequences. The orders, not their choices of lattice-chain index origins, are required to agree. An equality `h` of the subgroups `H^{m+1}(β₁,𝔄)` and `H^{m+1}(β₂,𝔄)` transports the first character set to the domain of the second. If their intersection there is nonempty, the transported set equals the second set. No intertwining or conjugacy premise is added.
