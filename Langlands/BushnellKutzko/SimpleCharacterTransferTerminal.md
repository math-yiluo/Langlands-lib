# Transfer for terminal defining sequences

The source is Bushnell and Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), `bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln`, SHA-256 `811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`, the terminal case of (3.6.1) on printed pp. 131–133 (PDF pp. 143–145). `simpleCharacterTransfer_nil` proves existence and uniqueness on the literal intersection when both defining sequences are terminal. The input hypotheses specify the common generated field and its normalization of the chains, the two exact initial levels, and equality of normalized character depths. Scalar elements are included.

Write `jᵢ = max(mᵢ, ⌊nᵢ/2⌋)`. The relative chain periods identify `nᵢ/eᵢ` with the negative uniformizer order of the same element of `F[β]`. The two high depths `jᵢ` consequently have equal normalized levels as well. At the initial depths the two determinant images are equal, so the given simple character's relative determinant character gives a character on the second relative group. At the higher depths, a relative unit in the second group has a determinant lift to the common high relative subgroup. The two trace formulas have equal values on units with that determinant. It follows that the prescribed determinant and trace characters on the second domain agree on their intersection.

The second relative group normalizes its high ambient group, and the trace character is invariant under that action. Gluing the two restrictions therefore gives a simple character on the second domain. The intersection factorization gives a common relative-unit factor and a common high factor for each element of the testing subgroup. The characters agree on the first factor by their common determinant character and on the second by the trace formula. The terminal uniqueness lemma then gives the required uniqueness.
