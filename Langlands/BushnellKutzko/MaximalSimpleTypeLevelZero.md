# The intertwining of maximal level-zero types

`tensorPowerOneEquiv` identifies the one-fold tensor product with its single factor by evaluation. The period-one lattice argument lifts the chosen layer basis, identifies the given reduction with matrix reduction, and transports the cuspidal intertwining calculation to automorphisms of the lattice chain.

A level-zero simple type is equivalent to the resulting cuspidal inflation as a pair. Isomorphism of pairs preserves self-intertwining, so the intertwining set is the product of the scalar units and the subgroup of the type. This gives the period-one case of Bushnell–Kutzko (1993), (5.5.11), printed p. 185, for simple types of kind (b), together with the reverse inclusion by centrality of the scalars.
