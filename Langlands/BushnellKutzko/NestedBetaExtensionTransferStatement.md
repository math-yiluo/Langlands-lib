# Transported `β`-extensions are `β`-extensions

Module: `Langlands.BushnellKutzko.NestedBetaExtensionTransferStatement`.

Source: C. J. Bushnell and P. C. Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open
subgroups* (1993), Proposition (5.2.7), printed p. 170 (PDF p. 182), and the reduction in its
proof "by (5.2.5)(ii)" to the maximal order, carried out as in (5.2.12), printed p. 174
(PDF p. 186). Definition (5.2.1) of `β`-extensions is on printed p. 166.

## Statement

`NestedBetaExtensionTransferStatement` quantifies over the following data:
- a nonarchimedean local field `F` and an `F`-vector space `V`, each in its own universe;
- an additive character `ψ_F` with the conductor fixed by `IsBKAdditiveCharacter`;
- simple strata `[𝔄₁, n₁, 0, β]` and `[𝔄₂, n₂, 0, β]` in `A = End_F(V)` with the same element
  `β`, every lattice of the chain of `𝔄₁` being a lattice of the chain of `𝔄₂`, together with
  defining-sequence choices. Write `B` for the centralizer of `β` and `𝔅ᵢ = 𝔄ᵢ ∩ B`.
- a simple character `θ₂ ∈ C(𝔄₂, 0, β)` and a representation `η₂` of `J¹(β, 𝔄₂)` lying over it
  (`IsHeisenbergRepresentation`);
- any representation `η₁` of `J¹(β, 𝔄₁)` and a `β`-extension `κ₁` of `η₁` (`IsBetaExtension`);
- a representation `κ₂` of `J(β, 𝔄₂)` on a space in its own universe, such that:
  - its restriction to `J¹(β, 𝔄₂)` is equivalent to `η₂`;
  - for all proofs of the inclusions involved, the representation of `Γ = U(𝔅₂) U¹(𝔄₂)` (the
    subgroup generated by `B^× ∩ U(𝔄₂)` and `U¹(𝔄₂)`) coinduced from the restriction of `κ₁` to
    `U(𝔅₂) J¹(β, 𝔄₁)` is equivalent to the representation coinduced from `κ₂`.

The conclusion is that `κ₂` is a `β`-extension of `η₂`. That is, `κ₂` is finite-dimensional and
smooth, restricts to `η₂` up to equivalence, and is intertwined by every element of `B^×`.

The second hypothesis on `κ₂` is the conclusion of `NestedBetaExtensionInductionStatement`
(BK93 (5.2.5)). The two statements together say that the representation obtained from a
`β`-extension by (5.2.5) is again a `β`-extension.

## Relation to the source

BK93 (5.2.7) asserts `I_G(κ) = J B^× J` for the representation `κ` of (5.2.3), which is obtained
from a representation `κ_M` of a maximal order by (5.2.5). Its proof first reduces to the maximal
order, and then treats that case at length. The present statement contains the reduction, for
any pair of nested orders: if `κ₁` is intertwined by `B^×`, so is every `κ₂` related to it as in
(5.2.5). Neither `𝔅₁` nor `𝔅₂` is required to be maximal. The maximal case of (5.2.7) (that
`κ_M` is intertwined by `B^×`) is not part of this statement.
