# Self-intertwining at all depths

`simpleCharacterSelfIntertwining` inducts on defining-sequence length. At finite critical depth `r` it applies the high formula directly for `m ≥ ⌊r/2⌋`. For `m < ⌊r/2⌋`, the outer subgroups at character indices `m` and `⌊r/2⌋` both equal cutoff J because the mixed lattice lies inside the cutoff J layer. Restriction to `H^{⌊r/2⌋+1}` gives the upper inclusion. On an overlap with a centralizer conjugate, the determinant accounts for the relative factor and the high formula accounts for the deeper factor. Hence the centralizer intertwines the original character. Invariance under J gives the lower inclusion. The scalar case uses the determinant and the singleton outer factor.

This gives the full intertwining formula in BK93 (3.3.2), printed pp. 105–112, including its low-depth overlap calculation.
