# The label bound for simple-character classes

`SimpleCharacterLabelBoundStatement` applies to a principal hereditary order
of period `e(𝔄)` in dimension `N ≥ 1` over a nonarchimedean local field,
with admissible `ψ_F` and levels `0 ≤ m < n`. It asserts the
existence of a function from `S^m_n(𝔄)` to the `labelSet` with parameters
`m, n, e(𝔄), N/e(𝔄), q_F`, where `q_F` is the cardinality of the residue
field. Pairs with the same label are `U(𝔄)`-conjugate.

For any pair in `Ĉ(𝔄,m,β)` its label `p` has `Label.d p = f(F[β]|F)` and
`e(F[β]|F) · Label.e p = e(𝔄)`. The printed (2.5) gives the residue degree
and expresses the ramification index as the quotient of `e(𝔄)` by
`Label.e p` in the principal case. The multiplicative identity can also
be recovered from the stratum label invariants in (1.14), using equality
of field invariants for a common character by BK93 (3.5.1).
