# Character intertwining and a dual lattice coset

`mem_congruence_overlap_iff` identifies the overlap of two congruence groups by subtracting one from their units. Every element of the intersection lattice lifts to a unit when the lattice lies in the first radical step. Under the covariance identity, the character values agree on the overlap precisely when the conjugation difference of the coefficient annihilates the intersection.

`mem_characterSelfIntertwining_iff_latticeCosetIntertwining` uses the supplied dual-of-intersection equality to turn annihilation into membership in the sum of two dual lattices. Decomposing the difference in this sum gives a common representative of the two affine cosets, and a common representative yields the same decomposition. This is the linear algebra in BK93 (3.3.5), printed pp. 107–108.
