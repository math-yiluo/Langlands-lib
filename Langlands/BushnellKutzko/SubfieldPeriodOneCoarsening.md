# Coarsening to an order of relative period one

The larger relative order in Bushnell–Kutzko (1993), (5.1.12), can be
chosen to have relative period one. To construct it, start with a
lattice chain normalized by the units of a subfield \(E\) and retain
the translates of the zeroth lattice by powers of a uniformizer of
\(E\). The extension uniformizer gives a positive shift \(d\) on the
original chain. Its base-field period is \(d\,e(E/F)\).

The reindexed chain has base-field period \(e(E/F)\). The shift of
every element of \(E^\times\) is a multiple of \(d\), so the new chain
is also normalized by \(E^\times\). The relative chain theorem of
(1.2.4) then gives its period over \(E\) as one.

The new order contains the original order since its defining lattice
conditions are imposed on a subsequence. A map lowering the new chain
by one step maps each original lattice into its successor. Each
original lattice lies between two successive retained lattices, and
its successor still contains the lower retained lattice. The two
filtration inclusions give the corresponding bounds for the
depth-zero and depth-one unit groups.
