# Fieldness and the critical bound for the cyclic lift

This is an internal proof module for BK93 (2.4.1)(iii). It assembles the cyclic model, the two leading-symbol comparisons, and the leading-commutant calculation. The use of graded centralizer information to bound the critical exponent is related to BK93 §2.5 (printed pp. 77–85); the Lean argument checks the required bound directly on the constructed representative.

Given a shift unit and a lifted monic irreducible polynomial for its normalized residual operator, `exists_simple_cyclic_lift` constructs a representative with the original leading symbol. Every nonzero element of the algebra generated by the representative is in the cyclic action range and acts by a chain shift. The field and normalizer criterion thus applies to that algebra. For a depth-zero endomorphism whose commutator with the representative is one step deeper than its shift, the power and product identities give leading commutation with both cyclic generators. The row calculation gives leading commutation with every unit of the actual generated field. `exists_subfield_lift_layers` then produces an endomorphism commuting with the representative and inducing the same maps on the depth-zero layers. This places the relevant adjoint lattice in the sum of the depth-zero centralizer lattice and the first filtration step, yielding the claimed critical-exponent bound.

Source: Bushnell–Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), registered as `bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln`.
