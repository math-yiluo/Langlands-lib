# Invariance of the field invariants of `F[β]` under conjugation

`ramificationIdx_map_algEquiv` proves that Mathlib's `Ideal.ramificationIdx` of an ideal `q` of
an `R`-algebra `S` equals that of its image `e(q)` under an isomorphism of `R`-algebras
`e : S ≃ S'`. If `q` is not prime, both indices are zero. Otherwise the proof follows Mathlib's
`Ideal.ramificationIdx_smul` (Mathlib commit `56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679`). It
uses `e` to identify the localizations at `q` and `e(q)` and the quotients by the image of
`q ∩ R`. It also checks that `e(q)` and `q` have the same contraction to `R`.

For fields `E`, `E'` over `F` and an isomorphism of `F`-algebras `e : E ≃ E'`,
`subfieldIntegersAlgEquiv` is the induced isomorphism of the rings of integers
`subfieldIntegers` (integral closures of `𝔬_F`). By `map_subfieldMaximalIdeal` it carries
`subfieldMaximalIdeal`, the Jacobson radical, onto the Jacobson radical. Hence
`subfieldRamificationIndex_congr` and `subfieldResidueDegree_congr` prove that the ramification
index and the residue degree of `SubfieldIntegers` and `ElementFieldInvariants` agree for `E`
and `E'`. The residue degree is compared through the quotient isomorphism
`𝔬_E/𝔭_E ≃ 𝔬_{E'}/𝔭_{E'}` of `𝔬_F`-modules.

For an `F`-linear automorphism `f` of `V`, `elementAlgebraAlgConj f β` is the isomorphism of
`F`-algebras `F[β] ≃ F[fβf⁻¹]` given by conjugation. `elementRamificationIndex_endConj` and
`elementResidueDegree_endConj` prove `e(F[fβf⁻¹]|F) = e(F[β]|F)` and
`f(F[fβf⁻¹]|F) = f(F[β]|F)` for `elementRamificationIndex` and `elementResidueDegree`. When
`F[β]` is not a field, neither is `F[fβf⁻¹]`, and both sides are the value zero assigned by
those definitions.

These are the invariants of the field generated by `β` used in BK93 (2.1.4), printed p. 50, and
(2.4.1), printed p. 65. Bushnell and Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open
subgroups* (1993). Literature ID `bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln`, SHA-256
`811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`.
