# Square-zero conjugation of simple H groups

For a simple stratum of index zero and a defining sequence,
`SplitBlocks.mem_normalizer_simpleHGroup_of_sq_eq_zero` gives a
lattice criterion for a unit \(1+w\) with \(w^2=0\) to normalize \(H^m\).
At positive depth, the criterion requires \(wx\), \(xw\), and \(wxw\)
to belong to the \(H\) lattice at depth \(m\) for every \(x\) in that lattice.

The proof uses the carrier formula for \(H^m\) and expands the conjugations
by \(1+w\) and its inverse \(1-w\). This provides the conjugation calculation
for upper-block units in Bushnell–Kutzko (1993), (8.2.26), using the lattice
estimates in (8.2.28).
