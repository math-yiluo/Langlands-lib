# Trace duality and conjugation

The auxiliary lemmas apply to a submodule-valued dual operator with the indicated pointwise descriptions. Additivity gives the rule for sums, and cyclicity of trace gives the rule for conjugation. For a finitely generated valuation-ring submodule, the double-dual argument separates a nonmember by a linear functional that maps the submodule into the maximal ideal. The nondegenerate trace product represents this functional. Applying double-duality to the two duals and their sum gives the intersection rule when both lattices and both duals are finitely generated.

These are the trace-duality operations used in BK93 (1.1.4), printed p. 22 (PDF p. 34), (3.1.19), printed pp. 96–97, and (3.3.5), printed pp. 107–108.
