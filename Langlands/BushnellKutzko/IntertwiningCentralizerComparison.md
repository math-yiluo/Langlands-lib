# Comparing `𝔅 + 𝔓` through intertwining sets

The module `IntertwiningCentralizerComparison` collects the lattice arguments behind BK93
(3.5.1), printed p. 117 (PDF p. 129). Throughout, `L` is an `𝔬_F`-lattice chain in a
finite-dimensional space `V` over a nonarchimedean local field `F`, with order `𝔄`
(`L.endFiltration 0`) and radical `𝔓` (`L.endFiltration 1`). For `β ∈ End_F(V)`,
`𝔅_β = 𝔄 ∩ ker a_β` is `centralizerLattice L β 0` and `B_β^×` is `centralizerUnitGroup β`.
All declarations are in the namespace `Langlands.BushnellKutzko.CentralizerResidue`.

## Equal double cosets give equal `𝔅 + 𝔓`

- `centralizerLattice_le_sup`: if every element of `Y ⊆ G` is congruent to `1` modulo `𝔓`
  and `B_{β₁}^× ⊆ Y B_{β₂}^× Y`, then `𝔅_{β₁} ⊆ 𝔅_{β₂} + 𝔓`.
- `sup_eq_of_mul_mul_eq`: if `1 ∈ Y_i`, every element of `Y_i` is congruent to `1` modulo `𝔓`,
  and `Y₁ B_{β₁}^× Y₁ = Y₂ B_{β₂}^× Y₂`, then `𝔅_{β₁} + 𝔓 = 𝔅_{β₂} + 𝔓`.

This is the step "`𝔅_{β₁} + 𝔓 = 𝔅_{β₂} + 𝔓`" in the proof of (3.5.1). The source passes
through the additive group generated by the intertwining set inside `𝔄` and a density
argument. The Lean proof writes each `x ∈ 𝔅_{β₁}` as `t − u` with `t ∈ 𝔬_F` and `u` a unit
of `End_F(V)`, choosing `t` outside the finitely many eigenvalues of `x`
(`exists_algebraMap_sub_isUnit`; `𝔬_F` is infinite by `infinite_integers`). Then `u` lies in
`B_{β₁}^×`, so `u = y b y'` with `y, y' ∈ U^1(𝔄)` (`mem_unitFiltration_one_of_sub_mem`) and
`b ∈ B_{β₂}^×`. Hence `b ∈ 𝔅_{β₂}` and `u ≡ b` modulo `𝔓` (`mul_mul_sub_mem`). Scalars in
`𝔬_F` lie in every `𝔅_β` (`algebraMap_mem_centralizerLattice`).

For subgroups `J_i ≤ U^1(𝔄)` and products `J_i B_{β_i}^× J_i`, the module
`CentralizerDoubleCoset` proves the same equality at every filtration level
(`centralizerLattice_sup_eq_of_doubleCoset_eq`). The lemmas here accept arbitrary sets
`Y_i`, as the outer factor of (3.3.2) is a set, `simpleCharacterIntertwiningFactor`.

## The outer factor of (3.3.2)

`simpleCharacterIntertwiningFactor S q m` is the set `Y` in BK93 (3.3.2), printed p. 105.
It contains `1` (`Langlands.BushnellKutzko.one_mem_simpleCharacterIntertwiningFactor`). Below both the level and the
critical depth `r = −k₀(β,𝔄)`, every element of `Y` is congruent to `1` modulo `𝔓`
(`sub_one_mem_of_mem_simpleCharacterIntertwiningFactor`). Indeed
`𝔔^{r−m} 𝔑_{k₀} ⊆ 𝔓^{r−m} ⊆ 𝔓` and `𝔍^{⌊(r+1)/2⌋} ⊆ 𝔓`, since `r > m ≥ 0`.
`lt_finiteCriticalDepth` converts the bound on the critical depth into one on its finite
value, and `criticalExponent_eq_of_simpleCriticalDepth_eq` shows that the critical depth
determines `k₀`.

## Invariants from `𝔅 + 𝔓`

- `invariants_eq_of_sup_eq`: two pure strata on one lattice chain with
  `𝔅_β + 𝔓 = 𝔅_{β'} + 𝔓` have `e(F[β]|F) = e(F[β']|F)` and `f(F[β]|F) = f(F[β']|F)`.
  This is BK93 (2.1.4)(iii), printed p. 50, in the form used in (3.5.1): the Library's
  `StratumEquivalence.ramificationIndex_eq_of_sup_eq` and
  `StratumEquivalence.residueDegree_eq_of_sup_eq` read both invariants off `𝓛` and
  `𝔅 + 𝔓`. Equivalence of the strata is not needed.
- `sup_eq_top_of_elementAlgebra_eq_bot`: `𝔅_β + 𝔓 = 𝔄` for scalar `β`.
- `elementAlgebra_eq_bot_of_sup_eq_top`: a pure stratum with `𝔅_β + 𝔓 = 𝔄` has scalar `β`,
  since then no `𝔑_k(β,𝔄) ⊆ 𝔄` escapes `𝔅_β + 𝔓`, while a nonscalar pure parameter has a
  critical index (BK93 (1.4.5), printed p. 36).
- `sup_ne_of_criticalExponent_eq`: let `[𝔄,n,r,β]` be pure with `k₀(β,𝔄) = −r` and let `γ`
  be a pure parameter on the same chain with `β − γ ∈ 𝔓^{−r}` and `k₀(γ,𝔄) < −r`. Then
  `𝔅_γ + 𝔓 ≠ 𝔅_β + 𝔓`. Otherwise `𝔑_{−r}(β) = 𝔑_{−r}(γ) ⊆ 𝔅_γ + 𝔓 = 𝔅_β + 𝔓` (BK93 (2.1.3)),
  so `−r` would not be a critical index of `β`.

The last lemma replaces the degree comparison in the source proof of the equality of the
critical exponents. BK93 derives a contradiction from `[F[γ]:F] = [F[β]:F]` using (2.4.1) and
(2.2.8); here equality of `𝔅 + 𝔓` contradicts the definition of `k₀` directly.

## Simple characters as pairs

`CharacterPair G` records a character with its domain. `pair_eq_of_apply` builds an
equality of pairs from equal domains and equal values. `pair_restrict_eq` restricts equal
pairs to equal subgroups. `characterSelfIntertwining_eq_of_pair_eq` notes that the
self-intertwining set depends only on the pair.

## Consequences of (3.3.2)

`sup_eq_of_pair_eq` takes `SimpleCharacterIntertwiningStatement` (BK93 (3.3.2)) as a
hypothesis. It considers simple strata `[𝔄,n,0,β]` and `[𝔄,n',0,β']` on one chain with
defining sequences, an index `m` below both levels and both critical depths, and simple
characters `θ ∈ C(𝔄,m,β)` and `θ' ∈ C(𝔄,m,β')` forming the same pair. It concludes that
`𝔅_β + 𝔓 = 𝔅_{β'} + 𝔓`: both intertwining formulas describe `I_G(θ)`.
`lt_simpleCriticalDepth_of_isSimple` gives the bound on the critical depth for a simple
stratum.

`exists_pair_eq_at_jump` passes to the first jump. Let `θ ∈ C(𝔄,m,β)` be computed from a
tail `γ :: tail` with finite critical depth `r > m`, `β − γ ∈ 𝔓^{−r}` and
`H^{r+1}(β) = H^{r+1}(γ)`. Suppose simple characters for `γ` restrict to index `r`. Then the
restriction of `θ` to `H^{r+1}(β)` is, as a pair, a simple character for `γ` at index `r`.
The proof unfolds the recursive definition (BK93 (3.2.1), (3.2.3), printed pp. 98–99): `θ`
agrees with `θ₀ ψ_{β−γ}` on `H^{j+1}(β)` for some `θ₀ ∈ C(𝔄,j,γ)`, where
`j = max(m, ⌊r/2⌋)`, and `ψ_{β−γ}` is trivial on `U^{r+1}(𝔄)` (`elementCharacter_eq_one_of_depth`).

This module does not prove (3.3.2) or the restriction property for `γ`; the lemmas above
take them as hypotheses.
