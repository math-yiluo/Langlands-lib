# The relative determinant criterion for simple-character domains

Bushnell–Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups*
(1993), (2.4.11), (3.3.18), and (3.3.20), printed pp. 72–73, 78, and
112–114 (PDF pp. 84–85, 90, and 124–126). Literature ID
`bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln`, SHA-256
`811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`.

This module applies the determinant criterion of (2.4.11) over the field
`E = F[β]` generated by an element `β` of `End_F(V)`. This is how (3.3.18)
and (3.3.20) use it. Both theorems take
`IntertwinedUnitCharacterStatement` as a hypothesis, at the universe
levels `(uV, uV)` because `E` is a subalgebra of `End_F(V)`. The theorem
`intertwinedUnitCharacter` in the module
`Langlands.BushnellKutzko.IntertwinedUnitCharacter` proves it.
The conclusion `factorsThroughCentralizerDet β θ` says that `θ`,
restricted to the elements of its domain coming from units of `End_E(V)`,
factors through the determinant of `End_E(V)` on that subgroup.

`factorsThroughCentralizerDet_of_relative_intertwining` assumes that
`F[β]` is a field whose units stabilize the lattice chain `L`. It also
assumes `k ≥ 1` and a subgroup `H` with
`relativeUnitFiltration L β k ≤ H ≤ U^k(L)`. Finally, `θ : H →* ℂˣ` must
be intertwined by every unit of the centralizer of `β`. Under these
hypotheses `θ` factors through the relative determinant. Restriction of
scalars identifies the units of `End_E(V)` with the centralizer units
(`relativeEndUnits_range_eq_centralizer`). Under it the unit filtration
`U^k` of the `E`-lattice chain underlying `L` is the preimage of
`U^k(L)`. By the two inclusions it is also the preimage of `H`. Pulling
`θ` back gives a character of this filtration subgroup that every unit
of `End_E(V)` intertwines. The statement over `E` factors it through the
determinant image. Composing with the inclusion of the determinant image
of the preimage of `H` into that of the filtration subgroup gives the
required homomorphism.

`factorsThroughCentralizerDet_of_intertwined_simpleHGroup` specializes
this to a pure stratum `S` with generator `β = S.b`, an arbitrary list
`tail : List A`, and the subgroup
`H = simpleHGroup S.chain S.n S.b tail (m + 1)`. Purity supplies the
field and stabilizer hypotheses. The inclusions
`simpleHGroup_le_unitFiltration` and
`relativeUnitFiltration_le_simpleHGroup` give the two filtration
inclusions. Both inclusions and the pullback argument hold for every
pure `S` and every `tail`, so the theorem is stated in that generality.

The group `simpleHGroup S.chain S.n S.b tail (m + 1)` is BK93's
`H^{m+1}(β, 𝔄)` only on the source input of (3.1.7) and (3.1.14),
printed pp. 90 and 95: `S.r = 0`, `S.IsSimple`, and `tail = q.tail` for a
defining sequence `q : S.DefiningSequenceChoice`. For other lists the
lattice recursion of `simpleStratumLattices` still produces a subgroup,
but it need not be `H^{m+1}(β, 𝔄)`. For example, the recursion treats
an empty `tail` as the terminal (minimal) case. So for a simple stratum
whose element `β` is not minimal, the empty list can give a group other
than BK93's.
