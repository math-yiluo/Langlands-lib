# Coprimality and residue generation on the layers

Consider two field units whose shifts are `t * ν` and `t * e`, where `t`
is the shift of an extension uniformizer. Their shift-zero combination
has a residue in the extension residue field. If every layer family
intertwining both units intertwines the whole field, the indicator
family forces `gcd(ν, e)` to be one. Bézout then gives a field unit with
shift `t`. If the residue generates a proper subalgebra, the orbit
construction for this unit produces an endomorphism family that still
intertwines the original two units but fails to intertwine an integer
unit.

Conversely, coprimality and residue generation force the commuting
subgroup of any such family to contain all integer-ring units and a
unit of shift `t`. Uniformizer-power factorization gives the entire
field-unit group.

This is a derived layer criterion for Bushnell–Kutzko (1993),
(1.4.14)–(1.4.15), printed p. 41.
