# Determinant factorization of intertwined unit characters

Bushnell–Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups*
(1993), (1.1.5), (2.4.11) and §2.5, printed pp. 22, 72–73 and 76–78
(PDF pp. 34, 84–85 and 88–90). Literature ID
`bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln`, SHA-256
`811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`.

For a lattice chain over a nonarchimedean local field and an index `k ≥ 1`,
let `χ` be a complex-valued multiplicative character on `U^k`. Suppose that
for every ambient invertible endomorphism `g`, the characters `χ` and its
conjugate agree on the intersection of their domains. The statement asserts
that `χ` factors through the image of the determinant of `U^k` in the units
of the base field. It uses `k = m + 1` in the notation of (2.4.11).

BK93 formulates the result for smooth characters. The statement extends
it to abstract homomorphisms into `ℂˣ` using elementary elimination in a
splitting basis. Write `Tᵢⱼ(a)` for an off-diagonal transvection belonging
to `U^k`. Multiplication of its entry by the integer-ring unit
`(π - 1)⁻¹` preserves the relevant filtration lattice. Conjugating
`Tᵢⱼ(a / (π - 1))` by the matrix that scales coordinate `i` by `π`
gives `Tᵢⱼ(a / (π - 1)) Tᵢⱼ(a)`. Full intertwining gives equality on
these two elements of `U^k`, so the character kills `Tᵢⱼ(a)`.
The diagonal entries of a positive-filtration unit are units of the
valuation ring. Row elimination therefore uses transvections in the
same subgroup. A diagonal in the subgroup is a product of single-coordinate
scales there, and intertwining with permutation matrices moves each scale
to one fixed coordinate with its value unchanged. Their combined entry
is the determinant, so the character descends to its image.
