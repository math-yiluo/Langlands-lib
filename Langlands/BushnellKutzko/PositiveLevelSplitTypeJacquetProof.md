# Jacquet nonvanishing for a split type of positive level

`positiveLevelSplitTypeJacquet` proves `PositiveLevelSplitTypeJacquetStatement`
with independent universes for the local field and the two representation
carriers. The conclusion is nonzero coinvariants for a conjugate of the
unipotent radical of a proper standard block parabolic.

`positiveLevelSplitTypeJacquet_of_unequal` splits the positive-level
type predicate into equal and unequal levels. At equal levels,
`SplitBlocks.diagonalSplitType_jacquet_nonvanishing` obtains nonvanishing
from the filtration of the upper block unipotent subgroup of a splitting
of the stratum. At unequal levels, `SplitBlocks.unequalPositiveSplitTypeJacquet`
uses the splitting of the corestricted stratum, conjugation to a
block-diagonal twist, and the shifted character transfers to obtain
nonvanishing for the corresponding upper block radical. The reduction
applies these results to the given type and its containment in the
ambient representation.
