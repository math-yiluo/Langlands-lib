# Invariants of equivalent strata: proof

Proof of the published statement `Langlands.BushnellKutzko.StratumEquivalenceStatement`
(Bushnell–Kutzko 1993, (1.5.2), printed p. 43, and (2.1.4), printed p. 50), as the theorem
`Langlands.BushnellKutzko.stratumEquivalence : StratumEquivalenceStatement.{uF, uV}`, with no
additional hypotheses.

**Statement.** Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field, `V` a finite-dimensional `F`-space,
and `S`, `T` equivalent strata (equal cosets `b + 𝔓^{-r}`). Then `S` and `T` have the same
order and the same `r`; if both parameters have exact level (`v_𝔄(b) = -n`), the same `n`;
and if both are simple, the same `𝔅 + 𝔓` (with `𝔅 = 𝔄 ∩ ker a_b`), the same critical
exponent, and their fields `F[b]` have the same ramification index and residue degree.

**Proof as implemented.**
1. Order, `r` and `n`: `order_eq_of_equivalent`, `r_eq_of_equivalent`,
   `n_eq_of_equivalent` from `StratumEquivalenceOrder` (BK93 (1.5.2)).
2. `𝔅 + 𝔓` and critical exponent: `sup_eq_and_criticalExponent_eq_of_equivalent` from
   `StratumEquivalenceCritical` (BK93 (2.1.3), (2.1.4)(i)–(ii)).
3. Ramification index and residue degree (BK93 (2.1.4)(iii)): `invariants_eq_of_equivalent`
   in this module. A simple stratum is pure, so `F[S.b]` is a field whose units normalize
   `S.chain`, and likewise for `T`. Both fields are finite-dimensional over `F`, being
   subalgebras of `End_F(V)`. The proof works on the single chain `S.chain`:
   - `mem_stabilizer_iff_of_equivalent`: equivalent strata have the same stabilizer
     `𝔎(𝔄)`, because by the published `normalizer` (item 2) the stabilizer of a chain is the
     normalizer of its order, and the orders agree. Hence `F[T.b]^×` also normalizes
     `S.chain`.
   - `fieldOrder_elementAlgebra`: for the field `F[b]`, the order
     `fieldOrder 𝓛 F[b] = 𝔄 ∩ End_{F[b]}(V)` of `StratumEquivalenceResidue` equals the
     published `centralizerLattice 𝓛 b 0 = 𝔄 ∩ ker a_b`: an endomorphism commutes with `F[b]`
     iff it commutes with `b`, since `F[b]` is generated by `b`.
   - The two strata have the same filtration (`endFiltration_eq_of_equivalent`), so the
     equality of `𝔅 + 𝔓` from step 2 transfers to `S.chain`:
     `fieldOrder S.chain F[S.b] + 𝔓 = fieldOrder S.chain F[T.b] + 𝔓`
     (`centralizerLattice_congr`).
   - `ramificationIndex_eq_of_sup_eq` (`StratumEquivalenceResidue`) and
     `residueDegree_eq_of_sup_eq` (`StratumEquivalenceResidueDegree`) give the equalities of
     `e` and `f`. The published `elementRamificationIndex`, `elementResidueDegree` unfold to
     `subfieldRamificationIndex`, `subfieldResidueDegree` because `F[b]` is a field.

**Helper modules** (each with its own Markdown file):
`StratumEquivalenceOrder`, `StratumEquivalenceCritical`, `StratumEquivalenceSplitting`
(public versions of the private splitting-basis lemmas of `RelativeFiltrationInversion`),
`StratumEquivalenceShift` (`shift = e' · v_E` on `E^×`, `e(E|F) · e' = e(𝓛)`),
`StratumEquivalenceResidue` (ramification index from the layer periods of `𝔅 + 𝔓`) and
`StratumEquivalenceResidueDegree` (`𝔬_E/𝔭_E ≅ Z(𝔅 + 𝔓)/(Z ∩ K)`).

**Relation to the source.** Steps 1 and 2 follow BK93 (1.5.2) and (2.1.3)–(2.1.4)(i)–(ii).
For (2.1.4)(iii), BK93 reads `e(F[β]|F)` and `f(F[β]|F)` off the structure of `𝔅/𝔔`
(Wedderburn theory for the hereditary order `𝔅` of `End_{F[β]}(V)`). The Lean proof uses a
different route that recovers both invariants directly from `𝓛` and `𝔅 + 𝔓`:
`e(E|F) = e(𝓛)/e'`, where the periods `d` of the condition `x L_j ⊆ L_{j+1}` on `𝔅 + 𝔓`
are exactly the multiples of `e'`, and `f(E|F)` is the `𝔬_F`-length of
`Z(𝔅 + 𝔓)/(Z ∩ K)`, a concrete form of the commutant of `𝔅 + 𝔓` acting on `L_0/L_1`.
