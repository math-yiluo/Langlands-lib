# Intertwining of maximal simple types

Source: C. J. Bushnell and P. C. Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993): Proposition (5.5.11), printed p. 185 (PDF p. 197), which puts the `G`-intertwining of a simple type `λ` inside `J W(𝔅_β) J`; the remark in the proof of Theorem (6.2.2), printed p. 203 (PDF p. 215), that for maximal `𝔅_β` the group `W(𝔅_β)` is the cyclic group generated by a prime element `Π(𝔅_β)` of `E` and is contained in `E^×`; and printed p. 200 (PDF p. 212), where the group `E^× J` normalises `F^× J` and the extension `Λ_ω` of `λ` to `F^× J`. Together these give `I_G(λ) = E^× J` and `λ^x ≅ λ` for `x ∈ E^×` in the maximal case.

`MaximalSimpleTypeIntertwiningStatement` quantifies over `F`, `V` (independent universes), `ψF` with `IsBKAdditiveCharacter`, a subgroup `J` of `G = Aut_F(V)`, and a representation `ρ` of `J` on a carrier `W` in an arbitrary universe. It asserts two implications.

- Kind (a). If `(J, ρ)` is a simple type of kind (a) exhibited by a simple stratum `S` and a defining-sequence choice `q` with `e(𝔅_β | 𝔬_E) = 1` (`IsSimpleTypeData ψF S q 1 ρ`, module `SimpleTypes`), let `E^×` be the set of `x ∈ G` lying in `E = F[β] = elementAlgebra S.b`. Then every `x ∈ E^×` normalizes `J` and `ρ`, in the form `IsPairIsomorphic (conjRepresentation x ρ) ρ` (module `PairConjugation`): `xJx⁻¹ = J` and `ρ^{x⁻¹} ≅ ρ`. Since `E^×` is a group, this is the same as `ρ^x ≅ ρ` for all `x ∈ E^×`. Moreover `I_G(ρ) = E^× J` (pointwise product of subsets of `G`).
- Kind (b). If `(J, ρ)` is a simple type of kind (b) exhibited by a lattice chain `L` with `e(𝔄 | 𝔬_F) = 1` (`IsLevelZeroSimpleTypeData L 1 ρ`), then `I_G(ρ) = F^× J`, where `F^×` is the image of `Fˣ` in `G` under `algebraMap F (End_F V)`.

`I_G(ρ)` is `representationSelfIntertwining ρ` (module `RepresentationIntertwining`). The general form of (5.5.11) for non-maximal types is not included. This is the proposition only; its proof is not part of this module.
