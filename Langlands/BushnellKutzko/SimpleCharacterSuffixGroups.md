# High groups of successive defining parameters

`criticalDepth_of_simple` obtains a positive finite critical depth for a nonscalar simple stratum of depth zero. The defining sequence bounds it by the stratum level. At a terminal term with finite critical exponent, `level_eq_of_terminal_of_criticalExponent_eq` identifies the critical depth with that level.

`exists_simpleCharacterSuffix` takes the next defining term, transports its suffix to the original chain and level, and reindexes it at depth zero. The original stratum depth of the next term gives the preceding critical depth `r`, strictly below the stratum level. The defining-sequence step gives the congruence at that depth and the strictly larger critical depth of the successor, which can be infinite. The high lattice identities and positive-depth membership criteria give equality of the `H^{m+1}` groups for `m ≥ ⌊r/2⌋` and of the `J^ℓ` groups for `ℓ ≥ ⌊(r+1)/2⌋`.

These facts use BK93 (2.4.2), printed pp. 65–66, the high-layer comparisons of (3.1.9) and (3.1.10), printed pp. 91–94, and (3.1.14), printed p. 95.
