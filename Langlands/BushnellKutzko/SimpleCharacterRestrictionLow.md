# Restriction and its fibres at and below the critical cutoff

The proof of the restriction formula in BK93 (3.2.5) uses the relative-unit product through the cutoff subgroup. At an intermediate index the same product description follows by including the cutoff subgroup in the intermediate domain. At both indices the relative units are the intersection of the character domain with the centralizer.

The defining conditions identify membership at the shallow index with determinant factorization and a simple deeper restriction. In the nonterminal case the indices use the same twist witness. In the terminal case the subgroup at the cutoff is the absolute unit group on which the trace character is prescribed. The relative normalizer fixes this trace character and acts by determinant-preserving conjugation on the relative-unit factor. The extension through the subgroup product gives surjectivity and the fibre consists of the indicated products with determinant-factoring characters in the restriction kernel.

For a scalar parameter, the defining sequence is terminal and the relative units equal the absolute unit group at each index. The product formula follows directly from nesting of the unit filtration. The precise restriction and fibre formula in `simpleCharacterRestriction_low_of_groupProperties` takes `SimpleStratumGroupPropertiesStatement` for the product and normalizer conditions in the nonscalar case.

The source is Bushnell and Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), (3.1.15) and (3.2.1)–(3.2.5), printed pp. 95 and 98–100. The product argument also applies with an intermediate restriction index at or below the cutoff.
