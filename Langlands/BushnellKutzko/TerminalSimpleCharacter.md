# Terminal simple characters

The terminal subgroup decomposition in `SimpleCharacterTransferMinimal` makes the character domain a product of the relative congruence group at depth `m + 1` and the ambient congruence group at depth `max(m + 1, ⌊n/2⌋ + 1)`. The relative order normalizer preserves the character on the relative factor by determinant and on the deep factor by trace. The trace formula applies when the summed depth `k + ℓ` reaches the deep factor. The bound `k + 2ℓ ≥ n + 1` controls the commutator error as in the minimal case of BK93 (3.2.6), printed p. 100, where `r = n`.

The lattice and character formulas are from BK93 (3.1.7), printed p. 90, and (3.2.1), printed pp. 98–99.
