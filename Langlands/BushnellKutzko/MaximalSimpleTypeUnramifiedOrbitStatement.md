# The unramified twist orbit of a maximal simple type

`MaximalSimpleTypeUnramifiedOrbitStatement` fixes a simple type
\((J,\lambda)\) with data of period one in \(\mathrm{GL}_n(F)\) and an
irreducible smooth \(\pi\) containing it. An irreducible smooth
representation \(\pi'\) contains \(\lambda\) if and only if it is
equivalent to \(\pi\otimes(\chi\circ\det)\) for some quasicharacter
\(\chi:F^\times\to\mathbb C^\times\) trivial on \(\mathfrak o_F^\times\).
Both kinds of data are included. The statement ranges over every
nonarchimedean local field and positive rank with independent carrier
universes for \(\lambda\), \(\pi\) and \(\pi'\). The twist is the
Library's smooth twist and equivalence is `Representation.Equiv` of the
underlying actions.

C. J. Bushnell and P. C. Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact
open subgroups* (1993), (6.2.3), final assertion, printed p. 204.
