# Trace dual calculations at the cutoff

The adjoint images for two parameters that are congruent at an ambient depth agree after adding the corresponding shifted step. The layers above and below a cutoff are obtained by intersection with an ambient step and by adjoining a relative step, respectively.

`traceDual_simpleHLayer_of_cutoff` extends the cutoff formula to all character indices. The upper case takes the dual of the filtration intersection. The lower case takes the dual of the relative sum and uses its complementary-depth adjoint lift to simplify the resulting intersection. These are the upward and downward extensions in the proof of BK93 (3.1.19), printed pp. 96–97.
