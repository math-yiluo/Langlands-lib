# Centralizer intertwining below the cutoff

`centralizer_intertwines_of_deep_intertwining` uses a common relative basis for the pair of ambient filtrations to split the centralizer part of their sum. The deeper H layer contains the full relative step at its depth, so the decomposition restricts to the sum of the deeper layers. This splits the low-depth overlap lattice into the relative overlap and the deeper overlap. Multiplicative stability under the relative lattice lifts this decomposition to units. The character agrees on the relative factor by determinant and on the deeper factor by the supplied intertwining.

The construction is the overlap step in BK93 (3.3.3)–(3.3.4) and (3.3.15)–(3.3.16), printed pp. 105–106 and 111–112. It uses the centralizer-sum property of (1.3.16).
