# The unit groups of a simple stratum

Source: C. J. Bushnell and P. C. Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), (3.1.14) and the notation in (3.1.15), printed p. 95 (PDF p. 107). The relative order is described in (1.2.2)–(1.2.4), printed p. 25 (PDF p. 37).

`simpleHCarrier` and `simpleJCarrier` are exactly the two intersections in (3.1.14), taking membership in `ℌ` and `𝔍` via the underlying endomorphism of a unit and using the published `LatticeChain.unitFiltration` for `U^m(𝔄)`. As for the lattices, a defining sequence supplies the successive approximation elements. `simpleHGroup` and `simpleJGroup` associate subgroups of `G` to those intersections by taking the subgroups they generate (algebraic generation, not topological closure); `simpleJ` is the latter at zero. This gives a group-valued operation even on arbitrary intermediate data. On the source's simple input the generator sets are already subgroups by (3.1.14); equality of the resulting carriers with the literal intersections is a separate statement target, not a proof field or input assumption. The monotonicity and inclusion in `U^m(𝔄)` here follow just from the corresponding ambient unit filtration properties.

The centralizer of `β` in `G` is `B_β^×`: an invertible endomorphism commutes with `F[β]` precisely when it commutes with the generator. `relativeUnitFiltration` and `relativeOrderNormalizer` are its intersections with `U^m(𝔄)` and `𝔎(𝔄)`. These realize `U^m(𝔅_β)` and `𝔎(𝔅_β)` inside `G` using the same lattices in the relative and absolute chain, as in (1.2.2)–(1.2.4); in particular, they do not add a choice of relative chain to the groups.
