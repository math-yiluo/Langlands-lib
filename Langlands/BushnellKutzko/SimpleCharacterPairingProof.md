# Nondegeneracy of the simple-character commutator pairing

`simpleCharacterPairing` proves `SimpleCharacterPairingStatement`, the evaluated commutator form of Bushnell–Kutzko (1993), (3.4.1), printed pp. 115–116 (PDF pp. 127–128). The registered source `bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln` has SHA-256 `811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`.

The statement applies over a nonarchimedean local field to a simple `[𝔄,n,0,β]` and a defining sequence starting at `β`. The base character is trivial on `𝔭_F` and nontrivial on `𝒪_F`. For `m=i+1` with `i ∈ ℕ`, `m ≤ n` and `m ≤ −k₀(β,𝔄)`, take `θ ∈ C(𝔄,i,β)`. For scalar `β` the critical depth in that last bound is infinite.

The proof applies `simpleCharacterPairing_of_mixedJ` using the simple-stratum lattice and subgroup properties and the mixed commutator formula of (3.2.8) on the supplied defining sequence. The subgroup properties give `H^m ≤ J^m` and put commutators in `H^m`. For a nonscalar parameter at or above the `J` cutoff, a filtered trace expansion and induction on the defining sequence compute the character value. Below the cutoff, a group factorization separates the relative units, whose commutators evaluate to one by the determinant and mixed commutator identities. For a scalar parameter the determinant identity applies to the full `J^m` group. The resulting value on `x,y ∈ J^m` is the additive adjoint character on `x−1,y−1`.

Every element in the `J` lattice layer lifts to a unit of `J^m` with that difference from one. The additive radical calculation then identifies the elements of `J^m` that evaluate trivially against all of `J^m` as exactly the elements of `H^m`. Commutator containment gives normality, and the trivial evaluation on `H^m` gives invariance of the character under conjugation by `J^m`. The commutator product identities give homomorphisms in both variables, which descend to `J^m/H^m` with the same values on representatives. The self-commutator identity proves alternation, and the radical calculation proves nondegeneracy.
