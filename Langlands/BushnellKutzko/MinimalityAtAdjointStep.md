# Minimality at the first adjoint step above the shift

If an order endomorphism has commutator in the step above the shift of
the field generator, its layer maps intertwine the generator and the
base uniformizer. When every such family intertwines the field units,
its extension-linear lift has the same layer maps. The difference lies
in the first filtration step. Conversely, periodic lifting over the
base realizes any intertwining family. Containment at the specified
adjoint step then gives a centralizer element with the same layer maps.
Commuting with the generator implies commuting with the whole field.

The exponent in a uniformizer-power factorization is the normalized
valuation in `IsMinimalOver`. Removing the base uniformizer power after
taking the ramification-index power of the generator gives a shift-zero
field unit. Its residue is the element in the published minimality
condition. The layer criterion for coprimality and residue generation
therefore gives the equivalence with the adjoint containment, for the
published definitions of Bushnell–Kutzko (1993), (1.4.14)–(1.4.15),
printed p. 41.

`MinimalityAtAdjointStepStatement` assumes that \(F\) is a nonarchimedean
local field, that \(\beta\) is a nonzero generator of \(E/F\), and that
multiplication by every unit of \(E\) stabilizes the lattice chain.
Its proof obtains the local integer-ring structure from these data.
