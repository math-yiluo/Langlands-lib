# Commutation on successive lattice quotients

Translation by a stabilizer unit induces a compatible isomorphism on
each successive quotient. The product and inverse rules follow by
applying the isomorphisms to representatives. Consequently the units
that intertwine a given family of endomorphisms form a subgroup.

For the family induced by an order element, the intertwining condition
compares two compositions on every quotient. If the unit has shift `d`,
their difference lies in `L_(i+d+1)` for each `i`, which is exactly
membership of the commutator in `endFiltration (d + 1)`. Field-linearity
in particular gives intertwining for the uniformizer of the base.

These are derived comparisons for Bushnell–Kutzko (1993), (1.4.15),
printed p. 41, using the adjoint filtration of §1.4, printed pp. 35–36.
