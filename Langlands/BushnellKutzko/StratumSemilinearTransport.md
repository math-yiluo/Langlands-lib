# Transport of lattice chains and strata along semilinear equivalences

This module shows that the lattice-chain constructions of BK93 §1 are transported by a change
of coordinates. The setting allows the base field to change along a field isomorphism, because
the derived strata of BK93 §2.2 live over the field generated by an element. The two settings are
commutative rings `O₁`, `O₂` with algebras to fields `K₁`, `K₂`, a `K₁`-space `V₁` and a
`K₂`-space `V₂`, each with a compatible module structure over its ring of integers. The
transport is along a ring isomorphism `σ : K₁ → K₂`, given with its inverse as a
`RingHomInvPair`, and a `σ`-semilinear equivalence `f : V₁ ≃ₛₗ[σ] V₂`.

## Conjugation

`endConj f` is Mathlib's `LinearEquiv.conjRingEquiv`, the ring isomorphism
`End_{K₁}(V₁) → End_{K₂}(V₂)`, `x ↦ f ∘ x ∘ f⁻¹`. `endConj_apply_apply` evaluates it at
`f v`, and `endConj_symm_endConj` and `endConj_endConj_symm` show that `endConj f.symm` is
its inverse. By `endConj_algebraMap` it carries the scalar `c` to the scalar `σ c`. `unitsConj f`
is the induced isomorphism of unit groups.

## Corresponding lattice chains

`LatticeChain.Corresponds f L₁ L₂` says that `v ∈ L₁ i ↔ f v ∈ L₂ i` for every `i` and `v`.
`Corresponds.symm` gives the corresponding statement for `f⁻¹`. For corresponding chains,
the module proves that `endConj f` matches membership in the following constructions of
the two chains:

- the filtration `End^n` of BK93 (1.1.2), printed p. 20, by `Corresponds.mem_endFiltration`,
  and the order `𝔄`, by `Corresponds.mem_order`;
- the valuation `v_𝔄` of BK93 (1.1.3), printed p. 22, by `Corresponds.valuation_eq`;
- the normalizer `𝔎(𝔄)` and the unit groups `U^n(𝔄)`, printed pp. 21–22, by
  `Corresponds.mem_stabilizer` and `Corresponds.mem_unitFiltration` on `unitsConj f`. The
  first proof uses `map_eq_lattice_iff`, which says `g L_i = L_j` exactly when
  `v ∈ L_j ↔ g⁻¹ v ∈ L_i` for every `v`;
- the adjoint lattices `𝔑_k(β,𝔄)` of BK93 (1.4.3), printed p. 36, by
  `Corresponds.mem_adjointLattice`;
- for every `β` and every integer `k`, the intersections `𝔓^k ∩ B` of the filtration with the
  centralizer `B` of `β` (`centralizerLattice`), by `Corresponds.mem_centralizerLattice`.

Both proofs go through `endConj_adjointMap`,
`endConj f (βx − xβ) = (fβf⁻¹)(fxf⁻¹) − (fxf⁻¹)(fβf⁻¹)`. The transport of `𝔓^k ∩ B` does not
need `β` to generate a field. Under extra hypotheses BK93 identifies these intersections: in the
local-field setting, if `E = K[β]` is a field and `Eˣ` normalizes `𝔄`, then BK93 (1.2.1)–(1.2.4),
printed pp. 24–25, gives `𝔓^k ∩ B = 𝔔^k`, where `𝔔` is the radical of `𝔅 = 𝔄 ∩ B`.

`Corresponds.order_eq_iff` says that equality of orders is preserved. Two general lemmas,
`mem_sup_of_corresponds` and `le_iff_of_corresponds`, say that an additive bijection matching
membership in submodules also matches membership in their sums and inclusions between them.
With them, `Corresponds.criticalIndexSet_eq` shows that the index sets of BK93 (1.4.5) are
equal. `LatticeChain.Corresponds.criticalExponent_eq` then gives
`k₀(fβf⁻¹, 𝔄₂) = k₀(β, 𝔄₁)` (BK93 (1.4.5)–(1.4.6), p. 36). This also uses
`elementAlgebra_endConj_eq_bot_iff`, which says that `K₂[fβf⁻¹]` is the base field exactly when
`K₁[β]` is.

## Generated algebras

`endConj_mem_elementAlgebra_iff` says that `x ∈ K₁[β]` exactly when `fxf⁻¹ ∈ K₂[fβf⁻¹]`. The
forward direction is an induction over `Algebra.adjoin`, and the converse applies it to `f⁻¹`.
`elementAlgebraConj f β` is the resulting ring isomorphism `K₁[β] ≃ K₂[fβf⁻¹]`, and
`isField_elementAlgebra_endConj_iff` says that one is a field exactly when the other is.

## Pure and simple strata

`Stratum.isPure_iff` restates `Stratum.IsPure` (BK93 (1.5.5)(i)–(iii), printed p. 43) without
choosing a field structure on `K[b]`. In this form `K[b]` is a field, every unit `u` of `A` with
`u, u⁻¹ ∈ K[b]` lies in `𝔎(𝔄)`, and `v_𝔄(b) = −n`.

`Stratum.Corresponds f S₁ S₂` says that the chains correspond, that `n` and `r` agree, and that
`b₂ = f b₁ f⁻¹`. Its consequences are the following:

- `Corresponds.isPure_iff` and `Corresponds.isSimple_iff`, for pure and simple strata
  (BK93 (1.5.5), p. 43);
- `Corresponds.criticalExponent_eq`, the equality of critical exponents;
- `Corresponds.mem_coset`, saying that `endConj f` matches the cosets `b + 𝔓^{−r}`, and
  `Corresponds.equivalent_iff`, saying that it matches equivalence of strata (BK93 (1.5.1),
  p. 43).

Given a chain `L` corresponding to `S.chain`, `Stratum.transport` is the stratum on `L` with the
same `n` and `r` and with element `f S.b f⁻¹`. `Stratum.corresponds_transport` says that it
corresponds to `S`.

## The image of a lattice chain

`LatticeChain.transport` constructs the image of a lattice chain. It needs a ring homomorphism
`σO : O₁ → O₂` with inverse such that `σ` and `σO` commute with the algebra maps `O_i → K_i`.
Then `f` is `σO`-semilinear (`integerSemilinear`), and the image lattices are
`f(L_i)`. They are lattices because finite generation passes to images and the `K₂`-span of
`f(L_i)` is the image of the `K₁`-span. They are strictly decreasing because `f` is
injective. For periodicity, `map_smul_semilinear` computes `f(I L) = σO(I) f(L)`, and
`map_ideal_ringJacobson` says that a bijective ring homomorphism carries the Jacobson radical
onto the Jacobson radical. `LatticeChain.corresponds_transport` says that the image
corresponds to the original chain.
