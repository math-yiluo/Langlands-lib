# Factoring mixed and J inputs

`SimpleCharacterCommutatorAt` groups the two commutator formulas of BK93 (3.2.6) and (3.2.8) for one character at a finite critical depth. Its fields give the membership and value formula with a mixed first input and a second input in the mixed lattice or the J group.

The successor calculations apply the formulas to the deeper factors after restriction. A relative factor normalizes the character, and two relative factors have trivial determinant commutator. The depth shift at the successor exponent supplies the two inequalities needed for each use of the inductive formulas. The arguments follow the factorizations in the proof of BK93 (3.2.6), printed pp. 100–102, and in the proof of (3.2.8) around (3.2.10), printed pp. 102–103.
