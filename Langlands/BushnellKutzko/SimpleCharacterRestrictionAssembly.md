# Assembling the simple-character restriction theorem

For a nonterminal defining sequence, the immediate successor has the same chain and level. The high-index domain equality identifies its character domain with that of the original parameter for \(m\ge\lfloor r/2\rfloor\), where \(r\) is the original critical depth. Formal intertwiners at approximation depth \(r\) lie in the double coset of the covariance identity for the successor. Applying the identity to the successor gives the reverse twist membership for \(\lfloor r/2\rfloor\le m<r\).

An arbitrary critical approximation is equivalent to the selected successor at that depth. The equivalent-stratum clause of `SimpleCharacterChoiceStatement` compares its characters with those of the successor by a trace twist. Composition gives the inverse twist in the restriction theorem.

For adjacent restriction with \(m+1\le\lfloor r/2\rfloor\), the subgroup-product formula gives surjectivity. For \(m\ge\lfloor r/2\rfloor\), the nonterminal case transports restriction for the shortened defining sequence, and the terminal case uses the high-index singleton. Nonemptiness follows by descending from the last index below the stratum level.
