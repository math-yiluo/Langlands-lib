# Successive quotients of a lattice chain

The quotient of `L_i` by the intersection with `L_j` comes with its map
from representatives and with maps induced by lattice-preserving linear
maps. For consecutive indices the quotient is nonzero by strict decrease.
The induced endomorphisms of two order elements agree on every such
quotient exactly when their difference lies in the first filtration step.

A stabilizer unit gives an isomorphism to the translated quotient because
it maps both lattices onto their translates. For a local coefficient ring,
the period relation shows that the maximal ideal annihilates a quotient
within one period. The original action therefore descends to the residue
field. In particular each successive quotient is a residue vector space.

These constructions implement the successive lattice quotients used to
prove Bushnell–Kutzko (1993), (1.4.15), printed p. 41, from the chain and
filtration of §1.1, printed pp. 19–22.
