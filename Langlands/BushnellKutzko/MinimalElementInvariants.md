# Ramification index and residue degree of a minimal generator

The module `Langlands.BushnellKutzko.MinimalElementInvariants` computes the ramification index
and residue degree of the field generated by a minimal element in terms of a lattice chain it
normalizes, as in Bushnell–Kutzko (1994), (1.5).

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field, `𝓛` an `𝒪_F`-lattice chain in `V` of period `e`, and
`E` a field acting on `V` whose units lie in `𝓛.stabilizer`, with `𝔬_E = subfieldIntegers F E` a
discrete valuation ring with fraction field `E`.

## Declarations

- `residue_adjoin_eq_top_of_mk`: if the class of `a ∈ 𝔬_E` generates `subfieldResidueRing F E`
  over `𝒪_F` (the form used in `IsMinimalOver`), its residue generates `ResidueField 𝔬_E`;
  the two are identified by `subfieldResidueEquiv`.
- `LatticeChain.ramificationIndex_and_residue_adjoin_of_isMinimalOver`: let `β ∈ Eˣ` shift the
  chain by `-n`, satisfy `IsMinimalOver F E β`, let `π` be a prime element of `𝒪_F`, put
  `g = gcd(e, |n|)` and let `w ∈ 𝔬_Eˣ` with `π^{n/g} β^{e/g} = w`. Then
  `subfieldRamificationIndex F E = e / g` and the residue of `w` generates `ResidueField 𝔬_E` over
  `𝒪_F`.
- `LatticeChain.isMinimalOver_of_mem_range`: if `β` generates `E` over `F` and lies in the image
  of `F`, then `IsMinimalOver F E β`.
- `natDegree_minpoly_eq_finrank_of_adjoin_eq_top`: when `algebraMap 𝒪_F 𝔬_E` is local, an
  integral generator `c` of `k_E` over `𝒪_F` has minimal polynomial over `k_F` of degree
  `[k_E : k_F]`.
- `subfieldResidueDegree_eq_finrank`: when moreover `[E : F] < ∞`,
  `subfieldResidueDegree F E = [k_E : k_F]`.

## Proofs

For `ramificationIndex_and_residue_adjoin_of_isMinimalOver`, write `β = u ϖ_E^ν` with
`u ∈ 𝔬_Eˣ` (`subfieldUnit_factorization`). By `subfieldNormalizedValuation_eq_of_factorization`
the exponent `ν` is the normalized valuation in `IsMinimalOver`, so it is prime to `e_E = e(E|F)`.
With `t` the shift of `ϖ_E`, `t e_E = e` (`fieldShift_uniformizer_mul_ramification`) and
`-n = t ν` (`fieldShift_eq_mul_of_factorization`). Hence `g = gcd(t e_E, t |ν|) = t`,
`e / g = e_E` and `n / g = -ν`. The element `y₀ = π₀^{-ν} β^{e_E}` of `IsMinimalOver`, for its
prime element `π₀`, differs from `w = π^{-ν} β^{e_E}` by the `ν`-th power of the unit `π / π₀` of
`𝒪_F` (`IsDiscreteValuationRing.associated_of_irreducible`). So the residue of `y₀` lies in the
`𝒪_F`-algebra generated by the residue of `w`, which is therefore everything.

For `isMinimalOver_of_mem_range`, `E` is the image of `F`, so every element of `𝔬_E` is the image
of an element of `F` integral over `𝒪_F`, hence of `𝒪_F` (`IsIntegrallyClosed.isIntegral_iff`).
Thus `𝒪_F → 𝔬_E` is an isomorphism, the image of a prime element `π₀` of `F` is a uniformizer
of `E` of shift `e`, and `e_E = 1`. With `β = u ϖ_E^ν`, the element `π₀^{-ν} β` is the unit `u`,
and every residue class comes from `𝒪_F`.

The degree statement goes through `IntermediateField.adjoin.finrank`; the residue degree statement
combines `PureApproximation.subfieldResidueDegree_eq_inertiaDeg` with
`Ideal.inertiaDeg_eq_of_isMaximal`.
