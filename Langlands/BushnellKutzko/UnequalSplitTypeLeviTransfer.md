# Trace duality for the centralizer and the next H layer

For a simple stratum of index zero with a chosen defining sequence, let `x ∈ h^m`. The lemma `mem_centralizer_sup_of_adjoint_orthogonal` assumes that `ψ_A((βy − yβ)x) = 1` for every `y ∈ Q^(r−m) N_(−r)`, with `r` the finite critical depth parameter. It concludes that `x ∈ Q^m + h^(m+1)`. The additive character has the Bushnell–Kutzko conductor, and the tame corestriction is taken for the field generated by `β`.

For nonscalar `β`, the proof takes the double trace dual of the sum. A member of its dual has one expression from the dual of `Q^m` and one from the dual of `h^(m+1)`. Their difference in the adjoint image is in `P^(−m)`, so adjoint exactness writes it as `βy − yβ` with `y` in the test lattice. This reduces the pairing to the given orthogonality and the trace dual formula for `h^m`. For scalar `β`, `x ∈ P^m` already implies `x ∈ Q^m`.

This is a trace-dual form of the adjoint control in Bushnell and Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), (3.1.16), printed pp. 95–97, and (8.2.17), printed p. 283. Formula (3.1.19), printed pp. 96–97, gives the trace duals of both H layers.
