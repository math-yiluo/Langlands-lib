# Matrices on the graded pieces of a lattice chain

The lattice-chain coordinates come from Bushnell–Kutzko, *The admissible dual
of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), §1.1, printed pp. 20–22. Scaling
each vector of a splitting basis by an integral power of a uniformizer puts
its last lattice index in the range from zero to the period minus one.

The entrywise criterion for the endomorphism filtration follows by testing
on the coordinate lines of the splitting basis. At depths zero and one the
criterion makes every entry integral. An entry from a column of index `j`
to a row of index less than `j + n` belongs to the maximal ideal at depth `n`.

Reducing the integral matrix gives a block triangular matrix, with grades
ordered by the negatives of the lattice indices. Each diagonal block is
multiplicative because terms through other grades vanish in the matrix
product. For units, the condition that every block is the identity is
exactly that the unit differs from one by a depth-one endomorphism.
