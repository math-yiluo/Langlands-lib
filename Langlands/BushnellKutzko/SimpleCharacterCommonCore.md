# A common restriction depth for two core approximations

`SimpleCharacterCoherenceFromRatiosStatement` derives the full published coherence proposition from the lattice, group, restriction, self-intertwining and relative determinant propositions and the ratio comparison `SimpleCharacterRatioIntertwiningStatement`. The last comparison is the cancellation consequence of the successor conjugation formula of BK93 (3.3.9) in the range stated in Lean. The implemented proof takes this comparison as a premise.

Source Bushnell and Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), (3.3.2), printed p. 105 (PDF p. 117), (3.3.9), printed p. 108 (PDF p. 120), and (3.5.5), (3.5.7), (3.5.8), printed pp. 119–121 (PDF pp. 131–133). The ratio comparison is formulated for a simple stratum over a nonarchimedean local field with the conductor of `IsBKAdditiveCharacter`. For two members at depth `m`, the double coset from the self-intertwining formula at `t` intertwines their quotient when `⌊t/2⌋ ≤ m` and both indices are below the level and critical depth. In `simpleCharacterRatioIntertwining_of_covariance`, the supplied conjugation formula multiplies each character by the same additive trace factor on the intersection of the domains. That factor is nonzero and cancels in the quotient.

For `m < ⌊n/2⌋`, choose the restriction depth `k = 2m + 1`. The bounded-core construction advances at a step of approximation depth `r` only when `⌊r/2⌋ ≤ m`, so each resulting core for a depth-zero input has approximation depth at most `k`. Its half critical depth is strictly above `m`, so `k` lies below its critical depth, with the level cutoff used at a scalar core. The level itself is also above `k`.

Equal orders and exact group carriers give equality of the two domains at `k`. The common character therefore has the same restriction, after transport, on both sides. This restriction is a core character on each side since the additive twist has vanished. Its self-intertwining gives the double coset at `k` for each core and contains both core centralizers. At depth `m` the correspondence multiplies all core characters by the same twist, which cancels in the ratios. Applying the ratio comparison with `⌊k/2⌋ = m` gives the intertwining input for the descent.
