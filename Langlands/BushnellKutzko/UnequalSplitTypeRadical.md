# Radical of the lower-corner commutator pairing

`mem_simpleHLayer_succ_of_orthogonal` takes a lower-corner element `x ∈ e₂ h^m e₁` orthogonal to `Q^(r−m) N_(−r)` and to `B` for the trace character pairing with `(β + c)x − x(β + c)`. Here `m < r`, the twist has depth at least `−m`, and the two idempotents lie in `B`. It assumes that a tame corestriction for `F[β]/F` detects the lower-corner graded commutator of the twist. Under these hypotheses, `x ∈ h^(m+1)`.

In the first pairing the contribution of `c` is trivial by its depth and `m < r`. Trace duality writes `x` as the sum of a depth-`m` centralizer element and an element of `h^(m+1)`, both projected to the lower corner. In the second pairing the contribution of `β` vanishes by commutation, and the deeper H layer contributes trivially. The trace criterion for tame corestriction converts the remaining orthogonality into a depth-one bound on the corestricted commutator. The graded injectivity hypothesis raises the centralizer element to depth `m+1`. This combines the adjoint and corestriction arguments of Bushnell and Kutzko (1993), (8.2.17) and (8.2.21)–(8.2.22), with the lower-corner injection from (8.2.4)(ii).
