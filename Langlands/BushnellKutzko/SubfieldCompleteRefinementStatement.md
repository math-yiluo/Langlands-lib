# Complete refinement of a subfield-normalized lattice chain

Bushnell–Kutzko (1993), (5.1.12), printed p. 162 (PDF p. 174), chooses
a relative suborder. In (5.2.3), printed p. 167 (PDF p. 179), the
relative suborder is taken minimal. In the lattice-chain description
of (1.1.1)–(1.1.2), printed p. 20 (PDF p. 32), and (1.2.4), printed
p. 25 (PDF p. 37), this choice comes from a complete refinement over
the parameter field. The retained source
`bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln` has SHA-256
`811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`.

Given an \(F\)-lattice chain \(L\) normalized by \(E^\times\) in
a compatible \(E\)-vector space \(V\), there is an
\(E^\times\)-normalized chain \(M\) containing all the lattices of \(L\)
and sharing its zeroth lattice. Its base-field period is
\(e(E/F)\dim_E V\). The depth-zero units of \(M\) lie in those of \(L\),
and the depth-one units of \(L\) lie in those of \(M\). The same
lattices with extension integer scalars give a chain of period
\(\dim_E V\). The base field, extension field, and carrier have
independent universes.
