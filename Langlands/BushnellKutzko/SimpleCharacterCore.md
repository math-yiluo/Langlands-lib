# Core approximations of simple character sets

The construction in `exists_simpleCharacterCore` advances through a defining sequence until it reaches a term whose half critical depth lies strictly above the given depth. At a terminal term the comparison uses half the stratum level. `SimpleCharacterCore` records the resulting simple stratum with the original chain and level, a defining sequence, congruence at the approximation depth, equality of the character domain and the bidirectional additive twist correspondence. These are the character and domain properties of the core approximation used in BK93 (3.5.3)–(3.5.5).

Source Bushnell and Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), (3.1.9)(ii), printed pp. 91–92 (PDF pp. 103–104), (3.3.18), printed p. 112 (PDF p. 124), and (3.5.3)–(3.5.5), printed pp. 118–119 (PDF pp. 130–131). The source identifier is `bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln` and the retained PDF SHA-256 is `811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`.

The existence theorem takes the lattice-property and restriction propositions as inputs. It starts from a simple stratum at a nonnegative index and a depth below half its level, with the base additive character trivial on the maximal ideal and nontrivial on the integer ring. Each advancing step uses the actual next term of the defining sequence. Equal orders give equal endomorphism filtrations and lattice-chain stabilizers, so that term and its suffix can use the original chain.

At a step of critical depth `r` with `⌊r/2⌋ ≤ m < r`, the critical-approximation twist clause of `SimpleCharacterRestrictionStatement` gives equality of groups and the character-set equivalence. The proof reverses the inverse twist in that equivalence. For `r ≤ m`, the lattice comparison clause gives equal filtered lattices, hence equal literal carriers and generated subgroups. The character recursion is untwisted in this range. Additive duality and the base conductor make the successive difference trivial there, so the same additive twist description applies. Successive twists compose by additivity in the parameter and the approximation congruences accumulate using the increasing stratum indices.
