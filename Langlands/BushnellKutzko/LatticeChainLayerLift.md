# Lifting endomorphisms of successive lattice quotients

A family of endomorphisms of the successive quotients lifts to an
endomorphism preserving the chain when it is compatible with translation
by a uniformizer. Over one period, the quotient of the first lattice by
the last is a vector space over the residue field. At each step the proof
chooses a retraction for the inclusion of the shorter flag and a section
for the map to its successive quotient. Their sum lifts the two actions.

Projectivity of the first lattice lifts the resulting residue endomorphism.
An integral basis extends to a basis of the ambient vector space, giving
a field-linear lift. The compatibility of the family with scalar
translation then propagates its action to every integer index.

This is a derived lifting lemma for the proof of Bushnell–Kutzko (1993),
(1.4.15), printed p. 41, using the lattice-chain period of §1.1,
printed pp. 19–22.
