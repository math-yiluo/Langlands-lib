# Mixed units and a congruence lattice

`mixedAdjointUnitGroup_le_normalizer_of_adjoint_bimodule` uses the adjoint lattice membership of a positive mixed unit and of its inverse to preserve the congruence lattice on both sides. `mixedAdjointUnitGroup_sup_eq_lattice` then identifies the subgroup product with the units obtained by adding the mixed and congruence lattices to one. In the reverse direction the additive decomposition lifts to units using positivity and multiplication stability.

This abstracts the group factorization in the remark after BK93 (3.3.2), printed p. 105, and the subgroup normalization used for (3.3.17), printed p. 112.
