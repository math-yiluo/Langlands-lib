# The period-one cuspidal layer in the centralizer

Restriction of scalars sends the `F[β]`-linear automorphisms to the centralizer of `β`. Commutation with `β` implies commutation with every element of `F[β]` by algebra generation, so an ambient unit in the centralizer and its inverse are `F[β]`-linear. The relative lattice chain has the same sets of vectors as the original chain. Thus the unit subgroup of the relative chain maps isomorphically onto the relative unit subgroup of the original chain.

The chosen reduction on the single layer transports through this isomorphism. The period-one lattice result gives its surjectivity and computes the cuspidal self-intertwining set inside the centralizer as the product of the `F[β]`-scalar units and the relative unit subgroup. This is the maximal-order case of the calculation in Bushnell–Kutzko (1993), (5.5.4)–(5.5.5), printed p. 182.
