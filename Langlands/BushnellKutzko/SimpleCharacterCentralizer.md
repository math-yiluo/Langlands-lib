# Relative units and determinant characters

`relativeEndUnitEquiv` identifies the units of the endomorphism algebra
relative to the field generated by `β` with the centralizer units in the
ambient endomorphism algebra. Restriction of scalars gives the map. A
unit commuting with `β` and its inverse both restrict from linear maps
over the generated field. Injectivity of restriction of scalars gives
the two inverse identities over that field.

`factorsThroughCentralizerDet.conj_eq` proves the relative determinant
comparison used in the proof of (3.3.3). It lifts an ambient centralizer
unit and an element in the relative domain to relative automorphisms.
The original element and its conjugate have equal relative determinants.
The factoring character of the determinant image therefore gives equal
values on both elements whenever both belong to the domain.
The determinant here is Mathlib's determinant. For a finite-dimensional
space over the generated field it is the ordinary relative determinant.

`mem_relativeOrderNormalizer_of_normalizes_relativeUnits` uses the same
unit equivalence to pull a normalizing unit back to the endomorphism
algebra over the generated field. The relative lattice chain has the
same lattice subsets as the original chain. Its unit filtration and
chain stabilizer therefore give the prescribed congruence subgroup and
normalizer in the centralizer. Applying the congruence-subgroup
normalizer theorem over the generated field gives the implication used
in the proof of (3.3.17).
