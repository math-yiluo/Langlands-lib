# Lowering the character level

`SimpleCharacterLoweringStatement` applies to two simple strata
`[𝔄,n,m,β₁]` and `[𝔄,n,m,β₂]` with `1 ≤ m` and the same depth-`m` character
pairs. Here the order is in a finite-dimensional endomorphism algebra over
a nonarchimedean local field, and the pairs use an admissible `ψ_F`.
The groups `H^m(β₁,𝔄)` and `H^m(β₂,𝔄)` agree for every pair of
defining sequences. There is a simple stratum `[𝔄,n,m,β₁′]` equivalent to
`[𝔄,n,m,β₁]` with `Ĉ(𝔄,m−1,β₁′) = Ĉ(𝔄,m−1,β₂)`. The equivalence is tested
at `m`, and the final equality compares the character pairs at `m−1`.
