# Transfer across the shallow lower corner of the H group

`LowerBlockIsotypicTransferStatement` takes a simple stratum of index zero with a chosen defining sequence, a Bushnell–Kutzko additive character, and the twist `ϑ = θ ψ_c` of a simple character `θ` on `H^m`. Here `m` lies below the stratum depth and the simple critical depth, `c` lies in `P^(−m)`, and the units of `F[β]` stabilize the lattice chain. The complementary orthogonal idempotents are in the integral centralizer and commute with `c`. The graded injectivity premise says that, for `x ∈ e₂ Q^m e₁`, depth at least one of the corestricted commutator `s(cx − xc)` implies `x ∈ P^(m+1)` after restriction of scalars.

Let `M` be any subgroup of the upper block unipotent group. An input vector outside the Jacquet relations of `M` transforms by `ϑ` on the subgroup of `H^m` whose lower block has depth at least `m+1`. The theorem `exists_isotypic_of_lowerBlock` gives a vector outside the same relations transforming by `ϑ` on all of `H^m`.

The proof takes upper-block units from a finite quotient of `Q^(r−m) N_(−r) + B`, with `r` the finite critical depth parameter. A trace computation expresses the ratio between the conjugated and original characters as the additive pairing with the commutator for `β + c`. The radical calculation identifies its kernel with the subgroup where the lower block has depth at least `m+1`. Orthogonality to the first summand puts the lower block in `Q^m + h^(m+1)`. Orthogonality to `B`, trace compatibility of the tame corestriction, and graded injectivity then raise the depth of the centralizer part. The upper-block conjugators centralize `M`, so the finite character transfer preserves nonvanishing modulo its Jacquet relations.

The trace calculation uses the intertwining-set formula (3.3.2) and the covariance calculation (3.3.9), printed pp. 105 and 108. The adjoint orthogonality uses (3.1.16) and (3.1.19), printed pp. 95–97. The graded injectivity premise is the lower-corner condition from (8.2.4)(ii), printed p. 276. Together these give an additive-pairing form of the shallow lower-block steps (8.2.17) and (8.2.21)–(8.2.26), printed pp. 283–289, with the same vector transfer for scalar and nonscalar parameters. C. J. Bushnell and P. C. Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993).
