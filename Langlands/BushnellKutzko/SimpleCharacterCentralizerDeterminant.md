# Determinant factorization on the centralizer of a simple stratum

`SimpleCharacterCentralizerDeterminantStatement` is an algebraic extension of the character clause of BK93 (2.4.11). For a simple stratum over a nonarchimedean local field, choose a defining sequence and a natural depth. An abstract homomorphism from the resulting domain to `ℂˣ` that is intertwined by every unit of the centralizer factors through the image of the determinant on its intersection with the centralizer. The target is used for the quotient of two characters in (3.5.8).

Bushnell and Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), (2.4.11), printed pp. 72–73 and proof p. 78 (PDF pp. 84–85 and p. 90), prove the implication for smooth characters by induction on a depth of triviality. Their adapted matrix description is in (2.5.1)–(2.5.2), printed pp. 76–77 (PDF pp. 88–89). The application to core approximations is in (3.5.8), printed pp. 120–121 (PDF pp. 132–133). The source identifier is `bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln` and the retained PDF SHA-256 is `811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`.

## Algebraic determinant argument

Let `U = 1 + 𝔓^k` with `k ≥ 1`, where `𝔓` is the radical of a hereditary order over a nonarchimedean local field with uniformizer `π`. Let `χ : U → ℂˣ` be a homomorphism intertwined by every ambient invertible matrix. Choose a basis splitting the lattice chain. Membership in `𝔓^k` is tested entry by entry in fractional ideals. Every diagonal entry of `𝔓^k` lies in the same ideal `𝔭^d`, where `d = ⌈k/e⌉` and `e` is the chain period. In fact the exponent of the lattice in any fixed coordinate increases by exactly one per period. The largest increase over any interval of length `k` is `d`.

For an allowed off-diagonal entry `x` in position `(i,j)`, both `a = x/(1−π)` and `πa` are allowed in that position. Conjugation by the diagonal matrix with `π` at `i` and `1` elsewhere carries `1+aEᵢⱼ` to `1+πaEᵢⱼ`. The intertwining hypothesis equates their character values. Their quotient is `1+xEᵢⱼ`, so this elementary unit has character value `1`.

In column `j` of a matrix in `U`, the diagonal pivot is in `1+𝔭^d` and is therefore a unit of the valuation ring. Dividing an off-diagonal entry by that pivot preserves its fractional ideal. Left multiplication by the corresponding elementary unit clears the entry. All intermediate matrices remain in `U`, and previously cleared columns are preserved because the pivot row has zeros in those columns. Successive elimination reduces the matrix to a diagonal one with unchanged determinant and character value.

Permutation matrices carry a single diagonal factor with entry `u ∈ 1+𝔭^d` to the first coordinate, with both factors in `U`. Intertwining therefore makes the character value of a diagonal matrix the value of `χ` on the first-coordinate factor whose entry is the product of its diagonal entries. The determinant image equals `1+𝔭^d` by elimination and by these first-coordinate factors. Restricting `χ` along this diagonal section of the determinant gives the factoring homomorphism. The argument applies to abstract homomorphisms without a depth-of-triviality hypothesis.

## Relative specialization in Lean

`simpleCharacterCentralizerDeterminant_of_unitFiltration` takes the algebraic filtered-unit assertion above as a hypothesis. The actual generated field `E = F[β]` inherits its finite-extension local field structure. The normalizing action gives the relative lattice chain over `E`, whose lattice subsets are the same as those of the original chain. The pullback of the simple character domain along restriction of scalars equals the unit filtration on the relative chain. One inclusion uses containment of the ambient character domain in the ambient unit filtration. For the other, the relative endomorphism commutes with `β`, so its restricted endomorphism lies in the order-zero centralizer summand of `ℌ`. Every relative unit is thus an intertwiner of the pulled-back character. The supplied algebraic assertion on the relative chain gives the factor through the determinant image required by `factorsThroughCentralizerDet`.
