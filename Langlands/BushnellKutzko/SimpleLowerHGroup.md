# Enlargement of the first H group in the lower corner

`SplitBlocks.simpleLowerJGroup` is the subgroup \(D\) of the lower
block units with increment in the first \(J\)-lattice. Here the
increment \(d-1\) satisfies \(e_2(d-1)e_1=d-1\), and \(e_1e_2=0\).
`SplitBlocks.simpleLowerHGroup` is the subgroup generated by \(D\)
and \(H^1\) for the supplied stratum and defining sequence.

For a simple stratum of index zero this enlargement lies in \(J^1\)
and is preserved by conjugation by \(J^1\). With the two projectors
in the integral centralizer, an element of the enlargement lies in
\(H^1\) exactly when its lower corner belongs to the first
\(H\)-lattice. In the factorization \(d h\), \(d\in D\) and \(h\in H^1\),
the cross term \((d-1)(h-1)\) is in the second \(H\)-lattice by
the product bound on the first \(J\)-lattice.

`SplitBlocks.exists_simpleLowerHCharacter` constructs the character
\(d h\mapsto\theta(h)\) when the projectors are complementary and
orthogonal, \(\theta\) is a simple character of \(H^1\) for a BK
additive character, and depth one lies below the level and critical
depth. The group \(D\) normalizes \(\theta\) and the character is trivial
on \(D\cap H^1\) by off-diagonal triviality. These facts give the
factorization rule and its multiplicativity.

This is the unshifted depth-one enlargement underlying Bushnell–Kutzko
(1993), (8.2.18) and (8.2.19), printed p. 284.
