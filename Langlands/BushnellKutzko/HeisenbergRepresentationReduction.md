# Heisenberg representations from the commutator pairing and character intertwining

Bushnell–Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), Proposition (5.1.1), printed p. 158 (PDF p. 170), uses (3.4.1), (3.3.2) and (3.1.15) for the local inputs. The registered source is `bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln` (SHA-256 `811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`).

`HeisenbergRepresentationReductionStatement` is the implication from `SimpleCharacterPairingStatement`, `SimpleCharacterIntertwiningStatement` and `SimpleStratumGroupPropertiesStatement` to `HeisenbergRepresentationStatement`. It retains separate universes for the local field, the stratum space and each of the three quantified representation spaces. The theorem `heisenbergRepresentation_of` proves this implication.

The descended pairing gives conjugation invariance of the simple character. Its trivial radical yields a nontrivial scalar commutator for each element outside `H^1`, forcing the trace there to vanish. A deep unit subgroup lies in the character kernel, and its normal core in `J^1` has finite index. Coinduction and a simple submodule of its finite-dimensional carrier produce an irreducible representation on `Fin d → ℂ`. The scalar action gives smoothness on the same deep unit subgroup. In any irreducible representation containing the character, the translates of a character eigenvector span the carrier and have the same eigencharacter on `H^1`.

On the finite quotient, the product of traces for two such representations is constant on the image of `H^1` and zero elsewhere. The character average gives a nonzero intertwiner, hence an isomorphism. For a representation paired with itself, Schur's lemma makes the average equal to one, giving \((\dim \eta)^2 = [J^1 : H^1]\). The ambient intertwining comparison uses the same trace product on the intersection of the two conjugate copies of `J^1`. The character intertwining statement and the level-zero double-coset identity give `J^1 B_β^× J^1`, including the scalar case where the centralizer is the whole group.
