# Intertwining of an inflated cuspidal representation over a discretely valued field

The integral general linear group reduces surjectively to the general linear group over the residue field. Entrywise lifts of an invertible residue matrix have unit determinant.

For the upper bound on self-intertwining, Cartan decomposition gives an antitone exponent tuple. If this tuple is nonconstant, take the cut after its largest exponents. The upper cross block of the resulting proper two-block parabolic is its radical. Every element of that radical lifts to an integral matrix with conjugate congruent to the identity, since each cross-block entry acquires a positive power of the uniformizer. Thus the image of an intertwiner consists of vectors fixed by the radical, so cuspidality forces the intertwiner to be zero. Constant tuples give scalar matrices, and scalar matrices and their integral translates intertwine.

This is the period-one matrix calculation underlying Bushnell–Kutzko (1993), (5.5.5) and (5.5.11), printed pp. 182–185. The argument applies directly to the Cartan decomposition in this setting.
