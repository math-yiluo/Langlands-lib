# Transport of defining sequences and of the groups `H^k`, `J^k`

Let `f` be an `F`-linear automorphism of `V`, and write `x ↦ fxf⁻¹` for `endConj f`. This
module shows that defining sequences in the sense of BK93 (2.4.2), printed pp. 65–66, and the
lattices and groups built from them in BK93 §3.1 are carried by `f` to the same constructions
for the conjugated data. The base is a commutative ring `O` with an algebra map to the field
`F`, as in `Stratum`.

## Derived strata over the conjugated field

Condition (2.4.2)(vi) quantifies over tame corestrictions (BK93 (1.3.3), printed p. 29). It
also refers to derived strata (BK93 §2.2, printed p. 51) over the generated field `E = F[β]`.
For the conjugated element, the field is `E' = F[fβf⁻¹]`, the image of `E` under conjugation
inside `End_F(V)`.

The first part of the module works with fields `E`, `E'` acting on `V`, a ring isomorphism
`σK : E ≃ E'`, and an `F`-linear `f` with `f(a v) = σK(a) f(v)`. `semilinearOfConj` views `f` as a
`σK`-semilinear equivalence. `endConj_restrictScalars` says that conjugation by `f` on
`End_F(V)` restricts to conjugation by this semilinear map on `End_E(V)`.

`NormalizationTransports O f E E'` is the hypothesis that `E'^×` normalizes every chain
corresponding under `f` to a chain normalized by `E^×`. Under it, a tame corestriction `s'`
relative to `E'/F` pulls back to `TameCorestriction.pullback`, `x ↦ f⁻¹ s'(fxf⁻¹) f`, relative
to `E/F`. The bimodule identities follow from `endConj_restrictScalars`. The order-image
property follows from applying the property of `s'` to the image chain
`LatticeChain.transport`.

`integralClosureConj` restricts `σK` to the integral closures of `O`. With it,
`derivedEquivalentToSimple_transport` shows the following. Suppose `S'` corresponds to `S`
and is generated by `E'`. If the derived stratum `D` of `S` for the pulled-back corestriction
is equivalent to a simple stratum `C`, then the images of `D` and `C` under the semilinear
`f` satisfy the same conditions for `S'`, `fbf⁻¹` and `s'`. The image chains are
`LatticeChain.transport` over the integral closures. Simplicity and equivalence are transported
by `Stratum.Corresponds.isSimple_iff` and `Stratum.Corresponds.equivalent_iff`.

The second part takes `E = F[β]` and `E' = F[fβf⁻¹]` with `σK = elementAlgebraConj f β`. The
semilinearity and the compatibility with `O` are `elementAlgebraConj_smul` and
`elementAlgebraConj_algebraMap`. `normalizationTransports_elementAlgebra` proves the
normalization hypothesis from `LatticeChain.Corresponds.mem_stabilizer`.
`Stratum.isGeneratedField_elementAlgebra` shows that `F[β]` is generated by `β`.
`Stratum.Corresponds.hasSimpleDerivedStratum` combines these. If `S` satisfies
`HasSimpleDerivedStratum b`, then every corresponding `S'` satisfies
`HasSimpleDerivedStratum (fbf⁻¹)`.

## Defining sequences

`Stratum.Corresponds.isDefiningSequence` transports each of the conditions of
`Stratum.IsDefiningSequence` from `S` and the terms `q i` to corresponding strata `S'` and
`q' i`. The conditions are purity, the order, level and simplicity of the terms, equivalence
of the first term, strict monotonicity of the indices, the critical-exponent and congruence
conditions, the terminal condition and the derived-stratum condition.
`Stratum.DefiningSequenceChoice.transport` takes the image of every term on the image chain
`LatticeChain.transport`. By `tail_transport` its tail is the image of the original tail under
`x ↦ fxf⁻¹`.

## Lattices and groups

For corresponding chains, the critical depths of BK93 (3.1.6) agree. These are
`LatticeChain.Corresponds.simpleCriticalDepth_eq` and `finiteCriticalDepth_eq`.
`LatticeChain.Corresponds.mem_simpleStratumLattices` proves by induction on the tail that
`x ↦ fxf⁻¹` matches membership in the lattices `ℌ(β,𝔄)` and `𝔍(β,𝔄)` of BK93
(3.1.7)–(3.1.8), printed pp. 90–91. The original lattices are computed on `L` from `β` and the
original tail. The image lattices are computed on `L'` from `fβf⁻¹` and the conjugated tail,
the image of the original tail under `x ↦ fxf⁻¹`. In each step of the recursion the critical
depths agree, and the sums with the centralizer lattice and the intersections with the
filtration lattices correspond. `LatticeChain.Corresponds.simpleHGroup_eq` and `simpleJGroup_eq`
follow, since the groups are generated by the units of the corresponding filtration that lie in
these lattices.
The groups `H^k` and `J^k` of BK93 (3.1.14), printed p. 95, for the conjugated data are the
images of the original groups under `unitsConj f`.
