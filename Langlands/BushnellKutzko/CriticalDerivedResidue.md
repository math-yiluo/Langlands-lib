# Residue comparison from degree-zero scalar symbols

Internal step in the proof of Bushnell–Kutzko (1993), (2.4.1)(iii), p. 65. The degree-zero comparison is developed here from the published scalar-shift criteria rather than taken as a separate statement from the book.

For finite extensions `E` and `K` of the given nonarchimedean local field acting on a common chain through normalizing field units, assume each `E`-scalar of degree at least zero matches a `K`-scalar of degree at least zero modulo one. The two scalar-depth criteria identify degrees at least zero and one with integrality and with zero residue (the latter for integral scalars), respectively. In particular, the residue of an integral `K`-scalar match is unique. Addition and multiplication on degree-zero symbols then give the ring map on the integral closure for `E` into the residue field for `K`, and the degree-one criterion kills the maximal ideal of the former. This proves a map on residue fields, not a map between the original extensions.

The module also records that any scalar matching an exact-depth symbol is nonzero and that its induced unit shifts the chain by precisely that depth.
