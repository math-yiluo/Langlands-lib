# The relative character specified by transfer

The source is Bushnell and Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), `bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln`, SHA-256 `811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`, the determinant parameter in (3.6.9) and the transfer construction on printed pp. 130–134 (PDF pp. 142–146).

A centralizer unit conjugates a relative endomorphism by an invertible map over `F[β]`, which leaves its determinant unchanged. This proves conjugation invariance of the factored character wherever both elements lie in its domain.

At equal normalized depths, the determinant images of the two relative filtered unit groups agree. A simple-character group meets the centralizer in its relative filtered unit group. These facts allow the character of the first determinant image to be transported to the second image. Composing with the second determinant gives the prospective relative restriction, with agreement for all equal-determinant pairs. A character on the whole second simple-character group with this restriction factors through the relative determinant by the same homomorphism on the image.
