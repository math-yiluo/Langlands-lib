# Simple characters at depths at least half the stratum level

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field and fix the base additive character `ψ_F` that is trivial on `𝔭_F` and nontrivial on `𝔬_F`, as specified by `IsBKAdditiveCharacter`. For a stratum of level `n` and a defining sequence starting at `β`, `SimpleCharacterHighIndexStatement` gives the domain and value formulas at natural depths `m ≥ ⌊n/2⌋`. The filtered character domain is `U^{m+1}(𝔄)` and every member of the simple character set agrees with `ψ_β` on that group. The value formula quantifies over members of the set. It applies to the total recursion in `simpleCharacters`, including depths `m ≥ n` in Lean.

Bushnell and Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), fix the base character in §1.1, printed pp. 19–20 (PDF pp. 31–32), prove the high-index formula in (3.2.4), printed p. 99 (PDF p. 111), and use the singleton argument in (3.5.8), printed pp. 120–121 (PDF pp. 132–133). The source identifier is `bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln` and the retained PDF SHA-256 is `811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`.

The proof extracts the successive critical depths and congruences from the defining sequence. Each nonterminal depth `r` satisfies `r < n`. Induction on the tail shows that the lattice `ℌ` contains `𝔓^{⌊n/2⌋+1}` and the identity, so every element of `U^{m+1}(𝔄)` is in the carrier of `H^{m+1}`. The opposite inclusion holds for the subgroup generated by the carrier.

At the last element the defining agreement with `ψ_β` applies on the entire domain. At a nonterminal step with next element `γ`, the recursive test depth `max(m,⌊r/2⌋)` equals `m`. For `m < r`, multiply the inductive value `ψ_γ` by `ψ_{β−γ}` to obtain `ψ_β`. For `r ≤ m`, the recursive comparison is untwisted. The element `β−γ` belongs to `𝔓^{-r} ⊆ 𝔓^{-m}`. The conductor condition on `ψ_F` is the input to `additiveDuality` that makes the twist trivial there. Extensionality then gives a subsingleton set. In `simpleCharacterCoherence_of_half_le` the common element identifies both transported sets with its singleton.
