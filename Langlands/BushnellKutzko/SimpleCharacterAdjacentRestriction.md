# Adjacent restriction and nonemptiness

At and above the critical cutoff, the two adjacent perturbation characters restrict compatibly because both are defined by the same trace formula. The subgroup identifications and twist equivalences therefore transport surjectivity from the approximating element. At the critical depth the twist is trivial on the deeper domain, so the argument includes the boundary of the extended notation following BK93 (3.2.3).

For \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\le m\) with \(m+1<n\), both sets in the restriction map are singletons and their characters are given by the same trace formula. Once adjacent restriction is surjective in the whole range, descending from the high-index singleton at \(n-1\) gives nonemptiness at each lower index, as in the induction underlying BK93 (3.3.21).
