# Invariants from a common simple character

`SimpleCharacterInvariantsStatement` fixes a hereditary order represented by
a lattice chain in a finite-dimensional vector space over a nonarchimedean
local field `F`, integers `0 ≤ m < n` and an admissible additive character
`ψ_F`. If two simple strata at those levels share a pair `(H, θ)`, their
critical exponents `k₀` are equal. Their generated fields `F[β]` also have
equal ramification indices and equal residue degrees over `F`. The shared
pair includes equality of the character domains `H^{m+1}`.

Bushnell and Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups*
(1993), (3.5.1), printed p. 117 (PDF p. 129). Literature ID
`bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual-gln`, SHA-256
