# Jacquet nonvanishing for character types in matrix coordinates

`CharacterTypeJacquetTransportStatement` gives a sufficient condition for an admissible smooth representation `π` of `GL_n(F)` to have a nonzero proper Jacquet module. It takes a smooth type on a compact open subgroup `K` that occurs in `π` and whose transport to `Aut_F(F^n)` is isomorphic to a character `χ` on a subgroup `K′`. The premise supplies, for each representation of `Aut_F(F^n)` on the carrier of `π` with finite-dimensional `χ`-isotypic subspace meeting the complement of the relations of `ker χ`, two nonzero complementary orthogonal idempotents for which that subspace also meets the complement of the upper block unipotent relations.

`jacquet_nonvanishing_of_characterType` transports the character to `K` and uses a nonzero intertwining map to obtain a nonzero isotypic vector in `π`. The stabilizer in `K` of a nonzero vector in the type representation is open and lies in the character kernel. Admissibility gives finite-dimensionality of the isotypic subspace. The character-twisted average over the compact type subgroup `K` fixes the chosen isotypic vector and annihilates the relations of the character kernel. Thus the vector survives those relations. Applying the premise after transport to `Aut_F(F^n)` gives the two idempotents. A basis adapted to their images supplies a proper standard block shape and a conjugate of its radical in `GL_n(F)`. Transport of relations and the isotypic vector yield nonzero coinvariants there.

This expresses the initial isotypy and final proper Jacquet module in the argument of Bushnell and Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), (8.2.5)(iv) and the descent on printed pp. 281–289, in matrix coordinates.
