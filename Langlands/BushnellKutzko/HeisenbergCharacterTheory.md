# The character argument for Heisenberg representations

The finite-index coinduced representation of a conjugation-invariant character admits an irreducible subrepresentation on which the subgroup acts through that character. For an irreducible representation containing the character, its eigenspace spans the carrier under the group action. An evaluated commutator different from one forces the trace to vanish at the conjugated element. The finite-quotient calculation uses Mathlib's character formula for the dimension of the space of equivariant linear maps.

This is the representation-theoretic argument underlying Bushnell–Kutzko, *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups* (1993), Proposition (5.1.1), printed p. 158 (PDF p. 170).
