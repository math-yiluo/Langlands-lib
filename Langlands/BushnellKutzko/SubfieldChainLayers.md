# Extension residue scalars on the successive lattice quotients

The original layer and the layer from the extension lattice chain have
the same vectors and the same quotient relation. The residue scalar
structure therefore acts on the original layers. A field unit transports
this action to the translated layer. An integer-ring unit fixes the
layer index and acts by multiplication by its residue.

For a family of base-linear layer endomorphisms, the extension residue
scalars commuting with the family form a subalgebra over the base
integer ring. The corresponding integer units are exactly the integer
units in the subgroup intertwining the family.

The residue comparison and scalar action are derived for the proof of
Bushnell–Kutzko (1993), (1.4.14)–(1.4.15), printed p. 41.
