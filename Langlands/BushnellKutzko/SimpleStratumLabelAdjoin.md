# The field `F[γ, α]` and its invariants

Let `E` act on `V` with a generator `γ_E` over `F` mapping to
`γ ∈ End_F(V)`, and let `α ∈ End_E(V)` generate a field `E[α]`. The
module proves:

- `restrictScalarsRingHom` and `restrictScalarsRingHom_injective`:
  restriction of scalars `End_E(V) → End_F(V)` is an injective ring
  homomorphism;
- `mem_adjoin_pair_iff`: its image of `E[α]` is the `F`-subalgebra
  `F[γ, α]` generated by `γ` and the restriction of `α`;
- `subalgebraInvariants_adjoin_pair`: with `E` carrying its spectral
  structure,

  `e(F[γ,α]|F) = e(E|F) · e(E[α]|E)` and
  `f(F[γ,α]|F) = f(E|F) · f(E[α]|E)`,

  for `subalgebraRamificationIndex`, `subalgebraResidueDegree`,
  `elementRamificationIndex` and `elementResidueDegree`.

## Proof

Restriction of scalars maps `E[α]` bijectively onto `F[γ, α]`, so
`F[γ, α]` is a field; it is an `E`-algebra through `E → F[γ] ⊆ F[γ, α]`
and the bijection is an isomorphism of `E`-algebras. The tower formulas
of `SimpleStratumLabelTower` for `F ⊆ E ⊆ F[γ, α]` and the invariance of
the invariants under `E`-algebra isomorphisms
(`PureApproximation.subfieldInvariants_of_algEquiv`) give the products.

This is the identity (1.12) of Bushnell–Kutzko 1994,
`bushnell-kutzko-1994-simple-types-conjugacy-classes`, printed p. 115,
for the field that the refinement theorem BK93 (2.2.8) attaches to a
refinement.
