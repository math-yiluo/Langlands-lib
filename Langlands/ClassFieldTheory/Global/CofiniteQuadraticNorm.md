# Cofinite quadratic norm representation at finite places

A principal idèle has valuation-unit coordinates outside a finite set of places. Away also from the ramified places of a number field extension, any such coordinate is the norm of a unit at a completion above the base place. In the completed tensor algebra, the proof uses that unit in one factor and one in the remaining factors. The product formula for the determinant norm gives the required principal coordinate.

For a quadratic norm form \(x^2-dy^2\) with \(d\ne 0\), the argument uses the quadratic field `QuadraticAlgebra K d 0` when \(d\) is nonsquare. The tensor basis of the quadratic field extends to the standard quadratic-algebra basis over each completion, identifying the determinant norm with the displayed form. When \(d=r^2\) in the base field, the two explicit coordinates \((a+1)/2\) and \((a-1)/(2r)\) represent \(a\) at every place.

The cofinite norm observation is used after Proposition 4.2 in Prasad and Rapinchuk, *Local-global principles for embedding of fields with involution into simple algebras with involution*, arXiv:0806.0596v5, p. 15.
