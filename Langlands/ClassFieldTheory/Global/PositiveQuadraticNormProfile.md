# Positive elements with prescribed quadratic local norm cosets

Let \(L/F\) be a quadratic extension of number fields with \(L\) totally complex. Let \(S\) be a finite set of finite places \(p\) of \(F\) for which the chosen completion of \(L\) above \(p\) has degree two over \(F_p\). If \(S\) has even cardinality, `positiveQuadraticNormProfile` constructs a unit of the field \(F\) that is positive at every real place and a local norm from the chosen completion of \(L\) exactly at the finite places outside \(S\). The local norm condition uses the actual completion maps. The fields are in a common Lean universe.

The construction applies `exists_relativeCohomology_of_finiteInvariants_archimedean` with invariant \(1/2\) on \(S\), zero elsewhere, and trivial archimedean invariants. The degree-two hypothesis puts the nonzero invariants in the relative local images. The even cardinality makes their sum zero. The cyclic carry-class construction then represents the resulting global class by a field unit. Localization carries it to the corresponding local cyclic classes. Injectivity of the relative invariant and the cyclic norm criterion identify its finite norm cosets. At each real place of \(F\), a complex place above it has nontrivial conjugation. The archimedean invariant there is the sign class of the embedded unit, so its vanishing gives positivity.
