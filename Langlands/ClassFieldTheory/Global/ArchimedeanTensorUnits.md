# Tensor units over an archimedean coefficient field

Let `B/F` be an extension of number fields, let `K = F_v` be an
infinite completion, and take a field `M/K` with its compatible
`F`-algebra structure. The units of `B ⊗[F] M` identify with the
product of the units of `B_w ⊗[K] M` over `w` above `v`.
The isomorphism is the tensor extension of the canonical product
comparison at `v`, with the tensor factors ordered as indicated.
It uses the finite-product tensor equivalence. Each coordinate
intertwines automorphisms of `M/K`, and the determinant norm is
the product of the norms in the completed factors.

For an algebraically closed finite Galois `M/K`, a crossed cocycle
with values in the original tensor units is a crossed coboundary.
Embed each `B_w` over `K` into `M`, then apply tensor-unit Hilbert 90
in each factor. The inverse coordinate equivalence combines the
resulting units into a single unit of `B ⊗[F] M`.
