# Prescribed local tensor norm classes

`Langlands.ClassFieldTheory.Global.PrescribedTensorNormClassesStatement` uses a diamond of number fields \(F\subset E,K\subset P\) with finite abelian extensions \(E/F\) and \(P/K\) and injective restriction \(\operatorname{Gal}(P/K)\to\operatorname{Gal}(E/F)\). Let \(a_v\) be units of \(F_v\otimes_F K\), equal to one at all but finitely many finite places of \(F\). If there is a single \(d\in F^\times\) for which \(dN(a_v)\) is a norm from \(F_v\otimes_F E\) at every place, there is a global \(x\in K^\times\) in the class of every \(a_v\) modulo the norm from \((F_v\otimes_F K)\otimes_K P\). The statement uses the determinant norms, actual completions, and a common universe for the four fields.

The proof glues the local family as a relative idèle, using the integral coordinates of the identity idèle where \(a_v=1\). On passing to ordinary idèles, the norm to \(F\) times the principal idèle of \(d\) is in the idèle norm range by the local tensor norm criterion. The principal idèle disappears in the quotient. The Artin norm–restriction square and injectivity give trivial Artin symbol for the original idèle class over \(K\). The Artin kernel gives a norm idèle class from \(P\). Choosing a representative gives a principal correction \(x\). The tower equivalence takes the ordinary idèle norm to the norm over the intermediate relative adele ring. Projecting the inverse of the norm witness at each place gives the displayed orientation \(x=a_v N(z_v)\).

The Artin square and principal correction are those in Prasad and Rapinchuk, *Local-global principles for embedding of fields with involution into simple algebras with involution*, arXiv:0806.0596v5, proof of Proposition 4.2, pp. 14–15, particularly equation (12).
