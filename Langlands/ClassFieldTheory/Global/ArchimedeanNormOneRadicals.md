# Archimedean radicals for norm-one torsion cocycles

The completed tensor `B ⊗[F] F_v` of a number-field extension at an
infinite place has unit coordinates in the completions `B_w` above `v`.
A global left-factor unit gives its ordinary images in the `B_w`.
Under a further coefficient extension `M/F_v`, the completed coordinates
become left-factor units in the tensors `B_w ⊗[F_v] M`. Consequently the
action of `Gal(M/F_v)` fixes every unit extended from the completion tensor.

Assume `M/F_v` is finite Galois and `M` is algebraically closed. A crossed
cocycle of units of `B ⊗[F] M` with norm one and `n`-th power one has a
tensor-unit radical `x` with coboundary `σ(x)/x` by tensor-unit Hilbert 90.
The power `x^n` is fixed, so each coordinate descends to a unit of `B_w`.
The inverse completed comparison gives `b` in the units of `B ⊗[F] F_v`
with image `x^n`. The determinant norm of `x` is also fixed and descends
to a unit `s` of `F_v`. Norm compatibility under scalar extension and
injectivity of `F_v → M` give `N(b) = s^n`.

This internal comparison applies fixed-point descent and the archimedean
tensor-unit comparison to the degree-one correction of norm-one torsion lifts.
