# Degree-zero Tate corestriction and field norms

This derived supporting unit proves the comparison of actual degree-zero Tate
corestriction with the determinant norm in a finite Galois tower `M/L/K`,
in common arbitrary universe `u`.

The invariant-unit construction needs only a field tower. The coset-action
formula assumes normality of both `L/K` and `M/K`. The coset-norm identity
assumes finite dimensionality of `L/K` and `M/K` and that these extensions
are Galois. The final Tate comparison uses finite dimensionality and the
Galois property for all three extensions. The separate degree-zero reindexing
identity needs only finite dimensionality of `M/K` and `M/L`. The
corestriction formula on individual base-unit classes does not require the
Galois property of `M/L`.

The invariant middle-field unit is identified explicitly after the published
group-coordinate change. Normal restriction identifies its coset action with
the action of the corresponding automorphism of `L/K`. Summing over the coset
quotient therefore gives the usual automorphism product, which is the
determinant norm. The existing degree-zero class formulas and surjectivity of
base-unit representatives then give the comparison on every Tate class.

This is derived from the Library Tate reindexing and degree-zero corestriction
class formulas, its actual unit-invariant and norm quotient comparisons,
the preceding Galois coset equivalence, and the determinant-norm automorphism
formula. No local reciprocity theorem is assumed or asserted.
