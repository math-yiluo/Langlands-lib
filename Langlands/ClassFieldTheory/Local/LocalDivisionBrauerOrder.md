# Exact Brauer order and the degree-two invariant

The actual restriction and invariant isomorphisms give the splitting
criterion in Serre, *Local Fields*, Chapter XIII, §3, Corollary 1 to
Proposition 7. Over a compatible finite field extension, a class becomes
zero exactly when its multiple by the extension degree is zero. On an
actual algebra, a zero class is equivalent to a split matrix chart.

For a finite-dimensional central division algebra of degree `n`, its
actual degree-`n` splitting field shows that `n` kills its class. The
class therefore has positive order dividing `n`. An unramified field of
degree equal to the order also splits the algebra, so the splitting-degree
divisibility theorem gives the reverse divisibility. This proves the
order computation in Corollary 3 in every characteristic.

In degree two, the invariant lies in the two-torsion of Q/Z and is
nonzero by the actual division-class nonsplitting theorem. The
denominator equivalence identifies the unique nonzero two-torsion class
with the image of the rational number `1/2`. Its nonvanishing follows
from the same equivalence.

The half-class nonvanishing lemma is available in the module defining `rationalClass`.
