# Norm images of unit filtrations in finite abelian local extensions

This module proves the Library statement
`Langlands.LocalClassField.FiniteAbelianNormUnitImageCardinality.{u,v}` as the
theorem
`Langlands.LocalClassField.NormUnitImage.finiteAbelianNormUnitImageCardinality`.
The statement says that for a finite abelian extension `E/F` of p-adic fields
(in independent universes `u` and `v`), arbitrary supplied topological data `T`
on `F` and `S` on `E`, and every real `a ≥ 0`, the image of `U_F^{⌈a⌉₊}` in
`Fˣ / N_{E/F}(Eˣ)` has as many elements as the upper ramification group
`Gal(E/F)^a`.

The mathematics is Serre, *Local Fields*, Chapter XV, §2 (Theorems 1 and 2),
with the prime-degree norm computations of Chapter V. The Lean proof does not
follow Serre's order of argument. Serre treats the totally ramified case first
and then passes through the inertia field. Here the proof uses the internal
divisibility claims `LevelClaims K L` of `NormUnitImageTower`. They hold in
prime degree (`NormUnitImagePrimeStep`) and pass up towers
(`levelClaims_tower`), and a finite abelian extension is a tower of
prime-degree steps. A final counting lemma (`card_map_mk_eq_of_dvd` in
`NormUnitImageIndexSupport`) turns the claims and the norm index equality
`[Fˣ : N(Eˣ)] = [E : F]` into the cardinality equality.

All four modules of this proof (`NormUnitImageIndexSupport`,
`NormUnitImageTower`, `NormUnitImagePrimeStep` and this one) work with the
canonical topological data `K.canonicalTopologicalData` of each field. The
last step below transports the result to arbitrary `T` and `S`.

## Degree one

- `ramificationIndex_eq_of_finrank_eq_one`: if `[L : K] = 1`, then `K` and `L`
  have the same absolute ramification index. Write `e(K)`, `e(L)` for these
  indices, `c` for the normalized `L`-order of the image of a uniformizer of
  `K`, and `f` for the relative residue degree. Then `e(K) · c = e(L)`
  (`ramificationIndex_mul_ord_relative_algebraMap` at the uniformizer, whose
  `K`-order is `1`). Also `e(L) · f = [L : K] · e(K) = e(K)`
  (`PadicField.ramificationIndex_mul_extensionResidueDegree` with
  `extensionDegree_suppliedAlgebra`). Both indices are positive, so `f ≥ 1`
  and `c ≥ 1`. Hence `e(L) ≤ e(K)` and `e(K) ≤ e(L)`.
- `levelClaims_of_finrank_eq_one`: an abelian extension of degree one
  satisfies `LevelClaims`. It is unramified by the previous lemma, so this is
  `levelClaims_of_ramificationIndex_eq` from `NormUnitImagePrimeStep`.

## Induction on the degree

`levelClaims_of_finrank_eq K n` proves `LevelClaims K L` for every finite
abelian `L/K` of degree `n`, with `L` ranging over `PadicField.{v} p`. The
proof is strong induction on `n`.

- If `n < 2`, then `n = 1` (degrees are positive), which is the degree-one
  case.
- Otherwise choose a prime `q ∣ n` (`Nat.exists_prime_and_dvd`). Since
  `|Gal(L/K)| = n` (`IsGalois.card_aut_eq_finrank`), Cauchy's theorem
  (`exists_prime_orderOf_dvd_card'`) gives `σ` of order `q`. Let `H` be the
  cyclic subgroup generated by `σ` and `E = Padic.fixedPadicField K L H`. This
  is the fixed field of `H`, packaged as a p-adic field in universe `v`. The
  compatibility `IsScalarTower (Padic p) K E` holds because the embeddings of
  `ℚ_p` into `K`, `E` and `L` agree. By Galois theory
  (`IntermediateField.finrank_fixedField_eq_card`), `[L : E] = |H| = q`. Then
  `[E : K] · q = n` (`Module.finrank_mul_finrank`), so `[E : K] < n`.
- `E/K` and `L/E` are again abelian, since subextensions of an abelian
  extension are abelian. The induction hypothesis gives
  `LevelClaims K E`, and `levelClaims_of_prime` gives `LevelClaims E L`
  because `[L : E] = q` is prime. The tower law `levelClaims_tower` gives
  `LevelClaims K L`.

`levelClaims_of_isAbelianGalois K L` applies this with `n = [L : K]`.

## Independence of the topological data

- `unitFiltration_eq_of_topologicalData`: two topological data `T`, `T'` on
  the same p-adic field give the same unit filtration. By
  `unitFiltration_eq_principalUnits`, `U^n` consists of the units `x` with
  `v(x) = 1` and `v(x - 1) ≤ exp(-n)` for the discrete valuation `v` of the
  data. `NYLL.discreteValuation_eq_of_algEquiv`, applied to the identity
  automorphism, says the two discrete valuations agree.
- For the upper ramification groups the analogous fact is the published
  `HerbrandBridge.upperRamificationGroup_eq_of_realization`: `G^a` computed
  from `S` equals `G^a` computed from `E.canonicalTopologicalData`.

## The main theorem

Fix `p`, `F`, `E`, `T`, `S` and `a ≥ 0`, and put `n = ⌈a⌉₊` and
`m = ⌈ψ_{E/F}(a)⌉₊`. After rewriting with the two transport lemmas, the
claim concerns the canonical data. Apply `card_map_mk_eq_of_dvd` in `Fˣ` with

- `Nm = N_{E/F}(Eˣ)` (`normSubgroup`), whose index is `[E : F] ≠ 0` by
  `normSubgroup_index_eq_finrank`;
- `Un = U_F^n`;
- `V = N_{E/F}(U_E^m)`, which lies in `Nm` (`Subgroup.map_le_range`);
- `c = |G^a|`.

The relative index of `V` in `Un` divides `c` by claim (V) of
`levelClaims_of_isAbelianGalois F E` at `a`. By claim (WZ),
`[Fˣ : Un ⊔ Nm] · c` divides `[E : F] = [Fˣ : Nm]`. The lemma then says that
the image of `U_F^n` in `Fˣ / N_{E/F}(Eˣ)` has exactly `c = |G^a|` elements.
