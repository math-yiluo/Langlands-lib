# The absolute local invariant on separable unit cohomology

Serre, *Local Fields*, Chapter XIII, §3, Proposition 6 defines the value on
Brauer cohomology by unramified inflation, valuation, the inverse connecting
map and arithmetic Frobenius. This module constructs the isomorphism on
continuous second cohomology with the discrete units of the actual
separable closure, for every nonarchimedean local field.

A finite unramified stage is presented by its open normal fixing subgroup.
The inflation exhaustion theorem gives a finite representative of every
continuous class. Intersecting two subgroups gives a common unramified
stage, since both contain inertia. Actual inflation is injective at that
stage. Representatives of the same continuous class therefore agree
there, and the finite invariant is preserved by refinement. The resulting
value is unique and additive. At each stage the formula is Serre's corrected
inverse-connecting computation by the spectral unramified invariant theorem.

Injectivity follows from injectivity of the finite values and exhaustion.
For surjectivity, choose a rational representative of a class in Q/Z and
an unramified stage of degree equal to its positive denominator. The finite
invariant has as image the classes killed by that degree, so this stage
supplies a preimage under the absolute value.
