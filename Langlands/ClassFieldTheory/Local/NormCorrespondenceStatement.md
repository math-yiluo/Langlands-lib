# Local norm-subgroup correspondence

This pair defines propositions, not proofs, for the lattice and separation
parts of local class field theory. It complements the separately submitted
norm properness, index, and existence family without assuming that family.

## Exact mathematical content

`Langlands.LocalClassField.NormSubgroupLattice` quantifies every prime `p`,
every `F : PadicField.{u} p`, and two finite abelian intermediate extensions
`E₁`, `E₂` in the actual `AlgebraicClosure F.Carrier`.
The finite-dimensional and abelian Galois hypotheses describe the two
extensions; they contain no reciprocity or norm-correspondence hypothesis.

The local abbreviation `N E` is the image of the actual multiplicative
field norm, `Units.map (Algebra.norm F.Carrier (S := E))`. The conclusion is:

- `E₁ ≤ E₂` if and only if `N E₂ ≤ N E₁`.
- The norm group of the compositum `E₁ ⊔ E₂` is `N E₁ ⊓ N E₂`.
- The norm group of the intersection `E₁ ⊓ E₂` is `N E₁ ⊔ N E₂`.

The lattice operations on fields are Mathlib's actual intermediate-field
operations. The supremum on subgroups means the subgroup generated by their
union. In this commutative multiplicative group it is their product. It
corresponds to Serre's sum notation for an abstract additive class formation.
The definitions do not identify a field compositum with a tensor product.

`Langlands.LocalClassField.NormSubgroupsSeparatePoints` states that each
nonidentity element of `F.Carrierˣ` is absent from the norm group of some
finite abelian subextension of the actual algebraic closure. This is the
assertion that the intersection of all finite abelian norm groups is the
identity subgroup, written elementwise. The witness field, its finiteness,
and its abelian Galois property are conclusions.

`Langlands.LocalClassField.NormCorrespondenceProperties` conjoins these
two targets. The arbitrary carrier universe is retained. No topology or
topological realization is an extra assumption: these are algebraic
consequences for all finite extensions of `Q_p`.

Worker 10 read Chapter XI, section 4, Proposition 4, printed pages 172-173
(PDF pages 178-179), and visually checked its two lattice formulas on PDF
page 179. That proposition gives exactly the reversed inclusion and the
two displayed lattice identities. Serre's abstract abelian extensions are
specialized here to actual characteristic-zero local-field extensions in
a fixed algebraic closure.

Chapter XIV, section 6, Corollary 2, printed page 219 (PDF page 224),
states the intersection-of-norm-groups conclusion. Chapter XI, section 4,
Proposition 3, printed page 172 (PDF page 178), identifies the norm group
of any finite extension with that of its largest abelian subextension.
Thus quantifying only finite abelian subextensions preserves the original
intersection statement. The original passages were read directly.

The correspondence is independent of the future arithmetic or geometric
Frobenius convention: inverting reciprocity leaves its norm kernels
unchanged. This pair introduces neither an Artin map nor an inverse map
into the nonabelian Weil group.
