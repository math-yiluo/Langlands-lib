# Vanishing of continuous H² of separable inertia

Let \(F\) be a nonarchimedean local field, \(\Omega\) its separable
closure, and \(U=\Omega^{I}\) the fixed field of separable inertia.
`separableInertiaH2_subsingleton` proves that \(H^2(I,\Omega^\times)=0\),
using the discrete topology on the actual unit group and the restricted
action of inertia.

The construction `galoisUnitDiscreteModule` packages the actual Galois
action on discrete units with its joint continuity.
`fixingSubgroupContinuousEquiv` upgrades the intermediate-field fixing
subgroup isomorphism to a continuous isomorphism for the Krull
topologies. For continuity of restriction of scalars, a finite
subextension of the base becomes an intermediate field generated by the
same finite set over the upper base. Its open fixing subgroup maps into
the original fixing subgroup. Compactness of the Galois group over the
intermediate field and the Hausdorff property give continuity of the
inverse. The associated unit-module isomorphism acts as the identity
on the underlying units.

For each open normal subgroup of \(\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/U)\), the
quotient action on fixed units identifies with the action on the units
of its finite Galois fixed field \(M\). For each intermediate field
\(E\) of \(M/U\), the separable inertia norm theorem gives
surjectivity of the norm from \(M^\times\) to \(E^\times\). The finite
norm criterion then makes each finite quotient's relative \(H^2\)
trivial. Finite quotient comparison identifies continuous \(H^2\)
with the colimit of these groups. Transport along the continuous
fixing subgroup equivalence gives the result for inertia itself.

This is the vanishing over the maximal unramified extension used in
Serre, *Local Fields* (1979), Chapter XII, §3, printed pp. 185–187,
and in the local invariant construction of Chapter XIII, §3,
Proposition 6. The Lean proof uses the Library's finite-stage norm
criterion and colimit comparison. The Krull continuity argument
adapts the finite-generator proof in
`Langlands/Galois/AbsoluteTopologyProof.lean`. All coefficient and
automorphism groups in the result come from the separable closure,
including when the local field is imperfect.
