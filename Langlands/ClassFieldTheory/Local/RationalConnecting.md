# The connecting isomorphism for rational classes

Serre, *Local Fields*, Chapter XIII, §3, Proposition 6 uses the inverse
of the connecting homomorphism from first cohomology with Q/Z coefficients
to second cohomology with integer coefficients. This is the direction
specified in the official correction to the displayed arrow on printed
page 193.

The coefficient sequence is short exact with the usual integer inclusion
and the quotient map to the actual rational classes modulo integers. All
three actions are trivial. In degree one a character from a finite group
to the rationals vanishes by torsion-freeness. In degree two, summing the
cocycle equation over its third argument and dividing by the group order
expresses each rational cocycle as the coboundary of its average. These
vanishings make the connecting homomorphism an isomorphism for finite
groups, with the direction used by the corrected formula.
