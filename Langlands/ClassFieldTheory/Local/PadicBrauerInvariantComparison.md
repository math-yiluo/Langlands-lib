# Comparison of the p-adic class-field and Brauer invariants

The spectral unramified Frobenius coordinate satisfies the arithmetic
residue condition for the canonical p-adic field bundle. The two base
integer rings agree by compatibility of the valuations. The two upper
integer rings therefore agree by their common integrality criterion over
the base integer ring.

The resulting residue-field equivalence respects the residue action,
since both integer automorphisms restrict the same field automorphism.
It carries the faithful residue action and the arithmetic power formula
to the canonical p-adic residue fields. This is an internal comparison
used to align the local Brauer invariant with the class-field family.

On a spectral unramified stage the cyclic class of the canonical
uniformizer has invariant `1/d`, where `d` is the local degree. Its
image generates the denominator subgroup and the spectral invariant
is injective. Thus arithmetic normalization gives agreement on the
entire stage.

Every central simple algebra has an unramified splitting chart.
Given a finite Galois chart, the two charts extend to a common
Galois field inside a separable closure. The relative invariant
is preserved under inflation, and the relative class is independent
of the chart over the common field. The unramified comparison
therefore identifies the relative class-field invariant with the
intrinsic Brauer invariant for the supplied finite Galois chart.
