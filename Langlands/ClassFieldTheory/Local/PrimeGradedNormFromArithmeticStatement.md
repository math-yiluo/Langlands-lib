# Conditional assembly of the prime graded norm

Author: Worker 96.

`PrimeGradedNormFromArithmetic.{u,v}` states that proofs of the published
`PrimeRamifiedTraceIdeals.{u,v}` and `PrimeNormTraceExpansion.{u,v}` imply
the entire published `PrimeRamifiedGradedNorm.{u,v}` proposition. It is an
internal assembly target. The two arithmetic propositions remain explicit
hypotheses; neither this statement nor a proof of it alone proves the closed
graded-norm theorem required by the host task.

The conclusion is the existing proposition itself, with no clause changed or
omitted. In particular, it retains independent field universes, the supplied
algebra and scalar tower, prime relative degree, Galoisness, total ramification,
the actual lower-group break pattern, residue-map bijectivity, both full-index
norm containments, the zero-level residue-unit power formula, and the below,
critical and above-break formulas for arbitrary supplied order-one uniformizers.
The coefficients remain literal integral norm or trace quotients with nonzero
residues; the formulas use the actual forward relative residue map.

The arithmetic hypotheses have independent content: the first identifies the
trace image of every nonnegative source ideal power, while the second expresses
the norm-expansion remainder as a trace from the doubled source ideal power.
Neither assumes any graded-coordinate formula. This proposition records their
intended use without asserting that either arithmetic input is already proved.
Its provenance is an internal composition of the three published propositions.
The mathematical calculation is Serre, *Local Fields* (1979), V.3, Proposition 5,
printed pages 84–85 (PDF pages 91–92); the registered 2019 author corrections,
PDF pages 2–3, contain no correction to that passage.
