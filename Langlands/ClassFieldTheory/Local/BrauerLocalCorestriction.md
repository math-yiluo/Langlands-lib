# Corestriction of local Brauer classes

Multiplication by a positive integer on Q/Z is onto. Given a rational
representative of a class, divide it by the integer and take the resulting
class. The scalar-extension degree law and the invariant equivalences then
give surjectivity of scalar extension on the actual Brauer group.

For a finite separable local extension, both tensor-descent corestriction and
continuous unit corestriction multiply by the field degree after their
respective restriction maps. The descent comparison intertwines the two
restriction maps. These identities give the corestriction comparison on
scalar extensions and hence, by surjectivity, on every upper Brauer class.
The resulting equality also identifies the two additive homomorphisms on
Brauer classes.

The cohomological invariant after corestriction of a restricted class
equals the degree times the source invariant, which is also the invariant
after restriction. Surjectivity and the corestriction comparison give the
preservation formula for the invariant on all actual Brauer classes.

The degree argument follows the use of surjective restriction in Serre's
*Local Fields*, Chapter XI, section 2, Proposition 1(ii), printed pages
167–168.
