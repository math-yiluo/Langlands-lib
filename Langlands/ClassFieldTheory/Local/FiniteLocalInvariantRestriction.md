# Restriction at finite unramified stages

This module proves the finite-stage computation for Serre, *Local Fields*,
Chapter XIII, §3, Proposition 7. The base embedding may be inseparable.
A lower finite unramified Galois stage has an upper unramified compositum
stage in the specified separable closures. The cohomology map uses actual
inflation to the compositum followed by restriction to the upper base.

Lower relative classes are positive carry classes. Under restriction to
the compositum the upper arithmetic Frobenius has lower coordinate equal
to the residue degree modulo the lower unramified degree. The upper cyclic
norm computation adds the carries along powers of this generator. The
carry identity turns that sum into the residue degree contribution to the
invariant modulo integers. The included defining unit has upper order
multiplied by the ramification factor. The product of the two factors is
the whole base-extension degree, giving the required finite-stage value.
