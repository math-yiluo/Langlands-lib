# Finite unramified composita and arithmetic Frobenius restriction

Let \(K\to L\) be a compatible embedding of nonarchimedean local
fields. For a \(K\)-embedding of their separable closures and an
intermediate field \(E\) of \(K^{\mathrm{sep}}/K\),
`unramifiedCompositum` is the intermediate field \(M\) of
\(L^{\mathrm{sep}}/L\) generated by the image of \(E\).
The image of a primitive element generates \(M\) if \(E/K\) is
finite. Normality of \(E/K\) makes its image stable under the
upper Galois group, so the compositum is Galois if \(E/K\) is.

Actual restriction of automorphisms along the separable-closure
embedding commutes with the maps on spectral residue fields.
Since those maps are injective, restriction carries upper inertia
into lower inertia. Thus the image of the lower inertia fixed
field lies in the upper inertia fixed field, and the finite
unramified maximality theorem makes \(M/L\) arithmetically
unramified when \(E/K\) is.

The restriction map on the finite stages commutes with the same
residue embeddings. Arithmetic upper Frobenius acts on residues
by the upper residue cardinality power. That cardinality is the
lower cardinality to the residue degree. Iterating the lower
arithmetic Frobenius gives the same power, and faithfulness of
the lower unramified residue action proves the restriction law.
The theorem applies to every pair of automorphisms with those
arithmetic Frobenius actions.

These are finite-stage forms of the unramified-extension and
distinguished-generator calculations used in Serre, *Local
Fields* (1979), Chapter XIII, §3, Proposition 7, printed
pp. 193–194. The Lean proof uses actual spectral residue fields
and allows arbitrary field characteristic and compatible base
extensions, including inseparable finite extensions.
