# Tate isomorphisms of a finite class formation

Author: Worker 272.

This module proves the published proposition
`Langlands.ClassFieldTheory.Local.ClassFormationTateIsomorphismsStatement`
(module `Langlands.ClassFieldTheory.Local.ClassFormationCupLawsStatement`).
The exact-target theorem is

```
theorem classFormationTateIsomorphisms :
    ClassFormationTateIsomorphismsStatement.{u}
```

in the namespace `Langlands.ClassFieldTheory.Local`. Unfolded, it says:
assume Tate's cup-product theorem `Langlands.Tate.TateCupIsomorphism.{u}`.
Then for every prime `p` and every raw invariant family
`I : InvariantFamily.{u,u} p` with `I.RelativeClassFormation`,
`I.TateIsomorphisms` holds. That is, for every p-adic field `F`, every finite
Galois extension `L/F`, every class `c` of `H²(Gal(L/F), Lˣ)` with
`I.relative F L c = 1/[L : F]` (`IsFundamentalClass`) and every integer `n`,
the left cup map `relativeTateCup F L n c : Ĥⁿ(Gal(L/F), ℤ) → Ĥⁿ⁺²(Gal(L/F), Lˣ)`
is bijective.

Tate's theorem is a hypothesis of the target proposition, not something this
module proves. Its proof is the separate task `7a725b31`. This module only
checks Tate's hypotheses on every Sylow subgroup.

## Source

Jean-Pierre Serre, *Local Fields*, GTM 67, Springer, 1979 (registered source
`serre-1979-local-fields`, SHA256
`109b461d42915da70771c70c4b551a363fc1f33412331ae2671c5c85be433112`):

- Chapter IX, §8, Theorem 14 and its Corollary (printed page 149, PDF page 155).
  Let `a ∈ H²(G, A)`. Suppose that for every Sylow subgroup `G_p`,
  `H¹(G_p, A) = 0`, and `H²(G_p, A)` is generated by `Res a` and has order
  `|G_p|`. Then cup product with `a` gives isomorphisms
  `Ĥⁿ(G, ℤ) → Ĥⁿ⁺²(G, A)` for every integer `n`.
- Chapter XI, §3 (printed page 168, PDF page 174). The class `u_{F/E}` of
  invariant `1/[F : E]` generates `H²(F/E)`, which is cyclic of order
  `[F : E]`; `Res(u_{F/E}) = u_{F/E'}`. "The classes `u_{F/E}` satisfy the
  hypotheses of the Tate–Nakayama theorem", which gives Theorem 1: cup
  product with `u_{F/E}` is bijective in every degree.

The Lean proof follows Serre's check. The one formal addition is the step
from a Sylow subgroup `P` of `Gal(L/F)` to the extension `L/E` with
`E = L^P`.

## Declarations

### `relativeGaloisSubgroup_intermediateField`

For a tower `K ⊆ M` and an intermediate field `E` of `M/K`, the published
subgroup `relativeGaloisSubgroup K E M` (the image of `Gal(M/E)` under
restriction of scalars) equals Mathlib's `E.fixingSubgroup`. Proof by
extensionality. An image `τ.restrictScalars K` fixes `E` by `τ.commutes`.
Conversely, an automorphism fixing `E` is the image of
`IntermediateField.fixingSubgroupEquiv E` applied to it.

### `InvariantFamily.relativeClassFormation_subgroupConditions`

Let `I` satisfy `I.RelativeClassFormation`. Let `F` and `E` be p-adic fields
with `E/F` finite and `IsScalarTower (Padic p) F E`. Let `L` be a common finite
extension with `L/F` and `L/E` Galois, and let `c` be a fundamental class of
`L/F` for `I`. Write `H = relativeGaloisSubgroup F E L` and
`A = unitsRepresentation F L`. Then:

1. `H¹(H, Res A)` is a subsingleton;
2. `Nat.card H²(H, Res A) = Nat.card H`;
3. the restriction `groupCohomology.map H.subtype (𝟙 _) 2 c` generates
   `H²(H, Res A)` (its `AddSubgroup.zmultiples` is `⊤`).

These are exactly the three Sylow conditions in `TateCupIsomorphism`, for an
arbitrary subgroup of this form. Proof:

- `e₁ = relativeSubgroupCohomologyIso F E L 1` and
  `e₂ = relativeSubgroupCohomologyIso F E L 2` identify `H^k(L/E)` with
  `H^k(H, Res A)`.
- (1) The first class-formation axiom (`LocalRelativeH1Vanishing`) at `L/E`
  makes `H¹(L/E)` a subsingleton, and this transfers along `e₁`.
- The published `fundamentalClasses_of_relativeImages` (from the
  `RelativeImages` axiom) gives a fundamental class `d` of `L/E`, with
  `addOrderOf d = [L : E]` and `zmultiples d = ⊤`.
- The published `fundamentalRestriction_of_relativeClassFormation` gives
  `relativeRestriction F E L 2 c = d`.
- The published `relativeRestriction_comp_subgroupCohomologyIso` says relative
  restriction followed by `e₂` is ordinary restriction to `H`. So the
  restriction of `c` to `H` is `e₂ d`.
- (2) `Nat.card H²(H, Res A) = Nat.card H²(L/E)` along `e₂`. This equals
  `Nat.card (zmultiples d) = addOrderOf d = [L : E]`. That is
  `Nat.card Gal(L/E)` (`IsGalois.card_aut_eq_finrank`), which is `Nat.card H`
  along `relativeGaloisSubgroupEquiv`.
- (3) Every element of `H²(H, Res A)` is `e₂ x` with `x = k • d`, so it is
  `k • e₂ d`.

### `classFormationTateIsomorphisms` (the target)

Fix `hTate : TateCupIsomorphism`, `I`, `hI`, `F`, `L`, a fundamental class
`c` and `n`. With the instance `Fintype.ofFinite Gal(L/F)` used in the
definition of `relativeTateCup`, the cup map is
`integralH2Cup (unitsRepresentation F L) n c`. Apply `hTate` to
`G = Gal(L/F)`, `A = unitsRepresentation F L` and `a = c`. For a prime `q`
and a Sylow subgroup `P`:

- `E := IntermediateField.fixedField P` is made a p-adic field
  `PadicField.ofField E`, with the composite algebra `Padic p → F → E`.
  `FiniteDimensional (Padic p) E` follows from `FiniteDimensional.trans`. The
  remaining structure is Mathlib's intermediate-field structure: the algebras
  `F → E` and `E → L`, the scalar tower `F → E → L`, finite-dimensionality,
  and `IsGalois E L`.
- `relativeGaloisSubgroup F E L = P`, by
  `relativeGaloisSubgroup_intermediateField` and Mathlib's
  `IntermediateField.fixingSubgroup_fixedField`.
- `relativeClassFormation_subgroupConditions` for this `E` gives the three
  conditions for `relativeGaloisSubgroup F E L`. Rewriting along the equality
  above gives them for `P`.

Implementation note. The instances on the carrier of `PadicField.ofField E`
are given as `let`/`have` with typed `inferInstance` terms. Using `letI` with
`inferInstanceAs` here creates auxiliary declarations that the kernel rejects
under the local `let`.
