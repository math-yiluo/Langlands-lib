# The local invariant on actual Brauer classes

For a nonarchimedean local field the invariant is the composite of the
actual Brauer comparison in Serre's descent convention and the absolute
invariant on continuous cohomology of the actual separable-closure units.
It is an additive isomorphism to Q/Z. On a finite unramified splitting
chart its value is the valuation, inverse connecting map and arithmetic
Frobenius applied to Serre's relative factor class. This implements the
construction in *Local Fields*, Chapter XIII, §3, Proposition 6 and the
correction to printed page 193. Unramified cohomological exhaustion and
finite chart realization give such an unramified splitting chart for
every central simple algebra. Vanishing is equivalent to splitting over
the base field.

The restriction formula follows from the finite unramified compositum
computation and compatibility of actual continuous restriction with
relative inflation. The same comparison intertwines restriction with
actual Brauer scalar extension, so Proposition 7 holds for every compatible
finite field extension, including inseparable ones. The identity case
uses scalar extension over the ground field, and a compatible field
isomorphism gives degree one.
