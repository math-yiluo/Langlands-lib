# Embedding a splitting field of reduced degree in the division algebra

A matrix splitting chart makes its vector representation a left module for the original division algebra. When the splitting field has degree equal to the reduced degree, comparison of the dimensions over the base field gives this module rank one over the division algebra. Choosing the rank-one equivalence identifies the field's scalar action with right multiplication by elements of the division algebra. Commutativity of the field makes this an algebra embedding over the original base.

`embeddingOfSplitOfReducedDegree` chooses that embedding. It will specialize to the actual unramified stage in the cyclic presentation of Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 B, printed p. 110.
