# Compatible finite norm equivalences

This internal supporting development selects fundamental classes from a single invariant family and uses the actual degree-minus-two cup product to define finite Galois abelianization/norm quotient equivalences. Its hypotheses explicitly retain fundamental-class existence, Tate bijectivity and transfer compatibility for that same family. The unconditional local class-formation theorem supplies these hypotheses when applying the support.

The corestriction identity is obtained by combining the all-integer fundamental-class corestriction square at degree minus two with the actual low-degree comparisons in `NormExistenceTransfers` and `NormExistenceNormTransfer`. Thus actual scalar restriction of automorphisms corresponds to the actual determinant norm between the corresponding quotients. This assertion concerns these cup equivalences, rather than the unrelated choice used only for finite cardinality in `NormExistenceFiniteSupport`.

All field carriers range in an arbitrary common universe. The construction is derived from existing Library cup and transfer maps and their comparison theorems; it makes no new original-literature claim and does not yet prove the exact norm-subgroup existence target.
