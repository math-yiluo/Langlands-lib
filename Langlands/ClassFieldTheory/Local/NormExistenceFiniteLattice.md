# Finite abelian norm correspondence and saturation

This is an internal consequence of the actual determinant norms, the compatible cup equivalences for a single invariant family, and finite Galois correspondence. The arbitrary common carrier universe is retained throughout. No existence theorem for prescribed norm subgroups or reciprocity completion is assumed.

`abelianNormCorrespondenceEquiv` identifies the actual Galois group of a finite abelian extension with its norm quotient, by composing the canonical isomorphism to its abelianization with the cup equivalence. Its corestriction theorem identifies scalar restriction with the actual quotient tower norm, using the fundamental classes, Tate isomorphisms and transfer compatibility of the same invariant family.

`normQuotientTowerNorm_range` computes the image of the quotient tower norm as the middle-field norm subgroup modulo the top norm subgroup, by representing quotient elements by units. Surjectivity of the middle-field cup equivalence and the corestriction square then identify this subgroup with the image of the relative Galois subgroup. For an actual intermediate field, the relative Galois subgroup is its fixing subgroup; the proof equips the upper extension and the intermediate field with the compatible p-adic algebra structures needed to apply the square.

`finiteAbelianNormOvergroup` proves saturation above a fixed finite abelian norm subgroup. For a subgroup H containing that norm subgroup, let q be the quotient projection and e the Galois/norm equivalence. The intermediate field is the fixed field of the inverse image under e of the image of H under q. Finite Galois correspondence identifies its fixing subgroup with that inverse image. The preceding image theorem and surjectivity of e identify the images of its norm group and H under q. Both groups contain the kernel of q, the first by norm transitivity and the second by assumption, so equality of their quotient images gives equality of the subgroups themselves. The intermediate field is automatically finite and abelian over the base.

`exists_normSubgroup_eq_of_le_finite_abelian` discharges the invariant-family inputs using one witness of the proved local absolute corestriction-compatible class formation. It asserts that every overgroup of the norm group of a specified finite abelian extension is the norm group of an intermediate field of that extension. It does not assert that an arbitrary open finite-index subgroup contains such a finite norm group; that cofinality step and the exact local norm-subgroup existence target remain separate obligations.
