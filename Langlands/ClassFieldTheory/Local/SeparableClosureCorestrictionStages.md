# Finite stages of corestriction on separable-closure units

For a finite separable extension \(L/K\), take a finite Galois stage
\(M\) of the chosen separable closure of \(K\) containing the
embedded copy of \(L\). The embedding \(L\hookrightarrow M\)
uses this copy. The isomorphism of separable closures also embeds
\(M\) into the chosen separable closure of \(L\), and the two
embeddings give the compatible extension tower over \(L\).

`separableClosureStageUnitInflation` transports ordinary relative
cohomology over \(L\) to ordinary relative cohomology over the embedded
copy, then takes its continuous inflation and identifies the upper
continuous cohomology through the isomorphism of closures.
In positive degree, the relative corestriction of the finite stage,
followed by lower inflation, is the absolute corestriction of this
upper inflation. The transports at the upper closure cancel, and
the finite relative transfer commutes with the base reindexing.
Every upper continuous class comes from such a finite stage.

This verifies the finite-stage description of continuous unit
corestriction in Serre, *Local Fields* (1979), Chapter XI, §§1–2,
pp. 166–168, for the two chosen separable closures in every
characteristic.
