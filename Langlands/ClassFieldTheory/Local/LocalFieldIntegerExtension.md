# Integer and residue arithmetic of finite local extensions

Serre, *Local Fields*, Chapter XIII, §3, Proposition 7 uses the product of
ramification index and residue degree as the degree of the local extension.
This module proves the degree formula for the actual compatible valuations
and residue fields in the statement of the local invariant problem.

The field embedding restricts to a local ring homomorphism on the two
integer rings. The normalized order-one element generates the maximal
ideal by the divisibility criterion for a valuation ring. The upper
order of the image of the base generator identifies the extended ideal
with the corresponding power of the upper maximal ideal.

In a finite extension with a continuous field embedding, the coordinates
of the compact upper integer ring in a lower-field basis are bounded.
Dividing all coordinates by a sufficiently large base scalar embeds
the upper integer ring into a finite free module over the base integer
ring. The base is Noetherian, so the upper ring is finite, and it is free
by torsion-freeness over a discrete valuation ring.

The quotient by the extended base maximal ideal has length equal to
the ramification factor over the upper integer ring. Restricting
scalars multiplies this length by the actual residue degree. Over the
base residue field the same quotient has dimension equal to the rank
of the integer extension and hence to the degree of the field
extension. These arguments do not require separability.
