# Finite-stage inflation for the absolute transfer comparison

For a finite separable extension \(L/K\), let \(M\) be a finite
Galois fixed field of an ambient open normal subgroup that fixes
the embedded copy of \(L\). The two maps on relative cohomology
used in the absolute transfer square agree with the ordinary
relative-to-continuous inflations used by the comparison with
Brauer classes.

The lower comparison uses the ambient Galois quotient and fixed
units. On the upper side the quotient of the fixing subgroup is
identified with \(\operatorname{Gal}(M/L)\) by restriction and
reindexing of the upper base. Its fixed coefficients identify
with \(M^\times\) under this same action. The finite cochain
comparison then factors the upper inflation through the inverse
of the continuous-to-ordinary comparison for the finite group.
On moving to the chosen separable closure of \(L\), the induced
cochain maps agree with restriction of automorphisms and
inclusion of units. In positive cohomological degrees, absolute
transfer of the inflated upper class equals inflation of its
finite relative transfer. This is the finite transfer computation
of Serre, *Local Fields* (1979), Chapter XI, §§1–2, pp. 166–168,
for the inflation maps in the Brauer comparison.

An embedded finite extension of the upper base, such as a finite
splitting field, is contained in one of these stages. Pull its image
back to the lower separable closure and take the open normal core
of its fixing subgroup. The resulting finite extension is Galois
over the lower base and retains the specified embedding over the
upper base.
