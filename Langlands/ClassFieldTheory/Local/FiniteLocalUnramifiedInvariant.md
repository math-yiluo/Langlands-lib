# The finite unramified invariant in cyclic coordinates

This module constructs the finite-stage map on the actual relative unit
cohomology of a compatible unramified finite Galois extension. Its cyclic
coordinate specifies the generator sent to one. It supplies the cyclic norm
and valuation computation for Serre, *Local Fields*, Chapter XIII, §3,
Proposition 6.

The normalized order of a base field unit, reduced modulo the degree, is a
surjective homomorphism from the field units. The norm-image criterion
identifies its kernel with the actual field norm subgroup. Thus it induces
an equivalence from the norm quotient to the cyclic group of that order.
The cyclic relative cohomology comparison with the actual norm quotient
and the denominator equivalence give the finite invariant. Its image in
Q/Z is precisely the subgroup killed by the extension degree.

The published calculation of the positive carry cocycle sends it through
the cyclic norm comparison to the class of its defining base field unit.
All relative classes have such a representative, and its value is the
rational class of the normalized order of that unit divided by the degree.
The formula includes degree one. An application to the local invariant
uses the coordinate defined by the arithmetic residue Frobenius.
