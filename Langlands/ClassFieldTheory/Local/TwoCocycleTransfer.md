# Relative corestriction on 2-cocycles

The ordinary cochain transfer evaluates a cochain on each tuple of subgroup
coordinates and sums the results after acting by the coset representatives.
The first homogeneous coordinate in the subgroup is the identity. The two
inhomogeneous coordinates in degree two are the successive Schreier elements
for the given pair of group elements.

The transfer on cocycles comes from the cycles map of the ordinary cochain
transfer. Naturality of the homology projection gives its compatibility with
the cohomology class of a cocycle. For a finite top field in a tower, restriction
of scalars identifies the middle-field Galois group with a subgroup of the
top-field Galois group over the base. Reindexing by this isomorphism and applying
ordinary transfer gives the project's positive-degree relative corestriction.
For field units, the sum in additive notation gives the product of the
conjugated Schreier factors.

This calculation is an internal step for comparing tensor descent with
cohomological corestriction. Serre's *Local Fields*, Chapter XI, section 2,
Proposition 1(ii), uses cohomological corestriction in the local invariant
formula.
