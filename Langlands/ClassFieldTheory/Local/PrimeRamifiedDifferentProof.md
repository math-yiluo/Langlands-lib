# The different at the supplied prime ramification break

Author: Worker 384.

The theorem `Langlands.LocalClassField.primeRamifiedDifferent.{u,v}` proves
exactly the closed Library proposition
`Langlands.LocalClassField.PrimeRamifiedDifferent.{u,v}`, with no additional
outer hypotheses. It retains every supplied prime, compatible field algebra,
finite-dimensional Galois extension of prime degree, total-ramification equality,
natural break t and complete lower-ramification-group pattern. Its conclusion is
the literal equality

```text
differentIdeal TF.Integers TL.Integers
  = (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal TL.Integers)^((t + 1) * (ell - 1)),
```

where TF and TL are the canonical data and ell is the supplied extension
degree. The tame case t = 0 is included. The target statement and its import
closure are unchanged.

All local results retain independent field universes and the supplied field
algebra with its compatible Padic scalar tower. The integer scalars are exactly
`relativeIntegerAlgebra`; the fraction-field scalars and both towers are the
published relative ones. The ideal called the different is Mathlib's
`differentIdeal` of these actual integer rings.

## Exact displacement order

`uniformizer_displacement_ord_of_single_break` applies to arbitrary supplied
topological data T and S, surjectivity of their actual `relativeResidueMap`, a
natural number t, and the complete lower-group pattern from the target. For
every order-one element π and every nonidentity automorphism σ, it proves both
σπ − π ≠ 0 and ord(σπ − π) = t + 1.

The published uniformizer criterion tests membership in the lower groups at
indices t and t+1. The pattern gives membership at t and nonmembership at t+1.
Nonmembership excludes zero displacement. The two remaining integer inequalities
force the asserted order. This includes t = 0 and uses the supplied break.
Worker 231's uniformizer criterion is imported by its Library name.

## Minimal polynomial and actual conjugates

The generic field lemmas require a finite-dimensional Galois extension E/K and
an element x with `Algebra.adjoin K {x} = ⊤`.
`minpoly_map_eq_prod_automorphisms_of_adjoin` factors the mapped minimal
polynomial as the product of X − σx over the actual K-automorphisms of E. A
power basis from the supplied adjoin equality proves distinct automorphisms
have distinct values at x. The linear factors are therefore pairwise coprime;
each divides the mapped minimal polynomial because σx is a root. Monicity and
the equality between the minimal-polynomial degree, the extension degree and
the automorphism cardinality turn divisibility into equality.

`aeval_derivative_minpoly_eq_prod_automorphisms_of_adjoin` extracts the identity
factor X − x, differentiates the product and evaluates at x. It obtains the
product of x − σx over all nonidentity automorphisms. The sign in this formula
is retained exactly; later passage to principal ideals removes a minus sign.

## From an integer generator to the different

`adjoin_fractionField_eq_top_of_integer_adjoin` is a generic tower lemma. If B
is generated by x over A and E is the fraction field of B, with a compatible
field K and finite-dimensional E/K, then E is generated by the image of x over
K. The K-subalgebra generated by that image contains the image of B by the
integer adjoin equality. Every element of E is a fraction of elements of B,
and finite dimensionality makes the denominator integral over K; the
subalgebra therefore contains its inverse.

`different_eq_span_prod_automorphisms_of_integer_adjoin` applies this to the
actual p-adic integer rings. It installs the integral-closure and finite-module
conclusions of the published `NYLL.relativeIntegerStructure`. Mathlib's
`conductor_mul_differentIdeal` then expresses conductor times different as the
principal ideal of the minimal-polynomial derivative. Actual integer generation
makes the conductor the unit ideal.

To identify the generator, the proof uses the injective inclusion of the target
integer ring into its field. Compatibility of minimal polynomials over an
integrally closed ring and its fraction field identifies the derivative with
the field derivative. The preceding product formula and the actual
`integerAutomorphism` restriction yield a product in the integer ring itself.
There is no assumed trace-image or different formula in this lemma.

## Principal ideals and the assembled conditional formula

`span_singleton_eq_maximalIdeal_pow_of_ord` proves that a nonzero integer d of
order n generates the nth maximal-ideal power. Membership in a principal ideal
is divisibility. A multiple of d has order at least n, unless it is zero.
Conversely, an integer a of order at least n has integral quotient a/d, giving
the required divisor. Zero is handled explicitly, in accordance with the
Library convention ord(0) = 0. The imported order criterion for maximal-ideal
powers and the order/integrality equivalence are published in Worker 231's
uniformizer-criterion module.

`different_eq_of_integer_adjoin_and_single_break` combines the preceding
results. It assumes actual relative residue surjectivity, the supplied complete
single-break pattern, and generation by a supplied order-one element π.
Every nonidentity conjugate displacement generates the (t+1)st maximal-ideal
power. Changing the sign of a displacement leaves its principal ideal
unchanged. The principal ideal of the product is the product of the principal
ideals. There are `[L:F] − 1` nonidentity automorphisms, so the different is
exactly the power with exponent `(t+1) * ([L:F]−1)`. This conditional lemma
does not require prime degree; it retains t = 0 and arbitrary supplied data.
