# Inducing tensors from the actual smooth scalar sections

The infinite coefficient space consists of linear maps from the finite Levi coefficient space into the smooth functions on the actual infinite adelic group. It requires that pairing with each finite inducing vector give the smooth slices of an actual scalar block section. These slices determine their section uniquely. Tensor evaluation therefore lifts linearly to the original section space, and injectivity follows whenever the paired slices distinguish the tensors.

The compact and enveloping operators act by postcomposition on the smooth functions. The existing comparisons on every fixed finite slice make this action preserve the infinite coefficient space and make tensor evaluation intertwine it. The right translation covariance of the finite evaluations gives the finite adelic intertwining identity.

This implements the finite and infinite tensor construction in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Lemma 1, printed p. 203, Corvallis Part I PDF p. 198, using the actual scalar section model.
