# Conrad's Theorem 4.5 assuming only his Lemma 4.6

`AdelicSpreadSmoothOpenOfLangWeilStatement` is the implication from
`UniformLangWeilStatement` to `AdelicSpreadSmoothOpenStatement`. Both are
published proposition definitions. The first states Lemma 4.6 and the second
states Theorem 4.5 of Brian Conrad, *Weil and Grothendieck approaches to
adelic points* (December 31, 2011), printed p.12. The registered source is
`conrad-2011-weil-grothendieck-adelic-points`, SHA-256
`fe4a9193aff4ef575665b40971f3da32f850a74d87b3f2d0fa28bba26b9cbabb`. The
module states the implication and contains no proof.

The conclusion is unchanged: in both characteristics, a smooth surjective
map with geometrically connected fibres between separated schemes over the
global field induces an open map on actual adelic points. The topologies are
those attached to arbitrary finite-type spreads of source and target. The
universe `u` is arbitrary and common to the premise and the conclusion.

## Relation to the source and derived content

Conrad proves Theorem 4.5 using Lemma 4.6, which he deduces from Deligne's
Weil II [Del1, Cor. 3.3.3]. All other ingredients of his proof on pp.12–13
are model existence, local openness, product reduction and Henselian
lifting. This derived statement isolates Lemma 4.6 as the only remaining
premise. It asserts nothing about Lemma 4.6 itself, which remains a separate
obligation of the whole-paper task.
