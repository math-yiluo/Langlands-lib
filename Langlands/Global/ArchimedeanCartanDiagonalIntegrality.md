# Diagonal integrality over actual characteristic coefficients

This internal construction uses the actual diagonal coordinates and full-group
characteristic invariants from `ArchimedeanCartanCharacteristicCoefficients`.
It retains `K : Type u`, and every natural rank, including zero. The monic
polynomial and coordinate integrality require only `Field K`; finite generation
and the finite-extension conclusions also use `NumberField K`.

`cartanDiagonalCharacteristicPolynomial` is the product of `X` minus each
actual diagonal coordinate at a specified real or complex place. It is monic
of degree `n`. `cartanDiagonalCharacteristicSubalgebra` is the complex
subalgebra generated by its coefficients of degrees zero through `n`, at
all actual places. Higher coefficients vanish, so every coefficient belongs
to it. `cartanDiagonalCoordinate_isIntegral` lifts this monic polynomial to
the coefficient subalgebra and evaluates at the chosen coordinate. The
factor indexed by that coordinate vanishes, proving integrality.

For a number field the family of coefficient generators is finite, proving
`cartanDiagonalCharacteristicSubalgebra_fg` and the Noetherian ring instance
`cartanDiagonalCharacteristicSubalgebra_noetherian`. The real coordinate
equivalence `realCartanDiagonalCoordinates` collects the real diagonal entries
at both real and complex places. Its coordinate basis, after complexification
and dualization, is precisely the existing diagonal coordinate dual family.
The comparison is checked on pure tensors and yields
`cartanDiagonalCoordinateDual_span`.

Linear spanning implies that adjoining the coordinate generators over the
complex numbers gives the entire diagonal polynomial algebra: the subalgebra
contains the image of every linear functional, and symmetric-algebra
induction then includes constants, sums and products. Restricting scalars
shows that adjoining the same coordinates over the characteristic coefficient
subalgebra also gives the whole algebra. The finite family consists of integral
elements, so the finite integral-adjoin theorem yields the instance
`cartanDiagonalPolynomialAlgebra_finite_characteristic`:

```lean
Module.Finite (cartanDiagonalCharacteristicSubalgebra K n)
  (CartanDiagonalPolynomialAlgebra K n)
```

Finally, `cartanDiagonalCharacteristicSubalgebra_le_invariant_range` proves
that the coefficient subalgebra lies in the image of the composite of
`cartanGroupInvariantRestriction` and
`cartanCompactInvariantDiagonalRestriction`. Each generator is the image of
`lieCharacteristicSymmetricInvariant` with its same place and coefficient
index. The adjoin universal property extends this to the whole subalgebra.
These are the actual maps and fixed subalgebras of the preceding modules.

The provenance is an internal derivation from those constructions, the
symmetric-algebra universal property, finite coordinate bases, and Mathlib's
monic polynomial lifting and finite integral-adjoin results. No external
invariant-theory finiteness theorem is assumed. This unit proves diagonal
finite extension and coefficient image containment. Finiteness of the actual
compact-invariant algebra over full-group invariants, enveloping central
generation and the final automorphic differential-growth theorem remain
separate obligations.
