# Probability measures under projections of compact radical quotients

Over a number field, a closed immersion of affine group schemes gives a topological group isomorphism from the source adelic points onto the adelic points of its range functor. A morphism between two such representatives commutes with the principal maps. It therefore induces a continuous map of the rational left quotients.

For the measure comparison, both ambient affine group schemes are locally of finite type over the number field and both rational left quotients are compact. The compact-quotient Weil theorem gives right-invariant probability measures for the two normalized quotient measures. If the representative morphism is surjective on adelic points, every target right translation lifts to a source right translation. The pushforward is therefore a regular right-invariant probability. Uniqueness of that measure gives `measurePreserving_radicalQuotientProjection`.

The lemma is an internal consequence of the compact-quotient Weil theorem and uniqueness of invariant measure. It supplies the measure comparison for restricting a nested constant term to a Levi fibre in the induction of Mœglin–Waldspurger I.3.4.
