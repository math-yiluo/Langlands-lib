# Smooth logarithmic parameters for the adelic split center

For an affine connected reductive group over a number field with the supplied
smooth archimedean atlas, `CentralLogParameterStatement` gives a smooth
additive parametrization of the positive split central factor in the
original infinite adelic point group. Each full adelic point is a norm-one
point times the infinite inclusion of a parametrized central point. For a
fixed positive-rank closed matrix representation, the exponential of the
parameter norm is bounded by a common polynomial in the full adelic height.

The positive and norm-one decomposition chooses the actual split component. A coordinate
splitting gives the parameter by applying real exponential at each infinite place and
putting identity at finite places. The closed diagonal representation detects smoothness
of these torus coordinates. Regularity of the inclusion into the ambient group and the
completion-wise manifold criterion then give smoothness into the original charts. The
centrality and the product formula follow from the adelic decomposition.

Finite index of the rational character restriction supplies an ambient character restricting
to a positive power of each torus coordinate. Applying the height bounds to the character
and its inverse bounds the absolute logarithm of the coordinate. Taking the finite supremum
gives the parameter estimate.

The decomposition is the one used in Borel–Jacquet, *Automorphic forms and automorphic
representations* (1979), §1.6(1), and Mœglin–Waldspurger, *Spectral Decomposition and
Eisenstein Series* (1995), I.2.1. The smoothness and coordinate height bound are derived
from that construction and the adelic heights of the Library.
