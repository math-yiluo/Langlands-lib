# Compact rational representatives for ambient norm-one root boxes

This internal construction treats the actual adelic points of a maximal split
torus in a connected reductive group over any number field. The set
`ambientNormOneRootBox` requires that the torus point map into the ambient
norm-one subgroup and that every actual simple-root norm lie between `b⁻¹`
and `b`. All character norms and topologies are the existing Library ones.

The proof first shows that ambient norm one makes the normalized norm of every
restricted ambient rational character equal to one. The published joint
spanning theorem for relative roots and restricted ambient characters, together
with the published simple-root spanning theorem for a generic cocharacter,
then gives a spanning family of the entire rational character lattice.

For `b ≥ 1`, the same two-sided box bounds the restricted characters as well.
The published compact representative theorem for a spanning character box in
a split torus therefore constructs one compact subset of the full adelic
torus that meets every principal rational left coset of this set.
This includes the central character directions, which simple roots alone
need not span. No semisimplicity, splitness of the ambient group, or
root-system realization hypothesis is added.

This is a derived result from Library proofs, rather than a new translation of
a literature statement. It does not supply the uniform root bounds or a
fundamental-set theorem, and does not assert general reductive quotient
compactness.
