# Conjugacy of infinite adelic maximal compact subgroups

`InfiniteAdelicMaximalCompactConjugacyStatement` gives conjugacy between any
two maximal compact subgroups of the actual infinite adelic point group of
a connected reductive group over a number field.

In a product group, maximality identifies a compact subgroup with the product
of its compact images. Were one image contained in a larger compact subgroup,
replacing that factor would give a compact overgroup of the original subgroup.
Thus all coordinate images are maximal compact. Use conjugacy at each
infinite place to choose a conjugating point in each factor, and carry the
resulting product conjugation back through the infinite adelic multiplicative
homeomorphism.

This internal result permits matching the infinite projections of adelic
reference compacts.
