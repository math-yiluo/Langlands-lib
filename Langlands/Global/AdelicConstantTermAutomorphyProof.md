# Proof that constant terms of automorphic functions are automorphic

Module `Langlands.Global.AdelicConstantTermAutomorphyProof` proves
`Langlands.Global.ConstantTermAutomorphyStatement` (module
`AdelicConstantTermAutomorphyStatement`) as the theorem `Langlands.Global.constantTermAutomorphy`.
For standard `Q ≤ P` of a compatible standard family, a subgroup `Kinf` of `G(F_∞)`, a closed
matrix embedding `rho` and `φ` automorphic on `U_P(𝔸) M_P(F) \ G(𝔸)`, it shows that the five
conditions of `IsAutomorphicFunctionOn` hold for `C_Qφ` on `U_Q(𝔸) M_Q(F) \ G(𝔸)`. Write
`Γ_P = U_P(𝔸) M_P(F)` (`parabolicQuotientSubgroup`).

## Left invariance and continuity

`rationalImage_le_parabolicQuotientSubgroup_of_le` shows that the image of `P_Q(F)` lies in
`Γ_P`: the containment `P_Q ⊆ P_P` (`le_iff_range_le`) factors `P_Q → G` through `P_P → G`, and
the rational points of `P_P` lie in `Γ_P` (`rationalImage_le_parabolicQuotientSubgroup`).
`rationalRadicalImage_le_of_le` restricts this to the radical `U_Q`. So `φ` is left invariant
under `P_Q(F)`, and `constantTerm_continuous_and_invariant` applies the Library's
`inducingConstantTermCovariance` for `P_Q` and its radical: `C_Qφ` is continuous and left
invariant under `P_Q(F)` and under `U_Q(𝔸)`. Since `M_Q(F)` lies in `P_Q(F)` and `Γ_Q` is
generated by `U_Q(𝔸)` and `M_Q(F)`, the left stabilizer of `C_Qφ` (`scalarLeftStabilizer`)
contains `Γ_Q`.

## Smoothness and the enveloping algebra

`IsRegularOn P L f` records that `f` is continuous, has plot-smooth infinite slices, is left
invariant under `Γ_P` and is right invariant under a given subgroup `L` of `G(𝔸_f)`. Openness of
`L` is not part of `IsRegularOn`: `isRegularOn_derivative`, `constantTerm_envelopingAction` and
`isCenterFiniteFunction_constantTerm` assume it as a separate hypothesis. `IsRegularOn` is
closed under sums and scalar multiples (`IsRegularOn.add`, `IsRegularOn.smul`), and the constant
term is additive on such functions (`constantTerm_add_of_isRegularOn`, using the integrability of
the constant-term integrand, `integrable_constantTermIntegrand_of_le`).

`isRegularOn_derivative` applies the Library's `adelicUnipotentConstantTermDifferentiation` to
the radical `U_Q` (smooth, connected and unipotent by `isUnipotentRadical`). Plot-smoothness of
a slice is the manifold smoothness of `isPlotSmooth_iff_contMDiff`. The theorem gives: every
right-orbit derivative `adelicDirectionalValue X f` is again regular (its left `Γ_P`-invariance
holds because left translation commutes with right-orbit differentiation); the slices of `C_Qf`
are plot-smooth; and the derivative of a slice of `C_Qf` in direction `X` is the slice of
`C_Q` of the derivative of `f`.

`constantTerm_envelopingAction` extends this to every element `a` of the complex enveloping
algebra of `G(F_∞)`: for a regular `f` there is a regular `f_a` whose slices are the images
under `a` of the slices of `f`, and the images under `a` of the slices of `C_Qf` are the slices
of `C_Q f_a`. The proof is by induction over the enveloping algebra through real Lie generators,
complex scalars, sums and products (the same induction as in the Library module
`AdelicRightConvolutionEnveloping`), with the generator step given by
`isRegularOn_derivative`, `plotSmoothEnvelopingAction_real_generator` and
`adelicDirectionalValue_pair`.

In the main theorem, the slices of `C_Qφ` are plot-smooth by `isRegularOn_derivative`, and
`C_Qφ` is right invariant under the level `L` of `φ` because the radical integral is a left
average. `isCenterFiniteFunction_constantTerm` keeps the ideal `J` of the center that kills the
slices of `φ`: for `z ∈ J` the function `f_z` above has zero slices, hence is zero, and `z`
maps each slice of `C_Qφ` to the corresponding slice of `C_Q f_z = 0`.

## Finiteness under `Kinf`

`continuousInvariantSubmodule P` is the space of continuous `Γ_P`-invariant functions and
`constantTermOnContinuous` the constant term as a linear map on it.
`isInfiniteSubgroupFiniteFunction_constantTerm`: the right translates `φ(· k)`, `k ∈ Kinf`, lie
in that space and span a finite-dimensional subspace, isomorphic to the span in `Kinf`-finiteness
of `φ`; the right translates of `C_Qφ` are the images of the right translates of `φ`, so they lie
in the finite-dimensional image of this subspace.

## Moderate growth

For an embedding of positive size, the main theorem uses the Library lemma
`StandardParabolicFamily.hasHeightGrowth_constantTerm` (module
`AdelicParabolicConstantTermGrowth`). It bounds the constant term in the submultiplicative
height (`HasHeightGrowth`, equivalent to moderate growth by
`hasModerateGrowthFunction_iff_hasHeightGrowth`) for a function left invariant under the
rational points of `P_Q`. That invariance comes from the `Γ_P`-invariance of `φ` by the Library
lemma `isLeftInvariantUnder_rationalImage_of_le` of the same module. The lemma's proof uses the
compactness of `U_Q(F) \ U_Q(𝔸)`: each class has a representative of bounded height, and the
quotient measure has mass one. For an embedding of size zero, `G(𝔸)` has a single point (the adelic
matrix map is a closed embedding into the trivial group of `0 × 0` invertible matrices) and the
bound holds with exponent zero.

The argument follows Mœglin–Waldspurger, *Spectral Decomposition and Eisenstein Series* (1995),
I.2.6, p. 27 (invariance, growth and smoothness of constant terms), with the commutation of the
constant term with right translations and with the enveloping algebra giving the two finiteness
conditions of I.2.17, pp. 37–38. This is the assertion of Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic
continuation of Eisenstein series* (2024), §6.2, p. 201, that `C_{P,Q}` maps `𝒜_P` to `𝒜_Q`.
