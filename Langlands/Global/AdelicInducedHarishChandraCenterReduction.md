# The uniform equation from a fixed parabolic center ideal

`UniformParabolicLeviCenterTransferStatement` states unnormalized restriction of an ambient central operator to every infinite Levi fibre of an inducing function. For each operator \(z\) there is one Levi enveloping operator \(y\). It is independent of the inducing function and of the adelic base point \(x\) of the fibre \(m\mapsto f(l(m)x)\). Acting by \(y\) on this restriction gives the restriction of the \(z\)-derivative in the infinite slice through \(x\). The formulation requires neither an algebra homomorphism nor finiteness of an extension of centers. It records the specific differential identity used in the convolution argument.

With this identity, `twistedConvolutionEnd_annihilatedByCenter` shows that localized transported convolution preserves annihilation by \(z\) in every infinite slice. For the input, the Levi operator \(y\) vanishes on every translated fibre. The formula `twistedConvolutionEnd_fibreEnveloping` integrates precisely these derivatives, with a weight independent of the Levi variable. At the identity of the output Levi fibre the restriction identity gives the value of the ambient \(z\)-derivative. The resulting vanishing holds at every adelic point.

The equation follows by applying general parabolic Harish-Chandra finiteness to the original quotient and compact. The proof fixes the cofinite ambient center ideal of the given inducing form, a right open finite level in the finite factor of the compact, and its finite-dimensional space of right compact coefficient functions. The span of continuous automorphic functions with these three constraints maps injectively into the finite-dimensional space given by the finiteness theorem. Localized convolution transported through the height twist preserves the center ideal, the finite level, the quotient invariance and moderate growth. Smoothness follows from smooth right convolution and smoothness of the height pairings. Left compact averaging restores the selected coefficient space. To see that the coefficients ensure compact finiteness, evaluation at each compact point defines a functional in the dual of that space, and the right compact translates are its images under one linear map. Linearity thus gives stability of the full span.

Localized smooth approximation with two compact reproducing weights supplies an invertible convolution at the chosen base parameter. The entire characteristic polynomial of this one transported operator supplies the scalar equation for every parameter. The theorem `inducedHarishChandraEquations_of_parabolicCenter` implements this reduction for the exact original proposition, using its own parabolic, radical and Levi data. It takes general parabolic Harish-Chandra finiteness, smooth Iwasawa height pairings and the uniform center transfer as premises. The zero matrix size case uses the corresponding normalized convolution on the subsingleton adelic group.

The finiteness input is the fixed-level, fixed-type, fixed-center version of Mœglin–Waldspurger, *Spectral Decomposition and Eisenstein Series* (1995), I.2.17, printed p. 38. The kernel selection and characteristic polynomial follow I.4.5, printed pp. 60–62, and Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series*, JAMS 37 (2024), Lemma 8.3, p. 223. The finite space here is constructed by retaining the ambient ideal under every transported operator.
