# Relative split Iwasawa measure on the rational parabolic quotient

The map `normOneRelativeParabolicQuotientCoordinates` multiplies
a radical point, a relative positive split point, an intrinsic
norm-one Levi point and an Iwasawa compact point. It takes the
class of the product in \(P(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A)^1\).
`relativeParabolicQuotientMeasure` expresses the counting-normalized
measure there as the pushforward of the four coordinate measures.

The relative split coordinate is unrestricted. In the ambient
norm-one Levi, the rational lattice acts only in the intrinsic
norm-one coordinate, so the product of the split component and a
strict intrinsic Levi fundamental domain is a strict transversal.
For the parabolic, first move the Levi coordinate to that
transversal and then move the radical coordinate. The Levi
projection determines the rational Levi translation, and its
kernel on rational points is the rational radical. This proves
uniqueness of the parabolic translation.

The proof chooses Haar on the norm-one parabolic compatibly with
ambient Haar and compact Haar probability. The radical Haar
measure is prescribed. The inverse modulus is a density only in
the relative split coordinate, and the intrinsic norm-one Haar
measure is prescribed. Restrict both parabolic factors to the
strict transversals and compose the measure pushforwards.

This is the radical, relative split, intrinsic Levi and compact
measure used in (7.7), prior to restriction to the height region.
The source is P. Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*,
Advances in Mathematics 426 (2023), 109107, pp. 23–24.
