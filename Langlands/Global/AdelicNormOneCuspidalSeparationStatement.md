# Norm-one separation of continuous cuspidal functions

`NormOneCuspidalSeparationStatement` states the analytic cuspidal case of Mœglin–Waldspurger I.3.4 in the fibrewise formulation of Bernstein–Lapid (6.8). Let `H` be an affine locally finite-type connected reductive group over a number field, with a supplied smooth relative dimension, a compact subgroup at infinity and a closed matrix embedding of positive size. The continuous, rationally invariant, moderate-growth input on `H(𝔸)` has vanishing normalized constant terms along all proper rational parabolics. For every right translate, its pairing on the norm-one rational quotient with each cuspidal automorphic function is absolutely convergent and zero. The tests use the indicated compact subgroup, the actual enveloping-center action and the closed matrix representation. The conclusion is vanishing at the identity. Applying the same assertion to right translates recovers pointwise vanishing everywhere.

This statement isolates the analytic assertion. The argument of I.3.4 uses compactly supported smooth convolution, rapid decay and density of cuspidal automorphic forms in the cuspidal Hilbert space of the norm-one quotient.
