# Compact unipotent factors in an actual standard Levi

The standard radical for the intersection of two standard parabolic
indices factors through the corresponding inner radical in one Levi and
that Levi's outer radical. This follows from the standard Levi
intersection decomposition and the compatibility of the intrinsic and
ambient standard families. The radical of the other parabolic is contained
in the radical for the intersection, so the factorization applies there.

For a compact set of adelic points, the inner factors may be taken in a
single compact set. Pull the original compact set back to the closed
parabolic, apply the continuous Levi projection, and pull that image back
through the closed immersion of the intrinsic radical. The factorization
on the adele test scheme gives membership in the compact set by the
formula for the Levi projection of a product of a Levi and a radical
point.

This factorization lets one use contraction in the upper Levi in the
decay estimate for the alternating terms of Arthur 2005, §13, (13.7).
