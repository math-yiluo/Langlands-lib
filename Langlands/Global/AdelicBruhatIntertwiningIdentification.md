# The actual intertwiner for one Bruhat piece

The geometric unfolding identity replaces the full stabilizer integral by a smaller intertwining integral of the twisted source constant term. The smaller Iwasawa height agrees with the restriction of the original height and the height twist is invariant under the smaller source radical. Consequently the resulting integrand is the one used by the actual intertwining operator.

Apply the same geometric unfolding identity to the complex-valued nonnegative norm of the twisted source. The triangle inequality bounds the norm of the smaller constant term by the corresponding average of the norm. Taking the real part after unfolding identifies the integral of this bound with the original absolute stabilizer integral. This uses the original parameter and its absolute bound when the restricted parameter may lie on a smaller chamber wall.
