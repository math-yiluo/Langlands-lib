# Kernel vectors of norm-one partial convolutions

Module `Langlands.Global.AdelicNormOneKernelVector`.

Notation as in `Langlands.Global.AdelicNormOnePartialConvolution`: `F` a number field, `H` an affine
group scheme over `Spec F`, locally of finite type, `i : T → H` with split central data (an
isomorphism `e` of `T` with a split torus `𝔾_m^I`, injective restriction of rational characters
`characterRestrict i` with finite-index image, centrality `hc`), and right invariant Haar measure
on the norm-one subgroup `H(𝔸)¹`. `Q = PositiveRationalQuotient F H T i` with projection
`q : H(𝔸) → Q`, `V = L²(Q)` for `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure F H T i`, and
`Θ_h[K](y) = normOnePartialConvolution T i K h y = ∫_{H(𝔸)¹} conj K(n⁻¹ y) h(q n) dn`, where an
`L²` class `h` is read through its chosen representative.

## Results

- `rightTranslateCc K y` is the compactly supported continuous function `z ↦ K(z y)`.
- `exists_positiveBump`: a compactly supported continuous function `ρ` on the positive points
  `T⁺` with `∫ ρ ≠ 0` for `normalizedPositiveHaarMeasure`.
- `normOnePartialConvolution_one_eq_integral`: for such `ρ`, a compactly supported continuous
  `K` and `h ∈ V`, the function `a ↦ normOneSmoothingKernel K ρ (a) h(q a)` is integrable on
  `H(𝔸)` and `Θ_h[K](1) = (∫ ρ)⁻¹ ∫_{H(𝔸)} normOneSmoothingKernel K ρ (a) h(q a) da`. Integrability
  comes from `quotientConvolutionKernel` (module
  `Langlands.Analysis.Local.QuotientConvolutionKernelProof`) and the identity from
  `positiveQuotientLpSmoothing_normOneSmoothingKernel` at the point `1`.
- `exists_kernelVector_bound`: for every compact `D ⊆ H(𝔸)` there is `C ≥ 0` such that every
  compactly supported continuous `K` with support in `D` and `‖K(z)‖ ≤ M` for all `z` has a vector
  `v ∈ V` with `⟪v, h⟫ = Θ_h[K](1)` for all `h ∈ V` and `‖v‖ ≤ C M`. The vector is
  `conj((∫ ρ)⁻¹) w(1)`, where `w` is the kernel of `quotientConvolutionKernel` for
  `normOneSmoothingKernel K ρ`. The bound of that theorem,
  `‖w(1)‖² ≤ sup|f| · ∫|f| · μ_Γ(Γ ∩ S_f S_f⁻¹)` (with `S_f` the support of `f` and `μ_Γ`
  the Haar measure `positiveRationalSubgroupHaarMeasure` of the closed subgroup generated by
  `H(F)` and the image of `T⁺`), is estimated with the compact set `S` (the image of
  `supp ρ × {n : n⁻¹ ∈ D}` under the decomposition `H(𝔸) = T⁺ × H(𝔸)¹`), which contains every such
  support.
- `normOneKernelVector T i hi I e hc K` is the unique `v ∈ V` with `⟪v, h⟫ = Θ_h[K](1)` for every
  `h ∈ V` (`inner_normOneKernelVector`; uniqueness `normOneKernelVector_eq`), for compactly
  supported continuous `K`. Mathlib's inner product is conjugate linear in its first argument.
- `exists_norm_normOneKernelVector_le`: for a compact `D` there is `C ≥ 0` with
  `‖normOneKernelVector K‖ ≤ C M` whenever `K` is supported in `D` and `‖K‖ ≤ M`.
- `normOnePartialConvolution_eq_inner`: `Θ_h[K](y) = ⟪normOneKernelVector (rightTranslateCc K y), h⟫`
  for every `y ∈ H(𝔸)`.
- `normOneKernelVector_sub`: the kernel vector of `K₁ - K₂` is the difference of the kernel
  vectors (by the integral formula above and linearity of the integral).
  `exists_norm_normOneKernelVector_sub_le`: a Lipschitz bound in the sup norm for kernels
  supported in a fixed compact set.
- `continuous_normOneKernelVector_comp`: if `p ↦ K_p` is a family of compactly supported
  continuous functions on `H(𝔸)` with `(p, z) ↦ K_p(z)` jointly continuous and with supports in a
  fixed compact set locally in `p`, then `p ↦ normOneKernelVector K_p` is continuous. Jointly
  continuous families converge uniformly on compact sets (`ContinuousMap.curry` and restriction
  to the compact set), and the Lipschitz bound applies.

## Provenance

This is an internal construction. It realizes the norm-one partial convolutions of `L²` classes
as inner products with continuously varying vectors of `L²(Q)`, the form in which they enter the
Riesz representation of the norm-one Levi pairings of Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic
continuation of Eisenstein series* (2024), §6.2, (6.8), p. 202.
