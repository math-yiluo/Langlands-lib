# Characteristic products and Siegel representatives

`characteristicHeightProduct C A` is \(\Omega_{N_0}M_0(A)K\) for a height set \(A\), with the minimal Levi point constrained to the ambient norm-one group. For Delorme's \(C_T^G\) this is the input product of `characteristicRegion`. The arbitrary height set also permits intersections of chambers for a shell.

`exists_siegelRepresentative_of_mem_characteristicHeightProduct` decomposes the minimal Levi point into its norm-one part and the positive torus of the minimal parabolic. The split-component decomposition applies because that torus is a maximal split central torus of the minimal Levi. The minimal Levi rational covering gives a rational Levi point and a compact Levi representative. Conjugating the unipotent coordinate by the rational Levi point keeps it in the minimal unipotent radical, so the unipotent covering gives a rational unipotent point and a compact unipotent representative. The product of the two rational points is in the minimal parabolic. The new representative has the same positive torus height. The strict lower inequality for \(A\) puts it in the common Siegel set.

In the reverse direction, `mem_characteristicHeightProduct_of_siegelRepresentative` combines the norm-one minimal Levi representative with the positive torus. The unipotent and compact coordinates are norm one in \(G\), so the combined minimal Levi point lies in the ambient norm-one group. Its height is the height of the original representative. `normOneCharacteristicHeightImage_eq` takes images in the rational quotient and identifies the two sets. `exists_strict_lower_cutoff` provides the open Siegel cutoff below the closed characteristic cutoff by subtracting the generic cocharacter in the real cocharacter space.

The source is P. Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*, Advances in Mathematics 426 (2023), 109107, pp. 22–24, the rational covers in (7.1) and the discussion of products and standard parabolic quotient images in (7.3)–(7.6).
