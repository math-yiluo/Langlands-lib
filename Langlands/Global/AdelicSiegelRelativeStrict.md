# Strict subunitarity on a relative Siegel ray

Fix an ordinary split-component eigenvector at `J` with unitary normalized
parameter and left invariance under the radical of `J`. Remove one Levi
simple root to obtain `Q`. Assume compatibility of the nested half moduli,
normalized exponent decomposition and Siegel chamber growth for the
ordinary `Q` constant term of the vector.
`AdelicSiegelRelativeStrictStatement` shows that when its `Q` weak term
vanishes, every nonzero component has normalized character norm strictly
below one at the specified relative ray point. The ray is represented in
the actual positive split component of `Q`, all its nonnegative integer
powers are in the weak chamber with threshold one, and its logarithm in
the removed direction is nonzero.

The ordinary eigencharacter commutes with the nested averaging on the
larger split component. Directness of the generalized eigenspaces then
recovers that eigencharacter on each nonzero component of the smaller
constant term. The compatible half moduli cancel at matching split
points, yielding unit modulus on the larger split component. A nonzero
component has a nonunitary label because the sum of the unitary
components vanishes. The ray growth bound gives the weak inequality
at the specified point. The relative axis criterion upgrades it to a
strict inequality.

This applies the exponent-sign argument of Delorme's Proposition 3.6
in the relative direction used in the proof of Lemma 3.10(i).

Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*, Advances in Mathematics 426 (2023),
109107, published PDF pp. 13 and 15–16.
