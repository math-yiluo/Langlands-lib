# Faithfulness of restriction for square-integrable inducing forms

`eq_of_ae_inducedRestriction` recovers equality on the adelic group from
almost-everywhere equality of the weighted Iwasawa restrictions of two
`A²_P` forms. The weight is continuous, nonnegative, and compactly supported,
with integral one.

The difference is in the complex submodule `squareIntegrableInducedForms`.
Its restriction represents zero in the weighted `L²` space. The published
comparison of inner products therefore makes its diagonal inducing inner
product zero. Apply positive definiteness of the inducing Gram form to the
singleton family consisting of this difference, with coefficient one.

This is a consequence of the weighted realization of the inducing norm and its
positive definiteness. See E. Lapid, *On Arthur's asymptotic inner product
formula of truncated Eisenstein series* (2011), §2, p. 311 (PDF p. 320),
registered as `lapid-2011-asymptotic-inner-product-truncated-eisenstein`,
SHA-256 `760161175f57a28d2e05de1f5638bc205d33123ae95f67ce4296b4b651fa950b`.
