# Arthur truncation on the norm-one adelic group

**Source.** J. Arthur, *An introduction to the trace formula* (2005), §13, printed p. 68 (PDF p. 80), especially the domain of (13.1), in `arthur-ellwood-kottwitz-2005-harmonic-analysis-shimura`. The formula there takes functions on `G(F) \ G(𝔸_F)¹`. The [full-adelic truncation module](ArthurTruncation.md) uses the same formula on `G(𝔸_F)`, as in Lapid 2011. This adapter interprets it on the smaller group without a new measure or a new Iwasawa height.

**Construction.** `Langlands.Global.ArthurTruncation.normOneTruncation` takes `f : normOneSubgroup F G → ℂ` and a norm-one argument `x`. It evaluates the published `truncation` on `normOneExtend f`, the function equal to `f` on the subgroup and zero off it. A function on the rational quotient is used here by pullback along the norm-one quotient map. The height is still read in `V T` via `hρ`; the sign, rational parabolic cosets, and constant terms with the L1 mass-one unipotent quotient measure are unchanged.

**Why this implements (13.1).** The only values of the input function appearing at `x` are at `n δ x`, with `δ` rational and `n` in a standard unipotent radical's adelic points. The rational image lies in `normOneSubgroup` by the product formula. For `unipotentPoints_le_normOne`, choose the standard cocharacter realizing the parabolic and its unipotent radical. The published dynamic-group results give a smooth, connected unipotent representative. Its rational quotient is compact. The ambient logarithmic character-norm homomorphism vanishes on principal points, so on these unipotent points it factors continuously through the compact quotient and has compact image in a real vector group. Such an additive subgroup is zero. This proves the norm-one inclusion for each standard root subset, for generic `ν`.

`truncation_congr_on_normOne` applies these inclusions inside every integral in the defining sums: equality of two functions on `G(𝔸_F)¹` suffices for equality of their truncations there. Thus `normOneTruncation_restrict` identifies the norm-one truncation of a full-adelic function's restriction with the restriction of its full truncation. This equality is pointwise, not an almost-everywhere restriction of an `L²` class. These are definitional compatibility results. Finiteness of the sums, convergence for locally bounded measurable functions, and the orthogonal-projection assertion are not proved in this module.
