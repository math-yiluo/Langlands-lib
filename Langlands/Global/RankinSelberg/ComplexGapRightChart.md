# The consecutive-block right Haar chart

`complexGapRightChart` expresses Haar measure on the complex general linear group of size `k + q + 1` in the two-block right chart with sizes `k` and `q + 1`. The chart multiplies the compact factor, the embedded tail matrix, the upper rectangle and the leading matrix. The density is the inverse normalized tail determinant to the power `k`.

The proof transports the two-block Haar theorem to the consecutive sizes. It identifies the upper rectangle by its four matrix blocks and the diagonal factor by the block sum. The measure-preserving size identifications transport the two product measures and the determinant density. This prepares the right-tail formula for the triangular and Fourier changes of variables. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, Section 10.2](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf).
