# One polynomial growth exponent on the compact real Godement span

Fix strictly dominant lower real position data, a leading parity and a reference leading exponent. Any complex-linear map on the compact Godement section span that gives the first-column evaluation on each compact Schwartz generator has one polynomial growth exponent for all vectors. The constant may depend on the vector.

The dominant lower Jacquet form is bounded by the compact supremum. The smooth coefficient estimate gives a common height exponent for all derivative words of all smooth lower vectors. The uniform column bound for that exponent gives an upper group exponent that works simultaneously on every generator. The norm triangle inequality and homogeneity extend the bound to the span with the same exponent. The linear map of `RealGodementSpanEquivariance` has the required generator values.

This is a derived consequence of the lower coefficient bounds and the growth estimate for the real version of Jacquet's column formula (7.7), *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §7.2, Proposition 7.2, pp. 48–49.
