# Compact Godement generation of real K-finite vectors

Every K-finite vector in the upper real position principal series lies in the complex span of Godement sections from the trailing position principal series, with smooth tests supported in compact full-row-rank sets. The lower vectors can be taken K-finite in their position principal series. This is the K-finite case of the compact-support generation assertion in Hervé Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, Section 7.1, Proposition 7.1(v). The measure on the lower group is Borel, right invariant, finite on compact sets, and positive on nonempty open sets.

Fix the upper vector and take the finite-dimensional span of its right orthogonal translates. For each approximating pair of cutoffs, integrate the weighted kernel at the identity to obtain a linear functional on that span. The kernel formula on the orthogonal group expresses the restriction of the approximating section as this functional applied to the right translate of the original vector. These restrictions all lie in the image of the dual of the finite-dimensional translate span.

Intersect this image with the restrictions of the span of compact Godement sections. The intersection is finite dimensional and closed in the product topology on functions of the orthogonal variable. To obtain the K-finite lower vectors, fix a Gram cutoff and approximate the angular cutoff by real polynomials. The continuous angle on the compact preimage of the Gram support has a bounded image. Polynomial approximation on a containing interval and dominated convergence on that preimage give pointwise convergence on the upper orthogonal subgroup. Each polynomial gives a K-finite lower section, and its upper restriction lies in the same finite-dimensional translate image. Closedness of the intersection with the restrictions of the span formed using K-finite lower vectors includes the original smooth cutoff there. The compact approximation with tolerance tending to zero then puts the restriction of the upper vector in this intersection. The section theorem puts every element of the Godement span in the smooth upper principal series by `compactSectionSpan_le_principalSeries`, and Iwasawa then propagates agreement on the orthogonal subgroup to agreement on the full real group.
