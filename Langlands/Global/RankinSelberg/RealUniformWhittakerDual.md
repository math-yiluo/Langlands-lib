# Weyl inverse-transpose of real uniform Whittaker coefficients

`RealUniformWhittakerCharacterDualStatement` takes a uniform Whittaker coefficient of the actual K-finite real standard module of a full list of character blocks. The function obtained by the Weyl inverse-transpose in the local integrals belongs to the uniform model for the reversed inverse list and the opposite additive character. The statement applies at arbitrary inducing exponents for character lists.

The pullback on the inducing functions reverses the diagonal characters and evaluates them on inverse arguments. The published twist of the position principal series and the absence of induction-block rotation conditions make this pullback a linear equivalence of the specified K-finite sources. It commutes with orthogonal right translations, and a real matrix right derivative is carried through inverse transpose as the derivative in the negative transpose direction.

The coefficient proof also works for any supplied source equivalence with these compact and Lie laws. It precomposes the coefficient map with the source equivalence and pulls its values back by the Weyl inverse-transpose. Reflection of upper-unipotent coordinates reverses the additive parameter. The squared-entry height is preserved because the twist interchanges the two matrix terms and permutes their entries. Thus the transported map has the original common growth exponent and realizes the transformed functions. The conclusion for the span follows by applying the linear pullback to the ranges of the uniform intertwiners.

This is a derived transport lemma for the contragredient functions in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §2 and §7.2, pp. 3–6 and 47–48, using the Weyl and upper-Borel conventions of the Library.
