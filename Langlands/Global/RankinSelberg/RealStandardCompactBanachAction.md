# The compact Banach action of a real standard module

For a real inducing list, `RealStandardCompactBanachAction` realizes the standard module in the closure of its compact restrictions in the sup norm on continuous functions on the orthogonal group. The smooth-source map into this Banach space is injective and has dense range. The representation is jointly continuous, and it acts on the embedded source by actual right translation.

For any continuous profile, the position exponents extend it to the general linear group by the Gram formula. The formula is a continuous scalar multiplier times evaluation at the signed orthogonal factor. For a smooth standard vector it recovers the vector from its compact restriction. Evaluating the extended profile along translated compact arguments defines a bounded linear operator on all continuous compact profiles. This operator preserves the range of smooth standard restrictions, hence also its closure. The identity and product laws on the dense original range give the representation laws on the closure. The joint continuity of the multiplier and of compact evaluation gives joint continuity of the induced action in the sup norm by currying.
