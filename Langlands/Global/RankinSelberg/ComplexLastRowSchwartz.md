# Schwartz decay along the complex last row

`ComplexLastRowSchwartzStatement` bounds a complex row Schwartz test on the last row of a positive diagonal times a unitary matrix. The bound gives arbitrary exponential decay in the positive part of the last logarithmic diagonal coordinate.

The last row of a unitary matrix has squared Euclidean norm one. Comparing that sum with the supremum norm of the row gives the lower bound needed for the Schwartz estimate. Multiplication by the positive diagonal scales the row by the exponential of its last logarithmic coordinate. The constant is a multiple of the maximum of finitely many order-zero Schwartz seminorms, so it depends continuously on the test and vanishes at the zero test.

This supplies the row estimate in Jacquet's convergence argument for the unfolded adjacent-rank kernel. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, Lemma 3.5 and §8.3](https://doi.org/10.1090/conm/489/09547).
