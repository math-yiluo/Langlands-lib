# Whittaker source data on the actual determinant band

Take the actual cuspidal operator realization over a number field with compact additive adèle quotient, a norm-one central character acting on it, and a unitary form that computes the determinant-band integral. Assume band square integrability on the original realization.

The band norm identity, unitarity of the coordinate restriction, joint vector recovery, and identification of the genuine real generators apply to the same source embedding. Consequently the canonical band source satisfies the hypotheses for extending the Whittaker functional. This is an internal specialization of the form-completion construction.
