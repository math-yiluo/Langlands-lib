# Exponential domination in the two-block complex torus

For the concatenated logarithmic Iwasawa coordinates of the two-block chart, the sum of positive coordinates is bounded by the rank times the sum of the positive simple-root differences and the positive part of the last coordinate. This follows by telescoping from every coordinate to the last. The last coordinate belongs to the tail block.

A large positive determinant exponent in the first block controls its negative coordinates. The positive coordinates of both blocks are controlled by simple-root decay and decay in the last positive coordinate. Additional simple-root decay and decay in the negative part of the first tail coordinate control all negative tail coordinates. For the stated exponents the envelope is bounded by the product of the two integrable exponentials of the negative sums of absolute coordinates.
