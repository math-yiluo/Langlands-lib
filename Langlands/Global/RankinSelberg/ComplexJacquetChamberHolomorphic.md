# Holomorphy of the complex Jacquet integral in the strict chamber

`ComplexJacquetChamberHolomorphicStatement r` fixes a continuous unitary profile, a group argument, an additive Haar measure on `ℂ`, and the Whittaker character parameter. It proves joint complex differentiability of the open-cell Jacquet integral in the strict chamber of the `r + 1` inducing exponents. The `compactSection` in the integrand is the Library's compact principal-series extension with the normalized Borel shift.

The positive-diagonal Iwasawa decomposition writes the section at `x` as the fixed profile on the unitary part of `x` times the exponential of the linear form `Lₓ(p + ρ) = ∑ᵢ (2 αᵢ(x)) (pᵢ + ρᵢ)`. Here `αᵢ` is the logarithm of the positive diagonal entry and `ρ` is the normalized Borel shift. The phase of each positive diagonal entry is one. The norm of the exponential is the radial spherical majorant at the real parts of the inducing exponents, whose open-cell integral is absolutely convergent in the strict chamber. The unitary profile is bounded by compactness, and the integrating character has norm one.

For an exponent tuple `p` in the chamber, choose `δ > 0` such that all real corner shifts by `±2δ` stay in the chamber. Write `S(x) = ∑ᵢ |2 αᵢ(x)|`, which bounds the operator norm of `Lₓ`. On the ball of radius `δ`, the absolute exponential increases by at most `exp(δ S(x))`. The derivative contributes a factor of at most `S(x)`, and `δ S(x) ≤ exp(δ S(x))`. Shifting each exponent by `2δ` with the sign of its corresponding logarithm gives the resulting factor `exp(2δ S(x))` exactly. The sum of the radial majorants over the finitely many corners therefore gives a single integrable bound. Applying `hasFDerivAt_integral_of_dominated_of_fderiv_le` proves the claimed joint holomorphy.

This is internal infrastructure for the convergent chamber integral of Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §7.2, p. 47. The chamber uses upper-Borel coordinates and decreasing exponents. The result treats fixed profiles and group arguments in that chamber. Continuation beyond the chamber is a separate statement.
