# Meromorphic continuation of the complex Godement section

Fix positive smaller rank, a compact Haar measure on its unitary group, and the character list of a smaller standard module. For every vector in that module, rectangular Schwartz test, and fixed larger group element, the Godement section for the determinant-density Haar measure on the smaller general linear group has a meromorphic continuation in the exponent of the distinguished character. It is an entire numerator divided by the reciprocal denominator of the pair factor of the distinguished character and the inverses of the retained characters, evaluated at the spectral shift plus one. On a right half-plane whose bound depends only on the smaller rank and retained list, the group integral converges absolutely, the denominator is nonzero, and the continuation agrees with the section.

The additive diagonal comparison identifies the group integral there with one product Tate integral. The Weil exponents and angular weights of the pair blocks give exactly the complex Gamma factors that normalize this Tate integral. Its entire continuation supplies the numerator after multiplication by the entire determinant prefactor and the positive Haar comparison constant. The quotient is meromorphic, and the nonvanishing of the reciprocal Gamma factors in the right half-plane gives the claimed equality there.

This proves the continuation and factor statement of Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §7.1, Proposition 7.1(ii), pp. 43–44, in the upper-Borel and determinant-density Haar conventions.
