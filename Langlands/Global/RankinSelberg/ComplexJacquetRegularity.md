# Group regularity of the canonical complex Jacquet evaluation

Every canonical Jacquet value of a compact-finite standard vector is smooth and has moderate growth in the exact matrix height, at all inducing exponents.

In rank one the entry-Haar integral is the original standard vector throughout the strict chamber, and the analytic identity theorem gives the same equality at all exponents. In higher rank, compact full-row-rank Godement sections span the whole compact-finite standard module. The exact generator formula gives smoothness on each of these sections. Linearity of the canonical evaluation and closure of smooth functions under sums and scalar multiples give smoothness on their span.

For growth, apply the local uniform simple-root bound at the chosen inducing parameter. The logarithmic estimate for the augmented matrix bounds the sum of absolute toral logarithms by a logarithm of the augmented matrix size and the positive simple-root sum. Choosing the simple-root decay order to absorb that sum leaves a power of the augmented matrix size. Its entries belong to the group matrix and its inverse, so the power is bounded by a constant times a power of the published matrix height.
