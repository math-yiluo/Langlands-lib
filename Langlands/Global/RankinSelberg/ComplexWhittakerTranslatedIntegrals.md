# Weighted unequal-rank identities on complex Whittaker right translates

A weighted equality between the two unequal-rank integrals on exact finite Whittaker vectors extends to fixed right translates of exact intertwiner values. The theorem retains a common integrable bound for each weighted kernel as its two translation factors vary over the respective unitary groups. The weights may be the radial Mellin functions of the unequal-rank pair identity.

For each right translate, take the sequence of positive unit-mass compact averages in the original finite model. Averaging each of the two factors preserves the integrable bound. The ordinary and dual kernels for the approximants are measurable by the exact quotient continuity results. Each weighted kernel converges pointwise to the kernel for the two translates. Dominated convergence and the equality on every pair of approximants give the asserted equality.
