# Positive compact approximation of complex Whittaker right translates

A genuine right translate of a value of an exact Whittaker intertwiner is a pointwise limit of vectors in the original finite model. Each approximating vector is given by an average of the translate with a nonnegative compact weight of integral one. The compact weights are common to all evaluation points.

Choose central polynomial weights with first displacement moments tending to zero. The compact projection theorem realizes each average inside the finite model and gives the same average after applying the Whittaker intertwiner. At a fixed evaluation point, smoothness gives a first-difference bound on the compact profile. The moment estimate bounds the averaging error by this fixed first-difference bound times the moment, which tends to zero.
