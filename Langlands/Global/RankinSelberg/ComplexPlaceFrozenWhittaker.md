# Complex Whittaker restrictions with a frozen infinite argument

adelic evaluation fixes commuting archimedean coordinates and the identity
finite coordinate. The statement uses the standard adelic character and its
specified nonzero scale at the varying place.

The global smooth Whittaker extension composed with the complex place
matrix and left multiplication by the fixed argument supplies smoothness.
The determinant norm is multiplicative, so this fixed argument contributes a
constant to the published determinant bound. The derivative of the matrix
composition carries the local right direction to the global right direction.

and Lie covariance give the two right action laws on the coefficient map.
These are direct consequences of the Library's archimedean Whittaker
regularity and complex place inclusion results.
