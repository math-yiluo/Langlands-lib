# Mellin pair weights with an arbitrary positive Gaussian rate

For each positive Gaussian rate and real linear shift, the ordinary and reflected reciprocal factors define explicit inverse Mellin weights. Both weights are measurable and rapidly decreasing at zero and infinity. Their Mellin transforms recover the stated ordinary and reflected factors with the same Gaussian multiplier.

The proof bounds the real part of the Gaussian exponent on every finite strip. Its negative quadratic term in the height absorbs the linear exponential bound and leaves integrable vertical decay. The inverse Mellin theorem then gives rapid decay and recovers both prescribed transforms.
