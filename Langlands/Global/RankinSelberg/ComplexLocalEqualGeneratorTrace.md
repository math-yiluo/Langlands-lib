# Ordinary equal-rank traces of compact recursive generators

Fix the standard complex additive character and a compact rectangular Schwartz test supported in the full-row-rank set. The first coefficient is its canonical Jacquet recursion with one adjoining exponent. The second coefficient is a fixed canonical Jacquet evaluation. Given a curve-holomorphic normalized square-test continuation with the two stated chamber values and strip growth at each adjoining exponent, the two ordinary equal-rank integrals have entire normalizations with strip growth and satisfy the normalized reflection equation at every adjoining exponent.

The primal and dual chamber unfoldings have a common positive Haar scalar. The rectangular dual recursion identifies the first dual coefficient with the opposite-character recursion times the leading character at minus one raised to the smaller rank. The mixed Fourier signs then contribute the smaller central scalar. Together with the double-integral phase, it gives the full equal-rank epsilon factor times the target central sign. The reciprocal Gamma products in the two unfolded traces are the full pair denominators. Joint holomorphy of the ordinary traces over bounded disks of adjoining exponents allows the identity theorem in that exponent to extend each chamber agreement to every exponent, on an appropriate spectral half-plane. Dividing the square witness by the positive Haar scalar gives the primal witness. Spectral reflection and multiplication by the full phase gives its dual and preserves strip growth.

This internal result implements the comparison of the double integral with its ordinary Rankin–Selberg traces in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §8.2, printed and PDF pp. 54–58.
