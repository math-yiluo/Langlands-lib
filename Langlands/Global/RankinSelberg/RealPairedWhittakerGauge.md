# Toral decay of genuine smooth real Whittaker pairs

`RealPairedWhittakerGaugeStatement` concerns the smooth vectors of the
completed Banach projective product of two continuous representations of
positive-rank real general linear groups. A continuous linear scalar form
is assumed to transform under the two independent upper unipotent groups
with the indicated opposite nonzero Whittaker scales. Fix a smooth product
vector and let one group argument vary over logarithmic torus points times
orthogonal matrices. Its paired coefficient decays to arbitrary order in
the positive simple-root coordinates, with a fixed exponential growth
allowance in the absolute logarithmic coordinates. The bound is uniform in
the other group argument after allowing a fixed power of its matrix height.
The height power and the growth exponent are independent of the vector and
the decay order.

The proof applies the genuine smooth moderate growth estimate to the norm
of the scalar form. The product unit height is bounded by a constant times
the product of the two coordinate matrix heights. Fix the other coordinate
and divide its coefficient by the appropriate power of that height.
The two slot embeddings commute, so the divided coefficient obeys the
Whittaker covariance in the varying coordinate. Its right derivatives
are scalar coefficients of axial Lie derivatives of the smooth vector.
Their polynomial estimates thus have one common degree and each derivative
has a single constant valid for the entire family of fixed coordinates.
The uniform derivative gauge gives the simple-root decay. Multiplying by
the height power restores the original paired coefficient.

The argument uses the simple-root estimate of Proposition 3.1 and extends
its moderate growth principle to two independent group coordinates, as
in the translated estimate of Lemma 3.8, pp. 15–16.
[H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009)](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf#page=15).

The single-slot embeddings are smooth in the product units chart, and units
in distinct slots commute. These properties apply to reflected scalar
coefficients in either coordinate.
