# Group covariance of the continued real Godement coefficients

For strictly dominant lower position data, fixed leading parity and any reference leading exponent, the continued Jacquet evaluation defines a complex-linear map on the full span of compact full-row-rank Godement sections. It gives the first-column value on each generating section, intertwines all right translations, and satisfies the left Whittaker law for the negative additive parameter.

Right pullback preserves compact support in the full-row-rank locus. The defining section integral and the first-column value acquire the same determinant factor when the upper group variable is right translated. The generator formula and linearity extend this identity to the span. For the left law, the ordinary convergent Jacquet integral has the Whittaker covariance in the dominant half-plane. Both expressions are entire at fixed group arguments, so the complex identity theorem gives the identity at the reference exponent.

This is the group-equivariant part of the derived continuation of the real version of Jacquet's (7.4)–(7.7), *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §7.2, pp. 47–48.
