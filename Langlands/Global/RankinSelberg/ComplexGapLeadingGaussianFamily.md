# Standard leading square Gaussians on the complex gap chart

`complexGapLeadingGaussianFamily` expresses the leading restriction of a standard rectangular polynomial Gaussian as a finite combination of fixed square polynomial Gaussians, all evaluated after one common positive scalar dilation. The dilation is given by the logarithmic tail coordinate. The coefficients depend continuously on the compact unitary coordinate.

The retained matrix acts on the first columns as the leading scalar followed by the compact matrix. Unitary left multiplication preserves the sum of squared entry norms, as does adding zero trailing rows. Expanding each real or imaginary coordinate of the rotated matrix gives a finite sum of square-coordinate polynomials with continuous coefficients. Polynomial addition and multiplication extend the expansion to the full multiplier. At the identity retained matrix one can prescribe any standard square polynomial Gaussian by assigning its polynomial to the first rows. This uses the leading matrix restriction of the split complex gap chart. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §10.2, pp. 72–73](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf#page=72)
