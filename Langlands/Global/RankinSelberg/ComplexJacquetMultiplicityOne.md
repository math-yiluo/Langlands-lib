# The exact complex Whittaker model is the canonical Jacquet range

`ComplexJacquetMultiplicityOneStatement` establishes that every exact Whittaker intertwiner with the given data and character is a scalar multiple of the canonical Jacquet intertwiner. Consequently the full Library Whittaker model equals the canonical range.

Extend identity evaluation of each of two exact intertwiners to the full smooth principal series. The extension also recovers the intertwiner at every group element by evaluating on a right translate. A nonzero intertwiner gives a nonzero smooth functional for that reason. Smooth multiplicity one compares the two extensions, and evaluation on the same translated vector gives equality of the original intertwiners. The canonical intertwiner and its nonzero value are supplied by the all-parameter continuation results. Every range in the defining supremum is then contained in the canonical range, and the canonical range occurs in that supremum.

The parameter-list lemma recovers an arbitrary inducing list of the required length. It takes the listed weights and exponents and checks the actual character equality at every element of the complex Weil group.
