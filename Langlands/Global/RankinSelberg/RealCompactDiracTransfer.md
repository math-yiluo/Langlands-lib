# Moving a translate from a principal-series vector to a test function

Module `Langlands.Global.RankinSelberg.RealCompactDiracTransfer`, namespace
`Langlands.Global.RankinSelbergGlobal.RealCompactDirac`.

Let `χ` be characters of `ℝˣ` of signed-power form with exponents `e`
(`PartialJacquet.IsSignedPower χ e`: `χ_l(x) = χ_l(sgn x) |x|^{e_l}` and `χ_l(1) = 1`), and
let `ξ` be in the smooth principal series `principalSeries ℝ n χ`. The dual exponents are
`dualExponent n e l = -e_l + (n - 1)/2 - l` (`shiftedExponent n (-e)`). `O(n)` carries the Haar
probability measure `RealPrincipalSeriesHilbert.haarOrthogonal n`.

## Main result

`integral_translate_eq`: for every continuous `ψ : O(n) → ℂ` and `h ∈ GL_n(ℝ)`,

`∫_{O(n)} ξ(k h) ψ(k) dk = ∫_{O(n)} ξ(k) Ψ(k h⁻¹) dk`,  `Ψ = extension (dualExponent n e) ψ`.

No covariance of `ψ` under the diagonal sign matrices is assumed.

## Steps

* `extension_mul_left_pos`: for every profile `φ`, positive diagonal `a` and upper unipotent
  `u`, `extension s φ (diag(a) u g) = (∏ₗ a_l^{s_l}) extension s φ g`. Proof: as the Library's
  `extension_mul_left`, from `extension_eq` applied to `g = diag(a') u' k` and to
  `diag(a) u g`; the sign matrices agree because `a > 0`.
* `isModulusEquivariant_translate_mul_extension`: `g ↦ ξ(g h) · extension (dualExponent n e) φ g`
  is `RealPrincipalSeriesDuality.IsModulusEquivariant`. On `exp(diag x)` the character of `ξ` is
  `∏ₗ e^{x_l (e_l + (n-1)/2 - l)}` (Library `principalSeriesCharacter_eq`, the sign factor being
  `1`) and that of the extension is `∏ₗ e^{x_l (-e_l + (n-1)/2 - l)}`; the product is the modulus
  character `∏ₗ e^{x_l (n - 1 - 2l)}`.
* `map_haarOrthogonal`: the image of `haarOrthogonal n` under
  `RealPrincipalSeriesHilbert.orthogonalCompactEquiv n` is the Library's `haarK n` on
  `unitaryCompact ℝ n` (both are Haar probability measures:
  `Measure.isHaarMeasure_map_of_isFiniteMeasure`, `Measure.isHaarMeasure_eq_of_isProbabilityMeasure`).
* `integral_haarOrthogonal_mul`: the Library's `kIntegralInvariance`
  (`∫ φ(k g) dk = ∫ φ(k) dk` for smooth modulus-equivariant `φ`) transported to `haarOrthogonal n`.
* `exists_mvPolynomial_of_mem_polynomialStar`: every element of the Library's
  `OrthogonalFinitePolynomial.polynomialStar n` (the star subalgebra generated by the matrix
  entries) is a polynomial in the entries (induction on `Algebra.adjoin`).
  `exists_polynomialStar_near`: these are uniformly dense in `C(O(n), ℂ)` (Stone–Weierstrass,
  `ContinuousMap.starSubalgebra_topologicalClosure_eq_top_of_separatesPoints`).
* `integral_translate_eq_of_polynomial`: the identity for polynomial `ψ = p`. The extension of
  a polynomial profile is smooth (Library `contMDiff_extension`), so
  `F(g) = ξ(g h) · extension (dualExponent n e) p g` is smooth and modulus-equivariant, and
  `integral_haarOrthogonal_mul` with `h⁻¹` gives `∫ F(k h⁻¹) dk = ∫ F(k) dk`; on `O(n)` the
  extension is `p` (`extension_unitary`).
* `continuous_extension_translate`: `k ↦ extension s φ (k h)` is continuous
  (`continuous_multiplier` and `RealCartanCompactSupport.continuous_signedOrthogonalPart`).
* `integral_translate_eq`: both sides are bounded linear functionals of `ψ` for the sup norm:
  `|ξ(k h) ψ(k)| ≤ C₁ ‖ψ‖` and `|ξ(k) Ψ(k h⁻¹)| ≤ C₂ ‖ψ‖` with
  `C₂ = sup |ξ(k) multiplier(k h⁻¹)|`. Approximating `ψ` within `ε` by a polynomial `p` shows
  that their difference has norm at most `(C₁ + C₂) ε` for every `ε > 0`.

## Provenance

Derived, internal infrastructure; no literature source is claimed. It is the `GL_n(ℝ)`-invariance
of the pairing between the principal series of `χ` and its dual, applied with a merely
continuous test function on `O(n)`.
