# Linear spanning of integrable polynomial normalizations

For a fixed measure and a fixed spectral factor, the kernels that are integrable on a right half-plane and have a polynomial normalization there form a complex linear subspace. For two kernels the larger of their half-plane thresholds suffices, and Bochner integration is linear there. Scalar multiplication of a kernel multiplies the polynomial by the same scalar.

The two normalization lemmas relate membership to an ordinary entire normalization given by polynomial evaluation, with absolute convergence where required. This is a derived linearity step in the polynomial generation from Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), Proposition 8.1, author-posted pp. 60–61.
