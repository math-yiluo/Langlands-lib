# A common growth exponent for real Godement column values

Fix the lower group Haar measure, nonzero real additive parameter, leading parity and leading exponent. Fix a growth exponent for the regular right derivatives of the lower Whittaker inputs. The first-column Godement values have one polynomial growth exponent in the upper group height for all such lower coefficients and rectangular Schwartz tests. The growth constant can depend on the lower coefficient and the test.

The specified derivative exponent determines the exponential growth term in the gauge estimate and therefore the Schwartz decay orders needed in the column Mellin bound. One continuous test seminorm suffices for all the lower coefficients. The integrable lower-group majorant can depend on the lower coefficient. The right-translation estimate bounds the test seminorm by a fixed power of upper group height, and the outer determinant weight adds a power depending on the leading exponent. The long Weyl matrix is orthogonal, so including it in the upper argument preserves the height.

The estimate is derived for the real version of Jacquet's column formula (7.7), *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §7.2, Proposition 7.2, pp. 48–49. It concerns the values of the column integrals at the specified parameters.
