# Compact Godement tests from the cofactor lift

Fix a compactly supported Schwartz cutoff on rectangular complex matrices whose support lies in the locus where the cofactor completion is invertible. Assume the cutoff is invariant under right multiplication by unitary matrices. For a larger-group upper-Borel standard vector, evaluation at the cofactor completion times the cutoff gives a compactly supported Schwartz test of full row rank. This assignment is linear in the larger vector. Translating the vector by a right unitary matrix translates the test and multiplies it by the distinguished character of the unitary determinant.

The cofactor completion is smooth in the rectangular entries. On its invertible locus, the standard vector is smooth in matrix coordinates, and off the cutoff support the product vanishes locally. A right inverse for the rectangular matrix is obtained from the inverse of its completed matrix. The transformation law for the cofactor completion and the upper-Borel law for the distinguished first character give the determinant twist. The unit determinant norm removes the modulus power in that law.

The module also constructs such cutoffs in any invariant open neighborhood of the unitary trailing rows. When the cutoff equals one on those rows, the test restricts to the compact vector divided by the distinguished determinant character. For a compact-finite vector, the right translates of the test lie in the linear image of the finite-dimensional orbit span of the vector.

This constructs compact test families for the Godement spanning assertion in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §7.1, Proposition 7.1(v), pp. 44–46.
