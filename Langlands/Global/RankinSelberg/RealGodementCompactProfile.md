# Fixed compact profiles for real character families

Fix the parities of a real character list. A compact profile is a continuous function on the orthogonal group that is finite under right translation and transforms by the specified parities under left diagonal signs. The same profile extends to a vector of the K-finite standard module at every set of inducing exponents. Each K-finite vector at those exponents is recovered by extending its orthogonal restriction. The family of extensions is jointly continuous in the inducing and group variables and holomorphic in the inducing variables at each fixed group element.

The Lean proof uses the positive triangular factor of the real Iwasawa decomposition. Its diagonal signs are trivial. The section formula on this factor is the exponential of the pairing of the normalized exponents with the logarithms of the positive diagonal entries, times the fixed profile. Continuity of the Iwasawa coordinates and holomorphy of this exponential give the parameter regularity. The extension theorem uses finite right translates and parity covariance to obtain standard-module membership. Iwasawa covariance determines a principal-series vector by its orthogonal restriction, which gives recovery of the original vector.
