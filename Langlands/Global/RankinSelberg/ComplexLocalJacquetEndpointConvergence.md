# Endpoint convergence for the complex Jacquet induction

This internal result supplies the two endpoint convergence packages used by the equal-rank and corank-one reductions. It covers the absolute convergence in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), Theorem 2.1(i), for those two rank differences on the Library's complex Whittaker models.

For equal rank, the published uniform simple-root estimates and the Schwartz last-row test give the logarithmic kernel bound. The opposing Whittaker characters make the product descend to the unipotent quotient, and continuity gives the measurability premise of the toral convergence theorem. The dual functions belong to the reversed inverse-character models. Their Fourier test has a Schwartz representative that agrees pointwise with the exact Fourier integral, so the same estimate applies.

For corank one, the published ordinary convergence theorem applies to the original and dual model vectors. The dual block test has zero rectangular rows, and the published single-point comparison transfers ordinary convergence of the duals to the specified dual kernel. In each case the maximum of the two lower bounds gives a common right half-plane.
