# Gaussian moment detection for Godement pairings

`complexGodementGaussianPairing` works in the sufficient convergence half-plane for a fixed smaller standard module. Against any continuous weight on the larger unitary group, the compact restrictions of polynomial Gaussian Godement sections are integrable. If the weight pairs to zero with all those restrictions, it also pairs to zero with the compact restriction for every compactly supported Schwartz test.

The sample matrix at \((k,h)\) is \(h\) times the trailing rows of \(k\). The proof puts its entries into a Euclidean space and writes the compact pairing as a single product integral. The weight in that integral includes both Godement character factors and the smaller vector at \(h^{-1}\). The half-strength Gaussian gives an integrable majorant. A polynomial Gaussian factors as that Gaussian times a bounded Schwartz function, which proves integrability for the polynomial Gaussian pairings.

A real-linear functional on the sample matrix is a degree-one polynomial in its real and imaginary entries. Its powers supply the directional moments required by Gaussian moment determination. That result gives the compact test pairing after the Euclidean change of coordinates. The same polynomial formula shows that right unitary translation preserves the class of Gaussian polynomial tests.

This is an internal step in the reduction to split tests for adjacent ranks. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, Proposition 7.1(i) and Section 8.3](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf).
