# Integrable orbital averages with an independent parameter

This internal comparison is used to justify the changes of integration order in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §8.2, printed pp. 54–56. The parameter measure is s-finite. The original kernel is jointly measurable and integrable for the product with the group Haar measure. The quotient measure is sigma-finite.

The Iwasawa homeomorphism gives a continuous representative of each left unipotent coset. Composing the kernel with this section and the unipotent action makes its orbit integral jointly measurable. The norm of the orbit integral is bounded by the integral of the norm. The positive Weil formula identifies the resulting quotient integral with the group integral of that norm at each parameter. Tonelli and input integrability give the desired product integrability.
