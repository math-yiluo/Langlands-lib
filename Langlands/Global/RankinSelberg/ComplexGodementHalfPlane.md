# Holomorphy of complex Godement sections on the convergence half-plane

`shiftedCharacter` keeps the angular weight and adds `s` to the exponent.
`godementSectionShift_eq_section` identifies the shifted section with the
Godement integral for that character. The determinant multipliers inside
and outside the integral are exactly the complex powers of the positive
normalized determinant modulus.

`complexGodementHalfPlane` proves holomorphy at each fixed larger group
element on a common right half-plane for a fixed smaller standard module
of positive rank. The uniform height estimate gives absolute integrability
at each real shift in that half-plane for the determinant-density Haar
measure. Translating the half-plane to the region of positive real part
allows the Library's Mellin theorem to give holomorphy of the integral.
The outside determinant multiplier is entire. The sufficient bound on the
real part of the shifted exponent depends only on the smaller standard
module.

[Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, §7.1, equation (7.1) and Proposition 7.1(i)–(ii), pp. 43–44](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf).
