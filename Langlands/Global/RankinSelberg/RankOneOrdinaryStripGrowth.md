# Normalized strip growth for all rank-one ordinary archimedean tests

`rankOneOrdinaryStripGrowth` proves the ordinary continuation and growth properties for both rank-one coefficient models, at any compatible quotient measure and opposite nontrivial additive scales. Every ordinary Schwartz test has an entire normalization, and every entire normalization of that integral by its prescribed pair L-factor has exponential strip growth.

The rank-one parameter constraints give singleton character lists. The model coefficient formulas identify each Whittaker vector with a scalar multiple of its determinant character. The real and complex rank-one growth theorems then supply a normalization of exponential strip growth for every test. The reciprocal denominators are nonzero on a right half-plane. This gives uniqueness by the identity theorem, so the growth bound holds for every choice of entire normalization.

This is the coefficient-model form of the rank-one Tate case of Hervé Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §2.1, (2.2), (2.6) and Theorem 2.1(i)–(ii), author-version pp. 5–7. The proof uses the exponential strip bounds for the scalar Tate normalizations in `RealRankZeroStripGrowth` and `ComplexRankZeroStripGrowth`.
