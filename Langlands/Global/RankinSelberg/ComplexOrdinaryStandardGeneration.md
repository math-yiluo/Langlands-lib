# Reduction of complex ordinary generation to the standard trace character

`complexOrdinaryStandardGenerationReduction` derives the published complex
ordinary generation statement from pointwise unequal-rank coverage at trace
parameter `1` and finite sums equal to one at that parameter in equal and
adjacent ranks. Its hypotheses use the same irreducible standard modules and
compatible quotient measures as the published statement.

For unequal ranks, choose a standard-character test whose entire normalization
is nonzero at the specified spectral point. The change of trace character
uses the transported quotient measure and a right translation of the larger
coefficient. The published Jacquet range comparison writes the rescaled larger
coefficient as an exact intertwiner value. Its genuine right translate has a
normalization obtained as the locally uniform limit of compact-finite test
normalizations. The integral comparison and uniqueness identify that limit
with the original normalization times a nonvanishing entire determinant
multiplier. At least one finite test therefore has nonzero value at the
specified point. The established normalized unequal-rank equations supply
the hypotheses of the approximation theorem.

In equal rank the two left translations suffice. At parameter `-1` the
larger unequal right correction is unitary and the determinant multiplier
is one. The finite sums consequently transfer to `-1` in both cases.
The two left translations preserve nontriviality of the Whittaker models.

This is a derived form of Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*
(2009), Remark 2.2, author pp. 7–8. The remaining standard-character inputs
are the coverage and finite-sum conclusions of Theorems 2.3, 2.6 and 2.7,
author pp. 8–10.
