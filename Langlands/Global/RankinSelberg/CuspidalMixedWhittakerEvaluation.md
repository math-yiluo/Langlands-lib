# Actual Whittaker evaluations on the mixed smooth space

At infinite identity, the original evaluations are the linear
functionals supplied by the actual cusp Whittaker integral. Their
finite covariance follows from right translation of the integral
and multiplication in the adelic product. Given continuous extensions
at the compact open levels and the derivative density of the original
smooth images, the gluing construction extends these evaluations to
the mixed smooth space and preserves finite covariance.

To evaluate at a general translate of an original vector, choose a
compact open level fixing that original vector. Archimedean translation
preserves the resulting Hilbert fixed level. The actual analytic
comparison then computes the translated evaluation, while the finite
covariance moves the finite factor into the argument of the functional.
This is an internal specialization of continuous extension gluing and
analytic comparison to the actual Whittaker integral.

For a full adelic upper unipotent, use its finite and infinite
coordinates together. Its translated value on each original fixed
vector satisfies the actual integral's left character law.
Continuity and derivative density at that level give the same law for
all mixed smooth vectors, with the actual adelic generic character.
