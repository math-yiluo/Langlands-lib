# Eigenvector decomposition for the complex coordinate square operator

Every unitary-finite principal series vector is a finite linear combination
of eigenvectors for the coordinate square operator. The real trace Casimir
acts by a scalar and the compact square sum is Hermitian for the integral
over the unitary subgroup. Each compact infinitesimal action is skew
symmetric, so its square is symmetric. The compact pairing is positive
definite because restriction to the unitary subgroup determines a
principal series vector.

The previously constructed invariant finite-dimensional subspace for
the coordinate square operator is also invariant under the compact
square sum. The finite-dimensional spectral theorem on this subspace
gives the claimed expansion.
