# Normalized reflection from ordinary dual tests to real paired tests

`ordinaryNormalization_eq` transfers the normalized functional equation from the entire pair in `HasRealPairNormalizedGrowth` to any two ordinary normalizations of the same integrals. Each reciprocal denominator is nonzero in a sufficiently far right half-plane. There the normalized values agree, so entire uniqueness gives equality throughout the plane.

`realPairedUnequalNormalizedReflection` assumes the normalized functional equation and strip growth for each genuine paired vector. The finite row comparison writes the reflected ordinary integral as a finite sum of rectangular integrals on the right. Sum their normalized functional equations and use the same entire uniqueness. At the reflected parameter the value of any normalization of the ordinary dual integral is the sum of the corresponding primal ordinary values at the original parameter, multiplied by the common sign and epsilon factor. The pure kernel lemma identifies the reflected ordinary kernel with the scalar ordinary kernel on the two Weyl inverse-transpose functions. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, Theorem 2.1(iii), p. 6, and §14.2, pp. 104–107](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
