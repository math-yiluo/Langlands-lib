# The normalized adjacent-rank equation at every inducing parameter

Assume the lower equal-rank inputs `HasAdjacentLowerEqualInputs` for each retained exponent tuple with the prescribed angular weights. `complexAdjacentCanonical` gives the normalized adjacent-rank equation for every larger canonical Jacquet evaluation and every value of the smaller exact intertwiner, for arbitrary larger inducing exponents. The input includes the lower equal-rank entire normalizations and the absolute convergence of the paired row-test integrals.

The chamber calculation gives a sufficient bound for the real part of the leading exponent with the retained tuple fixed. When the retained tuple is strictly ordered, choose a leading exponent strictly above both this bound and the first retained real part. The corresponding strict inequalities persist on a small disk along the line to any specified leading exponent. `complexUnequalCanonicalReflection` extends the equation to the endpoint. Now take an affine line from a tuple with strictly ordered retained real parts to the desired full tuple. The strict tail condition is open, so the first continuation supplies the normalized equation near the start of this second line. Applying the same continuation gives the result at its endpoint.

The specialization to adjacent ranks supplies the quotient measure instance from regularity and sigma compactness, then invokes `complexAdjacentCanonicalChamber`. The two-pass continuation is also available as `complexUnequalCanonicalFromChamber` for any smaller rank when the larger chamber result has this head threshold. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, Section 8.1](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf).

`complexAdjacentCanonicalGrowth` gives the strip-growing normalized equation at every larger inducing parameter, assuming the lower equal-rank input with strip growth. The strong-chamber theorem gives the starting pairs. Both affine parameter lines use the growth-preserving continuation of the exact canonical integrals. The uniform strip majorants provide its bounds along each line.
