# Absolute convergence on the real Jacquet open cell

`RealGodementOpenCellConvergenceStatement` concerns the positive-phase Jacquet integrand of a continuous function on \(GL_{t+1}(\mathbb R)\) with upper-Borel covariance. If the absolute value of the diagonal multiplier has powers \(\sigma_j\) with \(\sigma_{j+1}-\sigma_j<-1\) for \(j<t\), the integrand is absolutely integrable with the real self-dual upper-entry Haar measure, for every right translate. The signed-power principal-series specialization uses \(\sigma_j=\operatorname{Re} e_j+t/2-j\), so the condition is strict decrease of the real parts of the inducing exponents.

The proof transfers the upper entry measure to the strict lower entries of \(w_{t+1}u w_{t+1}\) by reversing both indices. Self-dual scalar measure differs from Lebesgue measure by a finite constant, and the coordinate permutation preserves the specified product. Apply the real Levi chart integrability theorem with singleton blocks to the product of trailing Gram determinants with powers \((\sigma_j-\sigma_{j-1})/2\) for \(j\ge 1\). At \(j=0\) the determinant is one. The existing Gram growth bound controls the right-translated function by this integrable product, using its supremum bound on the orthogonal group. The unitary character leaves its norm unchanged.

This is the absolute convergence of the individual open-cell integral used in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §7.2, p. 47, at (7.3). Weyl reversal gives the decreasing order of the exponents in the upper-Borel convention. The joint integral for a Godement section and parameter continuation are separate steps.
