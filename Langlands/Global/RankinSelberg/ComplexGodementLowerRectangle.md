# Lower rectangle insertion in the complex Godement integral

The lower-block argument in the dual Rankin–Selberg integral factors as a smaller general linear matrix padded with identities, followed by the lower rectangular unipotent matrix inside the once-padded group. Its inserted factor has determinant one. The continued Godement value at that argument therefore has the two-block integral with the half power of the smaller determinant and the original positive Haar chart constant.

The proof uses the existing lower-block multiplication rule and the compatibility of last-row padding with simultaneous reindexing. Applying the two-block restriction formula gives the value and proves absolute integrability of its kernel at each fixed lower rectangle. This is the pointwise identity used before integrating the lower rectangle in Jacquet's *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, Lemmas 9.1 and 10.1.
