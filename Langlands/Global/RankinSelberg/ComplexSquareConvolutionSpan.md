# Spanning by normalized square Gaussian convolutions

`complexSquareConvolutionSpan` gives the full compact-finite irreducible standard module as the span of normalized square Gaussian convolution values at each complex spectral point. It assumes nondecreasing angular weights, a nonzero Whittaker intertwiner and an arbitrary group Haar measure.

The ordered Gaussian witness gives a nonzero vector in the span. For a unitary translation, left Haar invariance moves the translation to the test and contributes a scalar from the determinant character. The inverse translate of the test is again a standard square polynomial Gaussian. For a real Lie direction, the derivative of the convergent convolution splits into a determinant slope term and a convolution with a differentiated Gaussian of the same scale. After Whittaker evaluation, both action identities hold on right half-planes. The scalar evaluations of the polynomial vector families and the determinant slope are entire, so the identities hold at the chosen point. Injectivity of the nonzero Whittaker intertwiner carries these identities back to the standard module. The span is therefore stable under the compact group and the complexified Lie algebra, and irreducibility gives the result. The square test and its normalization build on Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §8.2 and Proposition 8.1.
