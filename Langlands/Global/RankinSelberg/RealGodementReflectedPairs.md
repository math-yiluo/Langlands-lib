# Continued upper coefficients and their reflected real Godement columns

For a strictly dominant lower list of real characters and an arbitrary leading exponent, the construction gives a linear evaluation on the compact Godement section span. On each generator it equals the first-column value of the lower open-cell Jacquet integral. Its restriction to the upper K-finite standard module is a uniform Whittaker map. For each upper input, its coefficient and its Weyl inverse-transpose reflection belong to the span of paired Godement columns. In each generator pair the first column uses a compact full-row-rank Schwartz test. The second uses its Fourier transform, the opposite additive parameter, the dual lower coefficient and the negative leading exponent. Its scalar is the leading character at \((-1)^{t+1}\), with lower group of rank \(t+1\).

The K-finite lower generation theorem puts the upper input in the span of sections built from K-finite position-principal-series vectors. For a list of characters these are vectors of the lower standard module. The convergent lower open-cell Jacquet integral gives a uniform lower coefficient, and lower Weyl inverse-transpose puts its dual in the uniform model of the reversed inverse list. On each section generator the continued upper evaluation is the corresponding first column. Real column reflection supplies the second, with the same generator test and lower input. The map taking a coefficient to the pair with its dual is complex-linear. Thus the same linear combination represents both outputs. The reflection of each column is the real case of the calculation in Jacquet's *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, Section 7.3, Proposition 7.3.

`RealGodementReflectedIntertwinerStatement` records the uniform intertwiner and the paired span membership on K-finite inputs. `RealGodementReflectedPairsStatement` also specifies the evaluation on generators of the compact section span.
