# Joint holomorphy of the dual complex unequal-rank integral

The exact dual block integral is holomorphic in a finite-dimensional complex parameter for pointwise holomorphic Whittaker families that have continuous group slices and uniform simple-root bounds. The spectral exponent can also vary holomorphically with the parameter.

Weyl inverse transpose transfers the uniform gauge to each dual family. On a toral representative it reverses and negates the logarithms, preserving both the sum of their absolute values and the sum of positive simple roots. The left character of the dual function has absolute value one, so the bound holds on the whole group.

On a toral quotient representative, the rectangular matrix estimate controls the positive logarithms and the logarithmic rectangle size together by the larger positive simple-root sum. The determinant power controls negative directions once its real exponent exceeds a fixed threshold. A root decay order chosen from the upper real endpoint controls all remaining growth on the interval. The resulting majorant is a product of the integrable toral exponential and an integrable negative power of the rectangle size. The latter is bounded by a product of one-entry integrable functions for the specified self-dual measure.

The product kernel is continuous in the quotient and rectangular arguments and holomorphic at each fixed pair of arguments. Dominated holomorphic integration yields joint holomorphy for the product integral. Absolute convergence and Fubini identify it with the published iterated dual integral.
