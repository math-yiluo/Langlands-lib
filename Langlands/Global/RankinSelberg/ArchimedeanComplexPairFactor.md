# Rapidly decreasing Mellin pairs for the complex Rankin–Selberg factors

The complex pair factors and their duals admit measurable radial Mellin weights with arbitrary power decay at both ends. Their transforms are the reciprocal gamma products times the prescribed epsilon and Gaussian factors. Both transforms used for division are nonzero on a right half-plane. The theorem also gives the factor relation off a discrete set.

The proof applies the vertical-strip estimate for the reciprocal complex gamma function to each shifted block and multiplies the bounds. The power of the norm in each epsilon factor has exponential type on vertical strips. The generic inverse Mellin construction applies the Gaussian factor to these entire functions and produces the weights. This is the complex instance of the Mellin-pair construction in Jacquet's *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, §4.
