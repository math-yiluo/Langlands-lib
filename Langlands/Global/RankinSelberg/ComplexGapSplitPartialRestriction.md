# The alternate restriction of a complex Godement value

For a positive leading block size, a fixed padded argument, and a test
whose partial Fourier transform splits into a leading rectangle and trailing columns,
`complexGapSplitPartialRestriction` expresses the continued Godement value
by the alternate iterated integral. It gives absolute integrability of
the resulting outer marginal, including the determinant factor from
the column Fourier exchanges.

The padded restriction first gives an absolutely integrable kernel on
the smaller general linear group. The triangular Haar formula pulls it
back to the product of retained and inner tail variables. The trailing
columns and the last row are fixed by the embedded leading group in the
required positions. The split test and multiplicativity of the determinant
character give the ordinary block kernel. Apply the fixed-fiber column
Fourier identity and then Fubini's theorem. The Haar scaling and the
prefactor from the padded restriction yield the stated value.

This proves the alternate restriction for split tests in H. Jacquet,
*Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §10.2, formulas
(10.4)–(10.5) and Lemma 10.2, author pp. 72–73.
