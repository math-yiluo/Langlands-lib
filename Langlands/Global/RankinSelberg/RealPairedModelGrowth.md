# Ordinary normalized growth for smooth pairs of real model coefficients

`RealPairedUnequalModelGrowthStatement` and `RealPairedEqualModelGrowthStatement` apply normalized reflection to a genuine smooth pair whose two dense source families give coefficients in the specified ordinary real models. The ordinary exact continuation theorem is an explicit hypothesis. The conclusions use the given block factors and opposite Whittaker characters, with the rectangular dual integral at unequal rank and the Fourier row at equal rank.

On pure tensors, the ordinary and reflected paired kernels agree with their scalar counterparts. In unequal rank, absolute integrability on the product of the quotient and rectangle gives the iterated scalar dual integral by Fubini on a right half-plane. The exact block reflection theorem therefore applies to the dense pure sources. The weighted Mellin identity and density extend that equation with exponential strip growth to the entire joint smooth space.

The paired integrals and reflection follow the conventions of Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), Theorem 2.1, p. 6, and the discussion after Theorem 2.3, pp. 8–9. The dense extension uses the argument of §12.3, pp. 89–90. [Author-posted edition](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
