# The complex Jacquet recursions in the two Haar coordinates

This internal comparison identifies the full-unipotent recursion used in the equal-rank unfolding with the rectangle-coordinate quotient formula (7.8) of Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §7.2, printed pp. 47–50. One positive scalar relates the supplied unipotent Haar measures. It applies to both signs of the additive parameter.

For each pair of group variables the proof pulls back the rectangular Schwartz test by two invertible matrix multiplications. Inserting the long Weyl element puts the test in the form of the first, absolutely integrable kernel of the unipotent rectangle Fourier identity. Adding the simple-root and last-column phases identifies that kernel with the one in the Haar normalization. The final comparison moves the constant determinant factors through the quotient integral.
