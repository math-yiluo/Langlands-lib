# The quotient recursions span canonical complex Jacquet values

Fix the complex inducing parameters in rank \(r+2\), a scalar character
parameter \(a\), and compatible smaller-rank Haar and quotient measures. The
image of canonical Jacquet evaluation on the larger compact-finite standard
module lies in the complex linear span of `recursiveJacquetCoefficient`
with compact rectangular Schwartz tests supported on full-row-rank matrices.
These functions are the quotient recursions for \(-a\) on the rectangular
tests and canonical smaller Jacquet coefficients. The scalar measure
used in the Jacquet continuations is the self-dual measure for \(a\).

For a larger standard vector, `complexGodementFiniteProfiles` gives a finite
space of compact profiles containing the vector's profile. The space is
spanned at the fixed parameters by profiles of Godement sections formed from
a common family of compact full-row-rank tests and smaller profiles. Compact
restriction is injective on the standard module because the standard section
is recovered from its compact profile. Thus the chosen vector belongs to the
span of the corresponding Godement sections. Canonical evaluation is linear
and agrees on each moving section with its continued Godement generator.
The canonical smaller coefficient satisfies the continuity, covariance, and
simple-root bounds for the quotient recursion comparison, which identifies
that generator with the recursion times the upper-Borel sign. The sign is
absorbed in the linear span. Inclusion of the compact generators into all
rectangular Schwartz generators gives the original unrestricted span result.

This internal result formalizes the use of recursive generators for
compact-finite Jacquet coefficients in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg
integrals* (2009), §7.1–7.2 and the paragraph before §8.2 on printed p. 54.
