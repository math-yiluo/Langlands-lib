# Primal comparison at a strict lower parabolic cut

`RealPairedStrictPrimalStatement` works at a cut strictly below both ambient ranks and below the terminal comparison rank. The two representation spaces are complete. It assumes a smooth paired form that factors on pure tensors, joint continuity of the second Banach representation, opposite-character Whittaker covariance in the second factor, and first-factor lifts of a fixed lower coefficient for every compact rectangle profile, with the leading determinant half-power. It also assumes the paired rank transfer and entire terminal normalizations with right-half-plane absolute convergence. For every smooth vector of the second factor the ordinary lower unequal-rank integral for the reversed pair has an entire normalization by the terminal factor. Its value belongs to any complex subspace of functions containing the normalized terminal tests.

Factor the second coefficient restricted to the cut group into a finite sum weighted by compact rectangle profiles. For each such profile choose a first-factor lift. The resulting pure paired kernel at the lower cut equals the weighted lower ordinary kernel. Apply the rank transfer to each of these kernels. The finitely many terminal terms have a common right half-plane of absolute convergence and normalization agreement. On that half-plane the lower ordinary integral multiplied by the common positive comparison scalar equals the sum of the terminal integrals. The entire function obtained by dividing their normalized sum by this scalar gives the asserted normalization and subspace inclusion.

The construction implements the strict lower-rank case of the primal comparison, using the converse column factorization and the iterated rank transfer. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, Proposition 6.1 pp. 41–42, Proposition 14.3 and Lemma 14.1 pp. 104–107](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf#page=104).

`realPairedStrictPrimalAt` allows the cut in either slot, with the additive scale of the cut slot as the profile parameter and its negative as the uncut slot's character. The Banach representation in the uncut slot is jointly continuous. The two chosen vectors are inserted in their specified slots before applying the same rank transfer to the terminal tests.

`RealPairedStrictPrimalAtBanachStatement` and `RealPairedStrictPrimalBanachStatement` make the completeness hypotheses on the two representation spaces explicit.
