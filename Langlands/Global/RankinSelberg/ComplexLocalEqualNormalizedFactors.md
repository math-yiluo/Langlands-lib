# Exact local factors of the unfolded equal-rank products

This internal comparison records the factors and sign in the paired equal-rank products of Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §8.2, printed pp. 57–58.

The first block of the pair factors consists of products with the adjoining character. Shifting its exponent shifts the argument of that block by the same amount. The other block is the smaller pair with its two lists exchanged. The inverse adjoining character reverses the shift and gives the dual denominator.

The standard epsilon product depends only on the weights. Exchanging the two smaller character lists preserves their standard epsilon product, while negating the additive character contributes the product of their central signs with the respective rank powers. The extra sign of the dual unfolding cancels the resulting even power on the first smaller list. The remaining power is the target central sign of the second list.
