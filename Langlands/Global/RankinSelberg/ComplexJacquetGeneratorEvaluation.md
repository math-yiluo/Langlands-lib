# Canonical Jacquet evaluation of a compact Godement generator

The Jacquet evaluation of a compact, full-row-rank Godement section equals the continued partial Fourier formula at every inducing parameter. The test has a finite right orbit span for the unitary group, and the smaller input is the standard section of a fixed admissible compact profile.

The compact restrictions of the generator lie in a common finite-dimensional space. Expand the right translates of the test in a fixed basis of its orbit span. The corresponding coefficient functions of the unitary element are fixed as the exponents vary. The determinant factor is also fixed on the unitary group. These functions therefore span all compact restrictions of the generator. Their actual span consists of admissible profiles by linearity of the compact extension into the exact standard module at zero radial exponents.

Coordinates in a basis of this profile space are finite linear combinations of evaluations at unitary elements. The generator sections are holomorphic at each fixed group element, so all coordinates are holomorphic. The linear continued Jacquet family applied to these coordinates is entire at each group point. In the strict chamber it agrees with the open-cell integral and hence with the Godement formula. The complex analytic identity theorem extends the equality to all parameters.
