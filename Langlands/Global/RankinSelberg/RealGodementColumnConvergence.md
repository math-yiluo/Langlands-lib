# Absolute convergence of the real Godement column integral

For a real rectangular Schwartz test and a fixed lower Whittaker function with uniform right-derivative growth, the first-column kernel with any determinant parity is absolutely integrable for every complex determinant exponent. Its integral is an entire function of that exponent. The Library’s uniform real Whittaker model supplies the required derivative hypotheses.

In the integrand with the real Haar determinant density included, let `β` be the real part of the determinant exponent. The coefficient gauge estimate contributes a fixed exponential growth term in the absolute logarithmic Iwasawa coordinates and arbitrarily fast decay in the positive simple-root sum. The determinant logarithm is bounded in absolute value by the sum of the absolute coordinates. The real Iwasawa bound controls this sum by the positive simple-root sum and logarithms of the two Fourier argument norms. Choose the gauge decay large enough to absorb the resulting root term. Then choose both orders of partial Fourier decay large enough to absorb the remaining norm powers and leave any prescribed negative power in the matrix variable.

The resulting majorant is integrable against additive matrix Lebesgue measure. Undoing the determinant density gives the stated convergence against the supplied Haar measure. The existing differentiation-under-the-integral lemma applies on a right half-plane containing each chosen exponent. This is the absolute convergence and fixed-lower-coefficient holomorphy for equation (7.7) of Hervé Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, Section 7.2, Proposition 7.2.
