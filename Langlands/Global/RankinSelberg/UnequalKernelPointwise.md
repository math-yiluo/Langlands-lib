# Pointwise assembly of the unequal-rank kernels

This module supplies internal product calculations for the unequal-rank step of Cogdell's proof of Theorem 4.1 (2004, p. 219). `WhittakerProduct` records a product for one function on points integral at all finite places outside `S`. `WhittakerProduct.dual` applies it at the global dual argument, which preserves integrality at those places and projects to each local dual argument. This leaves its scalar unchanged.

The real equivariance lemmas use the published real-place covariance and character calculations. The elementary complex-place point and character calculations are adapted from `ComplexPlaceWhittakerEquivariance` in a private section so that the real and complex stable-operator developments, which declare colliding instance names, need not be co-imported. At a single infinite place the superdiagonal sum of the embedded matrix is supported at that place; the character-component hypothesis identifies its value. The inverse-character case follows by evaluating the inverse global character. Thus the local functions of the ordinary and dual formulas have the appropriate reciprocal transformations in the two ranks.

For both product identities, choose a representative of the class integral outside `S`, then evaluate the global and local kernels at that representative and its projected components. The pointwise product for each function gives its values on the padded or translated rectangular argument and on the smaller-rank argument. The determinant product gives the remaining weights, and regrouping the finite products proves the ordinary formula and the dual formula on the integral rectangular set. Off that set, one omitted finite place has a nonintegral entry. Sphericity and conductor zero give the vanishing of the first dual factor there, which proves the indicator formula everywhere. Neither identity assumes convergence of the global integral.
