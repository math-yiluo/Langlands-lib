# Subspaces stable under the commutant of the place actions at a complex place

Module `Langlands.Global.RankinSelberg.ComplexPlaceStableOperator`, namespace
`Langlands.Automorphic.OperatorRealization`. It is a step towards the bridge premise of the
conditional reduction `complexPlaceFunctionalEquationReduction` (complex-place Rankin–Selberg
functional equation). Provenance: internal. The proofs copy
the Library's proof that the free set at a complex place is stable under the operator algebra
(`OperatorRealization.complexFreeSet_operator` and the lemmas before it, module
`Langlands.Automorphic.PlaceComponentRealization`), with the free set replaced by an
arbitrary subspace `Z` satisfying the hypothesis below.

Let `S` be an operator realization of `GL_n` over a number field `F` and `w` a complex place.

## Definition

`IsComplexPlaceCommutantStable S w Z`, for a subspace `Z` of `S`, is the conjunction of:

1. every `ℂ`-linear endomorphism `P` of `S` that commutes with the compact action
   `complexPlaceCompactRepresentation w k` of every `k ∈ U(n)` and with the Lie action
   `complexPlaceLieAction w X` of every `X ∈ ℂ ⊗_ℝ 𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)` maps `Z` into `Z`;
2. `Z` is stable under the compact action at `w`;
3. `Z` is stable under the Lie action at `w`.

## Results

Assume `IsComplexPlaceCommutantStable S w Z`.

- `.finite`: `Z` is stable under the finite adelic action. The finite action commutes with the
  place actions at `w` (Library `finiteRepresentation_complexPlaceCompactRepresentation`,
  `finiteRepresentation_complexPlaceLieAction`), so clause 1 applies.
- `.realLie`: `Z` is stable under the Lie action at every real place, by clause 1 and the
  Library commutation lemmas `complexPlaceCompactRepresentation_realPlaceLieAction` and
  `realPlaceLieAction_comm_complexPlace`.
- `.complexLie`: `Z` is stable under the Lie action at every complex place `v`. For `v = w`
  this is clause 3. For `v ≠ w` it is clause 1 with the Library lemmas
  `complexPlaceCompactRepresentation_complexPlaceLieAction_of_ne` and
  `complexPlaceLieAction_comm_of_ne`.
- `.complexLieAction`: `Z` is stable under the action of the complexified Lie algebra of
  `GL_n(F ⊗ ℝ)`. By induction on tensors: `z ⊗ X = z • complexify X`, and the action of
  `complexify X` is a sum of place actions (Library `complexify_action_eq_sum_place`).
- `.enveloping`: `Z` is stable under the enveloping algebra, which is generated by the Lie
  algebra (Library `LieCasimir.enveloping_adjoin_range`; induction over `Algebra.adjoin`).
- `.compact`: `Z` is stable under the standard compact group `K_∞`. Write `k` as the image of
  `p` in the product of the place factors. Then `p` is the factor `p_w` at `w` times the
  element `p'` with `p_w` replaced by `1`. The element `p'` commutes with the compact factor
  at `w`, and conjugation by `p'_w = 1` fixes the Lie action at `w` (Library
  `compactRepresentation_complexPlaceLieAction`, `unitaryMatrixComplexLieConjugation_one'`).
  So clause 1 applies to the action of `p'`, and clause 2 to the action of `p_w`.
- `.operator`: `Z` is stable under `S.representation a` for every `a` of the concrete
  operator algebra `automorphicOperatorAlgebra`. Induction over the algebra generated by the
  finite, compact and enveloping operators, using the previous items.
- `.eq_bot_or_eq_top`: if `S` is irreducible (`S.IsIrreducible`), then `Z = ⊥` or `Z = ⊤`.
  `Z` with the stability of `.operator` is a subrepresentation, and the subrepresentations of
  an irreducible realization form a simple order.
