# The complex normalized pair with growth from its theta reflection

The complex pair factors specialize the rapid theta continuation to the normalized growth package used in the rank induction. The general theorem accepts a chart presentation with two positive determinant functions. A companion theorem applies to the ordinary and reflected unequal-rank quotient kernels themselves.

The epsilon formula expresses its inverse as a product of constant factors and powers of a positive real number. This gives exponential growth on strips for the inverse factor. For the exact quotient kernels, right-half-plane convergence gives the absolute moments and recovers measurability at the zero parameter. Fubini identifies the product-measure integral on the reflected side with the iterated integral in the local equation.
