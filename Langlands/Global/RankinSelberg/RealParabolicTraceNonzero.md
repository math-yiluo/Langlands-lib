# The nonzero scalar of the real compact parabolic trace

`realParabolicTraceNonzero` identifies the value of a nonzero continuous
Whittaker evaluation on compact pure parabolic sections with a fixed nonzero
scalar times the two supplied nonzero Levi evaluations and the transverse
Fourier moment. The three evaluations have the same specified Whittaker
character. `realParabolicTraceDetection` shows that some compact pure section
has nonzero evaluation for every nonzero ambient form.

For character lists, choose the two Levi inducing sections phased along
their longest Weyl orbits and a compact rectangle test phased by the
boundary character. The longest-cell geometry identifies the ambient
section on the full Weyl orbit. The Whittaker character of an
upper-unipotent matrix factors into the two block characters and the
boundary character. The full section thus has the same phase times a
nonnegative product, which is one at the Weyl element. The longest-cell
positivity criterion gives nonzero evaluation. The normalized position
characters identify the standard module with the smooth principal series,
and the bound by compact right derivatives applies to the continuous
functional under that identification.

The ascending quotient maps the character-series sections to the given
block lists and preserves the compact section construction. This proves
detection for the actual smooth block modules. The trace product formula
then forces its comparison scalar to be nonzero.

This gives the compact nonvanishing comparison underlying the split
restriction in Proposition 14.1, pp. 103–104 of the author-posted edition of
[H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009)](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf#page=103).
