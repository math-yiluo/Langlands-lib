# Common chambers for the finite normalized square tests

The normalized finite square products from Jacquet (2009), §8.2, initially agree with the corresponding unfolded integrals deep in two chambers. For approximation in the square Schwartz space, the comparison uses the convergence thresholds of the unfolded integrals, which depend on the fixed Whittaker coefficients.

In the primal chamber the proof holds the spectral variable far to the right and applies the identity theorem in the adjoining exponent. It then holds the adjoining exponent fixed and applies the identity theorem in the spectral variable. The absolute convergence estimate gives holomorphy along the two coordinate lines. For the Fourier chamber the same argument applies after negating the adjoining shift and reflecting the spectral variable, with the inverse of the finite product phase. The resulting pair retains the strip bounds of the original finite products.
