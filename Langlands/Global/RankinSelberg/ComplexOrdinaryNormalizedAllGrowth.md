# Entire complex ordinary normalizations and their strip growth

The proof supplies the ordinary complex continuation and strip-growth
inputs of the cuspidal pole construction. It applies in every equal
and unequal rank and at every nonzero trace-character scale.

The published unequal-rank argument proves normalized reflection
and growth by induction on the larger rank. The equal-rank
argument starts at the standard trace character and transports the
normalization to the actual character. In each case the ordinary
part of the normalized pair gives an entire function. On a common
right half-plane the reciprocal L-factor has nonzero denominator,
so this function is an `OrdinaryIntegralNormalization`.
Uniqueness of the entire continuation transfers the strip bound
to every ordinary normalization of the same test. The global
strip-growth input uses the compatible quotient measures selected
by the cuspidal pole construction.

The local result follows H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg
Integrals* (2009), Theorem 2.1(ii) on author pp. 6–7, the equal and
adjacent calculations in §8, and the larger-gap calculations in
§§9–10, author pp. 61–81.
