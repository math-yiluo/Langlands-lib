# Common Haar normalization of the equal-rank recursions

This internal comparison supplies the Haar normalization in the first change of integrals of Jacquet §8.2. The recursion for the larger Whittaker function integrates over the smaller unipotent group and the last column. The equal-rank unfolding uses the full larger unipotent group. The two compatible quotient measure packages can use independently scaled unipotent Haar measures.

The block Haar formula writes the larger measure as a positive multiple of the image of the smaller unipotent measure times the self-dual column measure. The upper rows of a joined unipotent matrix give the rectangle in the recursion, and its Whittaker character splits into the smaller character and the last column character. The given product integrability justifies iterating the block and column integrals. The positive scalar factors out of the remaining quotient integral and depends only on the measures.

The self-dual additive measure is the same for a character parameter and its negation. Thus the same scalar compares the full unipotent and rectangle recursions on the primal and dual sides.
