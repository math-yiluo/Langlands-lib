# Compact transverse tests as genuine smooth real standard vectors

`RealParabolicSmoothSectionMapStatement` sends a smooth compactly supported rectangle test to the corresponding genuine smooth vector in the real standard module for the two-block concatenation. The rank of the leading Levi factor is \(m+1\), the trailing rank is \(n\), and the upper-Borel block list places the trailing list before the leading list. The map is continuous and complex linear for the test-function topology and the topology of all word derivatives on the smooth standard module. Reconstructing the scalar function of a translated vector gives the right translate of `standardSection`.

The proof takes a smooth matrix cutoff supported in a compact subset of the invertible matrices and equal to one near the entire orthogonal subgroup. On each fixed transverse support, `RealParabolicLocalizedTests` gives a continuous map to ambient tests after multiplication by this cutoff. Each ambient right derivative agrees with the group right derivative near the orthogonal subgroup. Induction gives the agreement for each word. The ambient test differentiation operators and restriction to the orthogonal subgroup are continuous, so every compact-model derivative coordinate is continuous. The universal property of the test-function topology then gives continuity on all compactly supported transverse tests.

The transverse sections are those used in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, proof of Proposition 14.1, pp. 103–104 of the author-posted edition. The genuine smooth vectors here use the derivative topology of the real standard module in its compact Banach realization.
