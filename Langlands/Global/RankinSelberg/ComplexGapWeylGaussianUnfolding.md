# The alternate Weyl integral for Gaussian monomials

`ComplexGapWeylGaussianUnfoldingStatement` gives the alternate
quotient formula for every continued Gaussian monomial with the
Library’s Gaussian factor `exp(−∑ᵢⱼ |Aᵢⱼ|²)`. Its sheared chart
kernel is absolutely integrable under the two specified
right-half-plane inequalities. The equality retains the Weyl
determinant character, the Godement central sign and the chart
Haar constant.

The Weyl translation permutes the trailing exponents, leaving a
Gaussian monomial. Its partial Fourier test splits by columns.
Each frequency factor is Schwartz for the selected trace
character and self-dual measure. The finite column product is
Schwartz because splitting off the last column controls the
matrix norm by the two resulting norms. Reindexing that product
gives the joint frequency test for the exact sheared kernel.
The convergence theorem then supplies the premise of the
quotient substitution theorem.

This is the alternate expression from H. Jacquet, *Archimedean
Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §10.3, author p. 74, Lemma 10.3,
for the Library’s specified tests. The split occurs in §10.2,
author p. 72.
