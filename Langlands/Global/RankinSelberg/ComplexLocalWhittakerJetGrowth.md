# Uniform growth of the matrix jets of Whittaker coefficients

For an intertwiner and one inducing vector, this internal theorem supplies
one polynomial height exponent for all matrix derivatives at the identity
of the coefficient functions \(h\mapsto B(f)(gh)\). Each order has a
constant independent of \(g\).

The proof takes a finite-dimensional invariant subspace containing \(f\)
for the coordinate square operator. A basis of this subspace, together
with \(f\), gives a finite family on which the operator acts by constant
coefficients. The matrix-chart differential identity transfers that system
to the translates. After transport to Euclidean coordinates, the chart
operator has the same analytic coefficients and elliptic symbol for every
\(g\). The closed ball supplied by the interior estimate lifts to a
compact subset of the group.

The values and first derivatives of the finite family on that compact
set are bounded by a common power of the height of \(g\). The fixed
elliptic system estimate controls every higher derivative at the center
linearly in that bound. The coordinate change back to matrices multiplies
the estimate at each order by a fixed power of the norm of its linear map.
