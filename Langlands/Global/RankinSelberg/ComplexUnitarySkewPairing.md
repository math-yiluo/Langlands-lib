# Skew symmetry of the compact Lie derivatives in the unitary integral

The Hermitian pairing is the integral of the product of the conjugated first
function and the second function over the unitary subgroup. A skew-Hermitian
right derivative is skew symmetric on smooth functions whose derivatives are
continuous.

Conjugate transpose turns the left matrix direction `X k` into the negative
right matrix direction at `k⁻¹` on the unitary group. Apply the left
integration-by-parts lemma to the product of the matrix extensions, pulled
back by conjugate transpose. Haar invariance under inversion and the
product rule give the assertion.
