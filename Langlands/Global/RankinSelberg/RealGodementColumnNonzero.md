# Compact tests detect nonzero real lower coefficients

`RealGodementColumnNonzeroStatement` starts with a nonzero continuous function on the lower real general linear group. For each leading parity and complex exponent it gives a Schwartz test supported on a compact set of full-row-rank matrices whose leading Godement value at the upper long Weyl element is nonzero. The test can depend on the function, parity and exponent.

The proof uses the open embedding that joins a vector to an invertible square matrix as its first column. Every matrix in the image has a right inverse obtained by putting a zero row above the inverse square block. In these coordinates the product of the Fourier phase, lower coefficient and determinant weights is continuous. Choose a square block for which the lower coefficient is nonzero. Multiplying by the inverse of the resulting amplitude makes its real part positive on a neighborhood. A nonnegative smooth bump in this neighborhood is the test. The product Haar integral of the rotated amplitude against the bump has positive real part, by positivity on a nonempty open set. Compact support in the chart gives absolute integrability of the joint kernel. Fubini and the first-column Fourier formula identify the integral with the rotated leading value.
