# Right Lie derivatives of compact Godement tests

Differentiation along the real vector field `Y ↦ Y X` preserves the compact full-row-rank test class with finite unitary right orbit span.

The support can only shrink because a test vanishes on a neighborhood of every point outside its closed support. For compact finiteness, translating the derivative conjugates its matrix direction by the translating element. Expand that direction in a fixed real basis. Every translated derivative then belongs to the sum of the images of the original finite test orbit span under the basis derivatives. These maps are complex linear in the test, so the sum is finite dimensional. The identity pullback between the entrywise and operator norms preserves the pointwise test and transfers the conclusion to the norm used by the Godement section.
