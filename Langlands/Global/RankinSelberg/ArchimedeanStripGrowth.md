# Phragmén–Lindelöf for Gaussian regularization

An entire function with at most quadratic exponential growth on finite vertical strips is of strip exponential type if it has exponential bounds on all sufficiently far left and right vertical lines.

The proof widens the strip to two such lines. It multiplies the function by `exp(ε s²)` for positive `ε`. Completing the square bounds the damped function on both boundary lines. Quadratic exponential growth supplies the double exponential condition in the Phragmén–Lindelöf theorem. The estimate holds for every positive `ε`, and choosing `ε = (1 + |Im s|)⁻¹` for the point being estimated gives an exponent linear in the absolute height.
