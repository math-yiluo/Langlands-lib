# Iwasawa integration on the complex unipotent quotient

This internal comparison gives the full product integration formula in the continuous Iwasawa coordinates of `N \ GLₙ(ℂ)`. It is used for absolute convergence of the unfolded equal-rank integral in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §8.2, pp. 55–56. The compact factor has normalized unitary Haar measure, and the toral density is the one in the published toral marginal theorem for the supplied compatible quotient measure.

Right translation by a unitary matrix acts in the coordinates by right multiplication of the unitary coordinate. Integrating a nonnegative measurable function first over these right translates and then over the quotient preserves its quotient integral. Tonelli changes the order, and left Haar invariance on the unitary group makes the compact average a function of the toral coordinate alone. The published marginal formula then gives the exponential density on the logarithmic torus.
