# The leading Haar measure in the normalized gap chart

The leading matrix separates as one Haar factor of the normalized outer measure. The remaining factors are the weighted log-diagonal measure, compact Haar measure, and the measure of the first rectangular column. The retained matrix depends on these coordinates and the upper unipotent tail, independently of the leading matrix.

The proof moves the normalization density into the log-diagonal factor using the product measure identities for a measurable density. Two product associators then separate the leading variable and preserve the resulting measure. A separate product permutation allows the full complementary rectangle to be integrated first. These measure identities implement the independent leading integration used in Jacquet's Section 10.4.

The retained measure is sigma finite. Interchanging the first two factors of a right-associated product also preserves the corresponding product measure.
