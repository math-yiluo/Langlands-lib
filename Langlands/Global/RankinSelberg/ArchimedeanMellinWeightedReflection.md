# Weighted reflection by Mellin inversion and a contour shift

Assume that the two normalized Mellin integrals agree with their corresponding convergent integrals on a common right vertical line. The normalized functional equation and the specified transform factors give an equality of determinant-weighted integrals with positive scaling, as long as the entire vertical integrand has strip decay.

The proof evaluates the inverse Mellin weights on the two lines of absolute convergence. Fubini applies because on such a line the norm of a positive determinant to a complex power depends only on the real part. After reflection, the normalized functional equation identifies the two vertical integrands. The factor outside the reflected theta integral accounts for the difference between the exponents `s` and `s - 1`. The rectangle contour theorem then shifts between the two lines.
