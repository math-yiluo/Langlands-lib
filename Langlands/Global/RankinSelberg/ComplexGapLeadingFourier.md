# Fourier exchange for the leading rectangle

`complexGapLeadingFourier` identifies the positive trace transform of the leading restriction of a rectangular Schwartz test. The restriction evaluates the test after an invertible left matrix change on a matrix with complementary rows zero. The transformed test is integrated over all complementary rows at the inverse-transpose matrix change, with the inverse normalized determinant raised to the number of columns. Each complementary fiber is absolutely integrable by `integrable_rectTransform_complement`.

The proof flattens the matrix into its entry coordinates and reindexes the leading and complementary rows with their self-dual product measures. Partial Fourier inversion exchanges restriction to zero with integration in the corresponding dual row variables. The arbitrary left multiplication changes the rectangular Haar measure by the determinant power and moves to inverse transpose in the Fourier pairing. The leading restriction is Schwartz because row extension by zero is an injective linear map in finite dimension. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, Section 10.3, passage from (10.9) to (10.10)](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
