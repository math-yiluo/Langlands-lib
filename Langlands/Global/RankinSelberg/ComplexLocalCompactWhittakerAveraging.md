# Whittaker compatibility of compact projection

This internal comparison proves the compact averaging identity used in Jacquet §8.2 from the analytic regularity of smooth standard Lie intertwiners and connectedness of the invertible matrix domain. The inputs are the actual complex standard module and Whittaker intertwiner, and a family of projected inducing vectors with the prescribed compact restriction.

At the identity, the right translates of each inducing vector by compact elements span a finite-dimensional space. Integrating its orbit in finite coordinates lets the Whittaker map pass through the average. Injectivity of restriction identifies that average with the projected inducing vector.

For each fixed Whittaker evaluation point, one continuous compact weight expresses the evaluated projection for every inducing vector and every right translate. The difference between this scalar pairing and the averaged Whittaker coefficient is a linear map from the inducing space to smooth functions. Both averages commute with right differentiation. Their equality at the identity gives a zero jet for every vector, and the supplied analyticity and connectedness propagate the equality throughout the group.
