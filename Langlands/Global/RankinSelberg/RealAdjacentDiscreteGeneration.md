# A normalized real \(GL_2\times GL_1\) test for the discrete block

A real induced block of nonzero weight paired with an arbitrary real
character has a single ordinary scale-one test whose normalized value
is one on the entire plane. The rank-one quotient data are arbitrary
compatible data.

Take the discrete-block diagonal coefficient and the rank-one determinant
character. The smaller quotient identifies with the real units, and
its measure is a positive multiple of multiplicative Haar. The
coefficient vanishes at the negative units. On the positive units the
quotient integral is the Euler Gamma integral after collecting the
three determinant powers and the factor from multiplicative Haar.
The resulting Gamma argument is the spectral variable plus the two
exponents and half the absolute discrete weight. This is the argument
of the prescribed pair Gamma factor. Scaling the larger vector absorbs
the constant from the coefficient, the measure and the complex Gamma
normalization.

This is the compact-finite discrete-series by character case of the
generation calculation in H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg
Integrals* (2009), §12.4, pp. 90–91, and Theorem 2.7(i), p. 10.
