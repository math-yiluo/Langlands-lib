# Equal-rank finite products with a varying adjoining exponent

This internal result supplies the chamber form of the finite product identity in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §8.2, printed pp. 55–57. It starts with a fixed compact projection and finite coefficient expansion, together with matrix factors that agree with the matrix integrals on a right half-plane.

The radial shift is trivial on the unitary determinant, so the compact projection works for every shifted character. The absolute convergence theorem for the double kernel supplies independent thresholds on the adjoining exponent and the spectral real part. For the compact insertion and the matrix factors, the shift moves only the spectral argument of the matrix weight. The quotient factors retain their original spectral parameter, and ordinary corank-one convergence supplies a common bound for the finitely many terms. These estimates justify the fixed compact expansion on the entire right chamber.
