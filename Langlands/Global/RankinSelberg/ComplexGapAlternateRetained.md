# Logarithmic coordinates and endpoint estimates in the alternate gap chart

The retained matrix is the product of a unitary matrix, a diagonal matrix with the logarithmic tail, and an upper unipotent matrix with one rectangular column. Conjugating the inverse of the latter by the leading group inclusion keeps it upper unipotent. The logarithmic Iwasawa coordinate of the translated Whittaker argument is therefore the concatenation of the leading coordinate and the negative logarithmic tail.

The height is bounded using its submultiplicativity and the logarithmic diagonal height estimate. The entries of the rectangle and its inverse have absolute value at most one plus the norm of the retained rectangle. Removing the unitary factor from the first tail column exposes both that rectangle and the first diagonal entry. In the last inverse row the upper unipotent has no effect. Removing the unitary factor from that row exposes the inverse of the last diagonal entry.
