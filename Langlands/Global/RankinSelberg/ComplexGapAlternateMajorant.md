# Pointwise bounds for the complex alternate chart kernel

`complexGapAlternateMajorant` bounds the split alternate kernel with
the actual smaller Whittaker vectors. The leading test and the right
translation of the second Whittaker vector can use any maps that
preserve or lower matrix height. This includes inverse transpose.
An exponential in the absolute logarithmic tail coordinates can bound
the remaining tail weights.

The leading Schwartz bound separates the leading matrix and the lower
rectangle at polynomial cost in the tail height. The alternate height
estimate absorbs this cost into the two observed column norms and the
logarithmic exponential. The first Whittaker vector is controlled by
its simple-root estimate in the consecutive-block coordinates, and
the second by its translation estimate. The determinant norm of the
quotient representative is an exponential in its logarithms. The
result is a product of an integrable weighted leading matrix estimate,
a rapidly decreasing function of the lower rectangle, and the
alternate tail envelope.

This is a derived pointwise step for H. Jacquet, *Archimedean
Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), in the chart described in §10.2,
author pp. 72–73, and the estimates of §10.4, Lemma 10.7,
pp. 79–81.
