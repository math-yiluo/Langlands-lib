# Uniqueness of entire ordinary normalizations

If two entire normalized values have the same product with a common factor on right half-planes and the factor is eventually nonzero there, their values agree globally. The proof intersects the three half-planes, cancels the factor, and applies the analytic identity theorem.

`ordinaryNormalizationGrowthFromExistence` applies this observation to the ordinary local test families. Assuming the reciprocal entire denominator is nonzero on a right half-plane, one entire normalization with exponential strip growth for each test gives both ordinary continuation and the growth bound for every entire normalization. The continuation uses the given normalization and the bound transfers by equality of the entire values.

This is an internal derivation from the ordinary normalization interface. It relates constructions of one controlled normalization, such as the one from a local pair identity, to the universal strip-growth premise of the cuspidal pair reduction.
