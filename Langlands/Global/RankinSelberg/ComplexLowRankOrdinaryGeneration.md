# Complex ordinary normalized generation with smaller rank one

`complexLowRankOrdinaryGeneration` proves the full generation conclusions for the adjacent pair `GL₂(ℂ) × GL₁(ℂ)` and the equal pair `GL₁(ℂ) × GL₁(ℂ)`. For the adjacent pair the larger standard module is assumed irreducible. Coverage holds at all complex spectral points and all nonzero trace scales, for every compatible quotient measure. The finite sum at either standard trace scale uses one ordinary test. The equal-rank test works at every trace scale.

At the standard trace character the split adjacent formula uses an angular Tate Gaussian in each column. The Fourier transform of the second Gaussian reverses its angular weight and contributes a nonzero constant. The resulting rectangular test has finite unitary translate span. Transposition reduces this fact to finite left translates of a polynomial Gaussian. The continued Godement model comparison puts its larger coefficient in the exact Whittaker model. The product calculation then gives the full pair factor times a nonzero constant on a right half-plane. Rescaling that coefficient gives the entire normalization identically one. The trace-character transport gives the other scales. For the equal pair the rank-one Gaussian generation theorem supplies the single test directly.

This specializes the generation conclusions of H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), Theorems 2.6 and 2.7, author pp. 9–10. The product argument uses equation (8.3), author pp. 58–60, and the scalar Tate calculation of Appendix §16.1, author pp. 109–110.
