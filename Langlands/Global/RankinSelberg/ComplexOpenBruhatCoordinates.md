# Upper-unipotent coordinates in the open complex Gauss chart

The long Weyl element takes every upper-unipotent matrix, after the published reflected inverse transpose, to a Gauss representative. More explicitly, `openCoordinates` applies the reflected inverse transpose and then reads the upper entries. The corresponding Gauss representative is the long Weyl matrix times the original unipotent element.

`ComplexOpenBruhatCoordinatesStatement` also verifies smooth group multiplication, inversion and the standard Whittaker character in these coordinates. The reflected inverse transpose is a group automorphism. Multiplication of upper entries is polynomial, and the inverse is smooth by the invertible-matrix inversion theorem. The Whittaker character is the exponential of the trace of a sum of matrix entries.

The long Gauss domain is the actual open Bruhat cell. Right translation of an extended chart test agrees with extension of the translated test, by exact Borel covariance in the chart and the stability of its complement under right upper-unipotent multiplication.
