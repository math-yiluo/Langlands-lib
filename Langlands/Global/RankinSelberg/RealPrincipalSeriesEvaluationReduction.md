# Extending real Whittaker maps from their identity evaluations

`RealUniformPrincipalSeriesEvaluationReductionStatement` reduces extension
of the original real uniform Whittaker coefficient maps to an extension
of their scalar evaluations at the identity. Its premise is
`RealUniformPrincipalSeriesEvaluationExtensionStatement`. For a real
block list of the indicated rank with a nonzero induced weight, this
premise supplies a form on the smooth expanded position principal
series. The form has a finite compact derivative bound, transforms by
the Whittaker character under right unipotent translation, and agrees
with the original scalar evaluation on the included K-finite source.

The bounded form gives a uniform coefficient map on the expanded
K-finite source by evaluation on right translates. Restriction along
the original source inclusion preserves its Whittaker intertwiner
properties. Translation by the identity fixes the source vector, so
the restricted map has the specified evaluations. The identity
evaluation uniqueness theorem makes it equal to the original map.

This reduction leaves the bounded, equivariant extension of the
scalar evaluations as a premise. The original and expanded
position characters agree, so the smooth principal series in the
premise is the space for the original position characters as well.
Jacquet uses smooth Whittaker forms and exactness in the
principal-series comparison. Applying exactness still requires
bounded realization of the given evaluations on the original
smooth model.
