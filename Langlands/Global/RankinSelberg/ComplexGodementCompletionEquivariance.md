# Equivariance of the cofactor completion

For any rectangular complex matrix, the cofactor completion retains its rows and supplies the missing top row. Right multiplication by a unitary matrix commutes with completion after multiplying the new top row by the reciprocal unitary determinant. In particular the determinant of the completion is invariant under this right action. Left multiplication of the rectangular matrix scales the new top row by the conjugate of the left determinant.

The top row of the completion is the conjugate of the first adjugate column of the square matrix padded by a zero top row. Right multiplication commutes with zero padding. Multiplicativity of the adjugate and the formula for the adjugate of a unitary matrix give the transformation law for the top row. The retained rows transform by matrix multiplication. The left formula follows by factoring the left determinant out of each maximal minor.

These algebraic identities are used in the compactly supported Godement test construction related to Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §7.1, Proposition 7.1(v)–(vi), pp. 44–47.
