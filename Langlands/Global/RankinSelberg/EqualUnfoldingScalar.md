# Scalar points and the rational parabolic in the equal-rank unfolding

This is an internal helper module for `EqualUnfoldingReduction.md`. Provenance: internal. It
provides the center `Z_n(𝔸)` of `GL_n(𝔸)`, `n = r + 1`, as scalar matrices parametrized by
`GL_1(𝔸) = 𝔸^×`, together with the rational parabolic `P'_n(F) = Z_n(F) P_n(F)` that indexes
the Library's Eisenstein series. These are the objects Cogdell, *Analytic theory of
L-functions for GL_n* (2004), §2.3, printed p.210, uses when he unfolds the Eisenstein series
and integrates out the center. Namespace: `Langlands.Global.RankinSelbergGlobal.EqualUnfolding`.

## Ideles of `GL_1` (`Idele`)

- `ideleOfHom`: `z ↦ det z`, from `GL_1(𝔸)` to the idele group, as a monoid homomorphism. Its
  values are the Library's `ideleOf` (`ideleOfHom_apply`, by `rfl`). Consequences:
  `ideleOf_mul`, `ideleOf_inv`, `ideleOf_one`, `continuous_ideleOf`, `ideleOf_injective` and
  `exists_ideleOf_eq` (surjectivity).
- `detNorm_one_eq`: `detNorm F 1 z` is the idele norm of `ideleOf F z`.
- `ideleOf_rationalPoint`: the idele of a rational point of `GL_1` is principal. Hence
  `centralIdeleCharacter_ideleOf_rational`: an idele class character, through the Library's
  `Automorphic.centralIdeleCharacter`, is trivial on `GL_1(F)`.

## Scalar points (`ScalarPoint`)

- `scalarPoint F r z`: the scalar matrix `diag(a, …, a) ∈ GL_n(𝔸)` with `a = ideleOf F z`, as a
  monoid homomorphism `GL_1(𝔸) →* GL_n(𝔸)`. `adelicEquiv_scalarPoint` gives its matrix.
- `continuous_scalarPoint`, `scalarPoint_commute`, `scalarPoint_mem_center` and
  `scalarPoint_injective` hold. `exists_scalarPoint_eq` says every central point of `GL_n(𝔸)`
  is a scalar point.
- `lastRow_scalarPoint_mul`: `e_n (a g) = a · e_n g`.
- `detNorm_scalarPoint`: `|det diag(a, …, a)| = |a|^n`. `detPower_scalarPoint`: the
  corresponding identity for `|det|^s`, as `|a|^{n s}`.
- `scalarPoint_rationalPoint`: the scalar point of a rational point is the rational scalar
  matrix. `det_scalar_one`: the scalar `1 × 1` matrix `a` has determinant `a`.

## The rational parabolic (`Parabolic`)

- `eisensteinRationalImage F r`: the image in `GL_n(𝔸)` of the Library's
  `eisensteinParabolic F r` (the subgroup generated by the mirabolic subgroup and the center of
  `GL_n(F)`), that is `P'_n(F)`. It lies in `GL_n(F)` and contains `P_n(F)` and the rational
  scalar points (`eisensteinRationalImage_le`, `rationalPoint_mem_eisensteinRationalImage`,
  `mirabolicRationalImage_le_eisensteinRationalImage`, `scalarPoint_mem_eisensteinRationalImage`).
- `eq_one_of_scalarPoint_mem_mirabolic`: a rational scalar point in `P_n(F)` is `1`, since its
  last row is `e_n`.
- `exists_scalarPoint_mul_inv`: every `l ∈ P'_n(F)` is `diag(b, …, b) p⁻¹` with
  `b ∈ GL_1(F)` and `p ∈ P_n(F)`. The proof uses `Subgroup.mul_normal`, since the center is
  normal.
- `scalarPoint_mul_inv_injective`: this decomposition is unique, by
  `eq_one_of_scalarPoint_mem_mirabolic` and injectivity of `scalarPoint`.

Together these give `P'_n(F) ≅ GL_1(F) × P_n(F)`, the bijection used in the counting identity
of `EqualUnfoldingCount.md`.
