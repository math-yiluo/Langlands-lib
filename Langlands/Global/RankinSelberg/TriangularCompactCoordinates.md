# Diagonal coordinates in the triangular compact intersection

This internal helper proves the diagonal-coordinate facts needed for Iwasawa
descent. For an upper triangular unitary matrix over a real or complex field,
the triangular inverse has diagonal entries inverse to the original diagonal.
The unitary identity also identifies that inverse with the conjugate transpose.
Taking norms gives the square of each diagonal norm equal to one, so each
diagonal entry has norm one.

Apply this calculation at each real and complex place of a triangular matrix
in the actual standard adelic compact subgroup. Its diagonal ideles therefore
have archimedean coordinates of norm one. At the finite places both their
values and inverse values are integral, directly from the definition of the
standard integral subgroup. These conclusions concern the actual diagonal
unit map and the unit projections used by the simple-root row.
