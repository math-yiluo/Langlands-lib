# Central polynomial weights with small displacement moment

The sum of the squared entry displacements of a unitary matrix from
identity is a polynomial in its entries and their conjugates. It is
nonnegative and vanishes exactly at identity. Its trace expression
makes it invariant under unitary conjugation. After a fixed rescaling
and subtraction from one, this gives a nonnegative central polynomial
with maximum one attained exactly at identity.

On the complement of any open identity neighborhood the polynomial has
maximum strictly below one by compactness. Apply the peak integral lemma
to the continuous function equal to one minus the operator-norm
displacement divided by the prescribed tolerance. For some power of the
central polynomial its integral against that function is positive.
Dividing this power by its positive Haar integral gives a weight of
unit mass. The positive integral inequality then bounds the first
operator-norm displacement moment by the prescribed tolerance.
