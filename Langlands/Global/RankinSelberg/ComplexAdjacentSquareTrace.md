# Ordinary adjacent normalization through square convolution

`complexAdjacentSquareTrace` gives the entire normalization of an adjacent split Godement test at adjoining shift zero. It assumes a polynomial square convolution trace in the given Whittaker realization and a linear functional \(A\) that gives the lower equal-rank normalizations. The adjacent normalization applies \(A\) to the square convolution vector at the same spectral parameter and multiplies by the character value \(d((-1)^{r+1})\) from `complexAdjacentSplitIntegrals`. The lower test uses the split Fourier transform of the last column. The statement allows any nonzero trace scale and compatible quotient datum with s-finite quotient measure.

Jacquet's adjacent unfolding has an absolutely convergent product kernel on a right half-plane. Fubini puts the square integral inside the quotient integral. Substitution of the normalized square trace leaves the lower equal-rank integral of the polynomial output vector. Its finitely many coefficient vectors converge and satisfy their normalization identities on a common right half-plane. The two reciprocal factors multiply to the reciprocal factor of the full pair. The finite polynomial expansion also makes the normalized expression entire. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §8.3, equation (8.3), pp. 58–60](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf#page=58)
