# Ordinary equal-rank families of continued complex Godement tests

For a fixed smaller Whittaker vector and a Schwartz matrix test, the continued Godement coefficient is entire in the adjoining exponent at each group argument. If the larger Whittaker law holds on a right half-plane of adjoining exponents, the identity principle gives the law at every adjoining exponent.

The continued coefficient is continuous in the group variable and has uniform simple-root bounds over each bounded adjoining-parameter disk. Apply the ordinary equal-rank family theorem with these bounds and the fixed opposite Whittaker vector. This gives absolute convergence and joint holomorphy on a common right half-plane of spectral parameters over the disk.

This is a derived family step for continuing the polynomial trace in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), Proposition 8.1, author-posted pp. 60–61.
