# The scaled square comparison on the leading group

A finite leading Schwartz test and a fixed Whittaker evaluation point give the Gaussian-weighted square reflection at every positive determinant scale. The reflected integrand is written in the original leading variable, with the test and determinant evaluated at its inverse transpose.

The proof isolates the Mellin power in each square kernel and applies the weighted reflection theorem to the normalized square integrals and their strip growth. Inverse transpose preserves the leading Haar measure and is an involution. This gives the stated mixed-side integrand and the scalar equal to the positive scale. The identity is used for the square comparison between Jacquet's equations (10.10) and (10.9).
