# Complex equal-rank tests for \(\mathrm{GL}_1\times\mathrm{GL}_1\)

The proof uses the complex Tate proposition. Choose actual rank-one vectors nonzero at the adelic identity and restrict their Whittaker coefficients to the given place. The complex-place Whittaker bridge puts these restrictions in their parameter's local model. In rank one each restriction is a scalar multiple of the determinant character. The nonzero identity values make both scalars nonzero.

The test is the complex angular Gaussian with the weight of the product character, transported to the one-dimensional row space. On a right half-plane the ordinary integral is the product of nonzero constants and the complex Gamma factor. The local rank-one functional equation for these character functions supplies holomorphy for both integrals and transfers the nonvanishing to the dual integral of the same test. The archimedean quotient construction at rank one gives the compatible measure used in the existence theorem.
