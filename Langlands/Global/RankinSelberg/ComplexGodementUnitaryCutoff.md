# Invariant smooth cutoffs for complex Godement tests

Any open neighborhood of the trailing rows of unitary matrices which is invariant under right unitary multiplication contains the support of a compactly supported Schwartz cutoff. The cutoff is right unitary invariant, equals one on those trailing rows, and takes real values between zero and one.

The trailing rows form a compact set. Choose a smooth real cutoff supported in a compact neighborhood inside the supplied open set, then average it over right multiplication by the unitary group with Haar probability measure. The inverse right multiplications are uniformly bounded by compactness, so the compact Schwartz averaging theorem applies. The support of the average lies in the compact saturation of the original support. Haar invariance gives the right translation law, and every point in the orbit of a unitary trailing row still has cutoff value one.

This supplies invariant cutoffs for the Godement spanning construction related to Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §7.1, Proposition 7.1(v), pp. 44–46.
