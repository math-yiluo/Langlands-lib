# Leading and tail columns of the lower rectangle in the complex gap kernel

The matrix multiplying the tail chart on the right has the leading square block and the given lower rectangle in its first columns. Its other columns are the corresponding columns of the identity. In the split ordinary gap kernel the first columns therefore become the left-multiplied stack used in the leading Schwartz bound. The trailing test columns are unchanged.

The proof identifies the embedded block product with the reindexed lower block matrix. It computes both sets of columns in that index and then multiplies on the left by the chart matrix.
