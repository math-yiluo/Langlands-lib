# Right Lie differentiation of a compact Godement section

The right Lie derivative of the standard Godement section is the section formed with the differentiated test plus the original section multiplied by the derivative of its determinant character. The scalar is evaluated at the identity along the real matrix exponential.

On a compact neighborhood of the group argument the ordinary test and its derivative have a common compact support in the integration variable. The compact linear-parameter integration theorem differentiates the restricted integral with respect to the ambient group matrix. The tangent of the right exponential is `g X`, and the trailing-row map commutes with this right multiplication. Evaluation of the integral of linear maps on the tangent gives the integral with the right test derivative. The determinant character is multiplicative and differentiable on the scalar curve, which gives the other term by the product rule.
