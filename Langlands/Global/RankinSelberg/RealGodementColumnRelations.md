# Continuation of relations among holomorphic sections

`RealGodementColumnRelationsStatement` treats two finite families of entire columns in a fixed finite-dimensional complex space, with one entire scalar value for each column. Suppose that equal linear combinations of the two families give equal combinations of the scalar values throughout a neighborhood. Then the implication holds at every parameter where the square family is a basis.

The proof multiplies the second combination by the determinant of the square matrix and matches it to a combination of the first family using the adjugate matrix. This yields an identity of two entire scalar functions. The complex analytic identity theorem extends their equality to every parameter. At a parameter where the determinant is nonzero, the adjugate relation and scalar cancellation recover the implication.

This internal analytic lemma is for comparing choices of a basis of Godement sections when continuing their scalar Jacquet values. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §7.1, Proposition 7.1(v), and §7.2, pp. 46–48, use compact-type generation by Godement sections and continuation of their values from the dominant chamber. The lemma assumes entire matrix entries and scalar values and proves descent of relations under those hypotheses.
