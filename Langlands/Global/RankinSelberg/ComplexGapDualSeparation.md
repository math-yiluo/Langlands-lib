# Separating the dual Godement integral

The weighted joint integral on the full dual chart equals the integral of a separated kernel. The separated kernel is jointly integrable and contains the smaller-rank dual quotient kernel, the square Fourier factor at the inverse transpose of the leading matrix, and the retained tail coefficient and Weyl character.

The proof translates the quotient on the right by the inverse transpose of the leading matrix and moves the full complementary rectangle to the innermost integral. Both changes preserve the product measure. Evaluating at a quotient representative cancels the leading determinant from the first radial argument. The rectangle integration gives the positive square transform of the leading restriction. The remaining Whittaker arguments are those of the smaller dual kernel for the corresponding right translates. This implements the passage from the full dual chart to the separated factors in Jacquet's Section 10.4.
