# Continuous Schwartz row evaluations for exact real equal-rank tests

`realOrdinaryEqualRowEvaluation` assumes exact ordinary continuation in the real coefficient models and fixes two exact equal-rank Whittaker coefficients at opposite scales. Their whole-model range identities give compact-finite vectors whose pure tensor realizes them in the genuine pair. The paired kernel on that tensor agrees with the exact equal-rank kernel for every Schwartz row. Entire normalization on the exact tests thus supplies the required normalizations for the fixed paired vector. The continuous normalized row evaluation theorem gives a linear family agreeing with every entire normalization and continuous at each spectral point. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §12.3 and §15](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
