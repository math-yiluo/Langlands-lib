# Square-test bounds in the equal-rank convergence chamber

This internal result concerns the double integral of Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §8.2, printed pp. 54–57, with an arbitrary square Schwartz test. The spectral and adjoining real parts determine the absolute kernel. At a fixed spectral real part, the determinant weight for a compact interval in the adjoining real part is bounded by the sum of the two endpoint weights. Their continuous bounds in the Schwartz test give one bound for the entire interval.

Along an affine line in the two exponents, the kernel factors into its value at the origin of the line and one complex exponential of a real logarithmic determinant weight. Fubini identifies the product integral with the iterated unfolded integral. Differentiation of the exponential integral on its absolute-convergence domain gives the trace holomorphy used to compare it with the finite-product formulas.
