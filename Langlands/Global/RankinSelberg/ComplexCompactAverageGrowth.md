# Polynomial sampling bounds for complex normalized compact averages

For a positive contraction parameter ε at most one, the diagonal entries
are ε^i and their reciprocals are bounded by ε^(-n) in rank n. Entries of
unitary matrices and their inverses have norm at most one. Applying these
bounds to the products and inverse products in Jacquet's matrix height
gives a polynomial bound in ε^(-1), uniform in the compact variable.

The norm of the inducing character on the contracting diagonal is exactly
ε to the sum of the radial character exponents weighted by their diagonal
indices. The chart constant and the fixed opposite-cell mass contribute a
constant. The reciprocal Jacobian and character contribute another power
of ε^(-1), bounded by an integer power because ε^(-1) is at least one.

For a fixed sample section, the exact intertwiner has polynomial growth
on the group. The normalized Whittaker averaging integral uses only the
values of this fixed vector at the compact translates of the contracting
diagonal. The bound on the sample, the norm of the normalization, and
compact Haar mass one give a polynomial bound times any uniform compact
bound on the input vector.
