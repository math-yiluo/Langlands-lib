# Group continuity and differentiation of the Godement continuation

The module proves continuity in the larger group argument of the continued Godement value. It also differentiates the outer integral along a real curve of larger group elements whose right action on test matrices has a fixed linear vector field. In the derivative formula, the original Schwartz test is replaced by its derivative along that vector field before taking the right translate.

For the column integral, the local bound on right multiplication and its inverse controls the translated Schwartz weight seminorms on a neighborhood of a group element. The norm of a last column is bounded by the entrywise norm of the rectangular matrix containing it. This gives a uniform integrable polynomial tail in the column variable. A linear vector field applied to the Fréchet derivative preserves the Schwartz class by the bilinear Schwartz map for a function of temperate growth. This allows differentiation through the column integral under the same type of bound. The uniform translated-kernel estimate supplies the analogous domination to pass continuity and differentiation through the outer Haar integral.
