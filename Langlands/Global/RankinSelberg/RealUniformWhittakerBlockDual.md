# Weyl inverse transpose on real block Whittaker models

`realStandardBlockTransposeEquiv` identifies the compact-finite standard module of the dual blocks with the inverse-transpose twist of the original irreducible standard module. The inverse-transpose invariant bilinear pairing gives a map into the compact-finite dual. Its kernel and image are stable under the compact and Lie actions, with the Lie action on the image composed with the differential of inverse transpose. Irreducibility of the source and its finite dual makes this map bijective. Composing its inverse with the standard pairing for the dual blocks gives the equivalence.

`realBlockWhittakerDual` transports irreducibility across the equivalence and uses it on the coefficient maps. Pullback along the long Weyl element times inverse transpose changes the sign of the unipotent character. The existing equality of the exact and uniform models applies at the ranks of both lists. The involutions on the functions and on the inducing blocks give the converse membership implication. This is the compact-finite version of the dual Whittaker convention used in the ordinary real integrals. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §2, pp. 5–6](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
