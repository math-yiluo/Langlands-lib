# The normal derivative of a complex Bruhat stabilizer

The stabilizing family defines a smooth field in the product of the tangential and normal coordinates. At the zero normal coordinate the field and the inducing scalar vanish, and the derivative on the normal quotient is cube-zero. The family retains its exact Whittaker frequency.

A fixed upper direction differentiates a right Borel path, which stays in the same Bruhat cell. Therefore its coordinate field is tangent along the cell, and its derivative sends tangent vectors to tangent vectors. In a normal variation the stabilizing direction is fixed because the family depends only on the tangent coordinates. The normal derivative of the varying field thus descends from the cube-zero derivative of the fixed field at that point. The linear projection intertwines the two derivatives, which transfers the cube-zero identity to the normal coordinates.
