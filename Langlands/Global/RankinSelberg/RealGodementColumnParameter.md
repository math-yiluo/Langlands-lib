# Entire real Godement column values with exponential test families

For a fixed lower Whittaker coefficient with the regularity and common derivative growth used in the real column convergence theorem, multiply a compact rectangular Schwartz test by `exp(z b(x))` for a smooth complex multiplier `b`. The first-column Godement value at a fixed upper group element remains entire in the leading exponent `z`, which also governs the multiplier.

The first-column integral and the Haar integral are each linear in the test by absolute integrability. A common Schwartz seminorm bounds the translated column forms locally in the exponent. The difference quotient splits into the derivative holding the test fixed and the evaluation of the continuous Schwartz-valued test quotient. Its varying-test error tends to zero by the common seminorm. The outer determinant factor is an entire scalar exponential times its value at zero.

This is a derived parameter-dependent step for the real version of Jacquet's column formula (7.7), *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §7.2, pp. 48–49.
