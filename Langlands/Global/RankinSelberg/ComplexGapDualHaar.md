# Joint convergence for the full dual Godement kernel

For a rectangular Schwartz test split into a leading block and individual trailing columns, the dual Godement kernel is jointly integrable with two independent rapid determinant weights. The integration includes the full complementary rectangle, the smaller quotient, and the normalized dual Haar chart. The two weights have arguments given by the quotient determinant divided by the dual chart determinant and by the dual chart determinant itself.

The proof identifies the partial transform of the transformed split test and applies the coupled Haar estimate at the opposite character. Reindexing the complementary rectangle identifies its insertion matrix with the lower shear. The retained matrix, the tail reversal, and the lower shear have normalized absolute determinant one, so the chart determinant comes from the inverse transpose of the leading factor. The chart pushes its measure to Haar up to a positive scalar. Pulling back the integrable ambient kernel proves the joint convergence on the chart used before the complementary rectangle integration in Jacquet's Lemma 10.6.

The module also records sigma finiteness of the dual chart measure and the equality of the lower rectangle insertion with its consecutively indexed shear.

`complexGapDualHaarPowerBounds` transports the two independently chosen power bounds from the coupled Haar estimate through the same dual chart.
