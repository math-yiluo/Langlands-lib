# Stability under the real-place actions and their commutant

This internal helper for the real-place Rankin–Selberg model bridge is in namespace
`Langlands.Automorphic.OperatorRealization`. It adapts the complex-place
commutant argument to a real place, using the real-place factorization proofs in the
Library's `Langlands.Automorphic.PlaceComponentRealization`.

Let `S` be an operator realization of `GL_n` over a number field `F`, and let `w` be a
real place. `IsRealPlaceCommutantStable S w Z` says that the complex subspace `Z` is
stable under the compact `O(n)` action at `w`, the action of the complexified real
matrix Lie algebra at `w`, and every complex-linear endomorphism of `S.Space` that
commutes with both actions.

The implemented proof shows that this condition implies stability under the whole
concrete automorphic operator algebra. Finite adelic operators commute with the
chosen place actions. Lie actions at other places commute with them as well; at the
chosen place stability is a defining clause. Tensor induction and the sum over
archimedean places give stability under the full complexified Lie algebra.
Induction over the algebra generated by the Lie algebra then gives stability under
the enveloping algebra.

For the full infinite compact action, the proof writes a tuple of compact factors
as its factor at `w` times the tuple whose component at `w` is identity. The latter
commutes with the compact action at `w` and fixes its Lie action by conjugation, so
its action belongs to the commutant. The two defining stability clauses then apply
in sequence. Finally, induction over the finite, compact and enveloping generators
gives `IsRealPlaceCommutantStable.operator`.

`IsRealPlaceCommutantStable.eq_bot_or_eq_top` packages `Z` as a subrepresentation of
the full operator representation and applies `S.IsIrreducible`. No local
irreducibility, tensor-product theorem or analytic regularity assertion is used.
