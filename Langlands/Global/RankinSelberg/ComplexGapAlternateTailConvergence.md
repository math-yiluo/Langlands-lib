# Joint convergence for the alternate complex tail

Measurable kernels dominated by the alternate tail envelope are
absolutely integrable for a suitable simple-root decay order. The measure is the
product of the compatible quotient measure, Lebesgue measure in the
logarithmic tail, entry Haar measure on the upper unipotent tail, the
additive measure of the retained rectangle column, and compact Haar
measure. The required lower bound on the leading determinant rate
includes the norm of the quotient's logarithmic density functional.

The pointwise alternate tail bound separates the variables. The
quotient density is absorbed by an increase in the required leading
rate. The logarithmic tail has an integrable absolute-value exponential.
Inversion preserves the unipotent Haar measure. Its inverse matrix norm
dominates the supremum norm of the strict upper entries, whose additive
product measure integrates sufficiently large negative powers of the
norm. The same estimate handles the retained rectangle column, and
compact Haar has finite mass. Multiplication of the resulting integrable
functions gives the joint majorant.

This is a derived convergence step for the alternate chart of H. Jacquet,
*Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §10.2, formula (10.5),
author pp. 72–73, and the remaining tail integration in §10.4,
Lemma 10.7, pp. 79–81.
