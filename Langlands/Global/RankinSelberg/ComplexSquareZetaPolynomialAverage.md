# Left compact averages of standard square polynomial Gaussians

A continuous weighted compact average of inverse left unitary translates of a polynomial times the square Gaussian of scale `2π` is another polynomial times that Gaussian. The new multiplier has total degree at most the original degree.

Transposing the matrix turns inverse left translation into right translation by the inverse transpose. Right unitary translation preserves the squared norm and acts by a linear substitution in the real matrix coordinates. Transposition acts by renaming the polynomial variables. Integration in the finite-dimensional space of polynomial Gaussians with the original degree bound gives the claimed polynomial for the average.

This is a derived compact-averaging step for Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), Proposition 8.1, author-posted pp. 60–61, using the square coefficient calculation in §4.7, author pp. 30–31.
