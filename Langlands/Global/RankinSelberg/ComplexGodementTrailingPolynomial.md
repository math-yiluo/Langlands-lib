# Trailing-row polynomials for compact-finite complex principal series

Let a compact-finite vector belong to the upper-Borel complex standard module whose first character is the distinguished character. On the unitary group, divide its value by the distinguished character of the determinant. The resulting function is a polynomial in the trailing rows of the unitary matrix and their conjugates.

The proof completes any rectangular matrix by adjoining the conjugates of its signed maximal minors as the missing top row. For the trailing rows of a unitary matrix, the adjugate identity identifies the completion with left multiplication by the diagonal matrix whose first entry is the reciprocal determinant and whose other entries are one. This completion is unitary and has determinant one. The upper-Borel transformation law gives the distinguished-character factor. The polynomial description of compact-finite vectors on the unitary group then pulls back through the polynomial completion by substitution of multivariate polynomials.

This is a compact restriction step toward the standard Schwartz generation in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §7.1, Proposition 7.1(v)–(vi), pp. 44–47.
