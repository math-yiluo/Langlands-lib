# Joint continuation by the expanded real factors

Let the shapes of two real block lists be fixed and let their exponents be entire functions of a complex parameter \(z\). Expand each list into real characters by the position expansion. Let \(D(z,s)\) be the reciprocal pair denominator for the expanded lists. Suppose \(Z(z,s)\) is jointly holomorphic for \(|z|<3\) and \(\operatorname{Re}s>\sigma\), with a common threshold \(\sigma\). Suppose that for every \(z\) in some disk about zero the slice \(Z(z,\cdot)\) admits an entire normalization by \(D(z,\cdot)^{-1}\), agreeing with the integral times \(D\) sufficiently far to the right. The threshold for this slice agreement may depend on \(z\).

`realPositionFamilyContinuation` gives a jointly holomorphic function on \(|z|<3/2\) and all \(s\) that agrees with \(D(z,s)Z(z,s)\) throughout its original right half-plane. This is the expanded normalization used as input to the real block cancellation argument. The passage from an expanded normalization to the prescribed block factor follows the role of the principal-series expansion in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, Proposition 11.1, p. 82 of the author-posted edition.

The polynomial comparison writes \(D\) as a product of holomorphic linear factors and the denominator for the real block lists. Hence \(DZ\) is holomorphic on the original domain. On each slice near zero, multiply the normalization identity by the denominator on a sufficiently far right half-plane where the denominator is nonzero. The identity theorem gives the trace on the entire common right half-plane. The compact parameter continuation theorem then extends these slices jointly.
