# Analyticity of eigenfunctions of the complex matrix Hessian sum

On the open set of invertible complex matrices, a smooth function satisfying
the scalar Hessian-sum eigenfunction equation is real analytic. The coordinate
operator adds the negative eigenvalue as its order-zero coefficient. Its
second-order symbol is the sum of squares in the left matrix frame and is
unchanged by the order-zero term. Scalar elliptic regularity applies to this
homogeneous equation with analytic coefficients.

This is an internal criterion for applying analytic uniqueness to local
principal series and Whittaker coefficients.
