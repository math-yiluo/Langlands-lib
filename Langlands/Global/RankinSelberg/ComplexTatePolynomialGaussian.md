# Polynomial normalizations of complex Tate integrals of standard Gaussians

This derives the complex rank-one calculation in the base case of Jacquet,
*Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), Proposition 8.1, author
pp. 60–61. The calculation uses the angular Gaussian formula for the
complex Tate integral in the Library's twice-area normalization.

For a monomial in a complex number and its conjugate, the angular weight
is the sum of the character weight and the difference of the two monomial
degrees. When this weight is nonzero, rotation by the angle that gives
its value minus one forces the integral to vanish. When the weight is
zero, the common monomial degree shifts the spectral variable in the
matched Tate Gaussian integral.

The Gamma recurrence expresses the shifted integral as the original Gamma
factor times a polynomial. Its degree is bounded by the common monomial
degree. Integrating the finite monomial expansion gives a polynomial of
degree at most the total degree of the input polynomial. The identity
theorem identifies this polynomial with the entire Tate normalization.
