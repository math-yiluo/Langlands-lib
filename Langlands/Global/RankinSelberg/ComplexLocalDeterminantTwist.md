# Determinant twists of complex principal series

Jacquet’s square-matrix integrals in §8.2 and §8.3 motivate the determinant twist. The lemma starts with a smooth vector \(f\) in the normalized upper-Borel principal series for \(\chi\) on \(\mathrm{GL}_{r+1}(\mathbb C)\). For character data \(d\), the function \(g \mapsto d.\mathrm{character}(\det g) f(g)\) belongs to the normalized principal series with position characters \(z \mapsto d.\mathrm{character}(z)\chi_i(z)\). Applying this construction to a matrix coefficient also uses right translation and the transport of the linear functional that defines the coefficient.

The proof specializes the Library’s smooth principal-series twist theorem to `d.character` and commutes the two factors in each position character. The underlying twist theorem uses the determinant polynomial for smoothness and the upper-unipotent and diagonal determinants for normalized inducing covariance.
