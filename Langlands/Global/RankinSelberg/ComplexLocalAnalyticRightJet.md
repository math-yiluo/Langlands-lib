# Analytic uniqueness from right differentiation

This internal uniqueness lemma supplies the analytic step in comparing compact averages for Jacquet §8.2. It takes a family of smooth functions on the complex general linear group that is closed under all real right derivatives. If every member vanishes at a given unit, the proof shows that every member has a zero matrix Taylor jet there.

At an invertible matrix `y`, the differential applied to `yX` is the right derivative in direction `X`. The proof inducts on the order of the matrix derivatives. It first shows that the lower jets of the differential composed with multiplication by `y` vanish, using the corresponding right derivative members of the family. Composing with multiplication by `y⁻¹` recovers the original differential. The product derivative bound then gives its vanishing jets, which completes the induction.

For analytic matrix extensions, a zero jet gives local vanishing by the Taylor series. Analytic uniqueness on the preconnected domain of invertible matrices propagates the identity.
