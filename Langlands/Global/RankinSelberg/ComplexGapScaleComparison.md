# The two scaled comparisons on the gap charts

The theorem gives the two-scale integral identity for the separated ordinary and dual kernels, with the tail Weyl character on the ordinary side. It assumes the smaller-rank normalized equation with strip growth, compact finiteness of the leading square tests, and integrability of the ordinary, mixed and dual weighted chart kernels.

First the product measure separates the leading group from the retained data. The square comparison at a fixed quotient representative replaces the leading test by its positive Fourier transform and introduces the second scale. The smaller-rank comparison then treats the leading matrix and retained data as fixed right translations. Reindexing the smaller complementary rectangle preserves its product Haar measure and gives the exact dual chart kernel. The first scale from this comparison multiplies the second one. This implements the two comparisons in Jacquet's equations (10.7), (10.9), and (10.10).
