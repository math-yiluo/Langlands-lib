# Joint logarithmic and unipotent decay in the alternate gap chart

The alternate tail bound combines the retained matrix estimates and the uniform triangular Fourier estimate. The full family of earlier inverse-transpose columns leaves an integrable radial factor on the strict upper entries. The first retained column and the last inverse row provide independent endpoint damping. The first column also supplies a prescribed negative power in the retained rectangular variable.

The proof chooses the entry decay order before bounding its power of the retained matrix height. The height contributes a fixed exponential in the tail logs and a fixed polynomial in the rectangle. The torus estimate absorbs the resulting exponential by choosing the simple-root and endpoint decay orders. Schwartz seminorms absorb those endpoint orders and the remaining rectangular power. The resulting pointwise envelope is a product of the two logarithmic exponentials and the two radial factors.
