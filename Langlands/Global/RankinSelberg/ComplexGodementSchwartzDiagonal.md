# The Schwartz diagonal test of a complex Godement section

For a fixed larger matrix and each unitary smaller matrix, the map from
additive triangular entries to the argument of the rectangular test is
linear. Its left inverse multiplies on the right by the inverse larger
matrix and on the left by the inverse unitary matrix, then extracts the
diagonal and strictly upper entries from the trailing columns. These left
inverses depend continuously on the compact variable. Their bounded
operator norms give a uniform lower bound for the linear test maps.

For a Schwartz rectangular test, a continuous smaller-group input and a
finite Borel measure on the unitary group, the weighted compact average
is therefore Schwartz on the product of the diagonal and strictly upper
entry spaces. The Schwartz marginal theorem integrates out the strictly
upper entries against additive Lebesgue measure. The result is a Schwartz
function of the complex diagonal with the stated integral formula.

[Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, §7.1, proof of Proposition 7.1, p. 44](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf).
