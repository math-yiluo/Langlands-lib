# A common strip rate for the complex pair denominators

For a compact inducing parameter set and continuous exponent maps with
fixed angular weights, `ComplexPairStripRateStatement` bounds both
reciprocal Gamma products on every closed vertical spectral strip. The
bound has a strip-dependent constant and exponential rate `n m π`, where
`n` and `m` are the two character list lengths.

The single reciprocal complex Gamma block has rate `π` on an arbitrary
closed real strip. Each block's shift varies continuously on the compact
parameter set. Choose a common upper bound for the norms of the shifts
and enlarge the real strip by that amount. The imaginary part of a
shift changes the exponential bound by a constant at most `exp(π R)`.
Multiplying the block bounds adds their rates. Applying the same
argument to the inverse lists and taking the larger constant gives both
bounds with rate `n m π`.

This is an internal analytic estimate for the normalized integrals in
Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §8.1, pp. 53–54.
