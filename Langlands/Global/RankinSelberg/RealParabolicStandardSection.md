# Compact parabolic sections in the real standard module

Let the leading and trailing Levi groups have ranks \(m\) and \(n\). Given exact real standard functions for their respective block lists \(d_1,d_2\), and a compactly supported smooth test on the upper rectangle, `standardSection` forms the corresponding compact parabolic section. It uses determinant exponents \(-n/2\) on the leading block and \(m/2\) on the trailing block. The block Weyl element exchanges the two row blocks on the left and preserves the order within each block. `RealParabolicStandardSectionStatement` places the resulting function in `realStandardModule` for the list \(d_2\mathbin{+\!+}d_1\) in the upper-Borel convention.

The smoothness proof extends the section to the open set of invertible matrices and composes with the block Weyl element and the real-linear rank reindexing. Conjugation by the block Weyl element sends the upper Borel to block lower form. Each of its two diagonal blocks is the corresponding upper triangular Levi matrix. Splitting the product of the principal-series characters at the block boundary produces the determinant exponents in the section. The left block-lower translation law then gives the required Borel covariance.

Each induced two-dimensional block lies within one Levi block. Its rotation acts in that Levi block after conjugating by the block Weyl element, and its determinant factor is one. At a point in the open cell, the Fourier coefficient along this rotation is a scalar multiple of the corresponding Levi Fourier coefficient. Off the cell the rotated section vanishes by the left translation law. This proves every Fourier-window condition in `realStandardModule`.

This construction is the scalar section for the normalized induction by the lower parabolic in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §13.2, p. 100, and the proof of Proposition 14.1, pp. 103–104 of the author-posted edition. The left block exchange gives the section in the Library's upper-Borel convention.
