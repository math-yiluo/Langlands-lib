# Real determinant twists of the position principal series

**Provenance.** This is an internal bookkeeping step for Hervé Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §8.2, pp. 54–58. There the matrix integral is applied to a coefficient of `I_{ν,v}` multiplied by `μ_n(det g)`. That is the coefficient of the principal series whose inducing characters are all multiplied by the same real character. The proof follows the Library's complex counterpart `ComplexDeterminantTwist` (in `ComplexStandardDeterminantTwist`).

For a real character datum `c` (sign parity and complex exponent) and `g ∈ GL_n(ℝ)`, `realDetCharacter c g` is `c(det g)`. The file records that it is multiplicative and smooth. Its norm is `|det g|^Re(exponent)`, so it has polynomial height growth. At an inverse transpose it takes the reciprocal value. For an inducing list `ds`, `twistedPositionList n ds c` is the list of `n` real character blocks whose `i`th character is the `i`th position character of `ds` times `c`.

`RealPositionDeterminantTwistStatement` states three facts. First, the `i`th position character of the twisted list is `χᵢ · c` for `i < n`. Second, its position sign is the old sign plus the parity of `c` in `Fin 2`, and its position exponent is the old exponent plus that of `c`. Third, for every smooth section `f` of the principal series of `ds`, `g ↦ c(det g) f(g)` lies in the smooth principal series of the twisted list. Only the first `n` positions are compared, which is all the rank-`n` principal series uses. No compact derivative bound or zeta integral is asserted here.

**Lean proof.** Write `c(det g)` as the Library function `charFun` of the actual determinant via `RealCharacterData.character_eq`. On invertible matrices `charFun` is smooth because the determinant is polynomial and nonzero. The norm computation uses that the sign has absolute value one. On the Borel subgroup the determinant is the product of the diagonal units, since unipotent determinants are one. Hence `c(det)` multiplies the Borel character by `∏ c(aᵢ)`, which is the principal-series character of the twisted characters. The twisted list is `List.ofFn` of the products `charData(signᵢ, exponentᵢ) · c`. Its position characters, signs and exponents are read off from `realPositionCharacter_map_character` and two short inductions on lists of character blocks.
