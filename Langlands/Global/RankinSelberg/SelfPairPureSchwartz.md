# A product Schwartz function detecting both equal-rank residues

`PureSchwartzOriginWitnessStatement` chooses actual local Schwartz factors at every real and complex place and locally constant compactly supported factors at the finite places of \(S\). Their assembled adelic product is standard outside \(S\), has value one at the origin and has strictly positive real part of its Fourier transform at the origin.

The archimedean factors are real nonnegative smooth compactly supported functions with value one at zero. The finite factors are the indicators of the integral rows, and their supports are compact open additive subgroups. The restricted tensor construction combines them into a nonnegative Schwartz–Bruhat function. At the origin the Fourier kernel is one, so the Fourier value is the integral of this function. Continuity, its value at zero and the full support of the row Haar measure give a strictly positive integral.

This is a consequence of the Library's restricted tensor construction and row Fourier measure. The resulting test supplies the two origin evaluations in the residue formula of Cogdell 2004, §2.3, printed p. 210.
