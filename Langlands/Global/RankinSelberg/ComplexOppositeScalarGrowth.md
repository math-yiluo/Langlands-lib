# Polynomial growth of the scalar extension factor on the complex opposite cell

The scalar factor of the compact extension is the section with constant
compact profile one. Its spherical inducing characters are determined by
the given exponents after subtracting the normalized Borel shift. The
Library's complex principal-series height estimate therefore applies to
this fixed section.

On the opposite cell the matrix determinant is one, so the determinant
factor in the matrix height estimate equals one. The entrywise matrix
norm is at most one plus the supremum norm of the upper-entry
coordinates. Combining the height estimate for the section with this
coordinate estimate gives a polynomial bound on its scalar factor.
This controls the part of the compact approximation away from the local
Lipschitz neighborhood.
