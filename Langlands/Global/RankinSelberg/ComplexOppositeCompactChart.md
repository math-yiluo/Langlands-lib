# The complex compact picture in opposite-cell coordinates

The transposed upper-entry chart is affine in its strict lower entries.
Composing with the smooth polarized Gram expressions on the invertible
matrices gives a smooth scalar factor and a smooth matrix-valued compact
factor. For each entry of the compact factor, the Gram expression uses
zero exponents and the corresponding coordinate variable. The factors
agree with those in the Library's upper-Borel extension and take the
identity values at the origin.

Near the origin, the finite-dimensional mean value bound makes each
factor Lipschitz in upper-entry coordinates. Taking a common neighborhood
and bounding constant gives both first-order estimates. The estimate for
the compact factor uses the matrix operator norm. These local estimates
are used in the diagonal approximation of complex compact profiles.
