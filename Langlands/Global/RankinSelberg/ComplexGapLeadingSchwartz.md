# Schwartz domination of the leading rectangle in the complex gap recurrence

A Schwartz test on the stacked square and rectangular blocks, after multiplication by a complex invertible matrix, decays in the norms of both blocks. The two polynomial decay orders can be prescribed independently. The price of the multiplication is the power of Jacquet's height displayed in the theorem.

Multiplication by the inverse recovers the stacked matrix from its image. The entries of the inverse are bounded by the height, and the entrywise norm of the stack bounds the norm of each block. Apply the Schwartz seminorm estimate at the sum of the two requested decay orders.

The leading square factor also has an integrable majorant for the exact general-linear Haar measure. A sufficient power of the normalized determinant cancels the Haar density and the inverse-matrix denominator in the height. The remaining positive determinant power has polynomial growth in the additive matrix norm. Increasing the decay order leaves an integrable power of one plus that norm against matrix Lebesgue measure.
