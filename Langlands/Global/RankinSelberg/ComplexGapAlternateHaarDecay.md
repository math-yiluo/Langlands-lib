# Exponential decay for the weighted logarithmic tail Haar

The retained matrix has normalized determinant equal to the exponential of twice the sum of its logarithmic tail coordinates. The unitary factor has normalized determinant one, and the upper unipotent factor has determinant one.

The triangular Haar density consists of the tail modulus and the consecutive block determinant factor. Bounding each root contribution by an exponential in the sum of absolute logarithms gives a coefficient that dominates the entire density. The integrand decays at that coefficient plus one. The change-of-density formula reduces the finite-integral proof to the product of one-dimensional exponential integrals.
