# Bounded evaluation of uniform real Whittaker coefficients

Assume `RealSmoothCasselmanWallachExtensionStatement.{0, 0, 0}`. For an
irreducible real standard module and a uniform Whittaker coefficient map \(B\),
`RealWhittakerStandardEvaluationStatement` supplies a complex-linear
functional \(\ell\) on the smooth position principal series of the expanded
character list. It satisfies the finite compact right-derivative bound
`HasCompactDerivativeBound`. If \(f\) is an original compact-finite standard
vector, applying \(\ell\) to its right translate by any \(g\) gives \(B(f)(g)\).
The irreducibility and uniform coefficient hypotheses are part of the
statement. The extension to the full position model is a linear functional
with this bound and these evaluation identities.

Use the compact Banach representation for the source and the weighted
continuous functions vanishing at infinity for the target. The compact-finite
equivalence and the lifted coefficient map satisfy the two intertwiner
hypotheses of smooth Casselman–Wallach extension. Standard-module
admissibility gives the remaining algebraic hypothesis. Compose the
resulting smooth group map with the continuous weighted identity evaluation.
The intertwiner identity for actual group translation and its agreement on
compact-finite vectors give the coefficient identity on the smooth standard
module.

Continuity in the genuine derivative topology gives a bound by finitely many
word seminorms. Each Lie word on a standard vector is the restriction of
the corresponding scalar right-derivative word to the orthogonal group,
by iteration of the single-direction comparison. Expand the inducing list,
which preserves the principal-series covariance. On the resulting position
space take the seminorm given by the same finite sum of compact suprema
times the bounding constant. Hahn–Banach extends the functional from the
full smooth standard subspace with this bound. That subspace contains every
actual right translate of an original standard vector, giving the stated
identity on the expanded position space.

This is an internal reduction to smooth Casselman–Wallach extension for
using compact-derivative-bounded functionals in the square matrix zeta
functional equation. Whittaker equivariance on the whole expanded space
is an additional premise in the separate uniform coefficient extension
reduction.
