# Spectral shifts of compact complex Godement sections

`godementSectionShift` multiplies each determinant weight in the Godement
section by the complex power of the normalized determinant modulus with
exponent `s`. The formula writes each power as an exponential of `s` times
the real logarithm of the positive squared determinant norm.

Fix the larger group element and assume the smaller-group input is continuous
and the measure on the smaller group is a locally finite Borel measure. For a
continuous test with compact support in the full-row-rank locus, each real
exponential moment is continuous and inherits compact support from the test
on that smaller-group fiber. Local finiteness gives integrability of these
moments. The published differentiation theorem for exponential moments then
yields an entire integral in the shift, and the determinant factor outside the
integral is also entire. Jacquet's smooth induced input and Haar measure in
Proposition 7.1(iii) satisfy these continuity and local finiteness hypotheses.

[Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, §7.1, formula (7.1) and Proposition 7.1(iii), p. 43](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf).
