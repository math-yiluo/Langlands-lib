# Uniform entire Jacquet continuation in all complex ranks

`ComplexJacquetContinuation.complexJacquetUniformContinuation` constructs a family of Jacquet values for every compact-finite profile with the prescribed angular covariance. Each group value is entire in the inducing exponents. The group slices are continuous and have locally uniform simple-root estimates. The family agrees with the exact entry-Haar open-cell integral on the strict chamber.

For the induction step, fix the common finite-dimensional space of compact profiles spanned by the Godement generators at all parameters. Use pointwise continuation on a basis of that space. The linear chamber evaluation identifies the continued Godement values with the dot products of their compact-profile coordinates and those basis Jacquet values. Since the coordinates are given by finite sums of compact evaluations, they are holomorphic. The identity theorem extends the dot-product relations from the chamber to every parameter.

At each parameter, choose independent generator rows. On a small ball their matrix remains invertible. The inverse transforms the values of these finitely many generators into the basis Jacquet values. The coefficients for the original profile are continuous at the center, independent of the group argument and uniformly bounded on a smaller ball. The estimates of the generator families pass to the resulting finite sum. The rank-one integral is the standard section because the trivial unipotent group has entry-product Haar mass one. Induction uses the fixed Haar measures of the complex general linear groups.
