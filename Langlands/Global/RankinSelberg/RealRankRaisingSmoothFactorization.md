# Smooth-vector factorization in the real rank-raising coordinates

Let a jointly continuous complex Banach representation of the real general linear group of rank \(r+2\) act on a genuine smooth vector \(v\). Given the additive row Haar measure and a sigma-finite bi-invariant Haar measure on the trailing rank-one group, `RealRankRaisingSmoothFactorizationStatement` expresses \(v\) as a finite sum of smooth-vector averages along the compensating matrices \([a(h)^{-1},0;x,h]\). The formula holds after every continuous linear scalar evaluation on the genuine smooth vectors. Each integrand is Bochner integrable. The kernels are compactly supported Schwartz functions of the full bottom row with support contained in the locus of nonzero last entry. The coordinate measure is \(|\det h|\,dx\,d^\times h\).

Apply `smoothVectorSubgroupFactorization` to the closed exponential subgroup with its left Haar measure. The scalar integrands use the same vectors and compact kernels for every evaluation. `RealRankRaisingHaar` identifies the left measure as a weighted version of the right measure. `RealRankRaisingKernel` absorbs that weight into the Schwartz functions on the row. Their integrands are continuous and compactly supported on the subgroup before transport through its row chart, which proves integrability and the formula in the stated product coordinates.

This is the smooth-vector averaging step of Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, Lemma 14.1, pp. 106–107 in the author-posted edition, expressed for a continuous Banach representation of the ambient matrix group.
