# Uniform approximation of the compact values of complex Godement sections

In the right half-plane of absolute convergence of the complex Godement
integral, fix the smaller standard vector. For each compactly supported
rectangular Schwartz test, the section restricted to the larger unitary
group is uniformly approximated by finite sums of sections of dilated
Gaussian-polynomial tests with the same smaller vector.

The pointwise approximation error for the rectangular test has a fixed
Gaussian envelope. On the unitary group the right translating matrix
preserves the Gaussian, so the error integrand is bounded by a constant
times the norm of the Gaussian-test integrand at the identity. Absolute
convergence makes this envelope integrable. The determinant prefactor is
bounded on the unitary group. The approximation tolerance absorbs both
bounds. Finite integral additivity gives the sum of actual sections.

This is the compact uniform approximation used in the route to
Proposition 7.1(vi) of H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*
(2009), §7.1, author pp. 43 and 46–47. It uses the Library's convergence
half-plane and the variable Gaussian scale.
