# Real matrix functional equation for compact derivative functionals at parameter one

**Provenance.** This is the real position-principal-series case of the matrix zeta equation in Hervé Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §4.7, Proposition 4.4 (pp. 30–31), at positive Fourier parameter one. The formalized linear forms are precisely those supplied with `HasCompactDerivativeBound`. The implemented argument extends the evaluation calculation by differentiating under the integral, then taking a continuous linear functional of a compact-coordinate vector integral. The theta identity uses the particular convolved Tate pair, not an arbitrary supplied pair.

For `n = r + 1`, take a Haar measure on `GL_n(ℝ)`, a sigma-finite Haar measure on `O(n)`, a section `f` in the smooth principal series of the position characters of an inducing list, a compact-derivative-bounded complex linear form `ℓ`, and a square Schwartz matrix test `Φ`. Let `F(g) = ℓ(translate g f)`. The normalized integrals use `F(g)` and `F(g^-T)` with tests `Φ` and `matrixTransform 1 Φ` and exponent `s + r/2`. `RealMatrixCompactFunctionalEquationAtOneStatement` supplies absolute convergence and holomorphy on a common initial half-plane, meromorphic continuations `M, Md`, and the codiscrete equality

`torusL e u s * Md (1 - s) = torusEpsilon e * torusL e (-u) (1 - s) * M s`.

Here `e, u` are the parities and exponents of the position characters, with the product factors of `ArchimedeanDeterminantTheta`. This result does not identify the position factors with factors of a Langlands quotient and does not give the Whittaker-model equation.

**Theta identity for general kernel pairs.** `realMatrix_functional_theta_at_one_of_pair` proves the matrix theta reflection for `F(g) = ℓ(translate g f)` at every positive scale. It holds for any measurable, rapidly decreasing determinant kernel pair `(Hk, Kk)` that satisfies the torus theta identity for the position characters at every positive scale for all Schwartz tests on `ℝⁿ`. `realMatrix_functional_theta_at_one` is its instance for the convolved Tate pair `detH`, `detK`. The continuation and functional equation below use that instance.

**Lean proof.** Separate each theta integrand into a scalar weight (Schwartz test, rank shift and the rapid determinant kernel) and the coefficient. Every integer height moment of the scalar weight is integrable: apply the real matrix majorant with coefficient `H^m` and then rapid decay of the determinant kernel. The evaluation transport gives the theta equality at each point for the unintegrated translated section, including the factor `a` in the dual weight. Inverse transpose is continuous and preserves `H`. The compact-functional transfer proves the desired theta identity for `F` and its dual. Finally `compactFunctionalGrowth` gives the polynomial coefficient bound; the general matrix continuation of the determinant pair constructs the continuation, including holomorphy on the initial half-plane.
