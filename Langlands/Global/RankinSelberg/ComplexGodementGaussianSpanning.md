# Polynomial Gaussian Godement sections span in a convergence half-plane

Fix the smaller character list. For sufficiently large real part of the adjoining exponent, `complexGodementGaussianSpanning` identifies the algebraic span of the polynomial Gaussian Godement sections with the full compact-finite larger standard module. The tests are polynomials in the real and imaginary matrix entries times the same radial Gaussian, and the smaller inputs range over the compact-finite smaller standard module.

Godement convergence makes the section linear in the Schwartz test. The finite right unitary test orbit then gives a finite right orbit of the section through `godementSection_right_mul`. Its compact restriction is continuous because the linear functional given by evaluating the section at the identity is a finite combination of evaluations on the test orbit. The upper-Borel covariance and the continuous finite compact restriction identify the section with a compact-finite principal-series extension by Iwasawa decomposition.

For a compact full-row-rank Godement generator, keep its smaller input fixed. The Gaussian moment theorem says that each continuous weight annihilating the Gaussian section restrictions also annihilates the compact test restriction. Right unitary translation preserves polynomial Gaussian tests and contributes only the scalar determinant factor to a section. The compact pairing criterion therefore puts the compact generator's restriction in the algebraic span of the Gaussian restrictions. The injectivity of restriction on the standard module lifts the relation to the group. Compact Godement spanning then gives the claimed equality of submodules.

This implements the spanning step used to justify splitting the test in the complex adjacent-rank calculation, in the half-plane supplied by Godement convergence. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, Proposition 7.1 and Section 8.3](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf).
