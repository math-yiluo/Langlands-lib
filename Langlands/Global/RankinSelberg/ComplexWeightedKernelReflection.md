# Weighted reflection for the complex pair factors

The normalized complex pair equation with strip growth gives a weighted equality for both kernels at every positive scale. The ordinary weight uses the ordinary reciprocal Gamma product and the epsilon factor with the central sign. The reflected weight uses the reciprocal product of the inverse characters. Both weights share a freely chosen positive Gaussian rate and a real linear shift.

The proof chooses a vertical line in the two regions of absolute convergence. The determinant powers there give integrability of the relevant moment and recover measurability of each kernel at zero. Continuity bounds the normalized functions on the compact portion of a strip and extends the existing exponential bound to all heights. The Gaussian then gives the decay needed for the Mellin contour comparison.
