# Entire normalization on the retained gap chart

`complexGapRetainedNormalization` associates a continuous family of entire normalized values to a standard rectangular leading test and a smaller Whittaker input. For each retained point the family has exponential growth on vertical strips. The ordinary and reflected fiber integrals agree with the two stated values in the right half-plane for that retained point, whenever the corresponding product kernels are integrable.

The leading restriction is a finite combination of polynomial Gaussians with one common scalar dilation. The normalized square convolutions of those Gaussians have polynomial vector outputs in the smaller compact-finite standard module. Their dilated combination is therefore a curve in a fixed finite span. Evaluating the lower-rank normalized linear functional on this curve gives the entire family. Continuity follows from the continuous dilation, compact coefficients, retained matrix, and translated lower-rank normalization. The reflected identity uses the square and lower-rank reflection factors in succession.

This construction uses the square and lower-rank factors of [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §10.4](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf).
