# The actual cuspidal Whittaker lift to smooth archimedean functions

For a fixed finite adelic argument, `whittakerSmoothLift` collects the
original linear Whittaker evaluations into a smooth scalar function on
the infinite adelic group. The published regularity of the actual
Whittaker integral on the open set of invertible mixed matrices proves
genuine smoothness in the Library's coordinates.

The published derivative formula for the integral gives the right
differential of this lift in each real matrix direction. The matrix
generator equals the given real enveloping generator in mixed
coordinates. Induction on the real generators gives covariance with
every original enveloping operator. This is an internal lift of the
Library's existing Whittaker integral and its proved derivative formula.
