# Order-zero Jacquet distributions on the real reversal cell

`RealJacquetReversalDistributionStatement` packages the joint real Jacquet integral as a distribution on the reversal cell at every inducing parameter. For each compact test support contained in the cell, the order-zero bound holds uniformly over compact subsets of the parameter space. Every test evaluation is entire. The distributions satisfy the left and right infinitesimal upper-unipotent laws for the inverse additive character.

The norm of the inverse inducing weight is the product of powers of the absolute diagonal entries of the Borel element, with exponents given by the negatives of the real parts. The homeomorphism of the open cell with the unipotent and Borel variables pulls each fixed compact test support back to a compact integration set. The weight norm is jointly continuous and hence bounded there for compact sets of parameters. The Jacquet phase has norm one. Integration on that compact set bounds the evaluation by the order-zero seminorm, and the universal property of the test-function topology gives continuity.

The entire scalar formula was established for every reversal-cell test. In the nonempty open region of strict dominance, the distribution equals the restriction of the full convergent Jacquet test distribution. The ambient infinitesimal laws therefore hold on the reversal cell in that region. Applying the holomorphic identity theorem to both sides of each law gives the same identity at all exponents.

This internal result gives the initial family on the open stratum for extension through the Bruhat filtration. Its compact-support estimate takes the support inside the reversal cell.
