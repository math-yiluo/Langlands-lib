# Rapid determinant weights on the ordinary complex gap kernel

The ordinary block kernel on the lower quotient has two independent radial weights. They are evaluated on the quotient determinant and on the leading square block. For a partial Fourier test that splits into the stated three Schwartz factors, the kernel is jointly integrable against the compatible quotient measure and the actual right-tail chart measure, including its determinant density.

The block-column identities give the exact inputs of the three Schwartz factors. The translated Whittaker product is jointly continuous by descent to the quotient. Measurable radial weights therefore leave a measurable kernel. Each rapid weight is bounded by a sufficiently large real determinant power with a shift that accounts for the existing spectral and character factors. Moving the chart density to the integrand shifts only the fixed tail power. The ordinary joint convergence theorem then applies to the entire product.

The coupled-weight extension applies to the full ambient Haar integral. Before the quotient shear, the two weights use the quotient determinant divided by the ambient determinant and the ambient determinant itself. After the right-tail shear, they use the quotient determinant divided by the retained determinant and the leading determinant multiplied by it. The defining bounds of rapid decay replace each weight by one fixed power. Their additional powers of the retained determinant enter the arbitrary determinant exponent of the existing joint majorant. The quotient shear and the right-tail Haar formula transfer the resulting integrability to the joint integral with the full lower rectangle. This supplies the bound for the dual Godement kernel in Jacquet’s Lemma 10.6 after inserting its Fourier test.

`complexGapCoupledPowerBounds` and `complexGapCoupledHaarPowerBounds` allow one power bound per radial profile, with independently chosen exponents above the two convergence thresholds. The rapid-weight results choose exponents at the thresholds.
