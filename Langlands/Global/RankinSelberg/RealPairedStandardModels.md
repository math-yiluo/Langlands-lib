# Genuine paired smooth tests of real standard modules

`realPairedStandardModels` takes the standard compact Banach carrier in each slot. A nonzero exact coefficient map at each specified scale gives a continuous scalar evaluation on its genuine smooth vectors. Multiplicity one identifies the range of each map with the whole exact model. The product of the evaluations extends to the full product-group smooth space. Inserting a unipotent in one slot picks out that slot's character, since the character of the identity in the other slot is one. The opposite signs cancel under simultaneous leading padding.

The pure vectors from compact-finite sources have dense span in the product-group word topology. On those vectors the slot coefficients agree everywhere with the exact compact-finite coefficients. Under the exact ordinary continuation hypothesis, `realPairedStandardUnequalGrowth` and `realPairedStandardEqualGrowth` use this comparison and density to supply the ordinary and reflected entire normalized pair with strip growth. Equal ranks retain a Schwartz row on the primal side and its exact Fourier transform on the reflected side. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §12.3 and §14](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
