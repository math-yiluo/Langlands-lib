# The complex equal-rank quotient under a change of additive character

Let \(N\) be the upper unitriangular subgroup of
\(\mathrm{GL}_{r+1}(\mathbb C)\) and write \(t\) for the diagonal with
entries \(a^r,a^{r-1},\ldots,1\). This module supplies changes of
variables for the equal-rank integral over \(N\backslash\mathrm{GL}_{r+1}\)
in Jacquet's (2.6), printed and PDF p. 6 of *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg
integrals* (2009).

Conjugation by \(t\) and its inverse preserve \(N\). The published
quotient-automorphism construction gives a homeomorphism of the
left quotient. The proof pushes forward the group Haar measure, the
unipotent Haar measure and the quotient measure together and applies the
published compatibility result for such pushforwards. Right invariance
for the new quotient measure then proves that \(Ng\mapsto Ntgz\) is
measure preserving from the original quotient data to the new data for
every right factor \(z\). The choices \(z=1\) and
\(z=\operatorname{scalar}(a)\) therefore use the same new data.

For opposite characters, the product of two Whittaker functions is
left \(N\)-invariant. For the dual functions, inverse transpose
conjugated by the long Weyl matrix preserves \(N\) and negates the sum
of its entries on the first superdiagonal. Each dual function transforms
with the opposite character from its original function, and their
product is again left \(N\)-invariant. Since the last row and determinant
are left \(N\)-invariant, the proof descends the integrand to the quotient
and identifies its value at the class of any representative with the
formula at that representative.
