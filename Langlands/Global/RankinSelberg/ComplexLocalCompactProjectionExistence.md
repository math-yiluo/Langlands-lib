# K-finite inducing vectors from compact convolution

Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), pp. 56–57 and 59–60, uses a compact projection of a right-translated inducing vector in his finite expansions. This internal result constructs the inducing-side projected vectors for a continuous compact test whose left translates span a finite-dimensional space.

The convolution on the compact group is continuous by continuity of the parametric integral. Haar left invariance expresses each of its right translates as the convolution with a left translate of the compact test. The finite expansion of that kernel therefore bounds the span of the right translates. Left multiplication by a unitary diagonal pulls out the principal-series character, since the original inducing vector has that covariance. The Library’s complex principal-series extension theorem gives a K-finite inducing vector with the resulting compact restriction. This construction gives the whole family, indexed by the translating group element.

The result supplies the inducing-vector projection identity used by `ComplexLocalCompactProjectionReductionStatement`. The Whittaker averaging identity still requires a separate comparison with the Whittaker intertwiner.
