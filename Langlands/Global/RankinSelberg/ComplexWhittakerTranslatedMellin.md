# Mellin pair identities on genuine right translates

The complex unequal-rank radial pair identity for the finite Whittaker models extends to arbitrary fixed right translates of exact intertwiner values. The theorem assumes the pair identity on the original finite models. The common integrable bounds needed for the compact approximation follow from the rapid decay of the radial weights.

For a fixed vector and a compact family of right translates, Iwasawa factorization writes each change of logarithmic torus coordinate as the torus coordinate of a compact family. This bounded displacement preserves a common growth order and arbitrary simple-root decay. Apply the ordinary and dual half-plane majorants to the two compact translation families. Each rapid Mellin weight is bounded by a constant times a real determinant power in the corresponding half-plane. This supplies the bounds for the weighted compact approximation and proves the identity on the two genuine translates.
