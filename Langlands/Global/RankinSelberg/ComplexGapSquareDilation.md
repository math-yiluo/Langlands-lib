# Normalized square convolution under a common dilation

`complexGapSquareDilation` evaluates the ordinary and reflected normalized square integrals for a common scalar dilation of a finite linear combination of tests. Each original test has a polynomial square convolution trace in the same Whittaker realization. The dilated combination is assumed to have finite left unitary translate span. Both formulas hold on a right half-plane independent of the Whittaker evaluation point.

The scalar change of group variable gives the ordinary formula. It places the central character of the opposite inducing list and the adjoining determinant weight in `squareDilationWeight`. Moderate height bounds control the absolute integrals at both spectral ends, with the dependence on the evaluation point only in a multiplicative bound. The entire square reflection identifies the reflected normalization with the same finite combination at the reflected spectral value. Holomorphic uniqueness extends the reflected identity to the common right half-plane. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §4, Proposition 4.4, and §10.4](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
