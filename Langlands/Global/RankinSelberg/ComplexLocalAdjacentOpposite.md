# The opposite standard character in adjacent rank

This internal implication uses the contragredient symmetry of Jacquet's local equation, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §2, Theorem 2.1, and the adjacent case in §8.3, printed pp. 58–60. It takes the exact corank-one functional equation at the positive standard character as a premise.

In corank one the dual integral is the ordinary integral of the dual coefficients for the same quotient measure. Applying the positive equation to those coefficients exchanges the two continuations. Reversing the inverse inducing lists leaves the pair factors unchanged. At the reflected spectral argument the two opposite-character epsilon factors multiply to one, as follows from the character at `−1` and the square of the standard complex epsilon factor. Reflection then gives the negative-standard-character equation off a discrete set, with the same convergence and holomorphy clauses.

The normalized version applies the same comparison directly to the two entire normalized functions. It exchanges these functions and their vertical-strip bounds, uses the contragredient comparison to identify the two half-plane traces, and uses the epsilon cancellation in the everywhere normalized equation.
