# The two equal-rank adjoining-exponent chambers

This internal result provides the exact original and dual chamber identities in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §8.2, printed pp. 54–57. Each direction uses its own positive chamber. The Haar normalization from the full-unipotent and rectangular recursions remains common.

The product of the rectangular and row tests is Schwartz. On the appropriate chamber the double kernel is absolutely integrable and descends continuously. The Weil and Fubini theorem gives the unfolded formula. Applying that argument with inverse inducing character and opposite additive parameter handles the dual recursion. The dual mixed Fourier test changes to the positive square Fourier test with the central sign of the smaller list. The other sign, the adjoining character at `−1` raised to the smaller rank, is constant as the radial exponent varies.
