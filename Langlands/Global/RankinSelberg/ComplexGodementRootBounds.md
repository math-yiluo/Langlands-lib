# Uniform simple-root decay for continued Godement values

`ComplexGodementRootBounds.complexGodementRootBounds` upgrades the uniform smaller-rank estimates to the continued larger Godement family, assuming the left Whittaker law for the larger family and a bound on the real determinant exponent after the column change.

At a positive log-diagonal times a unitary factor, the determinant prefactor and the weighted norm of right translation have fixed exponential orders in the sum of the absolute diagonal logarithms. Each root derivative differentiates the Schwartz test before the diagonal translation. Continuity of that Schwartz operator and the bounded norms of unitary translates give constants uniform in the unitary factor and the exponents. The exponential order works for all root derivative orders.

The Whittaker law shows that differentiation along a simple-root line multiplies the value by the trace frequency and the corresponding torus root ratio. Taking sufficiently many derivatives along the largest positive root gives decay in the sum of the positive simple-root logarithms. If no root logarithm is positive, the estimate of derivative order zero applies. The Iwasawa decomposition and unit modulus of the left character extend the bound to the full larger group.
