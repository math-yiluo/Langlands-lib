# Joint continuity of the canonical complex Jacquet family

For fixed angular weights and a fixed compact profile,
`ComplexJacquetJointContinuityStatement` gives continuity of the canonical
Jacquet family on the product of the radial parameter space and the group.
The scalar additive Haar measure and the Fourier parameter are fixed.

The locally uniform simple-root estimate at decay order zero bounds the
coefficient on a parameter ball and a neighborhood of any group argument.
The neighborhood uses continuity of the sum of the absolute Iwasawa
logarithms. At every nearby group argument, the Cauchy estimate gives the
same bound for the parameter derivative on a smaller parameter ball.
The mean value inequality on this ball makes variation in the parameter
uniformly small in the group neighborhood. Continuity at the fixed parameter
then controls variation in the group argument.

This internal consequence of the Library's uniform Jacquet continuation
provides the continuity needed to integrate a family on a compact real
interval. The underlying continuation and parameter-uniform estimate are
based on Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §7.2,
Proposition 7.2 on printed p. 48.
