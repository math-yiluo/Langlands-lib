# Mixed Whittaker functionals on the actual band completions

Fix a cuspidal operator realization over a number field with compact additive adèle quotient. Supply a continuous norm-one central character acting on the realization and a unitary form computing the canonical determinant-band inner product. For a specified standard additive character, assume square integrability on the band and the actual Whittaker band bound for the original realization.

The form norm equals the band `L²` norm on each original vector. The archimedean restriction of the constructed adelic action preserves the inner product. The original vectors are smooth, and the source embedding preserves the finite and compact operators and the real Lie generators. Joint vector recovery gives derivative density at each compact open finite level. These facts identify the compatible source of the general Whittaker extension with the canonical mixed embedding of the actual realization.

The model supplies a linear functional with the global Whittaker character and derivative continuity at each finite level. It gives the original integral on every translated original vector, and it is nonzero when the original realization contains a nonzero vector. This internal result implements the passage to smooth Whittaker functionals in Shalika (1974), §5, pp. 187–188, using the determinant-band formulation of the Library.
