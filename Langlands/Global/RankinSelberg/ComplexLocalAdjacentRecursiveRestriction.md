# The complex corank-one restriction of the rectangular recursion

This internal comparison supplies the restricted original and dual recursions in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §8.3, printed pp. 58–59. The input is a square Schwartz test times a last-column Schwartz test, together with a smaller Whittaker vector.

The quotient recursion agrees with the absolutely convergent smaller-group formula by the simple-root estimate. Right multiplication by the padded smaller matrix acts on the square block and fixes the last column. The partial Fourier transform therefore gives the exact row test of the corank-one kernel. The determinant powers restrict to the smaller group, with the inner exponent equal to `r/2`. Applying rectangular recursion duality and the separated Fourier formula gives the stated reflected square test and the row Fourier transform for the dual restriction.
