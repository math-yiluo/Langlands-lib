# Ordinary normalized generation for the real ranks two and one

For \(GL_2(\mathbb R)\times GL_1(\mathbb R)\) the ordinary normalized
tests cover every spectral point at every nonzero additive scale.
At scale \(1\) or \(-1\) they admit a finite sum equal to one. The
parameter lists have total ranks two and one, the upper standard
module is irreducible, and the smaller quotient data are arbitrary
compatible data.

Total rank one forces a single real character. Total rank two gives
either two characters or one induced block. Two characters can be
put in weakly decreasing order of the real parts of their exponents.
The irreducible reordering theorem identifies the ordinary models
and the pair factor is permutation invariant. The closed-chamber
Gaussian test then gives the finite sum.

An induced block of nonzero weight uses the discrete-series test.
At weight zero the two real characters of opposite parity have
equal exponents. Expansion preserves the standard module and the
pair factor, so the closed-chamber test applies again. The identity
on the standard functions identifies the ordinary models for the
two presentations.

The character-change formula transfers the finite sum at unit
absolute scale and transfers pointwise nonvanishing at every
nonzero scale. This is the rank-two by rank-one case of Jacquet,
*Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), Remark 2.2 and
Theorems 2.6 and 2.7, author-posted pp. 7–10.
