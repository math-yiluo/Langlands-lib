# Polynomial normalizations of diagonal complex Tate products

A polynomial in the diagonal coordinates and their conjugates multiplied
by the product of the standard complex scalar Gaussians has a polynomial
Tate product normalization on the line with each parameter equal to the
common spectral variable plus a fixed complex shift. The output degree
is bounded by the total degree of the test polynomial.

Expand the polynomial into its finite monomial support. Each monomial
and Gaussian product separates coordinatewise. Fubini and the scalar
polynomial normalization give a product of shifted one-variable
polynomials, including the factor one-half in each coordinate for the
conversion from twice-area to area measure. The identity theorem extends
the resulting polynomial to the entire spectral line.

This is a derived diagonal step for the square coefficient integrals
in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §4.7,
equations (4.3)–(4.4), author pp. 30–31. Proposition 8.1 in §8.4,
author p. 60, uses polynomiality for these coefficient integrals.
