# Ordinary and dual adjacent-rank integrals of a split Godement value

Let \(U,V\) be smaller-rank complex Whittaker functions with characters
\(\psi_{-a},\psi_a\), respectively. Take the larger function to be the
continued Godement value of a split Schwartz test with square factor
\(\Phi\), column factor \(\phi\), adjoining character \(d\), and shift
\(p\). In a common right half-plane, `complexAdjacentSplitIntegrals`
expresses its ordinary and dual adjacent-rank integrals as absolutely
integrable products on the smaller group times the smaller quotient.

The ordinary product uses \(\Phi\), the positive row transform of
\(\phi\), \(U,V\), and the adjoining determinant weight of shift
\(p+r/2\). Its outside scalar is \(d((-1)^{r+1})\). The dual product
uses the positive square transform of \(\Phi\), the original column
test \(\phi\), the actual duals of \(U,V\), and the inverse adjoining
character with shift \(-p+r/2\). The reflection sign and inverse
adjoining sign cancel in that formula.

The proof applies the continued Godement reflection and the split
Fourier identities to compute the padded restriction on the dual
side. It obtains the two bounds on the real part from the unfolded
convergence theorem and applies the adjacent change of variables to
each restriction. The corank-one dual integral is the ordinary
integral of the dual functions because the rectangular factor has
dimension zero.

These are the unfolded formulas in equations (8.3) and (8.4) of
Hervé Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, 2009, DOI
`10.1090/conm/489/09547`, with the sign arising from the Library's
upper-Borel convention.
