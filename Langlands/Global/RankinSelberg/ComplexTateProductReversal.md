# Reversal of the normalized product Tate integral

`ComplexTateProductReversalStatement` states that reversing the diagonal variables preserves the entire normalized product Tate value when the angular weights and exponents are reversed with them. The test is pulled back by the continuous real-linear coordinate permutation.

In the integral formula, that permutation preserves product Lebesgue measure. Reindexing the products of coordinate weights and reciprocal Gamma factors gives equality on the convergence region. The proof then chooses an affine spectral line through the given parameters whose exponents have real part one at parameter one. The two entire functions agree in a neighborhood of that point by the integral formula. The identity theorem gives equality at parameter zero.

This identity is used with the reversal of inducing data on the dual side of the matrix zeta functional equation. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, §4.7](https://doi.org/10.1090/conm/489/09547).
