# Borel-invariant cutoffs in complex Gauss charts

`ComplexBruhatCutoffStatement` gives a compactly supported coordinate test in any open chart set, whose invariant extension equals one near a given point over that set. Multiplying any smooth principal-series section by the cutoff produces the extension of another compactly supported chart test. The latter test has support inside the support of the cutoff in the coordinates.

Choose angular weights zero and choose radial exponents to cancel the normalizing Borel exponents. The resulting Borel multiplier is one, so the existing chart extension gives a smooth Borel-invariant scalar function. A smooth bump in a closed ball inside the coordinate neighborhood supplies the cutoff.

The general recovery lemma restricts a section along the smooth chart representatives. Continuity of the section map puts the support of the restriction inside the given compact coordinate set. Exact Borel covariance recovers the section throughout the chart, and both sides vanish off the saturation of the compact set.
