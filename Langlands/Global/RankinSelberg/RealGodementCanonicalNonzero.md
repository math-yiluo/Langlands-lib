# Nonvanishing of the canonical real Jacquet maps

`RealGodementCanonicalNonzeroStatement` proves that the canonical map for each nonzero real additive parameter and each character parity and exponent vector has a nonzero value on some K-finite standard vector.

In rank one the Jacquet integral is evaluation of the section itself. Apply it to the determinant character, whose value at the identity is one. In the rank step, take a lower standard vector with nonzero canonical coefficient. Its right derivatives are regular and have a uniform polynomial growth exponent. Compact polynomial averaging supplies a test of finite orthogonal orbit with nonzero leading value. The test and the compact restriction of the lower vector give an upper frame generator. The all-exponent canonical column formula identifies the upper coefficient at the long Weyl element with the chosen nonzero leading value.
