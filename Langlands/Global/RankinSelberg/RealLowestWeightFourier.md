# Fourier inversion for the real lowest-weight Jacquet integral

The reciprocal power of the line with positive real intercept that occurs
in a real rank-two lowest-weight open-cell formula has an integrable
Fourier transform. Its transform is a positive-half-line exponential
times a power. The positive power makes the function continuous at the
origin.

The forward Laplace integral is evaluated for a complex rate of positive
real part by integration by parts on the positive half-line. Its base
is the elementary exponential integral. Applying this formula to the
ordinary Fourier transform gives the reciprocal power. The reciprocal
power is integrable by comparison with a rescaled inverse quadratic,
so Fourier inversion gives the stated formula.

This is a scalar Fourier calculation for the discrete-series block
integrals of H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009),
§12. The calculation uses the kernel \(e^{-2\pi i x y}\).
