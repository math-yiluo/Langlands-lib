# The open-cell Jacquet integral of a real Godement section

For rank \(m=t+1\), `additiveJacquetIntegral` integrates \(f(w_m v k)\theta_{a,m}(v)\) over the upper unipotent entry Haar measure. The long Weyl element \(w_m\) puts the open cell in the upper-Borel induction convention. For a rectangular Schwartz test \(\Phi\), `godementOpenCellKernel` expands the Jacquet integral of the Godement section using the fixed lower-rank group Haar measure in its defining inner integral.

`RealGodementAdditiveJacquetStatement` assumes absolute integrability of this expanded kernel on the product of the upper unipotent measure and the lower group Haar measure. It gives integrability of the Jacquet integrand of the section, integrability of the first-column group integrand, and equality of the two values. The first-column formula uses the lower open-cell Jacquet integral evaluated at \(w_m h^{-1}\) and the upper translator \(w_{m+1}g\).

The proof uses the last-column chart and writes \(B=w_m v w_m\). The expanded integrand is transported by the changes \(h_{\rm old}=hB^{-1}\) and \(x_{\rm old}=By\) in the group and column coordinates. For each \(v\), right Haar invariance preserves the first measure and \(\det B=1\) preserves the second. The first row of \(B\) fixes the first-coordinate phase. The group argument becomes \(w_m v w_m h^{-1}\) and the matrix argument of \(\Phi\) becomes \(h[y,1]w_{m+1}g\), with \([y,1]\) the first-column join of \(y\) and the identity matrix. Product Fubini gives the lower Jacquet integral in \(v\), then the first-column integral in \(y\), and the existing first-column Mellin calculation gives the stated value.

This is the product change of variables and integration in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §7.2, pp. 47–48, from the open-cell integral in (7.4) through (7.6) to (7.7). Reversing the rectangle entries and replacing the source's additive parameter \(b\) by \(a=-b\) converts the source's last-column inverse phase to the positive first-column phase with the same self-dual measure. Joint absolute convergence in the chamber and continuation outside it are additional steps.
