# Square Schwartz products for the complex equal-rank unfolding

This internal construction realizes the product `Φ₀` of the rectangular and last-row tests in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §8.2, printed pp. 55–56. Splitting the last row gives a real-linear equivalence with two blocks. The finite Schwartz tensor on the blocks pulls back to the exact square product.

When both factors are polynomial Gaussians of the same positive scale, the sum of the squared entry norms splits over the blocks. Renaming the polynomial variables to those of the square matrix and multiplying the two polynomials gives a square polynomial Gaussian. The finite-dimensionality result for unitary translations of such tests supplies the compact projection premise.
