# Real equal-rank normalized tests for two discrete blocks

For two irreducible nonzero-weight real discrete blocks, a polynomial times a radial
Schwartz Gaussian cancels the difference of the lowest angular weights on
the rotation component of the compact group. The same Whittaker coefficient
has zero value on the reflected component at positive diagonal coordinates.

The two positive diagonal variables then contribute one exponential Mellin
integral and one Gaussian Mellin integral. Their Gamma shifts are half the
sum of the absolute weights and half their absolute difference,
as in H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §12.4.

For the diagonal \(\operatorname{diag}(A T,T)\), the quotient density in logarithmic
coordinates is \(A^{-1}\). The scalar covariance of the two coefficients
supplies the powers of \(T\). Compact Haar averages the angularly constant
rotation value with the zero reflected value. The Mellin kernels that
remain have decay \(e^{-4\pi A}\) and \(e^{-\pi T^2}\), respectively. The
conversion to the two complex Gamma factors introduces opposite spectral
powers of two. Their product depends only on the weights, so the quotient
measure constant and the other scalar factors are absorbed into one
Whittaker vector. This gives a single ordinary test with normalized value
one at additive scale one. Character transport gives coverage at every
nonzero real scale and a finite sum at both unit-absolute-value scales.
