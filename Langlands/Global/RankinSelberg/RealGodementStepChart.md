# The unipotent coordinates of the real Godement rectangle

The free entries of `[X,v]`, with `v` lower unitriangular, are the strictly lower entries of an `(m+1) × (m+1)` matrix after omitting its first row. Inserting that first row and conjugating by the long Weyl matrix reindexes the free entries onto the standard upper-unipotent chart. The diagonal entries and the entries forced to vanish agree with the corresponding fixed entries of the rectangle. The simple-root phase becomes the sum on the first superdiagonal.

The reindexing preserves the product of the real self-dual scalar measures. The existing scalar-entry chart transports that product to the indicated Haar measure on the actual upper-unipotent group. These are the coordinates and measures used to relate the Jacquet integral to the real upper-Borel version of equation (7.8) in Hervé Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, Section 7.2.
