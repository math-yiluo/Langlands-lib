# Appending one row to a real Rankin–Selberg rectangle

For each prefix of the rectangular variable in the unequal-rank integral, `point` fills the remaining rows with zero. The empty and full prefixes give ordinary padding and the rectangular point, respectively. The next row is inserted by right translation by the exponential of a square-zero lower row matrix. The prefix point is also the leading lower-block matrix padded by the remaining identity.

`RealRectangularRowActionStatement` proves the complementary upper-column action. Right multiplication by the column just after the filled rows, with entries from the first block, is left multiplication by an upper unipotent. Its Whittaker character pairs the column with the last filled row. This follows from conjugating the column by the leading block and evaluating its last entry.

These identities give the row coordinates and modulation for the rectangular integral in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), equation (2.5), p. 6. The modulation uses the column action behind Proposition 6.1(ii), pp. 36–37. [Author-posted edition](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
