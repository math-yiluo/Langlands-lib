# Strip growth for normalized compact matrix integrals

This internal result supplies the normalized matrix estimates in the finite expansions of Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §§8.2–8.3, printed pp. 57–60. It uses the positive standard trace Fourier transform and the matrix Haar measure of the existing compact matrix equation.

Compact integration transfers the averaging from each coefficient to its Schwartz test. The triangular reduction expresses the resulting two integrals by matched diagonal Schwartz tests and a common Haar scalar. Their multiplicative diagonal kernels agree with the product Tate kernels for the self-dual additive measure, and the denominator of each character list is the product of its scalar Gamma denominators. The entire product Tate normalizations with exponential strip growth therefore give corresponding normalizations of the two actual matrix integrals.
