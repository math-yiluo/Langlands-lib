# Fixed coordinates for the compact projection

This internal comparison is used to carry the compact averaging step of Jacquet §8.2 through the Whittaker map. A linear functional on a finite-dimensional space of compact function restrictions is a finite linear combination of evaluations. The proof uses injectivity of restriction to show that the evaluations span the dual space.

For an orbit of finite span, its compact average is defined by integrating each coordinate in a finite basis. The coordinates are continuous because they are finite linear combinations of evaluations. Every linear functional of the average therefore equals the average of its value on the orbit.

For a family of compact projections of arbitrary right translates, the finite expansion of the convolution kernel bounds all the restrictions by one finite-dimensional space. Injective restriction gives a finite-dimensional space of inducing vectors containing every projection. The finite evaluation formula and Haar left translation express any fixed functional of all these projections by one weighted compact integral. The weight is continuous and works for every original vector and every translating group element.
