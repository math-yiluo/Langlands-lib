# Compact profile bounds for complex Borel averages

For a fixed compact subset of the invertible complex matrices and a
fixed derivative order \(J\), the compact profile of a Borel-averaged
standard section has a finite-difference bound controlled by the
supremum of the source test seminorms of orders at most \(J\).
The multiplicative constant depends on the compact support, the
inducing character, the Haar measure and the order. The integration
uses a Borel left Haar measure on the second-countable upper
triangular subgroup.

On the unitary group inversion is the conjugate transpose. For a
Borel matrix \(b\), the profile integrand therefore extends to the
ambient matrices by composing the test with the real-linear map
\(x\mapsto x^* b\). Compact Borel transport gives a single compact
set of such parameters that can contribute to any test supported
in the chosen compact set. The operator norm of the real-linear
map is bounded there by a common constant. The chain rule transfers
the source seminorm bound to the matrix derivatives of this
extension, and the Library's compact finite-difference bound
transfers it to the integrand on the unitary group.

Finite differences commute with the integral by linearity and
integrability. They vanish outside the compact parameter set.
The continuous inducing weight is bounded on that set, whose Haar
mass is finite, so integrating gives the asserted bound. This
derived estimate is the continuity input for evaluating a smooth
standard-module Whittaker functional on averaged matrix tests.
