# Holomorphy of complex equal-rank integrals in Whittaker families

This internal result supplies local uniform convergence for analytic continuation of the equal-rank unfolding in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §8.2, printed pp. 54–56. It starts with continuous families of oppositely equivariant functions, pointwise holomorphy in the parameter, and uniform simple-root estimates on an open parameter set.

The simple-root ratios and the last diagonal coordinate control the positive parts of all logarithmic coordinates. Root decay from one coefficient and last-row decay from the Schwartz test absorb those positive parts on any bounded interval of spectral real parts. The common lower spectral threshold absorbs the remaining growth at negative coordinates. In the resulting majorant the toral density cancels against its exponential inverse. The parameterized integral theorem then gives integrability and holomorphy on the common right half-plane.
