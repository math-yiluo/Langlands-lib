# Strip growth of normalized ordinary local integrals

`OrdinaryIntegralNormalization.stripGrowth_of_meromorphic` starts with an entire normalizing function and a meromorphic continuation of the raw integral. If the raw continuation and reciprocal \(L\)-denominator have exponential growth on each strip, the normalized value has exponential growth there too. The equation on the initial right half-plane and the eventual nonvanishing of the denominator identify the normalization with the product of the raw continuation and denominator. The meromorphic identity principle extends this equality to the codiscrete filter. Continuity of the entire normalization passes the norm estimate to every point in the smaller strip, by taking punctured-neighborhood limits within a slightly wider strip and beyond the growth threshold.

The real and complex specializations use the proved estimates for the Gamma denominators. The hypothesis on the raw continuation corresponds to the strip estimate at large absolute imaginary part in Jacquet 2009, Theorem 2.1, published p. 62. For the finite-place normalizations, the Laurent evaluation at the local spectral point is bounded on a closed vertical strip. This supplies the same exponential bound with exponent zero.

`NormalizedProductStripGrowthStatement` records stability of the growth bound under a finite product and multiplication by a constant. Applied to the finite and infinite local normalizations, this gives the growth estimate for the normalized partial integral when its product formula and local growth inputs are supplied.
