# Reference pure tensors and one-place Whittaker coefficient maps

For the actual spectral tensor presentation of a cuspidal realization, one pure tensor has nonzero global Whittaker coefficient at the adelic identity. The identity evaluation is a linear functional. Its vanishing on every pure tensor would imply vanishing on the whole presentation by extensionality for the ordinary and indexed tensor products. Surjectivity of the presentation would then contradict nonzero global identity evaluation.

At any infinite place, freezing the finite vector, the other vector factors and the other group coordinates gives a linear map from the actual local factor to functions on the local group. The bundled map uses partial application of the indexed tensor product and the global Whittaker functional. Freezing through the pure reference tensor and dividing by its global identity value produces a local map whose identity value is one on the reference vector.

This construction is internal to the Library's actual spectral tensor presentation. Proportionality of the other slices to the reference maps is a further requirement for the factorization.
