# Right Lie differentiation of continued Godement values

The partially transformed Godement value has the same right Lie derivative formula as the standard section. The derivative acts on the original Schwartz test and adds the determinant-character slope of the shifted inducing character times the original value.

Along the right matrix exponential, the directional integration theorem differentiates the translated test by `Y ↦ Y X`. The outside determinant factor is the same multiplicative character as in the shifted standard section, times a constant sign factor. Its product rule contributes the same scalar derivative at the identity as in the standard section. Smoothness of the continued integral identifies the resulting scalar curve derivative with the Library's right Lie derivative.
