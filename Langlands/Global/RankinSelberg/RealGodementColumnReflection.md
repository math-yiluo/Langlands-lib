# Reflection of the real Godement first-column integral

For positive lower rank `m = t + 1`, `godementColumnValue` is the first-column Mellin integral over the supplied lower group Haar measure, with the upper determinant prefactor of shift `m/2`. It uses the column Fourier transform for the scaled real additive character `a` and a lower coefficient `W` of sign `−a`. This is the upper-Borel form of equation (7.7) in Hervé Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §7.2.

`realGodementColumnReflection` proves absolute integrability of the two iterated group integrands and the reflection identity for these values. The upper argument on the left is the long Weyl element times `g` inverse-transposed. On the right the argument is `g`, the parameter is `−a`, the test is the rectangular Fourier transform for `a`, the lower coefficient is the actual Weyl inverse-transpose dual of `W`, and the inducing character is inverted. The factor on the right is the inducing character evaluated at `(−1)^m`. The two lower functions are assumed to belong to the uniform real Whittaker models at their respective signs, with their respective parameter lists.

The proof applies `realGodementColumnQuotient` to both sides and uses `realGodementQuotientReflection` for the resulting quotient integrals. Each column value carries a comparison factor from the supplied subgroup measure to the self-dual entry-product measure. Negating `a` preserves the real self-dual measure and hence its entry product and pushforward to the unipotent subgroup. The Haar scalar factor then shows that the two comparison factors are equal. This gives the reflection at the level of the convergent column formula in Jacquet's Proposition 7.3, pp. 50–51. The identification of these values with coefficients of induced sections requires the Jacquet integral comparison.
