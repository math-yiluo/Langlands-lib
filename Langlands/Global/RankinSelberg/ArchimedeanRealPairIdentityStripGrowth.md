# Real ordinary local normalizations from Jacquet’s pair identity

`realPairIdentityNormalizedStripGrowth` gives an entire normalization of exponential strip growth for the exact real ordinary equal- and unequal-rank Rankin–Selberg integrals. It assumes a common right half-plane of absolute convergence of the original and dual kernels and the real pair identity with a nonzero sign. The dual kernels, factors, Fourier parameter and measure scale are those of `ArchimedeanRealPairIdentity`.

The proof uses the published formula expressing each kernel as its value at zero times a power of the absolute determinant. Positivity, measurability and the measure finiteness properties come from the same module. The reciprocal real pair denominators have exponential type on each strip and are nonzero far enough to the right. The closed epsilon formula shows that its norm depends only on the real part. With differentiability and nonvanishing of epsilon, these are the inputs of `pairIdentityNormalizedStripGrowth`. The two corollaries on uniform coefficient models supply the joint convergence from uniform Whittaker regularity and insert the signs and dual lists of the existing uniform pair-identity premises. The equal-rank corollary concerns positive rank index, so both general linear groups there have rank at least two.

This specializes the strip deduction from Hervé Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §4.2 and Proposition 4.1, author-version pp. 22–25, to the exact real integral and dual kernels of §2.1, (2.4)–(2.6) and Theorem 2.1(i)–(iii), pp. 6–7.
