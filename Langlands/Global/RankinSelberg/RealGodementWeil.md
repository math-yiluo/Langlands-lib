# The real Weil integral formula for integrable functions

The continuous Iwasawa coordinates define a representative of each real left unipotent coset by removing its unipotent factor. Left multiplication of that representative gives a homeomorphism from the product of the upper unipotent group and the quotient to the real general linear group. The inverse reads off the unipotent coordinate and quotient class.

The compatible quotient measure satisfies the Weil formula for compactly supported continuous functions. Under the homeomorphism, that formula identifies integrals of compactly supported continuous tests against the pushforward of the product and the given group Haar measure. Both measures are regular, so equality of those integrals gives exact measure preservation. Fubini now gives the Weil formula for each integrable complex-valued function, including the integrability of its orbit averages and absolute convergence on almost every unipotent fiber. Right invariance of the subgroup measure gives the fiber assertion at every representative of each such coset.

This supplies the quotient integration used in Hervé Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, Section 7.2, in the passage to equation (7.8).
