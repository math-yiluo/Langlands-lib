# Entire Jacquet continuation on the compact real Godement span

Fix strictly dominant lower real position exponents, a leading parity and a reference leading exponent. Every vector in the span of the Godement sections with smooth compact full-row-rank tests admits an entire continuation of its Jacquet values at fixed compact restriction. The continued family is complex-linear in the vector. At the reference exponent its values on the generating sections are the first-column Mellin values for the original Schwartz test and the lower open-cell coefficient.

For each generator the Gram-compensated test family realizes the fixed compact picture. Its entire column values agree with the Jacquet integral in the dominant half-plane. The convergent chamber integrals and the compact-picture extension are linear, so finite sums and scalar multiples have entire representatives. The complex identity theorem on each group evaluation gives uniqueness. Linearity and the formulas on all generators follow from this uniqueness.

The construction is derived from the real form of the Godement calculation in Hervé Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §7.2, equations (7.4)–(7.7), pp. 47–48.
