# Adjacent ordinary coverage from equal-rank Gaussian coverage

At any trace scale of unit modulus, `complexOrdinaryAdjacentCoverageStep` lifts pointwise coverage by standard polynomial Gaussian tests for the retained and opposite lists to pointwise coverage by ordinary adjacent-rank tests for the enlarged and opposite lists. The enlarged and opposite standard modules are assumed irreducible. The quotient datum is the same in the two integrals.

Fix a lower test with a nonzero normalized value at the spectral point. Take the standard polynomial Gaussian inverse row Fourier test. With the first lower coefficient and the row test fixed, evaluation of the normalized equal-rank integral at that point is a nonzero linear functional on the opposite standard module. The square Gaussian convolution values span this module, so a generator has nonzero value under that functional. Its split rectangular product has finite right unitary translate span, and the continued Godement model comparison supplies the larger Whittaker vector. The adjacent trace identity gives the desired nonzero normalized value. The phase \(d((-1)^{r+1})\) comes from the continued Godement normalization in `complexAdjacentSplitIntegrals`. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §8.3, equation (8.3), p. 58](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf#page=58)
