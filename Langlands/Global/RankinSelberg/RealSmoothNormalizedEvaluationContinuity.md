# Continuity of entire normalized evaluations on smooth real standard modules

A linear family of entire functions on smooth real standard vectors has
continuous evaluation at every spectral parameter if each evaluation is
continuous on one nonempty open set. In H. Jacquet, *Archimedean
Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), Theorem 2.3 (author-posted pp. 8–9)
states continuity of the original smooth integral maps and §12.2
(pp. 87–89) proves it. The Banach–Steinhaus and Taylor argument below
independently gives the separate evaluation continuity used in the
compact-finite passage of §15 (pp. 108–109), given the entire family and
the initial continuity.

The genuine smooth derivative topology is complete by the Banach
realization of the standard module. A finite Lie algebra basis makes
the topology metrizable, and first countability at zero makes its
additive uniformity countably generated. Baire's theorem then gives a
barrelled source. Banach–Steinhaus preserves continuity under pointwise
limits of continuous linear evaluations. Applied to the difference
quotients in the open set, this gives every Taylor coefficient about a
point of the set. Applied to the Taylor partial sums, it gives continuity
at an arbitrary point of the complex plane.
