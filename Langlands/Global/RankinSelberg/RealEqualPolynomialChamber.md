# Real equal-rank polynomial normalization in the Godement chamber

`RealEqualPolynomialChamberStatement` assumes the smaller adjacent-rank
polynomial conclusion for character lists at the opposite additive
parameter. For each fixed tail spectrum there is a leading real-part
bound. If the inducing parameters lie in the strict chamber and the
leading real part meets that bound, every canonical coefficient paired
with the supplied opposite-character uniform coefficient and standard
Gaussian row has polynomial normalization. Absolute convergence and the
polynomial identity hold on a right half-plane for the supplied equal-rank
quotient measure.

The canonical map for the fixed tail spectrum has a uniform growth
exponent. The unfolded-kernel estimate uses that exponent and the gauge
of the other coefficient to choose the leading bound before the
Godement section is chosen. The standard dilated matrix tests give the
same span as the scale-one tests by central covariance. Apply the
polynomial square calculation and equal-rank unfolding to those tests.
The two subgroup Haar comparisons and the leading Weyl sign contribute a
scalar to the polynomial. The submodule of integrable polynomial kernels
then gives the result for the full compact-finite span.

The equal-rank step follows Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg
Integrals*, equation (8.1) and §8.4, Proposition 8.1, pp. 60–61. The proof
uses the strict chamber and the canonical map to realize the sections
spanning the upper principal series.
