# Weighted unitary averages of compact Godement tests

The weighted integral over the unitary group is Schwartz by the compact average theorem. Right multiplication is a continuous family of linear maps on the rectangular matrices. The inverse family has bounded operator norm on the compact group, giving the uniform lower bound needed for the theorem.

For a compact test with full row rank on its support, the support of the average lies in the image of the original support times the unitary group under right multiplication by the inverse. This image is compact, and right multiplication extends each right inverse by multiplication on its left by the inverse translating matrix.

Left invariance of Haar measure moves a right translate of the averaged test to a left translate of its polynomial weight. The Library's unitary coordinate separation expresses these translated weights as a finite sum of products in the translating and integration variables. A single separated product gives a scalar multiple of one averaged function. The proof uses span induction and linearity of the integral to add the corresponding finite-dimensional spaces. This gives finite-dimensional span for the right unitary translates of the averaged test.
