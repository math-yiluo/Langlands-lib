# Comparison with the canonical real Jacquet evaluation

Every rank-matched list of real characters can be indexed by its parities and
exponents. Adding the normalization exponent at each position identifies that
list with the normalized parameter convention in the distribution proof of
Whittaker uniqueness. Thus two compact-derivative-bounded Whittaker forms on
this full smooth character principal series are proportional if the first is
nonzero.

In the strict dominant chamber the convergent Jacquet integral gives a bounded
form. Its group translates are the Jacquet coefficients, and the covariance
of that integral gives the indicated Whittaker character. The canonical
continued map agrees with the convergent map on that chamber and is nonzero.
Consequently every bounded smooth Whittaker form gives a single scalar times
the canonical coefficient map on compact-finite vectors.

The entry proposition is `RealCanonicalWhittakerComparisonStatement`. The
comparison lemma `functionalCoefficient_eq_smul_canonical` also applies at
any parameter where `HasBoundedCanonicalEvaluation` has been established.
This is the internal comparison between smooth evaluation and the canonical
coefficient maps used for the real Rankin–Selberg integrals.
