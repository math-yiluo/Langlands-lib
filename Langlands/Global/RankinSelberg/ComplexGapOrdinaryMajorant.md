# The joint pointwise majorant for the ordinary complex gap kernel

The separated ordinary kernel has a product majorant in the first quotient, the leading square matrix, the tail matrix, and the two rectangular matrices. The two independent first-block determinant powers can be chosen sufficiently large. The fixed power of the tail determinant can be arbitrary.

The translated smaller Whittaker norm contributes a power of the height of the leading square matrix. The leading Schwartz test adds this matrix's additive decay and the independent decay of the lower rectangle. Its cost in the right-tail height is bounded by a polynomial in the trailing columns after multiplying by the normalized determinant. The fixed residual tail determinant power is bounded exponentially by the absolute logarithmic tail coordinates.

The concatenated logarithmic Iwasawa coordinates give the simple-root estimate on the other Whittaker norm. Apply the ordinary-tail domination to obtain the tail matrix and upper rectangle decay. The remaining tail determinant factor cancels the Haar density, and the leading square majorant is already Haar integrable. The extra first-quotient exponential rate can absorb the linear density of its logarithmic toral measure.
