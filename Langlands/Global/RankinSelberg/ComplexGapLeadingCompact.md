# Compact span of the leading Gaussian restriction

The leading square restriction of a polynomial Gaussian in Jacquet’s Section 10.2 has finite-dimensional span under left translation by the retained unitary group. This is a derived result for applying the matrix-coefficient Godement–Jacquet equation to the square test in (10.10).

In the determinant-normalized chart, the retained matrix sends a column supported on the leading block to the compact matrix times that column scaled by the common leading scalar. The triangular tail factors fix all such columns. The leading scalar commutes with the smaller unitary group, so a left translation of the square input lifts to conjugation of the embedded unitary by the compact matrix of the chart. The restricted translates therefore belong to the linear image of the finite-dimensional unitary span of the original rectangular polynomial Gaussian.

`packedTail_stack` records the trivial action of the upper packed tail on a leading row stack with zero trailing rows.
