# Smoothness in the complex open-cell Jacquet chamber

`contMDiff_openCellIntegral` proves smoothness in the group argument for
the absolutely convergent open-cell Jacquet integral of a complex
principal-series function with strictly decreasing real exponents.
`rightDerivative_openCellIntegral` shows that a right derivative is
the open-cell integral of the right derivative of the inducing function
when that derivative is a vector in the same standard module.

Iwasawa decomposition writes the integrand as the function evaluated at
a compact translate, multiplied by the Borel factor and the integrating
character. The modulus of this multiplier equals the value of the
positive spherical majorant in absolute value. The chamber estimate
makes it integrable. The proof includes this modulus in the measure
and pushes the measure to the product of the compact unitary group
and the unit circle, where the second coordinate records the phase.
The resulting finite measure is the same for all vectors of the
given standard module.

In matrix coordinates the translate acts by left multiplication.
The compact linear-parameter integration theorem gives a smooth
extension on the open set of invertible matrices and its Fréchet
derivative. Applying that derivative to \(gX\) gives the integral of
the right derivative by associativity of matrix multiplication.

These are the group-variable properties of the open-cell construction
in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §7.2,
p. 47, in the absolute convergence chamber.
