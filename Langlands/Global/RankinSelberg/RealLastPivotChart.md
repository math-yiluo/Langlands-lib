# The real lower-block quotient chart with one trailing coordinate

For the real lower-block quotient chart with one trailing coordinate,
right multiplication by the inverse of the matrix with the prescribed
last row gives a matrix with final row equal to the final coordinate row.
Its leading block is invertible, and upper column elimination produces
exactly the lower-block representative. These recovery coordinates depend
continuously on the original matrix wherever its last entry is nonzero.
The left quotient projection is open, and the lower-block chart is
injective. Thus the chart is an open embedding with range equal to the
classes with nonzero final entry.

The open upper-unipotent times invertible lower-triangular cell in the
whole real group has full Haar measure. On that cell the final entry is
the final diagonal entry of its lower-triangular factor and is nonzero.
The compatible quotient measure is the image of group Haar weighted by a
normalizing function. It therefore assigns measure zero to classes with
zero final entry.

These are the chart properties for the matrices used in the quotient
integration in H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009),
Lemma 14.1, author-posted p. 106.
