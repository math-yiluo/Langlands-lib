# Compact angular profiles of real standard vectors

`realDiscreteLeviCompactProfile` applies the product Fourier estimate to a compact-finite real standard vector. Choose any list of pairwise disjoint slots of its inducing list with nonzero absolute weights. The angular profile evaluates the vector after the corresponding left rotations and at a supplied orthogonal matrix. If the norm of the vector on the orthogonal group is bounded by \(R\), the product cell integrand `angularIntegrand` is integrable. The norm of its integral is bounded by \(CR\prod_i |s_i|^{k_i/2}\). The constant is chosen for the vector and the slots before the bound \(R\), the orthogonal matrix and the real frequencies are supplied.

Rotations in disjoint slots commute. Replacing a single angle is therefore the same as rotating its slot on the left of the product with that angle set to zero. For a fixed orthogonal argument, each resulting coordinate slice is a trigonometric polynomial of degree uniformly bounded by compact finiteness. The standard-module cutoff and parity, supplied by `RealInducedStandardBound.inClass_slots`, eliminate the forbidden modes. Every rotated argument is still orthogonal, so \(R\) also bounds the profile. These are the hypotheses of `realDiscreteLeviFourierBound`.

The compact profile can be used as the angular part of the inner Jacquet integral after separating the real discrete blocks. This estimate is derived from the Library's standard-module and Fourier constructions. The open-cell integral and the tempered majorization that motivates the estimate appear in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §7.2 and Proposition 3.4. [Author-posted edition, pp. 21 and 47](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
