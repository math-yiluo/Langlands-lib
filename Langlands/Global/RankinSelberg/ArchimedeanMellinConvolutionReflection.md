# Combining independently scaled determinant reflections

A pair of kernels has a two-scale theta reflection when the ordinary separated integral at positive scales `a, b` equals `a * b` times the reflected separated integral at the two reciprocal scales. A common absolute moment of the product determinant on each chart justifies integration over scalings with fixed product.

The proof specializes the published `mconv` and `theta_mconv` to two profiles. The measure-preserving evaluation of the sole logarithmic coordinate identifies the one-coordinate product measure with Lebesgue measure. Inversion sends the log-scale average to the opposite coordinate. The convolution thus gives a single theta reflection in the product determinant, with the same Mellin transform and rapid-decay conclusions as the general convolution theorem.
