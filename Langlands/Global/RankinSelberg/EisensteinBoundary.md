# Constant terms on the negative-height scalar fibre

These internal lemmas implement the zero-vector terms in the pointwise Poisson argument
for the mirabolic Eisenstein series in Cogdell (2004), §2.3, printed p.209. The fibre
height is the logarithm of the determinant norm of the scalar translate of `g`.

The character bound and the affine expression for the height give an integrable
exponential majorant on a logarithmic half-line. The normalized idele measure pushes
forward to Lebesgue measure under the log norm. Translation by an idele of norm one
kills the boundary integral for a character nontrivial on the norm-one ideles. For a
norm-power character the boundary integral is a one-sided exponential integral, and
the factor from its endpoint cancels the spectral dependence of the numerator.
