# The infinitesimal Whittaker equation in a complex Bruhat chart

Right differentiation of an extended chart test is the extension of a first-order operator on the test. Its vector field is the derivative of the Gauss coordinates along right matrix multiplication. Its scalar coefficient is the derivative of the normalized inducing multiplier in that direction. Both coefficients are smooth in the lower coordinates. The operator preserves the support of the test.

At the chosen chart representatives the upper factor is the identity. The product and chain rules therefore give the stated first-order operator there. The defining standard-module covariance extends the equality to the whole chart. On its complement the original extension is locally zero by the closed support saturation, so its derivative also vanishes. Applying the smooth Whittaker derivative law and the chart distribution construction gives the coordinate distribution equation with the exact trace-character frequency.
