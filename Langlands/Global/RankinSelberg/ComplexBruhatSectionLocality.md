# Locality of smooth principal-series functionals

Suppose that at each point of a region some open Gauss chart set contains its coordinate and the functional kills extensions of all tests supported in that chart set. `ComplexBruhatSectionLocalityStatement` says the functional then vanishes on every smooth section with support in the region.

Intersect the support of a section with the compact unitary group. Choose local coordinate cutoffs with extensions equal to one near each point of that intersection. Compactness gives a finite subcover. Each cutoff times a section is an extension of a test in the corresponding chart set, so the functional kills that product. Successively subtract the cutoff products from the residual section. Each step makes the residual zero where the cutoff equals one, while retaining its zeros from earlier steps. The last residual vanishes on the unitary group, and Iwasawa decomposition and Borel covariance make it zero throughout the group.

A separate chart lemma identifies the saturation of the representative preimage of a Borel-saturated set with its intersection with the chart domain.
