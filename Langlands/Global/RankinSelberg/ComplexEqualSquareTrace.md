# The normalized equal-rank trace of a separated square convolution

`complexEqualSquareTrace` compares an equal-rank ordinary integral with the lower adjacent normalized functional applied to a polynomial square convolution. The larger first coefficient is formed by the continued Godement operator on an unlinked retained list. The square test separates into the rectangular Godement test and the row test. The formula holds at every spectral value, with a nonzero factor given by the inducing character at a sign matrix and the inverse of the positive comparison scalar for the quotient and group Haar measures.

In the adjoining chamber, Jacquet's equal-rank unfolding identifies the two integrals through the square convolution at the shifted spectral parameter. The continued Godement family is holomorphic in the adjoining shift, and the fixed spectral parameter can be taken far enough to the right to keep the ordinary equal-rank integral holomorphic on a disk containing zero and a point of the chamber. The identity theorem first brings the adjoining shift to zero. The resulting expression is entire in the spectral parameter because the square convolution is a polynomial family and the lower adjacent normalized values are entire. This gives the claimed ordinary normalization. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §8.2](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
