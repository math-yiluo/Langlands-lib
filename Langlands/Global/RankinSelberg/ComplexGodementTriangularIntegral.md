# Triangular integration of complex Godement sections

In strictly upper coordinates the change under right unipotent translation
adds a correction at each entry determined by entries closer to the
diagonal. The triangular translation theorem gives preservation of product
Lebesgue measure. Inversion turns this right invariant measure into left Haar
measure on the unipotent subgroup. Applying the complex Iwasawa formula to
the inverted group measure and then undoing inversion gives the Haar
formula in unitary, upper-unipotent and positive-diagonal order for the
supplied bi-invariant measure.

For a continuous test and a smaller standard vector, assume the Godement
integrand is absolutely integrable. Fubini in these coordinates puts the
unitary integral outermost. In each unipotent fiber, the column change of
variables gives additive Lebesgue measure on the strictly upper entries.
The column Jacobian combines with the induced vector and determinant factors
to give `diagonalTateWeight`. The resulting integral uses unitary Haar
measure, Lebesgue measure in the logarithmic positive diagonal, and
Lebesgue measure on the complex strictly upper entries. Its scalar
factor depends only on the two supplied Haar measures and the rank.

[Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, §7.1, p. 44](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf).
