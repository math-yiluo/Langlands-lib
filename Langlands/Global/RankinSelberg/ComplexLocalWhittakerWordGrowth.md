# Uniform growth of bounded right derivative words

This internal consequence of the matrix jet estimate controls the
coefficient of every standard-module derivative word when the individual
matrix directions have a common norm bound. The constant may depend on
word length and the chosen norm bound. The height exponent is independent
of the word and its length.

In the chart of a left translate, the direction \(X\) acts by
\(h\mapsto D u(h)[hX]\). At the identity the linear field \(h\mapsto hX\)
has its value and first derivative bounded by \(\lVert X\rVert\), and
higher derivatives vanish. The differentiated product estimate for
evaluation of a linear map therefore bounds each jet after applying one
direction by a finite sum of jets of the input. The intertwiner identifies
the resulting function on the open matrix chart with the coefficient of
the differentiated standard vector. Induction on the word gives a
constant for each pair of word length and jet order. Order zero gives
the coefficient bound.
