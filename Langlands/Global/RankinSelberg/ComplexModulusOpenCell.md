# Compact integration in the full opposite unipotent coordinates

For every natural rank the integral of a smooth modulus-equivariant principal-series section over the unitary group is a positive constant times its integral on the transposed upper unipotent matrices, with Lebesgue measure in the strictly upper entries before transposition. The section is absolutely integrable in those coordinates by the chamber convergence theorem at the modulus inducing parameters. The Weyl reflection of upper entries, followed by insertion of the long Weyl element on the right, gives exactly the transposed matrices.

In rank zero both measures have total mass one. In the induction step the maximal-parabolic integration formula separates the last row. The Levi slice belongs to the smaller modulus principal series. Its determinant factor is one on the compact and transposed unipotent arguments, so the induction hypothesis converts the smaller compact integral to the smaller unipotent integral. The resulting order of the last-row and Levi factors is related to the exact ordered unipotent product by a linear row change of variables with determinant one. That change preserves Lebesgue measure in each row fiber. Fubini and the exact entry-product measure give the full unipotent integral. The new constant is the smaller constant times the positive last-row chart constant.
