# Strip growth at the base of the equal-rank induction

`complexLocalEqualRankZeroGrowth` treats \(GL_1(\mathbb C)\times GL_1(\mathbb C)\) at the standard additive parameter \(a=1\), with parameter \(-1\) for the second Whittaker model. The dual trace uses the inverse inducing characters and the Library's positive trace Fourier transform at \(a=1\). The reflected equation uses the standard pair epsilon factor.

Both Whittaker coefficients are scalar multiples of their inducing characters on the determinant. For the ordinary and dual character pairs, `complexRankZeroStripGrowth` gives entire witnesses with exponential growth on vertical strips. Right-half-plane nonvanishing of the reciprocal Gamma products gives the agreement of each witness with the corresponding ordinary trace multiplied by its reciprocal \(L\)-factor. The published rank-zero local equation and the normalized meromorphic comparison give reflection for the entire witnesses at every \(s\).

The proof uses the fixed-character \(GL_1(\mathbb C)\times GL_1(\mathbb C)\) specialization of Jacquet (2009), Theorem 2.1, printed pp. 4–6. The exponential strip bounds on the entire normalized witnesses are the additional conclusion used in both rank steps.
