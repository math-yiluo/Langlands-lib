# Moving compact coefficient averages to the square Schwartz test

`ComplexSquareZetaCoefficientAverageStatement` moves a weighted compact average of left translates of a coefficient into the square Schwartz test. The compact variable acts by a continuous map into the unitary subgroup. The new test is the weighted average of the inverse left translates. Uniform norm bounds on the unitary matrices and their inverses make this average Schwartz.

The coefficient has a polynomial height bound. Submultiplicativity of height and compactness give an integrable majorant on the product of the compact group and the general linear group in the stated half-plane. The determinant weight is unchanged by unitary multiplication. Left Haar invariance supplies the change of variables in each group slice, and the integral of the absolute value on each slice is preserved. Fubini then proves the formula and the absolute integrability of the original square zeta kernel.

This change of variables is used for the matrix coefficients in Jacquet's square zeta equation. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, §4.7](https://doi.org/10.1090/conm/489/09547).
