# Real Godement Fourier inversion on the two Weyl slices

The lower unipotent chart for the upper triangular inducing convention has the free column first and the lower unitriangular block last. Its simple-root phase is the sum of the entries immediately before the fixed ones in each row. The two integrals in the theorem use that same chart with the real self-dual coordinate measure. One evaluates the Schwartz test after right multiplication by the upper long Weyl matrix and uses the positive phase. The other evaluates the full positive rectangular Fourier transform after left multiplication by the lower long Weyl matrix and uses the negative phase. Both integrands are absolutely integrable, and their integrals agree.

Reversing the columns permutes the free coordinates onto the entries with index sum at least the lower rank. Reversing the rows permutes them onto the complementary set. The adjacent antidiagonals of ones are the fixed coordinates, and their entry pairings give the two phases. The coordinate permutations preserve the product measures. The rectangular slice identity then gives the result. This is the real upper-Borel version of Hervé Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, Section 7.3, Lemma 7.1, with the Library's positive Fourier sign.
