# Angular variables in the complex square zeta integral

`ComplexSquareZetaPhaseStatement` replaces the logarithmic positive diagonal in the upper-Borel square matrix zeta integral by the full complex diagonal with multiplicative Lebesgue measure. The strictly upper entries retain their additive measure, and the group-to-diagonal constant includes the logarithmic radius factor `2π` in each diagonal coordinate.

The proof absorbs a diagonal phase into the compact factor by left translation. Multiplying the upper triangular matrix on the right by the same phase multiplies the off-diagonal entries by their column phases, which preserve additive area. The angular factor from the standard module supplies precisely the diagonal phase of the multiplicative zeta weight. Logarithmic radii then give the same inner integral on a set of full multiplicative measure.

This is the phase and radial measure step for Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §4.7, equations (4.3) and (4.4), pp. 30–31. The formulation retains absolute integrability of the original group kernel.
