# Finite orthogonal orbit tests detect real Godement coefficients

`RealGodementPolynomialTestStatement` starts with a nonzero regular lower coefficient whose right derivatives have a common polynomial growth exponent. At each leading parity and complex exponent it gives a Schwartz test of compact full-row-rank support with finite-dimensional right orthogonal orbit span. The leading value of the test at the upper long Weyl element is nonzero.

First choose a compact test giving a nonzero value. All its orthogonal pullbacks have support in the image of the product of its compact support and the orthogonal group under inverse right multiplication. This image is compact and consists of full-row-rank matrices. The leading value at the long Weyl element is a first-column Mellin linear functional bounded by a continuous Schwartz seminorm. On the compact pullback family it is continuous and commutes with the weighted average. Its value at the identity is nonzero, so polynomial density supplies an averaging weight with nonzero integral pairing. By left Haar substitution the right translates of the averaged test are averages with the left translates of the weight. The separated-variable formula for an orthogonal polynomial confines those weights to a finite-dimensional subspace. Its linear image under averaging contains the right orbit of the test.
