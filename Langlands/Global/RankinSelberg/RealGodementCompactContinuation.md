# Leading-exponent continuation on real compact types

Fix the lower inducing data with strictly dominant position exponents, the parity of the leading character, and an upper group element. `RealGodementCompactContinuationStatement` gives an entire family of linear forms in the leading exponent. The domain is the intersection of the smooth position principal series restriction image with a fixed finite-dimensional space of functions on the upper orthogonal group that is stable under left translation.

The forms give the column Mellin value for every family with a fixed smooth lower principal-series vector and a fixed smooth test supported in a compact set of full-row-rank matrices, provided its compact restrictions belong to the prescribed ambient space at every leading exponent. If the real part of the leading exponent exceeds the real part of the first lower exponent, the forms recover the original upper open-cell Jacquet integral. The scalar continuation for a fixed compact restriction of a right-K-finite vector is unique.

Finitely many point evaluations express the coordinates of each compact column as linear combinations of entire section values. The columns span at each exponent. In the dominant half-plane, the compact restriction lift turns the original Jacquet integral into a linear form. Joint absolute convergence identifies its values on the columns with the separately established entire column Mellin integrals. Analytic descent through the spanning columns then constructs the family on the whole complex plane. The complex identity theorem gives scalar uniqueness.

The chamber and column formula come from Jacquet's equations (7.3)–(7.7) and Proposition 7.2. Jacquet invokes Wallach II, Theorem 15.4.1, for analytic continuation before identifying the continued coefficient with (7.7). The Lean proof constructs the continuation by finite-dimensional descent for a single varying exponent.
