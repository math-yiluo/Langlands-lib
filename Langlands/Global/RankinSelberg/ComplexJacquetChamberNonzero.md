# Nonvanishing of the complex open-cell Whittaker functional

`ComplexJacquetChamberNonzeroStatement` asserts that the exact chamber intertwiner has a compact-finite standard vector whose Whittaker value at the identity is nonzero. The inducing exponents have strictly decreasing real parts. The measure is the product entry Haar measure built from the given additive Haar measure of `ℂ`. The integral uses the character with parameter `a`, so the intertwiner has left character with parameter `−a` when the inducing list has the required length.

For an upper unipotent matrix `u`, write `k(u)` for the unitary part of `w u` in the positive-diagonal Iwasawa decomposition. The proof uses the weight `q(u) = ∏ᵢ |k(u)ᵢ,rev(i)|²`. Each row of a unitary matrix has squared norm one, so `0 ≤ q ≤ 1` and equality at one forces every row to be supported on its Weyl pivot. A unitary matrix with that support lies in the upper Bruhat cell of `w` and has identity unipotent coordinate. The compact part of `w u` also lies in that cell and has coordinate `u`, since it differs from `w u` by multiplication on the left by an upper triangular matrix. Continuity of the published Bruhat coordinate then excludes every matrix of weight one from the closure of the compact parts associated to the complement of any open neighborhood of `1`. Compactness gives a uniform upper bound `c < 1` on that complement.

`phaseFamily` uses the published phase vectors with the Weyl element on the right. Adding `j` to both powers at each pivot multiplies the compact restriction by the `j`-th power of the pivot product. Borel covariance gives the corresponding multiplication by `q(u)^j` at `w u`. For the vector with `j = 0`, the open-cell integrand `F` is continuous, absolutely integrable and equals `1` at `u = 1`. The proof chooses a compact neighborhood `L` of `1` where `Re F > 1/2` and `q > d` for some `c < d < 1`. It bounds the real part of the weighted integral below by `ν(L) d^j / 2 − c^j ∫ |F| dν`, which is positive for some `j` since `(c/d)^j` tends to zero. The corresponding compact-finite vector therefore has nonzero Jacquet value. The integral estimate is factored as `exists_pos_integral_pow_of_peak` for reuse with other continuous peak weights.

This is internal infrastructure for the chamber in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §7.2, p. 47. The argument uses compact localization specific to the oscillatory open-cell integral. It proves nonvanishing in the absolute-convergence chamber. Continuation and uniqueness of Whittaker intertwiners are separate prerequisites for the local functional equation.
