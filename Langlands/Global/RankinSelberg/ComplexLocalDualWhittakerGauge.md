# The simple-root estimate for Weyl inverse-transpose Whittaker functions

`ComplexDualWhittakerGaugeStatement` gives fixed exponential growth in
the sum of absolute Iwasawa logarithms and arbitrary decay in the sum
of positive simple-root logarithms for `ArchimedeanLocal.dualFunction`
on the exact complex Whittaker model. The bound holds throughout the
complex matrix group.

The proof applies the opposite-character covariance of the dual
function to remove the unipotent factor in norm. Weyl inverse transpose
reverses and negates the logarithms of a positive diagonal and takes
the unitary factor to another unitary factor. The sum of absolute
logarithms is preserved, and reversing the adjacent root indices
preserves the sum of their positive parts. The original simple-root
bound therefore applies with the same growth exponent and decay order.

This combines Jacquet's dual-function convention on pp. 4–5 with
the majorization in §3.2, pp. 15–16. It derives the bound directly for the
transformed function. The rectangular matrix estimates and integration
are further steps for the larger-gap dual kernel.
