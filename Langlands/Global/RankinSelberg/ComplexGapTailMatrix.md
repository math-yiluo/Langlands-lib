# Forward and inverse matrices of the complex right tail

The upper rectangle is the block matrix with identity diagonal blocks, the given upper rectangle, and a zero lower rectangle. This follows from the radical coordinates and the block triangularity condition. The right-tail chart is obtained by multiplying this matrix by the tail diagonal block and the unitary factor on the left.

In the resulting block matrix the lower-right block is the tail group variable. In the inverse it is the inverse tail group variable, and the upper rectangle is the negative of the original rectangle multiplied by that inverse. The proof multiplies the two displayed block matrices and uses the inverse identity of the tail group variable. The unitary factor moves to the right in the inverse.

The trailing columns bound the rectangular coordinate and the additive tail norm. Applying the inverse unitary multiplies these bounds by at most the ambient rank in the entrywise norm. The leading columns of the chart matrix are columns of the unitary factor. The determinant modulus of the right tail equals the determinant modulus of its tail block.
