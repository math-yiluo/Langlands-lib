# Whittaker evaluation on genuine translates

A polynomial weight on the unitary matrix group has finite left
translation span, by the separated expansion of its translates. Carry
that weight and Haar measure to the compact subgroup of the general
linear group. The exact compact projection theorem then applies to a
genuine group translate of a compact-finite vector and gives the
simultaneous convolution of the standard vector and its Whittaker
function. Pulling the integrals back writes both in unitary matrix
coordinates.

Apply this projection with central nonnegative polynomial weights of
unit mass and arbitrarily small first displacement moment. The compact
profile of the projected standard vector approaches that of the
genuine translate in every fixed difference bound. At the same time
the projected Whittaker identity value approaches the original
Whittaker value at the translating group element. For this second
estimate use the zeroth-order convolution error at identity on the
translated Whittaker function. A finite-order smooth extension thus
gives the original Whittaker value on the genuine translate.
