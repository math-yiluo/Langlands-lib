# Undoing determinant normalization on the two leading variables

The determinant-one chart multiplies the retained matrix on the right by a positive scalar in its leading block. The change of variables translates the smaller quotient on the right by that scalar, and multiplies the leading group variable on the left by its inverse. On the Fourier side it also applies inverse transpose to the leading group variable.

The scalar depends only on the logarithmic tail, which the change fixes. At each tail value, quotient right invariance and group left invariance give the two measure-preserving maps. Inverse transpose also preserves the complex group Haar measure. The product-measure formulas and Fubini give the stated measurable equivalence of the full triangular product measure.
