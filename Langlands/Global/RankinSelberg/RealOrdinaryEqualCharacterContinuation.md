# Equal-rank real ordinary continuation with weight-zero induced blocks

`RealOrdinaryEqualCharacterContinuationStatement` is the equal-rank clause of the exact real ordinary continuation statement for lists of real characters and weight-zero induced blocks. The exponents may be arbitrary. Every pair of exact model vectors with opposite additive scales and every Schwartz test has an entire ordinary normalization by the prescribed pair factor, for every compatible quotient measure.

For a weight-zero block the two position characters have the same exponent and opposite parities. Position expansion therefore agrees with the operation that splits these blocks while preserving the pair factor. Ascending expansion permutes the position characters and also preserves this factor. Equality of the exact and uniform models and the ascending principal-series quotient place the original coefficients in the uniform ascending models.

The equal-rank character growth theorem from the canonical range and evaluation supplies the entire expanded normalization. `HasRealPairNormalizedGrowth.ordinaryNormalization` divides the identity on the right half-plane by the nonvanishing reciprocal denominator there. The factor comparison identifies the resulting normalization with the one prescribed for the original lists. This gives the holomorphic-multiple conclusion of Jacquet's Theorem 2.1(ii) for the ordinary equal-rank integral (2.6) on these coefficient models with weight-zero induced blocks. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, author p. 6](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
