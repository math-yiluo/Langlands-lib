# The continued complex Godement value and the quotient recursion

At an actual adjoining character \(d\), `complexGodementRecursionBridge`
identifies the zero-shift value of the continued Godement column integral
with the complex quotient recursion. For a smaller Whittaker function
of character \(\psi_{-a}\), the recursion has parameter \(-a\). The
factor relating the two values is \(d((-1)^{r+1})\). The group and
unipotent Haar measures in the quotient formula come from the given
compatible complex quotient measure data.

The proof uses the last-column Fourier identity to identify the
single-group recursion kernel at \(h^{-1}\) with the iterated column
kernel at \(h\), times the outer determinant weight. The published
absolute convergence of the column integral supplies integrability for
the recursion kernel by Haar inversion invariance. Weil integration then
identifies the group integral with the quotient recursion, and the
definition of the continued value supplies the sign.

This compares the two forms of the integral in Sections 7.2 and 7.3 of
Hervé Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, 2009, DOI
`10.1090/conm/489/09547`.
