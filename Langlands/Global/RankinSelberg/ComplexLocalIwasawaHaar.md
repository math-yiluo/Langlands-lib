# Haar measure in complex Iwasawa coordinates

The positive triangular subgroup is the fiber of the unitary coordinate over
one. In diagonal-then-unipotent order its coordinates are `(y, u)`, where `y`
is the vector of logarithms of the positive diagonal entries. Left
translation adds a fixed vector to `y` and left translates the unipotent
coordinate by an element depending on `y`. The product of logarithmic
Lebesgue measure and unipotent Haar measure is invariant under these changes.
The coordinate homeomorphism transports it to a left Haar measure on the
positive triangular subgroup.

The compact factorization formula combines that Haar measure with a supplied
Haar measure on the unitary group. The resulting measure on the full group
agrees with the supplied group Haar measure up to a positive constant. These
coordinates give the Haar integration step for the triangular reduction of
complex Godement sections in Jacquet's proof of Proposition 7.1.

[Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, §7.1, p. 44](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf).
