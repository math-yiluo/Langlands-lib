# Compact-finite approximation in compact difference bounds

Right convolution of a smooth compact profile with a polynomial weight
has finite-dimensional right orbit. After the Haar change of variables,
the translated weights have a finite separated expansion. Integrating
its terms against the profile places every translated average in the
span of finitely many functions. The compact parameter integral is
continuous. Right convolution preserves the left phase-torus
transformation law. The compact extension theorem therefore gives a
compact-finite standard section with the averaged compact profile.

Choose a central nonnegative polynomial weight with Haar integral one
and small first displacement moment. Centrality allows the convolution
to be written using left multiplication, so it commutes with each
compact right difference. After applying a list of up to J differences,
subtract the unaveraged value inside the Haar integral. The result is
bounded by the order-(J + 1) difference bound times the product of the
list displacements and the first moment of the weight. This gives the
asserted order-J approximation with arbitrarily small error.

The compact-finite extension agrees with the actual right convolution
on the full general linear group. The convolution inherits the left
Borel transformation law from the input, so Iwasawa decomposition
extends equality of compact profiles to equality everywhere.
