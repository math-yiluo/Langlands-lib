# Normalized actual Whittaker intertwiners at the infinite places

Let an actual cuspidal realization be given over a number field with
compact additive adelic quotient. For a continuous nontrivial unitary
adelic additive character trivial on the diagonal field, choose a real or complex place, the
compatible nonzero additive scale, and a standard parameter for that
place. There is a compact and Lie intertwining map from the indicated
compact-finite standard module to the realization and an ordinary local Whittaker
intertwiner on that same module. The latter takes the value one
at the identity on some source vector. All its coefficient functions
are the restrictions of the image vectors' actual global Whittaker
functions along the chosen place, with finite coordinate one.

This is a derived result using the Library's proofs of adelic identity
nonvanishing, place isotypy, and archimedean Whittaker regularity. Identity
evaluation is a nonzero linear functional on the realization. The images
of the place intertwiners span that realization by the isotypy theorem
for the chosen parameter. If the evaluation vanished on all these
images, their sum would lie in its kernel. The proof therefore chooses
one map and one source vector with nonzero evaluation and rescales that
vector to make the evaluation one. The real and complex restriction
intertwiner theorems then give the ordinary Whittaker map and its
coefficient formula at every point.
