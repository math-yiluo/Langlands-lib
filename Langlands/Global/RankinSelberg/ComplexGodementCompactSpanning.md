# Compact Schwartz Godement sections span the standard module

Fix right-invariant Haar measure on the smaller complex general linear group. Let the larger upper Borel standard module be induced from an added character followed by a list of retained characters of length equal to the smaller rank. Its compact-finite submodule is the complex linear span of Godement sections with compact-finite smaller standard input and compactly supported Schwartz tests of full row rank whose right unitary translates span a finite-dimensional space.

Lean proves membership of each generator using smoothness, covariance and compact finiteness of the Godement section. For the converse, consider the finite-dimensional span of unitary translates of the target vector. Each fixed choice of admissible cutoff and smaller input gives a linear Godement operator on that span. The compact separation theorem shows that the common kernel of their evaluations at the identity is contained in the kernel of evaluation at the identity. Finite-dimensional dual annihilator duality puts the identity evaluation in the span of the operator evaluations.

The cofactor right-translation formula and the determinant weight in the Godement section give equivariance under the larger unitary group. Evaluating the dual-span relation on all compact translates shows that the compact restriction of the target vector belongs to the restrictions of the Godement span. The Iwasawa decomposition gives equality of standard vectors with the same compact restriction, so the target vector belongs to that span on the whole larger group.

This is a compact Schwartz version of the generation assertion in Jacquet's Proposition 7.1(v), using an invariant cutoff and finite-dimensional separation. The test class in this proposition is specified by smoothness, compact support in full row rank, and compact finiteness.

Hervé Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §7.1, Proposition 7.1(v), pp. 44–46.
