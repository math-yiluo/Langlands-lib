# Product determinant powers on the full dual chart

The weighted full dual Godement kernel is jointly integrable when both determinant profiles are the same complex power and its real part is sufficiently large. This supplies the absolute product-determinant moments for the scale integration and the spectral chart limits.

The right-tail estimate needs one power bound for each radial profile. The proof allows either exponent to increase above the corresponding threshold, while the determinant of the retained matrix contributes only a fixed real power to the majorant. The same Haar changes and dual pullback transport the bound to the full dual chart. Taking both exponents to be the real part of the Mellin parameter gives the result, since the determinant arguments are positive. The argument is the power-weighted form of the convergence in Jacquet's Lemma 10.6.
