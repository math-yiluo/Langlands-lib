# Nonvanishing of the continued Godement value on a localized test

Let the smaller coefficient be continuous and nonzero at some group argument.
With the Haar and inducing data fixed, `ComplexGodementLocalizedNonzeroStatement`
constructs a compactly supported rectangular Schwartz test for which the
continued Godement value at the identity is nonzero. Its support lies in the
full-row-rank locus, and the added exponent is arbitrary.

The open chart consists of the rectangular matrices whose first square block
is invertible. In this chart the kernel before the column Fourier integration
is a continuous amplitude in the smaller matrix and the last column. At the
inverse of a group argument where the coefficient is nonzero, and with last
column zero, this amplitude is nonzero. Multiply the integrand by the conjugate
of that value. Its real part is then positive in an open neighborhood of the
chosen point. Use a smooth nonnegative bump equal to one there and supported
in a compact subset of that neighborhood. Every matrix in its support has a
right inverse obtained from its first block. On the product of the smaller
group and the column space, the resulting integrand has compact support, and
its real part is nonnegative everywhere and positive at the chosen point.
The product Haar measure gives positive mass to open sets, so its integral is
nonzero. Fubini and the partial Fourier formula identify the integral with
a nonzero scalar multiple of the continued value at the identity.

This internal result applies localization to the continued formula in Jacquet,
*Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §7.2, formula (7.7), printed p. 48.
