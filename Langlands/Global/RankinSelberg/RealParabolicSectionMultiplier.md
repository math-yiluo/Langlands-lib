# Block Fourier operators on compact parabolic sections

`realParabolicSectionMultiplier` compares an ambient block operator with a local operator on one Levi group. They act on the genuine smooth real standard carriers. The relevant ambient rotation lies inside the chosen Levi block, and the two operators have the same Fourier symbol. Applying the ambient operator to a compact transverse section with pure Levi inputs gives another section with the same test, with the local operator applied to the chosen input. The two conclusions cover the trailing and leading blocks in upper-Borel order.

At a fixed ambient point, the normalized slice computes the ambient block coefficient from the corresponding Levi coefficient by evaluating the slice Fourier identity at the Levi identity. `RealParabolicLeviSlices` expresses the slice of the original section and of the section with the transformed input using the same coordinates and scalar. The two coefficient formulas agree after multiplying by the common symbol. Fourier uniqueness at the fixed point gives equality of the reconstructed standard functions and hence of the genuine smooth vectors. This derives the operator comparison for the compact sections used in [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, Proposition 14.1, pp. 103–104](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf#page=103).
