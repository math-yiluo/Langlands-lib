# Logarithmic Iwasawa coordinates in the right-tail chart

The diagonal-block embedding concatenates the logarithmic Iwasawa coordinates of its two factors. The proof embeds the two upper unipotent factors and the two unitary factors blockwise, then identifies the positive diagonal factor entry by entry.

The restriction kernel evaluates the smaller Whittaker function at the first-block argument multiplied by the inverse of the right-tail chart factor. Conjugating the upper rectangle by the first block gives another upper unipotent matrix. The logarithmic coordinates are invariant under this left multiplication and under the final unitary factor on the right. The remaining two blocks are the first-block argument and the inverse of the tail group variable.
