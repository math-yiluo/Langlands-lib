# Uniform integrable decay for the alternate triangular columns

The complex larger-gap Fourier kernel uses the columns at the earlier tail pivots of the inverse transpose. Their joint norm bounds every strict upper entry of the inverse unipotent argument. Multiplication by the transpose of the retained matrix recovers the original columns and contributes one matrix-height factor.

The proof bounds the Fourier transforms by Schwartz seminorms of a common order. It retains a power of the retained matrix height and a radial negative power in the strict upper entries. The upper-entry homeomorphism sends entry Haar to the product of the scalar Haar measures. This measure is invariant under inversion, and the radial power is integrable when the order exceeds the real dimension of the entry space.
