# Compact-finite detection in the continued Godement integral

The proof starts with a compact full-row-rank test with nonzero value at the identity. The translated outer integral gives a continuous function on the unitary group and is nonzero at the identity. The density of the unitary coordinate polynomials supplies a polynomial weight whose integral against that function is nonzero. The weighted Schwartz average has compact full-row-rank support and finite unitary translate span.

To evaluate the average, the proof first exchanges the compact and column integrals at each fixed element of the smaller general linear group. The translated tests there have jointly compact support in the column and unitary variables, since the inverse of the smaller group element recovers the column from the unitarily translated support. For the outer exchange, the exact iterated kernel is jointly measurable from its column-integral expression. The translated Godement bounds give an integrable majorant independent of the compact variable, where the weight and the translation size are bounded. Two applications of Fubini identify the averaged value with the polynomial-weighted integral of the translates. The original nonzero value implies that the common scalar at the identity is nonzero, completing the argument.
