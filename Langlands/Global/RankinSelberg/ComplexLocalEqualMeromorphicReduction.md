# Reduction to the meromorphic continuation and equation

`ComplexLocalEqualMeromorphicReductionStatement` implies the published
complex equal-rank local functional equation from two explicit premises.

- `ComplexLocalEqualPrimalContinuationStatement` gives a meromorphic
  continuation of each primal integral, agreeing on a right half-plane
  after the integral is known to converge there.
- `ComplexLocalEqualMeromorphicEquationStatement` gives the codiscrete
  identity for agreeing primal and dual continuations, with the factors
  and sign in the published proposition.

The proof supplies `ComplexLocalWhittakerUniformGrowthStatement` by the
finite-system elliptic estimate for Whittaker intertwiner coefficients.
The coordinate square operator preserves a finite-dimensional subspace
and transfers to a common matrix-chart differential operator on the
translated coefficients. The interior estimate bounds all matrix jets
with one height exponent. The right derivative word estimate then gives
one height exponent for words of every length.

The compact unitary factor bounds the norm of each of the finitely many
conjugated simple-root directions. On \(a(x)k\), with
\(a(x)=\operatorname{diag}(\exp(x_i))\), submultiplicativity and the diagonal
entries bound the matrix height by \(H\exp(2\sum_i |x_i|)\) for a constant
\(H\) independent of \(x,k\). Substituting this in the word estimate gives
the stated uniform-growth premise. The earlier convergence reduction now
supplies the complete implication with just the two meromorphic premises.

This is an internal proof of the growth input needed for the absolute
convergence argument in Jacquet's *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*,
§3.2, and of its use in the Library's form of Theorem 2.1. The fixed
elliptic-system estimate supplies uniformity for the Library's
intertwiner model, whose definition gives separate height bounds on
individual vectors.
