# Continued Godement generators with uniform Jacquet estimates

The module continues the Jacquet values of each compact full-row-rank Godement test, keeping the angular weights and the smaller compact profile fixed. The input is a continued smaller-rank family with group continuity, pointwise entire exponent dependence, local uniform simple-root estimates and strict-chamber agreement.

The partially Fourier-transformed integral gives joint holomorphy in the inducing exponents at each fixed group argument. The exact chamber comparison identifies it with the open-cell integral there. The left Whittaker law follows for all exponents by restricting the difference to an affine complex line from a chamber point and using the scalar identity theorem. Group continuity follows from the translated integral estimate. On a small parameter ball the real determinant exponent is bounded, and the continued left law gives arbitrary simple-root decay by the uniform Godement root estimate.
