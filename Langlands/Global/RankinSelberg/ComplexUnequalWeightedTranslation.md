# Weighted unequal reflection for fixed right translates

The normalized unequal-rank equation with strip growth on the finite Whittaker models gives the rapidly weighted identity for every fixed pair of right translates. On the ordinary side the determinant is scaled by a positive real number. On the reflected side it is scaled by its reciprocal, and the weight carries the corresponding outside scalar. The theorem also gives absolute convergence on both sides.

The proof applies the complex Mellin comparison to the finite-model kernels. The product form of the reflected integral agrees with the iterated integral on a sufficiently far right half-plane by Fubini. A compact family of right translates has a common integrable bound for each of the rapidly weighted kernels. The finite-model approximation theorem uses those bounds to pass the identity to the chosen fixed translates.
