# Unipotent equivariance of the smooth complex Whittaker extension

Extend the given exact identity evaluation to smooth standard sections
with its finite compact difference order. On genuine translates of a
compact-finite vector the extension already recovers the exact
Whittaker value, so the exact unipotent character law holds on each
such vector.

For a smooth section and a fixed unipotent element, choose a single
compact-finite approximant before and after that right translation.
The norm of the error in the character law is bounded by the sum of
the two evaluation errors, with the untranslated one multiplied by
the norm of the character value. Both errors can be made arbitrarily
small in the order controlling the extension. This proves the exact
character law on the full smooth standard module.
