# The equal-rank unfolding from product integrability

This internal comparison justifies the Weil and Fubini steps in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §8.2, printed pp. 54–56. It uses continuity and integrability of the final quotient-by-group kernel. It also assumes the first coefficient's unipotent recursion and the second coefficient's opposite left character.

Padding the smaller representative gives the change of variables in the group kernel. It cancels the two determinant characters, and subtracting the determinant shifts gives the smaller exponent `s − 1/2`. With the continuous Iwasawa representative this translation acts on each group fiber by a Haar-preserving shear. The parameterized Weil integrability theorem therefore applies to its orbital averages.

The group kernel written with the Library's chosen representatives agrees in the orbital integral with the one using the continuous sections. The two changes of representative act by left and right translations within the larger unipotent group and preserve its Haar measure. Fiberwise group integrability follows from the same change of variables. These two integrability facts discharge the hypotheses of the published unfolding comparison.
