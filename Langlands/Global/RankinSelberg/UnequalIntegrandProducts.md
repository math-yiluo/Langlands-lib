# Ordinary unequal-rank integrand products

`PairedWhittakerIntegrandProductStatement` gives the ordinary unequal-rank quotient kernel on the partial domain as the product of the real, complex and retained finite kernels. It assumes a paired Whittaker product on that domain with scalar `κ` and left-unipotent invariance of each local paired product. The kernel identity uses the same `κ` for every spectral parameter. Here the two ranks are `r + 1` and `t + 1` with `t < r`.

The proof evaluates the global kernel on a representative of the quotient class. The determinant-power product separates the weight with exponent `s - (r - t)/2`. Paired invariance and the unit determinant of upper unitriangular matrices allow the local kernels to use any representative of each projected quotient class. Multiplying the factors then gives the identity. This is the ordinary kernel step of the Euler factorization in Cogdell (2004), §4.1, printed pp. 219–220 (registered PDF pp. 222–223).

For actual opposite-character Whittaker functions, `unequalPair_invariant` proves cancellation on the padded smaller unipotent. `unequalIntegral_eq_blockZero` compares the ordinary local quotient integral with `unequalRankIntegralAt 0 k`. Their matrix inclusions agree entry by entry, and the two determinant weights agree for the positive normalized absolute value. The measure of the zero-dimensional rectangle is the unit point mass, so the rectangular integration evaluates its integrand at zero. The block-zero comparison implements the ordinary unequal-rank integral in Jacquet, Piatetski-Shapiro and Shalika (1983), §2.4, display (3), printed p. 387 (registered PDF p. 22).
