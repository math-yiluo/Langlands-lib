# Joint holomorphy of the ordinary equal-rank traces of a Godement generator

The first coefficient is the quotient recursion of a compactly
supported full-row-rank rectangular test and a fixed canonical smaller
profile. Its leading inducing exponent varies, and its other exponents
stay fixed. The second coefficient is a fixed canonical Jacquet value
for the opposite left character. The statement supplies a common
spectral abscissa on each bounded disk of adjoining exponents, absolute
convergence of both ordinary integrals, and joint holomorphy in the
adjoining exponent and the spectral variable.

The comparison with the continued Godement integral differs by a fixed
nonzero scalar determined by the adjoining angular character on minus
one. Along the adjoining line this gives pointwise holomorphy, continuity
in the group variable, exact left covariance, and uniform simple-root
bounds on compact parameter sets. The fixed canonical second coefficient
has the same properties. Weyl inverse transpose reverses both left
characters and transports the root bounds to the dual coefficients.
The ordinary equal-rank family theorem applied to the given row Schwartz
test and to its trace Fourier transform then gives the two half-planes.

This is an internal family step for Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg
integrals* (2009), §8.2, pp. 55–56, where the ordinary integrals are
continued separately in the adjoining exponent.
