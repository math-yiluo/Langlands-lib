# Propagating weighted real evaluation identities to right derivatives

**Provenance.** This is an internal calculus step for extending the real coefficient identity of Hervé Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §4.7, Proposition 4.4, p. 31. This Lean proof uses differentiation under the integral, not Jacquet's compact idempotent argument.

For a fixed smooth section of the real position principal series, consider its orbit evaluations with independent translation and evaluation points. A complex integration weight `W` is assumed a.e. strongly measurable and to have every nonnegative integer height moment, meaning `‖W‖ * H^m` is integrable for each `m`. For a continuous translation map of the integration variable that preserves `H`, `integrable_mul_orbitWord` gives integrability at any fixed evaluation point for every finite right derivative word. Two such weights and translation maps may be compared on the same (not necessarily Haar) measure. If their integrals of the translated section agree at every evaluation point, `integral_orbitWord_eq_of_evaluation_eq` gives agreement for all right derivative words at all points.

**Lean proof.** The joint continuity and separate powers from `RealPrincipalSeriesOrbitWord` give measurability and an integrable bound when the evaluation point is fixed. For a derivative in direction `X` use the curve `x exp(t X)` and `KIntegralInvariance.hasDerivAt_mul_matrixExpUnit_at`. The evaluation height is bounded for `t ∈ [-1,1]` by continuity on that compact interval. The resulting bound for the derivative of the integrand is an integrable moment of `W`, independent of `t` there. The dominated derivative theorem computes the derivative at zero. Starting with equality at every point, induction on the word uses uniqueness of this scalar derivative of two equal curves to adjoin one direction. No test function or local factor is specified in this calculus step.
