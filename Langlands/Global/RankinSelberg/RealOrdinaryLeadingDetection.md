# Pointwise detection from a leading real block at or above the comparison rank

Assume exact ordinary real continuation. In the upper-Borel list `d₂ ++ d₁`,
let the rank of `d₁` be at least the rank of the uncut list `es`. At a point
where the complementary pair denominator for `d₂` and `es` is nonzero,
`realOrdinaryLeadingDetection` transfers a nonzero exact normalized value for
`d₁` and `es` to one for `d₂ ++ d₁` and `es`, with the same quotient measure
and opposite additive scales.

Every lower coefficient has a smooth standard preimage under a single
nonzero Whittaker evaluation. When the lower ranks are unequal, a fixed
compact parabolic section profile is one at the padded basis row. For equal
lower ranks, continuity in the Schwartz row lets a compact section profile
detect the nonzero normalized value. The upper smooth test defined by these
sections has normalized value equal to the lower value multiplied by the
complementary denominator. Its pure kernel equals the ordinary scalar
kernel. Continuity of the normalized value on the paired smooth space and
density of the span of compact-finite pairs then give an exact upper test.

The compact restriction and determinant powers implement the first two
primal cases of Proposition 14.3 in §14.2, pp. 104–105, with smooth normalized
evaluation as in §12.3, pp. 89–90. [H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg
Integrals* (2009), author-posted edition](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf#page=104)
