# Compatible Whittaker source data from an adelic form completion

Take an adelic action on a form completion over a number field, with a smooth original finite action and genuine archimedean smooth original vectors. Assume the supplied source norm agrees with the form norm, the archimedean restriction preserves the inner product, and its real derivatives agree with the specified generators. Joint vector recovery is also an input.

The canonical smooth source supplies injectivity and Hilbert density. Joint vector recovery gives derivative density at every compact open finite level. The coordinate restrictions preserve the original finite and compact actions. These identities supply the source hypotheses used to extend the actual Whittaker functional, an internal step toward applying the smooth model in Shalika (1974), §5, pp. 187–188.
