# Local ordinary normalization and growth of its products

`HasOrdinaryNormalizationStripGrowth` requires exponential strip growth for every entire normalization of the indicated local integral family. A raw meromorphic continuation with exponential strip growth implies this property for the real or complex Rankin–Selberg pair factor. The proof uses the Library's comparison of the normalized value with the raw continuation multiplied by the reciprocal Gamma denominator.

A uniformly strip-bounded finite-place normalization has exponent zero. Products of the real, complex and retained finite normalizations have exponential strip growth, including multiplication by the constant in the measure and tensor comparison. This is an internal use of the Library's strip growth lemmas. The local estimates used as premises are formulated from Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, 2009, Theorem 2.1, author PDF pp. 6–7.
