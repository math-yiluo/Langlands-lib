# Normalized Levi slices of a compact parabolic section

`realParabolicLeviSlices` fixes an ambient group point and a compactly supported smooth transverse test. It supplies two Levi group elements and one complex scalar that work for every pair of smooth Levi functions. The normalized slice on the trailing row block in upper-Borel order is a right translate of the first input times the scalar and an evaluation of the second input. The leading row block gives the exchanged formula.

Conjugation by the block Weyl element exchanges the two diagonal factors after reindexing. The left lower-block translation rule therefore gives the two determinant weights and left multiplication on the arguments of the Levi functions. On the pivot cell the parabolic coordinates fix the two Levi group elements and the rectangle evaluation. The same translation rule gives zero off the cell, where the scalar can be taken to be zero. The slice factor cancels the determinant weight, leaving the right-translation formula. This derives the normalized slice formula from `RealParabolicSectionEquivariance` and `RealInductionStagesSlice` for the compact sections of [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §13.2 and Proposition 14.1](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf#page=100).
