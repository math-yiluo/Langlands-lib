# Simple-root derivatives of the continued Godement value

The simple-root curve is the transvection with real parameter `t` times the inverse trace-character scale. Its determinant is one. Moving a positive diagonal across the transvection scales `t` by the exponential of the corresponding simple-root logarithm. The opposite trace character on the transvection is the complex exponential of `−4πit`.

Along the curve `b * rootPath a i t * k`, the determinant factor of the continued Godement value is constant. The right translate of the test by `k` comes before differentiation along the root, and the translate by `b * rootPath a i t` comes after it. Applying the proved derivative under the outer integral repeatedly gives the exact formula for each higher derivative. The derivative at zero of an exponential along the real root line is computed separately for comparison with the Whittaker character.
