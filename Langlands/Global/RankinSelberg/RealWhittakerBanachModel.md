# A Banach smooth-vector model for uniform real Whittaker coefficients

`RealWhittakerBanachModelStatement` lifts a specified uniform real Whittaker intertwiner from the K-finite standard module into the genuine smooth vectors of a jointly continuous weighted right-translation representation. One height exponent serves the whole source. The lift intertwines the compact action and every real matrix Lie operator. A continuous functional on the smooth carrier recovers the given coefficient after every actual group translation of a lifted vector.

Each right-derivative word of a coefficient is the coefficient of another source vector, by the Lie intertwining law. A constant times the square of the unit height bounds the library's real matrix height. Thus the input's common exponent `N` gives exponent `2*N` for all derivative words after the height comparison. Dividing by the height to power `2*N+1` places the coefficients in the smooth carrier and identifies their genuine Lie derivatives. The ratio formula for the weighted representation carries compact equivariance over from the scalar coefficient. The recovering functional uses the empty word derivative coordinate and evaluates it at the identity with the inverse weight undone. The actual right translation formula then gives the recovery identity at each group element.
