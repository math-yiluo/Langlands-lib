# Continuous compact test realizations of real parabolic sections

`RealParabolicLocalizedTestsStatement` fixes exact standard functions in the two real Levi groups, a compact support for the transverse test, and a smooth ambient cutoff supported in a compact set of invertible matrices. Multiplying the normalized parabolic section by the cutoff defines a continuous complex-linear map from transverse tests with the fixed support to ambient matrix tests with the cutoff's prescribed support. Its restriction to the group gives the cutoff times `standardSection` at every point.

The proof chooses a transverse bump equal to one on the input support. On the group, the section for an input test is the bump section times the input test evaluated at the rectangle coordinate. This follows from the cell formula and also holds where the section vanishes. The bump section has support inside the open pivot chart. On the intersection of its ambient support with the fixed compact cutoff support, the rectangle map is smooth by matrix inversion at the pivot. A smooth cutoff extends this map globally while retaining its values on the intersection. The product of the ambient cutoff and the bump section is a smooth compactly supported weight. `CompactWeightedTestPullback` gives the continuous operator with the required group values.

The construction localizes the transverse test in the parabolic section from Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, proof of Proposition 14.1, pp. 103–104 of the author-posted edition. It uses the normalized upper-Borel coordinates of `RealParabolicStandardSection`.
