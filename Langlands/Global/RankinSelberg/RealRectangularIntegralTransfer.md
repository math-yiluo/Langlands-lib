# Finite integral comparison along rectangular prefixes

`RealRectangularIntegralTransferStatement` assumes a finite one-row comparison of kernels. A kernel on a rectangular prefix is a finite sum of last-row integrals of kernels on the next prefix. The statement yields a finite sum of integrals at any terminal rectangle and absolute convergence of the initial integral whenever the terminal terms are absolutely integrable. The same spectral parameter is used throughout.

The canonical product measure splits into the prefix measure and the measure of its final row. The splitting comes from the measure-preserving equivalence for splitting one coordinate from a finite product. Fubini gives the integral comparison and integrability at each step. Descending induction over the number of rows gives the result, with the finite sums combined using the sigma type of their indices.

This is an internal product-measure calculation for the finite rectangular Haar integrals in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), equation (2.5), p. 6. [Author-posted edition](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
