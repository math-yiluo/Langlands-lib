# Local uniform domination of the complex Godement Whittaker integral

`ComplexGodementWhittakerFamilyStatement` treats the partially Fourier-transformed kernel of Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §7.2, (7.7) and Proposition 7.2, pp. 48–49. It allows a finite-dimensional complex parameter on an open set, a fixed Schwartz test and determinant character, a holomorphic extra determinant exponent, and a family of smaller-rank functions. The smaller-rank functions are continuous in the group variable and pointwise holomorphic in the parameter on the open set. The conclusion gives absolute convergence on the open set and holomorphy of the integral for every Haar measure on the smaller group.

The input `HasUniformSimpleRootBound` requires a neighborhood of each parameter on which all the smaller functions have the same logarithmic Iwasawa growth order and admit arbitrary simple-root decay. The constant is allowed to depend on the decay order and the neighborhood. Holomorphy of the determinant exponent bounds its real part on a smaller neighborhood.

The proof uses `inverse_log_length_le` to bound the logarithmic Iwasawa size of \(h^{-1}\) by the positive simple-root sum and a logarithm of the norm of the augmented matrix \([h,h^{-\mathsf T}e_{\mathrm{last}}]\). The logarithm of the determinant and the Haar-density exponent contribute a bounded multiple of the same Iwasawa size. Choose a root decay order large enough to absorb the root sum. The remaining factor is polynomial in the norm of the augmented matrix, so a finite supremum of zeroth-derivative Schwartz seminorms bounds it times the Schwartz test by a prescribed inverse polynomial in the norm of \(h\). The actual Haar density cancels the determinant factor in the resulting majorant. Taking the decay order in the norm of \(h\) greater than the real dimension of the matrix space makes that majorant integrable.

`Langlands.Analysis.dominatedHolomorphicIntegral` applies the common bound to the pointwise holomorphic kernel and gives the claimed convergence and holomorphy. This proves the local integration step assuming the uniform smaller-rank estimates. Construction of those parameterized Whittaker families and the growth estimate in the larger group argument are separate results.
