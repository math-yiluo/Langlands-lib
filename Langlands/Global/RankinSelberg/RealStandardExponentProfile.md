# Exponent changes in compact profiles of real standard actions

When the inducing position exponents change by \(z a_i\), the extension
of a fixed continuous compact profile at \(kg\) is multiplied by
\(\exp(z B_a(g)(k))\). Here \(B_a(g)(k)\) is the weighted sum of half
the differences of the logarithms of successive Gram determinants of
\(kg\), with weights \(a_i\). The function \(g\mapsto B_a(g)\) is smooth
with values in the supremum norm on the compact group.

The Gram determinants are positive on invertible real matrices.
Writing their complex powers as exponentials of real logarithms gives
the shift formula, since the normalization in the position exponents
is fixed. The logarithmic sum is smooth on the invertible matrices.
Left multiplication by the compact parameter is jointly smooth on
the group, so compact restriction gives smoothness into the continuous
function space.
