# Smooth vectors of the compact Banach real standard module

`RealStandardCompactSmoothVectorsStatement` identifies all smooth vectors of
the compact sup-norm completion with the original real standard module by
compact restriction. This complex-linear equivalence preserves actual right
translations and identifies Lie derivatives of Banach orbit maps with scalar
right derivatives.

For a smooth standard function \(f\), express its orbit on compact profiles
by \((g,k)\mapsto f(kg)\). In the matrix chart this is the extension of \(f\)
to the invertible matrices, composed with matrix multiplication. Smooth
currying over the compact group proves smoothness into the ambient space
of continuous profiles. The closed-submodule smoothness criterion then
gives smoothness into the Banach completion. Evaluating the Banach derivative
at each compact argument gives the chain rule identity with the scalar
right derivative.

For a smooth vector of the Banach representation, the extension of its
profile is obtained by evaluating its orbit map at the compact identity.
This is smooth by continuity and linearity of evaluation. The principal
covariance and vanishing block Fourier coefficients hold on the dense range
of original smooth standard functions. For a fixed group argument, the
evaluations, and the block Fourier coefficient obtained by integrating a
jointly continuous kernel on \([0,2\pi]\), depend continuously on the
profile. The defining identities therefore hold on the whole completion.
Restricting the resulting standard function to the compact group recovers
the original profile.

This is an internal identification used to compare compact-finite
Whittaker intertwiners with maps of smooth representations.
