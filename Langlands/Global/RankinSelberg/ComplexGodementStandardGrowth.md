# A common Godement convergence half-plane for a complex standard module

`exists_height_bound_standard` proves polynomial height bounds on every
vector of a fixed positive-rank standard module. The exponent can be
chosen from the character list alone. In an upper-Borel Iwasawa
factorization, the diagonal entries and their inverses are entries of
the group matrix and its inverse after multiplication by a unitary
matrix. Each unitary entry has norm at most one. These facts bound the
absolute values of both diagonal coordinates and their inverses by the
rank times Jacquet's height. The normalized inducing character is a
product of real powers of these norms. The compact factor of the vector
is bounded by continuity on the unitary group.

The height is invariant under inversion. Applying the rectangular
Schwartz convergence theorem to the resulting inverse-argument bound
proves `ComplexGodementStandardConvergenceStatement`. Its sufficient
half-plane is uniform in the smaller vector and the test for a fixed
smaller character list.

[Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, height (2.1) and Proposition 7.1(i), pp. 3 and 43–44](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf).
