# Gaussian-polynomial Godement sections span in the convergence region

For a positive smaller rank, a fixed inducing list, and a sufficiently
large real part of the adjoining exponent, the span of the complex
Godement sections of dilated Gaussian-polynomial tests contains every
compact-finite vector of the resulting larger standard module. The
smaller vectors in these sections are compact finite.

Right unitary translation of a Gaussian test substitutes a polynomial
of bounded total degree. This supplies a finite-dimensional space of
tests containing the translated dilated Gaussian. Absolute convergence
gives a linear map from that space to Godement sections. A finite sum
of evaluations computes its value at the identity, which gives
continuity on the unitary group. The restricted sections also have
finite-dimensional right translate spans and remain in the span of
Gaussian sections under right translation.

The known spanning theorem using compact Schwartz tests and uniform
approximation on the unitary group approximate the given vector.
Compact convolution gives exact membership in the span on the unitary
group. Borel covariance and Iwasawa decomposition identify the section
on the whole group.

This gives the complex convergence-region spanning step of §7.1,
Proposition 7.1(vi) and its proof on author pp. 46–47, of H. Jacquet,
*Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009). The proof uses the
Library's Godement convergence half-plane and variable Gaussian scale.
