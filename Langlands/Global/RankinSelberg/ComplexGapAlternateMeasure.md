# Product measure transport for the alternate complex chart

`ComplexGapAlternateMeasureStatement` relates the product measure of
the joint absolute estimate to the consecutive Haar chart. The
lower rectangular variable can be discarded when its measure is a
probability measure, including the point mass at zero.

The transport negates the logarithmic tail, reindexes the logarithms
and unipotent matrix by the equality of trailing ranks, and
reassociates the six remaining product coordinates. Lebesgue measure
is invariant under negation. The reindexings preserve Lebesgue and
entry Haar measure by substitution of the dimension equality.

The actual Haar chart measure has the two tail densities against
this unweighted product. In the proof, they first combine on the
logarithmic tail. The four other products then carry that
density along the logarithmic projection without an additional
scalar.

This is the measure transport for the alternate chart in H. Jacquet,
*Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §10.2, author pp. 72–73.
[Registered author edition](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
