# One rank step for pointwise holomorphic Jacquet continuation

`ComplexJacquetContinuationStepStatement r` passes from smaller size `r + 1` to larger size `r + 2`. It assumes that each smaller fixed compact profile has a continuation holomorphic in its inducing tuple at each group argument. The smaller group slices are continuous and satisfy the locally uniform logarithmic Iwasawa estimates with arbitrary simple-root decay. Each family agrees with the specified open-cell integral in the strict chamber. The step gives an entire continuation of every larger fixed-profile value at each fixed group argument, with the same chamber agreement.

The finite-profile theorem supplies a common finite-dimensional space containing the larger profile and a fixed family of compact full-row-rank Schwartz tests and smaller compact profiles whose Godement sections span it in every fiber. Each Godement compact restriction has holomorphic coordinates in any fixed basis because the compact-support theorem gives holomorphic point evaluations, and linear functionals on a finite-dimensional space of functions are finite linear combinations of evaluations. At each tuple the spanning property supplies a nonsingular frame matrix.

For every generator, the pointwise Godement Whittaker value has the form (7.7) after the last-column change of variables. The identity pullback on the matrix space passes the test to the norm convention used by the partial Fourier transform. The uniform estimate for (7.7) applies after pulling the smaller family back along the retained-coordinate projection. It gives holomorphy of the transformed integral in the entire larger tuple. The exterior determinant factor is holomorphic in the added exponent.

In the strict chamber, the smaller continuation equals its open-cell integral. The chamber comparison then identifies the Godement Whittaker value with the chamber evaluation of the generator, using the supplied Haar measures and the upper-Borel sign factor. This gives the seed relation for the Cramer continuation theorem. Its holomorphic coefficients define the evaluation at the chosen larger compact profile and agree with the original chamber evaluation. The construction is made at each fixed larger group argument.

This is the pointwise continuation step based on Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §7.1, Proposition 7.1(v), and §7.2, (7.3)–(7.7) and Proposition 7.2, pp. 43–49. The locally uniform bounds for the smaller family are hypotheses. Uniform growth and differentiability of the larger family in the group argument are separate steps for repeating the construction.
