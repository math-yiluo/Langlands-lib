# Product Tate reduction of the complex square zeta integral

`ComplexSquareZetaProductTateStatement` expresses the absolutely convergent square matrix zeta integral of an upper-Borel standard vector as a positive measure constant times a product Tate integral. The Schwartz test on the diagonal is the upper triangular marginal of the matrix test averaged over right unitary translates, with the standard vector evaluated on the unitary factor as the weight. The angular parameters are the inducing weights, and the exponent in position `j` is `s + t_j`.

The proof converts multiplicative diagonal measure to additive measure with one inverse squared modulus per coordinate. For each fixed diagonal, uniform Schwartz decay in the strictly upper entries and boundedness of the compact weight justify exchanging the entry integral and compact average. The chosen self-dual measure in the entry marginal contributes its constant density in each strictly upper entry. The result retains absolute integrability of the original zeta kernel.

This is the diagonal product Tate reduction for Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §4.7, equations (4.3) and (4.4), pp. 30–31.
