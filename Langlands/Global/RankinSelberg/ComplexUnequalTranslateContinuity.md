# Continuous dependence of translated unequal-rank normalizations

`complexUnequalTranslateContinuity` assumes `HasComplexPairNormalizedGrowth` for the ordinary and iterated reflected integrals of every untranslated compact-finite Whittaker pair at the supplied rank-matched lists, opposite trace characters and compatible quotient measure. `complexUnequalNormalizedGrowth` supplies this premise. For genuine right translates of both coefficients, the theorem gives entire ordinary and reflected normalizations with exponential strip growth and the normalized functional equation. At every spectral point the ordinary normalized value is continuous in the two translating matrices.

A convergent sequence of translating matrices and its limit form compact sets. The translated simple-root estimates dominate both kernels on the corresponding right half-planes. The difference of their Mellin integrands on a vertical line has the same absolute value as at its real spectral value. Dominated convergence thus gives uniform Cauchy estimates on every right boundary line for both integrals. Normalized approximation and uniqueness give the claimed continuous dependence. This extends the normalized continuations built from Jacquet's scaled identity to the translation families present in the unequal-rank recurrence. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §§4 and 10](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
