# Smooth principal-series sections from Gauss chart tests

A complex test on the lower-coordinate space gives a smooth standard vector by multiplying its pullback by the character of the upper Gauss factor and setting the result to zero off the chart. Evaluating the section on the chart representatives recovers the test. Its topological support lies in the left upper-triangular saturation of the test's support.

The saturation of a compact coordinate set is closed in the group. It is the projection, over that compact set, of the closed incidence condition that the group element times the inverse chart representative is upper triangular. Gauss recovery identifies this projection with the part of the chart lying over the coordinate set. This gives an open neighborhood on which the extension is zero at each point outside the chart. Within the chart, the matrix of the Gauss factor is smooth, and its invertible triangular values have nonzero diagonal. The normalized Borel character is therefore smooth there. Its multiplicativity and the Gauss left multiplication formula give the original principal-series covariance. At a chart representative the upper factor is the identity.
