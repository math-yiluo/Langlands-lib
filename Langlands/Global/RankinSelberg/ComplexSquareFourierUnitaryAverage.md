# Fourier covariance of compact unitary averages of square Schwartz tests

`ComplexSquareFourierUnitaryAverageStatement` gives the Fourier transform of a weighted compact average of left and right unitary translates. Each unitary translating matrix is replaced by its inverse transpose in the transformed average. The test and its weighted average are Schwartz, and the weight and the unitary matrices depend continuously on the compact variable.

The matrix Lebesgue change of variables and the unitary determinant norm show preservation by left multiplication. Comparing the product self-dual measure with the scalar multiple of matrix Lebesgue measure transports this result to the required normalization. Transposition gives right multiplication. The rectangular Fourier change of variables at the negative frequency gives covariance for the square trace kernel. The compact Fourier exchange then proves the formula.

The left and right specializations apply to the compact averaging in the square zeta functional equation. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, §4.7](https://doi.org/10.1090/conm/489/09547).
