# Recovery of the exact equal-rank equation from normalized growth

This internal implication connects the normalized ordinary equal-rank equation at the standard additive character to Jacquet (2009), Theorem 2.1, in the form of the published Library proposition. It takes the normalized conclusion at every rank as a premise.

The endpoint convergence theorem gives a common right half-plane for the two integrals, on which the holomorphy theorem applies. The proof shrinks the half-plane to one where the entire witnesses agree with the normalized integrals and the denominators are nonzero. Dividing the witnesses by their respective denominators gives the two meromorphic continuations. Substituting the entire reflection identity into the meromorphic equation gives the prescribed reciprocal factors and epsilon. The additive-character reduction supplies the equation and analytic clauses for every nonzero parameter.
