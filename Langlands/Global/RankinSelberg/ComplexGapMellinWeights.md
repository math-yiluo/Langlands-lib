# Convolved complex gap weights and the adjoining central sign

The lower-rank unequal pair and the leading square pair use opposite trace-character parameters. Their weights with Gaussian rate `1/2` combine by logarithmic convolution into the rate-one pair for the enlarged list of characters. The ordinary weight includes the central value of the adjoining character raised to the smaller rank.

The proof splits both reciprocal Gamma products at the adjoining character. The sign-change formula for the square epsilon factor contributes the adjoining central value and one smaller central sign. The smaller central sign squares to one, so the product has the exact enlarged-rank normalization. The Mellin convolution theorem multiplies the two Mellin transforms and adds the Gaussian rates.
