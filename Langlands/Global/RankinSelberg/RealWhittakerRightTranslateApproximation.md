# Positive compact approximation of real Whittaker right translates

## Polynomial weights on the orthogonal group

* `displacementSquared k = Σ_{i,j} (k_{ij} - δ_{ij})²` for `k` in the orthogonal group
  `unitaryCompact ℝ n`; it is nonnegative, continuous and vanishes exactly at `k = 1`
  (`displacementSquared_nonneg`, `continuous_displacementSquared`, `displacementSquared_eq_zero`).
  It is the value of a polynomial in the matrix entries (`squareDisplacement`, in the star
  subalgebra `OrthogonalFinitePolynomial.polynomialStar` generated by the entries).
* `RealPolynomialWeightStatement`: for every rank `r + 1` and `ε > 0` there are a polynomial
  `w ∈ polynomialStar (r + 1)` and a continuous `W ≥ 0` on the orthogonal group with
  `compactWeight w = W` (the restriction of `w` along `unitaryCompactMatrix`), `∫ W = 1` and
  `∫ W(k) ‖k - 1‖ dk < ε` for the normalized Haar measure `haarK (r + 1)` (operator norm).
  `realPolynomialWeight` proves it: `q = 1 - C⁻¹ · displacementSquared` (with `C` a bound of the
  displacement) is a polynomial peak with maximum `1` attained only at the identity; the
  Library's peak lemma `exists_pos_integral_pow_of_peak`, applied to `1 - ε⁻¹ ‖k - 1‖`, gives a
  power `q^j` with `∫ (1 - ε⁻¹ ‖k - 1‖) q^j > 0`, and `W = q^j / ∫ q^j` works.

## Statement

`RealWhittakerRightTranslateApproximationStatement`: let `B` be a specified real Whittaker
intertwiner (`IsRealWhittakerIntertwiner r ds a B`) of the `O(r+1)`-finite standard module of a
block list `ds`, `f` a standard vector and `g ∈ GL_{r+1}(ℝ)`. There are standard vectors `p j`
and continuous weights `w j ≥ 0` of mass one on the orthogonal group such that for all `j`, `x`

`B (p j) x = ∫ w j (k) · B f (x k g) dk`

and `B (p j) x → B f (x g)` as `j → ∞`, for every `x`.

`realWhittakerRightTranslateApproximation` proves it: the weights are the polynomial weights
with moment `ε_j = 1/(j+1)`; their left translates span a finite-dimensional space
(`finiteDimensional_compactWeight`), so `realExactCompactProjection` gives `p j` with the
displayed formula. For convergence (`average_close`), continuity of `k ↦ B f (x k g)` at the
identity and its boundedness on the compact group bound
`‖∫ W(k) φ(k) dk - φ(1)‖` by `η + A ∫ W(k) ‖k - 1‖ dk` for any `η > 0`.

This is the real counterpart of the Library's `ComplexWhittakerRightTranslateApproximation`
(which used central weights and a derivative estimate; here plain continuity suffices). It
supplies the approximation of right translates used in the larger-gap recurrence of Jacquet,
*Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §10.3.
