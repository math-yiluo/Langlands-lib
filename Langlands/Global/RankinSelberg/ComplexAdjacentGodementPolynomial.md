# Polynomial normalization for rectangular complex Godement tests

At trace scale of unit modulus, suppose the smaller equal-rank integrals have polynomial normalizations for the standard Gaussian row tests. Then the adjacent ordinary integral of a continued rectangular polynomial Gaussian Godement coefficient is absolutely convergent on a right half-plane and admits a polynomial normalization.

The fixed standard dilation turns the rectangular test into a finite sum of products of square and column tests of scale `2π`. The split row transform preserves that Gaussian scale. The compact product expansion supplies a polynomial for each summand from the smaller equal normalization and the square zeta normalization. The subspace of integrable kernels with polynomial normalization is closed under the finite sum. The dilation of the entire rectangular argument multiplies the continued Godement coefficient by a fixed nonzero scalar, so the same subspace contains the original scale-one test.

This implements a derived Gaussian spanning step in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), Proposition 8.1, author-posted pp. 60–61.
