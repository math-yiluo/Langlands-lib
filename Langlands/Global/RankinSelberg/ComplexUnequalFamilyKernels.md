# Exact unequal-rank kernels for continuous Whittaker families

The ordinary and dual kernels descend continuously to the exact quotient carriers whenever the input functions are continuous and have the opposite left Whittaker characters. The proof needs the left transformation law of each function and therefore also applies to the continued compact-profile families.

Padding an upper unipotent matrix preserves its adjacent root-character sum. The two character factors cancel and the determinant power stays fixed. This gives invariance of the ordinary kernel. For the dual kernel, Weyl inverse transpose first reverses the left character, and multiplying the leading block on the left leaves the rectangular variable fixed. The same cancellation gives invariance. The explicit representatives then give continuity by the quotient map, using the product quotient map in the dual case.
