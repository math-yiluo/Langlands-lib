# Real determinant-weighted lower block measures

On the parameters
\(([g],x,h)\) of the class represented by \(\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} g&0\\x&h\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\),
use the compatible quotient measure in the first block, additive product
Haar measure on the rectangle and a sigma-finite right-invariant Borel
measure on the trailing lower triangular group. The first factor is
weighted by \(|\det g|^{-d}\), where \(d\) is the size of the trailing block.

Right multiplication by \((g_0,y,h_0)\) translates the quotient by
\(g_0\), sends the rectangle to \(xg_0+hy\), and translates the trailing
block by \(h_0\). The quotient density contributes \(|\det g_0|^d\)
to the pushforward. The rectangular determinant Jacobian is its
reciprocal. Right invariance of additive Haar handles the remaining
translation of the rectangle at each fixed trailing block. These
changes preserve the product measure.

At \(d=1\) this is the invariance calculation for the quotient measure
used in the proof of Lemma 14.1 of H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg
Integrals* (2009), author-posted p. 106. The result proves invariance
in the product coordinates.
