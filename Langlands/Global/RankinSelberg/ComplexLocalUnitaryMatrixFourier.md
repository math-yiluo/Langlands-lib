# Unitary left translation in the square Fourier integral

Jacquet applies a compact weight on the square test in §8.2 and transports the weight to the dual test by inverse transpose. This internal comparison proves the Fourier change of variables for each unitary left translate, using the Library’s positive Fourier convention and the self-dual product measure on matrix entries.

The proof first compares the pushforward of any additive Haar measure on the square matrix space with the original measure. Left multiplication is a continuous additive equivalence. To determine the Haar scalar, the proof takes a compact neighborhood of zero and saturates it under the compact unitary group. The resulting compact set has positive finite measure and is preserved by every unitary left multiplication. Comparing its two measures gives Haar scalar one. The square Fourier formula then follows from the entry pairing change of variables under left multiplication.
