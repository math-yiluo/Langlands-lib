# Equal and adjacent complex ordinary integrals in vertical strips

`complexOrdinaryEqualAdjacentStripGrowth` gives an entire normalization by
the prescribed complex pair factor for every equal or adjacent ordinary test.
Every entire normalization of the same test satisfies the exponential bound on
vertical strips. The statement uses the exact Whittaker models, every compatible
quotient measure, and every nonzero additive scale.

The equal-rank proof at the standard trace character gives an ordinary and a
dual normalization together with their growth bounds. For any other scale the
quotient comparison multiplies each integral by a complex scalar and a positive
real power with an affine spectral exponent. The norms of those multipliers
depend continuously on the real part alone and are bounded on closed vertical
strips. The epsilon scaling rule carries the normalized equation to the new
scale. The adjacent rank step uses this equal-rank input at the same scale and
gives both normalized witnesses for the adjacent pair.

The reciprocal Gamma denominator is nonzero sufficiently far to the right.
Cancellation there converts the ordinary witness into the Library's
`OrdinaryIntegralNormalization`. Uniqueness of entire continuation transfers
its strip bound to every normalization of the test.

This is a derived result from the character change of Theorem 2.1 and the equal
and adjacent induction in §§8.1–8.3, author pp. 52–60, of H. Jacquet,
*Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009).
[Registered author edition](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
