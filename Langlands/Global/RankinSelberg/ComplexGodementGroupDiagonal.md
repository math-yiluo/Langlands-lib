# The group-to-diagonal reduction of the complex Godement integral

For a bi-invariant Haar measure on the smaller complex general linear group
and a Haar measure on its unitary subgroup, the right Iwasawa formula
parametrizes the group in unitary, unipotent and logarithmic diagonal
coordinates. The measured coordinate permutation leaves product measure
unchanged. In each unipotent fiber the positive column scaling replaces the
unipotent entries by the additive triangular entries. The Jacobian converts
the integrand to the weighted diagonal kernel.

The proof compares the nonnegative absolute integrals first. This gives
absolute integrability of the original kernel exactly when the triangular
kernel is absolutely integrable. Fubini then gives the complex integral
comparison. The phase change and logarithmic radius comparison give the
full complex multiplicative diagonal integral with the factor of `2π` per
position included in the measure constant. The result assumes a continuous
test and a smaller standard vector. The constant depends only on the two
Haar measures and the rank.

[Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, §7.1, proof of Proposition 7.1, pp. 43–44](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf).
