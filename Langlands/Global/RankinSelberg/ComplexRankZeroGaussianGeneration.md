# Polynomial normalization and generation of complex rank-one Gaussian row tests

This proves the complex rank-one base for the standard Gaussian rows of
Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), Proposition 8.1(ii),
author p. 60, and Theorem 2.7(ii), author p. 10. It applies to each
compatible quotient measure and every nonzero additive scale.

For a one-coordinate row, the linear change from real and imaginary parts
to a complex coordinate and its conjugate identifies the row test with a
scalar polynomial Gaussian of scale \(2\pi\). The two Whittaker functions
are scalar multiples of the specified characters composed with determinant.
The scalar Tate calculation then gives a polynomial for the ordinary
pair normalization with the character exponent shift.

When both Whittaker models are nonzero, choose nonzero vectors and use
the angular Gaussian for their product character. The scalar and Haar
constants are nonzero. Dividing the row test by their product makes its
entire normalized integral identically one. That angular Gaussian belongs
to the same class of row tests since each of the two angular monomials is
a polynomial in the real and imaginary parts of the row entry.
