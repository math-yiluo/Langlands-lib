# Uniform bounds on compact parameter sets for complex Whittaker families

`ComplexLocalUniformDualBoundsStatement` takes a family of functions on the
complex general linear group with local uniform simple-root estimates at
every parameter. On each compact parameter set it gives an estimate of
that form for the family and for its Weyl inverse-transpose dual.

A finite collection of the parameter balls covers the compact set. The
proof uses the maximum of their growth exponents and, for each decay
order, the sum of their bounding constants.

For the dual, the proof factors the Weyl inverse-transpose argument using
the Iwasawa factors of the original argument. Weyl conjugation sends the
inverse transpose of the upper unipotent factor back to the upper
unipotent group. The transformed unitary factor is unitary and the new
positive diagonal logarithms are the negatives of the original ones in
reverse order. This transformation preserves the sum of absolute
logarithms and the positive simple-root sum. The same estimate therefore
bounds the dual family.

This internal estimate uses the simple-root gauge and locally uniform
family bounds of Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009),
(3.3) on p. 11 and Proposition 3.3 on p. 20.
