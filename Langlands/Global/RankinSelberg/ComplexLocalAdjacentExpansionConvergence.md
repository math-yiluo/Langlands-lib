# Convergence for the corank-one expansion kernel

This internal comparison supplies the joint integrability hypothesis of `ComplexLocalAdjacentUnfolding` for two smaller Whittaker-model coefficients and separated Schwartz data. In Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §8.3, printed p. 58, substituting the recursive Whittaker expression into the unequal-rank zeta integral and applying two changes of variables gives this kernel in (8.3). The convergence argument continues on p. 59.

The proof applies the published `complexAdjacentConvergence` theorem with the group Haar measure from the compatible quotient package and shift `r/2`. The general linear group is sigma-compact, and its continuous quotient map gives sigma-compactness of the quotient. Haar finiteness on compact sets and regularity of the quotient measure then give the two sigma-finiteness instances. Swapping the two factors transports integrability to quotient-first order. The squared determinant norm is positive on invertible matrices, so its complex power agrees with the exponential of its real logarithm times the exponent. This identifies the matrix weight after commuting the shift with the spectral parameter.
