# Real equal-rank tests for \(\mathrm{GL}_1\times\mathrm{GL}_1\)

Every rank-one adelic matrix is the scalar matrix of its determinant idele. Central-character covariance therefore makes a vector with a nonzero value somewhere nonzero at the identity. The rank-one Whittaker coefficient is the form itself. The resulting real-place restrictions at finite offset one have nonzero values at the identity, so their determinant-character descriptions have nonzero coefficients.

The Schwartz function on the one-dimensional row space comes from the real Tate Gaussian of the parity of the product character. The ordinary integral on a right half-plane is a nonzero measure factor times the two coefficients and the corresponding real Gamma factor. The published real rank-one functional equation gives holomorphy of both integrals on a right half-plane. Its meromorphic identity carries nonvanishing to the dual integral for the same Schwartz function and quotient measure.

The existence theorem chooses a compatible measure using the archimedean quotient construction at rank one.
