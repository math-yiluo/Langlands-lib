# Compact Godement generators over the real character exponents

`RealGodementSpectralFrameStatement` fixes parities and a finite-dimensional left-translation-stable space of functions on the orthogonal group. Its subspace of principal-series restrictions is spanned at every exponent vector by real Godement sections from fixed lower compact profiles and fixed Schwartz tests of compact full-row-rank support. The generator sections are holomorphic in the exponents at every group element.

For a compact restriction in the given finite space, evaluation of its left translates by linear functionals contains every right translate. This gives finite right-translate span and lets the profile extend to the K-finite standard module at each exponent. At a fixed exponent vector the compact Gram approximation gives smooth lower angular cutoffs. Polynomials in the angular coordinate approximate each of those cutoffs in the compact evaluations of its section. The polynomial lower inputs are K-finite and supply the fixed lower profiles. With the Gram test held fixed, every change of exponents leaves the compact integrand a scalar multiple of a left translate of the reference section. It therefore stays in the given space after integration. The span is closed in the topology of pointwise convergence because it is finite-dimensional, so it contains both limits of the approximation. Holomorphy of the generators follows by integrating the jointly continuous, pointwise holomorphic lower sections over a compact preimage of the test support.

The common compact space implements the spanning step in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §7.1, Proposition 7.1(v), and §7.2, pp. 46–48. The two approximation steps produce the fixed lower profiles for the spectral induction.
