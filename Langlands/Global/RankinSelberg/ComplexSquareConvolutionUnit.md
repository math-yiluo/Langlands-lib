# A nonzero polynomial square convolution at every spectral point

`complexSquareConvolutionUnit` gives a square polynomial Gaussian and a compact-finite inducing vector whose normalized convolution has constant nonzero inducing value at the identity. It assumes the inducing angular weights are in nondecreasing order. The statement allows any group Haar measure and any Whittaker intertwiner. The Whittaker evaluation of the polynomial family has the normalized integral formula on a common right half-plane for all group arguments.

Twisting the inducing list by the determinant character adds the same integer to every angular weight. This preserves the order used in the square Gaussian test. Dividing the inducing vector from that test by the determinant character recovers a vector in the given inducing module. The reciprocal factor for the twisted list equals the singleton pair factor. The square integral thus gives the nonzero constant on a right half-plane, and scalar evaluation of the polynomial family continues the identity to every spectral point. The angular test and convolution use the square matrix zeta integral of Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §8.2 and Proposition 8.1.
