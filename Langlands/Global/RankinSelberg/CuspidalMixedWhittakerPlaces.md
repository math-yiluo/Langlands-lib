# Local covariance and fixed-level continuity of a mixed Whittaker functional

At a fixed compact open finite level, the mixed functional agrees
with its derivative-continuous representative on the genuine smooth
vectors. Composing that representative with a continuous inclusion
in the derivative topology gives continuity of the pullback.

For a finite place, a unipotent matrix inserted at that place has
archimedean coordinate one and finite coordinate equal to the local
insertion. For a real or complex place it has the corresponding
archimedean coordinate and finite coordinate one. The published
generic character formulas give the multipliers from the prescribed
local additive components. Applying the actual mixed covariance
identity at each such point gives the local formulas. A finite
factor intertwiner transports those formulas to the covariance
predicate for finite adelic Whittaker uniqueness.

These are the functional restrictions required in the use of
local uniqueness in Shalika (1974), Theorem 5.5.
