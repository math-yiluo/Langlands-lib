# Normalization at an embedded leading unitary

`complexGapUnitaryFiber` evaluates a supplied retained normalization at an embedded leading unitary for opposite trace scales and a compatible smaller quotient measure. The reference square test is `Ψ(X) = Φ([X; 0])`, the identity leading restriction of the same rectangular test `Φ`. The supplied polynomial convolution curve `F(s)` satisfies the normalized square integral formula for `Ψ` on a right half-plane after applying the smaller Whittaker intertwiner. A supplied linear family `A` gives the entire normalizations of the unshifted lower integral, with absolute convergence and agreement on a right half-plane for each vector.

At each chosen embedded leading unitary, the supplied square output and entire retained value `J(s)` satisfy the corresponding square and lower ordinary normalization identities on a right half-plane. The square identity uses the leading restriction of `Φ` at that unitary, and the lower identity uses the translated larger Whittaker coefficient and the square output. Multiplying `J(s)` by the adjoining character of the embedded determinant gives `A(F(s))(s)` at every complex spectral point.

The left Haar substitution in the square integral produces the inverse of the adjoining determinant character and translates the square output. The right substitution in the smaller quotient cancels this translation against the larger Whittaker argument. The unitary determinant has normalized absolute value one. Because the square convolution is a polynomial curve in a fixed finite span, linearity and absolute convergence give the lower-rank formula on a common right half-plane. Entire uniqueness gives the stated value at every spectral point.

The square and lower-rank integrals are those separated in [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §10.4](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf).
