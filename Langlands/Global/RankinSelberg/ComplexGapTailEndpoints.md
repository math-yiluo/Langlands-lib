# Schwartz decay at the endpoints of the complex right tail

The first column of the trailing block and the last row of the inverse chart matrix detect the two endpoint logarithmic coordinates. Removing the left unitary and applying the Levi projection identifies the relevant entries with the diagonal of the triangular tail and its inverse. Matrix multiplication with a unitary matrix bounds each of those entries by the rank times the norm of the corresponding column or row.

The two endpoint tests can be arbitrary Schwartz functions. Their seminorms bound their product by arbitrary exponential decay in the negative part of the first logarithmic tail coordinate and the positive part of the last one. The constant is uniform in the upper rectangle, the unipotent tail and the unitary factor.
