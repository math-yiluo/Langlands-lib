# The toral shift in the complex equal-rank unfolding

This internal estimate prepares the absolute convergence argument for Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §8.2, printed pp. 55–56, and the double integral (8.1). Write `m = r + 1` and `n = m + 1`. The smaller logarithmic torus `x` is padded with zero. If `z` is the large group's Iwasawa logarithm, shifting `x_i` by `z_i − z_last` makes the resulting large torus a padded torus times a scalar.

Simple-root differences are unchanged by the common scalar. Summing coordinates identifies the two determinant weights after the shift. The shifted smaller determinant has exponent `s − 1/2`. The group determinant has residual exponent `n/2`, and the remaining spectral weight uses the last large logarithmic coordinate alone.

The padded torus has last coordinate zero. The published simple-root exponent inequality therefore gives the exponential envelope with exponent `−∑ |x_i|` once the smaller spectral exponent is large enough. The decay order may depend on that exponent. It does not enter the determinant weight of the matrix variable.
