# Smooth extension of real block Fourier multipliers

`Langlands.Global.RankinSelbergGlobal.RealStandardCompactBanach.RealBlockSmoothExtensionStatement`
starts with a block Frobenius map on the compact-finite real standard modules.
Its symbol satisfies the block recurrence and has polynomial growth. The theorem
extends the map continuously to the genuine smooth carriers with their
word-derivative topologies. The extension retains the given map on compact-finite
vectors and commutes with the orthogonal action and every real Lie derivative.

The compact Fourier bound estimates the output in the empty-word coordinate.
On the orthogonal group the conjugated rotation directions form a bounded set.
The continuous basis seminorm at the requisite derivative order therefore
bounds the high derivative term uniformly. The recurrence commutes the
multiplier past any word of real derivatives, so the same estimate bounds each
output coordinate as a continuous expression on the input. The compact-finite
vectors are dense and the target smooth carrier is complete. Continuous linear
extension and density then give the two intertwining identities.

This is an internal consequence of the block multiplier formulas and the
Library's genuine smooth-vector constructions. It applies in particular to the
polynomially bounded ascending tail symbols used for real induced blocks.
