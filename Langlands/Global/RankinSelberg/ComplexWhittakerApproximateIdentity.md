# Quantitative compact approximate identities for exact complex Whittaker intertwiners

Choose an opposite-cell sample section with nonzero mass and a polynomial
moment of order one above the polynomial bound for the dual probe. The
normalized compact projection gives the averaging operators. They commute
with right compact translation by left invariance of compact Haar applied
to the inverse coefficient.

The exact opposite-cell formula and the probe value estimate bound the
compact norm of each average by a common constant times the compact norm
of the input. For the error, subtract the identity evaluation inside the
opposite-cell integral, using its normalizing mass. The dual probe error
has a factor of the displacement of the contracted point. This is bounded
by ε times the original upper-entry displacement. The chosen moment of
the sample absorbs the polynomial growth of the probe and the additional
factor of the original displacement. The compact error is therefore at
most a common constant times ε times the sum of the compact value bound
and the first right-translation difference bound.

The same averaging operators have the exact compact sample integral for
every Whittaker intertwiner in the statement. Its polynomial sampling
estimate bounds the identity evaluation of the averaged vector by a
fixed inverse power of ε times the compact value bound. All constants
are uniform in the input and in 0 < ε ≤ 1.
