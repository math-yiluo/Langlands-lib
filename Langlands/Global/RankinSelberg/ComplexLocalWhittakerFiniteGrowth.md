# Moderate growth for finite families of local complex coefficients

For a finite family of inducing vectors, take the largest of the
intertwiner's pointwise growth exponents and add the nonnegative
bounding constants. The matrix height is at least one. The same
construction applies to the enlarged finite family containing the
first right derivatives in all real matrix basis directions.

On a fixed compact set of invertible matrices, both the height and
the inverse matrix norm are bounded. Submultiplicativity of the height
then gives a common growth bound for the values after left translation.
The matrix derivative in direction \(V\) is the right derivative in
direction \(h^{-1}V\) at the point \(gh\). The proof expands this direction
in the real matrix basis and uses the operator norms of the coordinate
functionals. This gives, on the same compact set, the common bound for
the first derivative in operator norm.

Only finitely many inducing vectors and first derivatives enter this
argument. The estimates supply the initial bounds for an interior
elliptic estimate on the left-translated coefficient family.
