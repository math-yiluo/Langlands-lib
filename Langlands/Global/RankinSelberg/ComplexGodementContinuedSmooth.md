# Smoothness of continued Godement values

The Godement integral with a partially transformed Schwartz test is smooth in the larger complex general linear group at each fixed spectral parameter. The smaller-rank function needs the continuity and simple-root estimate used to define the continued integral.

The existing directional integration theorem differentiates the translated test along `g exp(t X)` by the linear vector field `Y ↦ Y X`. The outer integral is linear in the test. Its derivatives in a finite real basis of matrices therefore form a continuous field of linear maps. Differentiation in exponential coordinates identifies the Fréchet derivative of the extended integral on the open locus of invertible matrices.

The ambient derivative is a finite sum of values at fixed differentiated Schwartz tests times matrix coordinates of the tangent direction multiplied by the inverse matrix. Matrix inversion is smooth on the invertible locus. Induction on the derivative order now gives smoothness of every translated integral. Multiplication by the smooth determinant factor gives smoothness of the exact continued Godement value.
