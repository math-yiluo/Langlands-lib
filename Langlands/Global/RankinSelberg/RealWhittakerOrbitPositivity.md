# Positivity on the longest cell detects real Whittaker values

`realWhittakerOrbitPositivity` applies to a nonzero Whittaker form on a normalized real character principal series with a compact derivative bound. The inducing function has topological support in the longest cell. Its value at `w₀ u⁻¹` multiplied by the Whittaker character of `u` has nonnegative real part for every upper-unipotent `u`, and the real part at `w₀` is positive. The form has nonzero value on this function.

`RealBorelAverageSurjectivity` produces a compact matrix test by multiplying the function by a nonnegative cutoff equal to one on the orthogonal subgroup. The Borel average is a positive scalar multiple of the input, and the support lies in the longest cell. The Whittaker orbit formula applies to this test. Left Borel equivariance cancels the inverse inducing weight, leaving the modular character and the cutoff times the phased value at `w₀ u⁻¹`. The real part of this continuous compactly supported integrand is nonnegative and is positive at the identity of the product group. Positivity of its Haar integral gives a nonzero value of the original form. This is an internal nonvanishing criterion for the compact sections used in [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, Proposition 14.1](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf#page=103).
