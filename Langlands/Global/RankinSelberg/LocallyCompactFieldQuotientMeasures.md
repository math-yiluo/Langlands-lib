# Compatible measures on archimedean unipotent quotients

This construction works on \(N_{r+1}(K)\backslash\mathrm{GL}_{r+1}(K)\) for a locally compact second countable normed field with its Borel measurable structure. In particular it gives the real and complex quotient measure data used in the local Rankin–Selberg integrals.

On the general linear group, left and right translation by a scalar matrix coincide. The Haar modular character is therefore one on scalars, and the Library's algebraic character lemma makes it one on the whole group. Thus the ambient Haar measure is right invariant.

On the upper-unipotent group, use the measure transported from the product of additive Haar measures in strictly upper entry coordinates. The published coordinate lemma proves left invariance. For right translation, the change in the \((i,j)\)-entry depends only on the entries with smaller gap \(j-i\). The finite triangular translation theorem gives preservation of the product measure, proving right invariance for the transported measure.

The upper-unipotent subgroup is closed and both Haar measures are regular. The Library's compatible quotient measure theorem supplies a right-invariant regular nonzero measure with the complex Weil integration formula.
