# Angular dependence of the real rank-two discrete-block coefficients

The two angular powers of the compact Gram polynomial have the
same right rotation character. Their normalized Gram entries
transform by ordinary matrix multiplication, while the Gram
determinant factors are right invariant. The same character therefore
passes through the open-cell Jacquet integral.

Conjugation by the first-coordinate reflection changes the additive
character to its opposite. The left reflection supplies the character
change, and the right reflection is an orthogonal translate of an
ordinary coefficient. The reflection inverts a rotation and commutes
with the diagonal. Thus the conjugated coefficient has the opposite
angular power and agrees on the diagonal. Moving a single right
reflection past the rotation instead reverses the first diagonal
entry, where the one-sided discrete-series formula applies.

These angular formulas prepare the diagonal and row calculations for
the two discrete blocks in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg
Integrals* (2009), §12.4, pp. 90–92 of the author-posted version.
