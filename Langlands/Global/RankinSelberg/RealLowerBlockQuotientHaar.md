# The compatible real quotient Haar measure in lower-block coordinates

The full lower-triangular cell in the real left-unipotent quotient is
open by iteration of the last-pivot chart. Its complement is null for
every compatible quotient measure by the conull upper-unipotent times
lower-triangular cell in the group and a quotient normalizing function.
Pullback to the triangular group is right invariant and finite on
compact sets.

The one-coordinate formula compares compatible measures on the two
unipotent quotients. The larger quotient measure is a positive scalar
multiple of the image of the smaller quotient measure weighted by
\(|\det g|^{-1}\), the additive Haar measure of the rectangular row, and
any nonzero right-invariant locally finite measure of the trailing
rank-one triangular group. The statement assumes that the trailing
measure is sigma-finite and finite on compact sets.

To make the comparison, transport the weighted smaller triangular
measure, row measure and trailing measure through the lower-triangular
block homeomorphism. Right invariance follows from the weighted
lower-block product invariance after the measurable embedding into
quotient coordinates. The transported measure is nonzero and finite on
compacts. Right Haar uniqueness compares it with the larger quotient
measure pulled back to the triangular group. Pushing the comparison
through the conull chart gives the asserted formula.

In rank one the trailing triangular group is the multiplicative real
line. The determinant density therefore gives the measure
\(C\,|\det g|^{-1}\,dg\,dx\,d^\times h\) in H. Jacquet,
*Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), Lemma 14.1,
author-posted p. 106.
