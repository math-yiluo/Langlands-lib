# Rectangular two-block Haar coordinates over the complex numbers

`ComplexTwoBlockHaarStatement` gives the ambient complex matrix Haar measure in coordinates \(k u(X)\operatorname{diag}(g_1,g_2)\). The unitary factor uses a supplied inversion-invariant Haar measure, the rectangular measure is the product of the supplied scalar additive measure, and the two diagonal blocks use the complex matrix Haar measures. A positive scalar accounts for the ambient Haar normalization.

The coordinate theorem for an actual block radical identifies the two-block radical with its single strictly upper rectangle. The product and inverse in these coordinates are addition and negation, since there are no intermediate blocks. The diagonal blocks identify the Levi with a product of two actual general linear groups. These identifications transport the factor measures and their inversion invariance into the block-parabolic Haar formula.
