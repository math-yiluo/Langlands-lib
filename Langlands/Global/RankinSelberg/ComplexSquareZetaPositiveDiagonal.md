# Positive logarithmic diagonal integration for complex square zeta integrals

`ComplexSquareZetaPositiveDiagonalStatement` identifies the absolutely convergent square matrix zeta integral of an upper-Borel standard vector with its integral in additive strictly upper entries, positive logarithmic diagonal coordinates, and the unitary factor. The constant is positive and depends on the group and compact measures.

The proof uses the left Iwasawa chart and the covariance of the standard vector. The upper-entry change of variables contributes the row Jacobian. The determinant half-shift, Borel half-modulus and row Jacobian leave the exponent `s + t_j` in row `j`, where `t_j` is the inducing exponent. Unitarity makes the compact determinant contribute a factor of one.

This gives the positive-diagonal integral computation for the square matrix zeta integral of Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §4.7, equations (4.3) and (4.4), pp. 30–31. The formulation retains absolute integrability of the original group kernel.
