# Comparison with the convergent real Jacquet integral

When the leading exponent is to the right of the first lower exponent, the continued Godement evaluation equals the open-cell Jacquet integral on the whole compact section span. It retains its uniform coefficient map on the upper K-finite standard module. Under the additional inequalities of Schwartz joint convergence, the column formula holds for any Schwartz test whose Godement section is in the span.

The lower dominance condition and the inequality on the leading exponent give dominance of the full list. The open-cell integral is therefore linear on the smooth upper position principal series. Its restriction to the compact span agrees on each generator with the continued column value by the compact-test unfolding. Equality of linear maps on the span gives the comparison. Substituting the joint integral identity then gives the Schwartz-test formula in its convergence range.
