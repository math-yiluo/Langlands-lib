# Linearity of entire ordinary integral normalizations

`ordinaryNormalizationLinear` starts with a complex-linear family of integrands, each absolutely integrable on some right half-plane, and an entire normalization for each vector by a common eventually nonzero factor. The entire normalized functions form a complex-linear map in the input vector.

On a common right half-plane, linearity of integration gives the normalization identities for sums and scalar multiples. The identity theorem gives the same linearity on the whole complex plane. This implements the dependence on test vectors in the ordinary generating integrals of Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, Theorem 2.1.
