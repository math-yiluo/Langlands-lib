# Complex two-block Haar integration with the first block on the right

The chart separates an element of the upper-left general linear group on the right. Its remaining factor is the product of the unitary matrix, the lower-right diagonal block, and an upper unipotent rectangle. The pushed product measure is proportional to Haar measure after inserting the reciprocal of the complex normalized determinant of the lower-right block to the power of the upper-left size.

Block conjugation on the radical sends `X` to `g₁ X g₂⁻¹`. Moving `diag(1,g₂)` left of the rectangle therefore replaces `X` by `X g₂`. The rectangular additive Haar change of variables gives the determinant factor for each fixed pair of diagonal blocks. Tonelli's theorem inserts this factor as a density on their product with the rectangle and the unitary group. Composing the pushforwards gives the formula. This is the measure change used in Jacquet's *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, §§9.1 and 10.1.
