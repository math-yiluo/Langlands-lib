# Block Haar measure for the complex upper unipotent group

Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §7.2 and §8.2, uses the decomposition of the upper unipotent group into its leading square block and the last column. This internal measure comparison uses the Library’s scalar entry coordinates for the actual upper unipotent groups. The strict upper entries of the larger matrix split into the strict upper entries of its leading square block and all entries of the last column above the diagonal. The resulting coordinate equivalence places the joined rectangle in precisely the order of the recursive Schwartz test.

With the same scalar self-dual Haar measure at each entry, splitting the finite index set preserves product measure. Transport through the two entry homeomorphisms gives the exact product formula for the coordinate Haar measures. Arbitrary supplied group Haar measures differ from these by positive scalars, so the comparison between the supplied larger measure and the product of the supplied smaller measure with the last-column measure retains one positive scalar. This scalar is the ratio of the two Haar comparison constants.

For an integrable function on the larger unipotent group, the measure comparison gives the iterated integral over the smaller group and the column with that scalar. The generic Whittaker character of the joined element is the product of the smaller character and the additive character of the column’s final entry. These identities allow the rectangular recursion to be compared with the larger unipotent integral.
