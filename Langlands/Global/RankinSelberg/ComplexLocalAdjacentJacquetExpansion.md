# The complex adjacent-rank equation from Jacquet’s finite expansion

This internal reduction implements the finite-sum argument in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §8.3, pp. 58–60. The larger inducing list is `d :: ds` and the smaller one is `es`. The two smaller lists have length `r + 1`. The matched expansions are identities for the actual corank-one integrals on a right half-plane. Each product consists of an equal-rank integral at size `r + 1` for `ds, es` and a meromorphic matrix continuation with the pair factors of `[d], es`. The dual expansion uses the corresponding smaller dual integral and matrix continuation with common multiplier `complexCentralSign es`.

The smaller equal-rank equation uses the original additive parameter and has scalar `complexCentralSign es ^ r`. The matrix equation uses the same parameter and scalar one. The two L-factors and the two epsilon factors multiply along the list split `d :: ds`. In each summand the scalar from the dual expansion increases the exponent `r` to `r + 1`, giving the exact adjacent-rank coefficient in the Library’s positive Fourier convention.

The proof takes the continuations supplied by the smaller equal-rank theorem and forms their finite sums of products with the matrix continuations. It enlarges the right half-plane to meet every equality and absolute convergence bound. Holomorphy follows for the ordinary integral and for the corank-one dual using the single-point rectangular measure. The reduction retains the smaller equal-rank equation, the paired expansions and matrix equations, and absolute convergence of the two adjacent-rank kernels as premises.
