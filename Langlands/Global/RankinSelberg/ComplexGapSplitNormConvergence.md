# Absolute convergence for the split alternate complex chart

`complexGapSplitNormConvergence` proves absolute integrability for
measurable kernels dominated by the split alternate norm product.
The measure uses the compatible quotient and retained tail chart,
Haar measure on the leading matrix, and a measure for the lower
rectangle that integrates a fixed inverse power of one plus its norm.
The leading matrix and quotient determinant powers have independent
thresholds.

The pointwise estimate separates the weighted leading matrix, the
lower rectangle, and the alternate tail envelope. The latter is
continuous in the retained variables. Its joint integrability follows
by selecting a suitable simple-root decay order and using the quotient
density estimate. The leading matrix bound and the hypothesis on the
lower rectangle give a product of integrable majorants for the
original kernel. The actual additive rectangle measure admits such a
bound, and a point mass at zero allows the same estimate when that
rectangle is held at zero.

This is a derived convergence result for the split chart of H. Jacquet,
*Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §10.2, after (10.5),
author pp. 72–73, and §10.4, Lemmas 10.7–10.8, pp. 79–81.
[Registered author edition](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
