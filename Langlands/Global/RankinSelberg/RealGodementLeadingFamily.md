# Entire continuation in the leading real Godement character

`godementLeadingFamily` varies the leading exponent while keeping the parity, lower coefficient, test and translate fixed. Its values are the first-column Mellin expression at the long Weyl element times the translate. For a coefficient in the lower uniform Whittaker model, the Mellin factor is entire by the column convergence theorem. The outside determinant power is entire because its base is the positive absolute value of a nonzero determinant.

`RealGodementLeadingFamilyStatement` specializes to the open-cell coefficient of a strictly dominant standard K-finite lower vector and a Schwartz test that vanishes outside a compact set of full-row-rank matrices. When the real part of the leading exponent exceeds the first lower position exponent, the expanded upper kernel is jointly absolutely integrable. The additive Jacquet comparison in this chamber identifies the entire family with the open-cell integral of the Godement section.

When the lower coefficient and its actual Weyl inverse-transpose dual belong to the two uniform models at opposite signs, `godementLeadingFamily_reflection` gives reflection of these entire column values. It replaces the leading exponent by its negative and uses the rectangular Fourier test and the lower dual coefficient. The scalar is the leading sign character on \((-1)^{t+1}\). This is the leading-exponent continuation and reflection of Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §7.2, pp. 47–49, and §7.3, pp. 50–51, in the Library's upper-Borel convention. Agreement with the raw upper open-cell integral is proved for the compact full-row-rank test in the chamber.
