# Boundary bounds for normalized square traces

This internal estimate applies to the double integrals in Jacquet (2009), §8.2. It controls a difference of normalized traces by a continuous function of the square Schwartz test difference and an exponential in the imaginary parts of the two exponents.

The difference of the absolute-convergence integrals is the integral of the difference test by integration on the quotient and matrix group product. The common chamber estimate bounds that integral at a fixed spectral real part and a bounded interval of adjoining real parts. Each entire Gamma denominator has exponential bounds on a vertical strip, with continuity on a compact rectangle supplying the bound at bounded imaginary part. The second block is evaluated at the sum of the spectral variable and the adjoining shift. The triangle inequality on imaginary parts gives the two exponents in the final bound.
