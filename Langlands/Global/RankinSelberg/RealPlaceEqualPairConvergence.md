# Convergence for actual real equal-rank place restrictions

The real-place bridge places the two actual restrictions in the uniform
Whittaker models for opposite characters. Their regularity gives the required
covariance and smoothness for all derivative words, with a common polynomial
growth exponent for the words of each coefficient. The real local convergence
theorem with the Fourier parameter of the specified place yields absolute
convergence and holomorphy of the ordinary and dual integrals on one right
half-plane, for the same Schwartz function and quotient measure.

This internal application of the published real convergence theorem, which
formalizes the convergence part of Jacquet 2009, Theorem 2.1, supplies the
analytic part of the test conditions for the actual restrictions.
