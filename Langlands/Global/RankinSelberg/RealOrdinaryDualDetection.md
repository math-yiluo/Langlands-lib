# Pointwise detection after reflection of ordinary real tests

`realOrdinaryDualDetection` assumes exact ordinary continuation, rank-correct real block lists, and irreducibility of both original standard \((\mathfrak g,K)\)-modules. For any nonzero real additive scale `a` and compatible quotient measure, a nonzero ordinary value for the dual lists at \(1-s\) with scales `-a` and `a`, normalized by the dual pair factor, gives a nonzero ordinary value at \(s\) for the original lists with scales `a` and `-a`, normalized by the original pair factor. Each comparison uses the same measure on the original and dual sides. Weyl inverse transpose identifies the corresponding exact compact-finite Whittaker models.

At unequal ranks, realize the dual coefficients as the reflections of a pure compact-finite pair. The normalized reflected comparison writes the dual value as a scalar times a finite sum of primal smooth normalized values. A nonzero sum has a nonzero term, and continuity and density give a nonzero exact primal test at that point. At equal ranks, the opposite-sign Fourier transform gives a Schwartz preimage of the dual row. The normalized equation of the two exact integrals directly gives a nonzero primal value. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, Theorem 2.1(iii), p. 6, and §14.2, pp. 104–107](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
