# Closed reverse intertwiners for real standard modules

`RealStandardClosedReverseStatement` asks for an operator from each positive
rank real standard module in Langlands order to the standard module with the
block list reversed. The operator on compact-finite vectors must be a nonzero
\((\mathfrak g,K)\)-intertwiner. Its continuous complex-linear extension must
have closed range in the genuine smooth carrier of the reversed module.

The reversal of the block list uses the Library's convention in the
[reverse-intertwiner kernel theorem](../../Representation/Archimedean/RealReverseIntertwinerKernel.md).
This isolates the existence and closedness needed from Casselman's
opposite-parabolic deformation argument in *Canonical extensions of
Harish-Chandra modules to representations of \(G\)* (1989), Theorem 10.4.
The statement records agreement on compact-finite vectors and makes no
particular choice of normalization.
