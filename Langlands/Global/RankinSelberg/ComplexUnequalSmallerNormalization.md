# Unequal complex normalization in the smaller coefficient

`complexUnequalSmallerNormalization` takes rank-matched inducing lists, opposite trace characters and a compatible quotient measure. It assumes `HasComplexPairNormalizedGrowth` for every untranslated compact-finite Whittaker pair at those data. This gives entire ordinary and reflected normalizations with exponential strip growth, agreement on a right half-plane, and the normalized functional equation. `complexUnequalNormalizedGrowth` supplies this premise. Given a larger Whittaker coefficient and a smaller Whittaker intertwiner, the theorem constructs, for each right translate of the larger coefficient, a linear map from the smaller compact-finite standard module to entire functions of exponential strip growth. Each normalized value is continuous in the translating matrix at a fixed spectral point. The ordinary and reflected right-half-plane formulas use the image of the smaller vector under the supplied intertwiner.

The translated normalization and uniform bounds on compact translation sets give the convergent right-hand integrals. Linearity there follows from linearity of the smaller Whittaker map and of the integral. Entire uniqueness extends linearity to every spectral point. `smallerNormalization_finiteFamily` applies the two right-hand identities to a finite family with spectral coefficients. In the reflected formula the smaller vector is evaluated at the reflected spectral point.

The ordinary and reflected block integrals are those of [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §2, equations (2.4) and (2.5)](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf).
