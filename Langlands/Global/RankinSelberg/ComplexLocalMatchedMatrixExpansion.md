# Matched compact matrix factors

This internal result supplies the compact factors shared by the finite expansions in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §8.2–8.3, printed pp. 56–60. It assumes the square Schwartz test has finite left unitary span and takes the second Whittaker coefficient as the image of a K-finite inducing vector under a Whittaker intertwiner. The square Fourier transform and matrix functional equation use the additive parameter one.

The finite span supplies a reproducing compact weight for the test with the inverse determinant character. Fourier transport gives the dual reproduction identity with inverse-transpose translation. The matched inducing projection gives both compact Whittaker expansions with one list of coefficient functions. Each companion matrix coefficient is a compact pairing of the same inducing vector. The exact matrix equation with determinant twist gives its two meromorphic continuations and integrability. The growth version applies the normalized matrix comparison to those same coefficients and continuations. It supplies an entire pair with exponential vertical-strip bounds for each summand. A bound for the finite set of individual thresholds gives a common half-plane for all the matrix integrals in the expansion.
