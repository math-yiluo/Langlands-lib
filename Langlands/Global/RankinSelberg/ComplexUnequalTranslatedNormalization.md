# Normalized unequal-rank evaluation on right translates

The Mellin integrals of a pointwise convergent sequence with common integrable
bounds on real spectral intervals converge uniformly along vertical lines in
the common half-plane. The norm of each determinant power depends only on the
real part of the argument, so dominated convergence on each real abscissa
bounds the difference of the integrals along its entire vertical line.

The positive compact averages of the larger right translate are vectors of
the original compact-finite model. Their common simple-root bounds follow
from the bounds for the compact family of translates being averaged. Apply
the ordinary and dual domination theorems with the smaller Whittaker function
fixed. The two Mellin estimates and the given finite-model normalized equations
then give locally uniform convergence of the normalized ordinary values to
an entire function. On the convergence half-plane that function is the
ordinary integral of the genuine translate times the reciprocal L-denominator.

This is a derived approximation step for Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg
integrals* (2009), Theorem 2.3, author pp. 8–9, and the change of trace
character in Remark 2.2, pp. 7–8. It supplies continuity for the specific
compact approximants of one right translate.
