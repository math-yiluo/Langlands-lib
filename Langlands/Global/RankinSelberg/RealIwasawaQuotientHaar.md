# Explicit real Iwasawa measure on the unipotent quotient

In the real Iwasawa chart, conjugation by the positive diagonal with
entries \(e^{y_i}\) scales the \((i,j)\)-th upper entry by
\(e^{y_i-y_j}\). Moving the unipotent factor to the left in group
Haar therefore inserts the inverse modulus
\(\prod_{i<j}e^{y_j-y_i}\) into the logarithmic diagonal measure.

The proof first computes this pushforward on product Lebesgue measure
in upper-entry coordinates. Haar uniqueness extends it to any
unipotent Haar measure. A normalized nonnegative bump on the unipotent
factor then removes that factor from the group integral by the
compatible quotient integration formula. The remaining measure is a
positive scalar multiple of weighted logarithmic diagonal measure
times orthogonal Haar, pushed to the unipotent quotient.

This supplies coordinates for the quotient measure in the ordinary real
integrals in H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009),
equations (2.4) and (2.6).
