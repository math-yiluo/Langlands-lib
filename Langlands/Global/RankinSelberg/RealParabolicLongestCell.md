# Longest-cell support and values of the real parabolic section

`realParabolicLongestCell` gives the support and Weyl-orbit formulas for
`standardSection`. If the two Levi functions have topological support in
their longest cells, the section has topological support in the full
longest cell. At the full Weyl representative times an upper-unipotent
block diagonal and an upper rectangle, the value is the product of the two
Levi Weyl-orbit values and the transverse test at that rectangle.

The compact rectangle support keeps the full support inside the parabolic
pivot chart. The leading pivot and the Schur complement are continuous at
each point of the chart. They take points in the support to points in the
supports of the respective Levi functions. Factor both pivot values on the
Levi longest cells. The row exchange conjugates the lower rectangle to an
upper Borel factor, and it changes the full reversal to the pair of Levi
reversals. These identities put the full point in the longest cell. On the
Weyl orbit the lower rectangle vanishes and the pivot determinant weights
are one, which yields the value formula.

This geometry gives the longest-cell comparison for the compact sections
used in the proof of Proposition 14.1, pp. 103–104 of the author-posted
edition of [H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009)](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf#page=103).
