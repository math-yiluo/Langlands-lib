# Weighted smooth vectors for moderate real coefficients

`RealWhittakerWeightedVectorsStatement` assumes that a smooth function on the positive-rank real general linear group has one unit-height exponent `N` for every iterated right derivative, with constants depending on the derivative word. The function divided by `H^(N+1)` is a genuine smooth vector in the Banach representation with this weight. Its Lie derivative in a matrix direction is the similarly weighted scalar right derivative. The module also bounds the library's real matrix height by a rank-dependent constant times the square of the unit height.

The extra power in the denominator gives vanishing at infinity through compact sublevel sets of the height. In invertible matrix coordinates, the pointwise derivative of the orbit at a matrix `g` in direction `X` is the translated scalar right derivative in direction `g⁻¹ X`, with the same fixed weight at the evaluation point. Expanding in matrix entries expresses the candidate derivative as a finite sum built from the translated right derivatives and the inverse matrix. A mean-value bound transfers continuity of this operator-valued derivative and pointwise differentiability to differentiability in the sup norm. Induction on smoothness order applies the same calculation to the right derivatives, which retain their common height exponent. At the identity, the derivative formula identifies the Banach Lie operator with the weighted right derivative.

`translate_weightedVector_apply` evaluates an actual right translate by translating the original function and dividing by the height power at the evaluation point.
