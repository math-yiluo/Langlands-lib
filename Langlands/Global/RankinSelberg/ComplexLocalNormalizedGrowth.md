# Entire normalized pairs for the complex rank induction

An internal normalized pair consists of two entire functions with exponential growth on vertical strips. On a common right half-plane they agree with the original functions multiplied by the corresponding reciprocal Gamma products. Their functional equation holds everywhere.

The comparison lemma starts with meromorphic continuations satisfying the multiplied-out equation. The identity theorem in meromorphic germs identifies each entire normalization with its reciprocal Gamma product times the corresponding continuation on the codiscrete filter. At points where the two denominators are nonzero, their cancellation gives the normalized equation. Continuity of the normalized functions and of the epsilon factor extends the equation to every point. At the two standard opposite additive parameters the epsilon factors are constant.
