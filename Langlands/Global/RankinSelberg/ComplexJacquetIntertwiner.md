# The canonical complex Jacquet intertwiner

At every inducing parameter the canonical Jacquet evaluation satisfies all requirements of the Library's complex Whittaker intertwiner, and its values belong to the exact Whittaker model with character parameter `-a`.

The right Lie derivative of a compact Godement standard section has two terms. The first differentiates the rectangular test, preserving compact support in full row rank and finite unitary orbit span. The second multiplies the original section by the determinant-character derivative. The continued Godement value has the same formula. The exact canonical evaluation of the original and differentiated generators thus intertwines the right Lie derivative on each generator. Compact Godement spanning and linearity extend the identity to every compact-finite vector. In rank one the canonical evaluation is the original standard vector.

The previously proved left character law, right unitary covariance, smoothness, and moderate growth complete the intertwiner conditions.
