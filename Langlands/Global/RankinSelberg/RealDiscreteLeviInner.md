# The inner Jacquet integral for real discrete blocks

`realDiscreteLeviInnerBound` estimates the integral over the lower simple-root coordinates of any list of pairwise disjoint nonzero-weight slots of a real standard module. The vector is compact finite, the final group argument is orthogonal, and the phases are the real Fourier phases at independent frequencies \(s_i\). The integral exists for product Lebesgue measure and its absolute value is at most \(CR\prod_i |s_i|^{k_i/2}\), where \(R\) bounds the norm of the vector on the orthogonal group and \(k_i\) is the absolute weight of slot \(i\). The constant works for every orthogonal argument and every frequency vector.

On one slot the element \(1+xE_{p+1,p}\) is the product of an upper-triangular matrix and the rotation by \(\arctan x\). For \(\rho=\sqrt{1+x^2}\), the upper-triangular matrix has diagonal \((\rho^{-1},\rho)\) and upper entry \(x/\rho\). The two triangular exponents differ by \(k+1\), so covariance gives the multiplier \(\rho^{-(k+1)}\). Triangular factors and rotations in disjoint slots commute. Applying covariance to each triangular factor gives `lowerBlockKernel_eq_angularIntegrand`, which holds for every final group argument. At orthogonal arguments the compact-profile estimate `realDiscreteLeviCompactProfile` gives the integral bound.

This is an inner integration formula and estimate from the concrete standard-module covariance. The Jacquet open-cell integral and the group-level tempered majorization that motivates it appear in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §7.2 and Proposition 3.4. [Author-posted edition, pp. 21 and 47](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
