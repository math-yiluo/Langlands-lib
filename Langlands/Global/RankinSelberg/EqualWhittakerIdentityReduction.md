# Equal-rank factorization from identity comparison and separate local witnesses

`EqualWhittakerIdentityReductionStatement` concludes the published `EqualArchimedeanFactorizationStatement`. Its identity comparison premise uses the actual spectral tensor presentation. After fixing the finite vector and all but one infinite vector, the global Whittaker functional evaluated at the adelic identity must be proportional to that of a reference pure tensor whose value there is nonzero. The real and complex premises each choose a compatible quotient measure and separate ordinary and dual witnesses with nonzero integral arbitrarily far to the right. Each witness uses actual place restrictions and a Schwartz test function. The dual integral takes the Fourier transform for the specified local additive component. All three premises are restricted to groups of rank at least two (`0 < r` for `GL_(r+1)`).

The proof first obtains a nonzero adelic identity value on an actual pure tensor. Smoothness, compact covariance and Lie covariance of the sliced coefficients extend proportionality of their identity evaluations to their analytic coefficient functions. Finite induction over the vector and group slots gives the normalized Whittaker product and its one-place slices.

The actual real and complex integrals converge absolutely and are holomorphic on right half-planes. With the quotient measure fixed, additivity in each of the two Whittaker coefficients and the Schwartz test combines the ordinary and dual witnesses into one test triple with both integrals nonzero. The proof then applies `equalWhittakerPositiveRankReduction`, which supplies the rank-one case and passes these data to the global factorization.

This internal reduction implements part of the pure-tensor separation and test selection used in Cogdell, *Analytic theory of L-functions for GL_n* (2004), §1.2 and the proof of Theorem 4.1 on pp. 219–220. The three stated locality and nonvanishing premises remain open here.
