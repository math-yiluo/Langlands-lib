# Normalized unequal pairs from the Godement monomial case

The retained premise is the normalized unequal-rank equation with exponential growth on vertical strips for each rotated Gaussian monomial generator. The conclusion, with the same growth condition, holds for every pair of the exact Whittaker models of those ranks.

Fix the smaller Whittaker vector. The canonical range theorem provides an inducing vector for it. The larger functions satisfying joint convergence and normalized reflection with strip growth form a submodule. In the strict chamber the rotated monomial values span the canonical evaluation space, so the assumed monomial reflection gives the canonical equation there. Holomorphic continuation in the inducing parameters removes the chamber and the adjoining exponent threshold. The canonical range theorem then identifies the resulting family of larger values with the exact model. The reduction follows Jacquet’s use of Godement generators and continuation in Section 10.
