# Change of trace character in the normalized equal-rank equation

At any fixed rank, `complexEqualNormalizedCharacterChange` takes absolute convergence and the exact entire normalizations for the standard trace character and gives the same result for every nonzero trace-character parameter.

The existing quotient and integrand changes transport convergence for the two kernels. Their ordinary and dual multipliers are entire in the spectral variable. Multiply the two standard-character entire functions by these multipliers. The integral comparisons show that the resulting entire functions have the required agreements on the right half-plane. The published epsilon comparison identifies the two multipliers at \(s\) and \(1-s\) with the change in the normalized reflection factor.

This is the normalized counterpart of the character change used for the equal-rank local equation. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, Theorem 2.1](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf).
