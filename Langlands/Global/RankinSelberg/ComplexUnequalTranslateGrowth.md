# Normalized growth for right translates of complex Whittaker pairs

`complexUnequalTranslateGrowth` takes rank-matched inducing lists, opposite trace characters and a compatible quotient measure. For every untranslated compact-finite Whittaker pair at these data, it assumes `HasComplexPairNormalizedGrowth` for the ordinary and iterated reflected integrals. This premise gives entire normalizations with exponential strip growth, agreement on a right half-plane, and the normalized functional equation. The theorem gives the corresponding normalized growth and reflection for any two fixed right translates, with the reflected integral now expressed over the product of the quotient and the lower rectangle.

The canonical Whittaker maps realize the two original coefficients. The scaled weighted identity for their translates follows from compact averaging of those maps. Uniform simple-root bounds for compact sets of translation parameters give absolute moments for the ordinary and reflected kernels. The Gaussian Mellin continuation applied to the scaled identity gives the normalized pair with strip growth. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §4, Proposition 4.1, and §§9–10](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
