# Independent rapid determinant weights on the alternate gap kernel

The two rapid weights are evaluated at the smaller quotient determinant and the leading square determinant. Each argument permits a positive scalar depending exponentially on the sum of the retained logarithms. The leading test and smaller right translate use either both original matrices or both inverse transposes. The remaining measurable multiplier has a fixed determinant power and a fixed exponential bound in the tail logs.

The proof descends the translated Whittaker product to the quotient and proves joint measurability of the split tests. It bounds the rapid weights by two independently chosen determinant powers. The effect of the scalar rescalings fits into the arbitrary fixed exponential in the joint alternate majorant. Inverse transpose preserves height. The resulting norm comparison gives joint absolute convergence on the exact triangular chart measure.

`complexGapAlternatePowerBounds` proves the same estimate for independently chosen powers above two lower bounds. The rapid-weight theorem chooses a bound at each threshold. This gives the power moments needed to integrate over the logarithmic scale of the convolution.
