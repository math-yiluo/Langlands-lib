# Two-block restriction of the complex Godement integral

The continued Godement value on a padded smaller matrix, with an optional right factor inside the padding, admits the weighted two-block integral of Jacquet's *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, §§9.1 and 10.1. The formula retains the determinant character of the inserted factor and the half power of the smaller determinant. The measure comparison supplies one positive constant for all input functions and exponents.

The proof pushes the right-tail coordinate measure to general linear Haar measure. The fixed-argument Godement kernel is absolutely integrable, so the pushforward transports its integral and absolute integrability. In the coordinates `h = h₂ diag(h₁,1)`, the smaller Whittaker argument becomes `diag(g h₁⁻¹,1) h₂⁻¹`. Its last row is the last row of `h₂⁻¹`, which is the final frequency column of the partially transformed test. Multiplicativity separates the two determinant factors.
