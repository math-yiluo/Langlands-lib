# Smooth complex Godement sections with compact support

`ComplexGodementCompactSmoothStatement` places a Godement section in the
larger smooth standard module for the list `d :: ds`. The test is smooth
with compact support inside the full-row-rank locus, the input belongs to
the smaller standard module, and the smaller group measure is locally
finite and right invariant.

The proof uses the common compact fiber bound near each larger group
element. On that neighborhood the section agrees with an integral over
a fixed compact subset of the smaller group. The test is evaluated on a
linear function of the larger matrix whose coefficients depend
continuously on the smaller matrix. Thus the published theorem on compact
integration of a smooth function with continuously varying linear maps
applies. The outer determinant weight is smooth on invertible matrices
by its integral angular power and positive radial powers. The previously
proved Borel covariance gives membership in the larger standard module.

[Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, §7.1, formula (7.1) and Proposition 7.1(iii)–(iv), p. 43](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf).
