# Real principal-series evaluation: square matrix continuation at the standard character

**Provenance.** This is an internal evaluation-coefficient case of the real square matrix functional equation in Hervé Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §4.7, pp. 30–31, in the positive Fourier normalization with parameter one. The analytic continuation uses the theta/Mellin mechanism of §4.3, with the convolved Tate pair of `ArchimedeanDeterminantTheta`. The result is for the individual position characters; it does not identify those products with the local factors of a general real Langlands quotient.

Fix `n = r + 1`, a Haar measure `μ` on `GL_n(ℝ)`, a sigma-finite Haar measure on `O(n)`, a smooth section `f` in the principal series for the position characters of an inducing list `ds`, and a square matrix Schwartz test `Φ`. The primal and dual kernels use `f(g)` and `f(g^-T)` respectively, the test `Φ` and `matrixTransform 1 Φ`, and determinant exponent `s + r/2`. For each position let `eᵢ` and `uᵢ` be its parity and exponent. `RealMatrixPositionEvaluationStatement` gives a common right half-plane of absolute convergence and holomorphy, meromorphic continuations `M, Md` of both integrals on `ℂ`, and the relation in the codiscrete filter

`torusL e u s * Md (1 - s) = torusEpsilon e * torusL e (-u) (1 - s) * M s`,

with `torusL e u s = ∏ᵢ Γℝ(s+uᵢ+eᵢ)` and `torusEpsilon e = ∏ᵢ I^eᵢ`. This is evaluation at the identity of the right-translated section, not a general compact-derivative-bounded linear form. There is no assertion of vertical strip bounds, normalized holomorphic multiples, the all-pair comparison, or a Whittaker-model functional equation.

**Lean proof.** The existing right-derivative height estimate with the empty derivative word bounds the section by a polynomial in the real matrix height. Inverse transpose preserves the height by swapping the matrix and inverse entries and transposing indices. `integrable_realMatrixBaseKernel_power` and the determinant power estimate give an initial domain for both kernels. Rapid decay of `detH`, `detK` gives absolute convergence of the two group theta integrals at every positive scale from one integrable power. The paired `RealMatrixTheta` module proves equality of these integrals with the reflection factor. Finally `determinant_pair_continuation` compares their Mellin transforms and the two products of Tate factors. The conjugate parameter in the dual formula is the negative exponent tuple, not complex conjugation. Initial holomorphy follows from `differentiableOn_realMatrixZetaIntegral` after shifting its total exponent by `r/2`; the half-plane is shrunk if needed so both initial convergence and agreement with continuation hold throughout.
