# Normalization at a fixed retained matrix

`complexGapRetainedFiber` gives two identities for the separated integrals that occur in Jacquet's rank induction. Each identity replaces the smaller coefficient of a lower-rank integral by the corresponding normalized square trace. The statement assumes absolute integrability of the product kernel and the square trace formula at the given spectral point.

The proof splits the pair reciprocal L-factor into its square and lower-rank factors. Fubini integrates the leading general linear group first. In the reflected integral, inverse transpose preserves Haar measure on that group and puts the square Fourier transform at its ordinary group argument. The remaining integral is the lower-rank reflected block integral with the substituted smaller coefficient.

The two kernels use the ordinary and reflected separations in [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §10.4](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf).
