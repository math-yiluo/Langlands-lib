# The integrated right Lie identity for canonical complex Jacquet coefficients

Take a continuous curve in the general linear group, with ambient matrix
derivative on the interior of an ordered real interval. Suppose this derivative
is the curve matrix multiplied on the right by a continuous Lie direction.
`ComplexJacquetIntegratedLieStatement` identifies the interval integral of the
canonical Jacquet coefficient of the corresponding standard Lie derivative
with the difference of the original canonical coefficient at the endpoints.
The inducing parameters and the compact-finite standard vector are arbitrary.

In the strict chamber the canonical coefficient is the actual open-cell
coefficient. The chamber intertwiner carries the standard Lie operator to
the group right derivative. The chain rule on the open matrix chart and the
fundamental theorem of calculus give the interval identity there.

For continuation, transfer the same compact profile to each parameter and
expand the moving Lie direction in a real matrix basis. Along each complex
exponent line the evaluated basis derivatives are affine combinations of
fixed-profile families. Their joint continuity in the exponent and curve
variable gives a holomorphic interval integral. The endpoint evaluations
are holomorphic on the same lines. A parameter ball inside the strict chamber
is a product of coordinate disks for the supremum norm. Applying the
one-variable identity theorem in turn to each coordinate extends equality
from that ball to the full parameter space.

This is an internal step toward the group differentiability of the canonical
continuation. It uses Jacquet's continuation and parameter estimates in
*Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §7.2, Proposition 7.2 on printed
p. 48.
