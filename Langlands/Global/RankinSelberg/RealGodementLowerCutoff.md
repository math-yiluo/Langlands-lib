# Smooth lower compact-picture cutoffs for real Godement sections

This module constructs smooth position principal-series sections whose restrictions to the orthogonal group can be localized near the diagonal sign matrices. The compact-picture test multiplies a smooth function of the sum of squared off-diagonal entries by the product of diagonal entries raised to the position parities. Diagonal sign matrices preserve the squared sum and multiply the product by the required principal-series character. The squared sum vanishes on the orthogonal group exactly when the matrix is a diagonal sign matrix.

The extension uses the Library's signed Gram–Schmidt factor and shifted exponent. On the invertible matrices the trailing Gram determinants are positive, so the signed orthogonal factor is smooth by its entry formula. Composing the ambient test with this factor and multiplying by the smooth Gram power gives the smooth section. The existing compact-picture extension laws give upper-Borel covariance and the exact restriction to the orthogonal group.

The cutoff supplies the lower-rank vector for a compact Godement generation argument associated with Hervé Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, Section 7.1, Proposition 7.1(v).

If the scalar angular cutoff is a polynomial, its product with the diagonal parity monomial is a polynomial in the entries on the orthogonal subgroup. The theorem on polynomial right translates gives a finite-dimensional span there. The principal-series extension with the same sign covariance then places the lower section in the K-finite position principal series.
