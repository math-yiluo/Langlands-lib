# Entire normalization at the base of the complex rank induction

`complexEqualNormalizedRankZero` gives absolute convergence on a right half-plane and the two entire normalized integrals for \(\mathrm{GL}_1(\mathbb C)\times\mathrm{GL}_1(\mathbb C)\), for every nonzero trace-character parameter. This supplies the normalized form of the base case used in the adjacent-rank induction.

The published rank-one result supplies convergence. Each Whittaker vector is a scalar times its inducing character. The quotient measure and scalar characters identify the ordinary integral and its dual with the one-coordinate product Tate integrals, with the same scalar. In the dual integral the inducing characters are inverted and the positive Fourier transform is the negative trace transform at the opposite parameter.

The entire product Tate functions on the affine spectral lines \(s+t\) and \(s-t\) provide the two continuations. Their Gamma normalizers agree with the ordinary and dual pair denominators. Scaled Tate reflection, evaluated at the opposite parameter, gives the exact pair epsilon factor. The central sign has exponent zero in this rank.

The computation uses Tate theory for the first step of the principal-series induction. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, Theorem 2.1 and Section 8.1](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf).
