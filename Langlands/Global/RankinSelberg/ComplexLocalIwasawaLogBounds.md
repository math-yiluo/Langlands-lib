# Bounds for the complex Iwasawa logarithms

This internal estimate controls the large group's logarithmic coordinates in the convergence argument for Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §8.2, pp. 55–56. The determinant norm is the exponential of twice the sum of the Iwasawa logarithms. Each positive diagonal entry of a unitary left translate is bounded by a fixed constant times the operator norm of the original matrix.

The unipotent factor has determinant one, so removing it reduces the determinant identity to the published torus and unitary formula. The positive diagonal entries are the diagonal entries of the triangular factor. That factor is the given matrix times the inverse of its Iwasawa unitary factor. The operator norm bounds each entry and is submultiplicative. Compactness bounds the norms of both unitary factors uniformly.
