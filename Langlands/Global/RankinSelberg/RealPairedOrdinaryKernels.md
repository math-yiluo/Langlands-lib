# Terminal tests on pure smooth real Whittaker pairs

`RealPairedOrdinaryKernelsStatement` compares the terminal kernel of a genuine smooth pair on a pure tensor with the ordinary real quotient kernels. It assumes the supplied paired functional takes a pure tensor to the product of its coordinate evaluations. At unequal ranks the common group has the smaller rank, and the pure paired kernel with no row test equals the ordinary unequal kernel with the larger coefficient first. Both orders of the two ambient groups give this identity. At equal ranks it gives the ordinary equal kernel with the same Schwartz row.

Apply the pure tensor formula at the simultaneous embeddings. Padding by an identity block of size zero is the identity. The half-sum of the two gaps is half the difference of ranks in the unequal case and zero in the equal case. In the case where the second factor is larger, commutativity of the two scalar values places it first in the unequal kernel. This identifies the terminal integrands in the rank comparison with the ordinary tests of [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, equations (2.5)–(2.6), p. 6, and Lemma 14.1](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf#page=6).
