# Exact Whittaker vanishing on lower-root-polynomial submodules

`RealWhittakerLeftPolynomialVanishingStatement` concerns a compact and Lie invariant subspace \(Y\) of an exact real standard module. Suppose that, for a fixed simple lower root and a fixed degree bound, every function of \(Y\) is polynomial along left translation by that root subgroup through every group element. Then every exact Whittaker coefficient map is zero on \(Y\), at every nonzero real additive scale.

If a coefficient were nonzero on \(Y\), the Iwasawa decomposition and compact equivariance would give a nonzero evaluation at a positive diagonal on the same subspace. Differentiating upper unipotent covariance makes this evaluation a nondegenerate infinitesimal Whittaker functional. Conjugation by the positive diagonal multiplies each simple-root coefficient by a positive ratio of diagonal entries.

The subspace inherits the ambient infinitesimal character. `Kostant.realWhittakerMaximallyFaithful` places its enveloping-algebra annihilator in the ideal generated by the central elements annihilating it. A central element killing a nonzero vector of \(Y\) kills the entire ambient module, because it acts by a scalar. The restriction of the ambient enveloping-algebra action to \(Y\) therefore satisfies the hypothesis of `realStandardCentralGrowth`. Its degree-\(d\) image has dimension bounded below by a positive constant times \((d+1)^{n(n-1)}\). The polynomial hypothesis gives an upper bound of order \((d+1)^{n(n-1)-1}\) by `realLeftPolynomialGrowth`. These estimates contradict each other.

This is an internal application of the infinitesimal Whittaker criterion and the two enveloping-algebra growth bounds. It applies to exact coefficient spaces in the principal-series reduction of Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), Proposition 11.1.
