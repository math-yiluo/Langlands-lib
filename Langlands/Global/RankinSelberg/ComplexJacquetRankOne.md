# Rank-one Jacquet continuation

`ComplexJacquetContinuation.complexJacquetRankOne` supplies the rank-one base for the uniform Jacquet family used in the Godement continuation. Here the unipotent group and long Weyl element are trivial. The entry-product Haar measure has mass one because the product of the scalar Haar measures is over the empty set of strict upper entries. The open-cell integral therefore equals the standard section at every group argument.

The existing compact-profile extension gives continuity in the group and entire dependence on the exponent. In rank one, the section equals its value at the identity times the inducing character of the determinant. Its absolute value follows from the character norm formula and the determinant in positive Iwasawa coordinates. On an exponent ball of radius one about `p₀`, any natural number at least `2 (‖p₀‖ + 1)` bounds the growth order. The only log-torus coordinate is the scalar diagonal coordinate, and there are no simple roots.
