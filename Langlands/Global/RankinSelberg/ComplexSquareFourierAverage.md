# Fourier integrals of compact averages of square matrix tests

`ComplexSquareFourierAverageStatement` permits exchanging the compact average and the square Fourier integral. It assumes that the pullbacks depend continuously on the compact variable and preserve the product self-dual measure of the matrix entries. The original test and its pointwise weighted average are Schwartz.

The product self-dual measure is additive Haar, so the original test is absolutely integrable. The Fourier character has norm one. Measure preservation in each matrix slice therefore makes the integral of the absolute value equal to the norm of the compact weight times the fixed absolute integral of the test. The compact weight is integrable by continuity and compactness. Fubini gives the result with the original self-dual measure and negative trace sign.

The exchange applies to the unitary translates used in the square zeta functional equation. [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, §4.7](https://doi.org/10.1090/conm/489/09547).
