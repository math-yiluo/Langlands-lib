# Canonical real Jacquet evaluation on Godement generators

`RealGodementCanonicalColumnStatement` identifies the canonical upper Jacquet evaluation of a compact spectral Godement generator with its first-column integral against the canonical lower coefficient. The identity holds at every vector of inducing exponents.

The generator's compact restriction lies in a fixed finite-dimensional space. In a fixed basis its coordinates are finite sums of compact point evaluations. These coordinates are holomorphic by the compact Godement parameter estimate. The upper evaluation of the generator is therefore a finite sum of products of holomorphic coordinates and canonical evaluations of fixed profiles. The lower canonical family has uniform derivative growth on parameter neighborhoods. Restricting the upper exponent vector to its lower entries preserves these local estimates, so the first-column integral is holomorphic as well.

On the strict dominant chamber both canonical coefficients agree with their convergent Jacquet integrals. The compact Godement unfolding gives the asserted equality there. The holomorphic identity theorem extends the equality to every exponent vector. This implements the continuation of the first-column identity in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §7.2.
