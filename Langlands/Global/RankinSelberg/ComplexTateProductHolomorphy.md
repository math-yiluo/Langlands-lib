# Holomorphy of the product of complex Tate weights

For a Schwartz test on a finite complex coordinate space, let the spectral parameter in coordinate `j` be `t j + c j * s`, with complex offset `t j` and real slope `c j`. The product Tate integral is holomorphic on the open set where the real parts of all these parameters are positive. The angular weights are fixed integers, and the measure is additive Lebesgue measure.

Near a point of this set, the kernel at the variable parameter factors as the kernel at the center times an exponential in the sum of the weighted log radii. The product identity also holds on the coordinate hyperplanes where the Tate weights vanish. A sufficiently small real shift of the center in either direction stays within the convergence region. The absolute integrals at the two shifted parameters and at the center majorize the differentiated rescaled kernel on a neighborhood. Dominated differentiation gives complex differentiability, and the open-set theorem for complex differentiable functions gives analyticity.

This supplies holomorphy on the initial regions used for the diagonal Tate integrals in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §7.1, proof of Proposition 7.1, p. 44.
