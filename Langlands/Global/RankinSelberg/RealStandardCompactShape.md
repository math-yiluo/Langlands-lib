# Compact shape determines the Banach carrier of a real standard module

The compact Banach carrier is the norm closure of the compact
restrictions in the continuous functions on the orthogonal group.
For source and target lists with compatible shape, the source
carrier is contained in the target carrier. Two rank-matched lists
with mutual shape compatibility therefore have the same compact
Banach carrier as a subspace of continuous functions.

Compact-finite genuine smooth vectors are dense in the smooth topology.
Applying the continuous empty word map gives a dense subspace of
compact-finite profiles in the Banach carrier. The exponent deformation
lemma transfers the compact-finite profile to the target with identical
compact values. Closedness of the target carrier gives inclusion on
the Banach closures. Mutual compatibility gives the reverse inclusion.
