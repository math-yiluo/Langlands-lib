# Real square matrix theta identity for the standard positive character

**Provenance.** This is an internal determinant-scale version of the real principal-series matrix identity in Hervé Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §4.7, pp. 30–31, specialized to evaluation at the identity and to the explicit convolved real Tate pair `detH`, `detK`. The Fourier phase is positive with parameter one; Jacquet's Fourier phase has a minus sign relative to their input additive character. No identification with a Whittaker integral is made here.

For `n = r + 1`, take a smooth section `f` of the real principal series for characters `χᵢ(x) = sgn(x)^eᵢ |x|^uᵢ`, Haar measures on the group and on `O(n)`, and a Schwartz square matrix test `Φ`. The base kernel is `Φ(g) f(g) |det g|^(r/2)`. The dual uses `matrixTransform 1 Φ` and `f(g^-T)` instead. `realMatrix_theta_at_one` states that the integral of the primal base kernel times `detH e u (a * |det g|)` equals `a` times the integral of the dual base kernel times `detK e u (a⁻¹ * |det g|)` for `a > 0`, assuming absolute convergence of these two group integrals.

`realMatrix_theta_at_one_of_pair` proves the same identity for any measurable determinant-scale kernels `Hk`, `Kk` in place of `detH e u`, `detK e u`. Its hypothesis is that `(Hk, Kk)` satisfies the torus theta identity at scale `a` for every Schwartz test on `ℝⁿ`. `realMatrix_theta_at_one` is its instance for the convolved Tate pair, whose torus identity is `determinant_theta_identity` with the Schwartz integrability bounds.

**Lean proof.** `realTriangularMatched` gives the two compact-diagonal integrations with the same Haar constant. The inner tests at `k ∈ O(n)` are the Schwartz upper-triangular marginal of the compact right translate of `Φ` and its positive Fourier transform. At parameter one, the self-dual real scalar measure is Lebesgue measure, and `realFourierTransform_one_eq_fourierInvN` identifies the positive diagonal Fourier formula with the determinant torus formula. For each fixed `k` the multiplicative diagonal integral becomes the additive Tate density integral by `realDiagonalTateIntegration`. The inverted character keeps its parity and negates its exponent; the determinant scale of a unit diagonal is exactly the product of absolute values. For the convolved pair, the Schwartz integrability bounds supply the two premises of `determinant_theta_identity`; in general the torus identity is the hypothesis. Finally Fubini is applied to the two integrable compact-diagonal functions, and the pointwise theta equality is integrated with the common compact factor `f(k)` and Haar constant. The functional equation itself additionally uses initial convergence and Mellin continuation.
