# Smooth vector factorization along real rectangular rows

`RealRectangularSmoothTransferStatement` compares a real rectangular prefix with the maximal rectangle for a smooth vector of a Banach representation. The larger group acts through a supplied homomorphism. The hypotheses give the commuting lower-row exponential lifts, the Whittaker covariance of the evaluation, and moderate growth for the upper columns used in Fourier averaging.

Smooth vector factorization along the lower row inserts one row integral with a compactly supported smooth weight. The upper-column character formula evaluates at the pairing with that row, so Schwartz averaging absorbs the weight into a genuine smooth vector. This averaging uses a single vector for all evaluation forms in the quotient integral. Changing Lebesgue measure to the supplied row Haar measure gives the corresponding inverse scalar in the profiles. The determinant weight remains fixed. Applying the finite Fubini comparison to these identities gives the terminal rectangular sum and absolute integrability of the prefix.

The rectangular integrals use equation (2.5) in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), p. 6. The representation-theoretic ingredients are the smooth factorization and column averaging of §6.1, pp. 36–37. [Author-posted edition](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf)
