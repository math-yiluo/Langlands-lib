# Right differentiation of compactly averaged complex coefficients

This internal lemma handles the Whittaker and inducing-function averages in Jacquet §8.2. Given a continuous compact family of group elements and a continuous scalar weight, the average of the corresponding left translates of a smooth function is smooth and commutes with every real right derivative.

On the open set of invertible matrices, the smooth scalar evaluations assemble into a smooth family with values in the continuous functions on the compact parameter space. Weighted integration is continuous linear on that space. The chain rule computes the derivative of the integral, and the continuous evaluation maps identify its integrand with the derivative at each compact parameter. The right derivative is the ambient derivative in direction `gX`. Left multiplication carries this direction to `(kg)X`, which gives the required identity.
