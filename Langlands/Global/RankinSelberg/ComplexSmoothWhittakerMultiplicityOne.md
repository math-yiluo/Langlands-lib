# Multiplicity one for smooth complex Whittaker functionals

`ComplexSmoothWhittakerMultiplicityOneStatement` compares smooth principal-series functionals with the same unipotent character and finite compact difference bounds. Each is proportional to any fixed nonzero functional of this kind.

The chart restriction is a continuous distribution. Multiply its tests by the Whittaker character in the open chart coordinates. Exact right covariance of the section extension and multiplicativity of the character make the resulting test distribution right invariant. Haar uniqueness expresses it as a scalar multiple of integration. Multiplication by the reciprocal character recovers the original restriction as the integral twisted by that reciprocal.

Restriction injectivity forces the Haar coefficient of the nonzero reference functional to be nonzero. Subtract its appropriate multiple from the other functional. The difference retains exact covariance and a bound of order equal to the larger of the two difference orders. Its open-chart restriction is zero, so restriction injectivity makes the difference zero on the entire smooth principal series.
