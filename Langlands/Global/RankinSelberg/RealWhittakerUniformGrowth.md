# Uniform growth in the exact real Whittaker model

For any real block list of total rank \(r+1\) and any nonzero additive
parameter, `realWhittakerIntertwinerUniformGrowth` gives a single
polynomial height exponent for the outputs of each specified
coefficient map. The constants may depend on the source vector.

`realStandardFiniteGeneration` gives a finite-dimensional generating
subspace of the actual compact-finite standard module.
`intertwiner_of_wordSpan_eq_top` extends the estimates for the images
of a basis of that subspace and their right derivatives to every
source vector.

The resulting equality `realWhittakerModels_eq` identifies
`realWhittakerModel` with `realUniformWhittakerModel` for all
rank-correct inducing lists. The inclusion in the other direction
uses the defining conditions on a uniform coefficient map. This
is an internal comparison between two spaces of coefficients of
the same standard module.
