# Absolute convergence of the Weyl-translated alternate integral

`ComplexGapWeylConvergenceStatement` proves absolute integrability
of the sheared kernel in `ComplexGapWeylUnfolding`. It assumes
Schwartz representatives for the leading test, the first physical
column test and the joint frequency test. The two right-half-plane
conditions are on the quotient determinant exponent and the
combined leading determinant exponent. Their thresholds are
independent of the split tests.

The proof evaluates the sheared kernel on the sparse representative
and groups the frequency factors as the given Schwartz test. The
continuous quotient section gives measurability of that expression.
The determinant character and quotient powers give the two real
determinant rates in the joint estimate. The Fourier Jacobian and
Haar densities are absorbed by the exponential in the absolute
tail logarithms. Product measure transport with a point mass at
zero handles the zero lower rectangle. The absolute estimate then
gives integrability on the actual weighted Haar chart.

This gives the convergence of the alternate expression for
H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), Lemma 10.3
on author p. 74, using the estimates in Lemmas 10.7–10.8 on
author pp. 79–81.
