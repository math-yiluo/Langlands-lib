# The equal-rank limit for arbitrary square Schwartz tests

This internal continuation step extends the finite square matrix products in Jacquet (2009), §8.2, to every square Schwartz test. It uses normalized smaller-rank integrals with exponential strip growth in the finite products.

Choose a sequence of left unitary finite tests converging in Schwartz space. The exact positive square Fourier operator sends this sequence to a sequence converging to the Fourier partner of the given test. Along a line whose adjoining shift is the spectral variable plus a fixed offset, the right boundary lies in the primal chamber and the left boundary lies in the reflected Fourier chamber. For a bounded interval of real offsets, both boundary lines can be chosen uniformly. The normalized trace estimates and continuity in the test give weighted Cauchy bounds there. The triangle inequality for imaginary parts gives the boundary exponents needed for the affine strip extension.

The resulting limit is holomorphic along entire parameter curves and has exponential vertical strip growth for every fixed adjoining shift. At each point of the two chambers, the same trace estimate gives convergence to the actual normalized square integral. Uniqueness of the pointwise limit identifies both traces.
