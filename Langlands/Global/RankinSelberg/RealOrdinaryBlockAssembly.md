# Real block continuation from character growth and tempered families

The deformation `deformBlocks` sends each exponent \(u\) to \(z\operatorname{Re}u+i\operatorname{Im}u\), with the discrete shapes fixed. It keeps the size of the general linear group and the determinant multiplier at \(-1\) fixed. At \(z=1\) it recovers the given list. At \(\operatorname{Re}z=0\) all exponents have zero real part.

`RealOrdinaryHolomorphicTestFamiliesStatement` asks for a lift of each pair of exact unequal-rank or positive-index equal-rank test vectors along this deformation. The family must recover the specified coefficients at \(z=1\) and lie in the corresponding exact Whittaker models for \(|z|<3\). With the additive parameter, compatible quotient measure and any Schwartz test fixed, the ordinary integral of the two families must be jointly holomorphic on that disk times a common right half-plane.

`RealOrdinaryHolomorphicTestFamiliesOn S` requires uniform ascending character-model membership on the parameter disk, and requires exact original block-model membership at points of \(S\) in that disk. `realOrdinaryBlockAssemblyOnImaginaryAxis` uses this family condition for \(S=\{z:\operatorname{Re}z=0\}\). The ascending memberships give the expanded normalization and meromorphic functional equation on all slices. For the half-plane convergence of the original and dual integrals, the proof applies the tempered bound to the original block memberships at imaginary parameters.

`realOrdinaryBlockAssembly` proves the ordinary normalized continuation statement conditional on four inputs. These are the unequal-rank and positive-index equal-rank character-pair normalized growth statements, the scalar tempered Whittaker coefficient bound at every positive index, and the holomorphic test family statement. The character-pair hypotheses give entire normalizations of both spectral functions with strip growth and the normalized functional equation. The resulting ordinary normalizations are by the original block factors and use the exact coefficient model and the original quotient measure. The two rank-one inputs are supplied by the proved rank-one ordinary normalization and the rank-one tempered bound.

For a family in the original block models throughout the disk, equality of the exact and uniform models and the ascending principal-series quotient supply the ascending memberships. The pair factors are invariant under permuting the lists. The proof transports the meromorphic functional equation to the block lists by equality of the gamma multipliers off a discrete set. Positive real part convergence on the imaginary parameter locus follows from the tempered bound, regularity and the toral gauges. The joint expanded continuation and holomorphic division by the shift polynomial give the prescribed normalizations at the endpoint. This assembles the passage from character principal series to arbitrary real block lists in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, Proposition 11.1, p. 82 of the author-posted edition.

For a fixed unequal test, `realOrdinaryUnequalBlockAssembly` takes character growth at its pair of ranks and a scalar tempered estimate at each of those ranks. For a fixed equal test, `realOrdinaryEqualBlockAssembly` takes both estimates at the common rank. Each theorem takes the specified test's family on the imaginary-axis locus and yields its entire normalization by the original block factor. The all-rank assembly applies these two theorems and uses the rank-one normalization at equal index zero.
