# Factorization of the compact real parabolic trace

`realParabolicTraceProduct` fixes a nonzero real additive scale and rank-matched block lists for the two Levi groups. Given a continuous Whittaker functional on the smooth standard module of their concatenation and nonzero continuous Whittaker functionals on the two smooth Levi modules, it gives a single scalar. On every compact transverse test section with pure Levi inputs, the ambient functional is the scalar times the two Levi evaluations times the negative boundary Fourier moment. The conclusion allows a zero scalar.

On a fixed transverse test, evaluation of the section at a fixed group element is a constant times the two Levi point evaluations. On the pivot cell the parabolic coordinates give that formula. Off the cell the constant is zero. Each point evaluation of a genuine smooth Levi vector is continuous by the empty-word coordinate, the fixed Banach translation, and compact evaluation at the identity. When one Levi vector is fixed, the graph of the resulting section map is the intersection of the closed scalar evaluation equalities. It is complete as a closed subspace of the product of the smooth carriers. The Fréchet open mapping theorem applied to the graph projection makes the section map continuous.

Evaluating at a transverse test of moment one gives the scalar-valued bilinear form from `RealParabolicTraceLevi`. Its two covariance identities and separate continuity let `RealExactWhittakerUniqueness` identify each slice with a multiple of the corresponding Levi functional. Evaluating the first slice at a vector of first Levi value one makes the resulting multiplier a slice for the second Levi. `RealParabolicTransverseTrace` supplies the moment for an arbitrary compact test. This derives the compact-test proportionality associated with the split trace in [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, Proposition 14.1, pp. 103–104](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf#page=103).
