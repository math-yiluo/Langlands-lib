# Haar measures on the real rank-raising subgroup

Use the coordinates \((x,h)\) of `rankRaisingHomeomorph` with leading size \(m=r+1\). Given an additive Haar measure for the row coordinates and a sigma-finite bi-invariant Haar measure on the trailing group, the left Haar measure has density \(|\det h|^{-m}\) relative to the product. Right Haar measure has density \(|\det h|\). `RealRankRaisingHaarStatement` proves left Haar, right invariance, finiteness on compacts and positivity on nonempty open sets on the right. It also proves that the left measure is the density \(|\det h|^{-m-1}\) times the right measure.

The product law is \((x,h)(y,k)=(x a(k)^{-1}+hy,hk)\), where \(a(k)=\operatorname{diag}(\det k,1,\ldots,1)\). For left multiplication, first shear the row by \(h^{-1} x a(k)^{-1}\), then apply the diagonal map \((y,k)\mapsto(hy,hk)\). The row measure acquires the inverse Jacobian \(|\det h|^{-m}\), and the translated trailing density acquires \(|\det h|^m\). For right multiplication, shear by \(k x a(h)\) and then apply \((y,k)\mapsto(y a(h)^{-1},kh)\). The row measure acquires \(|\det h|\), and the trailing density acquires its inverse. In each case the shear preserves the weighted product and the two factors cancel. The positive continuous weights preserve local finiteness and positivity on open sets. The chart transports the statements to the compensating subgroup.

Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, Lemma 14.1, p. 106 in the author-posted edition, uses this compensating subgroup and the right weight \(|h|\,dx\,d^\times h\). The left measure and its comparison with the right measure follow from the displayed multiplication law.
