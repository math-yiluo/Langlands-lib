# Normalized compact averages from opposite-cell contraction

Fix a compact-finite standard vector with nonzero mass on the opposite
cell. Average its genuine right translate by the diagonal with entries
ε^i against the input vector evaluated at the inverse compact variable.
The inverse coefficient has finite left translate span, by restriction of
the input's finite right translate span along inversion. The exact compact
projection theorem therefore gives a standard vector and the same
averaging formula for its image under any exact Whittaker intertwiner.
The uniqueness of its pointwise values and linearity of integration give
a complex-linear operator on the standard module.

At a compact point the average can be written as a compact pairing by
left invariance of Haar measure. The dual compact probe gives the same
pairing, and its product with the translated section has the modulus
character. The compact modulus integral replaces this pairing by an
absolutely convergent integral on the opposite cell. Diagonal contraction
gives the real Jacobian ε^D. The translated section at the contracted
point is the original section multiplied by its inducing character on
the contracting diagonal. These two factors and the rank-dependent
compact integral constant are removed by the normalization, leaving the
opposite-cell mass in the denominator. The Whittaker formula retains the
full normalization multiplying the original compact averaging integral.

This internal calculation connects the compact projection of an exact
intertwiner to the diagonal approximation in the smooth compact picture.
