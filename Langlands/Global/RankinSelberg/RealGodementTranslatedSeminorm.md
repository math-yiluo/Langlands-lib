# Polynomial Schwartz seminorm bounds for real rectangular right translates

For any continuous real seminorm on the rectangular Schwartz space and positive upper rank, the seminorm of a right translate of the test is bounded by a fixed power of the real group height times a constant and a finite supremum of defining Schwartz seminorms of the test. The exponent, constant and finite supremum are chosen before the group element and test.

Right multiplication has operator norm bounded by the row-sum matrix norm. The same bound for right multiplication by the inverse controls the loss of spatial decay. Each of these row-sum norms is bounded by the upper rank times the group height. The weighted derivative estimate for a linear pullback gives the result for a defining Schwartz seminorm. A continuous seminorm is dominated by a finite supremum of defining seminorms, so the largest sum of derivative order and decay order works throughout that finite set.

This is a derived estimate for the group growth in Jacquet's Godement calculation, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §7.2, Proposition 7.2, pp. 48–49.
