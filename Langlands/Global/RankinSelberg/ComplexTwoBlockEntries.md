# Matrix entries of the complex two-block Haar coordinates

The diagonal factor of the two-block Haar chart is the standard consecutive diagonal block sum. In particular, its first-block embedding is the padding used in the Rankin–Selberg integrands. Its determinant is the product of the two determinants, and inverse transpose acts on both blocks individually. Multiplying on the left by the first-block embedding preserves the last row.

The proof compares the block shape's indices with the first and second summands in the standard finite sum of indices. The determinant and inverse transpose identities then follow from the formulas for block matrices and simultaneous reindexing.
