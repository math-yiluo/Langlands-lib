# Descent to full-row-rank complex rectangles

A smooth complex standard-module vector divided by the leading determinant
weight depends only on the trailing rows. If two invertible matrices have
the same trailing rows, their quotient has the trailing rows of the
identity. It factors as a leading diagonal element times an upper
unipotent element. Its Borel character equals its leading determinant
weight, so this factor cancels in the normalized vector.

The explicit cofactor completion defines the descended function. Its
determinant is positive real on the full-row-rank locus, so the
character weight reduces there to a product of smooth powers of
positive real numbers. This gives the smooth rectangular function used
in the upper-Borel form of Jacquet's Proposition 7.1(v), pp. 45–46.
The proof adapts the Library's real row descent.
