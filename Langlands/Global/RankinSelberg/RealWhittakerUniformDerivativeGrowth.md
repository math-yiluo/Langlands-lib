# Uniform derivative growth in the real ordinary Whittaker model

Finite systems for the right-coordinate square sum give a single matrix
height exponent for every right derivative word of a real Whittaker
coefficient. The estimate applies to the coefficient maps on the ordinary
compact-finite standard module.

First bound the system and its first derivatives for all translations in a
fixed compact subset of the matrix chart. The local elliptic interior
estimate bounds every jet at the identity with the same height exponent.
Applying the product rule to the linear matrix vector fields gives the
corresponding bound for right derivative words.

Fix the exponent supplied by a nonzero compact-finite vector. The vectors
whose coefficients have bounds at that exponent for all right derivative
words form a subspace. Adding a matrix direction at the end of a word proves
Lie stability. Compact translation conjugates the directions in a word and
changes the height bound by a constant, which proves orthogonal stability.
Irreducibility makes this subspace the full standard module. The common
exponent for the empty word then places the coefficient map in the uniform
model. Taking the spans of the ranges gives equality of the two Whittaker
submodules in `RealIrreducibleWhittakerModelUniformityStatement`.

The construction uses the local growth setup in H. Jacquet,
*Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §2, pp. 3–4 of the
author-posted version, and the matrix elliptic estimates in the Library.
