# The compact picture as the leading exponent varies

`RealGodementCompactPictureStatement` gives a family of smooth real position principal-series vectors with fixed restriction to the orthogonal group. The leading exponent ranges over the complex plane and its parity stays fixed. Evaluation of the family at each fixed group element is entire.

Lean applies the signed Gram–Schmidt extension to the restriction of the reference vector. The diagonal sign characters stay the same as the exponent varies, so the extension has the required covariance. Smoothness comes from the smoothness of the reference vector on invertible matrices and the smooth signed orthogonal factor. To check entireness, the proof fixes an Iwasawa decomposition of the evaluation point. Each positive absolute diagonal entry is raised to an affine function of the exponent. The finite product of those entire powers multiplies a fixed compact value.

This is an internal compact-picture result for the leading-character continuation in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §7.2, pp. 47–48. It applies to the position principal series of the Library and uses the Library normalization of the diagonal character.
