# Holomorphic real Godement sections with varying lower data

Fix a continuous rectangular test that vanishes outside a compact full-row-rank set. Suppose that the lower vectors depend jointly continuously on the spectral and group variables, and holomorphically on the spectral variables at each fixed group element. A holomorphically varying leading exponent gives a holomorphic upper Godement section at every fixed upper group element.

The Lean proof uses the compact preimage of the test support for the fixed upper argument. The integrand vanishes outside this preimage. The determinant weight is jointly continuous by its exponential formula, and the integrand is holomorphic in the parameters. Continuity on the product of a compact parameter set and the compact preimage supplies the bounds for integration against the restricted finite Haar measure. The parameter integral theorem gives holomorphy, and multiplication by the outer determinant weight preserves it.
