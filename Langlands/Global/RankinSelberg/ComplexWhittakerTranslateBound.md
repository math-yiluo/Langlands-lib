# Polynomial dependence on complex Whittaker right translations

A local complex Whittaker vector has a common growth order in logarithmic torus coordinates for every simple-root decay power. When its argument is multiplied on the right by an arbitrary group element, the same bound on the original argument holds with a polynomial factor in Jacquet's matrix height of the multiplier.

The positive diagonal of the triangular Iwasawa factor and its reciprocal are entries of that factor and its inverse. Submultiplicativity of the height and compactness of the unitary factors control both by the height of the right multiplier. The absolute logarithmic coordinates of the Iwasawa displacement are therefore bounded by the logarithm of this height times a fixed constant. The triangle inequality for the coordinate sum and the estimate on the positive parts of adjacent differences turn the displacement into the displayed polynomial factor.
