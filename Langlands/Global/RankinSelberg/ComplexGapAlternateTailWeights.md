# Determinant and Haar weights in the alternate complex chart

`ComplexGapAlternateTailWeightsStatement` bounds the product of the
Fourier Jacobian, the norm of the adjoining character and the two
logarithmic Haar densities by an exponential in the sum of the
absolute tail logarithms. It applies to the fixed retained matrix
and every inserted upper-unipotent tail.

The retained determinant has normalized absolute value equal to
the exponential of twice the sum of the tail logarithms. The compact
and upper-unipotent factors have normalized determinant one. The
Fourier Jacobian and right-tail Haar factor contribute inverse
powers of this determinant. The tail modulus contributes an
exponential of a sum of root differences. The triangle inequality
bounds the character, determinant and root exponents by a
constant times the sum of absolute logarithms.

This bound is derived from the densities in H. Jacquet,
*Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), §10.2, author pp. 72–73,
at (10.5) and the subsequent alternate chart.
