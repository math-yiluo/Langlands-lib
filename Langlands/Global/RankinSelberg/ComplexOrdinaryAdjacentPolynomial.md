# Adjacent polynomial normalization from the lower equal rank

At a trace scale of unit modulus, polynomiality for the lower equal-rank integrals with standard Gaussian row tests implies polynomiality for every ordinary adjacent-rank test. The normalization uses the full pair L-factor of the prescribed lists. The equal rank-one base is the polynomial Gaussian Tate calculation.

In a strong convergence chamber the scale-one rectangular Gaussian Godement sections span the larger compact-finite standard representation. The Jacquet evaluation of each such section has a polynomial ordinary normalization by the split Gaussian formulas, and their linear span inherits this property. Along a holomorphic line in the inducing exponents, a fixed compact profile gives an ordinary integral that is jointly holomorphic on a common right half-plane over a bounded parameter disk. The polynomial-fiber continuation extends polynomiality along that line. Continuing first the adjoining exponent and then the retained exponents reaches all parameters. The exact Whittaker model is the range of the continued Jacquet map, so the conclusion applies to every prescribed test vector.
