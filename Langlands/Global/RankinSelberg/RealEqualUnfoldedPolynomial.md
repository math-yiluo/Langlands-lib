# Polynomial normalization of the unfolded real equal-rank integral

`RealEqualUnfoldedPolynomialStatement` assumes the ordinary polynomial
normalization at the smaller adjacent ranks and joint absolute
convergence beyond given leading and spectral abscissas. For a standard
square polynomial Gaussian, the unfolded equal-rank integral beyond the
given leading abscissa with positive leading real part is a polynomial in
the spectral variable times the prescribed pair factor. The spectral
abscissa can be increased. The polynomial may depend on the leading
exponent.

A compact reproducing weight gives a finite expansion of the larger
coefficient into Whittaker values and compactly paired principal-series
functions. The shifted products formula separates the adjacent integrals
from the square integrals. Each square term is normalized by the pair
factor of the leading sign character and the larger list, evaluated at
the shifted spectral variable. Combining it with the smaller adjacent
factor gives the prescribed factor for the list with the leading
character adjoined. The polynomial is a finite sum of the adjacent
polynomials times compositions of the square polynomials with the same
spectral shift. The module also checks that the square test built from a
standard rectangular matrix test and a standard Gaussian row has the
required polynomial Gaussian form.

This is the unfolded equal-rank step in Jacquet, *Archimedean
Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, equation (8.1) and §8.4, Proposition 8.1,
pp. 60–61.
