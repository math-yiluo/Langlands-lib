# Phased inducing sections on the longest real Bruhat cell

`realLongestCellSection` constructs a smooth vector in a normalized real
character principal series for any nonzero real additive scale. Its
topological support lies in the longest Bruhat cell. On the right
upper-unipotent orbit of the long Weyl element, its value equals the
Whittaker character times a nonnegative compactly supported function whose
value at the identity is one.

Multiplication by the long Weyl element changes the cell into an upper times
lower unitriangular chart. Its smooth inverse recovers the upper Borel
factor and the unipotent factor. The section multiplies the inducing
character of the first factor by the phase and a smooth bump of the second
factor. The support is contained in the product of the closed Borel with a
compact subset of the Weyl orbit. This product is closed in the group and
contained in the cell, so extension by zero is smooth. The inducing law
follows from multiplication of the left factor by a Borel element.

This is a derived construction for the compact sections in the proof of
Proposition 14.1, pp. 103–104 of the author-posted edition of
[H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009)](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf#page=103).
