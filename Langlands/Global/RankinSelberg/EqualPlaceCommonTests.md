# A common equal-rank test from separate actual place witnesses

Fix a compatible quotient measure at a real or complex place. The place lemmas
take absolute convergence and holomorphy on a right half-plane for every
ordinary and dual test. They also take one ordinary and one dual witness, each
with a nonzero value arbitrarily far to the right. The two
witnesses may use different actual Whittaker restrictions and Schwartz
functions. The lemmas find one pair of actual restrictions and one Schwartz
function giving both nonzero integrals.

Finite right translation absorbs the finite offset of each actual restriction.
With the finite offset set to the identity, evaluation at each single-place
point is the published linear Whittaker functional in a global cuspidal vector.
Dualizing these functions amounts to precomposing by the Weyl and inverse
transpose map, which preserves addition. The Fourier test map is additive by
integration of Schwartz functions against the bounded unitary character.

The absolute convergence hypothesis gives additivity of the integral germs on
right half-planes. The shared-test lemma then chooses two cuspidal vectors and
one Schwartz function with nonzero germs for both integrals. Holomorphy of
the resulting integrals follows from the holomorphy hypothesis. Each
nonzero germ gives a nonzero value beyond the corresponding convergence
threshold.
