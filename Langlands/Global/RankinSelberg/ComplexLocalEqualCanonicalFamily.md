# Ordinary equal-rank integrals along affine inducing lines

`ComplexLocalEqualCanonicalFamilyStatement` takes two canonical Jacquet
families, each following an affine complex line of inducing exponents
with a fixed compact profile. The two families carry opposite Whittaker
characters. For any disk in the line parameter and any row Schwartz
test, both the primal and dual ordinary equal-rank integrals converge on
uniform spectral right half-planes and are jointly holomorphic in the
line and spectral parameters there.

For each family the proof transfers its initial compact-finite vector
along the line by retaining its compact restriction. The transferred
vector gives the canonical Jacquet value at the new parameter, so the
exact intertwiner property supplies the left Whittaker law. The dual
coefficient has the opposite character. The two families are
holomorphic at each group argument and have uniform simple-root bounds
on the image of the closed parameter disk. Weyl inverse transpose
preserves the form of these estimates and preserves continuity of the
group slices.

The proof applies the ordinary equal-rank family theorem twice, using
the given row Schwartz function for the primal integral and the Schwartz
representative of its trace Fourier transform for the dual. It takes the
maximum of the two resulting spectral bounds. This internal family
estimate provides the ordinary half-plane inputs to the continuation
argument of Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009),
§8.1 and §8.2, pp. 53–55.
