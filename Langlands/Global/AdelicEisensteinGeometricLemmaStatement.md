# Standard parabolics in the adelic geometric lemma

Let \(P\) and \(Q\) be canonical standard parabolics for a common maximal split torus and a system of positive relative roots. Let \(n\) be a rational normalizer point such that its Weyl action sends the simple Levi roots of \(P\) to positive relative roots, and the inverse action has the corresponding property for \(Q\). Then the parabolics \(P_n\) and \(Q_n\) of the geometric lemma are represented by canonical standard parabolics, with actual inclusion morphisms into \(P\) and \(Q\). Their rational images in the adelic group satisfy `AdelicGroupPoints.IsGeometricLemmaDatum`.

The inclusions identify the ambient maps with the canonical standard maps. Consequently, their usual parabolic and geometric radical data are available from `Standard.parabolicStructure`. A compact subgroup with Iwasawa coverage for the minimal standard parabolic also has coverage for both smaller parabolics.

This is the rational subgroup clause of Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series*, Lemma 6.7(i)–(ii), in the form used by the adelic constant-term statement for (6.17).
