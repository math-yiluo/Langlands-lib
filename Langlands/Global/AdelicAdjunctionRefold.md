# Refolding the common quotient

A principal element of the common subgroup acts through each parabolic's inducing subgroup. The cutoff from the target height and the twisted product are therefore invariant under the common subgroup. The Iwasawa unfolding formula applies to this product.

In the resulting radical integral, the target test form and target height are independent of the radical variable. The remaining integral is the defining intertwining integral, with the target untwisting character. Integrability on the common quotient justifies Fubini and gives integrability of the pairing integrand. Rational Levi invariance then gives the pairing over the discrete Levi quotient. This is the refolding step of Mœglin–Waldspurger II.1.8.
