# Splitting off the finite factor of the adelic operator tensor

`splitFiniteTensor` identifies the adelic operator tensor with the ordinary tensor product of its finite factor and the indexed tensor over the infinite places. It uses the dependent tensor equivalence for a sum of index sets, evaluates the singleton finite tensor, and applies the canonical linear equivalence from each universe-lifted infinite slot to its original carrier.

The formula on pure tensors determines the comparison of operators. The finite slot endomorphism acts on the left factor. An endomorphism in one infinite slot acts on the corresponding place of the right factor after transporting it through the universe-lift equivalence. These identities follow by applying the pure tensor formula to the family of slot endomorphisms and extending linearly.
