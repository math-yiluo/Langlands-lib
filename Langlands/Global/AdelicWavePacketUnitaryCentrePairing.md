# Pairing a wave-packet lift against a unitary central character

`WavePacketUnitaryCentrePairingStatement` is the quotient product step used with split-centre Fourier inversion before Lemma 7.11 of Delorme 2023, p. 35. Let \(\Phi\) be left invariant under the principal rational subgroup and transform under the positive split centre by the character corresponding to \(i\nu\). Let \(\Theta\) be left invariant under the subgroup generated by the positive split centre and the principal rational subgroup. Multiply \(\Theta\) by `ambientWavePacketProfile` for a coefficient in any finite real parameter chart. If the central integral of the profile times the conjugate character is nonzero, the lifted pairing against \(\overline\Phi\) on the full rational quotient is absolutely integrable exactly when the pairing of \(\Theta\) against \(\overline\Phi\) is absolutely integrable on the norm-one rational quotient. The total Bochner integral on the full quotient is the central integral times the integral on the norm-one quotient. The statement takes the nonzero central integral as its premise and places no smoothness or support condition on the coefficient.

The proof constructs the product chart by transporting `PositiveRealTorus.rationalQuotientDecomposition` to the actual positive split centre. The normalization agrees by `intrinsicPositiveHaarMeasure_eq`, so the chart is measure preserving. On a product representative \([an]\), the profile depends only on \(a\), the relative function is unchanged by \(a\), and \(\Phi(an)\) is its value at \(n\) times the central character. The product formula gives the integral. Absolute integrability on the product gives integrable sections almost everywhere. If the relative pairing were nonintegrable, these sections would force the central factor to vanish almost everywhere, contradicting its nonzero integral. In the other direction, the integrable factors give an integrable product.
