# Positive logarithmic coordinates for a standard parabolic

The character logarithm is a multiplicative equivalence from the positive split component of the actual standard parabolic to the additive real character dual, written multiplicatively. The real dual is finite-dimensional.

Character restriction through the Levi and the split component is injective with finite-index image. A split-torus presentation then gives the published positive section of the ambient logarithm. This section takes values in the actual positive component and recovers every positive point from its logarithm. The same character restriction and torus coordinates give finite-dimensionality. This provides the positive coordinates used for the generalized exponent spaces in Bernstein–Lapid §6.1.
