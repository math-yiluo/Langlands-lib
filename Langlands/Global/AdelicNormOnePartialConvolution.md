# Norm-one partial convolutions of functions on the positive rational quotient

Module `Langlands.Global.AdelicNormOnePartialConvolution`.

Let `F` be a number field, `H` an affine group scheme over `Spec F`, locally of finite type, and
`i : T → H` a morphism from a commutative group scheme. The Library's positive rational quotient
`Q = PositiveRationalQuotient F H T i` is the left coset space of `H(𝔸)` by the subgroup
`positiveRationalSubgroup F H T i` generated by (the join of) `H(F)` and the image of the
intrinsic positive points `T⁺` of `T` (module `Langlands.Global.AdelicSplitCenterQuotient`); write
`q : H(𝔸) → Q` for the projection.

## Definition

`normOnePartialConvolution T i K h y = ∫_{H(𝔸)¹} conj K(n⁻¹ y) h(q n) dn` for a function `K` on
`H(𝔸)`, a function `h` on `Q` and `y ∈ H(𝔸)`, the integral running over the norm-one subgroup
`H(𝔸)¹ = normOneSubgroup F H` for its Haar measure. It is the convolution over the norm-one
subgroup (not over all of `H(𝔸)`) of the pullback of `h`. This integral is the one in
`NormOnePartialConvolutionRapidDecayStatement` (module
`Langlands.Global.AdelicNormOnePartialConvolutionDecayStatement`).

## Results without further hypotheses

- `normOnePartialConvolution_rational_mul`: the function is left invariant under rational points
  (substitute `n ↦ r n` with `r ∈ H(F) ⊆ H(𝔸)¹`; `r` lies in the subgroup defining `Q`, so the
  class `q(r n) = q n` is unchanged).
- `normOnePartialConvolution_mul_right`: right translation of the argument by `x` is right
  translation of the kernel, `y ↦ K(y x)`.

## Results for split central data

The remaining results assume the data under which the Library's quotient `L²` theory is built:
an isomorphism `e` of `T` with a split torus, an injective restriction of rational characters
`characterRestrict i` with finite-index image (`hi`), centrality of `i` (`hc`), and right
invariance of the Haar measure of `H(𝔸)¹`. With these, `H(𝔸)` is the product of the intrinsic
positive points `H(𝔸)⁺` (the image of `T⁺`) and `H(𝔸)¹` (`normOneDecomposition`), and `L²` classes
are taken in `Lp ℂ 2 (positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure F H T i)`.

- `continuous_positiveQuotientLpSmoothing`: for a compactly supported continuous kernel `κ` and an
  `L²` class `v` on `Q`, the smoothing `positiveQuotientLpSmoothing T i κ v`
  (`x ↦ ∫_{H(𝔸)} κ(a) v(q(x a)) da`) is continuous; it is the pairing of `v` with the continuous
  kernel vector of `Langlands.QuotientIntegration.quotientConvolutionKernel`.
- `hasZeroProperParabolicConstantTerms_positiveQuotientLpSmoothing`: if moreover `H` is connected
  reductive and `v` lies in the full almost-everywhere cuspidal subspace
  `fullAEL2CuspidalSubmodule F H parabolicDynamic hG T i hi I e hc`, the smoothing has vanishing
  constant terms along every proper parabolic at every point (`HasZeroProperParabolicConstantTerms`).
  The integrated action preserves the cuspidal subspace
  (`haarQuotientL2IntegratedAction_mem_fullAEL2CuspidalSubmodule`), its class is represented by the
  smoothing (`positiveQuotientLpSmoothing_ae_eq`), and the continuous representative of a full
  cuspidal class is pointwise cuspidal (`groupSchemeFullAEL2ContinuousCuspidality`,
  `hasZeroProperParabolicConstantTerms_of_normalized`).
- For compactly supported continuous functions `K` on `H(𝔸)` and `ρ` on the positive part
  `H(𝔸)⁺`, `normOneSmoothingKernel K ρ` is the function `a ↦ ρ(a₊) conj K(a₁⁻¹)` for the
  decomposition `a = a₊ a₁` of `H(𝔸)` into its intrinsic positive part and its norm-one part. It is
  continuous, and its support is compact: it lies in the image, under the decomposition, of the
  product of the support of `ρ` with the (compact) set of norm-one `n` such that `n⁻¹` lies in the
  support of `K`. `positiveQuotientLpSmoothing_normOneSmoothingKernel`: at norm-one points `x`,
  `positiveQuotientLpSmoothing T i (normOneSmoothingKernel K ρ) h x` equals
  `(∫ ρ) · normOnePartialConvolution T i K h x`. The proof transports Haar measure of `H(𝔸)` to the
  product of the normalized positive Haar measure and norm-one Haar measure
  (`measurePreserving_normOneDecomposition`), uses that the positive part is central and lies in
  the subgroup defining `Q`, and separates the product integral.
- `hasZeroProperParabolicConstantTerms_normOnePartialConvolution`: for connected reductive `H`, `h`
  in the full cuspidal subspace and `K` compactly supported continuous, the norm-one partial
  convolution has vanishing constant terms along every proper parabolic at every point of `H(𝔸)`.
  Write a point as `y = y₁ c` with `y₁` norm-one and `c` positive and central. The adelic points of
  the unipotent radical of a parabolic lie in the norm-one subgroup, so by the two previous results,
  applied to the kernel `z ↦ K(z c)` and a bump `ρ` of nonzero integral, the constant-term integrand
  of the partial convolution at `y` is `(∫ ρ)⁻¹` times the constant-term integrand at `y₁` of a
  smoothing of `h`. Integrating over the radical quotient, the constant term at `y` is `(∫ ρ)⁻¹`
  times the constant term of that smoothing at `y₁`, which is zero.

## Provenance

This is an internal construction. The partial convolutions are the kernels through which the
norm-one Levi pairings of Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series*
(2024), §6.2, (6.8), p. 202, meet the Iwasawa decomposition of a right convolution. The vanishing
of constant terms of smoothed cuspidal functions is the step used in Mœglin–Waldspurger, *Spectral
Decomposition and Eisenstein Series* (1995), I.3.4, printed pp. 45–46.
