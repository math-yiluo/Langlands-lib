# A reproducing kernel on reductive adelic groups

`Langlands.Global.adelicReproducingKernel` proves `Langlands.Global.AdelicReproducingKernelStatement`. For the given connected reductive group over a number field and the given rationally invariant, smooth, compact-finite and centre-finite function `Φ`, it gives a continuous compactly supported function `A` satisfying `Φ g = ∫ a, Φ (g * a) * A a ∂Measure.haar` at every adelic point. The hypotheses on `Φ` impose no growth condition.

## Proof

1. **A common elliptic equation.** `ofRationalSmoothFunction` puts `Φ` in the finite plot-smooth rational function space with the same evaluations. The finiteness comparisons give a finite-dimensional orbit under the archimedean compact subgroup and a finite-dimensional centre orbit. This compact subgroup is maximal by `IsMaximalCompactSubgroup.isMaximalCompact_comap_ofInfinite`. The adapted Casimir gives an elliptic sum of squares, and `exists_elliptic_slice_annihilator` finds a monic polynomial in it that kills the full function. The slice comparison gives the same left-invariant word equation on every archimedean slice. `wordSolutions` is the complex subspace of smooth solutions, formed as the image of a kernel on the smooth function space. Left translations preserve it because the operator commutes with them.
2. **Evaluation on the archimedean group.** `exists_lieWord_l2_evaluation` bounds evaluation at the identity on this subspace by the Haar `L²` norm on one compact set. `exists_compact_reproducingKernel_of_l2_evaluation` extends this bounded functional by Hahn–Banach and represents it by a compactly supported `L²` weight. Applying the weight twice gives its convolution square, a continuous compactly supported kernel `κ`. Haar inversion invariance used for continuity follows from the full adelic unimodularity theorem. The modular character of the archimedean factor is its value on the first-factor insertion, so archimedean Haar is also right invariant. Evaluating each left-translated slice at the identity now gives `Φ g = ∫ a, Φ (g * ofInfinite F G a) * κ a ∂Measure.haar` with the same `κ` for every `g`.
3. **The finite factor and Haar normalization.** Intersect the open finite stabilizer of `Φ` with the supplied compact open subgroup and call the result `U`. Its indicator divided by its positive finite Haar mass has integral one. Multiply this weight by `κ` on the product group. Right `U`-invariance of `Φ` and Fubini give the required equation for product Haar. The pushforward of that product under the inverse of `AdelicGroupPoints.split` is `c • Measure.haar`. Including the scalar `c` in the kernel gives the identity for the specified adelic measure.
