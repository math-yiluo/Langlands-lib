# Unitarity on a split component enlarged by one simple root

Remove one simple root from a standard Levi index. A parameter of the resulting
parabolic is unitary if it is unitary on the original split component and on one
positive point with nonzero logarithm in the removed root direction.

The real part of the parameter is a real linear form in the split-component
logarithm. From any such logarithm subtract the multiple of the chosen direction
that kills the removed root. The other roots of the original Levi were already zero.
The positive split-component criterion identifies the adjusted logarithm with a
point of the original split component. The real linear form vanishes there and on
the chosen direction, so it vanishes on all logarithms of the enlarged component.

`AdelicRelativeExponentAxisStatement` gives the strict inequality that follows from
this criterion. A nonunitary parameter with unitary restriction to the original
split component and character norm at most one on the removed direction must have
character norm strictly below one there. This is the simple-root separation used
in Delorme, *On the Spectral Theorem of Langlands* (2023), Lemma 3.10(i), pp. 15–16.
