# Parameters on nested split components

For an inclusion of standard parabolics, the split component of the containing parabolic maps into the split component of the smaller parabolic. A parameter on the smaller parabolic restricts along this map and is expressed as a parameter on the containing parabolic. The restriction is complex linear, preserves unitarity, and gives the same character value as evaluation on the actual adelic lift of the split point.

The characters of a parabolic restrict isomorphically to the characters of its Levi. Restricting further to the split component is injective with finite-index image. Tensoring the rationalized restriction with the complex numbers and canceling the intermediate scalar extension gives the complex parameter equivalence. The restricted parameter is the inverse image under this equivalence of the pullback along the nested split embedding.

The two adelic lifts of an outer split point have the same ambient image by the compatibility of the nested embeddings. Injectivity of the smaller parabolic inclusion identifies the lifts. Functoriality of character evaluation then proves the value identity. The real part commutes with character pullback, and the real parameter restriction to the split component is injective, which proves the unitarity assertion.

This identifies the outer split-component labels of the relative Eisenstein summands in Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*, Advances in Mathematics 426 (2023), 109107, (4.3)–(4.4).
