# Square integrability in the regular region

`IntertwiningRegularSquareIntegrableReductionStatement` states that the published uniform automorphy proposition implies preservation of square integrability in the regular region. The theorem `intertwiningRegularSquareIntegrable_of_automorphy` proves the implication. The supplied parabolic presentation realizes the source radical and Levi as the dynamic unipotent subgroup and centralizer of one cocharacter. Their product decomposition gives the radical–Levi Iwasawa expressions needed in the norm estimate.

Take the maximum of the automorphy threshold and the integrability threshold of the shifted scalar Iwasawa kernel. The quotient Haar balance proved in `AdelicIntertwiningLeviHaarBalance` gives the normalized left-translation formula. This formula propagates split-component equivariance and permits the weighted Cauchy–Schwarz estimate on the exact compact and Levi quotient measures. The squared norm of the normalized Levi value is the integrand defining Lapid's square-integrability condition.
