# Discrete quotient integration through group coordinates

The quotient measure induced by an ambient product formula is the pushforward of the same product with its subgroup factor restricted to a fundamental domain. At each right translate, the preimage of the ambient fundamental domain is a fundamental domain for the smaller action. The inverse image of a measurable quotient set is invariant, so the two domains give the same measure on that set. Tonelli gives equality of the pushforwards.

This is the measure step in the unfolding of Mœglin–Waldspurger, *Spectral Decomposition and Eisenstein Series*, II.1.8. The argument uses the Library's Borel transversals for discrete subgroups and the Mathlib comparison of fundamental domains on invariant sets.

A Borel transversal supplies a measurable section of the discrete quotient. This gives joint measurability for invariant functions on products with that quotient. Weighted pushforward identities compare the norm and Bochner integrals after putting the density inside the integrand. Equality of the norm and complex integrals on the radical fibres then transports integrability and the integral over the product. The refolding result also pushes the inner integral through the discrete Levi quotient using its principal invariance.
