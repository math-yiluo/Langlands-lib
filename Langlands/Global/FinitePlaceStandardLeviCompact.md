# One finite-place compact for all standard Levis

Assume availability of corrected Bruhat–Tits data. On the finite completion extend the base change of the given maximal split torus to a local maximal split torus. Rebase the data at that torus and choose a special vertex. Its special compact is maximal and serves every actual parabolic by the available Iwasawa theorem.

Each standard Levi after base change is the centralizer of a cocharacter factoring through the local torus. Move the cocharacter into the dominant chamber. A representative of the same relative Weyl element lies in the special parahoric and hence in the compact. It carries the specified centralizer to a standard Levi for the local torus and preserves the compact under conjugation. The available special Levi compact theorem and maximality at the Levi special vertex give maximality on the specified Levi's own point group. All of these Levi comparisons use the same ambient compact. The completion-point homeomorphisms transport this compact and its Levi inverse images to the original point groups.

The building existence input is the published `BruhatTitsAvailabilityStatement`.
