# Finite idele representatives modulo principal ideles and finite level

For an open subgroup of the finite ideles, `finiteIdeleLevelRepresentatives` chooses finitely many full ideles. Every full idele is a product of a principal idele, one of the representatives, an infinite idele, and an element of the specified finite subgroup.

The idelic norm section has finite component one. Dividing by it puts a given idele in the norm-one subgroup without changing its finite component. A compact fundamental set in the norm-one ideles meets the resulting principal coset. The inverse images of the finite-level cosets form an open cover of this compact set. Taking a finite subcover produces the representatives. The product decomposition of the actual ideles supplies the two remaining components after division by the representative and the principal idele.
