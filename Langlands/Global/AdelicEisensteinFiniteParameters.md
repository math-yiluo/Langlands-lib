# Finite holomorphic expansions of deformed finite translations

For a fixed finite right translation, the change in the Iwasawa height
is invariant under left parabolic translation and under right translation
by the infinite compact factor. The latter translations commute with
the finite translation. When the Iwasawa compact has the stated product
properties, all values of the height change are therefore obtained on
its compact open finite factor.

Near a point of that finite factor, conjugating the change in the point
by the fixed translating element stays in the finite factor by openness.
The right compact invariance of the height makes the height change
locally constant. Compactness gives finitely many values.

The ratio of the two height powers is the exponential pairing with this
height change. The deformed translate is the sum, over the finitely many
height changes, of a fixed scalar function supported on that height
fiber times the corresponding exponential in the parameter. In finite
linear coordinates the scalar coefficients are entire.

This is an internal finite-place implementation of the parameter-dependent
operator families in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic
representation* (1979), proof of Proposition 2, printed pp. 205–206
(Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 200–201).
