# Measure on the closed quotient of a discrete quotient

Let a discrete subgroup be contained in a closed subgroup of a locally compact second-countable Hausdorff group. Assume the discrete quotient of the closed subgroup has covolume one for its chosen invariant measure, and use a Weil-compatible measure on the closed quotient of the ambient group.

To test a measurable subset of the closed quotient, pull its indicator back to the ambient group and restrict it to the chosen discrete fundamental domain. The ambient integral is the mass assigned by the discrete quotient to the inverse image of the subset. Apply the orbit-kernel Weil formula. Each orbit cuts the discrete fundamental domain in a subgroup slice of mass one. The inner integral is therefore the indicator at the closed coset itself. Equality on all measurable subsets proves that the natural projection is measure preserving.

This internal measure identity is used when replacing an intertwining integral over a rational stabilizer by an integral over the full adelic stabilizer.
