# Period reduction for split lattices in semidirect products

This internal construction supports the all-proper versus standard maximal-block GL cuspidality comparison. Its provenance is the published `LeftCosetSemidirectFiniteVolume` construction, extended using Mathlib's fundamental-domain integral comparison and Fubini theorem. It makes no new claim about an original literature passage.

The hypotheses describe actual topological groups `N`, `M`, and `H`, in independent universes, with their locally compact, second-countable Borel structures. The homomorphisms `i : N →* H`, `j : M →* H`, and `p : H →* M` form a topological semidirect product: `i` is an embedding, `j` and `p` are continuous, `p ∘ j` is the identity, `p ∘ i` is trivial, and every element of the kernel of `p` is in the image of `i`. The full lattice `Γ ≤ H` and complement lattice `ΓM ≤ M` are discrete. The two splitting conditions are `j(ΓM) ⊆ Γ` and `p(Γ) ⊆ ΓM`. The normal lattice is `Γ.comap i`.

`productFundamentalDomain` is the image, under `(n,m) ↦ i n * j m`, of the product of the two previously chosen Borel transversals. `productFundamentalDomain_unique` proves that exactly one full lattice element carries each point into this set. The proof first reduces the complement coordinate using its lattice, then reduces the conjugated normal coordinate using the normal lattice. The same order gives uniqueness. The product homeomorphism makes the image Borel, so `isFundamentalDomain_product` supplies a genuine measurable fundamental domain. Normality of the lattice is not assumed.

`mulEquivHaarChar_conj_eq_one_of_finiteCovolume` assumes finite Haar covolume for the normal and complement lattices. Conjugation by a complement lattice element preserves the normal lattice, hence its Haar measure by the published automorphism lemma. The measurable positive conjugation character is therefore trivial on `ΓM`. The published finite-covolume character lemma then makes it trivial on all of `M`. The existing semidirect Haar theorem consequently expresses ambient Haar measure as a nonnegative real multiple of the pushforward of the two Haar measures.

`integral_fundamentalDomain_eq_zero_of_bounded` considers a bounded continuous complex-valued function `φ` invariant under left multiplication by `Γ`. If, for every `m : M`, the normal Haar period of `n ↦ φ (i n * j m)` vanishes on its chosen fundamental domain, the full Haar period also vanishes on the chosen full fundamental domain. The implemented proof first transfers the full integral to the product fundamental domain using lattice invariance. It then applies the derived Haar product formula and the product homeomorphism. The two restricted Haar measures are finite, and the global bound on `φ` proves absolute integrability on their product. Fubini, with the normal variable integrated first, reduces the result to the assumed zero normal periods. Neither a measure-transport identity nor an iterated-period identity is supplied as a premise.

`integral_fundamentalDomain_eq_zero` replaces explicit boundedness by compactness of `Γ \ H`. The continuous invariant function descends to this compact quotient and is bounded there. This version accepts a separately named normal lattice `ΓN` and the exact membership comparison `i n ∈ Γ ↔ n ∈ ΓN`; it still assumes finite normal and complement Haar covolumes. Finally, `integral_fundamentalDomain_eq_zero_of_compact` obtains these finite covolumes from compactness of the two factor quotients and the published local finiteness of their Haar quotient measures.

All conclusion integrals use the actual Haar measures and the existing chosen fundamental domains. These helpers prove the analytic period reduction for a supplied splitting. They do not classify GL parabolics, construct their splittings, compare normalized parabolic measures, or assert the final cuspidal comparison.
