# Common local normalization for finitely many continued sections

`exists_common_normalization` starts from a finite family of local presentations of the continued functions by holomorphic numerators and scalar denominators. Each denominator is nonzero on its open dense regular set in the local neighborhood. The theorem uses a ball contained in every local neighborhood, the intersection of their finite-level subgroups, and a common upper bound for their growth exponents.

The product of the denominators is regular on the intersection of the original regular sets with the ball. `commonNumerator` multiplies each original numerator by the product omitting its own denominator, then combines the resulting vectors linearly. The new numerators define a weakly holomorphic linear family on the coefficient space. On the common regular set its underlying functions agree with the original coefficient combinations multiplied by the product denominator. The regular set is open and dense in the ball.

This is the finite-dimensional common-denominator step of the construction of holomorphic Eisenstein families, using the uniform-growth spaces in Bernstein–Lapid, §7.1 and Corollary 8.6. Langlands uses locally normalized Eisenstein families in the proof of Proposition 2, printed pp. 205–207.
