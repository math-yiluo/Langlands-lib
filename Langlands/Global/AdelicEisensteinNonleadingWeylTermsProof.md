# Exclusion of competing Weyl exponents from the leading chamber

The two ambient parameter identities identify the orthogonal lift of
the transported real parameter with the `w`-translate of the inducing
ambient parameter. For each competitor `v`, the product `v w⁻¹` is
nonidentity and sends the selected simple roots of the reference
Levi to positive roots. Shifted Weyl separation now gives one
regularity bound for all the shifts. Beyond that bound the form
pairing of any proposed competing exponent with some ambient
simple root is negative.

For each root the invariant-form formula for its actual coroot
identifies the nonnegative form pairing with a nonnegative coroot
pairing. A leading exponent has nonnegative coroot pairing with every
ambient simple root. This contradicts the negative form pairing.
The proof implements the application of Lemma 6.8 in Bernstein–Lapid
§8.6.

The companion comparison theorem takes two automorphic families, a
cuspidal expansion for their difference in each standard parabolic,
and injectivity of global leading components. Applying the preceding
chamber bound to every error exponent gives equal leading sums and
one common family of global leading components. The injectivity premise
then gives equality of the two adelic functions throughout the
sufficiently regular part of the supplied domain.
