# Lifting a relative wave packet by an ambient spectral coefficient

`ambientWavePacketProfile eG b g` is the integral of `b(z) g^{i eG(z)}` in the ambient real character coordinates `eG`, using the existing spectral measure in those coordinates. The positive exponential is the Library's actual `parameterPower` evaluated at `imaginaryParameter`. The chart and coefficient depend only on `G`, not on a choice of parabolic.

`Langlands.Global.WavePacketAmbientLiftStatement` states that, for an actual presented parabolic of a connected reductive group over a number field, the pointwise product of this profile with an `IsRelativeWavePacket` is an actual `IsWavePacket` for the same ambient group and compact subgroup, when the coefficient is smooth and compactly supported. The two predicates are the existing relative split-centre and full-parameter wave-packet predicates. The full witness can use the original inducing forms and continuation functions and domain, with an enlarged coordinate block and product coefficients. The statement concerns functions on actual adelic points. It neither assumes nor asserts that the chosen coefficient gives a nonzero square-integrable profile, and it does not assert an isometry or density on a quotient.

Derived from the relative wave-packet formula in Getz–Hahn (2024), §10.5, (10.22)–(10.24), pp. 281–282 (PDF pp. 293–294), and the full imaginary parameter convention in Lapid (2011), Proposition 2 and the displayed packet before (4), p. 314 (PDF p. 323). The shift used in the reduction is the derived ambient character identity for the series of Bernstein–Lapid (2024), §2.1–2.2, pp. 190–191, (2.1), carried by uniqueness to the supplied common holomorphy domain. The product-coordinate construction retains the Library's `(2π)^{-n}` measure convention; no equality between arbitrarily chosen coordinate measures is asserted.
