# Weighted integrability for a norm-one Levi pairing

A continuous function on the ambient adelic group, invariant under the rational Levi and bounded by a nonnegative power of an ambient matrix height, gives an absolutely convergent pairing with a continuous rationally invariant Levi test of sufficient decay on norm-one representatives.

The product of the two factors descends continuously to the actual norm-one Levi quotient. Submultiplicativity of the matrix height bounds the contribution of the fixed external argument by a constant. On the chosen representatives the remaining height weight times the test norm is bounded. Finite volume of the norm-one rational Levi quotient therefore gives integrability. This derived estimate applies to the norm-one tests in Bernstein–Lapid §6.2, (6.8), for Bruhat terms with full target Levi intersection.
