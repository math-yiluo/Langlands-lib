# The normalized integral of the characteristic shell

Take a minimal-parabolic Iwasawa compact with maximal compact inverse image in the Levi and Siegel factors satisfying the rational covers of (7.1). Assume \(T-T_0\) is nonnegative on every ambient simple root and \(S\) is nonnegative on every Levi simple root. For a measurable function on the rational parabolic quotient that is integrable on the lifted shell and invariant in the radical coordinate of the Iwasawa product, the lifted shell integral equals an integral of normalized slices on the intrinsic norm-one Levi quotient. The other factors are the unweighted relative split Haar measure restricted to the split shell and the probability Haar measure of the ambient compact.

Choose the radical Haar measure with mass one on its rational fundamental domain. The rational radical quotient is compact, so its Haar mass before normalization is finite and positive. The relative Iwasawa measure formula fixes split Haar before the cutoffs and integrand are chosen. The rational parabolic lift is Borel, and its coordinate pullback is a product of the split and Levi restrictions. In the intrinsic norm-one Levi, the height inequalities cut out a Borel locus. The inverse image of its quotient image is the countable union of its rational left translates. The quotient image is therefore Borel.

Fubini removes the radical coordinate by invariance and mass one. Each remaining Levi slice descends through the rational Levi quotient because the split point commutes with the rational Levi and the rational Levi maps into the rational parabolic. Pushing forward the restriction to the Levi fundamental domain gives the restricted intrinsic quotient Haar measure. Applying the squared half-modulus cancellation replaces the split density by the displayed normalization of the slice.

This is the integral step for the weak pairing in Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*, Advances in Mathematics 426 (2023), 109107, (7.7), (7.23), and the formula for the weak shell integral on p. 29. The statement applies to a measurable integrable function on the lifted shell, so its rational invariance is on the parabolic quotient.
