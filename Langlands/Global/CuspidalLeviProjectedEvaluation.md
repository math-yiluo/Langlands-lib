# Restored cuspidal projections of a Levi coefficient tensor

A full operator intertwiner between actual block realizations induces a
map between their determinant twists by conjugation with the two twist
equivalences. The compact operator acquires the same character factor
on each side, and the real Lie operator acquires the same scalar shift.
The transported map therefore intertwines both operations.

For a tensor evaluating to a determinant-weighted Levi period,
`projectedRestoredLeviCoefficient` applies the chosen factor maps and
restores the corresponding determinant twists. Equivalently one can
restore the full period and apply the transported factor maps. The
compact, block Lie and radical equations of the restored period pass
through these maps to the projected coefficient.
