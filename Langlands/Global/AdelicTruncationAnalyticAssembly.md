# Assembly of the truncation comparison from unshifted spectral data

`TruncationAnalyticAssemblyStatement` is an analytic reduction for the characteristic-to-regularized comparison of Delorme's Theorem 7.1 in positive relative rank. It fixes the compact, rational quotient, cutoffs, Haar factors and forms used in that comparison. Its inputs are an actual unshifted weak pairing expansion on the generic imaginary parameters, continuous and integrable characteristic pairings at the identity of the split centre, and a holomorphic presentation of the regularized pairing on an open dense complex domain with that generic imaginary slice. The presentation includes the local affine hyperplane denominators at the imaginary locus. The statement also assumes the unshifted comparable-increment estimate of Lemma 7.2 and vanishing of the limit against smooth compactly supported tests in the generic region. For central translation the inputs are the two central eigenvalues, with an entire spectral dependence in the first and a sesquilinear product of absolute value one on the imaginary axis.

The proof enlarges the lower bound on the simple roots so that each regular truncation parameter has norm at least one. The root inequalities ensure eventual regularity in each prescribed strictly dominant sector. Continuity on the generic slice and the increment and smooth test hypotheses give the generic exponential bound by `norm_le_of_tested_truncationIncrements`. For a fixed regular cutoff there is a strictly dominant sector containing it. The generic bound there gives the local boundedness needed to divide out the affine pole factors. Applying `exists_holomorphic_extension_of_generic_comparison` yields an open holomorphy domain containing the imaginary locus. For a bounded set of imaginary parameters, apply the generic estimate on a larger open ball and use continuity to obtain the same bound at all parameters of the bounded set.

The central eigenvalues multiply the characteristic integrand and the weak pairing expansion by the same scalar. This preserves integrability, transfers the regularized sum with its original Haar factors, and multiplies the unshifted holomorphic extension by the entire central factor. Its absolute value is one on the imaginary axis, so the exponential error bound is uniform in the central shift. The proof chooses central polynomial exponent zero in the conclusion.

See P. Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*, Advances in Mathematics **426** (2023), 109107, §7, pp. 25–32, Theorem 7.1 and Lemmas 7.2, 7.4–7.7.
