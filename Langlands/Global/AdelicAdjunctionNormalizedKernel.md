# Normalized Levi coordinates on the adjunction kernel

Moving the target Levi point through the radical uses the measure scaling on the closed stabilizer quotient. The twisted input may include a continuous cutoff in the source height, since that cutoff is constant under the source radical. The normalized Haar balance converts the target Iwasawa density into the inverse half moduli of the two Levi restrictions. The inducing character and its target untwisting character cancel at the Levi point. The change of variables preserves both the complex fibre integral and the integral of its absolute value with these factors inserted.

This implements the Levi normalization of Bernstein–Lapid formula (6.5) in the unfolding of Mœglin–Waldspurger II.1.8.
