# Uniform growth of derivatives after taking a unipotent constant term

`AdelicConstantTermDirectionalGrowthStatement` concerns a closed immersion of a smooth connected affine unipotent group \(U\) into an affine group \(G\) over a number field \(F\). The ambient group is locally of finite type with a chosen smooth relative dimension, and a positive-rank matrix representation gives its adelic height \(H\).

Let \(f_q\) be continuous functions on \(G(\mathbb A)\), smooth on archimedean slices, invariant on the left by \(U(F)\) and on the right by a fixed open finite-adelic level. Suppose that for each finite word \(D\) of archimedean right-orbit derivatives there is a constant \(C_D\) such that

\[
  |D f_q(x)|\le C_D H(x)^R s(q)
\]

at every adelic point, with a common exponent \(R\ge 0\) and nonnegative family size \(s(q)\). The actual probability-normalized constant terms along \(U\) are continuous, smooth on archimedean slices, and invariant at the same right level. Every derivative word commutes with this constant term, and the resulting family satisfies bounds of the same form with exponent \(R\) and size \(s(q)\).

For continuity the integrand descends jointly through the open quotient by \(U(F)\), and its average is continuous by compactness of the quotient. Iteration of the one-direction differentiation theorem gives the commutation identity. A compact set of representatives for \(U(F)\backslash U(\mathbb A)\) bounds the height of its left factors by a single constant. Height submultiplicativity and probability normalization then bound each averaged derivative uniformly.

This is a uniform family estimate for the constant terms of Bernstein–Lapid, §7.3, p. 214, with the common exponent of the seminorms in §7.1, pp. 210–211.
