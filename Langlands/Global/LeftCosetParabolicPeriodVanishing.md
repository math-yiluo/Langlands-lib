# Vanishing of periods on discrete quotients

The ambient group is locally compact, Hausdorff, and second countable. The discrete lattice lies in the closed subgroup. The ambient and subgroup measures are finite on compact sets, and the subgroup measure is s-finite and invariant on both sides. The assumed Weil compatibility identifies the ambient measure with the integral of subgroup orbit measures against the quotient measure. A measurable ambient fundamental domain cuts each subgroup orbit in a fundamental domain for the restricted lattice. Apply the orbit kernel to the indicator of the ambient domain and use left lattice invariance to replace each slice by the fixed subgroup fundamental domain.

For a split semidirect group, finite covolumes of the normal and complement lattices make conjugation preserve the normal Haar measure. For an integrable period, Fubini on the product fundamental domain reduces the period to normal periods. The other case uses Mathlib's convention that a nonintegrable Bochner integral has value zero. These two internal measure identities support the elimination of proper-parabolic terms in the tested version of Bernstein–Lapid (6.18).
