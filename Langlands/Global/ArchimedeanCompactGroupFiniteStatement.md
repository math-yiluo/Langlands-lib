# Compact group finiteness and compact differential finiteness

Author: Worker 94. Module:
`Langlands.Global.ArchimedeanCompactGroupFiniteStatement`.
The proposition is
`Langlands.Global.ArchimedeanGL.CompactGroupFiniteDifferentialStatement`.

For every number field `K : Type u` and every natural rank `n`, including zero,
the statement quantifies over every actual complex-valued smooth function `f`
on the completion-based archimedean general linear group. Its hypothesis says
that the complex span of

```text
x ↦ f(x * k),   k ∈ standardInfiniteCompact K n,
```

is finite-dimensional. Its conclusion is `IsCompactLieFinite K n f`: the
complex span of the genuine differential translates of `f` by all elements
of `compactEnvelopingSubalgebra K n` is finite-dimensional.

## Exact interfaces

The hypothesis is `FiniteVectors.IsFiniteVector` for the restriction of
`smoothRightAction K n` along the actual subgroup inclusion
`(standardInfiniteCompact K n).subtype`. Both orbit spans live in
`SmoothFunctions K n`. The group action is the published right translation
`f(x * k)`; the conclusion uses the published enveloping action whose real
generator differentiates in direction `gX`.

The compact-generated algebra is the complex unital subalgebra generated by
the complexified skew-adjoint real matrix directions. The statement therefore
requires a bridge between these directions and the actual compact subgroup.
It does not take such a bridge, compact-orbit invariance under differentiation,
or finite-dimensionality of the ambient smooth-function space as a premise.
There is no connectedness, positive-rank, center-finiteness, or growth premise.
Only the displayed implication is stated; no converse is included.

## Provenance and proof obligation

This is an internal analytic prerequisite for applying the joint-finiteness
condition isolated in `ArchimedeanJointFinite`. It is not a verbatim theorem
from the original literature. Harish-Chandra, *Discrete series for semisimple
Lie groups II: Explicit determination of the characters*, section 8,
Theorem 1, printed and one-based PDF pp.18–19, uses the product of the center
and compact-generated enveloping algebra in its finiteness hypothesis.
The present statement connects the project's already defined compact-group
finiteness to the compact differential part of that hypothesis.

A possible proof differentiates curves in the compact subgroup inside the
finite-dimensional group orbit, then extends from real generators to their
complexification and generated unital algebra. Establishing the relevant
curves, the equality with the published derivatives, and the closure argument
are proof obligations. The existing matrix compact Lie construction alone
does not supply them. This file defines the target proposition and checks
its full parameter telescope; it contains no proof of that proposition.
