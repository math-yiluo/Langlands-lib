# Compact bounds for adelic Eisenstein terms

`IsIwasawaCompact.heightContinuousMap` bundles the Iwasawa height of the
published Eisenstein series as a continuous map into the real character dual.
The closed image of the adelic parabolic is homeomorphic to its domain. The
product of that image with the Iwasawa compact maps onto the ambient group by a
quotient map. The parabolic character log composed with the projection to the
parabolic factor descends along this map and agrees with the Iwasawa height.

For a factorization `x = p(q) k`, the difference between the heights of
`x * g` and `x` is the height of `k * g`. Tensor induction gives continuity in
the height for each fixed parameter and identifies the real part of the complex
pairing with the real parameter pairing. Expanding in the coordinate basis
gives joint continuity for the supplied finite parameter coordinates. The real
pairing is bounded above on the product of the compact parameter set with
`K * C`. Exponentiating that bound gives a positive constant controlling
`‖heightPower (e s) (x * g)‖` by `‖heightPower (e s) x‖` uniformly for
`g ∈ C` and `s` in the compact parameter set, for every `x`.

These are internal estimates derived from the published Iwasawa height
identities, the character-evaluation topology, and the compact-factor quotient
map. They serve the compact-uniform bound in the formalization of
Mœglin–Waldspurger II.1.5.
