# Weighted Iwasawa integration on the rational parabolic quotient

`normOneParabolicQuotientCoordinates` multiplies a radical point, an
ambient norm-one Levi point, and a compact point, then takes its class
in \(P(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A)^1\).
`exists_normOneParabolicQuotientIwasawaMeasure` gives the pushforward
formula for the counting-normalized Haar measure on this quotient.
It uses the prescribed radical Haar, the weighted Levi Haar, and
compact Haar probability. The two parabolic coordinates are
restricted to their principal fundamental domains.

The proof pulls the Iwasawa-compatible Haar measure on the parabolic
image back to the parabolic points. The isomorphism carries the
principal parabolic subgroup there to its actual image in
\(G(\mathbb A)^1\). The radical-first Haar formula fixes the radical
measure and normalizes Levi Haar before the choice of integrand.
The radical and Levi transversals multiply to a fundamental domain
for every measure. Taking a Dirac measure at each point gives
pointwise uniqueness of the translating principal element, as required
by the compact-factor quotient formula.

The restriction of the parabolic measure to that domain is the
pushforward of the product of the two restricted measures. This uses
the homeomorphism between the product and the parabolic, whose
preimage of the domain is the product of the original domains.
The compact-factor quotient formula and composition of measure
pushforwards give the result. In the dynamic case the published
parabolic modulus computation gives the inverse density of (7.7).
This is the unfolded measure before imposing the characteristic
region restrictions.

The source is P. Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*,
Advances in Mathematics 426 (2023), 109107, pp. 23–24, (7.7).
