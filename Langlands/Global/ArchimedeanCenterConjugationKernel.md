# An open kernel for archimedean conjugation on the enveloping center

The existing derivative formula for enveloping conjugation along a real exponential has value zero on every central element. The one-parameter group law moves this computation to every point of the curve. Each linear functional is therefore constant on that curve, so conjugation fixes the central element. The inverse function theorem gives a neighborhood of the identity of the actual archimedean group contained in the image of the exponential. Thus the center-conjugation homomorphism has an open kernel.

This derived step uses the real derivative formula in `ArchimedeanEnvelopingInvariantCenter` and the exact group conjugation in `ArchimedeanAdjoint`. It allows central finiteness of the induced sections to use joint compact admissibility in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), proof of Proposition 2, printed pp. 204–206, Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 199–201.
