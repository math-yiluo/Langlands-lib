# Enveloping derivatives of compact weighted integrals

Let \(M\) be an affine group over a number field with a smooth infinite adelic manifold, and let \(N\) be a real manifold. Fix a continuous map \(j:X\to N\) from a compact Borel space with a finite measure \(\mu\), and a continuous complex weight \(w\) on \(X\). A jointly smooth family \(f\) on \(M(F_\infty)\times N\) has plot-smooth slices at each parameter. The definition `compactIntegralPlotSmooth` also gives the plot-smooth function on \(M(F_\infty)\) obtained by integrating \(w(x)f(m,j(x))\) against \(\mu\).

The theorem `compactIntegralPlotSmooth_enveloping` computes every actual complex enveloping derivative of this integral by applying the same derivative to the first-variable slices under the integral. For a real Lie direction, the proof applies `CompactManifoldVectorIntegral.mfderiv_weightedIntegral_apply` to the family composed with left multiplication by the point of evaluation. The first-variable directional derivative is again jointly smooth. This follows by differentiating the smooth family \(((m,n),y)\mapsto f(my,n)\) at \(y=1\) in a fixed direction. Its joint smoothness lets the argument be iterated. Additivity and complex homogeneity use integrability on the compact parameter space. Induction from the real generators through complexification and the tensor algebra proves the formula for the entire enveloping algebra.

This derived differentiation identity is used on Levi fibres in the transported convolution argument of Mœglin–Waldspurger, *Spectral Decomposition and Eisenstein Series* (1995), I.4.5, printed pp. 60–62. The separate fibre formula supplies a compact weight independent of the Levi variable. The identity here is stated for arbitrary jointly smooth families and the Library's actual enveloping action on infinite adelic points.
