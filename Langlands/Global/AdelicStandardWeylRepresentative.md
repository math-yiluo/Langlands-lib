# Standard Weyl representatives for associated inducing parabolics

For an actual normalizer point that conjugates the inducing Levis, choose the minimum in its standard Weyl double coset. A target Levi normalizer factor can be moved across the original point into the source Levi. This realizes the minimum by multiplying the original rational point on the right by a rational point of the source Levi.

The resulting quotient conjugates the standard Weyl subgroups in both directions. At a Coxeter minimum, conjugating a source simple reflection into the target standard subgroup makes it a target simple reflection. The length increase gives positivity of the root image, so the Library's simple-reflection conjugacy criterion identifies the actual root labels.

This internal reduction supplies the simple-root transport used in the Weyl chamber comparison for Bernstein–Lapid §8.6. The change of representative for the actual intertwining operator is available separately.
