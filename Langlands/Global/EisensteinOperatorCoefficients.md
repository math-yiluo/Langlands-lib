# Extending actual Eisenstein coefficients by the free operator algebra

For each Taylor order and each seed curve, the weak holomorphy and
regular automorphy hypotheses give an actual stable automorphic form.
The free operator algebra acts on these vectors through its map to the
concrete matrix operator algebra. Thus the seed coefficients extend to
an operator-linear map from the free module on the seeds, with each
vector represented by finitely many operator coefficients.

At an adelic point, evaluation of this map is the normalized scalar
Taylor coefficient of a finite sum. Each summand applies the continuous
test for the corresponding concrete operator to its seed curve. The
operator test on a Taylor vector gives the derivative of the individual
summand, and the sum rule gives the derivative of the whole sum.

This is an internal implementation of the finite coefficient step in
Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), proof
of Proposition 2, printed pp. 205–206 (Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 200–201).
