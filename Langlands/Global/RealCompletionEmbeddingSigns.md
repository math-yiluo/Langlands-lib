# Prescribing a sign at one embedding into a real completion

Let \(E/F\) be an extension of number fields. Given one \(F\)-embedding of \(E\) into a particular real completion of \(F\), there is a unit of \(E\) that is negative at that embedding and positive at all other \(F\)-embeddings into the real completions of \(F\). Here a unit means a nonzero element of the field.

Each such embedding gives a real infinite place of \(E\). Restricting this place along \(F\to E\) recovers the specified place of \(F\). At a fixed place of \(F\), distinct embeddings into its completion give distinct real places of \(E\), since an infinite place has a unique real complex embedding. The proof then uses sign approximation on \(E\) at the singleton consisting of the designated real place.
