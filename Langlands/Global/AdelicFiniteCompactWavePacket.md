# A single standard inducing form under a finite change of compact

A packet induced from one square-integrable inducing form along a
standard parabolic can be realized as a packet for another maximal
Iwasawa compact with the same infinite projection. The statement takes the
finite compact comparison and the existence of imaginary-axis Eisenstein
continuations as premises.

The cutoffs on the height fibres give a fixed finite family of
square-integrable inducing forms for the second compact. The twist identity
produces a weighted sum of their Eisenstein series on the region of absolute
convergence. The weights are the exponential of the parameter paired with
each height difference. They are entire in the complex parameter. The
scalar meromorphic identity theorem continues the equality to the common
holomorphy domain, which contains the imaginary axis. Multiplication by
each weight preserves the smoothness and compact support of the spectral
test. Integrating the equality therefore gives exactly the packet of the
finite family for the second compact.

meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series*, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 37
(2024), (2.1), printed p. 191. The registered PDF
