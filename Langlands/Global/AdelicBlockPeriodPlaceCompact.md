# Compact Levi embeddings for the place evaluations of a block period

Putting compact Levi coordinates into one real or complex place
commutes with the ordered block embedding in the global infinite
compact. Both sides have the prescribed block diagonal matrix at the
chosen place and the identity at every other place. In particular,
putting a compact factor into a single block at that place produces
`blockShapeInfiniteCompactEmbedding` of that block's place factor.
These identities compare the compact operators of a global period with
the ones in the place block evaluation predicate.

`IsBlockPeriodCompactEvaluation` states the tensor evaluation equation
for each single global compact block. The two place results apply the
embedding identities and use the fact that the single coordinate
subgroups generate the compact Levi product.
