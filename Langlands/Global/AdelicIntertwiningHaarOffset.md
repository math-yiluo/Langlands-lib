# A fixed exponent for the Haar factor of the intertwining integral

A target Levi point conjugates the target radical and corresponds through the Weyl conjugation to a point of the source Levi. The latter normalizes the source radical. The membership criterion of the closed stabilizer then shows that the target action preserves it.

On the positive target component both actions and their inverse actions are jointly continuous. Their Haar moduli are multiplicative and continuous, and their ratio is a positive character. Restriction of target parabolic characters through the Levi and to its split component has finite-index image. Therefore the ratio is the character power of one target parameter, independent of the inducing parameter. This is the fixed Haar contribution to the unnormalized exponents in the convergent test expansion.
