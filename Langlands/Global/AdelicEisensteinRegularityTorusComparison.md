# Cofinal Eisenstein regularity tests under an isomorphism of presentation tori

`EisensteinRegularityTorusComparisonStatement` compares two compatible
presentations of the same parabolic, Levi, and split component. The supplied
torus isomorphism identifies the ambient inclusions and the component factors.
For each threshold in the first presentation there is a threshold in the
second whose regular parameters satisfy the first. The inverse torus
isomorphism gives the reverse comparison.

The two presentations have the same positive split-component roots. Each
orthogonal lift of a parabolic parameter is its actual restriction through the
Levi to the presentation torus. These restrictions correspond under the
isomorphism. Pulling back the second form gives another positive invariant
form for the same relative Weyl action. On any ambient root the coroot formula
relates the two evaluations by the positive ratio of the two squared root
lengths. A finite upper bound over the ambient roots gives a single threshold
that works for every parameter.

This internal comparison allows regularity hypotheses on the original target
to be used with the common standard-family presentation.
