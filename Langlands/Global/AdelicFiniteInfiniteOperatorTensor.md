# Finite and infinite tensor actions on the free adelic operators

The free operator action on a tensor product uses the finite translation on the first factor and the infinite compact and enveloping operators on the second. A finite intertwiner tensored with the identity intertwines all three kinds of operators, and hence the whole free algebra.

Tensoring a finite constituent diagram with a nonzero infinite carrier gives a full operator subquotient whose finite restriction is isotypic of that constituent. To see this, each pure tensor lies in the image of the finite representation obtained by fixing its second factor. A simple full operator subquotient of this tensor retains an embedding of the finite constituent. An injective intertwiner from the inducing tensor into an ambient section model transports the resulting invariant numerator and quotient there.

This derived argument gives the finite-place selection step for Langlands’ Lemma 1 in *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), printed p. 203, Corvallis Part I PDF p. 198. The application requires a nonzero infinite source that acts on the section model.
