# Logarithmic coordinates of the relative positive split component

Fix a standard parabolic \(Q\) and its split component \(A_Q\). The group of positive adelic points of \(A_Q\) whose images in \(G(\mathbb A)\) have norm one is topologically isomorphic to the additive real space \(\mathfrak a_Q\cap\mathfrak a_0^G\). `RelativePositiveLogCoordinatesStatement` uses a finite real chart on the maximal split torus's cocharacter space to topologize this intersection. The coordinate of a positive element is its normalized idele logarithm in the maximal split torus. In the parabolic itself the character logarithm is `splitDual` applied to the same vector.

The proof first identifies the real cocharacter space of a split torus with the real dual of its integral characters using the perfect character-cocharacter pairing. A torus point maps to an ambient norm-one point exactly when its logarithm annihilates the restricted ambient characters. For the standard Levi, embed the supplied split component into the maximal split torus. The image of this embedding on real cocharacters is the common kernel of the Levi simple roots, and the annihilator of the ambient characters is the real coroot span. The full positive-torus log is a topological group isomorphism. Restricting it to the ambient character kernel gives the claimed intersection. Functoriality of the logarithm identifies the image in the parabolic with the restricted functional. The auxiliary `minimalHeight_eq_of_torusLog` identifies the same logarithmic vector with the ambient minimal-parabolic Iwasawa height of a torus point.

The identification supplies the relative split variable in the Haar formula and the cone integrals in Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*, Advances in Mathematics 426 (2023), 109107, (7.7) and (7.9).
