# Maximal-parabolic constant terms of relatively cuspidal functions

`AdelicRelativeCuspidalConstantTermStatement` uses a compatible standard parabolic family for a connected reductive affine group over a number field. Let \(P\) be a standard parabolic with Levi simple-root set \(J\), and let \(\alpha\in J\). Write \(M_\alpha\) for the maximal standard parabolic whose Levi simple-root set is \(\Delta\setminus\{\alpha\}\), and \(Q\) for the standard parabolic whose Levi simple-root set is \(J\setminus\{\alpha\}\).

For a continuous function on the actual adelic group, left invariant under the rational minimal parabolic \(P_0(F)\) and under \(U_P(\mathbb A)\), the constant terms along \(M_\alpha\) and \(Q\) agree as functions. Each constant term uses normalized probability Haar measure on the actual rational quotient of the corresponding radical. In particular, if the function is cuspidal along \(P\), then the constant term along \(M_\alpha\) vanishes.

The proof uses the product \(U_Q(\mathbb A)=U_P(\mathbb A) U_{M_\alpha}(\mathbb A)\) and the two normalizations of these factors by \(U_Q(\mathbb A)\). At a fixed ambient point the integrand descends to the rational quotient of \(U_Q\). Averaging along the smaller unipotent quotient for \(U_{M_\alpha}\) preserves its outer integral by compact subgroup averaging. At any point with factorization \(u w\), left \(U_P(\mathbb A)\) invariance moves \(u\) through the averaging variable, leaving the average translated on the right by \(w\). The latter equals the original smaller average by invariance of its quotient measure. The resulting outer integral is the integral of this constant against a probability measure.

The unit also proves that the rational points of every standard radical lie in the rational minimal parabolic and that the adelic minimal parabolic normalizes every standard radical. These are consequences of parabolic order and normality of the standard radicals.

This identity connects relative cuspidality in Bernstein–Lapid, §7.4, p. 217, with the maximal-parabolic estimate in Mœglin–Waldspurger, §I.2.10, pp. 30–33.
