# Eigenline comparison of Iwasawa logarithms

The norm of any invertible coordinate of a primitive adelic vector is bounded by the primitive vector height. This follows place by place from the supremum norm and the common local normalization. Multiplying the coordinate by a nonzero principal scalar leaves its normalized idelic norm unchanged by the product formula.

Suppose one rational vector is a parabolic eigenvector and a nonzero coordinate of another rational vector records a second parabolic character. If the two inverse orbit vectors agree, use Iwasawa factorizations of their arguments. The eigenvector factors the first height exactly. The coordinate supplies a lower bound for the second primitive height. Uniform distortion on the compact Iwasawa subgroup bounds the compact factors. Taking logarithms gives `exists_heightCharacter_le_add_of_orbit_eq`. This is a derived height comparison for the eigenline calculation of the uniform Weyl estimate used in Arthur (1980), Lemma 1.1.
