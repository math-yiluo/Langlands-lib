# Continuity and integrability of the characteristic pairing

`CharacteristicPairingContinuityStatement` concerns the characteristic pairing \(\Omega^T_{P_0}\) of Delorme's Theorem 7.1. It takes a compact characteristic region and a fixed central translate. The family in the first slot is jointly continuous in real spectral coordinates and the group variable, and the function in the second slot is continuous. Both functions are left invariant under rational points. The conclusion includes integrability on the actual characteristic region for each spectral parameter and continuity of the characteristic integral in those parameters.

`characteristicPairingContinuity` first forms the product of the two centrally translated functions, with conjugation in the second slot. The inclusion of norm-one rational points into the rational subgroup gives invariance of the product. The open quotient map lets joint continuity descend to the quotient even though the integrand is written using a chosen representative. A continuous function on the compact characteristic region is integrable for the quotient Haar measure. Near any spectral coordinate, joint continuity bounds the product on a closed ball times the region. The measure restricted to the region is finite, so dominated convergence gives continuity of the pairing.

The source is P. Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*, Advances in Mathematics 426 (2023), 109107, pp. 23 and 25 and the conclusion of the proof of Theorem 7.1 on p. 32.
