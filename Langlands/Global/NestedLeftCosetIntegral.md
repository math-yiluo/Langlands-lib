# Integration over nested discrete left quotients

`NestedLeftCosetIntegralStatement` considers subgroups Γ ≤ Λ of a second-countable topological group with its Borel structure, where Λ is discrete. For a left invariant ambient measure and a nonnegative measurable function on Γ\H, its integral for the discrete quotient measure is the integral on a fundamental domain for Λ of the sum over Γ\Λ. Each term evaluates the function at the Γ-coset obtained by left multiplying the domain point by the chosen representative in Λ.

The proof translates the fundamental domain for Λ by the representatives of Γ\Λ. The translates are disjoint and their union is a fundamental domain for Γ. The quotient measure is independent of the choice of fundamental domain. Countable additivity, preservation of the ambient measure by left translations, and interchange of the nonnegative sum and integral give the formula.

This general measure identity supplies the counting integral behind the comparison of norms on enlarged Siegel domains in Bernstein–Lapid, §7.4, p. 215.
