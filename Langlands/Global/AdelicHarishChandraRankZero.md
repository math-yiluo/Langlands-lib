# Uniform convolution equations for the trivial adelic group

`Langlands.Global.HarishChandraRankZeroStatement` gives the uniform
convolution equation for every parameterized function family when the
given matrix representation embeds the group as a closed subgroup of
\(\mathrm{GL}_0\).

The adelic matrix evaluation is injective, and a matrix with empty
index type has just one possible value. Thus the adelic group is a
subsingleton. Every compact translate of a kernel, or of its indicated
inverse pullback, equals that function, giving both finite translate
spans. A smooth compactly supported kernel of Haar integral one acts
as the identity because \(xt=x\) for every adelic \(t\). A single
kernel with both scalar coefficients equal to one gives the equation.

This is the degenerate matrix case of the convolution equation in
Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series*,
§8.3, Lemma 8.3, p. 223
(`bernstein-lapid-2024-meromorphic-continuation-eisenstein-published`,
