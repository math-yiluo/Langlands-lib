# Eisenstein operators under a finite change of compact

Let \(K\) and \(L\) be maximal compacts with equal infinite projections and
Iwasawa decompositions for both parabolics. The finite change of source height
gives a fixed finite family of automorphic cutoffs of the inducing function.
The coefficient for the cutoff indexed by \(v\) is
\(\exp(\langle\lambda,v\rangle)\), where \(v\) is a value of the height
difference. The theorem gives a single source regularity threshold. Above it,
the Eisenstein series for \(K\) is the corresponding finite sum of series for
\(L\). The same coefficients compare the intertwining operators after
twisting each result at the Weyl-transported parameter using its own target
height.

The proof identifies the infinite subgroups by maximal compact factorization.
It obtains summability for each cutoff from the normal convergence theorem
on singleton sets and Bochner integrability from the rational-quotient
integrability theorem. For each cutoff it takes the maximum of the two
thresholds, then bounds all these maxima by the sum of their nonnegative
parts. The twist comparison carries the finite identity through the actual
sum and integral at every adelic argument.

This is a derived step in the change of compact for Bernstein and Lapid,
*On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series* (2024), §2.2,
journal p. 191, equation (2.1), the twisted intertwining integral, and
footnote 1. It uses the actual rational quotient and normalization already
fixed in the Library.
