# Fixation on the actual algebraic compact-type span

Author: Worker 94.

The source-character reproduction formula is passed through every
algebraic intertwiner from the compact type into the actual derivative smooth
functions. Compose an intertwiner with point evaluation. The resulting
complex linear functional on the finite-dimensional source is continuous,
so it commutes with the source integral. Intertwining identifies the scalar
integrand with the evaluation formula for the actual character average.
Injectivity of point evaluations gives fixation on every intertwiner range.

Every vector in the published algebraic isotypic span is a finite sum of
values of such intertwiners. Complex linearity of character averaging therefore
proves fixation on the entire compact-type submodule. This uses a left
invariant probability measure, without assuming the smooth carrier is complete
or interpreting its average as a vector-valued integral.

The argument is an internal consequence of the weighted averaging construction,
Schur conjugation averaging and the published algebraic isotypic definition.
