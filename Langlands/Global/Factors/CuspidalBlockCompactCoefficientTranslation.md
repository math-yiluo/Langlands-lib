# Compact and finite parabolic translation of cuspidal coefficients

The grouped coefficient equivalence writes the scalar coefficient of a pure tensor as the product of the evaluations in the inducing cuspidal blocks. In each block the chosen place actions give the actual compact translation of that block. The product identity extends to the nested tensor by multilinearity. `finiteBlockEvaluation` specializes the Library’s finite inducing evaluation to the references from the same spectral presentations. It takes values in the finite coefficients grouped by block.

The comparisons implement normalized scalar block induction in Getz–Hahn (2024), §10.1, printed pp. 270–271 (PDF pp. 282–283), `getz-hahn-2024-automorphic-representations`, SHA-256 `a125cf9c0f445972cbbd3552b4fb9905ca948108a05880f90eaa43558d09a507`.
