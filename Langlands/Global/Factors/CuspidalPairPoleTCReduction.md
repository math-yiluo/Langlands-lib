# Reduction of the cuspidal-pair pole theorem over totally complex fields

`CuspidalPairNormalizedContinuationTCAt` supplies the continuation and a matching normalized test over a fixed field \(F\). It takes a nontrivial continuous unitary additive character of the adeles trivial on the diagonal field, two cuspidal realizations trivial on their split centres, any certified finite pair factor choice, and parameters at the complex places. The continuation agrees on a right half-plane with the absolutely convergent finite Euler product times the indicated complex-place factors. Its pole profile is the condition in the target, with poles at \(0\) and \(1\) exactly for equal-rank contragredient pairs. `HasNormalizedStripTest` gives a test integral bounded in strips off disks about the two points and an entire normalized factor of exponential strip growth with a nonzero value. Their matching identity holds off the two points.

`cuspidalPairPoleAt_of_completedEquationTC` uses the standard adelic character and the conjugate cuspidal realizations as contragredients. It constructs finite spectral presentations of the conjugates in the same factor universe as the input presentations. The published local-data theorem supplies a finite factor choice, the exceptional set, and complex additive components. The published totally complex parameter theorem supplies parameters for the original and conjugate realizations. Every infinite place is complex, so the real-place parameter clauses are vacuous and `archimedeanPairProduct_eq_complex_of_totallyComplex` reduces the initial product to the complex-place product.

The completed equation and the dual right-half-plane estimate give bounds on arbitrarily far-left lines. The epsilon product has constant modulus on each vertical line. Comparison of certified finite factors allows the continuation to use any finite choice in the target. The original Euler product gives the far-right estimates. The strip transfer clears the possible poles and uses the matching test to control growth before applying Phragmén–Lindelöf. The resulting fieldwise conclusion includes the entire data-existence clause and the strip bounds away from the two discs.

`cuspidalPairPoleTCReduction` assembles the fixed-field conclusions into the exact `CuspidalPairPoleTCStatement` in universes `u` and `uQ`, conditional on the totally complex completed equation and the normalized continuations.

The pole profile comes from the normalization in Getz–Hahn (2024), (2.20), printed p. 68 (PDF p. 85), and Theorem 11.7.1 with the preceding shift formula, printed p. 309 (PDF p. 320). The matching of a continuation with a normalized integral is the form used in Cogdell (2004), Theorems 4.1–4.2, printed pp. 219–222 (PDF pp. 222–225). The reduction uses the Library's strip argument from the completed equation and the normalized test.
