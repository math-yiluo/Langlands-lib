# Scalar adelic sections from cuspidal compact evaluations

The compact evaluation pairs the finite tensor with the tensor of the original-order archimedean standard-module evaluations, at the two coordinates of the actual standard compact. The grouped coefficient equivalence turns this paired tensor into a function of the adelic Levi argument. Under the finite scalar covariance law, `compactEvaluation_left` identifies its left transformation under compact parabolic points. `compactTensorSectionMap` then uses the compact picture equivalence to extend those evaluations to normalized scalar sections on the adelic group.

This is the compact extension in Getz–Hahn (2024), §10.1, printed pp. 270–271 (PDF pp. 282–283), `getz-hahn-2024-automorphic-representations`, SHA-256 `a125cf9c0f445972cbbd3552b4fb9905ca948108a05880f90eaa43558d09a507`.
