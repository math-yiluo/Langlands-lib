# Recovering normalized pair L-polynomials from their pole orders

`meromorphicOrderAt_rescaledPairLFactor` computes the order at \(s=0\) of \(1/P(zq^{-s})\). Choosing \(s_0\) with \(q^{-s_0}=z\) translates this order to the order at \(s_0\) of \(1/P(q^{-s})\). The exponential has nonzero derivative there, so the order is minus the multiplicity of \(z\) in \(P\). `eq_of_unitCircle_rootMultiplicity` uses the multiset of roots, with multiplicities, to recover a complex polynomial with constant coefficient \(1\) when every root lies on the unit circle.

`weilDeligne_eulerPolynomial_root_norm` uses an eigenvector of Frobenius on the inertia-fixed monodromy kernel with eigenvalue \(z^{-1}\). The invariant positive form forces \(\lvert z\rvert=1\). For two unitary supercuspidal basis classes, `isUnitaryBasisPairLPolynomial_root_norm` uses the explicit JPSS (8.1) polynomials. In unequal rank the polynomial is \(1\). In equal rank each linear factor comes from a contragredient unramified-twist parameter of modulus one. `isPadicSymmetricLPolynomial_roots_of_unitarySupport` uses the product of these block polynomials given by JPSS (8.4) after realizing the two supports as normalized inductions.

The Lean proof uses `meromorphicOrderAt_eval_spectralPoint`, `exists_kernel_eigenvector_of_eulerPolynomial_eval_eq_zero`, `norm_eq_one_of_invariantPositiveForm`, `supercuspidalUnequalRankLFactor`, `supercuspidalEqualRankLFactor`, `IsContragredientTwistParameter.norm_eq_one` and `temperedLFactor`. The automorphic formulas are from Jacquet, Piatetski-Shapiro and Shalika (1983), Propositions (8.1) and (8.4), printed pp. 443 and 451–452.
