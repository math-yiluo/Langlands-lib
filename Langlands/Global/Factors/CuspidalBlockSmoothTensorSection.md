# Smooth sections from the cuspidal block tensor

`cuspidalSmoothTensorSection` takes the cuspidal realizations, their finite spectral presentations with irreducible admissible components, and real and complex place parameters for each block. The finite input is a smooth representation with a linear evaluation into the grouped finite Levi coefficients. Its evaluations satisfy finite right translation, the paired normalized finite parabolic law, and separation at finite integral compact arguments.

The map pairs the given finite evaluations with the actual original-order standard-module evaluations at the infinite places. For each pure tensor, fixing a finite compact argument and an adelic Levi argument gives a scalar linear functional of the infinite coefficient. `infiniteEvaluationScalarExtension` extends this scalar evaluation smoothly near the chosen infinite compact matrix. The infinite standard tensor is compact-finite, and its compact evaluations separate vectors. The grouped coefficient equivalence is injective. With the compact covariance law from `CuspidalBlockCompactTensorSection`, these facts give the hypotheses of `compactTensorSmoothEmbedding`. The resulting map is injective, intertwines finite and infinite compact right translation, and evaluates at a pair of compact arguments by applying the grouped coefficient equivalence to the tensor of the two evaluations.

This instantiates the normalized compact model of Getz–Hahn (2024), §10.1, printed pp. 270–271 (PDF pp. 282–283), `getz-hahn-2024-automorphic-representations`, SHA-256 `a125cf9c0f445972cbbd3552b4fb9905ca948108a05880f90eaa43558d09a507`.
