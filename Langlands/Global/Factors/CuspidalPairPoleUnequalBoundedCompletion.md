# Unequal-rank completions from finite normalized sums

Assume that the specified archimedean ordinary unequal-rank test integrals
have finite normalization sums equal to one. This is the adjacent-rank
input in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), Theorem 2.7
and §15, author pp. 9–10 and 108–109.

At each point the archimedean sums have a nonzero summand, and the finite
Laurent generator supplies a nonzero finite test. The prescribed-tuple
realization combines these into a covering family of actual partial
integrals. For the bounds, multiply the archimedean sum identities with
the bounded finite Laurent identities. Realize each tuple in this product
and divide its weight by the nonzero scalar from the realization.

The library's bounded-sum completion theorem for unequal ranks gives a
completion with the prescribed Euler product, removable singularities at
zero and one, and vertical-strip bounds. It uses the entire global
unequal-rank integrals to transfer the bounds. This follows the
finite-sum route in J. W. Cogdell, *Analytic Theory of L-Functions for
GL(n)* (2004), §4.1, printed pp. 220–221.
