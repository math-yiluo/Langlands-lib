# Finite covariance of restored cuspidal projections

On the finite Levi the coefficient before restoration has the scalar
which restores its determinant characters. The full block projections
intertwine the finite operators. Their tensor therefore preserves the
scalar covariance on the selected factors. Applying the restoring
equivalences cancels this scalar. Applying the inverse half-modulus
equivalence gives the normalized finite covariance of the restored
projected coefficient.
