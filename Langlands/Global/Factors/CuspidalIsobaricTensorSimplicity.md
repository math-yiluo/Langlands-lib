# Simplicity and realization of the specified constituent tensor

The constructed target tensor is irreducible for the common free action.
The finite constituent tensor is nonzero and has a dense translation
algebra. The chosen archimedean quotients have the compact and Lie density
properties of admissible standard quotients. The one-slot formulas for
the constructed action let these density results prove tensor irreducibility.

`HasTargetTensorSurjection` asks for an equivariant surjection from an
invariant subspace of genuine automorphic forms to the constructed tensor.
`hasTensorRealization_of_targetSurjection` uses this tensor as the witness
in `HasTensorRealization`. The verified generator formulas identify all
the specified place actions.

This is the algebraic input for the simple tensor constituent in Langlands
(1979), Lemma 1 and the proof of Proposition 2, printed pp. 203–206
(Corvallis I PDF pp. 198–201), and its use in Getz–Hahn (2024), Theorem
10.7.5, printed p. 288 (PDF p. 300).
