# Archimedean evaluations in realized cuspidal coefficients

The number field is supplied with compactness of `AdeleQuotient F`. The original block ranks sum to the ambient rank, and the supplied parameters have the corresponding block ranks at the selected real or complex place. `realCoefficientTensorEquiv` and `complexCoefficientTensorEquiv` identify the ordered tensor of the compact-finite block standard modules with the tensor of the chosen `placeDensityModels` subspaces.

`realPlaceEvaluation` and `complexPlaceEvaluation` take vectors of the compact-finite ambient standard module, in the original block order, to these coefficient tensors at each compact point. They compose the normalized standard evaluation in the given block shape with the corresponding coefficient equivalence. Right compact translation acts by right multiplication of the compact argument. Left translation by the compact Levi applies the actual local compact action on each realized block coefficient. The individual block equivalences preserve that action and the tensor maps agree on pure tensors. The family of evaluation maps separates ambient standard vectors because the original normalized evaluations separate them and the coefficient equivalence is injective.

Getz and Hahn (2024), §4.9, equation (4.32) and pp. 130–131 (PDF 145–146), give the normalized local covariance and right action. Theorem 6.4.3, p. 167 (PDF 181), describes factorization into irreducible local representations up to equivalence, and §10.1, pp. 270–271 (PDF 282–283), gives the scalar Levi slices. The comparison with the chosen coefficient subspaces is the internal construction in `CuspidalBlockStandardCoefficients` using the supplied parameters and isotypy.
