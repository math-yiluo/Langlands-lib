# Ordinary normalization for prescribed pure equal-rank tests

`poleEqualSchwartzTensor` constructs the restricted adelic tensor of the specified real, complex and retained finite row tests. It is Schwartz–Bruhat and standard outside the retained set. `poleEqualFiniteIntegral` is the ordinary equal-rank JPSS integral for the actual finite Whittaker pair and row test, using the canonical local quotient measure.

`exists_ordinaryEqualNormalization_of_pure_product` takes the prescribed opposite-character finite coefficient vectors and two infinite tensors in the actual cuspidal presentations. It assumes that the two infinite residuals factor with nonzero scalars, that each local archimedean pair has left-unipotent invariant product, and that the retained local integrals have entire normalizations for the specified factors. The finite components are canonical for a unitary adelic additive character that is trivial on the diagonal field. The resulting ordinary partial global integral has an entire normalization whose value is a fixed nonzero scalar times the product of the supplied local normalized values.

The proof separates the retained finite coefficient functions with their normalized reference vectors, substitutes the two prescribed infinite products and multiplies by the Schwartz tensor and determinant weight. The quotient kernel identities and the compatible quotient measure comparison identify the local integrals and give the formula for the normalized value. This implements the ordinary partial factorization in Cogdell (2004), §4.2, printed pp. 221–222 (registered PDF pp. 224–225). The finite ordinary integral uses Jacquet, Piatetski-Shapiro and Shalika (1983), §2.4, display (1), printed p. 387 (registered PDF p. 22).
