# Conditional comparison using actual Hecke twists

The theorem `Langlands.Global.FunctionalEquationComparison.heckeTwistComparison` proves the entire published `HeckeTwistComparisonStatement.{u}` from `Langlands.Global.Factors.HeckeTwistComparisonStatement`, for an arbitrary number-field carrier universe `u`. The public target is an internal derived formulation of Henniart, *On the local Langlands conjecture for GL(n): the cyclic case* (1986), section 4.3, formulas (2), (4), and (5), printed pp.157–159 / PDF pp.14–16. The registered original is `henniart-1986-cyclic`, SHA-256 `096fba993b7368b1af9f7cc1f0c053e5436ba447f291c2acd402f058eb44b0ba`. The implementation specializes the finite comparison engine to actual idele-class characters and keeps the character, analytic, preservation, and stability inputs explicit.

The proof applies the supplied `RamifiedTwistIsolationStatement` first with empty prescribed set and ramified set T. It then applies that statement with prescribed set {w}, trivial local prescription, and ramified set T minus {w}. The trivial prescriptions have positive finite order, and the prescribed and ramified sets are disjoint. These applications provide two actual finite-order Hecke characters, both trivial on the whole archimedean idele factor. The first is nontrivial at the requested principal-unit depth at every T-place; the second is trivial at w and has the requested nontriviality at every other T-place. The proof packages these properties as `TwoHeckeTwists`.

Only after choosing both characters does `CommonTwistEulerInputs` provide one finite enlargement B. It contains T and both characters are unramified outside B. The proof assembles two `ComparisonInputs` from the separately supplied admissibility conditions, full completed functional equations, original and dual `HasProd` limits, and outside-set L/dual-L matching. All these inputs use `D.eulerData χ B` and `E.eulerData χ B`. Their partial tails depend on this one B, while their local factors are definitionally the fixed `D.localFactors χ` and `E.localFactors χ`.

The retained block in the first comparison contains every actual infinite place. It lies in `exceptionalPlaces K B`, which also contains w because w belongs to T and T is contained in B. An infinite place cannot lie in the auxiliary complement. At every auxiliary finite place the proof splits membership in T: the supplied depth gives stability inside T, and `OutsideGammaStability` gives stability outside T. This accounts for every new member of B outside T, including additional ramification of either chosen character. The infinity-preservation input identifies each side's twisted factors with its own original factors.

The second comparison retains the entire archimedean block together with w. The finite-preservation input applies because the second character is trivial at w. Every remaining auxiliary finite place differs from w, so the same T-membership split supplies stability using the second character's depth condition on T minus {w} and the outside-T input elsewhere. Applying the published `twoTwistIsolation` theorem first yields equality of the whole original archimedean gamma product, then uses that identity and nonzero germs to isolate the original gamma germ at w. The final existential retains both character witnesses, their prescriptions, B, and unramifiedness outside B. Local factors at the trivial character, and therefore the two final comparisons, have no B dependence.

The result remains conditional on character isolation and every stated global analytic, preservation, and local-stability premise. It constructs the two witnesses from the isolation premise; it does not discharge that premise or prove factor existence, global functional equations, or stability. All conclusions concern punctured meromorphic germs, and every gamma product is finite. Equality of individual infinite-place factors is neither assumed nor concluded.
