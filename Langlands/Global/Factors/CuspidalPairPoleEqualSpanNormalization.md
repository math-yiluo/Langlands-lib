# Extending ordinary equal-rank normalizations to the spans of pure vectors

Fix two cuspidal presentations with irreducible admissible finite components and a retained finite set `S` containing both exceptional sets and the finite additive conductor support. The nontrivial unitary adelic additive character is trivial on the diagonal field and its canonical finite components have conductor zero outside `S`. Fix a Schwartz–Bruhat test standard outside `S` and the factor product `Linf(s) ∏ v ∈ S, c.finiteL v s` for the supplied finite choice `c`. Both the pure-pair normalization input and the normalization on the two spans use this fixed product. Each entire normalized value multiplied by this product agrees with its partial integral on a right half-plane.

`hasOrdinaryEqualNormalizationsOnPureSpans` extends the entire ordinary normalizations of all pure pairs to the two pure-vector spans for this test. The chosen global Whittaker kernel is bilinear in the two cuspidal vectors. Every vector in either span is spherical outside `S`, so the equal Euler factorization under the stated character and support hypotheses gives absolute integrability on a common right half-plane. Restricting to the partial domain preserves integrability. Span induction adds and scales the entire normalizations and verifies each new integral identity on a right half-plane.

This is an internal derivation from the Library's equal Euler factorization and pure tensors. The source is J. W. Cogdell, *Analytic Theory of L-Functions for GL(n)*, chapter 9 of Bernstein and Gelbart, eds., *An Introduction to the Langlands Program*, Birkhäuser, 2004, §2.3, printed p. 210 (PDF p. 213), and Theorem 3.3, printed p. 215 (PDF p. 218).
