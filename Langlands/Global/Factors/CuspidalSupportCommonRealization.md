# A common cuspidal realization from the actual selected period

`CommonCuspidalRealizationStatement` asks for a common restored cuspidal
coefficient realization of every irreducible automorphic operator
subquotient. The construction lemma starts with a cyclic source that
surjects onto that subquotient and embeds equivariantly into the
translated block constant terms. It assumes compact regularity, finite
smoothness, a continuous block central eigencharacter trivial on principal
ideles, and vanishing of the strict refinements of the period.

Unitarizing the character on each block gives a tensor of cusp-form
coefficients by the slice construction used for finite cuspidal support.
Each block realization decomposes as a direct sum of cuspidal
realizations. Their coordinate projections give the candidate tuples.
Algebraic tensor support bounds the nonzero projections of each vector.
Joint vanishing on all compact and finite translates forces the
translated period to vanish and hence kills the quotient image.

Restore each selected factor by the inverse determinant twist. The
restored tensors have the exact compact and real Lie equations. The
product of the restoring characters cancels the scalar in the finite
parabolic equation, and the inverse half-modulus equivalence gives
normalized finite covariance. The section selection argument chooses
one tuple and a cyclic source, yielding both the finite witness and the
restored archimedean coefficient realization.

Given a proof of the common realization and general real-place cuspidal
parameters, the final reduction gives `CuspidalSupportEveryPlaceStatement`.
The coefficient construction follows the period and projection argument
in Langlands (1979),
Proposition 2 and Lemmas 4–6, printed pp. 204–205, registered PDF
pp. 199–200. The local constituent step follows his Lemma 1, printed
p. 203, registered PDF p. 198, as in Getz–Hahn (2024), Theorem 10.7.1
and its remark, printed pp. 286–287, registered PDF pp. 298–299.
