# Left compact covariance of the infinite block evaluation

`infiniteCoefficientCompactMap` acts on the infinite coefficient tensor by the genuine compact action of each block at each place. Its source and target use the same grouping, first by place and then by block.

`infiniteEvaluation_left` compares left multiplication by a point of the actual infinite compact parabolic intersection with the action of its projected compact Levi blocks on the coefficients. At each place, the compact coordinate formula identifies the multiplier with the block diagonal embedding of those matrices. Applying the real or complex evaluation law and tensoring the resulting equalities gives the full infinite law.

This comparison is internal to the inducing model for `CuspidalIsobaricAutomorphyStatement`. Getz and Hahn (2024), §10.1, pp. 270–271 (PDF 282–283), give the scalar compact model and the compact Levi intersection.
