# Identifying a completed equation’s finite standard epsilon factor

`distinguishedFiniteStandardEpsilon` proves `DistinguishedFiniteStandardEpsilonStatement` on a supplied `FinitePairFactorChoice` for a cuspidal pair of ranks `r + 1` and `1`. At a chosen finite place `v`, the inputs are a bicontinuous field isomorphism from an actual nonarchimedean local field `F` to the canonical p-adic completion and an actual representation `ρ` over `F`. The two completed local actions, pulled back entrywise via the field isomorphism, must be equivalent to `ρ` and the trivial representation of `GL₁(F)`, respectively. For any supplied JPSS standard pair certificate of `ρ` against that trivial representation at the pulled-back additive component and measure, the certified coefficient and exponent equal `c.epsilon v`. Thus `c.finiteEpsilon v z` is the same epsilon monomial at the actual residue cardinality of `F`, for every `z`. Continuity can be transferred from the original completion topology by `CompletionPadic.completion_topology_eq`.

The additive character is composed with the given field isomorphism. The measure is pushed forward along its inverse, with the Borel structures; pushing it forward along the original isomorphism recovers the supplied finite measure. `completionHaarPullback_isSelfDual` proves intrinsic self-duality for the pulled-back character whenever the supplied measure is self-dual. These constructions use the finite components of the supplied factor choice, not substitute characters or rescaled measures.

The proof first uses the representation equivalences and normalized JPSS transport to move the original standard certificate to the two actual completed representatives. The equal- or unequal-rank certificate gives the first member a nonzero standard Whittaker functional and the second member a nonzero inverse-character functional. The inverse and standard characters agree for `GL₁`. With the representatives’ irreducibility and admissibility this gives genericity, without a separate genericity hypothesis. The completed factor choice’s class certificate gives a classification epsilon certificate on the same `padicObject` representatives by `isAutomorphicPairEpsilonFactor_iff`. The published `genericLanglandsPairEpsilonFactorComparison` turns that certificate into the actual JPSS certificate. Uniqueness identifies the two pairs in `ℂˣ × ℤ`, and the field transport lemma identifies the two residue cardinalities.

This is an internal comparison for the distinguished-place step toward `CuspidalSigmaLiftConductorStatement` (the conductor identity for the sigma-lift of a supercuspidal representation of `GL_n`, Arthur–Clozel 1989, Ch. 1, Prop. 6.9(ii)). It combines existing class, harmonic-data and integral transport, with the Library’s proof of the generic classification-to-JPSS comparison. It does not construct a globalization, the original local certificate, a completed equation, or any cyclic lift. It compares isomorphic fields and does not assert a trace relation on the additive components of two different fields or any cross-field epsilon or conductor identity.
