# Integrability of cuspidal block sections on the canonical Levi quotient

`exists_norm_le_normalizedSlice_of_normOne` gives a single bound for the normalized Levi slices of a smooth compact-finite cuspidal block section at all compact arguments and all Levi arguments of block determinant norm one. Each pure block coefficient is a product of cuspidal vectors and is bounded there by `CuspCentralBound.exists_norm_evaluate_le_of_absDet_eq_one`. Finite linear combinations inherit bounds. The compact slices occupy a common finite-dimensional coefficient space. A basis obtained by finite point evaluations gives continuous coefficient coordinates in the compact argument, so each coordinate is bounded.

`integrable_weightedSquare_of_cuspidal` descends the normalized Levi value using invariance under the rational Levi and equivariance under the positive split component. The descended function is continuous, since it is the descent of a continuous invariant function through the open quotient map. Its squared norm is the weighted square in `IsSquareIntegrableInducedForm`. Every quotient class has a norm-one representative by `canonicalNormOneQuotientHomeomorph`. On those representatives both half-modulus factors are one and every block has determinant norm one. This identifies the normalized value with the bounded compact slice. The Haar probability on the compact subgroup and `connectedReductiveQuotientFiniteVolume` then give integrability on the product with the canonical Levi measure.

The cuspidal bound uses the Siegel-chamber estimate at decay exponent zero on a bounded determinant range. The source for the decay is Mœglin–Waldspurger, *Spectral Decomposition and Eisenstein Series* (1995), Corollary I.2.12, p. 35, and its application to cuspidal forms in I.2.18, p. 39. The canonical quotient and its norm-one description follow Getz–Hahn, *An Introduction to Automorphic Representations* (2024), §2.6, (2.20)–(2.22) and Theorem 2.6.3, pp. 68–69.
