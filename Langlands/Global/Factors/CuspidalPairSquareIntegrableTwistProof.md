# Square-integrable imaginary twists of cuspidal-pair sections

`cuspidalPairSquareIntegrableTwist` proves the proposition `CuspidalPairSquareIntegrableTwistStatement` for each of its published inputs. The witnesses are the real combination `y` of the two block determinants and the transported section multiplied by `m_P^{-iy}`.

For the second block, the chosen contragredient has a unitary central character. The proof uses equivalence with the pointwise conjugate of the unitary-central-character realization and transports the conjugate central character through that equivalence. For each block, `NormPowerTwist.exists_normPower_twist_isTrivialOnSplitCentre` supplies a normalizing determinant twist. `CuspidalPartialPairOrderOne.normalizer_re_eq_zero` uses unitarity to put its exponent on the imaginary axis. The two real coefficients of those imaginary exponents define `y` in the block determinant parameter space. The defining exponential identities give `φ = ψ m_P^{iy}` after adelic transport.

`exists_smoothSection_parameterTwist` realizes `ψ` with the determinant-twisted inducing realizations. These realizations are cuspidal and jointly admissible, so `SchemeSection.isAutomorphicFunctionOn` gives all automorphy conditions, including finiteness under the centre of the enveloping algebra, for each supplied smooth atlas and commutator. The adjoint determinant comparison at block scalar points identifies the half modulus used by the matrix section with the scheme half modulus. The two twisted block realizations are fixed by positive real scalar ideles, which are the block projections of the canonical split component. Thus the normalized slices are invariant under this component, and Iwasawa factorization gives the half-modulus equivariance of `ψ` on the whole group. Finally `integrable_weightedSquare_of_cuspidal` integrates the descended squared norm of the normalized restriction on the finite-volume canonical quotient.
