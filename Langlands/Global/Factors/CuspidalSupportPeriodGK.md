# Weak continuity on the selected cyclic period

The compact action on the translated-period space is intertwined by
each period translation of the original automorphic realization. The
images of these translations span that space. Each scalar compact
orbit is therefore continuous by the continuity theorem for the
original realization. The cyclic subspace inherits this continuity
from its compact intertwining inclusion.

The continuity theorem supplies the weak continuity hypothesis of
`source_isRealGKModule` and `source_isComplexGKModule`. Compact finiteness
and the compact curve derivative on this cyclic source are provided by
`CuspidalBlockCharacterSource`.
