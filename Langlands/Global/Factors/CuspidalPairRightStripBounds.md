# Bounds of the completed pair product on far-right strips

`CompletedPairRightStripBounds` concerns any choice of finite pair \(L\)-data and any lists of real and complex archimedean blocks. If the initial product agrees with \(\Lambda\) on a right half-plane as in `FinitePairLChoice.IsCompletedLFunction`, then \(\Lambda\) is bounded on every closed vertical strip lying sufficiently far to the right. The bound may depend on the strip.

The finite part uses the uniform Satake power bounds of `RankinSelbergGlobal.exists_hasSatakeBound_presentation`. Outside the two exceptional sets, `FinitePairLChoice.finiteL_eq_partialEulerFactor` identifies the specified local factor with the spherical factor. The polynomial estimate from `CuspidalPairEulerConvergence.norm_inv_eval_pairEulerPolynomial_sub_one_le` majorizes the difference from one by a constant times \(q_v^{-((r+1)(t+1)+2)}\) uniformly on a right half-plane. At each exceptional place \(q_v^{-s}\) tends uniformly to zero as \(\operatorname{Re}s\) increases, and the certified polynomial has constant term one. Its reciprocal consequently has norm at most two on a sufficiently far-right half-plane. Adding the indicator of the exceptional set to the spherical majorant gives a single summable nonnegative function \(M_v\) with \(|L_v(s)|\le 1+M_v\). The finite products then have norm at most \(\exp(\sum_v M_v)\), and the existing convergence theorem passes that bound to the infinite product.

The archimedean part expresses both pair factors as finite products of Gamma blocks. On a sufficiently far-right strip the shifted Gamma arguments have positive real parts, so the Gamma integral bounds their norms by `norm_Gamma_le_on_strip`. The powers of \(\pi\) and \(2\pi\) in the block definitions contribute bounded prefactors there. Multiplying these bounds gives the estimate for the prescribed archimedean product, and multiplying by the finite bound gives the result for \(\Lambda\).

This estimate is derived from the Library's Satake and Gamma bounds. It supplies the right boundary line for the Phragmén–Lindelöf argument for the completed pair function.
