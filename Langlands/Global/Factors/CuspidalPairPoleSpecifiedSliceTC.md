# The specified slice comparison over totally complex fields

Fix a totally complex field with compact additive adelic quotient and an
actual split-centre-trivial cuspidal spectral presentation with irreducible
admissible finite components. The continuous nontrivial unitary adelic
additive character is trivial on the diagonal field. Each supplied complex
scale describes its restriction at the corresponding place, and the
supplied complex standard parameter belongs to the actual realization there.

The ordinary normalized Rankin–Selberg tests use a normalized actual
Whittaker slice model for the given character and another for its inverse,
with the full specified local range at each infinite place. Apply the
totally complex slice data theorem once to the supplied character and again
to its inverse. Inversion preserves triviality on the diagonal field and
negates every complex-place additive scale. The real-place range
conditions hold because the real-place index type is empty.

This is an internal consequence of the complex slice comparison and the
Library's adelic additive character inversion identities.
