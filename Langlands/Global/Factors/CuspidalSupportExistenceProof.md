# Cuspidal support at the finite places

`Langlands.Global.cuspidalSupportExistence` proves `Langlands.Global.CuspidalSupportExistenceStatement.{u}`. The proof keeps the original irreducible operator subquotient throughout. It follows the only-if implication of Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Proposition 2, printed pp. 204–205, Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 199–200. The passage to the finite local factors is the constituent argument of Lemma 1, printed p. 203, PDF p. 198.

Start with the normalized standard block constant terms. Order the cut sets by refinement and use the empty-cut term, which evaluates to the original form. Choose a cyclic realized source whose selected constant term still admits the original simple quotient and whose strict refinements vanish. Constant-term transitivity and the actual block-slice regularity give cuspidality and automorphy on each block. The companion result `eq_zero_of_orthogonalToCuspidalLeviProducts` is a compact-test form of Lemma 5. The selection in this proof uses the cut-set filtration and strict-refinement vanishing directly.

Take the span of the left block-centre translates of the selected constant terms, with the original operators acting equivariantly. The centre orbit is finite-dimensional at each vector. This uses the finite codimension of the enveloping-centre annihilator on Levi slices, compact norm-one idele classes, and finite parabolic double cosets at a fixed level. The algebraic argument of Lemma 6 selects a central eigenvector whose cyclic subspace still surjects onto the same simple quotient. Its eigencharacter is continuous and trivial on principal block scalars. It therefore restricts to Hecke characters of the individual blocks.

Unitarize each of these characters by a determinant twist. The resulting coordinate slices belong to actual fixed-character cuspidal realizations. At each vector a common finite level and two Casimir polynomials put all the slices in finite-dimensional subspaces of these realizations. Faithful product evaluation then gives an algebraic tensor of cuspidal coefficients. The finite right covariance, the block Lie formula with the determinant correction, and the vanishing for strictly upper block Lie directions are identities on this tensor. Compact differentiation and Iwasawa detection control the joint kernel of the evaluated projections.

Decompose the block realizations as algebraic direct sums of irreducible cuspidal realizations at unitary central character. The cyclic generator has only finitely many relevant projected sections at its compact type and finite level. A coordinate kernel lies in the kernel of the given simple quotient, so one tuple of actual cuspidal factors retains that quotient. Restore the determinant characters and absorb the inverse half modulus into these factors. This gives the finite adelic subquotient of normalized induction used in the final step.

Choose spectral presentations of the actual cuspidal factors from their irreducible admissible finite slices. The finite-place comparison uses the local tensor factors and their multiplicity spaces to produce separating maps to the canonical normalized local induction. Subquotient transfer gives irreducible admissible local constituents with injections into the original local restrictions. Smoothness of the finite adelic realization gives the spherical condition away from a finite set. All required carriers and presentations have the universe specified in the statement.
