# An actual equal-rank normalized test with strip growth

`exists_growthNormalizedEqualTest_of_local` obtains a standard-outside ordinary equal-rank test whose normalized partial integral is nonzero at the specified spectral point and of exponential strip growth. The two cuspidal realizations are trivial on the split centre and the specified presentations have irreducible admissible finite components. The retained finite set includes both exceptional sets and the finite additive conductor support of the nontrivial unitary adelic character trivial on the diagonal number field. Its canonical nontrivial finite components and their self-dual Haar measures are supplied. The specified real and complex parameters and the finite Euler factor choice determine the normalization factors. The archimedean premises are the slice comparisons for opposite characters with the specified model ranges, pointwise normalized generation, and growth of the ordinary normalized values.

Choose the local tests at the given point. The finite choices have uniformly bounded normalized values on each closed strip. The prescribed pure product theorem realizes the local choices on actual cuspidal vectors and a pure Schwartz–Bruhat test with a fixed nonzero scalar in the normalized identity. On a right half-plane the normalized value times the specified archimedean and retained finite factors equals the partial integral. Nonvanishing follows place by place, and the strip bound follows from that identity and the local growth bounds. The test is a member of `NormalizedEqualPoleTest` for the same finite set and can be used in the equal normalized family.

This is an internal derivation for the ordinary Rankin–Selberg argument in J. W. Cogdell, *Analytic Theory of L-Functions for GL(n)*, chapter 9 of Bernstein and Gelbart, eds., *An Introduction to the Langlands Program*, Birkhäuser, 2004, Theorem 4.1, printed pp. 218–221. The archimedean growth premises are framed using Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, 2009, Theorem 2.1, author PDF pp. 6–7.

`exists_growthNormalizedEqualTest_of_local_at` obtains real generation only at a real place. The complex generation and the placewise growth estimates enter as before.
