# The parabolic modulus at scalar points of a two-block Levi

`parameterPower_halfModulus_of_blockScalar` identifies the parabolic half modulus in `IsSplitComponentEquivariant` with the matrix half-character of the upper two-block parabolic at block scalar Levi points.

The proof uses the actual dual-number tangent points. Under the general linear group coordinates, the image of `Lie U` consists of the matrices `X` for which `1 + εX` has identity diagonal blocks and zero lower-left block. Extracting the upper-right block gives a basis with `(r + 1)(t + 1)` entries. Conjugation by the block scalars `z₀` and `z₁` acts on each basis entry by `z₀/z₁`. The determinant on `Lie P` restricts to the product of the determinants on `Lie U` and `Lie M`. The determinant on `Lie M` is trivial by connected reductivity. Therefore the resulting unit at the Levi point is `(z₀/z₁)^((r + 1)(t + 1))`. Its idelic norm is the matrix modulus by the determinant formula for scalar matrices. Taking the positive square root gives the required complex-valued half modulus.

The matrix calculation is derived from the actual conjugation action and the Levi decomposition of the adjoint determinant. The normalization is the one in Mœglin–Waldspurger, *Spectral Decomposition and Eisenstein Series* (1995), I.1.13, p. 18, where `ρ_P` is the half-sum for the action on the radical and the Iwasawa measure uses `m^{-2ρ_P}`.
