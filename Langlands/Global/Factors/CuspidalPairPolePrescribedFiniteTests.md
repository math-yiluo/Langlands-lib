# Ordinary equal-rank normalization for prescribed finite vectors

`factorCoefficientOfVector` transports a nonzero local Whittaker functional to the exact finite tensor factor. It records its coefficient on the specified vector and supplies a reference vector with evaluation one for that functional.

`exists_poleFiniteEqualNormalization_of_vectors` uses two arbitrary specified vectors on the exact finite factors and a specified row test. The irreducible admissible local actions of the split-centre-trivial cuspidal representations have opposite-character Whittaker models. The proof selects nonzero functionals, applies the coefficient maps to the supplied vectors, and uses the resulting Rankin–Selberg integral with the prescribed finite Euler polynomial. It obtains an entire normalization bounded on each vertical strip. The vectors in both finite factor witnesses are the supplied vectors themselves.

This is an internal derivation using the published local genericity and ordinary p-adic normalization results. The underlying equal-rank integral is that of Jacquet, Piatetski-Shapiro and Shalika, *Rankin–Selberg Convolutions*, 1983, §2.4, display (1), printed p. 387 (PDF p. 22).
