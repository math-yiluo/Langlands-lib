# Place Lie actions on the cuspidal block tensor and its smooth sections

`CuspidalSmoothTensorPlaceLieStatement` uses the given cuspidal realizations, their spectral presentations with irreducible admissible finite components, and their real and complex place parameters. It considers a complex-linear map from a finite factor tensored with the original-order infinite standard modules into the smooth compact-finite scalar section carrier. The map intertwines the infinite compact action. At infinite compact identity, its normalized Levi coefficient on a pure tensor is the grouped coefficient equivalence applied to the finite evaluation and the original-order infinite evaluation. The statement says that the map commutes with the complexified Lie operator at each infinite place.

The proof differentiates along the upper triangular parabolic line in one place. At infinite compact identity, both the standard evaluation and the scalar section coefficient give the same half-character derivative and a sum of diagonal-block Lie operators. `groupedBlockTensorEquiv_placeLie` identifies that sum after regrouping the place coefficients into the actual cuspidal block spaces. The paired comparison extends from pure tensors by linearity. `sectionLie_real_of_upper` and `sectionLie_complex_of_upper` add the skew-adjoint directions by differentiating compact equivariance and test all compact translates. Complex linearity then gives the result on the entire complexified Lie algebra at the selected place.
