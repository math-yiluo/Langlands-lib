# Cuspidal induction in the adelic compact picture

`HasFiniteScalarCovariance` expresses normalized left parabolic covariance of the finite inducing evaluations after pairing with arbitrary infinite coefficient tensors. `compactEvaluation_left` combines this formula with the compact Levi action on the archimedean tensor evaluations. The finite half character is one on the intersection with the finite integral group. Multiplication of the finite and infinite Levi points gives the projection of the original compact parabolic element, so the paired evaluations have the scalar covariance required by adelic compact induction.

The calculation implements the compact-picture comparison for normalized block induction in Getz–Hahn (2024), §10.1, printed pp. 270–271 (PDF pp. 282–283), `getz-hahn-2024-automorphic-representations`, SHA-256 `a125cf9c0f445972cbbd3552b4fb9905ca948108a05880f90eaa43558d09a507`.
