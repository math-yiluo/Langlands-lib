# Relative pair L-factors from certified poles

`Langlands.Global.PoleComparison.RelativeIsometryPairLFactorStatement.{uF,uW,uP}` is the measure existence and pair L-factor part of `RelativeIsometryFactorStatement` with the same field, arithmetic, pole and isometry hypotheses. It quantifies over every requested nontrivial additive character, every self-dual measure and every pair in the generated Weil lattice. `relativeIsometryPairLFactor` proves it.

For a witnessed pair, the pole data compare the arithmetic local tensor with the automorphic pair using the dual of the second component. Identify this component by `isSmoothDual_iff_eq_contragredient` and take its actual dual support by `isUnitaryLatticeSupport_contragredient`. The original arithmetic and automorphic denominator polynomials are normalized at zero and have all roots on the unit circle by `weilDeligne_eulerPolynomial_root_norm` and `isPadicSymmetricLPolynomial_roots_of_unitarySupport`.

For each point \(z\) of the unit circle, the unitary unramified character with value \(z\) on a uniformizer extends by `unitaryLocalCharacterExtension`. The determinant-multiple and association theorems `cuspidalDeterminantMultiple` and `presentedDeterminantMultipleAssociation` give another witnessed generator from the first. Geometric reciprocity identifies its Weil character by `localTransferReciprocity` and `weilCharacter_fieldUnramified`. The laws `arithmeticPairLFactor_unramifiedTwist` and `unramifiedTwistLFactor` substitute \(zX\) in the two original denominators. On the automorphic side, the twisted component and the local determinant twist have the same unitary support. The virtual L-factor is uniquely determined by this support by `isUnitaryPairLFactor_of_equiv_productInduced` and `isUnitaryPairLFactor_iff`.

`completedPoleTransfer` equates the distinguished orders at \(s=0\) for the twisted witnessed pair, using the supplied completed germs and their holomorphic nonzero omitted factors. `meromorphicOrderAt_rescaledPairLFactor` reads the two orders as the negatives of the original root multiplicities at \(z\). Since this holds at every point of the circle, `eq_of_unitCircle_rootMultiplicity` gives equality of the denominator polynomials. `unitaryPairLFactor_eq_of_expansion` identifies the arithmetic value with the value of the two witnessed lattice elements.

`relativeWitnessedDualClosure_of_centralComparison` supplies a dual witness for the second generator. Involutivity gives the comparison with the original second generator, and `relativePairLFactor_of_witnessed` extends it by integer bilinearity along the graph of the isometry. `IsUnitaryPairLFactor.change` allows any requested character and self-dual measure. `isSelfDual_localSelfDualHaar` supplies a measure for each character.

This proves the L-factor identity of Henniart (2000), §2.6 Proposition (b), in the witnessed finite-relative setting. The proof uses the same dual pair of factors as the pole argument in §2.8 and the unitary twisting property of §2.6 Proposition (a). Varying the twist recovers the entire L-polynomial from the certified orders at the origin.
