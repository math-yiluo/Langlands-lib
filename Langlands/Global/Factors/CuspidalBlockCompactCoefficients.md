# Compact actions in the cuspidal block coefficients

`blockInfiniteCompactAction` acts on the tensor of the actual place coefficients of one cuspidal block. It applies the action of each orthogonal or unitary matrix according to the corresponding coordinate of the given infinite compact element.

`blockVectorEquiv_compact` proves that this action agrees with right compact translation in the inducing cuspidal realization under the actual finite and infinite tensor equivalence. The published presentation equivalence proves the law for an element supported at one place on a pure tensor. These elements generate the finite product of local compact groups. Tensor extensionality extends the comparison in the infinite tensor to all its vectors, with the finite coefficient held fixed.

This is internal to the scalar inducing model for Task 6553. Flath (1979), §2, pp. 180–181 (PDF 176–177), gives the external tensor product context. Getz and Hahn (2024), §10.1, pp. 270–271 (PDF 282–283), give the scalar compact model.
