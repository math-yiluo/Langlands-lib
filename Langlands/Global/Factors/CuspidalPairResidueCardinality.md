# Residue cardinalities in cuspidal pair factors

`FinitePairFactorChoice.one_lt_residueCardinality` states that the canonical residue cardinality at every finite place of a number field is greater than one. It applies the existing bound for that place's canonical p-adic completion. It requires neither compactness of the adelic quotient nor any choice of pair factors.

The declaration is moved from [CuspidalPairPartialGammaReduction](CuspidalPairPartialGammaReduction.md) with its name, type and proof preserved for shared analytic use.
