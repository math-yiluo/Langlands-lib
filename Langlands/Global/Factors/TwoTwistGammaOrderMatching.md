# Two-twist isolation with matching auxiliary gamma orders

`TwoTwistGammaOrderMatchingStatement` compares two finite Euler data pairs. The first comparison retains the archimedean block and the second retains that block and one finite place. Each comparison assumes equality of local orders at the auxiliary places. The preserved archimedean product orders are matched between the two comparisons.

The proof uses the global finite gamma comparison on each pair. The order of each finite product is the sum of the local orders. Splitting off the auxiliary sum and cancelling it leaves equality on the retained block. The first comparison then identifies the archimedean contributions in the second, giving equality at the selected place. Admissibility makes every cancelled sum finite.

This is the order version of the two comparisons of Henniart–Herb (1995), §8.16, printed pp. 182–183, equations (13)–(19). It also applies when an auxiliary place has been added to the exceptional set and the two local orders agree there.
