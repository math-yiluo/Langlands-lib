# A cuspidal self-pair Euler product has no entire continuation

`Langlands.Global.CuspidalSelfPairAssembly.NoEntireSelfPairContinuation` says that for two spectral presentations of the same rank, no entire function is the value of their partial pair Euler product on a right half-plane. The product runs over finite places outside a finite set containing both exceptional sets. `not_exists_entire_selfPair` proves the proposition when the first cuspidal realization has unitary central character and the second realizes its contragredient.

The local logarithms and the exponential of the global series come from Jacquet and Shalika, *On Euler Products and the Classification of Automorphic Representations I* (1981), Theorem (5.3), equations (1)–(3), p. 556 (PDF p. 58). For the self-pair the coefficients are nonnegative. `selfPair_hasProd_exp` uses the Library's coefficient estimate and absolute summability for `Re s > 2` to identify the product with the exponential of this series. The proof of the obstruction applies `GeneralDirichlet.summableAt_of_exp`, the exponential form of Landau's theorem, to a hypothetical entire continuation. The series would then converge on a half-plane containing zero. Its subseries at any one finite place outside the exceptional set would define a holomorphic function on that half-plane.

The exponential of the one-place series times the local Euler polynomial evaluated at the spectral point equals one on a right half-plane. The identity theorem gives the same equality at zero. There the spectral point is one and the Euler polynomial vanishes, since the contragredient Satake multiset contains the inverse of each original Satake eigenvalue. The obstruction is derived from the series formulas and the Library's local Euler polynomial and contragredient spectrum identities.
