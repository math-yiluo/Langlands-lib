# Matrix coordinates of the adelic block Levi

`blockLeviAffineEquiv` identifies affine points of the scheme block Levi with tuples of invertible diagonal blocks. The represented-subgroup equivalence supplies the block diagonal matrix and `leviEquiv` extracts its diagonal blocks. Inclusion in the parabolic gives `leviInParabolic` in these coordinates. The scheme projections to the block general linear groups agree with the diagonal-block projections on affine points.

`determinantNorm_matrixLeviPoint_of_normOne` applies the defining norm-one identity to the determinant character of each block pulled back to the Levi. Thus a norm-one Levi point has determinant norm one in every block. For `exists_matrixLeviPoint_eq_positiveScalar`, the canonical positive subgroup is the positive image of a split central torus over the ground field. Each block projection has a section, so the projection of that torus is central in the block general linear group and factors through its scalar torus. The intrinsic positive-subgroup property applied to this character yields a positive real scalar idele in that block, with finite components one.

The comparisons are derived from the represented matrix subgroups, the scalar split component of a general linear group, and `RestrictionSplitCentre.canonicalSubgroup_eq_imageSubgroup`. The adelic conventions are those of Getz–Hahn, *An Introduction to Automorphic Representations* (2024), §2.6, (2.19)–(2.20), pp. 68–69.
