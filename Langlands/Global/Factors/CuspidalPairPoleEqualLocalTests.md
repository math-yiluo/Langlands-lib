# Equal-rank covering tests from the local coefficient models

`HasCoveringEqualPartialTests` asks for an actual equal-rank test at each spectral point. Its two cuspidal vectors are spherical outside the retained finite set `S`, and its adelic Schwartz–Bruhat function is standard there. The ordinary partial integral of the opposite-character Whittaker functions has an entire normalized value that is nonzero at the prescribed point. On a right half-plane the integral equals that value times the specified archimedean factor and the product of the specified finite factors in `S`.

`exists_coveringEqualPureTest_of_local` fixes two split-centre-trivial cuspidal realizations with spectral presentations having irreducible admissible finite components. The set `S` contains both exceptional sets and the finite additive conductor support. The nontrivial unitary adelic additive character is trivial on the diagonal number field. Its canonical nontrivial finite components and self-dual Haar measures are supplied at the places in `S`. The inputs at infinity are the exact real and complex parameters, the two opposite-character slice comparisons with the specified model ranges, and pointwise normalized generation in those models.

Apply the Library's irreducibility theorem to each supplied archimedean parameter and choose local normalized tests with nonzero values at the given point. Lift both families of infinite coefficients simultaneously using the normalized finite reference vectors. The pure equal-rank normalization formula gives the value as the product of the local values times a nonzero scalar.

This is an internal derivation for the global Rankin–Selberg argument of J. W. Cogdell, *Analytic Theory of L-Functions for GL(n)*, chapter 9 of Bernstein and Gelbart, eds., *An Introduction to the Langlands Program*, Birkhäuser, 2004. Theorem 4.1 gives the equal-rank normalized product at printed p. 219 (PDF p. 222). The pointwise nonvanishing and pole argument is in §4.2, printed pp. 221–222 (PDF pp. 224–225). The ordinary local integral and normalized generation premises follow the formulation in H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, 2009, display (2.6), Theorem 2.1(ii), Remark 2.2, and Theorems 2.6(ii) and 2.7(ii), author PDF pp. 6–10.

`exists_coveringEqualPureTest_of_local_at` invokes real generation only for an actual real place of the field. The all-rank interface supplies that premise at every field.
