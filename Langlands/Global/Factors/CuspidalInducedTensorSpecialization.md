# Automorphic specialization of a finite cuspidal constituent tensor

The specified local quotient maps give the finite restricted tensor subquotient. An injective tensor realization on the original smooth block sections, with a nonzero infinite operator carrier, gives a simple full operator quotient of an invariant numerator and retains an embedding of the prescribed finite tensor. The infinite carrier can be chosen without a prescribed infinite quotient.

Joint admissibility of the actual cuspidal inducing modules gives the compact projection on the block model. Under meromorphic continuation and convergence-region injectivity on finite projected ranges, specialization gives the same simple quotient from stable automorphic functions. Descent to the actual automorphic operators gives one irreducible automorphic subquotient. The embedding of the finite restricted tensor gives its local embeddings at every finite place.

This is a conditional assembly for Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Lemma 1 and Proposition 2, printed pp. 203–207, Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 198–202. It applies once the tensor source and analytic inputs are proved.
