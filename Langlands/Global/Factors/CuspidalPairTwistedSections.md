# Block determinant twists of actual adelic sections

For an ordered block shape, `determinantParameter` is the sum of the complex exponents tensored with the rational determinant characters of the diagonal blocks. `heightPower_determinantParameter` computes the resulting height power in an Iwasawa factorization `q k`. The logarithmic pairing evaluates each block character at the parabolic factor `q`, so exponentiation gives the product of the Hecke norm powers of the block determinants.

`exists_smoothSection_parameterTwist` realizes the scalar height twist as a smooth compact-finite section induced from the determinant twists of the original block realizations. Multiplication by the product of the determinant characters takes every pure block coefficient to the coefficient of the corresponding twisted vectors. Linearity extends the calculation to each normalized Levi slice. The height is left unipotent invariant and right invariant under the standard compact. The proof intersects the section's finite level with the standard integral subgroup and uses the right compact intertwiner to transfer compact finiteness. The smooth Iwasawa height gives smoothness on each archimedean slice.

This is the block-matrix realization of the twist `φλ = φ m_P^λ` in Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series*, JAMS 37 (2024), §2.1, p. 190 for the logarithmic pairing and §2.2, p. 191 for the twist on the parabolic quotient. The Library proof derives the formula and realizes it through the block determinant character comparison and the determinant multiplication equivalence of the actual operator realizations.
