# Equal-rank continuation from a covering family of selected tests

`EqualCoveringNormalizedProductAt` asks for a family of selected standard-outside tests at a fixed number field and rank. Their entire normalized values have no common zero. One normalization has the supplied test property. For a conjugate pair the family also contains a nonzero vector paired with its conjugate and a Schwartz function whose value and Fourier value at the origin are both nonzero. This formulation permits placewise-pure tests. `EqualCoveringNormalizedProductStatement` specializes the test property to a normalization that is nonzero somewhere and has exponential strip growth, at every field and rank.

`equalPairCompletion_of_covering_normalized_products_at` constructs a completion with the exact equal-rank pole profile. It also gives a bounded raw integral and a differentiable normalization with the supplied property. Their product identity with the completion holds off the two possible poles. A test with nonzero constant normalization can therefore give strip bounds directly from the raw integral.

`equalPairCompletion_of_covering_normalized_products` obtains the exact equal-rank completion and a test for the strip argument from the selected family. Unfolding and partial Euler factorization supply the initial matching of the normalized tests with the completed Euler product. For pairs failing the contragredient condition, invariance of the determinant-slab pairing rules out poles of all tests. For a contragredient pair, the same finite polynomials and archimedean parameter lists are transported to the conjugate pair. There the specified test has a positive period and yields both poles by the explicit residue coefficients. The entire normalized family gives the continuation through the quotient gluing theorem.

`EqualNormalizedTestFamily.exists_nondual_completion` uses the same gluing proof on a covering family for a nondual pair. The residue witness is discharged by nonduality. Coverage at zero supplies a test, whose required property is `True`. The result gives the completion and its pole profile.

`cuspidalPairPole_of_covering_normalized_products` combines this result with the unequal-rank reduction and the completed functional equation. Its conclusion is the unchanged pole statement in both universes. The completed equation and the two normalization existence statements enter as hypotheses. The fixed-rank formulation is derived by retaining the selected normalization through the same gluing and transfer argument. The proof uses the equal-rank unfolding, residue and continuation arguments of Cogdell 2004, §2.3 and Theorems 4.1–4.2, printed pp. 208–211 and 219–222.

`EqualNormalizedTestFamily.integral_eq_on_right` exposes the matching
on the initial half-plane for reuse with other strip-bound arguments.
