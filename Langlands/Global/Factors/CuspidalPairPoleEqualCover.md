# Equal-rank continuation from a covering family of selected tests

`EqualCoveringNormalizedProductStatement` asks for a family of selected standard-outside tests with entire normalized values having no common zero. One ratio is nonzero somewhere and has exponential strip growth. For a conjugate pair the family also contains a nonzero vector paired with its conjugate and a Schwartz function whose value and Fourier value at the origin are both nonzero. This formulation permits placewise-pure tests.

`equalPairCompletion_of_covering_normalized_products` obtains the exact equal-rank completion and a test for the strip argument from the selected family. Unfolding and partial Euler factorization supply the initial matching of the normalized tests with the completed Euler product. For pairs failing the contragredient condition, invariance of the determinant-slab pairing rules out poles of all tests. For a contragredient pair, the same finite polynomials and archimedean parameter lists are transported to the conjugate pair. There the specified test has a positive period and yields both poles by the explicit residue coefficients. The entire normalized family gives the continuation through the quotient gluing theorem.

`cuspidalPairPole_of_covering_normalized_products` combines this result with the unequal-rank reduction and the completed functional equation. Its conclusion is the unchanged pole statement in both universes. The completed equation and the two normalization existence statements enter as hypotheses. The proof uses the equal-rank unfolding, residue and continuation arguments of Cogdell 2004, §2.3 and Theorems 4.1–4.2, printed pp. 208–211 and 219–222.
