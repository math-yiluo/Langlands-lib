# Specified Whittaker slice ranges for GL₁

`rankOneSpecifiedWhittakerSliceComparisonAt` proves the rank-one case of the specified comparison for a cuspidal realization trivial on the split centre and its spectral presentation with irreducible admissible finite components. It takes a nontrivial unitary adelic character trivial on the diagonal field, its specified local scales, and the supplied real and complex standard parameters. It constructs normalized slice models for the character and its inverse, with exact ranges at both signs of the local scales.

The rank-one construction supplies the two slice models. Finite genericity and normalized references identify their ranges with actual place restrictions. The published forward membership theorem then places each range in the relevant ordinary Whittaker model. In rank one each member of that model is a scalar multiple of the specified character composed with determinant. The normalized slice has a vector with value one at the identity, so its range contains the character and all its multiples. This proves equality of ranges at every place.

This is an internal derivation from the Library's forward comparison and the rank-one central-character and local coefficient calculations. It gives the rank-one input to the ordinary global test construction.
