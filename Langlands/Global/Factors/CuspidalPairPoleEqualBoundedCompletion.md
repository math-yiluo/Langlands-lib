# Equal-rank strip bounds from the bounded sum of actual tests

For an equal-rank cuspidal pair, take a completion with the prescribed
right-half-plane Euler product and the two-point pole profile. Use the
ordinary normalized family to unfold each test in the bounded combination.
On its right half-plane the continued test integral equals the completed
function times the test's entire normalization.

Multiply the test integral and the completion by `s(s − 1)` and fill
their removable singularities. The entire functions of the test identity
agree by analytic continuation. Cancel the common polynomial away from its
two zeros. The finite weighted sum of these identities transfers the
published bounds of the individual test integrals to the completion.

This is the bounded-sum strip argument of J. W. Cogdell, *Analytic theory
of L-functions for GL_n* (2004), §4.1, printed pp. 220–221, applied
to the actual normalized tests and the existing equal-rank continuation.
