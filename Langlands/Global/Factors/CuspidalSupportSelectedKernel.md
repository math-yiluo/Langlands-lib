# One selected coefficient for the finite and archimedean place comparisons

The source is cyclic and maps equivariantly and surjectively onto the
irreducible operator quotient. Each candidate identity evaluation is
normalized by the inverse half-modulus equivalence. The finite covariance
equations give a map into each normalized section space, with kernel given
by simultaneous vanishing on the restored compact and finite translates.
Pointwise finite support, compact finiteness and finite smoothness bound
the support of the sections of the cyclic generator.

Stability under the full operator algebra uses the compact covariance,
weak continuity and compact differentiation laws, and the block Lie
equations. The Lie equations give equivariance in diagonal block
directions and vanishing in strictly upper block directions.

Selection uses the hypothesis that a vector killed by every compact and
finite translate of every projection is killed by the quotient map. It
retains one index and a cyclic subsource on which the selected kernel lies
in the kernel of the restricted quotient map. The normalized section on
this subsource gives the finite subquotient by range descent. The restored
identity evaluation on the same subsource descends through the stable
kernel and provides the archimedean equations for the same cuspidal block
tuple.
