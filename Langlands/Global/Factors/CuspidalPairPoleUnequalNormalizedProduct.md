# Covering normalized unequal-rank tests from archimedean products

`HasCoveringUnequalArchimedeanTests` supplies the archimedean input for the ordinary unequal-rank construction. For each tuple of prescribed finite data and each complex point, it asks for two infinite tensors and, at each infinite place, a pair of functions on the two local groups and quotient measure data. The product that evaluates the first function on the padded smaller matrix and the second on the smaller matrix must be left invariant under the smaller upper unipotent subgroup. Each ordinary local quotient integral equals its supplied L-factor times an entire normalized value on a right half-plane. Each normalized value is nonzero at the requested point. The two genuine global Whittaker functions attached to the cuspidal vectors with those infinite tensors and the prescribed finite reference vectors give the product of the specified local pairs on the partial domain, up to a nonzero scalar.

`nonempty_unequalNormalizedTests_of_archimedean_product` uses this input for two cuspidal realizations trivial on the split centres, with an adelic additive character trivial on the number field and its canonical finite components and self-dual measures. For each point it chooses finite tests with nonzero normalized values for the prescribed finite factors. The associated finite coefficient vectors and the supplied infinite tensors give actual cuspidal vectors spherical outside the retained set. The finite product identity and the assumed infinite product identity give a product of local kernels. The finite integrals are the ordinary JPSS block-zero integrals. The quotient measure comparison transfers the product of the entire normalized values to the partial global integral and preserves nonvanishing at the given point. The complex points index the resulting covering family of tests.

This is the normalized partial integral step of Cogdell (2004), §4.1, printed pp. 219–220 (registered PDF pp. 222–223), using the finite local ideal and the archimedean pointwise input separately.
