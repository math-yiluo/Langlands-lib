# The source of a selected Levi coefficient after stable kernel descent

An action of the actual automorphic operator algebra is regular on the
full compact if its vectors are compact-finite, all scalar compact
orbits are continuous and its compact curve derivatives are the given
infinitesimal action. These laws restrict along an injective intertwiner
and pass to a quotient along a surjective one.

For a chosen block tuple, let the stable kernel consist of vectors for
which every compact and finite translate has zero restored identity
coefficient. Assume the quotient map to the original irreducible
representation kills this kernel. The identity coefficient and the
quotient map descend, and the translated coefficients separate on the
new carrier. Compact and Lie block identities hold on representatives,
so they hold on the quotient. The associated place identities give the
common restored coefficient realization with the given finite
subquotient for this tuple.
