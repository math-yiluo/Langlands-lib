# Bounded combinations of actual normalized equal-rank tests

Suppose the prescribed archimedean normalized ordinary integrals admit a
finite sum equal to one at each infinite place. At each specified finite
place, the actual finite tensor factors supply the bounded combination
from the Laurent generator. Multiply these identities, indexing the
expansion by tuples of local tests.

The prescribed product factorization realizes each tuple as an actual
equal-rank partial integral with standard finite data outside the chosen
set. Its normalized function has the product of the prescribed local
normalized values times a nonzero scalar. Divide the weight of each tuple
by that scalar. The rescaled weights remain bounded on the stated
vertical strips and the sum is again one.

This is the test realization for the finite-sum strip argument of
J. W. Cogdell, *Analytic theory of L-functions for GL_n* (2004),
§4.1, printed pp. 220–221. The archimedean finite sums are the input
described in H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009),
Theorem 2.7, author pp. 9–10.
