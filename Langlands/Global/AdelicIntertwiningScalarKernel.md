# Absolute integrability of the scalar Iwasawa kernel

The constant function satisfies the stated automorphy conditions. Every tangent derivative of a constant vanishes. Tensor algebra induction then identifies the enveloping action on constants with its augmentation. On the center, the augmentation has kernel of codimension one. The compact orbit consists of the constant itself, and the growth estimate uses exponent zero.

Apply the proved intertwining integrability theorem to the constant function at the identity and at the parameter shifted by the source half modulus. The finitely many unipotent roots admit a common lower bound for their tests against the shift, so a single increase of the regularity threshold suffices. This produces the scalar factor in the square-norm estimate from the same rational-quotient measure as the actual intertwining integral.
