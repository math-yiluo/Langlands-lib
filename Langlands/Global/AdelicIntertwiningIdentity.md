# The identity element in the actual global intertwining integral

When the target radical equals the source radical and the Weyl
representative is one, the discrete stabilizer is the entire rational
radical. The normalized Haar on the radical induces the probability
measure of that quotient. Adelically unipotent invariance of the
inducing function and its height factor makes the integrand constant
with value equal to the original twist.

The retraction of the Levi and injectivity of restriction on characters
make the identity Weyl parameter the identity. Thus the inverse height
factor cancels the twist in the normalized operator. This is an internal
calculation using the Library convention for the intertwining integral
and Bernstein–Lapid's normalization in §2.2.
