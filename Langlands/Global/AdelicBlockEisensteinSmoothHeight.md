# Smooth Iwasawa height for actual block parabolics

The local triangular matrix factors at an infinite adelic point lift to the standard block parabolic. The triangular factor lies in the open set of units near the point, so the inverse units chart and the actual group-scheme diffeomorphism make its ambient image smooth. The unitary factor lies in the standard compact, and the two factors give the desired identity after inclusion into the full adeles. The general smooth-height comparison then makes each complex parameter pairing smooth on every infinite slice.

This is a derived comparison for the block-induced section family in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), proof of Proposition 2, printed pp. 205–206, Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 200–201. The local matrix factors and the standard compact comparison come from `ArchimedeanGL.exists_smoothLocalIwasawa` and `ArchimedeanGL.mem_standardInfiniteCompact_iff_mem_unitary`.
