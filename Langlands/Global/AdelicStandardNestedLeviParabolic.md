# Inner parabolics of a standard family

For nested standard parabolics, both original Levis contain the same maximal split torus. The represented intersection theorem constructs an inner parabolic of the outer Levi, together with its rational Levi and geometric radical. The outer radical lies in the smaller parabolic. Splitting a point by the actual outer Levi projection therefore identifies the smaller parabolic with the inverse image of the inner parabolic on every test scheme.

The inner radical maps into the smaller ambient radical. If the inner parabolic were the whole Levi, its inverse image would give containment in the reverse direction. Strict containment of the original standard indices consequently gives a proper parabolic in the Levi.
