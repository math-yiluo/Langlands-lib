# Bruhat pieces in the supplied standard family

A minimal Weyl representative determines two intersection indices. Apply the nested Levi construction to both indices and compare the represented images of the supplied parabolics and radicals with the canonical standard ones. This transports the two rational subgroup identities of the geometric lemma to the supplied objects.

The Weyl conjugacy also transports at the level of represented Levi subgroup functors. It gives an isomorphism of the supplied Levis compatible with rational conjugation in the ambient group. The Iwasawa condition transfers through equality of the actual parabolic images.
