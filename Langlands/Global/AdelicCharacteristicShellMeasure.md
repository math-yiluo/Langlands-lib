# Relative split measure of a characteristic shell

`presentedCharacteristicShellLift` uses the standard parabolic
homomorphism of the given family to form the rational quotient.
Its principal subgroup in the ambient norm-one group agrees with
the principal subgroup of the canonical standard model, because
both homomorphisms represent the same all-test subgroup. Thus the
measurability and injective shell projection proved for the
canonical model apply to the presented parabolic.

Fix a standard index and lower cutoff. The compact \(K\) is Iwasawa
for the minimal parabolic. The Siegel factors use \(K\) and satisfy
both minimal unipotent and intrinsic norm-one Levi rational covers
of (7.1). The radical measure \(dn\) is prescribed Haar. The unipotent
cover identifies the image using the compact factor with the image
using the full minimal unipotent in the rational parabolic quotient.

Apply the relative split Iwasawa measure formula to the presented
parabolic and its geometric radical. The standard cocharacter
identifies their dynamic images and so supplies the actual inverse
modulus. In radical, relative split, intrinsic norm-one Levi and compact
coordinates, the measure uses Haar restricted to the principal fundamental
domains on the radical and Levi, the inverse modulus times split Haar, and
compact Haar probability on \(K\).

The theorem chooses a positive simple-root threshold and one sigma-finite
split Haar measure before varying the upper cutoff and increment. For each
cutoff satisfying the threshold on every minimal simple root and each
increment, restrict the coordinate measure to the pullback of the Borel
parabolic lift of \(C^Q_{T+S,T}\). Composing with the rational quotient
projection gives the ambient shell with its restricted quotient Haar measure.
The source is
P. Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*, Advances in
Mathematics 426 (2023), 109107, pp. 22–24 and 26–27, (7.1), (7.6)–(7.7),
(7.13), and (7.20).
