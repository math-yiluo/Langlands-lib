# Relative split measure of a characteristic shell

`presentedCharacteristicShellLift` uses the standard parabolic
homomorphism of the given family to form the rational quotient.
Its principal subgroup in the ambient norm-one group agrees with
the principal subgroup of the canonical standard model, because
both homomorphisms represent the same all-test subgroup. Thus the
measurability and injective shell projection proved for the
canonical model apply to the presented parabolic.

Apply the relative split Iwasawa measure formula to the presented
parabolic and its geometric radical. The standard cocharacter
identifies their dynamic images and so supplies the actual inverse
modulus. This formula chooses split Haar before the truncation
parameter, increment or integrand. Restrict it to the preimage of
the Borel shell in the coordinate product. Then compose with the
projection to the ambient rational quotient, which preserves the
shell measures at the common regularity threshold.

The result is a measure-preserving map to the actual shell with
its restricted quotient Haar measure. The coordinate restriction
is expressed as the pullback of the shell. The source is
P. Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*, Advances in
Mathematics 426 (2023), 109107, pp. 23–24 and 26–27, (7.6)–(7.7)
and (7.20).
