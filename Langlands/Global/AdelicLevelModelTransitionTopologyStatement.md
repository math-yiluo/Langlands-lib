# Topological targets for actual changes of adelic level

This proposition targets the final assertion of Conrad's Theorem 3.6,
printed p.6, proved on p.8 of *Weil and Grothendieck approaches to adelic
points* (December 31, 2011). For S contained in T and a finite-type model
X over the actual S-integer ring, its original level transition into the
actual model pulled back to T-integers is continuous and open. If the
original model is separated over S-integers, the same map is an open
embedding, in particular injective. Separatedness is an additional premise
only for this last conclusion.

Both topologies are exactly the published arithmetic topologies induced
along the original coordinate maps into products of independent local
scheme-point topologies. The point map is the actual pullback lift of
coefficient extension, constructed in AdelicLevelModelTransition. The
statement introduces no alternative topology or assumed transition map.

The curve-place clause retains a compatible algebra over the rational
function field of k and finite-dimensionality over that field, expressed
by FunctionField k F. It does not require separability, perfectness or
finite constants. The number-field clause includes every infinite place.
All fields and models lie in an arbitrary common universe. Finite type is
expressed as LocallyOfFiniteType and QuasiCompact of the structure
morphism; models need not be affine or separated. All finite S and T are
allowed. The arbitrary-constant and empty-set formulations are derived
extensions of the level assertion, not quotations of Conrad's global-field
and nonempty-S conventions.

The proposed derivation compares the original coordinate maps with local
coefficient changes. At the finitely many newly inverted places these
are the original valuation-ring-to-field maps; elsewhere they are
homeomorphisms. Local coefficient openness, the base-change topology
comparison and finite-type level homeomorphisms give the topological
conclusions. Separated coefficient injectivity gives the open embedding.
This file states the target and provides no proof. Model descent,
independence, composition and the global adelic colimit remain separate
obligations.
