# Changing the intertwining representative by a right Levi point

Let \(w\) conjugate the source Levi to the target Levi. Replace \(w\) by
\(wz\) for a rational point \(z\) of the source Levi. If \(c\) denotes the
inverse conjugation on the target Levi, its replacement is \(c\) followed
by conjugation by \(z^{-1}\). The Weyl parameter map is unchanged because
rational characters are invariant under inner automorphisms.

The integral comparison works for right multiplication by a rational point
of the source parabolic. Normality of the source radical implies equality
of the rational stabilizers in the target radical, so the quotient and its
measure agree. The change in the argument of the source twist amounts to
left multiplication by the inverse of that rational point. The inducing
function is left invariant under \(U(\mathbb A)M(F)\), and the height power
is left invariant under \(P(F)\). Thus the two quotient integrands agree.
For a source Levi point, the equality of the Weyl parameters also preserves
the target height factor in the normalized operator.

This is the right-Levi part of the representative invariance in Mœglin and
Waldspurger, *Spectral decomposition and Eisenstein series* (1995), II.1.6,
Proposition (i), printed pp. 87–88. The proof uses the Library's actual
rational-intersection quotient and equality of the integrands.
