# Bounds on the adelic Iwasawa evaluations of positive-weight characters

Opposite weights have the same multiplicities on the presentation torus by
the relative Weyl reflections. Restriction to the split component adds the
multiplicities in each fiber, and negation gives a bijection of opposite
fibers. Consequently the two opposite positive-weight sums are negatives
of one another.

For every cocharacter admissible on the full opposite radical,
`oppositeAdmissibleWeightCharacter` pulls the negative adjoint determinant
of the containing dynamic parabolic back to the inducing parabolic. The
action on the Lie algebra of the dynamic radical restricts through the
centralizer of the cocharacter. The determinant formula there and the
opposite sum identity identify its restriction to the split component
with the desired positive-weight character.

The same compact subgroup and parabolic component of a factorization can
be used in the larger parabolic by applying the factor morphism. Its
Iwasawa determinant is bounded above on all rational points by the
adelic orbit-height estimate. Naturality of the character logarithm gives
the corresponding lower bound for the pulled-back negative determinant.
These are internal consequences of the dynamic determinant and adelic
height formulas for the convergence chamber in Mœglin–Waldspurger II.1.5.
