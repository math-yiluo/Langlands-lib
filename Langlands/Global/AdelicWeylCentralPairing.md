# Central translates of the square-integrable Weyl twists

For the terms of Delorme's (4.3) attached to conjugate Levis, a scalar parameter is transported by the Weyl isomorphism. `WeylCentralTwistStatement` assumes an actual Weyl conjugation between the inducing and target Levis, a retraction of the target parabolic to its Levi, an Iwasawa compact for the target, and a target inducing vector with the normalized split-component equivariance. It proves that the height twist of that vector with the transported parameter has the original inducing central character on the ambient positive split centre.

The target lift of a positive central point maps through the Weyl parabolic map to a point whose ambient image is conjugate to the same central point. Conjugation fixes it, and injectivity of the source parabolic embedding identifies the mapped point with the source lift. The parameter-power pullback therefore preserves evaluation on the central lift. The nested half-modulus identity reduces the half-modulus there to that of the full group, which is one. Consequently the normalized inducing vector itself is fixed by the central point. The twist transforms by its height power, using the Iwasawa height multiplication law.
