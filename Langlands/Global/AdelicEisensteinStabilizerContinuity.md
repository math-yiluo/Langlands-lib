# Continuity of an unfolded Eisenstein term

Representative invariance makes the original kernel jointly continuous on the rational stabilizer quotient and the ambient adelic group. The polynomial-weighted integrable bound gives absolute convergence at every external argument. The height power is continuous and locally bounded, so dominated convergence gives continuity of the integral.

The result applies to the terms in the original Bruhat unfolding. It uses the bound on the original source series and the resulting majorant on the stabilizer quotient.
