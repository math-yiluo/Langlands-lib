# Character duality and finite-index parameter restriction

On an actual group scheme over a field, `realCharacterDualEquivParameterDual` identifies the existing real character dual `Hom(X*(G), ℝ)` with the algebraic real-linear dual of `ℝ ⊗[ℤ] X*(G)`. The forward map is the Library's real parameter pairing with arguments flipped. The inverse evaluates a real-linear functional at `1 ⊗ χ`. The two inverse identities are checked on pure tensors and on the original characters. In particular, if a parameter pairs to zero with all real character functionals, it is zero; this is algebraic separation and requires no selected topology, basis, finite generation, or torsion-freeness assumption.

For an actual morphism `i : H ⟶ G`, if the integral character restriction is injective and has finite index in `X*(H)`, `realCharacterParameterPullbackEquiv i` is the corresponding real-linear equivalence of parameter spaces. The construction realifies the Library's rationalized restriction equivalence and cancels the intermediate rational base change. Its forward map is proved to be the existing `realCharacterParameterPullback i`, not a chosen isomorphism between spaces of equal dimension. No claim about the integral restriction being surjective is made.

These are derived algebraic constructions in the real parameter and logarithmic-dual convention of Bernstein–Lapid (2024), §2.1, p. 190 (PDF p. 4). In the application to the split component, the invertible comparison is over the reals; it does not identify the two integral lattices. The module imposes no adelic measure normalization and proves no analytic continuation.
