# Isomorphisms of norm-one adelic quotients

Let the two group schemes be affine and locally of finite type over the same number field. A group-scheme isomorphism over that field and its inverse preserve the actual norm-one groups by functoriality of rational character norms. They take principal points to principal points and induce a measurable equivalence on the principal left quotients.

The pushforward of the chosen source Haar measure on the norm-one group is regular Haar measure. Haar uniqueness identifies it with a finite positive multiple of the chosen target Haar measure. Discrete fundamental-domain transport carries the same positive scalar to the Library's chosen measures on the principal left quotients.
