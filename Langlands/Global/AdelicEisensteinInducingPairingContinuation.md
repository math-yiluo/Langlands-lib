# Scalar continuation of the norm-one tests of the inducing expression

The target inducing expression in the functional-equation comparison is a finite combination of fixed inducing forms with meromorphic coefficients, twisted at a parameter that varies linearly in the chart. For a contained standard parabolic, the existence of each fixed form's nested cuspidal projection supplies absolute integrability of the norm-one tests of its constant term. The constant term and pairing then distribute over the finite untwisted combination.

The height factor of the twist pulls out at the external adelic argument. In the chart this factor is entire. The tested constant term is the product of this factor and a finite sum of the meromorphic coefficients multiplied by the scalar tests of the fixed forms. At a parabolic outside the inducing parabolic, the parabolic constant term and the test are both zero.

This is an internal scalar calculation for the common inducing term in Bernstein–Lapid §8.6. The pairing used is the norm-one pairing in §6.2, equation (6.8) and the next display.
