# Density for simultaneous smooth right translates

For a family of smooth functions and a continuous complex linear functional on the family, `SmoothFamily.orbitCoefficient` is the scalar coefficient of simultaneous right translation. Continuity at zero gives a basic product neighborhood on which the functional has absolute value less than one. Any vector vanishing on the finite set of coordinates of this neighborhood has all its scalar multiples in the neighborhood. The functional therefore vanishes on that vector. Truncation on this finite set expresses the coefficient as a finite sum of the Library's smooth scalar orbit coefficients.

Suppose each coordinate has finite joint orbit under the center and the compact enveloping algebra. Each summand in the scalar coefficient belongs to the submodule of smooth functions with finite joint orbit, so the sum does too. For a submodule containing all simultaneous compact translates of enveloping derivatives, every continuous linear functional vanishing on the submodule gives a coefficient whose enveloping derivatives all vanish at each compact element. Each connected component of the group contains a compact element. The Library's analyticity and jet uniqueness therefore make the coefficient vanish on the whole group. The complex Hahn–Banach separation lemma places every simultaneous right translate in the closure of the submodule.

This is an internal extension of `ArchimedeanConditionalOrbitDensity.lean` from one smooth function to families. It supplies the density step in the simultaneous convolution construction motivated by Borel–Jacquet, *Automorphic forms and automorphic representations* (1979), §§1.6 (2) and 2.1, printed pp. 191 and 193, PDF pp. 186 and 188.
