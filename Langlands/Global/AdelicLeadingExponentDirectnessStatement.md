# Directness and leading parts of the unnormalized exponent decomposition

For one parabolic in a compatible standard family, take a finite family
of functions on the adelic group. Each term has the polynomial orbit
condition for the unnormalized exponent that labels it. If the sum is
zero, every term is zero. This is the directness in Bernstein–Lapid
(6.10) expressed for the Library's orbit condition.

For two such sums with an error that has only nonleading exponents,
the sums of their leading terms agree. The leading condition uses the
actual ambient closed positive chamber. Taking the error to be zero
gives independence from the chosen decomposition. This comparison
uses the directness of (6.10) in the leading projection of §6.3 and
the functional equation argument of §8.6.
