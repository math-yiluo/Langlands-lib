# Field points of a split rank-one group act on the binary form by similitudes

Author: Worker 10.

Let `D : CellData ρ j i α` be the cell data of `SplitRankOneCellData.md`, and
let `L ⊇ F` be a field. This module shows that every `L`-point `g` of `G`
acts on the binary form `ν(X, Y) = ∑_{l ≤ k} X^(k-l) Y^l w_l` through a
`GL₂`-substitution and a scalar. The multiplier `c` satisfies
`c^d = η(g)^2`, where `η = D.eta` is the rational character in the
line-character relation.

## Provenance

This is internal infrastructure for the split rank-one height bound
(`SplitRankOneAdelic.md`). It replaces the `GL₂ / SL₂ / PGL₂`
classification in Springer, *Reduction theory over global fields* (1994),
§3.3. Two facts do the work: field points are generated by the open big cell
`xm · T · xp`, and each factor of the cell acts by an explicit substitution.

## Definitions

- `baseProj L G`, `cellBC L f` and `cellBCMap L f`: the projection from the
  base change `G_L` to `G`, and the base change over `Spec L` of a morphism
  `f : X ⟶ G.X`.
- `cellPointDescend L f x`: turns an `L`-rational point of the base-changed
  source into an `L`-coefficient point of `X`.
- `etaUnits eta A : G.affinePoints A →* Aˣ`: evaluation of a rational character
  of `G` on `A`-points, with values in units.
- `similitudeSubgroup L D`: the subgroup of `L`-points `g` for which some
  `c : L` satisfies both
  `IsBinarySimilitude D.k (wvec ρ j D.xm L) (ρ(g)) c` and
  `c ^ D.d = etaUnits D.eta L g ^ 2`.
  It is closed under products and inverses by `IsBinarySimilitude.mul` and
  `IsBinarySimilitude.inv`.

## Results

- `baseChangeCoeffEquiv_cellBCMap`: compatibility of `cellPointDescend` with the
  Library's `baseChangeCoeffEquiv`.
- **Two-cell decomposition** (`exists_points_mul_eq_of_openImmersion`): let `F`
  be infinite and `G` connected reductive. Let `f : X ⟶ G.X` be an open
  immersion with an `F`-rational point `x₀`. Then every `L`-point `g`
  satisfies `(p ≫ f) * g = q ≫ f` for some `L`-points `p`, `q` of `X`. The
  proof works on `G_L`:
  - `G_L` is irreducible, because `G` is geometrically integral
    (`smoothConnectedAffineGeometry`).
  - `G_L` has dense `L`-rational points, because `G(F)` is dense
    (`reductiveRationalPointDensity`) and stays dense after extension
    (`dense_iUnion_range_rationalExtend`).
  - The base-changed cell contains the extension of `x₀`.
  - The Library's `exists_cell_points_mul_eq_of_dense` then applies, and the
    result is transported back by `baseChangeCoeffEquiv`.
- `etaUnits_torus` (definitional): on torus points, `etaUnits eta` is the value
  of `torusCharacterRestrict i eta`.
- `etaUnits_root`: a rational character is trivial on the points of a root map.
  The reason is that a homomorphism from a unipotent vector group to `𝔾ₘ` is
  trivial.
- `CellData.lower_mem` and `CellData.raise_mem` (for `F`, `L` infinite): points
  of `xm` and of `xp` lie in `similitudeSubgroup L D`, with `c = 1`.
- `CellData.torus_mem`: torus points lie in it, with
  `c = lam(τ)^2 · (α(τ)⁻¹)^k`. The identity `c^d = η(τ)^2` follows from
  `D.rel` (`d • lam = η|_T + b • α`) and `D.dk` (`d k = 2 b`).
- `CellData.cell_mem`: points of the triple cell `xm × T × xp` lie in it.
- **Field points act by similitudes** (`CellData.exists_similitude`): for `F`
  infinite and `G` connected reductive, every `L`-point `g` admits `c : L`
  with `IsBinarySimilitude D.k (wvec ρ j D.xm L) (ρ(g)) c` and
  `c ^ D.d = η(g)^2`. This uses the two-cell decomposition, with `D.openCell`
  and the identity of the cell as rational point: `g = (p f)⁻¹ (q f)` lies in
  `similitudeSubgroup L D`.
