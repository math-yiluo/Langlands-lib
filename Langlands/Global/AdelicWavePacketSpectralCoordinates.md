# Spectral coordinates with a prescribed central frequency

The real character parameter in any coordinates for a standard parabolic decomposes into a relative block and a central-frequency block. The central block evaluates the restriction to the maximal split torus on the prescribed basis of the central cocharacters. `WavePacketSpectralCoordinatesStatement` gives a linear chart for this decomposition and a Haar measure on the first block whose product with ordinary central Lebesgue measure gives the original Lebesgue measure. `wavePacketCentralFrequency_eq_iff` identifies equality of central frequencies with a character difference in the real root span.

The split-torus perfect pairing extends a prescribed functional on the central directions to a character of the torus. Ambient group characters restricted to the torus complement the root span, so the same functional is realized by the restriction of an ambient group character. Such a character also restricts through every standard parabolic. A complement of the kernel then splits the surjective frequency map. Haar uniqueness identifies the pullback of ordinary Lebesgue measure as a positive multiple of the product Lebesgue measure. The multiple is absorbed into the relative block. The comparison with the root span follows from double annihilation for the split-torus pairing.

This gives the coordinate and measure decomposition used in the first paragraph of E.
