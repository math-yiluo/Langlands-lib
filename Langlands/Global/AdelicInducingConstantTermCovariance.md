# Constant terms of inducing functions

The inducing function may have left invariance only under the rational points of the parabolic. Its average along the unipotent radical still descends through the rational radical quotient. Joint continuity of the descended integrand and compactness of that quotient give continuity of the average.

Right translation of the integration variable preserves the mass-one quotient measure and gives left invariance under the full adelic radical. Conjugation by a rational parabolic point preserves the radical and transports its mass-one measure to itself. Applying the inducing function's rational parabolic invariance after this change of variables gives the corresponding invariance of the average.
