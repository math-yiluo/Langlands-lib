# Finite sums and Arthur truncation

`TruncationFiniteSumStatement` applies to standard Arthur truncation on the adelic points of a connected reductive group over a number field. It uses a maximal split torus, a generic cocharacter, relative root reflection data, and the Iwasawa compact and character-space isomorphism for the minimal parabolic. For any truncation parameter and any finite collection of continuous, left rational-invariant complex functions, truncating their pointwise sum gives the pointwise sum of their truncations. The functions need the continuity and invariance hypotheses on the indices in the finite set.

In `truncation_finsetSum`, each standard parabolic has a smooth connected unipotent radical. Its rational points act through the rational subgroup of the ambient group. Consequently `integrable_subgroupConstantTermIntegrand` gives an integrable function on the compact radical quotient for each member of the finite collection. The constant term commutes with the sum by `integral_finsetSum`. For each parabolic the cutoff at the given parameter and adelic argument has finite rational coset support by `truncationFiniteness`. The same finite set works for every function. After restricting the coset sums to this set, the proof exchanges the finite sums over functions and cosets, and then those over functions and parabolics. `normOneTruncation_finsetSum` uses `normOneTruncation_restrict` to obtain the identity on `G(𝔸)¹` for restrictions of the given functions.

This identity follows from Arthur's truncation formula and its finite coset support, J.
