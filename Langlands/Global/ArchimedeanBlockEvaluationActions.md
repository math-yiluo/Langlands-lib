# Compact translation and scalar coefficients of the standard block evaluation

The local half modulus is positive and unchanged by right multiplication in the compact block factors. Testing two tensors on all Levi points and using the normalized evaluation formula gives both compact translation laws. Evaluating with the identity Levi point gives separation of standard vectors by the compact evaluations.

`scalarLeviEvaluation` is the linear evaluation of a block tensor at a fixed Levi point. For a fixed compact-finite standard vector, all its compact block tensor evaluations lie in the image of its finite-dimensional compact orbit span under evaluation at the identity. The maps `scalarLeviEvaluation` separate this image. A scalar linear functional on the tensor therefore agrees there with a finite linear combination of Levi evaluations. The normalized formula identifies each summand with the original standard function left translated by a fixed Levi point, with the reciprocal half modulus absorbed in its scalar coefficient. These formulas supply smooth matrix extensions of scalar compact evaluations.

This is an internal use of finite-dimensional coefficient separation and the normalized induction-in-stages calculation. Getz and Hahn (2024), §10.1, pp. 270–271 (PDF 282–283), describe the scalar compact model.
