# Smooth scalar sections from compact tensor evaluations

Suppose the scalar paired evaluations of each pure tensor admit a smooth ambient extension near every infinite compact matrix, with the finite compact and Levi arguments fixed. `hasSmoothCompactSlices_compactTensorSectionMap` proves the corresponding local smooth extension property for every tensor section. The exact normalized slice formula gives the property on pure tensors. On a sum, add the two ambient extensions and restrict to the intersection of their neighborhoods.

`smoothCompactTensorSectionIntertwiner` uses this property to restrict the compact intertwiner to the smooth compact-finite scalar carrier. The inputs include compact right covariance of the infinite factor, compact finiteness of its source vectors, and finite smoothness of the scalar image. The established smoothness criterion on normalized compact slices then gives smoothness of every full archimedean slice with fixed finite argument.

This is an internal consequence of the realized scalar compact picture. Getz and Hahn (2024), §10.1, pp. 270–271 (PDF 282–283), describe the scalar Levi slices. Langlands (1979), proof of Proposition 2, pp. 204–205 (PDF 199–200), uses smooth compact-finite scalar sections with cuspidal Levi slices.
