# Polynomial eigen-coordinates on standard unipotent radicals

`StandardUnipotentEigenCoordinatesStatement` fixes a locally finite type affine connected reductive group over a field, a maximal split torus, and a generic cocharacter specifying positive roots. For a subset J of the simple roots, let a closed immersion of an affine group represent the corresponding standard unipotent radical. The coordinate algebra of this radical has polynomial generators that vanish at the identity and transform under conjugation by the torus through individual characters. Each character is a positive relative root outside the additive monoid generated by J. The eigenfunction identity holds on points over every coefficient algebra in the given universe.

The standard cocharacter for J realizes the radical as a dynamic unipotent subgroup. Its Lie weight support is the set of roots positive on that cocharacter. The positive weight coordinate theorem supplies polynomial eigenfunctions in the augmentation ideal. The multiplicity comparison shows that the character of each generator is a Lie weight. Sign comparison for a standard cocharacter identifies these weights as the positive roots outside the additive monoid generated by J. The coordinate algebra isomorphism with global sections gives the stated identities on actual points.

This extends the Library's polynomial eigen-coordinates for the minimal radical to all standard radicals. In the maximal-parabolic case it identifies the weights used in the archimedean contraction argument of Mœglin–Waldspurger, §I.2.10, p. 31.
