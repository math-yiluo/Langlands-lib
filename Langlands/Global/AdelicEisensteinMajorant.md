# Absolute convergence from polynomially bounded rational representatives

`HasPolynomialRationalCosetRepresentatives` is the polynomial height
comparison used for choosing a rational representative in each coset.
For the Chevalley projective coset embedding, suppose its height bounds
the normalized matrix height of such representatives by a positive
constant times a nonnegative power of the projective height.

The arithmetic summability bound supplies an exponent for the inverse
power of projective height. Add the representative height exponent times
the fixed nonnegative growth exponent of the function. Regular parameter
decay supplies that larger inverse power at each rational representative.
The two compact translation estimates bound the growth and the height
power uniformly on compact sets, and representative independence passes
these estimates to the terms defined using the chosen quotient points.

Multiplying the two bounds cancels the added growth exponent. The
resulting majorant is a constant multiple of the arithmetic inverse
power pulled back by the injective projective embedding, so it is
summable. This implements the last step of the direct height proof of
Mœglin–Waldspurger II.1.5, given the polynomial representative comparison.
