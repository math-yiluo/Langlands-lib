# Unimodularity from a compact quotient by a discrete subgroup

`CompactDiscreteQuotientHaarRightInvariantStatement` asserts that a locally
compact Polish topological group, with its Borel measurable structure,
has right-invariant Haar measure whenever its actual left homogeneous
quotient by a discrete subgroup is compact. The group and subgroup are
arbitrary; there is no algebraic-group, number-field, normality or
commutativity assumption.

This is an internal analytic prerequisite for the actual unipotent
radical quotients. The existing compactness and discreteness results
supply its hypotheses there. Its conclusion would remove the analytic
right-Haar-invariance premise from their normalized quotient Weil
formula and subgroup constant-term invariance. It is not a proof of
general reductive or norm-one adelic unimodularity by itself.

The intended route uses the existing strict Borel fundamental domain and
its discrete Haar quotient. Local finiteness and compactness of that
quotient give the domain finite nonzero Haar mass. Its right preimage is
again a strict fundamental domain, so domain independence gives the same
mass. The existing Haar modular scaling identity would force every
modular factor to be one. This paragraph describes a proof plan; the
module only states the Prop-valued target and claims no proof.
