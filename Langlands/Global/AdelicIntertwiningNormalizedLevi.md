# Normalized left Levi translation of the adelic intertwining integral

`IsIwasawaCompact.intertwiningOperator_normalized_levi` derives Bernstein–Lapid's formula (6.5) for the actual adelic integral from an identity of Haar factors. Conjugation by a target Levi point preserves the closed intertwining stabilizer. Changing variables on its quotient moves that point into the integrand and introduces the forward quotient Haar factor. The target parameter power at that Levi point cancels the source parameter power through Weyl transport. The assumed Haar identity then converts the target inverse half modulus into the source inverse half modulus.

The formula uses the rational-quotient integral throughout its statement. The published closed-quotient change of variables justifies the proof for any continuous source function invariant by the source unipotent radical and for the total Bochner integral. The source is Bernstein and Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series*, registered as `bernstein-lapid-2024-meromorphic-continuation-eisenstein`, journal PDF SHA-256 `6acbe78adf3a7ddff4be0caa92b169f0e609d1245f674a71fbc6989d6aef9268`, p. 201, formula (6.5).

`intertwiningOperator_splitComponentEquivariant` uses the same formula at a positive central point of the target Levi. The canonical subgroup is the positive image of a split component. An isomorphism carries a split component to a split component, so the source split-component equivariance cancels the source inverse half modulus in the integral. Moving the target inverse half modulus across the equality gives the claimed target equivariance.
