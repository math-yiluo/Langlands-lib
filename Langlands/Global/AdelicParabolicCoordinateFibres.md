# Fibres of the relative parabolic quotient coordinates

Let \(P=MN\) be a parabolic in \(G\), with Levi \(M\) and unipotent radical \(N\). Consider radical-first coordinates \((n,b,m,k)\) for \(P(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A)^1\). Here \(b\) belongs to the relative positive split component, \(m\) belongs to \(M(\mathbb A)^1\), and \(k\) belongs to the compact subgroup \(K\) of \(G(\mathbb A)\). Assume that the subgroup \(K_M\) induced on \(M(\mathbb A)\) is maximal compact. The coordinate fibres preserve \(b\) and preserve the intrinsic class of \(m\) up to right multiplication by \(K_M\).

The Levi retraction maps the compact subgroup induced on \(P(\mathbb A)\) to a compact subgroup of \(M(\mathbb A)\). Its image contains \(K_M\) because the retraction fixes Levi points. Maximality gives equality. If two coordinates have the same rational parabolic quotient class, a principal parabolic point translates one product to the other. Cancelling their parabolic factors gives a point of \(P(\mathbb A)\) in the inverse image of \(K\). The retraction sends this point into \(K_M\) and sends the principal parabolic multiplier to a principal Levi point. The split Levi decomposition then gives equality of the split coordinates and identifies one intrinsic Levi representative with a compact right translate of the other, up to left multiplication by a rational Levi point.

`relativeParabolicCoordinateSaturation` applies this fibre relation to a subset specified by a split coordinate set and a right-\(K_M\)-invariant subset of the intrinsic Levi rational quotient. The preimage of its parabolic quotient image is the original subset. This is the fibre argument used to pass from the product description of the truncation shell to its restriction in relative Iwasawa coordinates in Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*, Advances in Mathematics 426 (2023), 109107, (7.7) and (7.23).
