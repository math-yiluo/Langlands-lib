# The finite parameter action on realized block sections

Transport the height-conjugated right action to the matrix realization. The factor multiplying an ordinary finite translate is left parabolic invariant. It therefore scales a normalized Levi slice by a single complex number and preserves the realized coefficient subspace. Integral finite translations agree with the original action, so the smooth vectors agree by intersecting every open stabilizer with the integral level. The deformed finite action commutes with the infinite compact action, which preserves compact finiteness. The action specializes at zero to the original finite action. On every infinite slice it translates the finite argument and multiplies by a scalar constant over the infinite argument. The finite range of the height displacement gives a finite holomorphic expansion on the compact-finite carrier.

This derived construction implements the finite part of the fixed-carrier holomorphic section family used in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), proof of Proposition 2, printed pp. 204–206, Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 199–201. It uses the actual adelic parabolic height and realized Levi coefficients.
