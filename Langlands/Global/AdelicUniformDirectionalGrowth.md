# Uniform growth for derivative words of Fréchet-valued families

`AdelicUniformDirectionalGrowthStatement` uses an affine group scheme over a number field, a chosen smooth relative dimension, and the scalar-commutator hypothesis of the actual adelic enveloping action. Fix a positive-rank closed matrix representation, a level subgroup \(L\le G(\mathbb A_f)\), and a real exponent \(R\ge 0\).

For a continuous complex-linear map from a normed space into \(\mathfrak F_L^R\), every word \(D\) in real right-orbit directions satisfies

\[
  |D(Sq)(x)|\le C_D H(x)^R\|q\|
\]

on the actual adelic group. Here \(H\) is the submultiplicative representation height and \(C_D\) depends on the map and the word. The exponent \(R\) is common to all words.

The proof identifies a word of real right-orbit derivatives with the ordered product of the corresponding generators in the actual complex enveloping algebra. Its weighted derivative is a continuous linear map from the Fréchet space into the bounded functions. Composing with \(S\) gives an operator norm, and evaluation in the supremum norm controls each point. The submultiplicative height bounds the source norm used in the Fréchet seminorms.

This expresses the derivative bounds of Bernstein–Lapid, §7.1, pp. 210–211, in the form used by the relative cuspidal decay estimate in §7.4, p. 217.
