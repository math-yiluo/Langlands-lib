# Compactness and stabilization for proper algebraic spaces

`Langlands.Global.AdelicSpaceProperStabilizationStatement` extends the two
consequences of Proposition 4.4 as described in the paragraph preceding
Proposition 5.8 on printed p. 17. It uses the adelic topology of that
paragraph, given by a separated finite-type model \(X_S\) with a specified
generic-fibre isomorphism to \(Y\). The structural morphism from \(Y\) to
the spectrum of the global field is proper.

The statement gives compactness of the actual adelic point space of \(Y\).
It also gives a finite level \(T\) containing \(S\) such that for every
larger level \(U\), the map from \(X_S(A_{F,U})\) to \(Y(A_F)\) is a
homeomorphism. The map is change of coefficients to the full adeles
followed by the generic-fibre point identification. Number-field levels
retain all archimedean factors. Function fields have finite constants and
start at a nonempty finite set of places.

The source is Brian Conrad, *Weil and Grothendieck approaches to adelic
points*, notes dated December 31, 2011, Proposition 4.4 on printed p. 11
and the paragraph before Proposition 5.8 on printed p. 17. The retained
source `conrad-2011-weil-grothendieck-adelic-points` has SHA-256
