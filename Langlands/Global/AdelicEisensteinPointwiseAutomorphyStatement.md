# Automorphy of pointwise Eisenstein continuations

`Langlands.Global.EisensteinPointwiseContinuationAutomorphyReductionStatement`
takes the published `EisensteinMeromorphicContinuationStatement` as a
premise. Its group, parabolic and automorphic data are the source-side
data in `EisensteinFunctionalEquationStatement`. This includes closed
matrix embeddings of any size.

Suppose every evaluation of `EP` is meromorphic in linear coordinates
on one common holomorphy domain `DP`, and `EP` agrees with the actual
`P`-series on a sufficiently regular region. In each linear chart `e`
the conclusion supplies an open dense subset of parameter coordinates
contained in `e ⁻¹' DP`. At each point of that subset the value of
`EP` is an automorphic form on `G(F) \ G(𝔸)` for the specified compact
and matrix embedding.

This is a derived consequence of the local function-valued formulation
of Bernstein–Lapid Theorem 2.3 (1) and the corollary in §8.6.
The common dense open set comes from the local function-valued
presentation domains, and pointwise identity extends agreement
from the initial regular region. The statement supplies the
automorphic values needed for the leading cuspidal component
comparison in the proof of Theorem 2.3 (4).
