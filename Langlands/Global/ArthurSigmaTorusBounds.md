# Torus bounds for Arthur's truncated sigma region

The torus region is the lower Siegel chamber with its weight truncation,
intersected with the support of the relative sigma function and the ambient
norm-one group. This is the torus region relevant to Arthur 2005, §13,
(13.7), printed pp. 71–72.

The abstract sigma root bounds apply to the projection of the height to
the coroot span. Actual roots and weights have the same values under this
projection. The Iwasawa height evaluates on a positive torus point as the
logarithm of the idelic character norm. After allowing for both truncation
parameters, the roots in the upper index have a common lower bound. The
absolute values of all simple-root logarithms are bounded by an affine
function of any common nonnegative upper bound on the active logarithms.

On the ambient norm-one locus every ambient rational character has norm
one. The simple roots together with the restrictions of ambient rational
characters rationally span the torus character space. The exponential
height bound for a positive torus point in spanning character coordinates
therefore bounds its matrix height by a constant times an exponential in
the supplied active-root upper bound.
