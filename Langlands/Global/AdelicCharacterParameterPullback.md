# Pullback of actual character parameters

Let `p : P ⟶ G` be a morphism of group schemes over a field. The constructions `realCharacterParameterPullback` and `parameterRestrict` take parameters of `G` to parameters of `P`. They are scalar extensions of the Library's actual rational-character restriction `χ ↦ χ ∘ p`: on a pure tensor `s ⊗ χ` both maps give `s ⊗ (χ ∘ p)`. No Levi, split torus, affine hypothesis, or choice of coordinates is needed. Both maps preserve identities and reverse compositions.

The pairings of the pulled-back parameters with a real character functional `H` of `P` equal the original pairings with `realCharacterDualMap F P G p H`, the functional on `G` obtained by precomposing with character restriction. The proof reduces to pure tensors and uses additivity, in both the real and the complex case. The real-part map commutes with pullback by the same argument. Over a number field, actual normalized adelic logarithms commute with the point map; consequently `parameterPower_pullback` identifies the value of the pulled-back parameter at `x` with the value of the original parameter at `p(x)`.

This is a derived functorial construction in the number-field parameter convention of Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series* (2024), §2.1, p. 190 (PDF p. 4): `𝔞* = X*(G) ⊗ ℝ`, `X = X*(G) ⊗ ℂ`, with `m^λ` obtained from the normalized character logarithm. The paper supplies the parameter and evaluation conventions; these laws are the tensor-functorial consequences of actual character restriction. This module does not assert convergence or continuation of an Eisenstein series.
