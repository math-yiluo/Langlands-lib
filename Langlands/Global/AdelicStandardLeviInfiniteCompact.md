# The maximal infinite compact on a standard Levi

A maximal compact intersection of an ambient adelic compact with a standard Levi induces a maximal infinite compact intersection in the sense used in the Levi cusp test. The infinite point inclusion commutes with a group-scheme point map because the infinite and finite projections are natural, and the finite factor of the infinite inclusion is the identity.

This identifies the infinite compact of the Levi cusp test with the infinite factor of the supplied adelic intersection. The published product decomposition of a maximal adelic compact then gives the result.
