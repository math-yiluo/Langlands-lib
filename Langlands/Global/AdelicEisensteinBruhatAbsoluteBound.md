# Polynomial majorants for the unfolded Bruhat terms

The rational stabilizer integrand descends continuously to the actual quotient and depends continuously on its external adelic argument. Applying the constant-term unfolding to the real norm of the original twisted function controls the sum of the absolute radical integrals in each Bruhat piece. The normalized target radical measure has mass one.

Compact representatives of the target rational radical quotient give a uniform polynomial weight in the external argument. Rational radical translation permutes the original Eisenstein cosets. The proof uses the summable global majorant of the original Eisenstein terms and retains its polynomial degree. This bound applies at the original source parameter, including pieces whose restricted parameter is on a wall for a smaller source parabolic.
