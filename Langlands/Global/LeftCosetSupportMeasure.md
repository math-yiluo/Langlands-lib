# Domination of a localized pushforward on a discrete left quotient

Suppose a countable subgroup \(\Gamma\) acts on a group \(H\) by left
multiplication, preserving the ambient measure. Equip the left quotient with
the pushforward of ambient measure restricted to a fundamental domain. For a
measurable subset \(A\) of \(H\), let \(B\) be a measurable set of cosets
containing its image. If \(D\) contains all products \(ab^{-1}\) with
\(a,b\in A\), then the pushforward of ambient measure restricted to \(A\) is
bounded by the quotient measure restricted to \(B\) multiplied by the extended
cardinality of \(\Gamma\cap D\).

For any orbit meeting \(A\), choose one point \(\gamma_0 x\) in the
intersection. Sending a representative \(\gamma x\) in \(A\) to
\(\gamma\gamma_0^{-1}\) injects the orbit intersection into \(\Gamma\cap D\).
Unfolding the indicator of \(A\) intersected with a preimage of a measurable
set of cosets reduces the measure inequality to this count on each orbit.

This is the localized measure form of the counting argument used in
Mœglin–Waldspurger, *Spectral Decomposition and Eisenstein Series* (1995),
I.2.4(a)(i), pp. 25–26. It allows the same estimate to be used with a weighted
quotient measure on a larger domain. The statement uses the existing
fundamental-domain quotient measure and assumes the subgroup action and
fundamental domain are supplied.
