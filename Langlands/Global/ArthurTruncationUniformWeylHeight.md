# Uniform Weyl height comparison for a compact Iwasawa subgroup

`exists_weylHeight_lowerBound` compares a full fundamental weight at `H₀(x)` with its Weyl translate at `H₀(w v x)`, for a fixed rational lift `w` and every point `v` of the minimal adelic radical. The additive error is uniform in `v` and `x` and may depend on the chosen compact Iwasawa subgroup.

The proof chooses an actual maximal-parabolic eigenline and applies the transported line-character bound. The original and transported line characters each equal the corresponding full fundamental weight times the same positive constant, plus a fixed ambient character restriction. The ambient character has equal evaluations at the two arguments. Subtract it and divide the remaining error by the positive constant. This derived uniform form is used in the support estimate corresponding to Arthur (1980), Lemma 1.1, p. 91.
