# Deviation from a unipotent constant term

`AdelicUnipotentConstantTermOscillationStatement` considers a smooth connected affine unipotent group U embedded as a closed subgroup in an affine, locally finite type group G over a number field. Fix smooth relative dimensions for their archimedean manifolds, a compact set of finite coordinates in G, and an open right finite level L. The statement chooses compact archimedean unipotent representatives and a jointly smooth family of paths from the identity to the representatives.

For a continuous adelic function with smooth archimedean slices, left invariance under U(F) and right invariance under L, suppose the archimedean derivatives of its values along those translated paths are bounded in norm by B on the unit interval at the given evaluation point. The norm of the function at that point minus its normalized unipotent constant term is at most B. The right-orbit direction of each derivative is the path velocity translated to the identity, mapped by the differential of U → G, and then conjugated by the inverse of the evaluation point in the archimedean group.

The proof applies the manifold chain rule and the real interval mean-value bound. The rational and finite-level invariances realize the integrand at every point of the unipotent quotient by evaluation at one of the archimedean endpoints. The integrand descends to a continuous function on the compact quotient. Integration of the pointwise difference against the normalized probability measure gives the conclusion.

This estimate is a step toward the decay argument of Mœglin–Waldspurger, §I.2.10, pp. 30–33. The estimate uses a path derivative bound as its input. For a vanishing constant term, it directly bounds the value of the function.
