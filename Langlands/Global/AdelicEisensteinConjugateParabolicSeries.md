# Rational conjugation of the inducing parabolic

Conjugate the embedding of \(P\) by a rational point \(s\) and replace the
inducing function \(\phi(g)\) by \(\phi(s^{-1}g)\). The original compact is
Iwasawa for the conjugated parabolic. Its Eisenstein series on the translated
function is the original series times the new height power at \(s\).

The parabolic quotient subgroup is carried to the new subgroup by
conjugation, so the translated function has the required left invariance.
Left multiplication by \(s\) gives a bijection between the original and
new rational cosets. The transported terms can be evaluated at the
corresponding representatives \(\gamma\) and \(s\gamma\), since the twists
are invariant under their rational parabolics. The same-compact height
identity gives the factor at \(s\) in each corresponding term. The proof
reindexes the unconditional sum and extracts this common scalar.

This is a derived comparison for the actual series of Bernstein and Lapid,
*On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series* (2024), §2.2,
journal p. 191, equation (2.1). In the functional equation its height
scalar cancels the scalar from conjugating the intertwining target.
