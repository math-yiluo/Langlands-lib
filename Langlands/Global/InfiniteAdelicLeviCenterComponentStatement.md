# The Levi component of a central element of the archimedean enveloping algebra: statement

Module `Langlands.Global.InfiniteAdelicLeviCenterComponentStatement`, namespace
`Langlands.Global`. It contains one `Prop`-valued definition, `LeviCenterComponentStatement`, and
proves nothing.

## Statement

The data are a number field `F : Type u`, group
schemes `G`, `Q`, `M'`, `V` over `Spec F` with affine total spaces for `G`, `Q` and `M'`,
morphisms `q : Q ⟶ G`, `iV : V ⟶ Q`, `l' : M' ⟶ Q` with `G` connected reductive, `q` parabolic,
`iV` a geometric unipotent radical and `l'` a rational Levi subgroup, and smooth structures
`hsmooth` on `G` (dimension `d`) and `hsmooth'` on `M'` (dimension `d'`). The statement writes

- `S_V ⊆ G(F_∞)` for the image of `V(F_∞)` under the point map of `iV ≫ q`;
- `U(𝔤) = AdelicEnvelopingAlgebra F G hsmooth` and `U(𝔪') = AdelicEnvelopingAlgebra F M' hsmooth'`
  for the complex enveloping algebras of the real Lie algebras of `G(F_∞)` and `M'(F_∞)`, and
  `Z(𝔤) = AdelicEnvelopingCenter F G hsmooth` for the center of `U(𝔤)`;
- `j = pointEnvelopingMap F hsmooth' hsmooth (l' ≫ q) : U(𝔪') → U(𝔤)` for the algebra map
  induced by the tangent map at `1` of the point map of `l' ≫ q`;
- `I_V = curveRightIdeal F hsmooth S_V` for the complex span of the products `ι(1 ⊗ X) · b` with
  `b ∈ U(𝔤)` and `X` the velocity at `0` of a smooth curve through `1` in `S_V`.

The conclusion is the conjunction of

1. for every `z ∈ Z(𝔤)` there is `y ∈ U(𝔪')` with `z - j y ∈ I_V`;
2. for every `y ∈ U(𝔪')`, `j y ∈ I_V` implies `y = 0`.

By the second clause the element `y` of the first is unique, and `j` is injective (`j y = 0`
lies in `I_V`).

## Sources and provenance

The statement is derived. For a complex reductive Lie algebra `𝔤` with a parabolic subalgebra
`𝔮 = 𝔩 ⊕ 𝔲`, A. W. Knapp and D. A. Vogan, *Cohomological Induction and Unitary Representations*
(1995) (`knapp-vogan-1995-cohomological-induction`), Lemma 4.123, printed p. 301 (PDF
p. 323), shows in (a) that `U(𝔤) = U(𝔩) ⊕ (𝔲⁻ U(𝔤) + U(𝔤) 𝔲)`, with `𝔲⁻` the opposite
nilradical, in (b) that the second component of a central `z` lies in `U(𝔤) 𝔲`, and in (c) that
its `U(𝔩)`-component is central in `U(𝔩)`. The statement is
the mirror form with the right ideal `𝔳 · U(𝔤)` generated by the Lie algebra of the unipotent
radical, which follows by applying the principal antiautomorphism. Knapp–Vogan Lemma 4.123 (a)
and (b) is the direct source of the congruence and of the uniqueness. Mœglin and Waldspurger,
*Spectral Decomposition and Eisenstein Series* (1995)
(`moeglin-waldspurger-1995-spectral-decomposition`), I.2.17, printed pp. 37–38 (PDF pp. 20–21),
use the normalized Harish-Chandra homomorphism in their comparison of the actions of the centers.
It is related to the unshifted Levi component of this statement by the shift by the half-modulus
character and by the conversion between right and left differentiation.
The formulation for the archimedean Lie group `G(F_∞)` of a group scheme, with the Lie algebra of
`V(F_∞)` given by the velocities of smooth curves in its image, and the uniqueness clause in the
form "`j y ∈ I_V` implies `y = 0`" are internal.
