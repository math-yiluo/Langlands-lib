# Proof of the canonical adelic Weil comparison

The exact target of `adelicProjectiveWeilComparison` is the proposition
`Langlands.Global.AdelicProjectiveWeilComparisonStatement`, the conjunction
of its function-field and number-field components in the same arbitrary
universe. Its source is Brian Conrad, *Weil and Grothendieck approaches
to adelic points* (December 31, 2011), Example 4.2, printed pp.9–10,
registered as `conrad-2011-weil-grothendieck-adelic-points`, SHA-256
`fe4a9193aff4ef575665b40971f3da32f850a74d87b3f2d0fa28bba26b9cbabb`.
This proof reuses the actual arithmetic model constructions and original
field comparison maps already in the Library.

`numberFieldAdelicProjectiveWeilComparison` introduces the number fields,
the quasi-compact upper scheme with its specified projective immersion,
and the two independent arithmetic spreads. It constructs
`numberFieldExtensionProjectiveModel` with empty initial lower and upper
supports. Those initial supports are internal choices; the constructor
enlarges the exceptional set to contain the needed coefficients. It then
uses `NumberFieldProjectiveWeilAdeleHomeomorph.adelicPointHomeomorphOfSpreads`
for the supplied independent spreads. The published identity
`NumberFieldProjectiveWeilAdeleEvaluation.adelicPointHomeomorphOfSpreads_eq_evaluation`
identifies its underlying function with the actual original field counit
evaluated along the literal full adele inclusion. Function extensionality
turns this pointwise identity into equality of functions. Rewriting the
goal with that equality reduces it to `Homeomorph.isHomeomorph`.

`curvePlaceAdelicProjectiveWeilComparison` follows the same route for
function fields over finite constants. First it derives the upper
RatFunc-algebra action, scalar towers and function-field presentation by
composition and transitivity, exactly as in the target. It chooses the
image of `RatFunc.X` in the lower field as parameter. The theorem
`FunctionField.FiniteDimensional.adjoin_algebraMap_X` supplies the required
finite-dimensionality over the subfield generated by that parameter.
The constructor `curvePlaceFunctionFieldProjectiveModel` then constructs
the geometric arithmetic model with empty initial supports, internally
enlarging to a principal exceptional set. The function-field independent
spread homeomorphism and its canonical counit identity finish the argument
by the same function equality and rewriting step.

The conjunction theorem combines these two exact components. Neither proof
assumes an arithmetic model, separability of the function-field extension,
separation of the supplied spreads, or a topological conclusion about the
comparison. The endpoint topologies remain their respective independently
defined spread topologies. All infinite number-field factors are retained.
The original scheme and its projective immersion remain fixed throughout.
The source's algebraic-space and tensor-ring assertions are separate targets.
