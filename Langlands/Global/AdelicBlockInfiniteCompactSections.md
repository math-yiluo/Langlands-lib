# Polynomial compact sections for the actual infinite block parabolic

The infinite compact parabolic intersection is identified with the closed parabolic subgroup inside the standard infinite compact. Its Levi projection is continuous and takes values in the product of the standard infinite compact block groups. The inclusion into the units of the real matrix algebra is continuous and injective.

Given a nonzero weakly continuous locally finite coefficient action, one nonzero orbit span gives a finite-dimensional representation with continuous coordinates. The polynomial averaging construction yields a polynomial in those coordinates that is nonzero at the identity and covariant under the actual compact parabolic. The coordinate embedding places its values in the original coefficient action. This supplies smooth compact sections without a classification of the inducing representations.

This derived argument constructs the nonzero archimedean compact-picture vectors used for Langlands’ Lemma 1 in *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), printed p. 203, Corvallis Part I PDF p. 198.
