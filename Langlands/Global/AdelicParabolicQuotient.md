# The parabolic quotient `X_P = U(𝔸_F) M(F) \ G(𝔸_F)`

Author: Worker 3659.

Mœglin–Waldspurger (1995), I.2.17, printed p. 37, consider functions
`φ : U(𝔸) M(k) \ G → ℂ` for a standard parabolic `P = M U` and define automorphic forms on that
space. Constant terms along `P` are functions there, and so are the inducing functions of
Eisenstein series. The Eisenstein series themselves are not: Mœglin–Waldspurger II.1.5, printed
p. 85, take `φ ∈ A(U(𝔸) M(k) \ G)` and define `E(φ, π)` on `G(k) \ G` by summing over
`P(k) \ G(k)`. In general it is not left `U(𝔸)`-invariant. This module constructs the quotient
for actual adelic points of group schemes over a number field. It contains no automorphic forms,
growth conditions or measures. Those are built on top of it.

## Data

- `F : Type u` is a number field.
- `G`, `P`, `U` and `M` are group schemes over `Spec F`.
- `p : P ⟶ G`, `iU : U ⟶ P` and `l : M ⟶ P` are homomorphisms. In the source, `P` is a
  standard parabolic of a connected reductive `G`, `iU` its unipotent radical and `l` its standard
  Levi subgroup. The general constructions need none of these properties. The general
  description of the subgroup as a set of products uses one explicit normalization hypothesis.
  The last section proves that hypothesis for the published standard representatives, which are
  defined as intersection functors and are not asserted to be geometric parabolics.

## The subgroup and the quotient

All in namespace `Langlands.Global.AdelicGroupPoints`.

- `parabolicUnipotentImage F p iU` is the image of `U(𝔸_F)` in `G(𝔸_F)` under the published
  continuous homomorphism `map F U G (iU ≫ p)`.
- `parabolicLeviRationalImage F p l` is the image of the principal rational points `M(F)` in
  `G(𝔸_F)`: the published `rational F M` followed by `map F M G (l ≫ p)`.
- `parabolicQuotientSubgroup F p iU l` is their join, the subgroup generated by the two images.
- `ParabolicQuotient F p iU l` is the published left coset space `LeftCosetSpace` of `G(𝔸_F)` by
  this subgroup. Two points are identified when they differ by left multiplication by an element
  of the subgroup. It carries the quotient topology and the right translation action
  `[x] ↦ [x g]` of the published `LeftCosetSpace` API. That API also provides the functions on
  the quotient (`LeftCosetSpace.lift`, `functionEquiv`) and the right regular action on them.

## Results

- `mem_sup_iff_exists_mul_of_le_normalizer` (namespace `Langlands.Global`, any group): if `Γ`
  normalizes `N`, then `x ∈ N ⊔ Γ` exactly when `x = n γ` with `n ∈ N` and `γ ∈ Γ`.
- `ParabolicQuotient.mk` is the projection `G(𝔸_F) → X_P`, with `mk_surjective` and
  `continuous_mk`.
- `ParabolicQuotient.mk_unipotent_mul` and `ParabolicQuotient.mk_leviRational_mul`: left
  multiplication by a point of `U(𝔸_F)` or of `M(F)` does not change the class.
- `ParabolicQuotient.rightTranslate_mk`: right translation by `g` sends `[x]` to `[x g]`.
- `mem_parabolicQuotientSubgroup_iff`: assume the hypothesis
  `hN : parabolicLeviRationalImage F p l ≤ Subgroup.normalizer (parabolicUnipotentImage F p iU)`,
  that is, `M(F)` normalizes `U(𝔸_F)`. Then the subgroup is exactly the set of products
  `ι_U(n) ι_M(γ)` with `n ∈ U(𝔸_F)` and `γ ∈ M(F)`, so it is `U(𝔸_F) M(F)` as a set.
- `parabolicLeviRationalImage_le_normalizer`: the hypothesis `hN` follows when the image of all
  of `P(𝔸_F)` normalizes the image of `U(𝔸_F)`.
- `ParabolicQuotient.mk_eq_mk_iff`: under `hN`, `[x] = [y]` exactly when
  `y = ι_U(n) ι_M(γ) x` for some `n ∈ U(𝔸_F)` and `γ ∈ M(F)`.

## The published standard representatives

Fix `G` with `IsAffineHom G.X.hom`, a commutative `T` with `i : T ⟶ G`, a cocharacter `ν` and an
index set `J`, with no further hypothesis. The published `Standard.parabolicScheme i ν J`,
`Standard.unipotentScheme i ν J` and `Standard.leviScheme i ν J` (module
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.StandardRepresentability`) are closed representatives of the
*standard intersection functors* `standardParabolic i ν J`, `standardUnipotent i ν J` and
`standardLevi i ν J`. These functors are intersections, over the cocharacters `ζ` with
`IsStandardCocharacter i ν J ζ`, of the dynamic parabolic, unipotent and centralizer subgroups of
`ζ`. Their inclusions are `Standard.parabolicHom`, `Standard.unipotentToParabolic` and
`Standard.leviToParabolic`.

As `StandardRepresentability.md` records, the names identify these functors and not their
geometric structure. If the indexing family is empty, all three functors are `G` itself. This
happens, for instance, when `J` is not a set of simple relative roots. In that case the
unipotent image below is all of `G(𝔸_F)` and `X_P` is a point. Identifying the representatives
with an actual parabolic, its unipotent radical and a Levi subgroup, as in Mœglin–Waldspurger
I.1.4 and I.2.17, needs separate hypotheses and comparison theorems. Examples are an actual
Siegel setup (`IsSiegelAlgebraicSetup`) for `J = ∅`, or the standard-parabolic structure
statements of the algebraic-groups program. This module does not supply them.

For these data, with no further hypothesis:

- `standardParabolic_range_eq` and `standardUnipotentImage_eq`: the adelic images of the
  parabolic and unipotent representatives are the functors `standardParabolic i ν J` and
  `standardUnipotent i ν J` evaluated on the adeles.
- `standardParabolic_range_le_normalizer`: the adelic points of the parabolic representative
  normalize those of the unipotent representative, for every `J`. This generalizes the
  published case `J = ∅`, `AdelicSiegelFactors.standardParabolicImage_le_normalizer`.
- `mem_standardParabolicQuotientSubgroup_iff`: consequently the subgroup
  `parabolicQuotientSubgroup` built from the three representatives is exactly the set of
  products `ι_U(n) ι_M(γ)`, with `n` an adelic point of the unipotent representative and `γ` a
  rational point of the Levi representative.

Wherever the representatives are known to be an actual parabolic `P = M U` with its unipotent
radical and Levi subgroup, these results give the product description of `U(𝔸_F) M(F)` for
the source quotient. That identification is a separate obligation.

## Proofs

**`mem_sup_iff_exists_mul_of_le_normalizer`.** The set of products `n γ` is a subgroup:
`n₁ γ₁ n₂ γ₂ = (n₁ (γ₁ n₂ γ₁⁻¹)) (γ₁ γ₂)` and `(n γ)⁻¹ = (γ⁻¹ n⁻¹ γ) γ⁻¹`, where the conjugates lie in
`N` by the normalization hypothesis. It contains `N` and `Γ`, so it contains their join. The
reverse inclusion is closure of the join under multiplication.

**`mk_unipotent_mul`, `mk_leviRational_mul`.** The published `LeftCosetSpace.mk_subgroup_mul`
applied to elements of the two images, which lie in the join.

**`mem_parabolicQuotientSubgroup_iff`, `mk_eq_mk_iff`.** The first lemma unpacked for the two
ranges, and then the published `LeftCosetSpace.mk_eq_mk_iff` (`[x] = [y] ↔ y x⁻¹` lies in the
subgroup).

**`parabolicLeviRationalImage_le_normalizer`.** The image of `γ ∈ M(F)` is the image of
`l(γ) ∈ P(𝔸_F)` under `map F P G p`, because the adelic-point maps compose.

**Standard representatives.** The range equalities are the published representability statements
`Standard.isRepresentedBy_parabolicHom` and `Standard.isRepresentedBy_unipotentHom` evaluated on
the adeles, using `Standard.unipotentToParabolic_comp`. For normalization, the published
membership criteria `mem_standardParabolic` and `mem_standardUnipotent` describe the two
functors through the dynamic parabolics and unipotent radicals of the standard cocharacters
`ζ`. The published `Dynamic.conj_mem_unipotent` says that conjugating a point of the unipotent
radical of `ζ` by a point of the parabolic of `ζ` stays in that radical. Applying it for each
`ζ` gives `g u g⁻¹` in the adelic points of `standardUnipotent i ν J`, and the same for `g⁻¹`. This is the argument of the published
minimal case, with `∅` replaced by `J`.

## Scope

The module proves no closedness of `U(𝔸_F) M(F)`, no Hausdorff property of `X_P`, and no
measure on it. For a triple `(p, iU, l)` other than the standard representatives the
normalization hypothesis remains explicit. The module does not identify the standard
representatives with an actual parabolic, unipotent radical and Levi subgroup. Closedness needs the topological decomposition `P(𝔸_F) = U(𝔸_F) ⋊ M(𝔸_F)`. It
also defines no automorphic forms on `X_P`. Mœglin–Waldspurger's `A(U(𝔸)M(k)\G)` belongs to
the later Eisenstein-series block of the parent program.
