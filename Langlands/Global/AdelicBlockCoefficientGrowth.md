# Full adelic growth of realized Levi coefficients

The actual general-linear comparison transports a realized vector to a rational-invariant function with one open finite stabilizer and polynomial growth on each infinite slice. Rational height reduction with finitely many Siegel translates gives one polynomial bound in the full dimension-scaled adelic height. The product of the block heights dominates each factor and therefore bounds the pure block coefficients. Finite linear combinations retain this bound.

This derived step provides the inducing coefficient growth for Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), proof of Proposition 2, printed pp. 204–206, Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 199–201. The growth comparison uses the height estimate of Mœglin–Waldspurger, *Spectral decomposition and Eisenstein series* (1995), I.2.2 (vii), printed pp. 20–21, chapter PDF pp. 3–4.
