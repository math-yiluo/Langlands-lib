# Arithmetic place facts for the adelic product subspace topology

Worker 208. This is derived infrastructure for Conrad, *Weil and Grothendieck
approaches to adelic points* (2011), printed p.10, Example 4.3. It supplies
arithmetic inputs to the non-local-compactness argument, rather than assuming
an infinite abstract place system or noncompact completed fields.

The place extension embedding uses the existing discrete valuation extension
construction and its exact comap identity. Rational-function places include
all nonzero polynomial prime ideals. A nonfield Jacobson Dedekind domain has
infinitely many nonzero prime ideals: otherwise a product of nonzero elements
chosen in all of them is nonzero and lies in the Jacobson radical of zero,
which is zero. This applies to the polynomial ring over any field. Lying over
for the integral inclusion of the integers in the ring of integers gives the
corresponding number-field infinitude statement, using the infinitude of natural
prime numbers and their distinct principal ideals in the integers.

The completion facts retain the actual adic completion carriers and topology.
The rank-one valuation norm has definitionally the original valued uniform
structure. Each completion contains an infinite field; the existing normed
field noncompactness theorem therefore applies. The function-field facts allow
arbitrary finite extensions of the chosen rational-function field without a
separability restriction, in an arbitrary common universe.

These facts do not themselves assert failure of local compactness of the adelic
subspace or the projective comparison.
