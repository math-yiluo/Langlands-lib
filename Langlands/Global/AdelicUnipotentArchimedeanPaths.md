# Smooth paths in the archimedean unipotent group

`AdelicUnipotentArchimedeanPathsStatement` fixes a smooth connected affine unipotent group over a number field and a relative dimension for its smooth adelic manifold. It constructs a path from the identity to each archimedean group point, smooth jointly in the point and in the real path parameter.

A split series over the number field gives an affine-space isomorphism of the underlying scheme. The proof identifies the group points in the infinite adeles with mixed archimedean polynomial coordinates. In one direction the coordinate functions are regular evaluations and hence smooth. In the other direction each regular evaluation is a polynomial in the coordinates and hence smooth. The path interpolates linearly between the coordinates of the identity and those of the endpoint.

The paths serve the archimedean derivative estimates in the relative rapid-decay argument. Mœglin–Waldspurger, §I.2.10, pp. 30–31, use archimedean unipotent Lie coordinates for the same purpose.
