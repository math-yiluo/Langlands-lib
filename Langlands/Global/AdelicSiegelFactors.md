# Actual adelic compact factors and chamber products

This derived construction forms the ordered product ΩN ΩM A K in the actual adelic group of a group scheme over an arbitrary number field. It connects actual represented standard subgroup functors, actual norm-one Levi points, and the actual intrinsic positive torus chamber. It retains structural hypotheses needed to identify the product with the Siegel sets of reduction theory.

`StandardFactorData` supplies actual group schemes N and M with actual closed inclusions into G representing the shared `standardUnipotent i ν ∅` and `standardLevi i ν ∅` subgroup functors. Representation here is the existing `SubgroupFunctor.IsRepresentedBy`: monomorphism and equality of pointwise range functors. The two membership lemmas verify that the resulting adelic maps land in those exact functors. This does not prove that the empty-index standard parabolic is minimal, that the chosen torus is maximal split, or that ν is generic.

`CompactFactors` supplies compact subsets ΩN of N(A) and ΩM of M(A)^1 and a compact subgroup K of G(A), with their actual point topologies. It imposes no coverage property. `compactPrefix` is their mapped product ΩN ΩM. Given an actual morphism j : T₀ → T and i : T → G, `positiveMap` is the continuous map from the intrinsic positive subgroup of T₀ through j and i into G(A). `productSet A` is ΩN ΩM A K through these maps. The explicit membership formula preserves this order. Enlarging A enlarges the product.

`weakSiegelProduct` takes A to be the normalized weak simple-root chamber of `RelativeRootChamber`; `strictSiegelProduct` uses its normalized strict chamber with indexed thresholds. With the same constant threshold the strict product is contained in the weak one. The chambers are intrinsic and retain the number-field degree in coordinate formulas, as proved in the imported chamber module. No splitting is selected here.

`CharactersTrivialAlong f` is the algebraic structural condition that every rational character of the target restricts to the identity character along the actual group-scheme morphism f. The implemented proof of `range_le_normOne_of_charactersTrivialAlong` pulls back actual character norms and evaluates the identity character. Consequently `productSet_subset_normOne` proves that every product point is norm-one when this condition holds on the N inclusion and the T₀ inclusion. The Levi factor is norm-one by functoriality, and K is norm-one by the proved compact-subgroup inclusion. Character triviality on N and on T₀ remains an explicit structural input; it is not inferred from the supplied names or encoded as a conclusion field in G.

For affine G, the mapped prefix is compact, and restricting A to any compact subset gives a compact product. The proof uses continuity of the actual maps and finite products of compact sets. It does not assert compactness of the unrestricted root chamber or Siegel product.

The original motivation is Getz–Hahn, *An Introduction to Automorphic Representations* (2024), §2.7, printed pp.71–72, equations (2.25)–(2.26): ΩN ΩM A_T₀(t) K in G(A)^1. Their setup requires a minimal parabolic, its unipotent radical and Levi, a maximal split torus T₀ in G_der contained in M, T = T₀ Z₀, and a suitable maximal compact with G(A) = P(A)K. The weak inequality uses the normalized archimedean product. Those structural identifications, maximal compact existence and Iwasawa decomposition are not supplied by this internal construction.

Mœglin–Waldspurger, *Spectral Decomposition and Eisenstein Series* (1995), I.2.1, printed pp.18–20, provides related strict chamber products. Their ω lies in the actual minimal parabolic and their positive torus and reference point belong to the minimal Levi. This module does not identify that construction with the present factored product; the Levi-character comparison, full positive-torus/central decomposition and general-parabolic adapters remain obligations.

Reduction coverage, rational saturation, finite volume, analytic Haar modulus, and all existence or minimality theorems remain separate proof obligations. This module defines actual factor products and proves elementary functorial, compactness and containment properties only.
