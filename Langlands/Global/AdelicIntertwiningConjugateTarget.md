# Rational conjugation of the target of an adelic intertwiner

Conjugate the target parabolic by a rational point \(s\) and use \(sw\) as
the Weyl representative. The abstract source and target Levis and the
inverse conjugation between them are unchanged. The same compact is
Iwasawa for the conjugated target.

Conjugation on the actual target radical takes the rational stabilizer
onto the new stabilizer. It preserves the normalized radical Haar measure
and hence the measures of the two discrete stabilizer quotients. The
change of variables \(v\mapsto svs^{-1}\), at the new argument \(sg\),
preserves the inducing argument \(w^{-1}vg\). The proof uses representative
independence to verify this directly on the quotients.

The normalized operators then differ by the exponential height of \(s\)
for the new target, at the negative transported parameter. This factor
depends only on \(s\) and the parameter. The comparison is derived from the
integral and normalization of Bernstein and Lapid, *On the meromorphic
continuation of Eisenstein series* (2024), §2.2, journal p. 191. It uses
the rational-quotient model of Mœglin and Waldspurger, *Spectral
decomposition and Eisenstein series* (1995), II.1.6, pp. 87–88.
