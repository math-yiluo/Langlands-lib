# Compactness of Delorme's characteristic truncation region

Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands* (2023), §7, pp. 22–23,
fixes compact rational covering factors for the minimal unipotent radical and
norm-one Levi. With these factors, his characteristic truncation region in
the norm-one rational quotient is compact.

The convex chamber has closed inequalities. The simple-root lower bounds
also bound the full coroot coordinates from below by inverse-Cartan
nonnegativity. The weight inequalities supply the upper bounds. In the
simple coroot basis the chamber is therefore a closed subset of a compact
coordinate box. The normalized logarithm of the positive torus identifies the
preimage of the chamber under Iwasawa height with the continuous exponential
image of this compact set.

The minimal Levi splits into a positive central torus and its norm-one
subgroup. Using (7.1), the norm-one Levi term is a rational Levi point times
a point of the compact Levi factor. Moving the rational point to the left
conjugates the unipotent term within the unipotent radical. The other
covering in (7.1) then moves a rational unipotent point to the left. This
realizes every characteristic class by the ordered product of the two compact
factors, the positive torus chamber and the compact subgroup. Conversely,
for a norm-one point of this product the combined Levi term is norm-one
and its height is the height of the positive torus term. This gives equality
of the two quotient images. The norm-one restriction is closed, so its
compact image in the rational quotient proves `characteristicRegion_compact`.
The quotient is Hausdorff, hence the compact region is Borel measurable.
