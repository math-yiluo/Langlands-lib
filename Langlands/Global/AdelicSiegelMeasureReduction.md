# Finite Haar measure of adelic Siegel sets from the modulus formula

Author: Worker 1179. Part of task 40747bb3 (finite volume of `G(F) \ G(𝔸_F)¹`), with
Worker 235.

The Library statement `SiegelProductFiniteMeasureStatement`
(`Langlands/Global/AdelicSiegelFiniteVolume.lean`) says that the actual adelic Siegel set
`Ω_N Ω_M A_{T₀}(t) K` meets `G(𝔸_F)¹` in a set of finite Haar measure. This module reduces it
to three propositions:

- `UnipotentRationalQuotientCompactStatement`, already in the Library
  (`Langlands/Global/TriangularRationalQuotientCompact.lean`): `U(F) \ U(𝔸_F)` is compact for
  every smooth connected unipotent `U`. It is used only to see that `N(𝔸_F) ⊆ G(𝔸_F)¹`;
- `PositiveTorusUnipotentModulusStatement`, new here: the modulus formula for conjugation by
  the positive torus on `N(𝔸_F)`;
- `SimpleRootBoxCompactStatement`, new here: boxes cut out by the simple-root norms in the
  positive torus `A_{T₀}` are compact.

The reduction is the theorem `siegelProductFiniteMeasureStatement_of_inputs`. The proof
combines:

- the abstract Siegel-set bound `SiegelSetMeasure.measure_mul_chamber_mul_lt_top`
  (`Langlands/Analysis/Local/SiegelSetMeasure.lean`);
- the Library theorem `CompactFactorization.exists_pos_measure_eq_of_mul_mem`
  (`Langlands/Analysis/Local/CompactFactorizationHaar.lean`), which says that for
  `G(𝔸_F)¹ = Q K` the Haar measure of a right-`K`-saturated set is a fixed multiple of the
  Haar measure of its trace on `Q`;
- an adelic Levi decomposition `Q = M ⋉ N(𝔸_F)` proved here from the scheme-theoretic
  Levi retraction.

Neither new statement is proved here.

## Notation

Throughout, the data are the following, all in universe `u`:

- `F` is a number field;
- `G` is an affine group scheme over `Spec F`, locally of finite type and connected reductive;
- `T, i, ν, T₀, j` satisfy `IsSiegelAlgebraicSetup F G T i ν T₀ j`
  (`Langlands/Global/AdelicSiegelReductionStatement.lean`).

The setup says the following:

- `i : T → G` is a maximal split torus and `ν` is a generic cocharacter;
- `P = Standard.parabolicScheme i ν ∅` is a minimal parabolic;
- `M = Standard.leviScheme i ν ∅` is a rational Levi subgroup of `P`, equal to the
  centralizer of `T`;
- `N = Standard.unipotentScheme i ν ∅` is a geometric unipotent radical of `P`;
- `j ≫ i` factors through a maximal split torus of the derived group;
- `T` is generated by the image of `T₀` and a central split component.

`Φ⁺ = positiveRelativeRoots i ν` and `Δ = simpleRelativeRoots i ν` are the positive and
simple relative roots, and `m_β = finrank F (weightSpace i β)` is the dimension of the
`β`-weight space of `T` on the Lie algebra of `G`. `A_{T₀} =
PositiveRealTorus.intrinsicSubgroup F T₀` is the positive torus, and for a character `χ` of
`T`, `|χ|(x) = PositiveRealTorus.normCharacter F T₀ (j ≫ χ) x` is the normalized idelic norm
of the restriction of `χ` to `T₀`, evaluated at `x ∈ A_{T₀}`.

## General lemmas

- `IsSemidirectDecomposition.of_retraction`: let `M, U ≤ Q` be subgroups of a group and
  `f : Q →* G` a homomorphism with values in `M`, equal to the identity on `M`, whose kernel
  is `U`. Then `Q = M ⋉ U` in the sense of the Library predicate `IsSemidirectDecomposition`.
  *Proof.* `Q` normalizes `U`: for `q ∈ Q` and `u ∈ U`, `f(q u q⁻¹) = f(q) f(q)⁻¹ = 1`, and
  for the converse apply this to `q⁻¹`. For the complement property, `m = f(q)` is the
  unique element of `M` with `m⁻¹ q ∈ U`. Indeed `f(f(q)⁻¹ q) = 1`, and if `m⁻¹ q ∈ U` then
  `1 = f(m⁻¹ q) = m⁻¹ f(q)`.
- `of_retraction_projection` says that the Levi projection of this decomposition is `f`. It
  follows from the Library characterization `projection_eq_iff`.
  `continuous_of_retraction_projection` says the projection is continuous when `f` is.
- `AdelicGroupPoints.isClosedEmbedding_map_of_isClosedSubgroup`: if `f : H → G` is a closed subgroup of an
  affine group scheme, the adelic map `H(𝔸_F) → G(𝔸_F)` is a closed embedding. *Proof.*
  Through the Library homeomorphisms `affinePointsAlgebraHomeomorph` between adelic points
  and algebra maps out of the coordinate ring, the map is precomposition with the pullback
  of coordinate rings (`affineSchemePointEquiv_natural`). This pullback is surjective for a
  closed immersion of affine schemes (Mathlib `isAffine_surjective_of_isAffine`). The
  Library lemma `AlgPoint.isClosedEmbedding_precomp_of_surjective` then applies, because the
  adele ring is Hausdorff.

## Adelic images

`standardParabolicImage`, `standardLeviImage` and `standardUnipotentImage` are the images
`P(𝔸_F)`, `M(𝔸_F)` and `N(𝔸_F)` in `G(𝔸_F)` of the adelic points of the three closed
standard subgroups, as subgroups of `G(𝔸_F)`. The lemmas below need only an affine `G` and
`T, i, ν, T₀, j`, not the Siegel setup:

- `standardLeviImage_eq` and `standardUnipotentImage_eq`: each image equals the
  corresponding standard subgroup functor evaluated on the adelic test object, by
  representability.
- `standardLeviImage_le_parabolic` and `standardUnipotentImage_le_parabolic`: both images
  lie in `P(𝔸_F)`.
- `standardParabolicImage_le_normalizer`: `P(𝔸_F)` normalizes `N(𝔸_F)`. The standard
  functors are intersections of the dynamic functors `P(ζ)`, `U(ζ)` over all standard
  cocharacters `ζ`. On every test scheme `U(ζ)` is normalized by `P(ζ)`
  (`Dynamic.conj_mem_unipotent`).
- `positiveMap_mem_standardLeviImage` and `positiveMap_mem_normalizer`: the positive torus
  maps into `M(𝔸_F)`, and hence into the normalizer of `N(𝔸_F)`.
- `isAffine_of_isClosedSubgroup` and `isClosed_range_map`: a closed subgroup of an affine
  group scheme is affine and has closed adelic image.
- `isClosed_standardParabolicImage`, `isClosed_standardLeviImage` and
  `isClosed_standardUnipotentImage`: the three images are closed in `G(𝔸_F)`.

## The two new statements

### `PositiveTorusUnipotentModulusStatement`

For every `F`, `G`, setup and `x ∈ A_{T₀}`:

  `δ_{N(𝔸_F)}(a) = ∏_{β ∈ Φ⁺} |β|(x)^{m_β}`, where `a = positiveMap F G T i T₀ j x`.

The product is a `finprod` over the set `Φ⁺`, which is finite. `δ_{N(𝔸_F)}(a)` is the
Library's `HaarModulus.closedNormalizerCharacter` of the closed subgroup `N(𝔸_F)` of
`G(𝔸_F)` at `a`. This is the value of Mathlib's `mulEquivHaarChar` at the automorphism
`u ↦ a u a⁻¹` of `N(𝔸_F)`. By `mulEquivHaarChar_smul_preimage`, it is the factor `c` with
`μ(a S a⁻¹) = c μ(S)` for a Haar measure `μ` on `N(𝔸_F)`.

*Provenance (derived).* The statement is the adelic form of the formula
`δ(a) = |det(Ad(a) | Lie N)|`, written with positive roots.

1. *Lie algebra of `N`.* Since `ν` is generic, every relative root `β` has
   `⟨β, ν⟩ ≠ 0`, so the roots split into `Φ⁺` and `-Φ⁺`. Every positive root is an
   `ℕ`-combination of simple roots (`positiveRelativeRoots_subset_closure_simpleRelativeRoots`).
   Hence a standard cocharacter `ζ`, which is positive on `Δ`, is positive on `Φ⁺` and
   negative on `-Φ⁺`. The Lie algebra of `N = ⋂_ζ U(ζ)` is therefore `⊕_{β ∈ Φ⁺} g_β`, and
   `det(Ad(t) | Lie N) = ∏_{β ∈ Φ⁺} β(t)^{m_β}` for `t ∈ T`.
2. *Local formula.* Getz–Hahn build Haar measures from invariant top forms (§3.6, printed
   pp. 87–88). Proposition 3.6.1 (printed p. 88) derives `δ_{G(F_v)} = |det Ad|_v` from the
   fact that `Ad*(g⁻¹)` scales the invariant form `ω` by `det Ad(g)`. The same argument, for
   the automorphism `u ↦ a u a⁻¹` of `N` with differential `Ad(a)|_{Lie N}`, gives
   `μ_v(a S a⁻¹) = |det(Ad(a) | Lie N)|_v μ_v(S)` on `N(F_v)`.
3. *Global formula.* A Haar measure on `N(𝔸_F)` is a restricted product of local ones
   (Getz–Hahn (3.11), printed p. 89). So the global factor is the product of the local
   factors, `|det(Ad(a) | Lie N)|_{𝔸}`, as in Getz–Hahn (3.12) (printed p. 89). For
   `a` the image of `x ∈ A_{T₀}` one has `β(a) = (j ≫ β)(x)`, and the full idelic norm of
   `∏ β(a)^{m_β}` is `∏ |β|(x)^{m_β}`.

Borel (1966), Lemma 1.9 (printed p. 23), uses the same quantity `χ = det Ad_u a` in the real
setting and writes it as a positive combination of simple roots. The proof below does the
same (`exists_simpleRoot_exponents`).

### `SimpleRootBoxCompactStatement`

For every `F`, `G`, setup and every `s : ℝ≥0`, the box

  `{x ∈ A_{T₀} | ∀ α ∈ Δ, s⁻¹ ≤ |α|(x) ≤ s}`

is compact. (For `s = 0` the condition reads `0 ≤ |α|(x) ≤ 0`, which is empty unless `Δ` is
empty.)

*Provenance (derived).* The restrictions of the simple roots to `T₀` span
`X*(T₀) ⊗ ℚ`, by the following argument.

- Borel–Tits, Théorème 5.3 (printed p. 96), says that the rank of the relative root system
  equals the semisimple `F`-rank. By Définition 4.23 (printed p. 93), this is the dimension
  of a maximal split torus of the derived group, here `T₀`.
- Every root is trivial on the central split component `Z₀`. Since `T` is generated by the
  images of `T₀` and `Z₀`, a rational combination of roots that vanishes on `T₀` vanishes
  on `T`. So restriction to `T₀` is injective on the span of the roots, whose dimension is
  `dim T₀`.
- The roots are `±` `ℕ`-combinations of simple roots, so the restricted simple roots span
  `X*(T₀) ⊗ ℚ`.

A splitting `T₀ ≅ 𝔾_m^I` identifies `A_{T₀}` with `(ℝ_{>0})^I`. The Library lemmas are
`fullIntrinsic`, `fullIntrinsic_surjective` and `isClosedEmbedding_full`. Under this
identification `|α|(x) = ∏_i x_i^{[F:ℚ] n_i(α)}` (`normCharacter_fullIntrinsic`). Spanning
means that the linear map `(log x_i)_i ↦ (Σ_i n_i(α) log x_i)_α` is injective. The box is
therefore closed with bounded logarithms, hence compact.

Borel (1966), Lemma 1.9 (printed p. 23), uses these simple-root coordinates on `A` when it
writes the integral over `A_t` as a product over `Δ`.

## The adelic Levi decomposition

- `exists_standardLeviRetraction`: under the setup there is a homomorphism of group schemes
  `q : P → M` with `leviToParabolic ≫ q = 𝟙`, whose kernel on every test scheme is the image
  of `N`. *Proof.* The setup gives that `M ⊆ P` is a rational Levi subgroup. The Library
  lemma `IsRationalLeviSubgroup.exists_unipotentRadical_retraction` gives such a `q` for
  some geometric unipotent radical `r : U → P`. The setup also says `N → P` is a geometric
  unipotent radical. Each of `U` and `N` is a smooth connected unipotent normal closed
  subgroup, and each contains every such subgroup (the `greatest` field). Hence `U` and `N`
  have the same image functor.
- `exists_standardLeviPart`: there is a continuous homomorphism
  `f : P(𝔸_F) → G(𝔸_F)` with values in `M(𝔸_F)`, the identity on `M(𝔸_F)`, and kernel
  `N(𝔸_F)`. *Proof.* Let `ι : P(𝔸_F) → G(𝔸_F)` be the closed embedding
  (`isClosedEmbedding_map_of_isClosedSubgroup`) and `e` the induced isomorphism onto its image. Put
  `f = ι ∘ (q ≫ leviToParabolic)_𝔸 ∘ e⁻¹`. Continuity of `e⁻¹` follows because `ι` is an
  embedding. The three properties come from `leviToParabolic ≫ parabolicHom = leviHom`,
  `unipotentToParabolic ≫ parabolicHom = unipotentHom`, `leviToParabolic ≫ q = 𝟙`, and the
  kernel description of `q`. Injectivity of `leviToParabolic` on points follows from
  `leviToParabolic ≫ q = 𝟙`.
- `standardUnipotentImage_le_normOne`: given `UnipotentRationalQuotientCompactStatement`,
  `N(𝔸_F) ⊆ G(𝔸_F)¹`. *Proof.* `N` is smooth, connected and unipotent (it is a unipotent
  radical), affine, and locally of finite type (a closed immersion into `G` composed with
  the structure map of `G`). So `N(F) \ N(𝔸_F)` is compact. By the Library lemma
  `mem_normOneSubgroup_of_compactSpace`, `N(𝔸_F) = N(𝔸_F)¹`, and `map_normOneSubgroup_le`
  maps `N(𝔸_F)¹` into `G(𝔸_F)¹`.

## Simple-root exponents

- `characterValue_add'` and `characterValue_zero'`: character evaluation turns sums of
  characters into products of values, and the zero character into `1`.
- `restrictedNormHom j x`: for fixed `x`, the map `χ ↦ |χ|(x)`, as an additive
  homomorphism from `X*(T)` to `Additive ℝ≥0`.
- `restrictedNorm_sum_nsmul`: `|Σ_α k_α α|(x) = ∏_α |α|(x)^{k_α}` for natural `k`.
- `exists_simpleRoot_exponents`: for split `T`, there are exponents `e : Δ → ℕ`, all
  nonzero, with `∏_{β ∈ Φ⁺} |β|(x)^{m_β} = ∏_{α ∈ Δ} |α|(x)^{e_α}` for every `x`. *Proof.*
  Write each positive root as `β = Σ_α n_{βα} α` with `n_{βα} ∈ ℕ`. For `β ∈ Δ` take
  `n_{β·}` to be the indicator of `β`; for other positive roots the coefficients exist by
  `positiveRelativeRoots_subset_closure_simpleRelativeRoots` and induction on the additive
  closure. Put `e_α = Σ_{β ∈ Φ⁺} n_{βα} m_β`. The product identity follows by
  `restrictedNorm_sum_nsmul` and exchanging the two finite products. The sum `e_α`
  contains the term `n_{αα} m_α = m_α`, which is positive: `α` is a root, so its weight
  space is nonzero, and the Lie algebra is finite-dimensional.

## The reduction theorem

`siegelProductFiniteMeasureStatement_of_inputs hU hmod hbox` proves
`SiegelProductFiniteMeasureStatement`. Given the data, `t`, compact factors `C` with
`IsParabolicIwasawaCompact` and `IsPositiveTorusCompatible`, write `H = G(𝔸_F)¹`, which is
closed in `G(𝔸_F)`. The proof applies `measure_mul_chamber_mul_lt_top` in `H` with:

- `Q = P(𝔸_F) ∩ H`, `M = M(𝔸_F) ∩ H`, `U = N(𝔸_F)` and `K = C.compactSubgroup`, all as
  subgroups of `H` (`subgroupOf`). They are closed because the images are closed. `K` is
  compact because compact subgroups lie in `H` (`compactSubgroup_le_normOneSubgroup`) and
  `H` is closed. `N(𝔸_F) ⊆ H` by `standardUnipotentImage_le_normOne`.
- The decomposition `Q = M ⋉ U` from `of_retraction` applied to `f` restricted to `Q`. `f`
  maps `Q` into `H`: `f(p)⁻¹ p ∈ N(𝔸_F) ⊆ H`, so `f(p) ∈ H`. Its projection is continuous.
- The constant `c` from `exists_pos_measure_eq_of_mul_mem` for `μ = Measure.haar` on `H`
  and `μ_Q = Measure.haar` on `Q`. The hypothesis `H = Q K` holds because
  `P(𝔸_F) K = G(𝔸_F)` (`IsParabolicIwasawaCompact`): if `g = p k` with `g ∈ H` and `k ∈ K`,
  then `p = g k⁻¹ ∈ H`.
- `Ω_U` and `Ω_M`, the traces on `H` of the images of `C.omegaN` and `C.omegaM`. These
  images are compact subsets of `H`, so the traces are compact.
- `a = positiveMapNormOneOfDerived`. The setup's derived-subgroup clause gives that
  `j ≫ i` has range in the derived subgroup. `a` takes values in `M`. It commutes with `M`:
  `M(𝔸_F)` is the centralizer of `T` on the adelic test object, because the setup says
  `standardLevi = torusCentralizer`. It commutes with `K ∩ Q` by `IsPositiveTorusCompatible`.
- `λ_α = |α|` for `α ∈ Δ`. `Δ` is finite (`IsSplitTorus.finite_simpleRelativeRoots`). The
  dyadic box `{1/2 ≤ λ_α ≤ 2}` is compact by `hbox` with `s = 2`.
- The exponents `e` from `exists_simpleRoot_exponents`. To check
  `∏ λ_α^{e_α} ≤ δ_U(a)`:
  - the Library lemma `closedNormalizerCharacter_subgroupOf_of_mem` identifies the modulus
    of `U` inside `H` with that of `N(𝔸_F)` inside `G(𝔸_F)`;
  - `hmod` evaluates that modulus;
  - `exists_simpleRoot_exponents` rewrites the result as `∏ λ_α^{e_α}`, so equality holds.

The result bounds `μ(Ω_U Ω_M a(chamber λ t) K)`, where `chamber λ t` is the weak chamber
`t ≤ λ_α`. It remains to show that the trace on `H` of `weakSiegelProduct` lies in this set.
An element of the Siegel product is `n m a k` with `n` the image of a point of `C.omegaN`,
`m` the image of a point of `C.omegaM`, `a` the image of `x` in
`PositiveRealTorus.weakSimpleRootChamber` and `k ∈ K` (`mem_productSet_iff`). Each factor
lies in `H`, so the element is the product of the four factors in `H`. The chamber condition
`t ≤ |α|(x)` for `α ∈ Δ` is exactly membership of `x` in `chamber λ t`.
