# Fourier inversion of the ambient wave-packet profile

`AmbientWavePacketProfileFourierInversionStatement` gives the Fourier integral of `ambientWavePacketProfile` over the positive split centre of a connected reductive group over a number field. It is the split-centre Fourier inversion used on p. 35 of Delorme 2023, just before Lemma 7.11. The positive constant records the normalization of `positiveCentreHaarMeasure` in the chosen parameter chart. For each smooth compactly supported coefficient and each imaginary character, the profile against the conjugate character is absolutely integrable. Its integral equals the positive constant times the coefficient evaluated at the character parameter in the chart.

The theorem `ambientWavePacketProfileFourierInversion` uses the basis-dual coordinates on the actual logarithmic character group followed by division by \(2\pi\). They identify the split-centre Haar measure with a positive multiple of Euclidean volume and convert the parameter character into the Fourier kernel. The coefficient becomes a Schwartz function on Euclidean space and the profile is its inverse Fourier transform times \((2\pi)^{-m}\). The latter is integrable and multiplication by the conjugate character preserves its norm. Fourier inversion evaluates the coefficient and Haar transport supplies the normalization factor.
