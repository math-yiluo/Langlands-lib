# Concrete operators on the scalar Eisenstein output carrier

`scalarFiniteSmoothOperatorAction` transports the concrete adelic operator algebra from finite-smooth rational-invariant matrix functions to the range of evaluation on the group-scheme points. On that range its operators agree with `scalarAutomorphicOperator`, the linear scalar extension used in the continuation tests. The transported action gives the composition law on the evaluated carrier.

The finite and infinite compact generators act by the corresponding right translations of scalar functions. The real Lie generator acts by the left-invariant derivative of the infinite slice at fixed finite coordinate. This uses the group-scheme to matrix comparisons for the adelic point product, the corresponding smooth slices and the identity tangent.

This internal comparison identifies the operators on the scalar outputs of the Eisenstein series in the holomorphic-section argument of Langlands, proof of Proposition 2, printed pp. 205–207 (Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 200–202).
