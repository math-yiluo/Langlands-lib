# Logarithms and complex parameters of the positive Levi split component

For a compatible parabolic presentation `P = M U`, let `S` be the image
in `P(𝔸)` of the intrinsic positive real subgroup of the split component
of `M`. The normalized logarithm maps `S` onto the full real dual of the
rational character lattice of `P`. The powers `a ↦ a^λ` on `S` distinguish
the complex parameters `λ ∈ X^*(P) ⊗ ℂ`.

These assertions give the logarithm surjectivity and parameter injectivity
in Bernstein–Lapid's number field identification in §6.2, following (6.1).
In particular, distinct labels in a decomposition by unnormalized
exponents give distinct eigenvalue functions of `S`.

[Bernstein–Lapid, On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series](https://doi.org/10.1090/jams/1020)
