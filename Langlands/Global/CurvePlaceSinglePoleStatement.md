# A single prescribed pole in a global function field

Let \(F\) be a finite extension of \(k(t)\) for a finite constant
field \(k\), and let \(v\) be an actual discrete curve place of \(F/k\).
`CurvePlaceSinglePoleStatement` asserts that some element of \(F\) lies
outside the valuation ring at \(v\) and inside the valuation ring at
every other place. The statement allows inseparable presentations of
\(F/k(t)\).

This is an arithmetic ingredient for the ring identity following
Remark 3.5 in Brian Conrad, *Weil and Grothendieck approaches to
adelic points*, December 31, 2011, p. 6. For nonempty \(S\), a function
with a single pole in \(S\) provides a parameter whose normalized
affine chart contains every place outside \(S\). The single-pole
assertion follows from the finite class group of a normalized affine
chart and the finite additive quotient of the overlap of two
reciprocal parameter charts.
