# The actual finite compact orbit as a stable continuous source

This internal construction transports the genuine smooth enveloping action to
the derivative smooth carrier. The resulting algebra homomorphism defines its
enveloping module structure, compatible with the existing complex module, and
its scalar action is the published `operator`. Published differential covariance
gives compatibility with the whole actual compact group.

Every finite-dimensional compact-invariant derivative subspace is stable under
the actual complex compact Lie generators. To prove this, transport the subspace
to actual smooth functions. Its compact invariance is preserved. The published
actual compact tangent curves and closed finite-dimensional difference-quotient
argument give stability under real compact differentials; the published scalar
extension then gives all complex compact generators. Transport back gives the
claimed stability in the derivative carrier.

For a compact-finite smooth vector, choose a finite basis of its actual whole
compact orbit span. The induced coordinate representation
`compactOrbitSource` is jointly continuous: the orbit subspace has its inherited
Hausdorff topological vector space structure, all its finite-dimensional linear
coordinate maps are continuous, and the published actual compact action is
jointly continuous.

`compactOrbitSourceMap` is the equivariant inclusion in those coordinates. Its
image is exactly the original actual compact orbit span. The finite invariant
subspace result proves that this image has the compact-generator stability
required for actual Cartan fixed-vector and Hom central generation. Number-field
universes and all natural ranks are retained.

This construction does not yet identify its enveloping-generated submodule with
the published span of compact translates of derivatives, compare an ambient
isotypic intersection with that submodule's isotypic span, or prove the
compact-type central-span or automorphic differential growth statements.
