# Constant terms of functions on nested standard parabolic quotients

The rational points of a smaller standard parabolic's radical lie in
the rational points of the larger parabolic. Their image acts trivially
on the inducing function, so the constant-term integrand descends
continuously to the compact radical quotient. Absolute integrability
then gives complex linearity on the automorphic function space.

Averaging an inducing function along its own radical leaves it unchanged
because the quotient measure has total mass one. The difference of two
cuspidal inducing functions is cuspidal by linearity along all smaller
parabolics. These are internal consequences of the Library constant
term and the nested standard parabolic order used in Bernstein–Lapid
§6.2.
