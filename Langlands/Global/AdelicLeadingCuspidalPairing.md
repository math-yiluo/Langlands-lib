# Levi cusp-test pairings and the split-component action

The pairing over the actual rational norm-one Levi quotient defines a complex
linear map on the submodule of functions for which the integrand is absolutely
integrable at every external argument. The positive split component lies in the
center of the Levi, so moving the corresponding left shift to the external
argument preserves integrability and commutes with pairing.

Restricting a scalar-subtracted split shift to the integrability submodule
and commuting its powers with the pairing proves that pairing preserves joint
generalized exponents. This is an internal consequence of the Library's
pairing for Bernstein–Lapid (6.8) and unnormalized action (6.10). It supplies
the algebraic step for applying scalar meromorphic continuation to cusp tests.
