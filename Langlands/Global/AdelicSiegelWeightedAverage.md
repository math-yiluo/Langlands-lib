# Pointwise Siegel majorants under unipotent averaging

`AdelicSiegelWeightedAverageStatement` transfers any real majorant depending on the
positive torus coordinate through a normalized standard constant term. Fix a weak
simple-root chamber and a compact set of right factors. The enlarged compact set
depends on these geometric data. Any function invariant under the rational points
of the radical that satisfies the majorant on the enlarged set gives the same
majorant for the constant term on the original set.

The radical lies in the minimal radical, and the compact unipotent quotient has
a compact set of representatives. Conjugating the representatives by the inverse
of a chamber point puts them in a fixed compact set by the contraction property
used in Delorme's (2.4). At each quotient point the integrand may be evaluated on
one of those representatives by rational invariance. The total mass of the
normalized quotient measure is one, so integration retains the majorant.

The source is P. Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*, Advances in
Mathematics 426 (2023), 109107, equation (2.4) in §2, PDF p. 7, and the use of
constant terms in the proof of Proposition 3.6, PDF pp. 13–14. The registered PDF
is `Delorme--On the spectral theorem of Langlands--2023.pdf`, SHA-256
