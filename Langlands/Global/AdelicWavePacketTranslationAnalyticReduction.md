# Analytic reduction of symmetric adelic wave-packet translation

`imaginaryEisensteinConvolution_of_meromorphicContinuation` applies local
Fréchet meromorphic continuation to the Eisenstein values already supplied
for the input and convolved inducing forms. Joint continuity follows in
real coordinates on the imaginary axis. For the convolution comparison,
the finite expansion gives entire scalar coefficients, and each
imaginary parameter belongs to the holomorphy domains of both supplied
continuations. The continued scalar identity therefore gives the
imaginary-axis equality with the actual right convolution.

`wavePacketTranslationAnalyticReduction` applies the finite convolution
assembly and the exact right-translation separation theorem. Scalar
holomorphy gives an all-parameter convolution expansion from a fixed
finite span on the imaginary axis. Meromorphic continuation together
with imaginary-axis holomorphy gives the new Eisenstein continuations,
using absolute convergence in the regular region. The wave-packet
inner-product theorem and the square-integrable intertwining expansions
give the \(L^2\) membership used in the approximation. The conclusion
is the stated \(L^2\) approximation on the full rational quotient,
with the original Weyl symmetry and spectral weights.

The argument composes the right-action identities and finite spectral
packet construction of Arthur, *An introduction to the trace formula*,
§7, pp. 32–35. The Fréchet continuation formulation is from
Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series*,
the corollary in §8.6, p. 226, and the wave-packet norm and imaginary-axis holomorphy
are the inputs from Lapid, *On the asymptotic inner product of truncated
Eisenstein series*, Proposition 2 and Proposition 6.
