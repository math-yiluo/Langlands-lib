# Compact equivariance of the adelic intertwining operator

Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series* (2024), §8.2, p. 222, use the commutation of the intertwining operator with the right compact action. Here both Iwasawa heights are right invariant under the common compact subgroup. Translating the inducing function by a compact element therefore changes the evaluation argument of the integrand by the same right translation. Equality of the integrands and of the target height powers gives equality of the normalized values. In particular, every finite level contained in the finite factor of the compact and fixing the inducing function also fixes the intertwined function.

`functionInfiniteSubgroupAction` restricts right translation on scalar functions to the given subgroup of the infinite adelic points. If the integrand of the inducing vector is integrable at every evaluation argument, the same holds for its right translates under the infinite compact subgroup. Span induction extends integrability to the linear span of the compact orbit. Linearity of integration and the right-translation identity make the operator on this span an intertwiner into the representation on all scalar functions. The original vector maps to the normalized intertwining value. If the inducing vector is finite under the infinite compact subgroup, its orbit representation is finite-dimensional, and so is the orbit span of its image. Each parameter uses the same source orbit representation. This construction implements the compact-equivariance step of the §8.2 lemma using a cyclic representation.
