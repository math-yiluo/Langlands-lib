# Canonical Levi quotient transport and normalized inducing functions

A Levi isomorphism maps canonical positive points to canonical positive points and principal points to principal points. Its map on the canonical quotients intertwines the inclusion of norm-one classes. The canonical measures are pushforwards of the norm-one quotient measures, whose transport differs by a positive scalar. The same scalar therefore relates the canonical measures.

`normalizedLeviValue` incorporates the inverse parabolic half modulus in a Levi restriction at an arbitrary adelic argument. Left rational invariance and the product formula handle principal points. Split-component equivariance cancels the inverse half modulus on the positive split component. The squared norm is exactly the weighted square in `IsSquareIntegrableInducedForm`. These are consequences of the quotient and character definitions used in Lapid's §2 norm formula and Bernstein–Lapid's formula (6.5).
