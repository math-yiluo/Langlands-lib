# Transformation rules for the height differential

The height differential is real-linear in the invariant direction and smooth on each fixed finite slice. Left parabolic translation adds a constant to the height pairing, so its derivative is unchanged. The finite compact elements fix the height along the entire right orbit used to take the derivative. Infinite compact translation conjugates the derivative direction by the actual tangent action. These rules make the derivative of the height pairing a multiplier on the corresponding compact-finite inducing sections.

This derived calculation supplies the archimedean variation in the holomorphic section argument of Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), proof of Proposition 2, printed pp. 205–206, Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 200–201.
