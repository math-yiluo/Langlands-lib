# Comparison of pointwise and function-valued Eisenstein continuations

Fix a linear chart of the parabolic character space. Suppose `EC` is a
continuation with the local Fréchet-valued meromorphy and automorphy
properties in `EisensteinMeromorphicContinuationStatement`. Suppose `EP`
is meromorphic pointwise and agrees with `EC` on a sufficiently regular
region. The theorem constructs a single open dense domain on which all
scalar evaluations of `EC` are holomorphic and its values are automorphic.
On the intersection with the holomorphy domain of `EP`, the two
continuations agree as functions on the adelic group.

Choose local function-valued presentations before evaluating at an adelic
point. Each point evaluation is continuous linear for the chosen
uniform moderate growth topology. Its scalar function agrees with the
evaluation of `EC` on that local holomorphy domain. The open-cover
meromorphy lemma gives scalar meromorphy of `EC` on the union of these
domains, which is independent of the adelic point. Each point of the
union also inherits the local automorphy assertion.

The regular region is open and nonempty. Apply the scalar identity
principle there at every adelic point and use function extensionality.
In the Eisenstein application both continuations agree with the same
series in the intersection of their initial regular regions. This
derivation connects the local formulation in Bernstein–Lapid §8.6 with
the universal pointwise continuations in the functional equation.
