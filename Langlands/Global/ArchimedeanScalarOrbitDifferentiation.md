# Genuine differentiation of continuous scalar orbit coefficients

Author: Worker 94. Internal derived helper for the smooth scalar coefficient
proof, using the actual published derivative topology and differential action.

The parameter derivative of each compact normed derivative coordinate is
identified pointwise by the continuous evaluation map. Joint smoothness
already supplies its normed Fréchet derivative; the two-variable interchange
identity computes that derivative.

Real Hahn–Banach extends a continuous scalar functional across the embedding
in the product of these normed coordinates. Its extension uses finitely many
coordinates, so their genuine derivatives identify the scalar derivative.
The resulting real generator identity is an equality of actual smooth
functions. No norm or differential calculus on the space of smooth vectors

The coefficient is complex-linear in the smooth vector. The transported
operators preserve the unit, addition, complex scaling and multiplication.

identity to every element of the complex universal enveloping algebra.
