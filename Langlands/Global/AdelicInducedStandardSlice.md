# Standard-module coordinates from block coefficient orbits

For a continuous scalar functional on the normed coefficient space of a normalized adelic section, `blockCoefficientOrbit` evaluates the functional after the action of one local diagonal block on a coefficient vector. All other coordinates of the Levi element are the identity.

The real and complex membership lemmas assume that each such coefficient orbit lies in the standard module of the corresponding block list. They evaluate the adelic section along \(y\iota_v(g)\), with \(y\) commuting with the embedded local parabolic. Evaluating each block's Borel relation at the identity and multiplying the resulting characters gives the inner character of the full block Levi. The normalized covariance comparison then places the coordinate in the principal series of the concatenated position characters.

At a real place, the outer determinant half-character is one on an embedded compact block. The left compact block slice through an arbitrary \(g\) is therefore the restriction of the coefficient orbit for the value of the section at \(y\iota_v(g)\). The compact-block Fourier criterion gives the required real standard-module membership. At a complex place the concatenated standard module is the principal series itself.

This is an internal step in the archimedean induction-in-stages comparison for Task 7041. Its premises concern the actual coefficient action. It supplies standard-module membership before taking compact-finite vectors.
