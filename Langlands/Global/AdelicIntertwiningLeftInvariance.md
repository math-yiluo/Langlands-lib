# Left invariance of the actual adelic intertwining operator

Right translation by a target radical point preserves the quotient measure because the radical is unimodular. It carries the integrand at the original argument to the integrand at the left-translated argument. The target height is unchanged by that radical.

Conjugation by a rational target Levi point preserves the target radical and its principal subgroup. A topological group automorphism preserving the principal subgroup preserves the ambient Haar measure normalized by its quotient mass. The same conjugation preserves the intersection with the conjugate source radical and therefore induces a measure-preserving automorphism of the rational intertwining quotient. After changing variables, the translating point becomes a rational point of the source parabolic under Weyl conjugation. Both the inducing function and the height power are invariant under this point. The target height is invariant under rational parabolic points. Invariance under the two subgroups gives invariance under their join.

This is the left invariance in Mœglin–Waldspurger, *Spectral decomposition and Eisenstein series*, II.1.6 (i), printed pp. 87–88, on the quotient by the actual target radical and rational Levi.
