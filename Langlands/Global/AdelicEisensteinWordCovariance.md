# Covariance of holomorphic operator words on a common convergence region

`sectionOperatorPair` combines the parameter-dependent operators on the inducing sections with the concrete operators on scalar finite-smooth rational functions. The product algebra records simultaneous composition and scalar action.

`eventually_scalarAutomorphicOperator_eisensteinSeries_word` assumes eventual compact majorants and smooth outputs for every constant inducing section. It uses finite holomorphic dependence for finite translations and real Lie generators and the scalar formulas for the finite, compact and real Lie actions. For a first derivative the proof obtains eventual bounds in each direction of a real tangent basis. Real linearity yields bounds for every direction at each of these parameters. The concrete convergence-domain comparisons give covariance on constant sections and hence on finite holomorphic expansions.

The real generators give every enveloping operator by complexification and tensor-algebra induction, and free-algebra induction gives the result for every operator word. The scalar equality uses the same concrete operator test as continuation. This is the convergence-domain step in Langlands' proof of Proposition 2, printed pp. 205–207 (Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 200–202).
