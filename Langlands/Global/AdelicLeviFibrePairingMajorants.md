# Integrable majorants for the finite Levi fibre pairing

Fix a standard parabolic \(P=MU\), a positive-size ambient matrix
representation with height \(H_G\), and its restriction with height
\(H_M\) on the Levi. Suppose \(f\) on the parabolic quotient has
height growth and \(t\) is rationally invariant on the Levi and has
weighted norm-one representatives. `exists_leviPairing_norm_bound`
uniformly bounds \(|f(\iota(x)g)t(x)|\) for norm-one \(x\) and \(g\)
in a supplied compact set. By rational invariance one may replace
\(x\) by a rational translate realizing the test's bound. Height
submultiplicativity and the compact set control the right translate,
and \(H_G(\iota(x))=H_M(x)\). The argument rounds the growth exponent
up to a natural number.

For continuous functions, the integrand descends continuously to the
norm-one rational quotient. The quotient has finite volume, so the
uniform bound proves absolute convergence. For the finite Iwasawa
formula, the right translates range over the image of a compact
Levi set multiplied by the compact subgroup. The same estimate
gives the required constant majorant for all these translates.

`leviPairing_leviFibreSmoothing_eq_integral` exchanges the positive
integration with the norm-one quotient integration for the smoothing
of a continuous family of Levi \(L^2\) classes. The assumptions include
the central split torus and right-invariant norm-one Haar, a
positive-size closed embedding of the Levi, a compactly supported
continuous ambient kernel, and a continuous rationally invariant
test with weighted norm-one representatives. The scalar integrand is
continuous and supported in one compact set in the positive variable.
On that set the reflected kernels have common compact support and a
common sup norm bound. The quotient-kernel estimate bounds the
evaluation by a height power times the norm of the class. The class
norm and inverse modulus are bounded on the compact set. The
weighted test bound then controls the product after replacing the
norm-one point by its rational representative. This gives an
integrable majorant on the product measure, and the norm-one
translation formula and Fubini give the stated exchange.

The estimates implement the rapid decay and norm-one pairing
arguments of Mœglin–Waldspurger, *Spectral Decomposition and
Eisenstein Series*, I.2.12 and I.2.18, for the fibrewise pairing in
Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein
series*, §6.2, equation (6.8).
