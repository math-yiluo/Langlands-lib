# Finiteness of constant terms under the positive split component

Module `Langlands.Global.AdelicConstantTermSplitFinitenessStatement` defines
`Langlands.Global.positiveCentralTorusPoints`. It also states
`Langlands.Global.ConstantTermSplitComponentFinitenessStatement` as a
proposition definition, and supplies no proof.

Take parabolics `P = MU ⊇ P' = M'U'` of a connected reductive group `G` over
a number field `F`, and an automorphic form `φ` on `U(𝔸)M(F) \ G(𝔸)`. The
statement says that the left translates of the constant term `C_{P,P'}φ`
by the positive real points `𝔄_{P'}` of the split centre of `M'` span a
finite-dimensional complex vector space. This finiteness is what lets
Bernstein–Lapid decompose `𝒜_{P'}` into generalized eigenspaces in (6.3). In
§8.5 they use it to choose exponents `μ_{P'}` with
`C_{P,P'}φ ∈ 𝒜_{P'}(μ_{P'})`.

## The definition

For a commutative group scheme `T`, a map `i : T → G` and a map
`j : M' → G` (all over `Spec F`), `positiveCentralTorusPoints T i j` is the
subgroup

`PositiveRealTorus.imageSubgroup F T G i ⊓ Subgroup.centralizer (Set.range (map F M' G j))`

of `G(𝔸)`. The first factor is the image under `i` of the points of `T(𝔸)`
all of whose characters are positive scalar ideles
(`PositiveRealTorus.intrinsicSubgroup`). A positive scalar idele is a
positive real placed at every infinite place, with `1` at every finite
place. The second factor is the centralizer in `G(𝔸)` of the image of
`M'(𝔸)` under `j`. The definition takes `i` and `j` arbitrary. For the data
of the statement it is MW's `A_{M'} = A_{M_0} ∩ Z_{M'}` and BL's
`𝔄_{P'} = 𝔄_0 ∩ S_{M'}(𝔸)` (see below).

## Statement

`ConstantTermSplitComponentFinitenessStatement.{u}` quantifies:

- a number field `F : Type u`; group schemes `G`, `P`, `M`, `U`, `P'`, `M'`,
  `U'` over `Spec F` and a commutative group scheme `T`, with `G` affine
  (`IsAffineHom G.X.hom`, `IsAffine G.X.left`) and locally of finite type,
  and `P`, `P'`, `U'` affine;
- closed immersions `p : P → G` and `p' : P' → G`; maps `iU : U → P`,
  `l : M → P`, `iU' : U' → P'`, `l' : M' → P'`, with `iU' ≫ p'` a closed
  immersion; and maps `i : T → G`, `τ : T → M'`;
- hypotheses:
  - `G` is connected reductive;
  - `p` and `p'` are parabolic subgroups;
  - `iU` and `iU'` are geometric unipotent radicals
    (`LeviSubgroup.IsGeometricUnipotentRadical`);
  - `l` and `l'` are rational Levi subgroups (`IsRationalLeviSubgroup`);
  - `i` is a maximal split torus of `G` (`IsMaximalSplitTorus`: `T` is a
    split torus, `i` a closed immersion, maximal among split subtori),
    and `τ ≫ l' ≫ p' = i`, so `T ⊆ M'`;
  - `SubgroupFunctor.range p' ≤ SubgroupFunctor.range p`, i.e. `P' ⊆ P`;
- a subgroup `K` of `G(𝔸)` that is a maximal compact subgroup
  (`IsMaximalCompactSubgroup F G K`) with `P'(𝔸)K = G(𝔸)`
  (`IsIwasawaCompact p' K`, which also records compactness);
- a matrix representation `rho` of `G` that is a closed embedding
  (`IsClosedSubgroup rho`), the smooth-structure data `hsmooth` and `hcalc`
  through which the Library acts by the centre `𝔷` of the enveloping
  algebra, and a function `φ : G(𝔸) → ℂ`;
- `IsAutomorphicFunctionOn F G hsmooth hcalc (parabolicQuotientSubgroup F p iU l)
  (K.comap (ofInfinite F G).toMonoidHom) rho φ`: `φ` is left invariant
  under `U(𝔸)M(F)`, smooth, finite under the archimedean part
  `K ∩ G(𝔸_∞)` of `K`, `𝔷`-finite and of moderate growth for the adelic
  norm of `rho` (MW I.2.17).

The conclusion is

`FiniteDimensional ℂ (Submodule.span ℂ (Set.range fun x => fun g => subgroupConstantTerm F (iU' ≫ p') φ (x * g)))`,

with `x` ranging over `positiveCentralTorusPoints T i (l' ≫ p')`.
Here `subgroupConstantTerm F (iU' ≫ p') φ` is the constant term
`C_{U'}φ(g) = ∫_{U'(F) \ U'(𝔸)} φ(u g) du`
(module `Langlands.Global.AdelicEisensteinConstantTermStatement`). So the
functions `g ↦ C_{U'}φ(x g)`, `x ∈ 𝔄_{P'}`, span a finite-dimensional
subspace of the functions on `G(𝔸)`.

## Correspondence with the source

The points in this section explain the statement. None of them is
formalized in this module.

- `positiveCentralTorusPoints T i (l' ≫ p')` is `𝔄_{P'}`.
  - `𝔄_0`. For split `T` the points of `T(𝔸)` whose characters are
    positive scalar ideles form `T(ℝ)^0`, embedded through
    `ℝ ↪ 𝔸_ℚ ↪ 𝔸_F`, for any splitting
    (`PositiveRealTorus.subgroup_eq_intrinsicSubgroup`). This is MW's
    coordinate construction of `A_{M_0}`. The maximal split torus `T` plays
    the role of BL's `S_0` and MW's `T_0`, the maximal split torus in the
    centre of `M_0 = C_G(T)`. So `PositiveRealTorus.imageSubgroup F T G i`
    is BL's `𝔄_0` and MW's `A_{M_0}`.
  - MW's form. Since `i = τ ≫ l' ≫ p'`, the group `𝔄_0` lies in the image
    of `M'(𝔸)`. So its intersection with the centralizer of `M'(𝔸)` is
    `𝔄_0 ∩ Z_{M'}`, which is MW's definition `A_{M'} = A_{M_0} ∩ Z_{M'}`
    (I.2.1).
  - BL's form. MW's lemma identifies this with BL's
    `𝔄_{P'} = 𝔄_0 ∩ S_{M'}(𝔸)` (BL §6.2, p.200). Take `x ∈ 𝔄_0` that
    commutes with `M'(𝔸)`. For every root `α` of `T` in `M'` it commutes
    with the root group `U_α(𝔸) ⊆ M'(𝔸)`. At an archimedean place `v` the
    adjoint action of `x_v` on the Lie algebra of `U_α` multiplies the
    weight spaces by the positive reals `α(x_v)` and `α(x_v)^2`. Hence
    `α(x_v) = 1`. So `x` lies in the positive real points of
    `(⋂_α ker α)^0`, which is the split centre `S_{M'}`; that is,
    `x ∈ 𝔄_0 ∩ S_{M'}(𝔸)`. Conversely `S_{M'}(𝔸)` is central in `M'(𝔸)`.
- The constant term. For `x ∈ 𝔄_{P'}` the function `g ↦ C_{U'}φ(x g)` is
  the left translate of `C_{P,P'}φ` by `x`. `subgroupConstantTerm` uses the
  quotient Haar measure scaled to total mass one, which applies because
  `U'(F) \ U'(𝔸)` is compact. This is BL's normalization (§2.2, p.191,
  footnote 1) and MW's (I.1.13, p.18).
  The integrand is evaluated at chosen representatives, but `φ` is left
  invariant under `U'(F) ⊆ P'(F) ⊆ P(F) ⊆ U(𝔸)M(F)`, so it descends to the
  quotient and the integral is BL's `C_{P,P'}φ`.
- Finiteness and the decomposition (6.3). The span of left translates by
  `𝔄_{P'}` equals the span of BL's twisted translates
  `a·ψ = δ_{P'}(a)^{-1/2} ψ(a ·)`, since the two differ by nonzero scalars.
  Let `ψ` be a function on `G(𝔸)` whose orbit maps `a ↦ ψ(a g)` on
  `𝔄_{P'}` are continuous. Then the following are equivalent:
  1. the translates of `ψ` span a finite-dimensional space `W`;
  2. there is a finite tuple `μ` of quasi-characters of `𝔄_{P'}` such that
     `a ↦ (a·ψ)(g)` lies in `𝒫_{𝔄_{P'}}(μ)` for every `g ∈ G(𝔸)`. This is a
     polynomial exponential condition on the orbit maps, uniform in `g`;
  3. `ψ` is a finite sum of functions `ψ_λ`, for finitely many exponents
     `λ`, such that `a ↦ (a·ψ_λ)(g) a^{-λ}` is a polynomial in `H_{P'}(a)` of
     degree at most some `n` independent of `g`.

  The argument for (1 ⇒ 3): the twisted action of the real vector group
  `𝔄_{P'}` on `W` is continuous, because the orbit maps are continuous and
  finitely many evaluations are coordinates on `W`. So `W` is the direct
  sum of its generalized eigenspaces `W_λ`. On `W_λ`, `a` acts by `a^λ` times
  a unipotent operator that is polynomial in `H_{P'}(a)`. Write
  `ψ = ∑_λ ψ_λ` with `ψ_λ ∈ W_λ`. For (3 ⇒ 2), repeat each exponent `n + 1`
  times. For (2 ⇒ 1), expand `(a·ψ)(g)` in a basis `e_1, …, e_r` of
  `𝒫_{𝔄_{P'}}(μ)` as `∑_k c_k(g) e_k(a)`. At `a = x` this shows that every
  translate `x·ψ = ∑_k e_k(x) c_k` lies in the span of the `c_k`.

  Now take `ψ ∈ 𝒜_{P'}`; its orbit maps are smooth. Then condition 2 is
  BL's `ψ ∈ 𝒜_{P'}(μ)` (6.14). The space `𝒜_{P'}` admits the left action of
  `𝔄_{P'}` (BL §6.2, p.201), so `W ⊆ 𝒜_{P'}`. The components `ψ_λ ∈ W`
  above are therefore automorphic, and condition 3 is the decomposition
  (6.3) of `ψ` into generalized eigenvectors in `𝒜_{P'}`. It is also the
  expansion (2) in the proof of MW I.3.2. Without automorphy, condition 1
  does not imply membership in `𝒜_{P'}(μ)`. For `G = P' = 𝔾_m` over `ℚ`, the
  indicator function of the ideles whose `2`-adic component is a unit is
  invariant under `𝔄_{P'} = ℝ_{>0}`. It is not invariant under the
  principal idele `2`, so it is not in `𝒜_{P'}`.
- Why the statement holds. By the reduction of the compact subgroup
  described below, it suffices to treat `φ ∈ 𝒜_P` in BL's sense. Then
  `C_{P,P'}φ` is an automorphic form on `U'(𝔸)M'(F) \ G(𝔸)` (BL §6.2,
  p.201). By MW I.2.17 it is `𝔷_{M'}`-finite for left translation, after the
  twist by the character `m_{P'}^{-ρ_{P'}}`.
  The enveloping algebra `𝔷(A_{M'})` of the Lie algebra of
  `A_{M'} = 𝔄_{P'}` is a subalgebra of `𝔷_{M'}`. So `C_{P,P'}φ` is
  `𝔷(A_{M'})`-finite for left translation: one ideal `I` of finite
  codimension kills it. By MW I.3.1, `I` contains some
  `𝔷(A_{M'}; Λ, N)`. So every orbit map lies in the finite-dimensional
  space spanned by the functions (2) for this `(Λ, N)`, which does not
  depend on `g`. This gives condition 2. It is the route of the proof of
  MW I.3.2, which works with one such special ideal for all `g` and obtains
  the expansion (2) on p.42.
- The parabolics. BL take `P' ⊆ P` standard with respect to a minimal
  parabolic `P_0` with Levi `M_0 ⊇ S_0`. Here they are arbitrary, but `M'`
  contains the maximal split torus `T`. A minimal parabolic `Q_0` of `M'`
  containing `C_G(T)` gives the minimal parabolic `P_0 = Q_0 U'` of `G`. It
  has Levi `C_G(T)` and satisfies `P_0 ⊆ P' ⊆ P`, so `P'` and `P` are
  standard for `(P_0, T)` and `M'` is the standard Levi subgroup of `P'`.
  The Levi `M` of `P` need not contain `M'`, but this does not matter:
  `U(𝔸)M(F) = U(𝔸)P(F)` for every rational Levi `M`, so the space `𝒜_P` and
  the hypothesis on `φ` depend only on `P`.
- The compact subgroup. BL fix a maximal compact subgroup `K_0` of `G(𝔸)`
  in good position with respect to `M_0` (§2.1, p.190, TeX lines 403–405,
  citing MW I.1.4, pp.4–5). Their space `𝒜_P` uses `K_0`-finiteness. Here
  `K` is any maximal compact subgroup with `P'(𝔸)K = G(𝔸)`, so also
  `P(𝔸)K = G(𝔸)`, since `P' ⊆ P`. The form `φ` is assumed finite only under
  the archimedean factor `K_∞` of `K`. Smoothness also makes `φ` right
  invariant under an open subgroup `L` of `G(𝔸_f)`. The source applies after
  a right translation:
  1. `K_∞` is a maximal compact subgroup of `G(𝔸_∞)`
     (`AdelicGroupPoints.IsMaximalCompactSubgroup.isMaximalCompact_comap_ofInfinite`,
     module `Langlands.Global.AdelicMaximalCompactFactors`). So is the
     archimedean factor `K_{0,∞}` of `K_0`. Two such subgroups are conjugate
     (`InfiniteAdelicGroupPoints.infiniteAdelicMaximalCompactConjugacy`,
     module `Langlands.Global.InfiniteAdelicMaximalCompactConjugacy`). So
     `t K_∞ t^{-1} = K_{0,∞}` for some `t ∈ G(𝔸_∞)`.
  2. The function `φ_t(g) = φ(g t)` is then automorphic on
     `U(𝔸)M(F) \ G(𝔸)` for `K_{0,∞}`
     (`IsAutomorphicFunctionOn.infiniteRightTranslate`, module
     `Langlands.Automorphic.GroupSchemeAutomorphicRightTranslation`). It is
     right invariant under `L`. The open subgroup `L ∩ K_{0,f}` of the
     compact finite factor `K_{0,f}` has finite index. So `φ_t` is also
     `K_{0,f}`-finite, hence `K_0`-finite, and `φ_t ∈ 𝒜_P` in BL's sense.
  3. Right translation by `t` commutes with the constant term along `U'`
     and with left translation by `𝔄_{P'}`:
     `C_{U'}φ_t(x g) = C_{U'}φ(x g t)`. So right translation by `t` maps the
     span for `φ` bijectively onto the span for `φ_t`, and finiteness for
     `φ_t` gives finiteness for `φ`.

  This reduction does not use `P'(𝔸)K = G(𝔸)`. That hypothesis gives the
  decomposition `G(𝔸) = U'(𝔸)M'(𝔸)K`, through which MW I.2.17 passes from
  forms on `U'(𝔸)M'(k)\G` to the functions `m ↦ m^{-ρ_{P'}} ψ(m k)` on
  `M'(k)\M'`.
- Use in §8.5. Given this statement, for every `x ∈ 𝔄_{P'}` the span `W`
  is stable under left translation by `x`. So the minimal polynomial `f` of
  that translation on `W` satisfies `∑_k f_k C_{U'}φ(x^k g) = 0` for all `g`.
  This is the form of the Library predicate
  `AdelicGroupPoints.IsTranslationPolynomialSolution ↑x f (subgroupConstantTerm F (iU' ≫ p') φ)`
  (module `Langlands.Global.AdelicLocalFinitenessStatement`). In §8.5 BL
  obtain such annihilating difference operators from their choice of
  `μ_{P'}`.

## Not asserted

- The decomposition itself: no generalized eigenspaces, exponents `μ_{P'}`,
  spaces `𝒫_{𝔄_{P'}}(μ)` or `𝒜_{P'}(μ)`, and no bound on the dimension of
  the span or on the exponents, uniform or otherwise.
- That `C_{P,P'}φ` is an automorphic form on `U'(𝔸)M'(F) \ G(𝔸)`, or any
  property of it besides the finiteness.
- The identification of `positiveCentralTorusPoints T i (l' ≫ p')` with
  `𝔄_0 ∩ S_{M'}(𝔸)`. The module also does not compare it with
  `Langlands.Global.StandardParabolicFamily.positiveSplitComponent` (module
  `Langlands.Global.AdelicLeadingCuspidalComponents`), which describes `𝔄_P`
  for standard parabolics as the positive image of a split component.
- The function field case, and parabolics `P'` not contained in `P` (for
  which BL set `C_{P,P'} = 0`).
