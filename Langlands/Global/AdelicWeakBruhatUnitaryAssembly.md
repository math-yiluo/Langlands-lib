# The unitary part of a continued Bruhat sum

`weakBruhatUnitaryAssembly` identifies the weak constant term with the sum of the continued full-source Bruhat terms under explicit continuation and exponent hypotheses. The full-source terms must agree with the actual Bruhat summands in a regular convergence chamber and be automorphic on the imaginary axis. The remainder is the actual constant term of the given family minus that sum. It must be pointwise meromorphic on a common domain containing the imaginary axis, have finite nonunitary affine exponent support of bounded polynomial degree in the regular chamber, and admit an automorphic exponent decomposition on the imaginary axis.

For the full-source terms, the proof transports each source parameter by its Bruhat Weyl map and restricts the result to the outer target split component. The resulting target parameter gives the exact character value on lifted split points and has zero real part for imaginary source parameters. The normalized character equation persists by meromorphic uniqueness. It gives an exponent with a constant polynomial orbit. The proof groups terms with coincident target parameters by summing their automorphic functions, which gives the required finite unitary decomposition even when labels collide.

For each affine label of the remainder, choose a positive split point that detects the real part of its offset. This choice uses injectivity of complex parameter evaluation on the positive split subgroup applied to the complexification of the real part. At imaginary parameters the linear part has unit-modulus character, so the absolute value of the raw character at the chosen point still differs from the target half modulus. Each translation-minus-character operator raised to one more than the corresponding degree kills that component. The product kills the full remainder and its scalar roots are entire in parameter coordinates. Scalar meromorphic uniqueness extends this equation from the regular chamber to the common holomorphy domain. On the imaginary axis the factors act injectively on the unitary exponent spaces, so the unitary projection of the remainder vanishes. Adding its decomposition to the full-source decomposition proves the weak constant-term identity.

This is the unitary projection step in Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*, Advances in Mathematics 426 (2023), 109107, (4.3)–(4.4). The support premise expresses the nonunitary affine labels used in Lapid, *On Arthur's asymptotic inner product formula of truncated Eisenstein series*, §6, (12)–(13) and Lemma 6.
