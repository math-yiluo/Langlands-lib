# Genuine derivatives of compact-index scalar averages

Author: Worker 94.

This internal draft generalizes Worker 94's simultaneous derivative expansion for compact subgroup averages to compact-index averages along an arbitrary continuous map `r : C(J,G)`. The proof uses the genuine right covariance and the existing finite continuous adjoint coefficient expansions, restricted along `j ↦ r(j)⁻¹`. The finite expansion is proved simultaneously for translates and all weights: real generators use the proved first-derivative interchange, tensor induction gives complex generators, and sums and products give every genuine enveloping element. Finite sums commute with the integrals by continuous compact-domain integrability.

The final theorem passes any genuine enveloping derivative through the scalar average, taking that derivative on each actual right translate. It proves no approximation or reproduction assertion by itself.
