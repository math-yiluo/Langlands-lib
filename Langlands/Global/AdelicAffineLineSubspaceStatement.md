# The standard affine line in adelic projective one-space

`AdelicAffineLineSubspaceStatement` is the affine-line subspace-topology assertion
in Conrad, *Weil and Grothendieck approaches to adelic points* (December 31, 2011),
printed p.10, Example 4.3. Registered source
`conrad-2011-weil-grothendieck-adelic-points`, SHA-256
`fe4a9193aff4ef575665b40971f3da32f850a74d87b3f2d0fa28bba26b9cbabb`.

The map in each clause is the actual standard affine-line point map [1:a] into
the actual polynomial-Proj projective line, evaluated on the original adelic
ring. Its target has the arithmetic adelic topology from the constructed
projective model and actual canonical generic-fiber isomorphism. Its source is
the original adelic carrier with the independently defined topology induced
by its inclusion in the full product of the completed fields.

The target asserts that this actual map is a topological embedding. This includes
its injectivity and equality between the induced projective subspace topology and
the full-product coefficient subspace topology. The source is not given its
ordinary arithmetic restricted-product topology. Neither continuity nor the
subspace comparison is assumed in the input.

Both global-field characteristics, every infinite number-field factor, finite
function-field constants, the specified rational-function action and compatible
scalar tower, potentially inseparable extensions, arbitrary common universe and
every finite initial set are retained. This target does not assert
non-local-compactness of this subspace; the final parenthetical consequence in
the source, rational-point density and the remaining Example 4.3 conclusions are
separate parent obligations.
