# Approximation by adelic convolution

[Left-finite convolution on the rational quotient](AdelicLeftFiniteConvolutionApproximation.md) gives an `L²` approximation to a right translate from `RightTranslationTestFunctionSeparationStatement`.

[Smooth adelic convolution kernels](AdelicSmoothConvolutionApproximation.md) approximate a finite family of continuous functions uniformly on a compact set. The kernels have integral one and can be supported in any identity neighborhood.
