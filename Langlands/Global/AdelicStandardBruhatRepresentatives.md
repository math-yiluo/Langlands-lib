# Rational lifts of the minimal Weyl representatives

For the supplied parabolics in a standard family, the actual rational double cosets correspond to the Weyl double cosets by the rational Bruhat comparison. Every Weyl double coset has a unique minimum-length element. The Weyl comparison sends any rational normalizer lift of that element to the corresponding rational double coset, so any choice of lifts gives the required family of representatives.

The lift for a prescribed minimal Weyl class can therefore be set equal to the original rational normalizer point in the functional equation. For other classes a lift is chosen by surjectivity of the quotient map.
