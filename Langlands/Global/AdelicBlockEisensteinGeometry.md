# Standard block Iwasawa geometry for Eisenstein series

The matrix realization of the block parabolic transports its standard Iwasawa decomposition to the points of the closed block parabolic scheme. The unipotent and Levi inclusions factor through this scheme. The standard compact is the product of the original real orthogonal or complex unitary subgroups and the integral finite matrices. The finite factor is a compact open subgroup of the scheme points, and the infinite factor is the standard compact used in the automorphic form condition. The realized scalar sections are left invariant under the resulting adelic radical and rational Levi subgroup. These comparisons allow the inducing section and Eisenstein module constructions to use the same actual adelic group and compact subgroups.

The geometry is used in the inducing sections of Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), proof of Proposition 2, printed pp. 204–206, Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 199–201, and in the Eisenstein construction of Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series* (2024), §2.2 and §3.1, printed pp. 191–192, article PDF pp. 5–6. The claims here follow from the Library's matrix Iwasawa and scheme point comparisons.
