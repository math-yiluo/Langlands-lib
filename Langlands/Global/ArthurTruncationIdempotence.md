# Pointwise constant-term vanishing and idempotence

`isTruncationIdempotent_of_unfolding_of_weylHeight` combines the geometric-lemma unfolding identity and the uniform Weyl height comparison to prove `IsTruncationIdempotent` for a specified compact Iwasawa subgroup. For the testing standard parabolic, a strictly positive full weight is preserved by left translation by its rational points. The regularity estimate carries it to each point in the Bruhat integrals. Cancellation on each cell proves part (a) of Arthur's proposition. Rational invariance of the first truncation and the fixed-point argument then prove part (b).

The result concerns the hypotheses and boundary in Arthur, *An introduction to the trace formula* (2005), Proposition 13.1(a),(b), pp. 68–69. Its proof uses the cancellation mechanism of Arthur, *A trace formula for reductive groups II* (1980), Lemma 1.1 and Corollary 1.2, pp. 89–91.
