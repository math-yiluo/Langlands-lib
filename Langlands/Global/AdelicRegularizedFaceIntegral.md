# The regularized integral along translated coordinate faces

In Delorme's (7.26), a translated negative cone is partitioned using pieces with a bounded relative split part and a negative face in a Levi. `RegularizedAffineFaceRecombinationStatement` gives the analytic continuation step for such a recombination when it is expressed in common coroot coordinates. It takes finitely many embeddings of face coordinates into the ambient coordinates, continuous base points on compact spaces with finite measures, and a fixed ambient polynomial. It assumes equality of the ordinary translated orthant integral and the sum of the iterated face integrals in the convergence chamber, where the real parts of all ambient spectral coordinates are positive. The measure on each bounded base is supplied with the normalization of this ordinary equality. The conclusion is the same identity for the regularized values where every ambient spectral coordinate is nonzero.

The polynomial is first written in the face variables with coefficients that are themselves polynomials in the base point coordinates. Evaluating those coefficients at a base point gives `translatedFacePolynomial`. Its evaluation in the face variables is the original polynomial at the translated point. The face regularization uses Delorme's one-variable factors (7.8) and the exponential at the base point. On the convergence chamber, the negative orthant integral formula recovers the ordinary iterated integral for each piece.

The fixed coefficient polynomial has finite support before evaluation in the base point. The regularized integrand can be summed over this fixed support, because terms whose evaluated coefficients vanish contribute zero. The resulting formula is holomorphic in the spectral coordinates on the common nonvanishing region and continuous in the base point there. Compactness of the base bounds the integrand on products with compact spectral sets. Holomorphy of parametric integration then gives holomorphy of each regularized face integral. For the original full orthant the base is a singleton with mass one. The common nonvanishing region is the product of punctured complex planes and is path connected. The scalar identity theorem extends the given recombination from the convergence chamber to that region.

See P. Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*, Advances in Mathematics **426** (2023), 109107, §7, pp. 24–25, (7.8)–(7.12), and p. 30, (7.26).
