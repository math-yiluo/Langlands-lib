# Ambient character parameters for Eisenstein series

The first identity uses the Library theorem that the restricted real
characters of a standard Levi annihilate its simple coroots. The
orthogonal lift also annihilates them. Each selected root restricts to
zero on the Levi split component, so its invariant form pairing with the
lift is zero. The coroot formula gives the required vanishing. Both
parameters restrict to the same vector on the split component by the
factorization in the compatible root presentation. Uniqueness of the
lift that annihilates the selected coroots then gives equality.

For the second identity, compose the actual Levi conjugation with the
target torus inclusion. Its image in the ambient group coincides with
the source torus inclusion composed with the inverse torus automorphism.
Cancel the inclusion of the source parabolic and use the identity of the
target retraction on its Levi. On pure tensors the equality is then
functoriality of character restriction, and tensor induction extends it
to all complex parameters.

These arguments compare the ambient embedding of real parabolic
parameters in Bernstein–Lapid §6.1 with the Library's regularity
predicate and express the conjugation parameter of §2.1 in the relative
Weyl action used in §8.6.
