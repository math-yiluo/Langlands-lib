# Second countability of number-field adeles

This internal derived construction concerns the actual coefficient rings in
`Langlands.Global.Adelic`, with their existing restricted-product and product
topologies. It adds no replacement adelic carrier.

For a countable family of second-countable spaces with open restricting subsets,
`restrictedProduct_secondCountableTopology` covers the cofinite restricted
product by the ranges of principal restricted products indexed by finite sets
of exceptional indices. Each principal piece embeds in the countable full
product, and its inclusion into the cofinite restricted product is an open
embedding. The integrality condition on a restricted-product element supplies
its finite exceptional set. Mathlib's countable open cover theorem then gives
second countability.

A number field is countable by finite dimensionality over the rationals.
Its ring of integers is a subtype, and its ideals are generated by finite
sets because that ring is Noetherian. Thus the ideals and finite places are
countable. Density of the number field makes every finite completion
separable; its metric then gives second countability. Each infinite completion
embeds isometrically into the complex numbers and is second countable as well.

The generic restricted-product argument proves
`finiteAdeles_secondCountableTopology`. The finite archimedean product gives
`infiniteAdeles_secondCountableTopology`; their product gives
`adeles_secondCountableTopology`. These results are derived from the actual
Mathlib constructions and elementary countability and topology arguments.
`adelesCountableBasis` then chooses Mathlib's countable basis of nonempty open
sets for the full adele ring. Its countability and topological basis property
are supplied by the two accompanying theorems. The basis is a choice; no
canonical enumeration is asserted.
They do not assert Polishness of an adelic quotient, invariant measures,
reduction theory, or finite volume.
