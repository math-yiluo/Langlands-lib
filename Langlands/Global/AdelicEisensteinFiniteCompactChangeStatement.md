# Finite changes of the adelic Iwasawa compact

Let \(K\) and \(K_2\) be maximal compact subgroups of the full adelic group with Iwasawa coverage for the same parabolic inclusion \(p\). Suppose their infinite projections agree. For every automorphic inducing function on \(U(\mathbb A)M(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A)\), there is a finite family of automorphic inducing functions for the same left quotient, infinite finiteness subgroup and growth representation. For every parameter, the twist using \(K\) is the finite sum of the twists of this family using \(K_2\), multiplied by exponential functions of the parameter. The family is independent of the parameter.

This is a derived change-of-compact identity for the actual Iwasawa parameter twist of Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series*, §2.2, at the level of inducing functions.
