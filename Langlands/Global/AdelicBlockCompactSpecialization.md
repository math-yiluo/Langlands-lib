# Compact lifts in the smooth adelic block model

The smooth block sections are finite under the infinite standard compact action. On each compact orbit span, finitely many adelic point evaluations give linear coordinates, and these evaluations vary continuously under compact translation. Compact complete reducibility therefore applies to the original action on the full section space.

Given an invariant numerator and a simple quotient at zero, joint admissibility gives a finite-rank projection to a joint compact type with a nonzero vector in the quotient. The finite integral and infinite compact operators are constant over the parameter space, so the uniform projection on finite-dimensional test spaces agrees with the operator family used in the Eisenstein specialization. The analytic hypotheses are exact continuation and convergence-region injectivity on finite-dimensional projected ranges. The conclusion is a surjective map from an invariant numerator of stable automorphic functions for the full free operator algebra.

The construction implements the compact projection in the proof of Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Proposition 2, printed pp. 205–207, Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 200–202.
