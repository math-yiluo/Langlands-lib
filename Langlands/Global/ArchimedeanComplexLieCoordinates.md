# Complex matrix factors of the actual archimedean Lie algebra

`complexLieProductMatrixCoordinates` identifies the complexification of the real archimedean Lie algebra with a finite product of complex matrix Lie algebras. The real Lie algebra already decomposes by the real and complex infinite places. Tensoring with the complex numbers commutes with those finite products. Each real matrix factor becomes a complex matrix factor, and each complex matrix factor, viewed over the reals before complexification, gives two complex factors.

The bracket comparison is pointwise. Each tensor projection is the scalar extension of the actual Lie projection to its place. On a simple tensor the real-place formula uses the ordinary scalar inclusion, and the two complex-place components use the matrix and its entrywise conjugate.

This derived coordinate comparison lets the block enveloping projection be applied to the actual archimedean action on constant terms, as used in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Lemma 4, printed p. 204.
