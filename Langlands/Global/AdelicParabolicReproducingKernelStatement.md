# Harish-Chandra's reproducing kernel on parabolic quotients

Module `Langlands.Global.AdelicParabolicReproducingKernelStatement` states the proposition
`Langlands.Global.ParabolicReproducingKernelStatement` (universe `u`) and proves nothing.

## Statement

The data and hypotheses are those of `CuspidalProjectionExistenceStatement` (module
`Langlands.Global.AdelicCuspidalProjectionExistenceStatement`): a number field `F`, an affine
group scheme `G` over `Spec F`, locally of finite type, with a compatible standard parabolic family
`fam : StandardParabolicFamily F G`; a maximal compact subgroup `K` of `G(𝔸)` with
`P₀(𝔸) K = G(𝔸)` for the minimal standard parabolic and with maximal compact inverse images in
all standard Levi subgroups; a closed matrix embedding `rho`, a smooth structure `hsmooth`, and
`Kinf = K ∩ G(F_∞)` (`K.comap (ofInfinite F G)`).

For every standard index `Q` and every `φ : G(𝔸) → ℂ` with
`fam.IsAutomorphicOn hsmooth Kinf rho Q φ`, there is `α : G(𝔸) → ℂ` such that

- `α` is a smooth compactly supported test function (`IsAdelicTestFunction F G α`, module
  `Langlands.Global.AdelicLocalFinitenessStatement`: continuous, with plot-smooth archimedean
  slices, compactly supported, and invariant on the right and on the left under open subgroups
  of `G(𝔸_f)`);
- the left translates `h ↦ α(k h)`, `k ∈ Kinf`, span a finite-dimensional space;
- `rightConvolution F G α φ = φ`, that is `∫ α(a) φ(g a) da = φ(g)` for every `g ∈ G(𝔸)`, for
  the Haar measure `Measure.haar` of `G(𝔸)`.

## Sources

- C. Mœglin and J.-L. Waldspurger, *Spectral Decomposition and Eisenstein Series* (1995),
  I.2.17, Lemma, p. 38: for finitely many standard parabolic subgroups `P_i = M_i U_i` and
  finite-dimensional spaces `V_i` of automorphic forms on `U_i(𝔸) M_i(k) \ G`, there is a smooth,
  compactly supported, right and left `K`-finite function `f` on `G` with `δ(f)φ = φ` for every
  `φ ∈ V_i`; here `δ(f)φ(g) = ∫ f(h) φ(gh) dh` (I.2.5, pp. 26–27), which is `rightConvolution`.
  The proposition is the case of one parabolic `P_1 = Q` and `V_1 = ℂφ`, keeping the left
  finiteness of the kernel under `K_∞`.
- Harish-Chandra, *Discrete series for semisimple Lie groups II*, Acta Math. 116 (1966), §8,
  Theorem 1, p. 18, which Mœglin–Waldspurger cite for the Lemma: for a connected real reductive
  group with maximal compact subgroup `K`, a smooth function `f` with values in a
  finite-dimensional space, whose transforms by the algebra generated by the centre of the
  enveloping algebra and the Lie algebra of `K` span a finite-dimensional space, satisfies
  `f * α = f` for a smooth compactly supported `α` invariant under conjugation by `K` and
  supported in any given neighbourhood of `1`.

## Correspondence

The Library's `AdelicReproducingKernelStatement` (module
`Langlands.Global.AdelicReproducingKernelStatement`) is the analogous assertion for left
`G(F)`-invariant functions without growth condition, with a kernel that is only continuous and
compactly supported. The present proposition concerns functions on `U_Q(𝔸) M_Q(F) \ G(𝔸)`, uses
the automorphy predicate of `StandardParabolicFamily.IsAutomorphicOn`, and asks for a smooth test
kernel with finite left `K_∞`-span, as in the Lemma of Mœglin–Waldspurger.

The derivation from Harish-Chandra's theorem runs as in that module. The compact `K` is the
product of `K_∞` and its finite part, and `K_∞` is maximal compact in `G(F_∞)`. An open compact
subgroup `L` of `G(𝔸_f)` fixes `φ` on the right; the archimedean slices of `φ` are smooth,
`K_∞`-finite with types in one finite set and annihilated by one ideal of finite codimension of
the centre `𝔷`. Left invariance under `U_Q(𝔸) M_Q(F)` makes the slice at `γ_f u b l`
(`γ ∈ M_Q(F)`, `u ∈ U_Q(𝔸_f)`, `l ∈ L`) the function `h ↦ φ_b(γ_∞⁻¹ h)`, where `φ_b` is the
slice at `b`; right convolution commutes with such left translations. The double coset space
`M_Q(F) U_Q(𝔸_f) \ G(𝔸_f) / L` is finite, by `G(𝔸_f) = P_Q(𝔸_f) K_f` and the finiteness of the
class numbers of `M_Q`. Harish-Chandra's theorem, applied on the identity
component of `G(F_∞)` to finitely many slices and their translates by representatives of the
components in `K_∞`, gives a kernel `α_∞` on `G(F_∞)` reproducing every slice; `α` is `α_∞`
times a multiple of the indicator function of `L`. Replacing `α_∞` by
`a ↦ ∫_{K_∞} e(k) α_∞(k⁻¹ a) dk`, where `e` is the sum of `dim τ · \overline{χ_τ}` over the
finitely many `K_∞`-types `τ` of `φ`, gives a kernel with finite-dimensional left `K_∞`-span that
still reproduces `φ`, since right convolution by it is the composite of right convolution by
`α_∞` and the projection onto those types.
