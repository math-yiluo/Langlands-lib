# Convergence-region injectivity for smooth block sections

`BlockEisensteinInjectivityReduction` applies meromorphic continuation and generic injectivity to the actual smooth adelic block model. The proof restricts the injective evaluation on scheme points to a finite-dimensional projection range. Its sections are automorphic on the parabolic quotient by joint admissibility of the inducing blocks. The finite-dimensional injectivity lemma then supplies the eventual convergence-region injectivity on this range.

The reduction assumes meromorphic continuation and generic injectivity as stated in Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series* (2024), Theorem 2.3(1), p. 191 (PDF p. 5), Corollary 8.6, p. 226 (PDF p. 40), and formula (8.4), p. 225 (PDF p. 39).
