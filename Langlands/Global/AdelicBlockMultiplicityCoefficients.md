# Grouping the actual finite and infinite Levi coefficients

The tensor interchange pairs each finite factor with its infinite multiplicity space. Evaluating each pair in the inducing realization and then taking the scalar Levi coefficient gives the grouped coefficient map. The actual multiplicity equivalences make this map bijective when the finite factors are nonzero, irreducible and admissible and the full inducing realizations are irreducible.

The scalar Levi coefficient differential is the sum of the projected enveloping generator in each block. Each multiplicity evaluation intertwines that generator acting on the infinite factor. Thus the grouped differential leaves the finite factor fixed and acts on the infinite multiplicity tensor.

Finite Levi translation acts on the finite tensor. Compact infinite Levi translation acts on the multiplicity tensor. Both comparisons follow by applying the tensor interchange to the slotwise evaluation identities and then using the scalar coefficient translation formulas on the adelic Levi.

This is the coefficient comparison for the original cuspidal induction in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Lemma 1 and Proposition 2, printed pp. 203–205, Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 198–200.
