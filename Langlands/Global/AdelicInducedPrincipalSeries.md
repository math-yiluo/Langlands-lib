# Principal-series covariance of adelic induced-section coordinates

For a finite ordered list of positive block sizes, `orderedBlockShape` uses their sum as the ambient rank. `borelParabolic` puts a diagonal matrix times an upper unipotent matrix into the corresponding block parabolic. Its Levi projection has the same form in each diagonal block. `borelBlockUnipotent` is the upper unipotent factor in that block. Since it has determinant one, the block determinant depends only on the restriction of the diagonal matrix.

The two local membership lemmas apply to an actual normalized adelic parabolic section. They evaluate the section at \(y\iota_v(g)\) and apply a continuous linear functional on the coefficient space. They assume that \(y\) commutes with the embedded local parabolic and that the functional transforms under the projected local diagonal and upper unipotent factors by the product of the normalized block characters. Section covariance then gives the outer determinant half-character times this product. The computation in `BlockPrincipalSeriesNormalization` identifies it with the full normalized principal-series character for the concatenated position characters. The real comparison uses the absolute determinant and the complex comparison uses the squared norm of the determinant.

This is an internal induction-in-stages calculation for the archimedean part of the proof of `CuspidalSupportEveryPlaceStatement`. It uses the existing adelic covariance, place embeddings and smooth scalar slices. The real Fourier conditions for standard-module membership and the construction of suitable coefficient functionals are separate mathematical steps.
