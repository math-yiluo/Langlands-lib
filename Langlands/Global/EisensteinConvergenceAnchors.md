# Common open convergence anchors for finite holomorphic families

`convergenceAnchorFilter` uses intersections of sufficiently regular regions with open dense parameter sets. The regions decrease with the threshold. The maximum of two thresholds and the intersection of two open dense sets give a common member. When all the regular regions are open and nonempty, each eventual identity holds on a nonempty open set. The local-to-global density lemma takes the interior of the full locus and shows that it contains the regular points from each supplied local window.

`sectionOutput` extends evaluation at each parameter from a submodule of sections to all sections by zero. If every constant section eventually belongs to the evaluation domain, a finite holomorphic expansion gives the same property for the corresponding family. `sectionCovarianceAlgebra` contains the pairs of section and output operators that preserve these expansions and commute with evaluation eventually. The proof uses linearity on the common evaluation domain for sums and complex scalars. For composition it applies the first operator identity to the family obtained by applying the second operator, and intersects the two eventual conditions. `mem_sectionCovarianceAlgebra_of_const` uses the common evaluation domain of the finitely many constant coefficients and their images to check such commutation on constant sections.

This internal construction prepares the operator identity on an open convergence region in Langlands' proof of Proposition 2, printed pp. 205–207 (Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 200–202). Regularity thresholds arise from absolute convergence. The open dense sets come from the local Fréchet-valued continuations described by Bernstein–Lapid, Section 3.1, printed p. 192 (PDF p. 6).
