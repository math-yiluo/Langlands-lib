# The parabolic criterion from norm-one cuspidal separation

`normOneCuspidalVanishingReduction` proves that this analytic assertion implies the exact `ParabolicCuspidalVanishingStatement`. The constant-term induction reduces to a relatively cuspidal function on a single parabolic quotient. Each Levi fibre is continuous, rationally invariant, moderate for the restricted representation and intrinsically cuspidal by `leviFibreCuspidality`. The closed Levi embedding restricts the compact subgroup and the matrix representation. The fibre pairing at a Levi translate `a` is precisely the published ambient pairing at the ambient translate obtained by multiplying by the image of `a`. Applying norm-one separation at the identity of the fibre gives the original function value at the chosen ambient point.

The reduction assumes `NormOneCuspidalSeparationStatement`. It retains the hypotheses and conclusion of the published parabolic criterion.
