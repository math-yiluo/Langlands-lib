# Continuation of the compact comparison

The statement takes the compact comparison on a nonempty open source patch.
There the continued source series for \(K\) agrees with the weighted sum of
continued series for \(L\). On the same patch the finite intertwining
expansions agree after twisting each with its own target compact and the
transported parameter. The coefficient functions and the continued source
series have a common holomorphy domain in a complex linear chart.

For each adelic argument the exponential height factor is entire in the
chart parameter. Thus both source expressions, and both finite sums of
target-twisted functions, are scalar meromorphic functions of the parameter.
The two equalities continue separately by the several-variable identity
theorem. At any point of the common domain where the target series are
summable, the continued equality of twists identifies the weighted sums of
target series term by term. If the continued source series for \(L\) satisfy
their functional equations there, substitution gives the comparison for
\(K\) at that point.

This is a derived compact transport step for Bernstein and Lapid,
*On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series* (2024), §2.2,
journal p. 191, equation (2.1) and the displayed intertwining integral.
It uses the continued operator values via their finite scalar expansions.
