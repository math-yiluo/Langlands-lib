# Cuspidal projections and their exponent decompositions

For a compatible standard family over a number field, take a maximal compact subgroup with minimal-parabolic Iwasawa coverage and maximal compact intersections in the standard Levis. If \(Q\subset P\) are in the family and \(\phi\) is automorphic on \(U_P(\mathbb A)M_P(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A)\), the constant term along \(Q\) has a cuspidal projection characterized by the norm-one Levi pairings. The projection decomposes into finitely many cuspidal automorphic functions with unnormalized generalized exponents.

This combines the constant term and cuspidal projection in Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series*, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 37 (2024), §6.2, pp. 201–202, with the unnormalized exponent decomposition in §6.3, p. 203. The formal pairing follows equation (6.8) and includes absolute convergence for every test Levi cusp form and adelic argument.
