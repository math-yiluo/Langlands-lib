# Infinite compact translation of scalar sections built from compact tensors

`compactTensorSectionIntertwiner` assumes that evaluation of the infinite factor intertwines its compact action with right translation on the infinite compact group. Multiplication by an infinite compact element changes the infinite coordinate of a standard compact element and preserves its finite coordinate. The normalized compact evaluations therefore intertwine this action on pure tensors. Tensor extensionality proves the equality on the whole source.

`compactTensorFiniteSmoothIntertwiner` restricts the codomain when all image sections have an open finite stabilizer. If all infinite source vectors have finite-dimensional compact orbit span, `compactFiniteTensorSectionIntertwiner` further restricts to the compact-finite scalar carrier. Fixing the finite factor of a pure tensor gives a compact intertwiner from the infinite source because the compact action on the finite factor is trivial. The finite-vector subspace therefore contains all pure tensors and their linear combinations. The section intertwiner transfers compact finiteness to its image.

This is an internal comparison with the realized scalar compact picture. Getz and Hahn (2024), §10.1, pp. 270–271 (PDF 282–283), describe the standard compact factor and the scalar Levi slices.
