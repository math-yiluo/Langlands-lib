# Adelic Iwasawa decomposition with a prescribed infinite compact

`IwasawaWithInfiniteCompactStatement` lets one use a given maximal compact subgroup
of the actual infinite adelic point group as the infinite part of an adelic Iwasawa
subgroup for a rational parabolic of a connected reductive group.

The adelic existence result follows from the real and complex local decomposition,
the finite-place decomposition, and the almost-everywhere integral family. The
initial infinite compact is conjugate to the prescribed one. Write a conjugating
point as a parabolic point times a compact point and project to the infinite factor.
The compact factor normalizes the initial infinite compact. Conjugating the adelic
compact by the remaining parabolic factor preserves the covering of the adelic group
and gives the prescribed infinite factor.

The factorization uses the Iwasawa decomposition of Getz–Hahn, *An Introduction to
Automorphic Representations*, Theorem 2.7.1, with the prescribed factor obtained by
conjugation in the actual point groups.
