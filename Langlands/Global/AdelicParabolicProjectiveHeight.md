# Projective embeddings of actual rational parabolic cosets

Suppose an actual finite matrix representation has a coordinate line whose
exact group-scheme stabilizer is the given parabolic. Then
`parabolicRationalProjectiveEmbedding` sends a rational right coset to the
projective class of the inverse representative acting on that line.
Equality of projective classes is unit-scalar equality of the nonzero orbit
vectors. Applying the second representative to this equality gives the
unit-scalar stabilizer equation for the quotient of the representatives.
The affine point comparison and injectivity of principal rational points
identify that equation with the parabolic right-coset relation. This proves
well-definedness and injectivity.

The actual parabolic coordinate-line character acts by the required adelic
scalar on the principal coordinate vector. The inverse-orbit height formula
therefore bounds its Iwasawa evaluation from above by a uniform constant
minus the logarithmic projective height of this embedding.

The construction is derived from the published exact coordinate-line
stabilizer and rational orbit point interfaces. Chevalley's line stabilizer
theorem supplies the initial representation for a closed parabolic. This
embedding allows height sums over projective space to bound the rational
coset sums in Mœglin–Waldspurger II.1.5.
