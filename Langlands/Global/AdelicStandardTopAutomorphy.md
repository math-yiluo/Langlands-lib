# Rational automorphy at the full standard index

At the full set of simple roots, the standard parabolic represents
the entire ambient group. The original parabolic morphism and its
Levi inclusion are therefore isomorphisms. Reductivity makes the
unipotent radical trivial on every test scheme. The Levi isomorphism
identifies its principal rational image with the original rational
subgroup of the ambient group.

Rewriting this equality identifies the two automorphy predicates.
The result is an internal consequence of the Library's endpoint
and Levi theorems. It allows the parabolic cuspidal projection
and leading-component statements to be applied to global functions.
