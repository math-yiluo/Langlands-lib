# Cofinal actual curve S-integers as principal chart localizations

This derived construction supplies an arithmetic step toward the integral
Weil model in Conrad, *Weil and Grothendieck approaches to adelic points* (2011),
Example 4.2, printed pages 9–10. Work in the original function field over finite
constants, with a parameter s such that the original field is finite over the
rational subfield generated by s. The normalized parameter chart is the actual
`CurveModel.chartRing`, already proved finite and Dedekind without separability.

`curvePlacePrincipalExceptional` is the finite union of all actual places at
which s is not integral and all actual places at which the inverse of a given
denominator is not integral. The finite-poles theorem constructs both finite
sets. For a nonzero denominator t in the normalized chart,
`curvePlacePrincipalSIntegersEquiv` identifies the actual integer subring outside
this exceptional set with the actual localization of the normalized chart at
powers of t inside the original field. The ring equivalence fixes every
underlying field element.

For the comparison, every nonzero chart prime gives an original curve place
where s is integral. If t is outside that prime, its valuation is one, so its
inverse is integral there too. The original outside-set integer condition
therefore gives all chart-prime tests required by
`mem_dedekindAwaySubalgebra_iff`. Conversely, a localization fraction has numerator
in the chart and denominator a power of t. At a place outside the exceptional
set the parameter and the inverse of t are integral; chart integrality and
closure under powers and products give integrality of the fraction.

`exists_curvePlacePrincipalExceptional_superset` proves that these constructed
exceptional sets are cofinal among all finite sets of original curve places.
For each prescribed place where s is integral, the corresponding nonzero chart
prime contains a nonzero chart element. Choose one such element for each place,
and use one at places where s is not integral. Their finite product d is nonzero.
At every prescribed place still on the parameter chart, the product belongs to
the center prime and is thus a nonunit in its valuation ring. Its inverse is not
integral there. The other prescribed places already occur among the parameter
poles. This proves containment of the entire prescribed set in the constructed
principal exceptional set; no cofinality or approximation premise is assumed.

The constant field and function field inhabit an arbitrary common universe.
Finite constants, the lower function-field presentation, and finiteness over the
specified parameter subfield are explicit. No separability assumption on that
presentation or on any later original field extension is imposed. The initial
finite exceptional set may be empty. Finite normalization and finite local
freeness for this cofinal family, its integral Weil generic fiber, and the adelic
topological comparison remain obligations of the host task.
