# Fundamental domains and Haar measures of topological semidirect products

Module `Langlands.Global.EisensteinConstantTerm.UnfoldingSemidirect`, namespace
`Langlands.Global.LeftCosetSpace.Semidirect`. It extends the semidirect-product toolbox of
`Langlands.Global.LeftCosetSemidirectFiniteVolume`: homomorphisms `i : N → H`, `j : M → H` and a
retraction `p : H → M` with `p ∘ j = id`, `p ∘ i = 1` and kernel `i(N)`, so that `H` is the
semidirect product `i(N) ⋊ j(M)`. In the topological results `i` is a topological embedding and
`j`, `p` are continuous, and `prodHomeomorph : N × M ≃ₜ H`, `(n, m) ↦ i n * j m`, is the resulting
parametrization; the groups are locally compact, second countable and Borel.

## Results

- `prodTransversal i j p … tN tM`: the set of `h` with `normalPart h ∈ tN` and `p h ∈ tM`,
  that is the image of `tN × tM`. `prodTransversal_unique`: if `Λ ≤ H` satisfies
  `j (p λ) ∈ Λ` for `λ ∈ Λ` (so `Λ` is the semidirect product of its intersections with `i(N)` and
  `j(M)`), and `tN`, `tM` are transversals of `i⁻¹(Λ)` and `j⁻¹(Λ)`, then `prodTransversal` is a
  transversal of `Λ`. The proof writes `λ = i κ * j α` and uses
  `i κ * j α * (i n * j m) = i (κ * α n α⁻¹) * j (α m)`.
- `mulEquivHaarChar_conj_eq_one`: if a discrete subgroup `ΓM ≤ M` of finite covolume is carried
  into a discrete `Γ ≤ H` and one compact set of `N` meets every orbit of `i⁻¹(Γ)`, then
  conjugation by every element of `M` preserves Haar measure on `N`. It assembles the Library's
  `eq_one_of_measurable_of_isFiniteMeasure`, `measurable_mulEquivHaarChar_conj`,
  `mulEquivHaarChar_conj_mul` and `mulEquivHaarChar_conj_eq_one_of_mem`.
- `smul_haar_eq_map_prod`: suppose conjugation by every element of `M` preserves Haar measure on
  `N` (the conclusion of the previous item). Let `Λ ≤ H` be countable with `j (p λ) ∈ Λ` for every
  `λ ∈ Λ`, and let `D ⊆ H`, `tN ⊆ N`, `tM ⊆ M` be measurable transversals for the left actions of
  `Λ`, `i⁻¹(Λ)` and `j⁻¹(Λ)`. If `a • haar`, `b • haar`, `d • haar` give mass one to `D`, `tN`,
  `tM` respectively, then `a • haar = map (prodHomeomorph) ((b • haar).prod (d • haar))`. The
  Library's `exists_haar_eq_smul_map_prod` gives the identity up to a constant, which the masses
  of the transversal `prodTransversal` and of `D` determine. Without the compatibility
  `j (p λ) ∈ Λ` the conclusion can fail: in `ℝ × ℝ`, the lattice generated by `ℤ²` and
  `(1/2, 1/2)` meets both factors in `ℤ`, but has covolume `1/2`.
- `setIntegral_transversal_eq_prod`, `setLIntegral_transversal_eq_prod`: let `μN`, `μM` be
  s-finite and `μH = map (prodHomeomorph) (μN.prod μM)` left invariant. Let `Λ' ≤ H` be countable
  with `j (p λ) ∈ Λ'` for every `λ ∈ Λ'`, and let `D`, `tN`, `tM` be measurable transversals of
  `Λ'`, `i⁻¹(Λ')`, `j⁻¹(Λ')`. Then for a left `Λ'`-invariant function `f` (vector-valued for the
  Bochner form, `ℝ≥0∞`-valued for the nonnegative form), the integral of `f` over `D` is the
  integral of `f (i n * j m)` over `tN ×ˢ tM` against `μN.prod μM`.
- `setIntegral_transversal_eq_of_invariant`: under the same hypotheses, if moreover
  `f (i n * h) = f h` for all `n`, `h`, the integral of `f` over `D` is
  `μN(tN) • ∫_{m ∈ tM} f (j m) dμM`.

## Provenance
