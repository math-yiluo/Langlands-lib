# Uniform Levi restriction of center derivatives

`infiniteLeviIdentitySlice` pulls back a plot-smooth function to the actual infinite Levi subgroup. An unnormalized center restriction at the identity says that for each ambient central operator \(z\), one Levi enveloping operator \(y\) gives the same derivative after this pullback, for every smooth function left invariant under the infinite unipotent radical. `hasUniformLeviCenterTransfer_of_identity` extends this identity to the infinite Levi fibre through any adelic point.

On the infinite plot-smooth carrier, a right translation by \(a\) transforms the actual enveloping operator by \(\operatorname{Ad}(a)\). The proof starts with the invariant directional derivative, uses linearity to complexify, and extends through the tensor algebra. If the adjoint action fixes the ambient center, every central operator commutes with right translation on this carrier. For an adelic point \(x=(a,b)\), the fibre through \(x\) is the identity restriction of the right translate by \(a\) of the slice at finite coordinate \(b\). That translate is left invariant under the infinite radical because the original adelic function is left invariant under the full adelic radical. Applying the identity restriction and the central commutation formula to the translate gives the same Levi operator \(y\) for every fibre.

The result uses the unnormalized parabolic center restriction. It gives the uniformity in the base point used in the fixed inducing-space argument of Mœglin–Waldspurger, *Spectral Decomposition and Eisenstein Series* (1995), I.2.17, p. 38 and I.4.5, pp. 60–62. The argument here derives the uniform transfer from the stated identity restriction and adjoint centrality.
