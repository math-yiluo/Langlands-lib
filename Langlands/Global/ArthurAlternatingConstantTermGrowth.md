# Uniform directional growth of Arthur's alternating constant terms

A finite-plot-smooth rationally invariant function has a common open
finite stabilizer and smooth archimedean slices. Its uniform moderate
growth gives a common nonnegative exponent for the normalized adelic
height, through the identification of a directional word with the
corresponding ordered product in the enveloping algebra.

Normalized unipotent constant terms preserve smoothness, the given
finite level, and the exponent for all directional words. Linearity of
directional words on finite sums of smooth slices and the triangle
inequality pass the same properties to the signed interval sum. This
supplies the derivative bounds for the alternating terms of Arthur
2005, §13, equation (13.7).
