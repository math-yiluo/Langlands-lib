# Restricted tensors in finite adelic block induction

`BlockInducedReferences` specifies local block coefficient vectors and induced sections at every finite place. Outside a common finite exceptional set, the sections are right integral-fixed and evaluate on the local integral group to the pure tensors of the specified coefficient vectors. `parabolicAction` uses the actual projections of a finite adelic parabolic element to the local parabolics. The local normalized inducing actions preserve the reference coefficients outside a finite set because the block Levi entries are integral there and the local half modulus is one.

`pointwiseInducedMap` maps the restricted tensor of the canonical local induced sections into the actual smooth induction from the finite adelic block parabolic acting on the restricted coefficient tensor. At an adelic matrix, the map uses the restricted tensor map of its local point evaluations. The local evaluations preserve the references at every place where the adelic matrix is integral and the place lies outside the exceptional set. The composition law for restricted tensor maps gives parabolic covariance and commutation with actual finite adelic right translation. A single open stabilizer for a source vector then fixes its image, giving the smooth section.

`pointwiseInducedMap_injective` assumes that the pure coefficient references are nonzero outside the exceptional set. The standard integral adelic group is the product of the local integral groups. On that group the map agrees with `pointwiseEvaluation`, and local Iwasawa decomposition makes each family of compact evaluations jointly faithful. Restricted tensor evaluation injectivity therefore gives the result.

This is an internal construction using the normalized local covariance of Getz and Hahn (2024), §8.1, p. 213 (PDF 226), and the restricted tensor maps of Flath (1979), §2, pp. 180–181 (PDF 176–177). The coefficient action here is specified by the normalized local actions.
