# Whittaker uniqueness for the finite adelic factor

The functional condition uses the actual finite adelic insertion of
each local upper unipotent group and the multiplier from a prescribed
nontrivial local additive character. The comparison applies to every
irreducible admissible complex representation of the finite adelic
general linear group, with the field and carrier universes free.

Flath factorization gives an algebraic restricted tensor product of
irreducible admissible local factors. The equivariance of the
factorization transports the two functionals to that product. Local
insertion acts on the corresponding slot at every stage containing
the place. Each slot functional therefore satisfies local Whittaker
covariance, and the published local theorem gives the one-dimensional
comparison. Proportionality on the restricted product descends to
the original representation by the same equivalence.

This is the finite-place comparison used with the archimedean
continuous comparison in Shalika (1974), Theorem 5.5.
