# Density for the finite adelic tensor

`Langlands.Global.FiniteAdelicGL.tensorTranslationAlgebra` is the subalgebra of
linear endomorphisms generated by translations on the finite adelic restricted
tensor. The theorem `tensorRepresentation_exists_eq_on` shows that an
operator of this subalgebra agrees with any given linear endomorphism on a
prescribed finite set. The hypotheses are irreducibility and admissibility of
every local factor and the fixed, nonzero reference vectors outside the given
exceptional set.

Put the supplied finite set of vectors and their images under the given
endomorphism in a common finite tensor stage. The stage embeds in the
restricted tensor, so a linear left inverse defines an endomorphism of that
stage with the prescribed values. The finite external tensor is irreducible
and admissible by the finite tensor theorems. Admissible Schur and Jacobson
density give a group algebra element with the required values on the stage.
Extend each group element in this expression by the identity in the other
places. The resulting adelic operator has the same action on the embedded
stage.

This is an internal algebraic consequence of the Library's restricted tensor
construction and admissible Schur lemma. It supplies the grouped finite
factor for the mixed tensor of finite and archimedean constituents used in
the assembly of Getz–Hahn (2024), Theorem 10.7.5, p. 288 (PDF p. 300).
