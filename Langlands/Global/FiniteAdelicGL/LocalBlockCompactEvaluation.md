# Compact evaluations for normalized local block induction

`localBlockCompactEvaluation` evaluates the canonical normalized smooth block induction at an element of the actual local integral matrix group. The local Iwasawa decomposition puts every matrix in the product of the block parabolic and that integral group. Consequently, the compact evaluations distinguish induced sections by parabolic covariance. `localBlockCompactEvaluation_rightTranslate` evaluates a right translate by factoring the translated argument and applying the canonical positive half modulus and Levi action.

The Levi projection of an integral parabolic matrix has integral blocks. The diagonal block of the inverse is the inverse of the diagonal block, so both integrality conditions follow from the corresponding conditions on the full matrix. Conversely, the block diagonal embedding of a tuple of integral blocks is integral. The canonical half modulus is one on the integral parabolic intersection, as each block determinant has normalized absolute value one. These facts identify the intersection's coefficient action with the tensor of the block actions.

`exists_localBlockReference` extends a pure tensor of integral-fixed block vectors to a right integral-fixed induced section. The construction uses the published extension from a coefficient fixed by the parabolic intersection and the equality of the completion and canonical matrix topologies. The resulting section takes the prescribed value everywhere on the integral group. If the block fixed spaces are lines, `exists_smul_localBlockReference_eq` shows that any integral-fixed section is a scalar multiple of this reference. Evaluation at the identity is fixed by the product of the block integral subgroups. The published fixed-vector tensor inclusion places this coefficient in the line spanned by the pure reference tensor. The compact evaluations then determine the entire section.

These are internal consequences of the canonical local induction model, the integral Iwasawa decomposition and the finite tensor fixed-vector lemma. Getz and Hahn (2024), §8.1, p. 213 (PDF 226), give the positive normalized covariance convention used by the local induction.
