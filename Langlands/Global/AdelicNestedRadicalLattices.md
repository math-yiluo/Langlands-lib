# Adelic coordinates and principal lattices for nested radicals

Given the split extension for two nested standard radicals, transport its three maps through the isomorphisms from adelic points to their represented adelic ranges. The normal and complementary maps are inclusions on ambient points. The scheme kernel identity and section identity give the corresponding adelic identities.

The principal lattices are the principal images of the represented groups. The complement and projection preserve these lattices by naturality of principal points. A principal point of the larger adelic radical that lies in the smaller closed radical comes from a rational point of that radical by descent along the diagonal coefficient extension. The proof also obtains compactness of the complementary principal quotient using smoothness and connectedness of closed unipotent subgroups over the number field.
