# Automorphy of pointwise Eisenstein continuations

`eisensteinPointwiseContinuationAutomorphyReduction` proves the published
implication using the local Fréchet-valued continuation as its premise.
That continuation needs a positive-size matrix embedding. The proof
uses the given representation when its size is positive. In size zero
the underlying group is trivial, and its size-one trivial representation
gives the same automorphy condition. Thus one may apply the published
continuation and transfer the resulting automorphy assertion back to
the given embedding.

The maximum of the two initial agreement thresholds gives a region
where both continuations equal the same series. The continuation
comparison builds one open dense set from the local Fréchet-valued
data in the given chart. The values there are automorphic, and
scalar uniqueness identifies them with the supplied continuation on
the intersection with its holomorphy domain. The intersection is
open and dense, as required.

The proof is derived from the meromorphy predicate of Bernstein–Lapid
§3.1 and the local Fréchet-valued continuation and automorphy in their
Theorem 2.3 (1) and the corollary in §8.6.
