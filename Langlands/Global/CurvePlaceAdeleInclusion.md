# Injectivity and level compatibility of the published curve adele inclusion

Worker 208 derives new comparisons for the published `curvePlaceAdeleInclusion k K L` from `Langlands.Global.AdelicTensorBaseChange`. That existing construction, its coordinate formula, continuity and full-field diagonal compatibility are imported and reused without redeclaration. This unit supplies additional arithmetic infrastructure for Conrad's Example 4.2, printed pp.9–10, in *Weil and Grothendieck approaches to adelic points* (December 31, 2011), registered as `conrad-2011-weil-grothendieck-adelic-points`, SHA-256 `fe4a9193aff4ef575665b40971f3da32f850a74d87b3f2d0fa28bba26b9cbabb`.

The fields k, K, and L lie in a common arbitrary universe, with a compatible tower k → K → L and an arbitrary finite extension L/K. No separability, perfectness or finiteness assumption on the constant field is imposed. The local wrapper `curvePlaceCompletionInclusion` is the original continuous completion map at an upper place q whose lower place is its actual restriction. It preserves and reflects the completed valuation rings and extends the original field embedding, by the published local theorems.

The published full adelic inclusion is the actual map of cofinite restricted products assembled from the original completion embeddings along the finite-fiber place restriction. Its definition, existing coordinate formula and existing restricted-product continuity are reused. The new above-place formula writes its coordinate with any specified original lower place p and an actual upper place over p, retaining the dependent original completed fields. Injectivity follows by choosing an upper extension of each lower place and using injectivity of that completion embedding.

For every finite lower exceptional set S, the new level-integrality equivalence says that the image belongs to the level outside its full upper preimage exactly when the lower adele belongs to the level outside S. The forward implication uses surjectivity of restriction and reflection of local integrality; the reverse implication uses preservation at every upper place. `curvePlaceAdeleLevelInclusion` restricts the published full inclusion to a continuous homomorphism of these two actual level rings with their inherited topologies. Its coercion is the published full inclusion. The integer-diagonal formula proves compatibility with the original outside-S integer extension and both level diagonals, coordinatewise by compatibility of the original field maps.

The existing full-field diagonal theorem in `AdelicTensorBaseChange` remains available under its original perfect-constant-field and specified function-field presentation hypotheses. This unit does not impose those extra assumptions on its new comparisons. Full integral-model counit and original-field point-space comparisons remain separate units.
