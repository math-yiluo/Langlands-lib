# The Levi transfer of the center from the normalized Harish-Chandra congruence

Module `Langlands.Global.AdelicLeviCenterTransferReductionStatement` contains one `Prop`-valued
definition and no theorems:

```lean
def LeviRestrictionCenterTransferReductionStatement : Prop :=
  LeviRestrictionCenterCongruenceStatement.{u} → LeviRestrictionCenterTransferStatement.{u}
```

The premise (module `AdelicLeviCenterCongruenceStatement`) is the algebraic statement: for
every central `z` of the enveloping algebra `U(𝔤)` of `G(F_∞)` there is `y` in the enveloping
algebra of `M'(F_∞)` with `z - j_Q(τ_Q y)` in the right ideal generated by the tangent
directions of `V(F_∞)` and `z - j_P(τ_P y)` in the right ideal generated by those of `U(F_∞)`.
The conclusion (module `AdelicLeviCenterTransferStatement`) is the analytic statement: the
same kind of `y`, acting by the Library's right differentiation on `M'(F_∞)`, sends the
normalized Levi restrictions `m ↦ δ_Q(m)^{-1/2} f(ι_Q(m) g)` of left `V(F_∞)`-invariant
plot-smooth `f` to those of `z f`, and likewise for `P`, `U(F_∞)` and `δ_P(c(m))^{-1/2}`.
Both statements have the same group-theoretic data. The module
`AdelicLeviCenterTransferReduction` proves the implication
(`leviRestrictionCenterTransferReduction`).

## Provenance

Derived. The conclusion is the compatibility of the right action of the center `𝔷` of `U(𝔤)`
with the left action of the center of `U(𝔪)` on normalized Levi restrictions, stated in
Mœglin–Waldspurger I.2.17 (`moeglin-waldspurger-1995-spectral-decomposition`, chapter PDF
`05.2_pp_18_40_Automorphic_forms_growth_constant_terms.pdf`, printed pp. 37–38). The
normalization of the output is restored as in the Sources section of
`AdelicLeviCenterTransferStatement`. The premise is a form of the decomposition of a central
element in Knapp–Vogan, Lemma 4.123 (`knapp-vogan-1995-cohomological-induction`, printed
p. 301), mirrored to right ideals. The normalizing factors are those of Bernstein–Lapid
(`bernstein-lapid-2024-meromorphic-continuation-eisenstein`). The twisted action
`a·φ(g) = δ_P(a)^{-1/2} φ(ag)` is their (6.2), p. 201, where `2ρ_P` is identified with the
determinant of the adjoint action on `Lie P`. The intertwining identity (6.5) on the same page
uses the same factors for `P` and `Q`. The proof of the Lemma in §8.2, p. 222, applies these
factors to the restrictions `m ↦ δ_P(m)^{-1/2} φ(mg)`.

The implication from the congruence to the transfer is derived in the module
`AdelicLeviCenterTransferReduction`, not taken from these sources. Right differentiation
commutes with restriction to `M'(F_∞)` along the tangent map. Multiplication by `δ^{-1/2}`
shifts each generator by the derivative of `δ^{-1/2}`. The right ideal generated by the
directions of `V(F_∞)` kills left `V(F_∞)`-invariant functions at the points of `M'(F_∞)`,
which normalize `V(F_∞)`. Right translation by `g` commutes with the center. The formulation
with the Library's objects is internal.
