# Transport of the Eisenstein formulas between represented parabolics

The principal images of two presentations with the same all-test subgroup functor agree, by applying the equality to the rational test and then inserting those points into the adeles. The inducing invariance subgroup is determined by the adelic radical image and the principal Levi image. Equal parabolic images give literally the same rational coset indexing for the Eisenstein series. Parameter restriction along a map over the inclusion gives equal twists, so the sums agree.

For a radical, the normalized subgroup constant term is a function of the represented all-test image and the ambient function. The intertwining integral can similarly be written in terms of the two radical images, the principal Weyl point and the inducing twist. The intersection defining the rational stabilizer and the Haar normalization are determined by those images. Equal data identify the quotient, measure and integrand.

These are internal transport identities for the conventions in Bernstein–Lapid §2.2. They apply to the totalized sums and integrals in the Library. They allow comparison of specified parabolic inclusions with the canonical standard family.
