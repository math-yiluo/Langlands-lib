# Measurable realizations of the weighted automorphic Hilbert space

`WeightedHilbertRealizationStatement` fixes a continuous log weight ℓ and gives a linear choice of measurable representatives on the rational quotient for the weighted `L²` space. Pullback along the quotient map yields a linear realization on the adelic group with pointwise rational left invariance. Suppose the weighted quotient measure for a measurable enlarged height region is at most `C` times the original quotient measure, with `C ≥ 0`. The squared integral of a realized vector over the enlarged chamber is then finite, with square root at most `√C` times its original Hilbert norm.

The proof takes the submodule of measurable square-integrable functions and maps it linearly onto `L²`. Every class has a measurable representative, so the map is surjective. A linear splitting gives the representative map. The ambient integral agrees with the quotient integral by pushing the restricted Haar measure forward through the parabolic and rational quotient maps. The general `L²` norm estimate under measure domination gives the enlarged-region bound.

This is an internal realization of the weighted Hilbert norms of Bernstein–Lapid, §7.4, p. 215. The measure domination is supplied. The realization and norm estimate provide the input for the actual smoothing operator on a thickened Siegel domain in Lemma (2), p. 216.
