# Actual field-extension data for Arthur descent

**Sources.** J. Arthur, *An introduction to the trace formula* (2005), §17, printed pp. 99–100 (PDF pp. 111–112), Lemma 17.5 and (17.13), `arthur-ellwood-kottwitz-2005-harmonic-analysis-shimura`. J. Arthur, *The invariant trace formula. I. Local theory* (1988), §7, printed pp. 355–357 (PDF pp. 33–35), the special subspace, Euclidean coefficient, section and Proposition 7.1, `arthur-1988-invariant-trace-formula-I`.

**The group and parameter maps.** `fieldCocharacterInclusion j` is the real scalar extension of actual base change of cocharacters of the split component `A_M`, followed by the torus map `j : (A_M)_K → A_{M₁}`. The parameter map goes in the same direction: extend a rational character of `M` to `M_K`, then restrict it to `M₁` and to `A_{M₁}`. `IsFieldParameterCompatible` checks on every rational character of `M` that in the chosen coordinates this map is the dual of the orthogonal projection to the image of `𝔞_M`. The check on characters specifies the real-linear comparison on their tensor extension; it does not identify an arbitrary orthogonal projection with base change. The cocharacter spaces are `RealTorusSpace.V` of the actual split components. The pairing with rational characters uses restriction to those components, as with the split-component character comparison in the Library.

**The fine and coarse weights.** `actualRootVectors` takes the finite support `relativeRoots` of the actual adjoint action of a split torus in the ambient group, represented by Riesz vectors of the root evaluations. The input torus is the split component of a Levi; its weights need not themselves form a reflection-stable root system. In the comparison predicate, the fine weights are those of `A_{M₁}` in `G_K`. Restricting the vectors orthogonally to the image `b` of `𝔞_M`, and omitting zero, must recover the actual weights of `A_M` in `G`. Chamber and face indices are the positive-weight subsets of the respective arrangements, in the convention of [GMFamilies](../LinearAlgebra/GMFamilies.md). Restriction to `b` identifies the nonzero coarse weights; positive subsets on `b` retain their indices in the fine root set so the condition `S ⊆ S₁` represents `P₁ ⊆ P`.

`HasSpecialWeightSums` uses `Module.finrank` of the actual Lie weight space, not a unit multiplicity on each distinct weight. It requires that for each nonzero restriction to `b` the sum of the fine Riesz vectors over that fibre, with these multiplicities, lies in `b`. A source of this condition is the determinant character of `M` on the corresponding `A_M`-weight space of `Lie G`, since `M` centralizes `A_M`: after base change its character on `A_{M₁}` is the sum of the fine weights with their multiplicities. With the parameter compatibility, its Riesz vector belongs to `b`. This describes a route to proving the comparison, not a theorem of this definition file.

**Center and normalizations.** In `HasFieldDescentFormula`, the Euclidean ambient space is `𝔞_{M₁}` (its submodule `⊤`), `b` represents `𝔞_M`, and `z = coarseCenter R b = b ∩ centralSpace R` is the coarse central direction. The coefficient is `dDescent b z ⊤ L₁`: the volume for the addition map of the orthogonal complements of `b` and `L₁` into the orthogonal complement of `z`, zero unless it is an isomorphism. The family uses `θ` from `GMFamilies`, including its Gram-determinant covolume and the convention `λ = iΛ`. New central directions over `K` need not lie in `b`. With the required compatibility of the fine-center projection, they form the orthogonal extra central summand of `bᗮ` and `zᗮ`. They contribute a unit determinant block and vanish in the roots. Thus for the route through Arthur 1988 one enlarges `b` by the fine center, uses the special-subspace condition there, and restricts the parameters to those of `b`. The section from `ξ` selects the chamber of the intersection with the shifted coarse space; the root signs ignore the extra central summand. The construction imposes regularity at the intersection for nonzero coefficients, the terms that affect the formula.

**The identity predicate.** `HasFieldDescentFormula` requires existence of a compatible fine chamber and equality of the chosen induced value with the value at *each* compatible chamber. It asserts the family condition and smooth representatives of the coarse `c_M` and the fine `c_{M₁}^{Q₁}`, agreeing with the defining quotients wherever their `θ` denominators are nonzero (`IsSmoothThetaExtension`). The descent equality is for all imaginary parameters on these smooth representatives, including `0` as in (17.13). `HasActualFieldDescent` combines the existence of the genuine torus map, the coordinate identifications, the character/weight and center comparisons, and this identity. No assertion that these data always exist, or proof of the identity, is made in this file; the [statement](GMFamilyFieldDescentStatement.md) supplies the precise nested-Levi hypotheses.
