# Actual archimedean local parabolic Iwasawa existence

Author: Worker 55.

`Langlands.Global.InfinitePlaceGroupPoints.ParabolicIwasawaStatement` is an
unproved proposition definition. For every number field `F : Type u`,
actual connected reductive group scheme `G` over `Spec F`, actual
parabolic inclusion `p : P ⟶ G`, and infinite place `v`, it asks for a
subgroup `K` of the existing group `InfinitePlaceGroupPoints F G v` with
three properties:

1. Its underlying set is compact.
2. Every compact subgroup `L` of this same point group containing `K`
   equals `K`.
3. The literal pointwise set product
   `Set.range (P.affinePointsTarget p (CommAlgCat.of F v.Completion)) *
   (K : Set _)` equals `Set.univ`.

The parabolic image is the left factor and the compact subgroup is the
right factor. In mathematical notation, the third property is

\[
  \operatorname{image}\bigl(P(F_v)\longrightarrow G(F_v)\bigr)K=G(F_v).
\]

The subgroup is existentially quantified after the field, group,
parabolic and place; it may depend on them. Maximality is among all
compact subgroups, with no chosen family or extra closedness premise.
The equality is a covering by products, not a statement about density,
closure or the subgroup generated by the factors.

## Actual carrier, topology and scope

The carrier and topology are imported unchanged from
[`InfiniteAdelicGroupProduct`](InfiniteAdelicGroupProduct.md).
`InfinitePlaceGroupPoints F G v` abbreviates
`G.affinePoints (CommAlgCat.of F v.Completion)`, the actual scheme
morphisms from the affine test scheme over the actual completion.
The group law comes from the group object. The existing topology is
induced by evaluating every global regular function on `G` in
`v.Completion`; `IsCompact` uses this topology.

The parabolic map is the existing group homomorphism
`P.affinePointsTarget p (CommAlgCat.of F v.Completion)`, induced by
postcomposition with the actual group-scheme homomorphism `p`. Its
range is taken on completion-valued points, not on rational points over
`F`. This module introduces no alternative point carrier, topology or
parabolic definition.

The proposition retains arbitrary universe `u` and the existing
`IsConnectedReductive G` and `IsParabolicSubgroup p` predicates.
It imposes no splitness, isotropy, matrix realization or properness
restriction on the parabolic. Non-split and anisotropic groups, and the
improper parabolic, remain in scope. A maximal compact subgroup, local
decomposition, comparison map or homeomorphism is never an input to
the existence target.

## Source and remaining proof obligations

The source is Getz–Hahn, *An Introduction to Automorphic Representations*
(2024), Appendix B: Theorem B.1.1 and equation (B.1), printed p.541
(one-based PDF p.546), and the archimedean Theorem B.4.1 with its proof,
printed p.547 (PDF p.552). Registered source:
`getz-hahn-2024-automorphic-representations`, SHA-256
`a125cf9c0f445972cbbd3552b4fb9905ca948108a05880f90eaa43558d09a507`.
The original passages were read. The five-page October 2026 author
errata was read in full and contains no Appendix B correction:
`getz-hahn-2024-errata-2026-10`, SHA-256
`e641ec2a5485443f792935afe5ae4940323755968f784ce290a96d9cd76fc1c2`.

This is the archimedean local existence conclusion of B.1.1, specialized
to number-field completions and expressed on the Library's existing
scheme-point carrier. B.4.1 gives the stronger simultaneous statement
for standard parabolic pairs, with maximal compact intersections in
their Levi subgroups and an intersection identity. Those additional
conclusions are not part of this target. No Levi subgroup or minimal
parabolic is supplied as a premise here.

The B.4.1 proof invokes Lie-theoretic Iwasawa existence and maximal
compact conjugacy and component results. Its citation to Knapp,
Proposition 7.31, is an external input cited by Getz–Hahn, not an
existing Lean proof. A proof of this target must establish the needed
Lie-theoretic results, including the relevant component behavior.
It must also justify base change of connected reductivity and
parabolicity to `v.Completion`, identify completion-valued points with
the actual base-changed group points, and establish any comparison
with a real or complex Lie-group realization while preserving the
existing regular-function evaluation topology. Any route through
minimal parabolics or Levi subgroups must establish their needed
existence and compatibility internally. These are proof obligations,
not additional hypotheses or assertions proved by this module.

This local target does not prove
`Langlands.Global.AdelicGroupPoints.ParabolicIwasawaStatement`.
The adelic theorem still requires the finite-place structural inputs,
almost-everywhere integral compatibility, and assembly in the actual
adelic group. This module supplies neither that global theorem nor
the proof of the local proposition.
