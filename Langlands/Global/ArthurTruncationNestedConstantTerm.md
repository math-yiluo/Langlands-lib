# Nested constant terms for measurable inputs

For represented compact radical quotients, adjoining an average over a smaller radical normalized by the larger radical preserves the latter integral. The outer measure is the pushforward of the normalized Haar measure restricted to a measurable fundamental domain. The original function descends to a measurable function there, and a compact rational cover and local boundedness give its integrability. Conjugation by outer rational points preserves the inner quotient measure. The proof then applies the integrable compact subgroup averaging identity.

For nested standard parabolics the earlier term over the smaller radical disappears on taking the constant term along the larger radical. The original parabolic's cutoff is unchanged on left translation by that radical. Thus the new kernel is the original cutoff times the constant term along the smaller parabolic. The nested transitivity is the comparable special case of the composition law in Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series* (2024), §7.3, p. 214. Here the proof works for Arthur's measurable, locally bounded, rational-invariant input class.
