# Iwasawa integration on an unfolded quotient

The compatible Iwasawa pushforward for the discrete quotient uses a fundamental domain of the radical intersection. A continuous invariant function descends to the radical quotient in the inner variable. Moving the Iwasawa density into the integrand then gives both the integrability criterion and the integral on the exact normalized radical quotient. These are the measure and integrability forms of the unfolding in Mœglin–Waldspurger II.1.8.
