# Uniform bounds for minimal-parabolic line characters on simple-root boxes

This internal derived module evaluates the algebraic relation of
`Langlands/GroupScheme/Rational/MinimalParabolicLineCharacter.lean` under the
normalized adelic character norms over a number field F. The result is the
highest-weight bound in Springer's proof of (F) (Springer 1994, §3.6,
pp. 215-216). There, the character of the line stabilized by a parabolic is
bounded on the torus part T(c) of a Siegel set intersected with the norm-one
points. The Lean route is the one described below.

`HasUniformCharacterBound F T psi S` (published) means that the normalized
adelic norm of the torus character psi has one upper bound on the set S of
adelic torus points. `normCharacter F T psi s` is that normalized norm.

`normCharacter_add` and `normCharacter_zero`: the norm of a sum of characters
is the product of the norms, and the trivial character has norm one. Both are
the published `characterNorm_add` and `characterNorm_zero`, restated for torus
characters.

`hasUniformCharacterBound_of_mem_closure`: if every character in a set Delta
is uniformly bounded on S, then so is every element of the additive submonoid
generated by Delta. The bounds multiply along sums, and the bound 1 serves for
the zero character.

`le_max_of_pow_le` (private): for positive real units x and C and a positive
natural d, x^d <= C implies x <= max(C, 1).

`hasUniformCharacterBound_of_pos_nsmul_sub_restrict`: let S consist of torus
points whose image in G lies in the ambient norm-one subgroup. Let d be
positive and theta an ambient rational character of G. If
d psi - (restriction of theta) is uniformly bounded by C on S, then psi is
uniformly bounded by max(C, 1) on S. The published
`normCharacter_pow_eq_of_integral_relation` applies to the relation
d psi = theta|_T + 1 (d psi - theta|_T). At norm-one points it gives
|psi(s)|^d = |(d psi - theta|_T)(s)|, and the previous lemma applies.

`hasUniformCharacterBound_torusCoordinateLineCharacter_of_minimalStandardParabolic`
(main result). Assume G is connected reductive with affine structure morphism,
i : T -> G is a maximal split torus, nu is generic, and rho is a
finite-coordinate representation. Assume its coordinate line j is preserved
by the minimal standard parabolic `standardParabolic i nu ∅` and by T. Then
for every positive real c, the torus line character
`rho.torusCoordinateLineCharacter i j hTline` is uniformly bounded on

    S_c = { s | map i s ∈ normOneSubgroup F G and
                normCharacter α s <= c for every simple relative root α }.

The algebraic main theorem gives d > 0 and theta with
d psi - theta|_T in the additive submonoid generated by the simple relative
roots. Each simple root is bounded by c on S_c, so the closure lemma bounds
d psi - theta|_T. The previous lemma then bounds psi, since S_c consists of
norm-one points.

`exists_hasUniformCharacterBound_torusCoordinateLineCharacter_of_minimalStandardParabolic`:
the same conclusion for every c. Here the torus preservation of the line is
derived from the minimal-parabolic hypothesis by
`preservesCoordinateLine_torus_of_minimalStandardParabolic`.

## Scope

The set S_c is the simple-root box of the published right-adelic
fundamental-set convention (`HasRightAdelicFundamentalSet`). The bound depends
on c, rho and j, and is not made explicit. No height, rational-orbit or
fundamental-set statement is proved here. The torus is maximal split in G, and
G need not be split.
