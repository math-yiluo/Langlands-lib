# Weighted right convolution into the uniform-growth Fréchet space

`AdelicWeightedSmoothingOperatorStatement` constructs a continuous complex-linear map that acts by actual adelic right convolution. It uses a closed inclusion of affine group schemes P → G over a number field, with G locally of finite type, an Iwasawa compact for this inclusion, and smoothness of fixed relative dimension with the adelic differential commutator law. Fix a closed matrix representation ρ of positive rank and a continuous log weight ℓ on the real character dual of P. The weight must satisfy exp(ℓ(Hₚ(g))) ≤ W htρ(g)ʳ for all adelic g, with W > 0 and r ≥ 0.

Each rational orbit must have a representative in a supplied set with polynomially controlled height and compactly bounded finite projection. The kernel is an `IsAdelicTestFunction`, meaning it is continuous, plot-smooth on the archimedean slices, compactly supported, and left and right invariant under open finite-adelic subgroups. The enlarged open height region contains the heights of the representative set and the heights of its right translates by the kernel support. The specified level L is a subgroup of G(𝔸_f) under which the kernel is left invariant. The output is right L-invariant.

The input is a linear realization of a complex normed space by measurable rational-invariant functions. The squared integral of each realization uses left Haar measure on the chosen fundamental domain for P(F) in G(𝔸), restricted to the preimage of the enlarged height region, with density exp(−2ℓ(Hₚ(g))). Equivalently it is the weighted quotient Haar integral on that region in P(F) \ G(𝔸). Each squared integral must be finite, with square root bounded by a common constant times the input norm. The map takes values in the uniform-growth Fréchet space at level L for every sufficiently large growth exponent.

The proof first derives local Haar integrability using the open chamber cover. Smooth convolution then gives a finite-level rational-invariant function. Polynomial control of the weight gives one height exponent for all enveloping derivatives at the chosen representatives. The polynomial comparison of heights and rational invariance of the differentiated functions extend the bounds to every adelic point. The relation between representation height and source norm gives a common growth exponent for the Fréchet space. The proof checks linearity through the integrable convolution formula. The norm bound for each weighted derivative gives continuity into the Fréchet space, whose topology is induced by these derivatives.

This is the smoothing step of Bernstein–Lapid, §7.4, Lemma (2), p. 216, expressed with the supplied geometric and weighted Hilbert norm comparisons that permit passage from a Siegel region to the global uniform-growth space. The result is also used in the finite-level Fréchet form of the proof in §7.6, pp. 219–220.
