# Logarithms and complex parameters of the positive Levi split component

Restrict characters along `A → M → P`. The map is injective by the
published parabolic split-component restriction theorem. The Levi
retraction makes restriction from `P` to `M` surjective, so the composite
has the same finite-index image as restriction from `M` to its split
component `A`. The positive-torus logarithm equivalence for a finite-index
restriction now represents every functional on `X^*(P)` as the logarithm
of a point in the actual positive split subgroup.

The complex parameter pairing is injective. Extend the injective
restriction to complex scalars and use a basis of the split torus
character lattice. Pairing the restricted parameter with each integral
coordinate functional recovers its coefficient in the extended basis.
Functoriality identifies each pairing with a pairing before restriction.

Equal powers on the positive subgroup therefore imply equality of the
parameters. For each real character functional `H`, use logarithm
surjectivity at `t H` for every real `t`. The two complex exponentials
of the linear functions of `t` agree. Their derivatives at zero give
equality of the pairings with `H`.

The result supplies the number field assertions about logarithm and
parameter injectivity in Bernstein–Lapid §6.2, following (6.1).
