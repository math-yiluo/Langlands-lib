# Detecting an irreducible specialization by holomorphic lifts in cyclic submodules

A `HolomorphicCyclicLift` is a vector in the cyclic submodule of a given
input together with a section curve. Its scalar coordinates are analytic
at the specialization parameter, its value there is nonzero, and the
normalized derivatives of its evaluated image give the indicated
coefficient vectors under observation.

Suppose the specialization surjects onto a simple module. Fix a target
vector and suppose every input specializing to it has a holomorphic
cyclic lift. The scalar family is holomorphic on a preconnected set
containing the specialization and an injective member. On its
finite-dimensional section space, this gives a uniform Taylor order.
If an input lies in the joint kernel up to that order and has nonzero
specialization, simplicity supplies an operator moving the specialization
to the fixed target vector. The new input and the chosen lift remain in
the joint kernel. The scalar Taylor test then forces zero section value
at the specialization, contradicting the lift condition.

The finite coefficient subquotient lemma now gives a surjection to the
simple target from a submodule of the coefficient space. For the stable
matrix forms with the free operator action, the concrete relation descent
makes this an actual irreducible automorphic operator subquotient and
identifies its supplied finite action. In the compact-type application,
the target vector is chosen nonzero in the projected type and the lift is
obtained from a finite combination of compact translates.

This is an internal cyclic-lift version of the finite Taylor detection
and subquotient argument in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic
representation* (1979), proof of Proposition 2 and Lemma 9, printed
pp. 205–207 (Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 200–202).
