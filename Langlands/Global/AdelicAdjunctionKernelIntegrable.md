# Absolute integrability of the normalized adjunction kernel

At a fixed radical coset, Iwasawa decomposition turns the normalized source value into a translate on the source compact and canonical Levi quotient. Conjugation by the Weyl representative transports the canonical Levi measure up to a finite positive Haar scalar. The square integral at every radical coset is therefore bounded by one fixed multiple of the square integral of the source vector. The product with the target vector is bounded by the sum of the two squared norms. The remaining scalar kernel is the absolute Iwasawa character power at the parameter shifted by the source half modulus. Tonelli with this integrable scalar kernel proves the absolute integrability needed for Mœglin–Waldspurger II.1.8 with Lapid's square-integrable data.
