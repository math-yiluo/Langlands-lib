# The square norm of the normalized adelic intertwining integral

The source normalized Levi value descends to the canonical Levi quotient. After removing the scalar Iwasawa height at the integration variable, the family of functions in Bernstein–Lapid's formula (6.5) is jointly measurable. This follows by descending continuous functions through the two quotients. Source radical invariance and its normalization by the source Levi justify the descent in the integration variable.

For each fixed unipotent variable, choose an Iwasawa decomposition of its translate by the inverse Weyl representative. The resulting function of the Levi class and compact variable is obtained from the source normalized restriction by the Levi isomorphism, right translation on the source Levi quotient, and left translation on the compact subgroup. The measures for these translations are invariant. The Levi isomorphism transports the quotient measure up to one positive scalar, so every section has the same squared norm up to that scalar. The weighted Cauchy–Schwarz estimate then applies to the scalar Iwasawa kernel. The choice of Iwasawa decomposition is used only for the estimate at a fixed integration variable.

The result assumes the normalized Haar identity, the Iwasawa decomposition in source radical–Levi coordinates, and integrability of the scalar kernel. It uses the specific canonical quotient measures and compact Haar probabilities in the square-integrability predicate.
