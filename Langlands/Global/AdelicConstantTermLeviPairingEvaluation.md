# Uniform-growth evaluation of a constant term against a Levi cusp test

Continuity and rational parabolic invariance of the constant term allow
the Levi pairing integrand to descend continuously to the rational
norm-one quotient. Choose a compact set of representatives of the
radical quotient. The weighted bound on the Levi representatives
dominates the product of the test value with the constant-term
integrand uniformly over that compact set.

The unit weighted derivative bounds the integrand of the inner
probability integral. The resulting integrable outer majorant gives
absolute convergence and bounds the pairing by the same seminorm.
Linearity and continuity follow on the uniform moderate growth space.
This is an internal construction for scalar continuation of the
pairings in Bernstein–Lapid (6.8).

The module derives the integrable majorant from weighted bounds on
norm-one representatives. Submultiplicativity controls both the compact
left translates and the fixed right translate. A constant majorant on
the quotient is integrable by its finite Haar volume.
