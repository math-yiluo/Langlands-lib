# Actual invariant restriction finiteness

`lieCharacteristicInvariantSubalgebra` is the complex subalgebra of the actual
full-group invariant symmetric tensors generated by
`lieCharacteristicSymmetricInvariant` at each real or complex infinite place
and each coefficient index in `Fin (n + 1)`. Its ambient algebra is
`lieSymmetricGroupInvariants`, whose fixed condition quantifies over every
element of the actual archimedean group. The construction retains every
number-field universe and every natural rank, including zero.

There are finitely many generators over a number field.
`lieCharacteristicInvariantSubalgebra_fg` proves finite generation, and
`lieCharacteristicInvariantSubalgebra_noetherian` supplies the Noetherian-ring
instance. `lieCharacteristicInvariantSubalgebra_diagonal_image` uses the
characteristic tensors' exact diagonal restriction identities and the image of
an algebraic adjoin to identify the image with
`cartanDiagonalCharacteristicSubalgebra`.

The finite diagonal extension therefore makes the diagonal polynomial algebra
finite over the source characteristic algebra: the map onto the coefficient
algebra is surjective, and its inclusion into all diagonal polynomials is
finite. This is `lieCharacteristicInvariantSubalgebra_diagonal_finite`.
A private algebra lemma implements the factorization using the surjective map
onto the image and the subalgebra equivalence induced by the image equality.

Injectivity of `cartanCompactInvariantDiagonalRestriction` then makes the
compact-invariant algebra a finite module over the Noetherian source
characteristic algebra. The implemented private lemma views the injective
restriction as a linear map over that source algebra and applies
`Module.Finite.of_injective`. This proves
`cartanCharacteristicInvariantRestriction_finite`. Finally,
`AlgHom.Finite.of_comp_finite` extends scalars to the whole actual full-group
invariant algebra and proves `cartanGroupInvariantRestriction_finite`.
Here finiteness of an algebra homomorphism means finite generation of its
codomain as a module over its domain via that homomorphism.

This is an internal derivation from the preceding actual characteristic,
diagonal, and trace-duality constructions and elementary Noetherian module
theory. It assumes no invariant-finiteness or enveloping central-generation
conclusion. It supplies actual invariant restriction finiteness; transfer to
the enveloping algebra and the final automorphic differential-growth theorem
remain separate open obligations.
