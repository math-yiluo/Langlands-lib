# Joint admissibility of actual scalar adelic block sections

This internal result supplies the admissible induced carrier for Task 6519. It uses the normalized scalar section model and its actual finite adelic and infinite compact right actions from `AdelicBlockScalarSectionActions`. It proves neither the factorization into place constituents nor any Eisenstein-series assertion.

For an open finite level `L`, write `Kf` for the standard finite compact and `H = Kf ∩ L`. `finiteSliceQuotient` is the coset space of `H` in `Kf` (cosets `xH`). It is finite because `H` is open in the compact group `Kf`. `finiteSliceRepresentative` chooses one representative per coset.

In `RealizedSections.isJointlyAdmissible`, apply `finiteSliceEvaluation` at these representatives. Each evaluation lands in a coefficient multiplicity space at its own open level `finiteSliceLevel`. The compact test is restricted along the actual block diagonal infinite compact inclusion. The two evaluation laws are proved in `AdelicBlockSliceEvaluation`; `FiniteTensor.isJointlyAdmissible_external` gives joint admissibility of the coefficient pair. To see that the evaluations detect the zero multiplicity map, use Iwasawa decomposition to reduce to compact points. Each such point is the representative followed by its infinite coordinate and an element of `L` in the finite group. Right `L`-invariance removes the last factor, and equivariance moves the infinite compact coordinate to the test vector. The evaluations vanish on all test vectors, so the resulting normalized slice is zero. The finite evaluation lemma then gives finite dimension.

`IsJointlyAdmissible.of_injective` is the one-evaluation transfer along a linear embedding intertwining both actions. Apply this to the two subspace inclusions to obtain `compactFiniteRealizedSections_isJointlyAdmissible` on the finite-smooth, infinite-compact-finite carrier, and then use the existing `CuspidalOperatorRealization.isJointlyAdmissible` on each block to obtain `cuspidalCompactFiniteRealizedSections_isJointlyAdmissible` under the Library's compactness hypothesis on the additive adele quotient.
