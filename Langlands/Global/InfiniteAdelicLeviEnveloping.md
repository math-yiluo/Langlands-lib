# Enveloping maps, character shifts and unipotent right ideals at infinity

Module `Langlands.Global.InfiniteAdelicLeviEnveloping`. It constructs, for group schemes over
a number field `F` with the Library's archimedean Lie group structures
(`smoothChartedSpace`, `smoothLieGroup`), the algebraic objects in which the normalized
Harish-Chandra congruence of `LeviRestrictionCenterCongruenceStatement` is stated. Throughout,
`AdelicEnvelopingAlgebra F G hsmooth` is the universal enveloping algebra of the
complexification `ℂ ⊗[ℝ] GroupLieAlgebra` of the identity tangent space of `G(F_∞)`, with
Mathlib's bracket of left-invariant vector fields. The content is internal: it derives from the
universal property of the enveloping algebra and the calculus of the module
`SmoothGroupHomLie`.

## Character shifts (namespace `Langlands.Algebra.ComplexLieExtension`)

For a real Lie algebra `L` and a real functional `λ : L →ₗ[ℝ] ℂ`:

- `linear_lie_eq_zero`: if `λ` vanishes on brackets, so does its complex-linear extension
  `linear λ` to `ℂ ⊗[ℝ] L`, by tensor induction and `⁅a ⊗ X, b ⊗ Y⁆ = ab ⊗ ⁅X, Y⁆`.
- `characterShiftLie λ hλ` is the complex Lie map `Y ↦ ι Y + λ(Y) · 1` into the enveloping
  algebra of `ℂ ⊗[ℝ] L`. It preserves brackets because scalars are central and `λ` kills
  brackets.
- `characterShift λ hλ` is its extension to an algebra endomorphism of the enveloping algebra;
  `characterShift_ι` gives its value on generators. Its inverse is the shift by `-λ`; this
  file does not use that.

This is the shift `τ_𝔲` of Knapp–Vogan (4.124), printed p. 302, for the functional
`X ↦ -ρ(𝔲)(X)`; here the functional is arbitrary.

## Maps at infinity (namespace `Langlands.Global.InfiniteAdelicGroupPoints`)

Let `H`, `G` be affine group schemes over `F` with smooth structures `hsmoothH`, `hsmooth`, and
`j : H ⟶ G` a morphism of group schemes.

- `contMDiff_affinePointsTarget`: the point map `H(F_∞) → G(F_∞)` of `j` is smooth. Smoothness
  for the Library atlas is coordinate smoothness (`contMDiff_iff_coordinateSmooth`), which is
  preserved by postcomposition with a morphism of schemes.
- `pointTangent F hsmoothH hsmooth j` is its derivative at the identity (`groupHomTangent`).
- `pointTangentLieHom` bundles it as a homomorphism of real Lie algebras. The bracket is
  preserved by `groupHomTangent_lie_of_separating`; the separation hypothesis holds because
  `G(F_∞)` is Hausdorff (`InfiniteAdelicGroupPoints.t2Space`) and modelled on a
  finite-dimensional space (`eq_zero_of_forall_mvfderiv_eq_zero`).
- `pointEnvelopingMap F hsmoothH hsmooth j` is the induced algebra map
  `AdelicEnvelopingAlgebra F H hsmoothH →ₐ[ℂ] AdelicEnvelopingAlgebra F G hsmooth`, the
  universal lift of the complexified tangent map; `pointEnvelopingMap_ι_tmul` gives
  `ι(z ⊗ X) ↦ ι(z ⊗ dj(X))`.
- `identityDerivative F hsmoothH χ` is the derivative at the identity of a function
  `χ : H(F_∞) → ℂ`, as a real functional on the identity tangent space.
- `envelopingCharacterShift F hsmoothH χ hχ` is `characterShift` for this functional, given a
  proof `hχ` that it vanishes on brackets: the automorphism `Y ↦ Y + dχ(Y)` of
  `AdelicEnvelopingAlgebra F H hsmoothH`. For a smooth multiplicative `χ` such a proof is
  `mvfderiv_character_lie`.
- `curveTangents F hsmooth S`, for a subset `S` of `G(F_∞)`, is the set of velocities
  `mfderiv c 0 1` of the smooth curves `c : ℝ → G(F_∞)` with `c 0 = 1` and all values in `S`.
  For `S` the image of the points of a closed subgroup these are tangent vectors of that
  subgroup; the definition needs no smooth structure on the subgroup.
- `curveRightIdeal F hsmooth S` is the complex span of the products `ι(1 ⊗ X) · b` with `X` in
  `curveTangents F hsmooth S` and `b` in the enveloping algebra. Mathematically this span is
  stable under right multiplication, so it is the right ideal generated by these directions.
  When `S` is the image of `V(F_∞)` for the unipotent radical `V` of a parabolic, it contains
  the right ideal `𝔳 U(𝔤)` generated by the complexified Lie algebra of `V(F_∞)` (a tangent
  vector of `V(F_∞)` is the velocity of a smooth curve in `V(F_∞)`); this file does not prove
  that inclusion.
