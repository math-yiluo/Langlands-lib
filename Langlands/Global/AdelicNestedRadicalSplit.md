# Splitting radicals for nested standard parabolics

The radical of the smaller parabolic lies in the larger parabolic, and it contains the larger parabolic's radical. Decompose its points using the Levi retraction of the larger parabolic. Removing the component in the larger radical leaves a point of the smaller parabolic's radical lying in the larger Levi.

This defines a projection of the smaller parabolic's radical onto its scheme-theoretic intersection with the larger Levi. The intersection inclusion is a section, and the kernel is the larger parabolic's radical on every test scheme. In particular, the same splitting applies on rational and adelic points.
