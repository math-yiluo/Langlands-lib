# Coherent arithmetic changes of level

This internal construction compares actual iterated model pullbacks with the
direct pullback using Mathlib's pullback pasting isomorphism and equality of the
literal integer-ring inclusions. Its first projection is the composite of the
two original first projections.

For curve places and number fields, the naturality formulas identify the original
changing-model level transition, followed by this canonical comparison and the
inverse fixed-base point equivalence, with change of coefficients by the literal
fixed-base algebra map. The proofs use the actual first projections and the
established transition projection formulas. Equality of points follows from
equality of their underlying scheme morphisms. The comparison uses the actual
isomorphism between twice-pulled and directly pulled schemes.

This is a derived coherence construction for the fixed-model system in Conrad
(3.5.1), rather than a separate assertion quoted from the paper. It preserves
arbitrary curve fields and their algebra structure, arbitrary schemes, all finite
initial sets including the empty set, and the arbitrary common universe. The
number-field construction uses the original full level rings, retaining every
infinite completion. There are no finiteness or separation hypotheses on the
models. Topological properties, generic-fiber identification and model
independence are separate.
