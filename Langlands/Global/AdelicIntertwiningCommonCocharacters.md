# Common cocharacters for the radical determinant comparison

The rational Weyl conjugation identifies the source Levi with the target Levi and carries the source dynamic unipotent radical to the one for the transported cocharacter. The target split component contains the transported source split component by the maximality of the central split torus. Both cocharacters consequently come from the maximal split torus specified by the target presentation, and their dynamic centralizers coincide as actual subgroup functors.

The target maximal split torus factors through the target Levi by its standard Levi presentation. This factor detects rational Levi characters because its composition with the supplied map from the central split component is the split-component inclusion. These are the geometric and character-restriction inputs to the determinant comparison in Bernstein–Lapid, formula (6.5), journal p. 201.

`intertwiningRadicalDeterminantBalance` applies the published equal-centralizer determinant comparison to the two transported cocharacters. It identifies the sum of the two full radical determinant characters with twice the intersection determinant character on the target Levi. The theorem uses any supplied representatives and finite tangent coordinates. It is the algebraic step in the modulus comparison of Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series*, registered as `bernstein-lapid-2024-meromorphic-continuation-eisenstein`, journal PDF SHA-256 `6acbe78adf3a7ddff4be0caa92b169f0e609d1245f674a71fbc6989d6aef9268`, p. 201, formula (6.5).
