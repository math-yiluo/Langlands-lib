# Weyl double coset representatives on actual adelic points

Index the family by the minimal elements in the relative Weyl
double cosets. This is a finite subtype of the relative Weyl group.
Choose a rational normalizer lift of each such element.
The rational Bruhat double coset equivalence sends the resulting
Weyl class to the double coset of that lift.

The principal adelic map is injective and commutes with actual
group-scheme morphisms. It sends each rational parabolic point
group onto the `rationalImage` used by the constant-term statement.
Thus a double coset relation on the rational group is equivalent
to the displayed relation in the adelic group. Existence and
uniqueness of a minimal element in each Weyl double coset give
existence and uniqueness of the index in the adelic relation.

Minimality gives the right length increases for the selected
source roots. The inverse is minimal for the reversed pair and
gives the target length increases. The relative Coxeter
realization translates the increases to the two root signs in
Bernstein–Lapid Lemma 6.7(i).

[Bernstein–Lapid, On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series](https://doi.org/10.1090/jams/1020)
