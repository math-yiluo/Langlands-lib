# Pointwise convolution of Eisenstein continuations on the imaginary axis

`ImaginaryEisensteinConvolutionStatement` combines joint continuity of the
Eisenstein values and continued pointwise convolution covariance on
the imaginary axis. The inducing forms are automorphic, and the
continuations are specified by their agreement with the defining series in
a sufficiently regular chamber and a domain of holomorphy containing
the imaginary axis. For covariance the normalized convolution of the
input form has a given finite expansion with entire scalar coefficients.
The conclusion uses the supplied continuations on both sides.

The formulas are those of Mœglin–Waldspurger, *Spectral Decomposition and
Eisenstein Series*, IV.1.7 and IV.1.9, pp. 139–140. The continuity condition
is joint in the spectral coordinates and the adelic argument. In the
function-valued formulation, evaluation and compactly supported
convolution followed by evaluation are continuous linear functionals.
Comparison with the given scalar continuations allows holomorphic
identities proved in a convergence chamber to be evaluated on the
imaginary axis. The positive-size condition refers to the matrix
embedding used for the spaces of uniform-growth functions.
