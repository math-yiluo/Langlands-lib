# Finite support of projected sections on a cyclic generator

Consider a family of linear coefficient evaluations at standard compact
archimedean arguments and finite adelic arguments. Assume that only
finitely many projections are nonzero at each finite adelic argument
with the compact argument equal to the identity. Right translation by
the compact group moves the compact argument to the vector. For a
compact-finite vector, it therefore suffices to consider a finite
spanning family of its compact orbit.

An open subgroup of the finite adelic group fixes a smooth vector.
Finite adelic Iwasawa and compactness supply a finite set of parabolic
double coset representatives for this subgroup. Right equivariance
and parabolic covariance preserve vanishing from a representative to
its double coset. Taking the union of the pointwise projection supports
over the representatives and spanning vectors bounds the support of the
whole section of the original vector.

If a projection has a full-operator-stable kernel, vanishing on a cyclic
generator implies vanishing on the full cyclic carrier. The finite
projection extraction lemma thus realizes the simple quotient inside
one of the finitely many remaining projected representations.

This is an internal finite-support step for the algebraic projections of
cuspidal Levi coefficients in the argument for `CuspidalSupportExistenceStatement`
(Langlands 1979, Proposition 2).
