# A block inducing tensor with an actual nonzero infinite factor

Use the actual irreducible admissible finite factors of the cuspidal realizations. The scalar Levi coefficient space is their finite tensor paired with the infinite multiplicity tensor. The grouped comparison gives normalized finite covariance and the infinite compact covariance at the parabolic intersection.

Faithful finite compact evaluations have a nonzero value and span the irreducible finite Levi coefficient space. Pairing them with the nonzero compact polynomial seed gives actual smooth scalar sections. Their full infinite slices yield a nonzero element in the invariant infinite coefficient space. Levi covariance and the scalar endomorphism property give injectivity of the resulting tensor evaluation. The definition `sliceTensorOperatorIntertwiner` packages this tensor evaluation, from the finite inducing space paired with the invariant infinite coefficients into the actual smooth compact-finite sections. It is an intertwining map from the tensor operator action to the original scalar section operator action at parameter zero, for finite evaluations that are covariant under finite right translation. Its finite, compact, and enveloping identities on the generators extend to every free operator. The theorem `exists_multiplicityTensorSource` assembles these facts for faithful finite evaluations into the actual irreducible admissible finite factors with normalized finite parabolic covariance (`HasFiniteInducingCovariance`). It gives a nontrivial infinite carrier and an injective intertwining map of this kind.

This constructs the original adelic block inducing tensor used in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Lemma 1, printed p. 203, Corvallis Part I PDF p. 198. The infinite factor is chosen by the construction, which suits the finite-place conclusion.
