# Regular parameter decay for rational parabolic cosets

The Lie-theoretic Levi limit identifies characters of the parabolic with
those of its Levi. The Levi split component detects these characters, and
flatness over the integers preserves this injectivity for real parameters.
`IsIwasawaCompact.splitComponentLinearHeight` composes the real Iwasawa
pairing with a linear retraction of the restriction map. This functional
agrees with the original Iwasawa pairing on every parabolic parameter.

At a principal rational point the functional vanishes on restricted
ambient characters by the product formula and compactness of the Iwasawa
subgroup. The lower bounds for the opposite-admissible determinant
characters therefore give the hypotheses of the published conic decay
estimate. The positive roots in the convergence region are the intrinsic
positive roots for the defining split-component cocharacter. The
Chevalley line bound controls the logarithmic height of the projective
coset by the absolute value of the functional at its line character,
up to a uniform additive constant.

In the supplied finite parameter coordinates the remaining finite sum of
absolute root evaluations is continuous. Compactness gives one bound for
that error term. Exponentiating gives any prescribed inverse power of the
projective coset height in the corresponding sufficiently regular region.
This internal estimate gives the rapid decay for the direct height proof
of Mœglin–Waldspurger II.1.5.
