# Transport of quotient-normalized measures on adelic subgroups

Let the two ambient group schemes be affine and locally of finite type over a number field. Fix an ambient isomorphism over that field and a subgroup functor in the target whose points on the full adeles form a closed subgroup. The isomorphism carries the adelic points of the inverse-image subgroup functor onto these target points. Its pushforward identifies the two Library measures `normalizedHaarMeasure`.

Start with left Haar measure on the source subgroup and its pushforward on the target. The two actual principal subgroups correspond, so transport of the discrete left quotient measures gives the same total quotient mass. Both ambient measures are rescaled by the reciprocal of this mass in the extended nonnegative reals. With finite positive quotient mass the rescaled ambient measures are Haar and the principal left quotients have mass one. With infinite quotient mass the rescaled measures are zero. For the unipotent radicals in the intertwining integral, compactness of the principal left quotient gives the mass-one case.

The companion result applies the same transport when the source and inverse-image subgroup functors are specified by an equality. It records the point map and the equivalence of membership in the two principal subgroups.
