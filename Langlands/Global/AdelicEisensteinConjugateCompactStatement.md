# Eisenstein operators for conjugate Iwasawa compacts

Suppose the compact subgroup \(K\) has Iwasawa coverage for \(P\) and \(Q\). For any \(t\in G(\mathbb A)\), its conjugate \(K'=t^{-1}Kt\) also has coverage for both. In the Eisenstein series for \(P\), evaluating at \(gt\) with \(K'\) gives the series for the right translate of the inducing function at \(g\) with \(K\), multiplied by the \(K'\)-height power at \(t\). The corresponding identity holds for the series for \(Q\) at the transported parameter.

For the normalized intertwining operator the scalar is the product of the \(P\)-height power at \(t\) and the \(Q\)-height power at \(t\) with negative transported parameter. The identities use the same rational coset sums and the same intertwining measure on both sides.

This is a consequence of the Iwasawa height and the formulas in Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series*, §2.2. The statement applies to the totalized Lean formulas for an arbitrary function.
