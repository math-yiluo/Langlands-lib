# Arthur truncation as an orthogonal projection on L²

**Sources.** J. Arthur, *An introduction to the trace formula* (2005), §13, (13.1) and Proposition 13.1, printed pp. 68–69 (PDF pp. 80–81), in `arthur-ellwood-kottwitz-2005-harmonic-analysis-shimura`. The paragraph after the proof of Proposition 13.1 states the extension to an orthogonal projection on `L²(G(F) \ G(𝔸_F))`; the preceding definition and the proposition use `G(𝔸_F)¹`. The full-adelic formula on locally bounded measurable functions also appears in E. Lapid, *On Arthur's asymptotic inner product formula of truncated Eisenstein series* (2011), §2, printed p. 312 (PDF p. 321), `lapid-2011-asymptotic-inner-product-truncated-eisenstein`. The `L²` contraction is used in the proof of Lapid's Lemma 9, p. 327 (PDF p. 336).

**Exact target.** `Langlands.Global.ArthurTruncation.TruncationOrthogonalProjectionStatement`. For a connected reductive group over a number field, choose the maximal split torus and the minimal standard parabolic as in [ArthurTruncation](ArthurTruncation.md), via a generic cocharacter. The statement asserts a single maximal compact `K` with the minimal-parabolic Iwasawa product and with the positive-torus compatibility of L1 (under every L1 Siegel algebraic setup). This is the choice of a suitable `K` used by `TruncationProjectionStatement`. With reflection data `D` and the character comparison `hρ` of [ArthurParabolicSpaces](ArthurParabolicSpacesStatement.md) supplied, there is a real threshold `C`: for `β(T) > C` for every simple relative root `β`, the pointwise truncation admits an orthogonal projection extension on each of

- `L²(G(F) \ G(𝔸_F)¹, normOneRationalQuotientHaarMeasure F G)`;
- `L²(G(F) \ G(𝔸_F), rationalQuotientHaarMeasure F G)`.

The measures are the two actual L1 quotient Haar measures in [AdelicGroupHaarMeasure](AdelicGroupHaarMeasure.md). The first pointwise operator is [normOneTruncation](ArthurNormOneTruncation.md); the second is `truncation`. In each case a function on the quotient is pulled back with `LeftCosetSpace.mk`, the formula is evaluated at `q.out`, and the asserted comparison is modulo null sets for the corresponding measure. The construction does not restrict an `L²` class on the full quotient to the norm-one quotient.

**Hilbert-space formulation.** `IsOrthogonalL2Extension` asserts a continuous complex-linear map on `MeasureTheory.Lp ℂ 2 m` satisfying Mathlib's `IsStarProjection`: self-adjoint for the Hilbert-space adjoint, and idempotent under composition. This is an orthogonal projection onto its range. For every measurable representative `f` bounded on each compact set and with `MemLp f 2 m`, the representative of the projected class agrees almost everywhere with the pointwise truncation of `f`. This is stronger than mere existence of a projection unrelated to the formula. It does not assume that the latter already represents an `L²` element. The extension from this domain is what is stated; the pointwise idempotence and pairing results are in [ArthurTruncationStatement](ArthurTruncationStatement.md). Neither an extension proof nor the pairing with non-square-integrable uniformly moderately growing functions from the proof of Lapid's Lemma 9 is supplied here.
