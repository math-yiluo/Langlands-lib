# Compact-projected specializations of continued adelic Eisenstein series

This module derives the input to `exists_automorphic_subquotient_of_compact_projected_seeds` from the adelic continuation and covariance theorems. The given scalar section model uses the deformed finite and real Lie actions and the fixed infinite compact action. `EisensteinSeriesInjectiveOn` means that the series of the realized sections separates the vectors of a specified section subspace. The hypotheses include meromorphic continuation, this injectivity on the range of the compact projection in the convergence region, and the algebraic realization of the projection on finite-dimensional subspaces.

Apply continuation on the convergence anchor filter to each constant section. For the finite basis of the projection range, the continued linear combination agrees there with the series of the section given by the same basis coordinates. Injectivity thus holds on a nonempty open subset. The dense regular parameter set from the local numerators meets this subset. The finite and differential generator expansions imply holomorphy of each finite operator vector, and covariance gives the required open identities for these vectors. Compact specialization now realizes the simple quotient on actual automorphic functions and retains its finite adelic action.

This is a derived step in the proof of Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Proposition 2, proof on printed pp. 205–207, Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 200–202. The compact type used to select finitely many seed sections is on printed p. 206. The module takes the analytic continuation and injectivity inputs as hypotheses.

`exists_freeOperatorQuotient_of_adelic_projected_specialization` carries the full operator action through the compact specialization as a surjective map from an invariant free-operator numerator. Its corollary descends to the concrete automorphic operator algebra and retains the finite adelic representation.
