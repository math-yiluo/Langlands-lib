# Continuity and rational parabolic invariance of compact constant terms

For a continuous rational-invariant function, evaluate the constant term
using its descended integrand on the actual radical quotient. Joint
continuity follows by pulling the integrand back along the open quotient
map in its first variable. Integration over the compact, mass-one
quotient preserves continuity in the external argument.

A rational point of the parabolic normalizes the radical on all test
schemes. Its inner isomorphism identifies the compact quotient measure
with itself and rational invariance removes the left translation of the
integrand. This proves invariance under left rational parabolic
translation. Both assertions are internal consequences of the Library
constant-term integral and its measure transport.
