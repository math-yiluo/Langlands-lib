# Nonzero infinite coefficients from compact seed slices

A compact seed family supplies actual smooth scalar block sections with a common infinite coefficient in the normalized compact picture. `infiniteCoefficientFunctions` extends the coefficient to an infinite adelic point `b`: it chooses an infinite parabolic–compact factorization `b = p k`, multiplies by the archimedean half-character extension at `p`, and evaluates the seed coefficient at `c k` on the infinite Levi argument of `p`. For a finite argument, the normalized finite parabolic covariance absorbs its finite Levi action into the finite inducing evaluation. This identifies the extended coefficient on every finite evaluation value with the corresponding actual smooth slice.

The finite evaluations span the coefficient space. Linearity then gives smoothness of the extended coefficient for every vector. If one seed section is nonzero, the extended family cannot vanish because smooth slices distinguish sections. The theorem `nontrivial_sliceTensorCoefficients_of_seed` records this as nontriviality of the invariant coefficient space `sliceTensorCoefficients`. This yields the nonzero element of the invariant coefficient space used in the tensor construction of Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Lemma 1, printed p. 203, Corvallis Part I PDF p. 198.
