# Conjugating the infinite compact for logarithmically square-integrable forms

Right translation by an infinite adelic point preserves the space of
automorphic forms with the logarithmically weighted square-integrability
condition, when the maximal compact is conjugated by that point. The
left split-centre character is unchanged by associativity. The right
translation theorem for automorphic functions transports the compact
finiteness and the other automorphy properties.

The submultiplicative adelic height bounds the pointwise growth of the
matrix height under right translation. Taking the infimum over the
rational orbit and taking logarithms bounds the quotient logarithmic
height by its original value plus a constant. Since the height is at
least one, its ratio under translation is bounded. Right translation is
measure preserving on the rational quotient of the connected reductive
group, so the translated weighted function is in the same `L²` space.
The bounded height ratio then gives the assertion with the original
weight on that quotient.

The theorem uses `rationalLogHeight F G rho` for an arbitrary matrix
representation. In the closed matrix embedding setting, this height uses
Delorme's §2 construction in (2.6)–(2.7) with the chosen truncated adelic
matrix norm. The transport applies to the form-level condition
\(\Phi/\sigma^m\in L^2([G])\). The remark preceding Lemma 7.10, p. 33,
uses \([G]\)-temperedness, which §3, p. 12, defines by the same weighted
condition for every archimedean enveloping-algebra derivative with a common
exponent. The source is P. Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*,
Advances in Mathematics 426 (2023), 109107. The published PDF in
