# Compact polynomial seeds in the actual inducing multiplicities

The infinite compact parabolic acts through its Levi projection on the tensor of the inducing multiplicity spaces. For each nonzero irreducible finite factor, its inclusion gives a nonzero multiplicity vector with finite compact orbit span. Evaluation at one nonzero finite vector is injective. Tensoring these evaluations and using the scalar coefficient realization gives injective scalar coordinates on the tensor of the chosen orbit spans.

The matrix coefficients in these coordinates are continuous by compact right translation of the actual scalar Levi functions. Polynomial averaging on the compact parabolic therefore gives a covariant section nonzero at the identity, with values in the actual infinite multiplicity tensor. This step constructs the compact seed used for Langlands’ Lemma 1, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), printed p. 203, Corvallis Part I PDF p. 198.
