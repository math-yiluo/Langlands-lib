# Character inequalities on the local-finiteness boundary

`exists_finite_ambient_characters_span` chooses finitely many characters of
the maximal split torus by restricting a basis of the ambient rational
characters. Their rational span together with the simple roots is the entire
rationalized torus character lattice. The proof expands every ambient
character in its integer basis and applies restriction and rationalization.

`AdelicLocalFinitenessBoundaryLogBoundsStatement` takes real log coordinates
on the torus character dual whose root coordinates are normalized by positive
rates and whose central zero subspace maps to zero in the ambient real dual.
Under lower bounds for all the root coordinates, upper bounds for the roots
outside a chosen Levi index, and absolute bounds for the central coordinates,
it gives a common lower bound for the simple-root log evaluations. It gives
a common absolute bound for the finitely many auxiliary character evaluations
and the simple-root log evaluations outside the Levi index. All constants
are uniform over real dual elements satisfying these coordinate bounds.

The lower bounds on the scaled root coordinates give the common lower bound
by a finite sum of absolute thresholds. The upper and lower bounds outside
the Levi control the same scaled root coordinates there. An auxiliary
character is evaluated entirely on the central coordinates because it is
restricted from the ambient group. The central coordinate box and the
finite auxiliary character set give one uniform bound by compactness.

These inequalities identify the boundary constraints in Bernstein–Lapid,
*On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series*, (7.6) and §7.7,
with the character bounds used for their relative cuspidal restriction
estimate in §7.4.
