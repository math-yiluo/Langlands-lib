# Regular Eisenstein parameters under ambient transport

An ambient group-scheme isomorphism transports a parabolic presentation,
retaining the abstract parabolic, Levi and split component. The resulting
Eisenstein regularity inequalities have the same threshold, parameter and
truth value.

The ambient isomorphism leaves the positive relative roots and their
restrictions unchanged. It also identifies the rational Weyl groups with
the same character action on the unchanged torus. Reindexing the finite
sum defining the Weyl-averaged form preserves that form. The induced
form on the split component is consequently the same.

This internal transport lemma applies to the Library's specific
Weyl-invariant form and regularity predicate. It supports the reduction to
a suitable rational conjugate of the target parabolic in the proof of
the functional equation.
