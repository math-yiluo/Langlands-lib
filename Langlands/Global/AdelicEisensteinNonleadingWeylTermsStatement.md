# Exclusion of competing Weyl exponents from the leading chamber

Fix a compatible standard parabolic family and a conjugation from
the inducing Levi to a reference Levi, represented by a point that
normalizes the common maximal split torus. Write `w` for its relative
Weyl class. The inverse of `w` sends each selected simple root of the
reference Levi into the selected simple roots of the inducing Levi.

Given a finite set of ambient real shifts, one regularity bound on
the transported parameter excludes every competing Weyl term
described in the statement. A competitor `v` differs from `w` and
sends the selected simple roots of the inducing Levi to positive
roots. The real part of the proposed exponent, embedded in the
ambient character space, is the `v`-translate of the inducing real
parameter plus one of the shifts. Such an exponent fails the
leading condition for the actual positive coroot chamber.

This is the chamber exclusion in Bernstein–Lapid §8.6, where
Lemma 6.8 is applied with the competitor `v w⁻¹`. The hypotheses
give the root action and ambient parameter of each term as inputs
to the chamber argument.

[Bernstein–Lapid, On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series](https://doi.org/10.1090/jams/1020)
