# Lower Iwasawa height along a standard parabolic cone

Lean module: `Langlands.Global.AdelicStandardParabolicConeHeightProof`.

## Statement

```
theorem Langlands.Global.standardParabolicConeHeight :
    Langlands.Global.StandardParabolicConeHeightStatement
```

The type is exactly the proposition `StandardParabolicConeHeightStatement` of the module
`Langlands.Global.AdelicSiegelChamberGeometryStatement`, with no further hypothesis. For an
algebraic Siegel setup `(T, i, ν, T₀, j)` of a connected reductive group `G` over a number field
`F`, a set `J ⊆ Δ = simpleRelativeRoots i ν` missing some simple root, the standard parabolic
`Q = Standard.parabolicScheme i ν J` with an Iwasawa compact `K` (`hKQ : IsIwasawaCompact ...`),
`δ > 0`, a threshold `t` and a compact `Ω ⊆ G(𝔸)`, it gives `w ∈ X^*(Q) ⊗ ℝ`, `c > 0` and `C`
with

`c R − C ≤ ⟨w, H_Q(z b ω)⟩`

for all `z ∈ A_G`, `b` in the weak simple-root chamber `|β(b)| ≥ t`, `ω ∈ Ω` and every real `R`
with `|log |β(b)|| ≤ R` for `β ∈ Δ` and `δ R ≤ log |α(b)|` for `α ∈ Δ \ J`.

## The height functional

Fix a simple root `α ∈ Δ \ J`. The published theorem `standardCocharacter` gives a standard
cocharacter `ζ` for `J` (`IsStandardCocharacter`: `⟨β, ζ⟩ = 0` for `β ∈ J` and `⟨β, ζ⟩ > 0` for
`β ∈ Δ \ J`) whose dynamic parabolic and centralizer are represented by
`Standard.parabolicHom i ν J` and `Standard.leviHom i ν J`. Let `U` be the dynamic unipotent
radical of the cocharacter `ζ` of `G` (composed with `i`; `Dynamic.Affine.unipotentScheme`) and let `χ` be the rational character
`Dynamic.adjointDeterminantCharacter` of `Q`: the determinant of the conjugation action of `Q`
on the Lie algebra of `U`. The functional is `w = 1 ⊗ χ`.

The torus `T` maps into `Q` by `t_Q`, the torus factor `standardLeviTorusFactor` of the Levi
followed by `Dynamic.centralizerToParabolic`, and `t_Q` composed with the inclusion of `Q` is `i`.
By `Dynamic.adjointDeterminantCentralizerRestriction` and
`characterRestrict_centralizerAdjointDeterminant`, the restriction of `χ` along `t_Q` is
`positiveWeightCharacter i ζ`, that is

`χ ∘ t_Q = ∑_{β ∈ s} m_β β`, `m_β = dim 𝔤_β` if `⟨β, ζ⟩ > 0` and `m_β = 0` otherwise,

where `s` is the finite support of `m` (finite because a split torus has finitely many weights
on the Lie algebra).

- Every `β ∈ s` is a positive relative root. It is nonzero and has a nonzero weight space. If
  `⟨β, ν⟩ < 0`, then `−β` is a relative root (`relativeRootNegation`) and positive, so
  `⟨−β, ζ⟩ ≥ 0` (`IsStandardCocharacter.pairing_nonneg_of_positive`), contradicting
  `⟨β, ζ⟩ > 0`.
- `α ∈ s` with `m_α > 0`: `⟨α, ζ⟩ > 0` because `α ∉ J`, and the root space of `α` is nonzero.

## Lower bounds for the root summands

The private lemma `exists_positiveRoot_log_lower_bound` uses
`positiveRelativeRoots_subset_closure_simpleRelativeRoots`: every positive relative root `β`
lies in the additive submonoid generated by `Δ`. Induction over that closure gives `n_β ∈ ℕ`
such that `n_β · lo ≤ ℓ(β)` for every additive map `ℓ` from the character lattice to `ℝ` and every
real `lo` with `lo ≤ ℓ(γ)` for all `γ ∈ Δ`.

## The estimate

By `RestrictionSplitCentre.imageSubgroup_eq_canonicalSubgroup`, `z` is the image of a point `a`
of the split component `Z` of `G` given by the setup, and `b` is the image of a point of `T₀`. Both
factors therefore come from `Q(𝔸)` through `t_Q`, and `IsIwasawaCompact.height_map_mul` gives

`H_Q(z b ω) = log_Q(t_Q a) + log_Q(t_Q b) + H_Q(ω)`.

Pairing with `1 ⊗ χ` (`realParameterPairing_tmul`) and using `characterLog_map`, each torus term
is `∑_{β ∈ s} m_β log |β(x)|`.

- The central term is `0`: every relative root restricts to zero on `Z`
  (`root_restrict_eq_zero_of_isCentral`).
- For `b`, `log |β(b)|` is the logarithm of the idele norm of `(j ≫ β)(b)`. The weak chamber
  gives `log |γ(b)| ≥ log t` for every `γ ∈ Δ` (`mem_weakSimpleRootChamber_iff_log`), so each
  summand with `β ≠ α` is at least `m_β n_β log t`. The cone hypothesis gives
  `m_α log |α(b)| ≥ m_α δ R`.
- The map `ω ↦ ⟨χ, H_Q(ω)⟩` is continuous (`continuous_dualEval` and
  `IsIwasawaCompact.continuous_height`), hence bounded below by some `B` on the compact `Ω`.

The result holds with `c = m_α δ > 0` and `C = −L − B`, where
`L = ∑_{β ∈ s \ {α}} m_β n_β log t`. The hypothesis `|log |β(b)|| ≤ R` is not needed.

## Source

The argument is the coordinate comparison on the cone `α(X) ≥ δ ‖X‖` (`α ∈ Δ \ Δ^Q`) in the proof
of Lemma 5.2 of P. Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*, Advances in Mathematics 426
(2023), 109107, p. 19. The Lean proof does not compare coordinates in `𝔞_Q^G`; it bounds the
modulus character `χ` of `Q` directly, with an additive constant for the weak chamber and the
compact right factor.
