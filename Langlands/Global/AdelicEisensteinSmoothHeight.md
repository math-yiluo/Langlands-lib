# Height smoothness from local Iwasawa factors

`HasSmoothLocalIwasawaFactors` asks for local factorizations of pure infinite elements by a parabolic point and an infinite compact point. It requires smoothness of just the ambient image of the parabolic factor. At a fixed finite argument, the right compact factor commutes with the finite point, so the height is the character logarithm of the parabolic factor plus the height of the finite point. Extend the character logarithm to an ambient smooth function at the parabolic factor and compose with the smooth factor. Tensor induction extends the argument to all complex parameters.

This derived step connects the actual adelic Iwasawa height of Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series* (2024), §2.2–3.1, printed pp. 191–192, article PDF pp. 5–6, to differentiation in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), proof of Proposition 2, printed pp. 205–206, Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 200–201.
