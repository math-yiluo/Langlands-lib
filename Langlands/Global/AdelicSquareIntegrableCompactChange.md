# Transfer of the inducing \(L^2\) condition between compact subgroups

`AdelicSquareIntegrableInducedCompactChangeStatement` asserts that the spaces
`A²_P` of a standard parabolic for two maximal Iwasawa compacts agree if the
compacts have the same archimedean projection.
`adelicSquareIntegrableInducedCompactChange` proves the equivalence. This is a
consequence of the Iwasawa-coordinate description of the square-integrable
inducing space in Lapid, *On Arthur's asymptotic inner product formula of
truncated Eisenstein series* (2011), §2, p. 311, and the Iwasawa
decomposition of Mœglin–Waldspurger, *Spectral Decomposition and Eisenstein
Series* (1995), I.1.13. Lapid fixes a compact in good position. The comparison
below requires Iwasawa coverage and equality of the archimedean projections.

With the same infinite projection, the compact intersection is relatively
open on both sides. The proof covers the second compact by finitely many
left cosets of the intersection. On each coset, it decomposes the
representative as a product of a parabolic factor and a point of the first
compact. Left radical invariance eliminates the unipotent factor. The Levi
factor right translates the canonical Levi quotient and multiplies the
integrand by a fixed modulus. The canonical quotient measure is preserved
under this right translation. The compact Haar measures pulled back from
the two cosets to the intersection are proportional by Haar uniqueness.
These comparisons give integrability on each coset. The common archimedean
projection gives the same compact finiteness group in both automorphy
conditions, and the argument in each direction proves equality of the spaces.
