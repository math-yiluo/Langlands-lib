# Differentiating the normalized standard block evaluation

The local half modulus is multiplicative on the block Levi. For a smooth function on a real or complex general linear group, a curve with matrix tangent `g X` at `g` computes the Library's right derivative in direction `X`.

`blockTensorDifferential` acts by the sum of the individual block operators. For a pure tensor, differentiation of the indexed evaluation along a Levi curve is the finite product rule. Linearity extends the formula to every tensor.

`evaluation_differential_parabolic` compares the ambient derivative evaluated in the compact model at the identity with those block derivatives. Along a parabolic curve through the identity, the normalized formula gives a product of the varying half modulus and the block evaluations through any fixed Levi point. Multiplicativity separates the half modulus at the fixed point. Uniqueness of the scalar derivative and injectivity of indexed evaluation identify the tensor derivative as the scalar derivative of the half modulus times the original tensor, plus the block tensor differential. The curve and its stated derivatives are inputs to the result.

This is an internal differential form of induction in stages. Knapp and Vogan (1995), Chapter XI, (11.196), and Getz and Hahn (2024), §10.1, pp. 270–271 (PDF 282–283), give the normalized evaluation models used in the comparison.
