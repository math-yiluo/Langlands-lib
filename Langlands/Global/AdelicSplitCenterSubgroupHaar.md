# Haar measure on the actual positive-times-principal subgroup

This is an internal construction from `AdelicNormOneDecomposition`,
`AdelicSplitCenterQuotient`, `AdelicSplitCenterHaar` and the actual principal
subgroup topology in `AdelicGroupHaarMeasure`. No external structural theorem
is added as a premise by this module.

Let `F` be a number field, `G` a group scheme over `Spec F`, `T` a commutative
group scheme, and `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G`. The module retains a supplied finite splitting
`e` of `T`, injectivity of `characterRestrict i`, finite index of its range,
affinity of `G`, and scheme-theoretic centrality `hc : IsCentral i`. The measure
results also require `G` to be locally of finite type. These are explicit inputs;
the construction does not prove existence of such a torus for every reductive
`G`.

Write `A = intrinsicSubgroup F T`, `N = normOneSubgroup F G`, and
`Gamma = rationalNormOneSubgroup F G`. All have their existing actual point
carriers and subtype topologies. Write `H = positiveRationalSubgroup F G T i`,
the existing subgroup generated by the positive image and the principal
rational subgroup in the full adelic group.

`positiveRationalDecomposition` is the continuous group equivalence

`A × Gamma ≃ₜ* H`,

sending `(a, q)` to `positivePointMap a * q.val.val`. Its inverse sends `x` to
`(positiveFactor x.val, normOneFactor x.val)`, with the second factor restricted
to `Gamma`. The existing identity `positiveRationalSubgroup_eq_comap` proves
that restriction. The inverse identities, multiplicative law and continuity
come from the actual ambient positive/norm-one decomposition and the subtype
maps. The three simp theorems record the forward formula and both inverse
coordinates. No normality of the rational subgroup is used.

`rationalNormOneSubgroup_count_isHaarMeasure` proves counting measure is Haar
on the actual discrete group `Gamma`: compact subsets of a discrete space are
finite, counting is left invariant, and nonempty open sets have positive
counting measure.

Let `muA = normalizedPositiveHaarMeasure F G T i hi I e hc`. This is the existing
positive Haar normalization for which `muA × Measure.haar` on `A × N` pushes
through actual multiplication to the existing ambient `Measure.haar` exactly.
The definition `positiveRationalSubgroupHaarMeasure` is exactly

`Measure.map positiveRationalDecomposition (muA.prod Measure.count)`.

Thus counting is used on the discrete principal factor `Gamma`. The full
subgroup `H` generally has a nondiscrete positive factor.

`positiveRationalSubgroupHaarMeasure_isHaarMeasure` transfers product Haar
through the continuous group equivalence. The sigma-finiteness theorem uses
the already proved sigma finiteness of `muA` and countability of `Gamma`.
The regularity theorem uses the proved closedness of `H` in the actual locally
compact Polish ambient group, together with the resulting subgroup topology
and Haar local finiteness.

`positiveRationalSubgroupHaarMeasure_isMulRightInvariant` transfers right
invariance of positive Haar times counting through the same group equivalence.
This proves right invariance for the subgroup measure without assuming right
invariance of ambient or norm-one Haar.

`measurePreserving_positiveRationalDecomposition` and its `_symm` companion
prove both directions preserve precisely these measures. For complex-valued
functions, `integrable_positiveRationalDecomposition_iff` separately transports
convergence. `integral_positiveRationalDecomposition` is the corresponding
equality of total Bochner integrals, valid without an integrability premise;
it does not itself assert convergence. Both follow from the measurable
equivalence associated with the actual homeomorphism.

Full quotient Weil compatibility with this subgroup measure remains a separate
obligation. So do general reductive torus existence and character restriction,
ambient analytic unimodularity, reduction theory and finite volume. No such
conclusion is claimed by this focused subgroup construction.
