# Meromorphy of constant-term evaluations of Eisenstein continuations

Fix a coordinate space and an adelic function family that has local
meromorphic presentations in the uniform growth spaces. The
presentations agree with the family on their specified domains.
For a represented closed subgroup with compact rational quotient,
the normalized constant term of the family, evaluated at any fixed
adelic argument, is meromorphic on the coordinate space. One open
dense holomorphy domain works for all arguments.

The assumptions on the local presentations are the analytic part of
the Library's function-valued continuation proposition. The scalar
consequence combines the weak meromorphy of Bernstein–Lapid §3.1, the
Fréchet-valued continuation in the corollary after §8.6, and the actual
constant term of §6.2.

[Bernstein–Lapid, On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series](https://doi.org/10.1090/jams/1020)
