# Weyl double coset representatives on actual adelic points

Fix two represented standard parabolics in a connected reductive
group over a number field. Their rational double cosets have a
finite family of representatives in the normalizer of the common
maximal split torus. Via the rational embeddings in the adelic
group, this family satisfies the `IsDoubleCosetRepresentatives`
predicate of the Eisenstein constant-term proposition.

The Weyl class of each representative sends the source Levi's
selected simple roots to positive roots. Its inverse does the
same for the target Levi's selected simple roots. These are the
two positivity conditions of Bernstein–Lapid Lemma 6.7(i). The
statement expresses the rational double cosets and the relative
root conditions together on the existing Lean group schemes.

[Bernstein–Lapid, On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series](https://doi.org/10.1090/jams/1020)
