# An adelic parabolic period vanishes when its radical constant term vanishes

The rational parabolic lies in the closed image of its intrinsic norm-one adelic group. That group is the semidirect product of the full adelic unipotent radical and its norm-one Levi. The rational lattice splits under the same Levi projection. The proof transports the intrinsic norm-one Haar measure to the closed image and uses the Weil formula to reduce the integrable ambient period to periods on that image.

Fubini on the split parabolic lattice reduces the latter periods to the radical constant terms. The compact rational radical quotient has positive finite Haar mass, so its mass-one normalization preserves vanishing. This gives the period step for eliminating the proper-parabolic summands in the cuspidal test of Bernstein–Lapid (6.18).
