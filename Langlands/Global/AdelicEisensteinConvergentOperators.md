# Operator identities for convergent Eisenstein values

`smoothConvergenceSections` consists of scalar sections with compact-uniform summable majorants at the given parameter whose actual Eisenstein sums are evaluated finite-smooth rational-invariant functions. Linearity of summation on the majorant domain gives `smoothEisensteinSeries` with values in the scalar range of the original smooth carrier.

The two translation lemmas identify finite translation of the twisted section and infinite compact translation of the untwisted section with the corresponding concrete actions on the sum. The infinite compact lemma uses membership in the Iwasawa compact so that the height factor cancels. The differential lemma uses smooth slices of the twisted section, compact majorants for all first directional terms, and the original smooth output. It applies termwise differentiation and identifies the result with the concrete real Lie generator on the finite-smooth function carrier.

These derived identities implement the equivariance of the convergent Eisenstein sum needed in Langlands' holomorphic-section argument for Proposition 2, printed pp. 205–207 (Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 200–202). The normalization of the twisted sum is that of Bernstein–Lapid, Section 2.2 and equation (2.1), printed p. 191 (PDF p. 5).
