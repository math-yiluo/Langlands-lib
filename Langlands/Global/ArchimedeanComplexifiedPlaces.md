# Complex matrix coordinates for the full archimedean Lie algebra

This internal comparison puts the Library's full archimedean Lie algebra
in coordinates for the parabolic center comparison of Harish-Chandra,
*Automorphic forms on semisimple Lie groups* (1968), Chapter I, §6,
printed pp. 19–20 (PDF pp. 29–30 of the registered edition).

Scalar extension commutes with the finite products of real and complex
place fields and with the finite matrix entry array. A real place gives
one complex factor. A complex place gives two complex factors because
$\mathbb C\otimes_{\mathbb R}\mathbb C$ splits through the identity and
complex conjugation on the second tensor factor. The resulting matrix
coordinate ring consists of matrices over the product of these fields.

The coordinate map preserves the complex Lie bracket. On pure tensors
the scalar-extension bracket agrees with the matrix commutator, and the
comparison preserves multiplication in the associative matrix algebra.
Additivity of both brackets extends the equality to the full
complexified Lie algebra. The entry formula records the real inclusion
and the two complex place maps explicitly.
