# Weighted pointwise adelic convolution

`Langlands.Global.AdelicWeightedPointwiseConvolutionStatement` gives a pointwise estimate for right convolution on a rational-parabolic quotient. Write `H` for the adelic height of a fixed positive-rank closed matrix representation, `H_P` for the Iwasawa height of a supplied compact subgroup, and `Γ` for the principal image of the parabolic. The real weight parameter `ell` is continuous and satisfies `exp(ell(H_P(g))) ≤ A H(g)^r` with `A > 0` and `r ≥ 0`.

For a compact set of finite components and a compact kernel support `C`, the estimate holds at every `x` with finite component in that set and `H_P(xC)` contained in a supplied measurable height set `S`. The input is a measurable left `Γ`-invariant function `f` with finite squared integral `I` on the chosen fundamental domain. The density there is `exp(-2 ell(H_P(g)))` on the preimage of `S` and zero off it. For every kernel `h` supported in `C` and square integrable for Haar measure restricted to `C`, the integrand `h(a) f(xa)` is integrable on the ambient adelic group and its integral has norm at most `B H(x)^R ‖h‖₂ √I`. The exponent `R` is fixed before both compact sets are chosen. The constant `B` can depend on them and on the weight bound.

The proof pushes the restricted Haar measure forward from the fundamental domain and writes the restriction of the quotient measure using an indicator. The resulting identity equates `I` with the integral of the squared norm of the quotient lift against the weighted quotient measure. In particular the lift defines an `L²` vector of norm `√I`.

Compactness gives `L ≥ 1` bounding `H` on `C`. Submultiplicativity bounds `exp(ell(H_P(xa)))` by `A L^r H(x)^r` there. Thus the density on the image of `xC` is bounded below by the inverse square of this quantity. The support domination theorem bounds the pullback measure by a multiple of the weighted quotient measure, and Cauchy–Schwarz gives integrability and the bound. If `d` is the power in support domination, the square root of its coefficient gives `R = d/2 + r`.

This is a derived form of the pointwise estimate used in Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series*, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 37 (2024), §7.4, Lemma (2), p. 216. The paper uses a Siegel domain whose right thickening by the kernel support is contained in an enlarged domain. Here the containment enters as a height-set hypothesis at the evaluation point. The measure proof implements the compact-support kernel bound through the rational conjugate count of Mœglin–Waldspurger, *Spectral Decomposition and Eisenstein Series*, I.2.4(a)(i) and I.2.5, pp. 25–27.
