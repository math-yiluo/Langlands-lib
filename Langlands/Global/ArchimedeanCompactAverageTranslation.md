# Finite-rank compact averages after simultaneous translation

A compact-index weighted average of a simultaneous translate lies in the algebraically stable submodule when the original family is jointly finite and the average has finite-dimensional image on the submodule. Here the averages use the actual derivative-smooth functions and the averaging parameters lie in the standard compact subgroup.

Within the submodule, the average lies in the closure of a finite compact orbit span. That span is closed and stays in the submodule. For the translated family, the simultaneous orbit density theorem gives membership in the closure of the submodule. For each group point and family index, evaluation of the average depends continuously on the original family. The proof writes it as the integral of a continuous compact-index function and uses continuity of integration on `Lp` of order one. The image of the submodule under these averaged point evaluations is finite-dimensional, hence closed in the pointwise product topology. The average of the translated family has evaluations in that image. Since the evaluations are injective, the average belongs to the finite-dimensional image of the original submodule, and therefore to the submodule itself.
