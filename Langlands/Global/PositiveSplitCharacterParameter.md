# Positive continuous characters as parabolic parameters

The normalized logarithm identifies the positive split torus with a finite-dimensional real vector space. A continuous character into positive reals becomes a continuous additive function after taking its real logarithm, so it is real linear. Expand it in the coordinate characters of the split torus.

Finite index of the integral restriction lifts a positive integer multiple of each coordinate character to the ambient rational character group. Dividing its coefficient by that integer gives a complex tensor parameter with the required value on every positive split point. The same construction applies in rank zero with an empty coordinate sum. This is the exponent construction for the positive Haar ratio in the intertwining integral.
