# Intertwiners of adelic tensors from intertwiners at the places

`tensorMap` tensors the finite adelic map with each real and complex map,
transporting the infinite maps through the canonical universe lifts. The
map is injective if every factor map is injective and surjective if every
factor map is surjective.

`tensorIntertwiningMap` equips that map with equivariance for the full
free operator algebra. The tensor map commutes with a one-slot operator
when the corresponding local map does. Thus it intertwines the finite
translations and each real and complex compact and Lie generator.
`intertwines_freeActionOfOperators_of_places` extends these identities
to the full actions on the two tensors.

This is the algebraic operation on the tensor of local constituents in
Langlands (1979), Lemma 1, printed p. 203 (Corvallis I PDF p. 198), used in
Getz–Hahn (2024), Theorem 10.7.5, printed p. 288 (PDF p. 300).
