# Ambient shifts of the Eisenstein parameter

Let `p : P ⟶ G` be the actual affine closed subgroup map over a number field and `K` a compact subgroup with `P(𝔸)K = G(𝔸)`. Denote by `p*ν = parameterRestrict p ν` the pullback of the ambient parameter `ν ∈ X*(G) ⊗ ℂ` (the Library's scalar extension of rational character restriction). This module gives two propositions, grouped as `Langlands.Global.EisensteinAmbientShiftStatement`:

1. For the actual defining sum on `P(F) \ G(F)`, shifting `λ` to `λ + p*ν` multiplies the result at `g` by the actual ambient character `g^ν = exp ⟨ν, H_G(g)⟩`. The first proposition uses the Library's unconditional `tsum` convention, including its default value for nonsummable series. It asserts no new convergence region.
2. For a connected reductive `G` and a presented parabolic, fix the inducing function and the evaluation point `g`. Let `E` be a scalar meromorphic function in the existing linear-coordinate sense, holomorphic on `D`, and equal to the defining sum at every parameter in `D` that is sufficiently regular in the existing positive-root convention. For `Re ν = 0`, the same shift identity holds when `λ` and `λ + p*ν` are both in `D`.

The source conventions are Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series*, JAMS 37 (2024), §2.1, p. 190 (PDF p. 4), and §2.2, (2.1), p. 191 (PDF p. 5), with meromorphy and local denominators as in §3.1, p. 192 (PDF p. 6). These are derived identities, not an assertion transcribed from the paper. The first follows from the ambient logarithm of `p(q)k` being the image of `H_P(p(q)k)` and the product formula on `G(F)`. The second uses uniqueness of meromorphic continuation after an imaginary translation, which preserves the regular tube. It requires no values at poles and does not assert existence of continuation, uniform-moderate-growth continuation, or holomorphy on the imaginary axis. It applies to imaginary-axis values when the supplied `D` contains the imaginary axis, as it does in the wave-packet definitions.
