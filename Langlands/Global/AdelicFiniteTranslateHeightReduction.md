# Rational height reduction from finitely many Siegel translates

The finite-volume covering construction puts a rational translate of each norm-one point in a weak Siegel product followed by one of finitely many right factors. Adjoin the finite projections of those factors to the compact set of finite coordinates. The positive central factor has trivial finite projection. For the height bound, remove the right factor before applying norm minimality on the weak Siegel product. The full height of that factor and its inverse are uniformly bounded over the finite set. Submultiplicativity and the polynomial bound on the central factor give one constant and exponent for all adelic points.

This derived proof uses the finite-translate covering in `AdelicFiniteVolumeProof`, the norm minimality theorem in `AdelicSiegelNormMinimalityProof`, and the positive central factorization in `AdelicCentralPositiveHeightProof`. It implements the height comparison of Mœglin–Waldspurger, *Spectral decomposition and Eisenstein series* (1995), I.2.1–2.2, printed pp. 20–21, chapter PDF pp. 3–4, with a finite union of right translates. The resulting rational height reduction applies to the finite-level slice-to-adelic growth comparison used for the inducing cusp forms.
