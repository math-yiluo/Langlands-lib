# Lebesgue coordinates for the split centre

For a real basis of the central directions in a maximal split torus, `WavePacketCentreCoordinatesStatement` supplies positive points of the actual split centre and a normalized norm-one Haar measure. The logarithm of the point in the maximal split torus evaluates characters against the corresponding linear combination of the basis. With Lebesgue measure in these coordinates, multiplication with the norm-one subgroup recovers ambient Haar integration. The corresponding full quotient chart preserves the counting-normalized quotient measures.

The construction uses the real cocharacter equivalence for the actual inclusion of the split centre in the maximal split torus. The perfect split-torus pairing takes a cocharacter to a character functional. The inverse positive logarithm then takes this functional to an adelic point. The logarithm calculation follows from functoriality and the basis expansion. The pullback of normalized positive Haar to these coordinates is a positive multiple of Lebesgue measure by Haar uniqueness. Moving the multiple to norm-one Haar leaves the ambient product measure unchanged, and the discrete quotient construction commutes with the same rescaling. This gives both measure identities with the same choice of norm-one Haar measure.

This is the coordinate and measure step of the split-centre Fourier transform in E.
