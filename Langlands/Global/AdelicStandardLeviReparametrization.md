# Reparametrization of the standard Levi models

A family of standard parabolics in a fixed connected reductive number-field group can use isomorphic scheme models for its Levis. The construction supplies smooth relative dimension data on the replacement models. It precomposes each Levi inclusion by the isomorphism and postcomposes the split component and maximal split torus maps by its inverse. The parabolic and radical maps are the original ones.

The lemmas compare the resulting rational inducing subgroups, positive split components, automorphy predicates on ambient functions, and unnormalized exponents. They also transport maximality of the Levi intersections of the same ambient compact subgroup. The proof uses `ParabolicRootPresentation.leviIsoComp` for the algebraic compatibility and transports the compact intersections by the isomorphisms of adelic point groups.

This is a change of model for the Levi cusp tests in the convergent comparison of Bernstein–Lapid, §6.10, equation (6.20).
