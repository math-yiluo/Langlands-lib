# The coroot chart of a displaced Weyl term

`WeylConeCorootChartStatement` identifies the affine coroot evaluations in a Weyl summand of the displaced formula in Delorme's Proposition 7.8. For inducing parabolic \(P\) and a summand at \(Q\), the chosen normalizer representative transports the Levi of \(P\) to the Levi of \(Q\). The linear Weyl map preserves the relative parameter space defined by annihilation of the ambient split centre. Composing its restriction with the real coroot coordinates at \(Q\) gives the continuous linear equivalence used for Fourier testing.

Along the displaced plane \(t\Lambda+i\lambda\), evaluation of the transported parameter minus the unitary test exponent \(i\mu\) on each projected coroot has real part \(t\) times the transported real slope. The imaginary coordinates are the Weyl-transformed coroot coordinates of \(\lambda\) minus those of \(\mu\). The real-part comparison uses the ambient Weyl action on restrictions to the common maximal split torus. The complex evaluations are affine holomorphic on the full coordinate space, and the displacement chart is smooth with its zero slice in the full imaginary plane.

`exists_weylConeDisplacement` chooses one relative direction for all Weyl terms whose normalizer class sends the simple roots of the inducing Levi onto those of the term's Levi. It uses the parameter with unit coroot values outside the inducing Levi, then applies the Weyl orientation theorem to the inverse class, which sends the term's Levi roots back to the inducing roots. The slopes are nonzero, and they are all positive exactly when the term has the inducing index and the normalizer class is the identity.
