# Deformed invariant differentiation on smooth block-induced sections

The ordinary matrix section differential agrees with the invariant derivative on each infinite slice of the scheme adeles. The derivative of the parameter height gives a multiplier that preserves smooth slices and compact finiteness. Adding this multiplier to the ordinary differential implements the derivative of the height-twisted section on the original carrier. The twisted slices detect the real Lie relations, giving an action of the original matrix enveloping algebra. Every real generator has a finite holomorphic expansion, and the entire enveloping action specializes at zero to the original action.

This derived construction implements archimedean differentiation of the holomorphic section family in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), proof of Proposition 2, printed pp. 205–206, Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 200–201.
