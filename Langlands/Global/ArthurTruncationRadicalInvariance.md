# Invariance under adelic radicals

For a closed represented smooth connected unipotent subgroup, right translation acts on the compact rational quotient and preserves its normalized Haar measure. Thus left translation by an adelic point of the subgroup preserves its constant-term integral, for a rational-invariant input.

The order of the standard parabolics reverses the order of their radicals. Every standard radical lies in the minimal radical. The minimal Iwasawa height is unchanged by left translation from that radical because all rational characters of a unipotent group are trivial. The same invariance holds for the cutoff factor. The two facts give left radical invariance of each truncation kernel. These are derived properties of the actual subgroup functors and the normalized quotient measures in the truncation formula.
