# Compact representatives in minimal-parabolic Iwasawa coordinates

`MinimalIwasawaCompactRepresentativesStatement` uses an actual Siegel algebraic setup for a number-field group \(G\), its canonical minimal parabolic \(P_0\), its maximal split torus \(T\), and a compact Iwasawa subgroup \(K\) for \(P_0\). It constructs a continuous section \(a\) from the real character dual of \(P_0\) to the intrinsic positive points of the full torus \(T\). The logarithm of \(a(h)\) in \(P_0\) is \(h\).

There is one compact subset \(\Omega\) of \(P_0(\mathbb A)^1\), with norm one taken in \(P_0\), so that each adelic \(x\) has a representative

\[
  qx = z\,a(H_0(x))\,k,\qquad q\in P_0(F),\ z\in\Omega,\ k\in K.
\]

The formula uses the actual inclusions into \(G(\mathbb A)\). Restriction to the Levi identifies its rational characters with those of \(P_0\). The lift of \(T\) is a split component of that Levi, so the further restriction to \(T\) is injective with finite index. The positive logarithm equivalence then gives \(a\). Dividing the parabolic factor of \(x\) on the right by \(a(H_0(x))\) leaves a norm-one point. The published compact cover of the minimal Levi and the unipotent radical give compact norm-one representatives in \(P_0\), producing the displayed formula.

For a compact subset of the height space, the formula puts its inverse image in the rational-parabolic quotient inside the continuous image of a product of compact sets. The inverse image is closed by continuity of height, so it too is compact. This proves the compact-preimage property used in Bernstein–Lapid, §7.1, p. 210, for the given Iwasawa compact.
