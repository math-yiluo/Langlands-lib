# Parameter twists in nested constant terms and Levi pairings

Let \(J\) be contained in \(P\) in the standard parabolic family. The inclusion into \(P\) factors the radical and Levi inclusions for \(J\). Characters of \(P\) are trivial on the unipotent radical of \(J\). Their restrictions to its Levi have absolute value one on the norm-one subgroup.

The \(P\)-height factor is therefore constant on the radical orbit used in the normalized constant term at \(g\). In the Levi pairing, the same factor is constant on the norm-one Levi orbit of \(g\). Both identities follow by pulling the scalar out of the actual integrals. Their Bochner integral formulations apply to arbitrary functions. In an application that uses pairing linearity, absolute convergence is a separate hypothesis.

This calculation uses the parameter twist of Bernstein–Lapid §2.2 and the norm-one pairing of §6.2, equation (6.8). It is needed for scalar meromorphy of the Levi pairings of the common inducing term in §8.6.
