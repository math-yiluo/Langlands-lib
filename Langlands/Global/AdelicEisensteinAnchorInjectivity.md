# Eisenstein injectivity on a finite-dimensional section space

Choose a basis of the space of inducing sections. Continuation supplies local Fréchet-valued meromorphic data for the basis sections. Evaluate at adelic points, apply coordinate gluing, and intersect the finitely many domains of holomorphy. The generic injectivity theorem then gives an open dense set on which a relation among continued basis sections forces the same relation among the inducing functions.

Increase the convergence threshold to cover the agreement and compact-majorant bounds. Linearity on the compact-majorant subspace identifies each ordinary convergent Eisenstein series with the linear combination determined by its inducing function. On the intersection with the open dense set, the generic relation criterion and injectivity of the section map give injectivity for the ordinary Eisenstein series. That intersection is a basic set of the convergence anchor filter.

The reduction uses Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series* (2024), Theorem 2.3(1), p. 191 (PDF p. 5), Corollary 8.6, p. 226 (PDF p. 40), and formula (8.4), p. 225 (PDF p. 39).
