# Invariance, continuity and growth of regular intertwining integrals of a standard family

Module `Langlands.Global.AdelicStandardIntertwiningRegularity` proves one theorem,
`StandardParabolicFamily.exists_intertwiningIntegral_regular`.

## Statement

Let `fam` be a standard parabolic family, `I` and `Q` standard indices, and `(n, c)` a Weyl
conjugation from `P_I` to `P_Q` (`IsWeylConjugation`) with a Levi projection `proj`,
`l_Q ≫ proj = 𝟙`. Let `K` be a maximal compact subgroup with `P_I(𝔸)K = P_Q(𝔸)K = G(𝔸)`, let
`rho` be a closed matrix embedding of positive size, and let `φ` be automorphic on
`U_I(𝔸) M_I(F) \ G(𝔸)` for `K_∞`. Write

`I_λ(g) = ∫_{(U_Q(F) ∩ n U_I(𝔸) n⁻¹) \ U_Q(𝔸)} φ_λ(n⁻¹ v g) dv`

for the intertwining integral `hKI.intertwiningIntegral (iU I) (iU Q ≫ p Q) n φ λ`. It is the
intertwining operator `M(n, λ)φ` twisted back at `wλ`
(`intertwiningIntegral_eq_parameterTwist`, module
`Langlands.Global.AdelicStandardFamilyPresentation`).

There is a threshold `R₀` such that for every `λ` that is `R₀`-regular for the standard
presentation of `P_I`, the function `I_λ`

1. is left invariant under `U_Q(𝔸) M_Q(F)`;
2. is continuous;
3. has moderate growth for `rho`.

## Proof

The general Library theorems are applied to the standard parabolics of the family. Their
side conditions are supplied by the family: the subgroups are affine as closed subgroups of the
affine group `G`, `P_Q` is parabolic with rational Levi `M_Q` and geometric unipotent radical
`U_Q`, and Levi-first multiplication `M_Q × U_Q → P_Q` is an isomorphism
(`ParabolicRootPresentation.isIso_subgroupMultiplication_of_radical`).

1. **Invariance.** `parabolicQuotientSubgroup` of `P_Q` is generated by `U_Q(𝔸)` and the
   rational points of `M_Q` (`isLeftInvariantUnder_sup`).
   `IsIwasawaCompact.intertwiningIntegral_radical_mul` gives invariance under `U_Q(𝔸)`, and
   `IsIwasawaCompact.intertwiningIntegral_levi_rational_mul` gives invariance under `M_Q(F)`.
   Both hold at every `λ`.
2. **Continuity.** `intertwiningAbsoluteConvergence` gives a threshold beyond which the
   integrand is dominated by one integrable function, uniformly for `g` in a compact set.
   `IsIwasawaCompact.continuous_intertwiningIntegral` then gives continuity by dominated
   convergence.
3. **Moderate growth.** `intertwiningModerateGrowth` gives a threshold beyond which
   `‖M(n, λ)φ(g)‖ ≤ C ‖g‖^r`. The height factor at `wλ` for `P_Q` is bounded by `C' H(g)^{e'}`
   (`IsIwasawaCompact.exists_norm_heightPower_le_adelicHeight`). The adelic height is a positive
   multiple `s · ‖g‖` of the adelic source norm (`adelicHeight_eq_scalar_mul_sourceNorm`). Since
   `I_λ` is the product of `M(n, λ)φ` and this height factor,
   `‖I_λ(g)‖ ≤ C C' s^{e'} ‖g‖^{r + e'}`.

The threshold is the larger of the thresholds of steps 2 and 3.

## Source

This is part of C. Mœglin and J.-L. Waldspurger, *Spectral decomposition and Eisenstein series*
(1995), II.1.6, Proposition (i), pp. 87–88 (chapter file `06.1_pp_78_100_Definitions.pdf`),
registered as `moeglin-waldspurger-1995-spectral-decomposition`: for `Re λ` in the cone of
convergence, the intertwining integral converges absolutely and locally uniformly in `g`, and
defines an automorphic form on `U_Q(𝔸) M_Q(F) \ G(𝔸)`. The theorem here keeps the three
properties used for the cuspidal tests, without the finiteness conditions.
