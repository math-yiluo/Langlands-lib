# Rational coset evaluation of adelic Eisenstein terms

The multiplication isomorphism of a rational Levi and its geometric
unipotent radical splits each rational parabolic point into two rational
factors. The greatest-subgroup property of the supplied radical identifies
the unipotent factor in its image, and naturality of the principal-point map
puts the two factors in the inducing subgroup. The automorphic function is
invariant under this subgroup, and the Iwasawa height is invariant under
rational parabolic translation by the product formula.

`IsIwasawaCompact.parameterTwistOnCosets` descends the parameter twist along
the rational right-coset quotient. The resulting value agrees with the
published Eisenstein term on every representative of the coset. This
internal comparison implements the representative independence in the
series of Mœglin–Waldspurger II.1.5 and Bernstein–Lapid §2.2.
