# Relative wave-packet tests of an automorphic form

`RelativeWavePacketTestedFormStatement` is the passage from full packet orthogonality to absolute convergence and vanishing for the relative tests on the norm-one quotient in Delorme 2023, p. 35, just before Lemma 7.11. The hypotheses include the presented parabolic and its Iwasawa compact, the minimal-parabolic Iwasawa datum for the ambient group, a closed matrix embedding, and a logarithmically weighted square-integrable automorphic form with a unitary split-centre character. Each presented relative packet is tested against the conjugate of that form on the norm-one quotient.

The proof assumes the ambient wave-packet lift, relative wave-packet descent and rapid decay of full packets. It translates the smooth compact spectral bump to the coordinate of the form's central character. The positive split-centre Fourier inversion gives a nonzero central integral. The ambient lift is a full packet, and the opposite logarithmic weights in the packet and form hypotheses give absolute integrability of the full product by Hölder's inequality. The original pairing is zero by orthogonality, and complex conjugation gives zero in the order used here. The quotient product pairing theorem returns integrability on the norm-one quotient, and cancellation of the nonzero central scalar gives vanishing.
