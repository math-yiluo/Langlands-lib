# Regularity under comparison of standard parabolic presentations

Two presentations in the same ambient maximal split torus, chamber and standard index have the same parameter regularity tests at corresponding characters. To see the tests in the ambient torus, remove the positive roots in the monoid generated by the chosen Levi simple roots. These are exactly the ambient positive roots whose restrictions to the split component are zero. The published equality between the orthogonal lift and restriction through the actual Levi then expresses the inequalities using a single ambient form.

A comparison of the parabolics over their inclusions identifies the two lifted torus maps into the original parabolic, by monicity of its inclusion. Character restriction along the comparison gives the same ambient real parameter. This proves equality of the thresholds, with each presentation retaining its own split component.

The transport is derived from the regularity convention of Bernstein–Lapid §2.2 and the Library's orthogonal lift and actual Levi character restriction.
