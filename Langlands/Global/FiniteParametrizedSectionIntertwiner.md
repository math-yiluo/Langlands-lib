# Intertwiners defined by linear section evaluations

For each standard compact parameter and finite adelic matrix, give a
linear map from the source of a smooth finite adelic representation to
the coefficient tensor. Assume finite parabolic covariance and the
evaluation equation for finite right translation. These maps assemble
into `linearEvaluationIntertwiner` into the Library's normalized
parametrized finite induction.

The open subgroup fixing a source vector fixes every evaluation of its
section by the translation equation. Pointwise extensionality proves
linearity and equivariance and identifies the kernel with the vectors
whose evaluations all vanish. This is an internal construction for the
projected Levi coefficient sections in cuspidal support.
