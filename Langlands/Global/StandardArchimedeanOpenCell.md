# A compact-factor archimedean open cell

Let `G` be a connected reductive affine group over a number field, with
a maximal split torus and a generic cocharacter. Write `U` for the
minimal unipotent radical of the cocharacter, `V` for that of its
negative and `M` for the standard Levi of the negative cocharacter.
`StandardArchimedeanOpenCellStatement` gives compact subsets of
`U(F∞)`, `M(F∞)` and `V(F∞)` whose ordered product contains a neighborhood
of the identity in `G(F∞)`.

For each archimedean completion, the multiplication `U × P⁻ → G` is an
open immersion of schemes for the opposite parabolic `P⁻`. The Levi
decomposition identifies `P⁻` with `M × V` in Levi-first order. At the
point given by the identities, the three factor maps take the identity
values and are continuous. The preimage of any three identity
neighborhoods is therefore a neighborhood of that point. Its image
under the open embedding on completion points gives the required
product neighborhood locally.

Choose compact neighborhoods for the three factors at each
archimedean completion. The finite product of the local product
neighborhoods is a neighborhood in the archimedean point group and
the three compact factor families assemble via the completion
homeomorphisms. Compatibility of completion with the group-scheme
maps identifies the assembled multiplication with the indicated product.

The open immersion and Levi multiplication are from
Conrad, Gabber and Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive groups*, second edition,
Cambridge University Press, 2015, Proposition 2.1.8(2)–(3),
printed p. 53 and PDF p. 79. The compact-factor conclusion follows
from those scheme results and the topology of the archimedean points.
