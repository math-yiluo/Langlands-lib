# Iwasawa height under conjugation of a parabolic

For an Iwasawa compact \(K\) of \(p:P\to G\), conjugate the parabolic map by a
rational point \(s\). The new map has the same source \(P\), so both heights
take values in the same real character dual. The compact \(K\) is Iwasawa for
the new map. Its height at \(sg\) is the old height at \(g\) plus the new
height at \(s\). Consequently their exponential height factors differ by a
scalar depending on the parameter and \(s\).

The proof factors \(s^{-1}\) into a parabolic point and a point of \(K\).
The conjugated parabolic image is then the conjugate by the inverse compact
factor, giving the same Iwasawa coverage. To compare heights, factor \(g\)
as a parabolic point times a point of \(K\), and rewrite \(sg\) using the
new parabolic image. Left multiplication by a parabolic point adds the same
character logarithm for both maps, and the right compact factor drops out.

This is a derived transport identity for the Iwasawa height of Bernstein
and Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series* (2024),
§2.1, journal p. 190, and its exponential in §2.2, p. 191.
