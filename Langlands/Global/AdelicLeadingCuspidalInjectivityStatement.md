# Injectivity of leading cuspidal components

Let \(G\) be a connected reductive group over a number field with a compatible family of standard parabolics. Let \(K\) be maximal compact with Iwasawa coverage for the minimal standard parabolic and with maximal compact intersections in the standard Levis. If two automorphic functions on \(G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A)\) have the same family of leading cuspidal components, they agree.

The family is specified by `IsGlobalLeadingCuspidalComponents`. Its clauses use the actual unipotent constant term, the norm-one Levi cuspidal pairing, and the direct sum of the exponent components in the closed positive coroot chamber. The statement is the injectivity clause of Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series*, Theorem 6.3, expressed using the relation for a supplied family of components. The compact assumptions state the two Iwasawa and Levi properties used in the source setup in §2.1.
