# Smooth and analytic restrictions to one archimedean place

`PlaceRestrictionStatement` uses the actual inclusions of the real and
complex local general linear groups into the mixed archimedean group of a
number field. Each inclusion is smooth in the real matrix charts and agrees on
unitary matrices with the standard compact inclusion. The
ambient insertion of a local matrix has identity entries at the other
places. Pullback along this insertion preserves real analyticity at each
unit where the original ambient function is analytic.

For a smooth scalar function on the full archimedean group, the
derivative of its restricted coordinate extension at a local unit
\(g\) in direction \(gX\) equals the original smooth differential in the
Lie direction obtained by inserting \(X\) at the same place. The value
on the original group is taken at the image of \(g\), with the usual
conversion from mixed to completion coordinates. At a complex place
the matrix direction is considered over the reals.

The proof compares the coordinate entries of the actual inclusion with
the Library's single-place linear insertion and affine base matrix. The
same comparison identifies the two inclusions on the compact group.
The unit-group charts give smoothness, and the affine maps are real analytic.
The linear part inserts zero at the other places. Multiplying an
inserted direction by the affine image of \(g\) gives the insertion of
\(gX\). The Fréchet chain rule then gives both derivative identities.
This internal result applies these coordinate formulas to arbitrary
smooth scalar functions, including the Hilbert coefficients used to
study the closure of a cuspidal place component.
