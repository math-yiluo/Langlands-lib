# Local degrees and Galois completions at finite places

Module `Langlands.Global.AdelicFunctoriality.LocalDegree`, namespace
`Langlands.Global.AdelicFunctoriality`.

This module is shared infrastructure for the global class field theory tasks. It gathers
facts about the finite places of a finite extension of number fields `L / K` and their
completions `L_w / K_v`. Here `K_v = p.adicCompletion K` and `L_w = q.adicCompletion L`
for finite places `p : HeightOneSpectrum (𝓞 K)` and `q : HeightOneSpectrum (𝓞 L)` with
`q` over `p` (`q.asIdeal.LiesOver p.asIdeal`). The module has one definition, `placeAbove`.
Everything else is a theorem. `K`, `L` and the base field `F` of a tower may lie in
different universes.

## Places above a place

- `finitePlacesAbove_nonempty` (instance): for every finite place `p` of `K`, the
  published finite type `FinitePlacesAbove L p` of finite places of `L` over `p` is
  nonempty. Mathlib's `Ideal.nonempty_primesOver` gives a prime of `𝓞 L` over
  `p.asIdeal`, and that prime is nonzero because `p.asIdeal` is
  (`Ideal.ne_bot_of_mem_primesOver`).
- `placeAbove L p : FinitePlacesAbove L p` (definition): one finite place of `L` over
  `p`, chosen with `Classical.arbitrary`. Nothing about the choice is canonical. Results
  about `placeAbove L p` should only use that it is some place over `p`.
- `exists_place_above`: for every finite place `p` of `K` there is a finite place `q`
  of `L` with `q.asIdeal.LiesOver p.asIdeal`. This needs only `[Algebra K L]` and
  `[NumberField L]`, so it holds without `[NumberField K]`.

## Local degrees

- `localDegree_eq`: `[L_w : K_v] = e(w|v) f(w|v)`. In Lean this is
  `Module.finrank (p.adicCompletion K) (q.adicCompletion L) =
  q.asIdeal.ramificationIdx (𝓞 K) * q.asIdeal.inertiaDeg (𝓞 K)`.
  The proof combines three published results. `completedLocalDegreeLowerBound` gives
  `e f ≤ [L_w : K_v]` at every place over `p`. `finiteLocalDegree_sum_le` gives
  `Σ_w [L_w : K_v] ≤ [L : K]`. `finitePlacesAbove_sum_ramification_inertia` gives
  `Σ_w e f = [L : K]`. So the termwise inequalities sum to an equality, and
  `Finset.sum_eq_sum_iff_of_le` then makes each one an equality.
- `localDegree_dvd`: if `L / K` is Galois, then `[L_w : K_v]` divides `[L : K]`. After
  `localDegree_eq`, this is Mathlib's Galois fundamental identity
  `Ideal.ncard_primesOver_mul_ramificationIdxIn_mul_inertiaDegIn`, which says
  `#{w | v} · e · f = |Gal(L/K)| = [L : K]`. That identity is used together with
  `Ideal.ramificationIdxIn_eq_ramificationIdx`, `Ideal.inertiaDegIn_eq_inertiaDeg` and
  `IsGalois.card_aut_eq_finrank`.
- `localDegree_dvd_localDegree`: take a tower `F ⊆ K ⊆ L` (with `IsScalarTower F K L`)
  and finite places `q | v' | v` of `L`, `K`, `F`. Then `[K_{v'} : F_v]` divides
  `[L_q : F_v]`. Both sides are `e f` by `localDegree_eq`, and `e` and `f` are
  multiplicative in towers (`Ideal.ramificationIdx_tower`, `Ideal.inertiaDeg_tower`).
  The statement assumes all three `LiesOver` instances, including `q` over `v`.

## Galois completions

- `subalgebra_eq_top_of_algebraMap_mem`: a `K_v`-subalgebra of `L_w` that contains the
  image of `L` is all of `L_w`. The published `finiteTensorMap_surjective` (the
  `K_v`-algebra map `K_v ⊗_K L → Π_{w | v} L_w` is surjective) shows that `L_w` is spanned over `K_v` by
  the image of `L`. The proof writes an element `x` of `L_w` as the image of a tensor
  whose value is `Pi.single w x`, then uses induction on tensors.
- `isGalois_adicCompletion`: if `L / K` is Galois, then `L_w / K_v` is Galois. Take a
  primitive element `α` of `L / K` (`Field.exists_primitive_element`) with minimal
  polynomial `f`. The proof shows that `L_w` is a splitting field of `f` over `K_v`:
  - `f` splits in `L` (`IsGalois.splits`), hence in `L_w`.
  - The `K_v`-subalgebra generated by the roots contains the image of `α` and of `K`,
    so it contains the image of `L = K[α]`. By `subalgebra_eq_top_of_algebraMap_mem` it
    is all of `L_w`.

  `f` is separable, so `IsGalois.of_separable_splitting_field` applies.

## Provenance

Derived. The mathematics is standard valuation theory as in Neukirch, *Algebraic Number
Theory* (`neukirch-1999-algebraic-number-theory`):

- Chapter II §8, (8.3)–(8.5), printed pp.164–165 (PDF pp.179–180):
  `L ⊗_K K_v ≅ Π_{w|v} L_w`,
  `[L : K] = Σ_{w|v} [L_w : K_v]`, and the fundamental identity `Σ e_w f_w = [L : K]`.
- Chapter I §9, printed p.56 (PDF p.72): in the Galois case the fundamental identity reads
  `n = e f r`.
- Chapter II §9, before Proposition (9.6), printed p.170 (PDF p.185): Galois theory of the local
  extensions `L_w / K_v` of a Galois extension `L / K`.

The Lean statements are adapted to the Library's `HeightOneSpectrum.adicCompletion`
setting. The proofs are routed through the published comparison results
`completedLocalDegreeLowerBound`, `finiteLocalDegree_sum_le`,
`finitePlacesAbove_sum_ramification_inertia` and `finiteTensorMap_surjective`. That
routing is internal to this formalization.
