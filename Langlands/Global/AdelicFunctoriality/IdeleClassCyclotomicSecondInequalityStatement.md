# Kummer case and cyclotomic reduction for the second inequality

This module states two explicit proof obligations for the existing actual idele-class norm. It supplies no proof of either obligation.

`Langlands.Global.AdelicFunctoriality.IdeleClassKummerPrimeDegreeSecondInequality.{u,v}` says that for a Galois extension of number fields `L/K` of prime degree, with a primitive root of unity of that degree in `K`, the range index of `ideleClassNorm` divides the degree. The two fields have independent universes. The primitive root is an element of the base field. Cyclicity is not an additional premise: a proof can derive it from the prime degree and the order of the Galois group. As the degree is a positive prime, the divisibility conclusion also forces the norm index to be nonzero, hence finite under Mathlib's index convention.

`Langlands.Global.AdelicFunctoriality.IdeleClassCyclotomicSecondInequalityReduction.{u,v}` is the implication from three named obligations to the previously published unrestricted prime-degree inequality. The antecedents are the finite norm-index/degree-power bound in universes `{u,v}`, the norm tower law in `{u,v,v}`, and the Kummer case in `{v,v}`. The conclusion retains independent universes and adds no cyclicity or root-of-unity hypothesis. This implication does not claim that any antecedent has been proved.

The universe pattern follows the intended reduction: take a cyclotomic extension of `L : Type v`, and inside it the intermediate field over `K` generated by a primitive root. Both auxiliary fields have carriers in `Type v`. Two towers, through `L` and through the root-generated field, then use the same `{u,v,v}` norm law. A proof must construct those fields, prove the required degrees and Galois properties, and establish the index divisibilities; none is encoded as an assumed arithmetic certificate here.

The source for the reduction is Artin and Tate, *Class Field Theory* (2009), VI §1, Reduction 2, printed pp.20–21 (PDF pp.29–30), together with the finite-index and tower lemmas immediately preceding it. The Kummer case is the intermediate goal proved in VI §3, printed pp.24–27 (PDF pp.33–36). That argument uses the first inequality on printed p.26; its proof obligation must retain that dependency explicitly during development and cannot use downstream global reciprocity, global fundamental classes or global idele-class H1 vanishing. Milne, *Class Field Theory*, v4.03 (2020), VII §6, Lemma 6.1, printed p.214 (PDF p.223), gives an alternative cyclotomic reduction through a norm-and-inclusion diagram.

The exact implication and universe organization are an internal decomposition of the source argument for the existing prime-degree proof task, rather than a separately numbered theorem in the source.
