# Full adelic growth of the smooth block sections

The smooth infinite slices and their common open finite stabilizer give continuity on the actual adelic group. The finite stabilizer covers the integral compact by finitely many cosets. Applying those finite translates to the finite-dimensional infinite compact orbit span gives a common finite-dimensional space of normalized Levi slices. Point evaluations give coordinates in this space, and continuity bounds those coordinates uniformly over the compact subgroup.

The polynomial estimates for realized coefficients and the positive half modulus now apply with a common exponent. The block projection bounds the product of the Levi heights by a power of the parabolic height. In an Iwasawa decomposition the inverse compact factor has bounded height, so the estimate is polynomial in the full ambient matrix height. Removing the dimension scaling gives moderate growth in the exact standard adelic source norm.

This derived argument supplies the inducing-section growth for Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), proof of Proposition 2, printed pp. 204–206, Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 199–201.
