# Projected Eisenstein specialization on the smooth block sections

The actual scheme block parabolic supplies its radical, Levi, split component and complex character coordinates. In these coordinates the smooth block section carrier has finite and enveloping operators with finite holomorphic expansions. The compact generators are independent of the parameter. The entire family at zero uses the original normalized finite, compact and enveloping actions.

The automorphic scalar realization on the parabolic quotient supplies the input to the projected Eisenstein specialization theorem. For a finite-rank compact projection preserving a nonzero quotient fiber, the theorem produces a surjective map from an invariant numerator of stable automorphic functions with the full free operator action. This step uses the exact meromorphic continuation and injectivity in the sufficiently regular convergence region on the projected seed space.

This derived assembly implements the specialization stage in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), proof of Proposition 2, printed pp. 205–207, Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 200–202.
