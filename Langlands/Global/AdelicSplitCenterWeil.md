# Full Weil compatibility of the actual positive/principal quotient

Author: Worker 112.

This internal construction establishes full subgroup Weil compatibility for
the actual adelic measures constructed in `AdelicSplitCenterHaar`,
`AdelicSplitCenterSubgroupHaar` and `AdelicSplitCenterMeasure`. It specializes

## Objects and hypotheses

Let `F` be a number field, `G` an affine group scheme over `Spec F` locally of
finite type, `T` a commutative group scheme, and `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G` a
scheme-theoretically central morphism. The actual rational-character restriction
`characterRestrict i` is assumed injective with finite-index range. An
isomorphism from `T.toGrp` to a split torus indexed by a finite type `I` is
supplied; the empty indexing type is allowed.

Write `A` for `intrinsicSubgroup F T`, `N` for `normOneSubgroup F G`,
`Gamma` for `rationalNormOneSubgroup F G`, and `S` for
`positiveRationalSubgroup F G T i`. These are the existing actual positive,
norm-one, principal norm-one and positive/principal point subgroups, with
their existing topologies and measurable structures. In particular, `S` is
the subgroup generated by the positive image and principal points. Its left
homogeneous quotient uses the existing right relation and right translations
`[g] ↦ [g * x]`; normality of the principal subgroup is not assumed.

The ambient measure is the fixed `Measure.haar` on `AdelicGroupPoints F G`.
The subgroup measure is `positiveRationalSubgroupHaarMeasure`: the exact
pushforward of `normalizedPositiveHaarMeasure × Measure.count` on
`A × Gamma` through actual multiplication. The quotient measure is the
existing `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure`, the pushforward of the fixed
norm-one principal quotient measure through actual inclusion. No measure is
rechosen in this module.

The main compatibility assertion and its consequences explicitly assume
`IsMulRightInvariant` for the fixed `Measure.haar` on `N`. The subgroup
average and convergence of its individual compact-test fibers do not need
that additional assumption.

## Full subgroup averaging and convergence

`positiveRationalQuotientAverage` is the actual `quotientAverage` over `S`
with its specified subgroup Haar measure. Its representative formula is

`positiveRationalQuotientAverage f [g] = ∫ h : S, f (h.val * g)`.

Right invariance of the subgroup Haar, already proved from the
positive-times-principal decomposition, makes this average well-defined.
For every continuous compactly supported complex function `f` on the full
adelic group, `integrable_positiveRationalSubgroup_fiber` proves integrability
of the displayed subgroup integral at every representative `g`. The proof
uses closedness of `S`, its Haar measure, and the compact-support fiber lemma
from the quotient integration API.

## Exact Weil compatibility

`positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure_isCompatible` proves all five fields of
`IsCompatible` for these exact measures: the quotient measure is nonzero
and regular, every ambient right translation preserves it, the subgroup
average of each continuous compactly supported complex test is integrable
on the quotient, and its integral is the ambient Haar integral.

The implemented proof first applies
`fullFactorQuotientMeasure_isCompatible` to the actual product `A × N`
and its subgroup `A × Gamma`. The input compatibility is the already
constructed norm-one principal quotient compatibility, using counting on
`Gamma`. The positive measure is normalized positive Haar; its sigma
finiteness, compact finiteness and right invariance are supplied by the
existing positive Haar construction. Counting on the actual discrete
countable group `Gamma` is Haar. The generic product argument integrates
the positive factor by convergent Fubini and retains the compact partial
integral needed for the norm-one compatibility formula.

The proof then uses the actual ambient continuous group equivalence
`normOneDecompositionMulEquiv : A × N ≃ₜ* AdelicGroupPoints F G`.
On the subgroup, the corresponding equivalence first identifies the
product subgroup with `A × Gamma` and then applies
`positiveRationalDecomposition`. On quotients it composes the
second-factor quotient homeomorphism with `positiveQuotientHomeomorph`.
The representative identity follows because the discarded positive
factor belongs to `S`.

These three maps send the three product measures to precisely the fixed
ambient, subgroup and quotient measures. The ambient map equality is
`map_normalizedPositiveHaarMeasure_prod`. The other two map equalities
follow by composition and inverse cancellation. Thus
`isCompatible_map_equiv` transfers every compatibility field, including
integrability, with no residual scalar. The proof also supplies the
Hausdorff, second-countable and Borel quotient structures needed to apply
that transport to the existing topologies and sigma algebras.

`integrable_positiveRationalQuotientAverage` and
`integral_positiveRationalQuotientAverage` expose the convergence assertion
and the exact formula separately:

`∫ q, positiveRationalQuotientAverage f q = ∫ g, f g`.

Here the left integral uses `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure`, the inner
subgroup integral uses `positiveRationalSubgroupHaarMeasure`, and the right
integral uses the fixed ambient Haar. Both assertions require continuity and
compact support of `f`. The separate fiber convergence result verifies the
inner integrals as well.

`compatiblePositiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure` bundles the existing quotient
measure with this full compatibility proof. Its value theorem identifies
the underlying measure exactly with `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure`.
