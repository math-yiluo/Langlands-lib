# Finite spans for normalized convolution at a presented parabolic

`NormalizedConvolutionFiniteSpanStatement` asks for a finite entire
expansion of the normalized convolution of a square-integrable inducing
form by a left-finite test function. The spanning forms belong to the
same square-integrable inducing space as the input. The coefficients
are entire in linear coordinates, and the equality is pointwise on the
adelic group at every complex parameter.

The finiteness mechanism is the one in Mœglin–Waldspurger,
*Spectral Decomposition and Eisenstein Series*, I.2.17, pp. 37–38.
The compact and level conditions of the kernel control the right
compact types and the finite level of the output. After normalizing by
the half-modulus, the left Levi center acts on the Levi slices and
commutes with convolution on the right. These controls give the
finiteness data for the inducing forms. The integrated action is the
one used for the covariance of Eisenstein series in IV.1.7, printed
p. 139, and for the right action on induced sections in Arthur,
*An introduction to the trace formula*, §7, pp. 32–34.

Mœglin–Waldspurger work with standard parabolics and a compact in good
position. The proposition here asks for the expansion at an individual
presented parabolic using its compatible Haar measure and the supplied
maximal compact with the Iwasawa factorization for that parabolic.
Relating these data to the source requires transport of the
normalization and the compact and level conditions, and the expansion
also requires preservation of the square-integrability condition.
