# Product coordinates on the actual characteristic shell

Take a standard parabolic \(Q\), a minimal-parabolic Iwasawa compact \(K\) with a maximal compact inverse image in the Levi, and compact factors satisfying both rational covers of (7.1). Assume that \(T-T_0\) is nonnegative on all ambient simple roots and that \(S\) is nonnegative on all simple roots of the Levi. Then the shell in \(Q(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A)^1\) pulls back under the relative Iwasawa map to the product of the full radical and compact factors, the split height shell, and the inverse image of the intrinsic Levi height region in its rational quotient. That region is the quotient image of the intrinsic norm-one Levi points whose ambient minimal heights lie in `characteristicLeviChamber` at the projected cutoffs.

The height description of the parabolic lift removes the choice of compact representative for the minimal radical. Surjectivity of relative Iwasawa multiplication then identifies the lift with the image of coordinate points whose sum of split and Levi heights lies in the characteristic shell. The real height identity (7.23) and uniqueness of the decomposition along the Levi coroot span separate the split and Levi requirements.

A second representative of an intrinsic Levi rational class is a rational left translate of the first. Making that change in the Iwasawa product conjugates the radical by the rational Levi point, which lies in the parabolic and centralizes the split factor. Thus the condition on the Levi coordinate can be imposed on its quotient class. It is invariant under the induced compact because minimal height is invariant under right multiplication by the ambient compact. The fibre relation for relative parabolic coordinates now gives the exact preimage.

For `characteristicRelativeProductMeasure_preserving`, the covering factors use the same \(K\), and Haar measure on the radical is prescribed. For each \(T_0\), the theorem chooses a positive simple-root threshold and a single sigma-finite split Haar measure before varying \(T\) and \(S\). With \(T\) above the threshold on every ambient simple root and with the two preceding dominance hypotheses, the restricted coordinate measure pushes to restricted Haar on the ambient rational quotient. The radical and intrinsic norm-one Levi measures are represented by Haar restricted to their principal fundamental domains. The split factor retains the inverse parabolic density and its shell restriction, the Levi factor has its quotient-region restriction, and \(K\) has Haar probability. See Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*, Advances in Mathematics 426 (2023), 109107, (7.1), (7.7), (7.20) and (7.23).
