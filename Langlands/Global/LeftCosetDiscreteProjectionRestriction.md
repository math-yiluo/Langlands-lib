# Measures on injective regions of discrete quotient projections

Consider two discrete subgroups \(\Gamma\leq\Lambda\) of a
second-countable topological group \(H\), and use one ambient
left-invariant measure for both counting-normalized quotients.
`measurePreserving_projectionOfLE_restrict` proves that the projection
from \(\Gamma\backslash H\) to \(\Lambda\backslash H\) transports the
restriction to a measurable region on which it is injective to the
restriction to that region's image.

First, if a measurable set of points in \(H\) maps injectively to a
discrete quotient, its distinct subgroup translates are disjoint.
Intersecting them with a Borel fundamental domain and summing their
masses shows that the image has the mass of the original set.
To compare the two quotients, lift the region to the chosen
fundamental domain for \(\Gamma\). The map to the larger quotient is
injective there because the quotient projection is injective on the
region. For an arbitrary measurable test set in the larger quotient,
the lift restricted to that test set has image equal to the
intersection of the test set with the region image. The mass identity
therefore proves equality of the restricted measures on all measurable
test sets.

This measure comparison is used when the injective parabolic shell
in Delorme's (7.6) passes to the ambient quotient in (7.7).
The source is P. Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*,
Advances in Mathematics 426 (2023), 109107, pp. 23–24.
