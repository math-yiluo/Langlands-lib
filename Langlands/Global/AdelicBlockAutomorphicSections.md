# Automorphic functions on the actual block-parabolic quotient

The injection into the compact-finite carrier transports joint admissibility to the smooth block sections. The original finite action commutes with the enveloping center action, and the infinite compact action normalizes it. The open kernel of center conjugation gives a finite image on the standard compact. Joint admissibility then gives a finite-dimensional central orbit and one cofinite ideal annihilating the section. The genuine matrix comparison transports this ideal condition to every infinite slice for the actual tangent enveloping center.

The scalar realization is left invariant under the adelic radical and the rational Levi. Its smoothness, compact finiteness, simultaneous center finiteness and full moderate growth give the automorphic-function predicate on the block-parabolic quotient. The coefficient hypothesis is joint admissibility of each inducing realization, which is available for the cuspidal realizations in Task 6519.
