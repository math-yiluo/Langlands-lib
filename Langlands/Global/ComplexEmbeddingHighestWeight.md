# Highest-weight characters of the totally complex archimedean center

`globalHighestWeightCharacter` is the scalar action of the actual global enveloping center on an upper highest-weight vector. The representation is the finite external tensor product of the upper Verma modules at the complex embeddings. Its distinguished vector is the tensor of the Verma generators. Applying one linear functional in each factor sends this vector to the tensor of ones, whose image under multiplication is one.

The Lie action is the sum of the coordinate actions on the tensor factors. Actions supported at different indices commute, so the sum respects the Lie bracket and extends to the actual enveloping algebra. At each embedding, the block center acts on the distinguished vector through `VermaShifted.upperCharacter`. The subalgebra of the global center that acts by a scalar on this vector contains every block center and hence is the whole center. Nonvanishing gives a unique scalar, and the action laws make this scalar an algebra homomorphism.

The real diagonal and strictly upper triangular directions in `IsHighestWeightVector` split into the corresponding matrix directions at the embeddings. The diagonal actions sum to `diagonalWeight`, and each strictly upper triangular action vanishes. This exhibits the required witness at the universe of the number field.

Conversely, for any witness of `IsHighestWeightCharacter`, isolate a matrix entry at all embedding coordinates and extend the resulting real matrix unit identity to the complexified Lie algebra by tensor induction. Specializing to a direction supported at a single embedding gives the upper Verma unit relations there. The one-block character theorem and nonvanishing identify the character on that block center. The global center is generated by the block centers, which gives `isHighestWeightCharacter_iff_block`.

This construction derives the block description of the Library's highest-weight predicate from the actual archimedean Lie split and the upper Verma character theorem. It supplies the global weight comparison used in the totally complex bridge.
