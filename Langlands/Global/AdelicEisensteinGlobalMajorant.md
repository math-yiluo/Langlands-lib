# Polynomial bounds for the absolute adelic Eisenstein series

Let \(P=MU\) be a parabolic of a connected reductive group over a number field, with the specified relative root presentation and an Iwasawa compact. Fix a closed matrix embedding of positive size and a function \(\phi\) of moderate growth invariant under \(U(\mathbb A)M(F)\). There is a single regularity threshold. For each parameter beyond it, there are a nonnegative summable majorant on \(P(F)\backslash G(F)\) and a nonnegative real exponent. The majorant times the corresponding power of the external matrix height bounds the absolute Eisenstein term for every coset and every external argument.

Take the infimum of the matrix height raised to a nonnegative exponent from moderate growth over each left orbit of the source parabolic quotient subgroup. The resulting positive envelope is invariant and has moderate growth. Submultiplicativity and invariance of \(\phi\) bound its right translates by the envelope times the same height power of the translating argument.

The projective-representative convergence proof gives a summable majorant for the envelope at the identity and the chosen parameter. The fixed-parameter polynomial estimate for the increment of the Iwasawa height power then gives the bound for \(\phi\) at arbitrary external arguments.
