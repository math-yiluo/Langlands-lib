# Strong approximation at the finite adeles for unipotent groups

`AdelicUnipotentStrongApproximationStatement` asserts that the image of the rational points of a smooth connected affine unipotent group over a number field is dense in its group of finite adelic points, with the actual topology of adelic points.

The proof first treats the additive group. Each neighbourhood in the finite adeles contains a translate of a finite-adelic lattice. Strong approximation with local lattice conditions finds a rational point in that translate by imposing the prescribed conditions at the support of the lattice levels and at the nonintegral places of the centre. For a unipotent group, splitness over the number field gives an affine-space isomorphism of schemes. The induced polynomial coordinates identify the topology of finite adelic points with the product topology and identify the rational-point image with the product of the additive diagonals.

This gives the approximation input used to reduce finite-level unipotent averages to archimedean coordinates in Mœglin–Waldspurger, §I.2.10, pp. 30–31.
