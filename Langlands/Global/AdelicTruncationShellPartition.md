# Characteristic cells in Siegel coordinates

`parabolicCharacteristicSiegelSet` consists of representatives in the full Siegel set at an auxiliary lower cutoff \(T_1\) whose height lies in Delorme's \(C_T^Q\) with the closed lower cutoff \(T_0\). The extra cutoff permits comparison with Arthur's open Siegel inequalities. `normOneCharacteristicSiegelRegion` takes the norm-one representatives and maps them to \(G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A)^1\).

`exists_mem_parabolicCharacteristicChamber` applies Arthur's weight and root partition to \(H-T\). It takes the subset of the simple roots obtained from the partition and translates the abstract weight and `tau` conditions into those of `parabolicCharacteristicChamber`. Consequently any representative in the common Siegel set whose height is in the relative coroot space and satisfies the closed lower inequalities belongs to a cell.

`exists_rational_cell_separation_threshold` uses Arthur's reduction theorem to choose a single root threshold for \(T\). A representative in a cell belongs to the truncated Siegel set for its standard index, and the complementary roots give `tau = 1`. The identity rational translate witnesses the Siegel indicator. If two such representatives are rationally equivalent, `partition_term_unique` gives the same index and shows that the translating point lies in the rational points of its parabolic. This proves injectivity on the image in the parabolic quotient. Applying the same argument to norm-one representatives whose images coincide in the full rational quotient proves that distinct cell images are disjoint.

These results implement the separation in (7.6) and the partition at the level of representatives. The hypotheses explicitly use the common Siegel representatives. Relating these representatives to the full products \(\Omega_{N_0}M_0(C_T^Q)K\) requires the minimal Levi and unipotent covering comparisons.
