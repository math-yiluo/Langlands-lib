# Cuspidal testing of a proper parabolic Eisenstein term

The product of the summand with the conjugated test is invariant under the rational points of the inducing parabolic. Its absolute coset sum is invariant under the rational points of the full Levi, so a uniform bound on representatives gives absolute integrability over the finite-volume norm-one quotient. Unfolding the tested coset sum gives the integral over the rational parabolic quotient.

The original summand is left invariant under the parabolic radical. The test's zero normalized constant term therefore implies zero radical constant term for the product. The adelic period vanishing theorem gives zero for the unfolded integral. The result supplies the proper-parabolic vanishing used in Bernstein–Lapid (6.18).
