# Adelic compact assembly with simultaneous standard Levi intersections

Fix a compatible standard family and suppose that at every place there is a maximal compact serving its minimal parabolic and meeting each standard Levi in a maximal compact. This gives one adelic compact with all of the corresponding properties.

The published good integral models give local Iwasawa coverage for the fixed representation family at almost all finite places. Restrict the ambient closed representation to each Levi to compare its integral family with the ambient inverse image. Independence of the closed representation gives equality outside finitely many places, so the Levi good models give local maximality there. The standard indices form a finite set, which gives one exceptional set for all comparisons.

Patch the exceptional local groups using the assumed local existence. The resulting family is uniformly integral and absorbs each adelic point at almost all places. The coordinate assembly gives ambient compactness, maximality and minimal Iwasawa coverage. For each Levi, a regular function lifts to the ambient group because the inclusion is a closed immersion between affine schemes. Thus the inverse-image family is also uniformly integral. Its coordinate subgroup is the adelic inverse image of the ambient compact, and its local compactness and maximality give the same properties adelically.

This is a local-to-global reduction using the published coordinate assembly and good integral models.
