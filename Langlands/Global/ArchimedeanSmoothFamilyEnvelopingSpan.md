# Enveloping spans of smooth families

`SmoothFamily.envelopingSpan` is the submodule over the actual enveloping algebra generated by the image of a finite-dimensional compact intertwiner into a family of smooth functions. The covariance law preserves this span under the compact action. The algebraic finite expansion for conjugates of each enveloping element bounds the compact orbit of a derivative of a generating vector by a finite sum of images of the original source. Taking sums gives finite compact orbit spans for all vectors of the generated module.

The finite-cover theorem takes two finite-dimensional continuous complex representations of the standard compact subgroup. The representation `η` is the source of the generating intertwiner, and `τ` is the common source of the intertwiners whose values are to be covered. Suppose that a center ideal with finite-dimensional quotient annihilates the generating image. Centrality extends the annihilator to the generated module, where the proof applies the Library's finite central covering. Each of the finitely many resulting central orbit spans is an image of the center quotient and is therefore finite-dimensional. Their sum in the ambient family gives one finite-dimensional subspace containing the values of every intertwiner from `τ` into the generated module.

This is an internal use of the Library's `exists_finite_central_cover_of_intertwiner_values` for simultaneous reproduction. Its algebraic role follows the finite generation over the center and passage to the center quotient in Borel–Jacquet, *Automorphic forms and automorphic representations* (1979), Lemma 2.1, printed p. 193, PDF p. 188.
