# Transport of full wave packets to a standard parabolic

`StandardWavePacketTransportStatement` says that, for a connected reductive group over a number field and a specified minimal parabolic `p₀`, every `IsWavePacket` function is an `IsStandardWavePacket` function for the same `K`. The latter predicate uses `IsParabolicWavePacket` over the full imaginary parameter space and requires the all-test containment `range p₀ ≤ range p`. `standardWavePacketTransport` proves this proposition pointwise on `G(𝔸)`.

The original presentation supplies a minimal parabolic inside its parabolic. Rational conjugacy moves this minimal parabolic to `p₀`, and `ParabolicRootPresentation.compAmbientIso` transports the presentation. The objects `P`, `M` and `U` and their internal maps serve for both embeddings into `G`. The transported compact coverage follows from `IsIwasawaCompact.of_conjugate_map`.

For a conjugating point `s`, `IsParabolicWavePacket.conjugate_parabolic` takes an inducing vector `v` to `g ↦ v(s⁻¹ g)`. Left translation transports automorphy and the split-component covariance. For square integrability, write `s⁻¹ = p(u m) k₀` by Iwasawa and the Levi decomposition. Normality moves the unipotent factor to the left where the vector is invariant. The weighted squared norm of the new vector at `(k, [x])` is the old integrand at `(k₀ k, [x m])` times the inverse of the nonzero modulus weight at `m`. The old weighted integrand descends to `[M]` by split-component covariance and invariance under `M(F)`. Both translations preserve the measures used by `IsSquareIntegrableInducedForm`.

For each original continuation, multiply by the transported exponential height factor at `s`. This factor is entire in the parameter, so the same domain of holomorphy works. On the regular region the new continuation agrees with the series for the transported vector by `eisensteinRegularityAmbientTransport` and `IsIwasawaCompact.eisensteinSeries_conjugate_parabolic`. Multiply the smooth compactly supported coefficients by the inverse height factor on the imaginary axis. Cancellation in each summand proves equality of the two packet integrals.

The transport uses Borel–Tits, *Groupes réductifs* (1965), Théorème 4.13(b), for rational conjugacy of minimal parabolics, and the sum in Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series* (2024), §2.2, (2.1), p. 191, for the conjugation identity implemented in the Library. The inducing and packet conventions are those of Lapid, *On Arthur's asymptotic inner product formula of truncated Eisenstein series* (2011), §§2–3, pp. 311 and 314 (PDF pp. 320 and 323). These sources enter through the Library's conjugacy, inducing-form and Eisenstein-series interfaces. The packet comparison addresses the use of standard parabolics in Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands* (2023), §6, Theorem 6.1 and Lemma 6.2, p. 22.
