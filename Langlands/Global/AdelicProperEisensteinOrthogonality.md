# Orthogonality of Eisenstein series induced from a proper parabolic

`ProperEisensteinOrthogonalityStatement` formulates the square-integrable orthogonality in Delorme's (4.6). It uses a standard family for a connected reductive group, a maximal compact with minimal Iwasawa coverage and positive torus compatibility, a closed matrix representation, and a normalized square-integrable inducing form for a proper standard parabolic. For an Eisenstein continuation holomorphic on the imaginary parameters and agreeing with the defining series in the regular convergence chamber, the conclusion is `IsSquareIntegrableOrthogonal` at the full-group parabolic. This predicate includes absolute convergence and vanishing of the pairing against each square-integrable test on each positive central slice. The predicate uses the compact probability and intrinsic Levi quotient measures from the normalized pairing. The temperedness assertion that accompanies (4.6) in the source is treated separately by the uniform temperedness statement.

The proved reduction `isSquareIntegrableOrthogonal_of_polynomial_pairing` implements the last step of the proof in (4.6) for any standard slice. For each test and slice it assumes integrability throughout the real spectral coordinates, continuity of the integral, and annihilation by a nonzero real spectral polynomial. The polynomial's nonvanishing locus has full Lebesgue measure by `ae_eval_ne_zero`. Cancellation there gives vanishing almost everywhere, and continuity for a measure positive on open sets gives vanishing at every parameter. The hypotheses of this reduction account for the analytic and infinitesimal-character inputs that Delorme obtains from uniform temperedness and the adjoint of a central enveloping operator. The proof in this file establishes the reduction under those hypotheses.

See P. Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*, Advances in Mathematics **426** (2023), 109107, §3.4, p. 15, and §4.2, pp. 17–18, (4.6) and its proof.
