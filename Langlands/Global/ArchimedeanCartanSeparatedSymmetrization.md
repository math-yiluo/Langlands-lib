# Actual Cartan and compact separated symmetrization

For every `K : Type u` with `[Field K]` and every natural rank `n`, write
`g` for the Library's full complexified archimedean matrix Lie algebra,
`p` for its actual complexified self-adjoint Cartan complement, and
`B = compactEnvelopingSubalgebra K n`. The latter is the unital subalgebra
of the genuine `U(g)` generated by the actual complexified compact Lie
directions. This construction uses these concrete Library objects for all
ranks, including zero. It does not need `[NumberField K]`.

`cartanSymmetricAlgebraSymmetrize K n` is the complex linear composite of
the actual symmetric inclusion `S(p) → S(g)` with the published normalized
whole-algebra symmetrization `S(g) → U(g)`. The application formula records
that composite, and the generator formula identifies its value at `ι_p(X)`
with the genuine enveloping generator of `cartanComplexInclusion K n X`.

`cartanCompactSymmetrization K n` is the complex linear map

```text
CartanSymmetricAlgebra K n ⊗[ℂ] compactEnvelopingSubalgebra K n
  →ₗ[ℂ] EnvelopingAlgebra K n
```

whose exact pure-tensor formula is

```text
P ⊗ b ↦ cartanSymmetricAlgebraSymmetrize K n P * b.
```

Thus complementary symmetric factors are on the left and genuine compact
enveloping factors are on the right.
`cartanCompactSymmetrization_surjective` proves that this map is onto the
entire actual enveloping algebra.

For the proof, `cartanCompact_envelopingGenerator_split` verifies the
splitting hypothesis of the generic separated-symmetrization theorem.
At the real level, the published projections satisfy
`compactProjection(X) + cartanComplementProjection(X) = X`.
For a pure complexification tensor `z ⊗ X`, use
`z ⊗ cartanComplementProjection(X)` as the complementary preimage and the
image of `z ⊗ compactProjection(X)` as the compact direction. The latter's
genuine generator belongs to `B` by its defining generation theorem.
Tensor additivity and induction cover every vector of `g`. The generic
characteristic-zero spanning theorem now applies over `ℂ`; its linear-family
map agrees definitionally with the actual symmetric inclusion.

The theorem `cartanSymmetricAlgebraSymmetrize_conjugation` also proves that
complementary symmetrization intertwines actual conjugation by every
element of the standard compact subgroup. It follows by composing the
published equivariance of the actual complementary inclusion with that of
whole symmetrization, and includes every component of the compact group.

The provenance is internal: this is a specialization of
`Langlands.Algebra.SeparatedEnvelopingSymmetrization` using the real Cartan
decomposition from `ArchimedeanCompactLieAlgebra`, the concrete complementary
symmetric inclusion, and actual whole symmetrization. The conclusion is
surjectivity of a linear tensor map. No tensor-map injectivity, algebra
isomorphism, central generation, admissibility, or automorphic growth is
established by this pair.
