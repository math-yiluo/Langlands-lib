# Smooth reproduction on parabolic adelic quotients

`Langlands.Global.parabolicReproducingKernel` proves `ParabolicReproducingKernelStatement` at arbitrary universe `u`. It gives a compactly supported smooth adelic test function whose left translates by the specified archimedean compact subgroup span a finite-dimensional complex space and whose right convolution fixes the given function.

## Proof

1. The archimedean factor of the specified maximal adelic compact is maximal compact. `exists_elliptic_functionInfiniteSlices` uses the adapted Casimir, the finite compact orbit and the common finite-codimensional center ideal to find one positive-order elliptic equation for every infinite slice. Its smooth solution space is a complex subspace of the continuous functions, and the equation commutes with left translations. The given matrix representation provides a closed linear realization for the supplied smooth atlas. Right invariance of adelic Haar gives right invariance on the archimedean factor through the product formula for the modular character.
2. The interior elliptic estimate bounds every derivative at the identity in a single compact supremum norm, with a constant for each derivative order. Translations and the higher-order chain rule extend this to the restrictions of a smooth one-parameter orbit on any compact set and interval. A compact bound on these derivatives lets the one-variable Dixmier–Malliavin identity decompose a compact evaluation functional into two smooth averages. Hahn–Banach extends the derivative functionals to the common compact domain and the coefficient estimate gives a norm-summable series of those extensions. Iterating the decomposition along the exponential coordinates gives a finite sum of smooth averages of compact functionals. The smooth Haar density in those coordinates supplies a smooth group kernel for each average. Commuting the compact functional with the integral and changing variables by right translation turns each average into a smooth compactly supported weight on the group. Their sum reproduces evaluation at the identity on the entire solution space. Applying it to the left translates of the infinite slices gives simultaneous reproduction at every adelic point.
3. The kernel is multiplied by the normalized indicator of a compact open subgroup contained in the right finite level of the function. Fubini and Haar uniqueness give reproduction for the fixed adelic Haar measure. The product is itself an adelic test function. A direct reproducing compact weight for the original function comes from its finite-dimensional space of compact translates. Left averaging the test kernel with that weight preserves the test conditions and bounds its left translate span by the finite translate span of the weight. The left averaging identity turns the convolution of the new kernel with the original function into the compact average that fixes the function.

The common equation holds for all slices before imposing left invariance or growth. This lets the proof apply to the parabolic quotient with the hypotheses in the statement, uniformly over the finite adelic variable.
