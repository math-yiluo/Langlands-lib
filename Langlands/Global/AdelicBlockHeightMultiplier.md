# Multiplication by a differentiated height pairing

The differentiated pairing is left parabolic invariant and fixed by the integral finite level. Multiplying a scalar section by it preserves the normalized Levi coefficients and a common finite open stabilizer. Its infinite compact translates lie in the complex span of the derivatives along a finite real tangent basis, by tangent conjugation. A product of two compact-finite scalar functions has a finite-dimensional orbit span in the product of their two orbit spans. These facts define multiplication by the differentiated pairing on the original compact-finite inducing carrier.

This derived construction supplies the zeroth-order term of the deformed archimedean action on the section family in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), proof of Proposition 2, printed pp. 205–206, Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 200–201. The construction uses the actual adelic height and the smoothness of its pairings.
