# Full-group covariance of finite-vector differentiation

This internal construction applies the existing scalar covariance theorem
coordinate by coordinate through `finiteVectorSmoothEquiv`.
`vectorSmoothRightAction_finiteVectorSmoothLieAction` identifies the original
right translation of an actual differentiated vector function with the
derivative of its right translation in the adjoint-conjugated complex Lie
direction. It holds for every vector function and every complexified Lie
vector, on the actual completion-based archimedean group.

The real differential underlying this action differentiates in the direction
gX. The adjoint convention is Ad(g)X = gXg⁻¹, exactly as in the scalar theorem.
The number-field and finite-coordinate index universes are independent.
No topology on the smooth function space or coefficient representation is
introduced, and the finite index may be empty.
