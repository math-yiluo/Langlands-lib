# The common quotient of the two intertwining pairings

The common principal subgroup is the intersection of the rational points with the target adelic parabolic and the Weyl conjugate of the source adelic parabolic. The radical intersection lemma identifies its restriction to the target radical with the defining discrete subgroup of the intertwining integral. The Weyl Levi comparison places the target principal Levi in the intersection.

Left translation by the inverse Weyl point carries this quotient to the corresponding quotient for the reverse intertwiner. The translate of a fundamental domain is a fundamental domain for the conjugated subgroup. Left invariance of adelic Haar therefore gives equality for the counting-normalized quotient measures. This implements the substitution in Mœglin–Waldspurger II.1.8, pp. 93–94.
