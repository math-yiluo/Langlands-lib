# Intersecting a local tower with a normal extension

The Galois correspondence sends a field intersection to the join of the fixing
subgroups. The join here is open because it contains the open fixing subgroup
of a finite field. For a normal second field, density of the Weil group gives
the same join formula inside the Weil group by matching restriction to that
finite normal field.

After adjoining the normal subgroup to both subgroups of a step, relative
normality survives and the new relative index divides the old one. Applied to a
cyclic step of prime degree, this gives an equality or another cyclic step of
the same prime degree. The comparison uses the relative fixing subgroups under
the actual intermediate-field closure equivalence. These lemmas justify
intersecting the parent tower with a child normal closure in the local
general-position argument of Harris, Proposition 4.8.
