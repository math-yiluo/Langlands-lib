# The completion Weil map is conjugate to a transported Weil subgroup

Author: Worker 5134. Module `Langlands.Global.Arithmetic.HenniartCompletionConjugation`.
It is a helper for the proof of `HenniartGlobalizationStatement` (Henniart 2000, §3.4).

Notation: `E` is a number field, `v` is a finite place of `E`, `E_v = v.adicCompletion E`,
and `K : PadicField p` is a p-adic field. `padicFieldAt v h` is the Library's canonical
p-adic field package on `E_v` for a proof `h : residuePrime v = p`. `padicField v` is the
same package with `p := residuePrime v`.

## Section `Base`

- `residuePrime_eq_of_natCast_mem v hv`: if the ideal of `v` contains the rational prime
  `p`, then `residuePrime v = p`. Proof: `p` lies in the contraction of `v` to `ℤ`. The
  contraction is generated by the Library's prime `natGenerator`
  (`Rat.HeightOneSpectrum.natGenerator_dvd_iff`). So this prime divides `p`, and two
  primes that divide each other are equal.
- `completion_ringHom_ext v K α β hα hβ hαβ`: two ring homomorphisms `E_v → K` that are
  continuous for the canonical topology of `K` and agree on `E` are equal. `E` is dense in
  `E_v` (`denseRange_algebraMap`), and `K` is Hausdorff (its canonical topological data
  are T2).
- `completionAlgEquivOfContinuous v K h ι hι` (definition): let `ι : E_v ≃+* K` be a
  ring isomorphism with continuous inverse. Then `ι⁻¹` is an isomorphism of
  `ℚ_p`-algebras `K ≅ E_v`, where `E_v` carries the algebra structure of
  `padicFieldAt v h`. The two continuous maps `ℚ_p → E_v`, `a ↦ ι⁻¹(a · 1)` and the
  structure map of `padicFieldAt v h`, agree on `ℚ`. Since `ℚ` is dense in `ℚ_p`, they
  are equal. Continuity of the structure map is the Library's `continuous_padicMap`
  after substituting `h`.
- `completionAlgEquivOfContinuous_symm_apply`: the inverse of this isomorphism is `ι`
  (`rfl`, simp lemma).

## Main result

Let `E'/E` be number fields, `w` a finite place of `E'` above `v`, `h : residuePrime v = p`,
and `f : K ≅ E_v` a `ℚ_p`-algebra isomorphism (with the structure of `padicFieldAt v h`).
Let `T := completionWeilTransport v h K f : W_K → W_{E_v}` be the transport map of the
statement `HenniartGlobalizationStatement`.

`exists_conj_completionWeilMap_range v w h K f F' φ hφ L hL`: suppose

- `F'` is a finite extension of `K` and `φ : E'_w ≃+* F'` is a ring isomorphism with
  `φ(x) = f⁻¹(x)` for `x ∈ E_v` (hypothesis `hφ`; `E_v` is embedded in `E'_w` by the
  completion map and `K` is embedded in `F'`);
- `L` is a finite extension of `E_v` with `T y ∈ W_L ↔ y ∈ W_{F'}` for all `y ∈ W_K`
  (hypothesis `hL`).

Then there is `g ∈ W_{E_v}` such that, for all `y ∈ W_{E_v}`,
`y ∈ range (completionWeilMap v w) ↔ g * y * g⁻¹ ∈ W_L`. Here `completionWeilMap v w`
is the Library's local Weil map `W_{E'_w} → W_{E_v}`.

Proof. Substitute `h`. Let `c : \bar{E_v} ≅ \bar K` be the Library's chosen closure
isomorphism over `f⁻¹` (`extensionClosureEquiv`). Define the embedding
`ψ : E'_w → \bar{E_v}` by `ψ := c⁻¹ ∘ φ`, where `F'` is regarded as a subfield of
`\bar K`. By `hφ` and `extensionClosureLift_algebraMap`, `ψ` restricts to the
structure map on `E_v`. Apply `exists_conj_mem_iff_extensionWeilRange` (module
`Langlands.Weil.Local.ExtensionWeilRangeConjugation`) with `H := W_L`. Its hypothesis
reads: for `z ∈ W_{E_v}`, `z ∈ W_L` exactly when the Galois projection of `z` fixes
`ψ(E'_w)`. To check it, write `z = T y`, using the Library's
`completionWeilTransportEquiv`, by which `T` is bijective. Then `T y ∈ W_L ↔ y ∈ W_{F'}`
by `hL`. Also `y ∈ W_{F'}` exactly when `σ := localProjection K y` fixes `F'`
(`IntermediateField.mem_fixingSubgroup_iff`). The projection of `T y` fixes `ψ x` exactly
when `σ` fixes `φ x`, by `extensionClosureLift_galoisMap` and injectivity of `c`. Since
`φ` is onto `F'`, the two conditions agree.

## Use

In the planned proof of `HenniartGlobalizationStatement`, `E` is the target field of
`henniartCommonBaseRealization` and `ι` is its isomorphism `E_{v₀} ≅ K`. Its paired-place
configurations give `p ∈ v₀` (so `residuePrime_eq_of_natCast_mem` gives `h`) and the
isomorphisms `φ`. `f := completionAlgEquivOfContinuous v₀ K h ι hι` is fixed once.
`completion_ringHom_ext` identifies the configuration's completion isomorphism with `ι`.
The output `g` is the input of
`ExactGrothendieckGroup.continuousInduce_characterClass_eq_along_conj`
(`Langlands.VirtualRepresentation.ExactInductionConjugation`). It rewrites an induced
generator `Ind_{W_L}[η]` as an induction along `completionWeilMap v w`.

## Provenance

These are standard facts about completions and conjugate embeddings of local fields:
`E` is dense in `E_v`, `ℚ` is dense in `ℚ_p`, and different `E_v`-embeddings of `E'_w`
into `\bar{E_v}` differ by a Galois element. Henniart (2000), §3.4 uses them implicitly
when comparing the local component of the global induced representation with `K`-side
data. The declarations are auxiliary and have no separate literature definition.
