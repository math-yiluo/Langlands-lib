# Complex-place parameters in the singleton Mackey case

`Langlands.Global.Harris.proposition48SingletonInducedParameter` proves
`Proposition48SingletonInducedParameterStatement`, the parameter identity for
the singleton branch of Harris's Proposition 4.8.

Let `A` be the image of `E'` in the normal closure `L` and put `M = D ⊔ A`.
For an infinite place `w` of `D` above the complex place `v` of `E`, restriction
from `M` to `A` is a bijection from the places over `w` to the places over `v`.
For injectivity, choose the embeddings at two places of `M` to extend the
same embedding at `w`. If they restrict to the same place of `A`, their
restrictions as embeddings of `A` agree because both extend the same nonreal
embedding of `E`. The embeddings then agree on the field generated by `D`
and `A`. For surjectivity, choose embeddings of `D` and `A` extending the
selected embedding at `v` and lift both to `L`. The normal-closure Galois
action relates these lifts by an automorphism. The single double coset
factors that automorphism as a product of one fixing `D` and one fixing `A`.
This gives an embedding of `M` with the two prescribed restrictions.
The identification `E' ≃ A` turns this bijection into the enumeration of
places of `E'` used in the conclusion.

Naturality of the idele class norm identifies the Mackey character with
`ξ` pulled back along the actual norm from `M` to `E'`. At a complex place
the local norm composed with the inclusion of completions is the identity
because the local degree is one. Evaluating the given character parameter on
units in that inclusion yields the parameter of `ξ` in the selected
coordinates on `E'`. The completion map reads as either the identity or
complex conjugation in those coordinates.

Along each of the two towers through `D` and `E'`, the combined changes
of coordinate on each block depend only on the restriction from `M` to `E`.
This follows by composing the embeddings and using the involution on complex
character data. With the enumeration composed with the place bijection, the
transported and induced concatenations therefore agree in the same order.
Mapping the input permutation through the coordinate change gives the
required `IsInducedComplexPlaceParameter` witness.

The derived identity uses the Langlands-sum formulas (2.4.1)–(2.4.2) and
the Mackey restriction and singleton case of Proposition 4.8 in M. Harris,
*The local Langlands conjecture for GL(n) over a p-adic field, n < p*,
Invent. Math. 134 (1998), printed p. 193 and pp. 201–203 (PDF p. 17 and
