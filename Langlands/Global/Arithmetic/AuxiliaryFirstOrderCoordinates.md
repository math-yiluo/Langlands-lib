# Finite-order characters with total-trace norm coordinates

Worker 84 implements this norm-coordinate family using the existing actual
local objects. Its separate prerequisite theorem modules retain their original
authorship and are formal dependencies of the final proof task.

For supplied data TF, TL and TM and a unit coefficient b in the actual
intermediate field L, use the public traceCharacter and scale constructors to
form psi_L(x)=psi_F(Tr(L/F)(b*x)). Its continuity and nontriviality are proved by
those constructors. The first-order family lemma chooses one offset C and at
each positive k gives a continuous finite-order character chi of L units with
scalar c=p^(C-2k) and level n=k*e_L.

The actual integer a=p^k has valuation exp(-n)<1 and the family's uniform
quadratic bound. The norm-trace comparison therefore puts the norm of every
unit z=1+p^k*y in principalUnits n and gives chi(Nz)=psi_L(c*a*Tr(M/L)(y)).
The product c*a is the actual image of p^(C-k) from F. The exact weighted-trace
tower lemma converts the value to
psi_F(p^(C-k)*Tr(M/F)(b*y)). Equality of actual field values identifies every
unit with the unit constructed by the comparison, so the conclusion applies to
every supplied z with that value rather than a special choice of representative.
The finite annihilating exponent is unchanged.

The coefficient is nonzero through its unit type. Every prime, independent
carrier universe and supplied topology is retained; k must be positive for the
strict unit bound. AuxiliaryCharacterFirstOrderProof supplies arbitrary-depth nontriviality, the
common detection parameter and the final subtype-topology transport. This file
proves its stated coordinate family; independent review covers the full bundle.
