# Towers over an intermediate local field

The closure comparison for the prescribed intermediate field identifies fields
above it with subfields of its chosen algebraic closure. It transports fixing
conditions on the absolute Galois and Weil groups and preserves cyclic
prime-degree steps. A finite chain with adjacent repetitions can be shortened
while retaining its endpoints and a witness in the original chain for each
remaining stage. These are internal steps for the compatible arithmetic Mackey
construction in the proof of Harris, Proposition 4.8.
