# Restriction and irreducibility of the singleton monomial family

`Langlands.Global.Harris.proposition48SingletonMonomialRestriction` proves
`Langlands.Global.Harris.Proposition48SingletonMonomialRestrictionStatement`.
Notation follows the statement: `Q` is an auxiliary-valid problem, `D` a
prime cyclic step in `Q.L` with one Mackey double coset, `M = D · E'` the
actual compositum `Q.mackeyField D 1`, `j : E' → M` the displayed embedding,
`b = [E':E] - 1`, and `ξalg = ξ · ‖·‖^{b/2}`.

## Realization and the family over `E`

`isAlgebraic_mul_normPower` shows that `ξalg` is algebraic. The parity
condition `IsAlgebraicAfterTwist b ξ` of the auxiliary datum states that the
archimedean component of `ξ` times `∏_τ |x_τ|^b` is the character of an
integral infinity type. At a complex place the idelic norm is the squared
absolute value (`mult = 2`), so the archimedean component of `‖·‖^{b/2}` is
exactly this factor. The rank-one premise gives a coefficient field
`L`, an embedding `ι : L → ℂ` and a compatible realization `ψ` of `ξalg`.
The induction premise for `E'/E` along `e` gives the family `R` of rank
`[E':E]`, its exceptional set `S` and its polynomials `P`. These are the
returned `R`, `S` and `P`.

## Normalization over `E`

`HeckeConjugateInverse.isUnitary_of_isConjugateInverse` gives unitarity of
`ξ`. `matchesHeckeInduction_normPower` of
`Langlands.Galois.HeckeInductionNormalization` converts the comparison of
`P` with the induction of `ξalg` into the comparison of the unitary
normalizations with the induction of `ξ`.

## The second induction over `D`

`isCompatibleLambdaAdicRealization_normComp` of
`Langlands.Galois.LambdaAdicNormPullback` shows that `ψ ∘ r_{ej}` realizes
the norm pullback `ξalg ∘ N_{M/E'}`, where `M` is an `E'`-algebra through
`j`. The induction premise for `M/D` along `eM` gives a family `R'` with
exceptional set `S'` and polynomials `P'`. These are the returned `SD` and
`PD`. The norm pullback is computed as follows:

- `mackeyCharacter_one_eq_normComp`: the norm pullback of `ξ` along `j` is
  the actual Mackey character `Q.mackeyCharacter D 1`. This uses the
  naturality `ideleClassNorm_equiv` for the isomorphism
  `E' ≅ conjugateField 1`.
- `normComp_mul` and `Langlands.LocalFactor.NonabelianBrauer.normComp_normPower`: the norm pullback is
  multiplicative, and it sends `‖·‖^s` on `E'` to `‖·‖^s` on `M`
  (`full_ideleNorm`).

So `ξalg ∘ N = mackeyCharacter D 1 · ‖·‖^{b/2}`. The Mackey character is
unitary (`normComp_isUnitary`). `matchesHeckeInduction_normPower` gives the
normalized comparison over `D`, the constant coefficient `1` and the weight
`-b` of the raw polynomials. `reverse_map_of_injective` and injectivity of
`ι` move these from `ℂ[X]` to `L[X]`.

## One-term Mackey restriction

`pullbackAlongClosure_induced_spec` treats one coefficient place `λ`. Let
`H` and `S` be the images of the absolute groups of `E'` and `M`, and let
`r_D` be the restriction along `eD`. Then:

- `exists_mul_of_card_mackeyIndex_eq_one`: the singleton double coset gives
  `Gal(L/E) = Gal(L/D) · Gal(L/E')` (`DoubleCoset.eq`).
  `exists_mul_of_galois_mul` and `exists_mul_mul_of_mul_cover` of
  `Langlands.Galois.ClosureMackeyGalois` turn this into `Gal(Ē/E) = H x r_D(Γ_D)`
  for the comparison element `x = mackeyElement e eD eM ej`. The image of
  `Γ_D` is normal because `D/E` is Galois.
- `mackeyElement_conj` shows that conjugation by `x` maps `r_D(S)` into `H`.
  Conversely, `mem_range_of_conj_mem` shows that an element that `x`
  conjugates into `H` lies in `S`. Its hypothesis, that `D`-algebra maps out
  of `M` are determined on `E'`, is `mackeyField_algHom_ext`, because `M` is
  generated by `D` and the image of `E'`.
- `restrictInduceEquivOfOneCoset` of
  `Langlands.Weil.Representation.InductionSeparatedLines` then gives
  `Res_{Γ_D} Ind_H(φ) ≅ Ind_S(φ ∘ κ)`, with `κ` conjugation by `x`.
  By `mackeyElement_conj`, `φ ∘ κ` composed with `Γ_M → S` is `ψ ∘ r_{ej}`.

This shows that the pullback `RD λ` is induced from `ψ ∘ r_{ej}`. The
character `φ ∘ κ` agrees with the inducing character of `R' λ`, so `RD λ` is
equivalent to `R' λ`. Then
`QuadraticFrobeniusPatching.hasEulerPolynomialAt_equiv` transfers
`MatchesOutside ∅ R' S' P'` to `MatchesOutside ∅ RD S' P'`.

## Irreducibility by relative regularity

Let `κ' : M̄ → L̄` be a closure embedding and `N` the image of the absolute
group of `L` in that of `D`. `N` is normal because `L/D` is normal, and
`N ≤ S` (`range_restrictionAlongClosure_closureThroughM_normal`,
`range_closureThroughM_le`).

Take `x', y'` in distinct right cosets of `S`. Suppose `φ ∘ κ` took the same
value at `x' n x'⁻¹` and `y' n y'⁻¹` for all `n ∈ N`. Substituting
`n = y'⁻¹ m y'` shows that `g = y' x'⁻¹` leaves the restriction of `ψ ∘ r_{ej}`
to `N` invariant under conjugation. `mem_range_of_transportConj_invariant`
derives `g ∈ S`, a contradiction:

- the transported automorphism `τ` of `L̄` restricts to `σ' ∈ Gal(L/D)`;
- the conjugated character is `ψ` restricted along a closure embedding over
  the twisted embedding `σ' ∘ (E' → L)`
  (`closureEmbeddingOfRingHom`,
  `eq_restrictionAlongClosureHom_ofRingHom`). By
  `isCompatibleLambdaAdicRealization_normComp_hom` it realizes
  `ξalg ∘ N_{σ'}`;
- `heckeCharacter_eq_of_lambdaAdic_eq` of
  `Langlands.Galois.LambdaAdicHeckeRigidity` gives
  `ξalg ∘ N_{σ'} = ξalg ∘ N`. Since `ξ` is unitary and `‖·‖^{b/2}` is positive
  real, `eq_of_mul_normPower_eq` removes the twist. With
  `ideleClassNorm_algebra_twist` this states that `ξ ∘ N_{L/E'}` is invariant
  under `σ'⁻¹`;
- `InducedReciprocityIrreducible.fixes_of_ideleClassNorm_invariant`, which
  uses relative regularity of `ξ`, shows that `σ'` fixes `E'`. Then `σ'`
  fixes `M` by `mackeyField_algHom_ext`, and
  `mem_range_of_restrictedTransport_fixes` gives `g ∈ S`.

`induce_twist_irreducible_scalar_of_separation` now shows that
`Ind_S(φ ∘ κ)` is irreducible with scalar endomorphisms, and
`isIrreducible_of_equiv` and `hasScalarEndomorphisms_of_equiv` transfer both
properties to `RD λ`.

Michael Harris, *The local Langlands conjecture for GL(n) over a p-adic
field, n < p*, Invent. Math. 134 (1998), 177–210,
`harris-1998-local-langlands`. Lemma 1.6, pp. 188–189 (companion
`math_9611226v1.tex` lines 704–744), and Proposition 4.8, pp. 202–203 (lines
1574–1614 and 1666–1674), for the monomial restriction and irreducibility.
The remark on p. 191 (lines 866–875) gives the half-integral twist.
Harris argues with Mackey constituents of the restricted induced family and
regularity of the norm-pulled characters. The Lean proof follows the same
route in the singleton case. It takes the representation-theoretic criterion
and the Hecke-character rigidity from the helper modules named above.
