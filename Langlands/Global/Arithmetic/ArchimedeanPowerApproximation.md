# Approximation of unit power classes at the infinite places

`RepresentsArchimedeanUnitPowers F n a x` means that the global unit `a` has the prescribed power class `x v` at each infinite completion of `F`. Here a field unit is a nonzero field element. The accompanying theorem proves that every tuple of local units has a representative for every positive exponent.

Weak approximation gives a nonzero element in an open set in the infinite adele ring. At real coordinates the set makes its ratio to the prescribed unit positive. A positive real has an `n`-th root, and every nonzero complex number has one. Applying the actual completion isomorphisms gives the unit power equations in the completions. This is an internal arithmetic input for choosing Kummer corrections in the adjoint Hasse argument.
