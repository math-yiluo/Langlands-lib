# Assembly of the actual auxiliary Mackey child

This lemma combines the global constructions for the raw Mackey children
in Harris’s Proposition 4.8 (1998, printed pp. 202–203, TeX lines 1574–1614
and 1630–1648). It takes the auxiliary local reciprocity and character
data together with their normalized reciprocity, general position and
finite-component properties.

The inducing field is the actual compositum `D · a(E')`, regarded
over `D`, and the top field is its relative normal closure inside `L`.
The normal closure of the parent CM extension is CM, so the intermediate
fields of the parent closure and of its extension over `D` are CM. The
embeddings into the parent use the identity on the two literal carriers
and inclusion on the top field. The raw global inducing character is
the norm pullback of the conjugate parent character, with the parent's
twist exponent. The local data use the actual restricted auxiliary place
throughout. The unused primary fields of the bundle use the same place
and local character.

The child's rank is `[D · a(E'):D]` by the chosen algebra structure.
Its relative normal closure has degree at most `[L:D]`, which is strictly
less than `[L:E]` because `[D:E]` is prime.
