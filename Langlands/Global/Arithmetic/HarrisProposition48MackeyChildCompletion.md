# The completed fields of the raw Mackey child

The restriction of the parent's auxiliary place to the selected global intermediate field completes to the corresponding stage of the local tower. The ring isomorphism is the restriction of the parent's specified isomorphism of top completions. Composing with the closure isomorphism for the literal intermediate field embeds the top completion into an algebraic closure of the child's base.

In this closure the child extension and its normal closure are the images of the respective completed global fields. Completion preserves the relative normal closure by the unique-place Galois correspondence, and the commuting embedding preserves it by normality and minimality. The child place datum uses the three restricted primes. The base identification and the range of the extension identification both follow from the composition rule for completion maps.
