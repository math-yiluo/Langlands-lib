# The last step of the auxiliary tower

The proof of Harris’s Proposition 4.8 on printed page 202 needs the step immediately above the base in the auxiliary general-position tower. The tower has positive length when the global rank exceeds one. A tower of length zero would make the completed normal closure the base by the endpoint identifications. At the unique auxiliary place this would give global normal-closure degree one, which also forces rank one.

The penultimate local field is Galois of prime degree over the base. The compatible completion identifications carry this intermediate field into the actual completion of the global normal closure. The finite Galois correspondence then selects a prime cyclic global intermediate field, whose completed image is precisely the penultimate local field.
