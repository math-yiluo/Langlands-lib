# The actual singleton child and the auxiliary fields

`Proposition48SingletonChildNormalizationStatement` uses the auxiliary-valid `Q`, a prime cyclic step `D` with singleton Mackey index, and the actual supplied child `R` for the identity coset. The other inputs are the child rank formula from the target, the child's inherited parity correction `ν` with local character `χs`, and the actual raw and normalized solutions `S,T` related by the determinant multiple. The subgroup of `D` and that of the copy of `E'` multiply to `Gal(Q.L/E)` in this case. Thus `D` and `E'` are linearly disjoint over `E`, and the Mackey inducing degree `[D · E':D]` equals `[E':E]`. Equality of the ranks makes the parity correction `ν = 1` by `IsAuxiliaryParityNormalization`. The actual determinant relation at character one gives a full operator equivalence of `S` and `T`. Hence the regular algebraicity of `T` passes to `S`. The raw solution has all four properties of `R.IsSolution`, with its original weak inducing character and original auxiliary place. This is the singleton rank consequence of Harris 1998, Proposition 4.8, pp. 202–203, TeX lines 1574–1614 and 1666–1674, in the Library's explicit parity formulation.

`ArithmeticComapRegularAlgebraicStatement` moves regular algebraicity along `S.arithmeticComap e` for an actual isomorphism `e : K ≃+* E` of number fields. The field isomorphism reindexes the embeddings defining the archimedean Lie algebra and its complexification. It carries the envelopes, their centers, and the diagonal highest weights to the corresponding data of `K`. The same weight regularity condition holds, and the operator transport intertwines the action of the transported infinitesimal character. This is a transport-of-structure consequence for the actual regular algebraicity predicate. It gives the additional regularity property when the singleton child is pulled back along `R.E ≃+* D` in the inverse direction. The weak induction, unitary central character, conjugate duality and supercuspidal transports have their separate shared interfaces.

`Proposition48AuxiliaryStepPlacesStatement` takes the actual CM step field `D` and any place `w` of `D` over the auxiliary base place `Q.Ps.v`. Every place of `D` over the base place has a place of `Q.L` above it by lying over. The uniqueness assumed in `IsAuxiliaryValid` thus gives uniqueness in `D`. CM conjugation commutes with the inclusion `Q.E → D`, so the actual conjugate of `w` lies over the conjugate base place. The same uniqueness argument applies there. This is the tower comparison for the finite places in the proof of Proposition 4.8, printed pp. 202–203, with the paired-prime setup of Harris §2, printed p. 192.

`Proposition48AuxiliaryTheorem12FieldStatement` gives the field hypotheses of `Harris1998Theorem12CuspidalStatement` over the original base. Take `E₀` to be the maximal real subfield as an intermediate field over `ℚ`. It is totally real of index two in `E`. The supplied imaginary quadratic subfield `Q.K₀` cannot lie in `E₀`, so `E₀ · Q.K₀ = E`. Its prescribed splitting at the rational prime `Q.p` applies to the residue characteristic of `Q.Ps.v` because that ideal lies over `Q.p`. The statement packages the arithmetic hypotheses of Theorem 1.2, printed p. 185, and the prescribed splitting of Proposition 4.8 and its Section 2 setup.
