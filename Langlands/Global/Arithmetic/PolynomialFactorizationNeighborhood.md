# Coefficient neighborhoods preserving polynomial factorization types

`splitCoefficientNeighborhood_nonarchimedean` gives a positive coefficient radius for a monic, separable, split polynomial of positive degree over a complete nontrivially normed nonarchimedean field of characteristic zero. Every monic polynomial of the same degree within that radius splits with distinct roots over the base field. `splitCoefficientNeighborhood_real` gives the same result over \(\mathbb R\).

The proof bounds the coefficient perturbation so that each original root has a new root within a radius smaller than half the separation between distinct original roots. The comparison takes place in a normed algebraically closed Galois extension. Distinct original roots give distinct new roots, and the degree bound exhausts the new roots. The isometric Galois action fixes each original root, so it preserves each of the disjoint root neighborhoods and fixes the new root within it. The roots therefore lie in the base field. In the nonarchimedean case the norm on the algebraic closure is the spectral norm. In the real case the norm on \(\mathbb C\) is invariant under conjugation.

`irreducibleCoefficientNeighborhood_nonarchimedean` gives a positive coefficient radius for a monic irreducible polynomial over the same complete nonarchimedean field. The Krasner comparison supplies a root of a nearby monic polynomial of the same degree generating the same intermediate field as the original root. Its minimal polynomial divides the nearby polynomial and has the same degree, so the two monic polynomials coincide.
