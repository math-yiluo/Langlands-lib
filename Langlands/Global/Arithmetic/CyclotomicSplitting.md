# Cyclic cyclotomic extensions with large local degrees

This module proves the existence statement that Artin and Tate use to reduce the global
reciprocity law to the cyclic cyclotomic case (Chapter VII §3, Proposition on printed
p.46). Fix a number field `F`, a prime `ℓ`, an integer `a` and a finite set `S` of
finite places of `F`. The main theorem `exists_cyclic_splitting` gives a cyclic Galois
extension `K / F` inside a cyclotomic extension of `F` such that

- every local degree `[K_w : F_v]`, for `v ∈ S` and `w | v`, is divisible by `ℓ ^ a`;
- every infinite place of `K` is complex.

The field is `K = F(ξ)` with `ξ = ζ - ζ⁻¹` for a primitive `ℓ ^ (k + 3)`-th root of
unity `ζ`, for `k` large. It is built in the same universe as `F`, as
`Langlands.ClassField.Global.CyclicCyclotomicReciprocity` requires of its fields.

All declarations are in the namespace `Langlands.Global.CyclotomicSplitting`.
Throughout, `n = ℓ ^ (k + 3)`. Writing the exponent as `k + 3` makes `n` at least `8`
and lets Mathlib's prime-power lemmas apply with exponent `(k + 2) + 1`.

## Definitions

- `twoPowUnit i : (ZMod (2 ^ (i + 3)))ˣ` is the unit `2 ^ (i + 2) - 1`. Its inverse is
  itself (`twoPow_sub_one_mul_self`). For a primitive `2 ^ (i + 3)`-th root of unity
  `ζ`, `ζ ^ (2 ^ (i + 2) - 1) = -ζ⁻¹`.
- `fiveUnit i : (ZMod (2 ^ (i + 3)))ˣ` is the unit `5`.
- `Layer F ℓ k` (abbreviation) is Mathlib's `CyclotomicField (ℓ ^ (k + 3)) F`, i.e.
  `F(ζ)`. `zeta F ℓ k` (abbreviation) is Mathlib's chosen primitive root of unity
  `IsCyclotomicExtension.zeta` in it.
- `xi F ℓ k = ζ - ζ⁻¹` in the layer.
- `xiStabilizer F ℓ k` is the stabilizer of `ξ` in `Gal(F(ζ)/F)`.
- `xiField F ℓ k` is the fixed field of `xiStabilizer F ℓ k`, an intermediate field of
  `F(ζ) / F`. By the Galois correspondence it is `F(ξ)`. The Lean development uses only
  the fixed-field description; `ξ ∈ xiField` is shown inside `isComplex_xiField`.

The instances `neZero_pow` (`ℓ ^ (k + 3) ≠ 0`), `isGalois_layer`, `numberField_xiField`,
`normal_xiStabilizer` (the Galois group of `F(ζ)/F` is commutative), `isGalois_xiField`
and `isGalois_layer_xiField` (`F(ζ)` over `xiField`) make these usable as number fields and
Galois extensions.

## Theorems

Divisibility.

- `pow_dvd_of_pow_add_dvd_mul`: for a prime `ℓ`, if `ℓ ^ (A + c) ∣ X * B` with
  `0 < B < ℓ ^ c`, then `ℓ ^ A ∣ X`.
- `eventually_pow_dvd_orderOf`: for primes `p ≠ ℓ` and every `A`, eventually in `k`,
  `ℓ ^ A` divides the multiplicative order of `p` modulo `ℓ ^ k`.

Groups.

- `isCyclic_of_ker_le`: if `f : G →* A` is surjective and `ψ : G →* Q` has cyclic target
  and `ker ψ ≤ ker f`, then `A` is cyclic.
- `eq_one_or_eq_of_mem_zpowers`: the powers of an element `g` of order `2` are `1` and `g`.
- `twoPow_sub_one_mul_self`, `val_twoPowUnit`, `four_dvd_two_pow`,
  `castHom_twoPowUnit` (`twoPowUnit i` reduces to `-1` modulo `4`), `twoPowUnit_ne_one`,
  `orderOf_twoPowUnit` (order `2`).
- `orderOf_fiveUnit`: `5` has order `2 ^ (i + 1)` modulo `2 ^ (i + 3)`
  (Mathlib's `ZMod.orderOf_five`).
- `fiveUnit_pow_eq_one`: a power of `5` in the subgroup generated by `twoPowUnit i` is `1`,
  because powers of `5` are `1` modulo `4` and `twoPowUnit i` is `-1` modulo `4`.
- `isCyclic_units_quotient_twoPowUnit`: `(ZMod (2 ^ (i + 3)))ˣ ⧸ ⟨2 ^ (i + 2) - 1⟩` is
  cyclic, generated by the class of `5`.

The field `F(ξ)`.

- `zeta_spec`, `zeta_ne_zero`: `ζ` is a primitive `n`-th root of unity, hence nonzero.
- `algEquiv_ext_of_apply_zeta`: an `F`-automorphism of `F(ζ)` is determined by its value at
  `ζ`.
- `apply_xi`: `σ ξ = σ ζ - (σ ζ)⁻¹`.
- `apply_zeta_of_mem`: if `σ` fixes `ξ` then `σ ζ = ζ` or `σ ζ = -ζ⁻¹`; `mem_of_apply_zeta`:
  if `σ ζ = -ζ⁻¹` then `σ` fixes `ξ`.
- `card_xiStabilizer_le`: the stabilizer of `ξ` has at most two elements;
  `finrank_layer_le`: `[F(ζ) : xiField F ℓ k] ≤ 2`.
- `restrictNormalHom_eq_one`: elements of the stabilizer restrict to the identity of
  `xiField F ℓ k`.
- `apply_zeta_of_autToPow_eq_twoPowUnit`: for `ℓ = 2`, the automorphism `σ` with
  `σ ζ = ζ ^ (2 ^ (k + 2) - 1)` (Mathlib's `IsPrimitiveRoot.autToPow` equal to
  `twoPowUnit k`) sends `ζ` to `-ζ⁻¹`.
- `isCyclic_xiField`: `Gal(xiField F ℓ k / F)` is cyclic.
- `isComplex_xiField`: every infinite place of `xiField F ℓ k` is complex.

Local degrees. Here `e` and `f` are Mathlib's `Ideal.ramificationIdx` and
`Ideal.inertiaDeg`, and local degrees are `Module.finrank` of the `adicCompletion` fields
with the published module structure from `Langlands.Global.AdelicFunctoriality`
(scope `FiniteExtension`, installed by `w.asIdeal.LiesOver v.asIdeal`).

- `pow_dvd_ramificationIdx_mul_inertiaDeg`: let `Z` be a cyclotomic field
  `ℚ(ζ_{ℓ ^ (k + 3)})` (`IsCyclotomicExtension {ℓ ^ (k + 3)} ℚ Z`) and `P` a prime of `𝓞 Z`
  over the rational prime `p`. If `A ≤ k + 2` when `p = ℓ`, and `ℓ ^ A` divides the order of
  `p` modulo `ℓ ^ (k + 3)` when `p ≠ ℓ`, then `ℓ ^ A ∣ e(P|p) f(P|p)`.
- `ramificationIdx_mul_inertiaDeg_le_finrank`: for a prime `P` of a number field `K` over
  `p`, `e(P|p) f(P|p) ≤ [K : ℚ]`.
- `pow_dvd_localDegree`: let `p` be the residue characteristic of the finite place `v` of
  `F` (`Ideal.absNorm (v.asIdeal.under ℤ)`) and `A = a + 2 + [F : ℚ]`. If `A ≤ k + 2` when
  `p = ℓ`, and `ℓ ^ A` divides the order of `p` modulo `ℓ ^ (k + 3)` when `p ≠ ℓ`, then
  `ℓ ^ a ∣ [K_w : F_v]` for `K = xiField F ℓ k` and every place `w` of `K` over `v`.
- `exists_pow_dvd_localDegree`: for every `a` and finite set `S` of finite places of `F`
  there is `k` with `ℓ ^ a ∣ [K_w : F_v]` for all `v ∈ S` and all `w | v`, where
  `K = xiField F ℓ k`.
- `exists_cyclic_splitting` (main theorem): for every `a` and finite set `S` of finite
  places of `F` there are a number field `K : Type u` with an `F`-algebra structure such
  that `IsGalois F K`, `IsCyclic (K ≃ₐ[F] K)`, there is `m > 0` with an `F`-algebra map
  `K →ₐ[F] CyclotomicField m F`, `ℓ ^ a ∣ [K_w : F_v]` for all `v ∈ S` and all `w | v`, and
  every infinite place of `K` is complex. The cyclotomic condition is the one in
  `CyclicCyclotomicReciprocity`.

## Proofs

Divisibility. In `pow_dvd_of_pow_add_dvd_mul`, write `B = ℓ ^ b B'` with `ℓ ∤ B'`. Then
`b < c`, `B'` is prime to `ℓ`, so `ℓ ^ (A + c) ∣ X ℓ ^ b` and `ℓ ^ A ∣ X`. In
`eventually_pow_dvd_orderOf`, take `N = ℓ ^ A (ℓ - 1)` and `k > p ^ N`. If `ℓ ^ A` did not
divide the order `o` of `p` modulo `ℓ ^ k`, then, since `o ∣ φ(ℓ ^ k) = ℓ ^ (k - 1)(ℓ - 1)`,
`o` would divide `N`. Then `ℓ ^ k ∣ p ^ N - 1`, which is impossible as
`0 < p ^ N - 1 < k < ℓ ^ k`.

Cyclicity. `ψ` factors through `G ⧸ ker ψ`, which embeds in `Q` and is cyclic. The
restriction `Gal(F(ζ)/F) → Gal(F(ξ)/F)` is surjective (`AlgEquiv.restrictNormalHom_surjective`).
For `ℓ` odd, `autToPow : Gal(F(ζ)/F) →* (ZMod n)ˣ` is injective and `(ZMod n)ˣ` is cyclic
(`ZMod.isCyclic_units_of_prime_pow`), so `isCyclic_of_ker_le` applies with trivial kernel.
For `ℓ = 2`, take `ψ = autToPow` followed by the quotient by `⟨twoPowUnit k⟩`. An element
of its kernel has `autToPow σ = 1`, so `σ = 1`, or `autToPow σ = twoPowUnit k`, so
`σ ζ = -ζ⁻¹` and `σ` fixes `ξ`. In both cases `σ` restricts to the identity of `F(ξ)`, and
the quotient is cyclic by `isCyclic_units_quotient_twoPowUnit`. Its order count is
`φ(2 ^ (i + 3)) / 2 = 2 ^ (i + 1)`, equal to the order of the class of `5`.

Stabilizer of `ξ`. If `σ ξ = ξ` and `x = σ ζ`, then `(x - ζ)(x + ζ⁻¹) = 0`, which is a
polynomial identity given `x - x⁻¹ = ζ - ζ⁻¹`. So `σ ↦ σ ζ` injects the stabilizer into
`{ζ, -ζ⁻¹}`, and the fixed field has codegree `|stabilizer| ≤ 2`
(`IntermediateField.finrank_fixedField_eq_card`).

Complex places. Let `φ` be the embedding of a real place of `F(ξ)`, `ψ` an extension to
`F(ζ)` and `z = ψ ζ`. Then `|z| = 1`, so `z⁻¹ = z̄` and `φ ξ = z - z̄`. A real `φ ξ` gives
`z = z̄ = z⁻¹`, hence `z ^ 2 = 1`, and `n ∣ 2`, contradicting `n ≥ 8`.

Local degrees. In `pow_dvd_ramificationIdx_mul_inertiaDeg`, Mathlib computes `e` and `f`
in `Z = ℚ(ζ_{ℓ ^ (k + 3)})`: for `p = ℓ`, `e = ℓ ^ (k + 2)(ℓ - 1)`
(`IsCyclotomicExtension.Rat.ramificationIdx_eq_of_prime_pow`) and `f = 1`; for `p ≠ ℓ`,
`e = 1` and `f` is the order of `p` modulo `n`
(`IsCyclotomicExtension.Rat.inertiaDeg_eq_of_not_dvd`).
`ramificationIdx_mul_inertiaDeg_le_finrank` is one term of the fundamental identity
`Ideal.sum_ramification_inertia_eq_finrank`.

For `pow_dvd_localDegree`, let `u` be a place of `F(ζ)` over `w` (`placeAbove`), and
`P₀ = u ∩ 𝓞 Z` for `Z = ℚ(ζ) ⊆ F(ζ)`
(`IsPrimitiveRoot.intermediateField_adjoin_isCyclotomicExtension`). The products `e f`
over `ℤ` are multiplicative in the towers `ℤ ⊆ 𝓞 Z ⊆ 𝓞 F(ζ)` and
`ℤ ⊆ 𝓞 F ⊆ 𝓞 K ⊆ 𝓞 F(ζ)` (`Ideal.ramificationIdx_tower`, `Ideal.inertiaDeg_tower`):

`ef(P₀|p) · ef(u|P₀) = ef(u|p) = ef(w|v) · ef(u|w) · ef(v|p)`.

The first theorem gives `ℓ ^ (a + 2 + [F : ℚ]) ∣ ef(P₀|p)`. Here
`0 < ef(v|p) ≤ [F : ℚ] < ℓ ^ [F : ℚ]`. Also `ef(u|w) > 0`, and `ef(u|w) = [F(ζ)_u : K_w]` divides `[F(ζ) : K] ≤ 2 < ℓ ^ 2`, by
the published `localDegree_eq` and `localDegree_dvd`. Two applications of
`pow_dvd_of_pow_add_dvd_mul` give `ℓ ^ a ∣ ef(w|v)`, which is `[K_w : F_v]` by
`localDegree_eq`.

For `exists_pow_dvd_localDegree`, the hypotheses of `pow_dvd_localDegree` hold eventually in
`k` for each `v ∈ S`. For `p = ℓ` this needs `k ≥ A`. For `p ≠ ℓ` it follows from
`eventually_pow_dvd_orderOf` along `k ↦ k + 3`. A finite set of eventually-true conditions
holds simultaneously for some `k` (`Filter.eventually_all_finset`). The main theorem takes
`K = xiField F ℓ k` for this `k`, `m = ℓ ^ (k + 3)` and the inclusion of `K` into
`F(ζ) = CyclotomicField m F`.

Only the axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` are used.

## Provenance

Derived. The statement is the prime-power case of the Proposition in E. Artin and
J. Tate, *Class Field Theory* (2009), registered as `artin-tate-2009-class-field-theory`,
Chapter VII §3, printed p.46–47 (PDF p.55–56; PDF page = printed page + 9). There it is
used to prove the reciprocity law (Theorem 6), with the requirement at real places
(`r_p = 2`) met by making `K'` totally complex. Artin and Tate state the Proposition over a
base field `k` that is `ℚ` or a function field. They take for `K'` the `ℓ`-part of
`ℚ(ζ_{ℓ ^ n})` when `ℓ` is odd and `ℚ(ζ - ζ⁻¹)` when `ℓ = 2`. Combining the primes `ℓ`
into one compositum gives general `r_p`. They leave the growth of local degrees in
`ℚ_p(ζ_{ℓ ^ n})` to the reader.

This module differs from that account as follows. It works over an arbitrary number
field `F` and uses `F(ζ - ζ⁻¹)` for every prime `ℓ`. It treats one prime `ℓ` at a time
and gives only divisibility by `ℓ ^ a`; combining the primes `ℓ` is left to the caller.
The growth of local degrees is proved through Mathlib's ramification indices and inertia
degrees in `ℚ(ζ_{ℓ ^ (k + 3)})`, using the divisibility arguments above. The cyclicity
argument for `ℓ = 2` (the quotient by `2 ^ (k + 2) - 1`, generated by `5`) is Artin and
Tate's, phrased with `autToPow`.
