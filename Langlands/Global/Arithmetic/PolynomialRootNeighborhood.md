# Simultaneous control of roots under coefficient perturbation

`Langlands.Global.FieldRealization.PolynomialRootNeighborhoodStatement` gives a coefficient radius around a monic separable polynomial of positive degree in a normed field. In the stated normed extension, every root of a sufficiently close monic polynomial of the same degree lies within the prescribed radius of an original root, provided both polynomials split there. The new polynomial is separable.

The uniform radius is bounded by every prescribed root radius and by half the distance between any two distinct original roots. The quantitative root estimate gives a new root in each of the resulting disjoint balls. This assignment is injective. The old polynomial has as many distinct roots as its degree, so the assignment exhausts the roots of the new polynomial and gives separability.

When Krasner's lemma holds for the extension, the module also shows that the field generated by each new root contains an original root. This uses the existing radius that bounds distances to distinct conjugates of each original root. The result supplies the root control needed in the coefficient approximation step of §5.4 of Kneser's *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups*, printed p. 69.
