# A CM rational Galois model of a finite Galois p-adic field

Module `Langlands.Global.Arithmetic.CMGaloisModel`, namespace
`Langlands.Global.CMRealization`. These are supporting constructions for the proof of
`Langlands.Global.HarrisPairedPlaceRealizationStatement`. The module adds no assumptions.

## Provenance

Henniart (2000), Section 3.3, printed page 449 (registered source `henniart-2000-simple-proof`),
globalizes a finite p-adic extension by a number field and adjoins quadratic fields split at
`p`. Harris (1998), Section 4, printed pages 200–201 (registered source
`harris-1998-local-langlands`), uses an imaginary quadratic field `K₀` in which `p` splits, and
a second pair of places. This module is the internal construction used for those steps. It
takes a single Galois CM number field containing the totally real model, a square root of a
negative rational and a square root of a positive rational, and it adds an automorphism that
moves the positive square root only. The ingredients are the published Library results
`rationalDefiningPolynomial_spec` (module `RationalGaloisModel`) and `padicSquareNonsquare`
(module `PadicSquareNonsquareProof`), together with Mathlib's splitting fields.

## Square roots

For a rational `c` and a characteristic-zero field `L`:

- `aeval_X_sq_sub_C`, `monic_X_sq_sub_C`, `map_X_sq_sub_C` are bookkeeping for `X² - c`.
- `splits_X_sq_sub_C` and `exists_sq_of_splits`: `X² - c` splits over `L` exactly when `L`
  contains a square root of `c`.
- `exists_sq_of_isSquare`: if `c` is a square in a subfield `K`, every extension of `K` has a
  square root of `c`.
- `exists_eq_algebraMap_of_sq`: if moreover `r² = c` in `L`, then `r` lies in the image of `K`,
  since `r` is plus or minus the image of a square root in `K`.

## Totally real elements

- `mem_maximalRealSubfield_of_root`: a root of a nonzero rational polynomial that splits over
  `ℝ` is totally real. Every complex embedding sends it to a complex root of the polynomial,
  and these all lie in `ℝ`.
- `mem_maximalRealSubfield_of_sq_pos`: a square root of a positive rational is totally real.
  Its complex images have zero imaginary part (`complexRoot_im_zero`).

## The constants `a` and `d`

Fix a prime `p`, and fix `Ω`, a finite Galois extension of `ℚ_[p]` of characteristic zero.

- `negSq p` is a rational `a < 0` that is a square in `ℚ_[p]`. It is chosen by
  `padicSquareNonsquare` with `K = ℚ` (`negSq_neg`, `negSq_isSquare`). It is not a rational
  square because it is negative (`negSq_not_isSquare`).
- `basePolynomial p Ω` is `f · (X² - a)`. Here `f = rationalDefiningPolynomial p Ω` is the
  monic, separable, totally real rational polynomial with a root `y ∈ Ω` such that
  `ℚ_[p](y) = Ω`.
- `posSq p Ω` is a rational `d > 0` that is a square in `ℚ_[p]` and not a square in the
  splitting field of `basePolynomial p Ω`. It is again chosen by `padicSquareNonsquare`
  (`posSq_pos`, `posSq_isSquare`, `posSq_not_isSquare`).
- `cmPolynomial p Ω` is `f · (X² - a) · (X² - d)`. It is monic, and it is divisible by
  `basePolynomial`, by `X² - a` and by `X² - d`. Its roots are the roots of `basePolynomial`
  together with the square roots of `d` (`root_cases`). The roots of `basePolynomial` are the
  roots of `f` together with the square roots of `a` (`base_root_cases`).

The instance `splittingField_numberField` records that the splitting field of a rational
polynomial is a number field.

## The model

`cmModel p Ω : Type` is the splitting field of `cmPolynomial p Ω` over `ℚ`. It is Galois over
`ℚ` (`cmModel_isGalois`).

- `cmPolynomial_splits`: `cmPolynomial` splits in `Ω`. The polynomial `f` splits there by
  `rationalDefiningPolynomial_spec`, and `a` and `d` are squares in `ℚ_[p] ⊆ Ω`. So
  `cmModelEmbedding : cmModel p Ω →ₐ[ℚ] Ω` exists, as the lift of the splitting field.
- `cmModelEmbedding_adjoin`: the image of the model generates `Ω` over `ℚ_[p]`. The root `y` of
  `f` with `ℚ_[p](y) = Ω` is a root of `cmPolynomial`. So `y` lies in the image, which is
  generated by the roots of `cmPolynomial` in `Ω`.
- `sqrtNeg p Ω` and `sqrtPos p Ω` are chosen square roots of `a` and `d` in the model
  (`sqrtNeg_sq`, `sqrtPos_sq`). Both are nonzero. Their images in `Ω` lie in `ℚ_[p]`
  (`cmModelEmbedding_sqrtNeg`, `cmModelEmbedding_sqrtPos`) by `exists_eq_algebraMap_of_sq`.
- `maximalRealSubfield_adjoin_sqrtNeg`: the model is generated over its maximal real subfield
  by `sqrtNeg`. The roots of `cmPolynomial` are of three kinds. Roots of `f` are totally real,
  because `f` splits over `ℝ`. Square roots of `d` are totally real. Square roots of `a` are
  `±sqrtNeg`. The roots generate the splitting field.
- Instance `cmModel_isCMField`: the model is a CM field, by `isCMField_of_adjoin_eq_top` from
  `CMSubfield`. Complex conjugation negates `sqrtNeg` and fixes `sqrtPos` (`conjQ_sqrtNeg`,
  `conjQ_sqrtPos`).

## The central flip

Let `K₁` be the image of the splitting field of `basePolynomial` in the model, via the
embedding `baseSplittingEmbedding`. Every root of `basePolynomial` in the model lies in `K₁`
(`mem_baseRange_of_root`). The image of `d` is not a square in `K₁`
(`posSq_not_isSquare_baseRange`), by the choice of `d`.

- `exists_flipPos`: there is an automorphism `s` of the model fixing every root of
  `basePolynomial` and sending `sqrtPos` to `-sqrtPos`. The model is normal over `K₁`. Both
  `sqrtPos` and `-sqrtPos` have minimal polynomial `X² - d` over `K₁`
  (`squareRoot_minpoly`). So some automorphism over `K₁` sends one to the other
  (`Normal.minpoly_eq_iff_mem_orbit`). It fixes `K₁`, hence every root of `basePolynomial`.
- `flipPos p Ω` is such an automorphism. It negates every square root of `d`
  (`flipPos_of_sq_pos`). It fixes `sqrtNeg` (`flipPos_sqrtNeg`), a root of `basePolynomial`.
- `flipPos_central`: `flipPos` commutes with every automorphism `σ` of the model. Both
  composites are algebra maps out of a splitting field, so it suffices to compare them on the
  roots of `cmPolynomial` (`AlgHom.ext_of_adjoin_eq_top`). If `r` is a root of
  `basePolynomial`, so is `σ r`, and both sides give `σ r`. If `r² = d`, then `(σ r)² = d`,
  and both sides give `-σ r`.
