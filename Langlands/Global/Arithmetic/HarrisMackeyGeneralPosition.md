# General position of a Mackey child

This local argument implements the tower restriction used for Harris's auxiliary condition (iii) in the proof of Proposition 4.8, printed p. 202. It uses the tower and irreducible Mackey constituents supplied by Definition 4.5.

Inside the chosen algebraic closure of the intermediate field, intersect the earlier tower stages with the child's normal closure. Each intersection step has prime cyclic degree or is an equality. Deleting repetitions yields a prime cyclic tower with the required endpoints.

At each projected stage, the Weil subgroup is the join of the old stage subgroup with the subgroup fixing the child's normal closure. The latter lies in every conjugate inducing subgroup. Restriction to the old stage therefore has a single Mackey term. The character comparison identifies this term with the corresponding parent constituent, whose irreducibility implies irreducibility of the child constituent.
