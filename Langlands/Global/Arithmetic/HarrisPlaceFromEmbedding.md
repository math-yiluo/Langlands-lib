# Places and completions of a tower of number fields from dense local embeddings

Module `Langlands.Global.Arithmetic.HarrisPlaceFromEmbedding`, namespace
`Langlands.Global.FieldRealization`. Supporting lemmas, written for the proof of
`Langlands.Global.HarrisPairedPlaceRealizationStatement`. Every result is a
theorem; the module adds no definitions and no new assumptions.

## Provenance

The module provides internal supporting arguments for the global realization of a local
extension in Henniart (2000), Section 3.3, printed page 449 (registered source
`henniart-2000-simple-proof`), and Harris (1998), Section 4, printed pages 200–201 (registered
source `harris-1998-local-langlands`). Those sources take the completion identifications, the
uniqueness of the place above `v`, and the matching of degrees and normal closures for granted.
The arguments below are the standard ones. They are implemented with Mathlib and with the
published Library modules `DenseCompletionComparison` (`densePrime`,
`exists_adicCompletion_ringEquiv`), `GaloisModelRealization` (`denseRange_of_adjoin_eq_top`,
`exists_spectralNormedField`, `exists_spectralNormedAlgebra`), `NormInvariantPrime`
(`prime_unique_of_norm_invariant`), `AdelicFunctoriality` (`FiniteExtension.completionMap`,
`finitePlaceEquiv`), `HarrisSetup` and `GeneralPositionStatement` (`galoisClosure`,
`le_galoisClosure`).

## Normed layer

Here `F` is a nontrivially normed ultrametric field, complete where completions are taken.

- `exists_densePrime_completion` and `exists_completion_of_mem_iff`. Let `j : K →+* F` be a
  dense embedding of a number field. The completion of `K` at `densePrime j` is isomorphic to
  `F` by a ring isomorphism that is continuous in both directions and extends `j`. The same holds
  at any prime `v` given by `a ∈ v ↔ ‖j a‖ < 1`. The proof applies
  `exists_adicCompletion_ringEquiv` and identifies the two primes by ideal extensionality.
- `liesOver_densePrime`. Suppose `K → L` is compatible with `F → F'` through `j` and `j'`, and
  `F → F'` preserves norms. Then the prime of `j'` lies over the prime of `j`. The proof compares
  the norm conditions defining the two primes.
- `completionMap_comm`. Two continuous maps out of `K_v` that agree on `K` are equal. This holds
  because `K` is dense in `K_v` (`DenseRange.equalizer`) and the target is Hausdorff. The lemma
  gives each commuting completion diagram
  `ψ' ∘ completionMap v w = algebraMap A B ∘ ψ` from the corresponding diagram of embeddings.
- `eq_of_completion_eq`. Let `φ₁` and `φ₂` be two completion isomorphisms at the same prime `v`,
  extending `j₁` and `j₂`. Then `ψ = φ₂ ∘ φ₁⁻¹` is a continuous ring automorphism of the target.
  It fixes `ℚ` and hence, by density, `ℚ_[p]`. So if `j₁ x` lies in the image of `ℚ_[p]`, then
  `j₂ x = ψ (j₁ x) = j₁ x`.

## Galois comparison (`finrank_isNormalClosure_of_galoisEquiv`)

The setting has field towers `E ⊆ E' ⊆ L`, with `L / E` finite Galois, and `F₀ ⊆ F₁ ⊆ F₂`,
with `F₂ / F₀` finite Galois. There are ring embeddings `j₁ : E' → F₁` and `j₂ : L → F₂`, where
`j₁` generates `F₁` as an `F₀`-algebra and `j₂ ∘ (E' → L) = (F₁ → F₂) ∘ j₁`. Finally there is a
group isomorphism `θ : Gal(L/E) ≃* Gal(F₂/F₀)` with `θ σ (j₂ x) = j₂ (σ x)`. Under these
hypotheses `[E' : E] = [F₁ : F₀]` and `[L : E] = [F₂ : F₀]`. If moreover
`IsNormalClosure F₀ F₁ F₂`, then `IsNormalClosure E E' L`.

- An automorphism of `F₂ / F₀` that fixes `ρ(j₁(E'))` fixes `ρ(F₁)`, because `j₁(E')` generates
  `F₁` over `F₀` (`AlgHom.ext_of_adjoin_eq_top`). This is the private lemma
  `fixes_of_fixes_range`.
- `θ` carries the fixing subgroup of the image of `E'` onto that of `F₁`. In one direction, use
  the previous point with `ρ = 1`. In the other, use the compatibility and the injectivity of `j₂`.
- Degrees. `[L : E] = |Gal(L/E)| = |Gal(F₂/F₀)| = [F₂ : F₀]` by `IsGalois.card_aut_eq_finrank`.
  The fixing subgroups correspond, so `[L : E'] = [F₂ : F₁]` by
  `IsGalois.card_fixingSubgroup_eq_finrank`. Each field is replaced by its range in the larger
  one via `AlgHom.equivFieldRange`. The tower law and cancellation then give
  `[E' : E] = [F₁ : F₀]`.
- Normal closure. The proof uses `Algebra.IsAlgebraic.isNormalClosure_iff`. Splitting follows
  from normality of `L / E`. For `normalClosure E E' L = ⊤`, take `σ` fixing that normal
  closure. Every `F₀`-embedding `g : F₁ → F₂` extends to some `ρ ∈ Gal(F₂/F₀)`
  (`AlgHom.liftNormal`), and `ρ = θ τ` for some `τ`. The field `τ(E')` lies in the normal closure
  of `E'` (`AlgHom.fieldRange_le_normalClosure`), so `θ σ` fixes `ρ(j₁(E'))` and hence `g(F₁)`.
  Since `F₂` is generated by these fields, `θ σ = 1`. So the fixing subgroup of the normal
  closure is trivial. By the Galois correspondence (`IsGalois.fixedField_fixingSubgroup`,
  `fixedField_bot`) the normal closure is `L`.

## Module-topology layer (`exists_placeData`)

The setting is a tower `E ⊆ E' ⊆ L` of number fields with `L / E` Galois, and a tower
`F₀ ⊆ F₁ ⊆ F₂` of finite extensions of `ℚ_[p]` with compatible scalar towers. Take embeddings
`j₀, j₁, j₂`, each generating its target over `ℚ_[p]` and compatible along both towers, and a
`θ` as above. The theorem then produces the following, for the `ℚ_[p]`-module topologies.

- primes `v | v' | ṽ`, with all three `LiesOver` instances;
- completion isomorphisms `ψ₀, ψ₁, ψ₂`, continuous in both directions, with `ψ₀` extending `j₀`;
- `a ∈ v ↔ spectralNorm ℚ_[p] F₀ (j₀ a) < 1`;
- `HasUniquePlaceAbove L v`;
- the three commuting completion diagrams;
- `θ`-equivariance of `ψ₂` on `L`.

Proof. The fields and algebras are first replaced by the spectral-normed structures of
`GaloisModelRealization`. Density comes from `denseRange_of_adjoin_eq_top`. Norm preservation
along the towers follows from `spectralNorm.eq_of_tower`, and Galois invariance of
`‖j₂ ·‖` from `spectralNorm_eq_of_equiv` applied to `θ σ`. The primes, lying-over relations,
completions and diagrams then come from the normed layer. Uniqueness comes from
`prime_unique_of_norm_invariant`. Continuity is transported along `eq_moduleTopology`.

## Distinctness and generation

- `eq_of_place_eq`. Let `j, j' : E → F₀` both generate `F₀` over `ℚ_[p]`, and suppose they cut
  out the same prime `v` through the spectral norm. If `j x` lies in the image of `ℚ_[p]`, then
  `j' x = j x`. The proof passes to the spectral-normed structure, builds completion
  isomorphisms at `v` with `exists_completion_of_mem_iff`, and applies `eq_of_completion_eq`.
- `ne_of_place_eq` is the contrapositive. If `j x` lies in the image of `ℚ_[p]` and
  `j' x ≠ j x`, then the primes cut out by `j` and `j'` differ.
- `adjoin_eq_top_of_adjoin_base`. Suppose `adjoin R s = ⊤` in `K`, `adjoin K t = ⊤` in `A`, and
  `algebraMap K A '' s ⊆ t`. Then `adjoin R t = ⊤`. The image of `K` lies in `adjoin R t`
  (`Algebra.map_top`, `AlgHom.map_adjoin`), and the conclusion follows by induction on
  `adjoin K t`.

## Conjugation

- `mem_finitePlaceEquiv_iff`. If `v` is cut out by `N ∘ j`, then `finitePlaceEquiv e v` is cut
  out by `N ∘ j ∘ e`. This is `finitePlaceEquiv_mem`. Since
  `conjPlace E v = finitePlaceEquiv (complexConj E).toRingEquiv v`, conjugate places are cut out
  by conjugate embeddings.
- `range_comp_ringEquiv`. Composing with an automorphism does not change the range, so
  generation is preserved.
- `natCast_mem_finitePlaceEquiv`. A rational integer `n` lies in `finitePlaceEquiv e v` exactly
  when it lies in `v`, since `e` fixes `n`.
- `hasUniquePlaceAbove_finitePlaceEquiv`. Suppose `cE` extends to `cL` on `L`, that is,
  `cL ∘ (E → L) = (E → L) ∘ cE`. If `L` has a unique place above `v`, then it has a unique place
  above `finitePlaceEquiv cE v`. The proof checks at the level of rings of integers that
  `finitePlaceEquiv cL⁻¹` maps places above `finitePlaceEquiv cE v` to places above `v`. That map
  is injective.

## Harris specialization

Here `F` is a `PadicField p`, `F'` is an `Extension F` and `F̃ = galoisClosure F F'`. The
topologies are exactly those of the target statement: `F.canonicalTopology`,
`(fieldData F F').topology` and `(fieldData F F̃).topology`.

- `exists_harrisPlaceDatum`. The inputs are as follows.
  - number fields `E ⊆ E' ⊆ L` with `L / E` Galois;
  - embeddings `jE : E → F`, `jE' : E' → F'` and `jL : L → F̃`;
  - `jE` generates `F` over `ℚ_[p]`, while `jE'` and `jL` generate `F'` and `F̃` over `F`;
  - `jL ∘ (E → L) = (F → F̃) ∘ jE`, and `jL ∘ (E' → L)` agrees with `jE'` inside
    `AlgebraicClosure F`;
  - `θ : Gal(L/E) ≃* Gal(F̃/F)` with `θ σ ∘ jL = jL ∘ σ`.

  The output is a `Harris.PlaceDatum P` and an isomorphism `φ : E'_{v'} ≃+* F'` with the
  following properties.
  - `P.v` is cut out by `spectralNorm ℚ_[p] F ∘ jE`, and `P.iso` extends `jE`.
  - `p ∈ P.v`, and `HasUniquePlaceAbove L P.v`.
  - `P.IsCompatible` holds: the base diagram commutes, and the image of `E'_{v'}` in
    `AlgebraicClosure F` is `F'`.
  - `P.iso`, `P.isoTilde` and `φ` are continuous in both directions.
  - `P.isoTilde` is `θ`-equivariant.
  - The two `φ`-diagrams of the target hold.

  Proof. The `ℚ_[p]`-algebra structures of `F'` and `F̃` are those of
  `FiniteIntermediateExtension.asPadicField`. So `PadicField.canonicalTopologicalData_topology`
  identifies the canonical topologies with the module topologies used by `exists_placeData`.
  `F'` is embedded in `F̃` by `IntermediateField.inclusion (le_galoisClosure F F')`. Generation
  over `ℚ_[p]` comes from `adjoin_eq_top_of_adjoin_base`. The compatibility `E → E'` comes from
  the two given compatibilities. For `p ∈ P.v`, use
  `spectralNorm (algebraMap p) = ‖(p : ℚ_[p])‖ = p⁻¹ < 1` (`spectralNorm_extends`,
  `Padic.norm_p`). The image condition of `IsCompatible` holds because `φ` is surjective.
- `harrisDegrees`. The inputs are those of `exists_harrisPlaceDatum` concerning `jE'`, `jL` and
  `θ`. The conclusion is `[E' : E] = [F' : F]`, `[L : E] = [F̃ : F]` and
  `IsNormalClosure E E' L`. This is `finrank_isNormalClosure_of_galoisEquiv`. Its inputs are the
  inclusion `F' → F̃`, the `IsGalois` instance of the normal closure (characteristic zero), and
  `IsNormalClosure F F' F̃` from `Algebra.IsAlgebraic.isNormalClosure_normalClosure`, where
  splitting holds because `AlgebraicClosure F` is algebraically closed.
