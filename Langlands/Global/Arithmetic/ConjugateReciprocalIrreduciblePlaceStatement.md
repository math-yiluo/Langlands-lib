# A place forcing irreducibility of conjugate-reciprocal polynomials

Module: `Langlands.Global.Arithmetic.ConjugateReciprocalIrreduciblePlaceStatement`.

`Langlands.Global.ConjugateReciprocalIrreduciblePlaceStatement.{u}` is a proposition; this
module states it and does not prove it.

## The statement

Let `F` be a number field and `E/F` a separable quadratic extension, with nontrivial
automorphism `conjugation F E` (`e ↦ ē`, from `Langlands.GroupScheme.SU3Root`). Let `z ∈ E`
with `z * conjugation F E z = 1`, let `n > 0`, let `b : Module.Basis (Fin 2) F E` be an `F`-basis of `E`, and
let `S : Finset (Global.Place F)` be a finite set of places. Call a polynomial over `E`, or
over `F_v ⊗[F] E` for a completion `F_v = Global.placeCompletion F v`, *conjugate-reciprocal*
when its coefficients satisfy

* over `E`: `g.coeff (n - i) = (-1) ^ n * z * conjugation F E (g.coeff i)` for every `i ≤ n`;
* over `F_v ⊗[F] E`: `f.coeff (n - i) = (-1) ^ n * (1 ⊗ z) * c_v (f.coeff i)` for every
  `i ≤ n`, where `c_v = Algebra.TensorProduct.map (AlgHom.id F_v F_v)
  (conjugation F E).toAlgHom` is `a ⊗ e ↦ a ⊗ ē`.

The coordinates of a polynomial `f` over `F_v ⊗[F] E` are
`fun (i : Fin n) (j : Fin 2) => (Algebra.TensorProduct.basis F_v b).repr (f.coeff i) j`, the
coordinates of the coefficients of `X⁰, …, X^{n-1}` in the `F_v`-basis `1 ⊗ b j`; a polynomial
`g` over `E` has coordinates those of its coefficients `1 ⊗ g.coeff i` in `F_v ⊗[F] E`. The
set `Fin n → Fin 2 → F_v` carries the product topology.

The conclusion asserts a place `v ∉ S`, a monic conjugate-reciprocal `f` of natural degree `n`
over `F_v ⊗[F] E`, and an open set `U` of `Fin n → Fin 2 → F_v` containing the coordinates of
`f`, such that every monic conjugate-reciprocal `g : E[X]` of natural degree `n` whose
coordinates at `v` lie in `U` is irreducible.

## Provenance (derived)

Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), source
`platonov-rapinchuk-1994-algebraic-groups-number-theory`, proof of Theorem 6.28, printed
pp. 365–366 (PDF pp. 375–376), choose two auxiliary places `v₁, v₂` outside a finite set
`S₀`, prescribe there local algebras with involution generated by unitary elements of norm `y`
(at `v₁` the tensor product of `L ⊗ K_{v₁}` with an unramified extension of degree `n`, at `v₂`
the split algebra), and approximate their characteristic polynomials by a global polynomial
`f` satisfying (6.44); the local conditions at `v₁` and `v₂` make `P = L[t]/(f)` a field.
The Lean statement isolates the polynomial part of this step: for the relations (6.44) with
`a_0 = (-1)ⁿ z`, written in the convention `g = ∑ g_i Xⁱ` as `g_{n-i} = (-1)ⁿ z ḡ_i`, an open
local coefficient condition at one place outside `S` contains a local conjugate-reciprocal
polynomial and forces irreducibility over `E` of every global conjugate-reciprocal polynomial
satisfying it. One place suffices, for instance a place `v` split in `E` at which an embedding
`E → F_v` carries `f` to an irreducible polynomial of degree `n` over `F_v` with constant term
`(-1)ⁿ z`; nearby polynomials over `F_v` remain irreducible
(`Langlands.Global.FieldRealization.irreducibleCoefficientNeighborhood_nonarchimedean`).
Irreducibility of `g` over `E` is the statement that `E[t]/(g)` is a field.
