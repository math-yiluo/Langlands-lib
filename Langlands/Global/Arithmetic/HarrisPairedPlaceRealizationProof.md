# Proof of the CM paired-place realization

Module `Langlands.Global.Arithmetic.HarrisPairedPlaceRealizationProof`. The theorem
`Langlands.Global.harrisPairedPlaceRealization` proves the existing Library proposition
`Langlands.Global.HarrisPairedPlaceRealizationStatement.{uF}` without changing it. The module
also contains the helper theorem `Langlands.Global.exists_galois_extension`. It adds no
definitions and no assumptions.

## Provenance

The statement is the field-and-place existence input of Henniart (2000), Section 3.3, printed
page 449 (registered source `henniart-2000-simple-proof`), in the form of Harris (1998),
Section 4, printed pages 200–201 (registered source `harris-1998-local-langlands`). The refinements
beyond the sources are explained in `HarrisPairedPlaceRealizationStatement.md`. The proof follows
Henniart's route: globalize the local normal closure, adjoin quadratic fields split at `p`, and
take the global fields that correspond to the local tower. It is a derived composition of the
supporting modules `CMSubfield`, `CMGaloisModel`, `HarrisPlaceFromEmbedding`,
`CMPairedPlaceAssembly` and `CMPairedPlaceModel`, and the published Library modules
`LocalNormalModel`, `GaloisModelRealization`, `RationalGaloisModel`,
`PadicSquareNonsquareProof` and `QuadraticPadicSplittingProof`.

## The proof

`exists_galois_extension`: every finite extension `K` of `ℚ_[p]` (a `PadicField`, in any
universe) is contained in a finite Galois extension `Ω` of `ℚ_[p]` of characteristic zero, in
the same universe. Take `Ω = localNormalClosure p K`, the normal closure of `K` over `ℚ_[p]` in
an algebraic closure of `K` (module `LocalNormalModel`). The result is stated as an existence
statement so that the main proof can introduce `Ω` and its instances as hypotheses.

`harrisPairedPlaceRealization`: let `p` be a prime, `F` a finite extension of `ℚ_[p]` and `F'` a
finite extension of `F` in the specified algebraic closure. Let `F̃` be the Galois closure
`galoisClosure F F'`.

1. Apply `exists_galois_extension` to `F̃`, viewed as a finite extension of `ℚ_[p]`
   (`asPadicField`). This gives `Ω`, finite Galois over `ℚ_[p]`, with a compatible embedding
   `F̃ → Ω`.
2. Give `F'` and `F̃` the `ℚ_[p]`-algebra structures of `asPadicField`, which are the composites
   through `F`, so that `ℚ_[p] → F → F'` and `ℚ_[p] → F → F̃` are scalar towers.
3. Take the CM rational Galois model `N = cmModel p Ω` (module `CMGaloisModel`), with its
   embedding into `Ω` (`cmModelEmbedding`), whose image generates `Ω` over `ℚ_[p]`
   (`cmModelEmbedding_adjoin`).
4. Apply `pairedPlaceRealization_of_model` (module `CMPairedPlaceModel`). The inputs are:
   `a = negSq p < 0`, a square in `ℚ_[p]`; `d = posSq p Ω > 0`; the square roots
   `x_a = sqrtNeg p Ω` and `x_d = sqrtPos p Ω`, both sent into `ℚ_[p]`; and the central flip
   `s = flipPos p Ω`, which fixes `x_a` and negates `x_d`.

The resulting global fields are the model fields `E ⊆ E' ⊆ L` inside `N`, all in `Type 0`. They
are CM, with `K₀ = ℚ(x_a)` split imaginary quadratic. The two place data are cut out by the
embeddings `E → F`, `E' → F'`, `L → F̃` and by their twists by the restrictions of `s`. The
four distinct places are these two together with their complex conjugates. In more detail:

- Fields. `E`, `E'` and `L` are the elements of `N` whose images in `Ω` lie in `F`, `F'` and
  `F̃` respectively. Complex conjugation of `N` is central, so it preserves them, and `x_a ∈ E`
  makes them CM fields. Each is generated by its maximal real subfield and `K₀`.
- Primes. `quadraticPadicSplitting` gives two distinct primes of `K₀` above `p`. The places of
  `E` are cut out by spectral norm less than one under the embeddings into `F`. The places
  coming from `jE`, `jE ∘ c`, `jE ∘ t` and `jE ∘ t ∘ c` are distinct, because `x_a` and `x_d`
  are sent into `ℚ_[p]` and are negated by `c` and by the restriction `t` of `s` respectively.
- Completions. The completion isomorphisms extend the dense embeddings and are continuous in
  both directions for the canonical topologies (module `HarrisPlaceFromEmbedding`, built on the
  published `DenseCompletionComparison`).
- Galois groups. A single `θ : Gal(L/E) ≃* Gal(F̃/F)` from `exists_galoisEquiv` serves both
  place data, because the restriction of `s` to `L` commutes with `Gal(L/E)`.
- Degrees and normal closure. These come from `θ` and the generation of `F'` by `E'`
  (`harrisDegrees`).

Every prime `p`, including `2`, is allowed, and the universe of `F` is arbitrary. The extension
`F' / F` need not be Galois.
