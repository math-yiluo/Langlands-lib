# Profinite positive-power quotients of actual idele classes

Authors: Worker 107 and Worker 450. The archimedean finite-index argument
in the S-unit power-quotient helper is adapted from the prescription draft
by Worker 53 and Worker 1232.

`Langlands.Global.ideleClassPowerQuotientProfinite_proof.{u}` proves the
published proposition `IdeleClassPowerQuotientProfiniteStatement.{u}`.
For every number field `K : Type u` and every positive natural number `m`,
the actual group `IdeleClasses K`, modulo the range of its `m`th-power
homomorphism, is Hausdorff, compact, and totally disconnected. The
topologies throughout are the existing subgroup, product and quotient
topologies. The universe of the number field is unrestricted.

The implemented proof uses an open S-unit idele subgroup. Write `I` for
the actual ideles, `P` for the principal ideles, and choose
`S = IdelePowerQuotientCover.places K`. The proved class-group construction
in `IdeleSUnits` supplies this finite set with global uniformizers outside
`S`. The theorem `IdelePowerQuotientCover.principal_sup` gives
`P ⊔ H = ⊤`, where `H = IdeleSUnits.sUnitIdeles K S`.
The theorem `SUnitIdeleTopology.isOpen_sUnitIdeles` proves that `H` is
open in `I` by simultaneous integrality of the finite value and inverse
coordinates outside `S`.

The helper `SUnitIdelePowerQuotient.sUnitIdeles_powerQuotient_profinite`
proves the three topological properties for `H/H^m`. Its product
description includes every infinite coordinate, with the real sign
components, the whole multiplicative groups at finite places in `S`,
and the valuation-unit groups elsewhere. Each local positive-power
subgroup is open by the inverse function theorem at one, and has
proved finite index. Its quotient is therefore finite and discrete.
The product power-quotient homeomorphisms identify `H/H^m` with the
product of these finite discrete quotients. This proves Hausdorffness,
compactness and total disconnectedness for the actual quotient topology,
without inferring openness from finite index.

The generic theorem
`Langlands.openSubgroupPowerQuotient_profinite_of_fg` now applies to
`G = I`, `P` and `H`. Its induced homomorphism
`H/H^m → (I/P)/(I/P)^m` is a continuous open surjection: the generation
condition gives surjectivity, while openness of `H` and of group quotient
maps gives openness. Its kernel is exactly the image of `P.subgroupOf H`
in `H/H^m`. The arithmetic theorem
`IdelePowerQuotientCover.principal_intersection_fg` proves that this
intersection is finitely generated by identifying it with the image of
the finitely generated group of `S`-units. Its image in `H/H^m` is
finitely generated and has exponent dividing the positive integer `m`;
the finiteness theorem for finitely generated torsion abelian groups
therefore makes this kernel finite.

Here is how the generic theorem establishes each required conclusion.
The finite kernel is closed in the Hausdorff source. Since the map is
a quotient map, the identity singleton in the target is closed, making
that topological group Hausdorff. In particular, the actual power
subgroup of `IdeleClasses K` is closed, as the preimage of this identity
under the canonical power-quotient map. Compactness follows from the
continuous surjection from the compact space `H/H^m`. For distinct
target points, lift the first point and choose a clopen source
neighborhood excluding the fiber of the second. Its image is open
because the map is open, and closed because it is compact in the
Hausdorff target. These clopen sets separate target points, proving
total separation and hence total disconnectedness.

Finally, `IdeleClasses K` is definitionally the actual quotient `I/P`.
The Lean proof installs the three properties of `H/H^m` as local
instances and applies the generic theorem with the proved openness,
generation, finite-generation and positivity hypotheses. Its conclusion
is exactly the published target on the actual double quotient.

The arithmetic context and target are Artin and Tate, *Class Field Theory*,
AMS Chelsea edition (2009), Chapter X, Section 2, Lemmas 3, 5 and 6 and
their proofs, printed pp.77–79 (one-based PDF pp.86–88). The registered
source is `artin-tate-2009-class-field-theory`, SHA-256
`af7ba934904a3c10012e6fd326cea493ffb6200d885d97583277bb45f935c3dd`;
the appended errata at PDF p.202 contain no relevant correction.
The S-unit and finite-kernel argument above is the implemented internal
proof route. It derives the conclusions from proved arithmetic and
topology results and assumes no norm-one compactness theorem or
class-field-theory package.
