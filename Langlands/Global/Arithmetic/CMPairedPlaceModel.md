# CM paired-place realization from a CM rational Galois model

Module `Langlands.Global.Arithmetic.CMPairedPlaceModel`, namespace
`Langlands.Global.CMRealization`. These are supporting theorems for the proof of
`Langlands.Global.HarrisPairedPlaceRealizationStatement`. The module adds no definitions and no
assumptions.

## Provenance

The construction follows the route of Henniart (2000), Section 3.3, printed page 449
(registered source `henniart-2000-simple-proof`): realize the local tower inside a global
Galois field and adjoin quadratic fields split at `p`. It produces the data that Harris (1998),
Section 4, printed pages 200–201 (registered source `harris-1998-local-langlands`), assumes.
The global fields are the model fields of the published Library module `GaloisModelRealization`
(`modelField`, `topModel`, `baseEmbedding`, `topEmbedding`, `adjoin_range_baseEmbedding`,
`adjoin_range_topEmbedding`, `exists_galoisEquiv`, `isGalois_topModel`, `modelField_le`). They
are taken inside a CM model instead of a totally real one. The split imaginary quadratic field
comes from the published `quadraticPadicSplitting` (module `QuadraticPadicSplittingProof`), with
`squareRoot_finrank`, `rationalRootField_totallyComplex` and
`rationalRootField_two_embeddings`. Degrees and the normal closure come from `harrisDegrees` in
`HarrisPlaceFromEmbedding`. The four places come from `pairedPlaces_of_embeddings` in
`CMPairedPlaceAssembly`.

## Helper lemmas

- `exists_restrict`: let `S → N` be number fields and `u` an automorphism of `N` over `ℚ` that
  preserves the image of `S`. Then `u` restricts to a ring automorphism `f` of `S`. The
  restriction is an injective `ℚ`-algebra endomorphism of `S`. It is bijective because `S` is
  algebraic over `ℚ` (`Algebra.IsAlgebraic.algHom_bijective`).
- `adjoin_eq_top_of_restrictScalars`: if `s` generates `A` as an `R`-algebra and `R → K → A` is
  a scalar tower, then `s` generates `A` as a `K`-algebra.
- `not_isSquare_of_neg`: a negative rational is not a rational square.
- `cmQuadratic`: let `E → E' → L` be number fields mapping into a CM field `M`, each image
  stable under `conjQ M`. Let `x ∈ E` satisfy `x² = a` for a rational `a < 0` that is a square
  in `ℚ_[p]`. Then `E`, `E'` and `L` are CM fields (`isCMField_of_sq_neg`). The field
  `K₀ = ℚ(x)` has degree 2 (`squareRoot_finrank`) and is totally complex. Its two embeddings
  into `ℚ_[p]` are distinct (`rationalRootField_two_embeddings`). So `quadraticPadicSplitting`
  gives two distinct primes of `K₀` containing `p`, which makes `K₀` split imaginary quadratic.
  Finally `E`, `E'` and `L` are each generated by their maximal real subfield and the image of
  `K₀` (`maximalRealSubfield_sup_eq_top`).
- `mem_modelField_of_padic`: an element of `N` whose image in `Ω` lies in `ℚ_[p]` lies in every
  model field.
- `modelField_central` and `topModel_central`: model fields are intermediate fields of the
  Galois field `N`, so they are stable under central automorphisms of `N` (`mem_of_central`).

## The main theorem (`pairedPlaceRealization_of_model`)

The hypotheses are:

- a finite Galois extension `Ω / ℚ_[p]` of characteristic zero;
- `ℚ_[p]`-algebra structures on `F'` and `F̃` (the Galois closure of `F'` over `F`) compatible
  with `F`, and a `ℚ_[p]`-compatible embedding `F̃ → Ω`;
- a Galois CM number field `N : Type` embedded in `Ω` with image generating `Ω` over `ℚ_[p]`;
- rationals `a < 0 < d` with `a` a square in `ℚ_[p]`, square roots `x_a` of `a` and `x_d` of `d`
  in `N`, both sent into `ℚ_[p]`;
- an automorphism `s` of `N`, commuting with every automorphism of `N`, with `s x_a = x_a` and
  `s x_d = -x_d`.

The conclusion is the body of the target statement for `F` and `F'`, including the canonical
topologies.

The proof:

1. Make `F ⊆ F' ⊆ F̃ ⊆ Ω` a tower of algebras and scalar towers over `ℚ_[p]`, using the inclusion
   `F' ≤ F̃` (`le_galoisClosure`). Record that `F'` and `F̃` are finite over `ℚ_[p]`, and that
   `F̃ / F` is Galois as a normal closure.
2. Set `E = modelField p Ω N F`, `E' = topModel p Ω N F F'` and `L = topModel p Ω N F F̃`. Then
   `E' ≤ L` by `modelField_le`, giving the tower `E ⊆ E' ⊆ L`. `L / E` is Galois
   (`isGalois_topModel`).
3. The elements `x_a` and `x_d` lie in `E` (`mem_modelField_of_padic`). Complex conjugation of
   `N` is central (`conjQ_central`), so `E`, `E'` and `L` are stable under it. `cmQuadratic`
   then gives the CM structures, the split imaginary quadratic field `K₀ = ℚ(x_a) ⊆ E` and the
   three compositum equalities.
4. The embeddings are `jE = baseEmbedding`, `jE' = topEmbedding` and `jL = topEmbedding`. They
   generate over `ℚ_[p]` (`adjoin_range_*`), hence over `F` (`adjoin_eq_top_of_restrictScalars`).
   They are compatible (`topEmbedding_algebraMap`, `algebraMap_topEmbedding` and injectivity of
   `F̃ → Ω`). `exists_galoisEquiv` supplies `θ : Gal(L/E) ≃* Gal(F̃/F)` with
   `θ σ (jL x) = jL (σ x)`.
5. `s` restricts to automorphisms `t`, `t'`, `tL` of `E`, `E'`, `L` (`exists_restrict` with
   `modelField_central` and `topModel_central`). These are compatible with the inclusions by
   injectivity into `N`. `tL` commutes with every `σ ∈ Gal(L/E)`: extend `σ` to `N`
   (`AlgEquiv.liftNormal`) and use the centrality of `s`.
6. The extension of complex conjugation of `E` to `L` is the complex conjugation of `L`. Both
   are restrictions of `conjQ N` (`algebraMap_complexConj`). Inside `E`, complex conjugation
   negates `x_a` (`conjQ_of_sq_neg`) and fixes `x_d` (`mem_maximalRealSubfield_of_sq_pos`).
   Also `t x_a = x_a` and `t x_d = -x_d`, and `jE x_a`, `jE x_d` lie in `ℚ_[p]` because their
   images in `Ω` do.
7. `harrisDegrees` gives `[E' : E] = [F' : F]`, `[L : E] = [F̃ : F]` and
   `IsNormalClosure E E' L`. `pairedPlaces_of_embeddings` gives the two place data and every
   remaining clause.

The instance arguments for the `ℚ_[p]`-algebras and scalar towers are named (`a₁`, `a₂`, `t₁`,
`t₂`, `aΩ`, `tΩ`). This lets the final proof pass them explicitly.
