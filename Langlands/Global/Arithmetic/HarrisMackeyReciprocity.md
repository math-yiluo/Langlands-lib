# Reciprocity after changing the base field

This comparison supports the local character restriction in Harris's auxiliary condition (iii) in the proof of Proposition 4.8, printed p. 202. A commuting field isomorphism identifies the child inducing field with its copy in the parent algebraic closure. The prescribed Weil inclusion carries the child fixing subgroup into the parent fixing subgroup of that field.

Norm compatibility and normalization identify each supplied reciprocity family with the family constructed from standalone geometric reciprocity. The two standalone reciprocity values then agree under the field isomorphism by the published comparison for compatible algebraic closure isomorphisms.

The fixing subgroup of the embedded compositum is the intersection of the two fixing subgroups. Since the image of the child Weil group already fixes the new base field, this reduces membership to fixing the conjugate inducing field. Norm compatibility takes reciprocity from the compositum to that conjugate field, and conjugation compatibility brings it back to the inducing field of the parent. Applying the parent character gives the pointwise Mackey character formula.

The field character is the composition of the map on units for the inducing-field identification, the continuous determinant norm, the inverse conjugation on units, and the parent field character. Both field isomorphisms are linear over the specified p-adic base and therefore continuous in the canonical module topologies.
