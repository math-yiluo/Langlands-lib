# Number-field extensions with prescribed local degree multiples

For a number field `F`, a finite set of its actual finite places, and a
positive integer `n`, `NumberFieldLocalDegreeMultiplesStatement` asks for a
finite Galois number-field extension of `F` in the same universe. At every
place of the extension above the specified set, the completed extension
degree is divisible by `n`.

Choose a nonzero parameter in `F` with the normalized value of a
uniformizer at every selected place. Weak approximation gives that
parameter by approximation in open balls on which the valuation is
constant. The extension is the splitting field of the polynomial
`X^n - a`. In the completion at any upper place, an `n`-th root of `a`
has integer valuation exponent. By the completion ramification formula,
the exponent of `a` is the negative of the ramification index there.
Thus `n` divides the ramification index and the local degree.

The subfield generated by one root gives a second construction with
global degree `n`. The minimal polynomial divides `X^n - a`, so the
global degree is at most `n`. Include one auxiliary place among the
uniformizer conditions to ensure there is a local degree divisible by
`n`, even for an empty prescribed set. The local degree bound by the
global degree then gives equality, both globally and at all selected
upper places.

This is an internal arithmetic construction using the actual
completions, the normalized completion valuations, and the Library's
mixed weak approximation theorem. It supplies a common relative
Galois stage and a degree-`n` extension that kills the local invariants
of finite Brauer profiles of exponent dividing `n`.
