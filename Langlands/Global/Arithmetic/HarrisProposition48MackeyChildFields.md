# Fields and places of an actual raw Mackey child

The child over `D` uses the compositum `D · a(E')` inside the parent normal closure `L`. Its top field is the normal closure of that compositum over `D`, taken inside `L`. The child rank is the degree of the compositum over `D`. The degree of the relative normal closure is bounded by `[L:D]`, which is strictly smaller than `[L:E]` because `[D:E]` is prime.

The split imaginary quadratic subfield is mapped from the parent base into `D`. The three auxiliary places are the restrictions of the parent's chosen top place. Uniqueness descends through the tower of fields and transfers to the conjugate place via arithmetic complex conjugation. These are the arithmetic field and place data used in the induction on printed pages 202–203 of Harris (1998), Proposition 4.8.
