# CM fields with the two compatible place pairs in Harris's setup

Worker 84. This contribution defines the proposition
`Langlands.Global.HarrisPairedPlaceRealizationStatement.{uF}`. It asserts
arithmetic existence and supplies no proof. Its fields, primes, completions,
maps and complex conjugations are the existing Library and Mathlib objects.

For every prime `p`, every `F : PadicField.{uF} p` and every finite
intermediate extension `F'` in the specified `AlgebraicClosure F.Carrier`,
the statement asks for CM number fields `E`, `E'` and `L`, compatible
algebra structures for `E ⊆ E' ⊆ L`, and `IsNormalClosure E E' L`.
The input `F'/F` need not be Galois. Its local normal closure is exactly
`GeneralPosition.galoisClosure F F'`.

The output number fields have carriers in `Type 0`; the input universe
`uF` is unrestricted. There is no restriction on the prime, including `2`,
the extension degree, or ramification.

The witnesses have the following properties.

- There is an actual intermediate field `K₀ : IntermediateField ℚ E`
  satisfying `Harris.IsSplitImaginaryQuadratic p K₀`: degree two over
  `ℚ`, totally complex, and two distinct actual primes above `p`.
- The compositum of `K₀` with `maximalRealSubfield E` is all of `E`.
  The images of the same `K₀` in `E'` and `L` similarly generate those
  fields together with their maximal real subfields. These are equalities
  of suprema of actual subfields inside each ambient field. Thus the
  usual expressions `E = E⁺K₀`, `E' = E'⁺K₀` and `L = L⁺K₀` are
  explicit assertions. They do not refer to unspecified isomorphism
  classes.
- The degrees satisfy `[E' : E] = [F' : F]` and
  `[L : E] = [F̃ : F]`, where `F̃` is that local normal closure.
- `P` and `Ps` are the existing `Harris.PlaceDatum F F' E E' L`.
  Each supplies actual places `v` of `E`, `v'` of `E'`, and `vTilde`
  of `L`, the three lying-over instances, a ring equivalence `E_v ≃ F`,
  and a ring equivalence `L_vTilde ≃ F̃`.
- The list `P.v, c(P.v), Ps.v, c(Ps.v)` is pairwise distinct.
  Here `c(v)` is `Harris.conjPlace E v`, defined from the actual CM
  involution. All four primes contain `p` and each has exactly one
  place of `L` above it, in the sense of `Harris.HasUniquePlaceAbove`.
- For both `P` and `Ps`, the base and normal-closure equivalences are
  continuous in both directions. They satisfy `PlaceDatum.IsCompatible`:
  the full base completion diagram commutes, and the image of the
  intermediate completion in the specified algebraic closure is
  exactly `F'.field`.
- One group equivalence
  `θ : Gal(L/E) ≃ Gal(F̃/F)` intertwines both normal-closure
  completion equivalences on every element of `L`. Thus the group
  comparison agrees with the chosen local maps at both places.
- Explicit ring equivalences `φ : E'_(P.v') ≃ F'` and
  `φs : E'_(Ps.v') ≃ F'` are continuous in both directions.
  Each extends the associated base equivalence through the actual
  `FiniteExtension.completionMap`. Its composite into the specified
  algebraic closure agrees with the corresponding normal-closure
  equivalence after the actual map from `E'_(v')` to `L_(vTilde)`.

`F` has `F.canonicalTopology`. Each finite intermediate local field has
the topology of `LocalFactor.CommonClosure.fieldData F`; these are the
existing canonical local-field topologies. Global finite completions
have their actual adic topologies. Continuity is required explicitly;
the statement does not rely on an unproved automatic-continuity claim
for arbitrary ring equivalences.

## Original-source correspondence

Guy Henniart, *Une preuve simple des conjectures de Langlands pour
GL(n) sur un corps p-adique*, Inventiones mathematicae 139 (2000),
439–455, §3.3, printed p.449. Registered source
`henniart-2000-simple-proof`, SHA-256
`be283c3e8187f1eed365537c72379c3e06e7791756954c2ac783c6beea2979de`.
The original printed page was read directly for this contribution.

That passage globalizes a finite Galois local extension by totally real
number fields, preserving the degree and the full Galois group at one
place. It then chooses disjoint imaginary and real quadratic fields
split at `p` and forms the CM composita. The real quadratic field
provides two local realizations. Henniart also records that intermediate
fields are stable under CM conjugation and are generated by their real
fixed fields and the imaginary quadratic field.

Michael Harris, *The local Langlands conjecture for GL(n) over a p-adic
field, n < p* (1998), §4, printed pp.200–201 (one-based PDF pages
24–25). Registered source `harris-1998-local-langlands`, SHA-256
`7934239ea37d0b91f741ae70c2ac157fe5d619cda4dd992ac24e39ae460c375b`.
Both original pages were read directly for this contribution.

On p.200 Harris fixes CM fields `E'/E` and the normal closure `L/E`,
an imaginary quadratic subfield split at `p`, compatible realizations
of `F` and `F̃` at a conjugate pair, and the induced realization of
`F'`. He then requires a second such pair, explaining that a compositum
with a real quadratic field split at `p` supplies it. On p.201 the
comparison of the global and local Galois groups identifies the
intermediate fields used in the proof of Proposition 4.8.

The proposition is a derived extraction of the arithmetic existence
input from these passages, rather than a verbatim numbered theorem.
Starting with arbitrary `F'`, one applies the normal-closure
globalization and takes the intermediate field corresponding to `F'`.
The exact intermediate image, the explicit intermediate equivalences,
all commuting diagrams, continuity in both directions and the common
Galois comparison make the source's compatibility requirements explicit.
The explicit pairwise distinctness and degree identities also retain
consequences of the source construction. These are obligations of a
future proof of this proposition; this file does not prove them or
infer them from the existence of arbitrary ring equivalences.

The supplied Harris source-folder `CORRECTION_SOURCE.md` was read. It
identifies corrections to Harris's 1997 paper contained on pp.180–182
of the 1998 article; it does not list an erratum to the present
pp.200–201 setup. No source-specific errata note was present in the
supplied public notes tree.

## Boundaries and reuse

The existing `TotallyRealGaloisRealizationStatement` and
`SplitAuxiliaryQuadraticsStatement` specify earlier construction
inputs. Neither states this CM-compositum and paired-place output.
`HarrisSetup` defines `PlaceDatum` and its compatibility relation but
does not assert that such fields or places exist. This statement reuses
those objects without changing them.

The assertion is existential in its global fields. It does not construct
the output from specified global fields or specified auxiliary
quadratics. It covers the base, the selected extension and its normal
closure; it does not supply a family of all global intermediate fields,
a cyclic-prime tower or functorial completion maps throughout such a
family. Those are further construction and comparison obligations.

There is no Hecke character, local character, reciprocity family,
general-position claim, infinity type, central-character condition,
Harris half-norm twist or automorphic representation in this statement.
In particular it does not assert `Harris.IsProposition48Datum`,
Harris's Proposition 4.8, or completion of the Henniart globalization.
It isolates one arithmetic input to H2 and, later, H3 of program
`34b81620-20d7-4059-9b08-6bedd1c1c029`.
