# Rational splitting models of finite p-adic Galois fields

For every prime `p` and every field `M : Type u` of characteristic zero with
a supplied finite Galois algebra structure over `Padic p`, this internal
derived construction combines rational real/p-adic coefficient
approximation with the explicit Krasner neighborhood. In an algebraic closure
of the supplied finite Galois p-adic field, equipped with the spectral norm,
a primitive element has a nearby root of a rational monic polynomial with
simple real roots. Equality of the generated intermediate fields places this
root back in the supplied field and makes it primitive there. Its minimal
polynomial has the prescribed degree, so it equals the p-adic image of the
rational polynomial. Normality then gives splitting over the supplied field.

This is the local polynomial step motivated by Henniart (1984), Lemma 3.6,
printed pages 34–35 (PDF pages 35–36), source `henniart-1984-gl3`, SHA256
`968076c8b63d4a94442c080040b684fe1f71b01a3c0933d111a49745369629f7`.
The real splitting requirement supports the totally real assertion in
Henniart (2000), Section 3.3, printed page 449 (PDF page 11), source
`henniart-2000-simple-proof`, SHA256
`be283c3e8187f1eed365537c72379c3e06e7791756954c2ac783c6beea2979de`.
The spectral norm and the formal degree argument are internal implementation
choices. The final global primes and completion identifications remain open.

`rationalDefiningPolynomial` chooses this polynomial, and
`rationalGaloisModel` is its rational splitting field. This field lives in
`Type 0` independently of the universe of the supplied local field. It is a
number field because a polynomial splitting field is finite dimensional, and
it is Galois because the polynomial is separable. Real splitting supplies a
real embedding of this normal field; every complex embedding is obtained
from it by a rational automorphism. Consequently all infinite places are
real.

`rationalGaloisModelEmbedding` is the splitting-field lift into the supplied
local field. Its image contains every root of the chosen polynomial: the
rational algebra generated by those roots is exactly the lift's range.
In particular it contains the primitive root constructed above. Its image
therefore generates the entire local field after adjoining the p-adic scalars,
as stated by `rationalGaloisModelEmbedding_adjoin`. This does not yet produce
the normal closure of an arbitrary local extension or its global primes and
completion comparisons.
