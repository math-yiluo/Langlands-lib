## Arithmetic comparison

Take a coefficient number field containing the coefficient fields of the two compatible systems and the values of the rank-one avatar of `η`, using the supplied complex embeddings, and choose a finite place off the excluded sets. The base change of each coefficient number field induces a completion embedding at the places below it. The `λ`-adic avatar of `η` is trivial on the actual absolute Galois group of `D`. On good Frobenius elements this follows from the norm formula for a uniformizer, and Chebotarev extends it to the whole group. The induced cyclic quotient character is faithful. In prime degree it suffices that it be nontrivial, which follows from the norm kernel property and the fact that the good uniformizer values determine a Hecke character. That last fact follows by weak approximation at the finitely many other places. These arguments use the rank-one realization on `η` and the existing finite norm kernel.
