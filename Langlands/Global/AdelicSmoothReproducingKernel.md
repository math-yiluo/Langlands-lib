# A smooth compactly supported reproducing kernel on reductive adelic groups

Module `Langlands.Global.AdelicSmoothReproducingKernel` proves
`Langlands.Global.exists_adelicTestFunction_rightConvolution_eq`. For a connected reductive group
`G` over a number field `F`, a maximal compact subgroup `K` of `G(𝔸)` with archimedean part
`K_∞ = K.comap (ofInfinite F G)`, a closed matrix embedding `rho`, `hsmooth` and `hcalc`, every
function `φ : G(𝔸) → ℂ` with `IsAdelicSmoothFunction F G φ` that is `K_∞`-finite
(`IsInfiniteSubgroupFiniteFunction`) and `𝔷`-finite (`IsCenterFiniteFunction`) satisfies
`rightConvolution F G α φ = φ`, that is `φ(g) = ∫ α(a) φ(g a) da` for every `g`, for some `α`
with `IsAdelicTestFunction F G α` (smooth, compactly supported, bi-invariant under an open
subgroup of `G(𝔸_f)`).

## Proof

The argument adapts the archimedean construction and finite level normalization in
`Langlands.Global.AdelicParabolicReproducingKernelProof`. The archimedean construction works for
all functions on `G(𝔸)` with smooth infinite slices, `K_∞`-finiteness and `𝔷`-finiteness.

1. `infiniteHaar_isMulRightInvariant` uses right invariance of Haar on `G(𝔸)`
   (`adelicHaarUnimodularity`). By the product decomposition `AdelicGroupPoints.split`, the modular
   character of `G(F_∞)` is the restriction through `ofInfinite` of the trivial modular character
   of `G(𝔸)`.
2. For `exists_smoothInfiniteReproducer`, the archimedean part `K_∞` is a maximal compact subgroup
   (`IsMaximalCompactSubgroup.isMaximalCompact_comap_ofInfinite`).
   `exists_elliptic_functionInfiniteSlices` gives one positive-order elliptic word equation,
   with symbol nonvanishing on nonzero covectors, satisfied by every infinite slice of `φ`. The
   smooth solutions of this equation form a subspace `S` of the continuous functions on
   `G(F_∞)`, stable under left translations. `lineDerivativeBounds_of_wordEquation` bounds their
   derivatives along smooth one-parameter subgroups on compact sets, and
   `exists_smoothCompact_reproducer` (with the closed linear realization
   `isClosedLinearRealization_matrixRealization_of_smooth` given by `rho`) gives a smooth compactly
   supported `κ` on `G(F_∞)` with `u(1) = ∫ κ(x) u(x) dx` for every `u ∈ S`. Applied to the left
   translate by the archimedean component of `g` of the slice of `φ` at the finite component of
   `g`, this gives `φ(g) = ∫ φ(g x) κ(x) dx` for every `g ∈ G(𝔸)`.
3. In `exists_adelicTestFunction_of_infiniteReproducer`, `U` is the intersection of an open level
   of `φ` with the compact open subgroup `FiniteAdelicGroupPoints.compactOpenSubgroup`, and `χ` its
   indicator divided by its Haar measure. The function `κ ⊗ χ` on `G(F_∞) × G(𝔸_f)`, transported by
   `split` and multiplied by the scalar comparing the pushforward of product Haar measure with the
   Haar measure of `G(𝔸)`, is an adelic test function `α`, bi-invariant under `U`. Fubini and the
   right `U`-invariance of `φ` give `rightConvolution F G α φ = φ`.
4. `exists_adelicTestFunction_rightConvolution_eq` combines steps 2 and 3. Proof irrelevance
   identifies the given `hcalc : AdelicDifferentialCommutator F G hsmooth` in the `𝔷`-finiteness
   hypothesis with the commutator proof used in step 2.
