# Normalized integration in semidirect product coordinates

For a split discrete lattice, the product of fundamental domains in the normal group and its complement gives a fundamental domain in the full group. Finite covolumes in the two factors make conjugation preserve Haar in the normal group. Thus ordinary ambient Haar is a scalar multiple of the transported product Haar. Dividing by the indicated full lattice masses cancels that scalar, and the normalized Haar measures agree exactly under the product coordinates.

The same coordinates give an iterated integral for a smaller split discrete subgroup with the same lattice in the complement, using the same Haar measures. The actual chosen domain and the product domain yield equal integrals for lattice-invariant functions. Measure transport preserves integrability on the product domain and permits Fubini.
