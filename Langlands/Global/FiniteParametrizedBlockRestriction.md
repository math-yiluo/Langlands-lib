# Restriction of normalized finite adelic block sections

The insertion at a finite place maps its actual upper block parabolic into
the finite adelic parabolic. Its Levi projection inserts each of the diagonal
blocks at the same place. The full adelic determinant norm of an inserted
block is the normalized determinant norm at that place. The pairwise products
of these norms agree on the two Levis, so the positive half-moduli agree.

Suppose the restriction of the finite adelic Levi coefficient has separating
intertwiners to the local Levi coefficient. The equality of half-moduli lets
each map intertwine the inflated and twisted parabolic coefficients. Apply
local slices to the normalized finite adelic sections. Every argument can be
written as a local matrix times an argument whose finite component at the
selected place is the identity. The latter argument commutes with the local
matrix group. The slices therefore separate the sections and take values in
the canonical normalized induction, with its actual local topology.

Langlands relates the global induced representation to the local inductions
in Lemma 1 and obtains an induced realization using a modulus twist in
Proposition 2. See *On the notion of an automorphic representation*,
Corvallis Part I (1979), printed pp. 203 and 205 (PDF pp. 198 and 200),
source `langlands-1979-notion-automorphic-representation`. The comparison
here uses the Library's section covariance and positive block determinant
formulas to identify the twists at a single place.
