# Unramified strictly Henselian DVR extensions with separable residue extension

The theorem
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.unramifiedStrictHenselianDvrExtension.{u}` proves the
proposition
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.UnramifiedStrictHenselianDvrExtensionStatement.{u}`:
every commutative domain discrete valuation ring `R : Type u` has a local
homomorphism to a strictly Henselian discrete valuation ring `S : Type u` such that
the maximal ideal of `R` generates that of `S` and the residue extension is
separable algebraic.

## Implemented proof

The proof refines the maximal-subalgebra construction of
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.strictHenselianDvrExtension` (module
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.StrictHenselianDvrExtension`) by adding a residue
separability condition to the predicate to which Zorn's lemma is applied. Fix an
irreducible element `t` of `R` and let `Ω` be an algebraic closure of the fraction
field of `R`.

### The predicate

For a subalgebra `S` of `Ω` over `R`:

- `FixedUniformizer t S` (module `MaximalUniformizerSubalgebra`): the image of `t`
  in `S` is a nonunit and every element of `S` is zero or a power of that image times
  a unit;
- `SeparableResidues t S`: every `y : S` admits a monic `f : R[X]` whose reduction
  `f.map (residue R)` is separable and an element `z : S` with
  `aeval y f = t * z`;
- `SeparableFixedUniformizer t S` is the conjunction.

`SeparableFixedUniformizer.bot`: the bottom subalgebra satisfies the predicate; an
element is the image of some `r : R`, and `X - C r` serves. `iSup`: a nonempty
directed union satisfies it, since an element of the union and the witnesses of its
condition lie in one stage, and the inclusion of that stage is an algebra map.
`exists_maximal`: Zorn's lemma gives a maximal subalgebra `S` for the predicate. As in
the Library construction, `FixedUniformizer.isDiscreteValuationRing`,
`FixedUniformizer.isLocalHom` and `FixedUniformizer.irreducible` make `S` a domain DVR
with local base map in which the image of `t` is irreducible.

### Residue fields

`exists_monic_separable_of_isSeparable`: if `T` is a DVR over `R` with local base map,
the image of `t` is a uniformizer of `T`, and the residue field of `T` is separable
over that of `R`, then `SeparableResidues`-type witnesses exist for every `w : T`. Lift
the minimal polynomial of the residue of `w` (separable by hypothesis) to a monic
polynomial `f` over `R` (`lifts_and_degree_eq_and_monic`); the residue of
`aeval w f` is the value of the minimal polynomial, zero, so `aeval w f` lies in the
maximal ideal of `T`, which is generated by the image of `t`.

`SeparableFixedUniformizer.isSeparable_residueField` is the converse for a DVR
subalgebra: the residue of `y` is a root of the separable reduction of the polynomial
`f` attached to `y`, because the image of `t` lies in the maximal ideal; so its
minimal polynomial divides a separable polynomial and is separable.

### Maximality

`SeparableFixedUniformizer.algebraMap_surjective` follows
`FixedUniformizer.algebraMap_surjective`. Let `T` be a local, flat, essentially finite
type, formally unramified DVR algebra over the maximal `S`. The helper
`EtaleDvrExtensions.exists_injective_algHom` embeds `T` in `Ω` over `S`; let `U` be the
image. The fixed-uniformizer property of `U` is transferred from `T` exactly as in the
Library proof. For the residue condition: the residue field of `S` is separable over
that of `R` (previous paragraph), the residue field of `T` is separable over that of
`S` (Mathlib's instance for formally unramified essentially finite type local
homomorphisms, Stacks Tag 00UW), so by `Algebra.IsSeparable.trans` the residue field
of `T` is separable over that of `R`. Applying
`exists_monic_separable_of_isSeparable` to `T` and transporting through the
isomorphism `T ≃ U` gives `SeparableResidues t U`. Since `S ≤ U`, maximality forces
`U = S`, and injectivity of the embedding gives surjectivity of `S → T`.

`exists_root_of_simple`, `henselianLocalRing` and `isSepClosed_residueField` are then
the Library arguments with this surjectivity result in place of the old one: a
simple root of a monic polynomial at a field point with kernel the maximal ideal comes
from a local standard étale extension (`EtaleDvrExtensions.exists_root_extension`),
which is surjected onto by `S`; applied to the residue map this is Hensel's
condition, and applied to a point of an algebraic closure of the residue field it
gives roots of separable irreducible polynomials over the residue field.

### Conclusion

`unramifiedStrictHenselianDvrExtension` takes the maximal `S`. It is a domain DVR,
Henselian, with separably closed residue field and local base map. The maximal ideal
of `R` is `(t)` and that of `S` is generated by the image of `t`
(`Irreducible.maximalIdeal_eq`), so `Ideal.map_span` gives
`(maximalIdeal R).map (algebraMap R S) = maximalIdeal S`. The separability of the
residue extension is `isSeparable_residueField`.

## Provenance and scope

The proposition is the existential content of the strict henselization (Stacks Tags
04GP and 0AP3). The implemented proof does not construct the strict henselization as
a filtered colimit of étale algebras: it uses a maximal subalgebra of an algebraic
closure, as the Library's `strictHenselianDvrExtension` does, with residue
separability added to the maximality condition. It makes no claim that the
constructed ring has the universal property of the strict henselization or is
canonical.

`Langlands.AbelianScheme.injective_algebraMap_of_map_maximalIdeal` proves injectivity of a map of discrete valuation rings when the maximal ideal extends to the maximal ideal.
