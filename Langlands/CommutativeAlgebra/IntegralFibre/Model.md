# Noetherian models of a section with coordinates

This module is part of the proof of
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.IntegralFibreProjectivityStatement` (Stacks Project, Tag 05FT).
It constructs the Noetherian model required by `locallySplit_of_noetherianModel`.

## Definitions

`modelE eT R₁ : B ⊗[R₁] (R₁ ⊗[C] B₀) ≃ₐ[B] T`, for `eT : B ⊗[C] B₀ ≃ₐ[B] T` and a subalgebra
`R₁ ⊆ B` over `C`. It is the composition of `Algebra.TensorProduct.cancelBaseChange` with `eT`.

## Statements

- `modelMap_modelE_tmul`, `exists_modelMap_eq`: write `z` as `eT (∑ bₖ ⊗ wₖ)`. If all `bₖ` lie in
  `R₁`, then `z` is in the image of the model.
- `modelMap_modelE_injective`: if `R₁ ⊗[C] B₀` is flat over `R₁`, the model map is injective.
  This is because `R₁ → B` is injective.
- `sub_mem_mul_of`, `sub_mem_add_of`, `sub_mem_of_section`, `sub_mem_span_of_adjoin`: let
  `ρ : A ⊗[C] B₀ → A` be a ring map retracting `a ↦ a ⊗ 1`, and let `B₀` be generated over `C` by
  `v`. Then every `x - ρ x ⊗ 1` lies in the ideal generated by the elements
  `1 ⊗ v j - ρ (1 ⊗ v j) ⊗ 1`.
- `ker_le_of_lifts`, `ker_le_of_model`: kernel generators whose images satisfy relations of the
  form `∑ aᵢ tᵢ + ∑ m m'` give `ker σ₀ ≤ span (range t₀) ⊔ ker σ₀ * ker σ₀`. This holds once the
  coefficients lift to the model.
- `exists_noetherianModel`: let `T` be a finitely presented flat `B`-algebra with section `σ`, and
  let `t : Fin d → T` satisfy `ker σ ≤ span (range t) ⊔ (ker σ)^2`. Then there exist a Noetherian
  ring `R₀` with an injective algebra map to `B`, and a Noetherian flat `R₀`-algebra `S₀`. They
  come with an isomorphism `e : B ⊗[R₀] S₀ ≃ₐ[B] T`, a compatible section `σ₀`, and lifts `t₀` of
  `t`. Finally, `ker σ₀ ≤ span (range t₀) ⊔ ker σ₀ * ker σ₀`.

## Proof of `exists_noetherianModel`

Take the presentation `P := Algebra.Presentation.ofFinitePresentation B T`. Then
`B₀ := P.ModelOfHasCoeffs P.Core` is a finitely presented model over the finitely generated
`ℤ`-algebra `P.Core`, and `eT := P.tensorModelOfHasCoeffsEquiv P.Core`.

Collect a finite set `sB ⊆ B`. It contains the values `σ (P.val j)` and the `B`-coefficients of
the elements `tᵢ`. It also contains the coefficients of chosen witnesses `aⱼᵢ`, `mⱼₗ`, `m'ⱼₗ`
for `P.val j - σ (P.val j) = ∑ aⱼᵢ tᵢ + ∑ mⱼₗ m'ⱼₗ`, which exist by `hJ`.

The Library lemma `Module.Flat.exists_fg_subalgebra_flat_tensorProduct` then gives a finitely
generated `P.Core`-subalgebra `R₁ ⊇ sB` of `B` such that `S₀ := R₁ ⊗[P.Core] B₀` is flat over
`R₁`. Both rings are Noetherian, being of finite type over `ℤ` and over `R₁` respectively.

The section `σ₀` is built from `MvPolynomial.aeval` at the values `σ (P.val j) ∈ R₁`. The
relations hold because `R₁ ⊆ B`. All needed elements lift to `S₀`, and `modelMap` is injective,
so the relations among the lifts hold in `S₀`. The kernel of `σ₀` is generated by the elements
`1 ⊗ Xⱼ - σ₀ (1 ⊗ Xⱼ)`, which gives the conclusion.
