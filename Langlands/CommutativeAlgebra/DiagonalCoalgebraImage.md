# Homogeneous coefficients in a diagonal coalgebra image

This internal algebraic lemma supports the closed-immersion locus for
homomorphisms from split tori into affine group schemes. Its proof uses the
standard diagonal coalgebra structure on the monoid algebra over an arbitrary
commutative ring.

Contracting the first tensor factor of the comultiplication against the
coefficient in degree `m` gives the homogeneous summand in degree `m`. For a
bialgebra map `B → R[M]`, comultiplication compatibility expresses the same
contraction as an element of the image: on a pure tensor it is a scalar
multiple of the image of the second factor. Tensor induction completes the
argument. No flatness or field hypothesis is required.
