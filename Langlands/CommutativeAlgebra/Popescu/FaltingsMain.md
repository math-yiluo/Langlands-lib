# Faltings' argument: independence of differentials after a regular base change

This module assembles Faltings' proof of Stacks, More on Algebra, Proposition 15.36.1 (07E5),
(2) ⇒ (4), for a single finite family `a₁, …, a_n`. All declarations live in
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.Faltings`.

## `exists_pRoots`

Let `char k = p` and `a : Fin n → k`. There is a field `k'` (in the same universe) with
`β : Fin n → k'` such that:
- the `β_j` generate `k'` as a `k`-algebra;
- `β_j^p = a_j`;
- `k'/k` is finite and purely inseparable.

Construction:
1. Choose `p`-th roots `β_j` of the `a_j` in an algebraic closure of `k`
   (`IsAlgClosed.exists_pow_nat_eq`), and let `k' = k(β₁, …, β_n)`.
2. Generation follows from `adjoin_subtype_eq_top`.
3. Finiteness follows from `IntermediateField.finiteDimensional_adjoin`, since the `β_j` are
   integral.
4. Pure inseparability follows from `IntermediateField.isPurelyInseparable_adjoin_iff_pow_mem`,
   since `β_j^p ∈ k`.

## `residue_adjoin_eq_top`

Let `A → A'` be a local homomorphism of local rings and suppose `γ : Fin n → A'` generates `A'` as
an `A`-algebra (`aeval γ` is surjective). Then the residues of the `γ_j` generate `κ(A')` as a
`κ(A)`-algebra. Proof:
1. The image of `A'` in `κ(A')` is generated over `A` by the residues (`AlgHom.map_adjoin`), and
   the residue map is surjective.
2. An `A`-subalgebra generated by a set is contained in the `κ(A)`-subalgebra generated by it.

## `independent_of_regular`

Setting:
- `k` is a field of characteristic `p`;
- `A` is a local `k`-algebra;
- `hreg`: `A ⊗_k k'` is a regular local ring for every finite purely inseparable `k'/k`. This is
  condition (2) of 07E5. The source restricts to `k' ⊆ k^{1/p}`, but the extensions used here are
  of that form.

Statement: if `da₁, …, da_n` are linearly independent in `Ω_{k/ℤ}`, then the
`1 ⊗ d(a_j|_A)` are linearly independent over `κ = κ(A)` in `κ ⊗_A Ω_{A/ℤ}`.

Proof:
1. Choose `k'` and `β` with `exists_pRoots`. Put `A' = A ⊗_k k'`.
   - `A` itself is regular local, by `hreg k` and `A ⊗_k k ≅ A`.
   - `A'` is regular local, by `hreg k'`.
   - `A → A'` is integral (`isIntegral_tensor`) and injective, hence a local homomorphism
     (`Algebra.IsIntegral.isLocalHom`).
   - `A` and `κ` have characteristic `p`.
2. Presentation. `k[x] → k'`, `x_j ↦ β_j`, is surjective with kernel `rootIdeal p a`
   (`ker_aeval_eq_rootIdeal`). So `A[x] → A'`, `x_j ↦ 1 ⊗ β_j`, is surjective with kernel
   `rootIdeal p (a|_A)` (`aeval_one_tmul_surjective`, `ker_aeval_one_tmul`). This gives a
   presentation `Q` (`Generators.ofSurjective`) with `Q.ker = rootIdeal`.
3. Residue fields. `κ' = κ(A')` is generated over `κ` by the residues of the `1 ⊗ β_j`
   (`residue_adjoin_eq_top`), whose `p`-th powers lie in `κ` (`val_pow_eq`). Cartier's equality
   for `p`-root extensions (`rank_eq_of_pRoots`) gives `dim H₁(L_{κ'/κ}) = dim Ω_{κ'/κ} < ∞`.
4. Embedding dimensions. By `rank_h1Cotangent_residueField_eq_finrank`, `dim H₁(L_{κ/A})` and
   `dim H₁(L_{κ'/A'})` are the embedding dimensions. They are finite and equal to `dim A` and
   `dim A'`, since both rings are regular (`IsRegularLocalRing.iff_finrank_cotangentSpace`). Since
   `dim A' ≤ dim A` (`ringKrullDim_tensor_le`), `dim H₁(L_{κ'/A'}) ≤ dim H₁(L_{κ/A})`.
5. `faltings_core` concludes.

The source uses `dim A' = dim A`; only the inequality `dim A' ≤ dim A` is needed.
