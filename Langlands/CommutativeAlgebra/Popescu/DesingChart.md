# Local structure of a presentation at a smooth point

Auxiliary results for `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.Desing.strictlyStandardDesingularization`
(see `StrictlyStandardDesingularizationProof.md`). They live in namespace
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.Desing`, are internal to that proof, and are not claimed as
Library results. The main result plays the role of Stacks, *Smoothing Ring Maps*, Lemma 2.2
(Tag 07C6) in the proof of Lemma 6.1 (Tag 07CP).

## `exists_chart`

Let `φ : k[x_1..x_n] → C` be surjective with kernel `(f_1..f_m)`, and let `C` be smooth over `k`
at a prime `q` (`Algebra.IsSmoothAt k q`). Then there are `c`, an injective `e : Fin c → Fin m`,
an injective `u : Fin c → Fin n` and `a ∈ k[x]` such that
- `φ(a · det(∂f_{e j}/∂x_{u l})) ∉ q`, and
- `a f_ℓ ∈ (f_{e 1}, …, f_{e c})` in `k[x]` for every `ℓ`.

Proof. Let `P = k[x]_{q₀}` with `q₀ = φ⁻¹(q)`, let `S = C_q`, and let `κ` be the residue field of
`P`.
- The map `P → S` is surjective, and its kernel is generated by the images of the `f_j`.
  `Ω_{P/k}` is free and finite, because `k[x] → P` is formally étale
  (`KaehlerDifferential.tensorKaehlerEquivOfFormallyEtale`). `κ` is made an `S`-algebra by
  lifting the residue map along `P ↠ S`.
- Mathlib's Jacobian criterion `Algebra.FormallySmooth.iff_injective_cotangentComplexBaseChange`
  gives injectivity of `κ ⊗_P ker(P → S) → κ ⊗_P Ω_{P/k}`. Using `d f = Σ_i ∂_i f dx_i`, this
  means: if a combination of the gradients `v_j = (∂_i f_j mod q₀)_i ∈ κ^n` vanishes, then the
  same combination of the `1 ⊗ f_j` vanishes.
- `exists_basis_minor` (linear algebra over `κ`): choose `e` with `(v_{e j})` a basis of the span
  of all `v_j`. Then choose columns `u` with `det(v_{e j}(u l)) ≠ 0`, using the rank of the
  `c × n` matrix and a basis among its columns.
- For each `ℓ`, writing `v_ℓ` in terms of the `v_{e j}` gives
  `1 ⊗ f_ℓ = Σ_j c_j ⊗ f_{e j}`. By Nakayama over the local ring `P`
  (`span_comp_eq_top_of_tmul`, built on `IsLocalRing.map_tensorProduct_mk_eq_top`), the
  `f_{e j}` generate `ker(P → S)`. Clearing denominators gives `a_ℓ ∉ q₀` with
  `a_ℓ f_ℓ ∈ (f ∘ e)`. Then `a = Π_ℓ a_ℓ`.
- `det(∂f_{e j}/∂x_{u l})` has nonzero residue, so it is not in `q₀`.
