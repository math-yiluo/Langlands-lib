# A regular system of parameters mapping to a regular sequence gives flatness

`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.RegularSequenceFlatStatement` (universe `u`). Let
`R`, `S : Type u` be Noetherian local rings and `R → S` a local homomorphism
(`[IsLocalHom (algebraMap R S)]`). Let `x : List R` be such that

- `Ideal.ofList x` is the maximal ideal of `R`;
- `x` is a weakly regular sequence on `R` (`RingTheory.Sequence.IsWeaklyRegular R x`);
- the image `x.map (algebraMap R S)` is a weakly regular sequence on `S`.

Then `S` is flat over `R` (`Module.Flat R S`).

## Source

Stacks Project, Chapter 10 *Commutative Algebra* (`stacks-commutative-algebra-chapter-ed88ff78`,
full chapter PDF, version ed88ff78), Lemma 10.128.2 (Tag 07DY, printed p. 319): "Let
R → S be a homomorphism of Noetherian local rings. Assume that R is a regular local
ring and that a regular system of parameters maps to a regular sequence in S. Then
R → S is flat." The smoothing chapter cites it in the proof of Lemma 10.3 (Tag 07FE)
as "Algebra, Lemma 128.2".

## Encoding

- "Regular system of parameters of a regular local ring" is encoded as a weakly regular
  sequence of `R` generating the maximal ideal. These agree. A regular system of
  parameters of a regular local ring generates the maximal ideal and is a regular
  sequence (Stacks Lemma 10.106.3, Tag 00NQ). Conversely, if the maximal ideal is
  generated by a regular sequence of length `d`, then `d ≤ depth R ≤ dim R`, while the
  maximal ideal needs at least `dim R` generators. So `R` is regular and the sequence is
  a regular system of parameters.
- "Maps to a regular sequence in S": the images lie in the maximal ideal of `S`, since
  the homomorphism is local, so the quotient by them is nonzero. The statement asks only
  for weak regularity of the images.
- The source's homomorphism of local rings is required to be local, as in its proof by
  the local criterion of flatness.
- All rings are in one universe `u`.
