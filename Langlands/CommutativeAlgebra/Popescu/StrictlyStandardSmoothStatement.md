# Inverting a strictly standard element gives a smooth algebra

`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.StrictlyStandardSmoothStatement` (universe `u`): for
commutative rings `R`, `A : Type u` with `A` an `R`-algebra and `a : A`, if
`IsStrictlyStandard R a`, then `Localization.Away a` is smooth over `R`
(`Algebra.Smooth`).

## Source

Stacks Project, *Smoothing Ring Maps* (`smoothing.pdf` in
`stacks-artin-axioms-approximation-2026-11-06`), Lemma 3.7 (Tag 07EZ, printed pp. 8-9).
The lemma lists conditions on `a ∈ A` for `R → A` of finite presentation, among them
"(2) `A_a` is smooth over `R` and `Ω_{A_a/R}` is stably free" and "(5) `a` is strictly
standard in `A` over `R`", and states "(d) (5) ⇒ (2)". The statement here keeps the
part "`A_a` is smooth over `R`" of condition (2). The proof of (d) in the source uses
Lemma 2.5 (Tag 07CA) for smoothness and Lemma 2.4 (Tag 07ET) for the conormal module.

## Encoding

- "Strictly standard" is `IsStrictlyStandard` (Definition 2.3, Tag 07C7, conditions
  (2.3.3) and (2.3.4)); see `Langlands/CommutativeAlgebra/Popescu/Basic.md`.
- The source assumes `R → A` of finite presentation. This is not a separate hypothesis
  here: `IsStrictlyStandard R a` provides a surjection `R[x₁, …, xₙ] → A` whose kernel is
  generated by finitely many polynomials, so `A` is finitely presented over `R`.
- `A_a` is `Localization.Away a`.
- All rings are in one universe `u`.
