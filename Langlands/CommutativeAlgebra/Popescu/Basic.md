# Basic notions for Popescu's theorem

Module `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.Basic` collects the definitions used by
the statements that decompose the proof of Popescu's theorem (Stacks Project,
chapter *Smoothing Ring Maps*, `smoothing.pdf` in the source folder
`stacks-artin-axioms-approximation-2026-11-06`; Theorem 12.1, Tag 07GC). Numbering
below is that of the chapter PDF (Lemma 8.4 of the PDF is Lemma 16.8.4 of the book).
All declarations are in the namespace `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu`.

## `HasSmoothFactorizations R Λ`

Source: Situation 8.1 and the definition of property PT (Tag 07F2, printed p. 20):
"We say PT holds for R → Λ if Λ is a filtered colimit of smooth R-algebras."

The Lean definition is the *mapping form* of PT: for every finitely presented
`R`-algebra `A` (in the universe of `R` and `Λ`) and every `R`-algebra map
`f : A →ₐ[R] Λ`, there are a smooth `R`-algebra `B` (same universe) and
`R`-algebra maps `g : A →ₐ[R] B`, `h : B →ₐ[R] Λ` with `h.comp g = f`.

Provenance (derived form): the chapter always replaces PT by this mapping property,
citing Stacks, Algebra, Lemma 10.127.4: "Let A → Λ be an R-algebra map with A of
finite presentation over R. According to Algebra, Lemma 127.4 we have to factor this
map through a standard smooth algebra" (proof of Lemma 3.5, printed p. 8), and "We
have to find a factorization A → B → Λ with B smooth over R, see Algebra, Lemma
127.4" (proofs of Proposition 5.3, p. 15, and Lemma 8.4, p. 21). The colimit form is
not formalized here; all statements of this development use the mapping form, which
is also the form of the target `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.PopescuFactorizationStatement`.
The definition fixes all of `R`, `Λ`, `A`, `B` in one universe `u`.

## `singularIdeal R A`

Source: Definition 2.1 (Tag 07C5, printed p. 2): the singular ideal `H_{A/R}` is the
unique radical ideal of `A` with `V(H_{A/R}) = {q ∈ Spec A | R → A not smooth at q}`.

Lean: `PrimeSpectrum.vanishingIdeal (Algebra.smoothLocus R A)ᶜ`, the ideal of
elements of `A` lying in every prime at which `A` is not smooth over `R`. For a
closed set `Z` the vanishing ideal is the unique radical ideal with zero locus `Z`,
and for finitely presented `A` Mathlib's `Algebra.smoothLocus` is open
(`Algebra.isOpen_smoothLocus`) and agrees with the Stacks smooth locus (a prime `p`
is in it iff some `D(g) ∋ p` has `A_g` smooth over `R`, by
`Algebra.basicOpen_subset_smoothLocus_iff_smooth`). Mathlib's `smoothLocus` consists
of the primes `p` with `A_p` formally smooth over `R`. The definition is stated for
any `R`-algebra; every use in this development has `A` finitely presented, where it
is exactly the Stacks ideal.

## `singularRadical R f`

Source: Situation 9.1 (Tag 07F7, printed p. 22): for an `R`-algebra map `A → Λ`,
"we denote h_A = √(H_{A/R} Λ)".

Lean: for `f : A →ₐ[R] Λ`, `((singularIdeal R A).map f).radical`, the radical of
the ideal of `Λ` generated by the image of `H_{A/R}`.

## `IsStrictlyStandard R a`

Source: Definition 2.3 (Tag 07C7, printed p. 3), conditions (2.3.3) and (2.3.4):
`a ∈ A` is strictly standard in `A` over `R` if there are a presentation
`A = R[x_1, …, x_n]/(f_1, …, f_m)` and `0 ≤ c ≤ min(n, m)` such that
`a = Σ_{I ⊂ {1..n}, |I| = c} a_I det(∂f_j/∂x_i)_{j = 1..c, i ∈ I}` for some `a_I ∈ A`, and
`a f_{c+j} ∈ (f_1, …, f_c) + (f_1, …, f_m)²` for `j = 1, …, m − c`.

Lean encoding:

- The presentation is a surjective `R`-algebra map
  `φ : MvPolynomial (Fin n) R →ₐ[R] A` with `RingHom.ker φ = Ideal.span (Set.range f)`
  for `f : Fin m → MvPolynomial (Fin n) R`; the bounds are `c ≤ n` and `c ≤ m`.
- Condition (2.3.3) is membership of `a` in the ideal of `A` generated by the images
  under `φ` of the minors `det (fun j k : Fin c => pderiv (e k) (f (Fin.castLE hcm j)))`,
  where `e : Fin c ↪o Fin n` runs over order embeddings, i.e. over the `c`-element
  subsets `I` of the variables listed increasingly. "For some `a_I ∈ A`" is exactly
  membership in this ideal. Rows are the first `c` equations (`Fin.castLE hcm j`),
  columns the variables in `I`; the transpose has the same determinant.
- Condition (2.3.4) is read, as usual, with a lift: there is `g` with `φ g = a` such
  that `g * f j ∈ Ideal.span (f '' {i | i < c}) ⊔ Ideal.span (Set.range f) ^ 2` for
  every index `j ≥ c` (zero-based indices; these are `f_{c+1}, …, f_m`). Two lifts of
  `a` differ by an element of `(f_1, …, f_m)`, whose product with `f_j` lies in
  `(f_1, …, f_m)²`, so the condition does not depend on the lift.

## `CanBeResolved R f q`

Source: text following Situation 9.1 (Tag 07F7, printed p. 22): "We say
R → A → Λ ⊃ q can be resolved if there exists a factorization A → B → Λ with B of
finite presentation and h_A ⊂ h_B ⊄ q."

Lean: there are an `R`-algebra `B` (in the universe of `A`) finitely presented over
`R` and `R`-algebra maps `g : A →ₐ[R] B`, `h : B →ₐ[R] Λ` with `h.comp g = f`,
`singularRadical R f ≤ singularRadical R h` and
`¬ singularRadical R h ≤ q`. Situation 9.1 also assumes `R` Noetherian, `A` finitely
presented and `q` prime; the definition itself does not need these, and the statements
using it impose them.
