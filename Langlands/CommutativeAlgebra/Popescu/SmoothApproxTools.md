# Tools for the assembly of Stacks Lemma 16.11.2 (07FH)

Auxiliary lemmas, namespace `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.SmoothApprox`, used by
`SmoothApproxFinal`. All proofs are complete and use only the standard axioms.

## Flat local maps

- `injective_of_flat_of_isLocalHom`: a flat local homomorphism of local rings is injective.
  It is faithfully flat (`Module.FaithfullyFlat.of_flat_of_isLocalHom`).
- `comap_map_of_flat_of_isLocalHom`: along such a map, `(I·E) ∩ D = I` for every ideal `I`
  (`Ideal.comap_map_eq_self_of_faithfullyFlat`).
- `flat_quotient_map`: if `B` is flat over `A`, then `B/IB` is flat over `A/I`
  (`Algebra.TensorProduct.quotIdealMapEquivQuotTensor` and base change).
- `exists_flat_truncation_map`: a flat `k[y]_𝔭 → Λ_q` with `𝔭Λ_q = qΛ_q` induces a flat map
  `k[y]_𝔭/𝔪ⁿ → Λ_q/qⁿΛ_q` sending `x mod 𝔪ⁿ` to the image of `x`. Here `𝔪ⁿ Λ_q = qⁿΛ_q`, so this
  is `flat_quotient_map` followed by `Ideal.quotEquivOfEq`.

The faithfully flat descent step in `SmoothApproxFinal` reuses
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.flat_of_flat_of_faithfullyFlat` from
`EtaleFlatLocalDescentProof`: for `P → D → L` with `L` faithfully flat over `D`
and flat over `P`, `D` is flat over `P` (Stacks 0584).

## Differentials

- `kaehler_basis_of_algEquiv`: a family whose differentials form a basis of `Ω_{F₁/k}` is carried by
  a `k`-isomorphism `e : F₁ ≅ F₂` of fields to a family with the same property.
  - Independence: compose with `KaehlerDifferential.map` for `e⁻¹` and restrict scalars along
    `e⁻¹`.
  - Spanning: the image of `span_{F₁}(dx_i)` under `KaehlerDifferential.map` for `e` lies in
    `span_{F₂}(d(e x_i))`.
- `basis_smul`: multiplying the members of a basis family by nonzero scalars gives a basis family.
- `exists_kaehler_basis`: for a local ring `A` essentially of finite type over a field `k`, there are
  `b₁, …, b_t ∈ A` whose residue differentials form a basis of `Ω_{κ_A/k}`. Since `Ω_{κ_A/k}` is
  finite (`KaehlerDifferential.finite`), extract a linearly independent spanning subfamily
  (`exists_linearIndependent'`) of the spanning family `a ↦ d(ā)`.
- `h1_finite_of_algEquiv`: finiteness of `H₁` of the naive cotangent complex
  (`Algebra.H1Cotangent`) transfers along a `k`-isomorphism of fields. The map
  `H1Cotangent.map` is surjective because it underlies `H1Cotangent.mapEquiv`.

## Truncations `Λ_q/qⁿΛ_q`

- `isLocalRing_localTruncation` (`n > 0`).
- `comap_maximalIdeal_localTruncation` and `map_maximalIdeal_localTruncation`: the maximal ideal
  of `Λ_q/qⁿΛ_q` is the image of that of `Λ_q`.
- `isLocalHom_mk_localTruncation`.
- `isArtinianRing_localTruncation`: the maximal ideal is nilpotent
  (`isArtinianRing_iff_isNilpotent_maximalIdeal`).
- `exists_residueField_equiv`: the residue field of `Λ_q/qⁿΛ_q` is `κ(q)`, compatibly with
  residue maps.

## Generators

- `exists_select_maximalIdeal`: if the maximal ideal of a Noetherian local ring is generated by
  `f₁, …, f_d`, some `spanFinrank`-element subfamily generates it. The images of the `f_i` span the
  cotangent space `𝔪/𝔪²`. A basis subfamily has `finrank (𝔪/𝔪²) = spanFinrank 𝔪` elements
  (`spanFinrank_maximalIdeal_eq_finrank_cotangentSpace`), and it generates `𝔪` by Nakayama
  (`CotangentSpace.span_image_eq_top_iff`).
- `span_mk_transport` and `spanFinrank_transport`: transport of these facts along an equality of
  ideals `I = J`. The `IsLocalRing` instances on `R/I` and `R/J` are propositions.
