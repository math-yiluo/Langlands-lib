# The gluing algebra of Stacks Lemma 9.4

This module proves the gluing step of Stacks Project, *Smoothing ring maps*,
Lemma 9.4 (tag 07FA). The main theorem is `canBeResolved_of_dimZero`. Lemma 9.3
(tag 07F9) supplies its hypothesis; see `LocalDimZeroResolutionProof`.

## Singular ideal and smooth localizations

Let `B` be a finitely presented `R`-algebra.

- `DimZeroResolutionProof.mem_singularIdeal_of_smooth`: if `B_b` is smooth over `R`, then
  `b ∈ singularIdeal R B`.
- `DimZeroResolutionProof.smooth_away_of_mem_singularIdeal`: conversely, every `b ∈ singularIdeal R B`
  has `B_b` smooth over `R`.

Both helpers are imported from `DimZeroResolutionProof`. They are read off from `Algebra.basicOpen_subset_smoothLocus_iff_smooth`, since
`singularIdeal R B` is the vanishing ideal of the complement of the smooth locus
(module `Basic`).

## The gluing algebra (namespace `DimZeroGlue`)

Fix a commutative ring `A`, natural numbers `n, m, r` and polynomials
`F : Fin m → A[x_1, …, x_n]`. The variables are `V n m r`, a sum of four types:
`x_i` (`i < n`), `y_k` (`k < r`), a single variable `z`, and `t_kj` (`k < r`,
`j < m`). The relations `rels r F` are

- `F_j(x) − Σ_k y_k t_kj` for each `j`;
- `z · y_k` for each `k`.

`Alg r F` is the quotient `A[x, y, z, t]/(rels)`, with quotient map `mk`. It is an
`A`-algebra, and an `R`-algebra for every `R`-algebra structure on `A`, with the
scalar tower `R → A → Alg r F`. `rel1` and `rel2` state the two relations in the
quotient. This is the algebra `B` from the proof of Stacks Lemma 9.4, in which
`y_k` stands for `a_k^N` and `z` for `λ`.

`isUnit_of_isUnit_mk` is a small auxiliary fact. If `J² = 0` and the image of `x`
in `T/J` is a unit, then `x` is a unit. If `x·w ≡ 1` modulo `J`, then
`w·(2 − x·w)` is an inverse.

### Smoothness after inverting `a · y_l`

`formallySmooth_away_y`: if `A_a` is formally smooth over `R`, then so is
`(Alg r F)_{a·y_l}`. The proof checks the infinitesimal lifting property with
`Algebra.FormallySmooth.of_comp_surjective`. Let `φ` be a map into `T/J` with
`J² = 0`.

1. Restrict `φ` to `A_a` and lift it to `α : A_a → T`, using the smoothness of
   `A_a`.
2. Choose arbitrary lifts `e_w ∈ T` of the images of all variables `w`. The lift
   of `y_l` is a unit (`isUnit_of_isUnit_mk`); call its inverse `v`.
3. Send `x_i, y_k` to their chosen lifts and `z` to `0`. Send `t_kj` to its lift
   plus the correction `v · ε_j` when `k = l`, where
   `ε_j = F_j(x) − Σ_k y_k t_kj` is evaluated at the chosen lifts. After the
   correction, every relation `F_j − Σ y_k t_kj` vanishes in `T`, and the
   relations `z · y_k` vanish because `z ↦ 0`.
4. The map factors through `Alg r F`. It sends `a · y_l` to a unit, so it extends
   to `(Alg r F)_{a·y_l}`.
5. It lifts `φ`:
   - `z` maps to zero in the localization, because `y_l` is a unit there and
     `z · y_l = 0`;
   - each `ε_j` lies in `J`, because the relation holds modulo `J`.

   Agreement is checked on generators (`MvPolynomial.ringHom_ext`,
   `IsLocalization.ringHom_ext`).

### Smoothness after inverting `z · c`

`formallySmooth_away_z`: assume that `C` is an `A`-algebra with a surjection
`φC : A[x] → C` whose kernel is generated by the `F_j`. If `C_{φC(c)}` is
formally smooth over `R`, then so is `(Alg r F)_{z·c(x)}`.

Inverting `z` kills every `y_k` (`rel2`), so the relations reduce to `F_j(x) = 0`.
The proof:

1. Construct the map `C_{φC(c)} → (Alg r F)_{z·c}` (`AlgHom.liftOfSurjective`,
   then `IsLocalization.Away.lift`).
2. Given a square-zero lifting problem, lift through `C_{φC(c)}` by smoothness.
3. Send `x_i ↦ α(φC(x_i))`, `y_k ↦ 0`, and send `z` and `t_kj` to chosen lifts.
   The lift of `z` is a unit.
4. Check the relations and the agreement with `φ` on generators as in the
   previous lemma.

## Stacks Lemma 9.4

`canBeResolved_of_dimZero` has the following hypotheses.

- `R` and `Λ` are Noetherian, `A` is finitely presented over `R`, and
  `f : A → Λ` is an `R`-algebra map.
- `q` is a minimal prime of `Λ` with `singularRadical R f ≤ q`.
- `f` factors as `A → C → Λ` (via `gC` and `hC`), where `C` is finitely
  presented over `R` and `H_{C/R} Λ ⊄ q`.

Its conclusion is `CanBeResolved R f q`. The proof follows Stacks 9.4.

1. Choose generators `a_1, …, a_r` of `H_{A/R}` (`A` is Noetherian).
2. Since `q` is minimal, the maximal ideal of `Λ_q` equals its nilradical.
   `Λ_q` is Noetherian, so the maximal ideal is nilpotent, say of exponent `N`.
   Each `f(a_k)` lies in `q`, so `f(a_k)^N` vanishes in `Λ_q`. Hence there is
   `λ ∉ q`, a product of witnesses, with `λ · f(a_k)^N = 0` for all `k`.
3. Present `C` over `A` (via `gC`) as `A[x]/(F_1, …, F_m)`. Let
   `B = Alg r F`. It is finitely presented over `A`, hence over `R`.
4. Define `B → Λ` by sending
   - `x_i` to the image of `x_i` in `Λ`;
   - `y_k` to `f(a_k)^N`;
   - `z` to `λ`;
   - `t_kj` to `0`.

   The relations hold: `F_j(x)` maps to `hC(φ(F_j)) = 0`, and
   `λ · f(a_k)^N = 0`. The composite `A → B → Λ` is `f`.
5. `h_A ≤ h_B`: by `formallySmooth_away_y`, each `a_k · y_k` lies in `H_{B/R}`. Its
   image `f(a_k)^{N+1}` lies in `H_{B/R} Λ`, so `f(a_k)` lies in `h_B`.
6. `h_B ⊄ q`: suppose `H_{B/R} Λ ⊆ q`. Take `c ∈ H_{C/R}` and write `c = φ(c₀)`.
   By `formallySmooth_away_z`, `z · c₀(x)` lies in `H_{B/R}`. Its image is
   `λ · hC(c)`, which lies in `q`. Since `λ ∉ q`, `hC(c) ∈ q`. This holds for
   every `c`, so `H_{C/R} Λ ⊆ q`, a contradiction.

The resolution required by `CanBeResolved` is `A → B → Λ`.
