# Lifting resolutions along a sequence (Stacks Lemma 9.2)

This module supports the proof of Stacks Project, *Smoothing Ring Maps*, Lemma 10.3
(Tag 07FE). Its main result is `canBeResolved_of_sequence`, Lemma 9.2 (Tag 07F8). The
other declarations are helper lemmas.

## Singular ideals

- `singularIdeal_eq_of_bijective`: the singular ideal does not change when the base ring
  is replaced by an isomorphic one. A bijection `R₁ → R₂` is formally étale, so
  `Algebra.FormallySmooth.iff_restrictScalars` identifies the two smooth loci.
- The imported `DimZeroResolutionProof.mem_singularIdeal_of_smooth` and
  `DimZeroResolutionProof.smooth_away_of_mem_singularIdeal` give, for a finitely
  presented algebra, `b ∈ H_{B/R}` if and only if `B_b` is smooth. These existing
  helpers follow from `Algebra.basicOpen_subset_smoothLocus_iff_smooth`.
- `includeRight_mem_singularIdeal`: if `a ∈ H_{A/R}`, then `1 ⊗ a ∈ H_{S ⊗ A / S}`.
  Indeed `(S ⊗ A)_{1 ⊗ a} ≅ S ⊗ A_a` (`IsLocalization.Away.tensorProductEquivTMulRight`),
  and smoothness is stable under base change.

## Strictly standard elements

- `IsStrictlyStandard.mul_right` and `IsStrictlyStandard.pow`: multiples and positive
  powers of strictly standard elements are strictly standard. Use the same presentation
  and multiply the lift by a lift of the factor.
- `IsStrictlyStandard.baseChange`: base change preserves strictly standard elements (the
  base-change part of Stacks Lemma 2.7, Tag 07CC). From a presentation
  `φ : R[x] → A` with kernel `(f)`, one gets the presentation
  `S[x] ≅ S ⊗ R[x] → S ⊗ A` of `S ⊗ A`. Its kernel is generated by the images of the
  `f_j` (`Algebra.TensorProduct.lTensor_ker`). Partial derivatives and determinants
  commute with `MvPolynomial.map`, so both conditions of the definition carry over.

## One step: `canBeResolved_of_quot`

This is the case `r = 1` of Lemma 9.2, from the published statement
`StrictlyStandardDesingularizationStatement` (Lemma 7.3, Tag 07F0).

**Setting.**
- `quotMap f π : (R/π⁸) ⊗ A → Λ/π⁸Λ` is the reduction of `f`.
- `π` satisfies `Ann(π) = Ann(π²)` in `R` and in `Λ`.
- `π` is strictly standard in `A`, and `π ↦ q`.

**Proof.** Let `D` resolve the reduced problem. Apply Lemma 7.3 to `D`. This gives
`A → B → Λ` with `B_π` smooth over `R` and `H_D Λ̄ ⊆ h_B mod π⁸`.

- **`π ∈ h_B`.** The element `π` lies in `H_{B/R}`, so `π⁸Λ ⊆ h_B`. Hence `h_B` is the
  preimage of its image in `Λ/π⁸Λ`.
- **`h_A ⊆ h_B`.** For `a ∈ H_{A/R}`, the element `1 ⊗ a` lies in the reduced singular
  ideal. So some power of `f(a) mod π⁸` lies in `H_D Λ̄ ⊆ h_B mod π⁸`, and `f(a) ∈ h_B`
  because `h_B` is radical.
- **`h_B ⊄ q`.** If `h_B ⊆ q`, then `H_D Λ̄ ⊆ q Λ̄`. The ideal `qΛ̄` is prime, so this
  contradicts `h_D ⊄ qΛ̄`.

## Lemma 9.2: `canBeResolved_of_sequence`

The proof is by induction on the length `r` of `π : Fin r → R`.

**Hypotheses.**
- each `πᵢ` maps into `q`;
- the annihilator conditions `πᵢ² x ∈ (π_j⁸ : j < i) → πᵢ x ∈ (π_j⁸ : j < i)` hold in `R`
  and in `Λ`;
- each `πᵢ` is strictly standard in `A`;
- the bottom condition `BottomCondition R Λ q (π_i⁸)` holds.

**Conclusion.** `R → A → Λ ⊃ q` can be resolved.

**`BottomCondition`.** `BottomCondition R Λ q K` says the following. Take any
surjections `σ : R → R₁` and `τ : Λ → Λ₁` with kernels `K` and `KΛ` that are compatible
with the algebra maps, and any prime `Q` of `Λ₁` over `q`. Then `Q` is a minimal prime
and property PT holds for `R₁ → (Λ₁)_Q`. Quantifying over all such presentations lets
the condition pass to the quotient by `π₀⁸` by composing surjections. No ring
isomorphisms are needed.

**Base case `r = 0`.** Take `σ = id` and `τ = id`. Then `q` is a minimal prime of `Λ` and
PT holds for `R → Λ_q`. Apply the published `DimZeroResolutionStatement` (Lemmas 9.3
and 9.4). If `h_A ⊄ q`, the identity of `A` already resolves the problem.

**Inductive step.** Apply `canBeResolved_of_quot` to `π₀`. The required resolution of the
reduced problem comes from the induction hypothesis for `R/π₀⁸`, `(R/π₀⁸) ⊗ A`,
`Λ/π₀⁸Λ` and the images of `π₁, …, π_{r-1}`.
- The conditions transfer by `comap mk (map mk I) = I ⊔ K` (`comap_mk_map_mk`), with
  `span_image_lt_succ` and `span_range_succ`.
- Strict standardness transfers by `IsStrictlyStandard.baseChange`.
