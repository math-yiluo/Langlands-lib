# The presentation `k' = k[x₁, …, x_n]/(x_j^p − a_j)`

This module formalizes the step in Faltings' proof of Stacks, More on Algebra, Proposition 15.36.1
(07E5), (2) ⇒ (4), that identifies `k' = k(a₁^{1/p}, …, a_n^{1/p})` with
`k[x]/(x_j^p − a_j)` when the `da_j` are independent. The source cites Stacks, Algebra, Lemma
10.158.3 for this. All declarations live in `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.Faltings`.

## Definitions

- `rootIdeal p a`: for `a : Fin n → R`, the ideal of `R[x₁, …, x_n]` generated by the
  `x_j^p − a_j`.
- `redMonomial r`: for `r : Fin n → Fin p`, the reduced monomial `∏ x_j^{r_j}` with all exponents
  `< p`.

## Spanning lemmas

- `quot_C_mul`: in a quotient of `R[x]`, the class of `C c * g` is `c •` the class of `g`.
- `redMonomial_mul_X`: in `R[x]/rootIdeal`, a reduced monomial times `x_j` lies in the `R`-span of
  the reduced monomials. If `r_j + 1 < p` the product is again reduced. Otherwise
  `x_j^{r_j+1} = x_j^p ≡ a_j`, and the product is `a_j` times a reduced monomial.
- `span_redMonomial`: the classes of the reduced monomials span `R[x]/rootIdeal` over `R`. The
  proof is by induction on polynomials (`MvPolynomial.induction_on`): constants, sums, and
  multiplication by `x_j`.
- `finrank_rootQuot_le`: over a field `k`, `k[x]/rootIdeal` is finite-dimensional of dimension at
  most `p^n`, the number of reduced monomials.

## `ker_aeval_eq_rootIdeal`

Let `char k = p` and let `da₁, …, da_n` be linearly independent in `Ω_{k/ℤ}`. Suppose a field `L`
is generated by `β₁, …, β_n` with `β_j^p = a_j`. Then the kernel of `k[x] → L`, `x_j ↦ β_j`, is
exactly `rootIdeal p a`.

Proof:
1. `rootIdeal` is contained in the kernel, so `k[x] → L` factors through `ψ : k[x]/rootIdeal → L`.
2. `ψ` is surjective because the `β_j` generate `L`.
3. The source of `ψ` has dimension at most `p^n` (`finrank_rootQuot_le`). The target has dimension
   exactly `p^n` (`finrank_pRoots`). By rank–nullity, `ψ` is injective.
4. Hence the kernel is exactly `rootIdeal`.
