# Proof of the unit enlargement of flat Artinian approximations

`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.Enlargement.artinianUnitEnlargement` has exactly the type
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.ArtinianUnitEnlargementStatement.{u}` and imports the
unchanged statement module `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.ArtinianUnitEnlargementStatement`.
It has no further hypotheses and uses only the standard axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`,
`Quot.sound`).

## Implemented proof

The `k`-algebra map `ψ : D → L` makes `L` a `D`-algebra, with `IsScalarTower k D L`. The map is
local and flat, and `IsScalarTower.toAlgHom k D L = ψ` (by `rfl` on elements). The proof splits
according to whether the residue `α ∈ κ_L` of the unit `ℓ` is algebraic over `κ_D`. Both cases
return the conclusion for `IsScalarTower.toAlgHom k D L`, which is then rewritten to `ψ`.

- `α` algebraic: `caseA` (`EnlargementAlgebraic`). A `p`-power `β = α^{p^n}` is separable over
  `κ_D`. `D' = D[X]/(H)`, with `H` a monic lift of the minimal polynomial of `β`, is finite free
  over `D`, local with `𝔪_{D'} = 𝔪_D D'`, and its residue field is generated by a root of that
  separable polynomial, so it is finite separable over `κ_D`. It maps to `L` by a Hensel root of
  `H` lifting `β`. Here `r = 0`.
- `α` transcendental: `caseB` (`EnlargementTranscendental`). `D' = D[X]_𝔐`, with
  `𝔐 = 𝔪_D D[X]` the preimage of `𝔪_L` under `X ↦ ℓ`. Its residue field is the rational function
  field `κ_D(T)`, `T` the residue of `X`, and `v = X` maps to `ℓ`. Here `r = 1`.

In both cases `u` is a `p`-th power, so `dū = 0`, and flatness of `L` over `D'` comes from
`flat_of_map_maximalIdeal` (`EnlargementTools`), which uses `𝔪_D D' = 𝔪_{D'}` and the Library's
local flatness criterion.

## Relation to the source

This follows the case distinction in the proof of Stacks Project, Smoothing Ring Maps, Lemma
16.11.3 (Tag 07FI) (source `stacks-artin-axioms-approximation-2026-11-06`, smoothing.pdf, printed
pp. 27–28). Stacks' case "`α ∈ F`" is covered here by the algebraic case: then `β ∈ κ_D`, its minimal
polynomial is linear, and `D' = D[X]/(X - c) ≅ D` for a lift `c` of `β`. In Stacks' algebraic case the
étale extension is mapped into the henselian ring `Λ_q/qⁿΛ_q`; here the Artinian local ring `L` is
itself complete, hence henselian.

Supporting modules: `EnlargementTools` (flatness transfer, Frobenius powers, completeness of
Artinian local rings, differentials under formally étale extensions) and `EnlargementKaehler`
(differentials of a simple purely transcendental extension `F(T)/F`).
