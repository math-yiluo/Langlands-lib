# The new relations of the symmetric-algebra retract

This module defines the relations `H_1, …, H_m` that later witness strictly standard elements in
`C = R[x, y]/J` (module `SymRetractAlgebra`). It also proves the polynomial identities behind
them. All declarations are in namespace `SymRetract`.

Throughout, `D = (∂f_k/∂x_i)` is the Jacobian matrix of the `f_k`, `P` is an `n × m` polynomial
matrix, and `u` is a polynomial. Lemma `exists_conormal_section` (module `ConormalSection`)
produces these data with `u = h^t`.

## Over `R[x]`

- `Et F P` is the matrix `E = D P`, with entries `E_kl = Σ_i ∂f_k/∂x_i · P_il`.
- The hypothesis used throughout is `hE1`: `u f_k − Σ_l E_kl f_l ∈ I²` for every `k`.
- `Et_mul_D_sub`: from `hE1`, `E D ≡ u D` modulo `I`. Differentiate the relation in `hE1`
  (`pderiv_mem_of_mem_sq`) and drop the terms that are multiples of the `f`'s.
- `Et_sq_sub`: `E² ≡ u E` modulo `I`, because `E² = E D P ≡ u D P = u E`.

The proof chooses polynomials `Θ_kjp` with `E_kj u − Σ_l E_kl E_lj = Σ_p Θ_kjp f_p`. This is
possible because `I` is generated by the `f_p`.

## Over `R[x, y]`

The definitions are below; `ιx` is left implicit in the formulas.

- `Gk`: `𝒢_k = Σ_j (u δ_kj − E_kj) y_j`. This equals `u y_k − Σ_j E_kj y_j` (`Gk_eq`). The
  coefficient vector `u e_k − E_k` satisfies `hE1`, so `𝒢_k ∈ J`.
- `Zt`: `Z̃_kp = Σ_j Θ_kjp y_j`.
- `Hk`: `H_k = u³ f_k + u² 𝒢_k − Σ_p (E Z̃)_kp f_p`.

`Et_Gk` proves the exact polynomial identity `E 𝒢 = Z̃ f`, that is,
`Σ_l E_kl 𝒢_l = Σ_p Z̃_kp f_p`. Both sides equal
`Σ_j (E_kj u − Σ_l E_kl E_lj) y_j`.

## Relation to the source

The source (proof of Stacks Lemma 3.7(e)) chooses relations that map to a basis of
`(J/J²)_a`, using that this module is free. Here the relations are written down explicitly.
Over `C_a`, `E/u` is an idempotent that splits `B_a^m = (I/I²)_a ⊕ K_a`. The `H_k` are the
corresponding basis vectors, cleared of denominators and corrected by the `Z̃` term so that their
Jacobian matrix is exact.
