# Stacks 07CR and 07CT over a base `D`

Auxiliary results for `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.Desing.strictlyStandardDesingularization`
(see `StrictlyStandardDesingularizationProof.md`). They live in namespace
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.Desing`, are internal to that proof, and are not claimed as
Library results.

## Main result: `exists_desing_lift`

Hypotheses:
- `π ∈ R` with `Ann_R(π) = Ann_R(π²)`, and `Ann_Λ(π) = Ann_Λ(π²)`;
- `A` with `π` strictly standard (`IsStrictlyStandard R (algebraMap R A π)`), and `f : A → Λ`;
- `D` finitely presented over `R`, and `ψ : D → Λ`;
- `σ : A → D ⧸ π⁴D` such that `f(a) − ψ(d) ∈ π⁴Λ` whenever `σ(a) = d mod π⁴`.

Conclusion: there are a finitely presented `B` and maps `g : A → B`, `j : D → B`, `h : B → Λ`
with `h ∘ g = f` and `h ∘ j = ψ`, such that:
- if `D_π` is smooth over `R`, then so is `B_π`;
- for every prime `𝔭` of `Λ` with `π ∈ 𝔭`, if `D` is smooth over `R` at `ψ⁻¹(𝔭)`, then `B` is
  smooth over `R` at `h⁻¹(𝔭)`.

This combines Stacks Lemma 7.1 (Tag 07CR), applied over the base `D` to `D[x] ⧸ (f)` (which is
`A ⊗_R D`), with the deduction of Lemma 7.2 (Tag 07CT). Unlike Stacks, the proof does not use
that `D` is Noetherian. It uses neither Krull's intersection theorem nor the syntomic criterion.

## Construction (as in Stacks 7.1)

Take the strictly standard presentation `φ : R[x_1..x_n] ↠ A`, `ker φ = (f_1..f_m)`. Choose
`r_i ∈ D` lifting `σ(φ(x_i))`. Then `f_j(r) ∈ π⁴D`, and `f(φ(x_i)) = ψ(r_i) + π⁴λ'_i`.
- `exists_lift_data`: reducing the minor relation (2.3.3) modulo `π⁴` through `σ` gives
  `ϖu = Σ_e b_e det(ρ_e)` with `ϖ = π`, `u ∈ 1 + ϖ³D` and `ρ_{ji} = (∂_i f_j)(r)`. Stacks,
  Algebra 15.5 (`exists_mul_eq_smul_one`) gives `S` with `ρ S = ϖu·1`. Set
  `y_i = ϖ² Σ_k S_{ik} v_k + ϖ³ w_i` (`ySub`) in `D[v, w]`. The Taylor expansion
  (`aeval_add_sub_mem`) shows that `F_j = f_j(r + y)` satisfies `F_j = ϖ³ G_j` for `j < c`, where
  `G_j − v_j ∈ (w_1, …, w_n) + (ϖ)` and `∂G_j/∂v_k ≡ δ_{jk}` modulo `ϖ`. It also
  shows `F_j ∈ (ϖ²)` for all `j`.
- `B = D[x, v, w] ⧸ idealB`, generated by `x_i − (r_i + y_i)` (`relH`), `f_j(x)` (`relF`) and
  `G_j` (`relG`), with `g : x ↦ x` and `h : x ↦ λ, v ↦ 0, w ↦ πλ'`. That `h(G_j) = 0` uses
  `π³ h(G_j) = 0`, `h(G_j) ∈ πΛ` and `Ann_Λ(π) = Ann_Λ(π⁴)`.

## Smoothness

- `isSmoothAt_B_of_notMem`: `B` is smooth over `D` at every prime not containing `π`. This is
  `isSmoothAt_of_strictlyStandard` with first block `(x − r − y, f_{<c}(x))`. The maximal minor
  along `x` and `w_{e k}` equals `(ϖ³)^c · minor_e(x)` (`det_jacobian_minor`, a block
  determinant). Hence `π^{3c+3}` lies in the ideal of minors plus the relations, and the
  remaining relations satisfy `π^{3c+3} F₂ ⊆ (F₁) + K²` by (7.1.1) and `F_j = ϖ³ G_j`.
  Together with `isSmoothAt_trans`, this gives that `B_π` is smooth over `R` when `D_π` is.
- `isSmoothAt_B'_of_mem`: the chart `B' = D[x, v, w] ⧸ (x − r − y, G)` is standard smooth over
  `D` near every prime containing `π`. Its Jacobian along `(x, v)` is `det(∂G/∂v)`
  (`det_jacobian_chart`), which is `≡ 1` modulo `ϖ`.
- At a prime over `π` where `D` is smooth over `R`, `B'` is smooth over `R`
  (`isSmoothAt_trans`), hence flat near it. In `B'` the ideal `N = (f_j(x))` satisfies
  `f_{<c}(x) = 0`, `N ⊆ (π²)` and `πN ⊆ N²` (from (7.1.1)). So `M = πN` satisfies `M ⊆ πM`.
  Nakayama (`Submodule.exists_sub_one_mem_and_smul_eq_zero_of_fg_of_le_smul`) gives
  `r ≡ 1 mod π` with `rM = 0`. After inverting `r` and an element where `B'` is smooth over
  `R`, each `f_j(x) = π² z` with `π³ z = 0`. Flatness gives `πz = 0`, so `f_j(x) = 0`, and
  `smooth_away_of_ker` transfers smoothness to `B`.
