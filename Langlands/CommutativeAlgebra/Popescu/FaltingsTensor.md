# The presentation of `A ⊗_k L` over `A`

In Faltings' proof of Stacks, More on Algebra, Proposition 15.36.1 (07E5), (2) ⇒ (4),
`A' = A ⊗_k k'` is presented over `A` as `A[x]/(x_j^p − a_j)`. This module derives that
presentation from the one of `k'` over `k` by base change. All declarations live in
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.Faltings`. Here `k` is a field, `A` a `k`-algebra, `L` a
field over `k`, and `β : Fin n → L`.

## `aeval_one_tmul_eq`

For `f ∈ A[x]`, evaluating at `x_j ↦ 1 ⊗ β_j ∈ A ⊗_k L` is the same as first identifying `A[x]`
with `A ⊗_k k[x]` (`MvPolynomial.algebraTensorAlgEquiv`) and then applying `id ⊗ aeval β`. Both
sides are ring maps out of `A[x]`. They agree on constants and on the variables
(`MvPolynomial.ringHom_ext`).

## `aeval_one_tmul_surjective`

If `aeval β : k[x] → L` is surjective, so is `A[x] → A ⊗_k L`, `x_j ↦ 1 ⊗ β_j`. The reason is that
`id_A ⊗ aeval β` is surjective (`LinearMap.lTensor_surjective`).

## `ker_aeval_one_tmul`

If moreover `ker(aeval β) = rootIdeal p a`, then `ker(A[x] → A ⊗_k L) = rootIdeal p (a_A)`, where
`a_A` is the image of `a` in `A`.

Proof:
1. Over a field the kernel of `id_A ⊗ aeval β` is the image of `A ⊗ ker(aeval β)`, that is, the
   ideal generated by the image of `rootIdeal p a` (`Algebra.TensorProduct.lTensor_ker`).
2. Transporting this along `algebraTensorAlgEquiv` turns the generators `x_j^p − a_j` into
   `x_j^p − a_j` with coefficients mapped to `A`.
3. `aeval_one_tmul_eq` identifies the two kernels.
