# Proof of Stacks Lemmas 9.3 and 9.4 from a local smooth factorization

This module proves `LocalDimZeroResolutionStatement.{u}` (Stacks Project,
*Smoothing ring maps*, Lemmas 9.3 and 9.4, tags 07F9 and 07FA) for every
universe `u`. The Library theorem is

- `localDimZeroResolution : LocalDimZeroResolutionStatement.{u}`.

It is `localDimZeroResolution_of` applied to two published Library proofs:

- `localizationStandardDescent : LocalizationStandardDescentStatement.{u}`
  (Lemma 8.3, 07F4, strictly standard form; module
  `LocalizationStandardDescentProof`);
- `strictlyStandardSmooth : StrictlyStandardSmoothStatement.{u}` (Lemma 3.7(d),
  07EZ: inverting a strictly standard element gives a smooth algebra; module
  `StrictlyStandardSmoothProof`).

The reduction itself is

- `localDimZeroResolution_of (h9 : LocalizationStandardDescentStatement.{u})
  (n0 : StrictlyStandardSmoothStatement.{u}) : LocalDimZeroResolutionStatement.{u}`.

It applies `exists_factor_singularIdeal_not_le`, the Lemma 9.3 step, and then
`canBeResolved_of_dimZero`, the Lemma 9.4 step (module `DimZeroGlue`).

## Lemma 9.3: `exists_factor_singularIdeal_not_le`

Data:

- `A` is a finitely presented `R`-algebra and `f : A → Λ` an `R`-algebra map.
- `q` is a prime of `Λ`, `p = q ∩ R`, and `T` is a smooth `R_p`-algebra.
- `u : A → T` and `v : T → Λ_q` are ring maps such that `u` is compatible with
  `R → R_p → T` and `v ∘ u = (Λ → Λ_q) ∘ f`.

Conclusion: `f` factors as `A → C → Λ`, where `C` is a finitely presented
`R`-algebra and `H_{C/R} Λ ⊄ q`.

No Noetherian hypothesis and no minimality of `q` is needed for this step.

The implemented proof differs from the proof of Stacks 9.3 (smoothing.pdf,
printed p. 23). That proof reduces to a standard smooth `C` over `R_p` and clears
denominators in a presentation of `C`. It then homogenizes the equations and
writes down a finitely presented `R`-algebra `B` with a smooth principal
localization `B_b`. The Lean proof instead combines two steps. First it factors
`T → Λ_q` through a localization of `Λ` (module `LocalFactorization`). Then it
applies Lemma 8.3 (07F4, the hypothesis `h9`) over `R[X]`, where `X ↦ ℓ`, to
produce the element of the singular ideal. The steps are as follows.

1. `exists_localization_factor` (module `LocalFactorization`) gives `ℓ ∉ q`.
2. Make `Λ` an `R[X]`-algebra by `X ↦ ℓ` (`Polynomial.aeval ℓ`). Let `S ⊆ R[X]`
   be the submonoid generated by the constants `C(s)`, `s ∉ p`, together with
   `X`. Every element of `S` maps into `Λ \ q`. Put `R' = S⁻¹R[X]` and
   `Λ' = S⁻¹Λ`, the localization of `Λ` at the image of `S`.
3. The image of `S` contains the image of `R \ p` and `ℓ`, and lies in `Λ \ q`.
   So step 1 gives `w : T → Λ'` with `w ∘ u = (Λ → Λ') ∘ f`.
4. The constants `C(s)`, `s ∉ p`, are units in `R'`, so there is a ring map
   `R_p → R'`. Let `B' = R' ⊗_{R_p} T`. It is smooth over `R'` (base change).
5. Define the following maps.
   - `u₁ : R[X] ⊗_R A → B'` sends `x ⊗ a` to `x · (1 ⊗ u(a))`.
   - `w` is `R_p`-linear for the structure `R_p → R' → Λ'`; this follows from
     the compatibility of `u` and from `w ∘ u = (Λ → Λ') ∘ f`, checked on `R`
     (`IsLocalization.ringHom_ext`). So `w` and `R' → Λ'` induce
     `v₁ : B' → Λ'`.
   - `f₁ : R[X] ⊗_R A → Λ` sends `x ⊗ a` to `x · f(a)`.

   Then `v₁ ∘ u₁ = (Λ → Λ') ∘ f₁`; it suffices to check this on pure tensors
   (`Algebra.TensorProduct.ext'`).
6. `R[X] ⊗_R A` is finitely presented over `R[X]`. Lemma 8.3 (`h9`) applied over
   `R[X]` with `S`, `f₁`, `B'`, `u₁` and `v₁` gives the following: a finitely
   presented `R[X]`-algebra `B`, maps `g : R[X] ⊗_R A → B` and `h : B → Λ` with
   `h ∘ g = f₁`, and `s ∈ S` whose image in `B` is strictly standard over `R[X]`.
7. By Lemma 3.7(d) (`n0`), `B_s` is smooth over `R[X]`. `R[X]` is smooth over `R`
   (it is isomorphic to `MvPolynomial Unit R`), so `B_s` is smooth over `R`
   (`Algebra.Smooth.comp`). `B` is finitely presented over `R`
   (`Algebra.FinitePresentation.trans`). Therefore `s ∈ H_{B/R}`
   (`DimZeroResolutionProof.mem_singularIdeal_of_smooth`).
8. Take `C = B`, with `A → C` the composite of `a ↦ 1 ⊗ a` and `g`, and with
   `h` viewed as an `R`-algebra map. Evaluating `h ∘ g = f₁` at `1 ⊗ a` gives
   `h ∘ g ∘ (1 ⊗ -) = f`. The image `h(s)` is the image of `s ∈ S` in `Λ`,
   which is not in `q`. So `H_{C/R} Λ ⊄ q`.

This step runs with `maxHeartbeats` raised to `1000000`.

## Lemmas 9.3 + 9.4: `localDimZeroResolution_of`

Under the hypotheses of `LocalDimZeroResolutionStatement`, step 9.3 gives
`A → C → Λ` with `H_{C/R} Λ ⊄ q`. Since `q` is a minimal prime containing
`h_A`, `canBeResolved_of_dimZero` turns this into `CanBeResolved R f q`.
