# Assembly of the descent of smooth factorizations from a localization

This module proves the strictly standard form of Stacks Project, *Smoothing ring
maps*, Lemma 8.3 (tag 07F4), from Lemma 3.4 (tag 07CH). The main theorem is

- `localizationStandardDescent_of : StandardSmoothRetractStatement.{u} →
  LocalizationStandardDescentStatement.{u}`.

Lemma 3.4 enters only as a hypothesis. The auxiliary declarations of Parts 1–3
(`isStrictlyStandard_of_presentation`, `exists_descent`, `scaleVars`,
`pderiv_scaleVars`, `exists_descent_scaled` and
`localizationStandardDescent_core`) are in the namespace
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.LocalizationStandardDescent`; the main
theorem is in `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu`. The derivative-vanishing
helper is reused as `SymRetract.pderiv_rename_of_notMem_range'` from
`SymRetractAlgebra`. The proof has four parts:

1. a presentation criterion for strictly standard elements;
2. clearing denominators over a localization;
3. the core case, where `B'` is standard smooth over `S⁻¹R`;
4. the reduction of the smooth case to the standard smooth case.

The source states the lemma with conclusion "`s` is elementary standard in `B`".
The Library statement asks only for the weaker conclusion that `s` is strictly
standard (`IsStrictlyStandard`); every elementary standard element is strictly
standard. The proof below establishes the strictly standard conclusion directly.
Notation: `R' = S⁻¹R` and `Λ' = S⁻¹Λ`.

## Part 1: presentation criterion

`isStrictlyStandard_of_presentation` gives the following criterion.

- Data:
  - finite index types `V`, `κ`, `τ`;
  - families `F : κ → R[V]` and `G : τ → R[V]`;
  - an injective map `emb : κ → V`;
  - a surjective `R`-algebra map `φ : R[V] → B` with kernel `(F, G)`.
- Hypotheses on `s ∈ R`:
  - `s ∈ (det(∂F_j/∂x_{emb k})) + (F)` in `R[V]`;
  - `s · G_l ∈ (F)` for every `l`.
- Conclusion: `algebraMap R B s` is strictly standard over `R`
  (`IsStrictlyStandard`).

The definition of `IsStrictlyStandard` uses variables indexed by `Fin N`,
equations indexed by `Fin m`, and the first `c` equations as the distinguished
ones. The proof converts the data to that form.

- Variables are renamed along `Fintype.equivFin V`.
- The equations are `Fin.append` of the renamed `F` (reindexed by `κ ≃ Fin c`)
  and the renamed `G` (reindexed by `τ ≃ Fin t`).
- The column embedding `Fin c ↪o Fin N` is `Finset.orderEmbOfFin` of the image of
  `emb`. The equivalence `κ ≃ Fin c` is chosen so that this embedding agrees with
  `emb` after reindexing.
- The kernel is transported with `Ideal.map_comap_of_surjective`.
- The minor is identified through `pderiv_rename` and
  `Matrix.det_submatrix_equiv_self`.

The element of the ideal of minors is then the image of
`a · det + z = C s`, where `z ∈ (F)` maps to zero in `B`.

## Part 2: clearing denominators

`exists_descent` uses the local instance `MvPolynomial.algebraMvPolynomial`, under
which `R'[V]` is the localization of `R[V]` at `C(S)` (`MvPolynomial.isLocalization`).

- Data:
  - `E : κ → R'[V]` vanishing at the image in `Λ'` of a point `μ : V → Λ`;
  - `G : τ → R[V]` whose images lie in `(E)`;
  - `1 ∈ (det(∂E_j/∂x_{emb k})) + (E)`.
- Conclusion: there are `F : κ → R[V]` and `s ∈ S` with
  - `F` vanishing at `μ`;
  - `C s ∈ (det(∂F_j/∂x_{emb k})) + (F)`;
  - `C s · G_l ∈ (F)` for all `l`.

The proof clears the denominators of each `E_k` with `IsLocalization.surj`. It
multiplies by a further element of `S` so that the equation vanishes at `μ`
itself, not just in `Λ'` (`IsLocalization.map_eq_zero_iff`). Then `F_k` is a unit
multiple of `E_k` over `R'`, so `(F)` and `(E)` generate the same ideal of `R'[V]`.
The Jacobian determinants differ by the unit `∏ C(s_k)` (`Matrix.det_mul_column`).
Finally, `IsLocalization.algebraMap_mem_map_algebraMap_iff` produces the elements
of `S` that bring `1` and each `G_l` back into the ideals over `R`. Their product
is the required `s`.

`exists_descent_scaled` deals with a point of `Λ'` of the form
`(d_v · μ_v)_v`, where `d_v` are units of `R'`. It applies `exists_descent` to
`E_k(d · X)`, using the substitution `scaleVars d : X_v ↦ d_v X_v`. Two facts are
needed:

- `pderiv_scaleVars`: `∂_v (p(d·X)) = d_v · (∂_v p)(d·X)`, so the Jacobian
  determinant changes by the unit `∏_k d_{emb k}`;
- the hypothesis that the substitution fixes the images of the `G_l`.

## Part 3: the standard smooth case

`localizationStandardDescent_core` proves the conclusion of the statement when
`B'` is standard smooth over `R'`. This is where the argument of Stacks Lemma 8.3
appears.

- **Presentations.** `B'` has a submersive presentation `P` with variables `ι`
  and relations `σ` (`Algebra.IsStandardSmooth.out`). `A` has a presentation
  `φA : R[x_1..x_n] → A` with kernel generated by a finite set `T`
  (`Algebra.FinitePresentation.out`).
- **Equations over `R'`.** The variables are `V = Fin n ⊕ ι`. The equations,
  indexed by `κ = Fin n ⊕ σ`, are
  - `X(inl i) − b_i(X ∘ inr)`, where `b_i ∈ R'[ι]` lifts `u(φA x_i)`
    (`P.σ`, `P.aeval_val_σ`);
  - the relations of `P`, read in the variables `inr`.

  Distinguished variables are `Sum.map id P.map`. The extra equations `G` are the
  elements of `T`, read in the variables `inl`. This is the presentation of
  `B' ≅ R'[x, y]/(x − b(y), P-relations)` used in Stacks Lemma 3.6.
- **The point.** `v ∘ P.val` is a point of `Λ'`. Clearing its denominators
  (`IsLocalization.surj`) gives `l : ι → Λ` and `s : ι → S` with
  `v(P.val x) · s_x = l_x`. With `d = (1, s⁻¹)` and `μ = (f ∘ φA ∘ x, l)`, the
  point `(f(φA x_i), v(P.val y))` equals `d · μ`. The rescaling fixes the
  equations `G`, which involve only the variables `inl`.
- **Vanishing.** The equations vanish at the point, by `huv` and
  `P.aeval_val_relation`. Each `G` lies in `J = (E)`. Modulo `J`,
  `X(inl i) ≡ b_i`, so `q(X ∘ inl) ≡ q(b)`. Since `q(b)` maps to `u(φA q) = 0` in
  `B'`, it lies in the ideal of the relations (`P.span_range_relation_eq_ker`).
- **Jacobian.** The Jacobian matrix is block upper triangular,
  `fromBlocks 1 * 0 D`. Here `D` is the image of the transposed Jacobian matrix
  of `P` (`P.jacobiMatrix_apply`), so its determinant is the Jacobian of `P`.
  Off-diagonal vanishing uses `SymRetract.pderiv_rename_of_notMem_range'`: the derivative of
  a renamed polynomial along a variable outside the range is zero. The Jacobian
  of `P` is a unit in `B'` (`P.jacobian_isUnit`). Hence some `h₀` satisfies
  `h₀ · det − 1 ∈ J`, and `1 ∈ (det) + J`.
- **Descent and conclusion.** `exists_descent_scaled` gives `F` over `R` and
  `s₀ ∈ S`. The algebra `B = R[V]/(F, G)` is finitely presented
  (`Algebra.FinitePresentation.quotient`).
  - `g : A → B` is `AlgHom.liftOfSurjective φA` of `x_i ↦ X(inl i)`. It is well
    defined because the elements of `T` are among the `G`.
  - `h : B → Λ` is induced by evaluation at `μ`. It is well defined because `F`
    vanishes at `μ`, and `G` maps to `f(φA q) = 0`.
  - `h ∘ g = f` holds on generators.
  - Part 1 shows that `s₀` is strictly standard in `B`.

## Part 4: the smooth case

`localizationStandardDescent_of` derives the statement from Lemma 3.4. For a
smooth `R'`-algebra `B'`, Lemma 3.4 gives a standard smooth `R'`-algebra `B''`
that is a `B'`-algebra, with a `B'`-algebra retraction `r : B'' → B'`.

- `B''` is made an `R`-algebra through `R → R' → B''`. The towers `R → R' → B''`
  and `R → B' → B''` hold (`IsScalarTower.of_algebraMap_eq'`).
- Put `u' = (B' → B'') ∘ u` and `v' = v ∘ r`. Then `v' ∘ u' = v ∘ u`, because `r`
  is a `B'`-algebra map.
- Part 3, applied to `B''`, `u'` and `v'`, gives the conclusion.
