# Tools for the proof of Stacks 07F0

Auxiliary lemmas for `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.Desing.strictlyStandardDesingularization`
(see `StrictlyStandardDesingularizationProof.md`). All are in namespace
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.Desing`. They are internal to that proof and are not
claimed as Library results.

- `isStandardSmooth_away_det_mul`: for relations `v_i ∈ R[X_σ]` (`i ∈ ι`) and an injective choice
  of variables `a : ι → σ`, the algebra `R[X_σ] ⧸ (v)` becomes standard smooth over `R` after
  inverting `det(∂v_j/∂X_{a i}) · t`, for any element `t`. The proof composes Mathlib's naive
  pre-submersive presentation with `PreSubmersivePresentation.localizationAway`. The Jacobian of
  the composite is a unit.
- `nonempty_algEquiv_away_of_ker` and `smooth_away_of_ker`: let `p : A₀ → A` be surjective, and
  suppose every element of `ker p` vanishes in `A₀[1/s]`. Then `A₀[1/s] ≅ A[1/p(s)]` as
  `R`-algebras, so smoothness transfers. The proof checks that `A₀[1/s]` is a localization of `A`
  away from `p(s)`.
- `isSmoothAt_of_smooth_away` and `smooth_away_of_isSmoothAt`: for finitely presented `A`,
  `A[1/s]` is smooth over `R` exactly when `A` is smooth over `R` at every prime not containing
  `s` (`Algebra.basicOpen_subset_smoothLocus_iff_smooth`).
- `isSmoothAt_trans`: if `B` is smooth over `D` at `q` and `D` is smooth over `R` at `q ∩ D`, then
  `B` is smooth over `R` at `q`. This uses `FormallySmooth.of_restrictScalars` through the
  formally unramified localization `D → D_{q∩D}`, then `FormallySmooth.comp`.
- `smul_eq_zero_of_flat`: in a flat `R`-module, `π² y = 0` implies `π y = 0` when this holds in
  `R`. The proof applies the equational criterion of flatness to one relation.
  `smul_eq_zero_of_pow_smul_eq_zero`: `Ann(π) = Ann(π²)` implies `Ann(π) = Ann(π^(k+1))`.
- `aeval_add_sub_mem` (first-order Taylor expansion): if all `y_i ∈ J`, then
  `p(a + y) − p(a) − Σ_i (∂_i p)(a) y_i ∈ J²`.
- `exists_mul_eq_smul_one` (Stacks, Commutative Algebra, Lemma 10.15.5(1), Tag 07DQ, cited as
  "Algebra, Lemma 15.5" in the proof of Smoothing Ring Maps, Lemma 7.1): if `b` is a combination
  of the maximal minors of a `c × n` matrix `M`, then `M S = b · 1` for some `n × c` matrix `S`.
  `S` is assembled from the adjugates of the minors.
- `isSmoothAt_of_strictlyStandard` (Stacks 07CA, first half, in a flexible form): let
  `K = (F₁) + (F₂)` in `D[X]`. Suppose `a` lies in the ideal generated by `K` and the maximal
  minors `det(∂F₁/∂X_μ)` (for injective `μ`), and suppose `a F₂ ⊆ (F₁) + K²`. Then `D[X] ⧸ K` is
  smooth over `D` at every prime not containing `a`. Proof: at such a prime some minor `J_μ` is
  nonzero. The determinant trick gives `s ≡ a^{|τ|}` modulo the image of `F₂` that kills the
  image of `F₂` in `D[X] ⧸ (F₁)`. The chart `(D[X] ⧸ (F₁))[1/(J_μ s a)]` is standard smooth by
  `isStandardSmooth_away_det_mul`, and it agrees with the corresponding localization of
  `D[X] ⧸ K` by `smooth_away_of_ker`.
