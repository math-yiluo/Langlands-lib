# Proof of Stacks Lemma 16.11.4 (07FJ) in positive characteristic

`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.charPResolution` proves
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.CharPResolutionStatement.{u}`: let `k` be a field of
characteristic `p > 0`, `A` a finitely presented `k`-algebra, `f : A →ₐ[k] Λ` a map to a
`k`-algebra `Λ` that is geometrically regular over `k`, and `q` a prime of `Λ` minimal
over `h_A = singularRadical k f`. Then `CanBeResolved k f q`.

## The proof

The theorem applies `CharP.charPResolution_of`, from module `CharPResolutionAssembly`
(see `CharPResolutionAssembly.md` for the argument, which follows steps (1)–(26) of the
source proof). It discharges that theorem's seven hypotheses with these published
Library proofs:

| Hypothesis | Source | Library proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `StrictlyStandardSmoothStatement` | Lemma 3.7 | `strictlyStandardSmooth` |
| `StrictlyStandardRetractStatement` | Lemmas 3.1 and 3.7 | `strictlyStandardRetract` |
| `AnnihilatorStabilizationStatement` | Lemma 10.1 (Ogoma) | `annihilatorStabilization` |
| `RegularLocalParametersStatement` | Algebra, Lemma 10.106.3 (00NQ) | `regularLocalParameters` |
| `RegularSequencePowersStatement` | Algebra, Lemma 10.68.9 (07DV) | `regularSequencePowers` |
| `ArtinianSmoothApproximationStatement` | Lemma 11.2 (07FH) | `artinianSmoothApproximation` |
| `SequenceDimZeroResolutionStatement` | Lemmas 9.2 and 9.4 | `sequenceDimZeroResolution` |

All names are in the namespace `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu`. Lemma and page
numbers refer to *Smoothing Ring Maps* unless marked "Algebra" (Stacks *Commutative
Algebra*).

## Supporting modules

Six modules besides this one support the proof.

- `CharPTools`: helper lemmas (ideals generated by powers, flat local homomorphisms,
  smooth factorizations of essentially smooth algebras, minimal numbers of generators,
  and maps out of the Library's `SmoothingAlgebra`).
- `CharPRegular`: regularity of powers of regular parameters and the resulting
  nonzerodivisor facts (`isWeaklyRegular_pow_append`, `mem_of_mul_mem_span_pow`,
  `ogoma_hyp`), from the statements for Algebra 00NQ and 07DV.
- `CharPParameters`: step (12), the annihilator condition for `γᵢ = πᵢ tᵢ`
  (`exists_step12`, `exists_step12_poly`), from Lemma 10.1.
- `CharPStep13`: the admissibility predicate `Adm` and a variant of step (13)
  (`exists_step13`) that does not enlarge `D`.
- `CharPBottom`: the bottom input of Lemma 9.2 used in step (18), proved along steps
  (19)–(26) (`exists_bottom`).
- `CharPResolutionAssembly`: `CharP.charPResolution_of`, which derives the target from
  the seven statements above.
