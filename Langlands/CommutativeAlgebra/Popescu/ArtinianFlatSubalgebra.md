# Proof of Stacks Lemma 11.1 (Tag 07FG)

`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.artinianFlatSubalgebra` proves
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.ArtinianFlatSubalgebraStatement.{u}`. Let `k` be a field
of characteristic `p > 0` and `(L, 𝔪, κ)` an Artinian local `k`-algebra with
`H₁(L_{κ/k})` finite-dimensional. Then every finite subset `E ⊆ L` lies in a `k`-subalgebra
`A ⊆ L` with the following properties:

- `A` is local and Artinian;
- `A` is essentially of finite type over `k`;
- `L` is flat over `A`;
- `𝔪_A L = 𝔪`.

The proof follows the source. The auxiliary modules are:

| Module | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `ArtinianFlatLift` | lifting maps from fields of characteristic `p` along square-zero extensions (formal smoothness over `𝔽_p`) |
| `ArtinianFlatTools` | `mem_subring_of_nilpotent`, a Nakayama-type lemma for subrings |
| `ArtinianFlatDerivation` | `fg_ker_mapBaseChange` and `exists_derivation_approx'` (the Jacobi-Zariski paragraph) |
| `ArtinianFlatClaim` | `CoeffClaim` and its inductive step `coeffClaim_of_surjective` |
| `ArtinianFlatInduction` | `coeffClaim_residue`: the claim for `L` |
| `ArtinianFlatConstruction` | local, Artinian, free and essentially-finite-type criteria for `A = σ(F)[t]` |

## Proof of `artinianFlatSubalgebra`

1. `fg_ker_mapBaseChange`: the kernel of `κ ⊗_k Ω_k → Ω_κ` is finitely generated.
2. Choose generators `t₁, …, t_d` of `𝔪`. `L` is Noetherian, and
   `Submodule.fg_iff_exists_fin_generating_family` gives them. Let `𝔪ⁿ = 0`. Then
   `residue(tᵢ) = 0` and `𝔪` is nil.
3. The claim (`coeffClaim_residue`) gives a section `σ : κ → L` of the residue map and a
   finitely generated `F = k(S_F)` with `k ⊆ Ψ_σ(F[x])`. Here
   `Ψ_σ : κ[x₁, …, x_d] → L` is `xᵢ ↦ tᵢ` with coefficients mapped by `σ`.
4. `Ψ_σ` is surjective (`eval₂_surjective`). Its kernel is generated by a finite set `G`
   (`κ[x]` is Noetherian). Choose preimages `pre(e)` of the elements `e ∈ E`.
5. Let `T` be the finite set of coefficients of the polynomials in `G ∪ pre(E)`. Let
   `F₁ = k(S_F ∪ T) ⊇ F`. Every polynomial in `G ∪ pre(E)` comes from `F₁[x]`
   (`mem_range_map_iff_coeffs_subset`).
6. Let `A` be the image of `Ψ₁ : F₁[x] → L` (coefficients through `σ`). It is a
   `k`-subalgebra: by step 3, `algebraMap k L a = Ψ_σ(h)` with `h ∈ F[x] ⊆ F₁[x]`.
7. Properties of `A` (module `ArtinianFlatConstruction`):
   - the residues of elements of `A` lie in `F₁` (`residue_eval₂`), and `σ(F₁) ⊆ A`;
   - hence elements with nonzero residue are units (`isUnit_of_residue_ne_zero`), `A` is
     local (`isLocalRing_of_residue`), and `𝔪_A = A ∩ 𝔪`;
   - `A` is Noetherian as a quotient of `F₁[x]`, hence Artinian
     (`isArtinianRing_of_residue`);
   - `E ⊆ A`, since `e = Ψ_σ(pre e) = Ψ₁(Q)` for the lift `Q` of `pre e`;
   - `A` is essentially of finite type over `k` (`essFiniteType_of_residue` with
     `S = S_F ∪ T`);
   - `ker Ψ_σ = (G) ⊆ (ker Ψ₁) κ[x]`, because each `g ∈ G` comes from `F₁[x]`. So `L` is free,
     hence flat, over `A` (`free_of_ker_le`);
   - `𝔪_A L = 𝔪`, since `tᵢ ∈ A` (`map_maximalIdeal_eq`).

## Comparison with the source

- The statement asks only that every finite subset lie in such an `A`. That is the form in
  which Lemma 16.11.2 uses the result. The source's "filtered colimit" is therefore not
  formalized (see the statement's Markdown).
- The source enlarges `F` by the coefficients of the `gⱼ` and proves the claim first. Here
  `F` is enlarged once more, by the coefficients of preimages of `E`. This puts `E` inside
  `A`, which the source gets from "`K` is the filtered union of the subfields `F`".
- The source's `A` is `F[x]/(g₁, …, g_t)`. Here `A` is its image in `L`, defined as a
  `k`-subalgebra. Freeness is proved by applying `K`-linear coefficient functionals instead
  of by base change. Essential finite type is proved with a Nakayama-type argument instead
  of Algebra, Lemma 54.4.
- The source's `n = 1` base case ("a field extension is a union of finitely generated field
  extensions") is not needed separately. The construction works uniformly, with `d` possibly
  `0`.

Uses only the standard axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.
