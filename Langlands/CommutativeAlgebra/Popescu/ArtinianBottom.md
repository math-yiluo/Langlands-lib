# The Artinian bottom of the proof of Stacks Lemma 10.3

This module supports the proof of Stacks Lemma 10.3 (Tag 07FE). It proves the
bottom condition of Lemma 9.2 (`BottomCondition`, module `LocalLifting`) for the
polynomial ring `R = k[x₁, …, x_d]`, where `xᵢ ↦ πᵢ` and the `πᵢ` form a regular system of
parameters of `Λ_q`. The main result is `bottomCondition_of_flat`.

## Monomial ideals and variables

- `RegularParameter.mem_span_monomial_of_X_mul`, imported from `RegularParameterTools`,
  and the local `mem_span_monomial_of_X_pow_mul`: a monomial ideal
  whose generators do not involve `xᵢ` is saturated with respect to `xᵢ`. This follows from
  `MvPolynomial.mem_ideal_span_monomial_image` and `support_X_mul`.
- `X_pow_annihilator`: the powers `x_i^c` satisfy the annihilator condition of Lemma 9.2
  in `k[x]` ("Assumption (1) holds for R by inspection").
- `RegularParameter.isWeaklyRegular_X`, also imported from `RegularParameterTools`: the
  variables form a weakly regular sequence of `k[x]`.

## Annihilators

- `mul_mem_of_pow_mul_mem` and `pow_annihilator`: if `Ann(π) = Ann(π²)` modulo `J`, then
  the same holds for `π^c`, `c > 0`. This is the parenthetical remark in the proof of
  Lemma 10.3.

## Flatness

- `ker_constantCoeff_eq` and `comap_eq_span_X`: the preimage of `q` in `k[x]` is the
  ideal `(x₁, …, x_d)`.
- `flat_localization_of_regular`: `Λ_q` is flat over `k[x]`.
  - The ring map `k[x]_{(x)} → Λ_q` is `Localization.localRingHom`, a local homomorphism.
  - The variables generate the maximal ideal of `k[x]_{(x)}` and form a weakly regular
    sequence there (`RegularParameter.isWeaklyRegular_X` with `IsWeaklyRegular.of_flat`).
  - Their images are the regular sequence `πᵢ`.
  - The published `RegularSequenceFlatStatement` (Algebra Lemma 10.128.2, Tag 07DY) gives
    flatness over `k[x]_{(x)}`. `Module.Flat.trans` then gives flatness over `k[x]`.

## Property PT modulo `K`

`hasSmoothFactorizations_quot` proves PT for `k[x]/K → Λ_q/KΛ_q` whenever
`K ⊆ (x)` and some power of each `xᵢ` lies in `K`. This is the end of the proof of
Lemma 10.3.

1. **Flatness.** `Λ_q/KΛ_q ≅ (k[x]/K) ⊗ Λ_q`
   (`Algebra.TensorProduct.quotIdealMapEquivQuotTensor`), which is flat over `k[x]/K`.
2. **Nilpotent lifting.** The ideal `I = (x)/K` is finitely generated by nilpotents, so it
   is nilpotent. The published `NilpotentLiftingStatement` (Proposition 5.3) reduces PT to
   `(k[x]/K)/I → (Λ_q/KΛ_q)/I`.
3. **The closed fibre.** This map is identified with `k → κ(q)` by two explicit ring
   isomorphisms built with `RingEquiv.ofBijective`:
   - `k → (k[x]/K)/I` is bijective, since every polynomial is congruent to its constant
     term modulo `(x)`;
   - `(Λ_q/KΛ_q)/I → κ(q)` is bijective, since `(x)Λ_q` is the maximal ideal.
4. **The field case.** PT for `k → κ(q)` is the published
   `CharZeroFieldFactorizationStatement`. It transfers along the two isomorphisms by
   `hasSmoothFactorizations_of_ringEquiv'`. This lemma is proved at the start of
   this module: PT transfers along ring isomorphisms of the bases and the targets
   that commute with the algebra maps.

## The bottom condition

- `le_of_between`: a prime `P` with `KΛ ⊆ P ⊆ q` equals `q`. Its extension to `Λ_q` is
  prime and contains every `πᵢ`, so it is the maximal ideal.
- `bottomCondition_of_flat`. Given surjections `σ` and `τ` with kernels `K` and `KΛ`, and
  a prime `Q` over `q`:
  - **`Q` is a minimal prime of `Λ₁`.** Use `le_of_between` on preimages.
  - **PT for `R₁ → (Λ₁)_Q`.** The map `Λ_q → (Λ₁)_Q` (`Localization.localRingHom`) is
    surjective (`RingHom.SurjectiveOnStalks`) with kernel `KΛ_q`. So
    `(Λ₁)_Q ≅ Λ_q/KΛ_q`, compatibly with `R₁ ≅ k[x]/K`, and PT transfers from
    `hasSmoothFactorizations_quot`.
