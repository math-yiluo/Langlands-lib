# Stacks 07CP: the lifting lemma

Auxiliary results for `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.Desing.strictlyStandardDesingularization`
(see `StrictlyStandardDesingularizationProof.md`). They live in namespace
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.Desing`, are internal to that proof, and are not claimed as
Library results.

## Main result: `exists_lift_D`

Hypotheses:
- `R` is Noetherian and `ϖ, ε ∈ R` with `ε = ϖ²`;
- `Ann_R(ϖ) = Ann_R(ϖ²)` and `Ann_Λ(ϖ) = Ann_Λ(ϖ²)`;
- `C` is finitely presented over `R ⧸ (ε)`, with `v : C → Λ ⧸ εΛ`.

Conclusion: there are a finitely presented `R`-algebra `D`, a map `ψ : D → Λ` and a map
`τ : C → D ⧸ ϖD` such that:
- (compatibility) if `τ(c) = d mod ϖ` and `v(c) = l mod ε`, then `l − ψ(d) ∈ ϖΛ`;
- (Stacks 6.1(b)) `D` is smooth over `R` at every prime not containing `ϖ`;
- (Stacks 6.1(c), in the form used for 7.3) if `y` lies in the singular ideal of `C` over
  `R ⧸ (ε)`, `v(y) = l mod ε`, and `𝔭` is a prime of `Λ` with `ϖ ∈ 𝔭` and `l ∉ 𝔭`, then `D` is
  smooth over `R` at `ψ⁻¹(𝔭)`.

Part (d) of Stacks 6.1 is not needed for 7.3 and is not proved.

## Construction

- Charts (`exists_chart_family`): choose a presentation `φ : R̄[x] ↠ C` with kernel `(f̄_1..f̄_m)`,
  where `R̄ = R ⧸ (ϖ²)`. For each prime of the smooth locus, `exists_chart` gives a chart. The
  smooth locus is quasi-compact (`C` is Noetherian), so finitely many charts `(e_i, u_i, a_i)`
  suffice. The singular ideal lies in the radical of the ideal generated by the
  `a_i · det(∂f̄_{e_i j}/∂x_{u_i l})` (`Ideal.radical_eq_sInf`).
- Lifting (`exists_lift_rel`): lift `f̄_j`, `a_i` and the witnesses to `R`. This gives exact
  relations `a f_ℓ = Σ_j h_{ℓ j} f_{e j} + ϖ² g_ℓ` in `R[x]`. On `range e` the witnesses are
  chosen trivially, so `p_{k, e j} = 0`. A `Chart` bundles these data.
- `D = R[x, z] ⧸ idealD`, where `idealD` is generated by `f_ℓ(x) − ϖ z_ℓ` (`relE`) and
  `p_{k,ℓ} = a_k z_ℓ − Σ_j h_{k,ℓ,j} z_{e_k j} − ϖ g_{k,ℓ}` (`relP`). The map `ψ` sends `x` to
  lifts `λ` of `v(φ(x))` and `z_j` to `ϖ μ_j`, where `f_j(λ) = ϖ² μ_j`. That `ψ(p) = 0` uses
  `Ann_Λ(ϖ) = Ann_Λ(ϖ²)`. The map `τ` is induced by `x ↦ x` on `R[x] ↠ C`, through
  `Ideal.Quotient.liftₐ`.

## Smoothness

- Identities in `R[x, z]` and `R[x]`: `relP_key` (`ϖ p_{k,ℓ}` is a combination of the
  `f − ϖ z`), `relP_cross` and `crossCoeff_rel` (comparing two charts), and `det_mul_mem_of_sum`
  (differentiating and multiplying by the adjugate kills the comparison coefficients modulo
  `(f) + (ϖ²)`).
- `isSmoothAt_D_of_notMem` (6.1(b)): on `R[x, z] ⧸ (f − ϖz)`, the Jacobian along `z` is
  `(−ϖ)^m`. By `relP_key`, the `p_{k,ℓ}` die once `ϖ` is inverted.
- `isSmoothAt_D_of_chart` (6.1(c)): the chart `D_k = R[x, z] ⧸ (f_{e j} − ϖ z_{e j}, p_{k,ℓ})`,
  for `ℓ ∉ range e`, has Jacobian `δ_k · a_k^#` along `x_{u j}` and those `z_ℓ` (`det_chart`, a
  block-triangular determinant). So it becomes standard smooth, hence flat, after inverting
  `δ_k a_k`. There all remaining relations of `D` vanish: the `f − ϖz` by `relP_key`, and the
  `p_{k',ℓ}` of the other charts by `relP_eq_zero_of_chart`, which combines the comparison
  identities with `Ann(ϖ) = Ann(ϖ²)` on the flat localization. The comparison
  `smooth_away_of_ker` then transfers smoothness to `D`.
- In `exists_lift_D`, `y^N` is a combination of the chart elements. If all of their images under
  `ψ` were in `𝔭`, then `l^N ∈ 𝔭`, since `ε Λ ⊆ 𝔭`. So some chart applies at `ψ⁻¹(𝔭)`.
