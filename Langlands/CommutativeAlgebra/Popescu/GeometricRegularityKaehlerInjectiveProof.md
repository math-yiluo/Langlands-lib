# Proof of `GeometricRegularityKaehlerInjectiveStatement`

This module proves `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.GeometricRegularityKaehlerInjectiveStatement`,
which is Stacks, More on Algebra, Proposition 15.36.1 (07E5), implication (1) ⇒ (4), at a prime
`q` of a geometrically regular algebra. The proof follows the source:
- (1) ⇒ (2) is immediate;
- (2) ⇒ (4) is Faltings' argument, formalized in the `Faltings*` modules of this development.

All declarations live in `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.Faltings`.

## `kaehler_injective_of_regular`

Setting:
- `k` is a field of characteristic `p`;
- `A` is a local `k`-algebra;
- `A ⊗_k k'` is regular local for every finite purely inseparable `k'/k`.

Statement: `κ ⊗_k Ω_{k/ℤ} → κ ⊗_A Ω_{A/ℤ}`, `c ⊗ ω ↦ c ⊗ ω|_A`, is injective, where `κ = κ(A)`.
The `ℤ`-algebra structures on `k` and `A` and the scalar-tower instances are arbitrary instance
arguments. All `ℤ`-algebra structures on a ring agree (`int_algebra_subsingleton`), so the proof
first substitutes the default `Ring.toIntAlgebra`. This lets the theorem apply to
`Localization.AtPrime q`, whose `ℤ`-algebra instance comes from the localization.

Proof:
1. The differentials `da` (`a ∈ k`) span `Ω_{k/ℤ}` (`KaehlerDifferential.span_range_derivation`).
   By `liftBaseChange_injective_of_finite` it suffices to show the following. If
   `da₁, …, da_n` are linearly independent, their images `1 ⊗ d(a_j|_A)` are linearly
   independent over `κ`.
2. The images are computed with `KaehlerDifferential.map_D`.
3. `independent_of_regular` concludes.

## `geometricRegularityKaehlerInjective`

`GeometricRegularityKaehlerInjectiveStatement.{u}` holds. Given `k`, `Λ`, `p`, geometric regularity
`hΛ` and a prime `q`, apply `kaehler_injective_of_regular` to `A = Λ_q`. The regularity hypothesis
is `isRegularLocalRing_tensor_atPrime k k' q hΛ`: geometric regularity makes `k' ⊗_k Λ` regular,
and `Λ_q ⊗_k k'` is a localization of it. The residue field `q.ResidueField` is by definition
`κ(Λ_q)`.

The proof uses only the axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.
