# The new relations generate the conormal module after inverting `u`

This module proves that `u⁸ J ⊆ (H_1, …, H_m) + J²`. It is the "relations generate `J/J²`
after inverting `a`" half of the strictly standard criterion (module
`StrictlyStandardCriterion`), for the algebra `C = R[x, y]/J` and the relations `H_k` of module
`SymRetractData`. All declarations are in namespace `SymRetract`.

## `(u − E) Z̃ E = 0` in `C`

`uC_sub_EC_mul`. Since `E = D P` and `Z̃ D = E Ỹ`, we have `Z̃ E = E Ỹ P`. Hence
`(u − E) Z̃ E = (u E − E²) Ỹ P = 0`, because `E² = u E` (module `SymRetractJacobian`).

## Computations in `R[x, y]/J²`

Several definitions are made in `R[x, y]/J²`:

- `SymAlg2 I F` is the ring `R[x, y]/J²` (a `def`, with a transferred `CommRing` instance), and
  `q2` is its quotient map;
- `φ21` is the projection onto `C`;
- `Jbar` is the image of `J`.

Basic facts:

- `mem_Jbar_of_φ21`: the kernel of `φ21` is `Jbar` (`Ideal.Quotient.factor_ker`);
- `Jbar_mul`: products of two elements of `Jbar` vanish;
- `mulVec_eq_zero_of_map`: if a matrix maps to `0` in `C` and a vector has entries in `Jbar`,
  then their product is `0`.

With `E2`, `Z2` the images of `E`, `Z̃`, and `fv`, `gv`, `hv` the classes of `f`, `𝒢`, `H`:

- `E2_mulVec_fv`: `E f = u f` (from `hE1`, since `ιx(I²) ⊆ J²`);
- `E2_mulVec_gv`: `E 𝒢 = Z̃ f` (`Et_Gk`);
- `hv_eq`: `H = u³ f + u² 𝒢 − (E Z̃) f`.

`U2_smul_E2_mulVec_hv` then shows `u · (E H) = u⁵ f`. Put `w = Z̃ f`; its entries lie in
`Jbar`.

- `E² w = u E w`: the matrix `E² − u E` maps to `0` in `C`.
- `u² w − u E w = 0`: the matrix `(u − E) Z̃ E` maps to `0` in `C`, and
  `((u − E) Z̃ E) f = u (u − E) w`.
- `E H = u⁴ f + u² w − E² w = u⁴ f + u² w − u E w`.
- Multiplying by `u` gives `u⁵ f + u (u² w − u E w) = u⁵ f`.

## Memberships

Write `Mid` for the ideal `(H) + J²`.

- `u5_F_mem`: `u⁵ f_k ∈ Mid`. By the previous identity, `u⁵ f_k − u Σ_l E_kl H_l ∈ J²`.
- `u5_mem_of_mem`: `u⁵ p ∈ Mid` for every `p ∈ I`, because `I` is generated by the `f_k`.
- `u7_Gk_mem`: `u⁷ 𝒢_q ∈ Mid`. Here `u⁷ 𝒢_q = u⁵ H_q − u³ (u⁵ f_q) + Σ_p (E Z̃)_qp (u⁵ f_p)`.
- `kappa_Et_mem`: if `Σ_j κ_j f_j ∈ I²`, then `(κ E)_l ∈ I`. Differentiation gives
  `κ D ∈ I`, and `κ E = (κ D) P`.
- `u_symGen`: `u · Σ_j κ_j y_j = Σ_q κ_q 𝒢_q + Σ_j (κ E)_j y_j`.
- `u8_symGen_mem`: for `κ ∈ K`, `u⁸ · Σ_j κ_j y_j ∈ Mid`. This follows from the previous
  three facts.
- `u8_mem`: `u⁸ G ∈ Mid` for every `G ∈ J`. The colon ideal `Mid : (u⁸)`
  (`Submodule.colon`, `Submodule.mem_colon_singleton`) contains both `ιx(I)` and the generators
  `Σ_j κ_j y_j` of `J`.
