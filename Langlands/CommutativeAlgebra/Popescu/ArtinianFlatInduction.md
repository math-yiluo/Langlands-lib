# The coefficient claim of Stacks Lemma 11.1 for an Artinian local algebra

This module supports the proof of Stacks Project, *Smoothing Ring Maps*, Lemma 11.1
(Tag 07FG), in `ArtinianFlatSubalgebra`. All declarations are in the namespace
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.ArtinianFlat`. It proves the source's "Claim" for
`(L, 𝔪, κ)` by induction over the quotients `L ⧸ 𝔪^{j+1}`. Each step applies
`coeffClaim_of_surjective` (module `ArtinianFlatClaim`).

## Declarations

- `mul_mem_pow`: `a ∈ Iᵐ`, `b ∈ Iⁿ`, `m' ≤ m + n` imply `ab ∈ I^{m'}`.
- `resQ j : L ⧸ 𝔪^{j+1} → κ`: the residue map of the quotient. `resQ_mk` gives
  `resQ j (mk x) = residue x`. `ker_resQ_le` says that its kernel lies in the image of `𝔪`.
- `coeffClaim_zero t`: the claim for `L ⧸ 𝔪 = κ`, with `σ` the identification `κ ≅ L ⧸ 𝔪`
  and `F = k`. The source says "It is clear for `n = 1`".
- `coeffClaim_succ p hV t ht j h`: the claim for `L ⧸ 𝔪^{j+1}` implies it for `L ⧸ 𝔪^{j+2}`.
  It applies `coeffClaim_of_surjective` to `g : L ⧸ 𝔪^{j+2} → L ⧸ 𝔪^{j+1}`. Its kernel
  `I = 𝔪^{j+1}/𝔪^{j+2}` satisfies `I² = 0` and `𝔪 I = 0` because `2(j+1) ≥ j+2` and
  `1 + (j+1) ≥ j+2`. The maximal ideal of `L ⧸ 𝔪^{j+2}` is nilpotent and generated by the
  images of the `tᵢ`.
- `coeffClaim_residue p hV t ht`: let `L` be Artinian local, `𝔪 = (t₁, …, t_d)`, `k` of
  characteristic `p`, and let `hV` say that the kernel of `κ ⊗_k Ω_k → Ω_κ` is finitely
  generated. Then `CoeffClaim k κ L residue t` holds: there are a section `σ` of the residue
  map and a finitely generated subextension `F` of `κ / k` with `k ⊂ Ψ_σ(F[x₁, …, x_d])`.
  **Proof.** Let `𝔪ⁿ = 0` (Artinian local). The claim holds for all `L ⧸ 𝔪^{j+1}` by
  induction on `j`. A last application of `coeffClaim_of_surjective` to
  `L → L ⧸ 𝔪^{n+1}` (kernel `0`) transfers it to `L`.

The source inducts on the least `n` with `𝔪ⁿ = 0` and passes from `Λ / 𝔪^{n−1}` to `Λ`. The
induction here goes up through the quotients of the fixed ring `L` instead. The steps are
the same.

## Source

Stacks Project, *Smoothing Ring Maps* (`smoothing.pdf` in
`stacks-artin-axioms-approximation-2026-11-06`), proof of Lemma 11.1 (Tag 07FG, printed
p. 26): "We will prove the claim by induction on the least integer `n` such that
`𝔪ⁿ = 0`. It is clear for `n = 1`. If `n > 1` set `I = 𝔪^{n−1}` and `Λ' = Λ/I`."
