# Smooth quotients by semisimple derivations

This is an internal algebraic prerequisite by Worker 1296 for the smoothness
of semisimple Lie-element centralizers. It is derived from Mathlib's
eigenspace results and the existing `GradedFixedQuotientSmooth` construction.
It does not yet construct a group-scheme conjugation derivation or identify
its image ideal with an actual centralizer ideal.

For a linear endomorphism of a possibly infinite-dimensional vector space
over a field, `LocallySemisimple` means that each vector lies in a
finite-dimensional invariant subspace on which the restricted endomorphism
is semisimple. `HasEigenDecomposition` means that the supremum of all
eigenspaces is the entire space. Over an algebraically closed field the first
property implies the second. The proof applies the finite-dimensional
semisimplicity theorem on each witnessing subspace and maps its eigenspaces
into those of the original endomorphism.

For a derivation `D : Derivation k A A` of a commutative algebra over a field,
`eigenspaceGradedMonoid` uses the Leibniz identity to show that products of
eigenvectors have degree equal to the sum of the eigenvalues; the unit has
degree zero. When the eigenspaces span, their independence gives the actual
graded-algebra structure `eigenGrading`, indexed by the additive group of `k`.
No finite-dimensionality of `A` is required.

The ideal `imageIdeal D` is generated by every value `D x`.
`imageIdeal_eq_fixedIdeal` identifies it with the ideal generated by all
nonzero-degree homogeneous elements. One inclusion decomposes an arbitrary
element into a sum of eigenvectors. On a zero eigenspace the derivation
vanishes, and on a nonzero eigenspace its value is a scalar multiple of a
generator of the fixed ideal. Conversely, a vector of eigenvalue `μ ≠ 0`
is the derivation of its scalar multiple by `μ⁻¹`.

The formal-smoothness and smoothness theorems then apply the existing graded
fixed-quotient theorems. With spanning eigenspaces they work over any field.
Their final variants derive spanning from local semisimplicity over an
algebraically closed field. Arbitrary characteristics and independent
universes for the field and algebra are preserved; the additive grading may
have characteristic torsion. These results are algebraic prerequisites,
and make no claim to prove Cartan generation or centralizer smoothness.
