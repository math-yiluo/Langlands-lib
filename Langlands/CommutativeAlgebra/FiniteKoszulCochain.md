# The finite subset Koszul complex and its generator homotopies

Author: Worker 1015.

Let `R` be any commutative ring in an arbitrary universe, let `g : ℕ`, and
let `a : Fin g → R`. This internal construction packages the differential
from `FiniteKoszulComplex` and the insertion identities from
`FiniteKoszulHomotopy` in the category of `R`-modules.

`finiteKoszulChain a` is the natural-number chain complex whose degree `n`
term is the function module on the `n`-element subsets of `Fin g`, with the
existing differential from degree `n+1` to `n`. These are finite free
modules because the index type is finite. The square-zero identity supplies
the chain-complex axiom.

`finiteKoszulChainInsertion a j i k` is insertion by `j` when `k = i+1`
and is zero otherwise. `finiteKoszulChainGeneratorHomotopy a j` packages
these components as a homotopy from multiplication by `a j` to zero. Its
commutativity field is the existing positive-degree insertion identity,
or the separate degree-zero identity at the bottom boundary.

`finiteKoszulCochain a` extends this chain complex along the embedding
`n ↦ g-n` into the integer cochain shape. Thus the term in degree `g-n`
is isomorphic to the module on the `n`-element subsets, as recorded by
`finiteKoszulCochainXIso`. Terms below zero vanish because no subset of
`Fin g` has more than `g` elements; terms above `g` lie outside the image
of the embedding. The corresponding strict bounds and termwise finite
freeness give `finiteKoszulCochain_isFiniteFree`.

`finiteKoszulCochainGeneratorHomotopy` extends the chain homotopy. The
proof identifies extension of the scalar identity with the scalar
identity on the extended complex, including degrees outside the embedding.
Consequently the homology map of multiplication by each `a j` is zero.
The theorem `finiteKoszulCochain_span_le_annihilator` concludes that the
ideal generated by the parameters annihilates every homology module.

This is an internal derivation from the existing differential, insertion
identities, and Mathlib's extension and homotopy APIs. It supplies no
exactness assertion and imposes no regularity assumption on the parameters.
