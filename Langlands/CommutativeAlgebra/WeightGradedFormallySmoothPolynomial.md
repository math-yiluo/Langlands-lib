# Weight-graded formally smooth algebras with positive heights are polynomial algebras

This module generalises the Library theorem
`Langlands.Algebra.GradedFormallySmooth.exists_homogeneous_aeval_bijective`
(`Langlands/CommutativeAlgebra/GradedFormallySmoothPolynomial`) from `ℕ`-gradings to gradings
by an arbitrary additive commutative monoid `Λ` equipped with a *height*, an additive
homomorphism `h : Λ →+ ℤ`. Let `k` be a field and `C` a nontrivial `k`-algebra that is of
finite type and formally smooth over `k`. Suppose `C` carries a grading
`𝒜 : Λ → Submodule k C` (`GradedAlgebra 𝒜`) such that

- `h0`: the weight-zero part is `k`, i.e. `𝒜 0 = LinearMap.range (Algebra.linearMap k C)`;
- `hpos`: every nonzero element of `𝒜 χ` with `χ ≠ 0` has `0 < h χ`.

Then `C` is a polynomial algebra over `k` in finitely many homogeneous generators of positive
height, which can be listed in order of nondecreasing height.

The intended application is the coordinate ring of a unipotent group with a torus action
whose weights all pair positively with a fixed cocharacter `ν`: there `Λ` is the character
lattice and `h` is pairing with `ν`.

All declarations are in namespace `Langlands.Algebra.WeightGradedFormallySmooth`.

## Main theorem

`exists_weight_aeval_bijective`: with `[Field k]`, `[CommRing C]`, `[Nontrivial C]`,
`[Algebra k C]`, `[AddCommMonoid Λ]`, `[DecidableEq Λ]`, `[GradedAlgebra 𝒜]`,
`[Algebra.FiniteType k C]`, `[Algebra.FormallySmooth k C]`, `h : Λ →+ ℤ`, and the hypotheses
`h0`, `hpos` above, there exist `n : ℕ`, weights `w : Fin n → Λ` and elements `x : Fin n → C`
such that

- `0 < h (w i)` for every `i`;
- `fun i => h (w i)` is monotone (heights are nondecreasing in the index);
- `x i ∈ 𝒜 (w i)` for every `i`;
- `MvPolynomial.aeval x : MvPolynomial (Fin n) k →ₐ[k] C` is bijective.

As in the `ℕ`-graded Library theorem, `Nontrivial C` cannot be dropped: the zero ring
satisfies the other hypotheses, but no map `MvPolynomial (Fin n) k → 0` is injective.

## Supporting declarations

Below `w : σ → Λ` assigns weights to variables, `weight w m` (Mathlib `Finsupp.weight`) is
the weight of the monomial exponent `m : σ →₀ ℕ`, and `y : σ → C` satisfies
`y j ∈ 𝒜 (w j)`.

- `height_weight h w m`: `h (weight w m) = weight (fun j => h (w j)) m`.
- `eq_zero_of_weight_eq_zero`: if all `h (w j) > 0` and `h (weight w m) = 0`, then `m = 0`.
- `height_lt_of_ne_single`: if all `h (w j) > 0`, `m i ≠ 0` and `m ≠ single i 1`, then
  `h (w i) < h (weight w m)`.
- `eq_single_of_weight_eq`: if all `h (w j) > 0`, `m i ≠ 0` and `weight w m = w i`, then
  `m = single i 1`.
- `decompose_aeval` (`k` a commutative ring): the `χ`-component of `aeval y f` is
  `aeval y (weightedHomogeneousComponent w χ f)`.
- `isWeightedHomogeneous_of_aeval_mem` (`k` a commutative ring): if `aeval y` is injective and
  `aeval y f ∈ 𝒜 χ`, then `f` is weighted homogeneous of weight `χ` for `w`.
- `weightedHomogeneousComponent_zero_eq` (`k` a field): if all `h (w j) > 0`, the weight-zero
  component of `f` is the constant `MvPolynomial.C (f.coeff 0)`.
- `coeff_zero_eq_zero` (`C` nontrivial): if all `h (w j) > 0` and `aeval y f = 0`, then
  `f.coeff 0 = 0`.
- `mem_adjoin_of_coeff_single_ne_zero`: if all `h (w j) > 0`, `aeval y f = 0` and the
  coefficient of `X i` in `f` is nonzero, then `y i` lies in the subalgebra generated by the
  `y j` with `j ≠ i`.
- `HasWeightGenerators 𝒜 h N` (a `Prop`, the new definition): there are `w : Fin N → Λ` and
  `y : Fin N → C` with all `0 < h (w i)`, `y i ∈ 𝒜 (w i)`, and
  `Algebra.adjoin k (Set.range y) = ⊤`.
- `exists_hasWeightGenerators` (`C` of finite type over the field `k`, `h0`, `hpos`):
  `HasWeightGenerators 𝒜 h N` for some `N`.
- `mem_pow_two_of_minimal` (`C` nontrivial): for a family `y : Fin N → C` as in
  `HasWeightGenerators` such that no family of length `M < N` exists, every `f` with
  `aeval y f = 0` lies in `(MvPolynomial.idealOfVars (Fin N) k) ^ 2`.

## Proof

The proof follows the Library's `ℕ`-graded proof, with the total degree of a weight replaced
by its height.

1. *Homogeneous generators* (`exists_hasWeightGenerators`). Take a finite generating set `s`
   of `C`. Each element is the sum of its graded components
   (`DirectSum.sum_support_decompose`). The weight-zero components lie in the image of `k` by
   `h0`. The nonzero components of nonzero weight have positive height by `hpos`. So these
   components generate `C`.
2. *Minimal family.* Let `N` be the least length of such a family (`Nat.find`) and `y` a
   family of that length. Then `aeval y` is surjective
   (`Algebra.adjoin_range_eq_range_aeval`).
3. *Relations lie in `I²`*, where `I = idealOfVars (Fin N) k` (`mem_pow_two_of_minimal`).
   Let `aeval y f = 0`. By `decompose_aeval` each weighted homogeneous component of `f` maps
   to zero. Since all variables have positive height, a monomial of weight `0` is `1`
   (`eq_zero_of_weight_eq_zero`), so the weight-zero component is the constant term and
   `f.coeff 0 = 0` because `algebraMap k C` is injective. If the coefficient `c` of a
   variable `X i` were nonzero, consider the weight-`w i` component of `f`. Any other monomial
   in it that contains `X i` would have height larger than `h (w i)`
   (`height_lt_of_ne_single`), which is impossible. So `y i = -c⁻¹ · (polynomial in the other
   y j)` (`mem_adjoin_of_coeff_single_ne_zero`). Deleting `y i` (via `Fin.succAbove`) gives a
   shorter generating family, contradicting minimality. Hence all coefficients of total degree
   `< 2` vanish, which is `f ∈ I²` (`MvPolynomial.mem_pow_idealOfVars_iff'`). Total degree
   here is the weight for the constant weight `1`; the Library lemma `eq_single_of_weight_le`
   shows that a nonzero exponent of total degree at most `1` is a single variable.
4. *Injectivity.* The Library lemma `aeval_injective_of_ker_le` (formal smoothness plus
   Nakayama's lemma; see the `ℕ`-graded module) gives injectivity of `aeval y` from
   surjectivity and the kernel bound of step 3.
5. *Ordering.* Reindex the variables by the permutation `Tuple.sort (fun i => h (w i))`, which
   makes the heights monotone (`Tuple.monotone_sort`). Composition with the renaming algebra
   isomorphism `renameEquiv` preserves bijectivity.

## Provenance

This is an internal supporting lemma for the proof of the Library proposition
`Langlands.GroupScheme.PositiveTorusTriangularCoordinatesStatement` (task 40747bb3, finite
volume of `G(F)\G(𝔸)¹`), where it is applied to the coordinate ring of the standard unipotent
subgroup, graded by torus characters with height given by pairing with a cocharacter. No
literature source is cited. The statement is the standard fact that a positively graded,
connected, smooth algebra of finite type over a field is a polynomial algebra on homogeneous
generators, here for a grading by a monoid with a positive height. The Lean statement and
proof are a direct generalisation of the Library's `ℕ`-graded theorem, so that the torus
grading by characters can be used without first collapsing it to an `ℕ`-grading.
