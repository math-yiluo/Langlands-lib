# Etale algebras realizing prescribed finite separable residue extensions

Author: Worker 1683.

This module is `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.EtaleResidueExtensions`.
It proves the algebraic realization needed for The Stacks Project, Algebra
Lemma 10.144.3, Tag 00UD. The registered original source is
`stacks-etale-residue-extensions-00UD-02LF-2026-11-05`, file `00UD.html`,
full statement and proof. Both that page and the scheme version in
`02LF.html` have no comments recording corrections to their statements.
No local errata entry for this source was found.

## Exact content

For an arbitrary commutative ring `R`, a prime `𝔭` of `R`, and a specified
finite separable field extension `K` of `𝔭.ResidueField`, the theorem
`exists_etale_residue_extension` constructs an etale `R`-algebra `A`, a
prime `q : PrimeSpectrum A`, and an actual ring equivalence
`e : q.asIdeal.ResidueField ≃+* K`. The prime lies over `𝔭`, and for
every `r : R` the equivalence maps the image of `r` in the new residue
field to its given image in `K`.

An `R`-algebra structure on `K` and its scalar-tower compatibility with
`𝔭.ResidueField` are explicit inputs. They assert that this is the
original residue-field extension, without imposing a different base map.
The equation on `R` determines the equation on the whole residue field:
Mathlib's `Ideal.ResidueField.ringHom_ext` proves equality of maps from
`κ(𝔭)` once their composites with `R → κ(𝔭)` agree. All carriers lie in
the same arbitrary universe `u`. No domain, Noetherian, reducedness, or
characteristic hypothesis is assumed.

## Implemented proof

Choose a primitive element `θ` of `K/κ(𝔭)` and its monic minimal polynomial
`mbar`. Apply the published `EtaleLocalSections.exists_away_lift` to its
finite set of coefficients. This gives a principal localization
`Rb = R[1/b]`, a map `β : Rb → κ(𝔭)` extending the original residue map,
and lifts of every coefficient. The monic-polynomial lifting lemma gives a
monic polynomial `m` over `Rb` mapping to `mbar`.

Let `E` be the standard etale pair `(m, m')` and let `A = E.Ring`.
This algebra is etale over `Rb`, hence over `R`. Make `K` an `Rb`-algebra
through `β`. The element `θ` is a root of `m` under this coefficient map,
and the value of `m'` at `θ` is nonzero because the minimal polynomial is
separable. The standard etale universal property gives an `R`-algebra map
`ρ : A → K` sending the distinguished generator to `θ`.

Let `q` be the kernel of `ρ`. Injectivity of `κ(𝔭) → K` proves that `q`
lies over `𝔭`. The residue-field universal property extends `ρ` to a
field homomorphism `η : κ(q) → K`. The induced base map
`κ(𝔭) → κ(q)` makes `η` a `κ(𝔭)`-algebra map; its compatibility is
checked on `R` using the residue-field extensionality theorem. The field
range of `η` contains `θ`, since `ρ` sends the generator to `θ`. It
therefore contains the entire extension generated by `θ`, which is `K`.
Thus `η` is surjective, and injectivity of a field homomorphism makes it
the desired ring equivalence. Its equation on `R` follows directly from
`ρ` being an `R`-algebra map.

## Relation to the source

The source clears denominators by scaling the primitive element and then
forms a standard etale algebra over `R`. The implemented proof keeps the
primitive element unchanged and lifts its coefficients over a single
principal localization of `R`. Composition with that localization gives
an etale algebra over the original `R`, so the conclusion has the same
arbitrary-base scope. The surjectivity of the residue-field map is proved
explicitly by the primitive-element field-range argument. This module
provides the algebraic input; its scheme construction is in the companion
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.EtaleResidueExtensions`.
