# Finite-type unramified models of etale approximations

This module turns the étale approximation predicate
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.EtaleCompletionApproximation` into an approximate
solution in a finite-type algebra over the polynomial ring. It formalizes two
steps of the proof of Stacks Project Lemma 98.10.1 (Tag 07XB, `artin.pdf` printed
pp. 15–16):

- the application of Smoothing Ring Maps, Theorem 16.13.2, to a finite system of
  equations;
- the final paragraph, which replaces an algebra essentially of finite type by a
  finite-type one.

Instead of identifying the completion of the étale algebra with `P̂`, the module
records the weaker property used later: `T₀ = S + u₀ ^ 2`. The provenance is
derived.

Let `R : Type u` be a Noetherian commutative ring, `ι : Type u` a finite type,
`S = MvPolynomial ι R`, and `𝔪` a maximal ideal of `S`. Let
`P = Localization.AtPrime 𝔪` and `P̂ = AdicCompletion (maximalIdeal P) P`.

`exists_model` assumes:

- `happrox : EtaleCompletionApproximation P`;
- a finite type `V`, a family `val : V → P̂`, and finitely many polynomials
  `rel k : MvPolynomial V R` vanishing at `val`;
- an order `N : ℕ`.

Polynomials are evaluated through `S → P̂` on coefficients. The theorem gives a
finite-type `R`-algebra `T₀ : Type u` with `σT : S →ₐ[R] T₀`,
`vT : MvPolynomial V R →ₐ[R] T₀` and a ring map `ι₀ : T₀ →+* P̂` such that:

1. `ι₀ (σT s) = algebraMap S P̂ s` for every `s`;
2. `vT (rel k) = 0` for every `k`, so the relations hold exactly in `T₀`;
3. `ι₀ (vT (X v)) - val v ∈ (maximalIdeal P̂) ^ N` for every `v`;
4. every `t : T₀` is congruent to some `σT s` modulo `(ι₀⁻¹ (maximalIdeal P̂)) ^ 2`.

## Proof

Let `C` be the `P`-subalgebra of `P̂` generated by the values `val v`. It is of finite
type, so the approximation predicate applies to its inclusion. This gives an étale
`P`-algebra `E` and `P`-algebra maps `g : C → E` and `ι : E → P̂` with
`ι ∘ g ≡ id` modulo `(maximalIdeal P̂) ^ N`. The `R`-polynomials evaluated at the
generators of `C` vanish because `C → P̂` is injective. Hence their images under `g`
vanish in `E`.

Choose finitely many `P`-algebra generators of `E`, and let
`W = ι ⊕ V ⊕ (generators)`. The ring map `ν : MvPolynomial W R → E` sends the first
variables to the images of the variables of `S`, the second to `g (val v)`, and the
third to the generators. Set `T₀ = MvPolynomial W R / ker ν`. Let `jT : T₀ → E` be
the injective induced map and `ι₀ = ι ∘ jT`. Properties 1–3 follow from the
construction and injectivity of `jT`.

For property 4, every element of `E` becomes an element of `T₀` after
multiplication by the image of some `w ∉ 𝔪`. This holds for generators, for
elements of `P` written as fractions, and is stable under sums and products
(`Algebra.adjoin_induction`). `E` is formally unramified and of finite type over `P`
because it is étale. Since `T₀ = σT(S) + u₀` by approximation modulo the maximal
ideal of `P̂`, `UnramifiedSubalgebraSquare.exists_sub_mem_sq` gives property 4.
