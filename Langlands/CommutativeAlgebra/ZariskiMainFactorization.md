# A finite subalgebra agreeing with a quasi-finite algebra away from one element

This module restates Zariski's Main Theorem in a factorization form. It is
internal infrastructure for the local finiteness part of the statement that
affine analytification detects quasi-finite maps. The algebraic input is
Mathlib's `Algebra.ZariskisMainProperty.of_finiteType`, which formalizes
Stacks Project Tag 00Q9. No external premise is introduced, and no further
source passage is formalized.

Throughout, `K`, `A` and `B` are commutative rings, `A` and `B` are
`K`-algebras of finite type, and `f : A →ₐ[K] B` is a `K`-algebra map.

- `exists_finite_subalgebra_pow_mul_mem`: assume `f.toRingHom` is
  quasi-finite (Mathlib's `RingHom.QuasiFinite`) and let `q` be a prime of
  `B`. Then there are a `K`-subalgebra `C` of `B` and an element `r` with
  the following properties.
  - `C` contains the image of `f`.
  - The corestriction `f.codRestrict C _ : A →ₐ[K] C` is finite as a ring
    map (`RingHom.Finite`).
  - `C` is of finite type over `K`.
  - `r ∈ C` and `r ∉ q`.
  - For every `b : B` there is `m` with `r ^ m * b ∈ C`.

  The last property means that `C[1/r] → B[1/r]` is surjective. It is also
  injective because `C` is a subring, so `C[1/r] = B[1/r]`.

Proof. Make `B` an `A`-algebra through `f`. It is of finite type over `A`
(`Algebra.FiniteType.of_restrictScalars_finiteType`) and quasi-finite at
`q`, because quasi-finiteness passes to localizations. Zariski's Main
Theorem in the form `Algebra.zariskisMainProperty_iff` gives `r ∉ q`,
integral over `A`, together with exponents `m x` such that each
`r ^ m x * x` is integral over `A`. Choose finitely many generators `s` of
`B` over `A`. Let `D` be the `A`-subalgebra generated by `r` and the
elements `r ^ m x * x` for `x ∈ s`. Its generators are finitely many and
integral, so `D` is a finitely generated `A`-module (`fg_adjoin_of_finite`).

Mathlib's `Subalgebra.saturation` of `D` with respect to the powers of `r`
is a subalgebra containing every generator `x ∈ s`. It is therefore all of
`B`, which is the last property. Take `C` to be `D` with scalars restricted
to `K`. The corestriction of `f` is the structure map `A → D`, hence finite.
Finite type over `K` follows by composing with `K → A`.

This is the same construction as Mathlib's
`Algebra.ZariskisMainProperty.exists_fg_and_exists_notMem_and_awayMap_bijective`.
That lemma works over the base ring of the algebra, whereas here the subalgebra
is over `K` and the finiteness is over `A`.
