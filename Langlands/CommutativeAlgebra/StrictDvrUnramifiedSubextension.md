# Finite unramified subextensions of a strict trait

A finite Galois extension of the original fraction field that
embeds into the fraction field of a uniformizer-preserving strict
trait has a finite étale model over the original DVR. The model
uses its integral closure in the supplied finite field extension.

The integral closure maps into the selected DVR by integral
closedness. At the prime cut out by its maximal ideal, ramification
index different from one would put the original maximal ideal in
the square of that prime. Under the map to the selected DVR this
would put its uniformizer in the square of its maximal ideal,
contrary to irreducibility of the uniformizer. Galois conjugacy
gives ramification index one at each prime over the base closed
point. Residue characteristic zero gives separability at those
primes and at the generic point. Flatness and finite presentation
of the integral closure then promote unramifiedness to étaleness.

This derived criterion is used to extend finite covers appearing
in the generic monodromy argument from the original DVR.
