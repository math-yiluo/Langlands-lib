# Positively graded formally smooth algebras are polynomial algebras

This module proves the following theorem from commutative algebra. Let `k` be a field and
`C` a nontrivial `k`-algebra that is of finite type and formally smooth over `k`. Suppose `C`
carries an `ℕ`-grading `𝒜 : ℕ → Submodule k C` (`GradedAlgebra 𝒜`) whose degree-zero part is
`k`, i.e. `𝒜 0 = LinearMap.range (Algebra.linearMap k C)`. Then `C` is a polynomial algebra
over `k` in finitely many homogeneous generators of positive degree.

All declarations are in namespace `Langlands.Algebra.GradedFormallySmooth`.

## Main theorem

`exists_homogeneous_aeval_bijective`: under the hypotheses above (`[Field k]`,
`[CommRing C]`, `[Nontrivial C]`, `[Algebra k C]`, `[Algebra.FiniteType k C]`,
`[Algebra.FormallySmooth k C]`, `[GradedAlgebra 𝒜]`, and `h0`), there exist `n : ℕ`, degrees
`d : Fin n → ℕ` and elements `x : Fin n → C` such that every `d i` is positive, every `x i`
lies in `𝒜 (d i)`, and `MvPolynomial.aeval x : MvPolynomial (Fin n) k →ₐ[k] C` is bijective.
The universes of `k` and `C` are independent.

The hypothesis `Nontrivial C` cannot be dropped. The zero ring satisfies every other
hypothesis (every `𝒜 d` is the zero module), but no map `MvPolynomial (Fin n) k → 0` is
injective.

## Supporting declarations

Below, `w : σ → ℕ` is a weight function on the variables and `y : σ → C` a family with
`y j ∈ 𝒜 (w j)` for all `j`.

- `eq_single_of_weight_le`: if all weights `w j` are positive, `m i ≠ 0`, and the weight of the
  monomial exponent `m` is at most `w i`, then `m = Finsupp.single i 1`.
- `decompose_aeval`: for a commutative ring `k`, the degree-`d` graded component of
  `aeval y f` is `aeval y` of the weighted homogeneous component of `f` of weight `d`.
- `coeff_zero_eq_zero` (with `k` a field and `C` nontrivial): if all weights are positive and
  `aeval y f = 0`, then the constant coefficient of `f` is zero.
- `mem_adjoin_of_coeff_single_ne_zero` (with `k` a field): if all weights are positive,
  `aeval y f = 0`, and the coefficient of `X i` in `f` is nonzero, then `y i` lies in the
  subalgebra generated by the `y j` with `j ≠ i`.
- `HasHomogeneousGenerators 𝒜 N` (a `Prop`): there are `e : Fin N → ℕ` and `y : Fin N → C`
  with all `e i > 0`, `y i ∈ 𝒜 (e i)`, and `Algebra.adjoin k (Set.range y) = ⊤`.
- `exists_hasHomogeneousGenerators`: if `C` is of finite type over the field `k` and `h0`
  holds, then `HasHomogeneousGenerators 𝒜 N` for some `N`.
- `mem_pow_two_of_minimal` (with `C` nontrivial): if `y : Fin N → C` is a homogeneous
  generating family as in `HasHomogeneousGenerators`, and no such family of length `M < N`
  exists, then every `f` with `aeval y f = 0` lies in `(MvPolynomial.idealOfVars (Fin N) k) ^ 2`.
- `aeval_injective_of_ker_le`: if `C` is formally smooth over the field `k`,
  `aeval x : MvPolynomial (Fin n) k →ₐ[k] C` is surjective, and its kernel lies in
  `(idealOfVars (Fin n) k) ^ 2`, then `aeval x` is injective.

## Proof

Write `P = MvPolynomial (Fin n) k` and `I = idealOfVars (Fin n) k`, the ideal generated by
the variables.

1. *Homogeneous generators.* Take a finite generating set `s` of `C`. The nonzero-degree
   graded components of the elements of `s` are homogeneous of positive degree. The
   degree-zero components lie in the image of `k` by `h0`. Each element of `s` is the sum of
   its components (`DirectSum.sum_support_decompose`), so these components generate `C`
   (`exists_hasHomogeneousGenerators`).
2. *Minimal family.* Let `N` be the least length of such a family (`Nat.find`), and
   `y : Fin N → C` a family of that length. Then `aeval y` is surjective
   (`Algebra.adjoin_range_eq_range_aeval`).
3. *Relations lie in `I²`.* Let `aeval y f = 0`. By `decompose_aeval`, the weighted
   homogeneous components of `f` also map to zero. The weight-zero component is the
   constant `C (f.coeff 0)`, so `f.coeff 0 = 0` because `algebraMap k C` is injective (`C`
   is nontrivial). If the coefficient `c` of a variable `X i` were nonzero, consider the
   weight-`w i` component of `f`. Since weights are positive, its monomials other than `c X i`
   do not involve `X i` (`eq_single_of_weight_le`). So `y i = -c⁻¹ · (polynomial in the other
   y j)`. Deleting `y i` (via `Fin.succAbove`) would give a shorter generating family,
   contradicting minimality. Hence all coefficients of total degree `< 2` vanish, which
   is `f ∈ I²` (`MvPolynomial.mem_pow_idealOfVars_iff'`).
4. *Injectivity from formal smoothness* (`aeval_injective_of_ker_le`). Let `K` be the kernel
   of `φ = aeval x`, with `K ≤ I²`. The induced surjection `ψ : P ⧸ K² → C` has square-zero
   kernel. Formal smoothness (`Algebra.FormallySmooth.liftOfSurjective`) gives a section
   `s : C → P ⧸ K²` of `ψ`. Lift each `s (x i)` to `y i ∈ P` and set `τ = aeval y : P → P`.
   Then `φ ∘ τ = φ`, so `τ a - a ∈ K` for all `a`. Also `τ` maps `K` into `K²`, since
   `τ` reduces to `s ∘ φ` modulo `K²`. Because `K ≤ I`, `τ` preserves `I`. A product
   computation (`Submodule.mul_induction_on`) gives `τ f - f ∈ I * K` for `f ∈ I * I`. For
   `f ∈ K ≤ I²` this yields `f = τ f - (τ f - f) ∈ I * K`, since `τ f ∈ K² ≤ I * K`.
   Hence `K ≤ I • K`. `K` is finitely generated (`P` is Noetherian), so Nakayama's lemma
   (`Submodule.exists_mem_and_smul_eq_self_of_fg_of_le_smul`) gives `r ∈ I` with `r f = f`
   for all `f ∈ K`. Then `(1 - r) f = 0`. Here `1 - r ≠ 0` because `r` has zero constant
   coefficient, and `P` is a domain, so `f = 0`.
5. Steps 2 and 3 give the hypotheses of step 4 for the family `y`, which proves the main
   theorem.

## Provenance

This is an internal supporting lemma. It is part of the explicit Hopf-algebra route of the
proof task for `Langlands.GroupScheme.RelativeRoot.CyclicWeightFiltrationStatement`, where it
shows that the coordinate rings of the cyclic-tail subgroups, which are positively graded by
a torus action, are polynomial rings on homogeneous coordinates. No literature source is
cited. The statement is a standard fact: a positively graded, connected, smooth algebra of
finite type over a field is a polynomial algebra on homogeneous generators. The Lean proof
above is self-contained modulo Mathlib.
