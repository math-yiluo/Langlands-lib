# Coherence of finitely presented valuation algebras

This module is an internal derivation from the existing
`FiniteFreeSubmoduleCoherent` predicate, the imported polynomial coherence
proof, Mathlib's finite presentations, and the imported etale chart dimension
bound. It introduces no new coherence or regularity predicate.

`submodulePresentationPullbackMap f K` is the actual linear map
`K.comap f → K` obtained by restricting `f` on its source and target.
The accompanying surjectivity theorem restricts a chosen preimage of each
element of `K` when `f` is surjective. These constructions allow independent
universes for the source and target modules.

For a commutative ring `R` satisfying finite-free-submodule coherence,
`finitePresentation_fg_submodule_of_finitePresentation` proves that a finitely
generated submodule `K` of any finitely presented `R`-module `M` is finitely
presented. The universe of `M` is independent of that of `R`.
Choose an actual finite free surjection `f` onto `M` and pull `K` back to
`L = K.comap f`. The image of `L` is `K`; its intersection with the kernel
is the kernel of `f`, which is finitely generated by finite presentation.
Thus `L` is finitely generated, hence finitely presented by coherence.
The restricted surjection `L → K` has a kernel whose injective image in the
finite free module is exactly the kernel of `f`. That restricted kernel is
therefore finitely generated, so its quotient `K` is finitely presented.

For a commutative `R`-algebra `S` that is finitely presented as an
`R`-module, `finitePresentation_of_finitePresentation_restrictScalars`
proves that any compatible `S`-module finitely presented over `R` is also
finitely presented over `S`. The universes of both rings and the module
are independent. A finite free `S`-module surjects onto the given module.
Its restriction to `R` is finite, and finite presentation of the target
makes the kernel finitely generated over `R`, hence over `S`. The
finite free `S`-presentation then proves the conclusion.

`finiteFreeSubmoduleCoherent_of_module_finitePresentation` applies these two
facts to show that a module-finitely-presented algebra over a coherent
coefficient ring is coherent. Indeed, a finite `S`-submodule of `S^n` is
finite over `R`, and `S^n` is finitely presented over `R`. The first result
makes that submodule finitely presented over `R`; the second changes
the coefficient ring to `S`. The actual restriction-of-scalars
equivalence identifies the two submodule carriers.

For an arbitrary valuation domain `R`,
`finiteFreeSubmoduleCoherent_of_finitePresentation_of_valuationRing`
proves coherence of every finitely presented commutative `R`-algebra `S`,
in an independent universe. Its algebra presentation supplies a genuine
surjection `P → S` from a finite-variable polynomial ring and a finitely
generated kernel. Consequently `S` is finitely presented as a `P`-module.
The imported polynomial coherence theorem and module permanence give
coherence of `S`. This statement needs no flatness assumption on `S`.

Finally,
`finitelyPresentedRegularRing_etale_mvPolynomial_of_valuationRing`
combines that coherence result with the imported finite-projective-dimension
bound on an etale algebra over a finite-variable polynomial ring. Here the
valuation domain, variable type, and etale algebra share one arbitrary
universe, and their displayed algebra tower is compatible. Finite
presentation over `R` follows by transitivity. The result is exactly the
existing conjunction `FinitelyPresentedRegularRing S`: finite-free-submodule
coherence and finite projective dimension for every finitely presented
module in the universe of `S`.

All valuation ranks and fields are included. This module proves algebraic
permanence and the displayed chart result; it makes no smooth-local
factorization, Picard extension, or geometric line-bundle extension claim.
