# Scalar extension of derivations and their image-ideal quotients

This internal supporting construction derives scalar extension and smooth
descent for image-ideal quotients from tensor products, the imported
semisimple-derivation quotient theorem, and Mathlib's faithfully flat smooth
descent. It uses no additional assertion from the literature.

For commutative rings `R`, `A`, and `S`, with `R`-algebra structures on the
last two, `baseChange S D` extends an `R`-derivation `D : A → A` to an
`S`-derivation of `S ⊗[R] A`. Its underlying linear map is the scalar
extension of `D`, and it sends `s ⊗ a` to `s ⊗ D(a)`. The proof verifies
Leibniz on pure tensors and extends the identity by tensor induction in
both arguments.

`span_range_baseChange` identifies the ideal generated by all values of
the extended derivation with the extension of the original image ideal
along `a ↦ 1 ⊗ a`. In one direction tensor induction expresses each
extended value as a sum of multiples of these generators. In the other,
each extended generator is itself a value at `1 ⊗ a`.
`quotientBaseChangeEquiv` combines this equality with the tensor-quotient
equivalence. `imageIdeal_baseChange` records the field specialization using
the imported `imageIdeal` notation.

For a field `k`, a smooth `k`-algebra `A`, and a faithfully flat field
extension `K`, `smooth_quotient_imageIdeal_of_eigenBaseChange` assumes an
eigenvector decomposition of the extended derivation. The imported
image-ideal theorem makes its quotient smooth over `K`. The quotient
equivalence transfers smoothness to `K ⊗[k] (A / imageIdeal D)`, and
faithfully flat descent gives smoothness of the original quotient over
`k`. Over an algebraically closed `K`, the final theorem obtains the
required eigenvector decomposition from local finite dimensional
semisimplicity.

All three underlying types have independent universes. The construction
and ideal comparisons require neither flatness nor fields; the smooth
descent theorems retain the displayed field and faithful-flatness
hypotheses.
