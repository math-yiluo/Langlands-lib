# Regular local charts from a presentation of a complete discrete valuation ring

Module `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.ArithmeticRegularLocalApproximation.Presentation`.

This module proves the core of
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.ArithmeticRegularLocalApproximationStatement` in a form that
covers all characteristics at once. The complete discrete valuation ring `W'` is presented as
`Λ[X] / (F)` with `X ↦ ϖ`, over a Noetherian local ring `Λ` that is regular over a ring
`R₀` generated by the integers. Popescu's theorem (`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.popescuFactorization`)
factors the inclusion of the finitely generated coefficient subalgebra into `Λ` through a
smooth `R₀`-algebra `D`. The chart is cut out in a localization of `D[X]` by a lift `F_D` of
`F` along `D → Λ`. Deligne, SGA 7 I, Exposé I, Lemma 0.5.1 uses Néron desingularization for
finite-data approximation over the bases of §§0.5.2–0.5.3 (pp. I-4–I-5). In equal characteristic
the construction here uses `Λ = W'` and `F = X - ϖ`.
In mixed characteristic `Λ` is a Cohen ring and `F` is an Eisenstein polynomial, generalizing
the Eisenstein presentation over the Witt vectors of a perfect residue field in §0.5.3.

## `exists_map_eq_of_coeff_mem`

If every coefficient of `q ∈ Λ[X]` lies in an `R₀`-subalgebra `E ⊆ Λ`, then `q` is the image of
a polynomial in `E[X]` (`Polynomial.lifts_iff_coeff_lifts`).

## `chartConclusion_of_presentation`

Hypotheses:

- `W' : Type u` is a discrete valuation ring complete for its maximal ideal, and `ϖ ∈ W'` is
  irreducible;
- `R₀ : Type u` is a regular ring all of whose elements are integers;
- `Λ : Type u` is a Noetherian local ring with an `R₀`-algebra structure such that
  `RegularAlgebra R₀ Λ` holds, and `ψ : Λ → W'` is a local homomorphism;
- `F ∈ Λ[X]` is monic, `F(ϖ) = 0` under `eval₂ ψ ϖ`, and its constant coefficient is not in
  `𝔪_Λ²`;
- every `y ∈ W'` is `q(ϖ)` for some `q ∈ Λ[X]`, and `F` divides every `q ∈ Λ[X]` with
  `q(ϖ) = 0`.

Conclusion: for every finite type `ℤ`-algebra `C` and ring map `F' : C → Frac W'`,
`ChartConclusion W' C F'` holds (module
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.ArithmeticRegularLocalApproximation.Chart`).

Proof.

1. Choose a finite set `s` generating `C` over `ℤ`. For each `c`, write
   `F'(c) · ϖ^{n_c} = a_c` with `a_c ∈ W'` (`exists_mul_pow_eq_algebraMap`) and
   `a_c = q_c(ϖ)` with `q_c ∈ Λ[X]`.
2. Let `T ⊆ Λ` be the finite set of coefficients of `F` and of the `q_c` for `c ∈ s`, and
   `E = R₀[T] ⊆ Λ`, a finite type `R₀`-algebra (`Subalgebra.fg_adjoin_finset`). Popescu's
   theorem applied to the inclusion `E → Λ` gives a smooth `R₀`-algebra `D` and `R₀`-algebra
   maps `g : E → D` and `h : D → Λ` with `h ∘ g` the inclusion. `D` is Noetherian
   (`Algebra.FiniteType.isNoetherianRing`) and its localizations at primes are regular
   (`SmoothDVRFactorialLocalizationsProof.regularLocal_localization_of_smooth`), so `D` is a
   regular ring.
3. `F = F_E` mapped to `Λ[X]` for some `F_E ∈ E[X]`, which is monic because `E → Λ` is
   injective (`Polynomial.monic_of_injective`), and each `q_c` likewise comes from some
   `q'_c ∈ E[X]`. Let `F_D ∈ D[X]` be the image of `F_E`. Then `F_D(ϖ) = F(ϖ) = 0` under
   `eval₂ (ψ ∘ h) ϖ`, and `h` of its constant coefficient is the constant coefficient of `F`.
4. `exists_regularChart` (module
   `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.ArithmeticRegularLocalApproximation.LocalChart`) gives a
   regular local ring `P` with finitely generated residue field, a local map `hP : P → W'` and
   `π : D[X] → P` with `hP ∘ π = eval₂ (ψ ∘ h) ϖ` and `π F_D = 0`.
5. Let `Θ = eval₂ (ψ ∘ (E → Λ)) ϖ : E[X] → W'` and `Ψ = π ∘ (E[X] → D[X])`. Then
   `hP ∘ Ψ = Θ`, checked on constants and on `X` (`Polynomial.ringHom_ext`). If `Θ p = 0`,
   then the image of `p` in `Λ[X]` vanishes at `ϖ`, so it is divisible by `F`; since `F_E` is
   monic and `E → Λ` injective, `F_E` divides `p` in `E[X]` (`Polynomial.map_dvd_map`), and
   then `Ψ p` is a multiple of `π F_D = 0`. So `ker Θ ⊆ ker Ψ`.
6. With `a₀ = X` one has `Θ X = ϖ ≠ 0`, and for `c ∈ s`,
   `F'(c) · ϖ^{n_c} = a_c = Θ (q'_c)`. `exists_away_factorization` (module
   `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.ArithmeticRegularLocalApproximation.Factorization`) gives
   `jAway₀ : P[1/t] → Frac W'` and `g₀ : C → P[1/t]` for `t = Ψ X`, with the required
   compatibilities.
7. Since `hP t = Θ X = ϖ` is irreducible, `chartConclusion_of_regularLocal` (module
   `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.ArithmeticRegularLocalApproximation.Chart`) completes `P` and
   concludes.
