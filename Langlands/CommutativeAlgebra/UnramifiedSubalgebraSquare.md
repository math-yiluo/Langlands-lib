# Unramified algebras over a local ring and finite-type models

This internal module supplies the unramifiedness input of the reduction. Stacks
Lemma 98.10.1 (Tag 07XB, `artin.pdf` printed p. 15, item (1) of its proof) uses
that the étale algebra `B` has residue field `κ(m′)` at `m_B` and completion `P`.
The reduction below needs only a weaker consequence for a finite-type model of the
étale algebra: modulo the square of the relevant ideal, the model is generated by
the polynomial ring. No flatness is used. The provenance is derived from that
step. The formulation is internal.

## Elements dividing their squares

`exists_sub_sq_mul_mem` assumes `P : Type u` is local and `E : Type v` is a
formally unramified, finite-type `P`-algebra. For every `x : E` it gives `y` with
`x - x ^ 2 * y ∈ (maximalIdeal P).map (algebraMap P E)`.

Let `E′ = E / (maximalIdeal P) E`. It is an algebra over the residue field `κ` of
`P` and is formally unramified and essentially of finite type over `κ`. Hence it is
finite and reduced over `κ` (`FormallyUnramified.finite_of_free`,
`FormallyUnramified.isReduced_of_field`), so it is Artinian. These instance steps
follow Mathlib's proof of `FormallyUnramified.isField_quotient_map_maximalIdeal`.
In the Artinian ring `E′`, some `n` and `y` satisfy `x ^ (n + 1) * y = x ^ n`
(`IsArtinian.exists_pow_succ_smul_dvd`). Then `(x * (1 - x * y)) ^ (n + 1) = 0`,
and since `E′` is reduced, `x = x ^ 2 * y` in `E′`.

## Finite-type models

Let `𝔪` be a maximal ideal of a commutative ring `S`, and let `E` be a formally
unramified, finite-type algebra over `P = Localization.AtPrime 𝔪`. Let `T₀` be a
ring with `σT : S →+* T₀`, and let `j : T₀ →+* E` be injective with
`j (σT s)` equal to the image of `s` in `E`. Assume:

- every `e : E` has `w ∉ 𝔪` and `t` with `j t = j (σT w) * e`;
- `u₀` is an ideal of `T₀` with `T₀ = σT(S) + u₀`;
- `𝔪.map σT ≤ u₀`.

Then `exists_sub_mem_sq` proves `T₀ = σT(S) + u₀ ^ 2`: for every `t` there is
`s` with `t - σT s ∈ u₀ ^ 2`.

First, every `t ∈ u₀` lies in `𝔪 T₀ + u₀ ^ 2`. Apply the previous lemma to `j t`.
Clear the denominators of `y` and of the element of `(maximalIdeal P) E`. The
latter step uses induction on the span of the image of `𝔪`. Injectivity of `j`
then gives
`σT (w * wy) * t = σT w * t ^ 2 * ty + σT wy * tz` with `tz ∈ 𝔪 T₀`. Since
`w * wy ∉ 𝔪`, choose `v′` and `i ∈ 𝔪` with `v′ * (w * wy) + i = 1`. Then
`t = σT v′ * (σT (w * wy) * t) + σT i * t` lies in `𝔪 T₀ + u₀ ^ 2`.

Second, every element of `𝔪 T₀` is congruent to an element of `σT(S)` modulo
`u₀ ^ 2`. This uses induction on the span together with `T₀ = σT(S) + u₀` and
`𝔪 T₀ ≤ u₀`. Combining both facts with `T₀ = σT(S) + u₀` gives the conclusion.
