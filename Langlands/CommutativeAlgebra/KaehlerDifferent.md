# Kähler differentials and the different of a Dedekind extension

Module `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.KaehlerDifferent`, namespace
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.KaehlerDifferent`. It imports only `Langlands.MathlibBase`.

The main result, `length_kaehler_eq_count`, is Serre, *Local Fields*, III §7, Prop. 14
(pp. 59–60) localized at a prime: for an extension `A ⊆ B` of Dedekind type with separable
residue extension at `P`, the module of differentials `Ω_{B_P/A}` has length equal to the
exponent of `P` in the different `𝔇_{B/A}`. Serre proves Prop. 14 after completion, where §6,
Prop. 12 gives a monogenic presentation `B̂ = Â[x]`. The proof here does not complete: it chooses
`x ∈ B` as in Serre III §6, Exercise 2 (p. 59), so that the conductor of `A[x]` in `B` is prime to
`P`; then `B_P` is a localization of `A[x]`, Mathlib's `conductor_mul_differentIdeal` gives
`𝔇_{B/A} B_P = g'(x) B_P` for the minimal polynomial `g` of `x`, and `Ω_{B_P/A}` is
`B_P ⧸ (g'(x))`, generated by `dx`.

## Monogenic algebras and their localizations

Variables: commutative rings `A`, `C`, `O` with a tower `A → C → O`, an element `x : C` with
`Algebra.adjoin A {x} = ⊤`, and a polynomial `g : A[X]` with `aeval x g = 0` that divides every
polynomial vanishing at `x` (hypothesis `hg`).

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `exists_aeval_eq` | every `c : C` is `aeval x h` for some `h : A[X]` |
| `derivIdeal O x g` | the ideal of `O` generated by the image of `g'(x)` |
| `mk_aeval_derivative_eq` | if `h₁(x) = h₂(x)` then `h₁'(x) ≡ h₂'(x)` modulo `derivIdeal O x g` |
| `quotDerivation O hx hg hgx` | the `A`-derivation `C → O ⧸ (g'(x))`, `h(x) ↦ h'(x)` |
| `quotDerivation_apply` | its value on `aeval x h` is the class of `aeval x h'` |
| `span_D_eq_top` | if `O` is a localization of `C` (`IsLocalization M O`), then `dx` spans `Ω[O⁄A]` |
| `aeval_derivative_smul_D_eq_zero` | `g'(x) • dx = 0` in `Ω[O⁄A]` |
| `kaehlerToQuot O M hx hg hgx` | the `O`-linear map `Ω[O⁄A] → O ⧸ (g'(x))` sending `dx` to `1` |
| `kaehlerToQuot_D` | it sends `dx` to `1` |
| `torsionOf_D_eq` | the annihilator of `dx` in `Ω[O⁄A]` is `derivIdeal O x g` |
| `quotEquivKaehler O M hx hg hgx` | `O ⧸ (g'(x)) ≃ₗ[O] Ω[O⁄A]` |
| `length_kaehler_eq` | `Module.length O Ω[O⁄A] = Module.length O (O ⧸ derivIdeal O x g)` |

`quotDerivation` is defined through chosen polynomial preimages (`exists_aeval_eq`); it is well
defined and additive, `A`-linear and Leibniz by `mk_aeval_derivative_eq`, since two preimages
differ by a multiple `q g` of `g` and `(q g)'(x) = q(x) g'(x)`. `span_D_eq_top` combines
`KaehlerDifferential.span_range_map_derivation_of_isLocalization` with
`Derivation.map_aeval` (`d(h(x)) = h'(x) dx`). `kaehlerToQuot` is the base change to `O` of the
map `Ω[C⁄A] → O ⧸ (g'(x))` induced by `quotDerivation`, composed with the inverse of
`KaehlerDifferential.tensorKaehlerEquivOfFormallyEtale` for the formally étale localization
`C → O`. In `torsionOf_D_eq`, an element `a` with `a • dx = 0` is sent by `kaehlerToQuot` to
the class of `a`, so `a ∈ (g'(x))`; the converse is `aeval_derivative_smul_D_eq_zero`.
`quotEquivKaehler` composes `Submodule.quotEquivOfEq` (by `torsionOf_D_eq`),
`Ideal.quotTorsionOfEquivSpanSingleton` and `LinearEquiv.ofTop` (by `span_D_eq_top`).

## Lengths over a localization of a Dedekind domain

`length_quotient_map_eq_count`: for a Dedekind domain `B`, a prime `P ≠ ⊥`, the localization `O`
of `B` at `P` and an ideal `I ≠ ⊥` of `B`,
`Module.length O (O ⧸ I O) = (normalizedFactors I).count P`. Proof: write `I = P ^ c * J` with
`J ⊄ P` (`Ideal.eq_prime_pow_mul_coprime`); in `O` the factor `J` becomes the unit ideal
(`IsLocalization.AtPrime.map_eq_top_of_not_le`) and `P` the maximal ideal
(`IsLocalization.AtPrime.map_eq_maximalIdeal`); `O` is a discrete valuation ring
(`IsLocalization.AtPrime.isDiscreteValuationRing_of_dedekind_domain`) and
`IsDiscreteValuationRing.length_quotient_pow_maximalIdeal` gives length `c`.

## The conductor and the choice of a generator

Variables: commutative rings `A`, `B`, `O` with a tower `A → B → O`.

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `isLocalization_adjoin` | if `conductor A x ⊄ P` for a prime `P` of `B` and `O = B_P`, then `O` is the localization of `A[x]` at the prime `P ∩ A[x]` |
| `adjoin_singleton_mk_eq_top` | `x`, as an element of `Algebra.adjoin A {x}`, generates it |
| `not_conductor_le_of_eq_sup` | for `B` finite over `A`, an ideal `p ≤ P ∩ A`, `A[x] → B ⧸ P` surjective and `P = pB + h₀(x) B`: `conductor A x ⊄ P` |
| `adjoin_eq_top_of_mem_conductor` | if the conductor of `A[x]` contains a nonzero `r`, then `x` generates the fraction field `L` of `B` over the field `K` (for `L` a localization of `B` at elements of `A` and `L/K` algebraic) |
| `mk_aeval_eq` | the class of `g(θ)` modulo `P` is `ḡ(θ̄)` for `P` over `p` |
| `exists_surjective_and_eq_sup` | for `B` Dedekind and finite over `A`, maximal ideals `P ≠ ⊥` over `p` with `pB ≠ ⊥` and `B ⧸ P` separable over `A ⧸ p`, some `x ∈ B` and `h₀ ∈ A[X]` satisfy: `A[x] → B ⧸ P` is surjective and `P = pB + h₀(x) B` |

`isLocalization_adjoin`: for `r` in the conductor outside `P`, every `b / s` with `s ∉ P` equals
`(r b) / (r s)` with `r b, r s ∈ A[x]` and `r s ∉ P`; equalities are handled the same way.

`not_conductor_le_of_eq_sup` is Nakayama's lemma for the finite `A[x]`-module `B`: with
`I = P ∩ A[x]` one has `I B = P` (both generators `p` and `h₀(x)` of `P` lie in `I`), and
`B = A[x] · 1 + I B` by the surjectivity of `A[x] → B ⧸ P`. Hence
(`Submodule.exists_sub_one_mem_and_smul_le_of_fg_of_le_sup`) there is `r ∈ A[x]` with
`r - 1 ∈ I` and `r B ⊆ A[x]`; such an `r` lies in the conductor and not in `P`.

`exists_surjective_and_eq_sup` follows Serre III §6, Exercise 2: take `θ ∈ B` lifting a nonzero
primitive element `θ̄` of `B ⧸ P` over `A ⧸ p` (`Field.exists_primitive_element`; `1` if the
primitive element is `0`) and `g₀ ∈ A[X]` lifting the minimal polynomial of `θ̄`, so that
`g₀(θ) ∈ P`, `g₀'(θ) ∉ P` (separability) and `g₀(0) ∉ p` (`θ̄ ≠ 0`). For a uniformizer `π ∈ P \ P²`
(`Ideal.exists_mem_pow_notMem_pow_succ`), one of `y = θ` and `y = θ + π` has `g₀(y) ∉ P²`
(Taylor expansion `Polynomial.binomExpansion` and `Ideal.IsMaximal.mul_mem_pow`). Write
`pB = P ^ e * Q` with `P ⊔ Q = ⊤` and choose `i ∈ P²`, `j ∈ Q` with `i + j = 1`; then `x = y j`
satisfies `x ≡ y mod P²` and `x ∈ Q`. With `t = g₀(x)`: `t ∈ P \ P²`, and `t` lies in no maximal
ideal `M ≠ P` containing `pB`. Indeed such an `M` contains `Q` (from `P ^ e * Q ⊆ M` and
`M ≠ P`), hence `x`, so `g₀(x) ≡ g₀(0) mod M` (`x` divides `g₀(x) - g₀(0)`); and `g₀(0) ∉ M`,
since `M ∩ A = p` and `g₀(0) ∉ p`. Hence `t = g₀(x) ∉ M`.
Therefore the ideal `pB + tB ⊆ P`, written `P * I'`, has `I' = ⊤`: otherwise `I'` lies in a
maximal ideal, which is `P` (then `t ∈ P²`) or another maximal ideal containing `pB` and `t`.
Surjectivity of `A[x] → B ⧸ P` holds because `x ≡ θ mod P` and `θ̄` generates `B ⧸ P`.

## The length of the differentials is the exponent of the different

Assumptions: `A` an integrally closed domain, `B` a Dedekind domain, torsion-free and finite over
`A`, `Algebra.IsSeparable (FractionRing A) (FractionRing B)` (for `FractionRing.liftAlgebra`, the
form of the hypothesis of Mathlib's `differentIdeal` results), `P ≠ ⊥` a prime of `B`, and `O`
with `IsLocalization.AtPrime O P` and `IsScalarTower A B O`.

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `length_kaehler_eq_count_of_not_conductor_le` | for `x ∈ B` generating `FractionRing B` over `FractionRing A` with `conductor A x ⊄ P`: `Module.length O Ω[O⁄A] = (normalizedFactors (differentIdeal A B)).count P` |
| `length_kaehler_eq_count` | **Serre III §7 Prop. 14, localized**: for maximal ideals `p ≠ ⊥` of `A` and `P` of `B` with `P.LiesOver p` and `B ⧸ P` separable over `A ⧸ p`: `Module.length O Ω[O⁄A] = (normalizedFactors (differentIdeal A B)).count P` |

`length_kaehler_eq_count_of_not_conductor_le`: by `isLocalization_adjoin`, `O` is a localization
of `C = A[x]`; the minimal polynomial `g = minpoly A x` divides every polynomial vanishing at `x`
(`minpoly.isIntegrallyClosed_dvd`), so `length_kaehler_eq` gives
`length Ω[O⁄A] = length (O ⧸ (g'(x)))`. Mapping
`conductor A x * differentIdeal A B = (g'(x))` (`conductor_mul_differentIdeal`) to `O`, where the
conductor becomes the unit ideal, identifies `(g'(x)) O` with `𝔇_{B/A} O`, and
`length_quotient_map_eq_count` computes the length (`differentIdeal_ne_bot`).
`length_kaehler_eq_count` chooses `x` by `exists_surjective_and_eq_sup` (`pB ≠ ⊥` as `p ≠ ⊥`),
gets `conductor A x ⊄ P` from `not_conductor_le_of_eq_sup`, and `x` generates the fraction field
by `adjoin_eq_top_of_mem_conductor`.
