# Infinitesimal lifting along a directed union

`directedEtaleLift` lifts a map through a nilpotent ideal when the source is
covered by a directed family of formally étale subalgebras. Each subalgebra
supplies a unique lift. The lifts agree under inclusion by formal
unramifiedness. A common stage for any two elements proves additivity and
multiplicativity of the glued lift. Reduction recovers the input map.

For a square-zero ideal, uniqueness follows by restricting both lifts to every
stage. The resulting bijection proves
`formallyEtale_of_directed_subalgebras`.

This is the directed-union step for the uncompleted coefficient ring in
Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2A, printed p. 109
(PDF p. 3). The construction is derived from the infinitesimal lifting
property of each subalgebra.
