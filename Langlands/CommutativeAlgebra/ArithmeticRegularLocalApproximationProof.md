# Proof of arithmetic regular local approximation

Module `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.ArithmeticRegularLocalApproximationProof`.

The theorem `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.arithmeticRegularLocalApproximation` proves
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.ArithmeticRegularLocalApproximationStatement` in all three universe
parameters `u`, `v`, `w`. Let `W` be a strictly Henselian discrete valuation ring with
uniformizer `π` and fraction field `L`, and let `f : C → L` be a ring map from a finite type
`ℤ`-algebra. The route is adapted from Deligne, SGA 7 I, Exposé I, Lemma 0.5.1,
§§0.5.2–0.5.3 (pp. I-4–I-5) and Variante 1.3 (pp. I-6–I-7). As Variante 1.3 does in mixed
characteristic, the proof passes to a complete trait, taking the completion `W'` of `W` in
every characteristic. The induced map `C → Frac W'` factors through `B[1/t]`, where `B` is
a Henselian regular local ring with a local homomorphism `B → W'` that sends `t` to a uniformizer.
Lemma 0.5.1 approximates by Henselian smooth essentially finite type algebras obtained by
Néron desingularization. The approximation step here uses Popescu's theorem
(`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.Popescu.popescuFactorization`) over the prime
subring of a ring `Λ` with `W' ≅ Λ[X] / (F)` (`Λ = W'` in equal characteristic, a Cohen ring of
the residue field in mixed characteristic), and the Henselian chart is the completion of an
essentially finite type regular local ring.

## Helper results in this module

- `Langlands.FieldTheory.isSepClosed_of_ringEquiv`: a field isomorphic to a
  separably closed field is separably closed. A monic irreducible separable polynomial `p` over
  `k'` is carried by `e⁻¹` to a separable polynomial of the same positive degree over `k`, which
  has a root `x` (`IsSepClosed.exists_root`); then `e x` is a root of `p`
  (`IsSepClosed.of_exists_root`).
- `ArithmeticRegularLocalApproximation.isPurelyInseparable_of_surjective`: a field extension
  given by a surjective ring map `φ : F → E` is purely inseparable, since every element of `E`
  is in the image of `F` and therefore integral over `F`.

## Proof of `arithmeticRegularLocalApproximation`

1. **The trait `W'`.** Take `W' = AdicCompletion (maximalIdeal W) W`, in universe `u`, and
   `i = algebraMap W W'`. By `DvrCompletion.isDomain_and_isDiscreteValuationRing` (module
   `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.DvrCompletion`), `W'` is a domain and a discrete valuation ring.
   `i` is a local homomorphism and `W'` is complete for its maximal ideal (Mathlib instances for
   completions of Noetherian local rings), so `W'` is Henselian
   (`henselianLocalRing_of_isAdicComplete_maximalIdeal` in
   `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.Henselian.Criteria`).
2. **Uniformizer and residue fields.** By `DvrCompletion.maximalIdeal_eq_span`, the maximal
   ideal of `W'` is generated by `i π`, so `i π` is irreducible
   (`IsDiscreteValuationRing.irreducible_iff_uniformizer`). The residue map
   `ResidueField.map i` is bijective (`AdicCompletion.residueField_map_bijective`); hence the
   residue field of `W'` is separably closed (`Langlands.FieldTheory.isSepClosed_of_ringEquiv` applied to
   `DvrCompletion.residueEquiv`), and it is purely inseparable over the residue field of `W`
   (`isPurelyInseparable_of_surjective`).
3. **The map `iFrac`.** The completion `W'` is a faithfully flat `W`-algebra via `i`
   (`DvrCompletion.faithfullyFlat W : Module.FaithfullyFlat W W'`), so `i` is injective
   (`FaithfulSMul.algebraMap_injective W W'`), and so is the composite `W → W' → Frac W'`
   (with `IsFractionRing.injective`). It extends to `iFrac : L → Frac W'` (`IsFractionRing.lift`), with
   `iFrac ∘ algebraMap W L = algebraMap W' (Frac W') ∘ i` (`IsFractionRing.lift_algebraMap`).
4. **The chart.** `chartConclusion_of_complete` (module
   `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.ArithmeticRegularLocalApproximation.Mixed`) applied to `W'`,
   the irreducible element `i π` and the map `iFrac ∘ f : C → Frac W'` gives the Henselian
   regular local domain `B`, the regular parameter `t`, the local map `j : B → W'` with `j t`
   irreducible, the finitely generated subfield `k` of the residue field of `B`, and maps
   `jAway : B[1/t] → Frac W'` and `g : C → B[1/t]` with
   `jAway ∘ algebraMap B B[1/t] = algebraMap W' (Frac W') ∘ j` and `jAway ∘ g = iFrac ∘ f`.
   These are exactly the remaining conditions of the statement.

The Henselian hypothesis on `W` is not used: the completion `W'` is Henselian by itself.

## The chart construction

`chartConclusion_of_complete` is proved in the modules under
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.ArithmeticRegularLocalApproximation`:

- `Chart` defines `ChartConclusion` and obtains `B` as the completion of a regular local ring
  `P` with finitely generated residue field and a local map `P → W'` sending a parameter to a
  uniformizer. The local map extends to `B → W'` (`chartConclusion_of_regularLocal`).
- `ResidueFields` and `RegularAlgebra` show that a discrete valuation ring `Λ` is a regular
  algebra over a ring generated by the integers when each prime number is zero, a unit or a
  uniformizer in `Λ`; `Base` applies this to the prime subring of `Λ`.
- `LocalChart` builds `P` as a localization of `D[X]` cut by one equation, for a regular finite
  type algebra `D`; `Factorization` factors `C → Frac W'` through `P[1/t]`.
- `Presentation` (`chartConclusion_of_presentation`) assembles these from a presentation
  `W' = Λ[X] / (F)`, `X ↦ ϖ`, with `F` monic and its constant coefficient not in `𝔪_Λ²`,
  using Popescu's theorem to factor the inclusion of the needed coefficient subalgebra into
  `Λ` through a smooth algebra `D`.
- `EqualChar` uses `Λ = W'` and `F = X - ϖ`; `Mixed` uses a Cohen ring `Λ` of the residue field
  and an Eisenstein polynomial `F`, and combines the two cases.
