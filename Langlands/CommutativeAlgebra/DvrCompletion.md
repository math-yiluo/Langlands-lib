# Adic completion of a discrete valuation ring

Worker 519.

For a DVR `R`, `DvrCompletion.isDomain_and_isDiscreteValuationRing` shows that
`AdicCompletion (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal R) R` is a domain and a DVR in the
same universe. `DvrCompletion.maximalIdeal_eq_span` identifies its maximal
ideal with the ideal generated by the image of any chosen uniformizer.
`DvrCompletion.faithfullyFlat` supplies faithful flatness of the completion.
`DvrCompletion.residueEquiv` is the canonical isomorphism induced by the
completion map on residue fields.

The ring-theoretic argument uses a separated local ring with maximal ideal
`(t)`. For any nonzero element, separation gives a least power of `(t)` that
does not contain it. Membership in the previous power gives a factorization
as that power of `t` times a unit. When multiplication by `t` is injective,
all powers of `t` are nonzero. The Library's power factorization criteria give
the domain and DVR properties. Mathlib supplies locality and adic separation
of the completion, identifies its maximal ideal as the extension of the
original maximal ideal, and gives flatness. Flatness preserves regularity of
the uniformizer, and locality upgrades flatness to faithful flatness.

The DVR conclusion is the forward implication of
[Stacks Project, Tag 0AP1](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0AP1).
The construction implements the passage to a complete DVR in Conrad's
appendix to Prasad and Yu, *On quasi-reductive group schemes*, author preprint,
pp. 31–32 (`prasad-yu-2006-quasi-reductive-author-preprint`).
