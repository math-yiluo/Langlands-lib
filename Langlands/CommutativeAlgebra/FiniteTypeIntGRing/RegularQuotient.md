# Quotients of regular local rings by part of a regular system of parameters

This helper module for the proof of
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.FiniteTypeIntGRingStatement` contains one
theorem, in the namespace `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.FiniteTypeIntGRing`.

## Statement

`isRegularLocalRing_quotient_span_of_linearIndependent f hfm hf`: let `R` be a
regular local ring with residue field `κ`, `ι` a finite type and `f : ι → R` a
family of elements of the maximal ideal `𝔪` (hypothesis `hfm`). If the images
of the `f i` in the cotangent space `𝔪/𝔪²` are linearly independent over `κ`
(hypothesis `hf`), then `R ⧸ (f i : i ∈ ι)` is a regular local ring.

This is the standard fact that a quotient of a regular local ring by part of a
regular system of parameters is regular. The module is internal to the
project's proof and does not follow a specific reference.

## Proof

Write `V = 𝔪/𝔪²`, `W ⊆ V` for the `κ`-span of the images of the `f i`, and
`d = dim_κ V`. Linear independence gives `dim W = |ι|`
(`finrank_span_eq_card`).

1. *Generators of `𝔪`.* Choose a basis `bq` of `V/W` (with `m` elements) and
   elements `t j ∈ 𝔪` whose classes map to `bq j` in `V/W`. Every `x ∈ V`
   differs from `∑ⱼ cⱼ · [t j]` (the coefficients `cⱼ` being those of the
   class of `x` in `bq`) by an element of `W`. So the classes of the `f i` and
   the `t j` span `V`. By Nakayama's lemma in the form of Mathlib's
   `CotangentSpace.span_image_eq_top_iff`, the `f i` and the `t j` generate
   `𝔪`.
2. *The quotient.* Put `R̄ = R ⧸ (f)`. The ideal `(f)` is contained in `𝔪`,
   so `R̄` is nontrivial and local, and its maximal ideal is the image of `𝔪`
   (`map_maximalIdeal_of_surjective`). The images of the `f i` vanish, so the
   maximal ideal of `R̄` is generated by the `m` images of the `t j`. Hence its
   minimal number of generators (`spanFinrank`) is at most `m`.
3. *Dimension.* Mathlib's `ringKrullDim_le_ringKrullDim_quotient_add_card`,
   applied to the finite set of values of `f` (which lies in the Jacobson
   radical `𝔪`), gives `dim R ≤ dim R̄ + |ι|`. Regularity of `R` gives
   `dim R = d` (`IsRegularLocalRing.spanFinrank_maximalIdeal` together with
   `spanFinrank_maximalIdeal_eq_finrank_cotangentSpace`). Since `R̄` is
   Noetherian local, its dimension is a natural number `e`, and
   `d ≤ e + |ι|`.
4. *Conclusion.* From `d = dim W + dim V/W = |ι| + m` the generator bound
   becomes `spanFinrank ≤ m ≤ e = dim R̄`. Mathlib's
   `IsRegularLocalRing.of_spanFinrank_maximalIdeal_le` concludes that `R̄` is
   regular.
