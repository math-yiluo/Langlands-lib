# Regularity of the formal fibres after a finite field extension

This helper module for the proof of
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.FiniteTypeIntGRingStatement` proves the
regularity of the formal fibres in the generality needed for prime
localizations of finite-type `ℤ`-algebras. It assembles the other helper
modules. All declarations are in the namespace
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.FiniteTypeIntGRing`, and `RingHom.ker_isPrime`
is a local instance.

## Statements

**Extending a map by new variables.** For a commutative ring `R₀`, types `σ`
and `μ`, a commutative ring `T`, a ring map `φ : R₀[X_σ] →+* T` and
`y : μ → T`:

- `sumExtend φ y : R₀[X_σ, Y_μ] →+* T` (with
  `R₀[X_σ, Y_μ] = MvPolynomial (σ ⊕ μ) R₀`) extends `φ` by `Y_j ↦ y j`;
- `sumExtend_rename_inl`: on `R₀[X_σ]` (embedded by `rename Sum.inl`) it is
  `φ`;
- `sumExtend_X_inr`: it sends `Y_j` to `y j`.

**The base localization.** Let `A'` be a local ring and
`g : R₀[X_1, …, X_n] →+* A'`. Write `P` for the preimage under `g` of the
maximal ideal of `A'`.

- `baseLift g : (R₀[X])_P →+* A'` is the map induced by `g` (values of `g`
  outside `P` are units of `A'`);
- `baseLift_algebraMap`: it extends `g`;
- `baseLift_surjective g hg`: it is surjective if every `a ∈ A'` satisfies
  `a * g v = g u` for some `u`, `v` with `g v` a unit (hypothesis `hg`).

**Generation.** `exists_eq_div_sumExtend g hg b z`: let `A'` be a commutative
ring with fraction field `κ`, `k` a field extension of `κ` (compatibly an
`A'`-algebra), `g : R₀[X_1, …, X_n] →+* A'` satisfying `hg` as above, and
`b` a basis of `k` over `κ` indexed by `Fin m`. Then every `z ∈ k` is a
quotient `ψ u / ψ v` with `ψ v ≠ 0`, where `ψ = sumExtend (A' → k ∘ g) b`.

**Main theorem.** `isRegularRing_tensor_fiber_adicCompletion q g hg hsmooth k'`:
let `R₀ : Type v` be a regular ring and `A : Type u` a Noetherian local ring
with completion `Â = AdicCompletion (maximalIdeal A) A`. Let `q` be a prime
of `A` with residue field `κ(q)`, and `g : R₀[X_1, …, X_n] →+* A ⧸ q` a ring
map such that every element `a ∈ A ⧸ q` satisfies `a * g v = g u` for some
`u`, `v` with `g v` a unit (hypothesis `hg`). Assume (`hsmooth`) that every
field `K : Type v` that is an essentially finite type `R₀`-algebra with
`ringChar K = ringChar κ(q)` is formally smooth over `R₀`. Then for every
finite field extension `k'` of `κ(q)` (in any universe), the ring
`k' ⊗[κ(q)] q.Fiber Â = k' ⊗_{κ(q)} (κ(q) ⊗_A Â)` is regular.

The main theorem is the case of the regularity of formal fibres used for the
G-ring property. Instead of the literature route (the integer case and
finite-type ascent, Stacks Project, More on Algebra, Section 15.51), the
project proves it directly by a Jacobian criterion. The module does not
follow a specific reference.

## Proofs

**`sumExtend`.** Two ring maps out of `R₀[X_σ]` agree when they agree on
constants and variables (`MvPolynomial.ringHom_ext`); `sumExtend_X_inr` is
evaluation.

**`baseLift`.** It is `IsLocalization.lift`. For surjectivity, given `a`,
choose `u`, `v` by `hg`. Since `g v` is a unit, `v ∉ P`, and `baseLift` sends
the fraction `u / v` to `g u * (g v)⁻¹ = a` (`IsLocalization.lift_mk'_spec`).

**Generation.** Let `F` be the subfield of `k` generated by the values of `ψ`.
It contains the image of every `g p` (as `ψ (rename Sum.inl p)`), hence the
image of every `a ∈ A'` (as `g u / g v`), hence the image of `κ` (fractions of
`A'`, `IsFractionRing.div_surjective`), and the basis vectors `b j = ψ (Y_j)`.
So `F = k`, because every `z` is a `κ`-linear combination of the `b j`.
Mathlib's `Subfield.mem_closure_iff` writes `z` as a quotient of two elements
of the subring generated by the values of `ψ`, which is the range of `ψ`
(`Subring.closure_eq`). If the denominator is zero, `z = 0 = ψ 0 / ψ 1`.

**Main theorem.** Write `A' = A ⧸ q`. It is local, its fraction field is
`κ(q)`, and `k'` is an algebra over `A`, `A'` and `κ(q)` through `κ(q)`, with
the corresponding scalar towers.

1. *Base ring.* Let `P` be the preimage of the maximal ideal of `A'` under
   `g` and `A₀ = (R₀[X])_P`. `baseLift g : A₀ → A'` is surjective, and `k'` is
   an `A₀`-algebra through it.
2. *Extension variables.* Let `b` be a basis of `k'` over `κ(q)`
   (`Module.finBasis`, `m` elements) and `ψ = sumExtend (A' → k' ∘ g) b`. Then
   `ψ (rename Sum.inl p)` is the image of `p` under `R₀[X] → A₀ → k'`
   (`baseLift_algebraMap`), and every element of `k'` is a quotient of values
   of `ψ` (`exists_eq_div_sumExtend`).
3. *Completed polynomial ring.* Let `Â₀` be the completion of `A₀` at its
   maximal ideal and make `Â₀[Y_1, …, Y_m]` an `R₀[X, Y]`-algebra by
   `sumExtend (C ∘ (R₀[X] → A₀ → Â₀)) X`. Then `X_i` maps to the constant
   image of `X_i` and `Y_j` to `Y_j` (`hB₁`, `hB₂`).
4. *Regularity of `Â₀[Y]`.* `R₀[X]` is regular (Mathlib's
   `MvPolynomial.isRegularRing_of_isRegularRing`), so its localization `A₀` is
   regular local. Its completion `Â₀` is regular local
   (`isRegularLocalRing_adicCompletion`, module `CompletionRegular`), hence a
   regular ring by the Library theorem `regularLocalLocalizations` (module
   `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.RegularLocalLocalizationsProof`). Mathlib's
   instance then makes `Â₀[Y]` regular.
5. *Smoothness of the residue field.* The residue field `κ(ker ψ)` is
   essentially of finite type over `R₀` (it is so over the finite-type
   `R₀`-algebra `R₀[X, Y]`; `Algebra.EssFiniteType.comp`). It is isomorphic to
   `k'` (`residueFieldKerEquiv`, module `PolynomialFiber`), and `k'` has the
   characteristic of its subfield `κ(q)` (`Algebra.charP_iff`). So `hsmooth`
   makes `κ(ker ψ)` formally smooth over `R₀`.
6. *Regular fibre.* `isRegularRing_fiber_completedPolynomial` (module
   `LocalDerivation`, with `M = P.primeCompl` and `I` the maximal ideal of
   `A₀`) shows that `(ker ψ).Fiber Â₀[Y]` is regular.
7. *Identification.* The ring isomorphisms are
   - `fiberEquivTensor` (module `PolynomialFiber`):
     `(ker ψ).Fiber Â₀[Y] ≅ k' ⊗[A₀] Â₀`;
   - `tensorAdicCompletionEquivOfSurjective` (module `SurjectiveCompletion`)
     for the surjection `A₀ → A'`: `k' ⊗[A₀] Â₀ ≅ k' ⊗[A'] Â'`;
   - the same for the surjection `A → A'`: `k' ⊗[A] Â ≅ k' ⊗[A'] Â'`;
   - Mathlib's `Algebra.TensorProduct.cancelBaseChange`:
     `k' ⊗[κ(q)] (κ(q) ⊗[A] Â) ≅ k' ⊗[A] Â`.

   Composing them, `IsRegularRing.of_ringEquiv` transfers regularity to
   `k' ⊗[κ(q)] q.Fiber Â`.
