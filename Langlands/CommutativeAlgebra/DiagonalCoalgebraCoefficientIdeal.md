# Coefficient ideals of finite-type maps into monoid algebras

For an algebra map into a commutative monoid algebra, `algebraCoefficientIdeal φ m` is the range of degree-`m` coefficient extraction composed with the map. The coefficient ideal after any commutative scalar extension is the extension of the original ideal. The proof calculates the coefficient of a pure tensor and uses additivity for one inclusion. The pure tensor with scalar factor one gives the reverse inclusion.

If the source algebra is of finite type, take finitely many algebra generators. Adjoin to the integers all coefficients in their images. The resulting subring is Noetherian, and each generator image lifts to a monoid algebra over that subring. Adjoin the lifted images there. Extending scalars on this subalgebra recovers the original map's range because both ranges are generated by the same elements over the original base. The coefficient ideal over the Noetherian subring is finitely generated, so its extension is finitely generated too.

For a bialgebra map into a group algebra, the degree ideal agrees with `HopfPointIntegralClosure.coeffIdeal`. The existing comultiplication argument makes this ideal idempotent. Finite generation then gives an idempotent generator over any commutative base. This is an internal algebraic step toward describing relative images of split tori.
