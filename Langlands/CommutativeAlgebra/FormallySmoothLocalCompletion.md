# Completions of formally smooth local algebras

Helper module `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.FormallySmoothLocalCompletion`, written as
part of the proof that every stalk of a scheme smooth over an algebraically closed field is a
unique factorization domain (task `7d63b52e-e715-47d0-a015-331cfa8a4b6d`). It contains the
step "the completion of the local ring is a power series ring".

## Main result

`exists_algEquiv_mvPowerSeries`: let `K` be a field and `R` a Noetherian local ring that is a
`K`-algebra. Assume `Algebra.FormallySmooth K R` and that `algebraMap K (ResidueField R)` is
surjective. Then for some `n : ℕ` there is a `K`-algebra isomorphism

`AdicCompletion (maximalIdeal R) R ≃ₐ[K] MvPowerSeries (Fin n) K`.

`n` is the dimension of the cotangent space `m/m²` over the residue field.

This is a version of the classical fact that a complete regular local ring containing a
coefficient field is a power series ring (Cohen's structure theorem). The Lean proof below
does not use dimension theory or Cohen's theorem. Formal smoothness takes the place of
regularity, and the argument stays at finite level. The result is derived internally for this
task; its statement is not quoted from a source.

## Construction and proof

Notation: `m = maximalIdeal R`, `K[T] = MvPolynomial (Fin n) K`, and
`J = idealOfVars (Fin n) K`, the ideal generated by the variables. Choose
`t : Fin n → R` with every `t i ∈ m`, and assume two hypotheses:

- `hspan`: every `r ∈ m` is congruent modulo `m²` to some `∑ algebraMap K R (a i) * t i`;
- `hind`: if `∑ algebraMap K R (a i) * t i ∈ m²`, then `a = 0`.

Together they say that the images of the `t i` form a `K`-basis of `m/m²`.

1. **Approximation (section `Approximation`).** Let `I` be an ideal of a ring `A` and `S` a
   subring. Suppose every element lies in `S + I`, and every element of `I` lies in
   `(S ∩ I) + I²`. Then `S + I^d` is all of `A` for every `d`
   (`exists_sub_mem_pow`). In particular, `S = A` when `I` is nilpotent
   (`mem_of_isNilpotent`). The proof is induction on `d`, using products of approximations
   (`exists_mem_sub_mem_pow_succ`).
2. **Noetherian rings (`injective_of_surjective_endo`).** A surjective ring endomorphism of a
   Noetherian ring is injective: the kernels of its iterates form an increasing chain, and
   this chain stabilizes.
3. **Polynomial lemmas.** `p - C (p.coeff 0) ∈ J`, and every `p ∈ J` is congruent modulo `J²`
   to its linear part `∑ C aᵢ * Xᵢ`.
4. **Level-wise isomorphism (section `Levelwise`).** `aeval t : K[T] → R` sends `J^k` into
   `m^k`.
   - Surjectivity onto `R/m^k` (`exists_sub_aeval_mem_pow`) applies step 1 with `S` the image
     of `aeval t`. The two hypotheses of step 1 come from the surjectivity of
     `K → ResidueField R` and from `hspan`.
   - Injectivity (`mem_pow_idealOfVars_of_aeval_mem_pow`: `aeval t p ∈ m^k` implies
     `p ∈ J^k`) uses formal smoothness. For `k ≥ 2`, the degree-2 map
     `K[T]/J² → R/m²` is an isomorphism by `hind` and `hspan`. The quotient map
     `K[T]/J^k → K[T]/J²` has kernel `J²/J^k`, which is nilpotent (its `k`-th power is zero)
     but in general not square-zero. Formal smoothness lifts along quotients by nilpotent
     ideals (`Algebra.FormallySmooth.lift`; Mathlib derives this from square-zero lifting by
     successive square-zero lifts). It lifts the inverse `R → K[T]/J²` to a `K`-algebra map
     `ψ : R → K[T]/J^k`. The map `ψ` sends `m` into
     `J`, so it factors through `R/m^k`. The composite
     `θ : K[T]/J^k → R/m^k → K[T]/J^k` is surjective: apply step 1 to the nilpotent ideal
     `J/J^k`, whose first-order approximations come from `hind`. Then `θ` is injective by
     step 2, since `K[T]/J^k` is Noetherian. Hence `K[T]/J^k → R/m^k` is injective.
   - `truncEquiv` combines the two into `K[T]/J^k ≃ₐ[K] R/m^k`. The `factor_truncEquiv`
     lemma shows these isomorphisms commute with the truncation maps.
5. **Completions (section `Completion`).** `adicCompletionAlgEquiv` turns a compatible family
   of isomorphisms `A/I^k ≃ₐ[K] B/J^k` into an isomorphism of adic completions. It does so with
   `AdicCompletion.liftAlgHom` in both directions and `AdicCompletion.ext_evalₐ`.
   `completionEquiv` composes this with the inverse of Mathlib's
   `MvPowerSeries.toAdicCompletionAlgEquiv`, which identifies `K⟦T⟧` with the `J`-adic
   completion of `K[T]`, after restricting scalars to `K`.
6. **Choosing `t` (`exists_algEquiv_mvPowerSeries`).** Take the basis
   `Module.finBasis (ResidueField R) (CotangentSpace R)`; the cotangent space is
   finite-dimensional because `R` is Noetherian. Lift the basis vectors through
   `Ideal.toCotangent` to elements `t i ∈ m`. Because `K → ResidueField R` is surjective,
   every residue-field coefficient comes from `K`. Then `hspan` follows from `Basis.sum_repr`,
   and `hind` follows from linear independence. Both use `Ideal.toCotangent_eq` and
   `Ideal.toCotangent_eq_zero`.

Universes are independent: `K : Type u_1`, `R : Type u_2`. Only the final theorem uses
`IsNoetherianRing R`, and only to make the cotangent space finite-dimensional.
