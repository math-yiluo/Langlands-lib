# Deligne--Henniart virtual stability from its arithmetic inputs

Lean module: `Langlands.LocalFactor.DeligneHenniartVirtualReduction`.

## Source

P. Deligne and G. Henniart, *Sur la variation, par torsion, des constantes locales
d'équations fonctionnelles de fonctions L*, Invent. Math. 64 (1981):

- 4.2 (printed p. 108, PDF p. 20): for a virtual representation `V` of `W` without tame
  constituent there is `γ ∈ K^*`, unique modulo `U(1)`, with (4.2.1)
  `ε(W ⊗ V, ψ) = det W(γ)` modulo `p`-power roots of unity for all `W` of dimension `0` with
  `β(W) < α(V)`; and (4.2.2) `v_K(γ) = a(V) + dim V · n(ψ)`.
- 4.5 (printed p. 110, PDF p. 22): `V` is written as `Σ n_i Ind_{H_i} χ_i`, with `H_i`
  containing the last ramification group `G(α(V))` and `χ_i` nontrivial on it. Then
  `α(χ_i) = ψ_{L_i/K}(α(V))`. With `a_i ∈ L_i^*` such that
  `χ_i(1 + y) = ψ ∘ Tr_{L_i/K}(a_i y)` for `v(y) ≥ α(χ_i)` (non-strict), the element
  `γ = Π N_{L_i/K}(a_i)^{-n_i}` satisfies (4.2.1).
- 4.6 (printed p. 110, PDF p. 22): there is `γ ∈ K^*`, unique modulo `U(α(V)/2)`, with
  (4.6.1) `ε(W ⊗ V, ψ) = det W(γ)` for all `W` of dimension `0` with `β(W) < α(V)/2`; it is
  congruent to the element of (4.2.1) modulo `U(1)`.
- 4.7–4.8 (printed pp. 110–111, PDF pp. 22–23): the same construction for (4.6.1), with
  tests `β(W) < α(V)/2`. Here `a_i` is required to linearize only on the half-break range:
  `χ_i(1 + y) = ψ ∘ Tr_{L_i/K}(a_i y)` for `v(y) > α(χ_i)/2` (strict).

The target `VirtualStabilityStatement` (Library module `DeligneHenniartStatement`) is 4.6
with its uniqueness coset, the compatibility with 4.2, and the valuation (4.2.2). This
module proves it from four propositions of the Library that are not proved yet (see
`virtualStability_of_inputs`).

The module first proves the per-generator form of 4.5 and 4.8. Fix one generator
`Ind_{W_L}^{W_F} [η]` and one linearizer `c` of the field character of `η`. The single
element `γ = N_{L/K}(c⁻¹)` is then both an exact witness (tests trivial on `W_F^{α/2}`)
and an approximate witness (tests trivial on `W_F^α`), and its valuation is the
generator's term of (4.2.2). The predicate `IsLabelWitness` names this property. It is
closed under sums and integer multiples, so it extends to the kernel span of one basis
class, and then (with the label lowered to the minimum break) to every lattice element.
The uniqueness cosets are the Library's theorems.

DH81 compute the valuation (4.2.2) from the uniqueness argument 4.2(iii). The proof here
is different and derived for this formalization: it compares linearizers. Using one
element for both relations makes the compatibility clause (`γ γ₀⁻¹ ∈ U^1`) trivial. The
first-order norm–trace congruence (DH81 3.12(i)) enters only as the hypothesis
`TopLayerNormTraceStatement` of the Library module `DeligneHenniartTopLayerStatement`.

## Setting and notation

- `F` is a `p`-adic field. `K = baseExtension F` is the bottom field. `L : Extension F`
  is a finite intermediate field of `AlgebraicClosure F` with `hL : K.field ≤ L.field`.
- `W_F` is `weilSubgroup F K`. Its upper groups are `baseUpperGroup F s`.
  `baseUpperAbove F α` is the subgroup generated by the `baseUpperGroup F s` with `s > α`
  (module `DeligneHenniartLastBreak`).
- `W_L^s` is `intermediateUpperGroup F L s`. `ψ_{L/F}` is `relativeHerbrandPsi L`.
- `e_E`, `f_E`, `ord_E` are the ramification index, residue degree and normalized order
  of `fieldData F E`.
- `n(ψ)` is `additiveConductor` and `a(χ)` is `multiplicativeConductor` (in
  `fieldData`). `ψ ∘ Tr` is `traceCharacter F K L hL ψ`.
- The unit-level hypotheses are the following.
  - `hWK`: for `s ≥ 0`, reciprocity maps `baseUpperGroup F s` onto `U_K^{⌈s⌉}`.
  - `hWL`: for `s ≥ 0`, reciprocity maps `W_L^s` onto `U_L^{⌈s⌉}` (for all `L` where
    stated so).

## Declarations

Index and degree:

- `subgroup_index_comap_of_denseRange`: let `φ : G' → G` be a homomorphism with dense image
  and `H` an open subgroup of `G`. Then `[G' : φ⁻¹H] = [G : H]`.
- `weilSubgroup_index`: `[W_F : W_L] = [L : F]`, with `W_F` the whole local Weil group.
- `degree_induce_base`: `deg Ind_{W_L}^{W_F} x = [L : F] · deg x`.
- `finrank_padic_tower`: `[E : ℚ_p] = [E : F] [F : ℚ_p]`.
- `ramificationIndex_mul_residueDegree_eq`: `e_L f_L = [L : F] e_K f_K`.
- `residueDegree_mul_ord_unitsNorm`: `f_K ord_K(N_{L/K} y) = f_L ord_L(y)` for
  `y ∈ L^×`.
- `ramificationIndex_mul_ord_unitsInclusion`: `e_K ord_L(c) = e_L ord_K(c)` for
  `c ∈ K^×`.
- `ramificationIndex_baseExtension_real`: `e_K = e_F`, where `e_F` is the ramification
  index of `F.canonicalTopologicalData`.

Conductors:

- `exists_multiplicativeConductor_eq`: for `M ≥ 1` there is a character of conductor
  exactly `M + 1`.
- `conductor_eq_of_break`. Hypotheses: `hWL` at `L`, a field character `χ` of `L`, and
  `B₀ ≥ 0`. The Weil character of `χ` is nontrivial on `W_L^{B₀}` and trivial on `W_L^s`
  for every `s > B₀`. Conclusion: `B₀ = B` for a natural number `B`, and `a(χ) = B + 1`.

The inducing character. Here `η : W_L → ℂˣ` is a continuous character, and `η` is trivial
on every `g ∈ W_L` lying in `baseUpperAbove F α`.

- `eq_one_of_baseUpperAbove` (with `α ≥ 0`): `η` is trivial on `W_L^{s'}` for every
  `s' > ψ_{L/F}(α)`.
- `exists_ne_one_of_baseUpperGroup` (with `α ≥ 0`). Hypotheses: `baseUpperGroup F α`
  lies in `W_L`, and `η` is nontrivial on it. Conclusion: `η` is nontrivial on
  `W_L^{ψ_{L/F}(α)}`.
- `conductor_fieldCharacterEquiv`. Hypotheses: `hWL` at `L`, `α > 0`, and both
  hypotheses above. Let `χ_L` be `fieldCharacterEquiv F e L η`, the field character of
  `η`. Conclusion: there is a natural number `B ≥ 1` with `ψ_{L/F}(α) = B` and
  `a(χ_L) = B + 1` (DH81 4.5: `α(χ_i) = ψ_{L_i/K}(α(V))`).

The valuation identity:

- `ramificationIndex_mul_relativeHerbrandPsi_add_conductor`. Hypotheses:
  `NormalizedReciprocity F e`, `hWK`, `hWL` at `L`, `α ≥ 0`, and `upperGroup F α ⊆ W_L`.
  Then for every additive character `ψ` of `K`:
  `e_K (ψ_{L/F}(α) + 1 + n(ψ ∘ Tr)) = e_L (α + 1 + n(ψ))`.

The definition:

- `IsLabelWitness F e ε ψ α x γ` (for `x ∈ R(W_F)` over `K` and `γ ∈ K^×`) holds when
  - `ord_K γ = deg x · (α + 1 + n(ψ))`,
  - `ExactWitnessOn F e ε K (baseUpperGroup F (α/2)) ψ x γ`, and
  - `ApproximateWitnessOn F e ε K (baseUpperGroup F α) ψ x γ`.

  `ExactWitnessOn` and `ApproximateWitnessOn` are the Library's relations (4.6.1) and
  (4.2.1) for `x`, tested on the degree-zero classes spanned by representations trivial on
  the given subgroup. This definition is internal to this formalization: it packages the
  properties of the element `N_{L/K}(a)^{-1}` of DH81 4.5 and 4.8 (one element for both
  relations, with the valuation (4.2.2)).
- `isLabelWitness_zero`, `IsLabelWitness.add`, `IsLabelWitness.zsmul`: given
  `AdditiveRelation F ε`, `γ = 1` is a witness for `x = 0`; witnesses `γ` for `x` and `γ'`
  for `y` give `γ γ'` for `x + y`; a witness `γ` for `x` gives `γ^n` for `n • x`
  (`n ∈ ℤ`).

The witness of one generator:

- `exists_witness_kernelGenerator`. Hypotheses:
  - `hsys : IsSystem F e ε`;
  - `hWK`;
  - `hWL` for every `L`;
  - `htop : TopLayerNormTraceStatement.{uF, uF, uF}`;
  - an additive character `ψ` of `K`;
  - `α > 0`;
  - `x ∈ kernelInductionGenerators F K (baseUpperGroup F α) (baseUpperAbove F α)`.

  Conclusion: there is `γ ∈ K^×` with `IsLabelWitness F e ε ψ α x γ`.

Assembly. The hypotheses `hsys`, `hWK`, `hWL` (for every `L`), `htop` and `ψ` are those of
`exists_witness_kernelGenerator`. `breakLabel F b` is the label (last break) of a basis
class `b`, `b.1` its rank, `realizeAtBase F` the realization of the lattice in `R(W_F)` over
`K`, and `wildArtinSum F v = Σ_b v_b · rank(b) · (1 + breakLabel F b)`.

- `degree_generator`: the lattice degree of a basis class is its rank.
- `exists_isLabelWitness_kernelSpan`: for `α > 0`, every `x` in the `ℤ`-span of
  `kernelInductionGenerators F K (baseUpperGroup F α) (baseUpperAbove F α)` has
  `γ ∈ K^×` with `IsLabelWitness F e ε ψ α x γ`.
- `exists_witness_generator`: for a basis class `b` with `breakLabel F b > 0` and
  `a ≤ breakLabel F b`, there is `γ ∈ K^×` with
  `ord_K γ = rank(b) · (breakLabel F b + 1 + n(ψ))`, exact relative to `W_F^{a/2}` and
  approximate relative to `W_F^a` for `realizeAtBase F (generator b)`.
- `exists_witness_lattice`: for a lattice element `v` with `PositiveBreakSupport F v` and
  `AllBreaksAtLeast F v a`, there is `γ ∈ K^×` with
  `ord_K γ = wildArtinSum F v + deg v · n(ψ)` (this is (4.2.2)), exact relative to
  `W_F^{a/2}` and approximate relative to `W_F^a` for `realizeAtBase F v`.

The reduction:

- `virtualStability_of_inputs`. Hypotheses (all Library propositions):
  - `hreal : AbsoluteReciprocityRealUnitImages`: an absolute geometric reciprocity map
    sends `U^{⌈v⌉}` onto the abelian upper group of index `v ≥ 0`;
  - `hgeo : CommonClosureGeometricStatement`: for a normalized common-closure reciprocity
    family, every candidate of `F` compatible with its bottom component, and its component
    at every finite extension `E` of `F` (transported to `E`), is a geometric Weil
    reciprocity map;
  - `hint : IntermediateGeometricStatement`: the same for the intermediate components
    `intermediateReciprocityCandidate F eC E`;
  - `htop : TopLayerNormTraceStatement.{uF, uF, uF}`: DH81 3.12(i).

  Conclusion: `VirtualStabilityStatement.{uF, uV}`.

## Proof (as implemented)

- **Index.** Mathlib's `IntermediateField.finrank_eq_fixingSubgroup_index` gives
  `[L : F] = [G_F : G_L]`. `localProjection F` has dense image and the fixing subgroup is
  open. So `subgroup_index_comap_of_denseRange` transports the index to the Weil group:
  the map on left cosets is injective by definition and surjective by density.
  `degree_induce_base` is the Library's `degree_induce` together with `W_F = ⊤`
  (`baseWeil_eq_top`).
- **Degrees and orders.** `e f = [E : ℚ_p]` (`ramificationIndex_mul_residueDegree`), with
  the tower formula `finrank_padic_tower`, and `[K : F] = 1`. The order relations are the
  Library's `residueDegree_mul_ord_relative_norm` and
  `ramificationIndex_mul_ord_relative_algebraMap`, with the algebra `K → L` given by
  `IntermediateField.inclusion hL`. `e_K = e_F` is `fieldData_ramificationIndex_eq` with
  the relative index `1` of `G_K = G_F`.
- **Conductor of a given value.** Let `ϖ` be the uniformizer and `u = 1 + ϖ^M`. Then
  `u ∉ U^{M+1}`, so by `mem_principalUnits_iff_bounded_characters` some `χ` with
  `a(χ) ≤ M + 1` has `χ(u) ≠ 1`. Since `u ∈ U^M`, also `a(χ) > M`.
- **Break and conductor.** `trivial_iff_of_map_reciprocityUnits` with `hWL` gives, for
  `s ≥ 0`: `χ` is trivial on `U^{⌈s⌉}` iff its Weil character is trivial on `W_L^s`.
  Nontriviality at `B₀` gives `⌈B₀⌉ < a(χ)`. Triviality at `⌊B₀⌋ + 1 > B₀` gives
  `a(χ) ≤ ⌊B₀⌋ + 1`. Together these force `B₀ = ⌊B₀⌋`. For the inducing character, write
  `s' = ψ_{L/F}(s)` (`exists_relativeHerbrandPsi_eq`). Then `s > α` by strict monotonicity, and
  Herbrand's comparison `coe_mem_upperGroup_iff` with
  `intermediateUpperGroup_eq_weilUpperSubgroup` moves between `W_L^{ψ_{L/F}(s)}` and
  `W_F^s`. `CommonClosureSpectralAnalytic.weilCharacter_fieldCharacterEquiv` identifies
  the Weil character of `χ_L` with `η`. `B ≥ 1` since `ψ_{L/F}(α) > ψ_{L/F}(0) = 0`.
- **Valuation identity.** Choose `M = ⌈2α⌉ + 1 > 2α`. Take a character `χ` of `K` of
  conductor `M + 1` and its linearizer `c` (`exists_linearization`), so
  `ord_K c = −(M + 1 + n(ψ))`.
  1. The norm pullback `χ_L = restrictCharacter F e K L hL χ` has conductor `B + 1`, where
     `ψ_{L/F}(M) = B` (`conductor_restrictCharacter`).
  2. The slope lemma `ramificationIndex_mul_relativeHerbrandPsi_sub_of_upperGroup_le`
     gives `e_K (B − ψ_{L/F}(α)) = e_L (M − α)`. This forces `B ≥ 1` and the depth bounds
     that `restrictCharacter_one_add` needs.
  3. For `x` with `ord_L x ≥ B`, `restrictCharacter_one_add` (the quadratic norm–trace
     expansion) gives `χ_L(1 + x) = (ψ ∘ Tr)(c x)`.
  4. A deep linearizer `d` of `χ_L` (`RankOneStability.exists_deep_linearization`) gives
     `χ_L(1 + x) = (ψ ∘ Tr)(d x)` on the same lattice, with
     `ord_L d = −(n(ψ ∘ Tr) + B + 1)`.
  5. `mul_inv_mem_principalUnits_of_eq_on_lattice` gives `c d⁻¹ ∈ U_L^1`, so
     `ord_L c = ord_L d`. With `e_K ord_L c = e_L ord_K c`,
     `e_K (n(ψ ∘ Tr) + B + 1) = e_L (M + 1 + n(ψ))`.

  Subtracting the slope identity gives the claim.
- **One generator.** Unfold the generator into `(L, hL, η, hN, hnt, hK)`.
  1. `conductor_fieldCharacterEquiv` gives `B ≥ 1` with `ψ_{L/F}(α) = B` and
     `a(χ_L) = B + 1`.
  2. `exists_linearization` gives `c ∈ L^×` with `ord_L c = −(B + 1 + n(ψ ∘ Tr))` and
     `χ_L(z) = (ψ ∘ Tr)(c (z − 1))` for every `z ∈ U_L^{(B+1) − ⌊(B+1)/2⌋}`, that is, at
     depth `⌈(B + 1)/2⌉`. This range contains the depth `B` needed for (4.2.1), so the
     linearization is stronger than the one DH81 4.5 asks for.
  3. The Library's `exactWitnessOn_characterClass_of_linearization_rel` gives an exact
     witness `c⁻¹` of `[η]` over `L` relative to `W_L^{B/2}`. Its relative Herbrand input
     is `herbrandInputRel_of_le`, and `hinert` is `absoluteUpperInertia` at `L`.
  4. `approximateWitnessOn_characterClass_of_linearization_rel` gives an approximate
     witness relative to `W_L^B`, with input `approximateHerbrandInput_of_le` (from
     `htop`). Both inputs come from the module `DeligneHenniartRelativeHerbrand`.
  5. `exactWitnessOn_induce_upper` and `approximateWitnessOn_induce_upper` induce these to
     `K`, relative to `W_F^{α/2}` and `W_F^α`, with witness `N_{L/K}(c⁻¹)`.
  6. For the valuation, combine `f_K ord_K(N c⁻¹) = f_L (B + 1 + n(ψ ∘ Tr))`, the
     valuation identity, and `e_L f_L = [L : F] e_K f_K`. This gives
     `e_K f_K ord_K(N c⁻¹) = e_K f_K [L : F] (α + 1 + n(ψ))`. Cancel `e_K f_K > 0`.
     The degree is `[L : F] · 1` by `degree_induce_base` and `degree_characterClass`.
- **Witnesses at a label.** The valuation clause uses `ord(1) = 0`,
  `ord(γ γ') = ord γ + ord γ'`, `ord(γ^n) = n ord γ` and the linearity of `deg`. The witness
  clauses are the Library's `exactWitnessOn_zero/add/zsmul` and
  `approximateWitnessOn_zero/add/zsmul`.
- **Kernel span.** `Submodule.span_induction`: generators by
  `exists_witness_kernelGenerator`, then `isLabelWitness_zero`, `IsLabelWitness.add`,
  `IsLabelWitness.zsmul` (with `hsys.2.1 : AdditiveRelation F ε`).
- **One basis class.** The Library's `realizeAtBase_generator_mem_kernelSpan` puts
  `realizeAtBase F (generator b)` in the kernel span at `α = breakLabel F b`. The witness is
  moved from `W_F^{α/2}` and `W_F^α` to `W_F^{a/2}` and `W_F^a` by `exactWitnessOn_mono`,
  `approximateWitnessOn_mono` and `baseUpperGroup_antitone` (`a ≤ α`). The degree is
  `degree_realizeAtBase` with `degree_generator`.
- **Lattice element.** `Finsupp.induction`. For `v = 0` take `γ = 1`. For
  `v = n · [b] + f` with `b ∉ supp f`, the hypotheses on `v` restrict to `f` and to `b`;
  take `γ_b^n γ_f`, with `wildArtinSum` additive (`Finsupp.sum_add_index'`,
  `Finsupp.sum_single_index`).
- **The reduction.** `weilTrivialityThresholdsNonempty`, `absoluteUpperInertia` and
  `unitFiltrationComparison` are Library theorems. `hWK` is
  `baseUpperGroup_map_reciprocityUnits` (with `geometric_baseReciprocityCandidate` from
  `hgeo`). `hWL` is `intermediateUpperGroup_map_reciprocityUnits` with `hint`. Apply
  `exists_witness_lattice` with `a = minimumBreak F v hv`
  (`allBreaksAtLeast_iff_le_minimumBreak`). `exactWitness_of_exactWitnessOn` and
  `approximateWitness_of_approximateWitnessOn` give the witness relations of the
  statement. The two cosets are `exactWitness_iff_mem_coset_of_isSystem` and
  `approximateWitness_iff_mem_coset_of_isSystem`. The approximate witness is the same `γ`,
  so `γ γ⁻¹ = 1 ∈ U^1`.
