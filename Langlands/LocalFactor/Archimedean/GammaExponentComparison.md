# Finite conductor exponents in the presence of archimedean Gamma factors

The module compares two products of shifted real and complex Gamma factors and the two corresponding products evaluated at `1 - z`. Assume equal counts after each complex factor is counted as two real factors. If the cross-multiplied ratio is `c · M^(-z)` away from a closed discrete set, with `c` a nonzero complex constant and `M` a positive real number, then `M = 1`. The proposition uses the Library's `GammaBlock`, `gammaProduct`, and reciprocal entire denominator.

This is an internal analytic reduction for the comparison of global functional equations in Arthur and Clozel, *Simple Algebras, Base Change, and the Advanced Theory of the Trace Formula* (1989), Chapter 1, §6, equations (6.15)–(6.17), printed pp. 69–70. It permits extracting the finite conductor powers at split infinite places using only the sizes of the archimedean products. The hypothesis on the ratio is the comparison after the finite L-functions and the archimedean additive-character factors have been compared.

Lean expands each complex block using `Γ_ℂ(z) = Γ_ℝ(z) Γ_ℝ(z + 1)`. It compares the two identities at `z` and `z + 2`, applying `Γ_ℝ(z + 2) = Γ_ℝ(z) z / (2π)` to the forward lists and to the reflected lists at `-1 - z`. The Gamma products are nonzero away from closed discrete sets. Cancelling the resulting common product gives an equality of polynomials up to the scalar `M^(-2)`. Their leading coefficients agree and are nonzero by the two count hypotheses, so `M^(-2) = 1`. Positivity gives the conclusion.
