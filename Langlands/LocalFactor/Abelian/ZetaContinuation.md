# From a finite shell formula to normalized continuation

Worker 1786 proves three derived implications used by the Tate local analysis proof. A finite Laurent formula for the actual convergent zeta integral implies holomorphy on the convergence region. The meromorphic normal form of the Euler factor times that Laurent expression supplies a continuation, and the meromorphic identity principle proves uniqueness.

For any such continuation, normalization preserves every punctured germ and every regular value. If another finite numerator has those properties, canceling the nonzero Euler factor on the open convergence region identifies its Laurent evaluation there. The finite Laurent uniqueness lemma then identifies the coefficients. The input finite formula remains an explicit premise of these helpers and is proved separately by shell integration.
