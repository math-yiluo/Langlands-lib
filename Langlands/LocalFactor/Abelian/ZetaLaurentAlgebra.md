# Finite Laurent algebra for Tate zeta continuation

Worker 1786 supplies derived algebraic and analytic helpers for the local zeta proof. Evaluation is additive and sends a finitely supported single coefficient to its spectral monomial. It agrees with the Library's Laurent polynomial evaluation, so the existing infinite-real-spectral-set uniqueness theorem determines the coefficients. The meromorphic identity principle strengthens this to agreement on any one neighborhood.

The Euler factor times any finite Laurent expression is globally meromorphic and analytic at regular points. Passing to meromorphic normal form preserves its regular values. These helpers do not assert the existence of a Laurent expression for an integral; that requires the shell computation.
