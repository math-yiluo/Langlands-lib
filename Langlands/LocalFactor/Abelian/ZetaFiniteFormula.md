# Finite prefix and geometric tail of the local zeta integral

Worker 1786 derives the exact shell series from the actual additive Haar integral. The shells are measurable and disjoint, their union contains the integrand's support, and the previously proved integrability permits summing their integrals.

Schwartz–Bruhat local constancy at zero makes the shell coefficients constant above a valuation cutoff. Splitting off the finite prefix therefore leaves an ordinary convergent geometric series. This is an internal proof of the finite formula from the valuation bounds and shell change of variables; it uses no functional equation or assumed rationality.
