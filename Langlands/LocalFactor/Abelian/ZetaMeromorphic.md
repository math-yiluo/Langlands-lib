# Normal meromorphic uniqueness for the local zeta integral

Worker 1786 proves that the explicit convergence half-plane is open and nonempty and contains no Euler pole. Two globally meromorphic functions agreeing there have identical punctured germs everywhere: the meromorphic difference has infinite order at one point, and the meromorphic identity principle propagates this along the connected complex plane. Normal form then identifies actual values. Finite Laurent expressions are entire, since they are finite sums of exponentials.
