# The conductor–discriminant formula

This module states `Langlands.Ramification.Padic.FiniteConductorDiscriminantStatement`, a
proposition definition, and defines a representative of an irreducible class. The proof is
`Langlands.Ramification.Padic.finiteConductorDiscriminant` (`ConductorDiscriminantProof`).

## Statement

```lean
noncomputable abbrev Langlands.FiniteRep.IrreducibleClass.out {G : Type uG} [Group G]
    (a : FiniteRep.IrreducibleClass G) : FDRep ℂ G :=
  Quotient.out (a.1 : Quotient (CategoryTheory.isIsomorphicSetoid (FDRep ℂ G)))

def FiniteConductorDiscriminantStatement : Prop :=
  ∀ (p : ℕ) [Fact p.Prime] (K : PadicField.{uK} p) (M : PadicField.{uM} p)
    [Algebra K.Carrier M.Carrier] [IsScalarTower (Padic p) K.Carrier M.Carrier]
    [IsGalois K.Carrier M.Carrier] (TK : K.TopologicalData) (TM : M.TopologicalData),
    letI := TK.valuativeRel
    letI := TM.valuativeRel
    letI := relativeIntegerAlgebra TK TM
    ∀ {ι : Type uι} [Fintype ι] [DecidableEq ι] (b : Module.Basis ι TK.Integers TM.Integers),
      (∀ N : ℕ, lowerRamificationGroup TM K.Carrier N = ⊥ →
        Ideal.span {Algebra.discr TK.Integers b} =
          maximalIdeal TK.Integers ^ ((inertia TM K.Carrier).index *
            ∑ i ∈ Finset.range N, (lowerGroupOrder TM K.Carrier i - 1))) ∧
      ∀ [Fintype (FiniteRep.IrreducibleClass (M.Carrier ≃ₐ[K.Carrier] M.Carrier))],
        Ideal.span {Algebra.discr TK.Integers b} =
          ∏ a : FiniteRep.IrreducibleClass (M.Carrier ≃ₐ[K.Carrier] M.Carrier),
            (maximalIdeal TK.Integers ^ artinExponent TM K.Carrier a.out.ρ) ^
              Module.finrank ℂ a.out
```

(name space `Langlands.Ramification.Padic`; level parameters `uK uM uι`).

In words: let `M/K` be a finite Galois extension of p-adic fields, with topological data `TK`, `TM`,
integers `O_K = TK.Integers`, `O_M = TM.Integers` (with the relative integer algebra of
`LocalField/RelativeIntegers`), Galois group `G` acting on `O_M`, lower ramification groups `G_i`
(`lowerRamificationGroup TM K.Carrier i`, of order `lowerGroupOrder TM K.Carrier i`) and inertia
`G_0 = inertia TM K.Carrier`. For every basis `b` of `O_M` over `O_K`, with `𝔡_{M/K}` the ideal
generated by the discriminant of `b`:

1. if `G_N = 1`, then `𝔡_{M/K} = m_K ^ ([G:G_0] · Σ_{i<N} (|G_i| − 1))`;
2. (**Führerdiskriminantenproduktformel**) `𝔡_{M/K} = ∏_χ 𝔣(χ)^{χ(1)}`, the product over the
   isomorphism classes of irreducible complex representations of `G` (any enumeration), with
   `𝔣(χ) = m_K ^ a(χ)` the conductor, `a(χ)` the Artin exponent (`Padic.artinExponent`) of a
   representative and `χ(1)` its dimension.

## Literature

Serre, *Local Fields*, GTM 67, Springer, 1979 (PDF page = printed page + 7; checked on PDF pages 71
and 111):

- Chapter IV, §1, Proposition 4 (Hilbert's formula), printed p. 64:
  `v_L(𝔇_{L/K}) = Σ_{s≠1} i_G(s) = Σ_i (Card(G_i) − 1)`; with `v_K(𝔡) = f · v_L(𝔇)` this is clause 1.
- Chapter VI, §3, Corollaries 1 and 2 of Proposition 6, printed p. 104: `𝔡_{K'/K} = 𝔣(s_{G/H})` and
  `𝔡_{L/K} = ∏_χ 𝔣(χ)^{χ(1)}` — clause 2. Serre states these for a global extension with `p`-parts
  (the conductor `𝔣(χ) = ∏ p^{f(χ, p)}`); the Library states the local formula, which is the
  `p`-part.
