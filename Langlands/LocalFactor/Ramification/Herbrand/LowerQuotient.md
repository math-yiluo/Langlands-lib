# Herbrand's theorem: lower groups of a quotient

`Langlands.LocalFactor.Ramification.Herbrand.LowerQuotient`. Herbrand's theorem for a finite group
`G` acting faithfully on a discrete valuation ring `R`: for a normal subgroup `H`, with `G ⧸ H`
acting on the invariants `R^H`,

`G_n H / H = (G/H)_⌈φ_H(n)⌉`,

where `φ_H = herbrandPhiRat R H` is the rational Herbrand function of `H` (`Herbrand/Rational.lean`).
The module proves it in three forms, of decreasing hypotheses on depths and increasing hypotheses on
rings, and derives the compatibility of the upper groups with quotients. The Library's
`Herbrand/QuotientProof.lean` (`HerbrandBridge`) specialises the last two to finite p-adic Galois
extensions.

## Statements

Name space `Langlands.Ramification`; `R` a discrete valuation ring, `G` with
`[MulSemiringAction G R] [FaithfulSMul G R]`, `H` a normal subgroup.

1. From the coset formula and the scaling of valuations, for finite `H` with `R^H` a discrete
   valuation ring:

   ```lean
   theorem map_lowerGroup_mk_eq_of_iInf_addVal_smul_sub_eq_sum_ramificationDepth
       {H : Subgroup G} [H.Normal] [Fintype H] [IsDiscreteValuationRing (FixedPoints.subring R H)]
       (hH : ∀ τ : G,
         (⨅ z : FixedPoints.subring R H,
             IsDiscreteValuationRing.addVal R
               (((QuotientGroup.mk τ : G ⧸ H) • z - z : FixedPoints.subring R H) : R)) =
           ∑ h : H, ramificationDepth R G (τ * (h : G)))
       (he : ∀ z : FixedPoints.subring R H,
         IsDiscreteValuationRing.addVal R (z : R) =
           (lowerGroupCard R H 0 : ℕ∞) * IsDiscreteValuationRing.addVal (FixedPoints.subring R H) z)
       (n : ℕ) :
       (lowerGroup R G n).map (QuotientGroup.mk' H) =
         lowerGroup (FixedPoints.subring R H) (G ⧸ H) ⌈herbrandPhiRat R H (n : ℚ)⌉₊
   ```
2. For `R` generated by `x` over a ring `A` of invariants of `G`, and `R^H` generated by `y` over
   `A`, with the same scaling hypothesis `he`: the same conclusion
   (`map_lowerGroup_mk_eq_of_adjoin_singleton_eq_top`); `hH` is supplied by
   `Herbrand/CosetDepth.lean`.
3. For a finite `G` with the maximal ideal of `R` over that of `R^G` and separable residue
   extension:

   ```lean
   theorem map_lowerGroup_mk_eq_of_isSeparable_residueField
       {R : Type*} [CommRing R] [IsDomain R] [IsDiscreteValuationRing R]
       {G : Type*} [Group G] [Finite G] [MulSemiringAction G R] [FaithfulSMul G R]
       [(IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal R).LiesOver (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal (FixedPoints.subring R G))]
       [Algebra.IsSeparable
         (FixedPoints.subring R G ⧸ IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal (FixedPoints.subring R G))
         (R ⧸ IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal R)]
       {H : Subgroup G} [H.Normal] (n : ℕ) :
       (lowerGroup R G n).map (QuotientGroup.mk' H) =
         lowerGroup (FixedPoints.subring R H) (G ⧸ H) ⌈herbrandPhiRat R H (n : ℚ)⌉₊
   ```
4. Under the hypotheses of 3, `UpperGroupRatQuotientCompat R G (FixedPoints.subring R H) H`, i.e.
   `G^v H / H = (G/H)^v` for `v ≥ 0` (`upperGroupRatQuotientCompat_of_isSeparable_residueField`).

## Proofs

1. Let `τ̄ ≠ 1` in `G ⧸ H` and choose `τ` of maximal depth in its coset
   (`exists_quotientMk_eq_max_ramificationDepth`). If `i_G(τ) = n + 1`, the elements `τh` have depth
   `min(i_G(τ), i_G(h))` (`ramificationDepth_mul_coe_eq_min`), so the coset sum is
   `∑_h min(n + 1, i_G(h)) = |H_0| (φ_H(n) + 1)` (`sum_lowerGroupCard_eq_sum_toNat_min_ramificationDepth`,
   `cosetRamificationDepthSum_toNat_eq`). By `hH` and `he` the depth of `τ̄` on `R^H` is
   `φ_H(n) + 1` (`RamificationDepthCosetLemma.toNat_ramificationDepth_quotient_eq`), and
   `τ̄ ∈ G_m H/H ⟺ m + 1 ≤ i_G(τ)` (`mem_lowerGroup_map_iff_max_depth`); comparing the two criteria
   gives `G_m H/H = (G/H)_⌈φ_H(m)⌉` (`RamificationDepthCosetLemma.depth_iff_depth`,
   `lowerHerbrandQuotientCompatCanonical_of_cosetLemma`).
2. `hH` is `iInf_addVal_smul_sub_eq_sum_ramificationDepth_of_adjoin_singleton_eq_top`.
3. `R^G` and `R^H` are discrete valuation rings (`isDiscreteValuationRing_fixedSubring`), `R` and
   `R^H` are finite over `R^G` (`FixedSubringValuation.module_finite_fixedSubring`), and both residue
   extensions over `R^G` are separable; so `R` and `R^H` are monogenic over `R^G`
   (`exists_adjoin_singleton_eq_top_of_isSeparable_residueField`), and the scaling `he` is
   `addVal_coe_eq_lowerGroupCard_zero_mul_addVal_fixedPoints`. Then 2 applies with `A = R^G`.
4. `upperGroupRatQuotientCompat_of_map_lowerGroup_mk_eq` of `Herbrand/RationalQuotient.lean`
   applied to 3.

## Literature

Serre, *Local Fields*, GTM 67 (1979), Chapter IV, §3, Lemma 5, printed page 75 (Herbrand's
theorem `G_u H/H = (G/H)_v` with `v = φ_{L/K'}(u)`) and Proposition 14, printed page 74 (the
compatibility of the upper numbering with quotients); Chapter IV, §1, Proposition 3, printed
page 63, for the coset formula. Serre's index is the real `u`, with `G_u = G_i` for the smallest
integer `i ≥ u` (printed page 73); for natural `n` the lower group `(G/H)_{φ(n)}` is
`(G/H)_⌈φ(n)⌉`.
