# The depth on the fixed subring as a sum over a coset

`Langlands.LocalFactor.Ramification.Herbrand.CosetDepth`. The first step of Herbrand's theorem: for
a normal subgroup `H`, the depth of `τ H` acting on the invariants `R^H` is the sum of the depths
of the elements of the coset `τ H` acting on `R`, both measured with the valuation of `R`. It is
proved here for a monogenic discrete valuation ring whose ring of invariants is monogenic too;
`Herbrand/LowerQuotient.lean` turns it into the lower-group formula `G_n H/H = (G/H)_⌈φ_H(n)⌉`.

## Statement

```lean
theorem Langlands.Ramification.iInf_addVal_smul_sub_eq_sum_ramificationDepth_of_adjoin_singleton_eq_top
    {R : Type*} [CommRing R] [IsDomain R] [IsDiscreteValuationRing R]
    {G : Type*} [Group G] [MulSemiringAction G R] [FaithfulSMul G R]
    {A : Type*} [CommSemiring A] [Algebra A R]
    (hA : ∀ (σ : G) (a : A), σ • algebraMap A R a = algebraMap A R a)
    {H : Subgroup G} [H.Normal] [Fintype H]
    {x : R} (hx : Algebra.adjoin A {x} = ⊤)
    {y : R} (hy : y ∈ FixedPoints.subring R H)
    (hy' : ∀ z ∈ FixedPoints.subring R H, z ∈ Algebra.adjoin A {y}) (τ : G) :
    (⨅ z : FixedPoints.subring R H,
        IsDiscreteValuationRing.addVal R
          (((QuotientGroup.mk τ : G ⧸ H) • z - z : FixedPoints.subring R H) : R)) =
      ∑ h : H, ramificationDepth R G (τ * (h : G))
```

Here `A` is a ring of invariants of `G` (`hA`) over which `R` is generated by `x` and `R^H` by `y`.
The left side is `fixedSubringRamificationDepth R G H τ̄`, the right side
`cosetRamificationDepthSum R G H τ`; the equation for every `τ` is the proposition
`RamificationDepthCosetLemma R G H`, which `ramificationDepthCosetLemma_of_monogenic` proves.

## Proof

By `ramificationDepth_eq_addVal_of_adjoin_singleton_eq_top` (`Depth.lean`), the depth of `τ h` is
`v (x - τh x)` and the depth of `τ̄` on `R^H` is `v (τ y - y)`, so the claim is
`v (τ y - y) = v (∏_{h ∈ H} (x - τh x))`. Let `P = ∏_{h ∈ H} (X - h x)` (`prodXSubSMulSubgroup`); its
coefficients are invariant under `H`. If `z = f(x)` is invariant, the `h x` are distinct roots of
`f - z` (the action is faithful and `x` generates), so `P` divides `f - z`; applying `τ` and
evaluating at `x` gives `∏_h (x - τh x) ∣ τ z - z` (`prod_sub_smul_dvd_smul_sub`). Conversely the
coefficients of `P` are polynomials in `y` over `A`, so `τ y - y` divides the coefficients of
`τ P - P`, hence its value at `x`, which is `∏_h (x - τh x)` (`smul_sub_dvd_prod_sub_smul`).

## Literature

Serre, *Local Fields*, GTM 67 (1979), Chapter IV, §1, Proposition 3, printed page 63:
`i_{G/H}(σ) = e_{L/K'}^{-1} ∑_{s ↦ σ} i_G(s)`. The present statement is the equation before the
division by the ramification index `e_{L/K'} = |H_0|`, the left side being measured with `v_L`; in
Serre's proof (after Tate, printed pages 63–64) it is the equality of the ideals generated by
`s(y) - y` and `∏_{t ∈ H} (st(x) - x)`, for generators `x` of `A_L` and `y` of `A_{K'}`.
