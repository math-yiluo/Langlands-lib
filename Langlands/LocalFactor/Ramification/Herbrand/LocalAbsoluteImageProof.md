# Closed right limits and wild inertia in any characteristic

The two theorems are in `Langlands.Ramification.Local`. Both take a
nonarchimedean local field `K` and a specified separable closure `Ω` in
independent universes. Their propositions are defined in
[LocalAbsoluteStatement.lean](LocalAbsoluteStatement.lean).

## Closed right-limit images

`absoluteUpperClosedRightImage` identifies the image of the Krull closure of
the supremum of the absolute upper groups at indices `w > v` with the
supremum of the corresponding finite upper groups, for every finite Galois
subextension and every `v ≥ -1`.

The restriction is continuous and the finite Galois group is discrete. The
preimage of the image of any subgroup is therefore closed and contains the
subgroup. It contains the subgroup's closure, so the image of the closure
equals the image of the subgroup. The proof commutes the image with both
indexed suprema using `Subgroup.map_iSup` and applies
`Wintenberger.localAbsoluteUpperFiniteImage` at each `w > v`.

## Wild inertia

`absoluteUpperWildInertia` identifies `localWildInertia K Ω` with the
intersection of the restriction preimages of the finite first lower groups.

At a finite level, the upper group at `w > 0` is the lower group at the
integer `⌈ψ(w)⌉`. This integer is at least `1` because `ψ(w) > 0`, so the
group lies in the first lower group by antitonicity. The first lower group
occurs at the positive index `φ(1)` by `upperRamificationGroup_phi`.
Consequently the closed right-limit image at `v = 0` is the first lower group.

The proof reconstructs a closed subgroup `H` of the absolute Galois group from
its finite images using `InfiniteGalois.fixingSubgroup_fixedField`. If an
automorphism has restriction in the image of `H` at every finite level, then
for each element `x` of the fixed field of `H`, its restriction to the finite
Galois subextension generated by the conjugates of `x` agrees with a member
of `H`. It fixes `x`, so it belongs to `H`. The reverse inclusion follows
from `Subgroup.le_comap_map`. Applying the reconstruction to the closed
positive upper limit proves the stated intersection formula.

## Source

The formulas are consequences of the finite-image and upper-numbering
conventions in Jean-Pierre Serre, *Local Fields*, translated by Marvin Jay
Greenberg, GTM 67, Springer (1979), Chapter IV §3, pp. 73–75, including
Proposition 14 and Remark 1. Chapter IV §2, Proposition 7 and Corollaries
1–4, pp. 67–68, gives the finite context for the first lower group.
