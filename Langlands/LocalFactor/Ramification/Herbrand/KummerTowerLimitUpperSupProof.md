# Upper ramification transitivity on the infinite Kummer tower

`Langlands.Ramification.Kummer.kummerTowerLimitUpperSup.{u}` proves
`Langlands.Ramification.Kummer.KummerTowerLimitUpperSupStatement.{u}`.
For a compatible system of uniformizer roots over a p-adic field `F`, write
`K_n` for the finite stages and `L = ⨆ n, K_n` for `kummerTower F πs`.
For every `v` in `[-1, kummerBreak F]`, the fixing subgroup of `L` and the
absolute upper ramification subgroup `G_F^v` generate `G_F`.

Fix `σ ∈ G_F`. The proof considers the set of elements `τ` that fix `K_n`
and satisfy `τ⁻¹ σ ∈ G_F^v`. Compatibility makes the generator of `K_n` the
`p`-th power of the generator of `K_{n+1}`. The fields therefore increase,
so their fixing subgroups and the sets of possible `τ` decrease. Each set
is closed because the stage fixing subgroup and `G_F^v` are closed in the
Krull topology, and the map `τ ↦ τ⁻¹ σ` is continuous.
`kummerTowerUpperSup` and normality of `G_F^v` write `σ = τ δ` with `τ`
fixing `K_n` and `δ ∈ G_F^v`, giving a point in each set.

The absolute Galois group is compact by `absoluteGaloisGroupCompactness`,
so `IsCompact.nonempty_iInter_of_sequence_nonempty_isCompact_isClosed`
supplies a common point `τ`. By `Subgroup.zpowers_le` and
`IntermediateField.le_iff_le`, every `K_n` lies in the fixed field of the
cyclic subgroup generated by `τ`. Their supremum lies in the same fixed
field, so `τ` fixes `L`.
The factorization `σ = τ (τ⁻¹ σ)` then gives membership in the required
subgroup supremum.
