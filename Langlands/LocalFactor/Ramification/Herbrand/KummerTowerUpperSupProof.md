# Upper ramification transitivity in a Kummer tower

`Langlands.Ramification.Kummer.kummerTowerUpperSup` proves
`KummerTowerUpperSupStatement.{u}` for an arbitrary universe `u`. Write
`K_n = F(π_n)` for the finite stage generated by the `n`th root in a
compatible root system for the uniformizer `ϖ`, and write `G_{K_n}` for
its fixing subgroup in `G_F`. For every `n` and every real index
`-1 ≤ v ≤ p e_F / (p - 1)`, the theorem proves `G_{K_n} ⊔ G_F^v = ⊤`.
This is the subgroup formulation of transitivity on the `F`-embeddings of
`K_n`. The induction follows the transitivity argument in Corollary
`cor:kummer`, using the preceding truncation lemma. See the operator's
*Numerical local Langlands for GL_n, counts on the two sides and the
recommended route* (`operator-numerical-llc-route-2026-11-16`, lines
876–897).

The proof first computes the order of `π_n` in `K_n` and the absolute
ramification index `e_n`. Compatibility gives `π_n^(p^n) = ϖ` by
induction, with `ϖ` mapped into the algebraic closure. The minimal
polynomial of `π_n` divides `X^(p^n) - ϖ`, so the degree of the simple
extension `K_n/F` is at most `p^n`. The canonical degree identity gives
`e_n f_n = [K_n : F] e_F` for the relative residue degree `f_n`. Since
`f_n` is positive, `e_n ≤ p^n e_F`. Restricting the normalized orders and
using `ord_F(ϖ) = 1` gives `e_F p^n ord_{K_n}(π_n) = e_n`. The order on
the left is an integer, and positivity and the index bound force it to
be `1`. The same identity then gives `e_n = p^n e_F`, hence
`kummerBreak F ≤ kummerBreak K_n`. This derives the stage arithmetic of
Remark 4.1(i) from the degree bound and the Library's normalization
identities. See the operator's *Formalizing the large external inputs
of the local Langlands correspondence for GL_n*
(`operator-external-inputs-plan-2026-11-16`, lines 586–603).

At stage zero every `F`-automorphism fixes `π_0` because it is the image
of `ϖ`. The simple-adjoin fixing criterion gives `G_{K_0} = ⊤`.

For the successor step, `intermediateClosureEquiv` transports
`π_(n+1)` to a root `α` in the algebraic closure of `K_n`. Compatibility
and the equivalence's commutation with the algebra maps show that
`α^p = π_n` there. The order computation makes `π_n` a uniformizer of
`K_n`. The published `kummerStepUpperSup` applied to this root at `v`
gives a supremum equal to the whole absolute Galois group of `K_n`.
Its two terms are the fixing subgroup of `K_n(α)` and the upper
ramification subgroup of `K_n` at `v`.

The induction hypothesis and `herbrandPsiTruncation`, applied with
both indices equal to `v`, give `relativeHerbrandPsi K_n v = v`. Under
`intermediateGaloisMap`, the first term of the one-step supremum maps
into `G_{K_(n+1)}` because an automorphism fixing `α` transports to one
fixing `π_(n+1)`. The second term maps into `G_F^v` by
`intermediateGaloisMap_mem_absoluteUpperRamificationGroup_iff` and the
truncation equality. The range theorem for `intermediateGaloisMap`
identifies the image of the whole group with `G_{K_n}`. Since subgroup
image preserves suprema, these two inclusions give
`G_{K_n} ≤ G_{K_(n+1)} ⊔ G_F^v`. Taking the supremum with `G_F^v` and
using the induction hypothesis proves the desired equality.
