# Lower ramification groups through the action on a uniformizer

`Langlands.LocalFactor.Ramification.Uniformizer`. Let a group `G` act by ring automorphisms on a
local domain `R` whose maximal ideal `m` is generated by `ϖ ≠ 0` (for a discrete valuation ring:
`ϖ` irreducible). Every `σ` maps `ϖ` to `u_σ ϖ` for a unique unit `u_σ`. This module relates the
lower groups `lowerGroup R G i` to the units `u_σ`; it is the ring-theoretic part of Serre's maps
`θ_i : G_i / G_{i+1} → U^i / U^{i+1}`, used by `LowerGroupQuotients.lean` and the tame part of the
Hasse–Arf theorem.

## Statements

Name space `Langlands.Ramification`; `R` a local commutative ring, `G` a group with
`[MulSemiringAction G R]`; `m = IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal R`.

- Existence and uniqueness of `u_σ`:

  ```lean
  theorem exists_smul_uniformizer_eq_unit_mul [IsDomain R] {ϖ : R}
      (hϖ : maximalIdeal R = Ideal.span {ϖ}) (σ : G) :
      ∃ u : Rˣ, σ • ϖ = (u : R) * ϖ
  theorem smul_uniformizer_unit_unique [IsDomain R] {ϖ : R} (hϖ0 : ϖ ≠ 0) {σ : G} {u u' : Rˣ}
      (hu : σ • ϖ = (u : R) * ϖ) (hu' : σ • ϖ = (u' : R) * ϖ) : u = u'
  ```
  (`exists_smul_uniformizer_eq_unit_mul_of_irreducible` for a discrete valuation ring); `u_1 = 1`
  (`smul_uniformizer_unit_one`); the cocycle relation `u_{στ} = u_σ · σ(u_τ)`
  (`smul_uniformizer_unit_cocycle`) and `u_σ · σ(u_{σ⁻¹}) = 1`.
- From the lower groups to the units: if `σ ∈ G_i` then `u_σ - 1 ∈ m ^ i`
  (`smul_uniformizer_unit_sub_one_mem_pow_of_mem_lowerGroup`), `u_{στ} - u_σ u_τ ∈ m ^ (i + 1)`
  (`smul_uniformizer_unit_mul_sub_mul_mem_pow_succ`), and for two generators `ϖ, ϖ'` of `m`,
  `u'_σ - u_σ ∈ m ^ (i + 1)` and `u'_σ u_σ⁻¹ - 1 ∈ m ^ (i + 1)`.
- Back: when `σ ϖ - ϖ` divides `σ x - x` for every `x` (as for a uniformizer that generates the
  ring over a ring of invariants),

  ```lean
  theorem mem_lowerGroup_iff_smul_uniformizer_unit_sub_one_mem_pow [IsDomain R]
      {ϖ : R} (hϖ : maximalIdeal R = Ideal.span {ϖ}) (hϖ0 : ϖ ≠ 0) {σ : G}
      (hdvd : ∀ x : R, σ • ϖ - ϖ ∣ σ • x - x) {u : Rˣ} (hu : σ • ϖ = (u : R) * ϖ) {i : ℕ} :
      σ ∈ lowerGroup R G i ↔ (u : R) - 1 ∈ maximalIdeal R ^ i
  ```
- Auxiliary: `pointwise_smul_span_singleton` (`g • span {a} = span {g • a}`),
  `maximalIdeal_eq_span_smul_of_eq_span`, `irreducible_smul`.

## Proofs

`σ ϖ` generates `σ • m = m`, so `σ ϖ = u ϖ` with `u` a unit; cancellation of `ϖ ≠ 0` gives
uniqueness and the cocycle relation from `(στ) ϖ = σ (u_τ ϖ) = σ(u_τ) u_σ ϖ`. If `σ ∈ G_i`, then
`(u_σ - 1) ϖ = σ ϖ - ϖ ∈ m ^ (i + 1) = ϖ ^ (i + 1) R`, and cancelling `ϖ` gives `u_σ - 1 ∈ m ^ i`; the
congruences for products and for another generator follow by expanding. Conversely
`σ x - x` is a multiple of `σ ϖ - ϖ = (u_σ - 1) ϖ`, which lies in `m ^ (i + 1)` when
`u_σ - 1 ∈ m ^ i`.
