# The depth of an automorphism of a discrete valuation ring

`Langlands.LocalFactor.Ramification.Depth`. For a group `G` acting by ring automorphisms on a
discrete valuation ring `R` with additive valuation `v = IsDiscreteValuationRing.addVal R`, the
depth of `σ ∈ G` is `ramificationDepth R G σ = ⨅ x, v (σ • x - x)` in `ℕ∞` (Serre's `i_G(σ)`).
The lower groups are its superlevel sets, and when `R` is generated by one element `x` over a
ring of invariants the depth is `v (σ • x - x)`. The module also shows that the lower groups of a
finite group acting faithfully become trivial.

## Statements

Name space `Langlands.Ramification`; `R` a discrete valuation ring (`[CommRing R] [IsDomain R]
[IsDiscreteValuationRing R]`), `G` a group with `[MulSemiringAction G R]`.

- The depth and the lower groups:

  ```lean
  noncomputable def ramificationDepth (σ : G) : ℕ∞ := ⨅ x : R, addVal R (σ • x - x)

  theorem mem_lowerGroup_iff_le_ramificationDepth {i : ℕ} {σ : G} :
      σ ∈ lowerGroup R G i ↔ (i : ℕ∞) + 1 ≤ ramificationDepth R G σ
  ```
  with `le_ramificationDepth_iff` (`n ≤ i_G(σ)` iff `σ` moves every `x` into `m ^ n`),
  `ramificationDepth_one : ramificationDepth R G 1 = ⊤` and, for a faithful action,
  `ramificationDepth_eq_top_iff : ramificationDepth R G σ = ⊤ ↔ σ = 1`.
- Valuation and powers of the maximal ideal: `mem_maximalIdeal_pow_iff_le_addVal`
  (`x ∈ m ^ n ↔ n ≤ v x`), `iInf_maximalIdeal_pow_eq_bot`, and invariance of the valuation,
  `addVal_smul : addVal R (σ • x) = addVal R x`.
- Termination. For a local ring `R` whose maximal-ideal powers meet in zero and a finite group
  acting faithfully,

  ```lean
  theorem exists_lowerGroup_eq_bot_of_iInf_eq_bot [Finite G] [FaithfulSMul G R]
      (hsep : ⨅ n, (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal R) ^ n = ⊥) :
      ∃ N : ℕ, ∀ i, N ≤ i → lowerGroup R G i = ⊥
  ```
  and for a discrete valuation ring the same without `hsep`
  (`exists_lowerGroup_eq_bot_of_isDiscreteValuationRing`). Both rest on
  `exists_eq_bot_of_antitone_of_iInf_eq_bot`: a decreasing sequence of subgroups of a finite
  group with trivial intersection is eventually trivial. (`Filtration.lean` has the same
  conclusion, stated with `∀ i ≥ b`, for Noetherian local rings.)
- Monogenic generators. If `σ` fixes the image of an algebra `A → R` and `Algebra.adjoin A {x} = ⊤`,
  then `σ • x - x` divides `σ • r - r` for every `r` and

  ```lean
  theorem ramificationDepth_eq_addVal_of_adjoin_singleton_eq_top {σ : G}
      (hfix : ∀ a : A, σ • algebraMap A R a = algebraMap A R a)
      {x : R} (hx : Algebra.adjoin A {x} = ⊤) :
      ramificationDepth R G σ = addVal R (σ • x - x)
  ```
  so `σ ∈ lowerGroup R G i ↔ i + 1 ≤ v (σ • x - x)`
  (`mem_lowerGroup_iff_le_addVal_of_adjoin_singleton_eq_top`), with variants for
  `[SMulCommClass G A R]` and for membership in `m ^ (i + 1)`.

## Proofs

`x ∈ m ^ n ↔ n ≤ v x` is the description of `m ^ n` as the ideal generated by `ϖ ^ n`; the
depth statements unfold the infimum. An element of a finite group that is not `1` leaves some
group of a decreasing sequence with trivial intersection; the largest such index over the
finitely many elements bounds the sequence. For the generator: an `A`-algebra endomorphism `σ`
satisfies `σ x - x ∣ σ (p(x)) - p(x)` for every polynomial `p` over `A`, because `x - y` divides
`p(x) - p(y)`; every `r` is such a `p(x)`, so `v (σ x - x) ≤ v (σ r - r)` and the infimum is
attained at `x`.

## Literature

Serre, *Local Fields*, GTM 67 (1979), Chapter IV, §1, Lemma 1 (printed page 61), Proposition 1
and the definition after it (printed page 62): the function `i_G(s) = v_L(s(x) - x)` for a
generator `x` of the ring of integers, the criterion `s ∈ G_i ⟺ i_G(s) ≥ i + 1`, and the
eventual triviality of the lower groups. Serre defines `i_G` through a generator; here the depth is
the infimum over all elements and the generator gives its value (the equivalence of b) and c) in
Lemma 1). Serre works with the Galois group of a finite Galois extension of a complete discretely
valued field with separable residue extension (printed page 61); here any group acts on a discrete
valuation ring.
