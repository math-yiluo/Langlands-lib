# The last upper group of an irreducible wild representation

Lean module: `Langlands.LocalFactor.DeligneHenniartLastBreak`.

## Source

P. Deligne and G. Henniart, *Sur la variation, par torsion, des constantes locales
d'équations fonctionnelles de fonctions L*, Invent. Math. 64 (1981), proof of
Theorem 4.2, 4.5 (printed p. 110, PDF p. 22). Let `V` be irreducible with last break
`α > 0`. Then `W^α` acts on `V` through an abelian quotient, `V^{W^α} = 0`, and `W^s` acts
trivially for `s > α`. DH81 then applies Variante 2.3 to the quotient of `W` by the part of
inertia acting trivially, which writes `V` as `Σ nᵢ Ind (χᵢ)` with `χᵢ` nontrivial on `W^α`
and trivial on `W^s` for `s > α`.

This module proves these facts for actual continuous representations of
`localWeilGroup F`, and proves the Brauer step on the base Weil subgroup
`weilSubgroup F (baseExtension F)` with the refined induction theorem
`weilAbelianNormalBrauer_kernel` of `Langlands.LocalFactor.DeligneHenniartRefinedBrauer`.

## Definitions

- `baseUpperAbove F α : Subgroup (weilSubgroup F (baseExtension F))` is the subgroup
  generated by the upper groups `baseUpperGroup F s` with `s > α`. It plays the role of
  `K` in `weilAbelianNormalBrauer_kernel`. An irreducible representation with last break
  `α` is trivial on it.

## Theorems

All are stated for a finite complex representation `A` of `localWeilGroup F`. The upper
groups are `upperGroup F r`, the preimages of `absoluteUpperRamificationGroup F r` under
`localProjection F`. The last break is `lastBreak F A`.

- `representation_eq_one_of_mem_rightLimit`. Let `A` be continuous, `0 ≤ a`, and suppose
  `IsTrivialAt F A s` for every `s > a`. If `localProjection F c` lies in the closed right
  limit `absoluteUpperRightLimit F a`, then `A(c) = 1`.
- `isTrivialAt_of_lastBreak_lt`: for continuous `A` with `0 < lastBreak F A` and
  `s > lastBreak F A`, `A` is trivial on `upperGroup F s`.
- `commute_on_lastBreak`: for continuous `A` with `α = lastBreak F A > 0`, the operators
  `A(x)` and `A(y)` commute for `x, y ∈ upperGroup F α`.
- `fixed_eq_zero_of_lastBreak`: for continuous irreducible `A` with `α > 0`, a vector
  fixed by every element of `upperGroup F α` is zero.
- `baseWeilInclusion_surjective`: the base inclusion
  `weilSubgroup F (baseExtension F) → localWeilGroup F` is surjective.
- The existing `FiniteRepresentation.isIrreducible_restrict_of_surjective`,
  imported from `CommonClosureIrreducibleReduction`, supplies irreducibility
  under restriction along a surjective group homomorphism.
- `baseUpperAbove_le_inertia`: for `0 ≤ α`, `baseUpperAbove F α` lies in the inertia
  subgroup `CommonClosureWeilDeligne.inertia F (baseExtension F)`.
- `realizeAtBase_generator_mem_kernelSpan b hb`: for a basis label `b` with
  `0 < breakLabel F b = α`, `realizeAtBase F (generator b)` lies in the integral span of
  `kernelInductionGenerators F (baseExtension F) (baseUpperGroup F α) (baseUpperAbove F α)`.
  These generators are the classes `Ind_{W_L}^{W_F} [η]` where `upperGroup F α ⊆ W_L`, `η`
  is nontrivial on it, and `η` is trivial on every element of `W_L` whose image lies in
  `baseUpperAbove F α`.

## Proof (as implemented)

- **Right limit.** The kernel of `A` is open because `A` is continuous
  (`localWeilContinuity`). Its intersection with `localInertia F`, transported to
  `F.absoluteInertia` by `localInertiaEquiv F`, is an open subgroup of a compact group, so
  its image `H` in the absolute Galois group is closed. Every
  `absoluteUpperRamificationGroup F w` with `w > a` lies in inertia
  (`absoluteUpperCanonicalInertia`) and acts trivially, so it lies in `H`. Since `H` is
  closed, it contains the closure of their union, which is `absoluteUpperRightLimit F a`
  (`Subgroup.topologicalClosure_minimal`). An element of the Weil group whose projection
  lies in `H` is the inertia element given by `localInertiaEquiv`
  (`localProjection_injective`), so `A` is trivial on it.
- **Above the break.** This is one direction of the Library's
  `lastBreak_lt_iff_isTrivialAt`.
- **Commuting.** `localProjection_commutator_mem_closedRightLimit` puts the projection of
  `(yx)⁻¹(xy)` into `absoluteUpperRightLimit F α`. The right-limit step makes `A` trivial
  on `(yx)⁻¹(xy)`, so `A(x)A(y) = A(y)A(x)`.
- **No fixed vectors.** `upperFixedSpace_eq_bot_or_eq_top` says the `upperGroup F α`-fixed
  space is `⊥` or `⊤` for irreducible `A`. If it were `⊤`, `A` would be trivial at `α`,
  and `lastBreak_lt_iff_isTrivialAt` would give `α < α`.
- **Brauer step.** For `b = [A]`, unfold `realizeAtBase` on the generator
  (`LocalWeil.realizeExact_ofRepresentation`, `ExactGrothendieckGroup.restrict_ofRepresentation`)
  to the class of the restriction `B` of `A` to the base Weil subgroup. `B` is continuous,
  and it is irreducible because the base inclusion is surjective (`mem_baseWeil`). Then
  apply `weilAbelianNormalBrauer_kernel` with `N = baseUpperGroup F α` (normal by
  `baseUpperGroup_normal`, in inertia by `baseUpperGroup_le_inertia` and
  `absoluteUpperInertia`) and `K = baseUpperAbove F α`. The hypotheses are the three facts
  above; triviality on `K` follows from `iSup₂_le` and `isTrivialAt_of_lastBreak_lt`.

## Provenance

The facts about `W^α` and the Brauer step are those of DH81 4.5 cited above. The
commutator estimate itself is the Library's
`localProjection_commutator_mem_closedRightLimit`. `baseUpperAbove` is an internal
definition that names the subgroup on which the induced characters must be trivial, so
that 4.5 can compute their conductors.
