# Hilbert's formula for the different

Helper module for the proof of `TopLayerNormTraceStatement`
(`DeligneHenniartTopLayerProof.md`). Let `M/K` be a finite Galois extension of
p-adic fields with arbitrary topological data `TK` and `TM`. Write
`G = Gal(M/K)` and `G_i = lowerRamificationGroup TM K i`. The module proves
Hilbert's formula (Serre, *Local Fields*, IV §1, Proposition 4, registered as
`serre-1979-local-fields`): the different of `O_M/O_K` is

`differentIdeal TK.Integers TM.Integers = m_M ^ Σ_{i < N} (|G_i| - 1)`

for every `N` with `G_N = 1` (`differentIdeal_eq_maximalIdeal_pow`). The
integer rings carry `relativeIntegerAlgebra TK TM`, and `|G_i|` is
`lowerGroupOrder TM K i`.

## Proof

- **A generator.** The published port
  `IsDiscreteValuationRing.exists_adjoin_singleton_eq_top_of_isSeparable_residueField`
  gives `θ ∈ O_M` with `O_K[θ] = O_M`. Its hypotheses hold here.
  - The map `O_K → O_M` is injective and local.
  - `O_M` is finite over `O_K` (`NYLL.relativeIntegerStructure`).
  - The residue fields are finite, hence perfect, so the residue extension
    is separable.
- **Different of a monogenic extension.** The published
  `LocalClassField.different_eq_span_prod_automorphisms_of_integer_adjoin`
  gives `different = (Π_{σ ≠ 1} (θ - σ θ))`.
- **Testing on the generator** (`sub_mem_span_of_adjoin_eq_top`,
  `mem_lowerRamificationGroup_iff_of_adjoin_eq_top`). Every `σ z - z` lies in
  the ideal generated by `σ θ - θ`. The proof is induction over
  `O_K[θ]`, using `σ(ab) - ab = σa (σb - b) + (σa - a) b`. Hence
  `σ ∈ G_i ⟺ σ θ - θ ∈ m_M^{i+1}`.
- **Depths** (`lowerDepth`, `span_sub_eq_maximalIdeal_pow_lowerDepth`).
  - `lowerDepth TM N σ` is the number of `i < N` with `σ ∈ G_i`.
  - For `σ ≠ 1` and `G_N = 1`, `θ - σ θ ≠ 0`. Write `d = ord_M(θ - σ θ)`.
    Then `σ ∈ G_i ⟺ i + 1 ≤ d`, and `d ≤ N`, so `lowerDepth TM N σ = d`.
  - Hence `(θ - σ θ) = m_M^{lowerDepth TM N σ}`.
- **Counting** (`sum_lowerDepth_erase_one`). Exchanging the sums gives
  `Σ_{σ ≠ 1} lowerDepth TM N σ = Σ_{i < N} (|G_i| - 1)`.
- The product of the principal ideals is `m_M` to the sum of the depths.
  This gives the formula.

The proof follows Serre's, and the `lowerDepth` bookkeeping follows the
Anthropic FLT formalization (`Def_DifferentFiltrationFormula`). It is written
directly for the published p-adic integer rings and lower ramification
groups.
