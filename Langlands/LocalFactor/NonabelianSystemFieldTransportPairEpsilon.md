# Arithmetic pair epsilon factors along an isomorphism of p-adic fields

Author: Worker 5731. This is a supporting lemma module for the proof of
`Langlands.Global.PoleComparison.HenniartCompositeFactorStatement` (Task 5230).
It is internal derived infrastructure: it states no new published mathematics
and has no external source. It shows that the Library's arithmetic pair epsilon
certificates (`Correspondence.IsArithmeticPairEpsilonFactor`, and their
multiplicity-weighted products `LocalWeil.IsUnitaryPairEpsilonFactor`) are carried
along an isomorphism of p-adic fields, using the transported local-constant system
of `Langlands.LocalFactor.NonabelianSystemFieldTransportSystem` (Worker 5130).

Throughout, `F` and `E` are p-adic fields with canonical topologies,
`g : F.Carrier ≃ₐ[ℚ_p] E.Carrier`, `σ` is the closure isomorphism `F̄ ≅ Ē` over `g`,
and `weilEquiv F E g : W_E ≅ W_F` is the Library's `extensionWeilEquiv`. On the
`E` side there are a reciprocity family `eE`, an assignment `ε` with
`hε : IsSystem E eE ε`, and a nontrivial additive character `ψ` of `E`. On the
`F` side there are the transported family and assignment
`transportFamily F E g eE`, `transportAssignment F E g ε` with a proof `hε'` that
they form a system, and a character `ψ_F` of `F` with `hψ : ∀ x, ψ (g x) = ψ_F x`.

## Unitary virtual classes (namespace `Langlands.VirtualRepresentation.LocalWeil`)

For any `φ : W_E ≃ₜ* W_F`:

- `restrictUnitaryLatticeEquiv_eq_equivMapDomain`: restriction of unitary virtual
  Weil classes along `φ` is `Finsupp.equivMapDomain` along the unitary basis
  bijection `restrictUnitaryBasisEquiv φ` (definitional).
- `restrictUnitaryBasisEquiv_mem_support_iff`: `φ`-restriction of a basis class is
  in the support of the restricted virtual class exactly when the basis class is
  in the support of the original one.
- `isUnitaryPairEpsilonFactor_restrictUnitaryLatticeEquiv`: suppose every
  basis-level certificate `IsArithmeticPairEpsilonFactor F … a b c m` (for some
  data on `F`) gives `IsArithmeticPairEpsilonFactor E … (φ^* a) (φ^* b) c m` (for
  some data on `E`). Then every virtual certificate
  `IsUnitaryPairEpsilonFactor F … x y e` gives
  `IsUnitaryPairEpsilonFactor E … (φ^* x) (φ^* y) e`. The proof relabels the
  basis-level certificates along the bijection; the products and sums defining `e`
  are unchanged because `equivMapDomain` only relabels the supports.

## Bottom-field data over an arbitrary intermediate field

`Correspondence.IsArithmeticPairEpsilonFactor K eC ε hε ψ X Y c m` (Library) says,
over the bottom field `baseExtension K` of `K̄`: admissible data exist; a geometric
Frobenius exists; and for all admissible data, all Frobenius lifts and all `s`, the
conditional pair epsilon value is `epsilonMonomial q c m s`. The bottom field
`σ(⊥)` of `Ē` reached from `F̄` equals `baseExtension E` only propositionally. So the
data and value clauses are restated over an arbitrary intermediate field `L` of
`K̄`:

- `IsImageCharacter K L ψ ψ'`: the character `ψ'` of `L` satisfies
  `ψ'(y) = ψ(y)` for every `y ∈ K`, viewed in `L` through `algebraMap`.
- `IsRestrictionDatum K L X A`: the pair `A` over `L` is equivalent to the
  restriction to `W_K(L)` of some representative of `X`. This is the body of
  `Correspondence.IsBaseRestriction` with `baseExtension K` replaced by `L`.
- `IsEpsilonFactorAt K eC ε hε L ψ X Y c m`: the data clause and the value clause of
  `IsArithmeticPairEpsilonFactor`, with `baseExtension K` replaced by `L` and
  `IsBaseCharacter` replaced by `IsImageCharacter`. The Frobenius clause is left out.

Lemmas:

- `isBaseCharacter_iff_isImageCharacter`: on the bottom field, `IsBaseCharacter`
  is `IsImageCharacter`. The proof uses Mathlib's `IntermediateField.botEquiv_def`
  and `botEquiv_symm`.
- `isArithmeticPairEpsilonFactor_iff`:
  `IsArithmeticPairEpsilonFactor K … ψ X Y c m` holds exactly when
  `IsEpsilonFactorAt K … (baseExtension K) ψ X Y c m` holds and a geometric
  Frobenius over `baseExtension K` exists.
- `IsRestrictionDatum.nonempty_equiv`: a restriction datum of `X` is equivalent to
  the restriction of every representative of `X`. This repeats the Library's
  `IsBaseRestriction.nonempty_equiv` with `L` in place of the bottom field.

## Comparison at `σ(⊥)` (namespace `Langlands.LocalFactor.NonabelianSystem.FieldTransport`)

- `mapExtension_baseExtension`: `σ(⊥) = ⊥`, that is,
  `mapExtension F E g (baseExtension F) = baseExtension E`. It follows from
  `mapField_bot`.
- `isImageCharacter_additiveCharacterMap`: if `ψ'` agrees with `ψ_F` on the image
  of `F` in `L`, then `additiveCharacterMap F E g L ψ'` agrees with `ψ` on the image
  of `E` in `σ(L)`. The transported character is `y ↦ ψ'(σ⁻¹ y)`, and
  `σ⁻¹(y) = g⁻¹(y)` for `y ∈ E`.
- `additiveCharacterMap_eq_of_isImageCharacter`: over `σ(⊥)`, which is generated by
  the image of `E`, a character agreeing with `ψ` on that image equals the
  transported character.
- `isSelfDual_map_iff`: the transported character and the pushed-forward measure
  are self-dual exactly when the original ones are. This is the Library's
  `PadicField.AbelianTransport.isSelfDual_iff` for `fieldEquiv F E g L`.
- `exists_haarMeasureMap_eq`: every measure on `σ(L)` that is self-dual for the
  transported character is the pushforward of a measure on `L` that is self-dual for
  the original character. This uses the Library's
  `PadicField.AbelianTransport.selfDualHaarEquiv`.
- `restrictRestrictEquiv L V`: restricting `V|_{W_F(L)}` along
  `subgroupWeilEquiv F E g L : W_E(σ(L)) ≅ W_F(L)` is the same representation as
  restricting `V ∘ weilEquiv F E g` to `W_E(σ(L))` (identity map).
- `datumEquiv f f'`: if `A` is a restriction datum of `V` over `L` (equivalence
  `f`) and `A'` is one of `V ∘ weilEquiv F E g` over `σ(L)` (equivalence `f'`), then
  restricting `A` along `subgroupWeilEquiv F E g L` is equivalent to `A'`.
- `transportPair L A`: the zero-monodromy pair over `σ(L)` whose representation is
  the Library's `universeModel` of the restriction of `A` along
  `subgroupWeilEquiv F E g L`. `isContinuous_transportPair` and
  `isRestrictionDatum_transportPair` show that it is continuous when `A` is, and
  that it is a restriction datum of the zero-monodromy class of `V ∘ weilEquiv F E g`
  when `A` is one of the zero-monodromy class of `V`.
- `isEpsilonFactorAt_transport` (the comparison at `σ(⊥)`): for
  `V, W : LocalWeilRepresentation F` with zero-monodromy classes `[V]`, `[W]`,
  `IsEpsilonFactorAt F (transportFamily …) (transportAssignment …) hε' ⊥ ψ_F [V] [W] c m`
  implies
  `IsEpsilonFactorAt E eE ε hε σ(⊥) ψ [V ∘ weilEquiv] [W ∘ weilEquiv] c m`.
  - Data: the transported character, the pushed-forward measure, and
    `transportPair` of the `F` data.
  - Value: given admissible data over `σ(⊥)`, the character is the transported one
    (`additiveCharacterMap_eq_of_isImageCharacter`), and the measure is the
    pushforward of a self-dual measure `μ_F` (`exists_haarMeasureMap_eq`). All pairs
    involved have zero monodromy (`monodromy_eq_zero_of_equiv`,
    `tensor_monodromy_eq_zero`), so by `conditionalEpsilonValue_of_monodromy_zero`
    both conditional pair epsilon values are the system evaluated at the spectral
    twist of the underlying class of the tensor product. By
    `transportAssignment_apply` and `virtualMap_virtualTwist`, the value over `F`
    is `ε` at `σ(⊥)` applied to the twist of `virtualMap` of the `F` class. By
    `virtualMap_ofRepresentation`, `restrictTensorIdEquiv`, `tensorEquivOfEquiv`
    and `datumEquiv`, that class is the `E` class. The value over `F` is
    `epsilonMonomial q_F c m s` by hypothesis (with the Library's
    `baseGeometricFrobenius F`), and `q_F = q_E` by
    `PadicField.CanonicalTransport.residueCardinality_eq`.

## The bottom field and unitary classes

- `isArithmeticPairEpsilonFactor_zeroMonodromy_transport`: with the notation above,
  `IsArithmeticPairEpsilonFactor F (transportFamily F E g eE) (transportAssignment F E g ε) hε' ψ_F [V] [W] c m`
  implies `IsArithmeticPairEpsilonFactor E eE ε hε ψ [V ∘ weilEquiv] [W ∘ weilEquiv] c m`.
  The proof uses `isArithmeticPairEpsilonFactor_iff` on both sides, rewrites
  `baseExtension E` as `σ(⊥)` by `mapExtension_baseExtension`, and applies
  `isEpsilonFactorAt_transport`. The Frobenius clause over `E` is the Library's
  `baseGeometricFrobenius E`.
- `isArithmeticPairEpsilonFactor_restrictUnitaryBasisEquiv`: the same for unitary
  basis classes `a`, `b` of `F` and their restrictions
  `restrictUnitaryBasisEquiv (weilEquiv F E g)`. It chooses representatives and
  uses Worker 5130's `unitaryBasisPairClass_eq_classOf` and
  `unitaryBasisPairClass_restrictUnitaryBasisEquiv`
  (`Langlands.Global.Factors.HenniartCompositeFactorWeil`).
- `isUnitaryPairEpsilonFactor_transport` (the interface used in the assembly):
  every `IsUnitaryPairEpsilonFactor F (transportFamily F E g eE) (transportAssignment F E g ε) hε' ψ_F x y c`
  gives
  `IsUnitaryPairEpsilonFactor E eE ε hε ψ (restrictUnitaryLatticeEquiv (weilEquiv F E g) x) (restrictUnitaryLatticeEquiv (weilEquiv F E g) y) c`.
  It combines the two previous results with
  `isUnitaryPairEpsilonFactor_restrictUnitaryLatticeEquiv`.

`isUnitaryPairEpsilonFactor_transport` depends only on the axioms `propext`,
`Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.
