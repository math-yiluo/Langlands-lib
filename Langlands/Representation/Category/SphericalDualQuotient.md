# Spherical quotients from smooth-dual embeddings

This internal construction works for a topological group with cofinal compact
open subgroups, with complex smooth representation carriers in any common
universe. Let `A` and `B` be admissible smooth objects, with `B` irreducible.
Suppose an actual injective intertwiner embeds `B` into the smooth
contragredient of `A`. Suppose also that `B` has a nonzero vector fixed by the
specified compact open subgroup `K`.

The constructed quotient map from `A` to the actual smooth contragredient of
`B` is pullback of smooth functionals along the embedding, composed with
canonical evaluation into the smooth bidual of `A`. Exactness of smooth duality
makes the pullback surjective; admissible bidual reflexivity makes canonical
evaluation surjective. Their composite is therefore surjective. The published
admissible duality result transfers irreducibility from `B` to its smooth
contragredient.

Compact-open fixed duality identifies the fixed smooth functionals with the
algebraic dual of the original fixed-vector space. A nonzero complex vector
space has a nonzero algebraic dual. This gives a nonzero `K`-fixed vector in the
smooth contragredient of `B`. The construction retains the actual surjective
intertwiner together with irreducibility and the nonzero fixed-space proofs;
the final lemma gives the corresponding irreducible-quotient relation.
