# Concrete coproducts of smooth complex representations

Author: Worker 1187. Derived internal infrastructure for small coproduct
preservation by parabolic induction.

The direct sum is the actual algebraic direct sum with componentwise group
action. Each vector is a finite sum of vectors in individual summands. Such a
component vector inherits an open stabilizer from its smooth representation;
finite sums are smooth by intersecting their open stabilizers. No group
compactness or finiteness condition is used.

The summand inclusions and coordinate projections are intertwiners. The cofan
of inclusions is a categorical coproduct: a cocone descends by the direct-sum
linear universal property, equivariance is checked on summands, and uniqueness
follows from the same generating-summand argument. The index universe is at
most the carrier universe, which is explicitly the maximum of the index and
remaining carrier parameters. The group universe is independent.

This module is category infrastructure and makes no claim about an induction
functor or the full represented-parabolic preservation statement.
