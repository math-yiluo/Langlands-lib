# Subrepresentations of actions with the same image

`subrepresentationOrderIsoOfRangeEq` identifies the lattices of subrepresentations of two
monoid actions on the same vector space when their images in the endomorphism algebra agree.
The isomorphism preserves each underlying submodule. It carries complementary subrepresentations
to complementary subrepresentations, giving `isSemisimpleRepresentation_iff_of_range_eq`.
