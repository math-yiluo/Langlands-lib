# Absolute convergence of individual Schur pair terms

Author: Worker 1767.

This internal analytic helper proves summability of the geometric weight
`r ^ sum α` over every finite tuple of natural exponents when `0 ≤ r < 1`.
The proof inducts on tuple length, using the head/tail equivalence and the
product of two nonnegative summable real series.

The fixed-rank exponential Jacobi–Trudi bounds in both supplied finite
alphabets then dominate the norm of every individual pair term by a constant
times such a geometric weight. Their reciprocal combined rate gives a positive
complex disk of absolute convergence for all natural tuple terms. Restricting
to antitone tuples preserves this absolute summability. The alphabets may have
arbitrary independent finite sizes; no distinctness or nonzero parameter
condition is used. Regrouping this individual-term sum as the Cauchy coefficient
series and identifying the quotient integral remain separate obligations.
