# Discrete torus bounds for actual bi-smooth matrix integrands

This internal derived module transfers the supplied matrix convergence bounds to genuine counting sums on the uniformizer torus. The definition `matrixUniformizerTorusIntegrand` evaluates the literal full-matrix integrand on `Iwasawa.torus pi e.toAdd`, keeping its spectral exponent and coefficient unchanged. Every coefficient belongs to the published finite complex span `biSmoothMatrixCoefficients σ`. No raw Whittaker convergence is assumed or asserted.

Every such coefficient is continuous and fixed on the right by an open subgroup. The proof uses smoothness of the vector for a generator and then induction over the finite linear span, intersecting stabilizers for addition. For every full square-matrix Schwartz test, the actual matrix integrand has a positive principal right stabilizer independent of the complex spectral exponent. The test is invariant under all right matrix translations in a neighborhood of the identity; the principal congruence basis shrinks this neighborhood together with the coefficient stabilizer. A compact principal subgroup has normalized determinant norm one, so it also fixes every spectral determinant weight.

The integrand is measurable: the test and coefficient are continuous, and complex exponentiation of the normalized absolute determinant is measurable. `tsum_enorm_biSmooth_matrixIntegrand_lt_top` applies DiscreteTorus to its genuine ambient integrability. It proves finiteness of the extended-norm sum of the same integrand on all independent integer powers of the supplied actual uniformizer. All ranks including the scalar boundary and arbitrary independent field and representation-carrier universes are retained.

`biSmooth_matrixTorus_convergesOnRight` preserves the exact common bound supplied by `Padic.MatrixConvergesOnRight`, uniformly over all tests, all actual bi-smooth coefficients, and all actual uniformizers. The spectral exponent remains exactly `s + jpssShift n`. The matrix test includes singular matrices; no test or coefficient family is replaced.

Provenance is source-free composition of the published matrix-coefficient laws, uniform local constancy of matrix-test translations, the actual principal congruence basis, the normalized determinant character on compact subgroups, and the Haar-restriction argument in DiscreteTorus. This is a convergence prerequisite for the gamma comparison, not a proof of that comparison or of raw Whittaker bounds.
