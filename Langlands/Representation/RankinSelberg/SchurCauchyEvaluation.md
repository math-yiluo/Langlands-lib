# Analytic Cauchy identity on individual partitions

Author: Worker 1767.

This internal derivation indexes natural antitone tuples by their total degree
and the existing finite degree-partition type. Forgetting each finite coordinate
bound is bijective: the total degree recovers that bound, and each coordinate
is at most the total degree. The Cauchy coefficient identity therefore identifies
the finite inner sum of individual Jacobi–Trudi pair terms with the scaled
coefficient. Absolute summability of the individual partition terms justifies
regrouping by the sigma type. The resulting sum is the previously constructed
complex Cauchy series value.

Finally intersect the disk of individual absolute convergence with the disk
of radius `(1 + sum of all parameter-product norms)^(-1)`. This second bound
makes every pair geometric series converge. The established series evaluation
then gives the inverse product of the factors `1 - x i * y j * t` as the actual
sum of all individual partition terms. The first alphabet has arbitrary finite
size and the second has size `n + 1`; repeated or zero parameters are allowed.
This is an analytic identity of symmetric-function values, independent of the
separate representation-theoretic identification of the quotient integral.
