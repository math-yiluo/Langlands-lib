# The self-pair denominator of a product of distinct unitary supercuspidals

For a uniform block product of pairwise inequivalent unitarizable supercuspidals, `unitaryCuspidalProduct_selfPair` constructs the certified self-pair denominator and computes its order at `1`. The smooth dual of the actual product is the product of the smooth duals. The tempered product formula and uniqueness identify the pair denominator as the product of the unitary Euler denominators of all ordered pairs of blocks. The parameters of each factor have absolute value one, so evaluation at the residue cardinality is nonzero. By smooth biduality a factor contributes a simple root at `1` exactly when the two original blocks are isomorphic. The diagonal pairs contribute one each, giving the block count.
