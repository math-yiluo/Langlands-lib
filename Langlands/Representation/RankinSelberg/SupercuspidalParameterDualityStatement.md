# Duality of contragredient twist parameters

This is an internal supporting statement for the JPSS (8.2) factor calculation.
The name records that application; the statement itself assumes only
admissibility, and does not assume irreducibility or supercuspidality.

For two admissible complex representations of the same positive-rank p-adic
general linear group, a complex number `a` is a contragredient twist parameter
of the original pair if and only if `a⁻¹` is a parameter of their smooth
contragredients. The parameter predicate is the existing one: `a=q^(-u)` and
twisting the first representation by `|det|^(-u)` gives an equivalence to the
smooth contragredient of the second. The field and both carrier universes are
independent. Inversion at zero causes no exceptional case because the
parameter predicate already excludes zero.

The derivation uses the published equivalence between the smooth dual of a
twist and the inverse-character twist of the smooth dual. The determinant
character has open kernel. Dualizing the twist equivalence therefore gives
the forward implication while replacing `u` by `-u`. For the converse,
dualize again and transport along the canonical smooth bidual equivalences,
which are bijective for admissible representations of these groups.

This statement does not assert a pair L-polynomial formula, a norm bound on
parameters, or existence of Rankin–Selberg factors. It supplies the parameter
inversion needed to compare original and dual supercuspidal products.
