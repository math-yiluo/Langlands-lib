# Actual first-block product fibers

This derived helper uses the real integral of the raw external-rectangle norm,
rather than the norm of a signed average. Joint continuity makes this profile
measurable, and actual block-quotient product convergence makes it integrable.
The exact normalized first-block kernel composition transfers this convergence
to almost every first-block fiber over mixed determinant slice zero. Its outer
real norm profile is measurable and integrable.

The representative formula for the kernel identifies the profile with the
actual small-quotient integral of the actual rectangle norm. Every rectangle
fiber is integrable, so the product-integrability criterion gives convergence
on the actual small quotient times the actual external rectangle. Only then is
Fubini used to exchange those two integrals in the first-block average. All
supplied Haar and quotient measures and arbitrary complementary rectangle sizes
are retained, and zero supplied functionals are allowed. No small epsilon
equation enters the proof.

This is derived integration infrastructure for JPSS (1983), Section 4.6 equation
(6), printed page 412, and Sections 4.9 and 4.10(2), printed pages 416–419. It does
not assert the lower convergence or final Laurent identity. Source:
