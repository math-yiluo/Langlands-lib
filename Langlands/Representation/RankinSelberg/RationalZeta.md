# Laurent modules of rational zeta values

This module constructs the algebraic objects used after rational continuation of
the JPSS integrals. The coefficient ring is Mathlib's actual Laurent polynomial
ring over the complex numbers, and the ambient field is `RatFunc ℂ`. The
embedding sends the Laurent variable to the rational indeterminate `X`. Its
injectivity is proved by clearing a power of the Laurent variable and using
injectivity of the polynomial embedding.
The ambient field is proved to be the fraction field of this Laurent ring.
Principality of Laurent ideals follows by extending the contracted ideal in
the polynomial PID through the localization at powers of `X`.

For a set of rational functions, `zetaSpan` is its Laurent submodule.
`HasCommonDenominator` requires one nonzero Laurent polynomial that clears every
denominator. The proof identifies this condition with Mathlib's `IsFractional`
predicate for the generated submodule. `fractionalZetaIdeal` then packages that
submodule as an actual fractional ideal, using the supplied proof of the
condition. Pointwise rationality by itself is not asserted to provide a common
denominator.
Mathlib's principality theorem for fractional ideals over a PID then proves that
the resulting zeta span has a rational generator. This is ordinary
principality; it does not supply the more restrictive normalized generator.

`IsNormalizedGenerator values P` states that `P` has constant coefficient one
and that the actual generated submodule is spanned by `1/P`. Such a polynomial
is proved nonzero, and its existence implies the common-denominator condition.
An equivalent criterion separates the two concrete obligations: multiplying
each generating value by `P` gives a Laurent polynomial, and `1/P` belongs to
the generated module. The forward implication uses membership in a singleton
span. The converse proves both submodule inclusions, using multiplication by
the nonzero embedded polynomial to recover the Laurent multiple of `1/P`.
Uniqueness of the normalized polynomial is proved. If a polynomial with
constant term one divides another polynomial after Laurent extension, clearing
a power of `X` gives polynomial divisibility after multiplication by that power.
The first polynomial is coprime to `X`, as follows directly from division by
`X` and its constant coefficient, so the power can be cancelled from the
divisibility statement. Equality of the two reciprocal spans therefore gives
mutual polynomial divisibility. The polynomials differ by a constant unit;
their constant coefficients force that unit to be one.

`NormalizedGeneratorExists values` is the explicit remaining existence
proposition. Only from a proof of it does `normalizedPolynomial` select the
unique polynomial. `normalizedFactor` constructs its reciprocal as a unit in
the rational function field and proves that it generates the actual span.
The uniqueness theorem identifies this construction with any polynomial
satisfying the defining relation. This is a constructor on proven normalized
modules, not yet a factor attached to arbitrary generic representations.
Rational continuation of their actual integrals and the analytic existence
prerequisite remain outstanding; this module does not supply those proofs.

## Original source and correspondence

H. Jacquet, I. I. Piatetski-Shapiro and J. A. Shalika, *Rankin--Selberg
Convolutions*, American Journal of Mathematics 105 (1983), 367--464,
Theorem 2.7(ii), printed pages 390--391. The source uses the variable
`X = q^(-s)`, the coefficient ring `ℂ[X, X⁻¹]`, and the fractional ideal
generated by the rational continuations of the zeta integrals. Its normalized
generator is `P(X)⁻¹` with `P(0)=1`. This module isolates that algebraic
construction and normalization relation; it does not assume or prove the
source's analytic rationality, convergence or functional equation. Ordinary
principality here is derived from the common-denominator hypothesis. Uniqueness
of a special generator is proved algebraically, and selection is conditional
on its stated existence relation. The source's analytic prerequisites and
existence assertion remain unproved.

## Dependencies and scope

There are no project mathematical dependencies. The environment is
`2ecc41bc-2901-4e34-96c5-6d98fbb3c74d`, version 1, with Lean 4.34.0
and Mathlib commit `5ed2965256430c3649e86755f9576b54eca72435`.
The construction reuses `LaurentPolynomial.eval₂`,
`LaurentPolynomial.induction_on_mul_T`, `Submodule.span`,
`FractionalIdeal.isFractional_span_iff`, `IsLocalization.map_under`,
`IsFractionRing.of_field`, and `FractionalIdeal.isPrincipal`.
Uniqueness also uses `IsCoprime.dvd_of_dvd_mul_right`, polynomial units being
constant, and the existing association theorem for mutual divisibility.
The coefficients are specifically complex; no coefficient-ring generalization
or topology on a representation space is introduced.
