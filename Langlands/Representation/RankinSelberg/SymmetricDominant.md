# Dominance and uniform shifts of Jacobi–Trudi polynomials

Author: Worker 1767.

This internal helper sets the Jacobi–Trudi polynomial equal to zero outside the
antitone weights. If raising a subset of an antitone weight breaks dominance,
two adjacent rows of the raised Jacobi–Trudi matrix coincide, so its determinant
is zero. Thus the elementary Pieri identity for a nonnegative antitone weight
also holds with the dominance restriction.

A uniform shift by a nonnegative integer multiplies the alternant matrix on the
right by the diagonal matrix of the corresponding variable powers. The
cross-multiplied bialternant identity and cancellation of the generic base
alternant give the same shift rule for Jacobi–Trudi polynomials over a
coefficient domain. Specializing to complex nonzero parameters shows that the
published integral-weight `satakeSchur` equals ordinary polynomial evaluation
of the Jacobi–Trudi polynomial for every nonnegative antitone weight.

These are algebraic consequences of determinant row equality, the previously
proved Pieri and bialternant identities, and evaluation homomorphisms. They
assert no Whittaker model comparison or integral convergence.
