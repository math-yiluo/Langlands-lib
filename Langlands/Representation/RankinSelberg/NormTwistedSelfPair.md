# Euler polynomials and orders for twisted self-pairs

A square-integrable irreducible positive-rank action and its smooth dual are generic by the square-integrable genericity theorem and smooth duality. A determinant twist by a character with open kernel preserves genericity. The generic Rankin–Selberg construction therefore supplies a certified pair polynomial for the action paired with the twisted dual.

If the original action is supercuspidal, its dual and twisted dual are supercuspidal as well. The equal-rank supercuspidal formula computes the order at `1` as the indicator of contragredience. The dual of the twisted dual is the inverse twist of the original action, using smooth biduality. Twisting the intertwiner back gives the equivalent stabilizer test using the original character.
