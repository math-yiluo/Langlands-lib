# Growth bounds for symmetric polynomial values

Author: Worker 1767.

This internal analytic helper chooses the rate `1 + sum_i norm (x i)`, at least
one and larger than each parameter norm. The complete generating series is
absolutely evaluable at its reciprocal by the finite geometric-product identity.
Each weighted coefficient is bounded by the sum of all weighted coefficient
norms, giving a positive uniform exponential coefficient bound.

Negative complete symmetric degrees are zero. A bound on the degree therefore
gives a common bound for every entry of a generalized Jacobi–Trudi matrix on a
natural weight tuple. The Leibniz determinant formula bounds its norm by the
number of permutations times the common entry bound to the matrix rank.
Rearranging the powers produces a positive constant and a rate at least one
whose power in the total weight bounds every fixed-rank Jacobi–Trudi value.
The constants are coarse; no optimal convergence region is claimed.
