# Finite products of explicit gamma expressions

`Langlands.RankinSelberg.TemperedLFactorGamma.rationalGammaExpression_prod` states that the explicit rational gamma expression multiplies over any finite index type: constants multiply, integer exponents add, and the numerator and reflected denominator polynomials multiply. The proof distributes products and uses the nonvanishing of the rational-function variable for integer powers.

This is the existing lemma moved from [TemperedLFactorGamma](TemperedLFactorGamma.md), with its name, statement and proof unchanged, for reuse by the spherical corank-one calculation.
