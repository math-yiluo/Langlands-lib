# Limits of the actual adjacent model integrals

This internal prerequisite proves pointwise convergence of the literal adjacent-rank model integrals under a sequence of Schwartz tests bounded in norm by one fixed Schwartz test and eventually equal to it at every full-row-rank rectangular matrix. The rank, field and original module universes remain unrestricted. The real twist uses the original representation-uniform square-matrix threshold, and the additive Haar measure is the supplied one.

Every rectangle sampled in the translated double kernel factors as `[h,0]` times an upper column matrix and the ambient group element. It therefore has full row rank. The sequence of double kernels is pointwise eventually equal to the limiting kernel; its norms are dominated by the norm of that kernel. The previously established convergence on the row-times-general-linear product measure makes this bound integrable. Dominated convergence and Fubini give convergence of the iterated integrals. Multiplication by the fixed outer determinant character and modulus, followed by the exact partial Fourier comparison, proves convergence of the actual model integral at every ambient group element.

The module also bundles coefficients of the one identity Jacquet integral as `realTwistCoefficientMap`. Its value at a section and ambient group element is the literal Jacquet integral at that element. Additivity and scalar linearity use the all-section convergence theorem. This contribution does not assert closedness of the coefficient range, full image equality, or nonvanishing of the common functional; those require further arguments.
