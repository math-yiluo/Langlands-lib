# Complete gamma certificates from fixed-functional transport

This internal derived assembly proves that actual determinant twists transport
the complete equal-rank and unequal-rank gamma-factor relations whenever they
transport the corresponding fixed-functional equations.

For each relation, the proof retains the original compatible-measure existence
certificate and twists the two supplied nonzero functionals. To prove the
universal clause, it takes an arbitrary compatible quotient measure and
arbitrary nonzero functionals of the twisted representations, untwists those
functionals, and applies the original universal clause and the supplied
fixed-functional transport. Twisting and untwisting retain the underlying
linear maps, so nonvanishing and the requested target functionals are preserved
directly.

The fixed-functional premise carries all nonzero rational witnesses, Fourier
partners in equal rank, and every rectangular decomposition in unequal rank.
This module preserves that complete premise. It does not assume or construct
the fixed-functional transport itself, and it does not assert initial gamma
factor existence.
