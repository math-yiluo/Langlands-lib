# Actual standard-module tail divisibility

Author: Worker 111.

This internal adapter transfers the published closed tail-divisibility theorem
from its literal two lifted carriers to an arbitrary actual two-block shape.
It uses actual representation equivalences for both the carrier lifts and the
two-block family. Uniqueness transfers its existential certified polynomials
to the supplied certificates.

The arbitrary-rank full Whittaker-type predicate uses the existing full type
at positive successor rank and is false at rank zero. Complete arbitrary-rank
L-polynomial certificates are transported by the published
`IsPadicRankLPolynomial.congr_equiv` of
[segment-pair pole orders](SegmentPairPoleOrder.md); no copy of that lemma is
declared here.

For a genuine Langlands datum with at least two blocks, each twisted block has
full type by the analytic-tempered real-twist adapter. The genuine tail has
positive rank and full type by the standard-module adapter. The actual ordered
head/tail regrouping equivalence then transfers the two-block result to the
original standard module. Its certified tail polynomial divides the original
standard polynomial against any full-type positive-rank test representation.
The supplied additive character and self-dual Haar measure are retained.

The provenance is internal composition of the published closed
[tail theorem](InducedTailLFactorProof.md), the actual
[head/tail standard-module equivalence](../Classification/LanglandsHeadTailStandardModule.md),
[heterogeneous-pair comparisons](../Induction/HeterogeneousConstruction.md),
and [full standard-module type](LanglandsTypeLPolynomialData.md).
