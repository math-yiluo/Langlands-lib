# Denominator roots give contragredient twists

This internal derived unit constructs `equalRankVectorResidue`, the
denominator-cleared Laurent continuation on the original representation
vectors, evaluated at a nonzero complex number. It assumes the actual
equal-rank analytic conclusion and a supplied normalized generator P.
The normalized-generator equality makes this trilinear form nonzero at
every nonzero point. Simultaneous right translation multiplies its value
by the corresponding integer monomial, by injectivity of the Laurent
embedding and the translation law of the actual integral continuations.

For the canonical p-adic topology, assume the full supercuspidal Kirillov
statement, an irreducible supercuspidal first representation, a nonzero
standard Whittaker functional, and an invariant quotient measure. At a root
q^u of P, Laurent regularity for tests vanishing at zero makes the cleared
form vanish on the kernel of row evaluation at zero. Its nonvanishing forces
that evaluation functional to be nonzero. Rescaling a test with nonzero
value therefore supplies a test of value one, and the published algebraic
descent theorem gives a nonzero bilinear form on the original vectors.
The normalized determinant-order formula identifies its covariance
with the actual weight |det|^u.

If the second representation is additionally irreducible and admissible,
the published p-adic covariant-pairing theorem identifies the first
representation twisted by |det|^(-u) with the smooth contragredient of the
second. This is the sign and contragredient convention in the target
equal-rank L-factor statement. The full Kirillov and analytic conclusions
remain explicit premises of this unit. The converse assertion that every
such twist gives a root, and the final polynomial product formula, are not
proved here.
