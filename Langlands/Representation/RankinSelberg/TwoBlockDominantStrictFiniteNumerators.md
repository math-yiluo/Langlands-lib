The test rank `r+1` is strictly larger than the induced rank `t+1`, and the independent universes and dependent ordered block carriers are retained.

`StrictTwoBlockFiniteNumerators` chooses both complete first-block factor certificates before every complementary row pair, section and actual large-model coefficient. For the actual induced coefficient series `a` and actual transported dual coefficient series `aD`, it asks for a unique Laurent polynomial `R` whose finite series is `(P1*P2).toLaurent` acting on `a`. The theorem then applies `twoBlock_strictInducedCoefficient_numerators`. Above one real bound, it returns absolute ordinary Jacquet integrability, a nonzero realizing functional, and the finite-numerator package for every compatible choice of the three quotient measures.
