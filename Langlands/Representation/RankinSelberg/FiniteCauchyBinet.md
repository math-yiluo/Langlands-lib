# Ordered finite Cauchy–Binet expansion

Author: Worker 1767.

This internal algebraic helper enumerates every injection from `Fin m` into a
linear order by its image subset and a permutation of the increasing subset
order. Row multilinearity expands the determinant of a finite rectangular
matrix product over all index functions. Repeated rows kill noninjective index
choices. Reindexing the injections by ordered subsets and permutations turns
the inner sum into the determinant of the first minor, multiplied by the
determinant of the second minor. The result is the finite Cauchy–Binet formula
over every commutative ring.
