# Normalized JPSS epsilon factors under a local-field isomorphism

`LocalFieldEpsilonTransportStatement` and `localFieldEpsilonTransport` apply to two nonarchimedean local fields with their existing value relations and topologies. Given a bicontinuous field isomorphism `σ : F ≃+* E`, their actual residue cardinalities agree. For supplied additive characters satisfying `ψ' (σ x) = ψ x` and regular additive Haar measures satisfying `ν'.measure = ν.measure.map σ` in the Borel structures, the actual symmetric JPSS epsilon certificates correspond under entrywise pullback of both representations. The two positive ranks and both complex carrier spaces are independent. The coefficient in `ℂˣ` and exponent in `ℤ` are unchanged. No characteristic-zero, self-duality, irreducibility, or genericity premise is needed for this equivalence of certificates.

The proof restricts `σ` to the actual valuation rings using `mem_integers_iff_of_continuous_ringEquiv`. The induced residue-field equivalence gives equality of cardinalities. For two nonzero elements, comparison of their integer orders is equivalent to integrality of their ratio. This comparison carries positivity of order across `σ`; comparison with the inverse image of the target uniformizer then forces the image of the source uniformizer to have order one. Comparing any nonzero element with the power of the source uniformizer of its own order yields equality of orders. The normalized absolute values now agree by their definitions in terms of the residue cardinality and order; at zero both vanish.

The valuation-ring comparison also transports the normalized group and upper-unipotent Haar measures through `generalLinearHaar_map` and `unipotentHaar_map`. The additive Haar measure on the target is finite on compact sets, and the target local field is sigma-compact, so this measure is sigma-finite. With these data and the common residue cardinality, `isPairEpsilonFactor_comp_iff` transports the pair certificate using the actual quotient-integral equations. Splitting on the rank order gives the symmetric version, with the same order on both fields.

This is an internal normalization and transport lemma for the distinguished-place step of the conductor identity for the sigma-lift of a supercuspidal representation (`Langlands.BaseChange.CuspidalSigmaLiftConductorStatement`). It specializes the Library's field-transport lemmas for JPSS pair factors. It neither supplies epsilon certificates nor relates factors of different local fields in a cyclic extension. In particular, it gives no global lift, global functional equation, or conductor identity.
