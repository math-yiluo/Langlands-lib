# Functional equations under fixed-character exchange

This supporting module transports rational functional equations by
the nonzero scalar supplied by the actual dual-integral transport. It proves
that multiplication by a nonzero constant preserves denominator regularity,
using reduced-denominator divisibility in both directions. Thus the equation
is transported on its entire stated domain.

If the old second-model scalar is beta and its square is one, multiplication
of the dual continuation by alpha^n beta^n changes beta^n into alpha^n. The
same algebra applies to the normalized epsilon equation and to the gamma
equation. The rational gamma function and epsilon monomial are retained.

The coefficient-equation lemmas use surjectivity of both translated model
maps to cover arbitrary target vectors. The original continuation and
Fourier pair are reused. The dual continuation is multiplied by the checked
dual-integral constant; original generators transfer by equality of value
sets and dual generators by equality of Laurent spans. The nonzero original
second functional makes its scalar involutive.

These are internal derived algebraic lemmas for the direct fixed-character
JPSS exchange proof. `EqualRankSwapFactors` lifts them to the universal
p-adic factor relations.
