# Equivalence invariance of full Whittaker type

This internal construction transports the published full `RankinSelberg.IsOfWhittakerType` predicate through an actual representation equivalence. The source and target carrier universes are independent. It preserves the same ambient general linear group, upper-unipotent subgroup and character. It assumes no irreducibility, finite-dimensionality, genericity, or preselected Whittaker functional.

For any commutative coefficient ring and group, `Langlands.representationAsModuleEquiv` promotes the supplied intertwining equivalence to a linear equivalence over the group algebra. Its forward and inverse maps are the original equivalence and its inverse under the existing `asModuleEquiv` identifications. The construction reuses Mathlib's `Representation.IntertwiningMap.equivLinearMapAsModule`; `finite_asModule_iff_of_equiv` then follows from `Module.Finite.equiv_iff`.

`Langlands.Whittaker.functionalsCongr` is the linear equivalence of entire character-equivariant functional spaces. The forward map sends a functional `l` to `l` precomposed with the inverse representation equivalence, and the inverse precomposes with the original equivalence. Both directions reuse the published `functionalsComap`. The inverse identities follow from the two inverse identities of the supplied equivalence, evaluated on every vector. Application lemmas state both formulas.

For representations of `GL_(n+1)(F)` over a topological field, `RankinSelberg.IsOfWhittakerType.of_equiv` combines the existing admissibility transport, the group-algebra finite-generation transport above, and composition of the functional-space equivalence with the given equivalence to complex scalars. `RankinSelberg.isOfWhittakerType_iff_of_equiv` applies this argument in both directions.

The provenance is algebraic and internal: these constructions are derived directly from the existing definitions and equivalences, including `RankinSelberg.AnalyticStatement`, `Whittaker.Transport`, and Mathlib's intertwining/group-algebra API. They preserve the JPSS Whittaker-type predicate already defined in the Library. No new analytic or literature theorem is asserted. In particular, this does not establish Whittaker type from genericity, nor its preservation by induction, nor existence or multiplicativity of local factors. It supplies the equivalence transport required in the finite-block gamma reduction and its carrier-universe lifts.
