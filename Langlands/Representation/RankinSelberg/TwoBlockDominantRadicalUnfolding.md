# Integrated radical unfolding of the unequal-rank section

This derived helper retains the actual unequal-rank matrix and the supplied
rectangular Haar measure in the first ordinary Jacquet unfolding. It constructs
the complex determinant weight on the standard unipotent quotient. The supplied
ordinary Jacquet realization identifies the signed radical average at each
external rectangle with the actual unequal-rank JPSS integrand.

A finite Schwartz expansion separates the external rectangle from diagonal
coefficient tests, including the empty expansion for a zero supplied functional.
For each diagonal term, raw absolute Jacquet convergence and compact norm support
on a determinant slice give actual integrability and the normalized radical
identity. The finite separated sums prove genuine product integrability on both
the standard quotient and the block quotient. Fubini and termwise unfolding then
give the integrated identity with exactly the radical integral-cube mass.
Convergence is supplied separately before any change of integration order.

This is internal infrastructure for JPSS (1983), Sections 4.5, 4.6, 4.9 and
4.10(2), printed pages 408–412 and 416–419, with the arbitrary complementary
rectangle and determinant-slice form derived using the published finite
expansion. It proves no lower-kernel convergence or final Laurent identity.
