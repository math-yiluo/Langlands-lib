# Formal Schur coefficient identity

Author: Worker 1767.

This internal algebraic development reindexes the finite Cauchy–Binet
coefficient sum by partitions of the prescribed degree. The exponent staircase
accounts for the fixed degree of the base alternant. The coefficient minors
become Jacobi–Trudi determinants, and the monomial minors become weight
alternants, using the proved finite degree equivalence.

Column factorization of the generating-series determinant identifies this sum
with the base alternant times the corresponding coefficient of the product of
rescaled series. In generic polynomial variables over a coefficient domain,
the bialternant identity expresses every weight alternant as its Jacobi–Trudi
polynomial times the base alternant. Cancelling this nonzero generic polynomial
gives the formal Schur coefficient identity for an arbitrary input series.
The input series may itself have coefficients in the polynomial ring. The
result is entirely formal and imposes no analytic convergence hypothesis or
restriction on later specializations of the variables.
