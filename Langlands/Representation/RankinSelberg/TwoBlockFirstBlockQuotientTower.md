# Normalized first-block quotient tower

This derived module proves an exact full-quotient composition and its same-slice restrictions for the actual first-block fiber kernel. It uses the canonical p-adic structures of `PadicField F`, any positive two-block shape, the published block subgroup `H = diag(N_a,N_b)`, and the published mixed subgroup `L = diag(GL_a^0,N_b)`. The measures are the specified `blockHaar` on `H`, the restriction-normalized `normOneHaar` on `GL_a^0`, and the transported product `mixedHaar` on `L`.

`NormalizedTowerStatement F h νA hInv νL νH` says that the kernel `fiberKernel F h νA hInv 0` composes with `νL` to give `νH`, and composes with the restriction of `νL` to every determinant slice `k` to give the restriction of `νH` to the same slice `k`. The small quotient measure `νA` is assumed s-finite and invariant under right multiplication by norm-one matrices, as required to construct this kernel. The theorem `normalized_tower` proves the statement when `νA`, `νL`, and `νH` are compatible with the specified small, mixed, and block Haar measures and their ambient general linear Haar measures. No disintegration hypothesis is assumed, and no scalar occurs.

The proof has four steps.

1. `lintegral_fiberPoint_quotientAverageReal` integrates the nonnegative block average of a compactly supported continuous real function over the small determinant-zero slice. The block Haar integral is unfolded into the two specified unipotent integrals. Multiplying a block-unipotent element by the first-block embedding gives the first block `a*u` and the second block `b`. The measurable first-block marginal therefore integrates against the small orbital kernel. `small_orbitKernel_comp_zero` identifies its composite on the zero slice with the exact pushforward of `normOneHaar`. Tonelli and the definition of `mixedHaar` give precisely the mixed-subgroup average, at every ambient representative.
2. `lintegral_comp_quotientAverageReal` applies integration against a measure-valued kernel, quotient induction, and its everywhere pushforward formula. It identifies the composite measure's integral of each nonnegative compactly supported block average with the `νL` integral of the corresponding mixed average.
3. `fiberKernel_zero_comp` applies compatibility to identify these integrals with the ambient Haar integral. Positive lifting realizes every nonnegative compactly supported continuous function on `H \ G` as a block average. Compact bump functions then prove finiteness of the composite measure on compact sets. Hausdorffness, local compactness, and second countability give regularity, and regular measure extensionality on compactly supported nonnegative tests identifies the composite with `νH`. The compact-finiteness and positive-lifting organization is adapted from Worker 235's `TwoBlockUnipotentQuotientTower.comp_eq_smul`, here with different groups, fibers, and coefficient exactly one.
4. `fiberKernel_zero_comp_restrict` uses the published determinant-order formula for `fiberPoint`: the small determinant order plus the base determinant order is the target determinant order. On the small zero slice, the target is in slice `k` exactly when the base is. Applying this support identity to indicators and then the full composition proves the same-slice restricted identity.

These are internal consequences of the existing quotient, Haar, and compatibility APIs, supporting the published JPSS Section 4.6 integration target. They do not yet establish the target's formula with the outer measure restricted to zero and the small measure restricted to arbitrary `k`; the separate determinant-shift argument is required for that formula. This module also does not claim the target's Tonelli, absolute-integrability, or Bochner conclusions. Its proof route and restriction normalization are derived infrastructure, rather than an additional assertion attributed to the original paper.
