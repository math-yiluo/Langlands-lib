# Endomorphisms of the coinduction of a character

`IsCharacterCompatible H χ w` expresses agreement of the two restrictions of `χ` to `H ∩ w H w⁻¹`. In the Mackey pairing for the one-dimensional representation attached to `χ`, this term has dimension one when the restrictions agree and dimension zero otherwise. These are internal constructions from the subgroup and its character.

When `G` is finite, the right translates of the function `δ` supported on `H` and equal to `χ` there span the coinduced representation. For each covariant function `f`, the expansion is `|H|⁻¹ ∑_{x ∈ G} f(x) R_{x⁻¹} δ`, where `(R_y δ)(g) = δ(gy)`. At `g`, only the terms with `x ∈ Hg` can contribute. Each of those `|H|` summands equals `f(g)` before normalization.

The kernel of an intertwiner is its value on `δ`. The kernel is covariant on both sides, and a nonzero value at `w` forces character compatibility there. If a group antiautomorphism preserves the subgroup and character and fixes a representative of every compatible double coset, it fixes all kernels. For finite `G`, the expansion makes the kernel map injective and gives the multiplication formula. Changing the summation variable by the antiautomorphism exchanges the two factors, proving commutativity of the endomorphism algebra.
