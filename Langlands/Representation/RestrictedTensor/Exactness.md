# Injective and surjective maps of restricted tensor products

This module supplies two algebraic lemmas for the finite-place
subquotient construction in Langlands (1979), Lemma 1, printed p. 203. The
lemmas use the existing direct-limit restricted tensor product. Component
linear maps must preserve the reference family away from a finite set.

For injectivity, choose a linear left inverse at each factor. The left
inverses preserve the reverse reference family at the same places. The
composition and identity formulas for induced maps make their restricted
tensor map a left inverse. For surjectivity, enlarge the stage of a target
pure tensor to include the source exceptional set and the finite bound of
the map. Extend the pure tensor by target reference vectors and lift each
coordinate at that stage. Linearity extends the lift to every element of
the stage, and every element of the direct limit comes from a stage.

These are internal algebraic ingredients of the assertion on restricted
tensors in R. P. Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation*
(1979), Lemma 1, printed p. 203 (PDF p. 198 of the Corvallis Part I volume,
`langlands-1979-notion-automorphic-representation`).
