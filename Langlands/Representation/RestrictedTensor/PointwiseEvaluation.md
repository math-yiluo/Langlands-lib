# Pointwise evaluation of restricted tensors

`pointwiseEvaluation` evaluates a restricted tensor at a tuple of local points. Its values lie in the restricted tensor of the coefficient spaces. Each local evaluation sends its source reference vector to its coefficient reference vector outside a specified finite exceptional set. Thus the evaluations use that set as a common finite bound for the induced maps. `pointwiseEvaluation_pure` and `pointwiseEvaluation_of` describe evaluation on pure tensors and on arbitrary vectors represented on a finite stage.

`pointwiseEvaluation_injective` assumes that each local family of evaluations distinguishes vectors, the local point types are inhabited, and the coefficient references are nonzero outside the exceptional set. On a finite stage, compose the evaluations with all linear functionals on the coefficients. Use the resulting scalar tests as the basis indices of free vector spaces. Each factor then embeds in the dual of its free test space. The tensor of those embeddings followed by the published tensor pairing is injective. Its values on the tensor basis of tests are determined by the coefficient-valued evaluations. For a restricted tensor, extend each tuple of stage points to the full family and use the injectivity of the stage inclusion on the coefficients.

The construction and injectivity are internal consequences of the restricted tensor maps and the algebraic tensor pairing. Flath (1979), §2, pp. 180–181 (PDF 176–177), gives the algebraic direct limit and induced maps with reference preservation outside a finite set.
