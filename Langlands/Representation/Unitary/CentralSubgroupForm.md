# Positive forms from a prescribed central quotient

This internal construction supports the square-integrable hypothesis of the actual-point Plancherel scalar target. It takes an irreducible smooth admissible complex representation of a locally compact Hausdorff second countable unimodular group in which every open subgroup contains a compact open subgroup, square-integrable modulo an arbitrary prescribed central subgroup `C`, and constructs an invariant positive definite Hermitian form on its original carrier. The subgroup `C` is not assumed closed.

The proof pushes the given quotient measure forward along the actual projection `G/C → G/Z(G)`. Surjectivity makes this measure left invariant and positive on nonempty open sets. The explicit compact-open cofinality assumption supplies a nonzero smooth functional by compact-open duality, and a nonzero value of one of its coefficients gives a nonempty open superlevel set of the squared coefficient norm. Square-integrability makes that set have finite measure. Translation then gives local finiteness, so the pushed-forward measure is Haar. Every coefficient remains square-integrable for this measure by the pushforward integration formula. The existing full-central-quotient construction `UnitaryCentralCharacter.unimodularCoefficientProductForm` supplies the positive definite invariant form.

The argument is a derived measure-transport construction, not a claim that an arbitrary pushforward of a Haar measure is Haar. The nonzero square-integrable coefficient is used precisely to establish local finiteness. The coefficient-integral form and compact-open duality are imported from `Unitary/SquareIntegrableProof`; full-central-quotient unimodularity is imported from `Unitary/CentralQuotientForm`. This module proves no regularity or positivity assertion for an intertwining scalar.
