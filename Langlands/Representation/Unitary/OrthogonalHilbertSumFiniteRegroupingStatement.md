# Regrouping a Hilbert sum along finite fibres

For an internal Hilbert sum of closed subspaces \(P_i\), let a labelling
\(c:I\to B\) have finite fibres. For each label \(b\), the proposition forms
the algebraic sum \(Q_b=\sum_{c(i)=b}P_i\) in the original Hilbert space. It
asserts that the \(Q_b\) are closed and form an internal Hilbert sum of that
space. A linear map preserving each \(P_i\) preserves each \(Q_b\). The
orthogonal projection onto \(Q_b\) is the finite sum of the projections onto
the \(P_i\) in its fibre. The empty fibre gives the zero subspace and the zero
projection.

Corollary 9.1.3 and (9.5), printed p. 241 (PDF p. 253), group irreducible
cuspidal Hilbert summands by their isomorphism classes. Each class has finitely
many summands. The proof of Theorem 9.7.3 at (9.40), printed p. 266
(PDF p. 278), uses the finite sum of copies within one such class. The
proposition gives the Hilbert-space regrouping for any finite-fibre labels. In
the cuspidal application the labels come from unitary equivalence of the given
irreducible summands.
