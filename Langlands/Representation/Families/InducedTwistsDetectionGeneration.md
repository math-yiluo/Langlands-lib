# Generation of an induced representation by fixed vectors from second adjointness

Author: Worker 70. Module
`Langlands.Representation.Families.InducedTwistsDetectionGeneration`, namespace
`Langlands.Renard.AlgebraicFamily.InducedTwistsDetection`. Internal helper for the
uniform induced-twist detector.

## Statement

`leviInduction_fixedVectors_generate`. The data are:
- a connected reductive `G` over a nonarchimedean local field `k`;
- an actual parabolic `P = MU` (`p`, `l`, `r`) and an actual parabolic `P̄ = M N̄`
  (`pbar`, `mbar`, `nbar`) with the same Levi `M`;
- an adjunction `adj : i_P ⊣ r_{P̄}` between the published functors
  `leviInductionFunctor.{u, max u v} p l r …` and
  `leviJacquetFunctor.{u, max u v} pbar mbar nbar …`;
- a compact open subgroup `K` of `G(k)` with
  `Jacquet.IsIwahoriContraction K (N̄(k)) (M(k)) X g`, where `N̄(k)` and `M(k)` are the
  point images of `nbar ≫ pbar` and `mbar ≫ pbar`;
- an irreducible smooth `σ` of `M(k)` with carrier in `Type (max u v)`, and a nonzero
  `v₀ ∈ σ` fixed by every `m ∈ M(k)` whose image lies in `K`.

Conclusion: `i_P σ` is generated by its `K`-fixed vectors. That is, every
subrepresentation `S` whose submodule contains `(i_P σ)^K` is `⊤`.

The adjunction is a hypothesis of this lemma. It does not depend on a
particular proof of second adjointness.

## Proof

Suppose `S ≠ ⊤` contains `(i_P σ)^K`, and let `Q = i_P σ / S` with quotient map `q ≠ 0`.
1. `Q^K = 0`, because `K`-fixed vectors lift through the surjection `q`
   (`Compact.fixedVectorSurjectivity`) and lie in `S`.
2. The right adjoint `r_{P̄}` is exact (`leviParabolicFunctorsExact`), so it preserves
   zero morphisms. Hence the adjoint transpose `φ : σ → r_{P̄} Q` of `q` is nonzero.
3. `σ` is irreducible, so `φ` is injective and `w = φ(v₀) ≠ 0`. By equivariance, `w` is
   fixed by `K ∩ M(k)` in the normalized Jacquet module of `Q`.
4. `Q^K = 0` is finite dimensional. The weak Jacquet lemma
   `exists_fixed_of_normalizedJacquet_fixed` (module `InducedTwistsDetectionJacquetLift`)
   then gives a nonzero vector of `Q^K`. This contradicts step 1.

This replaces the admissibility and finite-generation hypotheses of Renard's route. The
weak Jacquet lemma is applied to the quotient `Q`, whose fixed space is zero.
