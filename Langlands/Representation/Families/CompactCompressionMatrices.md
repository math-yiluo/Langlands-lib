# Matrices of compact-average compression

This internal construction supports the determinant argument in Renard,
*Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques* (2026), VI.8.4,
printed pp. 192–193 (PDF pp. 198–199). It gives coordinates for the actual
compact-average compression and compares matrix irreducibility with
representation irreducibility. It does not assert the existence of a compact
open subgroup detecting a prescribed family.

For a smooth complex representation `A`, compact open subgroup `K`, and
finite basis `e` of its fixed vectors, `fixedMatrix` is the matrix of the
operator which acts by a group element and then applies the normalized
finite-orbit average. `fixedMatrix_mulVec` identifies its action on
coordinates. These constructions use `Jacquet.compressedAction` and require
no Haar measure or unimodularity premise.

`fixedMatrix_isIrreducible` proves that an irreducible representation has an
irreducible matrix family. For a common invariant coordinate subspace `U`,
the proof takes the ambient subspace of vectors whose every translate has
average in `U`. It is group invariant. A nonzero vector of `U` makes it
nonzero, so irreducibility makes it the whole representation. Averaging the
identity translate then forces `U` to be the full fixed-vector space. The
zero fixed-vector space is allowed.

In the converse, nontriviality of the representation and detection of every
irreducible actual subquotient by `K` are explicit premises.
`averageMap_mem` first proves that averaging preserves invariant
subspaces by naturality for the subrepresentation inclusion.
`isIrreducible_of_fixedMatrix` uses the published detection consequences:
all nonzero subquotients have nonzero fixed vectors and all subquotients
are generated by their fixed vectors. The intersection of a
subrepresentation with the fixed-vector space is a common matrix
invariant subspace. A zero intersection forces the subrepresentation to
be zero by detection; a full intersection makes it the whole
representation by generation.
`isIrreducible_iff_fixedMatrix` packages the two implications.

The converse follows the algebraic route of the published
`Plancherel.CompactFixedFamilyOpenness` helper, replacing the Hecke
projection with the actual finite-orbit average. The forward proof uses
the same invariant-subspace argument as the published
`UnitarizabilityContinuation.fixedMatrix_isIrreducible`. These are
derived helpers, rather than a proof of the closed VI.8.4 proposition.
