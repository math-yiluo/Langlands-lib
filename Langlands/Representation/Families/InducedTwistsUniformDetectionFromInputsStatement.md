## The statement

`InducedTwistsUniformDetectionFromInputsStatement.{u, v}` is an iterated implication.

**Conclusion.** `InducedTwistsUniformDetectionStatement.{u, v}`, at the same universes.
For a nonarchimedean local field `k : Type u`, a connected reductive group scheme `G`
over `k`, an actual rational parabolic `P = MU` (rational Levi `M`, geometric unipotent
radical `U`, multiplication isomorphism) and an irreducible smooth complex
representation `ρ` of `M(k)` with carrier in `Type v`, there is one compact open
subgroup `K` of `G(k)` such that, for every unramified character `ψ` of `M(k)` (the
published `Plancherel.unramifiedCharacters`) and every irreducible smooth
representation `D` of `G(k)` with carrier in `Type (max u v)` that is a subquotient of
`i_P^G(ρ ⊗ ψ)` (`leviInductionFunctor` applied to `ρ.twist ψ`), `D` has a nonzero
`K`-fixed vector.

## Why these premises

They are the published results that a proof of the conclusion uses and that the Library
does not yet prove. The planned proof (shared draft of task
`eac5190f-3511-487f-9772-217270c7e0a3`, revision 1, entry theorem
`inducedTwistsUniformDetection_of_inputs`) runs as follows.

- An actual opposite parabolic `P̄ = M N̄` of `P`, with `P̄ = P(χ)` and `N̄ = U(χ)` for a
  cocharacter `χ`, comes from proved Library results on parabolic dynamics; premise 2
  then gives `i_P ⊣ r_{P̄}`.
- Fix a nonzero `v₀ ∈ ρ` and a compact open `Cρ ⊆ M(k)` fixing it. The proved
  `Jacquet.hasOppositeIwahoriContractionBasis_cocharPoint` gives a compact open
  `K' ⊆ G(k)`, with an Iwahori contraction relative to `P̄`, whose Levi points lie in
  `Cρ`. An unramified `ψ` is trivial on the compact `Cρ`. Second adjointness and the weak
  Jacquet lemma then show that `i_P(ρ ⊗ ψ)` is generated by its `K'`-fixed vectors, for
  every `ψ`.
- Premise 3 supplies `K₀`, its compactness, the minimal Iwasawa factorization and the
  basis `B`. Choose `K ∈ B` with `K ⊆ K'`. This `K` does not depend on `ψ`, and
  `i_P(ρ ⊗ ψ)` is generated by its `K`-fixed vectors.
- Premise 1, at carrier universe `max u v`, applies to `K`. Its standard-cocharacter
  hypothesis is the proved `standardCocharacter`. So every subquotient `D` is generated by
  `D^K`, and `D^K ≠ 0` when `D` is irreducible.

Renard's own argument uses the cuspidal support of the subquotients (via the proof of
VI.7.2), finitely many conjugate standard Levis, and the Jacquet lemma VI.6.1. The route
above avoids cuspidal support, admissibility and standardization of `P`. Its structural
inputs are exactly the three premises.

## Faithfulness notes

- **No change to the target.** The conclusion is the published target statement at the
  same universes `u, v`. Every premise is a published proposition used at stated
  universes. No premise mentions the specific `k`, `G`, `P`, `ρ` or `ψ` of the target, so
  this is a plain implication between closed statements.
- **Universes.** Premise 1 is used at field universe `u` and carrier universe `max u v`,
  the carrier universe of `i_P^G(ρ ⊗ ψ)` and of `D` in the target. Premise 2 at `u, v`
  concerns the functors `leviInductionFunctor.{u, max u v}` and
  `leviJacquetFunctor.{u, max u v}`, the induction used in the target. Premise 3 has
  only the field universe `u`.
- **Strength.** All premises are statements of results in the literature, published with
  their own provenance and owned by their proof tasks. None of them is the target or
  contains it. None adds an admissibility, finite-length, characteristic, splitness or
  supercuspidality hypothesis to the target.
