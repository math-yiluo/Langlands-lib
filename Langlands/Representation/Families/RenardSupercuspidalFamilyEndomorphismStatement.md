The Example reads: "Soit `P = MN` un sous-groupe parabolique de `G`, et soit
`(ρ, W)` une représentation supercuspidale irréductible de `M`. On reprend les
notations de la démonstration de la proposition VI.8.4. Soit `φ : V_B → V_B` un
morphisme de `(G, B)`-modules. Grâce au théorème d'irréductibilité générique, les
hypothèses de la proposition ci-dessus sont satisfaites, et l'on en conclut qu'il
existe un `b ∈ B` tel que `φ` est l'action de `b`." In the proof of VI.8.4,
`B = ℂ[Λ(M)]` is the algebra of functions on the variety `X(M)`,
`(ρ_B, W_B) = (ρ ⊗ χ_un, W ⊗_ℂ B)` is the `(M, B)`-module of the Example of
VI.8.1, and `(π_B, V_B) = i_P^G(ρ_B, W_B)`.

## Lean model

The binders are those of the published VI.8.5 statement
`GenericInducedIrreducibilityStatement` in
[RenardGenericIrreducibilityStatements](RenardGenericIrreducibilityStatements.md):
a nonarchimedean local field `k`, a connected reductive group scheme `G` over
`Spec k` with the canonical topology on its rational points, and an actual rational
parabolic `p : P ⟶ G` with rational Levi `l`, geometric unipotent radical `r` and
`IsIso (subgroupMultiplication l r)`. `M(F)` carries the published Levi point
topology. `P = G` is allowed.

- `(ρ, W)` is a `ℂ`-linear representation `σ : Representation ℂ (rationalPoints M) W`
  with `IsSmooth σ`. Irreducibility is `SmoothRep.IsIrreducible` and
  supercuspidality is the published `RepresentedParabolic.IsSupercuspidal`, both of
  `SmoothRep.of σ hσ`.
- `ν` is the family of rational norm characters of `M(F)`, indexed by
  `rationalCharacters M`, as in the VI.8.4 and VI.8.5 statements. `X(M)` is the
  published `Plancherel.unramifiedCharacters ν`.
- `B` is the published `Plancherel.unramifiedRegularFunctions ν`: the `ℂ`-algebra
  of functions on `X(M)` generated by the evaluations `ψ ↦ ψ(m)`, `m ∈ M(F)`.
- `(ρ_B, W_B)` is the published `Plancherel.universalUnramifiedRepresentation ν σ`
  on `B ⊗[ℂ] W`. It acts by `m · (b ⊗ w) = χ_un(m) b ⊗ σ(m) w`, where `χ_un(m)`
  is the evaluation at `m`, the source's `π_B(g)(v ⊗ b) = π(g) v ⊗ χ_un(g) b`
  with the factors swapped.
- `V_B` is `LeviInduced p l r hp hr hl hmul ρB`, with the action
  `leviInducedRepresentation`, from the published
  [RenardFamilyParabolicFunctors](RenardFamilyParabolicFunctors.md). This is
  Renard's normalized induction over `B`, which inflates the family twisted by the
  Levi half modulus (VI.1.2.3).
- A morphism of `(G, B)`-modules `V_B → V_B` is a `B`-linear map commuting with the
  action of every `g ∈ G(F)`.

Two elaboration devices do not change the meaning. The statement installs the
additive group and `B`-module structures of `B ⊗[ℂ] W` as Mathlib's
`TensorProduct.addCommGroup` and `TensorProduct.leftModule` with `letI`. These are
the structures that the published universal-twist module uses, and without them
Lean times out comparing instances. A `set_option synthInstance.maxHeartbeats` raises
the instance-search budget for the scalar action `b • f`.

## Declaration

`SupercuspidalFamilyEndomorphismStatement.{u, v}`: for all binders above and every
smooth `σ` on `W : Type v` such that `SmoothRep.of σ hσ` is irreducible and
supercuspidal, every `B`-linear map `φ` from `V_B` to itself that commutes with the
action of `G(F)` is of the form `f ↦ b • f` for some `b ∈ B`.

## Correspondence notes

1. The algebra `B`. The source's `B = ℂ[Λ(M)]` is the coordinate ring of `X(M)`.
   The statement uses the published algebra of evaluation functions on `X(M)`.
   The published `Langlands.Plancherel.rationalUnramifiedLaurentAlgebraEquiv`
   (`RationalUnramifiedCoordinateAlgebra`) identifies this algebra, for a connected
   reductive group and under the continuity of `rationalValuationImageProjection`,
   with the Laurent polynomial algebra in `rationalImageRank M` variables. The
   statement does not use that identification.
2. Tensor order. The source writes `W ⊗_ℂ B`; the published universal family is
   `B ⊗[ℂ] W`. The two are identified by the symmetry of the tensor product.
3. Only the first paragraph of the Example is stated. The second paragraph
   specializes to `M = P = G` and concludes that an element `z` of the centre of
   the category `M(G)[ρ]` acts on `W_B` through an element of `B`, recovering
   VI.4.5. It needs the block category `M(G)[ρ]` and its centre, and is not
   formalized here. For `P = G`, the statement covers the endomorphisms of
   `i_G^G(ρ_B)`, which is `ρ_B` up to the canonical identification of `i_G^G`.
   That identification is not proved here.
4. Hypotheses. The source's argument combines the VI.8.6 Proposition with the
   generic irreducibility Theorem VI.8.5 and the admissibility of `V_B`. The
   statement asserts only the conclusion, with the source's hypotheses
   (irreducible, supercuspidal). It assumes no admissibility. In the source,
   admissibility of `V_B` follows from that of `ρ` by the Example of VI.8.1 and
   the Lemma of VI.8.2.
