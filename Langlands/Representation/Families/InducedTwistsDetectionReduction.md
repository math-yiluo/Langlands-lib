# The uniform induced-twist detector from three structural inputs

Author: Worker 70. Module
`Langlands.Representation.Families.InducedTwistsDetectionReduction`.

## Statement

`Langlands.Renard.AlgebraicFamily.inducedTwistsUniformDetection_of_inputs` proves the
published `InducedTwistsUniformDetectionStatement.{u, v}` from three published
propositions that the Library does not yet prove:

1. `Jacquet.StandardFixedVectorSubquotientGenerationStatement.{u, max u v}` (Renard
   VI.9.4, Corollary; proof task `e8bd5ada-24df-4009-8256-ca4317b4190d`). Let `K` be a
   compact open subgroup with `K ≤ K₀`, normalized by `K₀`, and a standard-parabolic
   basis member for every standard `J`, where `K₀` is a compact subgroup with the
   minimal Iwasawa factorization. Then every subquotient of a smooth representation
   generated by its `K`-fixed vectors is generated by its own `K`-fixed vectors.
2. `RepresentedParabolic.LeviSecondAdjointnessStatement.{u, v}` (Renard VI.9.6.1; proof
   task `c0fa1878-6cee-4435-8484-2fc15ea04a17`): `i_P ⊣ r_{P̄}` for actual opposite
   represented parabolics, with carriers in `Type (max u v)`.
3. `BruhatTits.SpecialCompact.JointSpecialCompactBasisExistenceStatement.{u}` (Renard
   V.5.1–V.5.2; proof task `8c39faa0-2a70-4fc4-a027-914987aba924`). It supplies a
   special compact `K₀` with Iwasawa products, and a compact open normal neighbourhood
   basis `B` of `K₀`, each member of which is a standard-parabolic basis member for every
   standard `J`.

The module also contains three elementary lemmas: `generate_of_le` (generation by
`V^{K'}` implies generation by `V^K` when `K ≤ K'`), `exists_fixed_ne_zero_of_generate`
(an irreducible representation generated by `V^K` has `V^K ≠ 0`) and
`exists_ne_zero_of_isIrreducible`.

## Proof

Fix `k`, `G`, the parabolic `P = MU` and an irreducible `ρ` of `M(k)`.

1. *Opposite parabolic.* `exists_opposite_parabolic` (module
   `InducedTwistsDetectionOpposite`) gives `P̄ = M N̄` with `P̄ = P(χ)` and `N̄ = U(χ)`
   for a cocharacter `χ`, and `P(k) ∩ P̄(k) = M(k)`. Input 2 gives an adjunction
   `i_P ⊣ r_{P̄}`.
2. *A vector of `ρ`.* Choose `v₀ ≠ 0` in `ρ`, an open subgroup fixing it, and inside it
   a compact open subgroup `Cρ` of `M(k)` (`Jacquet.exists_isCompact_openSubgroup_subset`).
   `leviPointsEquiv` is a homeomorphism of `M(k)` onto its image with the subspace
   topology. Hence there is an open `O ∋ 1` in `G(k)` whose Levi points lie in `Cρ`.
3. *Contraction.* Take a unit `t` whose powers tend to `0`
   (`exists_unit_tendsto_pow_zero`). The proved
   `Jacquet.hasOppositeIwahoriContractionBasis_cocharPoint χ t` gives a compact open
   `K' ⊆ O` with `Jacquet.IsIwahoriContraction K' (U(χ)(k)) (Z(χ)(k)) (U(χ⁻¹)(k)) χ(t)`.
   By step 1, `U(χ)(k) = N̄(k)` and `Z(χ)(k) = M(k)`.
4. *The detector `K`.* Choose a maximal split torus and a generic cocharacter
   (`exists_maximalSplitTorus_genericCocharacter`), and apply input 3. Since `B` is a
   neighbourhood basis of `1`, some `K ∈ B` satisfies `K ⊆ K'`. This `K` is the output.
   It does not depend on `ψ`.
5. *Generation.* Fix an unramified `ψ`, put `τ = ρ ⊗ ψ`, and let `σ` be its universe
   lift to `Type (max u v)`. `σ` is irreducible. The vector `v₀` is fixed by every
   `m ∈ M(k)` with image in `K'`. Such an `m` lies in `Cρ`, which fixes `v₀`. As `Cρ` is
   compact, it lies in the common norm kernel
   (`Plancherel.compactSubgroup_le_rationalCommonNormKernel`), so `ψ(m) = 1`.
   `leviInduction_fixedVectors_generate` (module `InducedTwistsDetectionGeneration`)
   shows that `i_P σ` is generated by its `K'`-fixed vectors. Then `generate_of_le`
   gives generation by its `K`-fixed vectors, and
   `leviInduction_generate_of_universeLift` transfers this to `i_P(ρ ⊗ ψ)` with carrier
   in `Type (max u v)`.
6. *Subquotients.* Input 1 applies with the following data:
   - `K₀`, which is compact by `SpecialCompactProperties`;
   - the minimal Iwasawa factorization from `exists_standard_minimal_factorization`;
   - the standard cocharacters from the proved `standardCocharacter`;
   - `K ≤ K₀`, and `K₀ ≤ normalizer K` by `le_normalizer_of_subgroupOf_normal`;
   - the basis-member clauses of `B` at `K`.

   So every subquotient `D` of `i_P(ρ ⊗ ψ)` is generated by `D^K`. If `D` is
   irreducible, `D^K ≠ 0` by `exists_fixed_ne_zero_of_generate`.

The proof adds no admissibility, finite-length, cuspidal-support or standardness
hypothesis. Renard's cuspidal-support argument (printed p.193) is replaced by second
adjointness and the VI.9.4 Corollary. `lean_check` reports the axioms `propext`,
`Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` only.
