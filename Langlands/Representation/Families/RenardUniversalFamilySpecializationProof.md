# Renard VI.8.1 Example: proof that the fibers of the universal unramified family are twists

Worker 73.

This module contains one public **proof**, the theorem
`Langlands.Renard.AlgebraicFamily.universalSpecialization`, which proves exactly the
published statement
`Langlands.Renard.AlgebraicFamily.UniversalSpecializationStatement.{uG, uI, uV}` from
`Langlands.Representation.Families.RenardUniversalFamilySpecializationStatement`. The
universes `uG`, `uI` and `uV` of the group, the index type of the norm characters and the
representation space are independent. The theorem adds no hypothesis: it holds for every
group `G` with a topology, every family `ν : I → (G →* ℝ≥0ˣ)`, every complex
representation `π : Representation ℂ G V` and every `χ : Plancherel.unramifiedCharacters ν`.
No smoothness, admissibility, continuity, finiteness or reductivity premise is used. The
statement pair and everything it imports are unchanged, and no axiom is introduced: the
proof depends only on `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

## Notation

`B` is `Plancherel.unramifiedRegularFunctions ν`, the subalgebra of functions on the
unramified characters generated by the evaluations. As in the statement, `B ⊗[ℂ] V` gets
`TensorProduct.addCommGroup` and the `B`-module structure `TensorProduct.leftModule`, and
`ℂ` is a `B`-algebra through `(Plancherel.UnramifiedNormalized.evaluation ν χ).toAlgebra`.
So `b • z = b.val χ * z` for `b : B` and `z : ℂ`.

## Private helpers

The helpers are proof infrastructure for this module only. They are stated for an
arbitrary commutative ring `A` with `[Algebra ℂ A]`, `[Algebra A ℂ]` and
`[IsScalarTower ℂ A ℂ]`, and an arbitrary complex vector space `V`.

- `fiberCancel : ℂ ⊗[A] (A ⊗[ℂ] V) ≃ₗ[ℂ] V` is Mathlib's
  `TensorProduct.AlgebraTensorModule.cancelBaseChange ℂ A ℂ ℂ V :
  ℂ ⊗[A] (A ⊗[ℂ] V) ≃ₗ[ℂ] ℂ ⊗[ℂ] V`, followed by `TensorProduct.lid ℂ V`.
- `fiberCancel_tmul`: `fiberCancel (c ⊗ (b ⊗ v)) = (algebraMap A ℂ b * c) • v`.
  By `cancelBaseChange_tmul` and `lid_tmul`, the left side is definitionally `(b • c) • v`.
  `Algebra.smul_def` rewrites `b • c` as `algebraMap A ℂ b * c`.
- `fiberCancel_baseChange`: let `ρ` be an `A`-linear representation of a group `H` on
  `A ⊗[ℂ] V`, let `σ` be a complex representation of `H` on `V`, and fix `g : H`. Suppose
  `fiberCancel (c ⊗ ρ g (b ⊗ v)) = σ g (fiberCancel (c ⊗ (b ⊗ v)))` for all `c`, `b` and
  `v`. Then `fiberCancel (baseChange ℂ ρ g x) = σ g (fiberCancel x)` for every `x`. The
  proof inducts on `x` with `TensorProduct.inductionOn`, and on the second factor of a pure
  tensor `c ⊗ y` likewise. On `c ⊗ (b ⊗ v)`, `baseChange_tmul` turns the left side into
  `fiberCancel (c ⊗ ρ g (b ⊗ v))`, and the hypothesis applies. The two addition cases follow
  from `TensorProduct.tmul_add`, additivity of the linear maps and the induction hypotheses.

These helpers are generic so that the addition cases are elaborated with the generic
instances. The same steps written directly against the statement's instances on
`B ⊗[ℂ] V` exceeded the default heartbeat limits during elaboration.

## Proof of `universalSpecialization`

After introducing the data, the proof installs the statement's three instances: the
additive group and `B`-module structures on `B ⊗[ℂ] V`, and the algebra structure
`(evaluation ν χ).toAlgebra`. It then proves `IsScalarTower ℂ B ℂ` directly. The identity
`(c • b) • z = c • (b • z)` unfolds to `(c • b).val χ * z = c * (b.val χ * z)`. This
follows from `Subalgebra.coe_smul`, `Pi.smul_apply`, `smul_eq_mul` and `mul_assoc`.

The equivalence is `e := fiberCancel` with `A = B`.

**Clause 2.** By `fiberCancel_tmul` and `mul_comm`, `e (c ⊗ (b ⊗ v))` equals
`(c * algebraMap B ℂ b) • v`. Here `algebraMap B ℂ b` is definitionally `evaluation ν χ b`,
which is `b.val χ`, so the clause holds by `rfl`.

**Clause 1.** By `fiberCancel_baseChange` with `ρ = universalUnramifiedRepresentation ν π`
and `σ = Langlands.twist χ.val.toMonoidHom π`, it is enough to check pure tensors
`c ⊗ (b ⊗ v)`. The published `universalUnramifiedRepresentation_tmul` gives
`π_B(g)(b ⊗ v) = (unramifiedEvaluationRegular ν g * b) ⊗ π(g) v`. Clause 2, applied on
both sides, reduces the goal to

`(c * (unramifiedEvaluationRegular ν g * b).val χ) • π g v = twist χ π g ((c * b.val χ) • v)`.

By definition `twist χ π g w = (χ g : ℂ) • π g w`, and the proof states this form with
`change`. Linearity of `π g` (`map_smul`) and `smul_smul` reduce the goal to an equality of
scalars. The value of a product in the subalgebra at `χ` is the product of the values, and
`(unramifiedEvaluationRegular ν g).val χ = χ g` holds by definition. This is the identity
(V.2.4.1), published as `unramifiedEvaluationRegular_apply`. Together these turn the
scalars into `c * (χ g * b.val χ) = χ g * (c * b.val χ)`, and `ring` proves it.
