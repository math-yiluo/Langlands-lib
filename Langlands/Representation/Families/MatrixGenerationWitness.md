# Determinant witnesses for generation of a specialized matrix algebra

Worker 1444. This is an internal construction for the determinant step in
Renard VI.8.4. It works over any commutative complex coefficient algebra `B`,
with an arbitrary type of matrix generators and a finite matrix index type.

`specialize A χ` applies a complex algebra homomorphism `χ : B →ₐ[ℂ] ℂ`
to every matrix entry. Given a family of noncommutative polynomials `w`
indexed by pairs of matrix indices, `wordMatrix A w` has as columns the
flattened matrices obtained by evaluating those polynomials at `A`.
`wordDeterminant A w` is its determinant in `B`.

Evaluation of the words commutes with specialization by the universal
property of `FreeAlgebra`; determinants commute with the resulting ring
homomorphism. If the specialized determinant is nonzero, the specialized
column matrix is invertible. Consequently every matrix is a complex linear
combination of the specialized words and lies in the algebra generated by
the specialized generators.

At an irreducible specialization, the published finite matrix Burnside
equivalence gives surjectivity from the free algebra onto the full matrix
algebra. Choose preimages of the standard matrix basis. The specialized
word matrix is the identity, so its determinant equals one.
`exists_principal_neighborhood` packages these two intrinsic properties:
one element of `B`, equal to one at the original character, certifies
irreducibility at every character where it does not vanish. The argument
includes the empty matrix index type, under the published invariant
subspace predicate's convention.

The construction is motivated by David Renard, *Représentations des groupes
réductifs p-adiques*, 9 July 2026 version, VI.8.4 Proposition proof, printed
pp.192–193 / PDF pp.198–199, registered source
`renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, SHA256
`7b539996793b7c3620e72e1e389588eb42d1a8e91d6f68b2eead5c2e2ffc182c`.
Here the words replace the source's chosen Hecke operators; this is a
derived algebraic construction, not a representation of a Hecke algebra.
It imports Burnside through
`Langlands.Plancherel.FiniteMatrixOpenness.matrix_irreducible_iff_adjoin_eq_top`.

This unit supplies neither a detecting compact open subgroup for induced
unramified twists, a coefficient matrix model for their fixed vectors, nor
the conversion from a regular function to a polynomial in the canonical
torus coordinates. It does not prove
`InducedIrreducibilityOpenStatement`.
