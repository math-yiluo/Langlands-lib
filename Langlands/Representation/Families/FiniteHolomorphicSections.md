# Finite holomorphic families of sections

`finiteHolomorphicSections` is the span of entire scalar multiples of
constant sections. Every element has a finite expansion of that form.
Consequently its values lie in one finite-dimensional subspace and every
linear scalar test is entire. A linear map carries such expansions to
the target space. For an injective linear map, a linear left inverse
also recovers the expansion in the source.

A varying linear operator preserves these families if it sends each
constant section to such a family. Apply it to the finite expansion
and multiply the scalar coefficients. The corresponding property for
the generators of a free algebra extends through sums and products to
every operator, and finite sums give the same property for evaluating
vectors of the free module on the original constant sections.

This is an internal finite-family construction for the parameter-dependent
operator actions in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic
representation* (1979), proof of Proposition 2, printed pp. 205–206
(Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 200–201).
