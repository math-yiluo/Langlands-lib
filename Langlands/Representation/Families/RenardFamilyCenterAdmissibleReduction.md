# Reducing `B ⊗_ℂ ℨ(G)`-admissibility of `(G, B)`-modules to complex subrepresentations

Lean module: `Langlands.Representation.Families.RenardFamilyCenterAdmissibleReduction`
(namespace `Langlands.Renard.AlgebraicFamily`).

## Purpose and provenance

Renard 2026, VI.10.7, Variant (printed p.217 / PDF p.223), says only that the Theorem and the
Proposition of VI.10.7 "can be reformulated and proved" for `(G, B)`-modules (VI.8.1,
Definition, printed p.187 / PDF p.193). It gives no proof. This module is a **derived**
supporting unit. It supplies the reduction from the Variant of the Theorem to the complex
Theorem (published statement
`Langlands.Aubert.General.RepresentedParabolic.FiniteTypeCenterAdmissibleStatement`).
It contains no new mathematical object beyond two auxiliary linear maps, and it does not
assume the complex Theorem anywhere except as an explicit hypothesis `hC` of the last
theorem.

The route is the standard one. The complex subrepresentation `W` generated by finitely many
`B[G]`-generators of `V` is of finite type over `ℂ[G]`, and its carrier spans `V` over `B`.
The complex Theorem applied to `W` gives finitely many `ℨ(G)`-generators of `W^K`. Averaging
over `K` and naturality of the elements of the centre then show that the `B`-span of the
vectors `z · sⱼ` contains `V^K`.

## Setting

Throughout, `G` is any group. From `hom_asAlgebraHom` on it carries `[TopologicalSpace G]`,
and from `mem_fixedVectors_of_subrepresentation` on also `[IsTopologicalGroup G]`, which the
compact-average lemmas need (`mem_fixedVectors_of_subrepresentation` itself omits it). `B` is
a commutative ring with `[Algebra ℂ B]`. `V` is an additive group with `[Module B V]`,
`[Module ℂ V]` and `[IsScalarTower ℂ B V]`, and `ρ : Representation B G V`. The underlying
complex representation is the published `complexRepresentation ρ`, which has the same carrier
and operators. No reductive structure on `G` is used, and no flatness, Noetherian, finite-type
or reducedness hypothesis on `B` or `V`. Smooth representations are
`SmoothRep.{0, uG, uV} ℂ G`, and `ℨ(G)` is Mathlib's `CatCenter` of that category.

## Declarations

* `coefficientSMulIntertwiningMap ρ b`: multiplication by `b : B`, as an intertwining map of
  `complexRepresentation ρ` with itself. It is complex-linear because `ℂ` acts through `B`
  (`smul_comm`), and it commutes with every `ρ g` because `ρ g` is `B`-linear. The `@[simp]`
  lemma `coefficientSMulIntertwiningMap_apply` evaluates it.
* `span_mem_of_stable`: the `B`-span of a `ρ`-stable set of vectors is `ρ`-stable. The proof
  is `Submodule.map_span` followed by `Submodule.span_mono`.
* `hom_asAlgebraHom`: a morphism `f : A ⟶ A'` of smooth complex representations commutes with
  the action of the group algebra, `f (r • x) = r • f x` for `r : ℂ[G]`. The proof is by
  `MonoidAlgebra.induction_linear` and the intertwining property on the basis elements
  `single g a`.
* `subrepresentationAsModuleSubtype A S` and `subrepresentationAsModuleSubtype_injective`:
  the inclusion of the smooth subrepresentation `SmoothRep.subrepresentation A S` into `A`,
  as a `ℂ[G]`-linear map of the group-algebra modules `asModule`, and its injectivity. Its
  linearity is `hom_asAlgebraHom`, transported through `Representation.asModuleEquiv`.
* `finite_subrepresentation_ofSubmodule A N hN`: if `N` is a finitely generated
  `ℂ[G]`-submodule of `A.representation.asModule`, the smooth subrepresentation
  `Subrepresentation.ofSubmodule' N` is of finite type over `ℂ[G]`. The inclusion above has
  range exactly `N`, so the subrepresentation is linearly equivalent to `N`
  (`LinearEquiv.ofInjective`).
* `exists_finite_complexSubrepresentation ρ hτ hfin` (step 1). If `complexRepresentation ρ`
  is smooth (`hτ`) and `ρ.asModule` is finitely generated over `MonoidAlgebra B G` (`hfin`),
  there is a subrepresentation `S` of `complexRepresentation ρ` whose carrier spans `V` over
  `B` (`Submodule.span B (S : Set V) = ⊤`), and the smooth subrepresentation
  `SmoothRep.subrepresentation (SmoothRep.of (complexRepresentation ρ) hτ) S` is finitely
  generated over `ℂ[G]`. `S` is the complex subrepresentation `ofSubmodule'` of the
  `ℂ[G]`-span `N` of a finite `B[G]`-generating set `T`. The `B`-span of `S` is `ρ`-stable by
  `span_mem_of_stable`. It is therefore a `B[G]`-submodule of `ρ.asModule` that contains `T`,
  and so it is everything.
* `mem_fixedVectors_of_subrepresentation`: the image in `V` of a `K`-fixed vector of the
  subrepresentation lies in the `B`-submodule `fixedVectors ρ K`.
* `fixedVectors_le_span_center_of_subrepresentation ρ hτ S hspan K hK s hgen` (steps 3 and 4).
  Let the carrier of `S` span `V` over `B`, let `K` be a compact open subgroup, and let
  `s : Fin n → W`, with `W` the smooth subrepresentation, satisfy the following: every
  `K`-fixed `x` of `W` is `∑ i, (z i).app W (s i)` for some `z : Fin n → CatCenter`. Then
  `fixedVectors ρ K` is contained in the `B`-span of the vectors
  `(z.app τ) (ι (s j))`, where `τ = SmoothRep.of (complexRepresentation ρ) hτ`, `ι` is the
  inclusion `W ⟶ τ`, `j : Fin n` and `z : CatCenter`. Proof: let `e` and `e_W` be the
  published compact averages `Langlands.Compact.CharZero.averageMap` on `τ` and on `W`. The
  average `e` fixes every `K`-fixed vector (`averageMap_id`). It commutes with `ι` and with
  every `coefficientSMulIntertwiningMap ρ b` (`averageMap_commute`). The image `e_W w` is
  `K`-fixed in `W` (`averageMap_mem_fixedVectors`). By `Submodule.span_induction` over
  `x ∈ Submodule.span B S = ⊤`, `e x` lies in the target span. For a generator `w`,
  `e (ι w) = ι (e_W w) = ∑ i, ι ((z i).app W (s i))`, and naturality of each `z i` along `ι`
  rewrites the summands as `(z i).app τ (ι (s i))`. Sums are handled directly, and
  `B`-multiples by the commutation of `e` with `b •`. Finally `x = e x`.
* `centerAdmissible_of_complexCenterAdmissible hC ρ hτ hfin K hK` (the assembled reduction).
  The hypothesis `hC` is the conclusion of the complex Theorem for the group `G`: every
  `τ : SmoothRep.{0, uG, uV} ℂ G` finitely generated over `MonoidAlgebra ℂ G`, and every
  compact open `K`, have finitely many `K`-fixed `s i` with every `K`-fixed vector equal to
  `∑ i, (z i).app τ (s i)` for some `z i ∈ CatCenter`. Under `hC`, with `hτ` and `hfin` as
  above, there are `n` and `s : Fin n → V` with every `s j ∈ fixedVectors ρ K` and
  `fixedVectors ρ K ≤ Submodule.span B {y | ∃ j z, y = (z.app τ) (s j)}`, where
  `τ = SmoothRep.of (complexRepresentation ρ) hτ`. This is exactly the conclusion of the
  published Variant statement `FamilyFiniteTypeCenterAdmissibleStatement`. The proof applies
  `exists_finite_complexSubrepresentation`, then `hC` to the subrepresentation, and then the
  two lemmas above.

## Scope

This module proves neither the complex Theorem nor the Variant
`FamilyFiniteTypeCenterAdmissibleStatement`. The last theorem takes the complex conclusion
`hC` as an explicit hypothesis. The other declarations hold unconditionally.
