## Proof

The theorem applies `inducedTwistsUniformDetection_of_inputs` (module
`InducedTwistsDetectionReduction`) to the three premises. That theorem has exactly these
three hypotheses and the target as its conclusion. Its proof, described in
`InducedTwistsDetectionReduction.md`, uses four helper modules:

- `InducedTwistsDetectionOpposite`: `exists_opposite_parabolic` builds an actual opposite
  parabolic `P̄ = M N̄` of `P = MU` with `P̄ = P(χ)`, `N̄ = U(χ)` and
  `P(k) ∩ P̄(k) = M(k)`, from the proved parabolic dynamics and geometric unipotent
  radical results.
- `InducedTwistsDetectionJacquetLift`: `exists_fixed_of_normalizedJacquet_fixed`, the weak
  Jacquet lemma. Let `K` be a compact open subgroup with an Iwahori contraction and `D` a
  smooth representation with `D^K` finite dimensional. Then a nonzero `(K ∩ M)`-fixed
  vector in the normalized Jacquet module of `D` gives a nonzero `K`-fixed vector of `D`.
- `InducedTwistsDetectionGeneration`: `leviInduction_fixedVectors_generate`. Given an
  adjunction `i_P ⊣ r_{P̄}`, the induction `i_P σ` of an irreducible `σ` with a nonzero
  vector fixed by the Levi points of `K` is generated by its `K`-fixed vectors. Suppose a
  proper subrepresentation `S` contains `(i_P σ)^K`. The quotient `Q = i_P σ / S` has
  `Q^K = 0`, which is finite dimensional. The adjunction maps `v₀` to a nonzero
  `(K ∩ M)`-fixed vector of the normalized Jacquet module of `Q`, and the weak Jacquet
  lemma then gives a nonzero vector of `Q^K`, a contradiction.
- `InducedTwistsDetectionHelpers`: normality inside `K₀` as a normalizer inclusion, the
  minimal Iwasawa factorization from the joint basis, and transport of generation along
  the universe lift of the Levi representation.

The outline of `inducedTwistsUniformDetection_of_inputs`:

1. Premise 2 gives `i_P ⊣ r_{P̄}` for the opposite parabolic.
2. A nonzero `v₀ ∈ ρ` is fixed by a compact open `Cρ ⊆ M(k)`. The proved
   `Jacquet.hasOppositeIwahoriContractionBasis_cocharPoint` gives a compact open `K'` with
   an Iwahori contraction relative to `P̄`, whose Levi points lie in `Cρ`.
3. Premise 3 gives the special compact `K₀` and its basis `B`. Choose `K ∈ B` with
   `K ⊆ K'`; this is the detector, chosen before `ψ`.
4. For each unramified `ψ`, `ψ` is trivial on the compact `Cρ`
   (`Plancherel.compactSubgroup_le_rationalCommonNormKernel`), so `v₀` stays fixed in
   `ρ ⊗ ψ`. Hence `i_P(ρ ⊗ ψ)` is generated by its `K'`-fixed vectors, and so by its
   `K`-fixed vectors.
5. Premise 1, at carrier universe `max u v` and with the proved `standardCocharacter`,
   makes every subquotient `D` generated by `D^K`. For irreducible `D` this gives a nonzero
   `K`-fixed vector.

No admissibility, finite-length, cuspidal-support or standardness hypothesis is used.
`lean_check` reports only the axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.
