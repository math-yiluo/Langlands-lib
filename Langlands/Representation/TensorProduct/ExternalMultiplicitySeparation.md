# Separation by block multiplicity projections

Given a finite family of equivariant equivalences from block representations
to local block representations tensored with trivial multiplicity spaces,
use basis coefficient maps in the trivial spaces. Their external tensor
products are intertwiners to the external tensor product of the local
representations. A basis coordinate of the entire split tensor is a local
basis coordinate of the projection associated with the selected tuple of
multiplicity coordinates. Vanishing of all projected tensors therefore
implies that every coordinate of the original vector vanishes.

This is a derived algebraic step for the constituent extraction in Langlands,
*On the notion of an automorphic representation*, Corvallis Part I (1979),
printed p. 203 (PDF p. 198), source
`langlands-1979-notion-automorphic-representation`. It supplies the separating
coefficient maps used to compare the adelic and local inducements.
