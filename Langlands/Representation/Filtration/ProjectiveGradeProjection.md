# A split projection onto one grade of a projectively filtered object

A finite filtration with projective successive quotients admits sections of its graded projections. These sections, followed by inclusion into the full object, detect maps out of that object. Indeed, a map that vanishes on the sections has zero successive descents, and induction shows that it vanishes on every filtration step.

`exists_splitProjection_of_projectiveGrades` extends the graded projection of one step to the full object and uses the chosen section to split it. For a target with zero Hom from the other grades, precomposition with the projection is bijective. The section gives the inverse on Hom, since the resulting factorization agrees with the given map on every grade. This is the finite-filtration argument used with the mixed Jacquet modules in Renard (2026), VI.10.1 Lemma, printed p. 207 and PDF p. 213.
