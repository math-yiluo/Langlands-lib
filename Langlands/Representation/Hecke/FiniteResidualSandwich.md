# Finite sandwich data with arbitrary Cartan residuals

This derived supporting construction implements the finite residual Hecke
generation mechanism of Renard V.5.3, printed pages 124–125 / PDF pages
130–131, for the later fixed-level dimension argument in VI.2.3–VI.2.4.
Its hypotheses retain the actual group decomposition and level geometry.

The positive mass-one indicators form a monoid homomorphism, by the
Iwahori double-coset multiplication lemma. Induction on sums of coordinate
functions puts every orthant indicator in the algebra generated by the
coordinate indicators, which commute.

For each element of a supplied finite residual set, the finite residual
coset frontier gives finitely many translated indicators. The union of
these finite sets generates all residual translates as a left module over
the positive coordinate algebra. All indicators are elements of the actual
compact-open Hecke corner. Residual group elements need not normalize the
level, commute with each other, or commute with positive generators.

Finally suppose a supplied compact subgroup normalizes the level and
every group element is a double translate, by that subgroup, of a positive
orthant element times a member of the finite residual set. Compactness
gives finitely many subgroup representatives modulo the open level.
The common edge list consists of the representatives' double-coset
elements and the products of a residual Hecke generator with a right
representative. Double-coset spanning and the exact translation identities
then prove the span field of `FixedLevelSandwich`. The coordinate algebra
supplies its commuting middle list; the scalar converting a mass-one
indicator into the usual double-coset element is absorbed into the middle.

The result is representation independent and permits an empty coordinate
type. It proves no existence of the assumed reductive Cartan data,
simultaneous normal small levels, or common coefficient support on the
actual rational norm kernel. Those remain obligations of the host task.
