# Hecke sandwich data from an Iwahori factorization

This derived supporting construction supplies the group-theoretic
multiplication step between an ordered Iwahori factorization and the existing
Hecke sandwich theorem. It applies to an arbitrary group, and then to its
actual compact-open Hecke corner. It does not construct a reductive-group
Cartan decomposition or an Iwahori basis.

`iwahoriPositiveMonoid K N M Nbar` consists of the elements `a` for which
conjugation by `a` preserves `K ∩ N`, and conjugation by `a⁻¹` preserves
`K ∩ Nbar` and `K ∩ M`. These are inclusions, not equalities. The proof
that these elements form a submonoid composes the corresponding
conjugations in the appropriate order. Every element of the full abstract
center belongs to this monoid, since each conjugation is the identity.
No root system, nonempty index set, or rank assumption occurs.

`TestFunction.mul_mem_doubleCosetSet_of_iwahori` assumes that each element
of the subgroup `K` factors in the order
`(K ∩ Nbar) (K ∩ M) (K ∩ N)`. To control `a k b`, the proof factors
`k⁻¹ = l m u`, so that `k = u⁻¹ m⁻¹ l⁻¹`. The upper conjugation bound
places `a u⁻¹ a⁻¹` in `K`. The other two bounds place
`(b⁻¹ m⁻¹ b) (b⁻¹ l⁻¹ b)` in `K`. These are explicit left and right
factors exhibiting `a k b ∈ K (a b) K`. In this lemma the three
conjugates need only belong to `K`; the stronger intersection conditions
in the positive monoid ensure closure under multiplication.

`nonempty_fixedLevelSandwich_of_iwahoriCartan` assumes a compact subgroup
`Γ` normalizing the compact open level `K`, finitely many commuting
elements `D j` of the positive monoid, and a literal decomposition
`g = γ m γ'`, where `m` belongs to the monoid generated by the `D j`.
The preceding double-coset containment applies to any two positive
elements. The published
`HeckeAlgebra.Corner.exists_sandwich_of_decomposition` then supplies
finitely many edge elements and commuting middle elements spanning the
actual Hecke corner by their sandwiches. The result packages those data
as `FixedLevelSandwich μ K hK`, whose numerical dimension bound is
already proved in `UniformFixedLevelBounds`. The measure is the given
left Haar measure, and no representation is chosen during this
construction.

The source motivation is Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs
p-adiques*, edition dated 9 July 2026, VI.2.3–VI.2.4, printed
pp.140–142 / PDF pp.146–148: fixed-level uniform admissibility is the
input to a common positive-Hecke nilpotence exponent. The present
conjugation calculation is an internal derivation rather than a new
source theorem. It reuses the Library's general double-coset sandwich
construction and uniform dimension bound. All factorization,
normalization, finite generation and Cartan-existence hypotheses remain
explicit. In particular, this construction does not prove the actual
rational-norm-kernel uniform-support target or add premises to it.
