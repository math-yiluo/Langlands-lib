# Bi-invariant Schwartz–Bruhat functions on products

The finite tensor expansion of a Schwartz–Bruhat function on a product
can preserve invariance under the two product subgroups. The proof takes
the finitely many nonzero functions of the second variable that occur by
fixing the first variable. The first tensor factor is the indicator of
the corresponding fiber. Invariance in the first variable makes that
indicator bi-invariant, and invariance in the second variable makes
the sampled second factor bi-invariant.

The argument refines the Library's compactly supported sampled-value
expansion and uses `Hecke.TestFunction.biInvariant` on the actual
subgroups. It supplies the finite spherical sums for a two-block
constant term.
