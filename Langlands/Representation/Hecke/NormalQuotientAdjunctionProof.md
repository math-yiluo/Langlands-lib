# Normal-quotient and induction adjunctions

Worker 150. Source: Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*,
9 July 2026, III.2.10 Proposition and Corollary, printed pp.81–82 / PDF pp.87–88.

The proof restricts the published algebraic coinvariants/inflation adjunction
to the full categories of smooth representations. The normal coinvariant
functor already proves smoothness through the quotient topology. Its unit,
counit and triangle identities are the original algebraic maps and identities.

For the corollary, restriction to a subgroup is left adjoint to the existing
smooth function induction: the algebraic coinduction unit factors through the
smooth part and the counit evaluates at the identity. Composition with the
quotient adjunction gives the exact published corollary. Carrier and group
universes remain independent, with the universe bound prescribed by induction.
