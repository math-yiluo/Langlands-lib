# Intrinsic compact projectors and the block predicates

Worker 141 supplies internal proof infrastructure for Renard IV.1.6–IV.1.7,
printed pages 94–95 / PDF pages 100–101 of the registered French 2026 edition.
The mathematical definitions are the unchanged published block predicates.

The module specializes the published intrinsic isotypic projectors using the
published existence of their complementary subrepresentations. It records their
range, kernel, idempotence, naturality and orthogonality, and identity action on
an irreducible compact representative. These are wrappers of published results.

The avoiding predicate is equivalent to zero action of every projector in the
specified set. In the forward direction, a nonzero map from a representative
would embed that irreducible as a forbidden subquotient; all such maps therefore
vanish, making the projector range zero. In the reverse direction, naturality
transports zero action through a subquotient presentation, contradicting identity
action on the forbidden irreducible.

The isotypic predicate is equivalent to identity action of its projector. An
internal direct sum of copies of a class exhausts its intrinsic isotypic part.
Conversely, the published compact-isotypic complement theorem selects an
independent spanning family of injective maps from a representative. Their ranges
are irreducible and compact by equivariant isomorphisms with that representative.
Both carrier types lie in `Type v`, so the set of such intertwiners is also in
`Type v`, as the published predicate requires. Idempotence then identifies
exhaustion of the range with identity action.

These are derived internal comparisons. They add no hypotheses to any published
statement and make no category-decomposition assertion on their own.
