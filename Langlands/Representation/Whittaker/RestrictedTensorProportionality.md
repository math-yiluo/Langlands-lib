# Proportionality of functionals on an algebraic restricted tensor product

A nonzero linear functional is nonzero on some pure tensor because
pure tensors generate the restricted tensor product. For any test pure
tensor, enlarge the two stages to their union and pad both tensors by
the prescribed reference vectors. Each slot of the original functional
through the base tensor is nonzero, and division by the base value
normalizes it to have value one on the corresponding base vector.

If the slot restrictions of both functionals lie in the same local
spaces of dimension at most one, the finite multilinear product formula
gives both values in terms of the same product and their respective base
values. Their ratio on pure tensors is therefore the ratio of the base
values. Linearity extends the comparison to the entire carrier.
This is the algebraic tensor comparison for the use of local Whittaker
uniqueness in the proof of Shalika (1974), Theorem 5.5.
