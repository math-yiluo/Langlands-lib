# Entire coefficient ranges under surjective functional pullback

These internal algebraic lemmas extend the published `functionalsComap`
construction. A specified character-equivariant functional is pulled back
by precomposition with an actual intertwiner. If that intertwiner is
surjective and the specified functional is nonzero, its pullback is nonzero.

Intertwining gives
`(functionalsComap f ell) (rho g v) = ell (sigma g (f v))`.
Surjectivity then gives equality of the sets of whole functions of `g`
obtained as `v` or `f v` varies. The range equality is an equality in
`Set (G → R)`, not a separate assertion about ranges of values at each `g`.
The coefficient map may have a kernel. Neither irreducibility nor a
topology on either module is assumed. Both module universes are independent.

These lemmas are derived directly from the definitions and the
intertwiner relation; they introduce no additional source theorem.
