# Extending the scalar cell integral to a Whittaker functional

`TwoBlockJacquetFunctionalExtensionStatement` asserts that, for the actual
canonical ordered normalized induction, the scalar Jacquet integral on its
cell submodule is the restriction of an actual standard Whittaker functional
on the entire induced representation. The factor functionals satisfy their
standard Whittaker identities. The rectangular measure is any additive Haar
measure for the canonical p-adic topology and Borel structure.

The equality is asserted for every vector of the cell submodule. Compact
support of the scalar cell integrand gives its ordinary integrability; no
convergence is asserted for vectors outside this submodule. The extension
is an algebraic functional with the required unipotent character, so evaluating
it on induced translates produces an actual coefficient in the corresponding
coefficient-image model. Neither the factors nor the induction are required
to be irreducible. The resulting functional may be zero, and uniqueness is
not asserted.

This is a derived internal prerequisite for the JPSS extension construction.
Cell stability gives a representation of the full upper-unipotent group on
the cell submodule and an injective intertwiner into the restricted induction.
Cell integrability makes the scalar integral linear, and full-unipotent
covariance makes it a character functional. The existing smooth
subgroup-functional extension theorem applies because the ambient smooth
induction is smooth, its standard character is locally constant and the
p-adic upper-unipotent group is a limit of compact subgroups.

This file states the proposition. It supplies neither its proof nor the
prescribed Fourier sections and values required by the host extension theorem.
