# Character-localized heredity for upper block induction

Author: Worker 330.

The theorem `Langlands.inducedLocalization.{uF,uV}` proves exactly the published
closed proposition `Langlands.InducedLocalizationStatement.{uF,uV}`. Its proof
applies `Whittaker.inducedLocalization_of_prerequisites` to four published closed
theorems. No prerequisite remains as a parameter of the theorem.

For every prime, canonical p-adic field, positive total rank and ordered positive
block shape, the conclusion is existence of a full complex linear equivalence
between the actual character quotient of upper unnormalized smooth block
induction and the finite tensor of the actual block character quotients.
The block representations are admissible and of finite type on arbitrary
complex modules, including reducible representations and zero modules. The
raw additive character is continuous, nontrivial and unitary, as in the target.
The field universe `uF` and block-module universe `uV` remain independent.

The source is the localization of
`Induction.smoothInduced (B.parabolic F.Carrier)
  ((FiniteTensor.external π).comp (B.leviProjection F.Carrier))`
by the actual full upper-unipotent subgroup and
`Whittaker.entrywiseCharacter ψ (n + 1)`. Both endpoints use the published
relation spans generated by `ρ(u)v - (θ(u) : ℂ) • v`. The target tensor retains
the original block order and the unchanged positive character in every block.
The theorem asserts `Nonempty` of a full linear equivalence. It supplies no
chosen comparison map, evaluation formula or naturality assertion.

## Discharging the geometric assembly

The Lean proof supplies the constructor's four inputs in order:

- `Whittaker.unipotentOrbit.{uF}`, proved by Worker 179 with Worker 330,
  classifies the actual upper-unipotent double cosets and gives an ordered
  support filtration with the prescribed permutation representatives.
- `Whittaker.nonOpenCharacterWitness.{uF}`, proved by Worker 662 with
  Workers 330 and 179, gives an element in each other orbit stabilizer whose
  Levi projection is the identity and whose ambient character value is
  nonidentity.
- `Whittaker.compactLocalization.{uF,uV}`, proved by Worker 672, identifies
  the actual character quotient of compact induction from every closed
  subgroup of the canonical upper-unipotent group with the inducing
  representation's character quotient.
- `Jacquet.Localization.filtrationCollapse.{uF,max uF uV}`, proved by
  Worker 673, identifies the ambient character quotient with a selected
  graded quotient when all the other graded localizations vanish.

The collapse theorem uses carrier universe `max uF uV` because the induced
function space depends on both field and coefficient carriers. Orbit
classification and the character witness use only the field universe.
Compact localization uses coefficient universe `uV`, the universe of the
external tensor module.

The imported assembly installs the canonical topology, proves the character
kernel open and the character locally constant, and supplies compact containment
for the acting upper-unipotent group. Compactness of the parabolic quotient
compares compact and smooth parabolic induction.
`Geometric.closedSubgroupMackeyFiltration` identifies each graded representation
with compact induction from its actual stabilizer. The compact-localization
theorem then compares their full character quotients.

On each nonselected orbit, the witness element acts trivially on the
Levi-inflated coefficient representation while its character value differs
from one. `Localization.subsingleton_of_trivial_action` therefore annihilates
that quotient. The collapse theorem applies to the actual support filtration,
with no endpoint restriction on the selected index.

The surviving stored representative is `(B.reverseMatrix F.Carrier)⁻¹`,
corresponding to `B.reversePermutation.symm`. Its stabilizer uses the existing
convention `w⁻¹ * q * w ∈ P`. The published
`Whittaker.openStabilizerLocalizationEquiv` handles the block reversal and
character comparison explicitly and identifies this quotient with the tensor
of block quotients in their original order. The restriction and compact-to-smooth
equivalences finish the source comparison. These constructions and compositions
are imported from the assembly without copying their proofs.

Admissibility supplies smoothness of the block representations in that assembly.
Finite generation is retained from the target and is unused by this route.
The resulting proof therefore covers the target's reducible generality directly.
There is no passage through irreducible constituents or a dimension-one
criterion.

## Provenance and scope

This is the final internal application of the published geometric assembly and
the four prerequisite proofs. Their separate files document their constructions
and source relationships. No unpublished draft is reused in this application.
The target and all of its imported definitions remain unchanged.

The original source map is that of `InducedLocalizationStatement`: Rodier's
modified author manuscript, *Modèles de Whittaker des représentations admissibles
des groupes réductifs p-adiques quasi-déployés*, Chapter V.2, Theorem 4 and Lemma 6,
PDF pages 36–42, and Jacquet–Piatetski-Shapiro–Shalika, *Rankin–Selberg
Convolutions* (1983), printed pages 377 and 385–387. Registered source identifiers
are `rodier-whittaker-author-manuscript` and `jpss-1983-rankin-selberg`.

The quotient formulation is a derived strengthening, rather than a literal
transcription of Rodier's irreducible setup or JPSS's dimension-one criterion.
The implemented route proves the actual character quotient comparison through
the support filtration, non-open-cell vanishing and open-cell identification.
It works directly with upper unnormalized induction. The long-Weyl and
block-order transports occur in the imported open-stabilizer comparison.
This module proves the quotient theorem; the separate final theorem on
Whittaker functional spaces remains a consumer of it.
