# Finite external tensor products and character localization

The module constructs the canonical linear equivalence between the character-localized coinvariants of a finite external tensor product and the tensor product of the factor character-localized coinvariants. All public declarations are in `Langlands.FiniteTensor`.

The coefficient ring `R` is any commutative ring. The finite index type `ι`, the groups `H i`, and the modules `V i` have independent universe levels, also independent of the coefficient ring. The only additional structures are `Fintype ι`, `Group (H i)`, `AddCommGroup (V i)`, and `Module R (V i)`. A finite type presented with `Finite ι` can be used by installing `Fintype.ofFinite ι`. There are no dimension, nonzero-module, nonempty-index, topology, smoothness, or admissibility assumptions.

## Character and comparison maps

For subgroups `U i` and units-valued characters `θ i : U i →* Rˣ`, `productCharacter U θ` is a monoid homomorphism on the actual subgroup `Subgroup.pi Set.univ U`. Its value at `u` is

\[
\Theta(u)=\prod_{i\in\iota}\theta_i(u_i),
\]

where each coordinate is equipped with its subgroup-membership proof. `productCharacter_apply` records this formula in the unit group; `coe_productCharacter` records the corresponding product in `R`. This uses exactly the convention of `Langlands.Jacquet.Localization`: the relation submodule is generated by `ρ(u)v − (θ(u) : R) • v`.

`characterLocalizationToTensor ρ U θ` descends `PiTensorProduct.map (fun i => Localization.mk (ρ i) (U i) (θ i))` through the product-character quotient. `map_mk_external` proves the required covariance relation. On a pure tensor it follows from the factor quotient relations and multilinearity, which converts the coordinate scalars into their finite product. Tensor induction extends the equality to every tensor.

`tensorToCharacterLocalization ρ U θ` is the inverse map. It first descends the multilinear map sending a tuple to the class of its pure tensor through each factor relation submodule, and then applies `PiTensorProduct.lift`. The private coordinate-kernel lemma embeds `u : U i` as the identity in all other group coordinates. Its product character is `θ i u`; the global quotient relation therefore kills the factor relation in that tensor slot.

The private simultaneous-descent helper is constructed using `Quotient.finLiftOn`. Its well-definedness proof changes the coordinates over a finite set, using the linear quotient universal property in each slot. Its additivity and scalar compatibility follow by choosing quotient representatives and using the original multilinear identities. The chosen representatives are only used in these proofs; the descended map is defined by the quotient recursor.

## Equivalence and computation

`characterLocalizationEquiv ρ U θ` bundles the two maps as

\[
\operatorname{Coinvariants}(\operatorname{external}(\rho),\prod_i U_i,\Theta)
\;\simeq_R\;
\bigotimes_{i\in\iota}\operatorname{Coinvariants}(\rho_i,U_i,\theta_i).
\]

The theorems `tensorToCharacterLocalization_comp_toTensor` and `characterLocalizationToTensor_comp_toLocalization` state the two inverse equations as equalities of linear maps. The first uses quotient extensionality followed by tensor extensionality. The second uses tensor extensionality and surjectivity of each factor quotient map. Both reduce to pure-tensor evaluation.

The public evaluation lemmas are:

- `characterLocalizationToTensor_mk_tprod` and `characterLocalizationEquiv_mk_tprod`: the class of a pure tensor maps to the pure tensor of the factor classes.
- `tensorToCharacterLocalization_tprod_mk` and `characterLocalizationEquiv_symm_tprod_mk`: the pure tensor of factor classes maps back to the class of the original pure tensor.

The usual `LinearEquiv.apply_symm_apply` and `LinearEquiv.symm_apply_apply` also give the elementwise inverse equations. Surjectivity of the factor quotient maps makes the inverse pure-tensor formula applicable to every pure tensor in the target.

For an empty index type the character is the empty product, hence one, and the same construction and proofs apply. Zero factors require no special treatment. The construction never uses bases, dimensions, or tensor-dual identifications.

## Derived provenance and scope

This is derived internal algebra, with no external literature theorem asserted. Its inputs are the published action and pure-tensor formula in [Finite.lean](Finite.lean), the actual relation quotient and its `mk`, `mk_apply_subgroup`, `relations_le_ker_iff`, `lift`, and `hom_ext` API in [Localization.lean](../Jacquet/Localization.lean), and Mathlib's indexed tensor universal property in `Mathlib.LinearAlgebra.PiTensorProduct.Basic`. The finite simultaneous quotient recursor comes from Mathlib's `Data.Fintype.Quotient`. The narrower untwisted vanishing and single-active-factor results in `FiniteProperties` and `CoinvariantReflection` are not needed for this comparison. This module asserts no induction-heredity, Whittaker geometry, or infinite-dimensional dual factorization result.
