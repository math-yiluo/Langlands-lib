# Scaled Jacquet groups and the actual natural-number level

This internal unit connects the scale in the negative-level argument with the
unchanged ideal-power level subgroups and their actual fixed spaces. The source
convention is `q = ϖ^(-a)`. The two Jacquet invariances remain supplied hypotheses.

For a subring `O` of a field and an ideal `I` of `O`, suppose multiplication by
`q` sends every element of `I` into `O`.
`congruent_opposite_le_scaledOpposite` proves that the actual congruence subgroup
intersected with the last-column mirabolic lies in the scaled opposite Jacquet
group. Diagonal conjugation multiplies the final row off the diagonal by `q`.
The last column remains the final standard column. The integral congruence
condition supplies the required integral entries after multiplication by `q`.
The same argument is applied to the inverse, which remains in the integral
congruence subgroup. Thus both matrix and inverse integrality are retained.

For any valuation and a supplied `Iwasawa.IsUniformizingElement` witness,
`jacquetUniformizerInteger` is the actual element of the integer ring given by
that uniformizer. The maximal ideal equals its principal ideal: the witness
expresses every nonzero valuation as an integer power of `v(ϖ) < 1`, so
`v(x) < 1` is equivalent to `v(x) ≤ v(ϖ)`, which is divisibility by `ϖ` in the
integer ring. Ideal powers are therefore generated by `ϖ^a`, including `a = 0`.
This proves that multiplication by `ϖ^(-a)` sends the actual maximal-ideal power
into the integer ring. `level_opposite_le_scaledOpposite` specializes the
preceding subgroup inclusion to the unchanged `levelSubgroup F n a`.

For `0 < n`, `levelSubgroup_le_jacquetGenerated` combines that inclusion
with `fieldJacquetMirabolic_sup_congruent_opposite` to put the entire actual
level subgroup inside the subgroup generated by the two source groups.
The existing name `jacquetNegativeGenerated` denotes that subgroup supremum
for every scale; it imposes no negative-valuation condition here.

For any representation over a commutative coefficient ring, with independent
field, coefficient and carrier universes,
`mem_levelFixedVectors_of_scaled_jacquet_invariant` proves that a vector fixed
by both source groups belongs to the unchanged `levelFixedVectors π a`.
It restricts the scaled opposite invariance along the proved inclusion and
uses the preceding module's identification of the common Jacquet fixed space
with the actual level-fixed space. This step needs no topology, smoothness,
admissibility, irreducibility or genericity.

`right_level_invariant_of_scaled_jacquet_invariant` applies that result to the
right-translation representation on actual complex-valued functions. Thus
the two supplied right invariances give `f(g*h) = f(g)` for every `h` in the
actual natural-number level subgroup and every field general linear element
`g`.

Finally, for a rank-at-most-one valued field with a topology and a nontrivial
unitary additive character,
`right_level_toNat_invariant_of_uniformizer_jacquet_invariant` starts with a
nonzero standard Whittaker function and an integer scale `q = ϖ^(-m)`.
The imported negative-level obstruction proves `0 ≤ m`; hence the natural
number `m.toNat` casts back to `m`. The preceding function result then gives
right invariance under the unchanged `levelSubgroup F n m.toNat`.
The nonzero function and both source invariances are explicit premises.

The provenance is internal: explicit matrix conjugation, the definitions of the
congruence subgroup and valuation integer ring, and the existing Iwasawa
uniformizer predicate give the group and ideal calculations. The final steps
reuse the published integral generation theorem through `JacquetLevelInvariance`,
the ordinary right-translation representation and the negative-level Whittaker
obstruction. The imported Jacquet modules document the source motivation and
the corrected determinant-one boundary of that obstruction.

This unit leaves the analytic derivation of the two source invariances open.
It constructs no essential vector, epsilon-factor comparison, minimal fixed
level or dimension-one fixed space, and does not prove
`EssentialConductorStatement`.
