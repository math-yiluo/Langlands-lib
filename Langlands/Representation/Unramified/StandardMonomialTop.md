# Nonvanishing of the top coefficient of standard Hecke monomials

This module supplies the positivity half of the standard monomial triangularity argument. It is internal supporting mathematics for the direct generation proof in `SphericalHeckeGenerationStatement`. The motivating double-coset generation argument is Gross, *On the Satake isomorphism* (1998), section 2, Proposition 2.6 and formulas (2.7)–(2.9). The analytic positivity proof below is an internal derivation from the Library's Haar convolution and Mathlib's integration results; it does not invoke the Satake isomorphism or a classification theorem.

The implemented analytic argument uses the actual Haar convolution. A spherical Hecke function is called nonnegative when its values have zero imaginary part and nonnegative real part. This property is closed under convolution, since the integrand has those properties and is integrable. If two such functions are positive at `x` and `y`, their convolution is positive at `x*y`: the continuous nonnegative real part of the integrand is positive at `x`, and Haar measure gives every nonempty open set positive mass. Mathlib's `integral_pos_of_integrable_nonneg_nonzero` implements this step. Thus no numerical lower bound on the convolution is required.

The standard double-coset indicators are nonnegative and have value one on their defining diagonal. The corner unit is the zeroth standard element and takes value one at the identity. Induction on natural powers propagates nonnegativity and strict positivity to the corresponding powers of these points. The torus identities `standardTorus_add` and `standardTorus_zero`, followed by `standardDiagonal_pow_eq_torus`, identify products of diagonal powers with the torus element whose exponent is their sum.

The list induction in `standardMonomial_list_top` proves simultaneously that an ordered product of standard-element powers is nonnegative everywhere and strictly positive at that sum's torus element. Applying it to the finite list of all indices, then using `monomialExponent_eq_sum` from `StandardMonomialSupport`, proves `standardMonomial_top_re_pos`. Its consequence `standardMonomial_top_ne_zero` is the required nonvanishing of the leading torus coefficient. The list proof respects the order used to define `standardMonomial`; it requires no change to that definition or commutation of the factors.

Finally, `standardMonomialTriangularity (F) : StandardMonomialTriangularity F` combines this nonvanishing with `standardMonomial_support` from `StandardMonomialSupport`. It establishes exactly the two clauses of the internal triangularity proposition: the dominance support bound and a nonzero value at the leading torus element. It applies to every prime, finite p-adic extension, positive rank, and natural exponent tuple, using the canonical topologies and normalized spherical Haar measure. It does not itself assert the final spherical constituent uniqueness theorem.
