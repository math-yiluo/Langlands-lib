# Spherical vectors in normalized block products

This internal construction takes any upper standard block parabolic of
`GL_(r+1)(F)` for the canonical topology on a p-adic field. Each supplied
complex representation is assumed nonzero and trivial on the subgroup whose
determinant has normalized absolute value one. The field and factor carrier
universes are independent. The construction yields a nonzero vector fixed
by the actual spherical subgroup in the actual normalized product induction.
It does not require irreducibility, admissibility or an ordering of factors.

For an integral parabolic matrix, the diagonal blocks of both it and its
inverse are integral. Each block determinant therefore has norm one. The
positive half-modulus is one on this Levi subgroup. Every factor acts trivially,
so the normalized inducing action fixes the whole tensor carrier.

Upper triangular Iwasawa decomposition also supplies a decomposition for every
upper block parabolic, since every upper triangular matrix lies in it. The
published extension of an intersection-fixed vector to a smooth spherical
induced function applies to every tensor vector. A finite tensor product of
nonzero complex vector spaces is nonzero; choose a nonzero tensor and its
extension. Evaluation at the identity recovers that tensor and proves that the
constructed spherical vector is nonzero. The final lemma records that the
spherical fixed submodule is nonzero.
