# Spherical segment-socle products

This internal construction specializes the spherical block-product vector to an
actual product of segment socles. Its premise identifies the full cuspidal
content, with multiplicity, with a finite list of actual rank-one supercuspidal
representations. Each supplied object must act through the determinant by an
unramified character. No unlinkedness or order assumption is needed for the
fixed-vector construction.

Every segment base occurs in its cuspidal content and therefore in the total
content. Content equality identifies that base with one supplied rank-one
class. The class is a permissible representative in the existing segment
subrepresentation relation. The rank-one segment character computation gives
that the determinant norm-one subgroup fixes the entire segment carrier.
Irreducibility of each socle gives nontriviality of every factor carrier. The
spherical product construction then produces the claimed nonzero actual
spherical vector. The final lemma records nonvanishing of the fixed submodule.
