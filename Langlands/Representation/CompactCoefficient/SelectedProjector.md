# Finite selected-class projectors

This internal construction sums the published intrinsic projectors over a finite set of distinct compact-coefficient classes. It assumes intrinsic complementarity for each selected class on the ambient representation. It does not assume or construct complements for unselected classes. Group, representative-carrier and ambient-carrier universes are independent.

`selectedIsotypicSubrepresentation` is the supremum of the selected intrinsic isotypic subrepresentations. `selectedAnnihilatorSubrepresentation` is the infimum of their intrinsic annihilators. `selectedProjectorOfIsCompl` sums the conditional single-class projectors using the published `pointwiseFiniteSum`. The index subtype of a `Finset` is finite and has no repeated class, so pointwise finite support is automatic and the published Schur orthogonality result applies to distinct indices. The generic sum laws prove idempotence, identify the range and kernel, and give complementarity of the selected sum and common annihilator. Naturality follows from the single-class naturality law, including when the ambient carrier universes differ.

`selectedProjectorOfIsCompl_covariant` takes a topological group equivalence and an explicit equivalence between membership in the selected sets before and after class pullback. This restricts `pullbackEquiv` to a bijection of the finite index subtypes. The published single-class covariance theorem and `pointwiseFiniteSum_covariant` then imply covariance of the sum. For a normal subgroup, stability of the selected set under inverse conjugation allows the actual ambient group action to play the intertwiner in this result. `ambientSelectedProjector` is therefore an actual ambient equivariant endomorphism. Its idempotence, exact underlying range and kernel, and naturality are inherited from the subgroup sum.

The provenance is internal algebra over the published conditional projections, intrinsic class transport and pointwise finite sum laws. No new analytic result is used. In particular, this module does not supply the finite restriction family, identify which classes occur in a chosen constituent, prove their conjugation stability, prove intrinsic complementarity, establish semisimplicity or type coverage of the range, or prove the final block-isolation theorem. Those remain separate inputs and obligations. The construction also covers the empty selected set, when the sum is zero and the common annihilator is the whole carrier.
