# Finite-family block assembly

`finiteFamilyBlockPair` constructs the actual range and kernel of the selected ambient projector from a supplied finite family of compact irreducible subgroup representations with injective intertwiners spanning the restriction of an ambient object. Intrinsic complements are supplied for every compact class and smooth subgroup object. The finite-family conjugation theorem proves the class stability required by the ambient projector. This construction itself requires only a topological ambient group and normality of the subgroup; it does not require a Haar measure, local compactness, or irreducibility of the spanning ambient object.

This internal assembly starts with an irreducible smooth complex representation `D` of a topological group `G`, a normal subgroup `N` with its actual subtype topology, and a supplied finite family of actual irreducible subrepresentations spanning the restriction of `D`. The restriction must have compact smooth-dual coefficients. The subgroup is Hausdorff, locally compact and totally disconnected, with a Borel structure and a supplied left Haar measure. Intrinsic isotypic parts and annihilators are assumed complementary for every compact-coefficient class and every smooth subgroup representation in the chosen carrier universe.

For every ambient smooth representation `A`, the theorem constructs actual complementary invariant subspaces `S` and `Q`. The restrictions of `D` and `S` are semisimple. Every irreducible subrepresentation of the restriction of `S` is equivalent to a member of the supplied family, and `D` is not a subquotient of `Q`. The group, carrier and finite-indexing universes are independent; `A` and `D` share the carrier universe required by the existing subquotient relation.

The proof of `finiteFamilyBlockIsolation_of_isCompl` first inherits compact coefficients on each family member and packages their distinct isomorphism classes. Their actual subtype maps are injective and span the restriction. The conjugation lemma therefore proves stability of the selected finite class set. The `finiteFamilyBlockPair` construction gives `S` and `Q` as the selected ambient projector's actual range and kernel.

Every vector of `S` belongs to the total compact-isotypic part of the restriction of `A`, hence has compact ambient coefficients. Extension of smooth functionals gives compact coefficients on `S` itself. The published compact-coefficient semisimplicity theorem supplies semisimplicity of both restrictions. Type detection applies to the injection of an irreducible subrepresentation of `S` into `A`, whose image is disjoint from the selected common annihilator. Finally, irreducibility makes `D` nonzero, so its finite spanning family is nonempty. Any member supplies the selected restricted subrepresentation required by the published subquotient-exclusion lemma.

The provenance is internal composition of the selected conjugation, projector, coverage, exclusion and total compactness APIs, together with the published semisimplicity theorem. This result does not prove that a finite restriction family exists, that a cuspidal representation has compact coefficients on a determinant-unit subgroup, or that intrinsic complements exist. Those remain explicit inputs; this is not the final unconditional Levi block-isolation theorem. It does not reprove the separately tasked pure algebraic semisimplicity of intrinsic sums.
