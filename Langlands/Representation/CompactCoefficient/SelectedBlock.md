# Selected block pairs and conditional subquotient exclusion

This internal construction packages the range and kernel of the ambient selected projector as a pair of actual invariant subrepresentations. Idempotence makes their underlying submodules complementary. The hypotheses remain intrinsic complementarity for the selected classes and conjugation stability of the finite selection.

The selected projector fixes every selected representative: the identity intertwiner places each vector in its intrinsic isotypic part, hence in the selected projector range, and idempotence fixes that range. Assuming intrinsic complements for the selected classes on every smooth representation in the same carrier universe, an object embedded into the selected common annihilator has no selected representative as a subquotient. Naturality first makes its projector zero, then passes zero through an injective/surjective presentation of any subquotient. The selected representative is fixed and nonzero, giving a contradiction. The global complement assumption is explicit and is needed for the intermediate object in that presentation.

The ambient exclusion lemma then uses the published restriction/subquotient bridge: a selected actual irreducible compact-coefficient subrepresentation of the restricted candidate would be a subquotient of the restricted annihilator block if the original candidate were an ambient subquotient. The preceding exclusion rules this out. Both the selected subrepresentation and its compactness are explicit inputs.

These are internal consequences of conditional selected projectors and the actual subquotient presentation. This module does not supply analytic complements, finite restriction, semisimplicity of the selected range, or the final block-isolation proof.
