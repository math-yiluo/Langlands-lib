# Projective lifting for all compact-coefficient smooth objects

Worker 1740. This internal construction extends the published compact-coefficient irreducible lifting theorem to every smooth complex representation with compact smooth-dual coefficients. The topological group is Hausdorff, locally compact and totally disconnected; its universe and the carrier universe are independent. No finite length, finite generation or admissibility premise is used.

The general helper `SmoothRep.projective_of_semisimple_of_projective_subrepresentations` assumes semisimplicity and projectivity of every actual irreducible subrepresentation. Mathlib decomposes the actual group-algebra module as an algebraic direct sum of simple submodules. Each simple submodule gives an actual smooth irreducible subrepresentation, identified with that module by an explicit equivariant linear equivalence. Given an epimorphism and a morphism from the whole source, restrict the morphism along the summand maps, choose its projective lifts, and sum those lifts. The inverse module decomposition converts that sum to an ambient intertwiner. Its composite is the prescribed morphism because the equality holds on every summand. All sums have finite support on each vector, even when there are infinitely many summands.

For `CompactCoefficient.projective_of_compactCoefficients`, the published compact-coefficient semisimplicity proof supplies semisimplicity, after choosing a left Haar measure with the Borel measurable structure. Compact coefficients pass to each actual smooth subrepresentation by the published smooth-functional extension theorem and cofinal compact open subgroups. The published irreducible projectivity result therefore applies to every simple summand. No choice of a Haar measure remains as a hypothesis or in the conclusion.

`allProjectiveLift` chooses the resulting categorical lift, and `allProjectiveLift_comp` proves that it composes with the epimorphism to the original map. The provenance is entirely internal: Mathlib's semisimple-module decomposition and the Library's actual smooth subrepresentations, compact-coefficient semisimplicity, coefficient inheritance and irreducible projective lifting. This does not establish that an arbitrary norm-kernel restriction has compact coefficients, nor the block membership or generator conditions of the full small-progenerator target.
