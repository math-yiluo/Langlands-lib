# Finite class sets detected by compact irreducible types

This is an internal assembly argument for the compact-type comparison in the concluding remark of Renard VI.3.4. Its inputs are explicit; it does not prove fixed-level compact-coefficient finiteness, a rational-point intertwiner comparison, or cuspidality's compact-restriction consequence.

`fixedTypeClassEmbedding` takes a family of smooth ambient representations, chosen compact irreducible types on a subgroup `N` with nonzero vectors fixed by `L ≤ N`, and a separation hypothesis: a nonzero intertwiner between the restricted ambient representations forces equality of their indices. It maps an index to the isomorphism class of its chosen type in `CompactCoefficientClass.fixedStratum L`. Equality of these classes provides an actual type isomorphism. Composing the source projection, that isomorphism, and the target inclusion gives a nonzero restricted ambient intertwiner, so separation proves injectivity.

`finite_classes_of_compactRestriction` assumes a locally compact Hausdorff nonarchimedean group, an open subgroup `N`, a property `P` of smooth representations whose restrictions have compact smooth-dual coefficients, and a class assignment to irreducible ambient representations. Nonzero restricted intertwiners must give equality of these classes. For subgroups `K ≤ G` and `L ≤ N` with the image of `L` contained in `K`, finiteness of the compact-coefficient fixed stratum at `L` implies finiteness of the assigned classes represented by irreducible objects satisfying `P` and having nontrivial `K`-fixed spaces.

The proof chooses an actual representative for each such class. A nonzero `K`-fixed vector remains fixed by `L` after restriction. Openness gives local compactness of `N`; the nonarchimedean and Hausdorff properties give total disconnectedness. The compact-coefficient semisimplicity theorem, applied with a Haar measure on `N`, gives semisimplicity of every chosen restriction. The fixed-type detection construction supplies the types and projections. The embedding above places the class set inside the assumed finite stratum.

The group, ambient carrier, class, and family-index universe levels are independent. No finite-length, admissibility, finite-dimensionality, or decomposition premise is imposed on the ambient representations. The compact-coefficient and finite-stratum conditions, and the restricted-intertwiner comparison, remain explicit assumptions.
