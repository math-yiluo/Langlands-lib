# Native normalized Jacquet grades of full smooth support

This internal assembly combines `CoefficientClosedSubgroupMackey`, `CompactSmoothComparison` and `Jacquet.CoefficientRestrictedFiltration`. It constructs and compares actual support-image grades of full smooth induction over a complex coefficient algebra. It retains finite ordered orbit geometry as explicit input and does not claim the orbit-Jacquet calculation or the final universal-family finiteness theorem.

For a commutative ring `R`, a nonarchimedean topological group `G`, and a subgroup `P`, `fullSmoothInductionRepFunctor` is the full smooth induction functor on smooth covariance data, followed by the forgetful functor to representations. The coefficient, group and covariance-carrier universes are independent; the induced carrier has universe `max uG uV`.

For `s : OrderedDoubleCosets P Q n`, `fullSmoothSupportFiltration` restricts the published raw full-smooth support filtration to smooth inputs. Its kth step consists of actual functions whose projected nonzero support lies in the first k orbit unions specified by `s`. No representation-theoretic filtration is supplied as an extra premise.

Let `U` and `M` be subgroups of `G`, where `M` normalizes `U` and lies in `Q`. For any smooth scalar character `χ` of `M`, `nativeSmoothJacquetSupportFiltration` takes the original `Q`-stable support subspaces, maps them into the literal ambient native character-one `U` quotient, and twists by `χ`. It uses the existing `restrictedImageFiltration` and keeps its original order and both endpoints. The membership theorem states that a class belongs to the kth image exactly when it has a representative in the kth original full-smooth support step; twisting leaves that condition unchanged.

Assume that `G` is nonarchimedean, locally compact, Hausdorff and sigma-compact, that `P` and `Q` are closed, and that the actual quotient `P \ G` is compact. `fullSmoothSupportGradedOrbitIso` compares each actual full-smooth support grade with the compactly induced stabilizer representation of the same orbit. The proof first reverses the natural compact-to-smooth graded comparison, restricted to smooth inputs, and then applies the arbitrary-ring coefficient Mackey comparison. Compactness of `P \ G` is used only for the first comparison. The orbit piece uses the published stabilizer `Q ∩ w P w⁻¹`, conjugation by `w⁻¹`, and compact induction to `Q`.

Assume further that `R` is a complex algebra, `U ≤ Q`, and `U` satisfies `Jacquet.Localization.IsLimitOfCompactSubgroups`. `nativeSmoothJacquetSupportGradedOrbitIso` identifies each grade of the exact normalized ambient support-image filtration with the native subgroup quotient of that compact orbit piece, then twisted by the same `χ`. The quotient functor is `Coefficient.subgroupFunctor`, so the output acts on the original ambient subgroup `M`. The proof uses `twistedRestrictedImageFiltrationGradedIso`, with smoothness supplied by the actual smooth-induced action, followed by the preceding orbit isomorphism whiskered by the native quotient and scalar-twist functors. The result is natural and `R`-linear and preserves the original grade indices, including repeated steps or zero grades.

Inverse-half-modulus normalization is obtained by supplying that actual smooth character. Inducing covariance data can be obtained by precomposition with a smooth inflation and positive-normalization functor. No flatness, admissibility, finite generation, or Jacquet-finiteness hypothesis is used here. For the actual universal rational family, finite rational-parabolic orbit geometry, intersection data, the normalized orbit-Jacquet calculation, proper-piece elimination and fixed-module finiteness of the surviving pieces remain explicit proof obligations.
