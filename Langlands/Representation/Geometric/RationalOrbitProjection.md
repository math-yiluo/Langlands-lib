# The actual target projection on an adapted rational orbit stabilizer

This internal geometric unit uses an actual source parabolic `p : P ⟶ G`, an output cocharacter `χ`, and the original representative `w`, over an arbitrary field with affine ambient group scheme. It proves projection and section facts for the literal rational-point subgroups. There is no coefficient representation, topology, admissibility or finite-filtration premise.

Write `S` for the literal point image of `p`, `Q` for the dynamic output parabolic, and `L` for its dynamic centralizer. The existing full orbit stabilizer is `H = Q ∩ w S w⁻¹`, viewed as a subgroup of `Q`; its action convention is the orbit of `S w⁻¹`. Its intersection with the output Levi is `B = L ∩ w S w⁻¹`, viewed as a subgroup of `L`. The projection and section used throughout are those of the existing actual dynamic semidirect decomposition for `χ`.

The geometric input for projection preservation is the all-test inclusion `range χ ≤ range (p ≫ Int(w))`. It says that the actual output cocharacter is contained in the conjugate source parabolic. This is weaker than requiring containment in its specified Levi. `range_cocharacter_le_conjugateParabolic_of_commonTorus` derives this input when a supplied ambient maximal split torus lies in both the output centralizer and the conjugate source parabolic. It uses the existing theorem that a cocharacter centralized by a maximal split torus factors through that same torus. Existence of such a torus or representative is not asserted here.

`orbitStabilizer_projection_mem` derives projection preservation from closedness and invariance. The conjugate source parabolic is represented by the original map `p ≫ Int(w)` and is closed because `p` is parabolic. Containment of `χ` makes it invariant under the cocharacter's conjugation action: every subgroup is preserved by conjugation by its own elements. For `x ∈ H`, the actual output limit of `x` remains in this closed invariant subgroup by the proved closed-subgroup limit theorem. That limit is exactly the original dynamic Levi projection. The rational-point conjugation comparison then puts its value in `B`. The use of the closed-subgroup limit theorem is valid on every test scheme, rather than relying on a choice of field closure or a topological limiting sequence.

Conversely, the actual output Levi section sends every element of `B` into `H`, directly by the stabilizer definition. `orbitStabilizer_map_projection` combines these facts with the projection-section identity to prove the exact subgroup equality `H.map projection = B`. This is an equality of the prescribed actual subgroup image and actual intersection, not a supplied decomposition conclusion.

`rationalOrbitStages` is the literal preimage `projection⁻¹(B)` inside the original target parabolic. The remaining lemmas prove that it contains the original projection kernel, that its section preimage and the full stabilizer's section preimage both equal `B`, and that the section over this stages subgroup lands in the full stabilizer. Finally, under the cocharacter-containment input, `H ≤ rationalOrbitStages` follows from the proved projection preservation. These are the original subgroup and section conditions used by the split-stages calculation.

The provenance is internal: the unit composes the published actual orbit-stabilizer definition, rational-point conjugation comparison, maximal-split-torus factorization, closed invariant subgroup limit theorem and dynamic projection laws. It does not establish finite double cosets, adapted-representative existence, source-radical image comparisons, compatible measures, native orbit-quotient isomorphisms or the global Jacquet fixed-module finiteness theorem. Those remain separate obligations.
