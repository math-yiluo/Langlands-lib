# Normalizers of the common Levi of a maximal opposite pair

Renard (2026), VI.10.1 Lemma, printed p. 207 and PDF p. 213, compares induction from opposite parabolics with maximal Levi. The geometric filtration of VI.5.3(iii), printed p. 173 and PDF p. 179, indexes its grades by double cosets with a Levi normalizer as representative. This module supplies the group-scheme range comparisons for that application.

The maximal-Levi count of Renard V.4.8 gives at most two distinct parabolic ranges with the prescribed Levi. Conjugating by a normalizer permutes those two ranges. If one range is fixed, the second is fixed as well, and self-normalization of each parabolic puts the point in their intersection, the Levi.

For the identity double coset, write the point as \(xy\) with \(x\in P(k)\) and \(y\in Q(k)\). Conjugation by \(y\) preserves \(Q\). If \(xy\) exchanges the parabolic ranges, conjugation by \(x\) therefore carries \(Q\) to \(P\) while preserving \(P\). Cancellation of this ambient automorphism contradicts distinctness. Thus a Levi normalizer representing the identity double coset belongs to the Levi.
