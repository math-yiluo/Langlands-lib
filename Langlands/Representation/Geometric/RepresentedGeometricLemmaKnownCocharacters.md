# Actual geometric-lemma data from known source and target cocharacters

This internal construction preserves the original source and target parabolic, rational Levi and geometric radical maps. It constructs an actual `GeometricLemmaIntersectionDatum` from a torus-adapted representative when one cocharacter already realizes each original triple. It also constructs the simultaneous `DynamicRealization` in any such datum's fixed common torus. The construction does not require the universal `ParabolicDynamicStatement`. It assumes, rather than proves, the displayed representative and common maximal split torus; no finiteness or compactness conclusion is included.

The field and all group schemes use the arbitrary universe `u`. A topology on the field is retained because the actual intersection-datum interface includes a rational-point homeomorphism. There are no local-field, locally compact, characteristic or split-ambient-group restrictions in this unit. The ambient group is affine. The given triples have their original parabolicity, rational-Levi, geometric-radical and multiplication witnesses.

## Actual intersection datum

`RepresentedParabolic.geometricLemmaIntersectionDatumOfKnownCocharacters` takes cocharacters `chiP` and `chiQ` and six equalities on all relative test schemes: the ranges of the original source parabolic, Levi and radical are their three dynamic subfunctors for `chiP`, and the corresponding target ranges are the three dynamic subfunctors for `chiQ`. It also takes a rational point `z`, a maximal split torus `i : T -> G`, and factorizations of `i` through the source Levi and the target Levi conjugated by `z`.

The proof conjugates the displayed torus by `z` inverse to obtain a maximal split torus `iz` in the original target Levi and in the source Levi conjugated by `z` inverse. The target factorization puts the range of `iz` in the centralizer of `chiQ`; the source factorization puts the range of `i` in the centralizer of `chiP`. The published `IsMaximalSplitTorus.exists_cocharacter_factor_of_centralizing` factors the known cocharacters through these same tori. Postcomposing the source equality with the inner automorphism of `z` inverse makes the resulting source cocharacter factor through `iz` as well.

Three private helpers transport an equality with a dynamic parabolic, centralizer or unipotent subgroup through an ambient isomorphism. Each uses the all-test range/comap equality and the corresponding published dynamic functoriality equality. These produce the source triple identities after conjugation; the target identities use the factored target cocharacter directly. The published `geometricLemmaIntersectionDatumOfCommonCocharacters` then constructs both actual intersection triples, their original conjugation isomorphism and rational-point homeomorphism, and their common maximal split torus. The result uses the supplied maps and the supplied parameter `z`; it does not replace the orbit or radical.

## Simultaneous realization in the fixed common torus

`GeometricLemmaIntersectionDatum.dynamicRealizationOfKnownCocharacters` starts with any actual intersection datum for those same original triples and six cocharacter equalities. Its source ambient common torus is `D.t` followed by the source intersection and Levi maps. Its target ambient common torus is `D.targetCommonTorusHom` followed by the target intersection and Levi maps. Both are maximal split by the published datum API. Their ranges lie in the respective original Levi centralizers, so the same cocharacter-factor theorem supplies cocharacters of `D.torus`.

The published `targetCommonTorusHom_comp` identifies the target ambient torus with the source one conjugated by `z` inverse. Therefore the source factorization postcomposes to the target ambient torus. The three ambient-isomorphism transport helpers give the source parabolic, Levi and radical equalities there; the target factorization gives the other three equalities. These six equalities are exactly the fields of `D.DynamicRealization`. The proof constructs all fields for the datum's existing torus and actual algebraic conjugation maps.

## Provenance and boundary

The provenance is internal composition of `RepresentedGeometricLemmaCommonTorus`, `RepresentedGeometricLemmaCommonCocharacterConstruction`, and the published range and dynamic-subgroup functoriality equalities. The generic `geometricLemmaIntersectionDatumOfTorusFactors` obtains original cocharacters by assuming the universal parabolic-dynamic statement. This unit instead uses original cocharacters already supplied by a consumer, which is useful when the source is a compatible root presentation and the output is a cocharacter. It does not prove the universal parabolic-dynamic assertion or remove that assertion from the existing generic theorem.

There is no coefficient representation, admissibility assumption, finite-filtration conclusion or fixed-module finiteness assertion in these constructions. Existence of finite torus-adapted representatives, an ordering with open initial unions, and compactness of original target-intersection parabolic quotients are separate obligations for the native Jacquet consumer.
