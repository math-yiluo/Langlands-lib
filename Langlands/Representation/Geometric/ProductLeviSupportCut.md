# The coarse parabolic support cut

This internal construction identifies the coarse parabolic image in the left coset space of a flattened refining parabolic. The image is closed because its inverse image under the quotient map is the original closed coarse parabolic. Both fine parabolics lie in the coarse one. Membership in that image is therefore constant on each fine double-coset orbit, and holds exactly when the orbit representative is in the coarse parabolic.

The open-complement ordering lemma places this closed subset at the end of an existing ordered orbit cover. At the resulting cut, the raw support submodule consists exactly of induced functions vanishing on the coarse parabolic. The previous restriction-kernel theorem and its Jacquet image then identify the support-filtration step with the component kernel of the actual natural product-Levi quotient.

A separate matrix calculation proves that a permutation matrix belongs to a standard parabolic exactly when its permutation preserves each coarse block. Parabolic membership of the permutation and its inverse give the two inequalities between source and image block labels.

All assertions are internal consequences of the published coordinate identities, actual subgroup and support definitions, the previously constructed quotient, and elementary finite-order and quotient topology arguments. This module supplies geometry and the kernel identification; the final natural graded filtration is assembled separately.
