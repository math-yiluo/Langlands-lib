# Uniqueness of actual standard Levi refinements

This internal result proves that an actual standard block Levi subgroup over
any field determines its consecutive positive block shape. It also proves
that equality of the actual refined Levi subgroups inside a fixed coarse
product Levi determines the entire block refinement. The rank may be zero.

For two equal standard Levi subgroups, the identity coordinate permutation
is a Levi conjugacy. The published conjugacy results give its bijection on
block labels and equality of corresponding block sizes. Since the block
labels of standard coordinates are monotone, this block bijection is
strictly increasing. An order isomorphism between finite ordinal sets
preserves the underlying natural-number labels. The block counts and
corresponding sizes therefore agree, and the published block-shape
extensionality lemma proves equality of the shapes.

To recover a refinement from its subgroup, insert an arbitrary matrix in
one coarse factor and the identity in every other factor. Membership in the
product subgroup detects membership in the selected standard inner Levi.
Equality of the product subgroups consequently gives equality of each inner
Levi subgroup; the preceding result applies to every coarse factor.

These are derived algebraic and order arguments from the published actual
Levi-conjugacy and coordinate definitions. No external literature theorem
is asserted. They allow a refinement chosen existentially by the geometric
lemma to be identified with a prescribed refinement after actual Levi
subgroup equality has been established. They do not assert a geometric
filtration, grade occurrence or any multiplicity comparison.
