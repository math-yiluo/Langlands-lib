# The self-twist test for restriction across a cyclic quotient

This internal representation-theoretic module is used in the torsion-one and
wild-irreducibility steps of the proof of `WeilConductorCountStepStatement`
(Henniart's Corollaire 1.4). It works with finite-dimensional
complex representations of arbitrary groups, without a topology. Group and
carrier universes are independent.

## Constructions

- `eigenlineStabilizer R x` consists of group elements taking `x` to a nonzero
  scalar multiple of `x`. For a nonzero vector this is the stabilizer of its
  span. When that subgroup is the whole group, `eigenlineCharacter` selects the
  scalar, and scalar uniqueness implies the homomorphism law.
- `tracelessCentralizer ρ N` is the intersection of the invariants for the
  conjugation action of `N` on the endomorphism space with the kernel of trace.
  If `N` is normal, conjugation by any group element preserves both conditions.

## Implemented restriction test

Suppose `ρ` is irreducible, its restriction to the normal subgroup `N` is
semisimple, and the quotient is generated by one element (the quotient may be
infinite). If the traceless centralizer were nonzero, the generator's
conjugation operator on it would have a nonzero eigenvector `f` over `ℂ`.
The eigenvalue is nonzero because conjugation is invertible. The line of `f`
is also fixed pointwise by `N`, so the whole group acts on it via a character
`χ` trivial on `N`. The conjugation identity makes `f` a nonzero intertwiner
from the twist by `χ` to `ρ`. It is surjective by irreducibility of the target
and therefore bijective on the equal, finite-dimensional carriers. If there
are no nontrivial self-twists by characters trivial on `N`, then `χ = 1`.
Schur's lemma for `ρ` makes `f` scalar, and the vanishing trace makes that
scalar zero, a contradiction.

Thus every trace-zero intertwiner on the restriction is zero. Subtracting its
trace divided by the rank times the identity from an arbitrary intertwiner
shows that all endomorphisms are scalar. The semisimplicity assumption gives a
complement to any invariant subspace, and its equivariant projection, being
scalar, forces that subspace to be zero or the full space. Conversely, an
irreducible restriction forces a self-twisting equivalence by a character
trivial on `N` to be scalar and hence the character to be trivial. This converse
does not require cyclicity or normality.

In `isIrreducible_comp_of_cyclic_coprime`, taking determinants of a self-twist
shows that each character value has order dividing the rank. It also has
order dividing the quotient index since the character is trivial on `N`.
Coprimality makes both conditions force the value to be `1`. Here index is
`Subgroup.index` (zero for an infinite quotient); the application to wild
inertia uses the finite tame quotient. The module also proves that a
semisimple representation pulls back to a semisimple representation along a
surjective group homomorphism, by transporting complementary subspaces.

This is the abstract restriction argument, not the identification of Weil
stabilizers or of the actual tame quotient. Those require separate local
comparisons.
