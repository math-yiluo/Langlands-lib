# Weighted integration along the factors of a finite product

A weighted average integrates left translates against a continuous complex weight on a compact probability space. Compactness makes these integrands integrable, and continuity of the parameter integral gives an endomorphism of the space of continuous functions.

For a continuous monoid homomorphism from a finite product, the endomorphism at one factor translates by its coordinate inclusion. Composing the factor averages is the weighted average against the finite product measure, with the product of the weights. The proof splits the first coordinate from the others using the measure-preserving product equivalence. The tail inclusion followed by the first-coordinate inclusion is the original tuple. Fubini rewrites the two separate integrals as the single product integral.

This is an internal integral identity for assembling the compact block weights of the real Cartan trace.
