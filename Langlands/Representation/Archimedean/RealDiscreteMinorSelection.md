# The minor columns compatible with a shifted Jacquet parameter

This internal argument combines the signed residue order of the nested donor with
the squared-weight bound for products of minors. A match of the shifted Jacquet
exponents gives a permutation from receiver positions to donor positions. Compose
its inverse with the given permutation of signed inducing characters. The resulting
donor permutation preserves the residue of the signed exponent because both shifts
have the parity of the corresponding character.

The donor shifts are oppositely ordered to the real exponents within each residue.
The rearrangement inequality and the upper bound for the sum of squared column
weights force the matching permutation to preserve real exponents. Equal residues
at equal real exponents give equal signs, so the donor shift, which depends on those
two quantities, is fixed. Thus the column weights are the donor shifts transported
by the given permutation of inducing characters. The squared-weight bound is now
an equality. It forces the column family to be nested, and its sizes and weights
identify it with the transported row family.
