# Smooth surjectivity and controlled principal-series lifts

`RealPrincipalSeriesSmoothSurjectivityStatement` concerns an algebraic scalar
minimal principal-series quotient onto the compact-finite vectors of an
irreducible admissible jointly continuous Banach representation of the real
general linear group. The source identification of the irreducible module
with the compact-finite carrier preserves the compact and Lie actions.
The given algebraic quotient intertwines the same actions. The statement
asserts a surjective continuous complex-linear
full-group intertwiner between the genuine smooth carriers extending the given
algebraic quotient. The scalar principal series uses the actual compact
Banach realization of the rank-matched character list. The algebraic quotient
carrier and its Banach carrier have independent universes.

This is the smooth-quotient portion of Bernstein–Krötz, *Smooth Fréchet
globalizations of Harish-Chandra modules*, arXiv:0812.1684v3, registered as
`bernstein-kroetz-2014-smooth-frechet-globalizations-arxiv-v3` (SHA-256
`f5f2e79d87532c9ac46389d7eb1606e0301eac0ba7c7972ab628e278e091ca3e`),
Lemma 6.2 (p. 31) and Theorem 10.6 (p. 45). The smooth quotient of the
minimal globalization comes from Lemma 6.2. Goodness identifies both
minimal globalizations with the given genuine Banach smooth carriers.

`realPrincipalSeriesQuotientWordLift_of_smoothSurjectivity` applies the open
mapping theorem to the supplied map. It covers the compact-finite target
because its restriction agrees with the surjective algebraic quotient.
The controlled lift theorem for an open smooth quotient gives the genuine
word estimate on a compact-finite preimage. Transport through the compact
principal-series equivalence turns this preimage into the algebraic lift.
Combining the resulting lift estimate with the principal-series word bound
gives the full conditional Casselman–Wallach bound by the Library's quotient
reduction.
