# The kernel of the orbit map of a vector with `ℓ(Y) T = r(Yᵀ) T`

Module: `Langlands.Representation.Archimedean.SphericalKernel`, namespace
`Langlands.Archimedean.SphericalKernel`. Everything here is proved. The module ends with the
proposition `SphericalKernelStatement`, which restates the main theorem `mem_kerSpan` as a
`Prop`, and its proof `sphericalKernel` (see "The statement" at the end).

`U = U(𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ))` (`glU n`), `Z = center n` its center, `E_pq = Matrix.single p q 1` with image
`unitU n p q` in `U`. For a Lie algebra action `ℓ` of `𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)` on `V`, `Λ_ℓ = envelopingAction ℓ`
is its extension to `U`. `trU` is the transpose anti-automorphism of `U` (`trU(ι Y) = ι Yᵀ`,
module `BGChaseEnveloping`). `M⁻(μ) = LowerVerma n μ`, with generator `gen n μ` and defining left
ideal `lowerVermaIdeal n μ` (generated by the `E_pq`, `q < p`, and the `E_pp - μ_p`).

The main theorem `mem_kerSpan` says the following. Let `T ≠ 0` be a vector with
`ℓ(Y) T = r(Yᵀ) T`, where `ℓ` and `r` are commuting actions. Suppose `ℓ(trU z)` acts on `T` by the
central character of `M⁻(μ)`, and `Re(μ_p) + p` does not depend on `p`. Then the annihilator of
`T` in `U` is the left ideal generated by the `z - χ(z)`, `χ = χ_μ ∘ trU`. (It contains this ideal
by `hcen`; the theorem gives the other inclusion.)

This is internal content of the project. It is used for the spherical tempered core of
`ComplexTemperedStandardIrreducibleStatement`: there `V` is the space of `U(n)`-finite vectors of a
principal series of `GL_n(ℂ)` with all weights `0` and exponents of equal real part, `ℓ` and `r`
are the actions of the two factors of `𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ) × 𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)`, `T` is the `U(n)`-fixed vector and `μ`
is the exponent of evaluation at `1`. The proof combines the Bernstein–Gelfand chase
(Bernstein–Gelfand 1980, proof of Theorem 5.9 in 5.10; here in the variant `wtSpan_ne_top` of
module `BGChaseWeighted`), the simplicity of `M⁻(μ)` (module `LowerVermaSimple`) and Duflo's
theorem in the form `mem_span_of_action_eq_zero` (module `BGChaseKS`).

## Central characters

- `chiV μ : Z →ₐ[ℂ] ℂ`, `z ↦ eval μ (gamC n z)`: the central character of `M⁻(μ)`.
  `chiV_smul_gen`: `z · gen μ = chiV μ z · gen μ` (`eval_gamC_smul_gen`, module
  `LowerVermaCentral`).
- `trC : Z →ₐ[ℂ] Z`: `trU` restricted to the center (`trU_mem_center`). It is multiplicative there
  because `Z` is commutative. `trC_trC`: `trC` is an involution.
- `kerSpan χ`: the left ideal `Ideal.span {z - χ(z) : z ∈ Z}` of `U`. Since its generators are
  central, it is a two-sided ideal.
- `trU_mem_kerSpan`: `y ∈ kerSpan χ ⇒ trU y ∈ kerSpan (χ ∘ trC)`. Proof: by span induction, prove
  `trU y · b ∈ kerSpan (χ ∘ trC)` for all `b`. For a generator `y = z - χ(z)`,
  `trU y = trC z - (χ ∘ trC)(trC z)` is a central generator, so `trU y · b = b · trU y`.

## The transposed Verma ideal

- `trU_mul_mem`: let `S ⊆ U` be a subspace containing `E_pq b` (`p < q`) and `(E_pp - μ_p) b` for
  all `b`. Then `trU v · b ∈ S` for every `v ∈ lowerVermaIdeal n μ` and every `b`. Proof: span
  induction on `v`, generalizing `b`; `trU(E_qp) = E_pq` and
  `trU(x · E) = trU E · trU x`.
- `sub_mem_lowerVermaIdeal`: `z - chiV μ z ∈ lowerVermaIdeal n μ` (`chiV_smul_gen` in the
  quotient).

## The orbit map

- `orbitMap ℓ T : U →ₗ[ℂ] V`, `u ↦ Λ_ℓ(u) T`.
- `env_comm`: `Λ_ℓ(a)` and `Λ_r(c)` commute when `ℓ` and `r` commute (`env_commute` twice).
- `env_left_eq_right`: if `ℓ(Y) T = r(Yᵀ) T` for all `Y`, then `Λ_ℓ(b) T = Λ_r(trU b) T`.
  Induction on `b` (`envInduction`). For a product, `Λ_ℓ(ab) T = Λ_ℓ(a) Λ_r(trU b) T
  = Λ_r(trU b) Λ_ℓ(a) T = Λ_r(trU b) Λ_r(trU a) T = Λ_r(trU(ab)) T`.
- `env_trU_center_of_functional`: let `α : V → ℂ` be linear with `α(T) = 1`,
  `α(ℓ(E_pq) v) = 0` (`p < q`) and `α(ℓ(E_pp) v) = μ_p α(v)`, and suppose every central `z` acts
  on `T` by a scalar (`Λ_ℓ(z) T = c T`). Then `Λ_ℓ(trU z) T = chiV μ z · T` for every central `z`.
  Proof: the kernel `S` of `u ↦ α(Λ_ℓ(u) T)` contains the `E_pq b` and the `(E_pp - μ_p) b`, so by
  `trU_mul_mem` it contains `trU(z - chiV μ z)` (`sub_mem_lowerVermaIdeal`). With
  `Λ_ℓ(trU z) T = c T` and `α(T) = 1`, this gives `c = chiV μ z`.

## The main theorem

`mem_kerSpan ℓ r hcomm T hT0 hκ μ hre hcen hx`. Hypotheses: `ℓ`, `r` are commuting Lie algebra
actions of `𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)` on `V`; `T ≠ 0`; `hκ : ℓ(Y) T = r(Yᵀ) T` for all `Y`;
`hre : Re(μ_p) + p = Re(μ_q) + q` for all `p`, `q`; `hcen : Λ_ℓ(trU z) T = chiV μ z · T` for all
central `z`; `hx : Λ_ℓ(x) T = 0`. Conclusion: `x ∈ kerSpan (chiV μ ∘ trC)`.

Proof.

1. The kernel of `Φ = orbitMap ℓ T` is a two-sided ideal: `Φ(a y) = Λ_ℓ(a) Φ(y)`, and
   `Φ(y b) = Λ_ℓ(y) Λ_r(trU b) T = Λ_r(trU b) Φ(y)` by `env_left_eq_right` and `env_comm`.
2. `V' = range Φ` is stable under `ℓ` and `r` (`r(Y) Φ(u) = Φ(u · ι Yᵀ)`); let `ℓ'`, `r'` be the
   restricted actions (`restrictLie`) and `T' = T ∈ V'`. Apply `wtSpan_ne_top` (module
   `BGChaseWeighted`) to `V'` with `E = ℂ T'`: `E` is stable under `ℓ'(Y) - r'(Yᵀ)` (which kills
   `T'`), has weight `0`, and generates `V'` under `ℓ'`. The weight `μ` is `Z`-extremal by
   `VermaSimple.isZExtremal_of_re`. The center acts on `V'` through `rhoR ℓ'` by `chiV μ`, because
   `Λ_ℓ(trU z) Φ(u) = Φ(u) · chiV μ z` (`trU z` is central, then `hcen`). So
   `wtSpan ℓ' μ ≠ V'`.
3. Let `S = Φ⁻¹(wtSpan ℓ' μ)`. It contains the `E_pq b` (`p < q`) and the `(E_pp - μ_p) b`, so
   `trU v · b ∈ S` for every `v ∈ lowerVermaIdeal n μ` (`trU_mul_mem`).
4. Let `N = {trU y · gen μ : Φ(y) = 0} ⊆ M⁻(μ)`. It is a `U`-submodule, by step 1 (for `a ∈ U`,
   `a · trU y · gen = trU(y · trU a) · gen`). If `gen μ ∈ N`, say `trU y · gen = gen` with
   `Φ(y) = 0`, then `v = 1 - trU y ∈ lowerVermaIdeal n μ`, so for every `b`,
   `Φ(b) = Φ(trU v · b) ∈ wtSpan ℓ' μ` (since `Φ(y b) = 0`), contradicting step 2. So
   `gen μ ∉ N`, and `N = 0` by `VermaSimple.eq_bot_of_gen_not_mem`.
5. For every `u ∈ U`, `(trU x · u) · gen = trU(trU u · x) · gen ∈ N = 0`, since
   `Φ(trU u · x) = 0`. So `trU x` acts by `0` on `M⁻(μ)`. By `mem_span_of_action_eq_zero` (Duflo's
   theorem, with `lowerVermaWeight` and `lowerVermaDuflo`), `trU x ∈ kerSpan (chiV μ)`. Finally
   `trU_mem_kerSpan` and `trU (trU x) = x` give `x ∈ kerSpan (chiV μ ∘ trC)`.

## The statement

`SphericalKernelStatement.{uV} : Prop` (universe `uV` of `V`) quantifies over `n`, a complex vector
space `V`, Lie algebra maps `ℓ r : 𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ) →ₗ⁅ℂ⁆ End_ℂ V` with `ℓ X * r Y = r Y * ℓ X`,
`T : V` with `T ≠ 0` and `ℓ Y T = r Yᵀ T` for all `Y`, `μ : Fin n → ℂ` with
`Re(μ_p) + p = Re(μ_q) + q` for all `p`, `q`, and the hypothesis
`envelopingAction ℓ (trU z) T = chiV μ z • T` for all central `z`. Its conclusion is that every
`x : glU n` with `envelopingAction ℓ x T = 0` lies in `kerSpan ((chiV μ).comp trC)`. These are
exactly the hypotheses and conclusion of `mem_kerSpan`. `sphericalKernel` proves it by applying
`mem_kerSpan`.

Provenance: the statement is internal to the project. It is a step in the project's proof
that the spherical vector of a tempered spherical principal series of `GL_n(ℂ)` is cyclic
(module `SphericalCyclic`). In Duflo's treatment (M. Duflo, *Représentations irréductibles des
groupes semi-simples complexes*, LNM 497 (1975), ch. IV, §1.1, source key
`duflo-1975-representations-irreductibles-complexes`) this cyclicity is a proposition
attributed to Zhelobenko and Kostant. Duflo proves it with his ch. II, §3.4–3.5 and Dixmier's
*Algèbres enveloppantes* 9.6.7. Here the Bernstein–Gelfand chase and the simplicity of `M⁻(μ)`
are used instead.
