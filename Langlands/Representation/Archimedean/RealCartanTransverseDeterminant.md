# The rectangular factors of the transverse real Cartan determinant

The lower off-block coordinates are indexed by ordered pairs of blocks with the row block later than the column block. On each such rectangle, the transverse operator is the identity minus the scaled orthogonal conjugation. The radius factor is the column radius divided by the row radius. This is the operator `X ↦ X − Cᵢ⁻¹ X Cⱼ` with `Cᵢ` equal to the radius times the inverse of the fixed left orthogonal block.

The matrix of the transverse operator in the coordinate basis is block diagonal by these rectangles. Its diagonal blocks, reindexed by row and column indices inside the original blocks, are the matrices of the rectangular endomorphisms. The determinant formula for a block triangular matrix gives the product of rectangular determinants, and absolute value passes through that finite product.

This is the internal factorization connecting the projected compact argument derivative to the normalized off-block determinant calculation.
