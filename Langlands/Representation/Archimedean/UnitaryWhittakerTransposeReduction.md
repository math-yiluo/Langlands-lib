# From transpose symmetry to proportionality

This is the representation-theoretic reduction in Shalika's Theorem 3.1
(1974, printed pp. 183–185). The group is a second-countable real Lie group
with two-sided Haar measure invariant under inversion. The action on the
complex Hilbert space is jointly continuous, unitary and topologically
irreducible. Let `e` be a smooth anti-automorphism with smooth inverse whose
pointwise inverse `θ(x) = (e x)⁻¹` is a Haar-preserving group automorphism.
Conjugate pullback `J(f)(x) = conj(f(θ(x)))` is a semilinear equivalence and
intertwines left translation by `a` with left translation by `θ⁻¹(a)`.

Substitution of `e(x) θ(b)` for the integration variable proves that
pullback by `e` takes the adjoint convolution `f* * g` to `(Jg)* * Jf`.
If the self-coefficient of the nonzero reference functional and the mixed
coefficient with the same regularized reference are invariant under this
pullback, the convolution formula gives the two Hilbert pairing identities.
They are the Hilbert form of the pairing symmetry in Shalika's equation
(3.3), printed p. 184. Conjugating the first kernel in the source's bilinear
pairing yields the adjoint kernel convention in the Lean identity.
The closed invariant graph comparison makes the two regularizations
proportional. Gårding density for the finite word-seminorm bounds then
recovers proportionality of the continuous functionals on all smooth vectors.
