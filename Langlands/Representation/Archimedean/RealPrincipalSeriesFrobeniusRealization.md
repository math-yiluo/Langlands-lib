# The algebraic Frobenius map into the genuine smooth Hilbert carrier

For a nonzero Jacquet functional on an irreducible real GK module, the
Frobenius map into the compact Hilbert principal series is injective. Its
kernel is stable under the given compact action and the genuine Lie
operators on the Frobenius image. Tensor-product induction gives stability
under the full complexified Lie algebra. Evaluation at the identity recovers
the given Jacquet functional, so irreducibility forces the kernel to vanish.

Orthogonal projection onto the invariant Hilbert closure fixes the image of
each orbit. Composing with this bounded projection proves that the Frobenius
vectors are smooth for the restricted action, and the chain rule compares
the genuine Lie derivative on the closure with its ambient counterpart.
The resulting linear map into the word-derivative carrier intertwines the
given compact and real Lie actions. Images of finite-dimensional compact
orbit spans give compact finiteness of every image vector.

This internal construction uses the published scalar Frobenius evaluation,
the genuine Hilbert Frobenius derivative, and the restricted action on the
invariant Hilbert closure.
