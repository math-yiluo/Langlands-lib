# Recovering a closed invariant smooth subspace from its Banach closure

Let a units group act jointly continuously on a complex Banach space, with
the units taken in a finite-dimensional real Banach algebra. Give the genuine
smooth carrier its word-derivative topology. If a closed linear subspace of
that carrier is group invariant, it equals the preimage under the empty word
coordinate of the norm closure of its underlying vectors.

The proof averages against a smooth nonnegative inverse kernel supported
near the identity. For each finite set of word coordinates the compact
integral formula controls the difference between the average and the
original smooth vector. The average is continuous from the Banach space
into the genuine smooth carrier and preserves the closed invariant subspace.
It therefore maps the norm closure of the underlying subspace back into
the smooth subspace. The finite-coordinate approximation puts the original
smooth vector in the closed subspace.

This comparison lets the Banach quotient by the norm closure encode the
kernel of the corresponding map on smooth vectors.
