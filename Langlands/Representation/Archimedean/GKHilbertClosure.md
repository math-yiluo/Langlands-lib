# Hilbert realization of an invariant compact-finite closure

`GKHilbertClosureStatement` starts with a jointly continuous unitary action
on a Hilbert space and a linear embedding of an irreducible admissible
compact-finite module. The original image consists of genuine smooth
vectors, and the embedding respects the included compact group and the
real Lie generators. The norm closure of the image must be invariant
under the whole group.

On this actual closure, the restricted action is jointly continuous,
unitary, topologically irreducible and admissible for the included compact
action. The original embedding into the closure is injective and dense,
and its image is exactly the compact-finite subspace. It retains the
compact action, smoothness, and the real orbit derivatives.

The proof restricts the ambient action and derivatives, applies compact
Hilbert admissibility and recovery to the resulting dense carrier, and
uses the compact-to-Hilbert irreducibility criterion. This internal result
combines the Library's carrier recovery and genuine orbit derivative
interfaces on a prescribed invariant subspace.
