# Real algebraic separation by Hilbert traces

Two jointly continuous Hilbert actions of \(\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb R)\) are
admissible on \(\mathrm O(n)\). Their genuine smooth compact-finite vectors
are identified with two irreducible algebraic modules, with the indicated
compact, derived, and scalar exponential comparisons. The first module
satisfies the real \((\mathfrak g,K)\)-module conditions, and the first
Hilbert action preserves the inner product on \(\mathrm O(n)\). The
group measure is Borel Haar and right invariant, and the compact measure
is Borel Haar and inversion invariant.

Choose a nonzero vector in the first algebraic module. Its compact orbit
span supplies a finite-dimensional matrix type and its Hilbert image
supplies a diagonal coefficient weight. Under the same weight, both
compact averaging operators have finite-dimensional ranges by
admissibility. Each vector in these ranges is compact-finite and genuinely
smooth, hence comes from the corresponding algebraic module under the
identifications. The first average is nonzero and self-adjoint.

The squares of the two averages use a common new weight in the same
matrix type. They factor through the ranges of the original averages,
and the first compressed identity trace is nonzero because it is the
trace of the square of the first average. Equal smooth compact kernel
trace values now yield a linear equivalence intertwining both compact
and complexified Lie actions by algebraic trace separation.

This internal theorem applies the compact compression proof to the
actual real general linear group and its orthogonal subgroup.
