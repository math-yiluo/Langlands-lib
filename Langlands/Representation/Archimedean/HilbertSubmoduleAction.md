# Bounded action on an invariant subspace

`invariantSubmoduleRepresentation` restricts each bounded operator to a
subspace preserved by every group element. Joint continuity and inner
product preservation descend by the subtype topology and inner product.
For a complete subspace, a smooth ambient orbit valued in the subspace is
smooth there by composition with the orthogonal projection. Differentiating
its inclusion identifies the restricted orbit derivative with the ambient
one. The canonical embedding of an algebraic image into its norm closure
has dense range and keeps injectivity when the original map is injective.

These internal lemmas use the Library's genuine smooth-vector definitions
and Mathlib's bounded restriction, projection and manifold derivative APIs.
