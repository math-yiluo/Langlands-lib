# The actual replacement target when the inner weight is zero

This module treats a linked pair at the zero signed inner-weight boundary. Write `L=d.weight.natAbs`, `M=e.weight.natAbs`, `P=outerWeight d e k` and `H=innerWeight d e k`. Under `IsLinkedAt d e k`, `Even (P-M)` and `H=0`, `RealInducedPairZeroInnerMapStatement` asserts the existence of a complex-linear map from the actual compact-finite standard module of `[induced d, induced e]` to that of `replacementPair d e k`. The map intertwines both compact translations and real Lie derivatives exactly, and its kernel is the kernel of the actual `RealInducedPairOuterTail.outerMap`. The theorem `realInducedPairZeroInnerMap` proves this proposition.

The equality `H=0` gives `min L M=k+1`, so both original weights are positive. It also gives `P=L+M`. The canonical parity `Even (P-M)` therefore makes `L` even. The linked endpoint identities say that `first d` and `second e` have the same exponent, namely the center of `innerData d e k`. Their parities are respectively one and zero. Since `innerData` has weight zero, these are exactly the characters in its standard-module expansion.

`kf_reordered` applies the published zero-weight expansion identity after the outer induction block. Thus the actual reordered mixed target `[induced outerData, character (first d), character (second e)]` and the actual two-block `replacementPair` have equal compact-finite function spaces. `replacementEquiv` composes this identification with the published actual interlaced equivalence `RealInducedPairOuterReorder.reorderEquiv`. `replacementEquiv_exact` converts its standard intertwiner identity to exact intertwining modulo the zero subspace.

The additional lemma `replacementMap_comp_swap` composes the published incoming–outer-tail zero identity with the equivalence; it applies to every incoming multiplier datum having the specified final binomial.

This construction uses only published endpoint arithmetic, the actual interlaced equivalence, zero-weight expansion and the outer-tail/incoming-map infrastructure.
