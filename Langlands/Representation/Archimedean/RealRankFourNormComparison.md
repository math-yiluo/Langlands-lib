# A norm comparison for the real rank-four exponent bound

This supporting calculation is derived from the adjoint relations on one
three-dimensional column of trace-free symmetric four-by-four matrices. It
uses Cauchy–Schwarz for a positive Hermitian form. It is part of the local
necessity proof corresponding to Badulescu–Renard 2010, Proposition 9.4,
p.1133, for an inducing pair with equal positive weight.
