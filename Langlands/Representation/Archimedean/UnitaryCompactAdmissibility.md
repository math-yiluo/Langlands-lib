# Admissibility for the standard compact subgroup of matrix units

`UnitaryCompactAdmissibilityStatement` transfers admissibility from the
algebraic action of the matrix unitary group to the compact subgroup in
the smooth representation of the general linear group. This applies to
the real orthogonal and complex unitary groups.

A unitary matrix maps continuously to its corresponding element of the
compact subgroup of units. A matrix representation of that subgroup
restricts along the inclusion. Each intertwiner for the subgroup remains
an intertwiner for the restricted action, with the same underlying
linear map. This embeds the first intertwiner space into the second,
which is finite dimensional by the supplied admissibility.

This internal comparison connects the Library's compact-finite Hilbert
realization criterion with the compact subgroup used by its smooth
Whittaker uniqueness statement.

The supporting restriction lemma applies the stated smooth Whittaker
uniqueness premise and transports its scalar identity to any prescribed
algebraic carrier on which the first restriction is nonzero.
