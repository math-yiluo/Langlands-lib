# Generalized Whittaker coefficient of two regularizations

The coefficient applies the first smooth-vector functional to the smooth
regularization of the second. The second functional satisfies a finite
word-seminorm bound. The action is jointly continuous and unitary and the
Borel Haar measure is invariant under inversion.

The actual Gårding integral represents convolution by composition. When both
functionals satisfy the finite word-seminorm bounds, evaluation on the adjoint
convolution \(f^* * g\) pairs the first vector regularized by \(f\) with the
second vector regularized by \(g\). This is the Hilbert form of Shalika's
unnumbered convolution formula \(B(f,g)=T(\widehat f * g)\) following
Proposition 3.2 (1974, printed p. 184). Shalika regularizes the first
functional with inversion \(\widehat f(x)=f(x^{-1})\). Its representing
Hilbert vector is the Lean regularization on \(\overline f\), since
\((\overline f)^*=\widehat f\). This conjugation accounts for the
conjugate-linear first slot of the Hilbert pairing and the bilinear dual
pairing in the source.

For the left translation law, the first functional has Whittaker
equivariance and Haar measure is also right invariant. For the right
translation law, Whittaker equivariance of the second functional supplies
the conjugate multiplier.
