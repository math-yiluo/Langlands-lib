# A small submodule of the dual standard module from two growth bounds

Lean module: `Langlands.Representation.Archimedean.ComplexLinkedLanglandsQuotientGrowth`
(namespace `Langlands.Archimedean`; helpers in
`Langlands.Archimedean.ComplexLinkedLanglandsQuotientGrowth`).

This module defines two growth statements and proves that together they imply
`ComplexDualSubmoduleAnnihilatorStatement` (module `ComplexLinkedLanglandsQuotientDuality`).
That statement in turn implies the Library's `ComplexLinkedLanglandsQuotientNotLargeStatement`.
The growth statements themselves are not proved here.

Notation: `L = ℂ ⊗[ℝ] Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℂ` is the complexified Lie algebra of `GL_n(ℂ)`,
and `U = U(L)` is its universal enveloping algebra. `I(ds)` is
`kFiniteFunctions (complexStandardModule n ds) (unitaryCompact ℂ n)` with the actions
`complexStandardRep n ds` and `complexStandardLie n ds`. `φ = envelopingAction (complexStandardLie n ds)`.
`degreeFiltration ℂ L d` is the Library's degree filtration of `U`: the span of products of at most
`d` elements `ι x` (module `Langlands.Algebra.UniversalEnvelopingFiltration`).

## Definitions

- `hol X = ½ (1 ⊗ X - i ⊗ iX)` and `antihol X = ½ (1 ⊗ X + i ⊗ iX)` for `X ∈ 𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)`. Under
  `L ≅ 𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ) × 𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)`, `c ⊗ X ↦ (cX, c X̄)`, they are `(X, 0)` and `(0, X̄)`. These are the
  holomorphic and antiholomorphic factors.
- `holAlg n` and `antiholAlg n`: the subalgebras of `U` generated by the `ι (hol X)` and by the
  `ι (antihol X)`.
- `holFiltration n d = (span {ι (hol X)} ⊔ 1)^d`: the elements of degree `≤ d` of the
  holomorphic factor.
- `filtrationSpan π d E = Submodule.map₂ (envelopingAction π) (degreeFiltration ℂ L d) E`: the
  span of the `u · e` with `u` of degree `≤ d` and `e ∈ E`.

## The two statements

```lean
def ComplexStandardHolomorphicGrowthStatement : Prop :=
  ∀ (n : ℕ) (ds : List ComplexCharacterData), ∃ c : ℝ, 0 < c ∧ ∀ d : ℕ,
    c * ((d : ℝ) + 1) ^ (n * (n - 1)) ≤
      (Module.finrank ℂ ((holFiltration n d).map
        (envelopingAction (complexStandardLie n ds)).toLinearMap) : ℝ)
```

For every complex principal series, the elements of degree `≤ d` of the holomorphic factor act
through a space of operators of dimension at least `c (d + 1)^(n (n - 1))`. Mathematically, the
annihilator of a principal series in the holomorphic `U(𝔤𝔩_n)` is the minimal primitive
ideal `U · ker χ`, and `U / U ker χ` grows like the coordinate ring of the nilpotent cone, of
dimension `n² - n` (Kostant). A proof is outlined below; the Library's proof is in
`ComplexStandardHolomorphicGrowthProof`.

```lean
def ComplexLinkedSmallGrowthStatement : Prop :=
  ∀ (n : ℕ) (ds ds' : List ComplexCharacterData), ds.length = n → IsComplexLanglandsOrdered ds →
    ¬ IsComplexUnlinked ds →
    (∀ (i : ℕ) (z : ℂˣ), complexPositionCharacter ds i z * complexPositionCharacter ds' i z = 1) →
      ∃ E : Submodule ℂ (kFiniteFunctions (complexStandardModule n ds') (unitaryCompact ℂ n)),
        E ≠ ⊥ ∧ FiniteDimensional ℂ E ∧
        (∀ (k : Matrix.unitaryGroup (Fin n) ℂ), ∀ e ∈ E, complexStandardRep n ds' k e ∈ E) ∧
        ∃ C : ℝ, ∀ d : ℕ,
          (Module.finrank ℂ (filtrationSpan (complexStandardLie n ds') d E) : ℝ) ≤
            C * ((d : ℝ) + 1) ^ (n * (n - 1) - 1)
```

The hypotheses on `ds` and `ds'` are those of `ComplexDualSubmoduleAnnihilatorStatement`. The
statement asks for a nonzero finite-dimensional `U(n)`-stable subspace `E` of the dual standard
module whose span `U_d · E` grows at most like `(d + 1)^(n (n - 1) - 1)`. Mathematically, the
irreducible submodule of `I(ds')` has Gelfand–Kirillov dimension at most `n (n - 1) - 2` when
two characters are linked. When the linked pair is adjacent and suitably ordered, that
submodule lies in a module induced from a finite-dimensional representation `F_p ⊠ F̄_q` of a
`GL_2(ℂ)` block (Vogan 1978, Lemma 6.5 and Corollary 6.6, p. 94). Its lowest `U(n)`-type
gives `E`.

Provenance: both statements are internal intermediate statements of the proof of
`ComplexLinkedLanglandsQuotientNotLargeStatement`, in the
Gelfand–Kirillov form of Vogan 1978, §6. The growth exponents are written as explicit
inequalities. Gelfand–Kirillov dimension itself is not defined.

## The reduction

`complexDualSubmoduleAnnihilator_of_growth : ComplexStandardHolomorphicGrowthStatement →
ComplexLinkedSmallGrowthStatement → ComplexDualSubmoduleAnnihilatorStatement`.

The proof goes as follows. Let `E` and `C` come from the second statement and `c` from the
first, applied to `ds'`. Put `N = n (n - 1)`. A linked pair forces `n ≥ 2`
(`two_le_of_not_unlinked`), so `N ≥ 1`.

1. **Dimension count** (`exists_kill_of_finrank_lt`). Let `P` be a finite-dimensional space of
   algebra elements, `E` and `W` finite-dimensional, and `P · E ⊆ W`. Assume
   `dim E · dim W < dim φ(P)`. Then some `v ∈ P` kills `E` and acts nonzero. Proof: if every
   `v ∈ P` killing `E` acted by `0`, the kernel of `v ↦ (e ↦ v e) ∈ Hom(E, W)` would lie in the
   kernel of `v ↦ φ(v)`. Rank-nullity would then give `dim φ(P) ≤ dim Hom(E, W)`.
2. **Choice of degree.** Take `d` with `dim E · C < c (d + 1)` and `W = holFiltration n d · E`.
   Since `holFiltration n d ≤ degreeFiltration ℂ L d` (`holFiltration_le_degreeFiltration`),
   `dim W ≤ C (d + 1)^(N-1)`. Hence
   `dim E · dim W ≤ dim E · C (d+1)^(N-1) < c (d+1)^N ≤ dim φ(holFiltration n d)`. Step 1 gives
   `v` of holomorphic degree `≤ d` that kills `E` and acts nonzero on `I(ds')`.
3. **The submodule.** Put `S = U · E = Submodule.map₂ φ ⊤ E`. It contains `E`, so `S ≠ ⊥`, and
   it is `𝔤`-stable.
   - It is `U(n)`-stable. The Library's `IsComplexGKModule` gives
     `ρ(k) π(1 ⊗ X) = π(1 ⊗ k X k*) ρ(k)`. `rep_lie` extends this to all of `L` through `adK k`,
     the base change of `X ↦ k X k*`. `rep_envelopingAction` then yields, for each `u ∈ U`, a
     `u'` with `ρ(k) φ(u) = φ(u') ρ(k)`, by induction on `u`.
4. **The diagonal argument** (`kills_span_of_kills`): `v` kills `S`. The supporting facts:
   - `hom_hol_mul_antihol`: under any Lie algebra map `f` into an associative algebra,
     `f(hol X)` and `f(antihol Y)` commute. It is computed from
     `f(1 ⊗ Z) f(1 ⊗ W) - f(1 ⊗ W) f(1 ⊗ Z) = f(1 ⊗ (ZW - WZ))` (`hom_one_tmul_commutator`).
     The proof works with the base-changed bracket on pure tensors and with the algebra
     identity `comm_aux`. Consequently `holAlg n` and `antiholAlg n` commute (`holAlg_commute`).
   - `M = antiholAlg n · E` contains `E` and is stable under `antiholAlg n`.
   - It is stable under `π(hol X)`. Write `X = κ₁ + i κ₂` with `κ₁, κ₂` skew-Hermitian
     (`exists_skew_decomp`). Then `hol X = hol κ₁ + i hol κ₂` (`hol_add`, `hol_I_smul`) and
     `hol κ = 1 ⊗ κ - antihol κ` (`hol_add_antihol`). Since `E` is `U(n)`-stable, it is stable
     under `π(1 ⊗ κ)` (Library `ComplexJacquetFinite.skew_stable`). So `π(hol X) e ∈ M`.
     Moreover, `π(hol X)` commutes with `antiholAlg n`.
   - Since `1 ⊗ X = hol X + antihol X`, `M` is `π`-stable, hence `U`-stable, and so `S ≤ M`.
   - `v ∈ holFiltration n d ≤ holAlg n` (`holFiltration_le_holAlg`) commutes with
     `antiholAlg n` and kills `E`, so it kills `M`, hence `S`.

So `S` and `v` satisfy `ComplexDualSubmoduleAnnihilatorStatement`.

## Outlined proofs of the two statements

These outlines are not part of this module and are recorded here only as context. The Library
proves both statements: `ComplexStandardHolomorphicGrowthStatement` in
`ComplexStandardHolomorphicGrowthProof`, by a different argument (linear independence of symbol
products, through jets of test functions), and `ComplexLinkedSmallGrowthStatement` along the
second outline (`ComplexLinkedSmallGrowthAdjacent`, `ComplexLinkedSmallGrowthProof`).

- `ComplexStandardHolomorphicGrowthStatement`. The Taylor coefficients at `1` of `U(n)`-finite
  functions separate `U(𝔫̄)`, so evaluation at `1` embeds a Verma module into the dual of
  `I(ds)`. Verma modules have Whittaker functionals, so the Library's Kostant theorem
  (`glProductWhittaker`) shows that the holomorphic annihilator of `I(ds)` lies in `U · ker χ`.
  A symbol and Hilbert-function count for `U / U ker χ` finishes the proof, using the Library's
  charpoly Gröbner data in `Kostant/CharpolyLeading` and `Kostant/CoprimeGroebner`.
- `ComplexLinkedSmallGrowthStatement`. In the adjacent case, use the noncompact picture: right
  derivatives act on restrictions to the lower unipotent group by first-order operators with
  polynomial coefficients. The general case reduces to it by reordering isomorphisms for
  unlinked adjacent characters.
