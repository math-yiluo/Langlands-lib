# Recovery of the compact-finite Frobenius image by compact averaging

The starting data are a real \((\mathfrak g,K)\)-module and a Jacquet
functional satisfying the compact and Lie algebra character equations
for the chosen principal-series parameters. The Hilbert Frobenius closure
is the norm closure of the image of the resulting algebraic map into the
Hilbert principal series. The proof takes compact averages in the ambient
principal series, where each polynomial-weighted average has finite rank.

For an algebraic image vector, every compact average belongs to the image
of its finite-dimensional compact orbit span. For a fixed polynomial
weight, the intersection of the algebraic image with the range of the
average is finite-dimensional and closed. Norm density of the algebraic
image places the average of every vector in the Frobenius closure in that
intersection.

If a vector in the Frobenius closure has finite-dimensional compact orbit
span, each of its polynomial averages also belongs to that orbit span.
The intersection of the orbit span with the algebraic image is closed.
The orthogonal polynomial tests put the entire compact orbit in that
intersection, including its value at the identity.

For the group action restricted to the Frobenius closure, the derivative-smooth
carrier has a compact-finite subspace. The preceding argument shows that
the map from the algebraic module onto this subspace is surjective.
Irreducibility and a nonzero Jacquet functional give injectivity and hence a
complex-linear equivalence.

This internal argument uses the finite-rank Hilbert averages and the
polynomial detection theorem in the preceding modules.
