# Infinitesimal unitarity of consecutive Hermitian factors

Assume each factor in a finite consecutive grouping admits an actual standard equivalence to its Hermitian-dual block list. If the ambient standard module is irreducible and infinitesimally unitary, each factor is infinitesimally unitary.

The induced equivalences compose under concatenation. This gives the required equivalence for the tail of the grouping. The irreducibility-in-stages theorem gives irreducibility of both the first factor and the tail. Apply two-factor unitarity descent to obtain positive forms on both, and use list induction on the tail. The empty grouping has no factors to test.

This is an internal consequence of the Library's two-factor compact unitarity descent and irreducibility in stages.
