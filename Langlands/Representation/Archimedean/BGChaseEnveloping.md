# The enveloping algebra of `𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)` (helpers for the Bernstein–Gelfand chase)

Module: `Langlands.Representation.Archimedean.BGChaseEnveloping`, namespace
`Langlands.Archimedean.BGChase`. `U = U(𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ))` and `ι : 𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ) → U` is the canonical map.
For a representation `σ`, `envelopingAction σ : U → End` is the Library's extension of `σ` to an
algebra map (module `RealGenericLargeReduction`). Everything here is proved.

## Enveloping actions

- `env_intertwine`: a linear map intertwining two representations intertwines their enveloping
  actions;
- `env_commute`: an operator commuting with `σ(Y)` for all `Y` commutes with every
  `envelopingAction σ u`.

Both are proved by induction over `U` (`InfinitesimalCharacter.envInduction` from the Library).

## The adjoint representation

- `adU`: the adjoint representation `Y ↦ [ι Y, -]` of `𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)` on `U`, with `adU_apply`;
- `adU_mul`, `adU_algebraMap`: `ad(Y)` is a derivation that kills scalars;
- `lie_single_diag`, `isWt_adU_single`: `ι(E_ab)` has weight `e_a - e_b`;
- `isWt_adU_mul`, `intSpan_adU_mul_le`: products of weight vectors are weight vectors, so the
  integral span is closed under multiplication;
- `intSpan_adU`: the adjoint representation on `U` has integral weights (`U` is generated by the
  `ι(E_ab)`);
- `adU_mem_degreeFiltration`: `ad(Y)` preserves the Library's degree filtration
  `degreeFiltration ℂ 𝔤𝔩_n d` (by `commutator_degreeOne_mem`).

## The transpose anti-automorphism

- `trLie`, `trOp`, `trU`: the linear map `u ↦ uᵗ` of `U` induced by `Y ↦ ι(Yᵀ)` into the
  opposite algebra;
- `trU_mul` (`(ab)ᵗ = bᵗ aᵗ`), `trU_ι`, `trU_algebraMap`, `trU_one`, and `trU_trU` (involution);
- `trU_mem_center`: the transpose of a central element is central (using
  `Kostant.mem_center_of_commute` from the Library).

## The right action attached to a representation

For a representation `ℓ` on `V`, `rhoR ℓ u = envelopingAction ℓ (uᵗ)`. Then:

- `rhoR_mul`: `ρ(ab) = ρ(b) ρ(a)` (a right action);
- `rhoR_one`, `rhoR_algebraMap`, and `rhoR_ι`: `ρ(ι Y) = ℓ(Yᵀ)`;
- `rhoR_commute`: operators commuting with `ℓ` commute with every `ρ(u)`;
- `exists_rhoR_character`: if `V ≠ 0` and `ℓ` has an infinitesimal character (Library
  `HasInfinitesimalCharacter`), then the center acts through `ρ` by a character
  `χ : Z(U) →ₐ ℂ`. The scalars exist because `zᵗ` is central, and they are multiplicative because
  the center is commutative.
