# Infinitesimal unitarity of the complex standard module

An injective intertwining map pulls a positive invariant form back to the
compact-finite standard module. Lie equivariance gives skewness for each real
matrix direction. The existing irreducibility transfer and permutation theorem
then give an irreducible infinitesimally unitary standard module for every
ordering of a supplied parameter.

The invariant-form condition follows Knapp and Vogan, *Cohomological Induction
and Unitary Representations* (1995), display (0.5), printed page 6 (PDF page 28).
The transport is an algebraic consequence of that condition and the Library's
`HasComplexStandardParameter`.
