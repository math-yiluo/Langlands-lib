# Mixed functionals are determined by the dense original carrier

At a compact open finite level, finite equivariance of the source map
sends each original fixed vector to a mixed vector at that same level.
The two derivative-continuous representatives of the mixed
functionals agree on the original fixed vectors whenever the mixed
functionals agree on the source. Density extends the agreement to
the whole fixed level. Every mixed vector belongs to such a level.

Comparing with the zero functional gives nonvanishing on the original
carrier. Comparing with a scalar multiple transfers proportionality
from that carrier to the entire mixed space. The scalar multiple is
continuous in the same derivative topology at each level.
