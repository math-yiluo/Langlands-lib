# Smoothness of the actual action in the genuine smooth-vector topology

`CurveContDiffOn` expresses continuous iterated differentials on a domain in a topological real vector space. The differential is evaluated on a point and a tangent vector. The correctness clause computes the derivative after any differentiable real curve. In particular, affine curves recover the directional derivatives, and the induction supplies continuous evaluations of the higher derivatives on the iterated tangent domains. Higher directional derivatives in the base point are obtained by holding earlier directions fixed and differentiating the evaluated differential in directions with zero components in those fixed slots. This supplies the continuous iterated directional derivatives used for smoothness on locally convex spaces. The proof establishes the composition and product rules it needs and derives the criterion for ordinary smooth maps on open subsets of normed spaces.

For a jointly continuous bounded-operator representation of the units of a finite-dimensional real Banach algebra, the genuine action in the open units chart has continuous iterated differentials of every order. The first differential at `(g,v)` on `(Y,W)` acts by translating the vector `D_(g⁻¹Y) v + W`, using the genuine Lie derivative. The proof rewrites the differential as the original chart action composed with the map that fixes `g` and replaces `v` by this sum. Inversion and multiplication of chart coordinates are ordinary smooth maps. The genuine direction-variable operator is continuous and real bilinear, so its iterated differentials follow from the bilinear rule. Induction with the composition rule therefore proves the smoothness of the joint action in its published derivative topology.

The proof derives the calculus and the action property from the topological-vector-space derivative and the exact chart action derivative. It applies to the real algebra of matrices over either archimedean field.
