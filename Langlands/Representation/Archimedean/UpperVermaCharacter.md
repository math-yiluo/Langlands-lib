# Upper triangular highest-weight characters

This internal interface twists the Library's lower Verma module by conjugation with the order-reversing permutation matrix. For that matrix `R`, the element `(1 − R)/2` is idempotent, so the published conjugation lemma fixes the enveloping center. The twist exchanges the strictly upper and strictly lower matrix units and reverses the diagonal coordinates.

The lower shifted coordinates for the reversed weight are the upper coordinates `μ + ρ`, in reversed order and translated by the common shift `(n − 1)/2`. The shifted permutation criterion therefore gives the upper triangular character criterion and the comparison with the diagonal exponents of normalized induction.
