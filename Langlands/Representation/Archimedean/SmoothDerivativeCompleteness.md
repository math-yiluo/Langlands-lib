# Completeness of the genuine smooth-vector uniformity

Pulling back the product uniformity along the genuine word-derivative coordinate map gives a uniform additive group structure on `DerivativeSmoothVectors`. Its topology is the existing word-derivative topology.

For a jointly continuous complex normed-space representation of the units of a finite-dimensional real Banach algebra, the coordinate image is closed. In the open units chart, the derivative of the orbit of \(v\) at a unit \(g\) in direction \(Y\) is \(R(g)dR(g^{-1}Y)v\). Expanding \(g^{-1}Y\) in a finite real basis expresses the orbit derivative as a continuous function of the chart point and the tuple of derivatives at the identity in the basis directions. This continuity gives local uniform convergence in operator norm for the first derivatives of each word orbit when all word coordinates converge. The uniform limit theorem gives the derivative of each limit orbit. Induction on finite differentiability order makes all limit orbits smooth. At the identity the derivative formula identifies the limit for a word with the derivative of the limit for its tail. Hence all coordinates come from the smooth vector at the empty word.

If the representation space is Banach, the coordinate product is complete. The closed image then gives completeness for the pulled-back uniformity.

This is an internal consequence of the published word topology, the units chart, and the uniform limit theorem for derivatives.
