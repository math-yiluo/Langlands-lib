# The trace-free Casimir on two real induction blocks

The first and second traces of the matrix of enveloping-algebra generators
act by scalars by the published infinitesimal character theorem. To determine
the scalars, evaluate at the identity on a vector with nonzero value there.
The normalized principal-series equivariance makes an upper triangular
matrix \(X\) act on evaluation by \(\sum_i \delta_i X_{ii}\), where
\(\delta_i\) is the inducing exponent at position \(i\) plus \(3/2-i\) for
indices starting at zero.

In the quadratic trace, a term with a strictly upper triangular matrix
unit on the left contributes zero. Its reverse contributes
\(\delta_j-\delta_i\) for \(i<j\) by the matrix-unit commutator.
The diagonal terms contribute \(\delta_i^2\). Subtracting the square
of the action of half the identity leaves
\(\lvert\mathrm{weight}\rvert^2+4a^2-5\) for blocks of equal absolute
weight with exponents \(u+a,u-a\).

This calculation supplies the Casimir scalar for the local necessity
argument corresponding to Badulescu and Renard (2010), Proposition 9.4,
p. 1133. It is derived from the indicated equivariance and matrix
commutators.
