# Averaging continuous operator actions and testing with orthogonal polynomials

A jointly continuous family of bounded complex-linear operators on a
complex Banach space can be averaged against a continuous scalar weight and a
finite Borel measure on a second-countable compact space. The resulting
operator is complex linear by the laws of the Bochner integral. For continuity, the
weighted orbit is continuous into the supremum-norm space of continuous
functions, which maps boundedly into \(L^1\). With a probability measure,
averages preserve a closed submodule whenever
the orbit being integrated lies in that submodule.

For a continuous orthogonal-group profile in a complete normed complex
space, if every polynomial-weighted Haar integral belongs to a fixed closed
submodule, then every value of the profile belongs to that submodule. A
continuous complex-linear functional separating an exterior point can be
chosen to vanish on the submodule by Hahn–Banach and scalar multiplication.
Applying it to the profile gives a scalar function whose integrals against
all entry polynomials vanish. The published polynomial separation theorem
forces the scalar function to vanish.

This internal construction combines Bochner integration on the compact
parameter space with the Library's polynomial tests on the orthogonal group.
