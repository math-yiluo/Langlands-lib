# Norm closures of derivative-stable submodules are group invariant

This internal theorem combines [MixedOrbitCoefficient](MixedOrbitCoefficient.md) with the published Hahn–Banach separation `exists_functional_vanishing_on_closedSubmodule` of [OrthogonalGroupAveraging](OrthogonalGroupAveraging.md). It is the closure step of the topological-to-algebraic irreducibility comparison. Declarations are in `Langlands.Archimedean.MixedOrbit`, with the setting of `MixedOrbitCoefficient`. The algebra equivalence `ψ : ArchimedeanMatrix K n ≃ₐ[ℝ] A` and the representation `R` of `Aˣ` are as there, and `Kc` is a subgroup of `Aˣ`.

- `unitsMap_surjective`: the units map induced by `ψ` is surjective, since `ψ⁻¹` induces a section.
- `representation_mem_closure_of_stable`: let `R` be jointly continuous and `W` a submodule of the genuine smooth carrier `D` with three properties. It is stable under the genuine derivatives `dR(ψ X)` for every real direction `X`, and under `R∞(k)` for `k ∈ Kc`. Every vector of `W` is annihilated by a monic polynomial in `transportedCartanSquareSum`. Suppose also that every connected component of the completion-based group contains a point mapped into `Kc`. Then for every unit `g` and every `w ∈ W`, `R(g)(ε w)` lies in the norm closure of the image of `W` under the empty-word map `ε`.

Proof: if not, Hahn–Banach gives a continuous functional `L` vanishing on that closure but not at `R(g)(ε w)`. Choose a point `x` over `g` and a point `x₀` of its component lying over `k ∈ Kc`. Every coefficient of `W` for `L` vanishes at `x₀`, since `R(k)(ε w') = ε(R∞(k)w')` with `R∞(k)w' ∈ W`. The published-route theorem `orbitCoefficient_eq_zero_of_stable` then makes the coefficient of `w` vanish at `x`, which is `L(R(g)(ε w)) = 0`, a contradiction.
