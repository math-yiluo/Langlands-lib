# Two reduced words for the four-character reversal

This internal consequence of `RealAmbientCanonicalCharacterBraid` and
`RealBlockFrobeniusDisjoint` works on the compact-finite functions of the actual
four-character standard modules of \(\mathrm{GL}_4(\mathbb R)\). For each pair in
\([x,y,u,v]\) choose a canonical crossing multiplier. Its parameter may be
chosen independently of the actual characters' exponents. The six parameters
satisfy the additive relation on each of the four ordered triples. Each pair's
crossing is used at the position specified by the word.

With axes numbered from zero and crossings read chronologically, the proof
compares the words by

```
102102 → 120102 → 121012 → 212012 → 210212 → 210121 → 201021 → 021021
```

Both words send \([x,y,u,v]\) to \([v,u,y,x]\). The adjacent replacements are
the published ambient rank-three braid with the fourth character in the prefix
or suffix. The disjoint replacements use the published commuting Frobenius-map
square. As in the ambient braid, the compact polynomial braid for all indices
is a premise. The negative-inner filtration argument uses the first word for
analytic specialization and the disjoint initial crossings of the second word
for vanishing.
