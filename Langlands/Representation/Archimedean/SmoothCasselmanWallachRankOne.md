# The Casselman–Wallach bound for `GL₁(ℝ)`

`realSmoothCasselmanWallachRankOne` proves the bound with the empty word
for an irreducible admissible source module \(V\). The source representation
of `GL₁(ℝ)` acts jointly continuously by bounded complex-linear maps on a
complex normed space. The equivalence from \(V\) onto all genuine smooth
compact-finite vectors preserves the `O(1)` action and the genuine Lie
derivatives.

Joint continuity and these intertwining identities give the compatibility
required by `realPrincipalSeriesQuotient`. With irreducibility and
admissibility, this result gives a nonzero intertwiner into \(V\) from the
principal series of one real character. Irreducibility makes the map
surjective. Evaluation at the identity identifies the principal series
with `ℂ`, so \(V\) is finite dimensional. The finite-dimensional estimate
applies to the injective composition of the source equivalence and the
inclusion of the genuine smooth carrier.

The algebraic carrier and the two normed carriers have independent universes.
The rank-one argument allows any linear map into the target smooth carrier.
It uses the internal finite-dimensional norm comparison and the rank-one
principal-series and quotient results of the Library.
