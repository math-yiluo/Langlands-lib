# Induction on the universal enveloping algebra

For a complex Lie algebra `L`, `Langlands.Archimedean.InfinitesimalCharacter.envInduction` proves a predicate on `U(L)` from its values on scalars and Lie generators and its closure under products and sums. These hypotheses define a subalgebra. The universal property of `U(L)` identifies the composite of the induced homomorphism into that subalgebra and its inclusion with the identity.
