# Compact factor of the complex rank-one chart

The bottom row of the `GL₂(ℂ)` chart is `[1,z]`, and its determinant is −1. The `GLTwoKFinite` bottom-row decomposition compares the chart with a diagonal matrix times the determinant-one unitary matrix with normalized bottom row. The diagonal character contributes the common phase `(-1)^m` from the first character and the radial factor `(1 + |z|²)^(-D-1)`, where `D` is the difference of inducing exponents. Restricting a right translate to the embedded `GL₂(ℂ)` block gives the corresponding compact evaluation in `GLₙ(ℂ)`. The exponent shifts on the two restricted characters cancel in `D`.

This internal calculation specializes the rank-one intertwining integrals used by Vogan, *The unitary dual of GL(n) over an archimedean field* (1986), Theorem 11.5(f)–(h), p. 479, to the Library's chart and normalization.
