# The inverse-adjoint action of a Hilbert representation

The Hilbert contragredient is the action `g ↦ R(g⁻¹)†` on a complete complex inner product space. The adjoint reverses products, so applying it after inversion gives a representation by bounded complex linear operators. The inner product formula identifies it through the conjugate-linear Riesz correspondence with inverse precomposition on the complex continuous dual. The construction is involutive and the norm of each contragredient operator is the norm of the original inverse operator.

Topological irreducibility transfers because the orthogonal complement of a closed subspace invariant for the inverse-adjoint action is invariant for the original action. Taking its orthogonal complement again recovers the closed subspace. This internal construction uses the Hilbert adjoint and the original action, and supplies the normed dual model for the smooth contragredient comparison.
