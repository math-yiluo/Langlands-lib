# Angular Haar measure for the compact block factors

The probability Haar measure restricted to the determinant-one component of an orthogonal line is half the point mass at the identity. For an orthogonal plane it is the pushforward of angular Lebesgue measure on the interval from minus pi to pi, with density one over four pi.

The proof applies the rank-one and rank-two Haar integration formulas in `RealSmallOrthogonalHaar` to continuous functions multiplied by the indicator of the clopen determinant-one component. The reflections have determinant minus one. The angle integral is periodic and can be centered at zero. Agreement on continuous tests identifies the finite Borel measures.
