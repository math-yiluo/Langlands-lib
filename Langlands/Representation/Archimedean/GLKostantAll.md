# Kostant's multiplicity theorem for `𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)` at every central character

Module `Langlands.Representation.Archimedean.GLKostantAll`, namespace
`Langlands.Archimedean.GLReynolds`. It imports `GLSymbolTransport` (and through it the whole
`GLReynolds` … `GLKostantOne` development and the Library Kostant modules).

Main theorem:

```lean
theorem kostantMultiplicity_of_central (hC : VermaGL.CentralCharacterStatement) :
    VermaGL.KostantMultiplicityStatement.{uW}
```

It proves the Library statement `VermaGL.KostantMultiplicityStatement` (module `LowerVerma`)
from the Library statement `VermaGL.CentralCharacterStatement` (every character of the center is
the central character of some lower Verma module `M⁻(λ)`). Combined with a proof of
`VermaGL.CentralCharacterStatement`, it gives Kostant's multiplicity bound outright. No new
axioms are used (the checked axioms are `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`).

Notation: `U = U(𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ))`, `U_N` its degree filtration, `σ` a representation of `𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)` on
`W`, `W₀` its zero weight space, `d = dim W₀`, and `K_χ = cenIdeal χ` the left ideal generated by
the `z - χ(z)`.

## Results

### Covariants

* `IsCov σ f`: `f(σ(Y) w) = ι(Y) f(w) - f(w) ι(Y)` for all `Y`, `w`.
* `isCov_of_single`: covariance under the matrix units implies covariance
  (`Matrix.induction_on'`).
* `Cov σ N`: the subspace of covariants with values in `U_N`; `mem_Cov`.
* `exists_degree_bound`: a linear map from a finite-dimensional space to `U` has values in some
  `U_N` (bound the images of a basis).

### Lifting covariants modulo `K_χ`

* `adU_mem_cenIdeal`: `K_χ` is stable under `x ↦ ι(Y) x - x ι(Y)` (generators commute with
  `ι(Y)`; induction over the span).
* `exists_cov_lift`: let `W` be finite-dimensional with integral weights and `f` a covariant
  whose values lie in `U_M + K_χ`. Then there is `g ∈ Cov σ M` with `f(w) - g(w) ∈ K_χ` for all
  `w`.
  Proof: let `S = U_M` with the adjoint representation `τ` (`BGChase.restrictLie`,
  `BGChase.adU_mem_degreeFiltration`); `Hom(W, S)` has integral weights
  (`BGChase.intSpan_homLie`). Choose a linear `x₀ : W → S` with `f - x₀` valued in `K_χ`. The
  class of `x₀` in `Hom(W, S/(S ∩ K_χ))` is invariant (using covariance of `f` and
  `adU_mem_cenIdeal`), so by the invariant lifting theorem `exists_invariant_lift`
  (`GLReynolds`) it is the class of an invariant `x : W → S`; invariance of `x` is the
  covariance of `g = x`, and `f - g` has values in `K_χ`.

### Symbols at the Whittaker point

* `evPsi n`: evaluation of polynomials at `ψ = psiPt n` (`GLSymbolTransport`).
* `tauMap N f = (w ↦ σ_N(f(w))(ψ))`, a linear map from `Hom(W, U)` to the dual of `W`
  (`σ_N = symb n N`).
* `topSpace σ N = τ_N(Cov σ N)`, `topRank σ N` its dimension, `topBasis σ N` a basis, and
  `topCov σ N i ∈ Cov σ N` chosen covariants with `τ_N(topCov σ N i) = topBasis σ N i`.
* `CovIdx σ N_max = Σ_{N ≤ N_max} Fin (topRank σ N)` and `allCov σ N_max (N, i) = topCov σ N i`
  (the covariants `g_{N,i}`); `allCov_mem`.
* `exists_sub_tau_zero`: every `f ∈ Cov σ N` differs from a combination of the `g_{N,i}` by an
  element of `Cov σ N` with `τ_N = 0`.
* `eq_zero_of_tau_zero`: on `Cov σ 0` (scalar values), `τ_0 f = 0` forces `f = 0`.
* `exists_lower_of_tau_zero` (**reduction of degree**): if `f ∈ Cov σ (M+1)` and
  `τ_{M+1}(f) = 0`, then every value `f(w)` lies in `U_M + K_χ`, for every `χ`.
  Proof: the space `{σ_{M+1}(f(v)) : v ∈ W}` is stable under the coadjoint derivations of the
  off-diagonal matrix units (`coadDer_symb` with covariance of `f`) and vanishes at `ψ`. By the
  Library orbit theorem `Kostant.orbitVanishing` (applied with the regular nilpotent tuple of
  ones) its elements vanish on the nilpotent cone, and by `Kostant.nilpotentConeIdeal` they lie
  in `Kostant.charpolyIdeal`. Now apply `exists_sub_mem_of_symb` (`GLSymbolTransport`).

### Spanning and independence

* `exists_mem_span` (**spanning**, every `χ`): for `N ≤ N_max`, every covariant with values in
  `U_N + K_χ` is congruent modulo `K_χ` to an element of the span of the `g_{N',i}`,
  `N' ≤ N_max`. Induction on `N`: lift to `Cov σ N` (`exists_cov_lift`), subtract a combination
  of the `g_{N,i}` (`exists_sub_tau_zero`), and either conclude (`N = 0`,
  `eq_zero_of_tau_zero`) or descend to `U_{N-1} + K_χ` (`exists_lower_of_tau_zero`).
* `allCov_indep` (**independence**): if the center acts on the generator of `M⁻(λ)` through `χ`,
  and `Σ_k c_k g_k(w) ∈ K_χ` for all `w`, then `c = 0`.
  Proof by descending induction on the degree: let `N` be the largest degree with a nonzero
  coefficient. The combination lies in `U_N` and acts by zero on `M⁻(λ)`
  (`smul_eq_zero_of_mem_cenIdeal`), so its degree-`N` symbol vanishes at `ψ`
  (`eval_symb_eq_zero`, Matumoto's lemma). The `g_{N',i}` with `N' < N` contribute nothing to
  the degree-`N` symbol (`symb_eq_zero_of_mem`), so `Σ_i c_{N,i} topBasis σ N i = 0` and the
  `c_{N,i}` vanish.

### Kostant's multiplicity theorem

* `kostantMultiplicity_of_central`: let `σ` be finite-dimensional with integral weights and `χ`
  any character of the center. By `exists_kostantOne` (`GLKostantOne`) there are a character
  `χ₀` and covariants `F_1, …, F_d` independent modulo `K_{χ₀}`. Take `N_max` bounding the
  degrees of all `F_i` (`exists_degree_bound`). By spanning, each `F_i` is congruent modulo
  `K_{χ₀}` to a combination `h_i` of the `g_k`. If `|CovIdx σ N_max| < d`, the coefficient
  vectors of the `h_i` are dependent, which gives a nontrivial combination of the `F_i` in
  `K_{χ₀}`, contradicting `exists_kostantOne`. Hence there is an embedding
  `e : Fin d ↪ CovIdx σ N_max`. The covariants `g_{e(i)}` satisfy the conclusion at `χ`:
  `hC` gives `λ` with central character `χ` on `M⁻(λ)`, and `allCov_indep` gives the
  independence modulo `K_χ`.

## Provenance and route

The Library statement is Kostant's theorem that the multiplicity of `W` in `U/U·ker χ` is
`dim W₀` (B. Kostant, *Lie group representations on polynomial rings*, Amer. J. Math. 85 (1963),
327–404; stated as Bernstein–Gelfand 1980, 1.7, printed p.253), in the form of a lower bound by
covariants `W → U`. The formulation was checked against Bernstein–Gelfand 1.7; Kostant's 1963
paper was not available in the project and was not consulted. The formal route differs from
Kostant's harmonic-polynomial argument: a lower bound at one regular central character comes
from explicit lowest weight vectors (`GLKostantOne`), and the passage to every central character
uses only Matumoto's lemma (Whittaker functional on `M⁻(λ)`), Kostant's orbit and nilpotent cone
results from the Library, and the filtration reduction of `GLSymbolTransport`. The resulting
number of covariants `g_{N,i}` does not depend on `χ`.
