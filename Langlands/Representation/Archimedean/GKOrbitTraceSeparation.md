# Group orbit coefficient separation for algebraic archimedean modules

`orbitCoefficientProfile` associates a function on actual GL to a finite family of input vectors and a matching family of bounded linear functionals. The vectors enter by a linear map to the genuine smooth carrier, followed by its empty word coordinate. Applying the group operators and summing the evaluations gives the profile.

The separation theorem starts with two irreducible algebraic modules and linear maps comparing their compact and real Lie actions with the genuine smooth actions. It also uses the scalar derivative comparison for the actual exponential in each smooth carrier. If the two profiles agree on the group and have nonzero value at the identity, compact translation preserves their equality. Differentiating each translated profile along a real exponential preserves equality for the real Lie generators. Complex linearity and tensor induction handle every complexified Lie generator. An induction in the common free algebra then preserves equality after applying each polynomial in the generators.

At the identity, the profile after application of an algebra element is its finite compressed trace. This follows by writing the trace in the standard basis, inserting the given vector family and projecting by the given functionals. The compressed trace separation theorem produces a complex linear equivalence intertwining both actions. This is an internal comparison between group coefficient data and algebraic modules. Its hypotheses specify the coefficient equality and the comparisons to the genuine smooth actions.
