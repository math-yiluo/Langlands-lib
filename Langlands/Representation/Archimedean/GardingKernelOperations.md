# Operations on smooth compact kernels

Pullback by a diffeomorphism and its inverse gives an equivalence on kernels.
Pullback along group translations gives the two kernel translations. Conjugate inversion
interchanges the two translation conventions and defines a conjugate-linear
involution. The convolution of two smooth compact kernels is smooth by the
compact-kernel smoothing theorem. Its support lies in the product of the two
compact supports. Left invariance of the measure gives the reversed product
formula for conjugate inversion.

These are internal test-space operations for the regularization argument of
Shalika (1974), §3.
