# Scalar coefficients of Banach smooth-vector actions

`SmoothScalarCoefficientStatement` starts with a jointly continuous representation of the units of a finite-dimensional real Banach algebra on a complex normed space. For a genuine smooth vector and a continuous complex-linear functional in the word-derivative topology, the scalar orbit coefficient is smooth. The derivative at zero of its restriction to every right exponential line is the coefficient of the genuine derived vector at the base group element.

A continuous scalar form factors through finitely many word coordinates, and each such coordinate is a smooth Banach-valued orbit map. Composing their product with the bounded scalar form proves smoothness. The units-chart derivative gives the derivative in the genuine word topology along an exponential line. Applying the scalar form to the slope limit gives the claimed derivative.

This is an internal smooth-orbit calculation used for scalar coefficients in the tensor-product extension in [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*, §12.3, pp. 89–90](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf#page=89).
