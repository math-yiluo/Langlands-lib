# Bounds for real principal-series translation in the compact Hilbert norm

Use the smooth principal series parameterized by full diagonal exponents
and sign parities as in `RealJacquetFrobenius`. `restrictToL2` restricts its
functions to the orthogonal group and takes their classes in \(L^2\) for
normalized Haar measure. The map is injective by full support of Haar
measure and the Iwasawa decomposition. Its squared norm is the integral
over the compact group of the squared absolute value.

The proof of the translation bound uses a spherical weight constructed by
the existing Frobenius function with the trivial compact action and zero
parities. Its exponent in position \(i\) is half the modulus exponent
minus the real part of the principal series exponent. The weight is
smooth, nonzero, and one on the compact group. The squared absolute value
of the weighted function transforms by the modulus under positive
diagonal and upper unipotent matrices. The published compact integral
invariance thus identifies its integral after right translation with the
original squared norm. For right translations in a given compact set,
the inverse absolute value of the weight on the product with the
orthogonal group is bounded. Integrating the corresponding pointwise
inequality gives a common norm bound.

This is an internal consequence of the published real principal-series,
Frobenius, and compact integral invariance constructions. It provides
the bounded operators on the dense smooth domain used for Hilbert
completion.
