# Compressed trace separation for algebraic modules

The common free algebra has one generator for each compact group element and one for each element of the complex Lie algebra. The corresponding compact and Lie operators define each module's action. Any submodule of the free algebra action, viewed over the complex scalars, is stable under both families of operators. Consequently irreducibility of the algebraic representation gives simplicity over the free algebra.

`exists_gkEquiv_of_compressedTrace_eq` compares the finite-dimensional traces of these two free algebra actions after linear insertion and projection. The first compression must have nonzero trace on the identity. The simple-module trace comparison then produces an equivariant complex linear equivalence, and evaluation on the two families of generators gives compact and Lie equivariance. The proof is internal to the Library. Its trace equality hypothesis is the analytic input for applications to archimedean characters.
