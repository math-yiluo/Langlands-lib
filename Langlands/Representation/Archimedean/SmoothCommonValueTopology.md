# The word topology on a common space of smooth Banach vectors

Let two jointly continuous actions of the units of a finite-dimensional
real algebra act on the same complex Banach space. If their smooth
subspaces agree as subspaces of the Banach space,
`SmoothCommonValueTopologyStatement` identifies their genuine
word-derivative carriers by a continuous linear equivalence that
preserves the underlying vectors.

Use the linear identification of the two smooth subspaces. Its graph
is the kernel of the difference of the two continuous empty word maps
on the product of the derivative carriers. This closed graph is
complete in the inherited topology. Its coordinate projections onto
the two Fréchet carriers are continuous and surjective, hence open.
They make the value-preserving identification continuous in both directions.
