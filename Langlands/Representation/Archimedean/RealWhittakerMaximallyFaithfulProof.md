# Proof of Kostant's theorem for `𝔤𝔩_n` (`RealWhittakerMaximallyFaithfulStatement`)

Module: `Langlands.Representation.Archimedean.RealWhittakerMaximallyFaithfulProof` (namespace
`Langlands.Archimedean.Kostant`).

Main theorems:

* `Langlands.Archimedean.Kostant.realWhittakerMaximallyFaithful :
  Langlands.Archimedean.RealWhittakerMaximallyFaithfulStatement.{uV}`
* `Langlands.Archimedean.Kostant.glProductWhittakerMaximallyFaithful :
  Langlands.Archimedean.GLProductWhittakerMaximallyFaithfulStatement.{uV}`

## What is proved

`RealWhittakerMaximallyFaithfulStatement` (module `RealGenericLargeReduction`) is Kostant's
theorem (Kostant 1978, Theorem 3.9 = Theorem D) for `𝔤 = ℂ ⊗[ℝ] 𝔤𝔩_n(ℝ) ≅ 𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)`. Let `π` be
a representation of `𝔤` on a complex vector space `V` (no finiteness, irreducibility or
topology is assumed). Suppose `π` has an infinitesimal character (`HasInfinitesimalCharacter`)
and the data of `IsRealGenericGKModule n π`: a nonzero linear functional `ℓ` on `V` and real
numbers `a_p ≠ 0` with `ℓ(π(1 ⊗ X) v) = i ∑_p a_p X_{p,p+1} ℓ(v)` for real strictly upper
triangular `X`. Then `π` is maximally faithful (`IsMaximallyFaithful`): every element of
`U(𝔤)` acting by `0` lies in the left ideal generated by the central elements acting by `0`.

`GLProductWhittakerMaximallyFaithfulStatement` (module `GLProductWhittaker`) is the same theorem
for a finite product `∏ᵢ 𝔤𝔩_{n i}(ℂ)` and an arbitrary nondegenerate character
`η(X) = ∑_{i,p} a_{i,p} X^{(i)}_{p,p+1}` (all `a_{i,p} ≠ 0`). It is proved here as well, since
the complex case `ℂ ⊗[ℝ] 𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ) ≅ 𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ) × 𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)` needs it.

## The proof in this file

Both theorems apply the conditional forms in `Kostant.Main` to the two geometric inputs:

* `nilpotentConeIdeal` (`Kostant.NilpotentCone`): a polynomial on `∏ᵢ 𝔤𝔩_{n i}(ℂ)` that vanishes
  on all tuples of nilpotent matrices lies in the ideal generated by the non-leading
  coefficients of the characteristic polynomials of the generic blocks.
* `orbitVanishing` (`Kostant.OrbitVanishing`): a space of polynomials stable under the coadjoint
  derivations of the off-diagonal matrix units, all vanishing at one regular nilpotent tuple
  `ψ` with nonzero superdiagonal, vanishes at every tuple of nilpotent matrices.

`glProductWhittaker_of` applies `maximallyFaithful_of` (`Kostant.Assembly`) blockwise.
`realWhittaker_of` uses the one-block product: it transports `π` along the Lie algebra
isomorphism `toGL n : ℂ ⊗[ℝ] 𝔤𝔩_n(ℝ) ≃ 𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)` (`Kostant.RealTransfer`). The functional `ℓ`
becomes a Whittaker functional with coefficients `i a_p ≠ 0`, and the infinitesimal character and
maximal faithfulness are transported with `Kostant.Transport`.

## Overall route (helper modules under `Kostant/`)

The proof follows Matumoto (Invent. Math. 89 (1987), Theorem 1 and §2). It needs neither Verma
modules nor the Harish-Chandra isomorphism, and only the spanning half of
Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt.

1. **Symbols** (`Symbol`, `OrderedMonomials`, `AssocSymbol`). A degree-`N` symbol
   `σ_N : U_N → ℂ[X_c]` is built from a representation of `𝔤` by derivations on a polynomial
   ring. It is multiplicative and kills `U_{N-1}`. It turns commutators with `x ∈ 𝔤` into the
   coadjoint derivation `coadDer x` (`Coadjoint`). Its kernel on `U_N` is `U_{N-1}`. The
   ordered monomials `E^α` span `U_N`, with `σ_N(E^α) = X^α`.
2. **Central elements** (`Newton`, `Center`). The Gelfand elements `tr(E^k)` are central. By
   Newton's identities for matrices (Faddeev–LeVerrier), suitable combinations `Z_{i,k}` are
   central and have the characteristic polynomial coefficients of the generic blocks as symbols.
3. **Matumoto's lemma** (`Matumoto`). If `P ∈ U_N` acts by `0` and `ℓ ≠ 0` is a Whittaker
   functional, then `σ_N(P)` vanishes at the Whittaker point `ψ`. The proof grades by
   `ad h`, projects to the top degree by Lagrange interpolation, and evaluates words.
4. **Orbit** (`NilpotentConjugacy`, `OrbitVanishing`). The symbols of annihilating elements form
   a space that is stable under `coadDer` and vanishes at `ψ`. So they vanish on every
   nilpotent tuple. The proof uses transvection flows, generation of `GL_n` by transvections
   and diagonals, conjugacy of regular strictly upper triangular matrices, density, and
   triangularization of nilpotent matrices.
5. **Nilpotent cone** (`WeightLex`, `CharpolyOrder`, `CharpolyLeading`, `CoprimeGroebner`,
   `NilpotentCone`). For a monomial order weighted by column index and refined
   lexicographically (`ord n`), the coefficient of `t^k` of the characteristic polynomial of
   block `b` has as leading monomial the squarefree antidiagonal of the trailing
   `(n b - k) × (n b - k)` block, with leading coefficient `±1` (`CharpolyLeading`). After
   normalising, these generators are monic with pairwise disjoint squarefree leading monomials.
   By Buchberger's first criterion they form a Gröbner basis, and the squarefree initial ideal
   makes `charpolyIdeal n` radical (`CoprimeGroebner.isRadical`). Its zero locus is the set of
   nilpotent tuples (Cayley–Hamilton), so the Nullstellensatz identifies it with the ideal of
   the nilpotent cone.
6. **Assembly** (`Assembly`). Induction on `N`: for `P ∈ U_N` acting by `0`, steps 3–5 give
   `σ_N(P) = ∑_j a_j χ_j`. Lifting `a_j` to `A_j` and subtracting `∑_j A_j (Z_j - c_j)` lowers
   the degree, where `c_j` is the scalar of `Z_j`.
