# The invariant Hilbert closure of the real Frobenius image

The closure of the real Jacquet Frobenius image is a complete complex
Hilbert subspace of the completed principal series. The original Frobenius
map has dense range in this subspace.

To prove invariance under a matrix exponential, subtract the orthogonal
projection onto the closure from the Hilbert orbit of an image vector.
This projected difference is analytic in real time. Its first derivative
is the same projected orbit applied to the original Lie derivative of the
algebraic vector. Iteration shows that every derivative at zero vanishes,
as every iterated Lie derivative still belongs to the algebraic module.
The analytic identity theorem gives vanishing at every time.

Compact equivariance gives invariance under the orthogonal group. Closedness
and boundedness extend both invariance assertions from the Frobenius image
to its closure. The published Cartan decomposition then gives invariance
under all of the real general linear group. Restricting the bounded
principal-series operators gives a jointly continuous representation on the
closure.

comparison with the genuine Lie derivative in
