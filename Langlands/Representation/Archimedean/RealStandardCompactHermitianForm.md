# The compact sesquilinear form for a Hermitian-dual intertwiner

Given the split Fourier replacement of a rank-correct standard module and an actual standard equivalence `E` to the Hermitian-dual parameter, inverse pointwise conjugation gives a conjugate-linear map to the dual-block standard module. It retains compact equivariance and commutes with Lie derivatives in real directions. Composing the flipped compact bilinear pairing with this map gives the form

\[
B(v,w)=\int_{O(n)} (S w)(k)\,\overline{(E v)(k)}\,dk,
\]

where `S` is the reverse map of the supplied split replacement. The dual pairing separates the dual-block argument. Since the standard module is nontrivial and both equivalences are injective, the sesquilinear form is nonzero. Compact invariance and real Lie skew-invariance follow from those of the dual pairing.

This is an internal compact-model construction. The supplied Fourier lift makes the exact integrand available for localization in the compact realization.
