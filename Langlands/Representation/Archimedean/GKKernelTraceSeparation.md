# Algebraic separation by smooth kernel traces

`SameSmoothKernelTrace` compares two jointly continuous Hilbert actions of
the same archimedean general linear group. For every smooth compactly
supported complex kernel \(\kappa\), it compares the trace values of the
bounded operators with pointwise formula
\(\int \kappa(g^{-1})R(g)v\,d\mu(g)\) in both actions. The kernel measure is
Borel and finite on compact sets.

For the separation theorem the kernel measure is also right invariant and
positive on nonempty open sets. The two actions have finite-dimensional
factorizations of a common compact parameter average. The parameter space
is compact Hausdorff with a finite Borel measure, and the parameter map and
weight are continuous. The inserted vectors belong to the images of the
given algebraic modules under their smooth embeddings followed by the empty
word coordinate. The first compressed trace at the identity is nonzero.

Choose orthonormal bases of the two finite-dimensional intermediate spaces
and lift their insertions to vectors of the given modules. Combining the
basis functionals with the projections gives bounded functionals in the
ambient Hilbert spaces. The resulting orbit coefficient sums are the two
finite compression trace profiles. The smooth kernel comparison gives
equality of the profiles. Irreducibility, compact equivariance, and the
genuine derived and exponential comparisons then give a linear equivalence
intertwining both algebraic actions.

This is an internal composition of the finite Hilbert compression theorem
and the algebraic orbit coefficient separation theorem.
