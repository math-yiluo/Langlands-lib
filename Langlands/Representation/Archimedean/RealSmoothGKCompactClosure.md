# Compact-finite vectors in the closure of an admissible smooth image

Map an admissible real Harish-Chandra module into a genuine smooth
Banach representation by a compact and Lie intertwiner. Take the
norm closure of its image under the empty word coordinate. A
compact-finite smooth vector belongs to the algebraic image exactly
when its empty word coordinate belongs to that closure.

Identify the matrix orthogonal group with its image in the real
units and transport compact admissibility across this identification.
The image of the empty word map composed with the intertwiner is an
admissible quotient under the compact action. Compact-finite closure
for admissible algebraic subspaces then puts the value of a
compact-finite smooth vector in the algebraic image of the empty word
map. Injectivity of this coordinate recovers the original smooth
vector.
