# Continuity of bounded operators with genuine smooth ranges

Let a jointly continuous representation of the units of a finite-dimensional real Banach algebra act on a complex normed space. A bounded complex linear map from a complex Banach space whose image consists of genuine smooth vectors induces a continuous linear map into the smooth carrier with the genuine word-derivative topology.

For one real Lie direction, take bounded operator difference quotients along the line through the identity in the open units chart at the times `1/(n+1)`. For each input vector these quotients converge in the original norm to the genuine derivative of its image. The uniform boundedness principle therefore gives a common operator bound. Passing to the pointwise limit gives a bounded operator representing that derivative. Iterating this construction gives bounded operators for every word, since genuine differentiation preserves smooth vectors. The word-coordinate topology gives continuity of the lift. Its empty coordinate is the original operator.

This proof is internal to the genuine derivatives, the units chart and the Banach–Steinhaus theorem.
