# Induced operators on pure compact block products

`InductionStages.RealInducedCompactBlockProductStatement` gives the
compact block-diagonal restriction of an induced middle operator on a
standard vector with pure product restriction. The designated factor
comes from a middle standard vector. At each point of the subgroup the
compact-equivariant operator acts on that vector, and the product of
the other factor values supplies the scalar.

The proof first compares the block-diagonal embedding with the
coordinate embedding in the normalized slice. At a block-diagonal
compact element the slice is determined by its values on the middle
compact group. The pure product formula shows that these are the
translated middle factor times the constant product of the other
values. The compact equivariance of the local operator and its
linearity give the formula after evaluation at the identity.

This is an internal consequence of the Library's block embeddings and
normalized induction in stages. The formula gives the value of the
induced vector at each block-diagonal orthogonal matrix. In a compact
Levi integral it supplies the contribution of that vector to the
integrand.
