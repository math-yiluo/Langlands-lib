# Lower coordinates for the Cartan argument differential

The strictly lower entries split into off-block and within-block coordinates. Joining the two coordinate families is a continuous linear equivalence. It preserves Lebesgue measure by the two product-measure formulas for splitting and reindexing coordinates.

Filling the upper entries by skew symmetry identifies these lower coordinates with the real skew matrices. Conjugation by an orthogonal matrix preserves skew symmetry and induces a continuous linear equivalence on the lower coordinates. Its determinant is a continuous multiplicative function on the compact orthogonal group. Boundedness on powers and inverse powers gives absolute determinant one. This removes the orthogonal frame transport from the absolute Jacobian of the compact test argument.

These are internal coordinate facts about the previously computed differential.
