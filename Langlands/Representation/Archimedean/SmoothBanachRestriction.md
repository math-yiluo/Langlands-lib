# Genuine smooth vectors of a closed invariant Banach subspace

For a jointly continuous action of the units of a finite-dimensional
real Banach algebra, a closed invariant complex Banach subspace has
the restricted jointly continuous action. Its genuine smooth carrier
embeds into the original smooth carrier as the closed subspace of
vectors whose empty word coordinates belong to the Banach subspace.
The inclusion intertwines the group and preserves the underlying
Banach value.

The bounded inclusion intertwines the Banach actions and acts
coordinatewise on every word derivative. The closed embeddings into
word coordinates and the closed inclusion of the Banach subspace give
the closed smooth embedding. Conversely an original smooth orbit
whose values lie in the closed invariant subspace is smooth into
that subspace, by the closed-subspace differentiation theorem in the
open units chart. Isometric lifting of the source and target to a
common universe lets this argument keep the original carriers in
independent universes. This gives the asserted range.
