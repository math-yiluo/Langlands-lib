# Separation by the inverse principal series

Let \(W\) be a compact-stable subspace of the compact-finite real
standard module. For each vector outside \(W\), an inverse principal
series vector pairs to zero with \(W\) and pairs nontrivially with
that vector. The pairing is the integral of the product of their
compact restrictions.

In the scalar principal series with the original position characters,
a compact-stable subspace and its algebraic orthogonal complement
sum to the whole compact-finite space. Otherwise their sum would
admit a nonzero orthogonal vector, which would itself belong to the
sum. The component of the given vector in the orthogonal complement
is nonzero and pairs with the given vector by its squared norm.
Its conjugated compact restriction extends to the inverse scalar
principal series and gives the required bilinear functional.
