# Real trace contraction for matrix Lie algebras

The real trace pairing is the real part of the matrix product's
trace. Over the real or complex field, real and imaginary matrix
units pair with transposed real units and negative transposed
imaginary units. The imaginary units vanish over the real field.
The real and imaginary parts of each matrix entry give both
reconstruction identities.

For any real bilinear map out of the matrix space, its contraction
against these two families is invariant under a real linear
equivalence preserving the pairing. Expanding one family by the
first reconstruction identity and moving the equivalence across
the pairing reduces the contraction to the reverse reconstruction.
Matrix conjugation and transpose preserve the real trace pairing.
Swapping the two families also preserves the contraction, by
exchanging the matrix indices and moving the imaginary sign
across the bilinear map.

These identities supply the real and complex trace contraction
used to form the matrix Casimir in Shalika (1974), §2.
