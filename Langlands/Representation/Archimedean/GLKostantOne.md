# Kostant's lower bound at one central character

Module `Langlands.Representation.Archimedean.GLKostantOne`, namespace
`Langlands.Archimedean.GLReynolds`. It imports `GLVermaInject` and the Library module
`BGChaseEnveloping` (the adjoint representation `BGChase.adU` on the degree filtration of
`U(𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ))`, `BGChase.restrictLie`, `BGChase.homLie`, `BGChase.intSpan_homLie`,
`BGChase.exists_wtBasis`).

This module is part of a planned proof of Kostant's multiplicity bound
(`VermaGL.KostantMultiplicityStatement`, Library module `LowerVerma`). It does **not** prove
that statement. Its main theorem `exists_kostantOne` has the same conclusion except that the
character `χ` of the center is existentially quantified (one particular `χ`) rather than
universally quantified. No new axioms are used.

## Results

### The zero weight space

Let `σ` be a representation of `𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)` on `W` and `b` a finite basis of `W` with `b_i` a
weight vector of weight `μ_i` for `famOf σ`.

* `zeroWeightSpace_eq_span`: `VermaGL.zeroWeightSpace σ = ⋂_p ker σ(E_pp)` is the span of the
  `b_k` with `μ_k = 0`. A vector killed by every `σ(E_pp)` has zero coordinate at each `b_i`
  with `μ_i ≠ 0` (`coord_diag`, `GLTensorLowest`).
* `finrank_zeroWeightSpace`: hence `dim W₀` equals the number of indices `k` with `μ_k = 0`.

### Lifting covariants to the enveloping algebra

Let `V` be nonzero and finite-dimensional with an irreducible family `e` satisfying the
relations (hypothesis `hirr`, see `GLBurnside`).

* `exists_degree_surjective` (**Burnside's theorem with a degree bound**): there is `N` such
  that every operator on `V` is `envOf e he u` for some `u` in the degree filtration
  `U_N = degreeFiltration ℂ 𝔤𝔩_n N`. Lift each element of a basis of `End V`
  (`envOf_surjective`) and take the maximum degree.
* `exists_lift_covariant` (**lifting covariants**): let `σ` be a finite-dimensional
  representation with integral weights and `g : W → End V` a linear map with
  `g(σ(E_pq) w) = e_pq g(w) - g(w) e_pq`. Then there is a linear map `f : W → U(𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ))` with
  `f(σ(Y) w) = Y f(w) - f(w) Y` for every matrix `Y` and `w ∈ W`, and with
  `envOf e he (f(w)) = g(w)`.
  Proof: `U_N` is stable under the adjoint action (`BGChase.adU_mem_degreeFiltration`). Let
  `τ` be the restricted representation (`BGChase.restrictLie`), which has integral weights
  (`BGChase.intSpan_restrictLie_of_le`, `BGChase.intSpan_adU`). Then `Hom(W, U_N)` with
  `BGChase.homLie σ τ` has integral weights (`BGChase.intSpan_homLie`). Postcomposition with
  `envOf : U_N → End V` is a surjection `φ : Hom(W, U_N) → Hom(W, End V)` intertwining this
  family with `homFam (famOf σ) (ad e)`. The covariant `g` is an invariant of the target. By
  the invariant lifting theorem `exists_invariant_lift` (`GLReynolds`), it is `φ(x)` for an
  invariant `x`. Invariance of `x` gives the covariance identity on matrix units, and it
  extends to all `Y` by linearity (`Matrix.induction_on'`).
* `envOf_eq_zero_of_mem_span`: the left ideal of `U(𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ))` generated by the elements
  `z - centChar he hirr z` (`z` central) acts by zero on `V`, since each generator acts by
  zero (`envOf_center_eq`).

### The lower bound at one central character

* `exists_kostantOne`: let `σ` be a representation of `𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)` on a finite-dimensional `W`
  with integral weights (`VermaGL.HasIntegralWeights σ`). Then there exist a character
  `χ : VermaGL.center n →ₐ[ℂ] ℂ` and linear maps
  `f_i : W → VermaGL.glU n` (`i < dim W₀`) such that
  - `f_i(σ(Y) w) = ι(Y) f_i(w) - f_i(w) ι(Y)` for all `i`, `Y`, `w`; and
  - if `c` are coefficients with `Σ_i c_i f_i(w)` in the left ideal
    `Ideal.span {z - χ(z) : z central}` for every `w`, then `c = 0`.

  Proof: choose a weight basis `b` of `W` with weights `μ_i` (`BGChase.exists_wtBasis`) and set
  `A = n · Σ_i Σ_p |μ_i(p)|`, so that `(n - 1) Σ_p |μ_i(p)| ≤ A` for each `i`. Let
  `L = LowMod n A` with lowest weight vector `v₀ = lowGen n A` of weight
  `λ = A · (0, 1, …, n-1)` (`GLLowestModule`). `L` is irreducible
  (`eq_top_of_isStable_lowMod`). Take `χ = centChar` of `L`. For each `k` with `μ_k = 0`, the
  vector `ys k = R(c)(b_k^* ⊗ v₀) ∈ Hom(W, L)` is a lowest weight vector of weight `λ`
  (`isLowest_ys`). So there is a covariant `g_k : W → End L` with `g_k(w) v₀ = ys k (w)`
  (`exists_covariant`), and it lifts to a covariant `F_k : W → U(𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ))`
  (`exists_lift_covariant`). Index the `F_k` by `Fin (dim W₀)` using
  `finrank_zeroWeightSpace`. If `Σ_k c_k F_k(w)` lies in the left ideal for every `w`, it acts
  by zero on `L` (`envOf_eq_zero_of_mem_span`). Applying it to `v₀` gives
  `Σ_k c_k ys k = 0`, hence `c = 0` by `ys_eq_zero` (`GLVermaInject`).

The bound is a special case of Kostant's multiplicity theorem. The theorem is historically due to
B. Kostant, *Lie group representations on polynomial rings*, Amer. J. Math. 85 (1963), 327–404,
and is stated as Bernstein–Gelfand 1980, 1.7, printed p.253. The formulation was checked against
Bernstein–Gelfand. Kostant's paper was not available in the project and was not consulted, and
this proof does not follow it. Here the lower bound is obtained at a single, very regular central
character from explicit lowest weight vectors in `Hom(W, L)`. Extending the bound to every
central character is not done in this module (see `GLKostantAll`).
