# Symbols on `U(𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ))` through the one-block product

Module `Langlands.Representation.Archimedean.GLSymbolTransport`, namespace
`Langlands.Archimedean.GLReynolds`. It imports `GLKostantOne`, `LowerVermaDuflo` (the Whittaker
functional `VermaPBW.whitFun` on `M⁻(λ)`) and the Library modules `Kostant.Assembly` and
`Kostant.OrbitVanishing`.

The Library's Kostant development states its symbol calculus for the enveloping algebra of a
product `∏_i 𝔤𝔩_{n_i}(ℂ)` (`Kostant.GLProd`), with the PBW symbol `Kostant.glSymbol`,
Matumoto's lemma `Kostant.eval_symbol_eq_zero`, and the central elements `Kostant.zk` whose
symbols generate the ideal of the nilpotent cone. This module transports these results to
`U(𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)) = VermaGL.glU n` along the Lie algebra isomorphism with the product over the
one-point index type `Unit`. It is internal infrastructure for the proof of Kostant's
multiplicity bound in `GLKostantAll`; it introduces no new statement of the Library and no new
axioms.

## Results

### Enveloping maps and the degree filtration

* `map_mem_degreeOne`, `map_mem_degreeFiltration`: for a Lie algebra homomorphism `φ : A → B`,
  the induced algebra map `map φ : U(A) → U(B)` sends the degree filtration `U_N(A)` into
  `U_N(B)`. Proof by induction on `N`, using `U_{N+1} = U_N · U_1` and that `map φ` sends
  scalars to scalars and `ι(a)` to `ι(φ a)`.

### The one-block product

* `nU n : Unit → ℕ`, the constant block size `n`.
* `VermaPBW.toUnitProd n : 𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ) ≃ ∏_{i : Unit} 𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)`, `X ↦ (fun _ => X)`, the existing Lie algebra
  isomorphism from `LowerVermaDuflo`; `envEquiv n` is the induced isomorphism of enveloping algebras
  (Library `LieEnvelopingEquiv.enveloping`).
* `envEquiv_ι`, `unitEquiv_single` (matrix units go to `Kostant.unit`), `envEquiv_mem`,
  `envEquiv_symm_mem` (both directions preserve the degree filtration).
* `symb n N : U(𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)) → ℂ[X_c]` is the Library degree-`N` symbol composed with `envEquiv n`;
  its variables are the Library coordinates `Kostant.Coord (nU n)`.
* `symb_eq_zero_of_mem`: `symb n N P = 0` if `P ∈ U_M` with `M < N`.
* `symb_zero_algebraMap`: the degree-`0` symbol of a scalar `c` is the constant `C c`.
* `coadDer_symb`: for `P ∈ U_N` and a matrix `Y`, the degree-`N` symbol of the commutator
  `ι(Y) P - P ι(Y)` is the coadjoint derivation `Kostant.coadDer` (for `VermaPBW.toUnitProd n Y`) applied
  to the degree-`N` symbol of `P`. This is the Library `symbol_commutator` rule.

### Matumoto's lemma for lower Verma modules

* `psiPt n`: the point `ψ` with coordinate `1` on the superdiagonal entries `(p, p+1)` and `0`
  elsewhere.
* `eval_symb_eq_zero` (**Matumoto's lemma for `M⁻(λ)`**): if `P ∈ U_N` acts by zero on the lower
  Verma module `M⁻(λ)` (`VermaGL.LowerVerma n lam`), then `symb n N P` vanishes at `ψ`.
  Proof: the Whittaker functional `VermaPBW.whitFun n lam` (from `LowerVermaDuflo`) is nonzero
  (it is `1` on the generator) and is a Whittaker vector for the transported action with the
  character equal to `1` on each superdiagonal matrix unit (`VermaPBW.whitFun_action`). The
  transported element `envEquiv n P` acts by zero
  (`Kostant.envelopingAction_comp_enveloping`, `BGChase.envelopingAction_action`), so the
  Library lemma `Kostant.eval_symbol_eq_zero` applies.

### Reduction modulo the central ideal

* `exists_reduction` (for any product `∏_i 𝔤𝔩_{m_i}(ℂ)`): let `P ∈ U_{M+1}` and suppose its
  degree-`(M+1)` symbol lies in `Kostant.charpolyIdeal m`, the ideal generated by the
  coefficients `Kostant.gens m j` of the characteristic polynomials. Then for any scalars `c_j`
  there are `A_j` with `P - Σ_j A_j (Z_j - c_j) ∈ U_M`, where `Z_j = Kostant.zk` are the Library
  central elements with symbols `Kostant.gens m j` (`Kostant.symbol_zk_gens`).
  Proof: write the symbol as `Σ_j f_j · gens_j` (`Kostant.charpolyIdeal_eq`,
  `Ideal.mem_span_range_iff_exists_fun`), replace `f_j` by its homogeneous component of degree
  `M + 1 - deg_j`, lift these components to `A_j ∈ U_{M+1-deg_j}` with the given symbols
  (`Kostant.exists_symbol_eq`), and compare symbols: `P - Σ_j A_j (Z_j - c_j)` has zero
  degree-`(M+1)` symbol, so it lies in `U_M` (`Kostant.mem_degreeFiltration_of_symbol_eq_zero`).
* `cenIdeal χ`: for a character `χ` of the center `VermaGL.center n`, the left ideal of
  `U(𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ))` generated by the elements `z - χ(z)`, `z` central.
* `exists_sub_mem_of_symb` (**reduction**): if `P ∈ U_{M+1}` and `symb n (M+1) P` lies in
  `Kostant.charpolyIdeal (nU n)`, then for every `χ` there is `R ∈ cenIdeal χ` with
  `P - R ∈ U_M`. Apply `exists_reduction` to `envEquiv n P` with `c_j = χ(Z_j')`, where
  `Z_j' = envEquiv⁻¹(Z_j)` is central (`Kostant.mem_center_enveloping_symm`,
  `Kostant.zk_mem_center`), and transport back.
* `smul_eq_zero_of_mem_cenIdeal`: if the center acts on the generator of `M⁻(λ)` through `χ`,
  every element of `cenIdeal χ` acts by zero on `M⁻(λ)` (induction over the span; a central
  `z` commutes with every `u`, and `M⁻(λ)` is generated by the generator).

## Provenance

The transported results come from the Library Kostant development (modules under
`Langlands.Representation.Archimedean.Kostant`). These are Kostant's theorem on the nilpotent cone
and Matumoto's lemma, with the attributions given in those modules: B. Kostant, *Lie group
representations on polynomial rings*, Amer. J. Math. 85 (1963), 327–404; H. Matumoto, *Whittaker
vectors and associated varieties*, Invent. Math. 89 (1987), 219–224. This module uses only their
Lean statements and consults neither paper. The reduction lemma `exists_reduction` is the
standard filtration argument for `gr(U · ker χ) ⊇ S(𝔤) · S(𝔤)^G_+`, written here directly for
the generators of the Library.
