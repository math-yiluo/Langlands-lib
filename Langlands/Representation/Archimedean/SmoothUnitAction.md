# Joint continuity of the genuine smooth-vector action

`inverseConjugationCLM g` acts on a real normed algebra by `X ↦ g⁻¹ X g`. Its operator norm is bounded by `‖g⁻¹‖ ‖g‖`, with the norms taken in the algebra. For units of a complete algebra, differentiation in the open units chart identifies this map as the tangent of inverse conjugation at the identity. The chain rule for the orbit of a translated smooth vector then proves `D_X R(g)v = R(g) D_(g⁻¹ X g)v`. Iteration gives the corresponding identity for an entire word of Lie derivatives.

For a finite-dimensional real algebra, the genuine derived operator is jointly continuous in the direction and the smooth vector. Expand the direction in a finite real basis and use the continuous basis derivative operators. Given a jointly continuous action on the original complex normed space, word covariance expresses each coordinate of the transported action as the original action applied to a derivative with continuously varying directions. This proves continuity in every word coordinate and hence joint continuity for the genuine word topology. In particular, it applies to the actual transported actions of real and complex general linear groups.

This is an internal consequence of the real units chart, the published smooth-vector action, and the basis expansion for genuine word derivatives.
