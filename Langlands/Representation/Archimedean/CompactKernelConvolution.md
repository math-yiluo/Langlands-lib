# Compactly parametrized kernel convolution

Let the parameter space be compact Hausdorff with a finite Borel measure, a continuous map to the units group of a real Banach algebra, and a continuous complex weight. `compactKernelConvolution` is the scalar Bochner average of the left translates of a kernel by the images of the parameters. The support is contained in the product of the inverse of their compact image with the original support. For a smooth compact kernel, the Library's compact parameter integration theorem gives a smooth kernel with compact support.

Equip the units group with a right-invariant Borel measure finite on compact sets. In a jointly continuous complex Banach representation, applying a smoothing operator after the weighted compact orbit average gives smoothing by the convolved kernel. Bounded linearity commutes with the compact average. Right invariance moves the compact parameters from the group operators into the inverse kernel. The joint integrand is continuous with compact support, so the compact-support Fubini theorem interchanges the two integrals. This internal identity permits the use of a common smooth compact kernel when comparing traces after compact averaging.
