# Shifted Weyl invariance of lower Verma characters

The evaluated Harish–Chandra homomorphism acts on every vector annihilated by the strictly lower matrix units with the specified diagonal weight. Its scalar relation on the universal lower Verma generator lies in the defining left ideal, which also annihilates any such vector.

For an adjacent pair of indices, the powers of the raising matrix unit applied to the generator are nonzero by the published PBW functional. The matrix-unit commutators give their diagonal weights and the lowering coefficients. These identities are internal consequences of the Library's lower Verma construction.

At each nonnegative integer difference of adjacent weights, the appropriate power is a second lower-weight vector. Centrality gives the same scalar on it as on the generator. Specializing one coordinate to a univariate polynomial extends the equality to every weight, since the two evaluations agree at infinitely many points. Adjacent transpositions generate all permutations. In the converse direction, the published separation theorem for the Gelfand elements recovers the shifted orbit from the character.
