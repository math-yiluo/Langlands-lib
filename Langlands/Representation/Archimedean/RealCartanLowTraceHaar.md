# Weighted block Haar integration of the surviving real Cartan term

The Levi embedding sends an internal diagonal reflection to the reflection at its ambient position. It also sends an internal rotation to the ambient rotation at the shifted pair of positions. These identities follow by embedding the matrix units in their blocks.

For a character block the sign filter is the one-dimensional determinant-weight integral. For an induced block the two sign filters followed by the finite low projection give the two-dimensional parity-window integral. The position-character formulas and the embedding identities match the conventions of the individual Haar formulas to those of the original filters.

The grouped block operators compose into a single weighted integral over the product probability Haar measure. Substituting the actual continuous test column gives the double integral with the compact Cartan argument. The principal series radial extension is the original trace weight. This is the internal Haar formula for the surviving low-mode term at the maximal elliptic rank.
