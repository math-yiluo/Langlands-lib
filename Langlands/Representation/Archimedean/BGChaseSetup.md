# The map `E ⊗ U → V` (setup of the Bernstein–Gelfand chase)

Module: `Langlands.Representation.Archimedean.BGChaseSetup`, namespace
`Langlands.Archimedean.BGChase`. `U = U(𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ))`. Everything here is proved.

## Tensor lemmas

- `lid_rTensor_lTensor`: contracting the left factor of `X ⊗ Y` with a functional `α` commutes
  with applying a linear map `φ` to the right factor;
- `eq_sum_basis_tmul`: `t = Σ_i b_i ⊗ (b_i^* ⊗ 1)(t)` for a finite basis `b` of the left factor.

## The data

`BData n V` bundles the hypotheses of `ComplexJacquetBimoduleStatement` that involve no
finiteness: Lie algebra homomorphisms `ℓ`, `r : 𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ) → End V` that commute (`comm`), and a
subspace `E ⊆ V` with `ℓ(Y)e - r(Yᵀ)e ∈ E` for `e ∈ E` (`stable`).

- `kappa`: the representation `κ(Y) = r(Y) - ℓ(Yᵀ)` of `𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)` on `V`. It is a Lie algebra
  homomorphism because `ℓ` and `r` commute and `[X, Y]ᵀ = [Yᵀ, Xᵀ]`. `kappa_mem`: `E` is
  `κ`-stable.
- `kE`: `κ` restricted to `E`. `adP = κ ⊗ ad` on `E ⊗ U` (`adP_tmul`).
- `pmap : E ⊗ U → V`, `e ⊗ u ↦ ρ(u) e`, where `ρ = rhoR ℓ` is the right action
  `u ↦ Λ_ℓ(uᵗ)` of `BGChaseEnveloping`. Then:
  - `pmap_tmul_one`: `p(e ⊗ 1) = e`;
  - `pmap_rmul`: `p` turns right multiplication `rmul u` on the `U` factor into `ρ(u)`;
  - `pmap_adP`: `p` intertwines `adP` and `κ`, since `r` commutes with `ρ`;
  - `ℓ_pmap`: `ℓ(X) p(x) = p(rmul(ι Xᵀ) x)`;
  - `range_pmap`: if `E` generates `V` under `ℓ`, then `p` is surjective;
  - `rmul_mem_ker`, `adP_mem_ker`: the kernel of `p` is stable under right multiplication and
    under `adP`;
  - `pmap_eq_sum`: `p(x) = Σ_i ρ((b_i^* ⊗ 1)(x)) b_i` for a basis `b` of `E`.
- `intSpan_kE`: the integral-weight hypothesis of the bimodule statement (`E` spanned by vectors
  with `ℓ(E_pp)e - r(E_pp)e = m_p e`) says that `κ` on `E` has integral weights (of weight `-m`).
- `rhoR_eq_zero_of_mem_span`: if the center acts through `ρ` by a character `χ`, then `ρ`
  vanishes on the left ideal generated by the `z - χ(z)`.
