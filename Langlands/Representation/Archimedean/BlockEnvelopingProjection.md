# The relative enveloping projection for standard general linear blocks

For a finite product of complex matrix Lie algebras of rank `n` and an ordered `BlockShape n`, `blockMatrixInclusion` inserts the actual block diagonal product at each matrix place. The diagonal element with entry minus the block index gives each matrix unit the weight of its column block minus that of its row block. The positive directions are the strict upper block matrix units. The zero directions are the individual block matrix units, identified with the matrix basis of the actual Levi product.

`blockEnvelopingDecomposition` applies the triangular enveloping decomposition to these bases. It gives the algebra map from the ambient enveloping centre to the Levi enveloping centre and the congruence modulo the right ideal generated by the strict upper block directions.

This is the derived matrix algebra step for restricting infinitesimal centre actions in constant terms, as used in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Lemma 4, printed p. 204.
