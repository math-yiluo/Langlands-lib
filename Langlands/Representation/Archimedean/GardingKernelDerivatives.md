# Genuine derivative seminorm bounds for Gårding averages

The differentiated kernel is the derivative at the identity of its
left translate. It stays smooth by differentiating a jointly smooth
scalar family with one argument fixed at the identity. It vanishes
off the original closed support, since the orbit of each point
outside this support stays outside it in a neighborhood of the
identity. Thus the same compact support controls every successive
derivative kernel.

The genuine word derivative of an averaged vector moves to the
kernel, one generator at a time. For an isometric action its norm
is bounded by the `L¹` norm of the resulting kernel times the norm
of the original vector. The finite seminorm estimate for a
derivative-continuous functional therefore gives a Hilbert norm
bound after one Gårding average.

This implements the fixed-kernel boundedness in Shalika's
Proposition 3.2(1), *The multiplicity one theorem for GL(n)*
(1974), printed pp. 183–184.
