# Infinitesimal characters of irreducible unitary representations of product groups

Module `Langlands.Representation.Archimedean.ProductSmoothInfinitesimalCharacter`, namespace
`Langlands.Archimedean.ProductSmoothVectors`.

## Setting

`M` is a real Lie group modelled on `A`, `Φ` a topological group, `Γ` a group, and
`ιinf : M →* Γ`, `ιfin : Φ →* Γ` homomorphisms with commuting images
(`hcomm : ∀ a b, Commute (ιinf a) (ιfin b)`); some results also assume that `Γ` is generated by the
two images in the form `γ = ιinf a * ιfin b` (`hgen`). For `G(𝔸) = G(F_∞) × G(𝔸_f)` these are
`ofInfinite` and `ofFinite`. `ρ : Γ →* (E →L[ℂ] E)` acts on a complex inner product space `E`;
`ρ.comp ιinf` is its restriction to `M`. The enveloping algebra `𝔘` and its action
`envelopingAction` on the smooth vectors of `ρ.comp ιinf` are those of `SmoothVectorEnvelopingAction`.

## Declarations

- `smoothSubspace A ιinf ιfin ρ`: the vectors that are smooth for `ρ.comp ιinf` and fixed by
  `ρ (ιfin l)` for all `l` in some open subgroup of `Φ` (a subspace, since open subgroups are
  closed under intersection).
- `finite_comm`: `ρ (ιfin b)` commutes with `(ρ.comp ιinf) a`.
- `smoothSubspace_invariant`: under `hgen`, `smoothSubspace` is `ρ`-invariant. For
  `γ = ιinf a * ιfin b`, `ρ (ιfin b) v` is smooth (it commutes with the `M`-action) and fixed by
  `b L b⁻¹`, which is open because conjugation is continuous; `ρ (ιinf a)` preserves smoothness and
  commutes with the `Φ`-action.
- `envelopingAction_fixed`, `smoothSubspaceAction`: the enveloping action commutes with the
  `Φ`-action (`envelopingAction_comm`), hence preserves `smoothSubspace`; `smoothSubspaceAction y`
  is its restriction.
- `exists_smul_of_adjointInvariant` (**Segal's theorem in the form used here**): assume `E`
  complete, `ρ` unitary and topologically irreducible, `hgen`, `smoothSubspace ≠ ⊥`, and that
  `z ∈ 𝔘` is, for every `g ∈ M`, fixed by some algebra endomorphism `φ` of `𝔘` with
  `φ (ι (c ⊗ X)) = ι (c ⊗ Ad(g) X)`. Then there is `c : ℂ` with `dρ(z) w = c • w` for every
  `w ∈ smoothSubspace`.
- `conjugateSubgroup`, `isOpen_conjugateSubgroup`, `fixed_conjugateSubgroup`: the subgroup
  `b L b⁻¹`, open when `L` is, fixes `ρ (ιfin b) v` when `L` fixes `v`.
- `IsDistributionForm ιinf ιfin ρ ξ`: for every open subgroup `L` of `Φ` there are a finite set
  `S` of words and `C` with `‖ξ v‖ ≤ C · ∑_{Xs ∈ S} ‖dρ(Xs) v‖` for all smooth `L`-fixed `v`.
- `coefficient_contMDiff_and_mfderiv`: for such `ξ`, a smooth vector `w` fixed by an open `L`
  has smooth coefficient `a ↦ ξ (ρ (ιinf a) w)` with derivative the coefficient of `dρ(X) w`
  (`ClosedSubspaceFunctionalOrbit.contMDiff_and_mfderiv` with `F₀` the `L`-fixed subspace
  `SmoothInvariantSubspace.fixedSubspace`).
- `coefficient_contMDiff`: for `v ∈ smoothSubspace` and `b ∈ Φ`, the slice
  `a ↦ ξ (ρ (ιinf a * ιfin b) v)` is smooth (apply the previous lemma to `ρ (ιfin b) v` and
  `b L b⁻¹`).
- `coefficient_envelopingAction`: let `Θ : 𝔘 →ₐ[ℂ] Module.End ℂ S` act on a space `S` of functions
  on `M` containing the smooth functions, with `ι (1 ⊗ X)` acting by `groupDirectionalValue X`.
  For `v ∈ smoothSubspace`, `b ∈ Φ` and `y ∈ 𝔘`, `Θ y` maps the slice `a ↦ ξ (ρ (ιinf a * ιfin b) v)`
  to the slice `a ↦ ξ (ρ (ιinf a * ιfin b) (dρ(y) v))`. This is
  `SmoothVectorEnveloping.envelopingAction_coefficient` for the smooth vectors fixed by `b L b⁻¹`,
  with `envelopingAction_comm` for `ρ (ιfin b)`.

## Proof of `exists_smul_of_adjointInvariant`

Apply `FormalAdjointCommutant.exists_eq_smul_of_formalAdjoint_commute` to `W = smoothSubspace`,
`D = smoothSubspaceAction z` and `D' = smoothSubspaceAction z'`, where `z'` is a formal adjoint of
`z` from `SmoothVectorEnveloping.exists_formalAdjoint`. `D` commutes with every `ρ γ`: write
`γ = ιinf a * ιfin b`; `D` commutes with `ρ (ιfin b)` by `envelopingAction_comm`, and
`ρ (ιinf a) ∘ D = dρ(φ z) ∘ ρ (ιinf a) = D ∘ ρ (ιinf a)` by `apply_envelopingAction` and `φ z = z`.

## Provenance

Segal's theorem that the center of the enveloping algebra acts by scalars on an irreducible unitary
representation (Harish-Chandra, Trans. AMS 75 (1953), §11, for connected semisimple groups), here
for groups `M × Φ` and for the elements fixed by `Ad(M)`, which is all the argument uses. The
statements on coefficients make explicit, for distribution vectors, that differentiating a matrix
coefficient differentiates the vector, as used in the proof of Delorme, *On the spectral theorem
of Langlands*, Adv. Math. 426 (2023), Lemma 7.10.
