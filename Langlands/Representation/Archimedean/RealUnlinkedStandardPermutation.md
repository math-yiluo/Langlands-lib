# Permutations of pairwise unlinked real standard parameters

`realUnlinkedStandardPermutation` proves the existence of an injective `(𝔤, K)`-intertwiner between the actual standard modules of two permutations of a list of pairwise unlinked real blocks of total rank `n`.

The linkedness relation on Mœglin data is symmetric. Thus pairwise unlinkage is preserved along a permutation. An adjacent exchange with the larger exponent first is the Library's proved unlinked swap. Its map is surjective by the compact multiplicity comparison for swaps. In the other exponent order, the proof takes the inverse and checks that it intertwines the compact action and the real Lie action. Induction over list permutations, retaining the surrounding lists at each step, composes these injections.

The proof is derived from the Library's adjacent swap and surjectivity results. The unlinkage relation and the adjacent exchange come from C. Mœglin, *Representations of GL(n) over the real field* (1997), §10, Theorem 10b and the following paragraph, pp. 164–165 (PDF pp. 173–174).
