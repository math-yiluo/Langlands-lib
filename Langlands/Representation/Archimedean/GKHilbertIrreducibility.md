# Hilbert irreducibility from compact-finite recovery

`GKHilbertIrreducibilityStatement` concerns an inner-product-preserving
Hilbert representation with an injective dense carrier that intertwines
its compact action and its genuine real orbit derivatives. Every carrier
vector is compact finite, and every compact-finite Hilbert vector has a
preimage in the carrier. If the carrier is irreducible for the compact
and complexified Lie actions together, the Hilbert action is topologically
irreducible.

The proof pulls a closed invariant subspace back to the algebraic
carrier. This pullback is compact stable. Differentiating the equality
between a smooth orbit and its orthogonal projection gives stability
under real directions. Tensor induction gives stability under the
complexified Lie algebra. If the pullback is the whole carrier, density
makes the closed subspace the whole Hilbert space. If the pullback is zero,
projection of each original vector is compact finite because projection
commutes with the unitary compact action. Recovery puts that projection
in the embedded carrier, so it is zero. The projection vanishes on the
whole Hilbert space by continuity, and the closed subspace is zero.

This internal criterion uses the Library's compact-finite recovery
interface and the genuine derivatives in its smooth-vector model.
