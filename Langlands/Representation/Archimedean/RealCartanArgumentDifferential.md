# Differential of the compact argument at a fixed block flag

The lower block chart supplies an orthogonal factor with skew-symmetric
derivative. Multiplying that factor on the right by the positive
diagonal and taking the orthogonal part again gives the radially
scaled derivative. The local compact argument uses the transpose of
this second factor and the transpose of a compact angular variation.

At zero these two transposed factors and the lower chart's orthogonal
factor are identities. Differentiating their product therefore adds
the three derivatives. Transposition negates the skew-symmetric
derivatives of the scaled lower and angular factors. The fixed block
orthogonal factor conjugates the scaled lower term, and the fixed frame
conjugates the sum before taking its strictly lower entries.

If `w * g = D * R * w` and `k` commutes with `D`, the frame in the
translated chart is `κ(lower X) * k * w`. The compact argument with
left factor `a(Z) * k * R⁻¹ * k⁻¹` agrees with the local matrix formula.
This uses right orthogonal equivariance of the signed orthogonal part.
Writing `W = k * w` and `B = k * R⁻¹ * k⁻¹`, the based argument is
conjugation by `W⁻¹` of the product of the transposed scaled factor
conjugated by `B`, the transposed variation `a(Z)`, and `κ(lower X)`.
Its lower projection is smooth, vanishes at zero, and has the
derivative described above.

This internal calculation gives the derivative of the compact
argument in Euclidean coordinates for the local Haar integral.
