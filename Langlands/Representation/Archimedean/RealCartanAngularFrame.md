# Product angular coordinates for compact real Levi blocks

Restrict the product compact Levi Haar probability to the locus where every compact block has determinant +1. On this product of special orthogonal factors, the restricted measure is the angular frame pushforward of Lebesgue measure on the cube from minus pi to pi in each within-block lower coordinate. Its coefficient is the product of one half per line and one over four pi per plane. On this cube the identity frame has the unique coordinate zero.

The proof splits the within-block entries by Levi blocks. It checks the product Lebesgue identity on measurable boxes, applies the angular measure formula to each block, and takes the product of the coefficients. Each line has no lower entries. Each plane uses its single lower entry as the angle, oriented so its lower matrix entry is the sine.
