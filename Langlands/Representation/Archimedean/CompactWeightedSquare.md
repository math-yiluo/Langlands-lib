# Squaring a compact average within its weight translation type

Suppose \(K\) is a compact subgroup, \(\nu\) is a finite left invariant Borel
measure on it, and \(\tau\) is a continuous finite-dimensional matrix
representation. Let \(F\) be a linear family of continuous weights satisfying
\(F(\tau(k)u)(x)=F(u)(k^{-1}x)\). The vector
`compactWeightedTypeVector` is \(z=\int_K F(u)(k)\tau(k)u\,d\nu(k)\).

For every jointly continuous complex Banach action, the square of its
orbit average with weight \(F(u)\) equals its orbit average with weight
\(F(z)\). For a fixed initial vector \(v\), the average with weight
\(F(u)\) is the evaluation at \(u\) of the compact intertwiner \(T_v\).
Applying that average a second time and passing \(T_v\) through the
integral gives \(T_v(z)\). The finite-dimensional domain makes \(T_v\)
bounded. The same weight \(F(z)\) works for all the actions.

This is an internal consequence of the compact weighted orbit
intertwiner construction.
