# The rank-four energy inequality

On a nonzero compact highest vector of spins \((m,0)\), the lowering operator
is adjoint to the raising operator. Each column of symmetric matrices gives
three vectors of consecutive compact weights. The bracket identities give
the two raising tests and the squared norm difference used by the column
estimate. The parallelogram identity converts the estimate back to the
three real symmetric matrix actions.

The left compact square sum acts on the vector by \(-m(m+1)\), while the
right compact square sum kills it. The trace tensor identity then computes
the total symmetric energy from the trace-free Casimir. At the eigenvalue
\((m-1)^2+4a^2-5\), the three lower bounds imply \(4a^2\le 1\).

This derived Lie algebra calculation supplies the weak inequality for the
rank-four opposite-twist case of Badulescu–Renard (2010), Proposition 9.4,
p. 1133.
