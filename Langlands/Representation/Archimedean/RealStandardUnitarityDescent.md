# Infinitesimal unitarity descends to Hermitian standard factors

For two consecutive factors of a real standard module, assume that both factors and the ambient module are irreducible. Assume also that each factor has an actual standard equivalence to its Hermitian-dual block list. The theorem deduces infinitesimal unitarity of the factors from infinitesimal unitarity of the ambient module.

Choose a split Fourier replacement for each factor and form its invariant compact sesquilinear pairing with its Hermitian-dual equivalence. Induction in stages gives compatible ambient maps for the two reverse lifts and the two equivalences. Finite-dual Schur comparison identifies the ambient sesquilinear form with a nonzero scalar multiple of the given positive form. Normalize by the inverse of that scalar. The compact localization theorem then gives a real nonnegative diagonal integral on the compact Levi subgroup.

An arbitrary pair of local vectors has a pure product realization on that subgroup. The reverse lift and the Hermitian-dual equivalence act there factor by factor, so product Haar integration gives the product of the two local diagonals, with the normalization scalar. To test either factor, choose a vector with nonzero diagonal in the other factor. The resulting scalar multiple of the tested local form has a real nonnegative diagonal, and irreducibility makes it positive definite by polarization and vanishing of its invariant radical.

This deduction is carried out in the compact standard model. Infinitesimal unitarity uses the invariant positive form of Knapp–Vogan, *Cohomological Induction and Unitary Representations* (1995), display (0.5), p. 6 (PDF p. 28), registered as `knapp-vogan-1995-cohomological-induction`.
