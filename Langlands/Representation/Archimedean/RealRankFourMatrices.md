# Quaternionic matrices for the real rank-four exponent bound

The matrices are left and right multiplication on the real quaternions in the
basis \((1,i,j,k)\). Half of each imaginary multiplication gives one of the two
compact triples. Twice their pairwise products and half the identity complete
the sixteen-element basis. The rational entry computation proves the trace
coefficient identity for all matrix entries and the commutators used for the
three norm estimates. The coefficient signs are negative on the two compact
triples and positive on the symmetric matrices and the half identity.

The entry computations with the two-column plane used in
`RealRankFourCompact` check that the left raising matrix kills the plane,
that the third left generator acts on it by \(i/2\), and that the right
generators preserve the plane with traceless restrictions.

This is a derived matrix computation for the local necessity in
Badulescu–Renard (2010), Proposition 9.4, p. 1133. The coefficient identity
allows the same calculation in any complex linear action of the matrix algebra.
