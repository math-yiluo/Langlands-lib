# Density hypotheses for irreducible archimedean standard quotients

`compactQuotientProjection` is the linear quotient projection for a
compact-stable subspace, with equivariance for the induced quotient action.
For a compact-finite representation with continuous coefficients, compact
semisimplicity gives a section of this projection over the group algebra.
Composition with the section embeds each matrix intertwiner space of the
quotient into the corresponding finite-dimensional space of the original
representation. Compact admissibility therefore descends.

`gkModuleDensityHyp_quotient` combines this admissibility result with the
existing quotient lemmas for compact finiteness and continuous coefficients.
For an irreducible quotient this gives `GKModuleDensity.Hyp`, the hypotheses
of the Library's Jacobson density theorem for the algebra generated by the
compact and Lie actions. The two final lemmas apply this result to the
standard modules of `GL_n(ℝ)` and `GL_n(ℂ)`.

The construction is an internal combination of compact semisimplicity,
linear quotient actions and the Library's proofs of standard-module
admissibility. For the tensor-product construction in Langlands (1979),
*On the notion of an automorphic representation*, Lemma 1, printed p. 203
(PDF p. 198), this supplies an algebraic density input for the
archimedean factors.
