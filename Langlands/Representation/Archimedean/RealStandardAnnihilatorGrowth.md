# Growth of the enveloping algebra acting on a real standard module

Lean module: `Langlands.Representation.Archimedean.RealStandardAnnihilatorGrowth`.

## Statement

`Langlands.Archimedean.RealStandardAnnihilatorGrowthStatement`:

```lean
def RealStandardAnnihilatorGrowthStatement : Prop :=
  ∀ (n : ℕ) (ds : List RealBlock), realRank ds = n → ∃ c : ℝ, 0 < c ∧ ∀ d : ℕ,
    c * ((d : ℝ) + 1) ^ (n * (n - 1)) ≤
      (Module.finrank ℂ ((degreeFiltration ℂ (ℂ ⊗[ℝ] Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℝ) d).map
        (envelopingAction (realStandardLie n ds)).toLinearMap) : ℝ)
```

Notation:

- `𝔤 = ℂ ⊗[ℝ] Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℝ` is the complexified Lie algebra of `GL_n(ℝ)`, and
  `U = U(𝔤)` is its universal enveloping algebra.
- `I(ds)` is `kFiniteFunctions (realStandardModule n ds) (unitaryCompact ℝ n)`, the `O(n)`-finite
  functions of the Library's real standard module of `ds`. `realStandardLie n ds` is the action of
  `𝔤` on it by right derivatives, from the Library module `RealStandardModuleGKModule`.
- `φ = envelopingAction (realStandardLie n ds) : U → End(I(ds))` is its extension to `U`, from the
  Library module `RealGenericLargeReduction`.
- `degreeFiltration ℂ 𝔤 d`, from the Library module `Langlands.Algebra.UniversalEnvelopingFiltration`,
  is the span `U_d` of the products of at most `d` generators `ι x`, `x ∈ 𝔤`. It is
  finite-dimensional.

The statement says the following. Let `ds` be a list of real blocks of total rank `n`, with no
ordering or linkage condition. Then there is `c > 0` such that for every `d`, the space `φ(U_d)`
of operators on `I(ds)` has dimension at least `c (d + 1)^(n (n - 1))`.

`φ(U_d)` is isomorphic to `U_d / (U_d ∩ Ann I(ds))`. So the statement says that the Gelfand–Kirillov
dimension of `U / Ann I(ds)` is at least `n (n - 1) = 2 dim 𝔫`, where `𝔫` is the complexified
nilradical of the upper triangular Borel subalgebra. For `n ≤ 1` the exponent is `0`. The
statement then only asks that `φ(U_d)` be nonzero, that is, `I(ds) ≠ 0`.

This module contains only the statement. It is not proved here.

## Provenance

This is an internal statement, introduced in Task 7070 for the proof of
`RealLinkedLanglandsQuotientNotLargeStatement`. It is the lower half of the Gelfand–Kirillov
dimension comparison suggested in the task, and it is used in the module
`RealLinkedQuotientGrowth`.

Mathematically, the statement follows from two facts.

- A standard module has full Gelfand–Kirillov dimension `dim 𝔫`. This follows from Vogan 1978,
  Lemma 6.5 (p. 94) applied to induction from discrete series and characters.
- For a Harish-Chandra module `X`, `Dim(U / Ann X) = 2 Dim X`. Vogan 1978, Corollary 4.7
  (PDF p. 10, printed p. 84) states this for irreducible `X`.

Here Vogan 1978 is D. A. Vogan, Gelfand–Kirillov dimension for Harish-Chandra modules,
Invent. Math. 48 (1978), 75–98. Its PDF page is the printed page minus 74.

## Suggested proof route

This route is not formalized here and is recorded only as guidance. It avoids general
Gelfand–Kirillov theory.

1. Let `ℓ = ev₁` be evaluation at `1` on `I(ds)`. The Library module
   `RealPrincipalSeriesInfinitesimalCharacter` shows that every element of the upper triangular
   Borel subalgebra acts on `ℓ` by a scalar (`tmul_mem_scalarSubmodule`).
2. Show that `y ↦ ℓ ∘ φ(y)` is injective on the subalgebra of `U` generated by the strictly lower
   triangular elements: the Taylor coefficients at `1` of `O(n)`-finite functions in `I(ds)`
   separate `U(𝔫̄)`.
3. The span `Q = {ℓ ∘ φ(u)}` then carries a left `U`-module structure through the transpose
   anti-automorphism. It has an infinitesimal character (by
   `realStandardLie_hasInfinitesimalCharacter`) and a Whittaker functional for a nondegenerate
   character of `𝔫`. By the Library's Kostant theorem `Kostant.realWhittakerMaximallyFaithful`
   (module `RealWhittakerMaximallyFaithfulProof`), `Ann Q = U · ker χ`. Moreover
   `Ann I(ds) ⊆ Ann Q` after transposition.
4. A Hilbert-function count for `U / U · ker χ` gives the lower bound. The symbol of an element of
   `U · ker χ` vanishes on the nilpotent cone (Library `Kostant/Matumoto`, `Kostant/OrbitVanishing`),
   and the nilpotent cone of `𝔤𝔩_n` has dimension `n² - n`.
