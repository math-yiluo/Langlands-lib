# Hilbert trace profiles after finite-dimensional compression

A bounded insertion from a finite-dimensional Hilbert space and a bounded projection back give a trace profile of a complex Hilbert action. In a finite orthonormal basis the profile is a finite sum of matrix coefficients and hence is continuous for a jointly continuous action. Composition of the insertion and projection factors through the finite-dimensional space and is trace class. The trace after a bounded kernel average is the scalar kernel integral of the same profile, by rectangular trace cyclicity and commuting bounded linear functionals and finite sums with integration.

For units of a finite-dimensional real Banach algebra, we test with genuine smooth compact kernels. Suppose the two finite-dimensional factorizations represent the same weighted compact averaging operation in two Hilbert actions. The compact parameter space has a finite Borel measure, a continuous map to the group and a continuous weight. The group measure is Borel, finite on compact sets, right invariant and positive on nonempty open sets. After the common compact convolution, equality of operator trace values on all smooth compact tests gives equality of the integrals of the two trace profiles on all these tests. If a continuous scalar difference is nonzero at a point, rotate its value to have positive real part and choose a nonnegative bump supported in the inverse of a neighborhood where that real part stays positive. The integral is then positive, which contradicts equality of all the tests. The trace profiles consequently agree at every group element. This is an internal deduction from bounded averaging and the Library's Hilbert trace laws.
