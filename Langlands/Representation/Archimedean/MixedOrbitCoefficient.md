# Scalar orbit coefficients in the mixed archimedean matrix model

This internal development connects genuine smooth vectors of a Banach representation with the published analytic theory of [ArchimedeanJointFiniteAnalyticityProof](../../Global/ArchimedeanJointFiniteAnalyticityProof.md), [ArchimedeanEnvelopingJetProof](../../Global/ArchimedeanEnvelopingJetProof.md) and [ArchimedeanAnalyticJetProof](../../Global/ArchimedeanAnalyticJetProof.md). It supplies the analytic step of the Harish-Chandra comparison for a supplied representation. Irreducibility of a Harish-Chandra module is not proved here.

## Setting

`K` is a number field, `n` a size, and `ArchimedeanMatrix K n` the published real Banach algebra of matrices over the mixed real/complex space, with the operator norm. `A` is a finite-dimensional complete normed real algebra and `ψ : ArchimedeanMatrix K n ≃ₐ[ℝ] A` a real algebra equivalence. `R` is a representation of `Aˣ` by bounded complex-linear operators on a complex normed space `E`. `D` is its genuine smooth carrier `DerivativeSmoothVectors 𝓘(ℝ, A) R`, and `ε` the empty-word map `D → E`. All declarations are in `Langlands.Archimedean.MixedOrbit`.

- `algCLE` is `ψ` as a continuous real linear equivalence, by finite dimension.
- `unitsMap` is the induced monoid homomorphism of units, `unitsMap_val` its underlying matrix, and `contMDiff_unitsMap` its smoothness in the two units manifolds.
- For a continuous complex-linear functional `ℓ` on `E`, `orbitCoefficient ℓ : D →ₗ[ℂ] SmoothFunctions K n` sends `v` to the function `x ↦ ℓ(R(unitsMap(x))(ε v))` on the completion-based group, with `x` read in the published mixed coordinates `archimedeanGLCoordinates`. Its smoothness is that of the genuine smooth vector `ε v` composed with `unitsMap` and `ℓ`.

## Derivatives

`smoothDifferential_orbitCoefficient` (assuming joint continuity of `R`) proves that the published directional differential `smoothDifferential K n X` of a coefficient is the coefficient of the genuine derivative `dR(ψ X)v`, for every real direction `X`. At a unit `u`, both sides are derivatives along the line `t ↦ u + t·uX`. The coordinate expression has its ordinary Fréchet derivative there. After applying `ψ`, the published `hasDerivAt_unitsChartOrbit` gives the derivative `R∞(ψ u)(dR(ψ X)v)` of the chart orbit in the genuine topology of `D`. Applying the continuous functional `ℓ ∘ ε` to slopes transfers this to a scalar derivative. Near `0` the line stays among units, where the two curves agree. Uniqueness of derivatives gives the identity.

`transportedCartanSquareSum` is `∑ᵢ dR(ψ bᵢ)²` over the published normalized real basis `cartanAdaptedBasis K n`. `envelopingAction_cartanSquareSum_orbitCoefficient` identifies the action of the published `cartanSquareSum` on coefficients with this operator, since `envelopingAction` is an algebra homomorphism sending each real generator to `smoothDifferential`. A linear map intertwining two endomorphisms intertwines each polynomial in them, by induction on the polynomial. Hence `envelopingAction_aeval_orbitCoefficient` holds for every polynomial `p`.

## Analyticity and vanishing on components

`analyticOnNhd_orbitCoefficient`: if a monic `p` satisfies `p(transportedCartanSquareSum)v = 0`, every coefficient of `v` has a real-analytic coordinate expression on the open set of invertible matrices. The proof follows the published `jointFiniteAnalyticity`. The polynomial equation for the coefficient follows from the intertwining identity. The published `cartanPolynomialCoordinateRealization` realizes `p(cartanSquareSum)` by a scalar operator with analytic coefficients and principal symbol the `deg p`-th power of the positive form `ambientCartanCovectorForm`. That operator is therefore elliptic on units, and `Langlands.Analysis.scalarEllipticAnalyticity` applies.

`orbitCoefficient_eq_zero_of_stable`: fix one continuous complex-linear functional `ℓ` on `E`. Let `W ⊆ D` be a complex submodule stable under every genuine derivative `dR(ψ X)`. Let `w ∈ W` be annihilated by a monic polynomial in the transported square sum. Suppose that for each `w' ∈ W` the coefficient `orbitCoefficient ℓ w'`, formed with this same `ℓ`, vanishes at the point `x₀`. Then `orbitCoefficient ℓ w` vanishes on the connected component of `x₀`. The hypothesis concerns the single functional `ℓ`, not all functionals: vanishing of the coefficients of every functional at `x₀` would force `W = 0`, since `R(x₀)` is invertible, `ε` is injective and continuous functionals separate points. In the application `ℓ` annihilates the norm closure of `ε(W)`, as in Wallach's coefficient-by-coefficient test (*Real Reductive Groups I*, §1.6.6, PDF pp.54–55). The image of `W` under the single linear map `orbitCoefficient ℓ` among smooth functions is stable under every `smoothDifferential`. Since the published enveloping algebra is generated by its generators (`Langlands.Algebra.LieCasimir.enveloping_adjoin_range`), it is stable under the whole `envelopingAction`. So every enveloping derivative of the coefficient vanishes at `x₀`. The published `envelopingZeroJet` makes its full matrix jet vanish there, and analyticity with `analyticMatrixJetUniqueness` propagates vanishing along the component.

## Scope

The equivalence `ψ` and the representation are arbitrary; the intended application uses the rational mixed space and the actual real general linear group. No compactness, admissibility, unitarity or irreducibility is assumed. This route is the regularity argument behind analyticity of compact-finite matrix coefficients (Wallach, *Real Reductive Groups I*, Theorem 3.4.9, PDF p.105, registered `wallach-1988-real-reductive-groups-i`). The implementation uses the Library's elliptic regularity theorem rather than Wallach's proof. Producing the monic annihilating polynomial for compact-finite vectors is a separate step.
