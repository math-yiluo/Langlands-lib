# Integrated operators on the compact real Hilbert principal series

For a continuous compactly supported scalar function on real general linear units, take a bounded integrated operator given by the pointwise Bochner formula for an s-finite left- and right-invariant Borel measure finite on compact sets. Its compact-picture operator is represented by a continuous kernel on the square of the compact group. The proof compares the integral on each embedded smooth principal-series vector with the compact-profile integral and applies the right averaging formula. Boundedness and density extend the equality to the Hilbert completion.

The compact kernel is Hilbert–Schmidt on the ambient L² space. Precomposing with the Hilbert inclusion and postcomposing with the orthogonal projection gives the Hilbert–Schmidt property on the principal-series Hilbert completion itself.

This internal operator comparison is a step toward trace-class smoothing for the real pairing of the archimedean inverse-transpose pairing argument.
