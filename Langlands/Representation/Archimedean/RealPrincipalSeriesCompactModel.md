# Continuous real principal-series compact factors

The Gram formulas supply a continuous orthogonal factor and the scalar weight of the triangular factor. The triangular diagonal is chosen positive. Right orthogonal translation multiplies the compact factor and preserves the scalar weight. The original smooth real principal-series function at each group element is its compact restriction at this factor, multiplied by the weight. This uses exactly the exponents of the Frobenius realization, with the parities carried by the compact profile.

This internal comparison applies the Library's principal-series Gram formulas to the Hilbert compact model used for Task 7534.
