# Transport of mixed smooth vectors and continuous functionals

A bounded map intertwining the archimedean action preserves actual
archimedean smoothness and transports the derivative topology via the
genuine smooth intertwiner. If the same map also intertwines the
finite action, the algebraic smooth-part map restricts it to the mixed
smooth spaces and preserves each fixed level. The resulting map
retains both the finite and the archimedean intertwining identities.

An extension of a functional on a target fixed level pulls back
through the smooth intertwiner on the entire archimedean smooth
space. Its derivative continuity and the exact fixed-level
restriction give derivative continuity at that level of the source.
This is an internal consequence of the mixed and genuine smooth
intertwiner constructions.
