# Trace class of integrated real tests on the Frobenius closure

Restrict a real smooth test function on the open set of invertible real
matrices to the group of units, and regard its values as complex scalars.
Its support in the group is contained in the compact preimage of the test's
support under the open embedding of the units. Its extension by zero to
all matrices is the original test with complex scalars, and is smooth.

For a real \((\mathfrak g,K)\)-module with a compatible Jacquet functional,
the ambient real Hilbert principal series contains the invariant closed
Frobenius submodule. Suppose bounded operators on the two Hilbert spaces
satisfy their respective Bochner integral formulas with the restricted
test and a Borel measure finite on compact sets. The inclusion of the closure
commutes with the group action and passes through the integral of the
continuous compactly supported weighted orbit. Composing with the
orthogonal projection onto the closure identifies the smaller operator
with the compression of the ambient one.

For a measure that is also s-finite and left and right invariant, the
ambient operator is trace class by the smooth compact-picture kernel
theorem. The trace-class ideal property gives trace class on the Frobenius
closure. This internal argument uses those two results and the invariance
of the Hilbert closure.
