# The real generic unitary classification on irreducible standard modules

`RealGenericStandardUnitaryClassificationStatement` is a proposition about the actual `O(n)`-finite function model of `realStandardModule n ds`. It assumes rank `n`, irreducibility and infinitesimal unitarity. It asks for an inducing list `es` of the form `IsRealGenericUnitaryInducingData es` and `HasRealStandardParameter` for that list on the given module. The latter supplies equality of total rank and an injective compact and Lie intertwiner. On these irreducible models the intertwiner is onto, as in `RealStandardParameterUnitary`.

The statement is a specialization of Badulescu–Renard, *Unitary dual of GL(n) at archimedean places and global Jacquet–Langlands correspondence* (2010), Theorems 8.1 and 8.2, p. 1131 (PDF p. 18, `badulescu-renard-2010-unitary-dual`). Their remark after Theorem 8.1 gives genericity of irreducible inductions from essentially tempered data. The real standard model uses normalized induction from characters and essentially discrete-series blocks. An induced block of weight zero is a pair of equal-exponent characters of opposite parity. These cases correspond to their §9.2, including Remark 9.3 and equation (9.4), pp. 1132–1133 (PDF pp. 19–20). Thus the genericity condition in the source derives from the given irreducible standard presentation.

In the source argument, the irreducible admissible module is realized on a unitary Hilbert space by Knapp–Vogan, *Cohomological Induction and Unitary Representations* (1995), Introduction, Theorem 0.6(a), p. 7 (PDF p. 29, `knapp-vogan-1995-cohomological-induction`). The given positive form satisfies their display (0.5), p. 6 (PDF p. 28). `realStandardAdmissible` gives admissibility of the specific Library model. Badulescu–Renard Theorem 8.2 then supplies an irreducible product of the basis elements from `RealGenericUnitaryInducingData`. Normalized induction in stages expresses this product as the standard model of their concatenated inducing blocks. Definition 7.1, pp. 1129–1130 (PDF pp. 16–17), and Proposition 9.4, p. 1133 (PDF p. 20), give the complementary twists and their real normalization. In rank zero both presentations are empty.

This module records the existence proposition. The identification of its standard parameter with a given list uses a separate parameter uniqueness theorem.
