# Smooth vectors by averaging a Banach representation

`smoothedVector` is the Banach-valued integral of an orbit against an inverse kernel. For a jointly continuous representation of the units of a finite-dimensional real Banach algebra, a smooth compactly supported complex kernel produces a genuine smooth vector. The measure is left invariant and finite on compact sets. Moving the bounded action through the integral and changing variables by left translation identifies the new orbit with the actual smooth compact-kernel convolution of the original continuous orbit.

If the measure also gives positive mass to nonempty open sets, the published normalized-kernel construction provides a smooth nonnegative kernel supported in any identity neighborhood with inverse integral one. Choose that neighborhood so every corresponding translate of the vector differs from it by less than half the prescribed error. The norm of the difference of the averaged vector and the original one is bounded by the integral of the weighted pointwise error. Normalization gives approximation in the Banach norm and proves density of the genuine smooth vectors.

These are internal consequences of the published manifold convolution and bump constructions. Both conclusions apply to the actual real and complex general linear groups with a Haar measure and their real matrix models.
