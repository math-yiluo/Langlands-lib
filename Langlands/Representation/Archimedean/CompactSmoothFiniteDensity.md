# Density of finite compact orbits in the genuine derivative topology

Let a compact Hausdorff group act through a continuous homomorphism to a real
Lie group, with a supplied left invariant finite measure positive on nonempty
open sets. For each genuine smooth vector of a complex Banach representation
of the Lie group, compact integration defines a linear map from continuous
scalar weights to genuine smooth vectors. Its value has the original Bochner
integral as underlying vector.

Moving a bounded representation operator inside the integral and using left
invariance of the measure identifies the action of a compact element on the
average with left translation of the weight. The ambient integral is therefore
an actual intertwining map. It takes the finite dimensional translation orbit
span of a finite weight onto the orbit span of its average.

A supplied faithful continuous representation on a finite complex coordinate
space gives uniform density of the finite weights. Approximation by those
weights gives density of the original compact-finite smooth vectors for all
genuine word derivatives. This is an internal consequence of the scalar weight
density, compact smooth approximation, and finite-vector constructions.
`CompactSmoothFiniteDensityStatement` records the density with five free type
universes, and `compactSmoothFiniteDensity` proves it.
