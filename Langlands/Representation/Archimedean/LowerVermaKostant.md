# Kostant's multiplicity theorem for `𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)`

Module `Langlands.Representation.Archimedean.LowerVermaKostant`, namespace
`Langlands.Archimedean.VermaKostant`.

```lean
theorem lowerVermaKostant : VermaGL.KostantMultiplicityStatement.{uW}
```

This proves the Library statement `VermaGL.KostantMultiplicityStatement` (module `LowerVerma`):
for a finite-dimensional representation `σ` of `𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)` on `W` with integral weights and every
character `χ` of the center of `U(𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ))`, there are `d = dim W₀` covariant linear maps
`f_i : W → U(𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ))` (`f_i(σ(Y) w) = ι(Y) f_i(w) - f_i(w) ι(Y)`) that are linearly
independent modulo the left ideal generated by the elements `z - χ(z)`, `z` central
(Bernstein–Gelfand 1980, 1.7, printed p.253: `Hom_𝔤(L, U_ad)` is a free `Z`-module of rank
the multiplicity of the zero weight in `L`, quoted there from B. Kostant, *Lie group
representations on polynomial rings*, Amer. J. Math. 85 (1963), 327–404). The Library statement
is the corresponding lower bound modulo the left ideal of each central character `χ`
(`dim W₀` covariants independent modulo `U · ker χ`); it was checked against the
Bernstein–Gelfand formulation. No copy of Kostant's 1963 paper was available in the project and
it was not consulted; the formal proof does not follow it (see `GLKostantAll`).

## Proof

The proof is one line: the reduction `GLReynolds.kostantMultiplicity_of_central`
(module `GLKostantAll`), which derives `VermaGL.KostantMultiplicityStatement` from
`VermaGL.CentralCharacterStatement`, applied to `VermaCentral.lowerVermaCentral`
(module `LowerVermaCentral`), the proof that every character of the center is the central
character of a lower Verma module with `Z`-extremal parameter. The mathematics is in those
modules and the chain `GLReynolds`, `GLLowestWeight`, `GLLowestModule`, `GLBurnside`,
`GLTensorLowest`, `GLVermaTheta`, `GLVermaInject`, `GLKostantOne`, `GLSymbolTransport`,
`GLKostantAll` (see their Markdown files). No new axioms are used.
