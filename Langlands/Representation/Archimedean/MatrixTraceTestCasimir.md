# The trace Casimir on compact smooth tests

The test Casimir is the finite sum of compositions of the
actual left-translation derivative maps indexed by the real
trace dual families. Evaluation gives the smooth matrix
Casimir. The matrix field commutator also holds on these
bundled tests.

Split the matrix indices into diagonal indices and pairs of
opposite roots. In an opposite-root pair, exchanging the
indices moves the imaginary sign to the other derivative.
Reorder the term with the lower-root derivative applied last
using the matrix field commutator. The pair now has twice the
term with the upper-root derivative applied last and one
first-order commutator term. This is the test-function
rewriting used before differentiation of the Whittaker
equivariance in Shalika (1974), Proposition 2.10.
