# The transverse Casimir equation on a Bruhat chart

Assume an ambient Whittaker distribution has a scalar trace Casimir action and vanishes away from the cell within the chart target. Pass to real tests in the chart. The transverse part of the simple lower direction becomes differentiation in the last coordinate. The complementary part and the diagonal Casimir terms are tangent to the zero slice.

For each fixed rectangular support, the transported tangent operators preserve vanishing jets. The diagonal second-order operators are compositions of two such derivatives. Rearranging the actual scalar Casimir equation expresses the last coordinate derivative as a finite sum of these operators, the identity, and the complementary tangent derivative. The slice vanishing theorem kills all transported real tests on the rectangle. The chart neighborhood lemma transfers this to ambient vanishing near the chart center.

This formalizes the differential argument in Shalika's Proposition 2.10 on a given smooth chart. The chart geometry and the scalar Casimir action enter as the stated hypotheses.
