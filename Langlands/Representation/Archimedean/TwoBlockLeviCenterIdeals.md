# Cofinite center ideals for a complex matrix Levi

Extend the ambient ideal along the finite parabolic center map. Then
contract it to each block center using that factor's inclusion in the
Levi center. Extension and contraction preserve finite-dimensional
quotients in these steps.

An element in either contracted ideal embeds in the ambient
enveloping algebra as a sum of a term from the original central ideal
span and a term from the upper radical span. This holds for the images
of the original ideal elements by the parabolic congruence. Both
spans are stable under right multiplication, which extends the
congruence to the generated Levi ideal.
