# Growth of `U(𝔤)` modulo the central ideal of a real standard module

Module `Langlands.Representation.Archimedean.RealStandardCentralGrowth`. The helper lemmas are
in the namespace `Langlands.Archimedean.RealStandardCentralGrowth`. The definition
`realStandardCentralIdeal` and the two main theorems are in `Langlands.Archimedean`.

## Setting and notation

* `𝔤 = ℂ ⊗_ℝ 𝔤𝔩_n(ℝ)` is the complexified Lie algebra of `GL_n(ℝ)`, and `U = U(𝔤)` is its
  universal enveloping algebra, with the degree filtration `U_d = degreeFiltration ℂ 𝔤 d`.
* `I(ds)` is the space of `O(n)`-finite vectors of the Library real standard module
  `realStandardModule n ds`, with the action `realStandardLie n ds` of `𝔤`.
* For a representation `π` of `𝔤`, the *central ideal* is
  `Ideal.span {z ∈ Z(U) | envelopingAction π z = 0}`, the left ideal of `U` generated by the
  central elements acting by `0`. For `GLProd ℂ n` this is the ideal `centralIdeal n π` of
  `Kostant/BorelCentralIdeal`.
* `toGL n : ℂ ⊗_ℝ 𝔤𝔩_n(ℝ) ≃ GLProd ℂ (fun _ : Unit => n)` is the Library Lie algebra
  equivalence of `Kostant/RealTransfer`, and `enveloping e` is the algebra equivalence of
  enveloping algebras induced by a Lie algebra equivalence `e` (Library `LieEnvelopingEquiv`).

## Definition

`realStandardCentralIdeal n ds` is the central ideal of `realStandardLie n ds`:

`Ideal.span {z | z ∈ Subalgebra.center ℂ U ∧ envelopingAction (realStandardLie n ds) z = 0}`.

It is the ideal of `U` generated by the central elements acting by `0` on `I(ds)`. As an
`Ideal` of a noncommutative ring it is a left ideal, but its generators are central, so it is
also a two-sided ideal.

## Results

Transport (namespace `RealStandardCentralGrowth`, `e : L ≃ L'` a complex Lie algebra
equivalence):

* `map_degreeFiltration`, `map_symm_degreeFiltration`: `enveloping e` and its inverse map the
  degree filtration `U(L)_d` onto `U(L')_d`, and back.
* `enveloping_mem_span_center`: `enveloping e` maps the central ideal of `π` into the central
  ideal of `π ∘ e⁻¹`.
* `pow_le_finrank_map_of_equiv`: let `e : L ≃ GLProd ℂ n`, and let `ℓ ≠ 0` be a Borel
  eigenfunctional for `π ∘ e⁻¹` (`IsBorelEigen` of Kostant/BorelCentralIdeal). Let
  `T : U(L) →ₗ[ℂ] F` be linear, and assume every `u ∈ U(L)_d` with `T u = 0` lies in the
  central ideal of `π`. Then, with `m = |offLast n|`, in `ℝ`
  `(d + 1)^m ≤ (m + 1)^m · finrank ℂ (T(U(L)_d))`.

Borel eigenfunctionals and evaluation at `1`:

* `exists_isBorelEigen`: let `π` be a representation of `ℂ ⊗_ℝ 𝔤𝔩_n(ℝ)` and `ℓ` a functional
  such that `c ⊗ X` lies in `scalarSubmodule π ℓ` (that is, `ℓ ∘ π(c ⊗ X)` is a multiple of `ℓ`)
  for every `c ∈ ℂ` and every real upper triangular `X`. Then there is `μ` with
  `IsBorelEigen (π ∘ (toGL n)⁻¹) ℓ μ`.
* `kFiniteEval_one_ne_zero`: if `I(ds)` has a nonzero element, then evaluation at `1`,
  `kFiniteEval (realStandardModule n ds) 1`, is a nonzero functional on `I(ds)`.

Main theorems (namespace `Langlands.Archimedean`):

* `realStandardCentralGrowth n ds hne`: if `I(ds)` has a nonzero element, there is `c > 0` with
  the following property. For every `d`, every complex vector space `F` and every linear
  `T : U →ₗ[ℂ] F` such that every `u ∈ U_d` with `T u = 0` lies in
  `realStandardCentralIdeal n ds`, in `ℝ`
  `c (d + 1)^{n(n-1)} ≤ finrank ℂ (T(U_d))`.
  The proof gives `c = 1 / (n(n-1) + 1)^{n(n-1)}`.
* `realLinkedLanglandsQuotientNotLarge_of_quotientGrowth`:
  `RealLinkedQuotientGrowthStatement → RealLinkedLanglandsQuotientNotLargeStatement`. So a proof
  of the Library statement `RealLinkedQuotientGrowthStatement` (module `RealLinkedQuotientGrowth`)
  proves the Library statement `RealLinkedLanglandsQuotientNotLargeStatement` (module
  `RealLargeStandardIrreducibleReduction`).

## Provenance

The module is internal to the proof of `RealLinkedLanglandsQuotientNotLargeStatement`.
`realStandardCentralGrowth` is the lower bound of that proof, transported to the real
standard module from the complex-algebra statement `pow_le_finrank_map` of
`Kostant/BorelCentralGrowth`. The idea of the lower bound adapts Matumoto 1987, §2 (Library
`Kostant/Assembly`). There, a Whittaker functional forces the growth of the annihilator. Here,
evaluation at `1` is a Borel eigenfunctional and bounds the growth modulo the central ideal.
The reduction `realLinkedLanglandsQuotientNotLarge_of_quotientGrowth` follows the comparison of
growth exponents `n(n-1)` and `n(n-1) - 1` in Library
`realLinkedStandardQuotientAnnihilator_of_growth` (module `RealLinkedQuotientGrowth`). No statement
is quoted from a source.

## Proofs

1. **Filtrations** (`map_degreeFiltration`). `U(L)_d = (1 ⊔ ι(L))^d`, where `1` is the submodule
   spanned by `1`. The algebra map `enveloping e` sends `ι(x)` to `ι(e x)`
   (`enveloping_generator`). Since `e` is surjective, it maps `ι(L)` onto `ι(L')`. It maps `1`
   onto `1` (`Submodule.map_one`), and it commutes with powers of submodules
   (`Submodule.map_pow`). The inverse statement follows by composing with
   `(enveloping e)⁻¹ ∘ enveloping e = id`.

2. **Central ideal** (`enveloping_mem_span_center`). Induction on the span. A generator `z` is
   central with `π(z) = 0`. Its image is central (Library `Kostant.mem_center_enveloping`), and
   `(π ∘ e⁻¹)(enveloping e z) = π(z) = 0` (Library `Kostant.envelopingAction_comp_enveloping`).
   Sums and left multiples are handled by the ideal axioms.

3. **Transported count** (`pow_le_finrank_map_of_equiv`). Apply `pow_le_finrank_map` to
   `π ∘ e⁻¹`, `ℓ`, and `T ∘ (enveloping e)⁻¹`. Its hypothesis holds: if `u ∈ U(GLProd)_d` and
   `T((enveloping e)⁻¹ u) = 0`, then `(enveloping e)⁻¹ u ∈ U(L)_d` by step 1. By the assumption on
   `T` it lies in the central ideal of `π`, and step 2 puts `u` in `centralIdeal n (π ∘ e⁻¹)`.
   Finally `(T ∘ (enveloping e)⁻¹)(U(GLProd)_d) = T(U(L)_d)` by step 1.

4. **Borel eigenfunctional** (`exists_isBorelEigen`). For a complex upper triangular `X` in
   `GLProd ℂ (fun _ => n)`, the real and imaginary parts `X_re`, `X_im` of its entries are real
   upper triangular, and `(toGL n)⁻¹ X = 1 ⊗ X_re + i ⊗ X_im` (checked entrywise with
   `toGL_tmul` and `re + im·i`). So `(toGL n)⁻¹ X` lies in the submodule `scalarSubmodule π ℓ`.
   Apply this to the basis vectors `E_c` with `c ∈ upper` (`isBUpper_glBasis`), and choose
   the scalars `μ_c`. Put `μ_c = 0` off `upper`.

5. **Evaluation at `1` is nonzero** (`kFiniteEval_one_ne_zero`). Let `f ≠ 0` in `I(ds)` and
   suppose evaluation at `1` vanishes. For `k ∈ O(n)` it vanishes on `ρ(k) f`, so
   `f(k) = (ρ(k) f)(1) = 0`. Thus `f` vanishes on `O(n)`. Since `f` lies in the principal series
   (Library `realStandardModule_le_principalSeries`), Library
   `eq_zero_of_mem_principalSeries_of_eqOn_unitaryCompact` gives `f = 0`, a contradiction.

6. **Lower bound** (`realStandardCentralGrowth`). Let `ℓ = kFiniteEval 1`. By Library
   `tmul_mem_scalarSubmodule` (with `realStandardModule_le_principalSeries`), `c ⊗ X` acts on `ℓ`
   by a scalar for every real upper triangular `X`. Step 4 gives `μ` with
   `IsBorelEigen (realStandardLie n ds ∘ (toGL n)⁻¹) ℓ μ`, and step 5 gives `ℓ ≠ 0`. For one
   block of size `n`, `|offLast| = n(n-1)` (`card_offLast`). Step 3 with `e = toGL n` gives
   `(d + 1)^{n(n-1)} ≤ (n(n-1) + 1)^{n(n-1)} · finrank ℂ (T(U_d))`, which is the claim for
   `c = 1 / (n(n-1) + 1)^{n(n-1)}`.

7. **Reduction** (`realLinkedLanglandsQuotientNotLarge_of_quotientGrowth`). Take `n`, `ds` with
   a linked pair, a proper `(𝔤, K)`-submodule `R` that contains every proper
   `(𝔤, K)`-submodule, and suppose `I(ds)` is maximally faithful modulo `R`. The statement
   `RealLinkedQuotientGrowthStatement` gives a proper `(𝔤, K)`-submodule `W` and `C` with
   `finrank ℂ (T(U_d)) ≤ C (d + 1)^{n(n-1) - 1}` for `T(u) = (v ↦ u v mod W)`. By maximality,
   `W ≤ R`. Since `R ≠ ⊤`, `I(ds) ≠ 0`, so step 6 gives `c > 0`. Its hypothesis holds for this `T`:
   if `T u = 0`, then `u v ∈ W ⊆ R` for all `v`. Maximal faithfulness puts `u` in the ideal
   generated by the central `z` with `z · I(ds) ⊆ R`. Each such `z` acts by `0`: `I(ds)` has an
   infinitesimal character (Library `realStandardLie_hasInfinitesimalCharacter`), and Library
   `MaximallyFaithfulAnnihilator.envelopingAction_eq_zero_of_center` applies because `R ≠ ⊤`. So
   `u ∈ realStandardCentralIdeal n ds`. A linked pair forces `n ≥ 2` (Library `two_le_of_not_unlinked`),
   so `n(n-1) ≥ 1`. The bounds `c (d + 1)^{n(n-1)} ≤ finrank ≤ C (d + 1)^{n(n-1) - 1}` for all
   `d` contradict Library `RealLinkedQuotientGrowth.not_forall_le`.
