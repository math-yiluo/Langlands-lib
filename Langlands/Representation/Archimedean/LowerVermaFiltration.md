# Weights, central characters and filtrations for representations of `𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)`

Module `Langlands.Representation.Archimedean.LowerVermaFiltration`, namespace
`Langlands.Archimedean.VermaPBW`. It imports `LowerVermaPBW` and the Library module `BGChaseReps`.

This module collects generic tools used by the proofs of `LowerVermaHomBoundStatement`
(module `LowerVermaHomBound`) and `LowerVermaProjectiveStatement` (module
`LowerVermaProjective`). It proves no Library statement itself. Everything is proved, with no
new axioms.

Throughout, `ρ` is a representation of `𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)` on a complex vector space `V`, and
`envelopingAction ρ` is the induced algebra map `U(𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)) → End V`.

## 1. Weights and invariant subspaces (section `Rep`)

* `rootOf n c` is `ε_p - ε_q` for `c = (p, q)`. `shiftOf n l` is the sum of `rootOf` over the
  letters of a list `l` (`shiftOf_cons`).
* `IsWt n ρ v μ` means `E_pp v = μ_p v` for all `p`.
* `env_W`, `env_W_cons`: the enveloping action of a word `W n l` (`LowerVermaPBW`) is the
  product of the `ρ(E_c)`.
* `unitE_diag_eq`, `lie_unitE_diag`: `[E_pp, E_c] = (ε_{c.1} - ε_{c.2})_p E_c`.
  - `isWt_unitE`: `E_c` maps weight `μ` to weight `μ + rootOf c`.
  - `isWt_env_W`: the same for words.
  - `isWt_unique`: a nonzero vector has at most one weight.
* `IsInv n ρ F` means `F` is stable under all `ρ(E_c)`. Such an `F` is stable under the whole
  enveloping algebra (`env_W_mem`, `env_mem`, via the spanning basis `basisU`). Further:
  - `isInv_bot`: `⊥` is invariant.
  - `isInv_span`: the span of a set is invariant if the `E_c` map the set into its span.
* `env_ideal_mem`: suppose `G` is singular of weight `μ` modulo an invariant `F`, that is,
  `E_pq G ∈ F` for `q < p` and `E_pp G - μ_p G ∈ F`. Then every element of
  `VermaGL.lowerVermaIdeal n μ` maps `G` into `F`. So `u · gen ↦ u · G` is well defined from
  `M⁻(μ)` to `V / F`.
* `mem_span_eigen`: let `x` be in the span of eigenvectors of `T` with `T x = c x`. Then `x` is
  in the span of those eigenvectors with eigenvalue `c`. The proof uses
  `Module.End.eigenspaces_iSupIndep`. `mem_span_wt` is the joint-weight version, by induction
  over the coordinates `p`.

## 2. Central characters (section `Central`)

* `center_smul_gen`: a central `z` acts on `gen μ` by a scalar, because `z • gen μ` has weight
  `μ` (`lowerVermaWeight`).
* `sub_mem_lowerVermaIdeal`: then `z - c` lies in the defining ideal of `M⁻(μ)`.
* `env_center_sub_mem`: if `G` is singular of weight `μ` modulo `F` and
  `z • gen μ = c • gen μ`, then `z G - c G ∈ F`.

## 3. Weight bases (section `WeightBasis`)

Here `σ` is a representation on `W`.

* `exists_weight_basis`: if `σ` has integral weights (`HasIntegralWeights`) and `W` is
  finite-dimensional, then `W` has a basis `b : Fin D → W` with `σ(E_pp) b_k = m_{k,p} b_k`,
  where `m_{k,p} ∈ ℤ`. This is the Library's `BGChase.exists_wtBasis`, reindexed by `Fin D`.
* `wtC n m k` is the weight of `b k` as a complex vector. `htZ n m k = Σ_p (-p) m_{k,p}` is its
  height.
  - `htZ_cast`, `sum_dZero_rootOf`, `sum_dZero_shiftOf` evaluate heights through `dZero`.
  - `eq_nil_of_shiftOf_eq_zero`: a list of strictly upper indices with total weight `0` is
    empty.
  - `htZ_lt_of_wtC_eq`: if `wt(b k) = wt(b i) + (ε_p - ε_q)` with `q < p`, then
    `htZ k < htZ i`.
* `coord_unitE_diag` (from the Library's `BGChase.coord_comp_of_isWt`), `isWt_b`,
  `wtC_eq_of_coord_ne_zero`: the `k`-th coordinate of a weight vector of weight `μ` vanishes
  unless `wt(b k) = μ`. `wtC_eq_of_lower_coeff` applies this to
  `σ(E_c) b_i`.
* `shiftOf_mem_posRootCone`: the total weight of a list of strictly upper indices lies in
  `Q₊ = VermaGL.posRootCone n`.

## 4. The cone `Q₊` (section `Cone`)

* `exists_shiftOf_of_mem_posRootCone`: every element of `Q₊` is `shiftOf` of a list of
  strictly upper indices (induction over the closure).
* `posRootCone_antisymm`: `Q₊ ∩ -Q₊ = 0`. If `β = shiftOf l` and `-β = shiftOf l'`, then
  `shiftOf (l ++ l') = 0`, so `l ++ l' = []`.

## 5. Filtrations generated by weight vectors (section `FiltrationG`)

Let `G : ι → V` be a family of vectors and `T` a finite set of indices.

* `gensG n ρ G T` is the set of words `E_{c₁} ⋯ E_{c_r} · G j` with `j ∈ T`. `FG n ρ G T` is its
  span, an invariant subspace (`isInv_FG`). Also `FG_mono`, `G_mem_FG`, `FG_empty`.
* `PG n ρ G k` is the span of the words in strictly upper letters applied to `G k`.

Assume `G j` has weight `μ j` for every `j` (`hG`). Fix `k ∈ T` such that `G k` is singular
modulo `FG (T ∖ k)`, that is, `E_pq G_k ∈ FG (T ∖ k)` for `q < p` (`hlow`).

* `isWt_gensG`: the generators are weight vectors.
* `envG_lower_mem`: words in lower letters map `G k` into `FG (T ∖ k) + ℂ G k`.
* `envG_mem_sup`: every `u · G k` lies in `FG (T ∖ k) + PG k`. Write `u` in the basis
  `basisU`; there `E^α` is an upper word times a lower word. `FG_le_sup`:
  `FG T ≤ FG (T ∖ k) + PG k`.
* `mem_FG_weight` (weight separation): a weight vector of weight `ν` in `FG T` lies in
  `FG (T ∖ k)` plus the span of the upper words `w · G k` with `μ k + shiftOf w = ν`. This
  combines `FG_le_sup` with `mem_span_wt`; the zero vectors are dropped by `isWt_unique`.
* `FG_center_sub_mem`: for central `z` with `z • gen (μ k) = d • gen (μ k)`, `z x - d x` lies
  in `FG (T ∖ k)` for every `x ∈ FG T`. On a generator `w · G k` this is
  `w · (z G_k - d G_k)`, using `env_center_sub_mem`. Generators with `j ≠ k` already lie in
  `FG (T ∖ k)`.
* `FG_center_pow_sub_mem`: `(z - c)^N x - (d - c)^N x ∈ FG (T ∖ k)`, by induction on `N`.

## Provenance

These are standard facts about weight vectors, invariant subspaces and quotients of Verma
modules, written for this formalization. The filtration of §5 is the usual one whose
successive quotients are quotients of Verma modules (Bernstein–Gelfand 1980, 1.9(f);
literature id `bernstein-gelfand-1980-tensor-products`). It is handled through singular
vectors modulo the previous step rather than through explicit isomorphisms.
