# Strict dominance for the other constituents of real standard modules

For a list of real standard blocks in Langlands order, repeat the real
part of each inducing exponent by its block rank. For two lists of
the same total rank, `RealLanglandsStrictlyBelow es ds` says the
resulting lists have equal total sum, every initial sum for `es` is
at most that for `ds`, and at least one is strictly smaller.

`RealLanglandsConstituentDominanceStatement` states that an irreducible
admissible quotient of a proper \((\mathfrak g,K)\)-stable subspace of
the compact-finite standard module of `ds` has smaller Langlands
exponents in this sense. The list `es` supplies its Langlands quotient
presentation by a nonzero intertwiner and is also in Langlands order.

For a real general linear group these initial sums express the
dominance comparison on the real diagonal exponent parameters. This
formulation is based on Casselman, *Canonical extensions of Harish-Chandra
modules to representations of \(G\)* (1989), Proposition 4.9, p. 406,
which compares the Langlands parameter of a constituent in the maximal
proper submodule with the original inducing parameter.
