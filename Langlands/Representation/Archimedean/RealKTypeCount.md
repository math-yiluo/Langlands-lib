# Counting `O(n)`-types

Module `Langlands.Representation.Archimedean.RealKTypeCount`.

## Main result

`Langlands.Archimedean.RealKTypeCount.surjective_of_injective`. Let `V` and `W` be complex
subspaces of principal series of `GL_n(ℝ)`: `V ≤ principalSeries ℝ n χ` and
`W ≤ principalSeries ℝ n χ'` for some families of characters `χ`, `χ'`. Let

* `T` be an injective linear map from `kFiniteFunctions V (unitaryCompact ℝ n)` to
  `kFiniteFunctions W (unitaryCompact ℝ n)`, and
* `A` be an injective linear map in the opposite direction,

both commuting with right translation by `O(n)` in the sense of `IsRealKEquivariant` below.
Then `T` is surjective.

Informally: `A` and `T` show that every `O(n)`-type has the same finite multiplicity in the two
spaces, so an injective equivariant map is bijective. The proof does not use multiplicities
directly. It compares the dimensions of a filtration by finite-dimensional layers, so it needs
no admissibility statement.

## Definitions

* `RealKTypeCount.restrictLinear n`: restriction of a function on `GL_n(ℝ)` to `O(n)`, as a
  complex linear map to the functions on `Matrix.unitaryGroup (Fin n) ℝ`, through
  `Unitary.toUnits`.
* `RealKTypeCount.linearSpan n`: the span of the constant `1` and the `n²` matrix entries
  `OrthogonalFinitePolynomial.entry n (i, j)`, as functions on `O(n)`.
* `RealKTypeCount.degreeLE n N = linearSpan n ^ N` (power of submodules in the algebra of
  functions on `O(n)`): the polynomials of total degree at most `N` in the matrix entries.
* `RealKTypeCount.rightAlgHom k`, `RealKTypeCount.leftAlgHom k`: right and left translation by
  `k ∈ O(n)` on functions on `O(n)`, `φ ↦ (x ↦ φ (x * k))` and `φ ↦ (x ↦ φ (k * x))`, as
  algebra homomorphisms.
* `IsRealKEquivariant V W T` (a `Prop`): for every `k ∈ unitaryCompact ℝ n` and every
  `O(n)`-finite function `f` of `V`, the right translates `R_k f` and `R_k (T f)`
  (`Whittaker.rightTranslation`) are `O(n)`-finite functions of `V` and of `W`, and
  `T (R_k f) = R_k (T f)`. This is the first clause of `IsRealIntertwinerModulo n ds es ⊥ q`;
  `IsRealIntertwinerModulo.isRealKEquivariant` records that.
* `RealKTypeCount.layer V U`: for a subspace `U` of functions on `O(n)`, the
  `O(n)`-finite functions of `V` whose restriction to `O(n)` lies in `U`.

These are auxiliary constructions with internal provenance. They serve this counting argument
and correspond to no single definition of the literature.

## Lemmas and proofs

* `fg_linearSpan`, `finiteDimensional_degreeLE`: `linearSpan n` is finitely generated, so its
  powers are finitely generated (`Submodule.FG.pow`) and `degreeLE n N` is finite-dimensional.
* `degreeLE_mono`: the layers increase with `N`, since `1 ≤ linearSpan n`.
* `exists_mem_degreeLE`: every element of `OrthogonalFinitePolynomial.polynomialFunctions n`
  (the subalgebra generated by the entries) lies in some `degreeLE n N`. The union of the
  `degreeLE n N` is a subalgebra containing the entries.
* `map_rightAlgHom_linearSpan_le`, `map_leftAlgHom_linearSpan_le`, `right_mem_degreeLE`,
  `left_mem_degreeLE`: right translation by `k` sends the entry `(i, j)` to
  `∑ l, k l j · entry (i, l)`, and left translation sends it to `∑ l, k i l · entry (l, j)`.
  So translation preserves `linearSpan n`. Since translations are algebra homomorphisms, they
  preserve its powers (`Submodule.map_pow`).
* `restrict_injective`: on the `O(n)`-finite functions of `V ≤ principalSeries ℝ n χ`,
  restriction to `O(n)` is injective, by
  `eq_zero_of_mem_principalSeries_of_eqOn_unitaryCompact` (Iwasawa decomposition).
* `finiteDimensional_layer`: hence `layer V U` embeds into `U` and is finite-dimensional when
  `U` is.
* `mem_layer_degreeLE_of_isRealKEquivariant`: an equivariant `T` maps `layer V (degreeLE n N)`
  into `layer W (degreeLE n N)` when `V` lies in a principal series. On the finite-dimensional
  layer of `V`, the point evaluations `f ↦ f(κ)`, `κ ∈ O(n)`, separate points. So the
  functional `f ↦ (T f)(1)` agrees there with a finite combination `∑ i, c i · f(p i)`
  (`exists_sum_of_separating`). The layer is stable under right translation, and
  `(T f)(κ) = (R_κ (T f))(1) = (T (R_κ f))(1)`. Hence `(T f)(κ) = ∑ i, c i · f(p i * κ)`: the
  restriction of `T f` is a combination of left translates of the restriction of `f`, which
  lie in `degreeLE n N`.
* `exists_mem_layer_degreeLE`: every `O(n)`-finite function `f` of `V ≤ principalSeries ℝ n χ`
  lies in some degree layer. `f` is continuous (smooth), and the right translates of its
  restriction to `O(n)` lie in the image of the finite-dimensional span of its right
  translates by `O(n)`. So the restriction is a polynomial function
  (`OrthogonalFinitePolynomial.mem_polynomialFunctions_of_finiteDimensional`), and
  `exists_mem_degreeLE` applies.
* `surjective_of_injective`: given `g` in the `O(n)`-finite functions of `W`, choose `N` with
  `g` in the layer `N` of `W`. `T` and `A` restrict to injective maps between the
  finite-dimensional layers `N` of `V` and of `W`, so the layers have equal dimensions
  (`LinearMap.finrank_le_finrank_of_injective` both ways). The restriction of `T` is then
  injective with range of full dimension, hence surjective
  (`LinearMap.finrank_range_of_inj`, `Submodule.eq_top_of_finrank_eq`). So `g` is in the
  image of `T`.
