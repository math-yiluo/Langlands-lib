# Localization of a nonnegative compact standard-module diagonal

Suppose two operators on real standard vectors commute with left block-invariant compact multipliers. Consider the integral over the full compact group of the value of the first operator times the conjugate of the value of the second operator, with a fixed complex scalar. If this integral is real and nonnegative for every standard vector, the same holds when the integral is restricted to Haar probability measure on the compact Levi subgroup.

For a block-invariant real polynomial weight, the standard compact multiplier theorem provides an actual weighted standard vector. Its sign matrices are diagonal and each required rotation lies wholly inside one Levi block. Commutation with the weight multiplies the diagonal integrand by the square of the weight. Apply real compact localization to the real part of the integrand and to both signs of its imaginary part. Continuity on the compact subgroups gives the integrability needed to exchange each part with integration.

This internal lemma gives the localization step in positivity descent through real induction in stages.
