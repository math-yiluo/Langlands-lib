# Density of pure smooth tensors for the product action

`SmoothProjectiveProductDensityStatement` concerns a finite family of
finite-dimensional real Banach algebras and jointly continuous representations
of their groups of units on complex Banach spaces. The complex span of pure
tensors of individual genuine smooth vectors is dense in the genuine smooth
part of the completed Banach projective tensor product for the product-group
action. The density uses the topology of all word derivatives in the product
algebra.

Individual smooth vectors are norm dense by Banach orbit smoothing. The
product of their inclusion maps has dense range in the finite product of the
individual Banach spaces. Continuity of the pure tensor map shows that the
norm closure of the image of the pure smooth span contains every pure Banach
tensor. Algebraic tensors are spanned by the pure tensors and are norm dense
in the completion, so the image of the pure smooth span is norm dense.

The actual product action preserves the span and its closure for the word
topology. Compact smooth averaging maps the Banach carrier continuously into
the genuine smooth carrier and preserves closed invariant smooth submodules.
The image of each such averaging operator lies in the smooth closure of the
span by norm density. Kernels near identity approximate each smooth vector
in every prescribed finite set of word coordinates, so this closure is the
whole smooth carrier. In particular, a continuous linear form for the
word topology is determined by its values on the pure smooth tensors.

This is a derived density result for the product action. The use of the
smooth projective tensor product for pairing continuous Whittaker
functionals is in the proof of Lemma 14.1, p. 106 of the author-posted edition.
[H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009)](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf#page=106).
