# The Borel factor in the real Cartan trace

For a positive block-scalar diagonal and a block-diagonal orthogonal left factor, the absolute projected Cartan determinant is the product of the normalized pair determinants times the Borel half-density. The radial principal-series weight of the ordered real blocks has that same Borel half-density times the unshifted complex block twists.

Each rectangular action contributes the ratio of its column-block radius to its row-block radius raised to the number of rectangle entries. Its square root supplies half of the corresponding logarithmic radius difference for each entry. The sum over lower matrix entries gives exactly the Borel shift at each diagonal position. Within each block the radius is constant, so the within-block differences vanish. The two shifted position exponents of an induced block have opposite weight increments, leaving twice its determinant-twist exponent in the unshifted radial factor.
