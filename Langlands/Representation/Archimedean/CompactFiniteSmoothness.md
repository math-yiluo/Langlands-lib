# Genuine smoothness of norm compact-finite vectors

For a continuous finite-dimensional matrix representation \(\tau\) of a
topological group, `compactInverseCoordinateWeight` takes a vector \(u\)
to the scalar function \(k\mapsto(\tau(k^{-1})u)_i\). On the left, a
translate of the function is obtained by acting on \(u\) in the matrix
representation.

Consider a jointly continuous complex Banach representation of the units
of a finite-dimensional real Banach algebra with a Borel Haar measure.
Let a compact subgroup have a left invariant Borel probability measure,
and assume admissibility for that subgroup. A norm compact-finite vector
\(v\) generates a finite-dimensional compact orbit span, with continuous
coordinate matrices. For each basis vector \(b_i\) of the span, average
its compact orbit with the \(i\)-th inverse-coordinate weight of \(v\).
Each of these averaging operators has finite-dimensional range by
admissibility, and its entire range is genuinely smooth. At each compact
group element the sum of the integrands is
\(R(k)R(k^{-1})v=v\), by basis expansion. Normalization of the compact
measure therefore gives \(v\) as a finite sum of smooth vectors.

This internal argument identifies the norm compact-finite vectors with
the compact-finite vectors of the genuine smooth carrier.
