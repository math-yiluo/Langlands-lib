# A necessary exponent bound for unitarity of a real rank-two principal series

Worker 848.

`RealGLTwoPrincipalSeriesUnitaryBoundStatement` concerns the full normalized
standard module induced from two characters of `GL₁(ℝ)`. The hypothesis is
infinitesimal unitarity on its `O(2)`-finite vectors. If the two complex
exponents are \(c\) and \(d\), the conclusion is
\(\lvert\operatorname{Re}(c-d)\rvert<1\), with
\(\operatorname{Re}(c-d)=0\) when the inducing characters have opposite
parities. Together with a zero real central exponent
\(\operatorname{Re}(c+d)=0\), this gives the strict bound \(1/2\) for each
inducing exponent in the two-character case.

This statement comes from the compact-model Lie algebra calculation for the
Library's `realStandardRep` and `realStandardLie`. The hypothesis uses the
positive Hermitian form of Knapp–Vogan, *Cohomological Induction and Unitary
Representations* (1995), display (0.5), printed p. 6 (PDF p. 28). For the
common-unitary-character family with twists \(t\) and \(-t\), the exponent
difference is \(2t\). This is the rank-two case of the normalization in
Vogan, *The unitary dual of GL(n) over an archimedean field* (1986),
Definition 2.3 and Lemma 2.4(d)–(e), printed p. 452 (PDF p. 4), and
Proposition 2.5, printed p. 453 (PDF p. 5). The opposite-parity conclusion
comes from the rotation modes in the Library's compact model.
