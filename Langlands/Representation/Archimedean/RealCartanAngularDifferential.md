# Differential of the angular Levi frame

The angular frame in the full real orthogonal group is a smooth matrix function of the within-block lower coordinates. At zero its derivative fills the within-block skew matrix from those coordinates.

An orthogonal line contributes a constant and has an empty lower coordinate space. On a plane the derivative of the rotation at zero is its skew generator, with lower entry one. Assembling the block matrices preserves skew symmetry and gives the specified within-block coordinate entries and zero off-block entries.
