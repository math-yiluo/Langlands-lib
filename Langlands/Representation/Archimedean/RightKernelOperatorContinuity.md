# Operator-norm continuity of right translates of compact averages

Consider a jointly continuous complex Banach representation of the unit group of a finite-dimensional real Banach algebra. Equip the group with a right-invariant Borel measure finite on compact sets. The uniform boundedness principle bounds the original operators uniformly on each compact subset of the group. Precomposing a compact continuous kernel average with the original inverse action moves the right translate to the inverse kernel by a right-invariant change of variables.

Near the identity, all of these translated weights have a common compact support bound. Evaluating the weights there gives a continuous map into the sup-norm space of continuous scalar functions on that compact set. A norm integral estimate bounds the difference of the translated and original averaging operators by the sup-norm difference of the weights, times the uniform operator bound and the compact set's measure. The precomposed operator therefore converges in operator norm at the identity. The estimate is an internal consequence of joint continuity of the original action, compactness, and right invariance of the measure.
