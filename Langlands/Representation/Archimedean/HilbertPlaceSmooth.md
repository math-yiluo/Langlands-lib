# Smooth comparison at a commuting fixed level

Let a local Lie group act on a Hilbert space that realizes a specified
compact and Lie module. Let the global Lie group and a further group act
on a second Hilbert space, with commuting actions. A bounded local
intertwiner into the second space takes values fixed by a supplied
subgroup of the further group. The local inclusion and global action
satisfy the smooth commuting splitting and unitarity hypotheses.

If the image of a nonzero algebraic sample under the intertwiner is
smooth for the global action, the theorem obtains a continuous linear
map from the genuine local smooth vectors to genuine global smooth vectors
in the fixed Hilbert subspace. Its ambient value is the original bounded
intertwiner. Both smooth spaces use their word-derivative topologies.

For the proof, injectivity of the algebraic embedding gives a nonzero
Hilbert sample, and compact-finite recovery gives a nonzero finite vector.
Normalize the sample and apply bounded Hilbert intertwiner evaluation.
The fixed subspace is complete and its restriction has the same inner
product as the ambient representation. Smoothness of the evaluated
sample in the fixed restriction follows from the fixed-subspace
comparison. The split-group smooth extension supplies the required map.

This internal comparison composes the Library's Hilbert realization,
fixed-level and bounded evaluation interfaces.
