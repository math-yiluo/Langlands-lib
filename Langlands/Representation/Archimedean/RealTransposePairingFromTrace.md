# The real algebraic pairing from invariant Hilbert character traces

`HasTransposeInvariantSmoothKernelTrace` requires, for every smooth
compactly supported complex kernel, trace class of the averaged operator
at the ordinary-transposed kernel and equality of its trace with the trace
at the original kernel. The averaging convention is the inverse kernel.
The Hilbert continuous dual covariance gives equality of these kernel
traces for the original action and its inverse-transpose continuous dual.

Take a compatible real Hilbert globalization of an irreducible admissible
algebraic module, with unitary orthogonal action. The group measure is
regular right invariant Borel Haar, and the orthogonal subgroup carries
inversion invariant Borel Haar probability measure. Norm density of the
algebraic embedding follows from density in the genuine word topology and
norm density of all smooth vectors.

Restriction identifies the genuine smooth compact-finite vectors of the
twisted continuous dual with the algebraic finite dual. On the orthogonal
subgroup inverse transpose is the identity. For the derived comparison,
the inverse of inverse transpose of \(\exp(tX)\) is \(\exp(tX^t)\).
Differentiating evaluation of the two actual exponential orbits yields
the Lie action of the algebraic finite dual twisted by negative ordinary
transpose. The two signs in the algebraic dual and the twist cancel on
real elementary tensors.

Real compact trace separation now identifies the original algebraic
module with this twisted finite dual. Evaluating the identified
functional on the first variable gives a complex bilinear form. The
compact and Lie intertwining identities give both invariance conditions.
Injectivity of the equivalence and nontriviality of the original module
give a nonzero form.

This internal reduction handles the algebraic reconstruction from the
indicated transpose-invariant Hilbert character traces.
