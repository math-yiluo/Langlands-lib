# Norm closure of compact-invariant subspaces on compact-finite vectors

This internal comparison uses the actual compact-group action on a complex Banach space and the weighted orbit integrals of [CompactWeightedOrbit](CompactWeightedOrbit.md). It extends the coordinate reconstruction used in [CompactFiniteSmoothness](CompactFiniteSmoothness.md) to a norm-closure argument. No invariant inner product is needed.

For a jointly continuous bounded-operator action `R` of a topological group `G`, restrict it to a compact subgroup `K`. On a finite-dimensional subrepresentation `S` of that restriction, `continuous_normCompact_subMatrix` proves continuity of the matrices in the chosen basis. Each matrix entry is a continuous coordinate functional on the finite-dimensional normed subspace applied to a continuous orbit. `compactReconstructionWeight` is the continuous function `k ↦ (R(k⁻¹)v)ᵢ` for a vector `v` of `S`, using the chosen coordinates of `S`.

With a left-invariant probability measure on `K`, `sum_compactReconstructionWeight` writes `v` as the sum of the averages of the basis vectors with these weights. Before integration, the sum is the constant vector `R(k)R(k⁻¹)v = v` by basis expansion. For an admissible action, each of the weighted averaging operators in this formula has finite-dimensional range by `finiteDimensional_range_compactWeightedOrbit`, since its weight comes from a finite-dimensional continuous translation type.

`mem_compactSubrepresentation_of_mem_closure` proves that if `W` is a `K`-invariant subspace all of whose vectors are compact-finite, a compact-finite vector in the **norm** closure of `W` already belongs to `W`. An average of an element of `W` stays in its finite-dimensional orbit span: that span is closed, and the integral vanishes in the quotient by it. A finite-range average therefore maps the closure of `W` into `W`, since the intersection of `W` with the range is finite-dimensional and closed. The orbit span of the given vector lies in the closure by compact invariance. Applying the reconstruction formula to its basis puts all summands in `W` and hence the vector itself in `W`.

This supplies the closure-to-algebraic step for compact-finite submodules in the Harish-Chandra comparison. It does not prove density of the compact-finite vectors or invariance of the closure under the full group from infinitesimal invariance.
