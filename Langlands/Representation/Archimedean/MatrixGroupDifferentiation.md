# Fréchet differentiation on the complex matrix group from exponential curves

A complex-valued function on the group has an ambient real Fréchet derivative when its derivatives along the right exponential curves are given by a continuous field of real continuous linear maps. At a matrix `g`, the ambient derivative applies the field at `g` to the tangent direction multiplied on the left by `g⁻¹`.

In exponential coordinates based at `g`, subtract the proposed linear term and restrict to the segment from zero to a small matrix `X`. The scalar derivative of the remainder is bounded by the norm of `X` times the oscillation of the derivative field along the segment. Continuity makes that oscillation arbitrarily small. The mean value inequality gives the little-o estimate. The inverse function theorem transfers the derivative to the open matrix chart, since the exponential has derivative the identity at zero and left multiplication by `g` is invertible.
