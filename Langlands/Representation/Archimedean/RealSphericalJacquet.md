# Real Jacquet functionals on a spherical principal series

These internal lemmas construct a normalized spherical vector and describe
the Jacquet functionals on a spherical real principal series in the closed
Langlands chamber. The constant function on the compact group extends to
a finite vector by principal-series extension. Compact restriction
determines the extension, so the vector is fixed by the compact group.

The Frobenius map of a nonzero Jacquet functional intertwines the compact
and Lie actions. Its kernel is Lie-stable, so spherical cyclicity implies
that the map takes a nonzero value at every nonzero spherical vector.
This image is compact-fixed and is determined by its value at identity,
which is the original functional's value at the spherical vector.
The sign-reflection equations then force every Jacquet sign to be trivial.
