# Specialization of the four-character reversal at two resonances

This internal argument is the specialization used for negative inner weight.
The chronological word `102102` on \([x,y,u,v]\) uses the masked high inverse
at the first step, the shifted generic multipliers at the next two steps,
and the shifted regularized tail at the fourth. The two final choices are
fixed. The characters and the underlying standard modules stay fixed during
the variation of the multiplier parameters.

For a nonzero imaginary shift, all four varying generic denominators have
nonzero imaginary part. The assumed parameter relations at zero imply
the additive relations off zero. On the starting high Fourier modes the
first normalized crossing is the generic crossing divided by the shift.
The fourth is the generic crossing multiplied by the shift. These factors
cancel in the word. The conditional four-character reversal then identifies
it with `021021`, whose first two crossings kill the given vector.

Every vector belongs to a finite compact degree layer. Each Frobenius map
preserves the degree bound and takes modewise analytic multipliers and weakly
analytic families to weakly analytic families on the next layer. Restricting
the shift to the imaginary line gives continuous scalar evaluations at zero.
Uniqueness of limits in each compact evaluation proves vanishing at zero.
There the first and fourth maps are nonzero multiples of the inverse canonical
tail and the canonical tail respectively. Removing the nonzero scalars gives
vanishing of the specialized composite. The compact polynomial braid remains
an explicit premise here.
