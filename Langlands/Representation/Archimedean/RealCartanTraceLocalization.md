# Localization of the weighted compact Cartan trace

For Levi blocks of sizes one and two, suppose that the identity compact argument occurs over a finite family of disjoint compact block cosets. On each coset suppose the compact argument is one exactly at its specified translated left factor. A family of continuous tests supported eventually in every identity neighborhood then gives an exact sum of integrals in opposed and angular coordinates. The coordinates in each summand can be restricted to any fixed positive joint radius at most one and pi. The common positive chart coefficient depends on the block sizes.

The proof chooses continuous cutoffs equal to one on neighborhoods of the fixed cosets and supported where the lower chart coordinates are bounded. Compactness makes the complementary tests vanish eventually. Right Haar invariance translates the two compact factors to their chart centers. The determinant-one angular component contains all the fixed left factors after this translation. A second compactness argument removes the rest of the angular components. Inside the centered angle cube the identity frame has only the zero preimage, and the trailing-minor bound forces zero lower block coordinates at the fixed coset. Compact excision therefore shrinks the resulting product integral to the stated joint ball.
