# Growth of `U(𝔤)` modulo the central ideal of a module with a Borel eigenfunctional

Namespace `Langlands.Archimedean.Kostant.BorelCentralGrowth`. The setting and notation are those
of `Kostant/BorelCentralIdeal`: `𝔤 = GLProd ℂ n = ∏ᵢ 𝔤𝔩_{n i}(ℂ)`, `U = U(𝔤)` with degree
filtration `U_d`, a representation `π` of `𝔤` on `V`, and `centralIdeal n π`, the ideal of `U`
generated by the central elements acting by `0`.

## Definitions

* `offLast n` is the finite set of coordinates `⟨i, p, q⟩` with `p + 1 < n i`, the coordinates
  outside the last row of their block. `card_offLast`: its cardinality is `∑ᵢ n i (n i - 1)`
  (block `i` contributes `n i - 1` rows of length `n i`, counted with Mathlib's
  `Fin.card_filter_val_lt`).
* `E α` is the ordered monomial `orderedMonomial ℂ 𝔤 (glBasis ℂ n) (sortedCoords n) α` for an
  exponent `α : Coord n →₀ ℕ` (Library `Kostant/OrderedMonomials`, `Kostant/Matumoto`).
* `box n e` is the finite set of exponents `α` with `α c ≤ e` for `c ∈ offLast n` and `α c = 0`
  otherwise. `card_box`: it has `(e + 1)^{|offLast n|}` elements. `degree_le_of_mem_box`: its
  elements have total degree at most `|offLast n| · e`.

## Main result

`pow_le_finrank_map`: assume `IsBorelEigen π ℓ μ` and `ℓ ≠ 0` (Kostant/BorelCentralIdeal). Let
`T : U →ₗ[ℂ] F` be linear and `d : ℕ`, and assume that every `u ∈ U_d` with `T u = 0` lies in
`centralIdeal n π`. Then, with `m = |offLast n|`, in `ℝ`

`(d + 1)^m ≤ (m + 1)^m · finrank ℂ (T(U_d))`.

## Provenance

The module is internal to Task 7070. It is the counting half of the lower bound for the growth of
`U(𝔤)` modulo the central ideal. The independence of ordered monomials outside the last rows is
modelled on the classical fact that the coordinates outside one row form a transversal to the
nilpotent cone (Kostant 1963), here obtained from the Library's Gröbner basis of
`charpolyIdeal n` (Kostant/NilpotentCone, Kostant/CoprimeGroebner).

## Proof

1. **Gröbner step** (`not_mem_charpolyIdeal`). Let `f ≠ 0` be a polynomial whose monomials only
   involve the coordinates of `offLast n`. The ideal `charpolyIdeal n` is generated by the monic
   polynomials `gen n j` (Library `charpolyIdeal_eq_span_gen`), whose leading monomials
   `antidiag n b k` for the order `ord n` have pairwise disjoint supports. By Library
   `CoprimeGroebner.exists_degree_le`, every nonzero element of the ideal has a leading monomial
   divisible by some `antidiag n b k`. That monomial contains the variable `⟨b, n b - 1, k⟩` of the
   last row of block `b`. The leading monomial of `f` does not, so `f ∉ charpolyIdeal n`.

2. **Top coefficients** (`top_coeff_eq_zero`). Let `P = ∑_{α ∈ S} a_α E α ∈ centralIdeal n π`,
   where every `α ∈ S` is supported in `offLast n` and has degree at most `N`. Then `P ∈ U_N`, and
   `σ_N(P) = ∑_{α ∈ S, |α| = N} a_α X^α` (Library `symbol_orderedMonomial`). By
   `BorelCentralIdeal.symbol_mem_charpolyIdeal`, `σ_N(P) ∈ charpolyIdeal n`, so step 1 forces
   `σ_N(P) = 0`, that is, `a_α = 0` whenever `|α| = N`.

3. **Linear independence** (`coeff_eq_zero`). By induction on `N`, all `a_α` vanish. Step 2 kills
   the coefficients of degree `N`, and the remaining sum, over the `α` of degree at most `N - 1`,
   is handled by the induction hypothesis.

4. **Counting** (`pow_le_finrank_map`). Put `e = d / (m + 1)` (integer division), so that
   `m e ≤ d` and `d + 1 ≤ (m + 1)(e + 1)`. For `α ∈ box n e`, `E α ∈ U_d`. The family
   `T (E α)`, `α ∈ box n e`, is linearly independent. Indeed, a vanishing combination `∑ g_α T(E α)`
   makes `∑ g_α E α` an element of `U_d` killed by `T`, hence an element of `centralIdeal n π`,
   and step 3 gives `g = 0`. So `finrank ℂ (T(U_d)) ≥ (e + 1)^m`. Then
   `(d + 1)^m ≤ ((m + 1)(e + 1))^m = (m + 1)^m (e + 1)^m ≤ (m + 1)^m finrank ℂ (T(U_d))`.
