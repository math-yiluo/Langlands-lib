# Normalization of the finite real Cartan placement sum

Suppose the original ordered block list has the total rank of the source Cartan. Fix a list permutation to the given induced and character enumerations with positive induced weights. At every point of the source coordinates, multiplying the placement sum by the angular product gives the declared numerator.

A list permutation gives a bijection on positions that preserves the full block data. With that bijection, each size-preserving Cartan assignment is a permutation of the complex slots together with a permutation of the real slots. The compact densities on the fixed left factors are constant along the compact flags. Their Haar integrals give the parity-window and sign values, while the radial factors contribute the twists of the assigned blocks. Reindexing the original placement sum gives the Cartan window sum. The angular product turns the windows into their signed endpoint exponentials.
