# Smoothness of the Hilbert vectors of regularized functionals

A derivative-continuous smooth-vector functional factors through
the finite product of the Hilbert spaces of its controlling word
derivatives. Extend the functional on the image to that product by
Hahn–Banach with the sum-of-norms seminorm. Decomposing the extended
functional into its coordinates and applying Riesz on each coordinate
expresses the original functional as a finite sum of Hilbert pairings.

In a Gårding average each word derivative moves to the smooth compact
kernel. Moving a kernel integral across the unitary inner product
conjugates the scalar and inverts the group argument by invariance of
Haar measure. Consequently the Riesz vector of the bounded
regularization is the sum of the Gårding averages of the coordinate
vectors against those conjugate reversed derivative kernels. Each of
the latter averages is a genuine smooth vector.

This supplies smooth Riesz vectors for the generalized coefficient
pairing in Shalika (1974), §3.
