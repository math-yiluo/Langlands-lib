# Specialization of a simple constituent of an algebra action

This derived algebraic lemma applies finite-rank compression to a parameter
family. The example motivating it is a reorder of archimedean induction, whose
constituent comparison is part of Badulescu–Renard (2010), Remark 2.3 and
Proposition 2.4, pp. 1123–1124 (PDF pp. 10–11).

The generators of a free complex algebra act by affine functions of a complex
parameter. One extra generator acts by a fixed finite-rank endomorphism. The
action of each algebra element is a polynomial with coefficients in the
endomorphism algebra, evaluated at a central scalar. At each parameter, its
compression to the fixed finite-dimensional range has a matrix whose entries
are evaluations of complex polynomials. The determinant of the identity minus
this matrix is therefore also the evaluation of a polynomial.

An intertwining isomorphism identifies the two ranges and conjugates the
corresponding compressions. Infinitely many parameter values with such
isomorphisms give equality of the two determinant polynomials. The determinant
transfer criterion then applies at the chosen parameter to any simple
constituent on which the fixed generator acts nontrivially.
