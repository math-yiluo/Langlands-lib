# Evaluation on smooth vectors and smooth continuous functionals

`continuousDualRepresentation` acts on continuous complex linear functionals by precomposition with the inverse group operator. The conjugate-linear Riesz isometry intertwines this action with `hilbertContragredient`. The Riesz map and its inverse are continuous real-linear maps, so they preserve genuine smoothness of real orbit maps. Joint continuity of the continuous dual action on a Hilbert representation of the units of a finite-dimensional real Banach algebra follows from joint continuity of the inverse-adjoint action.

`smoothDualEvaluation` pairs the genuine smooth carriers of an original representation and its continuous dual. It evaluates their empty word coordinates, so the form is complex bilinear and jointly continuous in the two word-derivative topologies. The group actions cancel under evaluation. Along a real line through the identity in the open units chart, the derivative of this constant evaluation gives the negative transpose relation between the two derived actions. The product rule is applied to the real restrictions of complex linear functionals.

For a Banach representation for which both actions are jointly continuous, smooth averaging gives norm density of both smooth carriers. The continuous dual separates vectors by Hahn–Banach, and continuous evaluation extends an identity from either dense carrier to the whole corresponding normed space. This proves nondegeneracy of the smooth evaluation. The construction and comparisons are internal consequences of the Library's smooth action, density and Hilbert duality lemmas.
