# Finiteness of the Levi center over the ambient center

The comparison concerns the two-block parabolic center homomorphism for
one complex matrix factor in the constant-term argument of Harish-Chandra
(1968), Chapter I, §6, printed pp. 19–20 (PDF pp. 29–30).

Block diagonal insertion identifies the complex matrix Levi Lie algebra
with the product of the two smaller matrix Lie algebras. The enveloping
algebra and center of this product are the tensor products of the
corresponding block algebras. The product of the two diagonal
Harish-Chandra polynomial maps embeds the Levi center in the
polynomial ring on the full diagonal.

Evaluation of the ambient diagonal polynomial on an embedded Levi
operator agrees with the product formula. The proof checks the formula
on tensors of ordered monomials from the two blocks. The two block
images commute, allowing the strictly upper letters to be placed on
the left and the lower triangular letters on the right. The
Harish-Chandra functional evaluates these letters by their upper and
lower characters.

The upper radical congruence identifies the composite of the parabolic
projection and the Levi polynomial map with the ambient polynomial map.
The diagonal polynomial ring is already finite over the image of the
ambient center. Artin–Tate makes the ambient center Noetherian. The
injective Levi polynomial map is linear over this center, so its
domain is finite as a module over the ambient center.
