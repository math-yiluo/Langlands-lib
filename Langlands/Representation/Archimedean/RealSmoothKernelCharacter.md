# The real Hilbert character on smooth complex inverse kernels

A real test function on invertible real matrices gives a bounded
operator on a jointly continuous complex Hilbert representation for
a Borel measure finite on compact sets.
The kernel for the existing inverse-kernel operator evaluates the
test at the inverse matrix. Its inverse evaluation in the integral
therefore gives the usual integrated action. The Bochner integral
proves real linearity in the test.

A compactly supported smooth complex inverse kernel gives two real
tests by decomposing its inverse composition into real and imaginary
parts. The complex operator is the sum of the two real integrated
operators with coefficients \(1\) and \(i\). Trace class on all real
tests gives trace class for that sum.

Ordinary transpose on the group commutes with inversion. The two
components of the transposed inverse kernel are therefore the two
transposed real tests. If the real Hilbert character is invariant
under transposition, linearity of the trace gives equality of the
original and transposed complex traces. The transposed operator is
also trace class. This internal comparison uses the existing inverse
kernel operator and the test functions on the open matrix units.
