# Full polynomial braid on finite compact degree spaces

The adjacent compact rotation generators on the polynomial degree space
for \(\mathrm O(3)\) satisfy the three commutator relations of the full
central-factorial braid. Apply the braid to their complex-linear
endomorphism algebra. The normalization of each evaluated tail polynomial
is a scalar times the corresponding central-factorial factor. The
products on the two sides have the same scalar, since they use the same
three indices. This proves the polynomial premise of the rational
character braid in every finite compact degree, for all natural
parameters.
