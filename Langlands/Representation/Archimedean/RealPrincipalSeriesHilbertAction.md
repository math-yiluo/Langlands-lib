# The jointly continuous real principal series on the compact Hilbert closure

The carrier `Hilbert` is the closure in \(L^2(O(n))\) of the image of the
smooth real principal series. The map `embed` is the injective compact
restriction with codomain this closed subspace. Its range is dense.

The compact norm estimate gives a bounded extension of each
right translation. The operators agree with smooth right translation
on the dense image of `embed`. The identity and multiplication laws
follow on the completion by continuity and density, giving
`representation`. Each compact set of group elements has a common
operator norm bound inherited from the estimate on the smooth domain.

On an embedded smooth function, the orbit map is continuous because
the function \((g,k) \mapsto f(kg)\) is continuous, currying into
continuous functions on the compact group is continuous, and passage
from the uniform norm to \(L^2\) is bounded. For joint continuity at a
group element and a Hilbert vector, use a compact neighborhood of the
group element and approximate the vector by one embedded function. The
common operator bound controls both vector approximation errors, and
continuity of the embedded function's orbit controls the remaining
error.

This is an internal completion construction from the compact Hilbert
norm and translation estimate for the published smooth principal series.
