# Normal and tangent parts of the Whittaker direction

The first-order field in the trace Casimir equation has a common coefficient on all simple lower root matrices. Project it onto the normal entry pattern of the chosen cell. A nonlong permutation has at least one simple lower entry in that pattern, so the projection is nonzero whenever the common coefficient is nonzero.

Every remaining simple lower entry belongs to the inversion pattern. Conjugating its matrix by the inverse upper factor preserves that position among lower entries and can also contribute upper entries. The complementary sum therefore has zero normal entries at every point of the cell. This is the matrix-field calculation used in the formalization of Shalika's Proposition 2.10, printed p. 182.
