# Block splitting of the projected Cartan Jacobian

The lower entries of the skew matrix of an off-block perturbation occupy exactly the off-block coordinates. A block orthogonal conjugation preserves that subspace and acts in those coordinates by the published adjoint map `RealLeviChart.adLin`.

In the local Cartan differential, the within-block skew variation contributes negative identity on the within-block positions. Joining the two coordinate families, the derivative factors as the direct product of the transverse off-block difference and negative identity, followed by the orthogonal frame transport. Reindexing the domain by the lower coordinates puts the derivative on one real vector space. Multiplicativity of determinants, invariance under linear conjugation, and the unit absolute determinant of orthogonal transport give the absolute determinant of the transverse off-block difference.

This is an internal coordinate calculation for the compact test argument.
