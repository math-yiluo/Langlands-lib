# Common normalized denominator for the real Cartan

At a separated regular source Cartan the unordered product of normalized off-block determinants of the source blocks is nonzero. Every fixed flag on a size-preserving assignment has that same normalized product.

Each source block is its orthogonal factor times its positive radius. The two Gram products are therefore the squared radius times the identity. Separation makes the Gram scalars distinct, which gives nonvanishing for the off-block determinants. On a placed flag the fixed left factors yield the source blocks after the coordinate permutation and conjugation by the compact frames. The normalized determinants are invariant under these local coordinate changes and conjugations. Their unordered product is also invariant under the assignment of block labels.
