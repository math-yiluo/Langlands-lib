# Compact behavior of operators induced from two consecutive factors

The compact embedding of each Levi factor lies in the full block compact subgroup. Any compact multiplier invariant under the latter therefore commutes with an operator induced from either factor. On a pure compact product, the action of the induced operator changes just its own factor.

For two split Fourier replacements the shifted rotation slots are disjoint, since each lies wholly within one factor's coordinate range. The composite uses the actual induced reverse maps in reverse order. This supplies a reverse lift that commutes with invariant compact multipliers and applies both local lifts on pure compact products.

For two standard equivalences the ambient equivalence is the composition of the two induced equivalences. The same compact formulas give its action on weighted vectors and pure products.

These internal formulas use the coordinate identification between the embedded compact factors and the compact slices in real induction in stages.
