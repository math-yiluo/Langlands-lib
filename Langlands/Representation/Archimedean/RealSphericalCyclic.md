# Cyclicity of the spherical vector of a real unramified principal series (statement)

Module: `Langlands.Representation.Archimedean.RealSphericalCyclic`, namespace
`Langlands.Archimedean`. This module contains one statement and no proof.

## The statement `RealSphericalCyclicStatement`

Data and hypotheses, in the order of the Lean quantifiers:

- `n : ℕ` and a list `ds : List RealCharacterData` with `ds.length = n`.
- Every `d ∈ ds` has `d.parity = 0`. By `RealCharacterData.character_eq` the character of `d`
  is then `x ↦ |x|^{e}` with `e = d.exponent ∈ ℂ`.
- `IsRealLanglandsOrdered (ds.map RealBlock.character)`: the real parts of the exponents are
  weakly decreasing along the list. This is the Library's closed positive chamber for the upper
  triangular parabolic subgroup (`RealGenericParameterReduction`). Ties are allowed, and the
  imaginary parts are arbitrary.
- `T` is a vector of
  `RealTemperedCommutantReduction.KF n (ds.map RealBlock.character)`, the space of
  `O(n)`-finite functions of `realStandardModule n (ds.map RealBlock.character)`. It is fixed by
  `realStandardRep n _ k` for every `k ∈ O(n)`, and `T ≠ 0`.
- `W` is a complex subspace of the same space with `T ∈ W`, and
  `realStandardLie n _ Z w ∈ W` for every `Z ∈ ℂ ⊗_ℝ 𝔤𝔩_n(ℝ)` and every `w ∈ W`.

Conclusion: `W = ⊤`.

So the statement says: the `O(n)`-finite vectors are generated by a nonzero spherical vector
under the complexified Lie algebra action alone. `W` is not assumed to be `O(n)`-stable.

All blocks are characters (no induction blocks), so `realStandardModule n (ds.map
RealBlock.character)` is the principal series `principalSeries ℝ n (realPositionCharacter _)`.
Its functions satisfy `f(diag(a) u g) = ∏_i |a_i|^{e_i + (n-1)/2 - i} f(g)` for `u` upper
unipotent. The space of `O(n)`-fixed vectors in it is one-dimensional: such a function is
determined by `f(1)` through the Iwasawa decomposition. The statement does not use this fact.

## Provenance

The statement is derived: it is the case `G = GL_n(ℝ)` of Kostant's cyclicity theorem for the
spherical vector, B. Kostant, *On the existence and irreducibility of certain series of
representations*, Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 75 (1969), 627–642, Theorem 8. We cite it as quoted in
N. Wallach, *Cyclic vectors and irreducibility for principal series representations*,
Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 158 (1971), 107–113, Theorem 3.1 (printed p. 111). There, for `Λ - ρ`
with real part in the closed positive chamber, `U(𝔤) · 1_Λ`, restricted to `K`, is the space of
all `K`-finite functions on `K/M`. Kostant states the theorem for semisimple groups. For
`GL_n(ℝ)` the center acts on the principal series by a character and does not change the
statement. The formulation here (the Library function model, Lie-stable subspaces, the
ordering predicate `IsRealLanglandsOrdered` as the chamber condition) is ours.
