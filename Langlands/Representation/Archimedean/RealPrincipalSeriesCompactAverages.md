# Finite-rank compact averages on the real principal-series Hilbert space

The orthogonal matrices and the orthogonal subgroup of the matrix units
are continuously isomorphic. Averaging the compact action against a
continuous scalar weight and the Haar probability measure gives a bounded
operator on the completed real principal series.

On the embedded smooth principal-series functions, the compact profile
of an average is the ordinary right convolution of the original
profile. To prove this, pass the Bochner integral through the bounded
inclusion of continuous compact functions into \(L^2\), then pass it
through bounded point evaluations. If the scalar weight is a polynomial
in the orthogonal entries, the range of convolution on continuous functions
is finite-dimensional. Its image in \(L^2\) is closed and contains the
averages of the dense embedded functions. Boundedness places every Hilbert
average in the same finite-dimensional space.

This internal result combines `OrthogonalPolynomialConvolution` with the
compact restriction and bounded action in `RealPrincipalSeriesHilbertAction`.
