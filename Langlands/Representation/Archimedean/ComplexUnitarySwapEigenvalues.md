# Rank-one action on compact test vectors

The determinant-one compact chart exchanges the two rows up to its radial coefficients. At a disjoint pair of rows the chart leaves the test factor unchanged. At the two chart rows, averaging over `z` and `−z` cancels the cross terms of the squared absolute values. The remaining multiplier of the row difference is `1 − 2/(1 + |z|²)`. Integrating the two radial powers of `1 + |z|²` gives the base scalar `(-1)^m 2π` and the additional ratio `(1−D)/(1+D)` for the test in the chart rows, where `D` is the difference between the two inducing exponents. The base test at the identity proves that the defining functional is nonzero.

This computation specializes the adjacent intertwining operators of Vogan, *The unitary dual of GL(n) over an archimedean field* (1986), Theorem 11.5(f)–(h), p. 479, for the signature comparison of Proposition 10.5, p. 476.
