# Compact orbit integrals in the genuine smooth-vector topology

`SmoothCompactOrbitIntegralStatement` concerns a jointly continuous Banach
representation of the units of a finite-dimensional real normed algebra.
For a continuous group-valued map and a continuous complex weight on a
compact space with finite Borel measure, the weighted orbit of a genuine
smooth vector has an integral in the genuine smooth carrier. Each
word-derivative coordinate of that vector equals the Bochner integral of
the same coordinate of the weighted orbit. Applying a continuous linear
scalar functional also commutes with integration.

Project the word coordinates to an arbitrary finite collection. The
continuous compact kernel takes values in the range of this finite
projection from the smooth carrier. The projected integral lies in the
closure of the range because its image in the normed quotient by that
closure is the integral of zero. Projections of the coordinatewise
integral agree with these finite integrals by continuity of
coordinate evaluation. The full coordinatewise integral then belongs
to the range by the closedness of the genuine derivative coordinates in
the product topology. The empty coordinate recovers the Banach orbit
integral.

For scalar evaluation, continuity bounds the functional by a finite maximum
of word seminorms. Include the empty word so the finite coordinate map is
injective. Hahn–Banach extends the resulting bounded linear functional
on the finite coordinate range to the whole finite normed product. The
Bochner integral commutes with this bounded linear map.

This is an internal averaging lemma for compact projections of real
Whittaker coefficients and density in the genuine smooth topology.
