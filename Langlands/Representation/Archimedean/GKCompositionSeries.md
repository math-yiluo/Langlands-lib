# Composition series of compact- and Lie-stable submodules

The subspaces invariant under both actions form a bounded modular
lattice. Meet and join are the intersection and the sum of subspaces.
Modularity follows from that of the underlying submodule lattice.

If both strict order relations are well founded, each interval has a
finite composition series. Induct downward on the upper endpoint.
For a nontrivial interval, the ascending chain condition supplies a
maximal proper predecessor of the upper endpoint above the lower
endpoint. A composition series to this predecessor extends by one
covering step. These series are instances of the Jordan–Hölder
construction for modular lattices.
