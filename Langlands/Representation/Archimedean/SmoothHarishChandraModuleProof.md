# Proof of the real smooth Hilbert Harish-Chandra module statement

Worker 1028, Task 7767. This module proves the published proposition `Langlands.Archimedean.RealSmoothHilbertHarishChandraModuleStatement.{uH}` of [SmoothHarishChandraModuleStatement](SmoothHarishChandraModuleStatement.md), with its carrier universe free. It is one of the three premises of the published `realSmoothHilbertTransposePairing_of_casselmanWallach` in [SmoothHilbertTransposePairingReduction](SmoothHilbertTransposePairingReduction.md).

- `Langlands.Archimedean.smoothHilbertKFinite_nontrivial`: take a jointly continuous representation of `GL_n(ℝ)` on a nonzero complete complex Hilbert space, admissible for the orthogonal subgroup. Its genuine compact-finite smooth vectors are nonzero. By [OrthogonalCompactFiniteDensity](OrthogonalCompactFiniteDensity.md), vectors with finite orbit span under the orthogonal matrix group are norm dense, so one of them, `x`, is nonzero. Its orbit span under the corresponding subgroup of units is the same. The published `isSmoothVector_of_normCompactFinite` makes `x` genuinely smooth, using a Haar measure on `GL_n(ℝ)` and a normalized Haar measure on the compact subgroup. The published `exists_smoothHilbertKFinite_of_norm` then gives a compact-finite smooth vector with value `x`.
- `Langlands.Archimedean.realSmoothHilbertHarishChandraModule`: the statement holds. For `R` satisfying `IsIrreducibleAdmissibleHilbertRepresentation ℝ (r + 1) R`, the witnesses are `smoothKFiniteRepresentation` and `RealSmoothGK.lieAction` ([RealSmoothGKModule](RealSmoothGKModule.md)). The structure axioms are `RealSmoothGK.isRealGKModule`, and admissibility is `isAdmissibleGKModule_smoothKFinite`. The compatibility with the actual actions is `RealSmoothGK.isSmoothGKIntertwiner`. For irreducibility, nontriviality is the lemma above. A submodule stable under the compact action and every `π(1 ⊗ X)` maps to a submodule of the genuine smooth carrier satisfying the hypotheses of `RealSmoothIrreducible.eq_bot_or_smoothKFinite_le` ([RealSmoothKFiniteIrreducible](RealSmoothKFiniteIrreducible.md)). It is therefore zero or everything.

The submodule `smoothHilbertKFinite ℝ (r + 1) R` of the statement and `smoothKFinite ℝ (r + 1) R` are the same submodule by definition. Only standard Lean axioms are used. The Casselman–Wallach extension and the inverse-transpose dual intertwiner, the other two premises of the reduction, are not proved here.
