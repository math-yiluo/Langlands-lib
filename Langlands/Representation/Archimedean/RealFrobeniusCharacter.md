# The smooth realization and trace character of the real Frobenius closure

Take a real GK module with a Jacquet functional satisfying the compact
and Lie algebra character equations. The Frobenius map defines a
closed invariant submodule of the real Hilbert principal series.
For an irreducible admissible GK module and a nonzero Jacquet functional,
compact-finite recovery, density in the derivative topology, and
compact and Lie compatibility give the genuine smooth globalization
on that specific Hilbert closure.

The compact action is unitary. On the dense principal-series image,
the squared norm is the integral of the squared compact restriction.
Right invariance of compact Haar preserves that integral. Continuity
extends norm preservation to the completion, and the linear action
therefore preserves the inner product, including on the Frobenius
closure.

For an s-finite Borel measure finite on compact sets and invariant
under left and right group multiplication, the bounded real test
operators on the Frobenius closure are trace class by compression
from the Hilbert principal series. Their trace is a conjugation-invariant
distribution on the open invertible real matrices. These conclusions
use the internal trace-class and character constructions.
