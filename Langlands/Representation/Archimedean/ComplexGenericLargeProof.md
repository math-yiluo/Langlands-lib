# Irreducible admissible generic `(𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ), U(n))`-modules are large

`Langlands.Archimedean.complexGenericLarge : ComplexGenericLargeStatement.{uV}` proves the
Library statement `ComplexGenericLargeStatement` (module
`Langlands.Representation.Archimedean.ComplexGenericStandardInjectiveReduction`). The statement
says: for every `n`, every irreducible admissible generic `(𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ), U(n))`-module `(ρ, π)` is
large (`IsComplexLargeGKModule`). Here `π` is an action of `ℂ ⊗_ℝ 𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)` on a complex vector
space `V : Type uV`. Largeness means that every element of the enveloping algebra acting by
zero lies in the left ideal generated by the central elements acting by zero.

This is the complex case of Kostant's theorem (Kostant, *On Whittaker vectors and representation
theory*, Invent. Math. 48 (1978), Theorem 3.9), applied to the Whittaker functional that
genericity provides. The theorem adds no hypotheses and keeps the universe `uV` of the target.

## Proof

The proof is a single term that combines two Library theorems.

1. `ComplexGenericLarge.complexGenericLargeReduction` (module
   `Langlands.Representation.Archimedean.ComplexGenericLargeReduction`) proves
   `GLProductWhittakerMaximallyFaithfulStatement → ComplexGenericLargeStatement`. It
   transports `π` along the algebra isomorphism `ℂ ⊗_ℝ M_n(ℂ) ≅ M_n(ℂ) × M_n(ℂ)`,
   `z ⊗ X ↦ (zX, z·X̄)`. On the product side it proves that the transported action has an
   infinitesimal character and that the genericity functional is a Whittaker functional for a
   nondegenerate character. It then applies Kostant's theorem for the product and transports
   maximal faithfulness back.
2. `Kostant.glProductWhittakerMaximallyFaithful` (module
   `Langlands.Representation.Archimedean.RealWhittakerMaximallyFaithfulProof`) proves
   `GLProductWhittakerMaximallyFaithfulStatement`. This is Kostant's theorem for finite products
   of `𝔤𝔩_{n_i}(ℂ)`, proved along Matumoto's route (Invent. Math. 89 (1987), Corollary 5). That
   route uses the ideal of the nilpotent cone (`Kostant.nilpotentConeIdeal`) and the vanishing of
   coadjoint-stable polynomials on the nilpotent cone (`Kostant.orbitVanishing`).

Applying (1) to (2) gives the target.
