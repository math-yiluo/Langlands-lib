# Lifting compact-finite vectors through smooth quotients

Let \(Q\) be a surjective continuous linear map between genuine smooth
carriers of jointly continuous real general linear Banach representations.
Assume it intertwines the orthogonal actions. Then it lifts every
compact-finite target vector to a compact-finite source vector.

Take a basis of the target vector's finite-dimensional orthogonal orbit
span and choose smooth lifts of the basis vectors. The inverse-action
coordinates of the target vector give continuous compact-finite weights.
Continuity is checked by injecting the finite orbit span under the
empty word map into the target Banach space. Averaging the lifts against
these weights gives compact-finite smooth vectors. The sum of their images
under \(Q\) recovers the target vector, since the inverse action on the
coordinates cancels the action on the basis vectors pointwise. The proof
checks this equality through continuous linear scalar evaluations of the
genuine smooth compact integrals.
