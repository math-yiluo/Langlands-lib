# A local density for the compact Cartan trace

Let a map on a finite-dimensional real normed space be continuously
differentiable near a point `x` and have invertible derivative `D` at `x`.
For a complex weight `w` continuous at `x`, the local image integral
has a density `W` continuous at the image of `x`. Its value there is
`w x` divided by the absolute determinant of `D`. The source
neighborhood can lie inside any prescribed open neighborhood of `x`.

The inverse function theorem gives a local inverse. The source
neighborhood is shrunk so that the derivative exists and its
determinant stays nonzero. Composing the weight with the inverse
and dividing by the original Jacobian at that inverse image gives
`W`. The change of variables theorem for the additive Haar measure
then gives the integral identity on the two open neighborhoods.

This is an internal consequence of the inverse function theorem and
the additive Haar change of variables theorem, for use after introducing
local coordinates at a fixed block flag.
