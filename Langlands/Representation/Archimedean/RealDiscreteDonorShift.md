# A dominant spherical shift of the nested donor

This internal construction uses the tied real center and the integer coordinate
twice the distance of a character exponent from that center. For absolute weight
ℓ the low and high coordinates are `−ℓ` and `ℓ`. The signed residue subtracts twice
the parity and reduces modulo four. A low endpoint has residue zero or three,
according to whether ℓ is even or odd. A high endpoint has residue two or one.
Even center characters have residue zero and odd center characters have residue
two. The order of the donor therefore makes the real exponents weakly decreasing
within each residue.

The shifts are chosen in four successive ranges, one for each part of the donor.
The formulas grow with the low weights and decrease with the high weights.
The gap between the low and high ranges ensures that subtracting the shifts gives
weakly decreasing real exponents across the whole list. Each shift has the parity
of the corresponding character and is a function of the real coordinate and parity.
This construction is derived from the list and signs of
`RealDiscreteNestedPresentation`.
