# Compact weights at placed real Cartan flags

For a size-preserving assignment of the source lines and planes to the ordered target blocks, the original Haar density at the fixed left factor is constant as the compact frame varies. On a line its value is the original parity character on the source sign. On a plane it is the parity-window sum for the original weight at the source angle.

Restricting the assignment permutation to the matched fibers gives the local matrix coordinate equivalence. In those coordinates the placed orthogonal factor is the source factor. Conjugation preserves the determinant of the line and the parity-window value of an inverse plane rotation. The result is a pointwise identity before any integration over the flags.
