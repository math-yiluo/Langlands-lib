# Full real parameters of actual opposite standard Jacquet exponents

Worker 148. This internal specialization uses the original standard root
presentation for each label of the actual opposite Jacquet family. It keeps
exactly the already chosen split-component scheme, inclusion and factor into
the fixed maximal split torus. The spectra remain the spectra of the opposite
parabolics.

`realPart` is the published normalized real part of the actual exponent
character. `fullRealPart` applies the unique selected-coroot-annihilating lift
to put it in the common full torus space. Restriction recovers the input and
all selected coroot pairings are zero. These conditions characterize the lift.
The all-point norm formula follows from the actual smooth character's
published norm formula and the compatibility of the full pairing with the
component map. Vanishing of the lift is equivalent to unitarity on all
split-component points. Matching an actual unramified Levi character's
split-component real part identifies the lift with that character's published
full torus parameter.

Connected reductivity, the maximal split torus, the generic cocharacter and
supplied actual reflection data are retained. The field is nonarchimedean
local with the canonical point topologies, and the ambient rational-point
group has explicit local compactness and nonarchimedean group instances.
The full-lift laws also assume a locally finite type instance on the ambient
scheme and a monomorphism instance for the maximal split-torus inclusion.
The representation carrier universe remains independent of the field
universe. Admissibility and finite length are unnecessary for the parameter
of an individual supplied actual exponent.

This module derives its constructions from the published standard and
opposite family, standard presentation, split-torus norm formula, full-lift
and unramified full-parameter APIs. It constructs no positive-part
decomposition, inner product, tempered representation or Langlands triplet,
and does not prove V.3.18 or the Langlands classification.
