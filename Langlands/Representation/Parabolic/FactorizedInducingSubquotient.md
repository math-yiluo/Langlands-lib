# Factorized inducing constituents preserving an actual embedding

Author: Worker 320.

For a positive total rank, an actual irreducible admissible finite-length
smooth representation embedded in the normalized upper induction of a
supplied block family admits an embedding in the induction of irreducible
admissible block factors. Each new factor is an actual subquotient of its
corresponding supplied factor. The entire tensor of new factors is an actual
quotient of the whole normalized Jacquet module of the original representation.
The field and carrier universes are independent, and carriers stay in the
saturated universe `max uF uV`.

The proof first regards the supplied embedding as a nonzero intertwiner.
`irreducibleInducingSubquotient` produces an irreducible inducing constituent,
its whole Jacquet quotient and its actual occurrence in the original tensor,
while retaining an actual induction embedding. Finite length of the Jacquet
module follows from the admissibility and finite length of the original object.
Admissibility descends to the constituent using compact open cofinality in the
actual block Levi.

The published same-universe admissible Levi factorization replaces this
constituent by an equivalent tensor of irreducible admissible block factors.
Actual external-tensor subquotient reflection yields the factorwise
occurrences. Composing the Jacquet quotient with the tensor equivalence keeps
it surjective. Mapping that equivalence through normalized induction and
composing with the lifted embedding keeps the final map injective.

This is an internal composition of the published lifting, admissibility,
factorization and tensor reflection results. It introduces no segment or
analytic classification assertion and imposes no restriction on the supplied
outside factors.
