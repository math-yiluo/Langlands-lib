# Normalized scalar twists and central characters

Author: Worker 8. This pair imports the independently published
`Parabolic.LeviBridges` construction.

`Langlands.Parabolic.NormalizedCompatibilityStatement` is a conjunction
of five concrete proposition definitions. It covers actual standard upper
block parabolics of `GL_n` over finite extensions of `Q_p`, with complex
coefficients. It asserts no theorem proof.

Fix `F : PadicField p`, prime `p`, compatible topology `T`, and positive
ordered block shape `B` of rank `n`. Write `G = GL_n(F)`, `P` for the actual
block parabolic, `M` for its product Levi, and `N` for the actual radical,
the kernel of the diagonal-block projection. Field and representation
carrier universes are independent. The carrier is only a complex module;
it has no topology and need not be finite-dimensional.

The actual normalized actions are the smooth right-translation induction
of `twist (T.parabolicHalfModulus B) (inflate B sigma)` and
`twist (Jacquet.inverseHalfCharacter (T.leviModulus B)) (leviCoinvariants B rhoP)`.
The full modulus is the published product of residue-normalized block
determinant norms. Its induction half is the positive square root; Jacquet
uses its inverse. No arbitrary character is identified with this modulus.

## The five clauses

`AmbientCharacterRadicalClause` states that every group homomorphism
`chi : GL_n(F) -> Complex units` is trivial on the actual block radical.
This clause has no smoothness or topology hypothesis. It is a conclusion,
not an extra hypothesis imposed on the twisting characters below.

`NormalizedInductionTwistClause` takes a smooth ambient scalar character
`chi` (meaning its kernel is open) and a smooth product-Levi representation
`sigma`. Restrict `chi` to `M` along the actual embedding, obtaining
`chiM`. It asserts an actual representation equivalence

`chi tensor normalizedInd(sigma) = normalizedInd(chiM tensor sigma)`,

with the exact function formula `E(f)(g) = chi(g) f(g)`. Both sides use
the same positive block half-modulus.

`NormalizedJacquetTwistClause` takes a smooth ambient representation
`rho` and the same kind of smooth character. It asserts an actual
representation equivalence

`normalizedJ(chi tensor rho) = chiM tensor normalizedJ(rho)`,

prescribed by sending the class of each vector to the class of that same
vector. The first clause ensures equality of the actual radical relation
spaces; it is not silently replaced by an assumption. The inverse
half-modulus is the same on both sides.

`NormalizedInductionCentralClause` starts with an arbitrary character
`omega : F units -> Complex units` and the scalar-action identity

`sigma((a I_(n_i))_i) v = omega(a) v`.

It concludes that normalized induction has action `omega(a)` on the
ambient scalar matrix `a I_n`.
`NormalizedJacquetCentralClause` starts with that scalar identity for
an ambient representation and concludes it for the diagonal scalar
element of the product Levi on the normalized quotient. These are
identities on every vector, not just assertions that some central
character exists. The central clauses need neither irreducibility nor
admissibility nor an open-kernel hypothesis on `omega`. They remain valid
for zero representations. The imported block modulus is one on ambient
scalars, so its two half-character factors are also one.

For positive `n`, scalar matrices are exactly the center of `GL_n(F)`;
thus these identities are precisely preservation of a prescribed ambient
central character. They do not assert that every central element of the
larger product-Levi center acts by a scalar. The rank-zero case is also
allowed; all actual group elements are identities, and the displayed
scalar-action hypothesis retains its literal meaning.

## Sources and derived algebra

Bernstein--Zelevinsky, *Induced representations of reductive p-adic groups I*
(1977), Section 1.8, printed pp.444-445 / PDF pp.5-6, defines the normalized
right-translation function and inverse-half quotient actions. Proposition
1.9(f), p.445, gives the character-twist identities, with the localized
radical character changed by the restriction of the ambient character.
Sections 2.1-2.3, p.446 / PDF p.7, give the standard reductive triples and
the ordered-block `GL_n` specialization. Those original pages were read.

To specialize 1.9(f) to the ordinary Jacquet quotient, the restriction of
every ambient scalar character to the block radical must be one. Here is
the derived algebraic reason recorded in the first clause. The radical
is generated by its strictly upper off-diagonal block elementary
transvections. For each such root position `(i,j)`, conjugating the
transvection with entry `x` by a diagonal matrix whose `i`th entry is `2`
and whose other entries are `1` multiplies `x` by `2`. Its commutator with
that transvection therefore has entry `x`. The field has characteristic
zero, so `2` is a unit, even when the residue characteristic is two.
Thus every generating transvection is a commutator. Every homomorphism
to the abelian group of complex units kills those commutators.
This is an elementary derived prerequisite, not a literal additional
theorem quoted from BZ77, and its Lean proof remains an obligation.

The normalized twist formulas are then direct specializations of 1.9(f):
the induction map multiplies functions by `chi(g)`, and the quotient map
keeps vector representatives unchanged. These statements retain the
actual product-Levi embedding rather than an unspecified restriction map.

The central identities are derived directly from the same source's
action formulas. A scalar matrix commutes with every `g`; the left
covariance of an induced function gives the inducing scalar action.
On a quotient the scalar action descends through the quotient map.
The positive block product is one on an ambient scalar because, for each
pair of blocks, its numerator and denominator both equal
`|a|_F^(n_i n_j)`. That cancellation is already proved as
`leviModulus_scalar` in the published construction. Formal identification
of the block product with Haar scaling is still a separate theorem
obligation; it is not assumed in these elementary compatibilities.
