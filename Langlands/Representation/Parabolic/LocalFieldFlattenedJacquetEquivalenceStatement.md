# Equivalence of flattened and iterated normalized Jacquet restriction

`Langlands.AllField.Parabolic.LocalFieldFlattenedJacquetEquivalenceStatement`
compares standard upper normalized Jacquet restriction for the flattened
shape with normalized Jacquet restriction first to the outer Levi and
then to the refined Levi. The target is pulled back along the actual
flattened Levi coordinate equivalence. The vector class in the flattened
quotient maps to the iterated class of the same vector.

The proposition uses the same local-field data in both actions, allows
both characteristics and includes rank zero. It states transitivity
at the level of actual quotient representations on arbitrary complex
carriers, with their independent universe, and specifies the comparison
on quotient representatives.

Bernstein and Zelevinsky §1.8(b) define the normalized quotient and
§1.9(c) give transitivity. Sections 2.2 and 2.3(c) describe the ordered
block refinements and specialize transitivity to the standard
parabolic Jacquet functors.
