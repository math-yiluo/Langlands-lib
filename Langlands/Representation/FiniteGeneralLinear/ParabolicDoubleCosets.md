# Parabolic and unipotent double cosets over a field

The Library's Weyl minimal representatives give the standard parabolic double cosets over any field. The proof combines the field-general upper Bruhat factorization, reflection of equality of permutation-matrix cosets to the Weyl group, and the Weyl bijection. These are the algebraic ingredients of the Library's p-adic parabolic Bruhat theorem.

For the unipotent and parabolic double cosets, first replace the right-hand triangular group by its unipotent radical. Its diagonal factor can be conjugated through the permutation matrix into the left-hand parabolic. The triangular right group still detects equality of the original Weyl labels. Inverting representatives exchanges the sides and gives the stated permutation representatives with upper unipotents on the left. The construction is used internally for the finite-group Mackey formula.
