# Disjointness and order independence of finite Harish–Chandra series

The finite Mackey formula counts block bijections that preserve the ranked cuspidal labels. Each such bijection sends the coordinates in a block in increasing order to the matching block. This gives a conjugating minimal permutation, and the block coordinates determine it uniquely. A nonzero intertwining dimension therefore gives an equality of the two label multisets. The constituent multiplicity formula gives such a dimension whenever two inductions have a common irreducible constituent.

Equal multisets give a label-preserving bijection by matching the finite fibers of the block labels. Composing with this bijection equates the counts for the two self-pairings and the cross-pairing. The sum of squares of the differences of constituent multiplicities is then zero. Every multiplicity agrees, so the series is independent of the order of its blocks.

These are internal finite-group arguments for counting cuspidal representations using Whittaker constituents.
