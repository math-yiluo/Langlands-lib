# Additive weight projections over finite groups

Pairing a row with a variable column and applying a fixed nontrivial additive character gives every complex additive character of the column space. To prove this, test two distinct rows against a column supported in one coordinate. Rescaling that column distinguishes the two characters. Mathlib's finite additive dual cardinality then gives surjectivity.

The unnormalized weight projection is the sum of the translates of a vector with the inverse character as coefficients. Translating the summation index proves the eigenvector identity. Summing over the additive dual gives the group order times the original vector by finite character orthogonality. These are internal lemmas for constructing Whittaker vectors of finite general linear groups.
