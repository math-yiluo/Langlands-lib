# Constituent multiplicities of complex finite-group characters

The irreducible trace characters are linearly independent over the complex numbers by character orthogonality. They therefore form a finite family for a finite group. The Library's integral irreducible expansion in the split representation group gives a character expansion. Pairing it with an irreducible character identifies its coefficient with the dimension of the corresponding intertwiner space. Thus the expansion uses actual nonnegative multiplicities, and the pairing of two characters is the dot product of their multiplicity vectors.

The module also records symmetry of the intertwining dimension by inversion in the character sum, and the irreducibility criterion from the endomorphism dimension. These are internal counting steps for the finite Harish–Chandra series argument.
