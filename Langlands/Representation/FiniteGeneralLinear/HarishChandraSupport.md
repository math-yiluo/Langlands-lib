# Exhaustion of Harish–Chandra support over finite fields

A Harish–Chandra datum is an ordered block shape together with a cuspidal class in each block. Its induced representation is obtained by forming the external tensor of the block representations and applying Harish–Chandra induction.

For an irreducible finite-dimensional complex representation of `GL_n(k)` with `k` finite, take a block shape of maximal length among those with a nonzero radical-fixed space. Start with a shape whose unipotent radical is trivial. This shape has one block in positive rank and is empty in rank zero. The normal radical gives a Levi action on the fixed space. Choose an irreducible subrepresentation and factor it as an external tensor using the finite product theorem. If one factor had a nonzero fixed vector for a proper parabolic radical, then a pure tensor would give a nonzero fixed vector for the flattened refinement. The latter has more blocks, contradicting maximality. Thus every factor is cuspidal. The inclusion of the tensor into the original representation is equivariant for the inflation to the parabolic. Finite Frobenius reciprocity shows that the original irreducible occurs in the Harish–Chandra induction of this datum.

The proof is an internal form of the finite-group cuspidal support existence argument. It uses the Library's block flattening and finite representation constructions.
