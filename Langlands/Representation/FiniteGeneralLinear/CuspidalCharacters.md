# Cuspidal trace characters over finite fields

A nonzero vector in an irreducible representation has a spanning orbit. The orbit is finite for a finite group, so the representation has finite dimension in its given carrier universe. The finite-group character pairing in Mathlib gives the Kronecker delta on trace characters of distinct cuspidal classes. Pairing a finite linear relation against each character proves linear independence and hence finiteness of the classes.

In rank one the matrix group has the cardinality of the field's unit group. A nonzero scalar `a` gives the polynomial label `X + C a` and its constant coefficient recovers `a`, so the elementary group-order bound suffices. This is an internal step toward the finite general linear cuspidal count.
