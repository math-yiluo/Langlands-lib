# Matched cuspidal factors on a Harish–Chandra Mackey term

Each cuspidal label records its positive rank and its isomorphism class. Taking the multiset of block labels forgets their order and preserves their total rank.

A surviving minimal permutation conjugates the two Levis. It transports each source block in increasing order to a target block of the same size. Comparing the matrix entries on those blocks shows that the two projections of the stabilizer to the Levis correspond under this conjugation. Thus intertwiners on the stabilizer are precisely the intertwiners between the transported Levi representations. The external tensor dimension formula factors this dimension into the individual block dimensions. Character orthogonality makes the product one for matching labels and zero when some labels differ.

These are internal steps in the finite Harish–Chandra series count.
