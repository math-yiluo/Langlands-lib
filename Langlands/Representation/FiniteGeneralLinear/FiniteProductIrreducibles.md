# Irreducibles of products of finite groups

The product formula for intertwining dimensions gives endomorphism dimension one for an external tensor of irreducibles. For exhaustion, the external tensor of the regular characters is the regular character of the product group. Expanding the regular characters by their constituent multiplicities expresses this product as a sum of characters of external irreducibles. A given irreducible pairs with the regular character by its positive dimension, so it must be equivalent to at least one external irreducible. The product formula and orthogonality also identify each factor when the total intertwining dimension is nonzero.

The regular model comes from the group algebra. Its trace is computed on the basis of single group elements using the permutation action. These are internal steps toward finite Harish–Chandra support.
