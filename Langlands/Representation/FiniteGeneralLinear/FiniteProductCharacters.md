# External tensor characters and one-dimensional intertwiners

The external tensor product is the Library's finite tensor representation bundled as a finite-dimensional representation. In the tensor product basis its diagonal coefficients are products of the diagonal coefficients of its factors. Summing over the basis gives the character formula. The finite-group character pairing and factorization of the sum over the product group give the corresponding product formula for intertwining dimension. Reindexing the sum along a group isomorphism preserves this dimension.

Evaluation at the scalar `1` identifies maps from a one-dimensional character with vectors transforming by that character. The inverse sends a vector to scalar multiplication by that vector. These are internal constructions for finite-field parabolic induction and Whittaker heredity.
