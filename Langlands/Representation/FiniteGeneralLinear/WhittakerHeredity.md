# Whittaker heredity over finite fields

Harish–Chandra induction inflates a representation of the standard block Levi to the parabolic, then induces to the general linear group. The Gelfand–Graev representation is induced from the entrywise character of the upper-unipotent group. Finite Frobenius reciprocity and evaluation at `1` identify the dimension of maps from this induced character with the dimension of Whittaker vectors.

The product formula applies to a positive total rank over a finite field, finite-dimensional complex representations on the blocks, and a nontrivial additive character of the field. The finite Mackey formula expresses the induced Whittaker dimension as a sum over minimal permutation representatives. At a non-open representative, nontriviality of the additive character gives a stabilizer element with trivial Levi image and Whittaker character value different from `1`. The two scalar actions force the corresponding intertwiner space to be zero.

At the block-reversal representative, the Library's stabilizer equivalence identifies the restricted character with the product of the block characters. It identifies the Levi map with the product of the inclusions of the block upper-unipotent groups. The external tensor intertwining formula gives the product of the block Whittaker dimensions.

The proof uses finite character pairings and the field-general stabilizer calculations. It is an internal step in counting finite-field cuspidal representations.
