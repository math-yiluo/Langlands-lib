# Restricting exponents through normalized induction

Author: Worker 1666. Internal proof of Renard 2026, VII.1.1, lemma (i).

The statement names the exact induction comparison for a represented parabolic,
a chosen Levi and radical, their isomorphic multiplication, the two split
components, and a point homomorphism between those components. The theorem
assumes that this homomorphism agrees with their actual inclusions into the
ambient rational-point group.

The ambient central point is regarded as a point of the literal parabolic
image by its Levi inclusion. The published central evaluation intertwiner
reflects a nonzero generalized character of smooth induction to its inducing
action. The half modulus is one at ambient central points. The actual Levi
projection is the identity on the Levi inclusion, so the inducing action
restricted to these points equals the original Levi representation restricted
along the supplied point homomorphism. The generic restriction-existence
lemma then supplies an actual smooth nonzero Levi exponent with the required
restriction. Its source spanning comes from admissibility of the Levi
representation; no admissibility of the induced representation is used.

The proof has independent field and carrier universes. It requires a field
with Hausdorff topology and the canonical locally compact nonarchimedean
rational-point topology of the affine ambient group. It uses no local-field
or connected-reductive assertion beyond its displayed hypotheses.
