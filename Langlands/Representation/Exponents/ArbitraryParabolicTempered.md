# Normalized-exponent temperedness over all actual rational parabolics

This is a derived, explicitly normalized-parameter formulation of Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, French version of 9 July 2026, VII.2.1, printed p.227/PDF p.233. It uses the nonnegative root-combination cone of V.3.14–V.3.15, printed p.113/PDF p.119, and the actual normalized Jacquet exponents of VII.1.1, printed p.220/PDF p.226, with normalization VI.1.2, printed pp.135–136/PDF pp.141–142. The registered source is `renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, SHA256 `7b539996793b7c3620e72e1e389588eb42d1a8e91d6f68b2eead5c2e2ffc182c`.

## Actual objects and quantifiers

The namespace is `Langlands.Aubert.General.RepresentedParabolic`, shared with the published arbitrary-parabolic exponent construction. `SatisfiesNormalizedExponentConeCondition` fixes actual group-scheme maps `p : P ⟶ G`, `l : M ⟶ P`, and `a : A.toGrp ⟶ M`, with witnesses that they are a parabolic, a rational Levi, and its split component. It is the conjunction of existence of a compatible `ParabolicRootPresentation p l a` and the following condition: for every such presentation, every represented geometric unipotent radical `r : U ⟶ P` for which `subgroupMultiplication l r` is an isomorphism, and every actual normalized Jacquet exponent `χ`, its `Plancherel.SplitTorus.normalizedRealPart` belongs to `pres.nonnegCone`.

The exponent is defined from the actual normalized coinvariant representation on the literal Levi point image, with the actual split-component point map. No substitute functor, abstract exponent set, root system or cone predicate is assumed. A rational-Levi witness already includes existence of a matching represented geometric unipotent radical, so radical quantification is not over an uninhabited family. The separate nonempty presentation requirement prevents the presentation test from being vacuous. All compatible presentations are tested; no torus, cocharacter, root index or factor map is fixed globally.

`IsNormalizedExponentTempered hG π` is admissibility of the underlying smooth representation and this condition for every specified actual parabolic, rational Levi and split-component triple. `hG : IsConnectedReductive G` supplies smoothness and thus local finite type for the actual Lie-root cones. The canonical rational-point topology is used throughout. The field topology is explicitly assumed Hausdorff, as required by the actual closed-image Jacquet API. The ambient point group is explicitly assumed locally compact and nonarchimedean; deriving those conditions from the algebraic group and local field is a separate structural interface obligation. The field is any nonarchimedean local field in the existing `IsNonarchimedeanLocalField` interface, without a characteristic-zero restriction. The group scheme is in universe `u` and the representation carrier in independent universe `v`.

The cone lives in the **full** real character space `RealTorusSpace.Vstar A` and consists of nonnegative linear combinations of the restricted simple roots of the presentation. It imposes the central-direction condition through membership in that span. It is not the separate coroot-inequality chamber used elsewhere. There is no scalar central-character assumption, irreducibility assumption or finite-dimensional-carrier restriction.

## Parameter convention and retained source discrepancy

Write `q` for the residue cardinality and use the existing positive valuation. `normalizedRealPart` uses the parameter in which a smooth character has modulus `q ^ (-pair normalizedRealPart (valuation a))`; the complete norm formula is in the separate published `SplitTorusNormFormula` module. For a rational character raised to a real power, this is the convention associated with the source's `q^(-s)` exponential. The construction here imports `SplitTorusRealPart` and uses precisely its named parameter, without changing the valuation or the cone.

There is a concrete discrepancy within V.3.20, printed p.116/PDF p.122. The source first defines the real part by lifting through the exponential map based on `q^(-s)` (whose construction starts on printed p.115/PDF p.121), and later writes the real part as the raw natural logarithm of the character modulus. Under the fixed positive valuation these parameters differ by a negative scalar. The existing API states the distinction exactly:

`rawLogRealPart hA χ = (-Real.log (LocalField.residueCardinality k : ℝ)) • normalizedRealPart hA χ`.

Because `q > 1`, that scalar is negative. This pair defines only the explicitly named normalized version. It neither identifies the two parameters nor asserts that their membership in the same nonnegative root cone is equivalent. Exact correspondence with a corrected consistent reading of V.3.20 and VII.2.1 remains a separate obligation; this construction alone does not close source-definition coverage for VII.2.1.

## Intrinsic laws and remaining obligations

The three `SatisfiesNormalizedExponentConeCondition` lemmas extract presentation existence, membership for each specified presentation and exponent, and an actual presentation witnessing membership. Their proofs respectively project the first conjunct, apply the second conjunct, and combine those two operations. The three `IsNormalizedExponentTempered` lemmas extract admissibility, the condition for any specified triple, and presentation existence. They are direct projections and applications of the definition. No nonintrinsic theorem is proved here.

Global presentation existence, independence across all root choices, compatible split-component transport, reduction to standard parabolics, and comparison of actual point images remain separate structural or representation-theoretic proof obligations. The source's VII.2.1 remarks about the full-group exponent, unitary central characters when a central character exists, and the Casselman implication are also retained as later obligations. No analytic temperedness criterion, induction/restriction preservation, classification result or whole-book completion follows from this pair.
