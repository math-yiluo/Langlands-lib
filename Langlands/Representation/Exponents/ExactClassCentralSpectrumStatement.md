## Hypotheses

The proposition quantifies over a group `G : Type uG` with a topology and
`IsTopologicalGroup G`. It assumes that compact open subgroups are cofinal
among open subgroups:

```text
∀ H : OpenSubgroup G, ∃ K : OpenSubgroup G,
  IsCompact (K : Set G) ∧ K ≤ H.
```

It then quantifies over a commutative group `A : Type uA` with a topology
and `IsTopologicalGroup A`, a monoid homomorphism
`c : A →* Subgroup.center G`, and a proof `Continuous c`. The topology on
the actual centre is its subgroup topology. The map need not be injective
or surjective.

The objects `R S : SmoothRep.{0,uG,uV} ℂ G` are the Library's actual
smooth complex representations, with carrier universe `uV`. Their
separate witnesses `hR : R.HasFiniteLength` and `hS : S.HasFiniteLength`
assert finite length as modules over the group algebra `ℂ[G]`. They do
not assert finite dimension over `ℂ`. The hypotheses
`IsAdmissible R.representation` and `IsAdmissible S.representation` use
`Langlands.Representation.Smooth.Definitions`: smoothness and finite
dimension of the fixed-vector space for every open subgroup.

The last hypothesis is equality of their canonical classes

```text
SmoothRep.FiniteLengthGrothendieckGroup.ofRepresentation R hR =
  SmoothRep.FiniteLengthGrothendieckGroup.ofRepresentation S hS.
```

This is the existing exact Grothendieck group from
`Langlands.Representation.Smooth.FiniteLengthGrothendieck`. It is the
free integral module on genuine isomorphism classes of actual
finite-length smooth objects, quotiented by the submodule generated by
`[B] - [A] - [C]` for every actual short exact sequence
`0 → A → B → C → 0` of such objects. Its relations include nonsplit
sequences. The equality does not assume a splitting or an equivalence
between `R` and `S`.

## Conclusion

For every monoid homomorphism `χ : A →* ℂˣ`, the conclusion is

```text
IsGeneralizedEigencharacter
  (R.representation.comp ((Subgroup.center G).subtype.comp c)) χ ↔
IsGeneralizedEigencharacter
  (S.representation.comp ((Subgroup.center G).subtype.comp c)) χ.
```

For either representation `ρ`, this is the existing predicate of
`Langlands.Representation.Exponents.Jacquet`. It means that there exist
one natural number `k` and one nonzero vector `v` such that
`((ρ (c a) - (χ a : ℂ) • 1) ^ k) v = 0` for every `a : A`. The power
`k` is uniform over `A` for that vector. The two sides of the equivalence
may use different vectors and different powers. No continuity or
open-kernel condition is imposed on `χ`.

The invariant concerns occurrence of characters. It asserts no equality
of dimensions of generalized character spaces, no equality of their
nilpotence orders, and no chosen correspondence of vectors. There is no
central semisimplicity, distinctness of characters, multiplicity-one
assumption, finite-dimensional ambient carrier, decomposition, ambient
irreducibility, or scalar central action on the whole representation.
