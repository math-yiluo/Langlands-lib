# Square-integrable subrepresentations of unitary block products

This is the unitary block case of the local exclusion used in Harris–Taylor, *The Geometry and Cohomology of Some Simple Shimura Varieties* (2001), proof of Theorem VII.1.9, p. 229. The analytic input is the Library's strict cone theorem for an actual embedding into normalized induction, which implements the necessary direction of Casselman's square-integrability criterion, §4.4, Theorem 4.4.6.

Each irreducible unitarizable factor has a scalar character of norm one. On the split centre, the external tensor product acts by the product of the coordinate scalar characters. Its real part is the zero vector. The given embedding makes that vector satisfy the strict positive root cone condition. Two or more blocks would require its first partial sum to be positive. The total rank is positive, so there is exactly one block.

This derived result uses arbitrary supplied topological data for the p-adic field. The factor carriers and the carrier of the embedded representation are in `Type (max uF uV)`, which also accommodates the actual normalized induction.
