# Characteristic-space decompositions and exponents for represented parabolics: proof

Author: Worker 4684. Module `Langlands.Representation.Exponents.ArbitraryParabolicExponentProof`, namespace `Langlands.Aubert.General.RepresentedParabolic`.

`arbitraryParabolicExponent : ArbitraryParabolicExponentStatement.{u, v}` proves the published proposition `Langlands.Aubert.General.RepresentedParabolic.ArbitraryParabolicExponentStatement` unconditionally. The field universe `u` and the representation-carrier universe `v` are independent. The theorem's type is the unapplied proposition itself. It adds no hypothesis, so every quantifier and datum of the statement is kept: the field, valuation, topology, local-field and Hausdorff hypotheses; the connected reductive group with its canonical rational-point topology and the local-compactness and nonarchimedean-group instances; every represented parabolic `p`, Levi `l`, radical `r`, multiplication isomorphism and split component `a`; every split component `aG` of `G`; and every compatible point homomorphism `e`. Representations need only the admissibility and finite length that the statement already assumes. No Jacquet-admissibility, Iwahori-contraction, finite-generation, existence-of-`e` or irreducibility premise appears.

## The implemented proof

The proof is a composition of published Library theorems. It has three steps.

1. **Jacquet admissibility.** The published conditional theorem `arbitraryParabolicJacquetAdmissibility_of_dynamic` (module `ArbitraryParabolicJacquetAdmissibilityReduction`) proves `ArbitraryParabolicJacquetAdmissibilityStatement.{u, v}` from `ParabolicDynamicStatement.{u}` and `DynamicGeometricUnipotentRadicalStatement.{u}`. The proof supplies both inputs with published unconditional theorems:
   - `Langlands.GroupScheme.parabolicDynamic` (module `GroupScheme.Absolute.ParabolicDynamicProof`), Milne's Theorem 25.1 in the Library's form: over every field, a closed subgroup of a connected reductive group is parabolic exactly when its range is the dynamic parabolic `P(χ)` of a cocharacter `χ` defined over the field;
   - `Langlands.GroupScheme.dynamicGeometricUnipotentRadical` (module `GroupScheme.Dynamic.GeometricUnipotentRadicalProof`): the identity-limit subgroup of a cocharacter is the full geometric unipotent radical of its dynamic parabolic.

   This gives, for every admissible `π`, admissibility of the actual normalized Jacquet object `(jacquetFunctor p l r hp hr hl hmul).obj π`. Inside the imported reduction, `DynamicLeviConjugacy.exists_cocharacter_parabolic_levi` realizes the given parabolic and rational Levi by one cocharacter. The literal radical and Levi point images are then identified with the dynamic radical and centralizer. `RationalPointFamily.dynamic_hasOppositeIwahoriContractionBasis` supplies an Iwahori contraction basis for this realization. Finally `isAdmissible_normalizedJacquetFunctor_obj_of_iwahoriContraction` gives admissibility of the existing coinvariant quotient with its inverse-half modulus twist.

2. **Quotient compactness.** For every represented parabolic `p` with `hp : IsParabolicSubgroup p`, the published `compactSpace_leftQuotient_pointImage_of_dynamic` (module `ArbitraryParabolicFiniteSupportReduction`) gives `CompactSpace (Induction.LeftQuotient (pointImage p))` from `ParabolicDynamicStatement.{u}` and `BruhatTits.DynamicParabolicQuotientCompactStatement.{u}`. It writes the literal point image of `p` as the rational points of a dynamic parabolic and takes the `Pg`-quotient half of the dynamic compactness statement. The proof supplies the two inputs with `parabolicDynamic` and the published unconditional `Langlands.BruhatTits.dynamicParabolicQuotientCompact` (module `BruhatTits.Parabolic.QuotientCompactProof`). The latter identifies `G(F)/P(F)` with the points of the proper algebraic flag quotient, whose point space over a local field is compact. The proof term introduces the field, group, affineness instance, canonical topology, ambient instances and parabolic exactly as `ArbitraryParabolicQuotientCompactStatement` binds them, so it proves that statement at universe `u`.

3. **Assembly.** The published `arbitraryParabolicExponent_reduction` (module `ArbitraryParabolicExponentReduction`) proves the implication from these two statements to `ArbitraryParabolicExponentStatement.{u, v}`. Applying it to the terms of steps 1 and 2 gives the theorem. The reduction proves the clauses as follows.
   - Clause 1: `normalizedJacquetWeightSpace_decomposition_of_admissible` turns admissibility of the actual normalized Jacquet object into independence (`iSupIndep`) and spanning (`⨆ = ⊤`) of the characteristic spaces `normalizedJacquetWeightSpace` over `normalizedJacquetExponents`. For finite length, it installs the compactness instance of step 2 and applies `finite_normalizedJacquetExponents_of_hasFiniteLength_of_compactQuotient_of_spanning`. That theorem takes finite generation of `π` from finite length and passes it to the Jacquet module through the compact quotient. Together with spanning, this makes the exponent set finite.
   - Clause 2: `groupWeightSpace_decomposition` for the given `aG`, `haG` and admissible `V`.
   - Clause 3: `inducedExponent_restrict` gives part (i) and `normalizedJacquetExponent_restrict` gives part (ii), for the supplied compatible `e`. Part (i) reflects a nonzero `A_G`-characteristic space of the induced representation to the inducing Levi representation through the central evaluation intertwiner, using that the half modulus is one at ambient central points. Spanning of the admissible `W` then gives a Levi exponent whose composition with `e` is `χ`. Part (ii) reflects the restricted Jacquet exponent through the central coinvariant quotient, using that the modulus twist is trivial at central points.

The Lean check of this module reports only the axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

## Source correspondence and route

The statement is David Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, French edition dated 9 July 2026, VII.1.1, printed pp.220–221 / PDF pp.226–227, registered as `renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, SHA256 `7b539996793b7c3620e72e1e389588eb42d1a8e91d6f68b2eead5c2e2ffc182c`. Renard derives (VII.1.1.1) from the admissibility of `r_P^G(π)` (Corollary of VI.6.1, printed pp.176–177 / PDF pp.182–183). The implemented proof does the same: step 1 proves that admissibility unconditionally for every represented parabolic. Renard's proofs of lemma (i) and (ii) are the arguments of the two clause-3 theorems above.

For finiteness of `Exp(A, r_P^G V)`, Renard uses finite length of the Jacquet module (VI.6.4) and Schur's lemma. The implemented route instead uses finite generation of the Jacquet module, obtained from compactness of the parabolic quotient (Corollary of V.5.1, printed p.123 / PDF p.129), together with spanning. No finite-length preservation for the Jacquet functor is used. The compactness proof follows the algebraic flag quotient rather than Renard's adapted compact subgroup. The `P = G` remark is proved directly for `groupWeightSpace`, as the statement formulates it.

This module proves only `ArbitraryParabolicExponentStatement`. It does not state or prove Casselman's criterion (VII.1.2), the existence of an inclusion `A_G ⊂ A_M` for actual Levi subgroups, or any VII.2 temperedness result.
