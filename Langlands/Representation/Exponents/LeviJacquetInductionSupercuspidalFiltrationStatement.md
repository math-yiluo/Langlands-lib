# Jacquet modules of supercuspidal inductions along conjugate Levis

Author: Worker 6. Module `Langlands.Representation.Exponents.LeviJacquetInductionSupercuspidalFiltrationStatement`, namespace `Langlands.Aubert.General.RepresentedParabolic`. Statement `LeviJacquetInductionSupercuspidalFiltrationStatement : Prop`; nothing is proved here.

The source is David Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, French edition dated 9 July 2026, VI.5.3, Proposition, assertion (iii), printed p.173 / PDF p.179, with its one-line proof on the same page. The registered source ID is `renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, SHA256 `7b539996793b7c3620e72e1e389588eb42d1a8e91d6f68b2eead5c2e2ffc182c`. In translation, the Proposition says: let `P = MN` and `Q = LU` be parabolic subgroups of `G`, `(ρ, W)` a supercuspidal representation of `M`, and put `τ = r_Q^G i_P^G ρ`. Then (iii) if `M` and `L` are standard and conjugate, `τ` has a filtration by subrepresentations whose successive quotients are of the form `wρ`, `w ∈ W(L, M)/W_L`; in particular `τ` is supercuspidal. The proof says that this follows from Lemma V.4.6 (iii), printed p.120 / PDF p.126. Assertions (i) and (ii) are the separate published statements `LeviJacquetInductionVanishingStatement` and `LeviJacquetInductionNoSupercuspidalStatement`.

This is a **derived** formulation. The functors are the published normalized functors of the represented-parabolic interface. Supercuspidality is written out as the vanishing of normalized Jacquet modules. Conjugacy is expressed through isomorphisms of group schemes compatible with inner automorphisms. The standardness hypothesis is dropped, and the index set `W(L, M)/W_L` is replaced by a set of double cosets in `P(F)\G(F)/Q(F)` (see "Faithfulness notes").

## Statement

The statement quantifies over a field `k : Type u` with `[Field k] [ValuativeRel k] [TopologicalSpace k] [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField k] [T2Space k]`, so every nonarchimedean local field of any characteristic, and over a group scheme `G` over `Spec k` with `hG : IsConnectedReductive G`. It installs `hG.isAffineHom` and the canonical topology `RationalPointFamily.pointTopology` on `rationalPoints G`, and assumes `[LocallyCompactSpace (rationalPoints G)]` and `[NonarchimedeanGroup (rationalPoints G)]` for that topology. It then quantifies over two actual rational parabolic triples:

- `P = MN`: `p : P ⟶ G`, `l : M ⟶ P`, `r : N ⟶ P` with `hp`, `hl`, `hr`, `hmul`;
- `Q = LU`: `q : Q ⟶ G`, `lQ : L ⟶ Q`, `rQ : U ⟶ Q` with `hq`, `hlQ`, `hrQ`, `hmulQ`.

It assumes that there are `g : rationalPoints G` and `φ : M ≅ L` with `φ.hom ≫ lQ ≫ q = l ≫ p ≫ (G.innerIso g).hom`, that is, `g M g⁻¹ = L` as subgroup schemes of `G`. This is exactly the negation of the hypothesis of the (ii) statement. Here `(G.innerIso g).hom` is conjugation `x ↦ g x g⁻¹`; the published `mem_pointImage_comp_innerIso` records this direction on rational points.

On `rationalPoints M` and `rationalPoints L` it installs the affine instances, the canonical topologies `leviPointTopology l hl` and `leviPointTopology lQ hlQ`, and the derived local-compactness and nonarchimedean-group instances from `ArbitraryParabolicLeviPoints`. Let `ρ : SmoothRep.{0, u, v} ℂ (rationalPoints M)` be supercuspidal: for every actual rational parabolic `p' : P' ⟶ M` with rational Levi `l'`, matching radical `r'` and `hmul'`, `¬ IsIso p'` implies `Subsingleton ((jacquetFunctor p' l' r' hp' hr' hl' hmul').obj ρ)`. Write

`τ = (leviJacquetFunctor q lQ rQ hq hrQ hlQ hmulQ).obj ((leviInductionFunctor p l r hp hr hl hmul).obj ρ)`,

a smooth representation of `L(F)` on a carrier in `Type (max u v)`. The statement asserts the conjunction of two claims.

**The filtration.** Let `I` be the subtype of `DoubleCoset.Quotient (pointImage p) (pointImage q)`, the double cosets `P(F) x Q(F)` in `G(F)`, consisting of those cosets that contain some `w : rationalPoints G` with some `ψ : L ≅ M` such that `ψ.hom ≫ l ≫ p = lQ ≫ q ≫ (G.innerIso w).hom`, that is, `w L w⁻¹ = M`. There exist

- `m : ℕ` and a bijection `c : Fin m ≃ I`;
- for each `i : Fin m`, an element `z i : rationalPoints G` whose double coset is `(c i).1`, and an isomorphism `φ i : L ≅ M` with `(φ i).hom ≫ l ≫ p = lQ ≫ q ≫ (G.innerIso (z i)).hom`, so `z i` itself conjugates `L` onto `M`;
- for each `i`, a topological-group isomorphism `e i : rationalPoints L ≃ₜ* rationalPoints M` with `e i x = rationalPoints.map (φ i).hom x`, that is, `e i x = z i · x · (z i)⁻¹` inside `G(F)`;
- an `ObjectRepFiltration` `s` of `SmoothRep.toRep.obj τ` with `m` steps, such that for every `i` the `i`th graded representation `s.gradedRepresentation i` is isomorphic, by a `Representation.Equiv`, to `(SmoothRep.pullback (e i) ρ).representation`, the representation `x ↦ ρ(z i · x · (z i)⁻¹)` of `L(F)` on the space of `ρ`.

**Supercuspidality.** For every actual rational parabolic `p' : P' ⟶ L` with rational Levi `l'`, matching radical `r'` and `hmul'`, `¬ IsIso p'` implies `Subsingleton ((jacquetFunctor p' l' r' hp' hr' hl' hmul').obj τ)`.

## Faithfulness notes

- **Conjugates `wρ`.** Since `τ` is a representation of `L`, `wρ` for `w ∈ W(L, M)` (so `w·L = M`) is the representation `x ↦ ρ(w x w⁻¹)` of `L`. This is the convention of Remark 2 after the geometric lemma VI.5.1 (printed p.161 / PDF p.167): the functor `σ ↦ wσ` comes from the isomorphism `L ∩ w⁻¹·M → w·L ∩ M` induced by conjugation by `w`. The statement uses `SmoothRep.pullback (e i) ρ` with `e i x = z i · x · (z i)⁻¹`. This is the direction of the field `e` of `GeometricLemmaIntersectionDatum` in the published `RepresentedGeometricLemmaStatement`.
- **Index set.** In the source, `M` and `L` are standard and the quotients are indexed by `W(L, M)/W_L`, where `W(L, M) = {w ∈ W_G | w·L = M}` (V.4.3.2, printed p.119 / PDF p.125). The statement indexes the quotients by the set `I` of double cosets `P(F) x Q(F)` that contain an element conjugating `L` onto `M`, and each such coset contributes exactly one quotient. For standard `P` and `Q`, `I` is in bijection with `W(L, M)/W_L`, for the following reasons. By the consequence of the Bruhat decomposition in V.4.5 (printed p.119 / PDF p.125) and Lemma V.4.6 (i), the double cosets `P\G/Q` correspond bijectively to `W_M\W_G/W_L` and to the set `W_{L,M}` of minimal-length representatives. Let `x = a w b` with `a ∈ P`, `b ∈ Q`, `w ∈ W_{L,M}` and `x L x⁻¹ = M`. Then `xQx⁻¹ = a(wQw⁻¹)a⁻¹`, and `M = xLx⁻¹ ⊂ P ∩ xQx⁻¹`. Since `M` is a Levi factor of `xQx⁻¹`, the image of `P ∩ xQx⁻¹` in `xQx⁻¹/xUx⁻¹` is everything, and conjugating by `a⁻¹`, so is the image of `P ∩ w·Q` in `w·Q/w·U`. By the decomposition `P ∩ w·Q = (P ∩ w·L)(P ∩ w·U)` of Lemma V.4.6 (ii), this image is `P ∩ w·L`. Hence `w·L ⊂ P`, so the parabolic subgroup `L ∩ w⁻¹·P` of `L` is `L` itself, and so is its Levi factor `L ∩ w⁻¹·M`; thus `w·L ⊂ M`. The symmetric argument, with `Q ∩ w⁻¹·P = (Q ∩ w⁻¹·M)(Q ∩ w⁻¹·N)`, gives `M ⊂ w·L`. Hence `w·L = M` and `w ∈ W_{L,M} ∩ W(L, M)`. Conversely, a lift of such a `w` lies in its double coset and conjugates `L` onto `M`. So `I ≃ W_{L,M} ∩ W(L, M)`, which Lemma V.4.6 (iii) identifies with `W(L, M)/W_L`. This is the correspondence the source's proof cites.
- **Representatives.** The source's quotients are `wρ` for lifts `w` in `G` of elements of `W(L, M)`. The statement asks for one element `z i` of each coset in `I` with `z i · L · (z i)⁻¹ = M`, chosen existentially together with the filtration. It does not assert that the quotient is independent of this choice.
- **Standardness dropped.** The source assumes `M` and `L` standard so that `W(L, M)` is defined. The statement makes no standardness assumption, since standard parabolics are not part of the represented-parabolic interface. By V.4.7 (printed pp.121–122 / PDF pp.127–128), `P` and `Q` are conjugate to standard parabolics `P₁ = gPg⁻¹` and `Q₁ = hQh⁻¹` with `M₁ = gMg⁻¹` and `L₁ = hLh⁻¹`. The map `x ↦ g x h⁻¹` sends `P\G/Q` onto `P₁\G/Q₁` and the set `I` onto the corresponding set for `P₁` and `Q₁`, and the functors and conjugates transport along these conjugations. So for arbitrary `P` and `Q` the statement is the source assertion transported from the standard case.
- **Multiplicities and order.** The source's "quotients of the form `wρ`, `w ∈ W(L, M)/W_L`" is read as one quotient for each class, as in the geometric lemma, which has one graded piece for each double coset. The order of the quotients is left existential, through `c`. The source's order is a total order refining the Bruhat order (end of V.4.5, printed p.120 / PDF p.126).
- **Filtration on the underlying representation.** The filtration is of `SmoothRep.toRep.obj τ`, an abstract representation of `L(F)`. Its steps are `L(F)`-invariant subspaces of the smooth representation `τ`, so they are smooth subrepresentations, as in the source. The graded pieces are compared with `pullback (e i) ρ` as representations.
- **The homeomorphisms `e i`.** The `e i` are part of the existential, with `e_apply` fixing them as the point maps of `φ i`. The canonical topologies on `L(F)` and `M(F)` are functorial for isomorphisms of group schemes, so this adds no assertion beyond the mathematical content.
- **Supercuspidality.** The second conjunct is the source's "in particular `τ` is supercuspidal", stated as in the hypothesis on `ρ`. It is asserted for all `τ`, without irreducibility, admissibility or finite-length assumptions on `ρ`, as in the source.

## Remaining obligations

The proof is a separate obligation. The source derives (iii) from the geometric lemma VI.5.1 and Lemma V.4.6 (iii). The graded pieces of the geometric lemma are `i^L_{L ∩ w⁻¹·P} ∘ w ∘ r^M_{w·Q ∩ M}(ρ)` for `w` in the representative set `W_{Q,P}` of V.4.7. Supercuspidality of `ρ` kills every piece with `w·Q ∩ M ≠ M`. For the remaining `w`, `M ⊂ w·Q` and its Levi factor `w·L ∩ M` is `M`; conjugacy of `M` and `L` then gives `w·L = M`, and the piece is `wρ`. Removing the zero pieces gives the filtration. Supercuspidality of `τ` follows because Jacquet functors are exact (VI.1.2) and each `wρ` is supercuspidal. The relevant published statements are `RepresentedGeometricLemmaStatement`, `LeviParabolicFunctorsExactStatement` and the (i) statement `LeviJacquetInductionVanishingStatement`. A proof also needs to compare the representative set `W_{Q,P}` of the published geometric lemma with the set `I`, and to transport supercuspidality along `e i`.
