# Algebraic decomposition for a finite central action

Worker 1629 adapts the Artinian-algebra argument of Worker 23's published
`Langlands.Representation.Exponents.FiniteActionSpectrumProof` from the complex
field to an arbitrary algebraically closed field. The scalar field and all
carrier universes remain independent. These are internal helper results for
Casselman 1995, Proposition 2.1.9, p.21.

Every maximal ideal of a finite scalar algebra is the kernel of a scalar
character, since its residue field is finite and integral over the algebraically
closed coefficient field. The published
`Langlands.GroupScheme.KostantRosenlicht.algHom_eq_of_ker_eq` shows that kernels
distinguish characters, and the Artinian ring
has finitely many maximal ideals. Separating elements and nilpotence of the
Jacobson radical give elements annihilated by one power of each character
kernel whose sum is a unit. Applying these elements and the inverse of their
sum to any vector gives its decomposition. The defect of any group element
from its character value lies in the corresponding kernel. Therefore the SAME
power annihilates every such defect on each component. The result is the
supremum of uniform finite-exponent spaces, not an interchange of an infinite
intersection with an increasing union.

The implemented proof uses Mathlib's Artinian Jacobson-radical nilpotence,
finiteness of maximal ideals, finite scalar algebras, and algebraic closedness.
`finite_action_spanning` applies the argument to the commutative algebra
generated by a commuting group action on a finite-dimensional vector space.
The zero space is included. No topology or smoothness is asserted here.

The helper imports the unchanged target module
`Langlands.Representation.Exponents.CentralStatement` and Mathlib's
`LinearAlgebra.Eigenspace.Pi`, `RingTheory.Artinian.Ring` and
`RingTheory.Jacobson.Ideal`. Its main algebraic dependencies are
`IsAlgClosed.algebraMap_bijective_of_isIntegral`,
`IsArtinianRing.of_finite`,
`IsArtinianRing.setOfPred_isMaximal_finite`,
`IsArtinianRing.isNilpotent_jacobson_bot`, and `Ideal.mem_jacobson_bot`.
The commutative operator algebra is constructed by `Algebra.adjoin` and
`Algebra.isMulCommutative_adjoin`. It is finite over the scalar field because
it is a submodule of the finite-dimensional endomorphism space of the finite
carrier. The result has no characteristic-zero assumption.

The source is W. Casselman, *Introduction to the theory of admissible
representations of p-adic reductive groups*, draft of 1 May 1995, registered as
`casselman-1995-admissible-representations`, SHA-256
`78b921fc6331ad2b75389f37d15bd5ac3399e6cfc2d7555e1a4d666e0d42612a`.
These helper statements are internal algebraic prerequisites implementing the
commuting-operator facts invoked for Proposition 2.1.9 on printed/PDF p.21;
they are not additional claims of separately numbered results in that source.
