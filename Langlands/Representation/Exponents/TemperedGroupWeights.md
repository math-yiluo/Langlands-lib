# Generalized split-component weights of tempered representations

This internal derived result supports the Hom restriction step of Renard,
*Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques* (9 July 2026), VII.2.4,
printed p.229 / PDF p.235. It uses the published normalized-exponent
formulation of the full-group unitarity assertion in VII.2.1, Remark 1.

The identity parabolic has the canonical trivial geometric unipotent radical.
Its actual normalized Jacquet quotient intertwines the action of the central
split component because the modulus is one on ambient central elements. The
endpoint coinvariant equivalence makes this quotient injective. Consequently
every nonzero generalized eigenvector of the original representation remains
a nonzero generalized eigenvector in the identity Jacquet module. The published
full-group exponent theorem then gives zero normalized real part and pointwise
unitarity. The final lemma excludes a nonzero generalized weight space for any
character with nonzero normalized real part.

No scalar central character, admissibility of a Jacquet module, finite length,
or additional structural hypothesis is assumed. This is supporting algebra;
it does not by itself prove any of the three VII.2.3–VII.2.4 task statements.
