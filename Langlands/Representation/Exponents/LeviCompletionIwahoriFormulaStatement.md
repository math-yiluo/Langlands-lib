# The opposite Jacquet completion formula at all Iwahori indices

`Langlands.Aubert.General.RepresentedParabolic.LeviCompletionIwahoriFormulaStatement`
extends the component characterization in Renard, *Représentations des
groupes réductifs p-adiques*, French edition of 9 July 2026, VI.9.7, printed
pp. 205–206 (PDF pp. 211–212). The theorem there characterizes the
completion comparison by its components at all compact open subgroups
with an Iwahori decomposition.

Let \(P=MN\) and \(\bar P=M\bar N\) be opposite rational parabolics of a
connected reductive group over a non-archimedean local field \(F\), with
the same embedded Levi. For a smooth complex representation \(V\), fix
a compatible family \(x\) in \(\bar V^{N(F)}\) and a compatible family
\(y\) in the compact-open completion of the unnormalized opposite Jacquet
module \(V_{\bar N}\). Use the quotient map \(q:V\to V_{\bar N}\) and
denote by \(K_M\) the pullback of \(K\cap M(F)\) to the rational points of
the Levi.

If \(y_{L_M}=q(x_L)\) for every compact open \(L\) admitting Iwahori
contraction by an element central in \(M(F)\), in the order
\((L\cap N(F))L_M(L\cap\bar N(F))\), then \(y_{K_M}=q(x_K)\) for
every compact open \(K\) admitting the factorization
\((K\cap\bar N(F))K_M(K\cap N(F))\). Inversion exchanges the two
factorization orders. The implication isolates the extension of the
component formula from cofinal contraction indices using compatibility
under compact averaging and \(N(F)\)-fixedness of \(x\).
