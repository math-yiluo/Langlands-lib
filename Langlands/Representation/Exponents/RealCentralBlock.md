# Grouped generalized central characters and real central blocks

This file defines internal constructions for later central-block arguments. It
defines subrepresentations and their restricted maps only. It proves no
decomposition, isolation or Hom comparison theorem.

## Grouped generalized central-character spaces

Let `ρ` be a representation of a group `G` on an `R`-module `V`, where `R` is
a commutative ring. Let `P` be an arbitrary predicate on full central
characters `Subgroup.center G →* Rˣ`. Then `groupedCentralCharacterSpace ρ P`
is the actual `Subrepresentation ρ` whose underlying submodule is

`⨆ χ with P χ, generalizedCentralCharacterSpace ρ χ`.

The index type is the subtype of characters satisfying `P`. Here
`generalizedCentralCharacterSpace` is the existing uniform generalized space of
`Representation.Exponents.Central`. A vector `v` lies in it when there is
one `n` such that `(ρ(z) - χ(z) • 1)^n v = 0` for every central `z`.

The supremum is the submodule generated by the selected spaces. Its elements
are finite sums of vectors that may come from different selected characters.
A vector of the group need not have one character of its own. The definition
imposes no finiteness, independence or direct-sum condition on the summands.
Invariance under `ρ` is proved by induction over the supremum. Each selected
generalized space is invariant, and so are zero and sums.

The API consists of the following results.

- `generalizedCentralCharacterSpace_le_grouped`: the generalized space of every
  character satisfying `P` lies in the group.
- `groupedCentralCharacterSpace_mono`: if `P χ` implies `Q χ` for every `χ`,
  then the `P`-group lies in the `Q`-group.
- `map_mem_groupedCentralCharacterSpace`: an actual intertwiner
  `f : ρ.IntertwiningMap σ` maps the `P`-group of `ρ` into the `P`-group of
  `σ`. The proof again inducts over the supremum, using the existing
  `map_mem_generalizedCentralCharacterSpace` on each selected space.
- `groupedCentralCharacterMap f P`: the restriction of that same `f` to an
  actual intertwiner between the two grouped subrepresentations. The lemma
  `groupedCentralCharacterMap_apply` states that its underlying vector is
  `f v`.
- `groupedCentralCharacterEquiv e P`: the restriction of an actual equivariant
  linear equivalence `e : ρ.Equiv σ`. Its inverse is the restriction of
  `e.symm` to the same predicate.
- `groupedCentralCharacterSpace_eq_top_of_scalar`: suppose a character `χ` with
  `P χ` satisfies `ρ(z) v = χ(z) • v` for all central `z` and all vectors `v`.
  Then the group is `⊤`. Every vector lies in the exponent-one space of `χ`,
  by the existing `mem_centralCharacterSpace_one`. The lemma assumes no
  nonzero vector, irreducibility, smoothness or finiteness.

The universes of the ring, the group and the two carriers (`uR`, `uG`, `uV`,
`uW`) are independent.

## Real central blocks of product Levi representations

Let `T : F.TopologicalData` be p-adic topological data for `F`, let `B` be a
block shape, and let `ρ` be a complex representation of the product Levi
`B.Levi F.Carrier`. For a vector `x : Fin B.length → ℝ`, the definition
`T.realCentralBlock B ρ x` is `groupedCentralCharacterSpace ρ P`. The
predicate `P χ` on full central characters
`χ : Subgroup.center (B.Levi F.Carrier) →* ℂˣ` is the conjunction of the
following two conditions.

1. The kernel of `χ` is open in the centre. The topology is fixed by
   `letI := T.topology`: the Levi carries the product topology of its
   general linear factors (the instance that `T.leviTopology B` packages),
   and the centre carries the induced subspace topology. The condition has
   the same form as the open-kernel conditions in `IsCentralExponent` and in
   the `isOpen_ker` field of `UnitaryCentralCharacter`.
2. `T.IsRealPartOf (χ.comp (B.blockScalarToCenter F.Carrier)) x`. Let `ψ` be
   the restriction of `χ` along the actual block-scalar inclusion
   `blockScalarToCenter` into the full centre. The condition says that
   `‖ψ(a)‖ = ∏ i, T.normalizedAbs (a i) ^ x i` for every tuple of units `a`,
   which is the residue-normalized real part of `Exponents.Jacquet`.

The predicate is stated for the full character `χ` and its actual restriction.
It does not choose an extracted real-part function, a character extension, or
an inverse of the centre isomorphism `blockScalarCenterEquiv`. The coordinate
`x` is indexed by blocks and is the real part of the block-scalar restriction
itself. The file does not convert it to size-weighted twist coordinates; any
such twist bridge belongs elsewhere.

`generalizedCentralCharacterSpace_le_realCentralBlock` states that the
generalized space of each character satisfying both conditions lies in the
block. `T.realCentralBlockMap B f x` and `T.realCentralBlockEquiv B e x` are
the generic restrictions `groupedCentralCharacterMap` and
`groupedCentralCharacterEquiv` for this predicate. The universe `uF` of the
field and the carrier universes `uV` and `uW` are independent.

## Nilpotent directions

The block is built from the full generalized spaces, not from exponent-one
(scalar) eigenspaces. The exponent `n` is uniform in `z` for each vector, but
may vary between vectors. A vector of a qualifying generalized space therefore
lies in the block even when the centre does not act on it by scalars. The
block is a subrepresentation of `ρ`, and no quotient is taken. So nontrivial
self-extensions of a qualifying central character are kept. No semisimplicity
is assumed.

## Limitations

No smoothness, admissibility, finite length, dimension or support assumption
is placed on `ρ`. Without such assumptions the blocks need not exhaust the
representation. The file proves no independence or direct-sum statement for
different characters or different vectors `x`. It constructs no complement and
no canonical projector. It does not show that restrictions of surjective maps
are surjective. It makes no claim about Jacquet compatibility, finiteness of
central spectra, geometric filtrations, isolation of a leading block,
factorization of Hom spaces, or dimension bounds. These require additional
hypotheses and separate proofs.

## Provenance

This is an internal construction from public Library definitions: the uniform
generalized central-character spaces and their intertwiner preservation in
`Representation.Exponents.Central`, the block-scalar inclusion
`GeneralLinear.BlockShape.blockScalarToCenter` in
`Representation.Classification.ProductLeviCentralCharacter`, and the real-part
predicate `PadicField.TopologicalData.IsRealPartOf` in
`Representation.Exponents.Jacquet`. It is not a literal transcription of a
literature definition and relies on no new literature. The open-kernel
condition matches the convention of `IsCentralExponent`, which cites
Casselman's 1995 notes, section 2.1. The supremum construction itself is
elementary.
