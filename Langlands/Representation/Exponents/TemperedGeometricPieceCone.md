# Restriction preserves nonzero vectors in the nonnegative cone of `P'`

Author: Worker 1221. Module `Langlands.Representation.Exponents.TemperedGeometricPieceCone`,
namespace `Langlands.Aubert.General.RepresentedParabolic.GeometricLemmaIntersectionDatum`.
This is an internal supporting module for the proof of Renard VII.2.5
(`TemperedGeometricLemmaStatement`).

## Setting

`D : GeometricLemmaIntersectionDatum p l r … q lQ rQ … z` is an intersection datum of the
published represented geometric lemma (`RepresentedGeometricLemmaStatement`) for
parabolics `P = M N` and `Q = L U` of `G` and a point `z`. It provides the parabolic
`P' = M' N'` of `M` (maps `D.p'`, `D.l'`, `D.r'`), the parabolic `Q' = L' U'` of `L`
(maps `D.q'`, `D.lQ'`, `D.rQ'`) and the isomorphism `D.φ : L' ≅ M'`. The ambient
instances are `[IsAffineHom G.X.hom] [IsAffineHom M.X.hom] [Smooth G.X.hom]
[Smooth M.X.hom] [LocallyOfFiniteType M.X.hom]`. The data are:

- split tori `A`, `A'`, with `a : A → L` and `a' : A' → L'`;
- `τ : A ⟶ A'` with `hτ : τ ≫ a' ≫ D.lQ' ≫ D.q' = a`;
- a parabolic root presentation `presQ` of `(q, lQ, a)` and a parabolic root
  presentation `presM` of `(D.p', D.l', a' ≫ D.φ.hom)`.

`presM.nonnegCone ⊆ Vstar A'` is the published nonnegative cone of `P'` (the real cone
generated by its restricted roots). `rationalCharacterRestrictReal τ` is the real
restriction map `Vstar A' → Vstar A`. `presQ.splitComponentCocharacter` is the defining
cocharacter of `A` for `Q`.

## Declarations

| Lean name | Statement |
| --- | --- |
| `pair_restrictReal_nonneg_and_eq_zero τ hτ presQ presM hx` | For `x ∈ presM.nonnegCone`, the pairing of `rationalCharacterRestrictReal τ x` with `1 ⊗ presQ.splitComponentCocharacter` is `≥ 0`, and it is `0` only if `x = 0`. |
| `restrictReal_ne_zero_of_mem_nonnegCone τ hτ presQ presM hx hx0` | If `x ∈ presM.nonnegCone` and `x ≠ 0`, then `rationalCharacterRestrictReal τ x ≠ 0`. |

## Proof

The cone is the span of the restricted roots of `presM` over nonnegative reals
(`restrictedRootNonnegCone_eq_hull`), so the first lemma is by induction over that span.

- Generators: every restricted root of `presM` is `1 ⊗ β` for an intrinsic positive root
  `β` of `presM` (`restrictedRoots_subset_image_intrinsicPositiveRoots`). By the published
  `comp_mem_intrinsicPositiveRoots` (`RadicalWeightTransfer.lean`), `τ ≫ β` is an intrinsic
  positive root of `presQ`. By definition it pairs strictly positively with the defining
  cocharacter of `presQ`.
- Sums: two nonnegative numbers with sum zero are both zero.
- Nonnegative scalars `c • y`: the pairing is `c` times the pairing of `y`. If it is zero,
  then `c = 0` or `y = 0`.

The second lemma follows from the first: a zero restriction has pairing zero.

Thus the zero fibre of restriction on the cone is `{0}`. Distinct nonzero cone
vectors can have the same restriction; these lemmas do not assert injectivity on
the cone. Nonvanishing is the property used in `TemperedGeometricPiece`.

## Provenance

This is the last step of Renard's proof of VII.2.5 (David Renard, *Représentations des
groupes réductifs p-adiques*, 2026, VII.2.5, printed p.230 / PDF p.236). There, for
`χ' ∈ Exp(A_{w·L∩M}, r^M_{w·Q∩M} W)` with `ℜ(χ') ≠ 0`, one writes
`ℜ(χ') = ∑ c_α α + μ` with some `c_α > 0`. Then `(w⁻¹·α)|_{𝔞_L} ≠ 0` because `w⁻¹·α` is
a weight of `A_∅` on the Lie algebra of `U`, and `A_L` has no zero weight there.

The Lean route is different. It does not use standard parabolics, the simple roots
`Δ^M_∅` or a Weyl element `w`. It uses the intrinsic positive roots of the presentations:
the relative roots of the split component that pair positively with the defining
cocharacter. `RadicalWeightTransfer.lean` compares them through the weights on the
unipotent radicals. The argument then pairs with the defining cocharacter of `Q`, which is
strictly positive on every intrinsic positive root of `Q`. This strict positivity replaces
Renard's `(w⁻¹·α)|_{𝔞_L} ≠ 0` and applies directly to nonnegative combinations of several
roots. This module does not use temperedness. In `TemperedGeometricPiece`, temperedness of
`ρ` puts the real part of the exponent in the cone of `P'`, as in the source.
