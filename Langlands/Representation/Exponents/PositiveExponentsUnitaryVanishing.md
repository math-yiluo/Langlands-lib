# Strict normalized exponents force unitary Jacquet vanishing

This derived adapter keeps the original local field, its universe, the
independent representation-carrier universe, the ambient rational-point
topology, and the actual parabolic, Levi, geometric radical, multiplication
isomorphism and split component. The ambient group is connected reductive.
It takes one compatible root presentation and strict-cone membership for
each normalized exponent along that specified parabolic.

For a proper parabolic, the accompanying restricted-root construction
excludes zero from this strict cone. Therefore every actual normalized
exponent has nonzero normalized real part. The published characterization
`unitaryJacquetPart_eq_bot_iff` gives vanishing of the original unitary part.
The proof requires neither temperedness nor a central character.

The spectral positivity premise is explicit. This module proves no
analytic square-integrability assertion and assumes no version of
Casselman's criterion. To apply it to the forward implication of VII.2.4,
temperedness supplies presentation existence and the separately owned
Casselman criterion must supply the stated strict positivity.
