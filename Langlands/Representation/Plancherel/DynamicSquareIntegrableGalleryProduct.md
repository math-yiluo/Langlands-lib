# The represented gallery product for square-integrable inducing data

The inducing representation is smooth and irreducible on the literal Levi.
It is square integrable modulo the supplied central subgroup for the given
quotient Haar measure. The construction chooses a parameter at which
induction at the represented gallery vertex is irreducible. Start with the
published nonempty open locus of irreducible induction from this representation.
Transport one fiber through the represented induction equivalence and identify
its coefficient with the twist of the pullback along the Levi homeomorphism.

For an arbitrary pair of cocharacters with that common Levi, choose the
published distance-additive gallery. A same-chamber step supplies a constant
scalar factor. At a wall step, the irreducible parameter supplies a principal
irreducibility locus. Intersect it with the two ordered regularity loci and use
the wall scalar construction, whose factor is invariant under complement
translations. Intersect the finitely many edge loci and the gallery regularity
locus. The represented gallery product gives the scalar identity on this
dense ambient open set, including the fixed Haar constant.

The irreducible-fiber transport and the edge argument follow the proof of
`conditionalDynamicOppositeUnitaryScalar`. The construction here uses the
represented gallery between arbitrary endpoints. It supplies the scalar
product used in the common-Levi specialization of Waldspurger,
*La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques (d'après Harish-Chandra)*,
IV.1(12) and V.3.1, printed pp. 283 and 295.
