# Compact coefficient integrals under actual translation

`integral_leviIntegrand_translated` compares the two compact probability
integrals that define the coefficients before and after translation.
The translated product section is evaluated at
\((g^{-1}kx,g^{-1}k)\), with its inducing vector and smooth dual carried
to the conjugated Levi. The dual contraction is unchanged. The variable
\(g^{-1}k\) ranges over the same compact since \(g\) lies in it, and left
invariance of its Haar probability gives the integral identity.

This is the compact-point translation step for the coefficient identity
of Waldspurger V.3.1, printed p. 295 (PDF p. 61), with the compact
probability of I.1 and the coefficient formula of V.1.
