# The intertwining quotient inside an intermediate Levi

Suppose the target radical lies in an outer parabolic and that both radicals
contain the outer radical. Then the projection from the outer parabolic to
its Levi restricts to a split projection from the target radical to its
intersection with the Levi. Its kernel lies in the intersection of the two
ambient radicals. The section is the inclusion of the inner target radical.
The split left-quotient homeomorphism gives the exact inner standard
intertwining quotient. Pushforward takes a right-invariant ambient quotient
measure to a right-invariant inner quotient measure. The proof uses the
right-translation identity for the quotient chart and the fact that the
projection of the section is the identity.
