# Distance-additive galleries for dynamic parabolics with a common Levi

A generic rational segment crosses at most one proportional root class at each
crossing. The sparse-interval construction gives a finite chain along the
segment. Since each root is affine on the segment, if it is strictly positive
at both outer points of an ordered triple then it is strictly positive at the
middle point. The same holds for strictly negative signs. Perturbing the last
point within its chamber preserves these endpoint sign implications.

For actual cocharacters from positive integral realizations, the root
criterion for intersections of dynamic radicals turns the positive sign
implication into the first radical containment. Applying it to the negatives
turns the negative sign implication into the opposite radical containment.
These two containments are precisely the distance condition used by the
common-Levi composition theorem. The argument is derived from the Library's
finite hyperplane gallery and dynamic radical lemmas.

`HasDistanceAdditiveDynamicGallery` retains these two containments relative to
both endpoints at every step. Its proof factors both given cocharacters
integrally through one split component of the actual centralizer representative.
It takes the rational segment in the cocharacter space of that split component
and realizes the vertex directions by positive integral multiples. At a wall
the Library's wall model supplies the outer parabolic and Levi, as well as
the two opposite maximal proper inner parabolics with the original common Levi.
The outer radical is contained in both ambient radicals by the signs of the
roots nonzero on the wall. A same-chamber step identifies the parabolic and both
radicals by their root signs. The same identifications connect the chosen
integral realizations at the ends to the input cocharacters.
