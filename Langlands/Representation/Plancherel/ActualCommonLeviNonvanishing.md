# Nonvanishing of the actual continued common-Levi standard operator

Choose a small compact open subgroup with an Iwahori factorization for the
source and its opposite, on which the chosen inducing vector gives a test
section. The common-centralizer product chart writes each point of the target
radical as a product in the source radical and a complementary subgroup of
the source opposite radical. A point also belonging to the source parabolic
times the small compact subgroup therefore has its complement in that compact
subgroup. This supplies the local product property for the actual target
radical.

In the fixed compact picture, the test section has a parameter-independent
value under the standard operator at the identity. The value is the inducing
vector times the mass of a compact open quotient orbit, which is finite and
strictly positive for the actual quotient measure. The identity theorem for
linear tests of a continued operator gives this value at every regular
parameter of the actual continuation. Applying the construction to a nonzero
inducing vector gives nonvanishing of the compact and ambient operators. At
a regular parameter where the two induced representations are irreducible,
Schur's lemma gives bijectivity of the actual operator.

This implements Waldspurger (2003), IV.1(10), printed p. 283, PDF p. 49, for a
supplied actual common-Levi continuation and the original quotient measure.
