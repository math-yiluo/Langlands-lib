# Extracted scalar coefficients in the actual unramified function field

This is a derived algebraic construction toward the rational scalar in
Waldspurger IV.3, printed pp.285–286 / PDF pp.51–52 of
`waldspurger-2003-plancherel` (SHA256
`4b6a6162cd0b3863703fb2f65d1698f926caf813114917f2084c16e38f2e30f7`).
The source obtains scalarity of the opposite-intertwiner composite on a
nonempty generic locus and then regards its scalar as a rational function.
This module constructs the function field element of an extracted
coefficient of a supplied rational endomorphism family. It does not
identify that family with the source's actual standard intertwiners.

## Regular presentations and their values

For an arbitrary norm-character family and a parameter subset `Omega`,
`UnramifiedOperatorFamily.ScalarPresentation Omega f` consists of a regular
numerator `a`, a regular denominator `b` nonzero at every point of `Omega`,
and the identity `b(chi) * f(chi) = a(chi)` there. These are elements of the
existing algebra generated by actual unramified character evaluations.

If `Omega` is nonempty, its pointwise nonvanishing condition proves that
`b` is not the zero regular function. At every parameter of `Omega`,
`value_eq_div` gives `f(chi) = a(chi) / b(chi)`.

`scalarCoefficientPresentation A hA v ell` uses classical choice only to
select the numerator and whole-operator denominator provided by
`scalarCoefficient_numerator`. It presents exactly the existing coefficient
`ell (A(chi) v)`. It supplies no independent scalar normalization and
requires no topology on the complex vector space.

## The actual function field

For the remaining constructions, `G` is connected reductive over an
arbitrary-universe nonarchimedean local field of arbitrary characteristic.
A topology on its rational points and continuity of
`rationalValuationImageProjection G` are explicit. The character parameters
use every actual rational norm character of `G`. There is no splitness,
quasisplitness or positive-rank premise.

`ScalarPresentation.toFunctionField` takes a presentation on a nonempty
region and produces an element of the published
`rationalUnramifiedFunctionField G hp hG`. The published Laurent comparison
provides the domain property of the regular algebra. The denominator is
then a verified non-zero-divisor, so the construction uses the existing
localization fraction constructor. `toFunctionField_eq_div` identifies it
with the quotient of the embedded regular numerator and denominator.

For presentations of possibly different functions on possibly different
regions, agreement on a common nonempty ambient open subset forces their
regular cross-products to agree globally. The proof applies the published
`rationalUnramifiedRegular_eq_zero_of_isOpen` theorem to the difference of
the cross-products. The presentation identities and the assumed agreement
make that regular function zero on the open subset. The localization
cross-product identity therefore makes their function field elements
equal. Neither full parameter region is required to be open.

There is no evaluation map from the entire function field at a fixed
character: a denominator may vanish there. The value formulas here apply
only on the presentation's explicitly regular region.

## Extracting one function field element from an operator family

`rationalUnramifiedOperatorScalar` applies `toFunctionField` to the selected
coefficient presentation of the supplied rational endomorphism family.
`rationalUnramifiedOperatorScalar_spec` exhibits a nonzero regular
denominator, nonvanishing on the whole region, and a regular numerator.
Their quotient is the constructed function field element, and their
pointwise quotient is the extracted coefficient at every regular value.

`rationalUnramifiedOperatorScalar_eq_presentation` compares the selected
presentation with any other presentation of the same coefficient when the
region contains a nonempty ambient open subset. The final theorem also
allows different normalized pairs `(v,ell)` and `(w,m)` and different
rationality witnesses for the same operator family. If the operators are
scalar on that open subset, applying either normalized coefficient
recovers the same scalar there. Cross-product uniqueness gives equality
of the resulting function field elements.

These constructions do not prove generic scalarity or generic
irreducibility. They require the supplied rational-family premise and
do not construct its actual intertwining continuation, descent to the
representation orbit, independence of parabolic choice, duality, Weyl
invariance, nonvanishing of `j`, or the density `mu`. Those remain
separate source obligations. The module makes no main Plancherel
completion claim.
