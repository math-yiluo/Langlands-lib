# Compact cocharacter points with prescribed nonzero weights

`fieldUnitDisplacementNorm` is the additive Haar scale of the field
scalar \(1-s^d\), extended by zero at zero. For a field unit \(s\) of
normalized absolute value one, the algebraic factorization of
\(1-s^{-d}\) gives the same scale. The other factors are norm-one units.

A finite list of nonzero exponents excludes a finite union of root sets
of polynomials \(X^{|d|}-1\). The field units are nondiscrete, and the
norm-one subgroup is a neighborhood of the identity by local constancy
of the normalized norm. Hence every identity neighborhood contains a
norm-one unit avoiding all these root sets. Evaluation of the actual
cocharacter is continuous by Laurent polynomial evaluation on field
units. Pulling a compact open subgroup back by this evaluation supplies
the required identity neighborhood.

The module provides the compact point for the orbital test in the
internal Haar normalization argument for Waldspurger's Lemma V.3.1.
