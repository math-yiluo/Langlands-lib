# The exact generic compact probability adjunction

The ambient test sections lift to a source normalized vector and a target
positive-Levi section of the smooth dual of the twisted representation.
The normalized-induction duality expresses each pairing as its original
compact Haar probability integral. Restriction to the compact subgroup
and the value-preserving dual twist equivalence compute this integral
in the two fixed compact pictures.

For a nontrivial inducing carrier, the two continued operators give the
same nonzero fixed compact test value on the genuine generic locus.
At a point with admissible inducing twist and irreducible normalized
target, the Schur adjunction criterion gives scale one for all source
vectors and target dual sections. The result uses the actual forward
and inverse-parameter reverse-dual continuations in the V.3.1 comparison
and the exact probability pairing of IV.1(11).
