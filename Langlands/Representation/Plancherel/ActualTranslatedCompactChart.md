# The rational compact chart of the translated V.3 tensor

Waldspurger’s V.3 formula (1) composes left translation on the two
induced factors with the standard tensor on the conjugate Levi. IV.1
uses fixed compact pictures to express a rational operator with one
denominator for its entire carrier. The original is registered as
`waldspurger-2003-plancherel`, printed pp. 276–277 and 295, PDF pp.
42–43 and 61.

`agreesWithV3CompactChart` identifies the published compact composite
with the compact picture of the ambient comparison in the V.3.1 target.
The proof specializes the common-Levi compact square at the restricted
conjugate parameter. In the intermediate compact carrier the chart is
applied before the compact standard tensor. On the ambient route it is
applied after the ambient standard tensor through that same square.
This gives equality of linear maps after precomposition by the actual
coefficient translation and the inverse source chart.

`compactChartContinuation` starts with a global numerator and a regular
denominator for the actual pulled-back standard tensor on the simultaneous
generic locus. It composes that numerator with the polynomial compact
translation at each full source parameter. The denominator stays the
same for every input vector. The clearing identity follows from the
standard numerator identity, linearity of composition, and the compact
chart equality. The fixed compact carriers and the represented source
Levi’s regular algebra are those of the existing V.3.1 target.

`compactChart_rational_of_polynomialTranslation` gives whole-operator
rationality on the same locus with the explicit reverse-dual scalar
input and polynomiality of the actual compact translation there.
The polynomiality and scalar composition obligations are explicit
inputs to the respective results. Full unitary extension is the
separate closed V.3.1 target.
