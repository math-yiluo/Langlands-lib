# Compact product forms under actual translation

`leviProductForm_inner_translated` compares the two actual product
forms of normalized induction over the same compact subgroup. The
source inducing form is carried first through inverse conjugation of
the Levi and then through the given inducing equivalence. The separate
formal-degree multiplier can be compared using the prescribed quotient
Haar measures.

The published compact product integral formula applies to the
positive-Levi normalization because the normalization comparison
preserves evaluation. The actual product translation changes the
two arguments to \(g^{-1}k,g^{-1}l\). The corresponding inducing
coefficient-value conjugation and equivalence preserve the Hermitian
pairing. Invariance of each original compact Haar probability under
left translation by \(g^{-1}\) gives the equality for the integrals.

This is a derived compact form identity for the positive product
pairing in Waldspurger V.2, printed p. 293 (PDF p. 59), and the
comparison in V.3.1, printed p. 295 (PDF p. 61).
[Original publication](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1474748003000082)
