# Hermitian coefficient forms before and after rational conjugation

`conjugateValue_inner` shows that the actual conjugation of coefficient
values preserves the Hermitian tensor form. The conjugate inducing
representation has the original vector space and the positive form
pulled back along inverse conjugation. A smooth dual vector is carried
by the same linear functional. Consequently the Hermitian
identifications with the smooth dual agree under conjugation,
as do their inverses. The identity on the dual forms and the tensor
formula give the result on pure tensors, and additivity extends
it to all coefficient values.

This is the coefficient-value identity for the form in Waldspurger
V.2, printed p. 293 (PDF p. 59), used in the compact-point
translation of V.3.1. [Original publication](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1474748003000082)
