# Admissibility of the original universal tensor and conjugate induced pieces

This internal unit derives coefficient admissibility for the actual universal tensor and for its conjugate normalized inductions inside a literal dynamic output Levi. It retains the original complete rational-norm algebra `RegularAlgebra r` throughout. In particular it does not replace that algebra by one attached to the output Levi. The field/group and inducing-carrier universes remain independent.

The generic theorem `isAdmissible_universalUnramifiedRepresentation` assumes continuous positive norm characters on a locally compact nonarchimedean group and Noetherianity of their actual regular-function algebra. The existing common-kernel result supplies smoothness of the universal tensor. For an arbitrary open subgroup `K`, the compact-open basis theorem supplies a compact open subgroup `J ≤ K`. The proved canonical compact-fixed tensor comparison gives finite generation of the `J`-fixed module. The `K`-fixed module is a submodule of this module, so the existing Noetherian fixed-vector inclusion theorem gives its finite generation. Thus the conclusion uses the Library's all-open-subgroups convention for admissibility. No coefficient-Jacquet preservation theorem is used.

The two generic results in `UnramifiedNormalized` apply to representations over this same regular algebra, allowing the group that carries the norm characters to differ from the group in which induction takes place. For a supplied semidirect decomposition with continuous projection, the existing section theorem makes the projection an open surjection. The published inflation result therefore preserves admissibility. Twisting by the actual constantly extended positive full-parabolic half-modulus preserves it over the Noetherian ring. The existing compact-quotient smooth-induction theorem then proves admissibility of the actual full normalized induced representation. Compactness is assumed for the actual inducing quotient; it is not replaced by a finiteness assertion about representations.

`universalRepresentation_isAdmissible` specializes the tensor theorem to all rational-character norms in the root presentation. It uses the proved Noetherianity of `RegularAlgebra r`, continuity of every member of the complete norm family, and closedness of the literal inducing Levi subgroup to obtain its local compactness. Its sole representation-theoretic premise is admissibility of the original complex representation. It does not require irreducibility or supercuspidality.

For an output cocharacter `χ`, `conjugateOutputLeviRepresentation` takes a literal subgroup `N` of the ambient rational-point group, a containment `N ≤ outputLeviPoints χ`, and an element `w` satisfying `x ∈ N ↔ w⁻¹ * x * w ∈ parabolicLeviPointSubgroup p l`. It acts on the original tensor carrier through the continuous conjugation equivalence `x ↦ w⁻¹ * x * w`, composed with the canonical identification of `N.subgroupOf (outputLeviPoints χ)` with `N`. The underlying module remains `RegularAlgebra r ⊗[ℂ] E`. The corresponding admissibility theorem transports the established tensor admissibility through this actual continuous group equivalence.

`conjugateOutputInducedRepresentation` is the existing full normalized coefficient induction of this action inside the literal output Levi. It uses a supplied semidirect decomposition of an actual subgroup `Q` of that output Levi, with Levi `N.subgroupOf (outputLeviPoints χ)` and closed radical `V`. Local compactness of the output Levi is derived from closed representability of the dynamic centralizer and the pinned rational-point topology. `conjugateOutputInducedRepresentation_isAdmissible` applies the preceding generic induction theorem when the supplied projection is continuous and `Q` has compact left quotient in the output Levi. This proves finite generation over the original `RegularAlgebra r` of every open-subgroup fixed module of that actual full induced action.

The conjugate subgroup, its containment and membership identity, the semidirect data, projection continuity and compact quotient are explicit geometric inputs. This unit does not produce them or identify a native orbit Jacquet quotient with the constructed induced model. It therefore supplies the coefficient-finiteness step for surviving conjugate induced pieces once that geometric comparison is established, while leaving the rational orbit geometry, local native quotient calculation, Haar-normalization comparison and proper-piece elimination separate. It neither proves nor changes `RationalSupercuspidalJacquetFamilyFiniteStatement`.

The provenance is internal: the implementation composes the proved compact-fixed tensor comparison, Noetherian fixed-vector inclusion, continuous group transport, inflation, smooth scalar twist and compact-quotient induction results on their existing carriers. It claims no additional original-source theorem.
