# The two compact test sections for actual common-Levi adjunction

`CompactAdjunctionTest` uses one compact open subgroup in the supplied
Iwasawa subgroup. It pairs an inducing vector with a smooth dual
functional that evaluates to one on it. Both vectors are fixed by the
respective Levi projections of the test subgroup intersections.
Such a test exists for a nonzero inducing carrier, by smooth dual
separation and a sufficiently small source Iwahori factorization.

The source and target test sections are the compact sections supported
on the parabolic times the test subgroup. They use the unramified
parameter on the source and its inverse on the target smooth dual.
Their common support is the product of the Levi, the radical intersection
and the test subgroup. On that product the twists cancel and the two
positive Haar half-moduli multiply to the intersection modulus by the
supplied squared-modulus identity. Thus the scalar pairing equals the
normalized compact average pointwise. This is an internal test for
Waldspurger's IV.1(11).
