# Finite expansions of regular operators in unitary coordinates

`unitaryRegularOperator` evaluates a linear operator numerator divided by a
scalar regular function in the chosen unitary coordinates. The finite expansion
requires Laurent evaluation coordinates, a denominator nonzero throughout the
unitary torus, an operator numerator polynomial on every fixed vector, and a
finite basis of the input submodule under consideration.

Regular scalar functions restrict to smooth functions by induction on the
algebra generated by character evaluations. Their inverses are smooth where
they are nonzero. For each input basis vector the polynomial hypothesis gives
a finite list of output vectors with regular coefficients. Divide those
coefficients by the denominator and extend each input coordinate functional
to the ambient input module. Linearity and the basis expansion give one
finite formula for all vectors in the input submodule.

This is a derived algebraic step for the weighted weak constant term in
Waldspurger VI.3.1. At a fixed input level it gives smooth scalar multipliers
and a single finite span of output vectors for the regular weighted operator.
