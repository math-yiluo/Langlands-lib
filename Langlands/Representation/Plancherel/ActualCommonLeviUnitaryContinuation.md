# Actual common-Levi continuation for unitarizable inducing data

The continuation uses the original compact subgroup, factorizations and
quotient measures. An admissible unitarizable inducing representation has
bounded smooth coefficients. On the literal presentation Levi this gives
common coefficient growth with exponent zero. The character cone and
squared-modulus theorems give full convergence and local domination for
these inducing data. Finite length then gives rationality of the actual
compact intertwining operator on the twist saturation. The presentation
transport carries its restriction to the original open region, producing
an actual continued pair.

This specializes the IV.1.1 argument in Waldspurger (2003), printed p. 278
(PDF p. 44), to the unitarizable inducing data needed for V.3.1.
