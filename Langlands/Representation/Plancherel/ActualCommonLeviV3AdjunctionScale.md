# The adjunction scale and positive similarity on the genuine generic locus

Identify the two induced dual spaces with the corresponding smooth duals
using the original compact probability pairings. Compare the actual reverse
dual operator to the smooth pullback of the actual forward operator. These
are bijective on the genuine generic locus. When the target induced fiber
is irreducible, Schur's lemma on its smooth dual makes the two maps
proportional by a nonzero scalar. An equal and nonzero pair of test values
then gives the unit-scale adjunction on the entire fiber.

For an irreducible source induced fiber, the pullback of the target's
invariant positive form by the actual forward equivalence is a positive
multiple of the original source form. The published uniqueness theorem on
irreducible admissible representations gives the similarity factor. Both
forms use the original compact Haar probability.

These are internal reductions of the generic preservation in Waldspurger
(2003), Lemma V.3.1 and formula (1), printed p. 295, PDF p. 61. The analytic
calculation of equal nonzero test values for the actual quotient Haar
normalization remains an input to the unit-scale assertion.
