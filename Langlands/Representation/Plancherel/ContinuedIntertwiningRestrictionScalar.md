# Rational composite scalars under restriction

This derived result extends the linear comparison of continued operator
families to scalar extraction after a surjective restriction. The
parameter space is the actual unramified character group of a connected
reductive group over a nonarchimedean local field. The coefficient
carriers can have independent universes.

`HasOpenLinearSquare` asserts that two fixed linear maps give a commuting
square on a nonempty open subset of both original operator regions. Use
one square for the forward operator and a separate square for the reverse
operator. Continuation extends both identities to the relevant common
regular regions. Composing them gives the square for the composite.

Assume the ambient composite is scalar on a nonempty open set at points of
regularity. The existing rational continuation of an extracted coefficient
makes its scalar identity hold throughout its common regular region.
Surjectivity of the starting restriction then gives the same scalar on the
restricted composite at all points where the four continued operators are
regular. The two normalized coefficient presentations evaluate equally on
that nonempty open intersection, so the extracted fractions agree in the
actual function field.
