# The fixed compact restriction through a realized intermediate Levi

`exists_realizedFixedCompactRestriction` conjugates the surjective realized
stages map by the specified compact charts. The chart evaluation identities
reduce the result at a lower compact point to its ambient compact value
multiplied by the outer half-character. Compatibility of the identifications
of inducing vectors gives the same value for each parameter. This identifies
a single surjective restriction with the actual stages restriction in every
fiber. The lower compact points must lie in the ambient compact through
the specified Levi inclusion.
