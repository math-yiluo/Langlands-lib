# Transport of formal degree by a measured group isomorphism

`RepresentationFormalDegreePullbackStatement` concerns a continuous group
isomorphism \(e:H\to G\) carrying the chosen central subgroup of \(H\)
to that of \(G\). The induced map on the Borel quotients must carry the
inversion-invariant Haar of the first quotient to the exact Haar of the
second. Compact open subgroups are cofinal in each group. For an
irreducible representation and its pullback with the displayed
square-integrability witnesses, the target equates their Schur formal
degrees. The field of coefficients is complex, and the two groups and the
representation carrier have independent universes.

This is a consequence of the characterization of the formal degree in
Waldspurger III.1(3). Under the isomorphism the two coefficient factors at
\(h\) and \(h^{-1}\) become the factors at \(e(h)\) and \(e(h)^{-1}\).
Exact pushforward of the Haar measure preserves the integral, and the
underlying linear functionals and their crossed evaluations are unchanged.
In VIII.2.1 this gives the normalization comparison when a final Levi in
an induced constant term is viewed in two actual point models.
