# Exact generic preservation and the common-Levi unitary comparison

For irreducible inducing data that are square integrable modulo the
central subgroup, `genericComparison_preserves_unitary` proves the actual
coefficient and inverse-formal-degree identities on the genuine generic
unitary locus. The twist is square integrable for the same central quotient
Haar measure. On this locus the given generic comparison data supply
irreducibility of the two normalized inductions. The exact compact
probability adjunction and a positive forward similitude give the two
identities. The compact formal-degree model transports the form identity
to equality of norms.

`comparisonFamilyOfGenericPoles` takes a rational continuation of the
actual compact comparison and a pole presentation cross-related to its
numerator and denominator. The norm equality bounds each vector's image
on the unitary generic locus. Cancellation then gives a regular germ at
every unitary parameter. The comparison assembled from these germs
intertwines the full product action. Its specification proves regularity,
agreement on the unitary generic locus and uniqueness by rational germ.
Density and continuity extend both identities to the entire unitary
locus. Formal-degree preservation and the generic dimension comparison
give bijectivity there.

This is an internal development of Waldspurger V.3.1, printed p. 295
(PDF p. 61). It uses the compact probability and central quotient Haar
normalizations of the closed common-Levi task.
