# The concrete universal unramified twist

Worker 1615. This module gives a derived tensor construction over the
published concrete algebra `unramifiedRegularFunctions ν`. It supplies
supporting algebra for the conditional standard intertwining task. It does
not prove `ConditionalParabolicIntertwiningStatement`: the universal
Jacquet filtration, finite exponent separation, integral continuation, and
one denominator nonzero on the entire saturated region remain obligations.

Let `G` be a topological group, let `ν` be any indexed family of positive
real unit-valued group homomorphisms, and write `X` for
`unramifiedCharacters ν` and `B` for `unramifiedRegularFunctions ν`.
Thus `X` consists of the actual continuous characters trivial on the full
common norm kernel, and `B` is the subalgebra of functions `X → ℂ`
generated by all evaluations `χ ↦ χ(g)`. The group, index and coefficient
carrier universes are independent. The coefficient module `V` is any
complex vector space, with no finite-dimensionality or topology assumption.

## Tensor evaluation and finite polynomial expansions

`unramifiedTensorEvaluation ν V` is the complex-linear map
`B ⊗[ℂ] V → (X → V)` taking `b ⊗ v` to `χ ↦ b(χ) • v`.
The displayed pure-tensor formula is definitional. The bilinearity proofs
use pointwise addition and commutation of complex scalars.

`unramifiedTensorEvaluation_basisCoord` identifies each coefficient of a
tensor in any basis of `V`, evaluated at a character, with the corresponding
coordinate of its vector-valued evaluation. The proof is tensor induction.
`unramifiedTensorEvaluation_injective` chooses a basis of the arbitrary
vector space and applies that identity to all its coordinates. Equality of
the functions represented by each coefficient is equality in the concrete
subalgebra `B`. There is no density assertion, algebraic-torus comparison,
or restriction on the dimension of `V` in this argument. This proof follows
the elementary coordinate method also implemented for a different scalar
algebra in the published `Plancherel.SmoothTensorFamilies` module.

`unramifiedTensorEvaluation_isPolynomialOn` expresses any tensor as a finite
sum of pure tensors and obtains the finite expansion used by the existing
predicate `UnramifiedOperatorFamily.IsPolynomialOn`. Conversely,
`isPolynomialOn_iff_exists_unramifiedTensor` turns any finite expansion on
an arbitrary subset `Ω ⊆ X` into a tensor whose evaluations restrict to
that family. This equivalence asserts existence of a lift on `Ω`; it makes
no uniqueness claim on an arbitrary subset. The injectivity result uses
evaluation on all of `X`.

`unramifiedTensorEvaluation_regular_smul` states that specialization at
`χ` sends the scalar action by `b ∈ B` to multiplication by the actual
complex number `b(χ)`. Its proof is tensor induction and the pointwise
multiplication formula in the subalgebra.

## The universal action and its specializations

For any representation `ρ : Representation ℂ G V`,
`universalUnramifiedRepresentation ν ρ` is a representation over `B` on
`B ⊗[ℂ] V`. Its action is

`g · (b ⊗ v) = (unramifiedEvaluationRegular ν g * b) ⊗ ρ(g)v`.

The construction multiplies the scalar extension `(ρ g).baseChange B`
by the evaluation element. The representation laws use the published
multiplicativity of `unramifiedEvaluationRegular`, the laws for `ρ`, and
commutativity of `B`.
`unramifiedTensorEvaluation_action` proves by tensor induction that
specialization intertwines this action with the actual representation
`Langlands.twist χ.val.toMonoidHom ρ`. The action on the right is the
published twist itself; no representative or carrier replacement is chosen.

## Smoothness

`unramifiedEvaluationRegular_eq_one_of_mem_commonNormKernel` proves that
all evaluation factors at an element of the full common norm kernel equal
one. It evaluates the defining property of every character of `X`.

`isSmooth_universalUnramifiedRepresentation` assumes that `ρ` is smooth
and the full common norm kernel is open. For a pure tensor `b ⊗ v`,
intersect an open stabilizer of `v` with that kernel. The evaluation factor
is one on this intersection and the original representation fixes `v`.
The tensor is therefore fixed there. Tensor induction and the published
smooth-vector addition lemma prove smoothness of every tensor.

`isSmooth_universalUnramifiedRepresentation_of_continuous` discharges the
kernel openness premise using the first conclusion of the published
`commonNormKernelTopology G I ν hν`: here `G` is nonarchimedean and weakly
locally compact and every `ν i` is continuous. The entire indexed norm
family is retained, including infinite or empty families. No admissibility,
finite-length, geometric filtration or rationality property is claimed for
this universal action.
