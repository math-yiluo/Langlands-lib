# A single rational character direction for a maximal proper Levi

Author: Worker 359.

This internal structural prerequisite concerns an actual maximal proper
rational parabolic of a connected reductive group over an arbitrary field,
its rational Levi and a split component of that Levi. It uses the Library's
central-kernel character theorem for maximal parabolics, actual maximal
split torus factorization, rational character/root complement and finite
index character restriction to a split component.

For any group morphism `f : M → G` over a field,
`HasOneRationalDirection f` asserts that the rational character space of `M`
is generated by restrictions of rationalized characters of `G` and one
integral character of `M`. The proof `exists_one_rational_direction`
establishes this for the inclusion of the specified Levi in the ambient group.

The helper `mem_line_of_pair_kernel` is rational linear algebra on the
actual character and cocharacter lattices of a split torus. A rationalized
character that vanishes on the integral kernel of a character `alpha`
lies on its rational line. If the pairing with `alpha` vanishes identically,
perfectness forces the given rationalized character to be zero. Otherwise
choose one cocharacter on which `alpha` is nonzero. The integral
combination of any cocharacter and the chosen one that kills `alpha`
also kills the given character, proving the required proportionality.

For the main theorem, choose the central-kernel character on the Levi
split component and a maximal split torus of the ambient group factoring
through the Levi. The central split component factors through that torus.
Its embedding gives surjective rationalized restriction from the torus.
The actual root span and ambient rational character range complement one
another in the torus character space. Every restricted root-span element
vanishes on cocharacters central in the ambient group, so the
central-kernel character puts its restriction on a single line.
Consequently every character of the split component is an ambient
restriction plus a vector on that line.

Rational restriction from the Levi to its split component is bijective.
Lift the line generator to the rationalized Levi character space and
clear its denominator once to obtain an actual Levi character `chi0`.
Injectivity of restriction then gives
`exists_one_rational_direction`: every rationalized Levi character is a
rational multiple of `chi0` plus the restriction of an ambient
rationalized character.
