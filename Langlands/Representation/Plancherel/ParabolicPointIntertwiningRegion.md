# Canonical character regions on the literal supplied Levi subgroup

Worker 200. This is an internal, derived adapter between the canonical
identity-test rational points of a specified Levi scheme and their literal
subgroup in the ambient point group. It composes the existing represented
closed-subgroup point homeomorphism, full-family unramified-character
transport, regular-function transport and supplied-radical region laws.
It introduces no additional assertion from the literature.

## Actual subgroup and topology

Let `F : Type u` be any nonarchimedean local field with its given compatible
valuation and topology. The universe and characteristic are unrestricted.
Let `p : P ⟶ G`, `l : M ⟶ P` and `a : A.toGrp ⟶ M` be the actual maps in a
`ParabolicRootPresentation p l a`. The initial constructions assume
`IsAffineHom G.X.hom` and `IsAffine M.X.left`. The latter also follows from
the specified Levi's reductivity, but is retained as an explicit instance.

`parabolicLeviPointSubgroup p l` is the value of
`SubgroupFunctor.range (l ≫ p)` on the identity test scheme. Its elements
are actual ambient rational points in the image of this particular Levi
map. Its topology is inherited from the canonical
`rationalPointTopology F G.X`; the Levi's own points use
`rationalPointTopology F M.X`.

The two private lemmas derive closedness of the composite from the supplied
Levi and parabolic predicates, and its representability by that composite
from the underlying monomorphism. `parabolicLeviPointHomeomorph r` then
applies `rationalPointSubgroupHomeomorph` to obtain a multiplicative
homeomorphism from the Levi's own points onto this literal subgroup.
Its forward map, after inclusion, is exactly
`rationalPoints.map (l ≫ p)`. `isClosed_parabolicLeviPointSubgroup` proves
closedness in the canonical ambient point topology, using the local field's
Hausdorff property.

## Full character and regular-function transport

`parabolicLeviRationalNormCharacters r` retains every index
`χ : rationalCharacters M`. At a literal point `m`, its value is the actual
rational-character norm of the inverse image of `m` under the point
homeomorphism. The continuity theorem uses the canonical norm character
and continuity of that inverse. At a point coming from `M`, the norm is
exactly the original norm.

Write `E = parabolicLeviUnramifiedCharactersHomeomorph r`. This is the
existing unramified-character transport applied to the point homeomorphism
and the entire indexed norm family. Both character spaces therefore use
the existing continuous complex characters trivial on the respective full
common norm kernels and their existing topologies. The forward map is
`E(χ)(m) = χ(e.symm(m))`; its inverse is evaluation after `e`. No selected
character sublattice replaces the actual family.

`parabolicSplitComponentPointHom r` is the actual map from the points of
`A` into the literal Levi subgroup. Its ambient inclusion is precisely
`rationalPoints.map (a ≫ l ≫ p)`.

`parabolicLeviRegularFunctionsEquiv r` specializes the complex algebra
equivalence from `UnramifiedOperatorTransport`. It maps the regular
function `b` to `b ∘ E.symm` and its inverse maps `c` to `c ∘ E`.
The regular evaluation at a point of `M` maps to regular evaluation at
its actual image under `e`. These are the concrete algebras generated by
all character evaluations. No choice of free lattice or algebraic-torus
comparison is required for this construction.

## Exact regions and operator families

For a real bilinear form `B`, supplied maps `n : U ⟶ P`, `q : Q ⟶ G`,
`v : V ⟶ Q`, and any real threshold `R`, define

`parabolicPointUnramifiedIntertwiningRegion r B n q v R`

to be the inverse image of the existing
`parabolicUnramifiedIntertwiningRegion r B n q v R` under `E.symm`.
The preimage under `E` is exactly the original region.
`parabolicIntertwiningParameterEquiv` restricts `E` to an equivalence
between these exact region subtypes. Its forward and inverse value laws
are the restrictions of `E` and `E.symm`.

For arbitrary complex modules `E₁ : Type uV` and `E₂ : Type uW`, the
three `*_parabolicIntertwiningParameterEquiv_iff` lemmas prove equivalence
of polynomial vector families, polynomial operator families and rational
operator families on these corresponding regions. The universes of the
field and both vector spaces are independent. The vector-family proof
transports each scalar coefficient through the regular-function algebra
equivalence, retaining the same finite list of fixed vectors. The operator
polynomial law applies this argument to each input vector. The rational
law transports one regular denominator, proves its nonvanishing on the
other region, and applies the polynomial law to the cleared operator.
The denominator is chosen before all input vectors, while the finite
numerator expansion may depend on the input vector. These laws also apply
to empty regions and do not assume openness or nonemptiness.

## Complete root and ambient-form laws

The final section additionally assumes smoothness of the ambient group
and uses a second compatible presentation `r' : ParabolicRootPresentation
p' l' a` with `hM : l ≫ p = l' ≫ p'`. It retains the geometric unipotent
radical predicates on `n` and `v` and a representative `q` of the negative
dynamic parabolic defined by `-r'.splitComponentCocharacter`. These are
the same full hypotheses as the existing supplied-radical region laws.

`mem_parabolicPointUnramifiedIntertwiningRegion` says that a literal-point
character belongs to the region precisely when, for every actual root
`α ∈ relativeRoots (a ≫ l ≫ p)` with positive pairing against the first
presentation's cocharacter and negative pairing against the second,

`R < B (normalizedRealPart (χ restricted to the actual split component)) (1 ⊗ α)`.

The restriction uses `parabolicSplitComponentPointHom r`. Every such root
is tested, including multipliable roots. The parameter is the existing
residue-cardinality-normalized real part. The proof applies the canonical
scheme-character membership law to `E.symm χ`; the restricted characters
agree by construction. This law does not require a locally finite type
instance on the Levi.

`parabolicPointUnramifiedIntertwiningRegion_nonempty` assumes also
`LocallyOfFiniteType M.X.hom` and `B.Nondegenerate`. For every real `R`,
it maps the existing actual-image positive-character witness through `E`.
The finite type instance follows from the specified reductive Levi's
smoothness but remains explicit. Rank zero and empty root intersections
are included.

For the final comparison, the monomorphisms of the presentation's ambient
torus and factor map are derived from its maximal split torus, split
component, closed subgroup maps and exact composite equation. A `Fintype`
instance on its actual relative Weyl group is explicit.
`parabolicPointUnramifiedIntertwiningRegion_eq_ambient` uses the local
form `weylInducedCharacterForm`, built from the single ambient Weyl form
and its orthogonal lift. It identifies the literal-point region with the
`E.symm` preimage of the complete ambient character region, by applying
that preimage operation to the existing exact equality. The ambient
region retains every ambient root in the dynamic intersection, allowing
roots to merge on restriction. This equality needs no locally finite
type instance on the Levi.

The adapter assumes neither splitness nor quasisplitness of the ambient
group. It retains the compatible presentations and their realization
fields; it does not assert general presentation existence or independence.
These constructions and transport laws supply parameters for subsequent
analytic work. Absolute convergence, finite-length applications, actual
intertwiner continuation, orbit openness and descent, and the Plancherel
theorem remain separate obligations.

The local instances have explicit module-specific names to avoid generated-name
collisions when these developments are imported together. Their types, bodies
and local scope are unchanged.
