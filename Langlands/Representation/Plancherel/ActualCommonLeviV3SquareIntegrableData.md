# The genuine generic comparison for square-integrable inducing data

`genericComparisonDataOfSquareIntegrable` applies the actual continuation
for unitarizable inducing representations to the original inducing object
and its smooth contragredient. Square integrability gives unitarizability.
The invariant positive form identifies the representation with its smooth
dual by an equivariant conjugate-linear equivalence, so the smooth dual
is unitarizable as well. Irreducibility gives finite length, and duality
preserves admissibility and finite length.

The construction uses these three actual continuations in the Library's
generic comparison datum. This gives the scalar inverse, the full density
clauses, and the generic irreducibility used for the comparison. Its
rationality is the Library theorem `GenericComparisonData.rational`.
The central quotient and split-center condition are those of the closed
common-Levi V.3.1 statement (Waldspurger 2003, printed p. 295 / PDF p. 61).
