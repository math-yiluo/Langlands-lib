# Whole-domain continuation targets for the actual opposite integrals

`ActualOppositeFullDomainContinuationStatement` concerns a connected
reductive group over a nonarchimedean local field and a cocharacter with
literal Levi \(M(F)\). The source and opposite parabolics both factor
with the displayed compact open subgroup. The inducing representation
is irreducible and square integrable modulo the displayed central
subgroup with the given inversion-invariant quotient Haar.

The conclusion gives `ForwardContinuation` and `ReverseContinuation`
for the actual compact pictures at that compact. Each has a single
regular denominator and a polynomial operator numerator. The denominator
is nonzero throughout the corresponding full convergence domain, where
the numerator clears the original integral. Each full domain also
contains a nonempty open set. The two domains refer to the standard
integrals with the original source quotient measures.

Waldspurger's theorem IV.1.1 gives convergence and rationality in a
cone, printed p. 278 (PDF p. 44). The whole-domain requirement is the
derived continuation specification used in the construction of the
opposite composition scalar for IV.3 and the V.2 density. The
published `commonLeviContinuationExists_of_unitarizable` supplies open
convergence regions and continuations on those regions.
`forwardContinuationOfOpenFullDomain` and
`reverseContinuationOfOpenFullDomain` give whole-domain continuations
when the respective full domains are open and nonempty. This
proposition asks for the whole-domain witnesses and permits any proof
of that specification. [Original publication](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1474748003000082)
