# Actual discrete-series orbit measures under equivalences

This derived unit extends the published group-pullback comparison to norm
families with different index types and to the actual orbit measures. It is
used for the group-Levi comparison in Waldspurger VIII.4.2, printed p.330
(PDF p.96), keeping the I.1 and VI.3 finite-fiber normalization.

The inputs are a continuous group equivalence, a multiplicative homeomorphism
of the two actual unitary unramified character groups that evaluates by
pullback through that group equivalence, corresponding normal central subgroups
and Borel quotient Haar measures whose displayed pushforward agrees. These are
explicit structural and measure hypotheses of this auxiliary construction.
The two norm index types and representation carrier universe are independent.

The character evaluation identity and the existing smooth-dual class
pullback-twist theorem prove equivariance on actual discrete-series classes.
Injectivity of class pullback then identifies their self-twist stabilizers,
including cardinalities. The orbit homeomorphism is constructed from the two
published orbit-stabilizer homeomorphisms and the quotient of the character
homeomorphism. Its orbit-map identity is literal.

For finite self-twist stabilizers, the orbit measure is the inverse actual
stabilizer cardinality times the pushforward of the supplied character measure.
The orbit-map identity and equality of stabilizer cardinalities prove its
transport to the orbit measure of the pushed-forward character measure.
Neither character measure is assumed to be probability or rescaled.

No packet membership, cuspidality or closed Task 7440 target is proved here.
