# An ambient irreducibility open for actual common-Levi induction

Choose one irreducible unitary point using the published density theorem for
the actual square-integrable inducing object. The represented parabolic on
the common Levi identifies its induction at that point with the actual
normalized induction. Renard's principal openness theorem for admissible
induced representations supplies a principal open in the full parameter
group. Transport the irreducibility back through the same represented point
comparison at each parameter.

The polynomial defining this open gives a regular function of the actual
unramified characters through the canonical Laurent evaluation comparison.
Its value at the chosen point is nonzero. Its entire nonzero locus, including
parameters outside the unitary subgroup, consists of irreducible actual
induced representations.

For an irreducible admissible normalized fiber, the corresponding smooth
dual is irreducible. The original compact probability duality identifies
that dual with normalized induction of the smooth dual of the twist. The
positive-Levi normalization and the functional-preserving twist-dual
equivalence identify the latter with actual induction of the original
smooth dual at the inverse parameter.

This is an internal prerequisite for the genuine common-Levi generic locus
in Waldspurger (2003), Lemma V.3.1, printed p. 295, PDF p. 61. The route uses
the role of generic irreducibility in IV.2.2(i) and the opening of IV.3,
printed pp. 284–285, PDF pp. 50–51.
