# Compact-fixed openness for smooth families

This internal derived proposition transfers finite-matrix openness to actual
smooth complex representations of a topological group. The parameter space,
group, representation carriers, and finite basis index have independent
universes. Carriers may vary with the parameter.

There is one compact open subgroup `K` and a left Haar measure. At each
parameter a basis of the `K`-fixed space is supplied, indexed by the same finite
type. The matrices are exactly the published
`UnitarizabilityContinuation.fixedMatrix`: apply the group action to a basis
vector, project back onto `K`-fixed vectors with the normalized Hecke
indicator, and take coordinates.

The premises require that every representation carrier is nonzero, that every
irreducible actual smooth subquotient of every family member has a nonzero
`K`-fixed vector, and that every entry of these compressed matrices is
continuous. The conclusion is openness of the locus where the actual smooth
representation is irreducible, including the usual nonzero-carrier condition.
The subquotient relation is the published relation retaining actual invariant
submodules, quotient modules, and isomorphisms in the smooth category.

The rationale is the published detection-to-generation reduction and the
finite-matrix openness statement. Detection forces every subquotient to be
generated by its `K`-fixed vectors. A subrepresentation's fixed vectors give an
invariant subspace for the compressed matrices; matrix irreducibility and
detection then force that subrepresentation to be zero or everything.

This is not a transcription of Waldspurger IV.2.2 and does not discharge its
representation-theoretic inputs. For actual unramified parabolic induction,
the common detecting subgroup, the fixed-space bases, continuous entries, and
an irreducible parameter must still be obtained. No existence or nonemptiness
claim is part of this auxiliary proposition.
