# Averaging a compact open indicator over the Levi and radical

`compactLeviRadicalAverage` integrates the indicator of an open test
subgroup \(Q\) in Levi-then-radical coordinates after right translation
by the argument. It uses the supplied coordinate measures and takes values
in the extended nonnegative reals. Joint measurable multiplication and
s-finite coordinate measures give measurability. When radical points can
be moved past Levi points, the average is supported in the product of
the Levi, the embedded radical and \(Q\).

Right translation by \(Q\) preserves the average. Right invariance of
the intersection measure gives invariance under left multiplication by
the intersection and lets the average descend to the left quotient of
the parabolic radical by that intersection. For Levi covariance, the
Levi measure is right invariant and the intersection measure is regular
Haar. Left multiplication by a Levi point scales the average by the
Haar character of the supplied intersection automorphism whose inverse
acts by conjugation by the inverse Levi point.

At the identity the average is the product measure of the preimage of
\(Q\) under \((m,h)\mapsto m\,\iota(h)\). For a semidirect parabolic
decomposition and an open subgroup \(K\) giving Iwasawa coordinates, the
unfolding uses the supplied ambient left-invariant measure, the Levi and
radical Haar measures, and a quotient measure compatible with the radical
and right-invariant intersection Haar measures. The extended nonnegative
integral over this quotient and then over the ambient measure restricted
to \(K\) equals the ambient mass of \(Q\) times the Iwasawa
compact-coordinate mass for these same measures.

The average is an internal device for comparing the two compact test
pairings in Waldspurger's IV.1(11) and Lemma V.3.1.
