# The inverse scalar of the actual common-Levi comparison

The input is a pointwise scalar product formula for the composite of the two
continued compact operators on the smooth dual. It is evaluated at the inverse
of each genuine comparison parameter whose inverse belongs to a chosen dense
ambient open set. The constant allows the fixed Haar scales in the comparison
with a represented gallery. Each factor is invariant under its own complement
translations when both parameters belong to that open set.

Intersect the chosen open part of the genuine comparison locus with the
inverse image of the product locus. The pullback of each factor is invariant
under the same complement translations because inversion on the character
group inverts the scaling coordinates. At a parameter in the intersection,
the scalar identity for the actual continued composite determines the given
scalar quotient by evaluation on a nonzero compact vector, using nontriviality
of the inducing smooth dual. Inverting this
quotient and its pointwise product gives the dense rank-one product for the
exact inverse scalar used in the common-Levi tensor, with the two original
regular functions interchanged.

This is an internal attachment to the common-Levi specialization of
Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques
(d'après Harish-Chandra)*, Lemma V.3.1 and formula (1), printed p. 295.
