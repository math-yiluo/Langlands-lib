# Cancellation on full convergence regions stable under unitary twists

`nonempty_fullDomainContinuation_of_unitary_translates` concerns a linear operator family on a region of the actual complex unramified character torus. The inputs include a continuation on a nonempty open subset, a nonzero regular scalar making the family polynomial over the entire region, and continuity of separating scalar tests on every translate of the unitary torus by a parameter of the region. Each such translate lies in the region. At each translated origin a factored scalar controls the exceptional locus where a cleared numerator of an open continuation could be nonzero at a denominator zero.

Cross-multiplication with the full-region polynomial family gives a polynomial identity on the open subset and then on the full region. The nonzero locus of the full-region scalar is dense in each unitary translate. Continuity of the scalar tests and their separation extend the clearing identity of any open continuation to every point of that translate.

Choose an open-continuation denominator minimal for strict divisibility. If it vanishes at a point of the full region, a prime factor vanishes there. Translate that point to the identity and test the translated numerator by algebraic linear functionals. If the translated prime does not divide the controlling scalar, the product of that scalar and a scalar numerator test vanishes on the prime hypersurface. Complex hypersurface divisibility and primality then divide the test by the prime. If the prime divides the controlling scalar, its zero at the identity excludes the factor nonvanishing on the unitary torus. The prime is associated with a root in one coordinate of a lattice basis. The entire unitary slice through that root has vanishing cleared numerator by the clearing identity. The Laurent factor theorem on the slice gives the same scalar divisibility. Transporting back and using `exists_polynomial_operator_quotient_of_linear_tests` divides the whole polynomial numerator. The smaller denominator still represents the operator on the open subset, contradicting minimality.

The conclusion gives one denominator nonvanishing on the full region, a global polynomial numerator, and the clearing identity there. This is an internal extension of the Library's polynomial uniqueness, unitary slice divisibility and whole-operator cancellation results for the rationality problem of Waldspurger (2003), IV.1.1 and IV.1 (13), printed pp. 278–283 (PDF pp. 44–49).
