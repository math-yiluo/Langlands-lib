## Scalar algebra and scope

For a group `G` with a topology and any indexed family of positive characters
`ν`, the parameter group is the existing `unramifiedCharacters ν`. Its members
are actual continuous complex-unit-valued characters trivial on
`commonNormKernel ν`.

`unramifiedEvaluation ν g` is the complex-valued function `χ ↦ χ(g)`.
`unramifiedRegularFunctions ν` is the complex subalgebra of all functions on the
parameter group generated by these functions. `unramifiedEvaluationRegular`
packages each generator in that subalgebra. The multiplication and identity
lemmas follow from the character laws by function extensionality. Evaluation at
`g⁻¹` is an inverse for evaluation at `g`, so these generators already include
inverse monomials.

This construction makes sense without local compactness, a local field, or a
finite-rank lattice. Calling it exactly the coordinate algebra `B` of the
source's algebraic torus `X(M)` requires the rational-norm-character
specialization and its coordinate-algebra comparison. This module does not
claim that comparison for an arbitrary supplied family `ν`.

## Vector and operator conditions

All conditions are in `Langlands.Plancherel.UnramifiedOperatorFamily`, and all
are proposition-valued definitions.

For a subset `U` of the actual parameter group and `f : U → V`,
`IsPolynomialOn U f` means that there are a natural number `n`, regular
functions `b_i`, and fixed vectors `v_i` such that

`f(χ) = ∑ i : Fin n, b_i(χ) • v_i`

for every `χ` in `U`. The vectors and scalar functions are chosen before `χ`.
The empty sum is allowed. No topology or finite dimension of `V` is required.

For `A : U → V →ₗ[ℂ] W`, `IsPolynomialOperatorOn U A` requires this condition
on `χ ↦ A(χ)v` for each fixed input `v`. The finite expression can depend on
`v`. `IsRationalOperatorOn U A` requires a **single** regular function `b`,
nonzero at every point of `U`, for which `χ ↦ b(χ)A(χ)` is a polynomial
operator family. The denominator is chosen before the input vector, as in the
source. This is stronger than allowing an unrelated denominator for each input
or each scalar evaluation.

These are algebraic restriction conditions on any subset. The source applies
them on the pullback of an open subset of an actual representation twist orbit,
after identifying the varying induced representations with fixed compact
pictures. Openness, the actual compact-picture identification, and descent
under isomorphisms of representatives are separate application obligations.
This module does not turn an arbitrary parameter family into an orbit family.

## Implemented formal laws

Constant vector families have a one-term expansion with coefficient one.
Adding two expansions concatenates their finite index sets using `Fin.addCases`;
`Fin.sum_univ_add` proves the equality. Multiplication by a regular scalar
multiplies each coefficient, and applying a fixed linear map applies it to each
fixed vector. Restriction uses the same coefficients and vectors.

The corresponding polynomial operator laws are pointwise consequences.
Polynomial operators are rational with denominator one. Rational families
restrict using the same common denominator. Precomposition and postcomposition
by fixed complex-linear maps also preserve that denominator; postcomposition
uses the linear map's compatibility with scalar multiplication. These maps may
act on carriers in independent universes.

## Remaining obligations

There is no assertion of convergence, existence of a continued operator,
holomorphic dependence, equality of algebraic and analytic notions, or a
composition scalar. In particular the finite-length convergence cone and
rationality theorem IV.1.1 remain open. Actual reductive realization, the exact
torus-coordinate comparison, and representative-independent compact-picture
application must be supplied by their respective constructions and proofs.
