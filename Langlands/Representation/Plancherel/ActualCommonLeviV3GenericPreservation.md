# The generic common-Levi tensor and compact probability duality

Transport the positive-Levi dual induction to normalized induction and apply
the smooth-dual equivalence induced by the original compact Haar probability.
The resulting map on the dual factor gives the exact coefficient-tensor
transport, as verified on pure tensors. At each genuine generic point,
`genericNormalizedForwardEquiv` is the given bijective forward standard
operator transported to normalized induction.

Suppose the compact probability bilinear pairing of the forward value equals
the pairing against the reverse dual value times a scalar \(r\). Evaluate the
identity on the inverse of the actual reverse dual operator and the inverse
of the forward equivalence. The transported dual factor then acts by inverse
pullback of the forward equivalence times \(r\). Tensoring gives the same
scalar \(r\) on the whole comparison, and the canonical inverse-pullback
transport preserves every coefficient value. Thus the actual generic
coefficient has the adjunction scale \(r\).

For scale one and a positive similitude of the normalized forward operator,
`genericComparison_preserves_of_adjunction_similitude` proves the exact
coefficient and inverse-formal-degree form identities. Both parabolics use
the same inducing object and the same central quotient measure at that
parameter. The form calculation is the published coefficient-tensor
similitude theorem.
