# Continued compact operators and restriction to a represented Levi

`realizedContinuedIntertwining_square` preserves the square between the
actual compact standard operators and fixed stages restrictions on
continuation. On the ambient convergence open, the stages formula and
`HasIntegral.realized_restriction` transport the weak integral to the
represented Levi. The inner weak integral with its prescribed measure
is multiplied by the scalar comparing it to the pushforward. Uniqueness
of the smooth-dual integrals identifies its values with the restricted
ambient ones. `rationalOperatorContinuationData_natural` extends this
square to the common regular region of the given denominators.
