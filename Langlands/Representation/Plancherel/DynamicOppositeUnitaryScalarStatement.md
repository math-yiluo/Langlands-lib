# Nonvanishing of `j_P` and regularity and nonnegativity of `j_P⁻¹` at unitary points (target)

Worker 5. `Langlands.Plancherel.ConditionalDynamicOppositeUnitaryScalarStatement`
is a Prop-valued target. It says that the canonical scalar `j_P` of
`J_{P|P̄} J_{P̄|P}` for a square-integrable inducing representation is
nonzero, and that `j_P⁻¹` is regular with a nonnegative real value at every
unitary unramified character. This module does not prove the target. It
also defines two predicates for rational functions on unramified
characters: regularity at a point, and regularity with a nonnegative real
value at a point. It proves two elementary facts about these predicates.

## Source

J.-L. Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques
(d'après Harish-Chandra)*, J. Inst. Math. Jussieu 2 (2003), 235–333, DOI
10.1017/S1474748003000082, registered as `waldspurger-2003-plancherel`, PDF
SHA256 `4b6a6162cd0b3863703fb2f65d1698f926caf813114917f2084c16e38f2e30f7`.

- IV.3, printed p.285 / PDF p.51. On a nonempty Zariski open subset of
  `O_C` where `I_P^G π'` is irreducible, `J_{P|P̄}(π') J_{P̄|P}(π')` is a
  homothety. `j_P` is the rational function on `O_C` whose value at `π'`
  is its ratio. By IV.3(1), `j_P` does not depend on `P ∈ P(M)`.
- IV.3(6), printed p.287 / PDF p.53: "si `π` est de carré intégrable,
  `j(π) ≠ 0`". In the proof, `M` is reduced to a proper maximal Levi
  subgroup through (5). Positive definite hermitian products
  `(f, f') = ∫_K (f(k), f'(k)) dk` are put on the compact pictures. For a
  unitary unramified character `χ` they give the adjunction
  `(J_{P̄|P}(π ⊗ χ) f, f') = (f, J_{P|P̄}(π ⊗ χ) f')`, and hence
  `j(π ⊗ χ)(e, e) = (J_{P̄|P}(π ⊗ χ) e, J_{P̄|P}(π ⊗ χ) e)`. If `j(π) = 0`,
  these norms would tend to `0` as `χ → 1`. They lie in a
  finite-dimensional space, which contradicts the choice of `e` with
  `(J_{P̄|P}(π) e)(1)` regular and nonzero.
- V.2, printed p.292 / PDF p.58: `μ(ω) = j(ω)⁻¹ ∏_α γ(M_α|M)²` for `ω` in
  the unitary orbit `O = {ω₀ ⊗ χ; χ ∈ Im X(M)}`. Lemma V.2.1: `μ` is a
  rational function that is regular on `O`, and `μ(ω) ≥ 0` for every
  `ω ∈ O`. The proof, printed p.293 / PDF p.59, reads "Cela résulte des
  propriétés (2), (3), (5) et (6) de IV.3."

## Provenance of the target

The target is derived from these passages. It is not a verbatim statement.
Each `γ(M_α|M)` is a positive real constant, so `μ` is `j⁻¹` times a
positive constant. The first two claims of Lemma V.2.1 therefore say that
`j⁻¹` is regular at every point of `O`, with a nonnegative real value
there. Every unitary twist of a square-integrable representation is again
square-integrable, so IV.3(6) applies at every point of `O`. The target
states:

1. `j_P ≠ 0` in the field of rational functions;
2. for every unitary unramified character `z`, `j_P⁻¹` is regular at `z`
   with a nonnegative real value.

Item 1 is stated separately because `0⁻¹ = 0` in a Lean field. Without
it, item 2 would hold trivially for `j_P = 0`. Together, items 1 and 2 at
`z` say that `j_P` has no zero at `z`, because `j_P⁻¹` has no pole there.
At the base point `z = 1` this is IV.3(6) for `π = A`. Item 2 does not
say that `j_P` itself is regular on `O`. At a pole of `j_P` on `O`, the
value of `j_P⁻¹`, and hence of `μ`, is `0`.

The target omits the other assertions of V.2.1 (the product formula over
rank-one Levi subgroups, invariance under `W^G`, and invariance under
contragredience). It also omits IV.3(1)–(5).

Changing the Haar measures on the two radicals multiplies `j_P` by a
positive real constant. Such a change affects neither item. The target is
therefore stated for the Library's `K`-normalized measures, and no
comparison with Waldspurger's normalization is needed. The Lean statement
quantifies over any compact open subgroup `K` with both factorizations.
For such `K`, the products `∫_K` above are `G(F)`-invariant on the
normalized induced representations of a unitary representation. The
adjunction then holds up to a positive constant fixed by the measure
normalizations, and the positivity argument goes through unchanged.

## Rational functions: regularity and values

Section `Regular` works with a general group scheme `G` over `Spec F`. It
takes a topology on `G(F)`, continuity `hp` of the valuation image
projection, and `hG : IsConnectedReductive G`. These are the inputs of the
Library's `rationalUnramifiedFunctionField`. Local notation:

- `RG` is the Library's `unramifiedRegularFunctions` for the rational norm
  characters of `G`. This is the `ℂ`-subalgebra of functions on the
  unramified characters generated by the evaluations `z ↦ z(g)`. An
  element `m` is a genuine function, and `m.val z` is its value at `z`.
- `XG` is the Library's `unramifiedCharacters` for the same norm
  characters.

The rational function field `rationalUnramifiedFunctionField G hp hG` is
the fraction field of `RG`.

- `RationalFunctionRegularAt G hp hG f z`: there are `m n : RG` with
  `n(z) ≠ 0` and `f = m / n` in the field. This is the usual notion: `f`
  lies in the local ring at `z`.
- `RationalFunctionNonnegAt G hp hG f z`: there are `m n : RG` with
  `n(z) ≠ 0`, `f = m / n`, and a real number `r ≥ 0` with
  `m(z) / n(z) = r` in `ℂ`. Thus `f` is regular at `z` and its value there
  is a nonnegative real number.
- `rationalFunction_value_eq` (proved): two presentations `f = m/n = m'/n'`
  with `n(z) ≠ 0` and `n'(z) ≠ 0` give `m(z)/n(z) = m'(z)/n'(z)`. Hence
  the value at a point of regularity does not depend on the presentation,
  and `RationalFunctionNonnegAt` is a property of the value. The proof
  uses injectivity of `RG → field` (the Library's
  `rationalUnramifiedFunctionField_algebraMap_injective`) to get
  `m n' = m' n` in `RG`. It then evaluates at `z`.
- `RationalFunctionNonnegAt.regularAt` (proved): nonnegativity at `z`
  includes regularity at `z`.

## The target

The setting is that of the Library's `DynamicOppositeCanonicalScalar`, and
the target takes its inputs unchanged. `F : Type u` is a nonarchimedean
local field. `G` is a group scheme over `Spec F` with
`[IsAffineHom G.X.hom]` and `[LocallyOfFiniteType G.X.hom]`, and
`hG : IsConnectedReductive G` is explicit. `χ` is a cocharacter of `G`,
`P = P_G(χ)` (`parabolic χ`), `P̄ = P_G(χ⁻¹)` (`oppositeParabolic χ`), and
`M = Z_G(χ)` (`levi χ`), their common Levi subgroup. The module
re-enables, with `attribute [local instance]`, the local instances of
`DynamicOppositeCanonicalScalar`. These are the point topologies on `G(F)`
and on the points of the centralizer scheme, the Hausdorff, locally
compact, topological-group, nonarchimedean and second-countable
structures, and the closed-immersion, affine and finite-type instances
for the centralizer scheme. The scalar itself is stated with these
instances.

The universally quantified inputs are:

- the structural premises `hRefl : RelativeRootReflectionExistenceStatement`
  and `hCST : CentralSplitTorusCharacterStatement`, required by the
  scalar;
- a compact open subgroup `K` (`hK`) with `G(F) = P K` (`hPK`) and
  `G(F) = P̄ K` (`hOppK`);
- a canonical smooth complex representation `A : SmoothRep ℂ (levi χ)`;
- whole-operator rational continuations `p : ForwardContinuation …` and
  `q : ReverseContinuation …` of the forward and reverse compact
  intertwining families;
- open nonempty sets `DForward` and `DReverse` contained in the forward and
  reverse full convergence domains;
- an open nonempty set `D` of unramified characters `z` at which the
  normalized induction `UnramifiedLeviInduction` from `P` of `A`, twisted
  by `parameterTransport χ z`, is irreducible (`hi`);
- a normal subgroup `C` of `M(F)` contained in its center (`hC`), a Borel
  measurable structure on `M(F)/C`, a Haar measure `μM` on it, the
  finiteness `[Finite (levi χ ⧸ (commonNormKernel (leviNormCharacters χ) ⊔ C))]`,
  and `hsq : IsSquareIntegrableModCentralSubgroup C hC A.representation μM`.
  The last says that `A` is admissible, has a unitary central character,
  and has all smooth-dual matrix coefficients square-integrable on
  `M(F)/C`.

The convention for `C` and the finiteness of `M(F)/(M¹ C)` is copied from
the Library's `RationalInducedOrbitWavePacketOrthogonality`. Here `M¹` is
the common kernel of the norm characters. With `C` the split center
`A_M(F)` or the full center of `M(F)`, `hsq` is Waldspurger's
square-integrability of `ω₀ ∈ E²(M)`. Irreducibility of `A` is not a
separate hypothesis. If `A` is reducible, then normalized parabolic
induction (an exact functor that takes nonzero representations to nonzero
representations) makes every induced twist reducible, so no `hi` exists.
For such `A` the target is vacuous. The names `_hDForward`, `_hDReverse`,
`_hD` and `_hsq` start with an underscore only because the conclusion does
not mention them. They remain premises of the target.

The conclusion uses `j_P = conditionalCanonicalRationalScalar χ hRefl hCST
hG K hK hPK hOppK A p q hneForward hDForwardΩ hneReverse hDReverseΩ hne
hi`. This is the Library's element of `rationalUnramifiedFunctionField`
of the centralizer scheme `parameterScheme χ`. On the regular part of the
irreducibility locus, the continued composite `J_{P|P̄} J_{P̄|P}` of the
actual intertwining integrals (with `K`-normalized Haar measures) acts by
this element's value. The Library proves that it does not depend on `p`,
`q` or `D`. The conclusion is

```
j_P ≠ 0 ∧
  ∀ z : unramifiedCharacters (normCharacters χ),
    z.val ∈ unitaryUnramifiedCharacters (normCharacters χ) →
    RationalFunctionNonnegAt (parameterScheme χ) (parameterProjection_continuous χ)
      (parameterScheme_isConnectedReductive χ hG) j_P⁻¹ z
```

`unitaryUnramifiedCharacters` is the Library's `Im X(M)`: unramified
characters of absolute value one. They are characters of the points of
`parameterScheme χ`, and `parameterTransport χ` carries them to
characters of `levi χ` through the Library's homeomorphism. The trivial
character `z = 1` is the base point `π = A`.

## Intended use

Combined with `plancherelDensity = (∏_α γ(M_α|M)²) · j_P⁻¹` (a separate
module), this target gives the regularity and nonnegativity claims of
Lemma V.2.1 for the density `μ`.

## Not proved here

The target itself is not proved. In particular, this module proves none of
IV.3(1)–(6), Lemma V.2.1, the adjunction of the intertwining integrals,
or the existence of the continuations and of an irreducibility locus.

## Axioms

`rationalFunction_value_eq` and `RationalFunctionNonnegAt.regularAt` use
only the standard axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`).
