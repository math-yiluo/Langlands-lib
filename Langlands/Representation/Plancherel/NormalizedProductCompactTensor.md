# Fixed compact charts for normalized product induction

For a nonarchimedean group, `normalizedDualTwistEquiv` identifies the smooth dual of a twist with the inverse twist of the smooth dual. It applies `smoothContragredientTwistEquiv`, which preserves the underlying functional. The compact charts use a locally compact nonarchimedean group \(G\), a semidirect decomposition \(P=M\ltimes U\) with \(P\) and \(U\) closed, and a compact open subgroup \(K\) with \(G=PK\). They also use continuity of the projection \(P\to M\) and of the positive characters defining the unramified parameters. Under these hypotheses `normalizedDualCompactEquiv` applies the dual twist comparison to the inducing coefficients and then restricts the induced function by `unramifiedNormalizedInductionCompactEquiv`.

`normalizedCompactProductChart` identifies the tensor of the single-factor compact charts with `ProductInducedCompactPicture` for the base representation. Its construction extends both factors at a chosen unramified parameter, applies `normalizedProductInductionTensorEquiv`, and restricts via `productInducedCompactEquiv`. The factorization \(G=PK\) supplies compactness of the parabolic quotient via `compactSpace_leftQuotient_of_compact_representatives`. The product chart, its evaluation formula and its parameter independence also assume that \(G\) is second countable, as required by the product Haar modulus in `normalizedProductInductionTensorEquiv`.

`normalizedCompactProductChart_tmul_eval` combines the evaluation formulas of the three charts and `normalizedProductInductionTensorEquiv_tmul_eval`. It gives the pointwise tensor of the two compact sections on each pure tensor. Tensor induction and extensionality of compact sections then give `normalizedCompactProductChart_eq` for any two parameters.

The construction gives a fixed form of the tensor comparison used by Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques, d’après Harish-Chandra*, V.1, printed pp. 288–289, with the compact realizations of IV.1, printed pp. 276–277.
