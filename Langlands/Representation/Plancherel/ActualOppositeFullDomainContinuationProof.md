# Whole-domain continuations of the actual opposite standard integrals

`Langlands.Plancherel.actualOppositeFullDomainContinuation` proves the published `ActualOppositeFullDomainContinuationStatement`. The proof uses the independent field and representation-carrier universes of the statement. It gives one regular scalar and one global polynomial operator numerator for each of the two opposite compact families. Each scalar is nonzero throughout its corresponding full convergence domain, and the associated numerator clears the original integral at every point of that domain.

Square integrability for the supplied central quotient Haar gives an invariant positive form. Irreducibility gives finite length, and square integrability supplies admissibility. The open-region theorem `continuedPairOfUnitarizable` then supplies a nonempty open subset of full convergence and an initial continuation in each order. The compact induction pictures use the same compact open subgroup and the integrals use their original quotient Haar measures.

For each order, opposite Iwahori contraction bases on the actual point groups give a uniform polynomial multiple of the compact integral on the whole full domain. Parameter transport identifies the evaluation-generated regular algebras of the literal Levi and its representing scheme. Multiplying a convergent character by a unitary character preserves full convergence and gives continuous scalar evaluations on the resulting translate of the unitary torus. The separation tests evaluate the actual compact output at a compact point and pair it with a smooth functional of the inducing representation.

The exceptional scalar comes from `galleryPQDiscriminant` for the inducing representation twisted by the fixed character defining each such translate. Its binomials compare distinct valuation monomials. Where the scalar is nonzero, represented integral uniqueness forces the cleared compact numerator to vanish at a denominator zero. `nonempty_fullDomainContinuation_of_unitary_translates` uses this control and continuity on unitary translates to remove prime factors that vanish in the full domain. Specializing the ordered pair first to the cocharacter and its inverse, then to the exchanged order, recovers the exact forward and reverse families in the statement.

Waldspurger's IV.1.1 gives convergence on a cone and rationality of the standard integral. The Lean proof uses compact compression for a uniform polynomial multiple, followed by cancellation on unitary translates to meet the full-domain specification. The actual opposite composition of IV.3 and the density formula of V.2 motivate that specification.
