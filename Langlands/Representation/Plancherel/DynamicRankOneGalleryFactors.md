# Factors on principal open loci for rank-one gallery edges

The maximal proper parabolic in the wall Levi supplies integral product coordinates on the original valuation lattice. Its valuation kernel uses at most one coordinate. Each translation on the complement block extends to an unramified character of the wall Levi. This character is trivial on both inner radicals because their points lie in compact subgroups. The fixed-measure wall comparison preserves the scalar under such a translation.

On a nonzero principal open where the source induction is irreducible and both ordered pairs are regular, Schur's lemma supplies a unique scalar of the actual integral-normalized composite. Its uniqueness gives translation invariance whenever both parameters lie in that principal open.
