# Uniform functional bounds on polynomial operator ranges

This internal derived construction associates a finite-dimensional subspace to an actual finite polynomial vector family. All values of the family lie in this subspace. The ambient complex vector space has only its algebraic module structure; no topology or finite-dimensionality is assumed for it.

Given a positive definite algebraic inner-product core on that ambient space, every linear functional satisfies a uniform squared bound on the chosen finite-dimensional subspace: its absolute square is at most a positive constant times the real diagonal value of the core. The proof temporarily equips the space with the norm induced by its core. The restricted functional is continuous because its domain is finite dimensional, and its operator norm yields the estimate.

The polynomial numerator of one fixed input to a rational continued operator has such a finite-dimensional range subspace. Dividing by its scalar denominator keeps every regular continued value in the same subspace. Consequently one positive constant bounds the functional on every such regular value, in terms of the fixed positive quadratic form. No compactness or boundedness of the parameter set is used. This module supplies an analytic estimate for later inverse-scalar boundedness; it asserts no rational-function pole cancellation or regularity theorem.
