# Scalar product along the represented dynamic gallery

`RepresentedGalleryProductData` records a finite represented vertex set and
the ordered list of edges from the dynamic gallery. Every edge is a
same-chamber identification or an actual rank-one wall. If the representation
is `PQ`-regular for each ordered pair of vertices and the opposite composite
on each edge is scalar, the endpoint composite is the product of those
scalars times one positive constant.

The proof adds each edge in order. The two orientations of the anchored
triple apply the represented composition theorem, and both Haar factors are
canceled. The starting composite is the identity at the repeated vertex.
The factors are fixed before choosing the inducing representation.
