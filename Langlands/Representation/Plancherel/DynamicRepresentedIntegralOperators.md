# Integral-normalized operators on represented dynamic pairs

`representedIntegralOperator` is the unique integral-normalized operator on
a represented dynamic common-Levi pair at a `PQ`-regular inducing
representation and a fixed Haar measure. `representedPairHaar` fixes a
measure on each complement by normalizing at a single input compact open.
The measure comparison gives one positive scalar for all normalized maps on
the pair. The generic regularity theorem gives a nonzero principal open for
the actual intrinsic unramified twists of an irreducible admissible input.

The construction uses the Library's Renard integral uniqueness and generic
regularity. The two maps into the ambient group have exactly the same
embedded Levi, and the represented opposite intersects the source at that
Levi. Scaling the compact-test integral rescales the normalized map by Haar
uniqueness.
