# Hermitian adjunction with arbitrary compact Iwasawa data

This internal derived construction compares the original forward and reverse standard integrals with a common invariant positive Hermitian form on their coefficient representation and conjugate-inverse twisting characters. It retains the actual compact open Iwasawa subgroup `K` and the actual source quotient Haar measures. The two radicals have trivial intersection, their Haar moduli on the common Levi are reciprocal, and the quotient measures are compatible with the indicated radical and intersection measures of mass one on the trivial intersections.

The new real `hermitianAdjunctionScale` is the ratio of the compact-coordinate product Haar masses, in reverse over forward order. The set-integral adjunction states that the forward Hermitian pairing over `K` is this factor times the reverse pairing. Closedness of the parabolics, continuity of their Levi projections, and two-sided Levi Haar invariance are explicit. It assumes neither compact intersection is stable under Levi projection and does not normalize the radical or Levi compact intersections.

The proof uses the published pointwise scalar-integral identities and the same Hermitian integrand `Theta = b(f,f')` as `OppositeIntertwiningHermitianAdjunction`. Its right open stabilizer gives continuity and product integrability; reciprocal moduli and conjugate-inverse characters give left-Levi invariance. The new scaled Iwasawa exchange then proves the result. This is a derived extension of that implemented proof. A convergence proof for one standard integral or its conjugate partner is not supplied by this unit.
