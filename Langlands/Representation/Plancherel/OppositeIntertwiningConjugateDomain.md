# Conjugate-inverse convergence from forward convergence

This internal derived helper transfers full forward standard-integral convergence to scalar reverse convergence for an admissible representation with an invariant positive definite Hermitian form, at two twisting characters whose conjugate product is one. It uses the actual opposite semidirect data, reciprocal radical moduli, compatible quotient measures, an arbitrary compact open subgroup giving both Iwasawa factorizations, and the explicit compact-open cofinality condition needed for smooth Hermitian duality. No compact Levi splitting or reverse convergence is assumed.

Each reverse smooth functional is `b(v,-)` for a vector `v`, by admissibility and smooth Hermitian duality after the open-kernel twist equivalence. A compact local source section can be chosen with value `v` at the identity. It is constant with value `v` along its own unipotent radical. Forward full convergence makes the Hermitian integrand product-integrable on the forward Iwasawa coordinates. Scaled opposite Iwasawa exchange transfers this to the reverse coordinates. The integrand has an open right stabilizer, so an almost-everywhere integrable slice can be chosen inside a positive-measure identity neighborhood and then translated to the identity slice. That slice is exactly the prescribed reverse scalar integral. The quotient by the trivial radical intersection preserves integrability.

Under the original closed-Levi, reverse squared-modulus compatibility, and right-invariant reverse-radical Haar hypotheses, the published `scalarDomain_eq_domain` theorem upgrades this scalar convergence to the whole actual weak-integral domain. Admissibility of the open-kernel twist follows from the published twist theorem. `reverseDomainEquiv` packages that full domain as an identity linear equivalence with the original reverse source. The construction retains independent group and carrier universes. It proves no rational-continuation identity or scalar positivity.
