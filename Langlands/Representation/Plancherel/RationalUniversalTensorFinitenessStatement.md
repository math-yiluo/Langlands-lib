# Renard VI.8.1: actual universal tensor finiteness

Worker 1759. Statement and necessary comparison construction from David Renard,
*Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, 9 July 2026 version,
VI.8.1, printed pp.187–189 / PDF pp.193–195. Registered source:
`renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, SHA256
`7b539996793b7c3620e72e1e389588eb42d1a8e91d6f68b2eead5c2e2ffc182c`.

The proposition `RationalUniversalTensorFinitenessStatement.{uV,u}` ranges over
arbitrary nonarchimedean local fields and connected reductive group schemes,
actual supplied parabolic/Levi maps with a `ParabolicRootPresentation`, and an
admissible smooth complex action on an arbitrary carrier `E : Type uV`.
The field and group universe `u` is independent of `uV`. There is no finite
dimension, irreducibility, matrix-group or splitness hypothesis on the action.
`IsAdmissible` is the Library's smooth, all-open-subgroups finite-generation
predicate. Over complex vector spaces this agrees with the compact-open
finiteness convention in Renard; the target only asks compact-open finiteness.

`B = RationalNormalized.RegularAlgebra r` is the existing concrete subalgebra of
functions on the actual unramified character group, generated by evaluations.
Its defining norm family is `parabolicLeviRationalNormCharacters r`, indexed by
**every** member of `rationalCharacters M`. The common kernel is the intersection
of the kernels of that complete family; no finite replacement is used. The
Library's `parabolicLeviRegularFunctionsEquiv` compares this B with the scheme
Levi's canonical regular algebra, for which finite Laurent coordinates and
Noetherianity are already published. Neither fact is assumed as new model data.

The representation is precisely `universalRepresentation r σ` on the literal
`B ⊗[ℂ] E`, with action
`m (b ⊗ v) = (unramifiedEvaluationRegular m * b) ⊗ σ(m)v`.
`UniversalTensorFlatness` asks for Mathlib `Module.Flat` on this B-module.
`UniversalTensorFixedFiniteProjectivity` asks for both `Module.Finite` and
`Module.Projective` over B on the fixed submodule of this same action.

The necessary construction `universalTensorFixedInclusion` is the B-linear
base change of the original inclusion `E^J → E`, restricted to the universal
fixed submodule. Continuous positive norms are one on every compact subgroup
by the published compact-character theorem, applied to each rational norm.
Tensor induction proves that the map lands in the fixed submodule; no
admissibility or projectivity is used in this construction. Its pure-tensor
formula is `b ⊗ v ↦ b ⊗ v.val`.
`UniversalTensorFixedComparison` asserts an actual B-linear equivalence
`(B ⊗ E)^J ≃ₗ[B] B ⊗ E^J` whose inverse linear map is exactly this constructed
inclusion. Existence of that equivalence remains a proposition to prove.

The conjunction `UniversalTensorFiniteness` contains flatness and, for each
compact open J, this precise comparison and finite projectivity. These are
conclusions under admissibility of σ, with no target conclusion among the
premises. The source example on printed p.189 supplies the tensor construction,
its freeness/flatness, fixed comparison and admissibility; the proposition on
pp.187–188 supplies the finite-projectivity formulation.

The explicit topology premises are local compactness and nonarchimedeanness
of the **canonical actual** ambient rational-point topology, and affineness of
the supplied Levi. The verified presentation records the actual parabolic,
rational Levi and split component, not global splitness of G. Existence of such
presentations for all specified maps and derivation of the topology premises
from connected reductivity remain structural comparisons; this statement does
not claim those comparisons. Smoothness of the tensor action and openness of
the complete common norm kernel are already proved in
`UnramifiedRationalNormalizedModels` using the closed actual Levi.

No proof of tensor flatness, comparison bijectivity or fixed finite projectivity
is supplied here. VI.8.2 is a separate statement unit, and VI.8.3, VI.8.5 and the
VI.9 uniform-compression argument remain outside this module.
