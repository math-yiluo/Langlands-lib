# Compression of the actual rational induced family

This derived adapter applies the published native-coefficient compression
criterion to the actual universal normalized induced representation on rational
points. The field and inducing-carrier universes remain independent, and the
coefficient ring is the full rational-norm regular algebra.

`familyNativeFixedModule` equips the native fixed-vector submodule of the
actual universal induced representation with its regular-algebra module
structure. It is the existing submodule module; no scalar restriction to the
complex numbers changes this native module. The construction is local as a
typeclass instance, and the subsequent results use it for their native
finite-generation hypothesis.

`inducedCompressedAction_eventuallyStable_of_native_finite` requires finite
generation of the native induced compact-fixed module and the native normalized
output-Jacquet compact-fixed module. The output unipotent and Levi subgroups are
independent of the inducing parabolic. Their closedness, normalizer relation,
Iwahori contraction and Levi-centrality are retained. The actual regular algebra
is Noetherian by its published finite Laurent coordinates. The normalized
coefficient criterion removes normalization on the compact Levi intersection
and applies coefficient compression to the actual complex action obtained by
restricting constants.

`complexInducedCompressedAction_uniform_of_native_finite` chooses one positive
exponent from that universal action before introducing any unramified character.
The actual compact-specialization theorem transfers stability of precisely that
power to every specialized normalized induction. Bijectivity of every actual
full specialization is a genuine hypothesis at this level.

The two native finiteness assumptions and full specialization bijectivity are
explicit in this intermediate result. It does not yet assert stabilization for
every smooth representation or discharge the host uniform-compression target.
