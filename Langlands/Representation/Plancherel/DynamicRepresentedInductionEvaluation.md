# Evaluation-preserving represented induction on a dynamic common Levi

`representedPointInductionEquiv` identifies normalized induction from a
represented parabolic with the actual Levi-normalized induction on the same
parabolic, Levi and radical point images. The coefficient representations
agree by pullback along their common embedded Levi map. Its induced map
preserves the section's value at every ambient point.

`representedDynamicInductionEquiv` applies this construction to a dynamic
common-Levi vertex. Its represented induction uses the intrinsic rational
points of the original common Levi. The inducing carrier is unchanged and
the pointwise equality holds in both the forward comparison and its inverse.
