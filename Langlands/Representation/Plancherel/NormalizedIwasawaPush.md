# Normalized Iwasawa pushforwards without compact splitting

This internal derived construction normalizes the actual measure pushed from `K × U` to `M \ G` by the Haar product mass of the preimage of `K` in `M × U`. The group is second countable, the indicated spaces have their Borel structures, and ambient and subgroup measure hypotheses are explicit. For a closed parabolic subgroup, a compact open `K`, and a continuous semidirect Levi projection, that preimage is compact and open and nonempty, so its product Haar mass is finite and positive.

The actual factorization integration formula retains this mass rather than imposing compact splitting. Applied to the normalized cutoff times a left-Levi-invariant function, the Levi integral cancels its normalizing mass. Thus the Iwasawa coordinate integral is the compact-coordinate mass times the normalized-cutoff integral. On quotient functions this identifies the normalized Iwasawa pushforward with the quotient functional of that cutoff. For two actual semidirect decompositions with common Levi, closed parabolics, continuous projections, the same compact open subgroup and both Iwasawa factorizations, and a right-invariant Levi Haar measure, the normalized cutoff exchange proves equality of the two normalized pushforwards. No compact Levi splitting is assumed.

The construction uses only the published factorization integral, semidirect coordinate homeomorphism and the normalized cutoff identities; no new original-source theorem is asserted. It does not yet assert the complex integral or intertwiner adjunction.
