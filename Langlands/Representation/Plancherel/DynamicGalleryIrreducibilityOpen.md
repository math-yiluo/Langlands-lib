# A principal irreducibility locus at a represented gallery vertex

The published admissible irreducibility theorem supplies an irreducible principal neighborhood of any irreducible twist of the represented induction. Membership of the starting twist makes the defining polynomial nonzero. This applies directly to the original intrinsic Levi and its character space.

For an ordered pair of vertices, intersect that neighborhood with the simultaneous principal regularity locus of the two vertices by multiplying their nonzero polynomials. Both directions then have integral-normalized operators on a principal open where the source remains irreducible.
