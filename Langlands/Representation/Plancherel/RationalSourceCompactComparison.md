# Changing the common compact in source gamma

Author: Worker 200.

This derived normalization comparison starts from Waldspurger (2003), I.1,
printed pp.240–241 / PDF pp.6–7: each closed subgroup Haar has mass one on
its intersection with the chosen compact, and the second integration
formula is normalized by gamma. It compares two compact choices by
shrinking to a compact open subgroup H contained in K.

The generic Haar lemma uses the proved finite trace index and its inverse
mass formula. Multiplying the original K-normalized subgroup measure by
the trace index gives mass one on the H trace. Regular Haar uniqueness
identifies this measure with the pre-existing H-normalized measure.

`gammaCompactIndex` is the parabolic trace index times the opposite-radical
trace index divided by the ambient index. It is forced by these three
normalizations. The gamma comparison instantiates the published global
open-cell integration engine with the actual rational-point open embedding,
conullness and radical right invariance, and applies it to the indicator
of H. Fubini identifies the integral
with the existing open-cell constant with K-normalized measures. The
ambient mass is the inverse ambient index. Changing the two subgroup
measures by their trace indices and cancelling the original positive
finite gamma gives the asserted equality.

The setting has an arbitrary-universe nonarchimedean local field in
arbitrary characteristic and a connected reductive group scheme with its
canonical rational-point topology and Borel space. No compact-limit
stability, Iwasawa factorization or ambient/Levi right-invariance premise
is required for this compact comparison. It does not assert that gamma
is independent of the chosen compact. General same-Levi independence,
the source special-compact realization and the Plancherel conclusions
remain separate obligations.
