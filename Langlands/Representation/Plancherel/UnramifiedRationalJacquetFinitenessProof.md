# Native Jacquet finiteness for the actual universal induced supercuspidal family

Author: Worker 4981.

`Langlands.Plancherel.rationalSupercuspidalJacquetFamilyFinite` proves the
published proposition
`Langlands.Plancherel.RationalSupercuspidalJacquetFamilyFiniteStatement`
from
[UnramifiedRationalJacquetFinitenessStatement](UnramifiedRationalJacquetFinitenessStatement.md).
Its whole type is that proposition at universes `u` and `uV`. The field and
group schemes live in `u`, and the complex inducing carrier lives in the
independent universe `uV`. The theorem has no parameters, so every binder and
hypothesis of the statement is kept unchanged. These include:

- the arbitrary nonarchimedean local field and connected reductive group;
- the verified `ParabolicRootPresentation`;
- the geometric radical and the literal point semidirect decomposition;
- closedness of the radical, continuity of the projection and compactness of
  the inducing quotient;
- smoothness, admissibility, irreducibility and actual supercuspidality of the
  inducing representation.

For every output cocharacter `chi` and every compact open subgroup `J` of its
literal dynamic centralizer, the conclusion is

```lean
Module.Finite (RationalNormalized.RegularAlgebra r)
  (fixedVectors
    (RationalNormalized.outputJacquetRepresentation r h hU sigma chi)
    J.toSubgroup)
```

The coefficient algebra is generated by the complete
`parabolicLeviRationalNormCharacters r` family. The representation is the full
universal normalized induction on the actual tensor and covariant-function
carrier, and the output is the normalized native coefficient Jacquet action on
the literal character-one coinvariant quotient. No finiteness, admissibility or
filtration premise is added.

## Implemented proof

The proof applies the published conditional reduction
`Plancherel.rationalSupercuspidalJacquetFamilyFinite_of_structural`, from
[UnramifiedRationalJacquetStructuralReduction](UnramifiedRationalJacquetStructuralReduction.md).
That reduction has type

```lean
GroupScheme.RepresentedParabolicDoubleCosetStatement.{u} ->
  Aubert.General.RepresentedParabolic.ArbitraryParabolicQuotientCompactStatement.{u} ->
    RationalSupercuspidalJacquetFamilyFiniteStatement.{u, uV}
```

The proof supplies both premises from published unconditional theorems.

### Finite double cosets with torus-adapted representatives

`GroupScheme.RepresentedParabolicDoubleCosetStatement` says the following.
For two rational parabolics `p`, `q` with rational Levis `l`, `lQ` of a
connected reductive group over any field, the double-coset quotient
`P(k) \ G(k) / Q(k)` of the literal point images is finite. Moreover, every
double coset contains a rational point `z` and an ambient maximal split torus
that factors through `l ≫ p` and through `lQ ≫ q ≫ int(z)`.

The proof supplies it by the published assembly
`GroupScheme.representedParabolicDoubleCosetStatement_of_dynamic_structural_inputs`
from
[ParabolicDoubleCosetAssembly](../../GroupScheme/Rational/ParabolicDoubleCosetAssembly.md).
That assembly takes six premises, and each is supplied by its published proof:

| Premise | Published proof | Content (source) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `RationalBruhatStatement` | `GroupScheme.rationalBruhat` | Rational Bruhat decomposition for the dynamic parabolic of a generic cocharacter of a maximal split torus, indexed by the relative Weyl group |
| `GenericMinimalParabolicStatement` | `GroupScheme.genericMinimalParabolic` | A generic cocharacter of a maximal split torus gives a minimal rational parabolic |
| `MinimalParabolicConjugacyStatement` | `GroupScheme.minimalParabolicConjugacy` | Minimal parabolics are conjugate by a rational point (Borel–Tits 4.13) |
| `ParabolicIntersectionCentralizerStatement` | `GroupScheme.parabolicIntersectionCentralizer` | Two rational parabolics contain the centralizer of a common maximal split torus (Borel–Tits 4.18) |
| `ParabolicDynamicStatement` | `GroupScheme.parabolicDynamic` | Parabolics are the dynamic `P(λ)` of rational cocharacters (Milne 25.1) |
| `SmoothMaximalSplitTorusConjugacyStatement` | `GroupScheme.smoothMaximalSplitTorusConjugacy` | Maximal split tori are conjugate by a rational point (CGP C.2.3) |

As the assembly's Markdown records, finiteness uses the first three premises.
The torus-adapted representative uses the last three.

### Compactness of every represented parabolic quotient

`Aubert.General.RepresentedParabolic.ArbitraryParabolicQuotientCompactStatement`
asserts `CompactSpace (Induction.LeftQuotient (pointImage p))`. This holds for
every parabolic `p` of every connected reductive group over a nonarchimedean
local field, in the canonical point topology. The proof introduces the
statement's binders and installs the affine instance `hG.isAffineHom`. It then
applies the published helper
`Aubert.General.RepresentedParabolic.compactSpace_leftQuotient_pointImage_of_dynamic`
from
[ArbitraryParabolicFiniteSupportReduction](../Exponents/ArbitraryParabolicFiniteSupportReduction.md)
to `GroupScheme.parabolicDynamic` and `BruhatTits.dynamicParabolicQuotientCompact`.

- The helper obtains a rational cocharacter `λ` with `range p = P(λ)`. It
  evaluates this equality at the identity test scheme to rewrite the literal
  point image as the dynamic parabolic points. It then selects the `P(k) g`
  conjunct of the dynamic compactness statement.
- `BruhatTits.dynamicParabolicQuotientCompact`, from
  [QuotientCompactProof](../../BruhatTits/Parabolic/QuotientCompactProof.md),
  proves compactness of both coset conventions for dynamic parabolics. It uses
  the dynamic flag quotient and compactness of the rational points of proper
  schemes over local fields.

The helper does not use the retained instances `T2Space k`,
`LocallyCompactSpace (rationalPoints G)` and
`NonarchimedeanGroup (rationalPoints G)`; they stay as binders of the
statement. This composition is inlined and given no global name. That way the module neither
waits for nor duplicates a separately submitted proof of the same compactness
statement.

## How the coefficient finiteness is obtained

Once both premises are supplied, the published reduction derives the
conclusion. The route is as follows; the cited declarations are documented in
their own Markdown.

1. For an arbitrary output cocharacter `chi`, `Dynamic.affineDecompositionModel`
   supplies actual affine output parabolic, centralizer and unipotent schemes.
   Published theorems prove that they are parabolic, connected reductive and a
   geometric unipotent radical, and give the rational-Levi witness.
2. `RationalNormalized.nativeJacquetFiniteOrbitGeometryOfStructural` uses the
   double-coset premise to construct actual finite ordered orbit geometry,
   using `Geometric.closedSubgroupOrbitOrdering` and representative
   replacement. This geometry consists of open initial orbit unions, an actual
   `GeometricLemmaIntersectionDatum` at each inverse representative, and its
   simultaneous `DynamicRealization`. The source cocharacter is the
   root-presentation split-component cocharacter. The compactness premise,
   applied to the output Levi with its Levi point topology, gives compactness
   of each target-intersection parabolic quotient.
3. `RationalNormalized.outputJacquet_fixedModule_finite_of_geometry`, from
   [UnramifiedRationalJacquetGeometryFiniteness](UnramifiedRationalJacquetGeometryFiniteness.md),
   reduces to `universalNativeJacquet_isAdmissible` in
   [NativeJacquetSupportAdmissibility](../Geometric/NativeJacquetSupportAdmissibility.md).
   That theorem builds the actual finite support-image filtration of the
   normalized native quotient of the full universal induction. It identifies
   each grade with a normalized native orbit quotient, and
   `universalNativeOrbitJacquet_isAdmissible` proves each such quotient
   admissible. This uses the proper-source vanishing given by actual
   supercuspidality, and the coefficient admissibility of the surviving
   conjugate pieces. The grades are then assembled over the Noetherian
   `RegularAlgebra r` by the finite-filtration extension theorem.
4. `outputJacquet_isAdmissible_of_geometry` transports this admissibility
   along the subgroup equalities to the literal `outputJacquetRepresentation`.
   Admissibility covers every open subgroup, so its fixed-vector component at
   the given compact open `J` is the displayed conclusion.

This is the coefficient-geometric-lemma route of Renard VI.9.2. The proof
derives the compact-open fixed-module finiteness rather than assuming it.

## Source comparison

The source is David Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*,
2026 version, registered as
`renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, SHA256
`7b539996793b7c3620e72e1e389588eb42d1a8e91d6f68b2eead5c2e2ffc182c`.

- On printed p.199 / PDF p.205, Renard applies Proposition VI.5.3 (printed
  p.173 / PDF p.179) to the universal supercuspidal family `i_Q^G(Π_1, V_1)`.
  The Jacquet module `r_P^G i_Q^G(Π_1, V_1)` has a filtration whose quotients
  are `i^M_{w·L}(wΠ_1)` when `L` is conjugate to a Levi subgroup of `M`, and
  zero otherwise. Hence it is an admissible `(M, F)`-module.
- In the Lean proof, the filtration is the actual support-image filtration
  indexed by the finite ordered double cosets. Vanishing and conjugate
  inductions correspond to the proper-source and surviving-source
  alternatives of the orbit admissibility theorem.
- Renard takes the geometric inputs from Chapter V. The Corollary of V.4.5
  (printed pp.119–120 / PDF pp.125–126) gives finiteness of the orbits of `Q`
  on `P\G`. It is deduced from the Bruhat decomposition (referred to [7],
  5.15–5.20) and from conjugacy to standard parabolics. The Corollary of V.5.1
  (printed p.123 / PDF p.129) gives compactness of `G/P`. Here the finiteness,
  the torus-adapted representatives and the compactness are supplied by the
  published theorems named above.

The proof route differs from the source only in this presentation. It proves
the unchanged statement and does not concern the later VI.9.2 steps on
eventual stability or the bound `b ≤ c(G, K)`.
