# Points of the algebra of regular functions on unramified characters

Module `Langlands.Representation.Plancherel.UnramifiedRegularFunctionPoints`,
namespace `Langlands.Plancherel`.

## Provenance

Internal helper for the proof of Renard VI.7.1 (Theorem; Renard 2026, French version dated
9 July 2026, printed pp.180–181). Renard identifies `X(M)` with the complex points of the
torus with coordinate ring `ℂ[X(M)]` (Renard V.2). Here this is proved for the Library's
`unramifiedRegularFunctions ν`: the `ℂ`-subalgebra of functions on
`X = unramifiedCharacters ν` generated by the evaluations `χ ↦ χ(g)`
(`unramifiedEvaluation ν g`).

## Setting

`G` is a topological group, and `ν : I → (G →* ℝ≥0ˣ)` is a family of norm characters with
common kernel `commonNormKernel ν`. `X = unramifiedCharacters ν` is the group of continuous
characters `G → ℂˣ` that are trivial on that kernel.

## Statements

- `unramifiedRegularFunctions_separates`: distinct characters `χ ≠ χ'` are separated by some
  `f ∈ unramifiedRegularFunctions ν`.
- `unramifiedRegularFunctions_algHom_ext`: two `ℂ`-algebra maps from
  `unramifiedRegularFunctions ν` to any `ℂ`-algebra are equal if they agree on every evaluation
  `unramifiedEvaluationRegular ν g`.
- `unramifiedRegularFunctions_algHom_eq_eval`: assume `[IsTopologicalGroup G]` and that
  `commonNormKernel ν` is open. Then every `ℂ`-algebra map
  `ψ : unramifiedRegularFunctions ν → ℂ` is evaluation at some `χ : unramifiedCharacters ν`.

## Proofs

- **Separation.** If `χ(g) = χ'(g)` for every `g`, the two characters are equal. So some `g`
  has `χ(g) ≠ χ'(g)`, and the generator `unramifiedEvaluation ν g` separates them.
- **Extensionality.** Induction over `Algebra.adjoin` (`Algebra.adjoin_induction`): the maps
  agree on generators, on scalars, and on sums and products.
- **Evaluation.** Let `e g = unramifiedEvaluationRegular ν g`. The map
  `ω(g) = ψ(e g)` is multiplicative, because `e (g h) = e g * e h` and `e 1 = 1`. Its values
  are units, since `ψ(e g) ψ(e g⁻¹) = 1`, so `ω : G →* ℂˣ`. On the common norm kernel,
  `e g = 1`, so `ω` is trivial there. Since that kernel is an open neighbourhood of `1`, `ω` is
  locally constant at `1` and therefore continuous (`continuous_of_continuousAt_one`). So `ω`
  defines `χ ∈ unramifiedCharacters ν`. The maps `ψ` and "evaluate at `χ`" agree on every
  `e g`, so the extensionality lemma shows they are equal.
