# Integral-normalized composition for represented dynamic common-Levi triples

`exists_representedIntegralIntertwiner_comp_smul` applies to a distance-additive
triple of represented dynamic parabolics with the same embedded Levi. The
composite of two integral-normalized intertwiners is a positive multiple of
the direct operator at the specified three Haar measures. The factor is
chosen before the inducing representation and the operators.

The literal multiplication chart on the three opposite complements transports
to the represented point images. The two consecutive measures determine a
compatible Haar measure on the direct complement. Haar uniqueness compares
it to the specified direct measure. The compact-test identities give the
composite formula at one under that comparison, and `PQ`-regularity of the
direct pair gives equality of the equivariant maps.
