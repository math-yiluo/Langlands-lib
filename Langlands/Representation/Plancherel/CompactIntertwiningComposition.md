# Composition of rational families and actual compact intertwiners

This internal unit supplies composition calculus for the published
`UnramifiedOperatorFamily` predicates and `compactIntertwiningOperator`.
It is motivated by Waldspurger IV.1(12), printed p.283 / PDF p.49, but does
not prove the source's standard-integral factorization.

## Scalar algebra and composition

The parameter subset `Ω` is an arbitrary subset of the actual
`unramifiedCharacters ν`, where `ν` is a supplied family of norm
characters of a topological group. Its scalar algebra is the published
`unramifiedRegularFunctions ν`, generated by all actual character
evaluations. No openness, nonemptiness, descent to an isomorphism-class
orbit, finite-rank lattice or algebraic-variety comparison is assumed.

Polynomial vector families are closed under finite sums. A polynomial
operator family applied to a polynomial vector family is polynomial:
expand the vector family in finitely many fixed vectors, use the operator's
polynomiality on those vectors, and multiply each expansion by its regular
coefficient. Consequently polynomial operators are closed under varying
composition. Rational operators are also closed under varying composition;
the product of the two original regular denominators is nonzero on the
parameter subset and clears the entire composite operator. No
finite-dimensionality is assumed. The vector carriers have independent
universes.

The declarations are
`UnramifiedOperatorFamily.IsPolynomialOn.sum`,
`IsPolynomialOperatorOn.apply`, `IsPolynomialOperatorOn.comp` and
`IsRationalOperatorOn.comp`, in the same namespace. In the last proof,
if `a` clears `A` and `b` clears `B`, then `b * a` clears `B.comp A`.
Each input vector retains its own finite numerator expansion, while the
denominator is chosen once for the whole composite. The varying operator
application proof uses a finite sum over the expansion's `Fin n`;
the sum proof combines expansions by the existing addition theorem.

## Actual compact-picture families

For actual normalized inductions attached to three closed semidirect
decompositions with one common Levi and compact subgroup, the published
restriction/extension equivalences conjugate ambient intertwiners into
linear maps of fixed compact pictures. This conjugation is injective,
preserves identity and composition, and makes the ambient composition
equation equivalent to the compact composition equation. The proofs cancel
the intermediate equivalence and its inverse. All closedness,
unimodularity, compact factorization and continuity premises required to
construct those equivalences remain explicit.

More precisely, the ambient group is locally compact and nonarchimedean.
The supplied decompositions `Pᵢ = M Uᵢ` have closed radicals and closed
parabolics, and each radical has the displayed
`IsUnimodularModulus` proof. One compact open subgroup `K` gives every
factorization `G = Pᵢ K`; the Levi projections and all supplied norm
characters are continuous. The inducing representation `A` is an
arbitrary canonical complex `SmoothRep` of `M`. There is no
irreducibility, admissibility, finite-length or induced-irreducibility
premise in these algebraic transport lemmas.

`compactIntertwiningOperatorFamily` constructs the fixed-picture family
from a dependent family of actual ambient intertwiners at the parameters
of `Ω`. It uses the published `compactIntertwiningOperator` at each
parameter, so its source and target are the original compact induction
pictures and its input maps intertwine the original twisted normalized
ambient representations.

`compactIntertwiningOperator_injective` recovers the entire ambient map
by evaluating the compact equality on restrictions of source vectors,
then cancelling the target equivalence.
`compactIntertwiningOperator_id` and
`compactIntertwiningOperator_comp` give identity and composition.
`compactIntertwiningOperator_comp_eq_iff` reflects as well as preserves
an ambient composition equation.
`compactIntertwiningOperatorFamily_comp` gives the same identity for
parameter families.
`compactIntertwiningOperatorFamily_rational_comp` combines that
identity with product-denominator rationality. Its two rationality
premises are facts to be proved for any intended application; they are
not granted structural hypotheses for completing the Plancherel
program.

## Scope and provenance

These are algebraic facts about the existing concrete operators and
evaluation-generated scalar algebra. They neither supply the integral
factorization of IV.1(12), the root-distance condition there, nonvanishing,
duality, meromorphic continuation nor the Plancherel formula. In particular
they do not select arbitrary operator families in place of standard
integrals.

Specializing the ambient families to the published
`fullSourceIntertwiner` still requires its actual full-domain equalities,
compatible quotient Haar measures and squared-modulus identities.
This module does not prove those prerequisites. Equating the resulting
composite with a direct standard integral further requires the actual
quotient integral factorization and, for the source's general assertion,
the root-distance condition and continuation argument. None of these
remaining analytic or structural comparisons is claimed here.

Source: J.-L. Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes
p-adiques (d'après Harish-Chandra)*, J. Inst. Math. Jussieu 2 (2003),
235–333, DOI 10.1017/S1474748003000082, registered as
`waldspurger-2003-plancherel`. IV.1, printed pp.276–278, motivates the
single-denominator convention; IV.1(12), printed p.283, motivates
composition. The implemented results are internal algebraic deductions
from the previously published definitions, rather than translations or
proofs of that numbered source assertion.
