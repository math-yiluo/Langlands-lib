# Conditional vector and operator denominator cancellation

This derived supporting construction lifts conditional uniform scalar
cancellation to vector and operator families on the actual unramified character
space. It explicitly assumes
`RationalUnramifiedLocalHypersurfaceDivisibilityStatement.{u}`.
The local hypersurface proposition remains unproved here. The construction
adds no premise to the conditional standard intertwining statement.

## Predicates and elementary polynomial laws

`UnramifiedOperatorFamily.IsWeaklyContinuousOn tests f` says that, for each
supplied complex linear scalar test `tests j`, the function
`chi -> tests j (f chi)` is continuous on the parameter subtype `U`.
This definition works for any norm-character family. It assumes neither a
topology on the vector target nor separation by the tests. The latter is a
separate premise in the cancellation theorems that need it.

`isPolynomialOn_scalar_iff` identifies the existing finite-expansion
polynomial predicate for complex-valued functions with being the restriction
of a single element of `unramifiedRegularFunctions`. The forward direction
sums the regular coefficients multiplied by their fixed scalar vectors;
the reverse direction uses a one-term expansion.

`isPolynomialOn_of_linearMap_injective` descends a finite polynomial expansion
through any injective complex linear map. It applies the existing
`IsPolynomialOn.linearMap` law to an algebraic linear left inverse. Neither
carrier needs a topology for this step.

## Actual character parameters and common denominator

The cancellation results use an arbitrary nonarchimedean local field in
universe `u`, a connected reductive group scheme `G`, a supplied topology on
its rational points, and continuity of
`rationalValuationImageProjection G`. The norm family contains every
`rationalNormCharacters G`. The scalar algebra is exactly the existing
complex algebra generated by actual character evaluations for that family.
`U` is any open subset of the full unramified character space, with its
inherited compact-open topology. No characteristic, splitness,
quasisplitness, positive-rank or nonempty-region premise is imposed.

For a nonzero regular scalar `b`, write `d` for
`rationalUnramifiedCancelledDenominator G hp hG hdiv hU b hb`.
The preceding scalar module chooses `d` before every continuous scalar
function and numerator. It proves that `d` is a nonzero divisor of `b`, is
nonzero at every point of `U`, and turns every identity `b*f=a` on `U` into
a regular scalar numerator for `d*f`. All results in this module use that
same choice of `d`; they make no new choice depending on a vector or test.

## Continuous vector cancellation

`rationalUnramified_cancel_vector_numerator` assumes that the target `W` is
a Hausdorff topological complex vector space, that `f : U -> W` is
continuous, and that `b*f` has a finite expansion in fixed vectors with
regular scalar coefficients on all of `U`. It proves that `d*f` has such
an expansion on all of `U`.

Let `S` be the span of the finitely many fixed numerator vectors. At a
parameter where `b` is nonzero, the numerator identity implies `f` belongs
to `S`. Finite dimensionality makes `S` closed. The actual scalar density
theorem makes the nonzero locus of `b` dense in the open subtype `U`.
Continuity therefore implies `f` belongs to `S` at every parameter.

Regard `f` as a continuous `S`-valued function and choose a finite basis of
`S`. Each basis coordinate is continuous and has a regular scalar numerator
with denominator `b`. Conditional scalar cancellation gives regular
coefficients for all coordinates of `d*f`, using the same fixed `d`.
The basis reconstruction identity then gives a finite vector expansion of
`d*f`. The proof includes the zero-dimensional span and the empty region.

## Separating tests and operator families

`rationalUnramified_cancel_weak_vector_numerator` uses arbitrary scalar
linear tests that separate points of `W`, weak continuity under those tests,
and a finite regular numerator for `b*f`. It embeds `W` linearly into the
product `J -> C` of test values. This map is injective by point separation,
and the embedded family is continuous by coordinatewise continuity. The
product is a Hausdorff topological complex vector space, so the preceding
vector theorem applies. Polynomiality descends through the injective map
using its algebraic linear left inverse. No continuity under other
algebraic scalar functionals is assumed.

`rationalUnramified_isRationalOperatorOn_of_weak_continuity` assumes one
nonzero regular scalar `b`, a linear operator family `A : U -> V -> W`,
weak continuity for each fixed input vector, and the existing
`IsPolynomialOperatorOn` predicate for `b*A`. It proves the existing
`IsRationalOperatorOn U A` predicate. The witness is the same `d`, chosen
before all source vectors and nonzero everywhere in `U`. Each source vector
may have its own finite numerator support. The source and target vector
spaces have independent universes `uV` and `uW`; the test index has its own
universe `uJ`.

## Provenance and remaining obligations

The module is derived from the existing finite-expansion polynomial laws,
the preceding conditional scalar cancellation, finite-dimensional closedness
and coordinate continuity, and an algebraic left inverse. No external
representation-theoretic result is used as a proof of an omitted step.

The explicit local hypersurface divisibility premise remains a separate
proof obligation. The hypotheses also require actual continuity of the
family under the supplied separating tests and a single nonzero regular
scalar giving a finite vector numerator on all of `U`. This module does
not establish these hypotheses for the actual standard integral, its
convergence, or the host conditional standard intertwining theorem.
