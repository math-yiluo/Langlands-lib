# Multiplication of the opposite complements in a distance-additive triple

`oppositeComplementProduct` is the literal multiplication homeomorphism from
`(Ū₀ ∩ U₁) × (Ū₁ ∩ U₂)` to `Ū₀ ∩ U₂` for a distance-additive dynamic
common-Levi triple. Applying the first complement inclusion to the reversed
inverse triple puts the second factor in `Ū₀`. In the ordered chart on `U₂`,
both complement factors therefore lie in `Ū₀`. Membership in the direct
complement forces the first coordinate in `U₀ ∩ U₂` to lie in `U₀ ∩ Ū₀`,
so it is one. The inverse chart retains the other two coordinates.
