# The scalar on a same-chamber gallery step

The equality of both radical subgroups gives distance additivity for the gallery path from the first vertex to the second and back to the first. The integral composition theorem expresses its composite as a fixed positive scalar times the repeated-vertex operator, with the prescribed gallery Haar measures.

An irreducible admissible coefficient representation gives a nonzero principal open of twists where the repeated pair is `PQ`-regular and its operator is the identity. On that open, the scalar identity also assumes `PQ`-regularity in both orders of the original pair. The constant factor uses a rank-zero first valuation block and the actual positive scale from the composition theorem.
