# The common compact test integral in the two actual radical quotients

The ambient test descends through the two original intersection quotients.
The descended function has its ambient value at each representative.
Using the compatible Weil measures, its extended nonnegative integral
against the radical quotient and then the original compact Haar measure
is the Iwasawa compact-coordinate mass times the ambient mass of the test
subgroup, divided by the Levi and intersection average at the identity.
For compact open test subgroups that value is positive and finite.

When both parabolics have the same centralizer and the original compact
open subgroup gives both Iwasawa decompositions, the positive finite
integral is the same in the two orders. The intersection swap identifies
the two identity values. The orbital test for the actual radical Haar
measures identifies the two Iwasawa masses on the literal common Levi.
This is the measure calculation for the compact test of Waldspurger's
IV.1(11).
