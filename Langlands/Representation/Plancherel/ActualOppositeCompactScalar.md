# The canonical opposite scalar on actual compact continuations

`conditionalCanonicalRationalScalar_compact_spec` puts the published
`conditionalCanonicalRationalScalar_spec` on the fixed compact pictures.
`continuedInductionComposite_compact` conjugates the ambient round trip by
the prescribed compact equivalence. `compactIntertwiningOperator_scalar_iff`
identifies its action with the scalar in the canonical fraction. The
identity is valid on the common regular region of the two given
continuations. It uses their original quotient measures and their separate
nonempty open convergence regions.

The forward and reverse comparison lemmas apply the uniqueness of
continuation on each ordered pair's own nonempty open region. Thus the
compact scalar can also be read through continuations chosen on smaller
open convergence regions, on the intersection of the regular sets.
