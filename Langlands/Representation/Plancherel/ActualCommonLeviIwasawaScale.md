# The actual common-Levi compact Iwasawa scale

The two parabolic radicals carry their original Haar measures, normalized
on their intersections with the given compact open subgroup. The proof
places the two cocharacters in a common maximal split torus. A compact
value of the first cocharacter avoids the finitely many nonzero weights
of the Lie displacement. Equality of the actual centralizers ensures
the second radical's positive weights also have nonzero pairing with
the first cocharacter. The inverse of this value is the compact central
point used in the orbital test.

The displacement determinant is a product with actual root multiplicities.
Opposite factors have the same normalized absolute value at a norm-one
unit. Negating the mixed-sign weights matches the two products, which are
both nonzero. The radical Haar change of variables yields a positive
finite orbital mass shared by the two radicals. Evaluating the Iwasawa
pushforwards on the resulting common orbital test proves that their
normalization ratio is one for the given common Iwasawa subgroup.

This is the compact normalization calculation used toward the exact
probability adjunction in Waldspurger's IV.1(11) and Lemma V.3.1.
