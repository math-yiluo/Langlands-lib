# Integral-normalized operators at the vertices of a dynamic gallery

The represented vertex and opposite vertex are constructed with the same
embedded common Levi and same split component. `galleryIntegralOperator`
uses the actual Haar scale at the chosen compact open and is the unique
integral-normalized operator on each `PQ`-regular pair. The total function
is zero outside that locus. Fix an irreducible admissible representation of
the common Levi. For its unramified twists and any finite set of vertices,
the product of all ordered-pair regularity polynomials gives one nonzero
principal open valid for all its pairs.

For a distance-additive triple, the two-step composition theorem chooses
one positive factor for the three fixed measures, uniformly in the inducing
representation. The representation must be `PQ`-regular for the two
consecutive ordered pairs and for the direct ordered pair. When the repeated
pair `(x,x)` is `PQ`-regular, the complement is trivial and its Haar mass is
one, so uniqueness of integral normalization gives the identity.
