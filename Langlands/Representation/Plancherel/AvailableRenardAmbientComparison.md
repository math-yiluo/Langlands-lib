# The ambient Renard Cartan comparison on locally available KP data

This internal derived unit proves `availableSpecialCompactRenardComparison` at arbitrary universe `u`. Its building hypothesis is exactly `𝔅.IsAvailableFor F G hG`, for the given nonarchimedean local field and connected reductive group. For every generic cocharacter and special apartment vertex it proves `SpecialCompactRenardComparison` for the actual geometric compact subgroup of that vertex, the supplied ambient centralizer, and its original character valuation.

The proof imports `Langlands.BruhatTits.Cartan.availableCartan` for the parahoric Cartan conclusions. The inclusion of that parahoric into the geometric compact subgroup and compactness of the latter are the corresponding conclusions of `Langlands.BruhatTits.Iwasawa.availableIwasawa`. Normality of the zero normalizer is supplied by `ParahoricBuildingData.normal_normalizerZero`. These inputs discharge the hypotheses of `specialCompactCartanComparison_of_parahoric`, which yields coverage and classification for the same compact subgroup modulo the actual centralizer norm kernel. The second component is `dominantLattice_of_maximalSplitTorus`, applied to the given maximal split torus and unchanged centralizer valuation; smoothness of the reductive group supplies local finite type.

This is the ambient conjunct of `AvailableRenardCartanStatement`. Its standard-Levi conjunct, including comparison for the preimage of this same compact subgroup in each actual standard Levi, remains a separate obligation. No existence of available data or global validity is asserted or assumed. The unit makes no additional original-source assertion: it composes the named published results with their existing hypotheses.

`availableSpecialCompactChamberEquiv` packages the established geometric comparison as the existing `dominantChamberDoubleCosetEquiv`. Its domain is the actual image of the centralizer valuation cut by the dominant chamber, and its codomain consists of double cosets of the original special compact subgroup. It takes the same local availability, genericity and special-vertex hypotheses. This construction introduces no coverage or classification premise: those are proved by `availableSpecialCompactRenardComparison`.
