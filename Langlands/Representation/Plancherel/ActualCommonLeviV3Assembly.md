# Assembling the common-Levi unitary comparison from its generic identities

The fixed compact transport identifies the norm of the generic compact
comparison with the exact formal-degree norm. Apply the generic form
identity to the pullback of a fixed compact vector. The resulting norm in
the target model is the source model's norm of that vector, independent of
the parameter. Multiplying by the given whole-operator denominator gives the
cleared norm estimate on the dense unitary generic locus. Continuity of the
modeled polynomial numerator and of that same denominator extends the
estimate to every unitary point.

A factorization of the denominator in the actual scalar algebra supplies
the unitary root factors for cancellation. The resulting regular germs give
the full product-group comparison family `comparisonFamilyOfPoleFactorization`.
Generic agreement and uniqueness use the rational germ of the specified
standard tensor. The generic form identity extends to all unitary points,
so the comparison is bijective by the finite fixed-space argument. It also
preserves the entire coefficient function when the exact identity coefficient
is preserved on the unitary generic locus.

This internal assembly implements the dense-unitary estimate and regularity
argument invoked in Waldspurger (2003), Lemma V.3.1, printed p. 295, PDF p. 61,
with the pole cancellation from Corollary V.2.3, printed p. 294, PDF p. 60. The
continuations, genuine generic density, factorizable denominator and generic
identities are inputs to this construction.
