# The represented complement chart of the actual pair quotient

For two dynamic parabolics with the original common Levi, the product of
the intersection of their radicals and the intersection of the opposite
source radical with the target radical parametrizes the target radical.
Passing to left cosets of the first factor identifies the second factor
with the actual intertwining quotient. Each represented element of that
second factor has its original ambient element as a quotient representative.

The right invariance of the actual quotient measure pulls back along
multiplication to right invariance on the complement. The complement is
contained in the original opposite radical, whose compact open subgroups
give right invariance of its source-normalized Haar measure. Haar
uniqueness compares the two measures by a scalar. The original quotient
measure is nonzero, so the scalar is positive. The transport to the
represented subgroup preserves ambient elements and the original compact
open Haar normalization.

This is a derived comparison of the Library's actual standard integration
quotient and its represented integral normalization. Waldspurger uses the
opposite-complement chart in the adjacent-parabolic step of IV.1.1
(printed p. 278, PDF p. 44). The comparison applies to every pair in the
common-Levi specialization of V.3.1 (printed p. 295, PDF p. 61).
