# Canonical parameter maps for complete presentations

This is a derived structural adapter for the conventions of Waldspurger,
*La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques*, IV.1, printed
pp.276–278 / PDF pp.42–44 (registered source
`waldspurger-2003-plancherel`, SHA256
`4b6a6162cd0b3863703fb2f65d1698f926caf813114917f2084c16e38f2e30f7`).
The scheme and character maps are internal constructions connecting the
published complete-presentation and dynamic common-Levi interfaces; the paper
does not state these Lean-specific maps.

Fix an arbitrary nonarchimedean local field F, an affine locally finite type
group scheme G/F, an affine Levi scheme M, and maps p : P → G,
l : M → P, a : T → M and n : U → P. A complete compatible root presentation
is retained as `r : ParabolicRootPresentation p l a`. `Realizes χ ψ p l n`
requires equality of the *all-test* ranges of p, l ≫ p and n ≫ p with the
dynamic parabolic for ψ, centralizer for χ and radical for ψ, respectively.
It does not assume any comparison of analytic families. Connected reductivity
and an opposite presentation are unnecessary for these maps alone.

`leviPoints_eq`, `parabolicPoints_eq` and `radicalPoints_eq` evaluate those
functor equalities at Spec F. `subgroupHomeomorphOfEq` and
`representationOfEq` are identity-on-ambient-element transport and pullback
of a canonical SmoothRep, respectively; the representation carrier has an
independent universe. `leviPointComparison` is this constructed homeomorphism
for the literal Levi. Its ambient-element identity is `leviPointComparison_coe`.

The root presentation supplies closed immersions of the Levi and parabolic.
Uniqueness of closed representatives constructs
`leviSchemeComparison : M ≅ parameterScheme χ`, with the proved commuting
inclusion `leviSchemeComparison_hom_comp`. `parameterPointComparison` goes
from canonical centralizer points to the supplied literal Levi.
`parameterPointComparison_coe` and `parameterPointComparison_scheme` identify
it with the same ambient Levi elements and with the map induced by the scheme
isomorphism. No group or representation equivalence is supplied as a premise.

`parameterPointComparison_kernel` compares the common kernels of *every*
rational-character norm on both schemes, using functoriality for the scheme
isomorphism and its inverse. The index types need not agree.
`characterComparison` therefore constructs the multiplicative homeomorphism
from the canonical unramified characters to the presentation characters by
precomposition with `parameterPointComparison.symm`. Its inverse
precomposes with `parameterPointComparison`. The two evaluation lemmas display
these formulas.

`regularFunctionsComparison` is precomposition by the inverse character map.
It restricts to the full algebras generated by point evaluations: each
evaluation at a canonical point goes to the evaluation at its actual image.
The proof maps algebraic spans of these generators and uses surjectivity of
the point map for the reverse inclusion. The evaluation and inverse-evaluation
formulas are proved. Thus this preserves the canonical centralizer character
algebra, rather than assuming rationality preservation.

`parameterPreimageComparison` identifies exact corresponding character
subsets. `isPolynomialOn_comparison_iff`,
`isPolynomialOperatorOn_comparison_iff` and
`isRationalOperatorOn_comparison_iff` transport finite evaluation-polynomial
expansions and whole-operator rationality in both directions. The rational
comparison transports one denominator, chosen before the input vector, with
nonvanishing on the specified subset. Source and target module carriers have
independent universes. These are transport lemmas for a given family; they do
not assert rationality of the actual standard integral.

Comparison of normalized induction, compact restrictions and the source
quotient measures, and extraction of a nonempty rational starting region from
IV.1.1, are separate obligations. This unit proves no convergence or analytic
existence statement and makes none a structural hypothesis.
