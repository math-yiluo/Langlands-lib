# The scalar of the compact continued composite from the gallery

At each actual unramified parameter, the inverse compact restriction
followed by the inverse represented induction equivalence identifies
the compact picture with the represented induction. The whole
continued compact operator commutes with that identification.

For two continued maps in opposite directions, take the product of
their fixed positive gallery normalization constants. At a common
regular parameter where both ordered pairs are `PQ`-regular, every
scalar computed by the represented gallery composite gives the scalar
of the actual compact continued composite after multiplication by that
product. The argument conjugates both factors and uses linearity of
the transports. It applies in particular to the smooth-dual operators
evaluated at the inverse parameter in Waldspurger (2003), V.3.1,
printed p. 295.
