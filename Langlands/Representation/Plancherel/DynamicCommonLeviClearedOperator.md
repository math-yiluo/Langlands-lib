# Cleared actual endpoint operators on the represented regular locus

The actual endpoint actions on the compact pictures are polynomial.
The same intertwining equations therefore hold for the entire
polynomial numerator, including at zeros of its denominator. The
compact and represented induction equivalences give a represented
intertwiner from that numerator at every parameter.

A compact test section on the represented complement is the extension
of a fixed compact-picture vector. Its support on the complement stays
compact under all twists, and its point evaluations are polynomial.
After integrating the resulting finite locally constant family, the
compact-test identity on the original open set extends by polynomial
uniqueness to the cleared numerator. Evaluation at the identity is
zero when the denominator vanishes.

The represented boundary-vanishing test space has these compact
restrictions. Adding the cleared numerator to the fixed normalized
gallery map therefore gives another normalized map at every
`PQ`-regular parameter on the denominator zero locus. Uniqueness of
the normalized integral gives vanishing of the whole numerator
through the compact restriction equivalence.

This derived proof supplies the numerator-vanishing criterion for the
pole support step in Waldspurger (2003), IV.1(13) and V.3.1, printed
pp. 283 and 295.
