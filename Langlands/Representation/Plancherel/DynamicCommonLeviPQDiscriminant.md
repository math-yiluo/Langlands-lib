# A factored scalar controlling the represented `PQ`-regular locus

The finite regularity tests compare the character values at two
rational points with distinct valuation images. Divide the two
monomials and subtract the scalar in the test to obtain one ambient
regular binomial. Its exponent is nonzero, and the binomial is
nonzero at a parameter exactly when the test holds there.

Multiply these binomials and use the Library's unitary pole
factorization. Every parameter where the product is nonzero
satisfies the separation criterion for `PQ`-regularity of the
represented ordered pair. The scalar and its factorization are
independent of the compact model and of a numerator or denominator
chosen for a continued operator.

This derived construction retains the valuation data of the
geometric-lemma separation argument. It provides the factored
controlling scalar for the pole support step in Waldspurger (2003),
IV.1(13) and V.3.1, printed pp. 283 and 295.
