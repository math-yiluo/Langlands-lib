# Normalized models over the concrete unramified regular algebra

Worker 1585. Derived construction over the published interfaces in
`Plancherel.UnramifiedUniversalTwist`, `Aubert.General.Parabolic`,
`Induction.Definitions`, and `Jacquet.Localization`. It implements the
coefficient-ring aspect of Waldspurger 2003, I.5, printed p.245, and IV.1,
printed p.277 (registered original `waldspurger-2003-plancherel`). It makes
no admissibility or finiteness claim from those passages.

For the entire supplied family `ν : I → (L →* ℝ≥0ˣ)`, write
`B = unramifiedRegularFunctions ν`. This is the actual subalgebra of
functions on all actual unramified characters, generated by evaluation.
`evaluation ν χ` is its ring homomorphism `b ↦ b(χ)`.
`constantCharacter` maps a complex unit-valued character into `Bˣ` via
`Units.map (algebraMap ℂ B)`. Its evaluation is the original complex
character. `constantCharacter_ker` proves equality with the original kernel:
apply evaluation at the trivial unramified character for one implication,
and preservation of one for the other. Thus `constantSmoothCharacter`
retains an open kernel without needing a topology on B.

Assume G is locally compact and nonarchimedean, P, M, U are actual
subgroups, U is closed, and `h : IsSemidirectDecomposition P M U`.
`inductionCharacter` is the constant image of the actual positive
`HaarModulus.closedHalfCharacter U hU P h.le_normalizer` on the full P.
`jacquetCharacter` is the constant image of the inverse character on M,
`HaarModulus.closedInverseHalfCharacter U hU M h.levi_le_normalizer`.
Their open kernels are certified by the existing Haar character theorems
and the constant-character construction. No replacement of the full
P character by an inflated Levi character is made.

`inducingRepresentation` inflates any B-linear M-action along the actual
projection h.projection and twists by the positive full P character.
`Induced` is the entire existing smooth function space for this action,
not a compact-support subspace. `inducedRepresentation` is right
translation. `induced_covariant` is the defining covariance formula;
`induced_action` is the defining translation formula. Its smoothness follows
from the existing smooth-part construction, with one open right stabilizer
for the entire function. `normalizedInductionFunctor` bundles these
operations; its identity and composition laws are those of the composed
existing functors.

`Jacquet` is literally `Jacquet.Localization.Coinvariants ρ U 1`: the
quotient by the B-span of `ρ(u)v - v`. `jacquetRepresentation` twists the
descended normalizing-subgroup action by the inverse Levi half-character.
`jacquet_action_mk` exposes `m[v] = δ(m)^(-1/2)[ρ(m)v]`.
`isSmooth_jacquetRepresentation` uses the existing surjective quotient
smoothness theorem and preservation of smoothness under an open-kernel
twist. `normalizedJacquetFunctor` bundles the quotient and inverse twist;
its identity and composition laws follow from the existing functors.

The norm-family, acting-group and representation-carrier universes are
independent. The generic model uses a separate norm group L, so it also
applies when L is the actual Levi M. No splitness, characteristic,
finite-dimensionality, continuity of the semidirect projection, finite-type
or Noetherian hypothesis is needed. Continuity of that projection is not
needed for full smooth induction, whose smooth vectors are selected after
forming the covariant function module. This module asserts no fiber or
scalar-extension isomorphism, geometric lemma, filtration, exponent
separation, continuation or rationality result.
