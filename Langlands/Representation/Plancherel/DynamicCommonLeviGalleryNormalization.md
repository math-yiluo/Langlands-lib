# Normalization of the actual continued endpoint maps on the gallery

The actual common-Levi standard integral at the identity uses the
quotient of the target radical by its intersection with the source
radical. The quotient chart transfers its weak integral to the
represented opposite complement with the positive scale provided by
`galleryQuotientChart`. The compact test continuation theorem then
retains the formula for every algebraic linear functional at every
parameter where the continued denominator is nonzero.

A represented section satisfying the boundary-vanishing condition has
compact support upon restriction to the opposite complement. The
evaluation-preserving induction equivalences therefore give the
represented integral normalization for the actual continued map. At
every parameter where the two vertices are `PQ`-regular, uniqueness at
the scaled Haar measure identifies this map with the fixed gallery
operator multiplied by the positive chart scale. The scale uses the
ordered pair and compact open subgroup and is independent of the
parameter and inducing representation.

This comparison connects the Library's two normalizations of the
standard integral for the common-Levi specialization of Waldspurger
(2003), IV.1.1 and V.3.1, printed pp. 278 and 295.
