# A normalized test average on the actual common-Levi group

For closed radical subgroups \(U,V\), `intersectionSwap` identifies the
two presentations of \(U\cap V\) by their ambient elements. Both Haar
measures give mass one to the inverse image of the original compact open
subgroup. The identification therefore preserves these measures and gives
the same Levi and intersection average in both presentations.

`normalizedTestAverage` scales the common average by the inverse of its
value at the identity. For the actual dynamic parabolics with a common
centralizer and a compact open test subgroup \(Q\), this identity value
is positive and finite. The coordinate preimage is open and contains the
identity. It is compact because the intersection coordinates embed as a
closed subset of the full Levi and radical coordinates, where the
parabolic coordinate homeomorphism identifies the preimage with \(P'\cap Q\).
The normalized function is one at the identity and vanishes outside
the product of the Levi, the intersection and \(Q\). On that product,
for a connected reductive ambient group, it equals the Haar character
of the intersection conjugation by the Levi factor.

This is an internal compact test normalization for the adjunction in
Waldspurger's IV.1(11).
