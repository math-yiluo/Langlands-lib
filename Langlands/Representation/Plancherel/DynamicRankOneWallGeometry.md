# Parabolic extension data at a dynamic rank-one wall

The wall records an outer parabolic and two opposite parabolics in its Levi. The rational Levi witnesses supply a radical and the multiplication isomorphism for each of these three parabolics.

Each gallery vertex is the extension of the corresponding parabolic in the wall Levi. The rational point intersections follow by taking the point image of each scheme-level comap. The same ambient Levi homomorphism is retained in the extension data used by induction in stages.
