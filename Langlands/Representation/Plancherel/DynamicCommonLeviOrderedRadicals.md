# Ordered charts for distance-additive dynamic radicals

The two-factor chart specializes the published product homeomorphism for
common-Levi unipotent radicals to the order of `OrderedSubgroupFactorization`.
Each chart evaluates by literal multiplication in the ambient rational-point
group.

For three cocharacters with the same centralizer, decompose a point of
`U₁ ∩ Ū₀` in the chart on `Ū₀` as `c d` with `c ∈ U₂ ∩ Ū₀` and
`d ∈ Ū₂ ∩ Ū₀`. The value of the middle cocharacter at the contracting unit
lies in every pertinent centralizer. Its powers therefore normalize both
chart factors. Conjugating the point contracts it to the identity, so
continuity of the second chart coordinate gives contraction of `d`.
The dynamic contraction criterion puts `d` in `U₁`. The opposite inclusion
in the distance condition also puts it in `Ū₁`, whose intersection with
`U₁` is trivial. It follows that the original point belongs to `U₂`.

The positive inclusion in the distance condition places `U₀ ∩ U₂` inside
`U₁`. The first chart restricts to `U₁ ∩ U₂` because its second factor
already belongs to `U₂`, so the product belongs to `U₂` exactly when its
first factor does. Composing the restricted chart with the second chart
gives `(U₀ ∩ U₂) × (V × W) ≃ₜ U₂` by ordered multiplication, where
`V = U₁ ∩ Ū₀` and `W = U₂ ∩ Ū₁`. The construction packages all three
homeomorphisms and subgroup inclusions used by the composition theorem.

This structural argument uses the Library's open-cell product charts and
contraction criterion for the common-Levi composition route.
