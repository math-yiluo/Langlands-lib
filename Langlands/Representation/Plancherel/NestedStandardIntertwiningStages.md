# The normalized stages restriction on standard source models

This derived construction uses normalized induction in stages with its
supplied semidirect decompositions, half-character identity and continuous
outer Levi projection. It evaluates the outer induced function at a chosen
ambient group element. The ambient and inner models are converted from full
parabolic normalization to the actual Levi-inflated normalization by the
existing function-preserving equivalences. The resulting linear map has
the evaluation formula needed for `NestedRestrictionFormula`.

At the identity the map is surjective. The inner normalization equivalence
first gives an inner full-parabolic function. The evaluation theorem lifts
it through the outer smooth induction, using continuity of the outer
projection. The inverse stages and ambient normalization equivalences
then supply the desired preimage.

For a target radical contained in the outer parabolic, the outer
half-character takes value one on its intersection with the intermediate
Levi if the target radical is a limit of compact subgroups. This applies
the established half-character identity on compact subgroups to the
section of the inner target radical. It supplies exactly the character
condition used in the weak-integral comparison.
