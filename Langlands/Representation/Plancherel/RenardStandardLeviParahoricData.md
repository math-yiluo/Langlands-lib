# Parahoric data on the same restricted standard-Levi apartment

This internal derived construction supplies `standardLeviParahoricData` for every actual standard Levi of the ambient `ParahoricBuildingData`, with a generic cocharacter and a subset of its simple roots. The apartment `DM` is prescribed. The restriction hypothesis says that every point of `DM` is a literal root-valuation restriction of some ambient apartment point, in the published `IsLeviRestriction` predicate. No building axioms are assumed for `DM`.

The output is `ParahoricBuildingData` for `Standard.leviScheme B.i ν J`, with torus exactly `B.S`, inclusion exactly `Standard.leviTorusHom B.i ν J`, and apartment exactly `DM`. Its centralizer is `standardLeviCentralizerDatum`: the original ambient centralizer scheme, its original torus map, and its original valuation, with the canonical factor into the actual Levi. The published `isOmegaCompatible_standardLevi_of_restriction` transfers full centralizer compatibility. The output's zero datum reuses the ambient `zeroDatum.centralizerDatum` literally, so the subgroup of centralizer points `Z(F)^0` is unchanged, as the corresponding reflexivity theorem records.

For the remaining zero-datum fields the proof uses the connected reductivity conclusion of `standardLeviSplitComponentStatement` and chooses a simply connected derived cover of this actual Levi using `simplyConnectedDerivedCoverExistence`. These are genuine published existence theorems; no cover or reductivity proposition is added as a premise. Only these Levi derived-cover choices are new. The output contains no integral models or assignment.

This packages the algebraic and valuation inputs required to apply building results to the restricted apartment. It does not prove that the output satisfies apartment or building axioms, that a point is special, or that its compact subgroup equals the preimage of the fixed ambient compact subgroup. Those remain separate inheritance and comparison obligations in the Renard target. The construction makes no new original-source assertion and derives all fields from the named published results.
