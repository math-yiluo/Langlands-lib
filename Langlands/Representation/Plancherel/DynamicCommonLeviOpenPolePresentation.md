# Pole presentations on open common-Levi convergence regions

`pairContinuationWithPolesOfOpenSubset` concerns two actual dynamic parabolics of a connected reductive group over a nonarchimedean local field. It takes their common Levi, a split component of its parameter scheme, the compact Iwasawa factorizations, squared-modulus compatibility, and an irreducible admissible inducing representation. The parameter set is a nonempty open subset of the full convergence domain for `pairFamily`, with its compact-normalized quotient measure. The field and coefficient-carrier universes are independent.

`SmoothRep.hasFiniteLength_of_isIrreducible` supplies finite length. The open-subset rationality result `pairCompactRationalOn_of_isOpen_subset` and `rationalOperatorContinuation` construct a continuation whose denominator never vanishes on the given region. For the alternative denominator, `galleryPQDiscriminant` gives a scalar with a unitary pole factorization. Its nonzero locus gives `representedIsPQRegular` at the intrinsic twisted coefficient, using `intrinsicGalleryCoefficient_twist`. In that locus, `compactPairNumerator_eq_zero_of_PQRegular` makes every cleared numerator vanish on the zero fiber of its denominator. `RationalOperatorPolePresentation.ofClearedZeroFibers` supplies the alternative presentation with its global cross identity.

The construction gives the form of the rationality and pole support used in Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les groupes p-adiques, d’après Harish-Chandra*, IV.1, printed pp. 278–284, and the proof of V.2.3, printed p. 294. The source is registered as `waldspurger-2003-plancherel`, SHA-256 `4b6a6162cd0b3863703fb2f65d1698f926caf813114917f2084c16e38f2e30f7`. The proof uses exponent separation and the whole-operator zero-fiber argument to express the source’s pole support in the integral valuation lattice.
