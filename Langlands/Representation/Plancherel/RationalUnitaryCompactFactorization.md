# Transfer through a compact factorization

The estimate of a polynomial numerator with a single denominator passes
through an isomorphism of parameter schemes, a polynomial input map, and
a fixed linear output map. A cleared factorization on a nonempty ambient
open set gives a cross identity with the given continuation. Polynomial
uniqueness extends it, and cancellation on a dense unitary set then gives
the bound for the given continuation at every unitary point.

This is the compact-coordinate and continuation argument of the proof of
Corollary V.2.3 in Waldspurger, printed p. 294, where the induced spaces are
identified with compact pictures whose Hermitian norms do not depend on the
unitary parameter, and the norm identity of Lemma V.2.2, printed p. 293,
is used. Source `waldspurger-2003-plancherel`, SHA-256
