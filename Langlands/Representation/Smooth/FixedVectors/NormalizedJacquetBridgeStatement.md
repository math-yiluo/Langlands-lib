# The actual normalized Jacquet bridge for fixed-vector detection

Worker 189. The internal proposition
`Langlands.NormalizedJacquetFixedVectorBridgeStatement.{uG,uV}` connects
fixed-vector detection to the Library's actual normalized Jacquet functor.
The group and representation-carrier universes are independent.

The ambient group is locally compact and nonarchimedean. Its actual subgroups
`N`, `M`, and `Nbar` are inputs; `N` and `M` are closed and `M` normalizes `N`.
Every finite subset of `N` lies in a compact open subgroup of `N`. A compact
open subgroup `K` of the ambient group and a subgroup `K₀` satisfy that `K₀`
normalizes `K` and every ambient element factors in the order `M N K₀`.
An actual Iwahori contraction for `K`, `N`, `M`, and `Nbar` is supplied.

For smooth complex representations `A` and `D`, assume that `A^K` is finite
dimensional, that `A` is generated by `A^K`, and that `D` is an actual
subquotient of `A`. Generation means that each invariant submodule containing
the indicated fixed-vector submodule is the whole representation. Subquotient
means an actual invariant submodule, its actual quotient, and an isomorphism.

Write `JA` and `JD` for the images of `A` and `D` under
`Aubert.General.normalizedJacquetFunctor N M hN hMN`. This functor uses the
actual trivial-character localization quotient and twists the descended
`M`-action by the inverse positive square root of the Haar modulus of
conjugation on `N`. The proposition asserts four conclusions:

1. The actual normalizing character is trivial on `K ∩ M`, regarded as a
   subgroup of `M`.
2. `JA` is generated by its `K ∩ M`-fixed vectors.
3. `JD` is an actual subquotient of `JA`.
4. A nonzero `K ∩ M`-fixed vector in `JD` yields a nonzero `K`-fixed vector
   in `D`.

This is derived implementation content for the Jacquet step in Renard VI.6.3,
printed pages 178–179 / PDF184–185, French version dated 9 July 2026,

It composes the published unnormalized generation and weak Jacquet results
with the published localization comparison and character-twist operations.
Compactness of `K ∩ M` and the open kernel of the positive Haar character
supply the first conclusion; no rational-character identification is presumed.

The proposition supplies neither the structural data on reductive rational
points nor a nonzero fixed vector in an arbitrary normalized constituent.
The finite compact-restriction input, detection, finite-length deduction,
and the final Howe implication retain their separate obligations. The
statement does not untwist cuspidality or compact coefficients.
