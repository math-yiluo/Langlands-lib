# Inheritance of semisimple fixed-vector generation

This module states three internal algebraic inheritance properties as
`Prop`-valued definitions. It supplies statement targets, not proofs.
Worker 196 authored the mathematical interface and complete parent proof
draft; Worker 213 extracted the statements and their conjunction.

For a representation `rho` over a field `R` and a subgroup `K` of `G`,
generation by `K`-fixed vectors means that every invariant subspace `S`
with `fixedVectors rho K ≤ S.toSubmodule` equals the whole representation.
Here `fixedVectors` is the existing submodule of vectors fixed by every
element of `K`. Semisimplicity is Mathlib's
`Representation.IsSemisimpleRepresentation`: every subrepresentation has
an invariant complement.

## Individual statements

1. `Langlands.SemisimpleFixedVectorsGenerateOfSurjectiveStatement`:
   for every field `R`, group `G`, `R`-modules `V` and `W`,
   representations `rho` on `V` and `sigma` on `W`, subgroup `K`, and
   surjective intertwiner `f : rho.IntertwiningMap sigma`, if `rho` is
   semisimple and generated by its `K`-fixed vectors, then `sigma` is
   semisimple and generated by its `K`-fixed vectors. Surjectivity is
   assumed for the underlying map on the entire representation; no
   surjectivity on fixed vectors is assumed.

2. `Langlands.SemisimpleFixedVectorsGenerateOfInjectiveStatement`:
   for the same kinds of data, if `f : rho.IntertwiningMap sigma` is
   injective and `sigma` is semisimple and generated by its `K`-fixed
   vectors, then `rho` is semisimple and generated by its `K`-fixed
   vectors.

3. `Langlands.SmoothRep.SemisimpleFixedVectorsGenerateSubquotientStatement`:
   let `R` be any field and `G` any group equipped with a topology.
   Let `A` and `B` be smooth `G`-representations over `R`, and let `H`
   and `K` be subgroups with `K ≤ H`. Suppose the restriction of `A`
   to `H` is semisimple, and every `H`-invariant subspace containing
   the ambient subspace `fixedVectors A.representation K` is the whole
   representation. If `SmoothRep.IsSubquotient B A`, then the restriction
   of `B` to `H` is semisimple, and every `H`-invariant subspace containing
   `fixedVectors B.representation K` is the whole representation.
   The relation is the existing actual subquotient relation: `B` is
   isomorphic to the quotient of an invariant subrepresentation of `A`
   by one of its invariant subrepresentations. The same `H` and `K`
   appear in the hypotheses and conclusions.

`Langlands.SemisimpleFixedVectorsGenerationInheritanceStatement` is the
conjunction of these three statements, in the order above. It has independent
universe parameters `uR`, `uG`, `uV`, and `uW`. The first two clauses allow
independent source and target carrier universes `uV` and `uW`. The third uses
the same carrier universe `uV` for `A` and `B`, as required by the existing
actual subquotient relation; it remains independent of the coefficient and
group universes.

The first two clauses require no topology or smoothness. In the third,
the group topology supplies the meaning of smoothness; continuity of the
group operations is not assumed. None of the clauses assumes subgroup
normality, openness, compactness, admissibility, finite dimensionality,
finite length, or irreducibility.

## Internal provenance and proof boundary

These statements are internal algebraic consequences, not translations of
a separately cited literature theorem. Their interface is extracted from
Worker 196's shared parent draft, revision 9, module
`Langlands.Representation.Smooth.FixedVectors.GenerationInheritance`,
whose lemmas are `semisimple_fixedVectors_generate_of_surjective`,
`semisimple_fixedVectors_generate_of_injective`, and
`SmoothRep.semisimple_fixedVectors_generate_subquotient`.
The statement definitions universally quantify the data of those lemmas
without adding hypotheses or changing their conclusions.

The algebraic rationale is that semisimple group-algebra modules admit
equivariant splittings of injections, while equivariant surjections transport
generators. The subquotient clause applies the injection and surjection
properties after restriction to `H`. Identifying the restricted fixed space
for `K.comap H.subtype` with the ambient `K`-fixed space uses `K ≤ H`.
This explains the provenance and intended proof route; it is not a proof
publication.

Only the published smooth-representation definitions, actual subquotient
constructions, and Mathlib semisimplicity interface are imported.
The draft proof uses the elementary helper `fixedVectors_comp_subtype` from
the separate FiniteTypes development. This statement module does not import
that draft or assume its publication. A later proof contribution must resolve
that helper dependency and prove the full conjunction against this target.
These statements alone establish no cuspidal constituent theorem.
