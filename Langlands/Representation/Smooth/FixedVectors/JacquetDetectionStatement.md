# Detecting fixed vectors through actual Jacquet subquotients

Worker 189. `Langlands.JacquetSubquotientFixedVectorDetectionStatement`
is an internal proposition for the fixed-vector argument in Howe's
finite-length implication. The group, representation-carrier and finite-family
index universes are independent.

Let `N`, `M`, and `Nbar` be subgroups of a topological group `G`, with `M`
normalizing `N` and closed in `G`. Every finite subset of `N` must lie in
a compact open subgroup of `N`. Fix a compact open subgroup `K`, a subgroup
`K₀` normalizing `K`, the factorization `G = M N K₀`, and an actual
`IsIwahoriContraction K N M Nbar g` witness.

Let `H` be a normal subgroup of `M` containing `K ∩ M`, with Hausdorff,
locally compact, totally disconnected topology, its Borel measurable structure
and a supplied Haar measure. Suppose `A` is generated by its `K`-fixed
vectors and that space is finite dimensional. Let `D` be an actual smooth
subquotient of `A`. The object `JD` in the statement is the actual
unnormalized `N`-coinvariants of `D`, with its descended smooth `M`-action;
write `JA` for the same construction on `A`.

If an actual smooth subquotient `E` of `JD` restricts to `H` as the span
of a nonempty finite family of compact irreducible subrepresentations, then
`D` has a nonzero `K`-fixed vector. Compactness here is the predicate
`HasCompactSmoothDualCoefficients` on each actual subrepresentation.
Neither `D` nor `E` is assumed irreducible, admissible or of finite length.

The intended deduction is as follows. Compact containment in `N` makes
coinvariants preserve the injection in a subquotient presentation of `D`
inside `A`; right exactness preserves its surjection. Thus `JD` is a
subquotient of `JA`, and transitivity makes `E` one too. The factorization
and normalizer conditions transfer `K`-fixed generation of `A` to
`K ∩ M`-fixed generation of `JA`. Finite compact restriction then detects
a nonzero `K ∩ M`-fixed vector in `E`. Compact-open exactness lifts
nonvanishing to `JD`; closedness of `M` makes `K ∩ M` compact. The same
exactness and the injective part of the original presentation show that
`D^K` is finite dimensional. The actual weak Jacquet lemma then lifts
nonvanishing from `JD` to `D`.

This is derived from the proof of Renard VI.6.3, printed pages 178–179 /
PDF184–185, French version dated 9 July 2026, registered as
`renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, SHA256
`7b539996793b7c3620e72e1e389588eb42d1a8e91d6f68b2eead5c2e2ffc182c`.
The formulation isolates its algebraic composition using the existing actual
coinvariants and subquotient relation. Realizing the same geometric `K₀`,
the factorization, contraction and finite compact restriction for reductive
rational points remains separate work. This statement neither asserts those
realizations nor proves Howe's theorem.
