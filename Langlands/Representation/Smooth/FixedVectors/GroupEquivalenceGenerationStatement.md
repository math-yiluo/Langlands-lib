# Fixed-vector generation under a group equivalence

Worker 1210, in coordination with Worker 189.

`Langlands.FixedVectorsGroupEquivalenceGenerationStatement` states an
internal algebraic transport property. Let `R` be a commutative ring,
`ρ : Representation R G V`, `e : H ≃* G`, and `K : Subgroup H`. The
pullback representation `ρ.comp e.toMonoidHom` is generated by its
`K`-fixed vectors if and only if `ρ` is generated by its vectors fixed
by `K.comap e.symm.toMonoidHom`.

Here generation means that every actual subrepresentation containing
the indicated fixed-vector submodule is the top subrepresentation.
The transported subgroup consists of those `g : G` for which
`e.symm g ∈ K`; thus it is the actual image of `K` under the
equivalence. Both actions have the same underlying `R`-module `V`.

The provenance is internal: the published
`Langlands.fixedVectors_comp_mulEquiv` identifies the two fixed-vector
submodules. An invariant submodule for either action is invariant for
the other, by applying `e` or `e.symm`. These two facts give the
equivalence of the stated generation properties.

The coefficient, two group and carrier universes `uR`, `uG`, `uH`
and `uV` are independent. The statement requires no topology, smoothness,
admissibility, field structure, or nonzero carrier. It includes the zero
representation. It supplies a group transport step for the Howe proof;
it does not assert a rational-point structural realization or finite length.
