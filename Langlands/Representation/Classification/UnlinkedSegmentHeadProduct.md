# Highest derivatives of constituents of unlinked special-head products

Author: Worker 357.

The selected internal definition is
`Langlands.Classification.chooseUnlinkedSegmentHeadProductHighestDerivativeOrder`.
For every irreducible actual subquotient of an unlinked product of supplied
special heads, it selects highest positive derivative data and retains an
equality between its order and the ambient rank. Two grouped lemmas extract
top-derivative nonvanishing and irreducibility of the unlinked product.
The full `SegmentHeadProductIrreducibility` equivalence still requires the
linked converse.

## Parameters and returned data

Fix arbitrary `p : ℕ` with `Fact p.Prime`, a supplied
`F : PadicField.{uF} p`, its supplied topological data `T`, and a supplied
continuous nontrivial unitary additive character `ψ` for `T.topology`.
The field and base carrier universes `uF,uV` are independent.
For arbitrary `n : ℕ`, take
`d : T.SegmentProductDatum.{uV,uF} (n+1)`, supplied heads
`hd : d.HasSegmentHeads`, and `hunlinked : d.multisegment.IsUnlinked`.
Let `Q` be an irreducible actual smooth complex representation of
`GL_(n+1)(F)` with `SmoothRep.IsSubquotient Q d.induced`.
The returned type is exactly

```text
{w : GeneralLinearHighestDerivativeOrderData T ψ Q // w.order = n + 1}.
```

Thus it retains a natural-number order `k`, its feasibility `k ≤ n+1`,
positivity, nontriviality of the actual order-`k` derivative of `Q`,
and vanishing of every higher feasible derivative, together with `k=n+1`.
The derivative is the actual published `generalLinearDerivative`; no formal
symbolic derivative or Grothendieck-group surrogate is used.

Segments and their supercuspidal bases retain the original `uV`.
The induced representation, `Q`, and derivative carriers lie in
`Type (max uF uV)`. The construction preserves the original factor labels,
order and repetitions. Unlinkage allows repeated segments because a segment
is not linked to itself. `HasSegmentHeads` refers to the special quotient
of the increasing segment product, not its segment socle.
There is no precedence-ordering or additional admissibility hypothesis.
The ambient rank is positive, including rank one. A proper highest order
has positive residual rank; the top order, with residual rank zero, is
retained without invoking positive-rank support uniqueness there.

Irreducibility makes `Q` nontrivial. The general highest-order selector
provides `w`, with positive order `k`. Suppose `k` is not the whole rank.
Its feasibility then implies `0 < n+1-k`.
Irreducibility also makes `Q` finite length. The general derivative
finite-length theorem therefore makes its order-`k` derivative finite length.

The published untwisted quotient constructor applies to that nonzero
finite-length highest derivative. It supplies an actual nonzero irreducible
admissible invariant subspace `S` of `D_k(Q)`, and an actual surjection

```text
D_k(Q.contragredient) → E,
E = S.contragredient twisted by ν^(-1).
```

Here `ν` is the determinant absolute-value character in residual rank.
The constructor also supplies irreducibility of `E` and an actual
isomorphism `E.contragredient ≅ ν S`; the required compact-open cofinality
comes from the general theorem for the supplied actual p-adic general
linear group.

The inclusion of `S` into `D_k(Q)`, with the identity quotient map, gives
the actual occurrence `SmoothRep.IsSubquotient S (D_k(Q))`.
The identity inclusion of `D_k(Q.contragredient)`, followed by the retained
surjection, gives the actual occurrence of `E` in that derivative.
These are instances of the generic injective/surjective subquotient
construction, with constituent first.

These occurrences and the actual target-dual isomorphism satisfy every
premise of `chooseSegmentHeadProductShiftedSupportData`.
Its support multisets retain multiplicities before projection to their
underlying unions. The positive residual rank makes the shifted union
nonempty. Its finite truncation obstruction supplies original indices
`i,j` whose segments are linked. Both segments belong to
`d.multisegment`, which is the multiset of `List.ofFn d.segments`.
Applying `hunlinked` contradicts that linkage. Hence `w.order=n+1`,
as retained in the returned subtype.

The grouped theorem
`unlinkedSegmentHeadProduct_topDerivativeNonvanishing` applies this
constructor to every irreducible actual subquotient `Q` of `d.induced`.
It destructures the retained order data and substitutes `k=n+1`, yielding
nontriviality of its actual top derivative. Its conclusion is exactly
`ExactFunctorIrreducibility.NonvanishingOnIrreducibleSubquotients`
for the whole top-derivative functor.

Finally `unlinkedSegmentHeadProduct_isIrreducible` applies the published
`segmentHeadProductIsIrreducible_of_topDerivativeNonvanishing` to that
conclusion and the supplied heads. The additive character remains an
explicit parameter even though it occurs only in this proof.
This yields the sufficient direction for every finite product in the
original universe convention; it does not establish the linked converse.
