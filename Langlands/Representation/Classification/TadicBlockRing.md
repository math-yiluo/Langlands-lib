# The Grothendieck ring `R` for Tadić's Lemma 4.8(ii)

Author: Worker 5121. Module `Langlands.Representation.Classification.TadicBlockRing`,
namespace `Langlands.Classification.TadicBlockRing`. Helper module of the proof of
`Langlands.Classification.TadicBlockReductionFromInputs`; it adds no axiom and no
hypothesis-free claim beyond the theorems listed.

## Setting

`ringR F T` is the published commutative ring `Aubert.gradedInductionRing F T hx hl` on
`Aubert.GradedGrothendieckGroup F T`, with `hx := Aubert.binaryProductExactness F T` and
`hl := Aubert.gradedInductionRingLaws F T hx` (both published proofs). It is installed as a
local instance. `cls F T π hπ` is the class `gradedInclusion F T N (finiteLengthClass N π hπ)`
of a finite-length representation of `GL_N`. The module uses the published coefficient and
label vocabulary of `Aubert/SegmentPolynomialSurjectivity`: `basisClass`, `coeff`,
`IsEffective`, `zeta`, `IsLabel`, `labelWeight`, `weightedCount`.

## Contents and proofs

- **Effective classes.** `mem_basisSpan_of_isEffective` writes an effective class
  (nonnegative coefficients) as a sum of basis classes using `eq_sum_basisClass`;
  `isEffective_basisClass_mul` shows a product of two basis classes is the class of an actual
  binary product (`gradedMultiplication_homogeneous`, `binaryProductK0_classes`); hence
  `isEffective_mul`: products of effective classes are effective (closure induction).
- **Rank order.** `rankLE_add` and `rankLE_of_add_right`: the published order
  `Multisegment.RankLE` (equal cuspidal content, containment counts reversed) is compatible
  with addition and cancellable, since content and `containmentCount` are additive.
- **Labels.** With a fixed unitary additive character `ψ₀` (from the published existence
  theorem) and `zetaR a := zeta F T hx hl a`, the published triangularity
  `rankLE_of_coeff_zeta_pos` and multiplicity bound `labelCount_zeta_le_one` give:
  `eq_of_isLabel` (a label determines its multisegment, via `RankLE.antisymm`; this is the
  injectivity of `a ↦ ⟨a⟩`), `coeff_zetaR_sub_basisClass_eq_zero` and `label_unique` (a
  multisegment has one label, occurring once in `ζ(a)`). `isLabel_of_isZelevinskySocle` and
  `exists_isZelevinskySocle` (from the published `multisegmentConstituentDataExistence` and
  `orderedMultisegmentSocleExistence`) connect labels with Zelevinsky socles.
- **Zelevinsky 1980, Proposition 8.4.** `spanIn S` is generated by basis classes of labels of
  multisegments in `S`. `rankLE_of_coeff_mul_pos`: a label of `e` occurring in `u v` with
  `u ∈ spanIn S₁`, `v ∈ spanIn S₂` satisfies `e ≼ b₁ + b₂` for some `b_i ∈ S_i`
  (`[c₁][c₂] ≤ ζ(b₁ + b₂)` by `isEffective_zetaR_add_sub_mul`, then triangularity).
  `zetaR_sub_mem_spanIn`: `ζ(a) - [⟨a⟩]` only contains labels of `b ≺ a`.
  `one_le_coeff_basisClass_mul`: expanding `ζ(a + b) = ζ(a) ζ(b)` with
  `ζ(a) = [⟨a⟩] + E_a`, the label of `a + b` has coefficient `0` in `[⟨a⟩] E_b`, `E_a [⟨b⟩]`,
  `E_a E_b` (rank order additivity and antisymmetry), so it occurs in `[⟨a⟩][⟨b⟩]`.
  `one_le_coeff_prod` extends this to finite products by induction.
- **Classes of products.** `hasFiniteLength_product` (published
  `irreducibleProductFiniteLength`, empty product unit), `nonempty_iso_binary` (head/tail
  splitting of a normalized product via `headTailProductInductionIso`), and `cls_product`:
  the class of a normalized product of irreducible blocks over any block shape is the
  product of their classes (induction on the number of blocks, single-block and empty cases
  separately).
- **Irreducible classes.** `cls_eq_basisClass`, `eq_of_basisClass_eq`,
  `nonempty_iso_of_cls_eq` (irreducible representations with equal classes are isomorphic),
  and `isZelevinskySocle_of_cls_eq_prod`: an irreducible representation whose class is a
  product of label classes of `a_i` is a Zelevinsky socle of `∑ a_i`.
- **Factorization in `R`.** `exists_le_associated_of_dvd_prod` (a divisor of a product of
  primes in a cancellative monoid is associated to a sub-product, by induction),
  `segmentEquiv` (the published `segmentPolynomialEquiv` from `segmentPolynomialComparison`,
  Zelevinsky 1980, Corollary 7.5), `isDomain_R`, `isUnit_R` (units of `R` are `±1`, from
  `MvPolynomial.isUnit_iff_eq_C_of_isReduced` and `Int.isUnit_iff`) and
  `eq_or_eq_neg_of_associated`.

## Sources

Zelevinsky 1980 (`zelevinsky-1980-induced-ii`), Theorem 7.1, Corollary 7.5 and Proposition
8.4 (printed pp.191–196); the implemented proofs use only the published Library
triangularity and polynomial comparison, not Zelevinsky's own proof of 8.4.

The common ring definitions (`ringR`, `ψ₀`, `zetaR`, `segmentEquiv`,
`basisSpan`) and matching effectiveness, coefficient and label lemmas are
reexported from `TadicRectanglePrimeRing`. Their definitions and types agree
with the original helpers; the product-occurrence argument and block-specific
constructions remain in this module.
