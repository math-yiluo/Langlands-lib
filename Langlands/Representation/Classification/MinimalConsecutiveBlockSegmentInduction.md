# Segment factors from minimal consecutive cuspidal blocks

Author: Worker 320.

This internal composition starts with an actual irreducible admissible
finite-length representation embedded in an outer normalized product of
inner cuspidal inductions. Each inner family has positive length and
consecutive decreasing integer labels on a fixed positive-rank actual
supercuspidal twist line. The concatenated reference word has no smaller
realized permutation in the ambient representation.

Factorized inducing-subquotient lifting supplies irreducible admissible
outer factors, their actual occurrences in the inner inductions, a quotient
of the whole coarse Jacquet module onto their tensor, and the retained actual
outer induction embedding. Consecutive decreasing lists are weakly decreasing.
The minimal-block theorem therefore identifies the original consecutive word
as an actual word of each outer factor. The arbitrary-rank consecutive-word
theorem identifies that factor as the strong head of the explicit segment
whose lowest exponent is the original top label minus (the length minus one).
Its degree equality is retained in the actual representation transport.

The conclusion preserves the whole Jacquet quotient, the induction embedding,
and each factor's irreducibility and admissibility. All representations are
actual smooth complex objects over the supplied field and topology, with
independent field and carrier universes and saturated carriers. The head
relation retains equality of all irreducible quotient kernels.

This helper assumes the coarse decomposition and its embedding. It does not
construct a decomposition of an arbitrary realized word into ordered runs,
commute segment factors, or prove analytic square-integrability or its
classification.
