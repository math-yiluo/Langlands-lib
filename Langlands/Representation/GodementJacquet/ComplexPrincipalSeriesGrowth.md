# Height bounds for complex principal series vectors

Every smooth principal series vector with the supplied polar inducing characters has polynomial growth in Jacquet's matrix height. In a diagonal, upper-unipotent, unitary factorization its value is the compact value times the principal series character. The two triangular factors have reciprocal diagonal entries on taking the inverse. The height bounds both norms of each diagonal entry and their inverses, and compactness bounds the change from the triangular height to the full height.

The angular powers have norm one. The real exponent in coordinate \(i\) is \(2\operatorname{Re}(t_i)+r-2i\), with the zero-based index \(i\). Bounding the corresponding power by the ceiling of its absolute exponent gives a single polynomial, with total degree the sum of those ceilings.
