# Affine Fourier inversion after partitioning matrix coordinates

A predicate partitions a finite coordinate set into two disjoint blocks. The resulting linear coordinate equivalence preserves the self-dual product measure. Summation over the partition decomposes the trace pairing, so the coordinate Fourier transform becomes the two-block transform.

The affine inversion identity is thereby available in the original coordinate space for any partition. For rectangular complex matrices the uncurrying map identifies the entrywise Fourier transform with the transform on the product of row and column indices.
