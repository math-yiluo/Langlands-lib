# Left unitary averages and the complex matrix Fourier transform

The inverse left translation average of a matrix Schwartz test is Schwartz. The construction transposes the matrices and moves the compact measure and weight through the unitary inverse-transpose involution, reducing to the right averaging operation.

In the Fourier transform the translated frequency is multiplied on the left by the inverse transpose of the same inverse unitary matrix. The proof uses the Fourier transpose identity, right averaging covariance, and the involution on the compact group.
