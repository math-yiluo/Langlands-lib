is the closed internal proposition that the Tate matrix functional-equation
statement and the higher-rank supercuspidal matrix functional-equation
statement, at the same independently chosen field and representation-carrier
universes, imply the unchanged generic irreducible matrix functional-equation
statement.

The Tate premise covers actual rank-one irreducible admissible complex
representations. The supercuspidal premise covers actual rank at least two
irreducible supercuspidal complex representations, with both denominator
polynomials equal to one. Both premises are the existing closed propositions;
they are not weakened to scalar equations or to a chosen representation model.
The conclusion is exactly the existing `GenericMatrixFunctionalEquationStatement`.
Thus every prime, canonical p-adic field, supplied additive character and
self-dual scalar measure, positive rank, arbitrary independent-universe actual

`NormalizedMatrixFunctionalEquationData` are inherited without modification.

This is a conditional reduction statement. It does not prove either analytic
base, assert that the bases are available, or establish the unconditional
generic irreducible theorem. The original generic statement is preserved and
remains the host task's exact target.

The provenance is internal and derived. The intended reduction uses the
published arbitrary-carrier supercuspidal-product embedding and the admissible
Schur central character, restricts that character to scalar matrices, applies
finite-product closure of full matrix data, and descends along the actual image
of the embedding. Its image is a genuine subrepresentation and is equivalent
to the original source. These structural steps explain the implication; their
proof and the finite-product and descent inputs require their own validation
and independent review. No additional literature claim is introduced here.
