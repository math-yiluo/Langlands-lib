# Inversion of the triangular Haar factors

Right translation on the upper unipotent group acts on a strict upper
entry by adding a correction that depends only on entries of smaller
column-minus-row distance. The finite triangular translation theorem
then gives right invariance of the product of additive Haar measures.
Together with its left invariance this gives invariance under inversion.
The diagonal group is commutative, so its Haar measure is also invariant
under inversion.

The inverse transpose on the full matrix group is a continuous
involutive automorphism. Its positive Haar character has square one,
so the determinant-weighted matrix Haar measure is invariant under it.
