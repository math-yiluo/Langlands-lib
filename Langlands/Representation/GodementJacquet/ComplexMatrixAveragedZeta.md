# Matrix zeta integrals and a fixed pair of diagonal Schwartz tests

The primal matrix zeta integral and its inverse-transpose dual reduce to a fixed pair of Fourier-related Schwartz tests on the diagonal. The fixed pair is independent of the spectral variable. Both integrals have the same positive Haar comparison factor.

The triangular measure identity is applied first to the original absolutely integrable kernel. Fubini integrates the unitary factor first. The principal series transformation law and the unitary determinant norm move the diagonal and determinant factors outside this integral, leaving the compact Schwartz average. For the dual, inverse transpose and diagonal inversion preserve the corresponding measures. Fourier covariance then handles the compact average. Row and column rescaling remove the unipotent factors, and the lower Fourier marginal is the diagonal transform of the primal upper marginal.

This calculation supplies the two matrix integrals in the principal-series Godement–Jacquet equation used in Jacquet's Sections 4.7 and 8.
