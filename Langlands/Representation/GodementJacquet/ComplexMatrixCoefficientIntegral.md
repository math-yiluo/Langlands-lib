# Compact left translations in matrix zeta integrals

The compact average of left translates of a coefficient can be moved to the matrix test in the domain of absolute convergence. The resulting test average uses the inverse left translations.

The proof bounds the joint kernel using the compact bound for the height of the translating matrices and the polynomial height bound for the coefficient. A measurable change of variables on the product of the compact group and the matrix group preserves the product measure, by left Haar invariance in each matrix fiber. The determinant power is unchanged by a unitary translation. Applying Fubini before and after this change gives the equality of matrix zeta integrals.
