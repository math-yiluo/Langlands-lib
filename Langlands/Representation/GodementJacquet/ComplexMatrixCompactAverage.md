# Unitary averages of matrix Schwartz tests

`complexMatrixCompactAverage` integrates a matrix test after right translation by the unitary group with an integrable complex weight. Right multiplication varies continuously in operator norm. Compactness bounds both the operator and its inverse uniformly, enabling the Schwartz averaging theorem.

For a continuous weight the matrix Fourier transform commutes with this average and applies the inverse-transpose translation to the transformed test. Right multiplication by a unitary matrix preserves additive Haar, which gives an integrable majorant for Fubini from the integral of the norm of the test and the integral of the norm of the weight. Fourier covariance then gives the formula.
