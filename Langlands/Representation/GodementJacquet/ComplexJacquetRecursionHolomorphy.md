# Holomorphy in the adjoining complex exponent

Fix the rectangular Schwartz test, the smaller Whittaker
coefficient and the integer angular weight of the adjoining
character. The group kernel at exponent \(t\) is the kernel at zero
times the exponential of \(t\) times the sum of two log determinant
moduli. The two determinants are those of the evaluation point and
the inverse smaller-group variable.

The fixed-parameter convergence theorem gives absolute integrability
at every complex exponent using continuity, the smaller Whittaker
law and the simple-root bound. The holomorphy lemma for exponential
integrals then applies on the whole complex line. The Weil identity
identifies this entire function with the quotient recursion.

H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §7.2,
printed and PDF pp. 48–49, formula (7.7) and Proposition 7.2.
