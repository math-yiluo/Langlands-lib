# The complementary affine arrays in Jacquet's Fourier identity

The two rectangular arrays in Jacquet's Lemma 7.1 are complementary in their freely varying entries. The primal array is free where the sum of the zero-based row and column indices is less than the row count. The dual array is free in the remaining entries. Each fixed part is an indicator of an antidiagonal and supplies the phase of the other integral.

The proof applies affine Fourier inversion for this partition with the negative trace character. The change of sign preserves the self-dual additive measure, reverses the phase of the primal integral, and leaves the displayed positive phase on the dual side. The lemma includes absolute integrability of both array integrands. The signs are those in Jacquet's printed Lemma 7.1, page 51 of *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals*.
