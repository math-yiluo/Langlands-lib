# Coordinatewise complex dilation

Multiplication by a family of nonzero complex scalars changes the additive product measure by the inverse of the product of squared norms. The proof realizes this multiplication as an invertible diagonal linear map and applies the determinant scaling of additive Haar. The integral changes by the stated real scalar, and absolute integrability is preserved. The measure assigns full mass to the domain where every coordinate is nonzero.

These are auxiliary changes of variables for the product of complex Tate integrals.
