# The open-cell unfolding of a complex Godement section

In the upper-Borel convention, evaluate the section on the long
Weyl representative times the open-cell unipotent coordinates.
For each block and column, absorb the smaller Weyl representative
and the smaller unipotent block into the smaller general linear
integration variable using right Haar invariance. The product of
the Weyl determinants contributes the adjoining character at
`−1`, raised to the smaller rank.

Assume absolute integrability of the transformed joint kernel.
Fubini puts the smaller unipotent integration first, giving the
open-cell integral of the smaller section. The column change of
variables gives the last row of the inverse as the Fourier
frequency. Its determinant Jacobian lowers the determinant
exponent by one. Inversion in the smaller group then gives the
single-group integral in Jacquet (7.7) and proves integrability of
its kernel.

Right translation of smaller unipotent Haar shows that the
smaller open-cell integral transforms by the Whittaker character.
The Weil comparison therefore identifies the group expression
with the quotient recursion for the supplied compatible measures.

H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009),
§7.2, printed and PDF pp. 47–48, formulas (7.4)–(7.7).
The open-cell computation uses the upper-Borel convention and
retains joint absolute integrability as a premise.
