# Fourier inversion on complementary affine coordinate slices

On a product of two complex coordinate spaces, Fourier transformation in both blocks followed by weighted integration in the second frequency block evaluates the corresponding input block at a prescribed point. The remaining integral is the first-block Fourier transform of that affine slice. Both integrals are absolutely convergent for a Schwartz test.

Translation reduces inversion at the prescribed point to the existing inversion at zero. Fubini identifies the Fourier transform on the fixed first frequency with the Fourier transform of a weighted Schwartz marginal.
