# Strict-triangular marginals of the complex matrix Fourier transform

The square matrix coordinates consist of its diagonal, its strict upper
entries and its strict lower entries. The last block uses the transposed
indices of the second. Uncurrying the two matrix indices, reindexing the
entries and splitting the finite sums give a continuous linear
equivalence that preserves the corresponding self-dual product measures.
The same changes split the rectangular Fourier pairing into the three
coordinate pairings.

`integral_rectangularFourierIntegral_lower` applies complementary Fourier
inversion in these coordinates. For any fixed diagonal, integrating the
matrix Fourier transform over the strict lower entries gives the diagonal
Fourier transform of the strict upper marginal of the original Schwartz
test. The two tests in the identity use the exact rectangular Fourier
measure and its scalar factors.
