# Multiplicative Haar on the complex diagonal torus

`complexDiagonalHaar` is the measure on the product of complex multiplicative groups with one inverse squared-norm factor per coordinate against the product of self-dual additive measures. The coordinate inclusion is an open embedding. The additive measure restricted to its image scales under diagonal multiplication by the inverse Jacobian. Multiplication of the density cancels this factor, giving left invariance. Local finiteness and positivity on open sets follow from the continuous positive density on the group.

The pushforward to additive coordinates is the density formula on the full product space, since the union of coordinate hyperplanes has zero additive measure. This fixes the common normalization used in the triangular zeta calculations.
