# Compact determinant-unit supercuspidal coefficients

This internal bridge specializes the published product-Levi Harish-Chandra
theorem to the actual general linear group in positive rank over a p-adic field.
The representation carrier retains its independent universe, and no
irreducibility, central character, or analytic premise is added.

The one-block Levi embeds by evaluation at its sole block. Its determinant-unit
subgroup is homeomorphic to the actual subgroup `ker (T.determinantNorm d)`.
The published single-block cuspidality comparison turns the Library's
admissible conjugate-Jacquet-vanishing predicate into Levi cuspidality.
Harish-Chandra then gives compact support of each coefficient against a smooth
algebraic-dual vector. Pullback through the displayed homeomorphism proves
compact support on the actual determinant-unit subgroup.

This is a derived consequence of the existing Harish-Chandra and single-block
comparison theorems, used toward the cutoff coefficients in Godement and
Jacquet, *Zeta Functions of Simple Algebras* (1972), Proposition 5.5(2),
printed page 62 (one-based PDF page 72). The registered source is
