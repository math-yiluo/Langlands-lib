# Matched diagonal Schwartz tests for complex matrix integrals

`complexUpperMarginal a Φ k` right translates a square matrix Schwartz
test by the unitary factor `k`, restricts to the upper triangular
matrices and integrates against the self-dual additive measure in the
strict upper entries. The result is a Schwartz function of the
diagonal.

Right multiplication by a unitary matrix preserves the additive matrix
measure because its normalized determinant has modulus one. Fourier
covariance moves the translation to the inverse transpose on the dual
frequency. Applying the complementary matrix marginal identity gives
the diagonal Fourier transform of `complexUpperMarginal`.
