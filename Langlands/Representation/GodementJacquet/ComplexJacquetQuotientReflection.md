# Whittaker reflection preserves the complex quotient measure

`measurePreserving_complexReflectionQuotient` supplies the change of variables used in Jacquet's *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), Proposition 7.3, p. 51. It applies to every supplied compatible quotient measure on `N(ℂ) \ GL(ℂ)`.

The published quotient reflection is the quotient of the automorphism `k ↦ w k^ι w` followed by right translation by `w`. The automorphism and its restriction to the unipotent group are involutive, so their Haar moduli are one. The quotient automorphism scaling theorem and right invariance give the measure preservation.
