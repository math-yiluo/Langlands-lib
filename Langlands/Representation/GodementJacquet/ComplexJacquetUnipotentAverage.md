# The unipotent and last-column average

`complexJacquetUnipotentAverage` is the inner integral of (7.8) in Jacquet's *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), p. 50. Its Weyl identity uses Lemma 7.1 with the negative trace Fourier transform. The same self-dual measure serves both trace character parameters.

Under left multiplication by a unipotent matrix, the proof changes variables in the unipotent block and in the last column. Both measures are preserved. For the last column the determinant-one Jacobian follows from the finite-product change of variables. The last coordinate of the column is fixed, and the sum of simple-root entries is additive under unipotent multiplication. The character picked up by the integral is therefore the inverse of the character evaluated on the multiplying matrix.
