# The normalized diagonal Tate equation on the common strip

`complexDiagonalTateEquation_strip` gives the complex diagonal functional equation for the basic trace character when all coordinates belong to the scalar common strip. The proof uses the Gaussian clauses of `ComplexArchimedeanTateStatement`. The scalar test in coordinate \(i\) uses the angular integer \(m_i\).

Finite-product integration factors the Fourier transform and zeta integrals of the reference tensor. Its primal zeta value is the product of the scalar Gamma factors. Its dual value is the reflected product multiplied by \(\prod_i i^{|m_i|}\), where the base of each power is the imaginary unit. The two-test identity then gives the equation for any Schwartz test after division by the nonzero Gamma factors.
