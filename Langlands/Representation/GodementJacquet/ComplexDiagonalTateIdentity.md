# The two-test identity for complex diagonal zeta integrals

On the common product strip \(0<\operatorname{Re}u_i<1\), the product of a primal zeta integral of one Schwartz test and a dual zeta integral of another is invariant under exchanging the tests and their Fourier transforms in the stated order.

The proof applies the change of variables \(y_i=x_i z_i\) in the dual test. The angular factors in \(x_i\) cancel and the remaining powers cancel the additive Jacobian. This gives a joint integrand that is absolutely integrable by the slice computation and the initial convergence of both zeta integrals. Fubini and symmetry of the trace kernel then exchange the two tests. The calculation is the product-coordinate version of the complex scalar Tate two-test argument.
