# From the diagonal torus to the additive Tate kernel

The diagonal Haar density changes the ordinary inducing character times the determinant twist to the complex product Tate kernel. In each coordinate the angular power stays fixed and the real norm has exponent \(2(s+t_i)-2\). The measure in the additive coordinates is the product of the self-dual measures. The equality holds on the initial domain where all \(\operatorname{Re}(s+t_i)\) are positive.

The proof multiplies the additive kernel by the Jacobian to identify its values on the units, pushes the torus measure to additive coordinates, and cancels the inverse Jacobian almost everywhere. Inverting an actual character negates the angular integer and the spectral exponent.
