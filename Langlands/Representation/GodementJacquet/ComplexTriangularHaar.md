# Haar measure in complex triangular coordinates

The upper triangular subgroup is homeomorphic to the product of the
diagonal units and the upper unipotent group, with the diagonal factor
on the left. `exists_complexMatrixHaar_eq_triangular` expresses the
matrix Haar measure on the full group as a finite positive multiple of
the pushforward of the Haar measures of these two factors and the
right unitary group.

The diagonal-unit homomorphism gives the inverse triangular
coordinates. Under left multiplication in the triangular group, the
diagonal coordinate is translated, and the unipotent coordinate is
translated by an element depending on the diagonal coordinate. The
product of the two left Haar measures is invariant under that
transformation. The Library's compact-factorization theorem applies
to the full upper triangular group and the compact unitary group.
This supplies the measure change used in the matrix zeta calculation
for complex induced representations.
