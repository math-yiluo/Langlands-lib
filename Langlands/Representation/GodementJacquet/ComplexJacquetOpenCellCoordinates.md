# Long Weyl representatives in the complex Jacquet open cell

Write an upper unipotent element as the block embedding of a
smaller upper unipotent matrix times a right radical column. Its
simple-root sum is the sum of the smaller simple-root entries and
the last entry of the radical column.

Left multiplication by the long Weyl representative reverses the
row order. The trailing rows of the result are the leading rows
of the original matrix in reverse order. In the block coordinates,
these rows are the smaller long Weyl representative times the
smaller unipotent matrix times the rectangle formed from the
identity and the radical column. The row identity remains valid
after right multiplication by a general linear element.
