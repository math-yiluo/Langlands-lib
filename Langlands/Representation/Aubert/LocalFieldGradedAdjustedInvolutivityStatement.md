# Involutivity of the support-adjusted matrix Aubert operator

`Langlands.Aubert.LocalFieldGraded.LocalFieldGradedAdjustedInvolutivityStatement`
quantifies over every nonarchimedean local field with specified
`LocalFieldData`. The support-adjusted Aubert operator on the direct sum
of the exact finite-length smooth Grothendieck groups of
\(\mathrm{GL}_n(F)\), for \(n\ge 0\), squares to the identity. The
statement supplies the actual preservation witnesses for the
normalized induction and Jacquet maps in every rank.

This is the involution of Aubert's Definition 2.1 and Theorem 2.3.
The rankwise sign is determined by the cardinality of supercuspidal
support, including multiplicity.
