# Jacquet detection on the support of a segment induction

Take the actual increasing normalized product of determinant twists
from the beginning of a segment over a nonarchimedean local field.
The inducing supercuspidal represents the segment's first class.
The lemma `segmentFactor_isRepresentedBy` identifies its ordered
twists with the members of the segment, as in Zelevinsky (1980),
§3.1, printed pp. 180–181.
The full Jacquet functor uses the uniform block Levi with one block
per twist, each of size equal to the cuspidal rank. The proposition
uses explicit finite-length and exactness witnesses to descend this
functor to exact Grothendieck groups and applies the Jordan–Hölder
isomorphisms before and after restriction.

The premise on a virtual class is that its nonzero coefficients
occur only among the irreducible constituents of the increasing
product. Effectivity means that every coefficient is nonnegative.
The conclusion reflects effectivity from the full Jacquet image to
the original virtual class.

This is a derived use of the ordered-tensor multiplicity calculation
in Zelevinsky (1980), Proposition 2.1(a), printed p. 176, and the
distinctness of the determinant twists. Each nonzero subquotient
has a nonzero minimal Jacquet module, and quasicuspidality of such
a quotient forces the corresponding Levi to be associated to the
uniform Levi by Bernstein and Zelevinsky (1977), §2.13(b)–(c) and
§2.14(1), printed p. 449. Such a
Levi has the same ordered shape because all its
blocks have equal size. Thus each supported irreducible contributes
a nonzero column to the full Jacquet image. The full product has
multiplicity-free Jacquet class, so a coordinate of any such column
recovers the original coefficient. [Zelevinsky (1980)](https://www.numdam.org/item/ASENS_1980_4_13_2_165_0/)
