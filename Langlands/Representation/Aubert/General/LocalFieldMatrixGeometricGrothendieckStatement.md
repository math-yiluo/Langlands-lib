# The matrix geometric lemma on exact Grothendieck classes

`Langlands.Aubert.General.RationalPointFamily.GL.LocalFieldMatrixGeometricGrothendieckStatement`
concerns two standard upper-block parabolics of positive-rank
\(\mathrm{GL}_n(F)\) over a nonarchimedean local field. The exact class of
normalized Jacquet restriction after normalized induction equals the sum
of the classes of the actual smooth geometric pieces. Each piece is
formed by normalized refined Jacquet restriction, conjugation and
normalized refined induction. The standard intersections and the
enumeration of minimal Weyl double-coset representatives are chosen
uniformly for all finite-length inducing objects. The composite and
its pieces have finite length, as required for the class formula.

This is the exact Grothendieck consequence of the geometric lemma in
Bernstein–Zelevinsky, *Induced representations of reductive p-adic groups
I* (1977), §2.12, printed p. 448
(`bernstein-zelevinsky-1977-induced-i`). Each filtration step contributes
its class by the actual short exact sequence of that step. The statement
covers arbitrary characteristic and uses the carrier universes of the
matrix Aubert operator.
