# Exactness and finite length inside a standard matrix Levi

`Langlands.Aubert.General.RationalPointFamily.GL.LocalFieldRefinedParabolicPreservationStatement`
asserts that normalized parabolic induction and Jacquet restriction for a
block refinement of a standard product Levi preserve finite length and short
exact sequences of finite-length smooth complex representations. It uses the
actual smooth functors of the embedded refined parabolic and Levi. If the
outer Levi is \(\prod_i \mathrm{GL}_{b_i}(F)\), each refinement chooses a
standard upper-block parabolic in its corresponding factor. Normalization
uses the positive half of the product modulus for induction and the inverse
half for restriction. All nonarchimedean local fields, ranks including zero,
and the saturated carrier universes of the matrix Grothendieck group are
included.

This is the product-Levi case of Renard, *Représentations des groupes
réductifs p-adiques* (French 2026 version), VI.1.2, VI.6.2 and VI.6.4,
printed pp. 135–136 and 178–179
(`renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`). The product
Levi itself is connected reductive, and the product of the chosen
parabolics is parabolic in it. The character convention in Renard is
reciprocal to the positive determinant modulus in the matrix definitions.
