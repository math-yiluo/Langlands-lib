# Unitary representations and same-subgroup Jacquet vanishing

This internal algebraic unit supplies the conjugate-dual step used in the argument of Casselman, *Introduction to the Theory of Admissible Representations of p-adic Reductive Groups* (1 May 1995), proof of Theorem 7.1.4, printed page 68, registered source `casselman-1995-admissible-representations`. The implemented proof is a quotient construction using the published Hermitian-dual equivalence. It does not prove Theorem 7.1.4 or reducibility-to-self-association.

`Langlands.Jacquet.Localization.relations_one_le_comap_conjugate` proves that an equivariant conjugate-linear map takes each generator `rho(u)v - v` into the actual trivial-character relation space of the target. `conjugateCoinvariantsEquiv` descends an equivariant conjugate-linear equivalence and its inverse through those relation spaces. The compositions are identities on every quotient representative. `conjugateCoinvariantsEquiv_mk` records the exact representative formula. The group, source carrier and target carrier universes are independent; this part assumes no topology or smoothness.

`Langlands.Aubert.General.unitaryJacquetConjugateEquiv` applies that construction to the published `Langlands.InvariantPositiveForm.smoothDualEquiv`. This equivariant conjugate-linear Hermitian identification uses admissibility and compact-open cofinality. The normalized Jacquet functor has the actual localization-at-one quotient as its carrier, so the inverse-half-modulus twist does not change this carrier equivalence. The displayed equivalence is an equivalence of complex modules with conjugate scalar action. No equivariance for the normalized Levi actions is asserted. `unitaryJacquetConjugateEquiv_mk` retains the exact quotient formula, where `v` determines the functional `w ↦ b(v, w)`.

`unitaryJacquet_subsingleton_iff` consequently proves equivalence of vanishing for the same-subgroup normalized Jacquet objects of an admissible unitarizable smooth representation and its smooth contragredient. It applies to every locally compact nonarchimedean group, closed subgroup `U` and subgroup `M` normalizing `U`, with independent group and representation universes. It needs no semidirect decomposition, reductive group, irreducibility or finite length.

The declaration dependencies are the actual `Localization.relations`, `mapQ` and `mk` quotient operations; `General.normalizedJacquetFunctor`; the actual bundled smooth contragredient; and `InvariantPositiveForm.smoothDualEquiv` and `toSmoothDual_apply_action` in `Langlands.Representation.Unitary.HermitianDual`. The Hermitian equivalence proves surjectivity by finite-dimensional Riesz representation on compact-open fixed spaces and the published smooth-dual fixed-space comparison. This unit uses no classification, character independence, geometric lemma, adjunction, cuspidal-support theorem or opposite-Jacquet duality. Opposite-Jacquet duality and the analytic square-integrability criterion remain separate obligations for Casselman's irreducibility argument.
