# The finite torus product cover from actual Cartan representatives

The internal theorem `exists_finite_torus_product_cover` assumes the published
predicate `SpecialCompactRenardComparison` for specified actual apartment data,
point, torus, represented centralizer and character comparison, with the
factorization `s ≫ j = i`. It concludes that the actual rational-point group
has a cover `K a e K`, where `K` is the same geometric special compact subgroup,
`a` lies in the literal `torusPoints i`, and `e` lies in a finite set.

The lattice conclusions supply a finite lattice transversal `F0`. Their
dominant-section clause and `specialCompactSectionMap_bijective` give a Cartan
representative in `tildeC0Plus s F0`. Forget its dominant condition to express
it as `c * f` with `c` in `C0` and `f` in `F0`. By `mem_C0`, `c` is the image
under `s` of a uniformizer cocharacter point. Functoriality and `s ≫ j = i`
show that its image under `j` lies in the literal torus image. Take the finite
set `E = j(F0)` and expand the equality of double cosets.

This is a consequence of the explicit comparison predicate and existing
representative definitions. It neither constructs the geometric data nor
proves the comparison predicate, compactness, or general Cartan existence.
No power of a group element is asserted to lie in a single torus double coset.
