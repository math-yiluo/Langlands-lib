# Weyl conjugation preserves normalized matrix induction classes

`Langlands.Aubert.General.RationalPointFamily.GL.LocalFieldMatrixInducedWeylStatement`
compares the actual normalized inductions of a finite-length smooth complex
representation of a standard matrix Levi and its transport to a conjugate
standard Levi. The permutation matrix of the specified Weyl element
conjugates the embedded Levi groups, and the transported representation
acts by inverse conjugation. The conclusion gives finite length of both
inductions and equality of their classes in the exact Grothendieck group
of the same ambient \(\mathrm{GL}_n(F)\). The field is any nonarchimedean
local field, and rank zero and permutations inside blocks are included.

This is the matrix case of Ban and Jantzen, *The Langlands quotient theorem
for finite central extensions of p-adic groups II* (2016), Proposition 4.1(3),
printed p. 158 (`ban-jantzen-2016-duality`). The matrix route also uses the
identification of the product of homogeneous classes with normalized block
induction and commutativity in Zelevinsky, *Induced representations of
reductive p-adic groups II* (1980), §§1.7 and 1.9, printed pp. 172–174
(`zelevinsky-1980-induced-ii`).
