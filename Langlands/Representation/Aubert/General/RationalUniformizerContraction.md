# Contraction of standard unipotent points by uniformizer powers

Module `Langlands.Representation.Aubert.General.RationalUniformizerContraction`.

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field, `G` an affine group scheme over `F`
(`IsAffineHom G.X.hom`), `i : S → G` a maximal split torus, `ν` a cocharacter
of `S` and `J` a finite set of simple roots of `(i, ν)`. Write
`ϖ^y = uniformizerPowers i (.ofAdd y)` for the image in `G(F)` of `y(ϖ)`, where
`ϖ` is the chosen uniformizer of `F`, and `N_J = unipotent i ν J` for the
standard unipotent subgroup of `G(F)`. The module proves the contraction in
D. Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques* (French version
dated 9 July 2026, source id
`renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`), V.5.2 (iii)
(printed p. 123): conjugation by `ϖ^y` takes every `u ∈ N_J` into any given
neighbourhood of `1` once `y` is orthogonal to `J` and pairs to at least some
bound `L` with every simple root outside `J`.

The Library already has this contraction as a step of
`isCartanLatticeModel_rational` (module
`Langlands.Representation.Aubert.General.RationalCartanLatticeModel`), where it
is derived from a simultaneous basis of compact open subgroups
(`SimultaneousStandardBasisAt`). Here it is proved directly, without that
hypothesis, so `isCartanLatticeModel_rational_of_standard` is
`isCartanLatticeModel_rational` with the basis hypothesis removed.

## Proof route

The proof reads conjugates in coordinates. It uses the same tools as the
published `presentationConeContraction` (module
`Langlands.Representation.Exponents.PresentationConeContractionProof`).

1. The rational-point topology of `G(F)` is induced by the chart coordinates
   `Dynamic.AffineChart.pointRing` (`isInducing_pointRing`). Each chart
   coordinate `b` differs from an element `g'` of the augmentation ideal by the
   constant `o = g(1)`.
2. The augmentation ideal is the sum of the weight spaces of the torus
   conjugation action (`CoordinateWeights.coordinateWeightDecomposition`). For
   a weight vector `g` of weight `w`, the coordinate of `ϖ^y u ϖ^{-y}` is
   `ϖ^{⟨w, y⟩} · g(u)` (the eigenfunction property together with
   `characterValue_uniformizerSection`).
3. If `g(u) ≠ 0` and `u ∈ N_J`, then `⟨w, ζ⟩ ≥ 1` for every standard
   cocharacter `ζ` of `J` (`one_le_pairing_of_ne_zero`). This uses
   `Dynamic.CocharacterWeights.mem_unipotent_iff_weights` and the description
   `mem_standardUnipotent` of `N_J` as an intersection of dynamic unipotent
   subgroups.
4. Fix one standard cocharacter `ζ` of `J` and a bound `D ≥ 1` for its pairings
   with the simple roots. For `y` in the cone
   `cone i ν J K = {y | ⟨β, y⟩ = 0 for β ∈ J, ⟨β, y⟩ ≥ K for β ∉ J}` with
   `K ≥ m·D + 1`, the cocharacter `y - m·ζ` is again standard for `J`
   (`isStandard_sub_smul`). Applying step 3 to it gives `⟨w, y⟩ ≥ m + 1`
   (`add_one_le_pairing`).
5. `exists_uniformizer_pow_mul_mem` gives an `N` with `ϖ^n · g(u) ∈ V` for all
   `n ≥ N`. So each weight coordinate tends to `0` along the filter `coneFilter`
   generated by the cones (`tendsto_conjCoord_of_mem`). By induction on the sum
   of weight spaces the same holds for every element of the augmentation ideal
   (`tendsto_conjCoord_augmentation`). Hence `ϖ^y u ϖ^{-y} → 1` along
   `coneFilter` (`tendsto_conj`).
6. A neighbourhood of `1` therefore contains the conjugates for all `y` in some
   cone (`exists_cone_subset_of_mem`). This is
   `uniformizerPowers_conj_mem_of_standard`.

## Declarations in `Langlands.Aubert.General.UniformizerContraction`

These are auxiliary declarations.

- `characterValue_uniformizerSection`: a character `w` takes the value
  `ϖ^{⟨w, y⟩}` at `y(ϖ)`.
- `cone i ν J K`, `coneFilter i ν J`: the cones described in step 4 and the
  infimum of their principal filters. `cone_antitone`, `mem_coneFilter_of_subset`
  and `exists_cone_subset_of_mem` relate membership in the filter to inclusion
  of a cone.
- `exists_pairing_bound`: if `simpleRelativeRoots i ν` is finite, the pairings
  of one cocharacter with the simple roots are bounded by some `D ≥ 1`.
- `isStandard_sub_smul`, `add_one_le_pairing`: step 4.
- `one_le_pairing_of_ne_zero`: step 3.
- `conjCoord i g u y`: the Lie coordinate `g` of `ϖ^y u ϖ^{-y}`.
- `tendsto_conjCoord_of_mem`, `tendsto_conjCoord_augmentation`, `tendsto_conj`:
  step 5.

## `Langlands.Aubert.General.uniformizerPowers_conj_mem_of_standard`

```
theorem uniformizerPowers_conj_mem_of_standard (hi : IsMaximalSplitTorus i)
    (hfinite : (simpleRelativeRoots i ν).Finite) (J : Finset (SimpleRoot i ν))
    (hexists : ∃ ζ, IsStandardCocharacter i ν (rootSet i ν J) ζ)
    (u : rationalPoints G) (hu : u ∈ unipotent i ν J) :
    ∀ O ∈ 𝓝 (1 : rationalPoints G), ∃ L : ℤ, ∀ y : cocharacterLattice S,
      (∀ β ∈ J, simpleRootPairing i ν β y = 0) → (∀ β ∉ J, L ≤ simpleRootPairing i ν β y) →
        uniformizerPowers i (.ofAdd y) * u * (uniformizerPowers i (.ofAdd y))⁻¹ ∈ O
```

`G(F)` carries `rationalPointTopology F G.X`. The hypotheses are that `i` is a
maximal split torus, that `(i, ν)` has finitely many simple roots, and that some
standard cocharacter of `J` exists. Under these hypotheses, for every `u ∈ N_J`
and every neighbourhood `O` of `1` there is an integer `L` such that
`ϖ^y u ϖ^{-y} ∈ O` for every cocharacter `y` that vanishes on `J` and pairs to
at least `L` with each simple root outside `J`.

## `Langlands.Aubert.General.isCartanLatticeModel_rational_of_standard`

```
theorem isCartanLatticeModel_rational_of_standard [LocallyOfFiniteType G.X.hom]
    (hi : IsMaximalSplitTorus i) (hfinite : (simpleRelativeRoots i ν).Finite)
    (hexists : ∀ I : Finset (SimpleRoot i ν),
      ∃ ζ, IsStandardCocharacter i ν (rootSet i ν I) ζ)
    (hrep : (torusCentralizer i).IsRepresentedBy j) (hsj : s ≫ j = i)
    (hval : ∀ c : cocharacterLattice S,
      (centralizerValuation s h (rationalPoints.map s
        (Plancherel.RenardCartan.uniformizerSection S (Multiplicative.ofAdd c)))).toAdd =
          1 ⊗ₜ[ℤ] c)
    {F0 : Set (rationalPoints Z)} (hF0 : F0.Finite) :
    IsCartanLatticeModel (levi i ν) (unipotent i ν) (rationalCartanSet i ν j s h F0)
      (uniformizerPowers i) (simpleRootPairing i ν) (rationalPoints.map j '' F0)
```

Here `j : Z → G` represents the torus centralizer, `s : S → Z` satisfies
`s ≫ j = i`, and `h` is the bijectivity of `restrictionEvaluation s`. Under
these hypotheses, the standard Levi and unipotent families of `G(F)`, the
Cartan sets `rationalCartanSet i ν j s h F0`, the uniformizer powers, the
simple-root pairings and the image of the finite set `F0 ⊆ Z(F)` form an
`IsCartanLatticeModel` (module
`Langlands.Representation.Aubert.General.CartanLatticeContraction`). The fields
other than `contracts` are filled with the same published lemmas that
`isCartanLatticeModel_rational` uses. The field `contracts` is
`uniformizerPowers_conj_mem_of_standard`.
