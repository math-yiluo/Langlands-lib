# Generic `PQ`-regularity of unramified twists for same-Levi parabolics

This module proves that for represented parabolics `P = MN` and `Q = MU` with the same Levi,
an irreducible admissible `σ` becomes `PQ`-regular after an unramified twist `σψ`, for all
`ψ` off the zero locus of one nonzero polynomial in the published unramified coordinates
(`Plancherel.rationalUnramifiedPrincipalOpen`). This is the genericity step of Renard
VII.3. Renard states it for the regular parameters `ψ`, and here it is realized as a
polynomial principal open. The provenance is internal, following that route.

## `exists_splitComponent_scalar`

For a split component `a : A → M` and an irreducible admissible `σ`, there is a character
`c` of `A(F)` with `σ(a t) = c(t) · 1`. This is Schur's lemma
(`admissibleSchur_centralCharacter`) applied to the central image of `a`.

## `GeometricLemmaIntersectionDatum.exists_generic_piece_separation`

Fix a non-identity double coset `P(F) z Q(F)` with intersection datum `D`, a split
component `a`, and a character `c` of `A(F)`. For every irreducible admissible `σ` there is
a nonzero polynomial `poly` such that every `ψ` in its principal open satisfies two
conditions:

- the action of `A(F)` through `a` on the piece `D.pieceFunctor.obj (σψ)` has a
  finite-dimensional action quotient;
- no generalized eigencharacter `χ` of `A(F)` on that piece equals `c · (ψ ∘ a)`.

Proof:

1. `D.exists_isSplitComponent_factor` factors `a` as `f ≫ a' ≫ D.lQ' ≫ D.q'`, with `a'` a
   split component of `L'`. The strict displacement theorem
   `sameLeviPieceStrictDisplacement` gives `t ∈ A(F)` such that `a(t)` and its transport
   `(f ≫ a' ≫ φ ≫ l' ≫ p')(t)` have different valuation images in `M`.
2. For every irreducible admissible `ρ`, the Jacquet module `r^M_{P'}(ρ)` is admissible
   (`arbitraryParabolicJacquetAdmissibility`, transported along `leviPointsEquiv`). It is
   finitely generated by `leviJacquetFiniteType`, since an irreducible `ρ` is finitely
   generated (`moduleFinite_asModule_of_isIrreducible`). Hence the set `Θ` of normalized
   Jacquet exponents of `σ` along `a' ∘ φ` is finite
   (`finite_normalizedJacquetExponents_of_leviJacquet`).
3. For each `θ ∈ Θ`, `Plancherel.exists_ne_zero_rationalUnramifiedPrincipalOpen_apply_ne`
   gives a nonzero polynomial `P θ` such that on its principal open
   `ψ(a t) ≠ θ(f t) · c(t)⁻¹ · ψ((f ≫ a' ≫ φ ≫ l' ≫ p') t)`. Take
   `poly = ∏_{θ ∈ Θ} P θ`, which is nonzero.
4. For `ψ` in the principal open of `poly`, `σψ` is irreducible and admissible
   (`SmoothRep.isIrreducible_twist_iff`, `SmoothRep.isAdmissible_twist_iff`). Step 2 and
   `D.finite_actionQuotient_piece` then give the finite action quotient on the piece.
5. Let `χ` be a generalized eigencharacter of `A(F)` on the piece of `σψ`.
   `D.exists_untwisted_sourceJacquetExponent_of_twisted_piece` writes it as
   `χ = (θ ∘ f) · (ψ ∘ (f ≫ a' ≫ φ ≫ l' ≫ p'))` for some `θ ∈ Θ`. If `χ = c · (ψ ∘ a)`,
   evaluating at `t` contradicts the inequality from `P θ`. Membership of `ψ` in the
   principal open of the factor `P θ` follows from `map_prod` and
   `Finset.prod_ne_zero_iff`.

## `exists_generic_isPQRegular`

This theorem is stated in the same explicit universally quantified form as the target. For
a split component `a : A → M` and an irreducible admissible `σ`, there is a nonzero
polynomial such that `IsPQRegular p l r hp hr hl hmul q lQ rQ hq hrQ hlQ hmulQ a (σψ)` holds
for every `ψ` in its principal open.

Proof:

1. The represented geometric lemma (`representedGeometricLemma`) supplies representatives
   `z w` of every double coset and an intersection datum at each. These are fed to
   `pqRegularityPieceFiltration`, which gives a filtration `s` of the regularity quotient
   functor whose grades are naturally isomorphic (`e`) to the pieces `D.pieceFunctor` at
   the double cosets strictly below the identity. Those cosets differ from the identity
   coset, so `exists_generic_piece_separation` applies to each one.
2. Let `cσ` be the scalar character of `σ` on `A(F)` (`exists_splitComponent_scalar`).
   Choose the polynomial `Pi i` of `exists_generic_piece_separation` for each piece with
   `c = cσ`, and take `poly = ∏ i, Pi i`.
3. For `ψ` in the principal open, `σψ(a t) = (cσ · ψ ∘ a)(t) · 1`, because the twist
   multiplies by `ψ`. So the action quotient of `σψ ∘ a` is finite
   (`CentralActionQuotient.finite_actionQuotient_of_scalar`), and its only generalized
   eigencharacter is `cσ · ψ ∘ a`
   (`CentralActionQuotient.eq_of_isGeneralizedEigencharacter_of_scalar`). The pieces have
   finite action quotients and avoid this character, by the choice of `Pi i`.
4. `pqRegularity_of_disjoint_generalizedCharacters` turns the finiteness and the
   disjointness of generalized characters into `IsPQRegular`.
