# Rational-point realization of the GL Aubert operator

Aubert's Definition 1.5, printed p. 2181, sums the actual normalized
induction–Jacquet composites with one sign for each standard Levi. For
\(\mathrm{GL}_n\) over a nonarchimedean local field, the statement supplies
the finite-length and short-exact-sequence properties for both the
literal rational-point family and the matrix block family. It compares
the resulting endomorphisms on the exact finite-length Grothendieck
groups by pullback along the continuous equivalence between the two
groups.

The cocharacter is strictly decreasing in the diagonal chamber, so the
block-shape correspondence gives the sign \((-1)^{n-\#\mathrm{blocks}}\)
from the cardinality of the associated simple-root subset. The
quantifier covers rank zero, where the block shape and the root subset
are empty. All representations have complex coefficients.
