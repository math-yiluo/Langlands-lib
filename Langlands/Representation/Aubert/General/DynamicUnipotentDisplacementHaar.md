# Twisted conjugation on the actual unipotent radical

`unipotentDisplacementDeterminant` is the determinant of \(1-\operatorname{Ad}(m)\)
on the Lie kernel of the represented identity-limit subgroup. The proof uses
the actual adjoint action of the cocharacter centralizer. In the rational graded
coordinates of the radical, its differential has the block triangular matrix
of the published graded-coordinate construction. Subtracting that matrix from
the identity and taking weight blocks gives the induced linear displacements
on the actual vector quotients. The determinant of the Lie displacement is the
product of the determinants of these blocks.

When this determinant is nonzero, every quotient displacement is invertible.
The finite filtration result computes the pushforward of an arbitrary Haar
measure on the radical by \(u\mapsto(mum^{-1})^{-1}u\). Its scale is the inverse
of the absolute value of the Lie displacement determinant. The radical compact
open cover is the published dynamic unipotent cover. This is an internal measure
calculation for the compact normalization in Waldspurger's Lemma V.3.1.
