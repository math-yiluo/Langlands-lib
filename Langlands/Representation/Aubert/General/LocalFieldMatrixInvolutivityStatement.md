# Involutivity of the unadjusted matrix Aubert operator

`Langlands.Aubert.General.RationalPointFamily.GL.LocalFieldMatrixInvolutivityStatement`
asserts the square identity for the unadjusted operator on the exact
Grothendieck group of smooth finite-length complex representations of
\(\mathrm{GL}_n(F)\). The field \(F\) is equipped with `LocalFieldData`.
The statement includes rank zero and gives the finite-length and
short-exact-sequence preservation witnesses for the normalized matrix
maps used in the alternating sum.

This is the matrix case of Aubert's involutivity theorem. The operator
uses the integer sign of Definition 1.5 and the upper block shape model
of standard Levis.
