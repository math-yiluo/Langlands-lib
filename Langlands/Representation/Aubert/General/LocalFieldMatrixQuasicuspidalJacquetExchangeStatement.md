# The quasicuspidal reversed Jacquet formula for matrix Aubert duality

Let the rank be positive and let the local field be arbitrary,
including positive characteristic. Let \(C\) be an ordered block
shape. Reverse the order of its blocks and take actual upper
normalized Jacquet restriction of the finite-length smooth complex
representation to that Levi. Assume that the resulting representation
is quasicuspidal. Transport its class back to \(C\) by the actual
permutation which reverses the blocks while preserving their internal
coordinate order.

The actual Jacquet class of the matrix Aubert image at \(C\) equals
this transported class times \((-1)^{n-\mathrm{length}(C)}\).
The operators are the class maps of the existing normalized matrix
functors. The proposition takes explicit finite-length and
short-exactness witnesses for their descent to exact classes.

This is the specialization of Aubert, *Trans. Amer. Math. Soc.* 347
(1995), Theorem 1.7(2) and its quasicuspidal Levi sign computation,
printed pp. 2181–2183. Proper refined restriction of the reversed
Jacquet object vanishes by quasicuspidality. The whole refinement
therefore contributes the action on that object with the sign for
the internal simple roots of the Levi.
