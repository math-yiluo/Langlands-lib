# Actual normalized Jacquet restriction in stages

This internal adapter specializes the published character-localization tower to the actual normalized Jacquet functors. The subgroup data are `W ≤ M` and `H ≤ M`, with `M` normalizing `U` and `H` normalizing `W`. The ambient group is locally compact and nonarchimedean. The supplied closedness proofs are for `U` and `M` in the ambient group, for the actual copy `W.subgroupOf M` in `M`, and for `U ⊔ W` in the ambient group. No separation assumption is silently inferred from the group classes.

`positiveCharacter_eq_one_on_compactLimit` proves that a positive character with open kernel is trivial on a subgroup that is a limit of compact subgroups. A given element lies in a compact subgroup. The character's open kernel has finite index there, so the positive value has finite order and equals one.

`jacquetTowerDatum` uses the published `TowerDatum` with all three localization characters trivial. The second quotient uses the literal `W.subgroupOf M`, and the final action is transported from `H.subgroupOf M` to `H` by the canonical continuous subgroup equivalence. `innerJacquetModulus` is the actual Haar conjugation modulus of the second radical inside `M`, transported to `H`. `iteratedNormalizedJacquetFunctor` composes the two published normalized Jacquet functors and this final transport.

`normalizedJacquetStagesIso` compares that actual composite with normalized restriction at `U ⊔ W`. Its extra hypotheses are compact-limit containment for the inner radical and the explicit equality between the product of the two actual Haar modulus characters and the direct Haar modulus. The first normalization character is trivial on the second radical by the compact-limit argument. The published `TowerDatum.restrictedProduct_inverseHalfCharacter` identity identifies the final twist, and `TowerDatum.twistedEquivOfEq` gives the natural isomorphism on the actual coinvariant quotients by `[[v]] ↦ [v]`.

This adapter does not discharge the Haar modulus factorization or identify a rational-point radical with `U ⊔ W`. Those must be proved before the adapter can supply any part of the unconditional rational involutivity target. The ambient group and representation-carrier universes are independent (`uG` and `uV`); no carrier-size or finite-length restriction is imposed. It uses the published quotient universal property, quotient actions, character twists, and actual normalization definitions from `General.Parabolic` and `Jacquet.LocalizationTower`. The naturality proof is exactly `TowerDatum.twistedEquivOfEq_natural`, after identifying the normalized constructions definitionally. It does not use classification, character independence, the geometric lemma, adjointness, cuspidal support or Aubert Proposition 3.2.
