## Result

`Langlands.Aubert.General.RationalPointFamily.rationalCorrectedSurvivingSupport`
has type exactly `RationalCorrectedSurvivingSupportStatement.{u}`. That is the
published proposition of
`Langlands.Representation.Aubert.General.RationalCorrectedSurvivingSupportTarget`,
unapplied, at field universe `u`, with no theorem parameters.

The theorem covers everything the statement quantifies over:

- every nonarchimedean local field `k`, including positive characteristic;
- every connected reductive group scheme `G`, including nonsplit and
  non-quasisplit groups;
- every actual maximal split torus `i` and generic cocharacter `ν`;
- the canonical `pointTopology` on `G(k)` with the displayed locally
  compact, nonarchimedean and Hausdorff instances;
- all named structural premises, every linear order on the simple roots and
  every finite structure on the full-centralizer Weyl quotient;
- every Coxeter system `cs` satisfying the three displayed comparisons,
  including the empty simple-root set.

For every `I`, removed-root set `s`, valid position `j` of
`Support.rankOrderedList cs.length`, and actual normalizer lift `m` of the
inverse entry `w⁻¹`, assume `w` is minimal in `W_(S \ s) w W_I`. The theorem
then gives all four fields of `SurvivingSupportHypotheses`:

1. `groupSupport` at `j` is open in the point topology.
2. `groupSupport` at `j + 1` is open in the point topology.
3. The upper support is left `P_(S \ s)`-invariant.
4. Upper minus lower is exactly `{p * m⁻¹ * q | p ∈ P_(S \ s), q ∈ P_I}`.

The supports are the literal Library definitions. They use the implemented
decreasing-length list with its tie choices and the largest two-sided cores.
No other open-prefix enumeration is used. The only axioms are `propext`,
`Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`, as reported by `lean_check`.

### First conjunct: standard parabolics on rational points

1. `relativeTitsSystemStatement_of_bruhat_and_rankOneFactorization` takes two
   published unconditional proofs and returns `RelativeTitsSystemStatement.{u}`.
   The two proofs are `rationalBruhat : RationalBruhatStatement.{u}` and
   `rootRankOneFactorization : RootRankOneFactorizationStatement.{u}`. The result
   is a Tits system `(P_∅(k), N(k), N(k)/Z(k), S)` with
   `S = simpleRelativeReflections i ν`. Each simple relative root has a unique
   reflection.
2. `standardParabolicTitsRealization_of_statements` combines step 1 with the
   published proof `standardRelativeWeylGeneration`. The result is
   `StandardParabolicTitsRealization.{u}`: every `W_J` is generated by a subset
   of `S`, and every subset of `S` generates some `W_J`.
3. `standardParabolicPointsStatement_of_titsRealization` combines step 2 with the
   published proofs `standardParabolicEndpoints` and `standardParabolicOrder`.
   The result is `StandardParabolicPointsStatement.{u}`, with all four clauses.
   The fourth clause is the double-coset equivalence
   `W_J\W/W_K ≃ P_J(k)\G(k)/P_K(k)`. It maps the class of each normalizing point
   to the class of that same point.

### Second conjunct: compact parabolic quotients

`BruhatTits.dynamicParabolicQuotientCompact` is the published proof of
`DynamicParabolicQuotientCompactStatement.{u}`. For every rational cocharacter,
both `G(k)/P(k)` and `Induction.LeftQuotient P(k)` are compact.

of `G/P` (Renard V.5.1). None of them is assumed.
