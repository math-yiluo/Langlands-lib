# Admissibility for local-field product Levis

`Langlands.AllField.CuspidalSupport.irreducibleLeviAdmissibility` proves that an
irreducible smooth complex representation of a standard product Levi of
\(\mathrm{GL}_n(F)\) is admissible. The field and representation carrier
universes are independent, and \(F\) is any nonarchimedean local field.

The whole-block refinement has trivial radical, so its Jacquet module is
nonzero. Among refinements with a nonzero Jacquet module, choose one with the
largest number of blocks. The Jacquet module for the composed refinement
surjects onto the coinvariants of the first Jacquet module by any further
refined radical. For the surjection, the original radical acts trivially on
the first quotient and the first normalization character is trivial on the
further radical. Any proper refinement with a nonzero quotient would contradict
maximality. The resulting smooth normalized representation on the flattened
Levi is quasicuspidal.

The original representation is generated by the orbit of a nonzero smooth
vector. The compact refined parabolic coset space gives finitely many double
cosets for an open subgroup fixing that vector. The classes of its translates
by their representatives generate the Jacquet module. Finite generation gives
a maximal proper subrepresentation, whose quotient is irreducible. Radical
coinvariants preserve surjections, so this quotient is also quasicuspidal.

The refined first adjointness map sends a vector \(v\) to the function of
\(g\) obtained by applying the Jacquet quotient followed by the simple quotient
to \(g v\). The positive half-modulus in its covariance cancels the inverse
half-modulus in the Jacquet action. Evaluation at the identity recovers the
composite quotient map, so the intertwiner is nonzero and hence injective.
The published quasicuspidal admissibility theorem on the flattened Levi supplies
admissibility of the inducing object. Inflation through the continuous open
Levi projection, twisting by the half-modulus with open kernel, and induction
from the compact coset space preserve admissibility. The original representation
inherits admissibility along the injection.
