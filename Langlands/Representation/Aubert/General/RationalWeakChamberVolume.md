# Integral weak-chamber membership

This internal helper adapts the integral split-component lift in
`uniformizerPowers_conj_mem`. A cocharacter annihilating the selected simple
roots is central in the standard Levi, hence lifts integrally to its supplied
split component. The actual uniformizer section is natural for this map.
The normalized character values are `q` to the negative integral pairing;
nonnegative pairings make every outside-root character norm at most one.
Thus its evaluation lies in the actual weak chamber of that component.
No volume bound or existence of a simultaneous basis is asserted here.
