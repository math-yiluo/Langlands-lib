# Graded corrected-filtration targets on the actual rational points

Author: Worker 379. The two propositions in
`Langlands.Aubert.General.RationalPointFamily` restate the generic targets of
`CorrectedFiltrationStatements` for the actual group of rational points of a
connected reductive group, its actual standard subgroup families and its
actual relative Weyl group. They are proposition definitions only: neither
target is proved here.

## Setting

The group setting is that of `RationalSupportCategoryTargets.md`. The
statements quantify over every nonarchimedean local field `k`, connected
reductive `G` (`hG`), actual maximal split torus `i` (`hi`) and generic
cocharacter `ν` (`hν`). The ambient group is `RationalPoint k G.X` with
`pointTopology` and explicit local compactness, nonarchimedean and Hausdorff
premises. The families are literally `parabolic i ν`, `levi i ν` and
`unipotent i ν` at `rootSet i ν I`, and the finite enumeration of
`SimpleRoot i ν` is `simpleRelativeRootFintype`. Representations have carriers
in `Type (max (u + 1) v)`; the generic targets are instantiated at
`.{u, u, max (u + 1) v}`. No split, quasisplit or characteristic-zero
restriction is imposed, and the empty simple-root set is allowed.

Both statements quantify over every linear order on `SimpleRoot i ν`; it only
chooses the exterior coordinates of the complex, and no canonical order is
asserted.

## The actual Weyl group and its Coxeter structure

The subgroup `T` of the generic targets is `torusPoints i`, the image of the
torus points. `Weyl i` is then the actual quotient of its normalizer by its
full centralizer, `N_G(T)/Z_G(T)`. Its finiteness `[Fintype (Weyl i)]` is an
explicit premise; decidable equality is the classical one. Each statement
quantifies over every Coxeter matrix `CM` on `SimpleRoot i ν` and every Coxeter
system `cs : ActualCoxeterSystem (torusPoints i) CM`, that is
`CoxeterSystem CM (Weyl i)`, satisfying three published comparisons:

- `RootLengthComparison i ν hN hZ cs`: `ℓ(w s_a) > ℓ(w)` exactly when the actual
  character action of `w` sends the simple root `a` to a positive relative
  root;
- `PositiveSimpleReflectionComparison i ν hN hZ cs`: when `w a` is positive,
  `w s_a w⁻¹ = s_b` exactly when `w a = b`;
- `actualLeviWeylIdentification (torusPoints i) (levi i ν) cs`: for every `I`,
  the image in `Weyl i` of the actual Levi Weyl quotient
  `WeylIn (torusPoints i) (levi i ν I)` is the standard Coxeter subgroup `W_I`
  generated by the reflections in `I`.

The character action is `weylCharacterAction i hN hZ`, transported through the
exact scheme/point normalizer and full-centralizer comparisons `hN` and `hZ`.
So the length function, the length ordering of Weyl elements, the double-coset
minimality `IsMinimal cs (S \ s) I w` and the standard subgroups used by the
filtration are those of the relative root system, not of a free Coxeter
relabelling. As in the published `RationalCorollaryTargets`, the three
comparisons are intermediate premises; they are not proved here.

The vanishing target concerns only the filtration `step` and the support
category, which use neither unimodularity nor the order of the families.
`RationalCorrectedGradedVanishingStatement` therefore omits
`StandardParabolicOrderStatement` and the compact-limit premise `hUc`.
`RationalCorrectedGradedTransitionStatement` keeps both, because its maps
compare with the transitions of the normalized complex. Neither statement uses
`StandardParabolicEndpointsStatement`. Omitting an unused premise only
strengthens the statement. `IsAssociate` enters through the support category
`Alg({I})`; see `RationalSupportCategoryTargets.md` for the meaning of
`rootAssociation`.

## Targets

| Declaration | Generic target | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `RationalCorrectedGradedVanishingStatement` | `CorrectedFiltration.CorrectedGradedVanishingTarget` | erratum, pp. 4688–4689; Ban–Jantzen (2016), Section 4, pp. 160–162 |
| `RationalCorrectedGradedTransitionStatement` | `CorrectedFiltration.CorrectedGradedTransitionTarget` | erratum, pp. 4688–4690; Ban–Jantzen (2016), Section 4, p. 162 |

With these substitutions the vanishing statement says the following. Take `E`
in the actual support category of `L_I` and the `i`-th length-ordered element
`w` of the actual Weyl group. If the graded piece of the support filtration of
`r_I(E_J)` at `w` is nonzero, with `J = S \ s`, then for every actual lift `m` of
`w⁻¹` there is a standard `K ⊆ J` with `L_K = m⁻¹ L_I m`. The transition
statement gives, on every surviving grade, surjective actual intertwiners from
the step `i + 1` onto `Ad(w⁻¹)(r_{L_K}(E))` with kernel the step `i`. Under them
the actual Jacquet transitions of the normalized complex become identity maps.
The full reading of both generic targets, the conjugation direction and the
removed-root indexing are in `CorrectedFiltrationStatements.md`.

## Provenance

This is a derived specialization: the mathematical content is that of the
corrected proof of Aubert's Theorem 3.6 in the erratum, with the Ban–Jantzen
double-coset repair. The derivation pins the generic parameters to the actual
families, the actual Weyl group and the named structural inputs above. The
structural, root-sign, finiteness, closedness and topological premises remain
intermediate obligations, to be discharged or matched to the parent's
external structural ledger. No representation-theoretic statement is moved
into them.
