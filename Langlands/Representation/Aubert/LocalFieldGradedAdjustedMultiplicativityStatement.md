# Multiplicativity of the graded adjusted Aubert operator

`Langlands.Aubert.LocalFieldGraded.LocalFieldGradedAdjustedMultiplicativityStatement`
asserts that the support-adjusted induction–Jacquet operator preserves
normalized induction multiplication and its unit. It works on the
whole direct sum of the exact Grothendieck groups of smooth
finite-length complex representations of the actual \(\mathrm{GL}_n(F)\).
The field \(F\) is any nonarchimedean local field with specified
`LocalFieldData`, and the rank-zero summand contributes the trivial line
as the unit.

The proposition includes existence of the finite-length and exactness
witnesses for the binary product and the matrix induction and Jacquet
maps in every rank. The equations concern the operator and product
constructed from these witnesses.

The multiplicativity clause is the ring-homomorphism part of Aubert's
Theorem 2.3. It combines induction compatibility in Theorem 1.7(2)
with the tensor formula for the alternating sum on a product Levi and
the support sign of Definition 2.1.
