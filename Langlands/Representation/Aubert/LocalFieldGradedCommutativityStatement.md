# Commutativity of graded normalized GL induction

`Langlands.Aubert.LocalFieldGraded.LocalFieldGradedCommutativityStatement`
asserts commutativity of the graded exact Grothendieck multiplication
from actual binary normalized induction. The field may have
characteristic zero or positive characteristic. Both variables range
over the entire graded direct sum of exact groups, and the proposition
applies to every finite-length and separate exactness witness used to
construct the multiplication.

Zelevinsky 1980 §1.9, printed p. 174, identifies the Jordan–Hölder
constituents, with multiplicities, after interchanging two finite-length
factors. Together with the actual exact Grothendieck relations, this
gives commutativity on homogeneous classes. The multiplication is
bilinear, so the identity extends to all graded classes, including the
rank-zero summand.
