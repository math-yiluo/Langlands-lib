# Exact Grothendieck classes of the segment endpoints

For each Zelevinsky segment, `segmentSocleObject` chooses the actual
irreducible socle of the increasing normalized product from the
segment socle existence property. `segmentSpecialObject` chooses the
actual special irreducible quotient from the corresponding head
existence property. Their classes `segmentSocleClass` and
`segmentSpecialClass` lie in the exact finite-length Grothendieck
group in degree equal to the segment length times the cuspidal rank.
These classes can be included in the full graded group in that degree.

The class comparisons first transport the two actual ambient products
to the same supercuspidal representative of the segment beginning.
Uniqueness of the images of irreducible inclusions and of the kernels
of irreducible quotients gives equivariant isomorphisms between the
respective endpoints. The exact class equality follows from those
isomorphisms.

Both endpoints are subquotients of the increasing cuspidal product.
Consequently their support is the multiset enumerating the segment
members with multiplicity. Its cardinality is the segment length
\(k\). In group rank \(n\), the support sign formula gives
\((-1)^{n-k}\).

The endpoint conventions are those of Zelevinsky (1980), §§3.1 and
9.1. The support comes from §1.10. Aubert's sign is in *Trans. Amer.
Math. Soc.* 347 (1995), Notation 1.6 and Definition 2.1, printed
pp. 2181 and 2184. [Zelevinsky (1980)](https://www.numdam.org/item/ASENS_1980_4_13_2_165_0/)
