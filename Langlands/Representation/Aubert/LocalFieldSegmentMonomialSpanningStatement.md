# Spanning by segment induction monomials

The input is any nonarchimedean local field and a preservation
witness for actual normalized binary induction. A factor in a
monomial is the class of an actual irreducible segment socle,
included in the summand of its general linear group. The factors
are multiplied using the exact class maps of those binary
inductions. The empty monomial is the class of the trivial line
in rank zero.

The proposition says that the integer span of all these finite
products is the entire graded exact Grothendieck group, including
inhomogeneous virtual classes. Arbitrary representatives of a
segment socle give the same class. Folded products give the same
monomial for any ordering once associativity and commutativity
are applied.

This is the spanning clause of Zelevinsky (1980), Corollary 7.5,
printed p. 193, for the multiplication of §1.7. Aubert uses it in
the proof of Theorem 2.3 on printed p. 2185 to extend the segment
comparison to the whole ring. [Zelevinsky (1980)](https://www.numdam.org/item/ASENS_1980_4_13_2_165_0/)
