# Renard VI.4.4, last sentence of the Proposition: proof

Author: Worker 54. Module

`Langlands.CuspidalSupport`.

`rationalSummandCenterVariety : RationalSummandCenterVarietyStatement.{uF, uV}` proves the
published statement at arbitrary universes `uF`, `uV`. It adds no hypothesis and does not
change the statement's binders.

Source: David Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques* (registered source
`renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`), VI.4.4, the Proposition and its
proof, printed p.159 / PDF p.165. Renard deduces the last sentence from the isomorphism
`ℂ[T/⁰G] ≃ Z` and the identification of `ℂ[T/⁰G]` with the functions on `Irr(G)[π]`. The Lean
proof does the same. It proves the identification directly by Dedekind's independence of
characters.

## Notation

`H⁰ = rationalCommonNormKernel G`, `X = X(G)` the unramified characters,
`Stab = {s ∈ X | π ⊗ s ≃ π}`, and `T = ⋂_{ω, π⊗ω≃π} ker ω`, the subgroup of the published
`RationalSummandCenterGroupAlgebraStatement`.

## Generic part (`CenterVariety`, section `Generic`)

The setting is a group `G` with a normal subgroup `H₀`, a monoid `X`, and a homomorphism
`ev : G →* (X →* ℂ)` with `H₀ ≤ ker ev`.

- `quotientEval` is the induced map `G ⧸ H₀ →* (X → ℂ)`, `q ↦ (χ ↦ ev q χ)`.
  `quotientEvalAlg` is its extension to the monoid algebra (`MonoidAlgebra.lift`).
- `quotientEval_linearIndependent`: if `ev g = 1` implies `g ∈ H₀` (hypothesis `hsep`), the
  induced map to `X →* ℂ` is injective. The functions `quotientEval q` are then linearly
  independent by Mathlib's `linearIndependent_monoidHom` (Dedekind). So `quotientEvalAlg` is
  injective (`quotientEvalAlg_injective`).
- For a subgroup `T`, `subgroupQuotientMap : T ⧸ (H₀ ∩ T) →* G ⧸ H₀` is injective, and
  `subgroupEvalAlg = quotientEvalAlg ∘ mapDomain subgroupQuotientMap` sends the class of
  `t ∈ T` to `ev t` (`subgroupEvalAlg_of`). It is injective (`subgroupEvalAlg_injective`),
  since `mapDomain` along an injective map is injective.
- `subgroupEvalAlg_range`: assume `g ∈ T ↔ ∀ s ∈ Stab, ev g s = 1` (hypothesis `hT`). Then the
  range of `subgroupEvalAlg` is the set of `φ` in `Algebra.adjoin ℂ (range ev)` with
  `φ(χ s) = φ(χ)` for all `s ∈ Stab` and all `χ`.
  - (⊆) Each `ev t` with `t ∈ T` lies in the adjoin and is `Stab`-invariant, because
    `ev t (χ s) = ev t χ · ev t s` and `ev t s = 1`. Both properties pass to sums and scalar
    multiples (`subgroupEvalAlg_mem_adjoin`, `subgroupEvalAlg_invariant`).
  - (⊇) The adjoin lies in the range of `quotientEvalAlg`, since that range is a subalgebra
    containing every `ev g`. So `φ = quotientEvalAlg c` for some `c`. Invariance under
    `s ∈ Stab` gives `∑_q c_q (ev q s − 1) · ev q = 0`. Linear independence then gives
    `ev q s = 1` for every `q` in the support of `c`, so by `hT` every such `q` comes from
    `T`. Then `c` is the `mapDomain` of its `comapDomain` (`Finsupp.mapDomain_comapDomain`),
    so `φ` is in the range of `subgroupEvalAlg`.
- `exists_algHom_of_algEquiv`: for any `ℂ`-algebra isomorphism
  `e : ℂ[T ⧸ (H₀ ∩ T)] ≃ Z`, `Ψ = subgroupEvalAlg ∘ e⁻¹` is injective. Its range is the set
  above, and `Ψ (e a) = subgroupEvalAlg a`.
- `linearMap_eq_smul_of_forall_of`: let `L` be a linear map and `f` a linear form on a monoid
  algebra, and `v` a vector. If `L (of m) = f (of m) • v` for all `m`, then `L a = f a • v`
  for all `a`. The proof is induction over the monoid algebra (`MonoidAlgebra.induction_on`).

## Unramified characters (section `Unramified`)

Let `H` be a topological group and `ν` a family of norm characters.

- `unramifiedEvalHom ν : H →* (unramifiedCharacters ν →* ℂ)` is `g ↦ (ω ↦ ω(g))`.
- `commonNormKernel_le_unramifiedEvalHom_ker`: every unramified character is trivial on
  `commonNormKernel ν`, by definition of `unramifiedCharacters`.
- `mem_commonNormKernel_of_unramifiedEvalHom_eq_one`: assume `commonNormKernel ν` is open
  and the abstract characters of `H ⧸ commonNormKernel ν` separate points. Then every `g`
  on which all unramified characters are trivial lies in `commonNormKernel ν`. Otherwise
  some character `ω` of the quotient has `ω(g) ≠ 1`. The quotient is discrete
  (`QuotientGroup.discreteTopology`), so `ω` is continuous, and its pullback
  (`quotientCharacterPullback`) is an unramified character that is nontrivial at `g`.
- `adjoin_range_unramifiedEvalHom`: the algebra generated by the functions
  `unramifiedEvalHom ν g` is `unramifiedRegularFunctions ν`. This holds by definition (`rfl`).

## Main proof

After the binders and the instances of the statement are introduced, the published
`rationalSummandCenterGroupAlgebra` (Renard VI.4.4, first sentence of the Proposition) gives
`e : ℂ[T ⧸ (H⁰ ∩ T)] ≃ₐ[ℂ] Z(End I)`. For each `t ∈ T`, `e(class of t) ≫ φ = ω(t) • φ`
for every `ω` and every `φ : I ⟶ π ⊗ ω`. The proof restates this with its own name `T` for
the subgroup, so that later rewriting matches syntactically.

- With `ν = fun χ => (rationalNormCharacters G χ).toMul` and `ev = unramifiedEvalHom ν`, the
  hypothesis `H⁰ ≤ ker ev` holds by `commonNormKernel_le_unramifiedEvalHom_ker`. The hypothesis
  `hsep` follows from `mem_commonNormKernel_of_unramifiedEvalHom_eq_one`, with openness from
  `isOpen_rationalCommonNormKernel` and point separation from the published
  `rationalNormKernelQuotient_exists_character_ne_one` (VI.4.3).
- `hT` (`g ∈ T ↔ ω(g) = 1` for all `ω ∈ Stab`) unfolds the infimum (`Subgroup.mem_iInf`,
  `MonoidHom.mem_ker`, `Units.val_eq_one`).
- `exists_algHom_of_algEquiv` gives `Ψ` with items 1 and 2. In item 2,
  `unramifiedRegularFunctions` is rewritten as the algebra generated by the `ev g`
  (`adjoin_range_unramifiedEvalHom`).
- Item 3: write `z = e a`. Fix `ω` and `φ`. Then `a ↦ e a ≫ φ` is `ℂ`-linear, by
  `Preadditive.add_comp` and `Linear.smul_comp`, and `a ↦ subgroupEvalAlg a ω` is a linear
  form. They satisfy the required identity on group elements, by the published clause of
  `rationalSummandCenterGroupAlgebra` and `subgroupEvalAlg_of`. So
  `linearMap_eq_smul_of_forall_of` gives `z ≫ φ = Ψ z ω • φ`.

Axioms used: `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`.
