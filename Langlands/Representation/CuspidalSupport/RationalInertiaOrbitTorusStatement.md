## The objects

All objects are published; this file constructs nothing. The binders are those
of the published V.2.7 Lemma statement `RationalTwistStabilizerFiniteStatement`.

- `F` is a nonarchimedean local field and `G` a connected reductive group
  scheme over `Spec F`; `G(F)` has the canonical topology.
- `X(G)` is the published `unramifiedCharacters (fun chi =>
  (rationalNormCharacters G chi).toMul)`: the continuous characters
  `G(F) → ℂˣ` trivial on the common norm kernel `⁰G`. It is a group under
  pointwise multiplication.
- Renard's `F` is the published `unramifiedRegularFunctions` for the same
  family: the `ℂ`-subalgebra of functions `X(G) → ℂ` generated by the
  evaluations `χ ↦ χ(g)`. The published `rationalUnramifiedCoordinateAlgebraEquiv`
  and `rationalUnramifiedLaurentAlgebraEquiv` (Worker 200, module
  `Plancherel/RationalUnramifiedCoordinateAlgebra`) identify it with
  `ℂ[Λ(G)]` and with the Laurent algebra
  `AddMonoidAlgebra ℂ (Fin (rationalImageRank G) → ℤ)`; that is Renard's
  `F ≃ ℂ[Λ(G)]` and the torus structure of V.2.4. `rationalImageRank G` is
  the rank of the actual valuation image `Λ(G)`.
- `π ⊗ χ` is the published `rationalUnramifiedTwist G χ A`, and `≅` is
  isomorphism of smooth representations.
- Inline: `S` is the stabilizer `X(G)(π) = {s | π ⊗ s ≅ π}`, and `invariant`
  is `F^S`, the elements `f` of `F` with `f(χ s) = f(χ)` for all `s ∈ S` and
  all `χ`. This is the action of `X(G)` on its own functions by translation,
  restricted to `S`.

## The Lean statement

For every irreducible smooth representation `A` of `G(F)`:

1. **Homogeneous space.** For all `χ, χ'`: `A ⊗ χ ≅ A ⊗ χ'` if and only if
   `χ⁻¹ χ' ∈ S`. So `χ ↦ A ⊗ χ` identifies `X(G)/S` with the inertial class
   `D` of `A`.
2. **Torus.** There is a `ℂ`-algebra homomorphism
   `Φ : ℂ[ℤ^d] → (X(G) → ℂ)`, `d = rationalImageRank G`, that is injective and
   whose range is exactly `F^S`. So `F^S`, the algebra of polynomial functions
   on `D`, is a Laurent polynomial algebra in `d` variables.
3. **Points.** Every `ℂ`-algebra homomorphism `ℂ[ℤ^d] → ℂ` is `p ↦ Φ(p)(χ)`
   for some `χ ∈ X(G)`: every complex point of `Spec F^S` comes from `X(G)`.
4. **Orbit.** For all `χ, χ'`: `A ⊗ χ ≅ A ⊗ χ'` if and only if every element
   of `F^S` takes the same value at `χ` and `χ'`.

## Why this is Renard's Corollary

By 3 and 4, `A ⊗ χ ↦ (f ↦ f(χ))` is a well-defined bijection from the
inertial class `D` onto the complex points of `Spec F^S`. By 2, `Spec F^S` is
the split torus `𝔾_m^d` over `ℂ`, of the same dimension `d` as `X(G)`. With
Renard's definition of the algebraic structure of `D` (its polynomial
functions are `F^S`, Notations paragraph), `D` is therefore a complex
algebraic torus, as the Corollary asserts. The isomorphism depends on the base
point `A` and on the choice of `Φ`, which is the non-canonicity of the source.
Clause 1 is the identification `D ≃ X(G)/X(G)(π)` of the Notations paragraph.
The statement does not restrict `Φ` further: any two choices differ by an
automorphism of the Laurent algebra, and clauses 2–4 are independent of the
choice.

Renard's proof gives these clauses as follows (not formalized here). `S` is
finite (V.2.7, Lemma). Under `F ≃ ℂ[Λ(G)]`, the evaluation at `λ ∈ Λ(G)` is an
eigenvector of the translation by `s ∈ S` with eigenvalue `s(λ)`, so `F^S` is
the span of the evaluations at the sublattice `Λ′ = {λ | s(λ) = 1 for all
s ∈ S}`. As `S` is finite, `Λ′` has finite index in `Λ(G)`, hence is free of
rank `d`, and `F^S ≃ ℂ[Λ′] ≃ ℂ[ℤ^d]`, which is clause 2. Characters of `Λ′`
extend to `Λ(G)` because `ℂˣ` is divisible, and characters of `Λ(G)` are the
unramified characters, which is clause 3. By duality for the finite group
`Λ(G)/Λ′`, `χ⁻¹ χ′` lies in `S` exactly when it is trivial on `Λ′`, which with
clause 1 gives clause 4. Clause 1 follows from the compatibility of twists
with products of characters.

## Formal choices

- **Points rather than schemes.** The Library has no scheme structure on
  `X(G)` or on inertial classes. The torus is recorded through its coordinate
  algebra (clause 2) and its complex points (clauses 3–4), which determine the
  complex algebraic torus.
- **The action.** `S` acts on functions on `X(G)` by `f ↦ (χ ↦ f(χ s))`.
  Renard's `X(G)(π)`-action on `F` is this translation action.
- **Carriers.** `A` has carrier in an arbitrary universe `uV`, as in the
  published Lemma statement.
