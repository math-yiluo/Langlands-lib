# Actual standard cuspidal data and their inertial quotient

Author: Worker 1153.

This module constructs actual cuspidal data and their proved inertial equivalence quotient for an arbitrary connected reductive group scheme over a nonarchimedean local field. The source is David Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, 9 July 2026 version, VI.7.1, printed pp.180–181 / PDF pp.186–187, especially the definition immediately following the finite-fibre proposition on printed p.180. Registered source: `renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, SHA256 `7b539996793b7c3620e72e1e389588eb42d1a8e91d6f68b2eead5c2e2ffc182c`. The implementation is a derived construction using the published standard rational-point family, actual represented standard Levi schemes and same-carrier full rational unramified twists.

`BernsteinStandardFamily i ν` is a proposition recording structural hypotheses for the actual family indexed by finite subsets of the actual simple relative roots of the maximal split torus `i` and generic cocharacter `ν`. It includes connected reductivity, maximality, genericity, finiteness of those actual roots, the ambient semidirect decompositions and closedness of the actual unipotents and Levis, and the actual Levi-in-Levi decompositions. The ambient rational-point topology is the canonical affine evaluation topology. Local compactness and the nonarchimedean-group property of that topology are explicit instance hypotheses. The family contains no representation, block, support theorem or Bernstein decomposition. Its structural hypotheses can be supplied separately from the published standard-family construction statements; they are not proved in this module.

For a finite root subset `I`, `bernsteinLeviScheme i ν I` is exactly `Standard.leviScheme i ν (rootSet i ν I)`, embedded by `Standard.leviHom`. The standard closed immersion makes this scheme affine; the proved `BernsteinStandardFamily.locallyOfFiniteType` derives local finite type of the ambient group from connected reductivity. The inertia declarations take this as an explicit instance hypothesis, and composition with the closed immersion derives local finite type of each Levi. These provide the canonical topology and the hypotheses of full rational unramified twisting, without a chosen character subfamily.

A `BernsteinCuspidalDatum` consists of `roots = I`, an actual smooth representation `ρ` of that Levi scheme's rational points, a proof of irreducibility, and a proof of the published `Langlands.Aubert.General.IsLeviCuspidal` predicate. That predicate is applied after transport through the inverse canonical `leviRationalPointHomeomorph`, so it says that every normalized Jacquet module for a proper standard sub-Levi vanishes. The action and its transport have the same vector-space carrier. There is no admissibility or finite-length premise. Cuspidality relative to the actual standard family is the interface used here; comparison with vanishing for every represented rational parabolic remains a separate structural/functor comparison.

`IsBernsteinInertiaRelated a b` requires an actual ambient rational point and a group-scheme isomorphism of their embedded Levi schemes satisfying the all-test conjugacy equation of `RationalLeviConjugacy`. It then requires the actual source-Levi full rational inertia relation between `a.representation` and the pullback of `b.representation`. The proved `isBernsteinInertiaRelated_iff` rewrites this as the target-Levi relation: `b.representation` is isomorphic to an unramified twist of `a.representation` transported through the inverse conjugating isomorphism. Unfolding the imported relation gives precisely an actual full rational unramified character of the target Levi and an actual smooth equivariant isomorphism. Thus this is Renard's equation `g M₁ g⁻¹ = M₂`, `ρ₂ ≃ ρ₁ᵍ ⊗ ω`, expressed algebraically, rather than a relation supplied as a parameter.

Reflexivity uses identity conjugation and the trivial twist. Symmetry uses the inverse conjugacy and the proved source/target inertia comparison. Transitivity transports the second inertia witness to the first Levi, multiplies twists and composes their actual isomorphisms, then uses the actual pullback-composition isomorphism. `bernsteinInertiaSetoid` contains these proved laws. `BernsteinInertialClass` is its genuine quotient; `bernsteinInertialClass_eq_iff` is the exact equality criterion for representatives.

The field/group universe `uF` and carrier universe `uV` are independent throughout. This pair does not construct a variety structure, prove a cuspidal-support uniqueness or existence theorem, or assert Bernstein decomposition. Normalized induction, irreducible support and full block categories are separate constructions following this quotient.
