Renard's text, for `A = H(G, ρ)`, `ρ` an irreducible summand of `Res_{⁰G}^G π`:

- for `x ∈ G`, `F_x(ρ)` is the space of `φ ∈ A` with support in `⁰G x`; for `φ ∈ F_x(ρ)`,
  `φ(x) ρ(x⁻¹ g x) = φ(gx) = ρ(g) φ(x)` (`g ∈ ⁰G`), so `φ ↦ φ(x)` is a linear isomorphism
  `F_x(ρ) ≃ Hom_{⁰G}(ρ^x, ρ)`; hence `dim F_x(ρ) ≤ 1`, with equality if and only if `x` lies
  in the stabilizer `N = N_G(ρ)`, and `A = H(N, ρ)`;
- (VI.4.3.1) for `φ`, `ψ` supported in `⁰G x`, `⁰G y` (`x, y ∈ N`),
  `Supp φ * ψ ⊂ ⁰G xy` and `φ * ψ(xy) = φ(x) ψ(y)`; a nonzero such `φ` is invertible, with
  inverse supported in `⁰G x⁻¹`; since `⁰G \ G` is abelian, `φ * ψ = c ψ * φ` for some
  `c ∈ ℂˣ`;
- since `N` contains `⁰G Z(G)`, of finite index in `G` (V.2.6), `⁰G \ N` is a free abelian group
  of the same rank as `Λ(G) = ⁰G \ G`; for a basis `⁰G x₁, …, ⁰G xₙ` and nonzero
  `φᵢ ∈ F_{xᵢ}(ρ)`, (VI.4.3.2) `φᵢ * φⱼ = c_{ij} φⱼ * φᵢ`; `A` is generated by the `φᵢ`, every
  element is a linear combination of monomials in the `φᵢ` and their inverses ("une algèbre
  de polynômes de Laurent « tordue »"), and (VI.4.3.3) the units of `A` are the monomials
  `c φ₁^{e₁} * ⋯ * φₙ^{eₙ}`, `c ∈ ℂˣ`, `eᵢ ∈ ℤ`.

## Statement

The binders are those of the published `RationalNormKernelHeckeStatement`: a nonarchimedean
local field `F`, a connected reductive `G` over `F` with the canonical topology on `G(F)`, and
an irreducible smooth complex representation `ρ` of `H = ⁰G`. Write `A`, `conv` for
`rationalNormKernelHecke G ρ` and its convolution, and:

- `InF x φ`: `φ ∈ A` and `φ(g) ≠ 0` implies `g x⁻¹ ∈ H`, that is, `φ ∈ F_x(ρ)` (support in
  `H x`);
- `IsTwistHom x T`: `T ∘ ρ(h') = ρ(h) ∘ T` whenever `h, h' ∈ H` and `h' = x⁻¹ h x`, that is,
  `T ∈ Hom_H(ρ^x, ρ)` with `ρ^x(h) = ρ(x⁻¹ h x)`;
- `InN x`: some bijective `T` satisfies `IsTwistHom x T`, that is, `ρ^x ≅ ρ`, Renard's
  `x ∈ N`;
- `IsLatticeBasis n b S` (for `b : Fin n → G(F)` and `S ⊆ G(F)`): every `g ∈ S` is congruent
  modulo `H` to exactly one product `b₀^{e₀} ⋯ b_{n-1}^{e_{n-1}}` with `e : Fin n → ℤ`.

The statement asserts that there is `ε : G(F) → End ρ` with `InF 1 ε`, `ε(1) = 1` and
`conv ε φ = φ = conv φ ε` for every `φ ∈ A` (the unit of `A`), such that:

1. for every `x`: `φ ↦ φ(x)` is injective on `F_x(ρ)`, and `T ∈ Hom_H(ρ^x, ρ)` exactly when
   `T = φ(x)` for some `φ ∈ F_x(ρ)` (Renard's isomorphism `F_x(ρ) ≃ Hom_{⁰G}(ρ^x, ρ)`);
2. for every `x`: any two elements of `F_x(ρ)`, the first nonzero, are proportional
   (`dim F_x(ρ) ≤ 1`), and `F_x(ρ) ≠ 0` exactly when `x ∈ N`;
3. every `φ ∈ A` vanishes off `N` (`A = H(N, ρ)`);
4. (VI.4.3.1) for `φ ∈ F_x(ρ)` and `ψ ∈ F_y(ρ)`: `conv φ ψ ∈ F_{xy}(ρ)` and
   `conv φ ψ (xy) = φ(x) ψ(y)`;
5. a nonzero `φ ∈ F_x(ρ)` has a two-sided inverse `ψ ∈ F_{x⁻¹}(ρ)` (`conv φ ψ = ε = conv ψ φ`);
6. (VI.4.3.2) for nonzero `φ ∈ F_x(ρ)` and `ψ ∈ F_y(ρ)` there is `c ≠ 0` with
   `conv φ ψ = c • conv ψ φ`;
7. there is a subgroup `N` of `G(F)` with `g ∈ N ↔ InN g` and `H ≤ N`, and a natural number
   `n` with a lattice basis of `G(F)` modulo `H` (`Λ(G) ≅ ℤⁿ`) and a lattice basis of `N`
   modulo `H` made of elements of `N` (`H \ N ≅ ℤⁿ`): `H \ N` is free abelian of the same rank
   as `Λ(G)`;
8. `A` is the span of the `F_x(ρ)`, and an element of `A` has a two-sided inverse in `A`
   exactly when it is nonzero and lies in some `F_x(ρ)`.

## Correspondence

- **The unit.** Renard uses the unit `1_A` without naming it. The statement asserts its
  existence and characterizes it as the element of `F_1(ρ)` with value `1` at `1`; it is the
  function `h ↦ ρ(h)` on `H`, zero elsewhere. All invertibility claims refer to it.
- **Products and the order of factors.** The products `b₀^{e₀} ⋯` in item 7 are taken in a
  fixed order with `List.ofFn`; since `H \ G` is abelian (V.2.6), the order does not matter
  modulo `H`.
- **(VI.4.3.2) and the twisted Laurent algebra.** Renard states (VI.4.3.2) for chosen basis
  elements `φᵢ` and deduces it from the general commutation rule; item 6 is the general rule.
  The description of `A` as a twisted Laurent polynomial algebra in `φ₁, …, φₙ` is item 8's
  span statement together with items 2, 4 and 7: `A = ⨁_{x ∈ H\N} F_x(ρ)` with one-dimensional
  pieces multiplying by (VI.4.3.1), so each `F_x(ρ)` is spanned by the monomial in the `φᵢ`
  and their inverses whose support is `H x`.
- **(VI.4.3.3).** By items 2, 4 and 5, the monomials `c φ₁^{e₁} * ⋯ * φₙ^{eₙ}` with `c ≠ 0` are
  exactly the nonzero elements of the `F_x(ρ)`, `x ∈ N`. Item 8 states (VI.4.3.3) in this
  basis-free form. It is equivalent to Renard's for every choice of the basis and of the
  `φᵢ`.

## Faithfulness notes

- **Generality of `ρ`.** Renard's `ρ` is an irreducible summand of `Res_{⁰G}^G π` for an
  irreducible supercuspidal `π`. As in the published `RationalNormKernelHeckeStatement`, the
  statement is made for every irreducible smooth `ρ` of `⁰G`. Renard's arguments use only the
  irreducibility of `ρ` (through Schur's lemma for `Hom(ρ^x, ρ)`), the normality of `⁰G`, the
  commutativity of `⁰G \ G` and the finite index of `⁰G Z(G)` (V.2.6); the centre `Z(G)(F)`
  centralizes `⁰G`, so it lies in `N` for every `ρ`.
- **The convolution.** The published convolution uses the representatives `q.out`. On `A` the
  value does not depend on this choice; the statement does not assert this.
- **Not stated.** The Lemma and Corollary of VI.4.3 are the published statements listed above.
  Renard's identification of the simple right `A`-modules with `Hom_{⁰G}(ρ, Res(π ⊗ ω))`
  (proof of the Corollary) is not stated here.

## Remaining obligations

The proof is a separate obligation. Renard's argument is the direct computation sketched in the
source; items 7 and 8 need, in addition, that `Λ(G)` is free abelian of finite rank (V.2.3,
V.2.6), that a finite-index subgroup of `ℤⁿ` is free of rank `n`, and that the units of a
twisted group algebra of `ℤⁿ` are its nonzero homogeneous elements.
