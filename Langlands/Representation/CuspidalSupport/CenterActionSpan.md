# The operator span of the categorical centre

Author: Worker 87. Internal supporting construction for Renard VI.10.8; this is not a proof of any of the four Task 5227 targets.

`centerOperatorSpan A` is the scalar span of the actual linear endomorphisms `SmoothRep.hom (z.app A)` for `z` in Mathlib's `CatCenter` of the category of smooth representations in the same carrier universe as `A`. `IsCenterAdmissible` packages exactly the explicit finite-generation formula on compact-open fixed vectors used by the published VI.10.7 and VI.10.8 statements.

All results retain independent coefficient, group and carrier universes. Coefficients may be any field, and the group may have any topology until compact-open cofinality is explicitly needed. These are elementary internal constructions, motivated by David Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques* (9 July 2026), VI.10.8, printed p.217 / PDF p.223, registered source `renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, SHA256 `7b539996793b7c3620e72e1e389588eb42d1a8e91d6f68b2eead5c2e2ffc182c`.

The implemented proofs are by linear algebra. Span induction shows every centre-span operator commutes with the group action, so evaluating it on a fixed vector remains in the fixed subspace. Evaluation on a finite group-algebra generating family is injective: extend each operator to a group-algebra-linear map and use equality on generators. For a smooth representation of finite type, the published `commonFixedGenerators` supplies such generators in one open-fixed subspace. If the representation is admissible, that fixed subspace is finite-dimensional. Its finite power therefore contains an injective image of the centre operator span, proving that the latter is finite-dimensional. This route does not use Howe's theorem or Schur's lemma.

Conversely, when the centre operator span is finite-dimensional, any fixed space generated by finitely many centre orbits is the image of a finite power of that span under the sum of evaluation maps. This proves finite-dimensionality. For a weakly locally compact nonarchimedean group, compact-open subgroups are cofinal in all open subgroups, so the compact-open conclusion gives `IsAdmissible`, which here tests every open subgroup. Finally an admissible representation is centre admissible because a finite scalar generating family is also generated by scalar natural transformations in `CatCenter`.

The missing reductive inputs (the finite-type centre-admissibility theorem, Bernstein decomposition and the exact Howe finite-length implication) are neither proved nor assumed away. The elementary implications here do not establish finite length or admissible-plus-centre-finite implies finite type.
