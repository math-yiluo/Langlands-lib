## The source

**Corollary.** Let `K` be a compact open subgroup of `G`. Then the Hecke algebra `H(G, K)` is a finitely generated module over `ℨ(G)`. In particular, `H(G, K)` is a finitely generated module over its centre, which contains `e_K ∗ ℨ(G) ∗ e_K`.

In the proof, Renard takes the representation `𝒟(G/K)` of compactly supported locally constant functions on `G/K`, with `G` acting by left translation. It is generated by the characteristic function of `K`, and `𝒟(G/K)^K ≃ 𝒟(G, K) ≃ H(G, K)` as `ℨ(G)`-modules. The Corollary then follows from the VI.10.7 Theorem: representations of finite type are `ℨ(G)`-admissible.

## The Lean statement

`HeckeAlgebraCenterFiniteStatement` uses the same group data as the companion VI.10.7 Theorem statement `FiniteTypeCenterAdmissibleStatement` (Worker 6, candidate `e9b7e8ee-d4da-4c47-a6ad-76d84a3d0e18`):

- a nonarchimedean local field `k : Type u`, of any characteristic;
- a group scheme `G` over `Spec k` with `hG : IsConnectedReductive G`, whose rational points carry the canonical topology `RationalPointFamily.pointTopology` and are assumed locally compact and nonarchimedean;
- `K : OpenSubgroup (rationalPoints G)` with `IsCompact (K : Set _)`.

It uses three published objects.

- `D` is the published `Induction.smoothPermutation ℂ ℂ K K.isOpen`, the smooth representation of `G(F)` on finitely supported functions `G(F)/K →₀ ℂ`, with `(g a)(z) = a(g⁻¹ z)`. Since `G(F)/K` is discrete, this is Renard's `𝒟(G/K)` with left translation. It lies in `SmoothRep.{0, u, u}`.
- `H` is the published `Shimura.LevelHecke.LevelHeckeAlgebra ℂ K`, the ring of `K`-invariant elements of `ℂ[G(F)/K]`, i.e. the `K`-fixed vectors of `D`, with convolution. `emb h` is the underlying function `G(F)/K →₀ ℂ` of `h`, a `K`-fixed vector of `D`.
- `ℨ(G)` is Mathlib's `CatCenter (SmoothRep.{0, u, u} ℂ (rationalPoints G))`, the centre of the category of smooth representations, as in the companion statement. An element `z` acts on `D` through `SmoothRep.hom (z.app D)`.

The conclusion is the conjunction of:

1. **Finite over `ℨ(G)`.** There are `n` and `s : Fin n → H` such that every `h : H` satisfies `emb h = ∑ i, (z i)_D (emb (s i))` for some `z : Fin n → ℨ(G)`. This is the first sentence of the Corollary.
2. **`ℨ(G)` acts through the centre of `H(G, K)`.** For every `z ∈ ℨ(G)` there is `c ∈ Subring.center H` with `z_D (emb h) = emb (c * h)` for all `h`. The element `c` is `z_D(δ_K)`, Renard's `e_K ∗ z ∗ e_K`. This says that the centre of `H(G, K)` contains the image of `ℨ(G)`.
3. **Finite over its centre.** `Module.Finite (Subring.center H) H`.

## Remarks

- `LevelHeckeAlgebra` is normalized by counting measure on `G(F)/K`: its unit is `δ_K`, and no Haar measure appears. Renard's `H(G, K) = e_K ∗ H(G) ∗ e_K` uses a Haar measure. The two algebras are isomorphic by rescaling functions by `vol(K)`, and finiteness and centres are unaffected.
- Part 1 is the VI.10.7 Theorem for the cyclic representation `D`, generated by `δ_K`. Part 2 uses that `z` commutes with every `G`-endomorphism of `D`, in particular with right convolution by `h`, and with the action of `H(G, K)` on `K`-fixed vectors. Part 3 follows from parts 1 and 2. None of this is formalized here.
- The VI.10.7 Variant, for `(G, B)`-modules, is not stated here.
