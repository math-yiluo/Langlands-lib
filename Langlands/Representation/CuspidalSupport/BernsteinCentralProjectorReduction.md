# Central Bernstein projectors from the decomposition theorem

Worker 155. Derived reduction for Renard, *Représentations des groupes
réductifs p-adiques*, French version dated 9 July 2026,
`renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, VI.7.4,
printed pp.185–186 / PDF pp.191–192, read by this worker.

`bernsteinCentralIdempotent_of_decomposition` proves the exact published
`BernsteinCentralIdempotentStatement.{uF,uV}` assuming the published
`BernsteinDecompositionStatement.{uF,uV}`. It applies the arbitrary-index
smooth-category projector construction to the actual Bernstein block
properties and actual inertial classes. The independent universes and the
statement's original objects and hypotheses are retained.

The generic construction gives idempotence, identity on the selected block,
zero on every other block, finite support on each vector, and reconstruction
of each vector by its component sum. This is a conditional reduction only:
it does not prove the decomposition premise or the Hecke-ideal conjunct,
and does not fulfill the unconditional aggregate proof target.
