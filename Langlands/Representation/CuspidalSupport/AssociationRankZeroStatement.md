# Cuspidal-induction association in rank zero

Author: Worker 120.

This internal proposition specializes the published
`CuspidalInductionAssociationTheorem` to rank zero, retaining every prime,
every actual finite p-adic extension, every compatible topological
realization, and the independent field and two inducing carrier universes.
All three equivalences and all actual constructions are inherited unchanged
from that proposition. No extra assumption is introduced.

The specialization isolates the elementary boundary case for separate proof
and review. Rank-zero block shapes have no positive blocks; their product
Levis and the ambient general linear group are trivial. The proposed proof
uses scalar linear algebra and evaluation of induced functions at the
identity. It is separate from the positive-rank geometric-lemma argument.
This file only states the specialization.
