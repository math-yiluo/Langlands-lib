# Proof of the sandwich structure of compact-open Hecke corners of `G°`

This module proves the published proposition
`Langlands.CuspidalSupport.DeterminantUnitCornerStructureStatement.{uF}` as
`Langlands.CuspidalSupport.determinantUnitCornerStructure`. The proposition is
Bernstein--Zelevinsky (1976), Lemma 4.9, page 39, for the determinant-unit
subgroup `G° = determinantUnitSubgroup T B` of a product Levi
`B.Levi F = ∏ i, GL_{B.size i}(F)`.

The proof follows BZ76's route: compact open levels normalized by a compact
subgroup, dominant elements, and a Cartan decomposition. The level `N` is the
blockwise principal congruence subgroup of a large positive level. Then the
generic theorem `Hecke.HeckeAlgebra.Corner.exists_sandwich_of_decomposition`
(module `Langlands.Representation.Hecke.DoubleCosetSandwich`) gives the
sandwich structure. The main part is proved for any subgroup `G` of the Levi
cut out by valuation-one block determinants, over any nonarchimedean local
field `k`. The final theorem specializes it to `G°`.

## Determinants (section `Determinant`)

- `determinant_torus`: `det (diag(ϖ ^ lam i)) = ϖ ^ ∑ lam i`
  (`Matrix.det_diagonal`).
- `valuation_determinant_of_mem_integralRange`: an integral invertible matrix
  has determinant of valuation one, since its determinant is a unit of `O`.
- `sum_eq_zero_of_valuation_determinant_torus`: if `v(ϖ) < 1` and
  `v(det diag(ϖ ^ lam i)) = 1`, then `∑ lam i = 0`.
- `valuation_determinant_torus_of_sum_eq_zero`: the converse, for any `ϖ`.

## Blockwise subgroups and generators (section `Levi`)

- `integralLevi k B`: the product of the `GL_{B.size i}(O)`.
- `principalLevi k B a`: the product of the principal levels
  `rankPrincipalLevel k (B.size i) a`. `principalLevi_le_integralLevi` holds.
- `GeneratorIndex B`: pairs of a block `i` and a member of
  `dominantGenerators (B.size i)`. It is `Finite` by
  `dominantGenerators_finite`.
- `leviGenerator ϖ j`: the Levi element equal to `diag(ϖ ^ s)` in block `i`
  and the identity elsewhere, for `j = ⟨i, s⟩`.
  - `leviGenerator_apply`: every block of it is a dominant zero-sum torus
    element.
- `mulSingle_torus_mem_closure`, `torusFamily_mem_closure`: a Levi element
  whose blocks are all dominant zero-sum torus elements lies in the monoid
  generated by the `leviGenerator ϖ j`.
  - The proof first does one block, by induction on the additive closure from
    `mem_closure_dominantGenerators` with `torus_add`.
  - It then takes products over a finite set of blocks (`Finset.induction_on`).

Now fix a subgroup `G` and `hG : g ∈ G ↔ ∀ i, v(det g i) = 1`.

- `integralLevi_le`: block-integral elements lie in `G`.
- `leviGenerator_mem`: the generators lie in `G`.
- `dominantMonoid G ϖ`: the submonoid of `G` of elements whose every block is
  `diag(ϖ ^ lam)` with `lam` antitone.
  - `mem_dominantMonoid` unfolds membership.
  - `dominantMonoid_commute`: its elements commute pairwise.
- `dominantMonoid_sandwich`: assume `a > 0` and `v(ϖ) ≤ 1`. Let `m₁, m₂` be
  dominant and `x` in `N_a = (principalLevi k B a).subgroupOf G`. Then
  `m₁ x m₂ = y (m₁ m₂) z` with `y, z ∈ N_a`. This applies
  `exists_dominant_sandwich` (module `Langlands.Group.GL.PrincipalLevelDominance`)
  block by block.
- `exists_levi_cartan`: let `ϖ` be a uniformizer. Every `g ∈ G` is `γ m γ'`
  with `γ, γ'` block-integral and `m` in the monoid generated by the
  generators.
  1. In each block, the published Cartan decomposition
     `Cartan.cartanDecomposition` writes `g i = k₁ diag(ϖ ^ lam i) k₂` with
     `lam i` antitone and `k₁, k₂` integral.
  2. The middle element `t = γ⁻¹ g γ'⁻¹` lies in `G`, so each block determinant
     has valuation one. By `sum_eq_zero_of_valuation_determinant_torus`, each
     `lam i` has zero sum.
  3. `torusFamily_mem_closure` puts `t` in the generated monoid of the Levi.
     `MonoidHom.map_mclosure` moves this to the generated monoid in `G`.

## The corner structure (section `Corner`)

`exists_cornerStructure G hG K hK` holds for a nonarchimedean local field `k`
and any Borel measurable structure on `G`. The conclusion is the body of
`DeterminantUnitCornerStructureStatement` with `G` in place of `G°`.

1. Choose a uniformizer `ϖ` (`Iwasawa.exists_isUniformizingElement`).
2. `K` is open in the subspace topology. So some open `U` of the Levi with
   `U ∩ G = K` contains a box `∏ tᵢ` of identity neighborhoods.
3. `exists_pos_rankPrincipalLevel_subset` gives levels `c i > 0` with the
   principal level `c i` inside `tᵢ`. Let `r = 1 + sup c i`.
4. The level is `N = (principalLevi k B r).subgroupOf G`.
   - It is open, as the preimage of a finite product of open sets.
   - It is compact, because its image in the Levi is a product of compact sets.
   - `N ≤ K` because principal levels decrease (`rankPrincipalLevel_antitone`).
5. `Γ = (integralLevi k B).subgroupOf G` is compact
   (`isCompact_integralRange`). It normalizes `N` by
   `conj_mem_rankPrincipalLevel_iff` in each block.
6. Take `M = dominantMonoid G ϖ`. Its dominance property is
   `dominantMonoid_sandwich` with `r > 0`.
7. The generators are `leviGenerator ϖ j` for `j : GeneratorIndex B`,
   reindexed by `Fin l` (`Finite.exists_equiv_fin`). They lie in `M` and
   commute pairwise.
8. The decomposition hypothesis is `exists_levi_cartan`.
9. `exists_sandwich_of_decomposition` gives `q`, `x` and the commuting `a` for
   every Haar measure `μ`.

## The final theorem

- `normalizedAbs_eq_one_iff`: for `x ≠ 0`, `T.normalizedAbs x = 1` exactly
  when `v(x) = 1`. Both conditions say that `x` and `x⁻¹` lie in `O`. The proof
  uses `T.normalizedAbs_le_one_iff_mem_integers` and multiplicativity.
- `mem_determinantUnitSubgroup_iff_valuation`: `g ∈ G°` exactly when every
  block determinant has valuation one. It combines the published
  `mem_determinantUnitSubgroup_iff_normalizedAbs` with the previous lemma.
- `determinantUnitCornerStructure : DeterminantUnitCornerStructureStatement.{uF}`.
  After introducing `p`, `F`, `T`, `n`, `B`, it installs the topology,
  valuative relation, local-field structure, Hausdorffness and the Borel
  measurable structure of the statement on `F.Carrier` and `G°`. Then it
  applies `exists_cornerStructure` to `G°` with
  `mem_determinantUnitSubgroup_iff_valuation`.
