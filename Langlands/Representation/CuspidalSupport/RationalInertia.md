# Actual rational-point inertia classes and full smooth subcategories

This construction implements the inertia relation and the two object properties used in Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, French edition dated 9 July 2026, V.2.7, printed pp.108–109 / PDF pp.114–115, and VI.3.3, printed pp.144–145 / PDF pp.150–151. The registered source is `renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, SHA256 `7b539996793b7c3620e72e1e389588eb42d1a8e91d6f68b2eead5c2e2ffc182c`.

The group is the actual rational-point group of an affine locally finite-type group scheme over an arbitrary nonarchimedean local field, with the canonical rational-point topology. These constructions do not require reductivity; thus they apply in particular to the connected reductive groups of the source. The field and representation-carrier universes `uF` and `uV` are independent. All representations are complex and smooth. No admissibility, finite length, finite dimension, central character or lattice-image premise is imposed.

`rationalUnramifiedTwist` reuses `SmoothRep.twist` and the existing group `Plancherel.unramifiedCharacters` for the norms of **all actual rational algebraic characters**. It introduces no substitute structural data. Each character is trivial on `rationalCommonNormKernel`; its kernel is open by inclusion of that proved open subgroup. Hence the existing twist is smooth in the canonical topology. The continuity in the character definition does not exclude any source character trivial on this open kernel: such a character factors through the discrete quotient, as constructed in `RationalNormKernelTopology` and `UnramifiedCharacters`.

`IsRationalInertiaRelated G A B` means there is an actual unramified character `χ` and an actual smooth isomorphism `B ≅ A ⊗ χ`. The relation is defined on all smooth objects and used to define a setoid and quotient on the subtype of irreducible smooth objects. `RationalIrreducibleInertiaClass` therefore retains all irreducible smooth representations in the chosen carrier universe, without requiring admissibility. Equality of the quotient images is proved equivalent to the original existential relation. This realizes the orbit definition in V.2.7. Restricting the base representation to an irreducible supercuspidal object gives the relation used in VI.3.3; this module does not construct a separate quotient on the supercuspidal subtype or prove preservation of supercuspidality under twisting.

`rationalInertiaBlockProperty G π A` quantifies over **every** irreducible actual subquotient `D` of `A` and requires it to be inertia-related to `π`. `rationalInertiaComplementProperty` requires that none is related. They use `SmoothRep.IsSubquotient`, which records actual invariant submodules, actual module quotients and an isomorphism. `RationalInertiaBlock` and `RationalInertiaComplement` are the corresponding full subcategories. For an irreducible supercuspidal base `π`, these are exactly the definitions of the two full subcategories immediately preceding VI.3.3.1. The predicates remain meaningful for other bases, without asserting that a product decomposition then exists.

The intrinsic laws are derived construction content. Reflexivity uses the trivial character; symmetry twists an actual isomorphism by the inverse character; transitivity twists it by the next character and uses the product-twist isomorphism. Irreducibility preservation is the existing character-twist theorem. Both object properties are invariant under isomorphism, closed under actual subquotients by `smoothSubquotientTransitivity`, and closed under subrepresentation–quotient extensions by `SmoothRep.irreducibleSubquotient_dichotomy`. Both depend only on the inertia class of the base, by symmetry and transitivity of the relation. If an object satisfies both properties, the published theorem that every nonzero smooth object has an irreducible subquotient implies its carrier is a subsingleton. These are direct deductions about the constructions; they are not presented as separately numbered Renard theorems.

The module reuses published `Category.Twist`, the actual character and norm-kernel topology modules, and the published subquotient algebra and existence proofs. The closure-law organization follows the existing `Aubert.General.SupportCategory` pattern, but its predicate fixes an unramified orbit rather than a Levi support type. No existing source or declaration is edited.

The construction does not prove VI.3.2, identify norm-kernel restriction types with these classes, construct the VI.3.3 projectors, or establish the category product VI.3.3.1. The finite stabilizer, complex algebraic torus and invariant-coordinate-ring assertions of V.2.7 and VI.3.3, the bijection with conjugacy orbits of compact restriction types, and all supercuspidality/structural comparisons remain separate obligations.
