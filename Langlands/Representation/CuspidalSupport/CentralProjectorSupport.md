# Finite support of categorical central projectors

Author: Worker 87. Internal supporting construction for the centre-finite implication in Renard VI.10.8, not a construction of Bernstein projectors or a proof of any exact Task 5227 target.

`centralOperatorSupport A e` is the set of indices at which the supplied actual categorical-centre transformation acts by a nonzero linear endomorphism on the actual smooth representation `A`. Coefficients are any field; group, representation and index universes are independent. The family is supplied explicitly, and its existence or orthogonality for the Bernstein decomposition is not assumed without being displayed as a hypothesis of the relevant helper.

The linear-algebra proof first shows that a nonzero pairwise orthogonal idempotent family of linear operators is linearly independent. Apply one projection to a zero finite linear combination: orthogonality kills every other term and idempotence leaves its own coefficient times that nonzero projection, so that coefficient vanishes. Restricting the family of categorical-centre operators to its nonzero support embeds this linearly independent family in `centerOperatorSpan A`. Finite dimension of that span implies that the index subtype is finite, proving finiteness of `centralOperatorSupport A e`.

The second group of lemmas uses only naturality of the categorical centre. If an operator vanishes on an ambient representation, injectivity of an inclusion makes it vanish on the subrepresentation. Surjectivity makes vanishing pass to a quotient. Applying those steps and transporting along the actual isomorphism in `SmoothRep.IsSubquotient` gives vanishing on every actual subquotient. Therefore any central operator acting identically on a nonzero subquotient acts nontrivially on the original representation.

These are internal linear-algebra and naturality facts supporting the last paragraph of David Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, 9 July 2026, VI.10.8, printed p.217 / PDF p.223; registered source `renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, SHA256 `7b539996793b7c3620e72e1e389588eb42d1a8e91d6f68b2eead5c2e2ffc182c`. In the source, central projectors select Bernstein components, and a centre-finite representation can have only finitely many nonzero components. Applying the helpers to those components requires the actual Bernstein projectors, their orthogonality and the comparison of an irreducible constituent's inertial support with its block. Those representation-theoretic inputs remain separate obligations; the generic supplied family here does not replace them.
