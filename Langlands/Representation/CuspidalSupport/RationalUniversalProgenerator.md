For an affine group scheme G locally of finite type over an arbitrary nonarchimedean local field F, write H=G(F) with the canonical rational-point topology and H⁰=`Plancherel.rationalCommonNormKernel G`. This is the intersection of the kernels of **all** normalized rational-character norms. No finite norm subfamily is substituted. The objects are defined for every smooth complex π, with no irreducibility, cuspidality, admissibility, splitness or characteristic-zero premise. The weaker geometric assumptions here suffice for the constructions; the subsequent representation-theoretic statements specialize to connected reductive G.

`RationalNormKernelCompactFunctions G π` is literally
`Induction.CompactFunctions H⁰ (π.representation.comp H⁰.subtype)`.
An element is an actual function f:H→π satisfying covariance
f(kx)=π(k)f(x), having one open right stabilizer for the whole function, and having compact projected nonzero support in the left coset space H⁰\H. `rationalNormKernelCompactRepresentation` is the existing `Induction.compactInduced` on that carrier. `rationalNormKernelCompactInduction` bundles this same action with the published smoothness proof. This is the source's alternative Π₁, induced from the entire restriction of π. No summand of the restriction is selected.

`rationalNormKernelCompactEval` evaluates the underlying function. Three accompanying theorems expose covariance, compact projected support, and the action formula (g·f)(x)=f(xg). Covariance and support are the actual nested subtype certificates; translation is definitional. `rationalNormKernelCompactInductionFunctor` composes the published subgroup restriction, inclusion into algebraic representations, and compact-induction functors. It acts by postcomposition on functions, and its object equality with the bundled candidate is proved by reflexivity. No comparison isomorphism is an input to this construction.

`rationalUnramifiedRegularAlgebra G` is the existing concrete algebra B of complex-valued functions on actual continuous unramified characters, generated by all evaluations b_g(χ)=χ(g). The imported `RationalUnramifiedCoordinateAlgebra` identifies this algebra with the group algebra of the actual integer valuation image using the proved continuity of the canonical projection; for a connected reductive group it also identifies it with the corresponding finite-variable Laurent algebra. This file uses the concrete evaluation algebra directly and makes no new algebraic-torus identification.

`RationalUniversalTensor G π` is the actual carrier B⊗[ℂ]π. `rationalUniversalTensorRepresentation` restricts each operator of the published B-linear `universalUnramifiedRepresentation` to complex scalars. Its operator on a pure tensor is

g·(b⊗v)=(b_g b)⊗π(g)v.

The representation's identity and multiplication laws follow by applying the corresponding published B-linear operator identities to a tensor. Its pure-tensor formula is definitional. Smoothness reuses `isSmooth_universalUnramifiedRepresentation` and the proved `isOpen_rationalCommonNormKernel`; no openness premise is added. `rationalUniversalTensorSmoothRep` bundles that action over ℂ. Restricting scalars does not change the operators or tensor carrier.

The field/group universe `uF` and representation-carrier universe `uV` are independent. Complex scalars remain ℂ in universe zero. Both the actual function space and B⊗π have carrier universe `max uF uV`, which is displayed in their declarations and in both smooth bundles. It has carrier `ULift.{uF} π`, with action transported by `up` and `down`; this existing functor is fully faithful. There is no identification of categories at different fixed universes and no implicit resizing of π.
