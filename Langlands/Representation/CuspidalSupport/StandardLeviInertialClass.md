## Standard cuspidal data and the relative quotient

The input is the published `BernsteinStandardFamily i ν` on the actual standard parabolic and Levi subgroups of the connected reductive group. Here `i` is the maximal split torus embedding and `ν` is the generic cocharacter specifying positivity. For a finite set `J` of simple relative roots, `StandardLeviCuspidalDatum i ν family J` is a `BernsteinCuspidalDatum` together with inclusion of its root label in `J`. A datum retains its represented Levi scheme `L = M_I` for `I ⊆ J`, its actual smooth irreducible complex representation of `L(F)`, and its standard normalized-Jacquet cuspidality proof. This is the nested standard-family presentation in `M_J`, not a second choice of torus, positive roots, or Levi representatives for `M_J`.

`IsStandardLeviInertiaRelated` uses the same `RationalLeviConjugacy` as ambient Bernstein inertia, but requires the conjugating point to belong to `levi i ν J`, the actual subgroup `M_J(F)` of `G(F)`. In particular, the witness is not an abstract automorphism of a point group: the isomorphism of Levi schemes intertwines their embeddings by conjugation on all tests. The source-side relation compares the first representation with the pullback of the second along that isomorphism, up to a full rational unramified twist of the inducing Levi. The published `rationalInertiaRelated_pullback_iff` exchanges this with the equivalent target-side comparison used by Renard. A point outside `M_J(F)` cannot serve as the conjugator, and the unramified character of the inducing Levi need not extend to `M_J`.

The relative relation is reflexive by identity conjugation and the trivial character, symmetric by inverse conjugation and the transported inverse twist, and transitive by composing the conjugators and transporting and multiplying the twists. The conjugating points remain in `M_J(F)` by its subgroup laws. `standardLeviInertiaSetoid` packages these laws. `StandardLeviInertialClass` is the quotient (the standard-family model of `B(M_J)`), `standardLeviInertialClass` is its quotient map, and `standardLeviInertialClass_eq_iff` identifies equality of classes with the stated relative relation.

## Map of inertial classes

`standardLeviInertialToAmbient i ν family J` implements the second `i_{MG}` display of VI.7.3 on these data. It sends `[L, σ]_{M_J}` to `[L, σ]_G` by forgetting the inclusion of the root label in `J`, without changing `L` or its action `σ`. Forgetting the conjugating point's membership in `M_J(F)` gives an ambient inertia witness, so the representative map descends through `Quotient.lift`. `standardLeviInertialToAmbient_class` is the computation rule on representatives.

The construction uses the canonical rational-point topologies, the local compactness and nonarchimedean group hypotheses of the ambient data, and local finite type for the full twists (the standard Levis inherit it through their closed immersions). The field/group universe and the representation-carrier universe are independent. No Bernstein decomposition, support uniqueness, or fixed-vector theorem is assumed. This pair does not compare the nested presentation with one formed independently on `M_J` or with a quotient over arbitrary rational Levis. It does not construct the untwisted map on `Ω` or its variety structure, and it does not restate the induction–restriction proposition of VI.7.3.
