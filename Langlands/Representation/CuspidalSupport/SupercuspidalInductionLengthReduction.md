# The length of a supercuspidal induction via Jacquet restriction

`leviJacquetCoyoneda` sends a smooth representation `A` of `G(F)` to the module `Hom(B, r_P^G A)` over `(End B)ᵐᵒᵖ`, acting by precomposition. The proof uses `twistStabilizerDoubleCosets` for the set `W` in `SupercuspidalInductionLengthStatement`. A double coset of `P(F)` in `G(F)` belongs to this set if it has a representative that normalizes the Levi and whose conjugation carries the inducing representation `ρ` to an unramified twist of `ρ`.

## The geometric length count

`leviJacquetInduction_coyoneda_length_le` proves that `Hom(B, r_P^G i_P^G ρ)` has length at most `W.encard` over `(End B)ᵐᵒᵖ`. The group and parabolic are those of the length statement, and `ρ` is irreducible supercuspidal. The proof takes `B` projective and requires all of its irreducible subquotients to be unramified twists of the universe lift of `ρ`. The count uses the represented VI.5.3 (iii) filtration, `leviJacquetInductionSupercuspidalFiltration`. Its actual smooth graded objects are irreducible because each is equivalent to the pullback of `ρ` along the Levi isomorphism for the corresponding double coset.

For a projective `B` and an irreducible smooth representation `A`, `moduleLength_coyonedaObj_le_one` shows that `Hom(B, A)` has length at most one over `(End B)ᵐᵒᵖ`. If this module has a nonzero submodule, take a nonzero map `f : B ⟶ A` in it. This map is surjective by irreducibility. Any map `B ⟶ A` factors through `f` by projectivity, so the submodule is the whole Hom module. If there is no nonzero map, the Hom module has length zero.

In the geometric filtration a nonzero map `B ⟶ A` makes the grade `A` a quotient of `B`. The inertial hypothesis on `B` identifies `A` with an unramified twist of the lifted `ρ`. Composing the two representation equivalences and cancelling the carrier lift gives the twist isomorphism required by `twistStabilizerDoubleCosets`. Exactness of `preadditiveCoyonedaObj B` for projective `B` and `moduleLength_obj_eq_sum_of_filtration` bound the total length by the number of indices with coset in `W`. The latter lemma sums length additivity for the short exact sequences of the filtration, starting at zero. The coset indexing is injective, so the number of these indices is at most `W.encard`.

## Detection and comparison with representation length

`nontrivial_leviJacquetCoyoneda_of_isSubquotient` uses `B = Π_D`, the compact induction of an irreducible subrepresentation `ρ₀` of `ρ|_{⁰M(F)}`. Assume `B` is projective, VI.5.3 (ii), independence of the induced progenerator, and second adjointness. For each irreducible subquotient `A` of `i_P^G ρ`, the VI.5.3 (ii) argument gives an irreducible quotient `σ` of `r_Q^G A` for a parabolic `Q` with Levi conjugate to `M`. It also gives `σ` as a subquotient of `r_Q^G i_P^G ρ`. The VI.5.3 (iii) filtration identifies `σ` with an irreducible grade, hence with the pullback of `ρ` along the Levi isomorphism for that grade, with its carrier in the Jacquet module's universe.

There is a nonzero map from `B` to the universe lift of `ρ` by compact induction reciprocity. Its pullback along the same Levi isomorphism gives a nonzero map to `σ`. Projectivity of the transported `B` lifts this to a nonzero map into `r_Q^G A`. Second adjointness transposes the lift to a map from induction along the opposite of `Q`. Independence identifies that induction with `i_{P̄}^G B`, using the inverse of the geometric conjugating element and the unchanged Levi embeddings of the opposite parabolics. Second adjointness along the opposite of `P` transposes the resulting map to `B ⟶ r_P^G A`. Each transpose and composition with an isomorphism preserves nonzeroness.

`repLength_le_of_exactModuleFunctor` compares representation length with the length of its image under an additive functor to modules that preserves homology and detects the irreducible subquotients. A strict inclusion of subrepresentations has a nonzero quotient, which has an irreducible subquotient. Exactness and detection make the image of that quotient nonzero. Taking the ranges of the images of the subrepresentation inclusions therefore sends strict chains to strict chains of submodules.

## Conditional length bound

`supercuspidalInductionLength_of_structuralInputs` concludes both clauses of `SupercuspidalInductionLengthStatement` from independence of the induced progenerator, second adjointness and the VI.5.2 lemma on proper parabolic induction. The represented geometric lemma and VI.5.2 give VI.5.3 (ii). Finite restriction to `⁰M(F)` supplies `ρ₀`, the joint special-compact basis gives projectivity of `B`, and `rationalSummandInduction_mem_block` gives the inertial property of `B`. The composite `Hom(B, r_P^G −)` is exact by exactness of Jacquet restriction and projectivity. Detection and the comparison lemma give the first inequality, from the length of `i_P^G ρ` to the length of its image under the composite. The geometric count gives the second inequality, to `W.encard`. The induced representation is nonzero by `leviInduction_nontrivial`, so the bound `W.encard ≤ 1` forces length one and irreducibility.
