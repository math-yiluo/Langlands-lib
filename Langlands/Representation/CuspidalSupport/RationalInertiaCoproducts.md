# Concrete coproducts in rational inertia blocks

Worker 1740. This is an internal categorical construction supporting the coproduct-existence condition of Renard VI.4.1. It derives that condition from the published concrete smooth direct sum and the published irreducible-subquotient theorem for arbitrary invariant sums. It needs no cuspidality, irreducibility of the base, admissibility, finite length, or reductivity assumption. It does not establish the other small-progenerator conditions.

The group is the actual rational-point group of an affine, locally finite type group scheme over a nonarchimedean local field, with its canonical topology. The inertia block is the published full category defined by the condition on every irreducible actual subquotient. The index universe `uI` is independent of the field/group universe `uF`. The carriers have universe `max uI uV`, so that the actual finite-support direct sum remains in the same carrier universe.

First, `iSup_range_directSumι` proves that the invariant ranges of the concrete summand inclusions span the full direct sum, by induction on finite-support sums. The ambient direct sum is therefore a quotient of that invariant sum. The published `irreducibleSubquotient_iSup` supplies a summand range containing any given irreducible subquotient, and the range is a quotient of its source summand. Transitivity of actual smooth subquotients proves `irreducibleSubquotient_directSum`.

It follows directly that the actual direct sum of objects in an inertia block lies in that block. `rationalInertiaBlockDirectSum` bundles this membership, and `rationalInertiaBlockDirectSumCofan` bundles the same summand inclusions as morphisms of the full subcategory. `rationalInertiaBlockDirectSumIsColimit` transports the concrete direct sum's universal property through fullness: its descending map is the ambient descending intertwiner, and both factorization and uniqueness are the ambient equalities.

Finally, `rationalInertiaBlock_hasColimitsOfShape` constructs a colimit for each diagram over `Discrete I`, using the canonical isomorphism from a discrete diagram to the functor associated with its family of objects. In particular, taking `uI = max uF uV` proves the precise coproduct-existence condition in the published `IsRationalNormKernelSmallProgenerator`, without adding a coproduct premise to that target.
