# Supercuspidal support of a normalized unitary block product

This internal consequence of Zelevinsky 1980, §1.10, and Bernstein 1984, Corollary 8.2(a), applies the Library's binary support formula to a finite normalized product of irreducible unitarizable factors.

In length one, the single-block induction equivalence identifies the product with the sole factor, transported along the equality of ranks. With at least two blocks, the head and tail regrouping equivalence compares the product with the binary product of its first factor and its ordered tail. Bernstein's theorem gives irreducibility of the product, which transfers along this equivalence and the rank equality. The binary formula adds the head support to the tail support provided by induction on the block count. The sum over the original ordered blocks counts each support with its multiplicity.
