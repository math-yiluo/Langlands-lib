# Generic norm-kernel twist equivalence

This internal derived assembly combines `NormKernelTwistAlgebra` and `NormKernelTwistElementary`. It proves the three-way equivalence for representations of a topological group, assuming the common norm kernel is open and contains the commutator, its product with the centre has finite index, and both irreducible representations have scalar central action. These structural and scalar assumptions are explicit inputs to this helper. They must be proved for the actual rational-point group before using this helper in the exact Renard VI.3.2(iii) theorem. All four universes, for the group, norm-character index and two representation carriers, are independent.

Let A be equivalence of restrictions to the common norm kernel, B be equivalence from a twist of the first representation by an element of the published `unramifiedCharacters` subgroup to the second representation, and C be existence of a nonzero restricted intertwiner. The elementary module proves B implies A by removing the trivial scalar on the subgroup. It proves A implies C by injectivity of the equivalence and nontriviality of the source, supplied here by irreducibility.

For C implies B, `exists_twistIntertwiningMap_of_restrictedHom_nonzero` applies the conjugation action `σ(g) ∘ f ∘ ρ(g⁻¹)` to the span of a single nonzero intertwiner's orbit. The explicit finite-index and scalar-centre assumptions make that span finite-dimensional. Commutator containment makes the action commute, so the algebra module produces a common eigenvector and a character trivial on the subgroup. Evaluating the eigenrelation at `ρ(g)v` gives a nonzero intertwiner from `Langlands.twist χ ρ` to `σ`, in precisely the source-twist direction.

`unramifiedCharacterOfTrivialOnOpenKernel` obtains continuity from the discrete quotient and returns an element of the published unramified-character subgroup. Its underlying abstract character equals the original character by extensionality. `twistEquivOfNonzeroIntertwiner` uses irreducibility of the twist and target to turn the nonzero intertwiner into an equivalence. Finally, `normKernelTwistEquivalence_of_nonzeroIntertwiner` combines these three implications into `(A ↔ B) ∧ (A ↔ C)`.

The construction `unramifiedTwistEquivOfRestrictedIntertwiner` packages C implies B as a dependent pair of the unramified character and its representation equivalence. It uses classical choice to select the witnesses established by the algebraic helper. The theorem `normKernelTwistEquivalence_of_finiteIndex_scalar` consumes this construction to give the three-way equivalence just described.

The source motivation is David Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, French edition 9 July 2026, source `renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, SHA256 `7b539996793b7c3620e72e1e389588eb42d1a8e91d6f68b2eead5c2e2ffc182c`, V.2.3 (printed pp.105–106 / PDF pp.111–112) and VI.3.2(iii) (printed pp.143–144 / PDF pp.149–150). The orbit-span route avoids finite-dimensionality of the whole restricted Hom space and does not prove or use VI.3.2(ii).
