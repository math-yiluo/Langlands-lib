# Actual irreducible support and the full Bernstein blocks

Author: Worker 1153.

This is a derived construction of the objects needed in Renard VI.7.1–2, printed pp.180–182 / PDF pp.186–188, in David Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, 9 July 2026 version. Registered source: `renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, SHA256 `7b539996793b7c3620e72e1e389588eb42d1a8e91d6f68b2eead5c2e2ffc182c`. It constructs support relations and full categories, with elementary intrinsic closure laws. It does not prove the source's support uniqueness, support-map or Bernstein decomposition theorems.

The parameters are the actual connected-reductive standard family `BernsteinStandardFamily i ν` from `BernsteinData`, over an arbitrary nonarchimedean local field. Its actual structural decompositions and closedness, canonical ambient rational-point topology, local compactness and nonarchimedean-group hypotheses remain explicit. Local finite type of the ambient scheme is an instance hypothesis, available from `BernsteinStandardFamily.locallyOfFiniteType`. Neither admissibility nor finite length of any smooth object is assumed.

`bernsteinInduction a` is the published normalized induction of the actual smooth Levi representation of `a`, after transport to the literal standard subgroup by the inverse canonical point homeomorphism. Thus it uses the actual standard parabolic and its Haar-modulus normalization. `IsBernsteinCuspidalSupport a π` means that `π` is irreducible and is an actual subquotient of that induction: the imported relation retains an invariant subrepresentation, its invariant quotient, and a smooth equivariant isomorphism. The term “support” here names this induction-occurrence relation; identifying it with the source's Jacquet-defined support is a remaining comparison theorem.

`IsBernsteinInertialSupport s π` means that such an actual datum exists with quotient class exactly `s`. It existentially ranges over the whole inertial class; it does not select a representative. `isBernsteinInertialSupport_representative_iff` proves that, for `s = [a]`, this is precisely existence of an actual datum `b` related to `a` by actual ambient algebraic conjugation and full rational unramified twisting, with `π` in the induction of `b`. `isBernsteinInertialSupport_iff_of_related` and `bernsteinBlockProperty_iff_of_related` prove representative independence from the genuine quotient equality criterion. These results do not require a cuspidal-support theorem. The constituents of the induction of a single fixed datum need not be invariant under unramified twisting; no such claim is made.

The field/group universe `uF` is independent of the representation carrier universe `uV`. Normalized induction of a datum in `uV` has carrier in `max uF uV`. The saturated block property and `BernsteinSaturatedBlock` use actual data, class indices and objects in this common enlarged universe, so induction does not cause another enlargement. On every smooth object `V` in an arbitrary carrier universe `uV`, `bernsteinBlockProperty s V` quantifies over every irreducible actual subquotient `π` of `V` in that same universe, then explicitly uses the published carrier lift `SmoothRep.universeLift.{uF} π` in the support relation at `max uF uV`. The index `s` likewise lies in the inertial quotient at this common universe. The lift is the actual `ULift` action, equivariantly equivalent to the original action by `up` and `down`; it imposes no size, dimension, length or admissibility condition on `V`. At the saturated universe this equivalence is an actual categorical smooth isomorphism. `bernsteinBlockProperty_iff_saturated` proves agreement with the direct all-irreducible-subquotient definition there.

`BernsteinBlock s` is the full subcategory of all smooth objects satisfying `bernsteinBlockProperty s`. Hence all morphisms between two block objects are exactly their original smooth equivariant maps. The source defines this full category on printed p.182 by asking that every irreducible subquotient lie in the chosen inertial fibre. Here that fibre is the explicit actual induction-occurrence relation just described; its comparison with the source's single-valued Jacquet support is retained separately.

The proved closure laws use actual subquotient transitivity, transport by actual ambient isomorphisms, and the published irreducible-subquotient dichotomy for an invariant subrepresentation and its quotient. They prove closure under subquotients, isomorphisms and such extensions, without length assumptions. An actual smooth isomorphism of irreducibles also preserves both support relations. Every defining actual cuspidal induction belongs to its saturated block and, by the explicit lift comparison, to the corresponding full block property.

Remaining obligations are the existence and uniqueness of cuspidal support, its association and Jacquet comparisons, and consequently the assertions that these relations partition all irreducibles and that the full categories decompose the whole smooth category. No block projector, variety structure on the cuspidal-data quotient, `I`/`R` functor, or decomposition instance is supplied or used. Exact Prop-valued comparison targets follow in a separate module.
