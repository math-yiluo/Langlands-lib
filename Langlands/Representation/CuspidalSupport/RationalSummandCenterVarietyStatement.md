## Statement

With the binders above and `X = X(G)`, `Stab` as above, the statement asserts that there is a
`ℂ`-algebra homomorphism `Ψ : Z(End I) → (X → ℂ)` such that

1. `Ψ` is injective;
2. its range is exactly `F^Stab`;
3. for every central `z`, every `ω ∈ X` and every `G`-map `φ : I → π ⊗ ω`,
   `z ≫ φ = Ψ(z)(ω) • φ`.

Item 3 says that `Ψ(z)` is the function on `Irr(G)[π]` whose value at the point `π ⊗ ω` is the
scalar by which `z` acts on `Hom_G(Π, π ⊗ ω)` (the right `A`-module of VI.4.1, simple by the
equivalence of categories). It is well defined because this space is nonzero: by Frobenius
reciprocity it is `Hom_{H⁰}(ρ, Res(π ⊗ ω)) = Hom_{H⁰}(ρ, Res π)`, which contains the given
injection. Items 1 and 2 say that `z ↦ Ψ(z)` is an isomorphism of `Z` onto the algebra of
polynomial functions on `Irr(G)[π]`.

## Correspondence

- **Which isomorphism.** Renard's isomorphism `Z ≃ ℂ[Irr(G)[π]]` is the composite of
  `ψ_g ↦ g` (the Proposition) with `ℂ[T/⁰G] ≃ ℂ[Irr(G)[π]]`, `g ↦ (π ⊗ ω ↦ ω(g))`. With the
  published normalization of `ψ_t` (it acts by `ω(t)` on `Hom_G(Π, π ⊗ ω)`), this composite is
  exactly the `Ψ` of item 3. The statement fixes `Ψ` by item 3 rather than through the basis
  `ψ_t`; Renard's normalization `ψ_g(g⁻¹) = ρ_T(g⁻¹)` is replaced as in the published
  Proposition statement.
- **Functions on `Irr(G)[π]`.** Points of `Irr(G)[π]` are the `π ⊗ ω`; through the
  homogeneous-space description of V.2.7, functions on `Irr(G)[π]` are the `Stab`-invariant
  functions on `X(G)`. The statement uses this description, as the published V.2.7 Corollary
  does, with the published algebra `unramifiedRegularFunctions` of polynomial functions.
- **Base point.** As Renard notes, the isomorphism depends on the base point `π` of the inertial
  class and on the progenerator; both are fixed by the binders.

## Remaining obligations

The proof is a separate obligation. It follows from the published Proposition (the basis
`ψ_t` of `Z` acting by `ω(t)`) and the description `F^Stab = span{χ ↦ χ(t) | t ∈ T}`, which
uses the linear independence of the characters `χ ↦ χ(g)`, `g ∈ G/⁰G`, of `X(G)` and
`T = ⋂_{s ∈ Stab} ker s`.
