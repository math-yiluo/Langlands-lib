# Renard VI.3.3.1 on the actual rational-point categories

These proposition declarations formulate the two-factor decomposition in David Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, French edition dated 9 July 2026, VI.3.3, printed pp.144–145 / PDF pp.150–151, especially equation VI.3.3.1 on printed p.145. The definition and first remark of VI.2.1, printed p.137 / PDF p.143, supply the proper-standard-Jacquet formulation of supercuspidality; the theorem on printed pp.137–139 / PDF pp.143–145 relates it to compact restriction.

The objects are smooth complex representations of the actual rational-point group of a connected reductive group scheme over an arbitrary nonarchimedean local field. The topology is the existing canonical rational-point topology. The field universe `uF` and representation-carrier universe `uV` are independent. The group scheme's affineness and local finite type are derived from its reductivity fields: an affine structure map over the affine base gives an affine source, and smoothness gives local finite presentation and hence local finite type. Locally compact, nonarchimedean and Hausdorff topology hypotheses are displayed on that actual point group. No characteristic-zero, finite-extension-of-Q_p, split, quasi-split, finite-dimensional, finite-length or admissibility restriction is added.

`rationalInertiaDecompositionProperties G π` packages the existing two object properties in a `Bool` family. Its `true` factor is `rationalInertiaBlockProperty G π` and its `false` factor is `rationalInertiaComplementProperty G π`. The two reduction laws are definitional. Both properties quantify over every irreducible actual smooth subquotient, including subquotients of representations of infinite length; inertia membership means an actual isomorphism with an actual unramified twist of `π`. The corresponding full subcategories are precisely the published `RationalInertiaBlock G π` and `RationalInertiaComplement G π`.

`IsRationalInertiaCategoryDecomposition G π` applies the published `Langlands.Renard.IsCategoryDecomposition` from Appendix A.IX, printed p.268 / PDF p.274. It requires that **every** object of the whole smooth category be the vertex of an actual coproduct cofan with one component in each of these two full subcategories, together with vanishing of every morphism between different factors in both directions. Thus the conclusion expresses the categorical product notation in the source using its direct-sum and orthogonality definition. It assumes no chosen projector, splitting, finite restriction family or equivalence of replacement categories.

`RationalCompactRestrictionInertiaDecompositionStatement` is an intermediate target using VI.2.1(c): an irreducible smooth `π` whose restriction to `Plancherel.rationalCommonNormKernel G` has compact smooth-dual coefficients admits this category decomposition. The subgroup is the existing intersection of norm kernels of all actual rational algebraic characters. `HasCompactSmoothDualCoefficients` quantifies over every vector and every smooth functional of the full algebraic dual of the restricted action, with compact support in the actual subgroup topology. This is a representation-theoretic hypothesis of the intermediate assertion. It is neither part of the Chapter V structural ledger nor a substitute definition of source supercuspidality.

`RationalCuspidalInertiaDecompositionStatement` is the target using the actual standard parabolic family. Its inputs are the shared maximal split torus, a generic cocharacter, `StandardCocharacterStatement`, the actual relative-root-system condition and closedness of the actual standard unipotent point subgroups. The representation remains in the independent carrier universe `uV`; no universe bound by the field is imposed.
