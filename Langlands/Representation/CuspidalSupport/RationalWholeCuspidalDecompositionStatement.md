`Langlands.CuspidalSupport.RationalWholeCuspidalCategoryDecompositionStatement` concludes `IsRationalWholeCuspidalCategoryDecomposition` for those objects. Its `Bool`-indexed factors are the full category in which every actual smooth subquotient is cuspidal, and the full category in which no nonzero actual smooth subquotient is cuspidal. The former is indexed by `true`. Cuspidality means vanishing of every proper actual standard normalized Jacquet module. Both factors quantify over arbitrary subquotients, with no irreducibility or finite-length restriction. The nonzero qualification in the second factor excludes the zero representation, whose Jacquet modules vanish.

`Langlands.CuspidalSupport.RationalCuspidalInertiaProductDecompositionStatement` concludes `IsRationalCuspidalInertiaProductDecomposition` for the same objects. Its `Option`-indexed family has the preceding remaining factor at `none`. At `some c` it has the full category whose irreducible actual subquotients all have inertia class `c`. The index is the existing actual unramified-twist/isomorphism quotient restricted by the existence of an irreducible cuspidal representative. No representative is selected and no resizing or smallness assumption is added.

Each conclusion is the published Appendix A.IX `Renard.IsCategoryDecomposition` predicate: every ambient object is the vertex of an actual coproduct cofan with one component in each indexed full factor, and every morphism between distinct factors is zero. The assertion retains the possibly large inertia-class index without assuming coproducts for every family of that index size. It does not separately state an equivalence of categories or construct projection functors. The ambient category contains all smooth complex representations in the given carrier universe; neither admissibility, finite dimension, finite length, a central character nor a finite restriction family is a premise.
