# Renard VI.4.4, Corollary: proof

Author: Worker 5102.

`rationalSummandEndNoetherian : RationalSummandEndNoetherianStatement.{uF, uV}` proves
the published statement of Renard's VI.4.4 Corollary at arbitrary universes uF and uV,
with its premises unchanged. For π irreducible and cuspidal, ρ an irreducible smooth
representation of H⁰ = `rationalCommonNormKernel G` with an injective H⁰-map into
Res π, and I = ind_{H⁰}^G ρ, the rings End(I) and End(I)ᵐᵒᵖ are noetherian, that is,
End(I) is right and left noetherian.

The proof is one line:
`rationalSummandEndNoetherian_of_finiteRestriction rationalNormKernelFiniteRestriction`.

- `rationalNormKernelFiniteRestriction` (module
  `RationalNormKernelFiniteRestrictionProof`, Worker 4361) is the published
  unconditional proof of VI.3.2(ii), `RationalNormKernelFiniteRestrictionStatement`.
- `rationalSummandEndNoetherian_of_finiteRestriction` (module
  `NormalHeckeHomDetection`) derives the Corollary from it through Worker 1826's
  `NormalHecke.isNoetherianRing_end`. The center of End(I) is the group algebra of a
  subgroup of the finitely generated free abelian group G(F)/H⁰, which is finitely
  generated by `rationalReductiveValuationBasis`, so the center is noetherian. End(I) is
  a finite module over its center. Every one-sided ideal is a submodule over the center,
  so both End(I) and its opposite ring are noetherian. The hypothesis `hcyc` of
  `NormalHeckeCenter` is proved in `NormalHeckeHomDetection` without VI.4.1. The finite
  dimension of Hom(I, π) comes from the finite length of the restriction of π to H⁰
  (VI.3.2(ii)).

No premise is added, and admissibility, finite dimensionality and VI.4.1 are not used.

Source: David Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques* (registered
source `renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`), VI.4.4, Corollary,
printed p.158 / PDF p.164. Renard deduces it from the Theorem: the center is a Laurent
polynomial algebra and End(I) is finite over it ([25], 3.6.1–3.6.2). The implemented
argument has the same structure, with the center described by `NormalHeckeCenter`.
