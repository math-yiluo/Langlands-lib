`rationalAllCuspidalSubquotientProperty` says that every actual smooth subquotient is cuspidal, meaning that every proper actual standard normalized Jacquet module vanishes. `rationalNoCuspidalSubquotientProperty` says that no nonzero actual smooth subquotient is cuspidal. The `Nontrivial` condition excludes the zero representation, which is cuspidal under the vanishing definition. Unlike the inertia-block definitions, both properties quantify over arbitrary subquotients, with no irreducibility, admissibility or finite-length restriction. This preserves VI.3.5's wording; no equivalence with a test on irreducible subquotients is asserted. Their full subcategories are `RationalWholeCuspidalCategory` and `RationalNoncuspidalCategory`.

`rationalWholeCuspidalDecompositionProperties` is the `Bool`-indexed family with the all-cuspidal factor at `true` and the no-cuspidal factor at `false`. `IsRationalWholeCuspidalCategoryDecomposition` applies the existing Appendix A.IX `Renard.IsCategoryDecomposition` definition to that family. Its meaning is an actual coproduct presentation of each ambient object by one object in each factor, with vanishing morphisms between distinct factors. This is a definition of the desired assertion, not a proof that the assertion holds.

`RationalCuspidalInertiaClass` is the subtype of the existing `RationalIrreducibleInertiaClass` quotient consisting of classes with an irreducible cuspidal representative. Thus the equivalence relation is the already published actual unramified-twist/isomorphism relation. No new relation, representative choice, universe bound or smallness premise is introduced. `rationalCuspidalInertiaClass` sends a supplied irreducible cuspidal representation to that subtype.

`rationalCuspidalInertiaClassBlockProperty` says that every irreducible actual subquotient has a specified quotient class. `rationalCuspidalInertiaProductProperties` indexes these factors by `some c` and the no-cuspidal factor by `none`. `IsRationalCuspidalInertiaProductDecomposition` is the existing A.IX decomposition assertion for this entire `Option`-indexed family. This encodes the refined whole-category product displayed in VI.3.5 without selecting representatives or asserting any decomposition.
