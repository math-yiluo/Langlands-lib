# Native quotient comparison for restricted support filtrations

This internal adapter connects `CoefficientImageFiltration` with the original ambient quotient and its prescribed `RestrictedLocalizationFiltration`. It uses the literal native coefficient relation quotients, the canonical subgroup equivalence, and the actual filtration transports in `Filtration.GroupRestriction`, `Filtration.IsoTransport` and `Filtration.TwistGraded`. It is an auxiliary construction for the coefficient geometric lemma; it does not assert that the required rational-parabolic orbit geometry or orbit-Jacquet calculation has been proved.

Let `R` be a commutative ring and `G` a group, with subgroups `U`, `M`, and `Q`, where `M` normalizes `U` and `U,M ≤ Q`. Coefficient, group, carrier, category-object and category-morphism universes remain independent. `nested_normalizes` proves the corresponding normalizer inclusion for `U.subgroupOf Q` and `M.subgroupOf Q`. `toNestedNormalizer` is the inverse of the canonical equivalence from the nested copy of `M` to the original subgroup `M`.

`subgroupFunctor` first takes the native character-one quotient of a `Q`-representation by `U.subgroupOf Q`, with the nested `M` action, and then restricts that action back to the original `M`. `subgroupRestrictionIso` naturally identifies this quotient of a restricted ambient `G`-representation with its original ambient native `U` quotient. It uses `Localization.transportEquiv` with the identity on vectors and the canonical equivalence of the two copies of `U`. Both forward and inverse representative formulas send the class of an original vector to the class of that same vector. Equivariance is checked on representatives, and the original linear maps commute with this identification.

When `G` has a topology and `U` satisfies `IsLimitOfCompactSubgroups`, `nested_isLimitOfCompactSubgroups` transfers that property to the nested subgroup by the canonical continuous group equivalence. Images of compact sets and inverse images of the resulting compact subgroups supply the proof. No closedness hypothesis on `Q` is required.

For `F : C ⥤ Rep R G` and a natural subfunctor of `F` restricted to `Q`, `subgroup_image_transport` proves an equality of actual subfunctors: take its image in the nested native quotient, restrict to the original `M`, and transport by `subgroupRestrictionIso`; the result is exactly `restrictedImageSubfunctor` in the original ambient quotient. Membership on both sides means the existence of a representative in the same original submodule. The two representative formulas above transfer the equality of quotient classes in each direction. `subgroup_imageFiltration_transport` applies this equality at every original step and proves equality of the complete filtrations, including the same indices and endpoints.

For the graded comparison, assume in addition that `R` is a complex algebra, `G` is a topological group, `U` satisfies compact-subgroup containment, and every value of `F` is smooth. `restrictedImageFiltrationGradedIso` identifies each grade of the exact prescribed ambient image filtration with the native subgroup quotient of that same grade of the original restricted filtration. Its construction reverses the proved filtration equality, reverses the graded transport isomorphism, applies the group-restriction graded comparison, and then restricts the proved `Coefficient.imageFiltrationGradedIso`. Smoothness of the restricted ambient representation follows by continuous pullback; the compact-subgroup premise follows from the transfer just proved. The codomain action is on the original subgroup `M`, rather than its nested copy.

`twistedRestrictedImageFiltrationGradedIso` applies an arbitrary smooth scalar character of `M`. It combines `twistGradedIso` with the preceding natural comparison. Twisting retains the same actual image submodules and indices. This includes inverse-half-modulus normalization whenever that character is supplied, and assumes no finite generation, admissibility or flatness.

The supplied filtration is an actual filtration on the restricted input functor; this unit proves how its original images and grades behave under the native quotient. Constructing the actual rational-parabolic support filtration, identifying its orbit pieces, proving the normalized orbit-Jacquet formulas, eliminating the proper Jacquet pieces, and establishing compact-open fixed-module finiteness for the surviving pieces remain obligations of the global universal-family theorem.
