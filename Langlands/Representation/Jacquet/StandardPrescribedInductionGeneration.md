# Actual standard induction generated at the prescribed level

This internal construction proves actual normalized standard induction is
generated by the fixed vectors of the specified compact open subgroup `K`
whenever its smooth coefficient representation is generated by the fixed
vectors of the literal intersection `K ∩ M_I`.

The actual standard family is the published geometric family of a maximal
split torus in a connected reductive group. It is available unconditionally
from `Jacquet.standardBernsteinFamily`. The assumptions on the level are
those used here from the host target: a compact group `K₀` containing `K`,
the minimal-standard `M N K₀` factorization, and the compatible split
component and standard parabolic basis property of `K` for the chosen
standard parabolic. Normality of `K` in `K₀` is unnecessary for this
generation implication.

`fixedVectorOrbitSubrepresentation` is the actual subrepresentation whose
underlying submodule is spanned by the orbits of the specified fixed
vectors. The invariant-submodule definition of fixed-vector generation
shows that this orbit span is the whole coefficient space.

For the chosen face, `standardFamilyOppositeUnipotentPoints_eq_dynamic`
uses chamber negation and the published standard-dynamic comparison to
identify the actual opposite standard radical with the dynamic radical
of the opposite cocharacter. This helper takes a `BernsteinStandardFamily`
and a finite simple-root face; the separate published
`standardOppositeUnipotentPoints_eq_dynamic` in `StandardGeneralizedJacquetLift`
retains its arbitrary-face interface. The unconditional rational
opposite-Iwahori basis theorem therefore supplies a cofinal compact open
basis with factorization in the order required by compact-picture
generation. Inversion also changes the prescribed level's literal
`Ubar M U` factorization to the required `U M Ubar` order.

The compatible split component supplies its actual central strict element
and its Iwahori contraction at the prescribed level. The actual opposite
semidirect decomposition gives normalization of the opposite radical;
the all-test standard opposite-intersection theorem gives its trivial
intersection with the parabolic. Actual projection stability turns
coefficient fixed vectors into vectors fixed by the projected coefficient
intersection.

Since `K` is open and contained in `K₀`, the compact group `K₀` is open.
The minimal-standard factorization supplies `G = P_I K₀`, so `K₀` gives the
actual compact picture. Actual standard parabolic closedness, projection
continuity and unipotent unimodularity are furnished by published proofs.
The prescribed-level compact-picture generation theorem then applies,
using all fixed coefficient vectors as the orbit generators.

The field and coefficient-carrier universes are independent. No
admissibility, finite length, cuspidality or irreducibility of the
coefficient representation is assumed. The provenance is this internal
assembly of the published actual subgroup, contraction, compact-picture
and normalization constructions. The coefficient-generation premise
remains explicit; this theorem does not itself assert visibility of every
constituent or prove the host fixed-vector category theorem.
