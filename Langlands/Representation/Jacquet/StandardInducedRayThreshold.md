# A common actual ray threshold throughout the original unramified induced family

This derived module makes the chosen constant-term power-ray threshold
uniform over the entire original unramified inducing family.

At any compact-open ambient level, unramified characters are trivial on
compact subgroups after the actual Levi projection. The compact-trivial
twist equivalence therefore identifies each original induced fixed space
with the untwisted one. Admissibility of every induced twist follows from
source admissibility, open-kernel twist preservation and compact Iwasawa
representatives. The dimensions of these fixed spaces are exactly equal.

At a supplied standard basis level, the fixed-dimension tensor-ray formula
then applies with the single integer `standardInducedFixedOrder` of the
untwisted original induction. For every original unramified twist and every
original tensor coefficient bi-invariant at that level, its chosen standard
constant term agrees with the coefficient along every power at least that
integer of the original strict component element, using the original half
radical modulus. No unitary, square-integrable, generic-parameter or
tensor-rank hypothesis is needed for this uniform ray formula.

This concerns one strict ray and one common standard level. It does not
assert a full-chamber threshold, arbitrary translated asymptotic uniformity,
or the positive-decay theorem.

The contextual source is Waldspurger, *La formule de Plancherel pour les
groupes p-adiques, d'après Harish-Chandra* (2003), Proposition I.4.3 and
VI.2.3, printed pp.245 and 299–300, source `waldspurger-2003-plancherel`.
The explicit common fixed-space dimension and tensor averaging route are
derived project content.
