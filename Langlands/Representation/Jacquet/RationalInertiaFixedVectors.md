# Compact fixed vectors in actual same-group inertia blocks

Author: Worker 146. This internal unit proves the compact fixed-vector
comparison for actual full rational unramified twists and same-group inertia
blocks. It supplies part of the comparison needed in Task 5293, without
proving `StandardBernsteinFixedVectorVisibilityStatement` or the host target.

For an affine group scheme of locally finite type over a nonarchimedean
local field, `rationalUnramifiedTwist_fixedVectors_eq` proves equality of
the original and twisted fixed submodules for every compact subgroup `C`.
The published `compactSubgroup_le_rationalCommonNormKernel` puts `C` inside
the common norm kernel. Every full rational unramified character is trivial
on that kernel by its definition. The existing character-twist fixed-vector
identity then applies on the unchanged representation carrier.

`rationalUnramifiedTwistFixedVectorsEquiv` makes this equality into the
identity linear equivalence. `rationalInertiaFixedVectorsEquiv` combines its
inverse with the actual isomorphism in `IsRationalInertiaRelated`, obtaining
a linear equivalence of the compact fixed spaces of any two inertia-related
smooth objects. The associated nontriviality theorem records the resulting
equivalence of nonzero fixed spaces. These comparisons require neither
irreducibility, cuspidality, admissibility nor openness of `C`.

Under the canonical nonarchimedean group instance,
`rationalInertiaBlock_fixedVector_dichotomy` applies to every compact open
`K` and every object of the actual `rationalInertiaBlockProperty G π`.
If the fixed space is zero the conclusion follows directly. Otherwise the
published fixed-vector constituent theorem supplies an actual irreducible
subquotient with a nonzero fixed vector. Each other irreducible subquotient
is in the same rational inertia orbit, so the comparison above makes its
fixed space nonzero as well. Compact-open detection then proves that the
whole object is generated by its fixed vectors. No finite-length hypothesis
is used, and the field and carrier universes remain independent.

The provenance is internal: these results compose the published rational
norm-kernel theorem, full rational inertia relation, twist identity,
irreducible constituent construction and compact-open detection theorem.
They concern same-group unramified-twist orbits. Comparing subquotients of
inductions from different proper Levi data, including ambient conjugacy,
remains necessary for the full Bernstein visibility proposition. This unit
does not assert a Bernstein decomposition or special-level visibility.
