# The fixed-vector category proposition from VI.9.4 and VI.7.5

Author: Worker 5293.

This is an internal derived statement and a conditional reduction for
Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, VI.10.6,
Proposition (iii)–(iv) (printed p.214 / PDF p.220 of the French version
dated 9 July 2026). It does not establish the unconditional published target
`FixedVectorCategoryStatements`.

## Statement

`FixedVectorCategoryGenerationNoetherianReductionStatement` is the implication

```
StandardFixedVectorSubquotientGenerationStatement →
  SmoothRepNoetherianCategoryStatement →
    FixedVectorCategoryStatements
```

All three propositions are published Library statements, used unchanged and
with the same field and carrier universes `uF`, `uV`:

1. `StandardFixedVectorSubquotientGenerationStatement` is Renard VI.9.4:
   being generated by `K`-fixed vectors passes to subquotients.
2. `Aubert.General.RepresentedParabolic.SmoothRepNoetherianCategoryStatement`
   is Renard VI.7.5: the category of smooth representations of `G(F)` is
   Noetherian.
3. `FixedVectorCategoryStatements` is the target proposition, VI.10.6 (iii)–(iv).

## Provenance

This is internal and derived. The published reduction
`fixedVectorCategoryStatements_of_inputs` (module
`FixedVectorCategoryReduction`) proves the target from three inputs. Two are
the inputs above. The third is `StandardFixedVectorSplittingStatement`, the
objectwise splitting that the source proof gets from the Bernstein
decomposition. This module removes that third input. It proves the
splitting from the first two inputs with the elementary argument in
`FixedVectorSplittingProof`, so no new literature theorem is invoked.

## Proof

`standardFixedVectorSplitting_of_generation_noetherian` proves
`StandardFixedVectorSplittingStatement` from the two inputs. After
introducing the binders, the proof installs the rational-point topology and
its standard structures: topological group, Hausdorff field, local
compactness, nonarchimedean group and second countability. The VI.9.4 input,
specialised to the given data, gives subobject closure of
`fixedVectorGenerated K` through the published
`fixedVectorGenerated_subobjectClosure`. The VI.7.5 input gives
Noetherianity of the smooth category. Second countability makes every open
coset space small in the carrier universe
(`secondCountable_openQuotient_small`). Then
`FixedVectorSplitting.fixedVectorSplitting_of_noetherian` gives the
splitting.

`fixedVectorCategoryGenerationNoetherianReduction` proves the statement. It
applies `fixedVectorCategoryStatements_of_inputs` to the two inputs and to
the splitting just obtained.

Both inputs remain unproved in this unit. Neither the target statement nor
any of its imports is changed.
