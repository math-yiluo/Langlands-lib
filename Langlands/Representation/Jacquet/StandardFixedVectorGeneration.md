# Standard-parabolic factorization and Jacquet fixed-vector generation

Worker 1635. This is a supporting unit for Renard VI.9.4, derived from the
published actual-standard subgroup definitions, unconditional root comparisons,
dynamic decomposition, and the algebraic VI.6.3 fixed-vector generation lemma.
It proves neither the VI.9.4 subquotient-generation statement nor the Hecke
equivalence statement.

`standardJacquetAction i ν ρ J` constructs the actual unnormalized action of
`standardLeviPoints i ν J` on `Jacquet.Coinvariants ρ
(standardUnipotentPoints i ν J)`. It is the published `Jacquet.action` with
the normalizer proof supplied by this module, so consumers need no
separate normalization hypothesis. It makes no smoothness assertion.

For a group scheme over an arbitrary field `F`, the theorem
`standardLeviPoints_le_normalizer_standardUnipotentPoints` proves that the
actual rational points of the standard Levi normalize those of its standard
unipotent radical. Both are defined by intersections over standard
cocharacters. The imported `conj_mem_standardUnipotent` applies to every
standard-parabolic point, so it applies to Levi points and their inverses.
The two conjugation implications give the normalizer criterion. This step
needs only that the structure morphism of the group is affine.

For a connected reductive group, a maximal split torus, and a generic
cocharacter `ν`, `standardParabolic_empty_le_standard` proves `P_∅ ≤ P_J`.
It uses the canonical standard cocharacter `ν` for the empty face. For
every cocharacter standard for `J`, the nested-face root-pairing theorem
sends the nonnegative-root support of `ν` into that of the cocharacter.
The published unconditional `relativeRootNegation` and
`relativeRootSignInclusions` supply the structural root inputs. Dynamic
parabolic inclusion then gives membership in every member of the intersection
defining `P_J`. No standard-cocharacter existence hypothesis is needed for
this inclusion.

`standard_factorization_of_minimal` assumes the given factorization
`G(F) = M_∅(F) N_∅(F) K₀` and existence of a standard cocharacter for the one
index `J` under consideration. It proves `G(F) = M_J(F) N_J(F) K₀` with the
same subgroup `K₀`. The product of the minimal Levi and unipotent factors is
in `P_∅`, hence in `P_J`. The standard/dynamic comparison follows from
`standard_eq_dynamic_of_relativeRootNegation relativeRootNegation`, without
a relative-root-system premise. The dynamic decomposition imported through
`exists_standardUnipotent_mul_standardLevi` writes this product as `v l`.
Conjugation by `l⁻¹` rewrites it as `l (l⁻¹ v l)`, in Levi-then-unipotent
order.

Finally,
`fixedVectors_generate_standardJacquet_of_minimal_factorization` applies the
published `fixedVectors_generate_of_factorization` to these two structural
facts. If `K₀` normalizes `K` and the representation is generated by its
`K`-fixed vectors, its actual unnormalized standard Jacquet action is
generated by its vectors fixed under `K.comap M_J.subtype`. The field,
coefficient ring, and carrier universes are independent. No topology,
compactness, smoothness, admissibility, finite length, or finite-dimensionality
is required for this algebraic deduction. Existence of the local standard
cocharacter and the given minimal factorization remain hypotheses; they
are among the hypotheses of the VI.9.4 targets.
