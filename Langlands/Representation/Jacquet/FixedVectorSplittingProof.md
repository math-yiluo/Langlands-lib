# The fixed-vector splitting from subobject closure and Noetherianity

Author: Worker 5293.

This module proves `fixedVectorSplitting K` (defined in
`FixedVectorDualityReduction`) for a compact open subgroup `K` of a
topological group `G`. The proof does not use the Bernstein decomposition. It
uses two hypotheses:

1. the class `fixedVectorGenerated K` of smooth complex representations
   generated by their `K`-fixed vectors is closed under subobjects;
2. the category `SmoothRep ℂ G` is Noetherian in Renard's sense
   (`Langlands.Renard.IsNoetherianCategory`): every object of finite type is
   Noetherian.

A third hypothesis is a size condition. Every coset space `G ⧸ L` of an open
subgroup `L` must be small in the carrier universe, so that the permutation
representation `ℂ[G/L]` is an object of `SmoothRep ℂ G`.

## Provenance

This is internal, derived material, not a statement from the literature.
Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, VI.10.6
(printed p.214 / PDF p.220 of the French version dated 9 July 2026), proves
part (iii) of the proposition from the Bernstein decomposition. The
published reduction `fixedVectorCategoryStatements_of_inputs` isolates the
objectwise consequence used there as `StandardFixedVectorSplittingStatement`.
This module proves that splitting from the other two inputs of that reduction
instead (VI.9.4 subquotient closure and VI.7.5 Noetherianity). The argument
below was written for this project. It uses only the permutation
representations `ℂ[G/L]`, compact averaging and a positivity argument.

## Mathematical argument

Write `P = ℂ[G/L]` for an open subgroup `L ≤ K`, and `P^K` for its `K`-fixed
vectors. These are the elements of `HeckeSpace ℂ K L`. The proof has five
steps.

* **Pairing.** Let `⟨u, w⟩ = Σₓ u(x) w(x)` on finitely supported functions.
  Then `⟨g u, w⟩ = ⟨u, g⁻¹ w⟩`, and `⟨z, z̄⟩ = Σ |z(x)|²`. Hence
  `⟨z, z̄⟩ = 0` forces `z = 0`. Complex conjugation commutes with translations
  and preserves `K`-fixed vectors.
* **Pairing maps.** Inversion of double cosets `K g L ↦ L g⁻¹ K` gives a
  linear equivalence `heckeTranspose : ℂ[G/L]^K ≃ ℂ[G/K]^L`. For `y ∈ P^K`,
  Frobenius reciprocity gives the intertwiner
  `pairingMap y : ℂ[G/L] → ℂ[G/K]`. It sends `δ_{L}` to the transpose of `y`,
  and its value at the identity coset is `(pairingMap y u)(K) = ⟨u, y⟩`.
* **Agreement on the fixed-vector part (`exists_fixedSpan_eq_of_generated`).**
  Let `Φ : ρ → B` be an intertwiner into a representation generated by
  `K`-fixed vectors, and let `N` be a subrepresentation of `ρ`. Then for every
  `n ∈ N` there is an `s ∈ N` such that `Φ s = Φ n` and `s` lies in the span
  `fixedSpan ρ K` of translates of `K`-fixed vectors. To see this, note that
  by subobject closure `Φ(N)` is generated by its `K`-fixed vectors. Each such
  vector `Φ m` (with `m ∈ N`) equals `Φ(e_K m)`, where `e_K` is compact
  averaging over `K` (`averageMap`). This works because averaging commutes
  with intertwiners and preserves every subrepresentation.
* **Finitely many pairings (`exists_fixedSpan_sub_mem_pairingKernel`).** Let
  `t ⊆ P^K` be finite and `u ∈ P`. Induction on `t`, applying the previous
  step with `B = ℂ[G/K]` (transported to its small model) and `N` the common
  kernel of the pairing maps already handled, gives `s ∈ fixedSpan P K` with
  `pairingMap y (u - s) = 0` for all `y ∈ t`.
* **Positivity (`exists_fixedSpan_sub_mem_noFixedPart`).** Suppose the
  translates of `t` span all of `P^K`, and let `u - s` be as above. Then for
  every `g` the vector `z = e_K(g(u - s))` is `K`-fixed. It lies in the
  common kernel, so it pairs to zero with every translate of every `y ∈ t`.
  In particular it pairs to zero with `z̄ ∈ P^K`, hence `z = 0`. So `u - s`
  lies in `noFixedPart P`, the subrepresentation of vectors all of whose
  translates have zero `K`-average. This subrepresentation has no nonzero
  `K`-fixed vectors (`fixedVectors_noFixedPart`).

**Finiteness (`exists_finset_translateSpan`).** `ℂ[G/L]` is cyclic, so it is
of finite type (`smallFixedVectorGenerator_finite`). By Noetherianity its
module over `MonoidAlgebra ℂ G` is Noetherian. A maximal element among the
subrepresentations spanned by translates of finite subsets of `P^K`
(`set_has_maximal_iff_noetherian`) therefore contains all of `P^K`.

**Splitting (`fixedVectorSplitting_of_noetherian`).** For a smooth
representation `A`, take `S = fixedSpan A K` and `T = noFixedPart A`. Then
`S` is generated by its `K`-fixed vectors (`fixedSpan_generated`) and `T`
has none. To show `S ⊔ T = ⊤`, take `a ∈ A`. Smoothness gives an open `U`
fixing `a`; put `L = K ⊓ U`. The Frobenius map `f : ℂ[G/L] → A` with
`f(δ_L) = a` carries the decomposition `δ_L = s + (δ_L - s)` of the
positivity step to `a = f s + f(δ_L - s)`. Here `f s ∈ S` because `f` maps
fixed vectors to fixed vectors, and `f(δ_L - s) ∈ T` because averaging
commutes with `f`.
