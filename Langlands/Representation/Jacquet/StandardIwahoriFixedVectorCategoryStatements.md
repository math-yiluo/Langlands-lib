**The Proposition (printed p.214 / PDF p.220).**

> **Proposition.** Soit `K` est un sous-groupe ouvert compact de `G`, inclus et distingué dans
> `K₀` et admettant une décomposition d'Iwahori selon les sous-groupes paraboliques standards.
>
> (i) La sous-catégorie `M(G)_K` de `M(G)` des représentations engendrées par leur vecteurs
> `K`-invariants est stable par passage aux sous-quotients et
> `M(G)_K ≃ ∏_{𝔰 ∈ B(G)_K} M(G)_𝔰`.
>
> (ii) Le foncteur `V ↦ V^K` réalise une équivalence de catégories de `M(G)_K` avec
> `M(H(G, K))`, dont l'inverse est `M ↦ H(G) ⊗_{H(G,K)} M`.
>
> (iii) Pour tout `(π, V) ∈ M(G)`, `(π, V) ∈ M(G)_K` si et seulement si `(π̃, Ṽ) ∈ M(G)_K`.
>
> (iv) L'algèbre `H(G, K)` est noethérienne.
>
> *Démonstration.* La première assertion de (i) est établie dans la proposition VI.9.4. De ce
> qui précède, on déduit la décomposition de `M(G)_K`. [...]

The proof of (iii) splits `Ṽ = W₁ + W₂`, with `W₁` the subrepresentation generated by `Ṽ^K`,
"donc dans `M(G)_K ≃ ∏_{𝔰 ∈ B(G)_K} M(G)_𝔰`", and `W₂` in `∏_{𝔰 ∉ B(G)_K} M(G)_𝔰`; it then
shows `W₂ = 0`. The converse uses `V ↪ Ṽ̃`. The proof of (iv) says that each `M(G)_𝔰` is a
Noetherian category, hence so are `M(G)_K` and `M(H(G, K))`, and `H(G, K)` is of finite type
over itself.

`M(G)_𝔰` is the full Bernstein block of VI.7.2, and the product of categories is the one of
Appendix A.IX: `M = ∏ᵢ Mᵢ` for full subcategories `Mᵢ` of `M` when every object of `M` is a
direct sum of objects of the `Mᵢ`, one for each `i`, and every morphism between objects of
distinct `Mᵢ` is zero.

## Framework

Everything below is published; this module constructs nothing.

- `F` is a nonarchimedean local field of either characteristic and `G` an actual connected
  reductive group scheme over `Spec F` (`hG : IsConnectedReductive G`). `rationalPoints G`
  carries `rationalPointTopology F G.X` and `rationalPoint_isTopologicalGroup F G`.
- `i : S.toGrp ⟶ G` is a maximal split torus, `ν` a generic cocharacter and `_hstandard` the
  existence of a standard cocharacter for every `J ⊆ simpleRelativeRoots i ν`.
  `standardLeviPoints i ν J`, `standardUnipotentPoints i ν J` and
  `standardOppositeUnipotentPoints i ν J` are `M_J(F)`, `N_J(F)` and `N̄_J(F)` inside `G(F)`.
- `K₀` is a compact subgroup with the Iwasawa factorization `G(F) = M_∅(F) N_∅(F) K₀`
  (V.5.1 (1)).
- `K : OpenSubgroup (rationalPoints G)` is compact, `K ≤ K₀` and `K₀ ≤ normalizer K` (the
  source's "inclus et distingué dans `K₀`").
- `_hIwahori`: for every `J ⊆ simpleRelativeRoots i ν`,
  `HasIwahoriIntersectionDecomposition K.toSubgroup (standardLeviPoints i ν J)
  (standardUnipotentPoints i ν J) (standardOppositeUnipotentPoints i ν J)`. The published
  predicate says that `(K ∩ N̄_J) × (K ∩ M_J) × (K ∩ N_J) → K`, `(x, y, z) ↦ x y z`, is
  bijective: the source's Iwahori decomposition `K = K_N̄ K_M K_N` along `P_J` (V.5.2 (ii)).

These binders are copied verbatim from the published Iwahori forms of VI.9.4
(`StandardIwahoriFixedVectorSubquotientGenerationStatement` and
`StandardIwahoriFixedVectorHeckeEquivalenceStatement`) and from the published
`StandardRepresentativeFixedVectorCriterionStatement` (VI.10.6, Corollary). The Markdown of
`StandardIwahoriDecompositionStatements` explains why this is the source's hypothesis and why

"Generated by its `K`-fixed vectors" is the published `fixedVectorGenerated K.toSubgroup V`:

`⊤`. This is the formulation of all published VI.9.4 and VI.10.6 statements.

## `StandardIwahoriFixedVectorBlockDecompositionStatement`

After the binders above, the statement supplies the instances `T2Space F`,
`LocallyCompactSpace (rationalPoints G)` and `NonarchimedeanGroup (rationalPoints G)` by the
published `BruhatTits.localField_t2Space`, `rationalPoint_locallyCompactSpace` and
`Plancherel.rationalPoints_nonarchimedeanGroup`, and takes the actual standard Bernstein family
`family := standardBernsteinFamily i ν hG hi hν`. This is done exactly as in the published
`StandardBernsteinFixedVectorVisibilityStatement` and `StandardLeviFixedVectorSupportStatement`.
The inertial classes `BernsteinInertialClass.{uF, max uF uV} i ν family` are the classes
`𝔰 ∈ B(G)` of the actual standard cuspidal data. `bernsteinBlockProperty.{uF, uV} i ν family 𝔰`
is the full block `M(G)_𝔰` on representations with carriers in `Type uV`: all irreducible
subquotients have inertial support `𝔰`. This is the encoding of the published VI.7.2
statement `BernsteinDecompositionStatement`.

**`B(G)_K`.** The index type is the subtype of the classes `𝔰` with
`∃ π : SmoothRep.{0, uF, max uF uV} ℂ (rationalPoints G),
IsBernsteinInertialSupport i ν family 𝔰 π ∧ Nontrivial (fixedVectors π.representation K)`:
some irreducible smooth representation with inertial support `𝔰` has a nonzero `K`-fixed
vector. This is the source's `B(G)_K`, and the same condition as in the published
`StandardRepresentativeFixedVectorCriterionStatement`. The published
`CompactOpenInertialFinitenessStatement` (VI.10.6, Theorem) uses the same set, written with an
explicit nonzero fixed vector, and states that it is finite.

**The conclusion.** For every `V : SmoothRep.{0, uF, uV} ℂ (rationalPoints G)`:

`fixedVectorGenerated K.toSubgroup V ↔ Renard.IsCoproductOfComponents P V`, where
`P 𝔰 = bernsteinBlockProperty.{uF, uV} i ν family 𝔰.1` for `𝔰` in the index subtype.

The published `Renard.IsCoproductOfComponents P V` (module
`CategoryTheory/RenardCategoryDecomposition`, Appendix A.IX for one object) says: there are
objects `Y 𝔰` and morphisms `ι 𝔰 : Y 𝔰 ⟶ V`, one for each `𝔰 ∈ B(G)_K`, with `Y 𝔰` in `M(G)_𝔰`,
such that the cofan `(V, ι)` is a colimit, i.e. `V` is their coproduct.

This is how the statement reads `M(G)_K ≃ ∏_{𝔰 ∈ B(G)_K} M(G)_𝔰` in the sense of A.IX:

- (→) every object of `M(G)_K` is a coproduct of objects of the blocks `M(G)_𝔰`,
  `𝔰 ∈ B(G)_K`. This is the first condition of A.IX.
- (←) every such coproduct is in `M(G)_K`. In particular every object `Y` of `M(G)_𝔰`, for
  `𝔰 ∈ B(G)_K`, is in `M(G)_K`: take the cofan with `Y` at `𝔰` and zero objects elsewhere.
  A zero object lies in every block, because it has no irreducible subquotient. So the
  factors of the product are the full blocks `M(G)_𝔰`, as in the source, and not only their
  intersections with `M(G)_K`.
- The second condition of A.IX, that morphisms between objects of distinct blocks are zero, is
  part of the published `BernsteinDecompositionStatement` (VI.7.2) for all blocks and is not
  repeated.

The index runs over `B(G)_K` only, so (→) also says that the components of `V` along the
classes outside `B(G)_K` vanish.

**Universes.** `V` has carriers in an arbitrary universe `uV`, as in the published
`BernsteinDecompositionStatement`. The inertial classes and the representations `π` in
`B(G)_K` live at the saturated universe `max uF uV`, as in all published Bernstein statements.

## `StandardIwahoriFixedVectorCategoryStatement`

The binders are those above (without the family). The conclusion is copied word for word from
the published `FixedVectorCategoryStatements`, whose Markdown explains it:

1. **(iii)** For every `V : SmoothRep.{0, uF, uV} ℂ (rationalPoints G)`,
   `fixedVectorGenerated K.toSubgroup V ↔ fixedVectorGenerated K.toSubgroup V.contragredient`,
   with the published smooth contragredient `V.contragredient`.
2. **(iv)** `IsNoetherianRing H` and `IsNoetherianRing Hᵐᵒᵖ` for
   `H = Shimura.LevelHecke.LevelHeckeAlgebra ℂ K.toSubgroup`, the published Hecke algebra
   `ℂ[G(F)/K]^K` of `K`: `H` is left and right Noetherian. The published Markdown explains
   why this counting-measure algebra stands for the source's `H(G, K)` and why the right
   Noetherian property is included.

The only change from the published statement is the hypothesis `_hIwahori`. The published
statement assumes, for every `J`, a compatible split component `A`, `q` with
`StandardParabolicBasisMember i ν J q K`. This statement assumes only the Iwahori
decomposition, which is the first conjunct of `StandardParabolicBasisMember`.

## Why the statements are expected to hold under the source's hypothesis

Nothing in this section is proved here. It records the expected route, so that a reader can
check that the statements are not stronger than the source's results.

**The decomposition.** By the published VI.7.2 statement `BernsteinDecompositionStatement`,
every `V` is a coproduct `⊕_{𝔰 ∈ B(G)} V_𝔰` with `V_𝔰` in `M(G)_𝔰`. The source's "de ce qui
précède" refers to the VI.9.4 Corollary (first assertion of (i)) and to the Theorem and its
proof. The expected argument:

1. *Visibility.* If `𝔰 ∈ B(G)_K`, every irreducible `τ` with inertial support `𝔰` has
   `τ^K ≠ 0`. By Jacquet's subrepresentation theorem, `τ` embeds in `i_P^G(σ)` for a standard
   cuspidal datum `(M_I, σ)` in the class `𝔰`. The published
   `StandardRepresentativeFixedVectorCriterionStatement` (first claim of the proof of the
   VI.10.6 Corollary) gives `σ^{K ∩ M_I} ≠ 0`. Frobenius reciprocity gives a surjection
   `r_P(τ) → σ`, so `r_P(τ)^{K ∩ M_I} ≠ 0` (the fixed-vector functor of a compact open
   subgroup is exact). The Jacquet lemma (VI.9.1, whose hypothesis on `K` is the Iwahori
   decomposition along `P`) makes `τ^K → r_P(τ)^{K ∩ M_I}` surjective, so `τ^K ≠ 0`. This is
   the published `bernsteinFixedVectorVisibility` for `K` (the published
   `StandardBernsteinFixedVectorVisibilityStatement` states it only under
   `StandardParabolicBasisMember`).
2. The published theorem `bernsteinBlock_fixedVector_dichotomy` turns visibility into: every
   object of a block is generated by its `K`-fixed vectors or has none. With visibility, an
   object `Y` of `M(G)_𝔰`, `𝔰 ∈ B(G)_K`, with `Y^K = 0` has no irreducible subquotient, so
   `Y = 0`. Hence `M(G)_𝔰 ⊆ M(G)_K` for `𝔰 ∈ B(G)_K`, and (←) follows because `M(G)_K` is
   closed under direct sums.
3. If `𝔰 ∉ B(G)_K` and `Y` is in `M(G)_𝔰`, then `Y^K = 0`: a nonzero `v ∈ Y^K` generates a
   subrepresentation with an irreducible quotient in which the image of `v` is a nonzero
   `K`-fixed vector. So for `V` in `M(G)_K`, `V^K` lies in `⊕_{𝔰 ∈ B(G)_K} V_𝔰`, a
   subrepresentation, which is therefore `V`. Hence `V_𝔰 = 0` for `𝔰 ∉ B(G)_K`, and the
   remaining components form a coproduct. This is (→).

**(iii) and (iv).** The source's proof of (iii) uses the decomposition and the projection
`Ṽ → W₂`. The proof of (iv) uses the VI.9.4 Proposition, whose Iwahori form is published
(`StandardIwahoriFixedVectorHeckeEquivalenceStatement`), and the Noetherianity of `M(G)`
(published `SmoothRepNoetherianCategoryStatement`). Both proofs use the hypotheses on `K`
only through (i) and (ii), and through `e_K`, which needs only `K` compact open. The published
reduction `fixedVectorCategoryStatements_of_inputs` (module
`Jacquet/FixedVectorCategoryReduction`) proves the published statement from the VI.9.4
Corollary, Noetherianity, and the fixed-vector splitting `StandardFixedVectorSplittingStatement`
(every `V` is `S ⊔ T` with `S` generated by its `K`-fixed vectors and `T^K = 0`). The expected
proof of the new statement follows the same route with the Iwahori forms; the splitting
follows from the decomposition above.

  `CompactOpenInertialFinitenessStatement`;

  in the sense of A.IX, as in the published VI.7.2 statement, and not as the existence of
  some equivalence of categories.
