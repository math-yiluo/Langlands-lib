# The compact fixed-vector small progenerator

This internal construction continues the measure-free approach to the Hecke equivalence in Renard 2026, VI.9.4, printed pp.200–201 / PDF pp.206–207. It assumes an arbitrary topological group, an open subgroup `K`, and smallness of `G/K` in the independently prescribed carrier universe. Projectivity also assumes compactness of `K`. The small-progenerator and equivalence conclusions assume closure of fixed-vector generation under subobjects as well. These are supporting results; the reductive subquotient theorem and the final Hecke-functor comparison remain separate obligations.

The compact permutation representation is bundled in the actual full subcategory of fixed-vector-generated smooth representations. Evaluation at its identity-coset vector is an additive equivalence from its Hom group to the actual fixed vectors. Maps out of any fixed-vector-generated representation agree when they agree on the fixed vectors, by applying generation to the kernel of their difference.

Projectivity follows by lifting the identity-coset value through the surjection on fixed vectors supplied by compact averaging, then extending by Frobenius reciprocity. The full-subcategory inclusion preserves epimorphisms by the established quotient closure and categorical inclusion results. The evaluation correspondence also proves faithfulness of the integer-module Hom functor. The previously proved actual finite generation over the complex group algebra gives preservation of carrier-universe coproducts. Combining these three properties proves the published `HomSmallProgenerator` predicate, and the published small-progenerator theorem gives equivalence of the actual opposite-endomorphism-module Hom functor.
