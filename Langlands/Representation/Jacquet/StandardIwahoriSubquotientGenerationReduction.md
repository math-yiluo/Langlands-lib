# Renard VI.9.4 Corollary with the Iwahori hypothesis, from the special-vertex inputs

The two normalized-Jacquet bridge lemmas are renamed copies of
`nonzero_fixed_of_nonzero_lift` and `rationalLeviJacquetFixedVectorDetection_of_nonzero_lift`
from a module `StandardWeakJacquetDetection` that is not in the Library.

**This module is a conditional reduction. It does not prove
`Jacquet.StandardIwahoriFixedVectorSubquotientGenerationStatement`.** Its main theorem
proves that statement, unchanged, from one retained premise:
`CuspidalSupport.RationalSpecialVertexCompactInputsStatement.{uF}`. Through published
reductions that premise follows from the joint special-compact basis existence, from
`BruhatTitsAvailabilityStatement`, and from the Kaletha-Prasad
`Geometry.Building.GroupAxiomsExistenceStatement`. All three are unproved in the
Library. The relative root system (`GroupScheme.relativeRootSystem`) and the
norm-kernel finite restriction (`CuspidalSupport.rationalNormKernelFiniteRestriction`)
are used as the published theorems, not as premises. Unlike the published
`Jacquet.standardFixedVectorSubquotientGeneration_of_prerequisites`, this module does
not assume `GeneralizedJacquetLemmaStatement`.

The entry declaration is the proposition-valued definition
`Jacquet.standardIwahoriFixedVectorSubquotientGenerationOfSpecialVertexInputs`, whose
type is this proved implication (see the last section).

## Source

Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques* (2026), VI.9.4 "Une
conséquence", Corollaire, printed p.201 / PDF p.207: for a compact open `K`, contained
in and normal in `K₀`, with an Iwahori decomposition along the standard parabolics, every
subquotient of a smooth `V` generated by `V^K` is generated by its `K`-fixed vectors.
Renard (printed p.200 / PDF p.206) obtains it by observing that, once the generalized
Jacquet lemma is proved, the proof of Corollary VI.6.3 no longer needs admissibility.

The proof here takes another route. Nonvanishing of `K`-fixed vectors is enough, and
it follows from a weak Jacquet lemma that holds for every smooth representation. The
contracting element of that lemma is built from the standard cocharacter, so the target's
Iwahori hypothesis on `K` is the only hypothesis on `K` that is used. The VI.6.3 skeleton
(a cuspidal Jacquet subquotient, compact coefficients for it, then fixed-vector
detection) is the one in the published detection assembly.

## Declarations

### `Jacquet.exists_pow_isIwahoriContraction_of_eventualContraction`

Abstract group-theoretic lemma for a topological group `H`, a compact open subgroup `K`,
subgroups `N`, `M`, `Nbar` with `N` and `Nbar` closed, and `M` normalizing `N` and `Nbar`.
Assume `K` has the ordered decomposition `HasIwahoriIntersectionDecomposition K M N Nbar`.
Let `g ∈ M` commute with every element of `M`. Assume that for every compact `C ⊆ N` and
every identity neighbourhood `W`, eventually `g^n c g^{-n} ∈ W` for all `c ∈ C`, and
that the same holds for `g^{-n} c g^n` on compact subsets of `Nbar`. Then some power
`g^n` satisfies every clause of `Jacquet.IsIwahoriContraction K N M Nbar`.

Proof: choose `n₁` with `g^n (K ∩ N) g^{-n} ⊆ K` for `n ≥ n₁`, and `n₂` with
`g^{-n} (K ∩ Nbar) g^n ⊆ K` for `n ≥ n₂` (the sets `K ∩ N` and `K ∩ Nbar` are compact).
Take `n = n₁ + n₂ + 1`. Normalization keeps the conjugates in `N` and `Nbar`. Centrality
gives the Levi clause. The singleton `{x}` gives the exhaustion clause. The contraction
clause for an open subgroup `L` uses `L` as the neighbourhood, since `n·s ≥ s`.

### `Jacquet.exists_isIwahoriContraction_of_dynamic`

Let `χ` be a cocharacter of an affine group scheme `G` over a nonarchimedean local field
`F`. Every compact open subgroup of `G(F)` with the ordered decomposition along the
dynamic centralizer, the unipotent radical of `χ` and that of `χ⁻¹` has an element `g`
with `IsIwahoriContraction K U(χ) Z(χ) U(χ⁻¹) g`. The published
`BruhatTits.DynamicUnipotentContainment.exists_central_contractingPoint` gives
`χ(t)` for a unit `t` whose powers tend to zero. This point is central in `Z(χ)` and
contracts both radicals uniformly on compact sets. Closedness of both radicals is
`isClosed_unipotentPoints`. `Z(χ)` normalizes `U(χ⁻¹)` because
`Dynamic.centralizer_inverseCocharacter` gives `Z(χ⁻¹) = Z(χ)`. The previous lemma then
gives a power of `χ(t)`.

### `Jacquet.exists_standard_isIwahoriContraction`

Let `G` be connected reductive with maximal split torus `i`, generic `ν`, and a standard
cocharacter `zeta` for `J`. Then every compact open `K` with the Iwahori decomposition
along `standardLeviPoints i ν J`, `standardUnipotentPoints i ν J` and
`standardOppositeUnipotentPoints i ν J` has a contracting element for these three
subgroups. The proof rewrites the three standard subgroups as the dynamic ones of
`zeta ≫ i`, by the published `standard_eq_dynamic_of_relativeRootNegation` (with the
proved `relativeRootNegation`) and `standardOppositeUnipotentPoints_eq_dynamic`. It then
applies the previous theorem. No split component and no basis-member clause is used.

### `Jacquet.standardIwahoriFixedJacquet_exists_ne_zero`

This is the weak Jacquet lemma under the same hypotheses. Let `ρ` be a smooth
representation and `y` a nonzero vector of the unnormalized standard Jacquet module
`standardJacquetAction i ν ρ J` fixed by `K ∩ M_J`. Then `ρ` has a nonzero `K`-fixed
vector. The published `exists_pow_fixedJacquetMap_preimage_of_iwahoriContraction` gives
`s` and a `K`-fixed `x` whose Jacquet image is `g^s · y`. If `x = 0`, then `g^s · y = 0`,
so `y = 0` because the action of `g^s` is bijective. No finiteness, admissibility or
stabilization hypothesis is used.

### `NormalizedJacquetBridge.nonzero_fixed_of_nonzero_unnormalizedLift`

Let `N`, `M` be closed subgroups with `M` normalizing `N`, let `K` be compact open, and
let `D` be a smooth representation. Assume every nonzero `K ∩ M`-fixed vector of the
unnormalized Jacquet module forces a nonzero `K`-fixed vector of `D`. Then a nonzero
`K ∩ M`-fixed vector of the normalized Jacquet module also forces one. The published
`normalizedFixedVectorsEquiv` identifies the two fixed spaces. (A renamed copy of
`nonzero_fixed_of_nonzero_lift`.)

### `rationalLeviJacquetNonzeroFixedVector_of_lift`

This theorem has the hypothesis `RationalNormKernelFiniteRestrictionStatement` and the
binders of the published `rationalLeviJacquetFixedVectorDetection_of_surjective`, with
one change. The surjectivity hypothesis `_hsurj` on the fixed-vector Jacquet map is
replaced by the weaker lifting hypothesis `_hlift`: every nonzero Levi-fixed vector of
the unnormalized Jacquet module of `D` gives a nonzero `K`-fixed vector of `D`. The
conclusion is a nonzero `K`-fixed vector of `D`. The proof follows the published
detector's proof. The normalized Jacquet functor preserves
generation by fixed vectors and subquotients. Transitivity places the irreducible
`E` as a subquotient of `J(A)`. The Levi rational-point homeomorphism transports
everything to `L(F)`. `rationalNormKernelFixedVectorDetection` gives a nonzero
`K ∩ M`-fixed vector of `E`, hence of the normalized Jacquet module of `D` by
`nonzero_fixed_subquotient`. The previous lemma ends the proof. (A renamed copy of
`rationalLeviJacquetFixedVectorDetection_of_nonzero_lift`.)

### `Jacquet.standardIwahoriSubquotient_nonzero_fixed_of_specialVertexInputs`

Assume `RationalSpecialVertexCompactInputsStatement.{uF}`. Take the data of the target
statement: maximal split torus, generic `ν`, standard cocharacters for every `J`, `K₀`
with the minimal Iwasawa factorization, compact open `K` normalized by `K₀`, the Iwahori
decomposition for every standard `J`, and a smooth `V` generated by `V^K`. Then every
nontrivial actual smooth subquotient `D` of `V` has a nonzero `K`-fixed vector.
(Compactness of `K₀` and `K ≤ K₀` are not needed.)

Proof. The relative root system gives the standard-cocharacter statement (with
`relativeRootSignsDetermineDynamic`) and the standard-Levi intersection statement. Lift
`V` and `D` by `SmoothRep.universeLift.{uF + 1}`. The published
`exists_irreducible_cuspidal_jacquet_subquotient` picks a standard index `I` and an
irreducible Levi-cuspidal actual subquotient `E` of the normalized Jacquet module of the
lifted `D`. The Levi scheme is connected reductive by `standardLevi_isConnectedReductive`.
`leviCompactCoefficients_of_specialVertexInputs` gives compact smooth-dual coefficients
of `E` restricted to the common norm kernel. This is the only use of the premise. The
weak Jacquet lemma above, at `J = rootSet i ν I`, supplies `_hlift`. The proof also uses
`standard_factorization_of_minimal` (factorization with the given `K₀`) and
`standardUnipotentPoints_finite_compactOpenContainment`. Then
`rationalLeviJacquetNonzeroFixedVector_of_lift` with the published
`rationalNormKernelFiniteRestriction` gives a nonzero `K`-fixed vector of the lifted
`D`. `exists_nonzero_fixed_universeLift_iff` brings it back to `D`.
