# Native coefficient quotient exactness

This is an internal coefficient extension of the published character-localization exactness argument. It uses the actual relation spans and quotient maps of `Jacquet.Localization`, rather than a replacement quotient or an assumed exact functor.

For a commutative ring `R`, a subgroup `U`, and a character `θ : U →* Rˣ`, `relations_map_of_surjective` says that a surjective intertwiner maps the source relation submodule onto the target relation submodule. On a generator, the map sends `ρ(u)v − θ(u)v` to `σ(u)p(v) − θ(u)p(v)`. Conversely, lift the vector in a target generator through the surjection. This proves equality of the two submodules by their span universal properties.

`map_surjective` lifts a representative of a target quotient class through the original surjection. `map_range_eq_ker` proves middle exactness when the original range equals the kernel and the second map is surjective. If the image of a vector is a target relation, lift that relation to a source relation. Subtracting the lift puts the vector in the original kernel, hence in the original range. The subtraction does not change its quotient class. These three results work over every commutative ring and for every character. The subgroup `M` normalizes `U` and preserves the character, so all quotient maps are actual `M`-intertwiners.

For a complex algebra `R`, `complexIntertwiner` restricts an actual native intertwiner to complex scalars without changing its function. `mem_relations_one_iff` identifies the native character-one relation set with its complex relation set. It uses `CoefficientRestriction.coefficientRelations_eq_native`: the complex span of all differences is stable under the coefficient action, so it is exactly the native span as a set.

`map_one_injective_of_smooth` assumes that `U` is a limit of compact subgroups, the ambient topological group is a topological group, the original intertwiner is injective, and its target action is smooth. The published complex theorem `Localization.map_injective_of_smooth` applies after scalar restriction, with the constant character one. The equality of relation sets then transfers injectivity back to the literal native quotient. `map_one_injective` installs the canonical complex module structures by restriction along `ℂ → R`; no additional module structure is part of its assumptions.

The coefficient, group and three carrier universes are independent. No finiteness, admissibility, flatness or Noetherian hypothesis is used. Left exactness here concerns character one over a complex algebra; no left-exactness theorem for general coefficient characters or arbitrary coefficient rings is asserted. This auxiliary algebra does not establish rational-parabolic geometry, orbit-Jacquet calculations, or the final universal-family fixed-module finiteness.
