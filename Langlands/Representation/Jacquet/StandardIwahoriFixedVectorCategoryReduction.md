# Iwahori-form reduction of the fixed-vector category proposition

Module
`Langlands.Representation.Jacquet.StandardIwahoriFixedVectorCategoryReduction`,
namespace `Langlands.Jacquet`, universe parameters `uF` and `uV`.

This is internal derived content for the published
`StandardIwahoriFixedVectorCategoryStatement`, corresponding to Renard VI.10.6,
Proposition (iii)–(iv), printed p.214 / PDF p.220 of the French edition dated
9 July 2026. Its provenance is the already published reductions
`FixedVectorCategoryReduction` and `StandardBernsteinFixedVectorReduction`.
The implementation adapts their assembly to the Iwahori hypothesis of the
published target. It does not prove that target unconditionally.

`StandardIwahoriBernsteinFixedVectorVisibilityStatement : Prop` packages the
visibility input. Its binders are precisely those of the target: a
nonarchimedean local field, an actual connected reductive group, a maximal
split torus and generic cocharacter, standard-cocharacter existence, a
compact subgroup with the minimal-parabolic Iwasawa factorization, and a
compact open subgroup contained and normal in it, with an ordered Iwahori
intersection decomposition along every standard parabolic. It supplies
the canonical rational-point topology and the same three local-field
instances as the published block-decomposition statement. Its conclusion is
`bernsteinFixedVectorVisibility` for the actual `standardBernsteinFamily`:
among irreducible smooth representations with the same inertial support,
having a nonzero fixed vector for this compact open subgroup is equivalent.
The family is constructed by the published structural proofs. No basis-member
condition is substituted for the Iwahori decomposition.

`standardIwahoriFixedVectorCategory_of_inputs` proves the implication from
four statements, all explicit premises:

1. `StandardIwahoriFixedVectorSubquotientGenerationStatement`;
2. `Aubert.General.RepresentedParabolic.SmoothRepNoetherianCategoryStatement`;
3. `CuspidalSupport.BernsteinDecompositionStatement`;
4. `StandardIwahoriBernsteinFixedVectorVisibilityStatement`.

The conclusion is the exact published
`StandardIwahoriFixedVectorCategoryStatement.{uF, uV}`. Both field and
representation-carrier universes remain independent.

The proof installs the published affine, smooth and rational-point
topological instances. Second countability makes the open coset quotient
small in the carrier universe, as required for the small compact
permutation generator. The first premise supplies closure of fixed-vector
generation under subobjects through
`fixedVectorGenerated_subobjectClosure`. The third and fourth premises
supply `fixedVectorSplitting K` through
`fixedVectorSplitting_of_bernsteinDecomposition`, with the actual standard
family. This splitting says every smooth representation is the sum of a
subrepresentation generated by its fixed vectors and a subrepresentation
with zero fixed-vector submodule.

For (iii), `fixedVectorGenerated_iff_contragredient_of_splitting` combines
this splitting, subobject closure, compactness and the published
compact-open cofinality of the rational-point group to give equivalence
of generation for a representation and its smooth contragredient.
For (iv), `levelHecke_isNoetherianRing_of_smoothCategory` applies the
second premise to the small fixed-vector generator and the published
Hecke equivalence, giving left Noetherianity of
`Shimura.LevelHecke.LevelHeckeAlgebra ℂ K.toSubgroup`. The inversion
comparison `hecke_isNoetherianRing_op_iff` gives Noetherianity of its
opposite ring.

This module proves only the conditional implication. It supplies no proof
of any of the four premises. It changes neither the target pair nor any
of its imported files.
