# Renard VI.9.4, Proposition: fixed vectors and modules over the Hecke algebra

Worker 6. This module states one proposition,
`Langlands.Jacquet.StandardFixedVectorHeckeEquivalenceStatement`, at
universes `uF` (field and group schemes) and `uV` (representation carriers).
It is a statement, not a proof: the module asserts nothing to be true.
The Corollary of VI.9.4 is stated separately as
`Langlands.Jacquet.StandardFixedVectorSubquotientGenerationStatement`, with
the same hypotheses on `K`.

In translation, VI.9.4 says: a first consequence of the generalized Jacquet
lemma is that Corollary VI.6.3 now holds without the admissibility
hypothesis.

- **Corollary.** Let `K` be a compact open subgroup of `G`, contained and
  normal in `K₀` and having an Iwahori decomposition along the standard
  parabolic subgroups. Let `(π, V)` be a smooth representation of `G` such
  that `V^K` generates `V`. Then every subquotient of `V` is generated by its
  vectors fixed by `K`.
- **Proposition.** Let `K` be as above. Then the full subcategory of `M(G)`
  of representations generated by their `K`-invariant vectors is equivalent
  to the category of unital modules over the Hecke algebra `H(G, K)`.

The Proposition is deduced from the Corollary by the Theorem of I.3.2: for
an idempotent `e`, the functor `M ↦ e·M` from the full subcategory
`M(A, e)` of modules generated by `e·M` to unital `eAe`-modules is an
equivalence if and only if `M(A, e)` is closed under subquotients.

## Setting

The two VI.9.4 statements have the same data and hypotheses.

- `F` is a nonarchimedean local field of either characteristic, and `G` an
  actual connected reductive group scheme over `Spec F`. The topology on
  `rationalPoints G` is `rationalPointTopology F G.X`, with the topological
  group structure `rationalPoint_isTopologicalGroup F G`.
- `i : S.toGrp ⟶ G` is an actual maximal split torus and `ν` a generic
  cocharacter. They fix Renard's minimal parabolic `P_∅ = M_∅ N_∅` and the
  standard parabolics, indexed by `J ⊆ simpleRelativeRoots i ν`. The
  subgroups of `G(F)` used are `standardLeviPoints i ν J` (`M_J`),
  `standardUnipotentPoints i ν J` (`N_J`) and, inside the Iwahori
  predicate, `standardOppositeUnipotentPoints i ν J`.
- `_hstandard`: every standard index `J` has an actual standard cocharacter.
  This is the first conclusion of the published `StandardCocharacterStatement`,
  retained as an algebraic input exactly as in
  `Langlands.Jacquet.StandardCanonicalSectionStatement`. Without it the
  choice-free standard subgroups of `J` could be all of `G(F)`.
- `K₀` is a compact subgroup of `G(F)` with the Iwasawa factorization
  `_hIwasawa`: every `g` is `m * n * k` with `m ∈ M_∅(F)`, `n ∈ N_∅(F)` and
  `k ∈ K₀`. This is V.5.1 (1), `G = P_∅ K₀`.
- `K` is a compact open subgroup with `K ≤ K₀` and
  `K₀ ≤ Subgroup.normalizer K`, so `K` is contained and normal in `K₀`.
- `_hIwahori`: for every standard index `J` there are an actual split torus
  `A` and `q : A.toGrp ⟶ S.toGrp` with `IsCompatibleStandardSplitComponent
  i ν J A q` and `StandardParabolicBasisMember i ν J q K`. The latter is the
  published predicate for one member of the V.5.2 basis: the ordered
  decomposition `K = (K ∩ N̄_J)(K ∩ M_J)(K ∩ N_J)` of V.5.2 (ii), the
  normalization of `K ∩ M_J` by `C_A` and the contraction clauses of (ii),
  and the exhaustion, compact-containment, neighborhood-basis and
  cofinality clauses of (iii)–(iv), for the chambers of `A`.

"Generated by its `K`-fixed vectors" is expressed for a smooth
representation `V` as: every subrepresentation `T` of `V` whose underlying
submodule contains `fixedVectors V.representation K` is `⊤`.

## The statement

`StandardFixedVectorHeckeEquivalenceStatement` writes `P` for the object
property "generated by its `K`-fixed vectors" on
`SmoothRep.{0, uF, uV} ℂ (rationalPoints G)`. It concludes that
`P.ι ⋙ Langlands.Shimura.LevelHecke.heckeModuleFunctor ℂ K` is an
equivalence of categories (`Functor.IsEquivalence`), from the full
subcategory `P.FullSubcategory` to `ModuleCat.{uV} (LevelHeckeAlgebra ℂ K)`.
The published functor `heckeModuleFunctor` sends a smooth representation to
its `K`-fixed vectors, with the module structure of the Hecke algebra of
`K`, and an intertwiner to its restriction to fixed vectors. The statement
asserts that this particular functor is an equivalence; it does not name the
inverse functor, which the source gives as the induction `Z ↦ A ⊗_{eAe} Z`
of I.3.2.

The ring `LevelHeckeAlgebra ℂ K` is the published measure-free model of
`H(G, K)`: the `K`-invariant finitely supported functions on `G(F)/K`, with
convolution `(a * b)(z) = ∑_{yK} a(yK) b(y⁻¹z)`, unit the indicator of the
coset `K`, and action `a • v = ∑_{xK} a(xK) ρ(x) v` on `K`-fixed vectors.
Renard's `H(G, K)` is the Haar-measure corner `e_K * H(G) * e_K`, acting by
`π(f)v = ∫ f(g) π(g)v dg`. For a Haar measure `dg`, write `vol(K)` for the
measure of the compact open `K`. The map `f ↦ (xK ↦ vol(K) f(x))` is an algebra
isomorphism from the latter onto the former, compatible with the actions on
`V^K`, because `∫ f(y) h(y⁻¹x) dy = vol(K) ∑_{yK} f(y) h(y⁻¹x)` for
`K`-bi-invariant `f` and `h`.
This comparison is the reason for the choice. It is not part of the
statement, and the Library does not prove it. Modules over the unital ring
`LevelHeckeAlgebra ℂ K` in `ModuleCat` are unital modules, as in the source.

## Derived choices

- **The subgroup `K₀`.** In the source, `K₀` is the maximal compact
  subgroup of V.5.1 adapted to `A_∅`, which the Library constructs only
  through Bruhat–Tits data. The statements instead take an arbitrary compact
  subgroup `K₀` with the factorization of V.5.1 (1). The source proofs use
  `K₀` through the factorization `G = K₀P` for standard `P` (the VI.6.3
  Lemma), the normality of `K` in `K₀`, and the `K₀`-conjugacy of every
  parabolic subgroup to a standard one (V.5.1, Corollary), used together
  with VI.2.1 in the proof of the Corollary. The last property follows from
  the factorization: if `P = g P_J g⁻¹` and `g⁻¹ = p k` with `p ∈ P_∅`, then
  `P = k⁻¹ P_J k`. The VI.6.3 Proposition uses only that `K` is compact.
  By V.5.1 (1) and the Levi decomposition `P_∅ = M_∅ N_∅`, the adapted
  `K₀` of V.5.1 satisfies these hypotheses, so the statements cover the
  source's `K₀`.
- **The Iwahori decomposition.** `StandardParabolicBasisMember` contains the
  clauses (ii)–(iv) of V.5.2 for `K` and every standard parabolic. The
  source's hypothesis, an Iwahori decomposition along the standard
  parabolics, is the clause (ii). The hypothesis may therefore be stronger
  than the source's and the statements may cover fewer subgroups `K`. They
  cover every member of the neighborhood basis of V.5.2, hence arbitrarily
  small subgroups `K`. This follows the published
  `StandardCanonicalSectionStatement` for VI.6.1.
- **The Hecke algebra.** The measure-free `LevelHeckeAlgebra ℂ K` replaces
  Haar-measure `H(G, K)`, for the reason above.

## Related Library content

The published `Langlands.CompactOpenDetectionConsequencesStatement`
(Worker 1210, with Worker 189) derives generation of all subquotients from
detection of irreducible subquotients by `K`-fixed vectors, for an arbitrary
topological group. The published
`Langlands.Jacquet.FixedVectorsGenerationStatement` (Worker 189) is an
algebraic form of the VI.6.3 Lemma. In the source, the Proposition follows
from the Corollary and the Theorem of I.3.2, and the Corollary follows from
the VI.6.3 Lemma and Proposition, VI.2.1, and the generalized Jacquet lemma
of VI.9.1–VI.9.3. The derived partial statements of VI.9.1 are
`GeneralizedJacquetLemmaStatement` and `UniformCompressionStabilizationStatement`
(Workers 937 and 1587). None of these is a proof of the statement here.

Not stated here: VI.9.5, the identification of the inverse functor, and the
comparison of `LevelHeckeAlgebra ℂ K` with the Haar-measure corner
`Langlands.Hecke.HeckeAlgebra.Corner`.
