# Remaining inputs for the fixed-vector category proposition

Author: Worker 146. This is an internal derived statement and a conditional
reduction for Renard VI.10.6, Proposition (iii)–(iv), printed p.214 / PDF
p.220 of the French version dated 9 July 2026. It does not establish the
unconditional published target.

`StandardFixedVectorSplittingStatement` uses exactly the local-field,
connected-reductive, maximal-split-torus, generic-cocharacter, standard
cocharacter, compact subgroup, Iwasawa, compact open subgroup, normality and
Iwahori-decomposition binders of `FixedVectorCategoryStatements`.
Its conclusion is `fixedVectorSplitting K`: each smooth representation is
the sum of a subrepresentation generated by its fixed vectors and one
without fixed vectors. It preserves the independent field and carrier
universes. This is the weaker objectwise input extracted from the
Bernstein decomposition used in the source proof; it asserts neither a
category equivalence nor a canonical choice of projectors.

`fixedVectorCategoryStatements_of_inputs` proves the exact published
proposition from three explicit hypotheses:

1. `StandardFixedVectorSubquotientGenerationStatement`, the VI.9.4
   subquotient-generation result;
2. `SmoothRepNoetherianCategoryStatement`, the VI.7.5 proposition;
3. `StandardFixedVectorSplittingStatement`, the objectwise splitting above.

The proof derives subobject closure from the first input. It derives
compact-open cofinality, local compactness and a nonarchimedean-group
structure from the actual rational-point topology. Second countability
makes the open coset quotient small in the independent carrier universe.
Thus no size or topology premise is added to the conclusion.

The duality conjunct is the generic splitting argument in
`FixedVectorDualityReduction`. The left Noetherian conjunct follows from
the cyclic compact permutation generator and the existing Hecke
equivalence, as implemented in `FixedVectorHeckeNoetherianReduction`.
The inverse double-coset ring equivalence in `LevelHeckeInversion`
transfers it to the opposite ring. All three input propositions remain
unproved in this unit. The exact target and all its imports are unchanged.
