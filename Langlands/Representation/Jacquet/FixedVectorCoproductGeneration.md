# Fixed-vector generation on supplied coproducts

This module contains two internal lemmas for the coproduct step in Renard
VI.10.6, Proposition (i). Their provenance is the existing Library definition
`Langlands.Jacquet.fixedVectorGenerated` and the universal property of a
coproduct. They do not assume Bernstein decomposition or visibility of fixed
vectors on inertial classes.

Let `G` be any topological group, `K` any subgroup, and `A` a family of smooth
complex representations indexed by an arbitrary type `I`. The carrier universe
of the representations is independent of the universe of `I`. Given maps
`ι i : A i ⟶ V` whose cofan is a colimit,
`fixedVectorGenerated_iff_of_isColimit_cofan` proves that `V` is generated by its
`K`-fixed vectors if and only if every `A i` is generated by its `K`-fixed
vectors.

For the forward direction, each coproduct leg has a left inverse, the
corresponding `SmoothRep.cofanProjection`. Evaluating its defining identity
shows that the projection is surjective. The existing lemma
`fixedVectorGenerated_of_surjective` then passes generation to the component.
For the reverse direction, take an invariant subspace of `V` containing all
`K`-fixed vectors. Each leg maps fixed vectors into this subspace. The existing
lemma `fixedVectorGenerated_image_le` therefore puts the entire image of every
leg in the subspace. The images span the vertex by
`SmoothRep.iSup_range_cofan_eq_top`, so the subspace is all of `V`.

`fixedVectorCoproductGeneration` exposes the same proved equivalence as a
defined proof term with the same parameters. It delegates to
`fixedVectorGenerated_iff_of_isColimit_cofan`. It defines no new object
property and introduces no additional assumption.

`isZero_of_fixedVectorGenerated_of_fixedVectors_eq_bot` proves that a
representation generated by its `K`-fixed vectors is a zero object when its
fixed subspace is zero. The proof applies generation to the bottom invariant
subspace, showing it is the top subspace. Hence every vector is zero, and
`SmoothRep.isZero_iff_subsingleton` supplies the categorical conclusion.

Neither lemma needs compactness, openness, local compactness, or a reductive
group. These are elementary infrastructure for the proposed fixed-vector block
decomposition; this module does not assert that decomposition.
