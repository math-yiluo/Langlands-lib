# The fixed-level block decomposition from the Bernstein decomposition and visibility

This module proves the two directions of the equivalence in Renard VI.10.6
Proposition (i): `M(G)_K ≃ ∏_{𝔰 ∈ B(G)_K} M(G)_𝔰`, in the coproduct form used by
the Library statement `StandardIwahoriFixedVectorBlockDecompositionStatement`.
Each direction assumes one explicit premise:

- (⇐) assumes fixed-vector visibility at `K`;
- (⇒) assumes that every object is a coproduct of Bernstein block objects.

The module proves neither premise.

## Setting

The setting is that of `BernsteinFixedVectorSplitting`:

- `F` is a nonarchimedean local field;
- `G` is a group scheme over `F` with `IsAffineHom` and `LocallyOfFiniteType`
  structure maps;
- `G(F)` has the point topology and is locally compact and nonarchimedean;
- `family : BernsteinStandardFamily i ν`;
- `K` is an open subgroup of `G(F)`.

## Definition

`bernsteinFixedClass i ν family K s` (an `abbrev`, Prop-valued) is the set
`B(G)_K`. It says that some smooth representation `π` at carrier universe
`max uF uV` has inertial support `s` (`IsBernsteinInertialSupport`) and a
nonzero `K`-fixed vector. It is literally the predicate of the index subtype
in `StandardIwahoriFixedVectorBlockDecompositionStatement`. Membership in a
Bernstein inertial support already includes irreducibility, so this matches
Renard's definition of `B(G)_K` as the inertial supports of irreducibles with
nonzero `K`-fixed vectors.

## Statements

The visibility hypothesis `hvis` below is the Library predicate
`bernsteinFixedVectorVisibility.{uF, uV} i ν family K`, and `hK` says that `K`
is compact.

- `nontrivial_fixedVectors_of_bernsteinBlock`: assume `hK` and `hvis`, and let
  `s` satisfy `bernsteinFixedClass`. Then every nonzero object `Y` at carrier
  universe `uV` with `bernsteinBlockProperty i ν family s Y` has a nonzero
  `K`-fixed vector.
- `fixedVectorGenerated_of_bernsteinBlock`: under the same hypotheses, every
  object of the block `M(G)_s` is generated by its `K`-fixed vectors
  (`fixedVectorGenerated K.toSubgroup Y`).
- `fixedVectorGenerated_of_isCoproductOfFixedClasses` (direction ⇐): under
  `hK` and `hvis`, let `V` be the vertex of a coproduct cofan whose components
  are indexed by the subtype `{s // bernsteinFixedClass … s}` and lie in the
  blocks (`Renard.IsCoproductOfComponents`). Then `V` is generated by its
  `K`-fixed vectors.
- `subsingleton_of_isCoproduct_of_not_bernsteinFixedClass`: let `V` be
  generated by its `K`-fixed vectors, and the vertex of a coproduct cofan
  `Cofan.mk V inj` over all inertial classes whose components `A s` lie in
  the blocks. Then the component `A s` of every class `s ∉ B(G)_K` is zero
  (`Subsingleton`).
- `isCoproductOfFixedClasses_of_fixedVectorGenerated` (direction ⇒): assume
  `hdec`, which says every object is a coproduct of objects of the blocks,
  indexed by all inertial classes
  (`Renard.EveryObjectIsCoproductOfComponents` for `bernsteinBlockProperty`).
  Then every `V` generated by its `K`-fixed vectors is the vertex of a
  coproduct of block objects indexed by `{s // bernsteinFixedClass … s}`.

## Proof

`nontrivial_fixedVectors_of_bernsteinBlock`. A nonzero vector of `Y` is fixed
by some open subgroup `K'`. The published
`SmoothRep.hasFixedVectorConstituent_of_nontrivial` gives an irreducible
subquotient `D` of `Y` (at universe `uV`). By the block property, its
universe lift `SmoothRep.universeLift D` has inertial support `s`. Visibility
applied to it and to the witness `π` of `bernsteinFixedClass` makes the lift
have a nonzero `K`-fixed vector. That vector is transported back to `D`
through `SmoothRep.universeLiftEquiv` and `fixedVectorsEquiv`. Finally,
`SmoothRep.nontrivial_fixedVectors_of_isSubquotient` lifts it to `Y`, using
compactness of `K`.

`fixedVectorGenerated_of_bernsteinBlock`. The published dichotomy
`bernsteinBlock_fixedVector_dichotomy` makes `Y` either generated by its
`K`-fixed vectors or without nonzero `K`-fixed vectors. In the second case,
`Y` is zero by the previous theorem, and a zero object is generated.

Direction (⇐). Every component of the cofan is generated by its fixed
vectors, so the range of each coprojection lies in every subrepresentation
containing `V^K` (`fixedVectorGenerated_image_le`). The ranges of the
coprojections of a coproduct span `V` (`SmoothRep.iSup_range_cofan_eq_top`).

`subsingleton_of_isCoproduct_of_not_bernsteinFixedClass`. First,
`(A s)^K = 0`. Otherwise `hasFixedVectorConstituent_of_nontrivial` gives an
irreducible subquotient with a nonzero `K`-fixed vector. Its universe lift
has inertial support `s`, so `s ∈ B(G)_K`, a contradiction. Next, the
canonical projection `SmoothRep.cofanProjection hc s : V ⟶ A s` is
surjective, since it is a retraction of `inj s`
(`SmoothRep.inj_cofanProjection_self`). Hence `A s` is generated by its
`K`-fixed vectors (`fixedVectorGenerated_of_surjective`). It is therefore
generated by `0`, so it is zero.

Direction (⇒). `hdec` gives a coproduct cofan `Cofan.mk V inj` over all
inertial classes. The previous theorem makes every component with
`s ∉ B(G)_K` zero. The restricted cofan over `{s // s ∈ B(G)_K}` is again a
colimit: it is built with `Cofan.IsColimit.mk`. The descending map of a cocone
`c` over the subtype extends `c` by `0` on the classes outside `B(G)_K` and
uses the original colimit. Uniqueness holds because maps out of a zero
component agree.

Renard derives the decomposition "de ce qui précède". That is, he combines
the Bernstein decomposition (VI.7.2) with the fact that each block either
lies in `M(G)_K` or has no `K`-fixed vectors. That fact is visibility
together with the published dichotomy. The module isolates exactly these
two inputs as hypotheses (`hvis` for ⇐, `hdec` for ⇒). The zero-component
argument for (⇒) and the restriction of the cofan to `B(G)_K` are internal
category-theoretic steps.
