# Exact coefficients along a strict and a weak contraction

Author: Worker 4712. Module
`Langlands.Representation.Jacquet.JointIwahoriEigenCoefficient`, namespace
`Langlands.Jacquet`. This is a supporting module for the necessity half of
Casselman's criterion (Renard 2026, VII.1.2). The module adds no definitions.

Notation: `ρ` is a smooth complex representation of a topological group `G`,
`K` is a compact open subgroup, and `C g = compressedAction ρ hρ K hK g` is the
published compression on `K`-fixed vectors: act by `g`, then average over `K`
(`CanonicalLiftDefinitions.lean`). `IsWeakIwahoriContraction` is defined in
`WeakIwahoriBasis.lean`.

## Results

- `IsWeakIwahoriContraction.compressedAction_mul`: when `K` has an Iwahori
  factorization `K = (K ∩ Nbar)(K ∩ M)(K ∩ N)` and `g, h` are weak contractions,
  `C (g * h) = C g * C h`. The published `compressedAction_mul_of_factor` applies
  because its hypotheses are the `upper` field of `g` and the `lower` and `levi`
  fields of `h`.
- `IsWeakIwahoriContraction.compressedAction_pow`: `C (g ^ n) = (C g) ^ n` for a
  weak contraction `g`, by induction using `mul` and `pow`.
- `exists_joint_eigen_coefficient_of_jacquet_eigenvector` (main result). Assume
  `M` normalizes `N`, `b` is an Iwahori contraction for `(K, N, M, Nbar)`, `a` is a
  weak one, `a` and `b` commute, and the `K`-fixed vectors are finite-dimensional.
  Let `w ≠ 0` be a vector of the ordinary Jacquet module `V_N` that is fixed by
  `K ∩ M` and satisfies `b · w = μb w` and `a · w = μa w` for the unnormalized
  action of `M`. Then there are a `K`-fixed vector `x` and a smooth functional `ℓ`
  such that `ℓ(x) ≠ 0`, `ℓ` is `K`-invariant, and for all `m, n : ℕ`
  `ℓ(ρ(a ^ m * b ^ n) x) = μa ^ m * μb ^ n * ℓ(x)`.

## Proof of the main result

1. The published `exists_compressedAction_eigenvector_of_jacquet_eigenvector`
   (`IwahoriEigenCoefficient.lean`) gives a nonzero `K`-fixed lift `x` of `w` with
   `C b x = μb x`.
2. `μb ≠ 0`: if `μb = 0`, applying the action of `b⁻¹` to `b · w = 0` gives
   `w = 0`.
3. `C a` and `C b` commute, since `C a * C b = C (a b) = C (b a) = C b * C a` by
   `compressedAction_mul`.
4. `x` is also an eigenvector of `C a`. Put `y = C a x - μa x`. By the published
   `mk_compressedAction_of_factor`, `y` maps to `a · w - μa w = 0` in `V_N`, so
   `y` lies in the kernel of the map from `K`-fixed vectors to `V_N`. The published
   `powerKernelSup_eq_ker_of_iwahoriContraction` identifies that kernel with the
   generalized kernel of `C b`. Since `C a` and `C b` commute, `C b y = μb y`. As
   `μb ≠ 0`, `y` lies in every image `(C b)^r`, hence in the stable image. The
   published `powerKernelSup_isCompl_stableImage` (finite dimension) makes the
   generalized kernel and the stable image complementary, so `y = 0`.
5. Choose `ℓ0` with `ℓ0(x) ≠ 0` and put `ℓ = ℓ0 ∘ avg_K`. Then `ℓ` is
   `K`-invariant, hence smooth, and `ℓ(x) = ℓ0(x)` because `x` is `K`-fixed.
6. Since `a ^ m` and `b ^ n` are weak contractions,
   `C (a ^ m * b ^ n) = (C a)^m (C b)^n`. Also `avg_K (ρ(g) x) = C g x` by
   definition, so `ℓ(ρ(a ^ m b ^ n) x) = ℓ0((C a)^m (C b)^n x) = μa^m μb^n ℓ(x)`.

## Provenance

Derived. This is the matrix-coefficient identity used in the proof of Renard
VII.1.2 (printed pp.223–224 / PDF pp.229–230): for `z ∈ S'_M(n) ⊂ A_M^+(ε)`
with `n` large, `δ_P(z)^{1/2}` times
the coefficient equals the Jacquet-module pairing
`⟨r_P^G(π)(z) · j_N(v), j_N(λ)⟩`, and the coefficient is a pure exponential when
`j_N(v)` is an eigenvector. Here the identity is proved exactly, without
asymptotic constants, along the monoid `{a^m b^n}` generated by a strict
contraction `b` and a weak contraction `a`. That is the form the boundary test
points of the necessity proof need. Source: David Renard, *Représentations des
groupes réductifs p-adiques*, French version dated 9 July 2026
(`renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`).
