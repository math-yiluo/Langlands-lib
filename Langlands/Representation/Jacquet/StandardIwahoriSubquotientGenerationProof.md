# Renard VI.9.4 Corollary with the Iwahori hypothesis: proof

`Langlands.Jacquet.standardIwahoriFixedVectorSubquotientGeneration` proves exactly
`Langlands.Jacquet.StandardIwahoriFixedVectorSubquotientGenerationStatement.{uF, uV}`
(module `Langlands.Representation.Jacquet.StandardIwahoriDecompositionStatements`) at
both of its universe parameters: `uF` for the field and `uV` for the representation
carrier, independent of each other. [Statement](StandardIwahoriDecompositionStatements.md)

## What is proved

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field (either characteristic), `G` a connected
reductive group scheme over `Spec F`, `i : S → G` a maximal split torus, `ν` a generic
cocharacter, and assume that every set `J` of simple relative roots has a standard
cocharacter (the statement's hypothesis `_hstandard`). Let `K₀ ⊆ G(F)` be a compact
subgroup with the minimal-parabolic Iwasawa factorization `G(F) = M_∅(F) N_∅(F) K₀`, and
`K` a compact open subgroup with `K ⊆ K₀`, normalized by `K₀`, which has, for every
standard `J`, the ordered Iwahori decomposition
`HasIwahoriIntersectionDecomposition K M_J(F) N_J(F) N̄_J(F)`. If a smooth complex
representation `V` is generated by `V^K` (every subrepresentation containing `V^K` is
`V`), then every smooth `D` with `SmoothRep.IsSubquotient D V` is generated by `D^K`.

The theorem has no hypotheses beyond those of the statement. It assumes no
admissibility, finite generation or finite length of `V`, and none of the extra
normalization, contraction or cofinality clauses of `StandardParabolicBasisMember`. The
scope is that of the statement: no restriction to split groups, reduced relative root
systems, positive rank or `GL_n`. Its axioms are `propext`, `Classical.choice` and
`Quot.sound`.

## Proof

The Lean term is one application:

```
standardIwahoriFixedVectorSubquotientGeneration_of_jointBasis
  Langlands.BruhatTits.SpecialCompact.jointSpecialCompactBasisExistence
```

- `Langlands.Jacquet.standardIwahoriFixedVectorSubquotientGeneration_of_jointBasis`
  (module `Langlands.Representation.Jacquet.StandardIwahoriSubquotientGenerationReduction`)
  proves `JointSpecialCompactBasisExistenceStatement.{uF} →
  StandardIwahoriFixedVectorSubquotientGenerationStatement.{uF, uV}`.
  [Reduction](StandardIwahoriSubquotientGenerationReduction.md)
- `Langlands.BruhatTits.SpecialCompact.jointSpecialCompactBasisExistence`
  (module `Langlands.BruhatTits.SpecialCompact.JointBasisProof`) proves
  `JointSpecialCompactBasisExistenceStatement.{u}` for every universe `u`, here used at
  `u = uF`. [Joint basis](../../BruhatTits/SpecialCompact/JointBasisProof.md)

Neither theorem has a hypothesis left over, so the composition is unconditional.

## The route inside the reduction

The reduction composes two steps, both proved in the Library.

1. `CuspidalSupport.rationalSpecialVertexCompactInputs_of_jointBasis` (module
   `Langlands.Representation.CuspidalSupport.RationalCuspidalInertiaDecompositionReduction`)
   turns the joint witness into `RationalSpecialVertexCompactInputsStatement.{uF}`:
   for every maximal split torus and generic cocharacter, an apartment datum and a
   special vertex whose special compact subgroup has the special compact properties,
   the simultaneous standard basis and the finite Cartan covering. The covering comes
   from the Renard Cartan comparison of the joint witness.
2. `standardIwahoriFixedVectorSubquotientGeneration_of_specialVertexInputs` proves the
   target from these inputs. By `SmoothRep.fixedVectors_generate_subquotients_of_detection`
   it suffices that every nonzero smooth subquotient `D` of `V` has a nonzero `K`-fixed
   vector. For such `D`, `exists_irreducible_cuspidal_jacquet_subquotient` gives a
   standard index `I` and an irreducible Levi-cuspidal subquotient `E` of the normalized
   Jacquet module of `D` (after the universe lift `SmoothRep.universeLift`). The special
   compact subgroup of step 1, through `leviCompactCoefficients_of_specialVertexInputs`,
   gives compact coefficients of `E` on the common norm kernel. The published
   `rationalNormKernelFiniteRestriction` and `rationalNormKernelFixedVectorDetection`
   then give a nonzero `K ∩ M_I(F)`-fixed vector of `E`, hence of the normalized
   Jacquet module of `D`, and by `normalizedFixedVectorsEquiv` of the unnormalized one.
   A weak Jacquet lemma, `standardIwahoriFixedJacquet_exists_ne_zero`, lifts it to a
   nonzero `K`-fixed vector of `D`. That lemma holds for every smooth representation; its
   contracting element is a power of the image of the standard cocharacter, and it uses
   only the Iwahori decomposition of `K` along the standard parabolic `P_I`. The given
   `K₀` enters through `standard_factorization_of_minimal`, which derives the
   factorization `G(F) = M_I(F) N_I(F) K₀` from the minimal Iwasawa factorization, and
   through the normality of `K` in `K₀`.

The reduction's Markdown describes each lemma of step 2 in full.

## Source

David Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques* (version of 9 July
2026), VI.9.4 "Une conséquence", Corollaire, printed p.201 / PDF p.207; its admissible
special case is the VI.6.3 Corollary, printed p.179 / PDF p.185. The Iwahori
decomposition of `K` is the ordered decomposition of V.5.2 (ii), printed p.123 / PDF
p.129. Renard obtains the Corollary by rerunning the proof of the VI.6.3 Corollary with
the generalized Jacquet lemma of VI.9 in place of admissibility. The implemented proof
uses instead the weak Jacquet lemma above, which only needs nonvanishing of fixed
vectors; it keeps the VI.6.3 skeleton (a cuspidal Jacquet subquotient, compact
coefficients for it, then fixed-vector detection).
