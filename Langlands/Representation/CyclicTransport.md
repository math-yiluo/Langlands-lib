# Transport of cyclic representations under commuting refinements

This internal algebraic construction extends `CyclicSpan` using the existing
`FiniteVectors.map_orbitSpan` equality and Mathlib's injectivity alternative for
an intertwiner with irreducible source. It uses an arbitrary field, arbitrary
monoids, and independent scalar, group and vector-space universes.

`map_surjective` shows that the cyclic restriction of any intertwiner maps onto
the orbit span of the image of its generator. `mapEquiv` upgrades that map to
an equivalence when the source cyclic representation is irreducible and the
image generator is nonzero. Its action on the generator is the original map.

`commutingAlgebraMap` bundles the action of an element of the second monoid
algebra as an intertwiner for the first action. Its proof extends commutation
from monoid elements to finite linear combinations. Consequently
`exists_equiv_of_mem_commuting_orbitSpan` constructs a generator-preserving
equivalence between the first cyclic representations generated by v and w
whenever w is a nonzero vector in v's second-action orbit span and the first
cyclic representation of v is irreducible. The proof obtains an algebra element
representing w from the surjective cyclic presentation and applies `mapEquiv`.

These are internal constructions and direct consequences of the imported
algebraic APIs, not a new claim about a numbered source theorem. Their intended
use is to preserve previously chosen local factors during successive coordinate
refinements in the Flath existence proof. No smoothness, admissibility,
finite-dimensionality, topology or tensor-factorization conclusion is asserted
here; transport of such additional properties remains a separate application.
