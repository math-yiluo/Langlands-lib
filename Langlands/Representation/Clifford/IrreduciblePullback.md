# Pullbacks and intertwiners of irreducible representations

`Langlands.FiniteRepresentation.SplitDegree.isIrreducible_of_comp` shows that a complex representation is irreducible if its pullback along a group homomorphism is irreducible. Each invariant subspace of the original representation is invariant under the pullback.

`Langlands.FiniteRepresentation.SplitDegree.nonempty_equiv_of_ne_zero` gives an equivalence from a nonzero intertwiner between irreducible complex representations. Such an intertwiner is bijective.
