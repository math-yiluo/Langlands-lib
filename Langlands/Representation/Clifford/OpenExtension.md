# Extension of an open-kernel irreducible class to its stabilizer

This internal module proves that, over a compact nonarchimedean group `G` with
a normal subgroup `N` such that every commutator lies in `N` (`CommutatorsIn N`)
and, for every open subgroup `U` containing `N`, the powers of one element meet
every right coset of `U` (`CyclicOpenQuotients N`; then `U` is normal and
`G / U` is cyclic), every open-kernel irreducible
representation `A` of `N` extends to the stabilizer `H` of its class, with
open kernel. This is the extension step of Clifford theory for inertia over
wild inertia (J.-P. Serre, *Local Fields* (1979), registered as
`serre-1979-local-fields`, IV §2, Corollaries 1–4 to Proposition 7, printed
pp. 67–68, for the procyclic tame quotient).

## Results

- `exists_sup_extension`: for a normal subgroup `U` and a representation `A`
  of `N` trivial on `N ∩ U`, there is a representation of `N ⊔ U` that
  restricts to `A` on `N` and is trivial on `U`: write `k = y z` with `y ∈ N`
  and `z ∈ U`, and set `c k = A y`; two decompositions differ by an element
  of `N ∩ U`.
- `exists_openNormal_le_ker`: there is an open normal subgroup `U` of `G`
  with `N ∩ U` inside the kernel of `A`. The kernel is open in `N`, so it
  contains `N ∩ O` for an open neighbourhood `O` of one in `G`; `O` contains
  an open subgroup because `G` is nonarchimedean, and that subgroup contains
  an open normal subgroup because `G` is compact (Mathlib's
  `IsTopologicalGroup.exist_openNormalSubgroup_sub_clopen_nhds_of_one`).
- `le_stabilizer_of_ker`: such a normal subgroup `U` stabilizes `[A]`: for
  `u ∈ U`, the conjugate `n ↦ A (u⁻¹ n u)` differs from `A` by the value of
  `A` at an element of `N ∩ U`, so it is equal to `A`.
- `isOpen_stabilizer`: hence the stabilizer is open.
- `exists_extension_stabilizer`: there is a representation `θ` of `H` on the
  carrier of `A` with `θ|_N = A` and open kernel. Extend `A` to `N ⊔ U`
  trivially on `U`. The subgroup `N ⊔ U` is open and normal, and `G / (N ⊔ U)`
  is cyclic, generated by some `g`; then `H / (N ⊔ U)` is cyclic, generated by
  `g^[G:H]`, of order the relative index. The extension to `N ⊔ U` is
  invariant under conjugation by `g^[G:H]` because that element stabilizes
  `[A]` and fixes the trivial part on `U`. The extension lemma
  `exists_extension` of `Langlands.Representation.Clifford.Extension` then
  extends it to `H`, and the kernel contains the open subgroup `U ∩ H`.
