# An irreducible supercuspidal representation of `GL_{m+1}(F)` at level zero

Lean module: `Langlands.Representation.LevelZero.Existence`, namespace `Langlands.LevelZero`.

## Generic results

- `orbitSpan ρ x`: the subrepresentation spanned over `ℂ` by the orbit of `x`;
  `mem_orbitSpan_self`.
- `moduleFinite_asModule_of_span_orbit`: if the orbit of `v` spans `V` over `ℂ`, then
  `ρ.asModule` is a finitely generated (cyclic) module over the group algebra, generated by `v`;
  each `ρ g v` is `single g 1 • v` and each `c • u` is `single 1 c • u`
  (`Representation.single_smul`).
- `compactOpen_cofinal`: every open subgroup of `GL_n(F)` contains a compact open subgroup.

## Main result

`exists_irreducible_supercuspidal_smoothRep F m`: for a nonarchimedean local field `F : Type u`
and `m : ℕ` there is `τ : SmoothRep.{0, u, u} ℂ (GeneralLinear (m + 1) F)` that is irreducible,
admissible, and satisfies
`Subsingleton (Jacquet.Coinvariants τ.representation (B.conjugateUnipotent g))` for every block
shape `B` with `B.parabolic F ≠ ⊤` and every `g`.

**Proof.** Choose a uniformizer `ϖ` (`Iwasawa.exists_isUniformizingElement`), an irreducible
cuspidal finite-dimensional representation `σ` of `GL_{m+1}(k)`
(`FiniteGeneralLinear.exists_irreducible_cuspidal_fdRep`, the residue field being finite), and
an open subgroup `L` on which reduction is trivial. Let `E` be the smooth induced representation
`smoothInduced J Λ`; it is admissible (`smoothInduced_isAdmissible`). Let `v₀ ≠ 0` in `σ`,
`x₀ = f_{v₀}` (nonzero, since `x₀(1) = v₀`), and `A` the subrepresentation of `E` spanned by
the orbit of `x₀`. Then `A` is spanned by the orbit of `a₀ = x₀`, hence finitely generated over
the group algebra, and nonzero. By
`Aubert.General.JacquetCommonConstituent.exists_irreducible_quotient_of_moduleFinite` it has an
irreducible quotient `q : A → τ`.

- `τ` is admissible: `A` is admissible as a subrepresentation of `E`
  (`isAdmissible_of_injective`), and admissibility passes to quotients when compact open
  subgroups are cofinal (`Unramified.SphericalExistence.isAdmissible_of_surjective`).
- `w₀ = q(a₀)` is nonzero: otherwise `q` vanishes on the orbit of `a₀`, which spans `A`, so
  `q = 0`, contradicting surjectivity onto the nonzero `τ`. Since `τ` is irreducible, the orbit of
  `w₀` spans `τ` (`span_range_orbit_eq_top`).
- For `N = B.conjugateUnipotent g` and `h ∈ GL_{m+1}(F)`, `exists_sum_translate_eq_zero` gives a
  nonempty finite family `c i ∈ N` with `∑ i, E(c i) E(h) x₀ = 0`; since `A` sits injectively in
  `E`, the same holds in `A`, and applying `q` gives `∑ i, τ(c i) τ(h) w₀ = 0`. Hence `τ(h) w₀`
  lies in the coinvariant relations (`mem_relations_of_sum_eq_zero`), and the coinvariants vanish
  (`coinvariants_subsingleton_of_span`).

Provenance: Bushnell–Kutzko 1993 (`bushnell-kutzko-1993-admissible-dual`, SHA256
`811693b94c03fcbc2cfe33955bc5a00b0fc0b5645ca343f8cc1a3c6511de9c73`), Chapter 5 (simple types of
level zero) and Theorem (6.2.2), printed p. 203 (PDF p. 215): every irreducible representation
containing a simple type attached to a maximal order is supercuspidal. The Lean proof takes an
irreducible quotient of a finitely generated subrepresentation of the induced representation and
verifies admissibility and Jacquet vanishing directly; it does not prove that the compactly
induced representation is irreducible.
