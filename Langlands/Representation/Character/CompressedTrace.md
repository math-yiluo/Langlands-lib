# Simple modules and compressed traces

`compressedTrace` inserts a finite-dimensional vector space into the input of an endomorphism, projects its output back, and takes the trace there. The insertion and projection are linear maps over the ground field. In the comparison theorem the first pair of maps has nonzero compressed trace on the identity.

Given two inequivalent simple modules over the same algebra, every algebra-linear map between them is zero. The projection of their direct sum onto the first summand therefore commutes with all algebra-linear endomorphisms. Jacobson density realizes this projection on the images of the two finite bases by one algebra element. Its first compressed trace equals the trace of the compressed identity and its second equals zero. Equality of all compressed traces forces the modules to be equivalent. The argument is internal and uses the same density principle as the Library's finite-dimensional character comparison.
