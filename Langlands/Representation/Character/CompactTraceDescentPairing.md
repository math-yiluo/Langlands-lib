# A smoothed descent pairing detects compact interaction

Let \(G\) be a locally compact, totally disconnected Hausdorff topological group
and \(K\) a compact open subgroup. Let \(M\) be a compact second-countable
Hausdorff topological group with Borel Haar measure, and let \(i:M\to G\)
be a continuous homomorphism. Let \(\tau\) on \(K\) and \(\sigma\) on \(M\)
be finite-dimensional smooth complex representations. For a distribution \(D\)
on \(G\) and \(y\in G\), write \(\Phi(m)\) for \(D\) evaluated on the trace test
of \(\tau\) translated to \(yi(m)K\).

If the integral of \(\operatorname{tr}\sigma(m)\Phi(m)\) over \(M\) is nonzero,
`compactTraceDescentPairing` gives a nonzero value of \(\Phi\) and a nonzero
intertwiner on \(J=i^{-1}(K)\) from \(\tau\circ i|_J\) to \(\sigma|_J\).

On \(mJ\), the smoothing function \(\Phi(mj)\) is a linear functional of
\(\tau(i(j)^{-1})\). This follows by pulling the translation inside the trace
and using a matrix coefficient test on the endomorphism space of \(\tau\).
The slice character at \(mj\) is a linear functional of \(\sigma(j)\).
If the intertwining space is zero, the integral over \(J\) of their product
vanishes for each \(m\). Fubini and right invariance of the compact Haar
measure on \(M\) then give a zero integral over \(M\).

This gives the compact interaction step in Clozel, *Characters of nonconnected,
reductive p-adic groups*, §3.4, Lemma 5, p. 154.
