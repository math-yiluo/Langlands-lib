# Compact-type admissibility of irreducible characters

Let `G` be a locally compact Hausdorff totally disconnected group with its Borel σ-algebra. Let `π` be an irreducible admissible complex representation whose fixed space under the open subgroup `K₀` is nonzero. For a compact open subgroup `K` and a finite-dimensional smooth complex representation `τ` of `K`, the test on `yK` takes the value `tr τ(k)` at `yk` and vanishes off the coset. If the character of `π` is nonzero on this test for the chosen left Haar measure, then `τ` has a nonzero vector fixed by `K ∩ g K₀ g⁻¹` for some `g ∈ G`. With `τ` irreducible and `K ⊆ K₀`, this gives the intertwining conclusion of the admissibility argument for the full character distribution. [Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, §3.1–3.2, pp. 152–153](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)

The proof writes the compact trace test as a finite sum of diagonal coefficients in a basis. Left translation and restriction of Haar to `K` identify the corresponding action on `π` with the action on the restriction composed with `π(y)`. Nonvanishing of the character yields a nonzero coefficient operator. The orbit of a chosen nonzero `K₀`-fixed vector spans `π`, so that coefficient operator acts nontrivially on one of its translates. This translate is fixed by the intersection. Right invariance of Haar on the compact subgroup then makes the map from the coefficient parameter invariant under that intersection. Algebraic averaging of this parameter over its finite orbit preserves its nonzero image and gives a fixed vector of `τ`.
