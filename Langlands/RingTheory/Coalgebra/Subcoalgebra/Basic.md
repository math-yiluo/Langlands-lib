# Bundled subcoalgebras and the induced field coalgebra

For independent universes `u,v`, a commutative semiring `R : Type u`, and an actual Mathlib `R`-coalgebra `C : Type v`, `Langlands.Subcoalgebra R C` consists exactly of a submodule `D` and the condition that every `c ∈ D` has `Coalgebra.comul c` in the range of `TensorProduct.map D.subtype D.subtype`. No coalgebra structure or splitting is supplied as extra data.

The membership, extensionality, underlying submodule, order, zero and whole interfaces adapt the closure-only code from the Tau Ceti contributors, `Algebra/Coalgebra/Subcoalgebra/Basic.lean`, commit `8428d621`, registered as `tauceti-subcoalgebra-basic-map-8428d621`, under Apache 2.0. The element type argument to `PartialOrder.ofSetLike` is updated for the pinned Mathlib.

The ordinary subcoalgebra notion is the one used by Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017), proof of Theorem 12.18, printed pp. 237–238 (registered source `milne-2017-algebraic-groups`). Milne writes the comultiplication closure and uses the restricted counit. The official errata snapshot of 2026-11-02 makes no correction to this passage. Bundling, the zero and whole objects, and the Lean structural laws are implementation and derived content.

For a field `k : Type u`, `C : Type v` with `[AddCommGroup C] [Module k C] [Coalgebra k C]`, and every `D : Subcoalgebra k C`, the carrier is the actual subtype of its submodule, with that submodule's inherited additive group and `k`-module. There is no cocommutativity, finite dimensionality, nonzero-carrier, or universe equality assumption.

`tensorSquareInclusion D` is `TensorProduct.map D.carrier.subtype D.carrier.subtype`. It is injective because vector spaces are free, hence flat, and tensor products of injective linear maps are injective under these flatness hypotheses. `tensorSquareEquivRange D` identifies `D ⊗[k] D` with the range of this inclusion. `carrierComul D` first restricts the ambient comultiplication to `D`, corestricts to this range using the closure field, and applies the inverse of that equivalence. The counit is the ambient counit composed with the actual inclusion.

The constructed `instCoalgebraStruct` satisfies `map_comul_subtype` and `counit_subtype`: inclusion commutes with comultiplication and counit. The map-level form of the first identity is `map_comul_subtype_comp`. These identities use the ambient structure on `C` and the induced structure on `D`.

The derived helper `coalgebraOfInjective` is quantified over independently sized vector spaces `A,B` over an arbitrary field. Given structure maps on `A`, an actual coalgebra on `B`, and an injective linear map preserving these two maps pointwise, it proves all three coalgebra laws on `A`. For each counitality law, the implemented proof maps into `k ⊗[k] B` or `B ⊗[k] k` by an injective tensor map; a commuting diagram proved on pure tensors reduces the result to the corresponding ambient counit law. For coassociativity it maps injectively into `B ⊗[k] (B ⊗[k] B)`, checks both diagrams on pure tensors using naturality of the associator, and applies ambient coassociativity. `instCoalgebra D` applies this helper to the structure maps constructed from closure, so no extra coalgebra certificate is an input to a subcoalgebra.

`subtype D : D →ₗc[k] C` has the actual submodule inclusion as its underlying linear map (`subtype_toLinearMap`), evaluates to the subtype value (`subtype_apply`), and is injective (`subtype_injective`). The zero and whole subcoalgebras constructed in the general part receive the same carrier construction, including the zero coalgebra. Their underlying submodules are literally bottom and top. `mem_bot` characterizes zero membership, `mem_top` gives full membership, and `ext` identifies subcoalgebras by membership. These are derived structural laws packaged with the construction, rather than a proof of the finite-subcoalgebra existence theorem or any descent assertion.
