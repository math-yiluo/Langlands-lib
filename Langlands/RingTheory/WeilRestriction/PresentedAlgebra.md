# The algebra of a finite projective Weil restriction

Let `B` be a finite projective algebra over a commutative ring `A`. For a presentation `P` of a commutative `B`-algebra `C`, `OfPresentation A B P` represents the functor sending a commutative `A`-algebra `T` to algebra maps `C →ₐ[B] B ⊗[A] T`. The universal point is the map with values in the tensor product with the representing algebra itself. The equivalence `homEquiv` specializes this point along a map to `T`.

Finite projectivity supplies a finite family of vectors and linear functionals that reconstruct every element of `B`. Each generator of `P` gets one polynomial variable per functional. There are two sets of equations. The first makes each tuple of variables equal its coordinates after reconstruction, which accounts for relations among the chosen vectors. The second sets every coordinate of each evaluated defining relation of `P` to zero. In the quotient the universal tuples satisfy the relations of `C`. Conversely, the coordinates of any tensor-valued point give a map from the quotient. Reconstruction and the two sets of equations prove that these assignments are inverse.

Finite generation of the chosen generator set gives a finite-type algebra even with infinitely many relations. For finitely presented `C`, `Langlands.Algebra.WeilRestriction A B C` uses Mathlib's chosen finite presentation and is itself finitely presented. The file proves naturality in the test algebra, constructs the map induced by a homomorphism in `C` (which sends the universal point to the universal point and the identity to the identity), and identifies maps out of the restriction of a base-changed algebra using the tensor product's universal property.

The module imports [extensionality for pushouts](../IsTensorProduct/PushoutExt.md) and [base-change maps on pure tensors](../TensorProduct/BaseChangeMaps.md), so that importing it makes those lemmas available as well. [Algebra maps out of a presentation](../Extension/Presentation/MapsOut.md)
