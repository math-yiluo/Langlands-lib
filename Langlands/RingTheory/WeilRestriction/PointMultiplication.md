# Multiplication on the points of a Weil restriction

Let `H` be a finitely presented commutative bialgebra over a finite projective `A`-algebra `B`. The representing equivalence transports convolution of tensor-valued points of `H` to multiplication on the points of `W = WeilRestriction A B H`. This multiplication is associative and commutes with changing the test algebra.

Multiplying the two canonical maps into `W ⊗[A] W` gives the comultiplication on `W`. The unit point with values over `A` gives the counit. The proof reads the bialgebra identities as associativity and the two unit laws for points. Three identities for the tensor associator identify the inclusions of each factor in that calculation.

With this bialgebra structure, `pointsMulEquiv` identifies convolution on points of `W` with convolution on the classified points of `H`. It is natural in the test algebra. [Construction of the representing equivalence](PresentedAlgebra.md)
