# Generators of a quotient-Grassmannian chart algebra

The definition `Module.Grassmannian.ChartAlgebraGeneration.T` is the
subalgebra generated by quotient-basis coordinates of a supplied family
of module elements. The theorem
`Module.Grassmannian.adjoin_eq_top_of_isOpenImmersion_of_opensRange_eq`
proves that this subalgebra is the entire algebra when the family spans
the original module and the supplied affine test is an open chart of a
natural representing scheme.

## Objects and exact hypotheses

All ring and module carriers, algebra tests and index types are at
universe zero. Let `R` be any commutative ring, `M` any `R`-module with an
additive commutative group, and `k : ℕ`. Mathlib's
`Module.Grassmannian A (A ⊗[R] M) k` consists of submodules whose
quotients are finite projective of constant stalk rank `k`. The rank
therefore belongs to the quotient. The maps used throughout are the
existing `Module.Grassmannian.map` under arbitrary `R`-algebra
homomorphisms.

For `x : Fin k → M` and a commutative `R`-algebra `S`, the imported type
`ChartLinear.Chart R x S` consists of points `N` for which

\[
 (S^k\longrightarrow (S\otimes_R M)/N),\qquad
 c\longmapsto\sum_j c_j q_N(1\otimes x_j)
\]

is bijective, where `q_N = N.toSubmodule.mkQ`. Its inverse gives the
unique coordinate vector of each `q_N(1 ⊗ m)`.
For any family `g : ι → M`, the definition `T x N g` is

\[
 R\bigl[c_i\ \bigm|\ a\in\iota,\ i\in\operatorname{Fin}k,\
       \textstyle\sum_j c_jq_N(1\otimes x_j)=q_N(1\otimes g_a)\bigr]
       \ \subseteq S.
\]

This is the literal `Algebra.adjoin` of the displayed existential set.
The definition requires the supplied point to belong to the `x` chart;
it does not require `g` to span `M`. Coordinate uniqueness ensures that
the existential coefficients are exactly the coordinates just described.

The exported theorem additionally takes the following data.

- A scheme `Gr : Scheme.{0}`, a morphism `p : Gr ⟶ Spec R`, and
  equivalences `pt A` from the actual quotient Grassmannian over each
  commutative `R`-algebra `A` to actual morphisms `Spec A ⟶ Gr` over
  `Spec R`.
- Naturality of these equivalences for every `φ : A →ₐ[R] B`, with
  `(pt B (Module.Grassmannian.map φ N)).1 =
  Spec φ ≫ (pt A N).1`.
- Opens `V x` of `Gr` with the criterion, for every affine test `N`,
  that the whole image of `(pt A N).1` lies in `V x` if and only if
  the displayed `x` combination map is bijective.
- A family `g : ι → M` spanning `M` over `R`, a particular `x`, a
  commutative `R`-algebra `S`, and a point `N` over `S` such that
  `(pt S N).1` is an open immersion and its `opensRange` is exactly `V x`.

The conclusion is that the displayed `Algebra.adjoin` equals `⊤`.
There is no finite generation, freeness, projectivity or nontriviality
assumption on `R`, `M` or `S`, no positivity assumption on `k`, and no
finiteness assumption on `ι`. The theorem assumes the stated
representation and chart data; it constructs neither of them. It also
does not require that the full family `V` covers `Gr` or that each `V x`
has separately been proved affine.

## Implemented proof

The abbreviation `ptx` reuses the published concrete equivalence
`ChartLinear.chartEquiv` between chart points and `R`-linear coordinate
maps `ψ : M →ₗ[R] (Fin k → A)` normalized by
`ψ (x j) = Pi.single j 1`. The lemma `sum_ptx_smul` is the inverse
identity for `ChartLinear.coordEquiv`. The lemma `ptx_natural` takes a
coordinate of the published `chartEquiv_map` identity and supplies the
chart-membership proof `map_mem_chart`. Thus these coordinates commute
with the actual Grassmannian base changes.

By `Algebra.subset_adjoin`, all coordinates of every `g a` lie in
`T x N g`. The lemma `ptx_mem` applies `Submodule.span_induction` to
extend this membership to every `m : M`, using the spanning hypothesis,
linearity and closure of the subalgebra under scalar multiplication.
The definition `ψT` therefore views the coordinate map as an `R`-linear
map into `Fin k → T x N g`. Its normalization follows by checking the
underlying coefficients. The inverse chart equivalence gives a point
`NT` over this subalgebra. Naturality and injectivity of the coordinate
equivalence prove `map_val_NT`: base change of `NT` along the actual
subalgebra inclusion is `N`.

For the exported theorem, the image equality and the chart criterion
first prove that `N` belongs to the `x` chart. Write `T'` for its
coefficient subalgebra. The point `NT'` over `T'` determines
`t : Spec T' ⟶ Gr`. Its chart membership puts the image of `t` inside
the image of the open immersion `j = (pt S N).1`.
`IsOpenImmersion.lift` produces `l : Spec T' ⟶ Spec S` with `l ≫ j = t`.

The fully faithful spectrum functor recovers a ring homomorphism
`σ : S →+* T'` whose spectrum is `l`. Naturality and `map_val_NT`
give `Spec(T'.val) ≫ t = j`. Substitution and cancellation of the
monomorphism `j` yield `Spec(T'.val) ≫ l = 𝟙`. By
`Spec.map_eq_id`, the composite of `σ` followed by `T'.val` is the
identity on `S`. Every element of `S` consequently lies in `T'`, which
proves `T' = ⊤`. The proof only uses `σ` as a ring homomorphism; no
unproved compatibility with the `R`-algebra structures is needed.

## Scope within the CM task

This is a conditional coordinate-algebra result for an already supplied
Grassmannian representation. It gives neither a representing scheme nor
a projective embedding. It asserts no finite type, smoothness,
properness or projectivity conclusion.

It is an intermediate prerequisite for the geometric construction. The
polarized abelian moduli scheme, its PEL conditions, the natural
comparison with the normalized Harris CM presheaf, and the
admissibility, smoothness and projectivity in the host
`DivisionCMRepresentabilityStatement` remain separate obligations.
