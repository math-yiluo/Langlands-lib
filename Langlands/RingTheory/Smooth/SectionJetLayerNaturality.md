# Lift independence and coordinate changes for actual augmentation layers

Let `R` and `A` be commutative rings, with `A` an `R`-algebra, and let
`ε : A →ₐ[R] R` be an augmentation. Put `I = ker ε`. All results in this
module hold over an arbitrary commutative base ring. Formal smoothness and
finite generation are not assumed. The index types of the two coordinate
systems may differ and have independent universes.

This is an internal continuation of `SectionJetHomogeneousLayers`. Its
provenance is the Library's ring-generic
`Langlands.GroupScheme.TorusUnipotence.sub_mem_pow_succ` and the actual
homogeneous layer evaluation map. No external source claim is added.

`sub_mem_sq_of_classMap_eq` translates equality of the cotangent classes of
two elements of `I` into their difference lying in `I²`.
`aeval_sub_aeval_mem_pow_succ` then proves that two systems of lifts with the
same cotangent classes have evaluations agreeing modulo `I^(d+1)` on the
`d`th power of the variable ideal. The implemented proof applies the existing
two-algebra-map comparison to the variable ideal, generated by the variables;
each polynomial differs from its constant term by an element of that ideal.
It verifies explicitly that both evaluations take the variable ideal into `I`.

Consequently, `homogeneousToLayer_eq_of_classMap_eq` states that the actual
maps from homogeneous degree `n+1` polynomials to `Layer ε n` are equal for
any two such systems of lifts. The proof uses equality modulo `I^(n+2)` and
the quotient equality criterion. This proves independence of lifts for the
fixed cotangent-coordinate classes.

`homogeneousSubstitution f d` is substitution by a family of homogeneous
linear polynomials. It is an `R`-linear map on degree-`d` homogeneous
submodules. `homogeneousSubstitution_val` computes it by polynomial
evaluation. `augmentation_aeval_linear` verifies that a linear form
evaluated in augmentation-ideal elements again lies in `I`.
`homogeneousToLayer_substitution` states that such a substitution commutes
with actual layer evaluation whenever the substituted lifts give the desired
new cotangent classes. The proof combines composition of polynomial
evaluation with independence of lifts.

`linearForm c` is the polynomial `∑ s, c(s) • X s` associated to a finitely
supported coefficient vector. Its degree-one homogeneity and evaluation
formula are proved explicitly. For actual cotangent bases `b` and `b'`,
`cotangentBasisChange b b' d` substitutes the linear forms whose columns are
`b.repr (b' t)`. It maps degree-`d` polynomials in the `b'` variables to
degree-`d` polynomials in the `b` variables.

Finally, `homogeneousToLayer_cotangentBasisChange` proves that evaluating
after this specified substitution equals evaluating in arbitrary lifts of
the new basis. The cotangent calculation reduces to the reconstruction
identity for `b.repr`. In the formally smooth situation of
`SectionJetHomogeneousLayers`, both actual evaluation maps are isomorphisms,
so this equation computes the change of the actual layer coordinates. This
module does not construct sheaf gluing or a determinant formula.
