# Closed base locus for containing a neighborhood of a section

Let \(A\) be a smooth algebra over a noetherian ring \(R\), with section given
by an augmentation \(A \to R\). Each augmentation jet is a finite projective
\(R\)-module. For an ideal \(J\) of \(A\), take the sum of the coefficient
ideals of its images in the jets. Dual contractions and the arbitrary scalar
extension comparison for jets show that a test \(R\)-algebra kills this
coefficient ideal exactly when the base-changed closed subscheme contains all
jets of the base-changed section.

Over the universal quotient of \(R\) by the coefficient ideal, the extended
ideal lies in all positive powers of the augmentation ideal. Artin–Rees and
Nakayama give a function that equals one on the section and annihilates the
extended ideal. Its principal open is a neighborhood of the entire section
contained in the closed subscheme. The function and its annihilation identity
extend to every algebra over the universal quotient. Conversely, if a function
equals one on the section and annihilates the extended ideal, multiplication
by one minus that function fixes the ideal. This places the ideal in every
positive power of the augmentation ideal.

This is a derived ingredient for the construction of the smoothness locus
along a section in Raynaud, SGA 3, Exposé XV, Lemma 5.4, Gille–Polo recomposed
pp. 267–268. It applies the jet equations to a closed subscheme inside a
supplied smooth ambient algebra.
