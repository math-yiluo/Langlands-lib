# Finite-jet detection near a smooth section

Let `B` be a commutative `R`-algebra with an augmentation `ε : B →ₐ[R] R`
and let `J` be an ideal of `B`. The ideal `Algebra.SectionJet.jetCoefficientIdeal ε J n`
is generated by the values of all `R`-linear forms on the image of `J` in
`B / (ker ε)^(n + 1)`. The coefficient ideals increase with `n`, since a
linear form on a lower jet can be composed with truncation from a higher jet.
When `B` is formally smooth and of finite type over `R`, its jet algebra is
projective as an `R`-module. Dual contraction then shows that an `R`-algebra
`S` kills the coefficient ideal exactly when every element of `J` maps to zero
in the scalar extension of that jet.

If `R` is Noetherian, `exists_order_of_vanishing_near_section` gives a
single order `n` for every test algebra `S`. If `J` vanishes on that jet
over `S`, there is an element of `S ⊗[R] B` which evaluates to `1` at the
base-changed section and annihilates the image of `J`. Its principal open
contains the section, and the extension of `J` vanishes there. The test
algebra can be arbitrary.

The proof stabilizes the ascending chain of coefficient ideals and uses
the quotient of `R` by its stable value as the universal test algebra.
On it every jet vanishes. The arbitrary scalar-extension comparison for
section jets places the image of `J` in the intersection of the powers of
the new augmentation ideal. Artin–Rees and the determinant form of
Nakayama's lemma give an element of that ideal acting as the identity on
the intersection. Subtracting it from `1` gives the annihilator, which
then maps to each test algebra satisfying the order-`n` condition.

This internal lemma implements the affine coefficient and local vanishing
steps of the Noetherian argument in SGA 3, Exposé XI, proof of
Corollary 6.9, Gille–Polo recomposition, printed p. 116.
