# Conormal maps and polynomial coordinates near a section

An augmentation-preserving algebra map induces a linear map on the conormal
modules of the sections. The canonical splitting of the first jet into the
base ring and its conormal module identifies the map on first jets with the
identity on the base and this conormal map. A surjective augmented algebra
map gives a surjective conormal map, which gives finiteness of the conormal
of a finite-type algebra by taking a finite polynomial presentation.

If a principal open contains the whole section, the map to its coordinate
algebra induces an isomorphism on first jets and hence on conormal modules.
Projectivity of the conormal in a smooth principal neighborhood then gives
projectivity before localization. When lifts of a conormal basis are
specified, their images give a conormal basis in the smooth localization.
The polynomial coordinate comparison there and the invariance of all jets
under localization show that evaluation at the original lifts gives
isomorphisms on all section jets.

This is the affine converse in the construction of the smoothness locus
along the section used in SGA 3, Exposé XV, Lemma 5.4. The proof uses full
section jets and their first-order splitting.
