# Closed base locus for isomorphisms on section jets

Let \(P\) be a smooth algebra over a commutative ring \(R\), and let
\(f : P \to A\) preserve given augmentations to \(R\). Suppose the induced
maps between the actual quotients by powers of the augmentation ideals are
surjective at every order. Define an ideal of \(R\) by taking all coefficients
of the kernels, using the duals of the finite projective jets of \(P\).

For any \(R\)-algebra \(B\), the actual jets after scalar extension identify
with the tensor extensions of the original jets, compatibly with \(f\).
Right exactness of tensor product gives surjectivity and identifies the kernel
of each new map with the ideal generated by the image of the corresponding
original kernel. The coefficient criterion for projective tensor factors shows
that these images vanish at every order precisely when \(R \to B\) kills the
specified ideal. The quotient by this ideal therefore represents the condition
that all the actual jet maps are isomorphisms, including on nonflat tests.

This is a derived ingredient for the smoothness locus along a section in
Raynaud, SGA 3, Exposé XV, Lemma 5.4, Gille–Polo recomposition, pp. 267–268.
Raynaud imposes coefficient equations on successive graded layers. This
construction uses the same coefficient principle on the full jets of a
supplied smooth source algebra with surjective jet maps.
