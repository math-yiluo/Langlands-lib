# Testing cocommutativity with two points

For a commutative bialgebra `H`, the two inclusions of `H` into its tensor square commute under convolution exactly when the coalgebra is cocommutative.

The product in one order is the comultiplication. Interchanging the two tensor factors exchanges the inclusions and carries their convolution product to the reverse product. Equality of the products therefore says exactly that the flip fixes the comultiplication. [Comultiplication as a convolution product](../Coalgebra/TensorConvolution.md)
