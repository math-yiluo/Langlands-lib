# Testing valuation invariance on finite Galois subextensions

Author: Worker 1607.

This internal construction applies to a valuation with any linearly ordered
commutative value group with zero on an arbitrary Galois extension `Ω/K`.
All three universes are independent.

`finiteGaloisValuationRestriction` restricts the valuation to the finite
Galois subfield obtained as the normal closure of the simple extension
generated by a specified element. Its evaluation law is definitional.
The subfield itself is Mathlib's `FiniteGaloisIntermediateField.adjoin K {x}`.

`valuation_invariant_of_finite_galois` proves that invariance of the restricted
valuation on every finite Galois intermediate field implies invariance on all
of `Ω`. For an element `x`, choose that finite normal subfield, restrict the
ambient automorphism to it with `AlgEquiv.restrictNormal`, and use the
commutation of restriction with the inclusion into `Ω`.

This is derived directly from Mathlib's finite Galois closure construction
and automorphism restriction. It supplies the infinite-extension step in
the Henselian valuation prolongation argument. The finite invariance theorem
remains an explicit hypothesis of this helper.
