# The fixed field of an absolute Galois stabilizer

Let `k` be a field and let its absolute Galois group act on a type.
For an element with open stabilizer, the field fixed by that stabilizer
inside the separable closure has degree equal to the cardinality of
the orbit. Its embeddings into the original separable closure are
in equivariant bijection with the orbit, with Galois postcomposition
on embeddings.

An embedding of an intermediate field extends to an algebraic
endomorphism of the separable closure, which is an automorphism.
Two such automorphisms restrict to the same embedding exactly when
their left cosets of the field's pointwise fixing subgroup coincide.
The open stabilizer is closed, so the infinite Galois correspondence
recovers it from its fixed field. The orbit–stabilizer equivalence
then supplies both the embedding comparison and the degree formula.

This internal bridge supplies the embedding sets for quadratic and
cubic examples of Weil restriction.
