# Countably many Galois extensions suffice

Author: Worker 105.

Helper module for the second countability of rational points over local fields
(`EtaleChartSecondCountable`), used for Conrad's p.17 claims
(`Langlands.Global.AdelicSpacePointTopologyStatement`).

## Declarations

Namespace `Langlands.FieldTheory.CountableGaloisFamily`.

- `HasCountableGaloisFamily K`: there are countably many finite Galois extensions `L i` of `K`
  (in the universe of `K`) such that every finite separable extension of `K` admits a
  `K`-algebra map into some `L i`.
- `finiteDimensional_algebraicClosure`: let `K` have characteristic zero, with every
  irreducible polynomial of degree at most two. Then the algebraic closure of `K` is
  finite-dimensional.
  - Choose `α` of maximal degree. For any `β`, the field `K(α, β)` has a primitive element,
    whose degree is at most that of `α`.
  - Hence `K(α, β) = K(α)`, and `K(α)` is the whole algebraic closure.
- `hasCountableGaloisFamily_of_natDegree_le_two`: in that case the family consisting of the
  algebraic closure works, for instance for `ℝ` and `ℂ`.
- `exists_dense_generator`: let `K` be complete and non-archimedean, and `D ⊆ K` dense. Every
  finite separable extension `E/K` is generated by a root `γ ∈ E` of a monic polynomial
  `X^n + ∑ a_k X^k` with `a_k ∈ D`. The proof is the Krasner argument of
  `HenselianFiniteEtaleKrasner`, with approximation from `D`:
  - A primitive element `β` has minimal polynomial `h`. A monic `g₀` with coefficients close
    to those of `h` has a root `γ` close to `β` in the algebraic closure (spectral norm).
  - Krasner's lemma (`IsKrasner.krasner`) gives `K(β) = K(γ)`.
- `hasCountableGaloisFamily_of_isUltrametricDist`: let `K` be complete non-archimedean with a
  countable dense subset `D`.
  - The family is indexed by the finite coefficient lists `(n, a : Fin n → D)`. It consists of
    the separable closures of `K` in the splitting fields of the corresponding polynomials.
    These are Galois (`separableClosure.isGalois`).
  - A generator `γ` as above gives `E = K(γ) →ₐ[K]` splitting field, because its minimal
    polynomial divides `g₀` (`IntermediateField.algHomAdjoinIntegralEquiv`).
  - The image lands in the separable closure because `E` is separable.
