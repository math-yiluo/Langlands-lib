# Rings of integers of the prolonged valuation on a finite separable extension

Let `v : Valuation K (WithZero (Multiplicative ℤ))` have Henselian valuation
ring `v.valuationSubring`, and let `E/K` be a separable extension, in the same
universe as `K`. Write `w` for the normalized prolonged additive valuation
`henselianSeparableAddValuation v hH E` on `E`, with values in `WithTop ℝ`.

## The ring of integers

The declaration `henselianSeparableIntegers v hH E` is the valuation subring
`{x ∈ E | 0 ≤ w x}` of `E`. Closure under products, sums, negation and the
constants is the additive-valuation axioms. For the valuation-subring axiom,
a nonzero `x` has a finite real value `t`; then `x` lies in the subring when
`0 ≤ t`, and otherwise `x⁻¹` does, since `w(x⁻¹) = -t`.

`mem_henselianSeparableIntegers` unfolds membership. The lemma
`henselianSeparableAddValuation_base_pos_iff` states that a base scalar
`a ∈ K` has positive prolonged value exactly when `v a < 1`; it is the
published value formula for base scalars (minus the integer logarithm of
`v a`) read through the order of the integer logarithm.

## Structure over the base valuation ring

The algebra `henselianSeparableIntegers.algebra` is the codomain restriction
of `algebraMap K E` on `v.valuationSubring`, using the published criterion
that base scalars of nonnegative prolonged value are exactly the base
integers. The tower `v.valuationSubring → henselianSeparableIntegers → E` is
a scalar tower, and `henselianSeparableIntegers.coe_algebraMap` records that
the image of `a` is `algebraMap K E a`.

`henselianSeparableIntegersEquivDepthLattice` is the identity on elements,
as a `v.valuationSubring`-linear equivalence between the ring of integers and
the published depth-zero lattice `henselianSeparableDepthLattice v hH E 0`.
Under finite dimensionality of `E/K` and surjectivity of `v`, the published
finiteness and freeness of that lattice transfer along the equivalence:
`henselianSeparableIntegers_finite` and `henselianSeparableIntegers_free`.

## Maximal ideal and residue field

`mem_maximalIdeal_henselianSeparableIntegers` states that an integer lies in
the maximal ideal exactly when its value is positive. A non-unit of value `0`
would be nonzero with an integral inverse; conversely a unit of positive
value would give the product with its inverse a positive value, while that
product is `1`, of value `0`.

`henselianSeparableIntegers.isLocalHom` proves that the structure map from
the base valuation ring is a local homomorphism: an element of the base
maximal ideal has `v a < 1`, hence positive prolonged value, hence lies in
the maximal ideal upstairs.

`henselianSeparableIntegers_residueField_isAlgClosed` assumes in addition
that the residue field of `v.valuationSubring` is algebraically closed. The
ring of integers is finite over the base ring, so the residue field extension
is finite, hence integral, and Mathlib's
`IsAlgClosed.algebraMap_bijective_of_isIntegral` makes the residue algebra
map a ring isomorphism; algebraic closedness transfers along it.

`henselianSeparable_exists_quadratic_approxRoot` uses this residue field:
for integers `t, n ∈ E` the reduced polynomial `X² - t̄ X + n̄` has a residue
root, any lift `b` of which satisfies `0 < w(b² - t b + n)`.

## Integer normalization

`henselianSeparable_exists_integer_scale` assumes finite dimensionality and
surjectivity of `v`. It provides a real number `c > 0` such that `c · w(a)`
is an integer for every nonzero `a ∈ E`, together with an element `ϖ` of
value `1 / c`. Writing `n = [E : K]`, the published lattice-value lemma puts
every nonzero value in `(1/n) ℤ`. The integers `m` with `m / n` a value of a
nonzero element form an additive subgroup of `ℤ` containing `n` (a base
scalar of value `1`), hence a nonzero cyclic subgroup generated by some `g`.
With `d = |g|` the scale is `c = n / d`: every value is `k d / n` for an
integer `k`, and an element of value `d / n` is the required `ϖ`.

## Provenance

This module is derived supporting material for Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits
theory: a new approach* (2023), the introduction to Appendix C and Section C.2,
printed pp.697–698 (PDF pp.728–729). There a root group over `K` is identified
with a Weil restriction from a finite separable extension `L/K`, its integral
models are obtained as Weil restrictions along `O_L/O` of models over the ring
of integers `O_L`, and a non-normalized valuation is reduced to a normalized
one "by a simple rescaling". The ring of integers, its local and residue
properties and the integer scale here are the corresponding field-level
inputs; the arguments are internal to this project and use only the published
normalized prolongation and its depth lattices. No root group, integral model,
apartment comparison, or assertion of KP Lemma C.5.3 is made here.
