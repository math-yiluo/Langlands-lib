# The represented CM tower over the completed unramified local field

For a finite place $v$ of the CM base field $F$, `Global.FiniteCompletion.CompletedUnramified v` is the completed maximal unramified extension of the actual finite-place completion $F_v$. Its $F$-algebra structure is the composite of the embeddings $F\to F_v$ and $F_v\to \widehat{F_v^{\mathrm{nr}}}$. The instance for `IsScalarTower` records this equality of maps. The arithmetic Frobenius on the completed field is the extension of the arithmetic Frobenius on the inertia fixed field of $F_v$.

`cmUnramifiedOverTower` and `cmUnramifiedAnalyticTower` specialize the CM scheme tower, assumed represented at each sufficiently small level, and its localized analytification to this complete field. Their maps are the original level and Hecke transporters after field extension and analytification. The field, scheme and atlas indices are in universe zero. `cmUnramifiedOverTowerBaseChangeIso` gives the natural base-change isomorphism from this scheme tower to the successive algebraic base changes through the finite-place completion. The scalar tower identifies the composite of the two field-spectrum maps, and composition of the over-category pullback functors supplies the isomorphism.

`cmUnramifiedSchemeBaseChangeIso` identifies the underlying schemes with the base-changed tower used by `RestrictedLevelTowerGalois`. The isomorphism is the identity on each actual fibre product. `cmUnramifiedSchemeAut` acts on the field factor at each level after restriction of scalars to $F$, with the contravariant spectrum convention. `cmUnramifiedSchemeFrobenius` is its arithmetic Frobenius specialization. These automorphisms are semilinear over the field of the tower.

`cmUnramifiedBaseAnalyticTower` uses the finite-place base norm whose spectral extension defines the completed unramified field. The isometric normed algebra embedding over that base gives `cmUnramifiedScalarExtendedAnalyticTower` via the localized analytic ground-field functor. Its scalar projection to the base point tower is natural along all transporters, as recorded by `cmUnramifiedScalarExtendedProjection`. The comparison of analytic scalar extension with the analytification of the algebraic base change is a separate assertion.

Harris, *Galois properties of cohomological automorphic forms on GL(n)* (1999), §4, pp. 308–309, identifies the CM field with the reflex field in the setting of (4.3) and uses the maximal unramified base on the Shimura side of that display. The Lean tower gives that base change of the represented schemes in the complete analytic setting, for any of the specified CM data. The spectral sequence and the uniformization isomorphism require additional input.

Berkovich, *Spectral theory and analytic geometry over non-Archimedean fields* (1990), Theorem 3.4.1, pp. 63–64, gives scheme analytification over a complete nontrivially valued nonarchimedean field. Berkovich, *Étale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces* (1993), §1.4, p. 30, constructs the analytic ground-field extension and projection.
