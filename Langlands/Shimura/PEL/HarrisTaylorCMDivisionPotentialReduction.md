# Potential good reduction in the Harris division case

The conclusion `HasPotentialGoodReduction` supplies an actual abelian
scheme over a local extension of the discrete valuation ring. The associated
extension of fraction fields is finite. The conclusion includes an actual
isomorphism from the original variety after scalar extension to the generic
fibre of the abelian scheme.

The theorem takes potential semistable reduction and the abelian reduction
criterion as explicit inputs. Potential semistable reduction provides a
Néron model after finite scalar extension. The original rational action and
normalized relative dimension base change to its generic fibre. The
rational cocharacter dimension is the rank of the special torus, which is
bounded by the normalized abelian dimension. The division obstruction makes
those cocharacters vanish, so the special torus has rank zero and the
geometric special identity component is proper. The abelian reduction
criterion turns the supplied Néron model into an abelian scheme, using the
original generic identification.

## Provenance

The division step is the potential good reduction argument of Kottwitz,
*Points on some Shimura varieties over finite fields* (1992), §5, printed
p. 392. The two reduction hypotheses are the formulations of Bosch,
Lütkebohmert and Raynaud, *Néron Models* (1990), §7.4, Theorems 1 and 5.
