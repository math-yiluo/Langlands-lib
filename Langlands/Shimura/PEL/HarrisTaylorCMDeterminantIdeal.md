# Coefficient ideals and affine loci of the normalized Harris determinant

Author: Worker 344.

This module is an internal construction derived from the published normalized
Harris determinant condition, its finite integer generator reduction, actual
Lie base change, and Mathlib's ideal sheaves and closed immersions. It constructs
a closed determinant locus inside a supplied affine family with globally free
Lie module. The family and its rational algebra action are inputs; the ambient
moduli problem is not constructed here.

## Coefficient ideals

In `Langlands.Shimura.HodgeDeterminant`, `coefficientIdeal q` is the ideal in a
commutative ring `R` generated by all coefficients of the actual multivariate
polynomial `q`. The variable type and coefficient ring may have different
universes. The coefficient ring may be zero, nonreduced, or non-Noetherian.

`coefficientIdeal_eq_span_support` replaces all coefficients by the coefficients
in the finite support of `q`; coefficients outside the support are zero.
Consequently `coefficientIdeal_fg` proves finite generation without a
Noetherian hypothesis. `coefficientIdeal_map` proves that formation of this
ideal commutes with every commutative ring homomorphism by mapping its spanning
set and using the coefficient formula for `MvPolynomial.map`.

`coefficientIdeal_le_ker_iff` says that a ring map kills the ideal exactly when
it kills the polynomial. Applying this to a difference gives
`coefficientIdeal_sub_le_ker_iff`: the ideal of `p - q` lies in the kernel exactly
when the mapped polynomials agree. Taking the identity map gives
`coefficientIdeal_eq_bot_iff`.

`coefficientIdeal_eq_span_of_coeff_zero` allows any prescribed finite set of
exponents containing every possibly nonzero coefficient. For finite variable
type and homogeneous polynomials of degrees `m` and `n`,
`coefficientIdeal_sub_eq_span_homogeneous` applies this to the published
`homogeneousEquationIndices`, the union of those two degrees. It does not
require `m = n`.

## A closed locus of global polynomial equations

In `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.PolynomialCoefficientLocus`, let `p` and `q` be
polynomials over the full ring of global sections of a supplied scheme `X`.
`idealSheaf p q` is `Scheme.IdealSheafData.ofIdealTop` applied to the coefficient
ideal of `p - q`. `scheme p q` is its actual subscheme, and `ι p q` is the actual
closed immersion into `X`. These definitions do not require `X` to be affine.

For affine `X`, `factors_iff` proves, for every scheme `T` and map `f : T ⟶ X`,
that there is a factorization through `ι p q` exactly when mapping coefficients
by `f.appTop.hom` makes `p` and `q` equal. The forward direction uses the
inclusion's kernel ideal sheaf and reads the resulting containment on the top
affine open. The reverse direction turns the coefficient kernel containment
into an ideal-sheaf kernel containment and applies
`IsClosedImmersion.lift`. No affineness of `T` or injectivity of its section map
is assumed. The theorem asserts existence of the factorization.

## The actual normalized Harris ideal

In `Langlands.Shimura.CentralSimpleUnitaryData`, the arithmetic hypotheses,
Harris signature charts `C`, structure map `p : S ⟶ Spec F`, abelian scheme
`B/S`, and rational action `i : A →ₐ[ℚ] End0 B` are the existing ones. Write `g`
for `D.harrisHodgePELOrderGenerators C` and `α` for the induced rational action
of the actual integral order. The finite family `g` generates the order as an
integer module and is fixed independently of `B` and `S`.

For a supplied finite free Lie chart `e` over an open `U` and a smaller open
`V : Over U`, `harrisTaylorCMDeterminantIdeal` is the coefficient ideal of

```
lieRationalSectionDeterminant p B α e g V
  - map (secHom p V.left).hom
      (D.kottwitzReflexDeterminantBaseChange C hΦ (fun k => (g k : A))
        (D.harrisTaylorReflexToCM C).toRingHom).
```

Both polynomials have coefficients in the full actual section ring
`Γ(S, V.left)`. The original conjugated reflex-to-CM scalar map is retained.
The definition uses no equality between the chart rank and the Hodge rank.

`harrisTaylorCMDeterminantIdeal_fg` proves finite generation by polynomial
support. `harrisTaylorCMDeterminantIdeal_eq_bot_iff` identifies vanishing with
the existing determinant equality for every finite integral-order family on
the same chart and smaller open, using the published generator reduction.
`harrisTaylorCMDeterminantIdeal_overlap` proves equality on any common smaller
open of two Lie charts, including charts with different indexing types and
without an assumed rank equality. It uses actual Lie determinant basis
independence. `harrisTaylorCMDeterminantIdeal_restrict` uses the actual section
restriction map and the determinant and reflex coefficient restriction laws.

`harrisTaylorCMDeterminantIdeal_baseChange` proves equality between the mapped
ideal and the normalized ideal on the actual pulled-back abelian scheme,
action, and Lie chart. Its proof combines the coefficient-ideal map law,
`lieRationalSectionDeterminant_baseChange_of_naturality`, the proved
`lieBaseChangeNaturalityStatements`, compatibility of the rational order
action with base change, and `secHom_comp_app`. The morphism of base schemes
is arbitrary.

With a supplied global finite free Lie chart,
`harrisTaylorCMDeterminantIdeal_eq_bot_iff_global` identifies vanishing on the
top open with `HarrisTaylorCMRationalLieDeterminantCondition`, which quantifies
over every finite order family, every finite Lie chart, and every smaller
open. The proof first restricts the global equation to the smaller open and
then changes chart using the overlap identity. Conversely, it specializes the
global condition to the supplied chart and top open.

## Normalized closed determinant locus

For that supplied global chart, `harrisTaylorCMDeterminantLocus` and
`harrisTaylorCMDeterminantLocusι` specialize the polynomial coefficient locus
to the actual Lie polynomial and normalized reflex polynomial above. The
inclusion is a closed immersion.

For affine `S`, `harrisTaylorCMDeterminantLocus_factors_iff` proves that, for
every scheme `T` and every `b : T ⟶ S`, factorization through this inclusion
is equivalent to the full existing normalized determinant condition on
`(baseChange b).obj B` with rational action
`(rationalBaseChangeEnd b B).comp i` and structure map `b ≫ p`. It reduces the
generic factorization criterion to the mapped ideal being zero, uses actual
ideal base change, and then uses the global-chart detection theorem on `T`.
The condition on the right includes all target Lie charts and smaller opens;
no global free chart on `T` is added as an assumption because it is obtained
by pulling back the supplied chart on `S`.

This construction does not prove that the normalized CM presheaf is
representable. Local-chart gluing, an ambient polarized abelian moduli
construction, Rosati and level comparisons, rational-isogeny equivalence,
admissibility, smoothness, projectivity, and the required natural comparison
with the existing presheaf remain separate obligations. The exact published
`DivisionCMRepresentabilityStatement` and its imported pairs are preserved.
