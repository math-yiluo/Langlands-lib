# Cocharacters in the division case of Harris CM reduction

An actual commutative group scheme over a discrete valuation ring is given
with the Néron mapping property and an isomorphism from the original abelian
variety to the generic fibre. Its rational generic endomorphisms act on the
rational cocharacters of each field-valued fibre.

The mapping property extends the underlying morphism of each integral
endomorphism. Generic-fibre uniqueness on the unit and the product implies
that the extension preserves the group law. Restriction and extension
therefore give an additive and multiplicative bijection on endomorphisms.
Rationalizing this bijection, pulling back along the field-valued point,
and postcomposing with the resulting endomorphisms gives the cocharacter
action.

The degree formulas for the quadratic CM extension and the central simple
algebra give \(\dim_{\mathbf Q} A = 2[E:\mathbf Q]n^2\). The Harris
dimension is \([E:\mathbf Q]n^2\). The actual rational \(A\)-action of the
abelian variety induces an action on the fibre cocharacters. A nonzero
vector in this module would generate a copy of the rational regular
module of the division algebra, contradicting the supplied rank bound.

## Provenance

The dimension argument is the covariant cocharacter form of Kottwitz,
*Points on some Shimura varieties over finite fields* (1992), §5, p. 392,
registered as `kottwitz-1992-points-shimura-finite-fields`. The proof uses
the Library's Néron mapping property, generic-fibre injectivity,
rationalized categories, and division-algebra dimension obstruction.
The Néron model, its generic identification, and the cocharacter rank bound
are hypotheses of the result.
