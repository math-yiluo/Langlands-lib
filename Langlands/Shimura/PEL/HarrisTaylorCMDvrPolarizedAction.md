# Extending a faithful polarized rational action over a DVR

`extendDvrPolarizedAction` constructs the actual polarized action on a
specified good abelian model. Its rational algebra action is the unique
extension of the given generic action and its polarization is the
extension obtained through the diagonal Poincaré bundle. It takes the
dual witnesses and the additivity terms for the actual duals. The
faithfulness of the resulting action follows by restricting any equality
to the generic fibre.

The rational Hom restriction is injective between the abelian model and
its actual dual. Postcomposition by the canonical comparison of duals
also acts injectively. Applying these two injective maps to the desired
involution equation identifies it with the equation on the generic fibre.
The identification uses naturality of rational dualization, obtained by
tensoring the integral dual naturality square. The generic involution
equation survives twice the polarization by its homogeneity.

The construction is derived from the Library's actual polarized-action
bundle, rational Hom restriction, involution compatibility and canonical
dual comparisons. The base, model and extended rational action are the
same as in the determinant and dimension reflection.
