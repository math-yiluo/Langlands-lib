# The normalized polarized action lift on an Artinian test

`normalizedPolarizedActionLiftsAlong_spec_of_geometric` uses
`determinantDimensionCondition_iff` to combine the normalized determinant and
dimension conditions on the lower action. The geometric lifting hypothesis
then supplies an upper positive polarized action with an equivariant rational
isometry after restriction. The Artinian normalization theorem reflects the
joint condition along this isomorphism, and the same equivalence gives the
determinant and dimension conditions on the upper action separately. The
rational polarization isometry is the same comparison used in
`NormalizedPolarizedActionLiftsAlong`.
