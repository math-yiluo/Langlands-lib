# Reflection of the normalized joint condition on an equivariant Artinian lift

The normalized Lie determinant and the prescribed geometric dimension on the
thicker representative follow from the corresponding conditions on a rationally
isomorphic equivariant representative on the smaller Artinian base. The
thicker base has an arbitrary structure morphism to `Spec F` for the CM test
base field `F`, and the smaller base uses its restriction.

For the determinant, equality of actual rational action classes transfers
the condition to the pullback. The previously proved Artinian reflection
recovers the determinant on the thicker representative. For the dimension,
the thicker abelian scheme has connected total space over the one-point
Artinian spectrum, so its smooth relative dimension is constant. The rational
isomorphism gives the prescribed relative dimension on the pulled-back
abelian scheme. Both dimensions then hold over the target residue field,
where uniqueness of the relative dimension identifies them.
