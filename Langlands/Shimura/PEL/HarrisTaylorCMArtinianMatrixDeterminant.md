# Rigidity of the rational action matrices

For a finite-dimensional simple algebra over a characteristic-zero field, use
its published regular trace pairing and a trace-dual basis to average the
identity matrix into an intertwiner between two representations. When the
representations agree over the residue field, the averaged matrix reduces to
the identity. Its determinant is then a unit over the original local ring.
This serves as the linear-algebra step for reflecting the Harris Lie
determinant from a residue-field test.
