# Original CM coefficient diagrams on a fixed source cover

Author: Worker 57.

This derived specialization simultaneously trivializes the original CM
coefficient sheaves through a fixed exponent on that exponent's original
coefficient cover. It constructs isomorphisms of entire diagrams, both for
integral scalars and for the original quotient-ring sheaves after scalar
restriction. Their components are the actual invariant-section evaluations
along the original cover maps. Under these evaluations, the transitions are
the original full-lattice reductions.

## Inputs and retained geometric premises

All field, algebra, scheme, group and coefficient-module carriers have
universe zero. The unitary input is
`D : CentralSimpleUnitaryData E F A n`, with E totally real, F CM and quadratic
over E, the displayed algebra structures on A and their scalar towers.
The algebra A is finite over ℚ. An imaginary quadratic field L embeds in F,
and `τ₀ : L →+* ℂ` and the Harris signature charts C are fixed.
The coefficient inputs are a number field K, a finite-dimensional K-module W,
an algebraic representation ρ of `D.rationalInvolution.baseChange K` on W,
a rational finite place p, a small CM level U, a natural scalar m and
`hm : m ≠ 0`.

The module retains the premises of
[HarrisTaylorCMCoefficientLocalScalars](HarrisTaylorCMCoefficientLocalScalars.md).
The tensor premises are
`pullbackTensorComparisonIsIsoStatement`, `PullbackTensorCompositionStatement`,
`PullbackTensorUnitCoherenceStatement`,
`ExplicitPullbackTensorAssociativityStatement`,
`TensorMonoidalLeftUnitorComparisonStatement` and
`PullbackTensorSymmetryStatement`. The Picard, duality and polarization premises
are `PicardZeroPullbackStatement`, `MumfordBundlePicardZeroStatement`,
`PicardZeroUniversalBaseChangeStatement`,
`DualAbelianBaseChangeNaturalityStatement` and
`PolarizationBaseChangeStatement`. There are also
`RelativeFiniteWeilTransitionStatement`,
`h : Global.RationalProfiniteIntegerAdeleComparison` and
`htor : TorsionFiniteEtaleStatement`.

The argument
`q : CMRepresentabilityStatement D C preservation composition unitCoherence
associativity comparison symmetry stability square universality
dualityNaturality polarizationStability compatibility h htor`
is the exact representability statement for the existing original moduli
presheaf on `ComponentwiseAdmissibleCMBaseCategory`. Its componentwise connected,
locally Noetherian and geometric-point conditions remain those of that

`existence : RelativeDualSchemeExistenceStatement`,

`hRigid : CMGeometricCoarseRigidity ... U.obj` are explicit.
No additional local trivialization or evaluation equation is supplied as input.

## Original covers, coefficients and scalar comparison

Write X for the original CM tower scheme at U,
`J = Scheme.smallEtaleTopology X`, and put

```text
R   = HarrisCoefficientIntegers K p,
I   = harrisCoefficientScalarIdeal K p m,
S_r = R / I^r,
φ_r = Ideal.Quotient.mk (I^r).
```

R is the full product of integral coefficient rings at all places of K over p.
The ideal I is generated by the image of m. Let M be the integral coefficient
diagram `cmHarrisCoefficientDiagram D ρ p U m hm`, indexed by `ℕᵒᵖ`.
Its component M_r at `op r` is the full lattice reduction. Its transition
maps are the existing integral-linear lattice reductions.

Let Y be `cmHarrisCoefficientEtaleDiagram ...`, with
`Y_r = Y.obj.obj (op r)`. This is the original coefficient-kernel cover at
`cmHarrisCoefficientLevel D ρ p U m hm r`, with residual group
`SmallCMResidualGroup D U` of that level. The imported
`cmHarrisCoefficientEtaleDiagram_isFiniteGeometricTorsor` proves the finite
geometric torsor condition under the retained premises. The diagram maps are
the original CM forgetting maps. The inverse-right deck action and the
inverse-unop coefficient convention `g ↦ unop(g)⁻¹` are inherited unchanged.

Write A for `cmHarrisCoefficientSystem ...`, an R-module sheaf diagram on J.
Its objects are the integral associated sheaves and its transitions are
coefficient reduction followed by the inverse of the proved original-cover
comparison. Write B_r for the original S_r-module sheaf
`cmHarrisCoefficientSheaf ... r`, and let

```text
F_r = sheafCompose J (ModuleCat.restrictScalars φ_r),
e_r = cmHarrisCoefficientSheafRestrictIso ... r : F_r(B_r) ≅ A_r.
```

The original sheaves after scalar restriction form the existing
`B̂ = cmHarrisRestrictedCoefficientSystem ...`, with
`B̂_r = F_r(B_r)`. Its transitions are the previously constructed transports
`e_i.hom ≫ A.map f ≫ e_j.inv`. The natural isomorphism
`cmHarrisRestrictedCoefficientSystemIso ... : B̂ ≅ A` has components e_r.
This module reuses these exact sheaves and maps.

For any etale object Z over X, let
`L_Z = J.overPullback (ModuleCat R) Z`, localization to the site over Z.
All constant R-module sheaves below are formed on J first and then localized
by L_Z. No separate identification with the constant-sheaf construction on
the slice site is needed.

## Restricted original evaluation along a cover map

Fix `r : ℕ`, `Z : X.Etale` and `a : Z ⟶ Y_r`.
Let N_r be the original quotient-ring coefficient action
`cmHarrisCoefficientAction ... r`, and let Q_r be its constant S_r-module
sheaf on J. Its scalar restriction compares with the constant R-module sheaf
on M_r by
`c_r = (constantRestrictScalarsIso J φ_r).app N_r.V`.
This is the canonical sheafification comparison; no definitional identity
between those constant sheaves is assumed.

The map `cmHarrisRestrictedCoefficientEvaluationAlong r a` is

```text
Ê_r(a) : L_Z(F_r(B_r)) → L_Z(constantSheaf_J(M_r)).
```

Its definition applies
`sheafCompose (J.over Z) (ModuleCat.restrictScalars φ_r)` to the original
quotient-ring `associatedEvaluationAlong J ... a`, then composes with
`L_Z.map c_r.hom`. Localization precomposes the section functor and scalar
restriction postcomposes it, so their order here agrees definitionally on
sections and restriction maps. The original evaluation first localizes the
invariant inclusion and then restricts along the graph
`⟨b ≫ a, 𝟙 T⟩ : T → Y_r × T` at an object `b : T → Z` of the slice.

The theorem `cmHarrisCoefficientSheafRestrictIso_hom_evaluationAlong` proves

```text
L_Z.map e_r.hom ≫ E_r(a) = Ê_r(a),
```

where E_r(a) is `etaleAssociatedEvaluationAlong` for the actual integral
CM diagrams at `op r`. This equality holds for every such map a, rather
than only the identity on Y_r.

The proof localizes the already proved equation
`cmHarrisCoefficientSheafRestrictIso_hom_inclusion`. After expanding
functorial composition, this replaces the integral invariant inclusion
preceded by e_r with the restricted original inclusion followed by the
product-sheaf map of c_r. The imported `productEvaluationAlong_natural`
moves that constant comparison past evaluation along a. Reassociation
then gives exactly the defining composite for Ê_r(a).

## Simultaneous trivializations and transition equations

Fix `k : ℕᵒᵖ`. An object i of `Under k` is an arrow
`i.hom : k → i.right`. Since the index category is `ℕᵒᵖ`, its exponent
`i.right.unop` is at most `k.unop`. A morphism `f : i → j` supplies
`f.right : i.right → j.right` with the commuting triangle.
The fixed cover is `Y.obj.obj k`.

The three new source diagrams have codomain
`Sheaf (J.over (Y.obj.obj k)) (ModuleCat R)`:

- `cmHarrisCoefficientSourceSystem k` is
  `Under.forget k ⋙ A ⋙ L_(Y.obj.obj k)`.
- `cmHarrisConstantCoefficientSourceSystem k` is
  `Under.forget k ⋙ M.obj ⋙ constantSheaf J (ModuleCat R) ⋙ L_(Y.obj.obj k)`.
- `cmHarrisRestrictedCoefficientSourceSystem k` is
  `Under.forget k ⋙ B̂ ⋙ L_(Y.obj.obj k)`.

The integral isomorphism `cmHarrisCoefficientSourceTrivialization k` applies
the published `etaleAssociatedSourceTrivialization` to the actual CM cover
and coefficient diagrams and their proved torsor condition. It identifies the
first source diagram with the constant coefficient source diagram. Its theorem
`_hom_app` identifies the component at i with
`E_(i.right.unop)(Y.obj.map i.hom)`.
The generic construction derives invertibility from geometric graph
splitting on the original torsors and derives naturality from the actual
varying-cover transition equation. No trivializing isomorphism is chosen
independently of evaluation.

`cmHarrisRestrictedCoefficientSourceTrivialization k` identifies the third
source diagram with the same constant diagram. It first whisks
`cmHarrisRestrictedCoefficientSystemIso` by `Under.forget k` and L, using
Mathlib's `Functor.isoWhiskerLeft` and `Functor.isoWhiskerRight`, and then
composes with the integral source trivialization.
Its `_hom_app` theorem proves that the component at i is exactly
`Ê_(i.right.unop)(Y.obj.map i.hom)`. Unfolding the component reduces this
claim to the evaluation comparison proved above. Thus the simultaneous
trivialization uses the original quotient-ring evaluation after scalar
restriction and canonical constant comparison.

For either A or B̂, write T for its source trivialization. The corresponding
`cmHarrisCoefficientSourceTrivialization_transition` and
`cmHarrisRestrictedCoefficientSourceTrivialization_transition` theorems state

```text
L.map (system.map f.right) ≫ T.hom.app j
  = T.hom.app i ≫ L.map (constantSheaf.map (M.obj.map f.right)).
```

Each proof is naturality of the constructed isomorphism. Its right-hand map
is exactly the original full-lattice reduction, and the component formula
identifies the left-hand evaluations along the actual forgetting maps.
Both transition equations carry `@[reassoc]`.

## Provenance and source scope

The new diagram constructions are internal deductions from
[HarrisTaylorCMCoefficientScalars](HarrisTaylorCMCoefficientScalars.md),
[HarrisTaylorCMCoefficientSystem](HarrisTaylorCMCoefficientSystem.md),
[CrossCoverEvaluation](../Coefficients/CrossCoverEvaluation.md) and
[VaryingCoverLocalTrivialization](../Coefficients/VaryingCoverLocalTrivialization.md).
The generic machinery is imported by name, and its actual maps and the
global scalar comparison are specialized. The literature motivates these
objects and conventions; the new categorical equalities are not attributed
to a quoted source theorem.

Harris, *Galois properties of cohomological automorphic forms on GL(n)*
(1999), Section 3, printed pp.306–308, one-based PDF pp.8–10, introduces
the algebraic representation, coefficient algebra at a rational prime,
stable integral lattice, inverse function-equivariance (3.7) and the
normal congruence quotient with finite free torsion coefficients before (3.8).
The registered source is
`harris-1999-cohomological-automorphic-forms-525a222c4489ee9d`, SHA-256
`525a222c4489ee9d3fe86a52c6c17ffb30fcc7c31f24c16a64669e9615085b26`.

Harris–Taylor, *The Geometry and Cohomology of Some Simple Shimura Varieties*
(2001), III.1, printed pp.90–91, one-based PDF pp.98–99, gives the small-level
moduli, right frame composition and normal finite-etale covers. III.2,
printed pp.94–95, one-based PDF pp.102–103, describes stable-lattice torsion
sheaves using inverse-equivariant functions on cover components and compatible
coefficient and cover maps. The registered source is
`harris-taylor-2001-geometry-cohomology-simple-shimura`, SHA-256
`5be28164b61d6f6cc28dd9e6b990d669504f3470860b230ebbc97e3da10af56b`.
The complete supplied five-page author errata list contains no correction
to these passages; this observation concerns only that supplied list.

The exponent k may be zero, and m may be any nonzero natural number. These
parameters inherit the existing constructors' extension of the sources'
positive prime-power parameters. No nontriviality, faithfulness or freeness
is deduced from that extension.

Only exponents reachable in `Under k`, hence at most `k.unop`, are
simultaneously trivialized on this fixed cover. Applicable representability,
dual existence, coarse rigidity, source canonical-model comparisons,
identification with the source's component-function sheaves, independence of
choices, arbitrary-scheme inverse images, trivial intersection of congruence
kernels and the completed lisse adic/cohomology object remain separate
obligations. This contribution is a conditional coefficient prerequisite.
