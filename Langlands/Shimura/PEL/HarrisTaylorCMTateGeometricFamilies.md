# Compatible geometric families of full Tate-normalized CM classes

Author: Worker 344.

This internal construction packages the actual normalized rational-isogeny
quotient at every geometric point of an existing connected admissible CM base,
at one fixed original Harris level. Canonical point comparison is the existing
actual Tate-path comparison; its forward map forgets to the original CM
comparison. Injectivity of forgetting proves the identity and composition laws.

A normalized geometric family is a dependent family of actual quotient classes
compatible under these canonical comparisons. Pointwise forgetting gives an
injective map to the original unpointed CM value. Evaluation at any actual
geometric point is an equivalence, with inverse given by canonical extension.

The CM-field structure gives an injective ring map from the characteristic-zero
field F to every geometric point field, proving characteristic zero there.
Under the explicit generic invariant-lattice realization and proper-fibre
ample-affine-open statements, and source-stabilizer containment of the fixed
level, surjectivity at one point follows from the existing normalized-class
existence result. Canonical extension and original family coherence prove
surjectivity of family forgetting. The resulting equivalence uses that literal
forgetful map. No unconditional existence, representing scheme, base-change
presheaf, or preservation by every level transporter is asserted.
