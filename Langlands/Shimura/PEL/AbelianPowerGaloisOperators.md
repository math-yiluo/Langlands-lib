# Geometric cohomology operators on powers of a PEL abelian scheme

Harris–Taylor, *The Geometry and Cohomology of Some Simple Shimura
Varieties* (2001), III.2, p. 98, uses multiplication by two on each
factor of an abelian power and the algebra generated by the PEL action
and the factor permutations. This module constructs the pullbacks of
these maps using the actual relative powers and their projections.
The source is registered as
`harris-taylor-2001-geometry-cohomology-simple-shimura` with PDF SHA-256
`5be28164b61d6f6cc28dd9e6b990d669504f3470860b230ebbc97e3da10af56b`.

For the actual relative power over `S`, its composition with the
structure map of `S` makes it a scheme over `F`. Its cohomology is
computed on its algebraic-closure base change. It carries the ordinary
Galois action of the algebraic-closure factor and is extended from
`ℚ_p` to the field `Λ` along the given field map. Pullback uses the
actual map over `F` induced from the relative endomorphism over `S`.

The PEL action `ι` takes values in the rationalized endomorphism ring of
the abelian scheme. For a PEL-algebra element on a specified factor,
`integralMultiple` chooses an actual endomorphism whose rationalization
is the given action multiplied by a nonzero integer. The generator is
pullback by that endomorphism on the factor, scaled by the inverse of
the integer. A permutation is realized by the actual scheme permutation
map. One expression in the free `Λ`-algebra on these generators is
interpreted on the chosen abelian representative by these pullbacks.
The resulting operator is applied after the degree-one selector. The variable for the polynomial bound is
its upper degree index, which the source takes to be twice the relative
abelian dimension.

Normalized projector identities, including idempotence, Galois
intertwining and identification with a coefficient representation, are
mathematical assertions about these evaluated operators.
