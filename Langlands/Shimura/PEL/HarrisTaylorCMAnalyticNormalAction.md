# Residual actions on analytic CM levels

Take a normal inclusion of represented small CM levels `V ≤ U` and a complete
nontrivially normed nonarchimedean field \(K\) with an algebra structure over the
CM base. The field, scheme and atlas indices are in universe zero. The residual
group is `U/V`. The existing inverse right transporter defines a
left action of `U` on the local analytic fine level. The analytic tower
inherits the original target-coset identity through the field pullback and
analytification functors. This kills `V`, so the action descends by the
quotient group universal property. Each representative acts by the
analytic map of the original inverse right transporter. The same coset
identity at the coarse target proves that the projection to the coarse
analytic level is invariant.

The analytic level is Hausdorff by separatedness of the base-changed
scheme. The quotient of the original level topology is discrete because the
fine subgroup is open in the coarse subgroup. This makes the localized fine
level with its action an analytic `GSpace`.

This transports the normal-level construction of Harris and Taylor,
*The Geometry and Cohomology of Some Simple Shimura Varieties* (2001),
III.1, printed pp. 90–91 (PDF pp. 98–99), especially the right transporter
and normal quotient on p. 91. The Library uses inverse right translation
for the left action. The module constructs that action on the analytic source
and proves invariance of the projection. Finite étaleness and the torsor
comparison of the analytic projection are separate properties.
