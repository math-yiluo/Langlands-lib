# Scalar-compatible local evaluation for original CM coefficient sheaves

Author: Worker 57.

This derived specialization proves that the existing scalar comparison for
original CM coefficient sheaves preserves their actual local evaluation
trivializations. It first constructs the original quotient-ring trivialization
after scalar restriction. It then proves the forward evaluation square, equality
of the complete isomorphisms, and the square for the inverse global comparison.
All maps use the same original coefficient cover.

## Inputs and geometric premises

The field, algebra, scheme, group and module carriers have universe zero. The
unitary input is `D : CentralSimpleUnitaryData E F A n`: E is totally real,
F is CM and quadratic over E, with the displayed algebra structures and scalar
towers on A. The algebra A is finite over ℚ. An imaginary quadratic field L
embeds in F; `τ₀ : L →+* ℂ` and the Harris signature charts C are fixed.
The coefficient inputs are a number field K, a finite-dimensional K-module W,
an algebraic representation ρ of `D.rationalInvolution.baseChange K` on W,
a rational finite place p, a small CM level U, a natural scalar m and
`hm : m ≠ 0`. The exponent k can be any natural number, including zero.

Every premise of
[HarrisTaylorCMCoefficientScalars](HarrisTaylorCMCoefficientScalars.md)
is retained. The tensor premises are
`pullbackTensorComparisonIsIsoStatement`, `PullbackTensorCompositionStatement`,
`PullbackTensorUnitCoherenceStatement`,

`TensorMonoidalLeftUnitorComparisonStatement` and

`PolarizationBaseChangeStatement`. There are also

The exact representability argument is

It concerns the fixed original moduli presheaf on

## Rings, covers and evaluation maps

Write X for the original tower scheme at U and J for its actual
`Scheme.smallEtaleTopology X`. Put

```text
R   = HarrisCoefficientIntegers K p,
I   = harrisCoefficientScalarIdeal K p m,
S_k = R / I^k,
φ_k = Ideal.Quotient.mk (I^k).
```

R is the full product of integral coefficient rings over all places of K above
p; I is the ideal generated by the image of m. The action N_k is the original
S_k-module action `cmHarrisCoefficientAction ... k`. The R-module action M_k
is the component at `op k` of `cmHarrisCoefficientDiagram`. They retain the
same full lattice-reduction carrier. The cover Y_k is exactly
`cmHarrisCoefficientTorsor ... k`, whose fine level is
`cmHarrisCoefficientLevel D ρ p U m hm k`. Its quotient group is the original
`SmallCMResidualGroup` for that level. The inverse-right deck action and the
coefficient convention `g ↦ unop(g)⁻¹` remain those of the imported modules.

Let B_k be the original quotient-ring sheaf
`cmHarrisCoefficientSheaf ... k`, and let A_k be the integral-system component
`(cmHarrisCoefficientSystem ...).obj (op k)`. Write

```text
F_k = sheafCompose J (ModuleCat.restrictScalars φ_k),
L_k = J.overPullback (ModuleCat R) Y_k.V,
H_k = sheafCompose (J.over Y_k.V) (ModuleCat.restrictScalars φ_k).
```

Here F_k restricts scalars globally, L_k localizes to the slice over the
original coefficient cover, and H_k restricts scalars on that slice. Applying
H_k after localization over S_k gives definitionally the same section functor
as applying F_k before L_k. This is the restriction/localization identification
used in the definition; no arbitrary-scheme inverse-image theorem is invoked.

Write `ConstS_k` for the constant S_k-module sheaf on N_k.V and `ConstR_k`
for the constant R-module sheaf on M_k.V. The existing isomorphisms are

```text
e_k  : F_k.obj B_k ≅ A_k,
tq_k : (J.overPullback (ModuleCat S_k) Y_k.V).obj B_k
         ≅ (J.overPullback (ModuleCat S_k) Y_k.V).obj ConstS_k,
tR_k : L_k.obj A_k ≅ L_k.obj ConstR_k.
```

They are respectively `cmHarrisCoefficientSheafRestrictIso`,
`cmHarrisCoefficientTrivialization` and
`cmHarrisCoefficientSystemTrivialization` at `op k` for the latter.
Both local trivializations use the original invariant inclusion followed by
`productEvaluation`. On an object `a : T ⟶ Y_k.V` of the slice, this evaluation
restricts cover sections along the tautological graph
`⟨a, 𝟙 T⟩ : T ⟶ Y_k.V × T`. The imported geometric graph splitting proves
that these evaluation maps are isomorphisms.

Let c_k be `constantRestrictScalarsIso J φ_k` at N_k.V. Its target is
ConstR_k, using the identical underlying restricted coefficient module.
It compares the actual sheafified constant modules and is not declared to be
a definitional identity.

## Constructed trivialization and compatibility laws

`cmHarrisRestrictedCoefficientTrivialization k` constructs

```text
t̂_k : L_k.obj (F_k.obj B_k) ≅ L_k.obj ConstR_k
     = H_k.mapIso tq_k ≪≫ L_k.mapIso c_k.
```

The second line specifies the isomorphism by composition. Its `_hom` theorem
is the definitional formula

```text
t̂_k.hom = H_k.map tq_k.hom ≫ L_k.map c_k.hom.
```

Thus the construction restricts the original quotient-ring evaluation and
then applies the canonical constant-coefficient comparison. It does not
define t̂_k by transporting the integral trivialization along e_k.

`cmHarrisCoefficientSheafRestrictIso_hom_trivialization` proves the actual
forward evaluation square:

```text
L_k.map e_k.hom ≫ tR_k.hom = t̂_k.hom.
```

`cmHarrisCoefficientSheafRestrictIso_trivialization` strengthens this to
equality of the complete isomorphisms:

```text
L_k.mapIso e_k ≪≫ tR_k = t̂_k.
```

Consequently their inverse maps agree as well. Finally,
`cmHarrisCoefficientSheafRestrictIso_inv_trivialization` proves the equation
for the inverse global comparison:

```text
L_k.map e_k.inv ≫ t̂_k.hom = tR_k.hom.
```

The forward and inverse square lemmas carry `@[reassoc]`. These are equalities
of morphisms of R-module sheaves on the slice, so their component maps agree
on every object of that slice.

## Implemented proof and provenance

Let `incq_k` and `incR_k` be the original quotient-ring and integral invariant
inclusions. The imported scalar module has already proved

```text
e_k.hom ≫ incR_k
  = F_k.map incq_k ≫ (productSheaf J Y_k.V).map c_k.hom.
```

The proof applies L_k to this exact equation and expands functorial composition.
It then uses the published `productEvaluation_natural` at c_k.hom:

```text
L_k.map ((productSheaf J Y_k.V).map c_k.hom) ≫ evaluation_R
  = evaluation_restricted ≫ L_k.map c_k.hom.
```

Associativity identifies the resulting composite with the defining forward
map of t̂_k. Thus compatibility follows from the actual invariant inclusions
and evaluation naturality, rather than from an assumed comparison law.
`Iso.ext` gives equality of the complete isomorphisms. The inverse square
follows by composing the forward square with L_k.map e_k.inv and using the
inverse/hom identity. The module imports and specializes existing generic
results instead of introducing new scalar-restriction or site machinery.
