# Continuous Galois actions on inverse limits of fixed-level cohomology of the actual CM tower

This internal construction composes the fixed-level Galois actions of
`Langlands.Shimura.PEL.HarrisTaylorCMLevelGaloisCohomology` with Worker 61's
inverse limit topology on limits of discrete representations
(`Langlands.Cohomology.ContinuousLimit`). Take an inverse system `m ↦ M_m` of
sheaves of `R`-modules on `Spec F`, for instance the constant sheaves
`N / ℓ^m N`. The fixed-level cohomology modules
`H^d(X_U ×_F Spec F̄, p_U^* M_m)`, with their Galois actions, form an inverse
system. Its inverse limit carries the inverse limit topology of the discrete
levels and is Hausdorff. When every level is a smooth Galois representation,
the action of `Gal(F̄/F)` on the limit is jointly continuous for the Krull
topology. The same holds at the levels `K'` of the prime-to-`m` tower. All
declarations are in namespace `Langlands.Shimura.PEL.HarrisTaylorCMWeilLevel`.

## Motivation and scope

Harris (1999), *Galois properties of cohomological automorphic forms on
GL(n)*, §5, considers `λ`-adic representations of `Gal(F̄/F)` (printed
p. 313, before Theorem 5.7), and Theorem 5.7 on printed p. 313 concerns those
on the cohomology `H^{n-1}(S_K(N), W)` of the Shimura variety at the level
`K(N)`. Such representations are inverse limits of cohomology with torsion
coefficients, each with a continuous action of the Galois group. This module
provides these inverse limits for the fixed-level cohomology of the actual
CM tower, with coefficients pulled back from `Spec F`. It is the CM
analogue, at a fixed level, of the published `EtaleGaloisAdic` construction
for finite Galois towers.

The module does not identify the inverse limit with `ℓ`-adic cohomology in
any other sense, and it constructs neither Harris's coefficient systems `W`
nor `λ`-adic Galois representations over a field of characteristic zero. It
proves no finiteness of the limit. Smoothness of every level is a hypothesis
of the continuity results; it is not proved. For the constant sheaves
`N / ℓ^m N` the integer `ℓ` is arbitrary; no primality is assumed. No
statement of Harris is formalized.

## The shrinking-level tower at a fixed level

Let `U : SmallCMLevel D`, `d : ℕ` and `M : ℕᵒᵖ ⥤ ModuleSheaf base R`.

- `cmLevelAdicSystem ... R U d` sends `M` to the inverse system
  `k ↦ (cmLevelGaloisCohomologyFunctor ... R U d).obj (M.obj k)`
  (`cmLevelAdicSystem_obj_obj`). It is postcomposition with the fixed-level
  functor.
- `CMLevelAdicSpace ... R U d M` is `ContinuousLimit.ConeSpace` of the limit
  cone of this system: the inverse limit of the levels, with the coarsest
  topology making every projection to a discrete level continuous. An
  instance records that it is Hausdorff (`T2Space`).
- `continuous_cmLevelAdicAction ... M hM`: if every level is smooth, that is,
  `((Rep.ActionToRep R Gamma).obj (((cmLevelAdicSystem ... R U d).obj M).obj k)).ρ`
  is smooth for every `k`, the action `(σ, x) ↦ σ · x` of `Gamma` on
  `CMLevelAdicSpace ... R U d M` is jointly continuous.
- `cmLevelAdicContRepresentation`: the action as a representation by
  continuous linear maps.
- `cmLevelAdicContAction ... M hM`: for a topological ring `R` acting
  continuously on the limit, and smooth levels, the limit as an object of
  `ContAction (TopModuleCat R) Gamma`.
- `cmLevelAdicTopRepFunctor ... R U d`: for a discrete ring `R`, the
  composite of `cmLevelAdicSystem` with `limitTopRepFunctor`, a functor from
  inverse systems of sheaves on `Spec F` to `TopRep R Gamma`.
- `CMLevelEllAdicSpace ... R U d ell N`: `CMLevelAdicSpace` for the system
  `EtaleAdicSystem.ellPowerConstantSystem (Spec F) ell N` of constant sheaves
  of `N / ell^m N`.

## The prime-to-`m` tower at a fixed level

For `m : ℕ`, `hm : m ≠ 0`, a level `K₀` of
`(D.rationalInvolution.DividingPoints m)ᵐᵒᵖ` and
`K' : (cmPrimeToProperty D m hm K₀).FullSubcategory`, the declarations
`cmPrimeToLevelAdicSystem` (with `_obj_obj`), `CMPrimeToLevelAdicSpace` (with
its `T2Space` instance), `continuous_cmPrimeToLevelAdicAction`,
`cmPrimeToLevelAdicContRepresentation`, `cmPrimeToLevelAdicContAction`,
`cmPrimeToLevelAdicTopRepFunctor` and `CMPrimeToLevelEllAdicSpace` are the
same constructions for `cmPrimeToLevelGaloisCohomologyFunctor ... R m hm K₀ K' d`.

## Proof outline

Every declaration applies the corresponding declaration of
`ContinuousLimit` to the limit cone of the inverse system, which exists
because `Action (ModuleCat R) Gamma` has limits. The Hausdorff property uses
Mathlib's instances that the forgetful functors preserve limits.

## Provenance

This is an internal composition of the fixed-level Galois actions of
`HarrisTaylorCMLevelGaloisCohomology` with the published `ContinuousLimit`
construction; it adds no new proof technique. The continuity sources are
those recorded in the published `ContinuousLimit` Markdown. Harris (1999),
§5 and Theorem 5.7 on printed p. 313, is motivation only.
