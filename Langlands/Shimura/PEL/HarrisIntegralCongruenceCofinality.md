# Cofinality of the actual Harris principal congruence subgroups

This internal result concerns the same integral adelic source span and
principal congruence subgroups as `HarrisIntegralSourceCongruence`. It uses
the existing scalar-extension similitude topology, not a new group topology.
The comparison hypothesis is the existing rational profinite-integer adele
comparison. No division hypothesis or representability hypothesis is used.

Every neighbourhood of zero in the rational finite adeles contains all
`m`-multiples of integral adeles for some positive integer `m`. Indeed its
preimage in the profinite residue limit contains a neighbourhood given by
finitely many residue-coordinate constraints. A positive common multiple
of those moduli forces every one of those coordinates to be zero. This
statement passes to every neighbourhood of zero in the actual scalar-
extension source space: use the continuous finite basis expansion and
integral coordinates of the source span from `HarrisIntegralSourceResidues`.

`harrisIntegralAdeleCongruence_le_of_dvd` proves that `K(k) ≤ K(m)` when
`m | k`: the ambient differences for `K(k)` are multiples of `k` in the
source lattice, hence multiples of `m`. In particular the subgroups are
directed under reverse inclusion, by taking products of moduli. The filter
`harrisIntegralAdeleCongruenceFilter` is generated by those subgroups at
positive moduli.

`harrisIntegralAdeleCongruenceFilter_eq_nhds` identifies this filter with
the neighbourhood filter of the identity. The open congruence subgroups
are themselves identity neighbourhoods. Conversely, along the congruence
filter, the action on an integral vector converges to that vector by the
small-multiple result. The finite basis extends this to the forward
operator on every source vector. Inversion preserves the filter, so the
inverse operators also converge pointwise. The form attains one on a pair
of vectors, and its continuous evaluation on the transformed pair recovers
the actual multiplier. The same argument after inversion gives the inverse
multiplier. This proves convergence in the topology on adelic units, which
uses both the value and the inverse. These are all the coordinates of the
published induced similitude topology, proving convergence to the identity
there.

Consequently `exists_harrisIntegralAdeleCongruence_le` finds a positive
principal congruence subgroup inside any open subgroup of the actual
Harris Hodge similitude group. It does not make that subgroup normal in an
arbitrary containing subgroup of the ambient group. Normality remains the
relative normality inside the integral stabilizer proved in
`HarrisIntegralSourceCongruence`. No moduli existence or quotient theorem
is asserted here.
