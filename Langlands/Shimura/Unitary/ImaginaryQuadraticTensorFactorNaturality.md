# Semilinear maps of actual imaginary quadratic tensor factors

This internal extension constructs the actual semilinear map between coordinate
scalar-extension tensor modules from a coefficient-algebra map and a semilinear
intertwiner of the original field actions. Its source and target use the actual
induced tensor-algebra module structures and the chosen natural or conjugate
coordinate algebra structures.

The construction composes the proved tensor-factor comparison, the restriction
of the intertwiner to the full eigensubmodule, and the inverse target comparison.
The comparison square follows from the inverse law. Projector naturality and the
pure-tensor comparison formula prove that the resulting map sends `r ⊗ m` to
`f(r) ⊗ F(m)`. Thus this is the actual canonical tensor map, characterized on
tensor generators; an arbitrary map or tensor comparison is not an input.

No finiteness, flatness, reducedness or nontriviality hypothesis is used. This
module concerns actual modules over coefficient rings, without claiming sheaf
or scheme pullback comparisons.
