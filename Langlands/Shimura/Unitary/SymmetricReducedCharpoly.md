# The reduced characteristic polynomial of a symmetric element

Let \(K/E\) be a totally complex quadratic extension of a totally real number field. Let \(A\) be a degree-\(n\) central simple \(K\)-algebra with \(n>1\) and an involution inducing CM conjugation on \(K\). For an element fixed by the involution, `symmetricReducedCharpoly` constructs a monic polynomial of degree \(n\) over \(E\) whose coefficient extension to \(K\) is the reduced characteristic polynomial. Injectivity of the coefficient map gives uniqueness. The field universes are independent, and the input algebra is in the same universe as \(K\).

The proof uses a real embedding of \(E\) and its Hermitian matrix chart. A surjective matrix chart on a scalar extension that sends each original central scalar to its specified image extends to a matrix splitting chart over the coefficient field. The new map is surjective because the pure tensors of the original chart map into its image. Comparing dimensions makes it an equivalence. The ordinary matrix characteristic polynomial in the real chart therefore computes the coefficients of the reduced polynomial in \(\mathbb C\).

A fixed element maps to a matrix conjugate to its conjugate transpose by the Gram matrix. The characteristic polynomial is invariant under matrix conjugation and transpose. Its coefficients are consequently fixed by complex conjugation. Injectivity of the complex embedding and comparison with CM conjugation show that the original coefficients lie in the image of \(E\). The polynomial descent uses this fact for every coefficient.
