# An irreducible symmetric polynomial condition at a split finite place

Let \(A\) be a degree-\(n\) central simple algebra over a CM quadratic extension \(K/E\), with \(n>1\) and an involution inducing CM conjugation. At a split finite place of \(E\) with zero completed Brauer invariant at the chosen upper place, `splitIrreducibleCoordinateCondition` gives an open set in the completed coordinates of a supplied \(E\)-basis of \(A\). The set contains a local fixed element. Every global symmetric element meeting the condition has irreducible reduced characteristic polynomial over \(K\). The field universes are independent.

The split center and maximal-Witt chart give two actual matrix factors over the base completion. The first component of a fixed element can be any matrix. Take a preimage with zero second component and add its image under the involution, which has zero first component because the involution exchanges the central idempotents. For the first matrix choose multiplication by a primitive element of an unramified extension of degree \(n\). Its characteristic polynomial is its minimal polynomial, since it is monic, annihilates the element and has the minimal degree. A coefficient neighborhood preserves irreducibility.

The first matrix projection sends each original central scalar to its image in the split coefficient field. `reducedCharpoly_eq_of_centerCompatibleChart` computes the reduced characteristic polynomial in any such surjective matrix chart after coefficient extension. Thus membership in the open condition gives irreducibility after a field embedding, and hence over \(K\).
