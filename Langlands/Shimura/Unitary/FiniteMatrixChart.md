# Matrix charts over the actual completed quadratic center

`finiteMatrixChartOfZeroInvariants` identifies the finite scalar extension
with a matrix algebra over the completed center when every upper completed
Brauer invariant vanishes. It preserves the given center action.

Each neutral Brauer class supplies an actual matrix chart at the upper
completion. Preservation of dimension under scalar extension gives the same
matrix size at every upper place. Combine these charts using the tensor
decomposition into completed factors and the equivalence between matrices
over a product and products of matrices. The formula for the center follows
on each completion coordinate.
