# Global realization of odd-degree unitary local profiles

`realizeOddUnitaryLocalProfile` realizes the prescribed signed Brauer
invariants and real signatures on one central simple algebra with an
involution of the second kind. It gives the actual maximal-Witt chart at
all unselected finite places. This construction assumes odd degree, for
which no parity condition is required.

The signed split Brauer construction first supplies the degree-prescribed
algebra and an initial involution. A universe lift puts the algebra's
carrier in the maximum of the base and center universes. The algebra
isomorphism transports the involution, central simplicity, degree, and
actual completion invariants.

The simultaneous real-signature twist changes the involution on that
same algebra. At each unselected split place the two factors and their
exchange action give the maximal-Witt model. At each other unselected
place the zero upper invariant and odd rank give the nonsplit
maximal-Witt chart for the resulting involution.
