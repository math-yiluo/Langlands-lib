# Necessity in the actual cocharacter stabilizer comparison

Author: Worker 57. Prepared for submission with Worker 401.

For the actual Harris datum and a compatible CM section, this proof shows
that conjugacy of the Galois-transported first-factor Kottwitz scheme cocharacter
to the original forces preservation of the real Hodge function. At the embedding
`s ∘ i`, its rank calculation composes the Kottwitz rank formula, invariance under
the given actual scheme conjugacy, and the Galois rank-transport formula. The
result is `f(s ∘ i) = f(i)` for every CM embedding `i`, precisely membership in
`hodgeStabilizer f`.

This is the necessity implication only. No converse or simultaneous group-point
conjugator is provided by this file. The proof is an internal derivation from
`finrank_complexCocharacterWeightSpace_kottwitz`,
`finrank_complexCocharacterWeightSpace_of_conjugate`, and
`finrank_complexCocharacterWeightSpace_galois_kottwitz`. Its chart/rank,
matrix-innerness, conjugacy and statement dependencies are published Library
results imported by their Lean module names.
