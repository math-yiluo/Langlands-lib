# Correcting a finite set of signed determinant defects

Let \(A\) be a central simple algebra of even degree \(n>1\) with a unitary involution over a totally complex quadratic extension \(K/E\) of a totally real number field. The universe of \(E\) is independent of the common universe of \(K\) and \(A\). Assume the completed Brauer invariants of \(A\) vanish above every finite place outside a supplied finite set \(s\), and the involution already has the supplied real signature charts. Suppose \(S\) is a disjoint finite set of nonsplit finite places, of even cardinality, where the signed determinant of the Hermitian chart fails to be a local norm. The hypothesis specifies that the signed determinant is a norm at every other nonsplit place outside \(s\).

`evenNormCorrection` supplies one symmetric unit of \(A\) whose twist retains all the prescribed signature charts and gives actual maximal-Witt charts at every place outside \(s\).

Symmetric weak approximation gives an element with irreducible reduced polynomial over \(K\) and with descended polynomial split at \(S\) and at the real embeddings. The lower root field \(F\) is totally real, has degree-one completions over \(S\), and evaluates into the symmetric elements of \(A\). Its compositum with \(K\) is the upper root field and is totally complex quadratic over \(F\). The quadratic norm profile gives a totally positive unit of \(F\) whose norm to \(E\) fails to be a local norm precisely at \(S\) among the nonsplit finite places. This norm is the reduced norm of its image in \(A\), after inclusion of \(E\) into the center.

The positive unit preserves the real charts by conjugation through local symmetric square roots. At each nonsplit place outside \(s\), its reduced-norm factor matches the norm membership status of the signed determinant, so the twisted chart is maximal Witt. At a split place outside \(s\), vanishing of the completed Brauer invariants gives the maximal-Witt chart for the twisted involution directly.
