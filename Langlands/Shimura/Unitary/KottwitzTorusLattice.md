# The torus at a good prime: cocharacter points times integral points

Author: Worker 8. Module `Langlands.Shimura.Unitary.KottwitzTorusLattice`, namespace
`Langlands.Shimura.KottwitzUnramified`. This is a supplement to item 2 of Task 5424
(`KottwitzPrincipalSeries`). It proves one of the well-definedness properties that item 2
lists as an obligation.

At a good prime, every point of the diagonal torus `T(ℚ_p)` of a standard unramified chart
is the point `λ(p)` of a `ℚ_p`-rational cocharacter `λ` times a point of `T ∩ K_p`.
Consequently an unramified character of `T(ℚ_p)` is determined by its values at the
points `λ(p)`. Equivalently, its restriction to the lattice of rational cocharacters,
`latticeCharacter`, is injective on unramified characters. The principal-series
parameters of item 2, the Weyl equivalence and the base-change parameters of item 4 are
all read through these values. They therefore depend only on the unramified character
itself, and the identification
`Hom_unr(T(ℚ_p), Ωˣ) ↪ Hom(X_*(T)^{Frob}, Ωˣ)` is injective.

## Source

The statement is an instance of the standard description `T(ℚ_p)/T(ℤ_p) ≅ X_*(T)^{Gal}`
for an unramified torus. Cartier, *Representations of p-adic groups: a survey*,
Corvallis I (1979), source `cartier-1979-representations-padic-groups`, SHA-256
`3b9fe0145da81d870ef8967be83635cd440a2581fe043e64e63324e22dec4db5`, gives the exact
sequence `1 → °M → M → Λ → 1` of diagram (D) in §3.2 (printed p.135). Here `ord_M`
maps the minimal Levi subgroup `M` onto a lattice `Λ`, `°M` is its largest compact
subgroup, and the unramified characters of `M` are the characters of `Λ`. In §3.5(b)
(printed p.140) Cartier identifies `°M = K ∩ M`. For the diagonal torus of a standard
chart, `exists_cocharHom_mul_level` makes `Λ` explicit: the points `λ(p)` of rational
cocharacters represent all classes of `M/(K ∩ M)`. The proof here is internal and
derived. It uses the antidiagonal normal form of the chart and the valuation of
unramified completions; it is not transcribed from Cartier.

## Declarations

### Valuations of unramified completions

- `asIdeal_eq_span_residuePrime`: the prime ideal of a finite place `p` of `ℚ` is
  generated by the rational prime `residuePrime p` (from Mathlib's `span_natGenerator`).
- `valued_residuePrime`: `p` has valuation `exp(-1)` in `ℚ_p`, by Mathlib's
  `valuedAdicCompletion_eq_valuation`, `valuation_of_algebraMap` and
  `intValuation_singleton`.
- `valued_residuePrime_place`: at a place `w ∣ p` of `K` with ramification index one,
  `p` has valuation `exp(-1)` in `K_w`. This follows from the published
  `FiniteExtension.valuation_completionMap` (`v(ι x) = v(x)^{e(w|p)}`).
- `valued_primeUnit_zpow`: `v(p^z) = exp(-z)` in `K_w` for unramified `w`.
- `exists_valued_eq`: a nonzero `y ∈ K_w`, for unramified `w`, has `v(y) = exp(-z)` with
  `y · p^{-z}` of valuation one, where `z = -log v(y)`.
- `valued_map_of_integral`: a ring homomorphism `f : K_{w'} → K_w` between unramified
  completions that carries integers to integers preserves the valuation. Write
  `y = u p^z` with `v(u) = 1`. Then `f(u)` and `f(u)⁻¹ = f(u⁻¹)` are integral, so
  `v(f u) = 1`, and `f` fixes powers of `p`. This applies to the conjugation maps
  `c_w : K_{w̄} → K_w` of a standard chart.

### The decomposition

- `diag_unit_mem_units`: a diagonal unit of `Π_w M_n(K_w)` whose diagonal entries have
  valuation one lies in the units of the integral matrices `Π_w M_n(O_w)`. The inverse is
  diagonal, with inverse entries.
- `UnramifiedChart.exists_cocharHom_mul_level`: assume `IsUnramifiedAt p K`, the
  good-prime hypothesis of item 1. Then every point `t` of the torus of a standard chart
  is `t = λ(p) · u`, with `λ` a rational cocharacter and `u` a point of the torus in the
  level `K_p`. The proof sets `λ w k = -log v(t_{w,k})` for the diagonal entries
  `t_{w,k}` of the matrix coordinates of `t`, and `λ_c = -log v(c)` for the multiplier
  `c`. The diagonal entries of the similitude equation `Ψ(t) θ(Ψ t) = c` read
  `t_{w,k} · c_w(t_{w̄, rev k}) = c`. With `valued_map_of_integral` and the
  ramification-index-one valuation of `c` in `K_w`, this gives
  `λ w k + λ w̄ (rev k) = λ_c`, so `λ` is a rational cocharacter. Finally `λ(p)⁻¹ t` is
  diagonal with diagonal entries of valuation one, and lies in the level by
  `diag_unit_mem_units`.
- `UnramifiedChart.latticeCharacter ξ` is `ξ ∘ cocharHom`, the values of a torus
  character at the points `λ(p)`, as a character of the cocharacter lattice.
- `UnramifiedChart.unramifiedCharacter_ext` and `latticeCharacter_injective`: at a good
  prime, two unramified characters (trivial on `T ∩ K_p`) with the same values at all
  `λ(p)` are equal.

## Scope

The surjectivity half (every character of the lattice comes from an unramified character
of `T(ℚ_p)`) is not proved here. Item 2's parameters never need it. The module adds no
hypothesis to item 2's definitions; it proves a property of them.
