# A split CM place outside a finite set

For a totally real number field \(E\), a totally complex quadratic extension \(K/E\), and a finite set of finite places of \(E\), `splitPlaceOutside` chooses a place outside the set with an actual split center. The two field universes are independent.

Chebotarev realization supplies an unramified place with identity arithmetic Frobenius. In the residue field at the chosen upper place, the Frobenius power acts as the identity. Its order is the residue degree, so that degree is one. Unramifiedness gives ramification index one, and hence the upper completion has degree one. A nonsplit CM place would have a unique upper place of degree two, so the chosen place is split.
