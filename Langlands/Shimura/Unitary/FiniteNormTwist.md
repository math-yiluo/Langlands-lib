# A maximal-Witt chart after a symmetric norm correction

Let \(A\) be a central simple algebra of even degree \(n>1\) over a totally complex quadratic extension \(K/E\) of a totally real number field. The universe of \(E\) is independent of the common universe of \(K\) and \(A\). At a nonsplit finite place with zero upper completed Brauer invariants, `maximalWittChartOfNormTwist` applies to a symmetric unit \(t\) of \(A\) whose reduced norm equals the image of a supplied unit \(d\) of \(E\). It assumes that the image of \(d\) in the base completion and the initial signed Gram determinant have the same membership status in the actual quadratic norm subgroup.

In the initial local matrix chart the twisted involution has Gram matrix \(H T\), where \(T\) is the image of \(t\). The determinant of \(T\) is the localization of the reduced norm. The Hermitian chart constructed for the twisted involution uses the same algebra coordinates. Its Gram matrix is a scalar multiple of \(H T\) because the ratio of Gram matrices for the same adjoint commutes with all invertible matrices. The scalar is fixed by conjugation since both matrices are Hermitian. Its contribution to the determinant in degree \(n\) is the norm of its \(n/2\)-th power.

The product of the initial signed determinant and the localized \(d\) is a norm. The scalar correction then provides a norm witness for the signed determinant of the twisted Gram matrix. The even local Hermitian criterion gives an equivalence respecting the completed center and carrying the twisted involution to the standard maximal-Witt adjoint.
