# Affine cocharacter homomorphisms and rational-coordinate comparison

This is an internal construction from the published multiplicative-group point equivalence, the relative affine Harris cocharacter evaluation, and the canonical comparisons between iterated and direct scalar pullbacks. It constructs a homomorphism of point groups and establishes the compatibility of the existing evaluation models; it assumes neither of those compatibilities as input.

For the actual central-simple unitary datum `D`, a commutative complex algebra `T` in `Type`, and a cocharacter `χ` of `D.complexRationalGroupScheme`, `D.complexAffineCocharacterValueHom T χ` is a group homomorphism from `Tˣ` to the group of points on the relative affine test `Spec T → Spec ℂ`. It is the composite of the inverse affine unit-point equivalence and postcomposition by `χ` in the group-valued functor of points. The inherited homomorphism laws therefore hold intrinsically. `complexAffineCocharacterValueHom_apply` identifies its underlying function with `complexAffineCocharacterValue`; no signature charts or real scalar structure are needed for this construction.

The comparison uses three explicit underlying-map lemmas. `Gm.relativeAffineUnitPoint_self_left` specializes the identity-test unit comparison from `AffineTestComparison` to the base algebra itself. `GroupScheme.affineCocharacterValue_comp_left` computes the projection of a composite cocharacter: evaluate the unit point, compose with both group homomorphisms, and then take the first pullback projection. `complexCocharacterValue_left` specializes this formula to the canonical direct comparison for the Harris group; `complexAffineCocharacterValue_left` expands relative evaluation before projection.

`D.complexAffineCocharacterValue_rational χ v` states, for every complex unit `v`, that the iterated rational projection of the relative affine value equals the existing identity-test value `D.complexCocharacterValue χ v`. The Lean proof applies extensionality for morphisms over the rational base, rewrites the two explicit underlying maps, substitutes the identity-test unit comparison, and applies `complexDirectGroupSchemeIso_hom_fst`. Thus the agreement includes the specific canonical direct-versus-iterated pullback comparison already used by the rational evaluation.

For Harris signature charts `C`, a CM section proof `hΦ`, a Boolean choice `first`, a complex unit `v`, and a factor label `p = (τ, b)`, `harrisCocharacter_embedding_matrices` computes the matrix at the embedding `cmFactorEmbedding Φ p` of the actual rational point obtained by evaluating `D.harrisCocharacter C first`.

Set `r = n - 1` at the distinguished real place and `r = n` elsewhere. The scalar pair is `(v, 1)` when `first = true`, and `(1, v)` when `first = false`. In `ComplexMatrix.splitDiagonal`, the first matrix has the second scalar on indices `i < r` and the first scalar on the remaining diagonal entries; the second matrix reverses these scalars. Both matrices are zero off the diagonal. The embedding label `b = true` selects the first matrix and `b = false` selects the second. These are the inherited corrected Harris weights; this module does not change the convention or identify it with an inverse convention.

The final proof rewrites the identity-test value by `complexAffineCocharacterValue_rational`, applies the embedding-labelled affine matrix comparison, and then applies `harrisCocharacter_affine_matrices`. The complex number field supplies the compatible real algebra structure used by that theorem. All CM field, central-simple algebra, involution and signature assumptions are those of the existing `CentralSimpleUnitaryData`, `HarrisSignatureCharts`, and `IsCMSection` constructions.

The module establishes point-group evaluation and its rational and embedding-coordinate compatibilities. It does not prove Galois conjugacy of cocharacters, characterize the reflex field, prove smoothness or reductivity, or construct scheme levels or coefficient descent. Those remain separate obligations of the larger task.
