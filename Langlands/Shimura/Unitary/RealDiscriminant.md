# The discriminant invariant of a prescribed real signature

For a degree-\(2m\) unitary pair over a CM extension, supply an
`EvenDiscriminantPresentation`, place embeddings, and a `HasSignature`
witness with \(r\) positive lines at the embedding of the real place.
`realLocalInvariant_discriminant` gives the rational half times the
parity of \(m+(2m-r)\). The isomorphism of the actual real completion
with \(\mathbb R\) takes the base embedding to the one in the signature
witness. A center-compatible signature chart then uses complex
conjugation on its coefficients.

The signature matrix has \(2m-r\) negative diagonal entries, so its
signed determinant is \((-1)^{m+2m-r}\). Norms from \(\mathbb C\) to
\(\mathbb R\) are the strictly positive units, by the norm-square
formula and the real square root. The Hermitian comparison gives
zero discriminant exactly for even exponent. The real invariant
of a nonzero class is \(1/2\), which gives the stated map from
\(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\). The original number fields may have
different universes.
