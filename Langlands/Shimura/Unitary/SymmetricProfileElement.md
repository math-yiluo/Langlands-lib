# A symmetric element with a prescribed set of split spectra

Let \(A\) be a degree-\(n\) central simple algebra over a CM quadratic extension \(K/E\), with \(n>1\) and an involution inducing CM conjugation. Assume the upper completed Brauer invariants vanish away from a finite set of base places. Given a disjoint finite set of nonsplit places, `symmetricProfileElement` gives a symmetric element with irreducible reduced characteristic polynomial over \(K\). Its descended polynomial splits over the completion of each specified nonsplit place and over the reals at each real embedding. The universes of \(E\) and \(K\) are independent, and \(A\) is in the universe of \(K\).

Choose an auxiliary split place outside the given Brauer support. It is distinct from every specified nonsplit place. Its open coordinate condition forces irreducibility. At the specified nonsplit places and every real infinite place, the open coordinate conditions force splitting over the base after completion. Each open condition contains a symmetric point, so symmetric weak approximation in one basis supplies the global element. Every real embedding is the distinguished embedding of its associated real infinite place, giving the conclusion at all such embeddings.
