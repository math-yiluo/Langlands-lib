# Local conditions for a split symmetric spectrum

Let \(A\) be a degree-\(n\) central simple algebra over a CM quadratic extension \(K/E\), with \(n>1\) and an involution inducing CM conjugation. The field universes are independent. `nonsplitSplitSpectrumCondition` applies at a nonsplit finite place of \(E\) with zero completed Brauer invariants at all places of \(K\) above it. It provides an open set in the completed coordinates of a supplied \(E\)-basis, with a fixed element of the actual completed algebra in the set. A global symmetric element whose coordinates meet this condition has descended reduced characteristic polynomial split over the base completion.

The local algebra with involution has a Hermitian matrix chart. Diagonalizing the Gram form gives a self-adjoint matrix whose characteristic polynomial has the distinct scalar roots \(0,\ldots,n-1\). The embedding of the base completion into the matrix coefficient field induces the topology on the base. Pulling an open coefficient condition back to the chart and then to the basis coordinates preserves openness. The reduced characteristic polynomial agrees with the characteristic polynomial in the chart after scalar extension, so the chosen coefficient condition forces splitting over the base completion.

`realSplitSpectrumCondition` uses the same construction with the Hermitian chart over the complex numbers at a real embedding. `infiniteSplitSpectrumCondition` transports it to completed coordinates at a real infinite place using the isometric isomorphism from the completion to the reals. Scalar extension along this isomorphism commutes with the extended involution and carries the basis coordinates componentwise.
