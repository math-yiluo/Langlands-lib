# Evaluating full Harris derived source changes

Author: Worker 13.

This internal construction evaluates actual Harris derived source changes on
each reachable coefficient level. It constructs comparisons with the ordinary
source-restricted and reindexed sheaves placed in the same cochain degree,
recovers their source-change maps and invariant-section evaluations, and proves
the evaluated derived source-change square. The coefficient diagram is the
original whole Harris diagram, including exponent zero and all coefficient
places.

The construction uses
[HarrisEtaleDerivedSourceChange](HarrisEtaleDerivedSourceChange.md) and the
categorical helper
[ExactDerivedEvaluation](../../Cohomology/ExactDerivedEvaluation.md).
The coefficient and geometric provenance is that of
[HarrisEtaleCoefficients](HarrisEtaleCoefficients.md) and
[HarrisEtaleDerivedLocalTrivialization](HarrisEtaleDerivedLocalTrivialization.md).
The new assertions follow internally from those constructions and Mathlib's
canonical exact-derived comparisons; no new direct literature assertion is made.

## Inputs and actual derived categories

Use the same finite rational algebra `A`, involution `σ`, number field `L`,
finite-dimensional `L`-module `W`, algebraic representation `ρ`, rational finite
place `p`, compact open level `U`, and scalar `m : ℕ` as in the source-change
module. All carriers share universe `u`. Write

```text
R = HarrisCoefficientIntegers L p
D = harrisCongruenceGroups ρ p U m
M = harrisCoefficientDiagram ρ p U m
J = X.smallEtaleTopology.
```

Here `R` is the product of completed integer rings at all places of `L` above
`p`. The coefficient objects are the full stable lattice modulo powers of the
ideal generated by `m`, with their original reductions and actions. There is no
`m ≠ 0` hypothesis in this module; imported finiteness results keep theirs.
The scheme `X : Scheme.{u}`, compatible actual étale diagram
`Y : EquivariantDiagram D X.Etale`, and geometric hypotheses
`hY : ∀ n, IsFiniteGeometricTorsor X (Y.action n)` are supplied inputs.
They assume no sheaf trivialization or derived compatibility.

For `f : k ⟶ l` in `ℕᵒᵖ`, set

```text
C   = EquivariantDiagram D (ModuleCat.{u} R)
K   = Under k ⥤ Sheaf (J.over (Y.obj.obj k)) (ModuleCat.{u} R)
L_l = Under l ⥤ Sheaf (J.over (Y.obj.obj l)) (ModuleCat.{u} R)
B   = Under l ⥤ Sheaf (J.over (Y.obj.obj k)) (ModuleCat.{u} R)
T   = Sheaf (J.over (Y.obj.obj k)) (ModuleCat.{u} R).
```

There are independently chosen `HasDerivedCategory` instances for `C`, `K`,
`L_l`, `B`, and `T`, with localization universes `wC`, `wK`, `wL`, `wM`,
and `wT`. They are actual localizations of integer cochain complexes at
quasi-isomorphisms. Not every declaration requires all five: the mixed evaluation
functor needs only `B` and `T`; restriction recovery omits `K`, and reindexing
recovery omits `L_l`. The source-local sheaf and diagram categories are abelian
by the existing module-sheaf constructions. The actual restriction, reindexing,
and evaluation functors are additive and preserve finite limits and colimits.

For `i : Under l`, `harrisEtaleDerivedMixedEvaluation` is the actual exact
derived functor of evaluation at `i`, from `DerivedCategory B` to
`DerivedCategory T`. Its domain consists of whole diagrams before localization.
The indices start at `l`, while the sheaves live over `Y.obj.obj k`.
It has proved `CommShift ℤ` and `IsTriangulated` instances. This construction
does not replace the derived category of diagrams by diagrams in a derived
category.

## Endpoint and morphism comparisons

Write `H_j` and `N_j` for the ordinary local coefficient and constant diagrams,
`H_jᴰ` and `N_jᴰ` for their existing derived objects constructed from
`single_C(q).obj M`, and `e_jᴰ : H_jᴰ ≅ N_jᴰ` for the derived invariant
evaluation. The integer `q : ℤ` is a cochain degree, independent of every
coefficient exponent. Let `T_f` be `sourceSliceRestriction` and `P_f` be
`sourceSliceReindex`, with actual derived functors `T_fᴰ` and `P_fᴰ`.
Write `E_iᴰ` for mixed derived evaluation.

The four definitions

- `harrisEtaleDerivedRestrictedCoefficientEvaluationIso`,
- `harrisEtaleDerivedRestrictedConstantEvaluationIso`,
- `harrisEtaleDerivedReindexedCoefficientEvaluationIso`, and
- `harrisEtaleDerivedReindexedConstantEvaluationIso`

compare respectively

```text
E_iᴰ(T_fᴰ(H_lᴰ)) ≅ single_T(q)((T_f H_l).obj i)
E_iᴰ(T_fᴰ(N_lᴰ)) ≅ single_T(q)((T_f N_l).obj i)
E_iᴰ(P_fᴰ(H_kᴰ)) ≅ single_T(q)(H_k.obj ((Under.map f).obj i))
E_iᴰ(P_fᴰ(N_kᴰ)) ≅ single_T(q)(N_k.obj ((Under.map f).obj i)).
```

Each applies `exactDerivedEvaluationSingleIso` to the already constructed
restricted or reindexed single-degree comparison. It maps that comparison
through actual derived evaluation, then composes with the canonical evaluation
single-degree comparison.

The definitions `harrisEtaleDerivedCoefficientSourceChangeEvaluationIso` and
`harrisEtaleDerivedConstantSourceChangeEvaluationIso` apply `E_iᴰ.mapIso`
to the actual derived associated and constant source changes `a_fᴰ` and
`b_fᴰ`. The definitions
`harrisEtaleDerivedRestrictedTrivializationEvaluationIso` and
`harrisEtaleDerivedReindexedTrivializationEvaluationIso` are respectively
`E_iᴰ.mapIso(T_fᴰ.mapIso(e_lᴰ))` and
`E_iᴰ.mapIso(P_fᴰ.mapIso(e_kᴰ))`. Thus all four sides of the evaluated
source-change square are constructed actual derived isomorphisms.

## Recovery and compatibility proofs

For each of the four morphism isomorphisms, conjugating its forward map by the
corresponding endpoint comparisons recovers the ordinary sheaf map placed in
degree `q`. In the following display, `s` and `t` mean those source and target
comparisons, and `g` means the evaluated derived forward map:

```text
s.inv ≫ g ≫ t.hom = single_T(q).map(h).
```

The two theorems ending in `SourceChangeEvaluationIso_single` take `h` to
be the `i` component of the original ordinary coefficient or constant
source-change isomorphism. Their proofs first cancel the canonical endpoint
comparisons in the generic `exactDerivedSquareIso_hom_app_single` formula,
using the private categorical lemma `squareSingleRecovery`. They then apply
`exactDerivedEvaluation_single` to ordinary evaluation at `i`. The resulting
ordinary source-change maps have identity forward maps on common sections,
as proved in the imported source-change module.

The theorem
`harrisEtaleDerivedRestrictedTrivializationEvaluationIso_single` takes `h`
to be

```text
pullback(Y.obj.map f).map
  (etaleAssociatedEvaluationAlong X Y M i.right (Y.obj.map i.hom)).
```

The theorem
`harrisEtaleDerivedReindexedTrivializationEvaluationIso_single` takes `h`
to be

```text
etaleAssociatedEvaluationAlong X Y M i.right (Y.obj.map (f ≫ i.hom)).
```

Each proof applies `exactDerivedEvaluation_single` twice: first to the actual
restriction or reindexing functor and the proved local Harris single-degree
recovery equation, then to evaluation at `i`. Ordinary restriction and
reindexing identify the right sides with the displayed original and composite
cover evaluations by their definitions. No recovery equation is assumed as an
extra hypothesis of these Harris theorems. A finite `maxHeartbeats 1600000`
is scoped to each of the four specialized recovery theorems to permit kernel
checking their full coefficient specialization.

Finally `harrisEtaleDerivedTrivializationEvaluationIso_sourceChange` proves

```text
E_iᴰ(T_fᴰ(e_lᴰ.hom)) ≫ E_iᴰ(b_fᴰ.hom)
  = E_iᴰ(a_fᴰ.hom) ≫ E_iᴰ(P_fᴰ(e_kᴰ.hom)).
```

Its proof maps the existing actual derived Harris source-change square through
`E_iᴰ` and uses preservation of composition. This is an equality in the actual
derived category of sheaves over `Y.obj.obj k`. The four recovery theorems
identify its sides with the ordinary source-change and invariant-evaluation
maps, including the genuine composite cover map.

## Scope

These are local sheaf-diagram constructions for supplied genuine scheme
torsors. Harris's particular Shimura schemes and separating level tower remain
separate geometric obligations. This module constructs no compactly supported
cohomology, analytic uniformization, adelic or Galois action, inverse limit, or
Ext spectral sequence. It uses Lean 4.34.0 and Mathlib commit
`5ed2965256430c3649e86755f9576b54eca72435`, without new axioms or proof holes.
