# Compatible Harris coefficient trivializations through a fixed exponent

Author: Worker 13.

This module trivializes the finite portion of the whole Harris coefficient
diagram reachable from a fixed exponent, on that exponent's torsor cover.
It preserves the actual higher-to-lower coefficient reductions. The forward
map at the identity arrow agrees with the previously constructed
single-exponent trivialization.

## Provenance and conventions

This is an internal specialization of the published
[HarrisEtaleCoefficients](HarrisEtaleCoefficients.md) and the accompanying
[VaryingCoverLocalTrivialization](VaryingCoverLocalTrivialization.md).
The coefficient source and its exact original passages are recorded in
`HarrisEtaleCoefficients.md`. This module introduces no new direct
literature assertion. In particular, it does not identify these associated
sheaves with the constant coefficient sheaf used in Harris's
Proposition 4.4.

All algebra, field, representation, group and scheme carriers are in universe
`u`. The inputs are a rational algebra `A` finite as a module over `ℚ`,
an algebra involution `σ`, a number field `L`, a finite-dimensional
`L`-module `W`, an algebraic representation
`ρ : (σ.baseChange L).AlgebraicRepresentation W`, a rational finite place
`p`, a compact open level `U` in `σ.FiniteAdelicPoints`, and `m : ℕ`.

Write `R = HarrisCoefficientIntegers L p`, the product of completed
integer rings over every coefficient place of `L` above `p`, and
`M = harrisCoefficientDiagram ρ p U m`. Its module at `op n` is the
constructed stable full lattice modulo the `n`th power of the ideal
generated by the scalar `m`. Its transitions are the actual lattice
reductions. The acting group diagram is
`D = harrisCongruenceGroups ρ p U m`, formed from quotients of the original
level group by the kernels of these coefficient actions. All coefficient
places and exponent zero remain in the construction.

Let `X : Scheme.{u}`. The geometric inputs are
`Y : EquivariantDiagram D X.Etale` and
`hY : ∀ n, IsFiniteGeometricTorsor X (Y.action n)`.
They provide actual finite geometric torsors and compatible equivariant
cover maps. Neither associated sheaves nor their trivializations are input
fields. The scalar `m` may be zero subject to these hypotheses; no
unconditional finiteness of its coefficient reductions is asserted.
Imported coefficient-finiteness results retain their `m ≠ 0` hypothesis.

## Constructed diagrams and isomorphism

Put `J = Scheme.smallEtaleTopology X` and fix `k : ℕᵒᵖ`.
An object of `Under k` is an arrow from `k` to a lower or equal exponent.
Thus this index includes all exponents from zero through the exponent
underlying `k`.

`harrisEtaleLocalCoefficientSystem k` restricts
`harrisEtaleCoefficientSystem ρ p U m X Y hY` along `Under.forget k`,
then applies `J.overPullback (ModuleCat R) (Y.obj.obj k)`.
`harrisEtaleLocalConstantSystem k` similarly restricts the original
underlying coefficient diagram, forms constant sheaves on `J` and
applies that same localization. Both are functors from `Under k` to
`Sheaf (J.over (Y.obj.obj k)) (ModuleCat.{u} R)`. There is no additional
identification with a constant-sheaf functor formed on the slice site.

`harrisEtaleLocalCoefficientDiagramIso k` specializes
`etaleAssociatedSourceTrivialization` to `M`. The generic construction
uses the proved geometric graph-splitting theorem to make each invariant
evaluation invertible, and the proved transition/evaluation equation to
assemble an isomorphism of diagrams.

The lemma `_hom_app` gives its actual forward component at
`i : Under k`: it is `etaleAssociatedEvaluationAlong X Y M i.right`
along `Y.obj.map i.hom`. For a morphism `f : i → j` in `Under k`, the
theorem `_transition` gives

```text
localized associated transition(f.right) ≫ evaluation(j)
  = evaluation(i) ≫ localized constant coefficient reduction(f.right).
```

This is the naturality equation of the constructed diagram isomorphism.
The sheaf transition on the left is the published coefficient-reduction
map followed by the inverse of the proved torsor-cover comparison.
The map on the right is the original whole-Harris module transition
under the constant-sheaf and localization functors.

The lemma `_identity_hom` identifies the component at
`Under.mk (identity k)` with the forward map of
`harrisEtaleCoefficientTrivialization ... k`. Its proof changes the
component to `associatedEvaluationAlong`, uses the cover functor's
identity law, and applies `associatedEvaluationAlong_id`. The right
side is definitionally the previously published invariant evaluation.
This proves agreement of the actual forward maps.

## Remaining scope

The fixed cover trivializes the diagram through `k`. No assertion is made
about larger exponents on that cover, a common trivialization of an
infinite tower, or comparison between different slice sites.

The scheme and torsors have not been constructed as Harris's particular
Shimura levels. Coefficient-action kernels need not form his separately
chosen separating tower. The actual geometric realization remains an
independent obligation. Lisse-adic compatibility, inverse limits,
cohomology-limit comparison, compact supports, smooth adelic and continuous
Galois actions, and the uniformization and Ext spectral-sequence
constructions remain outside this module's claim.

Formal environment: Lean 4.34.0 and Mathlib commit
`5ed2965256430c3649e86755f9576b54eca72435`. Every intrinsic obligation is
inherited from the generic construction or proved by the displayed
identity and naturality laws. No new axiom or proof hole is introduced.
