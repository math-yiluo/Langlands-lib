# Finite rational-scalar coefficient reductions

Author: Worker 57. This module specializes the published stable-lattice coefficient system to the
actual ideal generated by a rational natural-number scalar in the completed
coefficient ring. It proves finiteness of the torsion carrier, separately
from finiteness of the congruence quotient group.

For a number field `L` and finite place `l`, `CoefficientReductionRing l m n`
is the quotient of `CoefficientIntegers l` by `(m)^n`. The scalar `m` is not
replaced by a uniformizer. The ring is finite when `m` is nonzero, for every
natural exponent including zero.
There is no primality assumption on `m` and no assumption that `l` lies over
a prime divisor of `m`. In particular, the result includes unit scalars and
the zero-exponent quotient.

`coefficientQuotient_finite` proves the more general finiteness of the quotient
by any nonzero ideal. The proof uses the discrete valuation ring structure and the published finite
residue field of the actual completed integer ring. Every nonzero ideal is a
power of the maximal ideal; each such quotient is finite. Characteristic zero
makes `(m)^n` nonzero. This is an algebraic proof and needs no continuity of an
action.

For an actual `StableCoefficientLattice`, `rationalTorsionSystem` is its
existing equivariant inverse system with ideal `(m)`. Every term has finite
carrier for nonzero `m`, by finite generation of the lattice over the coefficient
ring and finiteness of the scalar quotient. Transition maps are the existing
surjective module quotient maps. All action and functor laws are inherited
from the published construction.

`rationalTorsionAction` constructs the action at a fixed index with scalars
in `CoefficientReductionRing l m n`, using the published coefficient-reduction
functor. Its underlying quotient carrier is definitionally the term of
`rationalTorsionSystem`; the latter retains scalars in the integral ring so
that its varying-index transition maps belong to one category.
`rationalTorsionAction_mk` proves that the group action on a quotient
representative is the reduction of the original integral action.
`rationalTorsionAction_finite` proves finiteness of this same carrier.

The stable lattice input uses the actual coefficient-place representation:
a finite-dimensional rational algebra with anti-involution, a number field
`L`, an `L`-vector space `W`, and an algebraic representation of the
scalar-extended similitude functor. Its finite places `l` and `p` satisfy
the published lies-over condition, and its acting group is the chosen
subgroup `U` of finite adelic points. All carriers use universe `u`.
Finite-dimensionality of `W` is not an additional hypothesis here:
finite generation of the supplied full lattice already gives the needed
finite-module certificate. No action topology or continuity is used.

This is derived infrastructure for Harris's torsion coefficient construction.
It does not construct the initial stable lattice, prove algebraic-action
continuity, identify a geometric deck group, or construct the associated
geometric local system. It specializes to a single coefficient completion.
It makes no claim of an exact torsion cardinality or a constructed free basis.
