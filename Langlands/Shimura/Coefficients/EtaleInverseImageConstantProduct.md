# Constant product coordinates for geometric inverse image

This internal construction combines the existing product-sheaf base-change map
with the canonical constant-sheaf comparison. It supplies coordinates for
comparing the invariant-section formulas used by the actual geometric CM
coefficient systems. The construction and its three compatibility laws apply
to arbitrary schemes and scheme morphisms.

All declarations are in `Langlands.Shimura.Coefficients`.

## Objects, hypotheses and notation

Fix a universe `u`, schemes `X`, `Z`, `W : Scheme.{u}`, morphisms
`f : Z ⟶ X`, `g : W ⟶ Z`, a commutative ring `R : Type u`, an object
`V : X.Etale`, and a module `M : ModuleCat.{u} R`.

Write:

- `C_X(M)` for the constant module sheaf on the small etale site of `X`;
- `P_V` for `productSheaf X.smallEtaleTopology V`;
- `f^*` for `Langlands.Geometry.EtaleInverseImage.inverseImage f R`;
- `b_f` for `etaleObjectPullback f`;
- `c_f(M)` for `(inverseImageConstantIso f R).hom.app M`.

Here `f^*` is the existing geometric inverse-image functor, constructed by
Kan extension and sheafification with its direct-image adjunction. The notation
does not replace it by restriction along an etale morphism. No etaleness
assumption on `f` or `g` is imposed. The cover objects themselves belong to the
respective small etale categories.

`coverPullback J a F` is contravariant in the cover map `a`: if `a : V' ⟶ V`,
it maps `P_V(F)` to `P_V'(F)`.

## Constant product comparison

`inverseImageConstantProductMap f R V M` is the morphism

```text
f^*(P_V(C_X(M)))  →  P_(b_f V)(C_Z(M))
```

defined as the composite

```text
inverseImageProductSheafMap f R V (C_X(M))
  ; P_(b_f V).map(c_f(M)).
```

The first factor is the published product-sheaf comparison for arbitrary
geometric inverse image. The second changes its coefficient sheaf from
`f^*C_X(M)` to `C_Z(M)` using the published constant-sheaf isomorphism.
This file defines a morphism and proves its coherence laws; it does not
introduce an isomorphism wrapper or require a torsor structure.

## Naturality in the cover

For every actual cover morphism `a : V' ⟶ V`,
`inverseImageConstantProductMap_coverPullback` proves

```text
f^*.map(coverPullback a (C_X(M))) ; CP_f(V',M)
  =
CP_f(V,M) ; coverPullback (b_f.map a) (C_Z(M)).
```

Here `CP` denotes the constructed constant product map. The proof unfolds that
map, uses `inverseImageProductSheafMap_coverPullback`, and moves the coefficient
comparison across cover restriction by `coverPullback_natural`. The actual
pulled cover map is retained.

## Composition

`inverseImageConstantProductMap_comp` proves

```text
(inverseImageComp R f g).hom.app(P_V(C_X(M))) ; CP_(g;f)(V,M)
  =
g^*.map(CP_f(V,M)) ; CP_g(b_f V,M)
  ; coverPullback ((etaleObjectPullbackComp f g).hom.app V) (C_W(M)).
```

The left side starts with the actual comparison from iterated inverse image
`g^* f^*` to inverse image along `g ≫ f`. The cover comparison on the right
maps the single pullback `b_(g;f) V` to the iterated pullback `b_g(b_f V)`;
contravariance of `coverPullback` gives the displayed direction on sheaves.
Consequently both sides have the literal single-pullback product sheaf as
their target.

The implemented proof first reassociates and applies
`inverseImageProductSheafMap_comp`. Functoriality expands inverse image of the
constructed composite. Naturality of the product comparison moves
`c_f(M)` across the product comparison for `g`.
`inverseImageConstantIso_comp` then combines the two coefficient comparisons
through `inverseImageComp`. Finally, functoriality of the product sheaf and
`coverPullback_natural` move those comparisons across the actual cover
comparison. Only these established identities and associativity are used.

## Equality transport

For `k : Z ⟶ X` and an equality `e : f = k`,
`inverseImageConstantProductMap_congr` proves

```text
(inverseImageCongr R e).hom.app(P_V(C_X(M))) ; CP_k(V,M)
  ; coverPullback ((etaleObjectPullbackCongr e).hom.app V) (C_Z(M))
  =
CP_f(V,M).
```

This retains both genuine equality comparisons: the one on inverse-image
functors and the one on literal fiber-product covers. The proof substitutes the
base-morphism equality. Extensionality for etale over-morphisms followed by the
two fiber-product projections identifies the reflexive cover comparison with
the identity. `coverPullback_id` and `inverseImageCongr_refl` then close the
equation.

All three theorems have reassociated versions generated by `@[reassoc]`.

## Provenance and scope

This is an internal deduction from
`EtaleInverseImageAssociatedIso`, `EtaleInverseImageConstant` in the geometry
namespace, and `EtaleGeometricCoefficientDiagram`. It introduces no new
external-literature assertion. The Markdown describes the implemented Lean
proofs, which use the published geometric comparisons and literal cover maps.

No finiteness, torsor, group-action, rigidity, reduced lattice injectivity or
invertibility premise is used. This unit does not itself prove the CM
coarse-tower inverse-image comparison, composition of original lattice-system
morphisms, representability, existence of canonical models, or the remaining
source-specific Harris geometry. Those are separate consumer obligations.
