# Harris coefficient trivialization on actual derived categories

Author: Worker 13.

This internal specialization applies the actual source-local derived functors
to the whole Harris lattice coefficient diagram placed in one integer degree.
It constructs the derived local coefficient and constant objects, their
invariant-evaluation isomorphism, and canonical comparisons with the ordinary
diagrams in that degree. Conjugating by those comparisons recovers the existing
ordinary Harris trivialization, both as a diagram and at every reachable cover.

## Provenance and inputs

This module specializes
[VaryingCoverDerivedLocalTrivialization](VaryingCoverDerivedLocalTrivialization.md)
to the published coefficient and ordinary local constructions in
[HarrisEtaleCoefficients](HarrisEtaleCoefficients.md) and
[HarrisEtaleLocalTrivialization](HarrisEtaleLocalTrivialization.md).
The original coefficient-source passages and conventions remain those recorded
in `HarrisEtaleCoefficients.md`. There is no new direct literature assertion.
In particular, this module does not identify the constructed associated sheaves
with Harris's particular constant sheaf in Proposition 4.4.

Let `A : Type u` be a rational algebra with `[Ring A]`, `[Algebra ℚ A]` and
`[Module.Finite ℚ A]`. Let `σ : AlgebraInvolution ℚ A`, let `L : Type u`
be a number field, and let `W : Type u` be a finite-dimensional `L`-module.
The coefficient input is an algebraic representation
`ρ : (σ.baseChange L).AlgebraicRepresentation W`, a rational finite place
`p`, a compact open level `U` in `σ.FiniteAdelicPoints`, and a natural-number
scalar `m`.

Write

```text
R = HarrisCoefficientIntegers L p
D = harrisCongruenceGroups ρ p U m
M = harrisCoefficientDiagram ρ p U m.
```

The ring `R` retains every coefficient place of `L` above `p`. At exponent
`op n`, `M` uses the whole stable lattice modulo the `n`th power of the ideal
generated by `m`, with the original higher-to-lower reduction maps. The group
diagram `D` consists of the quotients of the actual level group by the kernels
of these coefficient actions. Exponent zero is included.

The geometric inputs are an actual scheme `X : Scheme.{u}`,
`Y : EquivariantDiagram D X.Etale`, and
`hY : ∀ n, IsFiniteGeometricTorsor X (Y.action n)`. Thus actual compatible
étale objects and their equivariant cover maps are supplied. The torsor
predicate includes finite deck groups, finite surjective structure morphisms
and unique deck translations between geometric lifts over the same base.
No associated sheaf or local comparison is assumed. The parameter `m` may be
zero subject to these hypotheses; the imported coefficient-finiteness results
continue to require `m ≠ 0`.

## Categories and conventions

Fix `k : ℕᵒᵖ` and put

```text
J = X.smallEtaleTopology
V = Y.obj.obj k
C = EquivariantDiagram D (ModuleCat.{u} R)
T = Sheaf (J.over V) (ModuleCat.{u} R)
S = Under k ⥤ T.
```

An object of `Under k` is an arrow from `k` to a lower or equal exponent.
The fixed cover therefore trivializes the portion of the coefficient diagram
through `k`. The target is the derived category of the entire diagram
category `S`. Both ordinary localized diagrams use `J.overPullback`; no
identification with a separately formed slice-site constant-sheaf functor is
made.

Scheme, group, ring and module carriers share universe `u`. The construction
uses actual choices `HasDerivedCategory.{wC} C` and
`HasDerivedCategory.{wS} S`. The evaluation construction additionally uses
`HasDerivedCategory.{wT} T`. These independent universes specify Mathlib's
quasi-isomorphism localizations of integer-indexed cochain complexes. Standard
choices `HasDerivedCategory.standard` are available at the corresponding
maximum object/morphism universes. The abelian structures are constructed from
the existing equivariant-diagram category and module sheafification on the
actual slice site; neither an abelian structure nor sheafification is assumed
as an extra geometric input.

The integer `q : ℤ` below is the cochain degree in
`DerivedCategory.singleFunctor`. It is independent of the coefficient exponent
index and denotes placement in one degree with zero objects in other degrees.
The surrounding derived categories use Mathlib's unbounded conventions.

## Derived coefficient objects and comparison

Let `F = etaleAssociatedSourceSystemFunctor X Y hY k` and
`G = etaleConstantSourceSystemFunctor X Y k`. Their exactness and the natural
isomorphism between their actual derived functors are constructed in
`VaryingCoverDerivedLocalTrivialization`. Let `Fᴰ` and `Gᴰ` denote those
derived functors, and let `eᴰ : Fᴰ ≅ Gᴰ` be that isomorphism.

`harrisEtaleDerivedLocalCoefficient ... k q` is
`Fᴰ.obj (single_C(q).obj M)`, and
`harrisEtaleDerivedLocalConstant ... k q` is
`Gᴰ.obj (single_C(q).obj M)`.
`harrisEtaleDerivedLocalCoefficientIso ... k q` is the component of `eᴰ`
at precisely `single_C(q).obj M`. Thus its forward map is the descended
invariant-section evaluation for the original full coefficient diagram.

Put `H = harrisEtaleLocalCoefficientSystem ... k` and
`K = harrisEtaleLocalConstantSystem ... k`.
`harrisEtaleDerivedLocalCoefficientSingleIso` and
`harrisEtaleDerivedLocalConstantSingleIso` are the components at `M` of
Mathlib's `mapDerivedCategorySingleFunctor` for `F` and `G`, respectively.
Write these as

```text
a : Fᴰ(single_C(q)(M)) ≅ single_S(q)(H)
b : Gᴰ(single_C(q)(M)) ≅ single_S(q)(K).
```

The theorem `harrisEtaleDerivedLocalCoefficientIso_single` proves

```text
a.inv ≫ harrisEtaleDerivedLocalCoefficientIso.hom ≫ b.hom
  = single_S(q).map (harrisEtaleLocalCoefficientDiagramIso ... k).hom.
```

The proof applies the generic source-local single-degree formula, then a
private categorical cancellation lemma. That lemma substitutes the comparison
formula and cancels the two isomorphisms using associativity and inverse laws.
The cancellation is proved abstractly before specializing to the full Harris
data.

## Actual derived evaluation and component recovery

For `i : Under k`, let `E_i : S ⥤ T` be ordinary evaluation at `i`.
`harrisEtaleDerivedLocalEvaluation ... k i` is `E_i.mapDerivedCategory`.
Ordinary evaluation is exact by pointwise limits and colimits. The resulting
derived evaluation commutes with integer shifts and preserves distinguished
triangles, with both instances exposed on the named functor.

`harrisEtaleDerivedLocalCoefficientEvaluationIso` first applies derived
evaluation to `a`, then composes with the component at `H` of
`E_i.mapDerivedCategorySingleFunctor q`. It gives

```text
a_i : E_iᴰ(Fᴰ(single_C(q)(M))) ≅ single_T(q)(H.obj i).
```

`harrisEtaleDerivedLocalConstantEvaluationIso` constructs the corresponding
`b_i` for `G` and `K`.
The theorem `harrisEtaleDerivedLocalCoefficientIso_evaluation` proves

```text
a_i.inv ≫ E_iᴰ.map(harrisEtaleDerivedLocalCoefficientIso.hom) ≫ b_i.hom
  = single_T(q).map
      (etaleAssociatedEvaluationAlong X Y M i.right (Y.obj.map i.hom)).
```

The right side is the original invariant-section evaluation along the genuine
map from the source torsor to the cover at `i.right`. The proof applies the
diagram-level recovery equation under `E_iᴰ`, combines its mapped
compositions, and uses the naturality of
`E_i.mapDerivedCategorySingleFunctor q`. The ordinary evaluation functor
reduces its final component to the stated map.

Finally, `harrisEtaleDerivedLocalCoefficientIso_identity` specializes this
equation to `Under.mk (𝟙 k)`. The right side becomes

```text
single_T(q).map (harrisEtaleCoefficientTrivialization ... k).hom.
```

This uses the existing ordinary identity-component theorem, so the recovered
derived forward map agrees with the original single-exponent invariant
evaluation. All three recovery equations have `reassoc` versions.

## Scope and formal environment

This construction derives local coefficient association and its comparison
maps on supplied genuine geometry. It does not construct the particular
Shimura schemes or torsors required by Harris, identify the coefficient-action
kernels with his separately chosen separating tower, or compare different
slice sites. It does not assert a common trivialization of the infinite tower
on one fixed cover.

These are derived objects in categories of local sheaf diagrams, without an
application of global sections or compactly supported sections. Derived
inverse limits, cohomology-limit comparisons, smooth adelic and continuous
Galois actions, analytic uniformization and the Ext spectral sequence remain
separate obligations. No existence or compatibility theorem from that scope
is an assumed field here.

The implementation uses Lean 4.34.0 and Mathlib commit
`5ed2965256430c3649e86755f9576b54eca72435`, in particular
`Algebra/Homology/DerivedCategory/ExactFunctor.lean`. It inherits the proved
exactness and localization obligations from the generic construction and
proves the displayed recovery equations by categorical identities. No new
axiom or proof hole is introduced.
