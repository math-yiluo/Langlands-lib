# Compact algebraic coefficients with derived continuity

Implementation: Worker 13. The compact-coefficient interfaces were designed
by Worker 57; the imported general algebraic orbit-continuity proof is by
Worker 255.

This is an internal specialization of published constructions. It removes
the explicit local orbit-continuity input from the compact algebraic
coefficient construction by supplying a published proof. It does not change
the existing generic definitions.

The inputs are a finite-dimensional rational algebra `A` with
anti-involution `σ`, a number field `L`, an `L`-module `W`, and the actual
published representation `ρ : (σ.baseChange L).AlgebraicRepresentation W`.
These three carrier types share universe `u`. A finite place `l` of `L`
determines the coefficient space `V = FiniteCompletion L l ⊗[L] W`, with
`coefficientTopology L l W`, definitionally its module topology over the
completion. Point groups retain their published module, unit and subtype
topologies.

`continuous_algebraicCoefficient_orbit` applies the published
`AlgebraicRepresentation.continuous_act` to the scalar-extended algebra
`L ⊗[ℚ] A` and the actual completion. Finite-dimensionality of that algebra,
and freeness over `L`, follow from the input finite-dimensionality and the
field structure. Thus every map `g ↦ ρ.act (FiniteCompletion L l) g x`
is continuous, without an action-continuity premise. This lemma does not
require `W` to be finite-dimensional.

For a finite rational place `p`, with `l` lying over `p`,
`continuous_algebraicAdelicCoefficient_orbit` supplies this proved result
to `continuous_placeAction_orbit`. That result composes the local orbit map
with the already constructed continuous `coefficientPlaceMap σ p l`,
so it proves continuity of the actual finite-adelic `ρ.placeAction p l`.

For the remaining constructions, `W` is finite-dimensional and `U` is an
actual compact open subgroup of `σ.FiniteAdelicPoints`.
`algebraicCompactCoefficientLattice` supplies the proved local continuity
to `compactStableCoefficientLattice`. Its carrier equality states that
the lattice remains `compactCoefficientLattice ρ p l U`: the integral
span of the `U`-orbit of the actual coordinate lattice. Its full-lattice
and stability proofs are those of the existing constructor. The new
openness and integral-action continuity lemmas reuse its established
properties. The integral action uses the actual lattice subtype topology.

For every natural scalar `m ≠ 0` and every `n : ℕ`,
`algebraicCompactCoefficient_congruenceKernel` proves openness of the
action kernel on reduction modulo `(m)^n` and finiteness of the actual
quotient group. Here `(m)` is `coefficientScalarIdeal l m`, generated by
the image of the rational natural number in `CoefficientIntegers l`.
It is not replaced by a maximal or uniformizer ideal. Exponent zero is
included.

`algebraicCompactFiniteCoefficientAction` is the existing
`rationalCongruenceAction` of that quotient group on the actual module
over `CoefficientReductionRing l m n`. The representative formula
`algebraicCompactFiniteCoefficientAction_mk` identifies its pullback with
the constructed rational torsion action. Its finiteness theorem supplies
both a finite acting group and a finite coefficient carrier for `m ≠ 0`.
The action itself is defined for all `m,n`.

`algebraicCompactCoefficientDiagram` assembles the integral-scalar
quotient modules and their existing reduction maps into an
`EquivariantDiagram` over the same `rationalCongruenceGroups m`.
Integral scalars are retained in this diagram because its successive
quotient rings differ. Its transition maps are surjective by the
published quotient-module transition theorem. This coefficient diagram,
including surjectivity, is defined for every `m`.

With `m ≠ 0`, `algebraicCompactCongruenceLevels` constructs the ambient
compact open levels from those open kernels. Given an arbitrary group
`Γ`, a `Γ`-space `X` with a topology, and a homomorphism
`ι : Γ →* σ.FiniteAdelicPoints`,
`algebraicCompactCongruenceSpaces` constructs the corresponding actual
double-quotient spaces with their varying congruence-group actions.
Its underlying functor is the constructed level tower composed with
`LevelSpace.tower`. The natural forgetful projection to the original
`U`-level is constructed, and its component surjectivity is proved.
`Γ` and `X` have independent universes `v` and `w`; the space category is
`TopCat.{max u w}`. No continuity of the `Γ`-action on `X` is assumed.

`algebraicCompactContinuousLevelSpaces` supplies the same proved local
continuity to the published continuous-action constructor. It yields an
inverse diagram in Mathlib's `ContAction TopCat U.subgroup`. The existing
constructor proves joint continuity using the actual quotient topology
on each `U/N`, which is discrete because the kernel is open, and
continuous restriction back to `U`. The displayed object equality
identifies its spaces with those of `algebraicCompactCongruenceSpaces`;
the transition formula sends `[x,a]` to `[x,a]` at the coarser level.
The shared identity `HasForget₂` helper is installed locally.

The provenance is the explicit composition of the published
`AlgebraicOrbitContinuityProof`, `AlgebraicTopology`, `CompactCoefficient`,
`FiniteQuotient`, `CongruenceDiagram`, `CongruenceLevels` and
`NormalLevelContinuous` implementations. No additional literature
assertion or comparison theorem is assumed. The construction treats one
completion `L_l`; it does not assemble all places above a rational prime.
Rational stabilizers remain in the geometric quotients. No cofinality,
geometric freeness or faithfulness, finite fibers, covering or torsor,
scheme realization, canonical model, sheaf descent or cohomological
comparison is concluded.
