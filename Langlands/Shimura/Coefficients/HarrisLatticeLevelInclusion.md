# Actual Harris lattice inclusions under compact-level refinement

This internal construction proves that the canonical full Harris lattices
are nested when the compact open level is refined. It constructs the actual
ambient inclusion, proves compatibility with the original lattice actions,
and induces a natural map of the entire simultaneous torsion systems.

## Inputs and original coefficient constructions

The types have a common universe `u`. Let `A` be a ring with a rational
algebra structure and finite as a rational module, let `L` be a number field,
and let `W` be a finite-dimensional `L`-module. Fix an algebra involution
`σ : AlgebraInvolution ℚ A`, an algebraic representation
`ρ : (σ.baseChange L).AlgebraicRepresentation W`, and a rational finite place
`p`. Let `U` and `V` be actual compact open levels in
`σ.FiniteAdelicPoints`, with `hVU : V.subgroup ≤ U.subgroup`.
Normality is not required.

Write

\[
R=\operatorname{HarrisCoefficientIntegers}(L,p)
  =\prod_{\ell\mid p}\mathcal O_{L_\ell},\qquad
B=L\otimes_{\mathbb Q}\mathbb Q_p,\qquad
W_B=B\otimes_L W.
\]

These are the existing completed coefficient algebra, full integral product
ring, and tensor coefficient module. The integral action on `W_B` uses the
published canonical embedding `R → B`. The full tensor module is related
to the product of completion modules by the existing
`harrisCoefficientIntegralEquiv`.

At a coefficient place `l` above `p`, let `Λ₀,l` be the existing
`coefficientLattice L l W`. For a compact open level `Q`, the published
`compactCoefficientLattice ρ p l Q` is

\[
\Lambda_{Q,l}
 =\operatorname{span}_{\mathcal O_{L_l}}
   \{\rho_l(g)y:g\in Q,\ y\in\Lambda_{0,l}\}.
\]

It is the carrier of `algebraicCompactCoefficientLattice`. Its original
construction proves fullness, finite generation, and stability from
continuity of the actual algebraic coefficient action. In particular,
the initial coordinate lattice `Λ₀,l` is the same for `U` and `V`.

The product lattice `harrisProductLattice ρ p Q` consists of tuples with
each coordinate in `Λ_Q,l`. The full lattice
`Λ_Q = harrisLattice ρ p Q` is its inverse image under the same canonical
integral splitting, inside the original `W_B`. Both lattices here are the
independently constructed canonical lattices for the indicated levels.

## Nested lattices and actual inclusion

`compactCoefficientLattice_mono` proves
`Λ_V,l ≤ Λ_U,l`. It applies monotonicity of submodule span: every generating
translate at `V` is the generating translate at `U` for the image of that
same group element under `Subgroup.inclusion hVU`.

`harrisProductLattice_mono` applies this containment at every completion
place. `harrisLattice_mono` pulls that containment back through the
unchanged integral splitting and proves

\[
\Lambda_V\ \le\ \Lambda_U.
\]

`harrisLatticeInclusion` is the resulting `R`-linear submodule inclusion
`j_VU : Λ_V → Λ_U`. Its `coe` theorem identifies its value with the same
vector of the original tensor coefficient module. The `injective` theorem
proves injectivity by equality of the underlying ambient vectors.

For a third compact open level `T ≤ V`, the `comp` theorem proves the
exact equality of linear maps

\[
j_{VU}\circ j_{TV}=j_{TU}.
\]

All three maps retain the same ambient vector; linear-map and subtype
extensionality prove the equality. No comparison isomorphism or lattice
containment is supplied as an additional premise.

## Original action compatibility

Let `A_Q = harrisLatticeAction ρ p Q` be the existing integral action on
`Λ_Q`. Its ambient operator at `g ∈ Q` is the actual
`ρ.harrisAdelic p g`. Let `ι : V → U` be `Subgroup.inclusion hVU`.

`harrisLatticeActionInclusion` constructs the action morphism

\[
A_V\longrightarrow\operatorname{res}_\iota A_U.
\]

Its underlying module map is exactly `j_VU`, as the `hom` theorem records.
Its commutation field is proved from the identical ambient coefficient
operators. The separate `harrisLatticeInclusion_action` theorem gives the
pointwise equality

\[
j_{VU}(\rho_V(g)x)=\rho_U(\iota(g))j_{VU}(x).
\]

Thus the action and subgroup conventions are the original ones; no new
group action or equivariance premise is introduced.

## Simultaneous torsion systems

For any natural scalar `m`, put
`I = harrisCoefficientScalarIdeal L p m`, the ideal generated by the
simultaneous integral scalar `m`. The existing full lattice system is

\[
T_Q(k)=\Lambda_Q/(I^{k.\mathrm{unop}}\Lambda_Q),
\qquad k\in\mathbb N^{\mathrm{op}},
\]

with its integral `R`-module structure and original group action.
Its exponent maps are the original quotient-module reductions.

`harrisTorsionSystemInclusion` constructs a natural transformation

\[
T_V\longrightarrow T_U\mathbin{\ggg}\operatorname{res}_\iota.
\]

At every exponent it applies the published ideal-power reduction functor,
lifted to `V`-actions, to `harrisLatticeActionInclusion`. The target action
is precisely the original `U` action restricted to `V`. Naturality follows
from the naturality of `AdicSystem.transition` at the actual lattice
inclusion, with its equality oriented for the exponent diagram.

The `mk` theorem states that the component sends the class of
`x ∈ Λ_V` to the class of the same ambient vector, viewed in `Λ_U`.
The `transition` theorem records that these components commute with
every original exponent reduction. The scalar `m` and the exponent may
both be zero: no finiteness or nontriviality assertion about these
reductions is made in this pair.

## Provenance and remaining scope

The only direct import is `HarrisReduction`. Its published dependencies
construct the canonical local orbit-span lattices, the product and tensor
lattices, the original integral actions, and the full simultaneous
torsion systems. The new monotonicity argument uses the same generators
and the actual subgroup inclusion. The linear/action maps use the proved
containment and the identical ambient operators. Applying the published
reduction functor constructs all reduced maps and proves their exponent
coherence. This is an internal deduction; it adds no new external-source
assertion.

The result compares the actual canonical lattices selected by the existing
construction at nested levels. It does not assert equality of those
lattices, injectivity or invertibility of their torsion comparison maps,
commensurability for arbitrary independently supplied lattices, or
independence of the resulting rational adic sheaves. Factoring these maps
through compatible finite residual groups and descending them on actual

arithmetic existence, and canonical-model obligations are unchanged.
