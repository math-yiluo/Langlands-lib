# Compact levels and actual stable coefficient lattices

Author: Worker 57. This is a derived construction of local coefficient data.

## Inputs and topology

Let `A` be a finite-dimensional rational algebra with anti-involution `σ`,
`L` a number field, and `W` a finite-dimensional `L`-vector space. The input
`ρ` is the published algebraic representation of the scalar-extended
similitude group. Choose a finite place `p` of the rationals, a finite place
`l` of `L` lying over `p`, and an actual compact open level `U` in the finite
adelic points of `σ`.

The coefficient space is `V = FiniteCompletion L l ⊗[L] W`, with its
canonical module topology over `FiniteCompletion L l`. Its integral ring
is the actual valuation ring `CoefficientIntegers l`. The hypothesis `hρ`
asserts that all orbit maps of `ρ.act (FiniteCompletion L l)` are continuous
for this topology. Continuity is an explicit hypothesis in this module;
it has not been proved here from algebraic naturality.

The action of `U` is exactly the restriction of `ρ.placeAction p l`.
The place action is already constructed using adelic evaluation, the
completion embedding and tensor scalar extension. The published continuous
coefficient-place map transfers `hρ` to continuous adelic orbit maps, which
remain continuous on `U` by the subtype inclusion. Compactness of `U` is
obtained from its compact-level certificate.

## Constructed lattice and action

`compactCoefficientLattice` is the span over `CoefficientIntegers l` of all
`U`-translates of the published coordinate lattice `coefficientLattice`.
Its carrier is defined even before supplying `hρ`.
`compactCoefficientLattice_properties` applies the published compact orbit
span theorem to prove that this explicit submodule is a full lattice,
stable under the actual restricted action, and open in `V`.

`compactStableCoefficientLattice` packages this carrier and these proved
properties in the existing `StableCoefficientLattice` type. Thus its
lattice and stability certificates are derived; no chosen stable lattice
is supplied as input. The existing type supplies finite generation and
freeness over the local coefficient ring.

The module also exposes the inherited topology, topological additive-group
structure and finite-module instance on the underlying module of any
`StableCoefficientLattice.action`. These instances use the actual lattice
subtype. `continuous_compactCoefficientAction_orbit` proves continuity of
the constructed integral action by lifting the continuous ambient orbit
maps into that subtype.

## Rational scalar reductions

For every natural number `m ≠ 0` and `n : ℕ`,
`compactCoefficient_congruenceKernel` proves openness of the actual kernel
of the action on the reduction modulo `(m)^n` and finiteness of the group
quotient by this kernel. It applies the published congruence-kernel theorem:
the lattice is finite as a module, its orbit maps are continuous, and the
reduction submodule is open. The last fact is exactly the published
`coefficientScalarPower_isOpen` applied to the constructed open lattice.
The theorem includes `n = 0` and does not require `m` to be prime.

`compactCoefficientQuotientAction` constructs the torsion representation
of this quotient group with the published quotient lift. Its pullback
along the quotient homomorphism agrees with the original torsion action,
as proved by `compactCoefficientQuotientAction_mk`. The factorization is
defined for all `m,n`; finiteness of the acting quotient uses `m ≠ 0`.
This is a finite quotient *group*. The module does not prove finiteness of
the underlying torsion coefficient carrier.

For a rational prime `ell`, the ideal used is generated by the image of
`ell` in `CoefficientIntegers l`. It is not replaced by the maximal ideal
or by a uniformizer ideal, so the construction retains the rational prime
powers required by the sources even at ramified coefficient places.

## Derived provenance and remaining scope

Harris (1999), *Galois properties of cohomological automorphic forms on
GL(n)*, Section 3, printed pp.307–308 (PDF pp.9–10), chooses a compact-local-
group-stable integral lattice, passes to continuous torsion coefficients,
and assumes factorization through a finite congruence quotient. Registered
source: `harris-1999-cohomological-automorphic-forms-525a222c4489ee9d`,
SHA-256 `525a222c4489ee9d3fe86a52c6c17ffb30fcc7c31f24c16a64669e9615085b26`.
Harris–Taylor (2001), *The Geometry and Cohomology of Some Simple Shimura
Varieties*, Section III.2, printed pp.94–95 (PDF pp.102–103), uses an
invariant finite free integral lattice and finite deck-group factorizations
of its rational-prime-power reductions. Registered source:
`harris-taylor-2001-geometry-cohomology-simple-shimura`, SHA-256
`5be28164b61d6f6cc28dd9e6b990d669504f3470860b230ebbc97e3da10af56b`.

The implementation is a derived prerequisite combining already proved
algebraic and topological results. It specializes to one actual coefficient
completion; it does not assemble Harris's tensor coefficient algebra over
all places above a rational prime. It does not prove coefficient-field
descent from an algebraic closure, unconditional continuity of arbitrary
algebraic representations, independence of the initial coordinate lattice,
or a finite torsion carrier. The constructed quotient is not yet identified
with a geometric deck group, and no scheme level or sheaf descent is asserted
by this module. Those remain obligations of the surrounding construction.
