# Principal reductions of local coefficient lattices

Author: Worker 57.

## Construction and hypotheses

For a number field `L`, a finite place `l`, and a natural number `m`,
`coefficientScalarIdeal l m` is the actual principal ideal generated by
`m` in `CoefficientIntegers l`, the valuation ring of `FiniteCompletion L l`.
The definition does not assert that this ideal is prime. When `m` is the
rational prime ell used in the coefficient construction, its powers are
precisely the rational-prime ideal powers, including at ramified coefficient
places. No uniformizer substitution is made.

`principalPower_smul_isOpen` applies to a commutative ring `R`, a field
`K` with an `R`-algebra structure, and a `K`-vector space `V` with a compatible
`R`-action and topology for which multiplication by each constant in `K`
is continuous. If the image of `a : R` in `K` is nonzero and `Lambda` is
an open `R`-submodule of `V`, then `(a)^n Lambda` is open in `V` for every
natural `n`. `principalPower_top_isOpen` proves that `(a)^n top` in the
module `Lambda` is open for its inherited topology. Neither result requires
finite generation, fullness, compactness, or an injective algebra map on
all of `R`; the nonzero image of the chosen scalar suffices.

`coefficientScalarPower_isOpen` specializes the latter result to an arbitrary
`L`-vector space `W` and any open integral submodule of
`FiniteCompletion L l tensor_L W`. The ambient topology is explicitly
`moduleTopology (FiniteCompletion L l)`. For every nonzero natural `m`, all
powers of `coefficientScalarIdeal l m` give open reduction submodules inside
the integral submodule. Finite dimensionality and a condition that `l` lie
above `m` are unnecessary for this openness conclusion.

## Implemented argument

`Ideal.span_singleton_pow` and `Submodule.ideal_span_singleton_smul`
identify the ideal-power submodule with multiplication by `a^n`.
`algebraMap_smul` identifies this with the nonzero field scalar
`(algebraMap R K a)^n`. Openness is then `IsOpen.smul₀`: the continuous
scalar map has a continuous inverse, given by the reciprocal scalar.

`Submodule.mem_smul_top_iff` identifies the reduction submodule inside
`Lambda` with the inverse image of the ambient ideal multiple under the
continuous subtype inclusion. Pulling back the open set proves the second
result. In the local specialization, characteristic zero of the actual
completion follows from its algebra structure over `L`, by
`Algebra.charZero_of_charZero`, so every nonzero natural scalar stays nonzero.

## Provenance and scope

This is derived elementary infrastructure for Harris (1999), Section 3,
printed pages 307--308 (PDF pages 9--10), and Harris--Taylor (2001),
Section III.2, printed pages 94--95 (PDF pages 102--103). Harris uses a
level-stable integral lattice and continuous torsion coefficient actions;
Harris--Taylor explicitly reduces an invariant finite free integral lattice
by rational-prime powers and factors the actions through finite deck groups.
Neither source states the general scalar-multiple lemma here verbatim.
This module constructs the relevant ideals and proves their open-reduction
property, an input to the separately stated congruence-kernel theorem.

This unit does not prove continuity of an algebraic representation, openness
of its congruence kernels, finite quotient cardinalities, or geometric
coefficient descent. It applies to a given open integral submodule; constructing
an open stable lattice and applying the congruence-kernel theorem are separate
steps. It uses only published imports and does not import the published
`Langlands.Shimura.Coefficients.LocalLattice` coordinate-lattice construction.
