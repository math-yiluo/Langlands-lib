# The Kummer sequence

`Langlands/GaloisCohomology/Field/KummerSequence.lean`, namespace `Langlands.GaloisCohomology`.

For a separable closure `L` of `K` and `n` invertible in `K`, the sequence of Galois modules
`0 → μ_n → Lˣ → Lˣ → 0` (the last map `x ↦ xⁿ`) is exact. With Hilbert 90 its long exact
sequence gives Kummer theory in cohomological form:

- `H¹(Gal(L/K), μ_n) ≅ Kˣ / Kˣⁿ` (`kummerEquiv`);
- `H²(Gal(L/K), μ_n) ≅ Br(L/K)[n]`, the `n`-torsion of `H²(Gal(L/K), Lˣ)` (`kummerH2Equiv`).

The file also contains the elementary Kummer theory of the source (`Kˣ/Kˣⁿ`, the Kummer group
of an extension and the Kummer pairing with the Galois group).
