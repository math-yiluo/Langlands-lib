# Group structure of the Galois group of a local field

`Langlands/GaloisCohomology/Local/EulerCharacteristic/GroupStructure.lean`, namespace `Langlands.GaloisCohomology.Local`.

Group theory for the reduction of the local Euler characteristic formula to simple modules: the
subgroup generated by the elements of order prime to `p` (`coprimePart`), by the elements of
`p`-power order (`primaryPart`), their preimages in a group with a cyclic quotient, and the
relative version for a normal subgroup of a subgroup. Applied to `G = Gal(L/K)` with inertia `G₀`
and wild inertia `G₁`, they give the subgroups used in `Main.lean`: one containing `G₁` with
cyclic quotient of `p`-power order and index over `G₁` prime to `p`, and for `l ≠ p` one with
quotient of order prime to `l` whose fixed field contains `μ_l`.
