# Section subgroup of the actual two-power torsion closure

`TwoPowerTorsionClosureSubgroupStatement` asserts that, for an actual abelian scheme `A` over a field `K`, the actual sections over the identity test scheme whose images belong to `closure (twoPowerTorsionLocus A)` form a subgroup of the existing section group. It also asserts that translation by every section in this subgroup preserves the entire closed subset of the underlying scheme. Both the point locus and all section operations are the existing constructions. The field has arbitrary universe; algebraic closedness and characteristic zero are not required for this particular consequence.

This is an internal geometric prerequisite for torsion density, derived from commutativity of the actual section group and continuity of scheme translations and inversion. It records only a subgroup of actual sections and topological invariance. It does not construct a closed subgroup scheme, identify its dimension, count its torsion, or prove density.

The motivating source is Edixhoven, van der Geer and Moonen, *Abelian Varieties*, preliminary version, Chapter V, §3, Theorem (5.30), printed page 84 / PDF page 88, registered as `edixhoven-vandergeer-moonen-abelian-varieties-draft`. The first paragraph of its prime-to-characteristic proof uses translations and inversion to analyze the reduced closure of prime-power torsion. The assertion here isolates the section and underlying-topology part of that argument and derives it over any field. The stronger scheme-theoretic subgroup assertion and subsequent dimension comparison are separate obligations.
