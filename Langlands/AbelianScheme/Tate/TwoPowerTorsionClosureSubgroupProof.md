# Actual sections in the two-power torsion closure

This module proves `TwoPowerTorsionClosureSubgroupStatement` and constructs `twoPowerTorsionClosureSectionSubgroup A`, a subgroup of the actual sections over the identity test scheme. Its carrier is exactly the set of sections whose image belongs to the Zariski closure of the existing `twoPowerTorsionLocus A`.

The proof first shows that actual two-power torsion is stable under inversion and multiplication: torsion exponents `2^n` and `2^m` are both killed by `2^(n+m)`. The inversion morphism is continuous, so it preserves the closure. Translation by a torsion section preserves the original locus and therefore its closure. If `s` is any section landing in the closure, commutativity and translation by a torsion section show that translation by `s` sends every torsion point into the closure. Continuity extends this inclusion to the entire closure. Applying the same argument to `s⁻¹` proves equality of the translated closure and the original closed subset.

All operations are those of the actual abelian scheme and its existing group of sections. The argument holds over any field at arbitrary universe. This is the section and topological part of the subgroup argument motivating Theorem (5.30) in Edixhoven, van der Geer and Moonen, *Abelian Varieties*, preliminary version, Chapter V, §3, printed page 84 / PDF page 88, registered as `edixhoven-vandergeer-moonen-abelian-varieties-draft`. The implementation is an internal derivation from group-object identities and continuity; it does not claim the subsequent assertion that a reduced closure is a subgroup scheme. It proves no dimension comparison, numerical torsion formula, or density theorem.
