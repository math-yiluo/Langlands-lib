# Density of actual geometric two-power torsion

`twoPowerTorsionLocus A` is the subset of the underlying topological space of the actual abelian scheme `A` consisting of images of sections over the identity test scheme `Spec Ω` whose `2^n`-th power is the identity, for some natural number `n`. The operation on sections is the existing group-object operation. This is the union of the actual geometric two-power torsion points; it uses neither a replacement group nor a chosen torsion basis.

`FieldTwoPowerTorsionDensityStatement` asserts that this subset is Zariski dense for every actual abelian scheme over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field, at arbitrary universe. This file states that density assertion without proving it.

The source is Edixhoven, van der Geer and Moonen, *Abelian Varieties*, preliminary version, Chapter V, §3, Theorem (5.30), printed page 84 (PDF page 88), registered as `edixhoven-vandergeer-moonen-abelian-varieties-draft`. Their theorem gives scheme-theoretic density for prime-power torsion, and topological density when the prime differs from the characteristic. The statement here is its specialization to the prime two in characteristic zero.

The internal derived theorem `hom_eq_zero_of_dense_twoPowerTorsion` proves the remaining geometric implication conditional on density, over any field. If the existing `GroupScheme.Tate.levelMap` vanishes at every two-power level, its evaluation on each torsion section says that the original homomorphism and the zero homomorphism agree on that section. The torsion locus therefore lies in the image of their equalizer in the over-category. Density makes the equalizer's underlying morphism dominant. The source is reduced because it is smooth over a field, and the target is separated because it is proper. Mathlib's `ext_of_isDominant_of_isSeparated` gives equality of the underlying scheme morphisms, and extensionality gives equality of the actual abelian-scheme homomorphisms. This bridge does not prove the density assertion or unconditional Tate faithfulness.
