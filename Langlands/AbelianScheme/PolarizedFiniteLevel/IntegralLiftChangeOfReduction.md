# Integral lifting equations under an isomorphism of reduction bases

Pullback along an isomorphism of schemes gives an equivalence on actual
abelian schemes, with inverse the pullback along the inverse isomorphism.
The comparison between direct and successive pullback is natural.
Consequently a marking over an isomorphic presentation of the reduction
descends to an actual abelian-scheme marking over the original base.
Full faithfulness then reflects all the endomorphism equations along
that marking.

For a dual-valued homomorphism, the canonical dual comparison is an
isomorphism. The composition rule and the naturality of this comparison
identify the pulled-back polarization equation with transport along
the descended marking. Cancellation of the dual comparison and
faithfulness give the integral equation over the original reduction.

For a surjective ring map `φ`, `quotientPresentationIso` uses the first
isomorphism theorem to identify its target spectrum with the spectrum
of the source modulo `ker φ`. Its compatibility with the two maps to
the thicker base applies the transport result to the usual quotient
used in the affine deformation calculation. These comparisons are
derived from actual abelian and dual base change.
