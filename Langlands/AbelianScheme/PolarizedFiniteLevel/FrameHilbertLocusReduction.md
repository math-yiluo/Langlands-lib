# Reduction of the frame Hilbert locus to the abelian and polarization loci

`frameHilbertLocus_of_pointedAbelian_and_cubicPolarization` proves the
published `FrameHilbertLocusStatement` from
`PointedProjectiveAbelianLocusStatement` and `CubicPolarizationLocusStatement`.
The conclusion is the unchanged universe-zero proposition.

The proof first cuts out incidence and coordinate normalization and restricts
to the basic open of the level. Over this base the zero mark supplies a
section of the Cartesian projective family. The pointed abelian locus gives
an abelian scheme of the prescribed dimension on the corresponding open,
with all the labelled sections carried along by the family isomorphism.

Over the abelian locus the closed marked full-level locus and the cubic
polarization locus are taken over the same base. Their fibre product makes
both witnesses available on one pullback of the abelian scheme. The datum
uses the supplied polarization and its dual witness and the full level on
that common pullback. Its embedding is the closed family, with the degree-one
twist compared by the naturality of the projective embedding. The closed
coordinate equations make it a normalized embedding. The theorem for
normalized cubic embeddings gives the full frame locus.

For the factorization property, any test realization satisfies the incidence,
normalization, and level-unit conditions. Its image isomorphism identifies its
abelian scheme with the pulled-back pointed family, yielding factorization
through the abelian locus. Pointed rigidity identifies that test abelian scheme
with the one pulled back from the abelian locus. The isomorphism transports
the test polarization, degree, and full level, with the original embedding
and labels. The two remaining locus properties give its factorization through
their fibre product.

The reduction follows the steps of Mumford, Fogarty and Kirwan, *Geometric
Invariant Theory*, 3rd ed. (1994), Proposition 7.3, printed pp. 132–134
(PDF pp. 145–147), registered source
`provided-ec58cb66687d0d54d9e39b6ec21281b651fe3a6d039f9b5d099c79791671233f`.
It works directly with the given projective family and fixes dimension and
polarization degree through the two named loci. The frame-specific coordinate
calculation supplies the evaluation condition on this normalized family.
