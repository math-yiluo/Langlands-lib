# Ampleness of the embedding bundle on geometric fibres

The relative `𝒪(1)` is ample over an affine base. The proof uses quasi-compactness from properness and the affine standard coordinate opens. The coordinate sections, transported to the first tensor power, have precisely those generation opens.

A closed immersion over the original base induces a closed immersion into projective space over the test by the Cartesian square. The relative projective-space twist comparison identifies the pulled-back embedding bundle with the pullback of `𝒪(1)` over the test. `closedImmersionPullbackAmple` gives ampleness for every affine test and therefore on geometric fibres.
