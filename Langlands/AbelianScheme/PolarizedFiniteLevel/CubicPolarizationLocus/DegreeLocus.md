# The prescribed-degree locus of an isogeny

`exists_degreeSquare_locus` represents the tests on which an isogeny of abelian schemes has native degree `d²` by an open subscheme of the locally noetherian base. The proof first shows that the locally constant rank at a point of the target equals the rank at the identity over the same base point, because the fibre is connected. It takes the inverse image of `d²` under the resulting locally constant function on the base. The isogeny base change square computes the rank at every point of a changed target. Evaluating at the changed identity recovers the factorization criterion in the converse direction.

This is the native-rank refinement of the degree condition in Mumford, Fogarty and Kirwan, *Geometric Invariant Theory*, 3rd ed.
