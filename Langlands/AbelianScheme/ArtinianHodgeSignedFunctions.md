# The inversion summands of the function complex

A compatible action of inversion on the chart indices induces a linear
involution on the function cochains and commutes with the Čech
differential. The cochain modules are flat because their affine
intersection rings are smooth over the base and the index sets are
finite. The projectors onto the signs commute with the differential.

A compatible function family is constant, and pullback by inversion
preserves that constant. This makes the degree-zero differential
injective on the negative summand. Over a field the canonical class
comparison and the inversion signs in structure-sheaf cohomology make
the positive summand exact in degree one and the negative summand exact
in degree two. The proof projects a coboundary primitive into the
same summand as its cocycle.
