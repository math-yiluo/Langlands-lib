# Integral Hom in finite-dimensional rational subspaces

`Langlands.AbelianScheme.HomLocalLatticeStatement.{u}` concerns actual abelian
schemes `A` and `B` over `Spec k`, where `k : Type u` is an algebraically closed
field of characteristic zero. For every finitely generated rational submodule
`V` of the existing `Hom0 A B`, it states finite generation over the integers of

```lean
((V.restrictScalars ℤ).comap (homToHom0 (A := A) (B := B)))
```

This is the submodule of actual integral homomorphisms `f : A ⟶ B` for which
`1 ⊗ f` lies in `V`. Here `Hom0 A B = ℚ ⊗[ℤ] (A ⟶ B)` and `homToHom0` is the
published canonical integer-linear map. The Hom group and its integer module
are the existing ones from `Langlands.AbelianScheme.Basic`.

For a rational submodule, `V.FG` is equivalent to finite dimensionality.
The proposition only asserts finite generation of the integral homomorphisms
in each such subspace. It asserts no dimension bound or finite dimensionality
for the entire rational Hom space, and supplies no proof or global instance.
It requires neither a chosen polarization nor an assumed Tate comparison.
The universe `u` is arbitrary.

The provenance is a derived intermediate result from the corrected proof of
J. S. Milne, *Abelian Varieties* (1986 article, author revision of 2 January
2022), Proposition 12.4 and Theorem 12.5, printed/PDF pp.21–22.
On p.22, for a simple abelian variety, the polynomial degree function separates
zero from nonzero integral endomorphisms in every finite-dimensional rational
endomorphism subspace. Milne deduces that the integral intersection is discrete
and therefore finitely generated, and records the common-denominator
consequence as (*). A finite-dimensional subspace of the rationalization is
spanned by finitely many integral endomorphisms after clearing their
denominators, so it is covered by this argument.

For general `A,B`, the subsequent paragraph of Milne's proof uses isogenies
with products of simple factors to inject `Hom(A,B)` into a finite product of
simple endomorphism groups. Rationalizing this injection sends any supplied
finite-dimensional subspace into finite-dimensional subspaces of those
endomorphism spaces. Their integral intersections are finitely generated by
the simple case; the original integral intersection embeds in their finite
product, hence is finitely generated over the noetherian ring `ℤ`.
This explains the general-Hom formulation here. It is an extracted consequence
of that proof, rather than an additional theorem explicitly numbered by Milne.

The complete registered author errata snapshot
`milne-abelian-varieties-1986-errata-2026-10-25` was read. It identifies a
correction to the proof of Theorem 12.5 and states that the corrected online
article incorporates it. The cited 2022 text contains the discreteness and
common-denominator argument.

This geometric local statement is intended as an independent prerequisite for
the finite-freeness proof. That proof must still establish finiteness modulo
an integer, control the global rational rank, and deduce finite generation and
freeness. None of those global conclusions is built into this proposition.
