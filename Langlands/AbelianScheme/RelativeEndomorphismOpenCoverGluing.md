# Gluing the relative endomorphism scheme

Suppose a base open cover has actual separated locally finite type
representatives for the relative endomorphisms of the pulled-back
abelian schemes. `globalRelativeEndomorphismRepresentation` constructs
an actual scheme over the original base. Its natural comparison uses
the actual relative endomorphisms on the full slice category.

The comparison of relative Hom functors under base change identifies
the supplied chart functors with restrictions of the original one.
Relative Hom is a sheaf for the subcanonical slice Zariski topology.
Forgetting the additive structure and lifting the value universe
preserve this sheaf property. The Zariski gluing theorem constructs the
scheme of the lifted functor. Full faithfulness of the lift recovers
the comparison in the original universe. Locality on the target
gives the locally finite type and separated structure map.

This is the base open-cover step for the relative endomorphism
construction used in the abelian scheme moduli problem of Kottwitz,
*Points on Some Shimura Varieties over Finite Fields*, §5, p. 391.
