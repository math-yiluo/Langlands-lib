# The Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich criterion from Néron models and the torsion criterion

`Langlands.AbelianScheme.NeronOggShafarevichReductionStatement.{u}` is the
implication

```
NeronModelExistenceStatement.{u} →
StrictHenselianRationalTorsionGoodReductionStatement.{u} →
NeronOggShafarevichStatement.{u}
```

and `Langlands.AbelianScheme.neronOggShafarevichReduction` proves it. The proof takes
two proposition-valued premises:

- `Langlands.AbelianScheme.NeronModelExistenceStatement`: every abelian variety over
  the fraction field of a discrete valuation ring has a Néron model (Bosch,
  Lütkebohmert and Raynaud, *Néron Models*, §1.3, Corollary 2);
- `Langlands.AbelianScheme.StrictHenselianRationalTorsionGoodReductionStatement`: let
  `N` be the Néron model over `V` of an abelian variety over `K`, let `ℓ` be a prime
  invertible in `V`, let `W` be a strictly Henselian discrete valuation ring with a
  local homomorphism `V → W` such that the maximal ideal of `V` generates that of `W`
  and the residue extension is separable, and let `L` be a fraction field of `W` with a
  compatible map `K → L`. If all `ℓ`-power torsion points with values in an algebraic
  closure of `L` are rational over `L`, then `N` is proper with geometrically connected
  fibres. The premise quantifies over every such `W` and `L`; for each of them the
  separable closure of `K` in `L` is the fraction field `K^sh` of a strict
  henselization, so the premise is *Néron Models*, §7.4, Theorem 5, (c) ⇒ (b).

The proof also uses two proved results:
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.unramifiedStrictHenselianDvrExtension` (over every
discrete valuation ring `V` there is, in the same universe, a strictly Henselian
discrete valuation ring `W` with a local homomorphism `V → W` such that the maximal
ideal of `V` generates that of `W` and the residue extension is separable; the
construction is a maximal subring of an algebraic closure of the fraction field and is
not identified with a strict henselization) and
`Langlands.AbelianScheme.Tate.PrimeAdic.hasRationalGeometricTorsion_of_rationalMonodromy_eq_one`
(module `Langlands.AbelianScheme.Tate.TrivialMonodromyRationalTorsion`).

The route is that of *Néron Models*, §7.4, Theorem 5, printed pp. 183–184. There, over
the fraction field `K^sh` of the strict henselization `R^sh` of `R = V` the inertia
group is the whole Galois group, so trivial inertia action on the Tate module makes
all `ℓ`-power torsion rational over `K^sh`, which is condition (c) of that theorem. The
Lean proof does the same over the fraction field `L` of the ring `W` constructed by
`unramifiedStrictHenselianDvrExtension`, which has the properties of `R^sh` listed above;
the torsion premise applies to it because it quantifies over all such `W`.

## Proof

Let `V`, `K`, `B`, `ℓ` and the hypothesis of `NeronOggShafarevichStatement` be given.

1. `CommutativeAlgebra.unramifiedStrictHenselianDvrExtension` gives a strictly
   Henselian discrete valuation ring `W` with a local homomorphism `V → W`, the
   maximal ideal condition `hmax` and the separability `hsep` of the residue
   extension. Let `L = FractionRing W`.
2. `injective_algebraMap_of_map_maximalIdeal` shows that `V → W` is injective: if a
   nonzero element `v = c ϖ^n` of `V` (with `c` a unit and `ϖ` a uniformizer) mapped
   to zero, then `ϖ` would map to zero because `W` is a domain, so the maximal ideal
   of `W`, which is generated by the image of the maximal ideal `(ϖ)` of `V`, would be
   zero, contradicting `IsDiscreteValuationRing.not_a_field`. Hence
   `V → W → L` is injective and `IsFractionRing.lift` extends it to a ring map
   `K → L`, used as the algebra structure; `IsFractionRing.lift_algebraMap` gives the
   commuting square `V → W → L = V → K → L`.
3. The images of the powers of `ℓ` in `Γ(Spec L, ⊤)` are units, as images of the units
   `ℓ ^ m` of `V` under `V → W → L → Γ(Spec L, ⊤)` (the last map is the inverse of
   `Scheme.ΓSpecIso`).
4. The hypothesis of the criterion, applied to `W`, `hmax`, `L`, the algebraically
   closed field `AlgebraicClosure L` and the geometric point
   `Spec.algebraMap L (AlgebraicClosure L)`, says that the fundamental group of
   `Spec L` acts trivially on the rational `ℓ`-adic Tate module of the base change
   `B_L = (baseChange (Spec.algebraMap K L)).obj B`. By
   `Tate.PrimeAdic.hasRationalGeometricTorsion_of_rationalMonodromy_eq_one`, every
   `ℓ ^ ν`-torsion point of `B_L` with values in `AlgebraicClosure L` is rational over
   `L`, for every `ν`.
5. The first input gives a Néron model `N` of `B` over `V`. The second input, applied
   to `N`, `W`, `hmax`, `hsep`, `L`, the commuting square, `ℓ` and step 4, shows that
   `N.group` is proper with geometrically connected fibres.
6. `NeronModel.toAbelianScheme` turns `N` into an abelian scheme over `V`, and the
   inverse of `NeronModel.toAbelianSchemeGenericIso` is an isomorphism from its
   generic fibre (base change along `Spec.algebraMap V K`, which is
   `NeronModelInfra.specGenericFibreInclusion V K` by definition) to `B`. This is the
   conclusion of `NeronOggShafarevichStatement`.

The hypothesis of the criterion is used at one ring `W` and one geometric point only.
