# Inversion on the relative differential frame

For a finite free sheaf the pullback of a global basis induces the basis
of the pulled-back sheaf. The identity section induces an injection on
global functions because its composite with the structural morphism is
the identity and every global function comes from the base. Applying
these facts to the section coordinates proves that evaluation of global
one-forms at the identity is injective.

The cotangent map at the identity is additive on homomorphisms between
commutative group schemes. Inversion is the negative of the identity,
so its cotangent map is minus the identity. The comparison of pullbacks
along the identity section and the maps of differential sheaves make
this sign hold on global sections. On each affine open, the map on
Kähler generators agrees with the map of the differential sheaf,
which gives the same sign on each restricted frame vector.

The cotangent and global section comparisons implement the identification
of the left end of the Hodge sequence in Lan, *Arithmetic compactifications
of PEL-type Shimura varieties*, revision of 14 March 2021, §2.1.6, printed
