# Henselian ascent for finite local DVR extensions

The adic valuation of the supplied upstairs DVR restricts to the
downstairs valuation ring. For the reverse inclusion, a fraction
outside the downstairs ring has its inverse inside. If the fraction
were integral upstairs, its inverse would become a unit upstairs
and hence a unit downstairs by locality.

For a Henselian downstairs DVR in characteristic zero, prolong the
upstairs adic valuation to the algebraic closure of its fraction
field. The restricted valuation is Henselian downstairs. The
Library's invariance theorem on the finite Galois subextensions
gives invariance under the whole Galois group. The valuation ring
in the algebraic closure is Henselian. Descent of simple-root
lifting along the Galois extension to the upstairs fraction field
therefore proves that the supplied upstairs DVR is Henselian.
