# Torus rank bounds for supplied Néron models

A supplied Néron model has the relative dimension of its identified abelian
variety. The abelian variety has irreducible underlying space because it is
smooth and connected over a field. Flatness makes the generic fibre dense
in the total space, which is then irreducible and connected. The smooth
relative dimension is constant on that space and agrees with the given
one on the abelian generic fibre.

For a supplied split semiabelian presentation of the geometric special
identity component, the torus enters the special fibre through its
scheme-theoretic kernel inclusion and the open identity-component
inclusion. This immersion bounds its topological Krull dimension by
that of the fibre. The split torus rank is its Lie dimension, bounded
by its topological Krull dimension. The smooth chart dimension bounds
the topological Krull dimension of the quasi-compact geometric special
fibre. Together these bounds give the asserted rank inequality.

## Provenance

This supplies the geometric dimension bound in the division argument of
Kottwitz, *Points on some Shimura varieties over finite fields* (1992),
§5, printed p. 392. The implemented proof uses the Library's smooth
relative dimension, actual fibre, split Lie rank and immersion results.
