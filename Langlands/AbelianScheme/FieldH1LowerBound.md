# The relative dimension of an abelian variety is at most `dim H¹(A, 𝒪_A)`

Author: Worker 372.

This internal helper module proves the lower bound `g ≤ finrank_k H¹(A, 𝒪_A)` for an abelian
scheme `A` of relative dimension `g` over `Spec k`, `k` an arbitrary field (`le_finrank`). Here
`H¹(A, 𝒪_A)` is `(DerivedSections.relativeCohomology A.X.hom 1).obj (structureSheaf A.X.left)`.
The argument uses no Picard scheme, no dual abelian variety and none of the representability
statements. It works with the formal hull of the Picard deformation problem at the trivial bundle
(`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Picard.PicardGroupoid.FieldMinimalPicardHull`) and with the
finite Mumford locus (`Langlands.AbelianScheme.FieldMumfordFiniteLocus`).

## Outline

Let `O` be the stalk of `A` at the unit point, with maximal ideal `𝔪`, and let `n` be the number
of basis vectors of the first-order classes of the trivial bundle on the residue fibre.

1. The Mumford class `Q` is trivial exactly on a closed subscheme `Z ⊆ A` finite over `k`. So `Z`
   is locally Artinian, and `𝔪_{Z,z} ^ c = 0` at the point `z` over the unit, for some `c`.
2. Pull `Q` back to the Artinian test `O/𝔪^{c+1}`. The result is trivial on the residue fibre.
   The hull has `n` generators. So the pulled-back class becomes trivial modulo an ideal `J`
   generated by `n` elements of the maximal ideal.
3. Then `Spec (O/𝔪^{c+1})/J ⟶ A` factors through `Z`, so it kills `𝔪^c`. Lifting the `n`
   generators to `O` gives `𝔪^c ⊆ (b'_1, …, b'_n) + 𝔪^{c+1}`. Nakayama and Krull give
   `dim O ≤ n`.
4. `dim O = g` because `A` is smooth of relative dimension `g` and the unit point is closed.
   `n = finrank_k H¹(A, 𝒪_A)` because the first-order classes are `H¹` of the residue fibre, and
   the residue fibre is `A` itself.

## Declarations

### Section `Stalk`

Inputs: `f : X ⟶ Spec k`, a section `e` with `e ≫ f = 𝟙`, and a `k`-algebra structure on the
stalk at `e (closedPoint k)` whose `Spec.map` is `X.fromSpecStalk _ ≫ f` (hypothesis `hO`).

- `stalkAugmentation f e he hO` is the `k`-algebra map from the stalk to `k` with underlying
  ring map `Scheme.stalkClosedPointTo e`. It is `k`-linear because
  `Spec.map (stalkClosedPointTo e) ≫ fromSpecStalk = e` (Mathlib
  `Scheme.Spec_stalkClosedPointTo_fromSpecStalk`) and `e ≫ f = 𝟙`.
- `stalkAugmentation_surjective`: it is surjective, since it sends `algebraMap k _ r` to `r`.

### Section `Hull` (`f : X ⟶ Spec k` proper and flat)

- `residueTrivial f` is the trivial line bundle on the residue fibre.
- `exists_span_trivializing T ℓ hℓ`: let `T` be an Artinian test over `k` with residue field `k`,
  and let `ℓ` be a relative Picard class on `T` whose pullback along `ArtinianTest.reduction T` is
  `0`. Then there are elements `b : Index f (residueTrivial f) → T.algebra` such that the ideal
  `J = span (range b)` lies in the augmentation kernel, and `ℓ` pulls back to `0` on the quotient
  test `T/J` (`ArtinianTest.quotient`).

  Proof. Pick a line bundle `L` representing `ℓ`. Its residue fibre is isomorphic to the hull's
  residue fibre, since both have class `0` (`hullResidueIso`). This makes `L` a pointed Artinian
  deformation `N`. Formal versality (`ArtinianChartVersality.exists_realization_point` with
  `isFormallyVersalAt`) gives a point `x` of the hull base over `T` with the right reduction and
  with `x^* hullBundle ≅ L`. `AffineQuotientTests.exists_affineBaseMap_eq` writes `x` as
  `Spec ψ` for a `k`-algebra map `ψ : hullRing → T`. Injectivity of `affineBaseMap` and the
  reduction equality give `T.augmentation ∘ ψ = hullAugmentation`. Put `b i = ψ (generator i)`;
  these lie in the augmentation kernel by `hullAugmentation_generator`. On `T/J`, `ψ` kills every
  generator, so `eq_constant_of_generator` makes it the constant map through the residue field.
  The point `x` restricted to `T/J` therefore factors through `hullPoint`. Since the hull bundle
  is trivial at `hullPoint`, the class `ℓ` pulls back to `0` on `T/J` (`Group.pullback_comp`).

### Section `Geometric` (`A : AbelianScheme (Spec k)`)

- `unitPoint A` is `unitSection A (closedPoint k)`. `stalkAlgebra A` is the `k`-algebra structure
  on its stalk given by `Spec.preimage (fromSpecStalk ≫ A.X.hom)`. `spec_stalkAlgebra` is the
  corresponding equation `hO`.
- `ringKrullDim_stalk_le_card A`: `ringKrullDim O ≤ Fintype.card (Index A.X.hom
  (residueTrivial A.X.hom))`, where `O` is the stalk at `unitPoint A`.

  Proof.
  1. `O` is Noetherian (`LocallyOfFiniteType.isLocallyNoetherian`). `ε` is
     `stalkAugmentation` for the unit section.
  2. `FieldMumfordFiniteLocus.exists_finite_plain_locus` gives `Q`, `Z` and `j`.
     `IsLocallyArtinian Z` follows from `IsLocallyArtinian.of_locallyQuasiFinite` for the finite
     morphism `j ≫ A.X.hom`. `ArtinianLocusStalk.exists_uniform_pow_eq_bot` gives `c`.
  3. `T = LocalAdicArtinianTests.test ε hε c` is `O/𝔪^{c+1}`, and `π` is its projection. The
     point `xT : base T ⟶ A.X` is `Spec π ≫ fromSpecStalk`. `hred` checks that
     `reduction T ≫ xT = 1`. It rewrites `1` as `u ≫ 1` (`MonObj.comp_one`) with
     `u : residueBase k k ⟶ Over.mk (𝟙 _)`. Then `augmentation_projection` reduces both sides to
     `unitSection A`, via `Spec_stalkClosedPointTo_fromSpecStalk`, with `algebraMap k k = id`.
  4. `ℓ = Group.pullback xT Q`. Its reduction is the pullback of `Q` along `1`, which is `0`.
     `exists_span_trivializing` gives `b` with `J = span (range b)`. The universal property of
     `Z` then makes `baseMap (quotientMap T J) ≫ xT` factor through `j` by some `g`.
  5. `ψ = quotientMap ∘ π : O → (O/𝔪^{c+1})/J` is local: composed with the augmentation of the
     quotient test it is `ε`, which is `stalkClosedPointTo` and hence local
     (`isLocalHom_of_comp`). `ArtinianLocusStalk.map_eq_zero_of_factor` therefore gives
     `π a ∈ J` for every `a ∈ 𝔪^c`.
  6. Choose `b' i ∈ O` with `π (b' i) = b i`. They lie in `𝔪`, because `ε (b' i)` is the
     augmentation of `b i`, which is `0`, and `ker ε = 𝔪`. Since
     `J = Ideal.map π (span (range b'))`, `Ideal.mem_quotient_iff_mem_sup` gives
     `𝔪^c ≤ span (range b') ⊔ 𝔪^{c+1}`.
     `LocalGeneratorsKrullDimension.ringKrullDim_le_card` concludes.
- `ringKrullDim_stalk_eq A g hg`: with `A.HasRelativeDimension g`, `ringKrullDim O = g`. This is
  the Library theorem `fieldSmoothClosedPointStalkDimension` together with
  `isClosed_singleton_section_point A.X 1` (module
  `Langlands.GroupScheme.HomogeneousQuotientDimension`).
- `card_index_eq_finrank A`: `Fintype.card (Index A.X.hom (residueTrivial A.X.hom))` equals
  `finrank_k H¹(A, 𝒪_A)`. The card of `Index` is the `finrank` of the first-order classes
  (`Fintype.card_ulift`, `Fintype.card_fin`). `ArtinianDualNumberComparison.classH1LinearEquiv`
  (with `Scheme.OrderedAffineCover.ofCompactSpace`) identifies the classes with `H¹` of the
  residue fibre `residueSpace`. Because `(residueBase k k).hom = 𝟙`, the second projection
  `e : residueSpace ≅ A.X.left` is an isomorphism, and `residueStructure = e.hom ≫ A.X.hom`.
  `Langlands.Scheme.Modules.pullbackUnitIso` and
  `DerivedSections.relativeCohomologyPullbackIso` then identify the two `H¹`. Their `k`-linear
  equivalence (`Iso.toLinearEquiv`) preserves `finrank`.
- `le_finrank A g hg`: `g ≤ finrank_k H¹(A, 𝒪_A)`, from the three results above.

## Provenance

The inequality is the classical one: the tangent space of the Picard functor at `𝒪_A` is
`H¹(A, 𝒪_A)`, and `φ_L : A → Pic` has finite kernel `K(L)` for ample `L`
(Edixhoven, van der Geer and Moonen, *Abelian Varieties*, registered source
`edixhoven-vandergeer-moonen-abelian-varieties-draft`, Lemma (2.19)). So `dim A` is at most the
dimension of that tangent space. The classical proof uses the Picard scheme. The Lean proof
replaces it by the minimal formal hull at `𝒪_A` and works at one Artinian level `O/𝔪^{c+1}`, so
no representability is needed. It also needs no perfectness of `k`, and no reducedness of the
kernel.
