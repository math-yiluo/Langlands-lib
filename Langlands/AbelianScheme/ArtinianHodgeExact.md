# The finite free degree-one Hodge sequence for Artinian abelian schemes

`artinianHodgeExact` proves `ArtinianHodgeExactStatement` for an abelian scheme over a local Artinian rational algebra and for its supplied finite affine cover. The two maps are the class of a compatible closed relative one-form and the projection from a total cocycle to its function cocycle. Together with `ker_toStructureH1`, the theorem gives the short exact degree-one sequence with finite free end modules.

If a closed-form class vanishes, it is the total differential of a function cochain whose Čech differential is zero. The function cochain glues to a global function. Smoothness, properness and geometric connectedness imply that this global function comes from the base, so its relative derivative vanishes. This gives injectivity on the supplied cover.

Translation gives a finite frame of the relative differential sheaf from the cotangent module at the identity. A compatible one-form has constant coefficients in this frame, by the same global-function result. Extend those coefficients to the finite cover consisting of the original charts and their images under inversion. The resulting one-form is odd under inversion. Its exterior derivative is both odd by naturality and even because it is Čech compatible and has constant coefficients in the wedge frame. It vanishes because two is invertible. Restriction to the original charts proves closedness there, and the scalar coordinates give finiteness and freeness of the closed-form module.

The function cochain quotient on each finite affine cover agrees with first structure-sheaf cohomology, so proper coherent cohomology gives finiteness. For freeness, the function cochain modules on the doubled cover are flat over the base, and their inversion sign summands commute with reduction to the residue field. On that actual fibre, inversion acts by minus one on first structure-sheaf cohomology by primitivity, and by plus one on second cohomology by generation by products. The even summand is therefore exact in degree one, and the odd summand is exact in degree two with injective differential out of degree zero. The Artinian residue-complex lemmas lift even exactness and prove that first odd cohomology is free. Projection to the odd summand represents every class, because the even cocycles are boundaries. Inclusion of odd cohomology is injective since any primitive of an odd cocycle can be projected to the odd summand. The structure-sheaf comparison transfers freeness back to the supplied cover.

To lift a function class, take an odd cocycle \(a\) on the doubled cover representing the class under the canonical comparison. Then \(da\) has even function coefficients in the one-form frame. These coefficients are Čech cocycles, so even exactness gives coefficient primitives and a one-form cochain \(b_0\) with \(\partial b_0 = da\). The odd projection \(b\) of \(b_0\) preserves this equation. The derivative \(db\) is compatible since \(\partial(db) = d(da) = 0\). It is both even by compatibility and odd by naturality, and thus vanishes. The pair \((a,b)\) is a total cocycle. Restricting it to the original charts preserves all three cocycle equations, and the induced restriction on structure-sheaf classes is pullback by the identity. Its total class therefore lifts the original function class on the supplied cover.
