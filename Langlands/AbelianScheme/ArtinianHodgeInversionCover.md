# A cover adapted to group inversion

Given a supplied affine cover, use two copies of its index type and keep
each chart in the first copy. In the second copy compose each chart map
with the involution of the scheme. Both maps are open immersions from
the same affine scheme, and the first copy ensures that the family covers.
The cover is finite whenever the original cover is finite.

Inversion interchanges the two copies. The section pullbacks on a tuple
compose to the pullback along the identity after exchanging the two
copies twice. Thus the Čech function pullback is an involution. This
implements the functoriality of the actual Čech model for the inversion
map of an abelian scheme.
