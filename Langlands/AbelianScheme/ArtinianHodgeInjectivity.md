# Injectivity of the degree-one closed-form class map

A degree-zero Čech cocycle of a sheaf glues to a global section. The
compatibility equation used in gluing is the two-term alternating
restriction formula on each pair of opens. This also applies when cover
members repeat.

For an abelian scheme over an affine base, the isomorphism on global
functions is `smoothProperConnectedGlobalFunctions`. Thus a function
Čech cocycle consists of restrictions of a scalar and its relative
universal derivative vanishes on every chart. If a closed-form class
vanishes, a total boundary represents it. The function component says
that the boundary's primitive is a Čech cocycle. The form component
then vanishes, proving injectivity.

This gives the left-hand injectivity in Lan, *Arithmetic
compactifications of PEL-type Shimura varieties*, revision of 14 March
2021, §2.1.6, equation (2.1.6.5), printed p. 135 (PDF p. 163).
