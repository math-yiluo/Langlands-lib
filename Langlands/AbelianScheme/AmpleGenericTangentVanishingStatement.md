# No nonzero invariant vector field generically tangent to the zeros of all sections of an ample generated bundle

`Langlands.AbelianScheme.AmpleGenericTangentVanishingStatement.{u}` is a
`Prop`-valued definition, not a proof. Let `K : Type u` be algebraically closed,
`A : AbelianScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of K))`, and `N : LineBundle A.X.left`.
Assume `N.IsAmple` and that each rational point has a nonvanishing global section
of `N`. For every nonzero tangent vector `t : Spec (CommRingCat.of (DualNumber K)) ⟶ A.X.left`
at the unit and every nonempty open `U` in `A.X.left`, the proposition rules out
this condition:

> For each rational `y` in `U` and each global section `σ` of `N`, if `σ`
> vanishes at `y`, its pullback along `t ≫ (GroupScheme.translation A.toGrp y).left`
> is the zero section on the dual-number scheme.

The rational points are morphisms `Over.mk (𝟙 (Spec (CommRingCat.of K))) ⟶ A.X`;
`y` lies in `U` if `y.left (IsLocalRing.closedPoint K) ∈ U`. The nonzero tangent
hypothesis `IsNonzeroTangentVectorAt A.X.hom 1.left t` uses the Library's
dual-number description: the map is over `Spec K`, its reduction is the unit,
and it differs from the constant map to the unit. Translation by `y` takes this
vector to its value at `y`. `VanishesAlong` tests zero pullback, not just zero
reduction; if the reduction is zero but the coefficient of the dual-number
parameter is nonzero, the section does not vanish along the vector. There is no
characteristic restriction.

## Provenance and role

Mumford, *Abelian Varieties*, Oxford University Press (1970), §17, printed
pp.164–165 (PDF p.87 of `mumford-abelian-varieties-full-scan`), deduces from failure
of `|3D|` to separate tangents that a nonzero invariant vector field would be
tangent to the divisor of a reduced section of `L`. In characteristic `p` the
argument passes from the vector field to a height-one group scheme and uses a
quotient and an Euler characteristic count. The present statement is a derived
variant: it assumes tangency for *all* sections through each point of a common
nonempty open, for an ample bundle with no base points. In the cube-separation
proof it is used for `N = L ⊗ L`. Given a fixed section `s` of `L`, applying
failure of tangent separation by `L³` to `τ_a^*σ ⊗ τ_{(a a)⁻¹}^*s` gives this
condition on an open where the factor from `s` is a unit. The variant does not
require a reduced section divisor of `L`.

## Mathematical justification (not formalized here)

On a chart where a section `σ₀` does not vanish, subtracting the value of
`σ/σ₀` at `y` times `σ₀` makes a section through `y`. The displayed hypothesis
therefore says that the invariant vector field kills all such ratios at `y`.
Schematic density extends this to an identity of regular functions in each
chart, so the field annihilates the complete linear system map. The latter is
finite onto its image by ampleness (it is proper and no fibre can have positive
dimension). In characteristic zero, generic separability then forces the vector
field to be zero. In characteristic `p`, its span together with its iterated
restricted `p`-powers defines a nonzero restricted Lie subalgebra of the abelian
variety, hence a nontrivial subgroup of the Frobenius kernel. The ratios are
annihilated by this entire subalgebra, so the complete map is invariant under
the subgroup (the height-one distribution algebra is generated by its Lie
algebra). The map factors through the finite flat quotient, which is again an
abelian variety. As for a finite étale translation quotient, all the coordinate
sections of the complete system descend, but the dimension of sections of an
ample bundle pulled back by an isogeny multiplies by the degree. This contradicts
positive `h⁰(N)` and a quotient degree greater than one.
