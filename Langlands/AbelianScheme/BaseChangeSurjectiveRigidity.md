# Rigidity of abelian-scheme homomorphisms under surjective base change

A homomorphism that reduces to zero after a surjective base change sends
every point to the identity section over that point's image in the base.
Surjectivity of the pullback projection gives this by evaluating the
reduced equality after projecting to the original scheme. The homomorphism
preserves the identity section. The canonical isomorphisms on relative
global functions of the smooth proper source, with geometrically connected
fibres, then give factorization through the identity section by
`factorizationOfGlobalFunctions`. Applying this to a difference of
homomorphisms gives injectivity.

The rational statement tensors the integral map with the flat
`ℤ`-module `ℚ`, using the tensor formula for `rationalBaseChangeHom`.
The argument uses actual pullback and applies, in particular, to a closed
immersion defined by a nilpotent ideal. This recovers the abelian-scheme
case of the homomorphism rigidity in Lan's thesis revision of 14 March
2021, Lemma 2.2.2.1, printed p. 143. The source is
`lan-2008-pel-thesis-revision-2021`, SHA-256
