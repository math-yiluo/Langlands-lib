# Rational Hom and End for abelian schemes

Author: Worker 6.

This derived construction imports `Langlands.AbelianScheme.Basic` and `Langlands.CategoryTheory.Rationalization`. It specializes the generic tensor construction to the published category of abelian schemes over an arbitrary scheme S. The underlying bundle follows Basic's explicit-commutativity convention: its group scheme is commutative, smooth, proper, and has geometrically connected fibres. This module does not prove the separate equivalence with the source definition lacking explicit commutativity.

`RationalCategory S` has precisely wrapped objects of `AbelianScheme S`, identified by `rationalObjectsEquiv`. `Hom0 A B` is Q tensor_Z Hom_S(A,B), using the actual additive group of group-scheme homomorphisms from Basic. The Z-linear equivalence `hom0TensorComm` identifies it with Hom_S(A,B) tensor_Z Q and sends q tensor f to f tensor q. The preadditive and Q-linear category instances are inherited from the generic construction.

`rationalization` is the canonical additive functor, with Z-linear morphism map `homToHom0`, sending f to 1 tensor f. `id0 A` is 1 tensor the identity of A. `comp0` is the actual Q-bilinear extension of composition and sends (q tensor f, r tensor g) to qr tensor (f followed by g). The specialized unit and associativity lemmas apply the generic tensor-induction proofs directly. `homToHom0_id` is definitional, and compatibility with integral composition follows from the generic pure-tensor formula.

`End0 A` is the actual categorical endomorphism Q-algebra of the corresponding object in `RationalCategory S`; its ring and algebra instances come from the constructed Q-linear category. The formula `end0_mul` records that a * b is b followed by a, consistently with Mathlib's End ring. `end0_algebraMap` identifies the scalar q with q tensor the identity. `endToEnd0` is the unital ring map from integral endomorphisms and agrees with `homToHom0` on its underlying function.

`end0PostAction A B` is a Q-algebra homomorphism from End0(B) to the Q-linear endomorphisms of Hom0(A,B), so its bundled laws give preservation of the identity, multiplication, addition, and rational scalars. `end0PreAction A B a` is Q-linear in the morphism being acted on and acts by precomposition with a. Its unit and reversed multiplication laws are specialized from the generic precomposition laws. The two actions commute by associativity and agree with integral precomposition and postcomposition through the canonical maps, as the final specialized lemmas state.

The provenance is the application of a constructed generic rationalization to the actual preadditive category from Basic; no rational vector space or algebra is supplied as extra data. Harris–Taylor, *The Geometry and Cohomology of Some Simple Shimura Varieties* (2001), III.1, printed pp.89–91 (PDF pp.97–99), motivates the use of End^0(A) and algebra embeddings into it. The registered source is `harris-taylor-2001-geometry-cohomology-simple-shimura`, SHA256 `5be28164b61d6f6cc28dd9e6b990d669504f3470860b230ebbc97e3da10af56b`. Those pages were read as application motivation; the tensor algebra laws are derived here from the imported construction, not attributed to that passage. No corresponding project errata note was present.

Rational morphisms are not asserted to be actual scheme morphisms. No faithfulness, invertibility of geometric isogenies, or localization property is asserted. The construction is valid over arbitrary bases without a uniform-denominator hypothesis; invertibility results require their own precise hypotheses and proofs.
