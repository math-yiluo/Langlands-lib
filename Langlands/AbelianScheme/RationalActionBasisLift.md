# Lifting a rational action from lifts of integral multiples of a basis

Let `B` be an abelian scheme over the thicker base and let `ρ` be an action
on its actual restriction. Liftability of nonzero integral multiples of
the action of the basis elements puts their rational images in the range
of the rational base-change map. The range is a rational subalgebra, so it
contains the entire action. For a surjective base map,
`rationalBaseChangeEndRangeEquiv` is the algebra isomorphism onto this range
given by faithfulness of rational base change. Its inverse constructs the
unique lifted algebra map with the specified reduction. This argument
derives the unit and multiplication laws by transport through the
isomorphism of algebras.
