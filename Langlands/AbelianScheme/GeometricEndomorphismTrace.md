# Geometric endomorphism trace through the actual prime Tate action

Worker 541's internal construction uses the Library's actual rational endomorphism algebra and actual prime Tate functor. `primeEndomorphismTrace A p` is the intrinsic linear-map trace of `Tate.PrimeAdic.rationalEndAction` on `Tate.PrimeAdic.Rational A (Over.mk (𝟙 (Spec k))) p`, restricted to rational scalars. It is defined without a finite-dimension assumption; finite-dimensionality is a separate unconditional geometric comparison target.

`geometricEndomorphismTrace A` selects a rational-linear lift of the actual 2-adic trace if one exists, and is zero otherwise. Rational scalars embed injectively in the 2-adic field, so such a lift is unique: `geometricEndomorphismTrace_eq` proves this by pointwise injectivity. The definition uses no polarization or positive form. The separate comparison target must prove that the lift exists for every actual abelian variety over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, and that it agrees with every prime Tate trace. Thus the zero fallback is a totalization convention, not a hypothesis or a substitute for rationality.

Milne, *Abelian Varieties*, January 2, 2022 author revision, §12 p.23 defines the geometric trace as the negative penultimate coefficient of the degree-defined characteristic polynomial. Proposition 12.9 on that page identifies this trace with the trace of the action on the rational prime Tate module. Consequently rationality and that proposition identify this constructed rational lift with Milne's geometric trace; the comparison is stated separately, not proved by this file.
