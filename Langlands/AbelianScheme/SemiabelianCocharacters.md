# The cocharacters of a split semiabelian identity component

A cocharacter of a group scheme over a field factors uniquely through the
represented identity component. The multiplicative group is geometrically
connected, and the factorization of group morphisms through the identity
component gives an additive equivalence on cocharacters.

Over a separably closed field, a cocharacter of the semiabelian identity
component has zero composite with its proper abelian quotient. It therefore
factors uniquely through the scheme-theoretic kernel. The presentation's
split-torus isomorphism identifies the kernel cocharacters with integral
coordinate tuples. Tensoring with the rationals gives the corresponding
rational coordinate tuples and computes the dimension.

If the torus rank is zero, the kernel is isomorphic to the trivial group
scheme. The smooth surjective quotient map is then a monomorphism and an
isomorphism. Properness follows from the abelian quotient.

## Provenance

This is the actual cocharacter and zero-rank comparison for the
semiabelian presentation in Bosch, Lütkebohmert and Raynaud,
*Néron Models* (1990), §7.4, printed p. 181. The proof uses the Library's
identity-component lifting, group-scheme kernels, and split-torus lattice
and rank calculations. The paired `ProperCocharacters` module proves
vanishing for the proper quotient by the valuative criterion.
