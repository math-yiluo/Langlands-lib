# Comparison with the geometric endomorphism trace

`Langlands.AbelianScheme.GeometricEndomorphismTraceComparisonStatement` is an unproved proposition about every actual abelian scheme over the spectrum of an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, in the same universe as the scheme. For every prime `p`, it asserts finite-dimensionality of the actual rational prime Tate module at the identity geometric point, dimension `2 * g` whenever the abelian scheme has relative dimension `g`, and equality of the scalar extension of `geometricEndomorphismTrace A a` with the trace of the actual prime Tate action of every `a : End0 A`.

The source is Milne, *Abelian Varieties*, January 2, 2022 author revision, §12 p.23, the definition of the characteristic polynomial and geometric trace and Proposition 12.9. Source ID: `milne-abelian-varieties-1986-author-2022`. The complete registered errata snapshot was read; all its corrections are incorporated in this revision. The source works in greater generality; this target restricts to algebraically closed characteristic-zero fields, so every prime is different from the characteristic.

Milne defines the rational geometric trace as the negative coefficient of degree `2g - 1` in the characteristic polynomial characterized by the degree of `a - r`. Proposition 12.9 identifies that characteristic polynomial with the one on the `2g`-dimensional rational Tate module. Thus the equality in this target identifies the concrete rational trace used in the Rosati form with the source's geometric trace, without adding a characteristic-polynomial interface to the Library. The dimension clause records the source's geometric rank in the Library's relative-dimension terminology.

At `p = 2`, the asserted equality gives a rational-linear lift of the actual 2-adic trace: the defined `geometricEndomorphismTrace` itself. Its uniqueness was proved in the definition module by injectivity of the rational scalar inclusion. Consequently the definition's zero fallback cannot replace the source comparison. Finite-dimensionality is a conclusion here, rather than an implicit premise that could allow the intrinsic linear-map trace to use its infinite-dimensional zero fallback.

The rank clause also provides the usual route to nonzero rational identity in positive dimension: the trace of the identity Tate operator is `2 * g`, which is nonzero for `g > 0`. This is an implication of the proposed comparison target, not a proof supplied by this statement file. The independently proved `id0_ne_zero_of_faithful` helper gives the direct route needed by the existing Harris–Taylor consumer.
