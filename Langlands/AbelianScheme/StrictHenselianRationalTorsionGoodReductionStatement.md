# Good reduction from prime-power torsion rational over a strict henselization

`Langlands.AbelianScheme.StrictHenselianRationalTorsionGoodReductionStatement.{u}`
is the implication (c) ⇒ (b) of the theorem of Bosch, Lütkebohmert and Raynaud on
abelian reduction: if all `ℓ`-power torsion points of an abelian variety become
rational over the fraction field `L` of a strictly Henselian discrete valuation ring
`W` over `V` whose maximal ideal is generated by that of `V` and whose residue
extension is separable, then its Néron model is an abelian scheme. The strict
henselization of `V` is one such `W`; for every such `W`, the separable closure of
`K` in `L` is the fraction field of the strict henselization (see the correspondence
below), so the hypothesis is the source's condition (c). The module states the
proposition and does not prove it.

## Statement

All types live in the universe `u`. The data are:

- a discrete valuation ring `V` with fraction field `K` (`IsFractionRing V K`);
- an abelian scheme `B` over `Spec K`, that is, an abelian variety over `K`;
- a Néron model `N : NeronModel V K B`: a commutative group scheme `N.group` over
  `Spec V` with the Néron model properties and an isomorphism of its generic fibre
  with `B`;
- a strictly Henselian discrete valuation ring `W` (`HenselianLocalRing W` and
  `IsSepClosed (ResidueField W)`) with an algebra structure `V → W` that is a local
  homomorphism;
- a fraction field `L` of `W` with an algebra structure `K → L`;
- a natural number `ℓ` that is prime, with `IsUnit (ℓ : V)`.

The hypotheses are:

1. `(maximalIdeal V).map (algebraMap V W) = maximalIdeal W`: a uniformizer of `V` is a
   uniformizer of `W`;
2. `Algebra.IsSeparable (ResidueField V) (ResidueField W)`: the residue field of `W`
   is separable algebraic over that of `V` (hence, being separably closed, a separable
   algebraic closure of it);
3. the square `V → W → L`, `V → K → L` commutes;
4. for every `ν : ℕ`,
   `HasRationalGeometricTorsion ((baseChange (Spec.algebraMap K L)).obj B) (ℓ ^ ν)`:
   every `ℓ ^ ν`-torsion point of `B_L` with values in `AlgebraicClosure L` is the
   restriction of an `L`-point (the predicate is defined in
   `Langlands.AbelianScheme.RaynaudSemiabelianCriterionStatement`).

The conclusion is `IsProper N.group.X.hom ∧ GeometricallyConnected N.group.X.hom`:
the Néron model is proper with geometrically connected fibres. Since a Néron model is
smooth, `N` is then an abelian scheme (`Langlands.AbelianScheme.NeronModel.toAbelianScheme`).

## Source

Bosch, Lütkebohmert and Raynaud, *Néron Models* (1990), §7.4, Theorem 5 and its proof,
printed pp. 183–184 (PDF pp. 190–191), registered source
`bosch-lutkebohmert-raynaud-1990-neron-models`.

The source fixes a separable algebraic closure `K_s` of `K`, a strict henselization
`R^sh` of `R = V` inside the localization `R_s` of the integral closure of `R` in `K_s`,
with fraction field `K^sh`, and the inertia group `I = Gal(K_s / K^sh)`. For an abelian
variety `A_K` with Néron model `A` and a prime `ℓ ≠ char k`, Theorem 5 proves the
equivalence of (b) `A` is an abelian scheme over `R`, and (c) for each `ν ≥ 0` the
inertia group acts trivially on the `ℓ^ν`-torsion `{}_{ℓ^ν}A_K(K_s)`, that is, the
canonical map `{}_{ℓ^ν}A_K(K^sh) → {}_{ℓ^ν}A_K(K_s)` is bijective: all `ℓ^ν`-torsion
points are rational over `K^sh`. The proof of (c) ⇒ (a) uses the maps
`{}_{ℓ^ν}A(K_s) ← {}_{ℓ^ν}A(K^sh) ≅ {}_{ℓ^ν}A(R^sh) → {}_{ℓ^ν}A(k_s)` and the structure of
commutative algebraic groups over a field to show that the identity component of the
special fibre is an abelian variety; (a) ⇒ (b) is the first paragraph of the proof.
The text after the proof, printed p. 184, records that over a strictly Henselian base
`A_K` has abelian reduction if and only if all `ℓ^ν`-torsion points are rational.

The correspondence with the Lean statement:

- `IsUnit (ℓ : V)` says that `ℓ` is not divisible by the residue characteristic, so
  `ℓ ≠ char k`.
- The pair `(W, L)` plays the role of `(R^sh, K^sh)`. `W` is strictly Henselian, a
  uniformizer of `V` is a uniformizer of `W`, and the residue field of `W` is a separable
  algebraic closure of that of `V`, so the strict henselization `R^sh` maps locally to
  `W` (universal property of the strict henselization) and `K^sh` embeds in `L` over
  `K`. A finite separable extension of `K` inside `L` has ramification index one and
  separable residue extension for the valuation induced by `W` (the value group of `W`
  is that of `V`, and the residue field of `W` is separable over that of `V`), so it is
  unramified and lies in `K^sh`. Hence the separable closure of `K` in `L` is `K^sh`.
- The `ℓ^ν`-torsion of `B` is finite étale over `K` (`ℓ` is invertible), so its points
  with values in an algebraic closure are separable over `K`. Hypothesis 4 therefore
  says that all `ℓ^ν`-torsion points of `B` with values in `K_s` are rational over the
  separable closure of `K` in `L`, which is `K^sh`: this is condition (c).
- The conclusion is condition (b) for `A = N.group`, the smoothness being part of the
  Néron model properties.

For the strict henselization itself, Stacks Lemma 10.155.2 (Tag 04GP) gives strict
Henselianity, the maximal ideal formula, and the separable residue field in
hypotheses 1 and 2. The Library statement
`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.UnramifiedStrictHenselianDvrExtensionStatement` (module
`Langlands/CommutativeAlgebra/UnramifiedStrictHenselianDvrExtensionStatement.lean`)
asserts that every discrete valuation ring has an extension `W` with the properties
required here; it does not identify `W` with a strict henselization.
