# Twists of an extension across a finite cyclic quotient

`HasScalarEndomorphisms` means every linear intertwining endomorphism of an actual finite-dimensional representation is a scalar on its carrier. In the application the restriction is absolutely irreducible, so it has this property by Schur's lemma.

`CyclicExtensionTwistClassificationStatement` takes a finite-index normal subgroup `N` of a group `G` with cyclic quotient, a faithful character `η : G/N → Kˣ` into the coefficient field, and a positive-rank actual representation `A` of `G` whose restriction to `N` has scalar endomorphisms. For any actual representation `B` of `G`, their restrictions to `N` are isomorphic exactly when there is a unique exponent `0 ≤ i < [G:N]` for which `B` is isomorphic to `A` twisted by the inflated `η^i`. The equivalences are on the given carriers. There is no requirement that the carriers have the same universe.

Given an equivalence on `N`, transport the action of `B` to the carrier of `A` through it. For `g ∈ G`, normality makes the ratio of the transported action of `g` to that of `A` commute with `N`. The scalar is unique because the carrier has positive rank, and it is a unit because the actions of `g` are invertible. Multiplicativity gives a character, trivial on `N`. Its value at a generator of the quotient is a root of unity of order dividing `[G:N]`. The powers of the faithful character exhaust those roots in the coefficient field. Changing the equivalence on `N` conjugates by a scalar, so the resulting character is independent of this choice. The twists all restrict to the given action on `N`.

This is the algebraic extension step used in Harris 1998, Lemma 1.6, pp. 188–189, companion TeX lines 724–744 (`harris-1998-local-langlands`). The coefficient field in the application contains the values of the `λ`-adic norm-kernel character, and the subgroup arises from restriction to the actual cyclic step field. The Lean statement is a derived version on actual linear representations and uses the scalar-endomorphism consequence of absolute irreducibility.
