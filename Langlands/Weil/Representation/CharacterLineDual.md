# Contragredients of actual character lines

This internal construction identifies the full algebraic dual of the
universe-lifted complex character line with the line of the inverse
character. The underlying linear equivalence first pulls a functional
back along the lift of the complex line, evaluates it at one, and lifts
the resulting complex scalar. Linear duality evaluates the original
action at the inverse group element, so linearity of the functional
gives the inverse-character action.

The existing exact virtual quotient identifies genuinely equivalent
representations. Applying this to the constructed equivalence proves
`continuousDual_characterClass` for each continuous character of any
topological group and in every representation-carrier universe. The
existing character-twist identity then identifies a twist of that dual
class with the class of the product of the twisting character and the
inverse original character. No representation decomposition, generation
theorem, or local-constant functional equation is used.
