# Absolute irreducibility under scalar extension

An actual finite-dimensional monoid action is absolutely irreducible when it becomes irreducible over the algebraic closure of its coefficient field. The module proves that irreducibility after any algebraically closed coefficient extension implies irreducibility after every coefficient extension. This transfers local irreducibility in Task 6846 to the shared coefficient field used to compare Euler polynomials.

Burnside's theorem gives full endomorphism span in the first extension. Base change of the space of endomorphisms identifies the extended span with the span of the extended representation operators. Faithful flatness reflects this fullness to the original field, and base change gives fullness again over the second extension. Rank preservation ensures that the carrier is nonzero. Since every rank-one endomorphism lies in the span, a stable subspace containing a nonzero vector also contains every vector. This yields irreducibility over the second field.
