# Integer degree of geometric Frobenius

For every `PadicField F`, `localDegreeInt_localGeometricFrobenius` states that the integer degree of its actual local geometric Frobenius is one. The proof unfolds the integer degree and uses `localGeometricFrobenius_degree`.

This lemma is moved unchanged from [HenniartCompositeFactorWeil](../../Global/Factors/HenniartCompositeFactorWeil.md), keeping its name and field scope, so local triangularization can reuse it directly.
