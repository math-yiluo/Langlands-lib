# Polynomial centralizers and coefficient sections of regular division units

For a regular division unit, evaluation at the distinguished root of its reduced
characteristic polynomial identifies the field adjoining that root with the
commuting algebra. At unit level this gives the commutativity of the centralizer
and transports the largest compact open subgroup of the field units. Applying
Skolem–Noether to two such evaluations gives conjugacy of units with the same
regular reduced characteristic polynomial.

To construct a local section of the reduced coefficients, choose a corresponding
regular elliptic matrix. The ordinary coefficient slice varies that matrix
within its polynomial centralizer. Evaluate the same elements of the common
polynomial field in the division algebra. On the open irreducible separable locus,
the prescribed monic polynomial annihilates the resulting value and has the same
degree as its reduced characteristic polynomial, which identifies the two.
Regular commuting elements have the same centralizer because both centralizers
are commutative.
