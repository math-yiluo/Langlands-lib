# Quotient onto the identity piece

Corollary 1.3 of A. V. Zelevinsky, *Induced representations of reductive
p-adic groups II* (1980), describes the quotient of the normalized
induction-Jacquet composite associated with the identity double coset.
For any two positive-total-rank block shapes and their supplied standard
intersection refinements at the identity, the proposition gives a natural
transformation to the actual normalized geometric piece. Each component is
a surjective intertwiner. The statement allows every nonarchimedean local
field and uses the supplied topology and modulus normalizations.
