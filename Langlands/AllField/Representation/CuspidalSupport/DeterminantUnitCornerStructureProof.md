## Contents

The module docstring of the port:

**Proof of the sandwich structure of compact-open Hecke corners of `G°`**

Bernstein--Zelevinsky (1976), Lemma 4.9, page 39, for the determinant-unit subgroup `G°`
of a product Levi. The level is the blockwise principal congruence subgroup `N` of a large
positive level inside `K`. Block-integral elements normalize `N`; dominant block-torus
elements satisfy `m₁ N m₂ ⊆ N m₁ m₂ N`; and the blockwise Cartan decomposition writes every
element of `G°` as `γ m γ'` with `γ, γ'` block-integral and `m` in the monoid generated by
finitely many dominant zero-sum block-torus elements. The generic Hecke sandwich theorem
then gives the corner structure.
