# Nonzero quasicuspidal Jacquet descent in an actual matrix Levi

`Langlands.AllField.CuspidalSupport.LocalFieldMinimalRefinedJacquetStatement`
applies to every standard matrix Levi over a nonarchimedean local field
with specified `LocalFieldData`, including the empty Levi in rank zero.
Every nonzero smooth complex representation on the product Levi has a
refinement with nonzero normalized Jacquet module. Under the actual
flattened coordinate equivalence the resulting representation is
quasicuspidal. Its Jacquet module along every proper standard refinement
of the flattened shape is zero.

This is minimal nonzero Jacquet descent applied inside the standard
product Levi. A nonzero restriction of the resulting representation
would give a nonzero restriction at a strictly finer refinement by
Jacquet transitivity. The use of a whole refinement provides the initial
nonzero module.
