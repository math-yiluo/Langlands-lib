# The dual standard polynomial from the forward and epsilon products

`StandardInductionDualPolynomialStatement` concerns the actual normalized
block induction and the standard pair with the trivial rank-one
representation. The additive measure is self-dual. The blocks have
Whittaker type and supplied standard epsilon, forward Euler and
inverse-transpose dual Euler certificates. The induced standard epsilon
and forward polynomial are supplied with the products of the block
coefficients, exponents and forward polynomials, respectively.

The proof chooses an actual dual polynomial for the induction using
Whittaker type. Gamma multiplicativity identifies the rational gamma
function with the product of the block expressions. The common epsilon
monomial and forward polynomial are nonzero and cancel. Reciprocal
substitution in the rational function field and the embedding of
polynomials into that field are injective. The actual dual polynomial
therefore equals the product of the supplied block dual polynomials,
at the negated additive character and the same underlying measure.

This is a derived consequence of Jacquet–Piatetski-Shapiro–Shalika
(1983), Theorem (3.1)(i), printed pp. 404–405, with the normalization
and forward product specified as premises.
