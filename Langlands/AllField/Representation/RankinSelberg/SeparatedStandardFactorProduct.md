# Standard local factors of a separated normalized product

`separatedStandardFactorProduct` works over a supplied nonarchimedean local field. The blocks have full Whittaker type, and one supplies certified standard epsilon data and the original and inverse-transpose dual L-polynomials, all tested against the trivial character of `GL₁`. The dual certificates use the inverse additive character and the same additive Haar measure. Assume that the product of the original block L-polynomials has no common nonzero root with the reflected dual product. The theorem certifies the product of the block L-polynomials and the product epsilon monomial for the actual normalized product induction. The monomial's coefficient is the product of coefficients and its exponent is the sum of exponents.

The proof applies the Library's all-field gamma multiplicativity and L-polynomial divisibility, from Jacquet–Piatetski-Shapiro–Shalika, *Rankin-Selberg Convolutions* (1983), Theorem (3.1)(i)–(ii), printed pp. 404–405. The symmetric relation reads the larger rank first; the proof explicitly exchanges the inputs at equal rank, using the equal-rank exchange theorems. Uniqueness identifies the factors of the blocks with the supplied ones. Full Whittaker type gives the induction's epsilon certificate and gives a dual L-polynomial at the negated character. Converting Laurent divisibility into normalized polynomial divisibility, the proof uses `eq_of_separated_rationalGammaExpression` to compare the induction's actual factors with the block products. It does not assume irreducibility of the induction or use any formula for the dual L-polynomial of the induction.

The separation hypothesis and the combined epsilon and L consequences are this project's derived interface, not the wording of Theorem (3.1).
