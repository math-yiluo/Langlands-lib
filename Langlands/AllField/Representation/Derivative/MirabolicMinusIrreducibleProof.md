## Contents

The module docstring of the port:

**Proof that unnormalized `Φ⁻` sends irreducibles to irreducibles or zero**

A nonzero class `q x` in a subrepresentation `σ` of `Φ⁻ ξ` is averaged against
`θ_ψ⁻¹` over a finite quotient of radical lattices. The average `x'` has
`q (ξ (levi g) x') ∈ σ` for every `g`, so the subrepresentation of `ξ` generated by
`x'` maps into `σ`. See the paired Markdown for the route.
