# Kummer characters with values in the base field

For a field `F`, a positive integer `n`, and a unit `b : Fˣ`, assume that
`(primitiveRoots n F).Nonempty`. If `β` in an extension field `E` satisfies
`β ^ n = algebraMap F E (b : F)`, then `kummerCharacter` is a homomorphism
`Gal(E/F) →* rootsOfUnity n F`. The theorem `algebraMap_kummerCharacter`
identifies the image of its value at `σ` in `E` with `σ β / β`.

The quotient has `n`-th power one because `σ` fixes the nonzero value `β ^ n`.
The roots-of-unity equivalence for `F → E` descends it to `F`. Each descended
value is fixed by every base-field automorphism, so composition of
automorphisms multiplies their values. Two roots of `b` differ by the image of a
base-field root of unity, which cancels from the quotient in
`kummerCharacter_eq`. In a tower `F ⊆ E ⊆ L` with `E/F` normal,
`kummerCharacter_comp_restrictNormalHom` gives the equality of homomorphisms
obtained by restriction to `E` and by evaluation at the image of `β` in `L`.
Thus a single automorphism of a common extension can be evaluated on several
radicals in it.

`KummerNorm.radicalField F n (b : F)` is the actual intermediate field of
`AlgebraicClosure F` generated by the roots of `X ^ n - C (b : F)`. Its
splitting-field and finite-dimensional instances are in
`Langlands.ClassFieldTheory.Local.KummerRadicalFields`. In a splitting field,
`kummerCharacter_injective` uses equality of characters to obtain equality of
automorphisms on each root, which determines their values on the generated
algebra. To prove `isAbelianGalois_radicalField`, choose a root `β` in the
splitting field and write the binomial there as the product of the linear
factors with roots `ζ ^ i * β`, for `0 ≤ i < n` and `ζ` primitive in `F`.
These roots are distinct by primitivity and nonvanishing of `β`, giving
separability. The faithful character into the commutative group of roots of
unity gives commutativity of the Galois group.

The hypotheses apply in every characteristic where a primitive root of order
`n` exists. They include `n = 1`, proper-degree radical fields, and `n`-th
powers in the base field.

## Source

John Milnor, *Introduction to Algebraic K-Theory* (1971), §15, Remark 15.10,
printed p. 152 (PDF p. 154), describes the action on a chosen radical by the
quotient of the conjugate root and the root. The construction here uses that
action for a single unit and proves its restriction property for towers.
