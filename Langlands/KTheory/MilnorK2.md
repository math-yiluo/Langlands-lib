# Steinberg symbols and symbolic Milnor K₂

The module `Langlands.KTheory.MilnorK2` defines the symbolic group in the namespace
`Langlands.KTheory`.

## Steinberg symbols

`SteinbergSymbol F A` is a function `Fˣ → Fˣ → A` that is multiplicative in each
argument and has value `1` on units `a, b` whose values in `F` satisfy `a + b = 1`.
Because `a` and `b` are units, this condition describes exactly the pairs
`(a, 1 − a)` for `a ≠ 0, 1`. The lemma `SteinbergSymbol.steinberg_mk0` applies the
relation with the second unit built explicitly from `1 − a`.
The field `F` and the commutative coefficient group `A` have independent universe
parameters. There is no size or characteristic hypothesis on `F`.

## The presentation

`MilnorK2.UnitTensor F` is the tensor product over `ℤ` of `Additive Fˣ` with itself.
Bilinearity gives the two multiplicativity relations when the group laws are
written multiplicatively. `MilnorK2.steinbergRelations F` is the integer span of
the pure tensors `a ⊗ b` with `a + b = 1` in `F`. The integer span is the additive
subgroup generated by those tensors, since integer scalar multiplication is
repeated addition with signs. Quotienting by it imposes the Steinberg relation
and its consequences in the tensor product.

`MilnorK2 F` is this quotient with Mathlib's `Multiplicative` tag. Multiplication
is addition in the underlying quotient, and the quotient zero is the identity.
`MilnorK2.ofTensor F` is the surjective quotient homomorphism in multiplicative
notation. `MilnorK2.symbol F` maps `a, b` to the class of the pure tensor `a ⊗ b`,
as recorded by `MilnorK2.symbol_apply`. The tensor product's laws give the
symbol's multiplicativity, and its values on the Steinberg pairs are trivial
because those tensors lie in the relation span.

## The induced homomorphism

For a Steinberg symbol `s`, `MilnorK2.lift s` is a homomorphism `MilnorK2 F →* A`.
The construction writes the group laws additively and turns the two
multiplicativity laws into `ℤ`-bilinearity. `TensorProduct.lift` then gives a
linear map to `Additive A`. Its kernel contains the relation span because `s`
sends every Steinberg pair to `1`. The map descends by `Submodule.liftQ` and is
read multiplicatively. `MilnorK2.lift_symbol` proves that the descended map sends
the class of `a ⊗ b` to `s a b`.
