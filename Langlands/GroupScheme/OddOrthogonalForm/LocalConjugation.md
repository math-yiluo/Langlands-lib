# Isometric conjugators on a finite splitting cover

A natural local automorphism of the full split odd norm-one group along
a field-extension cover is implemented, on every relative test scheme,
by one actual isometry matrix over the covering field. The matrix has
determinant one and preserves the complete quadratic form. Its actual
covering-field point is the unique conjugating point given by the full
inner-automorphism equivalence.

The local automorphism is first read as an actual automorphism over the
covering field through the full odd model base-change isomorphism.
The inverse inner-automorphism equivalence supplies the point. Its
original-field rational matrix satisfies the exact defining equations
by the actual affine-point equations. Naturality of the matrix chart
identifies its pullback with coefficient extension. Comparing projected
cover points then gives the same conjugation formula on every relative
test scheme, including nonaffine and nonreduced tests.

The supporting `GaloisStage` module supplies finite Galois local
isomorphisms for a form split over the separable closure. These are
internal adaptations of the published finite-stage and even orthogonal
local-isomorphism transport arguments, using the full odd model and its
unique isometric conjugators. No new external translation or field
characteristic restriction is used.
