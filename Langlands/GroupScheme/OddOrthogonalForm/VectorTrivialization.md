# A matrix basis trivialising the exact odd cocycle

For a local isomorphism to the full split odd norm-one group, in rank at
least two, the inverse conjugator cocycle consists of actual quadratic
isometries with determinant one. These equations follow by taking the
inverse of the actual covering-field group point.

If the covering-field extension is finite, matrix Hilbert 90 gives an
invertible matrix P satisfying c(σ) σ(P) = P for every automorphism of
the extension. Equivalently, the original conjugator is σ(P) P⁻¹.
The construction applies in every characteristic. It does not yet
construct a quadratic form over the original field.

This is an internal application of the existing semilinear vector-space
and matrix Hilbert-90 construction to the exact odd cocycle, with no new
external translation.
