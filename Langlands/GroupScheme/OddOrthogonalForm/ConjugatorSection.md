# Unique conjugating sections on a splitting cover

The chosen covering-field conjugator point gives an actual section of
the original split odd group over the covering test. Pulling that section
to any relative test has the coefficient extension of the same conjugator
matrix. The local automorphism is consequently conjugation by this
pulled-back section as an equality of actual points.

Sections inducing the same conjugation on every relative test are equal.
Their quotient is central on every refinement, and the full odd model's
scheme-theoretic centre is trivial. This uniqueness applies to arbitrary
cover tests, including nonreduced ones.

The construction uses the explicit field model isomorphism and the
base-change point equivalence. Matrix naturality and the all-test local
conjugation formula give the actual point equation. These are internal
comparisons for rank at least two, over every field and characteristic,
with no new external translation.
