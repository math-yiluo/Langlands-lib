# The actual group of the descended odd quadratic form

The Hilbert-90 basis `P` induces on each relative test over the finite
Galois cover a multiplicative equivalence from the norm-one isometries of
the descended form to the full split odd model. Its matrix formula is
`A ↦ P A P⁻¹`, using the section's coefficient map. Congruence modulo
alternating matrices identifies the complete quadratic equations, and
conjugation preserves the determinant. The resulting equivalences
commute with pullback of arbitrary tests.

The change of `P` along a Galois translate is left multiplication by the
same conjugator used in the twist of the given local isomorphism. The
comparison of the two local isomorphisms is therefore invariant under
every Galois automorphism. Finite Galois descent gives an actual
ground-field isomorphism to the reduced-norm-one kernel of the descended
form. Everything is computed on the full point functors, including
nonreduced and nonaffine tests.

This construction is internal. It applies the scalar matrix coordinates
and the existing descent comparison to the exact odd cocycle and its
quadratic class, in every characteristic and rank at least two.
