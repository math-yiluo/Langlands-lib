# Finite Galois stages for forms of the full split odd group

An actual affine field group that becomes isomorphic over the separable
closure to the full split odd norm-one group has natural local
isomorphisms to that split model along a finite Galois extension.
The split model's field-base-change isomorphism is recorded explicitly.

The supplied separable-closure isomorphism is first compared with the
base change of the ground-field split model. The published finite-stage
isomorphism theorem gives local isomorphisms at a finite separable
stage. Taking its normal closure inside the separable closure and
refining the cover produces a finite Galois stage.

This internally adapts the general finite-stage argument used in the
published even orthogonal construction; the actual full odd model and
its own base-change isomorphism are used here. No connected-component
substitution or new external translation is asserted.
