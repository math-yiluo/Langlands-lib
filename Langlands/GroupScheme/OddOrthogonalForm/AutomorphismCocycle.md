# Exact Galois cocycles for odd orthogonal forms

For a local isomorphism from a group to the full split odd norm-one group,
compare its evaluations at a cover section and at that section composed
with a field automorphism. The resulting natural local automorphism has
an actual conjugating section when the rank is at least two.

The identity field automorphism has identity section. Composition gives
an exact twisted product law for these sections: the section for a
product is the pullback of the second section times the first section.
Equality follows from equality of conjugation on every relative test and
triviality of the full scheme-theoretic centre.

The coefficient matrices obey the same exact product law. Inverting them
gives the usual left cocycle: `c(στ) = c(σ) σ(c(τ))`, with `c(1) = 1`.
The matrices are those of actual norm-one quadratic isometries, rather than
only scalar classes of matrices. The conversion uses the injective
coefficient embedding into the cover scheme's global sections.

This is an internal construction based on actual local isomorphisms and
the odd conjugating-section comparison. The twist construction follows
the existing even-form comparison pattern. It works over every field
and characteristic and does not assert that the extension is Galois or
finite unless those hypotheses are separately supplied.
