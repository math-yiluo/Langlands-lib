# Finite Galois refinement for matching cocharacters

Let `L/k` be a finite field extension with a specified `k`-embedding into a
normal separable algebraic extension `Ω/k`. Suppose a local isomorphism over
`L` matches two `k`-cocharacters after extension to `Ω` and conjugation by a
point of the target. If the target group is affine of finite type, the
coordinates of this point descend to a finite subextension of `Ω/k`. The
normal closure in `Ω` of its compositum with the image of `L` gives a finite
Galois extension of `k` containing that image. Faithful flatness on tests
descends the matching equation to this extension. Applying the inverse local
isomorphism to the target conjugator gives the source cochain that corrects
the trivialization.
