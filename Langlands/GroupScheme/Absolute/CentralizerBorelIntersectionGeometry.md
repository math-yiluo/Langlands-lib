# Geometry of the centralizer intersection with a Borel

Author: Worker 1479.

This internal construction assembles published all-test centralizer restriction, dynamic smoothness, inherited solvability, and Borel presentation transport. It uses the actual pullback `subgroupIntersection j b`, with first projection to the chosen ambient centralizer `M` and second projection to the prescribed Borel `B`. It works over an arbitrary field and universe and includes trivial and noninjective cocharacters. The conclusions do not assume that the ambient centralizer is connected or smooth.

For a closed subgroup `b : B ⟶ G` with affine source, an arbitrary cocharacter whose full range lies in `b`, and any closed representative `j : M ⟶ G` of its dynamic centralizer, `Dynamic.centralizerIntersectionIso` constructs an isomorphism from the actual pullback to the chosen affine centralizer of the restricted cocharacter in `B`. The construction uses `Dynamic.isRepresentedBy_intersectionSnd`, rewrites the proved restriction equation, and applies uniqueness of subgroup representatives. Its `hom_comp` theorem identifies the composite with the canonical internal-centralizer inclusion as the actual second projection to `B`.

If `G` is affine and `b` is Borel, `IsBorelSubgroup.centralizer_intersection_properties` proves that the first projection is closed and that the intersection is smooth, affine, and solvable. Closedness of the first projection is base change of `b`. The second projection is closed by base change of `j`; affineness follows by composing that closed immersion with the affine structure morphism of `B`. Smoothness follows from `dynamicSmooth` applied to the restricted cocharacter and the actual representative supplied by centralizer restriction. Solvability follows from the published `IsSolvableGroupScheme.of_mono` applied to the second projection. The final equation is the published all-test range identity for the pullback; it identifies the composite of the first projection with `j` as exactly the intersection of the two ambient subgroup functors.

Under the explicit additional premise that this actual intersection is connected, `IsBorelSubgroup.centralizer_intersection_borel_iff` equates Borel maximality of its first projection with the exact desired existence of a Borel of `M` contained in `B`. One implication takes the intersection itself. Conversely, an alleged contained Borel factors through `B` by `factorHom`, hence through the actual pullback by its universal property. The maximality of that Borel, together with the proved smoothness and solvability and the stated connectedness premise, makes the factor an isomorphism. Borel presentation transport then proves the claim about the first projection.

These are derived constructions and reductions, not a proof of `CentralizerBorelIntersectionStatement`. No connectedness theorem for the intersection and no Borel maximality or proper-quotient theorem is asserted. All containment and representative identities use every test scheme, including nonreduced and non-affine tests. No published statement or imported pair is changed.
