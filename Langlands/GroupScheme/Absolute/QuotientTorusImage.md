# Images of maximal tori in solvable groups

`QuotientTorusImage.IsMaximalTorusImage q t s` is the natural supplied-image relation: both embeddings are maximal tori, and some surjective homomorphism between their sources gives the commuting square with `q`. The definition imposes no ambient surjectivity or solvability. `isMaximalTorusImage_solvable` constructs this relation under the hypotheses below, using the original maximality theorem.

`Langlands.GroupScheme.QuotientTorusImage.isMaximalTorus_image_solvable` is an internal lemma for Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017), Proposition 17.20, pp. 357–358. It proves the maximal-torus image assertion for smooth connected solvable affine groups over an algebraically closed field. The image is supplied as a smooth closed subgroup with a surjective factor map. The homomorphism of ambient groups need only be surjective.

The proof uses the published rational unipotent-times-torus factorization of the source and the published split-torus quotient of the target. The image is a split torus because it is smoothly and surjectively dominated by the source torus. Its map to the target's split-torus quotient is monic, since a torus and a unipotent subgroup have trivial intersection on every test scheme. It is surjective on rational points: lift a quotient point to the source, factor it as a unipotent point times a torus point, and use triviality of homomorphisms from unipotent groups to split tori. The published criterion for monomorphisms of split tori then makes this map an isomorphism. Every larger torus maps monomorphically and with a section to the same quotient, so it has the same image and the proposed enlargement is an isomorphism.

This argument makes explicit the solvable-group step used in the source proof of Proposition 17.20 and does not assume that proposition. The official 2017 errata snapshot in the project literature directory was read; it has no correction to Proposition 17.20 or the solvable structure theorem 16.33 used by the published inputs.
