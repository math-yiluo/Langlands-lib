# Conditional proof of the envelope step

Construction by Worker 1393; compilation and documentation checked by Worker 1461.

This module proves the envelope step
`Langlands.GroupScheme.UnipotentAllBorelsEnvelopeStatement.{u}` *conditionally*. The
final theorem assumes three published structural propositions and does not prove them:

- `PointedImageGenerationStatement.{u}` (generation of a smooth connected closed subgroup
  by a pointed geometrically integral image);
- `SolvableUnipotentPartStatement.{u}` (the unipotent part of a smooth connected affine
  solvable group over an algebraically closed field, with its maximality property);
- `UnipotentClosedSubgroupStatement.{u}` (closed subgroups of unipotent groups are
  unipotent).

It is therefore not an unconditional proof of the envelope step or of
`ChevalleyUnipotentEnvelopeStatement.{u}`.

## Declarations

`exists_factor_of_algClosed_points`: let `k` be algebraically closed, `X` a reduced
scheme locally of finite type over `Spec k`, `φ : X ⟶ G.X` a morphism over `Spec k` and
`j : K ⟶ G` a closed subgroup. If every `k`-point `x` of `X` has `x ≫ φ` in the range of
`j`, then `φ` factors through `j`. Proof: the pullback of the closed immersion `j` along
`φ` is a closed immersion into `X`. Each closed point of `X` is the image of a `k`-point,
so it lies in that closed range. Closed points are dense in the Jacobson space `X`, so the
range is everything. A surjective closed immersion into a reduced scheme is an
isomorphism, and its inverse gives the factorization.

`leftTranslation G X` is the pointwise action of `G` on `G.X ⊗ X` by left multiplication
on the first factor; `leftTranslation_act` records its formula on points.

`conjugationMap_isEquivariant`: for any homomorphism `i : U ⟶ G`, the existing map
`SubgroupFunctor.conjugationMap i (𝟙 G)`, `(g, u) ↦ g i(u) g⁻¹`, intertwines
`leftTranslation G U.X` with the conjugation action of `G` on itself. This is a direct
calculation on points.

`conjugationMap_points_mem`: let `b₀ : B₀ ⟶ G` be a Borel subgroup and `h : Bu ⟶ B₀`
contain the range of every closed unipotent subgroup of `B₀`. Let `i : U ⟶ G` be a
closed unipotent subgroup whose range lies in that of every Borel subgroup. Then every
`k`-point `(a, u)` of `G.X ⊗ U.X` is sent by the conjugation map into the range of
`h ≫ b₀`. Proof: conjugating `b₀` by the inner automorphism of `a⁻¹` gives a Borel
subgroup (`IsBorelSubgroup.comp_iso`). It contains `U`, so the conjugate of `U` by `a`
lies in `B₀`: it factors as a closed subgroup `w : U ⟶ B₀`. Since `U` is unipotent, the
range of `w` lies in that of `h`. The point `a i(u) a⁻¹` is therefore in
the range of `h ≫ b₀`. This lemma does not use algebraic closedness.

`unipotentAllBorelsEnvelope_of_statements (hGen hSolv hSub)` concludes
`UnipotentAllBorelsEnvelopeStatement.{u}`. The proof is the following.

1. `V := G.X ⊗ U.X` is affine, locally of finite type and geometrically integral over
   `k`, by the published `smoothConnectedAffineGeometry`. With `f` the conjugation map,
   the point `(1, 1)` maps to `1`. `hGen` gives a generated image `D` of `f` with smooth,
   geometrically connected group `N := D.group`; its inclusion `D.inclusion` is closed.
2. Normality: `f` is equivariant for left translation and conjugation. The published
   `AutomorphicAction.closedImageEnvelope_isStable` makes the closed envelope of `f`, which
   `D.inclusion` represents, stable under conjugation by every point of `G`. That is the
   statement `SubgroupFunctor.IsNormal` of the range of `D.inclusion`.
3. Containment: each point `u` of `U` is `f(1, u)`, which lies in the closed envelope
   (`closedImageEnvelope_containsImage`).
4. Unipotence: `BorelExistence.exists_isBorelSubgroup` gives a Borel subgroup `B₀`, and
   `hSolv` gives its unipotent part `h : Bu ⟶ B₀`. By `conjugationMap_points_mem` and
   `exists_factor_of_algClosed_points` (applied to the reduced scheme `V`), `f` factors
   through the closed subgroup `h ≫ b₀`. Minimality of the generated image
   (`GeneratedImage.minimalHom`) then makes `N` a closed subgroup of `Bu`, so `hSub` shows
   that `N` is unipotent.
5. Connectedness of `N` follows from geometric connectedness over `Spec k`.

The resulting closed subgroup and its normality and containment statements are
statements of subgroup functors on every test scheme. Rational points are used
only to establish the factorization of the reduced source `V`; this does not
restrict the final containment to reduced or rational test schemes.

## Provenance

The route is internal and derived. It is not the source's route. In J. S. Milne,
*Algebraic Groups* (2017), Section 17.h (printed p.375), the envelope is the unipotent
radical. Its existence there uses Remark 17.31 (printed p.361) and Theorem 16.33(b)
(printed pp.335–336). This module instead uses the subgroup generated by all conjugates
of `U`. Its unipotence uses Theorem 16.33 and Corollary 14.7 (printed p.282), through the
two published statements named above, and the existence of a Borel subgroup. The
`k`-point factorization lemma is the standard density of closed points in a Jacobson
scheme; it is proved here, not assumed.
