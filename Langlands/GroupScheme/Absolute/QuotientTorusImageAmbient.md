# Maximal-torus images from Borel images

`QuotientTorusImage.BorelImagesExist q` is defined for a homomorphism `q : G ⟶ Q` of group schemes over any field `k : Type u`. It requires that every source Borel `b : B ⟶ G` admit a target Borel `c : C ⟶ Q` and a homomorphism `p : B ⟶ C` surjective on underlying scheme spaces, with `b ≫ q = p ≫ c`. The predicate itself imposes no algebraic-closedness, smoothness, connectedness or surjectivity assumption on the ambient groups or `q`.

`QuotientTorusImage.isMaximalTorus_image_of_borel_images` is an internal reduction of the maximal-torus part of Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017), Proposition 17.20, pp.357–358. Its field is algebraically closed. Its ambient groups `G` and `Q` are smooth affine group schemes over `k`, and `Q` is connected; `G` may be disconnected. Let `q : G ⟶ Q` be any homomorphism satisfying `BorelImagesExist q`. Let `T` and `S` be commutative group schemes, `t : T.toGrp ⟶ G` a maximal torus, and `s : S.toGrp ⟶ Q` a closed subgroup with `S` smooth over `k`. Given a homomorphism `f : T.toGrp ⟶ S.toGrp` surjective on underlying scheme spaces and satisfying `t ≫ q = f ≫ s`, the theorem proves that `s` is a maximal torus of `Q`.

Place the source torus in a Borel subgroup. Schematic dominance of the surjective map onto the smooth image torus shows that this torus factors through the Borel image on every scheme test. The solvable-group image lemma proves maximality there. Conjugacy of maximal tori in that Borel image and the existence of an ambient maximal torus in every Borel then prove ambient maximality. This is a derived reduction; it retains the Borel-image premise and does not claim an unconditional quotient theorem.
