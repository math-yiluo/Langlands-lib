# Lifting semisimple centralizers through normal unipotent quotients

If a rational point centralizes the image of the semisimple point in the
quotient, its lift has commutator in the kernel. For a commutative unipotent
kernel the commutator covering writes that commutator as a commutator in
the kernel times a fixed point. Conjugation by the adjusted lift sends the
semisimple point to its product with a commuting unipotent factor. Jordan
uniqueness makes the factor the identity, so the adjusted lift centralizes
the point. This gives surjectivity on rational points of the homomorphism
between represented centralizers.

The full scheme kernel of that homomorphism is the intersection of the
centralizer and the original quotient kernel. The subgroup intersection
and kernel comparisons identify it with the fixed subgroup of semisimple
conjugation in the original kernel, which is connected.
