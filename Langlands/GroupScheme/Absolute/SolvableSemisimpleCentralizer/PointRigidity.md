# Semisimple points and commuting unipotent factors

The matrix test for semisimplicity is preserved by group homomorphisms and
inner conjugation. If a commuting product of a semisimple point and a point
from a unipotent group is semisimple, its unipotent factor is the identity.
The proof uses a faithful matrix representation and uniqueness of additive
Jordan decomposition on its vector space. This gives the point cancellation
used in Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017), Lemma 16.39 and Theorem 16.40,
printed pp. 338–339. The retained source is `milne-2017-algebraic-groups`,
SHA-256 `899b0f44f3c3ccfbaf5ed1cf9c180be7a8016b1c0e9709ca0c206304e5bd1cd0`.
