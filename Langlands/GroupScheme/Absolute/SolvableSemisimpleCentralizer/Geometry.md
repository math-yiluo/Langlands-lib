# Rational points and connected kernels of affine group homomorphisms

For affine schemes of finite type over an algebraically closed field,
Chevalley's theorem and the Jacobson property turn surjectivity on rational
points into surjectivity on underlying spaces. A homomorphism of affine
algebraic groups has closed image by the fppf image factorization. When the
homomorphism is surjective on rational points, the image inclusion is a
homeomorphism onto the target. The fppf projection is open and has
geometrically connected fibers if its full kernel is connected. This proves
the connectedness implication used in Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017),
Proposition 5.59 and Theorem 16.40. The retained source is
