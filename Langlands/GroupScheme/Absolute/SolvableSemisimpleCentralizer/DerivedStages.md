# Smooth connected stages of the derived series

Starting from the ambient smooth connected affine group, the commutator
image provides the next smooth connected closed subgroup. Its rational points
are generated by the commutators. One inclusion follows from the universal
commutator map. In the other direction the dominant ordered product of that
map supplies the rational-point inclusion into the abstract commutator
subgroup. Iteration preserves normality of the composite inclusions and
identifies the rational ranges with successive terms of the abstract series.

The first derived subgroup is killed by maps to commutative groups. Applied
to the split torus quotient of a connected solvable group, this places any
smooth closed subgroup of the derived series in the unipotent kernel.
This is the derived-series input used in Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017),
Theorem 16.40, printed p. 339, with the smoothness and connectedness of the
derived group discussed in 6.19 and the solvable torus quotient in 16.33.
