# Actual closed subgroups for the coordinate-refined filtration

This internal construction realizes the coordinate-refined matrix filtration
as actual closed subgroup schemes over `Spec R`, for every commutative ring `R`.
It also constructs their full scheme-theoretic inverse images in any group
scheme with a matrix representation.

`refinedPosition a b i j` selects the entries at or below the `a`-th
superdiagonal and the entries in rows below `b` on the next superdiagonal.
`mem_refinedW_iff_entries` identifies membership with those entries having
their identity-matrix values. `refinedIdeal` is the ideal generated by
these entry equations in the coordinate ring of the general linear group.
`isCutOutBy_refinedW` verifies that the quotient ring corepresents the
matrix subgroups over every coefficient algebra.

`refinedScheme` is the spectrum of that quotient ring with its induced
group structure, and `refinedHom` is its closed inclusion into the general
linear group. `refinedFunctor` records its points on all test schemes.
The all-test point description follows from the published
`Corepresented.range_cutHom`.

For an actual matrix representation `ρ` of a group scheme `H`,
`filtrationScheme ρ a b` is the group-scheme pullback of `refinedHom` along
`ρ`. Its projection `filtrationHom` is closed. On every test scheme, its
range consists exactly of the points of `H` whose matrices satisfy the
original refined equations. Affineness and local finite type follow from
the corresponding property of `H` and the closed immersion.

The inverse-image subgroup functors decrease with the row bound. When `ρ`
is upper triangular on every test scheme, they are normal in `H`; this
uses the matrix conjugation formula without passing to reduced schemes.
For an actual homomorphism to the triangular block parabolic group,
`triangular_pointMatrix_mem_upper` supplies this all-test triangularity
and `isNormal_triangular_filtration` gives normality with no additional premise.
Killing all rows identifies the term with the next superdiagonal term.
If the representation is monic over the base, the final term is the
trivial subgroup on all test schemes, using cancellation of `ρ`.

No quotient or splitting section is asserted here. This is derived
categorical and coordinate-ring assembly of the published closed-subgroup
and inverse-image constructions with the coordinate refinement.
