# Coordinates on the upper triangular subgroup scheme of `GL_N`

Authors: Worker 1234.

This module is an internal helper for the proof of the published
`Langlands.GroupScheme.GeneralLinearBorelStatement` (module
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Absolute.GeneralLinearBorelProof`). It proves the first
part of that proof: the upper triangular subgroup scheme of `GL_N` is smooth,
and connected over a field. The source motivation is Milne, *Algebraic Groups*
(2017), source ID `milne-2017-algebraic-groups`, Example 17.7 (printed p.354,
PDF p.370). There the upper triangular group `T_n` is a connected smooth
subgroup. Milne gives no coordinate computation; the route below is internal.

Everything lives in the namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme.GeneralLinear.UpperTriangular`. Fix `N : ℕ`, a
commutative ring `R : CommRingCat.{u}` and a weight vector `e : Fin N → ℤ`.

## The localized polynomial ring

- `Position N` is the type of positions `(i, j)` in `Fin N × Fin N` with
  `i ≤ j`, on and above the diagonal.
- `Poly N R` is the polynomial ring `MvPolynomial (Position N) R`, with one
  variable for each such position.
- `diagonalProduct N R` is the product of the diagonal variables `X (i, i)`.
- `Coord N R` is `Localization.Away (diagonalProduct N R)`.
- `genericMatrix N R` is the matrix over `Coord N R` with entry `X (i, j)` for
  `i ≤ j` and `0` below the diagonal. `genericMatrix_isUpperTriangular` shows it
  is upper triangular. `genericMatrix_det` shows its determinant is the image
  of `diagonalProduct N R`, using `Matrix.det_of_isUpperTriangular`.
- `genericUnit N R` is this matrix as an element of `GL (Fin N) (Coord N R)`.
  It is invertible because `diagonalProduct N R` becomes a unit in the
  localization.

## The coordinate ring of the block parabolic subgroup scheme

The published block parabolic subgroup scheme
`blockParabolicScheme (Fin N) R e` (module `CorepresentedClosedSubgroup`) is
`Spec` of the quotient of the published `CoordinateRing (Fin N) R` of `GL_N` by
the published `entryIdeal (Fin N) R (fun i j => e i < e j) 0`.

- `lowerIdeal R e` abbreviates this ideal and `QuotRing R e` the quotient ring.
- `quotUnit R e` is the universal matrix: the published `point` of the
  quotient map, an element of `GL (Fin N) (QuotRing R e)`.
- `polyToQuot R e : Poly N R →ₐ[R] QuotRing R e` sends `X (i, j)` to the entry
  `(i, j)` of `quotUnit R e`.

From here on `e` is strictly decreasing (`he : StrictAnti e`). Then
`e i < e j` holds exactly when `j < i`, so the ideal is generated by the
entries strictly below the diagonal.

- `genericUnit_mem_blockParabolicSubgroup`: the generic matrix lies in the
  published `blockParabolicSubgroup (Coord N R) e`.
- `toCoord R he : QuotRing R e →ₐ[R] Coord N R` is the published classifying
  map `classify (genericUnit N R)` on `CoordinateRing (Fin N) R`. It factors
  through the quotient, by `classify_eq_zero_iff` and the previous item.
  `toCoord_mk` records its value on classes.
- `quotUnit_isUpperTriangular`: the universal matrix over the quotient is upper
  triangular. Each entry below the diagonal is a generator of `lowerIdeal R e`.
- `polyToQuot_diagonalProduct_isUnit`: the image of `diagonalProduct N R` is the
  determinant of the upper triangular invertible matrix `quotUnit R e`, hence a
  unit.
- `fromCoord R he : Coord N R →ₐ[R] QuotRing R e` extends `polyToQuot R e` to
  the localization (`IsLocalization.Away.liftAlgHom`). `fromCoord_algebraMap`
  and `fromCoord_genericMatrix` compute it on polynomials and on the entries of
  the generic matrix.
- `fromCoord_comp_toCoord` and `toCoord_comp_fromCoord` show that the two maps
  are mutually inverse. The first is checked on the universal point: the
  published `homEquiv` identifies algebra maps out of the coordinate ring of
  `GL_N` with invertible matrices, and the published `classify_entry` evaluates
  the classifying map on an entry. The second is checked on the variables of
  the polynomial ring (`IsLocalization.algHom_ext`,
  `MvPolynomial.algHom_ext`).
- `quotEquiv R he : QuotRing R e ≃ₐ[R] Coord N R` is the resulting algebra
  isomorphism. So the coordinate ring of the upper triangular subgroup scheme is
  the polynomial ring in the entries on and above the diagonal, localized at the
  product of the diagonal entries.

## Smoothness and connectedness

- `quot_smooth`: `QuotRing R e` is a smooth `R`-algebra. The polynomial ring in
  finitely many variables is smooth, a localization away from one element is
  smooth over it (`Algebra.Smooth.of_isLocalization_Away`), smoothness composes
  (`Algebra.Smooth.comp`), and `quotEquiv` transports it
  (`Algebra.Smooth.of_equiv`). This repeats the pattern of the published
  `GeneralLinearSmoothProof`.
- `smooth_blockParabolicScheme`: the structure morphism of
  `blockParabolicScheme (Fin N) R e` is smooth. The structure morphism is
  `Spec.map` of the algebra map of `QuotRing R e`, and
  `HasRingHomProperty.Spec_iff` and `RingHom.smooth_algebraMap` reduce scheme
  smoothness to `quot_smooth`.
- `diagonalProduct_ne_zero`: over a domain the product of the diagonal
  variables is nonzero (no hypothesis on `e`).
- `quot_isDomain`: over a domain, `QuotRing R e` is a domain. The localization
  of a domain at the powers of a nonzero element is a domain
  (`IsLocalization.isDomain_localization`), and `quotEquiv` transports this.
- `connectedSpace_blockParabolicScheme`: over a domain the underlying space of
  `blockParabolicScheme (Fin N) R e` is connected. It is `Spec` of a domain,
  hence irreducible, hence connected (Mathlib instances).

Over a field `k`, with `R = CommRingCat.of k`, these give the smoothness and
connectedness parts of `IsBorelSubgroup`. The results hold over any
commutative ring (smoothness) or domain (connectedness), with no restriction on
the characteristic or on `N`; only strict decrease of `e` is assumed.
