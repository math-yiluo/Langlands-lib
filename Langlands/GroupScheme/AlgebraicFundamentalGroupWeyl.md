# Normalizing points act trivially modulo the coroot image

Author: Worker 149 (Task 6302). This is a helper module for the proof of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.AlgebraicFundamentalGroup.AlgebraicFundamentalGroupComparisonStatement`
in `Langlands.GroupScheme.AlgebraicFundamentalGroupComparisonProof`. It supplies the
Weyl-triviality input `hC` of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.AlgebraicFundamentalGroup.Choice.comparison_of_conjugators`.

## Statements

- `Langlands.GroupScheme.toriAreSplit_of_isSepClosed E`: over a separably closed field `E`
  every torus is split (`ToriAreSplit E`).

The remaining names are in `Langlands.GroupScheme.AlgebraicFundamentalGroup`.

- `coweightEquiv_sub_mem_corootSpan P hw y`: let `P` be a root pairing over `ℤ` with
  weight lattice `M` and coweight lattice `N`. For every automorphism `w` of `P` in the
  Weyl group `P.weylGroup` and every `y : N`,
  `w.coweightEquiv y - y ∈ P.corootSpan ℤ`.
- `Choice.comp_mem_corootImage b iS φ h x`: let `S` be a commutative group scheme over
  `Spec E`, `iS : S ⟶ (C_b)_E` and `φ : S ⟶ (T_b)_E` homomorphisms with
  `iS ≫ (π_b)_E ≫ (d_b)_E = φ ≫ (i_b)_E`. Then `x ≫ φ ∈ b.corootImage E` for every
  cocharacter `x` of `S`.
- `Choice.corootSpan_le_corootImage hG b hP`: let `[IsSepClosure k E]`, `G` connected
  reductive, `b : Choice k G`. Let `P` be any root datum, with index type `J : Type u`, on
  the character and cocharacter lattices of `(T_b)_E`, such that
  `IsRootDatumOf (i_b)_E P`. Then `P.corootSpan ℤ ≤ b.corootImage E`. So every coroot of
  `G_E` with respect to `(T_b)_E` comes from the simply connected cover.
- `IsNormalizingPoint.exists_weylGroup hH hT hi hP hn`: let `K` be a field, `H` a connected
  reductive group over `Spec K`, `i : T ⟶ H` a split maximal torus, and `P` a root datum of
  `i`. Let `n` be a `K`-point of `H` normalizing `i` through `σ : T ≅ T`
  (`IsNormalizingPoint i n σ`). Then some `w ∈ P.weylGroup` acts on characters by
  `w.weightEquiv χ = σ.inv ≫ χ`.
- `IsNormalizingPoint.sub_mem_corootSpan hH hT hi hP hn ξ`: under the same hypotheses,
  `ξ ≫ σ.hom - ξ ∈ P.corootSpan ℤ` for every cocharacter `ξ` of `T`.
- `Choice.IsConjugationComparison.sub_mem_corootImage hG h ξ`: let `[IsSepClosure k E]`
  and `G` connected reductive. If `(n, σ)` is a conjugator from `b` to `b`
  (`b.IsConjugationComparison E b n σ`), then `ξ ≫ σ.hom - ξ ∈ b.corootImage E` for every
  `ξ : b.lattice E`.

## Provenance

These are internal lemmas. They formalize the step of Kaletha–Prasad (2023),
Construction 11.3.1 (printed p. 427, PDF p. 458) that the comparison does not depend on
the conjugating element. Two conjugating elements differ by an element of the normalizer
of the torus. The normalizer acts through the Weyl group, and the Weyl group, generated by
reflections `y ↦ y - ⟨α, y⟩α^∨`, acts trivially on `X_*(T)` modulo the coroot lattice,
which lies in the image of `X_*(T_sc)`. No literature statement is claimed for the Lean
declarations.

## Proofs

- `toriAreSplit_of_isSepClosed`: `torusFiniteGaloisSplitting` splits the torus over a
  finite Galois extension `K` of `E`. Since `E` is separably closed, `E → K` is bijective
  (`IsSepClosed.algebraMap_bijective`), so `Spec K → Spec E` is an isomorphism, and
  `IsSplitTorus.of_baseChange_of_isIso` descends the splitting.
- `coweightEquiv_sub_mem_corootSpan`: induction over the Weyl group
  (`RootPairing.weylGroup.induction`). For a reflection, `coreflection_apply` gives
  `y - (y - ⟨α_i, y⟩ α_i^∨) = ⟨α_i, y⟩ α_i^∨`, an element of the coroot span. The identity
  is trivial. For a product, the differences add up (`sub_add_sub_cancel`), using
  `coweightEquiv_comp_toLin`.
- `comp_mem_corootImage`: `mem_corootImage_of_lift` (module
  `Langlands.GroupScheme.AlgebraicFundamentalGroupCorootTransport`) with the lift
  `x ≫ iS`.
- `corootSpan_le_corootImage`: work over `E`, where tori split (previous item) and
  `(i_b)_E` is a maximal split torus (`Choice.isMaximalSplitTorus_baseChange`).
  1. `simplyConnectedDerivedCoverSplitTorus`
     (`Langlands.GroupScheme.SimplyConnected.DerivedCoverSplitTorusProof`), applied to the
     base-changed cover (`IsSimplyConnectedDerivedCover.fieldBaseChange`), gives split
     maximal tori `j : S_D ⟶ D_E` and `i_C : S_C ⟶ C_E`, isogeny parts
     `e : S_D ⟶ (T_b)_E` with `e ≫ (i_b)_E = j ≫ (d_b)_E` and `f : S_C ⟶ S_D`, and the
     compatibility `i_C ≫ (π_b)_E ≫ (d_b)_E = (f ≫ e) ≫ (i_b)_E`.
  2. `rootDatumExistence` (`Langlands.GroupScheme.Reductive.RootDatumExistence`) gives root
     data `U` of `j` and `V` of `i_C`. `IsMaximalSplitTorus.isMaximalTorus`
     (`Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.MaximalSplitComparison`) supplies the maximal torus
     hypotheses.
  3. `IsRootDatumOf.map_derived_corootSpan_eq_coweight_zero`
     (`Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.CoweightCocenterCharacters`), with `M = G_E`,
     `j = 𝟙` and coweight `0`, gives
     `(U.corootSpan ℤ).map e_* = Submodule.span ℤ (P.coroot '' {p | P.root' p 0 = 0})`,
     where `e_*` is `RealTorusSpace.cocharacterMapInt e`. Its relative-root hypothesis
     holds because every root pairs to `0` with the coweight `0`. The set
     `{p | P.root' p 0 = 0}` is everything, so the right side is `P.corootSpan ℤ`.
  4. `centralIsogenyRootDatumHom` (`Langlands.GroupScheme.CentralIsogeny.SplitRootDatumProof`)
     for the central isogeny `C_E → D_E` gives a root datum homomorphism `V → U` whose
     coweight map is `ν ↦ ν ≫ f`. By `RootPairing.Hom.coroot_coweightMap_apply`, each
     coroot of `U` is `V.coroot r' ≫ f`.
  5. Hence each coroot of `P` has the form `V.coroot r' ≫ (f ≫ e)`.
     `comp_mem_corootImage` with `iS = i_C`, `φ = f ≫ e` and the compatibility of step 1
     puts it in `b.corootImage E`.
- `IsNormalizingPoint.exists_weylGroup`: by `weylGroup`
  (`Langlands.GroupScheme.Reductive.RootDatumWeylProof`), `weylImage i` equals the image of
  `P.weylGroup` under `RootPairing.Equiv.weightHom`. The Weyl action of `⟨n, hm⟩` lies in
  `weylImage i`, which gives `w`. `weylAction_eq` identifies its action as
  `χ ↦ σ.inv ≫ χ`. The normalizer element is passed to `weylAction_eq` explicitly. Leaving
  it implicit makes Lean unify `↑?n` with `n`, which times out.
- `IsNormalizingPoint.sub_mem_corootSpan`: take `w` from the previous item. The pairing of
  `P` is the lattice pairing (`hP.1`, `latticePairing_apply`), and it is perfect
  (`LinearMap.IsPerfPair`). Pairing against all characters then shows
  `w.coweightEquiv y = y ≫ σ.inv`. Apply `coweightEquiv_sub_mem_corootSpan` to
  `ξ ≫ σ.hom`. This gives `ξ - ξ ≫ σ.hom` in the coroot span; negate.
- `IsConjugationComparison.sub_mem_corootImage`: over `E`, `G_E` is connected reductive and
  `(i_b)_E` is a split maximal torus. `rootDatumExistence` gives a root datum `P` of it. A
  conjugator from `b` to `b` is literally a normalizing point of `(i_b)_E`. Combine
  `IsNormalizingPoint.sub_mem_corootSpan` with `corootSpan_le_corootImage`.
