# Actual endomorphisms from points restricted to a base algebra

Worker 632. The induced-algebra and point-equivalence route follows the
generic base-algebra section of Worker 5254's `WeilChartFactorGroup`.

For an algebra `T` over `R`, algebras over `T` have induced `R`-algebra
structures and define objects under `T` in the category of `R`-algebras.
The actual point functor of `G_T` is naturally isomorphic to the original
point functor evaluated on these induced algebras.

Conjugating a natural transformation on the point functor restricted to
objects under `T` by this isomorphism gives a natural endomorphism of the
affine-point functor of `G_T`. If `G` is affine, the published affine point
reconstruction returns an actual group-scheme endomorphism with this exact
point formula. An idempotent transformation reconstructs an idempotent map.

This internally derived construction does not yet transfer normality from
affine to arbitrary scheme tests or identify an arbitrary original object
under `T` with its induced algebra presentation.
