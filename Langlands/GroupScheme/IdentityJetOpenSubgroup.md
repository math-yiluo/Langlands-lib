# Identity jets and rank along an open subgroup

An open subgroup inclusion of a separated group identifies every actual
identity neighborhood. The square of jet inclusions is cartesian by the
pullback of the ideal of the unit and its powers. The open subgroup contains
the support of the ambient jet, so the induced open immersion of jets is an
isomorphism.

For a locally finitely presented group this also identifies the corresponding
fixed-rank smoothness conditions. Smoothness at the unit is tested in the open
subgroup and flatness and rank are transported through the first-jet
isomorphism. For a smooth separated group, the locally constant rank of the
finite flat first jet gives open neighborhoods of fixed rank. The unit provides
a section of that jet and hence the rank is positive. These are the identity
neighborhood comparisons used in SGA 3, Exposé XV, §5.2–5.5.
