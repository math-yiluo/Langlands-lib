# Dominant homomorphisms to reduced groups

`DominantGroupHomFlatStatement` is a proposition target for group schemes over
an algebraically closed field. The source is of finite type, expressed by a
locally finite type and quasi-compact structure morphism. The target is reduced
and locally of finite type. A homomorphism whose underlying scheme map is
dominant must be flat, scheme-surjective and quasi-compact.

The intended internal derivation uses generic flatness on a nonempty open of
the reduced target, then propagates flatness by group translations.
Dominance ensures that rational points in the image meet every nonempty target
open. Translation of a flat open therefore covers the target. Once the
homomorphism is flat, its open image and the same translation argument imply
surjectivity. Neither affineness nor a rational-point surjectivity premise is
part of the statement. This is an internal geometric prerequisite for identifying
a quotient with its affine representation image.
