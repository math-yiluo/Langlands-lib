# Base change of the actual finite Cartier dual

`schemeDualBaseChangeIso` identifies the pullback of the original
finite Cartier dual with the finite Cartier dual of the pulled-back
group. On a test, pullback identifies points of the first scheme with
points of the original dual on the test over the original base. Use
the character representation there, transport characters by relative
base change, and recover the points of the dual over the new base.
Naturality in the test gives the actual group scheme isomorphism.

The comparison intertwines actual transpose maps. Under the character
representation this is the compatibility of base change with
precomposition. Evaluation on a pair of points gives the same global
unit after the comparison, by the base-change law for evaluation of
relative characters.

The construction uses the description of finite Cartier duality by
characters and evaluation in Demazure and Gabriel (1970), Chapter II,
§1, no. 2, §2.10(a,b,d), printed pp. 156–158 (PDF pp. 186–188).
It derives the comparison for the actual dual spectra over affine
base rings.
