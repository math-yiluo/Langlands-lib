# Additivity of actual finite Cartier dual maps

A dual point at an affine test is determined by its relative character. The
proof uses the actual affine-character equivalence of the dual Hopf spectrum
and the affine Hopf coordinate isomorphism of the original scheme. Evaluations
after arbitrary maps to the test determine the relative character.

This separation proves `schemeDualMap_add` by evaluating on the universal
dual point. The evaluation for a sum of homomorphisms is the product of the
two evaluations. Thus the transpose construction is additive on the actual
Hom groups and sends multiplication by a natural number to multiplication
by the same number on the dual group scheme.

The result is derived from the finite dual construction and character
pairing in Demazure and Gabriel, *Groupes algébriques* (1970), Chapter II,
§1, no. 2, §2.10(a,b), printed pp. 156–157 (PDF pp. 186–187). It supplies
the multiplication compatibility for the dual finite kernels in Tate,
*p-Divisible Groups* (1967), §2.3, printed p. 166 (PDF p. 5).
