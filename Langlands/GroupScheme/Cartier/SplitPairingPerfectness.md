# Perfectness of the alternating pairing on a finite split product

For a commutative finite locally free group scheme `G` over an affine base,
`splitPairingPerfect` identifies the points of the product of `G` and its actual
Cartier dual with the relative characters of that product. The character of
`(x, χ)` evaluates on `(y, ψ)` as `ψ(x) * χ(y)⁻¹` by
`splitPairingCharacters_evaluation`.

Relative characters of the product split by restriction along the two factor
inclusions. The inverse adds the two characters pulled back along the
projections. In these coordinates the alternating pairing sends `(x, χ)` to
the negative of the character of `χ` on the primal factor and the character
of the canonical bidual point of `x` on the dual factor. The comparison is
bijective on affine tests by the affine Cartier character equivalence for
each factor and the actual bidual coordinate isomorphism. Zariski descent for
points and relative characters gives the natural isomorphism on all tests.

This derives the finite-level perfectness of the tautological polarization
used in Rapoport–Zink, *Period Spaces for p-divisible Groups* (1996), §6.43,
printed p. 309 (PDF p. 321), before (6.17). The evaluation descriptions of
Cartier duality and biduality come from Demazure–Gabriel, *Groupes
algébriques*, Tome I (1970), Chapter II, §1, no. 2, §2.10(a,b,d), printed
pp. 156–158 (PDF pp. 186–188).
