# Exactness of finite locally free Cartier duality over an affine base

`Langlands.GroupScheme.Cartier.FiniteCartierDualExactnessStatement` states that
Cartier duality sends a short exact sequence of finite locally free commutative
group schemes over an affine base to the reversed short exact sequence. The two
dual arrows are the spectra of the transposes of the actual Hopf coordinate
pullbacks. The exactness condition asks for injectivity and equality of image and
kernel on each affine test, and for the final arrow to lift points after a
faithfully flat algebra extension of finite presentation.

Tate describes the exact sequence for a finite subgroup and its quotient, and
states exactness of the dual sequence in *p-Divisible Groups* (1967), §1.3,
printed pp. 159–160 (PDF pp. 1–2), equation (1) and the last sentence of §1.3.
Here the sequence is expressed through affine fppf test points. This formulation
allows the result to be applied directly to the scalar kernel diagrams of a
formal module.
