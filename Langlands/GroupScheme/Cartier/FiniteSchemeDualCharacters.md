# Relative characters represented by the actual finite dual spectrum

`schemeDualCharacters` identifies points of the actual dual spectrum
with relative characters of the original finite locally free
commutative group scheme. First use the character given by the Hopf
coordinate pairing. The affine character equivalence gives
bijectivity on each affine test. The two sheaf properties extend
this comparison to all tests. The affine Hopf-coordinate comparison
then transports the characters to the original group scheme.

Evaluation gives the actual finite Cartier pairing. Restriction of
the character is induced by restriction of the dual point. The
transpose map of the dual spectra corresponds to precomposition of
characters. To prove this, restrict to an arbitrary further test and
use adjointness of the original map and its dual for the evaluation.

See Demazure and Gabriel (1970), Chapter II, §1, no. 2, §2.10(a,b,d),
printed pp. 156–158 (PDF pp. 186–188), for finite Cartier duality and
its evaluation. The natural isomorphism here uses the actual finite
dual spectrum and the relative character functor used by the towers.
