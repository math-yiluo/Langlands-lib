# Recognizing and lifting torus centralizers over local nilpotent quotients

Let the ambient group be smooth and affine over a local ring and let the base be reduced modulo a nilpotent ideal. `LocalNilpotentTorusCentralizersStatement` has two assertions. A smooth closed subgroup whose reduction is the centralizer of a given closed torus is itself the centralizer of a closed torus lifting the given torus. Every closed torus over the quotient also admits a lift in the ambient group whose smooth closed centralizer reduces to the given centralizer. All comparisons use equality of subgroup functors on every test scheme.

For recognition, factor the given torus through the reduced subgroup. It is central there by the centralizer factorization theorem. The original subgroup is affine by its closed immersion into the affine ambient group. Smooth representability of closed tori in affine groups gives a lift inside this smooth affine subgroup. Nilpotent reflection of centrality makes the lift central there, so the subgroup lies inside its ambient centralizer. Both groups are smooth. The inclusion between them is an isomorphism modulo the ideal, by the compatibility of centralizers with base change. Nil-ideal reflection of isomorphisms makes it an isomorphism over the local ring. For the second assertion, lift the torus directly inside the ambient group and take its smooth closed centralizer. Base change preserves its subgroup functor. The recognition lemma specializes to the closed fibre over a local Artinian ring because its maximal ideal is nilpotent.

This derived statement formalizes the local lifting argument from SGA 3, Exposé XV, Proposition 7.1(ii) and the smoothness argument in the proof of 7.1(i), recomposed pp. 282–283. It assumes affineness of the ambient group explicitly and uses the given torus over a nilpotent quotient. The proof of recognition uses centrality reflection and isomorphism reflection in the form available in the Library.
