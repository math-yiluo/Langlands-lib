# Detection of closed subgroup containment on adic thickenings

`AdicClosedSubgroupContainmentStatement.{u}` concerns a homomorphism from a
relative torus and a closed subgroup of its target over a noetherian local
ring. If the range of the homomorphism lies in the base-changed subgroup on
every maximal-ideal power thickening, its range lies in the subgroup on every
test over the original base.

The pullback of the closed subgroup contains the thickenings of the torus.
The adic open-neighbourhood lemma supplies a factorization on an open near
the identity over the closed point. This open contains the entire identity
section by specialization. The product of two copies gives a smooth
surjective morphism to the torus by geometric connectedness of its fibres.
Its source is an open subscheme of the noetherian square of the torus, so the
product map is quasi-compact. Membership in the closed subgroup descends
along that fpqc map.

This is a derived containment result for the inheritance argument in Raynaud,
SGA 3, Exposé XV, Proposition 8.7(ii), Gille–Polo recomposition, PDF p. 60.
