# Residue-field homomorphisms of a formal split torus

Let \(R\) be a complete noetherian local ring. Let \(G\) be a smooth separated group scheme over \(\operatorname{Spec} R\) whose structure morphism is quasi-compact and whose actual residue-field fibres are affine. For a compatible system of homomorphisms from a split torus to \(G\) over the powers of the maximal ideal, `System.exists_splitTorusHom_on_residueField` proves `System.HasSplitTorusHomOnResidueFields`. Over each actual residue field there exists a homomorphism whose restriction to each positive-order torsion subgroup is the base change of the corresponding algebraized torsion homomorphism.

At the closed point, the residue-field map factors through the first quotient and the formal homomorphism there supplies the witness. At any other point, the completed DVR specialization theorem supplies a homomorphism over the completed valuation ring. Its residue-field restriction agrees on torsion with the original algebraized torsion after the residue-field extension. The target over the original residue field is affine, so Laurent coefficient descent along the field embedding gives a homomorphism over that field. Faithfully flat descent of equality gives the required torsion comparisons over the original residue field.

This is a derived version of the fibrewise descent in Raynaud's *SGA 3*, Exposé XV, Lemma 3.10 and the proof of Theorem 4.1(c), recomposed pp. 257 and 263–264 in the Gille–Polo edition dated 1 December 2008. Raynaud compares subtori after extension of residue fields. Here the source is a fixed split torus, and the formal system and its algebraized torsion restrictions specify the homomorphisms that are compared.
