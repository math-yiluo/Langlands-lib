# Closedness of algebraized finite-source embeddings

`AdicTorsionClosedEmbeddingStatement.{u}` turns the compatible restrictions
of formal closed torus embeddings into actual closed finite torsion
embeddings. It applies over a complete noetherian local ring and assumes
the ambient group is separated over the base.

A homomorphism from a finite source to a separated group is finite. Its
self-pullback is finite over the base, so equality of the two projections
is detected on all adic thickenings by finite-source Hom injectivity.
The closed reductions make the two projections agree at every level.
The actual homomorphism is therefore a finite monomorphism, hence a
closed immersion. Apply this criterion to each algebraized torsion map.

This is an adic proof of the closedness step for finite torsion maps in
SGA 3, Exposé XV, Lemma 6.7, Gille–Polo recomposition, PDF p. 44.
