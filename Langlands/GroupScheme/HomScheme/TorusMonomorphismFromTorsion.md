# Positive torsion detects monomorphisms from relative tori

Let `H` be a relative torus over a scheme `S` and let `G` be a separated group scheme over `S`. If the restriction of `f : H ⟶ G` to each positive integer torsion subgroup is a monomorphism of schemes, then the underlying morphism of `f` in `Over S` is a monomorphism.

On each residue field the full kernel is a closed subgroup of a torus, hence of multiplicative type. A finite-order point of this kernel on any test scheme lifts into the corresponding torsion subgroup of the source. Cancellation against the monomorphic torsion restriction shows that the lift is the unit. Density of all positive torsion makes the unit section of the field kernel an isomorphism. The relative unit section has flat, locally finitely presented source and locally finite type target, so its residue-field isomorphisms give a global isomorphism. Triviality of the full kernel gives the asserted monomorphism.

This is a derived step for SGA 3, Exposé XV, Proposition 8.1, where an algebraized homomorphism with closed formal restrictions must be shown to give an actual torus subgroup.
