# Actual regular kernel corrections and conjugacy

Let `R` be a commutative base ring. For the deformation statements, let `I` be square-zero, let `H` be flat and affine over `Spec R`, and let `G` be smooth over `Spec R`. Square-zero is supplied as `Fact (SmoothLifting.SqZero I)`. The target is allowed to be nonaffine and nonseparated. Coefficients are the actual base reduction-kernel module `M = Additive(NonaffineKernel.kernel G I)`.

`schemeMapFromPoint` identifies an actual point of `G` on the chosen affine source coordinate algebra with a regular map `H.X → G.X`. It composes that point with the inverse source coordinate isomorphism. The generic evaluation recovers the point, and evaluation at any affine test is its actual coefficient specialization.

`cochainKernelMap` applies this construction to a regular one-cochain `b`: evaluate `b` at the universal source point, apply the inverse canonical flat kernel comparison, and take its actual underlying point. On every flat test, `cochainKernelMap_flat` identifies its value with the inverse flat comparison of `b`'s value. This follows from the two naturality laws. The reconstructed scheme map becomes the identity after base change to `R/I`: its generic value is an actual reduction-kernel element, and `GenericReduction.baseChange_eq_one_of_generic_reduction` detects the scheme-level identity.

`isDefectCorrection_of_flat` proves that the regular correction identity can be checked on flat affine tests. Express its sides as two regular maps from the source product `H.X × H.X` to `G.X`. The source product is itself affine and flat, so the assumed identity applies over its chosen coordinate algebra at its presenting point. Cancel that isomorphism to equate the two regular maps, then precompose with any pair of points on an arbitrary test scheme.

`cochainKernelMap_isDefectCorrection` applies this detection result to an actual regular additive primitive for the multiplicativity defect. On flat tests, the inverse flat comparison sends the additive coboundary equation to the exact ambient group-valued correction equation. Therefore `b⁻¹ s` is multiplicative on every test scheme. The existing `correctedHom` construction produces an actual group-scheme homomorphism, and the reconstructed correction reduces to the identity. Consequently `exists_homLift_of_schemeLift` proves the following: if an actual scheme morphism `s : H.X → G.X` reduces to a specified homomorphism `f₀` over `R/I`, and the reduced source has `HasVanishingLowDegreeCohomology`, then there is an actual homomorphism `f : H → G` reducing to `f₀`. The initial scheme-map lift and the vanishing property are explicit inputs.

`basePointOfAffine` regards an actual `G(R)` point as a section over the identity of `Spec R`. Its constant affine values are its actual restrictions. A point in the reduction kernel gives a section restricting to the identity over `Spec(R/I)`.

`exists_kernelConjugator_of_cohomology` takes actual homomorphisms `f,f' : H → G` with equal base changes and the same reduced-source vanishing property. The reduced-point adapter supplies their equality on every reduced test, so the regular difference cochain has a primitive `m` in the actual base kernel. On each flat test, the inverse kernel comparison sends its coboundary equation to the actual pointwise one-coboundary equation. The elementary kernel difference theorem gives conjugacy by the inverse of the restricted primitive. At the universal source point, this is exactly conjugation by the constant value of the section associated to `m⁻¹`. Cancelling the source coordinate isomorphism proves `f' = f ≫ (G.innerIso g).hom`, where `G.constantPoint g (Over.mk r) = 1` for `r = Spec.algebraMap R (R/I)`.

This is an internal derived deformation argument. It combines actual flat-kernel comparisons, the proved regular cochain contractions under a supplied vanishing hypothesis, scheme reconstruction from affine coordinates, and the existing correction homomorphism. The reduced-point and generic-reduction adapters are included unchanged with their supporting kernel sources. The module does not prove the initial smooth scheme lifting theorem or general multiplicative-type cohomology vanishing, and it does not assemble the full relative or nilpotent lifting statement. No actual reduction-kernel comparison on nonflat tests is used or asserted.
