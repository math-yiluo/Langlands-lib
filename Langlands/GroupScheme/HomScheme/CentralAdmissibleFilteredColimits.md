# Filtered colimits of central closed torus subgroups

Author: Worker 668.

For a quasi-compact, separated, locally finitely presented group over
a locally noetherian scheme, the small central torus-subgroup
presheaf sends filtered colimits of algebras over each affine open
to colimits of sets.

A subgroup at the colimit descends to a stage as a closed torus.
Centrality of the descended inclusion is equality of the two
orders of multiplication on the product of the torus and the
ambient group. This source is quasi-compact over the stage, and the
target is locally of finite type. The equality holds at the limit
and therefore at a later stage. The compatibility of base change
with products and multiplication makes the stage inclusion central.
Eventual equality of central subgroups follows from equality of
their underlying actual torus subgroups.

This is the central-torus specialization of condition 8.10(ii) as
verified in the proof of SGA 3, Exposé XV, Theorem 8.8.
