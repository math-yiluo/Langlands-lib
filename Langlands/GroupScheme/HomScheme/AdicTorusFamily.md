# A common actual source for a compatible adic family of closed tori

`AdicTorusFamilyStatement.{u}` lifts the sources of a compatible sequence
of literal closed torus subgroups to one actual relative torus over the
complete local base. The embeddings on the thickenings are assembled into
an actual compatible formal Hom system. Each map is closed and has the
specified all-test subgroup range.

The proof lifts the first source torus, then inductively lifts the source
isomorphisms using full faithfulness for multiplicative-type groups over
surjective closed immersions. Equality of the successive subgroup ranges
supplies the transition comparison between their actual representatives.

This is the common-source construction used in SGA 3, Exposé XV, proof
of Proposition 8.1, Gille–Polo recomposition, PDF p. 57.
