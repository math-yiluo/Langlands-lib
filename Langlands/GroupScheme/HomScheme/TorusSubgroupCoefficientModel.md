# Finite coefficient models of actual closed torus-subgroup families

Author: Worker 668.

Fix a quasi-compact, separated, locally finitely presented group over the
spectrum of a noetherian ring. For any algebra over that ring, every
admissible subgroup of the pulled-back group has a finite coefficient
model. The coefficient ring is a finitely generated subalgebra of the
given algebra. The model's family evaluates to the supplied actual
closed torus subgroup as a literal all-test subgroup functor.

The construction starts with a finite coefficient model of the affine
smooth torus source. The homomorphism to the actual ambient pullback
descends by finite presentation. Enlarge coefficients to descend its
closed immersion, using quasi-compactness of the ambient group. Enlarge
again to descend the relative torus property of its source. The final
model retains closedness by arbitrary base change. Compatibility of the
actual pullback comparisons identifies the evaluated group map with
the supplied inclusion after a source isomorphism, preserving the
all-test range.

This internal model uses the Library's finite coefficient group and
homomorphism constructions, closed-immersion descent, and relative
torus approximation. It supplies existence of models for the limit
condition of Task 7705.
