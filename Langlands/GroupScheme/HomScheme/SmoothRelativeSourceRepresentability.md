# Actual Hom schemes with relative torus source

This internal construction extends the published graph-locus construction from split tori to relative tori over an arbitrary base scheme. If `H` is an etale-locally split torus and `G` is a smooth relatively affine group scheme, `smoothRelativeSourceHomModel` chooses a scheme over the base, an actual universal relative group homomorphism from `H` to `G`, and proofs of its universality and smoothness. No separation or quasi-compactness assertion is made for the parameter scheme.

Two etale splitting covers are refined to a common cover by pulling back the second and composing covers. The published all-test isomorphism-locus criterion on a common splitting cover then represents invertibility between two relative tori by the existing open locus. Graphs of actual homomorphisms from the relative torus are admissible closed torus subgroups in the product. The proof restricts the published smooth universal closed-torus subgroup family in `H × G` to the open locus where its first projection is an isomorphism. Recovering a homomorphism from each graph and uniqueness in the subgroup moduli establish universality on every test scheme.

The provenance is internal and derived: the module uses the existing `TorusIsomorphismLocus` criterion, `RelativeGraphRecovery`, `GraphLocusUniversal`, the published smooth torus-subgroup representability theorem, and affineness and smoothness of relative tori. Its proof follows the already implemented graph-locus argument and introduces no new premise on the base or the torus rank.
