# Representability of fibrewise torus centralizers by finite-jet charts

For a smooth separated quasi-compact group with geometrically connected
affine residue fibres over a noetherian affine base, this construction gives
a locally finite-type parameter for smooth geometrically connected closed
subgroups that are torus centralizers on the residue fibres. The parameter
carries the actual universal closed subgroup, and every such family is its
evaluation along a unique morphism of test schemes over the base.

On a test carrying a centralizer family, factorization through a fixed
rank and order chart is the intersection of the open of the prescribed
first-jet rank and the open where the family is open in that order's jet
stabilizer. This proves openness of the charts, with injectivity from
uniqueness of the actual jets. The residue-field detection theorem yields
local coverage at every point of every test. Effective Zariski descent and
the open chart gluing theorem then give the parameter and its actual family.
This implements the open-chart argument of SGA 3, Exposé XV, §5.2–5.5 and
its application to the fibrewise torus-centralizer locus in §6.
