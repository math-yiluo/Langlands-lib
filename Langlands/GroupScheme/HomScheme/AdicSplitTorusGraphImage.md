# Finite graph images of residue-field torsion realizations

Let \(A\) be a complete Noetherian local ring, \(H\) the split torus with character group \(\mathbb Z^L\) for a finite set \(L\), and \(s\) a compatible system of homomorphisms from \(H\) to \(G\) on the maximal-ideal quotients of \(A\). For each point of \(\operatorname{Spec} A\), choose a homomorphism on the actual residue fibre that realizes every positive algebraized torsion restriction of \(s\). `System.HasFiniteSplitTorusGraphImages` says that the schematic image of any map from a topologically Noetherian scheme into \(H\times_{\operatorname{Spec} A}G\) is finite over \(H\) if its range lies in the union of the chosen residue-field graphs. The product projection is the map to \(H\). `System.hasFiniteSplitTorusGraphImages` proves this property when \(G\) is smooth, separated and quasi-compact over the base and its residue fibres are affine.

The graph on each residue field is a closed immersion into the product over that field. Composing with the fibre inclusion gives a preimmersion into the original product whose first projection is the fibre inclusion into \(H\). Evaluation along a map of test schemes commutes with these graph maps by the two product projections.

For a point in the source of any test-scheme graph of a torsion realization, its residue-field test maps both to the given test scheme and to the residue-field test at its image in \(\operatorname{Spec} A\). The evaluated homomorphisms agree because they realize the same torsion. Evaluating at the point therefore puts its graph image in the chosen residue-field graph. For a DVR-valued point of \(H\), the realization theorem gives a homomorphism on the induced base test. Its graph, composed with the given point lifted to the base-changed \(H\), provides the lift required by the finite schematic-image theorem. The split torus is affine and Noetherian, and the product projection inherits the necessary morphism properties from \(G\).

This is a derived application of the valuation test in Raynaud, *SGA 3*, Exposé XV, proof of Lemma 3.10, recomposed pp. 257–258, PDF pp. 25–26 in the Gille–Polo edition dated 1 December 2008. The Lean module uses the residue-field graphs and their DVR lifts to obtain a finite schematic image. This intermediate finiteness result needs no normality assumption on \(A\).
