# Central torus subgroups and adic limits

For a smooth separated group with geometrically connected fibres over
a noetherian local ring, centrality of a relative torus homomorphism
can be checked on the maximal-ideal power thickenings. The actual
closed centralizer then contains the pulled-back ambient group on
each thickening. The adic neighborhood lemma produces an open around
the identity over the closed point that factors through the
centralizer. This open contains the entire identity section.

Multiplication on two copies of that open is smooth and surjective,
using the connected-field product result after residue field extension.
It factors through the centralizer. Pulling back this cover along
an ambient point and using faithful flatness shows that the original
torus homomorphism is central on every test scheme.

Consequently adic-limit bijectivity for actual closed torus subgroups
restricts to the central subpresheaf for a smooth separated group
with geometrically connected fibres. This supplies the transfer from
ordinary to central adic effectivity used in SGA 3, Exposé XV,
8.7(i). [Gille–Polo recomposition, PDF p. 60](https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~patrick.polo/SGA3/Expo15.pdf)
