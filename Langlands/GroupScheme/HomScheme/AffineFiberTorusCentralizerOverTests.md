# Geometry of torus centralizers over arbitrary tests of a Noetherian affine base

Let the ambient group be smooth, separated, and quasi-compact over a Noetherian affine base, with affine and geometrically connected residue fibres. Over any test scheme, the centralizer of a closed torus is represented by a smooth closed subgroup with geometrically connected fibres. Each residue fibre is the centralizer of the pullback torus.

Smoothness is local on the test scheme, so it suffices to consider an affine test. The filtered-colimit comparison descends the closed torus subgroup to a finitely generated algebra over the Noetherian base ring. At that stage, the Artinian affineness criterion gives smoothness of the actual closed centralizer. The original centralizer is isomorphic to its pullback because centralizers commute with base change and the two actual subgroups have the same range on every test. The fibre comparison gives geometric connectedness and the residue-fibre condition.

This is a finite-stage consequence of the Artinian proof of centralizer smoothness used in SGA 3, Exposé XV, Proposition 7.1(i), and the base-change property of the relative centralizer. It supplies the geometry of the families classified by the torus centralizer parameter.
