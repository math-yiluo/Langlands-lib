# Smoothness of a torus centralizer parameter scheme

Over a locally Noetherian base, suppose a locally finite-type parameter scheme has a smooth closed universal subgroup whose residue fibres are torus centralizers. Suppose every actual closed torus determines a unique parameter point classifying its centralizer on all test schemes. If the ambient group is smooth and becomes affine on local Artinian tests, the parameter scheme is smooth.

The proof uses the elementary local Artinian lifting criterion. It identifies the smaller ring with a quotient, recognizes its supplied subgroup as a torus centralizer using the closed fibre, and lifts the torus in the affine ambient group. The universal property then classifies the centralizer of the lifted torus. The resulting point reduces to the original point by base change for subgroup functors and uniqueness. Field-valued tests use the given residue-fibre tori via the factorization through their residue field.

This is the smoothness argument from SGA 3, Exposé XV, Proposition 7.1(i), recomposed p. 283. The statement isolates that argument from the construction of the parameter scheme.
