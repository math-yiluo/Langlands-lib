# Infinitesimal bijectivity for central closed torus subgroups

`CentralAdmissibleArtinianStatement.{u}` applies to a smooth group scheme whose
pullbacks to local Artinian affine tests are affine. Restriction in the actual
central closed torus presheaf is bijective along each surjection of test rings
with nilpotent kernel from a local Artinian ring.

For uniqueness over a nilpotent quotient, equality of the literal subgroup
ranges identifies the two reduced tori by an isomorphism over the ambient
group. Full faithfulness for multiplicative-type groups lifts its map to the
original base. Central infinitesimal rigidity identifies the composite with
the original inclusion. Applying this to both directions gives equality of the
ranges over the original base.

For existence, representability of closed torus subgroups of a smooth affine
group gives a point of a smooth parameter scheme. Lift the point over the
local nilpotent quotient. Evaluate the corresponding family and use the
actual pullback comparisons to recover the prescribed subgroup after
reduction. The reduced inclusion is central, so the nilpotent centrality
lifting theorem makes the lifted torus central as well. The quotient by the
kernel of an arbitrary surjection is canonically isomorphic to its target,
which transports bijectivity to that restriction map.

This proves the local Artinian form of the infinitesimal condition in the
verification of Raynaud, SGA 3, Exposé XV, Lemma 8.10(v), Gille–Polo
recomposition, PDF p. 62, using the published affine representability and
multiplicative-type rigidity results.
