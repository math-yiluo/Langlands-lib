# The presheaf of smooth closed subgroups with torus-centralizer fibres

Over a test scheme, a centralizer family is an actual all-test subgroup represented by a smooth closed geometrically connected subgroup whose residue fibres are centralizers of closed tori. The full kernel ideal of its closed representative determines the subgroup on every test and permits shrinking to the universe of the schemes. Pullback of the representative preserves the geometric conditions. Its range gives a choice-independent restriction by the actual family evaluation API.

This is an internal construction for the local chart argument for centralizer families in SGA 3, Exposé XV, §5.2. The Lean construction uses the literal residue-field condition and its base change comparison.
