# Adic Hom effectivity from a lift of the first quotient

Let the base ring be complete and local, let the source
group be of multiplicative type, and let the target be smooth. The
predicate `HasFirstQuotientLift` requires an actual homomorphism
restricting to the first quotient of a specified compatible adic
homomorphism system. The theorem
`exists_algebraization_of_firstQuotientLift` proves that this suffices
to algebraize the entire system.

On every square-zero affine test, the published low-degree
cohomology vanishing for multiplicative type gives the lifting of
prescribed conjugators. Nilpotent induction and the kernel-quotient
comparison apply this to the adjacent adic quotients. Starting with
the unit at the first quotient, lift the conjugator inductively to
obtain compatible actual points of the target. The local adic point
theorem algebraizes these points, and its equality test ensures
that the resulting point lies over the identity of the base.
Conjugation by that point gives exactly the specified homomorphism
at every level.

This internal result combines square-zero cohomology with adic
algebraization of points on any scheme over a complete local base.
It permits the residue-field lift to be constructed separately
from the correction of higher adic thickenings.
