# Reduction of infinitesimal homomorphism lifting to square-zero tests

`HasAffineSquareZeroConjugatorLifting H G` is the explicit square-zero input for a fixed pair of group schemes over an arbitrary scheme `S`. It requires `HasConjugatorLifting H G` on every square-zero affine quotient inclusion over `S`, uniformly over commutative rings and compatible structural maps in the fixed universe. This definition assumes a lifting property; the module does not prove it for smooth targets or multiplicative-type sources.

`hasConjugatorLifting_nilpotent` applies the generic affine-test nilpotent induction to obtain the same property for every nilpotent affine quotient inclusion. No smoothness or multiplicative-type assumptions are used in this formal implication.

`homLifting_of_hasConjugatorLifting` explains how the relative property on a map `S₀ → S`, regarded as an arrow to the identity test `S → S`, gives actual group-scheme homomorphism lifts. It also gives conjugacy by an actual base point restricting to the identity for any two homomorphisms with the same reduction. The existence proof removes identity pullbacks using the canonical `baseChangeIdIso` comparisons and the published `evaluate_identityBase` theorem. The conjugacy proof applies the relative property to the identity conjugator between the two restrictions. The specified-conjugator condition ensures the resulting base point restricts exactly to that identity.

The module supplies three comparison lemmas used in this extraction. `removeIdentityBase_map` follows from naturality of the identity pullback isomorphism on group objects. `pulledBackPoint_identity` compares the actual test-valued pullback point with the usual pulled-back base point by the injective point adjunction. `conjugateRelative_identityBase` then combines that comparison with compatibility of inner automorphisms with base change. Applying removal of identity pullbacks to the relative conjugacy equation gives the required equation between the original homomorphisms.

Finally, `multiplicativeTypeInfinitesimalLift_of_squareZero` has conclusion exactly the unchanged Library proposition `Langlands.GroupScheme.MultiplicativeTypeInfinitesimalLiftStatement`. Its hypothesis is the square-zero input for every smooth group-scheme target and multiplicative-type source over every base scheme in the universe. The proof specializes the nilpotent affine-test result to the identity map of `Spec A` and applies the preceding extraction theorem. It preserves arbitrary commutative rings, nilpotent ideals, nonsplit multiplicative-type sources, and smooth targets without affineness or separatedness hypotheses.

This is an internal derived reduction from the relative Hom evaluation, actual conjugation and identity-base-change APIs, together with generic nilpotent induction. It transcribes no new literature assertion and supplies no proof of the remaining square-zero deformation input. In particular, it is a conditional reduction, not a proof of the task's target.
