# Local detection of fibrewise torus centralizers by finite jets

Let the ambient group over an affine base be smooth, separated, and
quasi-compact, with affine residue fibres. Consider an arbitrary test scheme
over the base and a smooth geometrically connected closed subgroup of the
pulled-back group that is a torus centralizer on every residue fibre of the
test. Each test point has an open neighborhood on which the subgroup is open
in the closed stabilizer of its actual jet at some finite order. The
neighborhood can be chosen so that the subgroup is smooth along its unit and
its flat first jet has constant rank `1 + r` for some `r`.

Over the residue field, the jet stabilizers eventually agree with the full
normalizer. The torus centralizer is open in that normalizer. The open
immersion locus of the actual subgroup homomorphism to the jet stabilizer
then extends openness to a neighborhood of the test point. The first-jet
rank is locally constant and gives the rank refinement. This implements the
local exhaustion argument of SGA 3, Exposé XV, §5.2–5.5, using a jet order
chosen on an individual residue fibre.
