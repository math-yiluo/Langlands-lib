# The chart of torus centralizers of fixed rank and detecting jet order

Over a noetherian affine base, let the ambient group be smooth, separated
and quasi-compact with geometrically connected affine residue fibres. For
fixed rank and jet order the chart represents the smooth geometrically
connected closed subgroups with that rank that are torus centralizers in
the residue fibres and are open in the stabilizer of their actual jet.

The construction starts with the finite-flat subscheme parameter of the
ambient identity jet. On the fixed-rank smoothness locus along the unit of
its closed universal stabilizer, take the actual relative identity
component. Restrict to the open where its residue fibres are centralizers
of tori. There the inclusion into the ambient group is closed. The
parameter chart is the equalizer of the original jet point and the jet
point of this closed subgroup. The finite-flat subscheme parameter is
separated, so the equalizer is closed and the chart is locally of finite
type over the original base.

For an arbitrary test with a qualifying subgroup, its jet supplies the
parameter point. The subgroup is the relative identity component of this
stabilizer because it is smooth, geometrically connected and open in it.
The equality of actual jet ideals therefore gives the factorization through
the equalizer. The chart recovers the same subgroup functor on all tests,
and the parameter point is unique because the jet was unique and all
restrictions of the parameter were monic. This implements the fixed-order
argument of SGA 3, Exposé XV, Lemma 5.3, with the residue-fibre condition
added on the open where the relative identity component is closed.
