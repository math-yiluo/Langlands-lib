# Field coverage gives a Zariski cover of the admissible presheaf

Author: Worker 1726.

This internal module proves that `CoversFieldPoints` makes the coproduct of the
actual open-chart maps locally surjective for the big Zariski topology.

For each total section and each chart, `chartLocus` chooses the actual open
subscheme constructed in `OpenCharts.exists_open_pullback`. Its lifting
criterion, recorded as `chartLocus_spec`, holds after every morphism of schemes.
At a point of the parameter scheme, restrict the section to its residue field
and choose an actual closed torus representative. Field coverage places that
representative in a chart. The chart evaluation identity identifies this with
the restricted total section, and the lifting criterion puts the original
point in that chart's open locus. This proves `chartLocus_covers`.

These loci form an actual open cover of the whole parameter scheme. On each
member the lifting criterion gives a chart-valued lift of the restricted
section. Every arrow in the sieve generated by this cover therefore carries a
lift to the coproduct, using the appropriate coproduct inclusion and chart
naturality. Upward closure of covering sieves proves `isLocallySurjective`.

The proof is an internal consequence of the previously proved actual chart
pullback criterion, residue fields and the definition of field coverage. Field
points detect coverage of open loci here; all presheaf restrictions and chart
identities still concern arbitrary scheme tests, without reducedness or
affineness assumptions. Sheafness and effective admissible descent are separate
obligations.
