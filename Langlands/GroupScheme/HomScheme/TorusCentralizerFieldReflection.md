# Field extension and reflection of torus centralizers

A smooth closed subgroup of an affine group over a field is a torus centralizer precisely when it becomes one over a field extension. The centralizer is an equality of subgroup functors over all test schemes.

The proof intersects the centre of the smooth subgroup with a maximal torus and takes the largest subtorus of this intersection, a group of multiplicative type. After any field extension a central torus factors through the extended maximal torus, then through the intersection and its largest subtorus. If an extended subgroup is the centralizer of a torus, that torus factors centrally through the subgroup and hence through this extended largest central torus. The two centralizers agree over the extension. Faithfully flat reflection for represented subgroups gives the required equality over the original field. The forward implication uses base change for centralizers and relative tori.

This derived result supplies the field-extension comparison in the residue-fibre torus-centralizer condition of SGA 3, Exposé XV, §6.4.0 and Lemma 6.6. It uses the largest-subtorus construction in a group of multiplicative type and maximal torus base change.
