# Statement: square-zero homomorphism lifting from low-degree cohomology

`SquareZeroHomomorphismCohomologyStatement.{u}` isolates the geometric bridge between regular module-valued cocycles and actual group-scheme homomorphisms. It concerns arbitrary group schemes `H` and `G` over a scheme `S`, with `G` smooth. Choose a square-zero ideal `I` in any commutative ring `A`, together with compatible structural maps from `Spec A` and `Spec(A/I)` to `S`.

Assume that the source pulled back to `Spec A` is flat and affine, and that the source pulled back to `Spec(A/I)` has `HasVanishingLowDegreeCohomology` with the conventions of `Cohomology.RegularCochains`. The conclusion is `HasConjugatorLifting H G` on that actual affine quotient inclusion over `S`. Thus every reduced relative homomorphism lifts, and every specified point conjugating the restrictions of two relative homomorphisms lifts to a point conjugating those homomorphisms.

The source need not itself be of multiplicative type. Its reduced cohomology vanishing is the explicit input. In particular, this interface can be used for a flat affine source whose reduction is of multiplicative type, once the separate vanishing theorem is proved. Neither affineness nor separatedness of the smooth target is assumed.

The flatness hypothesis on the source is retained explicitly. The comparison of square-zero deformation coefficients with tensor-extended quasi-coherent modules is applied on the source and its powers, which are flat over the base. The statement does not assert that the pointwise kernel of reduction on every possibly nonflat test algebra is itself the tensor-extension functor of a fixed module.

Provenance: this is a derived relative-family formulation of Conrad, *Reductive group schemes* (2014), Proposition B.2.5 and Corollary B.2.6, pp.286–287. A scheme-map lift exists because the source is affine and the target is smooth. Its multiplicativity defect defines a regular degree-two cocycle with the quasi-coherent infinitesimal kernel coefficients, and vanishing corrects the defect. Differences between existing homomorphism lifts define degree-one cocycles; vanishing yields conjugacy by a point reducing to the identity. To lift a specified reduced conjugator, smoothness first lifts that point, after which the degree-one assertion supplies a correction in the kernel. Applying this argument after the structural base change gives the stated relative-family property.

The Lean file is a proposition definition only. It constructs neither the infinitesimal-kernel comparison nor the defect correction, and supplies no proof of the bridge. The companion `InfinitesimalCohomologyReduction` module verifies that its exact interface, together with the separate cohomology and source-geometry inputs, suffices for the unchanged task target.
