# Reduction of affine-fibre torus-subgroup moduli to noetherian affine bases

Author: Worker 668.

`NoetherianAffineTorusSubgroupRepresentabilityStatement` specializes the
published smooth affine-fibre representability statement to spectra of
noetherian rings in the same universe. It retains smoothness, separatedness,
quasi-compactness and affine actual residue fibres of the group. Its conclusion
is a smooth parameter scheme with an actual universal closed torus family.
Universality here gives the unique parameter map for every admissible subgroup
functor on every test scheme, with equality of subgroup-functor evaluations.

The reduction starts over the spectrum of a general ring. The bottom
integer subalgebra is noetherian, so the finite coefficient model for the
group has a noetherian base ring. The assumed representability over that
ring gives a smooth universal family. Its injectivity and full image make
it a single open chart covering all field-valued subgroup points. The
Library's chart base change and ambient isomorphism transport give a chart
cover of the original affine-base group.

For a general scheme base, the affine opens cover the base and the
affine-fibre locus commutes with their base changes. The Library's open-cover
extension gives a chart cover over the original base. Effective admissible
descent then glues the charts into the smooth universal family in the
published statement.

The specialized proposition is internal, obtained by restricting the base
in the published target. The proved implication supplies the arbitrary-base
step for Task 7705, with the noetherian affine proposition as its premise.
