# The small presheaf of actual central closed torus subgroups

Author: Worker 668.

The central admissible subpresheaf takes the sections of the small
actual torus-subgroup presheaf whose entire subgroup functor lies in
the centre. Equivalently, the actual closed torus representative is
central on all test schemes. This condition is preserved under every
restriction by base change of the representative and the canonical
ambient pullback isomorphism.

The subpresheaf satisfies the Zariski and singleton fpqc sheaf
conditions. The full torus-subgroup sheaf gives a unique amalgamation.
Centrality of this amalgamation is detected after the indicated base
change of its actual representative. Injectivity on DVR tests is
inherited from the underlying presheaf.

This is the central subtorus functor used by Raynaud in SGA 3,
Exposé XV, 8.0 and 8.8, in the Library's conventions for actual closed
relative tori, subgroup ranges and small presheaf values.
