# Lifting an abstract residue torus over a complete local ring

`AdicTorusSourceLiftStatement.{u}` gives an actual relative torus lifting
a given relative torus on the first maximal-ideal adic quotient, with an
actual isomorphism of its reduction with the supplied torus. The ambient
group used for the construction is a general linear group over the
complete ring. The proof embeds the given affine residue torus there
on the closed fibre by a faithful finite-dimensional representation,
then uses lifting of torus subgroups in smooth affine groups over a
Henselian local ring.

This supplies the abstract source lift used in SGA 3, Exposé XV, proof of
Proposition 8.1, Gille–Polo recomposition, PDF p. 57. The proof here uses
faithful linear representations and the affine ambient lifting theorem.
