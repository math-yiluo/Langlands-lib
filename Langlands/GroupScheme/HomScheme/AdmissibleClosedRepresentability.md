# Closed scheme representatives of actual subgroup membership

Author: Worker 1726.

This internal construction is derived from absolute subgroup membership,
actual admissible representatives, and group-valued representability transfer.
`SubgroupFunctor.IsSchemeRepresentedBy F j` means that every scheme map to the
ambient group belongs to the subgroup's absolute membership condition if and
only if it factors through the same actual morphism `j`. The quantifiers include
every scheme in the arbitrary universe `u`.

The module supplies the local closed representatives of absolute subgroup
membership over the base cover pulled back to the actual ambient group scheme.
They are the underlying inclusions of chosen admissible representatives of each
base-changed subgroup. The absolute membership adapters identify their complete
scheme factorization conditions with membership of the original subgroup.

An arbitrary global closed scheme representative of that absolute membership
condition becomes an object over the base using its ambient structural map.
Every scheme factorization respects that base map. The existing group-valued
representability transfer therefore constructs the group law and the actual
closed subgroup inclusion with the prescribed all-test range.

For a matching family, its descended subgroup has the original admissible base
changes. Once closed representability is known, the global representative is a
relative torus by the already established locality helper.

The global closed scheme gluing step is a separate input. This module does not
prove unconditional effective admissible descent or the chart gluing statement.
