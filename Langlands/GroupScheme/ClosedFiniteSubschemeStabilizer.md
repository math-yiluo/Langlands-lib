# Closed conjugation stabilizers of finite locally free subschemes

Let `G` be any group scheme over an affine base `Spec R`. A closed subscheme `Z` of `G` that is finite, flat and locally of finite presentation over the base has an actual closed stabilizer subgroup scheme. A point is in the stabilizer exactly when both its conjugation and its inverse conjugation preserve the represented subscheme after every refinement of the test. When `Z` is a subgroup, this is the usual normalizer functor.

The coordinate algebra of `Z` is finite projective over `R`. This allows the closed-factorization construction to detect preservation by testing the entire product of the given test scheme with `Z`. For the inverse condition, pull back the forward locus under inversion and intersect it with the forward locus. Its all-test points form a subgroup functor, which transfers the group structure to the closed locus.

This provides the jet stabilizer used in SGA 3, Exposé XV, Lemma 5.3. The proof uses the closed-factorization locus directly on a finite locally free subscheme.
