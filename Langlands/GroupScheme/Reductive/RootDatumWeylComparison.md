# Supplied root data and the integral normalizer image

This internal bridge uses an already supplied `IsRootDatumOf i P`. It does not
construct root data or prove the unconditional `WeylGroupStatement`.
Throughout, the group is affine over an arbitrary field and the torus
homomorphism has monomorphic underlying scheme morphism. The index type of
the supplied datum may lie in any universe.

`exists_reflectionRepresentative` unwraps the actual rational normalizing
point and torus automorphism included in `IsRootDatumOf`. Uniqueness of the
normalizing automorphism identifies its full integral character action with
the supplied abstract reflection. `reflectionRepresentative` chooses these
points. Mathlib's description of the abstract Weyl image as the subgroup
generated by reflections then proves `abstractWeylImage_le`.

`relativeRootIndex` chooses the unique index of each actual root, using the
datum's exact root-range equality. `relativeRootReflectionData` assigns that
index's coroot and chosen rational normalizing point. The datum's
root-coroot pairing and its reflection formula establish the two required
laws on the full integral character lattice. These are witnesses extracted
from the supplied datum, with no new reflection-existence premise.
`finite_index_of_finite_relativeRoots` transports finiteness of the actual roots along the injective
root embedding.

`weylImage_eq_of_positive_stabilizer` retains splitness, finiteness of the
actual roots, a generic cocharacter, and
`HasTrivialPositiveRootStabilizer i ν` as explicit hypotheses.
The published chamber-generation theorem applied to the extracted integral
reflection data proves that the representatives generate the actual rational
quotient `relativeWeylGroup i = N(k)/Z(k)`. Mapping this generation equality
through `relativeWeylCharacterAction` proves that every actual integral
normalizer action lies in the abstract Weyl image. Combining this with
`abstractWeylImage_le` gives equality on the entire character lattice.
`weylImage_and_ker_of_positive_stabilizer` adds the already proved
`IsSplitTorus.weylAction_ker`, which identifies the full character-action
kernel with the centralizer subgroup of the normalizer.

The provenance of this contribution is internal and derived from the
published `IsRootDatumOf`, `RelativeRootReflectionData`,
`RelativeWeylChamberGeneration`, and `RelativeWeylCharacterAction` interfaces,
and Mathlib's abstract Weyl-image theorem. The geometric triviality of a
positive-root stabilizer remains a premise; it is not established by this
bridge. Neither reductivity, maximality, nor reducedness is inferred here.
The unconditional arbitrary-field target remains a separate proof obligation
within the owning task.
