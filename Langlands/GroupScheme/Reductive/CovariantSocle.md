# The least nonzero covariant regular subspace

This internal derived construction uses the finite regular-span construction
and the unipotent normalized fixed-vector theorem. No external source theorem
is used. The field has arbitrary universe, and the product cell and invariance
hypotheses retain every original relative scheme test.

`affineCovariantSocle G f` is the finite right-regular subspace generated by an
actual coordinate `f` on an affine group `G`. Its construction needs no covariance
hypothesis. It contains `f`, is stable under every coalgebra right action, and
is nonzero when the counit of `f` is one. If `f` is left covariant through an
affine group morphism `i : H → G` for a supplied coordinate `χ` on `H`, the
constructed subspace is contained in the entire left-covariant subspace.

Add a morphism `j : U → G` with `U` unipotent, a nonzerodivisor coordinate `q`,
and a factorization `g = i(a) j(u)` on every test where `q(g)` is a unit.
Supply `f` left covariant, right invariant through `j`, and with counit one.
Then every nonzero right-regular subspace contained in the covariant space
contains `f`, even if it is infinite dimensional. Choose a nonzero element
of that subspace and take its finite regular span inside the subspace.
Unipotence and cell normalization applied there produce a vector whose
coordinate is `f`. Hence the entire regular span of `f` lies in the original
subspace.

Thus `affineCovariantSocle G f` is the least nonzero covariant regular subspace
under these supplied hypotheses. No equality with the entire covariant
space, entire-space finiteness, cofinal weight result or general Steinberg
big-cell existence result is asserted.
