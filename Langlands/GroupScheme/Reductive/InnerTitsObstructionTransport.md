# Transport of the inner Tits obstruction

field and any two supplied Galois pinnings. After base extension, a rational
inner correction aligns the chosen tori and the simple roots. For every such
compatible comparison, the integral weight equivalence preserves the Tits
obstruction. The identity group isomorphism gives independence of the chosen
pinning on the same group, with weights compared by actual inner alignment.
The vertex-innerness condition itself passes across the isomorphism for any
supplied pinning, since a base-changed ground-field isomorphism is invariant
under Galois pullback.

with the transported highest weight. Restriction along the aligned comparison
identifies its highest weight on the source. The rational inner correction

and Tits algebra transport theorems. It applies to the full absolute pinning
