# Weights of tensor products of representations

This module computes the weights of the tensor product `ρ ⊗ τ` (published
`MatrixRepresentation.tensor`) of two representations of a group `H` with a
split pinning `p`, over any field `F`. It proves that, when `ρ` and `τ` have
highest weights `λ` and `μ`, the weight `λ + μ` of `ρ ⊗ τ` has multiplicity
one and every weight of `ρ ⊗ τ` lies below it. This is the weight
bookkeeping in the proof of Corollary 3.5 of Tits, *Représentations
linéaires irréductibles d'un groupe réductif sur un corps quelconque*,
J. reine angew. Math. 247 (1971), p. 205: there `λ + μ` is a simple dominant
weight of the tensor product of the irreducible representations of highest
weights `λ` and `μ` (simple meaning multiplicity one, p. 200), and Proposition 3.4
(pp. 204–205) then supplies the absolutely simple subquotient of that weight.

## Order on weights

- `eq_zero_of_mem_positiveRootMonoid_of_neg_mem`: the published positive root
  monoid (monoid generated by the simple roots) is pointed. If `x` and `-x`
  both lie in it, then `x = 0`. Writing both as `ℕ`-combinations of simple
  roots (Mathlib's `AddSubmonoid.mem_closure_range_iff_of_fintype`), their sum
  is a vanishing combination with nonnegative coefficients. The simple roots
  are `ℤ`-linearly independent (`linearIndepOn_root` of the base).
- `eq_of_add_eq_of_sub_mem`: if `α ≤ λ`, `β ≤ μ` and `α + β = λ + μ`, then
  `α = λ` and `β = μ`.

## Weight vectors and weight spaces of tensor products

- `kronecker_mem_representationWeightSpace`: if `v` is a weight vector of weight
  `α` for `ρ` and `w` one of weight `β` for `τ`, then `v ⊗ w` is a weight vector
  of weight `α + β` for `ρ ⊗ τ`. The proof restricts to the torus (published
  `tensor_restrict`), uses the all-test characterization
  `mem_characterSpace_iff_affine`, the Kronecker formula
  `tensor_affineMatrix_entry`, and multiplicativity of characters
  (`rationalCharacters.comp_add`).
- `tensorWeightSpan ρ τ α β` is the span of all `v ⊗ w` with `v` of weight `α` and
  `w` of weight `β`. By `tensorWeightSpan_le` it lies in the `α + β` weight space.
  `kroneckerRightVec` and `kroneckerLeftVec` are `v ⊗ w` as linear maps in each
  variable.
- `iSup_tensorWeightSpan`: these spans together span the tensor product. Every
  representation of the split torus is the sum of its weight spaces (published
  `iSup_representationWeightSpace_eq_top`); two comap arguments and the basis of
  elementary tensors then give the claim.
- `iSupIndep.eq_of_le_of_iSup_eq_top` is a general lattice lemma. If an
  independent family of submodules contains, termwise, a family whose
  supremum is everything, the two families are equal (modular law).
- Weight spaces are independent by the published
  `SplitPinning.iSupIndep_representationWeightSpace`
  (`Rational/PathFundamentalWeights`), which is used below.
- `representationWeightSpace_tensor`: the `ν` weight space of `ρ ⊗ τ` is the
  supremum of `tensorWeightSpan ρ τ α β` over the pairs with `α + β = ν`.

## Highest weights

Assume `p.IsHighestWeight ρ λ` and `p.IsHighestWeight τ μ`.

- `tensorWeightSpan_eq_bot_left` and `tensorWeightSpan_eq_bot_right`: a span
  vanishes when one of the weight spaces does.
- `tensorWeightSpan_eq_bot_of_ne`: among the pairs with `α + β = λ + μ`, only
  `(λ, μ)` contributes. Every weight of `ρ` (resp. `τ`) is at most `λ`
  (resp. `μ`), and the order is pointed.
- `representationWeightSpace_tensor_add` and
  `finrank_representationWeightSpace_tensor_add`: the `λ + μ` weight space of
  `ρ ⊗ τ` is spanned by `v₀ ⊗ w₀`, the tensor product of the highest-weight
  lines. So it has dimension one.
- `sub_mem_positiveRootMonoid_tensor`: every weight `ν` of `ρ ⊗ τ` satisfies
  `λ + μ − ν ∈` the positive root monoid.

No restriction on the field or its characteristic is imposed.

The tensor-weight argument uses the pointedness of the positive root monoid for the supplied pinning. [Positive root monoid](../Rational/PositiveRootMonoidPointed.md)
