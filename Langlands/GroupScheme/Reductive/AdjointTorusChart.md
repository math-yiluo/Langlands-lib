# The adjoint torus on an affine pinned split chart

For a supplied `AffinePinnedModel M` over its arbitrary commutative ring,
`adjointChartProjection` evaluates the coordinate split torus on the
simple roots of `M.basis`. Its target `adjointChartTorus` is the split torus
with one coordinate per simple root.
`adjointChartProjection_isFppfQuotient` proves that this actual morphism is
flat, surjective and locally of finite presentation, with full
scheme-theoretic kernel exactly `(center M.group).comap M.inclusion`.

This is a local derived ingredient for the adjoint-torus presentation
motivated by Conrad, *Reductive group schemes* (2014), Proposition 7.2.11,
page 258. It concerns supplied split pinned data over one affine base.
It does not construct an atlas of a supplied Borel–torus pair or descend
the simple-root indexing set.

The proof reuses the published arbitrary-ring exponent projection.
Independence of the simple roots gives its fppf properties.
`splitTorus.kernel_range_exponentTorusProjection` identifies its full
kernel on every test scheme with the diagonalizable kernel of the
subgroup generated by the exponent vectors. Equality after the projection
is equivalent to killing each generator; closure induction gives all
relations. The reverse implication checks each coordinate.

The base property `span_int_root_support` identifies the subgroup
generated by the simple roots with the whole root subgroup, without
saturating it. The published
`AffinePinnedModel.centerKernel_isSchemeTheoreticCenter` identifies the
ambient range of this full root kernel with the whole centre.
Cancellation with the closed torus inclusion identifies its internal
range with the inverse image of the centre.

For any supplied quotient `q` with this full fppf kernel,
`adjointChartWeilIso` compares it with `adjointChartProjection` using full
fppf quotient uniqueness, then applies the published split Weil
isomorphism. Thus the actual supplied quotient is isomorphic to
`Res_{M.ring^Δ/M.ring} Gm`, where `Δ` is `M.basis.support`.
`adjointChartCoefficient_properties` records that this coefficient algebra
is finite, projective and étale. The empty support gives the zero algebra
and the trivial split torus.
