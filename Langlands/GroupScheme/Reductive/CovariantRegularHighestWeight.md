# The highest-weight line of an actual covariant regular representation

Let `k : Type u` be any field, `G` an affine group scheme over `k`, and
`p : SplitPinning G` a supplied split pinning. Let `V` be a subspace of the
actual affine coordinate algebra of `G`, equipped with a finite basis and
stable under the right regular action. Assume that every member of `V`
is left covariant under the actual negative Borel with the character
attached to `lam`.

The module proves that the `lam` weight space of the actual finite regular
representation has dimension at most one. If `V` contains a coordinate
`f` whose counit is one, this weight space is nonzero and has dimension
exactly one. It also specializes these statements and the imported
positive-root bound to the actual covariant socle representation.
The field universe and characteristic are arbitrary.

## Internal provenance

These are internal consequences of the actual restriction and character
comparison in `Reductive.CovariantRegularWeights`, together with the
existing complete split-torus weight decomposition. The finite regular
representation, its coordinate map, and the covariant socle are the
existing constructions from the imported regular-representation modules.
No new structural property of a group scheme is asserted.

## Proof of the dimension bound

`covariantHighestRestriction` restricts coordinates to the actual positive
unipotent subgroup and corestricts the resulting linear map to its
constant coordinates. The imported comparison sends
a vector of torus weight `lam` to a coordinate of conjugation weight
`lam - lam = 0`. The imported weight-zero theorem identifies that
subspace with the range of the constant-coordinate map
`Algebra.linearMap k Q`, where `Q` is the subgroup's coordinate algebra.

Restriction is injective on the given covariant subspace.
`affineRegularRepresentation_highest_finrank_le_one` therefore obtains an
injective linear map into the constant coordinates from this construction.
The dimension of the range of the constant-coordinate map is at most the
dimension of `k` over itself, which is one.

## Proof of occurrence

`affineRegularRepresentation_weight_eq_of_counit_ne_zero` compares the
two character equations at the identity. For a vector of torus weight
`mu`, its represented coordinate satisfies the right-character equation
with character `mu` and the left-covariance equation with character
`lam`. Their values at a torus point agree. The remaining factor is the
image of the coordinate's counit.

When the counit is nonzero, that scalar is a unit over the field and
remains a unit after every scalar extension. Cancelling it proves
equality of the character evaluations on every relative scheme test.
The existing character extensionality theorem then gives `mu = lam`.

For `affineRegularRepresentation_highest_ne_bot`, suppose that the
`lam` weight space is zero. Consider the linear functional on the finite
representation obtained by composing its coordinate map with the
counit. This functional vanishes on every weight space: on the `lam`
space by the supposition, and on every other space by the preceding
character-equality lemma. The complete split-torus decomposition says
that the supremum of all these weight spaces is the whole
representation, so the functional vanishes everywhere. Applying it to
the basis-coordinate vector representing `f` contradicts its prescribed
counit value one.

`affineRegularRepresentation_highest_finrank_eq_one` combines this
nonzero weight space with the upper bound.

## Covariant socle specialization and scope

`affineCovariantSocleRepresentation_weights` takes an existing witness
`hf : p.IsRealizedBy lam f`. Its left covariance, the established
covariance and right stability of the actual socle, and its normalized
counit give

```text
finrank k (p.representationWeightSpace
  (affineCovariantSocleRepresentation G f) lam) = 1.
```

For every occurring weight `mu`, the imported weight-bound theorem gives
`lam - mu ∈ p.positiveRootMonoid`. This is the monoid generated by the
simple-root characters in the existing highest-weight convention.
Although `IsRealizedBy` also includes right-unipotent invariance, the
comparison and multiplicity arguments here use its left covariance and
normalized counit.

The module supplies the weight-space conclusions for a realizing
coordinate already provided as input. Construction of such a coordinate,
irreducibility of the socle, and the exact unconditional
fundamental-representation existence theorem are separate obligations.

Integration gives the local instances used here module-specific names to permit
joint imports. Their types, values, and local scopes are unchanged.
