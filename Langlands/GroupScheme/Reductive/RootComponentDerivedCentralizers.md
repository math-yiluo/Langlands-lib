# Relative derived complementary centralizers from component coordinates

Worker 634.

RootComponentCoordinates determines closed split complementary subtori over
every scheme by its integral restriction and extension maps. Their explicit
retractions make the inclusions closed. Over the pinned model's affine base,
the module constructs their actual smooth reductive centralizers and actual
relative derived groups. Each candidate derived component is reductive and
closed in the original group. If the ambient geometric fibres are simply
connected, so are the candidate derived components. The entire coordinate
maximal torus factors through each centralizer and remains fibrewise maximal.
The normalized complementary inclusions commute with arbitrary base change.

RelativeCentralizerDerivedModel generalizes the earlier simply-connected
centralizer-derived data by imposing no simple-connectedness hypothesis.
It uses published smooth split-torus centralizer representability, reductivity
of that centralizer and relative derived-group existence. The stored admissible
derived inclusion is the full scheme-theoretic derived subgroup at every
specified field-valued fibre. The simply-connected specialization follows
from the earlier centralizer-derived comparison, and is a conclusion rather
than part of the data.

This is an internal construction motivated by the recovery step in Conrad,
*Reductive group schemes* (2014), Proposition 6.4.4, pp.232–233. No almost
simplicity, normality in the original group or product isomorphism is asserted.
