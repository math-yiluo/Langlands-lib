# Star-fixed elements of the root span and star-orbit sums of simple roots

This module supplies the lattice step of root-span vanishing for the Tits
obstruction (Tits, *Représentations linéaires irréductibles d'un groupe
réductif sur un corps quelconque*, J. reine angew. Math. 247 (1971),
Corollary 3.5: the kernel of `α` contains the star-fixed part of the root
lattice). The star action permutes the simple roots, and the simple roots
form a `ℤ`-basis of the integral root span. So the star-fixed part of the
root span is generated by the sums of simple roots over star orbits. It
therefore suffices to prove vanishing on these orbit sums.

## Declarations

- `starSupport σ i`: the simple root `(starAction σ).indexEquiv i`. It is
  simple because star automorphisms are based. `coe_starSupport`,
  `starSupport_one`, `starSupport_mul` and `starSupport_inv_starSupport`
  record that this is an action of `K ≃ₐ[k] K` on the simple roots.
- `starOrbitSetoid`: two simple roots are related when some star
  automorphism maps one to the other. `StarOrbit` is the quotient, the set of
  star orbits of simple roots.
- `starOrbitSum q = ∑_{i ∈ q} α_i`, the sum of the simple roots in the orbit
  `q`.
- `weightEquiv_root`: an automorphism of the root datum maps the root `α_i`
  to `α_{e i}`. This is Mathlib's `RootPairing.Hom.root_weightMap_apply`.
- `isStarFixedWeight_starOrbitSum`: orbit sums are star-fixed, because `σ`
  permutes the simple roots of each orbit (`Finset.sum_nbij'` with inverse
  `σ⁻¹`).
- `coeff_starSupport`: if a star-fixed `λ` equals `∑ c_i α_i` over the simple
  roots, then `c` is constant on star orbits. Applying `σ` gives a second
  expansion of `λ` with coefficients `c ∘ σ⁻¹`, and the simple roots are
  linearly independent over `ℤ` (`Base.linearIndepOn_root`).
- `mem_closure_starOrbitSum`: every star-fixed element of the integral root
  span `rootSpan ℤ` lies in the additive subgroup generated by the orbit
  sums.
  1. Since `span ℤ (simple roots) = rootSpan ℤ` (`Base.span_int_root_support`),
     write `λ = ∑ c_i α_i`.
  2. Group the sum by orbits (`Finset.sum_fiberwise`). This gives
     `λ = ∑_q c_q · s_q`, with `c_q` the common coefficient on the orbit `q`.
- `isStarFixedWeight_of_mem_closure`: every element of that subgroup is
  star-fixed (closure induction).
