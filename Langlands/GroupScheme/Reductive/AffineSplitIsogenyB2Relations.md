# The exceptional B2 relations for actual powered Weyl families

This internal supporting statement connects the literal B2 power calculation
to actual normalized root maps. It builds on Worker 685's rank-two arithmetic
and commutator transport and on the powered-word constructions.

`PoweredB2WordRelations` consists of the two standard source short-long B2
commutator formulas, expressed using the actual powered simple maps and their
Weyl family. The theorem assumes corresponding simple-root supports, a
normalized genuine target family for the transported descending words, and
the exchanged Cartan entries. The source entry is minus one, and the target
short-long and long-short entries are minus one and minus two.

The evaluated transpose equation forces the source short root's target
exponent to be twice the other exponent. The root-factor prime-power data
then force characteristic exponent two, and the original characteristic
hypothesis forces two to vanish in the coefficient ring. Thus these
conditions are derived from the root-datum isogeny rather than added as
extra ring hypotheses.

The principal relation is the target B2 formula with the arguments reversed
and the prescribed unequal powers applied. Word transport identifies every
resulting actual map with its source word expression. For the mixed relation,
the target long root and the long conjugate commute: the B2 root list has no
root in their positive cone. The genuine word conjugate is compared with a
root parametrization by the published local rigidity theorem, and scaling
preserves commutation. The source coefficient two therefore vanishes.

The generic helpers also record the all-test formula for a root power and
transport an arbitrary powered map through a reflection word. The paired
statement concerns the displayed B2 Cartan pattern over a local coefficient
ring. It supplies no other rank-two case, global normalization, ambient
extension, or final isogeny theorem.
