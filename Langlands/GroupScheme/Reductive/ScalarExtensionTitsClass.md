# Comparison of Tits classes for two scalar-extension pinnings

Two scalar-extension data for the same original pinning identify their
split groups with the original split group through the same tower
isomorphism. Their split tori identify through the original split torus.
This torus isomorphism is compatible with the identity on the split group.

The based root equivalence through the original root datum induces the
character map of that torus isomorphism. It sends a weight transported by
the first scalar-extension datum to the same weight transported by the
second. The Tits class comparison under pinned conjugation then identifies
the dominant star-fixed classes over the common new ground field.
