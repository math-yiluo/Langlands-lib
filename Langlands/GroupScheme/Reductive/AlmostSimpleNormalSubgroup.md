# Almost-simple normal factors and connected normal subgroups

Author: Worker 1454.

This derived helper proves `IsAlmostSimple.range_le_or_commute_of_normal` for
arbitrary fields and universes. If `G` is smooth affine, `a : A → G` and
`j : H → G` are closed normal subgroup inclusions, `A` is almost-simple and
`H` is smooth connected, then either the full range functor of `a` is contained
in that of `j`, or the two inclusions commute on every test scheme over the
field. No algebraic closedness or characteristic assumption is used.

The proof uses the published `normalCommutator` construction. Its group `D` is
the smooth connected normal closed subgroup generated by the actual commutator
morphism `A × H → G`. Normality of each input makes `D` factor through both
`A` and `H`. Its map to `A` is a closed normal subgroup inclusion, and `D` is
smooth connected. The defining almost-simple dichotomy therefore makes this
map either trivial or total. In the trivial case every test-scheme commutator
is the identity, which gives the stated commutation. In the total case every
point of `A` lifts to `D` and then maps into `H`, giving full range containment.

The argument is an internal consequence of the corrected almost-simple
definition in `AlmostSimple.md` and the published smooth connected commutator
generation theorem. It formalizes the normal-subgroup dichotomy used in the
second assertion of Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017), Theorem 21.51, printed
pp.440–441. It does not assume or prove almost-simple generation of `G`, a
finite decomposition, or existence of a normal complement.
