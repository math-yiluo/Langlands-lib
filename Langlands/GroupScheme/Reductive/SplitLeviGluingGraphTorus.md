# Maximality and root support of the actual graph torus

This internal support module supplies maximality of the actual common torus in the Levi comparison graph, bounds its actual adjoint roots by the common ambient roots, and transfers genericity of the chosen chamber. It retains the full dynamic-factor construction from the companion module. It does not identify every graph root with an ambient root or prove invertibility of a projection.

Let `k : Type u` be a field, `G₁`, `G₂` actual affine group schemes over `Spec k`, and `T` a common commutative group scheme, with inclusions `i₁`, `i₂` and actual `SimpleComparisonGraph D`. This record contains the split torus, actual chamber and simple-root directions, actual local Levi comparisons, and their generated graph envelope. The earlier construction obtains it from the rank-one local Levi hypothesis. The present results do not derive that local hypothesis from equality of roots.

`roots_subset_union` proves `roots D.envelope.torusLift ⊆ roots i₁ ∪ roots i₂`. It works over every field and introduces no ambient smoothness assumption beyond the supplied graph data. Its proof uses the actual all-test joint faithfulness of the two graph projections. If a nonzero character has zero weight space in both ambient Lie algebras, every graph tangent vector of that weight maps to zero under both differentials. Evaluating joint faithfulness on the dual-number test makes that graph tangent vector zero. Thus the graph weight space is zero too. The argument concerns actual adjoint weight spaces and does not assume that either projection has injective differential on its own.

`roots_subset_first hroots` specializes this to containment in `roots i₁` when `roots i₁ = roots i₂`. `chamber_generic hroots` uses this containment and the original chamber's genericity for `i₁` to prove that the chamber remains generic for the graph torus. These results require neither reductivity nor algebraic closedness and claim containment only.

Given `E : D.GraphDynamicFactors`, the companion construction already proves whole-torus self-centralization: `torusCentralizer D.envelope.torusLift = range D.envelope.torusLift`. The result `GraphDynamicFactors.isMaximalTorus E hi₁` combines that identity with splitness of the common torus and closedness of its graph lift, the latter obtained from `hi₁ : IsClosedSubgroup i₁`. The published self-centralizing torus criterion then makes the actual graph torus maximal. `isMaximalTorus_baseChange E hi₁ K` proves maximality after every extension field `K : Type u` with its specified `k`-algebra structure. These consequences of the supplied record require smooth affine ambient groups as in that record, but no additional algebraic closedness or ambient reductivity premise.

`GraphTorusGeometry D` records the actual `GraphDynamicFactors`, maximality of the actual torus lift, genericity of the chosen chamber on the graph, and containment of the graph's actual roots in `roots i₁`. Its primary constructor is
`graphTorusGeometryOfReductive D P₁ P₂ hP₁ hP₂ hroots hG₁ hmax₁`.
It assumes an algebraically closed field, smooth affine `G₁` and `G₂`, the actual graph data, realizing root data for both ambient inclusions, equality of their actual root sets, connected reductivity of `G₁` and maximality of `i₁`. The datum index types may have universes `v` and `w`, independently of each other and `u`. It obtains the full graph factors from `graphDynamicFactorsOfReductive` and applies the preceding maximality and weight-support results. It adds no unproved structural centralizer premise, no reductivity or maximality premise for the second ambient inclusion, and no chosen graph root datum.

The provenance is internal: the proof combines the companion full-factor result, the published self-centralizing torus maximality and base-change criteria, actual Lie functoriality, and joint graph faithfulness on all scheme tests. The separate graph-reductivity construction can be combined with these results when both ambient groups are reductive. Equality of the root sets in the reverse direction, root-factor restriction isomorphisms, projection invertibility, exact Levi gluing and the exact arbitrary-field common-torus theorem remain separate obligations.
