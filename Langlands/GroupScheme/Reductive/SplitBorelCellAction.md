# The torus action on the positive unipotent subgroup of a split pinning

This is an internal supporting module for the proof of
`SplitDominantHighestWeightRepresentationsExistStatement`. It extends
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Reductive.SplitBorelCell` with the conjugation action of
the split maximal torus on the positive unipotent subgroup
`p.positiveUnipotent = U_G(ν)`, and computes its Lie and coordinate weights. The
shape was agreed with the collaborator comparing highest weights (Worker 1744),
who needs a torus action on `U_G(ν)` compatible with conjugation in `G`, the
coordinate weights inside the monoid generated by the simple roots, and the
weight-zero coordinates equal to the constants.

The mathematics is standard: the torus acts on `U_G(ν)` by conjugation, the
weights of this action on `Lie(U_G(ν))` are the roots pairing positively with
`ν` (Conrad-Gabber-Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive groups*, Proposition 2.1.8), and
the weights on the augmentation ideal are the finite sums of those Lie weights
(CGP Example 3.3.2). Both facts are already Library theorems; this module
applies them to the data fixed by `SplitBorelCell` and adds the consequences for
the simple-root monoid. No declaration formalizes a new literature statement.

Throughout, `k : Type u` is a field, `G` is a group scheme over
`Spec (CommRingCat.of k)` with `[IsAffineHom G.X.hom]`, and `p : SplitPinning G`.
`ν` denotes `p.positiveCocharacter`, which pairs positively exactly with the
positive roots of `p.base` (`SplitBorelCell`).

## Positive roots

`root_mem_positiveRootMonoid`: if `p.base.IsPos i`, then `p.rootDatum.root i`
lies in `p.positiveRootMonoid`, the additive submonoid generated by the simple
roots (Library, `Rational/FundamentalRepresentations`). A root is a
nonnegative or nonpositive integral combination of simple roots
(`RootPairing.Base.exists_root_eq_sum_nat_or_neg`). In the nonnegative case it
is a sum of multiples of simple roots. In the nonpositive case its height would
be `≤ 0` (`RootPairing.Base.height_eq_sum`), contradicting
`RootPairing.Base.isPos_iff`.

`mem_positiveRootMonoid_of_mem_positiveRelativeRoots`: every element of
`positiveRelativeRoots p.inclusion ν` (a relative root of the torus pairing
positively with `ν`) lies in `p.positiveRootMonoid`. The relative roots are the
`p.rootDatum.root i` (range clause of `p.isRootDatum`), and positive pairing
with `ν` means `p.base.IsPos i`
(`latticePairing_root_positiveCocharacter_pos_iff`).

## The conjugation action

`positiveUnipotentAction : AutomorphicAction p.torus.toGrp p.positiveUnipotent`
is the restriction (`AutomorphicAction.onRepresentedSubgroup`) of the
conjugation action `AutomorphicAction.conjugation p.inclusion` of the torus on
`G` to the closed subgroup represented by `p.positiveUnipotentHom`. Stability of
`U_G(ν)` under this conjugation is the Library's
`Dynamic.unipotent_isStable_conjugation`.

`positiveUnipotentAction_act_comp`: on points, for `s : T ⟶ p.torus.X` and
`x : T ⟶ p.positiveUnipotent.X`, the image of `act s x` in `G` is
`s · x · s⁻¹` (all composed into `G`).

`isEquivariant_positiveUnipotentHom`: the inclusion `U_G(ν) ⟶ G` is
equivariant (`CoordinateWeights.IsEquivariant`) for `positiveUnipotentAction`
and the conjugation action on `G`, by
`CoordinateWeights.represented_inclusion_equivariant`.

`smooth_positiveUnipotent` and `connectedSpace_positiveUnipotent`: `U_G(ν)` is
smooth (the Library's `dynamicSmooth`, using smoothness of the connected
reductive group `G`) and connected
(`DynamicConnected.connectedSpace_of_isRepresentedBy_unipotent`).

## Lie and coordinate weights

`actionWeightSpace_positiveUnipotentAction_ne_bot_iff`: for a character `χ` of
the torus, the `χ`-weight space of the action on `Lie(U_G(ν))` is nonzero iff
`χ ∈ positiveRelativeRoots p.inclusion ν`. This transports the Library's
`Standard.actionWeightSupport_dynamicUnipotent` (Lie weights of conjugation on
the dynamic unipotent subgroup) along
`RelativeRoot.mem_actionWeightSupport_iff_onRepresentedSubgroup`.

`support_positiveUnipotentAction`: the augmentation weights
`CoordinateWeights.support positiveUnipotentAction` (weights of nonzero
eigenfunctions in the augmentation ideal of the coordinate algebra of `U_G(ν)`)
equal the additive subsemigroup generated by
`positiveRelativeRoots p.inclusion ν`. This is the first half of the Library's
`CoordinateWeights.coordinateSemigroupComparison` (CGP Example 3.3.2) for the
smooth connected affine group `U_G(ν)`, combined with
`RelativeRoot.actionWeightSupport_top` and the previous result.

Consequences, by induction on the semigroup closure:

- `mem_positiveRootMonoid_of_mem_support`: every augmentation weight lies in
  `p.positiveRootMonoid`;
- `latticePairing_pos_of_mem_support`: every augmentation weight `χ` satisfies
  `0 < latticePairing p.torus χ ν`;
- `zero_notMem_support`: the zero character is not an augmentation weight.

`weightSpace_zero_positiveUnipotentAction`: the weight-zero coordinates of
`U_G(ν)` are exactly the constants, the range of
`Algebra.linearMap k (CharacterUnits.Coord p.positiveUnipotent)`. This is the
Library's `TorusUnipotence.weightSpace_zero_eq` applied to
`zero_notMem_support`.

`mem_positiveRootMonoid_of_mem_weightSpace`: if `f` is a nonzero coordinate of
`U_G(ν)` in the `χ`-weight space, then `χ ∈ p.positiveRootMonoid`. For `χ = 0`
this is trivial. Otherwise `f` has counit zero, so it is a nonzero augmentation
eigenfunction (`TorusUnipotence.mem_support_of_mem_weightSpace`), and
`mem_positiveRootMonoid_of_mem_support` applies.
