# Weights of the split-isotypic part of a representation on a Levi comparison

Author: Worker 5662.

Let `d : GaloisPinningData F K G` with split torus data `s` (torus `S`) and Levi comparison
data `L : d.LeviComparison s ι e` for `ι : D ⟶ G` (module `Reductive/SplitLeviComparison`).
Let `ρ` be a representation of `G_K` on `Kⁿ` of highest weight `λ`. In Tits's comparison
of obstructions with the anisotropic kernel (Tits, *Représentations linéaires
irréductibles d'un groupe réductif sur un corps quelconque*, J. reine angew. Math. 247
(1971), §3.4 and §5.5), the relevant representation of `D` is the `S`-isotypic part `X₀`
of `ρ` for the character `λ|S`. Its weights for the torus of `D` are
`λ|T_D − Σ n_b α_b`, with `n_b ≥ 0` and `b` running over the simple roots vanishing on
`S`. So `λ|T_D` is a weight of `X₀` of multiplicity one that dominates all others. This
module constructs `X₀` and proves these statements for actual group schemes and actual
representations. The main instance is the Library's actual anisotropic kernel. The
Brauer-class consequence needs the actual Tits obstruction and is not part of this
module.

## Linear algebra

`inf_le_iSup_of_iSupIndep`: let `V` be an independent spanning family of submodules and
let `W`, `X` be independent families with `V i ≤ W (π i)` and `V i ≤ X (κ i)`. Then
`W a ⊓ X c ≤ ⨆ {i | π i = a ∧ κ i = c}, V i`. Write a vector of `W a ⊓ X c` as a finite
sum of components `vᵢ ∈ V i`, and split the sum into the part with `π i = a ∧ κ i = c`,
the part with `π i ≠ a`, and the part with `π i = a ∧ κ i ≠ c`. Independence of `W`
kills the second part and independence of `X` then kills the third.

## Coordinate subrepresentations

For a split pinning `q` of a group `H`, a representation `ρ`, a subspace `V` and split
coordinates `B`, `C` of `V` (`C B = 1`, columns of `B` in `V`, `B C` fixing `V`) that
are stable coordinates for `ρ`:

- `SplitPinning.representationWeightSpaceRestrictCoordinatesEquiv` is a linear
  equivalence from the weight space of the published coordinate subrepresentation
  `restrictCoordinates ρ B C` at `ν` onto `q.representationWeightSpace ρ ν ⊓ V`. It is
  given by `w ↦ B w`. The published `intertwines_restrictCoordinates` and
  `Intertwines.mem_characterSpace` show that `B` maps weight vectors to weight vectors,
  `coordinateInclusion_injective` gives injectivity, and the published
  `characterSpace_restrictCoordinates` gives the inverse `v ↦ C v`.
- `SplitPinning.finrank_representationWeightSpace_restrictCoordinates` and
  `SplitPinning.inf_ne_bot_of_representationWeightSpace_restrictCoordinates_ne_bot`
  are its consequences for dimensions and nonvanishing.

## The isotypic subspace

- `SplitTorusDatum.isAffineHom_baseChange`: the base change of the split torus is affine.
- `SplitTorusDatum.splitCharacter s μ` is the restriction of an absolute character `μ` of
  `T_K` to `S_K`, by precomposition with the base change of `s.torusMap`.
  `splitCharacter_sub` records additivity.
- `SplitTorusDatum.isotypicSubspace s ρ μ` (the entry definition) is the published
  character space of `ρ.restrict (s.splitInclusion)_K` for the character
  `s.splitCharacter μ`. For `μ = λ` it is the subspace `X₀`.
- `representationWeightSpace_le_isotypicSubspace`: every weight space `ρ_μ` lies in the
  isotypic subspace for `μ`. The proof uses `s.square` and the published
  `characterSpace_le_restrict`.
- `restriction_eq_zero_of_splitCharacter_eq`: equal split characters give a difference
  with zero restriction to `S`.

## Weights of `D`

Let `ρ' = ρ.restrict ι_K`.

- `LeviComparison.representationWeightSpace_le_restrict`: `ρ_μ` lies in the weight space
  of `ρ'` at `L.weightRestriction μ`. The proof uses `L.kernelSquare`.
- `LeviComparison.representationWeightSpace_inf_isotypicSubspace_le`: the weight space of
  `ρ'` at `ν`, intersected with the isotypic subspace for `μ₀`, lies in the sum of the
  `ρ_μ` with `μ|T_D = ν` and `μ|S = μ₀|S`. This is `inf_le_iSup_of_iSupIndep` together
  with the published facts that character spaces are independent
  (`characterSpace_iSupIndep`) and that those of a split torus span
  (`iSup_characterSpace_eq_top_of_isSplitTorus`).
- `LeviComparison.isStableSubspace_isotypicSubspace`: every isotypic subspace is stable
  under `ρ'`. This applies `isStableSubspace_characterSpace_of_centralizes` (module
  `MatrixRepresentation/CentralizerCharacterSpaces`) with `L.centralizes_baseChange`.
- `LeviComparison.exists_isotypicCoordinates`: split coordinates of an isotypic subspace
  exist and are stable coordinates for `ρ'`. The proof uses the published
  `exists_subspace_split_coordinates` and `IsStableSubspace.isStableCoordinates`.

For `ρ` of highest weight `λ` (published `IsHighestWeight`):

- `LeviComparison.sub_mem_zeroRootMonoid`: if `ρ_μ ≠ 0` and `μ|S = λ|S`, then `λ − μ`
  lies in the monoid generated by the simple roots vanishing on `S`. The published
  highest-weight condition puts `λ − μ` in the positive root monoid, and
  `LeviComparison.mem_zeroRootMonoid` applies.
- `LeviComparison.representationWeightSpace_inf_isotypicSubspace_eq`: the weight space of
  `ρ'` at `λ|T_D`, intersected with `X₀`, is the line `ρ_λ`. Every `μ` that contributes
  has `λ − μ` in the zero-vertex monoid with zero restriction to `T_D`, so `μ = λ` by
  `eq_zero_of_weightRestriction_eq_zero`.
- `LeviComparison.finrank_representationWeightSpace_inf_isotypicSubspace`: hence that
  intersection has dimension one.
- `LeviComparison.sub_mem_positiveRootMonoid_of_inf_ne_bot`: if the weight space of
  `ρ'` at `ν` meets `X₀` nontrivially, then `λ|T_D − ν` lies in the positive root monoid
  of `e` (`weightRestriction_mem_positiveRootMonoid`).
- `LeviComparison.eq_of_inf_ne_bot_of_sub_mem`: if moreover `ν − λ|T_D` lies in that
  monoid, then `ν = λ|T_D`, because the monoid is pointed.

On split coordinates `B`, `C` of `X₀` that are stable coordinates for `ρ'`, these become
statements about the actual coordinate subrepresentation
`ρ₀ = restrictCoordinates ρ' B C` of `D_K`:

- `LeviComparison.finrank_representationWeightSpace_isotypicCoordinates`: `λ|T_D` is a
  weight of `ρ₀` of multiplicity one;
- `LeviComparison.sub_mem_positiveRootMonoid_isotypicCoordinates`: every weight `ν` of
  `ρ₀` satisfies that `λ|T_D − ν` lies in the positive root monoid of `e`;
- `LeviComparison.eq_of_representationWeightSpace_isotypicCoordinates_ne_bot`: a weight
  `ν` of `ρ₀` with `ν − λ|T_D` in that monoid equals `λ|T_D`.

## The actual anisotropic kernel

`TitsIndexData.AnisotropicKernelIndex.exists_isotypicCoordinates_highestWeight` specializes
these results to `p.leviComparison`, for the Library's actual anisotropic kernel
`p : d.AnisotropicKernelIndex` and `K = SeparableClosure k`. Let `ρ` be a representation
of `G_K` of highest weight `λ`. There are split coordinates `B`, `C` of the isotypic
subspace of the actual maximal split torus for `λ`, stable for the actual kernel. On the
resulting actual representation of the base-changed kernel:

- the weight `p.comparison.weightRestriction λ` has multiplicity one;
- every weight `ν` satisfies that `p.comparison.weightRestriction λ − ν` lies in the
  kernel's positive root monoid.

No characteristic, perfectness, smoothness or reducedness assumption is made, and no
rank-one, local-field or inner-type hypothesis is used. The provenance is internal: the
module combines published Library results on character spaces, coordinate
subrepresentations and highest weights with module `Reductive/SplitLeviComparison`.
