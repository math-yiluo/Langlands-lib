# The actual action on an equivariant schematic image

This internal derived construction restricts an actual automorphic action to
the schematic image of an equivariant homomorphism between affine group schemes
over any field. The actual projection onto the schematic image is assumed flat,
surjective and locally of finite presentation. The acting group is arbitrary.

To prove all-test stability, lift a point of the image locally along the image
projection, using its pullback as a flat surjective cover. Equivariance puts the
transformed lift in the image. Effective descent for a point through a
monomorphism then gives membership on the original test scheme. The published
represented-subgroup action construction supplies the restricted action.
Cancelling the closed image inclusion proves that both the projection and
inclusion intertwine the given actions.

Provenance: derived from the published schematic image, fpqc reflection of
represented-subgroup membership and action on represented invariant subgroups.
This supplies action descent for root-image geometry; it does not identify a
root or prove the general isogeny comparison.
