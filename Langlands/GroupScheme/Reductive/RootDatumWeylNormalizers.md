# Weyl action of rational normalizer points on cocharacters

`IsRootDatumOf.exists_isNormalizingPoint_coweightEquiv` realizes the
cocharacter action of every Weyl element of an actual root datum by
a rational point normalizing the torus. For a root reflection, the
character action supplied by the actual datum determines the
cocharacter action by the perfect lattice pairing. The formula for
the coreflection is the transpose of the character reflection
for this pairing. Induction on the generating root reflections lifts
an arbitrary Weyl element, with the multiplication order given by
contravariance of the coweight map.

`IsRootDatumOf.exists_parabolic_coweightEquiv` applies the
normalizing-point identity to the dynamic parabolic of a
cocharacter. The resulting Weyl conjugacy is defined over the field
of the torus and is used to turn lattice comparisons into rational
parabolic transport.
