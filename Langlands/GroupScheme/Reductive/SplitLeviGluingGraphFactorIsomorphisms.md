# Actual graph isomorphisms from the factor maps

This internal assembly module turns invertibility of the four actual unipotent restriction maps into invertibility of both actual comparison graph projections. It does not prove those four invertibility assumptions.

The field is arbitrary in universe `u`. The two ambient groups are smooth and affine. A supplied `SimpleComparisonGraph D` contains the split common torus, generic chamber, indexed kernel directions, local comparisons and actual generated envelope. A supplied `D.GraphDynamicFactors E` represents the graph's full opposite unipotent factor, full chamber centralizer by its common torus, and full positive unipotent factor. A supplied `D.GraphFactorProjections E` contains the four actual restriction maps to the canonical ambient unipotent representatives, their inclusion squares and their faithfully flat geometry. This module uses the actual maps and squares; its explicit isomorphism assumptions are stronger than, and are not inferred from, the recorded quotient geometry.

`D.targetCentralizerRep i hroots hG hi` proves that the actual common torus inclusion into a connected reductive target with that maximal torus represents the full chamber centralizer, when its root support agrees with the first ambient group. Equality of root supports transports genericity of the chamber. The published generic-centralizer theorem followed by the existing maximal-torus-centralizer consequence of published structural results identifies the full centralizer with the torus. Closedness of the maximal torus supplies the representation's monomorphism condition.

`E.projectionIso` applies to any smooth connected affine target. It takes the actual graph-to-target map `p`, its common-torus square, a full representation of the target chamber centralizer by that torus, and actual positive and negative `UnipotentProjectionData` for `p`. If both restriction maps are isomorphisms, it constructs an isomorphism from the graph group to the target. The source factors are exactly the representations stored in `E`. The target factors are the canonical unipotent representatives and the common torus. The signed-cocharacter identity identifies the negative canonical representative with the opposite dynamic factor. The two supplied isomorphisms and the identity of the common torus commute with the inclusions by the recorded restriction squares and torus square.

The published `Dynamic.isoOfTripleCellIsomorphisms` then applies. It compares the open triple multiplication cells and uses their factor isomorphisms to produce the ambient isomorphism. `projectionIso_hom` identifies its forward morphism with the actual supplied `p`, so this establishes invertibility of that projection itself.

For `F : D.GraphFactorProjections E`, `E.firstProjectionIso F hG₁ hi₁` uses explicit isomorphism instances for `F.positiveFirst.hom` and `F.negativeFirst.hom`. Connected reductivity of the first target supplies connectedness and, with its maximal torus, the target middle-factor representation. The second projection construction uses the analogous two second restriction-map isomorphisms, connected reductivity and maximality of the second target, and equality of the two ambient root supports. Their forward maps are exactly `D.envelope.first` and `D.envelope.second`. The corresponding `isIso_first_of_factorIsomorphisms` and `isIso_second_of_factorIsomorphisms` expose those instances directly.

`E.comparisonIso` and `E.torus_comparisonIso` assemble the actual ambient comparison and its common-torus square once all four factor maps are invertible. No root datum or algebraic closedness is added to this assembly step, although earlier constructions of the input factor records may use them.

The provenance is internal composition of the actual graph factor records, the published dynamic triple-cell criterion, signed-cocharacter compatibility, generic centralizers and the existing maximal-torus-centralizer structural consequence. All factor representations are full subgroup functors on every scheme test. Kernel injectivity, the four actual factor-map isomorphisms, the unconditional algebraically closed graph projection theorem, exact Levi gluing and the main isomorphism theorem remain separate obligations.
