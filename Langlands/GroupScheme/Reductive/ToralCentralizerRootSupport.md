# Toral character quotients and centralizer roots

`exists_multiplicativeType_characterKernel` uses the absolute character anti-equivalence to realize the quotient of a torus's absolute character lattice by a Galois-stable integral submodule. The quotient map is an equivariant homomorphism of finitely generated discrete modules and corresponds to a homomorphism of multiplicative-type groups over the ground field. Its kernel on characters is the given submodule. This is a consequence of the published `multiplicativeTypeAbsoluteEquivalence`.

`roots_of_open_toralCentralizer` compares the roots for a split torus factoring through an open subgroup of the centralizer of a toral image. In one direction a root of the open subgroup is a root of the centralizer. The image acting by conjugation is central there, so the root restricts to the trivial character. The proof evaluates at the universal point of the affine source of the toral homomorphism. In the other direction an ambient root with trivial restriction has its root weight space in the zero weight space for the restricted action. This is the exact Lie image of the centralizer by `Lie.mem_torusCentralizer_iff_mem_weightSpace_zero`. The open immersion preserves that Lie image, and a nonzero ambient weight vector lifts to a nonzero vector of the same weight in the open subgroup.
