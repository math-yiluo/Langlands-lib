# The simple root subgroups generate the chamber unipotent subgroup

Author: Worker 712 (interface requested by Worker 1444).

This is a derived internal support module for the graph route to the
common-torus Levi gluing statement `SplitCommonTorusLeviGluingStatement`. It is
the second stage of `SplitLeviGluingSimpleGeneration`. Let `G` be a connected
reductive group over an algebraically closed field `k`, `i : T ⟶ G` a split
maximal torus realized by a root datum `P`, and `ν` a generic cocharacter. The
main theorem `unipotent_le_range_of_simpleRoots` shows the following. Every
closed subgroup `q : K ⟶ G` whose range contains the root subgroups of the
simple roots for `ν` contains the dynamic unipotent subgroup `U(ν)`, on every
test scheme. Only the positive simple root subgroups are assumed; the negative
ones and the torus are not.

Throughout, `k : Type u` is a field and all group schemes are over
`Spec (CommRingCat.of k)`. Containment `F ≤ F'` of subgroup functors means
containment on every test scheme. A rational point of a group scheme `H` is a
morphism `Over.mk (𝟙 (Spec (CommRingCat.of k))) ⟶ H.X`; the rational points
form a group. The notation is the Library's:

- `rationalPointImage H S` is the set of points of the underlying space of `H`
  at which the rational points in `S` are located (the image of the closed point
  of `Spec k`). `rationalPointClosure H S` is the set of rational points located
  in the closure of `rationalPointImage H S`.
- `simpleRelativeRoots i ν` is the set of roots positive on `ν` that are not a
  sum of two roots positive on `ν`. `RelativeRoot.subgroup i a` is the root
  subgroup functor of the character `a`.
- `Dynamic.unipotent χ` and `Dynamic.centralizer χ` are the dynamic unipotent
  subgroup and the centralizer of a cocharacter `χ : 𝔾ₘ ⟶ G`, as subgroup
  functors. For a cocharacter `ζ` of `T` the cocharacter of `G` is
  `CommGroupScheme.groupHom ζ ≫ i`.
- `IsRootDatumOf i P` and `IsGenericCocharacter i ν` are as in
  `SplitLeviGluingSimpleGeneration`: `P` has pairing `latticePairing T`, roots
  `roots i` and reflections realized by points normalizing `i`, and every root
  pairs nonzero with `ν`.
- `rationalClosureEnvelope G Γ` (Worker 712's `SplitLeviGluingGeneratedDensity`)
  is a smooth closed subgroup of `G` whose underlying set is the closure of
  `rationalPointImage G Γ`, for a group `Γ` of rational points.

## Definitions

All five are subgroups of the group of rational points of `G`. Here `a` ranges
over `simpleRelativeRoots i ν`.

- `simpleRootPoints i ν a`: the rational points of `RelativeRoot.subgroup i a`.
- `oppositeRootPoints i ν a`: the rational points of
  `RelativeRoot.subgroup i (-a)`.
- `chamberCentralizerPoints i ν`: the rational points of
  `Dynamic.centralizer (CommGroupScheme.groupHom ν ≫ i)`.
- `simpleRootGenerated i ν`: the abstract subgroup generated by all
  `simpleRootPoints i ν a`.
- `oppositeRootGenerated i ν`: the abstract subgroup generated by all
  `oppositeRootPoints i ν a`.

## Main results

In this section `k` is algebraically closed, and `G` is smooth, affine and has
connected underlying space.

- `dense_rationalPointImage_simpleRootCell hT hi hP hν hZT`: let `i` be a closed
  subgroup (`hi`) and a split torus (`hT`), realized by `P` (`hP`), with `ν`
  generic (`hν`). Assume the centralizer of `ν` is the range of `i` (`hZT`).
  Then the products `v * t * w`, with `v ∈ oppositeRootGenerated i ν`,
  `t ∈ chamberCentralizerPoints i ν` and `w ∈ simpleRootGenerated i ν`, have
  dense locations in `G`.
- `unipotent_le_range_of_simpleRoots_of_centralizer_eq hT hi hP hν hZT q hq hpos`:
  under the same hypotheses, let `q : K ⟶ G` be a closed subgroup (`hq`) whose
  range contains `RelativeRoot.subgroup i a` for every simple root `a` for `ν`
  (`hpos`). Then `Dynamic.unipotent (CommGroupScheme.groupHom ν ≫ i)` is
  contained in the range of `q`.
- `unipotent_le_range_of_simpleRoots hG hT hi hP hν q hq hpos`: the same
  conclusion when `G` is connected reductive (`hG`) and `i` is a maximal torus
  (`hi : IsMaximalTorus i`) that is split (`hT`). The hypothesis `hZT` is then
  proved, not assumed.

## Supporting declarations

- `mem_rationalPointClosure_comp φ hφ hx` (any field): let `φ : K.X ⟶ H.X` be a
  morphism of the underlying schemes of two group schemes, sending every point
  of `S` into `rationalPointClosure H S'`. Then it sends every point of
  `rationalPointClosure K S` into `rationalPointClosure H S'`.
- `range_subset_closure_of_rationalPointClosure f S hS` (`k` algebraically
  closed): let `f : P ⟶ H.X` with `P` locally of finite type over `k`. If
  `y ≫ f ∈ rationalPointClosure H S` for every rational point `y` of `P`, then
  the image of `f` lies in the closure of `rationalPointImage H S`.
- `comp_mem_rationalPointClosure_of_dense_tripleProduct m c u hopen V Y U hV hY hU hdense y`
  (any field): let `m`, `c` and `u` be homomorphisms into `G` whose triple
  multiplication `Dynamic.tripleCellMultiplication m c u` is an open immersion.
  Let `V`, `Y` and `U` be sets of rational points of the ranges of `m`, `c` and
  `u`, and assume the products `V * Y * U` have dense locations in `G`. Then
  `y ≫ u.hom.hom ∈ rationalPointClosure G U` for every rational point `y` of the
  source of `u`.
- `le_range_rationalClosureEnvelope Γ hj hΓ` (`k` algebraically closed, `G`
  affine and smooth): let `F` be a subgroup functor of `G` represented by
  `j : H ⟶ G` with `H` smooth, and assume the rational points of `F` lie in
  `Γ`. Then `F` is contained in the range of the inclusion of
  `rationalClosureEnvelope G Γ`.
- `dense_rationalPointImage_of_simpleRootLevis hT hi hP hν Γ hpos hneg hZ`:
  with `G` affine, smooth and connected, `i` a closed split torus realized by
  `P` and `ν` generic, let `Γ` be a group of rational points containing
  `simpleRootPoints i ν a` and `oppositeRootPoints i ν a` for every simple root
  `a` (`hpos`, `hneg`), and `chamberCentralizerPoints i ν` (`hZ`). Then `Γ` has
  dense locations in `G`.
- `simple_mul_opposite_subset_rationalPointClosure hT hP a`: with `G` smooth,
  affine and connected, `i` a split torus realized by `P` and `a` a simple root
  for `ν`, every product `x * y` with `x ∈ simpleRootPoints i ν a` and
  `y ∈ oppositeRootPoints i ν a` lies in the rational-point closure of the
  products `y' * t * x'` with `y' ∈ oppositeRootPoints i ν a`,
  `t ∈ chamberCentralizerPoints i ν` and `x' ∈ simpleRootPoints i ν a`.

## Proofs

`mem_rationalPointClosure_comp` is continuity of `φ.left`: it maps the closure
of the locations of `S` into the closure of the image of those locations, which
lies in the closed set `closure (rationalPointImage H S')`.
`range_subset_closure_of_rationalPointClosure` applies the Library's
`range_subset_closure_rationalPointImage` (closed points of a scheme locally of
finite type over an algebraically closed field are rational and dense) to the
set `rationalPointClosure H S`, whose locations lie in the closed set
`closure (rationalPointImage H S)`.

`comp_mem_rationalPointClosure_of_dense_tripleProduct` projects the open cell
onto its last factor. Let `pr` be the composite of the two second projections
`M × (Z × A) ⟶ A` with `u`. Given an open neighbourhood `O` of the location of
`y ≫ u`, its preimage `O'` under `pr` contains the location of the point
`(1, 1, y)`. The open immersion maps `O'` onto an open set meeting the image of
that point, so by density some product `v * s * w` from `V * Y * U` is located
in it. Writing `v`, `s` and `w` as points of the three sources, injectivity of
the open immersion identifies the corresponding point of `M × (Z × A)` with a
point of `O'`. Its image under `pr` is the location of `w`, so `w ∈ U` is
located in `O`.

`le_range_rationalClosureEnvelope`: the source `H` is reduced, being smooth over
a field. Every rational point of `H` maps into `Γ`, so
`range_subset_closure_rationalPointImage` puts the image of `j` inside the
closure of the locations of `Γ`, which is the underlying set of the envelope.
Worker 712's `factorsThrough_of_range_subset` (a morphism from a reduced scheme
with image inside a closed subgroup factors through it) gives `g` with
`g ≫ inclusion = j`. Every point `y ≫ j` of `F` is then `(y ≫ g) ≫ inclusion`.

`dense_rationalPointImage_of_simpleRootLevis`: let `E` be the closure envelope
of `Γ`. Each root subgroup of a nonzero character has a smooth closed
representative (`RelativeRoot.subgroup_has_smooth_closed_representative`), and
the centralizer of `ν` is represented by the smooth
`Dynamic.Affine.centralizerHom` (`dynamicSmooth`). By
`le_range_rationalClosureEnvelope`, the range of `E` contains all of them. The
first stage, `range_eq_top_of_simpleRootLevis` of
`SplitLeviGluingSimpleGeneration`, then makes this range everything. So the
identity of `G.X` factors through the inclusion of `E`, which is therefore
surjective on underlying points. Its image is the closure of the locations of
`Γ`, so that closure is the whole space.

`simple_mul_opposite_subset_rationalPointClosure` works in the Levi subgroup of
`a`. Let `ζ = leviCocharacter hT i _` (first stage). It pairs to zero with `a`,
and every root pairing to zero with it is linearly dependent on `a`. The
centralizer of `ζ` is represented by a closed subgroup `z : Z ⟶ G`, with `Z`
smooth, affine and connected, and a torus factor `t : T ⟶ Z` with `t ≫ z = i`.
The roots of `t` are roots of `i` pairing to zero with `ζ`
(`relativeRoots_subset_of_factor`,
`relativeRoot_pairing_eq_zero_of_le_dynamicCentralizer`). By
`mem_cyclicTail_of_not_linearIndependent`, those positive on `ν` are positive
multiples of `a`, and those negative on `ν` are positive multiples of `-a`. So
the first stage's `unipotent_le_comap_of_support` maps the dynamic unipotent
subgroup of `ν ≫ t` in `Z` into `RelativeRoot.subgroup i a`, and that of its
opposite (`groupHom_neg_comp_eq_oppositeCocharacter`) into
`RelativeRoot.subgroup i (-a)`. The centralizer of `ν ≫ t` in `Z` maps into the
centralizer of `ν` in `G` (`Dynamic.centralizer_le_comap`). The root subgroups
of `±a` lie in the centralizer of `ζ`
(`RelativeRoot.subgroup_le_dynamicCentralizer_of_pairing_eq_zero`), so `x` and
`y` are `x' ≫ z` and `y' ≫ z`, and `x * y = (x' * y') ≫ z`. Worker 712's
`rationalPointClosure_dynamicTripleProduct_eq_univ`
(`SplitLeviGluingRankOneClosure`), applied in `Z` to the cocharacter `ν ≫ t`,
puts `x' * y'` in the rational-point closure of the dynamic triple products of
`Z`. Each such product maps under `z` into the required set of products in `G`,
by the three containments above. `mem_rationalPointClosure_comp` transports the
closure along `z`.

`dense_rationalPointImage_simpleRootCell`: by `hZT`, every point of
`chamberCentralizerPoints i ν` is a point of the torus. The torus normalizes
every root subgroup (`RelativeRoot.subgroup_isNormalizedBy_range`), hence
normalizes `simpleRootGenerated i ν` and `oppositeRootGenerated i ν`
(`Subgroup.le_normalizer_closure_iff`). The theorem is then Worker 1444's
`dense_rationalPointImage_tripleProduct` (`SplitLeviGluingRationalDensity`)
with the families `simpleRootPoints` and `oppositeRootPoints`, the groups
`V = oppositeRootGenerated i ν`, `T = chamberCentralizerPoints i ν` and
`U = simpleRootGenerated i ν`. Its inputs are supplied as follows. Root
subgroups of distinct simple roots `a` and `-b` commute by Worker 1444's
`commute_simple_opposite` (`SplitLeviGluingMixedRoots`). The rank-one inclusion
is `simple_mul_opposite_subset_rationalPointClosure`. The density of the group
generated by all simple-root points, their opposites and `T` is
`dense_rationalPointImage_of_simpleRootLevis`.

`unipotent_le_range_of_simpleRoots_of_centralizer_eq`: let `χ = ν ≫ i`. The
dynamic big-cell multiplication `U(-χ) × Z(χ) × U(χ) ⟶ G` of the
representatives `Dynamic.Affine.unipotentHom` and
`Dynamic.Affine.centralizerHom` is an open immersion
(`Dynamic.isOpenImmersion_tripleCellMultiplication`). The root subgroup of a
character positive on a cocharacter lies in its dynamic unipotent subgroup
(`RelativeRoot.subgroup_le_dynamicUnipotent`). Since `-a` is positive on `-ν`,
`oppositeRootGenerated i ν` lies in the rational points of `U(-χ)`, and
`simpleRootGenerated i ν` lies in those of `U(χ)`. With the density of
`dense_rationalPointImage_simpleRootCell`,
`comp_mem_rationalPointClosure_of_dense_tripleProduct` puts every rational point
of `U(χ)` in the rational-point closure of `simpleRootGenerated i ν`. The
representative of `U(χ)` is smooth (`dynamicSmooth`), so
`range_subset_closure_of_rationalPointClosure` puts its image in the closure of
the locations of `simpleRootGenerated i ν`. By `hpos` this group consists of
rational points of the range of `q`. The image of the closed immersion `q` is
closed, so it contains the image of `U(χ)`. The representative of `U(χ)` is
reduced, being smooth, and `factorsThrough_of_range_subset` factors it through
`q`. Hence `U(χ)` lies in the range of `q` on every test scheme.

`unipotent_le_range_of_simpleRoots` proves `hZT`. The Library's
`genericCentralizer` identifies the centralizer of the generic `ν` with
`torusCentralizer i` for the maximal split torus `i`. The Library's
`maximalTorusCentralizer_of_structuralInputs`, applied to the published proofs
`dynamicCentralizerReductive`, `noTorusUnipotent` and `solvableTorusQuotient`,
identifies `torusCentralizer i` with the range of the maximal torus `i`.

## Relation to the literature

Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017), Section 23.b (proof of the isogeny theorem),
step 23.22 (printed p.491, PDF p.507) shows, in the graph group `G''` of that
proof, that
`C = V'' T'' U''` is open and dense. There `U''` and `V''` are generated by the
root groups of the simple roots and of their negatives. The proof is the
induction (149). It uses that `U''_α` and `U''_{-β}` commute for distinct
simple roots (23.21), and that `U''_α U''_{-α}` lies in `G''_α`, the closure of
`U''_{-α} T'' U''_α`.
`dense_rationalPointImage_simpleRootCell` is this density step in `G` itself,
on rational points over an algebraically closed field. The induction is Worker
1444's `dense_rationalPointImage_tripleProduct`, and the rank-one input is the
density of the big cell of the Levi subgroup of `a`. Milne needs that the
subgroups `G_α` generate the group (his 21.62, printed p.443, PDF p.459). Here
that input is the first stage `range_eq_top_of_simpleRootLevis`, turned into
density through the closure envelope. The projection to `U(ν)` through the
open big cell, which gives `unipotent_le_range_of_simpleRoots`, is not a
numbered statement of Milne's. It is the statement that the simple root groups
generate the unipotent radical of the Borel subgroup as an algebraic group.
This module proves it from the density step and makes no claim about the
abstract group of rational points. The official errata snapshot of 2026-11-02
has no entry for printed p.491 or p.443.
