# Generation by the simple-root Levi subgroups

Author: Worker 712 (interface requested by Worker 1444).

This is a derived internal support module for the graph route to the
common-torus Levi gluing statement `SplitCommonTorusLeviGluingStatement`. Let
`i : T ⟶ G` be a closed split torus of a smooth connected affine group over a
field, realized by a root datum `P`, and let `ν` be a generic cocharacter. The
main theorem `isIso_of_simpleRootLevis` shows the following. A closed subgroup
`q : K ⟶ G` whose range contains the root subgroups of the simple roots for `ν`,
the root subgroups of their negatives, and the centralizer of `ν` is an
isomorphism. The module is the form of Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017),
Proposition 21.62 used in the gluing proof. It does not assume `G` reductive and
uses no reducedness of the roots.

Throughout, `k : Type u` is a field and all group schemes are over
`Spec (CommRingCat.of k)`. Containment `F ≤ F'` of subgroup functors means
containment on every test scheme. The notation is the Library's:

- `roots i` is the set of nonzero characters with nonzero weight space on the
  Lie algebra of `G`. `relativeRoots i` is the same set.
- `positiveRelativeRoots i ν` is the set of roots `χ` with
  `0 < latticePairing T χ ν`. `simpleRelativeRoots i ν` is the set of positive
  roots that are not a sum of two positive roots. This makes sense for a
  nonreduced root system.
- `RelativeRoot.indivisiblePositiveRoots i ν` is the set of roots positive on
  `ν` that are not `m • b` for a root `b` and a natural number `m > 1`.
- `RelativeRoot.cyclicTail a 1` is the set `{n • a | n ≥ 1}` of positive natural
  multiples of `a`.
- `RelativeRoot.subgroup i a` is the root subgroup functor of the character `a`.
- `Dynamic.unipotent χ` and `Dynamic.centralizer χ` are the dynamic unipotent
  subgroup and the centralizer of a cocharacter `χ : 𝔾ₘ ⟶ G`, as subgroup
  functors. For a cocharacter `ζ` of `T` the cocharacter of `G` is
  `CommGroupScheme.groupHom ζ ≫ i`.
- `IsRootDatumOf i P` says three things. The pairing of `P` is
  `latticePairing T`. The roots of `P` are `roots i`. Every reflection
  `P.reflection j` is the action `χ ↦ σ⁻¹ ≫ χ` of a rational point normalizing
  `i` through an automorphism `σ` of `T`.
- `IsGenericCocharacter i ν` says that every root pairs nonzero with `ν`.

## Main results

- `leviCocharacter hT i ha`: for a split torus `i` (`hT : IsSplitTorus T.toGrp`,
  `G` affine and locally of finite type) and a nonzero character `a`, this is
  the Library's kernel cocharacter
  `RootReducedness.kernelCocharacter hT ha (hT.finite_relativeRoots i)`, chosen
  for the finite set of roots of `i`. `latticePairing_leviCocharacter` says that
  it pairs to zero with `a`.
  `not_linearIndependent_of_latticePairing_leviCocharacter` says that every root
  pairing to zero with it is linearly dependent on `a` over `ℤ`. Its centralizer
  is the Levi subgroup of `a` used below. The Lean statements make no claim
  about this centralizer beyond the theorems listed here.
- `leviCentralizer_le_range hT hP ha q hq hpos hneg hZ`: here `G` is smooth,
  affine and connected, `P` realizes `i`, and `a` is a simple root for `ν`.
  Suppose the range of the closed subgroup `q` contains `RelativeRoot.subgroup i a`,
  `RelativeRoot.subgroup i (-a)` and the centralizer of `ν`. Then it contains the
  centralizer of the Levi cocharacter of `a`.
- `isIso_of_simpleRootLevis hT hi hP hν q hq hpos hneg hZ`: here `G` is smooth,
  affine and connected, `i` is a closed subgroup (`hi`) and a split torus
  (`hT`), `P` realizes `i` (`hP`), `ν` is generic (`hν`), and `q` is a closed
  subgroup (`hq`). Suppose the range of `q` contains `RelativeRoot.subgroup i a`
  and `RelativeRoot.subgroup i (-a)` for every simple root `a` (`hpos`,
  `hneg`), and the centralizer of `ν` (`hZ`). Then `IsIso q`.
- `range_eq_top_of_simpleRootLevis`: under the same hypotheses,
  `SubgroupFunctor.range q = ⊤`.

## Supporting declarations

Root combinatorics, valid for any root datum on the lattices of `T`:

- `closure_nonneg_and_eq_zero`: on the additive monoid generated by a set where
  an additive map `f : X →+ ℤ` is positive, `f` is nonnegative and vanishes
  only at `0`.
- `exists_eq_nsmul_of_mem_closure`: let `Δ` be a `ℤ`-linearly independent set
  of `f`-positive elements and `a ∈ Δ`. If `x` and `z • a - x` both lie in the
  additive monoid generated by `Δ`, then `x = n • a` for a natural number `n`.
- `isAddTorsionFree_of_rootDatum`: the character lattice carrying a root datum
  is torsion free, since the pairing is perfect.
- `pairing_relations_of_not_linearIndependent`: for linearly dependent roots
  `α = P.root j` and `β = P.root l`, `⟨β, α^∨⟩ α = 2 β`, `⟨α, β^∨⟩ β = 2 α` and
  `⟨β, α^∨⟩ ⟨α, β^∨⟩ = 4`.
- `eq_one_or_two_or_four_of_mul_eq_four`: a positive integer `p` with
  `p * q = 4` is `1`, `2` or `4`.
- `exists_root_eq`, `pairing_eq_latticePairing`, `neg_mem_roots`,
  `reflection_mem_roots`, `latticePairing_smul_left`: for `P` realizing `i`,
  every root of `i` is a root of `P`, and `P.pairing l j` is
  `latticePairing T (P.root l) (P.coroot j)`. The roots of `i` are stable under
  negation and under the reflections of `P`. The lattice pairing is linear in the
  character.
- `eq_or_eq_two_smul_of_simple_of_not_linearIndependent`: a root that is
  positive on `ν` and linearly dependent on a simple root `a` is `a` or `2 • a`.
  `mem_cyclicTail_of_not_linearIndependent` restates this as membership in
  `RelativeRoot.cyclicTail a 1`.
- `disjoint_cyclicTail hP ζ`: for any cocharacter `ζ`, distinct indivisible roots
  positive on `ζ` have disjoint sets of positive multiples.
- `mem_closure_simple_of_positive`: every positive root lies in the additive
  monoid generated by the simple roots. This uses Worker 1444's
  `closure_simpleRoots_eq_positive` (`SplitLeviGluingSimpleRoots`).
- `exists_simple_pairing_pos`: a positive root `P.root l` has
  `0 < P.pairing l j` for some simple root `P.root j`.
- `reflection_mem_positive`: for `ν` generic, `P.root j` simple and `P.root l`
  positive and linearly independent from `P.root j`, the root
  `P.reflection j (P.root l)` is positive.
- `positive_induction hP hT hν Q h1 h2 h3`: suppose a property `Q` of
  characters holds on the simple roots (`h1`) and passes from a simple root `a`
  to `2 • a` (`h2`). Suppose it is also stable under each reflection
  `P.reflection j` with `P.root j` simple (`h3`). Then it holds on every positive
  root.

Group-theoretic steps:

- `subgroup_two_nsmul_le hT i ha`: for `G` smooth and affine and `a ≠ 0` in a
  torsion-free character lattice, `RelativeRoot.subgroup i (2 • a)` is contained
  in `RelativeRoot.subgroup i a`.
- `subgroup_weylAction_le n hn h`: let `F` be a subgroup functor containing the
  constant points of a normalizing point `n` on every test scheme, and
  containing `RelativeRoot.subgroup i χ`. Then `F` contains
  `RelativeRoot.subgroup i (weylAction i n χ)`. This needs `i` to be a
  monomorphism.
- `cocharacter_comp_eq_of_reflection hP hσ hζ`: if `characterAut σ` is
  `P.reflection j` and `latticePairing T (P.root j) ζ = 0`, then
  `ζ ≫ σ.hom = ζ`.
- `constantPoint_mem_centralizer hn hζ U`: a point normalizing `i` through `σ`
  with `ζ ≫ σ.hom = ζ` has its constant point in the centralizer of `ζ` on every
  test scheme.
- `exists_normalizer_reflection hP j hζ`: for `latticePairing T (P.root j) ζ = 0`,
  some normalizing point acts on characters as `P.reflection j` and has its
  constant points in the centralizer of `ζ`.
- `range_eq_top_of_isIso`: an isomorphism of group schemes has full range.
- `range_le_of_dynamicFactors z q hq χ hM hZ hU`: let `Z` be smooth, affine and
  connected, `z : Z ⟶ G` any homomorphism and `q` a closed subgroup of `G`.
  Suppose the preimage of the range of `q` under `z` contains the dynamic
  unipotent subgroups of a cocharacter `χ` of `Z` and of its opposite, and the
  centralizer of `χ`. Then the range of `z` lies in the range of `q`.
- `unipotent_le_comap_of_support hT z hzc t ht ζ hb hsupp hF`: let `z : Z ⟶ G`
  be a closed subgroup of `G`, with `Z` smooth, affine and connected, and
  `t : T ⟶ Z` with `t ≫ z = i`. Let `b ≠ 0` be a character such that every root
  of `t` positive on `ζ` is a positive multiple of `b`. Then the dynamic
  unipotent subgroup of `ζ` in `Z` maps into every subgroup functor `F` of `G`
  containing `RelativeRoot.subgroup i b`.
- `centralizer_le_range_of_simple hP hT ha hdep q hq hpos hneg hZ`: for `a`
  simple and a cocharacter `ζ` such that every root pairing to zero with `ζ` is
  linearly dependent on `a` (`hdep`), with the hypotheses of
  `leviCentralizer_le_range`. Then the centralizer of `ζ` lies in the range of
  `q`. `leviCentralizer_le_range` is the case `ζ = leviCocharacter hT i _`.
- `unipotent_le_of_disjoint_positive_rays hS i ν hdisj F hroot`: for a split
  torus `i` of a smooth connected affine group, assume the indivisible roots
  positive on `ν` have pairwise disjoint sets of positive multiples. Then every
  subgroup functor `F` containing their root subgroups contains
  `Dynamic.unipotent (groupHom ν ≫ i)` on every test scheme. The Library's
  `RelativeRoot.dynamicUnipotent_le_of_disjoint_positive_rays` states this on
  rational points. This all-test version has the same proof.

## Proofs

Combinatorics. `exists_eq_nsmul_of_mem_closure` splits the monoid generated by
`Δ` as the monoid generated by `a` plus the monoid generated by `Δ \ {a}`.
Write `x = n • a + c` and `z • a - x = m • a + c'`. Then `c + c'` is a multiple
of `a` in the span of `Δ \ {a}`, so it is zero by linear independence. Since `f`
is nonnegative on both and vanishes only at zero, `c = 0`. For two dependent
roots, Mathlib's `RootPairing.pairing_smul_root_eq_of_not_linearIndependent`
gives `⟨β, α^∨⟩ α = 2 β` in both orders. Cancelling `α` in the torsion-free
lattice gives the product `4`. In `eq_or_eq_two_smul_of_simple_of_not_linearIndependent`,
both roots are positive on `ν`, so `p = ⟨χ, a^∨⟩ > 0` and `p ∈ {1, 2, 4}`. The
case `p = 1` gives `a = χ + χ`, which contradicts simplicity. The cases `p = 2`
and `p = 4` give `χ = a` and `χ = 2 • a`. `disjoint_cyclicTail` uses the same
case split. A common multiple `n • a = m • b` makes `a` and `b` dependent. Then
`p = 1` and `p = 4` contradict indivisibility, and `p = 2` gives `a = b`.

`exists_simple_pairing_pos` argues by contradiction. If every simple `P.root j`
has `P.pairing l j ≤ 0`, then also `P.pairing j l ≤ 0`, by the sign symmetry of
the pairing for a finite root pairing (Mathlib's `zero_lt_pairingIn_iff'`). The
index set is finite by Worker 1444's `finite_rootIndex`. The map
`x ↦ ⟨x, (P.root l)^∨⟩` is then nonpositive on the monoid generated by the simple
roots, which contains `P.root l`. But it takes the value `2` there.
`reflection_mem_positive`: the reflected root is a root, and nonzero on `ν`
since `ν` is generic. If it were negative, `p • a - P.root l` would be positive,
where `p = P.pairing l j` and `a = P.root j`. Then
`exists_eq_nsmul_of_mem_closure`, applied with the linearly independent simple
roots (Worker 1444's `linearIndependent_simpleRoots`), makes `P.root l` a
multiple of `a`. That contradicts linear independence. `positive_induction` is
strong induction on the bound `⟨χ, ν⟩ ≤ N`. Write `χ = P.root l` and choose a
simple `P.root j` with `p = P.pairing l j > 0`. If `P.root j` and `χ` are
independent, then `P.reflection j χ = χ - p • P.root j` is positive with smaller
pairing with `ν`. Reflecting it back gives `χ`, by `h3`. Otherwise `χ` is
`P.root j` or `2 • P.root j`, by `h1` and `h2`. Unlike Mathlib's
`RootPairing.Base.induction_reflect`, this needs no reducedness.

Weyl steps. `subgroup_two_nsmul_le` writes both root subgroups as first weight
filtrations (`RelativeRoot.weightFiltration_one_eq_subgroup`). The positive
multiples of `2 • a` are positive multiples of `a`, so
`RelativeRoot.semigroupSubgroup_mono` applies. `subgroup_weylAction_le`
conjugates by the constant point `c` of `n`. By
`RelativeRoot.normalizer_conj_mem_iff`, `g` lies in the root subgroup of
`weylAction i n χ` exactly when `c⁻¹ g c` lies in that of `χ`. Then
`g = c (c⁻¹ g c) c⁻¹` lies in `F`. `cocharacter_comp_eq_of_reflection` uses the
perfect pairing. For every character `χ`, `⟨χ, ζ ≫ σ⟩ = ⟨σ ≫ χ, ζ⟩`, and
`σ ≫ χ = P.reflection j χ = χ - ⟨χ, α^∨⟩ α`, which pairs with `ζ` as `χ` does.
`constantPoint_mem_centralizer` rewrites the dynamic centralizer as the torus
centralizer of `ζ` (`torusCentralizer_gm_eq_dynamicCentralizer`). Conjugation
by `n` sends `ζ ≫ i` to `(ζ ≫ σ) ≫ i = ζ ≫ i`, and the base point pulls back to
every test scheme. `exists_normalizer_reflection` takes the normalizing point
supplied by `IsRootDatumOf` and identifies its Weyl action through
`weylAction_eq`.

Levi subgroups. `range_le_of_dynamicFactors` forms the intersection
`subgroupIntersectionFst z q`, a closed subgroup of `Z` whose range is the
preimage of the range of `q`. The dynamic big-cell criterion
`Dynamic.isIso_of_contains_dynamicFactors` makes it an isomorphism, so that
preimage is everything. `unipotent_le_comap_of_support` identifies the dynamic
unipotent subgroup of `ζ` in `Z` with the semigroup subgroup of the positive
characters of `ζ` (`RelativeRoot.dynamicSemigroupComparison`). It shrinks this
to the semigroup of positive multiples of `b`, using only the weights of `Z`
(`RelativeRoot.semigroupSubgroup_mono_on_weightSupport` and
`RelativeRoot.mem_actionWeightSupport_conjugation_iff`). It then maps it into
`G` (`RelativeRoot.semigroupSubgroup_map_le`), where it is the root subgroup of
`b`. `centralizer_le_range_of_simple` takes `Z` to be the representing scheme
of the centralizer of `ζ`. This is a smooth (`dynamicSmooth`), connected
(`DynamicConnected.connectedSpace_of_isRepresentedBy_centralizer`), closed
subgroup with torus factor `t`. It applies `range_le_of_dynamicFactors` to the
cocharacter `ν` of `Z`. The roots of `t` are roots of `i` that pair to zero with
`ζ` (`relativeRoots_subset_of_factor`,
`relativeRoot_pairing_eq_zero_of_le_dynamicCentralizer`). By `hdep` and
`mem_cyclicTail_of_not_linearIndependent`, those positive on `ν` are positive
multiples of `a`, and those negative on `ν` are positive multiples of `-a`. So
`unipotent_le_comap_of_support` handles both unipotent factors with `hpos` and
`hneg`. The opposite cocharacter is `-ν` by
`groupHom_neg_comp_eq_oppositeCocharacter`. The centralizer of `ν` in `Z` maps
into the centralizer of `ν` in `G` (`Dynamic.centralizer_le_comap`), and `hZ`
applies.

Main theorem. Let `R` be the range of `q`. For each simple `P.root j`,
`exists_normalizer_reflection` with the Levi cocharacter of `P.root j` gives a
normalizing point acting as `P.reflection j`. Its constant points lie in the
centralizer of that cocharacter, hence in `R` by `leviCentralizer_le_range`.
`positive_induction` is applied to the property
`RelativeRoot.subgroup i χ ≤ R ∧ RelativeRoot.subgroup i (-χ) ≤ R`. Simple roots
satisfy it by `hpos` and `hneg`. Doubles satisfy it by `subgroup_two_nsmul_le`
(with `-(2 • a) = 2 • (-a)`). Reflections preserve it by
`subgroup_weylAction_le`, since `P.reflection j (-χ) = -P.reflection j χ`.
Hence `R` contains the root subgroup of every positive root and of its
negative. `unipotent_le_of_disjoint_positive_rays` with `disjoint_cyclicTail`
gives `Dynamic.unipotent (groupHom ν ≫ i) ≤ R`. Applied to `-ν`, whose
indivisible positive roots are negatives of positive roots for `ν`, it gives
`Dynamic.unipotent (groupHom (-ν) ≫ i) ≤ R`. With `hZ`, the criterion
`Dynamic.isIso_of_contains_dynamicFactors` gives `IsIso q`.

The all-test radical generation theorem repeats the proof of the Library's
rational-point version. A semigroup realization of the positive characters of
`ν` has ordered products of the root-ray blocks
(`hasOrderedSemigroupProducts_of_unipotent`). The block of an indivisible root
`a` is the root subgroup of `a` (`cyclicRootFunctorComparison`). So every point
of the dynamic unipotent subgroup, on any test scheme, is an ordered product of
points of these root subgroups.

## Relation to the literature

Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017), Proposition 21.62 (printed p.443, PDF p.459),
states the following for a split reductive group `(G, T)` with a base `Δ`. `G`
is generated by the algebraic subgroups `G^α` (generated by `T` and `U_{±α}`) for
`α ∈ Δ`. In the reductive case the centralizer of a generic `ν` is `T` (no
root pairs to zero with `ν`). This identification is not used in Lean. With it,
`isIso_of_simpleRootLevis` is a closed-subgroup form of that statement. The
centralizer of `ν` replaces `T` because `G` is not assumed reductive here. The
proof follows Milne's outline: Weyl representatives in the simple-root Levi
subgroups, then their conjugates of the simple root groups. It replaces Milne's
Lie-algebra argument and his appeal to `W · Δ = Φ` (C.30). In their place it
uses the height induction above, which also covers nonreduced root systems, and
it ends with radical generation and the dynamic big cell instead of 21.49. The
Weyl representatives come from the hypothesis `IsRootDatumOf i P` rather than
from 21.11(d). They lie in the Levi subgroups by
`constantPoint_mem_centralizer`. The official errata snapshot of 2026-11-02 has
no entry for Chapter 21 at p.443 or for Proposition 21.62.
