# Independence of actual Tits indices from compatible choices: proof

Proof author: Worker 149 (Task 5622).

This module proves `Langlands.GroupScheme.TitsIndexIndependenceStatement` (module
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Reductive.TitsIndexChoiceStatement`). The theorem is
`Langlands.GroupScheme.titsIndexIndependence : TitsIndexIndependenceStatement.{u}`. It
has the single universe parameter `u` and adds no hypotheses. Declaration inspection
reports only the axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. The module
uses one helper module, `Langlands.GroupScheme.Reductive.TitsIndexCentralizerConjugacy`,
which has its own Markdown.

Only the independence half of `TitsIndexChoiceStatement` is proved here. The
existence half, `TitsIndexExistenceStatement`, is not addressed.

## The statement

Let `k : Type u` be a field. Let `G` be a group scheme over `Spec k` with `IsAffineHom`
and `LocallyOfFiniteType` structure map. Let `d e : TitsIndexData G` be two compatible
choices (`Langlands.GroupScheme.Reductive.TitsIndex`). Each choice consists of:

- a Galois pinning `pinning` over the separable closure `k_s`, with maximal torus `T`;
- a maximal split torus `S → G` over `k`, with a map `torusMap : S → T` satisfying
  `square`;
- a generic cocharacter `λ` of `S`, whose pairing with the restriction to `S` of
  every absolute root with nonzero restriction has the sign of that root's positivity
  (`compatible`).

The conclusion is `Nonempty (TitsIndexEquiv d e)`. Such an equivalence is a bijection
of simple vertices that preserves the integral Cartan pairings, the set `Δ₀` of
vertices with zero restriction, and the star action of every
`σ ∈ Gal(k_s/k)`. Tits states that the index does not depend on these choices
(literature id `tits-1966-classification-algebraic-semisimple`, §2.3, printed
pp.38–39). The Lean proof below is a direct argument from published Library theorems.

## Route

Write `S_d, S_e` for the split tori, `T_d, T_e` for the pinned maximal tori over
`k_s`, and `λ_d, λ_e` for the positive cocharacters. `res_d(a)` is
`d.rootRestriction a`, the restriction of the absolute root `a` to `S_d`.

1. Conjugate `S_d` onto `S_e` over `k`, putting the transported `λ_d` in the closed
   dominant chamber of `λ_e`.
2. Over `k_s`, conjugate `T_d` onto `T_e` by a point compatible with step 1, using
   conjugacy inside the centralizer of `S_e`.
3. The induced root-datum equivalence `e₀` transports restrictions along step 1. It
   therefore preserves zero restriction, and by dominance and genericity it preserves
   positivity of every root with nonzero restriction.
4. Correct `e₀` by an element `w` of the subgroup `W_s` generated by reflections in
   the roots of `e` with zero restriction. Then `w ∘ e₀` preserves positivity of all
   roots, and restrictions are unchanged.
5. Realize `w` by a normalizer point. The resulting conjugation carries base to base
   and zero restriction to zero restriction. The published star-action intertwining
   gives Galois equivariance.

### Section `RootData`

- `map_weightMap_of_mem_reflectionSubgroup`: let `r : M →+ Z` kill `P.root i` for
  every `i ∈ s`. Then `r (w.weightMap x) = r x` for every `w` in
  `Langlands.RootDatum.reflectionSubgroup P s`
  (`Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.LeviWeylLattice`). The proof is induction on the
  subgroup closure, using `RootPairing.reflection_apply` for a generating reflection.

### Section `Pinnings` (split pinnings over a field `K`)

- `exists_reflection_comp_isPos`: the inputs are split pinnings `p` of `G` and `q` of
  `H`, an equivalence `e₀` of their root data, and a set `s` of roots of `q`. Suppose
  that for every `w ∈ W_s`, positivity is matched (`q.base.IsPos (w (e₀ j)) ↔
  p.base.IsPos j`) at every `j` with `w (e₀ j) ∉ s`. Then some `w ∈ W_s` makes
  `w ∘ e₀` match positivity at every root. Proof:
  - Transport the positive cocharacter of `p` by `e₀`. Its pairing with every root
    of `q` is nonzero, since the cocharacter of `p` is regular
    (`SplitPinning.latticePairing_root_positiveCocharacter_ne_zero`).
  - `Langlands.LinearAlgebra.RootDatumWeylChambers.exists_same_signs` with the set
    `s` gives `u ∈ W_s` such that, at each root in `s`, the transported cocharacter
    has the same sign as the positive cocharacter of `q`. Finiteness comes from
    `SplitPinning.finite_rootDatum_weylGroup`.
  - Take `w = u⁻¹`. At roots in `s`, positivity matches by
    `SplitPinning.latticePairing_root_positiveCocharacter_pos_iff`. At the other
    roots it matches by hypothesis.
- `exists_conj_comp_weyl`: suppose `g` conjugates the torus of `p` onto that of `q`
  through `φ`, and `e₀` has weight map `χ ↦ φ⁻¹ ≫ χ`. For each `w` in the Weyl group
  of `q`, some point `g'` and isomorphism `φ'` satisfy the same relations for
  `w ∘ e₀`. The published `weylGroup` (`Langlands.GroupScheme.Reductive.RootDatumWeylProof`)
  identifies the Weyl group with `weylImage`. So `w` is the action of a normalizer
  point `n`, and `g' = n * g`, `φ' = φ ≪≫ normalizingAut n`
  (`isNormalizingPoint_normalizingAut`, `Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.Maximal`;
  `StarActionIndependence.conj_mul`).
- `exists_equiv_of_conj`: a conjugation `g` of the torus of `p` onto that of `q`
  through `φ` induces an equivalence of root data with weight map `χ ↦ φ⁻¹ ≫ χ`. It
  is `rootDatumEquivOfTorusCompatibleIso` and its `_weightMap` lemma
  (`Langlands.GroupScheme.Reductive.GeometricRootDatumComparison`), applied to
  `g⁻¹` via `StarActionIndependence.conj_inv`.

### Section `Cancel`

- `groupHom_cancel_mono`: two homomorphisms `x y : C ⟶ C'` of commutative group
  schemes are equal if they agree after composing with a subgroup inclusion
  `j : C'.toGrp ⟶ H` whose underlying morphism is a monomorphism.

### Section `Data` (two compatible choices `d e : TitsIndexData G`)

- `exists_rational_conj` (step 1). `smoothMaximalSplitTorusConjugacy`
  (`Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.SmoothMaximalSplitTorusConjugacyProof`) gives
  `g₁ ∈ G(k)` conjugating `S_d` onto `S_e`. `relativeRootSystem`
  (`Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.RelativeRootSystemProof`) gives
  `HasRelativeRootSystem` for `S_e`. Then
  `HasRelativeRootSystem.exists_normalizerPoint_dominant`
  (`Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.DominantChamberMove`) gives a normalizer point `n`
  of `S_e` with `0 ≤ ⟨α, (λ_d transported)⟩` for every
  `α ∈ positiveRelativeRoots S_e λ_e`. Output: `m = n * g₁` and `ψ : S_d ≅ S_e` with
  `S_d ≫ inn(m) = ψ ≫ S_e` and the dominance inequality for `λ_d ≫ ψ`.
- `exists_conj_data` (step 2). Base change `m` to `k_s`
  (`StarActionIndependence.IsConjugatingPoint.baseChangeBasePoint`). The conjugate of
  `T_d` by `m` is a maximal split torus of `G_{k_s}` (`IsMaximalTorus.comp_iso`,
  `Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.MaximalTransport`; `IsMaximalTorus.isMaximalSplitTorus`,
  `Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.MaximalSplitComparison`), and so is `T_e`. Both contain
  the base change of `S_e`: for `T_e` by `e.square`, and for the conjugate of `T_d` by
  `d.square` and step 1. The base change of `S_e` is split
  (`IsSplitTorus.baseChange`). So the helper theorem
  `exists_centralizing_conj_of_isMaximalSplitTorus` gives `z ∈ G(k_s)` centralizing
  `S_e` and conjugating the conjugate of `T_d` onto `T_e` through some `φ₀`. Output:
  `g₀ = z * m` (`innerIso_mul_hom`) and `φ₀ : T_d ≅ T_e` with
  `(torusMap_d)_{k_s} ≫ φ₀ = (ψ ≫ torusMap_e)_{k_s}`. The proof of this last equation
  composes with the monomorphic inclusion of `T_e` (`groupHom_cancel_mono`) and uses
  that `z` centralizes `S_e`.
- `restriction_transport` and `latticePairing_transport` (step 3). From the last
  equation, for every character `χ` of `T_d` the restriction of `φ₀⁻¹ ≫ χ` to `S_e`
  is `ψ⁻¹ ≫` (the restriction of `χ` to `S_d`). Restrictions are computed with
  `characterLattice.baseChangeEquiv`, `characterLattice.baseChangeEquiv_apply` and
  `characterLattice.baseChange_precomp`
  (`Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.SplitLatticeBaseChange`). The integral pairing
  satisfies `⟨ψ⁻¹ ≫ x, ν ≫ ψ⟩ = ⟨x, ν⟩` (`latticePairing_apply`,
  `Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.Lattice`).
- `rootRestriction_mem_relativeRoots`: a nonzero restriction of an absolute root is
  a relative root. It uses `mem_roots_iff_exists_splittingField_restriction`
  (`Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.AbsoluteRootRestriction`) with `d.square`.
- `isPos_iff_of_dominant` (signs). Suppose `res_e(j) = ψ⁻¹ ≫ res_d(a) ≠ 0` and the
  dominance inequality of step 1 holds. Then `j` is positive for `e` iff `a` is
  positive for `d`. Write `β = res_e(j)`.
  - `β` is a relative root of `S_e`.
  - `⟨β, λ_d ≫ ψ⟩ = ⟨res_d(a), λ_d⟩`, which is nonzero by `d.generic`.
  - By `compatible`, it suffices to show `⟨β, λ_e⟩ > 0 ↔ ⟨β, λ_d ≫ ψ⟩ > 0`.
  - Forward: `β` is then a positive relative root. Dominance gives
    `⟨β, λ_d ≫ ψ⟩ ≥ 0`, and the pairing is nonzero.
  - Backward: if `⟨β, λ_e⟩ ≤ 0`, then it is `< 0` by `e.generic`. Hence `−β` is a
    positive relative root (`HasRelativeRootSystem.neg_mem_relativeRoots`,
    `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.RelativeRootStandardCocharacter`), and dominance
    contradicts `⟨β, λ_d ≫ ψ⟩ > 0`.
- `exists_based_conj` (steps 3–5).
  - `exists_equiv_of_conj` gives `e₀` from `g₀`. Restriction transport gives
    `res_e(e₀ a) = ψ⁻¹ ≫ res_d(a)`, using
    `RootPairing.Hom.root_weightMap_apply`.
  - Let `s` be the roots of `e` with zero restriction. The restriction map to `S_e` is
    additive and kills the roots in `s`. By `map_weightMap_of_mem_reflectionSubgroup`
    it is invariant under `W_s`, so `res_e(w (e₀ a)) = res_e(e₀ a)` for `w ∈ W_s`.
  - Consequently, for `w ∈ W_s` and `w (e₀ a) ∉ s`, `isPos_iff_of_dominant` matches
    positivity. This is the hypothesis of `exists_reflection_comp_isPos`, which gives
    `w ∈ W_s` with `w ∘ e₀` matching positivity everywhere.
  - `Langlands.RootDatum.reflectionSubgroup_le_weylGroup` places `w` in the Weyl
    group. `exists_conj_comp_weyl` then gives `g`, `φ` and `ε = w ∘ e₀` with
    `T_d ≫ inn(g) = φ ≫ T_e` and weight map `χ ↦ φ⁻¹ ≫ χ`.
  - `ε` carries simple roots to simple roots by
    `StarActionIndependence.mem_support_iff_of_isPos`, because it matches positivity.
    It preserves zero restriction because `res_e(ε a) = ψ⁻¹ ≫ res_d(a)` and `ψ` is an
    isomorphism.

### Final theorem

`titsIndexIndependence` takes `g, φ, ε` from `exists_based_conj d e` and builds the
`TitsIndexEquiv`:

- `vertices` is `ε.indexEquiv` restricted to the simple roots;
- `cartan_eq` is `RootPairing.Hom.pairing ε.toHom`: equivalences preserve the integral
  pairing;
- `zero_iff` is the zero-restriction clause of `exists_based_conj`;
- `star_eq` follows from `StarActionIndependence.starAction_intertwine`
  (`Langlands.GroupScheme.Reductive.StarActionIndependenceProof`), applied to
  `d.pinning`, `e.pinning`, `g`, `φ`, `ε`. That theorem gives
  `ε ∘ d.starAction σ = e.starAction σ ∘ ε` for every `σ`, and `TitsIndexData.star`
  is `starAction` on indices.
