# Actual simple-root and torus generation of the graph

This internal support unit constructs the actual family of positive and negative simple-root maps and the common torus map in a `SimpleComparisonGraph`. Given the explicit ambient chamber-centralizer identity used in the preceding unit, it proves algebraic generation of that graph by this family on all scheme tests, over every field. Over an algebraically closed field it then proves density of the generated rational subgroup. This is generation of the constructed graph; generation of an arbitrary ambient reductive group by its simple root groups is a different result.

The universe of the field and schemes is arbitrary. No perfection, characteristic or root reducedness assumption is added. The main isomorphism proposition and the global Levi gluing proposition remain unproved here. The chamber-centralizer identity, or the dynamic-centralizer reductivity proposition in the supplied adapter, remains explicit.

## A closed-factorization criterion from the local cell

Let `K` be a smooth connected affine group over a field. Let `χ : Gm → K`, and let `m`, `z`, and `a` represent respectively the entire actual opposite unipotent functor, centralizer functor, and positive unipotent functor for `χ`. Let `f : K → H` be a group-scheme homomorphism to any group scheme over the same field, and let `q : Q → H` be an arbitrary closed subgroup inclusion. Assume that each of the composite factor maps `m ≫ f`, `z ≫ f`, and `a ≫ f` has all-test range in `range q`.

Then `f` factors through `q` as an actual morphism over the field. The published dynamic three-factor multiplication has dense image in `K`. The given range inclusions put its composite with `f` in `range q` on the cell itself as a scheme test, so that composite factors through `q`. The image of the local cell under `f` therefore lies in the closed underlying image of `q`. Continuity and density put the entire underlying image of `f` there. Smoothness makes `K` reduced, and the published `factorsThrough_of_range_subset` criterion upgrades this to an actual morphism factorization. The competitor `Q` is not assumed smooth or reduced. Neither algebraic closedness nor rational-point detection is used in this argument.

## Generation inside a graph envelope

An arbitrary `GraphEnvelope` is initially specified as a closed subgroup of the product of its two ambient groups generated by the paired local maps and the paired common-torus map. `GraphEnvelope.isGeneratedByLifts` shows that the actual local lifts and torus lift generate the graph as its own ambient group, with identity inclusion. A closed competitor inside the graph composes to a closed competitor in the product. The defining graph minimality gives range containment there, and the monic graph inclusion reflects that containment back to the graph. Thus the minimality remains against every closed competitor and on every scheme test.

## Constructed simple-root family

Take smooth affine groups `G1` and `G2`, the common torus object `T`, its homomorphisms `i1` and `i2`, and an actual `SimpleComparisonGraph D`. The record supplies splitness of `T`, a generic chamber for the actual roots of `i1`, the corresponding simple-root directions with their annihilation and rank-one properties, the actual local comparison groups, and the generated graph. As proved in `SplitLeviGluingSimpleGraph`, such a record is constructed from connectedness of `G1`, splitness, and exactly the existing local Levi gluing premise; these objects need not be additional data for the intended main theorem.

`rootFamilySources` and `rootFamilyMaps` construct the actual indexed family. Its index type is `Option (Sum D.SimpleIndex D.SimpleIndex)`. The `none` source is `T` with `torusLift`; the two `some` cases are the existing positive and negative root groups with their actual graph maps. Thus the torus is still present when the root family is empty. Every source is smooth, affine, and connected: for the torus this follows from splitness, and for the root groups it follows from the previously constructed intrinsic root representatives. `rootFamilyRationalPoints` is the subgroup of actual `Spec k` points generated by the union of the rational ranges of those maps.

## All-test algebraic generation

Assume in addition that `G1` is connected, that `i1` is closed, and that the dynamic centralizer of the ambient chosen chamber cocharacter equals `range i1`. Let `P1` be a root datum realizing the actual roots of `i1`. Under these hypotheses, `isGeneratedByRootFamily` proves `IsGeneratedByFamily rootFamilySources rootFamilyMaps (identity graph)`.

For an arbitrary closed competitor `q : Q → graph` containing the actual root and torus maps, consider each actual local Levi. The preceding unit constructs its root cell with the common torus as middle, using the explicit chamber-centralizer identity. The composites of its three factor maps with the local lift are precisely the graph negative-root map, torus lift, and positive-root map. All three lie in the competitor. The closed-factorization criterion above therefore puts the entire local lift in the competitor. The torus lift also factors through it by assumption. The graph-envelope generation theorem now finishes the required minimality. No algebraic generation hypothesis for this particular root family is assumed, and no assertion about rational-point generation is substituted for the all-test statement.
