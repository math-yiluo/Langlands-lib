# Ground isotypic representations on the actual anisotropic kernel

For a supplied Tits index over an arbitrary field, suppose that the
actual restrictions to the split and anisotropic kernel tori together
distinguish absolute characters. A maximal multiplicity-one weight of a
ground representation of the ambient group then restricts to a maximal
multiplicity-one weight of a ground representation of the chosen kernel.
Maximality here is in the order defined by positive combinations of the
simple roots of each group.

The kernel centralizes the maximal split torus, so it acts on the
corresponding ground character space. The proof takes split coordinates
of that space. Base change to the separable closure extends those
coordinates and gives the actual isotypic subspace for the restricted
character. Coordinate projection remains the identity on that subspace
by linearity. The representation on the extended coordinates agrees
with the base change of the ground representation by the affine
test-matrix formulas.

Inside the isotypic subspace, every kernel weight space lies in the sum
of the ambient weight spaces with the two required character
restrictions. Joint injectivity of the two restrictions identifies the
space at the restricted original weight with the original weight line.
A positive difference from the restricted weight lifts to a positive
ambient root combination supported on the zero vertices. Any ambient
weight contributing to the putative higher kernel weight therefore lies
above the original weight, and maximality forces equality. The
multiplicity-one space gives positive matrix size.

The construction uses the published split Levi comparison and
scalar-extension results. The injectivity premise is an integral character
calculation in the E6 branch-marked application.
