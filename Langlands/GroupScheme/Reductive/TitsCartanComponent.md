# Connected components of the actual absolute Cartan diagram

This internal interface uses the simple vertices and integral Cartan entries
of the published `TitsIndexData G`. It adds no group classification or arithmetic
assumption.

`TitsIndexData.CartanConnected d a b` is the equivalence relation generated by
the ordered pairs of distinct simple vertices with nonzero actual Cartan entry.
Its equivalence closure makes the construction independent of an orientation of
the edges. Thus it is connectivity in the underlying absolute Cartan diagram.
Diagonal entries do not create edges.

The reflexivity, symmetry and transitivity lemmas are the corresponding
constructors of `Relation.EqvGen`. Any nonzero Cartan entry connects its two
vertices: equal vertices use reflexivity, and distinct vertices give a generating
edge.

`CartanComponent d a` is the subtype of actual simple vertices connected to `a`.
`cartanComponentSelf d a` is its element with underlying vertex `a`.
No choice of a model root system or an abstract replacement matrix is involved.
The definitions apply at every universe parameter and in every field
characteristic.
