# Weyl normalization over arbitrary local rings

This internal construction assembles the pointed etale unit-cell data and the
original dual-number factorization and applies faithful normalization descent.
Its input is an affine reductive group over an arbitrary commutative local ring,
a closed fibrewise maximal split torus, its fibrewise root datum, an isolating
cocharacter and supplied opposite root parametrizations. The chosen unit
`normalizingScalar` gives the prescribed root reflection on the actual rank-one
centralizer torus. No reducedness is assumed.

The ambient-group theorem chooses the isolating cocharacter from the finite
reduced root datum, maps the normalizing point through the actual centralizer
inclusion and identifies the root-coroot reflection with the exponent reflection.
It proves existence of a unit rescaling for any supplied opposite root maps.
Existence of the root parametrizations themselves and the full Isomorphism
Theorem are separate results.
