# Faithfully flat restrictions on the actual graph unipotent factors

This internal support module constructs the four actual homomorphisms from the graph's positive and negative dynamic unipotent factors to the corresponding factors in each ambient group. Each homomorphism commutes with the given graph projection and the actual subgroup inclusions. Each is flat, surjective, locally of finite presentation and quasi-compact. Injectivity and isomorphism are not claimed.

The field is `k : Type u`, and all objects and morphisms are actual group schemes over `Spec k`. The two ambient datum index types may have independent universes `v` and `w`. Subgroup-functor containment means containment on every scheme test. Surjectivity of an underlying scheme morphism is not identified with surjectivity on points of every original test scheme.

## Restrictions and their closed images

`unipotentRestriction χ f a b ha hb` works over every field. Here `f : H ⟶ G` is a homomorphism, `a : A ⟶ H` represents the full `Dynamic.unipotent χ`, and `b : B ⟶ G` represents the full `Dynamic.unipotent (χ ≫ f)`. Dynamic functoriality puts the range of `a ≫ f` in the represented target functor, so the representability factorization gives an actual homomorphism `A ⟶ B`. Its composite with `b` is exactly `a ≫ f`, as proved by `unipotentRestriction_comp`. No isomorphism or geometric property of this restriction is assumed.

`unipotent_projection_geometry` is over an algebraically closed field. The ambient group `G` and the source `A` are smooth, connected and affine. It assumes a closed split torus inclusion `i`, an actual realizing root datum `P`, a generic chamber `ν`, and the explicit identity `Z_G(ν) = range i`. A closed inclusion `b : B ⟶ G` represents the full `U_G(ν)`. Given `p : A ⟶ B`, the range of `p ≫ b` must contain every full simple-root functor for `ν`. The result proves that `p` is flat, surjective, locally of finite presentation and quasi-compact. Smoothness of `B` is supplied by its dynamic representation rather than assumed separately.

The proof factors `p` through its smooth connected affine image `j : E ⟶ B`. The composite `j ≫ b` is a closed subgroup of `G` containing every simple-root functor. The companion `unipotent_le_range_of_simpleRoots_of_centralizer_eq` therefore puts all of `U_G(ν)` in its range. Its range also lies in `range b = U_G(ν)`, so `j ≫ b` and `b` represent the same entire subgroup functor. Their representing isomorphism is `j`, by cancellation with the target inclusion. Thus `j` is an isomorphism, and the faithfully flat geometry of the image quotient passes to `p`. The argument applies simple-root generation to the closed image in `G`; it does not apply a closed-subgroup theorem to an arbitrary projection range.

`UnipotentProjectionData p a ν i` records the actual map from the source of `a` to the canonical affine representative of `U_G(ν ≫ i)`, its composite equation with that canonical inclusion, and these four geometric properties. The general constructor `unipotentProjectionData` assumes the preceding smoothness, affineness, connectedness and algebraic-closedness hypotheses, a torus lift `t : T ⟶ H` satisfying `t ≫ p = i`, a full dynamic representation by `a`, and the same splitness, closedness, realizing datum, genericity, middle identity and simple-root composite-containment hypotheses. It constructs the actual restriction and applies the geometric criterion.

## Specialization to the actual graph

Let `D : SimpleComparisonGraph i₁ i₂` for smooth affine ambient groups `G₁`, `G₂`, and let `E : D.GraphDynamicFactors`. The graph record retains the actual split common torus, chamber, simple-root directions, local comparison isomorphisms and generated graph. The factor record supplies actual smooth affine connected negative and positive closure-envelope groups, whose closed inclusions represent the full graph unipotent factors. It is supplied as data here; the earlier `graphDynamicFactorsOfReductive` constructs it under its documented ambient and local hypotheses.

`GraphDynamicFactors.root_le_range_comp` is a factorization helper: a graph root map whose range lies in another closed graph subgroup factors through it. If its composite with an ambient projection represents a target subgroup functor, that full target functor is contained in the composite range of the larger graph subgroup and the projection. This is an all-test assertion.

`positiveProjectionData` and `negativeProjectionData` work for either actual graph projection, or another supplied homomorphism `p : graph ⟶ G` satisfying the same documented inputs. The target `G` is smooth affine connected and connected reductive, its common torus inclusion `i` is maximal, its actual root set agrees with the first ambient root set, and a datum `P` realizes it. The torus square must commute. The composite of each actual positive or negative graph simple-root map with `p` must represent the corresponding entire target root functor. Over the assumed algebraically closed field these results construct `UnipotentProjectionData` for the positive or negative factor, respectively.

They prove all inputs of the general restriction criterion. Equal actual root sets identify the simple roots for the chosen chamber and transfer genericity. The published centralizer-reductivity, maximal-torus-centralizer and generic-centralizer proofs identify the target middle factor with its torus; no structural centralizer statement remains an unproved premise. Full graph factor representation puts each actual graph simple-root map in the correct source factor. The composite representation then gives the required entire target root-functor containment. For the negative factor, `neg_mem_simpleRelativeRoots` proves that negating both root and chamber preserves simplicity, using actual root-set negation from `IsRootDatumOf`. Negating the chamber preserves the centralizer. The negative target is written using `-ν`; the source factor record uses the equivalent opposite cocharacter, with the published equality explicitly applied in the proof.

## Four actual maps

`GraphFactorProjections D E` contains positive-first, negative-first, positive-second and negative-second `UnipotentProjectionData`. The primary constructor is
`graphFactorProjectionsOfReductive D E P₁ P₂ hP₁ hP₂ hroots hG₁ hG₂ hmax₁ hmax₂`.
Its assumptions are: an algebraically closed field; smooth affine ambient groups with the actual graph `D`; the actual full factor record `E`; realizing root data for both ambient torus inclusions; equality of their actual root sets; connected reductivity of both ambient groups; and maximality of both torus inclusions. Connectedness of the ambient groups is obtained from reductivity. It applies the two preceding constructors to the actual first and second projections, their proved torus squares, and the actual graph simple-root composite representations. It adds no global isomorphism, kernel-triviality or factor-invertibility premise.

The provenance is internal: dynamic functoriality and representability construct the maps; smooth homomorphism images, the companion ambient simple-root unipotent generation theorem, and the existing structural centralizer proofs supply their quotient geometry. The constructions do not use the common-torus isomorphism target or a downstream classification result.

Injectivity of these restrictions, their promotion to factor isomorphisms, invertibility of the graph projections, exact Levi gluing and the exact arbitrary-field common-torus theorem remain separate obligations.
