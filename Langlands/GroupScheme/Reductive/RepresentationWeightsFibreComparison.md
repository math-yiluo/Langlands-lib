# Full root-data comparison from actual integral representation weights

This internal unit compares full root data on two actual field fibres of one smooth affine group over a commutative local ring. Its construction `CoordinateRootDatum.equivOfRepresentation` returns a `RootPairing.Equiv` between the supplied coordinate root data. Both lattice maps are identities on the entire integral coordinate lattices, including central directions.

Let `R : Type u` be a commutative local ring, let `I : Type u` be a finite coordinate index type with decidable equality, and let `i : splitTorus (Spec R) I ⟶ G` be a morphism into a smooth affine group scheme. Supply a finite free matrix representation `ρ` on `Fin N`, and assume that the underlying scheme map of `i` followed by `ρ` is a monomorphism. Supply two fields `k,l : Type u` with their original `R`-algebra structures and root data `P,Q` on the entire lattices `I → ℤ`. Their root-index types may lie in independent universes `v,w`. The two hypotheses assert that `fieldRealization k P` and `fieldRealization l Q` satisfy `IsRootDatumOf` for the actual base changes of `i`, using the canonical split-torus identifications.

The construction needs no root-spanning, finite-root-index, reducedness, reductivity, algebraic-closure, domain or flatness hypothesis beyond these supplied data. The supplied field algebra forces `R` to be nontrivial. The monomorphism assumption concerns precisely the torus-representation composite; no separate faithfulness assertion about the entire representation is required.

Realization of a full coordinate root datum intertwines its reflections with the actual character-lattice reflections. If the realization satisfies `IsRootDatumOf`, its pairing is the integer dot product and its coordinate root range is exactly the set of integral exponents of actual roots. For each root, `IsRootDatumOf` supplies an actual normalizing point and torus automorphism realizing the reflection. The result `NormalizerRepresentationWeights.weight_ne_zero_exponentAut` shows that these points preserve every occurring representation weight. The realization formula identifies that exponent automorphism with the supplied coordinate reflection, proving `weight_ne_zero_reflection`. This field argument requires a supplied representation, without the monomorphism hypothesis.

Over the local base, `mapsTo_weights_of_field` transports reflection stability from a field fibre to the actual integral representation support by `SplitTorusRepresentationWeights.weight_baseChange_ne_zero_iff`. Both the representation and the torus homomorphism are the actual pullbacks; the general-map helper `baseChangeHomAlong` agrees definitionally with the algebra-base-change `baseChangeHom` used here. Thus both reflection families preserve the same set of original integral exponent vectors, rather than sets merely having equal cardinality.

The two full pairings agree because both are the integer dot product, as recorded in `pairing_eq_of_fields`. The result `rootRange_eq_of_fields` uses `splitTorus.mem_roots_fieldBaseChange_iff_of_isLocalRing` to identify the actual adjoint roots on the two field fibres. Smoothness and affineness are used for that published local root-support comparison. The representation support is finite by `finite_weights` and generates the entire integral coordinate lattice by `closure_weights_eq_top` under the supplied monomorphism assumption. These facts feed `Langlands.LinearAlgebra.rootDatumEquivOfCommonGenerators`, whose finite stabilizer and integral reflection argument identify the full coroots as well as the roots.

The resulting equivalence has identity weight and contravariant coweight maps, with explicit formulas `equivOfRepresentation_weightMap` and `equivOfRepresentation_coweightMap`. Its root-index equivalence carries each root and coroot of `P` to the identical vector in `Q`, as proved by `equivOfRepresentation_root` and `equivOfRepresentation_coroot`. The derived `fieldEquivOfRepresentation` composes this full coordinate equivalence with the canonical realization equivalences to compare the two actual realized root data.

The provenance is internal: the proof combines the published realization, actual-normalizer and local adjoint-support APIs with the actual representation projector/support results and the common finite-generator comparison. It invokes no external classification result. It assumes the supplied integral representation and actual field root data, and supplies neither their existence nor an integral group-scheme isomorphism. The host `StrictHenselianGenericIsoStatement` remains a separate proof obligation.
