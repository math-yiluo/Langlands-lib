# Inverses of concrete pinned comparisons

These internal group and category consequences show that the actual comparison
predicates are closed under inversion. The torus square uses the inverse of its
supplied coordinate-torus isomorphism. The cocharacter equation is cancelled
against the comparison. On each local affine test the bijection on simple
vectors is inverted, and the original vector equation is postcomposed with the
inverse group isomorphism. The coefficient structures and actual points remain
those of the original test.

Consequently `IsLocallyPinned` is closed under inversion. For
`IsFppfLocallyPinned`, the same fppf cover works, since pulling back the inverse
comparison gives the inverse of the pulled-back comparison. These closure laws
do not assert existence or uniqueness of normalized comparisons.
