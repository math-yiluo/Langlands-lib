# Relative parabolic intersections in a central product

This internal construction restricts an actual supplied central product isogeny to a relative parabolic over an arbitrary base scheme. The central factor is contained in every relative parabolic: on an etale-local dynamic presentation it lies in the dynamic centralizer, hence in the parabolic, and the published etale-cover containment theorem descends this inclusion.

For a reductive normal closed factor, the actual scheme-theoretic intersection is a relative parabolic. Its affineness and monicity follow from closedness of the first projection, and its relative smoothness is the arbitrary-base normal intersection smoothness theorem. On residue fibres, the actual product base-change comparison transports the supplied central isogeny to the product of the two fibres. The published field central-product intersection theorem then proves parabolicity. The canonical pullback/intersection isomorphism transports this statement to the exact residue fibre of the global first projection.

`relativeIntersectionFactor` is the actual central multiplication restricted to this intersection and factored through the supplied relative parabolic. Its composite with the parabolic inclusion is the displayed multiplication map. It is a central isogeny whenever the original central product is, by the published cartesian restriction theorem. Constructing this factor itself requires no reductivity or normality of the second factor; those hypotheses enter the intersection-parabolicity result. No noetherian or field hypothesis is added to the global base. This proves the forward parabolic clause conditional on the supplied global central product; it does not assert the full derived correspondence or the inverse construction.
