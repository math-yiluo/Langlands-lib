# Actual rank-one root factors and their local cells

This internal support unit works over any field `k`, in arbitrary universes. It identifies both chamber unipotent factors of each actual simple-root Levi with its full intrinsic root functors, and constructs the corresponding local three-factor cell. No root reducedness or characteristic restriction is imposed. The main isomorphism proposition and the global Levi gluing proposition remain open.

## Positive roots on a simple ray

For a finite family `v` of elements of an additive commutative group, assume integral linear independence. A nonzero element `x` of the nonnegative additive monoid generated by that family, integrally dependent with one generator `v a`, is a strictly positive natural-number multiple of `v a`. The proof expands `x` with natural coefficients. An integral dependence relation and coefficient uniqueness force every coefficient away from `a` to vanish. The remaining coefficient is positive because `x` is nonzero. This lemma assumes neither a basis of the whole group nor any divisibility property.

For the group-scheme specialization, assume that `G` is affine and locally of finite type, that `T` is split, that `i : T → G` has an actual realizing root datum `P`, and that `ν` is a generic cocharacter. An actual positive root dependent with a simple root `a` is a positive integer multiple of `a`. This follows from the preceding lemma, the integral independence of the intrinsic simple roots, and the equality between the additive monoids generated by positive roots and by simple roots proved in `SplitLeviGluingSimpleRoots`. The multiple need not be one.

## Equality with local dynamic factors

A general auxiliary criterion takes a smooth connected affine group `L`, a homomorphism `t : T → L` from a split torus, a cocharacter `ch`, and a character `α` with strictly positive pairing with `ch`. Assume that every actual root of `t` with positive pairing is a positive integral multiple of `α`. Then the entire subgroup functor `Dynamic.unipotent (ch → T → L)` equals the intrinsic root functor `RelativeRoot.subgroup t α`.

The proof uses the published dynamic-to-semigroup comparison. On actual roots, the positive pairing semigroup has exactly the same membership as the first cyclic tail of `α`. Neither semigroup contains zero. The published comparison on actual root support therefore identifies their generated subgroup functors, and the published first cyclic-tail theorem identifies the latter with the intrinsic root functor. The result is equality on every scheme test, including nonreduced tests.

For the actual local specialization, retain the affine, locally finite type `G`, split `T`, realizing `P`, and generic `ν` above. Let `a` be an intrinsic simple root. Let `ζ` satisfy the exact rank-one direction predicate for `i`, and assume that `a` pairs to zero with `ζ`. Let `z : L → G` represent the full dynamic centralizer of `ζ`, let `L` be smooth connected affine, and let `t : T → L` satisfy `t ≫ z = i`. No connected-reductivity assumption on `L` is needed.

Every actual root of `t` is an ambient root annihilating `ζ`, by the published represented-factor and centralizer-root-support lemmas. The rank-one direction predicate makes it dependent with `a`. The positive-ray result now identifies `U_L(ν)` with `U_a`. Applying that result to the negative of a root, using the symmetry of the roots supplied by `P`, identifies `U_L(-ν)` with `U_{-a}`. This proves both full dynamic-factor equalities without assuming that the ambient root system is reduced.

## Constructed local root cells

Take smooth affine groups `G1` and `G2`, a common torus `T`, its homomorphisms `i1` and `i2`, and an actual `SimpleComparisonGraph D`. The record `D` supplies splitness of `T`, its chosen generic chamber for `i1`, the actual simple-root kernel directions with their annihilation and rank-one properties, the actual local Levi comparisons, and the generated graph. As proved in `SplitLeviGluingSimpleGraph`, such a `D` is constructed from splitness, connectedness of `G1`, and precisely the published local Levi gluing premise; it is not an additional input to the final intended isomorphism theorem.

Assume `G1` is connected and `P1` is a root datum realizing the actual roots of `i1`. For each simple root `a`, `local_positive_rep` and `local_negative_rep` apply the preceding equalities inside the left local comparison. The existing positive and negative root representatives therefore represent both entire local chamber dynamic factors. Smoothness, connectedness, and affineness of that local comparison follow from its actual centralizer representation and the hypotheses on `G1`.

`SimpleComparisonGraph.RankOneRootCell` stores the actual middle group, its closed inclusion representing the local chamber centralizer, and the two dynamic representation equations for the already constructed positive and negative root groups. The definition `SimpleComparisonGraph.rankOneRootCell` constructs this record with the canonical affine dynamic centralizer as its middle group. Neither its representative nor the endpoint groups are supplied as new choices among the constructor's inputs.

The published three-factor cell theorems give an open immersion and dense image for multiplication of the negative root group, this middle centralizer, and the positive root group into the actual local Levi. Over an algebraically closed field, the published rational three-factor density theorem then states that every rational point of that Levi lies in the rational-point closure of those products. Algebraic closedness is used only in this final rational-point conclusion.

This unit does not identify the middle centralizer with the common torus, transport the closure statement into the global graph, establish algebraic generation by simple roots, or prove either graph projection invertible. These remain separate obligations. All results are internal consequences of the named published interfaces and the earlier simple-root support units; no classification or global isomorphism result is used.
