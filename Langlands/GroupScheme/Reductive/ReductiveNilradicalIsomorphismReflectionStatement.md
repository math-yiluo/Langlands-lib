# Reductive isomorphisms and the nilradical quotient

`ReductiveNilradicalIsomorphismReflectionStatement` is an explicit remaining
prerequisite: for arbitrary commutative local rings `R` and reductive affine
group schemes `G` and `H` over `Spec R`, an isomorphism of their actual
pullbacks to `R / nilradical R` implies existence of an isomorphism over `R`.
The statement is unproved in this module. It has no split-torus assumption and
asks only for existence, without prescribing the reduction of the isomorphism.
No nilpotence exponent for the full nilradical is assumed.

This is an internally selected independent deformation statement. Its purpose
is to isolate the input for a reduction to the already proved reduced local
isomorphism theorem. It neither assumes that local target nor supplies its own
proof. The companion conditional reduction checks that this exact premise is
sufficient.
