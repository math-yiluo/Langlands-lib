# Closed relative identity components over discrete valuation rings

This is an internal composition of published geometric results toward
`Langlands.GroupScheme.ConnectedFibreLocusClosedStatement`.

The first theorem proves that a monomorphism from a reductive group into a smooth
affine group over a discrete valuation ring is a closed immersion. It factors
the homomorphism through the schematic closure of its generic image. The
published smooth generic-closure theorem makes this factor an open immersion
into a smooth affine group. Its generic fibre is an isomorphism by the canonical
generic-closure comparison. The published étale local isomorphism criterion
then makes the factor an isomorphism, and composing with the closed closure
inclusion proves the result.

The second theorem identifies the geometric fibres of an affine smooth
geometrically connected relative identity-component representative with the
actual identity components of the ambient geometric fibres. Thus full
reductivity of the ambient identity-component locus makes the representative
reductive.

The final theorem applies the published affineness of relative identity
components over arbitrary discrete valuation rings and the first two theorems
to show that the representative inclusion is closed. These arguments use no
connected-fibre closedness assumption.
