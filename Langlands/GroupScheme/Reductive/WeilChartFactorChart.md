# Chart description of the fixed points of the diagonal factor projection

Namespace `Langlands.GroupScheme.WeilChart`. This internal construction is the seventh step of the
proof of `AffineWeilPairSingleChartDescentStatement`. Let `T` be an `A`-algebra with an algebra
map `φ : B → T` and a chart map `g : A' → T`. The points of `G` over `T` fixed by the diagonal
factor projection `diagProj e P φ g` (module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Reductive.WeilChartFactorDescent`)
are identified with the points of `H` over `T`, regarded as a `C`-algebra through the map
`e (a ⊗ b) ↦ g a * φ b`. This identification is used both for `G ≅ R_{B/A}(G')` and for
`G'_C ≅ H`.

## Kernels of multiplication maps

For an algebra map `ρ : B → X` over `R` with graph idempotent `ε ∈ B ⊗[R] X`, `ker_graphMul`
says that the kernel of the multiplication map `graphMul ρ : B ⊗[R] X → X` is the ideal generated
by `1 - ε`. `graphMul_surjective` says that this map is surjective. The argument is the same as
`WeilFactor.ker_mulMap` (module `WeilFactorUniquenessReduction`), stated for an arbitrary algebra map.

## The chart description

The test object is `T` over itself, `Under.mk (𝟙 T)`, with its `A'`-structure through `g`
(`underAlgebra`). The chart factor projections act on this object.

- `chartRho e φ g : C → T` is `diagMap e φ g` (module `WeilChartDiagonal`) composed with the
  identity of `T` regarded as this test object. `chartEps e φ g ∈ C ⊗[A'] T` is the image of the
  diagonal idempotent. It is a graph idempotent of `chartRho` (`chartEps_spec`), in particular an
  idempotent (`chartEps_isIdempotentElem`).
- `chartObj e φ g` is `T` as a `C`-algebra through `chartRho`. `chartMu e φ g` is the
  multiplication `C ⊗[A'] T → T`, `c ⊗ t ↦ chartRho c * t`, as a map of `C`-algebras. It is
  surjective (`chartMu_surjective`), its kernel is generated by `1 - chartEps` (`ker_chartMu`),
  and it sends `chartEps` to `1` (`chartMu_chartEps`).
- `chartFactorEquiv`: the points `h` of `H` over `C ⊗[A'] T` whose restriction to the factor
  `(C ⊗[A'] T)/(chartEps)` is trivial correspond to the points of `H` over `chartObj`. This is the
  Library's `IdempotentPoints.factorEquiv` (from the module
  `Langlands.GroupScheme.AffineIdempotentPoints`) for `chartMu`.
- `affinePointMap_chartMu_proj`: pushing forward along `chartMu` kills the factor projection,
  because `chartMu (chartEps) = 1`.
- `underPointsEquiv_diagProj`: through the chart point equivalence `underPointsEquiv`,
  `diagProj e P φ g` is the factor projection `IdempotentPoints.proj H (chartEps e φ g)`.
  `diagProj_eq_self_iff`: a point is fixed by `diagProj` exactly when its image under the chart
  point equivalence is trivial on the factor `(C ⊗[A'] T)/(chartEps)`.
- `fixedKerEquiv` and `chartFixedEquiv` (**chart description**): the group of points of `G` over
  `T` fixed by `diagProj e P φ g` is isomorphic to the group of points of `H` over `chartObj e φ g`.
  `chartFixedEquiv_apply` gives the formula `q ↦ H(chartMu)(underPointsEquiv q)`.
  `chartFixedEquiv_diagProj`: on a point of the form `diagProj e P φ g q`, the description equals
  `H(chartMu)(underPointsEquiv q)`. The projection disappears.

## Provenance

This is internal. In Conrad, *Reductive group schemes* (2014), proof of Proposition 6.4.4, the
descended group `G'` becomes the given group `H` over the étale cover. Through a chart, the
statement here is that the factor of `R_{C/A'}(H)` cut out by the graph idempotent of a
`C`-algebra structure is `H` itself.
