For a coalgebra map `q : B →ₗc[k] C`, where `k` is a field, `B` is any
`k`-coalgebra and `C` is a commutative `k`-bialgebra, `coaction q` is
`(id ⊗ q) Δ`. Its contraction by a linear functional on `C` is precisely
the original convolution action `regularAction q`. The criterion
`coaction_eq_tmul_one_iff` says that this tensor equals `b ⊗ 1` exactly
when every functional vanishing on `1` annihilates `b` under that action.

For the reverse implication, subtract the scalar multiple `ell(1)` of the
convolution unit from any functional `ell`. The resulting functional
vanishes on `1`, so its action is zero. Thus every functional acts on
`b` by the scalar `ell(1)`. The published tensor dual separation theorem
then identifies the coaction tensor. Neither coalgebra is assumed finite
dimensional.
