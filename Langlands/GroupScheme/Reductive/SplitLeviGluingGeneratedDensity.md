# Rational points of a generated group have dense locations

Author: Worker 712 (interface requested by Worker 1444).

This is a derived internal support module for the graph route to the
common-torus Levi gluing statement `SplitCommonTorusLeviGluingStatement`.
Over an algebraically closed field, suppose a family of homomorphisms generates
a group scheme `H` in the sense of `IsGeneratedByFamily`. The module shows that
the locations of the rational points generated by the rational points of the
family are Zariski dense in `H`. This discharges the explicit generated-location
density premise `hgen` of
`LeviGluing.dense_rationalPointImage_tripleProduct`
(`SplitLeviGluingRationalDensity`) once the family is known to generate. It is
generic: it does not prove that any particular family, such as root subgroups
of simple roots and the torus, generates a graph subgroup.

Throughout, `k : Type u` is a field, `H` is a group scheme over `Spec k`, and a
rational point of `H` is a morphism `Over.mk (𝟙 (Spec k)) ⟶ H.X` over `Spec k`.
`rationalPointImage H Γ` is the set of locations `x.left (closedPoint k)` of
the rational points `x ∈ Γ`.

## Declarations

- `RationalClosureEnvelope H Γ`: for a subgroup `Γ` of the rational points of
  `H`, a record of a group scheme `group`, a homomorphism
  `inclusion : group ⟶ H` that is a closed subgroup (`IsClosedSubgroup`),
  smoothness of `group` over `Spec k`, and the equality of the set-theoretic
  image of `inclusion` with `closure (rationalPointImage H Γ)`.
- `rationalClosureEnvelope H Γ`: for `k` algebraically closed and `H` affine
  and locally of finite type over `Spec k`, one such envelope. It is chosen
  from the published existence theorem `rationalPointClosureSubgroup`.
- `factorsThrough_of_range_subset hj g hg`: let `j : K ⟶ H` be a closed
  subgroup and `g : P ⟶ H.X` a morphism over `Spec k` from a scheme `P` whose
  underlying scheme is reduced. If the set-theoretic image of `g` lies in that
  of `j`, then `g` factors through `j` (`FactorsThrough g j`): there is an
  actual morphism `P ⟶ K.X` over `Spec k` whose composite with `j` is `g`.
- `range_subset_closure_rationalPointImage f S hS`: let `k` be algebraically
  closed and `f : P ⟶ H.X` a morphism over `Spec k` with `P` locally of finite
  type. Suppose `y ≫ f ∈ S` for every rational point `y` of `P`. Then the image
  of `f` lies in `closure (rationalPointImage H S)`.
- `generatedRationalPoints H A a`: for a family `a : ∀ j, A j ⟶ H`, the
  subgroup of rational points of `H` generated by the union over `j` of the
  rational points of `SubgroupFunctor.range (a j)`.
- `factorsThrough_rationalClosureEnvelope hA j`: let `k` be algebraically
  closed, `H` affine and locally of finite type, and every `A j` reduced and
  locally of finite type over `Spec k`. Then each `a j` factors through the
  inclusion of `rationalClosureEnvelope H (generatedRationalPoints H A a)`.
- `range_rationalClosureEnvelope_eq_univ hA hgen`: under the same hypotheses,
  suppose `hgen : IsGeneratedByFamily A a (𝟙 H)`. Then the inclusion of that
  envelope is surjective on underlying points.
- `dense_rationalPointImage_generatedRationalPoints hA hgen`: under the same
  hypotheses, `rationalPointImage H (generatedRationalPoints H A a)` is dense.
- `dense_rationalPointImage_closure_of_isGeneratedByFamily hA hgen`: the
  specialization in the form requested for the gluing proof. Here `k` is
  algebraically closed, `H` is smooth and affine, each `A j` is smooth, affine
  and connected (`hA`), and `IsGeneratedByFamily A a (𝟙 H)` holds. The set
  `Subgroup.closure (⋃ j, range (a j) at rational points)` then has dense
  locations. Only smoothness of each `A j` is used, through reducedness and
  finite type. The affine and connected parts of `hA` are not used.

## Proofs

`factorsThrough_of_range_subset`. Let `p` be the first projection of the
pullback of the closed immersion underlying `j` along `g.left`. It is a closed
immersion, and its image is the preimage under `g.left` of the image of `j`
(`Scheme.Pullback.range_fst`), which is everything. A surjective closed
immersion onto a reduced scheme is an isomorphism
(`isIso_of_isClosedImmersion_of_surjective`). So `inv p` followed by the second
projection is a morphism `P.left ⟶ K.X.left` whose composite with `j` is
`g.left`. It commutes with the structure morphisms because `g` and `j` do. This
is an equality of actual morphisms, so it holds on every test scheme.

`range_subset_closure_rationalPointImage`. `P.left` is locally of finite type
over a field, so it is a Jacobson space and its closed points are dense
(`closure_closedPoints`). A closed point `x` is the location of the rational
point `pointOfClosedPoint P.hom x`, because `k` is algebraically closed. Its
composite with `f` lies in `S`, and its location is `f.left x`. So `f.left`
maps the closed points into `rationalPointImage H S`. By continuity it maps
their closure, the whole space, into the closure of that set.

`factorsThrough_rationalClosureEnvelope`. For a rational point `y` of `A j`,
`y ≫ (a j).hom.hom` lies in the range of `a j`, so it lies in the generated
group. By the previous theorem the image of `a j` lies in the closure of the
generated locations, which is the image of the envelope's inclusion. The
source `A j` is reduced, so `factorsThrough_of_range_subset` applies.

`range_rationalClosureEnvelope_eq_univ`. The envelope inclusion is a closed
subgroup through which every `a j` factors. The minimality clause of
`IsGeneratedByFamily A a (𝟙 H)` then gives
`range (𝟙 H) ≤ range (inclusion)` on all tests. On the test `H.X` with the
identity point, this gives a morphism `g : H.X ⟶ group.X` with
`g ≫ inclusion = 𝟙`. Hence every point `x` of `H` is the image of `g.left x`.

`dense_rationalPointImage_generatedRationalPoints` rewrites density as the
closure being everything, and that closure is the image of the envelope
inclusion. The smooth specialization obtains reducedness of each smooth `A j`
from `SmoothReducedProof.isReduced_of_smooth_over_field`, and finite type from
smoothness. The expanded set in its statement is
`generatedRationalPoints H A a` by definition.

## Provenance

The provenance is internal. The module combines the published
`rationalPointClosureSubgroup` (Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017), Lemma 1.40,
Theorem 1.45 and Proposition 1.26(b), as recorded in
`Absolute.RationalPointClosureStatement`) with the Library's
`IsGeneratedByFamily` interface and Mathlib's Jacobson-space,
algebraically-closed-field point and closed-immersion results. It supplies the
last step of the proof of Milne's 23.22 (printed p.491, PDF p.507,
source `milne-2017-algebraic-groups`). There, `C̄ = G''` follows from `C̄` being
stable under the generators `U''_α`, `U''_{-α}`, `T''`, because those subgroups
generate `G''`. Milne works over an algebraically closed field, where a
subgroup variety is determined by its rational points. Here that step is the
passage from algebraic generation to density of the generated rational
points.
