# Simultaneous local Borel and torus normalization

Every actual comparison of supplied affine pinned models admits an etale cover
on which an actual section of the target group corrects it to preserve both the
canonical Borels and coordinate tori. The common scheme is arbitrary.

The proof first uses canonical-Borel normalization. On each cover member it
applies maximal-torus conjugacy inside the aligned target Borel, retaining the
Borel square. It binds the resulting covers and multiplies the two actual
conjugators in the order prescribed by the inner-automorphism composition law.

The normalized pullback of a group section is its actual pulled-back section
followed by the inverse base-change composition isomorphism. Naturality of inner
conjugation identifies its inner automorphism with the normalized comparison
pullback. The existence of a simultaneous correction respects the explicit
direct-versus-iterated pullback comparison, so the composed cover supplies
corrections of the original comparison along its total cover maps.

This is an internal consequence of the preceding Borel and torus normalization
constructions and the published pullback and inner-automorphism coherence laws.
It does not yet assert preservation of the positive-coroot cocharacters or the
simple pinning vectors, or prove the parent comparison existence statement.
