# Comparison data for the derived group of the centralizer of a split torus

Author: Worker 5662.

Tits compares the obstruction of a group `G` with that of the anisotropic kernel `H`, the
derived group of the centralizer `M` of a maximal split torus `S` (Tits, *Représentations
linéaires irréductibles d'un groupe réductif sur un corps quelconque*, J. reine angew.
Math. 247 (1971), §3.4 and §5.5). The weight computation behind this comparison uses only
a few properties of the actual objects:

- `S` is split;
- `H` centralizes `S`;
- the simple roots of `H` are the simple roots of `G` vanishing on `S`, with matching roots,
  coroots and star actions;
- some cocharacter of `S` pairs positively with the other simple roots.

It needs neither maximality of `S` nor anisotropy of `H`. Both fail after a finite
separable extension `E/k`, where the same comparison is needed. This module records these
properties as a structure on Library Galois pinning data over an arbitrary field `F` with
an arbitrary extension `K`. It proves the root-theoretic lemmas and shows that the
Library's actual anisotropic kernel provides an instance.

## Pointed monoids

- `eq_zero_of_mem_closure_of_neg_mem`: let `v` be a linearly independent integral family
  indexed by a finite type. If `x` and `-x` both lie in the additive monoid generated by
  the `v i`, then `x = 0`. Write `x = Σ aᵢ vᵢ` and `-x = Σ cᵢ vᵢ` (Mathlib's
  `AddSubmonoid.mem_closure_range_iff_of_fintype`). Then `Σ (aᵢ + cᵢ) vᵢ = 0`, so all
  coefficients vanish.
- `eq_zero_of_mem_closure_of_map_eq_zero`: if `x` lies in the monoid generated by the
  `v i`, an additive map `f` makes the family `f (v i)` linearly independent, and
  `f x = 0`, then `x = 0`.
- `SplitPinning.eq_zero_of_mem_positiveRootMonoid_of_neg_mem`: the published positive
  root monoid of a split pinning (the monoid generated by the simple roots) is pointed.
  The simple roots are linearly independent (Mathlib's `RootPairing.Base`).

## Split torus data

Let `d : GaloisPinningData F K G` (Library module `Reductive/StarAction`). Its torus `T` is
defined over `F` and `T_K` is a split maximal torus of `G_K`.

- `GaloisPinningData.SplitTorusDatum d` consists of:
  - a commutative group scheme `splitTorus` over `F` (the torus `S`) with
    `isSplitTorus`;
  - a homomorphism `splitInclusion : S ⟶ G`;
  - a torus map `torusMap : S ⟶ T` with `square`: `torusMap ≫ d.inclusion = splitInclusion`;
  - a cocharacter `positiveCocharacter` of `S`.
- `SplitTorusDatum.restriction s` is the additive map from characters of `T_K` to
  characters of `S`. It precomposes with the base change of `torusMap` and then applies
  the inverse of the published descent equivalence `characterLattice.baseChangeEquiv` of
  the split torus `S`.
- `SplitTorusDatum.zeroVertex s` is the type of simple vertices of `d` whose roots
  restrict to zero on `S`. `SplitTorusDatum.zeroRootMonoid s` is the additive monoid
  generated by their simple roots.
- `GaloisPinningData.vertexStar d σ` is the published star action of `σ ∈ Aut(K/F)` on
  simple vertices. The star action is by based automorphisms, so it preserves the simple
  vertices.

## Levi comparison data

`GaloisPinningData.LeviComparison d s ι e`, for `ι : D ⟶ G` and
`e : GaloisPinningData F K D`, consists of:

- `kernelTorusMap : e.torus ⟶ d.torus` with `kernelSquare`:
  `kernelTorusMap ≫ d.inclusion = e.inclusion ≫ ι`;
- `centralizes`: `SubgroupFunctor.range ι ≤ torusCentralizer s.splitInclusion`, so `ι`
  takes values in the all-test centralizer of `S`;
- `vertexEquiv`: an equivalence between the simple vertices of `e` and
  `s.zeroVertex`;
- `pos`: the cocharacter of `s` pairs positively with the restriction of every simple
  root of `d` that does not vanish on `S`;
- `root_eq` and `coroot_eq`: the simple root of `e` at `b` is the precomposition of the
  simple root of `d` at `vertexEquiv b` with the base-changed torus map, and dually the
  pushforward of the simple coroot of `e` at `b` is the simple coroot of `d` at
  `vertexEquiv b`;
- `star_eq`: `vertexEquiv` intertwines the star actions on simple vertices.

Consequences:

- `LeviComparison.weightRestriction L` is restriction of absolute characters along the
  base-changed `kernelTorusMap` (the Library's `characterPrecompInt`).
  `weightRestriction_root_zero` restates `root_eq`.
- `LeviComparison.mem_zeroRootMonoid`: if `β` lies in the positive root monoid of `d` and
  `s.restriction β = 0`, then `β ∈ s.zeroRootMonoid`. Let `g(x)` be the pairing of
  `s.restriction x` with the cocharacter of `s`. By induction on the closure, `g(β) ≥ 0`,
  and `g(β) = 0` forces `β ∈ zeroRootMonoid`. A simple root vanishing on `S` has `g = 0`
  and is a generator. For any other simple root, `pos` gives `g > 0`. For a sum of two
  elements with nonnegative values, a zero total forces both values to vanish.
- `LeviComparison.weightRestriction_mem_positiveRootMonoid`: restriction carries
  `zeroRootMonoid` into the positive root monoid of `e`.
- `LeviComparison.eq_zero_of_weightRestriction_eq_zero`: restriction is injective on
  `zeroRootMonoid`. The restricted generators are the simple roots of `e`, reindexed by
  `vertexEquiv`, so they are linearly independent.
- `LeviComparison.centralizes_baseChange`: the centralizing property persists after base
  change to any field extension (module `MatrixRepresentation/CentralizerCharacterSpaces`).

## The actual anisotropic kernel

For actual Tits index data `d : TitsIndexData G` over `k` (with
`K = SeparableClosure k`):

- `TitsIndexData.splitTorusDatum d` is the actual maximal split torus `d.splitTorus`, with
  its inclusion, the published torus map and square, and the published positive
  cocharacter. `splitTorusDatum_restriction_root` identifies its restriction of roots with
  the published `d.rootRestriction`, so its zero vertices are the published
  `d.zeroVertices`.
- `TitsIndexData.AnisotropicKernelIndex.leviComparison p` is Levi comparison data for the
  Library's actual anisotropic kernel `p : d.AnisotropicKernelIndex`. Here
  `ι = p.derived ≫ p.inclusion`, the derived subgroup `p.kernel` of the actual centralizer
  representative, and `e = p.index.pinning`. The fields come from published results:
  - the torus map, square, roots, coroots, vertex equivalence and star compatibility from
    the published `KernelComparison` `p.comparison`;
  - `centralizes` from `p.represents`, since `p.inclusion` represents the centralizer of
    `S`;
  - `pos` from the compatibility field `d.compatible` and positivity of simple roots.
  `leviComparison_weightRestriction` identifies its restriction map with the published
  `p.comparison.weightRestriction`.

All statements hold over every field and in every characteristic. The provenance is
internal: these are deductions from published Library structures (`GaloisPinningData`,
`TitsIndexData`, `KernelComparison`, `AnisotropicKernelIndex`) and Mathlib's root-pairing
bases.
