# The exceptional G2 relations for actual powered Weyl families

This internal supporting statement connects Worker 685's literal
characteristic-three calculations to the actual powered Weyl maps.
`PoweredG2WordRelations` is the conjunction of the five standard G2
commutator formulas in source-word form.

The theorem assumes corresponding simple-root supports, a genuine normalized
target family for the transported words, a source Cartan entry minus one,
and the exchanged target entries minus one and minus three. The transpose
equation forces the two root factors to have ratio three. Their prime-power
description then forces characteristic exponent three; the original
characteristic hypothesis makes three vanish in the coefficient ring and
makes the smaller root factor odd.

The target G2 formulas supply the principal relation and all nonvanishing
mixed relations used by the literal transport. The target long conjugates
have root coordinates `(3,1)` and `(3,2)` in the target short-long simple
basis. No positive integral combination of these roots, or of the target
long simple root and `(3,2)`, is a root in the G2 list. A helper compares
genuine word-conjugate parametrizations with the corresponding target Weyl
family by local rigidity, so these cone exclusions prove the exact required
commutations without a second-group Weyl-conjugation premise.

The literal principal calculation reverses the target relation, applies
unequal powers, and puts the terms into source order using the proved long
commutation and the coefficient-minus-three short commutation. The mixed
calculations use the target long-long and short-short relations; every
coefficient-minus-three term vanishes. Actual powered-word identities
identify all resulting maps with the displayed source-word formulas.

The coefficient ring is an arbitrary local ring and all identities retain
nilpotent structure. Genuine target normalization and the specified Cartan
pattern remain explicit inputs. No extra characteristic, oddness, or
commutation hypothesis is assumed by the theorem. Global normalization,
the other scaling patterns, ambient gluing and the final isogeny theorem
remain separate obligations.
