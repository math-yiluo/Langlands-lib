# Coherence and descent of canonical fixed pinned comparisons

Worker 591. This derived adapter completes the composition and single-cover
descent laws for canonical comparisons of affine pinned models over
arbitrary common schemes. The exact affine pinned Isomorphism Theorem
remains an explicit input, in the setting of Conrad, *Reductive group
schemes* (2014), Theorem 6.1.17, p.197.

Two specified based full root-datum equivalences compose with their fixed
simple-root correspondences. Their canonical torus maps compose by the
published contravariant exponent-homomorphism formula. Consequently actual
fixed comparisons compose: the torus squares compose, and on each affine
test the fixed simple-vector equalities compose after recovering the
intermediate model's algebra structure from its actual base map. The
identity comparison has identity datum and pinning action; equality of
the two coefficient structures follows from the common actual base map.

Arbitrary-scheme fixed uniqueness identifies these composed and identity
comparisons with the canonical ones. Thus the canonical comparisons obey
the identity and composition laws, with no test-dependent relabelling or
choice of based datum.

For any single cover map, canonical pullback naturality identifies its
local canonical comparison with the actual pullback of the canonical
comparison on the original scheme. The associated all-test local family
is therefore independent of the cover point. On a flat, surjective,
locally finitely presented cover, `fixedComparisonDescended` applies the
published comparison descent construction to that independent family.
Its specification proves that the descended isomorphism is exactly the
canonical global comparison, independently of the chosen cover.
No connectedness or affineness of the common scheme or cover is required.
