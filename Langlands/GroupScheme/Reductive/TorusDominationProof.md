# Proof that connected reductive groups over infinite fields are dominated by a torus

Worker 1143, with Worker 1424.

`Langlands.GroupScheme.reductiveTorusDomination` proves
`Langlands.GroupScheme.ReductiveTorusDominationStatement`
(`Reductive/TorusDominationStatement.lean`): every connected reductive group
`G` over an infinite field `k` admits a torus `T` over `k` and a morphism
`f : T.X ⟶ G.X` of `k`-schemes with dense range.

The proof follows the derivation recorded in the statement's Markdown (the
structural step in the proof of Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017), Corollary
17.92, printed p.382). This module only assembles published results; the
mathematics lives in the modules it cites. It has three steps.

## Bridge from a finite power of a torus

`reductiveTorusDomination_of_powerDomination` derives
`ReductiveTorusDominationStatement` from
`ReductiveTorusPowerDominationStatement`
(`Reductive/TorusDominationReduction.lean`). Given an infinite field `k` and a
connected reductive `G`, the hypothesis supplies a torus `P`, a natural number
`n` and a morphism `g : finitePower P.X n ⟶ G.X` with dense range.
`exists_isTorus_dense_of_finitePower` (`Torus/PowerDomination.lean`) turns this
into a torus `T` and a morphism `f : T.X ⟶ G.X` with dense range. The torus is
the group-scheme product of `n` copies of `P`.

## Reduction to finite torus generation

`reductiveTorusDomination_of_generation` derives
`ReductiveTorusDominationStatement` from
`ReductiveFiniteTorusGenerationStatement`. It applies
`reductiveTorusPowerDomination_of_generation`
(`Reductive/TorusDominationReduction.lean`) to the generation hypothesis and
to the published proof `ImageGeneration.pointedImageGeneration` of
`PointedImageGenerationStatement` (`ImageGenerationPointed.lean`). Then it
passes the resulting power-domination statement to the bridge above.

## The final theorem

`reductiveTorusDomination` applies `reductiveTorusDomination_of_generation` to
the published proof `reductiveFiniteTorusGeneration` of
`ReductiveFiniteTorusGenerationStatement`
(`Reductive/TorusGenerationProof.lean`): over an infinite field, a connected
reductive group is generated by finitely many closed subtori (Milne 17.91
together with 17.84).

The proof uses only the standard axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and
`Quot.sound`.
