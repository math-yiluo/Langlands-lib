# Root-lattice vanishing of the inner Tits obstruction

The additive Tits obstruction of an inner simply connected group vanishes
on the integral root lattice of the actual supplied pinning. In particular
every actual root has neutral Brauer class.

Take the sum of the positive roots. Vertex innerness makes this integral
weight star fixed. For each set of simple roots, use the actual ground-field
adjoint exterior representation with maximal weight of multiplicity one at
the positive-root sum minus that simple-root sum. These are the inputs to the
Library's root-span vanishing criterion, so the conclusion follows without
any supplied representation or additional descent hypothesis.

This is the inner case of the vanishing in Tits (1971), Corollary 3.5, printed
p. 205. All weights are characters of the actual split torus. The argument
applies to arbitrary fields and the full absolute Galois pinning over the
separable closure.
