# Coefficient transport for the actual dual-number rank-one cell

This internal construction compares a cell over a commutative ring with its
actual base change in the standard additive and split-torus coordinates.
Pulling a dual-number factorization along coefficient extension preserves both
scalar coordinates exactly. The torus coordinate is transported by the
base-change point equivalence and the standard split-torus base-change
isomorphism. The identity comparison of the two coefficient tests preserves
global functions.

If the base-changed cell underlies supplied Weyl data in an affine group
scheme, its dual coefficient formula therefore identifies the image of the
original positive epsilon coefficient with minus the inverse beta unit.
Equivalently, the negative of the original coefficient maps exactly to the
inverse beta unit. These assertions hold over arbitrary commutative coefficient
rings; they do not require reducedness or a field-point argument.
