# Inner alignment of actual canonical Borels

This internal construction defines preservation of the canonical Borels by an
actual isomorphism of their sources and the exact ambient inclusion square.
Identity, composition and arbitrary normalized pullback preserve this clause.
Conjugation by an actual point of the target Borel preserves it.

The published arbitrary-base relative Borel conjugacy theorem supplies an etale
cover and target conjugators aligning the transported source Borel with the
target Borel. Equality of the represented subgroup functors gives an actual
Borel isomorphism. The proof carries it and the conjugating point through the
actual base-change composition isomorphisms at both endpoints.

No pinning-vector or canonical-cocharacter alignment is asserted here. This is
an internal consequence of relative Borel conjugacy, represented-subgroup
comparison and actual pullback coherence.
