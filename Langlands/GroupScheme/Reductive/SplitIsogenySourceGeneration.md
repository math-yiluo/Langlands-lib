# Source root cells and generation of local-isogeny graphs

This is a derived supporting construction for split reductive isogeny
realization. It applies to the actual `SimpleIsogenyGraph`, with distinct
source and target tori and possibly inseparable local isogenies. Its
provenance is the source-side rank-one factor and graph-generation machinery
in `SplitLeviGluingRankOneFactors`, `SplitLeviGluingRankOneGraphClosure`
and `SplitLeviGluingGraphGeneration`. Those arguments require only the
source local centralizers, their source root representatives, and their
actual homomorphisms into a generated graph.

`rootFamilySources` and `rootFamilyMaps` include the distinguished source
torus as well as both signs of every source simple root. The explicit torus
source also handles an empty root set. All sources are smooth, affine and
connected. `rootFamilyRationalPoints` is the abstract subgroup generated by
their actual rational ranges.

For a connected source with its actual root datum, `local_positive_rep`
and `local_negative_rep` identify the entire local dynamic factors with the
chosen source root representatives, on all scheme tests. They use the
published rank-one direction arguments, the generic source chamber and the
simple-root annihilation equations. No target-root comparison enters.
`local_torus_rep` identifies the local middle factor with the source
torus when the ambient chamber centralizer equals that torus. It pulls back
the ambient centralizer along the closed local inclusion and cancels that
monomorphism.

`isGeneratedByRootFamily` proves algebraic closed-subgroup generation over
every field. A closed subgroup containing the root and torus lifts
contains the three dynamic cell factors of every source local Levi. The
dominant local cell and reducedness of the smooth local Levi imply
factorization of its entire local lift through that subgroup. The defining
generation property of the actual graph then proves the conclusion.
Over an algebraically closed field, `dense_rootFamilyRationalPoints`
applies the published rational generation density theorem to this family.

Also over an algebraically closed field,
`range_localLift_subset_rationalPointClosure` transports the dense local
dynamic root cell through the local lift. It uses continuity of the
actual scheme morphism and compatibility of triple products with a
homomorphism. Consequently
`positive_mul_negative_subset_rationalPointClosure` proves the
equal-index product condition for the eventual three-factor word argument.

`GeneratedSimpleIsogenyGraph` retains the original graph and its proved
algebraic generation certificate. Its constructor
`generatedSimpleIsogenyGraph` discharges the ambient chamber-centralizer
identity using source connected reductivity, source-torus maximality and
the already published generic and maximal-torus centralizer theorems.

This module does not prove commutation at distinct simple indices, density
of the global three-factor graph cell, target-root transport, maximality of
the graph torus, or invertibility of the first projection. In particular,
it is not a proof of the general realization or fixed-torus extension
conjunction.
