# The scheme-theoretic centre of a reductive group over a base

`Langlands.GroupScheme.reductiveRelativeCenter.{u}` proves the unchanged
Library proposition `Langlands.GroupScheme.ReductiveRelativeCenterStatement.{u}`
for every universe. For every scheme `S` and reductive group scheme `G` over
`S`, it gives a group scheme `Z` and a closed inclusion `z : Z ⟶ G`
representing the full scheme-theoretic centre. The group `Z` is of relative
multiplicative type. For every scheme `S'` in the same universe and every map
`b : S' ⟶ S`, the actual pullback of this inclusion represents the full centre
of the pulled-back group.

The implemented proof first establishes the reduction
`reductiveRelativeCenter_of_affinePinnedModels`. The published construction
`AffinePinnedAtlas.ofReductive` provides an actual étale covering family of
the arbitrary base by affine pinned models. On a chart with isomorphism from
the pulled-back group to its model, compose the model's centre inclusion
with the inverse isomorphism. `IsSchemeTheoreticCenter.comp_iso` identifies
this composite with the full centre of the actual pulled-back group.
`exists_multiplicativeTypeCenter_of_etaleCover` then constructs the global
closed representative, transfers local diagonalizability to its pullbacks,
and supplies the full base-change clause for the chosen global inclusion.

The unconditional theorem supplies this reduction with
`AffinePinnedModel.centerKernel`, `centerKernelHom`,
`centerKernel_isSchemeTheoreticCenter` and `centerKernel_isDiagonalizable`.
These are the actual full root-character kernel and its inclusion on each
model. The local construction uses the coordinate character lattice modulo
the subgroup generated by all roots, without saturation. Consequently its
torsion, finite factors and possible non-smooth infinitesimal structure
are retained. The global centre predicate tests commutation on every test
scheme and every refinement, rather than only field-valued points.

This proof adds no hypotheses to the published statement and changes neither
that statement nor its imports. There is no assumption that the base is
affine, reduced, connected, nonempty, noetherian or quasi-compact. The atlas
exists from reductivity; a pinning or maximal torus is not an additional
input to the final theorem.

The construction follows the local root-kernel and descent proof of Conrad,
*Reductive group schemes* (2014), Theorem 3.3.4, pp. 98–99 (registered source
`conrad-2014-reductive-group-schemes`). The implemented final assembly uses
the Library's proved étale atlas and the supporting full local-centre and
closed-cover descent results. It proves the exact published relative-centre
proposition; the source's additional description of the maximal-torus
action kernel is not a further conclusion of this file.
