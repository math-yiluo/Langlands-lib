# Canonical based cocharacters have the prescribed split centralizer

This internal construction first proves a residue-fibre criterion. For a group
scheme affine and locally of finite presentation over any scheme, and a closed
smooth commutative subgroup with a supplied cocharacter, assume that the dynamic
centralizer of its ambient composite equals that subgroup on every actual
residue-field fibre. The same equality then holds as subgroup functors over the
whole base. The proof chooses a locally finitely presented representative of the
centralizer. The subgroup maps to it, and uniqueness of fibre representatives
makes every residue-fibre comparison an isomorphism. The Library smooth-source,
locally-finite-type-target criterion makes the comparison an isomorphism globally;
its inclusion equation identifies the ranges. This also applies to an empty base.

For an affine reductive group over any ring with a supplied closed coordinate
split maximal torus, full common fibrewise root datum and base, the centralizer
of the actual positive-coroot cocharacter is exactly the supplied torus. On each
residue field the existing positive-coroot exponent regularity theorem and the
regular-cocharacter centralizer theorem give the hypothesis of the criterion.
Regularity is used only after choosing a residue field; no field algebra over a
zero ring is assumed. Uniqueness of representatives gives an actual isomorphism
from any supplied representative to the coordinate torus, with the exact
inclusion-compatibility equation. The provenance is this internal composition of
published representability, field regularity and fibrewise isomorphism results.
No atlas, descent solution, or inner-form existence is asserted here.
