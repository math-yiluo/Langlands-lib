# The derived Levi pinning of a dominant cocharacter

`StandardLeviDerivedPinningStatement` takes an actual split pinning of a connected reductive group over a field and a cocharacter of its maximal torus pairing nonnegatively with the simple roots. Given representatives of the actual cocharacter centralizer and its derived subgroup, the statement gives a split pinning of the derived subgroup. Its root datum matches the roots and coroots on the zero face, and the simple roots match the simple roots of the ambient pinning pairing to zero with the cocharacter. If the ambient group is simply connected, so is the derived subgroup.

The pinned maximal torus factors through the centralizer. Its intersection with the derived subgroup is a maximal torus there and is closed in the original split torus. A torus closed in a split torus is split because restriction of geometric characters is surjective and commutes with Galois action, so all its geometric characters descend with trivial action. The derived centralizer root comparison identifies the based zero face. The cocharacter and its dominant split torus image have the same centralizer, so the theorem on derived centralizers of split subtori gives simple connectedness.

This specializes the actual derived centralizer root comparison to a dominant cocharacter of a split pinning. The resulting identification of the derived Levi type is used in the parabolic dimension arguments of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Theorem 6.26, pp. 354–356, and §6.8, Lemmas 6.31–6.32, p. 380.
