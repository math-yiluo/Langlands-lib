# Weights of Galois twists of representations

This module computes the weights of the Galois twist `ρ^σ` (module
`MatrixRepresentation/GaloisTwist`) of a representation `ρ` of `G_K`. Here
`G` is an affine group scheme over a field `k` with Galois pinning data
`d : GaloisPinningData k K G` (module `Reductive/StarAction`). The main result
is the step in Tits's construction that makes the Tits algebra exist (Tits,
*Représentations linéaires irréductibles d'un groupe réductif sur un corps
quelconque*, J. reine angew. Math. 247 (1971), proof of Théorème 3.3,
printed pp. 203–204, PDF pp. 8–9; the claim about the highest weight of the
representation precomposed with the split-form automorphism is completed at
the top of p. 204):
if `λ` is fixed by the star action of `σ` and `ρ` satisfies the published
predicate `IsHighestWeight ρ λ` (irreducible, spanned by weight spaces, with
one-dimensional `λ`-space and all weights below `λ`), then the conjugate `ρ^σ`
satisfies `IsHighestWeight ρ^σ λ`. This is
Tits's hypothesis that `ρ_d ∘ a` has the same dominant weight. Together with
the uniqueness clause of the highest-weight classification for irreducible
representations, it yields intertwiners `ρ ≅ ρ^σ`.

## Characters and characters spaces

- `affineUnits_transport_of_descentPoint` is the character analogue of
  `affineMatrix_transport_of_descentPoint`. If the descended point is `Θ ≫ y`
  with `Θ.left = Spec θ`, the transported character (published
  `characterLattice.transport`) has value `θ` of the original value. The proof
  uses `Gm.affineUnitsEquiv` as global sections of `Gm.pointsEquiv`, the
  published `characterLattice.transport_points`, `Gm.pointsEquiv_naturality`
  and `Scheme.ΓSpecIso_inv_naturality`.
- `descentPoint_comp_map` (descent of points commutes with homomorphisms over
  the base) and `pointMatrix_transport_restrict` (restricting a transported
  representation) follow from injectivity of `baseChangePointsEquiv` and the
  published `baseChangePointsEquiv_comp`.
- `scalarPoint_comp_map`: scalar points are natural for homomorphisms `H ⟶ G`
  over `k`.
- `conjugate_mem_characterSpace_galoisTwist` and
  `conjugate_mem_characterSpace_restrict_galoisTwist`: if `v` is a vector of
  character `χ` for `ρ` (or for a restriction of `ρ` along the base change of
  a homomorphism `f : H ⟶ G` over `k`), then `σ(v)` is a vector of the
  conjugate character `σχ` (published `rationalCharacters.galoisRepresentation`)
  for `ρ^σ` (or its restriction). The proof follows the published
  `conjugate_mem_characterSpace_baseChange`. It uses the all-tests
  characterization `mem_characterSpace_iff_points`, `pointMatrix_transport`,
  `scalarMap_galoisBaseMap` and `characterEval_galoisRepresentation`.
- `universalMatrix_galoisTwist_inv`: `(ρ^σ)^{σ⁻¹}` has the universal matrix of
  `ρ`.
- `characterSpace_restrict_eq_of_universalMatrix_eq`: character spaces of
  restrictions depend only on the universal matrix (module `GaloisCoefficients`).
  The published universal character matrix of a restriction is the universal
  matrix evaluated at the classifying map of the restricted universal point.

## Subspace conjugation

- `finrank_galoisComap`: the `σ`-preimage `galoisComap σ V` has the dimension of
  `V`. This uses Mathlib's `rank_eq_of_equiv_equiv` with the semilinear
  bijection `w ↦ σ ∘ w`.
- `galoisComapOrderIso σ` is `galoisComap σ` as an order isomorphism of the
  lattice of subspaces of `Kⁿ`, with inverse `galoisComap σ⁻¹`.

## Weight spaces and highest weights

Write `p = d.toSplitPinning`, and `g_σ` for the Galois action on characters of
the split torus `T_K` (published `characterLattice.galoisRepresentation`, the
weight map of `d.galoisAction σ`).

- `conjugate_mem_representationWeightSpace`,
  `mem_representationWeightSpace_galoisTwist_iff` and
  `representationWeightSpace_galoisTwist`: the weight space of `ρ^σ` at `g_σ μ`
  is `galoisComap σ⁻¹` of the weight space of `ρ` at `μ`, i.e. its `σ`-image.
  The converse inclusion applies the forward one to `ρ^σ` and `σ⁻¹`, using
  `universalMatrix_galoisTwist_inv`. This needs no affineness of the torus over
  `k`, only over `K`, which the split pinning provides.
- `finrank_representationWeightSpace_weyl`: for any representation of `G_K`,
  weight multiplicities are invariant under the Weyl group. The proof is
  induction over Mathlib's `RootPairing.weylGroup.induction`, using the published
  `finrank_representationWeightSpace_root_reflection` for each reflection.
- `smul_mem_positiveRootMonoid`: an automorphism of the root datum preserving
  the chosen base (`basedRootDatumAut`) preserves the published
  `positiveRootMonoid`, the monoid generated by the simple roots.
- From here on the group scheme is also assumed locally of finite type
  (`[LocallyOfFiniteType G.X.hom]`). This is the hypothesis under which the
  published `d.galoisAction` and `d.starAction` (`Reductive/StarAction`) are
  defined, so it is a premise of the remaining declarations.
- `galoisAction_smul` and `representationWeightSpace_galoisTwist_smul` restate
  this with the action of `d.galoisAction σ`.
- `isHighestWeight_galoisTwist`: suppose `p.IsHighestWeight ρ λ` and
  `(d.starAction σ) • λ = λ`. Then `p.IsHighestWeight (ρ^σ) λ`. The proof writes
  the star action as `w ∘ g_σ` with `w` in the Weyl group (published
  `exists_weyl_mul_galoisAction_eq_starAction`). It then checks the four
  defining conditions.
  - Irreducibility is `IsIrreducible.galoisTwist`.
  - The weight spaces of `ρ^σ` span, because they are the `σ`-images of those
    of `ρ` and `galoisComapOrderIso` preserves suprema.
  - The `λ`-space is one-dimensional: `λ = w(g_σ λ)`, so Weyl invariance and
    `finrank_galoisComap` reduce it to the `λ`-space of `ρ`.
  - Let `μ` be a weight of `ρ^σ`. Then `ν = g_σ⁻¹(w⁻¹ μ)` is a weight of `ρ`
    by Weyl invariance, so `λ − ν` lies in the positive root monoid. Applying
    the star action gives `λ − μ`.

All characteristics are allowed. No smoothness or reducedness hypothesis is
made beyond those of the published pinning data. The field `K` need not be
Galois over `k`.
