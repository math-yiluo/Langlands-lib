# Proof of star invariance and the relative simple-root comparison

This file proves `Langlands.GroupScheme.TitsIndexStarRelativeRootsStatement`
from `Reductive/TitsIndexRelativeStatement`. The main theorem is
`Langlands.GroupScheme.titsIndexStarRelativeRoots`. It is assembled from
`titsIndexStarInvariant : TitsIndexStarInvariantStatement` and
`titsIndexRelativeSimpleRoots : TitsIndexRelativeSimpleRootsStatement`. The
auxiliary declarations live in the namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme.TitsIndexStarProof`.

## Scope

The scope is the target's own: an arbitrary universe `u`, an arbitrary field
`k` of any characteristic (characteristic two included), an actual `k`-group
scheme `G` with affine and locally-finite-type structure morphism, and any
`d : TitsIndexData G`. Rank zero and empty diagrams are allowed. The proof
never uses the semisimplicity field of `d`, nor any local-field, local rank-one
classification or Kneser–Tits input. It never consumes
`TitsIndexSplitRankStatement`. All work happens over
`k_s = SeparableClosure k`, and no finite Galois splitting field is chosen.

## Provenance

This proves the assertions of J. Tits, *Classification of algebraic semisimple
groups* (1966), §2.3 and §2.5.1 (registered source
`tits-1966-classification-algebraic-semisimple`), in the form fixed by the
statement file. The Lean route is internal. It uses the Library's based
correction of root-datum automorphisms
(`RootDatumBasedCorrection.exists_positive_correction`), its face-height
calculus (`RootDatumFaceHeight`), the continuity of the Galois action on torus
lattices (`torusLatticeGaloisContinuity`), and the lemma
`IsLargestSplitSubtorus.galoisFixed_character_eq_zero` of the companion module
`Torus/LargestSplitGaloisFixed`. That lemma was written by Worker 151 and is
submitted here together with this proof. Several steps follow the
finite-Galois Library unit `Rational/FaceSimpleRootRestriction`, transported
to `k_s`.

## Notation

`T = d.pinning.torus` is the `k`-torus whose base change `T_{k_s}` carries the
absolute root datum `P = d.pinning.rootDatum` with base `B = d.pinning.base`.
`S = d.splitTorus`, with `f = d.torusMap : S ⟶ T`. For a root `i`, `rest i` is
`d.rootRestriction i`.

## Basic comparisons

- `nuK d` is the base change to `k_s` of `d.positiveCocharacter ≫ d.torusMap`,
  a cocharacter `ν'` of `T_{k_s}`.
- `baseChange_rootRestriction`: the base change of `rest i` is
  `f_{k_s} ≫ P.root i`. This holds because `rest i` is defined through the
  inverse of the canonical base-change equivalence for the split torus `S`.
- `root'_nuK`: `P.root' i ν'` equals the pairing of `rest i` with
  `d.positiveCocharacter`. The proof uses `IsRootDatumOf` (root pairing equals
  lattice pairing), the published `latticePairing_baseChange_comp`, and
  `latticePairing_baseChange`.
- `mem_relativeRoots_of_ne_zero` and `exists_of_mem_relativeRoots`: by
  `mem_roots_iff_exists_splittingField_restriction` (with `d.square`), the
  relative roots of `d.splitInclusion` are exactly the nonzero restrictions
  `rest i`.
- `rootRestriction_eq_zero_iff`: `rest i = 0` iff `P.root' i ν' = 0`. The
  converse direction uses genericity of `d.positiveCocharacter`.
- `isPos_iff_of_ne_zero`, `nonneg_of_isPos`, `isPos_of_pos`, `dominant`,
  `dominant'`: these are `d.compatible` rewritten through `root'_nuK`. In
  particular `ν'` is dominant for `B`.
- `coweightEquiv_nuK`: the ordinary Galois action fixes `ν'`, because its
  coweight map is the cocharacter Galois representation and `ν'` is a base
  change (`cocharacterLattice.galoisRepresentation_baseChange`).

## Star correction and the first conjunct

`exists_star_correction`: for every `σ`, the star automorphism
`(d.pinning.starAction σ).val` equals `w * d.pinning.galoisAction σ` for some
`w` in the reflection subgroup generated by the roots vanishing on `ν'`. The
correction lemma `exists_positive_correction` is applied with:

- the coweight `ν'`;
- the regular coweight `positiveCocharacter` of the split pinning, whose
  positive roots are the positive roots of `B`;
- the sign compatibility `isPos_iff_of_ne_zero`.

It yields such a `w` for which `w * galoisAction σ` preserves positivity.
Therefore `w * galoisAction σ` permutes `B.support`
(`mem_support_equiv_iff_of_isPos`), so it is based. The published uniqueness
`basedRootDatum_representative_unique` of based representatives in a Weyl coset
identifies it with the star automorphism.

`root'_mul_eq` (abstract) and `star_root'` then show that the star action
preserves every value `P.root' i ν'`. The reason is that the zero-root
reflections fix `ν'` (`fixes_coweight`), as does the Galois action.
`titsIndexStarInvariant` follows from `rootRestriction_eq_zero_iff`.

## Restriction invariance

- `resK d` is the additive map `χ ↦ f_{k_s} ≫ χ`.
- `resK_galois`: `resK` is invariant under the character Galois
  representation. The proof uses `characterLattice.galoisRepresentation_precompose`.
  Every character of the split torus `S_{k_s}` is a base change, and base
  changes are Galois-fixed.
- `map_smul_eq_of_mem` (abstract): an additive map killing the roots vanishing
  on `ν` is invariant under the reflection subgroup of those roots. This is
  closure induction on the reflection formula.
- `resK_star` and `rootRestriction_star` give `rest (star σ i) = rest i`.

## Indicator forms

For a base `B` and a set `O` of indices, `simpleForm B O` is the `ℤ`-linear
form on the integral root span sending a simple root to `1` if it lies in `O`
and to `0` otherwise.

- `simpleForm_simple` and `simpleForm_eq_sum` give its values.
- `simpleForm_root_of_eq_zero`: assume `ν` is dominant and `O` contains no
  simple root vanishing on `ν`. Then the form vanishes on every root vanishing
  on `ν`. Write the root as a sign times a nonnegative combination of simple
  roots (`exists_root_eq_sum_nat_or_neg`). Every term of the vanishing sum
  `∑ f_i ⟨α_i, ν⟩` is then zero.
- `simpleForm_comp_rootSpanMap_of_mem`: under the same hypotheses, the form is
  invariant under the zero-root reflection subgroup. This copies the proof of
  the published `faceHeight_comp_rootSpanMap_of_mem`.
- `simpleForm_comp_rootSpanMap_of_based`: a based automorphism preserving `O`
  preserves the form.
- `faceHeight_eq_simpleForm`: the published face height is the indicator form
  of the simple roots not vanishing on `ν`.
- `simpleForm_galois`: let `O` be a set of simple roots avoiding the zero
  vertices and stable under every star automorphism. Then its form is invariant
  under every `galoisAction σ`. Write `galoisAction σ = w⁻¹ * star σ` with the
  correction `w` and combine the two previous invariances.
- `faceHeight_comp_galois` is the case of the face height, using `star_root'`.

## Vanishing of invariant forms on zero-restriction elements

`form_eq_zero_of_resK_eq_zero` has the following hypotheses:

- every Galois-fixed character with zero restriction to `S_{k_s}` vanishes;
- `h` is a Galois-invariant linear form on the root span;
- `x` is a root-span element with zero restriction.

Its conclusion is `h x = 0`. By `torusLatticeGaloisContinuity` there is a
finite-dimensional intermediate field `L` whose fixing subgroup `H` acts
trivially on the characters of `T_{k_s}`. By Mathlib's
`finrank_eq_fixingSubgroup_index`, `H` has finite index. Let `y` be the sum of
the Galois translates of `x` over chosen representatives of the finitely many
cosets in `Gal(k_s/k) ⧸ H`.

- `y` is Galois-fixed: left multiplication permutes the cosets, and
  representatives of the same coset act equally.
- `y` restricts to zero, by `resK_galois`.
- Hence `y = 0` by the hypothesis, and `0 = h y = |Gal/H| · h x`.

`fixed_eq_zero_of_resK_eq_zero` discharges the hypothesis for `d`. It applies
`IsLargestSplitSubtorus.galoisFixed_character_eq_zero` with the following
inputs:

- `IsTorus` comes from `d.maximalTorus`.
- Affineness and finite type of `T` come from the closed immersion into `G`.
- `f` is a largest split subtorus by `IsMaximalSplitTorus.of_comp`
  (through `d.square` and `d.maximalSplit`) and
  `IsMaximalSplitTorus.isLargestSplitSubtorus`.

## The second conjunct

- `rootRestriction_mem_simpleRelativeRoots`: a distinguished simple root `a`
  restricts to a simple relative root. Its restriction is a nonzero relative
  root pairing positively with `d.positiveCocharacter`. Suppose
  `rest a = rest m₁ + rest m₂` with positive relative roots. Then
  `x = α_a - α_{m₁} - α_{m₂}` restricts to zero, so its face height is zero.
  But the face height is `1` on `α_a` and at least `1` on each `α_{m_j}`
  (`one_le_faceHeight_root_of_pos`), a contradiction. This argument follows
  the published finite-Galois `restrict_mem_simpleRelativeRoots`.
- `exists_star_of_rootRestriction_eq`: distinguished simple vertices `a, b`
  with equal restrictions lie in one star orbit. Let `O` be the star orbit of
  `a`. It is star-stable by `star_mul`, and avoids the zero vertices by
  `star_root'`. Its indicator form vanishes on `α_a - α_b`, which restricts to
  zero. Hence the form takes the value `1` at `b`, so `b ∈ O`.
- `exists_simple_of_mem_simpleRelativeRoots`: every simple relative root is
  the restriction of a simple root. It is `rest m` for a positive root `m`. The
  proof is Mathlib's induction `IsPos.induction_on_add`. If `m = i + j` with `j`
  simple, then `i` is positive (`IsPos.sub`) and `rest m = rest i + rest j`.
  If neither summand is zero, both are positive relative roots, contradicting
  simplicity. Otherwise the problem passes to `i`, or `j` is the answer.
- `relativeSimpleRoots_of_fix` assembles the conjunct for one index. The
  orbit/fiber equivalence combines `exists_star_of_rootRestriction_eq` with
  `rootRestriction_star`. The map `a ↦ rest a` descends to
  `d.DistinguishedOrbit`, is injective by the first part, and is surjective by
  the previous lemma. `Equiv.ofBijective` gives `e`, and
  `(e (d.orbitClass a)).val = rest a` holds by definition.

`titsIndexRelativeSimpleRoots` instantiates it with
`fixed_eq_zero_of_resK_eq_zero`. `titsIndexStarRelativeRoots` is the pair of
the two conjuncts. The inspected axioms of the main theorem are `propext`,
`Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.
