# Trivial Lie action of the full root kernel

This internal result implements the adjoint-action step in Conrad,
*Reductive group schemes* (2014), proof of Theorem 3.3.4, pp. 98–99.
The base ring is arbitrary. The ambient affine group is smooth, and the
supplied full coordinate root datum agrees with its roots on every
field-valued fibre.

A nonzero exponent outside the common root set has zero relative weight
space: every geometric fibre has zero weight space, so the finite
projective relative weight summand has rank zero at every prime and is
zero. A point killing all the common root characters therefore fixes
each universal adjoint coefficient, including the zero-weight term.
Summing those coefficients proves the identity on scalar extensions of
base Lie vectors. The smooth Lie scalar-extension equivalence and tensor
induction extend the identity to every Lie vector over every test algebra.

`CoordinateRootDatum.rootExponentSpan` is the subgroup generated by the
roots without saturation. `splitTorus.rootKernel` is the diagonalizable
group of the full quotient, and `rootKernelHom` maps it into the ambient
group through the given coordinate torus. Every relation subgroup
containing the roots gives a kernel whose adjoint action is trivial;
`adjoint_rootKernelHom_eq_self` specializes this to the root span. These
results concern the Lie action. Centrality and identification with the
full scheme-theoretic centre are separate steps.
