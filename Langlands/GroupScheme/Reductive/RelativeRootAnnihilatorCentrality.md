# Centrality of torus points annihilating the coordinate roots

This internal consequence of the actual local big-cell theorem concerns a
reductive group with its supplied fibrewise maximal coordinate torus and
fibrewise coordinate root datum over a commutative local ring. The predicate
`RootCharactersOne` says that every actual root character takes value one on
a relative torus point.

Over any local coefficient test ring with separably closed residue field,
conjugation by such a point fixes the torus by commutativity and fixes every
member of the chosen actual root family by torus equivariance. The published
big-cell point-homomorphism uniqueness theorem makes this conjugation the
identity on the whole test-point group.

Equality of morphisms into an affine scheme is detected by these local
coefficient test rings, including their nilpotents. Applying this detection
after every relative refinement shows that the original point maps into the
full scheme-theoretic centre. No reducedness, residue-characteristic,
connectedness of the test scheme or rational lifting premise is imposed.
The base ring is local in the current centrality theorem. This module does
not yet deduce the root-character equations from pinning preservation or
prove the pinned-comparison existence statement.
