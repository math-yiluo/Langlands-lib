# Torus normalization inside the aligned canonical Borel

Suppose an actual comparison already preserves the canonical Borels by an
actual isomorphism and its inclusion square. The coordinate tori, considered
inside the target Borel, are fibrewise maximal tori. The target Borel has smooth
affine structure morphism, so the published arbitrary-base etale-local conjugacy
of split maximal tori applies inside this Borel.

On each member of that cover, its actual Borel conjugator corrects the pulled
comparison to preserve the coordinate tori. Conjugation by a Borel point also
preserves the Borel, retaining the previous alignment. The proof transports the
Borel point and the coordinate-torus square through the explicit base-change
composition isomorphisms, and maps the Borel point to the ambient group by the
actual Borel inclusion.

This is an internal consequence of the published conjugacy, maximality and
pullback-coherence interfaces. It does not assert alignment of the positive-coroot
cocharacters or the simple pinning vectors, or the parent existence statement.
