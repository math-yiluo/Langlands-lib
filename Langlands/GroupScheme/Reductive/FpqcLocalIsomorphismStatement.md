# Statement: fpqc-locally isomorphic reductive groups are étale-locally isomorphic

Worker 75.

`Langlands.GroupScheme.ReductiveFpqcLocalIsomorphismStatement.{u} : Prop` is a statement-only,
`Prop`-valued definition. This module does not prove it, and it adds no other definitions,
theorems or axioms. It imports:

- Mathlib's `Mathlib.AlgebraicGeometry.Sites.Fpqc`, for `Scheme.fpqcPrecoverage`;
- Mathlib's `Mathlib.AlgebraicGeometry.Morphisms.Etale`, for `Etale`;
- [`Reductive.Basic`](Basic.md), for `IsReductive`; the base-change functor
  `GroupScheme.baseChange` of [`AbelianScheme.GroupScheme`](../../AbelianScheme/GroupScheme.md)
  comes transitively.

`GroupScheme S` is `Grp (Over S)`, and an isomorphism `H ≅ G` in it is an isomorphism of group
schemes over `S`.

## Predicates and constructions used

- `IsReductive G`: the structure morphism of `G` is smooth and affine, and every geometric
  fibre of `G` is a connected reductive group over an algebraically closed field. This is
  Conrad's reductive group scheme (Definition 3.1.1).
- `Scheme.Cover.{u} K S`, for a precoverage `K` on schemes, is Mathlib's type of covers of `S`
  in `K`: a family of morphisms `𝒰.f i : 𝒰.X i ⟶ S`, indexed by `i : 𝒰.I₀` in universe `u`,
  that belongs to `K`.
- `Scheme.fpqcPrecoverage` is Mathlib's fpqc precoverage, `qcPrecoverage ⊓ precoverage @Flat`.
  A family is in it when every member is flat, the family is jointly surjective, and it is a
  quasi-compact cover: every affine open `U ⊆ S` is the union of the images of finitely many
  quasi-compact opens of the members (`QuasiCompactCover`). These are the usual fpqc
  coverings. Mathlib's `fpqcTopology` is generated by this precoverage.
- `Scheme.precoverage @Etale` consists of the jointly surjective families of étale morphisms:
  the étale coverings. The Library uses the same encoding of étale covers, for example in
  [`Torus.Relative`](../Torus/Relative.md).
- For a morphism `f : U ⟶ S`, `(GroupScheme.baseChange f).obj G` is the pullback `G_U` of `G`
  to `U` with its induced group law.

## Statement and quantifiers

The proposition quantifies over every scheme `S` and every pair of group schemes
`G G' : GroupScheme S` with `IsReductive G` and `IsReductive G'`. It asserts that if

- there is an fpqc cover `𝒰` of `S` such that for every index `i` there is an isomorphism
  `G_{𝒰.X i} ≅ G'_{𝒰.X i}` of group schemes over `𝒰.X i`,

then

- there is an étale cover `𝒱` of `S` such that for every index `j` there is an isomorphism
  `G_{𝒱.X j} ≅ G'_{𝒱.X j}` of group schemes over `𝒱.X j`.

The isomorphisms are only asserted to exist (`Nonempty`); no compatibility between them on
overlaps is required or claimed.

## Source

Brian Conrad, *Reductive group schemes*, in *Autour des schémas en groupes* I,
Panoramas et Synthèses 42–43, SMF, 2014. It is registered as
`conrad-2014-reductive-group-schemes`, file
`Conrad--Reductive group schemes (SGA3 notes)--2014.pdf` (SHA-256
`1a63a17e4ccb7e23263d65f05d93a6651262861e978e9eb0c1c53a01fd59140a`). Page numbers are the
one-based PDF pages, which agree with the printed page numbers.

- Proposition 6.2.11, p.210: "Let G and G′ be reductive groups over a non-empty scheme S.
  1. If G and G′ are isomorphic fpqc-locally on S then they are so étale-locally on S."
- Proof of (1), p.210: "For (1) we may work étale-locally on S to reach the split case with
  the same root datum. Then we can apply the full faithfulness of (6.1.2)."

## Derivation from the source

- **Reductive groups.** Conrad's reductive groups over `S` are `IsReductive` (Definition 3.1.1).
- **"Isomorphic fpqc-locally on S".** Two `S`-groups are isomorphic locally for a topology when
  there is a covering family of `S` in that topology over each member of which they become
  isomorphic. Mathlib's fpqc precoverage has isomorphisms and is stable under base change and
  composition (it is a pretopology), so the covering sieves of `fpqcTopology` are those that
  contain a family from the precoverage; stating the hypothesis with a covering family loses
  nothing.
- **"So étale-locally on S".** The same with an étale covering family.
- **Index universe.** The covers are indexed in universe `u`, the universe of `S`. This is no
  restriction: any fpqc or étale cover of `S` has a subfamily indexed in universe `u` that is
  still a cover of the same kind (for each affine open of `S`, keep the finitely many members
  used to cover it).

## Scope

All schemes are in universe `u`. The base `S` is arbitrary; Conrad's assumption that `S` is
non-empty is dropped, since for empty `S` the empty family is an étale cover and the
conclusion holds trivially. No splitting of `G` or `G'` is assumed.

## Not stated

The statement does not assert Conrad's Proposition 6.2.11(2) (isomorphisms of pairs and
triples with split maximal tori and Borel subgroups over connected `S` with `Pic(S) = 1`,
and `G(S)`-conjugacy over affine such `S`). It does not state the Isomorphism Theorem or the
full faithfulness of (6.1.2) used in the proof, nor anything about the isomorphism functor
`Isom(G, G')` or its representability.
