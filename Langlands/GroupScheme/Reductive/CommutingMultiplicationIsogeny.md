# Central isogenies from commuting multiplication with finite kernel

Authors: Worker 1616 and Worker 1783.

This internal adapter starts from two closed commuting subgroup maps into a smooth affine group over an arbitrary field, scheme-surjective multiplication, and finiteness of its full actual kernel. It proves that the actual commuting multiplication homomorphism is a central isogeny. The closed factors are affine over the base, hence so is their product. This gives quasi-compactness of the multiplication. The published surjective closed-subgroup multiplication theorem gives flatness. Fpqc finite-kernel descent then gives finiteness. The target is locally Noetherian, so finiteness gives local finite presentation. Full-kernel centrality is imported from the kernel-centre module. No smoothness of the factors or of the kernel is assumed.

`commutingMultiplicationWithFiniteKernel` bundles this actual homomorphism with the proved central-isogeny property; its value theorem identifies it with the original multiplication. The module also proves that a central closed subgroup of a group with finite full centre is finite, using the actual represented centre factorization and the closed-immersion postcomposition criterion.

Provenance: internal composition of the published centre, represented-subgroup, commuting multiplication and fpqc finite-kernel interfaces. This unit supplies no complement, multiplication surjectivity or unconditional kernel finiteness.
