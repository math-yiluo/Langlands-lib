# Pinned isomorphisms of Galois pinning data and highest-weight representations

This module supplies the pinned-isomorphism notion needed to state that the Tits obstruction
is transported along isomorphisms of groups (`SimplyConnected/TitsObstructionTransport`). It also
proves the representation-theoretic and star-action compatibilities that the transport
proof uses. Nothing here uses simple connectedness, Tits algebras or Brauer groups.

## Split pinnings (namespace `SplitPinning`)

Fix a field `F`, affine group schemes `G`, `H` over `Spec F` and split pinnings `p` of `G`
and `q` of `H`, with split tori `p.torus`, `q.torus` and inclusions `p.inclusion`,
`q.inclusion`.

### Weight spaces

Let `E : G ≅ H` be an isomorphism of group schemes and `τ : p.torus ≅ q.torus` an
isomorphism of commutative group schemes with `p.inclusion ≫ E.hom = τ.hom ≫ q.inclusion`
(the hypothesis `hsq`).

* `representationWeightSpace_restrict_iso`: for an actual matrix representation `ρ` of `H`
  and a character `μ` of `q.torus`, the weight space of the restriction `ρ.restrict E.hom`
  at the character `(characterLattice.congr τ).symm μ = τ.hom ≫ μ` of `p.torus` equals the
  weight space of `ρ` at `μ`. Proof: `ρ.restrict E.hom` restricted to `p.torus` is
  `ρ.restrict q.inclusion` restricted along `τ.hom` (`MatrixRepresentation.restrict_comp`
  and `hsq`). The Library's `MatrixRepresentation.characterSpace_restrict_iso` then compares
  character spaces along the group isomorphism `q.torus ≅ p.torus` given by `τ.inv`.

### Base-preserving equivalences of root data

Let `e : RootPairing.Equiv p.rootDatum q.rootDatum` be an equivalence of root data with
`a ∈ p.base.support ↔ e.indexEquiv a ∈ q.base.support` for every index `a` (the hypothesis
`hb`). Its weight map is `e.weightMap` (a linear equivalence `e.weightEquiv`).

* `map_positiveRootMonoid`: the image of `p.positiveRootMonoid` (the additive submonoid
  generated by the simple roots) under `e.weightEquiv` is `q.positiveRootMonoid`. This
  holds because `e.weightMap (root a) = root (e.indexEquiv a)` (Mathlib's
  `RootPairing.Hom.root_weightMap_apply`) and `e.indexEquiv` matches the simple indices.
* `weightMap_mem_positiveRootMonoid_iff`: `e.weightMap χ ∈ q.positiveRootMonoid ↔
  χ ∈ p.positiveRootMonoid`.
* `isDominantWeight_weightMap_iff`: `q.IsDominantWeight (e.weightMap χ) ↔
  p.IsDominantWeight χ`. The pairing of `e.weightMap χ` with the coroot of index
  `e.indexEquiv a` equals the pairing of `χ` with the coroot of index `a` (Mathlib's
  `RootPairing.Equiv.toLinearMap_weightEquiv` and `RootPairing.Hom.coroot_coweightMap_apply`).

### Highest-weight representations

Assume `E`, `τ`, `hsq`, `e` and `hb` as above, and in addition that the weight map of `e`
is the character map of `τ`: `e.weightMap χ = characterLattice.congr τ χ` for every
character `χ` of `p.torus` (the hypothesis `he`).

* `representationWeightSpace_restrict_iso_weightMap`: the weight space of
  `ρ.restrict E.hom` at `χ` is the weight space of `ρ` at `e.weightMap χ`.
* `isHighestWeight_restrict_iso_iff`: `p.IsHighestWeight (ρ.restrict E.hom) λ ↔
  q.IsHighestWeight ρ (e.weightMap λ)`. All four conditions of the Library's
  `IsHighestWeight` correspond. Irreducibility uses
  `MatrixRepresentation.isStableSubspace_restrict_iso_iff`. The weight spaces correspond by
  the previous lemma, so their suprema agree after reindexing by the bijection
  `e.weightEquiv`. The one-dimensional highest-weight space is the same subspace. The
  condition `λ - μ ∈ positiveRootMonoid` corresponds by
  `weightMap_mem_positiveRootMonoid_iff`.

## Pinned isomorphisms of Galois pinning data (namespace `GaloisPinningData`)

Fix fields `k`, `K` with `Algebra k K`, and affine group schemes `G`, `G'` over `Spec k`.
For a group scheme over `k`, `_K` denotes the actual base change along `Spec K → Spec k`.
The module declares a local instance that this base change of an affine group scheme is
affine.

`GaloisPinningData.Iso d d'` for `d : GaloisPinningData k K G` and
`d' : GaloisPinningData k K G'` is a structure with fields:

* `groupIso : G ≅ G'`, an isomorphism of group schemes over `k`;
* `torusIso : d.torus ≅ d'.torus`, an isomorphism of commutative group schemes over `k`;
* `square`: `d.inclusion ≫ groupIso.hom = torusIso.hom ≫ d'.inclusion`;
* `rootDatumEquiv`, an equivalence of the root data of the split pinnings
  `d.toSplitPinning` and `d'.toSplitPinning` over `K`;
* `weightMap_eq`: the weight map of `rootDatumEquiv` is the character map
  `characterLattice.congr` of the base change of `torusIso` to `K`, that is
  `χ ↦ (torusIso_K)⁻¹ ≫ χ`;
* `base_iff`: `rootDatumEquiv.indexEquiv` maps the simple indices of `d` exactly onto those
  of `d'`.

The isomorphisms of groups and tori are defined over `k`, so their base changes intertwine
the Galois actions and the star actions (below). The structure does not require the
pinning Lie vectors to correspond. It is therefore weaker than the Library's
`SplitPinning.Iso` after base change. The Tits obstruction does not depend on the Lie
vectors.

For `f : d.Iso d'`:

* `baseChangeGroupIso f : G_K ≅ G'_K` and `baseChangeTorusIso f : T_K ≅ T'_K` are the base
  changes of `groupIso` and `torusIso`. `baseChange_square` is the base change of `square`.
* `weightMap f : X^*(T_K) ≃ₗ[ℤ] X^*(T'_K)` is `rootDatumEquiv.weightEquiv`.
  `weightMap_apply` unfolds it, and `weightMap_eq_comp` states
  `f.weightMap χ = (torusIso_K)⁻¹ ≫ χ`.
* `weightMap_galoisRepresentation`: for `σ : K ≃ₐ[k] K`, `f.weightMap` intertwines the
  Galois actions of `σ` on characters. This is the Library's
  `characterLattice.galoisRepresentation_naturality` for the `k`-morphism `torusIso.inv`.
* `isDominantWeight_weightMap_iff`: dominance is preserved and reflected.
* `isHighestWeight_restrict_iff`: for an actual matrix representation `ρ` of `G'_K`,
  `ρ.restrict (baseChangeGroupIso f).hom` has highest weight `λ` for `d` if and only if
  `ρ` has highest weight `f.weightMap λ` for `d'`.

With `G` and `G'` locally of finite type over `k`:

* `rootDatumEquiv_comp_starAction`: for `σ : K ≃ₐ[k] K`,
  `rootDatumEquiv ∘ ⋆_d(σ) = ⋆_{d'}(σ) ∘ rootDatumEquiv` as equivalences of root data. This
  is the Library's `GaloisPinningData.starAction_comp_of_character_intertwining` applied
  with `base_iff` and `weightMap_galoisRepresentation`.
* `starAction_weightMap`: its weight form, `⋆_{d'}(σ)(f.weightMap χ) =
  f.weightMap (⋆_d(σ) χ)`.
* `isStarFixedWeight_weightMap_iff`: `d'.IsStarFixedWeight (f.weightMap χ) ↔
  d.IsStarFixedWeight χ` (Library `Reductive/InnerStarWeights`).

## Provenance

Internal. The transport of the Tits obstruction is stated along actual group isomorphisms
and compatible pinning isomorphisms. `GaloisPinningData.Iso` is the notion of compatible
pinning isomorphism used for that statement. Its lemmas are routine
consequences of the definitions, obtained by combining the Library results named above with
Mathlib's root-pairing API. The transport of the obstruction itself is proved in a separate
module.
