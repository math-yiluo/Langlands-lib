# Dual-number coordinates of actual rank-one cells over rings

This internally derived construction uses the actual open rank-one cell over
a commutative ring. The point `xp(1) xm(ε)` lies in that cell: reduction modulo
the square-zero variable is the positive-root point at one, and the reduction
map is surjective on the underlying spectra.

The coefficient extension on dual numbers and the unit `1 + c ε` are defined
over arbitrary commutative rings. Given ring Weyl data and an affine ambient
group scheme, the arbitrary-coordinate rank-one relation and uniqueness of
the cell coordinates show that the coefficient of ε in the positive coordinate
is minus the inverse beta unit.
No reducedness or field hypothesis is used in this calculation.
