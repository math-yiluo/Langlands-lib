# Local split isomorphism from nilradical reflection

The theorem `localSplitIsomorphismTheorem_of_nilradicalReflection` derives the
unchanged full-universe local split isomorphism statement from the explicit
unproved `ReductiveNilradicalIsomorphismReflectionStatement`.

For a local ring `R`, its quotient `A` by the nilradical is nonzero because the
nilradical lies in the maximal ideal. The quotient is local by surjectivity of
the quotient map and reduced because its defining ideal is radical. The proof
pulls both actual reductive groups and their coordinate split-torus maps to
`A`. Reductivity, closedness of the torus inclusions, residue-fibre maximality,
and the full common coordinate root data survive this base change by the
imported transport results. The published reduced local theorem supplies an
isomorphism over `A`. The explicit reflection premise then supplies existence
over the original ring.

This is an internal composition of the published reduced theorem with
base-change and quotient-ring results. It adds no reducedness, noetherian,
henselian or characteristic hypothesis to the original target. The
nilradical reflection premise remains open; no unconditional local
isomorphism theorem is claimed.
