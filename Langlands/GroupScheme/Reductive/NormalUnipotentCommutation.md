# Normal unipotent subgroups and normal reductive subgroups

Worker 1372. Internal construction from pointed image generation,
equivariance of commutators, stability of closed envelopes, and triviality
of the unipotent radical.

The module constructs `normalCommutator`, the actual smooth connected
normal closed subgroup generated by the commutators of two smooth
connected normal closed subgroups of a smooth affine field group.
Its geometric conclusion includes a dominant positive ordered product
from the original source. The product source is geometrically integral
by the published smooth connected affine geometry theorem, and its pair
of identity sections supplies the point required for generation.

Ambient conjugation restricts to each normal input. The commutator
morphism is equivariant for the product action and ambient conjugation,
so stability of the closed image envelope proves full ambient normality.
Normality of each input also factors the commutator morphism through
that input; minimality gives actual closed homomorphisms from the
generated subgroup into both inputs.

If the first input is unipotent and the second has trivial ground-field
unipotent radical, the generated group is unipotent by the closed subgroup
theorem. Its inclusion in the second input is normal by cancellation
through that input's closed inclusion in the ambient group. Its
smoothness and connectedness therefore make it trivial. This proves
`commute_of_normal_unipotent_of_trivialRadical` for all points on all test
schemes over every field in universe `u`.

For a connected reductive second input, its ground-field radical is
trivial. The commutation theorem consequently places the full
scheme-theoretic intersection with the normal unipotent input in the
second input's centre. This includes nonreduced tests. Triviality of this
possibly nonsmooth central intersection, and the full radical-intersection
statement for a general normal subgroup, remain open here.
