# Full dynamic factors of the actual simple-root graph

This internal support module constructs smooth closed representatives of the two entire intrinsic unipotent factors of the actual Levi comparison graph, and proves that its common torus represents the entire middle dynamic centralizer. It does not prove that either graph projection is an isomorphism or prove the common-torus isomorphism theorem.

The graph data are an actual `SimpleComparisonGraph D` for torus inclusions `i₁ : T.toGrp ⟶ G₁` and `i₂ : T.toGrp ⟶ G₂` over a field `k`. Both ambient group schemes are smooth and affine. This record retains splitness of `T`, the generic chamber and simple-root directions, the actual local comparison isomorphisms and root maps, and their generated graph envelope. Its earlier constructor uses the rank-one local Levi hypothesis; the present module does not derive that hypothesis from equality of root sets.

## Closure envelopes and closed subcells

`RationalClosureEnvelope.rationalPoints_le` says that every generating rational point factors through its envelope inclusion. The smooth envelope has underlying image the closure of the locations of those points. The rational point source is reduced and has one underlying point, so the existing reduced-source factorization criterion applies. `range_le_of_rationalPoints_le` then says that containment of those rational points in a subgroup functor represented by a closed inclusion forces containment of the whole envelope, on every scheme test. Smoothness makes the envelope reduced. These two results work over every field, with an envelope supplied; they do not assert the existence of every such envelope without the hypotheses of its earlier existence theorem.

`tripleCell_factor_ranges_eq_of_dense` is an independent closed-subcell criterion, also over every field. Its larger three factors `M`, `Z`, `A` are smooth group schemes with closed homomorphisms `m`, `z`, `a` into a group scheme `H`. Their actual three-factor multiplication must be an open immersion. Its smaller factors `N`, `B`, `U` have closed inclusions `n`, `b`, `u` whose subgroup-functor ranges lie in the respective larger ranges. The locations in `H` of the literal rational products from `n`, `b`, `u` must be dense. Under precisely these assumptions, all three pairs of ranges are equal on every scheme test. Neither algebraic closedness, smoothness of the smaller factors, nor smoothness of `H` is an extra assumption of this criterion.

The proof factors the smaller maps through the larger ones and forms their product map. Density of rational-product locations implies dominance of the smaller multiplication. The open immersion of the larger cell reflects dominance to this product map. For each coordinate, the preimage of the smaller closed subgroup contains this dominant image and therefore the whole larger product. The coordinate section then puts the entire corresponding larger factor inside the smaller subgroup. The smooth larger source is reduced, which upgrades underlying-image containment to actual factorization. The three coordinate arguments give the three all-test range equalities. The auxiliary results `isDominant_of_comp_openImmersion`, `factorsThrough_of_dominant_coordinate`, and `rationalTripleProduct_locations_subset_range` express the steps used in this proof.

`isRepresentedBy_dynamicFactors_of_dense_closed_subcell` specializes this criterion to a smooth affine group `H` with a cocharacter `χ`. Three closed subgroup inclusions must lie, respectively, in `U(-χ)`, `Z(χ)`, and `U(χ)` and have dense rational three-factor product locations. The published affine dynamic representatives are smooth, and their multiplication is the published open dynamic cell. Consequently the three supplied inclusions represent the entire three dynamic subgroup functors. This specialization does not require connectedness of `H` or algebraic closedness of the field.

## Actual graph factors

`SimpleComparisonGraph.GraphDynamicFactors D` records two actual `RationalClosureEnvelope`s for the abstract rational subgroups `D.negativeGenerated` and `D.positiveGenerated`. Their closed inclusions represent the full graph unipotent functors for the opposite chamber and the chamber. Its `torusRep` field says that the actual common graph torus inclusion represents the full dynamic centralizer of the chamber. These are all-test representability assertions. They do not identify an abstract generated rational subgroup with all rational points of the dynamic group.

`graphDynamicFactors D hi₁ hZ P₁ P₂ hP₁ hP₂ hroots` constructs this record over an algebraically closed field. It additionally assumes connectedness of `G₁`, closedness of `i₁`, the explicit all-test identity `Z_G₁(chamber) = range i₁`, realizing root data `P₁`, `P₂` for the two ambient inclusions, and equality of their actual root sets. The two datum index types have independent universes. The construction chooses the smooth rational closure envelopes supplied by the earlier existence theorem. Their ranges lie in the respective graph unipotent factors because the actual generating root points do. The torus range lies in the graph dynamic centralizer. The previously proved dense actual graph cell is contained in the rational triple product of these three closed inclusions. The closed-subcell criterion therefore identifies all three full graph factors.

The primary entry `graphDynamicFactorsOfReductive D P₁ P₂ hP₁ hP₂ hroots hG₁ hmax₁` replaces the explicit closedness and chamber-centralizer identity by connected reductivity of `G₁` and maximality of `i₁`. It still assumes an algebraically closed field, smooth affine `G₁` and `G₂`, the actual graph data, both realizing root data, and equal actual root sets. It uses the published unrestricted `dynamicCentralizerReductive` proof in the earlier chamber-centralizer reduction, together with its already published structural inputs. No centralizer-reductivity statement remains as an unproved premise of this entry. No reductivity or maximality hypothesis on the second ambient inclusion is needed beyond the supplied graph and root-data assumptions.

Once this record is supplied, the two envelope groups are smooth, affine, and connected. The module registers these instances, using their envelope geometry and the published connectedness of represented dynamic unipotent factors. `torus_selfCentralizing` also proves that the actual graph torus is its own whole-torus centralizer: the latter lies in the represented chamber centralizer, and every torus centralizes its own image. These consequences do not add algebraic closedness or ambient connectedness assumptions beyond the supplied record.
