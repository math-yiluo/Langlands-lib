# Scalar extension of split pinnings along arbitrary field extensions

`SplitPinning.baseChangeField` extends a supplied split pinning along an arbitrary extension of its base field. Its torus and inclusion are the actual pullbacks, its root datum is transported by the canonical character and cocharacter lattice equivalences, and its base retains the supplied simple-root indices. The chosen simple-root vectors are the actual scalar extensions of the original vectors. Over an algebraic extension, this construction agrees with `SplitPinning.baseChange`.

The existing pinning base-change construction supplies the transported root datum, its reducedness, the simple-root base, and the trivializations of the pulled-back root lines, all without an algebraicity hypothesis. The only replacement in assembling the pinning is the use of the published preservation of connected reductivity under arbitrary field extension. Maximality of the torus uses the published maximal-torus base-change theorem. Thus every pinning field is verified on the actual group and its actual lattices.

Provenance: this is an internal composition of published group-scheme and pinning base-change results. It provides the split structure over arbitrary extension fields needed in Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017), discussion 22.5 (printed p.464), registered source `milne-2017-algebraic-groups`, SHA-256 `899b0f44f3c3ccfbaf5ed1cf9c180be7a8016b1c0e9709ca0c206304e5bd1cd0`.
