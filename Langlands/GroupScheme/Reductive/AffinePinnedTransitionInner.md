# Innerness of corrected transitions between pinned affine charts

Given a supplied natural relative pinned comparison, two pinned affine charts
of the same group, and maps from an arbitrary common test scheme to these
charts, the inverse corrected transition of the original pulled-back group
is the actual action of a point of any supplied full-centre fppf quotient.
The supplied action is required to restrict to conjugation along that
quotient.

The proof changes the source of the supplied quotient to the first pinned
model by the actual chart identification. The full centre transports under
this isomorphism, and the action on the model is obtained by transporting
the acted-on group. Its restriction along the changed quotient is still
conjugation. The supplied pinned comparison then gives an actual quotient
point whose action is the original model comparison followed by the inverse
corrected model comparison. Conjugating back through the chart identification
cancels the original comparisons and gives precisely the inverse transition.

Supporting lemmas record transport of a full-centre quotient, transport of
the conjugation restriction, and evaluation of a transported action at an
actual base point. They apply over any scheme. The provenance is internal
composition of the published all-test centre invariance, fppf quotient
transport, action transport and base-point evaluation constructions. This
result assumes the natural pinned comparison and does not assert its
existence or effective descent.
