# The centralizer of a fibrewise regular split-torus cocharacter

This internal result concerns a reductive group over an arbitrary ring, a closed
coordinate split torus that is maximal on every fibre, a full common fibrewise
root datum, and a supplied integer exponent nonzero on every root. It identifies
the dynamic centralizer of the actual exponent cocharacter with the range of
the original torus on every test scheme.

The proof chooses an actual locally finitely presented representative of the
centralizer and factors the torus inclusion through it. On every residue-field
fibre, the published regular-cocharacter centralizer theorem identifies that
centralizer with the torus. Uniqueness of actual representatives makes the
comparison an isomorphism on each fibre. The Library fibrewise-isomorphism
criterion applies because the split torus is smooth and the centralizer is
locally of finite type. Its global isomorphism and the factorization equation
give the asserted equality of subgroup functors. No reducedness or
Noetherian hypothesis on the ring is used. The provenance is this internal
composition of the Library's dynamic representability, field regularity,
and fibrewise isomorphism results.

The construction `centralizerIso` also identifies any supplied actual centralizer
representative with the original split torus. Its inclusion-compatibility equation
is the uniqueness comparison equation for the two actual representatives.
