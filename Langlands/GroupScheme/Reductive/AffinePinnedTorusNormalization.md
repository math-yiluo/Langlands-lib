# Etale-local inner alignment of the actual coordinate tori

This internal module proves the first geometric normalization step for an
actual comparison between two supplied affine pinned models pulled back to
an arbitrary common scheme.

The two coordinate torus inclusions remain fibrewise maximal under arbitrary
base change. Transporting the source inclusion by the comparison gives two
split maximal tori of the target group. The published etale-local maximal
torus conjugacy theorem supplies an etale cover, an actual target conjugator,
and a torus-source isomorphism on each member.

The proof transports these data through the actual direct/iterated pullback
identifications. Inner conjugation commutes with the target identification,
and the torus coordinate identifications give the required actual normalized
torus square.

The common scheme may be disconnected, nonaffine, empty or not quasi-compact.
This result aligns the tori only. It does not align the chosen bases,
cocharacters or pinning vectors and does not prove the full comparison target.
