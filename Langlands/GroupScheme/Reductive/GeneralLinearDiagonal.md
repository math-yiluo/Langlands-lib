# The diagonal torus of `GL_n` is a closed subgroup

Worker 274.

This is a helper module for the proof of `GeneralLinearRootDatumStatement`. It proves
`isClosedSubgroup_diagonalTorus n R`: over any commutative base ring `R`, the
underlying morphism of schemes of `GeneralLinear.diagonalTorus n R` is a closed
immersion. Milne (Example 17.88, printed p.380, PDF 396) takes the diagonal
torus `D_n ⊆ GL_n` as an algebraic subgroup. The scheme-theoretic proof over an
arbitrary base below is internal.

## Diagonal invertible matrices

`IsDiagonalGL M` says that the off-diagonal entries of `M : GL n A` vanish.
For such `M`, the lemmas `diag_mul_inv_diag` and `inv_diag_mul_diag` show that
the diagonal entries are units with inverses the diagonal entries of `M⁻¹`. The
proof expands `M M⁻¹ = 1` at `(c, c)`, where only one term of the sum survives.
`diagUnits hM` collects these units, indexed by `ULift n`, and
`diagonalTorusUnits_diagUnits` recovers `M`. The module also proves
`isDiagonalGL_diagonalTorusUnits`, `diagonalTorusUnits_injective`, and
`IsDiagonalGL.map`, which says that ring homomorphisms preserve diagonality.

## The diagonal locus

- `offDiagonalIdeal n R` is the ideal of the published coordinate ring
  `CoordinateRing n R` generated by the off-diagonal entry variables.
  `DiagonalRing n R` is the quotient.
- `diagonalLocus n R` is `algSpec` of the quotient, and `diagonalLocusι` is the
  spectrum of the quotient map. Its underlying morphism is a closed immersion
  (`IsClosedImmersion.spec_of_surjective`), hence a monomorphism over `Spec R`.
- `isDiagonalGL_diagonalLocusι`: the matrix of the inclusion is diagonal. This
  is computed through the published `pointsEquiv_eq_affinePointsEquiv` and
  `affinePointsEquiv_map`, since the quotient map kills the off-diagonal
  variables.
- `diagonalLocusLift x hx`: a point `x` of `GL_n` with diagonal matrix lifts
  through the diagonal locus (`diagonalLocusLift_comp`). The classifying
  algebra map of `x`, obtained from the relative `Spec`/`Γ` adjunction
  `algΓAlgSpecAdjunction`, kills the off-diagonal ideal by the published
  `adjunction_homEquiv_symm_apply` and `pointsEquiv_apply_apply`. It therefore
  factors through the quotient, and adjunction naturality gives the
  factorization.

## The isomorphism with the diagonal locus

The universal point of the diagonal torus has diagonal matrix, so it lifts to
`diagonalTorusToLocus : D_n.X ⟶ diagonalLocus` over `GL_n`. In the other
direction, `locusToDiagonalTorus` is the point of the split torus whose
coordinates are the diagonal entries of the inclusion of the locus. The two
composites are identities. One is checked after composing with the
monomorphism `diagonalTorus`, the other after composing with the monomorphism
`diagonalLocusι`. This gives `diagonalTorusLocusIso`. Thus the underlying
morphism of `diagonalTorus` is an isomorphism followed by a closed immersion,
and `isClosedSubgroup_diagonalTorus` follows.
