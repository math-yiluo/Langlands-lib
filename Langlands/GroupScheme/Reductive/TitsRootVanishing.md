# Vanishing of the Tits obstruction on the root span

Tits (*Représentations linéaires irréductibles d'un groupe réductif sur un
corps quelconque*, J. reine angew. Math. 247 (1971), Corollary 3.5) shows
that the obstruction `α` vanishes on the star-fixed part of the root
lattice. In the last paragraph of the proof of Theorem 3.3 (p. 204), a split
representation whose highest weight lies in the root lattice factors through
the adjoint group; Tits evaluates the resulting representation of the adjoint
group directly on its `k_s`-points to lift the projective cocycle. That
argument is valid. What can fail over an imperfect separably closed field is
the surjectivity of the map from the points of `G` to the points of its
adjoint group: for example, over `K = 𝔽₂(t)^sep` the class of `diag(t, 1)` is
a point of `PGL₂(K)`, and a lift to `SL₂(K)` would need `t` to be a square in
`K`; the nonsmooth centre `μ₂` is no obstacle to evaluating a representation
already defined on `PGL₂`.

The implemented argument is a different route that bypasses the
factorization through the adjoint group. It uses this development's
maximal-weight form of Proposition 3.4
(`Reductive/HighestWeightMaximalSubquotient`) for Galois-rational
representations: a star-fixed weight of multiplicity one that is maximal
among the weights of such a representation has trivial obstruction
(`titsObstructionValue_eq_zero_of_rational`). In this module the premise is
`HasRationalMaximalWeight` for `δ` and for each `δ - s_q`.
`Reductive/GaloisRationalBaseChange` derives it from the premise
`HasGroundMaximalWeight`: a matrix representation of `G` over `k` on
`k^{m+1}` whose base change to `K` has the weight with multiplicity one and
no weight strictly above it in the order of `Q⁺`.
`Reductive/GroundAdjointExterior` constructs those witnesses.

This module reduces root-span vanishing to the existence of such
representations. The hypotheses are:

- `hspan : corootSpan ℤ = ⊤` (simply connected case);
- `K/k` Galois.

## Declarations

- `titsObstructionValue_congr`: the value depends only on the weight.
- `titsObstructionValue_eq_zero_of_mem_rootSpan_of_starOrbitSum`: if the
  obstruction vanishes on every star-orbit sum `s_q`
  (`Reductive/StarOrbitRootSums`), it vanishes on every star-fixed element
  of `rootSpan ℤ`. Restrict `titsObstructionOn` to the subgroup generated by
  the orbit sums. It vanishes on the generators, hence on the whole subgroup
  by closure induction. That subgroup contains every star-fixed element of
  the root span (`mem_closure_starOrbitSum`).
- `titsObstructionValue_starOrbitSum_eq_zero`: for a star-fixed `δ`, if the
  obstruction vanishes at `δ` and at `δ - s_q`, it vanishes at `s_q`. This
  follows from additivity, since `δ = (δ - s_q) + s_q`.
- `HasRationalMaximalWeight ν`: some Galois-rational representation of `G_K`
  (`IsGaloisRational`, `Reductive/TitsMaximalWeight`) has `ν` as a weight of
  multiplicity one that is maximal among its weights.
- `titsObstructionValue_eq_zero_of_hasRationalMaximalWeight`: for star-fixed
  `ν` with `HasRationalMaximalWeight ν`, the obstruction of `ν` vanishes.
  This is `titsObstructionValue_eq_zero_of_rational`.
- `titsObstructionValue_eq_zero_of_mem_rootSpan`: let `δ` be star-fixed, and
  suppose `δ` and every `δ - s_q` have rational maximal weights. Then the
  obstruction vanishes on every star-fixed element of the integral root
  span.

The witnesses used in `SimplyConnected/TitsObstructionRootVanishing` are
exterior powers of the adjoint representation,
which is defined over `k`. Let `N` be the number of positive roots and `δ`
their sum. Then `δ` is the largest weight of `Λ^N(Lie G)`, with multiplicity
one. For each orbit `q`, `δ - s_q` is a maximal weight of multiplicity one of
`Λ^{N - |q|}(Lie G)`. Those representation-theoretic facts are not proved
here; they are the hypotheses of the last theorem.
