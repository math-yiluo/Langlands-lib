# Actual irreducibility of the finite covariant socle

This is a derived extension of `CovariantSocle`, using the coordinate coalgebra,
finite regular representations and rational stability detection already in the
Library. It invokes no external existence theorem or literature result.

Let `k : Type u` be a field and let `G` be an actual group scheme affine over
`Spec k`. For any coordinate `f` of `G`,
`Chevalley.affineCovariantSocleRepresentation G f` is the actual matrix
representation on `Fin (Module.finrank k (affineCovariantSocle G f))`. It uses
`Module.finBasis` of the finite singleton regular span and that span's
right-contraction stability. This construction requires no covariance, cell,
unipotence, characteristic, reducedness or algebraic-closedness hypothesis.

Three helpers apply to any finite right-stable coordinate subspace `V` and a
supplied finite basis `b`. First,
`affineRegularRepresentation_universalMatrix` identifies the transported actual
regular matrix at `algPoint G (𝟙 _)` with `subspaceMatrix V b`. The transport
isomorphism cancels at this universal point. Second,
`affineRegularCoordinateMap_rightAct` expands the comultiplication of the basis
vectors and commutes the finite sums to identify the coordinate of a
dual-contracted universal matrix translate with `rightAct` of the original
coordinate. Third, `rightAct_mem_map_affineRegularCoordinateMap` specializes
all-affine-test stability to this universal point. It proves right-contraction
stability of the coordinate image of any stable subspace.

The irreducibility theorems supply actual morphisms `i : H ⟶ G` and
`j : U ⟶ G`, with `H` affine and `U` unipotent, and a coordinate `χ` of `H`.
They also assume a coordinate `q` of `G` that is a nonzerodivisor and the following
product-cell property: on every relative test scheme `T`, whenever `q` evaluates
to a unit at `g : T ⟶ G.X`, there are `a : T ⟶ H.X` and `u : T ⟶ U.X`
with `g = i(a) * j(u)`. The tests here have no affineness or reducedness
restriction. The supplied `f` belongs to the tensor-defined left-covariant
subspace for `(i, χ)`, is right-invariant through `j` on every relative test,
and has counit one. No separate character or immersion condition is imposed
on `χ`, `i` or `j`.

`affineRegularRepresentation_isIrreducible_covariantSocle` proves irreducibility
for every finite basis of the socle. The coordinate vector of `f` is nonzero
because its counit is one. If `W` is a nonzero all-affine-test stable coordinate
subspace, its image in the coordinate algebra is nonzero by injectivity of the
basis-coordinate map, is covariant by containment in the socle, and is
right-stable by the third helper. The preceding socle leastness theorem forces
this image to contain the entire socle. Every coordinate vector therefore has
the same image as a vector of `W`; injectivity puts it in `W`. Thus `W` is top.
`affineCovariantSocleRepresentation_isIrreducible` specializes to the chosen
finite basis. These conclusions use the Library's actual `IsIrreducible`
condition: a nontrivial coordinate space and no proper nonzero subspace stable
on all affine tests, including nonreduced test algebras.

With the additional hypotheses `[IsAlgClosed k]`, `[IsReduced G.X.left]` and
`[LocallyOfFiniteType G.X.hom]`,
`affineCovariantSocleRepresentation_rationalRepresentation_isIrreducible`
proves irreducibility of the actual rational-point representation. A rational
subrepresentation is stable on all affine tests by the existing
`isStableSubspace_of_rationalMatrix`; the preceding result makes it bottom or
top. Nontriviality follows from the already nontrivial coordinate space.

The result concerns the finite socle generated by the supplied `f`. It does not
establish finiteness of the entire covariant space or its equality with this
socle. Existence of the group-theoretic witnesses and normalized coordinate,
cofinal Steinberg irreducibility and the general `SteinbergBigCellStatement`
remain separate obligations.
