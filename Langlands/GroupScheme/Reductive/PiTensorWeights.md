# Tensor products of finite families of representations and their top weights

This internal module builds the tensor product of a finite family of matrix
representations and computes its top weight. It supplies the representation
used in this development's proof of the integral-weight transfer law for
the Tits obstruction (`Reductive/TitsTransfer`), a derived analogue of Tits,
*Représentations linéaires irréductibles d'un groupe réductif sur un corps
quelconque*, J. reine angew. Math. 247 (1971), Proposition 5.1, printed
p. 207 / PDF p. 12, which is stated for `β` on central characters. For a weight `λ` fixed by
`Gal(K/E)`, that proof uses the tensor product of the conjugates `ρ^{ε_σ}` of
a representation `ρ` of highest weight `λ`, one for each embedding
`σ : E → K`. Its top weight `∑_σ ε_σ ⋆ λ` has multiplicity one. Tits uses the
same tensor product for `GL_1` of a division algebra in §5.3 (printed p. 208
/ PDF p. 13); the general family and its weight computation are this
development's own.

## The tensor product of a family (`MatrixRepresentation`)

Let `G` be a group scheme over a commutative ring and `n` a finite type.

- `piKronecker_fin_one`, `piKronecker_fin_succ`: the Kronecker product
  `piKronecker` (`Algebra/KroneckerInduction`) of a family indexed by
  `Fin 1` is its only factor, reindexed along `Equiv.funUnique`. Over
  `Fin (m + 1)` it is the binary Kronecker product of the first factor with
  the Kronecker product of the others, reindexed along `Fin.consEquiv`. Both
  are `Fin.prod_univ_one` and `Fin.prod_univ_succ` entrywise.
- `piTensor ρ` for `ρ : Fin (m + 1) → MatrixRepresentation G n`: the
  representation on the coordinates `Fin (m + 1) → n`, defined by recursion on
  `m` from the published binary tensor product `MatrixRepresentation.tensor` and
  the published reindexing `MatrixRepresentation.reindex`:
  `ρ 0` reindexed for `m = 0`, and `(ρ 0 ⊗ piTensor (ρ ∘ succ))` reindexed
  along `Fin.consEquiv` otherwise.
- `piTensor_affineMatrix`: on every affine test point the matrix of
  `piTensor ρ` is the Kronecker product `piKronecker` of the matrices of the
  `ρ i`. By induction, using `reindex_affineMatrix`, `tensor_affineMatrix` and
  the two `piKronecker` lemmas.

## Reindexing (`MatrixRepresentation`, over a field)

- `reindexMatrix e`: the coordinate permutation matrix of `e : n ≃ m`, so
  that `reindexMatrix e *ᵥ v = v ∘ e.symm`.
- `reindexMatrix_mul_reindexMatrix_symm`: `reindexMatrix e` and
  `reindexMatrix e.symm` are inverse to each other.
- `intertwines_reindex`: `reindexMatrix e` intertwines `ρ` with `ρ.reindex e`.
  Both sides of the intertwining equation are `ρ(x)` with rows reindexed by
  `e.symm` (`Matrix.submatrix_mul_equiv`).

## Top weights (`SplitPinning`)

Let `p` be a split pinning of a group `H` over a field `F`.

- `HasTopWeight p ρ λ`: `λ` is a weight of `ρ` of multiplicity one and every
  weight `μ` of `ρ` satisfies `λ - μ ∈ Q⁺` (the monoid generated by the
  simple roots). These are the last two clauses of the published
  `IsHighestWeight`, without irreducibility and without the weight
  decomposition (`IsHighestWeight.hasTopWeight`).
- `HasTopWeight.tensorWeightSpan_eq_bot_of_ne`,
  `HasTopWeight.representationWeightSpace_tensor_add`,
  `HasTopWeight.finrank_tensor`, `HasTopWeight.sub_mem_tensor` and
  `HasTopWeight.tensor`: if `ρ` and `τ` have top weights `λ` and `μ`, then
  `ρ ⊗ τ` has top weight `λ + μ`. The proofs are those of
  `Reductive/TensorWeights` for `IsHighestWeight`, which use only these two
  clauses. The weight space of `λ + μ` is spanned by the Kronecker products of
  weight vectors of weights `α`, `β` with `α + β = λ + μ`, and pointedness of
  `Q⁺` forces `α = λ` and `β = μ`.
- `HasTopWeight.eq_of_sub_mem`: a top weight is maximal: a weight `μ` with
  `μ - λ ∈ Q⁺` equals `λ`, by pointedness of `Q⁺`.
- `HasTopWeight.of_intertwines`: top weights are transported along an
  invertible intertwiner `C`. The weight spaces correspond under `C`
  (`Intertwines.map_weightSpace_eq_of_surjective`), and `C` is injective.
- `HasTopWeight.reindex`: hence top weights are preserved by reindexing.
- `HasTopWeight.piTensor`: if each `ρ i` has top weight `λ i`, then
  `piTensor ρ` has top weight `∑ i, λ i`. By induction on `m`, with
  `HasTopWeight.tensor` and `HasTopWeight.reindex`.
