# Statements: weight decomposition, root data of split reductive groups, and `GL_n`

Worker 274.

This module states five propositions as `Prop`-valued definitions. All of them are
over an arbitrary field `k : Type u` with base `Spec (CommRingCat.of k)`, and all
use the definitions of the modules `Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.Lattice`,
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.Weights`, `Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.Maximal` and
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Reductive.RootDatum`. Nothing is proved here.

## Sources

(printed page = PDF page − 16):

- Theorem 12.12, printed pp.234–235 (PDF 250–251): representations of
  diagonalizable groups decompose into weight spaces. §21.a, printed pp.424–425 (PDF 440–441),
  applies this to `Ad` on `g`.
- §21.c with Theorem 21.11 and Corollary 21.12, printed pp.428–430
  (PDF 444–446): the root datum of a split reductive group, the uniqueness of the
  coroot `α^∨` in 21.11(e), and the reducedness of the root datum.
- Proposition 21.1, printed p.424 (PDF 440): `W(G, T)(k) = N(k)/T(k)`.
  Theorem 21.37, printed p.435 (PDF 451), and Corollary 21.38, printed p.436
  (PDF 452): `W(G, T)` is generated by the reflections `s_α`, and
  `W(R) ≅ W(G, T)` canonically.
- Example 17.88, printed p.380 (PDF 396), Example 21.6, printed p.426 (PDF 442),
  and Example 21.40, printed p.436 (PDF 452): `D_n` is maximal in `GL_n`, the
  roots of `(GL_n, D_n)` are `eᵢ − eⱼ` (`i ≠ j`), and `W(GL_n, D_n) ≅ S_n`
  through permutation matrices.

Milne works with algebraic groups (group schemes of finite type) over a field.
The weight decomposition is stated for affine group schemes locally of finite
type, with the Lie algebra and weight spaces defined through actual dual-number
points. The formulation through Mathlib `RootDatum`, `RootPairing.weylGroup` and
`RootPairing.Equiv.weightHom`, and through normalizing base points, is
**derived**.

## The statements

1. `WeightDecompositionStatement`. Let `G` be a group scheme over `Spec k` with
   `IsAffineHom G.X.hom` and `LocallyOfFiniteType G.X.hom`, let `T` be a
   commutative group scheme with `IsSplitTorus T.toGrp`, and let
   `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G` be any homomorphism. Then
   `DirectSum.IsInternal (weightSpace i)`: the Lie algebra `Lie(G)(k)` is the
   internal direct sum of the weight spaces, over all `χ ∈ characterLattice T`.
   Decidable equality on characters is classical.
2. `RootDatumExistenceStatement`. Let `G` satisfy the published
   `IsConnectedReductive`, let `T` be split, and let `IsMaximalTorus i` hold.
   Then there are a type `J : Type u` with `Finite J` and
   `P : RootDatum J (characterLattice T) (cocharacterLattice T)` with
   `IsRootDatumOf i P` and `P.IsReduced`. The instance `IsAffineHom G.X.hom`
   used by `IsRootDatumOf` comes from `IsConnectedReductive`.
3. `RootDatumUniquenessStatement`. Under the same hypotheses, for any two root
   data `P` (indexed by `J`) and `P'` (indexed by `J'`) with `IsRootDatumOf i`,
   there is `e : J ≃ J'` with `P'.root ∘ e = P.root` and
   `P'.coroot ∘ e = P.coroot`.
4. `WeylGroupStatement`. Under the same hypotheses, for every `P` with
   `IsRootDatumOf i P`, two things hold:
   - `weylImage i = P.weylGroup.map (RootPairing.Equiv.weightHom P)`;
   - the kernel of `weylAction i` is `centralizerPoints i`, viewed as a subgroup
     of `normalizerPoints i` (`Subgroup.subgroupOf`).

   `weylAction i` needs the underlying morphism of `i` to be a monomorphism.
   This follows from the closed-immersion part of `IsMaximalTorus i`, which is
   provided as an instance.
5. `GeneralLinearRootDatumStatement`. For every `n : Type` with `Fintype n` and
   `DecidableEq n`, the following hold for
   `D = GeneralLinear.diagonalTorus n (CommRingCat.of k)`:
   - `IsMaximalTorus D`;
   - `roots D` is the set of characters `χ` with
     `splitTorus.characterLatticeEquiv k (ULift n) χ = e_{⟨i⟩} − e_{⟨j⟩}` for
     some `i ≠ j`;
   - `IsRootDatumOf D (GeneralLinear.rootDatum n k)`;
   - `(GeneralLinear.rootDatum n k).IsReduced`;
   - `weylImage D = (GeneralLinear.permutationAction n k).range`;
   - `GeneralLinear.permutationAction n k` is injective.
