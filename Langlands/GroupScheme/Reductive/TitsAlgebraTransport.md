# Tits algebras under restriction along isomorphisms defined over `k`

This module is the representation-theoretic part of the transport of the
Tits obstruction along isomorphisms of groups. Galois twisting commutes with
restriction along homomorphisms defined over `k`. Consequently the Tits
algebra of a representation of `G_K` (the `k`-algebra generated by its
coefficient matrices, `MatrixRepresentation/TitsAlgebra`) does not change
when the representation is restricted along the scalar extension of an
isomorphism `H ≅ G` defined over `k`. Both Tits algebras are the
twisted-fixed algebra of the same family of Galois intertwiners. The module
is internal infrastructure for comparing Tits classes along isomorphisms of
groups; the motivation is the descent construction in the proof of
Theorem 3.3 of Tits, *Représentations linéaires irréductibles d'un groupe
réductif sur un corps quelconque*, J. reine angew. Math. 247 (1971),
pp. 203–204.

## Declarations

- `ext_pointMatrix`: a matrix representation is determined by its point
  matrices. Taking the identity point, the underlying morphisms agree, since
  `GeneralLinear.pointsEquiv` is injective.
- `transport_restrict`: transport along an endomorphism `a` of `Spec R` over
  the base (`MatrixRepresentation/GaloisTwist`) commutes with restriction
  along the base change of a homomorphism `f : H ⟶ G` over the base. The point
  matrices agree by `pointMatrix_transport` and
  `pointMatrix_transport_restrict` (`Reductive/GaloisTwistWeights`).
- `galoisTwist_restrict`: the Galois twist by `σ` commutes with restriction
  along `f_K` for `f` defined over `k`. This is `transport_restrict` for
  `a = Spec σ`.
- `intertwines_restrict_galoisTwist`: an intertwiner `ρ → ρ^σ` is also an
  intertwiner `ρ ∘ f_K → (ρ ∘ f_K)^σ`.
- `IsIrreducible.restrict_iso`: restriction along an isomorphism preserves
  irreducibility, by the published `isStableSubspace_restrict_iso_iff`.
- `scalar_of_restrict_iso`: if all self-intertwiners of `ρ` are scalar, so
  are those of its restriction along an isomorphism. Restrict back along the
  inverse.
- `titsAlgebra_restrict_iso`: let `ρ` be irreducible with scalar
  self-intertwiners and invertible Galois intertwiners `P σ : ρ → ρ^σ`, and
  let `e : H ≅ G` be defined over `k`. Then
  `titsAlgebra (ρ.restrict e_K.hom) = titsAlgebra ρ`. By
  `titsAlgebra_eq_twistedFixed` (`MatrixRepresentation/TitsAlgebraFixed`),
  both sides equal `twistedFixed P`: the same `P σ` are Galois intertwiners
  for the restriction.
- `TitsAlgebraRestrictIsoStatement` packages the last theorem over all
  Galois extensions, groups and representations. `titsAlgebraRestrictIso`
  proves it.

The irreducibility comparison uses `IsIrreducible.restrict_iso` from `MatrixRepresentation/IrreducibleRestriction`.
