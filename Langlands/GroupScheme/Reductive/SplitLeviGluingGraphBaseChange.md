# Field extension of the actual comparison graph

This internal support module constructs the field extension of an actual generated graph envelope, with its actual torus and local lifts and both projection maps preserved. It also descends invertibility of those extended projections to the ground field. It does not prove their invertibility.

Let `k : Type u` be a field and `K : Type u` an extension field with its specified `k`-algebra structure. Let `G₁`, `G₂` be smooth affine group schemes over `Spec k`. Let `T` be a smooth connected affine group scheme over `Spec k`, with homomorphisms `i₁ : T ⟶ G₁`, `i₂ : T ⟶ G₂`. Let `I : Type v` be arbitrary, and let each `A j` be a smooth connected affine group scheme with maps `a₁ j`, `a₂ j` to the two ambient groups. No finiteness or nonemptiness of `I` is required. The universes `u` and `v` are independent. The extension may be inseparable, infinite or transcendental; no perfectness, algebraic closedness or characteristic restriction is assumed.

The supplied `D : GraphEnvelope A a₁ a₂ i₁ i₂` is the earlier actual smooth connected affine closed subgroup of `G₁ × G₂` generated by the paired maps and the distinguished paired common-group map. The common source `T` is treated here as a group scheme; it need not be supplied as a commutative group scheme or as a torus. In the intended common-torus application, splitness supplies its required geometry. The graph's generation property tests arbitrary closed competitors on every scheme test, not merely rational points.

## Transport of generation

`IsGeneratedByFamily.comp_iso` transports closed family generation by an actual ambient group-scheme isomorphism. Family factorizations compose with the isomorphism, and minimality follows by moving each closed competitor back along its inverse. This auxiliary result needs only the supplied generation property, with no smoothness or connectedness assumption.

`baseChange_lift_pair` identifies the pullback of a paired map, followed by the actual product comparison, with the pair of the two pulled-back maps. The proof checks both product projections using the existing base-change tensor isomorphism.

## The actual extended envelope

The primary entry is `GraphEnvelope.baseChange K D hA`. Its group is exactly the actual pullback of `D.group`. Its inclusion into `G₁_K × G₂_K` is the pullback of `D.inclusion` followed by `baseChangeTensorIso.hom`, which places the pullback of the original product in the product of the pullbacks.

Smoothness and affineness survive pullback. The original smooth connected affine graph is geometrically connected by the published theorem, so its pullback remains connected. The new `IsGeneratedByFamily.baseChange` theorem preserves its generation property for the actual pulled-back family. Transport along the product comparison then puts this generation statement in the required ambient product. A case split on the `Option I` index identifies the original paired common-source map and each original paired local map with their actual pulled-back pairs. The common source remains a generator when `I` is empty. Thus the returned object is an actual `GraphEnvelope` for the pulled-back data, with full minimality against arbitrary closed subgroup competitors.

`baseChange_first` and `baseChange_second` identify its actual first and second projections with the pullbacks of the original projections. `baseChange_torusLift` and `baseChange_localLift` identify its distinguished common-source and local lifts with the corresponding pullbacks. The latter equalities follow by the earlier all-test joint faithfulness of the graph projections and their two inclusion squares. They do not assume that either projection is injective individually.

## Descent of a proved comparison

`isIso_first_of_baseChange` and `isIso_second_of_baseChange` take invertibility of the corresponding extended projection as an explicit hypothesis and conclude invertibility of the original projection. The spectrum map of any field extension is flat, surjective and quasi-compact. The projection identifications therefore allow the published fpqc reflection of group-scheme isomorphisms to apply to the original actual homomorphisms.

`comparisonIsoOfBaseChange` assumes that both projections of this actual extended envelope are isomorphisms and returns the resulting actual isomorphism `G₁ ≅ G₂`. `torus_comparisonIsoOfBaseChange` proves that its forward map satisfies `i₁ ≫ comparison.hom = i₂`. These are conditional descent interfaces. Their projection-isomorphism hypotheses are not proved here and are not added to the final common-torus theorem as new premises.

## Provenance and remaining work

This is an internal composition of actual graph minimality, the new general preservation of generation under field extension, existing product base-change comparison, preservation of smooth connected affine geometry and fpqc reflection of isomorphisms. The accompanying `GeneratedFamilyBaseChange` pair supplies the general generation-preservation proof and is included as an exact helper.

To finish the common-torus theorem, the extended local comparison and root data must still be identified in the intended algebraic-closure setting, and invertibility of the graph projections must still be proved from those actual data. This module constructs the pulled-back graph and the descent interface; it proves neither exact Levi gluing nor the exact arbitrary-field common-torus isomorphism statement.
