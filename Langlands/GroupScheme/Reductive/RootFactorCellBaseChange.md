# Base change of actual ordered factor cells

For arbitrary schemes `S`, `S'` in universe `u`, a morphism `b : S' → S`, finite families `A : Fin m → GroupScheme S` and `B : Fin n → GroupScheme S`, and a middle group `D`, this internal helper constructs the actual source comparison between the pullback of their factor-cell source and the source formed from the actual pullbacks of the factors.

The comparison combines preservation of binary products by pullback with the published finite-product base-change isomorphism. Its three factor-projection formulas show that the negative ordered product, middle value and positive ordered product commute with this comparison. Multiplication therefore commutes with arbitrary base change. All factors and maps are the supplied actual group schemes and group homomorphisms; no reductivity, field, smoothness or root-datum hypothesis is used.
