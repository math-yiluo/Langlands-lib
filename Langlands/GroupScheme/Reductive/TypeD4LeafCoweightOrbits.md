# Based automorphisms and the Weyl orbits of leaf cocharacters

In a simply connected `D₄` root datum with the numbering of
`CartanMatrix.D 4`, the simple-root evaluations determine an
integral cocharacter. The proof writes the cocharacter in the
integral coroot coordinates. The dot products with the four simple
vectors determine the coordinates.

`exists_weyl_inv_coweightEquiv_leafParabolicCoweights` uses this
uniqueness to compare the leaf parabolic cocharacters under a
based automorphism. The simple-root evaluations of the numbered
positive leaf representatives are twice the corresponding unit
vectors. Each cocharacter in the zero-sum leaf triple is Weyl
conjugate to its positive representative. The automorphism
transports these evaluations to those of the new leaf, and
conjugation by the automorphism preserves the Weyl group because
it transports each generating reflection. The inverse coweight
map thus takes the initial cocharacter into the Weyl orbit for the
transported leaf.
