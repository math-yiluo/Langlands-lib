# Coefficient pinning equations on all affine tests

This internal coherence lemma compares coefficient extension of the actual
scalar-model pinning vector with the original model's dual-number pinning
point. The kernel base-change equivalence commutes with coefficient extension;
its first projection is the published scalar-extension pinning projection.
Composition of the coefficient maps gives the original chosen vector over the
test ring. The second pullback projection is the specified test base map.

An affine test's base-map equation supplies its scalar-tower compatibility,
including when the test carries independently supplied original coefficient
algebras. Naturality of the actual differential then extends equality of the
chosen vectors over the common coefficient ring to every local affine test.
No reducedness, nonemptiness, field, or locality assumption is made on the
common coefficient ring. This does not assert existence of such an equality
or of a pinning correction.
