# The full centre on a split reductive chart

This internal construction completes the local character-kernel step in Conrad,
*Reductive group schemes* (2014), Theorem 3.3.4, pp. 98–99. The ring is arbitrary.
The supplied coordinate split torus is a closed, fibrewise maximal torus in a
reductive group. Its common full root datum agrees with the actual roots on
every field-valued fibre, and every root weight module is supplied with a
linear trivialization as a free module of rank one. No reducedness or
domain assumption is imposed on the base or on the coefficient algebras.

`splitTorus.isSchemeTheoreticCenter_rootKernelHom` identifies the closed
inclusion of the full root-character kernel with the whole scheme-theoretic
centre. Its character group is the integral coordinate lattice modulo the
subgroup generated by all roots, without saturation. The construction retains
torsion and hence finite and non-smooth factors. `isDiagonalizable_rootKernel`
identifies the kernel with a relative diagonalizable model through the
published affine-to-relative comparison.

The forward containment uses the previously constructed full kernel's trivial
adjoint action over every coefficient algebra and the diagonalizable
centrality criterion for a reductive group. For the converse, the full centre
is contained in the given relative maximal torus. A central point acts
trivially on the actual Lie kernel: evaluating its centrality on the dual
numbers gives the conjugation identity. For each root, a functional on its
rank-one direct summand takes a chosen root vector to one. The smooth Lie
scalar-extension equivalence extends this functional to any coefficient
algebra. Applying it to the adjoint weight equation forces that root's
character value to be one. The full lattice quotient then factors the torus
point through the root kernel.

The helper `SubgroupFunctor.le_range_of_all_spectrum_tests` extends the
containment from affine spectrum tests to every test scheme: restrict to an
affine open cover, transport each member through its canonical affine-spectrum
isomorphism, and glue the local lifts through the monomorphic inclusion.
Thus the result identifies the functor on arbitrary test schemes and retains
its infinitesimal structure.

For a supplied `AffinePinnedModel`, `centerKernel` and `centerKernelHom` expose
the actual group and inclusion. `centerKernel_isSchemeTheoreticCenter` and
`centerKernel_isDiagonalizable` give the two local inputs needed for étale
descent to an arbitrary base. This module constructs and proves the local
representative; it does not itself assert the global descent theorem.
