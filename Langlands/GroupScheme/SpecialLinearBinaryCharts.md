# Affine chart restrictions of binary covariant coordinates

This derived construction restricts the determinant-one coordinate ring to the
two affine sections with bottom rows `(t,1)` and `(1,t)`. The restrictions are
actual algebra homomorphisms to a polynomial ring, and their specialization
formulas hold over every commutative test algebra.

Tensor covariance for the actual upper-triangular subgroup implies the matrix
covariance formula over each such algebra. The proof evaluates on its relative
spectrum, uses the all-test subgroup range comparison, and cancels the injective
spectrum counit. This retains arbitrary, possibly nonreduced algebras.

For any matrix with unit last bottom entry, multiplication by the inverse of
the first chart section gives an upper-triangular matrix whose last diagonal
is that entry. Covariance therefore recovers the coordinate from its first
restriction. The complementary formula uses the first bottom entry and the
second restriction. These are direct determinant-one matrix calculations,
with no density or smoothness premise.

The provenance is the published determinant-one classifier and actual upper
subgroup, ordinary matrix multiplication, and the spectrum adjunction. The
construction does not yet prove the degree bound, the joint injectivity of the
restrictions, or identification with the entire homogeneous polynomial space.
