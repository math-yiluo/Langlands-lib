# Closed submonoids with Noetherian affine squares are subgroups

This module works over an arbitrary base scheme `B` and an actual group scheme
`G : GroupScheme B`. It turns a closed submonoid of `G` into an actual closed
subgroup, provided the square of the submonoid is affine and locally
Noetherian. This is the closed-semigroup-to-subgroup step in the construction of
the group generated by a pointed integral morphism. Conrad–Gabber–Prasad
(Pseudo-reductive Groups, 2nd ed., paragraph before Proposition 3.3.5) cite
EGA IV₄ 17.9.6 for this step. The implemented argument is a different, purely
Noetherian one, so the provenance is internal.

`shearIso G` is the shear automorphism `(x, y) ↦ (x, x y)` of `G.X ⊗ G.X`, with
inverse `(x, y) ↦ (x, x⁻¹ y)`.

`isClosedImmersion_tensorHom_left`: if the underlying morphisms of `a : X ⟶ X'`
and `b : Y ⟶ Y'` in `Over B` are closed immersions, so is the underlying
morphism of `a ⊗ₘ b`. The underlying morphism is a map of fibre products over
`B`, and closed immersions are stable under base change and composition.

The remaining data are a morphism `j : Z ⟶ G.X` in `Over B` whose underlying
morphism is a closed immersion, a multiplication `m : Z ⊗ Z ⟶ Z` with
`m ≫ j = (fst ≫ j) * (snd ≫ j)`, and a unit `e : 𝟙_ (Over B) ⟶ Z` with
`e ≫ j = 1`. Associativity of `m` is not needed, since it is inherited from `G`.

- `shear m = lift (fst Z Z) m` is the restricted shear `(x, y) ↦ (x, x y)` of
  `Z ⊗ Z`. `shear_comp` shows `shear m ≫ (j ⊗ₘ j) = (j ⊗ₘ j) ≫ (shearIso G).hom`.
- `isIso_shear`: if `(Z ⊗ Z).left` is affine and locally Noetherian, `shear m` is
  an isomorphism. By `shear_comp`, its composite with the closed immersion
  `(j ⊗ₘ j).left` equals a closed immersion followed by an isomorphism, so
  `(shear m).left` is itself a closed immersion. Then
  `isIso_of_isClosedImmersion_endo` from `ImageGenerationNoetherian` makes it an
  isomorphism, and `Over.forget` reflects isomorphisms.
- `exists_inv_factor`: under the same hypotheses, for every test object `T` and
  `x : T ⟶ Z`, the inverse `(x ≫ j)⁻¹` factors through `j`. Apply the inverse of
  the shear to `(x, e)`. This gives `u` with `u ≫ fst = x` and `u ≫ m = e` on
  `T`, so `(x ≫ j) * (u ≫ snd ≫ j) = 1`.

With `[IsAffine (Z ⊗ Z).left] [IsLocallyNoetherian (Z ⊗ Z).left]`:

- `closedSubgroupFunctor j m hm e he : SubgroupFunctor G` has, on each test
  object `T`, the subgroup of points `x : T ⟶ G.X` factoring through `j`
  (`mem_closedSubgroupFunctor`). The identity, products and inverses lie in it
  by `he`, `hm` and `exists_inv_factor`; precomposition preserves it.
- `mono_of_isClosedImmersion_left`: `j` is a monomorphism in `Over B`.
- `closedSubgroup j m hm e he : GroupScheme B` is the published
  `SubgroupFunctor.representingGroupScheme` of this subgroup functor along the
  monomorphism `j`. Its underlying object is `Z` by definition
  (`closedSubgroup_X`).
- `closedSubgroupHom j m hm e he` is the corresponding actual group-scheme
  homomorphism into `G`. `closedSubgroupHom_hom` shows its underlying morphism
  is `j`, and `isClosedSubgroup_closedSubgroupHom` shows it is a closed subgroup
  in the sense of `IsClosedSubgroup`.

No field, smoothness, reducedness or connectedness hypothesis is used.
