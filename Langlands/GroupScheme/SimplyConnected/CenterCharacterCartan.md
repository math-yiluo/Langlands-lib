# Cartan relations on the actual full center

For the full center of a simply connected group with a chosen Tits index,
the fundamental weights of the absolute torus give generators of the
geometric center character group. Every integral Cartan row is the
coefficient vector of its simple root in the fundamental-weight basis.
Restriction to the center is surjective and kills exactly the integral
root span, so the Cartan rows are all the relations. An assignment to
the fundamental weights in any additive commutative group therefore
defines an integral linear map from the center characters if it
annihilates the Cartan rows.

For a numbering by `Fin n`, use the simple-root basis of the root span
and the fundamental-weight basis of the character lattice. Their
change-of-basis matrix is the transpose of the chosen Cartan matrix.
The determinant formula for the index of an integral submodule gives
the number of geometric center characters as the absolute value of
the Cartan determinant.

This internal construction makes the Cartan presentation of the actual
center available for arithmetic kernel comparisons.
