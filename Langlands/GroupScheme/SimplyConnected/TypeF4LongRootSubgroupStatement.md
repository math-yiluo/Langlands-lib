# Statement: maximal tori of type `F₄` groups lie in simply connected `D₄` subgroups

`Langlands.GroupScheme.TypeF4LongRootSubgroupStatement.{u}` is a `Prop`-valued
definition with one universe parameter. The module states it and proves nothing.

## Lean statement

```lean
def TypeF4LongRootSubgroupStatement : Prop :=
  ∀ (k : Type u) [Field k] [CharZero k]
    (G : GroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of k))) [IsAffineHom G.X.hom]
    [LocallyOfFiniteType G.X.hom] (d : TitsIndexData G) (e : d.Vertex ≃ Fin 4),
    IsSimplyConnected G → IsAbsolutelyAlmostSimple G →
    (∀ a b, d.cartan a b = CartanMatrix.F₄ (e a) (e b)) →
    ∀ (T : CommGroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of k))) (i : T.toGrp ⟶ G),
      IsMaximalTorus i →
      ∃ (H : GroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of k))) (j : H ⟶ G) (f : T.toGrp ⟶ H),
        IsClosedSubgroup j ∧ f ≫ j = i ∧ IsSimplyConnected H ∧
          IsAbsolutelyAlmostSimple H ∧
          ∃ (_ : IsAffineHom H.X.hom) (_ : LocallyOfFiniteType H.X.hom)
            (dH : TitsIndexData H) (eH : dH.Vertex ≃ Fin 4),
            ∀ a b, dH.cartan a b = CartanMatrix.D 4 (eH a) (eH b)
```

## Statement in words

Let `k` be a field of characteristic zero and `G` a simply connected, absolutely
almost-simple group over `k` of type `F₄`. Let `T` be a maximal torus of `G` over `k`.
Then `G` has a closed subgroup `H` over `k` that contains `T`, is simply connected and
absolutely almost simple, and is of type `D₄`.

## Objects and predicates

- `GroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of k))` is a group object of schemes over `Spec k`;
  `IsAffineHom` and `LocallyOfFiniteType` are hypotheses on the structure morphism.
- `IsSimplyConnected` (module `SimplyConnected.Basic`): semisimple, and every central
  isogeny onto the group from a semisimple group is an isomorphism.
- `IsAbsolutelyAlmostSimple` (module `Reductive.AbsolutelyAlmostSimple`): almost simple
  over `k` and after base change to an algebraic closure.
- `TitsIndexData` (module `Reductive.TitsIndex`): actual Tits-index choices, with a
  Galois pinning over the separable closure. `d.cartan a b` is the integral Cartan
  pairing of the simple roots `a` and `b` of that pinning. The type is fixed by a
  numbering of the simple vertices: `e` identifies the Cartan matrix of `G` with
  Mathlib's `CartanMatrix.F₄`, and `eH` that of `H` with `CartanMatrix.D 4`.
- `IsMaximalTorus i` (module `Torus.Maximal`): `T` is a torus, `i` is a closed subgroup,
  and `i` factors through no strictly larger closed torus of `G` over `k`.
- `IsClosedSubgroup j`: `j` is a homomorphism and a closed immersion. The equation
  `f ≫ j = i` says that the torus `T` lies in `H`.
- The two instance arguments under the existential make `TitsIndexData H` meaningful.
  Both already follow from `IsSimplyConnected H`.

## Source

V. Platonov and A. Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, Academic Press 1994,
registered as `platonov-rapinchuk-1994-algebraic-groups-number-theory` (SHA-256
`c333b953ca0ace6306663f648cd853a75da55023bf873e0ef2688d997216717f`; printed page `p` is
PDF page `p + 10`), §6.8, "Groups of type F₄", printed p. 371:

> To prove that ξ is trivial it suffices to show G has a simply connected K-subgroup
> H ⊃ T of type D₄. But the explicit description of the root system of type F₄ (cf.
> Bourbaki [4, Table 8]), implies that these requirements are met by the subgroup
> generated by the root subgroups G_α, where α runs through all the long roots of
> R(T, G).

The source works over a number field or a local field and with a maximal torus of the
quasi-split form. Its argument uses only the following facts, which hold for every
maximal torus of every group of type `F₄` over a field of characteristic zero.

## Derivation (provenance: derived)

Over a separable closure `T` splits and the roots `R(T, G)` form a root system of type
`F₄`. In the standard realization in `ℝ⁴` the long roots are `±eᵢ ± eⱼ` and the short
roots are `±eᵢ` and `½(±e₁ ± e₂ ± e₃ ± e₄)`. The long roots form a closed symmetric
subsystem of type `D₄`, the sum of two long roots being long whenever it is a root.

- *Rationality.* The Galois group acts on `R(T, G)` by automorphisms of the root system,
  which preserve root lengths. The set of long roots is therefore Galois stable, and the
  subgroup generated by `T` and the root groups of the long roots is defined over `k`.
  As in the analogous type `G₂` argument on printed pp. 368–369, it is a connected
  reductive subgroup containing `T` whose roots are the long roots.
- *Simple connectedness.* `G` is simply connected, so the cocharacter lattice of `T` is
  the coroot lattice of `F₄`. With `α^∨ = 2α/(α, α)` this lattice is spanned by
  `±eᵢ ± eⱼ`, `±2eᵢ` and `±e₁ ± e₂ ± e₃ ± e₄`, which is the lattice of integral vectors
  with even coordinate sum. The coroots `±eᵢ ± eⱼ` of the long roots span the same
  lattice, so the subgroup is simply connected by the criterion of Platonov–Rapinchuk,
  Theorem 2.6 C), printed p. 62.
- *Type.* The root system `D₄` is irreducible, so the subgroup is absolutely almost
  simple, and any Tits index of it has a numbering by `CartanMatrix.D 4`.

The statement asks only for the existence of such a subgroup, not for the identification
of the long-root subgroup. It makes no claim about the form of `H`: its star action may
be trivial or permute the three leaves of the `D₄` diagram.
