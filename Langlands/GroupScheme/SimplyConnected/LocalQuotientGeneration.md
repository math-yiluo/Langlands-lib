# Rational generation from quotient generation and kernel containment

Author: Worker 1757.

This module proves an abstract diagram chase on rational points of group
schemes. It is the group-theoretic core of the proof of Theorem A of
Prasad–Raghunathan (1985), §1.6, in the form used for nonarchimedean
local fields in §2. Every structural input of Theorem A is an explicit
hypothesis here. The module does not prove Theorem A or
`LocalKneserTitsStatement`.

All objects live in `Langlands.GroupScheme.QuotientGeneration`. Rational
points are the existing `rationalPoints G`, the sections of `G` over the
base, and `rationalPoints.map f` is postcomposition with a homomorphism
`f`.

## Kernel points

Let `k` be a commutative ring and let `d : D ⟶ M` and `q : M ⟶ Q` be
homomorphisms of group schemes over `Spec k`. The hypothesis `hker` says
that the full scheme-valued point range of `d` equals that of the actual
kernel inclusion `kernelι q`.

- `exists_kernel_point`: every rational point `x` of `M` with
  `q(x) = 1` is `d(v)` for some rational point `v` of `D`.
- `ker_map_eq_range_map`: the kernel of `rationalPoints.map q` equals
  the range of `rationalPoints.map d`.
- `eq_top_of_range_le_map`: let `H` be a subgroup of `M(k)` that
  contains the image of `D(k)`. If the image of `H` in `Q(k)` contains
  the image of all of `M(k)`, then `H = ⊤`.

The proof of `eq_top_of_range_le_map` is the standard argument: for `x`
in `M(k)` choose `h` in `H` with `q(h) = q(x)`. Then `h⁻¹ x` is killed by
`q`, so it comes from `D(k)` and lies in `H`, and `x = h (h⁻¹ x)` lies in
`H`.

## The family diagram chase

Take further a family indexed by a type `J`, with group schemes `M i` and
`Q i` and homomorphisms `m i : M i ⟶ M`, `qi i : M i ⟶ Q i` and
`φ i : Q i ⟶ Q`. Assume the squares commute: `m i ≫ q = qi i ≫ φ i`.

`generatedPoints m` is the subgroup of `M(k)` generated by the union of
the images of all `M i (k)`. It is an `abbrev` for this subgroup closure.

`generatedPoints_eq_top_of_surjective` assumes:

- every `rationalPoints.map (qi i)` is surjective;
- `hQ`: the image of `M(k)` in `Q(k)` lies in the subgroup generated by
  the images of all `Q i (k)`;
- `hD`: the image of `D(k)` lies in `generatedPoints m`.

It concludes `generatedPoints m = ⊤`, that is, the images of the
`M i (k)` generate `M(k)`. The proof applies `eq_top_of_range_le_map` to
`H = generatedPoints m`. For the quotient condition it suffices that each
generator `φ i (z)` lies in the image of `H`. Write `z = qi i (w)` by
surjectivity; then `m i (w)` lies in `H`, and commutativity gives
`q (m i (w)) = φ i (z)`.

## Surjectivity from cocycle vanishing

`generatedPoints_eq_top_of_finiteSeparable_vanishing` works over a field
`k`. It adds group schemes `D i` and homomorphisms `di i : D i ⟶ M i`
whose scheme-valued point ranges equal those of `kernelι (qi i)`. Each
underlying map of `di i` is monic, and each underlying scheme map of
`qi i` is smooth and surjective. It assumes that for every `i`, every
finite separable field extension `L/k` with `L : Type u` and every
actual `Cocycle (D i) (specOver k L)` is trivial. With `hQ` and `hD` as
above it proves `generatedPoints m = ⊤`. Surjectivity of each
`rationalPoints.map (qi i)` is the published
`QuotientPointCocycle.surjective_of_finiteSeparable_vanishing`.

`generatedPoints_eq_top_of_localCocycleVanishing` takes `k = F`, a
nonarchimedean local field (`Field`, `ValuativeRel`, `TopologicalSpace`
and `IsNonarchimedeanLocalField`). Assume also that each structure map of
`D i` is affine, each `D i` is simply connected (`IsSimplyConnected`),
and the existing proposition
`LocalSimplyConnectedCocycleVanishingStatement.{u}` holds. The
proposition is an explicit argument `hloc`. The theorem supplies the
vanishing hypothesis from `hloc` and concludes `generatedPoints m = ⊤`
under `hQ` and `hD`.

## Relation to Prasad–Raghunathan

In Theorem A, `G` is simply connected of `k`-rank at least 2, `M` is the
centralizer of a maximal `k`-split torus `S`, and `𝒢` is the derived
group of `M`. For each Galois orbit `Θ_i` in `Δ − Δ₀` (for the
`*`-action), `M_{Θ_i}` is the centralizer of `S_{Θ_i}` in `G_{Θ_i}`. By
§1.5 it is a subgroup of `M`. Theorem A says that the `M_{Θ_i}(k)`
generate `M(k)`.

The first step of the paper's proof shows that the subgroup generated by
the `M_{Θ_i}(k)` contains `𝒢(k)`. Take `m i` to be the inclusions of the
`M_{Θ_i}` and `d` the inclusion of `𝒢`; then this step is hypothesis
`hD`. The paper also shows that `M/𝒢` is isomorphic to the torus
`T_{Δ−Δ₀}`, which is the direct product of the tori `T_{Θ_i}`.

From there the paper's proof differs from the chase here. The paper maps
`M` into the Levi subgroups attached to the complements of the `Θ_i`. It
compares `M(k)/𝒢(k)` with a product of quotients by means of Theorem B,
and then identifies the resulting generators. The chase here instead
uses quotient maps `qi i` out of the factors themselves. It needs each
`qi i` to be surjective on rational points, and it needs `hQ`.

In the local case, §2 of the paper recalls that the first Galois
cohomology of a connected simply connected group over a nonarchimedean
local field is trivial. In this module that input is the explicit
hypothesis `hloc`. It gives the surjectivity of `qi i` on rational points
when the kernels `D i` are simply connected; Theorem B is not used.

One intended instantiation takes `Q = M/𝒢`, `D i` the derived group of
`M_{Θ_i}`, and `Q i = M_{Θ_i}/D i`. The expected source of `hQ` is then
the product decomposition of `M/𝒢`. This instantiation, and every
structural identification in this section, is not formalized here.

Source: G. Prasad and M. S. Raghunathan, *On the Kneser–Tits problem*,
Comment. Math. Helv. 60 (1985), §1.6 (Theorem A and its proof, printed
pp. 109–110) and §2 (printed p. 111).

## Scope

No reductivity, affineness of `M`, or splitting assumption is imposed in
the abstract parts. The local corollary keeps
`LocalSimplyConnectedCocycleVanishingStatement` as an explicit
hypothesis; it does not prove local cocycle vanishing. The hypotheses
`hQ` and `hD` are not proved for any actual centralizer. Nothing here
proves `LocalKneserTitsStatement`.
