# The star action of an actual norm-one presentation

An actual group isomorphism to the reduced-norm-one group of a central simple
algebra and an actual matrix splitting chart imply vertex innerness for the
supplied Galois pinning. The column representation is intertwined with every
one of its Galois twists by the chart conjugator. The ordinary Galois weight
transport and Weyl invariance of multiplicities together give invariance of
weight multiplicities under the star action. Applying this to the highest
weight and to the inverse action shows that this weight is star fixed.

For positive reduced degree at least two, align the supplied split pinning
with the standard diagonal pinning. The highest weight of the column is the
first fundamental weight. Its pairing with simple coroots singles out the
first vertex. A Cartan-preserving permutation of the type-A chain fixing that
vertex fixes every vertex. In degree one the simple support is empty. The
same proof applies to a chart over the separable closure of any field and
hence to the full absolute star action.

This is an internal application of the all-test chart intertwiners, Weyl and
Galois transport of weight spaces, and the arithmetic of the type-A chain.
