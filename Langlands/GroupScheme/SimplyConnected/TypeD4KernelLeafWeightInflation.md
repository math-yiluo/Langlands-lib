# Inflation of the actual fundamental weight of a D4 kernel leaf

For an actual anisotropic-kernel index `p` of a simply connected group,
`descendedComponentProjection` chooses the actual factor pinning and its
projection over the geometric factor's stabilizer field. The target of the
factor inclusion is the original kernel extended to that field. The
pinning of the extended group is obtained by the actual scalar-extension
comparison over the original separable closure.

For the exact D4 marking with only vertex 1 distinguished,
`weightInflation_descendedComponent_D4` identifies the inflation of the
fundamental weight of that factor with the transported fundamental
weight of the corresponding kernel leaf. It takes the integral coroot-span
condition on the factor pinning. The original kernel's condition follows
from simple connectedness of the ambient group.

The full Cartan comparison makes each component of the D4 kernel a
singleton. At an isolated vertex, twice the integral fundamental
character is the simple root. The proof checks that identity by pairing
with every simple coroot. Inflation along the compatible torus projection
sends the factor root to the root at the selected vertex of the extended
kernel. The scalar-extension root-datum equivalence identifies that
root with the transport of the original kernel root. Cancelling
multiplication by two in the torsion-free character lattice gives the
claimed equality.

This is an internal deduction from actual geometric descent, simple-root
inflation and the diagonal Cartan matrix of the D4 kernel. The equality
holds in every field characteristic since the cancellation takes place
in an integral character lattice.
