# Kneser–Tits for the isotropic almost-simple factors of a simply connected group: proof

Lean module: `Langlands.GroupScheme.SimplyConnected.KneserTitsFactorProof`,
namespace `Langlands.GroupScheme`.

## Statement

```
theorem Langlands.GroupScheme.simplyConnectedKneserTitsFactor :
    SimplyConnectedKneserTitsFactorStatement.{u}
```

The type is exactly the published proposition
`Langlands.GroupScheme.SimplyConnectedKneserTitsFactorStatement.{u}` of the
module `Langlands.GroupScheme.SimplyConnected.KneserTitsFactorStatement`, at an
arbitrary universe `u`, with no further hypothesis. Unfolded, it reads:

```
∀ (F : Type u) [Field F] [ValuativeRel F] [TopologicalSpace F]
  [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F]
  (G : GroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of F))) [IsAffineHom G.X.hom], IsSimplyConnected G →
  ∀ (S : CommGroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of F))) (i : S.toGrp ⟶ G),
    IsMaximalSplitTorus i →
  ∀ (A : GroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of F))) (a : A ⟶ G),
    IsAlmostSimple A → IsClosedSubgroup a → (SubgroupFunctor.range a).IsNormal →
    ¬ IsAnisotropic A →
    (rationalPoints.map a).range ≤
      Subgroup.closure (⋃ α ∈ relativeRoots i,
        ((RelativeRoot.subgroup i α).carrier (Over.mk (𝟙 _)) : Set (rationalPoints G)))
```

In words: let `G` be a simply connected group over a nonarchimedean local
field `F`, of any characteristic, with a maximal split torus `S`. For every
isotropic closed normal almost-simple subgroup `A` of `G`, the image of `A(F)`
in `G(F)` lies in the subgroup generated by the root groups `U_α(F)` of all
relative roots `α` of `S` in `G`.

## Proof

The Lean proof is the single application

```
simplyConnectedKneserTitsFactor_of_localKT_and_structural_inputs localKneserTits
  semisimpleNormalIsogenyComplement rationalBruhat rootRankOneFactorization
  parabolicDynamic smoothMaximalSplitTorusConjugacy
```

of the theorem
`simplyConnectedKneserTitsFactor_of_localKT_and_structural_inputs` (module
`SimplyConnected.KneserTitsFactorReduction`). That theorem derives the
statement from six hypotheses; each is discharged by a theorem proved in the
Library without hypotheses:

1. `localKneserTits : LocalKneserTitsStatement.{u}` (module
   `SimplyConnected.LocalKneserTitsProof`): local Kneser–Tits, in the form
   that a normal subgroup of `A(F)` containing all rational parabolic radicals
   is all of `A(F)`.
2. `semisimpleNormalIsogenyComplement` (module
   `Reductive.NormalIsogenyComplementProof`): a smooth connected closed normal
   subgroup of a semisimple group has a semisimple commuting normal complement
   with central-isogeny multiplication.
3. `rationalBruhat` (module `Rational.RationalBruhatProof`): the rational
   Bruhat decomposition.
4. `rootRankOneFactorization` (module `Rational.RootRankOneFactorizationProof`):
   relative-root rank-one factorization.
5. `parabolicDynamic` (module `Absolute.ParabolicDynamicProof`): parabolic
   subgroups are the dynamic `P(χ)`.
6. `smoothMaximalSplitTorusConjugacy` (module
   `Rational.SmoothMaximalSplitTorusConjugacyProof`): rational conjugacy of
   maximal split tori of smooth connected affine groups.

Inside the reduction, the normal-complement theorem and simple connectedness
make `A` a simply connected direct factor of `G`; the rational parabolic
radicals of `A` lie in the subgroup generated by the root groups, which is
normal by the Bruhat decomposition; and local Kneser–Tits, applied to the
inverse image of that subgroup in `A(F)`, gives the inclusion. The details are
on the page of `SimplyConnected.KneserTitsFactorReduction`. The statement is
not modified and no hypothesis is added.

## Literature

G. Prasad and M. S. Raghunathan, *On the Kneser–Tits problem*, Comment. Math.
Helv. 60 (1985), 107–121: the affirmative solution of the Kneser–Tits problem
over nonarchimedean local fields of arbitrary characteristic, and the product
decomposition of a simply connected group into its simple factors, as cited
on the statement's page; page reference to be added.

V. Platonov and A. Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994),
Chapter 7, §7.2, Theorem 7.6, printed p. 414: the Kneser–Tits conjecture holds
for every simple simply connected `K`-isotropic group over a non-Archimedean
locally compact field `K`. The book's local fields have characteristic zero
(printed p. 5); the Lean statement has no restriction on the characteristic.

T. Kaletha and G. Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023),
Chapter 2, §2.2, after Proposition 2.2.14, printed p. 76: `G(k)^+ = G(k)` for a
`k`-isotropic simply connected `G` over a non-discrete locally compact field,
citing [PR85].
