# Reduction of rank-one local Kneser–Tits to absolutely almost-simple groups

Authors: Worker 4953.

This module contains:

- the proposition definition
  `Langlands.GroupScheme.LocalAbsolutelyAlmostSimpleRankOneKneserTitsStatement.{u}`,
  with arbitrary universe `u`, which is not proved here;
- the theorem `localRankOneKneserTits_of_absolutelyAlmostSimple`, which
  proves `Langlands.GroupScheme.LocalRankOneKneserTitsStatement.{u}` from
  this proposition and three published statements about Weil restriction;
- the theorem
  `localKneserTits_of_rankOneLeviFactors_of_absolutelyAlmostSimple`, which
  proves `Langlands.GroupScheme.LocalKneserTitsStatement.{u}` from all the
  open inputs of the published route.

## The statement

`LocalAbsolutelyAlmostSimpleRankOneKneserTitsStatement.{u}` says: let
`K : Type u` have instances `[Field K] [ValuativeRel K] [TopologicalSpace K]
[IsNonarchimedeanLocalField K]`, and let
`G : GroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of K))` have `[IsAffineHom G.X.hom]`,
`IsSimplyConnected G`, `IsAbsolutelyAlmostSimple G` and `splitRank G = 1`.
Then

```
parabolicElementarySubgroup G = ⊤.
```

These are all the quantified data and premises. The characteristic of `K`
is unrestricted, residue characteristic 2 included. The statement
quantifies over every nonarchimedean local field structure on `K`.

- `IsAbsolutelyAlmostSimple G` (module
  `Langlands.GroupScheme.Reductive.AbsolutelyAlmostSimple`) means
  `IsAlmostSimple G` together with `IsAlmostSimple` of the base change of
  `G` to the algebraic closure `toAlgebraicClosure K`. It is the source's
  "absolutely simple". `IsAlmostSimple` is simplicity over the base field,
  in the convention of `Langlands.GroupScheme.Reductive.AlmostSimple`.
- `splitRank G` is the `ℕ∞`-valued split rank of
  `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.SplitTorus`. It is the supremum of
  `Nat.card I` over the closed-subgroup homomorphisms
  `splitTorus (Spec (CommRingCat.of K)) I ⟶ G` with finite `I : Type u`.
  The premise `splitRank G = 1` is the source's "`K`-rank 1".
- `IsSimplyConnected G` is the algebraic simple connectedness of
  `Langlands.GroupScheme.SimplyConnected.Basic`.
- `parabolicElementarySubgroup G` (module
  `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.ElementarySubgroup`) is the subgroup of
  `rationalPoints G` generated by the rational points
  `(rationalPoints.map (r ≫ p)).range` for all actual parabolic subgroups
  `p : P ⟶ G` (`IsParabolicSubgroup p`) and all geometric unipotent
  radicals `r : U ⟶ P` (`LeviSubgroup.IsGeometricUnipotentRadical r`). It
  is the source's `G(K)^+`.

By the published `parabolicElementarySubgroup_eq_top_iff` (module
`Rational.ParabolicWhiteheadQuotient`), the conclusion is equivalent to the
normal-subgroup formulation of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.LocalRankOneKneserTitsStatement`: every normal
subgroup of `rationalPoints G` that contains all these radical images is
`⊤`. So the proposition is the case of `LocalRankOneKneserTitsStatement`
in which `G` is absolutely almost simple, rather than only almost simple
over `K`, restated with the elementary subgroup.

## Source and provenance

G. Prasad and M. S. Raghunathan, *On the Kneser–Tits problem*, Comment.
Math. Helv. 60 (1985), 107–121, registered as
`prasad-raghunathan-1985-kneser-tits` (printed page = PDF page + 105).
Section 2, printed p. 111 / PDF p. 6, treats a `k`-simple simply connected
group of `k`-rank 1 over a nonarchimedean local field `k`. By Borel–Tits
6.21(ii) it is `R_{K/k}(G')` for a finite separable extension `K/k` and an
absolutely simple simply connected `K`-group `G'` of `K`-rank 1. "`K` is
again a nonarchimedean local field". By the classification of such groups
(Kneser in characteristic zero, Bruhat–Tits in general), `G'` is
`SL_2(D)`, a special unitary group of a hermitian form of Witt index 1 in
3 or 4 variables over a quadratic Galois extension, or a spin or
symplectic group of a form over a quaternion division algebra. The source
then states: "For each of the above groups G, it is known that
G(K)^+ = G(K); see, for example, [8]." Here [8] is J. Tits, *Groupes de
Whitehead de groupes algébriques simples sur un corps*, Séminaire Bourbaki
505 (1977), registered as `tits-1977-whitehead-bourbaki-505` (printed
page = PDF page + 216). Its 1.2, printed pp. 221–222 / PDF pp. 5–6, states
`W(G, K) = G(K)/G(K)^+ = 1` for every isotropic simply connected
quasi-simple group over a non-discrete locally compact field.

The proposition is derived. It is the conclusion `G'(K)^+ = G'(K)` that
the source obtains for the absolutely simple groups `G'` of rank 1, from
the classification and the cited case-by-case results, before transporting
it back along `R_{K/k}`. The proposition does not name the three families.
It is the special case of Tits 1.2 for absolutely almost-simple groups of
split rank 1. It is stated separately so that this module can carry out
the transport along Weil restriction, and so that a later proof can
combine the classification (statement task
`45286b38-e76d-4ab7-a663-9458e462226c`) with the Kneser–Tits property of
each family.

## The theorem

```
theorem localRankOneKneserTits_of_absolutelyAlmostSimple
    (hW : SimplyConnectedAlmostSimpleWeilRestrictionStatement.{u})
    (hrank : AffineWeilSplitRankStatement.{u})
    (hpar : AffineWeilParabolicElementarySubgroupStatement.{u})
    (habs : LocalAbsolutelyAlmostSimpleRankOneKneserTitsStatement.{u}) :
    LocalRankOneKneserTitsStatement.{u}
```

The three published hypotheses are the following.

- `SimplyConnectedAlmostSimpleWeilRestrictionStatement` (module
  `SimplyConnected.AlmostSimpleWeilRestrictionStatement`; Borel–Tits
  6.21(ii)). An almost-simple simply connected `G` over a field `k` is
  isomorphic to `AffineWeil.groupScheme k K G'` for a finite separable
  extension `K/k` and a simply connected absolutely almost-simple `G'`
  over `K`.
- `AffineWeilSplitRankStatement` (module
  `Rational.AffineWeilSplitRankStatement`). For finite separable `K/k` and
  connected reductive `G'`, the Weil restriction has the split rank of
  `G'`.
- `AffineWeilParabolicElementarySubgroupStatement` (module
  `Rational.AffineWeilParabolicElementaryStatement`; Borel–Tits 6.19,
  6.21(i)). In the same setting, `AffineWeil.rationalPointsEquiv k K G'`
  maps the parabolic elementary subgroup of the Weil restriction onto
  `parabolicElementarySubgroup G'`.

None of the four hypotheses is proved here.

### Proof

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field and `G` an almost-simple simply
connected group over `F` with `splitRank G = 1`.

1. By `parabolicElementarySubgroup_eq_top_iff`, it suffices to prove
   `parabolicElementarySubgroup G = ⊤`.
2. `hW` gives a finite separable extension `K/F`, a simply connected
   absolutely almost-simple `G'` over `K` and an isomorphism
   `e : G ≅ AffineWeil.groupScheme F K G'`. Simple connectedness gives
   semisimplicity, hence `IsConnectedReductive G'`.
3. `splitRank_eq_of_iso e` (module `Rational.SplitRankTransport`) and
   `hrank` give `splitRank G' = splitRank G = 1`.
4. `K` is given the spectral nonarchimedean local field structure of the
   published `Langlands.LocalField.FiniteExtension` (`normedField`,
   `valuativeRel`, `isLocalField`). `G'` is affine over `K`, so
   `IsAffineHom G'.X.hom` holds. Then `habs` gives
   `parabolicElementarySubgroup G' = ⊤`.
5. By `hpar`, the image of `parabolicElementarySubgroup` of the Weil
   restriction under the group isomorphism `rationalPointsEquiv` is `⊤`.
   This isomorphism also maps `⊤` onto `⊤`
   (`Subgroup.map_top_of_surjective`) and is injective on subgroups
   (`Subgroup.map_injective`). So the parabolic elementary subgroup of the
   Weil restriction is `⊤`.
6. `parabolicElementarySubgroup_eq_top_iff_iso e` (module
   `Rational.ParabolicWhiteheadIsomorphism`) transfers this to `G`.

The universe `u` is the same throughout: `hW` produces `K : Type u`, and
the other three hypotheses are applied at the same universe.

## The full route

```
theorem localKneserTits_of_rankOneLeviFactors_of_absolutelyAlmostSimple
    (hfac : RankOneLeviFactorStatements.{u})
```

The proof composes four theorems:

- `rankOneLeviPresentation_of_rankOneLeviFactorStatements` (module
  `SimplyConnected.RankOneLeviFactors`) turns `hfac` into
  `RankOneLeviPresentationStatement`;
- `localKneserTitsRankOneReduction_of_rankOneLeviPresentation` (module
  `SimplyConnected.RankOneLeviPresentation`) combines it with `hloc` into
  `LocalKneserTitsRankOneReductionStatement`;

  `SimplyConnected.LocalKneserTitsRankOneReduction`) combines the last two

None of them is proved here.

## Place in the local Kneser–Tits route

`LocalRankOneKneserTitsStatement` is one of the three inputs of the
published reduction of `Langlands.GroupScheme.LocalKneserTitsStatement`
(see `notes/group-schemes/local-kneser-tits-route.md`). This theorem
reduces it to the absolutely almost-simple case and to the three Weil
restriction statements. Those statements have their own proof tasks
(Task 5312 for the Weil restriction structure, Task 5336 for the split
rank, Task 5351 for the parabolic transfer). The
three statements of `RankOneLeviFactorStatements` have proof tasks
Task 5333, Task 5334 and Task 5335, and local cocycle vanishing has proof
task `306b2ea2-25ed-490a-9131-292ce36e6c84`.
