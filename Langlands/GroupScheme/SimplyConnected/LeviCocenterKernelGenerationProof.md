# Proof of kernel generation for the cocenter of the minimal Levi subgroup

Authors: Worker 187.

The module `Langlands.GroupScheme.SimplyConnected.LeviCocenterKernelGenerationProof`
proves

```
theorem Langlands.GroupScheme.leviCocenterKernelGeneration :
    LeviCocenterKernelGenerationStatement.{u}
```

`LeviCocenterKernelGenerationStatement` is the published statement in module
`SimplyConnected.LeviCocenterKernelGenerationStatement`, unchanged here. In a simply
connected group `G` over any field `k`, the image of `M(k)` in the cocenter `Q(k)` of
the minimal Levi subgroup `M` lies in the subgroup generated by the rational
kernels of the maps `π α : Q → R α`. Here `R α` is the cocenter of the standard Levi
subgroup `L α` of the simple relative root `α`. The proof shows more: every rational
point of `Q` lies in that subgroup. It uses only the standard axioms (`propext`,
`Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`).

The helper modules are `LinearAlgebra.BasisBlockProjection`,
`LinearAlgebra.RootDatumFaceHeight`, `Rational.FaceSimpleRootRestriction`,
`Torus.CharacterBlockKernelGeneration` and
`Rational.DynamicCentralizerCocenterComparison`, each with its own Markdown.

## Main argument: `mem_closure_ker_of_splitting`

Hypotheses: `E/k` is finite Galois. `f : S ⟶ T` and a maximal torus `amb : T ⟶ G`
satisfy `groupHom f ≫ amb = i`. `T_E` is split, and `amb_E` is a maximal torus.
`tM : T ⟶ M` satisfies `tM ≫ j = amb`. `Q` and the `R α` are commutative group
schemes. The other data are as in the statement. The conclusion is that every
rational point `x` of `Q` lies in the closure of the union of the kernels of the
`π α` on rational points.

1. **Setup over `E`.** `S` is split, and `f` is the largest split subtorus of `T`
   (`IsMaximalSplitTorus.of_comp`, `isLargestSplitSubtorus`). `G_E` is connected
   reductive and simply connected (`connectedReductiveFieldExtensionStatement`,
   `isSimplyConnectedFieldExtension`). `rootDatumExistence` gives a reduced root
   datum `P` of `amb_E`. Simple connectedness gives that the coroots span the
   cocharacters of `T_E` (`splitSimplyConnectedCorootSpan`).
2. **Face and restrictions.** Let `ν' = bc(ν ≫ f)` and `C = coweightFace P ν'`.
   For each absolute root `β`, choose `a β` with `f_E ≫ root β = bc(a β)`; base
   change of characters of the split torus `S` is bijective.
3. **Cocenter comparisons.** `M` represents the centralizer of `ν ≫ f` through
   `amb`. So `DynamicCentralizerCocenterComparison.comparison` applies to `M`.
   It gives: `Q_E` is split, and `g = baseChangePullback k E (tM ≫ q)` is injective
   with range the characters annihilating the coroots of the roots vanishing on
   `ν'`. The same theorem for `L α`, with maximal torus `tM ≫ m α`, gives: `R α_E` is
   split, and the range of the pullback along `tM ≫ m α ≫ r α` is the annihilator of
   the coroots of the roots vanishing on `bc(ζ α ≫ f)`.
4. **Basis of the cocenter characters.** The coroots span the cocharacters, so the
   fundamental weights `ω_β` of `C.base` form a basis of the characters of `T_E`
   (`RootDatum.fundamentalWeightBasis`). The coordinate of `χ` at `β` is
   `⟨χ, β^∨⟩`. Let `s` be the set of simple `β` with `root' β ν' = 0`. By
   `Face.annihilator_iff` and `mem_fundamentalWeight_complement_iff`, the range of
   `g` is the span of the `ω_β` with `β ∉ s`. Pulling this basis back along `g`
   gives a basis `bQ` of the characters of `Q_E`, indexed by the simple roots `β`
   of `C.base` with `root' β ν' ≠ 0`. Its coordinates are
   `bQ.repr y β = ⟨g y, β^∨⟩`.
5. **Blocks.** Put the index `β` in block `θ β = a β`. This is a simple relative root
   by `FaceSimpleRootRestriction.restrict_mem_simpleRelativeRoots`.
6. **Block condition.** Fix `α`. By `hπ`, `g` composed with the pullback along
   `π α` is the pullback along `tM ≫ m α ≫ r α`. By
   `FaceSimpleRootRestriction.root'_standard`, `bc(ζ α ≫ f)` is dominant for
   `C.base`. For simple `β` with `root' β ν' ≠ 0` it gives
   `root' β (bc(ζ α ≫ f)) = 0` iff `a β = α`. Now
   `RootDatumFaceHeight.mem_range_iff_of_dominant` (with `μ = ν'`) shows that `y`
   is pulled back from `R α` iff `⟨g y, β^∨⟩ = 0` for the simple `β` with
   `root' β ν' ≠ 0` and `a β = α`. That is: iff the coordinates of `y` in block `α`
   vanish.
7. **Conclusion.** `CharacterBlockKernelGeneration.mem_closure_ker` applies to `bQ`,
   `θ` and the `π α`.

## Assembly: `leviCocenterKernelGeneration`

Take the data of the statement.

1. `G` is connected reductive (`IsSemisimple.isConnectedReductive`), hence smooth.
   `j` is a closed subgroup (`DynamicCentralizerCocenterComparison.isClosedSubgroup`).
   So `M` is connected reductive (`dynamicCentralizerReductive`), smooth and affine.
2. Since `ν` is generic, `j` also represents the centralizer of the torus `i`
   (`genericCentralizer`).
3. `Q` and each `R α` are commutative (`isCommMonObj_cocenter`; `L α` is affine as a
   closed subgroup of `G`). They are bundled as commutative group schemes `Qc` and
   `Rc α`, and `Q` is a torus (`suppliedCocenterTorus`).
4. `torusCentralizerFiniteGaloisModel` (with `maximalTorusBaseChange`) gives a
   `k`-torus `T` with `f : S ⟶ T` and `amb : T ⟶ G` a maximal torus satisfying
   `groupHom f ≫ amb = i`. It also gives `tM : T ⟶ M` with `tM ≫ j = amb`, and a
   finite Galois extension `E` splitting `T` such that `amb_E` is a maximal torus.
5. For a point `q(y)` of the range, `mem_closure_ker_of_splitting` applies with these
   data and `π α` viewed as maps of commutative group schemes.

## Relation to the literature

The statement adapts the computation `M/[M,M] ≅ ∏ T_Θ` in the proof of Theorem A of
Prasad–Raghunathan, *On the Kneser–Tits problem* (1985), §1.3 and printed
pp.108 and 110 (registered as `prasad-raghunathan-1985-kneser-tits`). It uses
Borel–Tits, *Groupes réductifs* (1965), Proposition 6.8 (registered as
`borel-tits-1965-groupes-reductifs`). The Lean proof follows that computation: the
fundamental weights of the simple roots not vanishing on `S` form a basis of the
cocenter characters, grouped by the restrictions of their roots. It does not
construct the isomorphism `∏ ker π α ≅ Q`. Instead it descends the block projections
to endomorphisms `ε α` of `Q` with `∑ ε α = 1` and `ε α ≫ π α = 0`, which suffices
for the rational-points statement. The needed part of Borel–Tits 6.8 is proved in
`FaceSimpleRootRestriction` by a Galois-invariant height argument.
