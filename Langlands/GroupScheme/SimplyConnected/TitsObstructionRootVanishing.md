# Unconditional root-span vanishing of the Tits obstruction

Let `G` be an affine, locally finite type group scheme over a field `k`, let
`K/k` be Galois (for instance a separable closure), and let
`d : GaloisPinningData k K G` be Galois pinning data. This module proves that
the Tits obstruction vanishes on every star-fixed weight of the integral root
span, with no hypothesis beyond those of the obstruction itself. It
discharges the representation-existence premise `HasGroundMaximalWeight` of
the published reductions `titsObstructionValue_eq_zero_of_mem_rootSpan_of_ground`
(`Reductive/GaloisRationalBaseChange`) and
`titsObstruction_eq_zero_of_mem_rootSpan` (`Reductive/TitsObstructionSimplyConnected`).

The statement is the assertion of Corollary 3.5 of Tits,
*Représentations linéaires irréductibles d'un groupe réductif sur un corps
quelconque*, J. reine angew. Math. 247 (1971), p. 205, that the kernel of
`α_{G,k}` contains `Λ₀^Γ`. Here (§2.1, p. 200) `Λ₀` is generated by the root
lattice `Λ_rac` and the weights vanishing on `T ∩ 𝒟(G)`; for semisimple `G`
it is the integral root lattice `Λ_rac`, the integral span of the roots
(`rootSpan ℤ`), so `Λ₀^Γ` is the group of star-fixed weights in that span.
Tits proves the vanishing through
the adjoint group (last paragraph of the proof of Theorem 3.3, p. 204). The
route here is different and is this development's own: it uses
representations defined over `k` with a maximal weight of multiplicity one,
as explained in `Reductive/TitsRootVanishing`.

## Inputs

- `δ` is the published positive-root sum
  `IntegralWeylVector.positiveRootSum` of `d.toSplitPinning`. It is fixed by
  the full star action by the published `starFixedPositiveRootSum`
  (`Reductive/StarFixedPositiveRootSum`).
- For every finite set `O` of simple roots, the published
  `exists_groundRepresentation_positiveRootSum_sub_simpleRoots`
  (`Reductive/GroundAdjointExterior`) gives a representation of `G` over `k`,
  an exterior power of the adjoint representation, whose scalar extension to
  `K` has `δ - ∑_{i ∈ O} α_i` as a weight of multiplicity one and no weight
  strictly above it in the order of the positive root monoid. This is
  exactly `HasGroundMaximalWeight (δ - simpleRootSum O)`.

## Declarations

- `hasGroundMaximalWeight_positiveRootSum_sub`: for every finite set `O` of
  simple roots, `d.HasGroundMaximalWeight (δ - d.simpleRootSum O)`. The two
  statements are definitionally equal (`simpleRootSum O` is
  `∑ i ∈ O, P.root i`), so the proof is the published existence theorem.
- `titsObstructionValue_eq_zero_of_mem_rootSpan_unconditional`: if the
  integral coroot span is everything and `K/k` is Galois, then
  `d.titsObstructionValue hspan λ hfix = 0` for every star-fixed `λ` in
  `rootSpan ℤ`. The proof chooses a `Fintype` instance on the finite root
  index (`Fintype.ofFinite`) for `δ` and applies
  `titsObstructionValue_eq_zero_of_mem_rootSpan_of_ground` with
  `starFixedPositiveRootSum` and `hasGroundMaximalWeight_positiveRootSum_sub`.
- `titsObstruction_eq_zero_of_mem_rootSpan_unconditional`: for
  `IsSimplyConnected G`, the obstruction `d.titsObstruction hG` vanishes on
  every element of `d.starFixedWeights` lying in the integral root span. The
  value of `titsObstruction` is `titsObstructionValue` by definition.
- `rootSpanStarFixedWeights` (definition): the subgroup of
  `d.starFixedWeights` of weights lying in the integral root span, i.e. the
  preimage of `rootSpan ℤ` under the inclusion. In Tits's notation, for
  semisimple `G`, this is `Λ^Γ ∩ Λ₀ = Λ₀^Γ`. `mem_rootSpanStarFixedWeights`
  unfolds membership.
- `rootSpan_le_ker_titsObstruction`: the same vanishing as an inclusion of
  subgroups: `rootSpanStarFixedWeights` is contained in the kernel of
  `d.titsObstruction hG`.
- `titsObstructionRootQuotient` (definition): the homomorphism
  `d.starFixedWeights ⧸ rootSpanStarFixedWeights →+ BrauerGroup k` induced by
  the obstruction (`QuotientAddGroup.lift`, well defined by the previous
  inclusion); `titsObstructionRootQuotient_mk` says that it agrees with
  `d.titsObstruction hG` on classes. Its source, as implemented, is the
  lattice quotient `Λ^Γ / Λ₀^Γ` of the star-fixed weights by the star-fixed
  weights of the integral root span. Tits (§3.1, p. 203) notes the equality
  `C*^Γ = Λ^Γ/Λ₀^Γ` with the Γ-invariants of `C* = Λ/Λ₀`; through it, this
  homomorphism corresponds to Tits's `β_{G,k}` of Corollary 3.5. That
  comparison with `(Λ/Λ₀)^Γ` is not formalized in this module.

All characteristics, including imperfect fields of characteristic two, are
allowed; there is no inner-type hypothesis, and the full scheme-theoretic
centre is retained.
