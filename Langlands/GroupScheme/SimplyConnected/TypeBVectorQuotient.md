# The actual descended vector quotient of a numbered type-B group

A simply connected actual field group with supplied Tits-index data and
numbered type-B Cartan matrix has an actual affine central quotient over
the ground field. After extension to the separable closure, this quotient
is isomorphic to the full split odd norm-one isometry group, and the
quotient map recovers the selected split vector isogeny. The descended
quotient is locally of finite type.

Simple connectedness over the separable closure supplies the full
integral coroot span for the supplied absolute pinning. The split vector
isogeny construction then applies to that exact numbered datum. Its
character image is the integral root lattice, which every root-datum
automorphism preserves. In particular the ordinary Galois action
preserves it. The published torus-character-image descent theorem
therefore descends the full central kernel and its actual affine central
isogeny quotient. Local finite type descends through the recovering
isomorphism and the faithfully flat separable-closure map.

The proof adapts the published type-D vector-quotient architecture.
Here the root lattice alone is the character image, so no distinguished
star-fixed vertex is needed. All kernels and quotient equations are
actual scheme-theoretic ones on arbitrary test schemes. The quadratic
form realizing the descended quotient is not yet constructed in this
unit. These are internal consequences of the preceding type-B datum and
published descent results; no new external translation or field
characteristic restriction is used.

The proof applies the shared `TitsIndexData.pinning_corootSpan_eq_top` to the supplied absolute pinning.
