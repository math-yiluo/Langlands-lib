# Local Kneser–Tits for groups of split rank one

Authors: Worker 1757.

`Langlands.GroupScheme.LocalRankOneKneserTitsStatement.{u}` is a
proposition definition, with arbitrary universe `u`. It states the local
Kneser–Tits theorem for almost-simple simply connected groups of split rank
one over a nonarchimedean local field. It uses the same formulation with
normal subgroups and rational parabolic radicals as
`Langlands.GroupScheme.LocalKneserTitsStatement`. This module does not prove
the proposition.

## Exact statement

Let `F : Type u` have instances `[Field F] [ValuativeRel F]
[TopologicalSpace F] [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F]`. Let
`G : GroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of F))` have `[IsAffineHom G.X.hom]`,
`IsSimplyConnected G`, `IsAlmostSimple G`, and `splitRank G = 1`.

Let `N` be a subgroup of `rationalPoints G` with `N.Normal`. Suppose that
for all actual group schemes `P` and `U` over `F` and all maps
`p : P ⟶ G` and `r : U ⟶ P`,

```
IsParabolicSubgroup p →
LeviSubgroup.IsGeometricUnipotentRadical r →
(rationalPoints.map (r ≫ p)).range ≤ N.
```

Then `N = ⊤`.

These are all the quantified data and premises. The characteristic of `F`
is unrestricted. No splitting, quasi-splitting or absolute simplicity is
assumed, and no maximal split torus is chosen.

`splitRank G` is the `ℕ∞`-valued split rank of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.SplitTorus`. It is the supremum of
`Nat.card I` over the closed-subgroup homomorphisms
`splitTorus (Spec (CommRingCat.of F)) I ⟶ G` with finite `I : Type u`. The
premise `splitRank G = 1` is the source's condition "`F`-rank 1". It implies
`¬ IsAnisotropic G`, because `IsAnisotropic G` means `splitRank G = 0`.

`IsAlmostSimple G` means simplicity over `F`, in the convention of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Reductive.AlmostSimple`. It is the source's
"`k`-simple" and does not mean absolute simplicity. `IsSimplyConnected G`
is the algebraic simple connectedness of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.SimplyConnected.Basic`. `rationalPoints G`,
`IsParabolicSubgroup` and `LeviSubgroup.IsGeometricUnipotentRadical` are
used exactly as in `Langlands.GroupScheme.LocalKneserTitsStatement`; its
paired Markdown explains them.

## Source and reformulation

G. Prasad and M. S. Raghunathan, *On the Kneser–Tits problem*, Comment.
Math. Helv. 60 (1985), 107–121, registered as
`prasad-raghunathan-1985-kneser-tits`. The retained PDF has one cover page,
so printed page = PDF page + 105.

Section 1.6, printed p. 109 / PDF p. 4, deduces from Theorem A that the
Kneser–Tits problem over a field `k` has an affirmative answer if and only
if `G(k)^+ = G(k)` for every `k`-simple simply connected group `G` of
`k`-rank 1. Section 2, printed p. 111 / PDF p. 6, settles these rank-one
groups over a nonarchimedean local field `k`. By Borel–Tits 6.21(ii), such
a `G` is `R_{K/k}(G')` for a finite separable extension `K/k` and an
absolutely simple simply connected `K`-group `G'` of `K`-rank 1. The
classification of these groups over local fields (Kneser in characteristic
zero, Bruhat–Tits in general) lists `SL_2(D)`, special unitary groups of
hermitian forms of Witt index 1 in 3 or 4 variables over a quadratic Galois
extension, and spin and symplectic groups of forms over a quaternion
division algebra. For each of them `G'(K)^+ = G'(K)` is known (the source cites
J. Tits, *Groupes de Whitehead de groupes algébriques simples sur un corps*,
Séminaire Bourbaki 505, 1977, as [8]).

The source's `G(k)^+` is the normal subgroup of `G(k)` generated by the
rational points of the unipotent radicals of the parabolic `k`-subgroups
(introduction, printed p. 107 / PDF p. 2, and Section 1.4). As for the
general statement, the proposition is the universal-property form of
`G(F)^+ = G(F)`. Taking `N` to be the normal subgroup generated by the
radical images recovers the source equation, and conversely.

## Relation to the general statement

This proposition is the rank-one case of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.LocalKneserTitsStatement`. It has the same
conclusion and the premise `splitRank G = 1`, which is stronger than
`¬ IsAnisotropic G`. Therefore `LocalKneserTitsStatement` implies it. Theorem A of
Prasad–Raghunathan, rank-one Kneser–Tits for the `F`-simple subgroups
`G_Θ` of Section 1.5, the rational Bruhat decomposition and the vanishing
of local first Galois cohomology together give the general statement. This
module states the rank-one case separately so that its proof, which needs
the classification of the rank-one groups, can be developed independently
of that reduction. It asserts none of those results.
