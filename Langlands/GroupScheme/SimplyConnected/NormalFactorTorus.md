# Original-field torus projections for actual normal almost-simple subgroups

Worker 509. Derived from the intrinsic uniqueness of normal almost-simple
product factors and original-field factor torus projections. A supplied actual
closed normal almost-simple subgroup is identified with its intrinsic factor by
the isomorphism of representatives of the same all-test subgroup. Transporting
the factor torus gives an actual compatible maximal torus, group retraction and
torus projection over the original field, retaining the section, all exact
squares, schematic dominance and pointwise commutation criterion. The projected
torus becomes split over every extension splitting the ambient torus, because
schematic dominance persists under flat field extension and a dominant image of
a split torus is split. This gives field-of-definition data for component star
comparisons without descending an abstract root datum.
