# Existence of type-`D` spin covers aligned with the numbered vector vertex

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.SimplyConnected.ClassicalTypeDAlignedSpinCoverProof`.

## Statement

```
theorem classicalTypeDAlignedSpinCover : ClassicalTypeDAlignedSpinCoverStatement.{u}
```

This proves the Library proposition `ClassicalTypeDAlignedSpinCoverStatement` at every universe
`u`. The theorem has no hypotheses. Let `K` be a field and `G` an affine group scheme of locally
finite type over `K`. Assume `G` is simply connected and absolutely almost simple, and has a
Tits index whose vertices are numbered by `Fin n`, `n ≥ 4`, with Cartan matrix `D_n`. Assume
also that every element of `Gal(K^s/K)` acts by the star action as the identity on each vertex
numbered `i` with `i + 2 < n`, that is, on every vertex outside the fork. Then there are:

- a central division `K`-algebra `D` of degree `degree > 0`;
- a natural number `m` with `m * degree = 2n`;
- an involution `σ` of the first kind on `D` of Tits type 1, meaning
  `dim_K Sym(σ) = degree * (degree + 1) / 2`;
- a nondegenerate `σ`-quadratic form `q` on `D^m`;
- a spin cover `π : G ⟶ O(q)` such that the fundamental weight at the vertex numbered `0` is
  trivial on `ker π`.

There is no restriction on the characteristic, finite fields included, and `n = 4` is
included.

## Proof

The proof is a single application of
`classicalTypeDAlignedSpinCover_of_vectorQuotient` (module
`ClassicalTypeDAlignedSpinCoverReduction`, ported together with this module; see its Markdown for the
argument). That reduction derives the target from two statements. Both are proved in the
Library, and the proof supplies those proofs:

1. `TypeDVectorQuotientStatement`, proved by `Langlands.GroupScheme.typeDVectorQuotient`
   (`SimplyConnected/TypeDVectorQuotient`). It gives a central isogeny `p : G ⟶ H` onto a form
   `H` of the split `SO_{2n}`, aligned at the vertex numbered `0`. Over `K^s`, `p` is the
   vector map, whose character image is generated by the roots and the fundamental weight at
   vertex `0`. That lattice is Galois-stable because the star action fixes vertex `0`, so the
   quotient descends to `K`.
2. `Quaternionic.EvenSpecialOrthogonalFormStatement`, proved by
   `Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.evenSpecialOrthogonalForm` (`EvenOrthogonalForm/Proof`).
   This is Tits's realization of every form of `SO_{2n}`, `n ≥ 4`, as `O(q)⁰`. It holds in
   every characteristic and includes outer forms and `n = 4`.

The reduction composes `p` with the isomorphism `H ≅ O(q)⁰` and the inclusion
`O(q)⁰ ⊆ O(q)`. The composite is the spin cover. Its alignment at vertex `0` comes from the
alignment of `p` through the published transport lemmas
`FundamentalWeightTrivialOnKernel.comp_iso` and `.comp_identityComponentι`.
