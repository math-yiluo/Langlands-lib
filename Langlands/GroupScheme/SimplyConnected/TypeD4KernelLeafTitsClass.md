# The actual Tits class of a D4 anisotropic-kernel leaf

weight at a kernel vertex through the based root-datum equivalence for
scalar extension. The coefficient field is an intermediate field of the
original separable closure. The transported weight is dominant because
its simple-coroot pairings are identical to the original pairings.

When the intermediate field is the star vertex field of the corresponding
ambient vertex, every automorphism over that field fixes the original
kernel vertex. The proof uses pointwise fixation, the ambient stabilizer
criterion and the full-star kernel vertex comparison. Covariance of the
fundamental characters and the scalar-extension star identity show that
the transported character is fixed under every relative Galois element.

between the original kernel extended to the leaf field and its descended
