# Rank-one Levi factors generate the cocenter of the minimal Levi: proof

Authors: Worker 183, Worker 187.

The module `Langlands.GroupScheme.SimplyConnected.RankOneLeviCocenterGenerationProof` proves

```
theorem rankOneLeviCocenterGeneration : RankOneLeviCocenterGenerationStatement.{u}
```

This is the published statement of module `SimplyConnected.RankOneLeviFactors`. Let `G` be a
simply connected group over a field `k`, `S` a maximal split torus, and `M` the minimal Levi
subgroup (the centralizer of a generic cocharacter of `S`). Let `Q = M/D(M)` be the cocenter of
`M`. For each simple relative root `α`, let `A α` be a rank-one Levi factor and `φ α` the
induced map from the cocenter of `M ∩ A α` to `Q`. Then the image of `M(k)` in `Q(k)` lies in the
subgroup generated by the images of the maps `φ α` on rational points. The field and its
characteristic are arbitrary. The theorem uses only the standard axioms.

## Implemented proof

The proof is one line: it applies the published reduction
`rankOneLeviCocenterGeneration_of_leviCocenterKernelGeneration` (module
`SimplyConnected.RankOneLeviCocenterGenerationReduction`) to the published proof
`leviCocenterKernelGeneration` of `LeviCocenterKernelGenerationStatement` (module
`SimplyConnected.LeviCocenterKernelGenerationProof`).

- `LeviCocenterKernelGenerationStatement` is the absolute part. For each simple relative root
  `α`, let `L α` be the standard Levi subgroup of `α` and `π α : Q ⟶ L α/D(L α)` the induced map
  of cocenters. The statement says that the image of `M(k)` in `Q(k)` lies in the subgroup
  generated by the rational kernels of the maps `π α`.
- The reduction is the relative part. It shows that the rational kernel of `π α` lies in the
  image of `φ α` on rational points. To do so it proves that `φ α` is a monomorphism and
  descends points along the fppf quotients.

Both Markdown files of these modules describe their proofs in detail.

## Relation to the literature

The statement is clause 4 of the presentation of `M(k)` used by Prasad–Raghunathan, *On the
Kneser–Tits problem* (1985), proof of Theorem A, printed p.110 (registered as
`prasad-raghunathan-1985-kneser-tits`). There `M/[M,M]` is identified with a product of tori
`T_Θ`, one for each Galois orbit `Θ` in `Δ − Δ₀`. Borel–Tits, *Groupes réductifs* (1965),
Proposition 6.8, printed pp.107–108 (registered as `borel-tits-1965-groupes-reductifs`),
identifies these orbits with the simple relative roots. The Lean proof follows this route: the
absolute part computes the tori from the root data, and the relative part identifies each torus
with the image of the cocenter of `M ∩ A α`.
