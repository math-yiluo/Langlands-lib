# The local D4 leaf obstruction

In the centrally marked actual D4 index, a selected kernel leaf gives a
descended A1 factor over its actual component field. Its norm-one
presentation supplies a degree-two central division algebra. The first
fundamental weight of its norm-one chart has the positive Brauer class of
that algebra.

The factor comparison identifies its dominant Tits class with the
restricted leaf class for the original kernel after base change to the
component field. Comparing the two scalar-extension pinnings through
their common original torus gives the same class for the canonical
base-change pinning used in Tits transfer.

The extended Levi comparison restricts the original ambient leaf weight
to the corresponding weight of the original kernel extended to that
field. The field is the actual star vertex field. The embeddings into
its finite Galois normal closure account for every star translate exactly
once, so transfer takes the quaternion class to the ambient leaf-orbit
obstruction. The invariant of that class is one half in the rational
numbers modulo the integers, by the local invariants of quaternion
division algebras and compatibility with corestriction.

In the inner case each leaf is fixed. The three leaf classes therefore
have invariants with sum one half, whereas their weight sum is in the
integral root span and has zero obstruction. When there is no globally
fixed leaf, all three leaves form one orbit, so its weight already lies
in the integral root span and its invariant again gives a contradiction.
The proof keeps the original Levi comparison under extension and works
over nonarchimedean local fields in every field and residue characteristic.
