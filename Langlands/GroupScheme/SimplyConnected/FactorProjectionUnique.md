# Intrinsic uniqueness of actual normal-factor retractions

Derived from the full scheme-theoretic perfectness of actual
almost-simple groups and finite product coordinates. A morphism from a perfect
group with commutative all-test image is trivial: its closed kernel contains
every commutator and hence the full derived subgroup. Any retraction onto one
specified actual normal product factor is therefore the canonical product
projection. Each other factor has central image in the selected factor and so
has commutative image; perfectness makes it trivial. Restrictions to all actual
factor insertions determine the entire group-scheme homomorphism. No assumption
on rational point generation or algebraic closedness is used.

The transported-retraction corollary retains the actual subgroup recovery and
ambient isomorphisms, identifying the base-changed original-field retraction
with the canonical selected geometric product projection.

`FactorRetractionUniqueStatement` gives the universal arbitrary-field assertion;
`factorRetractionUnique` proves it from the intrinsic projection lemma.
