# The Brauer class of a maximal weight in a norm-one chart

Let `G` be an affine locally finite-type simply connected group over a
field `k`, with Galois pinning data over a Galois extension `L/k`. A
morphism from `G` to the reduced-norm-one group of a central simple
`k`-algebra `A` pulls the split column chart back to a representation
over `L`. For a star-fixed maximal weight of multiplicity one in the
dual representation, its Tits obstruction is the class of the opposite
algebra of `A`.

The Galois intertwiners of the dual chart pull back along the morphism.
Their twisted-fixed algebra is the original opposite algebra inside
the split matrices, and it spans the entire matrix algebra over `L`.
The maximal-weight obstruction formula identifies its Brauer class.

A morphism from a semisimple group to the units of a central simple
algebra factors through reduced norm one. Its composite with the
reduced norm is a rational character, so it vanishes.

This extends the fixed-algebra calculation in the norm-one case using
the maximal-weight formula from Tits, *Représentations linéaires
irréductibles d'un groupe réductif sur un corps quelconque* (1971),
§3, Proposition 3.4, printed p. 204.
