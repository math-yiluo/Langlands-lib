# Unitary splitting preserving the actual torus

This internal derivation strengthens a scalar trivialization of the inner
discrepancy family: its correction may be chosen to preserve the characters
of an already diagonal actual split torus. It uses the published blockwise
Hilbert 90 construction and does not assume any distinguished position.

If `P⁻¹ x(σ) σ(P)` is scalar, the honest coboundary
`c(σ) = P σ(P⁻¹)` represents the same all-test inner automorphism as `x(σ)`.
It is a normalized matrix cocycle. The published blockwise correction for
this cocycle commutes with every torus point, kills the inner discrepancies,
and retains the original diagonal torus coordinates.

The fields `K` and `M`, their algebra structure and the universe are arbitrary.
The subgroup `H` of cover automorphisms must be finite, and every element
of `H` must have the supplied all-test inner representative. The torus is
split and its given coordinates are diagonal on every test scheme.
`torusPreservingCorrectionOfScalar` chooses the correction established by
`exists_torus_preserving_correction_of_scalar`. This auxiliary construction
does not yet locate the distinguished vertices or prove the local enumeration.
