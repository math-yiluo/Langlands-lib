# Rank-one Levi factors generate the derived subgroup of the minimal Levi: proof

Authors: Worker 180.

The theorem `Langlands.GroupScheme.rankOneLeviDerivedGeneration` has type
exactly `Langlands.GroupScheme.RankOneLeviDerivedGenerationStatement.{u}`,
from the published module `SimplyConnected.RankOneLeviFactors`, at arbitrary
universe `u`. So for every field `k : Type u` (any characteristic, finite or
imperfect fields included), every group scheme `G` over `Spec k` with
`[IsAffineHom G.X.hom]`, `IsSimplyConnected G`, `IsAlmostSimple G` and
`¬ IsAnisotropic G`, every maximal split torus `i`, every generic
cocharacter `ν`, every `j : M ⟶ G` representing
`Dynamic.centralizer (CommGroupScheme.groupHom ν ≫ i)`, every family
`a α : A α ⟶ G` of rank-one Levi factors indexed by the simple relative roots,
and every derived subgroup `d : D ⟶ M`, it proves

```
(rationalPoints.map d).range ≤
  Subgroup.closure (⋃ α, (rationalPoints.map (subgroupIntersectionFst j (a α))).range)
```

in `rationalPoints M`. The theorem has no hypotheses beyond those of the
statement and uses only the standard axioms.

## Implemented proof

The proof is the term

```
rankOneLeviDerivedGeneration_of_dichotomy rankOneLeviFactorDichotomy
  simpleRootSubgroupGeneration isotropicRootSubgroupGeneration
```

combining four modules submitted with this one:

- `SimplyConnected.RankOneLeviDerivedGenerationReduction` proves the
  statement from three propositions
  (`rankOneLeviDerivedGeneration_of_dichotomy`). `D` is simply connected
  (`splitTorusCentralizerDerivedSimplyConnected`), so `D(k)` is generated by
  the rational points of its closed normal almost-simple subgroups `B`
  (`simplyConnectedAlmostSimpleRationalGeneration`). Each such `B` lies in
  some `A α` (`exists_range_le_rankOneLeviFactor`), and then the rational
  points of `B` map into the rational points of `M ∩ A α` by the pullback
  property of `subgroupIntersection`. The containment `B ⊆ A α` is proved by
  contradiction: if `B` lies in no `A α`, the dichotomy makes `B` commute
  with every `A α`; the closed centralizer of `B` then contains the torus and
  the root subgroups of `±α` for all simple `α`, hence all root subgroups,
  hence is all of `G`, and `B` would be commutative.
- `SimplyConnected.RankOneLeviFactorDichotomyProof` proves the dichotomy
  `RankOneLeviFactorDichotomyStatement` (`rankOneLeviFactorDichotomy`) through
  the simply connected derived group of the standard Levi of `α`, its
  decomposition as a direct product of `A α` and a complement `K`, and the
  proof that `K` lies in the derived subgroup of `M`.
- `Rational.SimpleRootSubgroupGenerationProof` proves the published
  `SimpleRootSubgroupGenerationStatement` (`simpleRootSubgroupGeneration`)
  by transporting simple root subgroups with reflection representatives
  along the Coxeter structure of the relative Weyl group.
- `Rational.IsotropicRootSubgroupGenerationProof` proves the published
  `IsotropicRootSubgroupGenerationStatement`
  (`isotropicRootSubgroupGeneration`) through the normal closed subgroup
  generated by `U(χ)`, `χ` and `U(χ⁻¹)`.

Each module's Markdown explains its proof in detail.

## Relation to the literature

The target is the first step of the proof of Theorem A in G. Prasad and
M. S. Raghunathan, *On the Kneser–Tits problem* (1985), printed p.110 /
PDF p.5, registered as `prasad-raghunathan-1985-kneser-tits`: every
connected normal `k`-simple subgroup of the derived group of `M` lies in some
`G_Θ_i`, hence in `ℳ_Θ_i`. The source argues with the Tits index. The Lean
proof instead follows the relative route described in the statement's
Markdown: a `k`-simple factor lying in no rank-one Levi factor would commute
with all of them, and Borel–Tits, *Groupes réductifs* (1965), 4.11 and 5.3
(`borel-tits-1965-groupes-reductifs`) together with almost simplicity of `G`
make it central.
