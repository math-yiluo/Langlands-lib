# Signed Tits algebras for an actual norm-one presentation

Let `A` be a central simple algebra over `k`, let `L/k` be a Galois splitting
extension, and choose a matrix chart. The column representation uses the
chart matrix acting on columns. The dual representation uses its inverse
transpose. The constructed chart conjugators are actual intertwiners with
the Galois twists. This follows by evaluating the transport on the universal
affine test and using the chart's relative point equations. Pulling back
through a group isomorphism defined over `k` preserves the same intertwining
equations.

For a star-fixed weight realized as highest weight of the pulled-back column
representation, the Tits algebra is the chart's actual Galois-fixed matrix
algebra. It is isomorphic over `k` to `A`. For a star-fixed highest weight of
the dual, the inverse-transpose equations cut out the fixed matrices of the
opposite chart, which are isomorphic to `Aᵒᵖ`. The corresponding classes in
the actual Brauer quotient are `[A]` and `−[A]`.

The calculation combines the Library's projective-intertwiner construction
of the Tits algebra with the explicit norm-one chart calculation. Its scope
includes the absolute separable closure and all affine test algebras in every
characteristic. The highest-weight hypothesis identifies the relevant weight
of the supplied pinning. The coordinate numbering is provided by the
special-linear chart weight comparison.
