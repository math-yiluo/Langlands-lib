# Conditional local obstructions from the full centre of an actual kernel

`AllAutomorphismsFixCenter G` is the proposition that every actual automorphism
of `G` fixes its full scheme-theoretic centre pointwise. This definition works
over an arbitrary base scheme and tests all points over all test schemes through
`AutomorphismFixesCenter`. By quantifier negation it is equivalent to
`¬ HasCenterMovingAutomorphism G`.

The local results prove the arithmetic and model-transport part of a route to
excluding the branch-marked E6 and E7 indices over a nonarchimedean local field.
All local results have an explicit `NormOneCenterAutomorphismOppositeStatement`
premise. The exceptional exclusions also retain an explicit
`RankOneTypeEKernelCenterMovementStatement` premise. Both structural propositions
remain unproved.

For a group scheme over a local field with an actual
`HasCentralDivisionNormOnePresentation G degree`, where `2 < degree`, every
automorphism fixes the full scheme-theoretic centre, conditional on the norm-one
prerequisite. Indeed, transport the automorphism through the actual norm-one
model. Failure of full-centre fixation would give an algebra anti-equivalence
of the central division algebra by that explicit prerequisite. The published
`not_hasAlgebraAntiEquivalence_of_two_lt` excludes it at the specified reduced
degree. The full-centre conjugation equivalence reflects fixation back to `G`.
The bundled theorem proves `AllAutomorphismsFixCenter G` by applying the
pointwise automorphism theorem to each automorphism. The next result excludes
`HasCenterMovingAutomorphism G` by the preceding logical equivalence.

For an actual rank-one index, exact E6 branch marking and trivial full star give
an actual norm-one model of its chosen anisotropic kernel of degree six.
The corresponding E7 data give degree seven. The proved model theorems retain
the full matrix, marking and star hypotheses. Applying the preceding local
obstruction contradicts the centre-moving automorphism requested by the
separate geometric prerequisite. The ambient group remains simply connected
and absolutely almost simple, with split rank exactly one, as required there.

The provenance is internal composition of the actual kernel-model theorems,
full-centre isomorphism invariance, and the published opposite-algebra local
degree bound. It adds no field- or residue-characteristic restriction and uses
no involutivity hypothesis. These are conditional reductions, not unconditional
E6/E7 exclusions or a proof of the local rank-one enumeration.
