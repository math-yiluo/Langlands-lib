# The distinguished E6 root and the characters of the actual kernel

The distinguished root is the actual simple root at vertex one of the
supplied numbering. The fundamental weight at that vertex is the sum of the
simple roots with coefficients `[1,2,2,3,2,1]`. Pairing the sum with all the
simple coroots verifies this identity in the integral character lattice.

For a simply connected ambient group, a character restricting to zero on
the kernel torus has zero pairing with every ambient simple coroot except
possibly the distinguished one. It is therefore an integral multiple of
the distinguished fundamental weight.
On the maximal split torus this weight restricts to twice the distinguished
root restriction, whose pairing with the positive cocharacter is positive.
Consequently the two actual restrictions together distinguish all absolute
characters.

The restriction of the distinguished root pairs to minus one at the middle
kernel vertex and to zero at the others. Any Cartan numbering of the actual
kernel has the same middle vertex because the A5 Cartan symmetries are
identity and reversal. Adding three times the first fundamental weight to
the restricted root gives twice the first simple kernel root plus the
second. The proof compares their pairings with every simple kernel coroot.

These integral calculations use the published E6 kernel comparison, E6 root
coefficients, and A-type Cartan symmetry calculation. They apply over every
field and refer to the particular anisotropic kernel chosen by the index.
