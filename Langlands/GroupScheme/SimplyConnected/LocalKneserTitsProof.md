# Local Kneser–Tits in terms of rational parabolic radicals: proof

Lean module: `Langlands.GroupScheme.SimplyConnected.LocalKneserTitsProof`,
namespace `Langlands.GroupScheme`.

## Statement

```
theorem Langlands.GroupScheme.localKneserTits : LocalKneserTitsStatement.{u}
```

The type is exactly the published proposition
`Langlands.GroupScheme.LocalKneserTitsStatement.{u}` of the module
`Langlands.GroupScheme.SimplyConnected.LocalKneserTitsStatement`, at an
arbitrary universe `u`, with no further hypothesis. Unfolded, it reads:

```
∀ (F : Type u) [Field F] [ValuativeRel F] [TopologicalSpace F]
  [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F]
  (G : GroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of F))) [IsAffineHom G.X.hom],
  IsSimplyConnected G → IsAlmostSimple G → ¬ IsAnisotropic G →
  ∀ (N : Subgroup (rationalPoints G)), N.Normal →
    (∀ (P U : GroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of F))) (p : P ⟶ G) (r : U ⟶ P),
      IsParabolicSubgroup p → LeviSubgroup.IsGeometricUnipotentRadical r →
      (rationalPoints.map (r ≫ p)).range ≤ N) →
    N = ⊤
```

In words: over every nonarchimedean local field `F`, of any characteristic,
let `G` be an affine, simply connected, almost-simple and isotropic group
scheme over `F`. Every normal subgroup of the rational points `G(F)` that
contains the images of the rational points of the geometric unipotent
radicals of all parabolic subgroups of `G` is all of `G(F)`.

## Proof

The Lean proof is the single application

```
localKneserTits_of_rankOneLeviFactors_of_classification
  ⟨rankOneLeviFactorExistence, rankOneLeviDerivedGeneration,
    rankOneLeviCocenterGeneration⟩
  localSimplyConnectedCocycleVanishing simplyConnectedAlmostSimpleWeilRestriction
  affineWeilSplitRank affineWeilParabolicElementarySubgroup
  ⟨localRankOneClassification, Quaternionic.localSymplecticKneserTits,
    Quaternionic.localSpinKneserTits⟩
```

of the theorem `localKneserTits_of_rankOneLeviFactors_of_classification`
(module `SimplyConnected.LocalRankOneClassificationReduction`), which derives
`LocalKneserTitsStatement.{u}` from six hypotheses. Each hypothesis is
discharged by theorems proved in the Library without hypotheses:

1. `RankOneLeviFactorStatements.{u}` (module `SimplyConnected.RankOneLeviFactors`)
   is the conjunction of three statements, given by the anonymous constructor
   from
   - `rankOneLeviFactorExistence` (module
     `SimplyConnected.RankOneLeviFactorExistenceProof`): every simple relative
     root has a rank-one Levi factor;
   - `rankOneLeviDerivedGeneration` (module
     `SimplyConnected.RankOneLeviDerivedGenerationProof`): the rational points of
     the derived subgroup of the centralizer of a generic cocharacter are
     generated by those of its intersections with the factors;
   - `rankOneLeviCocenterGeneration` (module
     `SimplyConnected.RankOneLeviCocenterGenerationProof`): the same for the
     cocenter.
2. `localSimplyConnectedCocycleVanishing` (module
   `SimplyConnected.LocalCocycleVanishingProof`): every cocycle of a semisimple
   simply connected group over a nonarchimedean local field on a finite
   separable field cover is a coboundary.
3. `simplyConnectedAlmostSimpleWeilRestriction` (module
   `SimplyConnected.AlmostSimpleWeilRestrictionProof`): an almost-simple simply
   connected group is a Weil restriction of an absolutely almost-simple simply
   connected group along a finite separable extension.
4. `affineWeilSplitRank` (module `Rational.AffineWeilSplitRankProof`): Weil
   restriction along a finite separable extension preserves the split rank.
5. `affineWeilParabolicElementarySubgroup` (module
   `Rational.AffineWeilParabolicElementaryProof`): the identification of the
   rational points of a Weil restriction carries the subgroup generated by the
   rational parabolic radicals onto the corresponding subgroup.
6. `LocalRankOneClassificationStatements.{u}` (module
   `SimplyConnected.LocalRankOneClassificationReduction`) is the conjunction of
   three statements, given by the anonymous constructor from
   - `localRankOneClassification` (module
     `SimplyConnected.LocalRankOneClassificationProof`): the classification of
     the absolutely almost-simple simply connected groups of split rank one over
     a nonarchimedean local field;
   - `Quaternionic.localSymplecticKneserTits` (module
     `Quaternionic.LocalSymplecticKneserTitsProof`): Kneser–Tits for the
     quaternionic symplectic groups of Witt index one;
   - `Quaternionic.localSpinKneserTits` (module
     `Quaternionic.LocalSpinKneserTitsProof`): Kneser–Tits for the quaternionic
     spin groups of Witt index one.

The route inside the reduction (reduction to rank one by Levi factors and
Weil restriction, vanishing of Galois cohomology, and the case analysis over
the rank-one classification) is described on the page of
`SimplyConnected.LocalRankOneClassificationReduction` and on the pages of the
modules it imports. The statement is not modified and no hypothesis is added.

## Literature

G. Prasad and M. S. Raghunathan, *On the Kneser–Tits problem*, Comment. Math.
Helv. 60 (1985), 107–121: the local case, by reduction to rank one, vanishing
of first Galois cohomology and the classification of the groups of rank one,
as described on the statement's page; page reference to be added.

T. Kaletha and G. Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023),
Chapter 2, §2.2, after Proposition 2.2.14, printed p. 76: by the positive
solution of the Kneser–Tits problem in [PR85], `G(k)^+ = G(k)` for a
`k`-isotropic simply connected `G` over a non-discrete locally compact field
`k`. The book cites this result and does not prove it.
