# Kneser–Tits for split-torus centralizers in simply connected groups: proof

Lean module: `Langlands.GroupScheme.SimplyConnected.KneserTitsCentralizerProof`,
namespace `Langlands.GroupScheme`.

## Statement

```
theorem Langlands.GroupScheme.simplyConnectedKneserTitsCentralizer :
    SimplyConnectedKneserTitsCentralizerStatement.{u}
```

The type is exactly the published proposition
`Langlands.GroupScheme.SimplyConnectedKneserTitsCentralizerStatement.{u}` of the
module `Langlands.GroupScheme.SimplyConnected.KneserTitsCentralizerStatement`,
at an arbitrary universe `u`, with no further hypothesis. Unfolded, it reads:

```
∀ (F : Type u) [Field F] [ValuativeRel F] [TopologicalSpace F]
  [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F]
  (G : GroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of F))) [IsAffineHom G.X.hom], IsSimplyConnected G →
  ∀ (S : CommGroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of F))) (i : S.toGrp ⟶ G),
    IsMaximalSplitTorus i →
  ∀ (Z : GroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of F))) (j : Z ⟶ G),
    (torusCentralizer i).IsRepresentedBy j →
    (rationalPoints.map j).range ≤
      (centralizerPoints i ⊓ Subgroup.closure (⋃ a ∈ relativeRoots i,
          ((RelativeRoot.subgroup i a).carrier (Over.mk (𝟙 _)) :
            Set (rationalPoints G)))) ⊔
        (Plancherel.rationalCommonNormKernel Z).map (rationalPoints.map j)
```

In words: let `G` be a simply connected group over a nonarchimedean local
field `F`, of any characteristic, with a maximal split torus `S` and the
represented centralizer `Z = Z_G(S)`. Every rational point of `Z` lies in the
subgroup generated by `Z(F) ∩ G(F)^+` and the norm-one subgroup `Z(F)^1`, where
`G(F)^+` is the subgroup of `G(F)` generated by the root groups `U_a(F)` of all
relative roots `a`, and `Z(F)^1` consists of the points `z` with `|χ(z)| = 1`
for every rational character `χ` of `Z`.

## Proof

The Lean proof is the single application

```
simplyConnectedKneserTitsCentralizer_of_factors simplyConnectedAlmostSimpleRationalGeneration
  simplyConnectedKneserTitsFactor simplyConnectedAnisotropicFactorCentralizer
```

of the theorem `simplyConnectedKneserTitsCentralizer_of_factors` (module
`SimplyConnected.KneserTitsCentralizerReduction`), whose type
`SimplyConnectedKneserTitsCentralizerReductionStatement.{u}` is the implication
from three statements to the target. Each is discharged by a theorem proved
in the Library without hypotheses:

1. `simplyConnectedAlmostSimpleRationalGeneration` (module
   `SimplyConnected.AlmostSimpleRationalGenerationProof`): the rational points
   of a simply connected group are generated by those of its closed normal
   almost-simple subgroups.
2. `simplyConnectedKneserTitsFactor` (module
   `SimplyConnected.KneserTitsFactorProof`): the rational points of every
   isotropic closed normal almost-simple subgroup lie in the subgroup generated
   by the root groups.
3. `simplyConnectedAnisotropicFactorCentralizer` (module
   `SimplyConnected.AnisotropicFactorCentralizerProof`): every anisotropic
   closed normal almost-simple subgroup centralizes the maximal split torus.

Inside the reduction, the isotropic factors contribute points of
`Z(F) ∩ G(F)^+`, and the anisotropic factors lie in `Z` and, being semisimple,
in `Z(F)^1`; the details are on the page of
`SimplyConnected.KneserTitsCentralizerReduction`. The statement is not modified
and no hypothesis is added.

## Literature

G. Prasad and M. S. Raghunathan, *On the Kneser–Tits problem*, Comment. Math.
Helv. 60 (1985), 107–121: the affirmative solution of the Kneser–Tits problem
over nonarchimedean local fields of arbitrary characteristic, with the product
decomposition of a simply connected group and the Bruhat decomposition
`G(k) = M(k) · G(k)^+`, as cited on the statement's page; page reference to be
added.

V. Platonov and A. Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994),
Chapter 7, §7.2: the definition of `G_K^+`, printed p. 406, and Theorem 7.6,
printed p. 414, the Kneser–Tits conjecture for simple simply connected
`K`-isotropic groups over a non-Archimedean locally compact field `K`. The
book's local fields have characteristic zero (printed p. 5).

T. Kaletha and G. Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023),
Chapter 2, §2.2, after Proposition 2.2.14, printed p. 76: `G(k)^+ = G(k)` for a
`k`-isotropic simply connected `G` over a non-discrete locally compact field,
citing [PR85].
