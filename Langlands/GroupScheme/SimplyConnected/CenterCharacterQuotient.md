# The full center character group as an integral quotient

A closed subgroup of a split torus is diagonalizable over the
same field. Choose diagonalizable models for the subgroup and
torus. Their closed inclusion is induced by a surjective map of
the model character groups. The evaluation-character equivalences
transport this surjectivity to restriction of actual characters.

The full center in the maximal torus of a Tits index is a closed
subgroup of that torus, so it has multiplicative type. Its
representability gives an isomorphism with any other representative
of the full center. Pass to the separable closure, where the
specified torus splits. Surjectivity of restriction and the
published identification of its kernel with the integral root
span identify the full center character group with the quotient.
On representatives this map intertwines the ordinary Galois
actions, by naturality of character precomposition.

This internal comparison supplies the actual character quotient
used to recognize the center of a simply connected cover.
