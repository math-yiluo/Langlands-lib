# Actual Weil inclusion for a selected geometric factor orbit

This derived internal construction starts with an actual almost-simple product
presentation over a separably closed Galois extension. The ground-field group
need not be almost-simple. Embeddings of the selected factor's exact stabilizer
field correspond bijectively to precisely its Galois orbit of actual factors.
The original embedding-conjugate group product therefore identifies with the
actual orbit subproduct in the entire upper-field group.

The resulting homomorphism retains every original embedding-coordinate inclusion.
Checking these coordinates proves compatibility with the full canonical Galois
descent action. Composing it with the canonical scalar-extended Weil comparison
gives a compatible homomorphism from the actual Weil restriction to the original
group. The published affine Galois homomorphism descent theorem descends it.
`orbitWeilInclusion_baseChange` recovers the precise original geometric map.

The extension field and extension-field group remain literally the selected
stabilizer field and its original descended factor. The construction uses no
root classification and no characteristic restriction.
