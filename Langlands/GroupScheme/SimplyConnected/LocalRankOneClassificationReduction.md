## Kneser–Tits for `SL₂(D)`

```
theorem CentralSimpleUnits.BinaryRoot.parabolicElementarySubgroup_eq_top_of_local_of_iso
    (K : Type u) [Field K] [ValuativeRel K] [TopologicalSpace K]
    [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField K]
    {D : Type u} [DivisionRing D] [Algebra K D] [FiniteDimensional K D]
    [Algebra.IsCentral K D]
    {G : GroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of K))}
    (hG : IsConnectedReductive G) (e : G ≅ groupScheme K D) :
    parabolicElementarySubgroup G = ⊤
```

Here `groupScheme K D` is the published binary reduced-norm-one group
`CentralSimpleUnits.BinaryRoot.groupScheme K D` (module
`Langlands.GroupScheme.CentralSimpleBinaryRoots`), that is, `SL₂(D)`. The
theorem says that over a nonarchimedean local field `K`, every connected
reductive group isomorphic to `SL₂(D)`, for a finite-dimensional central
division `K`-algebra `D`, is generated by the rational points of the
unipotent radicals of its parabolic subgroups.

Proof. The published
`CentralSimpleUnits.BinaryRoot.rationalPoints_range_le_parabolicElementarySubgroup_of_iso`
(module `CentralSimpleBinaryRadicalGeneration`) says: if the kernel of the
reduced norm on `Dˣ` is the commutator subgroup, then for every connected
reductive `G`, every `A ≅ groupScheme K D` and every homomorphism `a : A ⟶ G`,
the image of the rational points of `A` lies in
`parabolicElementarySubgroup G`. The kernel premise is the published
`Langlands.CentralDivision.localSK1 K D` (module
`Langlands.Algebra.LocalDivisionSK1Proof`), which proves
`LocalSK1Statement`. Apply this with `A = G`, the given `e` and `a = 𝟙 G`.
Every rational point `x` of `G` is its own image under `𝟙 G`, so it lies in
`parabolicElementarySubgroup G`.

The published `CentralSimpleBinaryLocalReduction` proves the same
conclusion for the Weil-restricted binary groups
`BinaryRoot.WeilRoot.groupScheme F E D`, with `LocalSK1Statement` as a
hypothesis. The theorem here is the ground-field form needed by family (i)
of the classification, with the hypothesis discharged.

## The bundled statement

```
def LocalRankOneClassificationStatements : Prop :=
  LocalRankOneClassificationStatement.{u} ∧
    Quaternionic.LocalSymplecticKneserTitsStatement.{u} ∧
      Quaternionic.LocalSpinKneserTitsStatement.{u}
```

It is the conjunction of three published statements, at the same arbitrary
universe `u`. Each has its own paired Markdown with sources.

- `LocalRankOneClassificationStatement` (module
  `SimplyConnected.LocalRankOneClassificationStatement`, Worker 5198). Over
  a nonarchimedean local field `K`, an affine, simply connected, absolutely
  almost-simple `G` of split rank one is isomorphic to one of the following:
  (i) `CentralSimpleUnits.BinaryRoot.groupScheme K D` for a
  finite-dimensional central division `K`-algebra `D`; (ii)
  `HermitianRoot.SchemeModel.groupScheme K L H w hw` for a separable
  quadratic extension `L/K` and an anisotropic diagonal Hermitian middle
  form `H` in 1 or 2 variables; (iii) `σ.symplecticGroup H` for a
  quaternion central division algebra `D` with a first-kind involution `σ`
  (`dim_K D^σ = 3`) and a nondegenerate antihermitian `H` of rank 2 or 3
  and Witt index 1. Or, still in (iii), `G` has a spin covering
  `π : G ⟶ σ.isometryGroup q` (`σ.IsSpinCover q π`) of a nondegenerate
  quadratic form `q` of rank 4 or 5 and Witt index 1.
- `Quaternionic.LocalSymplecticKneserTitsStatement` (module
  `Quaternionic.LocalSymplecticKneserTitsStatement`, Worker 5197). For the
  same `K`, `D`, `σ`, `m ∈ {2, 3}` and `H`,
  `parabolicElementarySubgroup (σ.symplecticGroup H) = ⊤`.
- `Quaternionic.LocalSpinKneserTitsStatement` (module
  `Quaternionic.LocalSpinKneserTitsStatement`, Worker 5197). For the same
  `K`, `D`, `σ`, `m ∈ {4, 5}` and `q`, and every `S` with a spin covering
  `π : S ⟶ σ.isometryGroup q`, `parabolicElementarySubgroup S = ⊤`.

The hypotheses on `D`, `σ`, `m`, `H` and `q` in the last two statements are
literally those of the (iii) disjuncts of the classification. So the
conjunction contains exactly what the proof below consumes.

### Provenance

The conjunction is internal: it groups the inputs of the case analysis.
The analysis is that of G. Prasad and M. S. Raghunathan, *On the
Kneser–Tits problem*, Comment. Math. Helv. 60 (1985), 107–121, registered
as `prasad-raghunathan-1985-kneser-tits` (printed page = PDF page + 105).
In §2, printed p. 111 / PDF p. 6, after listing the absolutely simple
simply connected groups of rank 1 over a nonarchimedean local field, the
source states: "For each of the above groups G, it is known that
G(K)^+ = G(K); see, for example, [8]". Here [8] is J. Tits, *Groupes de
Whitehead de groupes algébriques simples sur un corps*, Séminaire Bourbaki
505 (1977), registered as `tits-1977-whitehead-bourbaki-505` (printed
page = PDF page + 216). Its 1.2, printed pp. 221–222 / PDF pp. 5–6, gives
`SK₁(D) = 1` for local fields and `W(G, K) = 1` for isotropic simply
connected quasi-simple groups over a non-discrete locally compact field.
The classification statement covers the list. The two quaternionic
statements cover the Kneser–Tits property of family (iii). Families (i)
and (ii) are already proved in the Library, so they are not in the
conjunction.

## The reduction

```
theorem localAbsolutelyAlmostSimpleRankOneKneserTits_of_classification
    (h : LocalRankOneClassificationStatements.{u}) :
    LocalAbsolutelyAlmostSimpleRankOneKneserTitsStatement.{u}
```

`LocalAbsolutelyAlmostSimpleRankOneKneserTitsStatement` (module
`SimplyConnected.LocalRankOneWeilReduction`) says: over every
nonarchimedean local field `K`, an affine `G` with `IsSimplyConnected G`,
`IsAbsolutelyAlmostSimple G` and `splitRank G = 1` has
`parabolicElementarySubgroup G = ⊤`.

Proof. Split `h` into the classification `hcl` and the two quaternionic
statements `hsp` and `hspin`. Fix `K` and `G` with the hypotheses. Simple
connectedness gives semisimplicity, hence `IsConnectedReductive G`. Apply
`hcl` to `G` and treat the four cases.

1. Family (i): `e : G ≅ CentralSimpleUnits.BinaryRoot.groupScheme K D`.
   The theorem `parabolicElementarySubgroup_eq_top_of_local_of_iso` above
   concludes.
2. Family (ii): `H.IsAnisotropic` and
   `e : G ≅ HermitianRoot.SchemeModel.groupScheme K L H w hw`. The
   published `HermitianRoot.SchemeModel.parabolicElementarySubgroup_eq_top_of_iso`
   (module `Examples.HermitianRootModelRadicalGeneration`) concludes. It
   holds over any field, for a connected reductive group isomorphic to a
   Hermitian model with anisotropic middle form.
3. Family (iii), symplectic: `e : G ≅ σ.symplecticGroup H`. `hsp` gives
   `parabolicElementarySubgroup (σ.symplecticGroup H) = ⊤`, and the
   published `parabolicElementarySubgroup_eq_top_iff_iso e` (module
   `Rational.ParabolicWhiteheadIsomorphism`) transfers it to `G`.
4. Family (iii), spin: `π : G ⟶ σ.isometryGroup q` with
   `σ.IsSpinCover q π`. `hspin`, applied to `S = G` and `π`, concludes.
