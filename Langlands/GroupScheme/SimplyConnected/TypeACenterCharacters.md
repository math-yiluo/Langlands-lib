# Geometric center characters in type A

Let `G` be simply connected over a field and let its chosen Tits index have
type `A n` with `n ≥ 1`. The actual geometric center character group is
isomorphic to `ZMod (n + 1)`. In the specified numbering, the fundamental
character at the vertex numbered `i` goes to the class of `i + 1`, beginning
with `i = 0`.

The sequence of integers `i + 1` annihilates the Cartan rows modulo `n + 1`.
For an interior row this follows from the two adjacent differences. The end
row at the final vertex gives `n + 1` before reduction. The resulting map from
the Cartan presentation is onto because the first fundamental character
maps to `1`. Both groups have order `n + 1` by the determinant calculation.

The absolute Galois action permutes the fundamental center characters by the
star action. The type A numbering makes every such permutation either the
identity or the reversal of the path. Reversal sends the class of `i + 1`
to its negative. The fundamental characters generate, so the same sign holds
on every character for the given Galois element.

This internal consequence of the Cartan presentation supplies the cyclic
character module needed for the number-field central kernel comparison.
