# Descent to the factor stabilizer field

This internal construction by Worker 166 applies fixed-factor descent to the
actual finite separable field fixed by the selected factor's stabilizer.
The standard tower base-change isomorphism supplies the comparison and its
published projection equation. Every automorphism over the stabilizer field
fixes that factor, by the proved equality between the field's pointwise fixer
and the factor stabilizer.

The resulting data consist of an actual affine group over that field, a
closed inclusion into the original group base-changed to the field, and an
isomorphism recovering the selected factor over the upper field. The exact
inclusion equation is retained. Simple connectedness reflects from the
selected factor along the algebraic extension. Its inclusion is normal on
every test scheme, by the exact recovery equation and fpqc reflection.

The inputs are an actual smooth affine almost-simple product presentation
after an arbitrary Galois field extension, an affine original group whose
structure map is quasi-compact, quasi-separated and locally of finite
presentation, and a selected index. Neither existence of the product
presentation nor absolute almost-simplicity nor the final Weil-restriction
comparison is proved here.
