# Enumeration of actual rank-one Tits indices over local fields

`localRankOneTitsIndex` proves the published `LocalRankOneTitsIndexStatement`
for every universe parameter. It applies the actual-index reduction to E6
and D4 with the original hypotheses. The reduction uses the absolute Cartan
classification and actual component realization. The Cayley and
odd-quadratic results exclude G2 and higher B, and the kernel centre
argument excludes the inner E7 branch marking.

The remaining inner E6 branch marking would give a restricted triple weight
with nonzero obstruction in the degree-six division norm-one kernel. Its
ambient obstruction vanishes since the triple belongs to the integral root
span. The centrally marked D4 residuals would give a nonzero sum of
quaternion invariants of the leaf orbits, even though the sum of the actual
leaf weights is in the integral root span. For these two contradictions,
the supporting modules compare the obstructions with those of the actual
kernels and, in D4, the factors over the exact leaf fields.

The theorem returns the surviving disjunct directly, with its Cartan
entries, distinguished vertices, and full absolute Galois star image.
It covers every field and residue characteristic.

reached by the proof term.
