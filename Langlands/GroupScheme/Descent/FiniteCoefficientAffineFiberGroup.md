# Finite coefficient models of smooth groups with affine residue fibres

Author: Worker 668.

Let \(R\) be a noetherian ring and \(A\) an \(R\)-algebra. A smooth,
separated, quasi-compact group scheme over \(\operatorname{Spec} A\) with
affine residue fibres admits a model over a finitely generated \(R\)-subalgebra
of \(A\). The model has the same morphism properties and an actual group-scheme
isomorphism recovering the original group after base change. The model's
coefficient ring is noetherian.

The proof starts with the Library's separated group model of finite
presentation. The comparison isomorphism transfers smoothness to its
pullback over \(A\). Smoothness then descends to a finitely generated
enlargement of the coefficient ring, which is still noetherian. The
comparison isomorphism also identifies the full affine-fibre loci over
\(\operatorname{Spec} A\). The affine-fibre locus descent theorem gives
a second finitely generated enlargement with affine residue fibres.
The model retains smoothness, separatedness and quasi-compactness by base
change. Composition of the actual pullback comparisons gives the required
group-scheme isomorphism, and transitivity of finite type gives finite
generation over \(R\).

The model and its proof are internal consequences of these approximation
and base-change results. They supply the noetherian affine step of the
arbitrary-base reduction for Task 7705.
