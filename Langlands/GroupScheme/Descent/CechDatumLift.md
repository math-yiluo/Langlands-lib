# Lifting Čech descent data

A map on double overlaps pulls back a morphism between the two restrictions
of the group scheme. The direct and iterated pullback comparisons identify its
endpoints with the restrictions of the changed group scheme. This transport
preserves composition. It also commutes with restriction to the projections
of the triple overlap by the composition law for actual pullback and the
commuting squares of projections.

Bijectivity on the Hom sets over the double overlap lifts the transition map
and its inverse. Injectivity detects both inverse identities and, on the
triple overlap, the cocycle of the lifted isomorphism. The overlap of the
reindexed datum is the specified isomorphism, so the reindexed datum is equal
to the original datum on the new cover.

The construction is derived from the existing reindexing of group-scheme
Čech data and the pullback coherence theorems. It implements the lifting of
transition maps in SGA 3, Exposé X, Proposition 2.1, once Hom bijectivity on
the overlaps has been established.
