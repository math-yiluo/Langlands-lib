# Base change of Čech overlaps

A commuting square between scheme morphisms induces maps on their double and
triple overlaps. The maps are the universal maps of the respective pullbacks.
The projection to coordinates one and three commutes with the map of triple
overlaps because the two composites have equal projections to each coordinate.

For a cartesian square, the map of double overlaps is a pullback of the map
on the cover sources. The proof pastes the two pullback squares over the
second projection and cancels the bottom overlap square. The same argument
shows the map of triple overlaps is a pullback of the map of double overlaps.
These pullback squares allow properties stable under base change to be applied
at both levels. In particular, a surjective closed immersion gives a
homeomorphism of underlying spaces, by the embedding of a closed immersion
and point-surjectivity.

This construction is derived from the published definitions of actual Čech
data and the pullback pasting lemma. It is used to compare descent data across
nilpotent thickenings in the proof of SGA 3, Exposé X, Proposition 2.1.
