# Transporter descent with split-torus centralizer

The main result `PointwiseSchemeAction.exists_transporter_of_cover` descends a transporter from any affine flat surjective cover of the spectrum of a field. The source is an actual commutative split torus `T`, with two actual homomorphisms `i₁,i₂ : T → G`. The second map is monomorphic on underlying over schemes, and its all-test centralizer is assumed to equal its all-test image. From an actual transporter point over the cover the result produces an actual base point satisfying the transporter relation on every refinement of every test scheme. No reductivity, connectedness, or separability is assumed here: the centralizer equality is an explicit input to this internal construction.

The proof uses the published transporter convention `i₁(t) x = x i₂(t)`. For two such points `x,y`, their right difference `x⁻¹ y` centralizes `i₂`, as follows directly by multiplying their equations. Consequently the cocycle `Cocycle.coboundary x⁻¹` takes values in the torus image on every test scheme. The definition `PointwiseSchemeAction.transporterCocycle` lifts this cocycle along `i₂`, using its monicity and the supplied centralizer equality. It is a genuine torus-valued cocycle, with naturality and the cocycle equation inherited from the published `Cocycle.lift` construction.

The helper `PointwiseSchemeAction.correctedTransporter` multiplies the original cover point on the right by the image of a torus cochain. Such multiplication preserves its transporter equation. When the cochain trivializes `transporterCocycle`, its coboundary law says `x(a)⁻¹ x(b) = f(a) f(b)⁻¹` after applying `i₂`, so the corrected points `x(a) f(a)` and `x(b) f(b)` agree. The published field-cocycle construction `Cocycle.fieldTrivializingCochain` supplies such a cochain.

The published `exists_section_of_forall_eq` then descends this corrected point along the cover's effective epimorphism; Mathlib's fpqc result supplies that property from flatness, surjectivity, and quasi-compactness. Affineness implies the last condition. The auxiliary `PointwiseSchemeAction.isTransporter_of_cover` proves the all-test transporter relation for the descended section: pull each test scheme back along the original fpqc cover, use the relation on that cover, and cancel the epimorphic pullback projection. This auxiliary reflection result works over any base scheme for a flat, surjective, quasi-compact cover.

This is an internal assembly of the published all-test transporter and centralizer definitions, cocycle lifting, field split-torus cocycle trivialization, and effective descent. It does not yet descend an extension-field automorphism of a split torus, identify a Weyl quotient, or prove the host generic-fibre isomorphism theorem.
