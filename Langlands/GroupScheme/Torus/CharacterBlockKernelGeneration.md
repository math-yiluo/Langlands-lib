# Kernel generation from a block decomposition of characters

Authors: Worker 187.

The module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.CharacterBlockKernelGeneration` (namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme.CharacterBlockKernelGeneration`) proves a descent criterion.
If the characters of a torus split into blocks matching a family of homomorphisms,
then the rational points are generated by the rational points of the kernels. It is
an internal helper for `Langlands.GroupScheme.leviCocenterKernelGeneration` and has
no literature source.

## Setting

`k` is a field and `E/k` a finite Galois extension. `Q` and `R α` (for `α : A`) are
commutative group schemes over `k` whose base changes to `E` are split tori (`hQ`,
`hR`), and `π α : Q ⟶ R α` are homomorphisms. `b` is a `ℤ`-basis, indexed by a
finite type `I`, of the characters of `Q_E`, and `θ : I → A` sorts the basis
vectors into blocks. The key hypothesis is

```
h : ∀ α x, x ∈ range (characterLattice.baseChangePullback k E (π α)) ↔
      ∀ i, θ i = α → b.repr x i = 0
```

That is, the characters of `Q_E` pulled back from `R α` are exactly those whose
coordinates in block `α` vanish.

## Results

- `rationalPoints_map_sum`: on rational points, the map induced by a finite sum
  `∑ α ∈ s, f α` of homomorphisms `Q ⟶ Q'` is the pointwise product of the maps
  induced by the `f α`. Proof: induction on `s`, using `MonObj.comp_mul`.
- `exists_blockEndomorphisms`: there are endomorphisms `ε α : Q ⟶ Q` with
  `∑ α ∈ univ.image θ, ε α = 𝟙 Q` and `ε α ≫ π α = 0` for every `α`. Proof:
  1. By `h`, a character whose block-`α` coordinates vanish is pulled back from
     `R α`. The pullback commutes with the Galois action
     (`characterLattice.baseChangePullback_galois`), so its Galois translates are
     also pulled back from `R α`. Hence the Galois action preserves "block-`α`
     coordinates vanish", and by `BasisBlockProjection.proj_comm` it commutes with
     the block projection `proj b θ α`.
  2. A Galois-equivariant additive map on the characters of the split torus `Q_E` is
     the character pullback of a unique `k`-homomorphism
     (`IsSplitTorus.existsUnique_hom_of_equivariant_characterMap`). Let `ε α` be the
     homomorphism whose pullback is `proj b θ α`.
  3. By the same uniqueness for homomorphisms `Q ⟶ Q`, `∑ ε α = 𝟙 Q`, since the
     block projections sum to the identity (`sum_proj`). By uniqueness for
     homomorphisms `Q ⟶ R α` (`R α` splits over `E`), `ε α ≫ π α = 0`: its pullback
     sends a character of `R α` to the block-`α` projection of a character pulled
     back from `R α`, which is `0` by `h` and `proj_eq_zero_iff`.
- `mem_closure_ker`: every rational point `x` of `Q` lies in the subgroup generated
  by the union of the kernels of the maps `π α` on rational points. Proof: by
  `rationalPoints_map_sum` and `∑ ε α = 𝟙`, `x` is the product of the points
  `ε α (x)`. Each lies in the kernel of `π α`, since `ε α ≫ π α = 0`.
