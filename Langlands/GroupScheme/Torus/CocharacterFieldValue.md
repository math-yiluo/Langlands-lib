# Cocharacter values on a tower of fields

Let \(F\subset E\subset M\) be a tower of fields, \(T\) a commutative group scheme over \(F\), and \(\nu\) a cocharacter of \(T_E\). Evaluating \(\nu\) at a unit of \(M\) gives the same \(M\)-coefficient point of \(T\) as first extending \(\nu\) to \(M\) along the canonical comparison and then evaluating.

The proof compares the first projections of the direct and iterated pullbacks on the underlying schemes. The affine test comparison preserves the unit coordinate, and the actual base-changed cocharacter acts on that same unit. This gives the equality in the coefficient-point group used in the intermediate-field norm formula.
