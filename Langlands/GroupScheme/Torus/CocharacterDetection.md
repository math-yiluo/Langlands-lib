# Cocharacters detecting the characters of a torus

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.CocharacterDetection`. It imports
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.CocharacterSplitting`,
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.CocharacterInseparableDescent`,
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.CocharacterGaloisDescent` and the Library module
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.SplitComponentProof` (for
`finiteType_coordinateAlgebra`).

Setting: `k` is a field and `Z` a commutative group scheme over `Spec k`.
`IsTorus Z.toGrp` (Library, `Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.Lattice`) means that
for some field extension `K` of `k` and finite type `I`, the base change of
`Z` to `K` is isomorphic to the split torus `splitTorus (Spec K) I`. Points of
`Z` over a `k`-algebra `S` are the `Lie.AffinePoints Z.toGrp S`; `IsDatum χ m x`
(from `Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.CocharacterDatum`) says that the point `x`
over `S[Λ]` is multiplicative and that the character `χ` takes the value `X^m`
on it.

## Main result

`CocharacterDetection.exists_gm_characterRestrict_ne_zero_of_isTorus`:

```
theorem exists_gm_characterRestrict_ne_zero_of_isTorus [IsAffineHom Z.toGrp.X.hom]
    [LocallyOfFiniteType Z.toGrp.X.hom] (hZ : IsTorus Z.toGrp) {χ : rationalCharacters Z.toGrp}
    (hχ : χ ≠ 0) : ∃ φ : (Gm (Spec (CommRingCat.of k))).toGrp ⟶ Z.toGrp, characterRestrict φ χ ≠ 0
```

A nonzero rational character of a torus `Z` over `k`, affine and locally of
finite type over `k`, restricts nontrivially to some homomorphism
`φ : 𝔾_m ⟶ Z` defined over `k`. In other words, the cocharacters of `Z`
defined over `k` detect its nonzero rational characters.

## Intermediate statements (namespace `Langlands.GroupScheme.CocharacterDatum`)

- `pow_affineMap_eq_of_isTorus` (Frobenius on points of a torus). Let `Z` be
  an affine torus and `p` the exponential characteristic of `k`. If a
  `k`-algebra map `g : A → A` satisfies `a ^ (p ^ e) = (g a) ^ (p ^ e)` for all
  `a`, then `x ^ (p ^ e) = (g_* x) ^ (p ^ e)` for every point `x` of `Z` over `A`.
- `IsDatum.exists_finiteType` (descent to a finitely generated subalgebra).
  If the coordinate algebra of `Z` is of finite type, a datum of weight `m`
  over `K[Λ]`, `K` a field over `k`, comes from a datum of weight `m` over
  `B[Λ]` for a finitely generated `k`-subalgebra `B` of `K`.
- `IsDatum.exists_finiteDimensional` (specialization). A datum over `B[Λ]`,
  `B` a nonzero finitely generated `k`-algebra, gives a datum of the same
  weight over `L[Λ]` for a field `L` finite over `k`.
- `IsDatum.exists_separableClosure` (purely inseparable descent). For an
  affine torus `Z` and torsion-free `Λ`, a datum over `L[Λ]`, `L` finite over
  `k`, comes from a datum of the same weight over the separable closure
  `separableClosure k L` of `k` in `L`.
- `IsDatum.exists_of_isSeparable` (Galois descent). A datum of weight `m` over
  `F[Λ]`, `F` finite separable over `k`, gives a datum over `k[Λ]` of weight
  `n • m` for some natural number `n ≠ 0`.

## Proofs

- `pow_affineMap_eq_of_isTorus`. Choose a splitting `s` of `Z` over a field `K`.
  `CocharacterDescent.pow_eq_pow_tensorProductMap` (module
  `FrobeniusTensorPower`) turns the hypothesis on `A` into the same identity
  for `id ⊗ g` on `K ⊗[k] A`, and
  `CocharacterSplitting.pow_affineMap_eq_of_tensor s` concludes.
- `IsDatum.exists_finiteType`. Let `s` be a finite generating set of the
  coordinate algebra, and let `B` be the `k`-subalgebra of `K` generated by
  the finitely many coefficients of the elements `(coord x) c ∈ K[Λ]`,
  `c ∈ s`. By `exists_mapAlgHom_eq`, each `(coord x) c` with `c ∈ s` lies in
  the image of `B[Λ]`; by induction over `Algebra.adjoin` the same holds for
  all `c`. `exists_affineMap_eq` gives a point `y` over `B[Λ]` mapping to `x`,
  and `IsDatum.of_coeff_map` shows `y` is a datum of weight `m`.
- `IsDatum.exists_finiteDimensional`. Take a maximal ideal `𝔪` of `B`; the
  field `L = B ⧸ 𝔪` is a finitely generated `k`-algebra, hence finite over `k`
  by Zariski's lemma (`finite_of_finite_type_of_isJacobsonRing`). The datum is
  pushed along `B → B ⧸ 𝔪` by `IsDatum.coeff_map`.
- `IsDatum.exists_separableClosure`. Let `p` be the exponential characteristic
  of `k`; it is also that of `F = separableClosure k L`, and `L` is finite and
  purely inseparable over `F`. With
  `e = IsPurelyInseparable.exponent F L`, every `ℓ ^ (p ^ e)` lies in `F`
  (`IsPurelyInseparable.exponent_def'`). Apply
  `IsDatum.exists_of_pow_mem_range` (module `CocharacterInseparableDescent`)
  with its Frobenius hypothesis supplied by `pow_affineMap_eq_of_isTorus`.
- `IsDatum.exists_of_isSeparable`. Let `N` be the normal closure of `F` inside
  a separable closure of `k` (`IntermediateField.normalClosure k F
  (SeparableClosure k)`); it is finite Galois over `k`. Embed `F` into `N`
  (`normalClosure.algHomEquiv`, `IsSepClosed.lift`), push the datum along this
  embedding, and apply `IsDatum.exists_of_isGalois` (module
  `CocharacterGaloisDescent`), which gives weight `Nat.card (N ≃ₐ[k] N) • m`;
  this cardinality is positive.
- Main result. `Z` is affine (`isAffine_of_isAffineHom`). From the splitting
  over `K`, `CocharacterSplitting.exists_isDatum` gives a datum `x` of nonzero
  weight `m : PUnit → ℤ` over `K[PUnit → ℤ]` for (the lattice image of) `χ`.
  The coordinate algebra is of finite type (`finiteType_coordinateAlgebra`),
  so the four descent steps above give a datum over `k[PUnit → ℤ]` of weight
  `n • m` with `n ≠ 0`. Since `PUnit → ℤ` is torsion-free, `n • m ≠ 0`, and
  `CocharacterDatum.exists_gm_characterRestrict_ne_zero` produces `φ`.

## Provenance

Internal. The result is the standard fact that a torus over a field is
determined by its cocharacters: the pairing between the character group and
the cocharacter group of a torus is perfect over a splitting field, and the
characters and cocharacters defined over `k` are the Galois invariants. The
proof here is a different, direct argument designed by Worker 1001 for task
6d0882ee: it never constructs the cocharacter group. Instead it descends one
explicit cocharacter-like point from a splitting field to `k`: to a finitely
generated subalgebra, to a finite residue field (Zariski's lemma), through
the purely inseparable part (by a Frobenius argument), and through the Galois
part (by taking the product of conjugates, which multiplies the weight by a
positive integer). Worker 1178 wrote the descent modules
`CocharacterCoefficientDescent`, `FrobeniusTensorPower`,
`CocharacterGaloisDescent` and `CocharacterInseparableDescent` used here.
