# Smooth separated scheme quotients by torus centralizers

Let `A` be a relative torus over an arbitrary scheme `S`, let `G` be a smooth relatively affine group scheme, and let `t : A → G` be a group homomorphism. Suppose that `N` is another smooth relatively affine group scheme with a homomorphism `n : N → G`, and `c : Z → N` is a closed subgroup representing the preimage of the published all-test centralizer `torusCentralizer t`. If `Z` is smooth over `S`, `homogeneousQuotient` chooses an actual quotient scheme `Q` and a morphism `q : N → Q`. It retains separatedness, local finite type and quasi-compactness of `Q → S`, smoothness, surjectivity and quasi-compactness of `q`, and the exact all-test fiber criterion `g ≫ q = h ≫ q ↔ g⁻¹ * h ∈ range c`.

This construction does not require `N` to represent the normalizer, does not require `t` to be an immersion, and imposes no field or reductivity hypothesis. A group structure on `Q` and etaleness of `Q → S` are not asserted in this module.

The proof first takes a universal actual Hom family with relative torus source. The conjugation map from `N` into its parameter scheme has exactly the preimage-centralizer cosets as its fibers. The published smooth-relation quotient construction therefore supplies an algebraic-space quotient embedded into that scheme, hence an actual scheme quotient, with smooth surjective projection. This proves `exists_smooth_quotient`.

For separatedness, the actual coset relation `N × Z → N × N` is a closed immersion because `c` is closed. Its square with the quotient diagonal is a pullback, as proved by `HomogeneousCosetRelation.isPullback_diagonal_of_fibers`. The product of the two smooth surjective projections is fppf, so descent of closed immersions shows that the quotient diagonal is closed. To prove quasi-compactness of `Q → S`, the inverse image of each compact open in `S` is the continuous surjective image of its compact inverse image in `N`. Local finite presentation descends along the smooth surjective quotient projection. Finally, separatedness of `Q → S` and quasi-compactness of `N → S` imply quasi-compactness of `q`.

This is an internal derived construction from `HomScheme.SmoothRelativeSourceRepresentability`, `HomScheme.ConjugationMap`, the published smooth-relation scheme-quotient theorem, the actual homogeneous coset relation, its quotient-diagonal pullback comparison, and fppf descent of morphism properties. It changes no Library statement and transcribes no new literature assertion. The proof of separatedness uses the same diagonal descent mechanism as the open-subgroup quotient etaleness proof.
