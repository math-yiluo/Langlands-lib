# Actual coordinate-torus conjugacy inside a common dynamic parabolic

Let `G` be a smooth affine group scheme over an affine base `Spec R`, where `R` is an arbitrary commutative ring in universe `u`. Let `i` and `j` be supplied fibrewise maximal coordinate split tori, with finite coordinate types which may differ. Suppose the coordinate cocharacters `wt` and `wt'` define equal actual dynamic parabolic subgroup functors.

`splitTorus.etaleParabolicCoordinateConjugacyAt` constructs actual data through every base point `s`: an affine etale neighborhood covering `s`, one integral linear equivalence between the coordinate character lattices, and an actual conjugating section of the pulled-back ambient group. The conjugator belongs to the base change of the original dynamic parabolic. Its conjugation gives the exact normalized square between the original two pulled-back torus inclusions and the fixed coordinate isomorphism. Existence of a parabolic-valued conjugator, or of its coordinate matrix, is not an input. No nontriviality, reductivity, or connectedness assumption on the coefficient ring or the group is required beyond the stated smooth affineness of `G`.

The implementation chooses the actual affine representative of the first dynamic parabolic. Both tori factor through it by their pointwise range containment and represented-subgroup factorization. This representative is affine because its inclusion is closed, and smooth by dynamic subgroup smoothness. The published lemma `IsFibrewiseMaximalTorus.of_comp` proves that maximality in the ambient group implies maximality in the closed intermediate subgroup, by passing to every residue field and applying the field maximality property. The established etale coordinate-conjugacy construction is then applied inside this representative. Mapping the section and its exact square through the original inclusion uses the published compatibility of actual inner conjugation with group homomorphisms. The range certificate of the base-changed parabolic representative proves the asserted membership on the original affine base.

This module is derived internal infrastructure for comparing canonical filtration terms of relative radicals. It does not itself identify filtration terms, descend a filtration, or assert the full radical-filtration theorem. Its proof combines actual dynamic representatives and smoothness, maximal-torus inheritance in closed subgroups, and the existing etale coordinate-conjugacy construction; it adds no independent literature assertion.
