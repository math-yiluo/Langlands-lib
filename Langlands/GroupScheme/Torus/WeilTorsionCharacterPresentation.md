# Embedding presentations of Weil and norm-one torsion

Let `B/k` be a finite separable field extension. Over the separable closure of
`k`, restriction from the integral lattice indexed by the embeddings of `B`
gives two surjective character maps for positive exponent `n`. For the
`n`-torsion of the Weil restriction of the multiplicative group, the kernel
consists of the multiples of `n`. For the `n`-torsion of its norm-one torus,
the kernel is the sum of the multiples of `n` and the constant functions.
Both maps intertwine the canonical Galois actions, with inverse pullback
on the embedding lattice.

The Weil lattice already has the embedding coordinates. The norm-one
restriction is onto with kernel the diagonal integers. Composing with the
torsion restriction adds the multiples of `n` to the kernel, since a lift
of an `n`-multiple differs from an `n`-multiple by an element of the
original kernel. On any torus with integral geometric coordinates,
reduction modulo `n` gives the character coordinates of the torsion.
The split Weil torus has as many coordinates as the extension degree,
and the norm-one torus has one fewer, giving the respective cardinalities.

These internal computations give presentations for comparing the actual
torsion groups with other groups of multiplicative type.
