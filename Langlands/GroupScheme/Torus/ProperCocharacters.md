# Cocharacters of proper group schemes

Let a proper commutative group scheme over a separably closed field be given.
Each homomorphism from the multiplicative group to it is the zero
homomorphism.

The proof applies the valuative criterion to the fraction field of the power
series ring. The image of the invertible parameter extends over the ring.
For each ground-field unit, rescaling the parameter by this unit fixes the
closed point. On the generic point, the homomorphism law changes the
extended image by translation by the image of the unit. Separatedness
identifies the two extensions with the same generic restriction. Comparing
at the closed point and cancelling shows that the unit maps to the identity.
The rational points of the smooth multiplicative group over the separably
closed field are dense, so separatedness gives the homomorphism equality.

## Provenance

This proof uses the Library's affine unit coordinates on the multiplicative
group and rational-point density for smooth schemes. It uses Mathlib's
valuative criterion and the rescaling endomorphism of the power series ring.
