# Étale local coverage by ordered projector charts

A `SplitChart` records finitely many distinct weights generating the character lattice of a split torus. Its scheme is the affine scheme of ordered projectors for these weights. Its map to a given universal moduli classifies the projector subgroup.

Every moduli point has an étale splitting neighborhood for the universal torus. After passing to an affine neighborhood, the inclusion of the split torus is a diagonalizable representation into the explicit general linear group. On a basic open containing the chosen point, the projector construction gives a family with positive weight support. The inclusion remains closed, so the weights generate the character lattice.

The affine base change comparison identifies the subgroup of this projector family with the evaluation of the original universal family on the basic open. The representing property gives equality of the classifying maps. This yields an étale neighborhood of the point whose map to the moduli factors through the ordered projector chart.
