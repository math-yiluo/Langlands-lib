# Proof of Galois descent of prescribed torus character values

`Langlands.GroupScheme.torusEquivariantCharacterValueDescent.{u}` has exactly the proposition `Langlands.GroupScheme.TorusEquivariantCharacterValueDescentStatement.{u}` as its type. It retains every hypothesis of that statement, including arbitrary universe `u` and Galois extensions that need not be finite, and introduces no additional premise.

The proof is a direct application of the published theorem `Langlands.GroupScheme.exists_rationalPoint_characterValue_eq_of_equivariant` from `Langlands.GroupScheme.Torus.EquivariantCharacterValueDescent`. Unfolding the proposition gives exactly the dependent function type of that theorem, with the same order of field, algebra, Galois, group scheme, splitness, homomorphism and equivariance binders. No transport or strengthening of the conclusion is needed.

For the mathematical route supplied by this imported theorem, split-torus character evaluation first realizes the homomorphism by an `L`-point. Its proof uses `characterLattice.pointCharactersEquiv` to construct the point and `pointCharacters_bijective` to separate points. The identity `characterValue_inverseConjugate` identifies character values at a conjugate point with inverse-conjugated values at the Galois-transported character. Equivariance then makes this point fixed by every coefficient automorphism. The splitting hypothesis implies affineness of `T` by `SplittingAssembly.isAffine_of_isTorus`; `GaloisPoint.algebraicFixedEquiv` descends the fixed point over an arbitrary Galois extension. Finally, `affineBaseChangeEquiv_rationalBaseChangePoint` identifies its extension with the constructed `L`-point and recovers all prescribed values.
