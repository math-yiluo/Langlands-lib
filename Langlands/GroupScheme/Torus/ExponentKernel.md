# Full exponent kernels over arbitrary rings

This internal construction is the character-kernel step of Conrad,
*Reductive group schemes* (2014), proof of Theorem 3.3.4, pp. 98–99.
It applies to every commutative ring, including disconnected and nonreduced
rings.

For a subgroup `N` of the coordinate lattice `I → ℤ`, the construction is
the diagonalizable group with character group `(I → ℤ) / N`. The quotient
uses all of `N` and retains torsion. Restriction along the quotient map,
followed by the published diagonalizable identification of a coordinate
split torus, gives a closed subgroup of that torus.

The pointwise characterization is proved on every test scheme: its image
consists exactly of the points at which every exponent in `N` evaluates
to one. One implication evaluates quotient characters on zero classes;
the other factors a character through the full quotient. Thus this result
describes scheme-theoretic character kernels without any field or
reducedness hypothesis.
