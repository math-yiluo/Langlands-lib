# Multiplicative-type groups over nilpotent thickenings

The spectrum of the nilpotent quotient is a surjective closed immersion.
Full faithfulness follows from the Hom bijection for multiplicative-type
groups along every surjective closed immersion.

Étale-local diagonalizability supplies a splitting neighbourhood of every
point on the quotient spectrum. A standard étale affine chart through that
point lifts to an étale chart over the original ring. The cartesian square
of spectra identifies its reduction with the chosen chart. The resulting
covering family gives diagonalizable local models over the original base.
The chart assembly theorem lifts the transition maps, checks their cocycle,
and applies effective affine descent. The descended group is of
multiplicative type by fppf locality, so it is flat. Its reduction and the
given group solve the same reduced datum and are therefore isomorphic.

The proof follows SGA 3, Exposé X, Proposition 2.1 and Corollary 4.5
(registered source `sga3-expose-x-gille-polo-2009`, pp. 66–67 and 79).
The convention on multiplicative-type groups is that of Conrad,
*Reductive group schemes*, Definition B.1.1 (registered source
`conrad-2014-reductive-group-schemes`, p. 280).
