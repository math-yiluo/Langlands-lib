# Nilpotent rigidity of a diagonalizable action in affine coordinates

Worker 874. This internal consequence of the published natural character-action
grading and nilpotent module reduction lemmas is an ingredient for infinitesimal
rigidity of homomorphisms from groups of multiplicative type. It uses no additional
literature assertion.

For a commutative ring R, an abelian group M and an R-algebra B, a natural action
of the diagonalizable character functor on the affine point functor of B gives
homogeneous R-linear projections on B. Each projection is idempotent, by the
published coassociativity identity. If the coaction is congruent to the trivial
coaction modulo a nilpotent ideal I of R, its degree-zero projection equals the
identity and every other projection equals zero. This follows from the published
rigidity of commuting idempotent endomorphisms modulo I B; the comparison
endomorphism is respectively the identity or zero. Thus the coaction is trivial.

To check this congruence it suffices that the action is trivial on every R-algebra
annihilated by I. Use the universal character and universal point over the group
algebra of B/I B. Comparing its coefficients puts every projection difference in
I B. Evaluating the now-trivial coaction proves triviality on every algebra.

All objects are in an arbitrary universe u. The result assumes no finite
generation of M and no flatness, smoothness, finite generation or finite
presentation of B.
