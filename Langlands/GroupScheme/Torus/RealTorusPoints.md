# Complex character coordinates for real points of rational tori

Character evaluation passes through the base-change comparison of actual
points to give the character values of a complex point at any chosen splitting
field of a rational torus. The character values identify points with
homomorphisms from the character lattice to the complex units. This
identification is natural under pullback of characters along torus maps.

If an automorphism of the splitting field agrees with complex conjugation
under its embedding, evaluation at the conjugate point is obtained by applying
that automorphism to the character and conjugating its value. The proof compares
the twisted coefficient test for this automorphism with the original test via
the conjugation map of complex coefficient algebras.

These formulas identify the real points with the equivariant complex
characters. A real point is fixed by complex conjugation after extension of
coefficients. Conversely, equivariance produces a conjugation-fixed complex
point by character separation. The identity and complex conjugation are the
two automorphisms of the quadratic extension `ℂ/ℝ`, so the relative fixed-point
comparison descends this point to `ℝ`.

These internal comparisons use the existing split-torus character evaluation,
Galois action and actual base-change point equivalences.
