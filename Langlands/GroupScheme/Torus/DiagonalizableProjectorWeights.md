# Coefficient projectors of a diagonalizable representation

The coefficients of the group-algebra matrix at the universal point are matrices over the base. Only finitely many weights occur because the matrix has finitely many entries and each entry is a finite sum of monomials.

The Library's coefficient-column theorem says that the column of the projector at a given weight transforms by that character. At the universal point, comparing the coefficient of another weight shows that distinct projectors multiply to zero. Comparing the coefficient of the same weight gives idempotence. Evaluation at the identity shows that all projectors sum to the identity matrix.

On an arbitrary test scheme, the character-valued point evaluates the group-algebra functions in its ring of global functions. The entries of the matrix are therefore the evaluated character monomials with the corresponding projector coefficients. The proof uses the relative affine section comparison and the naturality of the point matrix.

This internal result extracts the ordered projector chart data locally from an arbitrary closed split torus representation. It applies to an arbitrary finite matrix index type and uses no rank or connectedness condition on the base.
