# Coordinate maps from a diagonalizable source

For an actual homomorphism `f : D_R(M) → G`, where `G` is affine,
`diagonalSourceCoordinateMap f` is the contravariant Hopf coordinate map
from `AffineCoordinates.hopfAlgebra G` to the standard group algebra `R[M]`.
The base ring is arbitrary and the abelian character group need not be free
or finitely generated. There are no smoothness, flatness, or noetherianity
assumptions in this interface.

The construction conjugates `f` by the canonical actual Hopf-spectrum
isomorphisms of the source and target, then uses the fully faithful
Hopf-spectrum functor to recover its coordinate map. The theorem
`diagonalSourceCoordinateMap_spec` states the exact conjugation identity.
Applying Hopf spectrum to an equality of coordinate maps and cancelling the
two comparison isomorphisms proves `diagonalSourceCoordinateMap_injective`.
`eq_of_diagonalSourceCoordinates_eq` gives the same equality-detection
criterion in terms of the underlying bialgebra maps.

`open_and_closed_of_diagonalSourceCoordinates` assumes that this coordinate
map is surjective and that its kernel is the ideal generated by an
idempotent. The published affine-spectrum criterion
`isOpenImmersion_SpecMap_iff_of_surjective` makes its spectrum an open
immersion. Surjectivity makes that spectrum a closed immersion. The exact
conjugation identity and the canonical isomorphisms transport both properties
to the actual homomorphism `f`.

This is internal derived content from published full faithfulness of Hopf
spectrum, the actual coordinate comparisons, and the affine open-immersion
criterion. It is the scheme-side adapter for an algebraic open-and-closed
immersion argument in the complete-local deformation proof. It does not
assert that the coordinate conditions hold for any particular special-fibre
isomorphism; proving those conditions is a separate Hopf-algebra obligation.
