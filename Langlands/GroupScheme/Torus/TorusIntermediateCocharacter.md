# Invariant cocharacters over an intermediate field

Let \(T\) be an affine commutative group scheme over a field \(F\). Suppose \(E/F\) is finite Galois, \(T_E\) is a split torus, and \(M\) is an extension of \(E\) with the compatible \(F\)-algebra structure. If an \(F\)-automorphism \(\sigma\) of \(M\) fixes a cocharacter of \(T_M\), its value at \(-1\) in \(T(M)\) comes from the value at \(-1\) of a cocharacter of \(T_N\) for an intermediate field \(N\) of \(E/F\). The image of \(N\) in \(M\) is fixed pointwise by \(\sigma\).

The proof uses the bijection from cocharacters of \(T_E\) to cocharacters of \(T_M\) and its compatibility with restriction of automorphisms. It takes \(N\) to be the fixed field of the stabilizer of the resulting cocharacter of \(T_E\). Galois descent gives the cocharacter over \(N\), and the restriction of \(\sigma\) belongs to the stabilizer. Evaluation through the tower \(N \subseteq E \subseteq M\) preserves the unit \(-1\) and composes the coefficient maps.

This internal comparison supplies the distinguished embedding's factor in the intermediate-field norm argument for real-place torus localization.
