# Recognizing Weil torsion groups by absolute characters

Let `Z` be a group of multiplicative type over `k`, and let `B/k` be finite
separable. Suppose a family of exponent-`n` geometric characters of `Z` is
indexed by the embeddings of `B` into the separable closure of `k`, with
positive `n`. The family must generate the character group by integral
linear combinations and intertwine Galois postcomposition on embeddings
with the ordinary character action.

For Weil torsion the integral combination map vanishes on exponent
multiples. For norm-one torsion it also vanishes on constant functions
when the supplied characters have sum zero. The character presentations
then give surjective equivariant maps from the respective geometric
torsion character groups to the characters of `Z`. The supplied matching
orders make the maps bijective. The absolute character anti-equivalence
gives the actual group isomorphisms over `k`.

These internal criteria recognize the exact Weil and norm-one torsion
groups used in the arithmetic kernels.
