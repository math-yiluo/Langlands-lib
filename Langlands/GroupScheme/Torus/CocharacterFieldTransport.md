# Cocharacter transport along towers of fields

Let \(F\subset E\subset M\) be a tower of fields and let \(T\) be a commutative group scheme over \(F\). If \(T_E\) is a split torus, scalar extension on its cocharacter lattice is bijective and commutes with compatible automorphisms of \(E\) and \(M\) over \(F\). If \(T_M\) is a split torus, the canonical isomorphism between direct and iterated scalar extension identifies the action of an \(E\)-automorphism of \(M\) with that of the same automorphism over \(F\).

The cocharacter map uses the canonical isomorphism between direct and iterated pullback. The proof pairs cocharacters with characters and uses the Library's compatibility of the character action with each scalar comparison. The perfect split-torus pairing detects equality of cocharacters. Bijectivity of scalar extension through a split torus follows from the previously proved split-lattice base change theorem.

`groupHom_towerIsoOf_inv` identifies the underlying group homomorphism of the inverse tower isomorphism.
