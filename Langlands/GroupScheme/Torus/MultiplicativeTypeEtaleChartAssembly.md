# Assembling lifted étale splitting charts

The lifted charts have étale structure maps and come with cartesian
identifications of their reductions. Their coproduct is étale by source
locality. It is surjective because the map from the special base to the
original base is surjective and the reduced charts cover the special base.

On each lifted chart take the diagonalizable group with the character group
of the given splitting on the reduced chart. Disjoint group gluing assembles
these into a group of multiplicative type over the coproduct. The structure
map is affine by the geometry of multiplicative-type groups.

The inverse images of the coproduct components decompose the pulled-back
cover. The cartesian chart squares give a map from each inverse image to
the corresponding reduced chart. The prescribed splitting, its pullback
along that map, and the split group's base-change isomorphism identify the
special-fibre group with the local model on each component. Disjoint gluing
assembles these isomorphisms. Effective étale descent now gives the required
flat lift.

The construction uses the disjoint group-gluing and étale effectivity
interfaces to implement the passage from lifted splitting charts to a
lifted group in SGA 3, Exposé X, Proposition 2.1 (registered source
`sga3-expose-x-gille-polo-2009`, pp. 66–67).
