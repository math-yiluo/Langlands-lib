# Split-torus representation weights over commutative rings

This internal generalization of the weight section of `Rational.CharacterRestrictionFiniteIndex` works over any commutative ring `k`, for any actual group scheme `G` over `Spec k`, any matrix representation `ρ` on `Fin N`, and any morphism `j` from the coordinate split torus of finite rank `I`. No reductivity, centrality or faithfulness is required for the projector identities. The matrix dimension and torus rank may be zero.

The universal matrix is the matrix of `ρ ∘ j` on the universal split-torus point with coefficients in `k[ℤ^I]`. The matrix `weight ρ I j m` consists of its coefficients at exponent `m`. The union of the finitely many entry supports is the finite set `weightSupport`; weights vanish outside it, its membership is equivalent to a nonzero weight matrix (`mem_weightSupport`), the set of nonzero weights is finite (`finite_weights`), and the universal matrix is reconstructed by its finite monomial expansion. Naturality of the affine point coordinates gives `affinePointsEquiv_eval`, the same finite expansion at every point over every commutative test algebra in the base universe. Evaluating at the identity shows that the weight matrices sum to the identity. Multiplying two independent universal torus points over `k[ℤ^I × ℤ^I]` and comparing the coefficient at `(m,n)` proves that the weights are orthogonal idempotents.

When `k` is nontrivial and the underlying scheme morphism of `ρ ∘ j` is a monomorphism, the exponents with nonzero weight matrix generate the full additive group `ℤ^I`. Let `Λ` be their additive closure. The torus point over `k[ℤ^I/Λ]` whose coordinates are the monomials of the standard basis classes has identity representation matrix. Monicity forces the torus point to be the identity; comparison of monomial coefficients, using `1 ≠ 0` in `k`, forces each standard basis class to vanish. These basis vectors generate the full group, so `Λ` is the full group.

The proofs generalize the already published universal-matrix and coefficient arguments of `Rational.CharacterRestrictionFiniteIndex`, retaining only the ring hypotheses they use. The monomial-unit helpers are imported from that module. The formula `weight_eq_field_weight` identifies these weights definitionally with the existing weights when the base is a field. This is internal provenance; no external structural theorem is invoked. The unit constructs projectors and proves their identities and the conditional full-lattice generation result. It does not construct a faithful integral representation or prove an isomorphism of reductive groups.
