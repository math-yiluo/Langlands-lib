# Absolute characters of torus torsion

Let `T` be a torus over a field `k` and let `n` be a nonzero integer.
Restriction to the actual kernel of multiplication by `n` identifies its
character group over the separable closure of `k` with the character group of
`T` over the same closure modulo the subgroup of all `n`-fold multiples.
On representatives, the comparison intertwines the canonical absolute Galois
actions.

The torsion inclusion is closed because the torus is separated. The closed
subgroup inherits multiplicative type. Multiplication is flat and surjective
on a splitting field by the split-torus multiplication theorem. These two
properties descend to `k`, and local finite presentation follows from local
finite type over a locally Noetherian scheme. Thus the torsion inclusion and
multiplication give a represented fppf quotient sequence. The absolute
character exactness theorem makes restriction surjective and identifies its
kernel with the image of pullback along multiplication. This pullback is
multiplication by `n` on characters. Naturality of the Galois action for
restriction yields the action formula.

This internal consequence of the absolute character exactness theorem
supplies the geometric character model for torsion in Weil and norm-one tori.
