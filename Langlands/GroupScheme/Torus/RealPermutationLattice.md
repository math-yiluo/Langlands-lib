# Permutation lattice with a fixed evaluation coordinate

Let a finite group act on a finite free integral lattice. Given a group element
and an integral linear form invariant under that element, the construction
embeds the lattice into an integral permutation lattice. The embedding splits
as a map of abelian groups and the supplied linear form is evaluation at one
coordinate fixed by the chosen element.

The coordinate set consists of all translates of the dual basis forms and of
the supplied form. Evaluation at those forms is equivariant for the inverse
permutation action on integer-valued functions. Evaluation at the original dual
basis forms recovers the vector and gives the additive retraction. The supplied
form is itself a coordinate, fixed by the chosen element by the invariance
hypothesis.

This is an internal construction for building an induced torus map that can
lift a specified real point.
