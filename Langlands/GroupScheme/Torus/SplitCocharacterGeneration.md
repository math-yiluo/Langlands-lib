# Rational points of a split torus are products of cocharacter values

Author: Worker 47 (Task 6502).

- `splitTorus.eq_prod_single`: every rational point `y` of the standard split torus
  `splitTorus (Spec K) I` (with `I` finite) is the product over `j ∈ I` of the values of the
  coordinate cocharacters `splitTorus.single j` at the coordinates of `y`. The proof applies
  `splitTorus.pointsEquiv`, which is multiplicative, and uses `pointsEquiv_comp_single` and
  `Finset.univ_prod_mulSingle`.
- `IsSplitTorus.exists_cocharacterPoints_prod`: let `S` be a commutative group scheme over a
  field `K` whose underlying group scheme is a split torus (`IsSplitTorus`, an isomorphism with
  some `splitTorus (Spec K) I`, `I` finite). Then there are a finite type `J` and cocharacters
  `ν_j` of `S` such that every rational point is a product
  `∏ j, CocharacterUniformizer.cocharacterPoints K S (ν j) (x j)` for some `x : J → Kˣ`. Here
  `ν_j` is the coordinate cocharacter `single j` followed by the inverse of the splitting
  isomorphism, and the identity is transported from `splitTorus.eq_prod_single` along that
  isomorphism.

The values `cocharacterPoints K S ν x` are the Library's evaluation of a cocharacter at a unit.
The Library computes characters on them by `rationalCharacterEval_cocharacterPoints`:
`χ(ν(x)) = x^{⟨χ, ν⟩}`.

## Provenance

This is internal infrastructure, and the fact is standard: a split torus is isomorphic to
`𝔾_m^n`, and its rational points are generated by the images of the coordinate cocharacters.
It is used for the finite orbits of the Galois action on the unramified building (Task 6502).
