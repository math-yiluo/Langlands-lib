# Faithful flatness onto the schematic image of a relative torus

Consider a group homomorphism from an affine group scheme that admits an étale
cover of the base with closed split-torus factorizations. Assume the homomorphism
is quasi-compact. Its canonical projection onto the schematic image is flat and
surjective.

The closed factor on a chart has a quasi-compact projection because its source
and the split torus are affine over the chart. The projection is flat and
surjective by construction, hence schematically dominant. The canonical
projection is also schematically dominant after flat base change. The two
closed dominant factorizations of the same map give an isomorphism between the
pullback of the image and the split torus. The isomorphism commutes with both
factorization maps, so the changed canonical projection is flat and surjective.
Pull back the étale cover to the canonical image. Descent on this fppf cover
gives both properties of the original projection.

The comparison and descent use the scheme-theoretic image and the given local
factorizations. They are internal steps in constructing the relative image torus.
