# Comparing ordered projector families by their weights

The entry of the universal invertible matrix is the sum of the monomials labelled by the weights, with coefficients the entries of the corresponding projectors. Distinct labels with distinct weights give a direct coefficient recovery formula. A projector with unit entry ideal over a nonzero ring has a nonzero entry. Consequently the set of nonzero matrix coefficients is exactly the set of labelled weights. Equality of the universal matrices identifies these sets and gives equality of the corresponding projectors after relabelling.

`WeightEquivalence` adds a character group isomorphism to the equivalence of the label sets. It requires this isomorphism to take each matched weight to the corresponding weight. When the weights generate, a label equivalence determines that isomorphism by its values on the weights. The type of possible matchings is therefore finite.

This is an internal step in describing the overlap of two ordered charts of the general linear subtorus moduli. The comparison can be applied after choosing a character lattice isomorphism on a split chart, and the finite set of possible matchings supplies the index set for the overlap.
