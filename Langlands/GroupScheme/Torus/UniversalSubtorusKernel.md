# Universal subtori from bounded-torsion quotients

These are internal derived lemmas for the largest-subtorus
construction.

The full pointwise kernel range commutes with every base change, by the
canonical kernel comparison isomorphism. A homomorphism whose composite with a
quotient is trivial therefore has its range in that kernel, on all test schemes.

If a closed relative torus is the full kernel of a homomorphism to a commutative
group killed by a fixed nonzero integer, it satisfies `IsUniversalSubtorus`.
After any base change the quotient is still killed by that integer.
The relative-torus multiplication lemma makes every torus homomorphism to the
quotient trivial. Its image lies in the full kernel, and the represented-subgroup
factorization constructs an actual group-scheme homomorphism through the torus.

No flatness, surjectivity, or representability of a quotient functor is asserted
or assumed. The input is a homomorphism and equality of its actual kernel range
with the closed-torus range.
