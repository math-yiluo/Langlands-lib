# Finiteness and affine images of ordered projector charts

Pulling back a split chart map to an étale neighborhood that factors through another split chart amounts to pulling back the second projection of their overlap. This projection is finite étale. Use the coproduct of the neighborhoods as one surjective étale map. Finiteness and étaleness of the pullback to this coproduct can be tested on the open cover by its summands. Both properties descend along the surjective étale map.

The image of a split chart is open because its map is étale. The factor to the image is finite by the pullback square along the image inclusion, and every point in the image has a preimage. The affine source and finite surjective descent give an affine open in the moduli.
