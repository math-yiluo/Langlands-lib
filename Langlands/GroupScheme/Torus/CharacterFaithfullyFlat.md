# Faithful flatness from actual character pullback

Author: Worker 1467.

This internal result is derived from the published canonical diagonalizable
point model of a split torus, and faithful flatness of a group-algebra map
induced by an injective homomorphism of abelian groups. It asserts no new
external-source attribution.

The proof identifies a homomorphism of actual split tori, in their canonical
diagonalizable models, with restriction along its full character pullback.
The all-test point description proves the comparison square. The published
group-algebra theorem makes restriction flat and surjective when character
pullback is injective. Composing with the two actual isomorphisms gives
flatness and surjectivity of the original homomorphism. The field and universe
are arbitrary; finite-index character inclusions are retained without
saturation or a smoothness assertion for the kernel.
