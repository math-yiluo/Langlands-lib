# Self-centralization of relative maximal tori

Internal support for the derived-group correspondence. A closed relative torus
in a reductive group scheme over an arbitrary scheme is its full centralizer
when its residue fibres are maximal tori. Consequently the whole centre is
contained in this torus on every test scheme. A central group homomorphism has
a canonical factorization through this torus, with the stated ambient composite.

For a split torus the proof checks the containment on an affine open cover of
the base and applies the split-affine self-centralization theorem. For a relative
torus it uses its étale splitting cover, regarded as an fppf cover, and descends
containment in its monomorphically represented range. The reverse containment
follows from commutativity of the torus. Arbitrary base change of fibrewise
maximality supplies maximality on each cover member. Central containment then
follows from containment of the centre in every torus centralizer; represented
subgroup factorization constructs the canonical homomorphism.

The result uses published self-centralizer, subgroup-locality and torus-transport
results and the fppf-locality support module. It asserts equality of subgroup
functors, including nonreduced test schemes, and imposes no affineness or
noetherian hypothesis on the base. It does not assert that derived intersections
are tori or that the full derived-group correspondence has been proved.
