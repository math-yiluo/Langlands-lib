# Local positive weight projectors of a diagonalizable representation

The entry ideal of each weight projector is generated by an idempotent. At a chosen prime, either that idempotent or its complement lies outside the prime. Inverting the product of all the chosen factors gives a basic open containing the point. Every chosen idempotent is then a unit and equals one. Each weight projector consequently vanishes on this open or has unit entry ideal throughout it.

The selected weights retain completeness because the omitted projectors vanish. Orthogonality survives base change. Enumerating the surviving weights by a finite type gives `localFamily`. Its interpretation in global functions reconstructs the actual base change of the original representation, as verified on every test scheme by the evaluated weight sum.

This internal construction produces the positive weight families needed for an ordered affine projector chart, while allowing weight support to vary over the original base.
