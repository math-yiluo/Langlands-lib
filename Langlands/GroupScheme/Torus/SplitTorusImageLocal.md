# Local closed factorizations of maps from split tori

A homomorphism from a split torus to an affine group scheme of locally finite type over an affine base admits a closed image factorization over a basic open neighborhood of every prime. The basic open is defined by an idempotent. The intermediate group is a split torus and the projection to it is flat and surjective.

Use the group algebra of the split character lattice and the actual affine coordinate Hopf algebra of the target. The clopen local group-algebra factorization identifies the localized map as a surjection onto the group algebra of a subgroup, followed by its standard inclusion. Hopf spectrum takes the surjection to a closed subgroup inclusion. The subgroup is finite free by the submodule basis theorem over the integers, so its Hopf spectrum is a split torus. The projection is faithfully flat by the coset basis of a group algebra over a subgroup algebra. The base-change comparison for Hopf spectrum and the scalar extension isomorphism for group algebras identify the composite with the prescribed pulled-back homomorphism.

This internal local form of the torus image argument supplies the split charts for the arbitrary-base factorization.
