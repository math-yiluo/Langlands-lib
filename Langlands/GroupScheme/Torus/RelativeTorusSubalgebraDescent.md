# Relative tori over finitely generated intermediate algebras

Author: Worker 668.

Let \(R\) be a noetherian ring, \(A\) an \(R\)-algebra, and \(G\) a smooth
relatively affine group over \(\operatorname{Spec} R\). If the actual pullback
over \(\operatorname{Spec} A\) is a relative torus, then it is already a
relative torus over the spectrum of some finitely generated
\(R\)-subalgebra of \(A\).

First descend the condition that the geometric connectedness locus is the
whole base. That locus is constructible and commutes with base change.
Over the resulting noetherian coefficient ring, each point in the image
of \(\operatorname{Spec} A\) has a torus fibre, by reflection over its residue
field extension. The Library's torus fibre spreading theorem gives an open
neighbourhood of each such point on which the group is a relative torus.
Their union is constructible by noetherianity. The inverse image of this
union becomes the whole spectrum over a further finitely generated
coefficient ring. The inverse images of the individual opens cover that
spectrum, so locality of the relative torus property gives the result.
Transitivity of finite type and the actual pullback comparisons give the
claimed subalgebra over \(R\) and the interpretation of the group there.

This is an internal consequence of the Library's constructible fibre-locus
and torus spreading theorems. It supplies the torus condition in finite
coefficient models of closed torus-subgroup families for Task 7705.
