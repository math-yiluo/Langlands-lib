# Fixed signs and norms on a character lattice

A complex point of a split torus can be written as a homomorphism from its free
abelian character lattice to the complex units. If the lattice has an involution
and the homomorphism intertwines that involution with complex conjugation, the
homomorphism is a product of two terms. The first evaluates an invariant integral
linear form at `-1`. The second is the norm of a complex point, given on a
character by multiplying its evaluation with the conjugate evaluation at the
involuted character.

The proof chooses logarithms on an integral basis and extends them additively.
The difference of the logarithm and its conjugate under the two involutions has
exponential `1`. Its coefficient in the period lattice `2πi ℤ` is an integral
additive map. Applying the two involutions to the difference proves invariance
of this map. Exponentiating half the additive logarithm gives the complex point
whose norm yields the second factor.

This is an internal character-lattice lemma for the real-place step in torus
approximation. Its proof uses the complex exponential and its period lattice.
