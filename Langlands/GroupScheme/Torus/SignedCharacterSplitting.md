# Splitting tori with signed absolute character action

`SignedCharacterSplittingStatement` concerns a torus whose absolute Galois action on its entire integral character lattice is by the identity or simultaneous negation. Such a torus is already split or splits over a quadratic Galois extension.

Choose a finite Galois splitting field inside the separable closure. The canonical extension of the split character lattice is injective and intertwines the finite and absolute actions. Since every finite Galois element has a lift, its action is also the identity or simultaneous negation. The kernel therefore has index at most two. Over its fixed field the character action is trivial, so the split torus descends. The degree-one case gives splitting over the original field.

This finite-stage argument supplies the quadratic splitting step for the invariant-root subgroup in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Theorem 6.26, printed pp. 353–354. [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
