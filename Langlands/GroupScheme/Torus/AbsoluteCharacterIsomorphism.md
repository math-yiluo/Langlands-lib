# Recovering group isomorphisms from absolute characters

An equivariant integral linear equivalence between the actual character groups
over a separable closure gives an isomorphism in the reverse direction between
the original groups of multiplicative type. Bundle the equivalence as an
isomorphism of representations. Full faithfulness lifts it to finitely
generated discrete Galois modules and then through the absolute character
functor. Taking the opposite gives the required isomorphism over the
original field.

For a diagonalizable group the evaluation characters of its integral
parameters exhaust the absolute character group. Scalar extension identifies
these characters with the evaluations on the base-changed group, so every
one is fixed by the absolute Galois group. If a group of multiplicative type
has characters identified with a finitely generated abelian group and every
character is fixed, the same construction identifies it with the
diagonalizable group of those parameters.

This is an internal interface to the published absolute character
anti-equivalence for comparing explicit center models.
