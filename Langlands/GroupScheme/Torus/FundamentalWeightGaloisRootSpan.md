# Star-fixed fundamental weights and Galois-stable integral lattices

This is an internal lattice construction for the kernel-invariance step
in type-D vector-cover descent. It uses the published integral
fundamental-weight basis and the existing comparison between the star
action and ordinary Galois action. It does not construct a group cover
or identify its kernel.

Let `P` be a root datum on lattices `X` and `Y`, let `B` be a supplied
base, and assume that the integral coroot span is all of `Y`. Then
`Langlands.RootDatum.fundamentalWeightRootSpan P B hspan v` is the
submodule of `X` generated by the integral root lattice and the
fundamental weight at the simple vertex `v`. The fundamental weight
is the actual character dual to the simple-coroot basis, using
`Langlands.RootDatum.fundamentalWeightBasis`.

The supporting lemmas establish the following facts.

- A Weyl automorphism moves every weight by an element of the integral
  root lattice. The proof passes from the full root-datum Weyl group
  to its reflection-group image and uses the existing theorem
  `DominantTriangular.sub_mem_rootSpan_of_mem_reflectionGroup`.
- Two root-datum automorphisms differing by a left Weyl factor induce
  the same map modulo the integral root lattice.
- Every full root-datum automorphism preserves the root lattice.
- A based automorphism permutes the actual fundamental weights by its
  permutation of the supplied simple vertices. In particular it fixes
  the fundamental weight of any fixed vertex. The proof uses the
  perfect lattice pairing, the contravariant coweight map, and the
  existing `fundamentalWeightBasis_permutation` theorem.
- If an automorphism fixes a fundamental weight modulo the root
  lattice, it preserves the lattice generated by the roots and that
  weight. Both inclusions follow by adding or subtracting the known
  difference in the root lattice.

For an actual `GaloisPinningData k K G`, assume that `G` is affine and
locally of finite type and that the pinning's integral coroot span is
the full cocharacter lattice. The declarations use the root datum and
base in `d.toSplitPinning`, which are the root datum and base supplied
by `d`. The theorem
`galoisAction_fundamentalWeight_sub_mem_rootSpan` says that if the star
action of `σ : K ≃ₐ[k] K` fixes `v`, then the ordinary Galois action
moves its fundamental weight by an element of the integral root
lattice. Indeed the star automorphism fixes that weight, and
`starAction_mul_galoisAction_inv_mem` supplies the required Weyl
correction. Negating their difference gives the stated direction.
The theorem `galoisAction_map_fundamentalWeightRootSpan` consequently
proves equality of the image of the generated lattice with itself.

These results hold for any field extension carrying the supplied
Galois pinning data; no Galois, finite-degree or characteristic
assumption on the extension is added. The coroot-span hypothesis is
explicit. There is no restriction on the Dynkin type or the choice of
vertex. Applying the lattice to a full subgroup kernel still requires
a separate comparison identifying that kernel with its character
annihilator. This unit makes no field-point test or reducedness
assumption and makes no claim that lattice stability alone has already
proved full subgroup invariance.
