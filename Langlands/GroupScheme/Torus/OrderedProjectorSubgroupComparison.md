# Relabelling the ordered projector subgroups

A matching of the two weight sets induces a diagonalizable group isomorphism by character restriction. On every test scheme the restriction has the action on characters prescribed by the matching. Once the projectors agree after relabelling, each unit scalar acts on the same matrix summand and the two sums agree. The all-test subgroup ranges therefore agree after relabelling.

Over a nonzero affine test ring the converse assumes that both weight maps are injective and fixes an equivalence of the character groups. The affine comparison identifies the homomorphisms with the group-algebra representations. Character restriction commutes with these comparisons and replaces each weight by its image under the character group equivalence. Equality of representations yields equality of their universal matrices. Injectivity of the two weight maps lets coefficient recovery identify the labels as well as the projector entries. The global functions isomorphism transports the projector equality back to the test scheme.

This internal comparison relates the algebraic weight matchings to equality of the actual subgroup families.
