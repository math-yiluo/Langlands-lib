# Geometric surjectivity of the scalar Clifford projection

For a quadratic form over a field with `2` invertible, `coefficientLiftEquiv` identifies the tensor-unit and matrix subgroup with the Clifford lift group over each commutative coefficient algebra.

For a regular scalar quadratic form of positive dimension over a characteristic-zero field with `2` invertible, the affine Clifford projection to the actual norm-one isometry group lifts every point over every algebraically closed coefficient field. The scalar extension preserves the regularity of the polar form by the Gram-form comparison. The column action of a target point is a determinant-one isometry and has an even Clifford lift after normalization by a square root in that field.

This also proves the underlying scheme map surjective. Over a scheme point, apply the field lifting to an algebraic closure of its residue field. The preceding argument supplies the surjectivity in the spin covering sequence discussed by Kneser, *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups* (1969), §5.11, p. 99.
