# Affine coefficient equations for scalar Clifford spin lifts

Let `Q` be a quadratic form over a field with `2` invertible, and fix a finite basis of its vector space. For each commutative coefficient algebra, the Clifford base-change equivalence and the basis matrix action identify spin lifts with pairs of a Clifford tensor unit and an invertible matrix. The subgroup consists of pairs with trivial grading and reversion defects, determinant one, and zero intertwining defect on each basis vector. The intertwining equality on the basis implies the corresponding linear equality on the entire space. The three tensor defects commute with extension of coefficients.

These are the coordinate equations of the Clifford covering described by the spinor-norm exact sequence of Kneser, *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups* (1969), §5.11, p. 99.
