# Affine families of products of two reflections

For a positive-dimensional scalar quadratic form over a characteristic-zero field with two invertible, `exists_reflectionPair_family` takes two vectors of nonzero value over an extension field. It gives an actual norm-one isometry point over an integral domain with two specialization maps to that field. The first specialization is the identity and the second has the prescribed two-reflection matrix action.

The family fixes the first reflection vector and interpolates linearly between the two vectors for the second. Localizing the polynomial ring at the value of the interpolating vector makes that value a unit. Both evaluations survive the localization, since both endpoint values are nonzero. The two unit-valued reflections preserve the extended form and their determinants multiply to one. At zero the reflections coincide, so their product is the identity. The coefficient change theorem gives the product at one.

This is a derived construction for the reflection products in Kneser, *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups*, §5.11, p. 99.
