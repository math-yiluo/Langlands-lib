# Connectedness of scalar norm-one orthogonal groups

`ScalarNormOneConnectednessStatement` asserts that the actual norm-one isometry group of a regular scalar quadratic form of positive dimension over a characteristic-zero field with two invertible is connected. The theorem `connectedSpace_scalarNormOne` establishes it.

For every pair of nonisotropic vectors over an extension field, the two-reflection product is the specialization of a point over a domain whose other specialization is the identity. Its connected parameter scheme maps into the identity component. The inclusion of that component is a group homomorphism, so every product of an even number of reflections belongs to its image on field-valued points. Cartan–Dieudonné writes each geometric norm-one point as such a product. Surjectivity of the inclusion on algebraically closed points implies surjectivity on the underlying space.

The argument uses the reflection products in Kneser, *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups*, §5.11, p. 99, with the connected families built from unit-valued reflection matrices.
