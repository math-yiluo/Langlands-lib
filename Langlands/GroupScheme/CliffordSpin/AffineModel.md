# The affine scheme of scalar Clifford spin lifts

Let `Q` be a quadratic form over a field with `2` invertible and with a chosen basis indexed by `Fin n`. The tensor product of the Clifford-unit coordinate algebra and the general-linear coordinate algebra corepresents pairs of a Clifford tensor unit and an invertible matrix. Quotient by the coefficients of the universal grading, reversion and vector-intertwining defects and by the determinant-minus-one coordinate. The resulting affine group scheme is a closed subgroup of the ambient pair group. Its affine points over every commutative coefficient algebra are the Clifford lift pairs with the stated equations, and its matrix projection is their vector action in the chosen basis.

This is the affine realization of the Clifford equations used for the scalar spin covering, whose spinor-norm sequence is discussed in Kneser, *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups* (1969), §5.11, p. 99.
