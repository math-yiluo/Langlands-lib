# Polynomial coordinates on the upper unitriangular group scheme

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.UnitriangularUnipotent.Coordinates`. It imports
`Langlands.GroupScheme.CorepresentedClosedSubgroup`,
`Langlands.GroupScheme.AffineLinearEmbedding.Coordinate` and
`Langlands.GroupScheme.UnitriangularUnipotent.WeightCore`.
This module identifies the coordinate ring of the concrete upper unitriangular group
scheme with the polynomial ring `MvPolynomial (Pos N) k` in the entries `x_ij`
with `i < j`. The final proof in `Langlands.GroupScheme.UnitriangularUnipotentProof`
uses that identification.

## Provenance

Milne (2017), *Algebraic Groups*, proof of Theorem 14.5 (b)⇒(c), printed p. 281
(PDF p. 297), uses `O(U_n) ≅ k[X_ij | i < j]`. That book is registered as source
`milne-2017-algebraic-groups`. This module proves that identification for the Library's
concrete scheme `GeneralLinear.blockUnipotentScheme (Fin N) (CommRingCat.of k)
(fun i => -(i.val : ℤ))`. Its lemmas are derived implementation lemmas and do not
appear separately in the literature.

## Matrix lemmas for `upperTri`

`upperTri v` comes from `WeightCore`. It is the matrix with `v p` at each position
`p` above the diagonal, `1` on the diagonal and `0` below it.

- `upperTri_apply_of_not_lt`: on and below the diagonal, `upperTri v i j` is the
  entry of the identity matrix.
- `isUpperTriangular_upperTri` and `det_upperTri`: `upperTri v` is upper triangular
  and has determinant `1`. The determinant is the product of the diagonal entries
  (`Matrix.det_of_isUpperTriangular`).
- `upperTriGL v`: `upperTri v` as an element of `GL (Fin N) S`. It is invertible
  because its determinant is `1`. `upperTriGL_val` says that its underlying matrix is
  `upperTri v`.
- `map_upperTri`: a ring homomorphism `ψ` applied entrywise gives
  `ψ (upperTri v i j) = upperTri (ψ ∘ v) i j`.

## The coordinate ring

- `weights N i = -i`: the weights in the target statement.
- `cutIdeal k N`: the ideal `entryIdeal (Fin N) k (fun i j => weights N i ≤ weights N j)
  (fun i j => δ_ij)` of the coordinate ring of `GL_N`. This is the ideal used by
  `GeneralLinear.isCutOutBy_blockUnipotent`.
- `corep k N`: the corepresentation
  `Corepresented.cutCorepresentableBy (isCutOutBy_blockUnipotent (weights N))`. The
  scheme `blockUnipotentScheme` is by definition the corepresented group scheme of
  this corepresentation.
- `Coord k N`: the quotient ring `CoordinateRing (Fin N) k ⧸ cutIdeal k N`, the
  corepresenting object.
- `u i j`: the image in `Coord k N` of the entry function `x_ij`.

`weights_le_iff` says that `weights N i ≤ weights N j` holds exactly when `j ≤ i`.
So `cutIdeal` is generated by the functions `x_ij - δ_ij` with `j ≤ i`. Hence
`u_of_not_lt`: on and below the diagonal, `u i j` is the entry `δ_ij` of the
identity matrix. As a consequence, `upperTri_u` says that the upper unitriangular
matrix built from the entries `u i j` with `i < j` has `u i j` at every position.

`hom_ext`: two `k`-algebra maps out of `Coord k N` that agree on every `u i j` are
equal. They agree after composing with the quotient map
(`Ideal.Quotient.algHom_ext`), because the Library equivalence
`GeneralLinear.homEquiv` sends a map out of the coordinate ring of `GL_N` to the
invertible matrix of the images of the entry functions, and is injective.

## The two inverse maps

- `theta k N : MvPolynomial (Pos N) k →ₐ[k] Coord k N` is `aeval` sending the
  variable `X p`, `p = (i, j)`, to `u i j` (`theta_X`).
- `chi k N : Coord k N →ₐ[k] MvPolynomial (Pos N) k` is induced on the quotient by
  `GeneralLinear.classify (upperTriGL X)`. That map sends the entry function `x_ij`
  to `upperTri X i j`. It kills `cutIdeal` by `GeneralLinear.classify_eq_zero_iff`,
  because `upperTri X` has the entries `δ_ij` at all positions with `j ≤ i`. So
  `chi_u` says `chi k N (u i j) = upperTri X i j`.

`chi_comp_theta` (and `chi_theta`) say that `chi ∘ theta` is the identity. It is
checked on variables with `MvPolynomial.algHom_ext`: `X p ↦ u i j ↦ upperTri X i j
= X p` for `i < j`. `theta_comp_chi` (and `theta_chi`) say that `theta ∘ chi` is
the identity. It is checked on the entry functions with `hom_ext`, using
`theta_upperTri`: `theta (upperTri X i j) = u i j`, by `theta_X` above the diagonal
and by `u_of_not_lt` on and below it.

## Points

`pt_apply`: for a `k`-algebra map `f : Coord k N →ₐ[k] A`, the matrix of the point
`AffineLinearEmbedding.pt (corep k N) f` of the unitriangular group functor has
`(i, j)` entry `f (u i j)`. This holds by definition (`rfl`). The point is the
invertible matrix classified by `f` composed with the quotient map.
