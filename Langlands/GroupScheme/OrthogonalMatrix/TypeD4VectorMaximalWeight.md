# The maximal vector weight of a split D₄ cover

For a supplied simply connected central cover of split `SO₈` over a field,
with a split pinning and a D₄ numbering aligned with its kernel at vertex
zero, the pulled-back dual vector representation has the vector fundamental
weight as a maximal weight of multiplicity one. The chosen integral
fundamental weight uses full coroot span in the pinned lattice.

The numbered vector basis describes the dual representation's torus weights
as the eight signed vectors. The simple-root difference and sum equations
identify the positive vector numbered zero with the specified fundamental
weight. Pairing with the integral cocharacter whose simple-coroot
coefficients are `(16, 24, 13, 15)` takes values `(16, 8, 4, 2)` on the four
positive vector weights. It is positive on each simple root. Hence the one
coordinate of value `16` gives the unique basis vector of that weight and
certifies maximality for the positive-root order.

Tits, *Représentations linéaires irréductibles d'un groupe réductif sur un
corps quelconque* (1971), §6.2, printed p. 212, identifies the vector
weight at the endpoint opposite the fork of the type-D diagram. The
calculation here uses the supplied split torus and the kernel alignment on
all relative test schemes.
