# Numbered type-D base on the actual split orthogonal torus

This is an internal construction from the published split orthogonal root formulas, actual root-datum existence, and the elementary integral calculations in `Langlands.LinearAlgebra.TypeDNumberedVectors` and `Langlands.LinearAlgebra.TypeDNumberedLinear`. It introduces no classification or spin-cover premise.

Let `k` be any field and let `P` be any actual root datum of `OrthogonalMatrix.identityTorus (Fin n) k`. For `n ≥ 3`, `TypeDNumbering.base P hP hn` constructs an integral root-datum base, and `TypeDNumbering.numbering P hP hn` identifies its support with `Fin n`. The simple root and coroot numbered `i` both have coordinates `e_i - e_(i+1)` when `i+1 < n`; the final root and coroot have coordinates `e_(n-2) + e_(n-1)`. The character and cocharacter coordinates are the actual lattice equivalences `GeneralLinear.characterCoordEquiv` and `GeneralLinear.cocharacterCoordEquiv`.

`simple_mem_roots` first checks these vectors against the actual orthogonal root formula. `simpleIndex` chooses their indices in `P`; `root_simpleIndex` and `rootCoord_simpleIndex` record the choice. For every root index, `corootCoord_eq_rootCoord` uses the published orthogonal coroot formula to identify the root and coroot coordinates when `n ≥ 3`. Thus `corootCoord_simpleIndex` gives the required simple coroots as well.

The independence proofs transport integral linear independence of the coordinate vectors through the two lattice equivalences. `simpleIndex_injective` then makes their image a finite support with no repetitions. For the monoid conditions in the definition of a base, `rootCoord_mem_or_neg_mem` writes each actual root as a signed pair of distinct coordinate vectors and applies the elementary positive-cone calculation. The private transport lemma pulls membership in this coordinate cone back through a lattice equivalence. The coroot condition follows from equality of root and coroot coordinates. These arguments construct `P.Base` directly over the integers; they do not claim that the simple vectors form a basis of the entire orthogonal character lattice.

`rootCoord_numbering` and `corootCoord_numbering` state the coordinate formulas for every member of the resulting support. For `n ≥ 4`, `pairing_simpleIndex` and `pairing_numbering` compute the actual lattice pairing and identify it with `CartanMatrix.D n` in precisely this order. In particular, at `n = 4`, vertex 0 is the first chain vector `e_0 - e_1`, while vertex 1 is the branch vertex. There is no relabeling by a diagram automorphism.

Finally, `exists_numberedRootDatum` combines actual root-datum existence for the connected reductive orthogonal identity component with this construction. It provides a finite reduced actual root datum, an integral base, its numbering, and all three pairing/root/coroot coordinate formulas for `n ≥ 4`. The field is arbitrary, including characteristic two. This unit supplies the numbered orthogonal base for a later cover comparison; it constructs no isogeny or spin cover and does not establish the host aligned-spin-cover theorem.
