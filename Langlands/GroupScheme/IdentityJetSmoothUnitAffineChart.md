# Closed charts for fixed-rank smoothness along the unit

Consider a separated locally finitely presented group over an affine locally
noetherian base with an affine neighborhood of the whole unit. On a basic
base open where specified augmentation elements span the conormal, the
coefficient ideal of their polynomial jet maps defines a closed subscheme.
`SmoothUnitRankTest G r g` is fixed-rank smoothness at the unit of the actual
pullback of `G` along `g`. On this basic open, factorization through the
closed subscheme on any test is equivalent to that predicate.

On an affine test the two successive scalar extensions of the neighborhood
algebra compare by tensor cancellation, preserving the augmentation. The
coefficient ideal criterion then applies. Factorization and the unit
condition descend from affine open covers of general tests. The chart
implements the local description in SGA 3, Exposé XV, Lemma 5.4.
