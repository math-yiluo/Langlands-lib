# Congruence neighborhoods without compactness

This is an internal derived unit. It combines the published coordinate and
congruence APIs of `IntegralModel`, the evaluation topology of affine points,
the spectral-norm topology of the actual inertia fixed field, and the proved
maximal-unramified arithmetic. It makes no additional source-backed claim.

For a domain `R`, its specified fraction field `K`, and an actual integral
model `M` of an affine group scheme, the definition `congruenceKernelFilter` is the infimum, over positive
integers `n`, of the principal filters of the actual subgroups
`M.congruenceKernelAt n`. It needs no topology on either ring. The general
results are:

- If `R` is local and a topological ring whose positive maximal-ideal powers
  are cofinal among neighborhoods of zero,
  `congruenceKernelAt_cofinal_of_maximalIdealPowers` proves that every
  neighborhood of the identity in the canonical topology of
  `M.integralSections` contains an actual positive reduction kernel.
- If `K` is also a topological ring and `algebraMap R K` is continuous,
  `congruenceSubgroupAt_cofinal_of_maximalIdealPowers` proves the corresponding
  containment for `M.congruenceSubgroupAt n` in the original generic group,
  with its canonical rational-point topology.
- `continuous_coordinateAlgHom_apply_generic` proves continuity of each
  original-model coordinate on the original generic points.
  `isOpen_integralPoints_of_isOpen_range` then proves that the actual image
  `M.integralPoints` is open whenever the image of `algebraMap R K` is open.
  These continuity and openness results require only a topology on `K`,
  with no topological-ring axiom on it and no topology or local-ring
  hypothesis on `R`.

The section cofinality proof uses `M.congruenceKernelFilter`. Antitonicity of the kernels makes these sets a directed
filter basis. A coordinate difference between a section and the identity
belongs to the maximal-ideal power whenever the section belongs to that
kernel. The assumed cofinality at zero therefore makes every coordinate
converge to its identity value. The evaluation embedding and the canonical
homeomorphisms of section spaces turn this into convergence to the identity
in the section topology. The filter basis extracts a single kernel contained
in the given neighborhood. Continuity of the actual generic-point map
transports the containment to its image subgroup.

For openness, finite type supplies finitely many generators of the original
model coordinate algebra. The published integrality criterion identifies
the original integral-point image with the finite intersection of inverse
images of the coefficient-ring image under these continuous coordinates.
Each such inverse image is open under the stated hypothesis.

For a nonarchimedean local field `F`, let `K = inertiaFixedField F` and
`R = fixedFieldIntegers F`. The theorem
`fixedFieldIntegers_maximalIdealPowers_cofinal` equips `K` with
`fixedFieldNormedField F` and `R` with its induced normed-ring topology.
The proved arithmetic supplies the actual discrete valuation ring
structure. Choose an irreducible uniformizer `π`; the maximal ideal is
generated by `π`. Membership in the valuation maximal ideal gives
`‖π‖ < 1`, and every `s : R` has `‖s‖ ≤ 1`. Thus an element of the
`(n + 1)`st ideal power, written `π^(n + 1) * s`, has norm bounded by
`‖π‖^(n + 1)`, and these bounds become arbitrarily small.

`congruenceSubgroupAt_cofinal_unramified` specializes the generic containment
to these actual `R` and `K`. `isOpen_integralPoints_unramified` specializes
openness: the coefficient-ring image is the closed unit ball, which is open
for the ultrametric spectral norm. Both conclusions explicitly use the
published `fixedFieldTopology F` and its canonical rational-point topology,
by the equality with the spectral-norm topology.

All fields and rings retain arbitrary universe `u`. These results use no
compactness of `R`, no finiteness of its residue field, and no completeness
of `K`. They do not assert that a model exists or identify its integral
points with a building fixer or a zero subgroup. The host unramified
parahoric-model statement remains a separate proof obligation.
