# Closed subgroups representing generated rational-point images

Internal derived lemmas for the field step of KP Lemma A.4.26, over an
algebraically closed field, with smooth affine ambient group and smooth affine
connected source groups.
The proof inducts on the finite set of generators. The empty family is represented
by the unit subgroup. At an insertion, apply pointed image generation to the
product of the previous subgroup and the new source group. Each factor maps
into the new closed subgroup by inserting the identity in the other coordinate.
Conversely a dominant finite power and the rational-point open-set argument in
`ConnectedGenerationFieldPoints` show that its entire rational-point image
lies in the subgroup generated by the indicated sources.

`exists_generated_subfamily_points` gives the equality for the supremum over
a specified finite set. `exists_generated_points` specializes to all indices
of a finite family and gives equality with `ConnectedGeneration.generatedImages`.
Both include the empty family.

This adapts the construction in `FiniteGeneration.exists_generated_subfamily`,
retaining the dominant-product information to identify the actual subgroup
of rational points. It implements the finite-family case of the generation
argument in Borel, *Linear Algebraic Groups*, second enlarged edition,
Proposition 2.2; the scheme constructions and geometric conclusions are
supplied by the Library's pointed image generation theorem. The cited source is
`borel-1991-linear-algebraic-groups-second`, SHA-256
