# The field step of KP Lemma A.4.26

`Langlands.GroupScheme.connectedGenerationField.{u}` proves the exact
`Langlands.GroupScheme.ConnectedGenerationFieldStatement.{u}` for arbitrary
universe `u`. Over an algebraically closed field, the subgroup generated by
the rational-point images of a finite family of smooth affine geometrically
connected group schemes equals the rational-point range of every supplied
relative identity-component representative, provided that generated subgroup
has finite index in the smooth affine ambient group's rational points.
The empty family is included.

The proof combines three steps:

1. Geometric connectedness gives connectedness of every source. The internal
   theorem `ConnectedGenerationField.exists_generated_points` constructs a
   smooth affine connected closed subgroup `j : H ⟶ G` whose rational-point
   range equals the subgroup generated by the source images. Its proof,
   in `ConnectedGenerationFieldGeneration`, inducts on the finite family.
   The unit subgroup handles the empty family. Pointed image generation and
   a dominant finite product handle insertion of a new source.
   `ConnectedGenerationFieldPoints` supplies the rational-point argument:
   Chevalley gives a nonempty open subset of the dominant image, and that
   open subset and a translate intersect in a rational point. Closure under
   multiplication and inverse then gives every point of the generated
   algebraic subgroup.
2. Transfer finite index along the equality of subgroups of `G(k)`.
   `ConnectedGeneration.componentRange_le_of_finiteIndex`, implemented in
   `ConnectedGenerationFiniteIndexProof`, shows that the rational-point
   range of any relative identity component is contained in this closed
   subgroup's range. Finitely many closed translates cover all rational
   points, hence all of `G` by density of closed points. The irreducible
   image of the identity component lies in one translate. Since it contains
   the unit, this translate is the subgroup itself on rational points.
3. The reverse containment is the existing
   `ConnectedGeneration.generatedImages_le_componentRange`: geometrically
   connected source images factor through every supplied relative identity
   component. Antisymmetry gives the required equality.

Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023), Lemma A.4.26,
printed p. 632 / PDF p. 663. Source ID:
