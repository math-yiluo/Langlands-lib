# Termination of the smoothening sequence: the integral functions are finitely generated

Author: Worker 348. This module proves `IntegralFunctionsFiniteTypeStatement.{u}` (module
`Langlands.GroupScheme.IntegralModel.IntegralFunctions`, Worker 50).

The statement: let `R` be a Henselian discrete valuation ring with algebraically closed residue
field and fraction field `K`. Let `A` be a flat, finitely generated, commutative Hopf algebra over
`R` with `K ⊗ A` smooth over `K`. Then `B = integralFunctions R K A` is a finitely generated
`R`-algebra.

In Kaletha–Prasad (literature ID `kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`), this
corresponds to the termination Theorem A.6.2. KP refer to BLR, *Néron Models* §3.3 Prop. 5 for its
proof. The route here is internal to this project: the smoothening sequence is realized inside `B`
(`TerminationChain`), and smoothness of the last member is proved without dimension theory
(`TerminationSmooth`).

Namespace: `Langlands.GroupScheme.IntegralModel.Smoothening.Termination`.

## Lemmas

- `smooth_of_torsion_eq_bot_of_le`: Lemma S (`smooth_of_torsion_eq_bot`) with the generic fibre
  `E = K ⊗[R] A` and the point `genericEval R K A (counitAlgHom R A)`.
  - The algebra structure `instC` of `C` and the Hopf structure `inst` are separate arguments,
    related by `hinst : inst.toAlgebra = instC`.
  - The counit is any `ε` agreeing pointwise with the counit of `inst`.
  - All hypotheses are stated for `instC`. For a subalgebra of `B` this is the subalgebra
    structure, and `hinst` holds by `rfl`. The proof substitutes `hinst` and `ε`.
- `eq_top_of_smooth`: let `R` be Henselian with algebraically closed residue field. A subalgebra
  `S ≤ B` containing the image of `A` and smooth over `R` is all of `B`.
  - For `b ∈ B` pick `s ≠ 0` with `s b` in the image of `A` (`exists_smul_eq_one_tmul`).
  - Every `R`-point `χ` of `S` is `point φ` restricted to `S` (`algHom_ext_sub`), so `s` divides
    `χ(s b)`.
  - Worker 50's `exists_eq_smul_of_smooth_algebra` gives `s b = s c'` with `c' ∈ S`, and
    torsion-freeness of `B` gives `b = c' ∈ S`.

## Main theorem

`integralFunctions_finiteType : IntegralFunctionsFiniteTypeStatement.{u}`.

Proof:
- Choose a uniformizer `ϖ`. Let `δ n` be the defect of the cotangent module of `chain R K A ϖ n`
  at the counit of `B`, and `ν n` the length of its `ϖ`-torsion.
- `δ 0` is finite (`cot_finite`, `defect_ne_top`, `chain_fg`).
- `core_step` gives `δ (n+1) + ν n ≤ δ n`, with the hypotheses supplied by `range_incl_le_chain`,
  `comul_mem_pureSpan`, `antipodeInt_mem` and `chain_fg`.
- `torsionByLength_ne_zero` gives `ν n ≠ 0` while `δ n ≠ 0`.
- `exists_eq_zero_of_drop` gives `N` with `δ N = 0`, so the cotangent module of `chain N` is
  torsion-free.
- `smooth_of_torsion_eq_bot_of_le` with `chainHopf` and the inclusion `chain N ≤ B ≤ K ⊗ A` makes
  `chain N` smooth.
- `eq_top_of_smooth` gives `chain N = B`, which is finitely generated by `chain_fg`.

The theorem depends only on the axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.
