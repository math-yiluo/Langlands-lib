# Termination of the smoothening sequence: the sequence inside the integral functions

Author: Worker 348. Internal helper module for Kaletha–Prasad, Theorem A.6.2
(literature ID `kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`). The source iterates dilatations of
schemes (Construction A.6.1). This module realizes the same tower as an increasing sequence of
subalgebras of `B = integralFunctions R K A`. The ring form is internal to this project.

Namespace: `Langlands.GroupScheme.IntegralModel.Smoothening.Termination`.

Setting: `R` is a discrete valuation ring with fraction field `K`, and `A` is a commutative Hopf
algebra over `R`. Here `incl R K A : A → B`, `point φ : B → R` (for an `R`-point `φ` of `A`) and
`comulInt`, `antipodeInt` come from Worker 50's modules `Smoothening.IntegralFunctionsBasic` and
`Smoothening.Hopf`.

## The sequence

- `jIdeal R K A C` is the ideal of a subalgebra `C ≤ B` consisting of the `c` with
  `point φ c ∈ 𝔪` for every `R`-point `φ` of `A` (`mem_jIdeal`).
- `chain R K A ϖ n`: `chain 0` is the image of `A` (`chain_zero`), and
  `chain (n+1) = dilat ϖ (jIdeal (chain n))`, the subalgebra generated by `chain n` and the
  quotients `j / ϖ` with `j ∈ jIdeal (chain n)` (`chain_succ`, with `dilat` from
  `TerminationDilatation`).
- `chain_le_succ`, `chain_mono`, `range_incl_le_chain`: the sequence increases and contains `A`.
- `mem_chain_succ_of_smul_mem`: if `b ∈ B` and `ϖ b ∈ chain n`, then `b ∈ chain (n+1)`. Indeed
  `ϖ b` has values in `ϖ R`, so it lies in `jIdeal`.
- `mem_chain_add_of_pow_smul_mem` and `exists_mem_chain`: every `b ∈ B` has `ϖ^m b ∈ A` for some
  `m`, so `b ∈ chain m`. Hence `B` is the union of the sequence.

## Comultiplication and antipode

- `lift_point_comp_comul`: the comultiplication of `B` followed by `point φ ⊗ point ψ` is the point
  `(φ ⊗ ψ) ∘ Δ_A`.
- `pairValue φ ψ` is the value map `B ⊗ B → R`, and `pairValue_comul_mem`: for `j ∈ jIdeal C`,
  `pairValue φ ψ (Δ j) ∈ 𝔪`.
- `pureSpan R K A S T` is the `R`-span of the pure tensors `s ⊗ t` (`s ∈ S`, `t ∈ T`) in `B ⊗ B`.
  The lemmas `tmul_mem_pureSpan` and `pureSpan_mono` are its basic properties.
- `jSet C` is the image of `jIdeal C` in `B`. `mem_sup_of_pairValue`: an element of the span of
  `C ⊗ C` whose pair values all lie in `𝔪` lies in `pureSpan C (jSet C) + pureSpan (jSet C) C`.
  This uses the tensor density `mem_sup_of_productForm` of `TerminationTensor`.
- `mul_mem_pureSpan`: `pureSpan C C` is closed under multiplication.
- `sup_le_map_smul`: `pureSpan C (jSet C) + pureSpan (jSet C) C` is contained in the image under
  multiplication by `ϖ` of `pureSpan D D`, where `D = dilat ϖ (jIdeal C)`. Each `j ∈ jSet C` is
  `ϖ b` with `b ∈ D` (`exists_eq_smul_of_dvd`).
- `comul_mem_pureSpan`: the comultiplication of `B` maps `chain n` into
  `pureSpan (chain n) (chain n)`. The proof is by induction. For a generator `j / ϖ`, the element
  `Δ j` has all pair values in `𝔪`, so `Δ j` lies in `ϖ · pureSpan (chain (n+1)) (chain (n+1))`.
  Divide by `ϖ`, which is possible because `B ⊗ B` is torsion-free.
- `point_comp_antipodeInt` and `antipodeInt_mem`: the antipode of `B` composed with `point φ` is
  `point (φ ∘ S_A)`. Hence it preserves `jIdeal` and every `chain n`.
