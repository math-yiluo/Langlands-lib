# Positive congruence kernels through an open model inclusion

This is an internal derived construction at arbitrary universe `u`. Let `R` be a commutative local domain with fraction field `K`, and let `N` and `M` be supplied actual integral group models, with a compatible model morphism `φ : N.Hom M f`. The construction assumes that the underlying scheme morphism of `φ` is an open immersion. The generic groups, their chosen identifications, the original integral section groups and the actual quotient maps by positive powers of the maximal ideal remain fixed. No local-field topology, smoothness, connectedness, reductivity or characteristic assumption is used.

`Hom.congruenceKernelMap` is the restriction of the original integral section homomorphism to the actual positive-level kernels. `Hom.openCongruenceKernelEquiv` makes this same map a group equivalence for every `n : ℕ+`.

The proof first identifies equality after base change with equality of the underlying restricted scheme maps. Since an open immersion is a monomorphism, the model inclusion reflects congruence at every level. A target section congruent to the identity at a positive level is also congruent modulo the maximal ideal. Its closed-point image therefore equals that of the identity section, which belongs to the open subgroup. Every point of the spectrum of a local ring specializes to its closed point, so the section's whole image lies in the open subgroup. The actual open-immersion lift supplies a unique source section, and reflection of congruence puts it in the same positive-level kernel. Restriction, injectivity and surjectivity give the displayed group equivalence.

When `f` is the identity of one supplied generic group, naturality of the original integral-point maps proves `Hom.congruenceSubgroupAt_eq_of_open`: the two models give literally equal congruence subgroups of the original generic rational-point group, at every positive level. Applying this to `RelativeComponentModelData.inclusion` gives `neutral_congruenceSubgroupAt_eq` for the supplied full and neutral KP model data, including a disconnected full special fibre.

All assertions are derived from the published actual congruence tower, compatible model-map laws, the supplied relative-component open immersion and Mathlib's open-immersion lifting and local-spectrum specialization theorems. No external-literature assertion is introduced. This unit supplies the passage from full-model to neutral-model positive kernels. It constructs neither an integral root-product open cell nor its root-filtration compatibility, and proves no simultaneous Iwahori basis.
