# Actual Weil-restricted binary norm-one images and elementary generation

This is an internal construction for the actual affine Weil restriction of the canonical binary reduced-norm-one group. The fields `F E : Type u` have a specified algebra structure and `E` is finite-dimensional over `F`. Initially `D : Type u` is a finite-dimensional central simple `E`-algebra with a possibly noncommutative ring structure. The universe and characteristic are arbitrary; there is no separability assumption and no `F`-algebra structure on `D` is required. The final full-generation and full-image results assume that `D` is a division ring and retain an explicit coefficient SK1 equality.

The generic helpers in namespace `AffineWeil` work over arbitrary commutative rings `A B : Type u`, with `B` finite projective over `A`, and actual affine locally finite type group schemes `G H` over `Spec B`. `testPointsEquiv_map` identifies the actual restricted map on any relative test `T` with the original map on `tensorTest T`. It specializes `Corepresented.groupSchemeMap_points`. `range_map_le_dynamicUnipotent` transfers containment of an actual homomorphism's range in the dynamic unipotent for an original cocharacter to the range of its actual restriction for the transferred cocharacter. Its proof chooses a range preimage, applies the original all-test containment on the tensor-spectrum test, and uses `mem_dynamicUnipotent_of_testPointsEquiv`. The test `T` is arbitrary, without an affineness assumption.

In namespace `CentralSimpleUnits.BinaryRoot.WeilRoot`, `groupScheme F E D` is the actual `AffineWeil.groupScheme` of `BinaryRoot.groupScheme E D`, the canonical reduced-norm-one group of `Matrix (Fin 2) (Fin 2) D`. For a coefficient basis `bD : Module.Basis (Fin d) E D`, `additiveScheme F E d` is the restriction of the original vector parameter group, and `elementaryMap F E D bD neg` is the actual restricted elementary homomorphism. The convention is `false` for the upper family and `true` for the lower family. Local affineness and finite-type instances for the vector group follow from its polynomial spectrum and finite-type algebra map.

`cocharacter F E D neg` is the actual transferred ground-field cocharacter from the original contracting cocharacter. `elementary_range_le_dynamicUnipotent` applies the generic transfer lemma to the proved original binary contraction. Thus both restricted elementary images contract to the identity on every relative test scheme. This conclusion concerns the actual maps and cocharacters, rather than a proposed equality of dynamic functors.

`rationalElementarySubgroup` is the supremum of the ranges of the two actual rational elementary maps. `map_rationalElementaryRange` proves that the multiplicative equivalence `AffineWeil.rationalPointsEquiv F E (BinaryRoot.groupScheme E D)` carries each range precisely to the original range. One inclusion maps a range witness and uses the point-map naturality theorem. The other applies the inverse additive rational equivalence to an original parameter and uses its inverse identity. `map_rationalElementarySubgroup` then follows from preservation of the supremum by subgroup image.

`rationalElementarySubgroup_eq_top_iff_original` says that full rational generation of the restriction is equivalent to full rational generation of the original norm-one group over `E`. The forward direction maps the full subgroup through the surjective comparison. For the reverse direction, every compared point has an elementary preimage, and injectivity identifies that preimage with the original point. When `D` is a division ring, `rationalElementarySubgroup_eq_top_iff` composes this equivalence with the proved original binary criterion, giving exactly

```text
rationalElementarySubgroup F E D bD = top
  iff (BinaryElementary.Reduced.coefficientNorm E D).ker = commutator Dˣ.
```

The norm on the right is the canonical reduced norm on the original coefficient units over `E`. No arithmetic assertion that its kernel is the commutator subgroup is proved here.

For any connected reductive actual target `G` over `Spec F` and actual homomorphism `f : groupScheme F E D → G`, `map_elementaryPoint_mem_parabolicUnipotentPointSet` puts the image of each original restricted elementary rational point into the actual geometric parabolic-radical generator set. For a division ring `D`, `rationalPoints_range_le_parabolicElementarySubgroup` assumes the displayed coefficient SK1 equality. It applies the preceding elementary-image containment using `Module.finBasis E D`, replaces the source elementary subgroup by the full subgroup using the proved equivalence, and rewrites its image as the range of the original rational map. `rationalPointsToParabolicElementarySubgroup` is that original full rational map with codomain restricted using this containment, and its coercion formula is again reflexive.
