# Connectedness of anti-affine field groups

This internal helper derives connectedness directly from the actual global-functions condition, without finite-type or smoothness hypotheses. A nontrivial clopen partition of a scheme decomposes its global-sections ring as a product of two nonzero rings, using the structure sheaf's disjoint-open product property. A domain has no such product decomposition, so a scheme with a domain of global functions is preconnected. For an anti-affine field group, the structure-map isomorphism identifies the actual global-functions ring with the field; the identity section supplies a point. The result is connectedness of the actual scheme.
