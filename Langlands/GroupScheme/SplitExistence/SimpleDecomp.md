# The decomposition `B = U'_i T U_i`

Author: Worker 1398.

For a positive root `i`, `U'_i` (`posMinusSub`) is generated by the root groups of the positive
roots other than `i`. The commutator relations of the datum show that `x_i(s)` conjugates each
generator of `U'_i` into `U'_i` (`x_conj_mem_posMinusSub`). Apply the `commutator` field with
`h = ℓ`; the resulting list of root exponentials is a product of group elements
(`coe_list_prod_x`). All roots `a i + b j` with `a, b ≥ 1` that occur are positive and differ
from `i`, by evaluating `ℓ`. Hence `U_i` normalizes `U'_i`,
`U⁺ = U'_i ⊔ U_i = U'_i U_i` (Mathlib `coe_mul_of_right_le_normalizer_left`), and every element of
`B` is `u' t x_i(a)` (`exists_borel_decomp`). Internal; Steinberg, *Lectures*, §3 Lemma 17.
