# Subgroups of the Chevalley group

Author: Worker 1398. Internal helper of the proof of the split reductive Existence Theorem.

For Chevalley data `D` and a commutative ring `K`, the module defines these subgroups of
`GL_n(K)`:

- the torus `torusSub`, the image of `torusHom`;
- the root groups `rootSub j`;
- `rootsSub S`, generated by the root groups of a set `S` of roots;
- `posSub = U⁺`, generated by the positive root groups;
- `borelSub = T ⊔ U⁺`;
- `chevSub = ⟨T, all U_j⟩`, the abstract Chevalley group `Γ(K)`.

For a field `K` it also defines `weylSub = ⟨T, n_i⟩`, which uses the Weyl elements `n_i` of
the simple roots (module `Weyl`).

The general group lemmas are `conj_mem_of_mem_closure` and `mem_normalizer_closure`. The
module also proves that the torus normalizes every `rootsSub S`, and the product formula
`coe_borelSub : B = T * U⁺` (Mathlib `Subgroup.coe_mul_of_left_le_normalizer_right`). Except
for `weylSub` and its two lemmas, everything holds over an arbitrary commutative ring. This
lets the subgroups be evaluated on the global sections of a test scheme.
