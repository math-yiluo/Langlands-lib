# Tits axioms for subgroups of an ambient group

Author: Worker 1398.

`IsConcreteTits B N s` states the Tits system axioms (Bourbaki, *Lie* IV §2.1) for subgroups
`B`, `N` of a group `H` and elements `s i ∈ N`, with representatives taken in `H`: `B ⊓ N` is
normalized by `N`; `N` is generated by `B ⊓ N` and the `s i`; `s i² ∈ B`; `s i ∉ B`; for every
representative `n` of `s i`, `m ∈ N` and `b ∈ B`, `n b m ∈ B m B ∪ B (n m) B`; and
`s i B s i⁻¹ ⊄ B`.

`isTitsSystem` turns these into the Library's `Langlands.Group.IsTitsSystem` on the subgroup
`Γ = B ⊔ N`, with Weyl group `W = N / (B ⊓ N)` (the quotient map `π`; `closure_S` shows that
the images of the `s i` generate `W` by induction over the closure). `eq_of_le` then applies the
Library's `IsTitsSystem.exists_eq_standardParabolic` (Bourbaki IV §2.5 Theorem 3): a subgroup `P`
with `B ≤ P ≤ B ⊔ N` containing no `s i` is the standard parabolic `B W_X B` of a set `X` of
simple reflections; every element of `X` would force some `s i` into `P`, so `X = ∅` and `P = B`.
Internal transfer lemma.
