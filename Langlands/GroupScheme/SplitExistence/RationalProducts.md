# Rational points of a group as products of points of a dominant image

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.SplitExistence.RationalProducts`, namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme.SplitExistence`. Internal helper of the proof of the split reductive
Existence Theorem (task 2132924b). It provides the step "every geometric point of the
generated group is a product of points of the generators" used to identify the geometric
points of the Chevalley group with the abstract group generated by the torus and the root
groups. The statement and argument are the classical one (Borel, *Linear Algebraic Groups*,
Proposition 1.3 and §2.2; Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017), Proposition 2.47). The
generic-flatness route is derived content.

## Declarations

- `isOpen_image_of_flatNear f hS h`: let `f : X ⟶ Y` be locally of finite type with `Y`
  locally noetherian, and `S ⊆ X` open such that `f` is flat near every point of `S` (the
  Library predicate `OrbitMapFlat.FlatNear`). Then `f '' S` is open. Near each point of `S`,
  the restriction `f.resLE U V e` is flat and locally of finite presentation, hence an open
  map. The image of `S ∩ V` under it is open in `U`, hence in `Y`.
- `exists_open_subset_range f y`: let `f` be locally of finite type and quasi-compact, and
  dominant onto a reduced, locally noetherian `Y`, and let `y : Y` be a point. Then the image
  of `f` contains a nonempty open set. The Library generic flatness theorem
  `OrbitMapFlat.exists_open_flatNear` gives a nonempty open `U` over which `f` is flat near
  every point. Dominance gives a point of `X` mapping into `U`. The open set is
  `f '' (f ⁻¹' U)`.
- The imported `HomogeneousClosedImmersion.exists_rational_lift p y hy` (`K` algebraically closed): let `p : X ⟶ Y` be a morphism of
  schemes locally of finite type over `Spec K`, and `y` a rational point of `Y` whose
  underlying point lies in the image of `p`. Then some rational point `x` of `X` satisfies
  `x ≫ p = y`. The fibre over the closed point `y` is nonempty and locally closed, so it
  contains a closed point of `X` (Jacobson). Mathlib's `pointOfClosedPoint` turns that point
  into a rational point. Two rational points with the same underlying point are equal. This
  is the argument of the Library's `DominantHom.rational_lift`, for an arbitrary morphism
  instead of a homomorphism.
- `exists_mul_mem_open hO hne g` (`K` algebraically closed): let `G` be a group scheme over
  `Spec K`, locally of finite type with irreducible underlying space, and `O` a nonempty
  open subset. Then every rational point `g` equals `u * v` for rational points `u`, `v`
  whose underlying points lie in `O`. The proof uses `σ = 𝟙⁻¹ * (toUnit ≫ g)`, the morphism
  `x ↦ x⁻¹ g`. It has a right inverse `x ↦ g x⁻¹`, so it is surjective, and `σ⁻¹(O)` is a
  nonempty open set. By irreducibility, `O ∩ σ⁻¹(O)` is nonempty. A closed point `c` of it
  gives the rational point `u`, and `v = u ≫ σ = u⁻¹ g`.
- `extMap K`, `extPoint K`: the base map `Spec K ⟶ Spec k` of a `k`-algebra `K`, and the
  object `Over.mk (extMap K)` of `K`-valued test points over `Spec k`.
- `pullbackPoint K a`: a rational point `a` of the pullback `(Over.pullback (extMap K)).obj W`,
  composed with the first projection, is a `K`-valued point of `W`.
- `baseChangeBasePointsEquiv_comp_map K p a`: the Library comparison
  `baseChangeBasePointsEquiv` sends `a ≫ (Over.pullback (extMap K)).map p` to
  `pullbackPoint K a ≫ p`.
- `exists_mul_of_isDominant K p g` (**main result**): let `k` be any field, and `G` a group
  scheme over `Spec k` that is smooth, affine and geometrically irreducible. Let `W` be
  affine and locally of finite type over `k`, and `p : W ⟶ G.X` a morphism whose underlying
  scheme morphism is dominant. Let `K` be an algebraically closed field with a `k`-algebra
  structure. Then every `K`-valued point `g : extPoint K ⟶ G.X` equals
  `(a ≫ p) * (b ≫ p)` for some `K`-valued points `a`, `b` of `W`. The product is the Library
  pointwise group law on points.

## Proof of the main result

Base change along `β = extMap K`. `G` is geometrically reduced (Library
`SmoothReducedProof.geometricallyReduced_of_smooth`), hence reduced, since `Spec k` is
reduced and noetherian and `G` is flat over it. Therefore the dominant quasi-compact morphism
`p` is schematically dominant. This persists under the flat base change `β` (Library
`EnvelopeBaseChange.isSchemeTheoreticallyDominant_pullback_map`, with flatness from
`fieldExtension_fpqc`), so the base-changed morphism `p'` is dominant. The base-changed group
`G'` is reduced, locally noetherian and of finite type, and it is irreducible because `G` is
geometrically irreducible. Generic flatness supplies a nonempty open `O` in the image of `p'`.
The rational point `g'` of `G'` corresponding to `g` (Library `baseChangeBasePointsEquiv`) is
written as `u * v` with `u`, `v` in `O`. Both lift through `p'` to rational points `a'`, `b'`.
Their images `pullbackPoint K a'`, `pullbackPoint K b'` satisfy the claimed equation, because
`baseChangeBasePointsEquiv` is multiplicative.

No perfection, characteristic or separability hypothesis on `k` is used.
