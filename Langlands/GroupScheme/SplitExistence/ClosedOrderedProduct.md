# Ordered products over a closed set of roots positive for a height

Author: Worker 1620.

Lean module: `Langlands.GroupScheme.SplitExistence.ClosedOrderedProduct`.

## Content

`IsClosedPositive P h Ψ`, for `h : X →+ ℤ` and a set `Ψ` of roots of the root datum `P`, says
two things. First, `h` is positive on `Ψ`. Second, `Ψ` contains every root `k` with
`root k = a • root i + c • root j`, where `a, c ≥ 1` and `i, j ∈ Ψ`.

For Chevalley data `D` over a commutative ring `K` (no field hypothesis):

- `listProd l c = ∏_{j ∈ l} x_j(c j)`, with `listProd_nil`, `listProd_cons`,
  `listProd_append`, `listProd_congr`, `listProd_zero` and `listProd_mem_rootsSub`;
- `coe_list_prod_x_ring` and `commutator_mem_rootsSub_ring`. These are the commutative-ring
  versions of the published field lemmas `coe_list_prod_x` and `commutator_mem_rootsSub`. The
  commutator of `x_i(s)` and `x_j(t)`, for roots on which `h` is positive, lies in `rootsSub S`
  once `S` contains every root `a i + c j` with `a, c ≥ 1`;
- `heightGe P h Ψ m`, the roots of `Ψ` of `h`-level at least `m`. It comes with
  `commutator_mem_heightGe`, `x_conj_mem_heightGe`, `rootsSub_le_normalizer_heightGe`,
  `inv_conj_mem_heightGe` and `level_step_heightGe`;
- `heightList P h Ψ`, the roots of `Ψ` listed once each and sorted by increasing `h`. It comes
  with `mem_heightList`, `heightList_nodup` and `heightList_sorted`;
- `heightOpGe`, `heightOpGe_eq`, `heightOpGe_mem` and `heightOpGe_step`;
- **`exists_listProd_of_mem_rootsSub`**: if `IsClosedPositive P h Ψ` holds, every element of
  `rootsSub K Ψ`, the subgroup of `GL_n(K)` generated by the root groups of `Ψ`, equals
  `listProd (heightList P h Ψ) c` for some `c : J → K`.

## Provenance and proof

This is Steinberg, *Lectures on Chevalley groups*, §3, Lemma 17. Steinberg states it for the
positive roots of a base. The Library's `OrderedProduct` module proves that case,
`exists_orderedProd_of_mem_posSub`, over a field. This module copies that proof word for word,
replacing the positive roots with `Ψ`, the height `D.ℓ` with `h` and the field with a
commutative ring.

The commutator field of `ChevalleyData` is stated for any `h` positive on both roots, so
`commutator_mem_heightGe` works as before. The closure property of `Ψ` puts the roots that can
occur back in `Ψ`, and their level is at least `h(i) + h(j)`. Hence `U_Ψ` normalizes every
`U_{Ψ,≥m}`. The level step and the descending induction on the level are then unchanged.
`List.filter_ge_eq_append` is reused from `OrderedProduct`.
