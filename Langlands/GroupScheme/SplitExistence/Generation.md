# The Chevalley group as a smooth connected closed subgroup of `GL_n`

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.SplitExistence.Generation`, namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme.SplitExistence.ChevalleyData`. Internal helper of the proof of the split
reductive Existence Theorem (task 2132924b). For Chevalley data `D` and any field `k`, it
constructs the group `G ⊆ GL_n` generated by the split torus `D(X)` and all root groups. It also
proves the property used by the Tits-system argument: the points of `G` with values in any
algebraically closed extension `K` of `k` lie in the abstract Chevalley group `Γ(K)`. This is
the classical definition of the Chevalley group as the algebraic subgroup of `GL(V)` generated
by the torus and the root groups (Steinberg, *Lectures on Chevalley Groups*, §5; Borel,
*Linear Algebraic Groups*, §2.2). The stage-by-stage construction is derived content. It
follows the induction in the Library's `FiniteGeneration.exists_generated_subfamily`.

## Declarations

- `chevPoints k T`: the subgroup of the `T`-points of `GL_n` whose matrix, under the Library
  identification `GeneralLinear.pointsEquiv`, lies in `D.chevSub Γ(T, ⊤)`. `mem_chevPoints`
  unfolds it.
- `torus_mem_chevPoints`, `root_mem_chevPoints`: points of the torus homomorphism
  `torusToGL` and of every root homomorphism `rootToGL j` lie in `chevPoints`, on every
  test scheme.
- `Stage k`: a group scheme `H` over `Spec k` with a closed subgroup embedding
  `i : H ⟶ GL_n`, with `H` smooth, affine and connected, and such that for every
  algebraically closed field `K` with a `k`-algebra structure, every `K`-valued point `x` of
  `H` has `x ≫ i ∈ chevPoints`.
- `baseStage k hT`: the torus `D(X)` with `torusToGL`. Here `hT` states that `D(X)` is a split
  torus, which gives smoothness and connectedness (Library `IsSplitTorus.smooth`,
  `IsSplitTorus.connectedSpace`). Closedness is `isClosedSubgroup_torusToGL`.
- `gak_geometry k`: the additive group is smooth, affine and connected.
- `Stage.exists_step s j`: for a stage `s` and a root `j`, there is a stage `s'` such that both
  `s.i` and `rootToGL j` factor through `s'.i`.
- `Stage.exists_steps s l`: the same for a list `l` of roots, by induction.
- `exists_chevalley_stage k hT` (`J` finite): there is a stage through which the torus and all
  root homomorphisms factor.

## Proof of `Stage.exists_step`

Apply the Library's `ImageGeneration.pointedImageGeneration` to
`f : H × 𝔾ₐ → GL_n, (h, s) ↦ i(h) · x_j(s)`, pointed at `(1, 0)`. The source is affine, of
finite type and geometrically integral by `smoothConnectedAffineGeometry`, and `GL_n` is
smooth (`GeneralLinear.smoothStatement`). This gives a generated image `E`: a smooth, affine,
geometrically connected closed subgroup with a dominant ordered power map
`E.powerMap n : (H × 𝔾ₐ)ⁿ → E`. Inserting `1` in either coordinate factors `i` and
`rootToGL j` through `E`.

For the points condition, `E` is geometrically integral (`smoothGeometricIntegrality`), and
`(H × 𝔾ₐ)ⁿ` is affine of finite type (`finitePowerGood_finitePower`). So the main result
`exists_mul_of_isDominant` of `RationalProducts` writes every `K`-valued point of `E` as
`(a ≫ powerMap) * (b ≫ powerMap)`. After composing with the inclusion, each factor is an
ordered product (`powerMap_comp_points`, `orderedPowerMap_points`) of values of `f`. Each value
of `f` is a product of a point of `H` mapped by `i`, which lies in `chevPoints` because `s` is
a stage, and a point of a root group, which lies in `chevPoints` by `root_mem_chevPoints`.

No minimality of the generated group is recorded or needed. No hypothesis on `k` is used.
