# The entire binary upper-covariant coordinate space

Let `k` be any field, let `m` be a natural number, and use the actual upper
triangular subgroup of `SL₂` and its last-diagonal character from
`SpecialLinearBinaryUpper`. The linear map `covariantRowEquiv m` identifies the
full degree-`m` binary homogeneous polynomial module with the entire actual
affine coordinate subspace satisfying left covariance for
`characterCoordinate k ^ m`. Its forward map is the existing bottom-row
polynomial map `covariantRowMap k m`. No infinitude or characteristic assumption
on the field is needed; degree zero is included.

The proof starts with the two chart restrictions in
`SpecialLinearBinaryCharts`. The determinant equation puts `1` in the ideal
generated by the two bottom-row entries. The localization maps away from these
entries are therefore jointly injective. The reconstruction formula in each
chart shows that a covariant coordinate whose two restrictions vanish is zero.
This argument applies the chart formulas over the localization algebras, with
no assumption that the determinant-one coordinate ring is a domain or that
test algebras are reduced.

If `P` and `Q` are the lower and complementary chart restrictions of a covariant
coordinate, the overlap formula is `P(v) = v^m Q(v⁻¹)` for every unit `v` in every
commutative `k`-algebra. Apply this over the fraction field of `k[X]` at its
nonzero indeterminate. The usual polynomial reversal identity and injectivity
of the fraction-field map give the polynomial identity
`X ^ Q.natDegree * P = X ^ m * Q.reverse`. If `P` is nonzero, taking degrees
and using `Q.reverse.natDegree ≤ Q.natDegree` proves `P.natDegree ≤ m`; the
zero case also satisfies the bound. If `P` is zero, the same identity forces
`Q.reverse`, and hence `Q`, to be zero. Joint injectivity then shows that lower
restriction alone determines the entire covariant coordinate.

The linear map `covariantRestriction m` transports this restriction through the
actual affine coordinate algebra equivalence. It is injective on the full
covariant subspace. The degree-`m` binary basis vector
`X₀^c X₁^(m-c)` restricts to `X^c`. For an arbitrary covariant coordinate with
lower restriction `P`, the finite sum of these basis vectors with coefficients
`P.coeff c`, for `c : Fin (m+1)`, therefore has restriction `P`. The degree bound
and the finite polynomial expansion justify this equality. Restriction
injectivity proves surjectivity of `covariantRowMap`, and its previously proved
injectivity supplies the displayed linear equivalence.

This is derived content: its provenance is the existing actual subgroup,
character, affine coordinate and chart constructions, together with elementary
localization, polynomial reversal, and the binary homogeneous monomial basis.
It identifies the whole covariant vector space, but does not yet transport the
right-translation representation, prove Borel status, or prove the general
`SteinbergBigCellStatement`.
