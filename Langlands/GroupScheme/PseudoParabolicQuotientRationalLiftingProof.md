# Rational lifting for pseudo-parabolic quotients over every field

Author: Worker 37.

`Langlands.GroupScheme.pseudoParabolicQuotientRationalLifting` proves exactly
the published `Langlands.GroupScheme.PseudoParabolicQuotientRationalLiftingStatement.{u}`
at an arbitrary universe `u`. Over every field `k`, let `G` be a smooth
connected affine group, `i : P ⟶ G` a pseudo-parabolic subgroup, and
`q : G.X ⟶ Q` a supplied quotient satisfying the statement's geometric and
all-test right-coset hypotheses. Then every rational point of `Q` lifts to a
rational point of `G`. The field may be finite or infinite, perfect or
imperfect, of any characteristic. No reductivity, density of `G(k)` or
properness of `Q` is assumed. The supplied quotient model is used as given.

## Proof

The published `PseudoParabolicSplitRadicalInduction.statement_of_trivial_split_radical`
performs strong induction on the Lie dimension through the split ground-field
unipotent radical. Its structural inputs are supplied by the published proofs:

- `SplitUnipotentRadicalExistence.splitUnipotentRadicalExistence`;
- `normalSubgroupQuotient`;
- `splitUnipotentAffineTorsorStatement`;
- `pseudoParabolicSplitRadicalQuotient`.

The remaining case is a group `G` whose split unipotent radical has trivial
range. It splits on whether `k` is finite:

- for finite `k`, the published
  `PseudoParabolicRationalLiftingAssembly.property_of_finite`;
- for infinite `k`, `OppositeEnvelopeLifting.property_of_trivial_split_radical`
  (module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Dynamic.OppositeEnvelopeLifting`). It lifts
  through the smooth connected subgroup generated by `U(χ)`, `χ` and `U(χ⁻¹)`,
  which has dense rational points and is normalized by `P(χ)`.

The proof does not use `TorusGeneratedEnvelopeNormalityStatement` or the
conditional assembly
`PseudoParabolicRationalLiftingAssembly.statement_of_structural_inputs`. Those
published declarations are unchanged.

## Source

The statement formalizes the rational-point conclusion of Conrad–Gabber–Prasad,
*Pseudo-reductive groups*, second edition (2015), Lemma C.2.1, printed
pp.592–593, as documented in the statement's paired Markdown. The implemented
infinite-field step differs from the source's use of the subgroup generated by
ground-field tori, as explained in `OppositeEnvelope.md` and
`OppositeEnvelopeLifting.md`.
