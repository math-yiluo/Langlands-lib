# Cocharacters over the base field from a point of infinite order

Author: Worker 1387.

This internal module, in the namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme.CentralSurjectionImage`, works over a field `k : Type u`
with a commutative group scheme `Z` over `Spec k`.

- `IsDatum.expMap`: a cocharacter datum of weight `m` over `S[Λ]` is transported
  along an exponent change `φ : Λ →+ Λ'` to a datum of weight `φ m`.
  Multiplicativity uses `expMapG_expMapG` and the identities
  `diag ∘ φ = (φ × φ) ∘ diag` and `inl ∘ φ = (φ × φ) ∘ inl` (similarly `inr`).
- `IsDatum.exists_restrict`: suppose `Z` is affine with finitely generated
  coordinate algebra and `S` is nontrivial. Then a datum of weight `m` over
  `S[Λ]` comes from a datum of weight `⟨m, _⟩` over `S[Γ]`, for a finitely
  generated subgroup `Γ ≤ Λ` containing `m`. `Γ` is generated by the supports of
  the images of finitely many algebra generators. An induction over
  `Algebra.adjoin` (with `support_coeff_mul_subset`) shows that all images are
  supported in `Γ`. The point descends along the injective exponent change
  `Γ → Λ` (`exists_affineMap_eq`, `mapDomain_comapDomain`). Multiplicativity and
  the value are then reflected by injectivity.
- `exists_addMonoidHom_int_ne_zero`: in a finitely generated abelian group, an
  element of infinite order is detected by a homomorphism to `ℤ`. Pass to the
  free quotient by torsion and use `Module.Projective.exists_dual_ne_zero`.
- `exists_gm_characterRestrict_ne_zero_of_point`: let `Z` be affine and locally
  of finite type, `χ` a rational character, and `x` a point of `Z` over an
  algebraically closed field `K` such that no positive power of `χ(x)` equals
  `1`. Then some `φ : 𝔾_m ⟶ Z` over `k` has `characterRestrict φ χ ≠ 0`.
  `exists_datum_of_point` (from `EigenDatum`) gives a datum over `K[Kˣ]`, which is
  restricted to a finitely generated exponent group and mapped to
  `PUnit → ℤ` with nonzero weight. The published descent steps
  `IsDatum.exists_finiteType` and `IsDatum.exists_finiteDimensional` follow,
  then `IsDatum.exists_separableClosure_of_comm` (from `InseparableRigidity`) and
  the published Galois step `IsDatum.exists_of_isSeparable`. The published
  `CocharacterDatum.exists_gm_characterRestrict_ne_zero` concludes.

This assembles the published cocharacter-datum descent pipeline of
`Torus.CocharacterDetection` without its torus hypothesis. It is internal
derived content.
