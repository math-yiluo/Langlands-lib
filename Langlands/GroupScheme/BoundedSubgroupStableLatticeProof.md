# Proof of the stable-lattice step of KP Proposition 2.2.13

Author: Worker 132.

`Langlands.GroupScheme.boundedSubgroupStableLattice.{u}` proves exactly
`Langlands.GroupScheme.BoundedSubgroupStableLatticeStatement.{u}`. The field
`K : Type u` has a valuative relation whose canonical valuation integer ring
`O = (ValuativeRel.valuation K).integer` is a discrete valuation ring. `G` is
an affine group scheme over `Spec K`, `ρ : MatrixRepresentation G (Fin n)` is
an actual representation and `B` is a subgroup of the rational points that is
algebraically bounded in the sense of KP Definition 2.2.1. The theorem gives a
full `O`-lattice in `Fin n → K` that is mapped into itself by every original
matrix `ρ.rationalMatrix g` with `g ∈ B`. No hypothesis is added.

## Construction

All auxiliary declarations are in `Langlands.GroupScheme.MatrixRepresentation`
and work for any finite index type `n` with decidable equality.

- `orbitColumns ρ B` is the set of vectors
  `ρ.rationalMatrix g *ᵥ Pi.single k 1` with `g ∈ B` and `k : n`, that is,
  the columns of all matrices of elements of `B`.
- `orbitLattice ρ B` is the `O`-span of `orbitColumns ρ B`. It is the
  `O`-module generated by the translates `ρ(g) · O^n`, `g ∈ B`, of the
  standard coordinate lattice, which is the module KP use.

## Steps of the Lean proof

1. **Stability** (`mulVec_mem_orbitLattice`). For `g ∈ B`, the map
   `v ↦ ρ.rationalMatrix g *ᵥ v`, restricted to `O`-scalars, is `O`-linear.
   By `Submodule.span_le` it suffices that it sends each generator into the
   span. The generator `ρ(h) *ᵥ e_k` with `h ∈ B` is sent to
   `ρ(g * h) *ᵥ e_k` by `Matrix.mulVec_mulVec` and multiplicativity of
   `rationalMatrix` (`rationalMatrix_mul_coe`), and `g * h ∈ B`.
2. **Full span** (`single_mem_orbitLattice`). Taking `g = 1`
   (`rationalMatrix_one_coe`, `Matrix.one_mulVec`) shows that every standard
   basis vector `e_k` lies in the orbit lattice. Hence the `K`-span of the
   orbit lattice contains the span of `Pi.basisFun K n`, which is everything.
3. **Entry bounds.** The published helper
   `hasBoundedRationalEntries_of_isAlgebraicallyBounded` gives, for each
   entry `(i, j)`, a nonzero `b i j : K` with
   `(ρ.rationalMatrix g) i j ≤ᵥ b i j` for all `g ∈ B`. The proof keeps one
   bound per entry; no common bound is chosen.
4. **Containment in a finitely generated module**
   (`orbitLattice_le_span_of_entries`). Each generator `ρ(g) *ᵥ e_k` is the
   sum over `i` of its `i`-th coordinate vectors. The `i`-th coordinate is
   `ρ(g) i k`, and `ρ(g) i k / b i k` lies in `O` by `div_vle_one_iff`. So
   each coordinate vector is an `O`-multiple of `b i k • e_i`. Hence the orbit
   lattice lies in the `O`-span of the finitely many vectors `b i k • e_i`.
5. **Finite generation.** That span is finitely generated
   (`Submodule.fg_span` of a finite range). A submodule of a finitely
   generated module over the Noetherian ring `O` is finitely generated
   (`Submodule.FG.of_le`); this is the only use of the discrete valuation
   ring hypothesis.
6. Steps 2 and 5 give `Submodule.IsLattice K (orbitLattice ρ B)`, and with
   step 1 this is `IsStableLatticeFor` (`isStableLatticeFor_orbitLattice`).
   The main theorem applies this with index type `Fin n` and the orbit
   lattice as witness.

## Relation to the source

The construction is the one in Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New
Approach* (2023), Proposition 2.2.13 (registered source
`kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`), first paragraph of the proof
of (1) and last paragraph of the proof of (3), printed pp. 74–75 / PDF
pp. 105–106. There KP fix a faithful representation `G → GL(V)` and a lattice
`L ⊂ V`, and take the `o`-submodule `Λ` generated by the translates `b · L`,
`b ∈ B`. They argue that the image of `B` in `GL(V)` is bounded, so by their
Lemma 2.2.7 all matrix entries of `B` are bounded; all coordinates of `L` are
bounded; hence all coordinates of `Λ` are bounded, `Λ` is bounded, and `Λ` is
`B`-stable by construction. In (3), `Λ` contains `L` and is therefore a
lattice.

The implemented proof follows this construction with `L` the standard
coordinate lattice `O^n`, whose translates `ρ(g) · O^n` are spanned by the
columns `ρ(g) *ᵥ e_k`; this is `orbitLattice`. The correspondence is:

- "all matrix entries of `B` are bounded" is step 3, supplied by the published
  helper `hasBoundedRationalEntries_of_isAlgebraicallyBounded` (each entry of
  `ρ` is the pullback of a general linear entry function, to which the
  algebraic boundedness of `B` applies);
- "all coordinates of `Λ` are bounded, hence `Λ` is bounded" is step 4, made
  explicit as containment in the `O`-span of the vectors `b i k • e_i`, and
  step 5, which turns this into finite generation;
- "`Λ` is stable under `B`" is step 1;
- "`Λ` contains `L`", which makes it open, is step 2.

Over a discrete valuation ring, finite generation and full `K`-span are the
translation of KP's "bounded" and "open" used by `Submodule.IsLattice` in the
statement module. Faithfulness of `ρ`, which KP assume, is not used: the
entry bounds hold for every actual representation. The Lean arguments in
steps 1–6 are internal derivations of this proof file.
