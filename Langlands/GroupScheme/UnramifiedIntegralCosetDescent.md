# Rational points in Galois-stable integral cosets

This internal descent lemma is derived from the published finite unramified integral cocycle vanishing theorem (`localUnramifiedIntegralCocycleVanishing`, KP Lemma 8.1.4). It is intended for the lifting step in KP Corollary 11.7.5 (printed p. 434, PDF p. 465), without assuming any Kottwitz or building theorem.

For a finite-type affine group over a nonarchimedean local field F and a smooth integral model M with connected special fibre, the proposition `UnramifiedIntegralCosetDescentStatement` asserts the following. If x in G(K), with K the actual maximal unramified extension, satisfies x⁻¹σ(x) integral for every coefficient automorphism σ, there are g in G(F) and an integral h in G(K) with g_K = xh. The model, its generic identification and its integral coordinate predicate are the existing Library ones. The group need not be reductive.

The implemented proof first proves `GaloisPoint.exists_finiteUnramified_definition` by taking finitely many algebra generators of the affine coordinate ring and the normal closure of their values inside the same separable closure. Over that finite Galois unramified field E, the elements x_E⁻¹σ(x_E) form an integral cocycle. Integral cocycle vanishing writes them as fσ(f)⁻¹ with f integral. Therefore x_E f is fixed and descends by effective finite Galois point descent. Extending f to K gives the required correction. Integrality both descends and extends through the actual coordinate maps.
