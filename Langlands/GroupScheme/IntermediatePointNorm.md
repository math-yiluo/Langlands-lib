# Norms of group points over intermediate fields

Let \(E/F\) be finite Galois with intermediate field \(L\), and let \(T\) be a commutative group scheme with affine underlying scheme over \(F\). The product of the images in \(T(E)\) of any \(x\in T(L)\), over all \(F\)-embeddings of \(L\) in \(E\), descends to \(T(F)\). Under an embedding \(E\to M\) over \(F\), its image in \(T(M)\) is the same product with each embedding followed by \(E\to M\).

The proof uses the actual coefficient-point groups. Each Galois automorphism of \(E/F\) permutes the embedding indices, so the product is fixed. The affine coefficient-point descent equivalence gives the norm in \(T(F)\). Functoriality of coefficient change gives the formula in \(T(M)\).
