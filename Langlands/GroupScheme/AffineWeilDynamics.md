# Cocharacters and identity limits under actual affine Weil restriction

This internal construction transports cocharacters, orbit-extension curves and limits through the actual affine Weil restriction in `AffineWeilRestriction`. The rings `A` and `B` lie in an arbitrary common universe `u`, are commutative, and have a specified `A`-algebra structure on `B`. The scalar and parameter comparisons require no finiteness assumptions. For the restricted group constructions, `B` is finite and projective as an `A`-module, and the actual source group scheme `G` over `Spec B` is affine and locally of finite type. No field, separability or characteristic assumption is imposed.

For every actual relative test scheme `T` over `Spec A`, `sectionAlgebra` is its relative algebra of global sections, `tensorAlgebra` is `B ⊗[A] Γ(T, ⊤)`, and `tensorTest` is the spectrum of that actual `B`-algebra. `tensorTestMap` takes the pullback on global sections, extends it on tensor coefficients and applies relative spectrum. The identity and composition lemmas show that these are actual covariant changes of test schemes. No affineness is assumed for `T`.

`includeSection` sends a function on `T` into the right factor of the tensor algebra and then into global sections of its spectrum using the inverse of `Scheme.ΓSpecIso`. Its naturality proof uses the naturality of that canonical spectrum comparison. `scalarGmPoints` transfers a multiplicative point by applying the induced homomorphism on units; `scalarLinePoint` transfers an affine-line point by applying the same ring homomorphism to its coordinate function. Both commute with every actual change of test scheme. The unit-inclusion lemma says that forgetting a unit agrees with transferring its underlying function. The zero-section lemma says that a function which is zero remains zero.

`gmComparison T` maps `tensorTest (T × Gm_A)` to `tensorTest T × Gm_B` by the transferred first projection and the scalar multiplicative point of the second projection. `lineComparison T` uses the analogous affine-line scalar point. The two comparison morphisms respect the inclusion `Gm → A¹`, and the line comparison respects the zero section. These equalities are proved by the two projections of the actual categorical product, using scalar naturality and functoriality of `tensorTestMap`. The comparisons are morphisms; no claim that they are isomorphisms is needed or made.

`testPointsEquiv` is the multiplicative equivalence from actual restricted-group points on `T` to actual original-group points on `tensorTest T`. It is the published corepresentation comparison on relative global sections. Its naturality lemma identifies precomposition by an arbitrary test morphism with precomposition by `tensorTestMap`.

Given an actual cocharacter `χ : Gm_B → G`, `cocharacterPoints` first transfers a scalar unit point, applies `χ`, and then inverts `testPointsEquiv`. The explicit evaluation lemma and scalar naturality prove naturality on every test scheme. `cocharacter` is the resulting actual homomorphism `Gm_A → Res(B/A) G`, constructed by the published `homOfPoints` interface. `testPointsEquiv_cocharacter` states its exact value on every scalar point. `testPointsEquiv_orbit` identifies its conjugation orbit with the pullback along `gmComparison` of the original `χ`-orbit of the corresponding point. The proof uses multiplicativity, preservation of inverses and the projection formulas; it retains the original point.

`transferExtension` sends an actual curve `e : tensorTest T × A¹_B → G` to the restricted-group point corresponding to `lineComparison T ≫ e`. `testPointsEquiv_transferExtension` records precisely this value. If `e` extends the `χ`-orbit of the corresponding original point, `transferExtension_isOrbitExtension` shows that the transferred curve extends the orbit of the original restricted point. Its proof combines the unit-inclusion square with the orbit comparison. `testPointsEquiv_evalZero_transferExtension` identifies evaluation at zero with the original zero value, by the zero-section square.

Consequently `hasLimit_of_testPointsEquiv` transfers an original limit to the corresponding restricted point and limit. `mem_dynamicUnipotent_of_testPointsEquiv` specializes this to the identity limit, using preservation of the identity by the multiplicative point equivalence. `mem_dynamicParabolic_of_testPointsEquiv` transfers existence of an orbit extension. These conclusions hold on all actual relative test schemes. They are implications from original tensor-spectrum membership, and do not assert the converse or equality of dynamic subgroup functors.

The provenance is the internal composition of the published affine Weil corepresentation, its all-test point comparison, the scalar descriptions of `Gm` and the affine line, and the definitions in `Dynamic.Basic`. This module supplies no classification of arbitrary group schemes, local division-algebra arithmetic, rational generation theorem or proof of the unrestricted local Kneser–Tits statement.
