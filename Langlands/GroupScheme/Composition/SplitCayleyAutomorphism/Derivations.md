# Derivations of Zorn's split Cayley algebra

- `rootDerFun α`, `rootDer α`: the linear term `X_α` of `x_α(r) = 1 + r X_α + r² X_α^{(2)}`
  (`rootFun_eq`), as a function and as a matrix. `torusDerFun a b`, `torusDer a b`: the diagonal
  torus derivation `diag(0, a, b, -a-b, -a, -b, a+b, 0)`.
- `IsDerivation f`: `f(xy) = f(x) y + x f(y)` for the Zorn product. Each `rootDerFun α` and each
  `torusDerFun a b` is a derivation.
- `eq_derivCombination`: over every commutative ring, every derivation matrix `D` equals the
  explicit combination `derivCombination D` of the twelve `rootDer α` and of `torusDer 1 0`,
  `torusDer 0 1`, with coefficients read off from entries of `D`. So the derivation algebra is free
  of rank 14 over any coefficient ring.
- `isAutomorphism_one_add_epsilonMatrix_iff`: over the dual numbers `A[ε]`, the matrix `1 + εD`
  is an automorphism iff `D` kills the unit and is a derivation. This identifies the Lie algebra
  of the automorphism group scheme with derivation matrices.

The proofs are entrywise ring identities; the coefficient certificates were generated by a
computer-algebra script and are checked by `linear_combination`.
