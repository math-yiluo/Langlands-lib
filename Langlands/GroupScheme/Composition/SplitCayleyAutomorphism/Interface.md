# Algebraic inputs for the split Cayley automorphism pinning (proposed interface)

Four Prop-valued statements of pure matrix algebra about Zorn's algebra, proposed for division of
labour inside Task 7589. They are stated here and proved in other files: `generation` in
`Generation.lean`, `stableSubspace` and `unitRigidity` in `RadicalAlgebra.lean`, and
`centerRigidity` in `Center.lean`.

- `GenerationStatement`: over an algebraically closed field, every automorphism matrix lies in the
  subgroup of `GL₈` generated by all `rootGL α r` and `torusGL s t`. Used with
  `IdentityComponent.connectedSpace_of_forall_mem_range` to prove connectedness (as for the
  symplectic group in `SymplecticMatrix/Reductive.lean`). This is the classical fact that a split
  group of type `G₂` over an algebraically closed field is generated by its maximal torus and root
  groups.
- `StableSubspaceStatement`: over an algebraically closed field, a subspace stable under root and
  torus elements and containing a vector off `k · 1` contains the coordinate vectors
  `u₁, …, v₃`.
- `UnitRigidityStatement`: over any commutative ring, an automorphism matrix with
  `g(e_i) ∈ e_i + A · 1` for `i = 1, …, 6` is the identity (from `u₁u₂ = v₃`-type products and
  `u₁ v₁ = e₀`).
- `CenterRigidityStatement`: over any commutative ring, an automorphism matrix commuting with every
  `x_α(1)` is the identity.

The last three give the trivial unipotent radical (applied to the 7-dimensional quotient
representation `k⁸ / k · 1`) and the trivial scheme-theoretic centre.
