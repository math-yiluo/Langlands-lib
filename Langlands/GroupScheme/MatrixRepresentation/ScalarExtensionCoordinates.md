# Scalar-extension coordinates and descent of actual intertwiners

This internal derived module compares the actual affine matrices of a
representation with their scalar extension. A compatible algebra over the
extension field has the same spectrum when viewed over the original field.
The canonical test-scheme isomorphism and the base-change point equivalence
therefore lift original points, and the global-section comparison shows
that the affine matrices agree over this common ring.

Consequently every original intertwiner extends coefficientwise and remains
an intertwiner on every affine test algebra. For an affine original group,
intertwining is equivalent to the matrix equation at its universal affine
point, by the naturality of the affine matrices.

In the other direction, apply an original-field linear functional to the
entries of an extension-field intertwiner. Test its equation over the tensor
product of the extension field and the original coordinate algebra, with
independent extension-field and coordinate coefficients. Applying the
functional to the first tensor factor gives exactly the original universal
intertwining equation. This requires no Galois, separability or finite-degree
hypothesis, and imposes no smoothness or reducedness condition on the group.
The provenance is a derivation from the Library's actual coordinate and
base-change definitions and its tensor coefficient operation.
