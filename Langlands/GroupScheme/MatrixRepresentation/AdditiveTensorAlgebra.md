# Conditional separation of actual additive tensor coefficient algebras

This internal derived unit concerns supplied actual additive-group
representations over a field `k : Type u`, on finite coordinate types. It uses
the actual diagonal tensor representation and its previously proved coefficient
convolution. It introduces no group-structure or highest-weight assumption.

`leftTensor` and `rightTensor` are the algebra homomorphisms `M ↦ M ⊗ₖ 1`
and `N ↦ 1 ⊗ₖ N`. Their product is `M ⊗ₖ N`. A subalgebra containing both
full matrix factors equals the entire product-coordinate matrix algebra:
each matrix unit is a product of such factors, and matrix units span all
matrices.

The central separation theorem starts with a subalgebra `A` that contains the
entire first matrix factor and every coefficient of the actual tensor
representation `ρ.tensor σ`. It proves that `A` contains `1 ⊗ₖ D_d(σ)`
for every degree `d`. Induction on `d` isolates the `(0,d)` term in the actual
convolution. Every other term has smaller second degree, so it is the product
of an available first-factor matrix and an already isolated second-factor
coefficient. Subtracting their sum leaves the desired coefficient. No
polynomial degree bound on `ρ` is required.

For a family `ρs` of actual additive-group representations, `coefficientAlgebra`
is the algebra generated by every degree coefficient of every member, and
`truncatedCoefficientAlgebra ρs N` uses only degrees strictly below `N`.
The index family may have arbitrary universe `v`; the coordinate types are
finite and in `Type`, as required by the actual matrix representation interface.

In prime characteristic `p`, suppose the first family's coefficients below
`p^e` generate its full matrix algebra. The low-degree Frobenius tensor formula
then puts the full first matrix factor inside the coefficient algebra of
`i ↦ (ρs i).tensor ((σs i).frobeniusPower p e)`.
The separation theorem puts every powered second-family coefficient in that
algebra as a second-factor matrix. If the coefficient algebra of the powered
second family is also full, the diagonal tensor coefficient algebra is full.

Both fullness hypotheses are explicit, substantive premises. No claim that
restrictedness implies a first-factor degree bound is used. The second premise
concerns the actual Frobenius-powered representations, including the
Frobenius transformation of their coefficient entries; it is not silently
identified with fullness before Frobenius. No perfectness or infinitude
assumption is needed for the stated conditional result. This unit does not
construct a generating root family or a Steinberg representation and does not
prove `SteinbergBigCellStatement`.
