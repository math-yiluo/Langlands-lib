# Inverse characters in a dual representation

Worker 1228.

This internal construction takes an actual finite matrix representation of an
affine group scheme over any field. It assumes that the sum of its rational
character spaces is the entire coordinate space. Under this explicit premise,
every character occurring on a nonzero vector occurs with its negative in the
actual dual representation.

The coefficient projector for a chosen character, followed by a coordinate
functional, is written as a vector by the dot product identification with the
dual. On each character space its pairing is either the chosen coordinate or
zero. Induction over the sum of the character spaces then gives its
transformation equation over every affine test algebra. Evaluating this
equation at the inverse of the universal point proves the inverse character
equation for the inverse transpose action. A nonzero coordinate of the original
vector makes the resulting dual vector nonzero.

The provenance is an internal derivation from the character coefficient
projectors, the actual contragredient representation and elementary linear
algebra. The spanning premise is retained. This file does not establish
stability or spanning of a normal subgroup's eigenline space, descend a
representation through a field extension, or construct a quotient.
