# The Tits algebra of a representation of a scalar-extended group

This module defines the Tits algebra `titsAlgebra ρ` of a representation `ρ`
of `G_K`: the explicit `k`-subalgebra of `M_n(K)` generated by the matrix
coefficients of `ρ` at `k`-linear functionals on the coordinate algebra
`O(G)`. This coefficient construction is a realization of the Galois descent
step motivated by the proof of Theorem 3.3 of Tits, *Représentations
linéaires irréductibles d'un groupe réductif sur un corps quelconque*,
J. reine angew. Math. 247 (1971), pp. 203–204, where Tits starts from an
absolutely irreducible highest-weight representation and twists `End(V)` by
a projective cocycle. The comparison of these algebras with Brauer classes of
weights is a separate result (`Reductive/TitsDominantClass`).

The conclusions hold under precise hypotheses:

- the `K`-span of `titsAlgebra ρ` is `M_n(K)` when `ρ` is irreducible and
  every self-intertwiner of `ρ` is scalar (`span_titsAlgebra_eq_top`);
- `titsAlgebra ρ` lies in `twistedFixed P` whenever each `P σ` intertwines
  `ρ` with its Galois twist (`titsAlgebra_le_twistedFixed`);
- `titsCSA` packages it as a central simple `k`-algebra of dimension `n²`
  when, in addition to the two previous conditions, `K/k` is Galois, the
  index `n` is nonempty and every `P σ` is invertible.

Irreducibility alone is not enough. For `k = K = ℚ` and the constant cyclic
group of order four acting on `ℚ²` by a rotation `J` with `J² = -1`, the
representation is irreducible and isomorphic to its Galois twists, but the
coefficient algebra is `ℚ[J] ≅ ℚ(i)`, which is not central and does not span
`M₂(ℚ)`; here `J` is a non-scalar self-intertwiner.

Notation: `k ⊆ K` are fields, `G` is an affine group scheme over `Spec k`,
`O(G) = CharacterUnits.Coord G`, and `ρ` is a representation of `G_K` on
`Kⁿ` with universal matrix `universalMatrix ρ` over `K ⊗[k] O(G)` (module
`GaloisCoefficients`).

## Base functionals and reconstruction

- `baseFunctional K φ` is the `K`-linear map `id_K ⊗ φ : K ⊗[k] O → K ⊗[k] k = K`
  attached to a `k`-linear functional `φ : O → k`. On pure tensors it is
  `c ⊗ o ↦ φ(o) c` (`baseFunctional_tmul`).
- `baseFunctional_galoisTensor`: `(id ⊗ φ) ∘ (σ ⊗ id) = σ ∘ (id ⊗ φ)`, because `σ`
  is `k`-linear.
- `exists_reconstruction`: let `Z` be a finite set in `K ⊗[k] O`. Then there are
  `k`-functionals `φ_1, …, φ_r` on `O` and vectors `o_1, …, o_r ∈ O` with
  `z = Σ_s (id ⊗ φ_s)(z) · (1 ⊗ o_s)` for every `z ∈ Z`. Each `z` lies in the
  `K`-span of `1 ⊗ T` for a finite `T ⊆ O`. Take a `k`-basis `o_s` of the span
  `F` of these sets, and extend its coordinate functionals from `F` to `O`
  (Mathlib's `LinearMap.exists_extend`).

## Density

- `operatorAlgebra_eq_top` applies to an affine group `H` over any field `F`.
  If a representation is irreducible and every self-intertwiner is a scalar
  matrix, then the published operator algebra
  `AffineCoefficientAlgebra.operatorAlgebra ρ` is all of `End_F(Fⁿ)`. By the
  published `isSimpleModule`, `Fⁿ` is a simple module over the operator algebra.
  By the published `intertwines_iff` and the hypothesis, its endomorphisms are
  scalars. Mathlib's `jacobson_density`, applied on the standard basis, then
  realizes every `F`-linear map by an element of the operator algebra.

## The Tits algebra

- `coefficientMatrix ρ φ` is `(id ⊗ φ)` applied entrywise to `universalMatrix ρ`.
- `titsAlgebra ρ` is the `k`-subalgebra of `M_n(K)` generated by all
  `coefficientMatrix ρ φ`.
- `coefficientMatrix_galoisTwist`: the coefficient matrices of `galoisTwist σ ρ`
  are the `σ`-conjugates of those of `ρ`.
- `coefficientMatrix_intertwines`: if `P` intertwines `ρ` with `ρ^σ`, then
  `P · X = σ(X) · P` for every coefficient matrix `X`. The universal intertwining
  identity is mapped by `id ⊗ φ`, using the published `map_mul_left` and
  `map_mul_right` from `AffineCoefficientAlgebra`.
- `titsAlgebra_le_twistedFixed`: given such intertwiners `P σ` for all `σ`, the
  Tits algebra lies in `twistedFixed P` of module `GaloisTwistedMatrix`.
- `map_mem_span_coefficientMatrix`: every coefficient `L(universalMatrix ρ)` at a
  `K`-linear functional `L` on `K ⊗[k] O(G)` is a `K`-combination of coefficient
  matrices. This uses `exists_reconstruction` on the `n²` entries.
- `coefficientMap_mem_span`: the published universal coefficient matrices of
  `ρ` over the coordinate algebra of `G_K` are of this form. The universal point
  of `G_K` is the image of the universal scalar point (`GaloisCoefficients`).
- `span_titsAlgebra_eq_top`: let `ρ` be irreducible with scalar self-intertwiners.
  Then the `K`-span of the Tits algebra is `M_n(K)`. That span is a
  `K`-subalgebra containing all published coefficient matrices of `ρ`, so it
  contains the operator algebra, which is everything by `operatorAlgebra_eq_top`.
- `titsCSA` is the Tits algebra as an element of Mathlib's `CSA k`. It requires
  `K/k` Galois (possibly infinite), `n` nonempty, `ρ` irreducible with scalar
  self-intertwiners, and invertible intertwiners `P σ : ρ → ρ^σ` for all `σ`.
  `titsCSA_carrier` records that its carrier is `titsAlgebra ρ`, independent of
  the choice of `P`. `finrank_titsAlgebra` gives `dim_k = n²`.

The construction makes no assumption on the characteristic, perfectness or
reducedness. Galois descent is used only through module `GaloisTwistedMatrix`.
