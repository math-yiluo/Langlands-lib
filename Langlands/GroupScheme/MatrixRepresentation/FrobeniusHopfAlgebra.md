# Hopf algebra of the actual Frobenius-origin quotient

This derived unit equips the genuine coordinate augmentation-power quotient
with inversion induced by the original affine group scheme. Coordinate inversion
classifies the inverse of the actual universal point. Naturality of the actual
point-coordinate equivalence proves its behavior on every commutative test
algebra, its involutivity and preservation of identity evaluation. The two
inverse laws of the actual point group prove the antipode axioms for the
existing original coordinate bialgebra.

Inversion carries each defining augmentation p^r power into another such power,
so the quotient ideal is a Hopf ideal. The canonical quotient Hopf algebra then
has the existing original quotient coproduct and counit; its antipode is the
class of original coordinate inversion and is involutive.

No external literature assertion is introduced. This supplies the actual
inversion structure and does not prove integral uniqueness or the general
Steinberg big-cell statement.
