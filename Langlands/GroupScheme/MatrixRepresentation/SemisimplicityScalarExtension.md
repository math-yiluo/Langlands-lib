# Descent of actual equivariant projections and semisimplicity

This internal derived module proves field-extension descent for actual
equivariant projections onto scalar-extended original subspaces, and hence
for the exact sum-of-simple-stable-subspaces semisimplicity condition.
The group is any affine group scheme over any field. The extension is any
field extension; Galois, separability, characteristic or finite-degree
hypotheses are unnecessary.

Suppose an extended linear map is an equivariant projection onto the
extension of an original subspace. Choose an original-field linear
functional on the extension field taking one to one, and apply it to the
projection matrix entries. Functional descent of intertwiners proves
that the resulting original linear map is equivariant. The
coefficient-extraction property of extended subspaces proves that its
range lies in the original subspace. Normalization of the functional
proves that it fixes that subspace. Thus it is an original equivariant
projection. A stable-subspace hypothesis need not be supplied separately.

If the extended representation is semisimple, every extended original
stable subspace has an equivariant projection onto it. Descend the
projection as above and take its stable kernel as a complement to the
original subspace. The stable-complement characterization then proves
original semisimplicity, including for the zero representation. These
results use arbitrary affine test algebras in their stability and
intertwining predicates, and assume neither smoothness nor reducedness.
