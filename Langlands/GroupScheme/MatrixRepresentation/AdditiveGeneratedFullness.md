# Coefficient fullness for supplied generating additive families

This internal derived unit connects the full actual additive-family matrix
coefficient algebra to the existing density and Burnside results. The field has
arbitrary universe `u`, the family index independent universe `v`, and all finite
decidable coordinate types are in `Type`.

`coefficientAction` is the natural `k`-algebra map from a family's full matrix
coefficient algebra to the endomorphisms of its original coordinate space. It is
the inclusion into matrices followed by the usual matrix-to-endomorphism algebra
equivalence.

In prime characteristic `p`, the earlier `AdditiveFamilyActionAlgebra` uses the
endomorphism algebra generated by the actual restricted coefficients in degrees
`p^r`. Each such operator is the image under `coefficientAction` of its actual
coefficient matrix. Thus the earlier endomorphism algebra lies in the range of
this action. Over an algebraically closed field, its published identification
with the algebra of the generated rational-point actions puts each generated
rational-point matrix in the full matrix coefficient algebra. Injectivity of
the matrix-to-endomorphism equivalence justifies this last passage.

For a reduced ambient group and an actual all-affine-test irreducible ambient
representation, assume the supplied additive family's generated rational points
are dense. The published `DenseRationalSpan` theorem says their matrices span
the entire matrix space. Since the coefficient algebra contains all these
matrices and is a vector subspace, it equals the full matrix algebra. This uses
the existing Burnside proof rather than asserting a new simplicity premise.

For an affine reduced ambient group locally of finite type, supplied geometric
generation by the additive family gives the required density by the existing
`AdditiveFamilyActionAlgebra` result. Consequently the matrix coefficient
algebra is full, and `coefficientAction` is surjective onto all endomorphisms.
The irreducibility premise always concerns the original entire representation.

The last theorem applies this conclusion to an irreducible second ambient
factor, transports its fullness through actual Frobenius over the algebraically
closed field, and uses the previous actual ambient tensor criterion. Both
coordinate types are assumed nonempty. The tensor is the full original
`ρ.tensor (σ.frobeniusPower p e)` and its irreducibility uses stability on every
affine test algebra, including nonreduced tests.

The first factor's coefficient algebra in degrees strictly below `p^e` must
still be full. The additive morphisms and their geometric generation are
supplied, not constructed here. No restrictedness-to-truncated-fullness
theorem, Steinberg-module construction, entire induced-space comparison,
general root-family existence theorem, big-cell decomposition, or proof of
`SteinbergBigCellStatement` is asserted.
