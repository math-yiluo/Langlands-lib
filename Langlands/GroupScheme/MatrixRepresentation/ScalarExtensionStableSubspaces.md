# Scalar extension and descent of actual stable subspaces

This internal derived module connects coefficientwise scalar extension of
finite subspaces to the actual all-affine-test stability predicate of matrix
representations. An extended matrix has as its range the scalar extension
of its original range. Applying an original-field linear functional to a
vector in an extended subspace yields a vector in the original subspace;
span induction proves this, allowing arbitrary functionals at each induction
step.

For any stable subspace over an extension field, its original vectors form
a stable subspace over the original field. To prove this, extend an arbitrary
original affine algebra test to its tensor product with the extension field.
The published compatible-test matrix comparison identifies the extended
matrix. Extend the original linear functional by tensoring, apply stability
over the extension field, and read the resulting equation as the original
coefficient vector extended to that field. This descent uses neither a
Galois nor a separability assumption.

Conversely, for an affine original group, choose split coordinates for a
stable subspace and its actual subrepresentation. Extend the equivariant
inclusion to the extension field. Its range is stable and is the extended
original subspace, proving preservation of stability. Membership reflection
then proves the converse. No reducedness or smoothness assumption is made;
the defining tests include all affine algebras.
