# Nilpotence of the actual Frobenius distribution augmentation ideal

This is a derived construction for an affine group scheme `G` over a field
`k : Type u`, in exponential characteristic `p`, and for any natural height `r`.
Write `Q = FrobeniusCoordinateModel.quotientAlgebra G p r` for the actual
coordinate algebra modulo the ideal generated by the `p^r` powers of its
identity augmentation ideal. Write `D` for the full convolution dual of `Q`,
with multiplication induced by the original group multiplication.

`augmentation` is the algebra homomorphism `D →ₐ[k] k` given by
`ell ↦ ell.ofConv 1`. Its unit law uses that the convolution unit is the
coordinate counit; its product law uses that the coordinate coproduct sends
`1` to `1 ⊗ 1`. `augmentationIdeal` is its kernel, a two-sided ideal in `D`,
and membership is exactly the equation `ell.ofConv 1 = 0`.

For the explicit bound, assume an actual split torus `S` acts on `G` by group
automorphisms. Fix a cocharacter `ν` of `S` and polynomial coordinates
`t : Fin d → CharacterUnits.Coord G`: evaluation is required to be bijective,
each `t i` has character `w i`, and each vanishes at the identity. Require
`0 < latticePairing S (w i) ν` for every coordinate. With

- `N = FrobeniusPolynomialBox.bound p d r`, whose entries are `p^r - 1`,
- `H = latticePairing S (Finsupp.weight w N) ν`, and
- `J = augmentationIdeal (G := G) p r`,

`augmentationIdeal_pow_eq_bot` proves `J ^ (H.toNat + 1) = ⊥`.
The theorem `isNilpotent_augmentationIdeal` records the resulting nilpotence.
The empty coordinate family and `r = 0` are included.

The implemented proof uses the actual character-graded coordinate coproduct
from `TorusUnipotence.CoordinateGrading`. First,
`augmentation_pow_vanishes_on_homogeneous` proves that every element of
`J ^ (n + 1)` vanishes on the class of every homogeneous coordinate whose
integer height is less than `n + 1`. For `n = 0`, strict positivity of the
coordinate heights makes a homogeneous coordinate of nonpositive height a
scalar, which an augmentation distribution kills. For the induction step,
write the ideal power as `J * J ^ (n + 1)` and use additive induction on its
product generators. In a coproduct term of weights `α` and `χ - α`, either
`α` has nonpositive height and the first factor vanishes, or its height is at
least one and the second factor vanishes by the induction hypothesis. The
contraction kernel contains the whole sum of these tensor weight spaces.
Finally every retained monomial has height at most `H`, so extensionality on
the genuine quotient monomial basis proves the explicit zero ideal power.

`isNilpotent_augmentationIdeal_of_positiveWeights` removes the supplied
coordinate premise when `G` is smooth and locally of finite type. It assumes
strict positivity on `ν` of every actual augmentation weight of the torus
action and obtains the required coordinates from the published theorem
`TorusUnipotence.exists_positiveWeight_coordinates`.

The provenance is internal: the argument combines the existing quotient,
convolution, homogeneous-coordinate and coproduct constructions, with ideal
power induction. It introduces no external literature assertion. It proves
nilpotence of the genuine convolution augmentation ideal and does not assert
integral uniqueness, construct a Steinberg module, or identify an entire
covariant induced space. The general `SteinbergBigCellStatement` remains
unproved.
