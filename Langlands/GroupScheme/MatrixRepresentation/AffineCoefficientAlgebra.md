# The operator algebra of an affine matrix representation

This is an internal reduction for Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017), Proposition
4.19, pp. 90–91 (registered source `milne-2017-algebraic-groups`). It uses the
published universal-coefficient construction in `AffineCoefficients`.

The algebra `operatorAlgebra ρ` is the unital subalgebra of the endomorphisms of
`kⁿ` generated by the universal coefficient matrices. A ground-field subspace is
stable on every affine test exactly when this operator algebra preserves it.
Thus irreducibility of the actual representation makes `kⁿ` a simple module for
this algebra. The module proof restricts scalars on an operator submodule and
uses the published characterization of actual stability.

An actual self-intertwiner commutes with every universal coefficient matrix, and
the converse holds. The forward implication applies a linear functional to the
universal intertwining identity. The reverse implication evaluates at any affine
test and uses separation by the algebraic dual. The two matrix extraction lemmas
justify commuting coefficient extraction with constant left and right factors.
All statements hold over arbitrary fields and affine group schemes, including
nonreduced schemes; no rational-point density is used.
