# Ordered products in the actual Frobenius distribution algebra

This is a derived construction over an arbitrary field `k : Type u`, for an
affine group scheme `G`, an exponential characteristic `p`, and any height
`r : ℕ`. Its substantive additional input is a finite ordered family of actual
additive group homomorphisms `x` whose actual ordered coordinate map
`OrderedAdditiveCoordinates.coordinateMap x` is bijective. It does not
construct this family or its coordinate isomorphism.

## Basis and axis indices

`convolutionBasis` transports the existing retained coefficient dual basis
`FrobeniusOrderedDistributions.distributionBasis` along the inverse of
`WithConv.linearEquiv`, so it is a basis of the full actual Frobenius-quotient
dual equipped with convolution. Its index set consists of exponent functions
bounded by `FrobeniusPolynomialBox.bound p d r`, namely exponent at most
`p ^ r - 1` in each coordinate. `convolutionBasis_ofConv` recovers the
underlying dual basis element.

`axisExponent b i` retains only the `i`th exponent of a bounded multi-index
`b`. It is still bounded because its other entries are zero.
`actionAlgHom_convolutionBasis` identifies the action of each basis
distribution on any finite original representation with the existing exact
ordered product of restricted divided powers. `coefficientOperator_axisExponent`
reduces the product for an axis exponent to its one divided-power factor:
all other exponents are zero, and divided power zero is the identity. The
implemented proof uses the fixed-order list product with exactly one possibly
nonidentity factor and does not rearrange factors.

## Multiplication in the actual algebra

`convolutionBasis_eq_orderedProduct` states that each actual coefficient
basis distribution equals the product of its axis coefficient distributions,
in the same order as the original additive family. The proof applies
`FrobeniusDistributionSeparation.distribution_ext_of_actionAlgHom`.
Every finite original representation acts by a unital algebra homomorphism;
the action of that product is the corresponding ordered product of divided
powers and hence the action of the original basis element. Separation proves
the equality in the full actual distribution algebra.

`convolutionBasis_zero` likewise identifies the zero-exponent basis
distribution with the convolution unit by testing every finite original
action. `axisSubalgebra` is the subalgebra generated by all retained axis
basis distributions, with redundant repeated indices permitted.
`axisSubalgebra_eq_top` proves that it is the full actual convolution
algebra: each coefficient basis element is a product of generators, and
those basis elements span the whole underlying vector space.

All results are derived from the imported Library ordered coefficient,
convolution-action, and separation constructions. No additional external
source is used. In particular this unit proves no existence of root
subgroups, Steinberg representation or extremal vector, no rectangular
support bound for an entire induced representation, and no irreducibility
claim.
