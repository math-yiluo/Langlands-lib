# Simultaneous diagonalization of actual tangent matrices

For any finite coordinate representation of an affine linearly reductive
group over an algebraically closed field of nonzero characteristic,
`exists_tangentEigenbasis_of_linearlyReductive` constructs one basis of
common eigenvectors of all its actual tangent matrices.
`exists_conjugate_tangentMatrix_diagonal_of_linearlyReductive` uses that basis
to conjugate the actual group-scheme representation by a rational matrix
point, making each of its tangent matrices diagonal.

The preceding semisimplicity and commutation results make the simultaneous
generalized eigenspaces span. Individual semisimplicity identifies those with
ordinary simultaneous eigenspaces. A basis extracted from their union consists
of common eigenvectors, and the published coordinate-change formula gives the
vanishing of each off-diagonal entry. This is a derived matrix form of the
toral tangent argument of Demazure–Gabriel, *Groupes algébriques I* (1970), IV
§3, Lemma 3.7, printed p. 510.
