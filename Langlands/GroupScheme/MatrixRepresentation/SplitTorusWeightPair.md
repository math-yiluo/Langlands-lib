# Nonreciprocal weights in faithful split-torus representations

Author: Worker 118.

This internal module provides actual universal coefficient vectors for the
rank-one isometry argument. Let `k : Type u` be any field, `I : Type u` a
finite index type, and `ρ : G_m^I → GL_N` an actual group-scheme
representation whose underlying scheme map is a monomorphism.

If `Nat.card I ≥ 2`, `nonreciprocalWeightPair I ρ` chooses two nonzero
integral exponent vectors `m,p`, with `p ≠ m` and `p ≠ -m`, and a nonzero
universal coefficient column for each. The structure
`NonreciprocalWeightPair I ρ` records these data and their properties.
The coefficient columns are those constructed in
`GeneralLinear.CocharacterDiagonal`; their all-algebra eigenvector identity
is the published `coefficientColumn_weight`.

The proof uses
`CharacterRestrictionFiniteIndex.closure_weights_eq_top`: faithfulness
makes the nonzero weight matrices generate the entire integer lattice
`ℤ^I`. If every pair of nonzero weights were equal or opposite, their
integer span would be generated by one vector. The finite-rank bound for
a singleton span would force `Nat.card I ≤ 1`. Two weight matrices
therefore have the required weights. A nonzero weight matrix has a
nonzero column, yielding the supplied vectors.

The module also proves two scalar separation lemmas over an **infinite**
field. A nonzero exponent has a nonzero coordinate; the published
`splitTorus.exists_units_zpow_ne_one` supplies a unit whose corresponding
power is not one. Putting this unit in that coordinate and one in the
others separates the exponent monomial from one.
`exponentValue_function_injective` follows by applying this to the
difference of two exponents.

The infinitude hypothesis belongs only to these field-unit separation
lemmas. The existence and construction of the universal weight pair
work over every field, including finite fields and characteristic two.
No quaternionic realization or Witt-index conclusion is asserted here.
