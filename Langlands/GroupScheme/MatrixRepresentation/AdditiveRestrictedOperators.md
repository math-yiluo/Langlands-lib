# Characteristic-p operators of actual additive-group representations

This module concerns an actual matrix representation of the one-dimensional
vector group over a field `k` of prime characteristic `p`. The coordinate
type `n` is finite with decidable equality. Write `D_m` for the coefficient
endomorphisms constructed in `AdditiveDividedPowers`. Define
`pPowerOperator ρ p r = D_(p^r)`.

`choose_pow_mul_cast` casts Mathlib's Lucas congruence
`Choose.choose_pow_mul_pow_mul_modEq_choose_nat` into `k`, using the
characteristic-p equality criterion. It proves
`(choose (p^r*a) (p^r*b) : k) = (choose a b : k)`.
Consequently `dividedPower_pow_mul_mul` states

```text
D_(p^r*a) * D_(p^r*b) = (choose (a+b) a : k) • D_(p^r*(a+b)).
```

Induction on `m` gives `pPowerOperator_pow`:

```text
pPowerOperator ρ p r ^ m = (m! : k) • D_(p^r*m).
```

The zero case uses `D_0=1`. The successor uses the reindexed composition
law with indices `1,m` and the factorial recurrence; no factorial is
cancelled. Since `p` divides `p!`, `pPowerOperator_pow_char` then proves
that every p-power coefficient has pth power zero.
`pPowerOperator_commute` inherits pairwise commutation from all coefficient
operators. This commutation theorem needs no characteristic or primality
assumption. `pPowerOperator_eventually_zero` uses the eventual vanishing of
`D_m` and the inequality `r < p^r` for prime `p`; this theorem needs primality
but not a characteristic assumption.

`dividedPower_mem_of_pPowerOperator_mem` proves that every unital
`k`-subalgebra of `Module.End k (n → k)` containing the p-power operators
contains all `D_m`. It uses strong induction on `m`. Degree zero is the
identity, and p-power degrees are supplied generators. For any other
positive degree, the contrapositive of Mathlib's
`Choose.eq_pow_multiplicity_of_choose_modEq_zero_nat` supplies an index
`1 ≤ a ≤ m-1` with `(choose m a : k) ≠ 0`. The multiplication law expresses
this nonzero scalar multiple of `D_m` as `D_a * D_(m-a)`. Both factors have
smaller positive degree. Closure under multiplication and multiplication
by the inverse scalar give `D_m`.

Applying this membership result in the generated subalgebra proves
`adjoin_range_pPowerOperator_eq_adjoin_range_dividedPower`. Applying it in
the subalgebra of endomorphisms preserving a specified `k`-subspace gives
`invariant_dividedPower_iff_invariant_pPowerOperator`. These results need
no infinite-field assumption.

For the one-dimensional vector group every multi-index is
`Finsupp.single 0 m`. Thus
`range_dividedPower_eq_range_coefficientOperators` identifies the two
indexing conventions over every field. Under the additional hypothesis
`Infinite k`, the previously proved value/coefficient recovery results give
`adjoin_range_pointOperator_eq_adjoin_range_pPowerOperator` and
`invariant_affinePoints_iff_invariant_pPowerOperator`. These identify the
unital algebra generated by all actual affine base-field point actions
and their invariant subspaces with those of the p-power coefficients.
Both comparisons concern base-field points and explicitly require
infinitude.

This is derived content using the actual universal representation matrix
and the imported, proved Mathlib Lucas theorems in
`Mathlib.Data.Nat.Choose.Lucas`. No external representation-theoretic existence
or irreducibility result is used. The supplied representation is an
assumption; this module constructs no
root subgroup or Steinberg module and does not prove
`SteinbergBigCellStatement`.
