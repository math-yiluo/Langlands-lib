# Universal actual Frobenius-origin points and distribution orbits

Let `k` be a field in universe `u`, let `G` be an actual affine group scheme
over `k`, let `p` be its exponential characteristic, and let `r` be a natural
number. This is an internal derivation from the actual point-coordinate
equivalence and the Frobenius-origin quotient. No external source is used.

Write `Q = FrobeniusCoordinateModel.quotientAlgebra G p r`. Its defining
ideal is generated by the `p^r` powers of elements of the actual identity
augmentation ideal. An actual point `g` over a commutative test algebra `A`
satisfies `IsOriginPoint p r A g` precisely when its coordinate map kills
this defining ideal. This follows from the universal property of an ideal
image and preservation of powers by the coordinate map.

`pointLift` factors that coordinate map through `Q`.
`pointLift_quotientMap` computes it on original coordinates, and
`pointLift_unique` proves uniqueness by surjectivity of the quotient map.
The genuine universal quotient point satisfies `IsOriginPoint`, and
`affineMap_pointLift` recovers the original point from it.
`originPointEquiv` therefore identifies algebra maps `Q →ₐ[k] A` with the
actual origin points of `G` over `A`. Origin points are preserved by every
test-algebra map, and `pointLift_naturality` identifies the corresponding
lift with composition. All test algebras have universe `u`; nonreduced
algebras and the zero algebra are included.

For a supplied finite matrix representation `ρ` of the original group,
`affineMatrix_pointLift` and `affineAction_pointLift` compare its actual
matrix and action with evaluation of the actual quotient matrix and action.
These are consequences of the original representation's naturality along
the unique lift.

`distributionAction_apply` identifies action by a linear functional on `Q`
with contraction of the genuine quotient-point action on a constant vector.
`contract_affineAction` shows that every scalar contraction at any intrinsic
origin point is such an action, by composing its scalar functional with
`pointLift`. Conversely every quotient-distribution action is itself one
of these intrinsic origin-point contractions.

Consequently `originOrbitSpan_eq_range` identifies the all-test origin orbit
span with the linear range of `distributionOrbitMap`. In particular this
range is already a subspace, without taking another span. The final
`stable_originPoints_iff` identifies stability under all intrinsic origin
tests with stability under all actual quotient distributions.

These results need no ordered additive coordinates, reductivity,
algebraic closedness, or characteristic positivity. They do not construct
a group-scheme Frobenius kernel or a convolution algebra on its dual.
They do not produce a Steinberg vector, identify an entire induced space,
or prove the host big-cell and Steinberg statement.
