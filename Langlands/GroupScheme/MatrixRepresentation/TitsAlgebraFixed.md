# The Tits algebra is the full twisted-fixed algebra

This short internal module compares two `k`-subalgebras of `M_n(K)` attached
to a representation `ρ` of `G_K`. Assume that `ρ` is irreducible, that every
self-intertwiner of `ρ` is scalar, and that for every `σ` an invertible
matrix `P σ` intertwines `ρ` with its Galois twist `ρ^σ` (with `K/k` Galois).
Then the two algebras coincide:

- `titsAlgebra ρ`, generated by the coefficient matrices of `ρ` (module
  `MatrixRepresentation/TitsAlgebra`);
- `twistedFixed P`, the matrices `X` with `P σ⁻¹ σ(X) P σ = X` for all `σ`
  (module `Algebra/GaloisTwistedMatrix`).

The dimension bound `dim_k twistedFixed P ≤ n²` uses only the common fixed
equations, for any family of units `P σ`. In the comparison setting the maps
`X ↦ P σ⁻¹ σ(X) P σ` form a semilinear Galois action on `M_n(K)`, because they
fix the `k`-algebra `titsAlgebra ρ`, which spans `M_n(K)` over `K`. So
`twistedFixed P` is the usual fixed algebra of a semilinear action, the
descent description motivated by the proof of Theorem 3.3 of Tits,
*Représentations linéaires irréductibles d'un groupe réductif sur un corps
quelconque*, J. reine angew. Math. 247 (1971), pp. 203–204. The equality
itself is an internal consequence of the coefficient and dimension lemmas.

## Declarations

- `GaloisTwistedMatrix.finrank_twistedFixed_le`: for a Galois extension `K/k`
  (possibly infinite) and invertible matrices `P σ`, the twisted-fixed algebra
  has dimension at most `n²` over `k`. A `k`-basis of it is `K`-linearly
  independent in `M_n(K)` (`linearIndependent_of_le_twistedFixed`), so it has at
  most `dim_K M_n(K)` elements.
- `titsAlgebra_eq_twistedFixed`: let `ρ` be irreducible with scalar
  self-intertwiners, and let `P σ` be invertible intertwiners from `ρ` to `ρ^σ`.
  Then `titsAlgebra ρ = twistedFixed P`. The Tits algebra is contained in the
  twisted-fixed algebra (`titsAlgebra_le_twistedFixed`) and has dimension `n²`
  (`finrank_titsAlgebra`). By the previous bound the inclusion is an equality.

- `TitsAlgebraEqTwistedFixedStatement` packages `titsAlgebra_eq_twistedFixed` as a
  proposition. It quantifies over all Galois extensions `K/k` in one universe,
  all affine `G`, and all representations on `Kⁿ` with `n : ℕ`. It is proved
  by `titsAlgebraEqTwistedFixed`.

No continuity of `σ ↦ P σ`, cocycle identity or finiteness of `K/k` is
assumed.
