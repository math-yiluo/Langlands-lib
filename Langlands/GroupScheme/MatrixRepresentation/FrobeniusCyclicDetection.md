# Cyclic detection by the actual top Frobenius distribution

This derived unit first treats any algebra representation on a vector space.
Evaluation at a vector gives a linear cyclic map. Its kernel is a left ideal
because the representation preserves multiplication in the original factor
order. If an algebra element belongs to every nonzero left ideal and acts
nontrivially on the vector, this kernel must be zero.

For the complete actual Frobenius distribution algebra, the preceding socle
detection theorem supplies exactly this left-ideal condition for the normalized
top distribution. The existing algebra homomorphism to endomorphisms is the
original distribution action. Removing its convolution wrapper therefore proves
injectivity of the full distribution orbit map at any vector with nonzero top
action. No distribution is replaced by its operator image.

The intrinsic origin orbit span, defined by all scalar contractions of origin
points over every commutative test algebra, is already proved to be the range
of this orbit map. Injectivity identifies its dimension with that of the full
dual of the actual quotient. The pointed polynomial coordinate equivalence
identifies the quotient dimension with `(p^r)^d`. Thus the entire origin orbit
span has precisely this dimension. If the entire original representation has
dimension at most this number, the span equals that whole representation.

The provenance is the original convolution action, the proved socle detection
result, and ordinary finite dimensional linear algebra. All eigen-coordinate,
split-torus, positive-height, nonzero-top-action and whole-space dimension-bound
hypotheses remain explicit. The unit constructs neither a Steinberg vector with
nonzero top action nor an upper bound for the entire covariant induced space.
It does not establish the general Steinberg big-cell statement.
