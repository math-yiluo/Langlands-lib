# Action algebras of supplied additive-group families

This derived unit concerns an actual matrix representation `ρ` of a group
scheme `G` over a field `k`, with a supplied family
`f i : VectorGroup.groupScheme (CommRingCat.of k) 1 ⟶ G`. The family index
type can be arbitrary, including empty. The finite coordinate type can also
be empty. No morphism, generation hypothesis or representation is asserted
to exist.

Write `D(i,r)` for
`AdditiveRestrictedOperators.pPowerOperator (ρ.restrict (f i)) p r`.
`coefficientAlgebra ρ f p` is the unital `k`-subalgebra of
`Module.End k (n → k)` generated by all `D(i,r)`. Its definition and
`coefficientAlgebra_le_iff` require only a natural number `p`.

Assume now that `p` is prime and `k` has characteristic `p`.
`invariant_coefficientAlgebra_iff` identifies invariance under this algebra
with `IsStableSubspace` under every restriction `ρ.restrict (f i)`.
The latter uses all affine test algebras, including nonreduced ones, and
every ground-field linear functional on those algebras. There is no
infinitude assumption in this comparison. The proof forms the subalgebra
of all endomorphisms preserving the subspace and uses
`AdditiveInvariantSubspaces.isStableSubspace_iff_invariant_pPowerOperator`.

`pointOperator_eq_rationalAction` identifies the affine base-field point
operator of a restriction with the ambient rational action after composition
with the supplied morphism. This identification uses
`FlagCore.baseAffineTestIso`, `rationalMatrix_baseAffineTestIso` and
`MatrixRepresentation.rationalMatrix_restrict`; it requires neither prime
characteristic nor infinitude.

With the additional hypothesis `Infinite k`,
`rationalAction_comp_mem_coefficientAlgebra` puts the rational actions of
each supplied source in the coefficient algebra. It uses the earlier
single-additive-group equality
`adjoin_range_pointOperator_eq_adjoin_range_pPowerOperator`.
The main algebra comparison is
`coefficientAlgebra_eq_adjoin_generatedRationalAction`: the coefficient
algebra equals the algebra generated by the actual rational actions of
the existing subgroup
`LeviGluing.generatedRationalPoints G (fun _ => Ga) f`.
One inclusion follows from each source's point-operator comparison and the
fact that its point image lies in this subgroup. For the other, the
implemented proof uses `Subgroup.closure_induction''`. An inverse generator
is still in the same source-image subgroup, so it is again a source action.
The unit and multiplication steps use the representation laws and
subalgebra closure.

Assume also `IsReduced G.X.left` and explicitly assume that the generated
rational points form a `HasDenseRationalPointFamily`.
`isStableSubspace_iff_invariant_coefficientAlgebra` then identifies ambient
all-test stability with invariance under the coefficient algebra.
`isStableSubspace_iff_forall_restrict` gives the equivalent criterion of
stability under every supplied restriction. The forward implication is
restriction of the affine matrix identity. The reverse implication uses
the algebra equality and
`MatrixRepresentation.isStableSubspace_of_denseRationalFamily`, which
recovers stability on every affine test from dense rational matrices.
Reducedness here is a hypothesis on the ambient group, not a restriction
on its test algebras.

Finally, over an algebraically closed field, with `G` affine and locally of
finite type, `hasDenseRationalPointFamily_of_isGeneratedByFamily` derives
the required density from the existing all-test geometric predicate
`IsGeneratedByFamily (fun _ => Ga) f (𝟙 G)`. The additive sources are smooth,
hence reduced and locally of finite type, so the proof applies
`LeviGluing.dense_rationalPointImage_generatedRationalPoints`. Each
rational-point location in that dense image lies in the union of the point
images defining `HasDenseRationalPointFamily`.
Adding reducedness of `G` gives
`isStableSubspace_iff_forall_restrict_of_isGeneratedByFamily`.

The provenance is internal and derived: these are algebra-generation and
stability comparisons assembled from the named preceding Library results.
The unit proves no general existence of root subgroups, generating
families, highest-weight modules or Steinberg modules. The general
`SteinbergBigCellStatement` remains a separate proof obligation.
