# Orbit weight bounds for a dynamic-parabolic eigenline

This internal derived development combines the published dynamic general-linear
matrix description, the opposite open cell theorem and geometric integrality
of smooth connected affine groups. It adds no external-source assertion.

Let G be a smooth connected affine group over an arbitrary field. Suppose a
cocharacter acts diagonally in an actual finite matrix representation, with
integer coordinate weights e, and its actual dynamic parabolic preserves the
coordinate line j. Every vector in the G-orbit of that coordinate vector has
zero coordinate a whenever e(a) is strictly greater than e(j). The conclusion
holds over every relative scheme test, including nonreduced tests.

The opposite dynamic unipotent has identity entries whenever the row weight
is at least the column weight. On its product with the parabolic, the selected
column is therefore zero strictly above its original weight. The published
opposite cell is a nonempty open subscheme of the geometrically integral
ambient group and is dense. Equality with zero of each offending coordinate
morphism extends across that dense morphism because the source is reduced and
affine space is separated. Pullback from the universal group test proves the
claimed all-test bound.

This bound does not by itself prove positivity of the line character. A Weyl
symmetry argument and the properness of its exact line stabilizer remain to be
used for that purpose. No adelic chamber coverage is asserted.
