# Perfect convolution evaluation on the actual Frobenius quotient dual

This derived unit works with the genuine Frobenius-origin coordinate quotient
of an affine group scheme. Given pointed polynomial eigen-coordinates with
positive cocharacter heights, the class of the top retained monomial is a
coordinate integral: multiplication by any coordinate equals multiplication
by its identity value. The proof uses the polynomial box quotient and shows
coefficient by coefficient that multiplying the top monomial retains just the
constant coefficient. Its pairing with the published top distribution is 1.

The previously constructed top distribution is a left convolution integral.
The generic normalized-integral transform therefore equals the actual quotient
antipode. The quotient antipode is involutive, so convolution followed by
evaluation on the top coordinate has zero left radical on the entire quotient
dual. The polynomial box makes this dual finite dimensional; the standard
finite dimensional bilinear form theorem supplies zero right radical too.
The resulting bilinear form is thus nondegenerate on both sides.

The final existence theorem obtains the required eigen-coordinates from
smoothness, local finite type, and strictly positive actual augmentation
weights under a split torus automorphic action. Its witnesses and convolution
use the actual coordinate quotient and the original group coproduct. No
cocommutativity or commutativity of the distribution algebra is assumed.

The construction is derived from the already developed polynomial quotient,
top integral, and Hopf structure; it invokes no new literature claim. It does
not prove the Steinberg dimension bound or irreducibility of an entire induced
space, and does not complete the big-cell statement.
