# Irreducibility from a unipotent fixed line and a spanning orbit

This internal derived companion concerns an actual supplied finite matrix
representation of an affine group scheme over a field. A supplied morphism
from a unipotent group scheme specifies the subgroup action; neither
closedness nor injectivity of that morphism is required.

The defined type `UnipotentCyclicVector` records a supplied nonzero
vector, unipotence of that source group, equality of the entire
subgroup-fixed space with its span, and equality of its rational-orbit
span with the entire coordinate space. It has no automatic inhabitant.
Its two irreducibility theorems certify the original representation
under the additional hypotheses described below.

Every nonzero subspace stable in the existing all-affine-test sense
contains a nonzero vector fixed by the unipotent group on every original
relative scheme test. The proof chooses finite split coordinates for the
subspace and restricts the actual representation in those coordinates to
the supplied unipotent group. The defining unipotence property gives a
nonzero fixed vector there. The split inclusion is injective, has image
exactly the chosen subspace, and preserves fixedness on every relative
scheme test. Affineness is required only for the ambient group in this
argument.

Now suppose the entire subgroup-fixed space is the line generated by a
specified nonzero vector, and the span of its actual rational-point orbit
is the entire coordinate space. Every nonzero stable subspace contains a
nonzero scalar multiple of that vector by the previous argument. Scaling
back gives the vector itself, and stability gives its whole rational
orbit. The spanning hypothesis makes the subspace the entire space.
This proves the existing all-test `MatrixRepresentation.IsIrreducible`
condition without a characteristic or algebraic-closedness assumption.

If the field is algebraically closed and the ambient group is reduced
and locally of finite type, the published characteristic-free rational
stability detection theorem turns every rational subrepresentation into
an all-affine-test stable subspace. The preceding result then proves
`rationalRepresentation.IsIrreducible` for the actual rational-point
representation on the entire coordinate space.

These are internal consequences of split-coordinate restriction,
unipotence, linear span and rational-point detection. No external
literature is invoked. The group, unipotent morphism, representation,
one-dimensional entire fixed space and orbit-spanning equality all
remain supplied hypotheses. This file constructs no Steinberg or
highest-weight representation and proves no existence or cofinal
irreducibility theorem for covariant function spaces.
