# Diagonal representations of height-one groups

Consider a finite affine algebraic group over a field of characteristic
`p > 1` whose coordinate functions have `p`th powers equal to the `p`th
powers of their values at the identity. A closed representation with all
actual tangent matrices diagonal has a diagonal universal matrix. It factors
as a closed subgroup of a split torus, so the group is of multiplicative type.

The diagonal coordinates span the cotangent at the identity. A functional
annihilating them would give a tangent point with zero matrix and would
vanish by faithfulness. Choose a cotangent basis from those coordinates.
The corresponding diagonal functions generate the algebra by cotangent
surjectivity and Nakayama's lemma. Their dual tangent points induce Euler
derivations on these generators. Every entry in a row has the same weight
for each derivation as the diagonal entry in that row. Dividing an
off-diagonal entry by its diagonal unit gives an element in the common
kernel of the derivations, hence a scalar. Its augmentation vanishes, so
the original entry is zero. The published universal diagonal factorization
then gives the actual closed immersion in the split diagonal torus.

This derived step supplies the height-one part of the multiplicative-type
argument of Demazure and Gabriel, *Groupes algébriques I* (1970), IV §3,
Lemma 3.7, printed pp. 510–511. It works with the actual affine coordinate
algebra, including its nilpotent elements.
