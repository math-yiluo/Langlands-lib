# Matrices out of finite direct sums

This internal linear algebra construction concatenates a finite dependent
family of matrices with common target. Its action sums the component images,
and its range is the supremum of the component ranges. Independent ranges and
injective component maps imply injectivity; spanning ranges imply surjectivity.

The matrix multiplication formulas prove equivariance of concatenated actual
intertwiners. The affine matrices of an extended direct sum are the block
diagonal matrices of its extended factors, by the affine base-change formula.
The identity matrix therefore intertwines the sum of the extensions with the
extension of the sum.

These are derived consequences of the published actual representation
interfaces and finite linear algebra. They support the descent assembly for
Task 7109 and assert no highest-weight classification or absolute simplicity.
