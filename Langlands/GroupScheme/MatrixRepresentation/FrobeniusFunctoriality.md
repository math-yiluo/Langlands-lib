# Functoriality of actual Frobenius quotients and distributions

Let `k : Type u` be a field of exponential characteristic `p`, let `r` be a
natural number, and let `G`, `H` and `J` be group schemes over `Spec k` whose
structure morphisms are affine. This module derives functoriality of the actual
Frobenius-origin quotient and its full convolution dual from an actual group
homomorphism `f : G ⟶ H`. It uses neither polynomial coordinates nor finiteness
of the quotient. It is internal derived infrastructure; it attributes no new
assertion to original literature.

For an actual group `G`, write `C(G)` for `CharacterUnits.Coord G` and `Q(G)` for
`FrobeniusCoordinateModel.quotientAlgebra G p r`. The latter is the ordinary
quotient of `C(G)` by the ideal generated by the `p^r` powers of its augmentation
elements. Its coproduct and counit are inherited from the original group as
proved in `FrobeniusDistributionAlgebra`. The convolution algebra is exactly
`DistributionAlgebra (G := G) p r`, the full `k`-linear dual of `Q(G)` with the
`WithConv` wrapper.

`isOriginPoint_comp_hom` proves that every actual origin point over every
`A : CommAlgCat.{u} k` maps to an actual origin point of `H`. Indeed the original
coordinate pullback `Lie.restrictionMap f` preserves evaluation at the identity.
An augmentation coordinate therefore pulls back to an augmentation coordinate,
so its evaluation at an origin point has vanishing `p^r` power. This argument
allows nonreduced test algebras and uses no characteristic assumption.

`quotientPullback p r f : Q(H) →ₐ[k] Q(G)` is the unique factor of the coordinate
map of the image of the genuine quotient point of `G`. On a quotient
representative it sends the class of `a` to the class of
`Lie.restrictionMap f a`. `affineMap_quotientPullback` identifies the induced
map of actual quotient points with composition by `f`. The coordinate pullback
preserves identity and reverses composition; quotient representative
surjectivity gives the same identity and contravariant composition laws for
`quotientPullback`.

`comul_restrictionMap` proves naturality of the genuine coordinate coproduct.
The proof uses injectivity of the equivalence between actual affine points and
coordinate maps: composing the coordinate coproduct multiplies the two points,
and the original group homomorphism preserves that multiplication. The quotient
counit and coproduct formulas then show that `quotientPullback` preserves both
structure maps. The coproduct proof checks representatives, composes tensor
maps and uses the quotient pullback formula in each factor. `quotientBialgHom`
packages precisely this algebra map as a bialgebra homomorphism.

`distributionPushforward p r f` is the unital algebra homomorphism from the
convolution dual of `Q(G)` to that of `Q(H)` given by precomposition with
`quotientPullback`. Its value at `ell` and `a` is exactly
`ell (quotientPullback p r f a)`. Preservation of the counit proves its unit
law; the coalgebra-homomorphism convolution identity proves its product law.
Precomposition and the quotient laws give identity and covariant composition
laws for `distributionPushforward`.

For a finite decidable index type `n : Type` and an original matrix
representation `ρ` of `H`, `coefficientMatrix_restrict` states that each quotient
coefficient of `ρ.restrict f` is the pullback of the corresponding coefficient
of `ρ`. It follows from naturality of the original representation matrix and
the actual quotient-point identity.
`actionAlgHom_distributionPushforward` consequently identifies the action of
any pushed distribution on `ρ` with the action of the original distribution on
`ρ.restrict f`. The proof checks contracted matrix entries and then uses the
same column-vector action in both expressions.

This unit does not prove injectivity or surjectivity of either functorial map.
It constructs no ordered distribution basis, top distribution, Steinberg module
or bound on an entire induced representation. It proves neither the structural
existence statements nor the irreducibility conclusion of the general
`SteinbergBigCellStatement`.
