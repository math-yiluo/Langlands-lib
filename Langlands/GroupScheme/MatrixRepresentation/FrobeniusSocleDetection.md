# Socle detection in the actual Frobenius distribution algebra

This derived unit combines actual augmentation nilpotence and the perfect
convolution pairing. The augmentation socle consists of all distributions killed
on the left by the augmentation ideal. Subtracting the scalar augmentation from
any distribution shows that a socle element is a left convolution integral.

The right nondegeneracy of convolution evaluation on the top coordinate implies
that every such integral is its value on that coordinate times the top
distribution. Indeed, their difference pairs to zero against every left factor;
the normalization of the top coordinate and top distribution makes the two
values agree exactly.

An elementary induction on a nilpotence exponent shows that every nonzero left
ideal contains a nonzero element killed by the augmentation ideal. If a current
nonzero element is not already killed, multiply it by a suitable augmentation
element and decrease the exponent. The preceding uniqueness theorem then shows
that every nonzero left ideal contains the top distribution itself.

All constructions use the complete actual Frobenius distribution algebra and its
original convolution. The eigen-coordinate, split torus and positive-height
hypotheses remain explicit. The argument is derived from the existing integral,
pairing and nilpotence results, with no additional literature claim. It does not
supply a Steinberg module, a nonzero top action on it, or the dimension bound
for the entire covariant induced space.
