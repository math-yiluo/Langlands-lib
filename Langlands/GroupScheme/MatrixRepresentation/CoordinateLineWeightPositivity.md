# Positive actual line-character integers

This internal derived result removes the diagonal-basis premise from the
geometric proper-parabolic eigenline positivity theorem.

`IsCoordinateLineBasis B a j` means the a-th column of the invertible rational
matrix B is the original j-th coordinate vector. `basisConjugate rho B` is the
actual representation obtained by conjugation with the rational general-linear
point of B inverse. Its matrix on every scheme test is B inverse times the
original matrix times B, after scalar extension of the same rational matrix.

The adapted column identity holds after every scalar extension. Multiplication
by B therefore identifies the scalar-action equations on the two coordinate
vectors, with the same scalar. Invertibility of B proves both implications.
Thus the new representation preserves its selected line if and only if the
original representation preserves the original line, on all scheme tests.
This applies to restriction to any subgroup as well as to the whole group.

The defining cocharacter of an actual represented dynamic parabolic factors
through that parabolic. Hence a parabolic coordinate eigenline is also a
coordinate eigenline for the cocharacter. The final theorem states this
cocharacter preservation as an explicit argument; the preceding theorem
constructs it from the parabolic preservation assumption.

Suppose G is smooth, connected and affine over an arbitrary field, the actual
dynamic parabolic and opposite unipotent of chi have the supplied
representatives, an actual rational point reverses chi, and the parabolic
preserves the chosen coordinate line while the whole group does not. The
prescribed-line diagonalization constructs an adapted matrix B, a new selected
coordinate a and diagonal weights e, with e(a) exactly the integer of the
original algebraic line character. The scalar-action equivalence transports
both parabolic line preservation and whole-group nonpreservation. The
geometric diagonal-basis positivity theorem gives e(a)>0, hence strict
positivity of the original actual line-character integer.

The representation need not be irreducible or faithful and the ambient group
need not be split. This result retains the actual reversing-point hypothesis.
Identification with a maximal-split-torus lattice pairing, the rank-one root
character relation, height estimates and adelic chamber coverage remain
separate obligations.
