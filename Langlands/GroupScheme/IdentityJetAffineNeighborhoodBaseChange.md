# Affine coordinates for the pulled-back unit neighborhood

Let `S`, `W`, and `T` be affine schemes. For a group `G` over `S`, take an
open immersion `W → G` through which its actual unit factors and an
arbitrary test `T → S`. The pullback of the neighborhood to `T` is affine.
The tensor comparison identifies the section augmentation on that
pullback with scalar extension of the original augmentation. The formula
follows from the two pullback projections and their composites with the
lifted unit.

When `G → S` is separated and locally of finite presentation, the
base-changed group satisfies the fixed-rank unit condition exactly when
its scalar-extended neighborhood satisfies the affine section condition.
On the scheme side the actual first-jet projection must be flat of rank
`1 + r` and the unit stalk maps formally smooth. On the ring side the
section localizations must be formally smooth and the conormal must have
dimension `r` over every base residue field.

For finitely presented augmented algebras, an augmentation-preserving
algebra equivalence transports a smooth principal neighborhood of the
section and the residue conormal dimensions. Scalar cancellation
identifies the actual augmentations for direct and iterated extension.

This is the affine-test comparison for the closed charts in the
smooth-section locus of SGA 3, Exposé XV, Lemma 5.4 under these hypotheses.
