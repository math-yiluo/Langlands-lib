# The diagonal torus and odd standard weights

The diagonal torus is the existing split even diagonal torus followed by
the determinant-corrected even inclusion into the actual odd norm-one
group. Its scheme map is a monomorphism, so it is a closed split subtorus.

On every affine coefficient algebra, including nonreduced ones, its
standard matrix is diagonal with entries `1,t_a,t_a⁻¹`. The correction on
the axis is one because the paired even diagonal entries have determinant
one over every commutative ring. The integral weight coordinates are
`0,e_a,-e_a`; the module records their exact finite-product character
formulas and evaluations at a single variable unit. A vector of the actual
Lie module belongs to a torus weight space precisely when each nonzero
entry of its differential matrix has that weight difference. Evaluating
at a universal Laurent unit proves an equality of integral character
coordinates in every characteristic, avoiding reduction of weights
modulo the characteristic.

This is an internal construction and computation using the existing even
diagonal torus and the actual odd matrix representation. It asserts no
maximality or root-system classification. The field universe is arbitrary,
and no characteristic restriction is imposed.
