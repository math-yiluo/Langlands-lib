# The actual faithful odd representation and its Lie coordinates

The scalar tensor matrix chart yields natural invertible matrices for every
actual test-scheme point of the odd norm-one group. Yoneda constructs an
actual group-scheme homomorphism to the general linear group. The map is
a monomorphism, and its all-test and affine coefficient matrices agree
with the previously constructed ambient and affine matrices, including
nonreduced coefficient algebras.

The differential of this actual representation is the Library linear map
on its dual-number Lie module. Its matrix agrees with the unique epsilon
coefficient already constructed in `TangentEquivalence`. Injectivity of
the representation gives injectivity of the differential; the explicit
normalized tangent points give its full image, exactly the submodule
`lieMatrices` of alternating linear defect and trace zero. These facts
produce a linear equivalence, compatible with the Library's actual
epsilon-scaling module structure, rather than only the previous group
equivalence on raw tangent points.

This is an internal Yoneda construction and comparison with the published
representation differential. There is no new literature translation or
root-datum, connectedness, or reductivity assertion. The field universe
is arbitrary, and no characteristic restriction is imposed.
