# Invariant subspaces of the odd standard column representation

For at least two hyperbolic indices, every nonzero subspace stable under
the embedded even Levi and alternating upper/lower generators and the
signed short matrices contains the full hyperbolic coordinate hyperplane.
The result holds over every field, including characteristic two, where
this hyperplane may be a proper invariant subspace.

Intersect the subspace with the hyperbolic hyperplane. If that intersection
were zero, subtracting any even upper or lower action from a nonzero
vector would vanish, since it lies in the intersection. Alternating
single-pair matrices then force every hyperbolic coordinate of that vector
to vanish. A short action minus the vector now forces its axis coordinate
to vanish as well, a contradiction. The intersection is therefore nonzero.
It is stable under the even generators, so the published irreducibility
lemma for the even standard representation makes it the entire hyperplane.

This is an internal linear-algebra lemma intended for controlling fixed
vectors of unipotent normal subgroups. It makes no group reductivity or
connectedness assertion. It uses neither a characteristic restriction nor
division by two and translates no new external literature.
