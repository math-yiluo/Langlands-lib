# The determinant-corrected even orthogonal inclusion

Every split even orthogonal matrix `g` acts on the odd split space as
`diag(det g,g)`. The polar relation and the involutivity of the even polar
matrix imply `(det g)² = 1` over every commutative coefficient ring. Hence
the block matrix has determinant one and preserves `z² + ∑ᵢ xᵢyᵢ` modulo
alternating matrices. This includes characteristic two and nonreduced rings.

The block formula is multiplicative and commutes with all coefficient-ring
maps. Through the scalar tensor chart and the existing even orthogonal
points equivalence it therefore constructs an actual morphism of group
schemes. The all-test alternating-defect and norm-one criterion factors it
through the actual odd norm-one group; its composite with the ambient
inclusion is exactly the prescribed block morphism.
Recovering the original matrix from the hyperbolic block proves monomorphy
of the ambient map and of its norm-one factor.

This internal construction is intended as a smooth source factor for the
odd tangent-surjectivity argument. It uses the published even orthogonal
polar equation and the scalar tensor chart, with no new literature
translation and no smoothness or reductivity assertion for the odd group.
