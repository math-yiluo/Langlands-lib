The split type-B vector map now has the full actual odd norm-one
isometry group as its target. It is a central isogeny, its torus square
commutes with the original diagonal torus, and its kernel on every test
scheme is exactly that of the earlier identity-component map.

The construction composes that map with the actual identity-component
inclusion isomorphism, established by full-group connectedness. The
published isomorphism transport theorem preserves finite flat
surjectivity and centrality of the full kernel. Cancellation by the
isomorphism proves the arbitrary-test kernel equivalence directly.
The original torus map and its root-lattice character image remain the
data of the supplied vector-isogeny package.

This is an internal actual-group transport argument over every field
and characteristic. Descent from a nonsplit type-B group is not done
here; it is carried out in `SimplyConnected.TypeBVectorQuotient`. No new
external translation is used.
