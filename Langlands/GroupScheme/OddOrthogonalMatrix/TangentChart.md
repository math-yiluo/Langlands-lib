# A smooth source chart for the odd identity tangents

The source is the actual finite product of the split even orthogonal group
and one additive group for each signed hyperbolic coordinate. Its factors
are enumerated with the same finite list used in the tangent factorization.
Each factor is smooth over the arbitrary ground field, hence the product
is smooth. The chart is the actual ordered multiplication of their even
and short images; it preserves the identity point and need not be a group
homomorphism.

For a coefficient matrix satisfying the linearized odd equations, the
actual normalized even tangent point and the short epsilon points assemble
into an actual normalized dual-number point of the source. Evaluation of
the chart equals the original odd tangent point by `TangentDecomposition`.
Consequently every actual identity tangent point in the Library reduction
kernel lifts to an actual identity tangent point of this smooth source.

The universe lift of the finite product index permits fields in every
universe. No smoothness of the target, characteristic restriction,
connectedness, or reductivity is assumed or asserted here. This is an
internal finite-product construction from the preceding actual point
factorization and the published smoothness of the even orthogonal and
additive groups; no new external source translation is used.
