# Trivial matrix centralizer of the odd torus and short families

A matrix over a field commuting with the odd diagonal torus over every
coefficient algebra is diagonal. If it also commutes with every short
parameter-one matrix, it is scalar. A scalar matrix satisfying the odd
quadratic isometry equations and determinant one is the identity.
Consequently these torus and short families have trivial centralizer
inside the odd norm-one matrix equations.

The torus argument tests each coordinate on the universal Laurent unit.
A nonzero matrix coefficient equates two Laurent exponents, so distinct
integral weights force off-diagonal coefficients to vanish. The entry
one from the axis column of each short matrix then equates its diagonal
coordinates, even in characteristic two. For a scalar `c`, the axis
quadratic equation gives `c^2 = 1`; the odd determinant equation gives
`c = 1`. This final scalar argument works over every commutative ring.

These are internal polynomial and integral-weight calculations, with
no new external translation or assertion of group connectedness.
