# Maximality of the actual odd diagonal torus

The actual Lie matrices contain every embedded even orthogonal coefficient
and every short coefficient in all characteristics. Their paired even
entries obey the usual swap identities, and the axis diagonal vanishes.

The zero weight space of the actual diagonal torus consists of diagonal
admissible matrices. The hyperbolic diagonal coordinates give a linear
bijection from this weight space to `K^l`: the opposite entries are their
negatives and the axis entry is zero. Thus the zero weight dimension is
exactly the rank of the split torus. The published Lie-dimension criterion
makes the actual torus maximal in the identity component of the smooth
odd norm-one group. Factoring the torus through that component leaves its
actual roots unchanged.

The nonzero actual adjoint weights are then computed exactly as `±e_a`
and `±e_a±e_b` with distinct hyperbolic indices. For a vector in a nonzero torus weight space, each nonzero matrix entry
identifies that weight with one of these characters. The forbidden double
short-root entries vanish.
Conversely, embedded even root matrices and the actual short coefficient
matrices realize every listed nonzero weight. The latter have a coefficient
one even in characteristic two, so no short weight disappears there.

The file uses integral weights `0,e_a,-e_a`, not their values in the field.
It does not assert reductivity or identify coroots. These
are internal matrix and Lie-dimension arguments from the preceding actual
representation and torus computations; no new literature translation is
used. The field universe and characteristic remain unrestricted.
