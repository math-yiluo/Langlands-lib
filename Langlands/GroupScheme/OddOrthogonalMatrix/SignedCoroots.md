# Signed integral coroots of the actual odd root datum

For any actual root datum of the identity torus, the coroot of a signed
coordinate short root is twice the same signed coordinate cocharacter.
The coroot of a signed long root is the corresponding difference of
signed coordinate cocharacters. The final theorem records the complete
root and coroot alternatives together.

The sum-root formula follows by reflecting a coordinate difference in a
short coordinate root. Its root pairing is minus two and the reverse
pairing is minus one. The root-datum coreflection therefore gives the
sum cocharacter. Opposite root indices have opposite coroots, which
supplies all remaining signs. The exact actual root set then exhausts
the alternatives.

These are internal consequences of the integral reflection axioms and
the previously computed actual roots and positive coroots. No field
characteristic restriction or evaluation of integral coordinates in the
field is used. No new external translation is asserted.
