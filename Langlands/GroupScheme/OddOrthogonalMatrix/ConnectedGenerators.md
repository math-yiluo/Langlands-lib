# Connected elementary images and fixed-vector stability

A constant even orthogonal point maps to the odd group by its
`diag(det g,g)` block. A signed short point with parameter one has its
explicit short matrix on every test scheme. If the even constant point
lies in the even identity component, its image lies in the odd identity
component. On Levi and alternating upper/lower generators the even
determinant is one, so this block matrix is exactly the axis-fixing even
action used in the invariant-subspace lemma. Every short point also belongs to the odd identity component,
since it comes from a connected additive group.

The file also proves the fixed-subspace stability argument used for
normal subgroups: a family of actual conjugating points whose matrices
are scalar extensions of a given invertible matrix preserves the fixed
subspace of any subgroup it normalizes. The proof transports the
normality equation through the actual matrix representation and uses
matrix associativity and inversion on arbitrary test schemes.

These are internal consequences of the preceding even and short maps,
the published identity-component factorization of maps from connected
groups, and the all-test fixed-vector definition. No target-group
connectedness or reductivity is asserted. The field has no characteristic
restriction, and the argument translates no new external literature.
