# Geometric reductivity of the actual odd identity component

For at least two hyperbolic indices, the actual identity component of the
split odd norm-one group is connected reductive over every field.
Smoothness, affineness and connectedness are already established. To
prove the geometric unipotent radical trivial, the identity-component
base-change isomorphism and the actual split-model field-change
isomorphism identify its algebraic-closure base change with the identity
component of the odd model over that algebraic closure. Its unipotent
radical is trivial by the proved characteristic-uniform fixed-vector
argument. Transport along the actual isomorphisms gives the required
geometric condition.

This is an internal transport of the preceding pseudo-reductivity result.
It retains every field, including imperfect characteristic two. It does
not yet identify coroots or prove connectedness of the full norm-one
group, and it translates no new external source.
