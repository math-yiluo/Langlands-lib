# Actual additive short-parameter maps into the odd norm-one group

The polynomial families in `ShortMatrices` define natural group homomorphisms
on points of every test scheme. Their coefficient matrices are interpreted
through the scalar tensor chart of `ScalarMatrixCoordinates`; this uses the
relative global sections of the test and allows nilpotents. Yoneda gives an
actual morphism from the one-dimensional vector group into the ambient
matrix-unit group.

The quadratic polynomial identity implies alternating matrix defect: testing
a quadratic expression on the coordinate vectors gives zero diagonal and
testing on their pairwise sums gives skew symmetry. The determinant identity
then puts the entire actual map in the norm-one isometry subgroup by its
all-test range criterion. Monomorphy of the ambient inclusion supplies the
group laws for the factor `shortMap`, and its composite is the prescribed
ambient polynomial morphism.
The matrix entry recovering the parameter proves that the ambient map is
a monomorphism. Its norm-one factor is consequently also a monomorphism.

This is internal transport of the explicit polynomial matrix families and
the published norm-one kernel criterion. No new literature translation is
used. These actual additive maps are constructed in every characteristic;
the identification with short roots and reductivity remain separate work.
