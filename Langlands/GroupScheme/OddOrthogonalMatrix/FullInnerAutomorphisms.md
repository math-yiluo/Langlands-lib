# Unique original-field conjugators for the full odd model

Actual rational points of the full split odd norm-one group identify
multiplicatively with all its group-scheme automorphisms by inner
conjugation. Every automorphism has a unique conjugating point over
the original field, for at least two hyperbolic indices and in every
characteristic.

The proof transports the preceding identity-component equivalence
through its actual inclusion isomorphism. A point equivalence and the
standard categorical automorphism equivalence record the two transport
steps. The compatibility of inner automorphisms with group homomorphisms
proves that the composite is precisely inner conjugation on the full
group. The inverse equivalence therefore supplies the unique point.

These are internal actual-isomorphism transport arguments, with no
new external translation or field-extension premise.

Point transport along the group-scheme isomorphism uses `GroupScheme.isoPointsEquiv`.
