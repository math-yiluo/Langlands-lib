# Semisimplicity of the actual odd identity component

The roots of the actual diagonal torus span the entire integral character
lattice: the short roots `e_a` already contain a basis. The same full-root-
span identity therefore holds for every actual root datum of the maximal
identity torus.

For at least two hyperbolic indices, the actual odd identity component is
connected reductive. The published root-datum existence theorem supplies
its finite reduced root datum, and the full integral root span makes the
character quotient trivial, hence finite. The published split
semisimplicity criterion gives an actual semisimple group over the
original arbitrary field.

This is an internal application of the computed actual roots and general
root-datum existence and semisimplicity interfaces. It does not yet
identify coroots, prove almost simplicity, or prove connectedness of the
full odd norm-one group, and it translates no new external source.
