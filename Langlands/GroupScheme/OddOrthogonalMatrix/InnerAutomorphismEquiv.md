# Original-field inner automorphisms of the actual type-B model

For a supplied numbered type-B pinning of rank at least two with full
integral root span,
inner conjugation is a multiplicative equivalence from its actual
rational points over the original field to all its group-scheme
automorphisms. Each automorphism therefore has a unique conjugating
point over that same field. In particular this applies to the actual
odd identity component. The full split odd norm-one group is adjoint,
with trivial full scheme-theoretic centre, over every field when it has
at least two hyperbolic indices.

The supporting general centre theorem puts the full centre in the
maximal torus. Every root evaluates to one on the universal centre
point, and full integral root generation makes every character evaluate
to one. The actual point-character equivalence makes that universal
point trivial, retaining all nilpotent tests. The identity map is thus
an actual adjoint quotient. The pinned automorphism decomposition and
triviality of based type-B automorphisms express every automorphism as
conjugation by an original-field point. Faithfulness of the adjoint
inner action gives uniqueness. The multiplicative equivalence packages
this bijective inner homomorphism.

The full odd group's adjointness transfers along the actual identity
component inclusion isomorphism. These are internal applications of
integral root generation, actual centres and the published pinned
automorphism theorem. They remove the earlier algebraically closed
premise by using triviality of the full centre. No new external
translation or characteristic restriction is used.
