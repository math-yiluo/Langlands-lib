# Evaluation of affine Hopf coordinates

This is a derived support construction for an actual affine group scheme `G` over `Spec R`, where `R` is a commutative ring in an arbitrary universe. It compares the existing coordinate constructions and evaluates their coordinate algebra on points over arbitrary test schemes. It is an elementary coordinate interface, not a proof of the positive-characteristic conjugation-map existence proposition.

For any algebra cogroup `C`, the imported `Unipotent.comul_cogroupCorepresentation` identifies the corepresented comultiplication with the algebra map underlying cogroup multiplication. The imported `Unipotent.counit_cogroupCorepresentation` identifies the counit with the cogroup unit. The two underlying maps of `cogroupRecoveryIso` are identities.

`AffineCoordinates.groupSchemeIso_hom_hom_hom` and its inverse counterpart identify the underlying corepresented coordinate maps with those of `coordinateCogroupIso`. The corresponding `hopfSpecIso` lemmas do the same for the Hopf-spectrum coordinates. Applying relative spectrum to the identity algebra map proves these comparisons. These equalities concern underlying morphisms over the base; the existing isomorphisms already preserve the full group structure.

`corepresentation_comul` and `corepresentation_counit` identify the linear maps of the coalgebra used for regular representations with those of `AffineCoordinates.hopfAlgebra G`. `corepresentation_coalgebra_eq` upgrades them to equality of the entire coalgebra structures. Its private record argument compares the two data fields; all coalgebra law fields are propositions, so proof irrelevance supplies equality of the law fields. The underlying algebra and module structures are those of the existing affine-coordinate construction.

`AffineCoordinates.pointAlgebraHom G T g` evaluates the chosen algebra on a point `g : T → G` for every scheme `T` over the base. Its codomain is the relative global-sections algebra `((algΓ R).obj T).unop`. It is obtained by transporting the point through `groupSchemeIso.inv`, applying `Corepresented.pointsEquiv`, and using the inverse corepresentation equivalence. `pointAlgebraHom_eq` identifies it with the algebra map from the relative spectrum adjunction.

`pointAlgebraHom_mul` says that evaluation at the product of two points is the composition of coordinate comultiplication with the tensor-product product map of their evaluations. `pointAlgebraHom_one` gives evaluation at the identity by the counit followed by the structural map from `R`. These follow from the group-valued point equivalence and the existing corepresented multiplication and identity equations. `pointAlgebraHom_naturality` says that a morphism of test schemes pulls every evaluated coordinate back by its relative global-sections map. No affineness or reducedness condition is imposed on the test schemes.

Over a field `k`, let `f : G → H` be an actual group-scheme morphism, with both structural maps affine. `AffineHomImageHopfAdapter.algSpec_map_pullback` identifies relative spectrum of the existing Hopf-coordinate pullback with `f` conjugated by the corepresented coordinate isomorphisms. The proof uses the Hopf-spectrum full-faithfulness equation and the comparisons above. `pointAlgebraHom_pullback` then proves that evaluation on the image point equals evaluation composed with this actual Hopf pullback. The proof uses naturality of the relative spectrum adjunction and cancels the coordinate isomorphism and its inverse.

Provenance is internal and derived: the module uses the existing coordinate constructions, the relative spectrum adjunction and categorical group laws. It invokes no external highest-weight or geometric-reductivity result. It constructs neither Steinberg modules nor their irreducibility, self-duality or exhaustion, and it asserts no finite-dimensionality or conjugation-map existence theorem.
