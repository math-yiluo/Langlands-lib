# Field base change of an actual Lie-element centralizer

`ElementCentralizerFieldBaseChangeStatement` identifies the all-test
centralizer of a tangent vector extended to a field with the field
base change of its centralizer functor. The ambient group is affine and
locally of finite type over the ground field.

On the spectrum of an algebra over the extension field, the two
conditions are the same adjoint equation. The kernel comparison sends
the extended tangent vector to its coefficient extension. Functoriality
of coefficient extension identifies the further extension to the test
algebra with the direct one. Both functors have closed affine
representatives, so the comparison on affine tests proves equality on
all tests.

This is a derived compatibility of the Library's actual tangent
centralizer and coefficient-extension definitions. It supplies the field
comparison used when a rational Lie vector is chosen by weak
approximation of maximal tori. See Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic
Groups and Number Theory* (1994), Theorem 2.18, printed pp. 104–105,
and Proposition 7.3, Corollary 3, printed p. 405.
