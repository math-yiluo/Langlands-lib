# The actual adjoint differential and the tangent bracket

Let `G` be affine over `Spec R` for a commutative ring `R`, and let `c : FiniteCoordinates G d` be a natural finite coordinate system for the actual tangent. `adjointDifferentialLieHom c` is the Lie algebra homomorphism into `Module.End R (Fin d → R)` obtained by differentiating the existing scheme adjoint representation and interpreting the resultant matrices as linear endomorphisms. Its underlying linear map is exactly `adjointDifferentialFamily c`.

Given a matrix representation `ρ` whose underlying over-scheme map is a monomorphism, the effect of this differential at `X` on the coordinates of `Y` is the coordinate vector of `⁅X, Y⁆`. To prove it, compare the two tangent vectors using the injective tangent map of `ρ`. Under this map the transported adjoint differential is the matrix commutator by the existing first-order conjugation computation in `AdjointDominance.lean`; the constructed bracket gives the same matrix commutator by `kernelDifferential_commutator`. This argument uses the actual tangent points in both comparisons. For affine finite-type groups over a field, the published affine linear embedding theorem supplies the faithful `ρ`, so the field result does not need one as an input. The finite coordinate system remains explicit in both results; smoothness and connectedness are not assumed.

**Source and scope.** Milne, *Algebraic Groups*, 2022 corrected reprint, §10d, 10.20–10.22 and equation (62), pp. 192–193, defines the bracket on the base-field Lie algebra by the differential of conjugation. The proof of Theorem 10.23 compares to the matrix commutator via a faithful embedding. This derived unit proves the adjoint-bracket comparison for the convolution construction. It does not prove the uniqueness assertion of Theorem 10.23, or bundle the action into a separate Lie algebra of derivations of the Lie bracket.
