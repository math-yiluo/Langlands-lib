# The quotient spectrum of the actual Lie-element centralizer

This internal derived construction uses the actual conjugation derivation and
its all-affine-test centralizer criterion from `Lie.ConjugationDerivation`, the
ideal quotient universal property, and the existing smooth-quotient theorem in
`CommutativeAlgebra.SemisimpleDerivation`. It makes no external literature claim.
It does not prove local semisimplicity for semisimple Lie elements, existence of a
centralizer group representative, or Cartan generation.

For any commutative base ring `R` and affine group scheme `G` over `Spec R`, let
`A` be the chosen coordinate algebra of `G`, and let `X` be an actual dual-number
identity-kernel point. `elementCentralizerQuotientAlgebra` is `A / I`, where `I`
is the ideal generated by values of the actual conjugation derivation.
`elementCentralizerQuotient` is its spectrum over `Spec R`.
`elementCentralizerQuotientι` is the quotient spectrum inclusion composed with
the chosen affine presentation isomorphism. These constructions have arbitrary
universe `u`; no reducedness, smoothness, field, or characteristic assumption is
needed here. The quotient is constructed as a scheme over the base, without
installing a group structure on it in this module.

`pointCoordinates_elementCentralizerQuotientι` identifies the coordinate map of
the inclusion with the quotient algebra map.
`elementCentralizerQuotientι_mem` applies the centralizer ideal criterion and the
kernel of the quotient map to show that this inclusion is an actual point of
the original centralizer subgroup functor. Conversely,
`exists_comp_elementCentralizerQuotientι` applies the ideal quotient lift to a
centralizing affine test point. Contravariance of `algSpec` gives its required
factorization. Thus these facts concern the original all-refinement centralizer
condition, including tests on algebras with nilpotents.

`isClosedImmersion_elementCentralizerQuotientι` follows from surjectivity of the
quotient map and composition with the affine presentation isomorphism. The
inclusion is consequently a monomorphism in the category over `Spec R`.

`exists_elementCentralizerQuotientIso` compares the quotient spectrum with any
affine group scheme `C` whose homomorphism `j : C → G` represents the actual
centralizer functor. Representability factors the quotient inclusion through
`C`. Applying the affine-test factorization to the universal point of `C`
constructs the inverse map. Monicity of both inclusions proves the two inverse
identities. The resulting isomorphism is over `Spec R` and commutes with the
inclusions into `G`. The variant
`exists_elementCentralizerQuotientIso_of_isClosedSubgroup` obtains affineness of
`C` from its closed inclusion into affine `G`.

Finally assume that the base is an algebraically closed field `k`, that `H` is
a smooth affine group scheme, and explicitly that its conjugation derivation
for `X` is locally semisimple as a linear operator on its coordinate algebra.
`smooth_elementCentralizerQuotient_of_locallySemisimple` transports smoothness
to the coordinate algebra, applies the existing smooth-image-ideal-quotient
theorem, and converts back to scheme smoothness. The equality between the
conjugation ideal and the derivation image ideal supplies the exact quotient
comparison. `smooth_elementCentralizer_of_locallySemisimple` then uses the
preceding isomorphism to prove smoothness of every closed group representative
of the centralizer. Algebraic closedness and the local semisimplicity premise
remain hypotheses of these final results. They include positive characteristic;
no dimension or connectedness hypothesis is introduced.
