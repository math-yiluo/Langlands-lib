# Functoriality of the affine tangent Lie bracket

Let `G` and `H` be affine group schemes over an arbitrary affine base `Spec R`, and let `A` be a commutative `R`-algebra. For `f : G ⟶ H`, `targetMapLieHom f A` is the Lie algebra homomorphism on the actual dual-number tangent modules. Its underlying linear map is exactly `targetMapLinear f A`, which postcomposes the given tangent point with `f`. The identity and composition laws follow from the same laws for these point maps.

`kernelCounitDerivationLinearEquiv_eq_snd` expresses a tangent as the `ε`-coefficient of the Hopf-coordinate algebra map classifying its dual-number point. This follows by comparing that map with the existing Lie-coordinate map via `lieCoordinateAlgEquiv`. Postcomposition of points corresponds to precomposition of these coefficients by the contravariant Hopf map `AffineHopfMorphism.pullback f`. Precomposition by this coalgebra homomorphism preserves convolution, hence its commutator. This proves `targetMapLinear_lie`.

For any map `a : A →ₐ[R] B` of commutative test algebras, the existing semilinear `lieKernelMapLinear G a` postcomposes the counit derivation with `a`. This preserves convolution by multiplicativity of `a`, proving `lieKernelMapLinear_lie`. Coefficient change commutes with the Lie differential, by the existing square on actual points (`targetMapLieHom_map`). No finiteness, smoothness, flatness, or reducedness is required. This is a naturality result, not a scalar-extension isomorphism.

**Source and provenance.** Milne, *Algebraic Groups*, 2022 corrected reprint, §10e, pp. 195–196, Proposition 10.29 and the paragraph after its proof, describes the field-valued Lie structure and its naturality in `G`. This derived unit proves the naturality for the convolution construction on the actual affine tangent at every test algebra and also gives preservation under change of test algebra. It does not formally identify the bracket with a bracket on invariant derivations or with a matrix commutator.
