# Matrix comparison for the affine tangent Lie bracket

Let `G` be affine over `Spec R` for a commutative ring `R`, and let `A` be a commutative `R`-algebra. For an actual representation `ρ : G ⟶ GL_n` with finite decidable matrix index type, `kernelDifferentialLieHom ρ A` upgrades the existing `kernelDifferential ρ A` to a Lie algebra homomorphism into `Matrix n n A` with its commutator bracket. No faithfulness, smoothness, flatness, characteristic or finiteness hypothesis on `G` is required beyond affineness.

To prove the comparison, evaluate the representation at the universal affine Hopf-coordinate point. Naturality identifies the matrix at a point classified by an algebra map with this universal matrix evaluated entrywise. The comultiplication on an entry `mᵢⱼ` is `Σₗ mᵢₗ ⊗ mₗⱼ`: this follows by multiplying the two universal points given by the tensor-product inclusions and using multiplicativity of the actual representation. The `ε`-coefficients of the matrix at the dual-number tangent point are therefore the values of its counit derivation on these entries. Applying convolution to an entry gives matrix multiplication in the same order, and subtracting the two ordered products gives `kernelDifferential_commutator`.

For the identity representation of `GL_n`, the tangent target map is the identity. Thus `GeneralLinear.DualNumber.pointKernelLieEquiv` is the existing linear `1 + εX` equivalence with a proof that it preserves the bracket. Its underlying linear equivalence is exactly `pointKernelLinearEquiv`. All comparisons hold on the actual group-scheme tangents, including on nonreduced test algebras. The file does not identify the differential of the adjoint representation with the Lie adjoint map.

**Source and provenance.** Milne, *Algebraic Groups*, 2022 corrected reprint, 10.7 and equation (57), p. 188, identifies the tangent of `GL_n` with matrices and defines the matrix commutator bracket; Example 10.3, p. 187, gives the associative-algebra bracket. The paragraph following Proposition 10.29, pp. 195–196, asks for the comparison with the invariant-derivation bracket at `GL_n`. Here the source convention is realized for the convolution bracket via the actual universal matrix and infinitesimal coefficients; the implementation is not a formal comparison with invariant derivations.
