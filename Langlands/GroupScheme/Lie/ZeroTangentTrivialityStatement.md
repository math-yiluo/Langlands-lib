# Connected affine groups with zero tangent space

`Langlands.GroupScheme.ZeroTangentTrivialityStatement` asserts that an actual
affine group scheme `G` locally of finite type over any field `k`, with
connected underlying space and a subsingleton actual identity tangent kernel
`lieKernel G k`, has an isomorphism as its structure morphism. It also asserts
that every point `x : T ⟶ G.X` is the identity for every scheme `T` over `k`.
The field, group and test schemes remain at arbitrary universe `u`.

`lieKernel` is the published kernel of reduction from actual dual-number
points to points over the field, not an independently chosen Lie algebra.
The subsingleton condition therefore says that every actual tangent at the
identity is constant. The all-test conclusion includes nonreduced tests.
No smoothness, perfectness, characteristic or splitness assumption is made.

This is an internal geometric prerequisite for actual relative-root subgroup
realization. Its expected algebraic route uses the published affine
coordinate and cotangent constructions: trivial tangent kernel makes the
augmentation cotangent space zero; the Noetherian augmentation ideal is then
generated by an idempotent; connectedness and the augmentation point force
that ideal to vanish. This statement file makes no proof claim. It is narrower
than the general full-Lie subgroup equality problem and needs no instance of
that unproved statement.
