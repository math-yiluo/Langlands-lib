# Lie bracket on the actual affine group-scheme tangent

Let `G` be an affine group scheme over `Spec R` for a commutative ring `R`, and `A` any commutative `R`-algebra. The carrier is the existing `Additive (lieKernel G A)`: the tangent at the identity defined by the kernel of reduction from `A[ε]/(ε²)` to `A`. Its addition and `A`-module structure are unchanged.

`kernelCounitDerivationLinearEquiv G` composes the existing identification of this module with identity derivations and precomposition by `lieCoordinateAlgEquiv G` from the actual Hopf coordinate algebra. The latter comparison preserves evaluation at the identity by `lieCoordinateAlgEquiv_counit`. Its inverse precomposes with the inverse algebra equivalence; the Leibniz rule ensures that the resulting linear map vanishes on `1` (`Derivation.mk'`). The equivalence therefore takes a kernel point to the `ε`-coefficient of its Hopf-coordinate evaluation, not the whole dual-number-valued homomorphism.

`instAffineKernelLieRing` and `instAffineKernelLieAlgebra` transport the Lie bracket from `counitDerivationLie` across this equivalence. The resulting `kernelCounitDerivationLieEquiv G` respects the bracket, and `kernelCounitDerivationLinearEquiv_lie` identifies the image of `⁅x, y⁆` with `f * g - g * f` for the two infinitesimal coefficients `f, g`. Thus the bracket is alternating, satisfies the Jacobi identity and is `A`-bilinear, including on nonreduced test algebras.

**Source and convention.** Milne, *Algebraic Groups: The Theory of Group Schemes of Finite Type over a Field*, 2022 corrected reprint, §10e, Propositions 10.28–10.29 and the paragraph after the proof of 10.29 (pp. 195–196), treats the field-valued tangent via the counit derivation and the bracket of left-invariant derivations. To see the convention behind the construction, if `Δ(b) = Σ b₁ ⊗ b₂`, the left-invariant extension of `f` is `D_f(b) = Σ b₁ f(b₂)`. Applying the counit to `D_f(D_g(b))` gives `Σ f(b₁)g(b₂)`, by the counit identity, so the order of the two factors is the one used here. The Lean implementation uses the direct convolution proof in `CounitDerivationBracket.lean` rather than constructing left-invariant derivations and proving a comparison with them.

The construction applies over arbitrary affine bases, with no smoothness, field, finite generation, or flatness premise. This file does not prove preservation under a group homomorphism or a change of test algebra, nor the comparison with the matrix Lie algebra of `GL_n` or a formal identification with the Lie algebra of left-invariant derivations.
