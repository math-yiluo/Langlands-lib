# Smooth scheme surjectivity from the actual identity differential

This module is an internal derivation from the published actual Lie-kernel and affine-coordinate constructions, the geometric integrality theorem for smooth connected affine groups, and Mathlib's scheme geometry. The final theorem `smoothSurjectiveOfSurjectiveDifferential` has exactly the published proposition `SmoothSurjectiveOfSurjectiveDifferentialStatement.{u}` as its type. It applies to every field in an arbitrary universe, to an actual homomorphism of smooth affine group schemes with connected target and surjective actual identity differential. It concludes Mathlib's `Smooth` and `Surjective` properties of the original scheme morphism. The source need not be connected. The algebraic smoothness part was proved by Worker 1454 in `SurjectiveDifferentialLifting`; Worker 1454 implemented the algebraically closed surjectivity argument, and Worker 336 supplied arbitrary-field descent and final assembly.

`exists_rational_lift_of_mem_range` treats an actual rational target point whose underlying scheme point lies in the image of an actual group homomorphism. The source and target are locally of finite type over an algebraically closed field. The target point is closed, so its inverse image is a nonempty closed subset of the Jacobson source. Choose a closed source point in this inverse image. Over an algebraically closed field this is a rational point. The construction `pointOfClosedPoint` gives the desired source section, and equality after evaluation at the unique point of the base identifies its image with the original rational target section. This argument only lifts rational points already in the scheme image.

`rationalPoints_surjective_of_smooth` assumes that the target is irreducible and that the homomorphism is smooth. Its image `U` on underlying scheme points is open because smooth morphisms are open, and it is nonempty because the source has its identity section. For a prescribed rational target section `g`, left multiplication by `g` is a scheme isomorphism `L`. Both `U` and `L⁻¹(U)` are nonempty open subsets, so irreducibility makes their intersection nonempty. The Jacobson target has a closed point `c` in that intersection. It yields a rational target section `r`, and the preceding lemma supplies rational source sections `a` and `b` with images `r` and `g * r`, respectively. Since the morphism is a group homomorphism, `b * a⁻¹` has image `g`. The argument uses no connectedness hypothesis on the source.

`surjective_of_smooth_of_irreducible` upgrades rational surjectivity to surjectivity on all underlying scheme points. For any target point `w`, choose a closed point `c` in the closure of `{w}`. Rational surjectivity gives a source point above `c`. Smoothness implies flatness, and a flat morphism is generalizing: the generalization from `c` to `w` lifts to a generalization upstairs. Its image is `w`. Thus the result is Mathlib's actual morphism property `Surjective`, not merely a conclusion about rational points.

`surjective_of_smooth_of_connected` passes to an arbitrary field. It assumes smooth source and target, affineness and connectedness of the target, and smoothness of the homomorphism. It does not require source affineness or connectedness. Form the actual group-scheme base changes `Hb`, `Gb`, and `fb` along `toAlgebraicClosure k`. The outer pullback and the target's base-change pullback identify the square with horizontal maps `fb` and `f` and vertical projection maps as a pullback square. Smoothness of `f` is stable in this square, so `fb` is smooth. The published `smoothConnectedAffineGeometry.1` makes the original target geometrically integral; its base change to the algebraic closure is therefore integral, hence irreducible. The structure maps of both base-changed groups are smooth, and in particular locally of finite type. The preceding algebraically closed result proves scheme surjectivity of `fb`.

The algebraic-closure spectrum map is surjective, so its pullback projection `Gb → G` is surjective. Composing this projection with `fb` gives a surjective scheme map. Commutativity of the actual base-change square identifies that composite with the source projection followed by `f`. Surjectivity of this composite implies surjectivity of `f` itself. This argument descends actual scheme surjectivity; it makes no claim about lifting every point over an unchanged test algebra.

Finally, `smoothSurjectiveOfSurjectiveDifferential` unfolds the unchanged published proposition and installs its given smoothness, affineness, and connectedness hypotheses. The theorem `smooth_of_surjective_differential` applies to the original actual dual-number identity differential. Its proof extends differential surjectivity to coefficient algebras by scalar extension, lifts identity-kernel points over square-zero ideals by projection onto the ideal, and corrects a preliminary source lift by group multiplication. This proves relative formal smoothness; finite presentation and affine coordinate transport then give scheme smoothness. The descent theorem applies to that same smooth homomorphism and gives scheme surjectivity. Pairing these two conclusions proves the exact proposition at arbitrary `u`, without an unproved geometric criterion or an additional hypothesis.
