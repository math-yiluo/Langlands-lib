# Unipotence of the adjoint kernel modulo the full centre

`Langlands.GroupScheme.Lie.smoothAdjointKernelCenterQuotient` proves
`SmoothAdjointKernelCenterQuotientStatement` in every universe. Let `G` be a
smooth connected affine group scheme over a field `k`, and let `N` be the
scheme kernel of its adjoint representation in the supplied finite tangent
coordinates. The quotient of `N` by the pullback of the full centre is
represented by an affine group scheme locally of finite type, and this group
scheme is unipotent.

The proof follows the finite-jet argument in Milne, *Algebraic Groups*, 2022
corrected reprint, Proposition 14.23, printed p. 285. The proposition is used
in the proof of Corollary 16.61(b), pp. 346–347. The implementation carries out
this argument over the ground field. [Milne, *Algebraic Groups*](https://www.jmilne.org/math/Books/iAG2022.pdf)

## The first jet quotient

Over a field, connectedness of `G` implies geometric connectedness.
`IdentityJet.exists_order_detects_center` then gives an order `n` whose
conjugation kernel is the centre as a subgroup functor. The affine chart on
all of `G` presents the order-`n` jet as the spectrum of a finite-dimensional
algebra `P`. Its augmentation `ε` classifies the identity point. The ideal
`J = ker ε` satisfies `J^(n+1) = 0` by the chart's augmentation-power formula.

For a commutative `k`-algebra `A`, let `T = A ⊗ₖ P`, let `J_A` be the ideal
of `T` generated by `J`, and put `D = T/J_A²`. Write `s : T → T` for
augmentation to `A` followed by the inclusion into `T`. The algebra map
from `T` to the dual numbers over `D` sends `z` to the pair consisting of
`s(z)` and `z - s(z)`, both reduced in `D`. This map respects multiplication
because products of two deviations vanish in `D`. The sum of the two
components recovers the quotient map `T → D`.

The restriction of this dual-number test to `P` is a jet point with identity
reduction, hence lies in the actual tangent kernel over `D`. The restriction
to `A` is the constant lift of the coefficient map `A → D`. For an `A`-point
of `N`, the coefficient change to `D` still belongs to the adjoint kernel.
`adjointRepresentation_affineAction` therefore fixes the tangent point by
actual conjugation. Applying `IdentityJet.conj_jetPoint` and the injectivity
of jet point coordinates gives equality after the dual-number test. Taking
the sum of its two components gives triviality of the jet action on `T/J_A²`.

## Filtration and fppf image

The product identity for the difference between an algebra endomorphism and
the identity shows inductively that conjugation sends each element of
`J_A^t` to itself modulo `J_A^(t+1)`. A basis of `P` adapted to the powers of
`J` gives coordinates after tensoring with `A`. In these coordinates, the
matrix of the difference vanishes in each row whose filtration degree is at
most that of the column. `IdentityJet.conjGL` uses conjugation by the inverse
point, which also belongs to `N`. Ordering the basis by decreasing degree
therefore puts its matrices in the upper unitriangular group over every
`A`. The universal affine point of `N` gives the actual morphism to that
closed subgroup scheme.

`affineHomImage` gives the represented fppf quotient by the kernel of that
morphism, followed by a closed immersion into the unitriangular group. The
kernel is computed on all test schemes. Vanishing of the jet representation
amounts to centralizing the jet, and the detecting order identifies this
condition with membership in the full centre of `G`. Thus the quotient
projection has kernel exactly the pullback of the full centre to `N`.
`unitriangularUnipotent` and `unipotentClosedSubgroup` give unipotence of the
image.
