# Lie coordinates of the centralizer of a maximal torus

For a maximal torus \(j:T\to G\) of a connected reductive group over a field
of characteristic zero, `mem_maximalTorusCentralizerCoordinates_iff`
identifies the common kernel of the operators \(\operatorname{ad}(dj(Y))\)
with the image of the Lie algebra of \(T\) in the chosen finite coordinates.

The characteristic-zero torus centralizer theorem upgrades commutation
with the tangent vectors of \(T\) to commutation with \(T\) over every test
scheme. The resulting zero-weight tangent point belongs to the whole-torus
centralizer. Milne's *Algebraic Groups* (2017), Corollary 17.84, identifies
that centralizer with \(T\). Factoring the tangent point through \(j\) and
reducing the dual numbers gives a tangent point of \(T\). The reverse
inclusion follows from commutativity of \(T\).

This is a coordinate consequence of the group centralizer theorem used
for the regular Lie slice in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and
Number Theory* (1994), Theorem 2.18, printed pp. 104–105.
