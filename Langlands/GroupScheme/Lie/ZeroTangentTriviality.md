# Connected affine groups with zero tangent space

This module proves the published proposition
`Langlands.GroupScheme.ZeroTangentTrivialityStatement.{u}` with theorem
`Langlands.GroupScheme.zeroTangentTriviality.{u}`. It imports the unchanged
statement from `Langlands.GroupScheme.Lie.ZeroTangentTrivialityStatement`.

Worker 1285 authored the Lean proof in shared draft revision 1 of task
`3d320396-74f0-40df-9182-b1efa7f71b82`. Worker 1368 reused that proof without
Lean source changes and prepared this documentation for the focused proof task.
The algebraic argument is derived from the published constructions and Mathlib
results described below; it is an internal proof rather than a transcription
of a cited literature proof.

For an augmented Noetherian algebra over a field, every functional on the
augmentation cotangent space gives an actual augmented dual-number lift, using
the published `cotangentDualNumberLift`. If all such lifts are constant,
every functional vanishes. Linear duality makes the cotangent space trivial,
so the augmentation ideal is idempotent. Its finite generation makes it
generated by an idempotent element. The associated basic open in the prime
spectrum is clopen. Connectedness makes it empty or the whole spectrum;
the augmentation prime excludes the latter. Injectivity of the correspondence
between idempotents and basic opens forces the generator to be zero.
The augmentation kernel is therefore zero, and the augmentation is surjective
because it retracts the base-field algebra map.

The route is derived from the published cotangent and affine-coordinate
constructions and Mathlib's duality and finitely generated ideal results.
It does not use smoothness, translation, a dimension formula, or the
unproved general Lie-subgroup equality statement.

For an actual connected affine group scheme locally of finite type over an
arbitrary field, the chosen affine coordinate algebra is finite type and hence
Noetherian. Its spectrum is connected through the published coordinate
isomorphism. `kernelCoordinates` identifies augmented dual-number lifts with
the actual identity tangent kernel. If that kernel is a subsingleton, every
lift is the constant identity lift. The algebraic argument makes the
augmentation bijective. Since it retracts the base algebra map, that map is
also bijective, and its spectrum is an isomorphism. The coordinate
isomorphism transfers this to the original structure morphism.

Cancellation with the resulting isomorphism proves that every point on every
test scheme is the identity. In particular, the conclusion is about actual
scheme-valued points and includes nonreduced tests. The field and group
remain at arbitrary universe `u`; the pure algebra lemma also permits the
coordinate algebra to have a separate universe.
