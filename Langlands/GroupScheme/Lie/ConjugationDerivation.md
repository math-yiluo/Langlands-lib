# The coordinate derivation of actual infinitesimal conjugation

Let `R` be any commutative ring in universe `u`, let `G` be an actual group
scheme over `Spec R`, and let `X : lieKernel G R`. The kernel here consists of
actual `G(R[ε])` points reducing to the identity.

For every commutative `R`-algebra `A` and actual point `g : G(A)`,
`infinitesimalConjugate G X g` is the dual-number point
`X_A * constant(g) * X_A⁻¹`, where `X_A` is the scalar extension of `X`.
Its reduction is `g`. The lemmas `constant_naturality` and
`infinitesimalConjugate_naturality` prove compatibility with every algebra
homomorphism `A →ₐ[R] B`.

The theorem `infinitesimalConjugate_eq_constant_iff` identifies equality with
`constant(g)` with membership in the existing actual
`Lie.elementCentralizer G X`. It uses the group commutation equation and
`mem_elementCentralizer_affine_iff`. In particular, the centralizer retains its
definition on every affine refinement of every test scheme.

Assume now that `G` is affine, and put `C := coordinateAlgebra G` using the
existing chosen affine presentation. The actual universal point
`universalPoint G : G(C)` corresponds to `AlgHom.id R C` under
`pointCoordinates G`. Every `G(A)` point is the extension of this universal
point along its coordinate homomorphism.

`conjugationLift G X : C →ₐ[R] DualNumber C` is the coordinate homomorphism of
the infinitesimal conjugate of the universal point. Reduction proves that its
constant part is the identity of `C`. Its infinitesimal coefficient therefore
defines `conjugationDerivation G X : Derivation R C C`. The action of `C` on
itself in this derivation is the canonical multiplication action. It is a
global derivation of `C`, rather than a derivation with values in the
augmentation module at the identity.

`derivationLift_conjugationDerivation` recovers the original algebra map from
the derivation. Naturality and universality then give
`pointCoordinates_infinitesimalConjugate`: the coordinate map for any
infinitesimal conjugate is the universal lift followed by coefficient extension
along `pointCoordinates G g`. Its infinitesimal coefficient at `c : C` is
`pointCoordinates G g (conjugationDerivation G X c)`.

Define `conjugationIdeal G X` to be the ideal of `C` generated by all values
of this derivation. The main criterion
`mem_elementCentralizer_affine_iff_conjugationIdeal` states

```text
g ∈ (elementCentralizer G X).carrier (Spec A)
  ↔ conjugationIdeal G X ≤ ker (pointCoordinates G g).
```

The proof first compares the constant and infinitesimal coefficients of the two
dual-number algebra homomorphisms. Equality with the constant lift is equivalent
to vanishing of every derivation value under the point's coordinate map.
`Ideal.span_le` turns this into annihilation of the generated ideal. Over a
field, `conjugationIdeal_eq_imageIdeal` identifies this ideal with
`Langlands.Algebra.SemisimpleDerivation.imageIdeal` for the constructed
derivation.

There are no finite type, smoothness, field, reducedness or characteristic
assumptions on the construction and centralizer criterion. They apply to every
commutative test algebra in the base universe, including nonreduced algebras.
The construction does not yet prove local semisimplicity of this derivation,
identify a chosen centralizer representative with the quotient spectrum, or
prove smoothness of the actual centralizer.

Provenance is internal and derived: the proofs use the existing actual
dual-number point operations, their naturality, the affine coordinate
equivalence and the elementary universal property of an ideal span. No
additional structural theorem or external literature comparison is asserted.
