# Left-invariant derivations and the affine tangent bracket

For a commutative bialgebra `B` over a commutative ring `R`, a derivation `D : B → B` is left invariant if `Δ ∘ D = (id ⊗ D) ∘ Δ`. `leftInvariantDerivations R B` is the Lie subalgebra of `Derivation R B B` cut out by this identity, with the commutator of composition; `mem_leftInvariantDerivations` proves the membership characterization.

For a counit derivation `f : B → R`, `extendLeftInvariant f` is contraction of the second factor of `Δ` by `f`: it sends `b` to `Σ f(b₂) • b₁`. This is the existing `IntegralLinearEmbedding.rightAct` linear operator. It satisfies the derivation rule by tensor induction and the counit law; it is left invariant by coassociativity. Conversely, composing an invariant derivation with the counit gives a counit derivation, and contracting its invariance identity with `id ⊗ ε` gives the original derivation. In particular, `counitLeftInvariantLieEquiv_symm_ofConv` identifies the inverse with composition by the counit.

The extension preserves the order of convolution: the operator for `f * g` is `D_f ∘ D_g`, by coassociativity and evaluation on the two tensor factors. Thus `counitLeftInvariantLieEquiv` preserves the bracket, with no sign reversal. For an actual affine group scheme `G` over `Spec R`, composing with the existing Lie equivalence from the dual-number kernel gives `kernelLeftInvariantLieEquiv G` on `Additive (lieKernel G R)`. The comparison is at the base ring `R`, not a comparison after an arbitrary scalar extension of the invariant derivations. There is no smoothness, flatness, finite generation or characteristic hypothesis.

**Source.** Milne, *Algebraic Groups*, 2022 corrected reprint, §10e, Proposition 10.29 and its proof, pp. 195–196, identifies the counit derivations with left-invariant derivations of the affine group over the field. The two inverse formulas agree with that source. The Lean comparison applies over a commutative base ring and proves explicitly that the convolution commutator realizes the left-invariant bracket on the actual tangent.
