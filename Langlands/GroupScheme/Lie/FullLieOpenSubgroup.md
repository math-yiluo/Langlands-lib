# Smooth closed subgroups with full Lie algebra are open

This derived unit extends the local and translation argument of the published
`Lie.SubgroupEqualityProof` to ambient affine groups that are locally of finite
type and may be nonsmooth or disconnected. It is internal infrastructure for
CGP Lemma 3.3.9; it does not yet prove semigroup intersection connectedness.

`isOpenImmersion_of_fullLie` takes a closed inclusion of a smooth group into
such an ambient group and surjectivity on the actual identity Lie kernel over
the field. It proves that the underlying scheme map is an open immersion.
`fullLieOpenRange` is its actual open range.

The proof reuses the published coordinate restriction, derivation, formal
smoothness, Krull intersection and translation lemmas. The source coordinate
algebra is formally smooth and the ambient algebra is Noetherian. A function
annihilating the restriction kernel is translated along the universal subgroup
point and then expanded in a vector-space basis. This produces an annihilator
`e` whose restriction is one. Thus `1-e` belongs to the kernel and is idempotent;
the kernel is the principal ideal generated by `1-e`. Mathlib's criterion for
an affine closed immersion to be open then applies. There is no use of
connectedness or smoothness of the ambient group in this argument.
