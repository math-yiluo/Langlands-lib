# The conjugation kernel and the jet centralizer

The actual identity jet embeds as a closed subscheme of the ambient group.
A point factoring through the jet inclusion uniquely gives a point of the jet
over the same test scheme. The action formula sends that point to conjugation
in the ambient group. Cancelling the closed inclusion and applying the group
law shows that fixing all jet points is equivalent to commuting with all group
points that factor through the jet. Both conditions quantify over every
refinement of the test scheme.

For a smooth separated quasi-compact group over an affine base with connected
residue fibres, the Library's connected-fibre comparison gives geometric
connectedness. The finite jet whose action kernel equals the full centre then
satisfies `JetCentralizerStabilizationStatement`. This is an internal
comparison between the Library's two descriptions of the same identity jet.
