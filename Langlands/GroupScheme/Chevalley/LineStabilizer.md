# Chevalley line stabilizers on all scheme tests

This assembly uses the proved coordinate, tensor, adapted-basis and exterior
constructions to realize the range of an arbitrary closed subgroup as the
unit-scalar stabilizer of a fixed exterior coordinate vector.

For a finite-type corepresented group over a field, the closed subgroup
gives a surjective map of actual coordinate bialgebras. The finitely
generated kernel determines one finite regular subspace. An adapted basis
puts its intersection with that kernel into coordinate-subspace form.
The actual regular matrix representation and its exterior power then
produce the desired line. The range criterion on every relative scheme
follows from relative global sections and the proved tensor-coordinate and
exterior-line equivalences.

The subgroup need not be smooth, reduced or connected. No restriction is
placed on the field characteristic, cardinality or perfection, or on the
test schemes.

The finite-index version reindexes the exterior basis by `Fin (n+1)`.
Its cardinality is positive because the selected exterior basis index
itself supplies an element, including when the original subspace has
dimension zero.
