# Normal subgroups with commuting rational points are central

Author: Worker 1499.

This internal helper module of the proof of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.CenterQuotientReductiveStatement` proves that, in a
smooth connected affine group with trivial unipotent radical over an
algebraically closed field, a closed normal subgroup whose rational points
commute has central rational points. It is derived content: the statements are
chosen for this proof and are not items of the cited literature. Everything is
in the namespace `Langlands.GroupScheme.CenterQuotient`.

Notation: `K` is a field and all group schemes are over `Spec K`. For points
`x, y` of a group scheme on a test scheme `T`, products and inverses are the
Library pointwise group structure. `toUnit T ≫ h` is a rational point `h`
pulled back to `T`. `center G` is the Library centre subgroup functor
(`Rational/Characters`).

## Ring lemmas

In any ring:

- `isNilpotent_list_prod_sub_one`: let `P` be a predicate such that `x - 1` is
  nilpotent whenever `P x`, and any two elements satisfying `P` commute. Then
  `l.prod - 1` is nilpotent for every list `l` of elements satisfying `P`.
  Induction on `l`, using `isNilpotent_mul_sub_one` from
  `CenterQuotientLinearAlgebra` and `Commute.list_prod_right`.
- `isNilpotent_units_conj_sub_one`: if `x - 1` is nilpotent and `u` is a unit,
  then `u x u⁻¹ - 1 = u (x - 1) u⁻¹` is nilpotent.

## The conjugated commutator map

Fix a rational point `h` of `G`.

- `conjCommutatorMap h : G.X ⊗ G.X ⟶ G.X` is the morphism
  `(g, y) ↦ g (y h y⁻¹ h⁻¹) g⁻¹`, built from the two projections and the
  pointwise group structure.
- `comp_conjCommutatorMap`, `lift_comp_conjCommutatorMap`: its values on
  points `v` and on pairs `lift g y`.
- `conjCommutatorMap_isEquivariant`: it is equivariant for the Library action
  `UnipotentAllBorelsEnvelope.leftTranslation G G.X` (left translation on the
  first factor) and the conjugation action of `G` on itself
  (`AutomorphicAction.conjugation (𝟙 G)`), since
  `(g g₁) c (g g₁)⁻¹ = g (g₁ c g₁⁻¹) g⁻¹`.
- `lift_one_one_comp_conjCommutatorMap`: it sends the rational point `(1, 1)`
  to `1`.

## Point vanishing through a morphism

- `eq_zero_of_forall_evalPoint_comp_eq_zero`: let `K` be algebraically closed,
  `X` reduced and locally of finite type over `K`, and `p : X ⟶ H.X` a
  morphism that is injective on global sections. A global function `F` on
  `H.X` whose evaluation at `w ≫ p` vanishes for every rational point `w` of
  `X` is zero. Its pullback along `p` vanishes at every rational point of `X`,
  so it is zero by the Library lemma `eq_zero_of_forall_appTop_eq_zero`;
  injectivity gives `F = 0`.

## Main result

- `mem_center_of_isNormal_of_commute`: let `K` be algebraically closed, `G`
  smooth, affine and connected with `HasTrivialUnipotentRadical G`, and
  `a : A ⟶ G` a closed subgroup (`IsClosedSubgroup a`) whose range is normal.
  Assume that the images in `G` of any two rational points of `A` commute.
  Then for every rational point `x` of `A`, the point `h = x ≫ a` of `G` lies
  in `center G`.

  Proof.
  1. The range of `a` is closed under conjugation, and every value
     `g (y h y⁻¹ h⁻¹) g⁻¹` of `f = conjCommutatorMap h` lies in it.
  2. The Library affine linear embedding (`affineLinearEmbeddingStatement`)
     gives a closed embedding `ρ : MatrixRepresentation G (Fin N)`. All
     conjugates `g h g⁻¹` lie in the range of `a`, so `ρ(h)` commutes with each
     `ρ(g h g⁻¹)`. By `isNilpotent_rationalMatrix_commutator_sub_one`
     (`CenterQuotientConjugation`), `ρ(y h y⁻¹ h⁻¹) - 1` is nilpotent. Its
     conjugate `ρ(f(g, y)) - 1` is therefore nilpotent too
     (`isNilpotent_units_conj_sub_one`). Any two values of `f` lie in the range
     of `a`, so their matrices commute.
  3. `V = G.X ⊗ G.X` is affine, of finite type and geometrically integral
     (`smoothConnectedAffineGeometry`). The Library pointed image generation
     (`ImageGeneration.pointedImageGeneration`, with the base point `(1, 1)`)
     gives the closed subgroup `D` generated by the image of `f`. It is
     smooth, affine and geometrically connected (hence connected), and for
     some `n` the ordered product map `D.powerMap n` from the `n`-fold power
     of `V` onto `D` is dominant.
  4. `D` is normal in `G`. The Library stability of closed-image envelopes
     (`AutomorphicAction.closedImageEnvelope_isStable`), applied with the
     equivariance of `f`, shows that the range of `D` is stable under
     conjugation.
  5. `D` lies in `A`. Every rational value of `f` lies in the range of `a`, so
     `f` factors through `a`
     (`UnipotentAllBorelsEnvelope.exists_factor_of_algClosed_points`), and the
     minimal homomorphism `D ⟶ A` (`D.minimalHom_comp`) composes with `a` to
     the inclusion of `D`. Hence any two rational points of `D` commute: their
     images in `G` are images of rational points of `A`, and the inclusion of
     `D` is a monomorphism.
  6. `D` is unipotent. Let `ρD` be the restriction of `ρ` to `D` and `M` its
     universal matrix, with entries in `Γ(D, ⊤)`. We show that
     `(M - 1)^N = 0`. Each entry is a global function on `D`, and
     `p = D.powerMap n` is dominant onto the reduced affine `D`, so it is
     injective on global sections. The `n`-fold power of `V` is integral,
     affine and of finite type (`ImageGeneration.finitePowerGood_finitePower`).
     So by `eq_zero_of_forall_evalPoint_comp_eq_zero` it suffices to evaluate
     at points `w ≫ p` with `w` rational. There `ρD(w ≫ p)` is
     `ρ` of the ordered product of values `f(v_i)`
     (`D.powerMap_comp_points`, `orderedPowerMap_points`), that is, the product
     of the commuting matrices `ρ(f(v_i))`. By `isNilpotent_list_prod_sub_one`
     and step 2, `ρD(w ≫ p) - 1` is nilpotent, so its `N`-th power is zero
     (`pow_card_eq_zero_of_isNilpotent`). Evaluating, every `ρD(d) - 1` is
     nilpotent for rational `d`, so every eigenvalue of `ρD(d)` is `1`
     (`eq_one_of_mem_roots_of_isNilpotent_sub_one`). The rational points of `D`
     commute, so the Library criterion
     `CentralIntersection.isUnipotent_of_eigenvalues` (with exponent `1`) gives
     that `D` is unipotent.
  7. `D` is smooth, connected, unipotent and normal, so trivial unipotent
     radical gives that its range is `⊥`. Hence the inclusion of `D` is `1`
     (as in `hom_eq_one_of_isCentral`, `CenterQuotientCommutator`), and so
     `f = 1` because `f` factors through `D` (`D.factor_comp`).
  8. For every test scheme `T` and point `y` of `G` on `T`,
     `f(1, y) = y h y⁻¹ h⁻¹ = 1` with `h` pulled back to `T`, so `h` commutes
     with `y`. This is the membership criterion `mem_center`.

In the proof of the target, `A` is the preimage in `G` of a smooth connected
central subgroup of a unipotent normal subgroup of the quotient, whose rational
points commute by `commute_of_lift` (`CenterQuotientCommutator`).
