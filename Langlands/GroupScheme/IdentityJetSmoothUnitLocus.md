# The fixed-rank smoothness locus along a group unit

For a separated locally finitely presented group over a locally noetherian
scheme, fixed-rank smoothness along the actual unit after base change is
represented by a closed subscheme of a base open. The factorization is unique
for every actual test scheme.

At any point of a satisfying test, choose an affine unit neighborhood over an
affine neighborhood of its image. The residue conormal rank gives a smaller
basic neighborhood with the required number of conormal generators. Its
closed chart is defined by the jet coefficient ideal. The locally closed
locus gluing theorem combines these charts and retains the exact test
condition. This is the smoothness-along-the-unit application of SGA 3,
Exposé XV, Lemma 5.4.
