# Rigidity of actions on affine groups with group-like coordinates

This internal construction transports an actual automorphic action through an
isomorphism of its target group scheme. The transported morphism first applies
the forward target isomorphism, then the original action, and finally the inverse
isomorphism. The point action and all three action laws agree with this formula.

For an integral affine acting group, its Hopf coordinate spectrum is isomorphic
to the actual group. Integrality transfers through this isomorphism, so the Hopf
coordinate algebra is an integral domain. Restricting the action through the
isomorphism and using the preceding Hopf-spectrum rigidity theorem proves
triviality on every scheme test when the target is a group-like-spanned Hopf
spectrum. Transporting an arbitrary affine target through its Hopf coordinate
isomorphism gives the same conclusion when that target's Hopf coordinate algebra
is spanned by group-like elements.

A smooth connected affine group over a field is geometrically integral by the
published `smoothConnectedAffineGeometry` theorem. Its underlying scheme is
therefore integral, and the preceding conclusion applies to every actual
automorphic action of such an acting group on an affine group with group-like
spanning coordinates. There is no reducedness, smoothness or finite-type
hypothesis on the target or the test scheme. All types share one arbitrary
universe. This is an internal deduction from published affine coordinate,
action and smooth connected geometry constructions. The centre-containment
consequence and the host semigroup comparison are separate obligations.
