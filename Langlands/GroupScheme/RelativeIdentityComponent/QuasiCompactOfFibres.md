# Quasi-compactness of group schemes from their residue fibres

This module proves a general criterion: a group scheme over a quasi-separated
base is quasi-compact over the base when every residue fibre is smooth,
connected and quasi-compact. The group is an arbitrary
`G : GroupScheme S`, that is, a group object of `Over S` with its actual
multiplication, unit and inverse. No noetherian, finiteness, flatness,
separatedness or projectivity hypothesis on `G` or `S` is used.

## A product lemma

`fiberι_mem_range_product` takes an open immersion `i : P ⟶ G.X` in
`Over S`, a base point `s`, smoothness of the residue fibre
`residueFiber G s`, and the hypothesis that the unit section at `s` lies in
the range of `i.left`. It proves that the image in `G.X` of every point of the
identity component of the residue fibre lies in the range of the product map
`(i ⊗ i) ≫ μ`.

The proof base-changes `i` along `S.fromSpecResidueField s`. The result is an
open immersion by base change of open immersions. Its range contains the
identity point of the residue fibre, because the identity point maps to the
unit section at `s` (`fiberPoint_unit`) and the pullback range formula
`range_of_isPullback` describes the range of the base change. The published
field lemma `identitySet_subset_range_fieldOpenProduct` then writes every point
of the identity component of the smooth field group as a product of two points
of this open. The published comparison `range_baseChangedProduct` identifies
the range of the base-changed product map with the preimage, under the fibre
inclusion, of the range of the original product map.

## The criterion

`quasiCompact_of_residueFibers` assumes `[QuasiSeparatedSpace S]` and, for
every `s : S`, that `(residueFiber G s).X.hom` is smooth and that the
topological fibre `G.X.hom.fiber s` is connected and compact. It concludes
`QuasiCompact G.X.hom`.

Fix `s` and write `f = G.X.hom`.

1. The set `f ⁻¹' {s}` is compact: it is the range of the fibre inclusion
   `f.fiberι s` (`Scheme.Hom.range_fiberι`), whose source is compact.
2. A scheme is a prespectral space, so
   `PrespectralSpace.exists_isCompact_and_isOpen_between` gives a compact open
   `W` of `G.X.left` containing this fibre. The unit section at `s` lies in the
   fibre, hence in `W`.
3. The preimage of `W` under the unit section is an open neighbourhood of `s`;
   `exists_isAffineOpen_mem_and_subset` gives an affine open `V ∋ s` whose
   unit section lies in `W`.
4. Let `P` be the open subscheme `W ∩ f⁻¹ V`, as an object of `Over S`, with
   its open immersion `i` into `G.X`. The morphism `W → S` has compact source
   and quasi-separated target, so it is quasi-compact
   (`quasiCompact_of_compactSpace`), and the preimage of the compact set `V` is
   compact. Hence `P` is quasi-compact, so is `P → S`, and the fibre product
   `P ⊗ P = P ×_S P` is quasi-compact.
5. `f⁻¹ V` equals the range of the product map `(i ⊗ i) ≫ μ`. For `y` with
   `t = f y ∈ V`, the residue fibre at `t` is connected, so its identity
   component is the whole fibre; `y` comes from a point of that fibre, and the
   unit section at `t` lies in the range of `i` because `t ∈ V`. The product
   lemma gives `y` in the range. Conversely, every product of two points of
   `P` lies over a point of `V`, by commutativity of the structural triangles
   of the product map and of the first projection.
6. Therefore `f⁻¹ V` is compact, being a continuous image of the compact
   `P ×_S P`. Since `V` is affine, `HasAffineProperty.iff_of_isAffine` turns
   this into quasi-compactness of the restriction of `f` over `V`.

Quasi-compactness is local on the target, so
`IsZariskiLocalAtTarget.of_forall_exists_morphismRestrict` concludes.

Steps 2–5 use the group structure essentially: an arbitrary morphism with
quasi-compact fibres need not be quasi-compact. Here a single compact open
neighbourhood of one fibre controls all nearby fibres through multiplication.

## Provenance

This is internal derived mathematics. The product-covering argument is the
classical fact that a connected smooth algebraic group over a field is
generated by any open neighbourhood of the identity, in the precise form of
the published field lemma; the rest uses Mathlib's prespectral topology,
affine opens and target-local quasi-compactness. The pullback adaptation in
`fiberι_mem_range_product` follows the published
`SmoothReduction.underlyingSet_subset_range_product`.
