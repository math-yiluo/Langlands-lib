# Relative identity components for groups smooth along their unit

For a locally finitely presented group whose structure morphism is smooth at every point of the unit section, the union of the fibre identity components is represented by a smooth, geometrically connected open normal subgroup. The subgroup satisfies the all-test criterion for the relative identity component and retains it after arbitrary base change.

The smooth locus carries an open subgroup structure. Inversion preserves smoothness by its isomorphism, and multiplication on the square of the smooth locus is smooth by shear and projection. Since that square is smooth over the base, local smooth descent puts its image in the smooth locus. The unit lies there by hypothesis. The proof takes the published relative identity component of this smooth open subgroup. Its fibre is the ambient identity component because the smooth open subgroup makes the ambient fibre smooth, and open subgroups of smooth field groups have the same identity component under inclusion. The existing all-test comparison then gives normality and base change.

This is the smooth-along-the-unit case of SGA 3, Exposé VI_B, Theorem 3.10 and Lemma 3.10.1, used in Exposé XV, Lemma 5.3(ii). The construction uses the open smooth locus and the existing relative identity component theorem.
