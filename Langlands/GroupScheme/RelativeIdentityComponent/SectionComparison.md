# Section components at the unit of a group scheme

This internal comparison identifies the generic section-component union at the
actual unit section with the underlying relative identity set. It uses no
smoothness or finiteness assumption for that equality. The first projection
of the base-changed unit is the original unit after the residue-field map;
the embedding of the residue fibre then identifies its unit point with the
point selected by the generic section construction.

The unit's affine section neighbourhood is the existing affine-neighbourhood
construction applied to that actual unit. Functoriality of section-selected
components shows that its component union maps into the ambient relative
identity set. Assuming the generic finite-presentation openness proposition,
smoothness of the ambient structure map makes this neighbourhood's component
union open: smoothness restricts, giving flatness, local finite presentation
and geometric reducedness, and the affine source and base give quasi-compactness
and quasi-separatedness.

These are consequences of the existing scheme pullbacks and neighbourhood
construction. The generic openness theorem remains an explicit premise.
This file does not yet show that the ambient relative identity set is open,
or conclude the parent existence and smooth structure statements.
