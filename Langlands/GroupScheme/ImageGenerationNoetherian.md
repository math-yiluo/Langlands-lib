# Noetherian finiteness inputs for generated images

This module collects three internal Noetherian finiteness facts used in the
construction of the group generated by a pointed integral morphism. They are
standard commutative algebra; no literature assertion is formalized here.

`exists_forall_eq_of_antitone_isPrime` states that an antitone sequence
`p : ℕ → Ideal A` of prime ideals of a Noetherian ring `A` is eventually
constant: there is `N` with `p m = p N` for every `m ≥ N`. The proof picks `N`
minimizing the Krull height `(p n).height` (heights are well-ordered in
`ℕ∞`). For `m ≥ N` we have `p m ≤ p N`, and the height of `p N` is at most that
of `p m`. Mathlib's `Ideal.eq_of_le_of_height_le` for a prime `p m` contained in
an ideal of no larger finite height then gives equality. Finiteness of heights of
primes in a Noetherian ring is supplied by Mathlib's Krull height theorem.

The imported `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.FormallySmoothLocalCompletion.injective_of_surjective_endo` states that a surjective ring endomorphism
`φ` of a Noetherian ring is injective. The kernels of the powers `φ ^ n` form an
ascending chain, which stabilizes at some `n`. Every power of `φ` is surjective.
If `φ x = 0`, write `x = (φ ^ n) y`; then `y` lies in the kernel of `φ ^ (n + 1)`,
which equals the kernel of `φ ^ n`, so `x = 0`.

`isIso_of_isClosedImmersion_endo` states that a closed immersion `φ : X ⟶ X`
from an affine, locally Noetherian scheme to itself is an isomorphism. The ring
of global sections of `X` is Noetherian. A closed immersion between affine
schemes induces a surjection on global sections, so the previous result makes
it bijective. Mathlib's `IsClosedImmersion.isIso_of_injective_of_isAffine` then
shows that `φ` is an isomorphism.

All statements hold in arbitrary universes and impose no hypothesis on a base
field or its characteristic.
