# Cartan generation from tangent-spanning families

Worker 1296.

This derived interface works over an arbitrary field `k : Type u`, with an
independent family index universe `v`. `LieSpanningFamily A a` requires an
affine ambient group and affine source group schemes. It states that the
supremum of the images of the actual linear differentials of the family maps
is the whole Lie module. The predicate requires no smoothness or connectedness
of these groups and no closedness of the family maps. It does not assert that
the maps generate the group.

`isGeneratedByCartanSubgroups_of_lieSpanningFamily` additionally assumes that the
ambient group and every source are smooth and connected. Every group is affine,
every map is a closed subgroup inclusion,
the family spans the Lie module, each source satisfies all-test Cartan
generation, and each inclusion preserves maximal tori. It concludes the
ambient all-test Cartan-generation property. Competitors are arbitrary closed
subgroups; they need not be smooth, reduced or connected.

The implemented proof first uses `exists_smooth_generated_family` to obtain
a smooth connected affine closed subgroup generated by the family. Each
family member factors through that subgroup. Monotonicity of tangent
submodules and tangent spanning give its full tangent image. The published
`lieSubgroupEquality` theorem then identifies its all-test range with top.
For any closed competitor containing every ambient Cartan, the published
Cartan-intersection argument puts each family member in the competitor:
Cartans of each member map into ambient Cartans because maximal tori remain
maximal. Minimality of the smooth generated subgroup puts it in the
competitor. Its top range forces the competitor's range to be top.

The smooth generated subgroup is essential to this proof: full tangent
image of an arbitrary nonsmooth subgroup is not used to infer equality.
The provenance is composition of the published generated-family,
represented-subgroup, Lie subgroup equality and Cartan induction interfaces.
This is internal derived content with no new literature assertion. No
infinitude, characteristic or perfection assumption is added. Applying this
to centralizers still requires their smoothness, their smaller-dimensional
Cartan generation, preservation of maximal tori and tangent spanning.
