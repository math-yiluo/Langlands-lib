# Unirationality from unirational Cartan subgroups

Worker 321. The theorem `cartanUnirationalityStatement` proves the unchanged
published `CartanUnirationalityStatement` in every universe. It follows the
finite-product route of Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017), Corollary 17.92
(printed p.382), using the published proof of Cartan generation (Theorem 17.91).

All actual Cartan embeddings are indexed by `CartanSubgroup G`. The published
torus-centralizer structure theorem supplies their smoothness and connectedness;
their closed immersions into the affine group supply affineness. The finite-family
generation theorem extracts finitely many of these embeddings from Cartan
generation, using the published pointed-image generation theorem.

The private finite-family lemma forms the actual product of the selected groups
and multiplies its coordinates in a fixed order. Each factor is geometrically
integral by the published smooth connected affine geometry theorem. Finite
product closure of `FinitePowerGood` gives an affine geometrically integral
source of finite type. The all-unit point maps to the identity. Pointed-image
generation constructs a closed subgroup receiving this multiplication map and a
dominant map to it from some finite power of the source.

Inserting one coordinate with all other coordinates equal to the identity shows
that every selected subgroup factors through this generated subgroup. Finite
generation therefore makes its all-test range equal to the entire group. Evaluating
that range equality on the identity of the ambient group gives a section of its
inclusion, hence surjectivity of the inclusion on scheme points.

The selected Cartan groups are unirational by the hypothesis. The derived finite
product parametrization theorem makes their product and each finite power
unirational. Finally, unirationality passes first along the dominant power map
and then along the surjective inclusion, proving unirationality of the ambient
group. No reductivity, perfection, separability of the multiplication map or
additional assumptions are imposed.
