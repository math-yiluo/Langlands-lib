# Cartan generation over infinite fields

This internal assembly proves exactly the published
`Langlands.GroupScheme.CartanGenerationStatement.{u}`. Every infinite field
`k : Type u` is permitted, including imperfect fields and fields of positive
characteristic. Every actual smooth connected affine group is covered. The
conclusion concerns arbitrary actual closed subgroup competitors containing
all actual Cartan embeddings: their ranges are the whole ambient group functor
on every test scheme. Competitors require no smoothness, reducedness or
connectedness assumption.

## Implemented proof

The proof adapts the published `cartanGeneration_of_isogenyReduction` induction
to use Cartan structure over the fixed infinite field. It supplies the existing
structural inputs by `nilpotentCentralTorus`, `unipotentSemisimpleStatement`,
`Lie.semisimpleCentralizerSpanning`, and `semisimpleIsogenyReduction`.

Strong induction on Lie dimension reduces the current group `A` by an isogeny
to a smooth connected affine group `B`, with lifts of all closed target tori.
If `B` is geometrically nilpotent, its central maximal torus makes its identity
embedding Cartan, so it is generated by its Cartan subgroups. Otherwise the
published semisimple-centralizer spanning and smoothness theorems supply an
induction step through identity components of proper semisimple centralizers.
The Lie dimensions of `A` and `B` agree, so these smaller groups also satisfy
the induction bound for `A`.

To show that maximal tori of these identity components remain maximal in `B`,
the proof uses the existing field-local Cartan tangent-lift interface. Each
Cartan subgroup of an identity component is connected and geometrically
nilpotent by `IsCartanSubgroup.structure_of_infiniteField`; semisimplicity of
its tangent lifts reflects along the actual closed subgroup embedding. The
resulting prescribed maximal-torus tangent lift proves maximality in `B`.
Finally, `isGeneratedByCartanSubgroups_of_isogeny` reflects generation to `A`
using the supplied torus lifts and smooth generated envelopes.

Only Cartan structure over the same infinite ground field is used. All
structural inputs are discharged; the theorem has no outer hypotheses. Lie
dimension equality under the isogeny does not assert differential
surjectivity and allows inseparable isogenies.
