# The jet of a smooth closed subgroup as a parameter point

For a separated ambient group, the actual jet of a closed subgroup over a
test scheme embeds as a closed subscheme of the pulled-back ambient jet. Its
defining ideal is the kernel of this actual morphism. Smoothness makes the
subgroup jet finite, flat and locally finitely presented over the test, so
the finite-flat subscheme parameter classifies it uniquely.

The square of subgroup jet inclusions over a map of tests is cartesian. This
uses the base-change squares for the ambient and subgroup jets and their
actual maps. It shows that the defining ideal pulls back to the ideal of the
new subgroup jet. A factorization of subgroup inclusions gives a factorization
of the corresponding jet inclusions, so equal closed subgroup functors give
equal jet ideals.

The canonical isomorphism of the jet with the subscheme defined by this
ideal respects their maps to the ambient group. Consequently a point
classifying a subgroup's jet pulls the universal stabilizer back to a
representative of the actual jet stabilizer. These comparisons implement
the map to the jet parameter in SGA 3, Exposé XV, §5.2–5.3.
