# Affine coordinates for finite matrix algebra unit points

This derived internal helper compares the all-test Weil tensor matrix coordinates
with affine coordinates. On an affine test Spec R, the actual relative global
sections are carried to R by the relative adjunction counit isomorphism. Tensoring
this isomorphism with the coefficient field produces the coefficient equivalence.

The matrix identity follows from the published corepresented affine comparison,
the naturality of the finite matrix algebra coefficient equivalence, and the
commutation of coefficient transport with exchanging tensor factors. It concerns
actual test points and imposes no Hermitian equation or rank conclusion.
