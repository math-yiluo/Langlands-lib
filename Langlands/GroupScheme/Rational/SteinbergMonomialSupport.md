Its value at each variable is the corresponding degree, and `steinbergCoefficientExponent_injective` proves injectivity by coordinatewise extensionality of the bounded natural numbers.

The proof applies coefficient extraction to the finite trace expansion in `SteinbergCoefficientTrace`. For a matching exponent, the existing coefficient-trace support theorem forces the trace to vanish if no such weight occurs. Dominance is supplied by the existing closed coefficient-path theorem.
