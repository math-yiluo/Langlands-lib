# Local congruence of a descended Hermitian Gram form

For an actual Hermitian matrix presentation and a supplied Galois Gram descent, `GaloisGramDescent.gramCongruentOver_of_baseChange_isTrivial` turns triviality of the cocycle on the full pullback to an extension field of characteristic zero into congruence over its full quadratic tensor coefficient algebra. The original cover is finite Galois. The result concerns the inverse of the original Gram matrix and the descended form.

Choose a finite Galois comparison field over the new base containing the original coefficient field over the given ground field. The pullback trivialization gives a special unitary primitive there. Comparing this primitive with the ambient primitive makes their quotient Galois fixed, hence a point of the ambient unit group over the new base. Acting with the inverse of that correction gives the desired inverse Gram congruence. Faithful extension of coefficients verifies the equation over the new base, and the matrix comparison identifies the full tensor algebra in which it holds.

This is a derived finite-cover formulation of the local Gram comparison in the preliminary unitary reduction of Kneser, *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups*, §5.5.
