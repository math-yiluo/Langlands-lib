# Isotropy of scalar special unitary groups over totally complex number fields

`NumberFieldTotallyComplexHermitianMatrixIsotropyStatement` gives positive split rank for an affine group scheme of locally finite type with an actual special unitary presentation by a nonsingular Hermitian matrix of size at least three. The base is a totally complex number field and the Hermitian form is over a separable quadratic extension.

If the Hermitian space were anisotropic, diagonalize it and take the ground-field quadratic form of its self-pairing. The polar form is nondegenerate because all the diagonal weights are nonzero. Its dimension is twice the Hermitian dimension. The form is isotropic at every complex completion and at every finite completion, where the anisotropic dimension bound is four. The number-field quadratic local-to-global theorem gives a nonzero isotropic vector for the original Hermitian space. The resulting totally isotropic line gives positive split rank in the actual group presentation.

This is the scalar quadratic-extension case of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Claim 6.2, p. 343, used for the A₂ subgroup on p. 353 and in Lemma 6.31 on p. 380. [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
