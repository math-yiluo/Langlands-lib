# Cubic splitting and trialitarian isotropy in type E₆

`NumberFieldTotallyComplexTypeE6CubicSplitIsotropyReductionStatement` reduces isotropy of a simply connected E₆ group split by a cubic Galois extension to isotropy of the trialitarian forms of D₄ over totally complex number fields.

A split maximal torus over the cubic field realizes the original actual Cartan numbering in a split pinning. If the E₆ group is anisotropic, the cubic parabolic intersection then gives an anisotropic simply connected absolutely almost-simple factor of type D₄ or D₅ over a finite extension.

For rank five the star action fixes the nonfork vertices, and the classical type D theorem gives isotropy over the totally complex extension. For rank four a leaf fixed by the full star action can be numbered as the vector vertex. This yields the classical branch of the same theorem. If every leaf has a mover, the trialitarian isotropy hypothesis applies. Each possibility contradicts anisotropy of the factor.

This is the lower-rank step in the cubic part of Platonov and Rapinchuk's *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Theorems 6.25 and 6.26, printed pp. 352–355. [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
