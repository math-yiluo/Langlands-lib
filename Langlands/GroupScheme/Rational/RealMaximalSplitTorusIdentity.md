# The real maximal split torus image in the identity component

This internal specialization extends the supplied maximal split torus to a
maximal real torus. The extension remains maximal over the complex numbers
and splits there. Simple connectedness ascends to the complexification of
the ambient group, so the coroots of the resulting split maximal torus span
its integral cocharacter lattice.

`FixedSimpleCorootSignsInIdentity` expresses the condition on its fixed
simple coroots. Each value at minus one is required to be the complex
extension of a point in the real identity component. The index action is
the ordinary complex conjugation action on the actual roots.

The ordinary Galois root-datum automorphism acts by conjugation on the
cocharacter lattice since conjugation is its own inverse. The split-torus
coroot reduction then puts the real points of the supplied split torus
in the identity component after mapping to the ambient group. The
identity-test point equivalence gives the same conclusion on the
evaluation-topologized real point type.
