# Simultaneous approximation in complex completions of a reductive group

The characteristic-zero smooth locus of a dominant parametrization
contains the generic point of its affine-space domain. Restricting to
a nonempty affine chart there gives a smooth morphism from an affine
open subscheme of an affine space. Over an algebraically closed field
its image and a translate of its image are nonempty opens of the
irreducible group. A point in their intersection gives two coefficient
points of the parameter scheme whose images have the prescribed
quotient.

For each place in the prescribed nonempty set of complex places,
continuity of the quotient map gives two open sets of parameter points
whose quotients belong to the prescribed group open. Apply weak
approximation in the coordinates of affine space to each family of
parameter opens, using the full coordinate space at every other place.
One prescribed place suffices to show that the resulting global points
lie in the parameter open subscheme, by reflection along its completion
field. Their quotient meets the prescribed group opens simultaneously.

The declaration is `exists_reductivePoint_mem_complexCompletion_opens`.
Its use of weak approximation at the complex places is the approximation
step of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*
(1994), Lemma 6.28, printed p. 370.
