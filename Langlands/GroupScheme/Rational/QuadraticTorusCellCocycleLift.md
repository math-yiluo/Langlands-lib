# Scheme cocycle lifting in the quadratic opposite torus cell

Coefficient points of the original group over the quadratic field are
identified with rational points of its scalar extension. Coefficient
conjugation identifies positive unipotent points with negative unipotent
points. These equivalences identify triples of the two opposite unipotent
points and a torus point with points of the source of the open cell.
Multiplication of the triple is injective because the cell is an open
immersion.

For a twisted cocycle value in the cell, the quadratic cochain identity
removes the two unipotent coordinates. The resulting torus-valued cocycle
has the specified middle coordinate as its value at the nonidentity
automorphism. Its image is cohomologous to the original cocycle on the
same scheme cover. This is the unique-coordinate calculation in
Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994),
Lemma 6.28, printed pp. 369–370.
