# Isotropy of quadratically split type E₆ over totally complex number fields

`NumberFieldTotallyComplexTypeE6QuadraticSplitIsotropyStatement` proves that a simply connected affine group of locally finite type with numbered E₆ Tits index has positive split rank over a totally complex number field if it splits over a quadratic Galois extension.

Under anisotropy, the descended centralizer construction gives a simply connected closed subgroup with a split A₂ pinning over the same quadratic field. The subgroup inherits anisotropy by monotonicity of split rank. Its split pinning supplies a scalar special unitary presentation of size three over the quadratic extension. The Hermitian isotropy theorem for totally complex fields gives positive split rank for that presentation, contradicting the inherited anisotropy.

This is the totally complex E₆ case of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.8, Lemma 6.31, printed pp. 379–380. [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
