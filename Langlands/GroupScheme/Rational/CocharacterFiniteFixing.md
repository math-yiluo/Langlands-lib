# Cocharacters over an algebraic extension are fixed by a finite-index subgroup

Author: Worker 47 (Task 6502).

Let `K/k` be a field extension and `G` an affine group scheme over `k`. Cocharacters of
`G_K = G ×_k K` are morphisms `ν : 𝔾_m,K → G_K`. The Library describes such a cocharacter by its
Laurent coordinates `GaloisCocharacterDescent.coordinates k K G ν`, an algebra map from the
coordinate algebra of `G` to `K[T, T⁻¹]`. It also defines the coefficient transport
`transportCocharacter` of `ν` along `σ ∈ Aut(K/k)`. This module proves that finitely many
cocharacters are fixed by the transport, and hence commute pointwise with Galois conjugation,
for all `σ` fixing one finite intermediate field.

## Statements

- `universalValue_transport`: the universal Laurent value of the transported cocharacter is
  the image, under coefficient conjugation `conjugateAlg k K σ`, of the universal value of `ν`.
  It combines the Library lemmas `universalProjection_transport` and
  `universalValue_of_projection`. The private helper `universalValue_of_projection'` restates
  the latter for a cocharacter given with the type of a transported one. Composing the two
  Library lemmas directly is correct but makes the kernel check impractically slow; the helper
  avoids this.
- `coordinates_transport`: the coordinates of the transported cocharacter are the coordinates
  of `ν` followed by `conjugateAlg k K σ`. This follows from `Lie.pointCoordinates_naturality`.
- `coordinates_injective`: a cocharacter is determined by its coordinates. The steps are:
  `Lie.pointCoordinates` is an equivalence, `laurentBaseIso` is an isomorphism,
  `baseChangePointsEquiv` is an equivalence, and `Gm.laurentPoint` is an isomorphism
  (the inverse of `relativeLaurentIso`).
- `transport_eq_of_coordinates`: if `σ` fixes the coordinates of `ν`, then the transport of
  `ν` along `σ` is `ν`.
- `exists_finite_fixing_coordinates`: assume `G` is locally of finite type and `K/k` is
  algebraic. For finitely many cocharacters `ν_j`, there is a finite-dimensional intermediate
  field `E` whose fixing subgroup fixes all their coordinates. Take generators `s` of the
  finite-type coordinate algebra (`Lie.coordinateAlgebraFiniteType`), and let `E` be the field
  generated by all Laurent coefficients of the coordinates of the `ν_j` at the elements of `s`.
  Conjugation acts on coefficients (`LaurentGaloisDescent.coeff_conjugate`), so it fixes
  these Laurent polynomials. An algebra map is determined by its values on generators
  (`AlgHom.ext_of_adjoin_eq_top`). The construction follows the Library's
  `IdentityComponent.exists_finite_fixing_algHoms`, which treats `K`-valued points.
- `exists_finite_fixing_cocharacterValues`: under the same hypotheses, every `σ` in the fixing
  subgroup of `E` satisfies
  `rationalConjugate k K G σ (rationalCocharacterValue k K G (ν j) t) =
   rationalCocharacterValue k K G (ν j) (σ t)` for all `t ∈ Kˣ`. Combine
  `transport_eq_of_coordinates` with `rationalCocharacterValue_rationalConjugate`, which says
  `σ(ν(t)) = (transported ν)(σ t)`.

## Provenance

This is internal infrastructure. It is the standard fact that a morphism of finite-type
schemes over an algebraic extension is defined over a finite subextension, specialised to
cocharacters and stated pointwise. It is needed for the finite orbits of the Galois action on
the unramified building (Task 6502).
