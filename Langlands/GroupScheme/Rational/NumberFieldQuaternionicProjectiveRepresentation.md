# Projective representation of a pure quaternion by a skew-hermitian form

`Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.NumberFieldQuaternionicProjectiveRepresentationStatement` concerns a nondegenerate skew-hermitian form over a quaternion division algebra over a number field. If it represents a specified nonzero pure quaternion at every completion, a nonzero global vector has value in the ground-field line of that quaternion. The coefficient is nonzero when the form is anisotropic. `numberFieldQuaternionicProjectiveRepresentation` proves this for every universe.

Choose the embedded quadratic field generated by the quaternion and form the nonsingular symmetric component over that field. At each place of the quadratic field, extend the given local vector from the completion at the underlying place. Scalar extension of the skew-hermitian pairing takes the same pure quaternion value. Projection to the tensor over the quadratic field gives a nonzero isotropic vector of the extended component. Hasse–Minkowski now gives a nonzero global isotropic vector of the component. Its value in the original pairing lies in the pure quaternion line by the coefficient calculation.

This implements the projective representation argument of T. A. Springer's appendix to Kneser, *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups*, Proposition 5.3.2, p. 109 (PDF p. 115). The conclusion records the case where the chosen nonzero vector has zero skew-hermitian value, which already gives isotropy of the original form.
