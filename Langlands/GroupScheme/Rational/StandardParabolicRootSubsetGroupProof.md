# The groups of the standard parabolic root triple

The theorem `Langlands.GroupScheme.standardParabolicRootSubsetGroup` proves
the exact published proposition `StandardParabolicRootSubsetGroupStatement`
at universe `u`. Over any field, for a connected reductive group and a maximal
split torus, it identifies every closed group satisfying the specified
generation property for the standard parabolic, Levi or unipotent root subset
with the corresponding standard subgroup functor. The ambient roots may be
the full relative roots or the indivisible relative roots. It also identifies
the root-subset Weyl group with the actual standard Levi Weyl group.

The published standard-cocharacter theorem supplies a cocharacter ζ vanishing
on Θ and positive on the other simple roots, together with the equalities
between the standard subgroup functors and its three dynamic subgroups.
The pairing descriptions in `StandardRootSubsetPairing` identify the
standard parabolic and Levi root subsets with the weak and zero ζ-pairing
loci. Negation stability then identifies the unipotent root subset with
the strict pairing locus. The pairing helper derives these identities directly:
the published indecomposable-root comparison gives induction in either
ambient set, and induction shows that every zero-pairing root lies in the
closed subset generated by both signs of Θ. Root-closure minimality and
the published standard-cocharacter sign lemma give the two descriptions.

`StandardRootSubsetRange` proves the three dynamic range equalities. Minimality
gives containment in the closed dynamic representatives, using the root subgroup
containment results for weak, zero and positive pairing. Conversely, the
parabolic uses published nonnegative-root closed generation. The centralizer
uses that theorem inside the dynamic centralizer, where the cocharacter
is central and its parabolic is the whole group. Intersecting the competitor
with the centralizer gives the required closed subgroup there. The radical
uses the published ordered product isomorphism of its indivisible positive
root groups. All these containments hold on every test scheme, and the
competitor need not be smooth or connected.

For the indivisible ambient set, any actual root is a positive natural
multiple of an indivisible root. The generic cocharacter or its negative
supplies the positive integral ray. Weak or zero ζ-pairing descends along
that ray, and the subgroup of a positive multiple is contained in the
subgroup of the indivisible root. Thus both allowed ambient root sets
supply the generators required by the parabolic and centralizer arguments.

The supporting module `StandardRootSubsetWeyl` proves the fourth conjunct using the published relative Weyl
Coxeter realization. A standard
cocharacter's pairing stabilizer is the standard Coxeter subgroup. Every
root of the Levi root closure pairs to zero, so its integral root reflections
preserve this pairing and lie in the stabilizer. Conversely, its simple
generators have integral reflection representatives with roots in Θ, hence
in the Levi root closure.

This is a derived proof of the already published group part of Renard V.4.2,
as formulated by `ClosedRootSubsetStatements`. Its implementation uses the
published results named in the imports and the four supporting modules
described above. It asserts neither existence of a generated closed subgroup
for an arbitrary root subset nor the separate root-subset intersection
statements.
