# The weights of a split component inside a relative root datum

**Exact target.** `Langlands.GroupScheme.LeviSplitComponentRootDatumStatement`.

Let `G` be a connected reductive group scheme over a field `F`, affine and locally of finite type over `Spec F` (explicit instance arguments), `p : P ⟶ G` a parabolic subgroup, `l : M ⟶ P` a rational Levi subgroup and `a : A ⟶ M` a split component. The statement asserts a commutative group scheme `T` whose underlying group is a split torus, a homomorphism `t : T ⟶ G` and a homomorphism `g : A ⟶ T` of commutative group schemes such that:

1. `g ≫ t = a ≫ l ≫ p`;
2. the real cocharacter map `cocharacterMapReal g : V A → V T` is injective;
3. its range is `rootAnnihilator T` of the set of relative roots `γ ∈ relativeRoots t` that vanish on that range, so that `V A` is the common kernel of the roots vanishing on it (a Levi flat of the root arrangement of `T`);
4. every relative root `γ` of `t` has a cocharacter `γv` of `T` with `latticePairing T γ γv = 2` such that, for every relative root `δ`, the reflected character `δ - ⟨δ, γv⟩ γ` is again a relative root and its weight space in `Lie G` has the same dimension as that of `δ`.

Clause 4 is required for every relative root, including multipliable and divisible roots of a non-reduced system. The torus `T` is not required to be maximal in the statement; a maximal split torus of `G` containing the image of `A` is the intended witness.

**Sources.** A. Borel and J. Tits, *Groupes réductifs* (1965), `borel-tits-1965-groupes-reductifs`: 4.15 b), printed pp.90–91 (PDF pp.37–38), the `k`-Levi subgroups of a parabolic are the centralizers of the maximal `k`-split tori of its radical; its proof compares the roots vanishing on a maximal torus of the radical with those vanishing on its maximal split subtorus; 5.1 and Théorème 5.3, printed pp.95–96 (PDF pp.42–43), the relative Weyl group of a maximal split torus is generated by the reflections in the hyperplanes of the `k`-roots and is represented by rational points of the normalizer; Corollaire 5.8, printed p.98 (PDF p.45), the `k`-roots form a root system with integral Cartan numbers. Representatives of the reflections in the normalizer conjugate weight spaces onto weight spaces, which gives the equality of dimensions in clause 4. For the maximal split witness, the image of `A` in `T` is the largest subtorus on which every root of `M` with respect to `T` is trivial; its real cocharacter space is the common kernel in clause 3. The homomorphism `t` is not required to be a closed immersion.

This file states the proposition and does not prove it.
