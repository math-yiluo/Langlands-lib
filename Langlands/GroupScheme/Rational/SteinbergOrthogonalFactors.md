# Actual Steinberg factors for orthogonal simple roots

This internal adapter applies the all-test theorem
`orthogonalSimpleRootCommutation` to supplied normalized simple-root maps
and supplied reflection representatives in the actual group scheme. It
then discharges the within-block commutation premise of the actual
Steinberg image-ideal descent construction.

The supplied representatives must belong to the rational point subgroups
generated by their positive and negative simple-root groups. The
proposition `SplitPinning.RootGeneratedSimpleReflectionRepresentatives`
records exactly this condition. It constructs no representatives.

For every test scheme `T` over the field and global scalar `c`,
`rootFactor_mem_generated` proves that the actual product
`x_a(c) n_a` belongs to `U(a)(T) ⊔ U(-a)(T)`. The root-map membership
comes from its represented-subgroup property. Precomposition preserves
membership in the subgroup supremum because it is a group homomorphism
and preserves each generating subgroup. In particular the rational
representative's membership pulls back along the structure morphism of `T`.
The generated subgroup is closed under the displayed multiplication.

`commute_rootFactors` applies the all-test orthogonal-simple-root theorem
to two such factors. The roots must be distinct orthogonal members of
the same supplied simple-root base. This holds on every scheme test,
including nonreduced tests, over arbitrary fields in universe `u`.

In `SteinbergOrbitImageDescent`, the actual base-changed group,
pinning, normalized root maps, representatives and coordinate equivalence
`d : Fin r ≃ I` are those of the existing comparison construction.
`factorK_mem_generated` pulls back the actual standard-coordinate factor
along its explicit polynomial-coordinate isomorphism.
`commute_factors` applies orthogonal-simple-root commutation over `K`
on that polynomial test and transfers the result through the
multiplicative affine base-change equivalence to the original group's
actual polynomial-valued points. `pairwise_factors` applies this result
to any ordered list of pairwise distinct orthogonal coordinate roots.

For an arbitrary Galois extension, `definingIdealDescent_of_orthogonal`
constructs descent of the full kernel ideal sheaf of the supplied actual
Steinberg morphism. Its premises are the root-generated membership just
described, the exact flattened coordinate order, preservation of each block
as a multiset, pairwise distinctness and orthogonality inside each block,
and transport of the actual factors under the combined coefficient and
variable action. It uses `pairwise_factors` to supply commutation to
`SteinbergOrbitImageDescent.definingIdealDescent`. Thus it retains the
actual morphism and full kernel ideal rather than replacing these by
sets of points or their radicals.

This is an internal derivation from the existing normalized-root
membership, all-test orthogonal-root commutation, actual polynomial-factor
comparison and conditional image-ideal descent APIs, together with the
universal property of point-subgroup suprema. No new assertion from
original literature is introduced. The independent orthogonal-root proof
is reused unchanged.

Compatible data existence, actual root-generated representative choices,
Galois factor transport and existence of the required orbit blocks remain
open. This adapter supplies neither immersion nor regularity, identifies
no canonical quotient and proves no rational lifting. It asserts no global
section in exceptional even type A and does not complete the parent
quasi-split quotient theorem. There is no perfectness, infinitude or
finite-degree hypothesis.
