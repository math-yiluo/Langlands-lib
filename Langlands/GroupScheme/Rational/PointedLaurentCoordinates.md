# Actual pointed Laurent pullbacks and primitive coordinate functions

Internal support for the split-torus domination criterion. For a corepresented
actual group scheme over an arbitrary field, primitive coordinate functions
form the existing coalgebra submodule of (1,1)-primitive elements. They are
exactly the functions additive on all algebra-valued points, vanish at the
identity, and are stable under the power given by the characteristic prime.

A pointed dominant morphism from an actual split torus to a reduced
corepresented group induces an injective map from its coordinate algebra into
a finite-rank Laurent algebra. The map intertwines the actual identity
augmentations. The construction presents the source torus by its diagonalizable
character group, uses full faithfulness of relative spectrum, and obtains
injectivity from dominance and reducedness of the target. Pointedness gives
the augmentation identity. The morphism need not preserve multiplication.

This unit does not provide finite primitive algebra generators or prove the
all-characteristic splitness criterion.
