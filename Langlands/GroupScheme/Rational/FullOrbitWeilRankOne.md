# Actual Weil restriction from a full orbit of rank-one factors

The Weil subgroup of a chosen geometric factor has the full group
as its range when that factor has full Galois orbit. This follows
by computing the range of the orbit subproduct over the splitting
field and reflecting equality along the field extension. The
closed inclusion is then an isomorphism.

The coefficient group is the descent of the chosen geometric factor
to the fixed field of its actual stabilizer. A one-vertex geometric
pinning gives a one-vertex Tits index for this coefficient group,
and absolute almost-simplicity and simple connectedness descend
as well. This identifies the Weil group used in the leaf part of
the trialitarian reduction of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic
Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed p. 374.
