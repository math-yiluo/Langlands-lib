# Compact subgroups of local unipotent rational points are pro-p

This internal argument uses the actual canonical rational-point topology and
the residue characteristic of a nonarchimedean local field.

`GeneralLinear.rationalPointsMatrixHomeomorph` equips the original rational
matrix equivalence with its topology. It agrees entrywise with the composition
of the canonical rational-to-affine homeomorphism and the affine matrix
homeomorphism.

A compact subgroup embedded as upper unitriangular matrices admits one diagonal
rescaling carrying all its elements into the actual standard principal
congruence subgroup. The rescaling and rational matrix comparison are
homeomorphisms, so the resulting homomorphism to that congruence subgroup is a
topological embedding. The congruence subgroup is pro-p, and pro-p transfers
to the original compact subgroup through this embedding.

For an affine unipotent group of finite type, the published closed
unitriangular representation supplies this embedding. Thus every compact
subgroup of its original rational-point group is pro-p, in its original
subspace topology. No splitness or smoothness assumption on the unipotent
group is added.

The provenance is the internal composition of the closed unitriangular
representation, strict compact rescaling, actual standard congruence pro-p
theorem and compact embedding inheritance. This result supplies a prerequisite
for the maximal pro-p theorem, without asserting its chamber or maximality
conclusions.
