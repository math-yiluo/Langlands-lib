# The solvable radical of the general linear group

For every field \(k\) and every positive matrix size, the scalar embedding
\(\mathbf G_m\to\mathrm{GL}_n\) is the solvable radical over \(k\) and over
the algebraic closure. In size zero the identity section of the trivial
torus gives the solvable radical. The predicates use the original
embeddings of the standard matrix group schemes.

This specializes the radical and centre assertions in David Renard,
*Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, French edition dated
9 July 2026, V.2.1, printed p. 104 (PDF p. 110). The calculation uses the
scalar-matrix description of the centre. Every invertible matrix of size
zero is the identity.
