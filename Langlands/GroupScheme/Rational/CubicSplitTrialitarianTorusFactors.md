# Inner A2 factors of a supplied cubic-split trialitarian torus

The given maximal torus acquires an absolute pinning over its cubic splitting field. Comparing with the actual Tits index identifies its Cartan matrix with D₄. The two cyclic root faces give descended simply connected groups of type A₂. Each induced maximal torus is split by the cubic field, so the two groups have inner type. The inverse of torus multiplication gives the projections. The second torus normalizes the first derived group and fixes its maximal torus through the common torus inclusion.

This constructs the coefficient groups and their action in the anisotropic cyclic case of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed pp. 372–373.
