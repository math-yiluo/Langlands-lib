# Symmetric closed subsets of relative roots

Worker 237, with a source and documentation contribution from Worker 613,
Task 6510. Module
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.SymmetricRootSubsetWeylProof`, namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme`.

`symmetricRootSubsetWeyl.{u}` proves the exact published proposition
`SymmetricRootSubsetWeylStatement.{u}` from
[ClosedRootSubsetStatements](ClosedRootSubsetStatements.md).

For an arbitrary field `k`, a connected reductive group scheme `G` over
`Spec k`, and a maximal split torus `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G`, let `R` be either
`relativeRoots i` or `RelativeRoot.indivisibleRoots i`. If `X` is closed in
`R` and stable under negation, the theorem proves:

1. every root `β ∈ X` has an integral root reflection in
   `relativeWeylGroup i`;
2. every `w` satisfying `IsRootReflection i w β` for any `β ∈ X` maps
   `X` onto itself;
3. every element of `rootSubsetWeylGroup i X`, the subgroup generated by
   those reflections, maps `X` onto itself.

Here an integral root reflection means that there is a cocharacter `c`
with `latticePairing T β c = 2` and action
`χ ↦ χ - latticePairing T χ c • β` on the entire character lattice.
The theorem adds no hypotheses to the published proposition.

## Provenance and scope

The source sentence is Renard V.4.2, printed p.118 / PDF p.124 of
David Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*,
French edition dated 9 July 2026, registered as
`renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, SHA256
`7b539996793b7c3620e72e1e389588eb42d1a8e91d6f68b2eead5c2e2ffc182c`.
The source defines a symmetric set as a closed subset of the reduced
indivisible root set that equals its negative, and asserts that it is a
root system with Weyl group contained in the ambient Weyl group.
The published target expresses this through integral reflections and
preservation by their generated subgroup; it does not request a new
Mathlib root pairing on `X`.

The source comparison, the distinction between the full relative roots
and the indivisible roots, and the separate source issues concerning
group intersections are documented in
[ClosedRootSubsetStatements.md](ClosedRootSubsetStatements.md).
This proof establishes the symmetric-set proposition for both permitted
ambient sets. As in the target, the base field is arbitrary; Renard works
over a nonarchimedean local field. The group-intersection and other
components of `ClosedRootSubsetStatements` are outside this theorem's
conclusion.

The proof uses published root-system results and Mathlib root strings.
Its three private helper lemmas are derived arguments implementing the
proof, rather than additional translations of source assertions.

## Published inputs

`relativeRootReflectionExistence` supplies integral reflection data
`D : RelativeRootReflectionData i` for every full relative root.
`relativeRealCoroot` supplies finiteness and two crystallographic real
root pairings: one indexed by the full relative roots, and one indexed
by the indivisible roots. Both live on `relativeRootSpanReal i`, with
root vectors `1 ⊗ α`; their coroot space is `D.realCorootSpan`.
See [RootReflectionExistenceProof.md](RootReflectionExistenceProof.md) and
[RelativeRealCorootProof.md](RelativeRealCorootProof.md).

The actual Weyl action preserves the full roots by
`weylAction_mapsTo_relativeRoots` and the indivisible roots by
`RelativeRootSystem.weylAction_indivisible`. Quotient induction gives
these preservation statements for every element of `relativeWeylGroup i`.
The injectivity of the character map into its real scalar extension is
`RelativeRootSystem.character_realTensor_injective`, using that `T` is split.

## Closure under a root-pairing reflection

The private lemma `add_nsmul_mem_of_steps` is an induction on the number of
additions. If `x` and `a` lie in a subset `Y`, every intermediate
`x + m • a` lies in the ambient set, and `Y` is closed under sums that
belong to that ambient set, then `x + n • a` lies in `Y`.

`reflection_root_mem_of_closed_symmetric` applies this to a finite
crystallographic real root pairing `P`. For linearly independent roots
`P.root a` and `P.root b`, Mathlib's
`root_sub_zsmul_mem_range_iff` and `root_add_zsmul_mem_range_iff`
describe the unbroken root string. The reflected root is itself a root;
using it as an endpoint gives bounds that include every intermediate
step between `P.root b` and its reflection.

The proof splits the integral Cartan entry `P.pairingIn ℤ b a` into a
nonnegative integer or a negative natural number. According to its sign,
it repeatedly adds `-P.root a` or `P.root a`. Symmetry supplies the
negative root, and closedness keeps all these steps in `Y`.
For linearly dependent roots,
`pairing_smul_root_eq_of_not_linearIndependent` makes the reflection of
`P.root b` equal to `-P.root b`, so symmetry proves the conclusion directly.

## Comparing an arbitrary integral reflection

The private lemma `reflection_mapsTo_of_real_pairing` applies to either
published real pairing. Fix any `w` and `c` witnessing
`IsRootReflection i w β`, where `β ∈ X`. Extend evaluation against `c`
to a real linear functional `f` on the root span by restricting
`(pair T).flip (1 ⊗ c)`.

The specified action formula shows that
`Module.preReflection (P.root β) f`, viewed in `Vstar T`, agrees on all
roots with the actual character action followed by scalar extension.
It therefore preserves the finite spanning set of roots, and
`f (P.root β) = 2`. Mathlib's
`Module.Dual.eq_of_preReflection_mapsTo` identifies `f` with
`P.coroot' β`: both functionals give pre-reflections preserving this
finite spanning set and take value two on the reflecting root.
Consequently the supplied action agrees with `P.reflection β` on roots.
This covers every `w` in the target's second clause; the chosen data `D`
are used to supply existence and the published pairings.

Let `Y` be the root-span vectors that are real images of characters in
`X`. The character-map injectivity transfers the closedness and
negation stability of `X` to `Y`, with ambient set `Set.range P.root`.
The preceding private root-string lemma proves that `P.reflection β`
preserves `Y`. Comparing it with the actual Weyl action, then applying
injectivity again, proves that `w` maps `X` into `X`.

For the indivisible case the proof uses the pairing indexed by the
indivisible roots themselves. Thus every intermediate root of its
root string already belongs to that ambient set, exactly where the
closedness hypothesis applies.

## Image equality and the generated subgroup

The formula for `IsRootReflection`, together with the self-pairing
`⟨β, c⟩ = 2`, gives involutivity of the character action by
`Module.involutive_preReflection`. Preservation in one direction
therefore gives `relativeWeylCharacterAction i w '' X = X`.

For existence, `X ⊆ R ⊆ relativeRoots i` lets the theorem take
`D.representative β`, its quotient Weyl element, and `D.coroot β`.
The fields `D.pair_self` and `D.action` give precisely the required
integral reflection formula.

Finally, `Subgroup.closure_induction` proves preservation for the
generated subgroup. The generator case uses the image equality just
proved, the identity case is immediate, and multiplication composes
the two images. For inverses, apply the inverse action to the known
image equality and use the group action law to reduce the composite
to the identity. These establish exactly the three conjuncts of the
published target.
