# Coboundaries near the identity of a complex torus

In coordinates for a split complex torus, the principal square root is
continuous at the identity. For a norm-one point `x` near the identity,
the product of its square root and the conjugate of that square root is
two-torsion and is near the identity. Each coordinate of a two-torsion
point is `1` or `−1`. An open neighbourhood excluding `−1` in every
coordinate therefore forces the product to be the identity. The original
point is the quotient of its square root by its conjugate.

The construction transports along the split-torus point homeomorphism
and a topological field isomorphism to `ℂ`. It gives the open
neighbourhood used for torus cocycles in Platonov and Rapinchuk,
*Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), Lemma 6.28, printed p. 370,
where the openness follows from Proposition 3.3.
