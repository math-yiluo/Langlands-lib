# Simultaneous archimedean correction through Weil torsion

Let the central kernel be identified with positive-exponent torsion in
the actual Weil multiplicative group for a number field extension.
At each infinite place, a trivialization of the projection gives a
cochain for the source and local kernel coefficients. Tensor-unit
Hilbert 90 gives a radical of these coefficients over the algebraic
closure of the completion. Its power descends to the completed tensor
by fixed unit descent in each completed coordinate.

Approximate all these local powers modulo the indicated exponent by
one extension-field unit. Its tensor root on a finite Galois refinement
gives a global kernel cocycle because the root's crossed coboundary
is killed by the exponent. At each place embed the refinement into
the same local closure compatibly with the original cover. Divide
the global root by the local radical and by the fixed completed root
of the power ratio. The quotient is a tensor root of unity. Its
kernel point compares the two cocycles by a crossed coboundary, so
the corrected source is trivial on the full local pullback cover.

This internal construction gives one natural correction for the
whole family of archimedean restrictions.
