# The split torus quotient of the E₆ branch Levi

The cocenter quotient of the connected reductive branch centralizer is a torus.
Over the separable closure the maximal torus of the centralizer maps onto its
cocharacter lattice. Every simple coroot except the short-arm coroot maps to
zero because it comes from the derived group. The rational branch coweight
has coefficient two at the remaining coroot in the integral coroot basis.
A character of the geometric cocenter that pairs to zero with the image of
the branch coweight thus pairs to zero with every cocharacter. The image of
the rational branch line is consequently surjective onto the cocenter, and
the quotient torus is split.
