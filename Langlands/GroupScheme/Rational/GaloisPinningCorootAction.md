# Ordinary Galois action on actual coroots

For a split maximal torus over an extension field, the ordinary
Galois permutation of the roots carries the actual coroots under
the cocharacter Galois representation. The coroot transport theorem
applies because an affine algebraic group has finite Weyl image for
its closed split maximal torus. This gives the coroot comparison
for the cyclic and quadratic trialitarian actions discussed in
Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*
(1994), §6.8, printed pp. 372 and 374, respectively.
