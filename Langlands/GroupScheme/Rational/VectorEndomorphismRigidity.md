# A scalar-linear vector map is determined by its differential

Worker 1368. Internal proof derived from the published natural vector-coordinate,
epsilon-point and finite Lie scalar-extension interfaces. The coefficient
calculation adapts the published `VectorLinearAction` proof for an action to
postcomposition by one group-scheme endomorphism.

`IsCoordinateLinear e f` says that postcomposition by `f` commutes with scalar
multiplication in the supplied vector coordinates on every test scheme.
The first lemma transports this equation to affine coefficient coordinates
through the canonical global-functions equivalence for a spectrum.

Multiplying the constant lift of an affine point by epsilon produces its
actual identity-kernel tangent point. Scalar linearity therefore identifies
the coefficients of the differential on that point with the coefficients
of its image under `f`. If the differential is identity over the ground
field, natural finite Lie coordinates extend this equality to every
coefficient algebra: tensor induction reduces to coefficient change of a
base vector, and the actual target differential commutes with that change.

The epsilon-point calculation then proves fixedness of every affine point.
Affine-test extensionality proves `f` is the identity on all test schemes.
This retains arbitrary characteristic and dimension, including dimension
zero. Scalar linearity and natural vector coordinates are explicit premises;
no unrestricted assertion that a vector-group map is determined by its
Lie differential is made.
