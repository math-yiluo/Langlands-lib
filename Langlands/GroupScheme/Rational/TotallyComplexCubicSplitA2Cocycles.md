# Cocycle vanishing through a cubic-split trialitarian torus

The projections of the torus cocycle give cocycles of the two inner A₂ groups. The second one trivializes by the inner type A theorem over a totally imaginary number field. Its action fixes the first maximal torus, so the same torus lies in the twisted first coefficient group. It remains split by the cubic field. The Cartan matrix is preserved by the geometric isomorphism of the two coefficient groups, so the cubic splitting forces the twisted group to have inner type A₂ as well.

The componentwise cocycle calculation now trivializes the image of the original cocycle. This is the two-factor argument of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed pp. 372–373.
