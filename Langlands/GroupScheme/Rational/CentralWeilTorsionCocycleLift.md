# Finite Galois lifting through Weil torsion

Let `B/F` be an extension of number fields. When a central kernel
is identified with the indicated torsion subgroup of the actual
Weil multiplicative group, its point coordinates on affine tests
are tensor roots of unity. The coordinate map factors through
the torsion inclusion into the Weil group and is natural in
the coefficient algebra.

For positive exponent, start with a target cocycle on a finite
Galois cover that is trivial on the complete pullbacks at all
places. Enlarge the cover to contain `B` and choose another
finite Galois refinement carrying pointwise lifts. In tensor-unit
cohomology the defect of these lifts vanishes by the global
Shapiro comparison and local triviality. Its values are killed
by the exponent, so finite Galois radical refinement gives a
primitive with values in the tensor roots of unity. The kernel
point equivalence carries that primitive to the central kernel
and the central lifting criterion gives a natural lifted
cocycle. Composing the field refinements records the lift
over the original coefficient cover.

This internal argument provides the global lifting step for
Weil torsion central kernels.
