# Invariant augmentation functions detect connected components

This derived supporting unit concerns the actual coordinate algebra of an affine
group scheme over an arbitrary field, with an action by a commutative group
scheme. `HasTrivialInvariantAugmentation b` means that the zero character does
not occur in the actual augmentation support. The final theorem
`connectedSpace_of_trivialInvariantAugmentation` proves that a smooth affine
group satisfying this condition is connected. No split-torus, characteristic,
perfection or algebraic-closure assumption is imposed on this result.

The coordinate and action conventions are the published ones in
`CoordinateAction`: eigenfunction identities hold for every coefficient algebra
and every point of both group schemes over that algebra. The identity component
and stability used here are the actual open and closed subgroup and all-test
stability from `IdentityComponent` and `IdentityComponentAction`. This is a
derived argument using these Library constructions and Mathlib's affine
open-immersion criterion, rather than a transcription of a source theorem.

## Coordinate kernels and stable idempotents

`mem_range_iff_coordinates_ker` expresses membership in the subgroup functor
represented by a homomorphism with surjective coordinate pullback as vanishing
of its coordinate kernel. The reverse implication factors the coordinate map
through that surjection and uses the affine point-coordinate equivalence to
construct a point of the source. If the kernel is generated by one element,
`mem_range_iff_coordinates_eq_zero` reduces the condition to vanishing of that
element. These assertions concern every coefficient algebra, including
nonreduced ones.

`idempotent_eq_of_span_eq` proves that two idempotents generating the same
principal ideal are equal: each generator is divisible by the other, and
idempotence gives that their product equals both generators.

`isEigenfunction_zero_of_stable_zero_locus` proves invariance of an idempotent
whose zero locus is stable on all coefficient algebras. Given points `s` and `g`
over `A`, pass to `A` modulo the principal ideal generated by `g(e)`. The image of
`g` now vanishes on `e`, so stability and naturality show that `(s.g)(e)` belongs
to that principal ideal. Applying the same argument to `s` inverse and `s.g`
gives the reverse inclusion. Evaluation preserves idempotence, so equality of
the two principal ideals implies equality of the evaluated idempotents. This is
precisely the zero-character eigenfunction identity.
`isEigenfunction_zero_of_stable_principal_kernel` applies this result using the
coordinate-kernel membership criterion for a stable represented subgroup.

## Connectedness criterion

`exists_idempotent_kernel_of_isOpenImmersion` conjugates an affine subgroup
inclusion by the actual coordinate presentations. Mathlib's
`isOpenImmersion_SpecMap_iff_of_surjective` then supplies an idempotent generator
of its kernel. Surjectivity of coordinate pullback and openness of the inclusion
are explicit hypotheses.

For the final theorem, take the actual identity-component inclusion `G⁰ → G`.
It is an open and closed immersion, and its coordinate pullback is surjective.
The preceding lemma supplies an idempotent kernel generator `e`. Because the
inclusion preserves the identity, `e` belongs to the augmentation. Stability of
the identity component makes it an invariant eigenfunction. Absence of the zero
augmentation weight forces `e = 0`. The coordinate pullback is therefore both
injective and surjective. Its algebra equivalence gives an isomorphism of the
actual affine schemes, and connectedness transfers from `G⁰` to `G`.

The result supplies a connectedness tool for the zero-excluded semigroup
intersection case. This unit does not itself prove the semigroup intersection
equality, the remaining intersection alternatives, or the host comparison
statement.
