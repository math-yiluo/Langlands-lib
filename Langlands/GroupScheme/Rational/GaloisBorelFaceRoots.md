# Absolute roots and coroots of a descended Levi derived group

The maximal torus of the derived group is the intersection with
the original maximal torus. Over the splitting field, the roots
of the derived group correspond to the roots of the ambient
group whose restriction to the image of the cocharacter is
zero. The torus centralizer and dynamic centralizer formulas
identify this condition with a zero cocharacter pairing. The
comparison restricts actual characters and pushes forward
actual coroots along the map of tori.

Nonnegative values on simple roots imply nonnegative values on
positive roots, by their expressions as nonnegative simple-root
sums. Thus the inherited base consists of the original simple
roots with zero pairing. This is the absolute root comparison
used for the derived-Levi factors of the opposite-Borel torus
in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*
(1994), §6.8, printed p. 374.
