# Cyclic cubic splitting of inner E₆ from quasi-split vanishing

`NumberFieldInnerTypeE6CubicSplittingStatement` concerns an affine locally finite-type simply connected absolutely almost simple E₆ group over a totally complex number field. Innerness means that the actual star action fixes every simple vertex. The conclusion gives a degree-three Galois extension inside the separable closure over which the group has a split maximal torus.

`numberFieldInnerTypeE6CubicSplitting_of_quasiSplitVanishing` assumes first cohomology vanishes on finite Galois covers for quasi-split simply connected E₆ groups over totally complex number fields. It chooses the quasi-split inner form and transports the Cartan numbering and the star action using the based root-datum comparison for an inner discrepancy. The quasi-split model therefore has a split maximal torus, and its central quotient to the adjoint group has kernel `μ₃`.

A finite Galois refinement supplies central lift data for the adjoint discrepancy cocycle. The cubic central lifting theorem makes this discrepancy the image of a cocycle of the quasi-split model after base change to a cyclic cubic field and refinement over that field. The base-changed model still has the same absolute type and the field is totally complex, so quasi-split vanishing trivializes the lifted cocycle. Triviality gives descent of the local isomorphism on the pulled-back cover. The descended isomorphism transports the split maximal torus of the model to the required one.

This is the splitting step for inner E₆ in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), Theorem 6.26, printed p. 354 (PDF p. 364).
