# Kummer cocycles of tensor radicals with prescribed norm

A tensor radical of a pair `(b,s)` over a coefficient field `E` is a
unit `z` of `B ⊗[F] E` with `z^n` equal to the left-factor image of
`b` and determinant norm equal to the scalar image of `s`.
For number fields and `n > 0`, a pair with `N(b) = s^n` has such a
radical after finite Galois refinement of any given finite Galois
coefficient cover. First enlarge the cover to contain `B`. Over a
further finite Galois refinement adjoin a tensor root of `b`. The
ratio `s/N(z)` is killed by `n` and lifts by the split tensor norm
to a tensor unit killed by `n`. Multiplying by this unit gives the
prescribed norm.

The tensor coboundary `σ(z)/z` is killed by `n` and has norm one
because its power and norm are coboundaries of fixed units. For a
central isogeny kernel identified with norm-one torsion, the inverse
kernel point equivalence converts these coefficients to actual kernel
points. The tensor-unit crossed identity and equivariance give the
natural cocycle on the finite Galois cover.

This internal construction applies finite Galois tensor roots and the
split tensor norm to the degree-one correction of a central lift.
