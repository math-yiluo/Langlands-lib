# The torus product of the two cyclic A2 faces

The norm-zero action on the cubic-split maximal torus gives two cyclic pairs whose coroots form an integral basis of its cocharacter lattice. The actual derived-Levi tori have those pairs as the images of their simple coroot bases. Restriction of characters is therefore bijective over the splitting field. The corresponding torus multiplication is an isomorphism by faithfully flat descent.

This proves the torus product identity of the two subgroups in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed p. 373.
