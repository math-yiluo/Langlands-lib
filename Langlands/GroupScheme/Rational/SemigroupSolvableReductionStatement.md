# The algebraically closed unipotent core and solvable reduction

This file states the unipotent core of the ordered-product theorem and the
reduction from that core to the exact published
`SemigroupOrderedProductStatement`. Both are unproved proposition definitions.
Neither is an axiom, a supplied geometric construction, or a proof of the
ordered-product theorem.

The original source is Conrad–Gabber–Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive Groups*,
second edition (2015), registered as
`conrad-gabber-prasad-2015-pseudo-reductive`, SHA256
`1b77af44c90211db9a3efa50896a3722f4fd2df332b7ce2ec52202eb243e7f54`.
Theorem 3.3.11 is on printed pages 114–116 (PDF pages 140–142). The first
two paragraphs of its proof, on printed page 115 (PDF page 141), pass to an
algebraic closure and reduce the solvable group to its unipotent radical.
The rest of the proof treats the unipotent core by central vector subgroups
and dimension induction.

`UnipotentSemigroupOrderedProductStatement` retains the actual automorphic
action of a split torus on a smooth connected affine group, and adds the
assumptions that the field is algebraically closed and the group is
unipotent. It uses the existing `IsUnipotent` predicate. A separate
solvability assumption is unnecessary in this core. It quantifies over every
finite ordered partition of all actual action weights and retains exactly
the original disjointness and cross-exclusion hypotheses: each original
block avoids the positive additive semigroup generated by each other block.
It does not require the generated semigroups to be disjoint. Zero is
included whenever it occurs, and `AddSubsemigroup.closure` does not insert
zero. Arbitrary orderings and `n = 0` are included.

The core's conclusion supplies actual smooth connected closed
representatives of the existing all-test semigroup subgroup functors and
requires the existing ordered multiplication morphism to be a scheme
isomorphism. Bijectivity on field-valued points does not express this
conclusion.

`SemigroupSolvableReductionStatement` is the implication from this core at
universe `u` to the unchanged `SemigroupOrderedProductStatement` at the same
arbitrary universe. In particular, its conclusion applies over every field,
not only algebraically closed fields. This implication packages the source
reduction as a separate proof obligation. It includes the required
compatibility with extension to an algebraic closure and descent of the
representatives and actual multiplication isomorphism.

Over the algebraic closure, the source chooses a maximal torus centralized
by the acting torus and writes the solvable group as its semidirect product
with the unipotent radical. If zero occurs, cross-exclusion puts it into a
unique original block and excludes it from every other generated semigroup.
The maximal torus lies in that block's subgroup and normalizes the other
subgroups. The source then uses the semigroup intersection comparison and
passes the torus factor through the ordered product. These geometric
constructions and comparisons remain obligations of a proof of the
reduction. This file neither assumes their outputs as additional premises
nor claims to implement them.

The core is the unipotent specialization of the original theorem. The
reduction implication is a derived formulation of the source's first
reduction, introduced to keep it separate from central vector subgroup
existence, equivariant quotients, and dimension induction. It leaves the
published full target unchanged.
