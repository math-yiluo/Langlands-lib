# Actual base-cotangent duality and action pairing

This module proves `CotangentActionPairingStatement` at every universe, over every field and every commutative coefficient algebra `A`, using the original affine group, its actual dual-number identity kernel, and the cotangent of its actual tensor coordinate augmentation. It requires no finite-type or smoothness hypothesis.

The construction extends the coordinate map of an actual dual-number identity-kernel point to an `A`-algebra map on `A ⊗[k] Coord(G)`, using the universal property of the tensor product. Its constant part is the original tensor augmentation. Taking its infinitesimal coefficient defines an `A`-linear map to derivations at that augmentation. This map is bijective: a derivation gives a first-order algebra lift, whose restriction to `Coord(G)` is the coordinate map of an actual identity-kernel point. Composing this equivalence with the split-augmentation duality from `CoordinateCotangentDualityProof` gives `kernelBaseCotangentEquiv`. On every class extended from the original augmentation ideal it evaluates to the prescribed `Lie.kernelDerivationEquiv` value.

The action calculation retains the original points throughout. Evaluating `actionCoordinates b s` at the extended kernel coordinate map acts on the actual point by the constant dual-number lift of `s`. This follows from naturality of the affine coordinate equivalence, the action's functoriality, and the extension's coefficient map. The tensor universal property then identifies the extended coordinate map of `b.differential s X` with the composite of the coordinate map of `X` and the direct pullback `actionPullback b s`. Taking infinitesimal coefficients and passing to representatives of the actual augmentation cotangent proves `kernelBaseCotangentEquiv_action` on every quotient class. The witness in the exact target is `b.differential s X`, whose underlying actual scheme point is the specified acted point by definition.

This proof implements the direct transpose convention `(s.f)(g) = f(s.g)` in Conrad–Gabber–Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive Groups*, second edition (2015), §3.3, printed p.107 / PDF p.133. The tensor-coordinate and derivation arguments are derived implementation infrastructure for that source comparison. No inverse acting point or inverse character enters the construction. The support comparison and the full conjunction are separate proof obligations.
