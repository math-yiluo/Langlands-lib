# Coxeter relations for actual opposite-root lifts

This internal supporting unit derives the Coxeter relations modulo the subgroup
generated by rank-one centralizer intersections. Its inputs include a relative
root-system realization, a split torus, a generic cocharacter, a Coxeter system
on the actual relative Weyl quotient, and a chosen normalizer lift for each
simple root. Each lift has the prescribed simple Weyl class and belongs to the
subgroup generated by its two opposite root groups. The unit does not construct
these data or prove the unconditional `RootGeneratedCentralizerRankOneStatement`.

The field and group schemes lie in an arbitrary universe. Reducedness and
nonemptiness of the root system are not assumed.

## Signed root labels

`SignedSimpleReflectionLaw` says that conjugation taking the simple reflection
labelled by `a` to that labelled by `b` takes the actual character `a` to `b`
or `-b`. The reflection action negates its root, by descending the published
representative formula. For a positive image root, the published
`HasRelativeRootSystem.root_eq_of_conj_simple_reflections` identifies the label
with `b`. Otherwise genericity makes the pairing negative. Composing the
conjugating element on the right with the reflection at `a` negates this
image, preserves the conjugacy equation, and reduces to the positive case.
Root negation stability comes from the supplied root-system realization and
splitness.

## Lift conjugacy and relations

Write `N` for the actual rational normalizer, `Z` for the whole-torus
centralizer, and `H0` for `rankOneCentralizerGeneratedPoints`. Conjugating a
chosen opposite-root lift at `a` by any `n` in `N` puts it in the
opposite-root generated subgroup at the character `n(a)`. When its Weyl
conjugacy class is the simple reflection at `b`, the signed label theorem
puts it in the opposite-root group at `b`, since this group is unchanged on
negating its label. It has the same Weyl class as the chosen lift at `b`.
The published helper `oppositeGenerated_lifts_eq_mod_rankOneCentralizer`
therefore identifies their images in `N/H0`. This proves the full
`CompatibleSimpleConjugacy` condition.

Each lift has square in `H0`, because its Weyl class is involutive and its
square remains in the same opposite-root generated subgroup. Applying
`CoxeterLift.isLiftable_of_conjugacy` now proves all Coxeter relations among
the lift images in `N/H0`; the generic criterion handles every matrix entry,
including zero and diagonal entries. The final theorem supplies the earlier
actual normalizer kernel inclusion without a separate lifted-relation premise.
