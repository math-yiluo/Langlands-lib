# Standard root closures in either relative-root ambient set

This internal helper module supports the proof of the published
`StandardParabolicRootTripleStatement` (Renard V.4.2, printed p.118).
It treats both the full relative roots of Borel–Tits and Renard's indivisible roots.

The published `HasRelativeRootSystem.indivisiblePositiveRootComparison` identifies
the indecomposable positive roots in the indivisible ambient set with the actual
simple relative roots. Together with the defining equality in the full ambient
set, it gives induction by the positive integer pairing with the generic
cocharacter. Each decomposition and its sum remain in the chosen ambient set.
Negation stability of that set follows from the published full-root stability
and, in the indivisible case, negation of any proposed multiple relation.

For a standard cocharacter ζ vanishing on Θ and positive on the other simple
roots, a closed set containing both signs of Θ contains every ambient root killed
by ζ. The induction proves this simultaneously for a positive root and its
negative: nonnegative pairings of the summands must both vanish. Genericity
reduces every root to this positive case.

The integral subgroup generated by Θ is killed by ζ, by the kernel property of
additive subgroup closure. Conversely, a root killed by ζ, or its negative,
is positive and lies in the additive monoid generated by Θ by the published
standard-cocharacter lemma. This proves the integral-span characterization on
roots. The same nonnegativity and negation argument shows that positive ζ-pairing
forces positivity for the original generic cocharacter.

These are derived lemmas for the implemented proof, rather than additional
claims attributed verbatim to the literature. The root-system and cocharacter
inputs are imported from their published Library modules.

The shared ambient-root inclusion lemma is imported from
[RootSubsetAmbient](RootSubsetAmbient.md).
