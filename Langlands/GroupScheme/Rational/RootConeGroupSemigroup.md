# Cone slices and character-semigroup groups

This internal helper identifies the closed subgroup generated by the whole-torus
centralizer and the actual root groups in a real cone with the published
character-semigroup subgroup for the integral characters in that cone. No
closedness hypothesis on the cone is needed for this identification.

The integral characters whose tensors lie in the cone form an additive
semigroup containing zero. Each selected root brings its positive integral
cyclic tail into this semigroup. The published cyclic-root comparison therefore
puts its root functor inside the semigroup subgroup. The whole-torus centralizer
is smooth, connected, stable, and has only zero tangent weight, so it too is an
admissible semigroup generator. Minimality of the supplied root-subset group
then puts that group inside the semigroup subgroup on every test scheme.

For the reverse identification, the selected weight span lies in the tangent
image of the supplied group: weight zero comes from its centralizer clause;
each nonzero occurring selected weight is an actual relative root and comes
from its root-group clause; nonoccurring weight spaces vanish. The initial
inclusion and the published exact semigroup Lie-image theorem give the opposite
tangent bound. The supplied group is smooth and connected by
`RootSubsetGroupGeometry`; the included smooth connected closed groups with
equal Lie image are equal by the published full-Lie subgroup theorem.

This is an internal derivation from the imported Library APIs. It does not
assert that two such groups have connected intersection or prove the final
convex-root-subset intersection statement.
