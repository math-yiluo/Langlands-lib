# A prescribed maximal torus in a pure inner twist

Over an infinite field, rational points of a maximal torus meet the
geometric strongly regular open subset. Faithfully flat descent of
factorization through the closed torus gives the all-test centralizer
criterion over the original field.

For a pure inner form of a quasi-split simply connected group, Steinberg's
rational conjugacy theorem transfers such a point from a prescribed torus
in the inner form. Adjust the local chart by the conjugating element over
the resulting finite Galois refinement. Its inverse chart identifies the
two rational points, so the inverse cocycle commutes with the point in the
inner form and factors through its torus. If its image is trivial in the
inner form, the cocycle difference criterion gives triviality on the
original cover. This gives the torus reduction used by Platonov and
Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), Proposition 6.18
and §6.8, pp. 371–373, for pure twists.
