# Standard-cocharacter spans and Levi inclusion

This internal auxiliary develops a direct consequence of the published
`IsStandardCocharacter`, `standardLevi`, and `Dynamic.centralizingCocharacters`
constructions. It is a derived proof step for `StandardParabolicOrderStatement`,
not an additional structural hypothesis or a proof of that full statement.

`IsStandardCocharacter.nonneg` observes that each simple relative root has zero
pairing on the selected subset and strictly positive pairing off it. Thus every
simple relative root has nonnegative pairing.

`IsStandardCocharacter.add_of_subset` says that if `J ⊆ K`, a standard
cocharacter for `J` plus a standard cocharacter for `K` is standard for `J`.
Both summands vanish on `J`. Off `J`, the first pairing is positive and the
second is nonnegative, so the sum is positive.

`standardCocharacterSpan i ν J` is the integer submodule generated by all
standard cocharacters for `J`. Its definition is choice free, including when
there are no such cocharacters. The lemma
`mem_standardLevi_iff_standardCocharacterSpan_le` identifies standard Levi
membership with inclusion of this span in the integer submodule of
cocharacters centralizing the given point. This is the universal property of
submodule span applied to the defining standard-Levi intersection.

`standardCocharacterSpan_antitone_of_exists` proves that if `J ⊆ K` and a
standard cocharacter `zeta` for `J` exists, then the span for `K` lies in the
span for `J`. For a standard cocharacter `eta` for `K`, both `zeta` and
`zeta + eta` are standard for `J`, so their difference `eta` lies in its span.
Taking spans gives the claimed inclusion.

`standardLevi_mono_of_exists` proves `standardLevi i ν J ≤ standardLevi i ν K`
from `J ⊆ K` and existence of one standard cocharacter for `J`. A point of the
first Levi centralizes the span for `J`. The span inclusion just proved
therefore puts the span for `K` in the published integer submodule
`Dynamic.centralizingCocharacters i g`. The standard-Levi membership equivalence
gives membership in the second Levi. Thus subtraction is used once in the
span-inclusion proof, before passing to centralizers.

The argument applies over any field at universe `u`, to an affine group scheme
and a morphism from a commutative group scheme. It uses the actual subgroup
functors on all test schemes, including nonreduced tests, and needs no finite
simple system, reductivity, or root-system hypothesis. The sole existence
premise is explicit; this file does not prove standard-cocharacter existence.
The empty simple system is allowed. No step assumes that the relative root
system is reduced.

Provenance: these elementary sum and subtraction arguments were derived for the
standard-parabolic order proof task from the imported Library definitions.
Their source is the definitions and submodule laws rather than a new external
structural result.
