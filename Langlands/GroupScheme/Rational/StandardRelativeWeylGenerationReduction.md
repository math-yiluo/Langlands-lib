# Standard relative Weyl generation from the Coxeter statement

Worker 1235. This internal reduction derives the published
`StandardRelativeWeylGenerationStatement` (Borel–Tits 5.17: the reflections in a set `J`
of simple relative roots generate the standard relative Weyl subgroup of `J`) from the
published `RelativeWeylCoxeterStatement`. This is the route that the task context
recommends. It keeps the universe `u`, an arbitrary field, nonreduced relative roots and
rank zero. The module proves neither statement unconditionally.

## Linear-algebra helpers

`Langlands.LinearAlgebra.dual_eq_of_isOfFinOrder_reflection_mul`: let `R` be a field of
characteristic zero and `x` a vector with `f x = 2` and `g x = 2`. If the product
`reflection g * reflection f` has finite order, then `f = g`. The proof shows that the
product is the shear `y ↦ y + (f - g) y • x`. Since `(f - g) x = 0`, its `n`-th power is
`id + n • (f - g).smulRight x`. If some positive power is the identity, then
`(f - g) y • x = 0` for all `y`, and `x ≠ 0` gives `f = g`. Mathlib's
`Module.Dual.eq_of_preReflection_mapsTo` uses a similar shear argument, but needs a
finite invariant spanning set instead of a finite-order hypothesis.

`RationalSpan.ratForm f` is the `ℚ`-linear form `q ⊗ m ↦ q * f m` on `ℚ ⊗[ℤ] M`, for an
integral form `f : M →+ ℤ`. `ratForm_tmul` records this formula.
`RationalSpan.ratAut_apply_of_forall_eq_sub` shows that an integral reflection formula
`σ χ = χ - f χ • a` gives `ratAut σ v = v - ratForm f v • toRat a`. Both sides are
`ℚ`-linear maps, and they agree on pure tensors.

## Relative Weyl group lemmas

`coe_relativeWeylAction_apply_eq_ratAut` holds by definition. The published action
`relativeWeylAction i w` on the relative-root span is the restriction of
`ratAut (schemeWeylCharacterAction i w)`.

The following lemmas assume a Coxeter realization `h : IsRelativeWeylCoxeterRealization
i ν cs`.

- `isRelativeReflection_simple`: `cs.simple a` is a relative reflection in the simple root
  `a`, in the sense of the published `IsRelativeReflection`. Clause (1) of the realization
  gives an integral form `f` with `f a = 2` and the integral reflection formula. The linear
  form is `ratForm f` restricted to the relative-root span. Its value at `a` is `2`, and
  `ratAut_apply_of_forall_eq_sub` identifies the action with `Module.reflection`.
- `eq_one_of_relativeWeylAction_eq_one` (also assumes `T` split): if `w` acts trivially
  on the relative-root span, then `w = 1`. By the published
  `IsSplitTorus.relativeWeylAction_mk_eq_one_iff`, a representative of `w` fixes every
  relative root. Then no positive root goes to a negative root, so the inversion set is
  empty. Clause (4) (length equals the size of the inversion set) gives length zero, so
  `w = 1`.
- `eq_simple_of_isRelativeReflection` (also assumes the relative Weyl group is finite and
  `T` is split): every relative reflection `w` in a simple root `a` equals `cs.simple a`.
  The product of the two reflections on the span has finite order because the group is
  finite. `dual_eq_of_isOfFinOrder_reflection_mul` then shows that the two linear forms
  agree, so `w` and `cs.simple a` act identically. The previous lemma, applied to
  `w * (cs.simple a)⁻¹`, gives equality.
- `closure_relativeReflections_eq` (also assumes finitely many simple roots): for
  `J ⊆ simpleRelativeRoots i ν`, the subgroup generated by `relativeReflections i J` is
  `standardRelativeWeylGroup i ν J`. Let `I` be the finite set of simple-root labels in
  `J`. Then `rootSet i ν I = J`. By the two previous lemmas,
  `relativeReflections i J = cs.simple '' I`. The subgroup that this set generates is
  `standardSubgroup cs I` by definition, and clause (5) of the realization identifies it
  with `standardRelativeWeylGroup i ν J`.

## Main theorem

`standardRelativeWeylGenerationStatement_of_relativeWeylCoxeter :
RelativeWeylCoxeterStatement.{u} → StandardRelativeWeylGenerationStatement.{u}`. The
hypothesis gives a finite relative Weyl group, finitely many simple roots and a Coxeter
realization. Maximal splitness gives the split torus. Then `closure_relativeReflections_eq`
proves the target equality. Clauses (2) and (3) of the realization are not used.

This conditional reduction does not prove `RelativeWeylCoxeterStatement`, and it
introduces no axioms.
