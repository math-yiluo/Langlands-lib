# Finite Galois split-torus domination

Worker 1333. Over every field `k : Type u`, a pointed dominant morphism from
an actual ground-field torus to a reduced affine group admits an actual
base-changed pointed dominant parametrization by a split torus over a finite
Galois extension of `k`. The parametrizing morphism is a morphism of schemes
over the field, with no group homomorphism assumption. Its value at the
identity remains the identity. The target is the actual base-changed group
scheme, and the morphism is the actual pullback of the original morphism.

`HasPointedTorusDomination.exists_finiteGalois_splitDomination_of_isReduced`
chooses the finite Galois splitting field of the supplied source torus with
the published unconditional `torusFiniteGaloisSplitting`. It retains both
`FiniteDimensional k L` and `IsGalois k L` in its conclusion. The existing
`PointedTorusDomination.isDominant_baseChange` proves dominance of the actual
pullback using the reduced affine target, and
`PointedTorusDomination.pullback_map_one` proves its identity equation.
These are precisely the witnesses required by the existing
`HasPointedSplitTorusDomination` predicate.

`HasPointedTorusDomination.exists_finiteGalois_splitDomination` supplies the
same conclusion for a smooth affine target. Pointed torus domination implies
connectedness of the target. The published smooth connected affine geometry
theorem then supplies geometric integrality and hence reducedness, reducing
to the first theorem.

`IsTorusGenerated.exists_finiteGalois_splitDomination` composes that result
with the published `IsTorusGenerated.hasPointedTorusDomination`. Thus every
smooth affine group generated by its ground-field tori has such a
parametrization after a finite Galois extension. This final specialization
requires no additional connectedness or solvability assumption. Generation
uses the unchanged closed all-tori envelope on all relative test schemes.

`FiniteGaloisSplitTorusDomination H` retains this actual data for downstream
constructions: the extension carrier with its field, algebra, finite-dimension
and Galois instances; the split torus; and the actual pointed dominant
parametrization of the base-changed group. The two definitions
`HasPointedTorusDomination.finiteGaloisSplitDomination` and
`IsTorusGenerated.finiteGaloisSplitDomination` choose this data from the
corresponding existence theorem by classical choice. The structure fields
retain each of the stated properties for that same chosen morphism.

The construction is internal composition of the named published results.
It strengthens the arbitrary-extension conclusion in
`Rational.TorusDominationBaseChange` to a finite Galois extension, preserving
its actual morphisms and predicates. It supplies no assertion that the target
is split solvable, and no existence, geometric comparison or splitness theorem
for its unipotent radical. Those remain separate structural obligations.
Finite fields, imperfect fields and all characteristics are included.
