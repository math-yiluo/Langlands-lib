# Intrinsic nonnegative cones of actual parabolics

Worker 17. This internal construction uses a supplied compatible root
presentation of an actual parabolic, rational Levi and split component.
`intrinsicNonnegCone` is the nonnegative cone hull of the real images of all
positive actual weights of that split component.

For a locally finite type ambient group, root negation suffices to identify
the existing restricted simple-root nonnegative cone with this intrinsic
cone. Every restricted complementary simple root is an intrinsic positive
root, giving one hull inclusion. Conversely, the published
`one_tmul_mem_nonnegCone_of_mem_intrinsicPositiveRoots` lifts an intrinsic
positive root to a positive ambient root and inducts through its positive-root
decomposition. Selected simple roots restrict to zero, complementary simple
roots are generators, and addition preserves the cone. The universal property
of the cone hull gives the other inclusion.

For a smooth ambient group, the intrinsic positive support is the nonzero
split-component tangent-weight support of any geometric radical of the
specified parabolic. It is therefore independent of all presentation choices.
The two hull comparisons prove equality of the nonnegative cones of unrelated
compatible presentations whenever both actual root sets are stable under
negation. In a connected reductive ambient group, the published relative-root
negation theorem supplies these two root hypotheses. The cone comparisons retain
the displayed locally finite type instance for the ambient group.

This is a derived consequence of the existing intrinsic root support and cone
membership theorems. It assumes no independence of simple roots, makes no
comparison of the restricted generator sets or strictly positive cones, and
does not construct compatible presentations or prove the remaining Renard
representation-theoretic targets.
