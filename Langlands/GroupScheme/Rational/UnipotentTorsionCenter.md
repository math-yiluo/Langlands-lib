# Smooth stable central subgroups killed by a prime

This derived development extracts a smooth connected commutative central
subgroup killed by p from a nontrivial smooth connected affine unipotent
group over any field of prime characteristic p. It retains the supplied
action of any affine group scheme and supplies the restricted actual action
and inclusion equivariance on every scheme test.

The construction starts from the previously constructed nontrivial smooth
connected affine central subgroup Z. Its closed inclusion makes Z
commutative. The uniform p-power exponent of the ambient unipotent group
passes to Z by monicity. The existing actual integer multiplication
morphism evaluates on every test point as the usual power. The predecessor
of the first trivial p-power multiplication morphism has nontrivial image
killed by multiplication by p.

Pointed image generation applied to this power morphism on the smooth
connected affine source Z gives a smooth affine geometrically connected
closed subgroup. Every automorphism commutes with powers, so the closed
envelope is stable under the given action. Composing its inclusion with
Z's central inclusion gives centrality in the original group, and cancellation
of that inclusion proves nontriviality.

Annihilation on all points of the generated subgroup follows from
closed-kernel minimality. The whole source image lies in the represented
closed kernel of multiplication by p. Consequently the closed envelope
lies in that same kernel on every test scheme. Monicity then transfers
the exponent p to the actual generated group. The argument does not
require points of the envelope to lift globally to the power-map source.

The general extraction theorem works with any natural q and any supplied
smooth connected affine commutative central subgroup with a uniform
q-power exponent. The prime-characteristic unipotent theorem obtains
those inputs from the earlier smooth-centre and exponent constructions.

All arguments are derived from the published integer multiplication,
closed-kernel, image-generation and envelope-stability interfaces, together
with the earlier uniform exponent and central subgroup results. No vector
coordinates, subgroup unipotence proof or equivariant linear structure
is asserted. The exact central-vector and ordered-product targets remain
open.
