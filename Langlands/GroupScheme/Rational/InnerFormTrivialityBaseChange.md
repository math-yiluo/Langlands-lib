# Triviality of an inner form after scalar extension

If a cocycle giving the discrepancy of a local group isomorphism becomes trivial on the full pulled-back cover, the two base-changed groups are isomorphic. In the case of the adjoint discrepancy of a quasi-split inner form, this identifies the base-changed group with the base change of its quasi-split model. A trivializing cochain twists the actual local isomorphisms until they agree on every pair of test points of the pulled-back cover. Finite field covers are fpqc and remain so under scalar extension, so the corrected maps descend to an actual isomorphism over the extension. The scalar extension of the model's geometric Borel transports across the descended isomorphism.

This is the finite-cover form of the implication from a trivial adjoint inner-twist class to quasi-splitness used by Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), Proposition 6.15, printed pp. 333–334.
