# Power reduction retaining a supplied pointed family

Author: Worker 1431.

This internal reduction continues `PointedCommutatorFamily`. It is motivated
by the passage to the last nontrivial p-power of a central curve in
Conrad–Gabber–Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive groups*, second edition (2015),
Proposition B.3.2, printed pp.572–573, one-based PDF pp.598–599.
The registered source is `conrad-gabber-prasad-2015-pseudo-reductive`,
SHA256 `1b77af44c90211db9a3efa50896a3722f4fd2df332b7ce2ec52202eb243e7f54`.
Appendix B assumes positive characteristic. The general power-family
construction here uses only its explicit uniform exponent hypothesis.

`PointedPowerFamily.word f q n` composes the supplied actual morphism `f`
with multiplication by `q^n` in its commutative target. Its zero-th member
is `f`; its successor applies multiplication by `q` to the previous member.
Every section of the original source on which `f` is the identity remains
identically the identity for each power-family member.

If `f` is nontrivial and one `q^r` kills all points of its target on every
relative scheme test, the power family eventually vanishes. The predecessor
of the least vanishing index is nontrivial. Selecting this index for the
supplied family, rather than for multiplication on the whole group,
preserves information from that family.

`exists_torsion_generatedImage` assumes a smooth affine commutative target
over any field and a pointed affine geometrically integral source locally of
finite type, expressed by `ImageGeneration.FinitePowerGood`. Published
pointed image generation constructs the smooth geometrically connected
closed subgroup generated by the last nontrivial family member. Its
factorization proves nontriviality. Vanishing of the next member says that
multiplication by `q` kills this source image. Published closed-kernel
minimality extends that identity to the entire generated subgroup on every
relative scheme test. Cancellation of the generated subgroup inclusion
proves that its factor retains every section killed by the original `f`.
No connectedness hypothesis on the ambient commutative target is required.

`exists_pointed_torsion_central_family_of_not_hasConstantAffineLineMaps`
starts with an actual smooth connected affine unipotent group over any
field of prime characteristic `p` and failure of affine-line constancy.
The published commutator-family reduction supplies a central smooth
connected generated subgroup and a nontrivial family on the affine line
with finitely many original group parameters. Centrality and the closed
inclusion make that subgroup commutative. The published all-test
unipotent exponent theorem passes through the monic inclusion, so power
reduction applies to this particular family. The resulting subgroup is
again an actual smooth connected commutative central closed subgroup,
now killed by `p`. Its factor remains nontrivial and is identically the
identity when the affine-line variable is zero and all group parameters
remain free.

This is an auxiliary reduction toward `UnipotentWoundCriterionStatement`.
It does not turn the retained family into a nonconstant ground-field
affine-line map, construct a one-dimensional vector subgroup, or prove
separable invariance or the full criterion. Those structural steps remain
separate obligations.
