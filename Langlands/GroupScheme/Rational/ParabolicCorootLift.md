# Full real lifts of actual Levi split-component parameters

Worker 148. This internal construction complements
`ParabolicCorootLiftUniqueness`. For a verified actual parabolic root
presentation `r` and supplied actual relative-root reflection data `D`, it
constructs the real linear map `r.selectedCorootLift hG D` from the actual
Levi split-component character space to the full maximal split-torus
character space. The ambient group is connected reductive, and its structure
map has a locally finite type instance.

The submodule `r.selectedCorootAnnihilator D` consists of full vectors whose
pairings with every selected actual coroot are zero. The theorem
`existsUnique_selectedCorootLift` says that every real split-component
parameter has exactly one preimage in this submodule. The map
`selectedCorootLift` chooses it. The subsequent laws prove that restriction
recovers the input, that every selected coroot pairs to zero, that a full
vector in the annihilator is the lift of its restriction, and that the lift
is injective.

The proof first establishes that the actual split-component factor into the
maximal split torus is monic, using the verified factorization through the
Levi and parabolic inclusions. The published split-torus restriction
surjectivity theorem therefore gives an arbitrary initial lift. The proof
averages this lift over the finite subgroup generated by the selected root
reflections. Restriction is unchanged by each generator because selected
roots vanish on the actual split component; subgroup closure induction
extends this to the whole subgroup. Left multiplication reindexes the Weyl
sum, making it fixed by each selected reflection. Evaluation on the
corresponding coroot, whose root-coroot pairing is two, then gives zero.
Dividing by the nonzero subgroup order preserves the prescribed restriction.
The published uniqueness theorem identifies any two such lifts and proves
linearity and the inverse laws.

This is a derived construction from the published selected-Weyl sum,
actual-character restriction, and coroot-lift uniqueness APIs; it does not
claim to formalize an additional statement from the literature. It retains
the full character space, including central directions. Reflection data are
supplied, and their existence remains a separate obligation. No positivity,
inner product or Langlands decomposition is assumed or constructed. This
pair does not prove V.3.18 or the Langlands classification.
