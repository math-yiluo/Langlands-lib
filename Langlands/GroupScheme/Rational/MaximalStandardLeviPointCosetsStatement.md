# Maximal standard Levi transporter cosets on rational points

`Langlands.GroupScheme.MaximalStandardLeviPointCosetsStatement` states
the count and parabolic conjugation at the opening of Renard VII.1.3.
Let \(G\) be a connected reductive group over a nonarchimedean local
field \(k\). A maximal split torus and a generic cocharacter determine
the standard parabolics \(P_I\) and their standard Levi subgroups
\(L_I\). All subgroup inclusions and conjugation equalities in the
proposition refer to the indicated rational-point subgroups.

For each \(I\), the set of cosets \(gL_I(k)\) for which
\(gL_I(k)g^{-1}=L_J(k)\) for some standard \(J\) has cardinality two
exactly when \(P_I(k)\) is maximal among the proper standard parabolic
point subgroups. In that case, for each such \(g\) outside \(L_I(k)\)
and for each standard cocharacter \(\zeta\) of the target \(J\), the
conjugate \(gP_I(k)g^{-1}\) is the group of points of the dynamic
parabolic defined by \(-\zeta\), the opposite of \(P_J\) with Levi \(L_J\).
The identity coset is represented by \(1\), which preserves \(L_I(k)\).
Thus the second assertion applies to every representative of the other
coset and to its actual standard target.

This is the rational-point form of the Weyl-side results in V.4.8.
The source of the converse in the cardinality assertion is V.4.8
together with the identification of the two coset sets, extended from
maximal standard Levis to every standard Levi using the argument of
V.4.3.3. The formulation uses left cosets as a set and uses the same
standard maximality predicate as `StandardMaximalLeviWeylStatement`.
