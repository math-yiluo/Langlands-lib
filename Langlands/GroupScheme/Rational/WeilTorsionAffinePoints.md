# Tensor-unit coordinates of torsion in the Weil group

Over a field `F`, take `n`-torsion of the actual Weil multiplicative
group for a finite commutative `F`-algebra `B`. On an affine `F`-algebra
test `R`, the torsion point equivalence gives the points of the Weil
group killed by `n`. Its affine-unit equivalence identifies these
with the units of `B ⊗[F] R` killed by the same power.

The coordinate of a torsion point is its image under the torsion
inclusion followed by the ambient affine-unit equivalence. Therefore
change of test algebra acts by the algebra map on the right tensor
factor.

This internal model supplies the actual kernel coefficients for
finite Galois lifting through a Weil-restricted torsion group.
