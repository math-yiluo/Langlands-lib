# Hasse reduction for split torsion central kernels

The proposition concerns central isogenies over number fields
with simply connected source and kernel identified with a finite
split product of roots-of-unity groups of positive exponents.
It asks for triviality on the original finite Galois cover of any
target cocycle trivial on the full pullback at every place.

Assume the simply connected finite Galois Hasse kernel. The
global split torsion lifting and simultaneous archimedean correction
give a corrected source cocycle on a finite Galois refinement.
It is trivial on the infinite pullbacks by construction and on the
finite pullbacks by local simply connected vanishing. The simply
connected Hasse kernel gives its global triviality. Projection and
reflection through the field refinements give the target result.

This internal reduction assembles the two arithmetic calculations
for finite split torsion kernels.
