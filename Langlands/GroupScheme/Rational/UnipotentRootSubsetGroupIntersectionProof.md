# Intersection with a unipotent root-generated group

The theorem `Langlands.GroupScheme.unipotentRootSubsetGroupIntersection`
proves exactly the published
`Langlands.GroupScheme.UnipotentRootSubsetGroupIntersectionStatement`
with its universe parameter. This is Borel–Tits 3.22 c), the possibly
nonreduced-relative-root formulation of Renard V.4.2 (ii). The sources and
the reduced-point convention are documented in
[`ClosedRootSubsetStatements.md`](ClosedRootSubsetStatements.md).
The target and its imports are unchanged.

The implemented proof uses the published semigroup intersection and
selected-root generation theorems. It does not require a choice of
coordinates over an algebraic closure.

Let `A` be the additive semigroup generated by `X`, and let `B` be the
underlying additive semigroup of the additive monoid generated by `S`.
Weyl positivity of `X` gives a cocharacter pairing strictly positively with
every root in `X`, so zero does not belong to `A`. Closed-root saturation,
proved in `ClosedRootSubsetAdditiveClosure`, identifies the relative roots
in `A` with `X`, and those in `A ∩ B` with `S ∩ X`. This saturation uses the
published finite crystallographic relative-root system and does not assume
that it is reduced.

The published rank-one factorization theorem supplies the opposite factor
pairs needed for the selected-root generation theorem. The comparison
lemma in `RootSubsetSemigroupComparison` then identifies the ranges of
`U(X)` and `U(S ∩ X)` with the actual semigroup subgroups for `A` and
`A ∩ B`, respectively. The lemma in `ClosedRootSubsetSemigroupBound`
places the range of `G(S)` in the semigroup subgroup for `B`; its proof
includes the torus centralizer using the generic cocharacter.

The published equality `RelativeRoot.semigroupSubgroup_inf_eq` identifies
the intersection of the subgroups for `A` and `B` with the subgroup for
`A ∩ B`, since `A` excludes zero. It gives the forward containment.
Defining closed-subgroup minimality gives the reverse containment:
the group generated by the root groups for `S ∩ X` lies in both given
groups. These arguments apply on every test scheme, and the exported
theorem specializes them to the exact reduced-test-scheme statement.

The helper lemmas are derived content for this proof. Their paired
Markdown files describe their implemented arguments and retained
hypotheses.
