# The tensor-unit obstruction of a central isogeny

Let `B/F` be a finite separable field extension embedded in a finite
Galois extension `L/F`. For a central isogeny over `F` with a map
from its actual kernel to `Res_{B/F} Gₘ`, central lift data on the
`L/F` cover give an actual tensor-unit two-cocycle. Its class in
the second cohomology of `(B ⊗[F] L)ˣ` is `kernelTensorObstruction`.
Naturality of the affine coordinates gives the equivariance needed
for the two-cocycle law.

Under Shapiro, the class maps to the relative unit obstruction
for the central isogeny over `B`. The scalar-extended cover
has coefficient field `L`. Its kernel character is the pullback
of the given kernel map followed by the diagonal character of
the Weil group. On cochains this is precisely restriction of
automorphisms followed by evaluation at the inclusion of `B` in `L`.

For number fields, a cocycle trivial at every place remains
trivial under base change to `B`, by the commutative square
of actual completions, and under refinement to the `L/B` cover.
The unit-valued obstruction over `B` vanishes by global unit
reciprocity. Injectivity of Shapiro gives zero tensor-unit class,
and the degree-two cohomology differential supplies a primitive
with values in the original tensor units.
