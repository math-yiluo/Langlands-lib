# Coefficient expansion and trace support for actual Steinberg products

For any field, actual affine group scheme with a supplied split pinning, finite matrix representation, ordering of the simple roots, normalized simple-root parametrizations, and simple-reflection representatives, this module expands the actual ordered Steinberg matrix into its root coefficient matrices. No irreducibility or highest-weight hypothesis is imposed.

`steinbergCoefficientDegrees` is the finite type of bounded degree tuples for the expansion. `steinbergPolynomialMatrix_eq_sum_coefficients` expresses the published polynomial matrix as a finite sum indexed by tuples of nonnegative degrees, with each degree strictly below the corresponding additive matrix's published `degreeBound`. Its summand is the product of the variable powers times the scalar extension of the actual `steinbergCoefficientMatrix` in the original factor order. The first private helper expands a list product of finite sums by induction, retaining noncommutative order. The second separates the commutative polynomial scalar from the mapped ordered matrix product. The factor identity follows by specializing the additive coefficient expansion to the polynomial coordinate algebra. Applying matrix trace gives `steinbergRestriction_trace_eq_sum_coefficients`, an equality for the existing restriction of the actual invariant trace class function.

`exists_representationWeightBasis` obtains a basis of actual all-algebra weight vectors from the published split-torus character spanning theorem and character-basis construction, and relabels the characters by the actual character lattice. `weightBasis_coord_representationWeightProjector` states that projecting a vector to the weight of one basis vector preserves that vector's coordinate. Equality of the relevant linear functionals is checked on the basis, using the universal projector's identity and zero action on character spaces.

For a fixed tuple of degrees, `steinbergCoefficientMatrix_trace_eq_zero_of_no_weight` proves that the ordered coefficient matrix has trace zero if no occurring weight has those simple-coroot pairings. Indeed, the existing coefficient-path projector theorem then kills the projected image of each weight-basis vector. The coordinate identity makes every diagonal entry vanish, and basis independence of trace gives the result. Its contrapositive, `exists_dominant_weight_of_steinbergCoefficientMatrix_trace_ne_zero`, produces an occurring dominant weight with exactly the prescribed integral simple-coroot pairings whenever the coefficient trace is nonzero. Dominance follows from the closed weight-path theorem. This implication does not assert the converse: a trace can vanish in positive characteristic.

These are derived consequences of the published actual polynomial-action, coefficient-path, split-torus spanning, and universal-character interfaces, together with finite-sum and matrix-trace identities from Mathlib. The mathematical motivation is Steinberg, *Regular elements of semi-simple algebraic groups* (1965), printed p.69 (PDF page 22), proof of 7.14, steps 1–3 and 6. That page was read directly; no source errata note was found. All supplied families and their order are retained, including the empty root family. The argument uses neither pointwise density nor factorial division. The nonzero highest coefficient, lower-variable triangularity, invariant generation, and canonical quotient isomorphism remain further work.
