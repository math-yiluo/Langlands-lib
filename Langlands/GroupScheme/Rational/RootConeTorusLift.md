# Lifting cone-selected groups to a larger split torus

This internal helper lifts a character cone along an actual homomorphism from
one split torus to another. Real scalar extension of character restriction is
linear; its preimage preserves the cone operations and closedness for the real
module topologies. The integral characters in the pulled-back cone are
exactly those whose restrictions lie in the original cone. In particular,
characters restricting to zero are retained.

The semigroup subgroup for the smaller torus equals the subgroup for this
preimage semigroup under the larger torus. For the proof, view the larger-torus
semigroup subgroup as a subgroup stable under the smaller torus. Its ambient
Lie image is the selected larger weight span. The published weight-restriction
theorem identifies that span with the selected smaller weight span. Smoothness,
connectedness and closedness of the larger-torus semigroup subgroup then allow
the exact Lie-image uniqueness theorem to identify it with the smaller-torus
semigroup subgroup. Equality is on every scheme test.

For every map between field spectra, the published base-change equivalence on
the character lattice of a split torus extends to an equivalence of real
character spaces. Taking the preimage under its inverse transports the original
cone to the new space. The integral characters in this transported cone are
exactly the image of the original integral semigroup under actual character
pullback. The proof writes every new character as a pullback using the lattice
equivalence. Cone transport commutes with intersection and preserves closedness;
continuity follows from the universal property of the module topology.

For a field extension and a smooth affine ambient group locally of finite type,
the semigroup subgroup of the transported cone is the base change of the
original cone semigroup subgroup. This follows from the published subgroup
base-change theorem, the preceding integral-semigroup equality, and compatibility
of the conjugation action with base change. It is equality on every scheme test.

This is derived from the Library's split-torus weight restriction and semigroup
realization results; no new original-source assertion is used. Torus maximality
is not required for these comparison lemmas.
