# Nonarchimedean local isotropy of type D

`LocalTypeDIsotropyStatement` starts with an absolutely almost-simple group of absolute type D of rank at least four over a field. Its scalar extension to a nonarchimedean local field has positive split rank.

Absolute almost-simplicity is preserved by comparing the two algebraic closures and extending the almost-simple geometric group between them. The Cartan matrices are compared by extending both actual split pinnings to the algebraic closure of the larger field. Their root data are equivalent, and a Weyl element aligns the bases. Both constructions apply to local completions as field extensions.

The classification of anisotropic groups over nonarchimedean local fields would give a type A Cartan numbering. Its cardinality would be the same rank \(n\). Reindexing preserves the determinant, whereas the type D determinant is \(4\) and the type A determinant is \(n+1\). Thus a type D group of rank at least four is isotropic.

The local classification is the input of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, Theorem 6.5 and §6.6, printed p. 352. The Cartan determinant comparison applies it to the actual type D group over the completion. [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
