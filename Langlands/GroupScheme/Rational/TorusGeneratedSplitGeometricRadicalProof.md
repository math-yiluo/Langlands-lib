# The split geometric radical of a torus-generated solvable group

Authors: Worker 1376 and Worker 1485.

`Langlands.GroupScheme.torusGeneratedSplitGeometricRadical.{u}` proves the
unchanged published proposition `TorusGeneratedSplitGeometricRadicalStatement.{u}`.
For every field `k : Type u` and every smooth connected affine solvable actual
group scheme `G` generated by its ground-field tori, it supplies an actual group
scheme `U` over `k` and a homomorphism `i : U ⟶ G` satisfying both
`LeviSubgroup.IsGeometricUnipotentRadical i` and
`RelativeRoot.IsSplitUnipotent U`. There is no additional premise.

## Implemented proof

The first theorem, `splitTorusDominatedSolvableQuotient`, discharges the premise of
[`SplitTorusDominatedSolvableQuotientReduction`](SplitTorusDominatedSolvableQuotientReduction.md)
with the unconditional theorem `solvableTorusQuotient` from
[`SolvableTorusQuotientProof`](../Absolute/SolvableTorusQuotientProof.md).
The reduction passes a pointed split-torus-dominated solvable group to its
algebraic closure, obtains an actual split-torus fppf quotient there, and descends
its target and homomorphism. Its canonical full kernel is the ground-field
unipotent subgroup; the actual kernel comparison and quotient reflection give
the required closedness, smoothness, connectedness, unipotence and represented
fppf quotient. This proves
`SplitTorusDominatedSolvableQuotientStatement.{u}` over arbitrary ground fields.

The final theorem applies
`torusGeneratedSplitGeometricRadical_of_structuralStatements` from
[`TorusGeneratedSplitGeometricRadicalAssembly`](TorusGeneratedSplitGeometricRadicalAssembly.md).
Its three structural inputs are supplied by:

- `splitTorusDominatedSolvableQuotient`, proved above;
- `unipotentSplitTorusDomination` from
  [`SplitTorusDominationProof`](SplitTorusDominationProof.md);
- `RelativeRoot.splitUnipotentStableVectorSubgroup` from
  [`SplitUnipotentStableVectorProof`](SplitUnipotentStableVectorProof.md).

The assembly chooses a finite Galois extension over which the torus
parametrization of `G` splits. The first input provides an actual torus quotient
of the extended group with a smooth connected unipotent full kernel. The other
two inputs give that kernel a split series. The existing finite-Galois
quotient-and-kernel descent then returns the actual ground-field inclusion with
its geometric radical predicate and its split series over `k`. All three
structural statements are discharged by the displayed theorems.

The geometric radical predicate includes greatestness over `k` and for the
actual algebraic-closure base change of the inclusion. The split-unipotence
predicate supplies the existing finite normal series with actual smooth
connected closed terms and represented fppf additive quotients over `k`.
Ground-field torus generation keeps its existing all-test closed-subgroup
meaning. The proof retains finite and imperfect fields, every characteristic,
and arbitrary universe `u`; it uses no maximal-torus conjugacy result.

## Provenance

This module is an internal composition of the named published reductions and
their unconditional inputs. The original mathematical target is Springer,
*Linear Algebraic Groups*, second edition (1998), Theorem 14.4.1(iv), printed
p.249 / PDF p.263, under the standing assumptions on printed p.248 / PDF p.262,
as documented in the unchanged
[`TorusGeneratedSplitGeometricRadicalStatement`](TorusGeneratedSplitGeometricRadicalStatement.md).
The implemented proof follows the quotient, full-kernel comparison and
finite-Galois descent route described above.
