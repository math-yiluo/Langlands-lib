# Isotropic planes over the completions of the quadratic component field

Worker 863, Task 7498.

`IsLocallyNonsingularIsotropic` means that over every completion of the base
number field the extended skew Gram form has an isotropic row with an invertible
coordinate. For an invertible Gram matrix and compatible quaternion coordinates,
`NumberFieldSymmetricComponentLocalPlaneStatement` gives two independent orthogonal
isotropic vectors of the symmetric component over each completion of its quadratic
field.

Over the place below each quadratic-field place, produce a normalized tensor partner
for the isotropic row by the inverse Gram matrix. The actual maps of completions for
the infinite and finite places preserve the skew pairings by coefficient naturality.
Apply the isotropic-plane calculation over the larger completion to the transported
pair.

This proves the local isotropic-plane step in Springer, appendix to M. Kneser,
*Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups* (1969), Theorem 5.3.3(i),
printed p. 109 (PDF p. 115).
