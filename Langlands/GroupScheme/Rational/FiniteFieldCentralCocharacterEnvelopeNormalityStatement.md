# Torus envelope normality over a finite field when every cocharacter is central

Worker 1387. This module states one proposition,
`FiniteFieldCentralCocharacterEnvelopeNormalityStatement.{u}`. It is not proved
here.

## Statement

Fix a finite field `k : Type u` and a group scheme `G` over `Spec k` that is
smooth and affine, with connected underlying space. Assume that every
cocharacter `χ : 𝔾_m ⟶ G` over `k` is central, in the form
`Dynamic.centralizer χ = ⊤`: on every test scheme, every point of `G` has
constant conjugation orbit under `χ`, that is, commutes with `χ`. The
proposition asserts that the existing `closedTorusEnvelope G` is normal,
`(closedTorusEnvelope G).IsNormal`: on every test scheme, conjugation by every
point of `G` preserves the points of the envelope. Nonreduced tests are
included. The envelope is the unchanged intersection of the ranges of all
actual closed subgroups containing every closed `k`-torus of `G`.

The cocharacters are the actual homomorphisms from the multiplicative group
`Gm (Spec k)`; split tori of `G` are generated by their images, so the
hypothesis says that every split `k`-torus of `G` is central.

## Source and derivation

The source is Conrad–Gabber–Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive groups*, second edition
(2015), Proposition A.2.11, printed pp.477–480 (one-based PDF pp.503–506),
registered as `conrad-gabber-prasad-2015-pseudo-reductive`. The proposition
asserts, for every smooth connected affine group over every field, that the
smooth connected `k`-subgroup `G_t` generated by the `k`-tori is normal. The
present proposition is the special case of a finite field and a group whose
cocharacters are all central. The source subgroup `G_t` is identified with the
fixed envelope by its least closed-subgroup property, on every test.

The finite-field proof in the source (printed pp.478–480) covers exactly this
case through two inputs. First, if every split `k`-torus is central then the
subgroup generated by the `G_S` for split tori `S` is a central split torus,
the quotient has no nontrivial split torus, and its maximal semisimple
quotient is trivial because a connected semisimple group over a finite field
contains a Borel `k`-subgroup and hence a nontrivial split torus; so `G` is
solvable. Second, the solvable case over a perfect field of positive
characteristic (printed pp.478–479, with Lemma A.2.12 and Theorem B.4.3)
gives `G^τ = G_t`, so `G_t` is normal.
