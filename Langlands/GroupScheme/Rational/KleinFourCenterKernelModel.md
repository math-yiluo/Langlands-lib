# Number-field kernel models for Klein four characters

Let `Z` be of multiplicative type over a number field with four actual
geometric characters of exponent two. If the absolute Galois action is
trivial, the constant coordinates give a split product of two copies of
the square roots-of-unity group.

Otherwise take a moved character. Its orbit lies in the three nonzero
characters and has two or three elements. The embeddings of the field
fixed by its open stabilizer give the same orbit with the same action.
Any two distinct nonzero characters generate the character group.
When the orbit has two elements, the quadratic Weil torsion model has
the required order and character action. When it has three, the third
character is the sum of the first two. This gives sum zero for the orbit
and the corresponding cubic norm-one torsion model, with extension degree
coprime to exponent two.

The proof applies the absolute character equivalence to obtain the actual
group isomorphisms. This is the internal kernel comparison used for the
noncyclic case of order four in the adjoint Hasse reduction.
