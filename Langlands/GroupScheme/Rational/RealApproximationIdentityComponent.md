# Real approximation on the identity component of a unirational group

For a smooth connected affine rational group with a unirational parametrization,
the closure of the image of its rational points contains the identity component
of its real points in the affine evaluation topology.

The dominant parametrization of the integral target sends the generic point of
the parameter space to the generic point of the target. The corresponding
extension of function fields is formally smooth in characteristic zero. The
smooth locus therefore contains a nonempty affine open of the parameter space.
That affine open has a rational point and has real approximation. After scalar
extension to `ℝ`, smooth openness and the base-change point homeomorphisms show
that its image in the target is a nonempty open set of real points. Continuity
places that open set inside the closure of the rational points of the target.

For a group, the closure is a subgroup, and its nonempty interior makes it open.
Being closed and containing the identity, it contains the connected component
of the identity. This is the unirationality argument for the topologically
connected case used in Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017), Theorem 25.70, p. 564.
