# Prime covers of quasi-split inner E₆

`NumberFieldTotallyComplexInnerTypeE6PrimeCoverReductionStatement` deduces vanishing on degree-two and degree-three Galois covers of a quasi-split simply connected inner E₆ group over a totally complex number field from isotropy of cubically split E₆.

The quadratic result applies directly in degree two. In degree three the quasi-split group is split by the triviality of its star action. A cocycle defines an actual pure twist. The conjugation discrepancy preserves the absolute Cartan numbering, and the isomorphism over the cubic cover transports the split maximal torus to the extended twist. Cubic splitting isotropy now applies to the twist. The split-source branch Levi theorem gives triviality of the original cocycle by the inner type A Hasse kernel.

This implements the prime-cover argument of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.8, Proposition 6.22 and Theorem 6.29, printed pp. 378–382. [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
