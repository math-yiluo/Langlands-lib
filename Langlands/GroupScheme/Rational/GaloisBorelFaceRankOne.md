# Absolute rank one for the singleton Cartan factors

In a diagonal Cartan face, the geometric almost-simple factor
attached to a simple root has that root as its entire simple base.
The factor torus and induced base give a split pinning with one
vertex. The corresponding vertex count for an actual Tits index
is obtained by extending its geometric pinning through a field
tower and comparing the two split pinnings.

If the full face has one vertex, there is only one geometric
almost-simple factor. The projection to it is an isomorphism,
so absolute almost-simplicity descends to the original field.
These are the rank and almost-simplicity inputs for the central
and leaf factors in the trialitarian decomposition of Platonov and
Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed
p. 374.
