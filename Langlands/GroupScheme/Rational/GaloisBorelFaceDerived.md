# Derived groups of the descended Levi subgroups

The given maximal torus lies in the descended Levi subgroup,
as is checked after extending scalars to its splitting field.
It remains a maximal torus of that Levi subgroup. Its full
intersection with the derived group is a maximal torus of the
derived group.

Over the splitting field the centralizer of the cocharacter
is the centralizer of its split torus image. Formation of the
derived group commutes with this extension. The derived group
of a split-torus centralizer in a simply connected group is
simply connected, and simple connectedness descends along
the algebraic extension.

Applying this construction to the central and leaf subdiagrams gives
the two subgroups in the trialitarian decomposition of Platonov and
Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed
p. 374.

The construction also takes Galois invariance of the geometric centralizer directly. This allows faces whose stabilizing action can change the positive roots within the zero-root subsystem.
