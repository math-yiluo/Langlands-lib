# The central and leaf factors of a trialitarian opposite-Borel torus

`TrialitarianOppositeBorelFactorsStatement` specifies the coefficient groups and morphisms used in the componentwise trivialization of a trialitarian cocycle. The input is a simply connected trialitarian group over a number field with a quadratic opposite-Borel torus.

The central group `H` is simply connected and absolutely almost-simple with a one-vertex absolute diagram. The leaf group is the Weil restriction of a simply connected absolutely almost-simple one-vertex group `A` over the field fixed by the stabilizer of a leaf. That field has degree three under the input hypotheses. The statement includes simple connectedness of the Weil restriction. The commutative groups `T₁` and `T₂` receive projections from `T` and map through `H` and the leaf group respectively. The images of the projections multiply to the original torus point on every test scheme. The image of the second commutative group acts by conjugation on the central group and fixes the image of the first commutative group there.

This is the factor data used in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed p. 374. Opposition and the D₄ Weyl inversion preserve the four pairs of opposite simple roots up to the star permutation. Its fixed central vertex gives the first group and the orbit of leaves gives the second. The simple coroot basis of the simply connected group gives the factor projections of the torus. The statement records their pointwise multiplication and action identities.
