# Finite Frobenius generators through an injective Laurent pullback

This internal algebraic support result transports `LaurentFrobeniusClosure`
through an algebra map. The coefficient field `K`, coordinate algebra `B`
and character index type `I` have independent universes.

Let `N` be a `K`-linear subspace of a commutative `K`-algebra `B`, and let
`σ : B →ₐ[K] AddMonoidAlgebra K (I → ℤ)` send every member of `N` to a
function vanishing at the identity. `pointedPullback` is the restriction
of `σ` to `N`, with values in the Laurent augmentation kernel. The algebra
generated by a pointed image is the image of the algebra generated by the
original functions.

Suppose `p` is prime, `K` has characteristic `p`, `N` is stable under
`f ↦ f^p`, and `σ` is injective. Every finite family `F` in `N` admits a
finite replacement `G` in `N`. The two families generate the same
`K`-subalgebra of `B`. Their pointed pullbacks have the same Frobenius
span, and the pullbacks of `G` belong to that of `F`. This span lies in a
finite primitive-character orbit space. The finite encodings of the
pullbacks of `G` satisfy `TorusLinearization.FrobIndep`.

The proof first shows that the pointed image of `N` is Frobenius-stable.
The Laurent closure theorem produces a finite independent replacement
inside that image. Finite choice lifts this family to `N`. Injectivity of
`σ` reflects equality of the generated subalgebras. No perfectness or
infinitude hypothesis is used, and no finite generation of the whole
subspace `N` is assumed. This result does not assert algebraic independence
or supply the primitive coordinate generators of a unipotent group.
