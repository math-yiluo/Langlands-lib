# Restriction of the star kernel to a disjoint quadratic field

The full star field is finite because its fixing subgroup is open in the Krull topology. It is Galois because the subgroup is the kernel of the action on the finite set of simple vertices. The action composes according to the star-action law on the actual Tits index.

`TitsIndexData.exists_star_trivial_restrictNormal_ne_one` applies to any nontrivial Galois intermediate field linearly disjoint from the star field. If every element fixing the star field also restricted trivially to the intermediate field, the Galois fixed-field theorem would put that whole field inside the star field. Their intersection is the base by linear disjointness. For quadratic extensions this gives a lift of the unique nontrivial automorphism that is trivial on the full star action, as used in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, p. 374.
