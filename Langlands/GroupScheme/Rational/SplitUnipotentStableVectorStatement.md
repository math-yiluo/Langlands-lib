# Central stable vector subgroups of split unipotent groups

`SplitUnipotentStableVectorSubgroupStatement` is a proposition target over
every field in universe `u`. It starts with a nontrivial smooth connected
affine unipotent group `U` that is split over the ground field and an actual
`AutomorphicAction H U` of a smooth affine group `H`. The acting group need
not be connected, solvable, or a torus.

The output is an actual group scheme `V`, a closed inclusion `z : V ⟶ U`,
an actual action `aV : AutomorphicAction H V`, a positive integer `n`, and
natural vector coordinates on every scheme test. The coordinates identify
the group of `V`-points with the additive group of `n` global functions.
`V` is smooth, connected, affine, unipotent, and split unipotent.
`SmoothOfRelativeDimension n V.X.hom` ties its relative dimension to the
coordinate dimension. The range of `z` is nontrivial and stable under the
given action on every test. `IsCentral z` places that range in the existing
all-refinement center of `U`. The final equation says that `z` intertwines
the actual actions on every test, including nonreduced schemes.

No linearity condition is imposed on the full `H` action. A torus action on
the resulting vector group can be handled by the separate
`TorusVectorActionLinearizationStatement`, applied to the actual restricted
action `aV.restrict i` for a torus homomorphism `i : T ⟶ H`. Coordinates
chosen for different restrictions may differ. The present target has no
algebraic-closedness, perfectness, infinitude, or characteristic restriction.

The source is Conrad–Gabber–Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive Groups*, second edition
(2015), registered as `conrad-gabber-prasad-2015-pseudo-reductive`, SHA256
`1b77af44c90211db9a3efa50896a3722f4fd2df332b7ce2ec52202eb243e7f54`.
This is a derived interface combining the following source constructions.

In positive characteristic, take the cckp-kernel `D` of `U`, defined in
B.3.1, printed p.571 (PDF p.597), as its maximal smooth connected
central subgroup killed by the characteristic. Corollary B.3.3, printed
p.573 (PDF p.599), explicitly makes `D` stable under any smooth acting
group. Theorem B.2.5, printed p.569 (PDF p.595), gives a canonical vector
subgroup `V` of `D`: over a separable closure it is generated by all
images of affine-line morphisms passing through the identity. This
description is invariant under group automorphisms. The smooth-group
density argument used in B.3.3 then makes `V` stable under the whole
action as a subgroup scheme, rather than merely under rational points.
The nontrivial split group `U` is not wound. Proposition B.3.2, printed
p.572 (PDF p.598), makes `D` non-wound, so the vector factor in B.2.5 is
nontrivial. This argument does not assume that every smooth subgroup of
a split unipotent group is split over an imperfect field.

In characteristic zero, one can take the identity component of the
scheme-theoretic center of `U`. It is a smooth connected commutative
unipotent group, stable under all group automorphisms. Its positive
dimension follows after algebraic closure from the last nontrivial term
of the descending central series, the construction used in the proof of
Theorem 3.3.11, printed p.115 (PDF p.141). The source's characteristic-zero
commutative-unipotent vector-group fact appears on printed p.112
(PDF p.138); uniqueness of the linear structure is also recorded in A.1.1,
printed p.466 (PDF p.492). Characteristic zero is perfect, so taking this
smooth connected center and verifying its positive dimension after
algebraic closure causes no inseparability obstruction. This canonical
choice is stable under the smooth acting group by the same density
argument. These passages are combined here; the source does not print
the entire proposed interface as a single theorem.

A proof must construct the represented subgroups and actions, justify
these structural facts for the Library's representation-theoretic
`IsUnipotent`, and express the vector-group identifications naturally on
all schemes. It must also establish the finite normal additive-group
series required by `IsSplitUnipotent`, the positive relative dimension,
and the stated equivariance. These comparisons are proof obligations,
not extra premises. This file proves neither this target nor maximal-torus
conjugacy. It does not assert a one-dimensional stable subgroup.
