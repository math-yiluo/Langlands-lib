# Orthogonal factors in the cubic splitting argument for E₆

`NumberFieldTotallyComplexTypeE6CubicSplitTypeDFactorStatement` starts with a simply connected anisotropic group over a totally complex number field that has a numbered split E₆ pinning over a cubic Galois extension. It gives a simply connected absolutely almost-simple anisotropic group of type D₄ or D₅ over a finite separable extension of the ground field.

The cubic orbit intersection gives an anisotropic factor outside type A of absolute rank at most five. The factor inherits its Cartan entries from simple vertices of the derived subgroup of the intersection over the separable closure. That derived subgroup embeds in split simply connected D₅. Four independent simple roots with F₄ entries would give at least forty-eight roots by reflection and hence a Lie algebra of dimension at least forty-eight. The D₅ Lie algebra has dimension at most forty-five, so injectivity of the tangent map excludes F₄.

The finite extension is totally complex. The absolute Cartan classification and the rank bound exclude the E families. The isotropy results in types B and C and the split rank calculation in type G₂ exclude those families as well. The resulting exact type D numbering has rank between four and five.

This is a reduction within Platonov and Rapinchuk's cubic argument in *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Theorem 6.26, printed pp. 353–355. It uses the quadratic form arguments for B, C and G₂ in Theorem 6.25 and the dimension bound on the parabolic orbit intersection.
