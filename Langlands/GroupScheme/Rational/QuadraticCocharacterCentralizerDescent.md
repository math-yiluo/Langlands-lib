# Cocharacter centralizer descent over a quadratic splitting field

Let \(G\) be an anisotropic affine group scheme of locally finite type over \(k\), with a torus \(T\) closed in \(G\) and split by a quadratic Galois extension \(K/k\). `QuadraticCocharacterCentralizerDescentStatement` gives a closed \(k\)-subgroup of \(G\) for each cocharacter of \(T_K\). After base change to \(K\), the subgroup is isomorphic over \(G_K\) to the actual centralizer of that cocharacter.

The nonidentity Galois automorphism acts by negation on the character lattice by anisotropy, so it also acts by negation on the cocharacter lattice by the perfect invariant pairing. Transport of the centralizer is the centralizer of the transported cocharacter. Negation leaves the centralizer equal on all test schemes. Closed-subgroup Galois descent therefore gives the subgroup and its inclusion.

This is the descent step for constructing root subgroups in the quadratic splitting argument of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Theorem 6.26, p. 353, and §6.8, Lemma 6.31, p. 380. [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
