# Square roots of norm-one points near the identity

The differential of multiplication at the pair of identity points is addition.
The differential of the square map at the identity is therefore multiplication by
two. The inverse function theorem gives a local homeomorphism for squaring.

Restrict the source to points whose inverses and automorphic images also lie in the
source. This restriction is open and contains the identity. For a norm-one point
in its image, the two points obtained by inverting the chosen root and by applying
the automorphism have equal squares. Injectivity on the source identifies them.
