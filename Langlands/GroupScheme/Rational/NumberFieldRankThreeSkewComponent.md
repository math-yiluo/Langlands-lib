# Hyperbolic symmetric components of rank-three skew forms

`NumberFieldRankThreeSkewComponentStatement` concerns an invertible skew-hermitian
Gram matrix of rank three over a central quaternion division algebra over a number
field. Its involution is standard quaternion conjugation. Assume that over every
completion the extended skew pairing has an isotropic row with an invertible
coordinate. There are quaternion coordinates whose distinguished pure quaternion
has reduced norm equal to the original Gram norm and whose symmetric component
is isometric over its quadratic field to three hyperbolic planes.

Use pure norm representation for the Gram norm and obtain the quadratic field
from the nonzero representative. Over that field the symmetric-component
determinant is the negative of a nonzero square. The actual maps between the
completions give an isotropic plane at every place of the quadratic field. The
number-field rank-six hyperbolicity theorem applies to this nondegenerate
symmetric component.

This proves the symmetric-component maximal-index step in Springer, appendix to
M. Kneser, *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups* (1969),
Theorem 5.3.3(i), printed p. 109 (PDF p. 115).
