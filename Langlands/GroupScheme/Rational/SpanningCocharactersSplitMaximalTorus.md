# Split maximal tori from two spanning rational cocharacters

`SpanningCocharactersSplitMaximalTorusStatement` gives a split maximal torus of a smooth affine group embedded as a closed subgroup of an ambient group. The two commuting rational cocharacters in the ambient group must base change to nonzero multiples of spanning cocharacters of a split maximal torus of the closed subgroup over the extension.

Faithfully flat descent factors the ambient cocharacters through the closed subgroup. Multiplication of their images gives a homomorphism from a split torus of rank two, with a closed split-torus image. After scalar extension the product factors through the supplied maximal torus. Schematic dominance of the image factor then places the entire image in that torus. The nonzero multiples in the image give the unscaled cocharacters by saturation of a closed torus. Since these generate the cocharacter lattice, the entire supplied torus is the image after extension. Maximality descends to the original field.

This criterion realizes the quadratic split torus in the outer E₆ centralizer argument of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Theorem 6.26, printed p. 356. [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
