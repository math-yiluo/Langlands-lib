# Galois-compatible merged-root data for adjacent simple roots

Author: Worker 90.

This helper module supports the proof of the even-chain Galois data
statement (Task 5375). That target is derived from Robert Steinberg,
*Regular elements of semi-simple algebraic groups*, Publ. Math. IHÉS 25
(1965), 49–80 (source `steinberg-1965-regular-elements-semisimple-groups`).
The Markdown of the target `EvenChainGaloisDataStatement` gives the full
source account. In a component of type `A_n` with `n = 2m` even,
Steinberg merges the middle pair of simple roots into the root
`a = α_m + α_{m+1}`. He uses an element `σ_a` normalizing the torus
according to the reflection in `a`, normalized to lie in the rank-one
group generated by `X_a` and `X_{-a}` (notation before Theorem 9.11 and
Theorem 9.11, printed p.74). The final paragraph of p.77 proves 9.7 e).
It notes that `a` is over `k`, so `T_a` and `X_a` are too, and forms
`σ_a` over `k` by 9.3 (printed p.72). There a cocycle with values in the
rank-one torus is made trivial by Hilbert's Theorem 90.

This module proves a derived finite-Galois rational-point version of that
step, for several pairs at once. Let a set `J` of pairs of adjacent simple
roots `a c`, `b c` carry a compatible action of the automorphism group
`Γ = Aut_k(K)`. The module builds one `SplitPinning.SteinbergModifiedData`
for each pair. Coefficient conjugation carries its root map, its
reflection representative and its coroot points exactly to those of the
pair at `σ c`. The construction is internal to this project. As in 9.3, a
cocycle is trivialized by Hilbert's Theorem 90, here with values in `Kˣ`
and twisted by the action on `J`. It is applied over the stabilizers of
the points of `J`, which parallels the orbit-stabilizer reduction in the
proof of 9.4 (printed pp.72–73) invoked on p.77. Hilbert 90 is proved
here from Dedekind's independence of characters. The representative is
then determined uniquely by the normalized root point, rather than by a
second cocycle as in 9.3.

Notation: `k ⊆ K` are fields, `G` is an affine group scheme over `Spec k`
and `D : GaloisCompatiblePinningData k K G` supplies a split pinning `p`
of `G_K`, a descended torus, the ordinary `Γ`-action `D.action` on the
simple roots and Galois-compatible normalized simple-root maps
`D.rootMaps`. `U(c)` is the intrinsic subgroup functor
`RelativeRoot.subgroup p.inclusion c`. `σ(-)` on rational points is
`GaloisPointComparison.rationalConjugate k K G σ`.

## Statements

### Galois twists (section `GaloisTwist`, `k`, `K` with `[FiniteDimensional k K]`)

- `exists_stabilizer_sum_ne_zero`: for a `Γ`-set `J`, a point `c`, and
  `f : Γ → K` with `f 1 ≠ 0`, there is `z : K` with
  `∑_{g ∈ Γ, g • c = c} f g · g(z) ≠ 0`.
- `exists_galoisTwist`: let `φ : Γ → J → K` take only nonzero values and
  satisfy `φ (σ τ) c = σ(φ τ c) · φ σ (τ • c)`. Then there is
  `mu : J → K` with all values nonzero and
  `mu (σ • c) = σ(mu c) · φ σ c` for all `σ`, `c`.

### Rescaled root parametrizations (`SplitPinning.RootParametrization`)

For a field `k`, an affine group scheme `G` over `Spec k`, and a split pinning `p`:

- `scale x d`: for a root parametrization `x` of index `α` and a unit
  `d : kˣ`, the root parametrization `LocalSplitCell.scaleHom d ≫ x.hom` of
  the same index.
- `scale_point`: on a test `T`, `(x.scale d)(c) = x(d_T · c)`, where
  `d_T = LocalSplitCell.baseScalar T d`.
- `scale_rationalPoint`: `(x.scale d).rationalPoint c = x.rationalPoint (d · c)`.

### Representatives around a root point (`SplitPinning`)

For a split pinning `p`, an index `α`, and a rational point `v ∈ U(α)` with
`v ≠ 1`:

- `rootPointOppositePair hv hv1`: an ordered pair `(q₁, q₂)` of rational
  points satisfying `RelativeRoot.IsOppositeFactorPair p.inclusion α v`,
  that is, `q₁, q₂ ∈ U(-α)` and `q₁ v q₂` normalizes the torus.
  `rootPointOppositePair_spec` records this property.
- `eq_rootPointOppositePair`: every ordered pair with this property equals
  the chosen one.
- `rootPointReflectionRepresentative hv hv1 : p.ReflectionRepresentative α`:
  the representative with point `q₁ v q₂` (`rootPointReflectionRepresentative_point`),
  whose torus automorphism is the induced normalizing automorphism and
  which acts on characters by the reflection of `α`.
- `rootPointReflectionRepresentative_mem_generated`: its point lies in
  `U(α)(k) ⊔ U(-α)(k)`.
- `rootPointReflectionRepresentative_rationalConjugate`: let `G_K` carry the
  pinning `p` whose torus is identified, through `eT` and `hT`, with the base
  change of a torus `T` over `k` mapping to `G` by `i`. Let `σ ∈ Γ`, and let
  `α`, `β` be indices whose roots correspond under `σ`, that is,
  `congr eT (root β) = σ · congr eT (root α)` in the character lattice of `T`.
  Let `v ∈ U(α)` and `w ∈ U(β)` be nonidentity rational points with
  `σ(v) = w`. Then `σ` carries the representative around `v` to the
  representative around `w`.

### Merged roots (`GaloisCompatiblePinningData`, for `D` as above)

- `mergedRootMap a b r hba hr`: let `a`, `b` be simple roots with
  `⟨b, a^∨⟩ = -1` and let `r` be an index whose root is `a + b`. This is
  the root map of index `r` obtained by conjugating `D.rootMaps b` by the
  canonical simple-reflection representative `n_a` of `a`.
  `mergedRootMap_rationalPoint` states that its value at `s` is
  `n_a x_b(s) n_a⁻¹`.
- `mergedRootMap_rationalConjugate`: if in addition
  `⟨σ b, (σ a)^∨⟩ = -1` and `r'` has root `σ a + σ b`, then
  `σ(mergedRootMap a b r (s)) = mergedRootMap (σ a) (σ b) r' (σ s)`.
- `exists_mergedRootMap_rationalConjugate`: let `(a', b')` be either
  `(σ a, σ b)` or `(σ b, σ a)`, with `⟨a', b'^∨⟩ = ⟨b', a'^∨⟩ = -1`, and let
  `r'` have root `a' + b'`. Then there is `f ≠ 0` in `K` with
  `σ(mergedRootMap a b r (s)) = mergedRootMap a' b' r' (σ s · f)` for all
  `s`.
- `coroot_galoisRepresentation`: if the roots of `α` and `β` correspond
  under `σ` (as above), so do their coroots in the cocharacter lattice of
  the descended torus.
- For `a b : J → Δ`: `mergedIndex a b c = s_{a c}(b c)`, the image of `b c`
  under the reflection in `a c`. If every `⟨b c, (a c)^∨⟩ = -1`, then
  `root_mergedIndex` states that its root is `a c + b c`, and
  `mergedFamilyMap` is the corresponding `mergedRootMap`.
  `root_mergedIndex_galois`: if each `σ` carries the pair at `c` to the
  pair at `σ • c` in one of the two orders, the merged roots at `c` and
  `σ • c` correspond under `σ`.

### Main theorem

`GaloisCompatiblePinningData.exists_mergedRootGaloisData`: assume
`[FiniteDimensional k K]`. Let `J : Type v` carry a `Γ`-action, and let
`a b : J → D.pinning.base.support` satisfy, for every `c`,
`a c ≠ b c`, `⟨a c, (b c)^∨⟩ = -1` and `⟨b c, (a c)^∨⟩ = -1`. Assume
moreover that for all `σ`, `c`, either `a (σ • c) = σ(a c)` and
`b (σ • c) = σ(b c)`, or `a (σ • c) = σ(b c)` and
`b (σ • c) = σ(a c)` (with `σ(-)` given by `D.action`). Then there is
`M : J → D.pinning.SteinbergModifiedData` such that for all `σ`, `c`,
`s : K`, `t : Kˣ`:

1. `(M c).a = a c`, `(M c).b = b c`, `(M c).ca = 1`, `(M c).cb = 1`;
2. `(M c).nAlpha.point ∈ U(α_c)(K) ⊔ U(-α_c)(K)`, where
   `α_c = root (M c).alpha`;
3. `σ((M c).xAlpha.rationalPoint s) = (M (σ • c)).xAlpha.rationalPoint (σ s)`;
4. `σ((M c).nAlpha.point) = (M (σ • c)).nAlpha.point`;
5. `σ(corootRationalPoint D.pinning (M c).alpha t) =
   corootRationalPoint D.pinning (M (σ • c)).alpha (Units.map σ.toMonoidHom t)`.

## Implemented proof

`exists_stabilizer_sum_ne_zero`: the automorphisms, viewed as monoid
homomorphisms `K →* K`, are `K`-linearly independent
(`linearIndependent_monoidHom`). A vanishing sum gives the coefficient
`f 1` at the identity equal to zero.

`exists_galoisTwist`: choose an orbit representative `rep c`
(`Quotient.out` of the orbit relation), constant on orbits. For each
representative choose `z` by the previous lemma, applied to `g ↦ φ g (rep c)`
(nonzero at `1`). Set `mu c = ∑_{g • rep c = c} φ g (rep c) · g(z)`.
Reindexing the sum by `g ↦ σ g` and using the cocycle identity gives
`mu (σ • c) = σ(mu c) · φ σ c`. The value at a representative is nonzero
by the choice of `z`. Every other value is then nonzero by this transport
law.

`scale`: the four fields of `RootParametrization` follow the published
`IsRootParametrization.scale` in `LocalWeylNormalizationStatement`. The
scaling map is an isomorphism (`LocalSplitCell.scaleIso`), so the
composite is a monomorphism and a closed immersion with the same image,
and torus equivariance follows from `LocalSplitCell.rootPoint_scaleHom`.
`scale_point` is `rootPoint_scaleHom`, and `scale_rationalPoint`
specializes it to the identity test.

Representatives: the opposite pair is chosen from the published existence
theorem `splitPinnedOppositeFactorPairs`. Uniqueness is the published
`RelativeRoot.IsOppositeFactorPair.eq`. The character action of
`q₁ v q₂` is the reflection by the published `oppositeFactorPair_weylAction`.
The membership statement holds because `q₁`, `q₂` lie in `U(-α)` and `v` lies in `U(α)`. For the conjugation
statement, the pair is moved to the descended torus
(`IsOppositeFactorPair.changeTorus`), conjugated by `σ`
(`IsOppositeFactorPair.rationalConjugate`), and moved back
(`isOppositeFactorPair_changeTorus_iff`). Uniqueness at `w` then
identifies it with the chosen pair at `w`. `rationalConjugate` is
multiplicative.

Merged roots: `mergedRootMap` is `RootParametrization.conjugate`. The Weyl action
of `n_a` sends `b` to `s_a(b)`, whose root is `a + b` by
`root_reflectionPerm_of_pairing_eq_neg_one`. The value formula is
`conjugate_rationalPoint`. Exact transport combines this formula with the
published Galois compatibility of the root maps
(`RootMapsGaloisCompatible.rationalPoints`) and of the canonical
representatives (`canonicalSimpleReflectionRepresentatives_rationalConjugate`).
In the swapped order `(σ b, σ a)`, both `mergedRootMap (σ a) (σ b) r'` and
`mergedRootMap (σ b) (σ a) r'` are root maps of the same index `r'`. They
differ by their `comparisonScalar`, which is nonzero
(`rationalPoint_eq_comparisonScalar`, `comparisonScalar_ne_zero`).

`coroot_galoisRepresentation`: transport the pinned root datum to the
base-changed descended torus (`torusTransportRootDatum`,
`IsRootDatumOf.torusTransport` along `D.torusIso`). The inclusion is a
closed immersion, being the pinning's inclusion composed with an
isomorphism. The Weyl image is finite (`splitTorusWeylImageFinite`).
The published Galois index permutation `galoisRootIndexEquiv σ` sends `α`
to `β`, because transported roots are injective and correspond under `σ`.
`IsRootDatumOf.galoisRootIndexEquiv_coroot` then gives the coroot
statement.

Main theorem: for each `σ`, `c`,
`exists_mergedRootMap_rationalConjugate` (with the orientation
hypothesis) gives a nonzero `φ σ c` with
`σ(y_c(s)) = y_{σc}(σ s · φ σ c)` for the unnormalized maps
`y_c = mergedFamilyMap`. Evaluating at `s = 1`, comparing `σ τ` with `σ`
after `τ` (`rationalConjugate_mul`), and using injectivity of `y`
(`rationalPoint_injective` from `AdjacentSimpleRootCommutator`) gives the
cocycle identity. `exists_galoisTwist` gives `mu`, and
`xAlpha_c = y_c.scale (mu c)` satisfies
`σ(xAlpha_c(s)) = xAlpha_{σc}(σ s)`. The representative is
`rootPointReflectionRepresentative` around the nonidentity point
`xAlpha_c(1)` (`rationalPoint_mem`, `rationalPoint_ne_one`). Its transport
follows from the conjugation statement above, since
`σ(xAlpha_c(1)) = xAlpha_{σc}(1)` and the merged roots correspond
(`root_mergedIndex_galois`). The coroot clause is the published
`corootRationalPoint_rationalConjugate` applied to
`coroot_galoisRepresentation`. The data take `ca = cb = 1`.
