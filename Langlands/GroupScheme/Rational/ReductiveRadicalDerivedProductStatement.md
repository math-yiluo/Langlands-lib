# Generation by the radical and derived subgroup

Let \(G\) be a connected reductive group over a field \(k\), \(R\) its
geometric solvable radical and \(D\) its scheme-theoretic derived subgroup.
Then the multiplication \(R\times_k D\to G\) is surjective as a morphism of
schemes, and the pullback \(D\times_G R\) is finite over \(k\). The
surjectivity concerns the underlying scheme and gives the geometric
generation assertion. The intersection is the full closed group scheme and
may have infinitesimal structure.

This is the algebraic-group recall on generation and finite intersection in
David Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, French
edition dated 9 July 2026, V.2.1, printed p. 104 (PDF p. 110). The following
section V.2.2 uses this product for the displayed abelianization formula.
