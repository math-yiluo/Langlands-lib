# A descended type-A2 derived group for a cyclic D4 root pair

The coroot norm vanishes by anisotropy. The cyclic action preserves the six coroots of each A2 pair. An integral cocharacter with those zero roots determines a Galois-stable Levi centralizer, and its derived group and induced maximal torus descend. The induced torus is split over the cubic field because it embeds as a closed torus in the original split torus. The compatible base identifies its two simple coroots with the ordered pair in the ambient D4 torus.

This constructs the actual rank-two group and torus appearing in the cyclic case of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed pp. 372–373.
