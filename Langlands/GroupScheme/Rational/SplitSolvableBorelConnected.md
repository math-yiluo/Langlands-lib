# Split-solvable containment in a split Borel of a connected group

This internal theorem is the connected ambient-group case of Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017), Theorem 17.105(a), pp.384–385.

The theorem assumes a connected smooth affine group over an arbitrary field, a geometric Borel subgroup that is split solvable, and a closed split-solvable subgroup. It produces a rational conjugate of the Borel containing the subgroup on every relative test scheme.

The proof constructs the smooth homogeneous quotient by the Borel, whose properness follows from its geometric parabolicity. Properness implies pseudo-completeness. The published split-solvable fixed-point theorem supplies a rational fixed coset for the subgroup action. Field cocycle vanishing lifts this coset to a rational group point. The fixed-coset factorization then places the conjugated subgroup inside the Borel, and reversing the inner automorphism gives the stated containment.
