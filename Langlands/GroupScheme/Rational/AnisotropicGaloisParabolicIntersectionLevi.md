# Levi embeddings for descended parabolic intersections in anisotropic groups

`AnisotropicGaloisParabolicIntersectionLeviStatement` concerns an anisotropic affine semisimple group \(G\) of locally finite type over a characteristic-zero field \(k\). Let \(K/k\) be finite Galois and let a cocharacter of \(G_K\) define a parabolic of Lie codimension at most \(c\). The statement gives a connected reductive closed subgroup \(H\) of \(G\) with \(\dim \operatorname{Lie}(G_K) \le \dim \operatorname{Lie}(H) + [K:k]c\). The inclusion of \(H_K\) in \(G_K\) factors through the actual parabolic, and its composite with the limit homomorphism to the Levi is a closed embedding.

Take the identity component of the descended intersection of the Galois orbit. Cartier smoothness and the open identity-component inclusion give equality of the Lie dimensions. Reductivity follows from anisotropy of the semisimple ambient group. The identity member of the Galois orbit ensures the inclusion after base change factors through the given parabolic. The kernel of the restricted limit map is a closed subgroup of the dynamic unipotent radical. This kernel is normal in \(H_K\), and in characteristic zero it is smooth and connected. Reductivity forces its full scheme-theoretic kernel to be trivial, so the restricted limit is monic and therefore a closed embedding.

These are the subgroup and dimension steps for parabolic intersections in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Theorem 6.26, pp. 354–356, and §6.8, Lemma 6.32, p. 380. [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
