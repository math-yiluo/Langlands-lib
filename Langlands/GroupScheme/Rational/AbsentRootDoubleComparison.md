# Intrinsic root groups when only the doubled root can occur

This internal comparison works over any field at arbitrary universe `u`, with a
smooth affine ambient group and a homomorphism from a split torus. The specified
character is nonzero. It is not an actual relative root, and none of its natural
multiples with multiplier at least three is an actual relative root. These
actual-root support bounds are explicit hypotheses; the unit does not derive
them from a generic fibre or facet.

The positive cyclic semigroup generated by the character and the one generated
by its double agree on every ambient tangent weight. A weight in the first
semigroup is a positive natural multiple. Split-torus torsion freeness makes it
nonzero, so it is an actual root. The support hypotheses force its multiplier
to be two. The converse containment of doubled positive multiples is immediate.

Ambient-weight congruence identifies the two semigroup subgroup functors.
The cyclic root functor comparison then gives
`subgroup i a = subgroup i (2 • a)` on every test scheme. In particular this
does not use rational-point density or assume occurrence of the doubled root.

`absentRootDoubleIso` applies uniqueness of represented subgroup functors to
construct an actual isomorphism between any supplied representatives of these
two root functors. The provenance is the composition of the named published
cyclic comparison, weight congruence and split-torus nonvanishing theorems. No
additional original-literature assertion or final facet-root claim is made.
