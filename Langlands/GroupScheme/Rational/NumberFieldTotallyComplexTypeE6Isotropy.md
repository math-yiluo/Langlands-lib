# Isotropy of type E₆ over totally complex number fields

`NumberFieldTotallyComplexTypeE6IsotropyStatement` asserts that every affine locally finite-type simply connected absolutely almost-simple group of type E₆ over a totally complex number field has positive split rank. The absolute type is specified by actual Tits index data and a numbering with Cartan matrix `CartanMatrix.E 6`. This formulation covers inner and outer forms.

This is the E₆ case of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), Theorem 6.25, printed p. 352. Theorem 6.26 and its proof on printed pp. 352–356 give the isotropy step conditional on trivial first Galois cohomology of the quasi-split group of the same inner type. The latter vanishing is established in the E₆ argument in §6.8.
