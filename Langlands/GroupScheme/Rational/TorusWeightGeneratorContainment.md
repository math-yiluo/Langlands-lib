# Admissible generators in the connected coordinate subgroup

This internal construction factors each smooth connected stable closed subgroup
whose actual Lie support is contained in an arbitrary additive character
semigroup through the connected coordinate weight subgroup of the original
smooth affine group. The semigroup may be empty or contain zero.

The proof restricts the original action to the represented subgroup. An
equivariant monomorphism preserves its intrinsic Lie support, so the existing
augmentation/Lie semigroup comparison bounds the subgroup's augmentation
support. Pullback of an ambient augmentation eigenfunction outside the
semigroup is therefore zero. Affinization commutes with the representing
inclusion, and the existing all-test coordinate range criterion gives
containment in the full coordinate weight subgroup. Connectedness then gives
factorization through its identity component.

`generatorWeightFactor` is this canonical morphism. Its composite with the
connected coordinate inclusion is the original representing inclusion.
`semigroupSubgroup_le_connectedWeightHom` follows by the universal property of
the all-test subgroup generated by the admissible generators.

The provenance is internal composition of the imported coordinate support,
equivariant transport, affinization and identity-component constructions. This
file proves one containment and does not assert the exact Lie image, the reverse
containment, maximality, unipotence or split unipotence.
