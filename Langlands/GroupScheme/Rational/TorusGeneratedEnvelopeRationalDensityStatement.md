# Rational density in the fixed closed torus envelope

Worker 1436. `TorusGeneratedEnvelopeRationalDensityStatement.{u}` asserts that,
for every infinite field `k : Type u`, each actual representative `D` of the
fixed closed torus envelope of a smooth connected affine group scheme `G` has
Zariski-dense actual `k`-rational points in `D.group.X.left`. The field may be
imperfect and have any characteristic. The ambient group need not be
reductive, perfect as an abstract algebraic group, solvable, or split.

The conclusion is the unchanged
`Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.PointTopology.HasDenseRationalPoints k D.group.X`.
It means that the union of `Set.range p.left`, over actual morphisms
`p : Over.mk (𝟙 (Spec k)) ⟶ D.group.X`, is dense in the Zariski topological
space of the actual representing scheme. It assumes no topology on the field
and makes no density assertion for the rational points of the ambient `G`.

The representative is the existing `TorusGeneratedEnvelope G`: its actual
closed inclusion represents the fixed `closedTorusEnvelope G`, defined as the
intersection of all actual closed subgroup ranges containing every closed
ground-field torus on every relative scheme test. No new envelope or pointwise
abstract generation model is introduced.

The source is Conrad--Gabber--Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive groups*, second edition
(2015), Proposition A.2.11, printed pp.477--480 / one-based PDF pp.503--506,
registered as `conrad-gabber-prasad-2015-pseudo-reductive`. The proposition
asserts that the smooth connected subgroup `G_t` generated by the ground-field
tori is unirational. Its first proof paragraph uses a finite product of tori
mapping surjectively onto `G_t` and unirationality of each torus. The present
density conclusion is the elementary consequence over an infinite field:
a dominant rational parametrization by affine space is defined on a nonempty
open of affine space, whose rational points are Zariski dense; their images
are rational points of `G_t` and are dense there. This is the density used
for `G_t` in Lemma C.2.1, printed pp.592--593 / PDF pp.618--619.

To prove this exact target, one must identify the source subgroup with the
fixed envelope by its least closed-subgroup property and transport density
along the existing isomorphism between representatives over `G`. No separate
smoothness or connectedness premise on `D.group` is needed, since the
structural existence and uniqueness results imply those properties for every
representative. Establishing that identification, smooth connected existence,
the source unirationality, and its density consequence are retained proof
obligations. Neither unirationality nor a density toolkit is assumed in the
Lean proposition.

This file states a focused prerequisite and supplies no proof. It asserts
neither density over a finite field nor density in a general ambient smooth
connected affine group. It does not prove the pseudo-parabolic rational-lifting
theorem, normality of the envelope, or compatibility with field extension.
