# Actual smooth connected closed coordinate blocks

For an affine group scheme with natural vector coordinates on every test
scheme, every coordinate block has an actual smooth connected closed
representative. An enumeration of the block defines an inclusion from the
standard vector group by extending its coordinates by zero. Reading back
the enumerated coordinates gives an actual group-scheme retraction.
The inclusion represents precisely the all-test coordinate subgroup.

The retraction proves that the inclusion is a monomorphism. The retraction
is separated because the ambient group scheme is separated over the field.
Its section is therefore a closed immersion. The representative is smooth
because its polynomial coordinate ring is smooth, and it is connected
because that ring is a domain. Empty blocks and zero vector dimension are
included.

This is an internal construction from natural coordinates, the standard
polynomial vector group, and the closedness of a section of a separated
morphism. It supplies actual representatives needed by the coordinate
product lemma. It does not identify coordinate blocks with semigroup
subgroups, diagonalize torus actions or prove the ordered semigroup
product assertion.
