# Weight subgroups of corepresented groups with split-torus actions

This module defines, for a split-torus action `σ : TorusAction F I` on a
corepresented group functor `F` (with `e : (F ⋙ forget GrpCat).CorepresentableBy B`,
see `Rational.TorusGrading`) and a subsemigroup `P` of the character lattice
`I → ℤ`, the subgroup `U_P` of points whose weight components outside `P` are
trivial. It shows that `U_P` is a closed, torus-stable subgroup, and that `U_{P'}`
is normalized by `U_P` when `P'` absorbs `P`. All declarations are in namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Corepresented`.

## Statements

- `eq_one_iff`: a point is the identity iff its coordinates are
  `algebraMap R A ∘ AffineLinearEmbedding.counit e`.

Kernel subgroups. For an `R`-algebra `A`, a subalgebra `S` of `A` and an ideal `J`
of `S`:

- `kerSubgroup F S J` is the subgroup of `F.obj A` of images of points over `S`
  that become trivial over `S ⧸ J` (the image under `F.map S.val` of the kernel of
  `F.map (S → S ⧸ J)`). `subalgebraHom` and `subalgebraQuotientHom` are the two
  algebra maps involved.
- `eq_map_ofCoord`: a point whose coordinates factor through `S` comes from `S`.
- `mem_kerSubgroup`: `y ∈ kerSubgroup F S J` iff its coordinates factor as
  `S.val ∘ g` with `g f - AffineLinearEmbedding.counit e f ∈ J` for all `f`.
- `kerSubgroup_le_range`, `conj_mem_kerSubgroup` (the kernel subgroup is normalized
  by all points coming from `S`), and `inv_mul_mem_kerSubgroup` (two points from
  `S` whose coordinates agree modulo `J` differ by an element of the kernel
  subgroup).

Supported elements of the group algebra `A[I → ℤ]` (`laurent I A`):

- `coeff_mul_eq_zero_of`: a coefficient of a product vanishes when no pair of
  nonzero coefficients has exponents adding up to it.
- `supportedSubalgebra I A M` for a submonoid `M` is the subalgebra of elements
  whose coefficients vanish outside `M`; `supportedIdeal I A M P hP`, for a set `P`
  with `M + P ⊆ P`, is the ideal of that subalgebra of elements whose coefficients
  vanish outside `P`. `mem_supportedSubalgebra` and `mem_supportedIdeal` unfold
  them, and `coeff_algebraMap` computes the coefficients of scalars.
- `insertZero P` is the submonoid `{0} ∪ P` of a subsemigroup `P`
  (`mem_insertZero`), and `insertZero_add_mem` says that `{0} ∪ P` absorbs `P`.

Weight subgroups. For `x : F.obj A`, the weight condition outside a set `P` is
`coord e x (comp e σ m f) = algebraMap R A (AffineLinearEmbedding.counit e (comp e σ m f))` for all `f : B`
and all `m ∉ P`.

- `coeff_coord_hat_sub` computes the coefficients of the coordinates of the twisted
  point `hat σ x` minus the counit.
- `weightCondition_iff`: for a submonoid `M ⊇ P` with `M + P ⊆ P`, the weight
  condition outside `P` holds iff `hat σ x` lies in the kernel subgroup of the
  supported subalgebra for `M` and its supported ideal for `P`.
- `weightSubgroupOf e σ P M hPM hP A` is the subgroup of `F.obj A` given by the
  weight condition outside `P`; its subgroup structure is the preimage of that
  kernel subgroup under the homomorphism `hat σ`.
- `weightSubgroup e σ P A` is `weightSubgroupOf` with `M = insertZero P`, for a
  subsemigroup `P`; `mem_weightSubgroup` states that membership is exactly the
  weight condition outside `P`, and `weightSubgroup_eq` shows that any admissible
  `M` gives the same subgroup. `hat_mem_kerSubgroup` records the kernel subgroup
  membership.
- `conj_mem_weightSubgroup`: if `P' ⊆ P` and `P + P' ⊆ P'`, then
  `x y x⁻¹ ∈ U_{P'}` for `x ∈ U_P` and `y ∈ U_{P'}`. Proof: with `M = {0} ∪ P`, both
  `hat σ x` and `hat σ y` lie in the kernel subgroup for `M`, and `hat σ` is a
  homomorphism, so this is `conj_mem_kerSubgroup`.
- `map_mem_weightSubgroup`: weight subgroups are preserved by all algebra maps.
- `act_mem_weightSubgroup`: weight subgroups are stable under the torus action
  (`coord_act_of_isHomogeneous` with `counit_comp`).
- `weightIdeal e σ P` is the ideal of `B` generated by
  `comp e σ m f - AffineLinearEmbedding.counit e (comp e σ m f)` for `m ∉ P`, and `isCutOutBy_weightIdeal`
  shows that `weightSubgroup e σ P` is cut out by it (`IsCutOutBy` from
  `CorepresentedClosedSubgroup`). Hence `U_P` is the closed subgroup scheme
  `Spec (B ⧸ weightIdeal e σ P)` of `Spec B`.

Restriction to a stable closed subgroup. For a family of subgroups `H` cut out by
an ideal `J` (`hJ : IsCutOutBy e H J`) and stable under the torus (`hH`):

- `TorusAction.restrict hJ hH` is the torus action on `cutFunctor hJ`.
- `coord_cut_mk`, `univ_cut` and `counit_cut_mk` compute coordinates, the universal
  point and the counit of the corepresentation `cutCorepresentableBy hJ` by `B ⧸ J`.
- `rho_restrict` and `comp_restrict`: the coaction and the weight components on
  `B ⧸ J` are those of `B` followed by the quotient map.

## Provenance

Internal supporting material for the proof of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.RelativeRoot.CyclicWeightFiltrationStatement` (task
023326af). For a nonzero character `a` and `r ≥ 1`, Conrad–Gabber–Prasad,
*Pseudo-reductive groups*, 2nd ed., §3.3 and Lemma 3.3.8 (printed pp.111-112),
consider the subgroups `U_{A_r}(G)` attached to the cyclic tails
`A_r = {n • a : n ≥ r}`, constructed there by the dynamic method. Here `U_P` is
instead defined directly by the weight decomposition of the coordinate ring: its
coordinate ring is the quotient of `B` by the weight components of weights outside
`P` minus their augmentations. The identification of these subgroups with the
Library's `weightFiltration` is not part of this module; the general set `P` and the
kernel-subgroup description of the subgroup structure are internal to this
formalization.
