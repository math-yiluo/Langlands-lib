# Actual simple relative roots as a rational basis

Author: Worker 1316.

This internal derived module constructs the basis and rank comparison from the
published actual root sets and rational root span. It does not prove the
unconditional `RelativeSimpleRootBasisStatement`: the last theorem explicitly
retains `RelativeRootSystemStatement` as its input. That structural statement
remains a separate proof obligation.

`ratSpan_simpleRelativeRoots_eq_relativeRootSpan` assumes that the chosen
integral cocharacter is generic and that actual relative roots are closed under
negation. The published
`relativeRoots_subset_addSubgroupClosure_simpleRelativeRoots` then puts every
actual root in the additive group generated by the actual simple roots. The
inverse image under `toRat` of the rational simple-root span is an additive
subgroup containing those simple roots, so it contains their generated group.
This gives one inclusion of rational spans. The other uses the defining
inclusions of simple roots into positive roots and into all relative roots.

`span_simpleRelativeRootVector_eq_top` transfers that equality through the
inclusion of `relativeRootSpan i` into the full rationalized character lattice.
Its vectors are exactly the published `simpleRelativeRootVector i ν α`.
`simpleRelativeRootBasisOfLinearIndependent` uses rational independence of those
same characters and the spanning equality in `Module.Basis.mk`. The application
lemma identifies every resulting vector with the actual published vector.
`relativeRootRank_eq_natCard_simpleRelativeRoots_of_linearIndependent` applies
`Module.finrank_eq_nat_card_basis` to this basis. No finiteness, nonemptiness or
positive-rank premise is added to these algebraic constructions.

`HasRelativeRootSystem.simpleRelativeRootBasis` uses the published
`HasRelativeRootSystem.neg_mem_relativeRoots` and
`HasRelativeRootSystem.linearIndependent_simpleRelativeRoots` for a split torus.
Its application lemma again identifies the basis vectors with the actual
characters. The construction uses the full actual root set and has no
reducedness requirement. An empty simple system gives a basis indexed by that
empty set, with rank zero.

`relativeSimpleRootBasisStatement_of_relativeRootSystemStatement` specializes
these results to each connected reductive group and its maximal split torus.
It extracts the affine and monomorphism instances from the published geometric
hypotheses. The root-system input supplies finiteness of the actual simple roots,
the constructed basis and the rank/cardinality equality. Both statement names
in its type are the unchanged published propositions at arbitrary universe `u`;
the field is arbitrary.

The provenance is internal and derived: the proof composes the published
positive-root induction, actual root-system consequences and Mathlib basis
linear algebra. No external structural theorem is asserted or proved here. In
particular, this conditional bridge does not resolve the general relative-root-
system proof gap flagged in Milne's p.549 errata or replace the proof obligations
of its owner task.
