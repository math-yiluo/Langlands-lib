# The isotropic type E₆ marking with unmarked short arm

Consider an affine, locally finite-type, simply connected group over a field
with an actual Tits index numbered by `CartanMatrix.E 6`. If the short-arm
vertex is unmarked and some vertex is marked, the distinguished vertices are
exactly the two long-arm endpoints.

Absolute opposition exchanges the two long arms. Each element of the star
action either exchanges those arms or fixes all vertices. The restrictions
of distinguished simple roots give independent relative simple roots on
the distinguished star orbits. Thus the rational coordinate map from the
marked orbit representatives is injective on vectors supported at those
representatives. Its projected signed root list maps onto the relative roots
together with zero.

The coroot of a positive multiple of one marked coordinate gives an integral
row on the projected coordinates. Its reflection preserves the projected root
list by the relative root system axiom and injectivity of the coordinate map.
The finite calculation then excludes the central vertex and both inner
long-arm vertices from the marking. Opposition and nonemptiness force both
endpoints to be marked. This checks the marking in the exceptional isotropic
diagrams of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*,
§6.8, diagrams (6.53) and (6.57).
