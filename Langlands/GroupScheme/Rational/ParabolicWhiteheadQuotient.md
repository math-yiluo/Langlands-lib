# The quotient by actual rational parabolic radicals

Authors: Worker 1757.

For a group scheme `G` over an arbitrary field `k : Type u`,
`parabolicWhiteheadQuotient G` is the actual group quotient
`rationalPoints G ⧸ parabolicElementarySubgroup G`. The imported elementary
subgroup is generated by the ranges of `rationalPoints.map (r ≫ p)` for all
actual rational parabolic embeddings `p : P ⟶ G` and represented geometric
unipotent radicals `r : U ⟶ P`. Its published normality instance supplies the
group structure on this quotient.

The definition makes sense without smoothness, affineness, reductivity, local
field, or simple connectedness hypotheses. It quantifies the radical
representatives through the existing elementary subgroup; it does not assert
their existence. The word Whitehead refers here to this precise parabolic
quotient, without an additional identification with a different definition of
an algebraic K-group.

`parabolicWhiteheadQuotientMk` is the canonical quotient homomorphism. The
module proves its surjectivity and identifies its kernel with the elementary
subgroup. It also proves that a homomorphism from `G(k)` into any group
`H : Type v`, with independent universe `v`, kills that subgroup if and only
if its composite with every represented parabolic-radical point map is the
trivial homomorphism. The proof combines the imported least-subgroup property
with extensionality of group homomorphisms and membership in their ranges.
In particular, every such radical maps trivially under the canonical quotient
homomorphism.

The quotient's universal property is stated as unique existence of a
homomorphism `q : parabolicWhiteheadQuotient G →* H` with
`q.comp (parabolicWhiteheadQuotientMk G) = f`, when `f` kills all the radical
images. The proof uses Mathlib's quotient lift and its homomorphism
extensionality theorem.

Finally, `parabolicWhiteheadQuotient_subsingleton_iff` says that the quotient
has at most one element if and only if the elementary subgroup is the whole
rational-point group. A quotient group has its identity, so this is the usual
triviality assertion.
`parabolicElementarySubgroup_eq_top_iff` identifies this equality with the
assertion that every normal subgroup containing all the actual radical
images is the whole group. The forward implication uses the elementary
subgroup's least-subgroup property. The reverse implication takes that
normal subgroup to be the elementary subgroup itself, using its published
normality and generator containment.

## Provenance and scope

This is an internal construction and a collection of elementary consequences
of the published `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.ElementarySubgroup` interface
and Mathlib's actual group quotient. It introduces no arithmetic input and
does not prove local Kneser–Tits. No external source is needed for these
quotient constructions or their universal properties.
