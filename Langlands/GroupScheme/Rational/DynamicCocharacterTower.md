# Dynamic subgroups of cocharacters extended along a tower

The cocharacter lattice extension through an intermediate field
is compatible with its composite into the original ambient group.
Naturality of the tower isomorphism identifies the two ambient
cocharacter composites. The dynamic centralizer and unipotent
subgroup then extend along the tower by their base change and
isomorphism comparisons.

The dynamic centralizer and unipotent subgroup of the Galois-translated
cocharacter equal canonical transport of the corresponding original
subgroups. The proof uses transport of the torus inclusion and the
all-test comparison for the dynamic subgroup functors. These comparisons apply to
the quadratic opposite-Borel torus of Platonov and Rapinchuk,
*Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), Lemma 6.28.
