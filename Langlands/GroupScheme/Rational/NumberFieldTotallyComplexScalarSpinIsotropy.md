# Scalar spin isotropy over totally complex number fields

Let a nondegenerate scalar quadratic form over a totally complex number
field have dimension greater than four. Any supplied spin cover of its
isometry group has positive split rank. The spin cover data include
smoothness of the isometry group and a central isogeny onto its identity
component.

The scalar polar matrix defines an ordinary quadratic form with the same
singular vectors. At a finite place the dimension of an anisotropic
quadratic form is at most four. At a complex place a diagonalization to a
sum of squares produces an isotropic vector in two coordinates. The
number-field quadratic isotropy theorem therefore supplies a nonzero
singular vector over the ground field. Its line gives a positive Witt
index. The split rank of the spin cover agrees with that of the identity
component of the isometry group, which is at least the Witt index.

This scalar-form calculation supplies an isotropy implication for the D₄
kernel appearing in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number
Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed pp. 379 and 382. The hypothesis is a scalar
quadratic presentation of the spin cover.
