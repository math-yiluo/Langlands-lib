# Binary special linear homomorphisms over orthogonal orbits of simple roots

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.OrthogonalOrbitSpecialLinear`, namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme.SplitPinning`.

This module provides rank-one homomorphisms `SL₂ → G` attached to simple roots of a
split pinning, and descends products of them over a Galois-stable set of pairwise
orthogonal simple roots to a homomorphism `SL₂(E) → G(k)` for a field `E` with
embeddings into a Galois splitting field `K`. It is internal glue between three
Library pieces:

- the homomorphism `LinkedRootParametrization.specialLinearHom` of module
  `GroupScheme.Reductive.LinkedRootSpecialLinearHom`;
- the orthogonal-orbit products of module `GroupScheme.Rational.OrthogonalOrbitCorootWords`
  (`rankData`, `rootsCommute_of_orthogonal`, `orbitXp`, `orbitXm`, `orbitCoroot`);
- Galois descent of rational points, `GaloisPointComparison.exists_rationalExtend_eq_of_fixed`
  (module `GroupScheme.Rational.OrthogonalOrbitDescent`).

No literature theorem is used as a hypothesis. The construction is the familiar
descent of a product of commuting `SL₂`'s over a Galois orbit of orthogonal simple
roots; every step is proved in Lean, and all statements here are internal.

## Split case

Section `Split` fixes a field `k`, a group scheme `G` over `Spec k` with affine structure
map, a split pinning `p` of `G`, and normalized simple-root parametrizations
`x : p.NormalizedSimpleRootParametrizations`.

- `rootSL2 x a : SL₂(k) →* G(k)` is `specialLinearHom` of the linked parametrization
  `(x a).linked`. Its hypothesis `⟨a, a^∨⟩ = 2` is
  `NormalizedRootParametrization.pairing_root_coroot`.
- `rootSL2_upper`, `rootSL2_lower`, `rootSL2_diag`: the upper unipotent `upper t`, the
  lower unipotent `lower t` and the diagonal `diag u` go to `(rankData x a).xp t`,
  `(rankData x a).xm t` and `(rankData x a).h u`. These are the three defining equations
  of `specialLinearHom`.
- `rootSL2_mem`: every value lies in `(rankData x a).rootSubgroup`, the subgroup
  generated by the two root groups. Proof: `SL₂` over a field is generated by
  transvections (`Matrix.SL2.transvection_induction`), and the transvections go to root
  elements.

## Galois case

Section `Galois` fixes fields `k ⊆ K`, a commutative group scheme `T` and a group scheme
`G` over `k` (affine structure map), `i : T ⟶ G`, and over `K` a split pinning `p` of
`G_K` whose torus is identified with `T_K` by `eT`, with `hT` saying that the pinning's
inclusion is `i_K` after `eT`. Further hypotheses:

- `x`: normalized simple-root parametrizations of `p`;
- `ρ : Gal(K/k) →* Perm(simple roots)` and `hx : RootMapsGaloisCompatible k K p x ρ`
  (Galois conjugation carries the parametrization of `a` to that of `ρ σ a`);
- `hroot`: the root of `ρ σ a` is the Galois transform of the root of `a`, transported
  by `eT`;
- `O`: a set of simple roots, stable under every `ρ σ` (`hOs`), whose members are
  pairwise orthogonal (`hO`).

Results:

- `rootSL2_rationalConjugate`: conjugating the coefficients of `rootSL2 x a g` by `σ`
  gives `rootSL2 x (ρ σ a)` at the matrix `g` with `σ` applied entrywise. Proof: both
  sides are homomorphisms out of `SL₂(K)`, so by `hom_ext` (module
  `SpecialLinearBinaryPresentation`) it suffices to compare them on upper and lower
  unipotents. The upper case is `RootMapsGaloisCompatible.rationalPoint`; the lower
  case is `RootMapsGaloisCompatible.negPointFamily_rationalConjugate`.
- `equivariantFamilies k K p ρ`: the subgroup of families `g : simple roots → SL₂(K)`
  with `g (ρ σ a) = σ(g a)` for all `σ` and `a`.
- For a field `E` over `k` and embeddings `τ : simple roots → (E →ₐ[k] K)` with
  `τ (ρ σ a) = σ ∘ τ a` for `a ∈ O` (hypothesis `hτ`):
  - `fieldFamilyPi k K p O τ : SL₂(E) →* (simple roots → SL₂(K))` sends `g` to the family
    whose value at `a ∈ O` is `g` with `τ a` applied entrywise and whose value off `O` is
    `1`. `fieldFamilyPi_apply` and `fieldFamilyPi_of_mem` unfold it.
  - `fieldFamilyPi_mem`: its values are equivariant families. On `O` this is `hτ`
    together with `specialLinearMap_comp` (applying `σ ∘ f` entrywise is applying `f`
    and then `σ`); off `O` both sides are `1` by `hOs`.
- With `[Fintype O]`:
  - `orbitSL2 k K p x O hO : (simple roots → SL₂(K)) →* G(K)` is the commuting product
    `RankOneRelation.prodHom` over `a ∈ O` of `rootSL2 x a (g a)`. The factors commute by
    `rootsCommute_of_orthogonal`. `orbitSL2_apply` unfolds it.
  - `orbitSL2_upper`, `orbitSL2_lower`, `orbitSL2_diag`: at families of upper unipotents,
    lower unipotents and diagonals, `orbitSL2` equals the Library's `orbitXp`, `orbitXm`
    and `orbitCoroot`.
  - `orbitSL2_rationalConjugate`: for an equivariant family `g`, `orbitSL2 g` is fixed by
    every `rationalConjugate k K G σ`. Proof: `RankOneRelation.map_prodHom_of_perm` with
    the permutation `ρ σ` restricted to `O`, and `rootSL2_rationalConjugate`.
  - `orbitSL2_congr`: `orbitSL2` depends only on the values on `O`.

## Descent

Section `Descent` adds `[FiniteDimensional k K] [IsGalois k K]`.

- `rationalExtend_injective_of_isAffineHom`: `rationalExtend k K G : G(k) → G(K)` is
  injective. Proof: through the coefficient equivalences `rationalCoeffEquiv` and
  `baseChangeCoeffEquiv`, this is `GaloisPoint.extend_injective` (the source is affine
  since `G.X.hom` is affine).
- `exists_orbitSL2_eq_rationalExtend`: for an equivariant family `g`, `orbitSL2 g` is the
  scalar extension of a point of `G(k)`. This is
  `GaloisPointComparison.exists_rationalExtend_eq_of_fixed` applied to
  `orbitSL2_rationalConjugate`.
- `orbitSL2Rational : equivariantFamilies k K p ρ →* G(k)` chooses that point. It is a
  homomorphism because `rationalExtend` is an injective homomorphism and `orbitSL2` is a
  homomorphism. `rationalExtend_orbitSL2Rational` is its defining equation.
- `fieldSL2 : SL₂(E) →* G(k)` is `orbitSL2Rational` composed with `fieldFamilyPi`.
  - `rationalExtend_fieldSL2`: its scalar extension at `g` is `orbitSL2` of the family
    `a ↦ (τ a)(g)` (by `orbitSL2_congr`, since `fieldFamilyPi` agrees with that family on
    `O`).
  - `rationalExtend_fieldSL2_upper`, `_lower`, `_diag`: the scalar extensions of
    `fieldSL2 (upper t)`, `fieldSL2 (lower t)` and `fieldSL2 (diag u)` are `orbitXp`,
    `orbitXm` of the coordinates `a ↦ τ a t`, and `orbitCoroot` of the units
    `a ↦ τ a u`.

The module does not identify the image of `fieldSL2` with a relative root group, does
not prove that the coordinates are bijective, and does not construct the coroot as a
morphism of group schemes; those are separate steps.
