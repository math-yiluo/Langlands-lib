# The even part of the trialitarian diagram action

The star action fixes the central vertex of the type D4 diagram. Its sign is therefore the sign of its permutation of the three leaves. If every permutation is even, the stabilizer of one leaf equals the kernel of the action, since an even permutation fixing both the centre and a leaf fixes every vertex. When the leaves are transitive, the field of the diagram action then has degree three.

If an odd permutation occurs, the sign kernel is an open normal subgroup of index two. Its fixed field is a quadratic Galois extension. The transported Tits index over this extension has the same numbered star permutations as the original index restricted to the sign kernel. These facts give the cubic and symmetric-group alternatives in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed pp. 371 and 373.
