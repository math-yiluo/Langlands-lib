# Local corrections with norm-one torsion coefficients

A local central correction records a cochain on the actual coefficient
points and the kernel coefficients of the source cocycle twisted by this
cochain. Triviality of the projected cocycle on the complete pullback
cover gives a coboundary formula on the chosen local coefficient field.
Over an algebraically closed coefficient field, lift the trivializing
point through the central isogeny. The resulting twist projects to one
and factors through the kernel at each Galois value. The crossed identity
of the restricted source cocycle gives the crossed identity in the kernel,
using injectivity of its point inclusion.

At an infinite place, assume the coefficient field is finite Galois over
the completion and algebraically closed. If the actual kernel is
identified with norm-one torsion for the number field extension `B/F`,
its tensor coordinates are a crossed cocycle of tensor units killed by
the indicated power and with determinant norm one. The archimedean
tensor radical lemma gives a radical, its power in the completion
tensor, its norm in the completion field, and the equality relating the
descended power and norm.

This internal comparison turns an actual source lift with trivial
archimedean projection into the local norm-power pairs for the
simultaneous approximation argument.
