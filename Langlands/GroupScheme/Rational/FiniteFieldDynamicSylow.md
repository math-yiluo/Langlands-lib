# Sylow subgroups from generic contracting subgroups over finite fields

This internal construction applies the split Tits-system Sylow constructor to
actual rational points of a connected reductive group over a finite field.
The maximal split torus and its generic cocharacter are specified. The whole
torus centralizer must have a specified closed representative which is a torus.
The result is an actual Sylow subgroup of the rational-point group whose
underlying subgroup is the dynamic contracting subgroup.

Rational points are finite by the existing finite-affine-point theorem and
the identity-test equivalence. A p-subgroup of a dynamic parabolic maps under
the limit homomorphism into its Levi. When this Levi is represented by a torus,
every element of that image killed by a power of the characteristic is the
identity. The kernel formula therefore puts the p-subgroup in the contracting
subgroup.

For a generic cocharacter, the Library identifies the dynamic centralizer with
the centralizer of the maximal split torus and the standard empty parabolic
with the dynamic parabolic. Its Levi decomposition splits every parabolic
point as a contracting point times a centralizing point. The centralizer
points are the kernel of the relative Weyl quotient. Contracting points form a
p-group by their actual split-unipotent representative and the unipotent
exponent theorem. The relative Tits system and the generic split-Tits Sylow
constructor now supply the claimed Sylow subgroup.

This construction does not yet identify every geometric Borel radical, and
it does not assert the full finite-field Sylow/Borel classification or the
parent maximal open pro-p proposition.
