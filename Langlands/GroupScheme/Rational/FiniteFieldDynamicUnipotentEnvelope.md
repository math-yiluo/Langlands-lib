# Dynamic contracting subgroups lie in the torus envelope over a finite field

Worker 1387. This internal module proves that, over a finite field, the
contracting subgroup `U_G(χ)` of every cocharacter `χ` of a smooth affine
group `G` is contained in the fixed `closedTorusEnvelope G`, on every test
scheme. The universe is arbitrary; no connectedness, reductivity or
splitting hypothesis on `G` is needed.

## The limit argument

`Dynamic.mem_of_mem_unipotent_of_normalizes` works over any base scheme. Let
`F` be a subgroup functor represented by a closed subgroup `j`, containing the
range of the cocharacter `χ`. Let `u` be a point of `U_G(χ)` over a test `T`
such that, after every refinement `a : V ⟶ T`, conjugation by `a ≫ u`
preserves `F`. Then `u` lies in `F`. Since `u⁻¹ ∈ U_G(χ)`, its orbit map
`t ↦ χ(t) u⁻¹ χ(t)⁻¹` extends over the affine line with value `1` at `0`.
Multiplying on the left by `u` gives an extension `e'` of
`t ↦ u χ(t) u⁻¹ χ(t)⁻¹`, which equals
`(u χ(t) u⁻¹) · χ(t)⁻¹` and so lies in `F` on `T ×ₛ 𝔾_m`. The published
`AffineLine.exists_lift_of_unitInclusion` lifts `e'` through the closed
immersion `j`. Its value at zero is `u · 1 = u`, which therefore lies in `F`.

## Application to the envelope

- `range_cocharacter_le_closedTorusEnvelope`: the range of a cocharacter of a
  smooth affine group over a field lies in the envelope. The published
  `torusImageClosedTorusStatement` factors `χ` through a closed torus.
- `rational_mem_closedTorusEnvelope_of_mem_unipotent`: a rational point `u` of
  `U_G(χ)` lies in the envelope. Rational points normalize the envelope by
  `closedTorusEnvelope_eq_comap_iso` applied to `G.innerIso u`, and
  `comp_eq_constantPoint` identifies the restricted points with the constant
  family of `u`.
- `unipotent_le_closedTorusEnvelope` (`k` finite): take the smooth
  representative `D = smoothTorusGeneratedEnvelope G`, the factor
  `χ' : 𝔾_m ⟶ D.group` of `χ`, and the closed representatives
  `iU : U → G` of `U_G(χ)` and `iV : V → D.group` of `U_D(χ')` from
  `Dynamic.Affine`. Both are smooth (`dynamicSmooth`), `U` is connected
  (`dynamicConnected`) and both are split unipotent
  (`Dynamic.isSplitUnipotent_of_isRepresentedBy_unipotent`). The composite
  `iV ≫ D.inclusion` lands in `U_G(χ)`, giving a closed subgroup
  `f : V ⟶ U`. Every rational point of `U` lies in the envelope by the
  preceding lemma, hence comes from `D`; by
  `unipotent_eq_comap_of_isClosedSubgroup` it lies in `U_D(χ')`, so it comes
  from `V`. `RelativeRoot.isIso_of_surjective_rationalPoints` makes `f` an
  isomorphism, so `U_G(χ) = range iU ≤ range D.inclusion`, the envelope.

## Source and provenance

The containment of `U_G(±λ)` in the subgroup generated by tori is used in the
finite-field part of the proof of Conrad–Gabber–Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive
groups*, second edition (2015), Proposition A.2.11, printed pp.479–480
(one-based PDF pp.505–506). There it is obtained from the solvable case and a
dense commutator map. The implemented route is different and internal: the
limit argument above applies to rational points, and the point count of
`SplitSeriesPointCount` replaces density, since rational points are not dense
over a finite field.
