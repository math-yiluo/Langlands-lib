# Actual fundamental highest-weight representations

Author: Worker 1284. Provenance: derived from Steinberg's description of fundamental representations, expressed using the Library's actual group schemes, integral torus characters and all-algebra character spaces.

For a supplied split pinning `p`, `positiveRootMonoid` is the additive monoid generated by its actual simple-root characters. Thus membership of `λ - μ` means that this difference is a nonnegative integral combination of simple roots. No choice of a real vector space or numerical embedding of the character lattice is used.

`representationWeightSpace p ρ λ` is the published `MatrixRepresentation.characterSpace` of the restriction of the actual representation `ρ` along `p.inclusion`. The published identification of rational characters of a commutative group scheme with its character lattice supplies the character. Affineness of the torus is supplied by the proof that it is split. These are character spaces defined at the universal affine point and satisfying the character equation on every coefficient algebra, including nonreduced algebras.

`IsHighestWeight p ρ λ` is a conjunction of four properties: actual irreducibility, the assertion that the supremum of the torus character spaces is the full original coordinate space, dimension one of the space at `λ`, and the assertion that every occurring weight `μ` satisfies the preceding nonnegative-root condition. Actual irreducibility is the published condition on subspaces stable on every affine test. This package records precisely the weight decomposition, highest line and weight bound needed for Steinberg's character calculation; it does not supply a formula for the root-group action.

`IsFundamentalWeight p a λ` is the integral duality equation
`⟨λ, α_b∨⟩ = δ_(b,a)` for every simple root `b`. A `FundamentalRepresentation p a` consists of a finite dimension, an actual homomorphism `G → GL_(Fin dimension)`, a torus character, and proofs that this character is fundamental and is its highest weight in the preceding sense. The data type asserts no existence. The proved comparison lemma identifies its highest weight with the published `Langlands.RootDatum.fundamentalWeightBasis` whenever the integral coroot span is the full cocharacter lattice. Its proof tests the perfect pairing on the simple-coroot basis and uses the two duality equations.

The sole unproved proposition in this file is the `Prop`-valued definition `SplitFundamentalRepresentationsExistStatement.{u}`. It quantifies over algebraically closed fields `k : Type u` of arbitrary characteristic, actual affine group schemes with a supplied split pinning, and the Library's actual `IsSimplyConnected` premise. It asks for a `FundamentalRepresentation` at each simple root. There is no additional coroot-span, geometric simple-connectedness, highest-weight or representation-existence premise. Passing from the Library's central-isogeny definition of simple connectedness to the requisite lattice and representation theory is part of proving this proposition.

This is a prerequisite statement, not a proof of representation existence, character independence, invariant generation, or the Steinberg quotient theorem. It does not address base change, root-map normalization, reflection representatives or descent to nonclosed fields. The data definitions themselves are available over arbitrary fields; the existence proposition is restricted to algebraically closed fields.
