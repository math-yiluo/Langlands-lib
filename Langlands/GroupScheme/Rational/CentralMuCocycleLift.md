# Global lifting through a central isogeny with roots-of-unity kernel

Let `f` be a central isogeny over a number field, with kernel
isomorphic over that field to `μ_n` for positive `n`. An everywhere
locally trivial natural cocycle for the target on a finite Galois
cover lifts to a natural source cocycle after finite Galois refinement.
The image of this lift is the original cocycle on the refined cover.

The first refinement gives lifts of all coefficients through `f`.
Since the kernel is `μ_n`, its points identify with the actual
`n`-th roots of unity, and the resulting defect values have `n`-th
power one. The local trivializations persist on this refinement.
The unit class of the defect vanishes by the finite and infinite
place computations and global injectivity. Its unit primitive can be
corrected to take values in the roots of unity on a further finite
Galois refinement.

The latter primitive identifies with a cochain of actual kernel
points. Its equation can be checked after the injective inclusion
into coefficient units. The central lifting criterion then gives a
natural lift on the further refinement. Composing both field
refinements identifies the result with a lift of the original
cocycle there. This internal construction supplies the global
lifting step for this kernel type.
