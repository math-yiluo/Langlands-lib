# A uniform exponent for maximal-torus cocycles in type E₆

`TypeE6MaximalTorusUniformPowerStatement` concerns an affine locally finite-type simply connected absolutely almost simple group over a field of characteristic zero. An actual Tits index has its absolute vertices numbered by the E₆ Cartan matrix. For any actual maximal torus of the group and any finite Galois cover, multiplication by \(2^{13}3^8\) takes every torus cocycle to a coboundary on that cover. `typeE6MaximalTorusCocycleOrder` deduces the two- and three-primary order statement `TypeE6MaximalTorusCocycleOrderStatement` by choosing the exponents 13 and 8.

`typeE6MaximalTorusUniformPower` refines the coefficient field by a finite Galois field splitting the specified maximal torus. It constructs the root datum of that torus over the larger field. The Cartan comparison with the supplied Tits index gives its E₆ numbering, and simple connectedness gives full integral coroot span. The ordinary Galois action on the root datum acts on weights by the character action of the torus. `typeE6PermutationSubgroup` supplies a permutation basis after restriction to a subgroup of index dividing \(2^{13}3^8\). The index annihilation theorem for tori proves triviality of the indicated power on the refinement, and finite Galois descent returns its coboundary to the specified cover.

This is the torus-exponent step of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, Proposition 6.21, printed pp. 375–377 (PDF pp. 385–387). Their argument uses a type D₅ subsystem to eliminate five. The proof here uses an integral permutation basis for an order-five Weyl element, induced-torus Hilbert 90 and the restriction–corestriction index law.
