# Geometric-point detection for root-subset intersections

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.RootSubsetIntersectionReduction`, namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme`, universe `u`.

This is an internal reduction for Borel–Tits 3.22(b), whose exact target is
`ClosedRootSubsetGroupIntersectionStatement` in `ClosedRootSubsetStatements`.
It does not prove that root-theoretic target or add an assumption to it.

`HasGeometricPointIntersection` tests three subgroup homomorphisms on every
`Spec K → Spec k` with `K : Type u` algebraically closed. The base map is explicit;
no finite generation or chosen algebra structure is assumed.

For a closed subgroup `j` and a reduced test scheme `U`,
`SubgroupFunctor.mem_range_iff_geometricPoints` proves that `g : U → G` factors
through `j` precisely when every algebraically closed point of `U` does. For each
point of `U`, the proof uses an algebraic closure of its residue field. The
assumed factorization there puts the image of that point in the closed subgroup's
underlying range. The pullback of `j` along `g` is therefore a surjective closed
immersion into the reduced scheme `U`, hence is an isomorphism. Its inverse gives
the required factorization over the base. This argument needs no finite-type,
smoothness, connectedness, or reductivity hypotheses.

`hasReducedPointIntersection_iff_geometricPoints` applies that membership
criterion to each of three closed subgroups. The forward implication specializes
the reduced-test property to spectra of algebraically closed fields; the reverse
implication restricts points to all geometric points and then applies the
criterion.

`IsRootSubsetGroup.range_le_of_subset` follows from the defining closed
minimality: a group generated by a larger root set contains all generators for
the smaller set. `IsRootSubsetGroup.range_inter_le` applies this twice to obtain
`G(S ∩ X) ≤ G(S) ∩ G(X)` on all test schemes, without root-set closedness or
convexity hypotheses. The difficult opposite inclusion for closed root subsets
with convex overlap remains a separate obligation.

The definitions and lemmas here are derived directly from the published
`SubgroupFunctor`, `IsRootSubsetGroup`, `HasReducedPointIntersection`, the
residue-field point construction, scheme pullbacks, and Mathlib's fact that a
surjective closed immersion into a reduced scheme is an isomorphism. Their
provenance is internal; no new external theorem is asserted.
