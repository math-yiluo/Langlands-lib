# A type A₂ subgroup in the quadratic splitting argument for E₆

`TypeE6QuadraticSplitTypeA2Statement` concerns a simply connected anisotropic E₆ group over an infinite field that splits over a quadratic Galois extension. It gives an actual simply connected closed subgroup over the ground field. The subgroup has a split pinning with numbered Cartan matrix of type A₂ over that same quadratic extension.

Choose a ground-field maximal torus that splits over the extension, and transport the numbered E₆ base to a split pinning with that torus. A dominant cocharacter pairs to zero with the adjacent vertices `0` and `2` and positively with the other four. Descend the centralizer of that cocharacter, using the fact that negation preserves it. The descended centralizer is reductive by field extension reflection. Its actual derived subgroup, after base change, is the derived subgroup of the split centralizer. The derived Levi pinning identifies its simple roots with the two chosen vertices, and their Cartan entries give A₂. Simple connectedness follows from the derived centralizer theorem over the splitting field and descends to the ground field.

This constructs the subgroup used in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Theorem 6.26, p. 353, and §6.8, Lemma 6.31, p. 380. [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
