# The generated line subgroup after extension to an algebraically closed field

This internal support module, in namespace `Langlands.GroupScheme.MaximalSplitSubgroup`, is
part of the proof of `MaximalSplitSubgroupStatement` (Borel–Tits 1965, Théorème 7.2). It
transports the group `F` of `MaximalSplitSubgroupGenerated` to a field extension `K / k`.

Notation: `bc = algebraBaseMap k K : Spec K ⟶ Spec k`, and `GroupScheme.baseChange bc` is base
change of group schemes along `bc`. `lineBaseChange K x : Ga K ⟶ G_K` is the base change of a
line `x : Ga k ⟶ G`, with source identified with `Ga K` (module `MaximalSplitLineBaseChange`).
For a group scheme `H` over `Spec K`, `rationalPoints H` is the group of its `K`-points
(module `Rational/Characters`), and `rangePts φ` is the subgroup of `K`-points of the range of
a homomorphism `φ` (module `MaximalSplitGeneratedCell`). `rationalPointImage H P` is the set
of points of the underlying space of `H` that come from the `K`-points in `P`.

## Declarations

- `baseChange_smooth_affine_connected`: if a group scheme `M` over `Spec k` is smooth, affine
  and connected, then so is its base change to `K`.
- `rangePts_lineBaseChange`: for `K` algebraically closed, the `K`-points of the range of
  `lineBaseChange K x` are the `K`-points of the range of the base change of `x`.
- `dense_baseChange_lineFamily`: let `K` be algebraically closed, `S` split, `F` smooth and
  affine over `Spec k`, and suppose that `F` is generated, as a subgroup of itself, by
  `lineFamily f x y`: a torus homomorphism `f : S ⟶ F` and lines `x d, y d : Ga k ⟶ F`.
  Then the subgroup of `F_K(K)` generated by the `K`-points of the ranges of all
  `lineBaseChange K (x d)`, all `lineBaseChange K (y d)` and the base change of `f` has dense
  image in the underlying space of `F_K`.

## Proofs

- `baseChange_smooth_affine_connected`. Smoothness and affineness are stable under the
  pullback `pullback.snd M.X.hom bc`. Connectedness is the published
  `connectedSpace_baseChange_of_smooth_affine`.
- `rangePts_lineBaseChange`. This takes `K`-points of `range_lineBaseChange` (module
  `MaximalSplitLineBaseChange`).
- `dense_baseChange_lineFamily`. The sources of the family are smooth, affine and connected
  (`lineFamilySource_smooth_affine_connected`). The published `IsGeneratedByFamily.baseChange`
  shows that `F_K` is generated by the base-changed family. The published
  `LeviGluing.dense_rationalPointImage_closure_of_isGeneratedByFamily` (over an algebraically
  closed field) then gives density of the subgroup generated by the `K`-points of the
  ranges of the members. Finally, the union over `o : Option (Bool × Idx)` of these ranges
  equals the union of the three families in the statement, by `rangePts_lineBaseChange` and
  a case split on `o`.

## Provenance

This is internal glue for the project's proof of Borel–Tits Théorème 7.2, with no
counterpart in the literature. It moves the generated subgroup to an algebraically closed
field. There the density and dynamic arguments of Conrad–Gabber–Prasad,
*Pseudo-reductive groups*, 2nd ed. (2015), Theorem C.2.29, printed pp. 625–628
(PDF pp. 651–654), apply. Nothing here uses Borel–Tits 3.22.
