# Proof: standard parabolics on rational points

Author: Worker 3790.

`Langlands.GroupScheme.standardParabolicPointsStatement_proof :
StandardParabolicPointsStatement.{u}` proves the published statement
`StandardParabolicPointsStatement` of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.StandardParabolicPointsStatement` at an arbitrary universe `u`.
The name `standardParabolicPoints` is already taken by the published subgroup `P_J(k)`.

The statement covers a field `k : Type u`, a connected reductive group scheme `G` over
`Spec k`, a maximal split torus `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G` and a generic cocharacter `ν` of `T`. Write
`Δ = simpleRelativeRoots i ν`, `B` for the rational points of the dynamic minimal parabolic
`Dynamic.parabolic (ν ≫ i)`, `P_J(k) = standardParabolicPoints i ν J` and
`W_J = standardRelativeWeylGroup i ν J`. All four clauses are proved:

1. `g ∈ P_J(k)` exactly when `g = p * n * q` with `p, q ∈ B` and `n` a normalizing point whose
   class lies in `W_J`;
2. a subgroup of `G(k)` contains `B` exactly when it is `P_J(k)` for some `J ⊆ Δ`;
3. each `P_J(k)` is its own normalizer in `G(k)`;
4. for `J, K ⊆ Δ`, an equivalence `W_J \ W / W_K ≃ P_J(k) \ G(k) / P_K(k)` of Mathlib's
   `DoubleCoset.Quotient`s that sends the class of every normalizing point `n` to the double
   coset of `n` itself.

The theorem has no premises. It adds no assumption of characteristic, perfectness, splitness,
quasi-splitness or reducedness of the relative roots, and `Δ` may be empty. It depends only on
the axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

## Proof as implemented

The module has three theorems.

### `standardParabolicOrder_of_relativeRootSystem`

This theorem proves `StandardParabolicOrderStatement` from `RelativeRootSystemStatement`. The
published `standardCocharacterStatement_of_relativeRootSystem_of_signs`, applied to the root
system and to the published `relativeRootSignsDetermineDynamic`, gives a standard cocharacter
for every `J ⊆ Δ`. The published `standardParabolic_order_of_root_comparison` then gives the
order statement. Its other inputs are the published proofs `relativeRootNegation` (the relative
roots are stable under negation) and `relativeRootSignInclusions` (inclusions of dynamic
parabolic and unipotent subgroups follow from inclusions of the root-sign sets). The same
derivation appears inline in the published
`Langlands.Jacquet.standardLeviIntersection_of_relativeRootSystem`.

### `standardParabolicPointsStatement_of_bruhat_and_rankOneFactorization`

This theorem proves `StandardParabolicPointsStatement` from `RationalBruhatStatement` and
`RootRankOneFactorizationStatement`. The steps are as follows.

- The published `relativeRootSystemStatement_of_rootRankOneFactorizationStatement` gives
  `RelativeRootSystemStatement`.
- The root system and rank-one factorization together form
  `RootGeneratedCentralizerStructuralPremises`. Its published projection
  `RootGeneratedCentralizerStructuralPremises.relativeWeylCoxeter` gives
  `RelativeWeylCoxeterStatement`. Then
  `standardRelativeWeylGenerationStatement_of_relativeWeylCoxeter` gives
  `StandardRelativeWeylGenerationStatement`: each `W_J` is generated by the reflections in the
  roots of `J`.
- The published `relativeTitsSystemStatement_of_bruhat_and_rankOneFactorization` gives
  `RelativeTitsSystemStatement`. This is the Tits system `(G(k), P_∅(k), N(k), S)`, with Weyl
  group `N(k)/Z(k)` and `S` the reflections in the simple relative roots.
- The published `standardParabolicTitsRealization_of_statements` combines the last two
  results into `StandardParabolicTitsRealization`. With `S` as above, every `W_J` is generated
  by a subset of `S`, and every subset of `S` generates some `W_J`.
- The published `standardParabolicPointsStatement_of_titsRealization` takes the realization,
  the published endpoint proof `standardParabolicEndpoints` and the order statement from the
  previous theorem, and returns the four clauses. The Markdown of
  `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.StandardParabolicPointsTits` describes that argument. In
  outline: the endpoint statement identifies `B` with `P_∅(k)`, and the order statement gives
  `B ≤ P_J(k)`. Abstract Tits-system theory (`Langlands.Group.TitsSystemBruhat`) gives
  `P_J(k) = B W_J B` and then the classification, the self-normalization and the double-coset
  equivalence with its value on normalizing points.

### `standardParabolicPointsStatement_proof`

The final theorem applies the previous theorem to two published proofs. They are
`rationalBruhat : RationalBruhatStatement`
(`Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.RationalBruhatProof`) and
`rootRankOneFactorization : RootRankOneFactorizationStatement`
(`Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.RootRankOneFactorizationProof`). The second is
derived from the published `RelativeRoot.kernelCentralizerComponentReductive`.

## Provenance

The target is Borel–Tits, *Groupes réductifs* (1965), §5.17, §5.18(i–ii) (printed p.101) and
§5.20 (printed p.102). Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, V.4.5 records
the double-coset version over nonarchimedean local fields. The statement module documents the
sources and the derived parts of the formulation. Classically, Borel–Tits verify the Tits axioms
in §5.15–5.16 and derive the four clauses from them in §5.17–5.20. The proof here follows the
same order, through the published Tits-system, Coxeter and abstract Bruhat results named above.
This module composes published results only. It adds no hypotheses and no new source claim.
