# Parabolics for marked faces of E₆

`TypeE6MarkedParabolicDimensionsStatement` realizes a star-stable set of distinguished vertices as the omitted simple roots of a rational dynamic parabolic of a simply connected group of type E₆. The Lie dimension of the group is \(78\), and the codimension of the parabolic is the signed absolute root count for that set of vertices.

The orbit comparison between the distinguished vertices and the relative simple roots permits a prescribed pairing on every relative simple root. A scaled cocharacter of the maximal split torus has constant positive pairing on the chosen set and zero on its complement. After scalar extension, these are the corresponding values on the absolute simple roots. Integral expansion in the simple roots shows that its negative roots agree with those of the split cocharacter used in the root count. The actual dynamic parabolic commutes with scalar extension, and the Lie dimensions agree by the Lie kernel comparison.

The short-arm end gives codimension \(21\). Its union with the central vertex gives codimension \(30\). These are the two parabolic dimensions used in the outer E₆ case of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Theorem 6.26, printed pp. 355–356.
