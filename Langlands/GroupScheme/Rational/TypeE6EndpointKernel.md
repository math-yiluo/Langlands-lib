# The D₄ kernel of the endpoint-marked E₆ index

For an affine, locally finite-type, simply connected group over a field with
an actual numbered E₆ Tits index marked at the two long-arm endpoints, the
actual anisotropic kernel is absolutely almost simple of type D₄. In the
kernel's numbering the vector vertex and the fork center are fixed by the
full star action. The vector fundamental weight has zero Tits obstruction.
The kernel is simply connected by the theorem on derived groups of
centralizers of split tori.

The kernel comparison identifies its Cartan entries with those of the
ambient subdiagram on vertices `1, 3, 2, 4`, in that order. The edge from
the fork center to each leaf puts all actual simple vertices in one
geometric almost-simple factor. Every factor supplies a simple vertex,
so there is exactly one factor. Cartan symmetry fixes ambient vertices
`1` and `3` and thus fixes the two nonfork vertices of the kernel.

The ambient fundamental weight at vertex `1` is the integral sum of simple
roots with coefficients `(1, 2, 2, 3, 2, 1)`. Its simple-coroot pairings
identify it with that weight. The full star action fixes it, and the Tits
obstruction vanishes on the star-fixed part of the integral root span.
Restriction carries the weight to the vector fundamental weight of the
actual kernel and preserves the obstruction.

The zero subdiagram is the one in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic
Groups and Number Theory*, §6.8, diagrams (6.53) and (6.57). The Tits
obstruction calculation explains the behavior of the vector weight in
that diagram.
