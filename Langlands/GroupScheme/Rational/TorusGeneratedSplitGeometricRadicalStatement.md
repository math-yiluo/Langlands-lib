# The split geometric radical of a torus-generated solvable group

Worker 1333. `TorusGeneratedSplitGeometricRadicalStatement.{u}` states that
every smooth connected affine solvable actual group scheme over every field
`k : Type u` which is generated by its ground-field tori has a represented
geometric unipotent radical that is split over `k`. The existing
`IsSolvableGroupScheme` and `IsTorusGenerated` predicates are unchanged. Finite
fields, imperfect fields, and all characteristics are retained.

`IsTorusGenerated G` means that the existing fixed `closedTorusEnvelope G`
equals the full subgroup functor. Thus generation is least closed-subgroup
generation on all relative test schemes. It is not generation of `G(k)` as an
abstract group.

The conclusion supplies an actual group scheme `U` and homomorphism
`i : U ⟶ G`. The existing
`LeviSubgroup.IsGeometricUnipotentRadical i` requires both
`IsUnipotentRadical i` and `IsUnipotentRadical` for the actual base-changed
homomorphism over `AlgebraicClosure k`. Each radical predicate includes
closedness, smoothness, connectedness, unipotence, normality on every test,
and greatestness among all smooth connected unipotent normal actual closed
subgroups over its field. The algebraic-closure condition is part of this
target; merely providing a ground-field radical does not meet it.

The additional conclusion `RelativeRoot.IsSplitUnipotent U` uses the existing
finite-series definition from `Rational.RootSubgroupStructureStatement`.
It witnesses a finite normal series with smooth connected closed terms and
represented fppf quotients isomorphic on every test to the one-dimensional
additive group. The zero-length case is the trivial group. No splitting field
or rational-point quotient is substituted for this ground-field series.

The original source is Springer, *Linear Algebraic Groups*, second edition
(1998), Theorem 14.4.1(iv), printed p.249 / PDF p.263, registered as
`springer-1998-linear-algebraic-groups`. The standing assumption at the start
of section 14.4, printed p.248 / PDF p.262, is a connected solvable group over
the ground field. Part (iv) says that if it is generated by its ground-field
tori, its geometric unipotent radical is defined over that field and is split
there. The Lean conclusion expresses that descent by a ground-field inclusion
whose actual algebraic-closure base change is the radical, retaining
greatestness on both fields. The split-series predicate expresses the source's
splitness condition without altering it to splitness after extension.

This is precisely the structural input invoked after the envelope reduction
in the proof of Theorem 14.4.3, printed p.250 / PDF p.264. This file is a
proposition target only. It supplies neither a radical existence proof nor
the remaining vector-subgroup, quotient, or rational conjugacy arguments.
