# Finiteness of rational conjugacy classes of maximal tori in the general linear group

For a characteristic-zero nonarchimedean local field \(F\) and any
matrix size \(n\), there is a finite family of maximal \(F\)-tori in
\(\mathrm{GL}_n\) meeting every conjugacy class under \(\mathrm{GL}_n(F)\).
The predicate `HasFiniteMaximalTorusConjugacyClasses` uses the actual
embedded group schemes. Rational conjugacy consists of a rational
conjugating element and a compatible isomorphism between the two tori.

This is the specialization of Renard's assertion for connected reductive
groups to the standard general linear group scheme. See David Renard,
*Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, French edition dated
9 July 2026, § V.2.1, printed p. 104 (PDF p. 110).
