# Isotropy of higher-rank skew-hermitian quaternion forms

`NumberFieldHigherRankSkewIsotropyStatement` concerns an invertible skew Gram matrix for canonical conjugation on a quaternion division algebra over a number field. The quaternionic rank is at least four. At every completion an isotropic row with an invertible coordinate is required. The conclusion is a global nonzero isotropic row for the original matrix.

Choose a nondegenerate quaternion line. The projective complement theorem gives an orthogonal vector whose value is a scalar multiple of the first line's value. If this vector is isotropic, it suffices. Otherwise the two lines extend to an orthogonal division-algebra basis. The resulting binary block has nonzero proportional diagonal values. At a completion, the original local row has a partner with pairing one. Transporting these two vectors through the basis change gives the normalized isotropic row used to find opposite invertible values of the binary block and its complement.

The binary block represents every skew value at all but finitely many finite places. Approximation at the other places gives a nonzero value of the complement whose opposite is represented by the binary block over every completion. Binary local-to-global representation gives the global binary row. Combining the rows and converting to the original basis yields a nonzero isotropic vector. This is the rank-at-least-four part of Springer's isotropy argument with the local hypothesis expressed in the original Gram coordinates. [Kneser, *Lectures on Galois cohomology of classical groups*, Springer appendix, §3 Theorem 5.3.3(ii), pp. 110–112](https://www.math.tifr.res.in/~publ/ln/tifr47.pdf)
