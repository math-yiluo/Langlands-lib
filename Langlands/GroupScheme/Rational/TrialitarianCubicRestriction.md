# Restriction of a quasi-split D4 cocycle to the diagram field

The pointwise fixer of the diagram field is the full star kernel. Thus the actual transported index has trivial star action. A quasi-split group over this field has a split maximal torus, by the quasi-split marking and character-lattice splitting theorem. In the totally imaginary case the vector vertex is distinguished. The classical type D vector-vertex Hasse kernel applies to finite Galois refinements of the scalar-extended cover, and triviality reflects to the full pullback.

For a transitive even star action the diagram field is cyclic cubic. This is the scalar-extension step in the anisotropic case of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed pp. 371–372.
