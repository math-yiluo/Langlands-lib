# Descent of a Levi subgroup from a signed based action

For any subset of the simple roots, a cocharacter of the split
maximal torus can be chosen to pair to zero on that subset and to
one common positive integer on its complement. This follows from
independence of the rationalized simple roots and clearing
denominators in a dual functional.

If the based Galois action preserves these simple-root pairings,
it preserves the pairing with every root by taking integral root
sums. The ordinary action can also negate all values. In both
cases the zero-root support is preserved. The dynamic centralizer
is determined by this support, so its actual closed embedding
descends by Galois descent and is connected reductive.

The descended Levi subgroups supply the two derived groups in the
trialitarian opposite-Borel decomposition of Platonov and Rapinchuk,
*Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed p. 374.

For an arbitrary cocharacter, preservation of its entire zero-root set by the ordinary Galois action is enough for invariance of the centralizer. The descended centralizer construction applies to any Galois-invariant centralizer. The signed based action gives one way to verify this hypothesis.
