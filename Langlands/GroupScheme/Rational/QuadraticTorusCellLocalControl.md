# Archimedean control of the middle coordinate in an open cell

The evaluation topology makes an open immersion of affine schemes an
open embedding on points over a nontrivially normed field. Membership in
the image of a scheme open immersion on field points reflects along
extension of the coefficient field. The proof uses surjectivity on the
underlying point sets of the test spectra and the universal property of
the open immersion.

The pullback adjunction and coefficient extension give the same map on
the actual test spectra as the ring-point comparison. When the
coefficient field is topologically isomorphic to the complex field, the
base change of the torus is split. Its open neighbourhood of coboundaries
among norm-one points transfers back to the original torus coefficient
points by the continuous multiplicative pullback adjunction.

Take the inverse image under the continuous local twisting map of the
open part of the cell whose middle coordinate is in this torus
neighbourhood. It contains a local cochain trivializing the cocycle,
because the twisted value of that cochain is the identity. The
quadratic cocycle equation places the middle coefficient of a
corresponding torus cocycle among the norm-one points. Its local
coboundary equation implies triviality on the whole pulled-back cover.
This is the local open-neighbourhood step of Platonov and Rapinchuk,
*Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), Lemma 6.28, printed p. 370.
