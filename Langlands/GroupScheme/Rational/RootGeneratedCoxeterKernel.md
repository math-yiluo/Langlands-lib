# The actual normalizer kernel for root-generated Coxeter lifts

Author: Worker 1746.

This internal supporting unit specializes the published constructions for the
actual root groups and normalizer to the group-theoretic Coxeter quotient-kernel
argument. It does not prove `RootGeneratedCentralizerRankOneStatement`.

For any field `k : Type u`, commutative group scheme `S`, affine group scheme
`G`, and monomorphism `i : S.toGrp ⟶ G`, set `H0` to be
`RelativeRoot.rankOneCentralizerGeneratedPoints i`. The abbreviation
`rankOneCentralizerNormalizerPoints i` views `H0` inside the actual rational
normalizer `N = normalizerPoints i`. Its normality was proved in
`RootGeneratedCentralizerSubgroup`; its containment in the centralizer ensures
that this restriction does not discard any points of `H0`.

For an arbitrary family `m : B → N`, `rankOneLiftNormalizerPoints i m` is the
subgroup of `N` generated by `H0` and the `m a`. The definition
`rankOneLiftPoints i m` maps that subgroup along the inclusion into all rational
points. There is no claim here that the family exists or generates all
root-generated points.

The first two lemmas concern a single actual opposite-root generated group.
If `a` is a relative root and two normalizing elements of that group have the
same relative Weyl class, their quotient lies both in that group and in the
whole-torus centralizer. It is therefore one of the generating intersections
for `H0`, and the elements agree modulo `H0`. Similarly, if such an element
has involutive Weyl class, its square lies in the same intersection and hence
in `H0`. These statements use the literal quotient definition of the relative
Weyl group and ordinary subgroup closure. They need no reductivity or root
system realization.

For the kernel theorem, suppose a Coxeter system `cs` on the actual relative
Weyl group is given, the images of `m a` are its simple reflections, and the
images of the lifts modulo `H0` satisfy every Coxeter relation. The quotient
projection `q : N → N/Z` kills `H0`. The internal theorem
`Langlands.Group.CoxeterQuotient.generatedBy_inf_ker_eq` then identifies the
intersection of the generated subgroup with `ker q` as exactly `H0`.
The kernel of this actual quotient is the actual centralizer in `N`. This
proves `rankOneLiftNormalizerPoints_inf_centralizer_eq`. Unpacking membership
in the image along `N → G(k)` gives
`centralizer_inf_rankOneLiftPoints_le`, the corresponding ambient inclusion.

The provenance is internal: elementary facts about the actual opposite-root
subgroups, the published actual normalizer/centralizer quotient, normality of
`H0`, and the Coxeter universal property. The Coxeter system, the simple
representatives and their relations modulo `H0` remain explicit inputs. In
particular, relations that hold only modulo the full centralizer do not suffice
for this theorem. Deriving all these inputs, and the covering of the subgroup
generated by actual root groups, remains necessary for the unconditional target.
