# The closed envelope of all ground-field tori

Worker 1333. This internal construction provides the first reduction in the
proof route for rational conjugacy of full maximal tori. It retains the existing
`IsTorus`, `IsClosedSubgroup`, `IsMaximalTorus` and `SubgroupConjugate` predicates
unchanged. No splitness, perfection, characteristic or infinitude restriction
is made, and the ground field lies in an arbitrary universe.

`SubgroupFunctor.ContainsTori F` means that the all-test range of every closed
ground-field torus inclusion is contained in `F`. `closedTorusEnvelope G` is
the intersection of the ranges of all actual closed subgroup inclusions whose
ranges have this property. Its points form subgroups and restrict along every
test-scheme morphism. This definition does not assume representability of the
intersection. It includes every ground-field torus and is contained in every
competing closed subgroup. It is an intersection of closed represented subgroup
functors; no claim identifies it with the pointwise abstract subgroup generated
by torus-valued points.

`IsTorusGenerated G` means that this closed envelope is the whole subgroup
functor. `TorusGeneratedEnvelope G` is actual representing data: a group scheme,
its closed inclusion into `G`, and its representation of that fixed functor.
The structure asserts neither existence nor smoothness or connectedness of
its group.

Given such a representative `D`, `torusFactor` constructs the unique actual
group-scheme factor of a closed torus inclusion through `D.inclusion`, using
the existing represented-subgroup factorization. The factor remains closed.
An ambient maximal torus remains maximal inside `D.group` by the existing
`IsMaximalTorus.of_comp` theorem. `minimalHom` constructs the unique morphism
from `D.group` into any competing closed subgroup containing the tori; two
representatives are consequently isomorphic compatibly with their inclusions.

`isTorusGenerated` proves that `D.group` is itself torus generated. For a closed
subgroup of `D.group` containing all its tori, compose with `D.inclusion`.
Every ambient torus factors through `D.group` as a closed torus, so that composite
contains all ambient tori. Minimality constructs a retraction of the closed
subgroup inclusion inside `D.group`. Cancellation against `D.inclusion` proves
that every point of `D.group`, on every test scheme, lies in that subgroup.
The intersection defining its torus envelope is therefore the whole functor.

`maximalToriAreConjugate` transfers rational conjugacy of maximal tori from
`D.group` to `G`. Both ambient tori factor as maximal tori in the envelope;
apply its supplied conjugacy and postcompose the conjugacy by the actual
inclusion. This transports the rational point and source isomorphism, giving the
unchanged `SubgroupConjugate` conclusion.

The final elementary lemmas prove that affineness is inherited by a represented
closed envelope and that solvability is inherited along every monomorphism of
group schemes. For solvability, pull back the monomorphism to the algebraic
closure and apply the abstract-group solvability theorem to its injective map
on geometric rational points.

The construction is derived from the published subgroup-functor,
represented-subgroup and maximal-torus transport interfaces. Its intended
source route is Springer, *Linear Algebraic Groups*, second edition (1998),
Theorem 14.4.3, first sentence of proof, printed p.250 / PDF p.264, which passes
to the subgroup generated by the ground-field tori; Theorem 14.4.1(iv), printed
p.249 / PDF p.263, then supplies its split geometric unipotent radical.
This module proves the elementary reduction after an actual representative is
supplied. Smooth connected representability of the fixed envelope, the
split-unipotent-radical theorem, and the remaining full conjugacy argument are
separate substantial proof obligations.
