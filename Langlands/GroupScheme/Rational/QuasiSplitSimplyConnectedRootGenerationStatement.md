# Rational root generation for quasi-split simply connected groups

Author: Worker 185.

`Langlands.GroupScheme.QuasiSplitSimplyConnectedRootGenerationStatement.{u}`
is a proposition definition. It asserts that, over any field `k : Type u`,
the rational points of a quasi-split simply connected group `G` are generated
by the rational points of its actual relative-root subgroup functors at any
supplied maximal split torus `i : S.toGrp ⟶ G`:

```text
relativeRootGeneratedSubgroup i = ⊤.
```

Here `G` has an affine structure morphism, `IsSimplyConnected G` includes
semisimplicity, and `IsQuasiSplit G` supplies a field-defined geometric Borel
subgroup. These are all the hypotheses. There is no restriction on the
characteristic, cardinality or perfectness of the field, and no valuation
or topology. The group need not be absolutely almost simple or simple over
`k`. The trivial group and relative rank zero are included. No generation
property or Kneser–Tits conclusion is assumed.

The existing `relativeRootGeneratedSubgroup` is the abstract subgroup
closure of the union of the carriers
`(RelativeRoot.subgroup i a).carrier (Over.mk (𝟙 _))`, for
`a ∈ relativeRoots i`. Thus the conclusion concerns the actual rational
points and root subgroup functors, rather than an abstract group with
root-group axioms.

## Source and derived formulation

V. Platonov and A. Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994),
§7.2, printed p. 406 / PDF p. 416, recalls the equality `G(k)^+ = G(k)`
for simply connected quasi-split groups, attributing it to Steinberg. On
that page `G(k)^+` is defined using rational points of unipotent radicals
of parabolic `k`-subgroups. This is the quasi-split case, over arbitrary
fields; the subsequent local-field Kneser–Tits theorem is a different
result and is not used to justify this statement. The source is registered
as `platonov-rapinchuk-1994-algebraic-groups-number-theory`.

This Lean statement is a derived formulation of that equality. The
source discussion is phrased for simple groups. Passage to a semisimple
simply connected group uses its product decomposition into `k`-simple
simply connected factors, each of which is quasi-split. Rational points
and the subgroups generated by the relative root groups respect that
product. A proof must establish this passage or use a proof that applies
directly to arbitrary semisimple simply connected groups.

The root-group comparison is the one detailed in
[RootGeneratedCentralizerRankOneStatement.md](RootGeneratedCentralizerRankOneStatement.md).
In particular, for an indivisible relative root the Library subgroup
is the usual positive-ray relative root group. For a divisible root its
Library subgroup lies in the subgroup for the associated indivisible
root, and every indivisible-root subgroup occurs among the generators.
No equality of the divisible-root subgroup with an exact-weight root
subgroup is asserted. Equivalently, one may use the ordered factorization
of each minimal-parabolic unipotent radical into indivisible relative
root groups and rational conjugacy of minimal parabolics to identify
the generated subgroup with the source's `G(k)^+`.

This file does not prove root generation or any of those comparison
ingredients. Its purpose is to expose the field-general algebraic
prerequisite independently of building theory. Combined with the
published rank-one generation theorem for the root-generated part of a
centralizer, it supplies the centralizer generation needed by the
simply connected normalizer argument over a maximal unramified field.
