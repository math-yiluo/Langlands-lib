# Exact absolute generation for a nonmultipliable relative root

`Langlands.GroupScheme.RelativeRootGroupExactGenerationStatement` concerns a
connected reductive group \(G\) over any field \(k\), a maximal \(k\)-split
torus \(S\), a maximal torus \(T\) containing \(S\), and a field extension
\(K/k\) splitting \(T\). Let \(a\) be a relative root for \(S\) such that
\(2a\) is absent from the relative roots. The base change of the actual
relative root group \(U_{(a)}\) to \(K\) is the smallest closed subgroup
containing the absolute root groups \(U_\beta\) for which
\(\beta|_{S_K}=a_K\). The inclusion of each generator and the minimality
among closed subgroups are stated as subgroup-functor inequalities, on
all test schemes over \(K\).

Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017), §25.19, printed p. 549 (PDF p. 565),
uses absolute roots restricting exactly to \(a\). Borel–Tits,
*Groupes réductifs* (1965), §§3.8(ii), 3.13 and 5.2, printed pp. 76, 79
and 96 (PDF pp. 23, 26 and 43), uses the positive integral multiples
of \(a\). The two generating families coincide for a nonmultipliable
root, since the only possible positive integral multiples in the
relative root system are \(a\) and \(2a\). The hypothesis applies to
all roots of a reduced relative root system and to nonmultipliable
roots of a nonreduced system. The field extension may be any extension
splitting the specified maximal torus.
