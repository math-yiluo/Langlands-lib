# The number-field Hasse kernel for absolutely almost-simple simply connected groups

`NumberFieldAbsolutelyAlmostSimpleSimplyConnectedGaloisHasseKernelStatement` is the assertion that an everywhere locally trivial natural cocycle on a finite Galois field cover of a number field is globally trivial when its coefficient group is simply connected and absolutely almost-simple. Local triviality uses the actual pullback of the cover at each completion. The assertion is the arithmetic premise in the accompanying reduction, using the specialization of the simply connected kernel to absolutely almost-simple groups.

`numberFieldAlmostSimpleSimplyConnectedGaloisHasseKernel_of_absolute` applies the published Weil-restriction theorem to an almost-simple simply connected group. The finite coefficient extension is itself a number field. The Weil cocycle transfer gives a locally trivial cocycle on a finite Galois cover of this number field, with absolutely almost-simple simply connected coefficients. A trivialization from the arithmetic premise is reflected back to the original cocycle through the transfer and the group isomorphism.

`numberFieldSimplyConnectedGaloisHasseKernel_of_absolute` composes this implication with the finite product reduction to handle every simply connected group.
