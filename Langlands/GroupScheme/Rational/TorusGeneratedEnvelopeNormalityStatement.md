# Normality of the fixed closed torus envelope

Worker 1436. `TorusGeneratedEnvelopeNormalityStatement.{u}` asserts that, over
every field `k : Type u`, any actual representative `D` of the fixed closed
torus envelope of a smooth connected affine group scheme `G` has normal image
in `G`. There is no reductivity, solvability, splitness, perfection, infinitude,
or characteristic restriction.

The existing `TorusGeneratedEnvelope G` includes an actual group scheme, an
actual closed inclusion into `G`, and representation of precisely the existing
`closedTorusEnvelope G`. That envelope is the intersection of the all-test
ranges of actual closed subgroups containing every closed ground-field torus.
No new generated-subgroup model is introduced. Normality is the unchanged
`SubgroupFunctor.IsNormal`: for every scheme `T` over `Spec k`, every
`g : T ⟶ G.X`, and every point `x` in the range of `D.inclusion` on `T`, the
point `g * x * g⁻¹` lies in that same range. The claim is about all relative
scheme tests, including nonreduced ones.

The source is Conrad--Gabber--Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive groups*, second edition
(2015), Proposition A.2.11, printed pp.477--480 / one-based PDF pp.503--506,
registered as `conrad-gabber-prasad-2015-pseudo-reductive`. The proposition
asserts normality in `G` of its smooth connected subgroup `G_t` generated by
the ground-field tori. Its proof obtains normality from compatibility of this
subgroup with extension of the ground field, whose proof occupies the rest
of the proposition.

To prove the present target, the source subgroup must be identified with the
fixed envelope by its least closed-subgroup property. The existing uniqueness
of representatives then identifies any supplied `D` with that source subgroup,
compatibly with its inclusion into `G`. For this reason no separate smoothness
or connectedness premise on `D.group` is needed. Smooth connected
representability and this structural identification remain proof obligations;
this file states no existence theorem and assumes no theorem-valued premise.

This is a focused prerequisite target for the infinite-field reduction in the
pseudo-parabolic rational-lifting argument of CGP Lemma C.2.1, printed
pp.592--593 / PDF pp.618--619. It supplies neither a proof of normality nor the
rational-lifting theorem. Rational density, the quotient by the envelope, and
compatibility with field extension are separate assertions.
