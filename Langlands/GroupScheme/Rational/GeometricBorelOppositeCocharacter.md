# Opposite dynamic Borels over the original field

A geometric Borel containing a specified maximal torus is the dynamic
parabolic of a cocharacter of that torus over the original field.
In a finite Galois splitting field of the torus, the split Borel theorem
gives a regular cocharacter. The fixed-torus descent theorem gives an
integral cocharacter over the original field with the same parabolic.
Equality of the two parabolic images detects the sign of the pairing at
each root and its negative, so the descended cocharacter is still regular
after scalar extension. Its centralizer over the splitting field is the
maximal torus, and equality descends. The negated cocharacter defines a
Borel over the algebraic closure, so it gives an actual opposite geometric
Borel over the original field.

The construction supplies the opposite open cell used in Platonov and
Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), Lemma 6.28,
printed pp. 369–370.
