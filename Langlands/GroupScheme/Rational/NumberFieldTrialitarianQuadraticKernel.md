# The trialitarian Hasse kernel on a quadratic quasi-splitting cover

`Cocycle.isTrivial_of_trialitarian_quadratic_quasiSplit` concerns a locally trivial cocycle in a simply connected trialitarian group over a number field. It assumes a totally imaginary quadratic extension over which the group is quasi-split and the cocycle is trivial on the pulled-back cover.

Relative cocycle descent gives a locally trivial representative on the quadratic cover. The opposite-Borel reduction lifts it to a torus cocycle trivial at the real places. The derived groups of the central and leaf Levi faces give the two torus projections. Local vanishing for simply connected groups supplies triviality at the remaining places. Reduced-norm locality and Weil cocycle transfer trivialize the leaf class over the ground field. The central class is transported to a form of the central group by twisting. Reduced-norm locality for that form gives triviality of the product.

The componentwise Hasse argument appears in Platonov and Rapinchuk,
*Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed pp. 374–375.
The opposite-Borel lift is Lemma 6.28 on printed pp. 369–370.
The signed diagram action allows any quadratic field satisfying the stated hypotheses.
