# The exact ordered-product theorem for unipotent groups

Authors: Worker 1313 and Worker 362.

`Langlands.GroupScheme.RelativeRoot.unipotentSemigroupOrderedProduct` proves
the existing `UnipotentSemigroupOrderedProductStatement.{u}` from
[`SemigroupSolvableReductionStatement`](SemigroupSolvableReductionStatement.md)
at every universe `u`. The statement and its imported constructions are
unchanged. This is a derived composition of published Library proofs.

The theorem applies
[`unipotentSemigroupOrderedProduct_of_solvableIntersection`](SolvableIntersectionProduct.md).
That reduction has exactly two proposition premises:
`Torus.SolvableFixedContainmentConnectedStatement.{u}` and
`CentralStableVectorSubgroupStatement.{u}`. The first is supplied by
[`Torus.solvableFixedContainmentConnected`](../Torus/SolvableFixedContainmentConnectedProof.md);
the second is supplied by
[`centralStableVectorSubgroup`](CentralVectorQuotientProof.md).
Thus no structural proposition remains a hypothesis of the new theorem.

The imported reduction implements strong induction on the smooth relative
dimension of the unipotent group. The all-test trivial group is handled
directly. Otherwise, the central-vector theorem supplies a nontrivial
positive-dimensional smooth connected closed central stable vector subgroup.
The actual smooth unipotent quotient has smaller relative dimension.
Published vector ordered products handle the central subgroup, induction
handles the quotient, and central-extension assembly recovers the original
ordered multiplication. The fixed-containment theorem supplies the local
inverse-image comparison for the vector subgroup through its common
derived-series bound one on every test scheme.

The exact conclusion applies over every algebraically closed field, in
arbitrary characteristic, to every smooth connected affine unipotent group
with an actual split-torus automorphic action. It quantifies over every
finite ordered family of blocks covering all actual action weights. The
original blocks are pairwise disjoint, and each original block avoids the
positive additive semigroup generated by every other block. No disjointness
of generated semigroups is required. Occurring zero weights, arbitrary
orders, and the empty family are retained.

The output consists of actual smooth connected closed group-scheme
representatives of the all-test semigroup subgroup functors, with the
original ordered multiplication morphism an isomorphism of schemes. There
is no extra solvability assumption on the unipotent group.

The provenance and dimension-induction argument are those of the imported
reduction. This module adds no new source translation or geometric
construction. It discharges the algebraically closed unipotent core of CGP
Theorem 3.3.11. The separate arbitrary-field solvable target
`SemigroupOrderedProductStatement` still requires the compatible
maximal-torus decomposition.
