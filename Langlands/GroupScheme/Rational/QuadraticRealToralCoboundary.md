# Torus coboundaries at inert real places

A square root of a quadratic cocycle value commutes with that value. The scheme centralizer on the coefficient field is the base change of the descended torus, so commutation on the upper completion gives a point of the torus there. The local Galois automorphism inverts the square root. The coboundary of the lifted point therefore maps to the given local cocycle value. The closed torus immersion detects this equality, and the identity and the nonidentity automorphism exhaust the local Galois group. The cover isomorphism then gives triviality on the actual real pullback.

This is a derived local step for the toral reduction of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (Academic Press, 1994), Lemma 6.28, pp. 369–370. It uses the scheme centralizer and the square-root identity to obtain the trivial torus restriction.
