# Rational maximal-torus conjugacy from quotient existence alone

Worker 1333.

This internal companion to `SolvableMaximalTorusStructuralAssembly` supplies
the published theorem `unipotentSplitTorusDomination.{u}` to the existing
retained-quotient and conjugacy constructions. The stable-vector and
torus-action linearization inputs are also supplied by their published
unconditional theorems. Only quotient existence remains an explicit
structural input.

For a smooth connected affine solvable group scheme `G` over any field
`k : Type u`, generated by its ground-field tori,
`IsTorusGenerated.splitGeometricRadicalTorusQuotientOfDominatedQuotient`
constructs `SplitGeometricRadicalTorusQuotient G`. Its sole theorem-valued
input is `SplitTorusDominatedSolvableQuotientStatement.{u}`. The output stores
an actual radical and inclusion over `k`, its geometric radical predicate,
a split-unipotent series over `k`, and an actual smooth torus quotient of
that same inclusion. The geometric predicate retains the actual
algebraic-closure comparison. The quotient retains its projection and
the full represented fppf kernel identity on every relative test scheme.

The implementation specializes
`IsTorusGenerated.splitGeometricRadicalTorusQuotientOfStructuralStatements`
with `unipotentSplitTorusDomination` and
`RelativeRoot.splitUnipotentStableVectorSubgroup`. The existing constructor
chooses finite Galois split-torus domination, applies the supplied initial
quotient statement, proves splitness of the specified full kernel, and
descends the radical together with its quotient. This companion retains
the resulting data without choosing another quotient.

`IsTorusGenerated.splitGeometricRadicalTorusQuotientOfTorusQuotient`
constructs the same type of data from
`SolvableTorusQuotientStatement.{u}` as its sole theorem-valued input.
It applies the existing
`splitTorusDominatedSolvableQuotient_of_solvableTorusQuotient` reduction
before the preceding constructor. Although this input asserts quotient
existence over algebraically closed fields, the returned data are over
the original arbitrary field.

The companion theorem
`solvableMaximalTorusConjugacy_of_dominatedQuotient` concludes the unchanged
published `SolvableMaximalTorusConjugacyStatement.{u}` from the dominated
quotient statement alone. It supplies the proved domination-splitness
theorem to the corresponding published structural assembly. That assembly
chooses the smooth torus-generated envelope, constructs its retained
radical and quotient, obtains a section and conjugacy there, and transports
the actual rational conjugacy to the original group.

`solvableMaximalTorusConjugacy_of_torusQuotient` concludes that same target
from the absolute quotient statement alone. It specializes the existing
absolute-quotient and domination-splitness assembly with the same proved
splitness theorem. No geometric-radical, stable-vector, linearization or
splitness statement remains a further input to either companion theorem.

All fields and characteristics, finite and imperfect fields, arbitrary
nonsplit maximal tori, and universe `u` are retained. The existing torus,
maximality, solvability and rational subgroup-conjugacy predicates are
unchanged. Conjugacy therefore still supplies an actual rational point and
an isomorphism of the source tori. Neither maximal-split-torus conjugacy
family is used.

The quotient-existence inputs remain hypotheses. This module does not
prove either quotient statement or the unconditional host target. Its
provenance is the explicit composition of the named published
constructions and theorems; it asserts no additional external result.
