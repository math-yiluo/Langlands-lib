# Hasse kernel in outer type A with distinguished endpoints

`NumberFieldOuterTypeAEndpointHasseStatement` starts with a simply connected absolutely almost-simple affine group of locally finite type over a number field. Its numbered Cartan matrix has type A, the full star image is the identity and reversal, and both endpoints are distinguished. The theorem gives the Hasse kernel on every original finite Galois field cover.

The published endpoint classification supplies all-test Hermitian matrix coordinates over the quadratic extension. The full image and naturality statements construct the actual Hermitian matrix presentation. The special unitary matrix Hasse theorem applies to it directly. The arithmetic step is the Hermitian matrix case of Kneser, *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups*, §5.5.
