# The Weyl group of a standard Levi root subset

This internal supporting module proves `Langlands.GroupScheme.rootSubsetWeylGroup_standardLevi`, the Weyl equality in `StandardParabolicRootSubsetGroupStatement`. Its field has arbitrary universe `u`; the ambient set may be the full relative roots or the indivisible relative roots.

The proof uses the published actual relative Weyl Coxeter realization and integral reflection existence theorem. It chooses the finite set of simple-root labels corresponding to Θ and a standard cocharacter ζ for this face. The published pairing-stabilizer theorem identifies the stabilizer of pairing with ζ with the Coxeter standard subgroup, and the realization identifies that subgroup with the actual standard Levi Weyl subgroup.

For the forward inclusion, the proof first checks that either ambient root set is stable under negation, using the integral reflection data. The selected simple roots lie in either ambient set; their indivisibility follows from the published linear independence of the simple roots. The set of ambient roots with zero ζ-pairing is closed and contains Θ and −Θ. Minimality of root closure therefore implies zero pairing for every root of the standard Levi root subset. The formula in `IsRootReflection` shows that every generating reflection preserves pairing with ζ on every character, so it belongs to the identified stabilizer.

For the reverse inclusion, the standard subgroup is generated by its labelled simple reflections. The integral reflection representative of each selected simple root agrees with its Coxeter simple reflection by the published uniqueness theorem for relative reflections. Its integral formula supplies `IsRootReflection`; the selected root belongs to the Levi root closure by the generator inclusion. Thus each generator lies in `rootSubsetWeylGroup`.

The auxiliary lemmas record ambient negation stability, zero pairing on the Levi closure, pairing invariance of a zero-pairing root reflection, and integral realization of a simple reflection. This is an internal derivation from the published definitions and proofs imported in the Lean module. It uses no unpublished root-triple statement and proves no subgroup-range equality; those are the other conjuncts of the parent target.
