# A factor outside type A in the cubic splitting argument for E₆

`TypeE6CubicSplitNonTypeAFactorStatement` starts with a simply connected anisotropic affine group of locally finite type in characteristic zero that has a split E₆ pinning over a cubic Galois extension. It produces a simply connected absolutely almost-simple anisotropic group of absolute rank at most five over a finite separable extension of the ground field. Its actual Cartan matrix has no type A numbering.

The reductive group obtained from the parabolic orbit intersection has dimension at least thirty. Over a separable closure, its maximal torus has dimension at most six by closed embedding into E₆. Its derived group embeds in split D₅, so the total rank of its derived root datum is at most five. A type A₅ component would exhaust that datum, contrary to the closed-subgroup exclusion. If all components have type A, the small-component root count gives at most twenty-two roots. The derived subgroup retains all roots of the reductive group, by the derived Levi pinning for the zero cocharacter. The Lie dimension formula would then give dimension at most twenty-eight.

Choose a Cartan component of the derived group outside type A. The component realization theorem gives a simply connected absolutely almost-simple factor over a finite separable extension with the same Cartan entries. Anisotropy passes to the closed derived subgroup and then to the realized factor.

The same factor has no F₄ numbering. Four independent simple roots with F₄ entries would give at least forty-eight roots in the derived group. Its Lie algebra injects into that of simply connected D₅, which has a five-dimensional maximal torus and at most forty roots.

This is the factor and dimension argument of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Theorem 6.26, printed pp. 354–355, and §6.8, Lemma 6.32, printed pp. 380–381. [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
