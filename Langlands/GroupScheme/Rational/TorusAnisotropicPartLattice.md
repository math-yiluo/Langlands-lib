# Integral invariants for the anisotropic part of a torus

This internal lattice lemma supports the proof of Renard V.2.2,
Remark 2. For an integral representation of a group on a finite free module,
`free_quotient_invariants` proves that the quotient by the invariant submodule
is free. The difference map sends
`x` to the family `g ↦ g(x) − x`; its kernel is precisely the invariant
submodule. The first isomorphism theorem identifies the quotient with a
submodule of a torsion-free module of families. Its range is finitely generated,
being an image of the original finite module, and is free by the finite
torsion-free module theorem over the principal ideal domain `ℤ`.
The separate orbit-sum lemma assumes the group is finite. The orbit sum is invariant
by permutation of the finite indexing group. No division by its order is
used, so this argument imposes no condition on the characteristic of a
field that later realizes the lattice.
