# Independence of the local-field rational-point topology from the linear embedding

`Langlands.GroupScheme.LocalFieldLinearTopologyStatement` concerns the
actual rational points of any finite-type affine group scheme over a
nonarchimedean local field. The field has its given compatible valuation
topology and may have either characteristic. The point group carries
the canonical evaluation topology `rationalPointTopology`.
[Rational-point topology](../../AlgebraicGeometry/PointTopology/RationalPoints.md)

The proposition asserts the existence of a closed linear embedding.
For each such embedding, the actual rational-point map followed by the
matrix point equivalence is a closed topological embedding into the
matrix general linear group. In particular the topology induced from
the matrices agrees with the canonical evaluation topology for every
choice of embedding. The proposition also gives the topological group,
Hausdorff, local compactness and total disconnectedness properties of
that canonical topology.
[Matrix point topology](../../Group/GL/AlgebraicPointTopology.md)

This is the topology comparison recalled in David Renard,
*Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, French edition dated
9 July 2026, V.2.1, printed p. 104 / PDF p. 110. The formulation applies
the comparison to affine group schemes of finite type and includes the
connected reductive case. The registered source ID is
