# Canonical transport of subgroup functors along a tower

The tower comparison reads the base change of a subgroup functor over
an intermediate field as a subgroup functor of the original ambient
group over the top field. The compatible coefficient actions commute
with this comparison. On each test scheme, rewriting membership by
the pullback adjunction gives two points over the intermediate base.
The established comparison for transported points identifies them
after precomposition with the isomorphism between the test schemes.
Subgroup membership is invariant under that precomposition.

Restriction of automorphisms to a normal intermediate field supplies
the compatible square of spectra. This is the all-test form of the
coefficient transport used for the opposite Borels in Platonov and
Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), Lemma 6.28.
