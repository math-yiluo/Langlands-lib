# Regular rational points of maximal tori in the general linear group

`Langlands.GroupScheme.GeneralLinearMaximalTorusRegularPointStatement`
uses any maximal torus embedding \(j:T\to\mathrm{GL}_n\) over an infinite
field \(F\). It asserts the existence of a point of \(T(F)\) whose image
\(\gamma\) has a separable characteristic polynomial in the standard
matrix realization. The same embedding \(j\) is required to represent the
scheme centralizer of \(\gamma\) on all test schemes over \(F\).

The regular locus on a maximal torus is the open condition that the
characteristic polynomial have nonzero discriminant. Over an algebraic
closure, the diagonal torus exhibits this locus by choosing distinct
diagonal entries. Zariski density of rational points of a torus over an
infinite field gives the rational point. Over the algebraic closure a
matrix commuting with the resulting diagonal matrix is diagonal on
every test algebra, since differences of distinct eigenvalues are units.
Descent gives the centralizer comparison over \(F\).

This is a matrix comparison for the maximal tori in David Renard,
*Représentations des groupes réductifs \(p\)-adiques*, 9 July 2026,
§ V.2.1, printed p. 104 (PDF p. 110).
