# Isotropy of quadratic pure twists of split type E₆

Let a simply connected quasi-split group over an infinite field have a numbered E₆ Tits index with trivial star action. Let a cocycle on a quadratic Galois cover give a local comparison with a pure twist, with the cocycle acting by conjugation. The original and twisted groups are affine and locally of finite type. For any Tits index of the twisted group, the proof obtains an E₆ numbering with trivial star action and shows that the twisted group is isotropic.

Passing the values of the cocycle to the adjoint quotient makes the comparison an inner local isomorphism in the star-action comparison theorem. The resulting based equivalence transports the Cartan matrix and the trivial action on simple vertices. Quasi-splitness and trivial star action make the original group split. The local comparison identifies the two groups over the quadratic extension and hence gives a split maximal torus there in the twisted group. The comparison also transfers simple connectedness through field extension. The quadratic opposite-Borel construction supplies a maximal torus over the ground field that splits on the cover. The E₆ Weyl-group obstruction to negation gives isotropy of the twist.

This is the inner E₆ case of the quadratic isotropy input to Theorem 6.29 of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, Lemma 6.31 and its application on printed pp. 380–381 (PDF pp. 390–391). The Lean proof uses the Weyl obstruction for inner E₆. [Chapter 6, Galois cohomology](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711).
