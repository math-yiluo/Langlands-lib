# The Hasse kernel of a scalar special orthogonal group

For a nondegenerate scalar quadratic form of positive dimension over a number field, `NumberFieldSpecialOrthogonalGaloisHasseKernelStatement` concerns the actual determinant-one isometry scheme. A cocycle on a finite Galois field cover is trivial if it is trivial on the full pullback at every completion.

The published quadratic isometry theorem gives the Hasse kernel for the full orthogonal group, so the cocycle has a primitive in that group. The primitive's determinant is either one or minus one, since its matrix preserves the invertible polar Gram matrix. A reflection in a ground-field vector of nonzero value supplies a rational point of determinant minus one. Multiplying the primitive by the inverse of a rational point of the same determinant changes its determinant to one and leaves its coboundary unchanged. The actual norm-one inclusion is the determinant-one condition by the scalar tensor matrix comparison, so the corrected primitive lifts on the original cover.

This is a derived scalar special orthogonal consequence of the quadratic Hasse principle in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.6, pp. 347–349. It supplies the isometry-group comparison in the scalar spin case.
