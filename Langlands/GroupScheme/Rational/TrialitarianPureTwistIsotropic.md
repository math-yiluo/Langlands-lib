# Cohomology of an isotropic pure inner twist in trialitarian type

A cocycle with values in a simply connected group acts on it by inner automorphisms. Factoring conjugation through the adjoint quotient yields a comparison of the original and twisted absolute Cartan matrices and full star actions. In type D4 with transitive leaf action the star orbits are the centre and the three leaves. If an isotropic group has an unmarked vertex, the marked vertices have just one orbit, so the split rank is one. The index theorem forces that orbit to be the centre. The cohomology of its split-torus centralizer then gives triviality of the original cocycle. If every vertex is marked, the twist is quasi-split and the Borel calculation gives the same conclusion.

This proves the isotropic case of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed p. 371, with reduced-norm locality as the arithmetic input.
