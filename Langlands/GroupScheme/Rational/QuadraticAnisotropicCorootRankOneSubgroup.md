# Rank-one subgroups through coroots of anisotropic quadratic tori

Module: `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.QuadraticAnisotropicCorootRankOneSubgroup`.

The module states one proposition,
`QuadraticAnisotropicCorootRankOneSubgroupStatement`, in an arbitrary universe `u`.
It contains no theorem; the statement is not proved here. The module also has a
local instance, `quadraticAnisotropicCorootRankOneSubgroupAffine`, saying that the
base change `G_L` of an affine group scheme is affine over `Spec L`; it is needed to
form `IsRootDatumOf` for the base-changed torus and is not exported.

## The statement

The proposition quantifies over:

- a field `F : Type u` and a group scheme `G` over `Spec F` with
  `IsAffineHom G.X.hom` and `LocallyOfFiniteType G.X.hom`, such that
  `IsSimplyConnected G` (so `G` is semisimple and simply connected);
- a field `L : Type u` with `[Algebra F L]`, `[FiniteDimensional F L]`,
  `[IsGalois F L]` and `Module.finrank F L = 2`; write `σ` for the generator of
  `Gal(L/F)` and `b = algebraBaseMap F L`;
- a commutative group scheme `T` over `Spec F` and `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G` with
  `IsMaximalTorus i`, such that the base change
  `(GroupScheme.baseChange b).obj T.toGrp` is a split torus and
  `IsAnisotropic T.toGrp` holds (exactly the hypotheses on `T` in
  `NumberFieldQuadraticAnisotropicToralKernelStatement`);
- a type `J : Type u` and a Mathlib root datum `P` on the character and
  cocharacter lattices of `T_L = (CommGroupScheme.baseChange b).obj T` with
  `IsRootDatumOf ((GroupScheme.baseChange b).map i) P`, i.e. `P` is a root datum of
  the split maximal torus `i_L : T_L → G_L`;
- an index `j : J`, a character `χ` of `T_L` and an endomorphism `π : T ⟶ T` of the
  commutative group scheme `T` over `F` whose base change is
  `(CommGroupScheme.baseChange b).map π = χ ≫ P.coroot j`, the composite
  `T_L → 𝔾_m → T_L` of `χ` and the coroot `α^∨ = P.coroot j`.

The conclusion: there are a group scheme `D` over `Spec F`, affine and locally of
finite type over the base, a homomorphism `d : D ⟶ G` and a homomorphism
`t : T.toGrp ⟶ D` such that

- `IsClosedSubgroup d` (the underlying morphism is a closed immersion);
- `t ≫ d = CommGroupScheme.groupHom π ≫ i`, i.e. `π ≫ i` factors through `D`;
- `IsSimplyConnected D` and `IsAbsolutelyAlmostSimple D`;
- `D` has Tits index data `x : TitsIndexData D` with `x.HasInnerTypeA 1`.

The last three conditions are the hypotheses on the group in
`NumberFieldInnerTypeASimplyConnectedGaloisHasseKernelStatement` with `n = 1`.

## Why it holds

Since `T` splits over `L` and is anisotropic over `F`, `Gal(L/F)` fixes no nonzero
character of `T_L`; as `σ² = 1`, the character `χ + σ(χ)` is fixed, so `σ` acts on
`X^*(T_L)` as `−1`, and likewise on `X_*(T_L)`. Let `α = P.root j`, and let `β` be
the primitive character spanning the saturated line `ℚα ∩ X^*(T_L)`. Let
`T_α ⊂ T_L` be the kernel of `β`, which is the reduced identity component of
`ker α`: a split subtorus of codimension one. (The scheme-theoretic identity
component of `ker α` can be nonreduced, for example when `α = 2β` in
characteristic `2`, so the reduced one is meant.) Since `σ(β) = −β`, the
subtorus `T_α` is `σ`-stable and descends to an `F`-subtorus of `T`. Its
centralizer `G_α` is reductive of semisimple rank one with roots `±α`, and its
derived group, the subgroup generated by the root subgroups `U_α` and `U_{−α}`, is
isomorphic over `L` to `SL_2` or `PGL_2` (Borel, Theorem 13.18 (4) and the proof of
Proposition 14.2 (3), for reduced groups over an algebraically closed field). Both
are defined over `F`; call the derived group `G(α)`. Since `G` is simply
connected, so is `G(α)`: over `L`, where `T_α` splits, it is the derived group of
the centralizer of a split subtorus (compare the published
`SplitTorusCentralizerDerivedSimplyConnectedStatement`), and simple connectedness
descends along `L/F`. Hence `G(α)_L ≅ SL_2`, so `G(α)` is absolutely almost
simple, and its absolute root datum has one simple root; the Cartan matrix is the
`1 × 1` matrix `(2)` of type `A_1` and the star action on the single vertex is
trivial, which is `HasInnerTypeA 1`. Finally the image of `π_L = χ ≫ α^∨` lies in
the image of `α^∨`, the one-dimensional maximal torus of `G(α)_L`; as `G(α)` is a
closed `F`-subgroup of `G`, the factorization of `π_L ≫ i_L` through `G(α)_L`
descends to a factorization of `π ≫ i` through `G(α)`. The Lean statement asserts
only the existence of some `D`; `G(α)` is one choice.

## Provenance

Derived. In V. Platonov and A. Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*
(1994), proof of Proposition 6.25 (printed p. 394), `L/F` is cyclic of degree `5`,
and for each root `α` the subgroup `G(α)` generated by the root subgroups of the
five Galois conjugates of `α` is shown to be a simply connected simple `F`-group of
type `A_4`; its maximal torus `T(α) = T ∩ G(α)` has dimension four, and the
componentwise argument there decomposes a class of `T` through the maps
`T(α) → T`. The present statement is the analogous construction for a quadratic
extension: the conjugates of `α` are `α` and `σ(α) = −α`, so `G(α)` has type
`A_1` and its maximal torus over `L` is the one-dimensional image of the coroot
`α^∨`. The hypothesis `π_L = χ ≫ α^∨` and the factorization of `π ≫ i` through
`G(α)` belong to this derived quadratic construction; they play the role of the
maps `T(α) → T` of the source. The statement is the rank-one input for the
componentwise argument applied to `NumberFieldQuadraticAnisotropicToralKernelStatement`.
