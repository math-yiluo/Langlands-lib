# Proof of the classification of simple representations by highest weight

Author: Worker 900.

This module proves `Langlands.GroupScheme.SplitHighestWeightClassificationStatement.{u}`
(module `Rational.SplitHighestWeightClassificationStatement`) as the theorem
`Langlands.GroupScheme.splitHighestWeightClassification`. The theorem's type is
exactly the published proposition. It adds no hypotheses: `k` is an arbitrary field,
`G` an affine group scheme over `Spec k` and `p : SplitPinning G` a split pinning. The
only axioms used are `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

The proof uses the Library's realized coordinates and covariant socles
(`SplitPinning.IsRealizedBy`, `Chevalley.affineCovariantSocleRepresentation`), and
these helper modules of the same contribution:

- `Reductive.SplitBorelCellSaturation`: saturation of realized weights over every
  field (`SplitPinning.IsRealizedWeight.of_nsmul'`).
- `Rational.HighestWeightIntertwiners`: inverse intertwiners, transport of
  `IsHighestWeight` along bijective intertwiners, dominance and uniqueness of highest
  weights.
- `Reductive.NegativeBorelEigenCovector`: an eigen-covector for the negative Borel
  subgroup.
- `Reductive.CovectorRegularEmbedding`: the matrix-coefficient embedding of an
  irreducible representation into a finite regular representation.
- `Reductive.IrreducibleCovariantSocle`: every irreducible representation is
  isomorphic to the covariant socle of a realizing coordinate.

## Auxiliary results in this module

- `SplitPinning.isRealizedWeight_of_isDominantWeight'`: over every field, every
  dominant character is realized. The Library's
  `exists_pos_nsmul_isRealizedWeight` (module `Rational.DominantHighestWeightProof`)
  realizes a positive multiple, and `IsRealizedWeight.of_nsmul'` removes the
  multiple.
- `SplitPinning.exists_isHighestWeight_isRealizedBy_intertwines`: an irreducible
  `ρ` has a character `lam` and a coordinate `f` with `p.IsHighestWeight ρ lam`,
  `p.IsRealizedBy lam f`, and a bijective intertwiner from the socle representation of
  `f` to `ρ`. The previous module gives `lam`, `f` and a bijective intertwiner from
  `ρ` to the socle. `Intertwines.exists_inverse` reverses it. The socle has highest
  weight `lam` by the Library's
  `IsRealizedBy.isHighestWeight_affineCovariantSocleRepresentation`, which
  `IsHighestWeight.of_intertwines` transports to `ρ`.
- `SplitPinning.IsRealizedBy.eq`: two coordinates realizing the same character are
  equal. The Library's `AffineCoordinates.eq_counit_smul_of_covariant_rightInvariant`
  (module `Reductive.CovariantCellFixedLine`), applied with the negative Borel
  subgroup, the positive unipotent subgroup and the cell coordinate, gives
  `f' = counit f' • f`. Here `counit f' = 1`.

## The three conjuncts

`splitHighestWeightClassification` introduces `k`, `G` and `p`, and installs the
affine instance on `G.X.left`.

1. Existence. For dominant `lam`, `isRealizedWeight_of_isDominantWeight'` gives a
   realizing `f`. The covariant socle representation of `f` has highest weight `lam`
   (`IsRealizedBy.isHighestWeight_affineCovariantSocleRepresentation`).
2. Isomorphism classes. Let `ρ` have highest weight `lam` and `σ` have highest
   weight `mu`.
   - If `C` is a bijective intertwiner from `ρ` to `σ`, then `σ` has highest weight
     `lam` (`IsHighestWeight.of_intertwines`), so `lam = mu` by
     `IsHighestWeight.eq`.
   - Conversely, let `lam = mu`. Both representations are irreducible. The previous
     module gives `lam₁`, `f₁` and a bijective intertwiner `D₁` from `ρ` to the socle
     of `f₁`. Its inverse shows that `ρ` has highest weight `lam₁`, so `lam₁ = lam`.
     `exists_isHighestWeight_isRealizedBy_intertwines` gives `lam₂`, `f₂` and a
     bijective intertwiner `C₂` from the socle of `f₂` to `σ`, and `σ` has highest
     weight `lam₂`, so `lam₂ = lam`. Now `f₁` and `f₂` both realize `lam`, so they are
     equal by `IsRealizedBy.eq`. The composite `C₂ * D₁` intertwines `ρ` with `σ`
     (`Intertwines.comp`) and is bijective.
3. Exhaustion. For irreducible `ρ`,
   `exists_isHighestWeight_isRealizedBy_intertwines` gives a highest weight `lam`. It
   is dominant by `IsHighestWeight.isDominantWeight`. Any `mu` that is a highest
   weight of `ρ` equals `lam` by `IsHighestWeight.eq`.

## Provenance

The target is Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017, registered as
`milne-2017-algebraic-groups`), Theorem 22.2, 22.3 and Theorem 22.19, as recorded in
the statement's Markdown. The proof route is internal and differs from Milne's:

- Milne obtains highest weights from primitive vectors of a Borel subgroup. Here an
  eigen-covector of the opposite Borel subgroup and its matrix coefficients
  identify each simple representation with the covariant socle of a realizing
  coordinate.
- Milne shows uniqueness up to isomorphism with the submodule of `V₁ ⊕ V₂`
  generated by `v₁ + v₂`. Here the uniqueness of the realizing coordinate of a
  character on the big cell is used instead.
- Existence over an arbitrary field uses the Library's realization of a positive
  multiple of a dominant character, then saturation via normality of the coordinate
  ring and geometric integrality of smooth connected groups.
