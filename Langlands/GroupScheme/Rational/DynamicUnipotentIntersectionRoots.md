# Rational points of an intersection of two dynamic radicals, bounded by roots

This internal module proves one containment criterion for dynamic unipotent
radicals of a smooth connected affine group `G` over an arbitrary field `k`.
Fix a homomorphism `i : S → G` from a split torus and three cocharacters
`mu`, `ν`, `κ` of `S`. Write `U(λ)` for the dynamic radical
`Dynamic.unipotent (groupHom λ ≫ i)`.

`RelativeRoot.dynamicUnipotent_inf_le_of_positiveRoots` assumes that every
actual relative root `β` of `i` with `⟨β, ν⟩ > 0` and `⟨β, mu⟩ > 0` also has
`⟨β, κ⟩ > 0`. It then proves `U(mu)(k) ∩ U(ν)(k) ⊆ U(κ)(k)` as subgroups of
`G(k)`. No root-system, reducedness or reductivity assumption is made, and the
cocharacters are arbitrary. In particular, they may be zero or nongeneric.

## Proof

The proof follows the construction in `DynamicRadicalRootGeneration`, using two
blocks instead of root rays.

- `semigroupRealization` represents the positive-weight semigroup subgroup of
  `ν` by a smooth connected affine unipotent group `U`. The induced torus action
  on `U` has weight support exactly the positive relative roots of `ν`
  (`actionWeightSupport_positiveSemigroup`).
- These roots split into two blocks: `Ψ₀`, the roots positive on `mu`, and
  `Ψ₁`, the roots nonpositive on `mu`. The additive semigroup generated by `Ψ₀`
  is positive on `mu`. The one generated by `Ψ₁` is nonnegative on `-mu`. So
  each block is disjoint from the semigroup generated by the other.
  `hasOrderedSemigroupProducts_of_unipotent` then gives an isomorphism from the
  ordered product of the two represented block subgroups onto `U`.
- `dynamicSemigroupComparison` identifies the dynamic radicals and parabolics of
  `i` with the corresponding semigroup subgroups.

Take a rational point `g ∈ U(mu)(k) ∩ U(ν)(k)`. It lifts to `U`, and the product
isomorphism writes `g = y₀ y₁`, where `yᵣ` lies in the semigroup subgroup
generated by the block `Ψᵣ`.

- The hypothesis makes every root of `Ψ₀` positive on `κ`, so `y₀ ∈ U(κ)(k)`.
  The roots of `Ψ₀` are also positive on `mu`, so `y₀ ∈ U(mu)(k)`.
- `y₁` lies in the dynamic parabolic of `-mu`. It also equals `y₀⁻¹ g` and so
  lies in `U(mu)(k)`, which is the radical opposite to that parabolic
  (`groupHom_neg_comp_eq_oppositeCocharacter`). Their intersection is trivial
  (`Dynamic.eq_one_of_mem_opposite_unipotent_of_mem_parabolic`), so `y₁ = 1`.

Hence `g = y₀ ∈ U(κ)(k)`.

## Provenance

The module is internal. It is a local consumer of semigroup realization,
transport, ordered-product and comparison results already proved in the
Library. Its role is to bound the intersections `N̄₁ ∩ N₂` and `N̄₂ ∩ N₃` in the
proof of the factorization `V₁₃ = V₁₂ V₂₃` in Renard VII.3.5 (i) (Renard 2026,
printed p. 239 / PDF p. 245). The source states this factorization as a direct
translation of the hypothesis `d(P₁, P₃) = d(P₁, P₂) + d(P₂, P₃)` and gives no
further argument.
