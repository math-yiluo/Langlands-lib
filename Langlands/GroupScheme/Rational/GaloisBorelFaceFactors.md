# Galois permutation of the actual factors of a derived Levi face

Actual cocharacter pushforward commutes with coefficient Galois
transport. The coroot comparison therefore intertwines the ordinary
Galois actions on both root index sets. The actual geometric factor
containing a simple root is unchanged when the root is replaced by
its negative, since reflection in that root fixes its factor.

Every geometric factor has a simple root. The fibres of the
simple-root map to the factors are the Cartan components, using
the induced split maximal tori in the factors. Consequently a
signed action on the ambient simple roots permutes the factors as
the unsigned action permutes the components of the chosen face.
This supplies the factor transport for the trialitarian
opposite-Borel construction in Platonov and Rapinchuk,
*Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed p. 374.
