# Regular twists inside prescribed opens of cochains

The Weil restriction supplies a nonempty Zariski open whose rational points
have strongly regular twisted values. A connected reductive Weil restriction
is unirational, so this open contains a rational point over an infinite field.

Over a finite extension of normed fields with the module topology, the
cochain comparison preserves the analytic topology. Each nonempty analytic
open of the Weil restriction has Zariski-dense underlying points. Its
intersection with the regularity condition is therefore nonempty. Scalar
evaluation on the affine coordinate algebra is continuous into the prime
spectrum, so the intersection is still analytically open.
