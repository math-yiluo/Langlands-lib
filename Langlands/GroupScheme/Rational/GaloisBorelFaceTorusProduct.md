# The torus product for complementary derived Levi faces

Over the splitting field, simple coroots form integral bases of the
cocharacter lattices of the simply connected groups. When the two
faces partition the simple roots, the two actual torus inclusions
send the two derived bases to disjoint parts of the full basis.
Duality of the integral character and cocharacter lattices makes
the combined character restriction bijective.

Multiplication of the two derived tori is therefore an isomorphism
after scalar extension and descends to an isomorphism over the
original field by faithfully flat reflection. This proves the
torus product used for the central and leaf parts of the trialitarian
diagram in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number
Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed p. 374.

The character criterion for the product map only uses the split character restrictions. The combined cocharacter bases can be compared across different choices of root bases.
