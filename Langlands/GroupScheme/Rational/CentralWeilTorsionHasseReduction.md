# Hasse reduction for central kernels of Weil torsion

Let a simply connected central cover over a number field have kernel
identified with positive-exponent torsion of the Weil multiplicative
group for a number field extension. The proposition asks that a target
cocycle trivial on the full pullback at every completion be trivial on
its original finite Galois cover.

Assume the simply connected finite Galois Hasse kernel. Global Weil
torsion lifting and the simultaneous archimedean correction give a
corrected lift trivial at all infinite places. Local simply connected
vanishing supplies the finite-place conditions. Applying the simply
connected Hasse kernel, projecting, and reflecting triviality through
the refinements give the desired trivialization.

This internal reduction combines the degree-two and degree-one
calculations for the Weil torsion kernel.
