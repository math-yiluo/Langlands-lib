# Cyclic weight subgroups of affine groups with split-torus actions

Let `k` be a field and `G` a group scheme over `Spec k` with an isomorphism
`ψ : groupScheme F e ≅ G` from a corepresented model with representing algebra `B`. Let `S`
be a torus with splitting `φ : S.toGrp ≅ splitTorus I` (`I` finite, with decidable
equality), and `b : AutomorphicAction S.toGrp G`. This module carries the weight subgroups
of the abstract layer (`Rational.TorusWeightSubgroups`) to subgroup functors of `G`. It
proves their normality, stability, smoothness and maximality, and identifies the target's
`weightFiltration` with them, given two Lie-algebra inputs. All declarations are in namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme.RelativeRoot`.

## Statements

- `modelAction e ψ φ b : TorusAction F I` is the torus action on algebra points of the model
  induced by the transported action `b.transport φ.inv ψ`
  (`TorusAction.ofAutomorphicAction`, Worker 1239's `Rational.TorusActionBridge`).
- `weightSubgroupFunctor e ψ φ b P`, for a subsemigroup `P` of `I → ℤ`, is the subgroup
  functor `(cutSubgroupFunctor (isCutOutBy_weightIdeal e (modelAction e ψ φ b) P)).comap ψ.inv`
  of `G`. By `mem_weightSubgroupFunctor`, a point `g` over `T` lies in it iff the algebra point
  `pointsEquiv F e T (g ≫ ψ⁻¹)` lies in `weightSubgroup e (modelAction e ψ φ b) P`.
- `weightSubgroupFunctor_isNormalizedBy`: if `P' ⊆ P` and `P + P' ⊆ P'`, then the functor of
  `P'` is normalized by the functor of `P`.
- `isStable_weightSubgroupFunctor`: these functors are `b`-stable.
- `isStable_transport_comap`: a `b`-stable subgroup functor `F'` gives a stable subgroup
  functor `F'.comap ψ.hom` of the model for the transported action.
  `modelAction_mem_affineSubgroups`: the model torus action preserves its affine points
  (`affineSubgroups`).
- `isSmoothConnectedClosed_weightSubgroupFunctor`: if `G` and the model are affine, and `B` is
  formally smooth and of finite type, the functor of a cyclic tail `cyclicTail a r` with
  `a ≠ 0` is smooth, connected and closed (`IsSmoothConnectedClosed`).
- `TangentRigid e ψ φ b P F'` says the following. Take any ideal `J` cutting out the affine
  points of `F'.comap ψ.hom`, with torus stability `hH`. Then every tangent vector of the cut
  functor whose infinitesimal coordinates are supported on a single weight outside `P` is
  trivial. This is the rigidity hypothesis of `le_weightSubgroup`.
- `le_weightSubgroupFunctor` (maximality): suppose `B` is of finite type and `0 ∉ P`. Then
  every smooth connected closed `b`-stable `F'` with `TangentRigid` for `P` satisfies
  `F' ≤ weightSubgroupFunctor e ψ φ b P`.
- `mem_cyclicTail_characterExponents`: `characterExponents φ χ` lies in the tail
  `cyclicTail (characterExponents φ a) r` iff `χ` lies in `RelativeRoot.cyclicTail a r`.
- `weightFiltration_eq_weightSubgroupFunctor`: suppose the exponent vector of `a` is nonzero,
  and assume:
  - `hL1`: the action weights (`actionWeightSupport`) of the weight subgroup functor of
    `cyclicTail a' r` lie in `cyclicTail a r`;
  - `hL2`: every generator (`IsSemigroupGenerator b (cyclicTail a r)`) is `TangentRigid`.

  Then `weightFiltration b a r` equals this weight subgroup functor.

## Proofs

Normality: on points, composition with `ψ⁻¹` and `pointsEquiv` are multiplicative
(`MonObj.mul_comp`, `GrpObj.inv_comp`), so this is `conj_mem_weightSubgroup`. Stability is the
bridge's `isStable_weightSubgroup` transported by `AutomorphicAction.isStable_transport`.
Smoothness, connectedness and closedness come from the polynomial coordinate ring
(`exists_aeval_bijective_cyclicTail`, the bridge's `isSmoothConnectedClosed_cutSubgroupFunctor`
and `IsSmoothConnectedClosed.comap_iso`).

Maximality: let `F'` be represented by a closed immersion `j` from a connected `K`. Then
`F'.comap ψ.hom` is represented by `j ≫ ψ⁻¹` (`IsRepresentedBy.comp_iso`), which is closed
(`IsClosedSubgroup.comp_iso`). `exists_ideal_range_of_connectedSpace`
(`Rational.TorusWeightClosed`) gives an ideal `J` cutting out its points, with `B ⧸ J` free of
nontrivial idempotents. The Library's `isCutOutBy_range` turns this into `IsCutOutBy` for its
affine points. Then `B ⧸ J` is noetherian since `B` is of finite type over a field, and the
abstract maximality theorem `le_weightSubgroup` (`Rational.TorusWeightTangent`) applies. A
point of `F'` over any test scheme is carried to the affine points by
`mem_range_iff_mem_affineSubgroups`.

Identification: the filtration is the subgroup generated by the generators, so `≤` follows
from maximality (`Subgroup.closure_le`). The weight subgroup functor is itself a generator by
the three properties above and `hL1`, which gives `≥` (`generator_le_semigroupSubgroup`).

## Provenance

Internal supporting material for the proof of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.RelativeRoot.CyclicWeightFiltrationStatement` (task 023326af). In
Conrad–Gabber–Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive groups*, 2nd ed., §3.3 (Proposition 3.3.6, the
construction before Lemma 3.3.8, and Lemma 3.3.8 itself, printed pp.111-112), the subgroups
`U_{A_j}(G)` of the cyclic tails are constructed by the dynamic method. Lemma 3.3.8 shows
`U_{A_j}(G)` normal in `U_A(G)`. Here the Library's `weightFiltration` is identified with
explicit weight subgroups, and normality is proved from the weight description. The route is
internal to this formalization.
