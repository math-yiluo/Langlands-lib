# Closed weight paths of actual Steinberg coefficient matrices

The coefficient of degree `m` in a supplied simple-root factor, followed by its supplied reflection representative, sends an actual weight `lam` to the reflected weight plus `m` times that simple root. `coefficientMatrix_mul_normalizer_mem_weightSpace` proves this by combining the existing normalizer transport with the existing root divided-power weight shift. Both inputs use the actual universal character spaces, and the coefficient and representative matrices are those of the original representation.

`coefficientWeightPath` records suffix weights, so that each step has the same order as the factors in `steinbergCoefficientMatrix`. The last weight is the original weight. The theorem `steinbergCoefficientMatrix_mem_weightSpace` follows by induction on the ordered factors and identifies the weight of their actual matrix action.

The internal linear-algebra lemma `closedIndependentPath_constant` telescopes the step differences of a closed path. Linear independence forces every step coefficient to vanish, and induction makes the path constant. Applied to the independent simple roots, it proves `coefficientWeightPath_constant_of_closed`: every weight along a closed path equals the original weight, and every nonnegative degree equals the integral pairing of that weight with the corresponding simple coroot. The converse follows because those pairing equations make each individual step fix the weight. This gives `coefficientWeightPath_closed_iff`, including the empty simple-root family.

In particular, a closed path can only occur at a dominant weight. The final projector theorem uses the existing universal character projector, which is the identity on its own character space and zero on every different character space. It states that projecting an ordered coefficient product back to its original weight space kills every degree tuple except the tuple of simple-coroot pairings.

The mathematics is an internal consequence of the actual coefficient covariance, normalizer transport, universal character projectors, root reflection formula, and independence of the supplied simple roots. It formalizes the closed-path step in Steinberg's character calculation, *Regular elements of semi-simple algebraic groups* (1965), printed p.69, steps 1–3 and 6 (registered source `steinberg-1965-regular-elements-semisimple-groups`, SHA-256 `bda20c4a4b3cf4a7e9e5b17a4eacd0147d0c9370063ab3a3778114a53c8349b9`). That page was read directly. No source errata note was found.

The results retain arbitrary fields, universes, supplied parametrizations, representatives, and orderings. They require no representation irreducibility or highest-weight hypothesis. The polynomial trace expansion, nonzero top coefficient, lower-variable triangularity, invariant generation, and canonical quotient isomorphism remain further work.
