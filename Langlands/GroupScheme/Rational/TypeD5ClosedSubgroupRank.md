# Maximal-torus rank and the exclusion of A₅ in type D₅

`TypeD5ExcludesClosedTypeA5Statement` rules out a closed embedding from a reductive group with an A₅ pinning into split simply connected D₅ over an algebraically closed field.

A maximal torus of a closed subgroup fits into an ambient maximal torus. The closed inclusion induces an injection on Lie algebras, so the subgroup torus dimension is at most the ambient one. In case of equality the map of tori is an isomorphism. The source pinning has five linearly independent simple coroots, and the simply connected D₅ target has an integral cocharacter basis of five simple coroots. A closed embedding would therefore make the source torus an ambient maximal torus. On that common torus the actual subgroup root and coroot pairs are inherited from the ambient root datum. This gives the A₅ pairing matrix inside D₅, contradicting the determinant obstruction.

The exclusion is used in the cubic splitting argument in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Theorem 6.26, printed p. 355. [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
