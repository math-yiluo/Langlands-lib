# The based root datum of the derived E₆ branch Levi

The construction takes a rational cocharacter realizing the specified branch
coweight, a connected reductive representative of its centralizer, and the
actual derived inclusion in that centralizer. The ambient maximal torus
centralizes the branch line. Its intersection with the derived group is a
maximal torus of that group. Over the separable closure the roots of the
derived group relative to this intersection torus correspond to the ambient
roots that vanish on the branch cocharacter, with the same Cartan pairings and
compatible integral coroots. The branch coweight is nonnegative on the
original simple roots and vanishes on all of them except the short-arm vertex.
The zero-face base is therefore the complement of that vertex.

When the separable closure of the ground field is algebraically closed, as in
characteristic zero, any actual Tits index of the affine, locally finite-type
derived group can be compared with the constructed zero-face pinning. This
gives a numbering in which all ordered Cartan entries agree with
`CartanMatrix.A 5`, the ordered chain left after deleting vertex `1` of E₆.
