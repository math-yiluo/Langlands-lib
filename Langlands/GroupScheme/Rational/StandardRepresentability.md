# Closed representatives of standard rational subgroup functors

For an affine group scheme `G` over a field, a commutative group scheme `T`, a homomorphism `i : T → G`, a cocharacter `ν` of `T` and a set `J` of its characters, this module constructs actual closed subgroup representatives of the existing choice-free functors `standardParabolic i ν J`, `standardLevi i ν J` and `standardUnipotent i ν J`. Their names in namespace `Langlands.GroupScheme.Standard` are `parabolicScheme`, `leviScheme` and `unipotentScheme`, with inclusions `parabolicHom`, `leviHom` and `unipotentHom`.

The construction uses the same family of cocharacters satisfying `IsStandardCocharacter i ν J` that occurs in the existing definitions. In affine coordinates on `G`, each dynamic subgroup is cut out by its explicit Laurent-coaction ideal. The representative is the spectrum of the quotient by the sum of those ideals. `CorepresentedIntersection` supplies this quotient group scheme and proves that it represents the pointwise intersection. Transport along `AffineCoordinates.groupSchemeIso` identifies that intersection with the original standard functor on every test scheme, including nonaffine and nonreduced tests.

No standard-cocharacter existence or comparison theorem is assumed. If the indexing family is empty, the existing standard functor is all of `G` and the ideal sum is zero; the construction retains this convention. No maximality, splitness, genericity or reductivity assumption is imposed on the input. Accordingly, the words “parabolic”, “Levi” and “unipotent” in the names identify the existing functors; this module establishes their closed representability, without asserting the corresponding geometric structure properties or the existence of compatible split components.

`isRepresentedBy_*Hom` and `isClosedSubgroup_*Hom` state the all-test representability and closedness conclusions. The generic `IsRepresentedBy.inclusionHom` constructs a group-scheme morphism from an inclusion of represented subgroup functors, using the natural inclusion on each group of points and the existing Yoneda lift. Applied to the existing inclusions of the Levi and unipotent functors into the parabolic functor, it gives actual group-scheme morphisms `leviToParabolic` and `unipotentToParabolic`; their composites with `parabolicHom` are the original inclusions into `G`. The existing general `IsRepresentedBy.pointsEquiv` consequently identifies points of each chosen representative with its standard subgroup on every test scheme.

This is an internal construction derived from `Rational.StandardParabolic`, `Dynamic.Representability`, `Dynamic.AffineRepresentability`, `AffineCorepresented`, `CorepresentedIntersection` and `RepresentedSubgroup`. It does not replace or prove the structural assertions in `StandardCocharacterStatement`, the standard parabolic order and classification statements, or `LeviSplitComponentStatement`.
