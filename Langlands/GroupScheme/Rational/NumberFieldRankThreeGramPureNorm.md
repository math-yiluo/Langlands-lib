# Pure quaternion representation of the rank-three Gram norm

`NumberFieldRankThreeGramPureNormStatement` concerns an invertible rank-three
skew-hermitian Gram matrix over a central quaternion division algebra over a
number field, with the standard conjugation. Assume that at each completion
there is an isotropic row vector with at least one invertible quaternion
coordinate. There is a nonzero global pure quaternion whose reduced norm is
the reduced norm of the original Gram matrix in the matrix algebra.

At a place where the quaternion algebra splits, every scalar is a pure
quaternion norm. At a division place the local isotropic vector is nonzero,
so the rank-three basis with opposite diagonal values gives a pure norm
representative. Coefficient naturality shows that the scalar in both arguments
is the image of the original Gram norm. The number-field pure norm theorem
gives the representative over the number field. It is nonzero because the
original Gram matrix is invertible.

This proves the discriminant representation step of Springer, appendix to
M. Kneser, *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups* (1969),
Theorem 5.3.3(i), printed p. 109 (PDF p. 115).
