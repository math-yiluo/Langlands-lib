# Reduction of `H¹ = 1` for quasi-split `E₇` over totally complex fields

Module: `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.NumberFieldTotallyComplexTypeE7Reduction`.

The module states `NumberFieldTotallyComplexTypeE7ReductionStatement` and proves it as
`numberFieldTotallyComplexTypeE7_of_inputs`. The statement is the implication

```lean
TypeE7MaximalTorusCocycleOrderStatement.{u} →
  NumberFieldTorusCocycleCyclotomicSplittingStatement.{u} →
    NumberFieldTotallyComplexTypeE7CyclicStatement.{u} →
      NumberFieldTotallyComplexTypeE7Statement.{u}
```

Its conclusion is the statement `NumberFieldTotallyComplexTypeE7Statement` of the module
`Rational/NumberFieldTypeE7HasseInputs`: over a totally complex number field `K`, every
cocycle of a quasi-split, simply connected, absolutely almost-simple group `G` of type `E₇`
(with Tits index data `d` and a numbering `e : d.Vertex ≃ Fin 7` of its simple vertices
matching `CartanMatrix.E 7`) on a finite Galois field cover `Spec L → Spec K` is trivial.
The three premises are:

- `TypeE7MaximalTorusCocycleOrderStatement` (module
  `Rational/NumberFieldTotallyComplexTypeE7Inputs`): over a field of characteristic zero,
  every cocycle of a maximal torus of such a group on a finite Galois cover has a trivial
  `2^a 3^b`-th power (Platonov–Rapinchuk, Proposition 6.21);
- `NumberFieldTorusCocycleCyclotomicSplittingStatement` (module
  `Rational/NumberFieldTorusCyclotomicSplitting`): a torus cocycle over a number field whose
  `m`-th power is trivial becomes trivial over every field containing a primitive `N`-th
  root of unity, for some `N > 0` whose prime factors divide `m` (Platonov–Rapinchuk,
  Proposition 6.20 and Lemma 6.20);
- `NumberFieldTotallyComplexTypeE7CyclicStatement` (module
  `Rational/NumberFieldTotallyComplexTypeE7Inputs`): over a totally complex number field,
  a quasi-split group as above has trivial cocycles on Galois covers of degree `2` and `3`
  (Platonov–Rapinchuk, Theorem 6.29).

The premises are not proved in this module. The proof follows the derivation of
`H¹(K, G₀) = 1` on printed pp. 377–378 of Platonov–Rapinchuk.

  change `ξ.baseChangePullback (algebraBaseMap K K⟮ζ⟯)` is trivial is itself trivial.

### Proof of the cyclotomic descent

Choose a `K`-embedding of `M₀` into the separable closure and let `M` be the compositum of
its image with `K⟮ζ⟯`. Then `M/K` is finite Galois (the compositum of two Galois
intermediate fields) and contains `K⟮ζ⟯` and a copy of `M₀`. Let `ξ_M` be the refinement of
`ξ` to `Spec M → Spec K`. For an intermediate field `F ⊆ M` call `ξ_M` a coboundary on
`Gal(M/F)` if `value ξ_M σ = f · σ(f)⁻¹` for one point `f ∈ G(M)` and every `σ ∈ Gal(M/K)`
fixing `F` pointwise (the private predicate `FixedCoboundary`).

1. *Top.* Since the base change of `ξ` to `K⟮ζ⟯` is trivial,
   `GaloisCocycle.exists_value_refine_of_baseChangePullback_isTrivial` (module
   `GaloisCocycleInflationRestriction`) makes `ξ_M` a coboundary on `Gal(M/K⟮ζ⟯)`.
2. *Steps.* For `t ∣ N` let `E_t = K⟮ζ^t⟯`; it is generated by a primitive `(N/t)`-th root
   of unity, hence a cyclotomic and therefore Galois extension of `K`
   (`IsPrimitiveRoot.intermediateField_adjoin_isCyclotomicExtension`,
   `IsCyclotomicExtension.isGalois`). If `t = q t'` with `q ∈ {2, 3}` prime, then
   `β = ζ^{t'}` satisfies `β^q = ζ^t`, so `E_t ⊆ E_{t'}` and `E_{t'} = E_t(β)` has degree at
   most `q` over `E_t`: the minimal polynomial of `β` over `E_t` divides `X^q − ζ^t`, and
   `E_t⟮β⟯` is all of `E_{t'}` (private lemma `fixedCoboundary_adjoin_pow`). The extension
   `E_{t'}/E_t` is Galois. If its degree is `1`, the two fields coincide. If it is `2` or
   `3`, every cocycle of `G_{E_t}` on `Spec E_{t'} → Spec E_t` is trivial by
   `typeE7Cyclic_intermediateField`, and the descent step
   `GaloisCocycle.exists_value_refine_of_restrict` passes the coboundary property from
   `Gal(M/E_{t'})` to `Gal(M/E_t)`. Here `E_t`, `E_{t'}` and `M` are intermediate fields of
   the separable closure, made into algebras over each other by their inclusions.
3. *Induction.* By strong induction on `t ∣ N`, splitting off one prime factor `q` of `t`
   (necessarily `2` or `3`), `ξ_M` is a coboundary on `Gal(M/E_t)` for every `t ∣ N`
   (private lemma `fixedCoboundary_pow_of_dvd`).
4. *Bottom.* For `t = N`, `E_N = K⟮1⟯ = ⊥`, which every automorphism fixes, so `ξ_M` is a
   coboundary on all of `Gal(M/K)`, and `GaloisCocycle.isTrivial_of_forall_value_refine`
   shows that `ξ` is trivial.

## Proof of `numberFieldTotallyComplexTypeE7_of_inputs`

Fix `K`, `G` with its hypotheses, a finite Galois extension `L/K` and a cocycle `ξ` of `G`
on `Spec L → Spec K`.

1. `G` is semisimple (`IsSimplyConnected.isSemisimple`) and quasi-split, so the published
   theorem `quasiSplitCocycleTorusReduction` gives a torus reduction `R` of `ξ`: a finite
   Galois extension `M₀ = R.carrier` of `K` with a refinement `Spec M₀ → Spec L`, a cochain
   `c` over `Spec M₀`, and a closed subgroup inclusion `i : T → G` of a `K`-torus `T`
   such that the twist by `c` of the refinement of `ξ` takes values in `T`. The resulting
   cocycle `τ₀ = R.torusCocycle` of `T` satisfies `τ₀.map i = (ξ.refine r).twist c` on every
   test scheme (`Cocycle.lift_toFun`). A torus is commutative (`IsTorus.isCommMonObj`), so
   `T` is a commutative group scheme.
2. By `exists_maximalTorus_extension_of_finiteType`, `i` factors as `i = f ≫ j` through a
   maximal torus `j : T' → G`. Put `τ = τ₀.map f`, a cocycle of `T'` on `Spec M₀ → Spec K`.
3. The order premise gives `a, b` such that the `2^a 3^b`-th power of `τ` is trivial. The
   cyclotomic premise, with `m = 2^a 3^b`, gives `N > 0` whose prime factors divide
   `2^a 3^b`, hence are `2` or `3`, such that `τ` becomes trivial over every field
   containing a primitive `N`-th root of unity.
4. The separable closure of `K` contains a primitive `N`-th root of unity `ζ`
   (`HasEnoughRootsOfUnity`), so `τ` becomes trivial over `K⟮ζ⟯`. Then so does `τ.map j`
   (`Cocycle.isTrivial_baseChangePullback_map`), and `typeE7_cyclotomicDescent`, applied to
   the cocycle `τ.map j` of `G` on `Spec M₀ → Spec K`, shows that `τ.map j` is trivial.
5. Since `f ≫ j = i`, the cocycle `τ.map j` has the same values as `(ξ.refine r).twist c`,
   which is therefore trivial (`Cocycle.IsTrivial.congr`). Hence the refinement of `ξ` is
   trivial (`Cocycle.IsTrivial.of_twist`), and so is `ξ`, because the refinement
   `Spec M₀ → Spec L` is fpqc (`Cocycle.isTrivial_refine_iff`).

The proofs of `typeE7_cyclotomicDescent` and of the private descent step use the options
`maxHeartbeats 1600000` and `synthInstance.maxHeartbeats 400000`, because instance search
over intermediate fields of the separable closure with inclusion algebras is slow.

## Source

V. Platonov and A. Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), registered as
`platonov-rapinchuk-1994-algebraic-groups-number-theory`; printed page `p` is PDF page
`p + 10`. Section 6.8, printed pp. 377–378: for a quasi-split simply connected group `G₀`
of type `E₆` or `E₇` over a totally imaginary number field `K`, every class of
`H¹(K, G₀)` lifts to a class in `H¹(K, T)` for some maximal `K`-torus `T` (Proposition
6.19). The torus class has order `2^a 3^b` (Proposition 6.21) and becomes trivial over
a finite subextension of the cyclotomic `{2, 3}`-extension of `K` (Proposition 6.20 and
Lemma 6.20). Theorem 6.29 applied along a tower of cyclic extensions of degree `2` and
`3` gives the triviality of the original class over `K`. The module carries out this
argument for type `E₇` with the Library's natural cocycles on finite Galois covers. It
uses the tower `K = K⟮ζ^N⟯ ⊆ … ⊆ K⟮ζ⟯` and the inflation–restriction sequence
(Serre, *Galois Cohomology*, I.§5.8(a), registered as `serre-1997-galois-cohomology`)
in the form proved in `GaloisCocycleInflationRestriction`.
