# Equivariant coordinates of rational torus points

Let `k` and `K` be arbitrary fields with an algebra structure, `G` an affine
group scheme over `k`, and `D` supplied Galois-compatible pinning data.
Assume that the integral coroot span of the actual pinned root datum is
the entire cocharacter lattice. This is an explicit hypothesis throughout;
the preceding coordinate module supplies it when the base changed group
is algebraically simply connected.

`torusCoordinates_symm_apply` identifies the inverse coordinate map with
the finite commutative product of the transported actual simple-coroot
points. `prod_torusCorootPoint_coordinates` reconstructs every point of
the base changed descended torus from these coordinates.

`torusCoordinates_rationalConjugate` proves covariance of the coordinates:
the coordinate at `D.action σ a` of the conjugate of `x` is the image under
`σ` of the coordinate at `a` of `x`. The proof conjugates the coroot product
using the actual point covariance from `SimpleCorootGaloisTransport`,
reindexes it through the supplied permutation of simple roots, and applies
the coordinate equivalence. It uses no independent assumption about the
fundamental weights under Galois action.

`rationalTorusCoordinates` is the multiplicative map that first extends a
base-field point of the descended torus and then takes its simple-coroot
coordinates. `torusCoordinates_rationalExtend_equivariant` proves
equivariance of these coordinates. It uses that coefficient conjugation
fixes scalar extensions of actual base-field points.
`exists_equivariant_torusCorootProduct` packages this as an equivariant
family of field units and an equality with their actual coroot product.

`exists_equivariant_simpleCorootProduct` gives the corresponding formula
in the ambient base changed group. The scalar extension of the original
torus point followed by the original inclusion equals the product in the
pinned torus followed by its actual pinned inclusion. Naturality of scalar
extension and the inclusion equality retained by `D` prove this last
comparison. The product is taken in the commutative torus before mapping
to the ambient group.

All results include empty simple-root support. Finiteness or Galoisness of
the field extension is unnecessary: the results hold for every algebra
automorphism of `K` over `k`. The module does not regroup the factors into
Galois orbits and does not prove membership in the subgroup generated by
relative root groups.

The provenance is internal composition of actual simple-coroot point
coordinates, their proved ordinary Galois covariance, coefficient-extension
fixedness, and the published naturality of scalar extension. It supplies
the coordinate interface used by the separate orbit-generation argument.
