# Outer quasi-split E₆ via the quadratic star field

`NumberFieldQuasiSplitTypeE6OuterReductionStatement` gives quasi-split simply connected E₆ vanishing over totally complex number fields from inner quasi-split E₆ vanishing and the outer type A Hasse kernel. Its conclusion covers every finite Galois cover. The inner premise concerns quasi-split groups with trivial star action.

For nontrivial star action, the actual star field is quadratic Galois. The group becomes split there by the quasi-split star criterion. Inner vanishing on a common Galois refinement makes the original cocycle trivial after restriction to the star field. Inflation–restriction reduces triviality over the base to the cocycles on the star-field cover.

On that cover, the actual pure twist also splits over the quadratic field and is isotropic by the quadratic splitting theorem. The short-arm vertex is distinguished for the twist by the isotropy-to-marking result. It is distinguished for the original group because all absolute roots restrict nontrivially to a quasi-split group's maximal split torus. The split-line centralizer factorization reduces the cocycle to its simply connected A₅ derived group. Reduced-norm locality gives the inner type A kernel and the stated outer type A premise gives the other kernel. All local classes used here are trivial over the totally complex base.

The proof combines the quadratic splitting and split-line centralizer steps in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.8, Lemma 6.31 and Proposition 6.22, printed pp. 379–383. [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
