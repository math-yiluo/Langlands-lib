# Torus envelope normality for solvable groups over a finite field

Worker 1387. This module states one proposition,
`FiniteFieldSolvableEnvelopeNormalityStatement.{u}`. It is not proved here.

## Statement

Fix a finite field `k : Type u` and a group scheme `G` over `Spec k` that is
smooth and affine, with connected underlying space. Assume that `G` is
solvable, `IsSolvableGroupScheme G`: the abstract group of `k̄`-points of the
base change of `G` to the algebraic closure `k̄` of `k` is solvable. The
proposition asserts that the existing `closedTorusEnvelope G` is normal,
`(closedTorusEnvelope G).IsNormal`: on every test scheme over `Spec k`,
conjugation by every point of `G` preserves the points of the envelope.
Nonreduced tests are included. The envelope is the unchanged intersection of
the ranges of all actual closed subgroups of `G` containing every closed
`k`-torus of `G`.

## Source and derivation

The source is Conrad–Gabber–Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive groups*, second edition
(2015), proof of Proposition A.2.11, printed pp.478–479 (one-based PDF
pp.504–505), registered as `conrad-gabber-prasad-2015-pseudo-reductive`.
For a smooth connected `k`-group `G` over a finite field, the source writes
`G_t` for the smooth connected `k`-subgroup generated by the `k`-tori of `G`,
and `G^τ` for the Galois descent of the normal subgroup `(G_{k_s})_t` of
`G_{k_s}`; `G^τ` is normal in `G`. The source then proves `G^τ = G_t` for
every smooth connected solvable group over a perfect field of positive
characteristic, using Lemma A.2.12 and Theorem B.4.3. A finite field is such
a field. So `G_t` equals the normal subgroup `G^τ`.

The present proposition is this solvable case for finite fields, with `G_t`
identified with the fixed envelope. Since `G_t` is a closed subgroup that
contains every `k`-torus, and every closed subgroup containing all `k`-tori
contains the subgroup they generate, the range of `G_t` on every test equals
`closedTorusEnvelope G`. Normality of `G_t` is therefore normality of the
envelope on every test.

## Intended use

Together with `FiniteFieldCentralCocharacterSolvableStatement` (a group over a
finite field whose cocharacters are all central is solvable), this
proposition implies `FiniteFieldCentralCocharacterEnvelopeNormalityStatement`,
the base case of the finite-field reduction in
`FiniteFieldMaximalTorusTransporterReduction`.
