# The central and leaf parts of the signed trialitarian diagram

The signed action preserves the zero-root condition on either of the
two parts of the \(D_4\) base. The singleton part consists of the
central vertex. The three mutually orthogonal leaves form the other
part. The central vertex is fixed by star, and the absence of a fixed
leaf implies transitivity on the three leaves.

Each leaf labels an actual rank-one geometric factor. Equality of
actual factor indices agrees with equality of the leaf labels, so the
stabilizer of a factor is the full star stabilizer of its original
vertex. The orbit is the whole geometric factor set. The descended
leaf group is therefore an actual Weil restriction from the cubic
fixed field of this stabilizer. This gives the leaf group in the
opposite-Borel decomposition of
Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*
(1994), §6.8, printed p. 374.
