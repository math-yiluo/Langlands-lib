# Simultaneous archimedean correction through norm-one torsion

A central correction of the source cocycle uses one natural kernel
cocycle on a finite Galois refinement. Its pointwise quotient with
the refined source must be trivial on all infinite pullback covers.

At one place, work on a common coefficient field for the original
cover and its refinement. Suppose the global kernel cocycle and
the local correction differ by the crossed coboundary of a kernel
point `t`. Multiplying the local cochain by the image of `t` gives a
trivialization of the corrected source cocycle, by centrality.
Triviality on the compatible local field cover reflects to the full
pullback cover.

For norm-one torsion, compare the scalar extension `z` of the global
radical with the local radical `x`. The simultaneous norm-power
equations give a completed tensor unit `q` for their power and norm
ratios. The quotient `z/(xq)` is killed by the exponent and has norm
one. Since `q` is fixed by the local coefficient action, its image
under the inverse kernel equivalence gives the required coboundary
comparison point.

Choose the algebraic closure of each infinite completion as a finite
Galois coefficient field. The projected local trivializations supply
local radicals and norm-power pairs. For a positive exponent and
quadratic degree or degree coprime to the exponent, approximate the
pairs simultaneously and construct a global radical on a finite
Galois refinement. Embed that refinement compatibly into the same
local closures. The norm and power comparisons then apply at every
place to its single kernel cocycle.

This internal construction supplies the archimedean correction after
global finite Galois lifting through norm-one torsion.
