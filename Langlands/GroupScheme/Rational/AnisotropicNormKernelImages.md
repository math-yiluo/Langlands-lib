# Images of anisotropic factors in arbitrary rational norm kernels

Authors: Worker 1676.

For a connected reductive anisotropic group `A` over a nonarchimedean local
field `F`, every group-scheme homomorphism `f : A ⟶ Z` sends all rational
points of `A` into `Plancherel.rationalCommonNormKernel Z`, the subgroup
`Z(F)^1` of points with norm one under every rational character. The target
`Z` is arbitrary: it need not be anisotropic, reductive, affine or a
represented centralizer. The field has arbitrary universe `u` and no
characteristic restriction.

`IsAnisotropic.map_mem_rationalCommonNormKernel` proves this assertion.
Its implemented proof combines the full norm-kernel equality from
`AnisotropicCentralizerNormKernel` with the published naturality lemma
`Plancherel.rationalCommonNormKernel_le_comap`. The former uses compactness
of the rational points of the anisotropic source; the latter restricts
each rational character of `Z` along `f`. No compactness assertion about
`Z(F)` or the whole centralizer is needed.

`IsAnisotropic.normKernelPointMap` constructs the actual group homomorphism
`A(F) →* Z(F)^1` by corestricting `rationalPoints.map f`. The coercion
formula `normKernelPointMap_coe` identifies it pointwise with the original
rational-point map. `range_le_rationalCommonNormKernel` gives the
corresponding subgroup containment. For any further homomorphism
`j : Z ⟶ G`, `map_range_le_map_rationalCommonNormKernel` maps that containment
into `G(F)`, retaining exactly the norm-kernel image attached to the
supplied `Z` and `j`.

The final two lemmas allow a family of connected reductive anisotropic
sources `Aᵢ` and maps `Aᵢ ⟶ Z`. The subgroup supremum of their actual
rational images is the subgroup generated by the images, so
`iSup_anisotropicPointMapRange_le_normKernel` proves that every element
generated by those images belongs to `Z(F)^1`.
`map_iSup_anisotropicPointMapRange_le_map_normKernel` carries that subgroup
containment through an arbitrary `j : Z ⟶ G`. The family has arbitrary
independent universe `v`, and no finiteness assumption is required.
Both proofs use the universal property of subgroup supremum and
monotonicity of subgroup image.

These are internal consequences of the named Lean results. In particular,
they apply to supplied anisotropic semisimple factors inside a general
centralizer, without assuming that the ambient group is anisotropic.
They do not construct a simple-factor decomposition or prove generation
by those factors and rank-one isotropic pieces. The isotropic
Kneser–Tits input, the full simply connected centralizer-generation
statement and the exact normalizer facet-fixing theorem remain open.
