# Geometric rigidity of integral and positive action weights

This internal supporting module defines `RelativeRoot.integralActionWeightSpan b a` as the sum of the intrinsic Lie weight spaces for all integer multiples of a character, for an arbitrary torus action on an affine group over any field. No connectedness, smoothness or finite type hypothesis enters this definition.

The helper `eq_top_of_smooth_closed_of_tangentSubmodule_eq_top` applies the published `lieSubgroupEquality` theorem to a smooth closed representative with full intrinsic tangent image inside a smooth connected affine group. The tangent image is the range of the actual derivative. Its fullness gives an actual Lie lift of each ambient identity-kernel point, exactly the hypothesis required by the published theorem. The representative therefore has full range on every test scheme. The subgroup representative itself need not be connected.

`tangentSubmodule_fixedSubgroup` identifies the intrinsic tangent submodule of the all-refinement fixed subgroup with the differential invariants over all coefficient algebras. It is the extensional form of the published tangent fixedness criterion. For a diagonalizable acting group with finitely generated character group, `fixedSubgroup_eq_top_of_diagonalizable_lieInvariants_eq_top` combines field fixed-subgroup representability, the published diagonalizable fixed-subgroup smoothness theorem and the preceding full-tangent helper. Thus a trivial differential forces the entire action to be trivial on a smooth connected affine group. The acting group may be nonsmooth, including torsion in the characteristic.

`integralActionWeightSpan_le_kernel_lieInvariants` evaluates each integer-multiple character at points of the actual character kernel. Its value is one, so every such vector is fixed by the restricted differential. Closure under sums gives the full span inclusion. `kernelActsTrivially_of_integralActionWeightSpan_eq_top` uses the published diagonalizable model of the character kernel, with its full quotient character group including torsion, and the preceding rigidity result. If the integral weight span is full, the actual kernel acts trivially on every test scheme. This theorem assumes the torus is split and the group is smooth, connected and affine. It does not require that the character be nonzero or primitive.

Finally, `unipotent_eq_top_of_positiveWeightSpan_eq_top` treats an arbitrary multiplicative-group action on a smooth connected affine group. The published unconditional `dynamicLie` theorem and arbitrary-action Lie transfer identify its identity-limit tangent with its positive intrinsic span. The published smooth closed identity-limit representative and the full-tangent helper then show that the identity-limit functor is the whole group on every test scheme.

All constructions are internal consequences of imported published results. This module supplies neither dynamic connectedness nor the final cyclic-root comparison.
