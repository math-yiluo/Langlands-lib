# Proof of the actual simple relative-root basis

Author: Worker 53.

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.RelativeSimpleRootBasisProof`.

## Result

`Langlands.GroupScheme.relativeSimpleRootBasis` has type exactly
`RelativeSimpleRootBasisStatement.{u}`, the published proposition of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.RelativeRankStatement`, at an arbitrary universe
`u`. For every field `k : Type u`, every `G` with `IsConnectedReductive G`,
every `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G` with `IsMaximalSplitTorus i` and every cocharacter `ν`
with `IsGenericCocharacter i ν`, it proves the following:

- the set `simpleRelativeRoots i ν` is finite;
- there is a `Module.Basis (simpleRelativeRoots i ν) ℚ (relativeRootSpan i)`
  whose vector at each `α` is `simpleRelativeRootVector i ν α`, the actual
  rationalized character `1 ⊗ α`;
- `relativeRootRank i = Nat.card (simpleRelativeRoots i ν)`.

The simple roots are the published ones: positive relative roots that are not a
sum of two positive relative roots, taken over the full root set. Nonreduced
relative root systems are therefore included. Anisotropic groups are also
included: their simple-root set is empty, the basis is indexed by the empty
type, and the rank is zero. No hypothesis is added to the statement.

## Implemented proof

The proof is one term:

`relativeSimpleRootBasisStatement_of_relativeRootSystemStatement
  (relativeRootSystemStatement_of_rootRankOneFactorizationStatement rootRankOneFactorization)`.

All three ingredients are published Library theorems.

1. `rootRankOneFactorization` (module `Rational.RootRankOneFactorizationProof`)
   proves `RootRankOneFactorizationStatement.{u}` with no premise.
2. `relativeRootSystemStatement_of_rootRankOneFactorizationStatement` (module
   `Rational.RootSystemFromFactorPairs`) turns it into
   `RelativeRootSystemStatement.{u}`. So for each such `i` it gives
   `HasRelativeRootSystem i`: a finite crystallographic root system on
   `relativeRootSpan i`, possibly nonreduced, with the evaluation pairing,
   whose roots are exactly the vectors `1 ⊗ α` for the actual relative roots
   `α`. The same composition is the entire proof of Worker 23's
   `relativeRootSystem` candidate for task
   `51ecd216-d68e-4f71-afeb-6d7ff3683ec9`. That candidate is still awaiting
   review, and this proof does not import it. The two steps are applied
   directly here.
3. `relativeSimpleRootBasisStatement_of_relativeRootSystemStatement` (module
   `Rational.RelativeSimpleRootBasis`) derives
   `RelativeSimpleRootBasisStatement.{u}` from `RelativeRootSystemStatement.{u}`.
   It fixes `k`, `G`, `i` and `ν`. It gets the affine instance from
   `IsConnectedReductive.isAffineHom` and the monomorphism instance from the
   split-subtorus part of `IsMaximalSplitTorus`. Then:
   - Finiteness: `HasRelativeRootSystem.finite_simpleRelativeRoots` (module
     `Rational.RootFiniteness`). The relative roots inject into the finite
     root range, because the rationalization of the character lattice of a
     split torus is injective. The simple roots form a subset of them.
   - Spanning: `HasRelativeRootSystem.neg_mem_relativeRoots` gives closure of
     the actual roots under negation. With genericity of `ν`, the published
     `relativeRoots_subset_addSubgroupClosure_simpleRelativeRoots` puts every
     actual root in the additive group generated by the simple roots. It
     argues by induction on the positive pairing with `ν`, and negates for
     negative roots. Hence the simple-root vectors span `relativeRootSpan i`.
   - Independence: `HasRelativeRootSystem.linearIndependent_simpleRelativeRoots`
     (module `Rational.RelativeRootStandardCocharacter`). It sends each actual
     simple root to an index of the Mathlib base `IsAddIndecomposable.baseOf`
     cut out by the functional `⟨·, ν⟩`. This assignment is injective. The
     roots at those indices are linearly independent by Mathlib's
     `RootPairing.linearIndepOn_root_baseOf`. Reducedness is not used.
   - Basis and rank: `Module.Basis.mk` turns independence and spanning into
     `HasRelativeRootSystem.simpleRelativeRootBasis`. Its application lemma
     identifies each basis vector with `simpleRelativeRootVector i ν α`.
     `Module.finrank_eq_nat_card_basis` then gives
     `relativeRootRank i = Nat.card (simpleRelativeRoots i ν)`.

`lean_check` reports that the theorem depends only on the axioms `propext`,
`Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

## Provenance

The proof is derived and internal: it only composes published Library theorems
with Mathlib linear algebra. The statement's own Markdown gives the source
locators in Borel–Tits (1965) and Renard.

The context of the official Milne errata entry for p.549 is preserved. The
structural input is the published unconditional `rootRankOneFactorization`,
not a citation of the incomplete classical proofs of the relative root system.
According to the published Markdown of the modules above, the reflection
representatives centralize the full, possibly nonsmooth, kernel of each root,
as in Conrad–Gabber–Prasad (2015), C.2.15. This module adds no new structural
argument.
