# An open of strongly regular points over a field

Start with the nonempty open of strongly regular points after extension to an
algebraic closure. Projection to the original group is open. The fibre of this
projection over a rational point consists of the unique point of its
scalar-extended section, which lies in the geometric open whenever the original
point lies in its image. Reflection of a maximal-torus centralizer along the
field extension gives the conclusion over the original field.
