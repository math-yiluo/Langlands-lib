# Norm descent for a quadratic norm-one central defect

Let a central isogeny over a number field `F` have kernel identified
with the `n`-torsion of the Weil norm kernel for a quadratic field
extension `B/F`. Take an everywhere locally trivial natural cocycle
with central lift data on a finite Galois cover `L/F` containing `B`.
Suppose a tensor-unit cochain killed by `n` has differential equal to
the tensor-unit defect. Suppose its norm has scalar Kummer radicand
`s ∈ Fˣ`, with the radical and the norm cocycle on `L/F`.
`numberFieldQuadraticNormOneRadicalDescent` shows that `s` is a norm
from `B` modulo `n`-th powers from `F`.

At a completion whose degree is one, the local norm modulo powers
condition follows from coprimality. At a completion whose degree is
two, the local field is identified with the full scalar-extension
tensor. The local trivialization of the projected cocycle supplies
an actual kernel-valued primitive after a finite Galois refinement.
Its tensor coordinates have norm one and `n`-th power one. Comparison
with the transferred original primitive gives the local norm modulo
powers condition. The finite coefficient extension is obtained from
a place of `L` above the selected place of `B` at finite places. At
infinite places the algebraic closure of the base completion is finite.
Quadratic norm modulo powers descent gives the global condition.
