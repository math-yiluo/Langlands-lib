# The stabilizer of an integral cocharacter

This internal helper concerns the actual character and cocharacter lattices of
a maximal split torus in a connected reductive group over an arbitrary field.
The relative Weyl group is the published quotient of rational normalizer
points by rational centralizer points.

`mem_rootSubsetWeylGroup_zero_iff_cocharacter_fixed` identifies the stabilizer
of an integral cocharacter with the subgroup generated by the published
integral root reflections in the relative roots having zero pairing with it.
The relative roots may be nonreduced, and no positive-rank assumption is made.
`WeylCocharacterStabilizerStatement` packages this equivalence uniformly, and
`weylCocharacterStabilizer` proves that proposition.

The implementation first shows that reflections in annihilating characters
preserve every pairing with the cocharacter; subgroup closure preserves this
property. In the other direction, a generic refinement of the given
cocharacter supplies a relative Coxeter system. Strong induction on Coxeter
length chooses a right descent. The descent root is positive and its Weyl
translate is negative for the refinement. Refinement and pairing preservation
therefore force its pairing with the original cocharacter to vanish. The
simple reflection lies in the required subgroup, and multiplication by it
reduces the length. The Coxeter reflection formula initially uses an integral
linear functional on the character lattice. Perfectness of the actual split
torus pairing represents this functional by an integral cocharacter, giving
exactly `IsRootReflection`.

Finally, invariance of the character pairings is equivalent to invariance of
the integral cocharacter by the published equivariant pairing and its
perfectness.

This is a derived internal consequence of the published relative Coxeter,
generic-refinement and lattice-pairing results. It does not assert an
intersection formula for closed root subsets or for their generated groups.
