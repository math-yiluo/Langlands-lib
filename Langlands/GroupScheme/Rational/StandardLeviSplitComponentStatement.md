# Split components in the actual standard Levi representatives

`Langlands.GroupScheme.StandardLeviSplitComponentStatement` states the structural realization needed to use the actual standard Levi family in rational and Bruhat–Tits constructions. For a connected reductive group over any field, a maximal split torus `i : T → G`, a generic cocharacter `ν` and every subset `J` of the associated simple relative roots, the specific group scheme `Standard.leviScheme i ν J` is connected reductive. It has a split component `a : A → Standard.leviScheme i ν J` and a map `g : A → T` whose composite with `i` equals `a` followed by the specific inclusion `Standard.leviHom i ν J`.

The quantifiers use the same maximal split torus for every index. They supply existence for each index; they do not choose a family of split components or assert compatibility of choices between different indices. The existing `StandardRepresentability` construction identifies this specific Levi representative with the standard subgroup functor on every test scheme. The target is a Prop-valued declaration and is not proved in this file.

The implemented theorem `standardLeviSplitComponent_of_structural_statements` is a conditional reduction to the already declared `StandardCocharacterStatement`, `SplitComponentStatement` and `LeviSplitComponentStatement`. The first provides a standard cocharacter and identifies its dynamic centralizer with the standard Levi functor. The existing all-test representative and closedness proofs make the chosen standard Levi a valid input to the generic Levi statement. The split-component statement supplies a split component of the ambient group, and the generic Levi statement then supplies the required split component and map into the same torus. The reduction discards the additional ambient-component factorization and character-restriction equation; it establishes none of its three premises.

This is an internal derived specialization of the existing structural targets to the newly constructed representatives. It adds no external structure assertion beyond their stated conclusions and preserves arbitrary fields and universe `u`, including nonsmooth centres. Independent proof of the target remains necessary; a conditional reduction is not its proof.
