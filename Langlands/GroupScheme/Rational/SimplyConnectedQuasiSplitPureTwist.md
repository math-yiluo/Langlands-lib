# Pure inner cocycles with quasi-split twist

A Borel subgroup of a simply connected group has an induced maximal torus.
A cocharacter of that torus presents the Borel as a dynamic parabolic and
its torus as the dynamic centralizer. The Levi retraction is split with a
smooth connected unipotent kernel. Over a perfect field the kernel has a
split unipotent series. Additive cocycle vanishing along that series and
Hilbert 90 for the induced torus give vanishing in the Borel.

For a pure inner twist between two quasi-split groups, choose rational
Borels on both sides. Over an algebraic closure of the original covering
field, conjugate the image of the first Borel to the second. The adjusted
local isomorphism preserves the two Borel ranges on all test schemes.
Parabolic self-normalization lifts its discrepancy to a cocycle in the
source Borel. Triviality there yields a coboundary on the original cover
by faithfully flat descent. This is the quasi-split case used in the
trialitarian argument of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and
Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, p. 373.
