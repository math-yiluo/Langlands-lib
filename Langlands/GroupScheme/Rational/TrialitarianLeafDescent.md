# Descent of the common Levi for three trialitarian leaf types

`TitsIndexData.exists_leafLevi_descent` applies cyclic triple descent
to a simply connected group of type `D₄`. The inputs include a split
torus over the cyclic cubic field, a comparison of its root data and
parabolic transports with the absolute pinning, and a lifted generator
cycling the three leaves. The resulting closed connected reductive
subgroup over the base has scalar extension rationally conjugate to
the dynamic centralizer of the difference of the first two leaf
cocharacters.

In the pinned root datum the integral leaf cocharacters sum to zero.
Every root takes a negative value on exactly one of them or vanishes
on all three. The zero roots of the first difference are precisely
the common zero roots. The comparison of root evaluations carries
these assertions to the split torus over the cubic field. A based
diagram automorphism sends each leaf cocharacter to the corresponding
Weyl orbit. Combining this with the Weyl correction from the ordinary
Galois action to the star action supplies the parabolic conjugacies
for the generator and its square. The descent theorem then applies.

This realizes the triple intersection of leaf parabolics used in
Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994),
§6.8, printed p. 372.
