# Descent of the inverse Gram form of a Hermitian cocycle

`HermitianGaloisGramDescentStatement` starts with an actual Hermitian matrix presentation over a separable quadratic extension of an infinite field. For a cocycle on a finite Galois field cover, `exists_galoisGramDescent` gives a primitive in the ambient matrix-algebra unit group and a nonsingular descended inverse Gram form. The primitive lives on the original cover.

The affine matrix coordinates give an injective group homomorphism into the finite-algebra units on every affine test. The isometry condition in column convention agrees with row congruence of the inverse Gram matrix. The ordinary crossed cocycle of tensor units is a coboundary by finite-algebra Hilbert 90. Transforming the inverse Gram matrix by the inverse primitive gives a fixed tensor. Its inverse is also fixed. The two tensors descend to inverse matrices over the quadratic field, and equivariance of the adjoint descends the Hermitian symmetry.

This is the matrix presentation of the preliminary norm-and-Gram reduction for special unitary cohomology in Kneser, *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups*, §5.5. The Lean reduction retains the original finite cover and extends the entire tensor algebra.
