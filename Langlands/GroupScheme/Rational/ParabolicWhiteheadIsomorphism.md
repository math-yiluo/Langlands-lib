# Actual isomorphisms on rational points and parabolic Whitehead quotients

This internal construction works with a specified group-scheme isomorphism over
the ground ring. `rationalPoints.mapEquiv` uses its actual rational-point map and
the map of the inverse; the category inverse identities prove that these maps
are inverse multiplicative equivalences. This part permits any commutative
ring in arbitrary universe `u`.

Over a field, the existing `parabolicElementarySubgroup_map_eq` proves that this
point equivalence carries the subgroup generated by all actual rational
parabolic radical images onto the corresponding subgroup in the target.
`parabolicWhiteheadQuotientCongr` is the quotient multiplicative equivalence
constructed from precisely that equality by Mathlib's `QuotientGroup.congr`.
Its formula on the canonical quotient maps is definitional. Surjectivity of
the canonical map proves that the construction preserves identity and
composition, by checking those equalities on images of actual rational points.

The existing quotient-triviality criterion and this equivalence prove
`parabolicElementarySubgroup_eq_top_iff_iso`: generation by actual rational
parabolic radicals is preserved and reflected by a specified ground-field
isomorphism. No classification or existence of a group-scheme isomorphism is
asserted. The construction is derived from the imported actual-point maps,
the already proved transport of actual parabolic radicals, and the standard
group-quotient construction; it has no external source premise.
