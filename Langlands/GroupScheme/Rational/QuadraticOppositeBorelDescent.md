# Descent of the intersection of quadratic conjugate Borels

Suppose a Borel and its conjugate by the nonidentity automorphism of a
quadratic extension intersect in a maximal torus over the extension.
Coefficient transport preserves the intersection as a subgroup functor.
The nonidentity automorphism exchanges the two Borel images and the
identity fixes each. Finite Galois descent of the closed subgroup
therefore gives an actual inclusion over the base field. The torus
property and maximality reflect along the extension, and the recovery
isomorphism gives the exact intersection equality on all test schemes.

This is the descent step of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and
Number Theory* (1994), Lemma 6.17, printed pp. 329–330.
