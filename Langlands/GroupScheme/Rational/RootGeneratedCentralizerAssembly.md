# Covering and kernel assembly for the root-generated centralizer

Authors: Worker 1325 and Worker 1746. The included earlier normalizer-separation
composition credits Worker 1226.

This internal supporting unit composes actual root-generated Bruhat
covering with the actual lift-generated normalizer kernel computation.
It proves the fixed-data centralizer inclusion under explicit structural
inputs. It does not prove the unconditional
`RootGeneratedCentralizerRankOneStatement`.

Write `H0 = RelativeRoot.rankOneCentralizerGeneratedPoints i`,
`L_a = RelativeRoot.oppositeGeneratedPoints i a`, and
`M = RelativeRoot.rankOneLiftPoints i m`. The field and universe are
arbitrary. The setup has an affine group scheme and a torus map whose
underlying scheme map is a monomorphism.

Four elementary lemmas identify the ambient-point behavior of `M`.
Every point of `H0` lies in the normalizer, so it gives a point of the
normalizer subgroup generated by `H0` and the lifts and hence lies in
`M`. Every point of `M` remains in the normalizer. Each lift belongs to
`M`. Finally `M ≤ ⟨H0, range (fun a => (m a).val)⟩`. The last proof
uses the subgroup map/comap correspondence: both the `H0` part and the
lift generators land in the stated ambient closure.

`HasRootGeneratedSimpleReflectionLifts i ν cs` is an internal input
predicate. It asserts existence of an actual normalizer-point family `m`,
indexed by the simple relative roots, whose Weyl classes are the chosen
Coxeter simple generators, whose points lie in their corresponding `L_a`,
and whose images in `N(k)/H0` satisfy all the Coxeter relations. This
unit does not prove that this input exists.

`hasRootGeneratedBruhatCovering_rankOneLiftPoints` assumes splitness of
the torus, an actual relative-root-system realization, a generic
cocharacter, simple-root unipotent factorization, actual opposite-pair
existence, a Coxeter system, and actual-reflection behavior of its simple
generators. Given lifts in `L_a` with the correct Weyl classes, it proves
the actual root-generated group is contained in `U(ν)(k) M U(ν)(k)`.
The four elementary lemmas supply the subgroup premises of the earlier
covering assembly. Each `Z(k) ∩ L_a` belongs to `H0` by its definition.
The lift class equality supplies the actual reflection condition. No
lifted Coxeter relation is needed for this covering theorem.

`centralizer_inf_generatedPoints_le_of_simpleLifts` additionally assumes
connected reductivity of `G`, maximal splitness of `i`, and
`HasRootGeneratedSimpleReflectionLifts i ν cs`. With all the preceding
structural premises retained, it concludes
`centralizerPoints i ⊓ relativeRootGeneratedSubgroup i ≤ H0`. The covering theorem
supplies the double-coset inclusion. Normalizer containment of `M` permits
application of the unconditional generic-reductive normalizer separation.
The lifted Coxeter relations give `Z(k) ∩ M ≤ H0` by
`centralizer_inf_rankOneLiftPoints_le`. The earlier extraction lemma
therefore proves the desired inclusion.

`centralizer_inf_generatedPoints_le_of_orderedRootPoints` replaces the
simple-root unipotent factorization premise by
`HasOrderedRootPointFactorization i ν`. It retains connected reductivity,
maximal splitness, genericity, actual root-system realization, opposite-pair
existence, the Coxeter system and its actual simple-reflection behavior,
and the lift predicate. The root-system realization supplies negation
stability, finiteness and linear independence of simple roots, so the
earlier ordered-product argument proves the unipotent factorization.
