# Factors of other components act trivially

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.EvenChainLocality`. All results hold over an arbitrary
field `k`, for an affine group scheme `G` with a supplied split pinning `p` and an actual
matrix representation `ρ`.

## Definition

- `SplitPinning.rootSup p r`: the subgroup of rational points of `G` generated by the rational
  points of the root subgroups of `α_r` and `-α_r` (the supremum of the two subgroups
  `RelativeRoot.subgroup p.inclusion (± α_r)` at the rational test object). It abbreviates the
  subgroup already used in `EvenChainData.nAlpha_mem` and in
  `NormalizedRootParametrization.reflectionRepresentative_mem_generated`.

## General triviality criteria

- `corootRationalPoint_mem_rootSup`: every rational point `α_r^∨(t)` of the coroot lies in
  `rootSup p r`. Proof: for a linked root parametrization (`nonempty_linkedRootParametrization`)
  the rank-one relation `x_+(a) x_-(b) = x_-(b') α_r^∨(u) x_+(a')` holds whenever
  `u = 1 + ab` is a unit, `a' = u⁻¹ a` and `b' = u⁻¹ b`. Taking `a = 1` and `b = u - 1` with
  `u` the unit of `t` expresses `α_r^∨(t)` as a product of root-subgroup points.
- `rationalMatrix_eq_one_of_isRepresentedBy`: let `ρ` have a complete weight decomposition and
  let `y` be an additive map, equivariant for a character `δ`, representing a subgroup. If for
  every weight `ξ` of `ρ` and every `m ≥ 1` the weight `ξ + m δ` is not a weight, then every
  rational point of the subgroup acts as the identity. Proof: the divided powers of `y` map the
  weight space of `ξ` into that of `ξ + m δ`
  (`dividedPower_mem_representationWeightSpace_of_equivariant`), so they vanish on every weight
  space and hence everywhere; `rationalMatrix_mulVec_eq_self_of_isRepresentedBy` then fixes
  every vector.
- `rationalMatrix_eq_one_of_mem_rootSup`: if every weight of `ρ` is orthogonal to the coroot of
  a simple root `z` and `⟨α_r, z^∨⟩ ≠ 0`, then `rootSup p r` acts trivially. Pairing with
  `z^∨` shows that `ξ ± m α_r` is never a weight, and the previous criterion applies to both
  root subgroups.
- `pairing_eq_zero_of_isHighestWeight` (weights stay in their components): let `ρ` be a
  highest-weight representation at `lam` and `Z` a set of simple roots such that
  `⟨α_w, z^∨⟩ = 0` for all `z ∈ Z` and simple `w ∉ Z`. If `lam` is orthogonal to the coroots
  of `Z`, so is every weight of `ρ`. Proof by induction on the `Z`-height of `lam - ξ`: write
  `lam - ξ = ∑ c_w α_w` with `c_w ≥ 0` and let `D` be its `Z`-part, so
  `⟨ξ, z^∨⟩ = -⟨D, z^∨⟩` for `z ∈ Z`. If some such pairing is nonzero then `D ≠ 0`, so the
  canonical form satisfies `B(D, D) > 0`; since
  `2 B(D, D) = ∑_{w ∈ Z} c_w B(α_w, α_w) ⟨D, w^∨⟩`, some `z ∈ Z` has `⟨ξ, z^∨⟩ < 0`. The simple
  reflection `s_z ξ` is again a weight (`finrank_representationWeightSpace_simpleReflection`)
  with strictly smaller `Z`-height, so by induction `⟨s_z ξ, z^∨⟩ = 0`, contradicting
  `⟨s_z ξ, z^∨⟩ = -⟨ξ, z^∨⟩ > 0`.

## Even-path data

For even-path data `C` and normalized root maps `x`:

- `EvenChainData.pairing_b_a`, `EvenChainData.pairing_alpha_a`: `⟨α_b, a^∨⟩ = -1` and
  `⟨α_α, a^∨⟩ = 1` for the merged pair `a, b` and merged root `α = a + b` of each path.
- `EvenChainData.rationalMatrix_block_eq_one`: if all weights of `ρ` are orthogonal to the
  coroot of the first merged simple root `a` of the path `c`, then every block of `c`
  (`EvenChainData.block`, either pattern value) acts as the identity: each factor
  (`u_a`, `u_b`, `x_α(s)`, the merged representative, `α^∨(t)`) lies in `rootSup` of a root with
  nonzero pairing against `a^∨`.
- `rationalMatrix_rootFactor_eq_one`: if all weights of `ρ` are orthogonal to `w^∨`, the factor
  `x_w(d) n_w` with the canonical representative acts as the identity.
- Representations at the end vertex of a path `c` (highest weight path-fundamental for
  `C.path c`): their weights are the path weights, orthogonal to the coroots off the path
  (`EvenChainData.pairing_eq_zero_of_not_mem_path`), so the blocks of all other paths
  (`EvenChainData.rationalMatrix_block_eq_one_of_ne`, using disjointness of the paths) and the
  factors `x_w(d) n_w` of simple roots off `C.path c`
  (`EvenChainData.rationalMatrix_rootFactor_eq_one_of_not_mem`) act trivially.
- Representations whose highest weight is orthogonal to the coroots of all path vertices, for
  instance fundamental representations at simple roots off all paths
  (`EvenChainData.fundamental_pairing_path_eq_zero`): by the component lemma with `Z` the set
  of path vertices (`EvenChainData.pairing_eq_zero_of_offPath`), all blocks
  (`EvenChainData.rationalMatrix_block_eq_one_of_offPath`) and all factors `x_w(d) n_w` of path
  vertices (`EvenChainData.rationalMatrix_rootFactor_eq_one_of_offPath`) act trivially.

## Provenance

This is the locality step (route step 3) of the task context of Task 5373, an internal
formalization device: in Steinberg, *Regular elements of semi-simple algebraic groups* (1965),
source `steinberg-1965-regular-elements-semisimple-groups`, the corresponding observations are
used without separate statement (e.g. step 4 of the proof of 9.16, printed p. 76, where factors
orthogonal to the relevant weights act trivially on `V_ω`). The component lemma adapts the
argument of `RootDatumPathWeights.exists_eq_pathWeight`.
