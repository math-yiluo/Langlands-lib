# The pullback of a quadratic cover at a real place

The sum of the upper completion degrees is two. An upper complex place has
local degree two and is therefore unique. The completed tensor-product
equivalence identifies the pullback with the field cover of the upper
completion. The projection to the original cover is induced by the original
coefficient inclusion. Transporting the coefficient automorphism through
this equivalence extends its action to the upper completion.

An upper real place has local degree one, so its completion identifies with
the lower completion. The resulting map from the original coefficient field
into the lower completion gives a section of the pulled-back cover. Evaluation
along the section gives a coboundary cochain for any cocycle on that cover.
