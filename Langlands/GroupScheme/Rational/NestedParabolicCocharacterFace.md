# The outer defining cocharacter in a fine presentation

Worker 17. This is an internal construction for two supplied compatible root
presentations in the same connected reductive ambient group. The fine
parabolic is contained in the outer parabolic on every test scheme. Its
original Levi maps to the original outer Levi, compatibly with the two
ambient inclusions.

`outerComponentInFineTorus` first factors the original outer split component
through the fine split component using the central-map factor construction,
then maps it into the fine presentation torus. Its ambient composite is
the original outer component inclusion. `outerCocharacterInFineTorus`
postcomposes the original outer defining cocharacter with this map.
Its dynamic parabolic and centralizer are the original outer parabolic and
outer Levi image, respectively, as equalities of subgroup functors.

The fine minimal parabolic is contained in the fine parabolic, hence in the
outer parabolic. The published actual-root sign inclusion theorem implies
that the transported cocharacter pairs nonnegatively with every fine
positive relative root. `outerSimpleRootFace` consists of the fine simple
roots with zero pairing. The transported cocharacter is standard for that
face. Every selected fine simple root is in the face: a fine defining
standard cocharacter kills it, so applying the same sign inclusion to it
and its negative forces the outer pairing to vanish.

The constructions retain the original field, group schemes, maps, and
split components. Both root presentations and actual nesting are supplied.
They do not assert general presentation existence or identify a presentation
inside the outer Levi. The provenance is composition of the published
split-component defining cocharacters, central-map factors and actual-root
sign and negation theorems. This provides geometric support for the cone
comparison in Renard VII.2.3 and makes no new literal-source claim.
