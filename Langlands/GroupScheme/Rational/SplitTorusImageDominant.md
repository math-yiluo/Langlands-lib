# Scheme-theoretic dominance in the split-torus image factorization

Authors: Worker 1479 and Worker 228. The construction and assembly adapt Worker 228's published proof in `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.SplitTorusImage`; Worker 1479 retains and proves schematic dominance of the factor map.

`SplitTorusImage.DominantFactorization φ` packages an actual commutative image group scheme, a homomorphism `factor` from the source, and a homomorphism `inclusion` into the target. It records that `inclusion` is a closed split subtorus, that `factor ≫ inclusion = φ`, that the underlying scheme map of `factor` is scheme-theoretically dominant, and that centrality of `φ` implies centrality of `inclusion`. Here schematic dominance means that the map's kernel ideal sheaf is zero. It is not a claim of surjectivity on points of every test scheme.

For an arbitrary universe `u`, any field `k : Type u`, any commutative group scheme `D` over `Spec k` satisfying `IsSplitTorus D.toGrp`, any affine group scheme `H` over `Spec k`, and any homomorphism `φ : D.toGrp ⟶ H`, `IsSplitTorus.dominantFactorization` constructs these data. The supporting theorem `IsSplitTorus.exists_dominant_factor_isSplitSubtorus` proves their existence. There is no characteristic, positive-rank, smoothness, connectedness, or injectivity premise on `φ`. The trivial source/image is included.

The proof uses the same image character lattice and closed embedding as the published image construction. After presenting `D` by a group algebra and `H` by affine coordinates, it takes the lattice of exponents occurring in the classifying map's range. The existing image theorem's coefficient-extraction argument proves that the corestriction onto the group algebra of this lattice is surjective, giving the closed embedding into `H`. Its lattice is free of finite rank, giving a split torus. The existing commutation argument supplies the centrality implication.

The additional dominance proof computes the classifying map of the factor: it is `latticeIncl`, the injective inclusion of the image lattice's group algebra into the original group algebra. The published `isSchemeTheoreticallyDominant_specMap` applies to this injective ring map, and composition with the source presentation isomorphisms preserves schematic dominance. The chosen-data definition then uses classical choice on the proved existence theorem.

This is an internal refinement of the Library's construction, using Mathlib's scheme-theoretic dominance interface. It is intended for the closed-subgroup and all-test centralizer comparisons needed by `CentralizerBorelIntersectionStatement`. This module does not assert that centralizer comparison or prove the Borel-intersection statement. No published target or imported pair is modified.
