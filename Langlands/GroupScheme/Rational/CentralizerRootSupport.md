# Centralizer comparison from actual zero-root support

This internal derived module compares the literal dynamic centralizer subgroup functors on all test schemes. Its two comparison theorems explicitly assume `DynamicConnectedStatement`; they do not prove that proposition or the unconditional twisted standard-Levi intersection statement.

Both comparison theorems work over an arbitrary field `k : Type u`. The ambient group scheme `G` is smooth, affine and connected; `T` is split, and `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G` is an arbitrary homomorphism. The single-centralizer comparison assumes that every actual relative root vanishing on the first cocharacter also vanishes on the second. The intersection comparison assumes that every actual relative root vanishing on both input cocharacters vanishes on the output cocharacter. Each theorem also takes `DynamicConnectedStatement.{u}` explicitly. Ambient connectedness is a separate hypothesis: the connectedness of the represented centralizer is derived from it by applying that statement.

The definition `cocharacterCentralizerIntersection i zeta eta` is the infimum of the dynamic centralizers of the two cocharacters mapped into the same ambient group through `i`. Its membership lemma identifies its points on every test scheme with simultaneous membership in the two centralizers. It names the subgroup to which the intersection comparison applies.

The intersection definition and its membership lemma, as well as the represented-subgroup centrality equivalence below, work over an arbitrary base scheme with a commutative source group scheme. Over a field, `relativeRoots_subset_of_factor` assumes that both the ambient and represented group schemes are affine, with no local-finite-type assumption. The root-pairing vanishing lemma assumes that the represented group scheme is affine and locally of finite type; it imposes no affineness hypothesis on the ambient group.

First, `isCentral_comp_iff_le_dynamicCentralizer` identifies containment of a represented subgroup in an ambient cocharacter centralizer with centrality of the lifted cocharacter inside that subgroup. Monomorphic functoriality of dynamic centralizers and the range/comap equivalence give the assertion on every test scheme. `relativeRoots_subset_of_factor` applies the injective actual differential to embed the subgroup's roots in the ambient roots. `relativeRoot_pairing_eq_zero_of_le_dynamicCentralizer` then restricts each such root along the lifted central cocharacter, using `root_restrict_eq_zero_of_isCentral`.

For `dynamicCentralizer_le_of_root_zero_imp`, the first centralizer is represented by the published affine closed representative. The proved theorem `dynamicSmooth` makes it smooth; the stated connectedness input makes it connected. The ambient split torus factors through it because its points commute with the cocharacter. Every actual root of this representative is an ambient root annihilated by the first cocharacter, hence is annihilated by the second cocharacter under the rootwise premise. The proved theorem `rootAnnihilatorCentrality` makes the second cocharacter central in this representative, which is precisely the required containment.

For `dynamicCentralizer_inf_le_of_root_zero_imp`, work inside that first centralizer, apply the single-centralizer comparison to the remaining two lifted cocharacters, and transfer its containment through the monomorphic inclusion. This uses the simultaneous zero conditions on actual roots rather than an intersection identity between their rational spans. The proof imposes no reductivity, maximality, characteristic, reduced-root-system or reduced-test-scheme restriction.

All constructions and arguments here are internal consequences of the named published interfaces. The only unproved structural proposition retained as a theorem parameter is `DynamicConnectedStatement`; `rootAnnihilatorCentrality` itself uses the published proofs `dynamicLie`, `dynamicSmooth` and `lieSubgroupEquality` through `RootAnnihilatorCentralityProof`.
