# Contraction from positive cyclic action support

This internal module defines `AutomorphicAction.IsContractingAlongCharacter b a`: the actual action `b` is restriction along the specified character `a` of a multiplicative-group action whose identity-limit subgroup functor is the entire group on every test scheme.

For a smooth connected affine group over any field, a split acting torus and any nonzero character, positive cyclic support of the full tangent space implies this property. The proof converts support containment into fullness of the first cyclic Lie span using the intrinsic split-torus decomposition. Every positive natural multiple is an integer multiple, so the integral action-weight span is full. The actual character kernel therefore acts trivially by the published geometric rigidity theorem, including its possible infinitesimal part. The fpqc character theorem descends the action to the multiplicative group. The exact character weight restriction formula makes the descended positive span full, and positive-weight rigidity gives contraction of every point.

The proof uses internal consequences of the actual action, tangent and descent constructions. It retains arbitrary field universes and nonprimitive characters. It does not assert the cyclic-root comparison or unconditional dynamic connectedness.

The transport lemma `map_unipotent_le_subgroup_of_character_equivariant` works over an arbitrary base. An equivariant homomorphism, with equivariance along the specified character, sends a multiplicative-group orbit extension to a character-orbit extension by composition. Composition also preserves its identity value at zero. This proves containment in the intrinsic root functor on every test scheme.

For each smooth connected closed invariant generator of the first cyclic semigroup, the represented-subgroup action transports its ambient tangent support to the full support of the representative. The contraction theorem then supplies a descended action with full identity-limit functor. The transport lemma places the representative's entire image in the intrinsic root functor. The defining closure universal property proves `weightFiltration_one_le_subgroup`. This containment needs affineness of the ambient group but does not require its smoothness or connectedness; smoothness and connectedness of each generator are part of the actual semigroup definition. The reverse containment remains outside this unit.
