# Simple root subgroups and their opposites generate every root subgroup

Authors: Worker 180.

`Langlands.GroupScheme.SimpleRootSubgroupGenerationStatement` is an
unproved proposition at arbitrary universe `u`. This module states it and
proves nothing.

## The proposition

For every field `k : Type u`, every group scheme `G` over
`Spec (CommRingCat.of k)` with `hG : IsConnectedReductive G`, every
commutative group scheme `S` and `i : S.toGrp ⟶ G` with
`IsMaximalSplitTorus i`, every cocharacter `ν : cocharacterLattice S` with
`IsGenericCocharacter i ν`, and every subgroup functor `F` of `G`: if

```
RelativeRoot.subgroup i α ≤ F  and  RelativeRoot.subgroup i (-α) ≤ F
```

for every `α ∈ simpleRelativeRoots i ν`, then
`RelativeRoot.subgroup i a ≤ F` for every `a ∈ relativeRoots i`.

The affineness instance needed by `IsGenericCocharacter`,
`simpleRelativeRoots` and `relativeRoots` is `hG.isAffineHom`.

## Exact objects

- `IsMaximalSplitTorus i` (module `Rational.SplitTorus`): `S` is a split
  torus, `i` is a closed subgroup, and every factorization of `i` through a
  split subtorus is an isomorphism.
- `relativeRoots i` (module `Rational.RelativeRoots`): the nonzero
  characters of `S` with a nonzero weight space in the Lie algebra of `G`.
  The set may be nonreduced: both `a` and `2a` may be relative roots.
- `IsGenericCocharacter i ν`: `ν` pairs nontrivially with every relative
  root.
- `simpleRelativeRoots i ν` (module `Rational.StandardParabolic`): the
  relative roots pairing positively with `ν` that are not the sum of two
  such roots. In a nonreduced system this excludes `2α`.
- `RelativeRoot.subgroup i a` (module `Rational.RootSubgroup`): the subgroup
  functor of points `g`, on every test scheme, whose torus-conjugation orbit
  `s ↦ i(s) g i(s)⁻¹` factors as `s ↦ e(a(s))` through a morphism `e` from
  the affine line with `e(0) = 1`. Its points contain the points of
  `RelativeRoot.subgroup i (2 • a)`, since `s ↦ e(a(s)²)` factors through
  `t ↦ e(t²)`.
- `F` is an arbitrary subgroup functor (module `SubgroupFunctor`): a
  subgroup of the points on every test scheme, stable under restriction.
  No closedness or representability of `F` is assumed. The containments
  `≤` are containments on every test scheme.

## Source and derivation

The proposition is derived; it is not a numbered result of one source.

A. Borel and J. Tits, *Groupes réductifs*, Publ. Math. IHÉS 27 (1965),
registered as `borel-tits-1965-groupes-reductifs` (printed page = PDF
page + 53):

- 5.3, printed pp.96–97 / PDF pp.43–44: the relative Weyl group
  `^kW = N(S)(k)/Z(S)(k)` is generated by the reflections in the
  hyperplanes annihilating the `k`-roots, and each such reflection has a
  rational representative;
- 5.8, printed p.98 / PDF p.45: the relative roots `^kΦ` form a root
  system (possibly nonreduced) in the sense of 2.1, with Weyl group `^kW`;
- 2.1, printed p.62 / PDF p.9: the Weyl group of a root system is generated
  by the reflections in its roots.

For a root system with a base `Δ`, the Weyl group is generated by the
reflections `s_α`, `α ∈ Δ`, and every indivisible root is the image of a
simple root under the Weyl group (standard facts on root systems, applied
to the reduced system of indivisible roots, which has the same Weyl group
and base). Each reflection `s_α` is represented by a rational point
`n_α = u′ · u · u′′` of the normalizer of `S` with `u` a nonidentity point
of `U_α(k)` and `u′, u′′ ∈ U_{-α}(k)`: this is Conrad–Gabber–Prasad,
*Pseudo-reductive groups* (2015), Proposition C.2.24, recorded in the
Library as `RelativeRoot.HasRankOneFactorization` with the published proof
`rootRankOneFactorization`. Conjugation by a normalizing point `n` carries
`U_a` onto `U_{w(a)}`, where `w` is the class of `n` (in the Library,
`RelativeRoot.normalizer_conj_mem_iff`). Since `n_α` lies in `F(k)`, every
root subgroup `U_{w(α)}`, `w ∈ ^kW`, `α ∈ Δ`, lies in `F`; this covers all
indivisible roots, and the multiple `2a` of an indivisible root is covered
by `U_{2a} ⊆ U_a`.

The proposition records exactly this consequence for the Library's actual
root subgroup functors. It makes no splitness, perfectness or
characteristic assumption on `k`, allows a nonreduced relative root
system and an empty set of roots, and assumes nothing about `F` beyond
being a subgroup functor.
