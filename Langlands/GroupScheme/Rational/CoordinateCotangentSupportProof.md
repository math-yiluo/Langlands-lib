# Equality of actual cotangent and Lie weight supports

The theorem `Langlands.GroupScheme.CoordinateWeights.cotangentLieSupport` proves the exact `CotangentLieSupportStatement.{u}` from the actual augmentation construction, at arbitrary universe `u` and in every field characteristic. The source comparison is Conrad–Gabber–Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive Groups*, second edition (2015), §3.3, printed p.107 / PDF p.133, with direct coordinate pullback and the same character labels on cotangent and Lie spaces. The intermediate naturality, character-cancellation and spanning lemmas are derived infrastructure for this comparison.

The actual kernel-to-cotangent-functional equivalence commutes with every coefficient-algebra map by its evaluation on augmentation classes and the existing naturality of actual coordinate derivations. Its surjectivity and separation by vector-space duals show that every nonzero cotangent class pairs nontrivially with an actual kernel vector. Distinct character labels are detected at the universal point of the acting affine group: a nonzero field scalar stays a unit after every coefficient extension and can be cancelled.

The linear-algebra support lemma uses spanning submodule families and a perfect duality, with orthogonality for distinct labels. A nonzero vector whose corresponding weight space on the other side vanished would annihilate every weight summand, hence the whole space. Surjectivity onto the full dual gives the converse argument. This argument only needs spanning, rather than independence of the weight families.

For the actual action, the transpose formula on the base-changed cotangent, coefficient naturality, and the two all-algebra eigenvector equations imply that the ground-field pairing coefficient times either character value is the same over every coefficient algebra. If that coefficient were nonzero, cancellation at the universal affine point would force the labels to agree. Hence distinct labels pair to zero.

The actual intrinsic Lie weight spaces span by `AutomorphicAction.isInternal_lieWeightSpace_of_isSplitTorus`. Cotangent spanning then gives equality of nonzero weight spaces by the general duality argument. `RelativeRoot.actionWeightSpace_eq_lieWeightSpace` and `mem_actionWeightSupport_iff` identify the Lie side with the original intrinsic support of the whole group. `cotangentLieSupport_of_coordinateWeightDecomposition` obtains the cotangent spanning hypothesis from the corresponding component of `CoordinateWeightDecompositionStatement`. Finally, `cotangentLieSupport` applies this reduction to `coordinateWeightDecomposition`.

The exact theorem retains the affine and locally finite type hypotheses on the group and the split-torus hypothesis on the acting commutative group. It compares the actual augmentation quotient with the original Lie-kernel eigenvectors over all coefficient algebras, and requires no smoothness, semigroup realization or commutator conclusion. This module proves the support component of `CoordinateLieComparisonStatement`; the full conjunction is assembled separately.
