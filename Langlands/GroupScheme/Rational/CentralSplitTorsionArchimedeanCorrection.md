# Simultaneous archimedean correction through split torsion

Let `f` be a central isogeny over `F` with a specified `F`-isomorphism
from its kernel to a finite product of roots-of-unity groups with
exponents `n_i`. Given a finite Galois cover `E/F` and supplied radical
equations `z_i^{n_i} = a_i` with `a_i` in `Fˣ` and `z_i` in `Eˣ`,
the crossed coboundaries give a natural cocycle of the actual kernel.
The crossed identities hold after applying each unit character,
and the character coordinates jointly detect the kernel points.

For the local construction, take compatible maps from `K` and `L`
into `M` over `F`, with `L/F` normal and `M/K` finite Galois and
algebraically closed. Triviality of the projected cocycle on the
full pullback `Spec(K ⊗[F] L)` gives a trivializing point over `M`.
Surjectivity of the central isogeny on these points gives a source
cochain and a crossed cocycle in the actual kernel. Hilbert 90 in
each coordinate supplies its Kummer radical and a unit of `K`.

For simultaneous correction, let `F` be a number field, `L/F` the
starting finite Galois cover, and every `n_i` positive. Use the
algebraic closure of each infinite completion as the finite Galois
coefficient field for the local construction. Approximate the local
power classes in each coordinate across the infinite places by a
unit of `F`. Adjoin all the required radicals in one finite Galois
refinement `E/F` of `L/F`. At each place compare the global and local
radicals in the same local closure. Each power ratio comparison
gives a root of unity. Their family defines a single point of the
actual kernel whose coboundary compares the localized global cocycle
with the local correction. The source corrected by this one global
kernel cocycle is trivial on `Spec(F_v ⊗[F] E)` at every infinite place.

This internal construction uses weak approximation and the finite
family of radical equations to obtain one kernel correction for
every infinite place.
