# Factorization through a quadratic centralizer

A point centralizer that becomes a maximal torus over an extension is already a
maximal torus over the original field. The proof compares the base change of the
closed representative of the centralizer with the supplied geometric torus. The
subgroup-functor equality gives the comparison isomorphism, and maximality reflects
along field extension.

For a cocycle on a quadratic Galois cover, the value at the nonidentity automorphism
is carried to its inverse by that automorphism. Its centralizer is therefore stable
under descent. If this centralizer is a maximal torus, the descended inclusion
contains both Galois values. The finite Galois overlap decomposition then factors
the cocycle through that inclusion on every test scheme.
