Here `complexTorus T` and `complexHom i` are local notations for the base changes
`(CommGroupScheme.baseChange b).obj T` and `(GroupScheme.baseChange b).map i` along
`b = algebraBaseMap ℝ ℂ`. In `fixedSimpleCoroot_neg_one_mem` the group `G` is implicit and has
the same instance arguments `[IsAffine G.X.left] [IsAffineHom G.X.hom]`.

The first declaration is a proved definition. Its type is a proposition, and the `defProp`
linter is switched off for this one declaration, following the convention for theorem-like
definition records. Its axioms are `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. Its value
is `fixedSimpleCoroot_neg_one_mem` with its arguments rearranged into the displayed
quantifier order. The proposition is written out in full, so the module adds no new
predicate. A connectedness argument that needs this condition for every conjugation-fixed
simple coroot can use the definition directly as a proof.

**Statement in words.** Let `G` be a connected reductive group over `ℝ` with affine total
space. Let `i : T → G` be a maximal torus defined over `ℝ` whose complexification `T_ℂ`
splits. Let `P` be a reduced root datum of `(G_ℂ, T_ℂ)` indexed by a finite type `J`
(`IsRootDatumOf`: its roots are the actual roots of `T_ℂ` in `G_ℂ`, with the actual
character and cocharacter lattices and pairing). Let `B` be a base of `P` and `j` a simple
index fixed by the permutation of `J` induced by complex conjugation
(`IsRootDatumOf.galoisRootIndexEquiv` at `Complex.conjAe`). Then the complex point
`α_j^∨(-1) ∈ G(ℂ)` is the scalar extension of a real point of `G` in the identity component
of `G(ℝ)`.

Notation used in the Lean statement:

- `identityComponentPreimage G` is the Library subgroup of the rational points `G(ℝ)` whose
  points lie in the connected component of `1` of `RingPoint ℝ G.X ℝ` for the evaluation
  topology (module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.RealPointTopology`).
- `rationalExtend ℝ ℂ G : G(ℝ) →* G_ℂ(ℂ)` is scalar extension of rational points.
- `cocharacterPoints ℂ (complexTorus T) λ u` is the value `λ(u) ∈ T_ℂ(ℂ)` of a cocharacter
  at a unit `u ∈ ℂˣ`. `rationalPoints.map (complexHom i)` maps it into `G_ℂ(ℂ)`.

Simple connectedness is not used. Only connected reductivity of `G` matters, and smoothness of
`G` follows from it.

Two local instances are used. One is `IsAlgClosure ℝ ℂ`. From it, instance search obtains the
`IsGalois ℝ ℂ` instance that the Galois descent lemmas assume and the Library does not
provide. The other is `IsAffineHom` for the structure morphism of the base change `G_ℂ`.

## Provenance

This is an internal step in the proof of connectedness of `G(ℝ)` for simply connected `G`
(Cartan's theorem, the first conjunct of `Langlands.GroupScheme.RealPointComponentsStatement`).
The mathematics is classical. If a root `α` of a real maximal torus is fixed by complex
conjugation, the root groups `U_{±α}` are defined over `ℝ` and isomorphic to the additive
group. Their real points are connected, and the rank-one identity in the group generated by
`U_{α}` and `U_{-α}` writes `α^∨(t)` as a product of four root-group elements whose
coefficients are rational functions of `t`. For real `t` the coefficients are real. The Lean
proof below follows this argument and states no source theorem, so no literature statement is
claimed to match it.

## Proof

1. **The Galois group.** `complexGalois_eq_one_or_conj`: every `ℝ`-automorphism of `ℂ` is the
   identity or `Complex.conjAe` (Mathlib's `Complex.real_algHom_eq_id_or_conj`).

2. **Fixed root-group points come from the identity component.**
   `mem_map_identityComponentPreimage_of_fixed_rootSubgroup`. Let `ν` be a real cocharacter of
   `T` and `β` a character of `T_ℂ` with `⟨β, ν_ℂ⟩ ≠ 0`. Let `g ∈ G_ℂ(ℂ)` be fixed by every
   `σ ∈ Gal(ℂ/ℝ)` and lie in the relative root subgroup `RelativeRoot.subgroup (complexHom i)
   β`. Then `g` lies in the image of `identityComponentPreimage G`. Proof: put `S = 𝔾_m` and
   `i' = i ∘ ν : S → G`. The character `β ∘ ν_ℂ` of `S_ℂ` descends to a nonzero character `a'`
   of the split torus `S` (`characterLattice.baseChangeEquiv`). The nonvanishing comes from
   `⟨β, ν_ℂ⟩ ≠ 0`. Root subgroups grow under composition with torus homomorphisms
   (`RelativeRoot.subgroup_le_subgroup_comp_torusHom`), so `g` lies in the root group of
   `i'_ℂ` at `a'_ℂ`. Since `g` is Galois-fixed, it is the extension of a real point `g₀` of the
   root group of `i'` at `a'` (`exists_mem_rootGroupPoints_of_fixed`). By the affine-line
   contraction, `g₀` lies in the identity component (`root_mem_connectedComponent_one`).

3. **Galois transport of root points** (section `Transport`). Let `p` be a split pinning of
   `G_ℂ` whose torus is identified with `T_ℂ` compatibly with `complexHom i`. Let `a` be a
   simple root whose character is fixed by `σ` and whose pinning vector is fixed by the
   semilinear Lie action `Lie.baseChangeGaloisEquiv ℝ ℂ G σ`. Then a normalized root
   parametrization `x` of `a` satisfies `σ(x(c)) = x(σ c)` on rational points
   (`rationalConjugate_rationalPoint_of_fixed`). This follows from the Library's
   `coefficientCurve_conjugate` and the evaluation of polynomial curves. The negative partner
   satisfies `σ(x₋(d)) = x₋(σ d)` (`rationalConjugate_negPoint_of_fixed`, from the Library's
   `negPoint_rationalConjugate`). The positive point of the linked pair satisfies
   `σ(x₊(c)) = x₊(σ c)` (`rationalConjugate_xpPoint_of_fixed`).

4. **Generic facts** (section `Generic`, over any field). The positive linked point lies in the
   root subgroup (`linked_xpPoint_mem`). The torus element `h(t)` of the rank-one relation data
   of the linked pair is, by definition, the image of the coroot point `α^∨(t)` of the pinned
   torus (`rankOneData_h_eq_map`, proved by `rfl`).

5. **A Galois-compatible pinning** (section `Pinning`). `complexFixedPinning hG i hi hsplit P
   hP B` is the split pinning of `G_ℂ` with torus `T_ℂ`, inclusion `complexHom i`, root datum
   `P` and base `B`. Its root vectors are the values at `1` of the Library's
   stabilizer-equivariant root-line coordinates `splitRootWeightEquivariantLinearEquiv`.
   Connected reductivity and maximality of `T_ℂ` come from `connectedReductiveFieldExtensionStatement`
   and `maximalTorusBaseChange`. If `σ` fixes the root `P.root a`, it fixes the corresponding
   pinning vector (`complexFixedPinning_rootVector_galois`, from
   `splitRootWeightEquivariantLinearEquiv_galois` and `σ 1 = 1`).

6. **Real root points** (section `Main`). Let the simple root `a` be fixed by all of
   `Gal(ℂ/ℝ)`, and let `ν` be a real cocharacter with `⟨a, ν_ℂ⟩ ≠ 0`. For any normalized
   parametrization `x` of `a` in `complexFixedPinning` and any real `r`, the points `x₊(r)` and
   `x₋(r)` are Galois-fixed by step 3, with `σ r = r`. They lie in `U_a` and `U_{-a}`
   respectively, so by step 2 they lie in the image of `identityComponentPreimage G`
   (`complexFixedPinning_xpPoint_mem`, `complexFixedPinning_negPoint_mem`).

7. **Coroot values at real units.** Let `u ∈ ℂˣ` with `u = r` real. The Library's
   `RankOneRelation.Data.h_eq_word` writes
   `h(u) = x₋(u⁻¹(u-1))⁻¹ · x₊(1) · x₋(u-1) · x₊(u⁻¹)⁻¹`. All four coefficients are real, so
   `h(u)` lies in the image subgroup (`complexFixedPinning_rankOne_h_mem`). By step 4 this is
   `α^∨(u)` mapped into `G_ℂ(ℂ)` (`complexFixedPinning_coroot_mem`).

8. **Fixed indices.** If complex conjugation fixes the index `j`, every element of `Gal(ℂ/ℝ)`
   does (`complexGaloisRootIndexEquiv_eq`, by step 1). By the Library's
   `IsRootDatumOf.galoisRootIndexEquiv_root` and `IsRootDatumOf.galoisRootIndexEquiv_coroot`,
   the root `P.root j` and the coroot `P.coroot j` are then Galois-fixed (`complexGalois_root_eq`,
   `complexGalois_coroot_eq`). The coroot comparison needs `complexHom i` to be a monomorphism
   and its Weyl image to be finite. Both are passed as explicit hypotheses, supplied by
   maximality of `T_ℂ` and `splitTorusWeylImageFinite`.

9. **Conclusion.** `fixedSimpleCoroot_mem_of_fixed`: the fixed coroot descends to a real
   cocharacter `ν = cocharacterLattice.descend ℝ ℂ T (P.coroot j) _` with `ν_ℂ = α_j^∨`
   (`cocharacterLattice.baseChange_descend`). Then `⟨α_j, ν_ℂ⟩ = 2 ≠ 0` (`P.root_coroot_two`
   and the pairing clause of `IsRootDatumOf`). A normalized parametrization of `j` exists
   (`nonempty_normalizedRootParametrization`), so step 7 applies to every real unit.
   `fixedSimpleCoroot_neg_one_mem_of_fixed` takes `u = -1` and `r = -1`, and
   `fixedSimpleCoroot_neg_one_mem` combines this with step 8.
