# Merged-root data for two adjacent simple roots

This module constructs the supplied data of Steinberg's modified products `N''` and `N'''`
(the published `SplitPinning.SteinbergModifiedData` of `Rational.SteinbergModifiedRootProduct`)
for two adjacent simple roots `a` and `b`. Adjacent means `⟨b, a^∨⟩ = -1`. The merged root is
`s_a(b) = a + b`. Its root map is the root map of `b` conjugated by a representative `n_a` of
`s_a`, and its representative is `n_a n_b n_a⁻¹`. If `n_b` is generated by the rational root
subgroups of `±b`, this representative is generated by those of `±(a + b)`. For consecutive
vertices of a path component, the module combines this with `Rational.CharpolyClassFunctions`.
The class function `det (1 - ρ)` then vanishes along `N''` for the constructed data, with no
remaining hypotheses on the data. This is an internal unit of the even type `A` case of the
quasi-split Steinberg quotient task.

## Setting

All results are in `Langlands.GroupScheme.SplitPinning`. They use an arbitrary field `k`, an
actual affine group scheme `G` over `Spec k` and a supplied split pinning `p`.

## Definitions

- `RootParametrization.conjugate x m hr`: for a root map `x` of a root `α`, a normalizer point
  `m` and a root index `r` with `root r = weylAction p.inclusion m (root α)`, the root map of
  `r` whose homomorphism is `x.conjugateHom m`. That is `x` followed by the inner automorphism
  of `m`, defined in `Rational.ModifiedProductImmersion`. `conjugate_hom` records the
  homomorphism.
- `ReflectionRepresentative.ofNormalizerPoint m hm`: a normalizer point `m` whose Weyl action
  is the reflection of `α`, as a reflection representative of `α`. Its torus automorphism is
  the published chosen `normalizingAut`. `ofNormalizerPoint_point` records the point.
- `adjacentModifiedData a b hab h xb na nb ca cb hca hcb`: for distinct simple roots `a`, `b`
  with `h : pairing b a = -1`, a normalized root map `xb` of `b`, simple-reflection
  representatives `na` and `nb`, and nonzero scalars `ca` and `cb`, the modified data with the
  following fields:
  - `alpha = reflectionPerm a b`;
  - `xAlpha = xb.toRootParametrization.conjugate na.normalizerPoint _`;
  - `nAlpha = ofNormalizerPoint (n_a n_b n_a⁻¹) _`.
- `pathAdjacentModifiedData hβ hi x na ca cb hca hcb`: for a path component `β`
  (`IsPathComponent β`) and `i + 2 ≤ L`, the data `adjacentModifiedData` at `a = β i` and
  `b = β (i + 1)`. It uses the root map `x (β (i + 1))` and the published canonical
  representative `(x (β (i + 1))).reflectionRepresentative` of `β (i + 1)`. The representative
  `na` of `β i` is arbitrary.

## Results

- `root_reflectionPerm_of_pairing_eq_neg_one`: if `pairing b a = -1`, then
  `root (reflectionPerm a b) = root a + root b`.
- `weylAction_conj_normalizerPoint`: `n_a n_b n_a⁻¹` acts on characters by the reflection of
  `reflectionPerm a b`.
- `adjacentModifiedData_nAlpha_mem`: if `nb.point` lies in the subgroup of rational points
  generated by the rational root subgroups of `root b` and `-root b`, then the merged
  representative lies in the subgroup generated by those of `root alpha` and `-root alpha`.
- `pairing_path_succ` and `path_ne_succ`: consecutive path vertices have pairing `-1` and are
  distinct.
- `restriction_pathAdjacent_detOneSubClassFunction_eq_zero`: let `ρ` be a highest-weight
  representation at the path fundamental weight of the end of `β` (the hypotheses `hρ`, `hβ`,
  `hlam` of `Rational.PathFundamentalWeights`). Let `x` be normalized simple-root maps and `e`
  any ordering of the complement. For the data `pathAdjacentModifiedData`, the restriction of
  `detOneSubClassFunction ρ` along `steinbergModifiedProduct D e x
  (canonicalSimpleReflectionRepresentatives x)` is zero.

## Proof as implemented

**Conjugated root maps.** The published `IsRepresentedBy.comp_iso` shows that `x.hom`
followed by the inner automorphism of `m` represents the preimage of the root subgroup of
`α` under the inverse inner automorphism. The published `innerIso_inv_hom` identifies that
inverse with the inner automorphism of `m⁻¹`. The published
`RelativeRoot.subgroup_comap_innerIso` at `m⁻¹` then identifies the preimage with the root
subgroup of `r`. This uses `weylAction m⁻¹ (root r) = root α`, from `map_inv` and
`symm_apply_apply`. Closedness and torus equivariance are `isClosedSubgroup_conjugateHom` and
`conjugateHom_torus_equivariant` of `Rational.ModifiedProductImmersion`, rewritten through
`AutomorphicAction.conjugation_act`.

**The merged root and representative.** Mathlib's `RootPairing.root_reflectionPerm` and
`reflection_apply_root` give `root (reflectionPerm a b) = root b - pairing b a • root a`.
For the representative, `weylAction` is a group homomorphism. By the published
`SimpleReflectionRepresentative.weylAction_normalizerPoint`, together with Mathlib's
`reflection_inv` and `reflection_reflectionPerm`, `s_a s_b s_a⁻¹ = s_{s_a(b)}`.

**Root generation.** The published `RelativeRoot.normalizer_conj_mem` at the base test
(`constantPoint_self`) says that conjugation by `m = n_a` carries rational points of the root
subgroup of `χ` into that of `weylAction m χ`. Mathlib's `Subgroup.map_sup` applies this to
the sup of the subgroups of `root b` and `-root b`, using `map_neg` for the negative root.

**Vanishing of `f`.** Consecutive path vertices have pairing `-1` by `IsPathComponent.pairing_eq`.
They are distinct because `pairing_same` would give `2 = -1`. The line of `ε_{i+1}` is nonzero
by `representationWeightSpace_ne_bot_iff`, so it contains a nonzero vector `v`. The canonical
representative of `β (i + 1)` is root-generated (published
`reflectionRepresentative_mem_generated`), so `adjacentModifiedData_nAlpha_mem` supplies the
remaining hypothesis of `restriction_steinbergModifiedProduct_detOneSubClassFunction_eq_zero`.

## Provenance and scope

The construction follows Robert Steinberg, *Regular elements of semi-simple algebraic groups*
(1965), §9. Before Theorem 9.11 (printed p.74, PDF p.27) he uses the root `α = α_m + α_{m+1}`
of the type `A_{2m}` component, with its root group `X_α` and an element `σ_α` of its rank-one
group. Lemma 9.12 records that `α` together with the other simple roots forms a simple system.
In the proof of 9.16, step 5 (printed p.76), Steinberg normalizes `σ_α` as a conjugate
simple representative. This module constructs both the root parametrization and the
representative by conjugation, with the exact interfaces needed to instantiate the
Library's supplied-data package. The vanishing theorem is the `N''` half of
Corollary 9.18 for this choice, in the representation-theoretic form of
`Rational.CharpolyClassFunctions`.

The module does not normalize `X_α` or `σ_α` as in Steinberg's 9.16, and it does not choose
the scalars `ca` and `cb`. It does not descend the data to a quasi-split form over a non-split
field, and it does not treat `N'''`. It proves no isomorphism of `N''` onto the zero set of `f`
(Corollary 9.19). These remain obligations of the host task.
