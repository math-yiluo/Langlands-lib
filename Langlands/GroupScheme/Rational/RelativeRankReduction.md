# Intrinsic rank reduction to three independent geometric statements

This derived unit constructs a chosen factorization of a central split torus through a prescribed maximal split torus and assembles the existing `RelativeRankIdentityStatement` conditionally from three existing geometric propositions. It does not prove any of those three propositions and does not discharge the unconditional rank task.

For an affine group scheme `G` over any field, `IsMaximalSplitTorus.centralSplitTorusLift hi ha` chooses an actual group-scheme morphism from a central split torus `a : A.toGrp ⟶ G` to the supplied maximal split torus `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G`. It applies the published `IsCentralSplitTorus.exists_factor_of_isMaximalSplitTorus` with the ambient subgroup inclusion equal to the identity of `G`. The companion theorem says that the chosen morphism followed by `i` equals `a`. This uses the existing product/image argument and maximality, without conjugacy or a character-detection premise.

Given the full existing `CharacterRestrictionStatement`, integral character restriction to any maximal split torus is injective. The proof chooses an actual split component using the published `splitComponentStatement`. Its restriction is injective by the assumed character-restriction proposition, and its inclusion factors through the maximal torus by the constructed lift. A character vanishing on that maximal torus therefore vanishes on the split component and is zero. The rationalized version applies the independently developed `RationalSpan.rationalization_injective`, which uses flatness of `ℚ` over `ℤ`.

The theorem `relativeRankIdentityStatement_of_complement_restriction_conjugacy` has exactly three explicit premises: the published propositions `RelativeCharacterRootComplementStatement`, `CharacterRestrictionStatement` and `MaximalSplitTorusConjugacyStatement`, all at arbitrary universe `u`. Its result is the full existing `RelativeRankIdentityStatement.{u}`. This remains a conditional theorem; the full type includes those premises.

The assembly uses `SplitTorusRationalRank` for finite dimensionality of the full torus character space, every actual relative root span in it, the rationalized rational characters of `G`, and every supplied split component's character space. `RelativeRankAssembly` combines the rational complement with the injectivity just proved to obtain the full rank sum, and transports the conjugacy statement's rank clause to `splitTorusRank`. For each actual supplied split component, `CharacterRestrictionRational` derives bijectivity of rationalized restriction and its rank equality from the integral restriction facts; substitution gives the final rank sum with that component. Thus all conjunctions in the original target, including finiteness and quantification over each split component, are retained. Empty lattices and nonreduced relative root sets are allowed.

The provenance is internal and derived from the published split-torus factorization and split-component existence theorems, the published rank and character constructions, and finite-dimensional tensor-product algebra.
