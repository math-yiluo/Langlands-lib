# Restriction of support in ordered semigroup products

This internal unit provides the support restriction used when an ordered-product
partition of an ambient group is applied to a central subgroup or a smooth
quotient. It derives its claims from the existing weight-partition and actual
semigroup-functor constructions, without a new literature assertion.

`WeightPartition.closure_restrict_support_iff` starts with blocks covering a set
`Ω` and the exact cross-exclusion of each original block from the positive
semigroup generated by each other block. For any subset `Ω'` of `Ω`, membership
on `Ω'` in the closure of `Ψ r ∩ Ω'` agrees with membership in the closure of
`Ψ r`. One direction is monotonicity of positive semigroup closure. In the other
direction the existing `closure_inter_support` forces an occurring weight of the
closure back into its original block, where it belongs to the restricted block.

`HasOrderedSemigroupProducts b` is the exact conclusion and partition premises
of `SemigroupOrderedProductStatement` for one supplied action `b`. It requires
actual smooth connected closed representatives of the generated all-test
subgroup functors and an isomorphism for their original ordered multiplication.
It does not assert that an arbitrary action has this property.

`semigroupSubgroup_closure_restrict_support` applies the closure membership
lemma and the existing agreement-on-ambient-weights theorem. Thus restricting
blocks to the actual support of the action preserves the actual generated
subgroup functors, not just their Lie spans.

`HasOrderedSemigroupProducts.for_larger_support` applies the supplied product
property to the intersections of the blocks with the action's own support. The
existing restriction lemmas give coverage and exact cross-exclusion; disjointness
of the original blocks also passes to intersections. The subgroup equality then
recovers representatives and the same ordered multiplication for the unchanged
original blocks. The assertion permits empty indexing, occurring zero, arbitrary
field and universe, and all orders. It does not require disjoint generated
semigroups and does not insert zero into positive generation.

The full ordered-product theorem, support containment for a particular subgroup
or quotient, and geometric realization of arbitrary factors remain separate
obligations.
