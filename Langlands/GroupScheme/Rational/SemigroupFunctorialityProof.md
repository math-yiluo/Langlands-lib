# Actual semigroup functoriality and normal adjoint comparison

Worker 1372.

`RelativeRoot.semigroupFunctoriality` proves the existing
`SemigroupFunctorialityStatement.{u}` for every universe `u`. It applies to every
field, smooth affine source and target group schemes, a split torus with actual
automorphic actions, every equivariant group-scheme homomorphism, and every
additive subsemigroup of the character lattice. It proves inclusion of the
actual subgroup-functor image on every test scheme. The homomorphism need not be
smooth or separable, and the semigroup may contain zero.

The implemented proof applies
`semigroupFunctoriality_of_imageGeneration_and_containment` to the published
`ImageGeneration.pointedImageGeneration` and the containment component of
`CoordinateWeights.coordinateSemigroupComparison`. In that reduction a smooth
connected source generator maps into the smooth connected subgroup generated by
its pointed image. Dominant coordinate support transport bounds the target
generator's weights, and the least-subgroup property gives the required
inclusion. Both inputs are proved without proposition premises. No realization,
quotient comparison, intersection comparison or semigroup functoriality premise
is used in this proof.

The two retraction corollaries discharge the general functoriality premise in
`SemigroupRetraction`. For an actual equivariant section and retraction between
smooth affine groups, they prove equality of the induced semigroup inverse
image along the section and equality of the semigroup image along the
retraction. The latter is surjectivity on every original test scheme because
the section supplies actual lifts. No connectedness or zero-exclusion is
required.

`semigroup_comap_eq_of_normalAdjoint` retains the independent structural
`NormalAdjointEquivariantRetractionStatement.{u}` as an explicit premise. Over an
algebraically closed field, it takes a closed inclusion of adjoint groups with
normal all-test range and compatible split-torus actions. Smoothness follows
from adjointness. The structural premise supplies the equivariant retraction,
and the first retraction corollary proves the intersection equality for every
additive semigroup. This corollary does not prove the structural premise.

The final quotient-square corollary makes the preceding reduction usable with
actual adjoint quotients of connected reductive groups. It assumes a commuting
square, an equivariant normal closed inclusion on the two adjoint targets, both
actual fppf images of the semigroup subgroups, and containment of each full
scheme-theoretic centre in its source semigroup subgroup. Each adjoint quotient
identifies its kernel range with that full centre. The generic
`SubgroupFunctor.comap_eq_of_fppfImage_quotient_square` then lifts the conditional
normal-adjoint intersection equality. No smoothness of either centre quotient
or its semigroup restriction is assumed. The centre containments and fppf
images are retained obligations; field-point or reduced-centre containment would
not suffice.

These corollaries are formal deductions from the named Library results and
explicit hypotheses. They do not construct radical quotients, adjoint quotient
squares, their action descent, their semigroup fppf images, or centre
containments. They leave the structural retraction theorem and the full normal
intersection case open, and do not prove `SemigroupComparisonStatement`.
