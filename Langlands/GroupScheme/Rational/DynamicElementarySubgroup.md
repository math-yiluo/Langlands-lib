These are internal consequences of the imported actual dynamic representatives, geometric parabolic recognition, geometric radical theorem, affine radical comparison, and definition of the parabolic elementary subgroup. No external source assertion is added. In particular, universal parabolic presentation, centralizer reductivity, and geometric radical existence statements are not supplied as additional hypotheses: the imported proved theorems discharge the needed facts for this actual cocharacter.
