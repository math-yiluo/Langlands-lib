# The torus quotient retained with a split geometric radical

Worker 1485 implements the construction; Worker 1333 proposed retaining the
quotient for the downstream section and conjugacy constructions.

This internal companion to `TorusGeneratedSplitGeometricRadicalAssembly`
preserves the actual torus quotient produced during finite Galois descent.
It composes existing Library constructions and adds no external theorem
or axiom.

For a group scheme `G` over `Spec k`, the structure
`SplitGeometricRadicalTorusQuotient G` stores an actual group scheme `U`
over the same field, an inclusion `i : U ⟶ G`, both the geometric radical
predicate and ground-field split unipotence for this `U`, an actual
`SmoothNormalQuotient i`, and torusness of that quotient.

The reused geometric radical predicate includes the ground-field
unipotent-radical predicate and its counterpart for the actual base change
to the algebraic closure. The quotient record stores its group scheme
`D`, projection `q : G ⟶ D`, smoothness, separatedness, local finite type
and quasi-compactness of `D`, and smoothness, surjectivity and
quasi-compactness of `q`. Its `isFppfQuotient` field identifies the range
of the full categorical kernel of `q` with the range of this same
inclusion `i` on every relative test scheme.

The definition
`IsTorusGenerated.splitGeometricRadicalTorusQuotientOfStructuralStatements`
constructs these data for a smooth connected affine solvable group
generated by its ground-field tori. It quantifies over every field in
the arbitrary common universe `u`. Its three explicit structural
premises are:

- `SplitTorusDominatedSolvableQuotientStatement`, which constructs the
  initial torus quotient with a closed smooth connected unipotent full
  kernel over the chosen extension field;
- `UnipotentSplitTorusDominationStatement`, which is the splitness
  criterion used by the kernel-domination construction;
- `RelativeRoot.SplitUnipotentStableVectorSubgroupStatement`, used for
  kernel splitness and finite Galois descent of the split series.

The implemented route first uses
`IsTorusGenerated.finiteGaloisSplitDomination` to choose a finite Galois
extension and a pointed dominant parametrization of the base-changed
group by a split torus. Smoothness and affineness persist under pullback;
the existing connectedness and unconditional solvability base-change
results supply the other hypotheses. The first structural premise then
provides actual quotient data over the extension field. The existing
`TorusKernelDomination.representative_isSplitUnipotent_of_stableVector`
proves splitness of its specified full-kernel representative using the
other two premises.

Finally, `finiteGaloisSplitTorusQuotientDescent` returns the actual
ground-field radical and its smooth torus quotient. The constructor
retains the radical, inclusion, geometric comparison, split series,
quotient record and torus predicate from this single descent result.
It does not choose a second quotient after forgetting the first one.

This interface gives downstream constructions the projection and fppf
kernel identity without a separate absolute torus-quotient statement.
The three structural premises remain explicit and unproved by this
module. The constructor therefore does not establish the unconditional
`TorusGeneratedSplitGeometricRadicalStatement`; it strengthens the data
retained by the existing conditional assembly.
