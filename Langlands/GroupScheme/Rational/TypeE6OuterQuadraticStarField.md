# The quadratic star field of an outer E₆ group

For an affine locally finite-type group with a numbered E₆ Tits index, suppose that the star action moves some vertex. Then the fixed field of the star kernel is a quadratic Galois extension. The scalar extension of the group to this field has an E₆ Tits index with trivial star action under the transported numbering.

Every automorphism of the E₆ Cartan matrix is the identity or the interchange of the two long arms. A nontrivial star action therefore has image of order two. Continuity gives an open kernel, and the fixed-field construction gives the quadratic Galois extension. The index over this field has the transported Cartan matrix. Its star action is the restriction of the original action along the separable-closure comparison, and each restricted automorphism fixes the star field and belongs to the kernel.

This construction gives the field over which an outer E₆ group becomes inner in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.6, Theorem 6.26, printed p. 355 (PDF p. 365). [Chapter 6, Galois cohomology](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711).
