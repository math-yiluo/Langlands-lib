# The absolute and inner types of a cubic-split A2 pinning

A split maximal torus after scalar extension supplies the root datum and pinning on that same torus. Extending the pinning to the separable closure and comparing with an actual Tits index preserves the Cartan matrix. The index's star permutation is the finite-stage star permutation under restriction of coefficient automorphisms.

In type A₂ the two vertices belong to a single geometric almost-simple factor by their nonzero Cartan pairing. A cubic Galois group has trivial permutation action on the two vertices, so the actual index is of inner type A₂.
