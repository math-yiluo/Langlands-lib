# Hasse reduction for norm-one torsion central kernels

The proposition concerns a central isogeny from a simply connected
group over a number field `F`. Its kernel is identified with the
positive-exponent torsion in the norm-one group of a number field
extension `B/F` of quadratic degree or degree coprime to that
exponent. If a target cocycle on a finite Galois cover is trivial
on every completed pullback cover, the proposition asks for its
triviality on the original cover.

Assume the finite Galois Hasse kernel for simply connected groups.
Finite Galois lifting produces a source cocycle on a refinement.
The archimedean kernel correction makes the source cocycle trivial
on the infinite pullback covers on a further refinement. At finite
places this follows from local simply connected vanishing.
The simply connected Hasse hypothesis now makes the corrected
cocycle globally trivial. Projection kills the kernel correction,
and fpqc reflection along the two field refinements gives triviality
on the original cover.

This internal reduction combines global lifting and simultaneous
archimedean correction for norm-one torsion. The final projection
and reflection apply to any central isogeny whose source has the
simply connected Hasse property and whose lift has a simultaneous
archimedean correction.
