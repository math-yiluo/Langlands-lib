# Actual boundary parabolics with the original inner Levi

This internal construction takes a compatible presentation of a supplied
parabolic and its Levi and split component in a connected reductive group.
For a vector in its nonnegative cone outside its positive cone, it constructs
actual models of the proper coarser boundary parabolic, its rational Levi and
its geometric unipotent radical. It also constructs the inner parabolic
intersection in that outer Levi, with the original supplied Levi group as its
Levi source, and an actual geometric unipotent radical.

`NestedProperParabolicModel` records those group schemes, inclusions,
parabolicity and Levi and radical proofs, and both actual Levi-first
multiplication isomorphisms. Its identities hold as subgroup functors on
every test scheme. In particular, the outer parabolic contains the original
one; the original Levi composite factors through the constructed inner Levi
inclusion; and the inner parabolic image is precisely the intersection of
the original parabolic image with the outer Levi image.

The boundary cocharacter factors through the original central split
component. Centrality therefore places the original Levi in its dynamic
centralizer. The affine dynamic decomposition construction supplies actual
outer models. The published dynamic recognition, centralizer reductivity
and geometric radical theorems establish their structural properties.
Properness follows from the boundary cocharacter's previously proved dynamic
properness: an isomorphic parabolic inclusion would have full all-test image.

For the inner models, the common-cocharacter intersection construction uses
the boundary cocharacter first and an original defining cocharacter second.
The resulting common Levi image equals the original Levi image, because the
original Levi lies in the outer centralizer. Uniqueness of representatives
gives an isomorphism from the original Levi group to this constructed common
Levi. Precomposing its inclusion with that isomorphism preserves rational
Levi status and the actual multiplication isomorphism. Thus the conclusion
retains the original Levi source and its original ambient composite.

This is a derived geometric ingredient for the boundary step in VII.2.4.
It neither assumes nor proves arbitrary-subset parabolic realization. It
does not choose an outer split component or root presentation, construct a
Jacquet exponent, or prove the complete square-integrability equivalence.
