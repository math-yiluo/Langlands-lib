# Vanishing roots of twisted standard-Levi intersections

The ambient data are an arbitrary field `k : Type u`, an affine group scheme
`G` over `k`, a commutative group scheme `T` over `k`, and a homomorphism
`i : T.toGrp ⟶ G` whose underlying scheme morphism `i.hom.hom.left` is a
monomorphism. This last hypothesis supplies the published Weyl character action.

This internal construction defines
`twistedStandardRelativeRootZeroSupport` as the actual relative roots that
pair to zero with a supplied right cocharacter and whose Weyl transforms pair
to zero with a supplied left cocharacter. For a minimal double-coset
representative, standard cocharacters for the two faces and their intrinsic
intersection face identify this set with the vanishing-root support of the
intersection cocharacter.

The proof first treats positive roots. Standard-cocharacter vanishing is
equivalent to membership in the additive monoid generated by its face.
`IsRelativeWeylCoxeterRealization.mem_closure_intersectionSubset_iff`
then identifies simultaneous vanishing with the intersection-face monoid.
For the reverse direction, the intrinsic intersection subset is contained in
the right face, so its monoid is contained in the right-face monoid.

Genericity divides each relative root into a positive root or a root whose
negative is positive. The all-root criterion retains negation stability of
the actual relative-root set as an explicit hypothesis. Linearity of the
Weyl action and pairing makes the zero conditions invariant under simultaneous
negation. The final lift theorem uses the published quotient-action formula,
so it applies to every normalizer lift without choosing a section.

The provenance is internal, derived from `PositiveRootInduction`,
`StandardLeviTwistedRootSupport`, and the actual quotient character action.
The result assumes a supplied actual Coxeter realization, genericity, standard
cocharacters, double-coset minimality and, for the all-root results, negation
stability. It does not prove unconditional negation stability, standard
cocharacter existence, or an equality of subgroup functors on test schemes;
those are separate obligations in the full twisted standard-Levi theorem.
