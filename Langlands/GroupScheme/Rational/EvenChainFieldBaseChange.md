# Field base change for even-path pattern products

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.EvenChainFieldBaseChange`. Throughout, `k → F` is a field
extension, `bc = algebraBaseMap k F`, `G` is an affine group scheme over `Spec k` and `G_F` its
pullback along `bc`. Data transports require `[Algebra.IsAlgebraic k F]` (the hypothesis of the
published pinning transport `SplitPinning.baseChange`); the descent result holds for every
extension. The module lets a statement about pattern products proved over an algebraically
closed field (for arbitrary data) be transported to an arbitrary field.

## Definitions

- `ClassFunctions.fieldExtension f`: the class function `invariants (1 ⊗ f)` of `G_F` attached
  to a class function `f` of `G` (published `ClassFunctions.BaseChange.invariants`).
- `fieldBaseChangePoint`: extension of rational points, `g ↦ baseChangeBasePoint bc G g`, as a
  group homomorphism `G(k) → G_F(F)`.
- `SplitPinning.RootParametrization.fieldBaseChange`: the extension of a root parametrization of
  an arbitrary root, built exactly like the published
  `NormalizedRootParametrization.fieldBaseChange` (pullback of the root homomorphism composed
  with the standard comparison of the additive line), without the Lie normalization.
- `SplitPinning.ReflectionRepresentative.fieldBaseChange`: the extended point with the pulled-back
  torus automorphism, as in the published `SimpleReflectionRepresentative.fieldBaseChange`.
- `SplitPinning.SteinbergModifiedData.fieldBaseChange`: merged pair `a, b` transported along
  `baseChangeSimpleEquiv`, the same root index `α`, the extended root map and representative of
  `α`, and the constants `c_a, c_b` mapped into `F`.
- `SplitPinning.EvenChainData.fieldBaseChange C`: the same halves, the paths composed with
  `baseChangeSimpleEquiv`, and the transported merged-root data.
- `SplitPinning.EvenChainData.restFieldBaseChange`: the induced equivalence of the unmerged
  simple roots `C.Rest ≃ (C.fieldBaseChange).Rest`.

## Results

### Descent of quotient equalities

- `SplitPinning.classEvaluation_eq_of_rho_eq`: equal images in the canonical quotient give equal
  values of all class functions (from `ClassFunctions.evaluation_eq_of_rho_eq`).
- `ClassFunctions.fieldExtension_val`: the extended class function is the coordinate restriction
  of `f` (from `invariants_inclusion`).
- `affineBaseChangeEquiv_baseChangeBasePoint` and `classEvaluation_baseChangeBasePoint`: at the
  extension of a rational point `g`, the extended class function takes the value
  `algebraMap k F (f g)` (from `ClassFunctions.BaseChange.point_restriction` and naturality of
  point coordinates).
- `rho_eq_of_baseChangeBasePoint_rho_eq`: if the extensions of two rational points have the same
  image in the canonical quotient of `G_F`, the points have the same image in the canonical
  quotient of `G`. Proof: for every class function `f`, the values of `fieldExtension f` at the
  extensions agree, i.e. `algebraMap (f g) = algebraMap (f g')`; injectivity of `k → F` and
  `rho_eq_of_classEvaluation_eq` conclude.

### Points

- `baseChangePointsEquiv_baseChangeBasePoint`, `eq_baseChangeBasePoint_of`: an `F`-point of `G_F`
  is the extension of `g` exactly when its projection is the constant family of `g`.
- `baseChangeBasePoint_mem_baseChange`, `SplitPinning.baseChangeBasePoint_mem_subgroup`,
  `SplitPinning.baseChangeBasePoint_mem_rootSup`: extension maps a subgroup functor into its base
  change, root subgroups into the transported root subgroups (`RelativeRoot.subgroup_baseChange`),
  and the subgroup generated by the root subgroups of `± α` into the transported one.

### Transported data

- `SplitPinning.baseChange_pairing_eq`: the transported root datum has the original pairings
  (`SplitRootDatum.baseChange_pairing`).
- `SplitPinning.isPathComponent_baseChange`: path components remain path components.
- `SplitPinning.EvenChainData.isMiddleOut_fieldBaseChange`: a middle-out ordering stays middle-out
  after composing with `restFieldBaseChange`.

### Compatibility of points and products

- `SplitPinning.baseChangeBasePoint_scalarPoint_comp`,
  `RootParametrization.baseChangeBasePoint_rationalPoint`,
  `NormalizedRootParametrization.baseChangeBasePoint_rationalPoint`,
  `NormalizedRootParametrization.baseChangeBasePoint_unitPoint`: the extension of `x(c)` is
  `x_F(c)` with `c` mapped into `F` (compare projections, using
  `VectorGroup.baseChangePointsEquiv_scalarPoint_inv` and `Scheme.ΓSpecIso_inv_naturality`).
- `NormalizedRootParametrization.baseChangeBasePoint_reflectionRepresentative`,
  `SplitPinning.canonicalSimpleReflectionRepresentatives_fieldBaseChange`: canonical
  representatives commute with extension. Proof: the extended opposite factor pair is an
  opposite factor pair for the extended unit point (root-subgroup memberships as above,
  normalizing points by `IsNormalizingPoint.baseChangeBasePoint`), hence equals the canonical one
  by uniqueness (`eq_oppositeFactorPair`).
- `SplitPinning.corootRationalPoint_fieldBaseChange`: the coroot point of `α` at the image of a
  unit `t` is the extension of `α^∨(t)` (via `Dynamic.baseChangeCocharacter` and the base change
  of `𝔾_m`).
- `SteinbergModifiedData.baseChangeBasePoint_ua`, `..._ub`,
  `EvenChainData.baseChangeBasePoint_block`, `EvenChainData.baseChangeBasePoint_patternProduct`:
  the extension of a pattern product (with canonical representatives) is the pattern product of
  the transported data, with transported root maps and canonical representatives, the same
  orderings `o` and `e.trans restFieldBaseChange`, and all coordinates mapped into `F`.
- `SplitPinning.baseChangeBasePoint_steinbergProductPoint`: the same for the ordinary ordered
  product `steinbergProductPoint` with canonical representatives at rational coordinates, with
  the transported ordering `fieldBaseChangeOrder`.

## Provenance

Internal formalization step (route step 2 of the task context of Task 5373: passing to an
algebraic closure). It adapts the published field base change of pinnings, root maps and
representatives (`Reductive/PinningBaseChange`, `Rational/SteinbergRootFieldBaseChange`,
`Rational/SteinbergFieldBaseChange`) and of class functions (`Rational/ClassFunctionsBaseChange`).
No result of Steinberg (1965) is formalized here.
