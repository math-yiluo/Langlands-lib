# Toral values at an inert real place

The tower isomorphisms identify the two base-changed groups over the upper
completion and compare their cocycle values in the original group's
coefficient points. A maximal-torus centralizer at the upper completion then
reflects to the original coefficient field.

The restriction of a twist has the twisted value defined by extending the
cochain. The field map into the upper completion intertwines the two Galois
automorphisms. `HasInertSquare` records that the local value has a square root
inverted by the coefficient automorphism. The open of strongly regular twists
with this property is pulled back through the point homeomorphism between the
two coefficient groups. This gives the open in the topology used for
approximation at the complex places.
