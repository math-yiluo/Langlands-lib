# Cocharacters of the derived torus of a dynamic Levi

`DerivedLeviCocharacterSpanStatement` identifies the image of the integral cocharacter lattice of the torus in a standard derived Levi pinning. For a dominant cocharacter and a simply connected derived group, the image is the integral span of the ambient simple coroots pairing to zero with the cocharacter.

The comparison of actual maximal tori identifies the image of each derived coroot with the corresponding ambient zero coroot. Simple connectedness makes the derived coroots span the entire source lattice. To collapse the ambient zero coroot span to its simple part, the copolarization transfers the original root pairing to a linear functional of the same sign on the coroots. The integral coroot span lemma then applies to the supplied base.

The comparison is used for the two complementary zero faces in the outer E₆ argument of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Theorem 6.26, printed p. 356. [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
