# Actual-root adapters for the rank-one Bruhat word argument

Authors: Worker 1325 and Worker 1226 (normalizer-separation composition).

This internal supporting unit connects the group word argument in
`Langlands.Group.RankOneBruhatCovering` with the actual rational-point root
groups. It does not prove `RootGeneratedCentralizerRankOneStatement`.
Every root subgroup is the Library's character-orbit-extension subgroup,
evaluated at the identity test object. The field and universe are arbitrary.

`HasRootGeneratedBruhatCovering i ν M` means that the subgroup generated by
all actual root points is contained in `U(ν) M U(ν)`.
`hasUnipotentNormalizerSeparation_of_isConnectedReductive` proves the exact
normalizer uniqueness predicate for a generic cocharacter of an actual
maximal split torus in a connected reductive group. It composes the
published generic-centralizer equality, affine opposite-cell injectivity,
and smooth-affine product contractions. It uses no covering premise.
`centralizer_inf_rootGenerated_le_of_bruhatCovering` assumes connected
reductivity of `G`, maximal splitness of `i`, genericity of `ν`, and
`M ≤ normalizerPoints i`. In addition it assumes the covering and
`centralizerPoints i ⊓ M ≤ H`. Under these hypotheses it removes both
unipotent factors from a covered point in the whole-torus centralizer and
concludes that the centralizing root-generated points lie in `H`. The
normalizer-containment premise lets the proof apply normalizer uniqueness
to the middle factor; the covering predicate itself still permits an
arbitrary middle subgroup.

`RelativeRoot.rootPoint_conjugation_sign` supplies the two-sign alternative
for any actual normalizer point. The inverse normalizer transports the root
to an actual root, whose pairing with a generic cocharacter is nonzero.
According to its sign, the conjugated positive or opposite root group lies
in `U(ν)`. No root-negation closure premise is needed for this argument.

`rootGenerated_le_closure_unipotent_union` assumes that each actual root can
be transported to a positive character by a normalizer point in `M`.
Conjugation carries each root point into `U(ν)`; conjugating back places it
in the subgroup generated by `U(ν)` and `M`. Subgroup-closure minimality
extends this to all root-generated points.

`rootPoint_factorization_of_oppositeFactorPairs` retains actual
`HasOppositeFactorPairs i` and containment in `M` of the normalizing points
in the subgroup generated by the two opposite root groups. For an opposite
pair `p`, the normalizing product is `p.1 * x * p.2`. It lies in that
opposite-root generated group, hence in `M`, and cancellation gives
`x = p.1⁻¹ * (p.1 * x * p.2) * p.2⁻¹`.

Provenance is internal composition of published Library results and
elementary subgroup calculations. The proofs assert no new geometric
existence theorem and discharge neither root-product factorization nor
opposite-pair existence nor a Coxeter presentation or its lifted relations.
