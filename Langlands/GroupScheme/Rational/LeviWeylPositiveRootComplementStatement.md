# The Levi Weyl group preserves complementary positive roots

`Langlands.GroupScheme.LeviWeylPositiveRootComplementStatement` uses the
relative roots of a maximal split torus of a connected reductive group
over a field. A generic cocharacter fixes the positive and simple roots.
A subset \(J\) of simple roots determines the standard Levi and its Weyl
subgroup through `standardRelativeWeylGroup`. For the chosen ambient
root set \(R\), `standardLeviRootSubset R J` is the relative Levi subsystem
generated by \(J\) and \(-J\).

Each element \(w\) of the Levi Weyl subgroup takes every positive root
of \(R\) outside that subsystem to a positive root of \(R\) outside that
subsystem. The action is the conjugation action on the actual character
lattice. Since the same property applies to \(w^{-1}\), each \(w\)
permutes the complementary roots.

This is the second root fact used in Renard, *Représentations des groupes
réductifs p-adiques*, French edition dated 9 July 2026, proof of
V.4.6(i), printed p. 121 and PDF p. 127. Renard uses the indivisible
relative roots. The proposition also permits the full relative roots.
In both cases the positive complementary roots are exactly the roots
pairing strictly positively with a standard cocharacter for \(J\).
Normalizing points in the Levi preserve that pairing, which explains
the extension to the full root set and to arbitrary fields.
