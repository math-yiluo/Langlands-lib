# A large reductive subgroup in the cubic splitting argument for E₆

`TypeE6CubicSplitLargeSubgroupStatement` applies to a simply connected anisotropic affine group of locally finite type in characteristic zero. A cubic Galois extension must give a split pinning with Cartan matrix of type E₆. The statement produces a connected reductive closed subgroup of dimension at least thirty over the ground field. Over the cubic field its actual derived group embeds as a closed subgroup of a split simply connected group of type D₅.

Choose the parabolic omitting the first long-arm endpoint. Its codimension is sixteen. The split derived Levi pinning identifies the remaining five vertices with the D₅ diagram. The identity component of the descended intersection of the three conjugates is reductive under anisotropy. The dimension inequality gives the lower bound of thirty, and the parabolic limit map embeds this component after scalar extension into the Levi. Functoriality of the derived subgroup and field base change give the claimed closed embedding into its derived group.

This is the parabolic intersection step of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Theorem 6.26, printed pp. 354–355, and §6.8, Lemma 6.32, printed pp. 380–381. [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
