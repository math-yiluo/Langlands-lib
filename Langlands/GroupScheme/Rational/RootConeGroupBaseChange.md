# Supplied cone-root groups after field extension and torus enlargement

This internal helper transfers the root-subset generation predicate along
equality of represented subgroup functors. For a maximal split torus, a supplied
closed subgroup whose range is the cone semigroup group is therefore the group
generated by the full-root cone slice: construct a generated representative,
identify its range by the cone semigroup comparison, and transfer the predicate.

Over a separably closed field, every supplied split subtorus of an affine group
locally of finite type factors through a split maximal torus. The proof uses
the published maximal-torus extension theorem and the published splitting
assembly with its proved coalgebra spanning prerequisite.

For a field extension, a supplied group for an indivisible-root cone slice
becomes the group for a full-root cone slice of a chosen larger maximal split
torus, whenever the extended ambient group is connected reductive. Its defining
cone is transported through the split character-lattice equivalence and pulled
back along character restriction. The proof identifies the extended range using
the all-test base-change and torus-restriction comparisons, then applies the
closed-representative criterion above. It does not assert maximality of the
original torus after extension.

These are derived comparisons using the Library's generation and torus
geometry theorems and the preceding cone helpers. No new external mathematical
premise is introduced.
