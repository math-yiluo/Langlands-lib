# Positive augmentation-weight semigroup comparison

`Langlands.GroupScheme.CoordinateWeights.coordinateSemigroupComparison` proves the unchanged `CoordinateSemigroupComparisonStatement.{u}` for every universe `u`. For every smooth connected affine group over any field and every actual split-torus action, the raw support of nonzero augmentation eigenfunctions equals the set underlying the positive additive semigroup generated by the original intrinsic Lie support. Its second component gives the equivalent containment criterion for every additive subsemigroup.

The source is Conrad–Gabber–Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive Groups*, second edition (2015), §3.3, Example 3.3.2, printed pp.107–108 / PDF pp.133–134. The registered original is `conrad-gabber-prasad-2015-pseudo-reductive`, SHA256 `1b77af44c90211db9a3efa50896a3722f4fd2df332b7ce2ec52202eb243e7f54`. This module assembles previously proved algebraic and actual-object results for that assertion.

The Lean proof applies `coordinateSemigroupComparison_of_prerequisites` to three published proofs: `Langlands.Algebra.GradedIdealSupport.positiveGeneration.{u, u, u}`, `coordinateWeightDecomposition.{u}`, and `cotangentLieSupport.{u}`. There are no remaining proposition premises on the final theorem.

The adapter specializes positive generation to the actual augmentation ideal. It supplies its properness, homogeneous multiplication, and Noetherian coordinate domain, and uses the actual decomposition and same-character quotient lifting to identify the generic cotangent-lift support with actual cotangent support. The cotangent/Lie theorem then identifies that support with the original `RelativeRoot.actionWeightSupport b ⊤`, under the direct coordinate-pullback convention. The generic algebra proof uses Krull intersection and induction on ideal powers; the final assembly does not require the source's local-ring proof route.

The adapter also derives containment from the universal property of `AddSubsemigroup.closure`. This closure uses positive sums, so empty Lie support gives empty augmentation support. No zero character is inserted by an empty sum; zero remains allowed when it occurs or is a positive sum of occurring characters. The theorem keeps all field characteristics and the original all-algebra Lie support, and assumes neither semigroup realization nor a commutator conclusion.
