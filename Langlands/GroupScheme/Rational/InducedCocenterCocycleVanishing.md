# Cocycles of a group with induced cocenter

Hilbert 90 trivializes the image of a finite-cover cocycle in the induced
torus quotient. A trivializing cochain lifts through the quotient over a
finite Galois refinement. Indeed the fiber over that cochain is smooth
and nonempty since the quotient has smooth kernel and is a surjective
fppf map. The twist by the lift takes its values in the represented
kernel on every test, so vanishing in the kernel gives triviality.
Faithfully flat descent gives triviality on the original cover.

This is the exactness argument for the split-torus centralizer in
Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8,
p. 371, stated with an induced torus quotient.
