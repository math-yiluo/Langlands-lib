# Lifting a cocycle through a μ₃ kernel over a cyclic cubic extension

`TotallyComplexCubicCentralLiftStatement` takes an actual central isogeny over a totally complex number field and an isomorphism of its kernel with `μ₃`. On the supplied finite Galois cover it takes a cocycle of the target and central lift data for it. It gives a degree-three Galois extension of the base in its separable closure and a finite Galois coefficient field over that extension containing the original coefficients. The scalar extension of the target cocycle is the image of a cocycle of the source on that cover.

`totallyComplexCubicCentralLift` evaluates the central lift defect at the faithful kernel character into the multiplicative group, obtaining an ordinary two-cocycle of units killed by cubing. The relative Brauer class is killed by three. The cubic Brauer splitting theorem supplies a cyclic cubic extension where the restriction of the inflated unit class vanishes. The primitive of its unit two-cocycle can then be made to take values in cube roots of unity over a further finite Galois refinement, using the Kummer correction. The scalar extension comparison transports these primitives to the central kernel points. The kernel character is injective there, so their coboundary equals the actual central defect. The central lifting criterion gives the required source cocycle.

This is the lifting step for inner E₆ that uses the cyclic cubic Brauer splitting in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), Theorem 6.26, printed p. 354 (PDF p. 364). It uses the same finite Galois defect and Kummer comparisons as the quadratic central lifting theorem.
