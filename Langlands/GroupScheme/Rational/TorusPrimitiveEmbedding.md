# Homogeneous primitive generators for split-torus actions

This internal support module works over an arbitrary commutative base ring `R : CommRingCat.{u}`, a group-valued functor `F : CommAlgCat R ⥤ GrpCat`, a corepresentation `e` by `B`, and a split-torus action `σ` with finite coordinate type `I`. All constructions retain the arbitrary universe `u`.

`Corepresented.IsPrimitiveFunction e f` means that `f` is additive on the points over every commutative `R`-algebra. `isPrimitiveFunction_comp` proves that each torus weight component of a primitive function is primitive. It applies additivity to two points twisted by universal Laurent monomials, uses multiplicativity of that twist, and compares coefficients with `coord_hat_coeff`.

`exists_homogeneous_primitive_generators` starts with a finite family `x` satisfying `VectorGroup.IsPrimitiveFamily e x` and surjectivity of `MvPolynomial.aeval x`. Its output is a finite family `y : Fin N → B` and weights `w : Fin N → I → ℤ`, with primitivity, homogeneity of each `y i` at `w i`, and surjectivity of `aeval y`. The proof takes every component in the finite coaction supports of the original generators. The original generators are sums of these functions, so the new family generates the algebra. It then reindexes this finite family by `Fin N`. The construction retains weight zero and allows an empty family.

`isClosedImmersion_quotientMap` proves that a primitive generating family gives a closed immersion from the actual corepresented group scheme to the polynomial vector group. `VectorGroup.quotientMap_left` identifies its underlying map as the spectrum of the surjective map `aeval y`. `coordinateMap_act_of_isHomogeneous` proves that the corresponding natural additive map on algebra-valued points transforms diagonally through the listed exponent characters.

These are derived support results for the split-torus, primitive-generator part of the equivariant additive embedding in Conrad–Gabber–Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive Groups*, second edition (2015), Proposition B.4.1, printed pp.575–576 / PDF pp.601–602; registered source `conrad-gabber-prasad-2015-pseudo-reductive`, SHA256 `1b77af44c90211db9a3efa50896a3722f4fd2df332b7ce2ec52202eb243e7f54`. The implementation uses the universal-point grading in `Rational.TorusGrading` and the primitive-family map in `VectorGroupQuotient`. It assumes the initial primitive generators, rather than deriving them from smoothness and p-torsion. It does not yet construct natural vector coordinates on the original group, prove its torus-fixed decomposition, or prove the complete split-torus linearization target.
