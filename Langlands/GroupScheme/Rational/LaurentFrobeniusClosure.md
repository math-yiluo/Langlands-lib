# Frobenius closures of finite pointed Laurent families

This internal consequence of `LaurentFiniteFrobeniusBasis` provides the
finite-family form needed by a coordinate-algebra argument. The coefficient
ring and the character index type have independent universes.

For a commutative ring `k`, an arbitrary index type `I`, and a prime `p`,
`pointedAdjoin S` is the `k`-subalgebra of `AddMonoidAlgebra k (I → ℤ)`
generated by the underlying functions in `S`. The Frobenius span contains
`S` and is monotone. Every member of `frobSpan S` lies in `pointedAdjoin S`,
since a subalgebra is closed under powers, linear combinations and scalar
multiplication. Consequently taking the Frobenius span leaves the
generated algebra unchanged. These assertions do not require characteristic
`p`.

In characteristic `p`, `frobSpan S` is Frobenius-stable: span induction
uses semilinearity and advances the exponent of each generating power.
It is therefore idempotent and is the smallest Frobenius-stable linear
subspace containing `S`, by `frobSpan_le`. For a finite family `F`, the
existing finite-support theorem places `F` in a finite primitive-orbit
span. Stability of that orbit span then places the entire `frobSpan F`
there as well.

Over any field of characteristic `p`, `exists_finiteFrobGenerators`
applies the finite Euclidean transport theorem to this closure. It
produces a finite orbit set containing `frobSpan F` and a finite family
`G` contained in `frobSpan F` with the same
Frobenius span and the same generated Laurent algebra. The finite
encodings of `G` satisfy `TorusLinearization.FrobIndep`. No perfectness
or infinitude assumption on the field is used.

This result concerns the Frobenius closure of the specified finite
family. It does not claim that the entire space of additive functions
of a group lies in that closure, prove algebraic independence of `G`,
or construct a group-scheme isomorphism.
