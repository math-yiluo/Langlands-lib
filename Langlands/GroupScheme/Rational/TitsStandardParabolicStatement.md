# Standard parabolics of the rational Tits system

This module connects two things for an actual connected reductive group:
- the standard parabolic subsets and representative maps of the rational Tits system;
- the published `standardParabolicPoints` and `standardRelativeWeylGroup`.

The Tits data are those of the realization target `RelativeTitsSystemStatement`
(module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.RelativeTitsSystemStatement`, submitted
separately):
- `B = standardParabolicPoints i ν ∅`, i.e. `P_∅(k)`;
- `N = normalizerPoints i`;
- `π = QuotientGroup.mk' ((centralizerPoints i).subgroupOf (normalizerPoints i))`, onto
  `relativeWeylGroup i = N(k)/Z(k)`;
- for `J ⊆ Δ = simpleRelativeRoots i ν`, `W_J = Subgroup.closure (relativeReflections i J)`
  (`Rational.RelativeReflection`).

The module states three proposition targets. It proves comparison theorems from the
published statements. It supplies no proof of the targets themselves and no structural
axiom.

## Common hypotheses

Every target quantifies over:
- all fields `k : Type u`;
- group schemes `G` with `IsConnectedReductive G`;
- homomorphisms `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G` with `IsMaximalSplitTorus i`;
- cocharacters `ν` with `IsGenericCocharacter i ν`;
- sets `J` of characters with `J ⊆ simpleRelativeRoots i ν`.

Affineness and the monomorphism property of `i` come from these hypotheses through
`haveI`. Relative roots may be nonreduced. There is no assumption on characteristic,
perfectness, splitness, quasi-splitness or local fields.

If there are no relative roots (semisimple `k`-rank zero), then `Δ = ∅`, because
`simpleRelativeRoots i ν ⊆ positiveRelativeRoots i ν ⊆ relativeRoots i`. Then the only
admissible `J` is `∅`, and `W_∅ = closure ∅ = ⊥`.

## Targets

- `StandardRelativeWeylGenerationStatement`. The reflections in the roots of `J` generate
  `standardRelativeWeylGroup i ν J`:
  `Subgroup.closure (relativeReflections i J) = standardRelativeWeylGroup i ν J`.
  The right side is the image in `N(k)/Z(k)` of the normalizing points in `L_J(k)`. This
  is Borel–Tits 5.17: "Le groupe de Weyl relatif de `Z_Θ` est `W_Θ`", where `W_Θ` is
  generated by the fundamental reflections `r_a`, `a ∈ Θ`.
- `NormalizerStandardParabolicStatement`. For each `n : normalizerPoints i`,
  `n ∈ P_J(k) ↔ π n ∈ standardRelativeWeylGroup i ν J`.
  - The direction `←` is proved unconditionally in
    `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.TitsStandardParabolic`
    (`coe_mem_standardParabolicPoints_of_mk_mem`). The target is stated as an iff so
    that consumers get the characterization of `N(k) ∩ P_J(k)` in one place.
  - The direction `→` is the content of Borel–Tits 5.17 (`P_Θ,k = P_k W_Θ P_k`) together
    with the disjointness of the Bruhat cells in 5.15.
- `TitsStandardParabolicStatement`. The standard parabolic subset of the Tits system
  equals the rational points of the standard parabolic:
  `P_J(k) = TitsSystem.standardParabolic (P_∅(k)) π (relativeReflections i J)`.
  The right side is `B W_J B`, i.e. the union of the Bruhat cells of `W_J`. This is
  Borel–Tits 5.17, `P_Θ,k = P_k · W_Θ · P_k`, with `B = P_∅(k)` in place of the dynamic
  minimal parabolic used in `StandardParabolicPointsStatement`.

## Proved comparisons

None of these assumes its own conclusion.

- `cell_mk_eq_doubleCoset`: for `B = P_∅(k)`, the Bruhat cell `TitsSystem.cell B π (π n)`
  is Mathlib's `DoubleCoset.doubleCoset n B B`. The proof uses `Z(k) ≤ P_∅(k)`.
- `titsStandardParabolicStatement_of_statements`:
  `StandardParabolicEndpointsStatement`, `StandardParabolicPointsStatement` and
  `StandardRelativeWeylGenerationStatement` together imply
  `TitsStandardParabolicStatement`. The endpoint statement identifies `P_∅(k)` with the
  dynamic minimal parabolic used in the published rational-point statement.
- `normalizerStandardParabolicStatement_of_statements`:
  `StandardParabolicPointsStatement` and `RationalBruhatStatement` together imply
  `NormalizerStandardParabolicStatement`. The forward direction uses the first clause of
  the rational-point statement and Bruhat disjointness. This comparison checks
  consistency only. Any proof of `StandardParabolicPointsStatement` that uses
  `NormalizerStandardParabolicStatement` must prove the latter independently.
- `relativeWeylDoubleCoset_bijective_of_statements`: `StandardParabolicEndpointsStatement`
  and `RationalBruhatStatement` together make the representative map
  `relativeWeylDoubleCoset i ν ∅ ∅ : W → P_∅(k) \ G(k) / P_∅(k)` bijective
  (Borel–Tits 5.15).
- `standardRelativeWeylGroup_empty_of_statement`: `StandardParabolicEndpointsStatement`
  implies `standardRelativeWeylGroup i ν ∅ = ⊥`. This is the `J = ∅` case of the
  generation target.
- `exists_relativeWeylDoubleCoset_equiv_of_statement`:
  `StandardParabolicPointsStatement` gives an equivalence
  `W_J \ W / W_K ≃ P_J(k) \ G(k) / P_K(k)`. Composed with the double-coset projection,
  this equivalence is the representative map `relativeWeylDoubleCoset i ν J K`
  (Borel–Tits 5.20; Renard V.4.5).

`RationalBruhatStatement` and `StandardParabolicPointsStatement` are imported for these
comparisons only, and their proof tasks remain independent:
- `StandardParabolicEndpointsStatement` (07f71149);
- `StandardParabolicPointsStatement` (e43da082);
- `RationalBruhatStatement` (2efe8bb8).

## Sources and provenance

- Borel–Tits, *Groupes réductifs* (1965), source `borel-tits-1965-groupes-reductifs`:
  - §5.15 (printed p.100, PDF p.47): Bruhat decomposition, disjoint cells
    `P_k · w · P_k`.
  - §5.17 (printed p.101, PDF p.48): `W_Θ` is generated by the fundamental reflections of
    `Θ`, `P_Θ,k = P_k · W_Θ · P_k`, and the relative Weyl group of `Z_Θ` is `W_Θ`.
  - §5.18 (printed p.101): 5.15 and 5.16 verify the Tits axioms.
  - §5.20 (printed p.102, PDF p.49): double-coset correspondence.
- Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques* (2026 version), source
  `renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`:
  - V.4.1 (printed p.118, PDF p.124): `(G, P_∅, N_G(A_∅), S_∅)` is a Tits system.
  - V.4.3 (printed p.119, PDF p.125): `W_M = N_M(A_∅)/Z_M(A_∅)`.
  - V.4.5 (printed p.119, PDF p.125): double cosets.
  - V.4.6 proof (printed p.120, PDF p.126): `W_M` equals the Weyl group of the root
    subsystem attached to `M`.

  Renard works over nonarchimedean local fields. Borel–Tits gives the arbitrary-field
  scope.

The following are **derived** formulations of the cited source content:
- expressing `W_Θ` as the closure of `relativeReflections i J`;
- expressing the relative Weyl group of `Z_Θ` as `standardRelativeWeylGroup i ν J`, i.e.
  normalizing points in the choice-free `standardLevi`;
- writing `P_k W_Θ P_k` as `TitsSystem.standardParabolic` on `P_∅(k)`.

The positivity and standard-Levi constructions are those of
`Rational.StandardParabolic`.
