# Integral rank-one characters modulo ambient characters

This is an internal derived unit. It combines the published actual
simple-relative-root spanning theorem with the published joint spanning by
relative roots and restricted rational characters. It makes no new assertion
from external literature.

Let G be a connected reductive group over an arbitrary field, let i be an
actual maximal split torus, and let a generic cocharacter have exactly the
simple relative root alpha. Every character chi of that torus has a positive
natural multiple of the form

`d • chi = restrict(eta) + z • alpha`,

where eta is an actual rational character of G and z is an integer. The
integer is signed; this result does not assert that it is positive.
`HasIntegralRootCharacterRelation` is the Prop-valued predicate for this
exact relation. Its definition itself needs neither reductivity nor splitness.

The proof first identifies the root span with the rational line generated by
alpha. Joint spanning then writes the rationalized chi as a rational multiple
of alpha plus an ambient rationalized character. Tensor induction supplies
a positive denominator for that ambient character. Multiplication by this
denominator and the denominator of the root coefficient gives the displayed
integral equality. Injectivity of rationalization for the actual split torus
returns the identity to its character lattice.

The tensor-induction helper works for any additive commutative group and
does not assume that its map to its rationalization is injective.

There is also a conditional positive version. Suppose that a cocharacter
zeta pairs to zero with every restricted ambient rational character and
pairs strictly positively with both alpha and chi. Applying the integral
lattice pairing to the exact relation gives
`d * <chi,zeta> = z * <alpha,zeta>`. Positivity forces z to be positive, so
it may be replaced by a positive natural number b.

The last theorem specializes this argument to the published actual integral
Weyl correction `normKernelCocharacter i nu`. It assumes a finite enumeration
of the actual relative Weyl group and a monomorphism instance for i; the
published root-system theorem and maximal splitness provide those in the
reductive setting. Every ambient character annihilates the corrected
cocharacter by the published computation. The root pairing is the original
positive pairing multiplied by the positive Weyl-group order. Only the
strictly positive pairing of chi with that corrected cocharacter is retained
as an additional premise.

The unit does not prove that a character coming from an adapted line has
this positive pairing. That geometric assertion, rank-one height estimates,
and adelic chamber coverage remain open.
