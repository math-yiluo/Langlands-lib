# Number-field kernel models for cyclic absolute characters

A group of multiplicative type with constant absolute action and character
group `ZMod n` in each member of a finite coordinate set has a split
product of roots-of-unity groups as its actual model. The absolute
character anti-equivalence gives the diagonalizable group of those
coordinates, and evaluation identifies that group with the product.
The exponent is positive.

For a group over a number field whose absolute characters are cyclic and
whose action is by sign, the constant case uses one split coordinate. If
the action is nontrivial, a cyclic generator and its negative form a
two-element orbit. The open stabilizer fixes a quadratic field, whose
embeddings index these two characters equivariantly. The two characters
have sum zero and generate the original cyclic group, so the norm-one
torsion comparison gives the actual quadratic model.

For a cyclic character group of order at most four, a surjective
endomorphism is multiplication by the image of `1`. That image is a
unit and is `1` or `−1` in each of the four orders, so the sign condition
holds automatically.

This internal criterion covers the cyclic center character cases of the
number-field Hasse reduction.
