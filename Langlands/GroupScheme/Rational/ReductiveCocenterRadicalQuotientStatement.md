# The cocenter as a quotient of the solvable radical

Let \(G\) be connected reductive over \(k\), with geometric solvable radical
\(r:R\to G\) and derived subgroup \(d:D\to G\). For any cocenter torus
\(q:G\to Q\) presented as the fppf quotient by \(D\), the composite
\(q\circ r\) presents \(Q\) as the fppf quotient of \(R\) by
\(R\times_G D\). This identifies \(G/D\) with \(R/(R\cap D)\) using the
original inclusion and quotient. Pullback along \(q\) is a bijection
on the character groups over \(k\).

This expresses the displayed abelianization formula and the preceding
character-group identification in David Renard, *Représentations des groupes
réductifs p-adiques*, French edition dated 9 July 2026, V.2.2, printed p. 105
(PDF p. 111). The algebraic-closure case uses the same assertion over \(\bar k\).
