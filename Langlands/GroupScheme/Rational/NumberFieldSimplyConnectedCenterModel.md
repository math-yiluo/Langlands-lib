# Arithmetic models of the full center

Let `G` be simply connected and absolutely almost-simple over a number
field. Any representative of its actual full scheme-theoretic center
has one of the arithmetic kernel models used in the adjoint Hasse proof.
The classification retains the model families from Platonov and
Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.5, pp. 331–332,
including the cubic norm-one two-torsion of the trialitarian case.

Choose a Tits index and compute the geometric characters of the full
center in its torus from the fundamental weights and the integral Cartan
relations. In type `A n` the characters are cyclic of order `n + 1` and
the action is by sign. Types `B`, `C`, and `E₇` have character group
order two, and `E₆` has order three. Type `D` has order four, with cyclic
and Klein four groups handled by their respective recognition theorems.
The character groups of `E₈`, `F₄`, and `G₂` are trivial. These
computations give actual kernel isomorphisms through the split and
Weil torsion comparisons. Representability transports the model from
the center in the chosen torus to any representative of the full
scheme-theoretic center.

For a simply connected cover of an absolutely almost-simple adjoint
group, the actual kernel represents the full center of the cover and
the cover is absolutely almost-simple. Applying the center
classification supplies the adjoint center input. The resulting
Hasse reduction proves the published finite-separable statement
conditional on the absolute simply connected Hasse kernel and the
number-field quadratic isometry theorem.
