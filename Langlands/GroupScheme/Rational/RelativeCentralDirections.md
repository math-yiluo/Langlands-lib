# Full-lattice relative Weyl actions and actual central-direction interfaces

Author: Worker 1230.

This internal construction extends the existing actions on actual lattices. It imports the published `Langlands.Geometry.Building.RealTorusAction`, using its `RealTorusSpace.V`, `Vstar`, `pair` and `relativeWeylRealAction` unchanged. Every field and group scheme has the same arbitrary universe parameter as those constructions. No splitness or reductivity is needed for the definitions.

For an actual monomorphism `i : T → G`, `relativeWeylLatticeAction i` reuses `relativeWeylCharacterAction i` from `RelativeWeylCharacterAction`. This shared construction descends the published normalizer action `weylAction i` to the existing quotient `relativeWeylGroup i = N(k)/Z(k)`. The proof that centralizer points act trivially is the published `centralizerPoints_subgroupOf_le_ker`; no quotient scheme or quotient sheaf is substituted for the rational-point quotient.

`relativeWeylFullRatAction i` and `relativeWeylFullRealAction i` extend this action to the full rational and real character spaces. The latter is an action on the existing `RealTorusSpace.Vstar T`. Their pure-tensor formulas send a scalar tensor `χ` to the same scalar tensor the integral action on `χ`. Their quotient-representative formulas recover the normalizer action and `characterRealAut (normalizingAut i n)`. The monoid laws follow from scalar extension preserving the identity and composition.

`pair_relativeWeyl` proves invariance of the existing real pairing under the full character action and the already published real cocharacter action. Quotient induction reduces it to `RealTorusSpace.pair_aut`. For affine `G`, `coe_relativeWeylAction_apply` proves that restriction of the full rational action to `relativeRootSpan i` is precisely the published `relativeWeylAction i`; quotient induction reduces this identity to the defining formula. `relativeWeylLatticeAction_mapsTo_relativeRoots` similarly transfers the published normalizer preservation of all actual relative roots to every quotient class.

`rationalCharacterRestrictReal f` is scalar extension of the actual `characterRestrict f` to `ℝ`. Its pure-tensor computation rule is definitional. In the namespace `RealTorusSpace`, `cocharacterMapInt f` sends an actual cocharacter `ν` to `ν ≫ f` for an actual homomorphism `f : A → T` of commutative group schemes. Additivity follows from the preadditive composition law. `cocharacterMapReal f` is its real scalar extension, with the stated pure-tensor formula.

`rootAnnihilator T R` is the intersection of the kernels of the published actual evaluation maps `eval T α` for all characters `α ∈ R`. Its membership theorem states exactly simultaneous vanishing. The empty set has annihilator the whole real cocharacter space, without a positive-rank premise. `relativeCentralDirections i` specializes this definition to all the actual relative roots. `relativeRootSpanReal i` is the real span of their vectors `1 ⊗ α` inside the full real character space.

`relativeWeylRealAction_mem_relativeCentralDirections` proves invariance of the annihilator subspace. Quotient induction and the published contragredient evaluation formula reduce the result to preservation of roots by the inverse normalizer element. Pointwise fixedness of this subspace, its identification with the image of an actual split component, dimension identities, and complementary root/character decompositions are left to separate intrinsic structural targets.

The provenance is internal: these quotient, scalar-extension, image and kernel interfaces are built from published actual constructions. Borel–Tits, *Groupes réductifs* (1965), §5.1 describes the full lattice actions and their scalar extensions; its Theorem 5.3 and Renard (French 9 July 2026), V.3.6–7 and V.3.12–13 motivate the later central-direction comparisons. No reflection, root-system realization, full-space faithfulness, split-center identification or rank theorem is asserted by these definitions and elementary compatibility proofs.
