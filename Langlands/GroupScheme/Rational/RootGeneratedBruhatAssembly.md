# Actual root-generated Bruhat covering from simple lifts

Author: Worker 1325.

This internal supporting module assembles actual rational root-generated
covering from explicit structural inputs. It does not prove the
unconditional `RootGeneratedCentralizerRankOneStatement`. All root groups
are the Library's actual character-orbit-extension groups at the identity
test object, over an arbitrary field and universe. The ambient setup is
a commutative group scheme `T`, an affine group scheme `G`, and a map
`i : T.toGrp ⟶ G` whose underlying scheme map is a monomorphism. Every
mathematical lemma in this unit additionally assumes `IsSplitTorus T.toGrp`
and an actual relative-root-system realization `HasRelativeRootSystem i`.
The generating-set definition uses the ambient setup alone.

`rootGeneratedBruhatGenerators i ν H m` is the union of the points of
`U(ν)`, a subgroup `H`, and the values of a chosen simple-root-indexed
family `m` of actual normalizer points.

The published `IsRelativeReflection.weylAction_apply_self` translates the
defining reflection identity on the rational root span to
`weylAction i n a = -a` on the actual character lattice, using injectivity
of rationalization for a split torus. This unit reuses that lemma.

`RelativeRoot.rootPoint_factorization_of_reflectionLift` assumes splitness
of `T`, an actual root-system realization, opposite-factor-pair existence, and a chosen
normalizing reflection lift `n` in `L_a = ⟨U_a(k), U_{-a}(k)⟩`. If a
subgroup `M` contains `n` and `Z(k) ∩ L_a`, then every nonidentity point of
`U_a(k)` factors as a point of `U_{-a}(k)`, a point of `M`, and another
point of `U_{-a}(k)`. For an opposite pair, its normalizing product `n'`
lies in `L_a` and represents the reflection in `a`. Uniqueness of that
reflection gives equal Weyl classes of `n` and `n'`. Thus `n⁻¹ n'` lies
in `Z(k) ∩ L_a`, so `n' ∈ M`. Cancelling the pair gives the factorization.
Containment in `M` of every normalizing point of `L_a` is unnecessary.

`rootGenerated_le_closure_unipotent_union_of_weylSurjective` assumes a split
torus, a generic cocharacter, an actual root-system realization and surjectivity
of the actual Weyl projection restricted to normalizing points in `M`.
Every positive root needs no transport. For a negative root, choose its
reflection in the Weyl quotient and lift it inside `M`; that lift sends
the root to its positive opposite. The earlier positive-transport lemma
then places the actual root-generated group in `⟨U(ν)(k), M⟩`.

`mul_mem_rootGenerated_doubleCosets_of_reflectionLift` assumes
`M ≤ N(k)`, splitness of `T`, the actual root-system realization,
genericity, simple-root unipotent factorization and opposite-factor-pair
existence. For a simple root `a` and a chosen reflection
lift `n ∈ M ∩ L_a`, with `Z(k) ∩ L_a ≤ M`, it proves left stability of
`U(ν)(k) M U(ν)(k)` under `n`. It uses the new factorization lemma for
nonidentity root points, the earlier actual sign alternative, and the
generic rank-one multiplication argument.

`hasRootGeneratedBruhatCovering_of_simpleLifts` fixes `H ≤ Z(k)`,
`H ≤ M ≤ N(k)` and a simple-root-indexed family of normalizer points `m`.
It retains all of the following assumptions:

- splitness of `T`, an actual root-system realization and a generic cocharacter;
- simple-root unipotent factorization and opposite-factor-pair existence;
- a Coxeter system on the actual relative Weyl group;
- each `m a` belongs to `M ∩ L_a`, represents that system's corresponding
  simple generator, and is an actual relative reflection in `a`;
- `Z(k) ∩ L_a ≤ H` for every simple root;
- `M ≤ ⟨H, range m⟩`.

The simple generators make the restricted Weyl projection of `M`
surjective. The preceding lemma then places the root-generated group
inside `⟨U(ν)(k), M⟩`, hence inside the subgroup generated by
`rootGeneratedBruhatGenerators`. Points of `H` normalize `U(ν)(k)`
because they centralize the whole torus. Each simple lift preserves the
double-coset union by the rank-one step. Its square lies in `Z(k) ∩ L_a`
and hence in `H`, so its inverse has the form `h * m a` with `h ∈ H`.
The generic subgroup word induction proves the covering.

Provenance is internal composition of published root-reflection,
conjugation and Coxeter-generation results with the earlier supporting
word lemmas. No existence of opposite pairs, root-system realization,
Coxeter system or ordered root products is asserted unconditionally.
