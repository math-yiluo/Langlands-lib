# Commuting partitions of relative roots

This internal supporting unit defines a partition condition for actual relative
root groups: the groups in the chosen set commute with all root groups outside
it on every scheme over the ground field. For an almost-simple group and a
maximal split torus, a chosen subset of roots satisfying this condition is empty
or contains every relative root. No assumption on characteristic is used.

The proof constructs the smooth connected closed subgroup generated by the
chosen root groups. Family generation propagates torus stability and
centralization by the outside root groups. Every root group therefore normalizes
the generated subgroup. Its closed normalizer also contains the split torus;
the published isotropic root-generation theorem makes this normalizer full.
Almost-simplicity then makes the generated subgroup trivial or full.

A root group cannot centralize the acting torus: its full positive Lie image
contains the nonzero root weight space, whereas any Lie vector centralizing the
torus has weight zero. Disjointness of distinct split-torus weight spaces gives
the contradiction. This excludes the trivial generated subgroup. An outside
root group would then centralize the whole group, hence the torus, giving the
same contradiction. These are deductions from published Library constructions,
not a new transcription of a literature result. Relative-root-system
irreducibility and the local affine-diagram target are not asserted here.
