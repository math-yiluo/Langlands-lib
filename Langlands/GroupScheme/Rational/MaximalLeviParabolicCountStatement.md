# Parabolics with a specified maximal Levi: the cardinality bound

`MaximalLeviParabolicCountStatement` states that a finite family of distinct
rational parabolic subgroup images with the same embedded maximal Levi has at
most two members. The field is arbitrary. The ambient group is connected
reductive, and maximality of the supplied proper parabolic means that every
factorization through a rational parabolic has one isomorphism factor.

The common Levi condition is the equality `lQ i ≫ q i = l ≫ p` of actual
group scheme morphisms. Distinctness is injectivity of their subgroup functor
images, so isomorphic presentations of one subgroup are counted only once.
There are no supplied root presentations, torus choices, or topology premises.

This is a derived structural formulation of the upper bound in Renard,
*Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, version of 9 July 2026,
V.4.8, printed p.122 / PDF p.128. The source identifies a maximal Levi by
`l(M) = 2`, with `l(M)` defined through Weyl cosets in V.4.4, printed p.119 /
PDF p.125. The present statement records the related upper bound for distinct
parabolic images with a fixed Levi, rather than a Weyl-coset equality. It does
not assert existence of two such images or construct the Weyl correspondence.

The intended use is the numerical step in the represented maximal-Levi
supercuspidal induction length bound. This statement alone supplies no
geometric filtration, no comparison between surviving double cosets and
parabolics, and no detection of irreducible subquotients.
