# Parabolic radical generation under affine Weil restriction

Author: Worker 5196.

## Lean meaning

`Langlands.GroupScheme.AffineWeilParabolicElementarySubgroupStatement` is a
closed `Prop`-valued definition at an arbitrary universe `u`. It asserts the
following. Let `k K : Type u` be fields with `[Algebra k K]`,
`[FiniteDimensional k K]` and `[Algebra.IsSeparable k K]`. Let `G'` be a
group scheme over `Spec (CommRingCat.of K)` with `IsConnectedReductive G'`.
Write `e := AffineWeil.rationalPointsEquiv k K G'`, the published
multiplicative equivalence

```text
rationalPoints (AffineWeil.groupScheme k K G') ≃* rationalPoints G'
```

between the `k`-points of the Weil restriction `R_{K/k} G'` and `G'(K)`.
Then

```text
(parabolicElementarySubgroup (AffineWeil.groupScheme k K G')).map
    e.toMonoidHom = parabolicElementarySubgroup G'.
```

So the image under `e` of the subgroup of `(R_{K/k} G')(k)` generated by
rational parabolic radicals is exactly the corresponding subgroup of
`G'(K)`. Since `e` is a bijection, this is equivalent to `e` mapping the
first subgroup into the second and `e.symm` mapping the second into the
first.

All objects are published Library objects:

- `IsConnectedReductive` (`Reductive.Basic`);
- `AffineWeil.groupScheme` (`AffineWeilRestriction`);
- `AffineWeil.rationalPointsEquiv` (`Rational.AffineWeilPoints`);
- `parabolicElementarySubgroup` (`Rational.ElementarySubgroup`).

For a group `H` over a field, `parabolicElementarySubgroup H` is the subgroup
of `H(k)` generated by the rational points in the images of all represented
geometric unipotent radicals `r : U ⟶ P` of all actual parabolic subgroups
`p : P ⟶ H`. Its Markdown explains that it is the Library's form of the
group `H(k)^+` of the Kneser–Tits problem. No new definition is introduced.

`AffineWeil.groupScheme` and `AffineWeil.rationalPointsEquiv` need
`[IsAffine G'.X.left]` and `[LocallyOfFiniteType G'.X.hom]`. Both are
obtained inside the statement from the fields of `IsConnectedReductive G'`:

- `hG'.isAffineHom`, through Mathlib's `isAffine_of_isAffineHom`, gives
  `IsAffine G'.X.left`;
- `hG'.smooth`, through Mathlib's instances, gives `LocallyOfFiniteType`.
  Smooth implies locally of finite presentation, which implies locally of
  finite type.

These instances are proof-irrelevant propositions, so no premise or datum is
added beyond those listed above.

This is a statement only; nothing is proved here.

## Source and derivation

Armand Borel and Jacques Tits, *Groupes réductifs*, Publ. Math. IHÉS 27
(1965), registered `borel-tits-1965-groupes-reductifs`, 6.17–6.21, printed
pp.111–113 / PDF pp.58–60. The setting of 6.17 (printed p.111 / PDF p.58) is
the following. `K/k` is finite separable inside `k_s`, `H` is a connected
`K`-group and `H' = R_{K/k} H`. Then
`μ° : H' → σ_1H × … × σ_dH` is a `k_s`-isomorphism.

Borel–Tits do not state the comparison of generated subgroups as one
sentence. It is derived from their results as follows; this is the reason
for the `derived` provenance.

1. **Parabolic subgroups.** By 6.19 (printed pp.111–112 / PDF pp.58–59),
   `P ↦ R_{K/k} P` is a bijection from the parabolic `K`-subgroups of `H`
   onto the parabolic `k`-subgroups of `H'`. The proof uses 6.18 and the
   fact that the parabolic subgroups of a direct product are the products of
   parabolic subgroups of the factors.
2. **Unipotent radicals.** Over `k_s`, `μ°` identifies `R_{K/k} P` with
   `∏ σ_iP`. The unipotent radical of this product is `∏ σ_i R_u(P)`. By
   6.17 (1) applied to `R_u(P)`, this is also `μ°(R_{K/k} R_u(P))`. The
   unipotent radical of the `k`-group `R_{K/k} P` and the `k`-subgroup
   `R_{K/k} R_u(P)` therefore become equal over `k_s`, so they are equal.
   This is the situation of 6.18 (printed p.111 / PDF p.58): connected
   `k`-subgroups of `H'` whose image under `μ°` is a product are exactly the
   restrictions of connected `K`-subgroups of `H`. For the standard parabolic
   subgroups attached to corresponding maximal split tori, Borel–Tits record
   the matching identities in 6.21(i), equations (2) and (3) (printed p.112 /
   PDF p.59): `R_{K/k} H*_ψ = H'*_{β(ψ)}` and
   `R_{K/k}(_K P_θ) = _k P_{β(θ)}`.
3. **Rational points.** The defining property of restriction of scalars
   (6.17) identifies `(R_{K/k} X)(k)` with `X(K)`. In the Library this is
   the published `AffineWeil.rationalPointsEquiv`, which is natural in
   homomorphisms (`AffineWeil.rationalPointsEquiv_map`).

Together these give a correspondence between the generators. They are the
rational points of unipotent radicals of parabolic `k`-subgroups of `H'` and
of parabolic `K`-subgroups of `H`. Hence the generated subgroups correspond
under `e`. This derivation explains the statement; it is not a Lean proof.

## Formulation choices

- **Generators.** The Library's elementary subgroup quantifies over all
  representatives `p : P ⟶ H` and `r : U ⟶ P`. It uses its own geometric
  parabolic and geometric unipotent-radical predicates. The statement uses
  this definition on both sides unchanged.
- **Form of the comparison.** The comparison is stated as `Subgroup.map`
  along the published equivalence, coerced to a monoid homomorphism. It is
  not stated through an abstract isomorphism of groups, so the map
  identifying the two subgroups is the canonical one.
- **Hypotheses on `G'`.** Borel–Tits assume `H` connected (6.17) and
  reductive (6.21(i)). `IsConnectedReductive G'` is the Library's form of
  this hypothesis and the only hypothesis on `G'`.
- **The extension.** Both fields lie in `Type u`. Finiteness and
  separability are explicit, as in 6.17. Separability is used in the
  `k_s`-product decomposition above. Nothing is asserted for inseparable
  extensions. The normalization `K ⊂ k_s` is not imposed, since an embedding
  into a separable closure always exists.
- **Generality.** The characteristic is arbitrary. There is no perfectness,
  infinitude, local-field or topological hypothesis.

## Role

The task that publishes this statement exists for the split-rank-one case of
the local Kneser–Tits problem (Prasad–Raghunathan, *On the Kneser–Tits
problem*, 1985, §2, registered `prasad-raghunathan-1985-kneser-tits`).
Suppose an almost-simple simply connected group `G` is isomorphic to
`R_{K/k} G'`, as in the published
`SimplyConnectedAlmostSimpleWeilRestrictionStatement`. The published
`parabolicElementarySubgroup_map_eq` transports the elementary subgroup along
that isomorphism. The present statement then compares `G(k)^+` with
`G'(K)^+`. The companion `AffineWeilSplitRankStatement` (module
`Rational.AffineWeilSplitRankStatement`) compares the split ranks.
