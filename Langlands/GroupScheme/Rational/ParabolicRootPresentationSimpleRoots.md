# Restricted ambient simple roots and intrinsic component roots

Fix a field, an affine group scheme `G`, and a compatible presentation `r` of
the actual maps `p : P → G`, `l : M → P`, and `a : A → M`. This internal
supporting unit identifies restricted complementary ambient simple roots with
the intrinsic indecomposable positive weights of the specified component,
under explicit ambient root independence and negation hypotheses. It does not
prove those root hypotheses or the unconditional aggregate statement.

## Integral standard-cocharacter lifting

`exists_standardCocharacter_lift` lifts every standard cocharacter of the
presentation torus integrally through the actual map `r.data.f`. The proof
represents the standard Levi by `l ≫ p` and factors the ambient torus through
that representative. The specified component map factors through this torus
map by cancellation of the Levi inclusion. Standard cocharacters are central
in that Levi; the published integral component-range equality therefore supplies
the desired lift. No finite-type assumption or equality of dynamic subgroups
for different standard cocharacters is used.

`standardCocharacterLift` chooses this lift, and
`cocharacterMapInt_standardCocharacterLift` records its exact image.

## Positive weights and simple lifts

The rest of the module assumes `LocallyOfFiniteType G.X.hom`, except for the
uniform cocharacter existence lemma, which explicitly omits it.
`selectedRoot_restrict_eq_zero` applies the published dynamic Lie theorem and
the actual standard-Levi central restriction theorem: every selected root
vanishes on the specified central split component. Restriction consequently
vanishes on the additive monoid generated by the selected roots.

`restrict_positive_eq_zero_or_mem` uses the standard defining cocharacter and
positive-root induction's zero-pairing criterion to show that each positive
ambient root restricts either to zero or to an intrinsic positive root. For
`exists_positiveRoot_restrict`, assume every actual ambient root has its
negative among the actual roots. Weight restriction supplies an ambient lift
of an intrinsic positive weight. Genericity and the nonnegative pairing of
standard cocharacters on positive roots rule out a negative ambient lift.

`exists_simpleRoot_restrict` then lifts every intrinsic simple weight to a
complementary ambient simple root, with the same explicit negation assumption
and without an independence assumption. Its positive-root induction follows
a summand whose restriction is nonzero. A selected simple root would restrict
to zero; two positive restricted summands would contradict intrinsic
indecomposability.

## Uniform heights and the comparison

Assume the rationalized ambient simple roots are linearly independent over
`ℚ`. `exists_uniform_splitComponentCocharacter` uses the published scaled
pairing construction to obtain a cocharacter taking value zero on selected
simples and one common positive integer `d` on complementary simples. Integral
lifting places it in the specified component.

`uniform_pairing_le_of_intrinsicPositive`, also assuming root negation, proves
that every intrinsic positive weight has pairing at least `d` with such a
component cocharacter. A positive ambient lift has pairing either zero or at
least `d`, by induction on positive roots. The common signs of two standard
cocharacters exclude zero. No independence of the restricted characters is
assumed, and distinct ambient roots may have equal restrictions.

`restrict_mem_intrinsicSimpleRoots` now proves that each complementary ambient
simple root restricts to an intrinsic simple root: its height is exactly `d`,
whereas a sum of two intrinsic positive weights has height at least `2d`.
Combining this with `exists_simpleRoot_restrict` proves
`restrictedRoots_eq_image_intrinsicSimpleRoots`. Its equality is in the full
real character space of `A`, using the canonical map `α ↦ 1 ⊗ α`.

## Provenance and remaining scope

This is derived internal content. It combines the published actual standard
Levi, split-component cocharacter range, central root restriction, dynamic Lie,
weight restriction, scaled lattice pairing, and positive-root induction APIs.
The intrinsic root definitions and support independence are supplied by
`ParabolicRootPresentationIntrinsicRoots`; the included dependency pair is
unchanged from that separately reviewed supporting candidate.

The field is arbitrary, and no reducedness, positive rank, characteristic-zero,
or fixed-torus comparison is imposed. The final comparison explicitly retains
rational independence and negation for the ambient presentation roots. General
presentation existence and the discharge of those root hypotheses remain
outside this unit. In particular this is not a proof of the exact aggregate.
