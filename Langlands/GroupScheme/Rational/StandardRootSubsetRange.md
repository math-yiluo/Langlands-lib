# Group ranges from pairing loci

These internal derived lemmas identify the closed groups generated by roots
in a weak, zero, or strict pairing locus with the corresponding dynamic
parabolic, centralizer, or unipotent subgroup. The ambient roots may be the
full relative roots or the indivisible relative roots, and the equalities
hold on every test scheme.

Minimality gives containment in a closed representative of each dynamic
subgroup, using the published root subgroup containments. In the reverse
direction the parabolic uses nonnegative-root closed generation; the Levi
uses the centralizer generation lemma in `StandardRootSubsetGeneration`;
the radical uses the ordered product of its indivisible positive root
groups. Reduction along positive integral rays supplies all roots needed
by the first two generation arguments, also for the indivisible ambient set.
