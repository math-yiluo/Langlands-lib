# Finite-place vanishing of the unit-valued central defect

Let a central isogeny over a number field `F` and a natural cocycle
on a finite Galois cover `L/F` be given, with central lift data over
`L`. A character of the actual kernel sends its affine points to
units, compatibly with coefficient algebra maps. Applying the
character to the multiplication defect gives a unit-valued two-cocycle
and its relative degree-two cohomology class `kernelUnitObstruction`.

Suppose the original natural cocycle is trivial on the pullback cover
at a finite completion `F_v`. At any place `w` of `L` over `v`, the
local primitive theorem gives a primitive in kernel points after
finite Galois refinement of `L_w/F_v`. Apply the character to this
primitive. The resulting unit equation represents the image of
`kernelUnitObstruction` under the actual finite-place map followed
by inflation to the refinement. This identification uses the formula
for the decomposition homomorphism and the compatibility of the
coefficient embeddings through the tower.

The relative degree-two inflation map for units is injective by the
published finite Galois exactness theorem. The image under
`unitsLocalMap` therefore vanishes already over `L_w/F_v`. These
comparisons use the given character and the actual coefficient maps.
