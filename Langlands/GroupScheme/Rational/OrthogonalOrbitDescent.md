# Descent of orthogonal-orbit coroot words to root-generated rational points

Author: Worker 4348.

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.OrthogonalOrbitDescent`.

This module completes the orthogonal-orbit case of the inclusion `Z_G(S)(k) ⊆ G(k)^+` for
quasi-split simply connected groups, at the level of base-changed rational points. It
builds on `Rational.OrthogonalOrbitCorootWords`.

## Pure algebra

`Langlands.Algebra.RankOneRelation.prodHom_mem`: a product
`RankOneRelation.prodHom hD f` lies in every subgroup `K` that contains all the factors
`f i`. This is Mathlib's `Subgroup.noncommProd_mem`.

## Galois descent of rational points

`k ⊆ K` are fields in one universe with `K/k` finite and Galois, and `G` is affine over `k`.

- `GaloisPointComparison.exists_rationalExtend_eq_of_fixed`: a rational point of `G_K`
  fixed by every `rationalConjugate σ` is `rationalExtend g₀` for some `g₀ ∈ G(k)`.
  Transport it by `baseChangeCoeffEquiv` to the affine coefficient points. There it is fixed
  by the coefficient action (`baseChangeCoeffEquiv_rationalConjugate`), so the published
  effective descent `GaloisPoint.mem_range_extend_iff` gives a preimage. Transport back with
  `rationalCoeffEquiv` and `baseChangeCoeffEquiv_rationalExtend`.
- `GaloisPointComparison.exists_mem_rootGroupPoints_of_fixed`: let `G` be smooth, `S` a split
  torus with `i : S ⟶ G`, and `a ≠ 0`. A Galois-fixed rational point of `G_K` that lies in
  the root group of `a_K` for `i_K` is `rationalExtend g₀` with `g₀` in the root group of
  `a` for `i`. This uses the published `rationalExtend_mem_rootGroupPoints_iff` (module
  `Geometry.Building.RootFunctionFieldRestriction`).

## The extension of `G(k)^+`

`relativeRootGeneratedExtension k K i` (definition) is the image of
`relativeRootGeneratedSubgroup i` in the rational points of `G_K` under `rationalExtend`.

## Orbit products

The setting is that of `Rational.OrthogonalOrbitCorootWords`: a split pinning `p` of `G_K`,
normalized simple-root maps `x`, a finite set `O` of pairwise orthogonal simple roots, a
torus map `g : S_K ⟶ p.torus` with `groupHom g ≫ p.inclusion = i_K` (`hg`), and a character
`a` of `S` such that every root in `O` restricts along `g` to `a_K` (`hres`).

- `orbitXp_mem_rootGroupPoints` and `orbitXm_mem_rootGroupPoints`: the products `orbitXp c`
  and `orbitXm c` lie in the root groups of `a_K` and of `(-a)_K` for `i_K`. Each factor
  does, by the published `subgroup_le_subgroup_comp_torusHom` (module
  `Rational.RelativeRootGroupAbsoluteGenerationProof`). The product does, by `prodHom_mem`.
- `exists_orbitXp_eq_rationalExtend` and `exists_orbitXm_eq_rationalExtend`: add Galois
  compatibility (`hx`, `hroot`, the descended torus `T` with `iT`, `eT`, `hT`), stability of
  `O` under `ρ` (`hOs`), splitness of `S` and `a ≠ 0`. Then for an equivariant family `c`,
  these products are extensions of rational points of the root groups of `a` and `-a`. They
  are Galois fixed (`orbitXp_rationalConjugate`, `orbitXm_rationalConjugate`), and the
  preceding descent applies.
- `orbitCoroot_mem_map_relativeRootGeneratedSubgroup`: if moreover `a` and `-a` are relative
  roots of `i`, then for every Galois-equivariant unit family `u`, the orbit product of coroot
  values `orbitCoroot u` lies in `relativeRootGeneratedExtension k K i`. Its word
  `orbitCoroot_eq_word` consists of four equivariant orbit products
  (`IsGaloisEquivariant.word`). Each is the extension of a point of a relative root group,
  hence of `G(k)^+` (`relativeRoot_subgroup_le_generated`).

## Provenance

Internal assembly of published descent and root-group results with the task's helper
modules. This is the orthogonal-orbit part of Steinberg's argument that, for a quasi-split
simply connected group, the rational points of the centralizer of a maximal split torus are
generated by root-group points. The decomposition of a rational centralizer point into orbit
coroot products, and the orbits containing an `A₂` pair, are separate steps.
