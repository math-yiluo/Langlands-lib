# Cohomology of inner type A over totally imaginary number fields

At a totally imaginary number field there is no real place to check in the
real-to-all-places comparison for simply connected groups. Comparing with
the unit cocycle thus proves local triviality at every completion. The Hasse
kernel for inner type A, obtained from reduced-norm locality, gives full
vanishing. The Weil restriction of a simply connected group of type A1 has
the corresponding vanishing by the Hasse comparison for Weil restrictions.
This is the reduced-norm input to Platonov and Rapinchuk,
*Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, pp. 371–373.
