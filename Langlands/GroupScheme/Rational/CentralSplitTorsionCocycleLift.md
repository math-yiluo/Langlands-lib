# Finite Galois lifting through a finite product of roots-of-unity groups

Let `F` have characteristic zero and let `L/F` be finite. A finite
family of units of `L` has roots of its prescribed positive exponents
on one finite Galois refinement over `F`. Choose the roots in the
algebraic closure of `L`, adjoin their finite set, and embed the
resulting finite extension in a finite Galois extension of `F`.
When `L/F` is finite Galois, a finite family of finite-order defects
with unit primitives gives one family of power cocycles. Apply
Hilbert 90 to those power cocycles and use the common radical
extension. Dividing the inflated primitives by the coboundaries
of the radicals gives finite-order primitives for the inflated
defects on the same refinement.

Let `f` be a central isogeny over a number field `F` with a specified
`F`-isomorphism from its actual kernel to a finite product of
roots-of-unity groups of positive exponents. The isomorphism identifies
coefficient points with families of roots, and each coordinate is
the corresponding character of the kernel. Start with a target
cocycle on a finite Galois cover that is trivial at every place.
Choose pointwise lifts on a finite Galois refinement. The character
obstructions of their defect vanish by local triviality. The
simultaneous finite-order primitive gives a kernel-valued primitive
by the coefficient-point equivalence. The central lifting criterion
provides a source cocycle whose image equals the refined target
cocycle on the common refinement.

This internal construction supplies the global lifting step for
finite split products with positive exponents.
