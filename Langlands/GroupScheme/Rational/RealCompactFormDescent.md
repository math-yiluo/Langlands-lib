# Group-scheme descent of the adjusted complex isomorphism

The real descent composes the supplied complex isomorphism with the automorphism induced by the normalizing matrix. Entrywise fixedness of the adjusted matrices implies fixedness of the corresponding rational points by the closed embedding. The extended real points are Zariski dense by the published reductive density theorem and its field-extension comparison. The canonical descent maps agree on this family, so they agree as morphisms from the reduced complex group into the separated target. Affine Galois descent then gives an isomorphism of real group schemes.

These auxiliary results supply the scheme argument for the real isomorphism consequence of Borel, *Lie Groups and Linear Algebraic Groups*, Sections 5.6–5.7, p. 40, source `borel-lie-groups-linear-algebraic-groups-2000`.
