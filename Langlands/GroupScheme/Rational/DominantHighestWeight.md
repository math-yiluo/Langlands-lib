# Highest-weight representation existence at integral dominant characters

Author: Worker 1744.

`SplitPinning.IsDominantWeight p λ` means that the integral character `λ` of the actual supplied torus has nonnegative pairing with every simple coroot of `p.base`. This uses the given perfect integral root-datum pairing. It places no condition on the component of a character perpendicular to all coroots. Nonnegativity on simple coroots is the usual dominance condition: positive coroots are nonnegative integral combinations of the simple coroots.

The unproved `Prop`-valued definition `SplitDominantHighestWeightRepresentationsExistStatement.{u}` quantifies over every algebraically closed field `k : Type u`, every actual affine group scheme `G`, every supplied `p : SplitPinning G` and every dominant integral character of its torus. Connected reductivity and a split maximal torus are supplied by `p`; simple connectedness and full integral coroot span are not hypotheses. The field can have any characteristic.

Its conclusion supplies a natural number `n`, an actual group-scheme homomorphism `ρ : MatrixRepresentation G (Fin n)` and the existing full `p.IsHighestWeight ρ λ` package. In particular, `ρ` is irreducible for subspaces stable over every coefficient algebra, its actual torus character spaces span the original coordinate space, the character space at `λ` has dimension one, and every occurring weight `μ` satisfies `λ - μ ∈ p.positiveRootMonoid`. The monoid is generated by the supplied simple roots. The conclusion does not assert uniqueness, a character formula or compatibility of choices with group isomorphisms.

This is a derived translation of the existence and weight properties in J. S. Milne, *Algebraic Groups: The Theory of Group Schemes of Finite Type over a Field* (2017), registered as `milne-2017-algebraic-groups`, SHA-256 `899b0f44f3c3ccfbaf5ed1cf9c180be7a8016b1c0e9709ca0c206304e5bd1cd0`. Definition 22.1 and Theorem 22.2, printed pp.463–464 (PDF pp.479–480), give existence for every dominant character, dimension one of the highest space and the bound by the simple-root order. The opening of Chapter 22 supplies finite dimensionality and the complete torus-weight decomposition. These pages were read in full.

The source uses a Borel containing the split torus, whereas this statement uses a supplied root-system base. Passing between these positive systems, realizing a finite-dimensional representation in a `Fin n` basis and comparing the source's weight spaces and irreducibility with the Library's all-algebra definitions are retained obligations of a proof. The algebraically closed restriction is weaker than the source's split-field formulation. No representation-existence theorem is inferred merely from the supplied root datum.

The registered author errata snapshot, `J. S. Milne - Errata for Algebraic Groups (2017), HTML snapshot 2026-11-02.html`, was read in full. It does not change Theorem 22.2. A proof using induced modules must respect the correction on printed p.468 to the opposite Borel and the expanded proof on printed p.469. This file defines a prerequisite proposition; it does not prove highest-weight or fundamental-representation existence.
