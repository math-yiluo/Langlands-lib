# Totally imaginary trialitarian vanishing from a cubic-split maximal torus

In the cyclic case the pure inner twist is split over the cubic diagram field. For an anisotropic twist a supplied maximal torus split by that field gives the A₂ coefficient groups and their cocycle vanishing. This trivializes the original cocycle by torus reduction through the pure inner twist. The isotropic twists use the simply connected kernel and cocenter calculation. The quadratic restriction argument gives the symmetric-group case from the cyclic case.

The single input is `TrialitarianCubicSplitMaximalTorusStatement`, the prescribed splitting result for the maximal torus used in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed pp. 371–373.
