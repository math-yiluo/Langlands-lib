# The Lie weights of the generated split subgroup

This internal derived support module, in namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme.MaximalSplitSubgroup`, is part of the proof of
`MaximalSplitSubgroupStatement` (Borel–Tits 1965, Theorem 7.2). It formalizes
part (ii) of Conrad–Gabber–Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive groups*, Theorem C.2.29, in
the setting of the companion module `MaximalSplitGeneratedCell`. That setting is
a group generated by a torus and pairs of opposite lines satisfying the rank-one
relation, over an algebraically closed field.

`b` denotes the conjugation action `AutomorphicAction.conjugation t` of `T` on
`H`, and `semigroupSubgroup b A` the Library's subgroup functor generated by the
smooth connected `T`-stable closed subgroups whose Lie weights lie in the
additive semigroup `A`.

## Declarations

- `cone a`: the additive subsemigroup of the character lattice generated by
  the family `a`.
- `cone_le_positiveSemigroup`: if every `a i` pairs positively with a cocharacter
  `λ`, the cone lies in `positiveSemigroup λ`.
- `cyclicTail_le_cone`: the positive multiples of each `a i` lie in the cone.
- `positive_of_mem_cone_neg`: under the same positivity, the cone of the family
  `-a i` lies in `positiveSemigroup (-λ)`.
- `range_line_le_semigroupSubgroup` (`H` smooth and affine, `T` split): a line of
  nonzero weight `c` lies in `semigroupSubgroup b A` whenever `A` contains the
  positive multiples of `c`.
- `unipotent_eq_semigroupSubgroup_cone`: under the hypotheses of
  `torusCentralizer_eq_range_of_generated` with `T` split, the dynamic unipotent
  subgroup of `λ ≫ t` equals `semigroupSubgroup b (cone a)` on every test.
- `mem_cone_of_mem_relativeRoots` (CGP C.2.29(ii)): under the same hypotheses,
  every relative root of `t` in `H` that pairs positively with `λ` lies in
  `cone a`.

## Proofs

`cyclicTail_le_cone` writes a positive multiple `(m + 1) • a i` as a sum and
inducts on `m`. `range_line_le_semigroupSubgroup` combines
`range_le_subgroup_of_isTorusEquivariant` (the line lies in the relative root
subgroup) with the Library's `weightFiltration_one_eq_subgroup`, which
identifies the relative root subgroup with `semigroupSubgroup b (cyclicTail c 1)`.
Monotonicity in the semigroup then gives the inclusion.

`unipotent_eq_semigroupSubgroup_cone` uses the Library's
`isSmoothConnectedClosed_semigroupSubgroup` (CGP Proposition 3.3.6) to represent
`semigroupSubgroup b (cone a)` and `semigroupSubgroup b (cone (-a))` by closed
subgroups `q` and `q'`. `semigroupRealization` and
`dynamicUnipotentSemigroupComparison_of_semigroupRealization` identify the
dynamic unipotent subgroups of `λ` and `-λ` with the semigroup subgroups of the
positive semigroups. Monotonicity puts `q` and `q'` inside them, where
`groupHom_neg_comp` converts `-λ` into precomposition with inversion. The lines
lie in `q`, respectively `q'`, by `range_line_le_semigroupSubgroup`. The theorem
`isRepresentedBy_dynamicFactors_of_generated` of the companion module then shows
that `q` represents the dynamic unipotent subgroup of `λ`.

`mem_cone_of_mem_relativeRoots`: the previous theorem and the dynamic comparison
give equality of `semigroupSubgroup b (positiveSemigroup λ)` and
`semigroupSubgroup b (cone a)`. The Library's
`actionWeightSupport_semigroupSubgroup` computes the weight support of each as
the full support intersected with the semigroup. So the full support meets
`positiveSemigroup λ` and `cone a` in the same set. A relative root positive on
`λ` lies in the first intersection by `actionWeightSupport_top_inter_positiveSemigroup`,
hence in `cone a`.

## Provenance

CGP Theorem C.2.29, Step 1 (PDF pp. 652–653): the weights of `U` lie in the
semigroup generated by `Δ` (CGP Proposition 3.3.5). Here the identification is
obtained from the dynamic-factor theorem of the companion module applied to the
semigroup subgroup itself, together with the Library's proofs of CGP 3.3.6 and of
the dynamic and semigroup comparisons. No statement is assumed.
