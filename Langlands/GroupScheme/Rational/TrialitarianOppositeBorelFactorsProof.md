# The actual central and leaf factors of the opposite-Borel torus

The quadratic opposite-Borel torus has a base on which the ordinary
Galois action is the star action up to simultaneous negation. The
one-vertex central face gives a simply connected absolutely almost-simple
group of absolute rank one. The complementary face gives a Weil
restriction of a simply connected absolutely almost-simple group
of absolute rank one over the exact cubic leaf field.

The derived maximal tori multiply isomorphically onto the original
torus. The inverse gives the two projections on every test scheme.
The leaf torus lies in the central Levi and acts by conjugation on
its derived group. The two smaller tori lie in the common original
torus, so the action fixes the central torus.

Platonov and Rapinchuk identify these subgroups and the torus product
in *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed p. 374.
The Lean construction uses the derived groups of descended Levi
subgroups and identifies the leaf group by descent from the field
fixed by the stabilizer of a geometric factor.
