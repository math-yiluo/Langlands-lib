# Cohomology of quasi-split E₆ over totally complex number fields

All three statements use an affine locally finite-type simply connected absolutely almost-simple group with actual Tits index data numbered by `CartanMatrix.E 6`.

- `TypeE6MaximalTorusCocycleOrderStatement` concerns a maximal torus of any such group over a characteristic-zero field. Each torus cocycle on a finite Galois cover has a trivial power with exponent of the form \(2^a3^b\), for natural numbers \(a,b\). The power is the image under the corresponding multiplication endomorphism of the torus.
- `NumberFieldTotallyComplexQuasiSplitTypeE6PrimeCoverStatement` says that over a totally complex number field every cocycle of a quasi-split such group on a Galois cover of degree two or three is trivial.
- `NumberFieldTotallyComplexQuasiSplitTypeE6VanishingStatement` says that over a totally complex number field every cocycle of a quasi-split such group on any finite Galois cover is trivial.

Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, give the torus-order statement in Proposition 6.21 on printed pp. 375–377 and the prime-degree vanishing in Theorem 6.29 on printed pp. 378–382. Their argument for quasi-split vanishing combines these with the torus reduction of Proposition 6.19 and the cyclotomic splitting of Proposition 6.20. These statements include the inner and outer quasi-split forms where quasi-splitness is assumed.
