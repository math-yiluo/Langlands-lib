# Isotropy of inner E₆ with a quadratically split maximal torus

Let an affine locally finite-type simply connected group scheme over a field have a numbered E₆ Tits index with trivial star action. If it has a maximal torus that splits over a quadratic Galois extension of the field, then the group is isotropic.

For any torus in an anisotropic affine locally finite-type group, the characters over a Galois extension have no nonzero invariant character. Over a quadratic Galois extension, a character added to its translate under the nonidentity automorphism is invariant. This forces the translate to be its negative. Because the torus in the statement splits over the quadratic field, extending characters to the separable closure is a bijection. A lift of the nonidentity automorphism therefore acts by negation on the entire geometric character lattice.

The proof constructs a pinning using the given maximal torus. Comparison of pinnings transports the trivial star action from the numbered index to this pinning, so the geometric automorphism acting by negation would belong to the Weyl group. Transport back to the numbered root datum contradicts the E₆ Weyl-group computation that negation is outside the Weyl group.

This specializes the opening of Lemma 6.31 of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), printed p. 380 (PDF p. 390). The lemma there proves isotropy over the fields of its arithmetic hypotheses when the ambient group splits over the quadratic extension. Here the supplied maximal torus and the inner E₆ Weyl-group obstruction give the stated conclusion over any field.
