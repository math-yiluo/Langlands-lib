# Induced maximal tori of Borel subgroups of simply connected groups

A maximal torus in a rational Borel splits over a finite Galois
extension. The positive roots defined by the Borel are stable under
Galois, so the simple coroots are permuted. In a simply connected
semisimple group these coroots are an integral cocharacter basis.
The dual fundamental weights thus give a permutation basis of the
character lattice, and the torus is induced.

An induced torus is a Weil restriction of the multiplicative group
from a product of finite separable fields. Picard triviality of
the finite coefficient algebra makes each natural cocycle a
coboundary on its original affine faithfully flat cover.
This gives the Hilbert 90 step at the end of the quasi-split
trialitarian argument in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups
and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed p. 373.
