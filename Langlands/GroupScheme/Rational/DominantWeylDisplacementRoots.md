# Dominant Weyl displacements as sums of simple roots

Author: Worker 2.

This internal helper module supplies the Weyl-group combinatorics used in the
proof of Springer's overlap estimate (Springer, *Reduction theory over global
fields*, 1994, Proposition 2.6; see the public errata note
`notes/errata/springer-1994-reduction-global-fields.md`). The provenance is
derived: the statements are the standard facts that a dominant weight `ψ` has
`ψ - wψ` in the positive root cone, and that this displacement contains the
simple root `α` when `ψ` is a positive multiple of the fundamental weight of
`α` and `w` lies outside the parabolic subgroup `W_(Δ ∖ {α})`. Here they are
proved directly in the integral character lattice, without rational
coefficients, root-system axioms, or a presentation of the parabolic by roots.

All results live in namespace `Langlands.GroupScheme.DominantDisplacement` and
concern a field `k`, a split torus map `i : T → G` into an affine group scheme,
and a cocharacter `ν`. "Sum of simple roots" means membership in the additive
submonoid `AddSubmonoid.closure (simpleRelativeRoots i ν)`.

## Elementary lemmas

* `positive_mem_closure`: every positive relative root is a sum of simple
  relative roots. This is the published induction principle
  `positiveRelativeRoots_induction` applied to closure membership.
* `mem_closure_diff_or_sub_mem`: a sum of simple roots either lies in the
  submonoid generated by `Δ ∖ {α}`, or remains a sum of simple roots after one
  copy of `α` is subtracted. The proof is induction on closure membership.
* `pairing_eq_zero_of_mem_closure`: a cocharacter whose lattice pairing
  vanishes on a set of characters vanishes on every sum of them.

## Coxeter labels

Assuming `Mono i.hom.hom.left` and a finite label type `SimpleRoot i ν`, the
finite set `complementLabels α` lists the simple-root labels different from
`α`. The lemma `rootSet_complementLabels` identifies its root set with
`Δ ∖ {α}`. Given a Coxeter system `cs` realizing the relative Weyl group in the
sense of the published `IsRelativeWeylCoxeterRealization`, the theorem
`standardRelativeWeylGroup_complement_eq` identifies the scheme-theoretic
standard relative Weyl subgroup of `Δ ∖ {α}` with the Coxeter standard subgroup
generated by those labels. This is the last field of the realization.
`standardSubgroup_complement_induction` is closure induction on that standard
subgroup.

## Main theorem

`displacement_mem_closure` takes:

* a Coxeter realization `cs` and a simple label `α`;
* a cocharacter `ζ` that is standard for `Δ ∖ {α}` in the sense of the published
  `IsStandardCocharacter`: it pairs to zero with every root in `Δ ∖ {α}` and
  positively with `α`;
* a character `ψ` fixed by the simple reflections of labels different from `α`,
  with `ψ - s_α ψ = m • α` for some natural number `m > 0`.

For every relative Weyl element `w`, it concludes that `ψ - wψ` is a sum of
simple roots. If `w` lies outside the standard relative Weyl subgroup of
`Δ ∖ {α}`, then `ψ - wψ - α` is also a sum of simple roots.

The proof is strong induction on the Coxeter length of `w`. If `w ≠ 1`, choose
a right descent `a`, so that `w = v s_a` with `v` shorter. The published
realization axiom says that `v(a)` is a positive root, hence a sum of simple
roots. The decomposition used is

`ψ - wψ = (ψ - vψ) + v(ψ - s_a ψ)`.

* If `a ≠ α`, then `s_a ψ = ψ`, so `ψ - wψ = ψ - vψ`. If `w` is outside
  `W_(Δ∖{α})`, then so is `v`, because `s_a` lies in that subgroup. The
  induction hypothesis for `v` then applies.
* If `a = α`, the second summand is `m • v(α)`. This gives the first claim. For
  the second, suppose `w ∉ W_(Δ∖{α})`. If `v ∉ W_(Δ∖{α})`, the induction
  hypothesis for `v` gives the claim. If `v ∈ W_(Δ∖{α})`, then `vψ = ψ`. This
  uses that `ψ` is fixed by the whole subgroup, proved by closure induction. The
  pairing with `ζ` is also invariant under the subgroup: each generating
  reflection has the form `χ ↦ χ - f(χ) • β` with `⟨β, ζ⟩ = 0`. So
  `⟨v(α), ζ⟩ = ⟨α, ζ⟩ > 0`, and the positive root `v(α)` cannot lie in the
  submonoid generated by `Δ ∖ {α}`. By `mem_closure_diff_or_sub_mem`, the
  character `v(α) - α` is a sum of simple roots. Writing `m = m' + 1`, the
  displacement minus `α` is `m' • v(α) + (v(α) - α)`, a sum of simple roots.

The theorem assumes the Coxeter realization and the standard cocharacter as
hypotheses. It does not construct them; the consuming proof obtains both from
published Library results.
