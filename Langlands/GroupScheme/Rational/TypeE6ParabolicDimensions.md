# Lie dimensions of the standard parabolics of split type E₆

Let a simply connected affine locally finite-type group over a field have a split pinning whose based root datum is numbered by the E₆ Cartan matrix. The ambient Lie dimension is 78. For every set of omitted simple vertices, there is an integral cocharacter whose pairing with each omitted simple root is three and whose pairing with every other simple root is zero. Its dynamic parabolic is an actual closed parabolic subgroup. Its Lie dimension plus the signed root count for the omitted set is 78. In particular, the Lie dimensions are 62 for either long-arm endpoint, 57 for the short-arm endpoint and 48 for the short-arm endpoint together with the central vertex.

The simple coroot basis gives torus dimension six, and the 72 roots give 72 one-dimensional root spaces. The columns of the integral matrix satisfying the E₆ Cartan equation give the required cocharacter. The tangent submodule of the parabolic consists of the nonnegative weights of the split torus. It has the negative weight span as a direct complement. Each negative weight is a root of multiplicity one. Applying the cocharacter pairing to the signed coefficient expansion gives three times the sum of the omitted coefficients, so the root count is exactly the codimension in the actual Lie algebra.

The four indicated parabolics are those used in the type E₆ arguments of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.6, Theorem 6.26, printed pp. 354–356 (PDF pp. 364–366), and §6.8, Lemma 6.32, printed p. 380 (PDF p. 390).
