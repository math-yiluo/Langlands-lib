# The modified product `N''` fixes the middle path line

This module is an internal prerequisite for the even type `A` case of the
quasi-split Steinberg quotient task. It proves the matrix-level form of the
inclusion of Steinberg's substitute product `N''` in the hypersurface `f = 0`,
where `f = det(1 - ·)` is taken in the first fundamental representation of the
`A_{2m}` factor (Steinberg 1965, §9.10–9.19, in particular the description of
`N''` before Theorem 9.11 and the strata of 9.19). Steinberg computes in the
standard representation of `SL_{2m+1}`: every factor of `N''` fixes the middle
basis vector. Here the representation is the highest-weight representation of
`Rational.PathFundamentalWeights`, and the middle vector is any nonzero vector of
the line of the path weight `ε_{i+1}` between the two merged simple roots
`β i`, `β (i + 1)`.

## Setting

All declarations are in `Langlands.GroupScheme.SplitPinning`. `p` is a supplied
split pinning of an actual affine group scheme `G` over an arbitrary field `k`.
`ρ` is an actual `MatrixRepresentation G d`. `D : p.SteinbergModifiedData` is
supplied merged-root data (two simple roots `D.a`, `D.b`, a root `D.alpha` with
`α_alpha = α_a + α_b`, a root parametrization `D.xAlpha` and a representative
`D.nAlpha`). `e : Fin q ≃ D.Complement` orders the remaining simple roots, `x`
is a family of normalized simple-root parametrizations and `n` a family of
simple-reflection representatives. `steinbergModifiedProduct D e x n` is the
published morphism `N''` from affine `(q + 1)`-space
(`Rational.SteinbergModifiedRootProduct`). Coordinate `0` is the merged-root
parameter, and coordinate `j + 1` is the parameter of the `j`-th complementary
simple root.

## Definition and matrix formula

- `affineMatrix_steinbergModifiedProduct`: for every commutative `k`-algebra `A`
  and every affine point `c`, the actual matrix of `ρ` at `c ≫ N''` is the
  ordered product
  `ρ(x_α(c₀)) ρ(n_α) ∏_j (ρ(x_(e j)(c_{j+1})) ρ(n_(e j)))`. Root factors are
  affine matrices of the restricted additive representations. Representative
  factors are rational matrices mapped to `A`.
- `steinbergModifiedPolynomialMatrix ρ D e x n` (definition): the same ordered
  product with entries in `MvPolynomial (Fin (q + 1)) k`. Its root factors are the
  universal matrices `univariateMatrix` evaluated at the coordinate variables, and
  its representative factors are constant matrices.
  `steinbergModifiedPolynomialMatrix_aeval` shows that evaluation at `c` gives the
  actual matrix above. `steinbergModifiedPolynomialMatrix_eq_affineMatrix`
  identifies it with the actual matrix at the universal point, which is the
  identity point of affine space.

## Results

- `affineMatrix_mulVec_eq_self_of_dividedPower`: if all positive divided powers
  of an additive representation kill `v`, its matrix at every affine coordinate
  fixes the scalar extension of `v`.
- `det_one_sub_eq_zero_of_mulVec_eq_self`: let `M` be a matrix over a commutative
  `k`-algebra fixing the scalar extension of a nonzero `v : d → k`. Then
  `det (1 - M) = 0`.
- `det_one_sub_affineMatrix_steinbergModifiedProduct_eq_zero` (general form):
  assume `p.IsHighestWeight ρ lam`, `IsPathComponent β`,
  `IsPathFundamental β lam`, and let `v ≠ 0` lie in the line of `ε_j`. Suppose
  no positive multiple of the merged root, or of any complementary simple root,
  shifts `ε_j` to a path weight. Suppose also that the merged representative and
  all complementary representatives fix `v`. Then for every `A` and `c`,
  `det (1 - ρ(c ≫ N'')) = 0`.
- `det_one_sub_affineMatrix_steinbergModifiedProduct_eq_zero_of_path`: suppose
  `D.a = β i` and `D.b = β (i + 1)` with `i + 2 ≤ L`, the complementary
  representatives are `canonicalSimpleReflectionRepresentatives x`, and
  `D.nAlpha.point` lies in the subgroup generated by the rational root subgroups
  of `± α_alpha`. Then for every nonzero `v` in the line of `ε_{i+1}` and every
  affine point, `det (1 - ρ(N''))` vanishes.
- `det_one_sub_steinbergModifiedPolynomialMatrix_eq_zero_of_path`: under the same
  hypotheses, `det (1 - M) = 0` for the polynomial matrix `M` of `N''`.

## Proof as implemented

The matrix formula pushes the monoid homomorphism `A ↦` matrix through the
point formula `comp_steinbergModifiedProduct`. It mirrors the published
`affineMatrix_steinbergProduct`. Root factors are rewritten with
`VectorGroup.coordinates_affinePoint`, `affineMatrix_restrict` and
`VectorGroup.affinePoint_eq_scalarPoint`. Constant factors are rewritten with
`affineMatrix_constantPoint`. The polynomial matrix is compared through
`univariateMatrix_aeval`, `Polynomial.aeval_algHom_apply` and
`MvPolynomial.aeval_C`. The universal point is handled with `VectorGroup.affinePoint_X`.

For the vanishing, each root factor fixes the scalar extension of `v`. Its
equivariance character is the root of the factor (the field
`torus_equivariant`), so by `PathRootActions.dividedPower_eq_zero_of_shift_ne`
all its positive divided powers kill `v`. Then the published
`affineMatrix_mulVec_eq_sum` expansion reduces to the constant term. Each
representative factor fixes `v` by hypothesis, so the ordered product fixes it
by induction on the list. Finally `adjugate (1 - M) * (1 - M) = det (1 - M) • 1`
gives `det (1 - M) • w = 0` for the extension `w` of `v`. A nonzero coordinate of
`v` is a unit of `k`, whose image is a unit of `A`.

In the path form, `α_alpha = (ε_i - ε_{i+1}) + (ε_{i+1} - ε_{i+2}) = ε_i - ε_{i+2}`
(`root_path_eq_sub`). By the shift classification of `PathRootActions`, no
positive multiple moves `ε_{i+1}` to a path weight. Consider a complementary
simple root `z`. If `z = β s`, then `s ≠ i, i + 1` because `z ≠ D.a, D.b`, and the
classification again applies. If `z` is off the path,
`pathWeight_add_nsmul_root_ne` applies. The representatives fix `v` by
`PathLineStabilizers.rootSup_pathRoot_fixes` (merged root),
`canonicalReflection_path_fixes` and `canonicalReflection_offPath_fixes`.

## Scope

The module does not construct the class function `f` or relate it to the
canonical quotient. It does not treat `N'''` or the torus factor, and it proves
no isomorphism onto the stratum `f = 0` (Steinberg 9.19). It does not show that
data with the stated properties exist. These remain obligations of the host task.

The additive fixed-vector helper reuses `MatrixRepresentation.AdditiveDividedPowers.affineMatrix_mulVec_eq_self_of_dividedPower_eq_zero`; the conditions `1 ≤ m` and `0 < m` on natural numbers coincide.
