# A maximal torus that splits over a quadratic splitting field

Let an affine locally finite-type connected reductive group over an infinite field have a split maximal torus after a quadratic Galois extension. Then it has a maximal torus defined over the original field that splits over this extension.

The split maximal torus over the quadratic field is its own centralizer, so the group over that field is quasi-split. The quadratic opposite-Borel construction gives a maximal torus over the ground field whose scalar extension is the intersection of a geometric Borel and its conjugate. In particular, the scalar-extended torus lies in that Borel.

A geometric Borel contains a maximal split torus of the ambient group. Conjugacy of maximal split tori shows that this torus is also maximal among all tori when the ambient group has a split maximal torus. The two torus inclusions factor through the Borel by the subgroup functor containments. They are both maximal in the Borel and are conjugate there over the quadratic field by rational conjugacy of maximal tori in smooth connected affine solvable groups. This makes the scalar extension of the descended torus split.

The conclusion is the choice of maximal torus used in Lemma 6.31, which cites Lemma 6.23, of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), printed p. 380 (PDF p. 390). [Chapter 6, Galois cohomology](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711).
