# Multiplicity one at the line weights of the generated split subgroup

This internal derived support module, in namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme.MaximalSplitSubgroup`, is part of the proof of
`MaximalSplitSubgroupStatement` (Borel–Tits 1965, Theorem 7.2). It proves the
multiplicity-one consequence of Step 2 of the proof of Conrad–Gabber–Prasad,
*Pseudo-reductive groups*, Theorem C.2.29, over an algebraically closed field. The
setting is the one of the companion modules `MaximalSplitGeneratedCell` and
`MaximalSplitGeneratedWeights`: a group `H` generated by a split torus `t : T ⟶ H`
and pairs of opposite lines `x i`, `y i` of weights `a i`, `-a i` satisfying the
rank-one relation.

`Ga` is the additive group `Ga k`. A *line of weight `c`* is a homomorphism
`x : Ga ⟶ H` with `VectorGroup.IsTorusEquivariant t x c`.

## Declarations

- `IsGeneratedByFamily.self`: if `v : V ⟶ G` is the closed subgroup generated by
  a family `a j : A j ⟶ G`, and `b j : A j ⟶ V` are lifts (`b j ≫ v = a j`), then
  `V` is generated, as its own ambient group (`𝟙 V`), by the family `b`.
- `smooth_ga`, `isAffineHom_ga`, `connectedSpace_ga`: `Ga` is smooth, affine and
  connected over `Spec k`.
- `IsIsolatingCocharacter a i₀ eta` (definition): the cocharacter `eta` kills
  `a i₀` and pairs positively with every other `a j`.
- `range_le_parabolic_of_lines`: if `eta` isolates `i₀`, every line `x j` lies in
  the dynamic parabolic subgroup of `eta ≫ t`. The line `x i₀` lies in the
  dynamic centralizer; the others lie in the dynamic unipotent subgroup.
- `range_subset_of_dense_rationalPoints`: let `φ : V ⟶ H` be a morphism of
  schemes and `e : E ⟶ H` a homomorphism that is a closed immersion. Suppose `φ`
  maps a set `S` of rational points of `V` with dense locations into the
  rational points of the range of `e`. Then the image of `φ` lies in the image
  of `e`.
- `centralizer_points_mem_range_line` (`k` algebraically closed, `H` smooth and
  affine): let `v : V ⟶ H` be the closed subgroup generated by the lines `x i`,
  with `V` smooth and affine. Suppose `eta` isolates `i₀` and `x i₀` is a
  monomorphism. Then every point of `V`, on any test, that centralizes
  `eta ≫ t` lies on the line `x i₀`.
- `weightSpace_le_semigroupWeightSpan`: the weight space of a weight `χ` in a
  semigroup `A` lies in `semigroupWeightSpan` of the conjugation action and `A`.
- `weightSpace_eq_lie_range` (consequence of CGP C.2.29, Step 2): assume the hypotheses of
  `torusCentralizer_eq_range_of_generated`, with `T` split: a cocharacter `cch`
  pairs positively with every `a i`, the lines commute across distinct indices,
  and the generated rational points are dense. Assume also that `eta` isolates
  `i₀` and `x i₀` is a monomorphism. Then the weight space of `a i₀` in the Lie
  algebra of `H` equals the range of the differential
  `Lie.targetMapLinear (x i₀) k` of the line.
- `finrank_weightSpace_eq_one`: under the same hypotheses this weight space has
  dimension one.

## Proofs

`IsGeneratedByFamily.self`: any closed subgroup `q` of `V` containing the lifts
gives a closed subgroup `q ≫ v` of `G` containing the family. Generation of `v`
puts `range v` inside `range (q ≫ v)`. Cancelling the monomorphism `v` gives
`range (𝟙 V) ≤ range q`.

`range_le_parabolic_of_lines` uses `range_le_subgroup_of_isTorusEquivariant`
(each line lies in its relative root subgroup) and the Library's
`RelativeRoot.subgroup_le_dynamicCentralizer_of_pairing_eq_zero` and
`RelativeRoot.subgroup_le_dynamicUnipotent`.

`range_subset_of_dense_rationalPoints`: the range of the closed immersion is
closed and contains the image of the dense set of locations. By continuity it
contains the image of its closure, which is everything.

`centralizer_points_mem_range_line`: the dynamic parabolic of `eta ≫ t` is
represented by a closed subgroup (`Dynamic.Affine.isRepresentedBy_parabolicHom`).
Generation of `V` and `range_le_parabolic_of_lines` put `V` inside it. Let `φ` be
`V` followed by the limit homomorphism `Dynamic.limitGroupHom` onto the dynamic
centralizer, and then by the inclusion into `H`. On points, `φ` is
`g ↦ lim_{s → 0} eta(s) g eta(s)⁻¹`. It fixes the points of `x i₀`, which
centralize `eta`. It kills the points of the other lines, which lie in the
dynamic unipotent subgroup (`Dynamic.limit_eq_one_iff`). So `φ` maps the
subgroup generated by the rational points of the lifted lines into the rational
points of `x i₀`. These generated points are dense in `V`, by the Library's
`LeviGluing.dense_rationalPointImage_closure_of_isGeneratedByFamily` applied to
`IsGeneratedByFamily.self`. The line `x i₀` is a closed subgroup
(`HomImage.isClosedSubgroup_of_mono`). Then `range_subset_of_dense_rationalPoints`
and `LeviGluing.factorsThrough_of_range_subset` (`V` is reduced, being smooth)
factor `φ` through `x i₀`. A point of `V` that centralizes `eta` is its own
limit, so it equals its image under `φ` and lies on `x i₀`.

`weightSpace_eq_lie_range`: the inclusion of the Lie range in the weight space
is `IsTorusEquivariant.lie_range_le_weightSpace`. Conversely, let `V` be the
smooth closed subgroup generated by the lines `x i`
(`FiniteGeneration.exists_smooth_generated_family`). It lies in the dynamic
unipotent subgroup `U` of `cch ≫ t`. The lines `y i` lie in the dynamic
unipotent subgroup of the opposite cocharacter, where `groupHom_neg_comp` turns
`-cch` into precomposition with inversion. So `isRepresentedBy_dynamicFactors_of_generated`
from `MaximalSplitGeneratedCell` shows that `V` represents `U`. Let `X` be in the
weight space of `a i₀`. It lies in the tangent space of `U`, since `a i₀` pairs
positively with `cch`, and in the tangent space of the dynamic centralizer of
`eta ≫ t`, since `a i₀` pairs to zero with `eta`. Both facts use the Library's
semigroup-weight-span descriptions (`tangentSubmodule_unipotent_eq_semigroupWeightSpan`,
`tangentSubmodule_centralizer_eq_semigroupWeightSpan`). As a point of `U` over
the dual numbers, `X` comes from a point of `V` that centralizes `eta ≫ t`. By
`centralizer_points_mem_range_line` it lies on `x i₀`, so `X` lies in the
tangent range of `x i₀` (`tangentSubmodule_range`).

`finrank_weightSpace_eq_one`: the differential of the monomorphism `x i₀` is
injective (`Lie.targetMapLinear_injective`). The Lie algebra of `Ga` is
one-dimensional, by the coordinate equivalence
`RelativeRoot.vectorLieCoordinates`.

## Provenance

CGP Theorem C.2.29, Step 2 of the proof (printed p. 627, PDF p. 653), shows
that for `a ∈ Δ` the `a`-root group of the generated group is the given line
`E_a`. Its Lie algebra is then the `a`-weight space, which is one-dimensional.
CGP use the ordered-product decomposition of CGP Theorem 3.3.11. The formal
proof instead projects with the limit homomorphism of an isolating cocharacter,
combined with density of the generated rational points. It proves only the
statement about the weight space of `a` itself. The definition
`IsIsolatingCocharacter` is a local abbreviation for the hypothesis on that
cocharacter. No statement is assumed.
