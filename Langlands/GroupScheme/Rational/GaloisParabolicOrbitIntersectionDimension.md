# Lie dimension of a descended Galois parabolic intersection

Let an affine locally finite-type connected reductive group over a field have a parabolic subgroup after a finite Galois extension. Suppose the Lie codimension of the parabolic over the extended field is at most a natural number `c`. The full intersection of its Galois translates descends as an actual closed subgroup over the ground field. The Lie dimension of the ambient group over the extended field is at most the ground-field Lie dimension of that subgroup plus `c` times the extension degree.

The tangent submodule of the intersection of two closed subgroup functors is the intersection of their tangent submodules. Their tangent dimensions therefore satisfy the subspace dimension inequality. Induction realizes each finite intersection as a closed subgroup and adds the codimension bounds. A parabolic is smooth and connected, so each Galois-translated parabolic has the same Lie dimension by preservation of smooth relative dimension. This includes all elements of the finite Galois group, even when their translates coincide. Permuting the translates makes the full intersection invariant, and closed-subgroup descent yields the subgroup over the original field with equality of subgroup functors after extension. Scalar extension preserves Lie dimension by the finite Lie coordinates.

This proves the dimension estimate used in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.6, Theorem 6.26, printed pp. 354–356 (PDF pp. 364–366), and §6.8, Lemma 6.32, printed p. 380 (PDF p. 390).
