# Binary skew forms with proportional diagonal values at finite places

`NumberFieldBalancedBinarySkewStatement` concerns a quaternion division algebra over a number field, with its standard conjugation. For a nonzero pure coefficient `x` and a nonzero ground-field scalar `s`, the binary Gram matrix with diagonal `x, s • x` represents every skew coefficient of the extended quaternion algebra at all but finitely many finite places.

Write `c` for the scalar square of `x`. The quadratic norm equation `a² - c b² = -s` is solvable at all but finitely many finite places. In the scalar extension of the embedded field generated by `x`, the corresponding element has conjugate product `-s`. Its image `z` in the quaternion algebra commutes with `x`, and `z * x * τ(z) = -(s • x)` in the completion. The row with entries `z, 1` is therefore isotropic and has an invertible second coordinate. The row representation theorem gives all skew values in the actual coefficient algebra.

This is a derived proof of the almost-everywhere representation used in Springer's appendix to Kneser's *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups* (1969), Lemma 5.3.1 and Theorem 5.3.3(ii), printed pp. 108–112 (PDF pp. 114–118), for the proportional diagonal constructed there.
