# Ground-field eigenvalues of anisotropic torus differentials

Let \(k\) have characteristic zero and let \(G\) be an anisotropic affine group scheme of locally finite type over \(k\). For a closed torus \(T\) of \(G\), the differential of a matrix representation of \(T\) at a \(k\)-rational tangent point can have a \(k\)-rational eigenvalue only if it is zero. `AnisotropicTorusTangentEigenvaluesStatement` expresses this for the tangent matrix defined by evaluation on dual numbers.

The proof extends to a finite Galois splitting field of \(T\). The character spaces span the representation, and each lies in the eigenspace for the derivative of its character. Independence of distinct eigenspaces ensures that the given eigenvalue arises from an occurring character. All Galois translates of this character occur with the same derivative, since the tangent matrix and eigenvalue come from the ground field. The sum of the translates is Galois-fixed and hence zero by anisotropy. The character derivative is additive, so the Galois group order times the eigenvalue is zero. Characteristic zero and injectivity of the scalar extension give the conclusion.

This is a differential consequence of the vanishing of Galois-fixed characters of tori in an anisotropic group. It is used to analyze connected subgroups of anisotropic groups in the parabolic intersection argument. See Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Theorem 6.26, pp. 354–356, and §6.8, Lemma 6.32, p. 380.
