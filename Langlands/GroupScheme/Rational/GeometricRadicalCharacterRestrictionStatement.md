# Rational and geometric character restriction to the solvable radical

Let \(r:R\to G\) be the solvable radical of a connected reductive group over
\(k\), with \(r\) remaining the solvable radical after passage to the
algebraic closure \(\bar k\). The actual base-change map embeds the rational
characters of \(G\) into the geometric characters of \(G_{\bar k}\).
Restriction along \(r_{\bar k}\) embeds the geometric characters into those
of \(R_{\bar k}\), with finite-index image. These are homomorphisms of
character groups with values in the multiplicative group scheme over the
indicated field.

This is the pair of lattice inclusions following the displayed abelianization
formula in David Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*,
French edition dated 9 July 2026, V.2.2, printed p. 105 (PDF p. 111).
Finite index expresses the full rank of the geometric character lattice
inside the character lattice of the geometric radical. Both inclusions use
the original scalar extension and subgroup maps.
