# The E₆ Levi omitting the short-arm endpoint and the center

`TypeE6ShortArmCenterLeviDimensionStatement` constructs the rational parabolic of a simply connected E₆ group when the short-arm endpoint and the center form a star-stable marked set. The positive pairings on those two simple roots define the cocharacter. The ambient group, its dynamic parabolic and its dynamic Levi have Lie dimensions \(78\), \(48\) and \(18\), respectively.

The Levi dimension is computed after scalar extension to a separable closure. The actual split maximal torus of the ambient pinning factors into the dynamic centralizer, where it remains maximal. Its root system in that centralizer is the set of absolute roots pairing to zero with the cocharacter. The signed E₆ coordinates give exactly twelve such roots. Their contribution and the six-dimensional torus give the dimension \(18\). Compatibility of dynamic centralizers and Lie dimension with scalar extension gives the same dimension over the original field.

These are the dimensions for the second parabolic in the outer E₆ argument of Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, Theorem 6.26, printed p. 356. [Chapter 6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079816908620711)
