# Absolute pinnings adapted to a quadratic opposite-Borel torus

The cocharacter of the opposite-Borel torus extends through the
canonical tower identifications on the torus and ambient group.
The extended centralizer and positive unipotent subgroup agree
with the base changes of their original subgroup functors.
Transport of the dynamic unipotent subgroup along a coefficient
automorphism is the unipotent subgroup of the transported
cocharacter. These comparisons use equality on every test scheme
and retain the original inclusion of the maximal torus.

The extended cocharacter is regular, since its dynamic centralizer
is the maximal torus. It defines a base of the absolute root datum.
The identity isomorphism of the ambient group identifies this based
datum with that of the given Tits index and intertwines their star
actions. The conjugacy of the two opposite unipotents shows that
each coefficient automorphism either preserves the positive roots
or interchanges them with the negative roots. In type \(D_4\) the
longest Weyl element acts by negation, so the ordinary Galois action
on this datum is the star action up to simultaneous negation.

The construction concerns the root geometry of the opposite-Borel
torus in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*
(1994), §6.8, printed pp. 369–370 and 374.
