# Proof of `StandardLeviSplitComponentStatement`

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.StandardLeviSplitComponentProof`.

## Statement

`standardLeviSplitComponentStatement : StandardLeviSplitComponentStatement.{u}` proves the
published statement `Langlands.GroupScheme.StandardLeviSplitComponentStatement` (module
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.StandardLeviSplitComponentStatement`) at an arbitrary universe
`u`. It uses all of the statement's hypotheses and nothing more.

Over every field `k`, let `G` be connected reductive, `i : T ⟶ G` a maximal split torus, `ν` a
generic cocharacter and `J ⊆ simpleRelativeRoots i ν`. Then the specific group scheme
`Standard.leviScheme i ν J` is connected reductive. It also has a split component
`a : A ⟶ Standard.leviScheme i ν J` and a homomorphism `g : A ⟶ T` with
`g ≫ i = a ≫ Standard.leviHom i ν J`. The torus `i` is the same for every index, and the
statement asks only for existence for each index.

The proof does not use `StandardCocharacterStatement` or the conditional reduction
`standardLeviSplitComponent_of_structural_statements`. It also does not use the hypotheses
`IsGenericCocharacter i ν` and `J ⊆ simpleRelativeRoots i ν`: the argument works for every
cocharacter `ν` and every set `J`.

## Proof

The core step identifies the choice-free standard Levi functor with one dynamic centralizer:
`exists_standardLevi_eq_dynamicCentralizer` shows that there is a cocharacter `ζ` of `T` with
`standardLevi i ν J = Dynamic.centralizer (groupHom ζ ≫ i)`. The theorem needs only
`IsConnectedReductive G` and `IsSplitTorus T`. Recall that `standardLevi i ν J` is defined as the
pointwise intersection of the dynamic centralizers of all standard cocharacters for `J`.

1. **Centralizer monotonicity** (`dynamicCentralizer_le_of_zero_imp`). Suppose every actual
   relative root that pairs to zero with `ζ` also pairs to zero with `η`. Then
   `Dynamic.centralizer (groupHom ζ ≫ i) ≤ Dynamic.centralizer (groupHom η ≫ i)`. The published
   `RelativeRoot.dynamicSemigroupComparison` (module `Rational.DynamicSemigroupProof`) rewrites
   each centralizer as the subgroup generated by the conjugation action for the semigroup
   `RelativeRoot.zeroSemigroup`. The published
   `RelativeRoot.rootSupport_imp_iff_actionWeightSupport_imp` (module
   `Rational.RelativeRootSignSupport`) turns the root hypothesis into containment on the ambient
   action-weight support; the zero character pairs to zero with every cocharacter. The published
   `RelativeRoot.semigroupSubgroup_mono_on_weightSupport` (module `Rational.WeightSemigroupSupport`)
   then gives the inclusion.

2. **Closure of standard cocharacters** (`isStandardCocharacter_nsmul_add`). If `ζ` and `η` are
   standard for `J`, so is `m • ζ + η` for every `m : ℕ`. Pairings are additive: they stay zero
   on `J` and stay positive on the remaining simple roots.

3. **A minimal standard cocharacter** (`exists_isStandardCocharacter_zero_minimal`). Assume the
   actual relative roots are finite and some standard cocharacter exists. Choose, with
   `Nat.find`, a standard `ζ` for which the set of relative roots pairing to zero with `ζ` has the
   smallest `Set.ncard`. Suppose some root `α` pairs to zero with `ζ` but not with a standard `η`.
   Let `M` bound `|⟨β, η⟩|` over all relative roots `β`, and set `ξ = (M + 1) • ζ + η`, which is
   standard by step 2. The arithmetic lemma `natCast_succ_mul_add_ne_zero` shows that
   `(M + 1) * p + q ≠ 0` when `p ≠ 0` and `|q| ≤ M`. So every root that pairs to zero with `ξ`
   already pairs to zero with `ζ`, while `α` pairs with `ξ` to `⟨α, η⟩ ≠ 0`. The zero set of `ξ`
   is therefore strictly smaller (`Set.ncard_lt_ncard`), contradicting minimality.

4. **The identification.** If some standard cocharacter exists, take `ζ` from step 3. Finiteness
   of the relative roots comes from the published `IsSplitTorus.finite_relativeRoots` (module
   `Rational.RootSupportFiniteness`), using smoothness of `G` for local finite type.
   `standardLevi_le_dynamicCentralizer` gives one inclusion. Step 1, applied to `ζ` and each
   standard `η`, gives the other through `mem_standardLevi`. If no standard cocharacter exists, the
   intersection is the whole group functor. Take `ζ = 0`: by `comp_groupHom_zero` the zero
   cocharacter has identity value at every point, so its orbit map fixes every point and its
   dynamic centralizer is also the whole group functor (`Dynamic.mem_centralizer`,
   `Dynamic.orbit`).

The main theorem then proceeds as follows:

- `Standard.isRepresentedBy_leviHom` (module `Rational.StandardRepresentability`), rewritten by the
  identification, shows that `Standard.leviHom i ν J` represents
  `Dynamic.centralizer (groupHom ζ ≫ i)`. `Standard.isClosedSubgroup_leviHom` shows that it is
  closed.
- The published `leviSplitComponentStatement` (module `Rational.LeviSplitComponentProof`), applied
  to `G`, `i`, `ζ` and this representative, gives connected reductivity of
  `Standard.leviScheme i ν J`. For every split component of `G` it also gives a split component
  `a` of the Levi with a map `g : A ⟶ T` satisfying `g ≫ i = a ≫ Standard.leviHom i ν J`.
- The published `splitComponentStatement` (module `Rational.SplitComponentProof`) supplies a split
  component of `G`. The theorem keeps `a`, `g` and these two facts and discards the extra
  ambient-component factorization and character-restriction equation.

A closing `example` checks the type `StandardLeviSplitComponentStatement.{u}` of the theorem. The
auxiliary results `dynamicCentralizer_le_of_zero_imp`, `isStandardCocharacter_nsmul_add`,
`natCast_succ_mul_add_ne_zero`, `exists_isStandardCocharacter_zero_minimal` and
`exists_standardLevi_eq_dynamicCentralizer` are in the namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme.StandardLeviSplitComponent`.

## Scope and provenance

This proof is internal. It combines the published proofs `leviSplitComponentStatement`,
`splitComponentStatement` and `RelativeRoot.dynamicSemigroupComparison` with an elementary
argument on finitely many root pairings. The proof shows that the standard Levi functor equals
the centralizer of one standard cocharacter whenever one exists. It does not prove that standard
cocharacters exist, and it does not prove the parabolic and unipotent parts of
`StandardCocharacterStatement`. Those remain with their own proof task. The mathematics follows
Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, V.3, as cited by the Markdown of the
generic Levi and split-component statements. The route here is the one described above.
