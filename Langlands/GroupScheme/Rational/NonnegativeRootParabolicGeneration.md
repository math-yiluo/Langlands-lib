# Closed generation of a dynamic parabolic by nonnegative roots

This internal result combines the published smooth connected generation, intrinsic root functors, dynamic semigroup comparison, torus centralizer geometry and Lie algebra comparison. It concerns a supplied smooth connected affine group over an arbitrary field and a supplied split subtorus. No reductivity or maximality hypothesis is imposed.

The nonnegative root functors lie in the cocharacter's dynamic parabolic, including roots with zero pairing, by the cyclic-tail semigroup comparison. Form the smooth connected closed subgroup generated by those root representatives and the whole-torus centralizer. All generators lie in the dynamic parabolic. Their tangent images contain its complete nonnegative weight span: nonzero weights come from their intrinsic root functors and the zero weight comes from the torus centralizer. The generated subgroup therefore equals the dynamic parabolic by the published smooth subgroup Lie equality theorem. Its minimality among closed subgroups gives containment in every closed competitor containing the centralizer and all nonnegative roots. This competitor need not be smooth or connected.

`HasNonnegativeRootParabolicGeneration i nu` is a proposition universally
quantifying the closed competitor and recording this all-test containment
criterion. `hasNonnegativeRootParabolicGeneration` proves it under the stated
smoothness, connectedness, affineness and split-subtorus assumptions. Every
declaration is at arbitrary universe `u`.

The result is a supporting internal composition. It does not identify an
integral-model image with the dynamic parabolic, prove that image parabolic,
realize every parabolic by a facet, or prove the descended facet order theorem.
