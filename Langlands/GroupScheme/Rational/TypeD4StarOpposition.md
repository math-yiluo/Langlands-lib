# The ordinary and star actions for D4 opposition

`GaloisPinningData.starAction_eq_negAut_mul_of_D4_opposite` compares the actual Galois and star actions on a simply connected integral D₄ root datum. Its hypothesis says the coefficient automorphism carries the simple base to its negative. The project root datum identifies that negative base with the image under full negation of characters and cocharacters. Negation belongs to the D₄ Weyl group, so the projection to based automorphisms sends it to the identity. Multiplying the ordinary action by negation preserves the base, and the based projection consequently returns that product.

This computation is the root-datum step used to identify the ordinary action of the automorphism exchanging two opposite Borels in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed p. 374. For an automorphism with trivial star action the identity in the conclusion determines the ordinary action as full negation.
