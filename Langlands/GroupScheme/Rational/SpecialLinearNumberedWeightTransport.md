# Numbered weights of an actual special-linear chart

An actual isomorphism from a split group to `SL_m` with `m ≥ 2` supplies a
numbering of the simple vertices of a supplied split pinning whose coroots
generate the integral cocharacter lattice. A rational
inner correction aligns both the torus and the simple roots with the standard
adjacent-root numbering for the diagonal torus of `SL_m`. The Cartan pairings
are preserved by the based root datum equivalence.

With this numbering, the pullback of the standard column representation
along the original isomorphism has first fundamental highest weight. The
based transport lemma identifies the highest weight after alignment, and the
matrix of the rational correcting point gives an invertible intertwiner on
every affine test. The sum of the first and last fundamental weights pulls
back the root given by the first diagonal character minus the last diagonal
character.

This internal comparison uses the actual split pinning, representation and
root datum. In particular it applies to the splitting chart of the reduced
norm-one group of a central simple algebra.
