# Quadratic cocycle values at real places

For the pullback of a finite Galois cover, extend the original
coefficients to a compatible local field. The comparison on the local
field cover intertwines Galois values with restriction of automorphisms.
If the local field is quadratic over the local base, the cocycle is a
coboundary precisely when its coefficient at the nonidentity
automorphism satisfies the crossed coboundary equation. The identity
coefficient is forced by normalization. The field cover is an fpqc
refinement of the whole pullback, so the local trivialization reflects
to the whole pullback.

For a complex place above a real place, the actual completion has local
degree two. Transport complex conjugation through the completion
isometry. It fixes the image of the real completion and cannot fix the
image of the original coefficient field, since its chosen infinite
place is complex. For a quadratic number-field extension it therefore
restricts to the unique nonidentity automorphism. This implements the
real-place coefficient comparison in the opposite-cell reduction of
Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994),
Lemma 6.28, printed p. 370.
