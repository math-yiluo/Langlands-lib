# Graded big-cell injectivity: definitions and interface statement

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.TorusUnipotence.BigCellStatement`, namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme.TorusUnipotence`.

This module fixes the objects of the "big cell" step of the proof of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.RelativeRoot.TorusActionUnipotenceStatement`. It states the injectivity
result as a proposition. The proof is `gradedBigCellInjectivity` in `BigCell`.

Throughout, `k` is a field and `A` a commutative `k`-algebra (in the same universe as `k`),
`𝒜 : ℤ → Submodule k A` is a `ℤ`-grading of `A`, `ε : A →ₐ[k] k` is an augmentation and
`Δ : A →ₐ[k] A ⊗[k] A` is an algebra map. In the application, `A` is the coordinate algebra of
the group `H`, `𝒜` is the grading by the pairing of characters with a generic cocharacter, `ε` is
the counit and `Δ` is the comultiplication.

## Definitions

* `plusIdeal 𝒜 ε`: the ideal of `A` generated by the elements of negative degree together with
  the degree-zero elements killed by `ε`. Geometrically, `A ⧸ plusIdeal 𝒜 ε` is the coordinate
  ring of the closed subscheme of points that flow to the identity under the positive part of the
  grading (the "positive big-cell factor" `U⁺`).
* `minusIdeal 𝒜 ε`: the same with positive degrees in place of negative degrees (the factor
  `U⁻`).
* `bigCellMap 𝒜 ε Δ`: the algebra map
  `A → (A ⧸ minusIdeal 𝒜 ε) ⊗[k] (A ⧸ plusIdeal 𝒜 ε)` given by `Δ` followed by the tensor
  product of the two quotient maps. Geometrically it is the comorphism of the multiplication
  map `U⁻ × U⁺ → H`.

## Statement

`GradedBigCellInjectivityStatement` says: for every field `k` and every commutative `k`-algebra
`A` that is a Noetherian domain and formally smooth over `k`, with a `ℤ`-grading `𝒜`
(`GradedAlgebra 𝒜`), an augmentation `ε` and an algebra map `Δ`, assume

1. `ε a = 0` for every homogeneous `a` of nonzero degree;
2. the left counit law `lid ∘ (ε ⊗ id) ∘ Δ = id`;
3. the right counit law `rid ∘ (id ⊗ ε) ∘ Δ = id`;
4. every degree-zero element of the augmentation ideal `ker ε` lies in `(ker ε) ^ 2`.

Then `bigCellMap 𝒜 ε Δ` is injective.

No coassociativity and no compatibility of `Δ` with the grading are assumed.

## Provenance

This is an internal interface statement for the proof of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.RelativeRoot.TorusActionUnipotenceStatement`, and no source states it in
this form. It is an algebraic version of the fact that the multiplication map
`U⁻ × U⁺ → H` is étale at the identity when the zero weight does not occur on the cotangent
space, in the spirit of the open cell of the dynamic method (Conrad–Gabber–Prasad,
*Pseudo-reductive groups*, 2nd ed., §2.1). Condition 4 is the algebraic form of the absence of the
zero weight.
