# Proof of rank-one generation of the root-generated centralizer part

Authors: Worker 4896.

`rootGeneratedCentralizerRankOne` proves the published proposition
`RootGeneratedCentralizerRankOneStatement.{u}` exactly, at arbitrary universe
`u`. Its type is that proposition, with no added hypothesis.

Fix any field `k`, any connected reductive `G` over `k` and any actual maximal
split torus `i : S.toGrp ⟶ G`. Write `U_a(k)` for the rational points of
`RelativeRoot.subgroup i a`. The theorem then gives

```
centralizerPoints i ⊓ Subgroup.closure (⋃ a ∈ relativeRoots i, U_a(k))
  ≤ Subgroup.closure (⋃ a ∈ relativeRoots i,
      (centralizerPoints i ⊓ Subgroup.closure (U_a(k) ∪ U_{-a}(k)))).
```

So every rational point of `Z_G(S)` in the subgroup generated by the rational
root groups is a product of elements of the groups
`Z_G(S)(k) ∩ ⟨U_a(k), U_{-a}(k)⟩`. As in the statement, the field is
arbitrary, `G` need not be semisimple, split or quasi-split, and the relative
root system may be non-reduced or empty.

## Implemented proof

The proof is the composition of two published theorems:

1. `rootRankOneFactorization` (module
   `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.RootRankOneFactorizationProof`) proves
   `RootRankOneFactorizationStatement.{u}` with no premise. That statement
   says that every nonidentity rational point of a relative root group `U_a`
   has a unique pair of opposite-root factors whose product with it normalizes
   the torus, and that this product represents the relative reflection for
   `a`.
2. `rootGeneratedCentralizerRankOneReduction` (module
   `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.RootGeneratedCentralizerRankOneReduction`)
   proves the implication
   `RootRankOneFactorizationStatement.{u} →
   RootGeneratedCentralizerRankOneStatement.{u}`. It derives the relative
   root-system statement from the rank-one factorization. It then applies the
   published word covering, normalizer-kernel and lifted Coxeter-relation
   argument, which extracts the centralizer bound.

Applying the second theorem to the first gives the target. Neither theorem
assumes `RationalBruhatStatement`, `RelativeTitsSystemStatement`,
`RelativeWeylCoxeterStatement`, `HasOppositeFactorPairs` or any other
unproved proposition. The opposite factor pairs used inside the reduction come
from the proved rank-one factorization. The Lean check reports only the
standard axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

This module adds no mathematics of its own. Its route is this composition of
Library results, and the paired Markdown of the two theorems describes their
internal arguments. That route may differ from the Borel–Tits argument
sketched in the statement's Markdown.
