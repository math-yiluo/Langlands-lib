# A real obtuse basis of the actual relative root span

This internal supporting construction for Renard V.3.18 extends the published
rational independence and spanning of actual simple relative roots to the real
character space. Flat scalar extension and the rational-to-real tensor
cancellation equivalence give independence; scalar extension of the published
rational span equality gives spanning. The resulting basis has exactly the
actual simple-root tensors as its vectors, including when the root system is
empty.

The supplied integral reflection data give a crystallographic root pairing.
The actual simple roots belong to its positive indecomposable base, so
Mathlib's base-pairing inequality gives their nonpositive off-diagonal integral
Cartan pairings. The proved coroot/form identity and positivity of the squared
root lengths transfer this inequality to every supplied symmetric positive
definite Weyl-invariant real form. No classification, chosen averaged form, or
additional assumption on the group is used.
