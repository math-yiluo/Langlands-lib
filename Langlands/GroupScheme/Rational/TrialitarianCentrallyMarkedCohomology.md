# Cohomology of the centralizer for a centrally marked trialitarian index

The actual anisotropic kernel of the split-torus centralizer has the three
leaves of the full type D4 diagram as its simple vertices. The full star
action is transitive on the leaves. Compatibility with the permutation of the
actual geometric factors makes the kernel the Weil restriction of the
selected simply connected A1 factor from its stabilizer field. Reduced-norm
locality gives vanishing for that group over a totally imaginary field and
hence for its Weil restriction.

The cocenter of the split-torus centralizer is induced. Cohomology exactness
with the kernel and Hilbert 90 gives vanishing in the centralizer. The
maximal torus supplied by the Tits index lies in the centralizer, so the
prescribed-torus reduction trivializes the original quasi-split cocycle.
This is the split-rank-one step of Platonov and Rapinchuk,
*Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, p. 371.
