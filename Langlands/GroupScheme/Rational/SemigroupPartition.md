# Exact cross-exclusion and the weight spans of ordered-product factors

This internal development extracts the combinatorics used by
Conrad–Gabber–Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive Groups*, second edition (2015),
Theorem 3.3.11 and its proof, printed pp.114–116 (PDF pp.140–142), registered
as `conrad-gabber-prasad-2015-pseudo-reductive`. It uses the theorem's exact
condition: an original block avoids the positive additive semigroup generated
by each other block. It does not require the generated semigroups to be
disjoint.

The generic `WeightPartition` lemmas apply to any additive operation and any
index type. `closure_inter_support` says that the semigroup generated by a
block meets the total occurring support in precisely that original block.
Its proof locates an occurring character in some block and uses
cross-exclusion to force that block to be the selected one.
`disjoint_of_cross_exclusion` derives disjointness of the original blocks.
`restricted_cover` and `restricted_cross_exclusion` preserve the hypotheses
after intersecting all blocks with a subset of the support; the closure step
uses monotonicity. No nonempty or finite index hypothesis is needed.

If zero occurs, `exists_unique_zero_block` gives its unique block and excludes
zero from the positive generated semigroups of all other blocks. This is the
zero-block observation in CGP's reduction from a solvable group to its
unipotent radical. Zero is never added to a closure by convention.

`actionWeightSpan b Ξ` is the supremum of the Library's actual intrinsic
weight spaces indexed by the characters in the set Ξ. It is an auxiliary
construction on the existing all-coefficient-extension action, with no
change to `actionWeightSpace`, `actionWeightSupport`, or `semigroupWeightSpan`.
`actionWeightSpace_eq_bot_of_not_mem_support` follows directly from the
definition of support at the full subgroup functor: a nonzero vector of any
nonzero weight space witnesses that support. Thus
`actionWeightSpan_inter_support` discards only zero subspaces.
`semigroupWeightSpan_closure_eq` combines this observation with
`closure_inter_support` to identify the canonical semigroup span of a factor
with the span of its original block.

These constructions and proofs are derived infrastructure. They prove neither
smooth connected representability of semigroup subgroup functors nor the
ordered multiplication scheme isomorphism. They make no eigenspace direct-sum,
finiteness, quotient-weight, or base-change assertion. All actual group-scheme
constructions retain the arbitrary field and universe of the Library.
