# Finite étaleness of the roots-of-unity coordinate algebra

Author: Worker 427.

`Langlands.GroupScheme.MuCoordinateAlgebraStatement.{u}` is the proposition

```lean
∀ (A : CommRingCat.{u}) (n : ℕ),
  0 < n → IsUnit (n : A) →
    Module.Finite A (AdjoinRoot ((Polynomial.X : Polynomial A) ^ n - 1)) ∧
      Algebra.Etale A (AdjoinRoot ((Polynomial.X : Polynomial A) ^ n - 1))
```

The universe `u` is arbitrary. Here `AdjoinRoot f` is Mathlib's actual quotient
of `Polynomial A` by the ideal generated by `f`, with its canonical `A`-algebra
structure. `Module.Finite` means finite as an `A`-module, and `Algebra.Etale`
means étale as an `A`-algebra. Both refer to precisely the same quotient
`A[T]/(T^n - 1)`, written inline in Lean. No algebra supplied by a caller and no
structure field containing either conclusion is used.

The hypotheses are exactly positivity of `n` and invertibility of its natural
number image in `A`. There is no nontriviality, noetherian, field,
characteristic, or chosen-root hypothesis. In particular, the quantifier also
includes the zero ring. The target does not assert the stronger freeness or
rank conclusions used in its source derivation.

## Original source and derived formulation

The Stacks Project, [Example 39.5.2, Tag 040M](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/040M),
identifies the roots-of-unity group scheme with
`Spec(ℤ[T]/(T^n - 1))` and describes its base changes and functor of points.
For positive `n`, the class `t` of `T` is already a unit, since `t^n = 1`.
This explains the coordinate quotient in the target.

The algebra assertion is derived from the **proof** of
[Lemma 59.28.1, Tag 03PL](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03PL),
rather than its sheaf-exactness conclusion. On an affine open that proof uses
`B = A[T]/(T^n - a)` for a unit `a` and positive invertible `n`, shows that `B`
is finite free over `A`, and observes that the derivative `n t^(n-1)` is a unit,
so the map is standard étale and hence étale. Specializing the unit parameter
`a` to `1` gives the present conjunction, retaining only module finiteness
from the finite-free conclusion. No further hypothesis is introduced.

The retained Tag 03PL comments are part of the reading: Comment 7888 explains
why invertibility is required, and Comment 8155 states that the natural numbers
in that section are positive. Thus Lean's `0 < n` explicitly records the source
convention. This is a mathematical derivation of a statement, not an implemented
Lean proof of the calculation.

## Boundary and downstream use

This module defines only a `Prop` target. It proves neither conjunct and installs
no finite or étale instances. It imports only unchanged Mathlib modules and does
not change any existing group-scheme construction or statement.

The quotient is the algebraic input for an affine comparison with the published
`Langlands.GroupScheme.mu`. An exact-target proof of this algebra statement,
the affine comparison, and the local-to-global assembly needed for
`Langlands.GroupScheme.MuFiniteEtaleStatement` remain separate obligations.
The existing Edixhoven–van der Geer–Moonen Chapter III Examples 2–3 citation
is construction provenance for the imported multiplicative group-scheme work;
it is not the source of the finite-étale assertion stated here.
