# The unitary dynamic centralizer and the represented Levi

`Langlands.GroupScheme.QuasiSplitUnitary.leviRepresentedStatement` proves the unchanged Library proposition `LeviRepresentedStatement`. For fields `F` and `E`, an `F`-algebra structure and `Algebra.IsQuadraticExtension F E`, a finite `F`-basis `b` of `Matrix (Fin 2) (Fin 2) E`, and a nonidentity `F`-automorphism `c` of `E`, the dynamic centralizer of the actual cocharacter `diag(t,t⁻¹)` is represented by
`leviHom b c : WeilGm.groupScheme (CommRingCat.of F) E ⟶ groupScheme b c`,
and this homomorphism is a closed subgroup.

The proof combines two results about the existing full unitary group scheme.

1. The theorem `centralizer_cocharacter` in `QuasiSplitUnitaryDynamicProof` identifies the dynamic centralizer with the diagonal subgroup functor. The range and monomorphism calculation in `QuasiSplitUnitaryLeviRange` shows that `leviHom b c` represents this diagonal subgroup. Rewriting by that equality gives `centralizer_isRepresentedBy_leviHom`. Its conclusion includes both a monomorphism of schemes over `Spec F` and equality of the range subgroup functor with the centralizer on every test scheme.
2. The theorem `isClosedSubgroup_leviHom` in `QuasiSplitUnitaryLeviClosed` supplies closedness. That helper constructs the ideal in the similitude coordinate ring generated by the multiplier minus one and all coefficients of the off-diagonal matrix entries. It identifies the Weil restriction with the resulting closed subgroup through natural point bijections, factors the composite of `leviHom` with the unitary inclusion through this isomorphism, and deduces that the underlying morphism of `leviHom` is a closed immersion.

The final theorem introduces the parameters of `LeviRepresentedStatement` and pairs these two conclusions. Both ingredients hold for every `F`-automorphism `c`, so the target's nonidentity assumption needs no further argument. The coefficient algebras and test schemes remain arbitrary, including non-reduced test schemes, and the proof excludes no characteristic.
