# Matrix realization of the actual quadratic tensor functor

This module brings the concrete root parameter group and its matrix realization together for the actual separable quadratic extension. The source is Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023), §2.7, printed pp.108–110 (PDF pp.139–141), especially the upper matrix preceding (2.7.2). Registered original: `kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`, SHA-256 `05ad9a284a2577b34d1a270228b74dfb28f520561ae7772d8f38dddb78938732`. The bar placements were visually checked on PDF p.141. The authors' November 2024 corrections, pp.24–26, contain no correction to this section.

`tensorEmbedding` specializes `Parameters.embedding` to `tensorInvolution k L R`, which is the actual extension of the unique nontrivial quadratic conjugation to `R tensor[k] L`. It is a homomorphism into Mathlib's general linear group with entries `[[1,u,v],[0,1,bar(u)],[0,0,1]]`, where bar is `id_R tensor conjugation`. The entry formula is definitional. Injectivity, determinant one, upper unipotence, preservation of the split Hermitian form, and the exact image characterization are direct specializations of the proved generic matrix results.

`tensorEmbedding_map` applies the generic involution-compatible naturality theorem to the actual coefficient map on the first tensor factor. Thus coefficient change of the group coordinates agrees with entrywise coefficient change of their invertible matrices. `rationalEmbedding_compatible` applies the same theorem to the canonical tensor left-unit equivalence, showing that the field coordinate matrix agrees with the rational tensor coordinate matrix after this identification. These direct specializations are derived implementation interfaces rather than extra literature assertions.

Importing this module supplies the coordinate group and its central trace-zero subgroup, the actual tensor group-valued functor, the matrix construction, and the trace-one rational quotient. All three field and test-algebra universes are independent, as are source and target test-algebra universes in naturality. Every result includes characteristic two. The implementation constructs no group-scheme representation or identification with root subgroups of arbitrary reductive groups.
