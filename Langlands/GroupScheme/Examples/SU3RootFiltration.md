# Actual SU3 coordinate valuation filtration subgroups

This module constructs `Parameters.filtration omega hinv r` and `Parameters.centralFiltration omega hinv r` as subgroups of the published `Parameters c`, for every real `r`. The field `L` has arbitrary universe and characteristic. The input is an explicit `AddValuation L (WithTop ℝ)` with the explicit invariance law `hinv : ∀ z, omega (c.bar z)=omega z`. The central subgroup is the published literal kernel `central c`, characterized by zero first coordinate.

The source is Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023), §3.2(b) and Fact 3.2.1, printed pp.148–149 / PDF pp.179–180, with the unshifted convention of Remark 3.2.10, printed p.155 / PDF p.186. Construction 6.1.17 and Proposition 6.1.19, printed pp.206–207 / PDF pp.237–238, explain the use of these subgroup and doubling computations for V 1 and V 4. The root filtration carrier is `(2*r : WithTop ℝ) ≤ omega x.second`. The companion depth module proves that this threshold implies `(r : WithTop ℝ) ≤ omega x.first`, using the trace–norm equation and invariant valuation. `crossTerm_ge` combines first-coordinate bounds at `r` and `s` with multiplicativity and invariance to give a bound of `r+s` for `u*bar(u')`. For two elements at depth `r`, the second coordinate of their product is `v+v'+u*bar(u')`; applying the valuation sum inequality twice proves closure. The inverse has second coordinate `bar(v)`, so invariance proves inverse closure. Identity membership is the valuation of zero. No closure certificate or desired subgroup law is assumed.

The central filtration carrier is `x.first=0` and `(r : WithTop ℝ) ≤ omega x.second`. Products have zero first coordinate and the cross term vanishes, so the valuation sum inequality proves closure. The inverse coordinate formulas give inverse closure. This is a subgroup of the ambient coordinate group, rather than a separately substituted abstract group.

`mem_filtration_iff_depth` proves the root depth-threshold description by the direct `halfTop` comparison. `mem_centralFiltration_iff_depth` states the central description for an element of the actual `central c` through its literal inclusion; the companion existential description also works for an ambient element. `filtration_antitone` and `centralFiltration_antitone` compare their real thresholds. A finite real lower bound for each extended depth proves both exhaustion assertions. An extended value above every real number is infinity; the depth/coordinate identity criterion then gives both elementwise intersection-equals-identity equivalences, including an ambient version for the central filtration.

`centralFiltration_double_eq_inf` is the exact subgroup equality `C_(2*r) = central c ⊓ U_r`, proved by extensionality and the displayed carriers. For the convention `x*y*x⁻¹*y⁻¹`, the published coordinate commutator theorem gives zero first coordinate and second coordinate `u*bar(u')-u'*bar(u)`. The first-coordinate bounds at `r` and `s` control both products at `r+s`, and the valuation difference inequality proves membership in `C_(r+s)`. This commutator estimate is a derived coordinate consequence.

For a field `M` in an independent universe, `comap_filtration` and `comap_centralFiltration` prove exact pullback equalities under the published coordinate homomorphism. They require both explicit involution compatibility and `omega' (f z)=omega z`, as well as the invariant target valuation needed to construct its subgroups. The proof evaluates the second coordinate; the central assertion additionally uses injectivity of a field homomorphism to reflect vanishing of the first coordinate. The companion depth module constructs the induced map of literal central subgroups and proves preservation of both depths. These compatibility laws are derived assertions, not a claim that arbitrary field maps preserve valuations.

Neither module requires nontriviality, discreteness, topology or characteristic different from two. They make no assertion of V 0, prescribed jump sets, a full valued root datum, arbitrary relative-root identifications, or building descent. An actual separable quadratic extension still needs its actual conjugation and invariant extension valuation as inputs. The other valued-root axioms and geometric applications remain separate obligations.
