# Parabolic radical generation for actual weighted Hermitian models

Let `k` and `L` be fields in an arbitrary common universe, with `L/k` separable
quadratic. Let `H` be a diagonal Hermitian form on a finite decidable
`Middle : Type`, with supplied units realizing its weights. Assume
`H.IsAnisotropic`, meaning that zero middle self-pairing forces the vector to
be zero. All characteristics are allowed.

Suppose `A` is an actual group scheme over `k`, with an actual group-scheme
isomorphism from `A` to the constructed weighted Hermitian scheme. For every
actual morphism `a : A ⟶ G`, a normal subgroup of `G(k)` containing the
identity-limit subgroup for the model's cocharacter composed with the inverse
isomorphism and `a` contains the entire image of `A(k)`.
The proof applies the established model containment to `e.inv ≫ a`, maps a
source point through `e.hom`, and cancels the actual isomorphism.

If `G` is connected reductive, the existing dynamic-to-parabolic theorem
therefore gives containment in `parabolicElementarySubgroup G`, the subgroup
generated by actual rational images of geometric unipotent radicals of
parabolic subgroup schemes. `rationalPointsToParabolicElementarySubgroup`
is the original rational-point homomorphism with its codomain restricted to
that subgroup; its coercion is exactly the original map.

If `A` itself is connected reductive, apply this containment to its identity
morphism to prove `parabolicElementarySubgroup A = ⊤`. Consequently any subgroup
of `A(k)` containing every such actual radical image is the whole rational
group. That final subgroup need not be normal: the universal containment
property of the subgroup generated by the radical images suffices.

This is an internal transport consequence of `HermitianRootAmbientGeneration`
and the ordinary group-scheme isomorphism laws, extending the corresponding
`SU3ModelRadicalGeneration` construction to the weighted model. The source
isomorphism and connected reductivity are explicit assumptions here; no claim
is made that arbitrary rank-one groups admit this model or that its geometric
properties have been proved. Those inputs, the other rank-one cases and the
unrestricted local Kneser–Tits theorem remain separate obligations.
