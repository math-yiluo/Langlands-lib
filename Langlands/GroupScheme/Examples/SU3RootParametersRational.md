# Rational quadratic root parameters and the additive quotient

This module implements the rational-point assertions of Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023), §2.7, Fact 2.7.1, printed p.110 (PDF p.141). The registered original is `kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`, SHA-256 `05ad9a284a2577b34d1a270228b74dfb28f520561ae7772d8f38dddb78938732`. The November 2024 corrections, pp.24–26, contain no correction to §2.7. The proofs here use the actual trace and the coordinate definitions rather than supplied surjectivity or quotient data.

`traceOne` chooses an element of the actual extension field with algebra trace one. Mathlib's surjectivity theorem applies because the extension is finite dimensional and separable. The quadratic trace formula from `SU3RootParametersQuadratic` proves that its sum with its conjugate is one. This construction uses no division by two and therefore includes characteristic two, with independent base-field and extension universes.

`rationalParameters` is the concrete parameter group on L with the extension's actual conjugation. `rationalLift u` has coordinates `(u, traceOne * (u * bar(u)))`. Expanding conjugation, using its involutivity, and factoring the trace-one identity proves the coordinate equation for every u. The first-coordinate formula proves `rational_firstHom_surjective`. The section is a function of sets; it is not asserted to preserve the group law.

`rationalCentralNormal` exposes the normality of this kernel. `rationalQuotientEquiv` applies Mathlib's first isomorphism theorem with this explicit right inverse. The central subgroup is definitionally the first-coordinate homomorphism's kernel, so the resulting group equivalence is `U(L)/U⁰(L) ≃* Multiplicative L`. The type synonym `Multiplicative L` means the additive group of L, written multiplicatively to match the quotient's group convention. `rationalQuotientEquiv_mk` proves that the quotient class of `(u,v)` maps to u.

`rationalTensor_bar` proves that the canonical algebra equivalence `k tensor[k] L ≃ₐ[k] L` commutes with conjugation, by induction on tensors. `rationalParametersEquiv` applies it to both coordinates, identifying the group-valued tensor functor at k with these field coordinate points. Its coordinate formulas and preservation and reflection of central-subgroup membership are proved explicitly.

These results prove surjectivity at rational points; they make no additional surjectivity assertion over arbitrary test algebras. The displayed trace-one formula supplies a representative even at u=0, but no bijectivity of its dependence on the trace-one element at u=0 is asserted. No identification with the root subgroups of an arbitrary reductive group or group-scheme representability is claimed.
