# Reindexing nonsingular Hermitian matrix forms

This is internal elementary matrix infrastructure for the general Hermitian Gram interface. It extends the relabeling operations in Mathlib to the actual Gram forms and determinant-one isometry subgroups defined in `HermitianMatrixBasic`. It uses no classification or Witt decomposition theorem.

For any commutative coefficient ring `A`, specified involution `c`, and equivalence `r : ι ≃ κ` of finite index types, `reindexGL r` is the group equivalence on invertible matrices induced by `Matrix.reindexRingEquiv A r`. Its matrix entries at `(i,j)` are the original entries at `(r.symm i,r.symm j)`. Its inverse uses `r.symm`. Relabeling preserves the actual unit-valued determinant, commutes with entrywise coefficient maps, and satisfies identity and composition laws. The index types and coefficient rings have independent arbitrary universes; no characteristic or nonempty-index assumption is imposed.

`Form.reindex H r` retains this reindexing of the actual invertible Gram matrix of `H`. The adjoint commutes with the relabeling, so Hermitian symmetry is preserved. The form operations satisfy identity, composition and inverse laws. `Form.reindex_map` states their compatibility with scalar extension along any ring map that intertwines the given coefficient involutions.

`preserves_reindex_iff` proves that reindexing the form and a matrix together preserves the full equation `adjoint c M * H.gram * M = H.gram` in both directions. Together with determinant invariance, it constructs `specialUnitaryReindexEquiv H r` between the actual determinant-one isometry subgroups. Its matrix formula is `Matrix.reindex r r`, and its inverse matrix formula is `Matrix.reindex r.symm r.symm`. The proof transports the preservation equation through the matrix ring equivalence and the determinant through simultaneous row and column relabeling.

`reindex_gram_map` relabels the actual Gram-compatibility hypothesis for a coefficient map. `specialUnitaryReindexEquiv_map` proves that the corresponding square of group maps commutes: applying coefficient change after the reindexing equivalence agrees with applying reindexing after coefficient change. This is a point-functor compatibility result over rings. Scheme-test restriction maps can use it when they are realized by these coefficient maps; this module does not construct a representing scheme or assert the existence of a presentation for any group.
