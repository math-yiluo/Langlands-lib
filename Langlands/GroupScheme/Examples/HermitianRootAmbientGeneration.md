# Full weighted Hermitian images in the parabolic elementary subgroup

Let `k` and `L` be fields in an arbitrary common universe, with a separable
quadratic extension `L/k`. Let `Middle : Type` be finite with decidable equality,
let `H` be a diagonal Hermitian middle form for the canonical quadratic
involution, and supply units whose values are its weights. The scheme in this
file is the actual weighted Hermitian scheme constructed in
`HermitianRootGroupScheme`, with its original ambient matrix inclusion.
Every characteristic is allowed.

`Parameters.mapEquiv` identifies the original parameter groups under a ring
equivalence compatible with both involutions and every middle weight. This
coefficient construction permits independent universes for the coefficient
rings and the middle index. Its inverse applies the inverse ring equivalence;
both inverse laws hold in each vector coordinate and in the scalar coordinate.
`specialUnitaryRingEquiv_upper` proves that the same coefficient equivalence
takes the actual upper matrix to the upper matrix of the transported parameter.

`rationalCoeffEquiv_rationalUpper` compares the original rational upper point
with the upper test point at the base affine test. It follows by applying the
injective all-test matrix equivalence and the compatible coefficient equivalence.
Surjectivity of `Parameters.mapEquiv` and restriction along the actual
base-test isomorphism give `rationalUpper_mem_upperFunctor`. The constructed
polynomial contraction then proves
`rationalUpper_range_le_dynamicUnipotent`. These results require no anisotropy.

Assume additionally that `H.IsAnisotropic`: zero middle self-pairing forces the
middle vector to be zero. For any actual group-scheme morphism from this model
to a target `G`, a normal subgroup of `G(k)` containing the identity-limit
subgroup for the composed cocharacter contains the entire rational image.
Indeed, its inverse image is normal, contains the rational upper family by
functoriality of contraction, and is therefore the whole source rational group
by the established normal-generation theorem.

When `G` is connected reductive, the existing theorem
`dynamicUnipotent_le_parabolicElementarySubgroup` places the composed
identity-limit subgroup in the actual subgroup generated by rational images of
geometric unipotent radicals of parabolics. That subgroup is normal, so the
preceding argument gives
`rationalPoints_range_le_parabolicElementarySubgroup`. Only the target is
assumed connected reductive.

This is an internal extension of `SU3AmbientGeneration` to the actual weighted
Hermitian model. Its inputs are the constructed cocharacter, the explicit
upper contraction, normal generation under middle anisotropy, and the existing
dynamic-to-parabolic-radical containment theorem. It adds no classification or
generation premise to the final containment result. Identifying arbitrary
rank-one groups with this model, proving its geometric properties, handling
other rank-one cases, and the unrestricted local Kneser–Tits theorem remain
separate obligations.
