# Actual rational points of the special unitary scheme

This internal construction works over fields `k` and `L` in an arbitrary common universe, with a separable quadratic extension `L/k`. There is no restriction on characteristic. The scheme is the actual equalizer `SU3Root.SchemeModel.groupScheme`, and its rational-point carrier is the Library's `rationalPoints`.

For involutive commutative rings `A` and `B` in a common universe, `twistFixedRingEquiv` applies an involution-compatible ring equivalence entry by entry to the actual fixed subgroups in `SL₃`. Its proof uses coefficient naturality of the inverse-adjoint endomorphism and injectivity of the coefficient equivalence. Composing with the existing matrix-preserving `fixedSpecialUnitaryEquiv` gives `specialUnitaryRingEquiv` on the determinant-one Hermitian subgroups of `GL₃`. The theorem `specialUnitaryRingEquiv_val` records its entrywise action on underlying matrices.

The test scheme `baseAffineTest` is the actual relative spectrum of the base algebra `k`. The coefficient equivalence `rationalCoefficientEquiv` first evaluates the actual relative global sections of that test scheme using `Corepresented.affineSectionsAlgEquiv`, tensors this equivalence with the identity on `L`, and then uses the tensor-unit equivalence `L tensor_k k ≃ L`. The theorem `rationalCoefficientEquiv_bar` verifies compatibility with the original quadratic conjugation. On a pure tensor this is compatibility of the `k`-algebra conjugation with scalar multiplication; additivity extends it to all tensors.

The multiplicative equivalence `rationalMatrixEquiv` composes the existing canonical rational-to-base-affine-point equivalence, the all-test `pointsEquiv` of the scheme equalizer, and the preceding involution-compatible coefficient equivalence. Its target is the actual subgroup of `GL₃(L)` defined by determinant one and `bar(g)ᵀ J g = J`, for `J = [[0,0,1],[0,-1,0],[1,0,0]]` and the nonidentity quadratic conjugation. The theorem `rationalMatrixEquiv_val` specifies the matrix: take the actual ambient tensor matrix of the included rational point and apply `rationalCoefficientEquiv` entry by entry.

The homomorphisms `rationalUpper` and `rationalLower` send the original root-parameter group into this exact rational-point carrier by the inverse matrix equivalence. Their images have exactly the original upper and opposite matrices, as checked by `rationalMatrixEquiv_upper` and `rationalMatrixEquiv_lower`. The theorem `eq_top_of_rational_roots` proves that any subgroup containing both images is the whole rational-point group: map the subgroup injectively into `GL₃(L)`, apply the already proved quadratic special-unitary matrix generation, and pull membership back through the injective map. In particular, `rational_root_ranges_eq_top` proves that the supremum of the two ranges is top.

The provenance is internal. The proof combines actual scheme-point and section equivalences with the previously proved concrete matrix-generation result; it assumes no new generation theorem. The root maps in this module are rational-point homomorphisms. Their realization by actual group-scheme root maps, containment in actual dynamics or parabolic-radical images, smoothness and reductivity of the constructed scheme, classification of general rank-one groups, and the unrestricted local Kneser–Tits theorem remain separate obligations.
