# Discrete depths on actual quadratic local-field root coordinates

Worker 1676 derives this module internally from the unshifted coordinate depths in `SU3RootValuation` and the actual normalized quadratic extension valuation in `SU3RootParametersLocalValuation`. The base field `F` is a nonarchimedean local field, and `L/F` is an actual separable quadratic field extension. Their universes are independent. No characteristic restriction or supplied topology or valuation on `L` is imposed.

`quadraticDepth` specializes `Parameters.depthA` to the actual `quadraticValuation`; it is half the value of the second coordinate, with infinity preserved. `quadraticCentralDepth` specializes `Parameters.depthDouble` on the literal central subgroup, so it is the value of the second coordinate itself. The inherited generic formulas and identity criteria apply directly.

For a nonidentity parameter, the second coordinate is nonzero by the published field-valued coordinate equation. Its actual extension value is `n/2` for an integer `n`. Halving gives `quadraticDepth_exists_quarter_integer`, with value `n/4`. For a nonidentity element of the central subgroup, equality of ambient elements would imply equality of the subtypes, so its second coordinate is likewise nonzero. The same field-value result gives `quadraticCentralDepth_exists_half_integer`. Both conclusions assert lattice containment only; neither identifies the exact value group or ramification, nor asserts occurrence of every lattice point.

`exists_central_parameter_of_depth_ge` constructs a nonidentity central parameter whose root depth and central depth are both at least any prescribed real number `r`. Apply the actual central-coordinate existence theorem at the second-coordinate bound `max (2*r) r`. The direct halving comparison gives the root-depth bound, and the other inequality gives the central-depth bound. The nonzero second coordinate implies nonidentity. Both depths are finite by the inherited identity criteria, including in characteristic two.

`quadraticDepth_finite_Icc` and `quadraticCentralDepth_finite_Icc` prove finiteness of the sets of real depth values that occur in any bounded closed interval. A private arithmetic lemma observes that lattice values `n/d` in `[a,b]`, for positive `d`, are images of integers in the finite interval from `ceil(a*d)` to `floor(b*d)`. A finite real depth cannot be the depth of the identity. Applying the preceding lattice-containment results with denominators four and two embeds each set of attained values into the corresponding finite arithmetic set. This is finiteness of depth values, not of the sets of parameters attaining them.

These are derived coordinate and arithmetic consequences, with no additional literature claim. They do not identify the coordinates with root subgroups of an arbitrary reductive group, construct or descend a building, establish a full normalizer-generation theorem, or prove the supplied-building facet-fixing statement. The separate generic filtration construction supplies subgroup and commutator laws; none is assumed here.
