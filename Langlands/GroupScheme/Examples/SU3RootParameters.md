# Quadratic root parameters over an involutive ring

This generic construction implements Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023), §2.7, (2.7.2) and the central-subgroup part of Fact 2.7.1, printed pp.109–110 (PDF pp.140–141). The registered original is `kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`, SHA-256 `05ad9a284a2577b34d1a270228b74dfb28f520561ae7772d8f38dddb78938732`. The authors' November 2024 corrections, pp.24–26, do not correct this section.

`RingInvolution` supplies only an involutive ring automorphism of a commutative ring. `Parameters c` is the actual subtype of pairs `(u,v)` with `v + bar(v) = u*bar(u)`. Its group operations are `(0,0)`, `(u+u', v+v'+u*bar(u'))`, and `(-u,bar(v))`. Closure and the group laws are proved by expanding ring operations and the coordinate equation. No characteristic assumption is used, and ring universes are independent.

`Parameters.map` applies any involution-compatible ring homomorphism to both coordinates. Its identity and composition laws are equality of group homomorphisms. `firstHom` maps to `Multiplicative A`, explicitly the multiplicative type synonym of the additive group of the ring. Its kernel `central c` is precisely the subgroup `u=0`. Direct coordinate multiplication proves this subgroup lies in the group center; this does not assert that it equals the full center over arbitrary rings.

`traceZero c` is an additive subgroup of the actual ring. `centralEquivTraceZero` is a multiplicative equivalence to `Multiplicative (traceZero c)` sending `(0,v)` to `v`. For the commutator convention `x*y*x⁻¹*y⁻¹`, `commutator_coordinates` gives `(0,u*bar(u')-u'*bar(u))` by coordinate expansion. These intrinsic proofs and the generic ring formulation are an internal reusable generalization of the displayed source formulas. The later tensor specialization is responsible for supplying the actual quadratic-extension conjugation. This file makes no group-scheme representability or arbitrary-relative-root-group identification claim.
