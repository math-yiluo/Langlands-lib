# Nontrivial filtration subgroups for actual quadratic local-field coordinates

Worker 1676 derives this module internally from `SU3RootFiltration` and `SU3RootParametersLocalDepth`. The base field `F` is a nonarchimedean local field, and `L/F` is an actual separable quadratic field extension. Their universes are independent. No characteristic restriction, topology on `L`, or supplied extension valuation is required.

`quadraticFiltration F L r` specializes the published root filtration to `quadraticValuation F L` and its proved invariance under the actual quadratic conjugation. It is a subgroup of the concrete `rationalParameters F L`, with carrier given by the inequality `2*r ≤ quadraticValuation F L p.second` in `WithTop ℝ`. `quadraticCentralFiltration F L r` specializes the central filtration as a subgroup of that same ambient coordinate group. Its carrier requires zero first coordinate and the inequality `r ≤ quadraticValuation F L p.second`. Closure and the generic antitonicity, exhaustion, separation, doubling and commutator laws are inherited from the existing subgroup constructions; they are not new assumptions or separate proofs here.

`mem_quadraticFiltration_iff_depth` expresses membership using the actual `quadraticDepth`. `mem_quadraticCentralFiltration_iff_depth` does so for an element of the literal central subgroup, through its inclusion in the ambient group and using `quadraticCentralDepth`. These are direct specializations of the proved generic depth comparisons.

`exists_ne_one_mem_quadraticFiltrations` proves that any independently prescribed real root level `r` and central level `s` contain a common nonidentity parameter. The actual depth module constructs a nonidentity central element whose root depth and central depth are both at least `max r s`. The two membership comparisons and the inequalities `r ≤ max r s` and `s ≤ max r s` put its ambient value in both subgroups. Equality of this value with the identity would imply equality of the central subtype element with the identity, contradicting its construction.

`quadraticFiltration_inf_central_ne_bot` uses that witness to prove the intersection of the two prescribed levels is not the trivial subgroup. `quadraticFiltration_ne_bot` and `quadraticCentralFiltration_ne_bot` give the individual nontriviality conclusions at every real level. The witness comes from actual nonzero trace-zero coordinates and base scalars of arbitrarily high integer order in the imported depth construction, including in characteristic two; the identity's infinite depth is not used as a nontriviality witness.

These are derived consequences for actual quadratic coordinate groups, with no additional literature claim. They do not compute exact jumps or ramification, identify these coordinates with the relative root subgroups of an arbitrary reductive group, prove all valued-root axioms, construct or descend a building, establish full normalizer generation, or prove the supplied-building facet-fixing statement.
