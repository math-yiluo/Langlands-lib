# Splitting the actual weighted Hermitian group scheme

This internal construction applies the existing quadratic tensor splitting and the weighted exchange-ring special-unitary equivalence to the actual group scheme of a nondegenerate diagonal Hermitian form. The field universe is arbitrary. The extension `L/k` is separable quadratic, the middle index is any finite type in `Type`, and every middle weight has a supplied unit witness. There is no characteristic or anisotropy hypothesis.

For every test scheme over `Spec L`, `coefficientSplit` identifies the actual tensor coefficient ring `L tensor[k] Γ(T, O_T)` with the product of two copies of its section ring. The existing `QuasiSplitUnitary.tensorSplit` sends `e tensor r` to `(r e, r bar(e))`. Its conjugation formula proves that the original scalar involution becomes factor exchange. The defining conjugation invariance of each original middle weight proves that both split weights are its same included section. The unit witnesses are included through the same algebra map.

`pointsSplitting` composes compatible coefficient change on the actual determinant-one isometry subgroup with `HermitianRoot.Exchange.specialUnitaryEquiv`. Thus it gives all special-linear matrices on `Index Middle`, with no additional matrix restriction. Its forward formula takes the first split coefficient entrywise. Its inverse formula applies the inverse coefficient splitting to the pair consisting of the original special-linear matrix and its weighted inverse transpose.

`basePointsEquiv` first uses the actual group-scheme pullback comparison and the original equalizer's all-test point equivalence. It then applies this matrix splitting. Its naturality is proved on entries: the original point comparison commutes with test restriction, and the first tensor splitting coordinate commutes with the induced section map, which respects the structural `L` action.

`splittingIso` uses these natural multiplicative equivalences and `isoOfPointModels` to construct an isomorphism from the actual scalar extension of `SchemeModel.groupScheme` to `SpecialLinear.groupScheme (Index Middle)` over `Spec L`. Both directions' actions on test points are proved. This is a derived construction from existing Library results, not a new assertion cited from literature. Geometric reductivity, general local rank-one classification, and unrestricted local Kneser–Tits remain separate obligations.
