# Unshifted SU3 coordinate depths

This module constructs depths on the published concrete coordinate group `SU3Root.Parameters c` and its literal subgroup `Parameters.central c`. The field `L` has arbitrary universe and characteristic. The input `omega : AddValuation L (WithTop ℝ)` is explicit; the inverse and coordinate-bound proofs require `hinv : ∀ z, omega (c.bar z) = omega z`. No extension valuation is constructed here.

The source convention is Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023), §3.2(b), printed pp.148–149 / PDF pp.179–180, and specifically the unshifted formulas of Remark 3.2.10, printed p.155 / PDF p.186, reused in Construction 6.1.17, printed p.206 / PDF p.237. The registered original `kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach` has SHA-256 `05ad9a284a2577b34d1a270228b74dfb28f520561ae7772d8f38dddb78938732`. The authors' 1 November 2024 corrections, registered as `kaletha-prasad-2024-corrections`, SHA-256 `23293f51e58d5ceecb91f834743f68058b98431797d2531142415d013fdf61ed`, were checked at PDF pp.26 and 32. They supersede original Remark 3.2.3; this module uses no general recovery of a filtration from a supremum.

`halfTop` and `doubleTop` use `WithTop.map` to preserve infinity and send finite real values to `t/2` and `2*t`. Case analysis proves that these maps are inverse and proves the finite threshold comparison for halving and for the sum `t+t`. The same case analysis proves existence of a finite lower bound and that a value above every real number is infinity. These elementary helpers are internal infrastructure.

`Parameters.depthA omega x` is `halfTop (omega x.second)`. `Parameters.depthDouble omega z`, on `z : central c`, is `omega z.val.second`. The second coordinate is zero exactly at the group identity: the coordinate equation with zero second coordinate gives `u*bar(u)=0`, and the field and involution laws give `u=0`. The valuation's `top_iff` then proves that both depths are infinite exactly at the identity. The published inverse coordinate formula and `hinv` prove inverse invariance for both depths. The inverse relation between halving and doubling gives `depthDouble omega z = doubleTop (depthA omega z.val)`.

`second_le_first_add_first` applies the valuation's minimum inequality to `v+bar(v)=u*bar(u)`. Invariance identifies the two entries of the minimum, and multiplicativity gives `omega v ≤ omega u + omega u`. The direct finite threshold comparison deduces `first_ge_of_second_ge`. No division of infinity through ordinary real arithmetic is used.

For a field `M` in an independent universe, `centralMap` restricts the published coordinate homomorphism associated with `f : L →+* M` and the explicit conjugation-compatibility law. Vanishing of the first coordinate follows from `f 0=0`, and the homomorphism laws are inherited from the coordinate map. Under the additional law `omega' (f z)=omega z`, `depthA_map` and `depthDouble_map` prove exact depth preservation by evaluating the second coordinate. These map compatibilities and elementary generalizations are derived coordinate consequences, rather than extra assertions quoted from the source.

This construction alone does not prove nontriviality, prescribed jump sets, arbitrary relative-root comparisons, other valued-root axioms, or building descent. Applying it to an actual quadratic extension still requires that extension's conjugation and an invariant valuation. The actual filtration subgroups and their intrinsic laws are constructed in the companion filtration module.
