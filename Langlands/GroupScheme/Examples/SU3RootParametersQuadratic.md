# Quadratic extension and tensor root parameters

Worker 17 specializes the concrete coordinate group of `SU3RootParameters` to the actual tensor algebra in Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023), §2.7, equation (2.7.2), printed pp.108–110 (PDF pp.139–141). The base field, quadratic extension and test algebras have independent universes. A quadratic separable extension is Galois; its automorphism group has order two. `conjugation` chooses the unique nonidentity algebra automorphism, `conjugation_unique` proves that every nonidentity automorphism is this one, and `conjugation_involutive` follows from its order. `fieldInvolution` packages this actual extension automorphism for the generic parameter group. `algebraMap_trace` identifies the usual algebra trace with `x + conjugation x` by summing the two Galois automorphisms. No characteristic restriction or division by two occurs.

For each commutative k-algebra R, `tensorConjugation` is exactly `id_R tensor conjugation` on `R tensor[k] L`, with its R-algebra structure. Tensor induction proves involutivity. `tensorInvolution` and `tensorParameters` therefore apply the already constructed coordinate group to the actual quadratic tensor algebra. They require no supplied group law or extension-specific relation beyond the actual field extension assumptions.

`tensorCoefficientMap` is the tensor map of a k-algebra homomorphism on the first factor and the identity on L. It commutes with conjugation by tensor induction. `tensorParametersMap` applies it to both coordinates. The coordinate formulas and identity/composition laws are proved, and `tensorParametersFunctor` bundles them as a covariant functor from commutative k-algebras to groups. The implementation uses Mathlib's tensor-product, Galois, algebra-category and group-category APIs. These are intrinsic constructions of the displayed source functor, not a claim of representability or identification with root subgroups of an arbitrary reductive group.
