# Scaling the quadratic root coordinates and their depths

Worker 1676 derives this module internally from the displayed coordinate equation and group operations of `SU3RootParameters` and the depth and filtration definitions of `SU3RootValuation` and `SU3RootFiltration`. It makes no additional literature claim.

Over any commutative ring `A` with the published involution `c`, `Parameters.scale a` sends `(u,v)` to `(a*u, (a*bar(a))*v)`. The trace of its second coordinate is the scalar `a*bar(a)` times the original trace. The coordinate equation therefore remains true. The multiplication formula is preserved because each cross term is multiplied by the same scalar `a*bar(a)`. These calculations prove that this is an actual group homomorphism, including for nonunits. The coordinate formulas are recorded separately.

`scale_one_apply` and `scale_mul_apply` prove the identity and composition laws by the coordinates. For a unit `a`, `scaleEquiv a` is a group automorphism: scaling by its reciprocal supplies both inverse identities. `centralScale a` restricts the same homomorphism to the literal subgroup with first coordinate zero. `scale_map` proves that scaling commutes with a coefficient homomorphism which commutes with the involutions.

For a field `L` of arbitrary universe and characteristic, the remaining results take an actual additive valuation `omega : AddValuation L (WithTop ℝ)` and the explicit invariance law under `c.bar`. Multiplicativity and invariance give the second-coordinate value after scaling as `omega(a)+omega(a)+omega(v)`. A private extended-value calculation proves that halving this is `omega(a)` plus the original half-value, including infinite values. Thus `depthA_scale` proves an exact root-depth shift by `omega(a)` for every scalar `a`, and `depthDouble_centralScale` proves a central-depth shift by twice that value.

For a unit whose value is the finite real number `t`, `comap_filtration_scale` proves that the pullback of the root level `r+t` is exactly the root level `r`. It applies the depth membership comparison and cancels the finite shift, treating infinity directly. `comap_centralFiltration_scale` proves the corresponding ambient central-subgroup equality with shift `2*t`. Multiplication by the unit reflects vanishing of the first coordinate, and the same second-coordinate value calculation cancels the finite shift. The value equality is an explicit hypothesis, not a claim that arbitrary units have value zero.

These are intrinsic coordinate automorphisms and valuation calculations. They do not identify a scaling automorphism with conjugation by a centralizer element of an arbitrary reductive group, or identify this coordinate group with an arbitrary relative root subgroup. They do not prove the full centralizer or Weyl compatibility axioms, a primitive translation comparison, building descent, normalizer generation, or the supplied-building facet-fixing statement.
