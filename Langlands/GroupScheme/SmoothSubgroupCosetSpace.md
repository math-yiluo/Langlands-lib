# Separated coset algebraic spaces for closed smooth subgroups

Fix an arbitrary universe `u`, a base scheme `S`, and actual group schemes `H` and `G` over `S`. Let `i : H → G` be a group homomorphism whose underlying morphism in the slice is a monomorphism, assume `G` is locally of finite type over `S`, and assume `H` is smooth over `S`. Neither smoothness nor affineness is assumed for `G`.

`HomogeneousQuotientSheaf.space i hH` packages the existing canonical smooth-relation quotient as an actual algebraic space. Its underlying presheaf is exactly the published `HomogeneousQuotientSheaf.presheaf i`, with the actual quotient transformation already provided there. The published smooth-relation theorem supplies the fppf sheaf property, representable diagonal and a surjective étale scheme atlas locally of finite type.

If `i` is also a closed subgroup, `isClosedImmersion_diagonal` proves that this actual quotient has closed diagonal. `separatedSpace i hH hi` retains the actual algebraic space together with this property. It does not replace the quotient by a different sheaf or by a quotient only on geometric points.

The proof first constructs the doubled presentation of the product of the quotient presheaf. Stability of represented smoothness and surjectivity under products shows that this is a smooth surjective presentation. The actual scheme kernel pair is the pullback of the quotient diagonal along that presentation; this is proved by the kernel-pair universal property and the scheme functor of points' product comparison. The corresponding map of schemes is the actual coset-relation inclusion into `G ×_S G`. The published `HomogeneousCosetRelation.inclusion_isClosedImmersion` proves this inclusion is closed. Comparing represented pullbacks transports this property to the diagonal's chosen scheme pullback.

The supporting `relativeProperty_of_smooth_atlas` then descends the property from that presentation. Its hypothesis explicitly requires stability under base change and descent along smooth surjective scheme morphisms. For closed immersions, `descendsAlong_smoothSurjective` obtains the required descent from the existing étale-cover descent and actual étale local sections of smooth surjections. It pastes the given pullback with a pullback along such a local section, then descends along the resulting étale surjective cover.

This is an internal derivation from `HomogeneousQuotientSheaf`, `HomogeneousCosetRelation`, the published smooth-relation quotient theorem, scheme-property descent and categorical pullback comparison. The route does not assume a represented scheme quotient. In particular, it does not yet prove that the quotient by a reduced subgroup is affine or finite; that is the remaining step toward smoothness of anti-affine algebraic groups.
