# Locality of fixed-rank smoothness along the unit

For a locally finitely presented group over a scheme, formal smoothness of
the actual stalk map at each unit point gives a smooth open neighborhood
of the unit. Pulling back that neighborhood proves preservation under
arbitrary base change. The stalk condition descends along a base open
cover because the source and base projections are open immersions.

The fixed-rank package assumes the group is separated and locally of
finite presentation over the base. Its actual first-jet projection is
flat of rank `1 + r`, with `r` the relative dimension at the unit. The
first-jet pullback square preserves flatness and computes the rank under
base change. The same square and target locality of flatness recover both
conditions from a base open cover. The functorial actual identity jets
give invariance under an isomorphism of actual groups.

These are the locality conditions for applying the smooth-section locus
of SGA 3, Exposé XV, Lemma 5.4 to the unit of such a group.
