# Prescribed dominant bases as actual coweight-face data

This derived unit constructs actual `RootDatumCoweightFace.Face` data with a
prescribed dominant base for a finite reduced coordinate root datum. It thereby
extends face-height independence from two supplied sign-refining face data to
any two prescribed bases dominant for the same coweight.

`support_eq_baseOf_of_signs` first characterizes the simple-root support of a
base by its indecomposable positive roots for any functional having the same
positive-root signs. A simple root has height one, so cannot be a sum of two
positive roots. Conversely a positive nonsimple root admits subtraction of a
simple root to a positive root, contradicting indecomposability.
`root'_nonneg_of_isPos` proves nonnegativity of a dominant coweight on all
positive roots by induction on positive-root addition.

`nonempty_face_of_dominant_base` chooses a regular coordinate cocharacter
positive exactly on the prescribed base's positive roots, then transports it
through the perfect root-datum pairing. Dominance shows that this regular
coweight refines the original coweight's nonzero signs: the original positive
roots are positive for the base, and the original negative roots are negative.
The support characterization and existing coroot-span equality supply all
fields of actual face data whose base is exactly the prescribed base.
`dominantBaseFace` chooses these data together with their base equality.

`faceHeight_eq_of_dominant_bases` uses the existing zero-root-reflection
alignment of the two constructed face data to prove exact equality of their
integral height functionals on the entire root span. This theorem retains the
explicit finiteness assumption on the reflection subgroup generated by the
original coweight's zero roots. The actual face construction itself needs no
such finiteness assumption.

Provenance is the existing positive-root/simple-root subtraction theory,
positive regular coordinate cocharacters, perfect root-datum pairing,
coroot-span equality for actual faces, and the exact face-base comparison.
Comparisons of actual group filtration terms under changing bases and tori,
and global descent, are subsequent work.
