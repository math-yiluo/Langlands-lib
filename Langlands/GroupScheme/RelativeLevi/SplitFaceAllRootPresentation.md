# All-root presentation of actual canonical face terms

For a supplied split parabolic face chart over an arbitrary commutative ring, `allRootWeight` extends its unscaled face weights to the entire root family by taking the nonnegative integer part of the face height. `allRootIndex` enumerates that full root family. `allRootSubgroup d` is the actual subgroup functor generated by all ambient root ranges of weight at least `d + 1`.

The face height of a negative root is nonpositive: negate the root, use nonnegativity of face height on positive roots, and apply linearity of the height form. Consequently any root with positive `allRootWeight` belongs to the chart's positive system. On the original positive-root indexing, `allRootWeight_index` identifies the full-root weight with the exact original natural secondary weight.

`range_inclusion_comp_eq_allRootSubgroup` proves that this full-root generated subgroup is exactly the actual original represented term's ambient range on every scheme test. The original ambient-range theorem expresses the term as a generated level of chart roots. In each direction, an active generator is matched through its actual root label, with the same subgroup range and the same weight. Zero and negative face weights contribute nothing at the positive cutoff. The proof does not assume a product order on the entire root family or an all-root product chart.

No field, nontrivial-ring, reducedness, or geometric existence premise is added. This presentation allows comparisons to match the whole root-index family rather than two possibly different positive-root lists. It does not itself provide a torus conjugator, root-range matching, or global descent.

Provenance: internal derivation from the original supported-coordinate weights, linearity of integral face height, and the exact ambient generated-root-range presentation.

The module imports the published `SplitParabolicFaceChart` and `RootWeightAmbientComparison` providers directly. It uses no cross-root-datum comparison theorem.
