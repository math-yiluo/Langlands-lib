# Actual root-weight terms under affine base change

Fix a commutative ring `R` and an arbitrary commutative `R`-algebra `A`, both in universe `u`. The generic results require only actual root maps from `Ga R` into a group scheme. They identify each base-changed root factor under the actual pullback point equivalence, and prove that every generated root-weight subgroup commutes with base change. The tests are arbitrary schemes over `Spec A`; neither their affineness nor flatness of `A` is assumed. Weights and cutoffs are unchanged.

For a supplied `SplitParabolicFaceChart C`, `C.affineBaseChangeSubgroup A d` is generated by the actual base-changed full root family with the original unscaled canonical weights at cutoff `d + 1`. The actual pullback of the original ambient term inclusion has exactly this range on every test. This construction does not choose or assume a base-changed auxiliary regular chart.

Suppose, additionally, that `R` is nontrivial, the original group is reductive, its coordinate torus is fibrewise maximal, and `P` is its supplied full fibrewise root datum. Let `D` be any supplied face chart over `A`, for the base-changed root family and the same coordinate cocharacter and root datum. Its positive base and auxiliary regular chart may be chosen independently. `faceHeight_eq_of_affineBaseChange` proves that its entire face-height functional equals that of `C`, using finite zero-root reflection geometry over the original ring and independence of the dominant positive base. It follows that the pullback of each original ambient term range equals the range of `D` on all tests. No nontriviality assumption is made on `A`.

`C.affineBaseChangeTermIso A D hG hmax hP d` is the resulting isomorphism of the actual group schemes. Its homomorphism composed with `D`'s original ambient inclusion equals the actual base change of `C`'s original ambient inclusion. The isomorphism is constructed by uniqueness of represented subgroups, with the monomorphisms and their base changes proved. This module compares supplied charts; it does not assert that the fresh chart exists or descend a global filtration.

This is derived internal infrastructure for finite vector-bundle filtrations of relative unipotent radicals. The implemented proof uses published root parametrization base change, pointwise subgroup ranges, images of generated weight levels, the all-root presentation of actual face terms, and the previously proved dominant-base independence. It adds no independent literature claim.

The face-height and Weyl-finiteness helpers are imported through
`RelativeLevi/SplitFaceBaseComparison`, which supplies the canonical
dominant-base and simple-zero-set comparison lemmas. The comparison keeps
the original weights and hypotheses.
