# Actual successive inclusions of the descended face terms

This is an internal continuation of effective descent of the original face
terms. It constructs their actual consecutive inclusions over the original
base.

The prescribed local maps show that each local original term range is
contained in the preceding one. The actual descended membership conditions
inherit this containment on every scheme test. Representability of the global
terms then constructs their successive homomorphisms. Their composites with
the original ambient inclusions are exactly the next original ambient maps.
Closedness of the ambient inclusions makes the successive homomorphisms closed
subgroup inclusions.

The actual chart recovery isomorphisms identify those global maps with the
prescribed original consecutive chart maps. This follows by cancellation of
the actual represented ambient inclusion and the already proved ambient
recovery equations. Each global term is affine over the original base, and
has an affine underlying scheme whenever the original base is affine.

No successive term map or compatibility datum is assumed. Quotient and vector
bundle descent and finite termination remain separate obligations.
