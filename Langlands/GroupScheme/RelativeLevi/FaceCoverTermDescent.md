# Effective descent of the actual canonical face terms

This internal construction produces the canonical terms as actual smooth
closed group schemes over the original base.

The constructed finite chart family is an actual etale cover, indexed in the
same universe as the base schemes. At each fixed original cutoff, the local
functors are the ranges of the prescribed original chart inclusions. The
proved arbitrary-base overlap comparison gives equality on pairwise overlaps.
Actual ambient square comparisons identify those equalities with the input to
all-test cover matching.

The descended subgroup condition consists of points satisfying the original
local conditions under every relative restriction. Matching recovers each
original local term exactly. Effective descent of closed subgroups constructs
an actual global closed representative of that condition. Its actual pullback
isomorphism to each original chart group preserves the original ambient map.
Smoothness of the original local terms therefore descends along the etale
cover.

All term matching and closed representability are proved from the supplied
relative radical and the constructed charts. No effectivity, global term,
or original-term matching premise is added. Quotients, vector bundles and
finite termination remain separate steps.
