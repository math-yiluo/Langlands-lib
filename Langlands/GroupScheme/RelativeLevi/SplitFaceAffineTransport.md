# Original face terms under coefficient and ambient transport

This is an internal consequence of the full-root presentation of the canonical
face terms. It supplies the compatibility needed to compare local terms after
putting their ambient groups in the same actual pullback of the original group.

First, two face charts with the same full root datum and the same integral
presenting weight have the same unscaled face functional, even when their
coefficient rings and ambient groups differ. Reducedness and the finite
zero-reflection subgroup come from the reductive source chart. The dominant
base comparison and equality of the simple zero roots prove the assertion.

Second, extend coefficients and compose the actual ambient group with a given
isomorphism. A fresh chart on the transported roots represents exactly the
pullback of the original term followed by that ambient isomorphism. The proof
uses the all-root presentation, the actual base-change root ranges, functoriality
of generated weighted subgroups, and the preceding equality of face
functionals. The source ring is nontrivial; the target algebra is arbitrary,
including the zero ring. The equality holds on every scheme test, at every
unchanged unscaled cutoff. No equality of successive terms or descent datum is
assumed.

The local affine instances have explicit module-specific names, allowing this
module to be imported together with the arbitrary maximal-torus comparisons.
