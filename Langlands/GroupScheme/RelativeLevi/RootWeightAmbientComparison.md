# Ambient root ranges and comparison of weighted filtration terms

This derived unit compares actual root-weight filtration terms by their
ambient root-subgroup ranges on every scheme test. The base ring is arbitrary,
and the tests need not be affine.

The group-theoretic helpers show that homomorphism images commute with the
subgroups generated at each weight cutoff and that a weight-preserving
relabelling of the factor subgroups leaves every level unchanged.
`SubgroupFunctor.range_comp_carrier` identifies the range of a composite with
the image of its first range on each test.

`rootFactor_eq_range` uses the all-test bijection between global functions and
points of the actual additive line to identify root factors with the ranges
of their actual root maps. `map_rootFactor` then identifies images under an
ambient homomorphism with the corresponding composite root-map ranges.
`range_weightInclusion_comp` proves that the represented weight term has
exactly the ambient subgroup generated by those actual root ranges at cutoff
`d+1`.

`RootWeightData.range_inclusion_comp_eq` compares two possibly different
regular charts. Its inputs are a bijection of their root indices, equality
of the corresponding natural weights, and equality of corresponding actual
ambient root-map ranges on every test. These are generator-level inputs,
not an assumed equality of filtration terms. It proves equality of the full
ambient term ranges at every level. Under monomorphic ambient inclusions,
`ambientTermIso` constructs the unique representative comparison and its
companion equation preserves the original ambient maps.

Provenance is the existing generated height-subgroup definition, its actual
represented weight inclusions, scalar-point bijectivity for the additive line,
subgroup images and represented-subgroup uniqueness. No comparison between
different maximal tori or global effective descent is claimed here.
