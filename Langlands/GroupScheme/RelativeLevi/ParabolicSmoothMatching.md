# Actual parabolic descent along smooth surjective parameter maps

This internal construction combines the published big étale sheaf of
actual parabolic subgroup functors with the covering-sieve lemma in the
paired supporting module `SmoothSingletonCover`. The ambient group is
reductive over an arbitrary scheme in universe `u`.

For any smooth surjective morphism `a : M ⟶ T` of parameter schemes,
`parabolic_isSheafFor_smooth` establishes the singleton sheaf condition.
It applies big étale sheafness to the covering sieve generated by `a`.
No affine or quasi-compact restriction is required.

`existsUnique_restrict_smooth` translates this sheaf condition into actual
parabolic descent for the published `IsMatchingAlong` condition: every
two maps into `M` with the same composite into `T` give equal restrictions
of the prescribed family. The result is an actual parabolic subgroup
functor on `T`, uniquely characterized by exact recovery on `M`.
`matchingSmooth` selects this family and `restrict_matchingSmooth` proves
its recovery equation. `restrict_injective_smooth` uses the same uniqueness
to detect equality of any two global families from their restrictions.

These results provide descent of actual families over supplied parameter
schemes. Construction and smooth proper representability of the parameter
scheme remain separate obligations of the host task.
