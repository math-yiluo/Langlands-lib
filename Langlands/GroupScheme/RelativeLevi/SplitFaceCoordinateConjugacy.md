# Equality of face terms under actual parabolic coordinate-torus conjugacy

For any group scheme, normalization of an actual subgroup functor by another subgroup identifies its preimage under conjugation by an allowed base point with itself. The same normalization leaves the actual range of any representing map unchanged by that conjugation. These statements are proved on every scheme test, using restriction of the allowed base point and ordinary subgroup normalization; the range proof uses the induced conjugation homomorphism on point groups.

The generic `unipotent_eq_of_same_suppliedRadical` lemma compares arbitrary cocharacters that present one specified relative parabolic and one supplied relative radical. The actual representative-identification theorem makes both contracting functors equal to the same ambient radical range. No common torus premise is imposed.

Now consider two supplied face charts of one affine reductive group over a nontrivial arbitrary commutative ring. Their coordinate tori, full root data, root-index types and all chart choices may differ. Assume their actual original contracting subgroup functors are equal. Supply an actual base point of the original parabolic, a fixed integral coordinate-torus equivalence, and the exact square asserting that conjugation by this point carries the first torus map to the second through the exponent torus isomorphism.

`range_inclusion_eq_of_coordinateConjugacy` proves equality of every original ambient face-term range on every scheme test. The actual coordinate-torus square determines a full root-data equivalence and exact root-range matches. Full original parabolic normalization of the positive term proves compatibility of the contracting functor with inner conjugation. The general root-range transport theorem then compares all terms after that conjugation. Finally, full original parabolic normalization of each term removes the conjugation from its range, giving equality of the original ranges themselves.

`coordinateConjugateTermIso` constructs the actual term isomorphism from this range equality. Its ambient inclusion equation retains the two original inclusions; no conjugator occurs in the resulting equation. The weights remain the original unscaled canonical face heights.

The coordinate matrix, actual parabolic conjugator and exact square are explicit inputs. This unit does not assert their local existence or perform base change, faithfully flat reflection, or global filtration descent. The nontrivial-ring premise is explicit from actual root-data/sign comparison.
