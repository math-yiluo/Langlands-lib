This internal construction identifies both endpoints of the actual descended
filtration, including on disconnected and non-noetherian bases.

the chart cover reflects to the original base. Representability uniqueness
therefore gives an actual isomorphism from the first global term to the
supplied radical, preserving its original composite inclusion into the ambient
group.

reflects along the etale cover. Consequently the descended terminal term
