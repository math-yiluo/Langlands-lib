# Actual radical, consecutive-term and vector-quotient comparisons

Let `C` and `D` be canonical face charts in one affine reductive group scheme over a commutative ring `R` in universe `u`. Their maximal coordinate split tori, full root data, root parametrizations and auxiliary chart choices may differ.

For a supplied radical `r : U → H` and a dynamic parabolic presentation of the prescribed map `p : H → G`, `C.suppliedRadicalIso` identifies the actual initial face term with the supplied group scheme `U`. Its homomorphism followed by `r ≫ p` equals the original initial-term ambient inclusion. This construction uses the given relative radical and the dynamic relative Levi identification; it does not replace `U` by another representative. This initial comparison does not require the coefficient ring to be nontrivial.

For the remaining comparisons assume `R` nontrivial, both coordinate tori fibrewise maximal, both full data fibrewise root data of the actual group, and all root maps genuine parametrizations. Suppose also that the two original dynamic parabolic functors agree and their original contracting functors agree. The canonical term isomorphisms from etale comparison intertwine the actual successive inclusion maps. The proof composes both sides with the target term's monomorphic ambient inclusion and uses the original ambient equations and the existing successive-inclusion equations.

`sameParabolicQuotientIso` is the resulting actual isomorphism between the full fppf vector quotients at each term. The source and target retain separate exact-support index types. Over the nontrivial base their exact-support cardinalities agree, as follows by passing the vector-group isomorphism to a residue field; both may be zero. It is induced by the actual consecutive-term comparisons, using full fppf quotient uniqueness and their actual kernel ranges. `quotientMap_comp_sameParabolicQuotientIso` records its equation with the original quotient maps. No global lift of quotient points is assumed. This module proves the actual quotient isomorphism, not that it is linear for the vector-bundle structures; scalar equivariance and linearity remain separate obligations.

Finally, when both parabolic presentations represent the same prescribed `p` and share a supplied relative radical `r`, `sameSuppliedRadicalTermIso` requires no separate equality assumptions on the dynamic parabolic or contracting functors. Parabolic equality follows from the two representing certificates. Contracting equality follows from the actual supplied-radical identification. The resulting term isomorphism again preserves the original ambient inclusions.

This is derived internal infrastructure for finite vector-bundle series of supplied relative radicals. It combines established etale face-term comparison, actual supplied-radical uniqueness, and the actual consecutive-term fppf quotient comparison. It does not itself descend a filtration or prove the final radical-filtration statement. The quotient comparison reuses the published `LinearQuotientComparison` module, which imports the published `VectorWeightLinearity` module.
