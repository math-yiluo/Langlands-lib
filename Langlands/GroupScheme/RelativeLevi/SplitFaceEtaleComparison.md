# Canonical face terms across arbitrary maximal split tori

Fix a nontrivial commutative ring `R` in universe `u` and an affine reductive group scheme `G` over `Spec R`. Supply two fibrewise maximal coordinate split tori, two full fibrewise root data and actual root parametrizations, and face charts `C` and `D`. The torus coordinate types, root-index types, positive bases, auxiliary regular cocharacters and parametrizations may differ. Suppose that the two original coordinate cocharacters define equal actual dynamic parabolic functors and equal actual contracting functors.

`range_inclusion_eq_of_sameParabolic` proves that the original ambient inclusions of `C` and `D` have equal ranges at every term `d`. These are their original unscaled canonical face weights and cutoff `d + 1`. The equality is of subgroup functors, hence holds on every scheme test, including nonaffine tests. A full root-data identification, matching root ranges, matching heights, and a coordinate conjugator are all derived during the proof rather than additional inputs of this theorem.

`C.sameParabolicTermIso D ... d` constructs the actual isomorphism of represented terms, with its homomorphism composed with `D`'s original ambient inclusion equal to `C`'s original ambient inclusion. The final equation contains no conjugating automorphism. The module does not assert that either chart exists, that the two dynamic functors agree automatically, or that an effective global filtration has been descended. The supplied-radical comparison separately supplies the contracting-functor equality when both presentations represent that radical.

The proof first works over a coefficient algebra with an actual coordinate-torus conjugator inside the pulled-back original parabolic. It constructs fresh charts on the base-changed root families, transports full root data and genuine parametrizations, and applies the established parabolic coordinate-conjugacy comparison. Actual base-change comparison identifies these fresh terms with pullbacks of the original ones. The intermediate coefficient extension uses the given algebra structure.

For an arbitrary specified affine map, the proof equips its coefficient ring with the algebra structure induced by that map and transports the complete dependent assertion along `Spec.map_preimage`. This retains the original pullbacks, sections and squares. Next, the actual parabolic-valued etale conjugacy data through every point give an etale cover. Each chosen neighborhood ring is nontrivial because its spectrum has a point mapping to the specified original point. On each neighborhood the previous comparison applies. Etale-local equality of monomorphically represented subgroup functors then proves equality on the original base. Uniqueness of represented subgroups produces the term isomorphism.

This is derived internal infrastructure for the finite vector-bundle series of supplied relative radicals. It implements the original-parabolic normalization and etale torus-conjugacy route, combining established local geometric and root-weight comparison results. It adds no separate literature claim.
