# Actual rational binary norm-one points and elementary generation

This is an internal construction from the canonical reduced-norm-one group, its actual rational-point comparison, the actual binary elementary morphisms, and the published algebraic binary generation criterion. Throughout, `k D : Type u`, `k` is a field, and `D` is a finite-dimensional central simple `k`-algebra with a possibly noncommutative ring structure. The universe and characteristic are unrestricted. The final coefficient criterion assumes that `D` is a division ring; none of the preceding constructions needs that additional assumption.

## Elementary subgroup and coefficient criterion

`rationalMatrixHom` includes `rationalMatrixEquiv` into `GL₂ D`. It is injective and has range exactly the canonical binary norm kernel. These statements use injectivity and surjectivity of the actual equivalence, with subtype extensionality.

`rationalElementarySubgroup k D bD` is the supremum of the ranges of the actual rational upper and lower elementary homomorphisms. It is therefore the subgroup generated by these two images on the original rational-point carrier. `map_rationalElementarySubgroup` identifies its image under `rationalMatrixHom` with `BinaryElementary.elementary D`, the subgroup generated by the original upper and lower elementary matrices. For one inclusion, each rational elementary image has the displayed matrix form. For the reverse inclusion, closure induction reduces to the two families, and `exists_rational_elementaryPoint` supplies their actual rational preimages.

`rationalElementarySubgroup_eq_top_iff_binary` says that these two actual rational elementary images generate all rational norm-one points if and only if `BinaryElementary.elementary D` equals the canonical binary norm kernel. The forward implication maps the full subgroup and uses the range formula. The reverse implication uses that formula to find a preimage in the elementary subgroup, and injectivity of `rationalMatrixHom` identifies it with the original point.

When `D` is a division ring, `rationalElementarySubgroup_eq_top_iff` composes this equivalence with `Reduced.elementary_eq_ker_iff`. The resulting exact criterion is

```text
rationalElementarySubgroup k D bD = top
  iff (Reduced.coefficientNorm k D).ker = commutator Dˣ.
```

Here `Reduced.coefficientNorm` is the canonical reduced norm on the original coefficient units, and `commutator Dˣ` is their commutator subgroup. Thus the result transfers the published binary algebraic criterion to the actual scheme rational points, without assuming its arithmetic right-hand side.
