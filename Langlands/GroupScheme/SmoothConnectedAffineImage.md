# Actual smooth connected affine image

Over an algebraically closed field, `smoothConnectedAffineImage f` constructs the actual smooth connected affine image of a homomorphism `f : G ⟶ A` from a smooth connected group scheme into a smooth affine group scheme. The source may be nonaffine. Neither the source nor target needs a separate quasi-compactness or finite-type premise: source quasi-compactness follows from the published smooth connected group theorem, and smoothness supplies local finite type. In particular, the construction applies when `A` is a general linear group.

The returned `SmoothConnectedAffineImage f` record contains an actual group `group`, an actual homomorphism `inclusion : group ⟶ A`, an actual homomorphism `projection : G ⟶ group`, and `comp : projection ≫ inclusion = f`. Its remaining fields prove smoothness, connectedness and affineness of the image, closed immersion of the inclusion, and flatness, surjectivity and quasi-compactness of the projection. The field hypothesis `IsAlgClosed k` is essential to the imported dominance-to-flatness theorem used here and is retained explicitly in this construction.

This is an internal derived construction from the published pointed image generation, source-factor transport and dominant homomorphism flatness results. Worker 856 proposed these ingredients for the nonaffine-source step in affinization geometry. The Lean proof implements the following argument.

First, quasi-compactness and geometric connectedness of the smooth connected source imply geometric integrality by the published smooth geometric integrality theorem. Choose an actual affine open containing the source's identity point. It is dense in the integral source, so its open inclusion is dominant and, since the source is reduced, schematically dominant. Its structural map is geometrically integral: the open restriction is surjective onto the one-point field spectrum, so `geometricallyIntegral_open_of_surjective` applies. Lift the actual identity section through the open inclusion; its composite with `f` is the target identity.

Apply `ImageGeneration.pointedImageGeneration` to this pointed geometrically integral affine open and the smooth affine target. It gives a generated actual closed subgroup, smooth and geometrically connected, with a dominant ordered power of the open's map. `GeneratedImage.sourceFactor` extends its factorization to the whole source across the schematically dominant open inclusion.

The supporting `GeneratedImage.sourceFactor_isMonHom` proves that this full-source factor preserves the unit and multiplication: compose both required laws with the monomorphic closed inclusion and use the corresponding laws of `f` and the inclusion. `GeneratedImage.sourceHom` packages it as an actual group homomorphism and retains its factorization equation.

The supporting `sourceHom_isDominant` transfers dominance of the generated ordered power to the ordered power of the full-source factor. Since that factor is a homomorphism, its ordered power is the source's actual ordered multiplication followed by the factor itself. Dominance of this composite forces dominance of the factor. The image is reduced because it is smooth over a field, and is locally of finite type by smoothness. Thus `DominantHomFlat.flat_surjective` applies over the algebraically closed field, with its nonaffine-source interface, proving all three retained properties of the projection. Geometric connectedness gives connectedness over the field; the generated image already supplies its closed inclusion and affineness.

Every group, scheme and map remains in the arbitrary universe `u`. The chosen affine neighborhood and generated representative are not asserted to be canonical; the record retains actual data and its exact factorization equation. This construction does not claim that the projection is smooth or that its kernel is smooth.
