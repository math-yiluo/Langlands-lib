# A full Isom scheme from one local affine outer quotient

Worker 812. This internal construction combines the full Isom sheaf quotient,
the new-base local-model adapter, published separated locally quasi-finite
fppf descent, and published affine atlas effectivity. It cites no new
literature theorem.

One represented separated locally quasi-finite pullback along a surjective
flat locally finitely presented scheme map gives a scheme representation of
the original fppf sheaf. To apply the published covering-sieve descent theorem,
the proof uses the sieve generated by that covering map. For every member,
factor it through the cover and pull back the supplied scheme model. Pasting
pullback squares gives the required representation, while the three local
geometric properties are preserved under base change.

`ActionQuotient.representationOfSingleLocalPresentation` applies this result
to a supplied faithful action and its parameter-preserving local quotient
model. It constructs an actual global scheme representing the canonical
quotient sheaf.

`GroupIsomorphismScheme.ofSingleLocalOuterQuotient` takes an actual étale
surjective base map, a full Isom representative for the pulled-back groups
whose parameter is smooth over the new base, and a supplied outer quotient
for the pulled-back faithful source action. Its outer parameter is assumed
étale and separated over the new base, and its class map is assumed affine.
The local-model adapter supplies finite presentation of that class map.

First descend the outer quotient to a global scheme. Its local quotient
scheme is the base change of this global scheme along the original étale
surjective base map. It therefore supplies a represented étale surjective
atlas of the global quotient. The local Isom scheme maps affinely to that
atlas. The square with the original Isom sheaf is cartesian, as follows by
transporting the elementary product pullback square through the two local
representations and the global quotient representation.

The original full Isom sheaf is already an algebraic space by its supplied
étale-local representative. The published
`AtlasEffectivity.exists_iso_of_isAffineHom` applies to its map to the global
outer scheme and the affine local square. Its identity target pullback is
then represented by an actual scheme. The all-test representation adapter
constructs the universal `LocalGroupIso` and its classification bijections.

This is a full Isom scheme constructor under explicit local geometric inputs.
It does not prove existence of those inputs from reductivity or a generic
isomorphism. The host target still requires those geometric existence results.
