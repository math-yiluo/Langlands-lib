# Base change of bilinear coordinate groups

This derived construction identifies the actual base change of the bilinear
coordinate group over a commutative ring `R` along a ring map `R → A` with the
bilinear coordinate group having the transported coefficients over `A`.
It also proves that every compatible linear vector-coordinate and scalar-coordinate
homomorphism commutes with these isomorphisms.

The pullback adjunction identifies points of the base change on every scheme
test with points of the original group on the same test over `R`. The global
sections remain the same ring, and the coefficient map from `R` is the composite
through `A`. The coordinate groups therefore have identical bilinear laws.
These natural multiplicative equivalences recover an actual group-scheme
isomorphism by Yoneda. The same all-test coordinate formulas, with the composite
coefficient map, prove compatibility with the actual linear homomorphisms.

The provenance is the bilinear law (C.4.2) and the special-fibre calculations
in the proof of Kaletha–Prasad (2023), Appendix C.4, Lemma C.4.4, printed
pp.700–702 / PDF pp.731–733. The source is registered as
`kaletha-prasad-2023-bruhat-tits-new-approach`, SHA-256
`05ad9a284a2577b34d1a270228b74dfb28f520561ae7772d8f38dddb78938732`.
This general change-of-base calculation does not identify a unitary integral
model, its transition map, or an actual unramified root group.
