# Connectedness of unipotent groups in characteristic zero

The entry declaration `Langlands.GroupScheme.charZeroUnipotentConnected` gives
connectedness of the underlying space of every affine group scheme locally of
finite type over a characteristic-zero field that is unipotent in the Library's
representation-theoretic sense. Affineness makes the structure morphism
quasi-compact, so these hypotheses express an affine algebraic group of finite
type. The field and group scheme belong to an arbitrary common universe `u`.

This is the conclusion of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.CharZeroUnipotentConnectedStatement`. The implementation
spells out its quantifiers so that this proof helper can be checked independently
of the pending publication of that statement. It is a proof-producing `def`,
following the Library's convention for intermediate derived results. It is
intended as the characteristic-zero connectedness input to the stabilizer
smoothness proof; the other input, smoothness from connected smooth fibres over
a reduced base, is separate.

The argument is derived from the existing Library and Mathlib results, rather
than from the component-group order proof of Milne, *Algebraic Groups*,
Corollary 14.15. It proceeds as follows.

For an algebraically closed field, choose the affine Hopf coordinates of the
actual group scheme. The coordinate algebra is finitely generated by
`AffineCoordinates.algebraFiniteType`. Cartier's theorem in the form
`HopfAlgebra.isReduced_of_finiteType_of_charZero` makes these coordinates
reduced. The coordinate group is isomorphic to the given group by
`AffineCoordinates.groupSchemeIso`, so it is unipotent. The coordinate result
`Unipotent.connectedSpace_primeSpectrum_of_isUnipotent` now makes the coordinate
spectrum connected. That result proves that rational translations are locally
unipotent, hence fix each of the finitely many idempotents, and uses reduced
rational-point density to make the idempotents constant. The scheme isomorphism
then carries connectedness to the original group.

For a general characteristic-zero field `k`, base change to
`AlgebraicClosure k`. Affineness and local finite type are preserved by base
change, as is unipotence by `IsUnipotent.baseChange`. The algebraically closed
case gives a connected base-changed group. The spectrum map of the field
extension is surjective by `fieldExtension_fpqc`, so its base-change projection
to the original group is surjective as well. A continuous surjective image of
a connected space is connected, proving the conclusion over `k`.

The proof does not assume prior connectedness, perfectness, smoothness or split
unipotence. Cartier reducedness supplies exactly the reducedness needed by the
coordinate argument. The proof gives connectedness, without asserting a
component-group order or a split structure.
