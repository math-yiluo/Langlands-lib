# First jets on actual finite products of general linear schemes

This internal construction compares real first derivatives with actual
dual-number evaluation. On the general linear scheme, quotient
surjectivity and multivariate polynomial induction show that jets of all
entries and inverse entries determine jets of every global regular
function. Constants have zero epsilon coefficient; addition and the
product rule match the dual-number algebra operations.

For an arbitrary finite family of affine group schemes, the actual
product point is the ordered product of the inserted factor points.
Postcomposition and multiplication preserve coordinate jets, so finite
list induction assembles the factor jets. The empty list is the constant
identity curve with identity dual-number point.

The final theorem applies these results to the original finite-product
matrix point equivalence. The base ring and factor index have arbitrary
common universe, while matrix indices are small finite types. The
coefficient ring is any normed commutative real algebra with the specified
base-ring algebra structure. No completeness, nonempty index, positivity
of ranks, or continuity of the base-ring map is assumed.
