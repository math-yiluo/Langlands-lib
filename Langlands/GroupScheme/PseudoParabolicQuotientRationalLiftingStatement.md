# Rational lifting for pseudo-parabolic quotients

Author: Worker 1373.

`Langlands.GroupScheme.PseudoParabolicQuotientRationalLiftingStatement` is a
Prop-valued definition stating the rational-point conclusion of
Conrad–Gabber–Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive groups*, second edition (2015),
Lemma C.2.1 and its proof, printed pp.592–593 (PDF pp.618–619). The registered
source is `conrad-gabber-prasad-2015-pseudo-reductive`, SHA-256
`1b77af44c90211db9a3efa50896a3722f4fd2df332b7ce2ec52202eb243e7f54`.
Both pages of the original passage were read. No project errata note for this
source was present when this statement was prepared.

The statement quantifies every field `k : Type u`, with arbitrary universe
`u`. Write `B = Spec (CommRingCat.of k)`. Its data are an actual group scheme
`G` over `B` with `Smooth G.X.hom`, `IsAffineHom G.X.hom` and
`ConnectedSpace G.X.left`, an actual group scheme `P`, and a homomorphism
`i : P ⟶ G` satisfying the published `IsPseudoParabolicSubgroup i`.
The public pseudo-parabolic module and its ground-field radical and
fppf-product imports are used unchanged.

For every relative scheme `Q : Over B` and morphism `q : G.X ⟶ Q`, the
premises are exactly the quotient conditions supplied by the public
`SmoothSubgroupQuotientExistenceStatement`:

- `IsSeparated Q.hom`, `LocallyOfFiniteType Q.hom` and `QuasiCompact Q.hom`;
- `Smooth q.left`, `Surjective q.left` and `QuasiCompact q.left`;
- for every relative scheme `T : Over B` and actual points
  `g h : T ⟶ G.X`,
  `g ≫ q = h ≫ q ↔ g⁻¹ * h ∈ (SubgroupFunctor.range i).carrier T`.

The last condition identifies right-coset fibers on all relative tests,
including nonaffine and nonreduced schemes. These premises specify a supplied
homogeneous quotient model. This statement neither invokes a proof of
quotient existence nor constructs a new quotient.

Its conclusion is

```lean
∀ x : Over.mk (𝟙 B) ⟶ Q,
  ∃ g : Over.mk (𝟙 B) ⟶ G.X, g ≫ q = x
```

Thus every actual `k`-rational point of the quotient has a `k`-rational lift.
Scheme surjectivity of `q.left` is a geometric premise of the quotient
interface; rational surjectivity is the conclusion. The assertion covers
finite fields as well as infinite fields, and imperfect fields as well as
perfect fields. It assumes neither reductivity nor pseudo-reductivity,
properness of `Q`, density of `G(k)`, nor a pre-existing lift or local
section. Smoothness and connectedness of `P` are not added as premises:
their structural justification from pseudo-parabolicity belongs to the
eventual proof.

CGP Lemma C.2.1 has two conclusions. The rational-point conclusion is stated
here separately from
`PseudoParabolicQuotientLocalSectionsStatement`, which requires `Infinite k`
and asserts sections near every topological point of `Q`. In the original
proof, the finite-field case uses Lang's theorem for the smooth connected
subgroup `P`, via vanishing of its degree-one Galois cohomology. For infinite
fields, the authors first remove the maximal split ground-field unipotent
radical. They then use the opposite dynamic cell and the smooth connected
subgroup generated by ground-field tori to address the possible failure of
Zariski density of `G(k)` over imperfect fields. Local sections give rational
lifts in this infinite-field case.

This file supplies a statement, not a proof. The finite-field torsor
argument, the structural properties of `P`, the infinite-field argument
and comparison of any supplied quotient model with the source's `G/P`
remain proof obligations. None is assumed as an additional hypothesis.
