# Identity and triviality opens of an idempotent

Worker 632.

For an actual closed retract image of a group-scheme endomorphism, the
identity open is the locus where its inclusion is étale at the unit.
When the source and target of this inclusion are smooth and the ambient
group has geometrically connected fibres, the existing smooth monomorphism
theorem makes the inclusion an isomorphism over this open. The image is
fixed by the endomorphism, so cancellation gives equality with the identity.

The zero open is the analogous unit-étale locus for the inclusion of the
trivial group into the retract image. When the image is smooth with
geometrically connected fibres, this inclusion is an isomorphism over the
zero open. Its composite with the original image inclusion is trivial;
cancellation and the exact factorization make the original map trivial.

An identity residue fibre makes the image inclusion an isomorphism, with
inverse the projection, so the point belongs to the identity open. A
trivial residue fibre makes the image inclusion trivial; the retraction
then makes the identity of the image trivial. The unit inclusion into the
image is consequently an isomorphism, placing the point in the zero open.
Thus a supplied identity-or-trivial dichotomy in every residue fibre makes
these two opens cover the base.

The equalities are equalities of actual group-scheme morphisms, over all
test schemes, including nonreduced schemes. This internally derived module
retains the residue-fibre dichotomy as an explicit premise for the cover.
It does not prove disjointness or construct an idempotent of the base ring.
