# Nilpotence of adjoint differences

Author: Worker 1487.

The internal construction `Lie.adjointDifference G g` is the linear
endomorphism `Ad(g) - 1` of the actual dual-number identity kernel, with
its published additive module structure. It is defined for an affine
group scheme over an arbitrary affine base and a point over any
coefficient algebra.

The underlying dual-number point of `(Ad(g) - 1) X` is the group
commutator of the constant lift of `g` with `X`. Induction therefore
places the value of the nth power in the nth lower central term of the
dual-number point group. If a field group has an all-test nilpotence
bound `d`, its adjoint differences have dth power zero over every
coefficient algebra.

The published geometric-to-all-test bound then proves that, for any
smooth connected affine geometrically nilpotent group over a field,
every coefficient-algebra point acts unipotently on the actual Lie
kernel. The field and all coefficients remain in arbitrary universe
`u`; coefficient algebras may have nilpotents. No least bound is
asserted.

This is a deduction from the existing point, Lie-module, adjoint and
uniform-nilpotence constructions. It does not yet prove that a torus
is central, nor construct the quotient in the central-torus theorem.
