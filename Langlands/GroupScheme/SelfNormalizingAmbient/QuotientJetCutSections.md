# Actual sections of identity jets of retained cut subgroups

This internal construction identifies the retained quotient `B/(I+aug^(n+1))`
with actual sections of the cut subgroup's identity jet after arbitrary base
change and on every affine base open. The augmentation of the quotient
corepresentation is the descended ambient counit, so its augmentation-power
quotient is the double-quotient algebra already used to construct the retained
jet ideal. The exact pure tensor formula is the restriction of the corresponding
ambient coordinate modulo `I`. No smoothness or flatness premise is required.
