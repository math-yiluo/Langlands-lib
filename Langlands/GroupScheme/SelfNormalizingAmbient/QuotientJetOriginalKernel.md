# Actual original subgroup jet kernel at the identity orbit point

This internal comparison defines the algebraic orbit linear map at the identity
using the retained ideal. It proves that the actual ambient tensor restriction
to the original subgroup jet is this orbit quotient followed by the actual
original jet section equivalence. The proof checks pure tensors in the fixed
ambient coordinates and extends linearly. Injectivity of the actual section
equivalence gives equality of kernels. This is the identity orbit comparison
only; arbitrary conjugate points and descent to the quotient are still needed.
