# A Krull intersection theorem for smooth algebras with a section

This module proves a Krull intersection theorem for the augmentation ideal of a smooth algebra
with a section over an arbitrary commutative ring, which need not be Noetherian. All rings live in
one universe `u`.

## Base change of a section

Fix commutative rings `A`, `R`, `B`, with `R` and `B` both `A`-algebras, and an `A`-algebra
section `ε : B ⟶ A`.

- `baseSection ε : R ⊗[A] B ⟶ R` is the base change of `ε` to `R`, an `R`-algebra map with
  `baseSection_tmul : baseSection ε (r ⊗ₜ b) = r * ε b` (with `ε b` mapped to `R`).
- `sub_mem_map_ker`: for every `x : R ⊗[A] B`, the difference between `x` and its value under
  `baseSection ε` (mapped back into `R ⊗[A] B`) lies in the ideal generated by the image of
  `ker ε` under `b ↦ 1 ⊗ₜ b`. Proved by induction on `x`. On a pure tensor `r ⊗ₜ b`, the
  difference is `(r ⊗ₜ 1) * (1 ⊗ₜ (b - ε b))`.
- `ker_baseSection`: `ker (baseSection ε)` is the ideal generated by the image of `ker ε` under
  `b ↦ 1 ⊗ₜ b`. One inclusion is `sub_mem_map_ker` and the other is `baseSection_tmul`.
- `eq_baseSection`: an `R`-algebra map `f : R ⊗[A] B ⟶ R` with `f (1 ⊗ₜ b) = ε b` for every `b`
  equals `baseSection ε`. Two `R`-algebra maps out of `R ⊗[A] B` agree once they agree on
  `1 ⊗ₜ b`.
- `mem_of_includeRight_mem`: suppose `A ⟶ R` is injective and `K` is an ideal of `B` with `B ⧸ K`
  flat over `A`. If `1 ⊗ₜ b` lies in the ideal of `R ⊗[A] B` generated by the image of `K`, then
  `b ∈ K`. Proof: `R ⊗[A] (B ⧸ K)` is identified with `(R ⊗[A] B) ⧸ K (R ⊗[A] B)` (Mathlib
  `Algebra.TensorProduct.tensorQuotientEquiv`), so `1 ⊗ₜ [b] = 0` there. Flatness of `B ⧸ K`
  makes `A ⊗[A] (B ⧸ K) ⟶ R ⊗[A] (B ⧸ K)` injective, so `1 ⊗ₜ [b] = 0` in
  `A ⊗[A] (B ⧸ K) ≅ B ⧸ K`.
- **`exists_one_sub_mul_eq_zero_of_baseChange`**: assume `B` is Noetherian, `A ⟶ R` is injective,
  and every quotient `B ⧸ (ker ε) ^ (n + 1)` is flat over `A`. If `1 ⊗ₜ b` lies in every power of
  `ker (baseSection ε)`, then `(1 - j) * (1 ⊗ₜ b) = 0` for some `j ∈ ker (baseSection ε)`. Proof:
  by `ker_baseSection` the `(n + 1)`-st power of `ker (baseSection ε)` is generated by the image
  of `(ker ε) ^ (n + 1)`. So `mem_of_includeRight_mem` gives `b ∈ (ker ε) ^ (n + 1)` for every
  `n`. The Krull intersection theorem in the Noetherian ring `B` (Mathlib
  `Ideal.mem_iInf_smul_pow_eq_bot_iff`) gives `r ∈ ker ε` with `r * b = b`, and `j = 1 ⊗ₜ r`
  works.

## Descent to a Noetherian base

- `exists_finset_mem_range_map`: for `A₀`-algebras `R` and `B₀` and `x : R ⊗[A₀] B₀`, there is a
  finite set `S ⊆ R` such that `x` comes from `A₁ ⊗[A₀] B₀` for every `A₀`-subalgebra `A₁` of `R`
  containing `S`. Proof by induction on `x`: a pure tensor `r ⊗ₜ b` needs `S = {r}`, and sums take
  unions.
- **`exists_descent`**: let `C` be a smooth `R`-algebra with an `R`-algebra section `ε : C ⟶ R`,
  and `l : C`. Then there are a Noetherian ring `A` with an injective ring map `A ⟶ R`, a smooth
  `A`-algebra `B`, an `A`-algebra section `ε₁ : B ⟶ A`, an element `b : B` and an `R`-algebra
  isomorphism `Φ : R ⊗[A] B ≅ C` with `Φ (1 ⊗ₜ b) = l` and `ε (Φ (1 ⊗ₜ y)) = ε₁ y` for all `y`.
  Proof: Mathlib `Algebra.Smooth.exists_subalgebra_fg` (smooth algebras descend to finitely
  generated subrings, `Mathlib.RingTheory.Smooth.NoetherianDescent`) gives a subring `A₀` of `R`
  that is finitely generated over `ℤ`, a smooth `A₀`-algebra `B₀` and `C ≅ R ⊗[A₀] B₀`. Then `B₀`
  is finitely generated over `A₀`, say by a finite set `s`. Let `A₁` be the `A₀`-subalgebra of `R`
  generated by the values of `ε` on `s` and the finite set `S` that
  `exists_finset_mem_range_map` gives for `l`. Then `A₁` is finitely generated over `A₀`, hence
  Noetherian. The section `ε` restricted to `B₀` takes values in `A₁`, so it extends to
  `ε₁ : A₁ ⊗[A₀] B₀ ⟶ A₁`, and `l` comes from some `b : A₁ ⊗[A₀] B₀`. Take `A = A₁`,
  `B = A₁ ⊗[A₀] B₀` (smooth over `A₁` by base change). Define `Φ` as Mathlib
  `Algebra.TensorProduct.cancelBaseChange`, `R ⊗[A₁] (A₁ ⊗[A₀] B₀) ≅ R ⊗[A₀] B₀`, followed by the
  inverse of the descent isomorphism.

## Main result

- **`exists_one_sub_mul_eq_zero_of_mem_pow`**: assume the jet quotients of smooth algebras with a
  section are flat: for all rings `A`, `B` in universe `u` with `B` smooth over `A`, every `A`-algebra
  section `ε : B ⟶ A` and every `n`, `B ⧸ (ker ε) ^ (n + 1)` is flat over `A` (hypothesis `hJ`).
  Let `C` be a smooth `R`-algebra with an `R`-algebra section `ε : C ⟶ R`. If `l` lies in
  `(ker ε) ^ n` for every `n`, then `(1 - j) * l = 0` for some `j ∈ ker ε`.

  Proof: descend with `exists_descent`. The smooth `A`-algebra `B` is finitely presented, hence
  Noetherian. By `eq_baseSection`, `ε ∘ Φ = baseSection ε₁`, so `Φ` maps `ker (baseSection ε₁)`
  onto `ker ε` and `1 ⊗ₜ b` lies in every power of `ker (baseSection ε₁)`.
  `exists_one_sub_mul_eq_zero_of_baseChange`, with flatness from `hJ`, gives `j`, and `Φ j` works.

The flatness hypothesis `hJ` is not proved in this module. It follows from the fact that jet
quotients of smooth algebras with a section are finite projective, which the final proof will
supply.

## Provenance

Internal helper results for the proof of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.SelfNormalizingProperAmbientStatement`. They are intended for the
ideal of the subgroup cut out by the intersection of a subgroup with its conjugate under the
universal stabilizing point. That ideal lies in every power of the augmentation ideal, and the
main result shows that each of its elements is killed by an element congruent to `1` modulo the
augmentation ideal. The Krull intersection theorem for Noetherian rings and the descent of smooth algebras to finitely
generated subrings are standard and taken from Mathlib. The reduction of the non-Noetherian case
to them, through a descended section and flat jet quotients, is internal.
