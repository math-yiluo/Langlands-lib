# Kernels under monomorphic postcomposition

`Langlands.GroupScheme.range_kernelι_comp_mono` states that postcomposing a group-scheme homomorphism with a monomorphism leaves the full subgroup-functor range of its kernel inclusion unchanged. It applies over an arbitrary base scheme. The proof checks every test scheme and cancels the monomorphism.

The existing declaration and proof were moved unchanged from [TriangularDiagonalImageReduction](Absolute/TriangularDiagonalImageReduction.md) so the triangular-image and [fundamental-weight transport](SimplyConnected/SpinCoverFundamentalWeightTransport.md) modules reuse one declaration with its original name.
