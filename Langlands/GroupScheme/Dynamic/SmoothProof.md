# Proof: smoothness of dynamic subgroups of a smooth affine group over a field

This module proves the published statement
[`DynamicSmoothStatement`](SmoothConnectedStatement.md):

```lean
theorem Langlands.GroupScheme.dynamicSmooth : DynamicSmoothStatement.{u}
```

Let `k` be a field and `G` a group scheme over `Spec k` with `G → Spec k`
smooth and affine. Let `χ : 𝔾_m → G` be a cocharacter and `j : H → G` a
homomorphism representing `Dynamic.parabolic χ`, `Dynamic.centralizer χ` or
`Dynamic.unipotent χ`. Then `H → Spec k` is smooth. The trivial cocharacter is
included, and `j` is arbitrary.

## Route

The proof does not follow Conrad–Gabber–Prasad, Proposition 2.1.8(3), and it
does not use `OppositeCellStatement`, `DynamicConnectedStatement` or
`DynamicLieStatement`. It works with the explicit representatives
`Spec (B ⧸ I)` of [`Representability`](Representability.md), where `B` is a
coordinate algebra of `G`. Their ideals are generated by coefficients of the
coaction `c : B → B[T, T⁻¹]`. The coaction is a `ℤ`-grading of `B`
([`CoactionGrading`](CoactionGrading.md)). For such gradings, the quotients by
the negative part and by the off-diagonal part inherit formal smoothness from
`B` ([`GradedFormallySmooth`](../../CommutativeAlgebra/GradedFormallySmooth.md)).
`U_G(χ)` is a retract of `P_G(χ)`, so it is formally smooth as well.

## Steps

All auxiliary declarations are in `Langlands.GroupScheme.DynamicSmooth`.

1. `smooth_algSpec_iff`: for an `R`-algebra `A`, `Spec A → Spec R` is smooth
   iff `A` is a smooth `R`-algebra. This uses
   `HasRingHomProperty.Spec_iff` for `Smooth` and `RingHom.smooth_algebraMap`.
2. `smooth_quotient`: over a noetherian `R`, a formally smooth quotient
   `B ⧸ I` of a smooth `R`-algebra `B` is smooth. It is of finite type as a
   quotient of `B`, hence of finite presentation
   (`Algebra.FinitePresentation.of_finiteType`).
3. `formallySmooth_parabolic` and `formallySmooth_centralizer`: for a
   corepresented `G = Spec B` with `B` smooth over `R`, and any cocharacter
   `χ`, the algebras `B ⧸ parabolicIdeal χ` and `B ⧸ centralizerIdeal χ` are
   formally smooth over `R`. These are `formallySmooth_quotient_negIdeal` and
   `formallySmooth_quotient_offIdeal` with `c = coaction χ`. The grading
   hypotheses are `coaction_algebraMap`, `sum_coeff_coaction` and
   `coaction_coeff_coaction`. `parabolicIdeal χ` and `centralizerIdeal χ` are
   by definition `negIdeal` and `offIdeal` of the coaction.
4. `smooth_parabolicScheme` and `smooth_centralizerScheme`: over a noetherian
   `R`, the constructed `parabolicScheme χ` and `centralizerScheme χ`, that is
   `Spec (B ⧸ I)` as objects `(algSpec R).obj (op (B ⧸ I))`, are smooth over
   `Spec R`. This combines steps 1–3.
5. `smooth_unipotentScheme`. The Library's `multiplicationIso χ :
   U × Z ≅ P` ([`AffineDecomposition`](AffineDecomposition.md)) gives
   morphisms over `Spec R`: `s : U → P`, `u ↦ (u, 1) ↦ u · 1`, and
   `r : P → U`, the inverse followed by the first projection. Their composite
   is `𝟙` (`lift_comp_hom_comp_inv_comp_fst`).
   `formallySmooth_of_algSpec_retract` transports this retraction to
   `R`-algebra maps through the fully faithful `algSpec`
   (`algSpec.fullyFaithful.preimage`). `formallySmooth_of_retract` then gives
   `B ⧸ unipotentIdeal χ` formally smooth, and steps 1–2 conclude.
6. `dynamicSmooth`. `G` is affine because `G → Spec k` is affine and `Spec k`
   is affine (`isAffine_of_isAffineHom`). Its chosen coordinate group scheme
   `Corepresented.groupScheme (AffineCoordinates.functor G) …` is isomorphic
   to `G` (`AffineCoordinates.groupSchemeIso`). Smoothness is invariant under
   isomorphism over the base (`smooth_of_over_iso`), so the coordinate algebra
   `AffineCoordinates.algebra G` is a smooth `k`-algebra by step 1. A field is
   noetherian. The explicit representatives `Dynamic.Affine.parabolicScheme χ`,
   `centralizerScheme χ` and `unipotentScheme χ` are the constructed schemes
   for the transported cocharacter `Dynamic.Affine.cocharacter χ`, so they are
   smooth by steps 4–5. An arbitrary representative `j` is isomorphic to the
   explicit one, with the isomorphism of group schemes
   `SubgroupFunctor.IsRepresentedBy.isoOfIsRepresentedBy`, using
   `Dynamic.Affine.isRepresentedBy_*Hom`. Its underlying isomorphism over
   `Spec k` (`Grp.forget`) transfers smoothness by `smooth_of_over_iso`.

## Supporting modules

- [`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.GradedFormallySmooth`](../../CommutativeAlgebra/GradedFormallySmooth.md):
  the ring-theoretic graded lifting lemmas and the retract lemma.
- [`Langlands.GroupScheme.Dynamic.CoactionGrading`](CoactionGrading.md): the
  coaction of a cocharacter is compatible with the base, has counit `T ↦ 1`,
  and is coassociative.
