# Representability of `P(λ)`, `Z(λ)` and `U(λ)` for the diagonal cocharacters of `GL_n`

Let `n` be a finite type with decidable equality, `R : CommRingCat` and `e : n → ℤ`. Let `λ` be
the published diagonal cocharacter `diagonalCocharacter n R e : 𝔾_{m,Spec R} ⟶ GL_{n,R}`,
`t ↦ diag(t^{e i})`. This module shows that the dynamic subgroup functors `P(λ)`, `Z(λ)` and
`U(λ)` (`Dynamic.parabolic`, `Dynamic.centralizer`, `Dynamic.unipotent`) are represented by
explicit closed subgroup schemes of `GL_{n,R}`, on every scheme `T` over `Spec R`.

## Sources

- Conrad–Gabber–Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive groups*, 2nd ed. (2015), Example 2.1.1, printed
  pp. 45–47 (PDF pp. 71–73): for `GL(V)` with a diagonal cocharacter, `P(λ)`, `U(λ)` and
  `Z(λ)` are the block parabolic subgroup, its block unipotent radical and the block Levi
  subgroup.
- Conrad, *Reductive group schemes* (2014), Theorem 4.1.7(1), p. 112: `P_G(λ)`, `U_G(λ)` and
  `Z_G(λ)` are represented by closed subgroups.

The general affine case is in [`Representability`](Representability.md). For `GL_n` this module
gives a second, explicit representing subgroup: the block subgroup schemes with entry-wise
equations, rather than the ideals generated by coaction coefficients.

## Content

The proofs combine two published results:

- the identification of `P(λ)`, `Z(λ)` and `U(λ)` with the block subgroup functors
  `blockParabolic n R e`, `blockLevi n R e` and `blockUnipotent n R e`
  (`parabolic_diagonalCocharacter`, `centralizer_diagonalCocharacter`,
  `unipotent_diagonalCocharacter` in `Langlands.GroupScheme.Dynamic.GeneralLinearOrbit`);
- the representability of the block subgroup functors by the closed subgroup schemes
  `blockParabolicHom n R e`, `blockLeviHom n R e` and `blockUnipotentHom n R e`
  (`isRepresentedBy_blockParabolic` etc. and `isClosedSubgroup_blockParabolicHom` etc. in
  [`CorepresentedClosedSubgroup`](../CorepresentedClosedSubgroup.md)).

Theorems:

- `isRepresentedBy_parabolic_diagonalCocharacter`: `P(λ)` is represented by
  `blockParabolicHom n R e`. The block parabolic subgroup consists of the invertible matrices
  with entry `(i, j)` zero whenever `e i < e j`.
- `isRepresentedBy_centralizer_diagonalCocharacter`: `Z(λ)` is represented by
  `blockLeviHom n R e` (entry `(i, j)` zero whenever `e i ≠ e j`).
- `isRepresentedBy_unipotent_diagonalCocharacter`: `U(λ)` is represented by
  `blockUnipotentHom n R e` (entry `(i, j)` equal to `δ_ij` whenever `e i ≤ e j`).
- `exists_isClosedSubgroup_isRepresentedBy_diagonalCocharacter`: the conjunction that each of
  `P(λ)`, `Z(λ)` and `U(λ)` is represented by some closed subgroup of `GL_{n,R}` (published
  `IsClosedSubgroup`). This is Conrad's Theorem 4.1.7(1) for `GL_n` and the diagonal
  cocharacters.

No hypothesis on `R` or on the weights `e` is needed; the weights need not be distinct or
ordered.
