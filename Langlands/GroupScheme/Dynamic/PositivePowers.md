# Positive powers preserve actual contracting subgroups

This internal derived extension by Worker 1335 imports
`Dynamic.PositiveScaling` and adds positive power invariance for the actual
identity-limit, or contracting, subgroup functor.

For any commutative ring `R : CommRingCat.{u}`, any group scheme `G` over
`Spec R` with affine underlying scheme, an actual cocharacter
`χ : (Gm (Spec R)).toGrp ⟶ G`, and any integer `n > 0`,
`Dynamic.unipotent_precomp_power` proves

```text
Dynamic.unipotent (groupHom (Gm.powerHom (Spec R) n) ≫ χ) =
  Dynamic.unipotent χ.
```

This is equality of subgroup functors on every scheme over the base, including
nonaffine and nonreduced tests. There is no assumption of finite type,
smoothness, connectedness, reductivity, or a field base. The positive integer
may be divisible by the characteristic.

`Dynamic.unipotent_zsmul` gives the same equality for the composites
`groupHom (n • ν) ≫ j` and `groupHom ν ≫ j`, for any commutative group
scheme `T`, any `j : T.toGrp ⟶ G`, and any actual integral cocharacter
`ν : cocharacterLattice T`. It requires no torus or splitness hypothesis.

`Dynamic.Affine.unipotentPowerIso χ hn` constructs a group-scheme isomorphism
from the published affine representative for the scaled cocharacter to the
representative for `χ`. Its supporting theorem
`unipotentPowerIso_hom_comp` identifies its forward morphism followed by the
latter inclusion with the former inclusion into `G`.

## Implemented proof

The imported module constructs Laurent substitution `T ↦ T^n`, proves its
comparison with the actual power map of `Gm`, and identifies the scaled
coaction with the original coaction followed by that substitution. It also
proves equality of the negative-coefficient ideals and the resulting dynamic
parabolic functors. These are imported results, not new constructions here.

`AffineLine.coeff_laurentPower_zero` specializes the imported coefficient
formula at exponent zero: substitution by a nonzero integer fixes the
constant coefficient. The defining ideal of the contracting subgroup is
the sum of the negative-coefficient ideal and the ideal generated by the
constant coefficients minus the counit values. The first summand is unchanged
by the imported positive scaling theorem. The second has exactly the same
generators by the new constant-coefficient identity.
`Corepresented.unipotentIdeal_precomp_power` therefore proves equality of the
whole defining ideals.

The published affine ideal descriptions and their all-test uniqueness theorem
give `Corepresented.unipotent_precomp_power`. Transport along the canonical
affine-coordinate group-scheme isomorphism gives the result for the original
`G`. The integral-cocharacter corollary uses the additive category identity
`n • ν = (n • 𝟙 _) ≫ ν`. Finally, the published uniqueness construction for
representatives of the same subgroup functor gives the actual isomorphism
and its inclusion compatibility.

## Provenance and retained obligations

Every new definition and proof is derived from the imported Library results,
the elementary coefficient identity, and uniqueness of subgroup
representatives. No separate original literature assertion is used.
This supplies an invariance step for later rank-one comparisons. It does not
prove that the contracting subgroup is unipotent, classify pseudo-parabolics,
construct an opposing normalizer, or prove intrinsic relative-root reflection
existence.
