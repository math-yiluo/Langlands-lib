# The dynamic triple cell over an arbitrary base

`Langlands.GroupScheme.Dynamic.isOpenImmersion_tripleCellMultiplication_overBase` states that the actual ordered multiplication of the negative dynamic radical, centralizer and positive dynamic radical is an open immersion for a smooth affine group over any scheme. Each factor may be any representative of the corresponding subgroup functor.

The proof uses arbitrary-base dynamic representability to choose the parabolic, identifies its underlying scheme with the centralizer–radical product, and composes the published opposite-cell open immersion with that isomorphism.

This module shares the equivalent independently accepted helper from [HenselianCellGeneration](HenselianCellGeneration.md) and [IntegralModel/DynamicTripleCell](../IntegralModel/DynamicTripleCell.md). It preserves their hypotheses and conclusion. The section-generation and integral-model constructions remain in those consumer modules.
