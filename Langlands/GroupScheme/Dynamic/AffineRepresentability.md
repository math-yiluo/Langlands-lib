# Closed dynamic subgroups of arbitrary affine group schemes

This internal construction works over `Spec R` for every commutative ring `R`, every group scheme `G` whose underlying scheme is affine, and every cocharacter `χ : Gm → G`. It constructs group schemes `Dynamic.Affine.parabolicScheme χ`, `centralizerScheme χ` and `unipotentScheme χ`, with inclusions into the original `G`. Each inclusion is a closed immersion and represents the corresponding published dynamic subgroup functor on every test scheme over `Spec R`, including nonreduced and nonaffine tests.

The construction chooses the existing `AffineCoordinates.groupSchemeIso G`, carries `χ` to its corepresented source, and uses the existing Laurent-coefficient quotient schemes in `Dynamic.Representability`. The three inclusions are the corepresented inclusions followed by the isomorphism back to `G`. Consequently the representatives depend on the chosen affine coordinates; their represented functors are the intrinsic published orbit-extension, centralizer and orbit-contraction functors.

The generic lemma `SubgroupFunctor.IsRepresentedBy.comp_iso` transports a represented subgroup through an ambient group-scheme isomorphism. Its monomorphism is the composite of a monomorphism and an isomorphism. Its all-test range equality follows by composing witnesses with the inverse isomorphism. The shared `IsClosedSubgroup.comp_iso` from `Rational.SplitRankTransport` proves closedness of the same composite over an arbitrary scheme base. The three dynamic representability proofs then use the published isomorphism-functoriality equalities from `Dynamic.Functoriality`; they do not infer representability merely from equality of field-valued points.

All hypotheses are explicit: this unit assumes affineness of `G` and an affine base, with no field, smoothness, connectedness, finite-type or reductivity premise. It does not establish any of those additional properties of the dynamic subgroups. It does not add a global-sections identification of the chosen coordinate algebra.

The provenance is an internal categorical derivation from `AffineCorepresented`, `Dynamic.Representability` and `Dynamic.Functoriality`. The mathematical content of the Laurent-ideal construction remains in its existing module; this unit supplies its transport to an arbitrary affine ambient group. There is no additional external structure theorem premise or new literature claim.
