# Functoriality of the actual adjoint determinant

`DynamicAdjointDeterminantFunctorialityStatement` is an unproved proposition
target over every commutative base ring. It uses the actual represented
dynamic parabolic and unipotent subgroup, the affineness inputs of the
conditional construction, and natural finite coordinates on the actual
Lie kernel. The separate structural and existence targets conclude these
inputs from connected reductivity over every field.

The first three conclusions quantify over every homomorphism `A → B` of
test algebras. The actual linear Lie-kernel coefficient map intertwines
the differentials. Their intrinsic determinant units are related by the
unit map induced by `A → B`. The values of the actual constructed
algebraic character obey the same identity. These statements include
every field extension and arbitrary nonreduced test algebra; no flatness
or injectivity of the algebra map is required.

The final conclusion quantifies over every affine group scheme `H` and
actual homomorphism `j : H → P`. Restricting the constructed character by
`characterRestrict j` equals the determinant character constructed from
the restricted actual action on the same `U`. The restricted differential
is the original differential evaluated after `j`. The separate Levi
comparison identifies this restricted action for the actual `M → P`.
Functoriality here concerns coefficient change and restriction of an
acting group; it makes no assertion about arbitrary ambient homomorphisms
changing the acted-on group.

This is derived internal packaging of the actual differential naturality,
the determinant naturality proved using natural finite coordinates, and
functoriality of composition with the algebraic determinant morphism.
It introduces an exact target for a focused proof successor and does
not claim a proof of the whole proposition.

For source context, Conrad–Gabber–Prasad, second edition (2015), §2.1,
printed p.52 (PDF p.78), gives natural tangent base change; §A.7,
printed pp.529–530 (PDF pp.555–556), especially (A.7.2), describes the
actual conjugation differential. Renard, 9 July 2026 version, §V.3.1–V.3.4,
printed p.110 (PDF p.116), and §V.5.4, printed pp.126–127
(PDF pp.132–133), and Waldspurger (2003), §I.1, journal pp.238–239
(PDF pp.4–5), supply the motivating parabolic, algebraic-character and
modulus conventions. The arbitrary-ring package here is derived rather
than a verbatim theorem of these local-field passages.

The action has orientation `p*u*p⁻¹`. Rank zero, arbitrary characteristic,
nonsplit groups and nonreduced roots are retained. Haar measures, absolute
values, square roots and analytic modulus identities are outside this target.
