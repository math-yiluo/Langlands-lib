# Intersections of three cocharacter parabolics

`parabolic_inf_parabolic_inf_parabolic_eq_centralizer` works in a smooth
connected affine field group with a split torus. Let the three cocharacters
sum to zero. If every root vanishing on the difference of the first two
vanishes on all three, the intersection of their dynamic parabolic functors
equals the dynamic centralizer of that difference.

The root-support inclusion places the centralizer in each parabolic. For the
reverse inclusion, the proof represents the intersections by closed
subgroups, using the smooth connected intersection theorem successively
for the three dynamic parabolics. The Lie algebra of the intersection lies
in all three nonnegative weight spans. The split-torus weight decomposition
and the zero-sum identity force the three root weights on this span to be
zero. Thus the included centralizer has the same Lie algebra as the
intersection, and the connected subgroup equality theorem gives equality
on every test scheme.

This is the intersection calculation for the triple of leaf parabolics
in the cyclic trialitarian construction of Platonov and Rapinchuk,
*Algebraic Groups and Number Theory* (1994), §6.8, printed p. 372,
expressed in terms of the root weights of the three cocharacters.
