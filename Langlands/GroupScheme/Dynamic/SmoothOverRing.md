# Smoothness of dynamic subgroups over an arbitrary base ring

This module proves `Langlands.GroupScheme.dynamicSmooth_ring`. Let R be any
commutative ring, G a smooth affine group scheme over Spec R, and
χ : 𝔾ₘ ⟶ G a cocharacter. Then every group scheme H with a homomorphism
j : H ⟶ G that represents one of the subgroup functors `Dynamic.parabolic χ`,
`Dynamic.centralizer χ` or `Dynamic.unipotent χ` is smooth over Spec R.

The Library already has this over a field, as `dynamicSmooth`
(`DynamicSmoothStatement`), and for the constructed representatives over a
noetherian ring (`DynamicSmooth.smooth_parabolicScheme` and its companions in
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Dynamic.SmoothProof`). The noetherian hypothesis was
used only to make the quotient algebras finitely presented. This module removes
it by showing that the three defining ideals are finitely generated.

## Provenance

This is an internal result, written for the local-ring Isomorphism Theorem task
(ab10ec3c), where root groups over a local ring are built as dynamic subgroups.
The statement is the smoothness part of Conrad, *Reductive group schemes*
(2014), Theorem 4.1.7(4) (printed pp.112–113), for an affine base. Conrad–Gabber–Prasad,
Proposition 2.1.8(3), is the field case. The proof combines the Library's formal
smoothness of the graded quotients
(`Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.GradedFormallySmooth`) with the finite generation
proved here.

## Finite generation of the graded ideals

Namespace `Langlands.CommutativeAlgebra.GradedFormallySmooth`. Here B is an
R-algebra with a ring map c : B → B[T, T⁻¹] such that c(r) = r for r ∈ R. In
the application, c is the coaction of χ.

- `mem_range_toLaurent_iff`: a Laurent polynomial is a polynomial exactly when
  its coefficients of negative degree vanish.
- `nonnegMod J` (definition): the subring of Laurent polynomials whose image in
  (B/J)[T, T⁻¹] is a polynomial. By `mem_nonnegMod`, these are exactly the
  Laurent polynomials whose coefficients of negative degree lie in J.
- `mapRingHom_eq_C_iff`: the image of q in (B/J)[T, T⁻¹] is the constant b mod
  J exactly when every coefficient of q − b lies in J.
- `negCoeffs c s` and `offCoeffs c s` (definitions): for a finite set s, the
  finitely many nonzero coefficients of negative degree of c(x), and the
  finitely many nonzero coefficients of c(x) − x, for x ∈ s
  (`negCoeffs_finite`, `offCoeffs_finite`).
- `negIdeal_fg`: if B is a finitely generated R-algebra, then the ideal
  `negIdeal c` (generated by all coefficients of negative degree of all c(b))
  is finitely generated. Choose finite generators s and let J be the ideal
  generated by `negCoeffs c s`. The elements b with c(b) ∈ `nonnegMod J` form
  an R-subalgebra: it is the preimage of a subring, and it contains R since
  c(r) = r. It contains s, hence all of B. So every coefficient of negative
  degree of every c(b) lies in J, and `negIdeal c = J`.
- `offIdeal_fg`: the same for `offIdeal c` (generated by the coefficients of
  c(b) − b). The subalgebra is the equalizer of the two ring maps
  b ↦ c(b) mod J and b ↦ b mod J into (B/J)[T, T⁻¹].
- `unipIdeal c ε` (definition), for a ring map ε : B → B with ε(r) = r on R:
  the ideal generated by the coefficients of negative degree of the c(b) and
  by the differences c(b)₀ − ε(b). `unipIdeal_fg` shows it is finitely
  generated when B is. The elements b with c(b) ∈ `nonnegMod J` and
  c(b)₀ − ε(b) ∈ J form an R-subalgebra. For closure under products,
  `coeff_zero_mul_mod` says the constant coefficient is multiplicative modulo J
  on `nonnegMod J`: modulo J both factors are polynomials, and the constant
  coefficient of a product of polynomials is the product of the constant
  coefficients.

## Smoothness

Namespace `Langlands.GroupScheme.DynamicSmoothRing`, for a corepresented group
with smooth coordinate algebra B and a cocharacter χ:

- `smooth_quotient_of_fg`: a formally smooth quotient of a smooth algebra by a
  finitely generated ideal is smooth (`Algebra.FinitePresentation.quotient`).
- `parabolicIdeal_fg`, `centralizerIdeal_fg`, `unipotentIdeal_fg`: the
  Library's ideals `Corepresented.parabolicIdeal χ`, `centralizerIdeal χ` and
  `unipotentIdeal χ` are `negIdeal`, `offIdeal` and `unipIdeal` of the coaction.
  For the last one, ε is the counit followed by R → B, and `counit_algebraMap`
  (from `Corepresented.algebraMap_comp_pointRing`) gives ε(r) = r.
- `smooth_parabolicScheme`, `smooth_centralizerScheme`,
  `smooth_unipotentScheme`: the Library's constructed representatives are
  smooth. Formal smoothness comes from `formallySmooth_parabolic`,
  `formallySmooth_centralizer`, and the retract argument of the Library's
  `DynamicSmooth.smooth_unipotentScheme` through the multiplication isomorphism
  U × Z ≅ P. The proofs are the Library's noetherian proofs, with
  `smooth_quotient_of_fg` in place of the noetherian quotient lemma.

`dynamicSmooth_ring` then follows as the Library's `dynamicSmooth` does. It
writes G through its affine coordinate algebra
(`AffineCoordinates.groupSchemeIso`) and transfers smoothness along the
isomorphism between any representative and the constructed one
(`IsRepresentedBy.isoOfIsRepresentedBy`).

`dynamicSmooth_ring` uses only the standard axioms `propext`,
`Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.
