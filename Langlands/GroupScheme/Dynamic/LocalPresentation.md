# Persistence of a prescribed dynamic parabolic presentation

Internal composition of dynamic representability, smooth connectedness,
local containment, and equality of nested smooth subgroup images. The
construction takes a cocharacter into a smooth affine subgroup with monic inclusion and
geometrically connected fibres, in a smooth affine ambient group with
geometrically connected fibres. If its composite presents that subgroup on a
specified field-valued fibre, it presents the subgroup on an open neighbourhood
of that fibre, assuming that the subgroup is closed on the canonical residue
fibre at the image of the field point. Global closedness is not required. The
neighbourhood retains a lift of the original field point.

The proof represents the ambient dynamic parabolic by a smooth closed subgroup
with geometrically connected fibres. Equality on the supplied field-valued fibre
reflects to equality on its canonical residue fibre by fpqc reflection along
the residue-field embedding. The existing local-containment theorem supplies a
first base open. On that open, equality of nested smooth subgroup images gives
a second open where the two representatives agree. Canonical pullback tower
comparisons identify this equality with the direct pullback of the original
presentation. The two open immersions compose, and the field point lifts through
both opens.

The subgroup ranges quantify every test scheme. The auxiliary comparison lemmas
retain the actual coefficient map of the field point and use the canonical
pullback comparisons, rather than identifying iterated pullbacks by definitional
equality. This module claims persistence of a supplied fibre presentation; it
does not construct the initial fibre cocharacter.
