# Positive integral scaling of actual dynamic parabolics

This is an internal derived construction and proof by Worker 146.

Let `R : CommRingCat.{u}` be any commutative ring, let `G` be a group scheme over
`Spec R` with affine underlying scheme, and let
`χ : (Gm (Spec R)).toGrp ⟶ G` be an actual cocharacter. For every integer
`n > 0`, `Dynamic.parabolic_precomp_power` proves

```text
Dynamic.parabolic (groupHom (Gm.powerHom (Spec R) n) ≫ χ) =
  Dynamic.parabolic χ.
```

Here `Gm.powerHom (Spec R) n = n • 𝟙 (Gm (Spec R))` is the published actual
power map, sending a unit `t` to `t ^ n`. The equality is of subgroup functors
on every test scheme over `Spec R`, including nonaffine and nonreduced tests.

More generally, for any commutative group scheme `T` over `Spec R`, any
`j : T.toGrp ⟶ G` and any actual integral
`ν : cocharacterLattice T`, `Dynamic.parabolic_zsmul` proves

```text
Dynamic.parabolic (groupHom (n • ν) ≫ j) =
  Dynamic.parabolic (groupHom ν ≫ j).
```

No splitness or maximality assumption is placed on `T` or `j`. Neither theorem
requires `R` to be a field or a domain, or `G` to be smooth, connected, reductive
or of finite type. In particular the field cases include positive
characteristic and integers divisible by the characteristic. The only numerical
restriction in these invariance theorems is the stated strict positivity of `n`.

`Dynamic.Affine.parabolicPowerIso χ hn` constructs an actual group-scheme
isomorphism from the published affine representative of the scaled dynamic
parabolic to the representative of `Dynamic.parabolic χ`. The supporting
theorem `parabolicPowerIso_hom_comp` states that its forward morphism followed
by the latter inclusion is exactly the former inclusion into the original `G`.

## Implemented proof

`AffineLine.scaleExponent n` is multiplication by `n` on the additive exponent
group `ℤ`. `AffineLine.laurentPower A n` is the corresponding ring endomorphism
of `A[T,T⁻¹]`, constructed with `AddMonoidAlgebra.mapDomainRingHom`. It sends
each coefficient term `a T^m` to `a T^(n*m)`. These constructions exist for
every integer `n`, including zero and negative integers.

For nonzero `n`, injectivity of multiplication by `n` on `ℤ` identifies the
coefficient at `n*m` after substitution with the original coefficient at `m`.
Every coefficient at a degree outside the multiples of `n` is zero.
Substitution fixes the coefficient ring, hence defines an actual morphism of
the Laurent test scheme over the original base. The theorem
`laurentPowerMap_comp_laurentPoint` identifies its map on the multiplicative
coordinate with the published power map, by the natural identification of
points of `Gm` with global units. This comparison also holds for every integer.

`Dynamic.orbit_precomp` expresses precomposition of a cocharacter as pullback
of its conjugation orbit on the multiplicative coordinate. The Laurent
comparison therefore identifies the coordinate coaction of the scaled
cocharacter with the original coaction followed by `laurentPower`.

The published affine representation of a dynamic parabolic is cut out by
the ideal generated by all negative-degree coefficients of this coaction.
`Corepresented.parabolicIdeal_precomp_power` proves that the ideal is unchanged
when `n > 0`. Every old negative coefficient occurs at the new negative degree
`n*m`. Conversely, a new negative coefficient either comes from an old negative
degree or is zero because its index is not divisible by `n`.

`Corepresented.IsAffineCutOutBy.eq_of_sameIdeal` uses the existing closed
subgroup construction and its all-test membership theorem to identify two
subgroup functors with the same affine ideal description. Applying it to the
two coefficient ideals gives the equality for a corepresented affine group.
The canonical affine-coordinate isomorphism transfers this equality to the
original `G`, using the existing dynamic-parabolic transport theorem.

The integral-lattice corollary uses the additive category identity
`n • ν = (n • 𝟙 _) ≫ ν`. Finally, the proved equality gives the same represented
subgroup functor for both affine parabolic representatives. The existing
`SubgroupFunctor.IsRepresentedBy.isoOfIsRepresentedBy` constructs their
isomorphism and proves compatibility with their inclusions.

## Provenance and scope

All definitions and proofs are derived from the imported Library constructions,
integer arithmetic and Laurent coefficient identities. No separate original
literature assertion is used. The result supplies the positive scaling step
needed when clearing denominators in a proposed fixed-torus parabolic
classification proof. It does not construct a cocharacter for a given
geometric parabolic, prove a central quotient comparison, or prove
`SplitParabolicCocharacterStatement`.
