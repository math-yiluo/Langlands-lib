# Closed products from an affine homogeneous quotient

This internal construction defines the actual factor-reversing inversion
isomorphism and the actual parabolic identity-coset orbit in a supplied
homogeneous quotient. Over any field and in every universe, if the ambient
group and supplied quotient are affine and the ambient group is smooth, the
parabolic orbit is closed for every cocharacter of the embedded subgroup.
The quotient action has smooth relation by smooth source descent; the embedded
subgroup fixes the identity coset on every relative test. The closed attracting
orbit theorem therefore applies to this actual identity coset.

The all-test coset equation identifies the actual product of the parabolic
and embedded subgroup with a scheme pullback over the supplied quotient.
Consequently its image is the inverse image of the closed orbit and is closed.
Inversion in the ambient group carries that product onto the product in the
opposite order. This uses actual factor-reversing inversion isomorphisms and
actual scheme morphisms, so closedness applies to the full underlying schemes.
The product statements retain monomorphy of the embedded subgroup.

The provenance is internal composition of the actual quotient action, the
closed parabolic orbit theorem, the exact product-pullback identity and
categorical group inversion. This result assumes a supplied affine quotient;
it does not prove reductive quotient affineness or the host properness target.
