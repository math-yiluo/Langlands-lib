# Fppf coordinate projections over arbitrary rings

Author: Worker 204.

This derived unit proves the geometric properties of restriction to any
finite enumerated block of the actual polynomial vector group over an
arbitrary commutative ring. The restriction is flat, surjective, and locally
of finite presentation. Any subgroup functor characterized on every scheme
test by vanishing of the selected coordinates is its represented fppf kernel.

The proof compares the Yoneda coordinate restriction of `VectorCoordinateBlock`
with the primitive-coordinate quotient construction of `VectorGroupQuotient`.
The selected variables and the complementary variables jointly give a
polynomial renaming isomorphism. A universe lift of the complementary finite
index set permits the construction at the arbitrary universe of the base ring.
The existing polynomial-extension proofs then give the three geometric
properties and the all-test kernel equality.

This is internal coordinate geometry for the relative radical filtration
program. It does not construct a height-group quotient, a vector bundle with
nonconstant rank, or a filtration for an arbitrary parabolic.
