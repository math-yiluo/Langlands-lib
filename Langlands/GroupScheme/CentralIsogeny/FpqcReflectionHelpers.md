This module proves the kernel-centrality component of the reflection statement only. Descent of finiteness, flatness, surjectivity and local finite presentation of the underlying map, and assembly of the exact target, are in `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.Morphisms.FpqcFiniteDescent` and `Langlands.GroupScheme.CentralIsogeny.FpqcReflection`.

`IsCentral.of_baseChange` reflects a central actual group-scheme homomorphism along any flat surjective base morphism, in arbitrary universe `u`. To test a point after any restriction of test schemes, the proof pulls that latter test scheme back along the base morphism. The point and the arbitrary comparison point correspond to points of the two pulled-back groups under `baseChangePointsEquiv`. The assumed centrality gives equality of the two products there. Transport through the point equivalence and naturality of multiplication gives equality after precomposition with the test-scheme projection. That projection is flat and surjective, hence an epimorphism of schemes, so the equality reflects.

Provenance is internal and derived from the all-test definition `GroupScheme.IsCentral` in `GroupScheme.Rational.Characters`, the actual pullback point equivalence and the scheme theorem `AlgebraicGeometry.Flat.epi_of_flat_of_surjective`. No centre is assumed representable, and the test schemes may be nonreduced. Quasi-compactness is unnecessary for this centrality component.

`isCentral_kernelι_of_baseChange` precomposes the base-changed kernel inclusion with `kernelBaseChangeIso.hom`, rewrites the composite to the base change of the original kernel inclusion, and applies the preceding centrality reflection. Thus the full scheme-theoretic kernel and all tests are retained. This helper assumes no geometric conclusion of the final target.
