# Cartesian identity jets of closed subgroups

The published map on actual identity jets takes identities and composites
to the corresponding identities and composites. Thus a group isomorphism
induces an isomorphism on every actual jet.

For a closed subgroup of a separated group, the jet inclusion square is
cartesian. First pull back the ideal of the ambient unit along the subgroup
inclusion. The unit square is cartesian by monicity, so the resulting ideal
is the ideal of the subgroup unit. Pulling back its powers then identifies
the actual closed jets. The map on jets also commutes with their actual
pullback maps over every change of base for separated groups.

These comparisons give the literal subgroup jets used in SGA 3,
Exposé XV, §5.2–5.3. The functorial jet map and its projection formulas
come from `IdentityJetSeparatedBaseChange`.
