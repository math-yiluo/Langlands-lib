# Closed integral additive root homomorphisms

Every ring-level additive root homomorphism is a closed immersion, including
over the zero ring. Its map on coordinate algebras sends a matrix entry
supporting the first divided power to the universal scalar times the
corresponding integral entry. Those integral entries generate the unit ideal
by the published primitivity lemma. Induction on their ideal span therefore
puts the universal scalar in the image. Polynomial induction gives
surjectivity of the entire coordinate map, hence a closed immersion of
spectra.

The root map is identified with the spectrum of that explicit map by first
expressing it as the map induced by the natural transformation of represented
functors. Their all-test-point formulas agree with the original root
exponential. In the generic Chevalley closure, cancellation of the closed
matrix inclusion then gives closedness of the constructed root factor.

This is an internal consequence of the published integral matrix and root
factorization constructions and the published first-divided-power primitivity
and entry formulas. No assertion of smoothness or reductivity of the integral
model, or comparison of all its field fibres, is made here.
