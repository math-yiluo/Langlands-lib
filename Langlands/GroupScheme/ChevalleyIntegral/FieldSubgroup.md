# Minimality of the field Chevalley group

This internal consequence of the published field construction proves that a
Chevalley group is contained scheme-theoretically in every closed matrix
subgroup containing its torus and all root maps. The receiving subgroup need
not be smooth or reduced.

On every test scheme, transport the receiving subgroup's points through the
matrix-points equivalence. The torus-points and additive-coordinate equivalences
show that the transported subgroup contains the torus and root generators.
Closure under group operations therefore puts every abstract Chevalley
matrix in that subgroup. The geometric points of the field Chevalley group
are abstract Chevalley matrices by its construction. Since that group is
smooth and locally of finite type over the field, it is reduced, and the
published geometric-point factorization criterion gives scheme-theoretic
containment. Representability of the receiving closed subgroup gives an actual
group homomorphism with the original matrix composite. Closedness follows
by cancellation from the original closed matrix inclusion.

This gives one direction of a possible field-fibre comparison. It does not
show that a receiving integral fibre has no extra components or nilpotents,
or establish smoothness, equality with the field Chevalley group, or integral
reductivity.
