# Integral existence from compatible field Chevalley comparisons

This internal reduction assembles the unchanged integral-existence target from a single flat, locally finitely presented affine family with a closed coordinate torus and compatible field Chevalley comparisons. It leaves construction of that family and its comparisons as explicit hypotheses.

`HasFieldComparisons D G i` means that, for each field with its supplied base-ring algebra structure, the actual base change of the fixed group `G` is isomorphic to a field Chevalley group constructed from the fixed integral Chevalley data `D`. The isomorphism takes the canonical base change of `i` to the coordinate torus map of that field group. All comparisons use the supplied algebra structure. This predicate does not assert existence of an integral family.

`smooth_of_fieldSmooth` is a general affine-base helper. For a flat locally finitely presented scheme morphism, it specializes the all-field smoothness hypothesis to each actual scheme residue field. The affine-spectrum map is the spectrum map of a ring homomorphism, which gives the required algebra structure. Mathlib's `Smooth.of_smooth_fiberToSpecResidueField` then gives smoothness of the original family.

The `HasFieldComparisons` lemmas transfer connected reductivity to each actual field pullback. Flatness and local finite presentation give integral smoothness by the helper, and `isReductive_of_fieldReductive` then supplies the precise Library reductivity predicate. Ambient-isomorphism transport gives maximality of the coordinate torus and its actual full root datum. The latter requires the original coordinate pairing to be the standard integer dot product, and uses the full field-coordinate comparison rather than only a root-support comparison.

`integralExistence_of_comparisons` is a conditional proof of `ChevalleyIntegralExistenceStatement`. Its hypothesis must construct a single flat locally finitely presented affine group over the lifted integers, a single closed coordinate torus, and all compatible field comparisons, for each standard-pairing coordinate datum with Chevalley data. The proof puts an arbitrary finite reduced root datum into compatible full coordinates, chooses a base and integral Chevalley data, applies this hypothesis once, and assembles reductivity, closedness, full root-pairing equivalence, fibrewise maximality and the common actual full root datum. Both full lattices and central directions are retained. No field-dependent choice is substituted for the integral existential quantifiers.

The provenance is internal: this module composes the Library's full coordinate normalization, integral Chevalley-data existence, field Chevalley assembly, actual torus and ambient-isomorphism transport, and the standard smooth-fibre criterion. It is a sufficient criterion and supplies no unconditional integral construction or good-reduction theorem.
