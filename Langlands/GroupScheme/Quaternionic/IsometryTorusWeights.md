# Actual quaternionic torus weights

This is an internal prerequisite for the rank upper bound. The field K and finite-dimensional division K-algebra D are arbitrary, and the coordinate type is finite. The constructions and row comparison impose no characteristic condition, nondegeneracy condition, or local-field hypothesis. Rational-weight detection and the final classification require Infinite K.

`isometryRegularRepresentation` is the actual homomorphism O(q) → GL of the underlying K-vector space of matrices, obtained by composing the published isometry inclusion with `FiniteAlgebraUnits.rightRegularHom`. It acts by inverse right multiplication. `isometryRowAction_of_regular_eigenvector` proves that a coefficient eigenvector corresponding to a matrix M gives that same central scalar eigenvalue on every row of M. The proof uses `rightRegularHom_affine_action`, the tensor-unit identification, and the inverse formula for the actual matrix-point homomorphism.

`torusRowAction` restricts the actual D-linear row operators to the K-rational points of a specified split-torus map into O(q). `scalarWeightSpace_ne_bot_of_regular_eigenvector` proves that a nonzero coefficient eigenvector of a fixed rational monomial supplies a nonzero D-submodule of common row eigenvectors: its corresponding matrix has a nonzero row, and the row comparison applies at every rational unit tuple.

The rational-monomial helpers establish the scalar identities needed by the previously proved `QuadraticWeightSpaces` obstruction. Addition of integer exponents multiplies scalar weights. Over an infinite field, injectivity of `splitTorus.rationalMonomial` implies that a nonzero exponent sum has nontrivial product value at some rational point. A nonzero integral exponent has nonzero double and therefore nontrivial square at some point. These are consequences of the published rational detection theorem, not an assumption about K having any prescribed roots of unity.

For any family of actual D-linear value-preserving operators indexed by the rational points of a split torus, `eq_neg_of_scalarWeightSpace_ne_bot` says that, when q has quadratic Witt index one, two distinct nonzero integral weights with nonzero D-weight spaces must be opposites. Otherwise the nontrivial squared weights and mutual product give a totally singular sum of D-dimension at least two, contradicting the index. Finally, `exists_weight_trichotomy` applies this to any family of occurring weights: all its weights lie in {0, m, -m} for some single integral weight m. The all-zero and empty cases are included.

The provenance is internal: all claims follow from the existing row action, faithful regular coefficient representation, rational monomial detection, and the singular-weight-space dimension obstruction. This module does not yet assert a split-rank bound or complete LocalSpinRankOneStatement.
