# Complementary subspaces for the rank-three skew-hermitian reduction

A symmetric bilinear form over a field with `2 ≠ 0` whose quadratic map is isometric to three hyperbolic planes admits a totally isotropic subspace of dimension three. The construction takes one coordinate axis in each hyperbolic plane and transports their direct sum along the isometry. Polarization then gives the vanishing of the bilinear form on the subspace.

For the symmetric component of a skew-hermitian pairing in quaternion coordinates, `jImage` is the image under the semilinear action of the second quaternion generator. Its dimension equals the dimension of the original subspace. If the three-dimensional totally isotropic subspace intersects its image nontrivially, a nonzero vector in the intersection has skew self-value zero. If the intersection is zero, the two subspaces are complementary in dimension six and each vector can be written as `u + jAction v` with both entries in the original subspace.

This is the subspace argument in Springer's appendix to Kneser, *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups* (1969), Theorem 5.3.3(i), printed p. 110 (PDF p. 116). The semilinear rank comparison and dimension formula give the complement.
