# Affine tensor coordinates for the actual symplectic point image

The unitary group is the actual affine isometry cut of the unit group of a matrix algebra over a finite-dimensional division algebra. `unitaryAffinePointsEquiv` compares its affine coefficient points with the subgroup of units preserving the Gram matrix in the tensor algebra. The comparison evaluates the affine corepresentation of the isometry cut. Its value in the ambient tensor algebra is the affine tensor unit of the image in the unit group.

An ambient affine point lifts to the unitary group exactly when the Gram defect of its affine tensor unit is zero. For an invertible antihermitian Gram matrix and a first-kind involution of symmetric dimension `d(d+1)/2`, where `d` is the reduced degree, the reduced-norm comparison gives the same criterion for the actual symplectic group. These are derived point comparisons for the quaternionic type C cocycle argument.
