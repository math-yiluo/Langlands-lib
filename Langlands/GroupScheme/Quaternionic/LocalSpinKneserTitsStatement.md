# Kneser–Tits for quaternionic spin groups of rank one over local fields

Authors: Worker 5197.

`Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.LocalSpinKneserTitsStatement.{u}` is a proposition
definition with arbitrary universe `u`. It states the Kneser–Tits property `G(K)^+ = G(K)`
for the spin groups in family (iii) of Prasad–Raghunathan (1985), §2. This module does not
prove the proposition.

## Exact statement

For every `K : Type u` with `[Field K] [ValuativeRel K] [TopologicalSpace K]
[IsNonarchimedeanLocalField K]`, every `D : Type u` with `[DivisionRing D] [Algebra K D]
[Algebra.IsCentral K D] [FiniteDimensional K D]` and `Module.finrank K D = 4`, and every
`σ : FirstKindInvolution K D` with `Module.finrank K σ.symmetricSubspace = 3`: for every
`m : ℕ` with `m = 4 ∨ m = 5`, every `q : σ.QuadraticForms (Fin m)` with

- `σ.IsNondegenerateQuadratic q`, and
- `σ.quadraticWittIndex q = 1`,

every group scheme `S` over `Spec K` and every `π : S ⟶ σ.isometryGroup q` with
`σ.IsSpinCover q π`,

`parabolicElementarySubgroup S = ⊤`.

These are all the data and premises. The form data are the same as in
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.LocalSpinRankOneStatement`. The characteristic of `K`,
including residue characteristic 2, is unrestricted.

## Meaning

- `D`, `σ`, `q`, `σ.isometryGroup q` and `σ.IsSpinCover q π` are as in the paired Markdown of
  `Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.LocalSpinRankOneStatement`: `q` is a nondegenerate
  `σ`-quadratic form in the sense of Tits on `D^m` of Witt index 1, and `S` is a spin group of
  `q`, that is, a simply connected group with a central isogeny onto the connected isometry
  group `O(q)⁰` (modules `Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.Forms` and
  `Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.GroupSchemes`).
- The statement is made for every spin group of `q`. Spin groups exist by the existence part of
  `LocalSpinRankOneStatement`, so the statement is not vacuous once that is proved. Different
  spin groups of `q` are isomorphic (universal property of simply connected central covers),
  and `parabolicElementarySubgroup` is invariant under isomorphism (module
  `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.ParabolicWhiteheadIsomorphism`), so quantifying over all
  spin groups asserts no more than the property for one of them. Neither fact is used in the
  statement.
- `parabolicElementarySubgroup S` (module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.ElementarySubgroup`)
  is the subgroup of the rational points `S(K)` generated by the `K`-points of the geometric
  unipotent radicals of all parabolic `K`-subgroups of `S`. The equation `= ⊤` says that these
  points generate `S(K)`. This is the equality `G(K)^+ = G(K)` of Prasad–Raghunathan for
  `G = S`.

## Sources

- G. Prasad and M. S. Raghunathan, *On the Kneser–Tits problem*, Comment. Math. Helv. 60
  (1985), 107–121 (`prasad-raghunathan-1985-kneser-tits`).
  - Introduction, printed p. 107 / PDF p. 2: "G(k)^+ [is] the normal subgroup of G(k) generated
    by the k-rational points of the unipotent radicals of parabolic k-subgroups of G". The
    set of these points is stable under conjugation by `G(k)`, since a conjugate of a parabolic
    subgroup is parabolic. So the subgroup it generates is already normal, and it is
    `parabolicElementarySubgroup G`.
  - §2 (iii), printed p. 111 / PDF p. 6: family (iii) contains "the spin group of a σ-quadratic
    form of Witt index 1 and rank 4 or 5 ...; where σ is an involution of the quaternion central
    division algebra D over K such that the dimension of D^σ, the space of symmetric elements,
    is 3". The same page continues: "For each of the above groups G, it is known that
    G(K)^+ = G(K); see, for example, [8]". The reference [8] is Tits 1977.
- J. Tits, *Groupes de Whitehead de groupes algébriques simples sur un corps*, Séminaire
  Bourbaki 505 (1977) (`tits-1977-whitehead-bourbaki-505`).
  - Introduction, printed p. 218 / PDF p. 2. For an isotropic quasi-simple group `G`, Tits's
    `G^+` is generated by the groups `A(K)`, where `A` runs over the `K`-subgroups isomorphic to
    the additive group. The Whitehead group is `W(G, K) = G(K)/G^+`. Prasad–Raghunathan cite
    `W(G, K) = {1}` from this source as their equality `G(K)^+ = G(K)`. The Lean conclusion
    uses Prasad–Raghunathan's definition through the parabolic radicals, as the task requires.
  - 1.1.6, printed pp. 220–221 / PDF pp. 4–5: for `D` of degree `d` with an involution of the
    first kind whose fixed space has dimension `½d(d + 1)`, "Supposons n ≥ 3 et q non
    dégénérée et isotrope, c'est-à-dire d'indice de Witt strictement positif ..., et soit
    G = Spin q. Alors, si d = 1 ou 2, on a W(G, K) = {1}". Here `d = 2` and `n = ½md = m`
    is 4 or 5, so `n ≥ 3`, and Witt index 1 is isotropic. This holds over every field.
  - 1.2, printed p. 221 / PDF p. 5: for `K` a non-discrete locally compact field and `G`
    quasi-simple, simply connected and isotropic over `K`, `W(G, K) = {1}`. A nonarchimedean
    local field is a non-discrete locally compact field. Printed p. 222 / PDF p. 6 adds that
    for p-adic fields the references and sketches of 1.1 give a complete proof.
- J. Tits, *Classification of algebraic semisimple groups*, Proc. Sympos. Pure Math. 9 (1966),
  33–62 (`tits-1966-classification-algebraic-semisimple`), Table II, types `¹D_n` and `²D_n`,
  printed p. 57 / PDF p. 62, describes the corresponding orthogonal groups (see the paired
  Markdown of `LocalSpinRankOneStatement`).

## Scope

The task allows the Kneser–Tits statement for all isotropic forms of these kinds when the
sources support it. Tits 1977, 1.1.6 covers all nondegenerate isotropic quaternionic quadratic
forms with `m ≥ 3`, over every field. The statement nevertheless keeps Prasad–Raghunathan's
exact family (rank 4 or 5, Witt index 1, nonarchimedean local `K`). This is the case needed by
`Langlands.GroupScheme.LocalRankOneKneserTitsStatement` through the classification of
rank-one groups over local fields, and it has the same hypotheses as the rank-one statement.
The larger generality is not stated here.
