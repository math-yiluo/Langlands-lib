# Split and division quaternion coefficient algebras

Worker 863, Task 7498.

`QuaternionCoefficientDichotomyStatement` concerns a central quaternion division
algebra scalar-extended to any field. The extended algebra is a domain or admits
an algebra isomorphism to the two-by-two matrix algebra over the extension field.
The domain conclusion gives a compatible division-ring structure by finite
dimensionality over the extension field.

Scalar extension preserves simplicity and dimension four. Artin–Wedderburn gives
a matrix algebra over a division algebra. The matrix size is positive, and its
square times the dimension of the division algebra is four. Size one makes the
extended algebra isomorphic to the division algebra. Size two forces its coefficient
algebra to have dimension one, so the coefficient algebra is the extension field.

The split and division cases are used in Springer's appendix to M. Kneser,
*Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups* (1969), §1, printed p. 105
(PDF p. 111), and Theorem 5.3.3(i), printed p. 109 (PDF p. 115).
