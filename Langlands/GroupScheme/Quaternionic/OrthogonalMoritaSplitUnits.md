# Arbitrary-degree split Gram matrix and isometry units

This internal construction uses a supplied matrix splitting `e` of a division algebra with a first-kind involution `σ`. The finite splitting index has cardinality `degree`, and the dimension of the symmetric subspace is `degree * (degree + 1) / 2`. The original matrix index is any finite type.

The existing `DegreeSplitting.splitGram` is `C = Φ(1 ⊗ Q) S`, where `Φ` is the flattened block chart and `S` is the symmetric block involution matrix. `splitGram_add_transpose` identifies its polar matrix with `Φ(1 ⊗ (Q + σ.matrix Q)) S`. `isUnit_splitGram_polar` proves that this matrix is a unit when the quadratic class of `Q` is nondegenerate, using the original polar unit and the explicit inverse of `S`.

For every compatible commutative coefficient algebra `R`, `mem_isometryUnits_split_iff` identifies the original isometry-unit condition with the alternating-matrix condition `Φ(y) C_R Φ(y)ᵀ - C_R = Y - Yᵀ`. It uses the published abstract defect-transport calculation in `MoritaIsometry`, the published base-change description of the quadratic kernel, and the arbitrary-degree involution chart. There is no restriction on characteristic or field cardinality. These are internal algebraic consequences of the published charts; they do not assert existence of a splitting or a hyperbolic basis.

Given matrices `P` and `P'` with `P P' = 1` and `P' P = 1`, and with `P C Pᵀ` congruent to the standard hyperbolic matrix modulo alternating matrices, `conjugatedMatrix` sends a tensor matrix `a` to `P_R Φ_R(a) P'_R`. Its identities prove multiplicativity, preservation of one, and injectivity. `conjugatedMatrix_mem_iff` combines the defect equivalence with `OrthogonalMatrix.isAlt_conj_sub_iff` to identify its orthogonal membership with original isometry membership.

`splitUnitsHom` is the resulting group homomorphism. Its bijectivity proof recovers a preimage of an orthogonal matrix `g` by taking the unit with split matrix `P'_R g P_R`; the inverse matrix is `P'_R invMatrix(g) P_R`. The two inverse identities follow from those of the basis matrices and the canonical orthogonal inverse. Injectivity follows by conjugating back and using injectivity of the chart. `splitUnitsEquiv` bundles this bijective homomorphism as a multiplicative equivalence, and `coe_splitUnitsEquiv` retains the actual matrix formula.

`splitUnitsEquiv_naturality` proves compatibility with every morphism of compatible coefficient algebras. It uses the existing tensor-unit coefficient map and naturality of the matrix chart; entrywise coefficient maps also preserve the two constant basis matrices. Thus this unit provides the exact natural all-coefficient group comparison from supplied splitting and hyperbolic data. Group-scheme reconstruction and existence of that data remain for the subsequent assembly.
