# Matrix charts on the scalar norm-one group

For a scalar quadratic form on a nonempty finite coordinate set, the
actual points on a relative test scheme inject into the invertible
matrices over its global sections. Their image consists precisely of the
matrices of determinant one whose quadratic defect is alternating. The
chart is multiplicative and pullback applies the section homomorphism to
every coefficient. These assertions include nonaffine and nonreduced
tests.

An equivalence of matrix groups identifying these images induces a
multiplicative equivalence of the actual point groups, with the same
matrix formula. The construction uses the tensor matrix chart for the
actual ambient inclusion and the exact range equations for that
inclusion. It is an internal comparison of existing constructions for
scalar forms in all characteristics.
