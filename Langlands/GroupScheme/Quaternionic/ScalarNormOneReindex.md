# Renumbering scalar quadratic coordinates

A bijection of the coordinate indices gives a scalar quadratic form by
renumbering the representative matrix. The same renumbering identifies
the invertible matrix groups on every relative test. It preserves the
alternating-defect equations and the determinant, so it gives an actual
isomorphism of the two reduced-norm-one kernels by the full point chart.

Renumbering the two vector arguments preserves the scalar pairing and
the quadratic values. It therefore preserves zero quadratic radical.
Matrix rank is invariant under reindexing, so the polar-kernel dimension
is invariant by rank and nullity. These are internal coordinate
comparisons and apply over every field.
