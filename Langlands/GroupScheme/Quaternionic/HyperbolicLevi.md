# The Levi subgroup and the Eichler subgroup of a hyperbolic pair

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.HyperbolicLevi`, namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.FirstKindInvolution.Eichler`.

## Setting

As in `RationalIsometry` and `EichlerTransformations`: `σ` is an involution of the first
kind of a division `K`-algebra `D`, and `q` is a Tits `σ`-quadratic form on row vectors
`D^n`. Its polar form is `h(x, y)` (linear in `x`, `σ`-semilinear in `y`) and its values are
`q(x)`, taken in `D` modulo the alternating subspace `Alt = {a - σ(a)}`. Matrices act on row
vectors on the right, so a product `M₁ * M₂` applies `M₁` first.

## Definitions

* `IsHyperbolic σ q e f`: `(e, f)` is a **hyperbolic pair**, meaning `q(e) = q(f) = 0` and
  `h(e, f) = 1`.
* `levi σ q hP : Dˣ →* (Matrix n n D)ˣ`: the **Levi homomorphism** `a ↦ ℓ(a)`, where `ℓ(a)` is
  the hyperbolic scaling `scalingMatrix e f a σ(a)⁻¹`. It sends `e ↦ a e` and
  `f ↦ σ(a)⁻¹ f`, and fixes `{e, f}^⊥` pointwise.
* `eichlerSubgroup σ q e f`: the subgroup `E` generated by the Eichler transformations along
  `e` and along `f` (`eichlerSet σ q e ∪ eichlerSet σ q f`).
* `weylMatrix σ q e f x c`: the **Weyl element**
  `m_{x,c} = E^f_{(σc)⁻¹x, (σc)⁻¹} · E^e_{x,c} · E^f_{c⁻¹x, (σc)⁻¹}`.
* `eichlerLeviSubgroup hP`: the subgroup `N_E = {a ∈ Dˣ | ℓ(a) ∈ E}` (the preimage of `E` under
  `levi`).
* `orthoReflectionSet σ q e f`: the reflections `ρ_{y,c}` with `y ⊥ e, f`, `c ≠ 0` and
  `[c] = q(y)`.

## Results

In what follows, `x ⊥ e, f`, `c ≠ 0`, `[c] = q(x)`, and `a₀ ∈ Alt` is nonzero.

* `IsHyperbolic.matrix_ext`: a matrix is determined by its values on `e`, on `f` and on
  `{e, f}^⊥`. This uses the decomposition `v = h(v,f) e + h(v,e) f + w₀`.
* `levi_mem`: every `ℓ(a)` is a rational isometry.
* `range_levi_le_normalizer`: the Levi subgroup normalizes `E`. It rests on
  `inv_mul_mul_mem_eichlerSet` (conjugating an Eichler transformation along `e` by an
  isometry `g` with `e g = d e` gives an Eichler transformation along `e`) and on the general
  group lemma `le_normalizer_closure`.
* `vecMul_weylMatrix_left/right/of_ortho`: `m_{x,c}` sends `e ↦ -(σc)⁻¹ f` and `f ↦ -c e`,
  and acts as `ρ_{x,c}` on vectors orthogonal to `e` and `f`.
* `exists_weyl_mem_eichlerSubgroup`: `m_{x,c}` is a unit lying in `E`.
* `levi_mul_reflectionMatrix_mul_weylMatrix`: `ℓ((σc)⁻¹ σ(a₀)) ρ_{x,c} m_{0,a₀} = m_{x,c}`.
* `weylMatrix_mul_levi`: `m_{x,c} ℓ(d) = ℓ((σc)⁻¹ (σd)⁻¹ σc) m_{x,c}`.
* `eichlerLeviSubgroup_normal`: `N_E` is normal in `Dˣ`.
* `levi_mul_mem_eichlerSubgroup`: `ℓ((σc)⁻¹σ(a₀)) ρ_{x,c} ∈ E`.
* `mem_sup_of_reflection`: `ρ_{x,c} ∈ ℓ(Dˣ) ⊔ E`.
* `algebraMap_mem_eichlerLeviSubgroup`: nonzero scalars `t ∈ K` lie in `N_E`.
* `inv_mul_mem_eichlerLeviSubgroup`: `d⁻¹ (σc)⁻¹ (σd)⁻¹ σc ∈ N_E` for every unit `d`.
* `commutator_mem_eichlerLeviSubgroup`: for `z = σ(c) σ(a₀)⁻¹`, every `g⁻¹ z g z⁻¹` lies in
  `N_E`.
* `mem_closure_orthoReflectionSet` (a Cartan–Dieudonné-type statement): if `{e, f}^⊥` is
  anisotropic, i.e. `y ⊥ e, f` and `q(y) = 0` force `y = 0`, then every rational isometry
  fixing `e` and `f` is a product of reflections in `orthoReflectionSet`.
* **`mem_sup_of_vecMul_eq_smul`**: suppose `{e, f}^⊥` is anisotropic and `a₀ ∈ Alt` is
  nonzero. Then every rational isometry `u` with `e u = a e` and `f u = b f` lies in
  `ℓ(Dˣ) ⊔ E`.

## Proof notes

* The Weyl-element formulas are computed step by step with the explicit action of Eichler
  transformations on vectors `v` with `h(v, ·) = 0` or `1`.
* The identities between `ℓ`, `ρ` and `m` follow from `IsHyperbolic.matrix_ext`.
* The Cartan–Dieudonné-type statement is proved by induction over the set of standard basis
  vectors `s` not yet fixed. Let `y = s w - s` and `c = h(s w, y)`. Then
  `[c] = q(y)`, `c ≠ 0` by anisotropy, `y` is orthogonal to `e`, `f` and to every
  standard basis vector `w` already fixes, and `w ρ_{y,c}` also fixes `s`.
* For the main theorem, `ℓ(a)⁻¹ u` fixes `e` and `f` (the isometry property gives
  `a σ(b) = 1`). Each reflection lies in `ℓ(Dˣ) ⊔ E` by `mem_sup_of_reflection`.

## Provenance

Internal. These are the classical constructions for isotropic quadratic or hermitian spaces
(the hyperbolic Levi factor, Eichler–Siegel transformations, Weyl elements built from
them, and generation by reflections). Here they are written for Tits's `σ`-quadratic forms
over a division algebra, with an arbitrary characteristic. They serve the proof of the
Kneser–Tits property for quaternionic spin groups of Witt index one (Task 5398).

* `mem_sup_of_vecMul_eq_smul` describes, at the level of rational points, the centralizer of
  the cocharacter `t ↦ ℓ(t)` modulo the Eichler subgroup.
* `N_E` controls which Levi elements already lie in `E`.
