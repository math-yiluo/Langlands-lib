# Tensor coordinates in a division-algebra basis

For a skew-hermitian pairing on a left vector space over a division algebra `D` over `F`, an arbitrary finite `D`-basis identifies the extension of the vector space to a field `R` with rows over `R ⊗ D`. The isomorphism first extends the basis coordinate map over `F`, then distributes the tensor product across the finite row.

In these coordinates, the scalar extension of the pairing is the row expression with Gram entries `1 ⊗ B(bᵢ,bⱼ)` and the scalar-extended involution on the second row. Scaling the scalar-extended pairing by a ground-field scalar scales these Gram entries by the same scalar. The proof checks pure tensors and uses bilinearity to obtain the formula for all vectors.

The tensor formula is an auxiliary calculation for the change to a complementary basis in the local value argument of Springer's appendix to Kneser, *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups* (1969), Theorem 5.3.3(i), printed p. 110 (PDF p. 116).
