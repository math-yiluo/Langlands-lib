# Unitary matrix groups over a division algebra with involution

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.UnitaryMatrices`.

Throughout, `K` is a field, `D` a division `K`-algebra, `σ : FirstKindInvolution K D` an
involution of the first kind, `n` a finite index type and `H : Matrix n n D` a Gram matrix.
`σ.matrix n M` is the conjugate transpose `ᵗM^σ` (published in `Quaternionic.Involution`).
Vectors are rows, so `g` acts by `x ↦ x g` and preserves the form `h(x, y) = x H ᵗy^σ`
exactly when `g H ᵗg^σ = H`.

## Definitions

- `unitaryMatrixGroup H : Subgroup (Matrix n n D)ˣ`: the invertible matrices `g` with
  `g * H * σ.matrix n g = H`. This is the group of `K`-points of the unitary group of the
  form, written in matrix coordinates; it is a plain subgroup of `GL_n(D)`, with no group
  scheme involved.
- `unitaryOfIsometry hH hg`: for invertible `H`, the element of `unitaryMatrixGroup H` with a
  prescribed underlying matrix `g` satisfying the isometry equation.
- `IsContracting w g` for a weight function `w : n → ℤ`: `g i j` equals the identity
  entry at every position with `w i ≤ w j`. So `g - 1` is supported on positions with
  `w i > w j`, the positions where conjugation by the cocharacter `t ↦ diag(t^{w i})`
  multiplies the entry by `t^{w i - w j}` with a positive exponent.
- `contractingElementarySubgroup H w`: the subgroup generated by the elements of
  `unitaryMatrixGroup H` of contracting shape for `w` or for `-w`.
- `Realized E M`: the matrix `M` is the underlying matrix of an element of the subgroup `E`.
- `symmetricDefect a`: the `K`-linear map `t ↦ a t - σ(a t)` from the symmetric elements
  to the alternating elements `{x - σ x}`.

## Results

- `map_inv`, `map_eq_zero_iff`, `map_ne_zero`: `σ` commutes with inverses and is injective
  at zero. `map_eq_neg_of_mem_alternating`: alternating elements are skew.
- `mem_unitaryMatrixGroup`, `isometry_mul` (the isometry equation is closed under products),
  `isUnit_of_isometry` (an isometry of an invertible Gram matrix is invertible: `g` has the
  right inverse `H ᵗg^σ H⁻¹`, and a one-sided inverse in a matrix ring over a division ring is
  two-sided).
- `contractingElementarySubgroup_le`: the generated subgroup lies in the unitary group.
- `Realized.one`, `Realized.mul`, `Realized.of_mul_left`, `Realized.of_mul_right`,
  `Realized.mem`: closure properties used to assemble factorizations.
- `isometry_of_realized`, `realized_of_isometry`: a matrix realized in the contracting
  elementary subgroup is an isometry; an isometry of contracting shape for `w` or `-w` is
  realized in it.
- `ker_id_sub_eq_symmetricSubspace`, `finrank_alternatingSubspace`: if `dim_K D = 4` and
  `dim_K D^σ = 3`, then `{x - σ x}` is a line (rank–nullity for `id - σ`).
- `exists_eq_mul_symmetric`: under the same hypotheses every `a ∈ D` is `s t` with `σ s = s`,
  `σ t = t` and `t ≠ 0`. Proof: `symmetricDefect a` maps a `3`-dimensional space to a line,
  so it has a nonzero kernel element `t₀`; then `a t₀` is symmetric and `a = (a t₀) t₀⁻¹`.

## Provenance

Internal. These are elementary linear-algebra facts about sesquilinear forms over a division
algebra with involution, derived directly from the published `FirstKindInvolution.matrix`
(conjugate transpose), subgroup closure and rank–nullity. The contracting shape anticipates
the cocharacter `t ↦ diag(t^{w i})`: conjugation multiplies the `(i, j)` entry by
`t^{w i - w j}`. No statement about group schemes is made here.

The inverse and alternating-element identities are imported from
[AlternatingConjugation](AlternatingConjugation.md); their duplicate copies were removed.
