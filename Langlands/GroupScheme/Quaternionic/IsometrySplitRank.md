# Split rank at quadratic Witt index one

`Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.WittIndexOneSplitRankStatement` is the following proposition at arbitrary universe u. For any infinite field K, finite-dimensional division K-algebra D, first-kind involution σ, finite coordinate type n, and σ-quadratic form q, if q is nondegenerate, its quadratic Witt index is one, and its actual isometry group O(q) is smooth, then both O(q) and its identity component have split rank one. The same module proves this proposition as `wittIndexOneSplitRankStatement`. It requires neither a characteristic restriction nor a quaternion dimension or involution-type hypothesis. Smoothness is an explicit premise, to be supplied by the separate quaternionic geometry development when applying this result to the local spin statement.

The stronger intermediate upper bound `FirstKindInvolution.splitRank_isometryGroup_le_one` uses only quadratic Witt index one. It does not assume that q is nondegenerate or that O(q) is smooth. To prove it, `card_le_one_of_mono_isometryTorus` first considers any actual monomorphism from a finite split torus T_I to O(q). Compose it with the exact closed inclusion into matrix-algebra units and the faithful inverse-right-regular representation from `FiniteAlgebraRightRegular`. The composite is a monomorphism of relative schemes.

The published `GeneralLinear.CocharacterDiagonal.exists_diagonalizingMatrix` supplies one matrix B and integral weights w_j valid over every K-algebra. Each nonzero column of B is a coefficient eigenvector at rational points. `IsometryTorusWeights.scalarWeightSpace_ne_bot_of_regular_eigenvector` extracts a nonzero actual D-row weight space for each such weight. Those D-linear row operators preserve the full quotient-valued quadratic form by `IsometryRowAction.quadraticValue_isometryRowAction` (in the FirstKindInvolution namespace).

The weight obstruction `IsometryTorusWeights.exists_weight_trichotomy` confines all weights to {0, m, -m}: two distinct nonzero nonopposite weights would span a totally singular D-subspace of dimension at least two. The infinite-field assumption enters here through rational monomial detection. `GeneralLinear.CocharacterDiagonal.card_le_one_of_weight_trichotomy`, in `Rational.SingleWeightRepresentation`, then factors the conjugated faithful representation through the single actual exponent character for m, proving Nat.card I ≤ 1. Taking the supremum over the closed split subtori in the unchanged definition of splitRank gives the upper bound for O(q).

For smooth O(q), the closed inclusion of its identity component carries this upper bound to the connected isometry group. Nondegeneracy and positive Witt index supply a hyperbolic pair; `HyperbolicTorus.one_le_splitRank_connectedIsometryGroup_of_quadraticWittIndex_pos` supplies the matching lower bound using the actual hyperbolic torus. Antisymmetry gives the connected rank equality. The identity-component inclusion and the upper bound also give the full isometry-group equality.

Provenance is internal. The proof combines the actual group schemes, the established characteristic-free hyperbolic torus, the regular-representation and row-action comparisons, rational monomial detection, the singular-weight-space dimension obstruction, and the cocharacter-lattice rank bound. This unit completes the rank comparison needed by LocalSpinRankOneStatement. Existence of spin covers, smoothness, and almost simplicity remain separate obligations.
