# Lifting Eichler transformations to a spin cover

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.SpinEichlerLift`.
`multiplication_gm_eq_groupHom` is in namespace `Langlands.GroupScheme`. The other results are
in `Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.FirstKindInvolution.Eichler`.

## Setting

* `D` is a finite-dimensional division algebra over a field `K`, with an involution of the
  first kind `σ`. `q : σ.QuadraticForms n` has polar form
  `h(v, w) = σ.sesqForm (σ.polar q) v w`. Matrices act on row vectors, `v ↦ v M`.
* `h : σ.IsHyperbolicPair q e f` means `q(e) = 0`, `q(f) = 0` and `h(e, f) = 1`.
  `M(s) = σ.hyperbolicScalingMatrix q e f s` is the hyperbolic scaling for `s ∈ Kˣ`. It sends
  `e ↦ s e` and `f ↦ s⁻¹ f`, and it fixes `{e, f}^⊥`.
* `μ = h.hyperbolicIsometryTorus : 𝔾_m ⟶ O(q)` is the Library's hyperbolic cocharacter
  (`HyperbolicTorus`, needs `[Infinite K]`).
* `isometryRationalMatrix σ q` is the matrix of a rational point of `O(q)`
  (`EichlerDynamic`).
* `π : S ⟶ σ.isometryGroup q` is a spin cover, `hπ : σ.IsSpinCover q π`. So `S` is simply
  connected, `O(q)` is smooth, and `π` is a central isogeny `p : S ⟶ O(q)⁰` followed by the
  inclusion of the identity component.
* `levi σ q hP : Dˣ →* (Matrix n n D)ˣ` sends `a` to `ℓ(a)`: `e ↦ a e`, `f ↦ σ(a)⁻¹ f`, the
  identity on `{e, f}^⊥`. `eichlerSubgroup σ q e f` is the subgroup `E` generated by the Eichler
  transformations along `e` and `f` (`HyperbolicLevi`).
* `parabolicElementarySubgroup S` is `S(K)⁺`, the subgroup of `S(K)` generated by the rational
  points of the unipotent radicals of the parabolic subgroups.

## Definitions

* `spinRationalMatrix π : rationalPoints S →* (Matrix n n D)ˣ` sends `s` to
  `isometryRationalMatrix σ q (π(s))`, the matrix of the image of `s` in `O(q)`
  (`spinRationalMatrix_apply`, by `rfl`).

## Results

* `multiplication_gm_eq_groupHom`: multiplication by `m` on `𝔾_m` equals
  `CommGroupScheme.groupHom (Gm.powerHom S m)`.
* `isHyperbolic_of_isHyperbolicPair`: `σ.IsHyperbolicPair q e f` implies
  `IsHyperbolic σ q e f`.
* `eq_smul_left_of_vecMul_hyperbolicScalingMatrix`: suppose `s² ≠ 1` and `v M(s) = s v`. Then
  `v = h(v, f) e`. *Proof.* Pairing with `e` gives `s h(v,e) = s⁻¹ h(v,e)`, so `h(v,e) = 0`.
  Then `(s - 1)(v - h(v,f) e) = 0`.
* `hyperbolicScalingMatrix_swap`: `M_{e,f}(s) = M_{f,e}(s⁻¹)`.
* `eq_smul_right_of_vecMul_hyperbolicScalingMatrix`: suppose `s² ≠ 1` and `v M(s) = s⁻¹ v`.
  Then `v = h(v, e) f`.
* `isometryRationalMatrix_map_hyperbolicIsometryTorus`: the matrix of `μ(x)`, for a rational
  point `x` of `𝔾_m` with value `t`, is `M(t)`.
* **`exists_mem_parabolicElementarySubgroup_of_mem_unipotent`**: let `ν` be any cocharacter of
  `O(q)`. Every rational point `g` of `O(q)` in `Dynamic.unipotent ν` equals `π(s)` for some
  `s ∈ S(K)⁺`. *Proof.*
  1. `g` lies in `O(q)⁰` (`AutomorphicAction.unipotent_le_identityComponent`). It is contracted
     by the factorization `ν₀` of `ν` through `O(q)⁰`
     (`Dynamic.unipotent_eq_comap_of_isClosedSubgroup`).
  2. Take `N > 0` killing the kernel of `p`. The power lift
     `IsCentralIsogeny.exists_comp_eq_multiplication_comp` gives `χ : 𝔾_m ⟶ S` with
     `χ ≫ p = [2N] ≫ ν₀`.
  3. Positive powers do not change contracting subgroups (`Dynamic.unipotent_precomp_power`),
     so `g ∈ U(χ ≫ p)`.
  4. `Dynamic.exists_unipotent_lift` lifts `g` to `s ∈ U(χ)(K)`.
  5. `s ∈ S(K)⁺` by `dynamicUnipotent_le_parabolicElementarySubgroup`. The connected
     reductivity of `S` comes from `IsSimplyConnected.isSemisimple`.
* **`eichlerSubgroup_le_map`** (`[Infinite K]`, `[LinearOrder n]`): `E` is contained in the
  image of `S(K)⁺` under `spinRationalMatrix π`. *Proof.* It suffices to check
  the generators. Eichler transformations along `f` and along `e` are matrices of rational
  points contracted by `μ` and by `h.symm.hyperbolicIsometryTorus`
  (`exists_mem_unipotent_of_mem_eichlerSet`, `_left`). Then apply the previous result.
* **`parabolicElementarySubgroup_eq_top_of_levi_of_kernel`**: let `K` be a nonarchimedean local
  field with `[Infinite K]`, and `[LinearOrder n]`. Assume:
  - `h : σ.IsHyperbolicPair q e f`;
  - `a₀ ∈ σ.alternatingSubspace` with `a₀ ≠ 0`;
  - `{e, f}^⊥` is `q`-anisotropic (`hanis`);
  - `hπ : σ.IsSpinCover q π`;
  - (`hlevi`) for every rational point `s` of `S` and `a ∈ Dˣ`, if the matrix of `π(s)` is
    `ℓ(a)`, then `ℓ(a) ∈ E`;
  - (`hker`) every rational point `s` with `π(s) = 1` lies in `S(K)⁺`.

  Then `parabolicElementarySubgroup S = ⊤`.

  *Proof.* Lift a power of the hyperbolic cocharacter to `χ : 𝔾_m ⟶ S` with
  `χ ≫ π = [2N] ≫ μ`. By the Library's
  `centralizer_rationalPoints_sup_parabolicElementarySubgroup_of_localField`,
  `Z_S(χ)(K) ⊔ S(K)⁺ = S(K)`. So it suffices to show `Z_S(χ)(K) ≤ S(K)⁺`.

  Let `s ∈ Z_S(χ)(K)` and let `u` be the matrix of `π(s)`. Choose `t ∈ Kˣ` with
  `t^{4N} ≠ 1`; this needs `K` infinite. Then `s` commutes with `χ(t)`
  (`Dynamic.commute_cocharacter_of_mem_centralizer`). So `u` commutes with `M(t^{2N})`. By the
  eigenvector lemmas, `e u ∈ D e` and `f u ∈ D f`. `mem_sup_of_vecMul_eq_smul` then gives
  `u ∈ ℓ(Dˣ) ⊔ E`. Since `ℓ(Dˣ)` normalizes `E`, `u = ℓ(a) ε` with `ε ∈ E`.

  Lift `ε` to `s₁ ∈ S(K)⁺`. Then `π(s s₁⁻¹)` has matrix `ℓ(a)`, so `ℓ(a) ∈ E` by `hlevi`. Lift
  `ℓ(a)` to `s₂ ∈ S(K)⁺`. Now `π(s s₁⁻¹ s₂⁻¹) = 1`, because the matrix map is injective
  (`isometryRationalMatrix_injective`). So `hker` gives `s s₁⁻¹ s₂⁻¹ ∈ S(K)⁺`, hence
  `s ∈ S(K)⁺`.

## Provenance

Internal. This is a reduction step for Task 5398 (Kneser–Tits for quaternionic spin groups of
Witt index one over nonarchimedean local fields). The Lean proof combines the Library's dynamic subgroup, power-lift, central-isogeny lifting and
local-field centralizer results with the explicit computations in `HyperbolicLevi` and
`EichlerDynamic`. The two hypotheses `hlevi` and `hker` concern the spin cover itself and are
left as hypotheses here. In Task 5398, `hlevi` is to follow from a reduced-norm condition via
`EichlerReducedNorm`, and `hker` from a description of the kernel of the spin cover.
