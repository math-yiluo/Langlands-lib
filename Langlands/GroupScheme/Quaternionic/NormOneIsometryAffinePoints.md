# Affine tensor coordinates on the actual norm-one isometry kernel

This module identifies affine coefficient points of the actual norm-one
isometry group with the kernel of canonical reduced norm on the tensor
isometry subgroup. The coefficient algebra is arbitrary, including
nonreduced algebras. The division algebra remains finite-dimensional and
central over the ground field, and its involution is of the first kind.

The existing cut-out isometry group supplies its affine points equivalence.
The actual reduced-norm homomorphism agrees with canonical reduced norm on
these affine tensor units. The generic kernel-points equivalence is then
transported through the two coordinate equivalences. The underlying tensor
unit is exactly the affine point followed by the actual ambient inclusion.

These are internal coordinate constructions for the published isometry
cut and norm-one kernel, with no new literature translation or structural
classification claim. The ambient finite nonempty index lies in `Type`,
and the field, division algebra and coefficient algebras have arbitrary
universe `u`.
