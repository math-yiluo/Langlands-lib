# Original-algebra equations for affine isometry-cut points

Author: Worker 118.

This internal helper transports the existing isometry-cut equation from
relative global sections to the original algebra over the base field.
The algebra is associative and may be noncommutative; it has finite
dimension over the field, and the involution is of the first kind.

Tensor induction proves that the tensor unit map commutes with the
extended involution. For an actual affine base-field point of the
zero-subspace isometry cut, its composite with the closed inclusion lies
in the range of that inclusion. The published range theorem gives the
zero-defect equation over relative global sections. Coefficient
naturality and the canonical affine-section comparison give the same
equation in the original affine coefficient algebra. The tensor unit map
then gives the equation in the original algebra.

Applying this result to the inverse group-scheme point gives the inverse
isometry equation as well. No nondegeneracy, antihermitian condition or
characteristic restriction is used. These are affine equations for the
general isometry cut; the module does not construct the rational-point
bridge or converse for the reduced-norm-one symplectic group.
