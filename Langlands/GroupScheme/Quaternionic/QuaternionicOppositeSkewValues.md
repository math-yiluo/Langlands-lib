# Opposite invertible skew values after scalar extension

`QuaternionicOppositeSkewValuesStatement` concerns two invertible skew Gram matrices for canonical quaternion conjugation extended to a field over the ground field of characteristic zero. Both index sets are nonempty. Two rows in the orthogonal sum with zero first self-pairing and cross-pairing one imply that the two summands represent opposite invertible skew values.

The coefficient algebra is either division or a two-by-two matrix algebra. In the division case the chosen row gives opposite values directly when its two self-pairings are nonzero. If they both vanish, the normalized partner guarantees that at least one component is a nonzero isotropic row. That block represents every skew value. The other block has a nonzero diagonal value on some vector, by separation of its pairing and nontriviality of the conjugation. In the split case the two scalar rows give the isotropic plane required by the split quaternion chart theorem. The module also proves that invertibility and the negative adjoint equation pass to the scalar-extended Gram matrices. This implements the opposite-value step used in Springer's higher-rank argument. [Kneser, *Lectures on Galois cohomology of classical groups*, Springer appendix, §3 Lemma 5.3.4(a), p. 111](https://www.math.tifr.res.in/~publ/ln/tifr47.pdf)
