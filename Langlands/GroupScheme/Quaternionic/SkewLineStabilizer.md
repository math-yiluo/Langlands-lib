# Matrix stabilizer of an adjoined nonsingular skew line

`SkewLineStabilizerStatement` works with the scalar extension of a central division algebra to any commutative coefficient algebra. For the Gram matrix obtained by appending an invertible coefficient as a line, a norm-one matrix isometry fixes the last standard row exactly when it extends a norm-one isometry of the original block by the identity on the added line.

The condition on the last row determines the bottom blocks of the isometry matrix. The Gram equation, applied to the last row and each old column, forces the other off-diagonal block to vanish because the line coefficient is invertible. The complementary matrix is invertible, with inverse the corresponding block of the original inverse. The Gram equation then restricts to that block. To compare the reduced norms, extend the coefficients to a splitting chart and reindex the scalar matrix as the two diagonal blocks. Its determinant is the smaller determinant times the determinant of the identity. This is the matrix comparison for the nonsingular-line stabilizer in the spin induction. [Kneser, *Lectures on Galois cohomology of classical groups*, §§5.9–5.10, pp. 93–95](https://www.math.tifr.res.in/~publ/ln/tifr47.pdf)
