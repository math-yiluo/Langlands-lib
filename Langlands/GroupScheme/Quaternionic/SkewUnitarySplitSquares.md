# Special unitary groups of anisotropic forms over a split real quaternion algebra

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.SkewUnitarySplitSquares`.

## Reflections of an anisotropic quadratic form

Namespace `Langlands.QuadraticForm`. Here `reflection Q z hz` is the reflection
`x ↦ x - (polar Q x z / Q z) • z` (module `LinearAlgebra.QuadraticWittExtension`).

* `polar_isometryEquiv`: isometries preserve the polar form.
* `reflection_reflection`, `reflection_symm_apply`: a reflection is an involution.
* `reflection_congr`: equal vectors give equal reflections.
* `reflection_smul_apply`: `r_{c • z} = r_z` for `c ≠ 0`.
* `reflection_conj`: for an isometry `g`, `g ∘ r_z ∘ g⁻¹ = r_{g z}`.

Assume `K` has characteristic zero, every element of `K` or its negative is a square, sums of two
squares are squares, `-1` is not a square, and `Q` is anisotropic.

* `isSquare_div`: `Q(u)/Q(w)` is a square for `Q(u), Q(w) ≠ 0`. Otherwise `Q(u) = -μ² Q(w)`. The
  binary form `Q(u + s w)` then has the square discriminant `β² + (2 μ Q(w))²`, `β = polar Q u w`,
  so it vanishes at some `s₀`. Anisotropy gives `u = -s₀ w`, hence `(s₀² + μ²) Q(w) = 0`; then
  `-1 = (s₀/μ)²` if `μ ≠ 0`, and `Q(u) = 0` if `μ = 0`.
* `trans_reflection_eq_or`: for `Q(u), Q(w) ≠ 0`, either `r_w ∘ r_u = 1`, or there is `m` with
  `Q(m) ≠ 0` and `r_w ∘ r_u = (r_m ∘ r_u)²`. With `λ² = Q(u)/Q(w)` let `m = u + λ w`. If `m = 0`
  the reflections coincide. Otherwise `polar Q u m = Q(m)`, so `r_m(u) = -λ w`, and by
  `reflection_conj`, `r_m ∘ r_u ∘ r_m = r_w`.

## The split chart

Namespace `Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.SkewUnitary`. For a split quaternion algebra
`e : A ≃ₐ[R] M₂(R)`, `splitChart e : M_I(A) ≃+* M_{I×2}(R)` flattens matrices.

* `splitSkewScalarGram_injective'`, `splitSkewScalarGram_eq_chart_mul`,
  `splitChart_adjoint_mul`: for an involution `τ` with `e(τ a) J = J (e a)ᵀ`, `J = gramTwo`,
  conjugate transposition becomes `X ↦ J' Xᵀ J'⁻¹` with `J'` block diagonal.
* `splitSkewScalarGram_congruence'`: the scalar Gram matrix (`splitSkewScalarGram`) of `P H ᵗP^τ`
  is `ψ(P) C ψ(P)ᵀ`, where `ψ = splitChart e` and `C` is the scalar Gram matrix of `H`.
* `chartR_eq_self`, `matrixReducedNorm_eq_det_splitChart`: over `R ⊗ D` split by `e`, the reduced
  norm of a matrix is the determinant of its chart.

These follow the private constructions of `Quaternionic.QuaternionicProperWittSplit`.

## Matrices of isometries

For a symmetric matrix `C` with quadratic form `Q = C.toQuadraticForm'`,
`isoMatrix g = (toMatrix' g)ᵀ` is the matrix with `v ᵥ* isoMatrix g = g v`.

* `isoMatrix_trans`: `isoMatrix (g.trans h) = isoMatrix g * isoMatrix h`, where `g.trans h`
  applies `g` first.
* `isoMatrix_refl`: the identity isometry has matrix `1`.
* `det_isoMatrix`: its determinant is the determinant of `g`.
* `isoMatrix_gram`: `isoMatrix g * C * (isoMatrix g)ᵀ = C`.
* `exists_isoMatrix_eq`: an invertible `Y` with `Y C Yᵀ = C` is `isoMatrix g` for an isometry `g`.

## Main result

`mem_closure_squares_of_split σ hdim hsym Hs K hK1 hK2 hK3 e hHs hu hanis M`. Let `D` be a central
quaternion division algebra over a characteristic-zero field `F`, with orthogonal involution `σ`
and quaternion conjugation `τ`. Let `Hs` be an invertible matrix of positive size with
`ᵗHs^τ = -Hs`, and `K ⊇ F` a field as above with a splitting `e : K ⊗ D ≃ₐ[K] M₂(K)`. Assume the form
`(τ.baseChange K).matrixSesqBilin (gramOver n Hs K)` is anisotropic. Then every element `M` of
`suGroup τ Hs K` lies in the subgroup generated by squares.

## Proof

1. With `C` the scalar Gram matrix of `H = gramOver n Hs K`, a matrix `X` is special unitary iff
   `ψ(X) C ψ(X)ᵀ = C` and `det ψ(X) = 1`.
2. `Q = C.toQuadraticForm'` is anisotropic. If `Q(v) = 0`, the row `x` over `K ⊗ D` with scalar
   rows `(v, 0)` (`fromSplitSkewRows`) has `e(h(x, x)) J = 0`
   (`matrixSesqBilin_splitSkewScalarGram`), so `x = 0` and `v = 0`.
3. Every proper isometry `g` gives a special unitary matrix with chart `isoMatrix g`.
4. By `exists_even_reflectionWord`, `ψ(M)` is the matrix of an even word of reflections. Each pair
   `r_b ∘ r_a` is `1` or the square of a proper isometry (`trans_reflection_eq_or`). So `ψ(M)` is
   the chart of a product of squares of special unitary matrices, and `ψ` is injective.

## Provenance (derived)

The generation of proper isometries by pairs of reflections is the Cartan–Dieudonné theorem
(`exists_even_reflectionWord`). The bisector identity expresses that a rotation in a definite plane
is the square of the rotation by half the angle. Over `ℝ` the group is a compact special orthogonal
group. The module replaces the topological fact that this group is connected by this algebraic
generation statement.
