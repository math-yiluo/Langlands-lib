# The geometric kernel of a scalar even spin cover

`ScalarEvenSpinKernelDegreeStatement` specifies a regular scalar quadratic form in dimension `2n` for `n ≥ 3` over a characteristic-zero field. A central isogeny from a simply connected source onto the identity component of its smooth isometry group has two kernel points over the algebraic closure.

`natCard_scalarEvenSpinKernel` proves the point count. The algebraic-closure splitting isomorphism of the scalar isometry group identifies its identity component with the split even orthogonal identity component. Simply connectedness and the central isogeny persist under field extension. The full split kernel is the one-factor square-root-of-unity group by the lattice index calculation. The pullback adjunction and kernel base-change isomorphism give the same two points in the original kernel with algebraically closed coefficients.

This calculation supplies the geometric degree of the cover in the scalar spin argument in Kneser, *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups*, §5.11, p. 99.
