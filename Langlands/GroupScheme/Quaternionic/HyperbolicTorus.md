# Actual hyperbolic cocharacter and split-rank lower bound

This internal derived module constructs a nontrivial cocharacter into the actual
isometry group from a hyperbolic pair. It works over every infinite field and
finite-dimensional division algebra with involution of the first kind, in every
characteristic. If the isometry group is smooth, its connected isometry group
has split rank at least one. A positive quadratic Witt index and a nondegenerate
polar form supply the required hyperbolic pair.

The two singular-line matrices are orthogonal idempotents. Applying the explicit
finite-algebra idempotent construction gives an actual morphism from the
multiplicative group into matrix-algebra units. The affine/global-sections
comparison identifies its ground-field coefficient points with hyperbolic
scaling matrices. Their quadratic defects lie in the actual defining kernel.
The tensor-unit isomorphism transfers this membership to the scalar tensor
algebra, and the isometry group's quotient corepresentation supplies the lifts.

Rational-point detection on a split torus over an infinite field factors the
actual morphism through the closed isometry inclusion. Cancellation against
that monomorphism supplies the group law for the factor. A unit different from
one changes the first nonzero singular vector, proving nontriviality. The
connected source then factors through the identity component. If that component
had split rank zero, the published anisotropic split-torus theorem would make
the cocharacter trivial, a contradiction. This establishes the rank lower bound;
the upper bound remains a separate obligation.
