# Proof of the Kneser–Tits property of quaternionic spin groups of Witt index one

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.LocalSpinKneserTitsProof`, namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic`.

Authors: Worker 5398, Worker 14.

## Result

`localSpinKneserTits : LocalSpinKneserTitsStatement.{u}`, at arbitrary universe `u`.

This is the published statement `LocalSpinKneserTitsStatement`, unchanged. Let `K` be a
nonarchimedean local field, `D` a central division `K`-algebra with `dim_K D = 4`, and `σ` an
involution of the first kind with `dim_K D^σ = 3`. Let `m = 4` or `5`, and let
`q : σ.QuadraticForms (Fin m)` be nondegenerate of Witt index `1`. Then every spin cover
`π : S ⟶ σ.isometryGroup q` (`σ.IsSpinCover q π`) has `parabolicElementarySubgroup S = ⊤`:
`S(K)` is generated by the `K`-points of the unipotent radicals of the parabolic
`K`-subgroups of `S`. The characteristic of `K` is unrestricted, and there are no hypotheses
beyond those of the statement.

## Proof

The proof is a single term. It applies `SpinKernel.localSpinKneserTits_of_spinKernel` (module
`SpinKernelAssembly`) to proofs of its three hypotheses. These hypotheses are statements about
a spin cover `π`, a hyperbolic pair `h` and Levi lifts `mu : 𝔾_m ⟶ S`, `psi : SL₁(D) ⟶ S`
(`IsLeviLift h π mu psi`, lifts of the Levi morphism of `h` through `π`).

1. **`SpinLeviLiftStatement`: Levi lifts exist.** This is
   `SpinKernel.spinLeviLift_of_degreeTwoNormOneSimplyConnected` (module `SpinLeviLift`)
   applied to `CentralSimpleUnits.degreeTwoNormOneSimplyConnected` (module
   `DegreeTwoNormOneSimplyConnectedProof`). The latter proves
   `DegreeTwoNormOneSimplyConnectedStatement`: the norm-one group `SL₁(A)` of a central simple
   algebra `A` of dimension 4 is simply connected. The lifts are then obtained by lifting the
   binary action `SL₂ × SL₁(D) ⟶ O(q)` of `h` through the spin cover.
2. **`SpinKernelGeneratorStatement`: the kernel is `μ₂`, generated through the lifts.** For
   all Levi lifts `(mu, psi)`, the map `z(w) = mu(w) psi(w · 1)` is a bijection from `μ₂(A)`
   onto the points of `ker π` over every `K`-algebra `A`. This is
   `SpinKernel.spinKernelGenerator` (module `SpinKernelGeneratorProof`).
3. **`SpinKernelElementaryLiftStatement`: `z(-1)` is elementary.** For all Levi lifts, the
   rational point `z(-1) = mu(-1) psi(-1)` of `S` lies in `parabolicElementarySubgroup S`.
   This is `SpinKernel.spinKernelElementaryLift` (module `KneserThreeSpinSubgroupProof`,
   included in this contribution). That theorem applies
   `SpinKernel.spinKernelElementaryLift_of_kneserThreeSpinSubgroup` (module
   `SpinKernelElementaryReduction`, also included) to
   `CentralSimpleUnits.degreeTwoNormOneSimplyConnected` and to
   `KneserThree.Quaternion.kneserThreeSpinSubgroup` (module `KneserThreeSpinSubgroupProof`).
   - `kneserThreeSpinSubgroup` proves `SpinKernel.KneserThreeSpinSubgroupStatement` (Tits's
     `A₃ = D₃` subgroup). For `(2 : K) ≠ 0` and every hyperbolic pair, it factors the binary
     action as `j ≫ c` through a simply connected group `C`, and it places `j(-1, -1)` in
     `C(K)⁺`. Here `C` is the special unitary group of a Hermitian form in four variables
     (a hyperbolic plane plus an anisotropic diagonal form `⟨-1, δ⟩`) over a quadratic
     subfield `L ⊂ D`, namely `HermitianRoot.SchemeModel.groupScheme` with `Middle = Fin 2`.
     It is simply connected by `HermitianRoot.SchemeModel.isSimplyConnected`, and
     `C(K)⁺ = C(K)` by `HermitianRoot.SchemeModel.parabolicElementarySubgroup_eq_top`.
     The morphism `c` is `QuatData.modelHom` (module `KneserThreeModelHom`) followed by
     `SubspaceScheme.isometryHom` (module `SubspaceIsometryExtension`). The morphism `j` is
     `QuatData.binaryHom` (module `KneserThreeBinary`). The paired Markdown of
     `KneserThreeSpinSubgroupProof` gives the details.
   - `spinKernelElementaryLift_of_kneserThreeSpinSubgroup` handles characteristic 2 directly,
     since there `-1 = 1` and `z(1) = 1`. Otherwise it lifts `c` through `π`. By uniqueness of
     Levi lifts it identifies `(mu, psi)` with the lifts through `j`, and it maps
     `C(K)⁺` into `S(K)⁺` (`parabolicElementarySubgroup_map_le_of_hom`).

Inside `localSpinKneserTits_of_spinKernel`, the three statements give the two spin inputs of
`localSpinKneserTits_of_spinInputs` (module `LocalSpinKneserTitsReduction`):

- Levi elements that lift to `S(K)` have square reduced norm
  (`leviReducedNormSquare_of_kernelGenerator`);
- the rational kernel points of `π` are `1` and `z(-1)`, hence lie in `S(K)⁺`
  (`kernelElementary_of_kernelGenerator`).

The Eichler-transformation argument of `SpinEichlerLift` then generates `S(K)`.

## Provenance

Internal assembly (Task 5398). The route is the one recorded in `SpinKernelAssembly`: Tits's
proof of `W(Spin q, K) = 1` for quaternionic forms of Witt index `≥ 1`
(Tits 1977, Bourbaki exposé 505, 1.1.6), arranged around the Levi lifts of a hyperbolic pair and
the kernel `μ₂` of the spin cover.
