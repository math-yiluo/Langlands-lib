# Hyperbolic pairs in antihermitian division spaces

For a left-separating antihermitian pairing with two invertible, a nonzero
isotropic vector `x` has an isotropic partner `y` with `B(x,y) = 1`. Rescale
a vector that pairs nontrivially with `x`, then subtract half its self-pairing
times `x` to make the rescaled vector isotropic.

When the involution `τ` moves some coefficient `a`, the difference
`t = a - τ(a)` is nonzero and satisfies `τ(t) = -t`. The vectors `x + t y`
and `x - t y` are orthogonal and have opposite nonzero self-pairings.

In rank three, split off these two nondegenerate lines in order. The remaining
orthogonal complement has rank one. The resulting basis has the two opposite
values in its first two positions. This proves `RankThreeIsotropicBasisStatement`.

This division-algebra calculation is the change of coordinates used in Springer,
appendix to M. Kneser, *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups* (1969),
Theorem 5.3.3(i), printed p. 109 (PDF p. 115).
