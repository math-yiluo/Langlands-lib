# Field extension of the unitary Gram equation

`UnitaryGramFieldExtensionStatement` identifies the base change of the unitary group of a Gram matrix with the group defined by the extended Gram matrix and extended involution. The ground coefficient algebra is a finite-dimensional division algebra. The extended isometry cut is defined over the tensor algebra, including when that algebra splits.

On sections of each relative test scheme, associativity moves the new field into the coefficient tensor factor and the matrix coefficient equivalence puts it into the matrix entries. On pure tensors this chart commutes with conjugate transpose and transports the original Gram matrix to its scalar extension. Naturality gives the ambient isomorphism of unit group schemes.

In the ambient isomorphism, the two represented subgroups have the same image on every test scheme because the tensor algebra chart carries the entire Gram equation to the extended one. Equality of those images gives the isomorphism of the isometry groups.

This is the scalar-extension compatibility of the form presentations used in Platonov and Rapinchuk, *Algebraic Groups and Number Theory*, §6.6, printed pp. 343 and 350. The first page discusses skew-hermitian forms and their scalar extensions, and the second introduces their isometry groups in the local classification.
