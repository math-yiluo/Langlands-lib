# Proper Witt transitivity over quaternion division coefficients

`QuaternionicProperWittDivisionStatement` applies to a quaternion division algebra in characteristic zero, an involution of the first kind, and its quotient-valued quadratic form on rows. Two vectors of the same nonzero quadratic value are carried to one another by a rational isometry whose reduced norm is one.

For vectors \(x,y\), take the reflection in \(x-y\) with coefficient \(c=h(x,x-y)\), where \(h\) is the polar pairing. The equality of values makes the class of \(c\) equal to the value of \(x-y\). If \(c\) is nonzero, the reflection takes \(x\) to \(y\). Its reduced norm equals that of \(-c^{-1}\sigma(c)\) by the rank-one update formula, and this coefficient has norm one. If \(c=0\), the same construction takes \(x\) to \(-y\), since the coefficient for \(x+y\) is twice the nonzero self-pairing of \(x\). Compose with the negative identity, whose reduced norm is one in quaternionic degree. The reflection calculation proves the division-algebra case of the proper Witt transitivity used in the spin Hasse argument. [Kneser, *Lectures on Galois cohomology of classical groups*, §2.6 Lemmas 1–2, pp. 31–33, and §5.10, p. 95](https://www.math.tifr.res.in/~publ/ln/tifr47.pdf)
