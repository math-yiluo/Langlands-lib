# Generation of the rational points of quaternionic symplectic groups of Witt index one

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.SymplecticGeneration`.

Setting: `K` a field (any characteristic), `D` a finite-dimensional central division
`K`-algebra, `σ : FirstKindInvolution K D`. `parabolicElementarySubgroup G` is the published
subgroup of `G(K)` generated by the rational points of the geometric unipotent radicals of the
parabolic `K`-subgroups of `G` (module `Rational.ElementarySubgroup`).

## Results

- `neg_pairWeight`: `-pairWeight a b = pairWeight b a`.
- **`FirstKindInvolution.parabolicElementarySubgroup_eq_top_of_le_contracting`** (generation
  criterion). Hypotheses: `a ≠ b`, `H₀` supported where `pairWeight a b` adds to zero,
  `IsConnectedReductive (σ.symplecticGroup H₀)`, and
  `σ.unitaryMatrixGroup H₀ ≤ σ.contractingElementarySubgroup H₀ (pairWeight a b)`. Conclusion:
  `parabolicElementarySubgroup (σ.symplecticGroup H₀) = ⊤`.

  Proof. Each generator of the contracting elementary subgroup is an isometry of contracting
  shape for `pairWeight a b` or for `-pairWeight a b = pairWeight b a`. By
  `exists_rationalMatrix_eq_of_contracting` it is the matrix of a rational point in the dynamic
  unipotent subgroup of a cocharacter of `G`. The published
  `dynamicUnipotent_le_parabolicElementarySubgroup` (needs connected reductivity) places that
  point in `parabolicElementarySubgroup G`. So the contracting elementary subgroup lies in the
  image of `parabolicElementarySubgroup G` under the injective `rationalMatrix`. Every rational
  point has an isometry as matrix (`rationalMatrix_mem`), so injectivity puts every rational
  point in `parabolicElementarySubgroup G`.
- `FirstKindInvolution.parabolicElementarySubgroup_gramTwo_eq_top`: for
  `dim_K D = 4`, `dim_K D^σ = 3` and connected reductive `σ.symplecticGroup (gramTwo D)`. The
  generation hypothesis is `unitaryMatrixGroup_gramTwo_le`, since `weightTwo = pairWeight 0 1`.
- `FirstKindInvolution.parabolicElementarySubgroup_gramThree_eq_top`: the same for
  `gramThree c`, `c ∈ {a - σ a}`, `c ≠ 0`, by `unitaryMatrixGroup_gramThree_le`.
- **`FirstKindInvolution.parabolicElementarySubgroup_eq_top_of_wittIndex_eq_one`**: for
  `dim_K D = 4`, `dim_K D^σ = 3`, `m = 2` or `3`, `H ∈ M_m(D)` antihermitian, invertible, of Witt
  index `1`, with `σ.symplecticGroup H` connected reductive:
  `parabolicElementarySubgroup (σ.symplecticGroup H) = ⊤`. `exists_gramTwo` or `exists_gramThree`
  (`HyperbolicBasis`) gives an invertible `P` with `P H ᵗP^σ` standard. `symplecticGroupCongr`
  identifies the two symplectic groups. The published `parabolicElementarySubgroup_eq_top_iff_iso`
  and `IsConnectedReductive.of_iso` transport the conclusion and the hypothesis.
- **`localSymplecticKneserTits_of_isConnectedReductive hred : LocalSymplecticKneserTitsStatement.{u}`**:
  the published statement follows once connected reductivity of these symplectic groups is
  supplied. The premise `hred` asks for connected reductivity for every field `K` (with no
  local-field instances), every `D`, `σ`, `m ∈ {2, 3}` and `H` satisfying the hypotheses of the
  statement, including Witt index `1`. This is stronger than what is used, because the statement
  only concerns nonarchimedean local fields. It is supplied by the published
  `FirstKindInvolution.isConnectedReductive_symplecticGroup`, which holds over every field. The
  local-field structure of `K` is not used in the argument.

## Provenance

Internal. The argument combines the explicit matrix generation of `StandardGenerationTwo/Three`,
which is specific to quaternion algebras whose involution has a `3`-dimensional symmetric part,
with the published passage from dynamic unipotent subgroups to parabolic radicals
(`dynamicUnipotent_le_parabolicElementarySubgroup`). It realises, for these groups, the
Kneser–Tits property `G(K)^+ = G(K)`, which the task's context attributes to Tits (Séminaire
Bourbaki 505, 1977, 1.1.5). No general theorem about unitary groups is invoked.
