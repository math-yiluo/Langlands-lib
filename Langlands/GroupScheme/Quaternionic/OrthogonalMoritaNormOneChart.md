# The norm-one chart of the orthogonal Morita comparison

Let \(D\) be a finite-dimensional central division algebra over a field \(K\), let \(m>0\), and write \(A=M_m(D)\). Fix an involution of the first kind on \(D\), a quadratic class \(q\) of size \(m\), and a field extension \(L/K\) with a matrix splitting chart for \(D\). Assume the symmetric subspace of the involution has the dimension of the symmetric matrices in that chart. Supply mutually inverse matrices that put the split Gram matrix of \(q\) into the standard hyperbolic form modulo alternating matrices, and a reindexing from the hyperbolic coordinates to the reduced-degree coordinates of \(A\).

`NormOneChartOrthogonalStatement` concerns an affine group scheme \(G\) with a morphism to the isometry group of \(q\) and a morphism to the reduced-norm-one group of \(A\). If their composites to the units of \(A\) agree, their column representations over \(L\), in the supplied chart and reindexing, agree as group-scheme morphisms. The norm-one chart uses exactly the same conjugation as the orthogonal Morita comparison.

`conjugatedChart` first forms the block matrix splitting of \(A\) and then conjugates by the supplied basis. Tensor induction proves that its extension to an arbitrary \(L\)-algebra is the conjugated coefficient chart. The affine norm-one formula follows from `representation_pointMatrix_val` using the affine sections isomorphism with its relative scalar structure. The isometry point formula is `UnitComparison.affineMatrix_splitPoint`. Both formulas apply the same algebra chart to the same unit on every affine test. `AffinePointComparison.hom_ext` then gives the asserted equality.

This is a coordinate comparison derived from the actual orthogonal Morita split chart and the norm-one column chart. It supplies an identification of the representations used to compute the vector Tits algebra in the orthogonal case of J. Tits, *Représentations linéaires irréductibles d'un groupe réductif sur un corps quelconque* (1971), §6.2, printed p. 212.
