# Isometry groups of twisted forms as closed subgroups of an algebra-unit group

Authors: Worker 5197.

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.IsometryCut`, namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.IsometryCut`. This is an internal construction. It
builds the unitary groups and the orthogonal groups of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.GroupSchemes` as actual affine closed subgroup schemes of
the unit group scheme of a finite-dimensional algebra, for every characteristic.

## Setting

`K : Type u` is a field and `A : Type u` is a `K`-algebra, possibly noncommutative.
`τ : FirstKindInvolution K A` is an involution of the first kind. `M : Submodule K A` is a
subspace and `Q : A` is an element. For a commutative `K`-algebra `R`, the scalar extensions
are `R ⊗ M = M.baseChange R` (Mathlib's `Submodule.baseChange`: the image of `R ⊗[K] M`),
`Q_R = 1 ⊗ Q`, and `τ_R = τ.baseChange R`. In the applications `A = M_n(D)` and `τ` is the
`σ`-conjugate transpose. `M = ⊥` gives unitary groups and `M = {x - τ x}` gives isometry
groups of quadratic forms.

## Coordinates modulo a subspace

Assume `[FiniteDimensional K (A ⧸ M)]`.

- `coordinate M j : A →ₗ[K] K` is the `j`-th coordinate of the class in `A ⧸ M` for the
  canonical basis `Module.finBasis K (A ⧸ M)`. It vanishes on `M`
  (`coordinate_eq_zero_of_mem`).
- `coord M R j : R ⊗[K] A →ₗ[R] R` is its `R`-linear extension, with
  `coord (r ⊗ a) = r * coordinate a` (`coord_tmul`). It commutes with every change of
  coefficients `φ : R →ₐ[K] S` (`coord_map`).
- `baseChange_repr_mkQ`: the coordinates of `y` are the coordinates of its image in
  `R ⊗[K] (A ⧸ M)` for the base-changed basis.
- `mem_baseChange_iff`: `y ∈ R ⊗ M` if and only if `coord M R j y = 0` for all `j`. One
  direction checks the generators. For the other, vanishing coordinates mean that `y` is in the
  kernel of `id_R ⊗ (A → A ⧸ M)`. By right exactness of the tensor product (Mathlib's
  `lTensor_mkQ`) this kernel is the image of `R ⊗ M`. No flatness is used.

## The defect and the subgroups of isometries

- `defect τ Q y = y * Q_R * τ_R y - Q_R` for `y ∈ R ⊗[K] A`.
  The lemmas `defect_one`, `defect_mul` (`defect (y z) = y defect(z) τ_R(y) + defect(y)`),
  `defect_units_inv` (`defect(u⁻¹) = -(u⁻¹ defect(u) τ_R(u⁻¹))`) and `defect_sub_defect`
  (the effect of changing `Q`) are ring identities. They use only that `τ_R` reverses
  products and fixes `1`.
- `IsStable τ M` requires that, for every commutative `K`-algebra `R : Type u` and all `y`,
  `z ∈ R ⊗ M` implies `y z τ_R(y) ∈ R ⊗ M`.
  - `isStable_bot`: the zero subspace is stable.
  - `alternatingSubspace_baseChange` identifies `R ⊗ {a - τ a}` with `{z - τ_R z}`, as
    `R`-submodules.
  - `isStable_alternatingSubspace`: `{a - τ a}` is stable, since
    `y (w - τ_R w) τ_R(y) = v - τ_R v` for `v = y w τ_R(y)`. This uses involutivity of `τ`.
- `isometrySubgroup hM Q R : Subgroup (R ⊗[K] A)ˣ`, for `hM : IsStable τ M`, consists of the
  units `u` with `defect τ Q u ∈ R ⊗ M`, that is, `u Q_R τ_R(u) - Q_R ∈ R ⊗ M`
  (`mem_isometrySubgroup`). Closure under products and inverses follows from the identities
  above and stability.
- `defect_mem_iff_of_sub_mem`: if `Q' - Q ∈ M`, the conditions for `Q` and `Q'` are
  equivalent. The defining condition depends only on `Q` modulo `M`.
- `unitOf` regards a value of the Library's unit functor
  `FiniteAlgebraUnits.functor K A` at `R` as a unit of `R ⊗[K] A`.
- `isometryFamily hM Q` is the same family of subgroups, typed as subgroups of the values of
  that functor (`mem_isometryFamily`).

## The closed subgroup scheme

Assume `[FiniteDimensional K A]`. Let `unitsBasis K A = Module.finBasis K A` and let
`B = FiniteAlgebraUnits.candidateRing (unitsBasis K A)` be the coordinate ring of the
Library's unit group scheme `FiniteAlgebraUnits.groupScheme (unitsBasis K A)`, whose points are
`R ↦ (R ⊗[K] A)ˣ`.

- `generator τ M Q j = coord M B j (defect τ Q g) ∈ B`, where `g` is the universal unit
  `FiniteAlgebraUnits.algebraicUniversalUnit`.
- `ideal τ M Q` is the ideal of `B` generated by these elements.
- `isCutOutBy hM Q` proves `Corepresented.IsCutOutBy` for the Library corepresentation
  `FiniteAlgebraUnits.corepresentableBy`, the family `isometryFamily hM Q` and `ideal τ M Q`.
  For a unit `x` over `R`, its classifying map `φ : B → R` sends the universal unit to `x`.
  `φ (generator j)` is then the `j`-th coordinate of `defect τ Q x` (`coord_map`,
  `map_defect`), and `mem_baseChange_iff` concludes.
- `groupScheme hM Q` is the Library construction `Corepresented.cutGroupScheme`, the actual
  affine group scheme `Spec (B ⧸ ideal)` over `Spec K`.
- `inclusion hM Q` is the induced homomorphism into the unit group scheme. It is a closed
  subgroup (`isClosedSubgroup_inclusion`).
- `mem_range_inclusion_iff`: on every test scheme `T` over `Spec K`, a point `x` of the unit
  group lies in the range of `inclusion hM Q` if and only if its unit
  `u = FiniteAlgebraUnits.pointsEquiv _ T x` over the global sections `R = Γ(T)` satisfies
  `u Q_R τ_R(u) - Q_R ∈ R ⊗ M`.

## Provenance

Internal. The construction specializes the Library's closed-subgroup criterion
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Corepresented.IsCutOutBy` / `cutGroupScheme` (module
`Langlands.GroupScheme.CorepresentedClosedSubgroup`) to the unit group scheme of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.FiniteAlgebraUnits`. The identities are elementary ring computations,
and the coordinate criterion is right exactness of the tensor product. No external source is
cited. The mathematical groups obtained are compared with the sources in the paired Markdown of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.GroupSchemes`.
