# Open congruence orbits of invertible pure quaternion values

`QuaternionicPureCongruenceOpenStatement` considers the scalar extension of a central quaternion division algebra to a complete nontrivially normed field of characteristic zero. The tensor algebra carries the module topology. In a neighborhood of an invertible pure value `x`, every pure `y` has the form `u * x * τ(u)` for a unit `u`.

The reduced norm is continuous by its polynomial expression in finite basis coordinates. Near `x`, the ratio `N(y) / N(x)` has a square root `s` tending to `1`, from the local inverse of squaring at `1`. The element `g = 1 + (s⁻¹ • y) * x⁻¹` tends to `2`, hence `N(g)` tends to `4` and stays nonzero. The second ratio `s / N(g)` lies near `1/4`, so it is also a square by local invertibility of squaring at `1/2`. The explicit pure quaternion congruence then applies.

This proves the orbit openness used in Springer's appendix to Kneser's *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups* (1969), Lemma 5.3.4(b), printed p. 111 (PDF p. 117). The proof uses the same coefficient-field argument at archimedean and nonarchimedean completions.
