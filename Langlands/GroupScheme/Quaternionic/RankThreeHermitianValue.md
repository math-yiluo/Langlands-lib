# Orthogonal pairs from a represented Hermitian value

Let `E/F` be a separable quadratic field extension and let `G` be a left-separating Hermitian pairing on a rank-three `E`-vector space with compatible scalar actions. Write `ρ` for the nonzero determinant of its Gram matrix in a chosen basis. For nonzero `d` in `E`, a vector of self-value `-ρd` yields an orthogonal pair `u, v` with `v ≠ 0` and `G(u,u) + d G(v,v) = 0`.

The vector of value `-ρd` spans a nondegenerate line. Extend it by an orthogonal basis of its binary complement with values `a, β`. For the determinant `p` of the change of basis, the Gram calculation gives `p κ(p) = -d a β`, where `κ` is quadratic conjugation. Rescaling the first complement vector by `p/a` makes its value `-dβ` and preserves orthogonality.

This is the algebraic implication from equation (6) to equation (5) in Springer's appendix to Kneser, *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups* (1969), Theorem 5.3.3(i), printed p. 110 (PDF p. 116).
