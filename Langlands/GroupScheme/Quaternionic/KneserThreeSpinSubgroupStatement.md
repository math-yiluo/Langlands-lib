# A simply connected subgroup through the binary quaternionic group (Kneser's `A₃ = D₃`)

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.KneserThreeSpinSubgroupStatement`, namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Quaternionic.SpinKernel`.

The module states one proposition, `KneserThreeSpinSubgroupStatement`, and proves nothing.
The module `SpinKernelElementaryReduction` uses it, together with
`CentralSimpleUnits.DegreeTwoNormOneSimplyConnectedStatement`, to derive
`SpinKernelElementaryLiftStatement` (module `SpinKernelAssembly`).

## Statement

**`KneserThreeSpinSubgroupStatement`** (a `Prop`, universe `u`). For every nonarchimedean local
field `K` (with an `Infinite K` instance argument) with `(2 : K) ≠ 0`, every central division
algebra `D` over `K`
with `dim_K D = 4` (`hD`), every first-kind involution `σ` of `D` with
`dim_K D^σ = 3` (`hsym`), every `m ∈ {4, 5}`, every nondegenerate quadratic form
`q : σ.QuadraticForms (Fin m)` of Witt index `1`, and every hyperbolic pair
`h : σ.IsHyperbolicPair q e f`, there exist

* a group scheme `C` over `Spec K`,
* a homomorphism `c : C ⟶ O(q)` (`σ.isometryGroup q`), and
* a homomorphism `j : SL₂ × SL₁(D) ⟶ C`,

such that

1. `IsSimplyConnected C`;
2. `j ≫ c = AmbientBinaryScheme.isometryHom σ hD hsym h`;
3. the rational point of `C` corresponding (through `rationalCoeffEquiv`) to
   `kernelPoint hdeg (upperCocharacter ≫ firstFactor ≫ j) (secondFactor ≫ j) K negOneRoot`
   lies in `parabolicElementarySubgroup C`.

The point in item 3 is `j(diag(-1, -1), 1) · j(1, -1) = j(-1, -1)`, the image under `j` of the
central `K`-point `(-1, -1)` of `SL₂ × SL₁(D)`. `hdeg` is the reduced degree `2` obtained from
`hD` (`FirstKindInvolution.reducedDegree_eq_two_of_finrank_four`).

The `Infinite K` argument is needed to form `AmbientBinaryScheme.isometryHom`. Every
nonarchimedean local field is infinite
(`Langlands.LocalField.infinite_of_isNonarchimedeanLocalField`), so the argument restricts
nothing.

The hypothesis `(2 : K) ≠ 0` excludes characteristic `2`. In characteristic `2` we have
`-1 = 1`, so `negOneRoot = 1` and the point of item 3 is the identity (`kernelPoint_one`). The
statement is used only where it matters: `SpinKernelElementaryReduction` treats
characteristic `2` directly and applies this statement only when `(2 : K) ≠ 0`.

## Provenance

This is a derived statement. It isolates the step of Kneser's argument, reported by
Tits, that the proof of `SpinKernelElementaryLiftStatement` needs.

* Tits, *Groupes de Whitehead de groupes algébriques simples sur un corps*, Séminaire Bourbaki
  exposé 505 (1977), source `tits-1977-whitehead-bourbaki-505`, n° 1.1.6 (printed pp. 220–221,
  PDF pp. 4–5). For a nondegenerate isotropic quadratic form `q` in the sense of that number
  over a division algebra of degree `d = 2` with an involution of the first kind whose fixed
  space has dimension `d(d + 1)/2 = 3`, and `n ≥ 3`, `W(Spin q, K) = 1`. The proof is by
  induction on the dimension, starting from the isomorphism between `A₃` and `D₃` (an idea of
  M. Kneser).
* The same source, n° 1.1.4 (printed p. 220, PDF p. 4): let `D'` be a division algebra whose
  center is a quadratic extension `L` of `K` and whose degree is square-free, with an involution
  of the second kind inducing the nontrivial automorphism of `L`, and `h` a Hermitian form on
  `D'^m`, `m ≥ 2`. Then `W(SU(h), K) = 1`.
* The same source, n° 1.2 (printed p. 221, PDF p. 5): over a non-discrete locally compact field
  `K`, `W(G, K) = 1` for every quasi-simple, simply connected, isotropic `G`.

The intended witness, which the Lean statement does not mention, is as follows (`char K ≠ 2`).
Let `P` be the hyperbolic plane of `h` and `v` an anisotropic vector orthogonal to `P`. Take
`C = Spin(P ⊕ Dv)`, an isotropic simply connected group of type `D₃ = A₃`; over a local field it
is a special unitary group `SU₄(h')` of a Hermitian form over the discriminant field `L` of
`P ⊕ Dv`, that is, the case `D' = L` (degree `1`) of n° 1.1.4. `j` is the inclusion of
`Spin(P)`, which is `SL₂ × SL₁(D)` (the binary group is simply connected and
`AmbientBinaryScheme.isometryHom` has kernel the diagonal `μ₂`). `c` is the covering map onto
`SO(P ⊕ Dv)` followed by the extension by the identity on the orthogonal complement. As
`char K ≠ 2`, the point `j(-1, -1)` is the nontrivial element of the kernel of `C ⟶ O(q)`.
Item 3 follows from `C(K) = C(K)⁺`, which holds by n° 1.1.4 (with `D' = L`) and, over local
fields, by n° 1.2.
