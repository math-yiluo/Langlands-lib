# Finite generating subfamilies of smooth connected subgroups

Worker 1342.

This is an internal derived interface for the finiteness step in the
reductive rational-point density project. Its ambient group `G` is smooth
and affine over an arbitrary field `k : Type u`. The family index type
`I : Type v` has an independent universe. The maps `a j : A j ⟶ G` are
actual group-scheme homomorphisms; they need not be closed embeddings.

`IsGeneratedBySubfamily A a s i` states that the actual closed inclusion
`i : H ⟶ G` contains the finite subfamily indexed by `s`, and that its
all-test subgroup functor lies in every competing closed subgroup containing
that subfamily. Containment uses the existing `FactorsThrough` on morphisms
of schemes over the field. Competitors need not be smooth or reduced. The
predicate itself imposes no smoothness or connectedness on `H`.

`FiniteGeneration.exists_generated_subfamily` explicitly assumes
`PointedImageGenerationStatement.{u}` and smoothness, affineness and
connectedness of every `A j`. It constructs a smooth connected affine
generated representative for each finite subfamily. The empty family's
representative is the identity subgroup, with source the trivial group over
`Spec k`. The identity section is closed because the ambient group is
affine, and its all-test range is the bottom subgroup. For the induction
step, if `i : H ⟶ G` represents the previous family, apply the assumed
pointed-image generation statement to

`H × A t → G`, `(h, x) ↦ i(h) * a t(x)`.

The published theorem `smoothConnectedAffineGeometry` supplies affineness,
local finite type and geometric integrality of this actual scheme product.
The pair of identity points maps to the identity. The generated image is
smooth, affine and geometrically connected; geometric connectedness over
the one-point field spectrum gives connectedness. Inserting the identity
in either coordinate factors both `i` and `a t` through this generated
image. Conversely, a closed competitor containing the enlarged family
contains `H` by the previous representative's minimality. The existing
represented-subgroup factorization API constructs the homomorphism from
`H` to that competitor. Multiplying its composite with the first
projection and the given factor of `a t` through the second projection
factors the product map. `GeneratedImage.range_le` then proves the required
all-test minimality. The dominance clause in the assumed pointed-image
statement is not used.

`FiniteGeneration.exists_finite_generating_subfamily` assumes instead that
every finite subfamily already has a smooth connected affine generated
representative. It also assumes that every closed subgroup containing the
whole family has top range. It chooses a finite family's representative
of largest Lie dimension: closed immersion gives an injective tangent map
into `G`, so these natural-number dimensions form a nonempty bounded set.
For any further member of the family, the representative generated after
adjoining it contains the selected representative. The represented-subgroup
factorization API gives the homomorphism between them, which is closed by
`IsClosedSubgroup.of_comp`. A proper closed embedding into a smooth
connected affine group strictly decreases Lie dimension by
`Lie.dimension_lt_of_proper_closedSubgroup`. Maximality therefore makes
that homomorphism an isomorphism. Every family member already factors
through the selected representative, so the all-family generation
hypothesis identifies its range with top. Minimality then gives generation
by the selected finite subfamily. These equalities hold on every test
scheme, including nonreduced tests.

`FiniteGeneration.exists_finite_generating_subfamily_of_pointedImageGeneration`
combines the two results. Both pointed-image generation and all-family
generation remain explicit premises. The module proves neither of those
premises, generation by Cartan subgroups, identification of Cartan subgroups
with tori, `ReductiveFiniteTorusGenerationStatement`, nor rational-point
density. It supplies the generic finiteness step needed by such arguments.

The predicate and these conditional API lemmas are derived content, not a
verbatim statement from the literature. The geometric motivation for finite
generated representatives is Milne, *Algebraic Groups* (2017), Propositions
2.50 and 2.53, printed pp.55–56 (PDF pp.71–72), registered as
`milne-2017-algebraic-groups`. Those propositions assert smoothness of the
subgroup generated by a geometrically reduced source and connectedness for
a geometrically connected pointed source. Their conclusions motivate the
retained premise; this module does not prove those source propositions.
The implemented route uses binary products and the existing pointed-image
interface, rather than reconstructing Milne's scheme-theoretic closure.
The complete registered errata snapshot
`milne-2017-algebraic-groups-errata-2026-11-02` was read during this task;
it has no correction to Propositions 2.50 or 2.53.

All fields and characteristics are retained. No perfection, infinitude,
splitness, or positive dimension is used in this finiteness argument.
