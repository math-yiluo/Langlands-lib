# The affine blow-up algebra on flat tests

Let \(A\) be a commutative \(R\)-algebra, \(I\) an ideal of \(A\), and \(r\in R\).
`blowupAlgebra r I` is `Dilatation.coordinateAlgebra` with denominator the
image of \(r\) in \(A\). Its structural map `blowupMap` is the canonical
\(R\)-algebra map from \(A\). The construction allows powers of a uniformizer.

For a domain \(R\), a nonzero \(r\), and a flat \(R\)-algebra \(B\),
`existsUnique_liftBlowup_iff` identifies the maps from the blow-up algebra
extending an \(R\)-algebra map \(f:A\to B\) by the condition
\(f(I)\subseteq rB\). Localization at \(r\) is injective on \(B\) since flatness
allows cancellation of all powers of \(r\). The localized map sends each
generator \(a/r\) to the image of an element of \(B\) precisely when \(f(a)\)
is divisible by \(r\) in \(B\). The lift restricts the localized map to the
subalgebra generated by these elements and \(A\). Uniqueness clears a power
of \(r\) in each element of the blow-up algebra and cancels it in \(B\).

The blow-up algebra of a flat \(A\) is torsion-free as an \(R\)-module, as it
embeds in a localization of \(A\). Over a Dedekind domain this gives flatness.
For finite generation over \(A\), the division map \(a\mapsto a/r\) is
\(A\)-linear in the localization, so the divided images of any finite ideal
generating set generate the blow-up algebra. In particular, if \(A\) is of
finite type over a Noetherian \(R\), then \(I\) is finitely generated and the
blow-up algebra is also of finite type over \(R\).

Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023), Construction
A.5.2, Proposition A.5.3 and Remark A.5.14, printed pp. 632–633 and 637
(PDF pp. 663–664 and 668). The source works over a Henselian DVR. The
affine algebra proofs apply with the ring hypotheses listed above. At the
source's special-fibre centre, \(r\) is a uniformizer and \(I\) contains its

is the \(n\)th power of a uniformizer.
