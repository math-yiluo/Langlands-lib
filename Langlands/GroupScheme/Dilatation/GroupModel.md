# Dilatations of affine integral group models

For a DVR \(R\) with fraction field \(K\), let `M : IntegralModel R K G`
be an actual affine flat finite-type group model of the supplied generic group
\(G\). Let \(\varpi\) be a uniformizer, supplied as an irreducible element of
\(R\), and let `j : Y ⟶ M.specialFibre` be a group homomorphism whose
underlying scheme morphism is a closed immersion.

`M.dilatation` is the integral group model whose underlying affine scheme
is the spectrum of the blow-up algebra. `M.dilatationHom` is the model
homomorphism to `M` inducing the identity on \(G\). Its generic
identification is the generic pullback of the structural homomorphism,
which is an isomorphism, followed by `M.genericIso`. The more general
`dilatationAtQuotient` uses the same construction for a closed subgroup over
\(R/J\) when \(J\) is generated by the supplied nonzero scalar.

The quotient reduction of the structural map factors through the centre.
On flat affine tests, this condition is preserved by the unit, inverse, and
product operations of the original group, since the centre is a subgroup.
The scheme, its products over the base, and the base itself are flat affine
tests. The unique factorization property therefore lifts the three
operations. Cancellation into the original model verifies the group axioms
and the compatibility of the structural map. The same argument lifts group
homomorphisms. `existsUnique_dilatationGroupIso` gives uniqueness of the
representative among flat affine group schemes with the stated test property
and injective postcomposition on their endomorphisms. Compatible ambient and
centre maps induce `dilatationMap`. Their squares, identity and composition
laws follow from the uniqueness of the lift. At the level of integral
models `Hom.dilatationAtQuotientMap` commutes with the supplied generic maps.

The flat affine lifting criterion uses the actual quotient pullback. On the
base test, the canonical comparison of the pulled-back terminal object with
the terminal object identifies the pullback of a section with
`baseChangeSectionHom`. At the residue base this is `M.reduction`.
`range_dilatationIntegralSections` gives the literal equality in
`M.integralSections` of the image of the constructed section map and
`(rationalPoints.map j).range.comap M.reduction`.
`integralPoints_dilatation` gives its image in `rationalPoints G` using the
original `M.integralPointMap` and the generic compatibility square.

## Source

Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach* (2023), Proposition
A.5.3, Fact A.5.5 and Lemmas A.5.6–7, printed pp. 633–634 (PDF pp. 664–665).
The source assumes a Henselian DVR. These constructions use the DVR

in Remark A.5.14, printed p. 637 (PDF p. 668).
