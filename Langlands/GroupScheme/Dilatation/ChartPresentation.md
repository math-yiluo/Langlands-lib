# Polynomial presentation of a regular dilatation chart

Let \(A\) be a flat algebra over a discrete valuation ring with uniformizer \(t\). Let \(I\) be generated by \(t,x_1,\ldots,x_n\), and assume the images of the \(x_i\) form a weakly regular sequence in \(A/(t)\).

In the polynomial ring over \(A/(t)\), the reductions of the equations \(x_i-tT_i\) are the constant polynomials \(x_i\). Flatness of the polynomial algebra preserves the regularity of the sequence. `chartPresentation_regular` then applies cancellation for lists of equations to show that \(t\) acts injectively on the presentation \(A[T_1,\ldots,T_n]/(x_i-tT_i)\). Over a discrete valuation ring this implies flatness, since every nonzero scalar is associated to a power of \(t\).

`chartEquivBlowup` identifies the presentation with the constructed subalgebra \(A[I/t]\) of \(A[1/t]\). The map to the subalgebra sends \(T_i\) to the divided generator \(x_i/t\). The map back uses the universal property on flat algebras, since each generator of \(I\) is divisible by \(t\) in the presentation. Uniqueness proves one inverse identity. The defining equations and cancellation of \(t\) prove the other on the variables.

`blowupResidueEquivPolynomial` reduces the chart modulo \(t\). The equations become \(x_i=0\), so the result is the polynomial algebra \((A/I)[T_1,\ldots,T_n]\), with the scalar map of `blowupResidueMap`. This is the affine coordinate calculation for the chart in Kaletha–Prasad, *Bruhat–Tits Theory: A New Approach*, Appendix A.5.9–10, printed p. 635.
