# Quotients of affine groups by primitive functions

Let `A` be a commutative Hopf algebra over a commutative ring `R`, and let `f ∈ A` be a
primitive element: `ε(f) = 0` and `Δ(f) = f ⊗ 1 + 1 ⊗ f` (Mathlib's
`Bialgebra.IsPrimitiveElem R f`). This module builds the following for the affine group
scheme `Spec A = (hopfSpec R).obj (op A)` over `Spec R`:

- the closed normal subgroup `Spec (A ⧸ (f))`, the spectrum of the Hopf quotient;
- the homomorphism `Spec A ⟶ 𝔾ₐ` given by the function `f`;
- the fact that the kernel of this homomorphism is `Spec (A ⧸ (f))` on points of every test
  scheme.

Over a field `k`, assume also a `k`-algebra map `σ : A →ₐ[k] k[X]` with `σ f = X`, i.e. a
section of the homomorphism to `𝔾ₐ`. The module then proves the *split step*
`splitStep`. It says that `Spec A ≅ Spec (A ⧸ (f)) × 𝔾ₐ`, that the homomorphism is an fppf
quotient, and that smoothness and connectedness pass from `Spec A` to the subgroup. So a
split series of length `n` for the subgroup gives one of length `n + 1` for `Spec A`.

Everything is in namespace `Langlands.GroupScheme.HopfPrimitive`. The vector group
`𝔾ₐ = VectorGroup.groupScheme R 1`, its coordinates `VectorGroup.coordinates`, and the
predicates `RelativeRoot.IsFppfQuotient`, `RelativeRoot.IsVectorGroupOfDimension` and
`RelativeRoot.HasSplitSeries` come from the Library. A *point* of a scheme `X` over `Spec R`
means a morphism `T ⟶ X` from a test scheme `T : Over (Spec R)`. For a point `x` of
`Spec A`, `x.left.appTop ((Scheme.ΓSpecIso (CommRingCat.of A)).inv f)` is the pullback of
the function `f` to `T`; below this is called "`f` at `x`".

## Provenance

This is internal infrastructure for the proof of
`RelativeRoot.TorusActionSplitUnipotenceStatement`. The mathematics is standard: the kernel
of the homomorphism to `𝔾ₐ` defined by a primitive function, and the splitting of a group
extension that has a scheme-theoretic section. No single source contains these statements
in this form, and none is cited.

## The Hopf quotient and the subgroup

- `isHopfIdeal_span hf : (Ideal.span {f}).IsHopfIdeal R` (for a Hopf algebra `A` over a
  commutative ring `R`, as types). The ideal generated by a primitive element is a Hopf
  ideal. The counit kills `a f` since `ε(f) = 0`. Since `Δ(a f) = Δ(a)(f ⊗ 1 + 1 ⊗ f)`, the
  comultiplication of `a f` maps to zero in `A ⧸ (f) ⊗ A ⧸ (f)`. The antipode sends `a f`
  to `S(a) · (-f)`, which lies in `(f)`.
- `quotient A hf : CommHopfAlgCat R` is the quotient Hopf algebra `A ⧸ Ideal.span {f}`
  (Mathlib's Hopf algebra structure on a quotient by a Hopf ideal). Its carrier is
  `A ⧸ Ideal.span {f}` definitionally (`quotient_carrier`, proved by `rfl`).
- `quotientHom A hf : A ⟶ quotient A hf` is the quotient map as a Hopf algebra morphism.
  `quotientHom_apply` says it sends `a` to `Ideal.Quotient.mk _ a`.
- `subgroupHom A hf : (hopfSpec R).obj (op (quotient A hf)) ⟶ (hopfSpec R).obj (op A)` is
  the homomorphism of group schemes `Spec (A ⧸ (f)) ⟶ Spec A` induced by the quotient map.
  `subgroupHom_left` says its underlying scheme morphism is
  `Spec.map (CommRingCat.ofHom (Ideal.Quotient.mk (Ideal.span {f})))`.
- `isAffineHom_hopfSpec B`: for every commutative Hopf algebra `B` over `R`, the structure
  morphism of `Spec B` to `Spec R` is affine.
- `appTop_specMap_inv φ r`: for a ring map `φ : R ⟶ S`, pulling back the global function
  of `Spec R` corresponding to `r` along `Spec.map φ` gives the global function
  corresponding to `φ r`.

## The coordinate homomorphism to `𝔾ₐ`

- `cogroup A` is the cogroup object of `CommAlgCat R` attached to `A`. It comes from
  Mathlib's equivalence between commutative Hopf algebras and cogroup objects.
- `mul_unop_hom_apply A hf g h`: for two points `g, h` of this cogroup with values in an
  algebra, the algebra map underlying the product `g * h` sends `f` to `g(f) + h(f)`. This
  is where primitivity of `f` is used.
- `pointsHom A hf` is the natural group homomorphism `g ↦ g(f)` from the points of
  `cogroup A` to the points of `𝔾ₐ` on algebras.
- `cogroupHom A hf` is the cogroup morphism with these points, obtained by full
  faithfulness of `yonedaGrp`. `cogroupHom_hom` says that its underlying algebra map is
  `aeval (fun _ => f) : R[X₀] → A`, i.e. `X₀ ↦ f`.
- `coordinateHom A hf : (hopfSpec R).obj (op A) ⟶ VectorGroup.groupScheme R 1` is the
  group scheme homomorphism `Spec A ⟶ 𝔾ₐ` obtained by applying `(algSpec R).mapGrp` to
  `cogroupHom A hf`. `coordinateHom_left` says its underlying scheme morphism is
  `Spec.map` of `aeval (fun _ => f)`.
- `coordinates_coordinateHom`: the coordinate of the image in `𝔾ₐ` of a point `x` of
  `Spec A` is `f` at `x`.
- `mem_range_kernelι_coordinateHom_iff`: a point `x` of `Spec A` lies in the kernel of
  `coordinateHom A hf` (in `SubgroupFunctor.range (kernelι _)`) iff `f` at `x` is zero.

## The kernel

- `exists_comp_algSpec_map_mk_eq g x h`: for an `R`-algebra `B`, `g ∈ B` and a point `x`
  of `Spec B` at which `g` vanishes, `x` factors through `Spec (B ⧸ (g))`. The adjoint
  algebra map `B → Γ(T)` kills `(g)` and so descends to the quotient.
- `mem_range_subgroupHom_iff`: a point `x` of `Spec A` lies in the image of `subgroupHom`
  iff `f` at `x` is zero.
- `range_kernelι_coordinateHom`: on every test scheme, the kernel of `coordinateHom A hf`
  equals the image of `subgroupHom A hf`, as subgroup functors.
- `isNormal_range_kernelι q`: for every homomorphism `q : G ⟶ H` of group schemes, the
  kernel subgroup functor is normal.
- `isNormal_range_subgroupHom`: the image of `subgroupHom A hf` is a normal subgroup
  functor. This follows from the two previous items.
- `isClosedSubgroup_subgroupHom`: `subgroupHom A hf` is a closed subgroup. Its underlying
  morphism is `Spec` of a surjective ring map, hence a closed immersion.

## Splitting off a section

`exists_iso_tensor_of_section j q hjq s hs hlift` holds in any cartesian monoidal category.
Let `H, V` be group objects, `q : H ⟶ V` a homomorphism and `j : K ⟶ H` a monomorphism
with `j ≫ q = 1`. Assume that every `x : H ⟶ H` with `x ≫ q = 1` factors through `j`
(`hlift`). Let `s : V ⟶ H` be a section of `q`, i.e. `s ≫ q = 𝟙 V`; it need not preserve
the unit. Then there is an isomorphism `Θ : K ⊗ V ≅ H` with `Θ.hom ≫ q = snd K V` and
`j ≫ Θ.inv ≫ fst K V = 𝟙 K`. In particular `K` is a retract of `H`.

Proof: replace `s` by the normalized section `s' = s · s(1)⁻¹`. It is still a section of
`q` and sends the unit to the unit. Let `r : H ⟶ K` be the lift through `j` of
`h ↦ h · s'(q h)⁻¹`. Then `Θ.hom = (x, t) ↦ j x · s'(t)` and `Θ.inv = lift r q`, and the
identities are checked by computing in the hom-groups.

The hypotheses hold for `Spec A`:

- `subgroupHom_comp_coordinateHom`: `subgroupHom A hf` followed by `coordinateHom A hf` is
  the trivial homomorphism `1`.
- `exists_comp_subgroupHom_eq`: every point `x` of `Spec A` with
  `x ≫ coordinateHom A hf = 1` factors through `subgroupHom A hf`.
- `mono_subgroupHom` (instance): `subgroupHom A hf` is a monomorphism in `Over (Spec R)`,
  because its underlying morphism is a closed immersion.
- `algSpec_map_comp_map_eq_id a b h`: if `b ≫ a = 𝟙` for algebra maps `a, b`, then
  `Spec a ≫ Spec b = 𝟙`.

## The split step over a field

From here on `k` is a field, `A : CommHopfAlgCat (CommRingCat.of k)`, `f` is primitive and
`σ : A →ₐ[k] Polynomial k`.

- `sectionHom A σ : 𝔾ₐ ⟶ Spec A` is the morphism of schemes over `Spec k` dual to the
  algebra map `A → k[X₀]`, `a ↦ σ(a)(X₀)`. Here `σ` is composed with the identification
  of `Polynomial k` with `MvPolynomial (Fin 1) k`.
- `sectionHom_comp_coordinateHom hf hσ`: if `σ f = X`, then
  `sectionHom A σ ≫ coordinateHom A hf = 𝟙`.
- `exists_iso_tensor A hf σ hσ`: if `σ f = X`, there is an isomorphism
  `Θ : Spec (A ⧸ (f)) ⊗ 𝔾ₐ ≅ Spec A` over `Spec k` with `Θ.hom ≫ coordinateHom = snd`.
  Also `subgroupHom ≫ Θ.inv ≫ fst = 𝟙`, so `Spec (A ⧸ (f))` is a retract of `Spec A`.
  This applies `exists_iso_tensor_of_section` with the facts above.
- `smooth_subgroup A hf σ hσ hSmooth`: if `Spec A` is smooth over `k`, so is
  `Spec (A ⧸ (f))`. The retraction makes `A ⧸ (f)` formally smooth
  (`DynamicSmooth.formallySmooth_of_algSpec_retract`). A formally smooth quotient of a
  smooth algebra over a noetherian ring is smooth (`DynamicSmooth.smooth_quotient`).
- `connectedSpace_subgroup A hf σ hσ hConn`: if the underlying space of `Spec A` is
  connected, so is that of `Spec (A ⧸ (f))`. The retraction `Θ.inv ≫ fst` is surjective
  on underlying spaces, and a continuous image of a connected space is connected.
- `isFppfQuotient_coordinateHom A hf σ hσ hSmooth`: if `σ f = X` and `Spec (A ⧸ (f))` is
  smooth over `k`, then `coordinateHom A hf` is an fppf quotient by the image of
  `subgroupHom A hf` (`RelativeRoot.IsFppfQuotient`). The underlying morphism is surjective,
  because it has the section `sectionHom`. It is flat and locally of finite presentation,
  because it equals `Θ.inv` followed by the projection
  `Spec (A ⧸ (f)) ×_k 𝔾ₐ ⟶ 𝔾ₐ`. That projection is a base change of the smooth morphism
  `Spec (A ⧸ (f)) ⟶ Spec k`. The kernel is `range_kernelι_coordinateHom`.
- `isVectorGroupOfDimension_groupScheme_one`: `𝔾ₐ = VectorGroup.groupScheme k 1` is a
  vector group of dimension one, via its coordinates and their naturality.
- `splitStep A hf σ hσ hSmooth hConn`: assume `σ f = X` and that `Spec A` is smooth over `k`
  with connected underlying space. Put `K = (hopfSpec _).obj (op (quotient A hf))`. Then
  `K.X.hom` is smooth, `K.X.left` is connected, `K.X.hom` is affine, and for every `n`,
  `HasSplitSeries n K → HasSplitSeries (n + 1) ((hopfSpec _).obj (op A))`. The series for
  `Spec A` uses `K` with `subgroupHom A hf`, and the quotient `𝔾ₐ` with
  `coordinateHom A hf`.
