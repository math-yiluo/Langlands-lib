# Openness in a closed identity-jet stabilizer

A smooth closed subgroup is open in its order-indexed jet stabilizer when
that stabilizer has an actual closed representative and the canonical
factorization through it is open. An isomorphism of two representatives
preserves the factorization, so the definition is independent of choices.
A point of the ambient finite-flat jet parameter supplies the actual closed
stabilizer for every smooth closed subgroup jet.

When the ambient group is separated and locally of finite type, the base
locus for the factorization to be open is the unit étale locus of this group
monomorphism. This description classifies arbitrary base changes. An open
smooth geometrically connected subgroup is the relative identity component.
On each residue fibre the smooth open subgroup gives ambient smoothness,
and the image of the subgroup then equals the identity component. This is
the open-subgroup comparison used in SGA 3, Exposé XV, §5.3 and Lemma 5.5.
