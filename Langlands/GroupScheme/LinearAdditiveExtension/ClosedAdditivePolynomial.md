# The additive polynomial of a closed subgroup of the additive group

Module `Langlands.GroupScheme.LinearAdditiveExtension.ClosedAdditivePolynomial`, namespace
`Langlands.GroupScheme.ClosedAdditivePolynomial`.

Let `k` be a field, `𝔾ₐ = VectorGroup.groupScheme (CommRingCat.of k) 1`, and let
`a : K ⟶ 𝔾ₐ` be a homomorphism of group schemes over `Spec k`. The subgroup may be finite or
nonreduced. This module attaches to `a` a polynomial `f` that cuts out the image of `a`. It then
proves the algebraic facts about `f` that the splitting proof uses. This is Milne, *Algebraic
Groups* (2022), Exercise 14-3 (printed p. 301, zero-based PDF index 311): every algebraic subgroup of `𝔾ₐ` is the kernel of an
endomorphism `x ↦ f(x)` with `f` additive. That exercise is used in the proof of Theorem
15.34(d). The Lean development is internal. It generalizes the published
`RelativeRoot.AdditiveGroupQuotient` kernel polynomial from scheme kernels to arbitrary closed
subgroups, and reuses its coordinate API and the algebra of `RelativeRoot.AdditivePolynomial`.

## The polynomial and the membership criterion

- `ga` abbreviates the coordinates `VectorGroup.coordinates (CommRingCat.of k) 1`, and `ga_comp`
  is their naturality.
- `closedIdeal a` is the preimage, under the published coordinate map
  `AdditiveGroupQuotient.coordRingHom : k[X] → Γ(𝔾ₐ)`, of the kernel of the ring map on global
  sections induced by `a`.
- `closedPolynomial a` is the principal generator of this ideal, divided by its leading
  coefficient. `closedIdeal_eq_span` says it generates, and `closedPolynomial_monic` says it
  is monic when it is nonzero.
- `ker_appTop_eq`: the kernel of `a` on global sections is generated by `f`.
- `mem_range_iff_eval`: assume `IsClosedSubgroup a`, so the underlying morphism is a closed
  immersion. On every test scheme `T`, a point `x` of `𝔾ₐ` lies in the published all-test
  range `SubgroupFunctor.range a` exactly when `f(coord x) = 0` in `Γ(T)`. The coefficients
  map through `AdditiveGroupQuotient.structureMap T`. The forward direction pulls back the
  vanishing function. The converse turns the inclusion of ideals into an inclusion of ideal
  sheaves, through Mathlib's `Scheme.ker_of_isAffine` and `IdealSheafData.equivOfIsAffine`,
  and factors `x` through `IsClosedImmersion.lift`.

## Affine tests

`affTest k A` is `Spec A` over `Spec k`. `gammaHom_structureMap`, `gammaHom_injective`,
`gammaHom_coord` and `gammaHom_eval₂` compare the global-function form with the coefficient
algebra `A`. `mem_range_iff_eval_affine` is the membership criterion in the form
`f.eval₂ (algebraMap k A) (coord x) = 0`. `eval_add_eq_zero` says the roots of `f` in `A` are
closed under addition, because the range is a subgroup.

## Additivity

- `taylor_mem_span`: `f(X + Y) ∈ (f(X), f(Y))` in `k[X][Y]`. Over the quotient algebra
  `k[X][Y] / (f(X), f(Y))`, the classes of `Y` and `X` are roots of `f`, so their sum is a root.
  `algebraMap_poly_poly` identifies the scalar map `k → k[X][Y]` with `C ∘ C`.
- `eval_zero`: `f(0) = 0`, since the identity point lies in the range.
- `natDegree_pos`: a nonzero `f` has positive degree. A monic polynomial of degree zero is `1`,
  and `1` does not vanish at `0`.
- `eval₂_add`: a nonzero `f` is additive in every commutative ring with a ring map from `k`.
  This applies the published `AdditivePolynomial.taylor_eq_of_mem_span` and
  `AdditivePolynomial.eval₂_add_of_taylor_eq`.

## Scaling by a character

Let `S` be a commutative group scheme over `Spec k`, affine over the base, with a character
`χ`. Assume weight stability `hst`: on every test scheme, the published linear action
`VectorGroup.weightAction χ` preserves the range of `a`. Let `N` be the degree of `f ≠ 0`.

- `comp_C_mul_X_eq`: over the coordinate algebra `R` of `S`, let `c₀` be the value of `χ` at the
  universal point. Then `F(c₀ X) = c₀^N F(X)`, where `F` is `f` with coefficients in `R`. Over
  `R[X]/(F)`, the class of `X` is a root, so by stability `c₀ X` is a root. Hence `F(c₀ X)` lies
  in `(F)`. The difference `F(c₀ X) - c₀^N F` has degree below `N` (Mathlib's
  `Polynomial.comp_C_mul_X_coeff` computes its coefficients), and the remainder by the monic
  `F` kills it. `R` is nontrivial because it has a `k`-point, the identity.
- `eval₂_charCoeff_mul` (affine tests) and `eval₂_charValue_mul` (all test schemes):
  `f(χ(d) y) = χ(d)^N f(y)` for every point `d` of `S` and every `y`. They specialize the
  universal identity along the coordinate map of `d`. On a general test scheme this map is the
  composite of `Scheme.ΓSpecIso` with the pullback along `d` followed by the inverse of the
  published coordinate presentation `PointedCoordinateWeights.coordinateIso`.
- `algebraMap_polynomial`, `eval₂_algebraMap_eq_comp` and `pmap_universalPoint` are the helper
  identities used here.
