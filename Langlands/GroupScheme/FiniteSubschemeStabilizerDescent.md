# Conjugation stabilizers of finite locally free subschemes over any base

The actual conjugation stabilizer of a finite, flat, locally finitely presented
closed subscheme of a group scheme is represented by a closed subgroup scheme.
A point belongs to the stabilizer when conjugation and inverse conjugation
preserve factorization through the subscheme on every refinement of that test.

This extends the affine-base construction by descent. For each refinement of
a test, pull back the open cover of the test. The conjugates factor through the
subscheme on this cover. Their lifts agree on the overlaps by the monomorphism
property of the closed immersion, and hence glue. This gives Zariski locality.
The stabilizer commutes with pullback of the subscheme, so the affine-base
representatives on the affine cover of the base give a closed representative
of the original stabilizer by closed Zariski descent.

This is a derived construction for the normalizer of the prescribed identity
neighborhood in Raynaud, SGA 3, Exposé XV, Lemma 5.3, proof, recomposed p. 266.
