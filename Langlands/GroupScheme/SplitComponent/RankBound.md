# A uniform bound on the rank of image lattices

Author: Worker 1178.

This internal module proves `exists_rank_bound`: if `F'` is corepresented by
an algebra `B'` of finite type over a field `k`, there is `N` with
`finrank ℤ (imageSubgroup d α) ≤ N` for every homomorphism `α` from a lattice
torus of a free `ℤ`-module of finite rank.

`supportedSubalgebra P` is the subalgebra of `k[Λ]` of elements supported on
an additive submonoid `P` (closed under products by
`AddMonoidAlgebra.support_coeff_mul_subset`).

`card_support_le`: if `Δ' h = ∑_{(a,b) ∈ S} a ⊗ b`, then the support of `ψ h`
has at most `|S|` elements. By the coefficient identity, every `[m]` with `m`
in the support lies in the span of the `ψ a`, and the `[m]` are linearly
independent (the standard basis of `k[Λ]`).

Proof of the bound: choose generators `s` of `B'` and, for each `h`, a
decomposition `S h` of `Δ' h` (`TensorProduct.exists_finset`); put
`N = ∑_{h ∈ s} |S h|`. For any `α`, let `F` be the union of the supports of
the `ψ h`, `h ∈ s`, so `|F| ≤ N`. The range of `ψ` is generated by the
`ψ h`, so it lies in the subalgebra supported on the span `P` of `F`
(`Algebra.adjoin_le_iff`). Hence the image lattice lies in `P`, and its
rank is at most `finrank ℤ P ≤ |F| ≤ N`.

## Provenance

Internal. It replaces the dimension bound for split tori in an algebraic
group by an elementary counting argument with the comultiplication; no
dimension theory is used.
