# The binary upper-triangular subgroup and its character

This derived construction supplies the actual closed subgroup scheme of binary
special linear matrices whose below-diagonal entry is zero, over any commutative
base ring. The subgroup is represented by the quotient of the determinant-one
coordinate ring by that entry. The published ideal-cut subgroup construction
proves it is a closed subgroup, with exactly the prescribed matrices on every
relative test scheme. No reducedness or affineness assumption is imposed on
the test scheme.

The determinant equation makes the last diagonal entry a unit, with inverse the
first diagonal entry. Multiplication of upper-triangular matrices multiplies
these units. Their naturality on all test schemes constructs an actual rational
character to the multiplicative group by Yoneda. The evaluation theorem
identifies its value with the last diagonal entry on every test scheme.

Over a field, the chosen affine coordinate of this character therefore has
exactly the evaluation formula required by the homogeneous-row covariance
lemma. The full binary homogeneous polynomial space of every degree maps
injectively into the covariant coordinate subspace for the corresponding power
of this character coordinate.

The provenance is elementary: the entry ideal and determinant equation give the
subgroup and units; the existing corepresentation and pointwise Yoneda adapters
give actual group-scheme maps; and the existing homogeneous-row embedding and
all-test covariance theorem give the final map. This construction does not
identify the entire covariant space, prove that this subgroup is a Borel, or
prove the general Steinberg big-cell proposition.
