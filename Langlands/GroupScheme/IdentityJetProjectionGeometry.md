# Geometry of the actual identity-jet projection

The actual order-`n` identity jet of a smooth separated group is finite,
flat and locally of finite presentation over the base. Its pushforward of
the structure module has finite local bases on the base. These conclusions
apply over arbitrary schemes.

The proof combines the Library's finiteness theorem for the projection
with its flatness, local finite presentation and finite local basis
theorems. They implement the finite locally free normal invariants used
for the finite-jet parameter in SGA 3, Exposé XV, §5.2.
