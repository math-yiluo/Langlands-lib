# Reduction of fixed-subgroup representability to a closed-factorization locus

This internal helper proves three conditional reductions. Each theorem assumes the exact published proposition `Langlands.AlgebraicGeometry.ClosedFactorizationLocusStatement.{u}` explicitly. It does not prove that geometric existence proposition or any of the three unconditional task targets. A later proof of the geometric proposition can be passed to these reductions to prove the exact targets without changing their statements.

For the general target, the implemented proof applies the closed-factorization locus to the acting scheme `H.X`, the target scheme `G.X`, and the closed inclusion of the equalizer of action and projection inside `H × G`. The coordinate-projectivity predicate on a group scheme unfolds to precisely the generic predicate on its underlying scheme. Separatedness of `G` gives closedness of the equalizer.

The resulting closed immersion `i : Z → G.X` represents maps whose entire product with `H` factors through that equalizer. The all-test comparison in `FixedActionEqualizer` identifies this condition with membership in `a.fixedSubgroup`. The proof installs the closed-immersion instance, obtains that `i` is a monomorphism in the over category, and applies the transfer theorem in `RepresentabilityTransfer`. That theorem equips the same scheme `Z` with its actual group-scheme structure and gives the representing closed subgroup inclusion. This proves `fixedSubgroupRepresentability_of_closedFactorizationLocus`.

The theorem `fieldFixedSubgroupRepresentability_of_closedFactorizationLocus` applies the published `fieldFixedSubgroupRepresentability_of_general` from Worker 1150's `FixedRepresentabilityFieldReduction`. The theorem `fieldCentralizerRepresentability_of_closedFactorizationLocus` then applies `fieldCentralizerRepresentability_of_fixed` from the same module. In those imported reductions, vector-space projectivity, field flatness and group-scheme separatedness supply the general hypotheses, and conjugation identifies the all-test fixed subgroup with the existing centralizer functor.

All three reductions retain the arbitrary universe `u` of their input and output propositions. Their general output retains every commutative base ring and every test scheme, including nonaffine and nonreduced tests. No hypothesis on smoothness, finite type, finite generation, reducedness, or rational-point surjectivity is introduced. The geometric existence proposition remains an explicit premise in these helper theorems. The three target declarations and their imports are unchanged.

The provenance is internal: this module combines the published action-equalizer comparison, scheme-to-group representability transfer, and field reductions. It invokes no unproved geometric existence claim, and does not assert that these reductions alone complete the closed-representability task. The original literature correspondence for the input and target propositions remains in their published statement modules.
