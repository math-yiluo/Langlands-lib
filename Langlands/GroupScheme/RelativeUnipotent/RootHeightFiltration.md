# Represented root-height filtration terms

This derived construction takes a scheme chart by finitely many actual additive root maps over an arbitrary commutative ring. The heights are positive integers, and the standard commutator of two root-factor subgroups belongs to the subgroup generated by factors of height at least the sum. Both the ordered-product formula and this commutator bound hold on every scheme test. The chart need not preserve the group law.

`RootHeightChart` records precisely these inputs. `subgroup d` is the actual subgroup functor generated by factors of height at least `d`. Ordered collection, using `HeightFiltration.exists_coords`, gives root parameters with lower-height coordinates zero. The scheme chart makes these parameters unique, giving `mem_subgroup_iff`. Conversely a supported ordered product belongs to the generated subgroup term by term.

`groupScheme d` applies `CoordinateFiltrationTerm` to this exact all-test coordinate support description. It represents the height subgroup by a smooth affine group scheme whose underlying scheme is the standard affine space on the retained coordinates. `inclusion d` is an actual closed group-scheme immersion and represents exactly `subgroup d`.

Positive heights imply `subgroup 1` is the full group. The commutator sum bound implies every term is normal in that full group. A term above every supplied height is the identity subgroup on every test scheme. The zero-dimensional chart and empty coordinate blocks are included.

The provenance is internal: the implementation combines the general ordered collection theorem, the all-test root-factor functor from `LocalSplitHeightSubgroup`, and the previously constructed supported-coordinate representability transfer. No root-datum or reductivity theorem is assumed implicitly. Applications must supply the actual chart and its commutator bound.

This unit constructs the subgroup terms and their geometry. It does not yet construct the successive quotient morphisms, descend coordinate data, or prove the general relative-radical filtration statement.
