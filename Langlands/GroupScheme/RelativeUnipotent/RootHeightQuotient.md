# Represented quotients of root-height terms

For an arbitrary-ring `RootHeightChart` and a positive height, `quotientMap` is the actual homomorphism from the represented height term to the standard vector group of exact-height coordinates. It is built by Yoneda from the additive homomorphism in `RootHeightLayer`, composed with the representing inclusion on all scheme tests.

The point formula shows naturality and identifies its underlying scheme morphism with ordinary coordinate projection from the affine-space scheme of the retained coordinates. The exact-height enumeration is transported through the enumeration of the retained block. The existing arbitrary-ring coordinate-projection theorem therefore proves flatness, scheme surjectivity and local finite presentation.

The provenance is internal: this is an application of the exact-height group quotient formula, supported-coordinate inclusions and the polynomial coordinate-projection geometry. No field, noetherian, connectedness or constant global-rank assumption enters this local coordinate construction. Descent of these local constructions is a separate obligation.
