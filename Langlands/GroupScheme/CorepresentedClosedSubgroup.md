# Closed subgroup schemes cut out by ideals, and the block subgroups of `GL_n`

This module has two parts.

1. A general construction. Let `F` be a group-valued functor on commutative `R`-algebras,
   corepresented by an algebra `B`, so that `Spec B` is an affine group scheme over `Spec R`
   (`Corepresented.groupScheme F e`). Let `H A ⊆ F A` be subgroups such that a point lies in
   `H A` exactly when its classifying algebra map `B → A` kills an ideal `I` of `B`. Then
   `Spec (B ⧸ I)` is an affine group scheme, and the induced homomorphism
   `Spec (B ⧸ I) → Spec B` is a closed immersion. It represents the subgroup functor of
   `Spec B` given by `H` on every scheme `T` over `Spec R`.
2. An application to `GL_n` over `Spec R`. For a weight vector `e : n → ℤ`, the published
   block parabolic, block Levi and block unipotent subgroup functors (`blockParabolic`,
   `blockLevi`, `blockUnipotent` in `Langlands.GroupScheme.Dynamic.GeneralLinear`) are
   represented by closed subgroup schemes of `GL_n`.

The base ring `R` is an arbitrary commutative ring. Test schemes are arbitrary schemes over
`Spec R`, not only spectra of algebras or fields.

## Sources

- Conrad, Gabber, Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive groups*, 2nd ed. (2015), Example on `GL(V)` before
  equation (2.1.1), printed p. 46 (PDF p. 72). In an adapted basis with weights
  `e₁ ≥ ⋯ ≥ eₙ`, `g = (a_rs)` is a point of `P_G(λ)` exactly when `a_rs = 0` whenever
  `i_r > i_s`, and `λ(t) g λ(t)⁻¹ = (t^{e_r - e_s} a_rs)`. The same page describes `U_G(λ)` as
  the points acting trivially on the graded pieces, and the limit as the block diagonal part.
- Same book, Lemma 2.1.4, printed p. 49 (PDF p. 75): for an affine `k`-scheme `V` with a
  `GL₁`-action, the closed subscheme `Spec(k[V]/J)`, with `J` generated by the negative weight
  spaces, represents the functor of points whose limit exists. Lemma 2.1.5, printed p. 50
  (PDF p. 76): `P_G(λ)` is a closed subgroup scheme, `Z_G(λ) = P_G(λ) ∩ P_G(-λ)`, and `U_G(λ)`
  is a closed subgroup scheme.
- Conrad, *Reductive group schemes* (2014), Theorem 4.1.7(1), p. 112: `P_G(λ)`, `U_G(λ)` and
  `Z_G(λ)` are represented by closed subgroups of `G`. The proof on p. 113 notes that existence
  as closed subgroup schemes does not need finite presentation.

The sources work with the ideal generated by all negative weight spaces of `k[G]` for a general
affine group. For `GL_n` with the diagonal cocharacter of weights `e`, this module uses explicit
generators instead: the entry functions `x_ij` (weight `e i - e j` under conjugation) and, for
the unipotent part, the functions `x_ij - δ_ij`. The block subgroups are the explicit matrix
descriptions of `P`, `Z` and `U` from the cited example. This module does not itself prove that
they agree with the dynamic subgroup functors of the diagonal cocharacter; that comparison is
the published statement `Langlands.GroupScheme.GeneralLinear.DynamicStatement`, whose proof is a
separate unit.

## General construction (namespace `Langlands.GroupScheme.Corepresented`)

Fix `e : (F ⋙ forget GrpCat).CorepresentableBy B`, where `F : CommAlgCat R ⥤ GrpCat`.

- `IsCutOutBy e H I` (a `Prop`): for every `R`-algebra `A` and every `x : F A`,
  `x ∈ H A ↔ ∀ b ∈ I, (e.homEquiv.symm x) b = 0`. Here `e.homEquiv.symm x : B ⟶ A` is the
  algebra map classifying `x`.
- `IsCutOutBy.map_mem`: such a family is stable under every algebra map `A ⟶ A'`, because the
  classifying map of `F.map f x` is the composite of that of `x` with `f`.
- `cutFunctor hI : CommAlgCat R ⥤ GrpCat` is `A ↦ H A`, with maps restricted from `F`.
- `quotientAlgebra I` is `B ⧸ I` as an `R`-algebra; `quotientMap I : B ⟶ B ⧸ I` is the
  quotient map.
- `cutCorepresentableBy hI`: `B ⧸ I` corepresents `cutFunctor hI`. An algebra map
  `f : B ⧸ I ⟶ A` corresponds to the point of `F A` classified by `quotientMap I ≫ f`, which
  lies in `H A` since that map kills `I`. The inverse sends `x ∈ H A` to the map `B ⧸ I ⟶ A`
  induced by the classifying map of `x` (`Ideal.Quotient.liftₐ`).
- `cutGroupScheme hI` is the affine group scheme `Spec (B ⧸ I)` over `Spec R` built from this
  corepresentation by the published `Corepresented.groupScheme`.
- `cutInclusion hI : cutFunctor hI ⟶ F` is the inclusion of subgroups, and
  `cutHom hI : cutGroupScheme hI ⟶ groupScheme F e` is the induced homomorphism of group schemes
  (`Corepresented.groupSchemeMap`).
- `cutHom_left`: the underlying morphism of schemes of `cutHom hI` is
  `Spec.map (Ideal.Quotient.mk I) : Spec (B ⧸ I) ⟶ Spec B`. Proof: by `groupSchemeMap_hom`
  it is `Spec` of the classifying map of the image of the universal point, and that map is
  `quotientMap I`.
- `isClosedImmersion_cutHom` (instance): this morphism is a closed immersion, by Mathlib's
  `IsClosedImmersion.spec_of_surjective` for the surjective quotient map.
  `isClosedSubgroup_cutHom`: `cutHom hI` is a closed subgroup in the sense of the published
  `IsClosedSubgroup` (`Langlands.GroupScheme.Reductive.Basic`), which asks exactly for this.
- `mono_cutHom` (instance): `cutHom hI` is a monomorphism of schemes over `Spec R`
  (closed immersions are monomorphisms).
- `cutSubgroupFunctor hI : SubgroupFunctor (groupScheme F e)`: on a scheme `T` over `Spec R`, the
  points `x : T ⟶ Spec B` whose image under the published point comparison
  `pointsEquiv F e T : (T ⟶ Spec B) ≃* F Γ(T, 𝒪_T)` lies in `H Γ(T, 𝒪_T)`
  (`mem_cutSubgroupFunctor`). Stability under restriction uses `pointsEquiv_naturality` and
  `IsCutOutBy.map_mem`.
- `range_cutHom`: the points of `Spec B` over `T` that factor through `cutHom hI` are exactly
  the points of `cutSubgroupFunctor hI`, on every `T`. Proof: `groupSchemeMap_points` identifies
  the composite of a point `y` of `Spec (B ⧸ I)` with `cutHom hI` with the image of
  `pointsEquiv` of `y` under the inclusion. Conversely, a point in `H` is the image of the
  corresponding point of `Spec (B ⧸ I)`.
- `isRepresentedBy_cutHom`: `cutSubgroupFunctor hI` is represented by `cutHom hI` in the sense of
  the published `SubgroupFunctor.IsRepresentedBy` (a monomorphism whose range is the subgroup
  functor).

## Block subgroups of `GL_n` (namespace `Langlands.GroupScheme.GeneralLinear`)

Here `n` is a finite type with decidable equality, `R : CommRingCat` and `e : n → ℤ`. The
coordinate ring `CoordinateRing n R` of `GL_n` and the classifying map `classify g` of an
invertible matrix `g` are the published ones from `Langlands.GroupScheme.GeneralLinear`.

- `entryIdeal n R p c`, for `p : n → n → Prop` and `c : n → n → R`: the ideal of
  `CoordinateRing n R` generated by the functions `x_ij - c_ij` for all positions with `p i j`.
  Here `x_ij` is the image of the variable `Coordinate.entry i j`.
- `classify_eq_zero_iff`: for an `R`-algebra `S` and `g : GL n S`, `classify g` kills
  `entryIdeal n R p c` if and only if `g i j = c i j` (mapped to `S`) for every position with
  `p i j`. Proof: `classify_entry` evaluates the generators, and `Ideal.span_le` reduces the
  converse to the generators.
- `isCutOutBy_entryIdeal`: a family of subgroups of `GL_n` given by such entry conditions is cut
  out by `entryIdeal n R p c`.
- `isCutOutBy_blockParabolic`, `isCutOutBy_blockLevi`, `isCutOutBy_blockUnipotent`: the
  published matrix subgroups `blockParabolicSubgroup A e`, `blockLeviSubgroup A e` and
  `blockUnipotentSubgroup A e` are cut out, respectively, by
  - the entries `x_ij` with `e i < e j` (`c = 0`);
  - the entries `x_ij` with `e i ≠ e j` (`c = 0`);
  - the functions `x_ij - δ_ij` with `e i ≤ e j` (`c = 1`, the identity matrix).
- `blockParabolicScheme n R e`, `blockLeviScheme n R e`, `blockUnipotentScheme n R e`: the
  corresponding affine group schemes `Spec (CoordinateRing n R ⧸ entryIdeal …)` over `Spec R`
  (`Corepresented.cutGroupScheme`).
- `blockParabolicHom n R e`, `blockLeviHom n R e`, `blockUnipotentHom n R e`: their inclusions
  into `groupScheme n R` (`Corepresented.cutHom`).
- `cutSubgroupFunctor_blockParabolic`, `cutSubgroupFunctor_blockLevi`,
  `cutSubgroupFunctor_blockUnipotent`: the subgroup functors of these cut-out families are, by
  definition (`rfl`), the published `blockParabolic n R e`, `blockLevi n R e` and
  `blockUnipotent n R e`.
- `isRepresentedBy_blockParabolic`, `isRepresentedBy_blockLevi`,
  `isRepresentedBy_blockUnipotent`: each of these three published subgroup functors is
  represented by the corresponding inclusion.
- `isClosedSubgroup_blockParabolicHom`, `isClosedSubgroup_blockLeviHom`,
  `isClosedSubgroup_blockUnipotentHom`: each inclusion is a closed immersion.

## Scope

- Only subgroup families cut out by an ideal of a corepresenting algebra are treated. The module
  does not construct the ideal generated by negative weight spaces for a general affine group
  with a `𝔾_m`-action (CGP Lemma 2.1.4), and it does not prove representability of the dynamic
  subgroup functors `P_G(λ)`, `U_G(λ)`, `Z_G(λ)` of a general group scheme.
- For `GL_n`, representability of the dynamic subgroup functors of the diagonal cocharacter
  follows by combining these results with the equalities `parabolic = blockParabolic`,
  `centralizer = blockLevi` and `unipotent = blockUnipotent` of the published statement
  `DynamicStatement`; that combination is not stated here.
- Finite presentation of the quotients, flatness and smoothness of the block subgroups are not
  treated.
