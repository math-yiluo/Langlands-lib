# Rational invariance of the individual matrix Levi slices

The cut-count weights agree with the Library's weight function for the
original block shape. Rational block parabolic matrices therefore transport
to rational points of the weight-block parabolic, whose principal adelic
points preserve the normalized term on the left. The transport uses the
affine coefficient-point comparison and the base-point equivalence.

Block diagonal embedding commutes with the principal coefficient map.
This gives rational invariance of the product Levi slice, and then of an
individual slice with the other adelic block coordinates held fixed. The
coordinate update is continuous, giving `blockShapeLeviSliceFactor` in
the original continuous rational-invariant function space of that block.
An open finite stabilizer of the original form translated by `g` pulls
back along the continuous inclusion of the specified finite block.
This stabilizes the entire individual slice.

These are the rational-invariance and finite-smoothness properties of the slice in
Langlands (1979), Lemma 4, printed p. 204 (Corvallis Part I, PDF p. 199).
