# Source-vector values of the two completed operators

The bounded action comparisons become identities on source vectors
by evaluating each at the canonical Hilbert image and composing with
the corresponding completed operator's identity on that image.
The lemma is an internal consequence of `BoundedActionCompatibility`
and applies to the finite and compact actions of the same positive form.

For adelic completions the formula in archimedean matrix coordinates
identifies the compact inclusion with the given adelic compact action.
This yields the two source-vector formulas for the coordinate actions.
