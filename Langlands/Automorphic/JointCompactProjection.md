# Projecting to a fixed finite level and infinite compact type

`jointCompactProjection` applies the isotypic projection for an
irreducible infinite compact type, followed by the normalized average
over a compact open subgroup at the finite places. It fixes every
vector of that type already fixed by the finite subgroup. A map
intertwining both actions commutes with the composite.

When the actions commute, averaging any map from the compact type
again gives a compact intertwiner, now taking values fixed by the finite
subgroup. The image of the composite therefore lies in the range of the
finite sum of evaluations on the joint multiplicity space. Joint
admissibility makes this range finite-dimensional.

Suppose the finite and infinite compact generator actions are constant
across a family of algebra actions. On any finite-dimensional subspace
one element of the algebra acts as the joint projection at every
parameter. For the infinite compact type, apply naturality of the
isotypic projection to a tuple of basis vectors. The projected tuple
lies in its cyclic module for the compact group algebra. For the finite
average, take the mean of the tuple of projected basis vectors. Each
coordinate has a finite orbit under the compact open subgroup, so the
tuple mean is a finite linear combination of joint orbit vectors.
Lifting the two combinations to the supplied algebra and multiplying
implements the projection on the basis, hence on the subspace.

This is an internal form of the fixed compact-type reduction in
Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), proof
of Proposition 2, printed pp. 205–206 (Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 200–201).
