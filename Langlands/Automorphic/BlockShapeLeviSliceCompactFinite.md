# Compact-finiteness of an individual Levi slice

The standard compact of a single block embeds in the ambient standard
compact by inserting the identity on the other blocks. Real and complex
coordinate evaluation commutes with taking block diagonal matrices.
With the given consecutive block numbering, block diagonal orthogonal
and unitary matrices are again orthogonal and unitary.

Fix an original automorphic form, an ambient argument with standard
compact infinite component `k` and arbitrary finite component, and all
but one Levi coordinate. The resulting single-block slice is linear in
the original form. Translating the free variable by a block compact member
translates the original form on the right by the ambient compact element
obtained by conjugating that member's block embedding by `k⁻¹`. This uses
the parabolic slice evaluation and the multiplication rule for the actual
finite and infinite factors. Thus the block compact orbit span lies in
the linear image of the finite-dimensional ambient compact orbit span.
The slice is compact-finite in its actual continuous rational-invariant
function space.

This derives the compact-finiteness assertion for the individual Levi
slices in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation*,
Corvallis Part I (1979), Lemma 4 and the proof of Proposition 2, printed
pp. 204–205 (PDF pp. 199–200). The registered source is
`langlands-1979-notion-automorphic-representation`.
