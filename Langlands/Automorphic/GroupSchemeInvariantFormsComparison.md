# Rational-invariant scalar automorphic functions and the original form carrier

`ofRationalSmoothFunction` places a scalar function in the original
continuous rational-invariant carrier and keeps its given smooth
infinite slices and open finite stabilizer. Evaluation recovers the
original function.

`rationalAutomorphicFunctionComparison` proves the published comparison
proposition. Compact finiteness is preserved and reflected by the
injective evaluation of the entire compact orbit. An ideal in the
center annihilates the function precisely when it annihilates all
infinite slices, since those slices determine the adelic function and
intertwine the center action. Both growth predicates use the same
adelic source norm.

`rationalAutomorphicFunctionSubmodule` is the image of the original
automorphic submodule under evaluation. Its membership criterion says
that the scalar predicates on the rational quotient describe exactly
this image.

This is an internal comparison of the Library's two formulations of
automorphic functions on the rational quotient. It allows the regular
scalar values of continued Eisenstein series to be used with the
submodule structure of actual forms.
