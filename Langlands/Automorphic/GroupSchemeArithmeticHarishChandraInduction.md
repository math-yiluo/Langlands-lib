The conclusion concerns an affine connected reductive group over a number
field with its supplied closed matrix representation and archimedean smooth
and differential data. At a compact open finite level \(L\) and finite point
\(b\), take the infinite images of the rational points whose finite components
fix \(bL\). The theorem bounds the complex span of the plot-smooth functions
invariant under this arithmetic subgroup, with moderate growth in the prescribed
height, a common cofinite annihilator ideal in the enveloping center, and right
profiles in one finite-dimensional space on the prescribed maximal compact.

Induction on the Lie dimension proves adelic rational finiteness. The
constant terms along proper standard parabolics have finite-dimensional
images by restriction to the corresponding Levis. The simultaneous kernel
is finite-dimensional by cuspidality and the fixed-profile estimate.

finite level and recovers the input by restriction to the chosen slice. Its
recovery property places the arithmetic span inside the restriction of the

The induction follows Harish-Chandra, *Automorphic Forms on Semisimple Lie
Groups* (1968), I §2, Theorem 1, and I §6, printed pp. 8 and 17–20
(PDF pp. 18 and 27–30). The passage between the two quotients uses the
fixed-data decomposition of Borel–Jacquet,
*Automorphic forms and automorphic representations* (1979), §4.3 (1)–(2),
printed p. 195 (PDF p. 190).
