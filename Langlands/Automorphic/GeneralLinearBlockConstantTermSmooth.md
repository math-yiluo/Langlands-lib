# Smooth archimedean coordinates of block constant terms

The compact radical quotient has a measurable fundamental domain with
compact closure. At a fixed finite adelic argument, a common open
stabilizer of the original function divides this closure into finitely
many compact sets according to the resulting finite adelic coset. On
each set the integrand is an original smooth archimedean slice composed
with left multiplication by the archimedean radical matrix.
The compact linear-parameter integral proves smoothness of each term
on the open set of invertible matrices. Their sum gives the original
normalized constant term.

This is the archimedean smoothness step for the constant terms in
Langlands (1979), Lemma 4, printed p. 204 (Corvallis Part I, PDF p. 199).
