# A single-block period of a constant term

The group isomorphism that inserts a two-block unipotent radical into the
coarse Levi identifies the corresponding rational lattices. Its image of
the source fundamental domain is a fundamental domain for the target
lattice. The pushforward of Haar measure differs from target Haar
measure by a positive constant, which cancels on normalization.

`blockShapeSingleCutPeriod` integrates the original one-coordinate Levi
slice over its two-block radical. The constant term for the inserted
global cut is an iterated period by the normalized semidirect-product
formula. The transported outer integrand multiplies the variable block
on the left by its radical. Multiplicativity of the block embedding
then identifies this iterated period with `blockShapeSingleCutPeriod`.

This comparison is used for the iterated constant term in Langlands
(1979), Lemma 4 and the proof of Proposition 2, printed pp. 204–205
(Corvallis Part I, PDF pp. 199–200). The registered source is
`langlands-1979-notion-automorphic-representation`, SHA-256
