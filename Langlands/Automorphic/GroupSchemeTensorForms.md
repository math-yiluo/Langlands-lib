# Tensor-smooth forms with actual full compact finiteness

This module restricts the earlier broader form construction by the
actual algebraic-tensor function condition of GH (6.1), printed p.158
(PDF p.172). It keeps the infinite smooth and finite locally constant
factors, as in Definition 5.2.1, printed p.136 (PDF p.151). It does not
replace tensor membership with separate slice conditions.

The full compact subgroup is the actual subgroup `Kinf*C` of the
original adelic point group, with `C` a specified compact open subgroup
of the finite point group. The original continuous rational-function
action is restricted to that subgroup. Its finite-vector condition is
proved equivalent to the previous whole-function infinite finite-vector
condition on the finite plot-smooth carrier, using the faithful original
function inclusion and the actual compact-product comparison.

The finite-level carrier imposes no extra restriction on a tensor-smooth
function satisfying full compact finiteness. Indeed, full compact
finiteness implies finite dimensionality of its finite `C` orbit.
Tensor membership gives finite-slice local constancy. The actual
finite-level stabilizer construction therefore supplies one compact
open subgroup fixing the whole function. Together with the smooth
infinite slices, this constructs the earlier finite plot-smooth
realization of the exact same function, with its infinite finite-vector
condition proved.

The tensor-restricted automorphic subrepresentation is the intersection
of the previous broader form space with tensor membership. Its
membership theorem displays exactly tensor smoothness, whole-function
full compact finiteness, the existing actual enveloping-center finite
orbit condition, and the full adelic moderate-growth condition. These
are the function conditions of GH Definition 6.3.3, printed p.163
(PDF p.177), realized on the original carrier. The existing common
finite-codimensional ideal theorem supplies the equivalent center
condition. The hypotheses on the connected reductive group, closed
matrix embedding, infinite maximal compact, positive matrix dimension,
smooth algebraic dimension and differential commutator remain explicit.

Positive finite translation preserves the intersection and its
inclusion into the earlier form space is injective and equivariant.
The finite action is smooth and every such form has actual compact
finite level. No equality with the broader form space is asserted.
Closure under the full archimedean enveloping action, automatic uniform
growth, cusp and L2 restrictions and full old-GL form comparisons are
not proved in this module.
