# Compact actual quotient: Hilbert–Schmidt convolution and discrete decomposition

`Langlands.Automorphic.GroupSchemeFunctions.GroupSchemeCompactQuotientL2DecompositionStatement`
is a proposition definition. It supplies no proof.

## Setting

The objects are the Library's actual positive rational quotient
`PositiveRationalQuotient F G T i`, together with its measure, `L²` space
and right action.

- `F` is a number field and `G` an affine group scheme over `Spec F`,
  locally of finite type.
- `T` is a commutative group scheme with a split-torus isomorphism `e`.
  The morphism `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G` is central. Its character restriction is
  injective with finite-index range.
- The Haar measure of the norm-one subgroup is right invariant. These
  are exactly the hypotheses of the existing canonical quotient measure
  `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure` and of
  `haarQuotientL2BoundedRightAction`.
- The quotient is the left coset space of `positiveRationalSubgroup`,
  generated by the positive real points of `T` and the rational points.
  It is the Library's version of `[G] = A_G G(F) \ G(A_F)`.

The added hypothesis is that `PositiveRationalQuotient F G T i` is
compact. No connected reductivity is assumed, and compactness is not
derived from anisotropy.

## Conclusion

Write `μG` for the Haar measure of the adelic point group, `μQ` for
`positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure F G T i`, and `π` for the original
unitary right action `haarQuotientL2BoundedRightAction` on `Lp ℂ 2 μQ`.

1. For every compactly supported continuous `f : C_c(G(A_F), ℂ)`, the
   existing integrated operator
   `haarQuotientL2IntegratedAction F G T i hi I e hc μG f hf`, that is
   `R(f) = ∫ f(a) π(a) dμG(a)`, satisfies
   `Langlands.Analysis.OperatorIdeals.IsHilbertSchmidt`.
2. There is a set `A` of closed subspaces of `Lp ℂ 2 μQ` with three
   properties. Every member is nonzero, closed, `π`-invariant and
   topologically irreducible. The inclusions of the members form a
   Mathlib `IsHilbertSum` of the whole space. For each `P ∈ A`, only
   finitely many `Q ∈ A` admit a `π`-intertwining linear isometric
   equivalence `P ≃ₗᵢ[ℂ] Q`.

No classification of the summands, trace-class property or uniqueness of
`A` is asserted.

## Sources and provenance

This is a derived statement. The source is Getz–Hahn, *An Introduction to
Automorphic Representations* (2024), registered as
`getz-hahn-2024-automorphic-representations`, SHA-256
`a125cf9c0f445972cbbd3552b4fb9905ca948108a05880f90eaa43558d09a507`.

- Theorem 9.1.2 and Corollary 9.1.3, printed p.241 (one-based PDF p.253),
  state the trace-class property of the cuspidal restriction of `R(f)` and
  the discrete decomposition with finite multiplicities of the cuspidal
  subspace.
- Lemma 9.3.3, printed pp.247–248 (one-based PDF pp.259–260), states
  the Hilbert–Schmidt criterion.

For connected reductive `G`, `[G]` is compact exactly when the derived
group is anisotropic over `F`, by a standard theorem not formalized here.
Then there is no proper rational parabolic subgroup, cuspidality is
vacuous, and the cuspidal space is all of `L²([G])`. This statement
records that compact case on the actual Library objects. The weights are
compactly supported continuous functions on `G(A_F)`, not functions on
`A_G \ G(A_F)` as in GH; the Hilbert–Schmidt conclusion is stated for
those operators only.

## Intended proof

A private proof compiles. It specializes
`CompactQuotientIntegratedHilbertSchmidtStatement` and
`CompactQuotientDiscreteDecompositionStatement` for general compact left
coset spaces, both submitted separately, to the following inputs:

- `H = positiveRationalSubgroup`, closed by `isClosed_positiveRationalSubgroup`;
- its subgroup Haar measure, which is Haar, σ-finite and right invariant
  by existing theorems;
- the ambient Haar measure;
- the canonical quotient measure, which is compatible by
  `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure_isCompatible`.

The right action preserves norms by
`norm_haarQuotientL2BoundedRightAction` and has continuous orbits by
`haarQuotientL2OrbitMap`. It agrees almost everywhere with the right
translation of representatives by `coeFn_quotientL2RightTranslate`. The
quotient's two right translations, `LeftCosetSpace.rightTranslate` and
`QuotientIntegration.rightTranslate`, agree pointwise on every coset.

## Remaining obligations

Compactness of `[G]` for connected reductive `G` with anisotropic derived
group, the Borel–Harish-Chandra and Godement criterion, is not proved
here. It is an explicit hypothesis. The noncompact cuspidal case needs
the cusp-form estimates of GH §9.4 and is outside this statement.
