# Common elliptic estimates at fixed compact profiles and center ideal

Let a connected reductive group over a number field have the actual
archimedean smooth structure of the Library. Fix a maximal compact subgroup,
a finite-dimensional space of functions on it and a cofinite ideal of the
actual enveloping center. For each actual enveloping derivative,
`fixedProfileInteriorEstimate` chooses a compact set and a constant before
choosing the smooth function or the point of evaluation. If the ideal kills
the function and every compact profile belongs to the prescribed space, the
derivative is bounded by the constant times a bound for the function on the
corresponding left translate of the compact set.

The adapted Casimir identifies the sum of squares of a spanning tangent
family with a central element plus twice the sum of the compact tangent
squares. The central element satisfies a monic polynomial modulo the ideal
by finiteness of the quotient algebra. The compact operator satisfies one
monic polynomial on the whole family by finite dimensionality of the smooth
profiles. The two operators commute on the intersection of their polynomial
kernels, where each generates a finite algebra. Their sum is the image of
an element of the tensor product of these finite algebras. Integrality of
that element supplies a common monic polynomial for the sum.

The highest word degree of that polynomial in the full sum of squares has
principal symbol a power of a sum of real squares. The tangent vectors span,
so that symbol is nonzero at each nonzero covector. The Library's elliptic
interior estimate applies to all left translates of every solution with the
same equation. The word-action identity identifies the resulting bounds with
bounds for the actual enveloping action.

The Casimir construction follows Harish-Chandra, *Two theorems on
semi-simple Lie groups* (1966), Lemma 33 and its proof, printed pp. 108–109
(PDF pp. 35–36). The registered source

uniform over the fixed-data family.
