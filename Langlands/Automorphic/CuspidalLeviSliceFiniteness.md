# A finite-dimensional space for the slices of one coefficient

At a fixed period vector, intersect the common block level with the open kernel of the determinant character. The twisted slices in that block are fixed by this intersection. The original shifted matrix Casimir polynomial transports through the faithful ambient inclusion to the realized twisted slices.

On the compact side, the source action restricts to the block compact and is multiplied by the same determinant character as the target twist. Each resulting compact orbit vector is a scalar multiple of a vector in the original finite-dimensional compact orbit span. The uniform compact Casimir theorem gives a single nonzero polynomial killing all equivariant images of the fixed vector, including all its twisted slices.

The common level and the two common polynomial kernels put the family in a finite-dimensional subspace of the cuspidal realization with unitary central character. This supplies the algebraic tensor step of the proof of Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Proposition 2, printed pp. 204–205.
