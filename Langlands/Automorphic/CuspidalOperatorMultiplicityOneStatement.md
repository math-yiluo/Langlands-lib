# Multiplicity one for actual cuspidal operator realizations

Module `Langlands.Automorphic.CuspidalOperatorMultiplicityOneStatement`.

## Statement

`Langlands.Automorphic.CuspidalOperatorMultiplicityOneStatement.{u}` is a Prop-valued
definition, not proved here. It states exactly:

```lean
∀ (F : Type u) [Field F] [NumberField F] [CompactSpace (AdeleQuotient F)] (r : ℕ)
  (T T' : CuspidalOperatorRealization F r),
  Nonempty (T.Equivalence T') → T.realization = T'.realization
```

`AdeleQuotient` is in `Langlands.Global`; the other names are in `Langlands.Automorphic`.

## Meaning

`T` and `T'` are [actual cuspidal operator realizations](CuspidalOperatorRealization.md) of
`GL_(r+1)` over the number field `F`: subspaces of the Library's stable automorphic
functions on `GL_(r+1)(F)\GL_(r+1)(𝔸_F)`, stable and irreducible under the automorphic
operator algebra (generated by the finite adelic group `GL_(r+1)(𝔸_f)`, the standard compact
subgroup `K_∞` at infinity and the real Lie algebra `𝔤_∞` acting by derivatives), with
vanishing proper constant terms. `T.Equivalence T'` is an isomorphism of representations of
that operator algebra, that is, a linear bijection commuting with the finite, compact and
differential operators together. `T.realization = T'.realization` says that the two
realizations are the same subspace of automorphic functions.

So the statement says: **two equivalent irreducible cuspidal subspaces are equal**, that is,
an irreducible representation occurs at most once in the space of cusp forms.

## Source

J. W. Cogdell, *Analytic theory of L-functions for GL_n* (2004), registered as
`cogdell-2004-analytic-theory-gln`, printed p.202 (PDF p.205): "Multiplicity one. Let
`(π, V_π)` be an irreducible smooth admissible representation of `GL_n(𝔸)`. Then the
multiplicity of `π` in the space of cusp forms on `GL_n(𝔸)` is at most one." Cogdell
attributes it to Piatetski-Shapiro and Shalika and derives it on pp.201–202 from the
Fourier expansion `φ(g) = Σ_γ W_φ(γ g)` (so `φ` is determined by `W_φ`) and the uniqueness of
the global Whittaker model, which follows from local uniqueness (Theorem 1.2: Gelfand and
Kazhdan for nonarchimedean fields, Shalika for archimedean fields).

**Correspondence.** Two irreducible cuspidal subspaces `V ≠ V'` that are isomorphic would
give multiplicity at least two, and conversely; so "multiplicity at most one" is exactly the
statement that isomorphic irreducible cuspidal subspaces coincide. The Library's
realizations are spaces of automorphic forms acted on by `GL_n(𝔸_f)`, `K_∞` and `𝔤_∞`
(a `(𝔤_∞, K_∞) × GL_n(𝔸_f)`-module) rather than smooth representations of `GL_n(𝔸)`; this
is the usual formulation of multiplicity one for automorphic forms. In it the archimedean input is the
uniqueness of Whittaker functionals that are continuous for the topology of the smooth
completion, which the Whittaker functionals of cusp forms are; purely algebraic Whittaker
functionals on a Harish-Chandra module need not be unique, so the statement is not a
consequence of algebraic uniqueness alone.
