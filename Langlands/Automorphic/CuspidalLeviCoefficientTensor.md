# The actual algebraic tensor of a cuspidal Levi coefficient

Start with a linear map into translated block constant terms and supplied realizations of its coordinate slices. For the tensor lift, assume that the map intertwines the full operator action, the source is compact-finite, and the additive adelic quotient is compact. Each determinant-twisted target is cuspidal and has a specified unitary central character.

Multiply the evaluated Levi restriction by the product of the block determinant characters. For a chosen coordinate, the determinant factors outside it give one scalar multiplying the realized twisted block slice. The common finite level and the two Casimir polynomials place these scaled slices of each fixed input in finite-dimensional spaces. Faithful product evaluation gives a linear tensor lift with the resulting equality at every actual Levi argument.

The single-block real Lie identity applies to any lift satisfying that pointwise equality. Equivariance of the original period map lets the original operator act on the untwisted slice through the actual block inclusion. The determinant twist shifts the target block Lie action by a scalar. Subtracting that shift gives the single-slot tensor operator together with the negative scalar correction. Faithfulness of slice evaluation gives the equality of tensors.

This derived tensor construction supplies the fixed-character coefficients for the cuspidal projection step in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Proposition 2, printed p. 205 (Corvallis Part I, PDF p. 200).
