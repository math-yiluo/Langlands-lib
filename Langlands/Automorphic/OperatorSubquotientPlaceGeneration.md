# Place intertwining for smooth operator subquotients

Author: Worker 3657.

Lean module: `Langlands.Automorphic.OperatorSubquotientPlaceGeneration`. It
imports `Langlands.Automorphic.OperatorSubquotientPlaceComponentComplex` for
the place actions and `Langlands.Global.FiniteAdelicGL.OpenSubgroupInduction`
for the finite adelic argument.

The Library's `automorphicOperatorAlgebra F n` is, by definition, the
subalgebra generated by the finite adelic translations `finiteOperator d`,
the compact translations `compactOperator k` (`k` in the standard maximal
compact subgroup at infinity) and the enveloping operators
`differentialOperator u`. An operator subquotient `Q` also carries place
actions (Library and Task 6815): `Q.localRepresentation v` of `GL_n(F_v)` at
a finite place `v`, and at a real (complex) place `v` the compact action
`Q.realPlaceCompactRepresentation v` of `O(n)` (`Q.complexPlaceCompact…` of
`U(n)`) and the complexified Lie action `Q.realPlaceLieAction v` of
`ℂ ⊗_ℝ 𝔤𝔩_n(ℝ)` (`Q.complexPlaceLieAction v` of `ℂ ⊗_ℝ 𝔤𝔩_n(ℂ)`). This
module extends stability and intertwining for the place actions to the
whole operator action using smoothness for the finite adelic translations.

## Abstract generation (any module with an operator-algebra action)

Data: `ρ : automorphicOperatorAlgebra F n →ₐ[ℂ] Module.End ℂ V` and a
subspace `Γ ⊆ V`.

- `subspaceStabilizer Γ`: the subalgebra of endomorphisms mapping `Γ` into
  itself; `mem_subspaceStabilizer` unfolds it.
- `operator_mem_subspaceStabilizer`: if `ρ` of every finite, compact and
  enveloping generator stabilizes `Γ`, so does `ρ a` for every `a`
  (`Algebra.adjoin_induction`).
- `finite_mem_subspaceStabilizer`: if every vector of `Γ` is fixed by
  `ρ (finiteOperator ℓ)` for `ℓ` in some open subgroup and `Γ` is stable
  under the local translations `ρ (finiteOperator (ι_v g))`, then `Γ` is
  stable under every finite translation. Proof: for `x ∈ Γ` apply
  `FiniteAdelicGL.induction_localInclusion` to `P d := ρ(d) x ∈ Γ`.
- `compact_mem_subspaceStabilizer`: stability under the single-place compact
  translations `realPlaceCompactInclusion v k`,
  `complexPlaceCompactInclusion v k` gives stability under all compact
  translations. Proof: the preimage of the stabilizer is a submonoid of the
  product of the place factors (`ArchimedeanCompactFactors`, which maps onto
  the compact group by `toStandardCompact_surjective`); it contains the
  single-place elements `Pi.mulSingle v k` on the real and on the complex
  side, hence all of each side (`Submonoid.pi_mem_of_mulSingle_mem`, finitely
  many places) and their products.
- `differential_mem_subspaceStabilizer`: stability under the enveloping
  generators of the complexified place Lie elements gives stability under the
  whole enveloping algebra. Proof: the preimage of the stabilizer in the
  complexified Lie algebra `ℂ ⊗_ℝ 𝔤` is a complex subspace; it contains
  `complexify X` for every real `X` because `X` is the sum of its place
  components (`sum_placeLieInclusions`), hence every `z ⊗ X = z • complexify X`
  and so everything (`TensorProduct.inductionOn`); the enveloping algebra is
  generated by the image of `ℂ ⊗_ℝ 𝔤`
  (`Langlands.Algebra.LieCasimir.enveloping_adjoin_range`).
- `operator_mem_subspaceStabilizer_of_place`: the combination: smoothness
  on `Γ` plus stability of `Γ` under the six families of place generators
  gives `ρ a ∈ subspaceStabilizer Γ` for every `a`.

## Operator subquotients

`Q Q' : OperatorSubquotient F n`.

- `operatorAction_finite`, `operatorAction_localInclusion`,
  `operatorAction_realPlaceCompact`, `operatorAction_complexPlaceCompact`,
  `operatorAction_realPlaceLie`, `operatorAction_complexPlaceLie`,
  `operatorAction_apply`: the operator action of `Q` on the generators is the
  corresponding place action, and `Q.operatorAction a = Q.representation a`
  (all `rfl`).
- `representation_mem_of_placeStable Z`: a subspace `Z` of `Q.Space` stable
  under the local translations at all finite places and the compact and Lie
  actions at all real and complex places is stable under `Q.representation a`
  for every `a`. Smoothness comes from the Library's
  `finiteRepresentation_isSmooth`.
- `pairAction Q Q'`: the operator algebra acting on `Q.Space × Q'.Space`
  diagonally (`LinearMap.prodMapAlgHom` after `AlgHom.prod`), with
  `pairAction_fst`, `pairAction_snd`.
- `pairAction_mem_subspaceStabilizer_graph`: an operator `a` with
  `φ ∘ Q(a) = Q'(a) ∘ φ` maps the graph of `φ` into itself.
- `representation_comm_of_placeComm φ`: a linear map `φ : Q.Space → Q'.Space`
  commuting with all place actions commutes with `Q.representation a` for
  every `a`. Proof: apply `operator_mem_subspaceStabilizer_of_place` to
  `pairAction Q Q'` and the graph of `φ` (smoothness from the intersection of
  the two open stabilizers), and read off `(x, φ x) ↦ (Q(a) x, Q'(a) φ x)` in
  the graph.
- `equivalenceOfPlaceComm φ`: a linear equivalence commuting with all place
  actions is a `Q.Equivalence Q'` (`Representation.Equiv.mk`).
- `localRepresentation_comm_of_ne`, `realPlaceCompactRepresentation_comm_of_ne`,
  `realPlaceCompactRepresentation_comm_complexPlace`,
  `complexPlaceCompactRepresentation_comm_of_ne`: place actions at distinct
  places commute (local translations from the Library's
  `FiniteAdelicGL.localInclusion_commute`; compact actions from Task 6815's
  `compact_comm_realPlaceCompact`, `compact_comm_complexPlaceCompact`). The
  other commutation relations needed for the reverse implication are already
  in the Library or in Task 6815's modules.

The statements about Q-terms and Q'-terms together are proved through the
abstract `ρ`, converted by the `rfl` lemmas above with `congrArg`; this keeps
elaboration fast.

## Provenance

The restricted-product action is determined by its local actions through
the Hecke algebra identification in Flath, *Decomposition of representations
into tensor products* (1979), §2, Example 2, p. 181 (PDF p. 177).
The paragraph after the definition on p. 182 (PDF p. 178) describes the
extension to the archimedean actions in Theorem 3. The registered source is
`flath-1979-tensor-products`, SHA256
`3b9fe0145da81d870ef8967be83635cd440a2581fe043e64e63324e22dec4db5`.
The Lean proof supplies the corresponding stability argument for the
Library's concrete operator algebra.

## Use

The reverse implication of `LocalComponentDeterminationStatement` (Task 6817)
builds a linear equivalence commuting with every place action and turns it
into `Q.Equivalence Q'` with `equivalenceOfPlaceComm`. The commutation lemmas
give the commuting families required by
`Langlands.CommutingFamily.exists_linearEquiv_of_isotypic`, and
`representation_mem_of_placeStable` turns operator irreducibility of `Q` into
joint irreducibility of the place actions.
