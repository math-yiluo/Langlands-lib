# A common kernel at fixed level and cofinite center ideal

`OperatorRealization.annihilatedMultiplicitySpace` consists of the compact intertwiners whose values have the given finite level and are killed by the given center ideal. `multiplicityValueSpan` is the submodule spanned by the values of all these intertwiners. It is contained in the level-fixed submodule and in the kernel of every operator from the ideal.

The universal map from the finite compact source into the smooth family uses one coordinate for each intertwiner and finite adelic point. In that coordinate it takes the derivative-topology slice of the intertwiner value. The slice map intertwines the compact and enveloping actions. The simultaneous reproduction theorem therefore gives one compactly supported smooth kernel for the entire family.

Each condition that a given slice be fixed by smoothing is the kernel of a linear map. Since it holds for every intertwiner value, it holds on their span. Reproduction in a finite slice gives the convolution identity at the associated finite adelic point. Translating in the infinite factor gives the identity at an arbitrary adelic point.

The construction is internal to the adelic finiteness argument. It combines the reproducing function of Borel–Jacquet, *Automorphic forms and automorphic representations* (1979), §1.6 (2), printed p. 191, PDF p. 186, with the fixed type and level of §4.3 (i), printed p. 195, PDF p. 190.
