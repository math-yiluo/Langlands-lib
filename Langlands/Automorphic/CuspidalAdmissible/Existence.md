# Every cuspidal realization contains an irreducible admissible finite slice

This module proves the Library statement
`Langlands.Automorphic.CuspidalIrreducibleAdmissibleFiniteSliceExistenceStatement`
(module `Langlands.Automorphic.CuspidalAllLocalIrreduciblePresentation`). The theorem is
`Langlands.Automorphic.cuspidalIrreducibleAdmissibleFiniteSliceExistence`.

Statement: let `F` be a number field whose adele quotient `𝔸_F / F` is compact, let `r` be a
natural number and let `S` be an irreducible cuspidal operator realization of `GL_{r+1}`.
Then `S` contains a nonzero finite slice whose finite adelic action is irreducible and
admissible.

The mathematics is the finite-adelic part of Harish-Chandra's finiteness theorem for cusp
forms (Harish-Chandra, *Automorphic forms on semisimple Lie groups*, LNM 62, 1968;
Borel–Jacquet, *Automorphic forms and automorphic representations*, Proc. Sympos. Pure
Math. 33 (1979), Part 1, §1–§4), followed by the standard minimal-fixed-dimension argument.
The implemented route is a sup-norm argument and does not use `L²` theory or Schur's lemma.
It uses the companion modules:

- `CuspidalAdmissible/Finiteness.lean`: cusp forms with a common level, a common reproducing
  kernel and a common central law span a finite-dimensional space.
- `CuspidalAdmissible/CentralEigen.lean`: the central derivative `D₀` (the derivative along
  the scalars `e^t · 1`) has an eigenvector, and each eigenvector satisfies the central law.
- `CuspidalAdmissible/Reproduction.lean`: one smooth compactly supported kernel reproduces a
  realized vector at every adelic point; such vectors form a subrepresentation.
- `CuspidalAdmissible/Irreducible.lean`: the minimal-fixed-dimension argument, and the fact
  that in an irreducible realization every nonzero finite subrepresentation meets the fixed
  vectors of a compact open subgroup with nonzero fixed vectors.

## Helper lemmas (`OperatorRealization`)

- `finite_inf_fixedVectors S hS hn μ hκ hκc lam V₀ hR hE K`: let `S` be cuspidal and
  `0 < n`. Let `V₀` be a subrepresentation of the finite action contained in
  `reproducing S μ hκ hκc` (all vectors reproduced by the continuous compactly supported kernel
  `κ` at every adelic point) and in `centralEigenspace S lam`. Then `V₀ ∩ S^K` is
  finite-dimensional for every open subgroup `K`.

  Proof: apply `finiteDimensional_of_reproduced` to `W := V₀ ∩ S^K` with level `L := K`.
  For the level condition, a `K`-fixed vector satisfies `f(g l) = f(g)` by
  `evaluate_finiteRepresentation`. The reproducing condition is `mem_reproducing`. The central
  law is `evaluate_mul_centralTorus`.
- `exists_compact_fixedVectors S f`: every realized vector is fixed by some compact open
  subgroup. The finite action is smooth (`finiteRepresentation_isSmooth`), and every open
  subgroup contains a compact open one (`finiteAdelicGL_exists_compact_le`).

## Proof of the theorem

Write `T := S.realization`, a representation of `GL_{r+1}`; its carrier is nonzero because
`T` is irreducible.

1. `exists_centralEigenvector` gives `λ` and a nonzero `f₀` with `D₀ f₀ = λ f₀`.
2. Install the Borel structure on `GL_{r+1}(F ⊗ ℝ)` and take its left Haar measure `μ`
   (`borelMeasurableSpace`, `leftHaarMeasure`). `exists_reproducingKernel` gives a smooth
   compactly supported kernel `κ` reproducing `f₀` at every adelic point.
3. Let `V₀ := generated T.finiteRepresentation (ℂ f₀)`, the finite adelic subrepresentation
   generated by `f₀`. By `generated_le`, `V₀ ≤ reproducing T μ κ` and
   `V₀ ≤ centralEigenspace T λ`. By `finite_inf_fixedVectors`, `V₀ ∩ T^K` is
   finite-dimensional for every open `K`.
4. Choose a compact open `U` fixing `f₀` (`exists_compact_fixedVectors`). Then
   `V₀ ∩ T^U ≠ 0` and `T^U ≠ 0`, since both contain `f₀ ≠ 0`. By `inf_fixedVectors_ne_bot`
   (irreducibility of `T`), every nonzero subrepresentation of the finite action meets `T^U`.
5. `exists_irreducible_admissible_le` gives a nonzero subrepresentation `A ≤ V₀` that is
   irreducible and admissible. `CuspidalOperatorRealization.exists_finiteSlice_of_subrepresentation`
   turns `A` into the required finite slice.
