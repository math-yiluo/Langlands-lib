# Finiteness of the cuspidal multiplicity space

`cuspidalAnnihilatedFormMultiplicitySpace` is the subspace of the published fixed-level, cofinite-center multiplicity space whose intertwiner values are cuspidal. At rank zero the adelic group is a singleton and one point evaluation determines any form.

In positive rank, first work in a cuspidal operator realization. The span of the images of the intertwiners is fixed by the given open level, killed by the given center ideal, and reproduced by one smooth compactly supported archimedean kernel. The real Lie generator in the scalar identity direction preserves that span. It commutes with finite translations and with all operators in the center ideal, and conjugation by the archimedean compact group fixes the identity direction.

On each eigenspace of this generator, the scalar differential equation gives the central law for the positive scalar torus. The Library's Siegel-set estimate for a common kernel gives finite dimension of that eigenspace. In the finite-dimensional center quotient, the class of the scalar generator satisfies a monic polynomial. The induced operator on the whole image span satisfies the same polynomial. Over the complex numbers this polynomial factors into linear factors. The kernel of a product is the preimage of the first kernel under the second operator, so finite-dimensionality of the kernels of the individual factors gives finite-dimensionality of the full kernel. This includes the generalized central eigenspaces.

The multiplicity space embeds into linear maps from the finite source to this finite-dimensional span. For the original forms the faithful equivalence with the image of the cuspidal realization lifts each intertwiner linearly and retains its finite level, compact equivariance, and center annihilation.

The implemented route is the adelic cuspidal step for Borel–Jacquet, *Automorphic forms and automorphic representations* (1979), §4.3 (i), printed p. 195, PDF p. 190. It uses the reproducing function discussed in §1.6 (2), printed p. 191, PDF p. 186, and the Library's `finiteDimensional_of_reproduced` for its scalar-central estimate. Harish-Chandra, *Automorphic forms on semisimple Lie groups* (1968), Chapter I, §6, Lemma 18, printed p. 18, PDF p. 28, proves the corresponding cuspidal finiteness step by a boundedness argument on the arithmetic quotient.
