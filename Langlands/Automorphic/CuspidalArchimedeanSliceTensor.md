# The tensor functional slot on an actual cuspidal slice

For the actual chosen cuspidal tensor decomposition over a number field
with compact additive adele quotient, hold the finite vector and the
infinite vectors away from a chosen place fixed. Evaluating an original
linear functional on the resulting pure tensor with one varying slot
agrees with restricting that functional along the algebraic slice map.

Both maps evaluate the same dependent pure tensor. The proof takes pointwise
equality and applies linear map extensionality. This is an internal
identification of the two Library slice interfaces.
