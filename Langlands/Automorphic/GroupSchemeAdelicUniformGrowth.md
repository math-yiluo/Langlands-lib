# Uniform differential growth on the original finite-smooth adelic carrier

Fix a number field, an affine group scheme smooth of relative dimension
`d`, and an algebraic matrix representation of positive dimension. The
existing finite plot-smooth rational-invariant continuous adelic carrier
is unchanged. It requires smooth infinite slices and one common open
finite stabilizer. The selected atlas and identity-tangent Lie algebra
are exactly those of `GroupSchemeEnvelopingAction`, with its explicit
scalar-commutator premise. The abbreviations expose those existing
types and that existing action with their original local instances.

`HasAdelicUniformModerateGrowth` extends the bound quantifiers of
Getz--Hahn Definition 6.3.1, printed pp.161--162 (physical PDF
pp.175--176), to this finite-smooth carrier: one real exponent precedes
all actual complex enveloping elements, each of which has its own
strictly positive constant bounding the derivative at every adelic
argument for the exact full matrix/inverse product norm.

There is a separate smooth-space distinction. GH (6.1), printed p.158
(physical PDF p.172), explicitly defines its smooth adelic functions
using an algebraic tensor product, hence finite sums of separated
infinite and finite functions. The carrier used here imposes no such
global finite-tensor condition, and this module does not prove one.
Thus these are extended differential growth bounds on the original
finite-smooth carrier, not an identification with the literal GH
uniform-smooth function space. No compact or center finiteness argument
is claimed to imply the missing tensor condition.

`HasAdelicUniformPolynomialGrowth` uses one natural exponent for the
previous dimension-scaled height and nonnegative derivative-dependent
constants. The two bound conditions are equivalent: the fixed scaling
factor and the natural exponent are chosen before the enveloping element.
The natural-exponent condition gives a complex submodule. Every actual
enveloping differential preserves the same exponent, by testing `a*b`;
the identity differential recovers ordinary full adelic growth. The
existing finite-translation commutation and height submultiplicativity
give a finite adelic subrepresentation and a restricted actual
enveloping algebra homomorphism.

This is a derived construction. Literal GH tensor-space comparison,
automatic uniform growth from compact and central finiteness, full
infinite translation stability, embedding independence, exponential
derivative comparison and original matrix-form comparison remain
separate obligations. The scalar commutator premise remains explicit.
