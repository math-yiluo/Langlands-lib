# Place closure compatibility on a form completion

`PlaceClosureCompatibility` records agreement on embedded vectors at the
compact points and invariance of the closure of every algebraic subspace
stable under the compact and Lie operators. The real and complex lemmas
derive this compatibility for a Hilbert completion with the actual local
group and Lie inclusions and a supplied differential comparison.

The compact point agrees with the chosen global compact inclusion. The
completed compact action then agrees with the algebraic action on the
embedded vectors. The derivative identity and this compact agreement give
the conditions of the analytic Hilbert closure lemma. These are internal
consequences of the form completion, its scalar coefficients and the
actual place restrictions.
