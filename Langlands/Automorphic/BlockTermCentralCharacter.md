# Continuous Hecke characters from the selected block term

`exists_translatedBlockTerm_centralCyclicQuotient` applies the finite-orbit character selection to the space of actual translated block periods. A simple quotient of the original realization whose projection kills the kernel of the selected period remains a quotient of the cyclic space generated by a character eigenvector.

A represented period is a continuous function because it is a finite sum of translated normalized periods. Evaluating the character relation at a point where the function is nonzero expresses the complex value of the character as a continuous function divided by a nonzero constant. The original periods are invariant under principal block scalars, and this invariance holds on their translates by commutativity of the block centre. The character is therefore trivial on every principal scalar tuple. `blockTermHeckeCharacter` restricts it to one block and descends through the actual idele class quotient.

This gives the continuous characters for the actual block period after the algebraic step of Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Lemma 6, printed p. 205.
