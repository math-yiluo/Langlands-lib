# Commuting operators on compact and Lie multiplicities

This internal companion to `ArchimedeanTensorEvaluation` defines
postcomposition on simultaneous compact and Lie intertwiners by any
endomorphism of the target commuting with both action families.

Postcomposition preserves both intertwining identities by the supplied
commutation laws. Tensor evaluation carries its action on the second
factor to the original target operator. In particular the evaluation
range is stable under every such commuting operator. Its existing
compact and Lie covariance also makes this range stable under the chosen
local actions themselves. These three range-stability results are the
inputs to actual one-place tensor decomposition; they assert no
surjectivity or pairing.
