# Finite-dimensional Levi slices and the constant-term reduction

`separateSliceSubmodule` consists of linear families of scalar functions on a product whose slices in either variable lie in a specified subspace. If both specified subspaces are finite-dimensional, so is this submodule. Point evaluations on the first slice space span a subspace of its finite-dimensional dual. A finite subset of those evaluations generates that span. Each chosen evaluation specifies a position in the first variable, so the second-variable slices at those positions determine every product function. The resulting map into a finite product of copies of the second slice space is linear and injective.

`finiteDimensional_annihilatedFormMultiplicitySpace_of_slices` applies this argument to the actual maximal-block constant terms, assuming finite-dimensional bounds for the two types of slices for each block and finite translate. A finite set of compact coset representatives at the given open level suffices. The range of the joint constant-term map lies in a finite product of the finite-dimensional product-function spaces. Its kernel embeds into the cuspidal multiplicity space by the actual constant-term kernel lemma. The finite-dimensional kernel and range yield finite dimension of the original space.

This internal reduction implements the kernel and image argument from Harish-Chandra, *Automorphic forms on semisimple Lie groups* (1968), Chapter I, §6, proof of Theorem 1, printed pp. 19–20, PDF pp. 29–30, for the adelic fixed-level space of Borel–Jacquet, *Automorphic forms and automorphic representations* (1979), §4.3 (i), printed p. 195, PDF p. 190.
