# Analytic comparison of scalar lifts at a fixed finite level

Two linear scalar lifts respecting the original compact and enveloping
actions have joint-finite values. The original finite action commutes with
both operators. Consequently an original fixed vector remains fixed
after any enveloping operator and any subsequent compact translation.

Agreement at the identity on the original fixed vectors therefore gives
agreement of every enveloping derivative at every standard compact point.
The Library's joint-finite analytic comparison then identifies the two
smooth functions on the full infinite adelic group. This is an internal
application of compact component coverage and analytic jet uniqueness.
