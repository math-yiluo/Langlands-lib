# Converting the algebraic slice comparison to tensor coordinates

The original cuspidal realization over a number field with compact
additive adele quotient maps by a supplied finite intertwiner to a mixed
smooth carrier. For the supplied unitary adelic additive character,
assume that restrictions of any two mixed Whittaker functionals along
an original algebraic slice are proportional when the first restriction
is nonzero.

The corresponding tensor slots satisfy the same comparison. Equality of
each slot with the algebraic restriction transports nonvanishing and the
scalar equality. This is an internal consequence of the slot identity.
