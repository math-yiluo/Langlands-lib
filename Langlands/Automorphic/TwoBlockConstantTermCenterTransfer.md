# Cofinite center ideals for adelic two-block constant terms

The center transfer implements the comparison in Harish-Chandra
(1968), Chapter I, §6, printed pp. 19–20 (PDF pp. 29–30) for the
actual archimedean operator algebra of the adelic form space.

The proof writes the complexified archimedean Lie algebra as a
finite product of complex matrix Lie algebras. The actual block Lie
inclusions act by the two diagonal insertions in each factor. A
factor's upper radical matrix unit lies in the complexification of
the real upper block. The proof obtains its scalar coefficient from
the inverse of the scalar place-coordinate equivalence and inserts
it at the specified matrix entry. This identifies its enveloping
products with elements of the upper radical span used in the
constant-term vanishing theorem.

In each factor the ambient center ideal contracts to a cofinite
ideal. The one-factor parabolic projection then gives cofinite
ideals in the two local block centers. Their embedded operators
belong to the sum of the ambient center ideal span and the upper
radical span.

In each lower rank the preimage of this sum under the corresponding
block inclusion is an ideal of the lower-rank center. Stability of the
sum under right multiplication gives closure under multiplication by
lower-rank central elements. The contraction of this ideal to each
factor contains the local cofinite ideal, so the quotient of the
full lower-rank center is finite-dimensional by the finite-product
criterion. The resulting ideals depend only on the block cut and
the ambient center ideal.
