# Automorphic descent for a tensor with prescribed slot actions

`Langlands.Automorphic.HasTensorSlotActions` is a condition on a
representation on an indexed tensor. For each factor, it identifies the
action of the specified monoid elements with the chosen endomorphisms in
that slot and the identity in the other slots.

`Langlands.Automorphic.tensor_isIrreducible_of_slot_density` applies this
condition to a finite tensor of nontrivial factors. If the complex algebra
generated by the specified endomorphisms in each factor agrees with every
endomorphism on each finite set of vectors, `eq_top_of_slot_density` makes
every nonzero invariant subspace the whole tensor. The nontrivial factors
give a nontrivial tensor.

`Langlands.Automorphic.exists_irreducibleOperatorSubquotient_with_slotEmbeddings`
applies the free operator descent to a stable subspace of actual automorphic
functions surjecting onto such an irreducible tensor. The descent supplies
one automorphic subquotient with an equivalence for the full free operator
action. The proof inserts each factor into its slot with a fixed nonzero
tensor in the complementary slots and composes with the inverse equivalence.
The resulting injection intertwines all the specified operators of that
factor with their actions on the same automorphic subquotient.

This is an internal algebraic step for the restricted tensor constituent in
Langlands (1979), Lemma 1, p. 203 (Corvallis I PDF p. 198), and the operator
quotient in the proof of Proposition 2, pp. 205–206 (PDF pp. 200–201).
The simultaneous embeddings supply the place actions used for Getz–Hahn
(2024), Theorem 10.7.5, p. 288 (PDF p. 300).
