# Joint finiteness of covariant scalar lifts of original vectors

A complex linear scalar lift that intertwines the original enveloping
operators takes the center orbit of an original vector onto the center
orbit of its image. The published finite-dimensional center orbit
therefore gives center finiteness of the scalar lift.

Compact equivariance likewise carries the finite compact group orbit span
to the orbit span of the lift. The Library converts this to finiteness
under the compact enveloping subalgebra and then combines it with center
finiteness. This is an internal consequence of the original operator
finiteness properties. The hypotheses are the two supplied covariance
identities on the actual scalar functions.
