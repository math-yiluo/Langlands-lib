# Smooth representatives and growth of positive-quotient convolution

`groupSchemePositiveQuotientL2Smoothing` proves
`GroupSchemePositiveQuotientL2SmoothingStatement` for an affine locally
finite-type group over a number field with a supplied smooth relative
dimension and positive-size closed matrix embedding. It uses a central
morphism from a specified finite split torus whose rational-character
restriction is injective with finite-index image, and right invariance of
the norm-one Haar measure. On the actual positive rational quotient a
compact smooth adelic kernel gives a smooth finite-level representative of its convolution
on each quotient `L²` class. One height exponent bounds every archimedean
real derivative word where the finite component lies in a fixed compact
set. The bound is proportional to the input `L²` norm. Its constant can
depend on the derivative word, kernel and compact set of finite components.

For local integrability, take a continuous compact cutoff equal to one
on a given compact subset of the full group. The quotient kernel estimate
makes the product of that cutoff and the pulled-back class integrable.
Differentiation can then pass to the compact kernel. The resulting
pointwise integrals are pairings against the `L²` kernel vectors. The
polynomial bound on the mass of positive-rational subgroup conjugates
bounds their norms, with the same exponent for all differentiated kernels.
The pointwise smoothing descends to the quotient and its class agrees
almost everywhere with the Bochner-integrated right action.

This is the polynomial growth step for the smoothed evaluations used in
Getz–Hahn, *An Introduction to Automorphic Representations* (2024),
Lemma 9.3.3 and (9.14), pp. 247–250. The growth estimate applies to
arbitrary quotient `L²` inputs and is polynomial in the adelic height.
