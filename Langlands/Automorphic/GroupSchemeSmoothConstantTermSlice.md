# Smooth constant-term slices and the enveloping action

For an affine smooth connected unipotent subgroup, the normalized
constant-term integral is a linear map on the actual continuous rational
functions because the unipotent rational quotient is compact. Restriction
at a fixed finite component gives `plotSmoothConstantTermSlice` from the
actual finite plot-smooth rational functions to the actual plot-smooth
archimedean functions. The published comparison identifies its value with
`subgroupConstantTerm`, and differentiation under the integral gives
plot smoothness and the identity for every real tangent derivative.

Complexification gives the identity for all complex Lie algebra
generators. Tensor-algebra induction then gives
`plotSmoothConstantTermSlice_enveloping`, including every element of the
center. Scalars, sums and products use linearity and composition of the
existing actions.

The construction is derived from the unipotent averaging and differential
identity in Mœglin–Waldspurger, *Spectral Decomposition and Eisenstein
Series* (1995), I.2.6 and I.2.10, and uses the normalized unipotent
quotient measure of the Library.
