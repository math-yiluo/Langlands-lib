# Mixed Whittaker uniqueness on a compatible cuspidal completion

Take an actual cuspidal realization over a number field with compact
additive adele quotient and a supplied adelic Hilbert completion of its
unitary form. The supplied finite intertwiner into the mixed smooth
space and the supplied genuine archimedean smooth map agree on their
smooth values. Assume derivative density of the images at the compact
open finite levels and the archimedean comparison through this
intertwiner for every standard unitary additive character.

For every standard adelic additive character, two mixed Whittaker
functionals for the character are proportional if the first is nonzero.
The proof applies the tensor comparison, using finite adelic uniqueness
and the supplied archimedean condition, and the fixed-level density
argument. This is an internal application of those interfaces.
