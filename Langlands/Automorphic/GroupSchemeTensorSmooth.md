# Algebraic-tensor smooth functions on the actual adelic group

GH (6.1), printed p.158 (PDF p.172), uses the algebraic tensor product
of the infinite smooth functions and finite smooth functions. Definition
5.2.1, printed p.136 (PDF p.151), defines the latter as locally constant
functions. They are not assumed to have one open right stabilizer.

This module evaluates that algebraic tensor product on the actual
infinite and finite projections of the actual adelic point group. The
infinite factor is the existing plot-smooth function module. Its
identification with ordinary manifold smoothness is the published
`isPlotSmooth_iff_contMDiff` theorem, under the supplied smooth algebraic
dimension. Membership in the evaluation image is proved equivalent to
one finite sum of separated products valid at every adelic point.

Positive right translation acts on each factor. The actual tensor
evaluation intertwines these translations with the original full
adelic right translation, so its image is a subrepresentation. Every
evaluated tensor has smooth infinite slices and locally constant finite
slices. Under the supplied smooth algebraic dimension its evaluation is
jointly continuous.

The rational-invariant continuous tensor space is the inverse image of
this actual function image in the existing continuous rational-function
carrier. It embeds in the existing slice-smooth space. A separate
intersection imposes one open finite stabilizer; that intersection has
an equivariant inclusion into the previous finite plot-smooth carrier.
No converse or equality with the broader carrier is claimed. The module
does not infer the tensor condition from compact or center finiteness,
and does not yet construct its automorphic-form, cusp or differential
restrictions.

The evaluation image and its action laws are an internal implementation
of the source algebraic-tensor formula. No injectivity of the tensor
evaluation, finite-volume, analytic growth or spectral theorem is
claimed.
