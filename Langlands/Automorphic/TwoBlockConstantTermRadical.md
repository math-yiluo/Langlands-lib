# Infinitesimal radical vanishing on the two-block Levi

Harish-Chandra, *Automorphic forms on semisimple Lie groups* (1968),
Chapter I, §6, proof of Theorem 1, printed pp. 19–20 (PDF pp. 29–30),
passes ambient central operators through the constant term by
discarding terms with an infinitesimal radical factor. This module
proves the needed vanishing for the unnormalized adelic two-block
constant term on the infinite Levi at every fixed finite argument.

For an off-diagonal matrix $X$, the curve $u(tX)$ has matrix
$1 + tV$ in mixed archimedean coordinates, where $V$ has just the
specified upper block. The block diagonal group moves this curve
to the left by the formula
$\operatorname{diag}(A,D)u(tX)=u(A(tX)D^{-1})\operatorname{diag}(A,D)$.
Left unipotent invariance of the constant term makes the resulting
scalar curve constant. Its zero derivative gives vanishing after
applying the real right Lie derivative to the source function.

`upperBlockEnvelopingSpan` is the complex span of products of a
complexified real upper-block generator with an arbitrary enveloping
element on its right. Such a product first applies the latter element
to the finite-smooth source function and then differentiates in the
upper-block direction. The real-direction result and linearity give
zero constant-term evaluation throughout the span.
