# Smooth source maps for an adelic form completion

Given genuine smoothness of the original embedded vectors, the
canonical Hilbert embedding restricts to the smooth vectors of the
infinite coordinate action. The source-vector formulas for the completed
finite and compact actions give the matching identities by subtype
extensionality. The same argument lifts the real-generator identity.
The embedding is injective and its underlying Hilbert image is dense
in the completion. Smoothness of the original finite representation
then puts the source image in the mixed smooth carrier. These
constructions preserve the actual source values of the supplied form.
Joint recovery of the finite-smooth compact-finite vectors gives
density at each fixed finite level in the derivative topology.
