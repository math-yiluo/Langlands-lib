# Annihilation by strict upper block Lie directions

`blockLeviTermRestriction` is the original normalized block constant term evaluated at actual Levi points. `finiteSmoothMatrixEnvelopingAction` expresses the actual finite-smooth differential action in the complex archimedean matrix factors.

A single-entry real Lie direction in a strict upper block has square zero. Its one-parameter subgroup has the form `1 + tX` in mixed coordinates and belongs to the actual block radical in adelic coordinates. The constant term is constant along its right-translation curve at any Levi point, since the parabolic Levi projection of this subgroup is the identity. The formula for the derivative of the block period along this curve proves that the corresponding differential evaluates to zero.

The real linear projection onto one matrix entry complexifies to the projection onto that entry in every complex matrix factor. The preimage of a strict upper block matrix unit therefore lies in the complex span of the real directions at that entry. Linearity gives annihilation by every strict upper block generator. Each generator acts on any finite-smooth function before period restriction, so the whole right ideal generated by them annihilates the restriction.

This is the derived differential step in the Levi restriction of Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Lemma 4, printed p. 204. The square-zero exponential identity is reused from the Library's archimedean Whittaker calculation.
