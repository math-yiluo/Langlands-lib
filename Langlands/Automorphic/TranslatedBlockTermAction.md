# Evaluation and compact differentiation of translated block periods

Each original constant term gives an equivariant map to the translated quotient after a fixed left block-centre translation. These maps span the quotient. Compact-finiteness and the algebraic dual derivative along compact curves pass from the original realization through these maps. The block centre commutes with the descended operator action.

Evaluating the descended finite and compact operators gives the actual right translates of the represented function. Evaluation along a real right Lie curve differentiates to the represented action of its generator. On the left, the actual parabolic acts through its Levi projection because its unipotent part fixes every normalized period and the same property holds for every scalar translate. Using archimedean Iwasawa, the Levi restrictions after all compact and finite right translates detect the represented quotient element.

The finite adelic action on the translated quotient is smooth. Each spanning map intertwines the smooth finite action on the original realization with the action on the quotient.

These are internal evaluation identities for the actual functions used in the standard parabolic step of Task 6823.
