# The Levi restriction of a translated period is another actual Levi restriction

For a fixed full operator realization, `realizedBlockLeviRestriction` takes its normalized constant term on the actual Levi. The image of this map is preserved by the separate block scalar ideles. In one block, a finite scalar can be applied to the original form as a finite right translation. A real sign scalar at the archimedean places belongs to the actual standard compact subgroup, so it also acts on the original form. Each remaining archimedean scalar is an exponential. The one-parameter result places its translation of the restriction in the full Levi enveloping-centre orbit. Every element of this orbit is the period of some ambient enveloping derivative of the original form.

The product of these coordinate operations preserves the image. The restriction to the Levi of a finite sum of translated periods thus lies in the same image by linearity. In particular, for fixed input in the translated quotient there is one original realized form whose period agrees with it at every Levi point. This allows the individual slice bounds and the strict-refinement vanishing of the original realization to be used for the selected block-character space in Task 6823.

The restriction map is defined in [Operators on translated block constant terms](TranslatedBlockConstantTermOperators.md).
