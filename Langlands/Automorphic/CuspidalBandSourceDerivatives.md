# Unitarity and real generators for the band coordinate action

Fix an actual cuspidal operator realization over a number field with compact additive adèle quotient. Supply a norm-one central character acting on the realization and a unitary form that computes the determinant-band inner product.

Restrict the constructed adelic action to the archimedean coordinates. Preservation of the Hilbert inner product follows from the corresponding equality for the adelic operator at the included element. The published coefficient-lift and smooth-kernel results identify the genuine real derivatives of this same action on the original embedded vectors with the original real Lie generators.

These internal consequences of the band construction prepare the use of smooth Whittaker functionals in Shalika, *The multiplicity one theorem for GL(n)* (1974), §5, pp. 187–188.
