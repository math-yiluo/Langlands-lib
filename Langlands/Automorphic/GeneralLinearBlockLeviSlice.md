# Continuous rational-invariant Levi slices of block constant terms

`normalizedBlockLeviSlice` evaluates the intrinsic normalized block constant
term at `m g`, with `m` in the actual adelic Levi subgroup. The integrand is
jointly continuous after descent along the open quotient map from the
unipotent group. On a compact neighborhood of each ambient argument it is
bounded uniformly over the compact unipotent quotient. Dominated convergence
gives continuity of the term and hence of the slice.

A rational point of the block parabolic conjugates the radical into itself,
and its inverse has the same property. The rational conjugation formula for
the normalized quotient integral therefore gives invariance under left
multiplication by that point. This applies to the rational Levi points since
the Levi is contained in the parabolic.

This is the continuous rational-invariant part of the Levi slice in
Langlands (1979), Lemma 4, printed p. 204 (Corvallis Part I, PDF p. 199).
