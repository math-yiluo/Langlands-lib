# Trace-class convolution products on the compact positive rational quotient

`Langlands.Automorphic.GroupSchemeFunctions.groupSchemeCompactQuotientL2TraceClass`
proves
`Langlands.Automorphic.GroupSchemeFunctions.GroupSchemeCompactQuotientL2TraceClassStatement`
(module `Langlands.Automorphic.GroupSchemeCompactQuotientL2TraceClassStatement`)
for the original positive rational quotient
`PositiveRationalQuotient F G T i`.

## Hypotheses

The statement, and hence the theorem, quantifies over the following data and
assumptions, all of which the proof uses.

- `F` is a number field.
- `G : GroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of F))` is a group scheme over `F`
  whose underlying scheme is affine (`IsAffine G.X.left`) and whose
  structure morphism is locally of finite type
  (`LocallyOfFiniteType G.X.hom`). The quotient measure
  `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure`, the subgroup measure
  `positiveRationalSubgroupHaarMeasure`, the original action
  `haarQuotientL2BoundedRightAction` and the closedness, measure and
  compatibility lemmas cited below are stated under these assumptions.
- `T : CommGroupScheme (Spec (CommRingCat.of F))` is a split torus over `F`:
  `e : T.toGrp ≅ splitTorus (Spec (CommRingCat.of F)) I` identifies it with
  the split torus `𝔾_m^I` (`splitTorus`), for a finite type `I`.
- `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G` is a morphism of group schemes that is central
  (`hc : IsCentral i`: every point of `T` maps into the centre of `G`).
- Restriction of rational characters along `i`,
  `characterRestrict i : rationalCharacters G →+ rationalCharacters T.toGrp`,
  is injective (`hi`) and has finite-index range (the instance
  `[(characterRestrict i).range.FiniteIndex]`).
- The Haar measure `Measure.haar` on the norm-one subgroup
  `normOneSubgroup F G` is right invariant (the instance
  `[(Measure.haar : Measure (normOneSubgroup F G)).IsMulRightInvariant]`),
  so the norm-one group is unimodular. This is a hypothesis on `normOneSubgroup F G`, not on the positive rational
  subgroup; right invariance of the latter's measure is proved (see below).
- The positive rational quotient `PositiveRationalQuotient F G T i` is
  compact.

The quotient, its measure and the right action are those of the statement,
built from this data: `PositiveRationalQuotient F G T i` is the left coset
space of `positiveRationalSubgroup F G T i`, the subgroup of
`AdelicGroupPoints F G` generated by the image under `i` of the adelic points
of `T` all of whose characters are positive scalar ideles, and by the rational
points. The conclusion is that for all compactly supported continuous
`f₁ f₂` on `AdelicGroupPoints F G` the Haar convolution
`g ↦ ∫ a, f₁ a * f₂ (a⁻¹ * g) ∂μG` (with `μG = Measure.haar`) is integrable
and the integrated operator `haarQuotientL2IntegratedAction` of the original
right action on `L²(PositiveRationalQuotient F G T i)` at this weight is
trace class in the sense of `Langlands.Analysis.OperatorIdeals.IsTraceClass`.

### Where the hypotheses enter

- The central morphism `i`, the split structure `e`, and the injectivity and
  finite index of `characterRestrict i` give the decomposition of the adelic
  group into the positive image of `T` and the norm-one subgroup
  (`positiveFactor`, `normOneFactor` and, by centrality, the continuous
  homomorphism `normOneProjection` and the topological group isomorphism
  `normOneDecompositionMulEquiv`). Through these constructions they are inputs
  of `isClosed_positiveRationalSubgroup` (the subgroup is the preimage of the
  closed rational norm-one subgroup under `normOneProjection`),
  `positiveRationalQuotient_borelSpace`,
  `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure_isLocallyFiniteMeasure`, the subgroup
  measure `positiveRationalSubgroupHaarMeasure` and its properties, the
  compatibility `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure_isCompatible`, and the
  right invariance `haarQuotientMeasureRightInvariant` of the quotient
  measure. All of these take `hi`, `I`, `e` and `hc` as arguments.
- Right invariance of the norm-one Haar measure is an instance hypothesis of
  `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure_isCompatible` (the Weil compatibility
  of the three measures) and of `haarQuotientMeasureRightInvariant`, which
  enters the definition of `haarQuotientL2BoundedRightAction`, the original
  right action whose integrated operator appears in the conclusion.
- Compactness of `PositiveRationalQuotient F G T i` is the compactness
  hypothesis of the generic theorem, which makes the operators of compactly
  supported continuous weights Hilbert–Schmidt on the whole quotient `L²`
  space.

## The Lean proof

The proof specializes the generic theorem
`Langlands.QuotientIntegration.compactQuotientConvolutionTraceClass`
(module `Langlands.Analysis.Local.CompactQuotientConvolutionTraceClassProof`),
which proves `CompactQuotientConvolutionTraceClassStatement` for a compact
left coset space `H \ G` of a Hausdorff, second countable, locally compact
group by a closed subgroup. It is applied with the following data.

1. **Group, subgroup and instances.** `G` is `AdelicGroupPoints F G` and
   `H` is `positiveRationalSubgroup F G T i`. The quotient
   `PositiveRationalQuotient F G T i` is by definition
   `LeftCosetSpace (positiveRationalSubgroup F G T i)`, and both it and the
   generic `LeftQuotient H` unfold to `Langlands.Induction.LeftQuotient H`,
   so the generic statement applies to the original quotient with no
   transport. The subgroup is closed by
   `isClosed_positiveRationalSubgroup F G T i hi I e hc`, which uses the
   central morphism `i`, the split structure `e` and the injective,
   finite-index character restriction `hi` through `normOneProjection`. The
   Borel structure on the quotient, local finiteness of the quotient
   measure, and the Haar, σ-finiteness and right-invariance properties of
   the subgroup measure are the Library facts
   `positiveRationalQuotient_borelSpace`,
   `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure_isLocallyFiniteMeasure`,
   `positiveRationalSubgroupHaarMeasure_isHaarMeasure`,
   `positiveRationalSubgroupHaarMeasure_sigmaFinite` and
   `positiveRationalSubgroupHaarMeasure_isMulRightInvariant`, each applied
   to the same `hi I e hc` and to the affine, locally finite type group
   scheme `G`; the proof installs them as local instances. The last of
   these is right invariance of the measure on the positive rational
   subgroup, proved from its decomposition as the positive image of `T`
   times the discrete rational norm-one subgroup; it is distinct from the
   hypothesis on `normOneSubgroup F G`.

2. **The same measures.** The subgroup measure is
   `positiveRationalSubgroupHaarMeasure`, the group measure is the adelic
   Haar measure `Measure.haar` (the measure `μG` of the statement), and the
   quotient measure is `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure`, the measure
   of the statement's `L²` space. The theorem
   `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure_isCompatible F G T i hi I e hc`,
   whose hypotheses include right invariance of the Haar measure on
   `normOneSubgroup F G`, states that these
   three measures are compatible in the sense of
   `Langlands.QuotientIntegration.IsCompatible`: the quotient measure is
   nonzero, regular and right invariant, and satisfies the quotient
   integration formula
   \[
   \int_{H\backslash G}\int_H f(hg)\,d\mu_H(h)\,d\nu(Hg)=\int_G f\,d\mu_G
   \]
   for compactly supported continuous `f`.

3. **The same action.** The action is the original right action
   `haarQuotientL2BoundedRightAction F G T i hi I e hc`, the action that
   `haarQuotientL2IntegratedAction` integrates. It is right translation
   of `L²` classes, which preserves the quotient measure by
   `haarQuotientMeasureRightInvariant F G T i hi I e hc`; this right
   invariance again uses `hi I e hc` and the right invariance of the Haar
   measure on `normOneSubgroup F G`. Its norm preservation is
   `norm_haarQuotientL2BoundedRightAction`, and almost-everywhere strong
   measurability of its orbits for the adelic Haar measure is
   `haarQuotientL2Orbit_aestronglyMeasurable`. The generic statement also
   asks that the action be right translation almost everywhere. By
   `coeFn_quotientL2RightTranslate`, applied with
   `haarQuotientMeasureRightInvariant`, the translate of `φ` by `a` agrees
   almost everywhere with `q ↦ φ (LeftCosetSpace.rightTranslate _ a q)`.
   On each coset `Hx` both this map and the generic
   `rightTranslate H a` give `Hxa`, so after induction on the quotient
   class the two functions agree by `rfl`.

4. **Conclusion.** With the hypothesis that
   `PositiveRationalQuotient F G T i` is compact (`hcompact`), the
   generic theorem yields, for compactly supported continuous `f₁ f₂`,
   integrability of the Haar convolution
   `g ↦ ∫ a, f₁ a * f₂ (a⁻¹ * g) ∂μG` and trace class of
   `integratedIsometricAction μG π _ _ (f₁ ⋆ f₂) _`. Since
   `haarQuotientL2IntegratedAction F G T i hi I e hc μG (f₁ ⋆ f₂) h` is by
   definition this same integrated operator, the generic conclusion is
   the statement's conclusion with no further rewriting.

The generic theorem itself obtains the result by writing the operator of
`f₁ ⋆ f₂` as the composition of the operators of `f₁` and `f₂`, which are
Hilbert–Schmidt on a compact quotient, and using that a product of two
Hilbert–Schmidt operators is trace class.

The proof uses only the standard axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and
`Quot.sound`.

## Correspondence with the source

J. R. Getz and H. Hahn, *An Introduction to Automorphic Representations*
(2024), registered as `getz-hahn-2024-automorphic-representations`,
SHA-256 `a125cf9c0f445972cbbd3552b4fb9905ca948108a05880f90eaa43558d09a507`.

- **Quotient.** Formulas (2.19)–(2.22), printed pp. 68–69 (PDF pp. 85–86),
  define `G(A_F)^1`, the group `A_G`, the adelic quotient
  `[G] = A_G G(F) \ G(A_F)` with its right action, and its comparison with
  `G(F) \ G(A_F)^1`. `PositiveRationalQuotient F G T i` is the Library's
  version of `[G]`: the left coset space of the subgroup generated by the
  image under `i` of the adelic points of `T` all of whose characters are
  positive scalar ideles (the role of `A_G`) and the rational points, with
  the right action of the adelic group.
- **Trace class.** The last paragraph of the proof of Lemma 9.3.3, printed
  pp. 247–248 (PDF pp. 259–260), writes a smooth compactly supported weight
  as a finite sum of convolutions `f₁ * f₂` and concludes that `R(f)` is a
  finite sum of products of two Hilbert–Schmidt operators, hence of trace
  class. This proof carries out that step for one convolution.

The differences from the source are the following.

- The quotient is assumed compact, and the Hilbert–Schmidt property of the
  factors comes from this compactness on the whole `L²` space, not from the
  estimate (9.13) of Lemma 9.3.3 on a closed invariant subspace. Neither
  reductivity nor connectedness of `G` is assumed, and compactness is not
  derived from anisotropy (compare Theorem 2.6.3 of the source).
- The weights are compactly supported continuous functions on the full
  adelic group `G(A_F)`, not smooth functions on `A_G \ G(A_F)`; no
  Dixmier–Malliavin factorization is used.
- The convolution and the integrated operator use the Library's left Haar
  measure `Measure.haar` on the adelic points and the quotient measure
  `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure`, with their normalizations as
  defined in the Library.
