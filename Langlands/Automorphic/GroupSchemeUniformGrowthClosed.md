# Closedness of the uniform-growth derivative realization

`derivativeCoordinates` assembles the weighted derivatives into the product of bounded-function spaces. For an open finite level its range is closed. Given a limit of coordinate vectors, multiply the limiting bounded functions by the common weight. Continuity of the weight converts uniform convergence of each bounded coordinate into local uniform convergence of the corresponding unweighted derivatives.

Rational invariance and right invariance under the fixed finite level pass to the pointwise limits. On each infinite slice the invariant derivative limit theorem identifies the original smoothness and the genuine directional derivatives. Algebra induction then identifies the full limiting enveloping action. Each limiting coordinate norm gives the required bound with the original growth exponent.

This is a derived closedness result for the uniform moderate growth spaces of Bernstein–Lapid, §7.1, used in the continuation formulation of their Corollary 8.6.
