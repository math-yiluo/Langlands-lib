# Cuspidal block slices after all strict refinements vanish

Every nontrivial maximal parabolic inside a block corresponds to
inserting one cut in the original ordered parabolic. The weight of that
cut agrees with the weight in the original two-block constant term.
The intrinsic integral comparison and the nested period identity
therefore show that this constant term of the slice vanishes whenever
the refined ambient term vanishes.

`blockShapeCuspidalLeviSliceFactor` is the one-coordinate Levi slice
with this proof of membership in the original continuous cuspidal
carrier. This implements the cuspidal vanishing in Langlands (1979),
Lemma 4 and conditions (a)–(b) in the proof of Proposition 2, printed
pp. 204–205 (Corvallis Part I, PDF pp. 199–200). The registered source is
`langlands-1979-notion-automorphic-representation`, SHA-256
`3b9fe0145da81d870ef8967be83635cd440a2581fe043e64e63324e22dec4db5`.
