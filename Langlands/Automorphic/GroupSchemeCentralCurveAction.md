# Central curves and their archimedean generators

Let an affine connected reductive group over a number field have its supplied
smooth archimedean atlas. `CentralCurveEnvelopingStatement` sends the real
tangent of a smooth central curve through identity into the center of the
actual complex enveloping algebra via the real-generator map. For a smooth
one-parameter subgroup, the scalar derivative along each right orbit is the
right-orbit derivative in that tangent direction.

In each closed local matrix representation, differentiate pointwise commutation of the
curve with an arbitrary group element at time zero. This gives commutation of its matrix
derivative with every represented point. Differentiating this second identity at the group
identity gives commutation of the two matrix differentials. The Lie bracket in the group
Lie algebra maps to the matrix commutator, and joint injectivity of the local realization
differentials gives zero Lie bracket. Commutation with the images of the real generators
then gives membership in the complex enveloping center. The orbit formula uses the chain
rule and the additive parameter law at the given time.

This supplies the infinitesimal action on the positive split factor in the reductive
decomposition used by Borel–Jacquet, *Automorphic forms and automorphic representations*
(1979), §1.6(1).
