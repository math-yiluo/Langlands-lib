# Joint admissibility of irreducible operator subquotients

`Langlands.Automorphic.irreducibleSubquotientAdmissibility` proves
`Langlands.Automorphic.IrreducibleSubquotientAdmissibilityStatement.{u}`.
For each number field in universe `u` and each natural rank `n`, the theorem
gives joint admissibility for every irreducible operator subquotient of the
automorphic forms on `GL_n`. The multiplicity spaces are those in the
published statement, for every open subgroup of the finite adelic group
and every representation of the standard archimedean compact group on
`Fin m → ℂ`. [Statement](AutomorphicAdmissibilityStatement.md)

The Lean proof applies
`irreducibleSubquotientAdmissibility_of_generatedFormAdmissibility`
to `generatedFormAdmissibility`. The reduction chooses a nonzero vector in
the irreducible quotient and lifts it to an automorphic form in the upper
realization. The operator realization generated by that form maps onto the
quotient. Each multiplicity map in the quotient lifts to the generated
realization after restricting to a compact open subgroup contained in the
given open subgroup. The lift uses compact Maschke for equivariance at
infinity and finite-orbit averaging for fixed vectors at the finite adeles.
The quotient multiplicity space lies in a linear image of the
finite-dimensional multiplicity space of the generated realization.
[Reduction](IrreducibleSubquotientAdmissibilityReduction.md)

A. Borel and H. Jacquet, *Automorphic forms and automorphic representations*,
Proc. Sympos. Pure Math. 33, Part 1, AMS, 1979, derive the admissibility of
irreducible automorphic subquotients in §4.6 from Proposition 4.5. Both
locators are on printed p. 196, PDF p. 191 of the registered Corvallis volume
`borel-jacquet-1979-automorphic`, SHA256
`3b9fe0145da81d870ef8967be83635cd440a2581fe043e64e63324e22dec4db5`.
