# The actual operator action on sums of block-centre translates of constant terms

`realizedBlockConstantTerm` takes the actual normalized block period of a vector in an operator realization. The translated space consists of finite sums of its left block-centre translates. A finite or compact right translation of the represented sum agrees with applying the original right translation to the coefficient forms.

For a real Lie direction, differentiation of the actual period along its one-parameter subgroup gives the period of the original differential. Taking a finite sum shows that the corresponding differential preserves the kernel of evaluation. Complex linearity extends kernel stability to complex Lie generators. The enveloping algebra and the full automorphic operator algebra are generated by the displayed operators, so the entire action descends. This constructs `translatedBlockTermOperatorAction` on the space of actual functions.

Every vector in this space has a finite-dimensional orbit under the separate block scalar ideles. This applies the finite full constant-term orbit theorem to each summand. The resulting operator space supplies the actual-function input for Langlands's central-character argument in *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), printed pp. 204–205.

[Operators on translated block constant terms](TranslatedBlockConstantTermOperators.md) constructs the quotient and its coefficient action. The orbit-finiteness theorem in this module applies to that quotient.
