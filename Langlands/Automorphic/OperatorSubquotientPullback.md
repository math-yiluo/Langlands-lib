# Descending a quotient from a common operator algebra

`freeOperatorProjection` evaluates the free complex algebra on finite adelic
translations, infinite compact translations and enveloping operators in the
actual algebra acting on `StableAutomorphicFunctions`. Its range is the
algebra generated by those operators, so it is surjective.

`exists_irreducibleOperatorSubquotient_of_pullback` uses an irreducible
quotient of a stable form subspace for an operator monoid mapping onto the
concrete operators. The numerator and the kernel are invariant under the
concrete operators because every concrete operator has a lift. The kernel
quotient equivalence intertwines the lifted operators. Irreducibility
descends along the surjection, and the supplied finite translation lifts
give an equivalence of finite adelic representations.

This is an internal algebraic construction for the final subquotient step
of Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979),
Proposition 2 and its proof, printed pp. 204–206 (Corvallis Part I PDF
pp. 199–201). The common algebra permits the action on families of induced
sections to be compared with the action on their Eisenstein coefficients.
