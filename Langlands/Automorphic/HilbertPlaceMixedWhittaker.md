# Comparing mixed Whittaker restrictions on a single Hilbert place component

Suppose a local general linear group over the real or complex numbers
acts on an actual place closure with its compatible fixed-level smooth
map. Two global mixed functionals have the same local Whittaker character
along this place. Each is computed at the specified finite level by a
continuous functional of the ambient archimedean smooth vector. Assume
local unitary Whittaker uniqueness and that the first functional is
nonzero on the original algebraic component.

The restrictions of the two functionals to the algebraic component are
proportional. The proof pulls back both evaluations to the same genuine
smooth space via its fixed Hilbert level. The pullback preserves
continuity and the local character. The embedded original vectors are
smooth by the Hilbert realization data and their images in the mixed
space agree with the given algebraic map by equality of underlying
Hilbert vectors. The local proportionality theorem then applies and its
identity restricts to the original algebraic vectors.

This internal comparison uses the conditional unitary uniqueness
statement and the fixed-level smooth map on the actual image closure.
