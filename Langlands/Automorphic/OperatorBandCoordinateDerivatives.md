# Genuine derivatives in the actual band coordinates

The original operator realization has a smooth archimedean slice at
each finite coordinate. The published enveloping action commutes with
forming that slice. Its real basis generators differentiate the slice
in the exact directions obtained by left multiplication of the
ambient real matrix basis.

Apply the compact coordinate derivative bound to the family of
original slices indexed by enveloping operators. Its frame rule is
the generator identity composed with the given operator. Evaluating
the bound at the identity operator yields the claimed estimate for
the original slice, uniformly over the finite coordinate and the
original vector. This comparison is an internal step in the direct
Sobolev estimate on the determinant band.
