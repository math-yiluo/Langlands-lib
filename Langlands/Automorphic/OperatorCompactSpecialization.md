# Compact projection for simple operator specializations

`operatorNumeratorQuotient` regards a complex intertwining map from an invariant numerator as a linear map for the acting algebra. A quotient in this form can be used in the compact-projected specialization of Eisenstein series.

For `exists_jointCompactProjection_of_operatorSpecialization`, restrict the finite and compact actions to the invariant numerator. Smoothness passes through its inclusion and quotient maps. Compact semisimplicity and finite-dimensionality of the complex span of each compact orbit also pass to the numerator and quotient. Choose a nonzero quotient vector of an irreducible compact type and a compact open finite level in the initial level. Naturality of projection gives a lift inside the numerator over the same vector. Joint admissibility of the source makes the range of the projection finite-dimensional. Independence of the two compact actions from the parameter implements the projection on each finite-dimensional source subspace by one algebra element.

This is a derived algebraic step for the compact-type reduction in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), proof of Proposition 2, printed p. 206, Corvallis Part I PDF p. 201.
