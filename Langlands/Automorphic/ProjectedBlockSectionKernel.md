# Full operator stability of a projected block section kernel

On an algebra module for the concrete automorphic operators, fix a linear
evaluation of projected coefficients at the identity. Its section kernel
consists of the vectors for which this evaluation is zero after every
standard compact and finite adelic translation. The two groups preserve
this kernel because their actions commute.

At a translated evaluation, a real Lie derivative moves to the base
evaluation by commutation with finite translations and conjugation by
the compact argument. Decompose the resulting direction into the
actual block Lie directions, the strict upper block direction and the
compact direction. The base evaluation kills the upper block term by
hypothesis, and its kernel is stable under each block Lie direction by
hypothesis. For the compact direction, pair the evaluation against an
arbitrary algebraic linear functional and differentiate along a compact
curve. The scalar curve vanishes on a vector of the section kernel, so
its derivative also vanishes. Algebraic linear functionals separate
the projected coefficients.

This gives stability under all real Lie generators. Scalar extension
and the enveloping algebra give stability under every differential
operator. The resulting subrepresentation is stable under the entire
concrete operator algebra. The argument takes compact differentiation
against linear functionals on the chosen carrier and applies to the
quotient carriers used in the constant-term argument of Task 6823.
