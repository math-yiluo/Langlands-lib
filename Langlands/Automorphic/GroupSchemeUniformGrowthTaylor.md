# Taylor coefficients in the actual uniform moderate growth space

At an open finite level, the product of the weighted derivative maps induces the growth topology and has closed range. For a weakly holomorphic complex curve, each weighted coordinate is analytic in its Banach norm. The first derivative therefore exists in the product as the limit of the difference quotients. Closedness places that derivative in the original growth space. Convergence in the defining topology shows that any continuous linear scalar test commutes with this derivative.

Repeating the argument for the derivative curve gives `taylorCoefficient`, with factorial normalization. Each coefficient has the original growth exponent and finite level. `apply_taylorCoefficient` identifies its value under every continuous linear scalar test, including evaluation of any genuine enveloping derivative at an adelic point.

This is the Taylor extraction in the uniform moderate growth topology used in the Bernstein–Lapid continuation formulation, §7.1 and Corollary 8.6. It supplies the smooth, growth-bounded Taylor vectors for the derivative argument in Langlands' proof of Proposition 2.
