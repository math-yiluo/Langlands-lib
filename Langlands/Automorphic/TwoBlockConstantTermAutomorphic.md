# Automorphic lifts of maximal-block constant-term slices

Each lift takes a cofinite ideal in the varying block's enveloping center. Its embedded operators must belong to the sum of the ambient center ideal's right span and the upper radical's right span. Centrality makes the first span annihilate each original form. The upper radical differentiation identity makes the second span vanish after constant-term evaluation on the Levi. The enveloping identity of the finite-smooth slice therefore makes the lower-rank operators annihilate the slice at every finite argument.

The lift uses the restricted compact representation on the same finite-dimensional source. Equivariance places every compact orbit of a slice value inside the range of the continuous slice map from that source, so this orbit spans a finite-dimensional space. Finite-level invariance, smoothness, polynomial growth in the lower-rank height, and the cofinite annihilator give an actual automorphic form for every source vector. Linearity and equivariance lift through the faithful inclusion of forms into continuous functions. The resulting intertwiner belongs to one specified lower-rank annihilated multiplicity space for every original intertwiner and every value of the fixed Levi argument.

The operator criterion and the use of the resulting Levi forms are from Harish-Chandra, *Automorphic forms on semisimple Lie groups* (1968), Chapter I, §6, proof of Theorem 1, printed pp. 19–20, PDF pp. 29–30. The adelic form and fixed-level multiplicity conditions are those of Borel–Jacquet, *Automorphic forms and automorphic representations* (1979), §§4.1–4.3, printed pp. 194–195, PDF pp. 189–190.
