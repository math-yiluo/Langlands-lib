# CM conjugation of the concrete automorphic operator algebra

Worker 2. This internal construction conjugates the existing actual operators by
the argument pullback constructed in `SmoothArithmeticActions`. Its differential
calculation uses the proved arithmetic chain rule and enveloping covariance in
`CenterFiniteArithmeticTransport`. It introduces no original-literature
identification or assumed preservation theorem.

For an actual CM number field `K : Type uK` and every natural rank `n`, write
`C = finiteSmoothComplexConj n`. This is the continuous complex-linear
involution on the existing common-finite-stabilizer function space. Complex
values are unchanged. The first lemmas prove that its inverse equals itself
and specialize the existing covariance to the actual finite and standard
infinite compact operators and to the genuine enveloping action.

`finiteSmoothCMConjugation` is the complex algebra automorphism of the actual
endomorphism algebra given by `A ↦ C⁻¹ A C`. The generator formulas are

\[
 R_f(d)\longmapsto R_f(c(d)),\qquad
 R_K(k)\longmapsto R_K(c(k)),\qquad
 D(u)\longmapsto D(c_U(u)).
\]

Here `c(d)` and `c(k)` are the existing arithmetic CM matrix maps, and `c_U`
is `ArchimedeanLieTransport.envelopingEquiv` applied to the actual CM field
involution. It fixes the complex coefficient field. Each formula follows
by applying the injective pullback to the desired equality and using the
corresponding covariance.

Conjugation is involutive because `C` is. Induction through the algebra
generated by these three operator families proves that their existing
concrete subalgebra is preserved. Consequently `cmOperatorConjugation` is
an actual involutive complex algebra automorphism of
`automorphicOperatorAlgebra K n`; its inverse is the same map. Its generator
formulas and underlying endomorphism formula are proved. The final identity
is the whole-algebra covariance

\[
 A(Cf)=C((c_A A)f).
\]

This unit does not require compactness of the additive adele quotient. It
does not assert that all automorphic forms are closed under compact
translations or differentials, nor that the stable core equals all forms.
