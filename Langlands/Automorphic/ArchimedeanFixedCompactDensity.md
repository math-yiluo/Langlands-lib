# Compact-finite derivative density at a fixed adelic level

The Library's complexification of the infinite adelic matrix group restricts
to a faithful representation of the standard compact subgroup. Passing from
matrices to linear maps gives the action on a finite complex coordinate
space. Continuity of each orbit follows from the finite matrix-vector sum.

The bounded restrictions of a full adelic action to the two adelic factors
commute. At a subgroup fixed level, the inherited infinite action therefore
preserves the closed fixed subspace. The generic compact-finite density
theorem applies there with probability Haar measure and the faithful
coordinate representation. The subspace inclusion intertwines the compact
action and carries finite orbit spans to finite orbit spans. The fixed
derivative equivalence transports density to the ambient derivative
subspace topology.

The theorem `dense_compactFinite_fixedSmooth` gives this internal
specialization for a full bounded adelic action on a complex Hilbert space
and a supplied compatible compact representation. It is used to recover
the original finite-level carrier in the cuspidal application.
