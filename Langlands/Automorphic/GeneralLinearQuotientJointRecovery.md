# Joint-vector recovery for represented forms on the positive quotient

`Langlands.Automorphic.GroupSchemeFunctions.GeneralLinearQuotientJointRecoveryStatement`
and its proof `generalLinearQuotientJointRecovery` concern the original
positive-central rational quotient of `GL_{r+1}` over a number field `F`. The
proposition is fully quantified, in the style of the published realization
statements. It quantifies over the number field with compact additive adele
quotient and the central split torus data: `T`, `i`, injective character
restriction `hi` with finite-index image, finite `I`, the splitting `e` and
centrality `hc`. It also quantifies over right invariance of norm-one Haar
measure. The proof uses these hypotheses for the canonical bounded right action
and for full support of the quotient Haar measure. This corrects the rejected
candidate `825af1d6-cb0f-4a35-82c6-b82d1709040a`. That proposition omitted the
finite-index and Haar-invariance hypotheses its proof used (operator ruling 8).

Let `R₁` and `R₂` be operator realizations of rank `r + 1`, with `R₁` jointly
admissible for its finite adelic and standard archimedean compact actions.
Let `j₁`, `j₂` be complex-linear maps to quotient `L²` that send every realized
vector to its actual form class (`IsGeneralLinearFormClass`). If
`range j₂ ⊆ closure (range j₁)`, then `range j₂ ⊆ range j₁`. No irreducibility,
cuspidality or central character is assumed for either realization.

The module also proves `generalLinearFormClass_form_eq`: one quotient `L²` class
is the form class of at most one continuous matrix-model function.

## Implemented proof

Faithfulness: two continuous quotient functions equal almost everywhere for
the canonical quotient Haar measure are equal, because that measure is
positive on nonempty open sets (`haarQuotientMeasure_isOpenPosMeasure`). Hence
`generalLinearFormClass_form_eq` holds, and `j₁` is injective since realized
vectors are determined by their continuous functions.

Let `H` be the closure of `range j₁`. By `generalLinearQuotientFormClassClosure`
it is invariant under the full bounded adelic right action. Restricting that
action to `H` and composing with the matrix-model inclusions of the finite
adelic group and of the standard compact subgroup gives two representations
`σ` and `τ` on `H` that preserve the inner product. The bounded quotient
translations are linear isometries. By `generalLinear_formClass_finiteRepresentation`
and `generalLinear_formClass_compactRepresentation`, the corestrictions of `j₁`
and `j₂` to `H` intertwine the finite and compact actions of `R₁` and `R₂` with
`σ` and `τ`.

A private generic lemma does the representation theory. The Hilbert inner
product of `H` is a `σ`-invariant positive form. Pulled back along the injective
corestriction of `j₁`, it is `InvariantPositiveForm.comap`, and it is also
compact invariant. The corestriction of `j₁` has dense range by
`rangeClosureEmbedding_denseRange`. The published
`Langlands.jointVectorRecovery_of_dense` then shows that every finite-smooth and
compact-finite vector of `H` is in the image of `R₁`. It uses joint admissibility
of `R₁`, its commuting smooth finite action and finite compact action, and
cofinality of compact open subgroups. The image of a vector of `R₂` is such a
vector, because finite smoothness and compact finiteness transfer along
intertwining maps. Hence it equals the class of some vector of `R₁`.

## Provenance and scope

This is a derived step for the summand-to-realization direction of Getz–Hahn,
*An Introduction to Automorphic Representations*, Springer GTM 300 (2024),
Theorem 9.7.3, pp. 265–267 (PDF pp. 277–279). There, (9.40) identifies the
cusp forms in an irreducible summand with its `K`-finite vectors. Here the
corresponding recovery is applied to class images of actual operator
realizations on the original quotient. Joint admissibility of `R₁` is a
hypothesis. For a realization generated by one automorphic form it is supplied
in the Library by `generatedFormAdmissibility_of_harishChandraFiniteness` with
`harishChandraFiniteness`. This module does not prove density of stable form
classes in a summand or the algebraic irreducibility statement itself.
