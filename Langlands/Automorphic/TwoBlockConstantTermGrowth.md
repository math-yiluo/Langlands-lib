# Polynomial growth of the adelic two-block constant term

Harish-Chandra, *Automorphic forms on semisimple Lie groups* (1968),
Chapter I, §6, proof of Theorem 1, printed pp. 19–20 (PDF pp. 29–30),
uses constant terms on the Levi in the rank induction. This module
proves polynomial growth of the adelic block constant term in the
ambient infinite factor, at each fixed finite argument.

A compact set of representatives of the matrix quotient is obtained
by taking compact neighborhoods of lifts and extracting a finite
subcover of their open quotient neighborhoods. The block unipotents
of these representatives, followed by the fixed finite argument,
lie in a compact set in the adelic group. Its finite components
meet finitely many cosets of any open level fixing the source
function. The maximum of the exponents and the sum of the constants
on the corresponding source slices bound the source integrand.

The infinite components of the same compact set have bounded matrix
height. Submultiplicativity of the height gives a bound polynomial
in the infinite argument. The probability Haar integral preserves
that pointwise bound.
