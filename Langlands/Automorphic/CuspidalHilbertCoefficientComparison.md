# Analytic comparison of infinite Hilbert coefficients

A smooth joint-finite scalar function on the actual infinite matrix group is
determined by its genuine enveloping derivatives at the standard compact
subgroup. For the difference of two such functions, the enveloping derivatives
at a compact representative of a component give zero matrix-chart Taylor
jets. The published joint-finite analyticity and analytic jet uniqueness
theorems give equality throughout that component.

`JointFiniteCoefficientLifts` records smooth joint-finite scalar functions
that compute the Hilbert orbit coefficients of the given source vectors and
carry the prescribed differential action. The arbitrary Hilbert test vector
may be mapped into a different coefficient space if the scalar values are
identified. For a bounded extension `L`, test the source action against
`L.adjoint w` and the target action against `w`. Compact covariance identifies
the two scalar values on the given source vectors and differential covariance
does so for every enveloping derivative. Analytic equality on the infinite
group, Hilbert inner product separation and density of the given source vectors
give the infinite intertwining identity on the completions. Finite covariance
also extends by density. Each adelic group element is the product of its
infinite and finite parts, so the two identities give full covariance.

This is an internal consequence of the published compact component coverage,
joint-finite analyticity and jet comparison results.
