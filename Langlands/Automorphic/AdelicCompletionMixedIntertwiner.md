# Full adelic intertwiners on the mixed source maps

A continuous linear map between two adelic form completions that intertwines the adelic operators also intertwines the finite and archimedean coordinate actions. Apply the mixed smooth map for these two restrictions. Its Hilbert value is the original continuous linear map.

If that map extends a map of original source vectors, the two mixed source vectors agree by equality of their underlying Hilbert vectors. This internal compatibility allows the two original Whittaker functionals to be compared through the Hilbert extension of an operator equivalence.
