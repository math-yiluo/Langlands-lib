# Enveloping operators on archimedean smooth Hilbert vectors

A continuous representation of the units of the mixed archimedean matrix algebra has the Library's genuine smooth-vector carrier. Its directional derivative satisfies the real Lie bracket law. Complex extension and the universal enveloping property then give an action of the actual archimedean enveloping algebra, with the original derivative on every real generator.

For a unitary action, differentiating the constant inner product of two translated vectors shows that each real generator is skew-symmetric. Induction on the enveloping algebra gives a formal adjoint on the same carrier. Addition adds the adjoints, complex scaling conjugates the scalar, and left multiplication by a real generator moves the negative generator to the right of the adjoint. These identities supply the pairing used in the Library's graph argument for unitary representations.

This is an internal step in the formalization of the smooth vectors in Getz and Hahn, *An Introduction to Automorphic Representations* (2024), Lemma 9.7.2. The first-order pairing follows the Library's unitary matrix Casimir proof, now for the mixed number-field matrix algebra.
