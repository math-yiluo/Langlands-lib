# Fixed-level differentiability on the actual norm-one adelic group

**Sources.** J. Arthur, *An introduction to the trace formula* (2005), §13, the definitions of `C^∞` and `C^r(G(F) \ G(𝔸_F)¹ / K₀)` and left-invariant differential operators, printed pp. 70–71 (PDF pp. 82–83), `arthur-ellwood-kottwitz-2005-harmonic-analysis-shimura`. See also Arthur, *A trace formula for reductive groups II* (1980), §1, printed pp. 93–95 (PDF pp. 8–10), `arthur-1980-trace-formula-reductive-groups-II`, where the operators in Lemma 1.4 come from words in the complexified enveloping algebra. The field `F` here is a number field and the infinite factor is the product over its archimedean places.

All declarations are in `Langlands.Automorphic.GroupSchemeFunctions`.

- `infiniteNormOneSubgroup` is the preimage of the existing `normOneSubgroup F G` along the actual [infinite-factor inclusion](../Global/AdelicGroupProduct.md). It is closed because the inclusion is continuous and the ambient norm-one subgroup is closed. `infiniteNormOneInclusion` maps it into the actual full norm-one group. The two fibre lemmas show that its positive right orbit through a norm-one point `x` is exactly the set of norm-one points with the same finite component as `x`: for the converse the ratio `x⁻¹y` has trivial finite component.
- `finiteLevelInclusion` sends a compact subgroup `K₀` of the finite factor into `G(𝔸_F)¹`. The image under `ofFinite` is compact, so the logarithms of the ambient character norms vanish on it. This action on norm-one points is by right multiplication, not an independent action on a different quotient.
- `NormOneLieAtlas` specifies the induced Lie structure by a model `Fin e → ℝ`, compatible smooth group operations in the inherited topology, and a smooth immersion of the subgroup into the published infinite adelic manifold. Here the smooth structure is supplied as data on the **actual closed subgroup**, not on a substitute abstract group. The unit specifies the data but does not prove its existence.
- `IsNormOneCr` is the pullback condition on functions of norm-one adelic points: left invariance under the actual principal subgroup, right invariance under the included compact `K₀`, and `ContMDiff` of every slice `a ↦ f(xa)` through a fixed finite component. The intended levels are open and compact; openness is a hypothesis on the chosen level in the applications. Smoothness uses order `∞` for the same condition. This does not impose an algebraic tensor decomposition or require `f` to have been defined on the full adelic group.
- `NormOneDifferentialWord` uses finite complex combinations of words in the coordinate basis of the real identity tangent space, with the empty word the identity. `OrderLE` bounds the length of every word with nonzero coefficient. `normOneDifferential` applies the published `lieWordAction` to `a ↦ f(xa)` at the identity of the infinite norm-one group. This is the positive right orbit, hence yields **left-invariant** derivatives, as in Arthur. The word presentation describes differential operators without identifying different words representing the same operator in the enveloping algebra. A differential word of length at most `r` is meant for inputs of class `C^r` or higher; the total Lean operation uses the ordinary manifold derivative and does not by itself assert differentiability of its inputs.

This is a definition and comparison unit for Proposition 13.2. It does not assert the truncation estimates or construct an embedded Lie atlas for every reductive group.
