# Smooth fixed-level embeddings of the actual cuspidal tensor slices

Let \(F\) be a number field with compact additive adele quotient. Take an
actual cuspidal operator realization of \(\mathrm{GL}_{r+1}\) over \(F\),
a supplied continuous unitary character acting as its central character,
and a supplied unitary form whose inner product is the canonical
determinant-band integral.

In the chosen cuspidal tensor decomposition, fix a nonzero finite vector
and a nonzero vector at every infinite place other than the selected
one. The remaining component embeds in the original cuspidal realization
as a single-place slice. In the Hilbert completion for the supplied form,
the slice's image closure has the actual compatible local Hilbert
representation. There is a compact open subgroup of the finite adelic
group such that the closure's genuine local smooth vectors map by
inclusion into the genuine global smooth vectors of the fixed Hilbert
subspace. The map is complex-linear and continuous for the genuine
word-derivative topologies.

The algebraic slice theorem supplies irreducibility, admissibility and
a common compact open stabilizer. The fixing identity holds on the
whole linear range of the slice by its definition. The actual cuspidal
fixed-level comparison then gives the asserted map in the real and
complex cases. The map allows the global Whittaker functional to be
restricted continuously to the local smooth vectors in the
multiplicity-one argument. The result is an internal consequence of the
algebraic slice and Hilbert fixed-level comparisons.
