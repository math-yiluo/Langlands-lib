# Operator identities for translated Levi coefficients

Each translated period is smooth under the finite adelic action. The translates of original realized forms span the quotient, and the open-subgroup fixed-vector condition passes through each intertwiner and is closed under finite linear combinations.

The actual right action of a finite or compact operator in a block agrees with the corresponding ambient block operator on the slice. The real Lie action has the same compatibility because both derivatives are taken along the same curve in the adelic Levi. A strictly upper block real derivative restricts to zero by the corresponding result for each original period in a representative finite sum. Finally, the finite parabolic acts on the evaluated coefficient through right translation by its actual finite Levi projection.

These are derived identities for the coefficient construction in the proof of `CuspidalSupportExistenceStatement`, using the normalized period of Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Lemma 4 and the argument preceding Lemma 6, printed pp. 204–205.

The ambient finite action and its smoothness come from [Actions on translated block terms](TranslatedBlockTermAction.md).
