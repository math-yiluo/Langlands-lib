# Normalized parabolic vanishing in the finite-smooth form space

The ambient space is the broader finite-smooth construction in
`GroupSchemeForms`: actual continuous rational-invariant adelic
functions with smooth infinite slices, a common finite open stabilizer,
full adelic growth and whole-function compact and center finiteness.
Connected reductivity, a specified closed positive-dimensional matrix
embedding, a specified maximal compact infinite subgroup and the exact
scalar-commutator premise are retained.

The literal GH smooth carrier has an additional distinction: (6.1),
printed p.158 (physical PDF p.172), explicitly uses an algebraic tensor
product and thus finite sums of separated infinite and finite functions.
The carrier here has no proved global finite-tensor condition. This
module therefore extends the parabolic vanishing construction to the
implemented broader form space; it does not identify that space or its
vanishing subspace with the literal GH form or cusp-form space.

`adelicCuspidalSubrepresentation` imposes vanishing of the actual
mass-one constant term for every proper rational parabolic and every
adelic argument. The intrinsic full radical family retains its explicit
`ParabolicDynamicStatement` prerequisite. Measures are constructed on
the actual compact unipotent rational quotients; none is supplied as an
arbitrary family. Compactness, continuity and the probability law prove
absolute Bochner convergence separately from vanishing.

The vanishing quantifiers follow GH Definition 6.5.1 and (6.18), printed
p.167 (physical PDF p.181), applied here to the specified broader carrier.
Finite adelic translation preserves the conditions, its restricted
action is smooth, every vector has actual compact finite level, and
inclusions into the broader form space and original continuous
functions are injective and equivariant. The optional uniform-growth
refinement uses the same carrier and extended one-exponent bounds.

Every genuine initial Haar on a radical gives the same normalized
constant-term value, by equality of the normalized measures. This
supports the mass-one convention in Moeglin--Waldspurger I.1.13, first
paragraph, printed p.18, chapter
`05.1_pp_1_18_Hypotheses_and_general_notation.pdf`.

This is a derived intersection and inclusion construction. Literal GH
tensor comparison, automatic square integrability or positive-center
descent, full almost-everywhere cuspidal L2 identification, Hilbert
closedness/density, standard-parabolic reduction, Levi-valued automorphy
and automatic uniform growth remain separate obligations.
