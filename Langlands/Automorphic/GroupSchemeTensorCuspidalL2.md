# Tensor-smooth cusp forms in the actual quotient L2

This module retains the literal algebraic-tensor function condition in
GH (6.1), printed p.158 (PDF p.172), through the all-proper-parabolic
cusp construction and its natural actual quotient L2 realization. The
input form restriction is `tensorAdelicAutomorphicSubrepresentation`;
its exact full compact, center and growth conditions are proved in
`GroupSchemeTensorForms`.

The tensor cusp subrepresentation imposes vanishing of the actual
mass-one constant term for every proper rational parabolic, as in
GH Definition 6.5.1 and (6.18), printed pp.167–168 (PDF pp.181–182).
It has a faithful equivariant inclusion into the earlier broader cusp
space, preserving the original functions. Absolute convergence is
proved separately using the existing compact actual radical quotients,
finite Borel probability measures and continuity; vanishing is not
used to infer convergence. Actual compact finite level is inherited
on the identical functions.

On the actual positive rational quotient, tensor membership of the
original continuous pullback is imposed in addition to the earlier
form L2 conditions. The membership theorem is proved equivalent to
existence of an original tensor cusp form equal to that pullback at
every adelic point, together with genuine `MemLp` at exponent two.
Positive-center descent and square integrability therefore remain
separate explicit conditions.

The resulting finite action maps equivariantly into the unchanged
earlier quotient cusp-form L2 realization and thence into the actual
quotient `Lp` space. The representative agrees almost everywhere with
the original continuous quotient function; full support makes the map
injective. Its range is the algebraic invariant image in that actual
space, with no Hilbert-closedness assertion.

The original proper-parabolic dynamic theorem, smooth dimension,
differential commutator, connected reductivity, closed representation,
infinite maximal compact and positive matrix dimension remain
explicit. The quotient measure and its right invariance are explicit,
and faithfulness requires full support. This module proves neither
automatic L2 integrability nor positive-center descent, archimedean
enveloping closure, equality with the broader cusp space or full
Hilbert-cuspidal closedness.
