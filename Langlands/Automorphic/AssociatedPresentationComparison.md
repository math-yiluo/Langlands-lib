# Local comparison across actual cuspidal presentations

This supporting module of the gamma comparison for weakly associated cuspidal pairs (module `Langlands.Automorphic.AssociatedPresentationComparison`) proves that two supplied spectral presentations of the same actual cuspidal operator realization have equivalent local representations at a place where both local components are irreducible. The presentation universes are independent. It also proves equality of their smooth isomorphism classes and, when the two components are additionally admissible, of their classes in the canonical p-adic category. These are helper results for the presentation quantifiers of `AssociatedGammaStatement`; they do not prove that statement.

The proof uses the published `CompactSplitting.isotypicPart`, the sum of the images of all intertwiners from a fixed representation. The generic helpers show that this subspace is preserved by the group action and by postcomposition with an intertwiner. If an irreducible target is entirely of one irreducible source type, not every intertwiner can vanish. Mathlib's Schur lemma then makes a nonzero intertwiner bijective and supplies a representation equivalence.

For an actual operator realization, the local isotypic part is stable under every finite adelic translation. Given an adelic element, split off its component at the selected place. The remaining element projects to the identity there, so it commutes with the selected local group, as checked by all local projections. Its action is consequently a local intertwiner. The published compact and enveloping compatibility identities likewise make the infinite compact and enveloping actions local intertwiners. Algebra-adjoin induction extends stability under these three operator families to the whole concrete automorphic operator algebra.

Full operator irreducibility therefore makes any nonzero local isotypic part equal to the whole realization. An injective copy of an irreducible local representation makes its isotypic part nonzero. Pulling the resulting equality back along a second local injection, using the published `isotypicPart_comap_of_injective`, makes the second irreducible local representation entirely of the first type, and the generic equivalence criterion applies.

The actual-presentation adapters first embed each local component into the full finite image, using its inclusion in the presented finite slice. The published `localFormEquiv` identifies that image with the original full realization restricted to the local group. The resulting injections allow the algebraic comparison above to apply. The smooth and p-adic class equalities then follow from the published equivalence criteria for those classes. The p-adic conclusion retains the original actions and canonical topology.

These are internal deductions from the existing concrete definitions and Mathlib's irreducibility results, rather than a formalization of a separate literature theorem. They use no existence theorem for tensor presentations, local admissibility theorem, genericity result, or global functional equation. Both local irreducibility hypotheses remain essential premises of the comparison as implemented. In particular, the weak-association witness presentation in `AssociatedGammaStatement` has no supplied irreducibility hypotheses, so these lemmas alone do not transport its unramified spectra to every compared presentation.
