# The actual adelic completion and its operator comparisons

For one actual cuspidal operator realization with a specified unitary central
character, a supplied positive form and its determinant-band identity,
`adelicCompletion` packages the reconstructed adelic action and original
enveloping action. Its proof fields supply the joint-finite coefficient lifts
and compatibility with the original completed compact and finite operators.
The fields use the existing reconstructed action, coefficient lifts and
bounded compatibility theorem. This is an internal construction for applying
the bounded-extension criterion to two actual realizations.
