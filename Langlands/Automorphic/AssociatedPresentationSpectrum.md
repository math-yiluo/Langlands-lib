# Spherical spectra across actual cuspidal presentations

This supporting module of Task 5003 (module `Langlands.Automorphic.AssociatedPresentationSpectrum`, written by Worker 1679) extends the local comparison work to the unramified spectral data in a weak-association witness. Its generic helper transfers a scalar compact-open Hecke corner action from a smooth source to a smooth target when the sum of all source intertwiner images is the entire target. Neither source nor target is assumed irreducible.

The proof composes the target corner action, minus its prescribed scalar, with the actual normalized fixed-vector projection. Naturality of both operators makes this linear map vanish on every source intertwiner image, hence on their sum. Its vanishing on a target fixed vector gives the scalar identity because the projection fixes that vector. This is an internal deduction from the published actual Hecke action and projection identities.

For an actual full cuspidal realization, the sum of the intertwiner images of any presented local component is the whole local restriction. Its nonzeroness follows from the faithful local inclusion and the published nonzeroness of the component. Full operator irreducibility and the full stability lemma from `AssociatedPresentationComparison` then make the sum exhaustive; the source component need not be irreducible.

Applying the generic transfer theorem to the canonical spherical Hecke corner gives `localComponent_standardHeckeOperator_eq`: the witness component's scalar equations hold on every compared local component. Injectivity of the compared component's inclusion pulls the equations back from the full realization. Outside both finite exceptional sets, the compared presentation supplies a nonzero spherical fixed space, so `localComponent_hasSpectrum_of_other` transfers the whole spectrum relation.

`Unramified.eq_of_standardHeckeScalar_eq` extracts the numerical separation argument from the published proof in `Unramified.SphericalSpectrumUniquenessProof`. The canonical residue cardinality exceeds one, so the positive square root and every power of it are nonzero. Cancelling that normalization in the standard scalars gives equality of the signed Euler coefficients. Cancelling the powers of minus one gives equality of every coefficient through the rank; higher coefficients vanish by the published degree formula. The published injectivity of `Eigenvalues.eulerPolynomial` then gives equality of the spectra, including multiplicities. This numerical lemma has no representation input.

`LocalRealization.sphericalHeckeSpectrum_unique` compares the scalar equations on a single nonzero spherical vector and applies numerical separation. It requires neither irreducibility nor admissibility. The distinct name allows it to coexist with the published `eq_of_hasSphericalHeckeSpectrum`, which retains both hypotheses. Combining this with spectrum transfer proves `CuspidalSpectralPresentation.spectralSystem_eigenvalues_eq` at every place outside both presentations' exceptional sets. Consequently `spectralSystem_sameEulerPolynomials` holds, with pointwise equality wherever both systems are defined.

The relative Weil helper `IsWeaklyAssociated.of_sameEulerPolynomials` intersects the cofinite set of arithmetic matching places with the cofinite polynomial-equality set and the complement of the target exceptional set. It retains the original continuity, rank and local-map conditions and rewrites only the numerical Euler polynomial. Finally, `IsWeaklyAssociatedClass.presentation` obtains a witness presentation of the supplied actual representative through the published `isWeaklyAssociatedClass_isoClass_iff`, compares its spectra with any supplied presentation, and transports weak association.

The number-field universe, the relative Weil carrier universe, the witness-presentation universe and the compared-presentation universe are independent. No local component irreducibility or admissibility is added. These comparison and transport lemmas are internal deductions from the published actual operator, Hecke, spectral and association interfaces; they do not assert a separate literature theorem. This file does not prove gamma equality or assert the missing analytic prerequisites.

The numerical lemma `Langlands.Unramified.eq_of_standardHeckeScalar_eq` is reused from [TwistedHeckeScalars](../Representation/Unramified/TwistedHeckeScalars.md); this module does not redeclare it.
