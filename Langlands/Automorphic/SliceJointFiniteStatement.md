# Joint differential finiteness of actual automorphic slices

Author: Worker 94. Module:
`Langlands.Automorphic.SliceJointFiniteStatement`.
The proposition is
`Langlands.Automorphic.AutomorphicSliceJointFiniteStatement`.

For every number field `K : Type u` and every natural rank `n`, including zero,
the statement says that every `f : SmoothRationalFunctions K n` satisfying
the existing `IsAutomorphicForm K n f` has

```text
ArchimedeanGL.IsJointFinite K n (smoothInfiniteSlice K n b f)
```

for every finite adelic argument `b : FiniteAdelicGL K n`.
The actual slice is the smooth function
`a ↦ evaluate K n f.val (AdelicGL.pair K n a b)`.
The conclusion means finite complex dimension of its `jointOrbitSpan`,
the span of all

```text
envelopingAction K n (centerInclusion K n z * c.val)
  (smoothInfiniteSlice K n b f)
```

with `z : EnvelopingCenter K n` and
`c : compactEnvelopingSubalgebra K n`. These are the published genuine
differential actions. The compact algebra is the complex unital subalgebra
generated by the complexified skew-adjoint matrix directions.

## Unchanged automorphic hypothesis

The premise is exactly the predicate from `Automorphic.Forms`. It requires
finite adelic level, finite orbit under the actual standard infinite compact
subgroup, simultaneous center finiteness of the entire family of infinite
slices, and polynomial growth separately on each finite slice.
In particular, the single common finite-codimensional center ideal is retained
through the existing `Automorphic.IsCenterFinite` condition and its published
ideal characterization. Separate center finiteness of individual slices is
not substituted for that premise.

The conclusion is asserted separately for each slice. It does not assert
finite dimension of the ambient smooth-function space or a uniform bound on
the joint-orbit dimensions as `b` varies.

## Internal provenance and proof obligation

This is an internal composition target for published interfaces, rather than
a new transcription of a source theorem. Its definitions come from
`Automorphic.Forms`, `Automorphic.CenterFinite`, and
`Global.ArchimedeanJointFinite`; no new literature theorem is invoked.
The intended proof uses the following separately stated prerequisites:

1. `SliceFinitenessStatement` transports the unchanged simultaneous center
   condition to a slice's center orbit and the infinite-subgroup condition
   to that slice's smooth right-translation orbit. Apply the latter with
   `standardInfiniteCompact K n`.
2. `ArchimedeanGL.CompactGroupFiniteDifferentialStatement` converts this
   compact-group orbit finiteness to `IsCompactLieFinite` of the same slice.
3. `ArchimedeanGL.SeparateJointFiniteStatement` combines its center and
   compact differential finiteness to obtain `IsJointFinite`.

These names specify the proposed composition route. They are not hypotheses
of `AutomorphicSliceJointFiniteStatement`, and their definitions alone supply
no proof of it. The Lean file defines the proposition and checks its full
parameter telescope; proving the implication remains a separate obligation.
Finite level and polynomial growth remain in the automorphic premise even
though the proposed finiteness argument only needs its compact and center
parts.

No reproducing kernel, convolution approximation, analytic regularity, or
polynomial derivative-growth theorem is asserted here. The parent
`AutomorphicDifferentialGrowthStatement` and all its imports remain unchanged.
