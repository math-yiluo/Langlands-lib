# Conjugation fixes the center of the adelic enveloping algebra

`adelicEnvelopingCenterConjugation` proves `AdelicEnvelopingCenterConjugationStatement` for the original adelic tangent Lie algebra and every supplied smooth relative dimension. The result gives the pointwise invariance used in Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series*, §8.1, proof of Lemma 8.1, pp. 221–222, where the finite-dimensional solution space is asserted to be invariant under the enveloping center. The registered PDF is `bernstein-lapid-2024-meromorphic-continuation-eisenstein` with SHA-256 `6acbe78adf3a7ddff4be0caa92b169f0e609d1245f674a71fbc6989d6aef9268`.

The proof chooses closed matrix representations of the archimedean base changes. Their combined differential at the identity is injective by `eq_zero_of_zeroDeriv_realization`. A continuous real-linear left inverse then expresses each tangent adjoint coefficient as a polynomial in the entries and inverse entries of the realized point. Differentiating at the identity gives the Lie bracket by `LinearAdjoint.mfderiv_conjugation_apply`.

The finite expansions in `EnvelopingAdjointOrbit` express scalar tests of the enveloping adjoint orbit in the same coefficient algebra. They differentiate by a commutator in the enveloping algebra, so for a central element all these tests are constant near the identity. On each factor of the completion product they are real regular functions on the corresponding real Weil restriction. This restriction is connected reductive and unirational. Zariski density extends the equality from the real open neighbourhood to every point of the factor, and linear functionals separate elements of the enveloping algebra. The factor subgroups generate the finite completion product, so multiplicativity of the adjoint action gives the result on the full infinite adelic point group.
