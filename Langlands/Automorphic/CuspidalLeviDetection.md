# Detection by products of cuspidal block forms

This is a compact-test form of Lemma 5 of Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), printed p. 204, Corvallis Part I PDF p. 199. The hypothesis tests the actual block Levi restriction at all ambient arguments. The test functions are products of genuine cuspidal automorphic forms on the original blocks, and the pairing is the Haar integral against the conjugate of the product on each compact set in the Levi.

For a nonzero realized form, the empty-cut constant term supplies a nonzero term. Choose a cut set of maximal cardinality with nonzero term. Every strict refinement vanishes, so its one-block slices are cuspidal. Archimedean Iwasawa decomposition finds a point with nonzero Levi value at a compact and finite right translate. Fixing all the other coordinates at this point gives one nonzero cuspidal automorphic block form in each coordinate. Their product is a test function with nonzero value at the point. The product of the period with the conjugate test is continuous. A continuous complex function with this property has a nonzero integral over some compact set, by rotating its value to have positive real part locally and using a compact neighbourhood of positive Haar measure.
