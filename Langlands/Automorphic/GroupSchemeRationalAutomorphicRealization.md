# Realizing rational-invariant automorphic functions on the smooth carrier

The unbundled automorphy predicate on the actual rational adelic quotient gives a continuous rational-invariant function on the published carrier. Its infinite slices are plot smooth, and a single open finite subgroup fixes it, giving a vector in the finite plot-smooth carrier.

Evaluation injects this carrier into the space of functions and intertwines right translates. It therefore identifies the infinite compact orbit span with the prescribed finite-dimensional span of translated functions. The finite-codimensional central ideal kills the vector because it kills every infinite slice and the slice maps intertwine the central action and separate vectors. Moderate growth is the same pointwise bound under evaluation.

This comparison is internal to the two existing Lean representations of automorphic forms on the same rational quotient.
