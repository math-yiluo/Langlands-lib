# Embedding independence of the original automorphic and cuspidal form spaces

Fix a number field `F`, an actual affine group scheme `G`, a specified
infinite subgroup `Kinf` with its maximal-compact premise, a connected-reductive
premise, and one smooth atlas `hsmooth` with its scalar commutator premise
`hcalc`. Let `rho` and `sigma` be two positive-rank scheme-theoretic closed
matrix embeddings of `G`. All data except the matrix embedding and its rank
are held fixed.

The module proves equality of the existing `adelicAutomorphicSubrepresentation`
for the two embeddings. Its compact-finiteness and enveloping-center-finiteness
factors are literally unchanged. The moderate-growth factors are equivalent
by the published `closedImmersionAdelicGrowthIndependence` theorem on the
same original continuous rational-invariant function. The uniform refinement
also agrees: the uniform part of that theorem keeps the common exponent
outside the quantifier over the original enveloping algebra. The literal
tensor-smooth form restriction agrees because its tensor-membership factor
is independent of the embedding.

With the existing locally-finite-type instance and one fixed
`ParabolicDynamicStatement`, the module proves equality of the existing
cuspidal, uniform cuspidal and tensor cuspidal subrepresentations. Each uses
the same actual proper rational parabolics, unipotent radicals and intrinsic
normalized quotient measures. The proof leaves the normalized parabolic
vanishing factor unchanged and applies the preceding form equality; the
uniform case also applies uniform growth independence. The original
constant-term convergence results remain available on these unchanged
functions and quotients.

For each of these six subrepresentations, `ChangeRepresentation` is the
complex-linear equivalence obtained by restricting the identity to the
equal submodules. Its forward and inverse value lemmas preserve the original
finite plot-smooth function, hence also the underlying continuous function.
Each finite-right-action lemma intertwines the existing actions with the
positive convention `R(c)f(x) = f(x*c)`.

This is an internal consumer of the published full adelic growth-independence
proof and the published original form-space definitions. It introduces no
external-source assertion. It compares embeddings for a fixed smooth atlas,
compact subgroup and parabolic data. Tensor-smooth and broader finite
plot-smooth forms remain distinct restrictions; the module proves no equality
between those two carriers. No new quotient measure, reduction theorem,
square-integrability result, enveloping stability of compact-finite forms,
or full archimedean-translation stability is asserted.
