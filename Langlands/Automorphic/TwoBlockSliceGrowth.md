# Growth after restricting a constant term to one Levi variable

The norm of the continuous linear diagonal insertion map bounds both the ambient matrix and its inverse in terms of the corresponding block matrices. Since each individual matrix height is at least one, their product bounds the height of the block diagonal pair up to one constant. Fixing either argument gives a constant multiple of the height in the other argument.

At a fixed finite argument, the ambient maximal-block constant term has polynomial growth by `constantTerm_hasPolynomialGrowth`. Applying the height comparison to its block diagonal argument gives the two slice growth results in the exact adelic polynomial-growth convention of `AutomorphicForms`.

This supplies the growth condition for the Levi restriction in Harish-Chandra, *Automorphic forms on semisimple Lie groups* (1968), Chapter I, §6, proof of Theorem 1, printed pp. 19–20, PDF pp. 29–30. Borel–Jacquet, *Automorphic forms and automorphic representations* (1979), §4.2(d), gives slow increase at each fixed finite argument. Section 4.3, equation (4) and the following sentence, give the adelic norm formulation. Both passages are on printed p. 195, PDF p. 190.
