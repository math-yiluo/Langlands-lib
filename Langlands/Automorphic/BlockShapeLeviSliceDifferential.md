# Differential operators of an individual Levi block

The linear part of the affine inclusion of a single block is
multiplicative on matrices. It gives the real Lie map, its complexification
and the induced map of universal enveloping algebras.
The block diagonal matrix homomorphism commutes with the exponential.
Consequently the corresponding real Lie curve is precisely the
exponential in that block with the identity on every other block.

The derivative of the slice along this curve agrees with the
derivative of the normalized constant term along the ambient curve.
The actual period differentiation theorem identifies it with the
constant term of the ambient derivative. Linearity under
complexification and the universal enveloping algebra induction
extend this identity to every block enveloping operator.

These are the coordinate operators on the Levi slices in Langlands
(1979), Lemma 4, printed p. 204 (Corvallis Part I, PDF p. 199).
