# Nonvanishing of the fixed factors of a cuspidal tensor slice

A linear functional with nonzero restriction to a single-place tensor
slice forces the fixed finite vector and all the fixed vectors at other
infinite places to be nonzero. If the finite vector is zero, the binary
pure tensor is zero. If a fixed infinite coordinate is zero, the
multilinear pure tensor at infinity is zero. Either equality makes the
restriction the zero linear map. This is an internal consequence of the
slice construction and multilinearity.
