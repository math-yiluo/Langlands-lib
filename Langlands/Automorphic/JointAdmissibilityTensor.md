# Joint admissibility of finite external tensors

This internal construction is used for the inducing coefficient representation in adelic block induction. The theorem concerns the actual indexed tensor and coordinate actions of `Langlands.FiniteTensor.external`; it does not assume any factorization of the compact test representation into tensor factors.

`slotContraction i ell` is the linear map taking a pure tensor `⊗ⱼ vⱼ` to `(∏ⱼ≠ᵢ ellⱼ(vⱼ)) • vᵢ`. It intertwines an action pulled back to the `i`th coordinate with the action on `Vᵢ`. In `mem_blockTensorRange_of_slotContractions`, take bases of the designated subspaces `Uᵢ` and of their complements. A tensor-basis coefficient with any index in a complement vanishes if all contractions in that slot land in `Uᵢ`: the tensor coefficient is the corresponding basis coordinate of a slot contraction. Expanding in the tensor basis then places the tensor in the range of `PiTensorProduct.map` of the inclusions `Uᵢ → Vᵢ`.

For `isJointlyAdmissible_external`, pull back an arbitrary open level in the product of the finite groups along each continuous coordinate inclusion. Restrict the compact test along the corresponding compact coordinate inclusion, keeping its domain unchanged. Postcomposing a joint multiplicity map with a slot contraction now gives a joint multiplicity map for that factor. By joint admissibility of the factor, the images of these maps lie in a finite-dimensional `Uᵢ`, constructed as the sum of the evaluations of its multiplicity space on the finitely many test basis vectors. The contraction criterion bounds the common image in the finite-dimensional tensor range. Restricting the codomain to this range embeds the original multiplicity space into a finite-dimensional space of linear maps.
