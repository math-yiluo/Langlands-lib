# Hilbert realizations of actual cuspidal place components

`OperatorRealization.CuspidalHilbertPlaceRealizationStatement` works in the
actual reconstructed determinant-band Hilbert completion for a cuspidal
operator realization with a specified unitary central character and a
form computing its band inner product. At each real or complex place, an
irreducible admissible algebraic subspace for the original compact and
Lie operators gives a unitary, jointly continuous, topologically irreducible
Hilbert action on the closure of its own image. The restricted completed
compact action is admissible and the embedded subspace is exactly the
compact-finite carrier. The original vectors are genuinely smooth and
their compact operators and real Lie derivatives agree with the
corresponding original operators.

The proof uses joint continuity and inner product preservation of the
reconstructed adelic action, and the genuine smooth embedding theorem
with the published scalar coefficient and smooth-kernel results. The
form-level realization theorem then applies at each place. Its input Lie
operator agrees with the original operator by the place inclusion into
the global complexified Lie algebra. The closure and its representation
are those of the specified completion throughout.

This is an internal prerequisite for comparison of smooth Whittaker
functionals at archimedean places.
