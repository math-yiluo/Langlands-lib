# Harish-Chandra finiteness on the adelic general linear group

`Langlands.Automorphic.harishChandraFiniteness` proves `HarishChandraFinitenessStatement` at the number field universe `u`. The proof adapts Harish-Chandra's constant-term induction directly to the adelic general linear group. It treats every open finite level, every compact representation on `Fin m → ℂ` as an algebraic representation, and every cofinite ideal of the actual enveloping center. Compactness of the additive adele quotient supplies the normalized maximal-block constant terms.

At a fixed rank, restrict the forms to the maximal-block Levis at finitely many finite translates. The finite cover of the standard adelic compact group by finite components modulo the open level makes the kernel of this restriction the cuspidal multiplicity space by Iwasawa decomposition and compact equivariance. A common reproducing kernel is obtained using the continuous finite-dimensional image of the original compact source in the family of values. The cuspidal Siegel-set estimate bounds the eigenspaces of the infinitesimal scalar operator on their value span. A monic annihilating polynomial of this operator splits into linear factors. Finiteness for the kernel of a product of operators then bounds the full span, including generalized eigenvectors.

The complexified archimedean Lie algebra splits into complex matrix factors, one at each real place and two at each complex place. The factorwise parabolic projection and the finite-product criterion for center quotients supply a cofinite ideal in each block's enveloping center. Every embedded element of this ideal belongs to the sum of the ambient center ideal's right span and the upper radical's right span. Centrality makes the first span annihilate each original form. The second span vanishes upon evaluating a constant term on the Levi. The slice operator identities therefore give one annihilator for all source intertwiners and all values of the other block. The lower-rank compact action and conjugated finite level are restricted along the block embeddings, and the growth estimate uses the matrix and inverse height of the varying block. These facts place the slices in two fixed lower-rank annihilated multiplicity spaces.

The two block ranks are strictly smaller than the original rank. Induction makes both lower-rank spaces finite-dimensional, and actual evaluation gives two finite-dimensional slice spaces of scalar functions. A finite set of evaluations detecting the first slice space determines which second slices to retain, giving a finite-dimensional bound for the joint constant-term image. Combining the kernel and image proves the rank step. The rank-zero case is included because there are no proper block cuts and a form on the singleton group is determined by one evaluation.

The cuspidal estimate and rank reduction follow Harish-Chandra, *Automorphic forms on semisimple Lie groups* (1968), Chapter I, §6, Lemmas 17–18 and the proof of Theorem 1, printed pp. 17–20, PDF pp. 27–30. The adelic target is Borel–Jacquet, *Automorphic forms and automorphic representations* (1979), §4.3 (i), printed p. 195, PDF p. 190. The construction of the common kernel uses the reproducing-function method of §1.6 (2) and Lemma 2.1, printed pp. 191 and 193, PDF pp. 186 and 188.
