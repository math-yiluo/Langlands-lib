# Genuine local Hilbert derivatives from coefficient lifts

A local group inclusion preserves joint continuity of the ambient adelic
action and preserves genuine smoothness of an original vector when the
inclusion is smooth in the mixed archimedean charts. The local generator
comparison identifies the derivative of that smooth orbit in a real
matrix direction with the original enveloping generator for the inserted
Lie direction.

The proof tests the derivative against each Hilbert vector and uses the
smooth scalar coefficient lift for that test vector. In the local units
chart, a coefficient is the restriction of its scalar extension along
the matrix insertion. Its derivative is the ambient derivative of that
local extension at the identity. The local chain rule and enveloping
covariance identify this scalar with the coefficient of the embedded
original generator. Hilbert separation gives the vector identity.
These internal lemmas use the previously established coefficient lifts
and place restriction formulas.
