# Hilbert realization of a place component from ambient data

`placeClosureRepresentation` is the restricted action on the norm closure
of the image of a stable algebraic subspace. Its carrier is the closure
of the range of the original embedding restricted to the subspace. The
range formula for a composition identifies this with the closure in
`PlaceClosureCompatibility`.

If the subspace is irreducible and admissible and the ambient action
has the stated continuous, unitary, compact and derivative comparisons,
`isPlaceHilbertRealization_of_ambient` gives the full compact-finite Hilbert
realization on this closure. The proof restricts the compact and Lie
actions algebraically, then applies the internal Hilbert closure theorem.
