# The pairing of functions with a common unitary character on a left quotient

Author: Worker 112.

This internal construction gives the usual inner product `∫ conj f * g` on
continuous functions that are left invariant under one subgroup and transform
under a second subgroup by a unitary character. The integral is taken over the
actual left quotient. It is generic: `H` is any topological group with
continuous multiplication, in an arbitrary universe. The module does not
specialize it to adelic groups or to `[G]`.

A typical intended use takes `H = G(A_F)`, `Γ₀ = G(F)` and `Z` a group of
central adelic points with a unitary character `ω`. The square-integrable part
of the module then consists of continuous functions in `L²(Z G(F) \ G(A_F), ω)`,
with the Petersson-type inner product. That specialization is not formalized
here.

The module imports only the published `Langlands.Global.LeftCosetSpace`, the
published `Langlands.Representation.Unitary.Basic` (`InvariantPositiveForm`,
`IsUnitarizable`) and Mathlib.

## Data and hypotheses

- `H : Type u` is a group with a topology and `[ContinuousMul H]`. No
  topological-group, Hausdorff, local compactness or second countability
  assumption is used.
- `Γ₀` and `Z` are arbitrary subgroups of `H`. Neither is assumed normal,
  central or closed, and `Z` need not commute with `Γ₀`.
- `ω : Z →* Circle` is a unitary character of `Z`. No compatibility of `ω`
  with `Z ⊓ Γ₀` is imposed. (Elementary remark, not formalized: if `ω` is
  nontrivial on `Z ⊓ Γ₀`, the character conditions below force every
  function to vanish.)
- The quotient is `LeftCosetSpace (Z ⊔ Γ₀)`, the published actual left quotient
  of `H` by the subgroup generated by `Z` and `Γ₀`. It carries the published
  quotient topology and the published right translation
  `LeftCosetSpace.rightTranslate`, `[x] ↦ [x * g]`.
- For the `L²` part, the quotient has any `MeasurableSpace` instance with
  `[OpensMeasurableSpace]`, and `μ` is any measure on it.
- `RightInvariant Γ₀ Z μ` is the proposition that every
  `LeftCosetSpace.rightTranslate (Z ⊔ Γ₀) g` preserves `μ`. It is an explicit
  hypothesis wherever it is used.
- Definiteness of the form uses `[μ.IsOpenPosMeasure]`.

## Right translation and descent

- `rightAction H : Representation ℂ H C(H, ℂ)` is positive right translation,
  `rightAction_apply : rightAction H g f x = f (x * g)`.
- `leftStabilizer h` is the subgroup of `γ` with `h (γ * x) = h x` for all
  `x`; `mem_leftStabilizer` unfolds it. `sup_le_leftStabilizer` proves that a
  function fixed by left multiplication by `Γ₀` and by `Z` is fixed by
  `Z ⊔ Γ₀`. This is how the module handles a non-central `Z`.
- `descend Γ₀ Z h hh` is a continuous function `h` on `H` with
  `hh : Z ⊔ Γ₀ ≤ leftStabilizer h`, as a continuous function on the quotient.
  It uses the published `LeftCosetSpace.lift` and `continuous_lift`, and
  `descend_mk` holds by definition.
- `conj_mul_circle : conj z * z = 1` for `z : Circle`.

## Functions with character `ω`

- `characterSubmodule Γ₀ Z ω` is the complex submodule of `C(H, ℂ)` of those
  `f` with `f (γ * x) = f x` for `γ ∈ Γ₀` and
  `f (z * x) = ω z * f x` for `z : Z`. `mem_characterSubmodule` unfolds it.
- `characterSubrepresentation` proves that it is stable under `rightAction H`.
  `CharacterFunctions Γ₀ Z ω` is its carrier and `characterAction` is the
  restricted representation, with
  `characterAction_apply : (characterAction g f).val x = f.val (x * g)`.
  `CharacterFunctions.rational` and `CharacterFunctions.character` restate the
  two conditions.
- `pairingIntegrand f g` is the continuous function `x ↦ conj (f x) * g x`
  and `normIntegrand f` is `x ↦ ‖f x‖`. Their lemmas `_le_leftStabilizer`
  prove left invariance under `Z ⊔ Γ₀`. The `Z` case uses
  `conj (ω z) * ω z = 1` and `‖ω z‖ = 1`.
- `pairingDensity f g` and `normDensity f` are the descended continuous
  functions on the quotient, with `pairingDensity_mk` and `normDensity_mk`
  by definition.
- Pointwise lemmas: `normDensity_nonneg`,
  `norm_pairingDensity` (`‖pairingDensity f g q‖ = normDensity f q * normDensity g q`),
  `pairingDensity_self` (`pairingDensity f f q = (normDensity f q)²`),
  `pairingDensity_conj`, `pairingDensity_add_left/right`,
  `pairingDensity_smul_left` (factor `conj c`), `pairingDensity_smul_right`,
  `normDensity_add_le`, `normDensity_smul`, `normDensity_zero`.
- `pairingDensity_characterAction` and `normDensity_characterAction`: right
  translating the functions by `g` right translates the descended densities by
  `rightTranslate (Z ⊔ Γ₀) g`.
- `eq_zero_of_normDensity_eq_zero`: a function whose descended absolute value
  vanishes everywhere is zero.

## Square-integrable functions

- `l2CharacterSubmodule Γ₀ Z ω μ` is the submodule of `CharacterFunctions`
  of those `f` with `MemLp (normDensity f) 2 μ`; `mem_l2CharacterSubmodule`
  unfolds it. Closure under addition uses `normDensity_add_le` and
  `MemLp.mono`.
- `l2CharacterSubrepresentation Γ₀ Z ω μ hμ` proves stability under
  `characterAction`, from `hμ : RightInvariant Γ₀ Z μ` and
  `MemLp.comp_measurePreserving`.
- `l2Submodule Γ₀ Z ω μ` is the image of `l2CharacterSubmodule` in
  `C(H, ℂ)`. `mem_l2Submodule` characterizes it directly and
  `l2Submodule_le_characterSubmodule` holds. `l2Subrepresentation` proves
  stability under `rightAction H`, `L2Functions Γ₀ Z ω μ hμ` is its carrier and
  `l2Action` the restricted representation, with
  `l2Action_apply : (l2Action g f).val x = f.val (x * g)`.
- `toCharacter f` views `f : L2Functions` as an element of
  `CharacterFunctions`. `toCharacter_mem` proves that it lies in
  `l2CharacterSubmodule`. `toCharacter_val`, `toCharacter_l2Action`,
  `toCharacter_add`, `toCharacter_smul` and `toCharacter_zero` hold by
  definition. `toCharacter_injective` holds.
- `integrable_pairingDensity`: for `f` and `g` in `l2CharacterSubmodule`,
  `pairingDensity f g` is `μ`-integrable. The proof is Hölder's inequality with
  exponents `2, 2` (`MemLp.integrable_mul`) and `norm_pairingDensity`.

## The pairing

- `pairing Γ₀ Z ω μ f g` is the Bochner integral
  `∫ q, pairingDensity f g q ∂μ`, defined for all functions with character
  `ω`. By Mathlib's convention it is `0` when the density is not integrable;
  `pairing_def` restates the definition.
- For all `f` and `g` with character `ω`: `pairing_conj`
  (`conj (pairing g f) = pairing f g`), `pairing_self`
  (`pairing f f = ∫ (normDensity f)²`, a real number), `re_pairing_self_nonneg`,
  `pairing_smul_left` (`pairing (c • f) g = conj c * pairing f g`) and
  `pairing_smul_right`.
- For functions in `l2CharacterSubmodule`: `pairing_add_left` and
  `pairing_add_right`, by `integrable_pairingDensity` and `integral_add`.
- `pairing_characterAction hμ g f f'`: right translation of both functions
  preserves the pairing, for all `f`, `f'` with character `ω`. The proof
  changes variables along the measure-preserving map `rightTranslate g`
  (`integral_map`, `MeasurePreserving.map_eq`).
- `eq_zero_of_pairing_self_eq_zero` assumes `[μ.IsOpenPosMeasure]`. A function
  in `l2CharacterSubmodule` with `pairing f f = 0` is zero. The integral of the
  nonnegative integrable function `(normDensity f)²` vanishes, so it is zero
  almost everywhere (`integral_eq_zero_iff_of_nonneg`). Because it is
  continuous and `μ` charges nonempty open sets, it is zero everywhere
  (`Continuous.ae_eq_iff_eq`); then `eq_zero_of_normDensity_eq_zero` applies.

## The invariant positive form

- `sesquilinearPairing Γ₀ Z ω μ hμ` is the pairing on `L2Functions` as a map
  `L2Functions →ₗ⋆[ℂ] L2Functions →ₗ[ℂ] ℂ`, conjugate linear in the first
  argument and linear in the second. `sesquilinearPairing_apply` gives the
  integral.
- `invariantPositiveForm Γ₀ Z ω μ hμ`, under `[μ.IsOpenPosMeasure]`, is a
  published `InvariantPositiveForm (l2Action Γ₀ Z ω μ hμ)`. Its inner product is
  `pairing (toCharacter f) (toCharacter g)`, and
  `invariantPositiveForm_inner` gives the integral. Hermitian symmetry,
  nonnegativity, additivity and conjugate homogeneity in the first argument,
  definiteness and invariance under every `l2Action g` come from the lemmas
  above. `isUnitarizable_l2Action` concludes `IsUnitarizable (l2Action ..)`.
- `invariantPositiveFormComp Γ₀ Z ω μ hμ φ`, for any monoid `K : Type w` and
  `φ : K →* H`, is the same form as an `InvariantPositiveForm` for the
  composite representation `(l2Action Γ₀ Z ω μ hμ).comp φ`. Examples include
  the inclusion of a subgroup or of finite adelic points.
  `invariantPositiveFormComp_inner` and `isUnitarizable_l2Action_comp`
  follow.

## Scope

The form lives on continuous functions. The module does not complete
`L2Functions`, identify it with a subspace of Mathlib's `Lp`, or prove density
of continuous functions. It constructs no right-invariant measure on a
specific quotient: right invariance and positivity on open sets are
hypotheses. It does not prove that cusp forms or other automorphic forms are
square integrable. Any such statement is a separate obligation.

## Provenance

This is a standard construction (the Petersson-type inner product on
functions with a unitary central character), assembled here from the
published actual left quotient and the published notion of an invariant
positive form. No external theorem is asserted, and no source locator is
claimed. The Lean proofs use only Mathlib integration lemmas (Hölder's
inequality, `integral_map`, `integral_eq_zero_iff_of_nonneg`) and the
published `LeftCosetSpace` interface.
