# Local Haar integration within the actual determinant band

The rational subgroup of the adelic group is discrete. The quotient
covering map therefore has an open injective neighborhood at every
point. For a point lying in the band, intersect the neighborhood with
the open preimage of the band and transfer it to the actual matrix
coordinates.

On any measurable matrix patch of this kind, the integral of the
squared norm of an actual original vector is bounded by the
determinant-band quotient integral. The proof transports the patch
under the original adelic homeomorphism and uses the same pushed
forward Haar measure and quotient map. The local discrete-quotient
integral estimate applies to the exact squared norm function.
Taking square roots gives a bound for the patch `L²` seminorm by the
norm of the original vector in the band `L²` space.
This is an internal comparison with the actual band measure.
