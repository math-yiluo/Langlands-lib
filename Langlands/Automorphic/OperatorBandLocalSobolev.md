# The Sobolev bound near an actual determinant-band point

Choose an injective product patch at the given band point, with a
closed matrix ball and a finite coset contained in the chosen level
coset. All genuine enveloping derivatives of a level-fixed vector
are still fixed at that level. Their additive chart integrals are
therefore bounded by their actual determinant-band `L²` norms.

The compact frame estimate bounds the coordinate derivatives by
finitely many such enveloping derivatives. Integrate that estimate
on the closed ball, then apply the uniform local Sobolev bound at
every point of the half-radius ball. Finite-level invariance gives
the same estimate on the entire finite coset. Its product with the
smaller open ball is the required neighborhood.
