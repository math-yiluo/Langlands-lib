# Fixed profile and center conditions under the parabolic constant term

`parabolicConstantTermLinear` is the normalized integral over the actual compact
rational quotient of the unipotent radical. It maps finite plot-smooth rational
functions linearly to functions on the ambient adelic group.

The image is invariant under the adelic radical and the rational Levi. The first
invariance is right invariance of the quotient measure. For the second, rational
Levi points preserve the measure by conjugation and act trivially on the input
function by left translation. The smooth slice formula gives continuity and
plot smoothness, and right invariance under the specified finite level passes
through the integral. Compact representatives for the radical quotient bound
the height in the integrand. The normalized and representation heights differ
by a fixed positive factor, so the integral has moderate growth. Intertwining
with the actual enveloping action retains the original center ideal on each
infinite slice.

For a fixed ambient point, each integrand gives a right compact profile in the
original finite-dimensional profile space. Finite point evaluations express all
these profiles using vectors of that space. Integrating the resulting scalar
coefficients expresses the constant-term profile in the same space.

The construction and regularity correspond to Mœglin–Waldspurger,
*Spectral Decomposition and Eisenstein Series* (1995), I.2.6 and I.2.17,
and the constant-term restriction in Harish-Chandra, *Automorphic Forms on
Semisimple Lie Groups* (1968), Chapter I, §6.
