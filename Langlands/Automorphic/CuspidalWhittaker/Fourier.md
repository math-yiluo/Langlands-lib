# Fourier uniqueness on powers of `𝔸_K / K`

Author: Worker 1709.

This is internal infrastructure for the proof of
`Langlands.Automorphic.CuspidalUnitaryLocalGenericityStatement`. All results are proved, with
no assumed hypotheses beyond `CompactSpace (AdeleQuotient K)`, which is the hypothesis of the
target statement. The module uses the torus integrals of `QuotientTorus` and the character `ψ`
of `AdelicCharacter`.

## Characters and coefficients

For a finite type `ι` and `a ∈ K^ι`:

- `pairingCharacter a y = ψ(Σᵢ aᵢ yᵢ)`, with values in `ℂ`, is a function on `𝔸_K^ι`. It is
  multiplicative in `a`, and `pairingCharacter (−a)` is its complex conjugate. It has norm one,
  it is continuous, and it is periodic under `K^ι` (`isPeriodic_pairingCharacter`) because `ψ`
  is trivial on `K`.
- `fourierCoeff f a = torusIntegral ι (y ↦ f y · conj(pairingCharacter a y))`.

## Density

`torusCharacterMap K ι a` is the induced continuous function on the torus `(𝔸_K / K)^ι`.
`torusCharacterSubalgebra K ι` is the star subalgebra generated by these functions, and it is
closed under `star` because `star χ_a = χ_{−a}`. As a submodule it equals the linear span of
the characters, since products of characters are characters (`torusCharacterSubalgebra_coe`).

`torusCharacterSubalgebra_separatesPoints`: let `s ≠ t` in the torus, differing at coordinate
`i`, and let `d` be the difference of the chosen lifts at `i`. Then `d ∉ K`. By the
self-duality theorem `exists_eq_algebraMap_of_orthogonal`, there is `c ∈ K` with
`ψ(c d) ≠ 1`, and the character with `a = Pi.single i c` separates `s` from `t`.

Stone–Weierstrass on the compact Hausdorff torus
(`ContinuousMap.starSubalgebra_topologicalClosure_eq_top_of_separatesPoints`) then gives
`span_torusCharacterMap_closure_eq_top`: the span of the characters is dense in
`C((𝔸_K / K)^ι, ℂ)`.

## Uniqueness

`eq_zero_of_fourierCoeff_eq_zero`: a continuous periodic `f : 𝔸_K^ι → ℂ` with
`fourierCoeff f a = 0` for every `a ∈ K^ι` vanishes identically. Let `h = f ∘ torusLift`, which
is continuous. The functional `p ↦ ∫ h · p` is continuous on `C((𝔸_K / K)^ι, ℂ)` and vanishes
on every character, because the coefficient at `−a` is zero. By density it vanishes on
`star h`, so `∫ |h|² = 0`. Hence `h = 0` almost everywhere. Haar measure on the torus charges
every nonempty open set, so the continuous function `h` is zero, and therefore `f = 0`.
