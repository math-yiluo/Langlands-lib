# Continuous differentials on the uniform moderate growth spaces

The spaces in `GroupSchemeUniformGrowthFrechet` use the sup norms of weighted enveloping derivatives. Each weighted derivative is a continuous linear map into the bounded functions, and continuity into the growth space can be checked on these maps.

An enveloping element preserves the growth exponent because applying a further differential composes the two elements in the enveloping algebra. It preserves the right finite level because every enveloping differential commutes with finite right translation. The same composition identity gives continuity for the defining topology. Evaluation of an enveloping derivative at an adelic point is continuous linear by evaluating its bounded weighted derivative and multiplying back by the weight.

These are derived continuity results for the actual spaces in Bernstein–Lapid, §7.1, and the Fréchet formulation of Eisenstein continuation in their Corollary 8.6. They supply scalar tests for weak holomorphy after applying differential operators.
