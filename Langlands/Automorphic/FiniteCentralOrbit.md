# Character vectors retaining a simple subquotient

This internal module isolates the algebraic part of Langlands (1979), Lemma 6, printed p.205 (Corvallis Part I, PDF p.200). The registered source is `langlands-1979-notion-automorphic-representation`, SHA-256 `3b9fe0145da81d870ef8967be83635cd440a2581fe043e64e63324e22dec4db5`.

Let a commutative group act on a complex module by endomorphisms that commute with the module operators. Assume every group orbit spans a finite-dimensional complex space. The main theorem retains a given simple subquotient in a cyclic module generated by a vector on which the commuting group acts by a character. The character is algebraic. In an automorphic application its continuity also uses continuity of the function under translation.

The proof minimizes the dimension of the orbit span among cyclic generators whose maps to the quotient are nonzero at the generator. If a central operator does not act by a scalar at a minimizing generator, choose an eigenvalue of its restriction to the orbit span and subtract it. If the quotient map kills the kernel, it descends to the image and the image generator has a smaller orbit span. Otherwise a vector in the kernel maps to a nonzero quotient vector. That vector is an operator multiple of the original generator. Multiplication by the same operator maps the original orbit span onto the kernel vector's orbit span and kills the nonzero shifted generator, so the dimension decreases again. The common eigenvalues multiply by the group law and are nonzero by invertibility. The final map is surjective by simplicity.
