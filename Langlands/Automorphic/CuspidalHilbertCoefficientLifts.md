the reconstructed action has joint-finite smooth coefficient lifts. The source
vectors are the original vectors embedded in the Hilbert completion of that
form. The lifts carry their genuine enveloping action.

The isometric identity embedding pairs an arbitrary completed Hilbert vector
with the prescribed translated band vector for the orbit of a source vector.
The band inner product is the actual scalar coefficient integral. The published
coefficient theorem supplies a smooth joint-finite lift and all its
enveloping-action identities, which transfer through this scalar equality.

and coefficient theorem.
