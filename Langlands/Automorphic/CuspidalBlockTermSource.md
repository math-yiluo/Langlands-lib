# The actual cyclic source of the minimal block constant term

`cyclicOperatorRealization` identifies the module generated by a realized vector with its image in the actual stable automorphic functions. The equivalence preserves the finite-smooth function underlying each vector.

For the original simple operator quotient, choose the block constant term and cyclic numerator given by minimal constant-term selection. This selection annihilates the terms for all strict refinements of the selected cuts. The cyclic numerator maps onto its image in the term quotient, and the chosen surjection on that image still reaches the given simple module. The corresponding surjection from the actual cyclic realization kills precisely the required zero-term kernel in the sense of `HasCuspidalBlockTermSource`. The strict-refinement vanishings pass to that realization since every vector represents one of the selected numerator forms.

This is the concrete realization of the minimal standard parabolic and cyclic quotient step in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), printed pp. 204–205. The proof uses the constant-term kernel filtration ordered by the number of cuts.
