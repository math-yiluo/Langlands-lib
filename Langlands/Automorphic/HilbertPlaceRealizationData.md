# Real and complex Hilbert realizations on a form completion

The real and complex place lemmas apply the general Hilbert closure
realization to the actual local group inclusions. The supplied completion
has a jointly continuous unitary action, smoothness of its original vectors
for the mixed archimedean group and the stated generator comparison.
The input algebraic component is irreducible and admissible, and the
original compact vectors are finite under the action at that place.

Restriction of the action gives joint continuity, unitarity and genuine
smoothness at the place. Its scalar coefficient lifts identify the real
orbit derivatives with the algebraic place operators. At a complex place
this applies to the whole underlying real matrix Lie algebra. Place closure
compatibility then supplies the invariant Hilbert subspace and the
comparison on original compact vectors. The general closure theorem
supplies irreducibility, admissibility and compact-finite recovery there.
These internal lemmas use a supplied form completion and the actual
place geometry, so their proofs are independent of the original carrier's
implementation as a subspace of automorphic functions.
