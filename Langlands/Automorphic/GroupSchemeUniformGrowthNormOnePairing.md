# Norm-one pairings in the uniform moderate growth topology

For an actual morphism \(j:M\to G\), a fixed adelic point \(g\), and a continuous rationally invariant test function \(\psi\) on \(M(\mathbb A)\), the integrand
\[
 m\longmapsto f(j(m)g)\overline{\psi(m)}
\]
descends continuously to \(M(F)\backslash M(\mathbb A)^1\). Rational invariance uses naturality of the principal point map. The integral is a continuous complex-linear functional on the specified uniform moderate growth space if an integrable function bounds \(\|j(m)g\|^R|\psi(m)|\) at some representative of almost every class. The proof bounds the norm of the functional by the unit weighted derivative seminorm times the integral of the majorant.

The integral formula is the norm-one Levi pairing in Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series*, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 37 (2024), §6.2, p. 202, equation (6.8) and the display immediately after it. The continuity estimate for the uniform moderate growth space follows from the fixed height weight and the supplied integrable majorant.
