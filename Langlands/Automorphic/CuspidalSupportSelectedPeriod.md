# Selected block period for cuspidal support

Minimal constant-term selection gives an actual form space whose block term
still carries the given irreducible operator quotient. Its strict block
refinements vanish. The positive block sizes are expressed as successors
using their predecessors, which gives the block-size parameters of
`CuspidalSupportExistenceStatement`.

The translated constant-term space has a cyclic subspace with the same
quotient and a central eigencharacter. The cyclic generator is nonzero
because its cyclic span surjects onto a nonzero irreducible quotient. The
eigencharacter is continuous and trivial on tuples of
principal ideles. The quotient map is an intertwiner for the full concrete
operator algebra. The vanishing of strict refinements transfers to the
actual finite-smooth constant terms using the equivalence of the adelic
matrix models.

The construction combines the minimal parabolic step and the application
of Lemma 6 in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation*,
Corvallis Part I (1979), printed pp. 204–205 (PDF pp. 199–200), proof of
Proposition 2. The registered source is
`langlands-1979-notion-automorphic-representation`.
