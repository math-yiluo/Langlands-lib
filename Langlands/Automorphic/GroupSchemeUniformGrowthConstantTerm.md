# Constant-term evaluation in the uniform moderate growth topology

For a represented closed adelic subgroup with compact rational quotient,
evaluation of its normalized constant term at a fixed adelic argument
is a continuous complex-linear map on the uniform growth space.
The quotient is the one used by `subgroupConstantTerm`. The descended
integrand agrees with evaluation at its chosen representative and is
continuous by rational invariance of the original function. On the
compact quotient it is integrable, which gives linearity of the integral.

A single compact set supplies representatives of all quotient classes.
After the fixed right translation the growth weight is bounded on that
compact set. The unit weighted derivative is the original function
divided by this positive weight. Its supremum norm thus bounds the
absolute value of the integral up to a fixed factor, since the
normalized quotient has mass one. The seminorm topology makes this
weighted derivative continuous, so the integral functional is continuous.

The construction supplies scalar constant-term evaluations on the
Fréchet-valued meromorphic families of Bernstein–Lapid §7.1 and the
corollary following §8.6. The constant term uses the source's unipotent
quotient normalization in §2.2 and the actual Library measure.

[Bernstein–Lapid, On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series](https://doi.org/10.1090/jams/1020)
