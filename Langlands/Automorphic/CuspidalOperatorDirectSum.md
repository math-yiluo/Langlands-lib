# Direct sums of actual cuspidal operator realizations

Apply the algebraic decomposition of a semisimple module to the action
of the concrete automorphic operator monoid algebra. The resulting
family of simple submodules has sum equal to the whole carrier, with a
unique finitely supported expression for each vector.

Map each simple submodule into the ambient stable automorphic functions.
The concrete inclusion identifies its monoid-algebra module with the
original simple module, so its operator representation is irreducible.
Cuspidality passes to this subrealization through its actual functions.
The direct-sum coordinate maps become intertwiners for all the operators,
and the finite sum of their included values recovers each vector. The
structure records that each projection recovers its own included factor
and vanishes on the other included factors. These equations make the
finitely supported expression unique.

`cuspidalOperatorDirectSumOfUnitaryCentralCharacter` applies this to the
band-form semisimplicity theorem in positive rank, with compact
`AdeleQuotient F` and a fixed unitary central character. The construction
supplies the algebraic projection of the Levi coefficients used in
Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation*, Corvallis
Part I (1979), Proposition 2, printed p. 205 (PDF p. 200).
