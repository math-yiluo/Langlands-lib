# Compact differentiation under linear maps

For fixed compatible compact and Lie actions, the vectors that satisfy
compact differentiation against every algebraic linear functional
form a complex submodule. This follows from linearity of the two
actions and of differentiation along real compact curves.

A map intertwining both actions carries these vectors to such vectors
by composing any target functional with the map. When a family of these
maps has images spanning the target, the whole target has the compact
differentiation property. An injective map also transfers the property
back to its source, because an algebraic linear functional extends
along a linear left inverse of the map.

This internal transfer supplies compact differentiation for the cyclic
submodule of the translated period carrier in Task 6823. The period
carrier is spanned by images of actual automorphic realizations.
