# Norm-holomorphic derivative coordinates of growth-space curves

A complex curve with values in a Banach space is analytic if all its continuous linear scalar tests are holomorphic. Uniform boundedness on the double dual bounds the curve in norm on each sufficiently small closed disk. The scalar Schwarz estimate and the dual characterization of the norm give continuity. The curve then agrees with its Cauchy integral on the disk, since scalar tests commute with that integral and separate vectors.

Every weighted derivative coordinate of a weakly holomorphic curve in the uniform moderate growth space is therefore an analytic curve with values in the Banach space of bounded functions. `weightedTaylorCoefficient` gives its factorial-normalized coefficient in that norm. These are derived tools for the local continuation formulation in Bernstein–Lapid, §7.1 and Corollary 8.6.
