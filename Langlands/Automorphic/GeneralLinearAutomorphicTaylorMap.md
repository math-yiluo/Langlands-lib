# Linear Taylor maps into the actual automorphic module of \(GL_n\)

The evaluation map reads a stable matrix automorphic form at the adelic
point corresponding to a group-scheme point. It is linear and injective
because the form inclusions are injective and the adelic comparison is
surjective.

Let \(f(t)\) be a linear map from a complex vector space to the uniform
moderate-growth space at a fixed open finite level. Suppose the curves
\(t\mapsto f(t)w\) are weakly holomorphic on a common preconnected open
set and give automorphic functions on a nonempty open subset. The
existing automorphic Taylor construction applies to each \(w\). The
comparison preserves its evaluation at every adelic point, which is the
scalar derivative of the corresponding evaluated curve divided by the
factorial of the order. Addition and scalar multiplication of those
derivatives, together with injective evaluation, make the construction a
linear map into the stable automorphic module at each order.

This is an internal linearization of the Taylor-vector step used in
Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), proof
of Proposition 2, printed pp. 205–206 (Corvallis Part I PDF pp. 200–201).
