# The block idele centre orbit of an actual full constant term

`blockConstantTermScalarAction` is the action on functions on the full adelic matrix group by left composition with the separate scalar ideles of the Levi blocks. `blockShapeConstantTerm_isFiniteVector` proves finite-dimensionality of the orbit of the actual normalized block constant term of an automorphic form.

Choose a finite spanning family from the standard archimedean compact orbit of the original form. Its right finite stabilizer gives a finite double-coset cover modulo the finite block parabolic. Together with archimedean Iwasawa, this expresses every ambient argument as a parabolic point times an infinite compact and finite representative, followed by an element of the right finite stabilizer. Parabolic covariance of the constant term replaces the first point by its Levi component. These identities persist for all linear combinations of left centre translates.

For each pair of compact spanning point and finite representative, restrict the orbit functions to the corresponding translated Levi. These restrictions detect the orbit functions because at fixed finite representative and Levi point the evaluation depends linearly on the compact translate of the original form. The orbit at each sampled restriction is finite-dimensional by the Levi orbit theorem applied to the actual right translate of that form. The evaluation injection into the finite product of these orbit spaces proves the full bound used before Lemma 6 of Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), printed pp. 204–205.
