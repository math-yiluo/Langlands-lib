# Spherical Hecke spectra of contragredient cuspidal realizations

This module proves that contragredient cuspidal realizations have inverse
spherical spectra at every finite place where both have a presented spectrum.
It works with the published actual operator realizations
`Langlands.Automorphic.OperatorRealization F n` of `GL_n` over a number field
`F : Type uF`. These are subspaces of automorphic forms with their finite adelic,
standard infinite compact and enveloping-algebra actions. It uses the published
contragredient relation `OperatorRealization.IsContragredientOf` of
`Langlands.Automorphic.Contragredient`. Local groups `GL_n(F_v)` carry the
published canonical topology of the completion `CompletionPadic.padicField v`,
declared as local instances as in the other local modules.

Provenance: Henniart (2000), *A simple proof of the Langlands conjectures for
GL(n) over a p-adic field*, §2.2 (printed p. 444), states that association is
preserved by contragredients. The indicated route is local inversion of Satake
parameters. This module is an internal derivation of that local inversion for
the actual realizations. It shows that the presented spectrum of one local
component controls every spherical vector of the whole realization, because the
realization is irreducible. It then applies the Hecke adjointness of
`Langlands.Representation.Unramified.PairedHeckeScalars` to the invariant pairing
supplied by the contragredient relation. No local smooth-dual compatibility,
Satake inversion, or presentation-independence statement is assumed.

## Generators of the operator algebra

- `generatorSubrepresentation` makes a subrepresentation of the whole concrete
  operator algebra out of a subspace `N`. The subspace must be stable under the
  finite translations, the compact translations and the enveloping action. The
  operator algebra `automorphicOperatorAlgebra` is the subalgebra generated by
  these three families, so `Algebra.adjoin_induction` proves stability under
  every element. Generators, scalars, sums and products are handled in turn.
- `eq_top_of_generatorStable` applies the published irreducibility
  `OperatorRealization.IsIrreducible` (simplicity of the subrepresentation
  lattice). A nonzero subspace stable under the three families is everything.

## Local restriction

- `localFormInclusion` is the inclusion of the realization in automorphic forms,
  as an intertwiner from the published local restriction
  `S.localRepresentation v` to local right translation of forms. Its
  intertwining property is that of the finite action at `localInclusion g`.
- `localRepresentation_isSmooth_canonical` proves smoothness of the local
  restriction for the canonical topology. It pulls back the published smoothness
  `automorphicLocalRightAction_isSmooth_canonical` along the injective
  intertwiner `localFormInclusion` (`Langlands.isSmooth_of_injective`).
- `localRepresentation_apply`, `finiteRepresentation_mul_apply` and
  `localRepresentation_mul_apply` are definitional unfolding and
  multiplicativity, stated as equations for later term proofs.
- `commute_localInclusion_away` proves that `d (ι_v d_v)⁻¹` commutes with
  `ι_v g`, for every finite adele `d` and local `g`. The two sides agree
  componentwise (`ext_localProjection`). At `v` the component of `d (ι_v d_v)⁻¹`
  is `1`, and away from `v` the component of `ι_v g` is `1`.

## The defect core

Fix `S : OperatorRealization F (r + 1)` and a spectrum `s`. Write `K` for the
spherical subgroup at `v`.

- `standardDefect v s i` is the defect `PairedHecke.heckeDefect` of the `i`-th
  standard Hecke element and the scalar
  `standardHeckeScalar s i`, for the local restriction. `standardDefect_commute`
  proves that it commutes with every operator commuting with the local action,
  by `PairedHecke.heckeDefect_intertwiner`.
- `defectCore v s` is the subspace of vectors `f` such that every local
  translate `g f` has vanishing standard defects. `mem_defectCore_iff` unfolds it.
- Stability:
  - `localRepresentation_mem_defectCore`: under local translations, since
    `g (h f) = (g h) f`;
  - `mem_defectCore_of_commute`: under every operator commuting with the local
    action;
  - `finiteRepresentation_mem_defectCore`: under finite adelic translations. Write
    `d = (d (ι_v d_v)⁻¹) (ι_v d_v)`. The first factor commutes with the local
    action by `commute_localInclusion_away`, and the second factor is a local
    translation;
  - `compactRepresentation_mem_defectCore` and
    `envelopingAction_mem_defectCore`: the compact and enveloping actions commute
    with finite translations, by the published
    `finiteRepresentation_compactRepresentation` and
    `finiteRepresentation_envelopingAction`.
- `defectCore_ne_bot` takes a nonzero local realization `L` inside the local
  form image with spherical Hecke spectrum `s`. It shows that the core is
  nonzero. Choose `f₀ ≠ 0` in `L` and `t` with form `f₀`. For every `g` and `i`,
  the image in forms of the defect of `g t` is the defect of the translate
  `g f₀` in `L`. The two applications of `heckeDefect_intertwiner` are for
  `localFormInclusion` and for the inclusion of `L`. That defect vanishes by
  `PairedHecke.heckeDefect_eq_zero`, because `L` has the scalars of `s` on its
  whole fixed space. Injectivity of the form inclusion gives `t ∈ defectCore`,
  and `t ≠ 0`.
- `defectCore_eq_top` combines stability, nonvanishing and irreducibility.
- `corner_eq_smul_of_mem_defectCore` proves that a `K`-fixed vector of the core
  has the standard Hecke scalars of `s`. Take `g = 1` and apply
  `PairedHecke.corner_eq_smul_of_heckeDefect`.
- `corner_eq_smul_of_localComponent`: in an irreducible realization, one nonzero
  local component inside the form image with spectrum `s` forces every `K`-fixed
  vector of the whole local restriction to have the scalars of `s`.

## Spherical vectors and inverse spectra

- `mem_spherical_of_formInclusion`: a vector whose form is a spherical vector of
  a local component is `K`-fixed for the local restriction. This follows from
  the intertwining property of `localFormInclusion` and injectivity of the form
  inclusion.
- `exists_spherical_ne_zero_of_le`: a local component inside the form image with
  a nonzero spherical vector gives a nonzero `K`-fixed vector of the local
  restriction. The proof is a term proof; the comment in the source records that
  `obtain` against this subtype-valued goal exhausts `whnf`.
- `eq_map_inv_of_isContragredientOf` takes `S'` realizing the contragredient of
  `S`. It assumes that every `K`-fixed vector of `S` has the scalars of `s`, that
  every `K`-fixed vector of `S'` has the scalars of `s'`, and that `S'` has a
  nonzero `K`-fixed vector `b`. It concludes `s' = s.map (·⁻¹)`. The
  contragredient pairing `B` is invariant under the finite action, hence under
  the local action. Its partial map `B.flip` is injective, so `B y b ≠ 0` for
  some `y`. By `PairedHecke.pairing_sphericalProjection`, the spherical
  projection `a` of `y` still satisfies `B a b ≠ 0`.
  `PairedHecke.eq_map_inv_of_pairing` then concludes.

## Presentations

For `T : CuspidalOperatorRealization F r` and a presentation
`P : CuspidalSpectralPresentation.{uF, uV} T`:

- `CuspidalSpectralPresentation.localComponent_le_image`: the local component
  of `P` lies in the local form image of `T.realization`. It lies in the finite
  slice (`presentation.localComponent_le`), which lies in the image
  (`finiteSlice.le_image`).
- `CuspidalSpectralPresentation.corner_eq_smul`: for `v ∉ P.exceptional`, every
  `K`-fixed vector of `T.realization` has the standard Hecke scalars of
  `P.spectralSystem.eigenvalues v hv`. This uses irreducibility of `T`, the
  nonvanishing of the local component, and the published `P.hasSpectrum`.
- `CuspidalSpectralPresentation.exists_spherical_ne_zero`: for
  `v ∉ P.exceptional`, the local restriction of `T.realization` has a nonzero
  `K`-fixed vector. The published `HasSphericalHeckeSpectrum` contains
  `SphericalVectors ≠ ⊥`.
- `CuspidalOperatorRealization.eigenvalues_eq_map_inv_of_isContragredientOf`:
  let `T'.realization.IsContragredientOf T.realization`, and let `P` and `P'`
  be presentations of `T` and `T'` in independent universes `uV` and `uW`. At
  every `v` outside both exceptional sets,
  `P'.spectralSystem.eigenvalues v hv' =
  (P.spectralSystem.eigenvalues v hv).map (·⁻¹)`.
