# Finite level and compact action on the two-block constant term

Harish-Chandra, *Automorphic forms on semisimple Lie groups* (1968),
Chapter I, §6, proof of Theorem 1, printed pp. 19–20 (PDF pp. 29–30),
restricts constant terms to the Levi and uses the restricted compact
action in the induction. This module implements the finite-level and
compact covariance of the adelic restriction.

The block diagonal embedding commutes with the rational, finite and
infinite coordinate maps. The product Levi restriction is continuous and
invariant under left multiplication by pairs of rational matrices.

For a finite translation $d$ and a level $L$, the Levi level is the
preimage of $L$ under $x \mapsto d^{-1}\operatorname{diag}(x)d$. This is
an open subgroup because both the embedding and conjugation are
continuous. A right translate of the restricted function by $x$ is the
restriction of the source form translated by $d^{-1}\operatorname{diag}(x)d$,
so this open subgroup fixes all restrictions of $L$-fixed forms.

Reindexing commutes with conjugate transposition. At each infinite place
the two unitary diagonal blocks give a unitary matrix, which gives the
block diagonal inclusion of standard compact subgroups. The restricted
compact action then follows from right equivariance of the constant term
and commutation of the finite and infinite adelic factors.
