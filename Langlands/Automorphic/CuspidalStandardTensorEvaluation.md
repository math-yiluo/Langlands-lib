# Tensor evaluation at a cuspidal standard-module parameter

`CuspidalStandardTensorEvaluationStatement` concerns an actual cuspidal realization `T` in positive rank over a number field with compact adele quotient. At a real or complex place, suppose `T` has a standard-module parameter with compact-finite space `V`. Write `M` for the complex vector space of all linear maps `V → T.Space` intertwining the compact and complexified Lie actions at that place. The statement makes the existing evaluation `V ⊗ M → T.Space`, sending `v ⊗ A` to `A v`, bijective. The published `GKTensorEvaluation.evaluation_compact` and `evaluation_lie` identify the actions of the chosen place with the corresponding operators on `V` tensored with the identity on `M`.

`CuspidalOperatorRealization.standardTensorEvaluation` uses the standard-module irreducibility and admissibility theorems. The published compact-finiteness and continuity results supply the other hypotheses of Jacobson density, so `GKTensorEvaluation.evaluation_injective` gives injectivity. For surjectivity the proof takes an embedding `A` furnished by the parameter. Its Lie equivariance extends to the complexified Lie algebra by tensor induction, and its range is an irreducible stable subspace. Cuspidal place isotypy spans the whole realization by images of intertwiners from this range. Composing each such intertwiner with `A` restricted to its range shows that each image lies in the image of tensor evaluation.

This is a one-place consequence of the tensor-product description in D. Flath, *Decomposition of representations into tensor products* (1979), Theorem 3 and its number-field formulation in §3, printed pp. 181–182 (PDF pp. 177–178 of `flath-1979-tensor-products`). The argument uses the Library's isotypy of cuspidal place actions and evaluation of intertwiners. The parameter is supplied, and the factorization concerns the actions at that place.
