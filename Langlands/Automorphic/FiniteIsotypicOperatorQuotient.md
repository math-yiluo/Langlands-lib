# A simple operator subquotient preserving a prescribed finite representation

Let an algebra act on a nonzero complex space and contain representatives for the operators in two group actions. Suppose the first group acts isotypically with a specified irreducible representation. The cyclic algebra submodule generated by a nonzero vector is finitely generated, so a maximal proper submodule gives a simple quotient. The group operators on the cyclic submodule and its quotient are the restrictions of the algebra operators.

For the first group, the ambient module is the isotypic component of the specified irreducible type, so it is semisimple. Its submodule and the resulting nonzero quotient are again semisimple and isotypic of that type. A simple summand of the quotient therefore gives the required embedding. In the finite-place application the second group is the infinite compact group. The algebra also includes the enveloping operators, so its simple quotient is irreducible for the full action.

This elementary algebraic step is derived for the finite-place version of Langlands’ Lemma 1, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), printed p. 203, Corvallis Part I PDF p. 198. It applies after forming a nonzero quotient that is already isotypic for the finite representation.
