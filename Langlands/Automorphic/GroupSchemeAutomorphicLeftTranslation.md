# Left translation of adelic automorphic functions

The pullback \(x\mapsto \phi(ax)\) satisfies the actual automorphy predicate
for the conjugated left invariance subgroup \(a^{-1}\Gamma a\). It has the
same infinite finiteness subgroup and the same matrix representation for its
growth bound.

Left pullback commutes with right translation, so the original finite level
still works and the infinite orbit span is contained in the linear image of
the original finite-dimensional span. In a fixed finite slice the pullback
changes the finite coordinate of the original slice and left translates its
infinite variable. Plot smoothness is preserved. The differential operators
commute with left translation on each smooth slice. Induction through the
tensor algebra extends the commutation to the enveloping action and hence
retains the original central annihilator ideal.

Moderate growth follows from the submultiplicative normalized adelic
height, and the conversion back to the same matrix source norm. At matrix
size zero the matrix image is constant, and the growth bound is a uniform
bound. This is a derived preservation property of the automorphy conditions
in Mœglin and Waldspurger, *Spectral decomposition and Eisenstein series*
(1995), I.2.17, printed p. 37.
