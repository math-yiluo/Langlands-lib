# Algebraic coefficient probes of cuspidal block tensors

Fix a number field with compact additive adelic quotient, a finite family of cuspidal realizations of ranks `m i + 1`, and a standard-module parameter for each block at one real or complex place. `standardTensorEvaluation` identifies each carrier with its compact-finite standard module tensored with its space of simultaneous compact and Lie intertwiners. Choose one algebraic functional on each intertwiner space, contract the corresponding second factor, and evaluate the resulting standard function at the identity. `realCoefficientProbe` and `complexCoefficientProbe` take the product of these functionals on the finite algebraic tensor of block carriers.

The slice lemmas use `exists_productFunctional_slice` to choose a single carrier vector for all endomorphisms of the selected block. Tensor evaluation is equivariant and contraction commutes with the standard-module operators. This converts the carrier vector into one compact-finite standard vector. Its value at the identity recovers the probe, its restriction to the block compact group gives the probe after translation in that slot, and its derivatives at the identity give the probe after applying the Lie operators in that slot.

The Jacquet equations apply the standard-module evaluation formulas to that vector. The real formula vanishes on strictly upper triangular directions and gives `RealBlockPosition.shiftedExponent` on the diagonal units. Translation by `signReflection` gives the sign of the position character, namely `(-1)` to the power of its parity. The complex formula also vanishes on strictly upper triangular directions. On the direction `a E_jj` the eigenvalue is `ComplexSwapJacquet.kappa`. If the position character has exponent `t` and weight `l` in a rank `n` block, that scalar is `Re(a) * (2t + n - 1 - 2j) + Im(a) * I * l`, with positions starting at zero.

At each block, vanishing of all probes on all compact translates forces each contracted vector to vanish by Iwasawa decomposition in the principal series. The algebraic dual of the multiplicity space separates the remaining tensor factor. Applying separation by finite product functionals then proves the claimed separation on the whole tensor of block carriers.

This derived coefficient construction uses the Library's single-place cuspidal tensor evaluation, which is based on the algebraic factorization of D. Flath, *Decomposition of representations into tensor products* (1979), §3, Theorem 3 and the number-field formulation, printed pp. 181–182 (PDF pp. 177–178). The probes serve the application of the finite-tensor argument in R. P. Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Lemma 1, printed p. 203 (PDF p. 198), to the inducing data of Proposition 2, printed pp. 204–205 (PDF pp. 199–200).
