# Continuous changes of exponent and finite level

`weakenCLM` maps a uniform moderate growth space into one with a larger real exponent and a smaller right-invariance subgroup. It preserves the underlying function. The adelic source norm is a fixed positive multiple of the scaled height, which is at least one. Consequently the ratio of the two weights is bounded. In the defining coordinates of the growth spaces the inclusion acts by multiplication by this bounded ratio.

A finite family of sections with possibly different growth exponents and open invariance subgroups can be viewed in one Library growth space by bounding the exponents above and intersecting the subgroups. The comparison uses the Library's matrix-height weights. The scale of fixed-growth spaces in Bernstein–Lapid, *On the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series* (2024), §7.1, printed pp. 210–211, motivates the inclusion.
