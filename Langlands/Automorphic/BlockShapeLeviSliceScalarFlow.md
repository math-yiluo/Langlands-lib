# Scalar one-parameter subgroups on the finite Levi centre orbit

`blockLeviOneParameter` inserts the infinite adelic one-parameter subgroup of a real Lie direction into one block of the full adelic Levi. When the direction has a central enveloping generator, right composition with this flow preserves the image of the full Levi enveloping-centre orbit map of any ambient centre-finite function.

On the orbit map, the derivative is given by multiplication by the included central generator. This follows by evaluating the constant term along the one-variable section and using its differential intertwining identity. Multiplication descends to the image because a zero orbit function has zero derivative at every Levi point. The finite-dimensional differential equation for evaluation then keeps the entire translated function in that image. The restriction of the original constant term belongs to the same image by applying the unit of the enveloping algebra.

This proves the one-parameter part of the finite orbit under the idele centre used in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), printed pp. 204–205. The flow identity derives that part from the infinitesimal centre bound in the formalization of Lemma 4.
