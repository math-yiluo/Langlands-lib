# Operator commutation with the compact factors away from a place

The real and complex compact complements consist of the archimedean
compact tuples whose chosen place coordinate is one. Every full compact
element decomposes as an element of that complement followed by an
element at the chosen place. The two factors commute by coordinatewise
multiplication.

At the operator level finite translation commutes with compact
translation and with differentiation. A complementary compact factor
also commutes with the derivatives at the chosen place, since
the conjugating unitary matrix in the place adjoint identity is one.
These equalities take place in the actual automorphic operator algebra,
so they hold in every module for that algebra.

The assertions are derived from the Library's product compact
coordinates and the finite and archimedean differential covariance
identities. They compare compact coefficient evaluations based at
different finite and complementary compact points.
