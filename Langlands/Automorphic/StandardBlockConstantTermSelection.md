# Cyclic selection of a standard block constant term

The finite filtration lemma applies to the maps from the original numerator
to its quotients by the block constant-term kernels. The empty-cut map has
trivial kernel. The selected term of the cyclic numerator keeps a surjection
to the original irreducible quotient, and every strict refinement of its
cut set vanishes on that cyclic numerator. This is the minimal-parabolic
selection step in Langlands's proof of Proposition 2, printed p. 204, final
paragraph (Corvallis Part I, PDF p. 199). The registered source is
`langlands-1979-notion-automorphic-representation`, SHA-256
`3b9fe0145da81d870ef8967be83635cd440a2581fe043e64e63324e22dec4db5`.
