# Local components of automorphic representations at the finite places

Lean module: `Langlands.Automorphic.LocalComponentFinitePlace`. It imports
`Langlands.Representation.Smooth.JointIsotypicComponent` (the helper module of
this proof), `Langlands.Automorphic.OperatorSubquotientLocalComponentStatement`
(the target statement), `Langlands.Automorphic.CuspidalContragredientLocalCompatibilityProof`
(the local centralizer), `Langlands.Automorphic.OperatorSubquotientArchimedean`,
`Langlands.Global.FiniteAdelicGL.CompactOpenRefinement` and
`Langlands.Global.FiniteAdelicGL.PadicComparison`.

This module is part of the proof of
`Langlands.Automorphic.LocalComponentExistenceStatement`. It
proves the **finite-place conjunct** of the target and reduces the target to
its real-place and complex-place conjuncts. Those conjuncts are proved in
`OperatorSubquotientPlaceComponent` and
`OperatorSubquotientPlaceComponentComplex`, and the target itself in
`LocalComponentExistenceProof` (`Langlands.Automorphic.localComponentExistence`).

## Main theorems

```lean
theorem Langlands.Automorphic.localComponentExistence_finitePlace (r : ℕ)
    (Q : IrreducibleOperatorSubquotient F r) (hQ : Q.subquotient.IsJointlyAdmissible)
    (v : FinitePlace F) :
    ∃ A : AutomorphicObject.{u, u} (padicField v) r.succPNat,
      Q.subquotient.IsFinitePlaceIsotypic v A.obj.representation

theorem Langlands.Automorphic.localComponentExistenceStatement_of_infinitePlaces
    (h : ∀ (F : Type u) [Field F] [NumberField F] (r : ℕ)
      (Q : IrreducibleOperatorSubquotient F r), Q.subquotient.IsJointlyAdmissible →
      (real-place conjunct) ∧ (complex-place conjunct)) :
    LocalComponentExistenceStatement.{u}
```

The first is clause 1 of the target verbatim (for `F : Type u`). In the
second, the hypothesis `h` is clauses 2 and 3 of the target, copied verbatim
from the statement; the proof inserts the first theorem as clause 1.

## Proof of the finite-place conjunct

Write `n = r + 1`, `S = Q.subquotient`, `σ = S.localRepresentation v` (the
restriction of `S` to `GL_n(F_v)`) and `κ = S.compactRepresentation` (the
standard compact subgroup at infinity).

1. **Commuting operators** (`localCentralizerOperators`,
   `commutesWith_localCentralizerOperators`). `C` is the set of operators
   `S.representation a` for `a` in the concrete operator algebra whose
   underlying ambient operator lies in the published `localCentralizer n v`
   (it commutes with every local translation at `v`). These commute with `σ`.
2. **Joint irreducibility** (`generator_mem_of_localStable`,
   `representation_mem_of_localStable`,
   `isJointlyIrreducible_localCentralizerOperators`). A subspace stable under
   `σ` and `C` is stable under the whole operator algebra, by induction over
   `Algebra.adjoin` (the algebra is generated by finite translations, the
   compact action and the enveloping action). A finite translation `d` is
   `inc_v(g) · d'` with `proj_v(d') = 1` (`exists_localSplit`), so it acts by
   `σ g` times an element of `C` (`finite_mem_localCentralizer`); compact and
   enveloping generators lie in `C` (`compact_mem_localCentralizer`,
   `enveloping_mem_localCentralizer`). Irreducibility of `S` finishes.
3. **The subspace `P`** (`awaySubgroup`, `awayFixed`, `awayFixedLocal`,
   `awayFixedCompact`). Take `x₀ ≠ 0` and an open `L₀ ≤ GL_n(𝔸_f)` fixing it
   (`finiteRepresentation_isSmooth`). `P` is the space of vectors fixed by the
   elements of `L₀` with identity `v`-component. It is stable under `σ`
   (those elements commute with `inc_v(GL_n(F_v))`,
   `localInclusion_commute_of_localProjection_eq_one`) and under `κ`
   (`finiteRepresentation_compactRepresentation`), and contains `x₀`.
4. **The type `τ`.** `E` is the `κ`-orbit span of `x₀`, finite-dimensional by
   `compactRepresentation_isFiniteVector`; `τ` is `κ` on `E`, transported to
   `Fin m → ℂ` along a basis (`LinearEquiv.conjAlgEquiv`). The map
   `φ₀ : Fin m → ℂ → S.Space` (inverse coordinates, then inclusion) is
   `K_∞`-equivariant with values in `P`, and `φ₀` of the coordinates of `x₀`
   is `x₀ ≠ 0`.
5. **Finiteness** (`comparisonSubgroup`,
   `finiteRepresentation_eq_of_mem_comparisonSubgroup`,
   `equivariantInto_le_jointMultiplicitySpace`). For an open `L ≤ GL_n(F_v)`,
   let `L̃ = proj_v⁻¹(L) ∩ L₀ ∩ (inc_v ∘ proj_v)⁻¹(L₀)`, an open subgroup of
   `GL_n(𝔸_f)`. Every `d ∈ L̃` is `inc_v(proj_v d)` times an element of `L₀`
   with identity `v`-component, so it fixes every vector of `P` fixed by `L`.
   Hence the `K_∞`-equivariant maps from `τ` into `P ∩ V^L` lie in the joint
   multiplicity space of `L̃` and `τ`, which is finite-dimensional by joint
   admissibility (`Langlands.IsJointlyAdmissible`).
6. **Existence and isotypy** (`exists_isotypic_subrepresentation`).
   `JointIsotypic.exists_irreducible_admissible_isotypic_of_typeSpan`, with
   compact open subgroups cofinal in `GL_n(F_v)` (`compactOpen_cofinal`),
   gives an irreducible admissible subrepresentation `U` of `σ` such that the
   images of the intertwining maps `U → σ` span `S.Space`.
7. **The correspondence object.** `U` is admissible for the completion
   topology on `GL_n(F_v)`; `isAdmissible_canonicalTopology` transports this
   to the canonical p-adic topology of `AutomorphicObject` along
   `FiniteAdelicGL.localGL_topology_eq`, as `PadicLocalCategory.lean` does.
   `A = ⟨SmoothRep.of U.toRepresentation _, irreducible, admissible⟩`; its
   carrier is a submodule of `S.Space`, in universe `u`.

## Provenance and route

The target is the existence half of Getz–Hahn, *An introduction to automorphic
representations* (2024), Theorem 6.3.7, p.165 (PDF 179) with its proof on
p.166 (PDF 180) (`getz-hahn-2024-automorphic-representations`, SHA256
`a125cf9c0f445972cbbd3552b4fb9905ca948108a05880f90eaa43558d09a507`; in
Chapter 6, printed page = PDF page − 14), and of Flath's Theorem 3, p.181
(PDF 177) (`flath-1979-tensor-products`, SHA256
`3b9fe0145da81d870ef8967be83635cd440a2581fe043e64e63324e22dec4db5`; printed
page = PDF page + 4), read through isotypic restriction at one place as in
Flath's proof of Theorem 1, p.180 (PDF 176): "the restriction of `W` to
`G_i` is `W_i`-isotypic".

The implemented proof splits the operators
into those at `v` and the commuting ones, but it does not build a tensor
factorization `Q ≅ π_v ⊗ π^v`. It finds `π_v` as an irreducible
subrepresentation by the minimal-dimension and averaging argument of the
published two-factor proof, and proves isotypy directly from joint
irreducibility (see `JointIsotypicComponent.md`). Joint admissibility enters
only through the finiteness in step 5. Getz–Hahn's route is not
used.
