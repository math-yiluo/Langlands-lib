# Reconstructing finite-level adelic functions from smooth slices

This is an internal derived construction over the published actual adelic group
and real-smooth archimedean functions, for every number field universe and every
natural rank. It imposes one specified open finite subgroup fixing the entire
family of slices and the explicit rational covariance identity. The resulting
submodule consists of actual smooth functions, not abstract representation data.

The reconstructed function evaluates the slice indexed by the finite projection
at the infinite projection. Its joint continuity follows locally: near a finite
argument b, the open coset bL has exactly the same slice, so evaluation
agrees with a continuous function of the infinite coordinate alone. Rational
covariance proves rational-left-invariance. Reconstruction is complex linear,
retains real smoothness and the specified open finite stabilizer, and is injective
because evaluation on each finite/infinite pair recovers the original slice.

This unit supplies a prerequisite for descending actual differential operators
to finite-level adelic functions. It does not yet assert that differentiation
preserves rational covariance, nor define automorphic forms or claim full
infinite-translation stability of center-finite or compact-finite vectors.
