# A common differential polynomial from fixed compact profiles

The proof replaces the supplied profile space by its finite-dimensional
right-stable hull. Along a smooth compact curve through the identity, the
pointwise derivative of a translated profile stays in this hull because a
finite-dimensional subspace of the product space is closed. Thus the compact
real generators and the scaled sum of their squares preserve the space of
smooth functions whose profiles belong to the hull.

Restrict that space to the compact subgroup. A function vanishing on the
subgroup has zero derivative along every one of the compact curves at every
point of the subgroup. The sum of squares therefore descends to an
endomorphism of the finite-dimensional image of restriction. Its minimal
polynomial kills the restriction of the sum-of-squares operator applied to
any lifted function. Actual left pullback commutes with the differential
operators and takes the profile at the identity to the profile at a general
point. Applying the same polynomial to all left pullbacks proves the common
annihilator on the entire infinite point group.

Harish-Chandra, *Two theorems on semi-simple Lie groups* (1966), Lemma 33,
printed pp. 108–109, uses the algebraic finiteness of the compact part of the
Casimir in constructing an elliptic equation. The argument here obtains a
single compact polynomial from the common profile-space hypothesis.
