# Pair L-factor comparison under norm twists at spherical places

`WeakInductionGoodPairTwistStatement` compares a cuspidal pair over a base field with a finite product of pairs over an extension at one base place. All original ordinary and dual presentations are spherical at the places in the comparison, and every upper place has ramification index one. If the two ordinary L-values and the two dual L-values agree before twisting, the same equalities hold after determinant twisting the first member by a Hecke character downstairs and by its idele-norm pullback upstairs. The equalities are pointwise in the original spectral parameter.

`weakInductionGoodPairTwist` calculates on the original presentations. For an unramified character the local ordinary polynomial scales by its uniformizer value and the local dual polynomial by the inverse. The local norm raises the downstairs uniformizer value to the residue degree. That is also the power relating the upstairs spectral point to the downstairs one. A single translation of the spectral parameter therefore works in each upper completion, and its negative gives the dual translation. For a ramified character, norm surjectivity on valuation units keeps each upper component ramified. All the relevant L-polynomials are then one by the rank-one block calculation for twists of spherical pairs.

Henniart and Herb, *Automorphic induction for GL(n) (over local non-archimedean fields)* (1995), give the untwisted ordinary pair comparison in Lemma 8.13, p. 179, and the partial dual L-factor equality in §8.16, equation (3), p. 181. The statement here takes both untwisted identities as inputs and supplies the L-factor calculation for twists at spherical auxiliary places, in the setting of the comparison around equations (16)–(19) on p. 183.
