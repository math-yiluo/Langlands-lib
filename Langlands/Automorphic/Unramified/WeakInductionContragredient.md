# Contragredients of cuspidal weak inductions

The contragredients of a given cuspidal weak induction satisfy the same arithmetic weak-induction relation on their supplied genuine presentations. The rank and the finite-place hypotheses are inherited from the original relation. The field and presentation universes are independent.

At a place where all four spectral systems are defined, `eigenvalues_eq_map_inv_of_isContragredientOf` inverts the Satake spectrum. Inversion takes the Euler polynomial to its reverse divided by its leading coefficient. The proof shows that this operation is multiplicative and commutes with substitution of a positive power of the indeterminate. Applying it to the original induced Euler identity yields the identity for the contragredients.
