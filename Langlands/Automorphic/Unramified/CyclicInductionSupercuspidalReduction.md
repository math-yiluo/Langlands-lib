# Reduction of unique-place supercuspidality to local induction and matching

`CyclicInductionMatchingAtUniquePlaceStatement` is the matched-existence input for the finite-place comparison in Henniart (2012), Theorems 3 and 5, printed pp. 5 and 7. It specializes the comparison to prime degree, a unique place upstairs and a supplied unitary regular source with a supercuspidal local factor. It asks for a genuine cuspidal weak induction with an actual spectral presentation and the local conditions of Theorem 6 on the indicated factors. The rank equation is the rank of induction already determined by weak matching.

`CyclicLocalInductionSupercuspidalCriterionStatement` formulates the prime-degree case of Henniart–Herb (1995), Proposition 5.5, printed pp. 152–153, in the local characterization of Henniart (2012), Theorem 6. For the criterion, the input representation has vanishing proper Jacquet quotients and trivial Galois stabilizer. The conclusion is vanishing proper Jacquet quotients and admissibility for the downstairs representation. Identification of the local characterization with the Henniart–Herb lift is part of the input required to prove this criterion by that route.

`isCyclicAlgebra_of_galois_generator` uses the degree formula for the order of the Galois group. An element fixed by the generator is fixed by every group element, so it belongs to the image of the base field. The proof transfers generation through `uniquePlaceCompletionAutEquiv` to apply the lemma to the actual completions. Their degree equals the global degree.

`LocalRealization.isSupercuspidal_iff_representation` passes between the canonical p-adic class formulation and the Jacquet-vanishing property of the original action. For the forward implication it chooses a representative of the supercuspidal class and uses the equivalence supplied by class equality. For the reverse implication the original action itself represents a supercuspidal class. The completion topology agrees with the canonical p-adic topology.

`cyclicWeakInductionSupercuspidal_of_matching` first deduces the two regularity conditions from the actual conjugate factors. Global regularity permits the matched-existence input to supply an induction. Local stabilizer triviality and the local criterion then give supercuspidality for its matched factor. Euler constituent recovery identifies that weak induction with the class supplied in the target. The theorem takes matching, the local criterion and rigidity as explicit premises. The totally complex specialization introduces the extra base-field instance.
