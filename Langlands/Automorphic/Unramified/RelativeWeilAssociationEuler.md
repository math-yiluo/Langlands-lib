# Weak association under Euler-polynomial equality

`IsWeaklyAssociated.of_sameEulerPolynomials` transports weak association of a finite relative Weil representation between two spectral systems whose Euler polynomials agree outside a finite set. It retains the original representation, continuity and rank data and intersects the three cofinite conditions needed for local matching.

The lemma is moved unchanged from [IsobaricWeilAssociation](IsobaricWeilAssociation.md) for reuse by actual spectral presentations. It needs no compactness assumption on the adelic quotient.
