The proof chooses the actual realizations and presentations witnessing the two spectral attachments and uses the common unitary normalization. Universe lifting preserves their numerical spectra and permits use of a single factor universe in the partial pair statements. The actual unitary contragredient of the second normalized realization has a genuine presentation. Flipping its nonzero invariant pairing puts the contragredient relation in the orientation of the pole criterion. Both products use that same second presentation. Their first-argument presentations come from the given spectral attachments by actual twists. In particular, the self-pair pole criterion applies directly to the zero-power twist's own presentation.

The character is norm-trivial, so its degree-th power is one and its powers are unitary. At a cofinite set of places the order of its uniformizer value is a positive residue degree, dividing the global degree. The equality of residue-degree powers and the local cyclotomic identity give equal sums of orders for the cross and self products with one finite omitted set. The self product has a term of order `-1` at the zero power, and every self term has nonpositive order. If no cross term were a contragredient pair, each cross term would have order zero. Equality of the sums therefore yields a power for which the first normalized realization, twisted by that power, is a contragredient of the common second realization. Uniqueness of the full-operator contragredient identifies it with the second normalized realization.

Actual determinant multipliers commute by pointwise commutativity. Twisting both sides of the resulting equivalence by the inverse of the common normalizing character cancels that character. The remaining twist is a determinant multiple by a finite-order norm-trivial character. Transporting the finite-order determinant twist equations along the full-operator equivalence gives the conclusion for the original classes.
