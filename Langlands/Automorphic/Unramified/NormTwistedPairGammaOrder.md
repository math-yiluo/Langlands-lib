# Localization of the gamma order for norm-twisted cuspidal pairs

`NormTwistedPairGammaOrderStatement` derives a local equality of gamma orders from the functional-equation inputs for the supplied determinant-twist families and cofinite spherical matching of both ordinary and dual pair L-values. The downstairs gamma order at a finite place equals the sum of the orders for the given upper pairs at every place above it. This holds at each complex spectral point. The factors come from fixed automorphic pair setups with actual cuspidal spectral presentations and contragredients.

`normTwistedPairGammaOrder` forms an initial exceptional set containing the chosen place and the complement of the cofinite spherical matching. It chooses two characters trivial at infinity. The first is sufficiently ramified at every place of the initial set. The second is trivial at the chosen place and sufficiently ramified elsewhere in the set. The thresholds are chosen so that the downstairs pair and every upstairs pair have gamma order zero at the places where high ramification is prescribed. The upper thresholds follow by norm pullback from the same downstairs choice.

For each twist, the supplied inputs give a finite exceptional set and Euler products and a functional equation for each finite enlargement. The upper equations multiply and group along the restriction of places. Outside the initial exceptional set, the two ordinary and the two dual L-values agree under all the chosen twists. On any additional finite place in the enlargement, these identities give equal gamma orders because each finite epsilon factor is analytic and nonzero. The first equation comparison identifies the archimedean product orders. The archimedean factors are the same on each side for the second twist, so the second comparison isolates the order at the chosen finite place. The trivial local component of the second character, and its trivial pullback to every completion above the place, recover the original gamma orders.

This is the order comparison from the two-twist method of Henniart and Herb, *Automorphic induction for GL(n) (over local non-archimedean fields)* (1995), Lemma 8.13 and §§8.14–8.16. Epsilon factors contribute order zero at the auxiliary finite places.
