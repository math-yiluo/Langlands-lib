# Square-integrability under split completion comparison

This internal lemma supplies the square-integrability transport used in the split-place argument in Arthur–Clozel, *Simple Algebras, Base Change, and the Advanced Theory of the Trace Formula* (1989), I.5, pp. 48–49 and III.5.1, pp. 212–213, and in Harris–Taylor, *The Geometry and Cohomology of Some Simple Shimura Varieties* (2001), VII.1.9, p. 229. It takes the local representation equivalence as a hypothesis.

Local degree one makes the actual completion map bijective. Its embedding property makes the resulting field isomorphism a homeomorphism, and the actual and canonical completion topologies agree. Applying this isomorphism entrywise gives the group equivalence used in the comparison. Irreducibility transports along the intertwining equivalence and through the surjective change of group.

For each Haar measure on the upper quotient by the centre, the proof pushes the measure to the lower quotient by the inverse group equivalence. It applies the lower square-integrability predicate to the resulting Haar measure and pulls the coefficient bounds back. Composing the two changes of group recovers the upper representation. The fields may lie in independent universes.
