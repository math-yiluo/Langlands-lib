# Proof of the totally complex reduction of cyclic stable descent

`Langlands.Automorphic.Unramified.cyclicStableDescentTCReduction` proves
`CyclicStableDescentTCReductionStatement.{uF, uE, uV, uW}`, stated in
`Langlands.Automorphic.Unramified.CyclicStableDescentReductionStatement`: its nineteen
premises, at the universe levels written there, imply
`CyclicStableDescentTCStatement.{uF, uE, uV, uW}`. The premises are hypotheses of the
theorem; this module does not prove them. Every premise is used. The auxiliary results
below are in the namespace `Langlands.Automorphic.Unramified.CyclicStableDescentTC`.

Throughout, `E/F` is a Galois extension of number fields whose Galois group is generated
by `σ`, `F` is totally complex, and `T` is an actual cuspidal realization over `E` with
unitary central character whose class is stable under `σ`. "Weak base change" is the
published class relation `IsoClass.IsWeakBaseChangeOf`, and "twist" is the actual
finite-order determinant twist on full cuspidal classes.

## The unitary weak descent witness

The proof first constructs an actual cuspidal realization `S` over `F` with unitary
central character whose class has `T` as weak base change. This is
`exists_unitary_weakBaseChange`, with the three cases below.

### Degree one

`exists_unitary_weakBaseChange_of_finrank_eq_one`. When `[E : F] = 1` the algebra map is a
ring isomorphism `e : F ≃+* E` (Mathlib's `Module.Free.bijective_algebraMap_of_finrank_eq_one`),
and `S` is the arithmetic pullback of `T` along `e`. The pullback of the unitary central
character is a unitary central character of `S` (`HeckeCharacter.isUnitary_comap_iff`,
`CuspidalOperatorRealization.arithmeticComap_hasCentralCharacter_iff`).
`CuspidalSpectralAttachmentExistenceStatement` gives genuine spectra `s` and `t` attached to
`T` in the factor universes `uV` and `uW`, and `CuspidalSpectralArithmeticComapStatement`
attaches the reindexed system `s.reindex (finitePlaceEquiv e)` to the pullback. Every place
`w` of `E` above a place `v` of `F` is the image of `v` under the inverse of the place
equivalence `finitePlaceEquiv e` (by `finitePlaceEquiv_under`), and has ramification index one
(`ramificationIdx_eq_one_of_finrank_eq_one`, from the fundamental identity) and inertia degree
one (`inertiaDeg_eq_one_of_finrank_eq_one`). Outside a finite set of base places `v`, both
systems are defined at these places and `t` and `s` have the same eigenvalues there
(`IsoClass.HasSpectralSystem.eigenvalues_eq`). This is the weak base-change relation in
residue degree one.

### Prime degree

When `[E : F]` is prime, `CyclicPrimeStableCuspidalDescentTCStatement` gives `S` directly; its
norm-kernel character is supplied by the published `HeckeCharacter.cyclicNormKernelCharacter`.

### Composite degree

`exists_bottomPrimeStep` chooses the bottom prime step. Let `n = [E : F]`, let `p` be its
least prime factor, and let `K` be the fixed field of the subgroup generated by `σ ^ p`. The
Galois group is commutative, so this subgroup is normal and `K/F` is Galois. Mathlib's
`IntermediateField.finrank_fixedField_eq_card` gives `[E : K] = n / p`, hence `[K : F] = p`
and `[E : K] < n`. The group `Gal(E/K)` is generated by the image of `σ ^ p` under
`IntermediateField.fixingSubgroupEquiv`, and `Gal(K/F)` by the restriction `τ` of `σ`
(surjectivity of `AlgEquiv.restrictNormalHom`); `σ` and `τ` agree along `K → E`. The field
`K` is totally complex by `NumberField.isTotallyComplex_of_algebra`, and its adele quotient is
compact by `compactSpace_adeleQuotient`.

`exists_unitary_weakBaseChange_composite` then argues as follows.

1. Stability under `σ` passes to its powers (`galoisConj_pow_eq`), and conjugation depends
   only on the underlying ring automorphism (`galoisConj_eq_of_toRingEquiv_eq`), so `T` is
   stable under the generator of `Gal(E/K)`. The descent for the smaller degree `[E : K]`
   gives a cusp `S_K` over `K` with unitary central character whose class has `T` as weak
   base change.
2. `IsobaricWeakBaseChangeEquivarianceStatement`, applied to the singletons with `τ` on `K`
   and `σ` on `E`, and the stability of `T` show that the conjugate of `S_K` by `τ` also has
   `T` as weak base change.
3. The two upper systems are attached to the same class, so they agree wherever both are
   defined; this gives the hypothesis `SameResidueDegreePowers` of the fibre theorem for the
   two lower systems (`sameResidueDegreePowers_of_isArithmeticWeakBaseChangeOf`).
   `CyclicCuspidalFiberStatement` over `E/K` gives a character `χ` of `K`, trivial on the
   norms from `E`, with the conjugate of `S_K` by `τ` the twist of `S_K` by `χ`.
4. `CyclicPrimeNormCharacterRestrictionStatement` for `F ⊂ K ⊂ E` makes `χ` invariant under
   `Gal(K/F)`. With `CuspidalDeterminantTwistArithmeticComapStatement`, naturality of the twist
   under pullback along `τ⁻¹` iterates the relation: the conjugate by `τ ^ k` is the twist by
   `χ ^ k` (`isFiniteOrderDeterminantTwist_galoisConj_pow`). Since `τ ^ p = 1`, the class of
   `S_K` is fixed by the twist by `χ ^ p`.
5. The self-pair count over `E/K` (below) shows that a character of `K` trivial on norms from
   `E` and fixing the class of `S_K` is trivial; hence `χ ^ p = 1`.
6. If `χ ≠ 1`, the restriction statement makes the restriction of `χ` to the idele classes of
   `F` have kernel exactly the norms from `K`, and `CyclicPrimeTwistedCuspidalObstructionTCStatement`
   for `K/F` excludes the twisted conjugation of step 3. Hence `χ = 1` and the class of `S_K`
   is stable under `τ`.
7. `CyclicPrimeStableCuspidalDescentTCStatement` for `K/F` gives `S` over `F` with unitary
   central character whose class has `S_K` as weak base change, and
   `IsobaricWeakBaseChangeTowerStatement`, applied to the three singletons, composes the two
   weak base changes.

### Universes

The intermediate field is a type in the universe `uE` of the upper field.
`exists_unitary_weakBaseChange_sameUniverse` proves the existence for two fields in `uE`,
with factor universe `uW` on both sides, by induction on a strict upper bound for the degree:
degree one, prime degree, and the composite step with the induction hypothesis for `E/K`.
`exists_unitary_weakBaseChange` treats `F` in `uF` with lower factor universe `uV` by the
same three cases, using the same-universe result for `E/K` in the composite step. These are
the universe levels at which the statement supplies its premises.

## The self-pair count

`eq_zero_of_isFiniteOrderDeterminantTwist_self`: let `η` be a character of `F` with kernel
exactly the norms from `E`, let `S` over `F` and `T` over `E` have unitary central characters,
and let the class of `T` be a weak base change of the class of `S`. Apply
`CyclicWeakBaseChangePairMultiplicityTCStatement` with both lower data equal to the singleton
of `S` and both upper data equal to the singleton of `T`; the singletons are unitary by
`isUnitarizable_of_hasUnitaryCentralCharacter`. The upper count is one. The `k`-th lower
summand is one when the twist by `η ^ k` fixes the class of `S`, and zero otherwise; the
summand for `k = 0` is one. Therefore no exponent `0 < i < [E : F]` has the twist by `η ^ i`
fixing the class. With `HeckeCharacter.cyclicNormKernelCharacter`, which writes every
norm-trivial character as such a power, `eq_one_of_isTrivialOnNorm_of_selfTwist` concludes that
a norm-trivial character fixing the class is trivial.

## The conclusion

With `c` the class of `S`, `cyclicStableDescentTCReduction` proves the four clauses of
`CyclicStableDescentTCStatement`.

- `T` is a weak base change of `c`, by construction.
- For each `i < [E : F]` the twist of `c` by `η ^ i` exists: `η ^ i` is trivial on norms, so of
  finite order, and the published `IsoClass.determinantTwist` constructs the twisted class.
- A cuspidal class `c'` over `F` has `T` as weak base change exactly when it is the twist of
  `c` by some `η ^ i` with `i < [E : F]`. Forward: the two upper systems are compared as in
  step 3, and `CyclicCuspidalFiberStatement` over `E/F`, which has no unitarity hypothesis,
  gives a norm-trivial `χ` relating `c` and `c'`; `HeckeCharacter.cyclicNormKernelCharacter`
  writes `χ = η ^ i` with `i < [E : F]`. Backward: `CuspidalWeakBaseChangeNormTwistStatement`.
- If the twists of `c` by `η ^ i` and by `η ^ j` are the same class, with `i ≤ j < [E : F]`,
  composing the two twist relations fixes `c` under the twist by `η ^ (j - i)`, and the
  self-pair count over `E/F` gives `j - i = 0` (symmetrically if `j ≤ i`).

## Sources

The route follows the paired Markdown of the statement. Arthur and Clozel, *Simple Algebras,
Base Change, and the Advanced Theory of the Trace Formula* (1989) (`arthur-clozel-1989-base-change`),
Chapter III: Theorem 3.1, printed p. 201 (the fibre statement); Theorem 4.2(d), printed
p. 202, proved on pp. 206–207 (prime stable descent); Proposition 4.4(iii), printed p. 210
(equivariance of weak base change); Lemma 6.3, printed p. 217, whose proof reduces to the
prime case through a tower, uses the transitivity of the lifting identities (1.1), and obtains
distinctness of the twists from the orders at `s = 1` of the partial `L`-functions in
`L^S(s, π_E × π̃_E) = ∏_i L^S(s, π_F × π̃_F ⊗ η^i)`. Clozel and Rajan, *Solvable base change*,
arXiv:1806.02513v1 (`clozel-rajan-2018-solvable-base-change`): Theorem 1, PDF p. 2 (the twisted
obstruction, used here in prime degree with a finite-order character), and the descent for
cyclic extensions at the end of §2, PDF p. 7, which supplies the stability of the intermediate
descendant missing from the proof of Lemma III.6.3. Both sources take a prime step at the top
of the tower; the Lean proof takes the prime step `K/F` at the bottom, descends through `E/K`
by induction, and obtains the stability of the descendant over `K` from the fibre theorem,
the character restriction, the self-pair count and the prime obstruction, as described in
the statement's Markdown.
