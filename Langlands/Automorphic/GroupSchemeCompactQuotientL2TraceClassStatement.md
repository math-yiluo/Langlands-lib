# Trace-class convolution products on a compact actual quotient

`Langlands.Automorphic.GroupSchemeFunctions.GroupSchemeCompactQuotientL2TraceClassStatement`
is a proposition definition. It supplies no proof.

## Setting

The objects are the Library's actual positive rational quotient
`PositiveRationalQuotient F G T i`, together with its measure, `L²` space
and right action, unchanged from
`GroupSchemeCompactQuotientL2DecompositionStatement`.

- `F` is a number field and `G` an affine group scheme over `Spec F`,
  locally of finite type.
- `T` is a commutative group scheme with a split-torus isomorphism `e`.
  The morphism `i : T.toGrp ⟶ G` is central. Its character restriction is
  injective with finite-index range.
- The Haar measure of the norm-one subgroup is right invariant. These
  are exactly the hypotheses of the existing canonical quotient measure
  `positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure` and of
  `haarQuotientL2BoundedRightAction`.
- The quotient is the left coset space of `positiveRationalSubgroup`,
  generated by the positive real points of `T` and the rational points.
  It is the Library's version of `[G] = A_G G(F) \ G(A_F)`.
- `μG` is the Haar measure of the adelic point group
  `AdelicGroupPoints F G`.

The added hypothesis is that `PositiveRationalQuotient F G T i` is
compact. No connected reductivity is assumed, and compactness is not
derived from anisotropy.

## Conclusion

For all compactly supported continuous `f₁ f₂` on `AdelicGroupPoints F G`,
the Haar convolution `f₁ ⋆ f₂ : g ↦ ∫ a, f₁ a * f₂ (a⁻¹ * g) ∂μG` is
integrable, and the published weighted quotient action
`haarQuotientL2IntegratedAction F G T i hi I e hc μG (f₁ ⋆ f₂) _`, the
Bochner integral of `f₁ ⋆ f₂` against the original right action on
`L²(PositiveRationalQuotient F G T i)` for the published quotient Haar
measure, is trace class in the sense of
`Langlands.Analysis.OperatorIdeals.IsTraceClass`.

No trace-class assertion for a general smooth weight, no trace formula and
no statement for noncompact quotients or the cuspidal subspace is made.

## Sources and provenance

Getz–Hahn, *An Introduction to Automorphic Representations* (2024),
registered as `getz-hahn-2024-automorphic-representations`, SHA-256
`a125cf9c0f445972cbbd3552b4fb9905ca948108a05880f90eaa43558d09a507`:
Theorem 9.1.2, printed p.241 (PDF p.253), and the convolution step in the
proofs of Lemma 9.3.3 (PDF pp.259–260) and of Theorem 9.1.2 (§9.5, PDF
p.274). When `[G]` is compact, the Hilbert–Schmidt estimate of Lemma 9.3.3
holds on the whole `L²` space, with no cuspidality, rapid decay or reduction
theory. This statement is the compact-quotient case of that convolution
step, for compactly supported continuous weights on `G(A_F)` rather than
smooth weights on `A_G \ G(A_F)`.

It specializes
`Langlands.QuotientIntegration.CompactQuotientConvolutionTraceClassStatement`
to the actual quotient, exactly as
`GroupSchemeCompactQuotientL2DecompositionStatement` specializes the
compact-quotient Hilbert–Schmidt and decomposition statements.

## Intended proof

A private proof compiles. It supplies the instances and the compatibility
`positiveRationalQuotientHaarMeasure_isCompatible` of the actual quotient,
identifies the original right action with the coset right translation
(`coeFn_quotientL2RightTranslate`), and applies the compact left coset
space statement. It will use that statement's proof once published.
