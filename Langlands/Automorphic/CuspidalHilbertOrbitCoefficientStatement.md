# Actual determinant-band Hilbert orbit coefficients

Authors: Worker 270 and Worker 805 (initial paired draft).
Module: `Langlands.Automorphic.CuspidalHilbertOrbitCoefficientStatement`.
The four coefficient definitions are in
`Langlands.Automorphic.CuspidalHilbertOrbitCoefficient`; the proposition is
`Langlands.Automorphic.CuspidalHilbertOrbitCoefficientStatement.{u}`.

This pair states an internal analytic prerequisite for actual cuspidal operator
multiplicity one. It proves no coefficient regularity theorem.

Fix a number field `F` in an arbitrary universe with the existing compact
`AdeleQuotient F` convention, a natural `r` and an actual irreducible cuspidal
operator realization `T` of `GL_(r+1)` with unitary central character. Let `Q`
be the actual rational matrix quotient, `μ` its canonical quotient Haar measure
restricted to the open band `1 < |det| < 2`, and `w` any vector of the full
space `Lp ℂ 2 μ`. The space of `w` is not replaced by a completion chosen from
`T`, its embedded image, or a finite-vector subspace.

More precisely, the four definitions use `n = r + 1` and the following data.

- `bandMeasure F r` is
  `(OperatorDeterminantSlice.quotientHaarMeasure F n).restrict
  (OperatorDeterminantSlice.band F n)`. The quotient is the existing
  `OperatorDeterminantSlice.Quotient F n`, namely the rational left-coset
  quotient for the actual general linear group scheme, canonically identified
  with `GL_n(F) \ GL_n(A_F)`. This is the rational quotient before quotienting
  by the center. The open band uses the descended adelic determinant norm:
  `1 < quotientDeterminantNorm(q) < 2`. Its measure is the existing quotient
  of the canonical adelic Haar measure, with no additional normalization.
- `BandHilbertSpace F r` abbreviates `Lp ℂ 2 (bandMeasure F r)`. These first
  two definitions depend on `F` and `r` and are independent of `T`.
- `integrand T w f g` is the function on the whole quotient given by the
  pointwise Hermitian product below. Here `g : InfiniteAdelicGL F n`.
- `coefficient T w f g` is the Bochner integral of that integrand against
  `bandMeasure F r`.

Writing `f̄ = OperatorDeterminantSlice.toQuotient F n T.realization f`, the
coefficient is exactly

```text
C_w(f)(g) = ∫_Q inner ℂ (w(q)) (f̄(q · ofInfinite(g))) dμ(q).
```

`toQuotient` faithfully descends the original vector without changing its
underlying function. The notation `q · ofInfinite(g)` is exactly
`OperatorDeterminantSlice.translate F n (AdelicGL.ofInfinite F n g) q`.
It uses the published positive right-translation homeomorphism: a point
represented by `x` is sent to the point represented by `x * ofInfinite(g)`.
The integration band stays fixed as `g` varies.
The inner product is conjugate-linear in `w` and linear in `f`. Coercing `w`
uses its existing `Lp` representative. Integration and integrability depend
only on its almost-everywhere class. The separate integrability conclusion
prevents the totalized integral from standing in for a divergent coefficient.

The proposition universally quantifies `F : Type u` with `Field F`,
`NumberField F` and `CompactSpace (AdeleQuotient F)`, every `r : ℕ`, and every
`T : CuspidalOperatorRealization F r`. Its sole further premise is the existing
`Langlands.ClassField.Global.RelativeWeilGroup.HasUnitaryCentralCharacter T`:
there exists a continuous idele-class character `χ` with `‖χ(x)‖ = 1` at every
point and `T.HasCentralCharacter χ`. Thus `r = 0`, representing `GL_1`, is
included, and the number-field universe `u` is free.

For each fixed `w` the proposition requires integrability for every `f` and
`g`, and one complex-linear map `C : T.Space →ₗ[ℂ] SmoothFunctions F n`
whose pointwise values are exactly `coefficient T w f g`. The target consists
of actual functions on the completion-based archimedean group that are
infinitely differentiable over `ℝ` in the existing mixed matrix coordinates.
The chosen `C` may depend on `w`; no linear choice in `w` is part of the
statement.

The same map must satisfy the equality of genuine enveloping actions
`C (T.realization.envelopingAction a f) = envelopingAction F n a (C f)`
for every `a : ArchimedeanGL.EnvelopingAlgebra F n` and every `f`. This is the
full universal enveloping algebra over `ℂ` of
`ℂ ⊗[ℝ] ArchimedeanGL.RealLieAlgebra F n`. All real directions at complex
places are retained. At a real matrix generator `X`, the target differential
is the genuine right differential, with tangent direction `gX`, equivalently
the derivative of `C(f)(g exp(tX))` at zero; it changes the source vector by
its genuine Lie operator, with no sign or adjoint.

Finally each `C f` must satisfy `ArchimedeanGL.IsJointFinite F n`. This means
that the complex span of
`envelopingAction F n (centerInclusion F n z * b.val) (C f)`, as `z` runs
through the actual enveloping center and `b` through the enveloping subalgebra
generated by the complexified compact Lie directions, is finite-dimensional.
This is finiteness of each joint differential orbit; the full band Hilbert
space is not required to be finite-dimensional.

The provenance is internal derivation from published Library interfaces,
rather than transcription of a new original-source theorem. The actual
quotient, determinant norm and faithful function descent are supplied by
`OperatorDeterminantSliceGeometry`; the positive translation is in
`OperatorDeterminantSlicePairing`, and the measure is in
`OperatorDeterminantSliceMeasure`. The genuine target smooth carrier and
actions are in `ArchimedeanGL`, `ArchimedeanLieAlgebra`,
`ArchimedeanEnvelopingAction` and `ArchimedeanJointFinite`.

In `CuspidalUnitaryForm`, `integral_inner_translate` supplies
arbitrary-adelic-translate invariance of the band pairing under unitary central
character. `exists_band_cover_uniform`, `memLp_supNorm`, and the dominated
differentiation argument in `integral_inner_lieOperator_add` provide prospective
inputs for integrability and differentiation against arbitrary band `L²`
vectors. A future proof must establish scalar real smoothness in all matrix
coordinates, justify moving actual derivatives through these integrals, and
transfer the source joint finiteness through `C`. These are proof obligations,
not results established by this proposition definition.

The published smooth orbit coefficient construction and
`isJointFinite_smoothOrbitCoefficient` motivate this analogue, but use continuous
functionals for the derivative compact-open topology. In particular,
`compactLieOrbitDensity` in that topology does not supply density in the
Hilbert band topology on the noncompact quotient. No such topology comparison
is assumed here.

The intended consumer applies the published joint-finite analyticity,
enveloping-to-matrix zero-jet, analytic component uniqueness and compact
component coverage results to scalar annihilators of the actual embedded
carrier. That may supply closure invariance needed for Hilbert globalization.
The present statement does not assert that invariance, construct any full
archimedean Hilbert action, identify differentiable smooth vectors, or prove
Whittaker uniqueness or unconditional cuspidal operator multiplicity one.
