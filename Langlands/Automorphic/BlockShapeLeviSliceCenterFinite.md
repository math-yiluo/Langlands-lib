# A common infinitesimal centre bound for the block Levi slices

Fix a finite-smooth automorphic function and an ordered block shape. The period restricted to the Levi annihilates the right ideal generated by the strict upper block Lie directions. An ambient central operator and its relative Levi projection therefore agree after restriction. This equality persists after left multiplication by Levi operators because the right ideal is stable under that multiplication.

The Levi enveloping centre is generated by finitely many elements as a module over the ambient enveloping centre. The image of its orbit map into functions on the Levi is contained in the finite sum obtained by applying these generators to the original finite-dimensional ambient centre orbit and then restricting the period. The actual single-block enveloping centre maps into the full Levi centre, and the actual block differential intertwines the resulting orbit maps.

`blockShapeLeviSliceCenterAnnihilator` is the kernel of the single-block orbit map into functions on the whole Levi. Its vanishing means that the operator kills every one-variable section in that block. This kernel is an ideal, since the single-block centre acts on those sections, and it has finite complex codimension by the range bound. Each section thus satisfies the Library's simultaneous centre condition with the same ideal, for all choices of the other block variables.

This supplies the infinitesimal-centre step in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Lemma 4, printed p. 204, using the relative enveloping centre projection for the actual matrix block Levi.
