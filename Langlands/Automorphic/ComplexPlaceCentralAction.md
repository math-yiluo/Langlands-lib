# Scalar action from complex-place standard modules

This is an internal transport lemma for the bridge. An actual place-parameter
embedding intertwines the given real matrix directions. Complex linearity extends
this equality to the complexified Lie algebra, and the published enveloping
intertwining theorem extends it to every element of the place enveloping algebra.

Each embedding block center maps into the global center through the enveloping
algebra of the corresponding complex place. That image commutes with the finite
action by the published compatibility, and with the enveloping action by
centrality. For the compact action, the proof uses adjoint covariance on the
actual place inclusion. Under the two-coordinate Lie equivalence the local
adjoint is conjugation by the pair of a unitary matrix and its complex
conjugate. The published matrix conjugation theorem fixes the local enveloping
center, which contains the image of the block center.

The image of the block center therefore acts by a scalar on an irreducible
realization by the published Schur lemma. Evaluating on the image of a
nonzero compact-finite vector determines the scalar from the standard-module
calculation. The character on the global center acts by these same scalars on
every block by the restriction hypothesis. The block centers generate the
entire center, so the two algebra maps to endomorphisms agree on it.
