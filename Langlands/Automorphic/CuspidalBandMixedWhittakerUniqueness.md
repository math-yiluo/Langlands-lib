# Mixed Whittaker uniqueness on the actual cuspidal band completion

Assume continuous unitary Whittaker uniqueness for the real and complex
general linear groups. For each number field with compact additive adele
quotient, take an actual cuspidal operator realization, a supplied
continuous unitary character acting as its central character, and a
supplied unitary form computing the determinant-band inner product.
For a standard adelic additive character, two mixed Whittaker functionals
on that completion are proportional if the first is nonzero.

The original vectors have genuinely smooth orbits in the actual band
completion and joint admissibility gives the exact joint vector recovery.
The archimedean slice comparison supplies the remaining condition in the
tensor argument. The completion-level theorem combines this condition
with finite adelic uniqueness and density at every compact open level.
This is an internal reduction to the stated local archimedean uniqueness
premise.
