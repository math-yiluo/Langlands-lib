# Fixed-level smooth embeddings for actual cuspidal place components

For a cuspidal operator realization over a number field with compact
additive adele quotient, choose its unitary central character and a
unitary form computing the band inner product. At each real or complex
place, the actual Hilbert completion has the fixed-level comparison for
every irreducible admissible component fixed by the supplied finite adelic
subgroup. The comparison is a continuous linear map of genuine smooth
vector spaces and its value in the ambient completion is the original
closed-subspace inclusion. The local representation acts on that same
closure and realizes the specified compact and Lie operators.

The proof combines the actual cuspidal place Hilbert realization and
smoothness of the original embedded vectors with unitarity of the bounded
adelic action. The real and complex fixed-level assertions then follow
from the corresponding adelic completion comparison. This is an internal
application of the published cuspidal realization and the fixed-level
smooth map construction.
