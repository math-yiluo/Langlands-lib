# Product patches for the actual band Sobolev estimate

At a point in the open determinant band, choose an open matrix
neighborhood where the rational quotient map is injective and stays
in the band. Inside its product coordinates choose a closed
archimedean matrix ball and a translate of a compact open subgroup of
the specified finite level. The entire closed product patch stays in
the injective neighborhood.

For a level-fixed vector its actual values on that patch are constant
in the finite direction. The additive chart integral is therefore
bounded by a multiple of the group Haar integral on the patch.
Cauchy–Schwarz and the local quotient measure bound control that
integral by the actual determinant-band `L²` norm. The constants
depend on the patch and its finite level and apply to all the original
vectors fixed by that level.
