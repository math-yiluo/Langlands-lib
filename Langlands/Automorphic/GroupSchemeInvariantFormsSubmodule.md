# The space of adelic automorphic functions

For a fixed left invariance subgroup, archimedean finiteness subgroup,
smooth structure and growth representation, `automorphicFunctionsOn`
is the complex submodule cut out by the existing predicate
`IsAutomorphicFunctionOn`. This gives the linear operations on the
spaces used for the inducing functions in Bernstein–Lapid §2.2 and §8.6.

Left invariance and smoothness are preserved by linear operations,
using the intersection of the two finite levels for sums. The
archimedean orbit of a sum lies in the sum of the original
finite-dimensional orbit spans. The intersection of the two
annihilating ideals annihilates the sum. Its quotient embeds in the
product of the two finite-dimensional quotients.

The proof bounds a sum by the larger exponent of the two bounds
for the normalized height. The positive-dimensional representation
relates that height to the stated source norm by a positive scale.
In size zero the matrix height is constant and the sum is bounded.
Scalar multiplication changes the growth constant.
