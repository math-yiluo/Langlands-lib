# Extending place intertwiners to the common free operators

A linear map intertwining all the individual compact subgroups intertwines
the full infinite compact. The compatible elements form a submonoid.
The real and complex coordinate products belong to it because their
individual coordinate insertions do. The product maps onto the actual
infinite compact.

A complexified Lie element is the sum of its actual place directions.
Tensor induction lifts the real place decomposition to the complexification.
A linear map intertwining the Lie generators at all places therefore
intertwines every global Lie generator. The universal enveloping induction
extends this identity to scalars, sums and products.

`freeActionOfOperators` extends the finite translation endomorphisms, the
compact representation and the enveloping action through the free algebra.
`intertwines_freeActionOfOperators_of_places` combines the two
archimedean extension results with equivariance for each finite
translation. Induction on the common free algebra extends the resulting
generator identities to the full action.

These are internal steps for the simultaneous place comparison in Getz–Hahn
(2024), Theorem 10.7.5, printed p. 288 (PDF p. 300), and the operator action
used in the proof of Proposition 2 of Langlands (1979), printed pp. 204–206
(Corvallis I PDF pp. 199–201).
