# Levi slices in the fixed-character cuspidal realization

The actual slice map first lands in the finite-smooth function space. Its automorphic property gives membership in the full stable form space, and the strict-refinement vanishing and block-scalar eigenrelation give membership in the specified central-character cuspidal realization. Restricting the codomain along these inclusions gives linear maps with the original evaluated functions.

The faithful ambient inclusion transfers the finite, compact and real Lie action identities to the realized slices. Applying a Hecke determinant twist defines new realized slices whose evaluation is the original slice times the determinant character. Both identities are expressed directly on the translated-period quotient.

This is the realization step for the cuspidal Levi coefficients in Task 6823, following Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Lemma 4 and the deduction of Proposition 2 from Lemma 6, printed pp. 204–205.
