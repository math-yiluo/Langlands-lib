# Detection at an infinite place of an irreducible operator realization

Module `Langlands.Automorphic.PlaceComponentRealization`. Worker 174 wrote it as a step
towards `Langlands.Automorphic.CuspidalArchimedeanIrreducibleAdmissibleComponentStatement` (G1,
Task 5992). Provenance: internal; it applies `PlaceComponentDetection` to the Library's place
actions.

Let `S` be an operator realization of `GL_n` over a number field `F` and `v` an infinite place
of `F`. The place actions at `v` are the compact action of `K_v = O(n)` or `U(n)` and the Lie
action.

## Brackets and commutation

- `lie_realPlaceLieInclusion_of_ne`, `lie_complexPlaceLieInclusion_of_ne` and
  `lie_realPlace_complexPlaceLieInclusion` (namespace `Langlands.Automorphic`): images of the
  Library's place Lie inclusions at distinct places have zero bracket. The proof goes through
  the Lie isomorphism `realLiePlaceCoordinates` with the product of the place factors. Their
  coordinates are supported at different places.
- `lie_realPlaceComplexLieInclusion_of_ne`, `lie_complexPlaceComplexLieInclusion_of_ne` and
  `lie_realPlace_complexPlaceComplexLieInclusion`: the same for the complexified inclusions.
  The proof is by induction on tensors, using `LieAlgebra.ExtendScalars.bracket_tmul`.
- `unitaryMatrixComplexLieConjugation_one'`: conjugation by `1` is the identity (a public copy
  of a private Library lemma).
- `OperatorRealization.realPlaceLieAction_comm_of_ne`, `complexPlaceLieAction_comm_of_ne` and
  `realPlaceLieAction_comm_complexPlace`: the corresponding Lie actions commute. The Library's
  `complexLieAction` is a Lie homomorphism into endomorphisms, whose bracket is the
  commutator.

## Local finiteness and weak continuity

`isLocallyFinite_realPlace` and `isLocallyFinite_complexPlace`: orbit spans are finite-dimensional
and stable (Library `realPlaceCompactRepresentation_isFiniteVector`, `FiniteVectors.orbitSpan`).
`isWeaklyContinuous_realPlace` and `isWeaklyContinuous_complexPlace` are the Library's
`continuous_dual_realPlaceCompactRepresentation` and its complex version.

## The free set is invariant

`realFreeSet S v τ` (or `complexFreeSet`) is `PlaceComponent.freeSet` for the place actions
at `v`. It is preserved by the following operators.

- Finite translations (`realFreeSet_finite`): they commute with the place actions (Library
  `finiteRepresentation_realPlaceCompactRepresentation`, `finiteRepresentation_realPlaceLieAction`).
- Lie actions at all places (`realFreeSet_realLie`, `realFreeSet_complexLie`): at `v` they
  preserve hulls; elsewhere they commute with the place actions (Library
  `realPlaceCompactRepresentation_realPlaceLieAction_of_ne` and its variants, and the
  commutation above).
- The complexified global Lie action (`realFreeSet_complexLieAction`). By tensor induction it
  reduces to `complexify X`, which is a sum of place actions (Library
  `complexify_action_eq_sum_place`).
- The enveloping action (`realFreeSet_enveloping`). The enveloping algebra is generated by
  the Lie algebra (Library `LieCasimir.enveloping_adjoin_range`), and `envelopingAction` is an
  algebra homomorphism.
- Compact translations (`realFreeSet_compact`). Write `k = toStandardCompact p`
  (`toStandardCompact_surjective`) and `p = q_v · p'`, with `q_v` the `v`-factor and `p'`
  equal to `1` at `v`. Then `p'` commutes with the `v`-factor. It commutes with the Lie
  action at `v` by the Library's full compact covariance, where conjugation by `1` is
  trivial. The `v`-factor preserves hulls.
- The whole concrete operator algebra (`realFreeSet_operator`), by `Algebra.adjoin_induction`
  over its three generator families.

`realFreeSet_eq_bot`: if `S` is irreducible (`IsSimpleOrder` of its subrepresentations) and
some vector is not free, the free set, a subrepresentation, is `⊥`.

## Detection

`exists_realPlace_detection` and `exists_complexPlace_detection`: if `S` is irreducible and
nonzero, there is a continuous matrix representation `τ` of `K_v` with a nonzero
`τ`-intertwiner into every nonzero place-stable subspace. Take a nonzero vector, get `τ` from
`PlaceComponent.exists_not_mem_freeSet`, and apply the previous result.

## Lean notes

The proofs avoid `rw`, `obtain` and `subst` on goals that contain the realization's
operators, which timed out. They build equations with `congrArg` and `Eq.trans` and transport
membership with `Eq.mpr`.
