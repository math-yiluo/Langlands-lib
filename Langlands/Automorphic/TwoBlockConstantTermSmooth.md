# Differentiating two-block constant terms in the infinite factor

Harish-Chandra, *Automorphic forms on semisimple Lie groups* (1968),
Chapter I, §6, proof of Theorem 1, printed pp. 19–20 (PDF pp. 29–30),
uses the compatibility of differentiation and constant terms in the rank
induction. Here the actual unnormalized adelic block constant term of any
finite-smooth rational-invariant function has smooth infinite slices.
The real right Lie derivative of such a slice is the slice of the
constant term of the differentiated source function.

For each integration point, a lift to an adelic unipotent matrix expresses
the integrand in a fixed source slice. In the open chart of invertible
archimedean matrices, the parameter enters that slice by left
multiplication. Its derivative at $x$ in direction $xV$ evaluates the
actual source Lie derivative in direction $V$.

To pass this calculation under the integral, expand a general chart
direction in coordinates after left multiplication by $x^{-1}$. Each
coefficient of the derivative is then a constant-term integrand for a
fixed real Lie derivative of the source. These integrands are jointly
continuous on the quotient and the archimedean group. Compactness of the
quotient and of a small closed chart ball gives the uniform bound for
dominated differentiation. The resulting finite sum for each chart
derivative proves smoothness by induction on the order, since the
source Lie derivatives again belong to the finite-smooth space.
