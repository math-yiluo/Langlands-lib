# Constructibility of finite incidence quotient counts

This internal construction supplies the finite combinatorial step in geometric
component-count arguments. It uses Boolean closure of constructible sets,
`Quot.congr`, `ENat.card_congr`, and the finite closed-cover quotient-component
formula. The parameter space is any topological space; no Noetherian or
quasi-compactness hypothesis is required.

Let `R p i j` be a family of relations on one fixed finite label type, indexed
by points `p` of a parameter space. Suppose each edge locus
`{p | R p i j}` is constructible. `finiteRelationPattern R Q` defines the cell
where every edge has precisely the truth value prescribed by `Q`. Such a fixed
complete truth pattern is a finite intersection of these edge loci and their
complements, hence constructible.
There are finitely many binary relations on a finite set. The locus where the
quotient generated by `R p` has exactly `n` classes is the union of the complete
truth-pattern cells for the relations whose quotient has `ENat.card = n`.
This is a finite union of constructible sets. Equal truth patterns give
equivalent quotients, which justifies the cardinality comparison.

`isConstructible_componentCount_of_finite_incidence` applies this result when
the actual connected-component count of a family of topological spaces is
given by that finite quotient. The final specialization,
`isConstructible_componentCount_of_closedCoverIncidence`, concerns a family
of quotient maps `A p → B p`, each supplied with a finite closed cover of its
source by nonempty connected subspaces using the same fixed labels.
Constructibility of the predicates that two cover members have intersecting
images implies constructibility of every component-count locus in the family
`B p`. It uses `componentCount_eq_closedCoverIncidence` to compute the counts.

These statements do not produce the common finite labels, the connected closed
covers, or constructibility of their incidence predicates for a geometric
family. Those are the separate geometric inputs needed for the algebraic-space
target. Empty label sets and empty fibres are included.
