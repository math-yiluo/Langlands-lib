# Berkovich's site `P(G)` of discrete `G`-sets

Author: Worker 21.

For a topological group `G`, this module defines Berkovich's category `P(G)`
of discrete `G`-sets and its Grothendieck topology. A `G`-set is discrete when
every stabilizer is open. The coverings are the surjective families of
`G`-maps. Namespace: `Langlands`, with the site in `Langlands.DiscreteGSet`.
Every statement in the module is proved.

## Original source

Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic spaces* (unfinished
manuscript, 1995–1996), registered as
`provided-7bb3903652336bd799aad8853b8efd7ff3bcc0c7652745417f4745a14ac08e90`,
§1.2, printed p.4:

> Let P(G) denote the category of G-sets endowed with the Grothendieck
> topology generated by the pretopology for which the sets of coverings
> consist of surjective families of G-maps. It is well known that any sheaf
> F ∈ P(G)~ is representable by the G-set ∪_N F(G/N), where N runs through
> open subgroups of G.

In this passage "G-set" means discrete `G`-set. On the same page Berkovich
calls the target of `Γ_{X{G}}` "the category of (discrete) G-sets". In the
proof of Proposition 1.3.1 (printed p.5), to show that the action of `G` on a
set is discrete he shows that each stabilizer contains an open subgroup. The
representability claim above also needs open stabilizers. So this module
defines discrete as "all stabilizers are open". For a subgroup, containing an
open subgroup is the same as being open.

Berkovich uses `P(G)` as the target of the morphism of sites
`γ : X(G)_qét → P(G)` given by `Σ ↦ X_Σ(G)`, on the same page. That functor
is in module `Langlands.Geometry.Analytic.SetInducedSite`.

## Definitions

Let `G : Type z` be a group.

- `Action.instMulActionType S`: the action of `G` on the underlying type `S.V`
  of `S : Action (Type z) G`, with `g • s = S.ρ g s` (`Action.smul_eq_ρ_apply`,
  which holds by `rfl`). This is Mathlib's `Action.instMulAction` restated for
  `Type z`, as Mathlib already does for `FintypeCat`, so that type class
  inference finds it.
- `Action.gMap f : S.V →[G] T.V`: the `G`-equivariant map underlying a morphism
  `f : S ⟶ T` of `G`-sets, with `Action.gMap_apply`, `Action.gMap_id` and
  `Action.gMap_comp`. These connect Mathlib's `Action (Type z) G` with the
  `MulAction` API used for `X_Σ` in module `SetInducedSpace`.

Now let `G` also carry a topology.

- `discreteGSetProperty G`: the property of `S : Action (Type z) G` that
  `MulAction.stabilizer G s` is open for every `s : S.V`.
- `DiscreteGSet G`: Berkovich's category `P(G)`, the full subcategory of
  `Action (Type z) G` on the discrete `G`-sets. Its morphisms are all
  `G`-maps.
- `DiscreteGSet.forget G : DiscreteGSet G ⥤ Type z`: the underlying set.
- `DiscreteGSet.of G S hS`: the discrete `G`-set of a type `S` with
  `[MulAction G S]` and a proof `hS` that its stabilizers are open.
- When `G` is a topological group: `DiscreteGSet.isOpen_stabilizer_quotient`
  proves that the stabilizers of `G ⧸ N` are open when `N` is an open subgroup.
  The stabilizer of `gN` is `g N g⁻¹`, the preimage of `N` under the
  continuous map `k ↦ g⁻¹ k⁻¹ g`. `DiscreteGSet.quotient G N hN` is the
  discrete `G`-set `G ⧸ N`. These are the `G`-sets `G/N` of Berkovich's
  passage.
- `DiscreteGSet.precoverage G`: a presieve on `S` covers when its arrows are
  jointly surjective on underlying sets. It is the pullback along `forget G`
  of Mathlib's `Types.jointlySurjectivePrecoverage`. These are Berkovich's
  "surjective families of G-maps".
- `DiscreteGSet.topology G`: the Grothendieck topology generated by
  `precoverage G` (Mathlib's `Precoverage.toGrothendieck`). This is the
  topology of `P(G)`.

Lemmas:

- `DiscreteGSet.mem_precoverage_iff`: `R` covers `S` exactly when every
  `s : S.V` is in the image of some arrow of `R`.
- `DiscreteGSet.ofArrows_mem_precoverage_iff`: the same for a family
  `f i : T i ⟶ S`.
- `DiscreteGSet.generate_mem_topology`: the sieve generated by a covering
  presieve is a covering sieve.

## Choices

- Berkovich speaks of a pretopology. Mathlib's `Precoverage` asks for no
  pullback or composition axioms, and `Precoverage.toGrothendieck` generates a
  Grothendieck topology from any precoverage, so no such axiom is needed here.
  The generated topology is the one Berkovich describes.
- Mathlib's `ContAction` does not apply. It needs a forgetful functor from `V`
  to topological spaces, and `Type z` has none. For a topological group `G` and
  the discrete topology on `S.V`, open stabilizers are the same as continuity
  of the action (Mathlib's
  `continuousSMul_iff_stabilizer_isOpen`); this module does not use that
  lemma.
- `G` and the underlying sets of the `G`-sets are in the same universe `z`, so
  that `G ⧸ N` is an object and `X_Σ` of module `SetInducedSpace` applies.
- The module does not formalize Berkovich's representability claim or the
  equivalences `P(G) ≃ P(G)~` and `P(G) ≃ P(Ĝ)` stated after it.
