# Associated-subset laws for the longest parabolic representative

`Langlands.Group.RelativeWeylIndexing.associatedSubsetLaws_proof` proves the
exact published proposition `associatedSubsetLaws cs` from
`Langlands.Group.RelativeWeylIndexingTargets`.

The parameters are `B : Type u` and `W : Type v` with `[Group W]`,
`[Fintype B]` and `[Fintype W]`, a Coxeter matrix `M : CoxeterMatrix B` and
`cs : CoxeterSystem M W`. The universes are independent. Write
`d = longestRep cs J` for the constructed maximal-length member of
`D(J,∅)`. For every `J : Finset B` the theorem proves:

1. `MapsIntoSimple cs d⁻¹ J Finset.univ`: for each `j ∈ J`,
   `d⁻¹ s_j d` is a simple reflection;
2. `(associatedSubset cs J).card = J.card`, where
   `associatedSubset cs J = simpleImage cs d⁻¹ J`;
3. `standardSubgroup cs (associatedSubset cs J) =
   conjugateStandardSubgroup cs J d⁻¹`, i.e. the subgroup generated by the
   associated simple reflections equals `d⁻¹ W_J d`.

Empty `J`, `J = Finset.univ` and an empty index type `B` are covered by the
same argument and need no separate case.

## Provenance and scope

The original-source provenance of the target is recorded in the unchanged
`RelativeWeylIndexingTargets.md` and `RelativeWeylIndexing.md`: Aubert 1995,
printed pp.2180–2183, with `J' = w_J⁻¹(J)`. The proof implemented here is an
internal Coxeter argument and does not follow a particular printed proof. It
does not use uniqueness of the longest representative: it works for whichever
maximal-length member of `D(J,∅)` the construction `longestRep` chose. No
other relative-Weyl target is a premise. The result concerns Coxeter simple
reflections only. It does not identify them with relative roots, which would
need a separate sign comparison, and it makes no assertion about actual Levi
subgroups.

## Reduced words in a standard subgroup

The private `reduce_word_on` reduces any word with letters in `I` to a reduced
word with letters in `I` and the same product. It is a copy, under a new name,
of the private `reduce_standard_word` of the published
`RelativeWeylMinimalUniqueProof` module, which cannot be imported. Inductively
reduce the tail of `i :: α` to `β`. If `s_i` lengthens `πβ`, then `i :: β`
is reduced. Otherwise `simple_left_exchange` deletes a letter of `β`.
Combined with `exists_standard_word`, this gives the private
`exists_reduced_word_on`: every `x ∈ W_I` is `πα` for a reduced word `α`
on `I`.

## Prefix step and length splitting

`length_prefix_step` assumes

\[
\ell(x^{-1}a)+\ell(x)=\ell(a),\qquad \ell(s_ia)<\ell(a),\qquad
\ell(s_ix)=\ell(x)+1,
\]

and proves `ℓ((s_i x)⁻¹ a) + ℓ(s_i x) = ℓ(a)`. Choose reduced words `α` for
`x` and `η` for `x⁻¹a`. Then `a = π(α ++ η)`, and `s_i` is a left descent of
this product, so `simple_left_exchange_append` deletes one letter.

* If the letter lies in `α`, cancelling `πη` gives `s_i x = π(α.eraseIdx k)`.
  Then `ℓ(s_i x) ≤ |α| - 1 = ℓ(x) - 1`, contradicting the third hypothesis.
* If the letter lies in `η`, then `(s_i x)⁻¹ a = π(η.eraseIdx k)`, so its
  length is at most `ℓ(x⁻¹a) - 1`. Together with `ℓ(s_i x) = ℓ(x) + 1` the
  sum is at most `ℓ(a)`. The reverse inequality is `cs.length_mul_le`
  applied to `a = (s_i x)((s_i x)⁻¹a)`.

`length_inv_mul_add_of_descents` assumes that every `s_i` with `i ∈ I` is a
left descent of `a`, and proves `ℓ(x⁻¹a) + ℓ(x) = ℓ(a)` for every
`x ∈ W_I`. Write `x = πα` with `α` a reduced word on `I` and induct on `α`.
The empty word is trivial. For `i :: α`, the tail `α` is reduced
(`IsReduced.drop`). Reducedness of `i :: α` gives
`ℓ(s_i πα) = ℓ(πα) + 1`, so `length_prefix_step` extends the induction
hypothesis. The induction uses reduced words: for an arbitrary word, a letter
that shortens the prefix would not satisfy the third hypothesis.

## Longest elements conjugate simple reflections to simple reflections

`exists_simple_conj_of_longest` takes `b ∈ W_I` of maximal length in `W_I`
and `i ∈ I`, and produces `k` with `b⁻¹ s_i b = s_k`. Each `s_j b` with
`j ∈ I` lies in `W_I`, so it is no longer than `b`. By the length dichotomy
`cs.length_simple_mul` it is shorter by one. Applying
`length_inv_mul_add_of_descents` to `x = s_i b` gives

\[
\ell(b^{-1}s_ib)=\ell(b)-\ell(s_ib)=1,
\]

and `cs.length_eq_one_iff` identifies `b⁻¹ s_i b` as a simple reflection.

## The longest representative

The private `longestRep_conj_simple` proves that, for `j ∈ J`, `d⁻¹ s_j d` is
a simple reflection. Choose `b ∈ W_J` of maximal length in `W_J` by
`Finset.exists_max_image`. `IsMinimal cs J ∅ d`, taken with right factor `1`,
makes `d` a left `W_J`-coset minimum. Left additivity then gives

\[
\ell(b^{-1}d)=\ell(b)+\ell(d).
\]

The element `w = b⁻¹d` has maximal length in all of `W`. For any `y`, take a
minimum `z` of `W_J y` (`exists_minimal_in_doubleCoset` with right subset
`∅`, whose subgroup is trivial), so `y = a⁻¹z` with `a ∈ W_J`. Left additivity
at `z` gives `ℓ(y) = ℓ(a⁻¹) + ℓ(z)`. Here `ℓ(a⁻¹) ≤ ℓ(b)` by the choice of `b`,
and `ℓ(z) ≤ ℓ(d)` by `length_le_longestRep`, because `z ∈ D(J,∅)`.

Apply `exists_simple_conj_of_longest` twice. With `I = J` and `b`,
`b⁻¹ s_j b = s_m` for some `m`. With `I = Finset.univ` and `w`, using
`standardSubgroup_univ`, `w⁻¹ s_m w = s_k` for some `k`. Since `d = bw`,

\[
d^{-1}s_jd=w^{-1}(b^{-1}s_jb)w=w^{-1}s_mw=s_k .
\]

## The three conjuncts

`associatedSubsetLaws_proof` restates the preceding result as
`d⁻¹ s_j (d⁻¹)⁻¹ = s_k`.

1. `MapsIntoSimple` takes `k ∈ Finset.univ`.
2. The cardinality uses `Finset.card_bij` from `J` to the associated subset,
   sending `j` to a chosen `k` with `d⁻¹ s_j d = s_k`. It lands in the subset
   by the definition of `simpleImage`, unfolded in the private
   `mem_simpleImage`. It is injective because conjugation is injective and
   `simple_injective` recovers the index. It is surjective because, for
   `k` in the subset with witness `j ∈ J`, the chosen index and `k` have the
   same simple reflection, so they are equal by `simple_injective`.
3. For the subgroups, `MonoidHom.map_closure` rewrites
   `conjugateStandardSubgroup cs J d⁻¹` as the closure of the conjugates
   `d⁻¹ s_j d`, `j ∈ J`. That generating set equals the set of `s_k` with
   `k` in the associated subset, by the definition of `simpleImage` and the
   result above.
