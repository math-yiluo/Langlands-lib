# Finite relative-Weyl cancellation and actual subgroup targets

These declarations are individual Prop-valued targets for the finite
combinatorics and subgroup comparisons needed by the general Aubert program.
No target is proved by this module or supplied as a field of a construction.
The intrinsic parameters are exactly those of `RelativeWeylIndexing`;
the final three targets specialize the Coxeter group to the actual quotient
`N_G(T)/Z_G(T)` and display the actual family of subgroups `L`.

## Intrinsic targets

| Declaration | Exact content and source role |
| --- | --- |
| `minimalityLengthCriterion` | Whole-double-coset minimality is equivalent to strict length increase for left simple reflections in `I` and right simple reflections in `J`. This is the intrinsic Coxeter comparison used in Renard V.4.6(i). |
| `minimalRepresentativeUnique` | Two minimal elements in the same actual double coset are equal. Existence has already been constructed; uniqueness remains a target. |
| `standardIntersection` | For `w ∈ D(I,J)`, `W_J ∩ w⁻¹ W_I w` is generated by `intersectionSubset cs I J w`. This is the intrinsic standard-intersection assertion needed before relating the multiplicities to actual Levi intersections. |
| `solomonCancellation` | For every `K ⊆ J`, the sum over every subset `I ⊆ B` of `(-1)^|I| a_{J,I,K}` is `(-1)^|K|`. Here `intersectionMultiplicity` constructs `a` by counting minimal `w` for which `W_J ∩ w⁻¹ W_I w = W_K`. Aubert p.2182 cites Solomon through Carter Proposition 2.7.7; no direct reading or implementation of Carter is claimed. |
| `jacquetCancellation` | For minimal `w ∈ D(J,∅)` and `K ⊆ J`, sum `(-1)^|I|` over `I ⊆ S_w` whose simple image intersects `J` in `K`. The result is `(-1)^|K|` if every simple index in `S_w` conjugates into `J`, and zero otherwise. This is the coefficient calculation in (1.9). |
| `longestRepresentativeUnique` | Every maximal-length member of `D(J,∅)` equals the constructed `longestRep`. |
| `longestSurvivor` | For `w ∈ D(J,∅)`, all indices of `S_w` map to simple indices of `J` precisely when `w = w_J`. This isolates the argument spanning pp.2182–2183. |
| `associatedSubsetLaws` | Inverse conjugation by `w_J` maps every simple reflection in `J` to a simple reflection, the constructed associated subset has cardinality `|J|`, and its subgroup is `w_J⁻¹ W_J w_J`. |

The `solomonCancellation` sum has integer coefficients and includes both
the empty and full subsets. `jacquetCancellation` uses the constructed
length-increasing subset for `S_w`. Its `MapsIntoSimple` condition requires
an image for every index; it cannot discard an image which is not simple.
Its image operation concerns reflections, so the relative-root positivity
comparison is still necessary when passing to Aubert's root formulation.
For `I ⊆ S_w`, that comparison is exactly what recovers the positive
root from its reflection. No assumption of a nonempty simple set is made.

## Actual subgroup targets and retained interface boundary

`actualLeviWeylIdentification T L actual` compares the range of
`RelativeWeyl.inclusion T (L I)` with the subgroup generated by the
actual quotient's simple reflections. This uses the actual quotient map,
whose injectivity is already proved. The family `L` is an explicit
parameter here; these declarations are realization obligations to be
instantiated by the shared standard-parabolic adapter, not assertions
that every arbitrary family of subgroups has the stated properties.
That adapter must separately identify `L ∅ = Z_G(T)`, prove
`Z_G(T) ≤ L I`, and identify `B`, its simple reflections and the
Coxeter system with the actual relative-root data.

`actualLeviIntersection` states, for every lift `n` of a minimal `w`,
the equality of actual subgroups
`L_J ∩ n⁻¹ L_I n = L_(intersectionSubset I J w)`.
It is stronger data than an equality of Weyl subgroups and remains a
separate structural deduction. The universal lift quantifier makes the
required independence visible.

`actualAssociatedLeviConjugation` states, for every lift `n` of `w_J`,
`n L_(J') n⁻¹ = L_J`, with `J'` the constructed inverse-image subset.
The actual multiplicative equivalence then uses
`RelativeWeyl.conjugationEquiv`; continuity, the pullback of smooth
representations along its inverse, and the induced exact K0 map are
separate actual-functor obligations of the shared adapter owners.
None of these targets states Weyl-conjugacy of induced K0 classes.
That is a distinct representation-theoretic target in the companion
Corollary 1.10/conjugacy work.

The parent owns the later proof tasks and their assembly. These targets
do not discharge representation-theoretic, classification or geometric-lemma
premises, nor do they enlarge the permitted external structural boundary.
