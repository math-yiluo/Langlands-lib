# Principal congruence with an integral block Levi

The subgroup generated by the integral block Levi and the first
principal level is the product of those two subgroups. The integral
group normalizes every principal level because, in integral
coordinates, that level is the kernel of reduction. Over a
nonarchimedean local field the subgroup is compact and open.

`LeviCongruenceIntersectionsStatement` identifies the three intersections
with the Levi and the two radicals. The Levi intersection is the
integral Levi. Each radical intersection is the intersection with the
first principal level. In the proof, factor the principal-level term
by the positive-level block factorization and conjugate the lower
factor by the integral Levi term. The resulting ordered factorization
is already unique in the full integral group. For a point in either
radical its factor in that radical must therefore lie in the principal
level.

This is a construction from the existing principal block factorization
for use with the strictly contracting elementary tests of
Henniart–Lemaire, I.4.1 and I.5.3. Singleton blocks give the upper and
lower unipotent groups and the integral diagonal torus.
