# One-step contraction of principal congruence radicals

`StrictPrincipalContractionStatement` treats a block scalar whose
positive root ratios have valuation at most that of a uniformizer.
Its conjugation sends the upper radical at any principal level into
the next level. Its inverse conjugation has the same effect on the
opposite radical.

The proof applies the entry criterion to the matrix and its inverse.
The diagonal blocks are identities, the zero off-diagonal blocks
stay zero, and each remaining block is multiplied by a ratio of
valuation at most that of the uniformizer. This is the strict
principal-level version of the block contraction calculation, used
to compare compact double cosets with the regular elementary supports
of Henniart–Lemaire, I.4.1.
