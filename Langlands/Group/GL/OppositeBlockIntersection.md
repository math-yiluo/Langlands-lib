# Opposite parabolics and upper block radicals

These are the published elementary block-subgroup lemmas extracted unchanged
from `Langlands.Representation.RankinSelberg.RankOneExtensionCompactSectionProof`.
Their namespace remains `Langlands.GeneralLinear.BlockShape`.

For every ordered block shape and commutative coefficient ring,
`eq_one_of_mem_oppositeParabolic_of_mem_unipotentSubgroup` proves that the lower
parabolic meets the upper radical only in the identity, by comparing block
matrix entries. `oppositeUnipotentSubgroup_le_oppositeParabolic` and
`leviSubgroup_le_oppositeParabolic` give the two subgroup inclusions used in
Iwahori-factorized compact section constructions.

This shared module supplies both the rank-one and two-block constructions.
No hypotheses, statements, or proof bodies were changed during relocation.
