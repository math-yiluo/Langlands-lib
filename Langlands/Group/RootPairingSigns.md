# Sign changes of simple reflections

This internal module derives reusable sign-change lemmas from the coefficient
argument implemented privately in `RootPairingCoxeterProof`. It works with a
finite crystallographic root pairing over a linearly ordered field and a linear
functional nonzero on every root. No reducedness or spanning premise is added.

Positive roots lie in the additive monoid generated by the functional base.
Linear independence of that base implies that, if two elements of this monoid
sum to a scalar multiple of a simple root, each summand is a natural multiple
of that root. Applying this to a positive root and the negative of its reflected
image shows that a positive root made negative by a simple reflection is a
natural multiple of its simple root.

`Indivisible` means that a root is not a natural multiple, with multiplier at
least two, of any root. Simple roots are indivisible by the same coefficient
argument, and Weyl transport preserves indivisibility by applying the inverse
action to a proposed factorization. Consequently a positive Weyl image of a
simple root made negative by another simple reflection equals that second
simple root. Conjugate simple reflections act by negation on the transported
root, giving the positive-conjugacy label conclusion.

All assertions are derived from published root-pairing, monoid-generation and
linear-independence results; no additional literature assertion is introduced.
