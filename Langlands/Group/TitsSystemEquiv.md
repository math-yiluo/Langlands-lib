# Tits systems under multiplicative equivalences

This internal derived unit transports a supplied Tits-system datum along a
multiplicative equivalence of ambient groups. The full image of the source
normalizer is required to equal the target normalizer. `equivNormalizer`
restricts the ambient equivalence using that equality, and `equivProjection`
precomposes the source Weyl projection with its inverse. The Weyl group and
the set of generators are unchanged, in independent universes.

The existing epimorphic-image theorem supplies every Tits axiom, since an
equivalence has trivial kernel. Its saturation theorem also supplies the
intersection-of-conjugates identity on the full image subgroups.

Bruhat cells, standard parabolic subsets for every type, and their conjugates
have exactly the images described by the equivalence. The conjugating element
maps through the ambient equivalence and the type remains unchanged. This
proves preservation and reflection of admissible parabolic subgroups. The
same formulas identify each restricted apartment and then the entire set of
restricted apartments, as sets of subgroup-valued facets. The reverse
inclusion pulls arbitrary target subgroups back through the equivalence.

The provenance is the unchanged definitions of Tits systems, admissible types,
parabolic subsets, saturation and restricted apartments, together with the
existing epimorphic-image proof. No new source translation, root datum,
building existence or axiom witness is asserted.
