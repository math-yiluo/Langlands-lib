# Commutator bounds for generated subgroups

This internal group-theoretic unit passes commutator bounds to generated
subgroups while keeping the required normalization assumptions explicit. It
uses the actual subgroup supremum, which is the subgroup generated by the
family.

For any group `G`, `commutator_iSup_left_le_of_normalizes` says that
`[sup A_j, B]` lies in `K` if every `A_j` normalizes `K` and every `[A_j,B]`
lies in `K`. The fixed subgroup `B` need not normalize `K`.
`commutator_iSup_le_of_normalizes` applies this result twice: if both
generating families normalize `K` and all pairwise commutators lie in `K`,
their generated subgroups have commutator in `K`.

`le_normalizer_iSup_of_commutator_le` supplies the normalization premise in a
useful form. A subgroup `A` normalizes `sup B_j` if `[A,B_j]` is contained in
that supremum for each `j`.

`normalizingCentralizerModulo B K` is the subgroup of elements that normalize
`K` and commute modulo `K` with every element of `B`. The usual product and inverse
commutator identities prove closure; their conjugation terms are controlled
by normalization. The universal property of the subgroup supremum then
proves the first theorem. The final theorem also uses that each generating
subgroup lies in the generated subgroup, which normalizes itself.

These are derived results from Mathlib's subgroup and commutator identities,
with no additional source assertion. They do not assert that commutators
distribute over arbitrary subgroup suprema without normalization. They are
intended for the actual root-filtration commutator construction and assert no
building comparison.
