# Topological subgroup conjugation and compact open inclusions

This internal construction restricts the inner automorphism of a topological group to a continuous multiplicative equivalence from any subgroup onto its actual conjugate. Both subgroup topologies are induced from the ambient group. The previously proved transport theorem therefore preserves the pro-p predicate under conjugation.

The measure-theoretic helpers assume a locally compact group equipped with its Borel measurable structure. For a group with trivial modular character, left translation preserves Haar measure by definition, and right translation preserves it by the modular character formula. The regularity of the image of Haar measure under the right-translation homeomorphism supplies the regular-measure uniqueness formula, without assuming inner regularity on all measurable sets. Composing the translations proves that conjugate open subgroups have equal Haar measure.

For nested open subgroups of equal Haar measure, compactness of the smaller subgroup makes its measure finite. Their difference is open because the smaller subgroup is closed, and has zero measure by subtraction. Positivity of Haar measure on nonempty open sets makes this difference empty. Thus the two subgroups are equal. Combining the two results, a compact open subgroup contained in a conjugate equals that conjugate when the modular character is trivial.

These are derived topological-group constructions and arguments used to prove corrected KP Proposition 13.5.2; they make no building-theoretic assumptions.
