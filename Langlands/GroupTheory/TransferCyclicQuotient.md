# The transfer to the kernel of a degree map modulo `f`

Author: Worker 5137.

This helper module of the proof
`Langlands.ClassField.Global.RelativeWeilGroup.presentedDeterminantMultipleAssociation`
computes a group-theoretic transfer in the situation of an unramified place. It is
a standard fact about the transfer (see for example Serre, *Local Fields*, VII §8, or
any treatment of the transfer through cycle decompositions); the Lean proof is
self-contained over Mathlib.

## Statement

`Langlands.GroupTheory.Transfer.transfer_eq_of_degree`: let `G` be a group, `A` a
commutative group, `d : G →* Multiplicative ℤ` a homomorphism (a "degree"), `f` a natural
number and `H` a subgroup of finite index such that `g ∈ H` exactly when `f` divides the
degree of `g` (hypothesis `hH`). If `y` has degree one (`hy`), then for every
`ϕ : H →* A` the transfer `MonoidHom.transfer ϕ y` equals `ϕ (y ^ f)`; the element
`y ^ f` lies in `H` because its degree is `f`.

There is no hypothesis `f > 0`: if `f = 0` then `H` is the kernel of `d`, whose cosets
`y ^ k H` are pairwise distinct, so the finite-index assumption already rules this case
out whenever an element of degree one exists.

## Proof

- `hcoset`: `y ^ k • aH = bH` exactly when `f` divides `deg b - deg a - k`, by
  `QuotientGroup.eq` and `hH`.
- `hsub`: the action of the cyclic group generated by `y` on `G ⧸ H` has a single
  orbit: `aH` is reached from `bH` by `y ^ (deg a - deg b)`.
- `hperiod`: every coset has minimal period exactly `f` under `y`, because
  `y ^ n • aH = aH` exactly when `f` divides `n`.
- `key`: for any coset representative `r`, the element `r⁻¹ y ^ f r` of `H` is the
  conjugate of `y ^ f` by `y ^ (-deg r) r ∈ H`, since `y ^ f` commutes with powers of `y`.
  As `A` is commutative, `ϕ (r⁻¹ y ^ f r) = ϕ (y ^ f)`.
- Mathlib's cycle formula `MonoidHom.transfer_eq_prod_quotient_orbitRel_zpowers_quot`
  writes the transfer as a product over the orbits of `y`; there is one orbit
  (`Fintype.prod_subsingleton`), of length `f`, and its factor is `ϕ (y ^ f)` by `key`.
