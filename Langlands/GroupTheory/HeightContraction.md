# Fixed cosets of an endomorphism of a height-filtered ordered product

Module `Langlands.GroupTheory.HeightContraction`. This is an internal
group-theoretic lemma. It is the contraction step in the uniqueness argument of
`Langlands.Geometry.Building.GenericSpecialFixedPoint`: a regular torus element
fixes no nontrivial coset of the depth-zero positive root groups.

The group `G` is an arbitrary group in universe `v`. The family is
`H : Fin n → Subgroup G` with heights `w : Fin n → ℕ`.

## Declarations

* `prod_ofFn_eq_mul_update`: in a monoid, suppose `f r = 1` for every `r < t`.
  Then `(List.ofFn f).prod = f t * (List.ofFn (Function.update f t 1)).prod`.
* `tailFactor H t r` is `H r` when `t ≤ r` and `⊥` otherwise. `tail H t` is
  `⨆ r, tailFactor H t r`, the subgroup generated by the factors with index at
  least `t`. The basic facts are `tailFactor_of_le`, `tailFactor_of_lt`,
  `tailFactor_le`, `le_tail`, `tail_zero` (`tail H 0 = ⨆ r, H r`) and
  `tail_eq_bot` (`tail H t = ⊥` for `n ≤ t`).

The next results assume that `w` is monotone and that the commutator bound
`hcH` of the published `HeightCollection` holds:
`⁅H i, H j⁆ ≤ ⨆ k, ⨆ (_ : max (w i) (w j) < w k), H k` for all `i, j`.

* `lt_of_height_lt`: `w r < w k` implies `r < k`.
* `commutator_tailFactor_le`: the tail factors satisfy the same commutator
  bound.
* `exists_coords`: every `u ∈ tail H t` is a natural-order product
  `(List.ofFn c).prod` with `c r ∈ H r` for all `r` and `c r = 1` for `r < t`.
* `conj_mem_tail_succ`: for `g ∈ H τ`, conjugation by `g` preserves
  `tail H (τ + 1)`.
* `map_mem_tail`: a monoid endomorphism that maps every `H i` into itself also
  maps every tail into itself. This one needs neither the monotone heights nor
  the commutator bound.
* `mem_iSup_of_inv_mul_map_mem`, the main theorem. Assume, besides the above:
  * `X i ≤ H i` are subgroups satisfying the same commutator bound;
  * natural-order products are injective on coordinates, that is,
    `(List.ofFn f).prod = (List.ofFn g).prod` with `f i, g i ∈ H i` for all `i`
    forces `f = g`;
  * `s : G →* G` maps every `H i` into `H i` and every `X i` into `X i`;
  * the rank-one condition: for `h ∈ H i`, `h⁻¹ * s h ∈ X i` implies `h ∈ X i`.

  Then, for every `u ∈ ⨆ i, H i`, `u⁻¹ * s u ∈ ⨆ i, X i` implies
  `u ∈ ⨆ i, X i`.

## Implemented proof

The main theorem is proved by downward induction on `t`. The statement at
`t` says that every `u ∈ tail H t` with `u⁻¹ * s u ∈ ⨆ X` lies in `⨆ X`. At
`t = n` the tail is trivial. For the step from `t + 1` to `t`, write
`τ = t` as an element of `Fin n`.

1. `exists_coords` gives natural-order coordinates of `u`, trivial before
   `τ`. `prod_ofFn_eq_mul_update` splits off the first factor:
   `u = a * u'` with `a ∈ H τ` and `u' ∈ tail H (τ + 1)`.
2. Put `φ(a) = a⁻¹ * s a ∈ H τ`. A direct computation gives
   `u⁻¹ * s u = φ(a) * m` with
   `m = φ(a)⁻¹ * u'⁻¹ * φ(a) * s u'`. By `conj_mem_tail_succ` and
   `map_mem_tail`, `m` lies in `tail H (τ + 1)`.
3. The published `HeightCollection.range_orderedProduct_eq_iSup` gives
   natural-order coordinates `x` of `u⁻¹ * s u` in the family `X`. On the
   other side, `exists_coords` for `m` and `prod_ofFn_eq_mul_update` express
   `φ(a) * m` as a natural-order product whose coordinate at `τ` is `φ(a)`.
   Injectivity identifies the two coordinate families, so `φ(a) = x τ ∈ X τ`.
   The rank-one condition then gives `a ∈ X τ`.
4. Put `y = a * u' * a⁻¹ ∈ tail H (τ + 1)`. Then `u = y * a` and
   `y⁻¹ * s y = a * (u⁻¹ * s u) * (s a)⁻¹`, which lies in `⨆ X` because
   `a, s a ∈ X τ`. The induction hypothesis gives `y ∈ ⨆ X`, hence
   `u = y * a ∈ ⨆ X`.

Only injectivity of the natural-order product is used, because the heights
are monotone: the factors at indices above `τ` form a subgroup normalized by
`H τ`.

This is internal content: a standard filtration argument written for an
