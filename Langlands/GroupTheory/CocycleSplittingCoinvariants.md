# Splitting-module coinvariants and extension abelianization

This internal development compares the concrete cocycle splitting representation
with the abelianization of the actual cocycle extension. A vector `(a,f)`
maps to the abelianized coefficient inclusion of `a`, plus the sum of the
coordinates `f x` times the abelianized section value at `x⁻¹`.

Conjugation does not change a value in the abelianization. Together with the
section multiplication formula for the cocycle and the zero coordinate sum of
`f`, this proves invariance of the displayed map. It therefore descends to
the actual representation coinvariants.
