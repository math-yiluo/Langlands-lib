# Compatible p-adic data on a supplied relative local field

For a nonarchimedean local field, powers of an element converge to zero
exactly when its valuation is below one. A continuous field homomorphism
therefore sends elements of valuation below one to elements of valuation
below one. Applied to inverses, this reflects integer membership. Forward
integer membership follows from the compactness of the base integer ring.
Applying the resulting equivalence to quotients gives compatibility of
the valuative relations.

A finite extension over a supplied p-adic field inherits a p-adic
structure through the scalar tower. Its field map from the p-adic base
is continuous by composition. This gives continuous scalar multiplication
for the original topology, which is the module topology by finite
dimensionality over the complete p-adic field. The construction keeps
the original upper topology, valuation relation and local-field witness.
