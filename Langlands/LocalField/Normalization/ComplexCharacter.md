# The normalized absolute value as a complex quasicharacter

This internal construction restricts the published normalized absolute value
to the multiplicative group of the actual field and embeds its positive real
values into the complex units. Strict positivity supplies the nonzero
certificate. The published multiplicative and unit identities prove the
homomorphism laws. The continuity theorem for the normalized absolute value,
together with the embedding of complex units into the complex field, proves
continuity for the supplied field topology.

The construction uses the residue-normalized absolute value throughout and
introduces no selected reciprocity map or functional-equation assertion.
