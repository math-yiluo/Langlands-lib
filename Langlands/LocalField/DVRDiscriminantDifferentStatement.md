# The discriminant of an extension of DVRs is the norm of its different

This module states `Langlands.LocalField.DVRDiscriminantDifferent`, a proposition definition. Its
proof is `Langlands.LocalField.dvrDiscriminantDifferent` (`DVRDiscriminantDifferentProof`).

## Statement

```lean
def DVRDiscriminantDifferent : Prop :=
  ∀ (A : Type uA) (B : Type uB) (K : Type uK) (L : Type uL)
    [CommRing A] [IsDomain A] [IsDiscreteValuationRing A]
    [CommRing B] [IsDomain B] [IsDiscreteValuationRing B]
    [Field K] [Field L]
    [Algebra A B] [IsLocalHom (algebraMap A B)]
    [Algebra A K] [IsFractionRing A K]
    [Algebra B L] [IsFractionRing B L]
    [Algebra K L] [Algebra A L]
    [IsScalarTower A K L] [IsScalarTower A B L]
    [FiniteDimensional K L] [Algebra.IsSeparable K L]
    [IsIntegralClosure B A L]
    [Module.Finite A B]
    [Algebra.IsSeparable (IsLocalRing.ResidueField A) (IsLocalRing.ResidueField B)],
    haveI := isTorsionFree_of_isFractionRing_tower A B K L
    haveI : Module.Free A B := Module.free_of_finite_type_torsion_free'
    ∀ {ι : Type uι} [Fintype ι] [DecidableEq ι] (b : Module.Basis ι A B),
      Ideal.span {Algebra.discr A b} = Ideal.spanNorm A (differentIdeal A B) ∧
      ∀ d : ℕ, differentIdeal A B = IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal B ^ d →
        Ideal.span {Algebra.discr A b} =
          IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal A ^ ((IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal B).inertiaDeg A * d)
```

(name space `Langlands.LocalField`; level parameters `uA uB uK uL uι`).

In words: let `A → B` be a finite local homomorphism of discrete valuation rings, with fraction fields
`K → L`, `L/K` finite and separable, `B` the integral closure of `A` in `L`, and the residue
extension separable. Then `B` is a free `A`-module (torsion-free, `isTorsionFree_of_isFractionRing_tower`
of `DVRTraceIdealsStatement`, and finite over the principal ideal domain `A`), and for every basis `b`:

1. the discriminant ideal `(disc_A(b))` is the norm `N_{B/A}(𝔇_{B/A})` of the different
   (`Ideal.spanNorm`, the ideal generated by the norms of the elements of the different);
2. if `𝔇_{B/A} = m_B^d`, then `(disc_A(b)) = m_A^{f d}`, `f` the inertia degree of `m_B`; that is,
   the discriminant has valuation `f · d`.

The discriminant is Mathlib's `Algebra.discr A b = det(Tr(b_i b_j))`, the different Mathlib's
`differentIdeal A B`.

## Literature

Serre, *Local Fields*, GTM 67, Springer, 1979, Chapter III (PDF page = printed page + 7; checked on
PDF pages 57, 63, 64):

- §3, the definitions of the discriminant `𝔡_{B/A}` (the ideal generated by `det(Tr(e_i e_j))` for a
  basis) and of the different, and Proposition 6, `𝔡_{B/A} = N_{L/K}(𝔇_{B/A})`, printed p. 50 —
  clause 1. Serre states it for any Dedekind `A` with `B` the integral closure; the Library states
  it for DVRs with separable residue extension (the setting of its proof).
- §6, Proposition 12 (`B = A[x]` for DVRs with separable residue extension), printed p. 57.

Clause 2 is clause 1 with the norm of the maximal ideal, `N(m_B) = m_A^f`.
