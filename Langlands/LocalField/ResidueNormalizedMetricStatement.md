# The topology of the residue-normalized metric

`Langlands.LocalField.ResidueNormalizedMetricStatement` asserts that the
distance \(d(x,y)=|x-y|_F\), using the residue-normalized absolute value,
is an ultrametric whose topology is the given topology of the
nonarchimedean local field \(F\). It applies in either characteristic.

This formalizes the metric construction in David Renard,
*Représentations des groupes réductifs \(p\)-adiques*, 9 July 2026,
§ V.1, printed p. 104 (PDF p. 110), using the nonarchimedean inequality
for the normalized absolute value.
