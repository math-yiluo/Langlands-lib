# The X-adic Laurent-series local field

Worker 27 constructs the actual Laurent-series fixture for arbitrary `k : Type u` with `[Field k] [Finite k]`. This is a derived construction from pinned Mathlib and the published general local-field API, not a numbered theorem from KP or a classification of local fields.

## Topology and local-field structure

`Langlands.LocalField.Laurent.valuativeRel k` is explicitly `ValuativeRel.ofValuation` of Mathlib's existing `Valued.v : Valuation (LaurentSeries k) ℤᵐ⁰`. It is deliberately installed only locally in this module. A consumer installs `letI := Langlands.LocalField.Laurent.valuativeRel k`; the `localField` instance then supplies `IsNonarchimedeanLocalField (LaurentSeries k)`. The `vle_iff` theorem identifies the relation with comparison of the existing X-adic valuation. `valuationCompatible` and `isValuativeTopology` connect it to the topology already induced by Mathlib's Laurent-series valued uniform structure. No uniform structure or topology on the carrier is replaced.

`valuationRankOne` composes the value-group embedding with the strictly increasing exponential into nonnegative reals of base two. This auxiliary base is used only to apply Mathlib's properness criterion; it is not a new residue normalization. The Laurent monomial `HahnSeries.single 1 1` has valuation `WithZero.exp (-1)`, which is neither zero nor one. This supplies valuation nontriviality and hence `nontrivialValuativeRel`.

`powerSeriesEquivValuedIntegers` and `valuedResidueEquiv`, described below, transfer Mathlib's power-series DVR structure and identify the actual residue field with `k`. Finiteness of that residue field is derived from `[Finite k]`. Inside the scoped `Valued` normed-field construction, `locallyCompactSpace` applies Mathlib's criterion `Valued.integer.properSpace_iff_completeSpace_and_isDiscreteValuationRing_integer_and_finite_residueField` to Mathlib's existing Laurent-series completeness, this actual DVR, and this actual finite residue field. The scope's normed structure retains the original uniformity. Properness implies local compactness; `localField` combines it with the established topology compatibility and nontriviality. Neither compactness nor a local-field instance is an additional hypothesis.

## Actual integers, coefficients, and residue

Mathlib's `LaurentSeries.powerSeriesEquivSubring` identifies power series with their image in Laurent series. `LaurentSeries.val_le_one_iff_eq_coe` identifies that image with the integer ring of the existing X-adic valuation. Their composition is `powerSeriesEquivValuedIntegers`; its coercion theorem says the underlying Laurent series is exactly the usual inclusion of the given power series.

`valuedIntegers_eq_ringOfIntegers` uses equivalence of compatible valuations to identify this subring with `Langlands.FML.ringOfIntegers (LaurentSeries k)`, the actual canonical valuation ring used throughout `ResidueNormalization`. `valuedIntegersEquivIntegers` preserves the underlying field element, and `powerSeriesEquivIntegers` composes the two comparisons. These constructions work for every coefficient field, without the finiteness hypothesis.

`residueEquiv` is the actual ring equivalence from the residue field of this canonical valuation ring to `k`: it maps residue through the inverse power-series ring equivalence and then applies Mathlib's `PowerSeries.residueFieldOfPowerSeries`. The `residueEquiv_residue` theorem proves that reducing the image of any power series maps to its constant coefficient. The proof first establishes this functorial calculation with an abstract ring equivalence, then specializes it; this avoids excessive kernel reduction of the concrete equivalences. `coefficientInclusion` includes constant power series into the canonical integer ring, its coercion theorem identifies the field inclusion, and `residueEquiv_coefficientInclusion` proves reduction followed by `residueEquiv` is the identity on `k`. Thus the residue comparison uses the actual coefficient field, not another finite field of the same size. `residueCardinality_eq` follows from this ring equivalence by `Nat.card_congr`.

## Reuse and limits

The pinned sources are `Mathlib.RingTheory.LaurentSeries`, `Mathlib.RingTheory.PowerSeries.Inverse`, `Mathlib.RingTheory.LocalRing.ResidueField.Basic`, `Mathlib.Topology.Algebra.Valued.LocallyCompact`, and `Mathlib.NumberTheory.LocalField.Basic`. The module imports Worker 607's published `Langlands.LocalField.ResidueNormalization` and its existing FML valuation dependency by Lean module name; it does not copy their sources. The X-adic multiplicative sign is `v(X^n) = exp(-n)` for natural exponents and `v(single n 1) = exp(-n)` for all integer exponents. Comparison with the existing FML additive order, residue-normalized absolute-value evaluations, and the common p-adic consumer are subsequent adapter units, not asserted here. This fixture constructs no building, parahoric model, or general classification.
