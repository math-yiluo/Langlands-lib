# The maximal-ideal neighborhood basis of a local field

`Langlands.LocalField.maximalIdealNeighborhoodBasis` proves
`Langlands.LocalField.MaximalIdealNeighborhoodBasisStatement` for the given
topology on a nonarchimedean local field \(F\). This is the additive
neighborhood assertion in David Renard, *Représentations des groupes
réductifs \(p\)-adiques*, French edition dated 9 July 2026, § V.1, printed
p. 104 (PDF p. 110). The source is registered as
`renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, SHA-256
`7b539996793b7c3620e72e1e389588eb42d1a8e91d6f68b2eead5c2e2ffc182c`.

Choose a uniformizer \(\pi\) as an irreducible element of the valuation ring
\(\mathcal O_F\). The maximal ideal \(\mathfrak m\) is generated by \(\pi\),
so the image of \(\mathfrak m^n\) in \(F\) is \(\pi^n\mathcal O_F\) for
every \(n\in\mathbb N\). The valuation ring is the compact closed valuation
ball of radius one and is open. Multiplication by \(\pi^n\) is a
homeomorphism of \(F\), giving compactness and openness of each image.

Pull a neighborhood of zero back along the continuous inclusion
\(\mathcal O_F\hookrightarrow F\).
`LocalFieldTheory.exists_maximalIdeal_pow_subset_nhds_zero` gives an ideal
power contained in that preimage, so its image lies in the original
neighborhood. Conversely, every set containing one of the images is a
neighborhood of zero because the image is open and contains zero. Together
these implications give the filter basis.
