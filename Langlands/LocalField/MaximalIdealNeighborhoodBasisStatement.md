# The maximal-ideal neighborhood basis of a local field

`Langlands.LocalField.MaximalIdealNeighborhoodBasisStatement` states that the
images of the powers of the maximal ideal of the valuation ring in \(F\) are
compact, open and form a neighborhood basis of zero. Each image is an
additive subgroup of \(F\). The zeroth power gives the valuation ring. The
statement uses the given topology and applies to nonarchimedean local fields
in either characteristic.

This is the additive neighborhood assertion in David Renard,
*Représentations des groupes réductifs \(p\)-adiques*, 9 July 2026,
§ V.1, printed p. 104 (PDF p. 110).
