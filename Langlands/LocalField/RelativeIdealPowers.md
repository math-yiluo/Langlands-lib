# Relative maximal ideal powers

Authors: Worker 521 implemented the module and proofs. Worker 512 suggested the
host interface and the proof route by mapping the absolute prime ideal identities
and cancelling the positive base ramification index.

This internal derived support lemma uses the published relative integer map and
the published absolute prime ideal power identity. It applies to arbitrary
supplied topological data `T` and `S` on independent p-adic field universes,
with the supplied field algebra and its compatible scalar tower.

`map_maximalIdeal_of_ramificationIndex_eq_mul` proves that if
`S.ramificationIndex = e * T.ramificationIndex`, the ideal generated by the
image of the actual source maximal ideal is exactly the `e`th power of the
actual target maximal ideal. It assumes neither relative Galoisness nor a prime
relative degree.

The implemented proof first checks that the relative integer map is injective
by its underlying field embedding. A uniformizer of the source therefore has
nonzero image. Mathlib's DVR ideal classification writes the extended maximal
ideal as the `k`th power of the target maximal ideal. Mapping the published
identity `span {p} = maximalIdeal ^ ramificationIndex` between the actual integer
rings identifies its `T.ramificationIndex`th power with
`maximalIdeal S.Integers ^ S.ramificationIndex`. Mathlib's coheight formula for
DVR ideal powers identifies the exponents. Cancellation of the positive
absolute ramification index gives `k = e`.

`map_maximalIdeal_pow_of_ramificationIndex_eq_mul` extends this equality to
every nonnegative source ideal power, multiplying its exponent by `e`.
It uses `Ideal.map_pow` and the natural power multiplication law, so it includes
the zeroth power. `map_maximalIdeal_algebraMap_of_ramificationIndex_eq_mul`
states the maximal ideal equality after locally selecting exactly
`relativeIntegerAlgebra T S`. Its algebra map is definitionally the published
relative integer map.

For the prime trace-ideal task, specialize `T` and `S` to the canonical data of
the base and extension fields, and specialize `e` to their supplied relative
field degree. The target's total-ramification hypothesis is then exactly the
index equality required here.

This is a derived algebraic argument from `Langlands.LocalField.RelativeIntegers`,
`Langlands.LocalField.Normalization.Proof`, and Mathlib's
`IsDiscreteValuationRing` results. It is intended as a small bridge for the prime
ramified trace-ideal proof; it contains no trace, different, or norm conclusion.
The small helper is grouped with that host proof for submission.
