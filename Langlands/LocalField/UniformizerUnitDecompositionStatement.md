# Decomposition of a field unit by a uniformizer

`Langlands.LocalField.UniformizerUnitDecompositionStatement` fixes any
uniformizer \(\varpi\) of additive order one in a nonarchimedean local
field \(F\). The map \((u,n)\mapsto u\varpi^n\) is a bijection from the
embedded valuation-ring units \(\mathfrak O_F^\times\) times \(\mathbb Z\)
to \(F^\times\). The formulation uses the normalized discrete additive
order and applies in either characteristic.

This is the unique factorization of a field unit in David Renard,
*Représentations des groupes réductifs \(p\)-adiques*, 9 July 2026,
§ V.1, printed p. 103 (PDF p. 109).
