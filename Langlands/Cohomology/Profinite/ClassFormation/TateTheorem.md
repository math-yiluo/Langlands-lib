# Tate's theorem for class formations

`Langlands/Cohomology/Profinite/ClassFormation/TateTheorem.lean`, namespace
`Langlands.ProfiniteCohomology.GroupCoh` (finite groups, Mathlib's `groupCohomology`).

Tate's theorem: let `Γ` be a finite group, `M` a `Γ`-module and `u ∈ H²(Γ, M)` such that for
every subgroup `S`, `H¹(S, M) = 0` and `H²(S, M)` is cyclic of order `|S|` generated by the
restriction of `u`. Then cup product with `u` is an isomorphism `Ĥⁿ(Γ, ℤ) ≅ Ĥⁿ⁺²(Γ, M)` of Tate
cohomology groups for all `n`. The Library proves this (`Langlands.Tate.tateCupIsomorphism`, in
`Langlands/Cohomology/Tate/TateTheoremProof.lean`, for Sylow subgroups, over its
`IntegralScalar = ULift ℤ`). This file reads it in positive degrees in ordinary cohomology, in
the form the class formation argument uses: every class of `H^{n+2}(Γ, M)` (`n ≥ 1`) is `u ∪ y`
for a class `y ∈ Hⁿ(Γ, ℤ)` killed by a nonzero integer.

## Declarations

- `ordinaryToTate_eqToHom`: the Library's comparison `ordinaryToTate` (ordinary to Tate
  cohomology) commutes with the identifications of equal degrees.
- `ordinaryToTate_injective_of_neZero`: in positive degree the comparison is injective (it is an
  isomorphism, `TateCohomology.isoGroupCohomology`).
- `map_eqToHom_groupCohomology`: coefficient maps commute with the identifications of equal
  degrees.
- `ordinaryCup_scalarPairing`: the Library's ordinary cup product for the scalar pairing
  `M ⊗ ℤ → M` is the image under the right unitor of the cup product for the tensor pairing
  (from `Langlands.Tate.ordinaryCupLaws`).
- `exists_cupH_of_tate M u h1 h2 h3 n x`: under Tate's hypotheses on all subgroups (`h1`:
  `H¹(S, M) = 0`; `h2`: the restriction of `u` to `S` has order `|S|`; `h3`: it generates
  `H²(S, M)`), for `n ≥ 1` every `x ∈ H^{n+2}(Γ, M)` is
  `(ρ_M)_*(cupH u y)` for some `y ∈ Hⁿ(Γ, ℤ)` with `m • y = 0` for some `m ≠ 0`. Here `cupH` is
  the cup product of finite group cohomology of `Cup/Inflation.lean` (the Library's
  `ordinaryCup` for the tensor pairing).

The proof applies `tateCupIsomorphism` (surjectivity in degree `n`) to the image of `x` in Tate
cohomology, lifts the preimage to ordinary cohomology (`ordinaryToTate_surjective`), and compares
cup products with `ordinaryTateCupLaws`; the torsion of `y` is `card_smul_groupCohomology`.

## References

- J. Tate, *The higher dimensional cohomology groups of class field theory*, Ann. of Math. (2) 56
  (1952), 294–297, Theorem 2, p. 296 (proof pp. 296–297), under Axiom 1 of Theorem 1, p. 294: for
  `r > 2` the map `ζ ↦ α ∪ ζ` is an isomorphism of `H^{r-2}(G, ℤ)` onto `H^r(G, A)`. This is the
  ordinary cohomology in positive degrees used here; Tate cohomology in all degrees is only
  announced there (p. 297).
- J.-P. Serre, *Local Fields* (Springer GTM 67, 1979; translation of *Corps locaux*), chapter IX,
  §8, Theorem 14 and its Corollary, p. 149 (Tate's theorem; Theorem 13, p. 148, is the general
  theorem of Tate and Nakayama). Serre assumes the hypotheses only for the Sylow subgroups.
- J. Neukirch, A. Schmidt, K. Wingberg, *Cohomology of Number Fields*, 2nd edition (Springer 2008;
  corrected printing 2013), chapter III, §1, Theorem (3.1.4), p. 150 (with Definition (3.1.3) of
