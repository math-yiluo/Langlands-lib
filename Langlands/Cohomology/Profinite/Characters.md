# Characters, `ℚ/ℤ`, `H¹(G, ℚ/ℤ)` and `H²(G, ℤ)`

`Langlands/Cohomology/Profinite/Characters.lean`, namespace `Langlands.ProfiniteCohomology`.

The characters of a profinite group `G` are the continuous homomorphisms to `ℚ/ℤ` with the
discrete topology, i.e. the homomorphisms with open kernel. For a trivial module `M`,
`H¹(G, M)` is the group of continuous homomorphisms `G → M`; and `H²(G, ℤ) ≅ H¹(G, ℚ/ℤ)` through the
connecting map of `0 → ℤ → ℚ → ℚ/ℤ → 0`, because `Hⁿ(G, ℚ) = 0` for `n ≥ 1`.

## Declarations

- `QZ`: `ℚ/ℤ` with the discrete topology. As a type it is the Library's
  `Langlands.ClassFieldTheory.Local.RationalModIntegers` (and Mathlib's `AddCircle (1 : ℚ)`):
  `QZ.toRationalModIntegers`, `QZ.toAddCircle` are identities. `QZ.mk q` is the class of `q : ℚ`, with
  `QZ.mk_surjective`, `QZ.mk_eq_zero_iff`, `QZ.mk_nsmul`, `QZ.mk_zsmul`, `QZ.nsmul_mk_one_div`
  (`n • (1/n) = 0`), `QZ.addOrderOf_mk_one_div`, `QZ.exists_eq_nsmul_mk_one_div` (the `n`-torsion is
  generated by `1/n`).
  `QZ.mk_half_ne_zero`, `QZ.mk_half_add_mk_half` (`1/2 ≠ 0` and `1/2 + 1/2 = 0`; from the source's
  `LocalClassFormation/CupSymbolReal.lean`).
- `QZDual A`: the continuous homomorphisms `A → QZ`, with `QZDual.map`, `map_apply`, `map_id`,
  `map_comp`, `QZDual.toAddMonoidHomEquiv` (for discrete `A` every homomorphism is continuous) and
  `QZDual.eval` (the map to the double dual).
- `locallyConstantHom G M`, `locallyConstantHomQZEquiv`: homomorphisms that are locally constant on
  `G` are the continuous characters.
- `GroupCoh.shortComplex_exact_of_forall`, `GroupCoh.intToRat`, `GroupCoh.ratToRatModInt`,
  `GroupCoh.ratSES`, `GroupCoh.ratSES_shortExact`: the exact sequence `0 → ℤ → ℚ → ℚ/ℤ → 0` of
  trivial representations; `GroupCoh.bijective_nsmul_of_module_rat`.
- `bijective_nsmul_of_dvd hmn hn`: multiplication by a divisor `m` of `n` is bijective on an abelian
  group when multiplication by `n` is:

  ```lean
  Langlands.ProfiniteCohomology.bijective_nsmul_of_dvd.{u_1} {A : Type u_1} [AddCommGroup A] {m n : ℕ} (hmn : m ∣ n)
    (hn : Function.Bijective fun a => n • a) : Function.Bijective fun a => m • a
  ```
- `GroupCoh.cyclicChar σ hσ`: for a finite cyclic group generated by `σ`, the character with
  values in `ℚ/ℤ` (as `AddCircle (1 : ℚ)`) sending `σ` to `1/|G|` (`GroupCoh.cyclicChar_apply_gen`,
  `GroupCoh.orderOf_ofAdd_div_card_dvd`).
- `cochainsMap_nsmul`, `cohomologyMap_nsmul_id`: multiplication by `m` on cohomology.
- `isZero_cohomology_of_bijective_nsmul`: for a profinite `G` and a module on which every nonzero
  `m` acts bijectively, `Hⁿ(G, A) = 0` for `n ≥ 1` (cohomology is torsion);
  `isZero_cohomology_trivial_rat`: `Hⁿ(G, ℚ) = 0` for `n ≥ 1`.
- `cochains_d_zero_one_trivial`, `d_one_trivial_apply`, `dLocallyConstant_one_trivial_eq_zero_iff`,
  `ker_sc_one_g_trivial`, `mem_ker_sc_one_trivial_iff`, `cocyclesOneTrivialEquiv`,
  `cohomologyOneIsoOfIsTrivial`, `cohomologyOneIsoOfIsTrivial_hom_cohomologyπ`: for a trivial module
  `M`, the `1`-cocycles are the locally constant homomorphisms and `H¹(G, M)` is their module.
- `cohomologyOneRatModIntIso`: `H¹(G, ℚ/ℤ) ≅ Additive G →ₜ+ QZ`; `isIso_δ_ratSES`,
  `cohomologyOneRatModIntIsoTwoInt`, `cohomologyTwoIntIso`, `cohomologyTwoIntAddEquiv`:
  `H²(G, ℤ) ≃+ (Additive G →ₜ+ QZ)`, and `cohomologyTwoIntAddEquiv_symm_apply`: the inverse sends `χ`
  to `δχ`.
