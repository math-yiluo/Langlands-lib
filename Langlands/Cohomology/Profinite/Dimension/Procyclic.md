# Cohomology of procyclic groups

`Langlands/Cohomology/Profinite/Dimension/Procyclic.lean`, namespace
`Langlands.ProfiniteCohomology`.

Let `σ` generate a topological group `G` topologically. For a smooth module `A`,
`H⁰(G, A) = ker(σ - 1)`; if moreover `σ` is a generator of `ℤ̂`-type (every `ℤ/n` is a
quotient of `G` by an open subgroup, `σ` going to `1`) and `A` is torsion,
`H¹(G, A) ≅ A / (σ - 1)A` by evaluation of cocycles at `σ`, and `H²(G, A) = 0`. For the trivial
module `ℤ`, `H²(G, ℤ) ≅ ℚ/ℤ` through the characters of `G` evaluated at `σ`. These are the facts
behind the cohomology of the Galois group of a finite field and of the unramified extensions of
a local field.

## Declarations

- `IsTopologicalGenerator σ`: the subgroup generated by `σ` is dense. `IsZHatGenerator σ`:
  `σ` is a topological generator and for every `n > 0` there is a homomorphism `G → ℤ/n` with
  open kernel sending `σ` to `1`.
- `Langlands.IsLocallyConstant.eq_of_eqOn_dense`: two locally constant maps agreeing on a dense
  set are equal. `IsTopologicalGenerator.eq_of_forall_zpow`, `eq_of_forall_zpow_pi`: locally
  constant functions on `G` (on `Gⁿ`) are determined by their values at the powers of `σ`.
- `inhomogeneousD_zero_apply`, `inhomogeneousD_one_apply`, `cocycle_mul_of_d_eq_zero`,
  `eq_const_of_fin_one`: formulas for the inhomogeneous differential in degrees `0` and `1`.
- `Langlands.MonoidHom.isLocallyConstant_of_isOpen_ker`: a homomorphism with open kernel is
  locally constant. `exists_pow_mem_of_isOpen`: in a compact group some positive power of every
  element lies in a given open subgroup; `IsTopologicalGenerator.eq_top_of_isOpen`.
- `IsTopologicalGenerator.cocycle_eq_zero`: a locally constant `1`-cocycle vanishing at `σ`
  vanishes. `sum_range_mul_eq_zero`, `IsZHatGenerator.exists_cocycle`: for a `ℤ̂`-generator and
  an element `a` of finite order of a smooth module, there is a locally constant cocycle with
  value `a` at `σ`.
- `cocycleFun`, `isLocallyConstant_cocycleFun`, `d_cocycleFun`, `cocycleFun_toCycles`,
  `cocycleFun_injective`: the function underlying a cocycle of degree `1`.
- `evalCocycles A σ`, `H1Eval A σ : H¹(G, A) → A / (σ - 1)A` (evaluation at `σ`), with
  `evalCocycles_apply`, `toCycles_evalCocycles`, `cohomologyπ_H1Eval`,
  `H1Eval_cohomology_class_apply`. `H1Eval_injective` for a topological generator,
  `H1Eval_surjective` for a `ℤ̂`-generator, a compact group and a torsion module.
- `invariants_eq_ker`, `H0IsoKer hσ : H⁰(G, A) ≅ ker(σ - 1)`; `H1Iso hσ hA : H¹(G, A) ≅ A / (σ - 1)A`
  (`H1Iso_hom`).
- `isAddTorsion_discreteCoind`, `mem_range_sub_one_discreteCoind`, and
  `IsZHatGenerator.isZero_cohomology_two`: for a `ℤ̂`-generator of a compact group and a smooth
  torsion module, `H²(G, A) = 0`.
- `isAddTorsion_QZ`; `evalCharacter σ` (value at `σ` of a continuous character `G → ℚ/ℤ`),
  injective for a topological generator, surjective for a `ℤ̂`-generator
  (`evalCharacter_injective`, `evalCharacter_surjective`, `evalCharacterEquiv`,
  `evalCharacterEquiv_apply`).
- `cohomologyTwoIntIsoQZ hσ`, `cohomologyTwoIntAddEquivQZ hσ : H²(G, ℤ) ≃+ ℚ/ℤ` (for a profinite
  group in universe `0` with a `ℤ̂`-generator), and `cohomologyTwoIntAddEquivQZ_δ`: the image of
  the connecting map applied to a character `χ` is `χ(σ)`.

- `IsZHatGenerator.map_continuousMulEquiv (e : G ≃ₜ* H) (hσ : IsZHatGenerator σ) :
  IsZHatGenerator (e σ)`: `ℤ̂`-generators are carried to `ℤ̂`-generators by isomorphisms of
  topological groups.
- `card_cohomology_one_eq_card_invariants [CompactSpace G] (hσ : IsZHatGenerator σ) (A)
  [IsSmoothRep A] [Finite A] : Nat.card (cohomology A 1) = Nat.card A.ρ.invariants`: by `H1Iso`
  and `invariants_eq_ker`, both sides are `|A / (σ - 1)A|` and `|ker (σ - 1)|`, which agree for
  finite `A`.

Here `QZ` is the discrete `ℚ/ℤ` of `Characters.lean` (the Library's `RationalModIntegers`).

## References

- J.-P. Serre, *Galois Cohomology* (English translation of *Cohomologie galoisienne*, Springer 1997;
  corrected printing 2002), chapter I, §3.2, Example 1, p. 18 (`G = ℤ̂`: `cd_p(G) = 1` and
  `H²(G, ℤ) ≅ H¹(G, ℚ/ℤ) = ℚ/ℤ`), and J.-P. Serre, *Local Fields* (Springer GTM 67, 1979;
  translation of *Corps locaux*), chapter XIII, §1, pp. 188–189 (cohomology of `ℤ̂`: Proposition 1,
  `H¹ = A'/(F - 1)A`; its Corollary, the characters of `ℤ̂` form `ℚ/ℤ`; Proposition 2,
  `H²(ℤ̂, A) = 0` for `A` torsion or divisible). Serre treats the group `ℤ̂` itself; here `G` is a
  compact group with a generator of `ℤ̂`-type.
- J. Neukirch, A. Schmidt, K. Wingberg, *Cohomology of Number Fields*, 2nd edition (Springer 2008;
  corrected printing 2013), chapter I, §7, p. 78 (procyclic groups), and Proposition (1.7.7),
  pp. 78–79 (for `G = ∏_{p ∈ S} ℤ_p` and `A` `S`-torsion, `H¹(G, A) ≅ A_G` by evaluation of cocycles
  at a generator; `Hⁿ(G, A) = 0` for `n ≥ 2` if `A` is torsion or `S`-divisible).
