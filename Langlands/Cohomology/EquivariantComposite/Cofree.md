# Smooth cofree representations

Author: Worker 141.

This internal construction supports the compact composite comparison. It constructs
smooth functions on a group, with left translation, as the smooth part of the
representation on all functions. Evaluation at the identity and orbit functions
prove the adjunction with the underlying module functor. A set theoretic section
of any surjective linear map lifts a function and preserves its uniform open
stabilizer. Thus the cofree functor is exact. The exact underlying module functor
also shows that cofree representations of injective modules are injective.
No compactness or restriction on the commutative coefficient ring is used.
