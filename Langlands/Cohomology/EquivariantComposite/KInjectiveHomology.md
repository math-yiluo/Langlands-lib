# Derived morphisms into K-injective complexes

Author: Worker 134.

This internal comparison combines the published termwise Hom/cohomology-class
comparison with Mathlib's localization theorem for K-injective targets. It
identifies cohomology of termwise Hom from an object with shifted morphisms
from that object in degree zero in any supplied derived-category model.

Additivity follows by representing classes by cocycles and using additivity of
localization and the cocycle-to-shifted-chain-map equivalence. Naturality in the
target complex is proved on cycle representatives. The cycle coefficient maps
by postcomposition; the associated cochain and shifted chain map do so as well.
Naturality of localization's shift comparison gives the required equality.
The homology and derived-category universes are independent.
