# Compact saturations and smooth functions

Author: Worker 141.

This internal helper derives continuity of the joint action from the existing
stable-neighbourhood hypothesis and continuity of each group translation. Near
`(g,x)`, choose an open subgroup `H` and an `H`-stable neighbourhood `V` of `x`
inside the inverse translate by `g` of the target open. Then `gH × V` maps into
that target open. Consequently the saturation of a compact subset by a compact
group is compact, with no separation assumption on the space.

A function in the smooth cofree representation is constant on a right coset
`H g` of its uniform open fixing subgroup. It is therefore locally constant,
and compactness of the group makes its range finite. These are internal
consequences of the definitions used in the compact composite proof.
