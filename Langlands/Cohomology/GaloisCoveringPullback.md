# Pullback of a Galois covering

Let `G` be a `GaloisCovering J Γ V' V` on a category with Grothendieck topology `J`. Given an arrow `b : B ⟶ V` and a pullback of `G.p` along `b`, write `a : P ⟶ V'` and `p' : P ⟶ B` for its projections. `GaloisCovering.pullback` constructs a Galois covering of `B` with source `P`, covering arrow `p'` and the same group `Γ`. The construction uses the given square and applies to groups of any cardinality.

The deck arrow for `γ` is the unique lift with projections `a ≫ G.deck γ` and `p'`. The identities of the original deck arrows and the universal property give the lifted action laws. The sieve generated by `p'` is the pullback of the sieve generated by `G.p`, so it covers by stability of `J` under pullback. For two arrows into `P` over `B`, their projections to `V'` lie over the same arrow into `V`. The original covering transitivity sieve gives local deck translates on those projections. The equalities over `B` and `V'` give the required local equalities in `P`. If a lifted deck arrow fixes a test arrow, its projection to `V'` is fixed by the original deck arrow, so original local freeness applies.

This is an internal consequence of the local site formulation in `Langlands.Cohomology.GaloisCovering` and of the categorical pullback property. It makes descent along a Galois covering available after changing the base to a test object.
