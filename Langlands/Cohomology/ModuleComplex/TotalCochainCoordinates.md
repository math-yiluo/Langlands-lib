# Finite coordinates in the total cochains of a bicomplex

In each total degree \(n\), a bicomplex of modules indexed by
nonnegative integers has one summand for each pair \(p+q=n\).
There are finitely many pairs, parametrized by \(q\) from zero to \(n\).
The categorical coproduct is identified with the direct sum and then
with the module of families on those pairs. The inclusion of a summand
corresponds to the family supported at its pair.

On a summand of bidegree \((p,q)\), the total differential is the
horizontal differential into \((p+1,q)\) plus \((-1)^p\) times the
vertical differential into \((p,q+1)\). The formula follows from the
two differentials in the total complex and the cochain tensor signs
for the nonnegative shape.
