# Proof: irreducible representations with compact coefficients are injective and projective

Author: Worker 1498, with supporting modules by Worker 1643.

This module proves the published statement
`Langlands.Cohomology.SupercuspidalExtCharZero.CompactCoefficientInjectiveProjectiveStatement`
of Worker 3's module `Langlands.Cohomology.SupercuspidalExtCharZero.Statement`, unchanged.

## Theorem

`compactCoefficientInjectiveProjective k G μ :
CompactCoefficientInjectiveProjectiveStatement k G μ`. It holds for every field
`k : Type u` with `CharZero k` and every group `G : Type u`. The group is a Hausdorff,
locally compact, totally disconnected, σ-compact topological group with a regular
left Haar measure `μ` that is also right invariant. For every irreducible
`ρ : SmoothRep.{u, u, u} k G` with `HasCompactCoefficients ρ`, it gives
`Injective ρ ∧ Projective ρ`.

The measure, σ-compactness and the measurable structure are not used. Only the
Hausdorff, locally compact and totally disconnected topology of `G` enters. Haar
integrals are replaced by counting sums over the cosets of compact open subgroups.

## Proof

The proof combines three results.

- Projectivity: `projective_of_hasCompactCoefficients ρ hirr hc`, from
  `Langlands.Cohomology.SupercuspidalExtCharZero.Projectivity`.
- Admissibility: Worker 1643's `finiteDimensional_fixedVectors ρ hirr hc K hK`, from
  `Langlands.Cohomology.SupercuspidalExtCharZero.Admissibility`. It gives
  finite-dimensional fixed vectors under every compact open subgroup `K`.
- Injectivity: `injective_of_hasCompactCoefficients ρ hirr hc hadm`, from
  `Langlands.Cohomology.SupercuspidalExtCharZero.Injectivity`, with `hadm` the
  admissibility result.

## Route and provenance

The Markdown of the statement module sketches a proof through formal degrees over an
algebraically closed field, followed by descent. That route uses Vignéras (1996), I.7.4,
7.5, 7.7, 7.9 and 7.10. The implemented proof is different and works over `k` directly.

1. `CosetSum` defines finite sums over left cosets of an open subgroup. They replace
   Haar integrals, as in Vignéras I.2.4 (printed pages 12–13).
2. `Positivity` shows that a bi-invariant compactly supported `F : G → k` with
   `F 1 ≠ 0` has a nonzero self-convolution sum `∑_{pU} F (x⁻¹ p⁻¹) F (y⁻¹ p)` for some
   `x` and `y`. It proves this over `ℂ` by positivity of `∑ |h|²`. It then transports
   the statement from the finitely generated `ℚ`-algebra generated by the values of `F`
   to `ℂ` through a residue field, which is a number field.
3. `CoefficientMap` uses this to find a compact open `U`, a `U`-fixed functional `ξ` and
   a `U`-fixed vector `w`. The coefficient map `v ↦ ∑_{gU} ξ (ρ g⁻¹ v) • ρ g w` is then a
   nonzero self-intertwiner, hence bijective for irreducible `ρ`. These maps are the
   counting-sum analogue of the equivariant maps in the proof of Vignéras I.7.7 (ii).
4. `Projectivity` lifts maps along epimorphisms with the coefficient map into the
   source of the epimorphism, as in the proof of Vignéras I.7.9 a). It inverts the
   bijective self-map instead of using a formal degree.
5. `Retraction` builds an intertwiner `r` back from any smooth representation into
   which `ρ` embeds, with `r` restricting to the bijective coefficient map. It uses the
   admissibility of `ρ` through a finite-dimensional bidual argument. `Injectivity`
   then exhibits `ρ` as a retract of an injective object, using the enough-injectives
   instance `Langlands.SmoothRep.enoughInjectives`. This replaces the
   contragredient argument of Vignéras I.7.10.

Worker 1643 authored compact-open averaging in characteristic zero,
`Langlands.Representation.Compact.CharZeroAverage`, and the admissibility module. Worker
1498 authored the remaining modules and this one. Steps 2–5 are internal derivations
for this project, and the Markdown of each module records its details.

All are new and are in the directory `Langlands/Cohomology/SupercuspidalExtCharZero/`,

  `SmoothRep.epi_iff_surjective`) and
