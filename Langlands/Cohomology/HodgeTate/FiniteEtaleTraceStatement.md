# Finite étale trace and proper smooth Hodge–Tate comparison

`ProperSmoothHodgeTateFiniteEtaleTraceStatement` concerns the natural
proper smooth Hodge–Tate family over a finite extension \(K/\mathbb Q_p\).
Let \(Y\to X\) be a finite étale Galois cover of a proper smooth scheme
over \(K\), with group \(\Gamma\). The schemes and the finite deck group
are in universe zero. The cover and deck maps are actual scheme
morphisms. The cover is proper smooth by composition, so both endpoints
use the same comparison family.

In degree \(q\) the proposition asserts injectivity of each pullback
and existence of trace on both completed cohomologies. Write \(b\)
for the covering map. On each side the trace is uniquely characterized
by
\[
   b^*\operatorname{Tr}_{Y/X} = \sum_{\gamma\in\Gamma}\gamma^*.
\]
The equation specifies the unnormalized transfer. The comparison
intertwines the two trace maps by an equality of linear maps over the
completed algebraic closure of \(K\). The étale side is the rational
adic cohomology of the geometric scheme after scalar extension. The
coherent side is the product of the scalar-extended groups of relative
forms, with the semilinear Galois actions and twists inherited from the
proper smooth comparison.

Milne's trace for a finite étale cover is defined by the sum on a
common finite Galois cover. In the Galois case this gives the displayed
identity. Trace after pullback is the degree times the identity, which
implies injectivity after passing to the rational coefficients. The
finite-stage trace is natural in the coefficient sheaf, so the identity
passes through the adic limit and scalar extensions. The natural
Hodge–Tate comparison intertwines the actual pullback by \(b\) and by
each deck transformation. Transporting the étale trace through that
family gives the coherent trace with the same norm characterization.
This comparison for Galois covers supplies the transfer step once the
appropriate common upper levels of a Hecke correspondence are given.
