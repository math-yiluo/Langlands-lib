# Higher-derived sections from finite augmented test systems

This internal construction connects the finite augmented test-system criterion
to the actual `sectionsDerived` functors for module sheaves on a site.

Let `C : Type w` have morphisms in universe `w`, let `J` be a Grothendieck
topology on `C`, and let `R : Type w` be a commutative ring. Assume module
sheafification exists. The equal universe levels ensure that both the module
of sheaf morphisms and the module of sections lie in `ModuleCat.{w} R`.

For an object `U`, `freeRepresentableSectionsIso` identifies the linear Hom
functor from the free module sheaf generated by `shrinkYoneda U` with the
Library's `sheafSectionsModule J R U`. Its components are the module
isomorphisms from `freeRepresentableHomLinearEquiv`. Naturality follows from
postcomposition of sheaf morphisms and evaluation on sections.

Assume also that the category of module sheaves has enough injectives.
`freeRepresentableSectionsDerivedIso` identifies the right-derived functors
of that linear Hom functor with `sectionsDerived J R U q` for every degree
`q`. It derives the forward and inverse natural transformations of the
representing isomorphism. Preservation of composition and identities by
`NatTrans.rightDerived` proves that the two derived transformations are
inverse.

For a family `U : ι → C`, supply a `FiniteCechSystem` whose test object at
`i` is the free module sheaf on `shrinkYoneda (U i)`. For any coefficient
module sheaf `F` satisfying this system's `HomAcyclic` predicate,
`FiniteCechSystem.isZero_sectionsDerived_of_homAcyclic` proves
`IsZero ((sectionsDerived J R (U i) (n + 1)).obj F)` for every `i` and
natural number `n`. It transports the higher-derived linear Hom vanishing
from `derivedHomAcyclic_of_homAcyclic` through the derived representing
isomorphism. `FiniteCechSystem.sectionsDerivedZeroIso` gives the resulting
isomorphism to the zero module.

The provenance is internal: the construction combines the free-sheaf
adjunction and Yoneda representing equivalence with functoriality of right
derivation and the finite augmented test-system dimension-shift argument.
It does not construct a finite test system on an analytic site or establish
`HomAcyclic` for a restriction of an injective equivariant sheaf. Those
geometric and coefficient exactness obligations remain necessary for the
analytic compact comparison theorem.
