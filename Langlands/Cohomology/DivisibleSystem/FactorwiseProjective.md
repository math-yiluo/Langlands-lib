## Why a factorwise hypothesis

The LevelProjective module proves its results under `IsUnitMulUniformizerPow ℓ π e`: `(π)` is a
maximal ideal, `e > 0` and `ℓ = u π ^ e` for a unit `u`. Harris's `𝒪` is a finite product of
discrete valuation rings, one for each place of `L` above `ℓ`. A maximal ideal of a finite
product of local rings is the product of the maximal ideal of one factor and the other factors.
So an element generating it is a unit in every other factor, and so is `ℓ = u π ^ e`. The
hypothesis therefore fails for `𝒪` when `ℓ` is a nonunit in two factors. In Harris's case this
happens when `L` has two or more places above `ℓ`. The module uses the factor decomposition
instead. This remark explains the design and is not formalized.

## The factorwise hypothesis

`R` is a commutative ring, `ℓ : ℕ`, `ι` is a finite type, `ε π : ι → R` and `e : ι → ℕ`.

- `IsFactorwiseUnitMulUniformizerPow ℓ ε π e` (a structure, `Prop`-valued) has four fields.
  Each `ε i` is idempotent (`idem`), and `∑ i, ε i = 1` (`sum_eq_one`). Each `π i` generates a
  maximal ideal of `R` (`isMaximal`). For each `i` there is a unit `u` with
  `ℓ * ε i = u * π i ^ e i * ε i` (`exists_unit`). So on the factor `R ε i`, `ℓ` is a unit
  times `π i ^ e i`. The exponent `e i` may be `0`, when `ℓ` is a unit on the factor.
  Orthogonality of the `ε i` is not required.
- `IsFactorwiseUnitMulUniformizerPow.exists_unit_pow`: there is a unit `u` with
  `ℓ ^ n * ε i = u ^ n * π i ^ (e i * n) * ε i` for every `n`. The proof is by induction on `n`.
- `IsFactorwiseUnitMulUniformizerPow.pow_smul_smul_eq_zero`: if `ℓ ^ m` kills a module `M`,
  then `π i ^ (e i * m) • ε i • x = 0` for every `x : M`. This holds because
  `π i ^ (e i * m) * ε i = (u⁻¹) ^ m * (ℓ ^ m * ε i)`.
- `IsUnitMulUniformizerPow.factorwise`: `IsUnitMulUniformizerPow ℓ π e` gives the case of one
  factor, with `ι = Unit` and `ε = 1`.

## Finite products of rings

`S : ι → Type*` is a family of commutative rings, `ι` is finite, and `∏ i, S i` is the product
ring.

- `IsFactorwiseUnitMulUniformizerPow.span_mulSingle_eq_comap`: the ideal generated by
  `Pi.mulSingle i a`, which has `a` at `i` and `1` elsewhere, is the preimage of `(a)` under
  evaluation at `i`. If `x i = a * r`, then `x = Pi.mulSingle i a * Function.update x i r`.
- `IsFactorwiseUnitMulUniformizerPow.pi` takes two hypotheses: each `π i` generates a maximal
  ideal of `S i`, and `ℓ = u * π i ^ e i` in `S i` for a unit `u`. Then `∏ i, S i` satisfies
  `IsFactorwiseUnitMulUniformizerPow ℓ` with idempotents `Pi.single i 1`, elements
  `Pi.mulSingle i (π i)` and exponents `e`. The ideal `(Pi.mulSingle i (π i))` is maximal by the
  previous lemma and `Ideal.comap_isMaximal_of_surjective`, since evaluation is surjective. The
  unit is the image of `u` under `MonoidHom.mulSingle S i`. The identity `exists_unit` is checked
  coordinatewise: it is the hypothesis at `i`, and `0 = 0` elsewhere.
- `IsFactorwiseUnitMulUniformizerPow.exists_pi_of_isDiscreteValuationRing`: suppose every `S i`
  is a discrete valuation ring and `ℓ ≠ 0` in every `S i`. Then there are `π` and `e` for which
  the product satisfies the hypothesis with the idempotents `Pi.single i 1`. The proof chooses an
  irreducible `π i` in each factor (`exists_irreducible`). Then
  `eq_unit_mul_pow_irreducible` writes `ℓ = u * π i ^ e i`, and `(π i)` is the maximal ideal by
  `irreducible_iff_uniformizer`. No nonunit hypothesis is needed, because `e i = 0` is allowed.

## Lifting from a module with flat powers of `π`

- `PowQuotientFlat.exists_lift_smul`: let `(π)` be a maximal ideal and let `M` satisfy
  `PowQuotientFlat π N M`. Let `p : X → Y` be a surjective linear map, and suppose
  `π ^ N • s • x = 0` for all `x : X`. Then for every linear `g : M → Y` there is a linear
  `f : M → X` with `p (f y) = s • g y`. `exists_presentation` gives generators `v` of `M` whose
  relations have all coefficients divisible by `π ^ N`. Choose `x j` with `p (x j) = g (v j)`.
  The map `c ↦ s • ∑ c j • x j` vanishes on relations, since `c = π ^ N • d`. It factors
  through `M` by `Submodule.liftQ` and `LinearMap.quotKerEquivOfSurjective`. For `s = 1` this
  is the argument of `PowQuotientFlat.powTorsionProjective`.

## The levels of an `ℓ`-divisible system of modules

`T` is an `ℓ`-divisible system in `ModuleCat.{v} R` and
`h : IsFactorwiseUnitMulUniformizerPow ℓ ε π e`. The factor `ε i • T m` is the submodule
`ε i • ⊤` of `T.obj m`, the pointwise scalar multiple of the top submodule, that is, the image
of `x ↦ ε i • x`. Since `ε i` is idempotent, it acts as the identity on the factor.

- `DivisibleSystem.powQuotientFlat_factor`: `ε i • T m` satisfies
  `PowQuotientFlat (π i) (e i * m)`. The first condition is `pow_smul_smul_eq_zero`. For the
  second, let `a + b = e i * m` with `a > 0` (the case `a = 0` is trivial), let `x = ε i x`
  with `π i ^ b • x = 0`, and let `j : T m ⟶ T (a + m)` be the inclusion. Then `e i > 0`. The
  axiom `flat a m` gives `j x = ℓ ^ a • z`. So `j x = ε i • j x = π i ^ a • w` with
  `w = (u ^ a * π i ^ (e i * a - a) * ε i) • z`, and `ε i • w = w`. Then
  `ℓ ^ m • w = u ^ m • π i ^ b • π i ^ a • w = u ^ m • j (π i ^ b • x) = 0`. So `w = j y` by
  `torsion_exact`, and `π i ^ a • (ε i • y) = x` because `j` is injective. This is the proof of
  `DivisibleSystem.powQuotientFlat_obj`, carried out on the factor.
- `DivisibleSystem.powTorsionProjective_obj_of_factorwise`: `PowTorsionProjective ℓ m (T.obj m)`.
  Let `p : X ⟶ Y` be an epimorphism with `ℓ ^ m` killing `X`, and let `g : T m ⟶ Y`. Then
  `π i ^ (e i * m)` kills `ε i • X`. So `exists_lift_smul` with `s = ε i`, applied to `g`
  restricted to the factor, gives `f_i` with `p ∘ f_i = ε i • g` there. Composing with
  `y ↦ ε i • y` gives `f_i' : T m → X` with `p (f_i' y) = ε i • g (ε i • y) = ε i • g y`.
  Then `f = ∑ i, f_i'` satisfies `p ∘ f = (∑ i, ε i) • g = g`.
- `DivisibleSystem.exists_free_generators_factor`: the factor has a family of generators `v`,
  indexed by a type in universe `v`, such that `∑ c j • v j = 0` if and only if
  `π i ^ (e i * m) ∣ c j` for all `j`. So the factor is free over `R ⧸ (π i ^ (e i * m))`.
  This is `PowQuotientFlat.exists_free_generators` applied to `powQuotientFlat_factor`.
- `DivisibleSystem.underlyingPowTorsionProjectiveBelow_of_factorwise`: let `h` be the hypothesis
  in a commutative ring `k`. Then `UnderlyingPowTorsionProjectiveBelow N K` holds for every
  group `Γ`, every `N` and every `ℓ`-divisible system `K` of `ℕ`-graded cochain complexes in
  `Rep.{w} k Γ`. For each degree `q`, the forgetful image `(K.degree q).map (forget₂ _ _)` is an
  `ℓ`-divisible system of `k`-modules, and the previous lemma applies to it. This is the proof of
  `underlyingPowTorsionProjectiveBelow_of_isUnitMulUniformizerPow`.

## Harris's coefficient ring

`L : Type u` is a number field and `p : FinitePlace ℚ`.

- `coefficientIntegers_natCast_ne_zero`: for a finite place `l` of `L` and a natural number
  `ℓ ≠ 0`, `(ℓ : CoefficientIntegers l) ≠ 0`. The completion `FiniteCompletion L l` has
  characteristic zero by `Algebra.charZero_of_charZero L`. The algebra map from
  `CoefficientIntegers l` sends `ℓ` to `ℓ`, which is nonzero there.
- `harrisCoefficientIntegers_isFactorwiseUnitMulUniformizerPow`: for every natural number
  `ℓ ≠ 0` there are `π` and `e` such that `HarrisCoefficientIntegers L p` satisfies
  `IsFactorwiseUnitMulUniformizerPow ℓ` with idempotents `Pi.single l 1` and elements
  `Pi.mulSingle l (π l)`. The proof applies `exists_pi_of_isDiscreteValuationRing` with Mathlib's
  instance `IsDiscreteValuationRing (v.adicCompletionIntegers K)` and the previous lemma. The
  decidable equality of `FinitePlacesAbove L p` needed by `Pi.single` is classical. `ℓ` and `p`
  are independent here: Harris's case, in which `p` is the place of the prime `ℓ`, is not
  singled out.

## Over Harris's coefficient ring

`ℓ` is a natural number with `[NeZero ℓ]`, and `L`, `p` and `ℓ` are explicit arguments. Each
result takes `π`, `e` and `h` from `harrisCoefficientIntegers_isFactorwiseUnitMulUniformizerPow`
and applies the factorwise result above.

- `DivisibleSystem.powTorsionProjective_obj_harrisCoefficientIntegers`: every level `T m` of an
  `ℓ`-divisible system of `HarrisCoefficientIntegers L p`-modules is projective relative to
  modules killed by `ℓ ^ m`.
- `DivisibleSystem.underlyingPowTorsionProjectiveBelow_harrisCoefficientIntegers`: the hypothesis
  `UnderlyingPowTorsionProjectiveBelow N K` holds for every `ℓ`-divisible system `K` of
  `ℕ`-graded cochain complexes of representations over `HarrisCoefficientIntegers L p` and every
  `N`.

The remaining results concern Harris's complexes `C_m^{•,H}`. For the truncated smooth Godement
resolutions these are `smoothGodementInvariantTotalSystem h T D H hterm hres`. In the pro-`q`
case they are `proPSmoothGodementInvariantTotalSystem h T D hP hH hR hterm hres`, for a normal
pro-`q` subgroup `H` of finite index with `q` a unit in the coefficients. The letter `q` is used
because `p` names the place. The variables are those of the InvariantTotalProjective lemmas, with
coefficients `HarrisCoefficientIntegers L p`; `G` and `X` are in the universe of `L`. `W` is an
`ℓ`-divisible system of representations of `G ⧸ H` for the `homSystem` lemmas, and of
`ℤ`-graded cochain complexes of such representations for the `homComplexSystem` lemmas. Each
proof applies the InvariantTotalProjective lemma to
`underlyingPowTorsionProjectiveBelow_harrisCoefficientIntegers`.

- `TermwisePowTorsionProjective` of `C_m^{•,H}`:
  `termwisePowTorsionProjective_smoothGodementInvariantTotalSystem_harrisCoefficientIntegers` and
  `termwisePowTorsionProjective_proPSmoothGodementInvariantTotalSystem_harrisCoefficientIntegers`.
- The transitions of `T_m(j) = Hom(C_m^{j,H}, W_m)` are surjective (Harris's (4.10.3)):
  `smoothGodementInvariantTotalSystem_degree_homSystem_map_surjective_harrisCoefficientIntegers`
  and
  `proPSmoothGodementInvariantTotalSystem_degree_homSystem_map_surjective_harrisCoefficientIntegers`.
- The inverse system of the `T_m(j)` satisfies the Mittag-Leffler condition:
  `isMittagLeffler_smoothGodementInvariantTotalSystem_degree_homSystem_harrisCoefficientIntegers`
  and
  `isMittagLeffler_proPSmoothGodementInvariantTotalSystem_degree_homSystem_harrisCoefficientIntegers`.
- The inverse system of Hom complexes `Hom^•(C_m^{•,H}, W_m)` satisfies the Mittag-Leffler
  condition in every degree:
  `isMittagLeffler_smoothGodementInvariantTotalSystem_homComplexSystem_eval_harrisCoefficientIntegers`
  and
  `isMittagLeffler_proPSmoothGodementInvariantTotalSystem_homComplexSystem_eval_harrisCoefficientIntegers`.

These results keep the other hypotheses of the InvariantTotalProjective lemmas: `h` (a proof of
the unproved proposition `SmoothGodementZeroExactStatement`), `T`, `D`, `H`, and the
surjectivity hypotheses `hterm` and `hres`. In the pro-`q` case they also keep `hP` (the unproved
proposition `ProPInvariantsExactStatement`), `hH` and `hR`. The forms for
`groupStandardTotalSystem` and `quotientInvariantsTotalSystem` over
`HarrisCoefficientIntegers L p` follow the same way from
`underlyingPowTorsionProjectiveBelow_harrisCoefficientIntegers` and are not restated.

## Provenance and interpretation

Harris, *Galois properties of cohomological automorphic forms on GL(n)*,
J. Math. Kyoto Univ. 39-2 (1999), printed pp.304, 306, 307 and 312 (one-based PDF pages 6, 8, 9
and 14).

1. Harris's coefficient ring. On p.306 Harris says that "`ℓ` is a finite prime in `ℚ`", and he
   writes "`L_v = L ⊗_ℚ ℚ_v`". On p.307: "Let `𝒪` denote the ring of integers of `L_ℓ`, and let
   `𝓛_W` be a `K_ℓ`-stable `𝒪`-lattice in `W(L)`." The Library's model of `𝒪` is
   `HarrisCoefficientIntegers L p` from the HarrisLattice module. It is the product of the
   valuation rings of the completions of `L` at the places above the finite place `p` of `ℚ`.
   This identification is that module's, through `harrisCoefficientIntegersMap`, and is not
   reproved here. The integer `ℓ` is a natural number, as in the Library's
   `harrisCoefficientScalarIdeal L p ℓ = (ℓ)`. Nothing links `ℓ` to `p`, and the results hold
   for every `ℓ ≠ 0`.
2. Harris's use. On p.312 (proof of Proposition 4.10), Harris deduces the surjectivity of
   (4.10.3) from the freeness of `C^j_m(ẽ)^{H_p}` and of the target over
   `𝒪/ℓ^m𝒪[S(ẽ)/H_p]`. The InvariantTotalProjective module records the quotation and replaces
   freeness by relative projectivity, keeping the hypothesis
   `UnderlyingPowTorsionProjectiveBelow`. The LevelProjective module proved the hypothesis when
   `ℓ = u π ^ e`, which covers `ℤ`, `ℤ_[p]` and a single discrete valuation ring. This module
   proves it over `HarrisCoefficientIntegers L p`, so the Harris-ring results above depend only
   on the remaining hypotheses listed.
3. The route. The hypothesis `IsFactorwiseUnitMulUniformizerPow`, the lifting lemma
   `exists_lift_smul` and the assembly of the factor lifts are ours. The decomposition of a
   module over a finite product of rings by orthogonal idempotents is standard commutative
   algebra. Here only `∑ i, ε i = 1` and idempotence are used. The facts that the completions
   have discrete valuation rings and characteristic zero come from Mathlib.
4. Not shown. Harris's definition of an `ℓ`-divisible system (p.304, quoted in the
   LevelProjective Markdown) requires `T_m` to be free over `ℤ/ℓ^mℤ`. This module does not show
   that the level `T m` is free over `R ⧸ (ℓ ^ m)`. Its factors are free over the rings
   `R ⧸ (π i ^ (e i * m))`, and freeness over the product ring would need their ranks to agree.
   The terms of `C_m^{•,H}` are not shown to be free over `(𝒪/ℓ^m𝒪)[G ⧸ H]`. As in the
   LevelProjective module, only the underlying modules are treated, which is what the
   InvariantTotalProjective lemmas need.

## Scope

The module proves no freeness over `R ⧸ (ℓ ^ m)` or over group algebras, and no
`Module.Free` instance. It treats modules and the underlying modules of terms of complexes of
representations, not sheaves. The surjectivity hypotheses on `H`-invariants (`hterm`, `hres`)
and the unproved propositions of the parent modules stay explicit.
