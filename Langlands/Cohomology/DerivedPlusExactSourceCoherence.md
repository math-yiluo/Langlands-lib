# Comparisons for exact bounded-below derived functors

Exact additive functors between abelian categories with derived categories act on bounded-below derived objects. `mapDerivedPlusIdIso` and `mapDerivedPlusCompIso` lift the canonical identity and composition isomorphisms from the ordinary derived category along the full inclusion of bounded-below objects. The categories have independent object, morphism and derived universes.

On a bounded-below complex representative, the composition isomorphism is the localization of the termwise composition comparison, using the localization factorizations. On a single-object complex it agrees with the canonical comparisons with the single complex of the image. The degree-zero single-object identification for the identity functor is the identity comparison. These equations follow by the faithful inclusion from Mathlib's `Algebra/Homology/DerivedCategory/ExactFunctor`.

Let \(T\colon C\to D\) and \(S\colon D\to E\) be exact additive functors, and let the source categories have enough injectives. For additive functors \(F\), \(G\) and \(H\) to a common abelian category with a derived category, `rightDerivedComparison_comp` composes the comparisons induced by \(F\to G\circ T\) and \(G\to H\circ S\). After the exact derived composition identification, the result is the comparison induced by \(F\to H\circ S\circ T\). The proof uses the universal property of right derivation to check the comparison on bounded-below complex representatives, where the equality follows from naturality of the derived units and the equality of the termwise maps. The same argument with the identity functor gives `rightDerivedComparison_id`. The single-object identifications give `derivedComplexComparison_comp` and `derivedComplexComparison_id` on coefficients in degree zero.
