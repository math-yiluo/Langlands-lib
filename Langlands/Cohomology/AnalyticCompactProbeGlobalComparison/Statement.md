# Ordinary and smooth cohomology comparisons on the compact-probe site

`CompactProbeQuasiEtaleGlobalComparisonStatements` concerns quasi-étale
module sheaves on a Hausdorff analytic space `X` over a fixed complete
ultrametric field with a continuous analytic action of a topological
group `G`. The smallness of `G` is relative to the coefficient
universe and the sheaves use a fixed commutative ring. The finite
equivariant refinements come from the compact-probe covering test and
the geometric lifting and compatibility results.

- If `X` is paracompact, the statement asserts invertibility in each
  degree of the natural map from the right derived functors of
  `F ↦ Γ(X,F|_X)` to ordinary cohomology of the restriction.
- If `X` is compact, the degreewise comparison is the one from the
  cohomology of the smooth-section functor, after forgetting the
  action, to the cohomology of the ordinary restriction. The derived
  clause gives invertibility of the specified lift in the derived
  category of modules. The smooth representation and module derived
  categories use independent localization universes.
- The compact clause also includes
  `CompactProbeQuasiEtaleSpectralSequenceStatement`. The ordinary
  cohomology of the restricted sheaf has the action obtained from
  derived sections on the twisted site. Its underlying module is
  identified with ordinary compact-probe cohomology by exact
  restriction. The statement asserts smoothness for this action
  and identifies the second page of the covariant hyperext sequence
  with smooth group cohomology, computed by Ext from the trivial
  smooth representation. The finite first-quadrant filtration has
  abutment given by the right derived functors of invariant global
  sections.

Both maps use the actual exact restriction from the twisted
compact-probe site to its ordinary site. The smooth comparison extends
the inclusion of smooth sections into ordinary sections. The derived
map uses exact postcomposition to forget the action, the derived map
of that inclusion and the comparison for the derived composite along
restriction.

The source is Berkovich, *Étale equivariant sheaves on p-adic analytic
spaces*, Theorem 1.6.1(i)–(ii), printed and PDF pp. 13–14. The
manuscript proves the compact quasi-étale comparison with finite
Čech refinements and computes the paracompact comparison using change
of site. The derived form follows from the degreewise comparison and
vanishing of negative cohomology for complexes of derived sections.
