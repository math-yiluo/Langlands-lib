# Pointed Galois refinement over the ambient analytic base

The inverse image of an open inside an analytic domain can be
computed inside the domain. The nested-domain isomorphism identifies
the two restrictions. Reidentification of the target of a Galois cover
preserves its deck graphs.

A pointed étale neighbourhood with cofinal inverse images of base opens
admits a distinguished base-open restriction inside any prescribed
neighbourhood, on a Hausdorff base with continuous topological group
action. First choose a compact analytic domain containing an open slice
at the marked point. Cofinality makes it contain the inverse image of a
smaller base neighbourhood. Choose a distinguished ambient open below
that neighbourhood. Its inverse image within the compact domain has
analytic-domain complement, and the compact-domain map to the ambient
base is quasi-étale.

A relative finite Galois cover with singleton fibre over the marked
point gives a pointed source neighbourhood. Properness follows from
the finite deck action. Shrink the ambient base until the restriction
of the target lies in the relative cover's base open and the source
restriction is distinguished. Open restriction of the cover and the
target isomorphism then give the ambient Galois cover and retain its
factorization through the original étale arrow.

This internal bridge gives `HasPointedEtaleGaloisRefinements` on a
Hausdorff base with continuous action, assuming the relative pointed
refinement statement. Applying the Cartan reduction yields the
requested higher-direct-image conclusion under that single geometric
premise and the original hypotheses.
