# Pointed étale neighbourhoods for analytic Galois germs

`EtaleGermNeighborhood X y` consists of an étale object and a point over
`y` such that every open neighbourhood of its point contains the inverse
image of a neighbourhood of `y`. The base space represents such a pointed
neighbourhood. Restriction to a base open containing `y` preserves the
condition. For a proper map to an object with this condition, a singleton
fibre over the chosen point gives the condition on the source. Closedness
of the map allows an open around the singleton to contain the inverse image
of an open around the point below it.

An étale object determines a diagram by restriction to the ordinary
neighbourhoods of `y`. On a Hausdorff space with a continuous analytic
action of a topological group, the published stable distinguished
neighbourhood theorem gives a cofiltered and initial subsystem of
distinguished opens inside any given neighbourhood.

This internal construction supplies neighbourhood indices for the section
germs of analytic Galois covers and for the ordinary germ at the base.
