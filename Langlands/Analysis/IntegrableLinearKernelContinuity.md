# Continuity of integrable scalar kernels on barrelled spaces

Let a measurable-space argument index continuous complex-linear forms on a
sequential barrelled topological complex vector space. Assume that the
coefficient of every vector is integrable for the given measure.
`IntegrableLinearKernelContinuityStatement` asserts that the integral of
the pointwise norm defines a continuous seminorm and that the scalar
integral defines a continuous linear form.

The triangle inequality and scalar homogeneity of the norm give the
seminorm. For any convergent sequence of vectors, continuity of each
pointwise form gives convergence at each measure-space argument. Fatou's
lemma preserves the seminorm sublevel bounds at the limit. The sublevel
sets are therefore sequentially closed and hence closed. Barrelledness
makes the lower semicontinuous seminorm continuous. The integral is
linear by the integrability hypothesis and is bounded in norm by this
seminorm, so it is continuous at zero and everywhere.

One use is the continuity of determinant-weighted integrals of smooth
Whittaker pairs. Jacquet uses continuity of both weighted forms to pass
from pure tensors to the completed tensor product in §12.3, pp. 89–90.
The general continuity criterion here uses Fatou's lemma and the
barrelled topology of the source.
[H. Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009)](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/PerfectRankinSelberg.pdf#page=89).
