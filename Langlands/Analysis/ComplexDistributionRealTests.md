# Real tests determine a complex distribution

Precompose the complex-linear functional with the continuous map that regards a real test as complex valued. This gives a real-linear distribution with complex values. Real and imaginary parts of a complex test have support in that of the original test. Complex linearity and the decomposition into real and imaginary parts prove equivalent vanishing on each specified support set.

This comparison lets the proof apply the real-test slice estimates to the complex distribution from the Whittaker functional.
