# Angular averages of Schwartz tests

`schwartzAngularAverage` integrates a complex-coordinate Schwartz function over the angles with a prescribed integral character. Its value is a Schwartz function of the real radii. The coordinatewise rotations give a continuous family of real linear isometries, so the theorem for Schwartz averages applies with both uniform bounds equal to one. The angular measure is the product of Lebesgue measures restricted to \((-\pi,\pi)\).

The same module separates Mathlib's product polar coordinate domain into the product of the radial and angular measures by a measurable equivalence. These constructions are used to express complex diagonal zeta integrals as real Mellin integrals.
