# Vector fields in a smooth chart

The coordinate field at \(x\) applies the inverse chart derivative at \(e(x)\) to the original vector at \(e(x)\). The field is smooth on the chart source. The chain rule for the local inverse shows that it equals a prescribed coordinate vector when the forward chart derivative sends that vector to the original field.

For a test transported to the target, the chain rule identifies its derivative along the original field with the transported coordinate derivative. It suffices that the coordinate field agree on the test's support, since the derivative vanishes outside that support. This supplies the operator transport in the lower Bruhat cell argument.
