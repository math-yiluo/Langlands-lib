# Joint smoothness and the topology of tests on a compact support

The iterated derivatives in the test variable of a jointly
smooth function are jointly continuous. This follows by
restricting each full Fréchet derivative to the directions
of the test variable. Restrict each derivative family to
the compact support and view it as a continuous function
with the supremum norm. Joint continuity then gives
continuity into that normed space.

The derivative seminorm of the difference of two tests is
bounded by the corresponding supremum norm of the difference
of the compact derivative families. The fixed-support
test-function topology is generated by these seminorms, so
the bundled test family is continuous. This supplies the
continuity needed when differentiating the covariance of
smooth matrix distributions in Shalika (1974), §2.
