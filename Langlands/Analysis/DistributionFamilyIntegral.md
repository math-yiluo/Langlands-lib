# Integrals of smooth test families

This is an internal analysis construction for the open Bruhat cell argument in Task 7525. It packages differentiation under the integral sign and a continuity argument for distributions; it does not state or prove Shalika's open-cell proposition or multiplicity-one theorem.

`SmoothCompactFamily Φ` says that each slice `Φ m : A → E` is smooth, that all its iterated derivatives are jointly continuous in `(m, x)`, and that the slices are zero outside one compact set of parameters. The integration lemmas use a measure finite on compact sets and a finite-dimensional real normed space `A`. On the product of the compact parameter set and a closed ball about the differentiation point, the derivative is bounded. This gives a local integrable dominating function, with value zero off the compact parameter set. The derivative theorem for dominated integrals and the curry isometries for continuous multilinear maps give the iterated derivative identity. Applying the one-derivative argument to every order gives smoothness of the integral.

`JetSpace A k` is the finite product, with its supremum norm, of the spaces of bounded continuous multilinear-map-valued functions for orders `0` through `k`. `JetSpace.jetCLM K k` sends a fixed-support test function to its iterated derivatives in that product. It is injective by the order-zero component. This normed-space construction is internal: its purpose is to realize finitely many defining seminorms of the fixed-support test space as norms of components of one map.

`map_integral_family` takes a complex distribution `T` on an open set `Ω`. In addition to `SmoothCompactFamily`, its hypothesis requires all slices to vanish off a common compact `K ⊆ Ω`. If `φ m` denotes the test function of the slice and `ψ` the test function whose values are the pointwise integrals, the result is `T ψ = ∫ m, T (φ m) ∂μ`. To prove it, restrict `T` to the fixed-support space. A finite set of derivative seminorms controls this restriction, so its functional on the range of a sufficiently large jet map is norm bounded. Complex Hahn–Banach extends it to the whole jet space. Each jet component varies continuously: on `K` this follows from joint continuity and compactness, and off `K` the derivatives vanish. The resulting jet family is Bochner integrable by compact parameter support. Evaluating the integral component by component and point by point identifies it with the jet of `ψ`, by the derivative identity. The extended functional commutes with this Bochner integral, giving the desired equality.
