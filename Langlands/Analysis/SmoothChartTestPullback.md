# Pullback of tests along an open chart

For an open partial homeomorphism \(e\) that is smooth on its source, compose a test on the target with \(e\) and extend by zero off the source. A compact support \(K\) in the target maps under the inverse to a compact subset of the source. This controls the support and makes the extension smooth at points off the source.

On each fixed support, the chain rule bounds an output derivative seminorm by a constant times the maximum of the input seminorms of orders at most that derivative order. Smoothness gives uniform bounds on the chart derivatives on the inverse image of the compact support. The fixed-support maps therefore give a continuous linear pullback for the test function topology. The scalar field can be real or complex. On the source, the derivative obeys the usual chain rule. If the inverse chart is also smooth, its pullback recovers the original test.

This auxiliary construction passes distributions to the local coordinates used in Shalika's Propositions 2.10 and 2.11.
