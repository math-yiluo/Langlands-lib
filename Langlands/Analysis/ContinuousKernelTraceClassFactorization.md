# Trace class by continuous compact-kernel factorization

The continuous kernel composition is the composition of two bounded L² kernel operators through an intermediate compact space. Both spaces have finite Borel measures, and the original space is second countable. If a continuous kernel on the square of the original space is the integral of the product of the two factors, its L² operator is this composition. The proof uses Fubini. The fixed continuous kernels are bounded on their compact domains, and every input L² vector is integrable on a finite measure space. The two factors are Hilbert–Schmidt, so their composition on the original space is trace class.

This internal operator criterion is used for the trace character of the real compact-picture model in Task 7534.
