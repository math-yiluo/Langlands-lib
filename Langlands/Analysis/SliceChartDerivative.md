# Fixed-support derivatives in chart coordinates

A real test on the slice chart can be made complex valued and transported by the inverse chart. The chain rule for complexification and pullback identifies its derivative along the original field with the transported derivative along the coordinate field. Agreement of the field on the fixed compact support suffices for this identity.

A smooth cutoff extends the local coordinate field globally without changing it on the compact set. Tangency on the zero slice is preserved by scalar multiplication and by vanishing off the chart. The resulting derivative on fixed-support tests therefore preserves the vanishing jets used in the slice-distribution argument.
