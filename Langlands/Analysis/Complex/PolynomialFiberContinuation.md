# Continuation of polynomial fibers

Let `F z s` depend holomorphically on the parameter `z` in a connected open subset of the complex plane for each spectral point `s` in a right half-plane. If its spectral fibers are polynomials on a nonempty open parameter subset, each spectral fiber on the full domain agrees with a polynomial on that half-plane. The resulting polynomials have a common finite degree bound.

At each candidate degree, interpolate from a fixed finite set of spectral points in the right half-plane. For every spectral point the discrepancy from the interpolant is holomorphic in the parameter. The closed sets where all discrepancies vanish cover the open subset by the polynomial hypothesis. The Baire category theorem gives an interpolant whose discrepancy is zero on an open parameter set. The identity principle extends the interpolation formula to the connected domain.

This is a derived continuation argument for the use of holomorphic parameters in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg Integrals* (2009), Proposition 8.1, pp. 60–61 of the author version, registered as `jacquet-2009-archimedean-rankin-selberg`, SHA-256 `7a11766eadf25fb7452ce7faa193469791796f572fc654c8fe90a8608bb46afe`.
