# Entire continuation by estimates on vertical lines

This internal analytic lemma supports the passage from standard tests to general Schwartz tests in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009), §8.1, printed p. 54. It assumes a sequence of entire functions with exponential strip growth and exponentially weighted Cauchy estimates on pairs of vertical lines enclosing any specified compact set.

Multiplication by \(\exp(\varepsilon z^2)\) makes the boundary estimates uniform in the imaginary coordinate. The Phragmén–Lindelöf principle transports these estimates into the strip. At each evaluation point the proof chooses \(\varepsilon=(1+|\operatorname{Im}z|)^{-1}\), which gives an exponential interior estimate with constants controlled by the two boundary lines. Applied to differences, the estimate gives a locally uniform Cauchy sequence and an entire limit. Comparing this limit to one fixed member of the sequence transfers the exponential growth bound to the limit.
