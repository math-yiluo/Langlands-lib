# Pointwise analytic families in finite-dimensional ranges

Let a finite-dimensional complex vector space map linearly to a space of complex-valued functions. Allow the map to vary with a complex parameter, with entire value at each input vector and evaluation point. Assume a set of parameters determines entire scalar functions uniquely. If a pointwise entire family lies in the corresponding range at every point of this set, it lies in the range at every parameter where the map is injective.

At the specified parameter, express the coordinate functionals of the range in a basis as finite sums of evaluations. Applying these evaluations to the moving basis gives a square matrix that is the identity at the specified parameter. The adjugate gives a linear combination of basis images equal to the given family times the determinant on the determining set. Every evaluation in this identity is entire. Scalar uniqueness extends it to the specified parameter, where the determinant is one. The resulting coefficients give the required preimage.

The argument uses finite-dimensional duality and the complex identity theorem. It applies to families of canonical Jacquet coefficients with a fixed finite space of compact profiles.
