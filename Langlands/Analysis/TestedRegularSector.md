# Decay from comparable increments at regular truncation parameters

`TestedRegularSectorStatement` treats the comparison of Delorme's Lemmas 7.2 and 7.7 when the increment estimate uses a regularity condition in addition to a sector. The regularity condition contains the sector outside some ball and is preserved by dilation by a factor at least one. The sector is stable under positive scaling and contains a unit vector. The increment bound only needs to hold when its starting point in the sector is regular and has norm at least one. Assuming the local uniform increment bounds, eventual continuity, and vanishing limits of the smooth compactly supported spectral tests, the decay estimate holds at each regular starting point of norm at least one.

`testedRegularSector` first applies `testedExponentialSector` beyond the radius where regularity is automatic. For a regular point \(t\) of norm at least one, take the unit vector \(u=t/\|t\|\) and the sequence \(t_j=(\|t\|+j)u\). The sequence stays in the sector and every term is regular by the dilation hypothesis. The radial bound makes the values tend to zero along this sequence. Since \(t_{j+1}-t_j=u\), each increment is bounded by \(B e^{-\epsilon\|t\|}(e^{-\epsilon})^j\). Summing the geometric series and using \(t_0=t\) gives the same bound \(B e^{-\epsilon\|t\|}/(1-e^{-\epsilon})\) at the original point.

For the application, the fixed lower bound on the simple roots of \(T\) gives regularity. The sector inequality \(\alpha(T)\geq\delta\|T\|\) makes that regularity automatic beyond a radius that can depend on \(\delta\). The result keeps the simple-root regularity requirement fixed when passing from increments to the final bound.

The source is P. Delorme, *On the spectral theorem of Langlands*, Advances in Mathematics 426 (2023), 109107, Lemma 7.2, (7.27), and Lemma 7.7, pp. 26 and 31–32.
