# Derivative estimates near a slice with vanishing jets

Successive mean-value estimates bound each lower derivative of a
smooth function by a power of the radius of a closed ball. The
estimate assumes that the lower derivatives vanish at the center
and uses a uniform bound on the highest derivative.

Apply the estimate in a product space to a function whose full
derivatives through order `m` vanish along the slice with second
coordinate zero. Its compact support bounds the derivative of
order `m + 1`. Multiply by a cutoff in the normal coordinate
whose width is `ε`. Each term of the iterated product rule
combines an inverse power of `ε` from the cutoff with a higher
power from the flat function. For each order at most `m`, the
result is bounded by a constant times `ε`, uniformly on the
product space.

This is the cutoff estimate behind the finite normal-jet
description of supported distributions used in Shalika (1974),
Lemma 2.4. The lemma here concerns smooth functions and their
actual Fréchet derivatives.
