# Smooth compact restriction of a manifold family

A smooth map on a product of real manifolds restricts along a continuous map from a compact space in its second variable. The result is smooth in the first variable with values in the supremum-norm space of continuous functions on that compact space. The proof uses the jointly smooth partial derivative in a chart, the compact-family derivative criterion and induction over the finite derivative orders. This is the function-valued counterpart of compact parameter integration in `Langlands.Analysis.CompactManifoldVectorIntegral`.
