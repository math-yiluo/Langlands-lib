# Smooth evaluations of Schwartz families on a common compact support

A smooth joint test family with a fixed compact support in its spatial variable has smooth scalar values under any complex-linear form on the supported Schwartz space bounded by a continuous real Schwartz seminorm. The parameter ranges over an open subset of a real normed space on which the family is represented by the joint function. Given a supported Schwartz representative of the pointwise derivative in a parameter direction, the scalar derivative is the form applied to that representative.

The seminorm is dominated by finitely many defining Schwartz seminorms. On the fixed compact support the spatial weights are uniformly bounded, and the iterated spatial derivatives vanish off that set. The form therefore factors through a continuous linear functional on a finite product of spaces of continuous derivative coordinates, using Hahn–Banach to extend the factor from the range of the coordinate map. The compact restriction theorem makes the coordinates smooth in the parameter. Commutation of the two partial derivatives, iterated in the spatial variable, identifies the derivative of every coordinate with the corresponding coordinate of the pointwise parameter derivative. The continuous linear functional then gives the scalar derivative formula. This is an internal input for differentiating the translated Godement column value.

`exists_compactJet_factor` gives the finite coordinate factorization used in the proof. `derivativeCoordinate` is the map taking a test to one spatial derivative restricted to the compact set.
