# Distributional support from annihilating symbols

`DistributionSymbolSupportStatement` formalizes the support implication in Delorme 2023, Lemma 7.4, pp. 31–32. A complex distribution on an open subset of a finite-dimensional real normed space is tested on compactly supported smooth functions. If multiplying any test by any member of a family of smooth complex symbols always gives a test annihilated by the distribution, the distributional support is contained in the common zero set. The distribution vanishes on tests supported in the complement, and a finite common zero set gives finite distributional support.

For a test supported where one symbol is nonzero, divide the test by that symbol. Division is smooth near its support and the quotient is zero on the open complement of the support. The quotient test has support contained in the original support, and multiplication recovers the original test. The local division gives the support inclusion. The local vanishing theorem assembles vanishing for the entire complement. In Delorme's application the multipliers are the polynomial infinitesimal characters associated with the adjoint of the annihilating ideal. The hypotheses for applying the support implication are multiplier annihilation and finiteness of the common zeros.
