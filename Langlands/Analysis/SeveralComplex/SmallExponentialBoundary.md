# Small exponential bounds from a nearby vertical segment

`smallExponentialAtZero_of_rightBoundary` considers an indexed family of entire functions and a nonnegative size function \(S\) on the index set. Suppose the functions are bounded by \(C\exp(MS)\) on the closed disk of radius two. On the segment \(\operatorname{Re} z=\delta\), \(|\operatorname{Im}z|\leq 1\), suppose the bound improves to \(A_\delta\exp(D\delta S)\) for each \(0<\delta<1\). Then for every \(\epsilon>0\) the family at zero is bounded by \(B_\epsilon\exp(\epsilon S)\).

Use the rectangle with real interval \([\delta-1,\delta]\) and imaginary interval \([-1,1]\). For \(P(z)=(\delta-z)(2-\delta+z)\), the real part of \(P\) is nonnegative on the rectangle and is at least one on the three edges other than the right edge. Multiplication by \(\exp(-MSP(z))\) absorbs the ambient bound on those three edges and retains the better bound on the right edge. The maximum modulus principle bounds the modified family at zero. Undoing the multiplier contributes at most \(\exp(2M\delta S)\), so a sufficiently small positive \(\delta\) gives the asserted estimate. This is a direct application of the maximum modulus principle to the holomorphic parameter of the family.
