# Continuous reproducing kernels from local $L^2$ bounds

`exists_compact_reproducingKernel_of_l2_evaluation` applies to a complex subspace of continuous functions on a locally compact Hausdorff second-countable group with an inversion-invariant left Haar measure. The subspace is stable under left translations, and evaluation at the identity is bounded by a fixed multiple of the $L^2$ norm on a compact set `V`. The result is one continuous compactly supported kernel that reproduces evaluation on the entire subspace.

The linear map to $L^2(\mu|_V)$ sends a function to its restriction class. The estimate puts its kernel in the kernel of evaluation, and bounds the induced linear functional on the range. Hahn–Banach extends that functional, and the complex Hilbert representation theorem expresses it by an inner product against a vector in $L^2(\mu|_V)$. Conjugating a measurable representative and multiplying by `1_V` gives a measurable weight `k` in $L^2(\mu)$ supported in `V`. Evaluation against `k` works on all left translates of each function in the subspace.

The convolution square is `A x = ∫ y, k y * k (y⁻¹ * x) ∂μ`. It is a matrix coefficient of the continuous left translation action on $L^2(\mu)$. The first vector is the complex conjugate of `k` and the second is `k` composed with inversion. The Haar invariance hypotheses make both operations well defined on $L^2$ and preserve the norms used in the continuity argument. The kernel is supported in the compact product `V * V`. Finally `k` belongs to $L^1$ by its compact support. The double integral of `f (y * z) * k y * k z` is absolutely integrable because `f` is bounded on `V * V`. Fubini and the left Haar change of variables identify it with integration against `A`, while the original reproducing identity applied twice gives `f 1`.

This is an internal consequence of the local evaluation bound, Hahn–Banach, Hilbert representation and left Haar invariance. The proof uses Mathlib's continuous composition on `Lp` for the translated coefficient.
