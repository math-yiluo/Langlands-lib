# Differentiation in exponential product coordinates

In a real Banach algebra, the factor \(\exp(tX)(1+m(Y))\) is smooth when \(m\) is continuous linear. At zero its derivative in the normal coordinates is \(tX+m(Y)\). If the factor multiplies a smooth function of the tangent coordinates, differentiation in the last coordinate at any point is left multiplication of the product by \(X\).

The proof uses Mathlib's exponential derivative and product rule. These auxiliary identities apply to the matrix chart used in the lower-cell argument for Shalika's Proposition 2.10.
