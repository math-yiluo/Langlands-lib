# Finite rank in a holomorphic profile family

`profileMatrix` is the square matrix of point evaluations of a finite family of functions. Finite linear independence admits an invertible evaluation matrix, and an invertible evaluation matrix detects independence.

For a holomorphic family with finite rank at each point of a nonempty open regular set, an unbounded rank would produce a nonzero evaluation determinant of every size. The nonvanishing locus of each such determinant is open and dense in the regular set by the analytic identity principle. Baire's theorem would give a point of the regular set with arbitrarily many independent profiles. The resulting bound permits choosing a maximal independent finite family. Wherever its minor stays nonzero on the holomorphy domain, maximality forces every profile into its span.

This derived analytic argument will control the finite-dimensional orbit spans of coefficients using the compact and center finiteness of the regular Eisenstein values.
