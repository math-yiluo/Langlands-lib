# Scalar meromorphy on an open cover

The continuous linear image of a weakly meromorphic function is meromorphic
on the same holomorphy domain. In each test for weak holomorphy, compose
the scalar test map with the given continuous linear map. Linearity
preserves the local scalar denominator and applies to the numerator.
Equality on the holomorphy domain lets one change values away from that
domain, since the same numerator identities still hold there.

Consider a scalar function whose restrictions to an open cover are
meromorphic, with an open dense holomorphy domain in each open subset
of the cover. Transport the local holomorphic functions to the
ambient space using the open-subtype chart. Its forward map is the
subtype inclusion. The union of the local holomorphy domains is open
and dense, and at every point of that union one local
differentiability assertion gives ambient differentiability.

For the local denominator at an arbitrary point, choose one piece of
the cover containing it. A local denominator and numerator in that
piece give differentiable functions on the image of their connected
open neighborhood. There, the local holomorphy domain is dense.
Continuity of the denominator times the function and of the
numerator extends their equality to the whole part of the global
holomorphy domain in this neighborhood. These presentations give
meromorphy on the ambient space. The domain is the specified union,
so it can be used for all scalar evaluations of the same local
function-valued data.

These lemmas use the local denominator predicate of Bernstein–Lapid
§3.1 and apply to their local Fréchet-space formulation of the
continuation in §8.6.
