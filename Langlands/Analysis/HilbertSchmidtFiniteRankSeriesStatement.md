# A Hilbert–Schmidt criterion for series of finite-rank operators

`Langlands.Analysis.OperatorIdeals.HilbertSchmidtFiniteRankSeriesStatement` concerns a family `T` of bounded operators with finite-dimensional range on a complete complex Hilbert space. Suppose the operators sum in operator norm to `A`, with `HasSum T A` using the net of finite partial sums. If the series with terms `sqrt(rank(T j)) * ‖T j‖` is summable, then `A` is Hilbert–Schmidt in the Library's Hilbert-basis sense. The Hilbert space and the index type have independent universes.

This is a derived operator-ideal estimate. For a finite-rank operator the square of its Hilbert–Schmidt norm is bounded by its rank times the square of its operator norm. Parseval's identity in the finite-dimensional range and Bessel's inequality on the source give this bound first for every finite set of basis vectors. The triangle inequality for finite tuples of images gives a common bound for the corresponding partial Hilbert–Schmidt norms of the series. Passing to the operator-norm limit preserves that bound for each finite subset of the Hilbert basis. Bounded nonnegative partial sums give Hilbert–Schmidt summability.
