# Coordinate product bounds for Schwartz kernels

A Schwartz seminorm on a finite coordinate space controls the
Schwartz test times each coordinate's polynomial weight. The
product of those weights is bounded by the corresponding power
of one plus the supremum norm.

The resulting product of scalar decay bounds proves integrability
against a product of coordinate weights. It suffices that each
scalar weight have integrable norm after the fixed polynomial
damping. This estimate applies to the half-planes of absolute
convergence of the real radial and complex diagonal Mellin integrals.
