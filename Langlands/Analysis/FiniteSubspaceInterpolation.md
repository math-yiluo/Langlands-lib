# Interpolation in a finite sum of function spaces

Let a finite family of finite-dimensional complex subspaces consist of functions on the same set. There are finitely many evaluation points and weight functions for each subspace. Every function in their sum is recovered from its values at these points, with the contribution assigned to each subspace given by its own weights and lying in that subspace.

The proof chooses a linear section of the sum map and uses the evaluation isomorphism on its finite-dimensional image. The section applied to the inverse images of the coordinate vectors gives the weight functions. This derived interpolation allows a single family of ambient functions with prescribed exponents to recover the tested values of a Bruhat term.
