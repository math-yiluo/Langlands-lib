# Tangential operators preserve vanishing jets

Constant real directional derivatives commute by symmetry of the
second Fréchet derivative. Inducting through a tuple of derivative
directions moves a constant directional derivative outside the
iterated derivative evaluated on that tuple.

At a point of a flat slice, decompose a smooth tangential vector
field into its constant value there and a remainder vanishing at
that point. The constant value points along the slice, so its
derivative of a zero jet along the slice is zero. The product rule
for the remainder pairs its undifferentiated value with the highest
derivative, and every term involving a derivative of the remainder
uses a lower derivative of the test function that vanishes. Thus a
tangential derivative preserves the order of the vanishing jet.
Smooth scalar multiplication also preserves the order.

These are the test-function filtration properties used by the
tangential differential operators in Shalika (1974), §2.
