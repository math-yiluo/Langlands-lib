# Finite jets for distributions supported on a slice

Restrict a distribution to smooth real test functions supported
in a fixed compact subset of its domain. The topology on this
space is given by the uniform norms of all Fréchet derivatives.
Continuity bounds the restriction by a finite sum of those
seminorms.

Suppose the distribution vanishes on tests supported away from
the slice with second coordinate zero. Multiply a test function
by a smooth cutoff of width `ε` in the second coordinate. The
difference is supported away from the slice, so the value of the
distribution is unchanged. When the jet of the test function
through the controlling derivative order vanishes on the slice,
the derivative estimates for cutoffs bound this value by a
constant times `ε` for every `0 < ε ≤ 1`. It follows that the
distribution annihilates the test.

The theorem gives the finite-jet dependence on each fixed compact
support in the local coordinate argument of Shalika (1974),
Lemma 2.4. Its assumptions refer to the classical LF distribution
space and the usual support predicate.
