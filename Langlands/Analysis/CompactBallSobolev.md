# Uniform Sobolev bounds in the interior of a compact ball

A ball of half the radius around any point in the smaller
concentric ball lies in the original closed ball. Apply the averaged
Taylor estimate to this translated ball. Each of its derivative
integrals is bounded by the corresponding integral on the original
ball. Additive Haar translation invariance gives the same finite
positive denominator at every center. This yields a single
pointwise bound from the common derivative integral bound.

The module also integrates a finite pointwise sum bound. This
allows the derivative integral bound to be assembled from the
integrals of the original scalar functions.

The estimate is an internal consequence of the existing local
Sobolev estimate and uses its derivative orders through one more
than the real dimension of the model space.
