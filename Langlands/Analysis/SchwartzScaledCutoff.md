# Convergence of scaled cutoffs in Schwartz space

`SchwartzScaledCutoffStatement` gives convergence in the full Schwartz
topology of the products `x ↦ g(t • x) f(x)` to `f` as the real dilation
parameter `t` tends to zero, provided `g` is Schwartz and `g(0) = 1`.

The mean value inequality bounds the difference of the zeroth-order
cutoff values by a constant times `‖t‖ ‖x‖`. At a positive derivative
order the linear dilation contributes a power of `‖t‖`. The Leibniz
bound then controls each Schwartz seminorm of the product difference by
`‖t‖` times a finite sum of fixed seminorms. The same convergence with
a compactly supported smooth bump gives density of compactly supported
smooth functions on each finite-dimensional real normed space.

This is an internal approximation step for extending the standard-test
calculation in Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals* (2009),
§§8.1–8.2, pp. 54 and 56–57, to general Schwartz tests.
