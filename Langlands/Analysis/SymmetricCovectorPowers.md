# Pure powers of continuous covectors

The degree `n` pure power of a real continuous covector is the continuous multilinear form obtained by multiplying its values on `n` arguments. The power depends continuously on the covector and precomposition in each argument agrees with precomposition of the covector itself.

On a finite-dimensional real space, these powers span the symmetric continuous multilinear forms. To see this, apply a projection with kernel equal to the pure-power span to the multilinear product of arbitrary covectors. Its diagonal values vanish. Differentiating the diagonal function to degree `n` gives the sum over permutations of the covector products. This sum therefore belongs to the span. Expanding an arbitrary multilinear form using coordinate covectors and summing over permutations proves the symmetric case, since the permutation count is nonzero over the reals.

A basis can then be selected from the pure powers. The corresponding coordinate functionals extend continuously to the full space of multilinear forms via a linear projection. This gives a fixed finite expansion for symmetric forms, including degree zero, and allows the same expansion for their smoothly varying coefficient tests.

These internal lemmas supply the finite-dimensional algebra and continuity used in the leading normal jet argument.
