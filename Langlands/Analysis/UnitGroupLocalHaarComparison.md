# Local comparison of additive and multiplicative Haar measure on units

For the open unit chart of a finite-dimensional real normed algebra,
the pullback of additive Haar measure is dominated on each compact
set by a finite multiple of a supplied multiplicative Haar measure.

Left multiplication by a unit is a real linear equivalence of the
ambient algebra. The additive Haar change-of-variables formula gives
its inverse absolute determinant as the scaling factor for the
unweighted chart measure. The same factor is multiplicative in the
unit. Weighting the chart measure by this factor gives a left
invariant measure, finite on compact sets by continuity. Haar
uniqueness expresses that measure as a scalar multiple of the
supplied measure. The factor has a strictly positive minimum bound on
each compact subset, giving the claimed domination.

For a compact product patch whose second coordinate set is nonempty
and open, the second Haar measure has finite positive mass there.
Tonelli's theorem then compares the product integral of a function
constant in that coordinate with its first-coordinate integral. The
group splitting transports the original Haar measure to a positive
multiple of the product Haar measure. Combining these facts gives an
additive chart integral bound from the original group Haar integral.

This internal comparison uses the real vector space underlying the
algebra, including for products of real and complex matrix algebras.
