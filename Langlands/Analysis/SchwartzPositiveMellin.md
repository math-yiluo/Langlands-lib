# Positive orthant Mellin integrals of Schwartz functions

`coordinateMellin` integrates a Schwartz test against independent
complex powers on the product of positive real half-lines. For
positive real parts the coordinate product majorant reduces absolute
convergence to a damped integral in each variable.

Along an affine complex line with real coordinate slopes, the
kernel is a fixed measurable factor times `exp(z B(x))`, where
`B(x)` is a real linear combination of the logarithms of the
coordinates. The holomorphic parameter-integral theorem applies
on the open intersection of the convergence half-planes.
