# From a chart rectangle to open vanishing

A small closed ball around zero in the right-associated product model is a normal coordinate box with the same radius in the last coordinate. It lies in the chart source when the radius is small enough. Real tests supported in its interior give fixed-support tests on that box. Their vanishing gives vanishing on complex chart tests by real and imaginary decomposition.

The chart image of the open ball is an open neighborhood of the center. Pulling an ambient test back to the chart, then transporting it forward, recovers the test. This gives ambient vanishing on the image of the ball.
