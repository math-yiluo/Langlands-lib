# Compatibility of product quotient measures

`ProductQuotientCompatibilityStatement` concerns two second-countable locally
compact Hausdorff groups \(G_1,G_2\), closed subgroups \(A,B\), and compatible
Weil measures on \(A\backslash G_1\) and \(B\backslash G_2\). The ambient and
subgroup measures are finite on compact sets, and the subgroup measures are
right invariant and sigma-finite. The product quotient measure is the
pushforward of the two quotient measures through the inverse of the canonical
product homeomorphism. It is compatible with the product ambient measure and
with the product subgroup measure transported to \(A\times B\).

The proof uses the quotient by \(A\times\{1\}\) to partially average a
compactly supported continuous test function along \(A\). The partial
average is continuous and compactly supported on
\((A\backslash G_1)\times G_2\). Fubini identifies the full product average
with the average of this function along \(B\). Applying the two supplied
Weil formulas to its slices gives the product Weil formula. Closed subgroup
fibers ensure integrability for compact tests, and the product of the two
right actions preserves the product measure.

This is an internal consequence of the Library's Weil quotient formula and
its topological product quotient. It supplies the exact product normalization
for separating one finite factor of an adelic orbital integral in the global
comparison of Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, §4.
