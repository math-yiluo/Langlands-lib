# Compact open unit subgroups in affine coordinates

A continuous linear equivalence from a proper ultrametric space to a
complete normed algebra transfers the norm to additive coordinates on
the algebra. The operator norms of the equivalence and its inverse
bound multiplication in these coordinates. Choose the radius so that
the multiplication bound times the radius is less than one and all
affine points in the ball are units.

The product of two units in the coordinate ball differs from one by the
sum of their coordinate differences and their product. The ultrametric
bound keeps this in the ball. For an inverse, write its difference from
one as the negative of the original difference minus the product of
the two differences. If the inverse difference exceeded the radius,
both terms in this expression would have strictly smaller norm, a
contradiction.

The affine unit map is an open embedding and its image on the subgroup
is exactly the specified ball under the linear equivalence. Openness and
compactness of the ball therefore give a compact open subgroup.
