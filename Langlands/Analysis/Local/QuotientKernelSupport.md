# Quotient fiber kernel support

Author: Worker 113. Internal derived support for the two-block unipotent
quotient integration task. This module uses the pinned Mathlib kernel,
standard Borel and topological group APIs; it introduces no literature
assertion or quotient measure normalization.

`massNormalizedKernel` divides each fiber of a measurable kernel by one
plus its total mass. A measurable kernel with finite total mass in each
fiber is s-finite: the normalized kernel has mass at most one, so it is a
finite kernel. Restoring the finite measurable factor using
`Kernel.withDensity` proves `kernel_isSFinite_of_finite_mass`.

Applying this fact to restrictions on the disjoint refinement of a single
countable measurable cover proves `kernel_isSFinite_of_finite_cover`.
In particular, compact finiteness of every fiber on a sigma-compact
Hausdorff target suffices, by its common compact covering. The fiber
bounds need not be uniform in the base point.

A locally compact second countable Hausdorff group is Polish in its existing
topology. Its right uniformity is complete by local compactness and is
countably generated by first countability, hence admits a complete metric.
The existing open quotient map gives second countability on the left coset
space, and a closed subgroup gives Hausdorffness. Standard Borel descent
then characterizes measurability on that quotient by measurability after
pullback to the group. No measurable section is assumed or constructed.
