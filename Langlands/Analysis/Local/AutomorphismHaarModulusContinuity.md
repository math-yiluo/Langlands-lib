# Continuity of Haar modulus in a family of automorphisms

Let \(X\) be a locally compact parameter space and \(G\) a locally compact Hausdorff topological group with its Borel sigma-algebra and a regular Haar measure. For a family of continuous group automorphisms \(\alpha_x\), assume joint continuity of \((x,g)\mapsto\alpha_x(g)\) and \((x,g)\mapsto\alpha_x^{-1}(g)\). Then the real Haar modulus of \(\alpha_x\) is continuous in \(x\).

Use a continuous compactly supported real test with a nonzero Haar integral. Joint continuity of inverse evaluation gives a common compact set supporting the translated tests near each parameter. Joint continuity of forward evaluation gives continuity of their integrals. The scaling formula expresses the modulus as the fixed integral divided by the translated integral, which is also nonzero.

For the geometric quotient \(H(\mathbb A_F)\backslash U_Q(\mathbb A_F)\) in Bernstein–Lapid (6.5), \(H=U_Q\cap wU_Pw^{-1}\). Applying the lemma to conjugation on \(U_Q(\mathbb A_F)\) and its restriction to \(H(\mathbb A_F)\) gives continuous factors. The compatible quotient measure scales by their ratio. Applying that formula to the Library's rational intertwining integral uses comparison with the geometric quotient by the full adelic stabilizer with compatible normalized Haar measures.
