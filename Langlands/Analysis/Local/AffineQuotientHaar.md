# Quotient Haar measure in affine transverse coordinates

In a sufficiently small ball, factor a unit uniquely as a subgroup
unit followed by a transverse affine unit. The product map, after
subtracting one, has as its derivative the chosen linear coordinate
equivalence. Its exact additive Haar identity on the ball therefore
gives product coordinates for the restricted Haar measure on units.

The subgroup Haar measure in its coordinate ball has density `cS`,
and the ambient Haar measure in its affine ball has density `cG`.
With transverse density `cG / cS` the two measures multiply to the
ambient chart measure. The compact open quotient chart theorem now
identifies the compatible quotient restriction, including its scale.
