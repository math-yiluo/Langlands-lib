# Compatible quotient measures and group equivalences

This internal transport construction restricts a continuous multiplicative
equivalence to subgroups whose membership corresponds. It proves that pushing
a right invariant measure forward along any continuous multiplicative
equivalence preserves right invariance, directly by conjugating translations.

For corresponding group, subgroup and quotient homeomorphisms, the actual
orbital average computed with the pushed subgroup measure equals the original
average of the pulled function. This is the Bochner change of variables on
the subgroup, followed by the representative formula for the quotient map.

The compatibility theorem pushes all three measures forward simultaneously.
Nonzero and regularity transport through the quotient homeomorphism. Right
translation invariance follows by conjugating the quotient translations.
Integrability and the Weil integral formula follow from the average identity
and change of variables on the quotient and the ambient group. Compact support
of the pulled function follows from the group homeomorphism. No measure is
rescaled or independently reselected, and subgroup normality is not assumed.
