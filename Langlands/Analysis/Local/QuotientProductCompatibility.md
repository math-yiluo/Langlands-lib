# Compatibility of product quotient measures

The homeomorphisms of the existing product subgroup and its left quotient
transport the product of the two subgroup measures and the product of the
two quotient measures. When the two factor quotient measures satisfy the
Weil formula for the supplied two-sided Haar measures, the transported
product quotient measure satisfies the Weil formula for the product Haar
measures.

The proof uses existence of a compatible quotient measure for the
product data. The candidate product quotient measure is nonzero,
regular, and invariant under right translation. Uniqueness up to scale
compares it with the compatible measure. A product test function whose
factor integrals are nonzero determines the scale. On that test
function the subgroup average factors by Fubini and the quotient
integral is the product of the two factor Weil formulas.

This is an internal consequence of the Library's left coset homeomorphisms,
compatible measure existence and invariant measure comparison. It supplies
the exact product normalization used for the ordinary and twisted orbital
integrals on the two-block Levi.
