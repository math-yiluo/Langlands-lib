# Right translation in the quotient variable of a product measure

`QuotientRightShearStatement` concerns the change `(h, Ng) ↦ (h, Ngh)`. It preserves the product of an s-finite group measure and an s-finite right-invariant measure on the left quotient. Joint continuity of the right translation follows from the open quotient map and multiplication on the group. Replacing `h` by `h⁻¹` in the quotient coordinate gives the continuous inverse. The product measure theorem for a fiberwise change of variables applies because each fixed `h` preserves the quotient measure.

This is the quotient-variable change in the passage to the unfolded integral (8.3). [Jacquet, *Archimedean Rankin–Selberg integrals*, §8.3](https://doi.org/10.1090/conm/489/09547).
