# Exact Haar normalization on a complementary factor

`ProductHaarNormalizationStatement` uses a topological group
isomorphism from the product of two second-countable locally compact
groups to the ambient group. The first factor is Hausdorff and all
three groups have their Borel measurable structures. The first factor
and the ambient group have prescribed Haar measures, and the ambient
measure is also right invariant. The statement supplies a regular,
right-invariant Haar measure on the second factor for which the
product measure pushes forward to the prescribed ambient measure.

The proof transports the whole group measure to the direct product.
Haar uniqueness identifies that measure with a positive multiple of
the product formed with any Haar measure on the second factor. It
absorbs that multiple into the second factor measure. Right
translation in the second coordinate preserves the product measure.
Evaluating this identity on rectangles with a compact first coordinate
of finite positive measure gives right invariance on the second factor.

This internal normalization is used to integrate an orbital integral
on the actual norm-one group using the prescribed finite-place Haar
measure.
