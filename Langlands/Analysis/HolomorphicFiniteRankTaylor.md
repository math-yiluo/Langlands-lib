# Finite rank of Taylor coefficient profiles

A maximal holomorphic profile family has a determinant that is nonzero somewhere in the connected domain. `profileCramer` uses its point-evaluation matrix to express each profile after multiplication by that determinant. The scalar identity principle extends the relation to the entire holomorphy domain.

At the point of Taylor expansion the determinant has finite order. Its leading coefficient times each coefficient of any profile is the corresponding Cauchy product minus terms involving lower coefficients. Induction shows that all profiles of one Taylor degree lie in the span of finitely many coefficients of the chosen family. The result uses finite rank of the original profiles only on a nonempty open regular set.

This derived argument applies to compact translates and central derivatives of normalized Eisenstein families.
