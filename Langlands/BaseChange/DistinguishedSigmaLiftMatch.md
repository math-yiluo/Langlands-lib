# Identification of the distinguished sigma-lift after globalization

`DistinguishedSigmaLiftMatchStatement` assumes the exact sigma-lift uniqueness
proposition. The original extension is a cyclic extension of local fields with
characteristic-zero base. Compatible bicontinuous field isomorphisms to a
second realization respect the scalar embedding and the specified
automorphisms. The linear groups in the second realization are Hausdorff,
locally compact and totally disconnected.

Take actual admissible distributional sigma-lifts on the two realizations.
The original lower and upper representations are irreducible, as is the
second upper representation. Identify the original representations with
pullbacks from corresponding representations on the second fields. Assume
the second lower representation is a determinant twist of the corresponding
lower representation by a given quasicharacter. The second upper
representation is then the determinant twist of the corresponding upper
representation by that quasicharacter composed with the relative norm.

Pull the second lift back using field transport. Twist the original lift
by the pulled-back quasicharacter and its norm pullback, then identify the
two lower representations. Uniqueness on the original extension identifies
the upper representations there. Norm compatibility and surjectivity of
the upper field map carry the equivalence back to the second realization.

This supplies the identification at the distinguished place used in
Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, proof of Proposition 6.9(ii), printed p. 69.
The twist is allowed by the Library globalization used in the formal proof.
