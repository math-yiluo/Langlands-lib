# A fixed-level finiteness reduction for σ-discrete series of GL

`sigmaDiscreteFixedLevelFiniteness_of_squareIntegrable` proves
`SigmaDiscreteFixedLevelFinitenessReductionStatement`. The base \(F\) is a characteristic-zero
nonarchimedean local field and the cyclic extension \(E\) is a nonarchimedean local field with
its module topology over \(F\). The proposition assumes ordinary fixed-level finiteness of
square-integrable classes up to determinant twist in ranks at most \(n\). It covers the
σ-discrete series of positive-rank \(\mathrm{GL}_n(E)\) visible at a given open level by
finitely many invariant unramified twist orbits.

The decomposition of a σ-discrete series gives a normalized product of the conjugates
of an irreducible square-integrable representation of \(\mathrm{GL}_m(E)\), with \(s m=n\)
for the orbit length \(s\). There are finitely many such rank and length pairs. For each
pair the fixed-vector detection theorem supplies a compact open level for the first
block. The assumed square-integrable finiteness gives a finite family of possible first
blocks up to unramified twist. Every determinant-order character is invariant under
the field automorphism because the normalized absolute value is invariant. The common
determinant twist of the first block therefore gives the corresponding global determinant
twist of its orbit product. For each family with a σ-discrete member, choose such a member
as the representative. Inverting one twist and composing with another expresses each
remaining member as an invariant unramified twist of the chosen representation.

This is a GL reduction for the finiteness argument in Rogawski (1988), Proposition 10.1
and its proof on printed pp. 224 and 228. For the orbit description it uses the forward
implication of Arthur–Clozel (1989), Chapter 1, Lemma 2.8, and Henniart–Lemaire (2011), II.4.6.
