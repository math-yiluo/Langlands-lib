# Prescribed supercuspidals in an actual spectral presentation

`PrescribedSupercuspidalsInCuspidalPresentationStatement` assumes
`GeneralLinearSupercuspidalGlobalizationStatement`,
`GeneralLinearCuspidalSummandRealizationStatement`, and
`ParabolicDynamicStatement`. The last premise identifies parabolics
with the dynamic subgroups of cocharacters over the base field. The
statement fixes a number field with compact adelic quotient, the
central torus and Haar data for the positive-central quotient, and the
finite local topological and measurable structures. A nonempty finite
set of places carries the prescribed irreducible supercuspidal
actions, and a separate finite place is supplied for globalization.

The Hilbert globalization gives a complete irreducible cuspidal
summand with a nonzero local occurrence of an unramified determinant
twist at every prescribed place. Both the globalization and the
summand realization use the supplied parabolic classification. The
summand realization gives a form realization and a quotient class map.
Its irreducible admissible finite slice gives a spectral presentation
by the finite tensor decomposition.

A character trivial on the units of valuation one is the unramified
quasicharacter determined by its value on a uniformizer. Its determinant
twist remains irreducible and supercuspidal. The isotypy of the local
action on the Hilbert summand identifies this twist with the local
component of the spectral presentation. Transport the equivalence back
to the matrix group at the completion. The actual component is
supercuspidal in the Library's class-based interface, since its
irreducible admissible representative has vanishing proper Jacquet
modules.
