# The distinguished tensor sigma-lift at its unique completion

`DistinguishedTensorLiftTransportStatement` concerns a finite place of
a number field with exactly one place above it in the Galois extension.
It assumes admissible actual local components and a distributional
sigma-lift for the action of `id ⊗ σ` on the completed tensor. In that
lift the upper representation is pulled back from the upper completion
through the canonical projection.

The unique-place projection is a bicontinuous algebra isomorphism. It
matches the tensor automorphism with the completed automorphism on the
upper field. The proof uses that isomorphism to give the tensor its field
structure while retaining its ring operations. It then transports the
distributional identity through the inverse isomorphism and identifies
the transported representations with the original local components.
The resulting intertwiner and constant give the lift at the completion.

This is a transport step for the distinguished place in
Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, §6.3 and proof of Proposition 6.9(ii),
printed pp. 55–57 and 69–70.
