# Comparison of completed pair equations at a distinguished completion

The statement compares a finite family of actual pair factor choices over a number
field with one over an extension. The index set has the cardinality of the relative
degree. Supply the completed equations at standard additive scale, including their
Gamma counts, their constant archimedean epsilon units, and the places outside which
the finite epsilon factors are trivial. Also supply the placewise forward and dual
Euler-factor comparisons and equality of the positive epsilon bases away from the
chosen lower place. If the chosen place has one upper place, the two epsilon products
there agree up to one nonzero constant.

The proof forms the union of the lower exceptional sets and the projections of the
upper exceptional set, with the chosen place included. Enlarging each equation to the
corresponding common set preserves the equality since the added finite epsilon factors
are one. The relative degree gives matching real Gamma counts. The ratio of the
archimedean epsilon units is constant. The distinguished-place comparison then cancels
the common Euler products and the positive epsilon bases at the other places. This
implements the localization of the completed equations (6.15) and (6.16) in the proof
of Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, Proposition 6.9(ii), printed p. 70 (PDF p. 84).
