# The trace of the differential of an exact lift

The matrix centralizers of two regular points of the same rational Weyl
torus coincide. The proof uses a cyclic vector for each matrix and
commutativity of the centralizer of a cyclic matrix.

Near the origin a normalized lift takes values in the extended torus.
Differentiating its commutation with the base-changed norm shows that the
derivative takes values in the corresponding matrix centralizer. The
cyclic norm of the normalized lift is the base change of the affine
rational torus unit. Each factor of the ordered norm is one at the
origin, so the derivative of the product is the sum of the conjugates
of the derivative of the lift. Uniqueness of the derivative identifies
this sum with the scalar inclusion of the rational Lie algebra.
