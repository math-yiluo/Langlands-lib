# Associated Haar measures at an exact norm

When the norm equation holds with the identity conjugator, the ordinary and twisted centralizers are isomorphic by coefficient extension. Any other conjugator of the same norm acts the same way on the twisted centralizer. This follows from commutativity of the scalar-extended ordinary centralizer at a regular semisimple element.

The associated-measure condition thus identifies the two measures after coefficient extension and inclusion in the extension group. The twisted centralizer is a closed subgroup, so its inclusion is a measurable embedding. Injectivity of pushforward along this embedding gives the equality on the centralizer itself. The converse uses the identity witness in the Library's `IsAssociatedMeasure` definition. This is the exact-norm form of the measure correspondence in Henniart–Lemaire, I.2.5.
