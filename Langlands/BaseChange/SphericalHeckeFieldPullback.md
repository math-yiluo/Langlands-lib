# Spherical Hecke pullback along a p-adic field isomorphism

An isomorphism of finite extensions of the same p-adic field preserves the integer rings, residue cardinalities, and normalized orders. The induced isomorphism of the matrix groups carries the two compact open subgroups onto each other. Haar uniqueness with compact subgroup mass one identifies the two spherical Haar measures under transport.

Precomposition preserves the actual spherical Hecke functions and their convolution by change of variable. The image of each standard diagonal differs from the other standard diagonal by a diagonal element of the integral group. Its entries are ratios of uniformizers or ones. Thus the standard Hecke generators correspond, independently of the uniformizer choices.

The Satake character scalars of the generators agree because the residue cardinalities agree. The transported homomorphism satisfies the degree-one Satake condition of Henniart–Lemaire, I.1.3 and I.3.1. The published uniqueness theorem identifies it with every homomorphism satisfying that condition.
