# Relative quotient Haar masses for rank-one concordance

The twisted centralizer contains the scalar matrix given by the rational prime. Its normalized order upstairs is positive. Multiplication by its powers puts the normalized order of every coset representative in a fixed finite interval. These finitely many valuation slices form a compact set, so the full twisted left quotient is compact.

Normalize a compactly supported real test function upstairs to have centralizer average one for the transported base Haar measure. Its restriction to the base group has integral one. For associated centralizer measures, the two fiber averages change by the same Haar scalar factor. The two Weil integral formulas therefore give the quotient mass ratio as the upstairs integral of the normalized test. This integral is positive.

In rank one, ordinary conjugation is trivial and the field norm is constant on twisted conjugacy classes. A scaled norm pullback with base support in the norm range consequently satisfies the concordance definition of Henniart–Lemaire I.2.5, printed pp. 17–18.
