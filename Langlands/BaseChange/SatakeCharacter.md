# Existence and separation of Satake characters

`Langlands.BaseChange.satakeCharacter` proves
`Langlands.BaseChange.SatakeCharacterStatement.{u}`. For every prime `p`,
`F : PadicField.{u} p`, and `r : ℕ`, every parameter of `GL_{r+1}(F)` has a
Satake character, and these characters separate `SphericalHeckeAlgebra F r`.
The proof uses the canonical field and group instances of the standard Hecke
elements.

## Existence

For `s : Eigenvalues (r + 1)`, `Eigenvalues.ofTuple_surjective` chooses a tuple
`z : Fin (r + 1) → ℂˣ` representing `s`. The theorem
`SphericalExistence.sphericalConstituentExistence` gives a spherical constituent
of the unramified principal series for `z`.
`sphericalConstituentHeckeScalar` identifies its spherical Hecke spectrum with
`s`. The theorem `exists_isSatakeCharacter_of_hasSphericalHeckeSpectrum` then
gives the complex algebra homomorphism `ω` with `IsSatakeCharacter F r s ω`.

## Realizing prescribed scalars

Write `n = r + 1`, and let `c₁, …, cₙ` be complex numbers with `cₙ ≠ 0`.
The private lemma `exists_standardHeckeScalar_succ` extends the tuple by
`c₀ = 1` and sets `Q` to the square root of the residue cardinality, viewed in
`ℂ`. Put `aᵢ = Q^(i*(n-i)) * (-1)^i`. Each `aᵢ` is nonzero because the residue
cardinality is greater than one. The lemma constructs the complex polynomial

`P(X) = ∑_{i=0}^n (cᵢ / aᵢ) X^i`.

Its value at zero is one. The sum bounds its degree by `n`, and the coefficient
at `n` is nonzero, so its natural degree is `n`. The published
`Eigenvalues.exists_eulerPolynomial` realizes `P` as `eulerPolynomial s` for a
multiset of `n` complex units. By the definitions of `elementaryCoefficient`
and `standardHeckeScalar`, the scalar at `i` is `aᵢ` times the coefficient of
`X^i` in `P`, which equals `cᵢ`. The lemma records the values at the positive
indices, written as `i.succ` in Lean.

## Separation

Suppose every Satake character vanishes on `f`. Write `Tᵢ` for the standard Hecke
element at index `i`. The proof equips the existing algebra with a local
`CommRing` instance using `sphericalHeckeAlgebra_mul_comm`.
`sphericalHeckeGeneration` gives a power `Tₙ^m` for which `Tₙ^m * f` belongs
to the subalgebra generated by all the `Tᵢ`. Since `T₀ = 1`, this element
belongs to the subalgebra generated by the positive indices. The identity
`Algebra.adjoin_range_eq_range_aeval` expresses it as `R(T₁, …, Tₙ)` for a
complex multivariate polynomial `R`.

For any tuple of nonzero complex numbers `c₁, …, cₙ`, the private lemma
provides a parameter with these scalars. Apply its character from the
existence proof to `R(T₁, …, Tₙ) = Tₙ^m * f`. Compatibility of algebra
homomorphisms with polynomial evaluation gives `R(c₁, …, cₙ) = 0`.
Each coordinate set `ℂ \ {0}` is infinite, so `MvPolynomial.funext_set` gives
`R = 0`. It follows that `Tₙ^m * f = 0`. The power is a unit by
`standardHeckeElement_last_isUnit`, so multiplication by it is injective and
`f = 0`.

## Provenance

The target is the character formulation of the Satake parametrization in
Henniart–Lemaire, *Changement de base et induction automorphe pour GL_n en
caractéristique non nulle* (2011), §I.3.1, printed pp. 24–25
(PDF pp. 32–33, source ID
`provided-a0a5445aba86b871bcd83f08813de3234fddb463ff9bb3c9dfd60d2d2fcdae61`).
Gross, *On the Satake isomorphism* (1998), Proposition 3.6 and formula (3.14)
(PDF pp. 6 and 8, source ID `gross-1998-satake-isomorphism`), gives the
isomorphism and the standard normalization. The Lean proof derives existence
and separation from the Library's principal-series, Euler polynomial,
generation, commutativity, and unit theorems.
