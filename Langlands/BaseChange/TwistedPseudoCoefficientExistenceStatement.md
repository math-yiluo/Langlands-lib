# Existence of twisted pseudo-coefficients of σ-discrete series representations of `GL_n(E)`

This module states, as a proposition, that for a cyclic field extension
`E/F` of nonarchimedean local fields of characteristic zero, every σ-discrete
series representation of `GL_n(E)`, together with any isomorphism `A` onto
its σ-twist, has a twisted pseudo-coefficient with its central character. It
is not proved here. The predicates `IsSigmaDiscreteSeries`,
`HasCentralCharacterRep` and `IsTwistedPseudoCoefficient` are defined in
`Langlands.BaseChange.PseudoCoefficient`; `IsCyclicAlgebra` and `entrywiseAut`
are from `Langlands.BaseChange.Norm`; `SigmaIntertwiner` and the topology
lemmas for `GL_n(E)` are from `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedCharacter`.

## Statement

`TwistedPseudoCoefficientExistenceStatement.{u, v, uW} : Prop` says the
following.

Let `F : Type u` be a nonarchimedean local field
(`[Field F] [ValuativeRel F] [TopologicalSpace F] [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F]`)
of characteristic zero (`[CharZero F]`). Let `E : Type v` be a field that is
an `F`-algebra, with a topology making it a topological ring that is its
module topology over `F` (`[IsModuleTopology F E]`). Let `σ : E ≃ₐ[F] E` and
`l n : ℕ` with `IsCyclicAlgebra σ l`: `E` is finite over `F` of dimension
`l`, reduced, `σ` has order `l`, and the elements of `E` fixed by `σ` are
those of `F`. As `E` is a field, `E/F` is a cyclic Galois extension of degree
`l` whose Galois group is generated by `σ`. Give `GL_n(E)` the topology
induced from matrices over `E`, with the facts that it is Hausdorff, locally
compact and totally disconnected supplied by the theorems of `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedCharacter`
(`generalLinear_t2Space_of_finite`, `generalLinear_locallyCompactSpace_of_finite`,
`generalLinear_totallyDisconnectedSpace_of_finite`, using the finiteness of
`E` over `F` from `IsCyclicAlgebra`), and its Borel σ-algebra, introduced
locally as in the Library's `HasCharacterValue`. Then for

- every complex vector space `W : Type uW` and every representation
  `Π : Representation ℂ (GL_n(E)) W` that is admissible (`hΠ : IsAdmissible Π`)
  and a σ-discrete series representation (`IsSigmaDiscreteSeries σ n Π`:
  irreducible, tempered, σ-stable, and not isomorphic to the induction of a
  smooth σ-stable representation from a proper standard parabolic subgroup);
- every character `χ : Subgroup.center (GL_n(E)) →* ℂˣ` of the centre with
  `HasCentralCharacterRep Π χ` (that is, `χ = ω_Π`);
- every isomorphism `A : SigmaIntertwiner σ n Π` of `Π` onto
  `Π^σ = Π ∘ entrywiseAut σ n`;
- every Haar measure `μ` on `GL_n(E)`,

there is `φ : SchwartzBruhat (GL_n(E))`, a locally constant compactly
supported function, with
`IsTwistedPseudoCoefficient μ (entrywiseAut σ n) χ Π hΠ A φ`:
`Θ^A_Π(φ) = 1`, and `Θ^{A'}_{Π'}(φ) = 0` for every admissible, irreducible,
tempered representation `Π'` of `GL_n(E)` on a complex vector space in the
universe `uW`, with central character `χ`, that is not isomorphic to `Π`, and
every isomorphism `A'` of `Π'` onto `Π'^σ`. Here `Θ^A_Π` is the twisted
character `twistedDistribution μ Π hΠ A`, `f ↦ trace(Π(f) ∘ A)`, of
`Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedCharacter.Basic`, applied to the underlying
linear map of `A`.
In the Lean source the representation is named `τ`, since `Π` is reserved.

## Sources

- Henniart–Lemaire, *Changement de base et induction automorphe pour `GL_n`
  en caractéristique non nulle* (2011), II.5.13, Proposition, printed p. 113
  (PDF page 121): for a strongly irreducible σ-representation `(Π, A)` of
  `G(E)` such that `Π` is a σ-discrete series, pseudo-coefficients for
  `(Π, A)` exist. The Définition just before it (same page) is
  `IsTwistedPseudoCoefficient`; see the Markdown of
  `Langlands.BaseChange.PseudoCoefficient`. The proof (pp. 113–114) applies
  Rogawski's twisted Paley–Wiener theorem (HL II.5.7, printed p. 105) to
  obtain `φ̃ ∈ C^∞_c(G(E))` and takes `φ = φ̃_χ`. Throughout §II.5, HL
  assume that the cyclic algebra `E` is a field (II.5.1, printed p. 100).
  HL II.2.6–II.2.7, printed pp. 66–67 (PDF pages 74–75), define the
  average `φ ↦ φ_χ` over the centre and relate the twisted traces of `φ`
  and `φ_χ`; see "Test functions" below.
- Arthur–Clozel (1989), Chapter 1, Corollary 2.10, printed p. 18 (PDF
  page 32): for a σ-discrete `Π` with central character `χ` there is `φ`,
  compactly supported modulo the centre with `φ(zg) = χ(z)^{-1} φ(g)`, with
  `trace(Π(φ) I_σ) = 1` and `trace(T(φ) I_σ) = 0` for every tempered
  σ-stable `T ≇ Π` with central character `χ`. AC's setting (Chapter 1,
  §2.1, printed p. 10) is a cyclic extension `E/F` of nonarchimedean local
  fields of characteristic zero.

## Comparison with the sources

**Arbitrary `A`.** HL state the existence for every σ-representation
`(Π, A)`; AC for their normalized operator `I_σ`. For irreducible admissible
`Π`, Schur's lemma (not proved here) gives `A = c I_σ` with `c ≠ 0`, so a
pseudo-coefficient `φ` for `I_σ` gives the pseudo-coefficient `c^{-1} φ` for
`A`, and the two forms agree. HL also ask that `A^d` be a scalar; this holds
automatically for irreducible `Π` by the same lemma and is not imposed.

**Test functions.** HL and AC take the pseudo-coefficient in
`C^∞_c(G(E), χ)` and compute its twisted traces by integration over
`Z(E) \ G(E)`; here it is a compactly supported `φ`, tested only against
representations with central character `χ`, and twisted traces are
computed with the Haar measure `μ` on `G(E)` itself. HL's proof produces
exactly an average `φ̃_χ` of a compactly supported `φ̃` over the centre
against `χ` (printed p. 114), formed with the averaging map of II.2.6,
which is defined by a Haar measure `dz` on `Z(E)`. For
representations with central character `χ`, the twisted trace of `φ̃` for
`μ` equals the twisted trace of `φ̃_χ` for the quotient measure `μ / dz`,
formed with the same `dz` (HL II.2.6–II.2.7, printed pp. 66–67); for an
incompatible quotient measure the two traces differ by a constant factor.
See the Markdown of `Langlands.BaseChange.PseudoCoefficient` (remark "Test
functions"). This comparison is not formalized.

**σ-discrete series.** `IsSigmaDiscreteSeries` follows HL's precise
definition II.4.6 (printed p. 88) and AC Chapter 1, §2.3. It uses HL's
proper standard block parabolic subgroups `P_α(E)`, `l(α) > 1` (II.3.1,
printed p. 78), with unnormalized induction of every smooth σ-stable
representation of `G_α(E)`. See the Markdown of
`Langlands.BaseChange.PseudoCoefficient` (item 7 and remark "Induction").
It explains why this excludes the same representations as HL's normalized
`ι_α` of smooth irreducible σ-stable representations. It also explains how
it reads the informal "proper σ-stable Levi subgroup" of HL II.1.7. The admissibility
hypothesis `hΠ` is also part of `IsSigmaDiscreteSeries`; it is given
separately because `IsTwistedPseudoCoefficient` takes it as an argument.

**The split case is not covered.** `E` is a field (`[Field E]`). The split
case `E = F^l`, with `σ` permuting the factors cyclically, is a separate case
in HL (§II.5 assumes that `E` is a field, II.5.1), and the programme treats it
in a later statement task.

**Haar measure and universe.** Every Haar measure on `GL_n(E)` (Mathlib's
`Measure.IsHaarMeasure` for the Borel σ-algebra) is quantified. `GL_n(E)` is
locally compact and second countable, so every Haar measure on it is a
positive multiple `cμ₀` of a fixed one (Mathlib's
`Measure.isMulLeftInvariant_eq_smul` and
`Measure.haarScalarFactor_pos_of_isHaarMeasure`). Replacing `μ` by `cμ`
multiplies every twisted character value by `c`, so quantifying over all
Haar measures is equivalent to using a single fixed one. This remark is not
formalized. The test family ranges over representations on spaces in the
universe `uW` of `W`.

**`CharZero F`.** Kazhdan (1986) and Arthur–Clozel work in characteristic
zero. HL's argument does not depend on the characteristic, but the other
base-change statements of this programme assume `CharZero F`: the transfer
statement `Langlands.BaseChange.RegularTransferStatement` and the twisted
character statements `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedCharacterRegularValuesStatement`
and `Langlands.BaseChange.SigmaLiftCharacterIdentityStatement`. This statement follows them.
