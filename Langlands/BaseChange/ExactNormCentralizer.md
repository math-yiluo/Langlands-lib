# The twisted centralizer at an exact regular norm

`normCentralizerEquiv` identifies the ordinary and twisted centralizers by
coefficient extension followed by conjugation by an association witness.
At an exact norm equality, the identity is an association witness. The
proof evaluates the published equivalence there and uses its surjectivity
to identify the twisted centralizer with the subgroup image of the
ordinary centralizer under `baseChange`. This is the exact-norm specialization
of the regular centralizer identification in Henniart–Lemaire (2011),
I.2.3, printed p. 14.

For the p-adic specialization, the scalar map between compatible p-adic
extensions is isometric for the extended base norm. Multiplication then
makes the larger field a topological vector space over the smaller field.
Completeness of the smaller field and finite dimension identify its module
topology with the larger field's canonical topology.
