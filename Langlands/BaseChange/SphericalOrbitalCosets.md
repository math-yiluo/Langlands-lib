# Spherical double cosets at a norm pair

Let `E/F` be an extension of p-adic fields in the compatible scalar tower
over `ℚ_p`, with `IsCyclicAlgebra σ l` specifying a cyclic Galois group of order
`l` generated by `σ`. Let `s` conjugate the cyclic norm of `δ` to the scalar
extension of a regular semisimple `γ`. The map on representatives sends `y`
to the product of `s` and the scalar extension of `y`. Conjugated scalar
extension identifies the ordinary centralizer with the twisted centralizer.
Scalar extension preserves and reflects membership in the spherical subgroups.
These facts give an injective map on double cosets, with an explicit formula on
representatives. The canonical relative module topology used for the centralizer
equivalence follows by the minimality of the module topologies in the scalar
tower.

The equivalence `sphericalOrbitalCosetsEquiv` assumes that `E/F` is unramified,
expressed by equality of the canonical absolute ramification indices of `E`
and `F`. It also assumes that the twisted conjugate `u` of `δ` by `s` has
cyclic norm equal to the scalar extension of `γ` and lies in the spherical
subgroup after every conjugation that makes its norm spherical. The map takes
each contributing ordinary orbit to a contributing twisted orbit.

For surjectivity, write a contributing extension representative as `s * z`.
Taking the cyclic norm of its spherical twisted conjugate shows that the scalar
extension of `γ` conjugated by `z` is spherical. The conjugate of `u` by `z` is
therefore spherical as well. Removing it from the twisted conjugate gives the
right-coset fixedness test `z⁻¹ σ(z) ∈ K_E`. Unramified spherical coset descent
uses the equality of ramification indices to produce a base matrix extending to
`z * k` with `k ∈ K_E`. Its conjugate of `γ` is spherical by reflection, and it
represents the required ordinary orbit. This comparison is derived for the unit
fundamental lemma.

The relative topology uses the [module-topology theorem for a tower of p-adic fields](../LocalField/PadicTowerModuleTopology.md).
