# Transport of the distinguished standard epsilon comparison

The theorem starts with a comparison of the finite standard epsilon factors
of a family of Hecke twists and one upper cuspidal presentation. The two
completion isomorphisms identify the corresponding local representations up
to the specified unramified determinant twists. The Hecke restrictions are
indexed by a supplied bijection with a finite set of local characters.

On the lower side, pullback commutes with the Hecke determinant twist, which
commutes in turn with the unramified determinant twist. The local factor
identification then preserves the epsilon exponent of each twist. The same
identification applies to the upper factor. The isomorphisms of local fields
preserve the residue cardinalities. Reindexing along the given bijection gives
the product comparison for any choices of the actual local standard epsilon
certificates at the pulled-back standard additive characters and self-dual
measures. The constants of the individual factors enter only through one
constant multiplier.

This is the local identification in the globalization proof of Arthur–Clozel,
Chapter 1, Proposition 6.9(ii), printed pp. 69–70 (PDF pp. 83–84).
