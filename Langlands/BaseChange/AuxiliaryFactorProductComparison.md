# Finite Euler values and positive epsilon bases at auxiliary places

`AuxiliaryFactorProductComparisonStatement` converts the local polynomial substitution and epsilon exponent sum into the equality of finite Euler values and positive epsilon bases used in the global comparison. The global twists are supplied with their restrictions to the local twists. Each local twist occurs with multiplicity equal to the number of places above the base place. The upper factors have a common polynomial and exponent.

Grouping the global product by its restriction fibers gives the corresponding power of the local product. The residue cardinality identity changes the upper spectral variable by the residue-degree substitution and rescales the upper epsilon exponent. This is the factor regrouping used at auxiliary places in Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, Lemma 6.13. The representation and character restriction hypotheses are explicit.
