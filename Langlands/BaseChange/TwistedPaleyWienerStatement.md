# The twisted trace Paley–Wiener theorem for `GL_n(E)`

The proposition `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedTracePaleyWienerStatement.{u, v, z}`
states the direction (A) ⇒ (B) of Henniart–Lemaire II.5.7 for a cyclic
field extension `E/F` in characteristic zero, with compact domination
(i)' from their Remarque (1). Henniart–Lemaire use this direction in their
construction of twisted pseudo-coefficients in II.5.13, the result stated
by `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedPseudoCoefficientExistenceStatement`.

## Statement

Let `F : Type u` be a nonarchimedean local field of characteristic zero.
Let `E : Type v` be a field and a topological `F`-algebra with the module
topology over `F`. Let `σ : E ≃ₐ[F] E` and `l n : ℕ` satisfy
`IsCyclicAlgebra σ l`. Thus `E` is finite and reduced over `F` of dimension
`l`, `σ` has order `l`, and the fixed elements of `σ` are the image of `F`.
In this field setting `E/F` is cyclic Galois of degree `l`, generated by `σ`.
Give `GL_n(E)` its Borel σ-algebra. The topology instances come from
`generalLinear_t2Space_of_finite`, `generalLinear_locallyCompactSpace_of_finite`
and `generalLinear_totallyDisconnectedSpace_of_finite`, as in
`TwistedPseudoCoefficientExistenceStatement`.

For every Haar measure `μ` on `GL_n(E)` and every linear functional
`Φ : Module.Dual ℂ (Distribution (GL_n(E)))`, the proposition assumes both
conditions below.

- (i)' `IsCompactlyDominatedFunctional (entrywiseAut σ n) μ Φ` requires a
  compact open subgroup `J` such that `Φ(Θ^A_π) = 0` for every admissible
  irreducible `π` on a complex vector space in universe `z` with no nonzero
  `J`-fixed vector and every `A : π ≅ π^σ`.
- (ii) `IsSigmaInducedRegularFunctional σ μ Φ` requires that for every
  block shape `B` of `n`, every admissible irreducible representation `Ξ`
  of the Levi product `∏ᵢ GL_{nᵢ}(E)` on a complex vector space in universe
  `z` and every `BΞ : Ξ ≅ Ξ^σ`, there are a finite set `s` of elements of
  the Levi product and coefficients `c` with
  `Φ(Θ^{ι(BΞ_ψ)}_{Ind(Ξ ⊗ ψ)}) = ∑_{m ∈ s} c(m) ψ(m)` for every σ-invariant
  unramified character `ψ` of the Levi product and every proof of
  admissibility of `Ind(Ξ ⊗ ψ)`. Here `Ind` is the unnormalized
  `Induction.smoothInduced` from the standard upper block parabolic via its
  Levi projection. `BΞ_ψ` is `twistTwistedIntertwiner` with underlying map
  `BΞ`, and `ι(BΞ_ψ)` is `TwistedPaleyWiener.inducedSigmaIntertwiner`
  applied to the twisted pair. The block shapes include the one-block
  parabolic `GL_n(E)` when `n > 0`.

The conclusion `IsTwistedTraceFunctional (entrywiseAut σ n) μ Φ` gives
one `φ : SchwartzBruhat (GL_n(E))`, a locally constant compactly supported
function, with `Θ^A_π(φ) = Φ(Θ^A_π)` for every admissible irreducible `π`
on a complex vector space in the same universe `z` and every `A : π ≅ π^σ`.
Here `π^σ = π ∘ entrywiseAut σ n`, and
`Θ^A_π = twistedDistribution μ π hπ A.toLinearMap` sends `f` to
`trace(π(f) ∘ A)` with integrated action taken using `μ`. The three
predicates are defined in `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedPaleyWiener`.

## Comparison with the sources

For `n > 0` and `σ ≠ 1`, the restriction of scalars of `GL_n` from `E` to
`F` has `F`-points `GL_n(E)`. The entrywise action fixes its standard
minimal parabolic and is outer because it acts nontrivially on the center.
This is the setting of Rogawski's Proposition 11.2.

An irreducible smooth representation of `GL_n(E)` or of a Levi product
is admissible by the admissibility theorem for reductive p-adic groups.
For such a representation `Π` and an isomorphism `A : Π ≅ Π^σ`, the power
`A^l` is equivariant. It preserves a nonzero finite-dimensional space of
compact open fixed vectors and has an eigenvector there. Irreducibility
forces the eigenspace to be the entire representation, so `A^l` is a
nonzero scalar. This gives the strongly irreducible pairs of
Henniart–Lemaire II.5.2. Rogawski's normalization `A^l = 1` is obtained by
rescaling `A`, and the values on twisted characters are homogeneous in `A`.

The functional `Φ` induces the form `Φ'(Π, A) = Φ(Θ^A_Π)` on the free
abelian group on isomorphism classes of strongly irreducible pairs.
`twistedDistribution_eq_of_equivariant` gives invariance under isomorphism
of pairs and `twistedDistribution_smul` gives homogeneity. Compact
domination for `Φ'` is condition (i)' of Remarque (1). The irreducibles
with `J`-fixed vectors meet only finitely many Bernstein components, as
used in that remark. The conclusion is (B) for `Φ'`.

On an induced class, `Φ'` agrees with `Φ` applied to the distribution of
the whole induced representation. Parabolic induction preserves finite
length by II.5.5. The `l`th power of the induced intertwiner is scalar
because iterating its formula applies `BΞ^l` to the section values and
`σ^l = 1`. The finite-rank trace adds along a composition series of
σ-pairs because both the integrated action and the intertwiner preserve
each subspace of the series. By the lemma of II.5.4, an irreducible
σ-pair with reducible underlying representation is a direct sum of
summands cycled by its intertwiner. Its twisted trace vanishes because the
integrated action preserves the summands and the cyclic shift has zero
diagonal. Homogeneity also makes the trace vanish on the root-of-unity
sums in the quotient of II.5.4. These relations identify the values of
`Φ'` on induced classes in (ii) with the values computed from distributions.
The Lean distribution takes the admissibility proof as an argument and
its value is independent of the proof. Admissibility of the induction
follows from admissibility on the Levi and compactness of `P \ G`. For each
compact open `J`, a `J`-fixed section is determined by its values at
finitely many double cosets in `P \ G / J`, in finite-dimensional fixed
spaces on the Levi.

For the Levi product with `r` blocks, the unramified characters have the
form `ψ(m) = ∏ᵢ zᵢ^{ord_E(det mᵢ)}` with `z ∈ (ℂˣ)^r`. This agrees with
triviality on compact subgroups in `IsUnramifiedCharacter`. Compact
subgroups have zero block determinant valuations, and the common kernel
of those valuations is generated by the integral general linear groups
and elementary unipotents. Each unipotent generator lies in a compact
subgroup. The valuation is invariant under `σ`, so the full torus consists
of σ-invariant characters in this setting. A regular function on the torus
is a Laurent polynomial. Each monomial is `ψ ↦ ψ(m)` for some `m`, since
powers of a uniformizer in a single diagonal entry of each block realize
any specified tuple of determinant valuations. Thus the finite evaluation
sum in (ii) expresses regularity on this torus.

The sources use normalized induction. For the parabolic `P` of `B`, the
normalized induction of `Ξ ⊗ ψ` is unnormalized smooth induction of
`Ξ ⊗ (ψ δ_P^{1/2})`, where `δ_P` is the parabolic modulus on the Levi.
Its half-power is unramified and σ-invariant. Translation by that point
of the torus preserves Laurent polynomials. The induced intertwiner has
the formula `f ↦ (g ↦ BΞ(f(σ⁻¹g)))` in both conventions, because twisting
by `ψ` and by `δ_P^{1/2}` leaves the underlying map `BΞ` unchanged. This
gives the regularity condition (ii) of II.5.7 for every strongly irreducible
inducing pair.

Scaling `μ` by a positive constant scales both sides of the trace
identity and the Laurent coefficients by that constant. The universe
`z` bounds the carriers of the irreducibles in compact domination and
in the conclusion, and the carriers of the inducing irreducibles in
regularity. The induced model uses functions from `GL_n(E)` to the
inducing carrier. The irreducibles have countable dimension because they
are spanned by the orbit of a nonzero vector fixed by an open subgroup
of a second-countable group, so their carriers can be realized in any of
the quantified carrier universes.

For `E = F`, `σ = 1` and `l = 1`, the cyclicity hypothesis is supplied by
`isCyclicAlgebra_one`. By Schur's lemma every intertwiner is a scalar
multiple of the identity, so the distribution formulation specializes to
the good-to-trace direction of Bernstein–Deligne–Kazhdan for `GL_n(F)`.
The preamble also allows `n = 0`, where the group is trivial and the
finite-dimensional double dual of its test function space is identified
with that space by evaluation.
