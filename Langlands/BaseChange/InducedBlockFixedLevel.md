# Fixed-vector detection on the factors of a normalized GL product

For a standard block shape and an open subgroup \(K\) of \(\mathrm{GL}_n(E)\), the theorem
`inducedBlockFixedLevel` chooses a compact open subgroup in each block group. For every family
of complex block representations, a nonzero \(K\)-fixed vector in the actual normalized product
forces a nonzero fixed vector in each factor at its chosen level. The field is a nonarchimedean
local field, and the factor carriers have one common, independent universe.

The finite double-coset model for smooth induction gives a common detecting level on the Levi
product. The proof replaces that level by the product of compact open coordinate subgroups
inside it and the determinant norm-one subgroup, on which the half modulus is trivial.
A vector fixed by this product lies in the tensor product of the fixed spaces of the factors.
If any factor has zero fixed space, the corresponding coordinate map sends every pure tensor
to zero and the tensor product of fixed-space inclusions vanishes. This forces every
ambient \(K\)-fixed section to vanish.

This is a fixed-level comparison for the inducing factors used in Rogawski (1988), §10,
in particular the use of square-integrable inducing data in the proof of Proposition 10.1
on printed p. 228. It provides a uniform level for those factors in normalized GL induction.
