# Rank-one sigma-lifts over characteristic-zero local fields

`CharZeroRankOneSigmaLiftNormPullbackStatement` assumes a cyclic extension of
characteristic-zero nonarchimedean local fields with its relative module
topology and a rank-one distributional sigma-lift between two admissible
representations equivalent to supplied quasicharacter models. It identifies
the upper character with the pullback of the base character by the field
norm. It keeps the two supplied field and representation carriers and
their independent universes. The theorem `cuspidalSigmaLiftConductorRankOne`
proves the original first-fixed-level identity in this rank under the
hypotheses of `CuspidalSigmaLiftConductorRankOneStatement`.

The characteristic-zero field constructor supplies compatible finite p-adic data on the original base carrier. The given relative module topology supplies the compatible upper p-adic data. The earlier rank-one concordance and conductor results then apply directly.

The rank-one norm description is Henniart–Lemaire II.2.9, Remark (3), printed p. 70. The ensuing conductor identity is the exponent form of Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, (6.7), printed p. 60.
