# Standard factors of the actual spherical finite components

At a finite place outside the exceptional set of a cuspidal spectral presentation,
`SphericalStandardFactorsAtCompletionStatement` fixes one tuple in increasing order of absolute value that realizes its supplied
Satake multiset. For any Hecke character, the actual finite component of the twisted
presentation has standard L-polynomial equal to the product of the Tate polynomials
for that tuple twisted by the local Hecke component. Its epsilon exponent is the
sum of the corresponding Tate exponents. The statement supplies the original
presentation with irreducible admissible finite components and takes a nontrivial
local additive character, a self-dual measure, and the local pair-factor certificates.
The second component is supplied by an actual rank-one presentation whose local action
is equivalent to the trivial representation. The support lemmas give this equivalence
and the required local properties for the constant rank-one presentation.

The Library equivalence for the embedded constant component supplies the trivial
one-dimensional representation on its actual carrier. The spherical Hecke equations on
the original component supply an ordered unramified quotient. The actual twist
of the presentation has local action equivalent to the determinant twist on this
component. The proof transports the local pair-factor certificates across these
representations and applies the standard factor formulas for the twisted quotient.
These are the formulas used in Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, Lemma 6.13.
