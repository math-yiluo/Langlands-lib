# Descent of σ-discrete series

This module `Langlands.BaseChange.SigmaDiscreteDescentStatement` (namespace
`Langlands.BaseChange`) states, as the proposition `SigmaDiscreteDescentStatement`, that every
σ-discrete series of `GL_n(E)` is the σ-lift of an irreducible square-integrable
representation of `GL_n(F)`. The statement is **not** proved here.

## Setting

`GL_n(R)` is the Library's `GeneralLinear n R`. The statement quantifies over:

- a nonarchimedean local field `F : Type u` of characteristic zero (`[Field F]
  [ValuativeRel F] [TopologicalSpace F] [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F] [CharZero F]`);
- a field `E : Type v` that is an `F`-algebra with a topology making it a topological ring
  that is the `F`-module topology (`[Field E] [Algebra F E] [TopologicalSpace E]
  [IsTopologicalRing E] [IsModuleTopology F E]`);
- `σ : E ≃ₐ[F] E` and `l n : ℕ` with `hσ : IsCyclicAlgebra σ l` (module
  `Langlands.BaseChange.Norm`). For a field `E` this says that `E/F` is a cyclic extension of
  degree `l` with Galois group generated by `σ`.

The first conjunct `hσ.1` of `IsCyclicAlgebra` gives `Module.Finite F E`. With it, the
topologies of `GL_n(F)` and `GL_n(E)` are made Hausdorff, locally compact and totally
disconnected inside the statement (`haveI`) by the Library's
`LocalFieldTopology.generalLinear_t2Space` and companions and by
`generalLinear_t2Space_of_finite` and companions of `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedCharacter`.
These are the instance arguments of `IsSigmaLift`.

## Statement

`SigmaDiscreteDescentStatement.{u, v, uV, uW}`: for every complex vector space `W : Type uW`,
every representation `ρ : Representation ℂ (GL_n(E)) W` with `hρ : IsAdmissible ρ` and
`IsSigmaDiscreteSeries σ n ρ`, and every `A : SigmaIntertwiner σ n ρ`, there exist a complex
vector space `V : Type uV`, a representation `π : Representation ℂ (GL_n(F)) V` with
`hπ : IsAdmissible π`, and `c : ℂ` such that `π.IsIrreducible` (Mathlib),
`JacquetLanglands.IsSquareIntegrable π` and `IsSigmaLift σ l π hπ ρ hρ A c`.

`IsSigmaDiscreteSeries σ n ρ` already contains `IsAdmissible ρ`; the separate proof `hρ` is
needed because `IsSigmaLift` takes it as an argument. The universe `uV` of the carrier of `π`
is arbitrary. This is true because an irreducible admissible representation of `GL_n` over a
nonarchimedean local field has countable dimension, so it has a copy in every universe; this
remark is not formalized.

**Names.** HL write `Π` for representations of `GL_n(E)`. The letters `Π` and `Σ` are reserved
tokens in Lean, so the Lean statement writes `ρ` for HL's `Π`, and `π` as in HL.

## Explanations

- **σ-lifts.** `IsSigmaLift σ l π hπ ρ hρ A c` (module
  `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedCharacter`, Task 7432) is HL's σ-relèvement (II.2.7,
  printed p. 67): `c ≠ 0`, and for all Haar measures on `GL_n(F)` and `GL_n(E)` and every
  pair `(φ, f)` of concordant test functions (`AreConcordant` of
  `Langlands.BaseChange.OrbitalIntegral`, Task 7416), the twisted trace
  `trace(ρ(φ) ∘ A)` equals `c` times the trace `trace(π(f))`. Task 7432's
  `SigmaLiftCharacterIdentityStatement` (HL II.2.9, Proposition) states its equivalence with
  the Shintani identity `IsShintaniLift` of AC Definition 6.1.
- **The constant, and every `A`.** HL show that `c = 1` when `A` is the Whittaker-normalized
  intertwiner `I_σ(Π)` (II.4.6, Théorème (1)). That normalization is not defined in the
  programme, so the statement asserts only that some constant works; it is nonzero by the
  definition of `IsSigmaLift`. Replacing `A` by a nonzero multiple `a • A` replaces `c` by
  `a * c` (`twistedDistribution_smul` of
  `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedCharacter.Basic`). Since `ρ` is irreducible and admissible,
  Schur's lemma (not proved here) makes every intertwiner a nonzero multiple of `I_σ(Π)`, so
  the statement may quantify over every `A`.
- **Discrete series.** HL's "série discrète" is an irreducible square-integrable
  representation with unitary central character, which is
  `JacquetLanglands.IsSquareIntegrable` (Library,
  `Langlands/JacquetLanglands/EssentiallySquareIntegrable.lean`: admissible, unitary central
  character, matrix coefficients square-integrable modulo the centre).
- **σ-discrete series.** `IsSigmaDiscreteSeries σ n ρ` (module
  `Langlands.BaseChange.PseudoCoefficient`, Task 7442) is AC's σ-discrete
  representation (Chapter 1, §2.3), which is HL's "série σ-discrète" (II.1.7, II.4.6):
  irreducible, admissible, tempered, σ-stable and not induced from a σ-stable representation
  of a proper standard Levi subgroup.
- **Field.** HL's Theorem II.4.6 and its proof by the global method (II.6.1, printed p. 118)
  assume that `E` is a field, and so does this statement. In characteristic zero, AC prove
  Theorem 6.2 (§6.3) and HL refer to that proof (II.6.1). The degree `l` is not assumed prime.
- **`CharZero F`.** As in Tasks 7416, 7432 and 7442: the Library's `IsRegularSemisimple` asks
  for a separable characteristic polynomial, while HL's regular elements have squarefree
  characteristic polynomial; the two agree in characteristic zero, where HL's results and
  AC's apply verbatim.
- **Proof route.** The statement is proved by comparing the simple trace formula with its
  twisted form (HL II.6; AC §6.3). It uses twisted pseudo-coefficients of σ-discrete series
  (`IsTwistedPseudoCoefficient` of `Langlands.BaseChange.PseudoCoefficient`, whose existence is
  stated as `TwistedPseudoCoefficientExistenceStatement`). A later proof task must not derive
  this statement from a result that depends on it.
