# Twisted pseudo-coefficients killing properly induced twisted characters

The module `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedProperInducedPseudoCoefficient` proves that the twisted
trace Paley–Wiener theorem and the spectral splitting of the twisted-character space give, for
every σ-discrete series `τ` of `GL_n(E)` with `0 < n`, a twisted pseudo-coefficient of `(τ, A)`
whose twisted traces vanish on the irreducible representations properly induced from σ-stable
irreducible admissible representations of standard Levi subgroups. These are clauses (a) and (b)
of `TwistedCuspidalPseudoCoefficientExistenceStatement` (module
`Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedCuspidalPseudoCoefficientExistenceStatement`).

Throughout, `F` is a nonarchimedean local field of characteristic zero, `E` a field that is a
cyclic `F`-algebra of group `⟨σ⟩` of order `l` (`IsCyclicAlgebra σ l`) with its module topology,
and `GL_n(E)` carries its Borel σ-algebra. The three universe parameters `u`, `v`, `uW` are those
of `F`, `E` and the carriers of the representations; they are the same in premises and
conclusions.

## Statements

- `TwistedProperInducedFunctionalExistenceStatement.{u, v, uW}`. For `2 ≤ n`, a σ-discrete
  series `τ` (admissible, carrier in `Type uW`) with central character `χ`
  (`HasCentralCharacterRep τ χ`), an isomorphism `A : τ ≅ τ^σ` (`SigmaIntertwiner σ n τ`) and a
  Haar measure `μ`, there is a linear functional `Φ` on the distributions on `GL_n(E)` that
  1. is compactly dominated (`IsCompactlyDominatedFunctional`) and regular on all induced
     families (`IsSigmaInducedRegularFunctional`), the two hypotheses of
     `TwistedTracePaleyWienerStatement`;
  2. takes the value `1` at the twisted character `Θ^A_τ = twistedDistribution μ τ hτ A`;
  3. vanishes at the twisted character of every irreducible tempered representation with central
     character `χ` that is not isomorphic to `τ`, for every σ-intertwiner;
  4. vanishes on `sigmaProperInducedSpan σ μ` (module `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedInductionSpans`).
     This is the span of the twisted characters of the admissible representations `I(Ξ)`
     unnormalizedly induced from irreducible admissible σ-stable representations `Ξ` of proper
     standard Levi subgroups. Each `I(Ξ)` is taken with the induced intertwiner
     `TwistedPaleyWiener.inducedSigmaIntertwiner σ B Ξ BΞ` built from an isomorphism
     `BΞ : Ξ ≅ Ξ^σ`. The induced representation need not be irreducible.

  Items 1–3 are the conclusion of the Library's `TwistedPseudoCoefficientFunctionalExistenceStatement`
  (module `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedPseudoCoefficientFunctionalStatement`). Item 4 puts into
  one condition the two sources of vanishing on proper inductions in Henniart–Lemaire's
  construction (II.5.13):
  - The subgroup `G^L_ind(G(E), σ)` of II.5.11 (printed p. 109). It is generated by the twisted
    standard classes `(Π̃_ξ, Ã_ξ)` of the induced σ-triplets of Langlands. By II.5.11, Lemme 2,
    its image in the quotient of the twisted Grothendieck group by `G_{0+}(G(E), σ)` (II.5.4) is a
    direct summand. The functional of the proof of II.5.13 is extended by zero on that image
    ("On prolonge Φ par 0 sur G^L_ind(G(E), σ)", printed p. 113).
  - The strongly irreducible essentially tempered σ-representations that are not essentially
    σ-discrete. The functional is `0` on them by construction, since on essentially tempered
    classes it is supported on the classes `(Π_ψ, λA_ψ)` of unramified twists of `(Π, A)`.

  In the proof of II.5.13 (printed p. 114), a proper family `ι_α(Ξ_ψ, B_ψ)` induced from a
  tempered strongly irreducible `(Ξ, B)` lies in `G^L_ind(G(E), σ)` (Cas 1). Otherwise it is a
  strongly irreducible essentially tempered σ-representation that is not essentially σ-discrete
  (Cas 2). The Remarque after the proof records that `φ̃` has twisted trace `0` on both classes.
  The proof of II.5.14, Proposition 2 (1) (printed pp. 115–116), combines them for an arbitrary
  irreducible σ-stable inducing representation. By the Corollaire of II.5.10 its class is a
  combination of twisted standard classes of σ-stable Langlands triplets of the Levi, modulo
  `G_{>0}`. By transitivity of parabolic induction and II.5.12, Lemme 1, the induced class is then a
  combination of classes `[Π_i, A_i]` modulo `G_{0+}`, and each `[Π_i, A_i]` lies in one of the two
  classes. Twisted traces vanish on `G_{0+}` (II.5.4, Remarque), so the twisted trace at `φ̃` of
  the proper induction is `0`. Henniart–Lemaire state the conclusion for the central average
  `φ = φ̃_χ`.
  Item 4 records this conclusion for the whole span, including reducible admissible inductions.
  In the Lean proof, `Φ` is extended by zero on the complementary span of the premise
  `TwistedPseudoCoefficientSpectrumSplittingStatement`. That span contains
  `sigmaProperInducedSpan σ μ` by definition, so both sources of vanishing are covered at once.
  The disjointness of the orbit span from the complementary span is an assumption of that
  premise, not proved here. Its Markdown relates the premise to II.5.10, II.5.11, Lemme 2, and
  the two cases of II.5.13.
- `TwistedProperInducedPseudoCoefficientExistenceStatement.{u, v, uW}`. For `0 < n`, `τ`, `χ`,
  `A` and `μ` as above (with `0 < n` in place of `2 ≤ n`), there is `φ ∈ C^∞_c(GL_n(E))` such that
  - (a) `φ` is a twisted pseudo-coefficient of `(τ, A)` with central character `χ`
    (`IsTwistedPseudoCoefficient μ (entrywiseAut σ n) χ τ hτ A φ`);
  - (b) for every block shape `B` of `n` with at least two blocks, every irreducible admissible
    σ-stable representation `Ξ` of the Levi product `B.Levi E` (`IsLeviSigmaStable σ B Ξ`, carrier
    in `Type uW`) whose unnormalized smooth induction from the upper block parabolic subgroup
    `B.parabolic E` is irreducible and admissible, and every isomorphism `A'` of that induced
    representation onto its σ-conjugate, the twisted trace of the induced representation with
    `A'` vanishes at `φ`.

  The two clauses are copied from clauses (a) and (b) of
  `TwistedCuspidalPseudoCoefficientExistenceStatement`, with the same binders. Clause (b)
  allows every intertwiner `A'` of an irreducible induction, not only the induced intertwiner
  used in the span of item 4. The two are related by Schur's lemma: two σ-intertwiners of an
  irreducible admissible representation differ by a nonzero scalar.

## Theorems

- `twistedProperInducedFunctionalExistence_of_spectrumSplitting` proves
  `TwistedPseudoCoefficientSpectrumSplittingStatement.{u, v, uW} →
  TwistedProperInducedFunctionalExistenceStatement.{u, v, uW}`.
- `twistedProperInducedPseudoCoefficientExistence_of_spectrumSplitting` proves
  `TwistedTracePaleyWienerStatement.{u, v, uW} →
  TwistedPseudoCoefficientSpectrumSplittingStatement.{u, v, uW} →
  TwistedProperInducedPseudoCoefficientExistenceStatement.{u, v, uW}`.

The premises are Library statements: `TwistedTracePaleyWienerStatement` (module
`Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedPaleyWienerStatement`; Rogawski 1988, Proposition 11.2;
Henniart–Lemaire 2011, II.5.7) and `TwistedPseudoCoefficientSpectrumSplittingStatement` (module
`Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedPseudoCoefficientOrbitSeparation`). This module does not prove them.

## Proof of the functional statement

The proof is the one of `twistedPseudoCoefficientFunctionalExistence_of_spectrumSplitting`
(module `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedPseudoCoefficientSpectralConstruction`), which constructs the
same functional but does not record item 4. Fix `τ`, `χ`, `A`, `μ` and `θ = entrywiseAut σ n`.

1. `exists_compact_openSubgroup_symm_mem` gives small compact open subgroups stable under `θ`
   (`θ` is continuous of order dividing `l`), and the unramified characters trivial on the
   centre form a finite set (`finite_unramifiedCharacter_trivialCenter`).
   `exists_twistOrbit_test_values` (module
   `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedPseudoCoefficientInterpolation`) then gives a test function `f₀`
   with value `1` at `Θ^A_τ` and value `0` at the twisted characters of the unramified twists of
   `τ` with central character `χ` not isomorphic to `τ`.
2. The splitting statement gives a compact open subgroup `J`, the orbit span
   `L = twistedOrbitSpan μ θ τ`, and the complementary span
   `N = twistedPseudoCoefficientComplementSpan σ μ τ J`, the sum of the span of the other
   essentially tempered twisted characters, `sigmaProperInducedSpan σ μ`, and the span of the
   twisted characters without nonzero `J`-fixed vectors. `L` and `N` are disjoint and `L ⊔ N`
   contains every irreducible twisted character.
3. `exists_dual_agreeOn_of_disjoint` extends `d ↦ d(f₀)` on `L` to a functional `Φ` that kills
   `N`. In particular `Φ` kills `sigmaProperInducedSpan σ μ ≤ N` (item 4) and the span of the
   twisted characters without `J`-fixed vectors (compact domination).
4. Regularity: on a family induced from a block shape with at least two blocks, each value is a
   twisted character of a proper induction of the twisted inducing representation
   (`twistedDistribution_mem_sigmaProperInducedSpan`, with `twistTwistedIntertwiner`), so `Φ` is
   identically zero there. On one-block families,
   `exists_oneBlock_regular_of_spectrum_split` (module `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedSingleBlock`)
   gives regularity from the stability of `L` and `N` under σ-invariant unramified twists
   (`characterTwistDistribution_map_twistedOrbitSpan_le` and the three lemmas for the summands
   of `N`, module `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedPseudoCoefficientSpectrumTwists`).
5. Values: `Φ(Θ^A_τ) = Θ^A_τ(f₀) = 1`. For an irreducible tempered `τ'` with central character
   `χ`, not isomorphic to `τ`: if `τ'` is an unramified twist of `τ`, `Φ` agrees with evaluation
   at `f₀`, which is `0`; otherwise `Θ^{A'}_{τ'}` lies in the span of the other essentially
   tempered characters, contained in `N`.

## Proof of the pseudo-coefficient statement

Fix `n > 0`, `τ`, `χ`, `A` and `μ`.

- If `n ≤ 1`, `GL_n(E)` equals its centre (`generalLinear_mem_center_of_rank_le_one`) and
  `exists_isTwistedPseudoCoefficient_of_center_eq_top` (module
  `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedPseudoCoefficientSmallRank`) gives (a). A block shape of `n ≤ 1`
  has at most one block (`BlockShape.length_le`), so (b) is vacuous.
- If `n ≥ 2`, take `Φ` from the functional statement and `φ` from
  `TwistedTracePaleyWienerStatement` applied to `Φ`, so that every irreducible admissible
  representation `π` on a carrier in `Type uW` with any `A_π : π ≅ π^σ` has
  `Θ^{A_π}_π(φ) = Φ(Θ^{A_π}_π)`. Items 2 and 3 give (a).
  For (b), let `I` be the induced representation of `Ξ`. Its carrier is a type of functions on
  `GL_n(E)`, in a universe that need not be `uW`, so trace Paley–Wiener does not apply to it
  directly. Since `GL_n(E)` is second countable (`generalLinear_secondCountable_of_finiteAlgebra`),
  `exists_irreducible_smoothRep_equiv_of_separable` (module
  `Langlands.Representation.Smooth.SmallRealizationProof`) gives an irreducible representation
  `ρ` on a carrier in `Type uW` and an equivalence `e : I ≃ ρ`. Then `ρ` is admissible
  (`isAdmissible_of_equiv`), `Θ^{A'}_I = Θ^{A'_e}_ρ` for the transported intertwiner
  `A'_e = SigmaIntertwiner.transport A' e` (`twistedDistribution_eq_of_equivalent`,
  `SigmaIntertwiner.transport_toLinearMap`, module `Langlands.BaseChange.SigmaLiftTransport`), so
  `Θ^{A'}_I(φ) = Φ(Θ^{A'_e}_ρ)`. Finally `Θ^{A'_e}_ρ ∈ sigmaProperInducedSpan σ μ`
  (`twistedDistribution_mem_sigmaProperInducedSpan_of_equiv`, with the σ-stability isomorphism
  `BΞ` of `Ξ`), and item 4 gives `0`. That lemma compares `A'_e` with the transported induced
  intertwiner `inducedSigmaIntertwiner σ B Ξ BΞ`. By Schur's lemma
  (`exists_twistedDistribution_eq_smul_of_equiv`) the twisted distribution with `A'_e` is a scalar
  multiple of a generator of the span.

## Sources

- G. Henniart and B. Lemaire, *Changement de base et induction automorphe pour `GL_n` en
  caractéristique non nulle*, Mém. SMF 124 (2011):
  - II.5.11 (printed pp. 109–112): the subgroup `G^L_ind(G(E), σ)` generated by the twisted
    standard classes of the induced σ-triplets of Langlands, and Lemme 2, the direct sum
    decomposition along which the functional is extended by zero.
  - II.5.13, Définition, Proposition and its proof (printed pp. 113–114). The definition gives
    the trace values of a pseudo-coefficient: twisted trace `1` at `(Π, A)` and `0` at the
    tempered σ-representations `(Π', A')` with central character `χ` and `Π' ≇ Π`. The proof
    constructs the functional and extends it by zero on `G^L_ind(G(E), σ)`. It treats proper
    families in two cases and obtains `φ̃ ∈ C_c^∞(G(E))` from the twisted Paley–Wiener
    theorem II.5.7 (printed p. 105).
  - The Remarque after II.5.13 (printed p. 114): `φ̃` has twisted trace `0` on every element
    of `G^L_ind(G(E), σ)`, and on every strongly irreducible essentially tempered
    σ-representation `(Π', A')` such that `Π'` is not essentially σ-discrete.
  - II.5.14, Proposition 2 (1) and its proof (printed pp. 115–116). The proof decomposes the
    inducing data and combines the two classes of the Remarque, so that every proper induction
    from an irreducible σ-stable representation has twisted trace `0` at `φ̃`. It then passes to
    the central average `φ = φ̃_χ`, for inducing data whose central character extends `χ`.
- J. Arthur and L. Clozel, *Simple algebras, base change, and the advanced theory of the trace
  formula*, Annals of Mathematics Studies 120 (1989), Chapter 1:
  - Corollary 2.10 (printed p. 18): a pseudo-coefficient of a σ-discrete `Π` with central
    character `χ` has `trace(Π(φ) I_σ) = 1`, and `trace(Π'(φ) I_σ) = 0` for every tempered
    σ-stable `Π' ≇ Π` with central character `χ`. These are the trace values of clause (a)
    (`IsTwistedPseudoCoefficient`). There they are stated for the normalized intertwiners
    `I_σ`; `IsTwistedPseudoCoefficient` states them for an arbitrary intertwiner `A` of `τ`,
    and `A'` of `τ'`.
  - Lemma 2.12 and its proof (printed p. 19). The descent argument for the twisted orbital
    integrals of a pseudo-coefficient uses `trace(Π'(φ) I_σ) = 0` for every σ-stable `Π'`
    properly induced from a σ-stable representation. This induced-trace vanishing is clause (b).
- J. Rogawski (1988), Proposition 11.2, the source cited by the premise
  `TwistedTracePaleyWienerStatement`.

Henniart–Lemaire and Arthur–Clozel work with functions compactly supported modulo the centre
with a central character, the central averages of compactly supported functions. Here `φ` is
compactly supported, as in `IsTwistedPseudoCoefficient`, and plays the part of Henniart–Lemaire's
`φ̃`. In particular, clause (b) is the vanishing at `φ̃` from the proof of II.5.14,
Proposition 2 (1), before the central average is taken. It covers all irreducible σ-stable inducing data,
without a condition on their central characters.
