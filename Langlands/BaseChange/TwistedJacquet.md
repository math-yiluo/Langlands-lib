# The σ-intertwiner on a Jacquet module

This module implements the descent of the twisting operator in J. D. Rogawski, *Trace Paley–Wiener theorem in the twisted case*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 309 (1988), 215–229. The covariance convention is in the introduction (printed p. 215, PDF p. 1), and the Jacquet quotient and its operator are in §3 (printed p. 218, PDF p. 4). Source ID: `rogawski-1988-twisted-trace-paley-wiener`; SHA-256: `86fbe930fc1db80a3a0f279db5d83af85b7e37dc7e041d813ebc9f5fdcb7c274`.

The operator satisfies `A π(g) = π(θ(g)) A`. When `θ` preserves the unipotent radical `N`, `A` induces an operator on the quotient by the span of `π(n)v − v`, given by `[v] ↦ [A v]`. The construction first gives this linear isomorphism on the quotient and then the intertwiner for the **unnormalized** Levi action.

## Definitions and lemmas

- `Langlands.Jacquet.twistedCoinvariantsEquiv ρ N θ hN A hA`: let `ρ` be a representation of `G` over a commutative ring, `θ` a group automorphism preserving the subgroup `N` in both directions, and `A` a linear isomorphism satisfying `A(ρ(g)v) = ρ(θ(g))(A v)`. Then the actual `N`-coinvariants `Jacquet.Coinvariants ρ N` have a linear isomorphism sending `Jacquet.mk ρ N v` to `Jacquet.mk ρ N (A v)`, as recorded in `twistedCoinvariantsEquiv_mk`. This is the quotient operation from Rogawski §3 without the normalizing scalar or the restrictions to complex coefficients, algebraic groups and finite-order automorphisms.
- `Langlands.BaseChange.parabolicAut σ B`, for an `F`-algebra automorphism `σ` of `E` and a `BlockShape B` of `n`: the restriction of the entrywise action `entrywiseAut σ n` to the standard upper block parabolic `B.parabolic E`. `parabolicAut_mem_unipotentRadical_iff` says it preserves `B.unipotentRadical E`, the kernel of the block Levi projection. This is the concrete block version of the stability of `P` and `N` in Rogawski §3.
- `Langlands.BaseChange.jacquetSigmaIntertwiner σ B A`, for a representation `ρ` of `GL_n(E)` with `A : SigmaIntertwiner σ n ρ`: the isomorphism of `Parabolic.leviCoinvariants B (ρ.comp (B.parabolic E).subtype)` onto its composite with `leviAut σ B`. The module is the Library's `B.JacquetModule ρ` with its unnormalized Levi action. `jacquetSigmaIntertwiner_mk` states the quotient formula `A_N [v] = [A v]`. The linear map is the same for normalized Jacquet: multiplying the action by a σ-invariant character does not change the intertwining identity. This last comparison is not formalized here.

## Construction

`Jacquet.lift` factors the composite of `A` and the quotient map through the coinvariants: the identity `A(ρ(n)v) = ρ(θ(n))(A v)` and stability of `N` show it is constant on `N`-orbits. The analogous map for `A⁻¹` and `θ⁻¹` is its inverse by surjectivity of `Jacquet.mk`. For a block parabolic the existing `entrywiseAut_mem_parabolic_iff` gives stability of the parabolic, and `leviProjection_entrywiseAut` induces preservation of the radical because it is the kernel of the projection. The intertwining identity on the whole Jacquet module reduces on quotient representatives to the original intertwining identity for `A` at `B.leviEmbedding E m`; the identity `entrywiseAut_leviEmbedding` compares this embedding with `leviAut`. No new quotient or Jacquet action is defined.
