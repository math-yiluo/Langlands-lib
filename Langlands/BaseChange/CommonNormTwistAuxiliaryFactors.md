# Finite auxiliary standard factors after common norm twists

`CommonNormTwistAuxiliaryFactorsStatement` fixes a finite place of a cyclic
Galois extension of number fields. The local factor choices use actual
original representations, their determinant twists and trivial rank-one
partners. The lower twists are the given common Hecke character times the
restricted norm characters. Each upper twist is the corresponding component
of the idelic norm pullback of the common character. The lower local norm
characters are exhausted, each with multiplicity the number of upper places.

The local comparison takes either spherical spectra of the original
representations related by the residue-degree power at every upper place,
or the polynomial and epsilon exponent formulas for the highly ramified
standard factors on both sides. The latter formulas state that the Euler
polynomial is `1` and that the exponent is the rank times the Tate exponent
of the twisting character. The conclusion gives equality of the grouped
Euler values at every complex parameter and of the positive epsilon bases.

First group the lower choices by the restricted norm character. A common
local twist determines the same representation class, and uniqueness at
the canonical self-dual measure makes the factor values equal in each fiber.
For each upper place, use the actual completed norm to compare the upper
character and use trace to compare the standard additive characters. The
spherical calculation gives an Euler denominator comparison and an exponent
sum. In the highly ramified case the Euler denominators are `1`, and the
exponent sum is the rank times the Tate norm comparison. The substitution
by the residue degree evaluates the polynomials at the correct residue
cardinalities. The fiber count gives the product over all upper places.
