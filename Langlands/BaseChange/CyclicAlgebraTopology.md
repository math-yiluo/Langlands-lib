# Topology of finite cyclic algebras and their general linear groups

The vector space basis of a finite algebra over `F` supplies a linear
equivalence with a finite power of `F`. The module topology makes both
directions continuous. The resulting homeomorphism transfers
Hausdorffness, local compactness, total disconnectedness and second
countability. These properties pass to finite matrix spaces and to their
unit groups. The Library's compact-open basis theorem then gives local
profinite topology on the general linear group. Continuity of the
entrywise automorphism follows from linearity over `F`.

For the closed embedding of general linear groups, a linear retraction of
the injective scalar map gives its induced topology. Its image is closed
because it is exactly the fixed subspace of the continuous cyclic algebra
automorphism. The closed embedding passes first to the finite matrix spaces
and then to units.

These are internal topological facts for the regular transfer argument of
Henniart–Lemaire (2011), I.2.5–I.2.8. They use the topology already specified
on the algebra by the Library statement.
