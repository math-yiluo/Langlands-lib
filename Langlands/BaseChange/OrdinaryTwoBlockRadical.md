# The ordinary two-block radical change of variables

Write \(\gamma\) for the block diagonal element with entries
\(\gamma_0,\gamma_1\). Conjugation by the upper radical coordinate \(x\)
replaces the radical argument by \(x-\gamma_0^{-1}x\gamma_1\).
An element in the kernel makes its upper unipotent matrix commute
with \(\gamma\). Coprime block characteristic polynomials put this
commuting matrix in the Levi, so its upper right block is zero.

The existing rectangular coordinates express the difference map by
the identity minus a Kronecker product. Its determinant is nonzero and
invariant under conjugation in the Levi. The published determinant
change of variables for additive Haar then gives the radical integral
with the inverse normalized absolute value of that determinant.

This is the ordinary radical Jacobian used in the parabolic descent
formula of Laumon (1996), Proposition 4.3.11. The matrix and measures
here are those of the Library's two-block parabolic integration.
