# Integral comparison for concordant functions

`ConcordantIntegrableIntegralEqualityStatement` asks for equality of the Haar integrals
of an integrable complex function on GLₙ(E) and an integrable complex function on GLₙ(F)
whenever they satisfy the Library's `AreConcordant` condition. It ranges over cyclic
algebras with the module topology over any nonarchimedean local field, every matrix size,
and all ambient Haar measures on the Borel spaces.

The proposition is the comparison derived by combining the ordinary and twisted Weyl
formulas of Henniart–Lemaire 2011, II.2.10, printed pp. 70–72. At common regular torus
points concordance identifies the orbital factors, and off the norm image it makes the
ordinary factor zero. Integrable inputs let this comparison apply to a test function
multiplied by a locally integrable character.
