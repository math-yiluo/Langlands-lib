# Identifying the discrete σ-orbit block with the local self-pair identity

The proof uses a supplied discrete orbit product over bundled p-adic fields
and the self-pair identity for certified lower and upper denominators. The
lower input is an irreducible square-integrable supercuspidal. The upper
block is an irreducible square-integrable representation with exact period.

Galois pullback gives the square-integrable block family. The upper
self-pair denominator factors into its block-pair denominators. The
comparison at the residue cardinalities gives nonvanishing of the upper
denominator and its diagonal factor for the first block. The discrete-series
self-pair criterion makes that block supercuspidal. Its conjugates are
pairwise inequivalent by the period hypothesis. The upper order at one is
therefore the orbit length.

The theorem `cuspidalSelfPairDescentProfile` computes the same order as the
norm-twist stabilizer cardinality. It sums the individual lower self-twist
indicators and uses preservation of order at one under substitution by `X^f`.

The argument is Henniart–Lemaire, *Changement de base et induction
automorphe pour GL_n en caractéristique non nulle* (2011), II.4.12,
printed p. 96 (PDF p. 104). The cross-field polynomial identity of
II.4.6(4), printed p. 89 (PDF p. 97), enters as an explicit hypothesis.
