# Removing central averages at a fixed determinant order

`SphericalCentralAverageReductionStatement` concerns a cyclic norm pair over
p-adic fields with equal ramification indices and two spherical tests
supported at the associated determinant orders. The central integrations use
Borel measurable subgroups of the scalar groups, with weights equal to one
on integral scalar units and measures giving those units mass one.

Multiplication by a scalar changes the determinant order by the scalar's
order times the matrix rank. At an element in the support degree of a
homogeneous spherical test this leaves only the integral scalar units in
the central average. Their matrices belong to the spherical subgroup.
The test and the central weight are constant there, and the scalar-unit
mass is one. Thus the average has the original value at that degree.

Ordinary and twisted conjugation preserve determinant order. At an
associated norm pair the ordinary order is the cyclic degree times the
upper order. If these orders are the support degrees of the two tests,
the central averages and the original tests have identical descended
orbital integrands. Otherwise both original integrands vanish. This
passes concordance from the averaged tests to the original tests for
all associated compatible measures. The proof is
`sphericalCentralAverageReduction`.

This is the removal of the central averages in Henniart–Lemaire,
*Changement de base et induction automorphe pour GLₙ en caractéristique
non nulle* (2011), I.6.10, printed pp. 51–52 (PDF pp. 59–60).
