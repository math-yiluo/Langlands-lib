# The actual field invariants at an auxiliary finite completion

For a place above a finite place of a cyclic number-field extension,
`CompletionRelativeFieldInvariantsStatement` identifies the relative module topology,
abelian Galois structure and residue degree of the canonical p-adic completion packages.
Their residue degree is the inertia degree of the upper prime. The statement also supplies
the relative trace-codifferent exponent. The supporting trace formula identifies the
standard additive character upstairs as the local trace pullback of the standard one
downstairs.

The base completion map is continuous in the canonical topologies. Finite dimension gives
its relative module topology. The decomposition map embeds the local Galois group in the
cyclic global one. The residue cardinalities agree with the absolute ideal norms by the
integral and residue comparisons, and the norm relation gives the inertia power formula.
The different exponent follows from the trace dual of the upper valuation ring. The
additive character formula restricts the equality of the standard adelic characters along
the single-place embeddings.

These data are used in the auxiliary factor comparison in Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1,
(6.7) and Lemma 6.13.
