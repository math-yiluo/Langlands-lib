# Reduction to the ordinary and twisted orbital descent formulas

The exact spherical fundamental lemma follows from the regular elliptic case,
existence of the spherical base-change homomorphisms and the stated ordinary
and associated orbital descent formulas. Both p-adic field universes remain
independent.

The ordinary descent premise expresses the orbital integral at a regular
two-block element through products of block orbital integrals. The associated
descent premise uses compatible quotient measures for associated pairs in
each block and supplies the same scalar in the ordinary and twisted
formulas. Both premises act on expansions of the normalized radical
integrals with Haar measures of mass one on the integral rectangles.

Lean obtains the spherical expansions using restriction to the closed
parabolic, partial integration and bi-invariance. The Satake comparison
identifies the base-field expansion with the lower-rank base changes of
the factors of the extension-field expansion. At a non-norm block, the
lower-rank vanishing theorem kills each summand. At an exact norm pair,
the lower-rank matching theorem identifies the two products in each
summand. The common scalar in the orbital formulas gives concordance.
The previously proved block reduction extends this result to the exact
Library target under the regular elliptic premise.

This follows the nonelliptic step of Arthur–Clozel (1989), Chapter 1,
Theorem 4.5, and Henniart–Lemaire (2011), I.6.6. The two orbital formulas
isolate the use of Laumon (1996), 4.3.11 and 4.4.9, with the measure
conventions of the Library's `ConcordAt`. The proof implements the
constant-term compatibility from Laumon 4.2.4 for spherical functions.
