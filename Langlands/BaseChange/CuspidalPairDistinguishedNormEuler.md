# The distinguished Euler product for actual weak base-change pairs

`cuspidalPairDistinguishedNormEuler` compares actual weak base-change
cuspidal pairs of arbitrary positive ranks over a cyclic extension of number
fields with compact adelic quotients. The distinguished lower place has a
single upper place. Given the completed cuspidal pair functional equation,
the global norm characters with their local restriction multiplicities,
and open-unit-disk nonvanishing for the forward and dual distinguished
polynomials, it identifies the upper forward polynomial composed with
`X^f` with the product over all lower norm-character twists. Here `f` is
the residue degree at the distinguished upper place. Both pairs are in
standard global additive normalization.

The proof chooses a common auxiliary Hecke character trivial at the
lower distinguished place and with trivial norm pullback at the upper one.
The finite comparison gives equality of the norm-twisted lower Euler
products with the grouped upper Euler products at every other finite
place, for both the forward and dual presentations. The standard completed
equation supplies Gamma blocks and nonzero archimedean epsilon constants
for the actual twisted presentations. Uniqueness of the canonical
additive component, self-dual measure and certified finite factors
identifies the factors of these equations with the choices used in the
away-place comparison.

At the distinguished places, the auxiliary twist preserves the actual
chosen polynomials because the finite twisting characters are unchanged.
The residue cardinalities are related by the inertia degree. The grouped
completed-equation comparison clears the distinguished factors from the
finite products and uses the root bounds to identify their forward
polynomials.

The construction is the global-to-local Euler comparison of Henniart and
Lemaire, *Changement de base et induction automorphe pour GL_n en
caractéristique non nulle* (2011), II.6.19, equations (3)–(5), printed pp.
133–134 (PDF pp. 141–142), adapted to actual number-field completions and
archimedean Gamma factors. The retained source is registered as
