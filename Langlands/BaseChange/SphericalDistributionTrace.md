# Character distributions on the spherical corner

For an irreducible admissible representation of the canonical p-adic
matrix group, every integrated spherical test acts into the spherical
fixed space. This gives zero finite-rank trace when that space is zero.

When the space is nonzero, the Library theorem `sphericalFixedLine` gives
its dimension one. The Satake character gives the scalar of the corner
operator there. The ordinary and twisted traces can be taken on this
fixed space by the finite-rank trace restriction theorem. For the twisted
trace, the intertwiner preserving the spherical subgroup is assumed to
act as the identity on the fixed line, so it leaves the scalar unchanged.

This is the spherical trace calculation in Henniart–Lemaire, *Changement
de base et induction automorphe pour GLₙ en caractéristique non nulle*
(2011), I.5.5, Lemma 2, printed p. 39 (PDF p. 47). It applies to any
irreducible admissible spherical object with the indicated spectrum and
intertwiner normalization.
