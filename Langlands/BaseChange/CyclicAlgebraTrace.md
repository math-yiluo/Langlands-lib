# A trace-one element of a cyclic algebra

The sum of the powers of the cyclic generator acts as a linear map on the
coefficient algebra. Over a splitting field its value in each coordinate
is the sum of all coordinates, since the powers of the rotation enumerate
the coordinates. Applying this sum to a coordinate vector shows that the
scalar extension of the original linear map is nonzero.

The sum of any cyclic orbit is fixed by the generator. It therefore lies
in the image of the base field. Scaling an element with nonzero sum by the
inverse of that base-field value produces an element of trace one.

This gives the trace-one input for the differential of the torus norm in
Henniart–Lemaire, *Changement de base et induction automorphe pour GLₙ en
caractéristique non nulle*, §II.2.10, printed p. 71. The source identifier is
