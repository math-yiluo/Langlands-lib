# Twisted trace Paley–Wiener conditions on Levi products

The module `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedLeviPaleyWiener` defines the objects
in which the twisted trace Paley–Wiener theorem of Rogawski (1988),
Proposition 11.2, is stated for a Levi product `M_B = ∏ᵢ GL_{Bᵢ}(E)` with the
entrywise action `leviAut σ B`, using the standard parabolics
`relParabolic E B C` of `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedLeviParabolic`. The
compact-domination condition and the trace condition are the generic
`IsCompactlyDominatedFunctional` and `IsTwistedTraceFunctional` of
`Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedPaleyWiener`, applied to `θ = leviAut σ B`.

## Twisted characters of a general group

For a Hausdorff, locally compact, totally disconnected group `G` with a
left-invariant Borel measure `μ` finite on compacts and an automorphism `θ`,
`twistedSpectrum μ θ` is the set of distributions
`twistedDistribution μ π hπ A` for irreducible admissible representations `π`
on complex vector spaces in `Type z` and isomorphisms `A : π ≅ π ∘ θ`. Its
span is `twistedSpectrumSpan μ θ (fun _ _ _ _ => True)` of
`Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedInductionSpans`.
`twistedDistribution_mem_twistedSpectrum` introduces an element, and
`isTwistedTraceFunctional_iff` restates `IsTwistedTraceFunctional θ μ Φ` as
agreement of `Φ` with evaluation at one test function on `twistedSpectrum μ θ`.

## Regularity on induced families

`IsLeviSigmaInducedRegularFunctional σ B μ Φ` is condition (A)(ii) of
Henniart–Lemaire (2011), II.5.7, and condition (II) of Rogawski (1988), §11,
for `M_B`, at carrier universe `z`. For every block shape `C` with
`C.parabolic E ≤ B.parabolic E` (the refinements of `B`, including `B`),
every irreducible admissible representation `Ξ` of `M_C` on a space in
`Type z` and every `BΞ : Ξ ≅ Ξ ∘ leviAut σ C`, there are a finite set `s` of
elements of `M_C` and coefficients `c` such that, for every unramified
character `ψ` of `M_C` with `ψ ∘ leviAut σ C = ψ` and every proof of
admissibility of the induction,

```text
Φ (Θ^{ι(BΞ_ψ)}_{Ind(Ξ ⊗ ψ)}) = ∑_{m ∈ s} c m * ψ m.
```

Here `Ind` is the unnormalized smooth induction from `relParabolic E B C`
along `relProjection E B C`, `BΞ_ψ` is `twistTwistedIntertwiner` with
underlying map `BΞ`, and `ι` is `relInducedSigmaIntertwiner`. For the
one-block shape of positive `n`, the refinements of `B` are all block shapes
of `n`, and the block-diagonal isomorphism of `M_B` with `GL_n(E)` matches
these families with those of `IsSigmaInducedRegularFunctional`;
`Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedPaleyWienerReduction` proves that the transported
condition on `GL_n(E)` implies this one. The Markdown of
`Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedPaleyWienerStatement` explains why finite
evaluation sums express regularity on the torus of σ-invariant unramified
characters and why unnormalized induction gives the same condition as
normalized induction; the same arguments apply blockwise on `M_B`.

## Properly induced twisted characters

`leviProperInducedSpan σ B μ` is the span of the twisted characters
`twistedDistribution μ (Ind Ξ) hI (relInducedSigmaIntertwiner σ B C Ξ BΞ)`
for block shapes `C` with `C.parabolic E ≤ B.parabolic E` and more blocks than
`B`, irreducible admissible `Ξ` on `M_C` (carriers in `Type z`), any
`BΞ : Ξ ≅ Ξ ∘ leviAut σ C` and any admissibility proof `hI` of the
induction. The induced representation need not be irreducible. For the
one-block shape this is the analogue on `M_B` of `sigmaProperInducedSpan`.
`twistedDistribution_mem_leviProperInducedSpan` introduces a generator. In
Rogawski's notation these are the twisted characters of the elements of the
subgroup `R(G*)^1` of §9, printed p. 223, which is generated by `R(G*)°` and
the proper inductions; the twisted characters of `R(G*)°` vanish.

## Induction on test functions

`IsLeviInductionTransfer σ B μ C ν CT` says that a linear map
`CT : SchwartzBruhat (M_B) → SchwartzBruhat (M_C)` realizes twisted
induction at carrier universe `z`: for every admissible representation `Ξ` of
`M_C`, every `BΞ : Ξ ≅ Ξ ∘ leviAut σ C` and every admissibility proof of its
induction,

```text
twistedDistribution ν Ξ hΞ BΞ ∘ₗ CT
  = twistedDistribution μ (Ind Ξ) hI (relInducedSigmaIntertwiner σ B C Ξ BΞ).
```

The twisting operator on the induced representation is the one of Rogawski
(1988), §3, printed p. 218, and Henniart–Lemaire (2011), II.4.4, printed
p. 85. The identity is the transpose form of the descent formula of
Henniart–Lemaire (2011), II.4.5, formula (2), printed p. 86 (the twisted
version of van Dijk's formula). For a standard parabolic `P = MU` of
`GL_n(E)` with `M = ∏ᵢ GL_{αᵢ}(E)`, admissible σ-stable representations `Πᵢ`
with `Aᵢ : Πᵢ ≅ Πᵢ^σ`, `Ξ = Π₁ ⊗ ⋯ ⊗ Πₘ` and `B = A₁ ⊗ ⋯ ⊗ Aₘ`, it reads

```text
Θ^{ι_α(B)}_{ι_α(Ξ)}(φ) = Θ^B_Ξ(φ_{P,σ}),
φ_{P,σ}(m) = δ_P(m)^{1/2} ∫_{U(E) × K_E} φ(k⁻¹ m u σ(k)) du dk,
```

with `ι_α` normalized induction and the Haar measures giving volume `1` to
`K_E = GL_n(o_E)` and `M(o_E)`. `IsLeviInductionTransfer` is a derived form of
this formula, which differs from it in four ways. The comparisons below
explain the formulation of `IsLeviInductionTransfer`; neither this module nor
`Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedLeviPaleyWienerStatements` proves them, and the
latter states the existence of such transfer maps as an input. The Library
has the normalized tensor-product case on `GL_n(E)`:
`Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedVanDijk.twistedVanDijk` (module
`Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedVanDijk.Proof`) proves formula (2) for
normalized induction from a standard Levi subgroup of `GL_n(E)`, tensor
products of admissible representations of its blocks with tensor products of
σ-intertwiners, and Haar measures of volume one on `GL_n(o_E)`, `M(o_E)`
and `U ∩ GL_n(o_E)`.

- The ambient group is the Levi product `M_B`, not `GL_n(E)`. For a refinement
  `C` of `B`, `relParabolic E B C` is the product over the blocks of `B` of
  standard parabolics of the factors `GL_{Bᵢ}(E)`, and `σ` acts factor by
  factor. Van Dijk's computation, which proves the formula, only uses the
  decomposition
  `M_B = P K` with `K` the product of the groups `GL_{Bᵢ}(o_E)` and the
  corresponding integration formula, so it holds on `M_B` with the same `φ_{P,σ}`.
- `Ξ` is an arbitrary admissible representation of `M_C` with any
  `BΞ : Ξ ≅ Ξ ∘ leviAut σ C`, not a tensor product of representations of the
  factors with a tensor product of isomorphisms. The same computation of the
  trace of `∫ φ(g) Ind(Ξ)(g) ι(BΞ) dg` through the integration formula applies to
  every admissible pair; the tensor-product form is not used in it.
- The induction is unnormalized. The unnormalized induction of `Ξ` is the
  normalized induction of `Ξ ⊗ δ_P^{-1/2}`. The character `δ_P^{-1/2}` is
  σ-invariant, so `BΞ` is also an isomorphism for the twisted representation
  and the induced operator `(ι f)(g) = BΞ(f(σ⁻¹ g))` is unchanged. Since
  `Θ^{BΞ}_{Ξ ⊗ χ}(h) = Θ^{BΞ}_Ξ(χ h)`, the formula holds with `φ_{P,σ}` replaced
  by `δ_P^{-1/2} φ_{P,σ}`, that is, by `∫_{U × K} φ(k⁻¹ m u σ(k)) du dk`.
- The Haar measures `μ` and `ν` are arbitrary. Twisted characters are linear in
  the measure, so if `μ = a μ₀` and `ν = b ν₀` for the normalized measures, the
  map `(b⁻¹ a) · (φ ↦ δ_P^{-1/2} φ_{P,σ})` satisfies the identity for `μ` and `ν`.
