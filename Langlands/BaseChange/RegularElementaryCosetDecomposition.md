# Finite coset decomposition of the regular elementary test

Let \(I=T(\mathcal O)K_1\) and let \(D=ItI\) for a strict
weighted diagonal \(t\). `RegularElementaryCosetDecompositionStatement`
expresses the regular elementary indicator as a sum of the twisted
pullbacks of \(1_D\), using one representative of each left coset
in \(K/I\).

For a point in the elementary support, first express it as the
twisted conjugate of a weighted integral diagonal by \(h\in K\).
A representative \(k\) contributes when the twisted conjugation
by \(hk\) brings that diagonal into \(D\). The double-coset support
identity then gives a second conjugator in \(I\). Comparing orders
forces their difference to be integral diagonal, hence in \(I\).
Conversely every \(hk\in I\) contributes. Thus the contributing
coset is exactly \(h^{-1}I\). At every point outside the elementary
support all summands vanish.

This is a finite-average form of the elementary support used in
Henniart–Lemaire, I.4.1 and I.5.3. It permits trace comparison by
conjugation invariance and a single compact-open double-coset trace.
