# Irreducibility of a supercuspidal sigma-orbit product

`SupercuspidalSigmaOrbitIrreducibilityStatement` concerns a supplied p-adic
field, an automorphism over an intermediate field, and an irreducible
supercuspidal of positive rank. Its normalized orbit induction is irreducible,
with the same statement for any universe of the original carrier. The orbit
blocks can repeat.

Real unitarization gives a single determinant twist unitarizing the original
supercuspidal. Automorphisms preserve the normalized absolute value, so the
same character twists every orbit block. The irreducibility theorem for the
unitarizable orbit product applies, and compatibility of normalized induction
with a common determinant twist then gives irreducibility of the original
product. An intertwining universe lift permits the unitarization argument for
an arbitrary original carrier.

This is the local irreducibility step for the orbit product in the setting of
Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, Proposition 6.9(ii).
