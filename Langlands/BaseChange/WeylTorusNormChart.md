# Local openness of the rational Weyl torus norm

In the affine coordinate \(t\mapsto t-1\), the rational torus is the open
subset of its Lie algebra on which \(1+x\) is a unit. For a coefficient
\(c\) with cyclic trace one, parametrize the affine slice by
\(X\mapsto 1+cX_E\), where \(X\) ranges over the rational torus Lie
algebra and \(X_E\) is its coefficient extension. The sum of the cyclic
translates of \(cX_E\) is \(X_E\). Thus the ordered norm polynomial on
this slice has derivative the base-field inclusion at zero. A linear
retraction identifies that derivative with the identity in rational
coordinates.

Near zero, the affine factors are invertible and commute with the
defining regular matrix. The product is therefore the ordered cyclic
norm of an extended torus point. Its affine coordinate downstairs equals
the retracted polynomial. The strict inverse function theorem gives a
partial homeomorphism. The chart is restricted to the open set where
the polynomial derivative is invertible, so its inverse is strictly
differentiable at every point of the target. This target gives a
neighborhood of the identity in the norm image. Multiplicativity makes
the full image open, and intersection with the open regular locus gives
openness of the regular norm domain.
