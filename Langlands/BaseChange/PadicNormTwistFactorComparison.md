# Rank-one Euler factors and epsilon exponents at auxiliary places

`PadicNormTwistFactorComparisonStatement` compares the actual Tate factors for an unramified base quasicharacter on a finite abelian extension of supplied p-adic fields. It realizes each norm-trivial twist as a continuous quasicharacter, gives equality of the product of the rank-one Euler values with the Euler value of the norm pullback, and matches the epsilon exponents after multiplication by the residue degree upstairs.

The rank-one Euler product uses the uniformizer values of the unramified twists and the residue-degree substitution. The epsilon exponents use the actual multiplicative and additive conductors. The multiplicative part is the cyclic norm conductor comparison. To compare the additive parts, normalize the base additive character to conductor zero by scaling by a power of a uniformizer. The trace codifferent then gives the upper conductor. Undoing the scaling uses the norm order of an element from the base. The number of twist factors is the index of the norm subgroup, by duality for finite abelian groups.

This is the rank-one factor calculation in Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, (6.7) and Lemma 6.13, printed pp. 60 and 70. The comparison concerns the epsilon exponents. The constants in the epsilon factors are independent of the spectral argument.
