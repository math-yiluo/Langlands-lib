# Auxiliary standard factors for spherical norm base change

`SphericalNormTwistFactorsStatement` concerns a finite abelian Galois extension of supplied p-adic fields with their canonical topologies and valuations. The relative module topology, the residue degree and the different exponent are supplied. Both components are spherical, and the upper spectrum is the residue-degree power of the lower spectrum. This power relation is a hypothesis also at ramified field places.

The additive characters are nontrivial and related by trace. Each comes with a self-dual Haar measure. The input supplies continuous models for all norm-trivial quasicharacters, equivalent representatives of the corresponding determinant twists, trivial GL₁ partners, and the actual Langlands pair L-polynomial and epsilon certificates for the lower twists and the upper component. The product of the lower Euler denominators equals the upper denominator after the residue-degree substitution. The sum of the lower integer epsilon exponents equals the residue degree times the upper exponent.

Choose ordered parameters realizing the two spherical components. The standard factors are products and sums of Tate factors of the rank-one inducing characters, with the given twists on the lower side. The rank-one norm comparison gives each term of the lower product and sum. The upper Euler polynomial depends only on the multiset of uniformizer values, while all upper rank-one epsilon exponents are the additive conductor. This implements the factor calculation in Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, Lemma 6.13.
