# Closed regular twisted orbits over a cyclic field extension

The cyclic norm takes a twisted conjugate to an ordinary conjugate of the norm. Conversely, the Library's cyclic field conjugacy theorem shows that elements with conjugate cyclic norms lie in the same twisted orbit. Thus the twisted orbit is the preimage under cyclic norm of an ordinary conjugacy orbit.

When the norm is regular semisimple, its ordinary orbit is closed. Cyclic norm is continuous, so the twisted orbit is closed as well. The closed embedding of the twisted centralizer quotient into the ambient group then gives continuity and compact support of the actual twisted orbital integrand for every continuous compactly supported test function. This gives the orbit support property used in the cyclic-field setting of Henniart–Lemaire, I.2.3–4.
