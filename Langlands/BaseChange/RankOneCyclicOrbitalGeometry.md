# Centralizers and concordance geometry in rank one

In rank one the determinant identifies `GL₁` with the field's units. The actual matrix norm is the determinant field norm, and norm association is equality with this norm. The ordinary centralizer is the full group and its identification is a continuous group isomorphism. The twisted centralizer is the entrywise fixed subgroup. The cyclic algebra condition identifies that subgroup with the image of the original base group. A linear retraction onto the base field gives continuity of the inverse on the image.

These are rank-one specializations of Henniart and Lemaire, *Changement de base et induction automorphe pour GL(n) en caractéristique non nulle* (2011), I.1.2 and I.2.2, and they describe the groups used for the rank-one concordance argument.
