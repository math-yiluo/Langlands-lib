# Twisted traces of σ-stable proper inductions vanish on the σ-elliptic set (statement)

Module `Langlands.BaseChange.SigmaInducedEllipticVanishingStatement`, declaration
`Langlands.BaseChange.SigmaInducedEllipticVanishingStatement` (level parameters `u v uW`). The
module states one proposition and does not prove it.

## Statement

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field of characteristic zero. Let `E` be a field that is a
cyclic `F`-algebra of degree `l` with generator `σ`, with its module topology over `F`. Let
`n : ℕ`, and give `GL_n(E)` its Borel σ-algebra. Let `τ` be an irreducible admissible
representation of `GL_n(E)` on a complex vector space `W : Type uW`. Suppose that for some
block shape `B` of `n` with at least two blocks and some smooth representation `Ξ` of the Levi
product `M_B(E) = ∏ᵢ GL_{nᵢ}(E)` on a space `X : Type uW` with `Ξ ≅ Ξ^σ`, `τ` is isomorphic to
the smooth induction of `Ξ`, inflated to the standard upper block parabolic `P_B(E)` through
the Levi projection `P_B(E) → M_B(E)`. Then for every isomorphism `A` of `τ` onto `τ^σ`, every
Haar measure `μ` on `GL_n(E)` and every Schwartz–Bruhat function `φ` on `GL_n(E)` that vanishes
at every element that is not σ-elliptic,

`tr(τ(φ) ∘ A) = 0`.

In Lean, the binders are, in this order: `F : Type u` with `[Field F] [ValuativeRel F]
[TopologicalSpace F] [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F] [CharZero F]`; `E : Type v` with
`[Field E] [Algebra F E] [TopologicalSpace E] [IsTopologicalRing E] [IsModuleTopology F E]`;
`σ : E ≃ₐ[F] E`; `l n : ℕ`; `hE : IsCyclicAlgebra σ l`; then, by `haveI`/`letI`,
`Module.Finite F E` from `hE.1`, the instances `generalLinear_t2Space_of_finite`,
`generalLinear_locallyCompactSpace_of_finite` and
`generalLinear_totallyDisconnectedSpace_of_finite` on `GeneralLinear n E`, the Borel σ-algebra
`borel _` and its `BorelSpace` instance. The proposition continues

`∀ {W : Type uW} [AddCommGroup W] [Module ℂ W] (τ : Representation ℂ (GeneralLinear n E) W)
(hτ : IsAdmissible τ), τ.IsIrreducible →
∀ (B : BlockShape n), 2 ≤ B.length →
∀ (X : Type uW) [AddCommGroup X] [Module ℂ X] (Ξ : Representation ℂ (B.Levi E) X),
IsSmooth Ξ → IsLeviSigmaStable σ B Ξ →
Nonempty (τ.Equiv (Induction.smoothInduced (B.parabolic E) (Ξ.comp (B.leviProjection E)))) →
∀ (A : SigmaIntertwiner σ n τ) (μ : Measure (GeneralLinear n E)) [μ.IsHaarMeasure]
(φ : SchwartzBruhat (GeneralLinear n E)),
IsSigmaEllipticSupported σ l ⇑φ → twistedDistribution μ τ hτ A.toLinearMap φ = 0`.

## Library notation

- `IsCyclicAlgebra σ l` (module `Langlands.BaseChange.Norm`): `E` is a finite reduced
  `F`-algebra of dimension `l`, `σ` has order `l`, and the elements fixed by `σ` are those of
  `F`. Here `E` is a field, so `E/F` is a cyclic extension of degree `l` with Galois group
  generated by `σ`.
- `BlockShape n` (module `Langlands.Group.GL.Blocks`, namespace `Langlands.GeneralLinear`): a
  sequence of positive block sizes `B.size i`, `i < B.length`, summing to `n`; `B.length` is the
  number of blocks. `B.parabolic E` is the
  standard upper block triangular subgroup of `GL_n(E)`, `B.Levi E` the product of the
  `GL_{nᵢ}(E)` over the blocks, and `B.leviProjection E : B.parabolic E →* B.Levi E` takes the
  diagonal blocks.
- `Induction.smoothInduced P ρ` (module `Langlands.Representation.Induction.Definitions`): the
  smooth induction from the subgroup `P` of a representation `ρ` of `P`, on the functions
  `f : G → X` with `f(p g) = ρ(p) f(g)` that are invariant under right translation by some open
  subgroup, with `G` acting by right translation. It is not normalized by the square root of the
  modulus character.
- `IsLeviSigmaStable σ B Ξ` (module `Langlands.BaseChange.PseudoCoefficient`): `Ξ` is isomorphic
  to `Ξ^σ = Ξ ∘ leviAut σ B`, where `leviAut σ B` applies `σ` to every matrix entry of every
  block. The modulus character of `P_B(E)` is invariant under `σ`, so this is the same as
  σ-stability of the normalized inducing representation `Ξ ⊗ δ_P^{-1/2}`.
- `SigmaIntertwiner σ n τ` (module `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedCharacter`): an isomorphism of
  `τ` onto `τ^σ = τ ∘ entrywiseAut σ n`, that is, a linear automorphism `A` of `W` with
  `A ∘ τ(g) = τ(g^σ) ∘ A`.
- `twistedDistribution μ τ hτ A.toLinearMap φ` (module
  `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedCharacter.Basic`): the trace of the finite-rank operator
  `τ(φ) ∘ A`, where `τ(φ) = ∫ φ(g) τ(g) dμ(g)`.
- `IsSigmaEllipticSupported σ l φ` (module `Langlands.BaseChange.EllipticSupport`): every `δ`
  with `φ(δ) ≠ 0` is σ-elliptic (`IsSigmaElliptic`, module `Langlands.BaseChange.Norm`: norm
  associated with a regular elliptic element of `GL_n(F)`).

## Remarks on the form

- **Every `A`.** `τ` is irreducible and admissible, so by Schur's lemma any two isomorphisms of
  `τ` onto `τ^σ` differ by a nonzero scalar. The statement therefore holds for every `A` as soon
  as it holds for one, for instance the isomorphism induced from an isomorphism of `Ξ` onto
  `Ξ^σ`, which is the one in the source.
- **Test functions supported in the σ-elliptic set.** The source states the vanishing of the
  twisted character function at σ-elliptic points. The statement here is its consequence for the
  twisted distribution on test functions supported in the σ-elliptic set, which is open
  (`isOpen_setOf_isSigmaElliptic`, module `Langlands.BaseChange.EllipticLocusClopen`). This form
  needs no character function.
- **Carrier universe of `Ξ`.** `X` lies in the universe of `W`, as in the non-induction clause of
  `IsSigmaDiscreteSeries` (module `Langlands.BaseChange.PseudoCoefficient`), whose negation this
  hypothesis is.
- **No condition on `Ξ` beyond smoothness and σ-stability.** Irreducibility and admissibility of
  `Ξ` are not assumed; the irreducibility and admissibility of `τ` are.

## Why it is true (sketch; not part of the Lean)

1. **Normalizing `A`.** Let `B₀ : Ξ ≅ Ξ^σ`. Since `P_B(E)` and the Levi projection are stable under
   the entrywise action of `σ`, `f ↦ B₀ ∘ f ∘ σ⁻¹` is an isomorphism `A₀` of the induced
   representation onto its σ-conjugate (the Library's
   `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedPaleyWiener.inducedSigmaIntertwiner`, module
   `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedPaleyWiener`). By Schur's lemma `A` is a scalar multiple of the
   transport of `A₀` to `τ`.
2. **Trace on the induced space.** As in the untwisted case (the Library's
   `Induction.distribution_smoothInduced_eq_zero_of_vanishes_on_conjugates`, module
   `Langlands.Representation.Induction.CharacterVanishing`, which proves that the trace of an
   induced representation vanishes on test functions vanishing on all conjugates of the
   inducing subgroup), choose a σ-stable compact open subgroup `K` of `GL_n(E)` under which `φ`
   is left and right invariant, and compute the trace of `(induced)(φ) ∘ A₀` on the `K`-fixed
   vectors, in coordinates given by the double cosets `P_B(E) x K`. The diagonal coefficient at
   `x` is an integral of `φ` over the set `x⁻¹ P_B(E) σ(x) K`; by right `K`-invariance it only
   involves the values of `φ` at elements of the form `x⁻¹ p σ(x)` with `p ∈ P_B(E)`.
3. **No such element is σ-elliptic.** If `δ = x⁻¹ p σ(x)` with `p ∈ P_B(E)`, the norm
   `N(δ) = δ σ(δ) ⋯ σ^{l-1}(δ)` is conjugate to `N(p) ∈ P_B(E)`. The characteristic polynomial of
   `N(p)` is the product of those of its diagonal blocks `N(pᵢᵢ)`, and each of these is fixed by
   `σ`, hence lies in `F[X]`. So the characteristic polynomial of an element of `GL_n(F)`
   associated with `δ` is a product of at least two monic polynomials of positive degree over
   `F`, and that element is not elliptic. Hence `φ` vanishes on all the elements of step 2, and
   the trace is `0`.

Henniart–Lemaire obtain the same vanishing from the twisted induced-character formula: the
twisted character of `ι_α(Ξ)` for `ι_α(B)` vanishes outside the σ-regular `δ` with
`N(O_σ(δ)) ∩ M(F) ≠ ∅`, and a regular elliptic element of `GL_n(F)` is not conjugate into a
proper Levi subgroup. The Library's `TwistedInducedCharacterFormulaStatement` (module
`Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedInducedCharacterFormulaStatement`) states formula (3) of II.4.5 for
normalized product inductions of irreducible block representations.

## Provenance

This is derived content, formulated for this Library. It is the consequence, for test functions
supported in the σ-elliptic set, of Henniart–Lemaire 2011, II.4.5 (printed pp. 86–88, PDF
pp. 94–96): "la fonction caractère `Θ^{ι_α(B)}_{ι_α(Ξ)}` est nulle en dehors des éléments
σ-réguliers δ de G(E) tels que l'orbite N(O_σ(δ)) rencontre M(F)" (printed p. 88). The source
proofs use the corresponding pointwise vanishing of the character function on the σ-elliptic
set: in the proof of II.5.8, Lemme (printed p. 106: "d'après 4.5, si α est différent de
(n), alors la fonction caractère `Θ^A_Π` est nulle sur `G(E)_{σ-e}`"), and in Arthur–Clozel
1989, Chapter 1, proof of Lemma 2.11 (printed p. 19: "If M is proper, the formulas for induced
twisted characters … show that `Θ_{Π,σ} = 0` on the σ-elliptic set"). Differences from the
sources: the induction is unnormalized, which changes neither the induced representation class
in question nor σ-stability, since the modulus character is σ-invariant; the conclusion is
stated for every isomorphism `A` and for the twisted distribution on test functions supported in
the σ-elliptic set rather than for the character function; and `Ξ` is any smooth σ-stable
representation of the Levi product whose induction is irreducible and admissible.
