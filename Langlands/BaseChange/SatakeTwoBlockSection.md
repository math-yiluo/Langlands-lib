# The spherical section on a two-block parabolic

For the standard two-block Levi, restrict the ordered unramified parameter
tuple to each block in its consecutive order. At a parabolic point with
Levi coordinates \(L\), the normalized spherical section of the ambient group
is the product of the two normalized spherical sections at the block
coordinates of \(L\), multiplied by the parabolic half modulus at \(L\).

For upper triangular blocks, refining each block into singleton blocks
recovers the upper Borel. The flattened modulus formula and its positive
square root give the factors of the half modulus. The determinant of each
singleton projection is its diagonal entry, so the inducing character
product factors over the same consecutive blocks.

For arbitrary blocks, the block Iwasawa decompositions give upper triangular
blocks and a block diagonal integral factor on the right. Moving this factor
past the radical changes its rectangular coordinate. Right invariance of
the sections under the standard compact subgroups and triviality of the
half modulus on the integral Levi remove this factor.

The proof uses the normalized principal series associated with the Satake
parameters in Henniart–Lemaire (2011), I.3.1. It specializes the Library's
flattened refinement modulus and Iwasawa decomposition for the spherical
constant-term comparison in Laumon (1996), 4.2.4.
