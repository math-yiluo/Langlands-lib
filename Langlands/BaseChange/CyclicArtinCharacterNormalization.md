# Finite-order representatives for the conductor of a norm pullback

The bundled p-adic field and its underlying local-field data have the
same principal units at every level. Their multiplicative conductors
agree because each is the first level on which the character is
trivial. Agreement on the full integral-unit subgroup gives agreement
on every principal-unit subgroup.

The published extension from level-zero principal units gives a
finite-order representative of any quasicharacter on the full unit
subgroup. Twists of these representatives agree with the original
twists on integral units. Their pullbacks along the determinant norm
also agree on integral units, since the relative norm-order formula
sends a unit of order zero to one of order zero. These are the
principal-unit comparisons used to reduce to finite Artin characters.
