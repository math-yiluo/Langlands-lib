# Characteristic coefficients for regular base change orbits

The ordinary parameter consists of the coefficients below the leading term
of the characteristic polynomial. For a cyclic algebra the norm lemma
provides a rational associate of each twisted element and makes any two
associates conjugate. The twisted parameter takes the ordinary coefficients
of an associate. After scalar extension they are the coefficients of the
cyclic norm. A linear retraction of the scalar inclusion recovers the
parameter from those coefficients. The ordinary map is continuous over a
topological field, and the topology of the cyclic algebra is assumed to be
its module topology to obtain continuity of the twisted map.

Equality with the coefficients of a regular semisimple element is equivalent
to norm association. The shared monic characteristic polynomial implies
ordinary conjugacy, and the norm lemma identifies all twisted elements with
a given regular parameter as one twisted conjugacy orbit.

Over a T1 field of characteristic zero the regular parameter set is open. The resultant
of the monic polynomial and its derivative is polynomial in the coefficients,
and its nonvanishing is exactly separability. Pulling back the open locus
along the two continuous parameter maps gives the ordinary and twisted
regular loci.

This internal construction gives the invariants for the local orbit charts
in the proof of Henniart–Lemaire I.2.5–I.2.8.
