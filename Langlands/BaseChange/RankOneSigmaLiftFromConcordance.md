# Rank-one character pullback from distributional concordance

`RankOneSigmaLiftNormPullbackStatement` gives character pullback for a rank-one sigma-lift defined by concordant Schwartz–Bruhat tests. The norm is the actual field norm, on the supplied p-adic carriers with compatible relative module topology.

The sigma intertwiner makes the upper character invariant under the generator. Hilbert 90 then makes it trivial on the norm kernel. Choose a compact open base neighborhood of one inside the base character kernel and the norm image, with its full norm preimage inside the upper character kernel. Use the indicator of the preimage upstairs and scale the base indicator by the positive quotient mass ratio. Translate the indicators together by an upper element and its norm. The ordinary and twisted orbital integrals satisfy concordance for every associated pair and compatible quotient measure.

Each character distribution on these tests is the character value at the translating element times a factor independent of that element. The distribution identity at the two identity elements identifies the factors. The base factor is nonzero by positivity of Haar measure on the compact open neighborhood and by the nonzero lift constant. Cancelling it gives norm pullback.

This is the rank-one consequence of Henniart–Lemaire, *Changement de base et induction automorphe pour GL_n en caractéristique non nulle*, II.2.7 and II.2.9, printed pp. 67–70. The proof uses the concordance definition directly in rank one.
