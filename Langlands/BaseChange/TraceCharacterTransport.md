# Additive conductors and trace characters under local-field isomorphisms

`TraceCharacterTransportStatement` is proved by `traceCharacterTransport`. Let `E/F` be nonarchimedean local fields, with an `F`-algebra structure on `E`, the module topology over `F`, and different exponent `d` in the sense of `IsDifferentExponent`. Let `F'` be a nonarchimedean local field and `E'` an `F'`-algebra. Suppose `σF : F ≃+* F'` is continuous in both directions and `σE : E ≃+* E'` satisfies `σE (algebraMap F E x) = algebraMap F' E' (σF x)`. If `ψF'` has Tate additive conductor zero, then `ψF = ψF' ∘ σF` and `ψE = ψF' ∘ Tr_{E'/F'} ∘ σE` satisfy `TraceEpsilonCharacterCondition ψF ψE d`: `ψE` is the pullback of `ψF` along `Tr_{E/F}`, `n(ψF) = 0`, and `n(ψE) = d`. The four universe parameters are independent. No topology or local-field structure on `E'` and no continuity of `σE` is assumed.

The module also proves two general lemmas. `LocalFieldData.mem_additiveLattice_map_iff` says that a bicontinuous field equivalence maps each fractional ideal `π^(-n) O` onto the corresponding ideal of the target. For nonzero elements, membership is `-ord x ≤ n`, and orders are preserved by `ord_of_continuous_ringEquiv`. `additiveConductor_eq_of_continuous_ringEquiv` deduces that Tate's additive conductor is unchanged by pullback along such an equivalence: the two characters are trivial on exactly corresponding lattices, so their largest triviality levels agree.

`trace_map_of_ringEquiv` identifies `Tr_{E'/F'} (σE y)` with `σF (Tr_{E/F} y)`, using Mathlib's `Algebra.trace_eq_of_equiv_equiv` and the compatibility of the two algebra maps. Pointwise, this gives the trace relation for the pulled-back characters, and conductor invariance gives `n(ψF) = n(ψF') = 0`. The published `IsDifferentExponent.additiveConductor_of_trace` then gives `n(ψE) = d` from the trace codifferent; no conductor of a character of `E'` is used.

This internal lemma is a step toward the distinguished place in Task 7843. There, `F'` and `E'` are the completions at the place below and above. The Library theorem `traceAdditiveCharacter_single` identifies the component of `ψ_K ∘ Tr_{𝔸_L/𝔸_K}` with the component of `ψ_K` composed with the local trace. The lemma does not construct the completion identifications or a conductor-zero global character, and it says nothing about epsilon factors, the global lift or the conductor identity. It treats only conductor-zero base characters. It does not prove the general relation `n(ψ_E) = d + e·n(ψ_F)`.
