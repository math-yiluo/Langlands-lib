# Concordant continuous tests over a split cyclic algebra

`splitContinuousMatching` proves `SplitContinuousMatchingStatement`.
Let \(F\) be a Hausdorff topological field whose general linear group
in the chosen rank is locally compact and second countable. The two
matrix groups have their Borel measurable structures. Give the base
matrix group any Haar measure and give the matrix group over \(F^{m+1}\)
its product measure in split coordinates. For continuous compactly
supported factor functions on the base group, the tensor upstairs and
its ordered convolution downstairs are continuous, compactly supported
and `AreConcordant` for the cyclic shift.

Take a regular associated pair and any compatible associated measures.
The associating conjugator moves the twisted matrix to a representative
whose ordered norm is exactly the scalar extension of the base matrix.
This change of representative transports the upper centralizer and
quotient measures. Their first-coordinate centralizer measure agrees
with the chosen lower measure by pushing forward the associated
measure identity. The lower regular orbit is closed and its orbit map
from the centralizer quotient is a closed embedding. The cyclic
product convolution calculation then gives equality of the integrals.
Every base matrix is a norm from the split algebra, which gives the
off-norm part of concordance.

This internal proof gives the split tensor/convolution comparison
used by Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, Theorem 4.5 proof, printed pp. 43–44,
with the Library's exact product Haar and compatible quotient measures.
It applies to the continuous compactly supported tests at infinite
split places, including the orbital comparison of smooth tests.
