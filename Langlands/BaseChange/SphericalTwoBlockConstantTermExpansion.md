# Finite spherical expansions of two-block constant terms

The normalized radical integral of a spherical Hecke function has a finite
expansion in products of spherical Hecke functions on the two actual blocks.
The proof restricts the function to the closed parabolic and pulls it back
along the existing Levi and rectangular radical coordinates. Partial
integration and multiplication by the locally constant half modulus give
a compactly supported locally constant function on the Levi.

Integral block diagonal matrices preserve the half modulus and lie in the
ambient spherical subgroup. Left invariance follows by translating the
integrand. Right translation changes the radical coordinate by left and
inverse-right multiplication by the integral blocks. Their determinant
norms are one, so the rectangular Haar scaling formula makes this change
measure preserving. The function on the Levi is therefore bi-invariant
under the product of the two spherical subgroups.

The bi-invariant tensor expansion gives the two finite families, which
the existing Hecke corner equivalences identify with actual smaller-rank
spherical Hecke functions. This specializes the spherical constant term
used in Laumon (1996), 4.2.4, to the Library's two-block coordinates.
