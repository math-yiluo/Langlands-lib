# Local epsilon data on characteristic-zero fields and their trace characters

`CuspidalSigmaLiftCharZeroEpsilonFactorsStatement` assembles actual local factor and conductor certificates on a finite extension of characteristic-zero nonarchimedean local fields with its module topology. The inputs are an irreducible supercuspidal representation over the base, an irreducible supercuspidal orbit block over the extension, a representation equivalent to the rank-reindexed normalized orbit product, and a finite set of norm-trivial characters. The two additive characters and self-dual measures in this statement are arbitrary supplied inputs.

`cuspidalSigmaLiftCharZeroEpsilonFactors` presents the base as a finite extension of the p-adic field for its residue characteristic. It composes scalar maps to give the extension the same p-adic base, and obtains finite dimension from the scalar tower. The algebra map of the given extension is continuous by its module topology, so `suppliedPadicExtension` gives a compatible realization on the extension. Both packages retain the original field carriers, topologies and valuative relations. They also retain the original representation carriers and actions. The self-dual measure subtype for each supplied p-adic realization uses the same Haar measure and intrinsic self-duality predicate as the all-field subtype. The proof reuses these measures and applies the supplied-p-adic assembly to obtain the actual epsilon and conductor certificates of the base twists and the orbit blocks, and actual epsilon multiplicativity for the induced representation.

`CuspidalSigmaLiftTraceEpsilonFactorsStatement` instead supplies the additive characters and measures. The proof obtains a nontrivial unitary character of the base, scales it by the base uniformizer to the negative of its signed additive conductor, and uses the scaling and normalization lemmas to show the resulting character has conductor zero on the original field. The upper character is the actual trace pullback, continuous and nontrivial by the finite separable trace theorem. The general local-field self-dual measure theorem gives a measure for each character. Applying the arbitrary-character result at these measures supplies the same factor and conductor certificates as above.

This is the same-field part of the local data for the conductor identity `CuspidalSigmaLiftConductorStatement`, derived by composing the Library's p-adic recognition, character and measure constructions and the supplied-data factor assembly. For the conductor reduction, finite dimension follows from `IsCyclicAlgebra`, and the trace conductor and fixed-level transport are already treated by `CuspidalSigmaLiftConductorFromTraceEpsilon`. This file does not assert `EpsilonMonomialProductComparison` across the two fields and does not use a base-change theorem or the target conductor identity.
