# Orbit preimages for regular elementary tests

The weighted integral diagonal tuples are precisely the unit tuples
whose normalized orders are the prescribed weights. At such a tuple
with strictly decreasing weights, the preimage of the upper elementary
support under the twisted orbit map is \(T(E)K_E\). An element of that
preimage takes the diagonal to another of the same orders after a
twisted conjugation by \(K_E\). The strict diagonal intertwiner lemma
shows that its product with the inverse compact conjugator is diagonal.
Conversely, a diagonal conjugator preserves the coordinate orders.

The intersection of \(T(E)\) with \(K_E\) consists of the diagonals of
order zero in every coordinate. This follows from integrality of
the matrix and of its inverse. Over an unramified pair, splitting
each diagonal entry into a power of the common uniformizer times an
integral unit yields \(T(E)K_E=T(F)K_E\), where \(T(F)\) acts through
scalar extension.

The ordered cyclic matrix norm of an upper diagonal is the scalar
extension of the ordinary diagonal of the coordinate field norms.
The proof takes the ordered product in the diagonal group and applies
the rank-one determinant–norm identity to each coordinate.

These internal lemmas implement the regular-weight support calculations
for Henniart–Lemaire (2011), I.4.1–I.4.2, pp. 26–30.
