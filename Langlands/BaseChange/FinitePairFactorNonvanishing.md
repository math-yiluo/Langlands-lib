# Nonvanishing of the finite factors on a common right half-plane

`FinitePairFactorNonvanishingStatement` gives a common right half-plane on which every actual finite L-factor of the pair and of its supplied dual pair is nonzero. The finite factor choice has its unramified description away from a finite set containing the exceptional sets.

At an exceptional place the certified polynomial has constant coefficient one, so it is nonzero near zero. The spectral point tends to zero in norm as the real part grows. A common half-plane works for the finitely many exceptional places. Elsewhere a uniform Satake bound makes every linear Euler denominator nonzero. This proves the local nonvanishing needed to compare the convergent products in Arthur–Clozel's completed equations (6.15) and (6.16) in the proof of Proposition 6.9(ii), printed p. 70 (PDF p. 84).

The completed-equation adapter combines this nonvanishing with a supplied completed equation to give the package used for multiplying equations.
