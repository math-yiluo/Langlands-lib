# Exact norm centralizers in the Levi and the ambient group

Blockwise exact cyclic norms give the ambient exact norm because both cyclic norm and coefficient extension commute with the Levi embedding. The ordinary and twisted block centralizers are then isomorphic componentwise by coefficient extension.

The product of twisted block centralizers identifies with the ambient twisted centralizer by passing through the corresponding ordinary centralizers and the ambient exact-norm isomorphism. Coefficient extension commutes with the Levi embedding, so this composite is the actual block diagonal inclusion on elements. This identifies the two natural paths between the block and ambient centralizers for the measure transport in Henniart–Lemaire, I.2.5.
