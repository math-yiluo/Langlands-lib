# The rank-one fundamental lemma from the unit and Satake statements

`RankOneFundamentalLemmaReductionStatement` is the rank-one restriction of
`FundamentalLemmaStatement`, conditional on the unit fundamental lemma and
the Satake character propositions over both fields. It uses the same
association, quotient measures, and concordance predicate as the full statement.

For an associated pair \((\delta,\gamma)\), the order of the determinant of
\(\gamma\) is \(l\) times that of \(\delta\). The rank-one Hecke formula then
gives \(b(\phi)(\gamma)=\phi(\delta)\). Each of the two orbital integrands
is constant. Ordinary conjugation is trivial in rank one, and twisted
conjugation preserves the order since the extension automorphism preserves it.
Every rank-one spherical function is constant at a given order.

The rank-one centralizers are independent of the element. Conjugation on
the extension group is trivial, so associated centralizer measures for
\((\delta,\gamma)\) are also associated at \((1,1)\) after identifying the
centralizers. The two unit orbital integrands at \((1,1)\) are identically
one. The unit fundamental lemma gives equality of the real masses of the
two quotient measures. Integrating the equal constant function values
against those measures proves concordance at the original pair.

In rank one the determinant identifies the group with the field units.
The published unramified scalar norm theorem therefore identifies the
matrix norms with the orders in \(l\mathbb Z\). The Hecke formula gives
pointwise vanishing at every complementary order, so the ordinary orbital
integrals vanish there.

This is an internal reduction for the rank-one induction base of
Henniart–Lemaire I.6.6. It uses their description of the Laurent group
algebra in I.6.11 (printed p. 52) and the unit comparison in I.3.1
(printed p. 25).
