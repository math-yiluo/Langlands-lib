# Regular elliptic norm pairs at a split auxiliary place

`SplitEllipticAuxiliaryStatement` constructs an associated norm pair in a
split cyclic algebra over any characteristic-zero nonarchimedean local
field. The ordinary member is regular elliptic and has nonzero matrix trace.
For rank one its characteristic polynomial is \(X-1\). For rank \(n \ge 2\)
the proof uses \(X^n+\varpi X^{n-1}+\varpi\), with \(\varpi\) a uniformizer.
It is Eisenstein over the valuation ring, separable over the field, and
has nonzero constant and next-to-leading coefficients. These yield an
invertible matrix with nonzero trace. The split norm is surjective, giving
the associated upper member.

Conjugation preserves trace, while multiplying a matrix by a scalar
multiplies its trace by that scalar. A nonzero trace therefore forces the
scalar to be one in a scalar conjugacy relation. The regular elliptic
nonzero-trace condition is open by continuity of trace and openness of
the regular elliptic locus.

The construction supplies the local condition at the second split
auxiliary place in Arthur–Clozel (1989), Chapter 1, § 4, printed pp. 42–43
(PDF pp. 56–57), and Henniart–Lemaire (2011), I.6.7, printed p. 48
(PDF p. 56).
