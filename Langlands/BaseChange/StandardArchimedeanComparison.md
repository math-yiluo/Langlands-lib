# Archimedean factors at the standard additive scale

The Gamma blocks attached to the actual real and complex pair parameters evaluate to the infinite L-product of the completed equation. Counting real Gamma factors gives the pair rank at a real place and twice the pair rank at a complex place. The total count is therefore the pair rank times the absolute degree of the number field. `ArchimedeanPairGammaCountComparisonStatement` uses the degree formula in a tower to compare the concatenated base lists with the upper list. It applies to either the original pairs or their duals.

The standard trace character has additive scale one at every infinite place. The actual archimedean pair epsilon products at that scale are constant complex units. The field-degree count and the constant-unit comparison supply the archimedean hypotheses of the Gamma calculation used to compare Arthur–Clozel's completed equations (6.15) and (6.16) in the proof of Proposition 6.9(ii), printed p. 70 (PDF p. 84).
