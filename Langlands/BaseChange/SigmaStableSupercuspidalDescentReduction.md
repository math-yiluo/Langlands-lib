# Descent of σ-stable supercuspidals from the supercuspidal σ-lift converse

The module `Langlands.BaseChange.SigmaStableSupercuspidalDescentReduction` states and proves

- `SigmaStableSupercuspidalDescentReductionStatement.{u, v, uV, uW}`: the implication
  `CuspidalSigmaLiftExistenceStatement.{u, v, uV, uW} → SigmaStableSupercuspidalDescentStatement.{u, v, uV, uW}`
  (fields in `Type u` and `Type v`, the representation of `GL_n(F)` on a space in `Type uV`, the
  representation of `GL_n(E)` on a space in `Type uW`);
- `sigmaStableSupercuspidalDescentReduction`, its proof, at all universe parameters.

`CuspidalSigmaLiftExistenceStatement` (module `Langlands.BaseChange.CuspidalSigmaLiftExistenceStatement`)
is Henniart–Lemaire 2011, II.4.12, Remarque, in the case `a = 1`: for an irreducible
supercuspidal `P₁` of `GL_{n₁}(E)` whose stabilizer in `⟨σ⟩` is generated by `σ^s`, the product
`P₁ × P₁^σ × ⋯ × P₁^{σ^{s-1}}` is irreducible and a σ-lift, for some σ-intertwiner and some
constant, of an irreducible supercuspidal representation of `GL_{s n₁}(F)`.
`SigmaStableSupercuspidalDescentStatement` (module
`Langlands.BaseChange.SigmaStableSupercuspidalDescentStatement`) asserts, for a field `E` that is
a cyclic `F`-algebra with its module topology and `0 < n`, that a σ-stable irreducible
supercuspidal `τ` of `GL_n(E)` is a σ-lift of an irreducible supercuspidal `π` of `GL_n(F)` for
every given σ-intertwiner `A`.

## Auxiliary results

- `SigmaIntertwiner.nonempty_pow`: a σ-intertwiner `A` of `τ` gives `τ ≅ τ^{σ^i}` for every `i`
  (induction on `i`: compose `A` with the isomorphism `τ^σ ≅ τ^{σ^{i+1}}` obtained from
  `τ ≅ τ^{σ^i}` by precomposition with `g ↦ g^σ`, using `σ^{i+1} = σ^i σ` and
  `entrywiseAut_mul_apply`).
- `exists_isSigmaLift_of_rank_eq`: for `h : m = n`, a σ-lift `(π, P, A₀, c)` at rank `m`, with
  `π` supercuspidal and irreducible and `P` equivalent to `τ` composed with the inverse of the
  entry-preserving reindexing `GeneralLinear.reindex (finCongr h.symm)`, gives a supercuspidal
  irreducible `π'` of `GL_n(F)` on the same space and a σ-intertwiner of `τ` for which `τ` is a
  σ-lift of `π'` with the same constant. After `subst h` the reindexing is the identity
  (`GeneralLinear.reindex_finCongr_rfl`), and `IsSigmaLift.transport` (module
  `Langlands.BaseChange.SigmaLiftTransport`) moves the lift along the resulting equivalence
  `P ≅ τ`.

## Proof of `sigmaStableSupercuspidalDescentReduction`

Let `F`, `E`, `σ`, `l`, `n`, `τ`, `A` be as in `SigmaStableSupercuspidalDescentStatement`.

1. *Local-field structure on `E`.* `E` is finite over `F`; `LocalField.FiniteExtension.valuativeRel F E`
   (the spectral valuation) and `LocalField.FiniteExtension.isLocalField_of_isModuleTopology F E`
   make `E`, with its given module topology, a nonarchimedean local field, as
   `CuspidalSigmaLiftExistenceStatement` requires. The topology of `E`, hence of `GL_n(E)`, is
   unchanged.
2. *Stabilizer.* By `SigmaIntertwiner.nonempty_pow`, `τ ≅ τ^{σ^i}` for every `i`, so the
   stabilizer condition holds with `s = 1` (both sides of `… ↔ 1 ∣ i` are true).
3. *The converse with `s = 1`, `n₁ = n`, `P₁ = τ`* gives an admissible
   `sigmaOrbitProduct σ 1 hn τ` on `GL_{1·n}(E)`, a space `V`, a supercuspidal irreducible `π` of
   `GL_{1·n}(F)` on `V`, a σ-intertwiner `A₀` and a constant `c₀` with the σ-lift property.
4. *Back to rank `n`.* `sigmaOrbitProduct_one_equiv` (module `Langlands.BaseChange.CuspidalSigmaLift`)
   identifies the product with `τ` composed with the inverse reindexing along `n = 1 · n`;
   `exists_isSigmaLift_of_rank_eq` with `Nat.one_mul n` yields a supercuspidal irreducible `π'`
   of `GL_n(F)`, a σ-intertwiner `A'` of `τ` and the σ-lift `IsSigmaLift σ l π' _ τ _ A' c₀`.
5. *The given intertwiner.* By `SigmaIntertwiner.exists_smul_toLinearMap` (module
   `Langlands.BaseChange.CommonShintaniLiftNonvanishing`, Schur's lemma for irreducible
   admissible representations), `A = a A'` for some `a ≠ 0`. Since `twistedDistribution` is
   linear in the intertwiner (`twistedDistribution_smul`), `τ` is a σ-lift of `π'` with
   intertwiner `A` and constant `a c₀ ≠ 0`.

## Derived content

The implication is formulated for this Library. It records that the descent of σ-stable
supercuspidals, for a field `E` with its module topology and an arbitrary σ-intertwiner, is the
orbit-length-one case of the converse of HL II.4.12, Remarque.
