# Transport of local twisted integrals and characters

`TwistedDistributionEquivStatement` concerns a continuous group isomorphism
between the given locally compact, totally disconnected groups. The twisted
distribution of the pulled-back admissible representation, at the supplied
Haar measure and test function, equals the original distribution with the
pushed-forward measure and transported test function. The ordinary distribution
is the case with identity endomorphism.

The proof changes variables in the algebraic integral defining the Hecke action.
The resulting finite-rank operator is unchanged, so its composition with the
same endomorphism has the same trace. For the geometric comparison, a group
isomorphism intertwining the two twists identifies their twisted centralizers.
The induced homeomorphism of the actual left coset spaces carries the
orbital integrand to the transported one. The integral against the pushed-forward
quotient measure therefore equals the original integral against the pulled-back test.

These are internal change-of-variables results for transporting the distributional
sigma-lift identity between the local fields and the completions selected in
Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, §6.3 and the proof of Proposition 6.9(ii).
