# Twisted local Haar pushforwards for Weyl integration

The statement uses a cyclic algebra over a nonarchimedean local field, with
the module topology. Give the ambient group over the algebra a Haar measure
and the rational Weyl torus a Haar measure. Equip the base-changed rational
subgroup with a right invariant Haar measure whose pushforward under
inclusion agrees with that of rational torus Haar measure under base change.
The fixed left quotient measure is compatible with those measures.

The regular torus norm domain is measurable, and the statement requires a
choice of exact cyclic norm lifts on that domain. For the resulting twisted
Weyl map, each domain point has an open embedding patch. The source measure
is the product of the fixed quotient measure and the pullback of rational
torus Haar measure to the regular norm domain, weighted by the absolute
ordinary discriminant at the norm. On each patch the pushforward of this
measure restricted to the patch is the ambient Haar measure restricted to
its image.

This internal statement specifies the local measure input for the twisted
Weyl formula in Henniart–Lemaire (2011), II.2.10, formula (1). The global
fiber count supplies the reciprocal rational Weyl order after summing the
local pushforwards. The local pushforwards remain a proof obligation.
