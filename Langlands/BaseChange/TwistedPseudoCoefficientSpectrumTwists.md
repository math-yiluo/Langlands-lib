# Stability of spans of twisted characters under unramified twisting

Multiplication of tests by an invariant unramified character pulls a twisted
character back to the twisted character of the corresponding representation
twist. The twist preserves admissibility, irreducibility and the underlying
linear map of the twisting operator. This proves stability for any span of
characters whose class of irreducibles is preserved by the twist.

The orbit and the other essentially tempered classes are preserved by
multiplying or inverting unramified characters. The classes with zero fixed
space at a compact open level are preserved since the character is trivial
on that subgroup.

For the proper-induction span, the restriction of an ambient unramified
character to a block parabolic factors through the Levi projection. An
unramified character of positive-rank `GL_n` factors through determinant
order. A block parabolic element and the block diagonal matrix of its Levi
projection have the same determinant. The induced-twist comparison then
expresses the pullback of a generator as a twisted distribution of the
induction from the restricted character twist of the Levi representation.
The latter representation is again irreducible and admissible. The
restricted character is invariant because the Levi embedding is equivariant.

These are derived stability properties of the spaces used in the
pseudo-coefficient construction of Henniart–Lemaire II.5.13. They use
actual smooth induction and distribution characters.
