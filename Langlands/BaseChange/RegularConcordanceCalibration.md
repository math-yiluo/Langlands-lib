# A regular concordant pair detecting a prescribed norm

`RegularConcordanceCalibrationStatement` assumes regular transfer for
a cyclic algebra over a characteristic-zero nonarchimedean local field.
The algebra has its finite module topology as in the transfer statement.
The two matrix groups use their Borel structures and the supplied Haar
measures.

For each regular semisimple norm \(\gamma\) and each neighborhood of
\(\gamma\), there is a concordant pair of Schwartz–Bruhat functions.
The upper function is supported on the \(\sigma\)-regular set. The
ordinary function is regularly supported, equals one at \(\gamma\), and
has closed support in the neighborhood. Its orbital integral at
\(\gamma\) is positive for every right Haar measure on the centralizer
and compatible measure on the Borel centralizer quotient. Concordance
therefore gives the same nonzero value at any associated upper point
with associated measures.

The regular norm locus is open. The proof takes the indicator of a
compact open coset in the intersection of that locus and the prescribed
neighborhood. Every conjugate of a non-norm is a non-norm, so the
ordinary orbital integral of this indicator vanishes off the norms.
Regular transfer supplies the upper function and the published orbital
positivity theorem gives the positive ordinary orbital value.

This is an internal consequence of regular transfer in
Henniart–Lemaire 2011, I.2.5, and the orbital positivity used in
II.6.8. It supplies a local test pair for comparing the weighted
one-orbit terms in the global argument.
