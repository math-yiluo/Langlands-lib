# Topology of the ordinary Weyl discriminant

The Weyl discriminant is continuous on the matrix group of a field with a
ring topology. In the standard basis of the matrix algebra, the entries of the
adjoint defect matrix depend continuously on the group element and its
inverse. The coefficients of the characteristic polynomial are polynomial
functions of those entries.

On regular torus points with nonzero discriminant, the residue-normalized
absolute discriminant is positive. When it is nowhere zero on the regular
torus, the weight is locally constant there. A nonzero valuation has a
singleton neighborhood in the valuation topology. Continuity of the valuation
composed with the discriminant therefore makes the valuation, and hence the
real weight, locally constant. In Henniart–Lemaire (2011), II.2.10, the
ordinary differential supplies the required nonzero discriminant.
