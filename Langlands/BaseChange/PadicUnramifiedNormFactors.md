# Rank-one unramified factors under cyclic norm pullback

The determinant field norm multiplies the upper normalized order by the residue degree. An unramified character is determined by its uniformizer value, so the unramified norm-trivial twists are indexed by roots of unity of order dividing that degree.

For an unramified base quasicharacter, each ramified twist has Tate Euler denominator one. The product of the remaining linear factors is the Euler denominator of the norm pullback with the residue-degree substitution.
