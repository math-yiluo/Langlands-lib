# Existence of the trace-codifferent exponent

`DifferentExponentExistenceStatement` gives a natural different exponent in the trace
convention for a continuous finite separable extension of nonarchimedean local fields.
The valuative extension hypothesis identifies the valuation rings with the rings used for
integral closure and trace duality.

After identifying the fraction fields of the two valuation rings, the proof uses the
nonzero trace dual to show that the different is a nonzero integral ideal. The upper
valuation ring is a discrete valuation ring, so the different is a power of its maximal
ideal. Inversion gives its trace-dual fractional ideal generated by the inverse of that
power of a uniformizer. This is the specified membership condition for `IsDifferentExponent`.
The result supplies the different exponent for the auxiliary-place factor comparison in
Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, (6.7) and Lemma 6.13.
