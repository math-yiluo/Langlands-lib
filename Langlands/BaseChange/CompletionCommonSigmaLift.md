# A common sigma-lift at a finite completion

`CompletionCommonSigmaLiftStatement v w` applies to a cyclic extension of the actual completions at a finite place `v` and a place `w` above it. Suppose an irreducible supercuspidal action over the upper completion has trivial Galois stabilizer, and its orbit product is a sigma-lift of a supplied downstairs action. Given `CuspidalSigmaLiftExistenceStatement`, the two actions in the conclusion, the supplied action and an irreducible supercuspidal witness, share an irreducible sigma-lift. The witness is essentially square-integrable. Both lift identities use the original downstairs rank and the same admissible upstairs action.

`exists_commonSigmaLift_of_orbitProductLift_at_completion` uses the actual completion embedding to obtain the module topology. Trivial stabilizer makes the period of the upper action equal to the degree of the cyclic algebra. The existence input therefore supplies a supercuspidal witness whose lift is the full orbit product, along with irreducibility of that product. The p-adic square-integrability theorem gives the property of the witness for the completion topology. The equality of valuative relations puts that result in the same local-field instance as the lift.

The rank equality and supplied lift identity are the fields of `HasSigmaOrbitProductLift` at positive rank. After eliminating the equality, the matrix reindexing is the identity, and transport of `IsSigmaLift` recovers the lift identity for the original action. The resulting common-lift data are used by the fibre comparison of Henniart–Lemaire (2011), II.4.8, Lemme (2), printed pp. 90–91. The existence input is from II.4.12, Remarque, printed p. 97.
