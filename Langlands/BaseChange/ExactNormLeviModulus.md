# Standard compact and Levi modulus at cyclic norms

A field automorphism over the p-adic base preserves the canonical norm. It therefore preserves integral matrix entries and entries of the inverse, which proves stability of the standard compact subgroup. It also preserves the residue-normalized determinant norm.

The residue-normalized determinant norm of a scalar-extended element is the original determinant norm to the relative field degree. The determinant norm of a cyclic norm is the determinant norm of the twisted element to the same degree. The degree is positive, so these equal powers identify the ordinary and twisted determinant norms at an exact norm. Applying the product formula for the Levi modulus and its positive square root gives equality of the half moduli for blockwise exact norms. Invariance under twisted Levi conjugation follows from invariance of each block determinant norm under the field automorphism.

These identities implement the normalization of the block constant terms used in the ordinary and twisted descent formulas of Laumon, Propositions 4.3.11 and 4.4.9.
