# Semisimple points in twisted matrix conjugacy-orbit closures

Let a finite reduced commutative algebra over a complete nontrivially normed field of characteristic zero have its module topology. For an algebra automorphism σ with finite order, `twistedSemisimpleOrbitClosure` proves that the closure of every orbit `g ↦ g δ σ(g)⁻¹` in `GL_n` contains a matrix ε for which the operator `X ↦ ε σ(X) ε⁻¹` is semisimple over the base field. A positive exponent with σ to that power equal to the identity suffices. This is the form of the semisimple orbit-closure result for the components of the cyclic matrix extension. [Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, Lemma 8, p. 156](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)

On the column space the map from a matrix to its product with coefficientwise σ is an injective base-linear map into the base-field endomorphism space. The semilinear covariance identity takes twisted conjugation to ordinary conjugation by coefficient-linear units. The coefficient algebra is semisimple by finite dimension and reducedness, so the semilinear orbit-closure theorem supplies a semisimple invertible operator in the image closure. Finite-dimensional closed embedding returns a matrix in the matrix orbit closure. It is invertible since the semilinear operator differs from it by a coefficient automorphism of the column space. The embedding of the unit group into the matrix algebra then places it in the orbit closure in `GL_n`.

Conjugation by the limiting linear automorphism acts semisimply on the base-field endomorphisms. The coefficient-linear endomorphisms intertwine this conjugation with the twisted matrix adjoint. Semisimplicity passes through this injective intertwiner.
