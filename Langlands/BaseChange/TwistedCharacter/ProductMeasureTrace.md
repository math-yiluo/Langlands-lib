# Twisted traces on product tests with a supplied Haar measure

`ProductMeasureTwistedTrace` expresses the twisted trace on a product of
Schwartz–Bruhat test functions as a product of factor traces, times a
nonzero scalar depending only on the Haar measures. There are finitely
many factors, each a second-countable locally compact Hausdorff totally
disconnected topological group. The factors and their product carry their
Borel measurable structures and supplied left Haar measures. The factor
representations are admissible complex
representations in a common carrier universe. Their endomorphisms are
arbitrary linear maps, and the endomorphism on the external tensor product
is their tensor product.

The proof identifies the two Borel measurable structures on the product
and writes the supplied measure as a positive multiple of the product
measure by Haar uniqueness. The integrated action scales by that multiple.
Taking the finite-rank trace preserves the scalar, and
`twistedDistribution_external_piTensor` supplies the identity for the
product measure. The scalar is independent of the representations,
endomorphisms and tests.

The trace convention is that of Rogawski, *Trace Paley–Wiener theorem in the
twisted case* (1988), §5, printed pp. 218–219. The formula follows from
the Library's external tensor trace and Haar measure uniqueness.
