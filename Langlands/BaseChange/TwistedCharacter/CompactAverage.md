# Algebraic averaging of operators over compact spaces

This module supplies the compact integration used in the adaptation of Harish-Chandra, “A submersion principle and its applications” (1981), §4, printed pp. 98–100. The finite-fiber construction and the precise trace comparison are internal lemmas for that argument.

On each vector, a pointwise locally constant family of linear endomorphisms on a compact space gives a simple function. Its finite sum integral is linear in the vector and gives the scalar Bochner integral after applying any algebraic linear functional. If all images lie in a subspace, the average has image there by testing against the annihilator. Restriction to a common finite-dimensional subspace commutes with averaging, and the diagonal entries in a basis show that its trace commutes with averaging.
