# Semisimple descent of the character on a cyclic matrix extension

Let `F` be a characteristic-zero nonarchimedean local field and `E` a finite cyclic `F`-algebra with its module topology. Let `s` be an adjoint semisimple element of the cyclic extension `H` of `GL_n(E)`, and let `S` be the matrix algebra fixed by its adjoint automorphism `α`. For an admissible representation of `H` and any left Haar measure `ν` on `H`, `CyclicGLSemisimpleDescentStatement` constructs a slice distribution `T` and an invariant distribution `T₀` on `S`. The source group measure `μ` is the pullback of `ν` under `g ↦ i(g)s`, where `i` embeds `GL_n(E)` in `H`. The source slice measure is the restriction of an additive Haar measure on `S` to the open domain `U` of the map `(g,Y) ↦ g(1+Y)α(g)⁻¹`.

The theorem identifies the pullback of the actual coset character, by the test pushforward of the displayed map, with the tensor of Haar integration for `μ` and `T`. It gives a clopen neighborhood `C` of zero contained in `U`. The ambient distribution `T₀` is supported on `C` and agrees with `T` on zero extensions of slice tests supported in `C`. The adjoint automorphism on units is conjugation by `s` under `i`, which gives the normalized twisted action on the coset character. Right invariance of Haar on `GL_n(E)` and invariance of the fixed-algebra trace pairing provide the two measure symmetries. The Haar tensor identity and equivariant clopen cutoff then give the stated distributions. The construction uses the additive chart `1+Y`. [Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, §3.4, pp. 154–155](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)
