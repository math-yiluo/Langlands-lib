# Haar measure in a finite semidirect extension

Let \(G\) be a locally compact second-countable Hausdorff topological group
and \(C\) a finite discrete group. Give \(G\rtimes C\) the product topology,
assumed to make it a topological group, and give both groups their Borel
measurable spaces. `finiteSemidirectHaar` proves that a left Haar measure
on \(G\rtimes C\) is right invariant if its pullback to the open copy of \(G\)
is right invariant.

Right translation by an element of the open subgroup already preserves the
ambient measure. Consequently its modular character is one there. Every
element of the semidirect product raised to the order of \(C\) lies in the
open subgroup, by applying the projection to \(C\). The modular character
takes positive real values, so its value on the original element is also
one. This gives right invariance of the ambient Haar measure.

In the application to a finite cyclic component group, this allows compact
conjugation tests to be carried out in the whole semidirect group. See
Arthur and Clozel, *Simple algebras, base change, and the advanced theory
of the trace formula*, Chapter 1, Proposition 2.2, pp. 11–12, for the
passage to the cyclic component.
