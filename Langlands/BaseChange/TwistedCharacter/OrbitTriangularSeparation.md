# Triangular separation of the orbit of a σ-discrete series

Over a `p`-adic field `E` with topological data `T`, let `σ` be an
automorphism with `σ ^ d = 1` for some `d > 0`, with the supplied
`F`-module topology, and write `θ = entrywiseAut σ n`. Let `τ` satisfy `IsSigmaDiscreteSeries σ n τ`
on a carrier in universe `z`, and let `μ` be a Haar measure on `GL_n(E)`,
`n > 0`.

- `headRankMeasure a` is the natural number that measures the rank order
  of a multisegment `a` within its cuspidal content, as in the proof of
  `wellFounded_rankLT`. `headRankMeasure_lt` says that it strictly
  decreases along `RankLE` between distinct multisegments.
- `disjoint_twistedOrbitSpan_otherTempered_sup_properInduced`: the orbit
  span `twistedOrbitSpan μ θ τ` is disjoint from
  `twistedOtherEssentiallyTemperedSpan μ θ τ ⊔ sigmaProperInducedSpan σ μ`.
  The proper-induction span includes all irreducible admissible σ-stable
  inducing data on proper standard Levis, tempered or not.

## Proof

Index lines by `StableIrreducibleClass θ` in universe `max v z`. The lines
`x c` are the class traces, which are linearly independent
(`linearIndependent_stableIrreducibleClass_trace`). For each class choose
a σ-stable ordered tempered datum `tc c` with Langlands head the class
representative (`exists_sigmaStable_temperedLanglandsHead`) and block
intertwiners. Also choose its head multisegment and the order on the head
kernel (`temperedLanglandsHeadOrder`). The standard lines `y c` are the
σ-stable standard traces of `tc c`. The rank of a class is the
`headRankMeasure` of its multisegment.

1. Triangularity. `y c - a • x c` lies in the span of the `x` of strictly
   smaller rank, for some `a ≠ 0` (`twistedDistribution_sub_smul_head_mem`).
   Every irreducible subquotient of the head kernel has a head
   multisegment of strictly smaller rank. Multisegment head
   classification identifies it with the multisegment of its class.
2. Let `P` be the classes in the invariant unramified orbit of `τ` and `Q`
   its complement. `triangularSpanSeparation` gives
   `Disjoint (span (x '' P)) (span (y '' Q))`.
3. The orbit span lies in `span (x '' P)`: lift carriers from universe `z`
   to `max v z` and use the class lines.
4. The other essentially tempered span lies in `span (y '' Q)`. Its
   generators have one-block data (`shape_length_eq_one_of_twist_tempered`),
   so the standard module is irreducible and equivalent to the head. Its
   trace is therefore a multiple of `x c`, and also of `y c`.
5. The proper-induction span lies in `span (y '' Q)`. By
   `sigmaProperInducedSpan_le_span_flatRealFamilySet` it suffices to treat
   a block family on at least two blocks at real exponents. By
   `exists_standardTrace_eq_smul_blockFamilyTrace` such a family is a
   nonzero multiple of the standard trace of a grouped datum `t`. Its
   Langlands head `π` exists (`glRegularLanglandsHeadExistence`) and is
   σ-stable (`nonempty_twistingEquiv_of_isIrreducibleHead`). It lies in
   `Q` by `not_isUnramifiedTwistOf_groupedHead`. By
   `standardTrace_eq_smul_of_head`, the standard trace of `t` is a
   multiple of `y` of the class of `π`.

In Henniart–Lemaire (2011), the separation needed for pseudo-coefficients
of σ-discrete series is carried out in Grothendieck groups of
σ-representations, with the following steps.

- II.5.10, pp. 108–109. The Proposition decomposes the Grothendieck group
  `𝒢(G(E),σ)` as the direct sum of the lines `Z(Π̃_ξ, Ã_ξ)` of standard
  σ-representations. Here `ξ` runs over the Σ-orbits of isomorphism
  classes of all σ-triplets of Langlands. The Corollaire writes
  `𝒢(G(E),σ)` as the sum of the lines of the σ-stable σ-triplets and the
  subgroup `𝒢_{>0}(G(E),σ)`. That subgroup is generated by the irreducible
  σ-representations `(Π, A)` with `s(Π) > 1`.
- II.5.11, p. 109, and Lemme 2, pp. 111–112. The Proposition of II.5.10
  gives `𝒢(G(E),σ) = 𝒢_{e.t}(G(E),σ) ⊕ 𝒢^L_{ind}(G(E),σ)`. The two
  subgroups are generated by the essentially tempered irreducible
  σ-representations and by the standard σ-representations of induced
  σ-triplets. Lemme 2 states that their images in the quotient
  `Ḡ(G(E),σ) = 𝒢(G(E),σ) / 𝒢_{0+}(G(E),σ)` again form a direct sum.
  Twisted traces factor through that quotient (II.5.4, Remarque).
- II.5.13, pp. 113–114. An element `Φ` of `𝒢^{*}_0(G(E),σ)` (II.5.7,
  p. 105) is built that factors through `Ḡ(G(E),σ)`. It is defined on
  the essentially tempered part from the orbit, is zero on the other
  essentially tempered classes, and is extended by zero on the induced
  part by Lemme 2 of II.5.11. A proper induction from tempered data either lies in the
  induced part (Cas 1), or is an unramified twist of an irreducible
  tempered induction that is not essentially σ-discrete (Cas 2).

The Lean proof uses neither Grothendieck groups nor the quotient
`Ḡ(G(E),σ)`. It works in the space of distributions on `GL_n(E)`, with
the twisted traces of stable irreducible classes
(`StableIrreducibleClass.trace`) and σ-stable standard traces as lines.
Linear independence of the class traces and the triangular relation
between the two families give the disjointness of the spans.
