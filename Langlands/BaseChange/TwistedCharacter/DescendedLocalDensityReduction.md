# Twisted integrability from a density on the descended slice

`CyclicGLDescendedLocalDensityStatement` formulates the local summability input on the actual fixed matrix algebra at an adjoint semisimple point of a cyclic extension. It concerns an irreducible admissible complex representation of the extension and its character for an arbitrary left Haar measure. The slice data are the test pushforward for normalized conjugation, the Haar tensor identity, and the invariant distribution on the fixed algebra obtained by restricting to a clopen neighborhood of zero. The asserted density is locally integrable for the chosen additive Haar measure and represents that distribution on all Schwartz–Bruhat tests supported in some open neighborhood of zero.

`twistedCharacterFromDescendedLocalDensity` derives the published twisted integrability proposition from this input. It obtains the slice data from semisimple descent. The translated subgroup is clopen, so the recovery theorem carries the slice density to the ambient character near the semisimple point. Haar uniqueness gives the same local density for every left Haar measure. Semisimple globalization and the regular-value theorem then yield the twisted density with local constancy on the regular locus. The argument keeps the universes of the field, cyclic algebra and representation space independent. [Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, §3.4, pp. 154–156](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)
