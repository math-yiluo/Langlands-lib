# Haar density in full and upper triangular matrix coordinates

This internal construction applies the compact group Haar comparison to the source of twisted conjugation. The two charts take the matrix entries of a compact open subgroup of the full general linear group and a compact open subgroup of the upper triangular group. They take values in the full and upper triangular matrix algebras.

An algebra finite over a Hausdorff topological field acts on itself by left multiplication. In a basis this gives an injective linear map into square matrices. A linear left inverse is continuous for the module topologies, so the map is a topological embedding and induces an embedding on units. On the matrix ring, the locus of units is open because its determinant is nonzero, and matrix inversion is continuous there by the adjugate formula. This makes the matrix unit value map an open embedding. Left multiplication reflects invertibility, so pulling back the open range of the matrix unit value map gives the open range of the finite algebra's unit value map.

The upper triangular subgroup is identified with the units of Mathlib's block triangular subalgebra for the identity block index. A group element and its inverse both lie in that algebra. The chart on the upper triangular subgroup takes the same entries as the full matrix chart and has the induced embedding. Its image is the preimage of the unit locus under inclusion of the subalgebra, so it is open. Restricting the charts to the two compact open subgroups and taking their product gives `compactMatrixHaarChart`.

`MatrixHaarChartStatement` uses a nonarchimedean local field and a finite commutative algebra with its module topology as coefficients. The proof gets local compactness and second countability for the full matrix space from finite coordinates. The triangular space inherits these properties because its defining entry equations give a closed subtype. For any additive Haar measure on their product, the generic comparison supplies a locally constant nonnegative real density whose topological support is exactly the chart image. Its density measure equals the pushforward of the two normalized compact group Haar measures. All four factors use their Borel measurable structures.
