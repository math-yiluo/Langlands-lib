# Recovering a character density from the semisimple slice

Let `R` be a complete finite-dimensional normed algebra over a proper nontrivially normed field. Assume its norm topology is locally compact, second countable and totally disconnected, and that its unit value map is an open embedding. Let `j` embed `Rˣ` as a clopen subset of a second-countable locally compact Hausdorff totally disconnected space `H`. Use the Borel structures, a measure `ν` on `H` finite on compact sets and positive on nonempty open sets, the Haar measure `ν.comap j` on `Rˣ`, and an additive Haar measure `ρ` on the fixed algebra of `α`.

`SemisimpleDensityRecoveryStatement` assumes test pushforward for normalized conjugation, the Haar tensor identity for that pullback of the ambient distribution, and agreement of the open-slice distribution with an ambient fixed-algebra distribution on tests supported in an open neighborhood of zero. A locally integrable density of the fixed-algebra distribution near zero then gives a density of the original distribution near `j 1`. Its representative is integrable for `ν` and gives equality on all Schwartz–Bruhat tests supported in the resulting open neighborhood.

The proof cuts the slice density off on a compact clopen neighborhood inside the two domains of the slice identities. The comap change of variables restricts this density to the open slice, and Fubini gives a locally integrable density for the tensor with Haar. Zero extension along `j` is the test pushforward for `ν.comap j` and `ν`. Composing it with the normalized conjugation pushforward gives the map that sends the pair consisting of the identity and zero to `j 1`. The local density transfer for test pushforward applies there. This recovers the ambient density in the semisimple descent step. [Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, §3.4, pp. 154–155](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)
