# Additive coordinates for Haar measure of a compact group of units

This internal measure comparison supplies the compact source measures for twisted compact averaging. Suppose a compact topological group maps homomorphically into the units of a locally compact second-countable Hausdorff topological ring, and the map taking each group element to its ring value is an open embedding. Both group and ring carry their Borel measurable structures. The image is compact and open, so any additive Haar measure gives it finite positive mass.

Left multiplication by an image unit is a continuous additive automorphism. It preserves the image of the group, so its pushforward of additive Haar gives that image the same mass as the original measure. Haar uniqueness forces the scalar between the two additive Haar measures to be one. The pullback of additive Haar to the compact group is therefore left invariant. Its total mass is the additive mass of the image. The proof applies Haar uniqueness on the compact group and then pushes the normalized measure back into the ring.

The resulting measure has a locally constant density equal to the reciprocal of the image mass on the image and zero elsewhere. Its topological support equals the compact open image. `CompactOpenHaarChartStatement` gives the corresponding result for two such embeddings and an arbitrary additive Haar measure on the product ring. The proof applies the single-ring comparison to the product ring and uses Haar uniqueness to identify probability Haar on the product group with the product of the two probability Haar measures. The four carrier universes are independent.
