# Proper and one-block standard twisted traces

Use an ordered tempered datum over a \(p\)-adic field with its supplied
module topology over the base field of `σ`. A stable standard trace with
at least two blocks belongs to the proper induction span for every
inducing carrier universe. Incorporate the invariant Levi half modulus into the
external tensor of the twisted blocks. The unnormalized induced action
and its twisting operator then give the same normalized standard trace.
Admissibility and irreducibility of the inducing action follow from
the block properties and character twisting. The separable Levi permits
transport to the carrier universe used to define the span. The trace
uses a left-invariant measure finite on compact sets.

For a single block, its standard module is equivalent to the determinant
power twist of its tempered block, transported by the singleton block
reindexing, so the standard module is irreducible. The inverse of the
determinant character is invariant under the field
automorphism and trivial on compact subgroups, and twisting by it
recovers a tempered representation.

These are the two types of stable standard traces in Henniart–Lemaire
(2011), II.5.11 and II.5.13.
