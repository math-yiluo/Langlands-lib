# Test integration for semisimple conjugation

Let `R` be a finite-dimensional complete ultrametric normed algebra over a proper nontrivially normed field, with a second-countable locally compact totally disconnected topology. Use the Borel structures on the algebra, its units and the fixed algebra of `α`, and assume the units value map is an open embedding. For the fixed-algebra domain `adjointConjugationDomain α`, the map `adjointConjugationOnUnits` takes `(g,Y)` to the unit `g(1+Y)α(g)⁻¹`. This is the conjugation map for a center with adjoint `α` after right translation by the inverse of that center. For any unit-group Haar measure and additive Haar measure on the fixed algebra, `semisimpleDescentPushforward` supplies the complex-linear test pushforward with the product of those measures on the source and group Haar on the target. The slice measure is the pullback of its additive Haar measure to the open subspace. This is the integration map used to pull a distribution back along the semisimple conjugation submersion. [Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, §3.4, p. 154](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)

The proof applies the additive submersion result to the open product chart in the ambient vector spaces. The source measure in that application is the product additive Haar measure, and the target measure is additive Haar on the ambient algebra. Pulling it back along the product chart gives the product of the corresponding pullbacks on each factor. The target open chart for units and the supported pushforward identity transport the tests to the unit group. The inverse left-multiplication modulus gives a positive locally constant weight converting additive chart measure to a left Haar measure, and Haar uniqueness supplies the scalar for the given measure. Absorbing these weights into the source and target test maps proves the formula. Harish-Chandra, *A submersion principle and its applications*, §3, p. 97.
