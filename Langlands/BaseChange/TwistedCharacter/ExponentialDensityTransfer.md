# Returning an additive density through the exponential chart

`ExponentialDensityTransferStatement` compares two distributions in a chart consisting of a homeomorphism from an open additive source to a compact space and an open embedding of the compact space into the target coordinates. Assume the homeomorphism preserves the measures pulled back from the ambient space and that the two distributions agree on tests transported through this chart. A density on the additive source, supplied by a locally integrable function, then gives a density on the image in the target coordinates.

The proof extends the transported density by zero outside the compact image. Its integrability follows from integrability on the compact source and equality of the pulled-back measures. Any test supported in the target image is the extension of its restriction to the compact chart. Changing variables on the two open embeddings expresses both evaluations using the same measure and the same test on that chart. For exponential congruence charts the target embedding is the coordinate `m − 1`.

This is the measure-preserving transport of the local density back from the Lie algebra in the semisimple descent argument. [Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, §3.4, pp. 155–156](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)
