# Interpolation on finitely many character-twist families

Let `p` be a locally constant group homomorphism from the test group
to an abelian degree group. Characters of the degree group separate
its points, and their pullbacks act by multiplication on tests.
Suppose the finitely many base distributions are invariant under
left translations by a subgroup `C` whose degree image has finite
index. `FiniteTwistFamilyInterpolationStatement` asserts that a
linear functional with finite character expansions on these twist
families is evaluation at one test on the entire families.

The proof uses finite character interpolation on the parameter group
to extract the coefficient at each degree. The same functional
evaluated on a twist by a character of the finite degree quotient
gives that coefficient times the character at the chosen degree.
On the finite-dimensional span of those twisted distributions, the
functional is evaluation at a test. Fourier averaging restricts the test
to the prescribed coset. Splitting its compact support into individual
degrees and translating each part by an element of `C` makes all those
degrees equal. These translations preserve the values on the base
distributions. The resulting test gives one monomial on each twist
family, and the finite sum gives the desired evaluations.

The finite unramified orbit interpolation of Bernstein–Deligne–Kazhdan,
*Trace Paley-Wiener theorem for reductive p-adic groups* (1986),
§4.2, pp. 184–185, motivates this formulation. The finite degree
quotient plays the role of the finite fibres of restriction to
central characters. The argument applies to twisted trace
distributions satisfying the stated translation and degree hypotheses.
