# Local constancy of the twisted operator from compact pushforwards

This internal result adapts the compact averaging argument of Harish-Chandra, “A submersion principle and its applications” (1981), §3, Lemma 1 and §4, Theorem 2, printed pp. 97–100. The hypotheses specify a compact subgroup and Cartan representatives covering the group, a uniform contraction into open subgroups, and local constancy of compact pushforward measures. The twist is a continuous automorphism with a compatible endomorphism of the representation.

Smoothness and compactness give a finite orbit of a nonzero vector under the compact subgroup. An open subgroup fixes this finite set. Uniform contraction then supplies one compact open subgroup of the auxiliary subgroup that fixes every vector obtained by applying a Cartan representative to this set.

On each such fixed vector, the twisting relation allows an extra probability Haar average over that auxiliary compact open subgroup. After applying an algebraic linear functional, Fubini gives the scalar integral against the compact pushforward measure. Equality of the measures gives equality of the operators on all these vectors on a single neighborhood. Equivariance under the compact subgroup extends equality to the full orbit, which spans by irreducibility.
