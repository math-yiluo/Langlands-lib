# Entrywise action on the parabolic orbit subgroups

Fix standard block parabolics of shapes `B` and `C` in `GL_n(E)` and an
`F`-algebra automorphism `σ` of `E`. The algebraic calculations apply over
commutative rings. For a permutation representative fixed by `σ`, the
stabilizer in the target parabolic is invariant. Its defining condition is that
conjugation by the inverse representative lands in the source parabolic.
Entrywise action commutes with this conjugation and preserves the source
parabolic. This also makes conjugation followed by the source Levi projection
commute with the source Levi action. The target Levi section commutes with
entrywise action on the diagonal blocks, which gives invariance of the
intersection subgroup in the target Levi.

In the local-field setting with the `F`-module topology on `E`,
`parabolicOrbitNestedKernelAut` transports the restriction of the continuous
entrywise action on the target parabolic to its radical along
`parabolicOrbitNestedRadicalEquiv`. The nested intersection is the preimage of
the stabilizer under the inclusion into the target parabolic. Its invariance
is therefore the stabilizer membership equivalence.

These identities describe the fixed orbit geometry used in Rogawski's proof
of Lemma 8.1, *Trace Paley–Wiener theorem in the twisted case*, Trans. Amer.
Math. Soc. 309 (1988), p. 222.
