# From Fourier coset vanishing to twisted character integrability

`CyclicDescendedDensityFromFourierStatement` gives a conditional local density theorem for the actual descended distributions. Its three premises are small exponential product straightening for complete ultrametric normed algebras, compact conjugacy containment of the unit characteristic sublevel together with characteristic conic finiteness for finite-dimensional semisimple local algebras, and Harish-Chandra's local summability statement for Fourier transforms of invariant distributions.

`cyclicDescendedDensityFromFourier` constructs an invariant distribution in exponential coordinates. The invariant trace pairing gives a self-dual bicharacter and preserves the additive Haar measure under conjugation. The Fourier transform therefore remains invariant. First the conic restriction theorem chooses a cone, and then the high frequency calculation gives vanishing outside that cone on the cosets of the annihilator of a small ball. Conic restriction gives a compact-conjugacy-supported distribution with the same evaluations on the tests invariant under an enlarged lattice. The double-annihilator theorem and Harish-Chandra's summability premise give a density on the annihilator of that lattice. A smaller exponential chart preserves Haar measure in the slice coordinates and transfers the density to an open set containing zero. [Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, §§3.4 and 3.9](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)

`twistedCharacterFromFourier` composes that local density theorem with the semisimple descent and recovery implication. Its conclusion is `TwistedCharacterLocalIntegrabilityStatement` with the original three universes, under the same three premises.
