# Exact supercuspidal support of constituents of induction after Jacquet

Over a p-adic field with its supplied topology, let an irreducible
representation of positive-rank GL have exact supercuspidal support \(s\).
After normalized Jacquet restriction to any standard block Levi and
normalized induction back, every irreducible subquotient has support \(s\).
The carriers are in `Type (max v z)`, where `v` is the field universe.

Realize the input representation as a subquotient of the normalized
induction of a tensor product of supercuspidal factors representing \(s\).
The Levi tensor is irreducible and has zero Jacquet modules along proper
refinements. Lift its carrier to the universe of the constituent-support
theorem, using the induced equivalence to identify the cuspidal products.
The constituent-support theorem for induction after Jacquet places each
constituent in the original cuspidal induction. The same ordered family
of supercuspidal factors supplies its support.

This is the ordinary support preservation used for the operators
\(i_M r_M\) in Rogawski (1988), proof of Proposition 11.2. The proof uses the
constituent-level consequence of the geometric lemma for quasicuspidal
inductions. It records the exact cuspidal multiset, including multiplicities.
