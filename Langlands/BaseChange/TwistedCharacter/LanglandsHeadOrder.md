# Strict descent for constituents of a tempered standard head kernel

`TemperedLanglandsHeadOrderStatement` identifies the multisegment of the
irreducible head of an ordered tempered standard module. Every irreducible
subquotient of the head kernel has a strictly smaller multisegment in the
Library's well-founded rank order.

Refine the tempered data into a precedence-ordered product of special
segments without changing the induced object. The support-adjusted
Aubert operator sends the class of this product to the segment monomial
and the class of each Langlands head to the corresponding Zelevinsky
label. The ordinary composition multiplicity is therefore the monomial
coefficient of that label. The Library's rank-order form of Zelevinsky
(1980), Theorem 7.1, bounds the label and gives coefficient one at the
head. Additivity along the actual head quotient leaves no occurrence of
the head label in the kernel.

The resulting strict descent supplies a well-founded induction for
the standard trace expansion in Henniart–Lemaire (2011), II.5.10.
This calculation concerns ordinary constituent support and multiplicity.
