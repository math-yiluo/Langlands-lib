# The distribution on the semisimple slice

Let `R` be a finite-dimensional complete normed algebra over a proper nontrivially normed field, and let `α` be an algebra automorphism. Write `S` for its fixed algebra and `U` for `adjointConjugationDomain α`. Assume the unit group is Hausdorff, locally compact and totally disconnected. Use second-countable topologies and Borel structures on the unit group and `S`. The proof uses a bi-invariant Haar measure `μ` on the unit group and a Borel measure `ν` on `S` with s-finite pullback to `U`. The measure invariance required of `ν` is preservation by each fixed-unit conjugation homeomorphism.

Suppose `D` on the unit group is invariant under `x ↦ g x α(g)⁻¹`. For the Haar test pushforward `P` of the normalized conjugation map, `SemisimpleDescentDistributionStatement` gives `D.comp P = μ ⊗ T` for a distribution `T` on `U` invariant under fixed-unit conjugation. The product formula is the normalization of the descended distribution for the chosen measures. In additive coordinates it uses `1+Y` on the slice, and the semisimple center has been removed by right translation. This is the distribution construction underlying integration along the conjugation fibers. [Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, §3.4, p. 154](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)

The proof compares two actions on the source product. Left multiplication in the first coordinate intertwines the map with the adjoint action on the translated component. Haar preservation and the pushforward covariance give left invariance of `D.comp P` in the first factor. Haar tensor factorization then gives `T`. Right translation by the inverse of a fixed unit in the first coordinate, combined with conjugation by that unit in the second, leaves the image point unchanged. The product measures are invariant under this action, so the tensor factorization passes its symmetry to `T`.
