# Adjoint actions and compact frequency sets in cyclic descent

`cyclicAdjointCompactSaturation` uses a topological field and a commutative
algebra with its topological ring and module topology. For any compact
subset \(\omega\) of the matrix algebra, the adjoint images of its points
under the entire cyclic semidirect product are contained in the unit
conjugation saturation of one compact set. Take the union of the images
of \(\omega\) under the finitely many entrywise component automorphisms.
They are continuous for the module topology, so this union is compact.
The cyclic adjoint applies the entrywise automorphism and then conjugates
by the unit component. In particular the orbit of a compact dual lattice
has the compact saturation bound used in the high-frequency argument.

The inverse adjoint induces inverse conjugation on the unit subgroup.
At a translated fixed unit \(s i(m)\), the adjoint on the algebra sends
\(x\) to \(m\alpha(x)m^{-1}\), where \(\alpha\) is the adjoint of \(s\).
The proof uses the unit conjugation identity to commute \(s\) and \(i(m)\)
and then uses the multiplicativity of the cyclic linear adjoint. The
regular trace bicharacter is invariant under every cyclic adjoint
because left multiplication is transported by algebra automorphisms.
These comparisons identify the adjoints in the compact intertwining
calculation and the transverse estimate. [Clozel 1987, §§3.6–3.7, printed pp. 158–164](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)
