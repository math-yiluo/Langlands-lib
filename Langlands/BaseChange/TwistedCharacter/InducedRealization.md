# Changing the carrier universe of actual twisted inducing data

An irreducible smooth representation of a separable Levi has an
equivalent realization in any prescribed carrier universe. Transport
the inducing action and conjugate its twisting operator by that
equivalence. The induced equivalence is postcomposition on sections.
It commutes with the induced twisting operators, whose defining section
formula is the one in Henniart–Lemaire (2011), II.4.4.

Consequently the twisted distribution is preserved. An actual proper
induced trace from stable irreducible admissible data belongs to the
proper-induction span defined using carriers in the prescribed universe.
The traces use the same left-invariant Borel measure finite on compact
sets and assume admissibility of the actual induction. Admissibility
of its transported copy follows from the induced equivalence.
