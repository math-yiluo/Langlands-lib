# Fixed algebras of semisimple adjoint operators

Let `B` be a finite-dimensional semisimple associative algebra over a field of characteristic zero. For an algebra automorphism `α` that is semisimple as a linear operator, `isSemisimpleRing_adjointFixedAlgebra` proves that its fixed subalgebra is semisimple. `isSemisimpleRing_twistedAdjointFixedAlgebra` applies this to the twisted adjoint of an invertible matrix over a finite reduced commutative coefficient algebra. The fixed algebra gives the centralizer directions in the semisimple slice. [Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, §3.4, pp. 154–155](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)

`leftRegularTrace` takes the trace of left multiplication on `B`. For a nonzero `x`, project the left regular module onto its principal left ideal generated by `x`. The image of `1` is a nonzero idempotent `y x` for some `y`. Left multiplication by this idempotent has nonzero trace in characteristic zero, and cyclicity also gives nonzero trace for `x y`. This proves separation of the ambient trace pairing. Conjugating the left multiplication operator shows that an algebra automorphism preserves the trace.

A semisimple automorphism gives an invariant complement to the fixed subspace, and `1 − α` is invertible on the complement. The complement pairs to zero with each fixed vector by trace invariance, so the restriction of the pairing to the fixed subalgebra is nondegenerate. The Jacobson radical of the fixed subalgebra is nilpotent because the subalgebra is finite dimensional. Products of radical elements with fixed elements therefore have zero ambient trace. Separation forces the radical to vanish, which proves semisimplicity. The module also supplies the symmetric nondegenerate pairing `adjointFixedTracePairing` and proves its invariance under conjugation by units of the fixed algebra.
