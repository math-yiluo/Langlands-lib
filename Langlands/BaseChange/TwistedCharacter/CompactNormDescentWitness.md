# Compact trace witnesses for a normalized semisimple descent

Let \(A\) be a complete, proper, second-countable ultrametric normed
algebra of finite dimension over a proper, nontrivially normed ultrametric
field of characteristic zero. Give \(A\) a compact open exhaustion and take
its unit group to be second countable. Embed the units openly by \(i\)
into a locally compact totally disconnected Hausdorff group \(H\).
Let \(s\) act on the units through an algebra automorphism \(\alpha\).
Use bi-invariant Haar measure on \(H\), additive Haar measure on the fixed
algebra, and the Borel structures. Let \(T\) be a slice distribution with
the normalized semisimple descent tensor identity for the character of
an irreducible admissible representation of \(H\).

Assume exponential product straightening on \(A\) and an invariant
self-dual bicharacter there. For sufficiently small ambient and slice
norm levels, suppose \(T\) evaluates nontrivially on the extension of a
smooth finite-dimensional slice trace in the unit-minus-one chart.
`compactNormDescentWitness` gives a smooth finite-dimensional ambient
compact type with its enlarged congruence orbit and exponential trace
formula. It has a nonzero intertwiner with the slice type on the slice
intersection. At a translated slice point its ambient trace test has
nonzero character evaluation, and the type intertwines with its conjugate
on the intersection with the conjugate ambient norm subgroup. For each
open subgroup with a nonzero fixed vector in the ambient representation,
the compact type has a nonzero fixed vector on the intersection with
some conjugate of that subgroup. Such a fixed-vector subgroup can be
chosen to be an ambient norm level of arbitrarily small radius.

The compact conjugation pushforward evaluates as the compact conjugator
volume times the slice evaluation. Smooth the nonzero pushforward test
by the finite norm level trace expansion and apply the descent pairing
to a summand with nonzero evaluation. This supplies the slice
intertwiner and the nonzero translate. The translated trace theorem and
compact-type admissibility give the other two intersections. See Clozel,
*Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, Lemma 5 and
§3.6. [Original publication, printed pp. 154, 158–159](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)
