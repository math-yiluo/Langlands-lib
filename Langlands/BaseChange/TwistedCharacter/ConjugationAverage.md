# Conjugation averages for smooth representations

This module implements the conjugation averaging in Harish-Chandra, “A submersion principle and its applications” (1981), §4, printed pp. 98–100. Its statements about arbitrary endomorphisms and integrated operators are internal steps of the twisted-character argument.

The two orbit maps occurring in conjugation are locally constant on each vector by smoothness. Changing variables by left multiplication in the normalized Haar integral gives equivariance under the compact subgroup. For an integrated test function, bi-invariance bounds the image by a space of fixed vectors. A smaller compact open subgroup normalized by the averaging subgroup gives a common finite-dimensional image bound for all conjugates. The finite-rank trace then commutes with averaging and is unchanged by each conjugation.
