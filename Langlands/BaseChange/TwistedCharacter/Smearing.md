# Local character values from constant operator averages

This internal step adapts the trace argument in Harish-Chandra, “A submersion principle and its applications” (1981), §4, printed pp. 98–100, to a smooth representation with a twisting endomorphism.

For any vector and algebraic linear functional, scalarization expresses the conjugation average of the integrated operator as an iterated integral. The scalar kernel is continuous by smoothness and has compact support because the averaging subgroup and the support of the test function are compact. Fubini gives the integral in the opposite order. When the operator average is constant on the support of the test function, this expresses the integrated operator average as the constant operator times the integral of the test function.

A compact clopen neighborhood inside the region of constancy has an indicator with nonzero Haar integral. Its integrated operator average has finite rank, so the constant operator has finite rank as well. For each Haar measure, the trace of the integrated operator average gives the twisted distribution there. Linearity of trace identifies its local value with the trace of the constant operator.
