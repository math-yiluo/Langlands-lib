# A clopen neighborhood for semisimple descent

In a finite-dimensional complete normed algebra over a complete nontrivially normed ultrametric field, suppose the linear map underlying `α` is semisimple. `SemisimpleClopenDomainStatement` gives a clopen neighborhood of zero in the fixed algebra `S`, contained in `adjointConjugationDomain α` and invariant under `Sˣ`. Such a neighborhood permits restricting a descended distribution and using extension by zero in additive coordinates. Clozel uses an invariant clopen domain with additional exponential-coordinate conditions in the Lie-algebra part of descent. [Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, §3.4, pp. 154–155](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)

The proof projects the ambient algebra to the fixed subspace along the image of `1−α`. Evaluating the conjugation differential on `(X, P X)` gives a continuous family of endomorphisms `T(Y)`. The complementary decomposition makes `T(0)` surjective. Fixed-unit conjugation preserves both summands and conjugates `T(Y)` to the corresponding endomorphism at the conjugated point, so their determinants agree. The determinant of left multiplication by `1+Y` on `S` is invariant as well and has value one at zero. The inverse images of clopen balls around the two nonzero determinant values give the desired neighborhood. Both determinants stay nonzero there, which gives the unit condition and the required surjective differential.
