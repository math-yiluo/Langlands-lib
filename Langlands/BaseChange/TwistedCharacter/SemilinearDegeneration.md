# Semisimple operators in closed semilinear conjugacy sets

Let a coefficient algebra act on a finite-dimensional vector space over a perfect nontrivially normed base field. Assume the vector space is a semisimple module over the coefficient algebra. `IsCoefficientSemilinear` fixes the coefficient automorphism in the scalar action equation, and `restrictCoefficientEnd` forgets coefficient linearity. Let a closed subset of base-field endomorphisms be stable under conjugation by coefficient-linear units and contain an invertible semilinear endomorphism `T`. If the coefficient automorphism has `l`th power the identity, `exists_semisimple_power_in_closed_conjugacyInvariant` finds an invertible semilinear endomorphism in the same set with semisimple `l`th power. Continuity of addition and base-field scalar multiplication is sufficient for the topology on endomorphisms. In characteristic zero and for positive `l`, `exists_semisimple_in_closed_conjugacyInvariant` gives a semisimple endomorphism with the same coefficient automorphism.

The proof takes a polynomial `P` giving the semisimple part of `T^l` and a squarefree annihilator of `P(T^l)`. The nilpotent part `N = T^l − P(T^l)` is coefficient-linear. Split its kernel as a coefficient submodule. Both `T` and its inverse preserve the kernel, so the diagonal-block algebra map gives an invertible semilinear operator in the original closed set by the conjugation limit. Polynomial identities are preserved by this algebra map. The nilpotent part vanishes on the kernel, and its next-to-last power has image in the kernel. After taking diagonal blocks its nilpotence exponent drops by one. Induction reaches a semisimple power. In characteristic zero, if the minimal polynomial `p` of an invertible operator's positive power is squarefree, `p(X^l)` is squarefree by the derivative chain rule, coprimality of `p` with its derivative, and `p(0) ≠ 0`. It annihilates the original operator.

This gives semisimple detection at the level of semilinear operators in the orbit-closure step of descent. [Clozel, *Characters of non-connected, reductive p-adic groups*, Lemma 8, p. 156](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)
