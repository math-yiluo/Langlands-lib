# Smoothing ambient tests by compact norm-unit trace sums

Let \(A\) be a second-countable proper finite-dimensional ultrametric
normed algebra with a compact open exhaustion over a complete, nontrivially
normed ultrametric field of characteristic zero. Embed \(A^\times\) openly
in a locally compact totally disconnected Hausdorff group \(H\). Use the
Borel structures on the algebra and the groups. Suppose the algebra has
a conjugation-invariant self-dual bicharacter and the small exponential
product straightening property.

`ambientNormTraceSmoothing` gives a positive radius bound below which
any ambient Schwartz–Bruhat test is fixed by right convolution with a
normalized finite sum of compact trace tests. The types are representations
of the image of the norm-unit subgroup at that radius. Each has the
exponential character formula for its orbit under the enlarged congruence
subgroup, with the representative in the annihilator of the refinement
level. The trace tests are extended by zero to \(H\).

Choose a compact open right stabilizer of the test and pull its neighborhood
of one back to the unit group. It contains a norm congruence subgroup, which
can be intersected with the original norm-unit subgroup. The finite trace
expansion of that level indicator transports through the open subgroup
image equivalence. Divide the trace sum by its positive integer coefficient
and the ambient Haar volume of the level. This is the normalized subgroup
indicator, whose right convolution fixes the test by the right averaging
identity. This gives the smoothing step for compact tests in Clozel's
Lemma 5. [Clozel 1987, §3.4, printed p. 154](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1987-008-3)
