# Heads of grouped standard data lie outside the orbit of a σ-discrete series

Over a `p`-adic field `E` with topological data `T`, let `L` be a shape
with at least two blocks carrying irreducible tempered σ-stable blocks
`δ`. Let `t` be an ordered tempered datum obtained by grouping equal
exponents. That is, a map `q` from the blocks of `L` to those of `t.shape`
has the following property: at every unitary parameter `z`, the twisted
normalized product over `L` is equivalent to that over `t.shape` (the
output of `exists_groupedTemperedDatum`). This module shows that the
Langlands head of `t` is never in the σ-invariant unramified orbit of a
σ-discrete series.

- `determinant_reindex_symm_eq`: reindexing a matrix by a bijection of
  `Fin` types preserves the determinant.
- `nonempty_standardModule_equiv_of_singleBlock`: if `t` has a single
  block with exponent `s`, its standard module is equivalent to the
  normalized product over `L` of the blocks `δ k` twisted by `|det|^s`.
  The proof has three steps.
  1. Specialize the unitary-point equivalence at `z = 1`, where the twists
     are trivial (`determinantUnramified_one`).
  2. Identify one-block normalized products with their block
     (`singleBlockNormalizedProductEquiv`).
  3. Move the determinant twist through the product
     (`normalizedProductDeterminantTwistEquiv`).
- `not_isUnramifiedTwistOf_groupedHead`: let `τ` satisfy
  `IsSigmaDiscreteSeries σ n τ` on a carrier in universe `w`, and let `π`
  be the Langlands head of `t`. Then `π` is not a σ-invariant unramified
  twist of `τ`. The proof has two cases.
  - Several groups. The representation `τ` is tempered, so an invariant
    unramified twist of `π` would be tempered.
    `shape_length_eq_one_of_twist_tempered` then forces `t` to have one
    block, a contradiction.
  - One group. The standard module is irreducible
    (`singleBlockStandard_irreducible_essentiallyTempered`), so it is
    equivalent to its head `π`. By the previous lemma it is the
    normalized product over `L`. That product is an unnormalized
    induction from the proper Levi of `L` of the half-modulus twist of the
    block tensor. That Levi representation is σ-stable, because it carries
    the tensor of the twisted block intertwiners. The Levi is
    second-countable, so the Levi representation has an equivalent
    realization on a carrier in universe `w`
    (`exists_irreducible_smoothRep_equiv_of_separable`). An unramified
    twist of `τ` would therefore be equivalent to a proper σ-stable
    induction on the carrier universe of `τ`. This contradicts
    `sigmaDiscreteOrbitNoninduction`.

The two cases follow the two cases in the proof of the Proposition of
Henniart–Lemaire (2011), II.5.13, p. 114. There one considers
`ι_α(Ξ_ψ, B_ψ)` for a tempered strongly irreducible `(Ξ, B)` on a proper
Levi `G_α(E)` and an unramified character `ψ`. The dichotomy is taken
from Henniart–Lemaire, *Pseudo-coefficients des séries κ-discrètes de
GL(n, F)*, Israel J. Math. 177 (2010), 1.3, Lemme 2.

- Cas 1: the image of `ι_α(Ξ_ψ)` in the Grothendieck group lies in the
  subgroup generated by the inductions of induced Langlands triplets.
  The Proposition of II.5.10 and the untwisted Langlands basis theorem
  then place `ι_α[Ξ_ψ, B_ψ]` in the induced part. Here
  this corresponds to several distinct exponents, where the head is not
  essentially tempered.
- Cas 2: there is a real unramified character `η` of `G(E)` such that
  `η⁻¹ ψ` is unitary on `G_α(E)`. Then `ι_α(Ξ_ψ, B_ψ)` is the `η`-twist
  of a strongly irreducible tempered induction, which is essentially
  tempered but not essentially σ-discrete. Here this corresponds to a
  single exponent.

The source argues with classes of induced representations in
Grothendieck groups. The theorem here concerns the Langlands head of the
grouped datum.
