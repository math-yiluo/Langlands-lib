# The socle of a representation with a twisting isomorphism

The Library socle is defined as the sum of images of irreducible embeddings.
These images are the atoms of the lattice of invariant subspaces. Their sum is
semisimple over the group algebra. In a nonzero Artinian representation, an atom
exists and is contained in the socle.

Let `A` intertwine a representation `ρ` with its pullback by `θ`. Composing an
irreducible embedding `π → ρ` with `A` gives an embedding of the pullback of `π`
by `θ⁻¹` into `ρ`. That representation is irreducible, so the composition has
image in the socle. Apply this argument also to the inverse twisting isomorphism
to show that `A` maps the socle onto itself.

These facts identify the subrepresentation used in a socle filtration for
finite-length twisted pairs. Such filtrations and additivity of twisted traces
underlie the passage to twisted irreducible classes in Henniart–Lemaire (2011),
II.5.2–II.5.4.
