# Visibility of unramified twists at a common compact open level

An unramified character is trivial on every compact subgroup, so twisting by
it preserves the space of vectors fixed by a compact open subgroup. Transport
along an equivalence preserves the existence of a nonzero fixed vector.

For each member of a finite family of nonzero smooth representations, take
an open stabilizer of a nonzero vector. Refine the intersection to a compact
open subgroup preserved by the inverse twisting automorphism. This subgroup
sees the entire invariant unramified twist orbit of each family member.
For a finite family of finite-length smooth representations, take all grades
of chosen composition series as the finite family of representatives. The
Library's `simpleSubquotientFactor` identifies each irreducible constituent
with one of these grades. The same level therefore sees all unramified
twists of the constituents.

For one orbit, the chosen subgroup ensures that every irreducible with zero
fixed space is outside the orbit. The other essentially tempered span is
already generated by characters outside the orbit. Their sum is therefore
contained in the span of irreducible characters outside the orbit, and
`disjoint_twistedSpectrumSpan` gives disjointness.

These consequences of smoothness, unramifiedness and twisted character
independence concern only spans generated by irreducible distributions.
Separation from the additional span of proper inductions requires information
on their twisted character expansions.
