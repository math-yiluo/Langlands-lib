# Localization of concordant test functions

This module supplies derived lemmas for the forward direction of Henniart–Lemaire (2011), II.2.9 and II.2.11. It uses the concordance relation of I.2.5 and the Library's quotient-space orbital integrals.

A clopen indicator times a test function is locally constant and has compact support. Its support is the intersection of the two supports. If the original test function is itself a compact clopen indicator, the product is the indicator of the intersection.

On each quotient representative the invariant parameter has the value at the orbit base point. On the entire quotient the cutoff therefore keeps the orbital integrand or makes it zero. This preserves vanishing at regular non-norms. For associated regular pairs, both cutoffs make the same choice, preserving equality for every compatible pair of quotient measures.

To localize the ordinary test function, take the image under the parameter map of the closed part of its support outside the desired open set. The image is compact and misses the distinguished parameter by the fiber hypothesis. A clopen neighborhood of that parameter in the complement removes the unwanted part of the support. This argument uses compactness of the transferred support and yields a cutoff on both test functions.
