# Rank-one sigma-lift conductors over a supplied p-adic base

`CuspidalSigmaLiftConductorRankOneOverPadicBaseStatement` gives the rank-one instance of the conductor identity on the original field carriers, with the finite p-adic structure supplied on the base. The extension field keeps its given topology and valuation. Its p-adic structure is constructed through the base algebra map.

The rank equality forces a single block of rank one, so the orbit-product equivalence makes the lift irreducible. The two representations admit rank-one character models. The distributional concordance identity identifies the upper character with the norm pullback. The cyclic conductor formula for those characters gives the sum of the principal-unit conductors of the norm-trivial twists. The character models identify those integers with the first fixed levels in the conductor statement.

The rank-one norm comparison follows the formulation of Henniart–Lemaire II.2.9, Remark (3), printed p. 70. The arithmetic formula is the exponent form of Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, (6.7), printed p. 60.
