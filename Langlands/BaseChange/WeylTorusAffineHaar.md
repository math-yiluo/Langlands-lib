# Additive Haar coordinates on a rational Weyl torus

The units of the centralizer subalgebra are the rational torus. A torus
point and its inverse both belong to the centralizer algebra, so the
unit inclusion is a topological group isomorphism onto the torus.
The underlying vector space is the kernel of the ordinary adjoint
defect.

Transport the compact open subgroup and its Haar measure to the algebra
units. Apply `unitsAffineHaar` there and carry the result back to the
rational Lie algebra. Subtracting one in the centralizer algebra gives
the same affine coordinate as subtracting one in the ambient matrices.
The restricted Haar measure in these coordinates is a positive multiple
of restricted additive Haar.
