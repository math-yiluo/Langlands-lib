# Associated spherical orbital descent at exact block norms

The ordinary and twisted orbit formulas use the same scalar. The product transport of block centralizer measures identifies the ambient Haar scales. The Jacobian on the upper radical agrees with the ordinary Jacobian at exact block norms, and the Levi half moduli agree blockwise. The twisted orbit integrand is continuous and compactly supported by the closed regular twisted orbit theorem. It is right spherical invariant because the entrywise field automorphism preserves the standard compact subgroup.

The quotient integration formula restricts the twisted orbit integrand to Levi and radical coordinates. The established twisted change of variables applies at each Levi conjugate, with the same Jacobian throughout. A finite spherical constant-term expansion and Fubini on the product of the two compatible quotient measures give the product of the block twisted orbital integrals. The proof chooses the ordinary and twisted block measures by the Library's orbital measure existence theorem and applies the ordinary formula with those same ordinary block measures. This proves the associated orbital descent premise used by the spherical reduction.

This is the two-block spherical case of the ordinary and twisted descent formulas in Laumon, Propositions 4.3.11 and 4.4.9. It uses the associated measures of Henniart–Lemaire, I.2.5. The resulting reduction to the Library's `FundamentalLemmaStatement` retains the `RegularEllipticFundamentalLemmaStatement` and `UnramifiedBaseChangeHomStatement` premises, with both field universes free. The regular elliptic case is the next step in the proof of Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, Theorem 4.5 and Henniart–Lemaire, I.6.6.
