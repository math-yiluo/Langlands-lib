# Unramified principal series detected by Borel Jacquet fixed vectors

An irreducible admissible \(p\)-adic representation with a nonzero
finite-dimensional integral-diagonal fixed space in its normalized Borel
Jacquet quotient admits an embedding into an actual unramified normalized
principal series. The compact diagonal subgroup is the subgroup of Levi
elements with each determinant of normalized absolute value one.

The proof selects a nonzero common generalized weight space of the
commuting operators on the compact fixed vectors and obtains a common
dual eigenfunctional there. It extends that functional to the Jacquet module by
compact averaging. The resulting character is trivial on integral diagonal
units. Rank-one determinant coordinates express it as the product of
unramified characters with their uniformizer values. The functional maps
into the inducing tensor line, and normalized first adjointness turns it
into a nonzero intertwiner with the principal series. Irreducibility of the
source makes this intertwiner injective.

This gives the principal-series detection needed after the Jacquet trace
formula for regular elementary tests in Henniart–Lemaire (2011), I.5.3
Corollary and I.5.4, printed pp. 32–33 (PDF pp. 40–41). The proof uses
normalized first adjointness directly from the integral-diagonal Jacquet
fixed space.
