# Reduction of the supercuspidal σ-lift structure to unitary data

`DiscreteSigmaLiftOrbitIdentificationStatement` concerns a square-integrable
supercuspidal `π` and its σ-discrete lift `P` over a cyclic extension `E/F` of
nonarchimedean local fields of characteristic zero. It takes a specified
decomposition of `P` as the normalized product of the `t` Galois conjugates of
an irreducible square-integrable block of exact period `t`, with the Library's
rank transport. It concludes that the block is supercuspidal and that `t` is
the cardinality of `normTwistStabilizer E π`. The field and representation
carrier universes are independent.

The theorem `cuspidalSigmaLiftStructure_of_normalized` uses this identification
along with existence and uniqueness of σ-lifts and the orbit decomposition of
σ-discrete series. It chooses a real determinant twist making the lower
supercuspidal square-integrable. The corresponding norm-pullback twist of the
upper representation preserves the σ-lift identity. Uniqueness identifies
that action with a σ-discrete lift supplied by existence. Orbit decomposition
and identification then give the supercuspidal block and its period.

The inverse of the common norm twist commutes with the rank-transported orbit
product. It preserves the block's irreducibility and supercuspidality. Its
Galois invariance preserves every isomorphism condition in the period clause,
and commuting determinant twists preserve the lower norm-twist stabilizer.
The result has the exact conclusion of the Library's structure proposition.
The rank-one proof and the norm-quotient divisibility are independent of the
four inputs of the reduction.
