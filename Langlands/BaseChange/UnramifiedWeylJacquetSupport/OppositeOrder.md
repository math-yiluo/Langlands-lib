# Sphericity detected by an opposite-order Jacquet weight

A tuple in the actual Jacquet multiset gives a nonzero joint generalized
weight space on the integral diagonal fixed module. The dual eigenvector
construction gives a nonzero functional with the specified eigenvalues
on all the diagonal uniformizer elements. Extend it to the whole
Jacquet module using the compact average. Averaging commutes with the
uniformizer actions, and the integral diagonal acts trivially on the
functional. Every diagonal element is a product of an integral
diagonal element and integral powers of the coordinate uniformizers.
The functional therefore intertwines the full diagonal action with
`borelCharacter F z`.

Multiplying the functional by the nonzero inducing tensor yields an
intertwiner from the normalized Borel Jacquet module to the inducing
line for `principalSeriesOfTuple F z`. Normalized first adjointness
gives a nonzero map into that principal series. Irreducibility of its
source makes the map injective.

When the norms decrease weakly, the inverse tuple has weakly increasing
norms. Smooth contragredients turn the embedding into a nonzero
quotient of the principal series of the inverse tuple. Ordered spherical
generation forces this quotient to have a spherical vector. Compact
fixed-vector duality transfers sphericity back to the original
representation. This implication applies to every irreducible
admissible representation in the stated scope, so in particular it
excludes opposite-order weights from the nonspherical generic
subquotients.
