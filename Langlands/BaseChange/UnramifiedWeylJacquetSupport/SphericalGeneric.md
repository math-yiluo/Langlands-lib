# The Jacquet multiset of a spherical generic constituent

Sort the uniformizer values by increasing norm. Spherical constituent
existence and uniqueness for the unordered spectrum make the given
constituent a subquotient of the sorted series. Ordered spherical
generation upgrades it to an actual quotient using compact fixed-vector
lifting.

For the sorted series, the condition in
`Derivative.OrderedCuspidalProductNondegeneracy.good_product`
holds for the inducing rank-one blocks. A twist by `|det|` would strictly
decrease the norm of the uniformizer value, which the assumed order
excludes between an earlier and a later block. Transport from the lifted
rank-one character lines identifies the normalized product with the
published principal series. Its top derivative is one-dimensional, and
every nonzero subrepresentation has nonzero top derivative by the
mirabolic nondegeneracy theorem and the comparison with the derivative of
the restriction.

The Whittaker functional on the quotient makes its top derivative
nonzero. Derivative exactness on the kernel, principal series and
quotient then forces the kernel to have zero top derivative, hence the
kernel is zero. The exact sequence uses the actual quotient by the kernel
in the universe of the principal series and transports the functional along
the equivalence with the given quotient. The resulting isomorphism
preserves the Jacquet multiset. Reindexing all Weyl permutations carries
the sorted parameter multiset back to the original one.
