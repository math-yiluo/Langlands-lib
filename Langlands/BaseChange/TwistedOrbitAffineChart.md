# Strict differentiation with a varying norm lift

For the representative `1 + j(w)`, the affine chart multiplies its
inverse, the centered norm lift, and the twisted adjoint of the
representative. Subtracting one centers the value at zero. The
inverse has derivative minus the identity at one. The product rule
therefore gives the lift derivative on the rational torus coordinate
and the twisted adjoint minus the identity on the transverse coordinate.
