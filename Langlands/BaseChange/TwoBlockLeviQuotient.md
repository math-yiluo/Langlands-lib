# Levi quotient and radical coordinates in the ambient quotient

Suppose the subgroup in the ambient general linear group is the image
of a subgroup of the two-block Levi under a continuous group
isomorphism. The parabolic coordinate map descends from the left quotient
of the Levi times the rectangular radical to the actual ambient left
quotient. The quotient topology gives continuity.

For a compactly supported ambient quotient function, a compact set of
representatives meets every coset in its support. Any representative
of a coset intersecting the parabolic stays in the parabolic, since the
subgroup giving the cosets is in the Levi. Applying the parabolic
coordinates to this closed intersection gives a compact set that
contains the support of the pulled back function.

This is an internal quotient-coordinate step for the use of parabolic
integration in orbital descent. It uses the Library's existing left
quotients and actual parabolic coordinates.
