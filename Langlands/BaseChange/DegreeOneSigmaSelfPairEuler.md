# The self-pair Euler identity in cyclic degree one

`degreeOneSigmaSelfPairEuler` concerns a cyclic degree-one extension `E/F`
of bundled finite extensions of the same `ℚ_p`, with the compatible scalar
tower and module topology. It applies at every positive rank, with
independent universes for the fields and the two representation carriers.
Let `Π` be an actual distributional σ-lift of `π`, with both actions
irreducible and admissible. The theorem proves
`Q_E(X^f) = ∏_{κ∈K(E/F)} Q_F(κ)(X)`, where `f` is the residue degree and
`K(E/F)` consists of all characters trivial on the norm of `Eˣ`.
`Q_E` certifies the pair `(Π, Π∨)` and `Q_F(κ)` certifies
`(π, (κ ∘ det) · π∨)`, where `∨` denotes the smooth dual. The certificates
use independently chosen additive characters and self-dual Haar measures
over the two fields.

The degree-one cyclic hypothesis forces the generator to be the identity
and the scalar embedding to be an isomorphism. For the corresponding Haar
measures, each upper test function and its pullback form a concordant pair.
Indeed every element is a norm of its image, and at an exact regular norm
the identical centralizer transport carries the associated measures and the
compatible quotient measure. The general conjugation property of concordance
reduces an associated pair to the exact norm case.

The lift relation on these pairs makes the upper twisted character
proportional to the character of the transported lower action. Character
transport and separation of twisted distributions give an equivalence of
actual representations. Pullback commutes with smooth duality because the
scalar isomorphism preserves the smooth vectors of the algebraic dual.
Transport of the two JPSS certificates and invariance under change of
additive character and Haar measure then identify the polynomials. The
residue fields have equal cardinality, so the residue degree is one. The
norm on units is surjective, so the product of the lower factors has just
the untwisted self-pair factor.

This is the degree-one p-adic case of the self-pair identity of
Henniart and Lemaire, *Changement de base et induction automorphe pour GL_n
en caractéristique non nulle* (2011), II.4.6(4) and II.4.12, printed pp. 89
and 96 (PDF pp. 97 and 104). The direct proof uses their concordance and
lift definitions in I.2.5, printed p. 17
