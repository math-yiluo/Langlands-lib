# The actual distinguished completions in cyclic globalization

`DistinguishedCompletionGlobalizationStatement` starts with the distinguished
lower completion and the field tensor from cyclic globalization, together with
their bicontinuous identifications with the prescribed local fields. The field
tensor has a unique place above the distinguished place. The statement identifies
the upper field bicontinuously with that actual completion, with compatibility
for the scalar embeddings and for the two specified automorphisms.

The comparison uses the canonical projection from the completed tensor product
to the unique completion. On a pure tensor with second coordinate one it is the
actual completed scalar inclusion. This gives compatibility of scalar maps
through the supplied lower field isomorphism. The projection also commutes with
the global generator acting on the tensor, which gives the required identity at
the upper completion.

This is the completion comparison used for the distinguished place in
Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, §6.3 and the proof of Proposition 6.9(ii).
