# Central characters on scalar norms in Shintani lifts

`ShintaniCentralCharacterOnNormsStatement` concerns two admissible complex actions of `GL_n(F)` and `GL_n(E)` for a cyclic field extension `E/F`. The actual groups are Hausdorff, locally compact and totally disconnected. The actions have specified central characters `χ` and `ξ` and an explicit Shintani identity. Given one associated regular semisimple pair `(δ, γ)` with a nonzero ordinary character value at `γ`, the statement concludes `ξ(z I) = χ(N_{E/F}(z) I)` for every unit `z` of `E`. The fields and representation spaces have independent universes.

The proof translates compactly supported locally constant tests on the left. The integrated action of a translated test is the product of the translating group action and the original integrated action. When the translating element acts by a scalar, taking the finite-rank trace scales the distribution by that scalar. Left invariance of Haar measure transports the local value definition to the translated neighbourhood. The ordinary character calculation uses the twisted calculation with the identity linear map.

The cyclic product of `z I · δ` is the product of the conjugates of `z` times the cyclic product of `δ`. The rank-one determinant formula identifies that scalar with the image of the field norm. The original conjugating matrix therefore associates `z I · δ` with `N_{E/F}(z) I · γ`. Scalar multiplication by a unit preserves regular semisimplicity. The proof substitutes the variable scaled by the inverse unit in the characteristic polynomial, then uses the derivative chain rule and coprimality with the derivative.

The Shintani identity at the original and translated pairs now supplies two values of the same upstairs twisted distribution at the translated pair. Uniqueness of local values and cancellation of the nonzero character value and identity constant give the stated equality. For two downstairs actions with a shared irreducible upstairs lift, `IsShintaniLift.centralCharacters_agree_on_norms` uses `commonShintaniLiftNonvanishing` to obtain a nonzero value for the second action and admissible Schur to obtain the upstairs central character. Both downstairs characters then agree on the scalar norms.

This derived statement formalizes the scalar-translation step in G. Henniart and B. Lemaire, *Changement de base et induction automorphe pour GL(n) en caractéristique non nulle*, Mémoires de la Société Mathématique de France 124 (2011), II.2.9 Remark (3), printed p. 70, and the agreement used in the proof of II.4.8, printed p. 91. The Lean comparison takes the pointwise Shintani identity and nonzero regular norm value as explicit inputs.
