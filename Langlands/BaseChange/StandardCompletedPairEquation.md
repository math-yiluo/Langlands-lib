# The completed pair equation in standard additive normalization

`StandardCompletedPairEquationStatement` takes the Library's completed global cuspidal
pair functional equation as a hypothesis. For a number field and actual cuspidal and
contragredient presentations with irreducible admissible finite components, it gives the
functional equation for the standard adelic character and a certified finite factor
choice. Each archimedean L-product is written in Gamma blocks. The number of real Gamma
factors is the pair rank times the absolute field degree, and the infinite epsilon
factor is constant. All finite epsilon factors outside the equation's finite set are
trivial.

The proof uses the archimedean standard-module parameters supplied by the completed
equation and the standard additive components at infinity. The rank formulas and Gamma
blocks give the counts for both sides. Finite factor nonvanishing on the right half-plane
converts the completed equation into the package used for comparison of the global
equations in Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, proof of Proposition 6.9(ii), completed equations
(6.15) and (6.16), printed p. 70 (PDF p. 84).
