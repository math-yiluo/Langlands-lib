# Determinant norm twists of sigma-lifts over cyclic fields

`SigmaLiftFieldTwistStatement` gives compatibility with determinant twisting over a cyclic field extension, using the original field norm and the actual distributional lift. For a continuous quasicharacter on the base, both twisted representations are admissible and the original twisting operator intertwines the upper twist with its sigma-conjugate. The lift constant is the same.

The determinant of a norm-associated base point is the field norm of the upper determinant. Multiplying each test by the corresponding character therefore preserves the orbital comparison. The two weighted tests are Schwartz–Bruhat because the characters are locally constant. Applying the integrated action of a character twist to a test is the original integrated action on the weighted test, so the distributional lift identity applies.

This is the field-extension case of Henniart–Lemaire II.2.9, Remark (4), printed p. 70. In the conductor argument it transports a lift through the unramified determinant twist introduced by globalization.
