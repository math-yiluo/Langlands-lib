# Intertwiners of strict twisted diagonals

Let \(a,b\) be diagonal unit tuples over a p-adic field \(E\) with
equal coordinate orders and with distinct orders across coordinates.
The automorphism \(\tau\) is over \(\mathbb Q_p\). If \(x^{-1}a\tau(x)=b\),
then \(a_i\tau(x_{ij})=x_{ij}b_j\). A nonzero off-diagonal entry would
give equality of the \(i\)-th and \(j\)-th orders because \(\tau\)
preserves order. Hence \(x\) is diagonal. Its diagonal entries are
nonzero by the determinant formula for a diagonal matrix.

This is the regular-weight specialization of the diagonal conjugacy
argument in Henniart–Lemaire (2011), I.4.1, Lemme 2, pp. 27–28.
