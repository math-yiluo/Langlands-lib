# Spherical base change in degree one

The statement restricts the Library's full spherical fundamental lemma to cyclic degree one and retains the independent p-adic carrier universes and every positive matrix rank. This is a degree-one specialization of Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, Theorem 4.5, and Henniart–Lemaire, I.6.6.

The identity automorphism is the only generator in this degree. Its fixed elements fill the upper field, so the scalar extension map is a p-adic algebra isomorphism. The norm uses one factor, and every base element has its scalar extension as a norm. For the spherical functions the Satake condition gives precomposition with the matrix isomorphism.

Conjugate a norm pair to make the norm equal to scalar extension, transporting the associated measures. At this pair the matrix isomorphism gives a homeomorphism of the centralizer quotients. The centralizer measures correspond by association, and the ambient spherical Haar measures correspond by the normalized compact subgroup property. Compatibility and uniqueness of quotient measures identify the quotient measures under transport. Evaluating both integrands on corresponding cosets then gives equality with the supplied associated measures.
