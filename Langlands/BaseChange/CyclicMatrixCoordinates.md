# Scalar extension of the matrix and norm coordinates

Tensoring a matrix space over the base field with a splitting field
commutes with the two finite products of matrix entries. Applying the
split coefficient coordinates and rearranging these products gives one
matrix space for every coordinate of the cyclic algebra.

Evaluation in a split coordinate is an algebra homomorphism. The cyclic
generator shifts the evaluation index by one. Evaluation in coordinate
zero of the ordered cyclic norm is therefore the ordered product of
the split matrices. The exact base-change norm equation identifies that
product with the base change of the rational matrix.
