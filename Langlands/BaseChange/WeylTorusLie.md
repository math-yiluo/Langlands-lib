# Lie algebra of a rational Weyl torus

The matrix Lie algebra of the torus at a regular point is the kernel of
the ordinary adjoint defect. Its inclusion after scalar extension is the
entrywise linear map on matrices. The cyclic vector of a regular matrix
remains cyclic under scalar extension. Thus a matrix in the extended
torus commutes with every element of the scalar-extended Lie algebra.

A matrix fixed entrywise by the cyclic generator and its inverse both
descend entrywise. Injectivity of scalar extension shows that their
descents are still inverses. The ordered cyclic norm of a torus point
commutes with the point itself, by commutativity of the centralizer.
The cyclic norm identity therefore makes its entries fixed. Descending
them gives a unit in the rational torus. Commutativity in the extended
torus gives a multiplicative norm map whose range is the Weyl norm image.
