# Completed standard equation for a cuspidal Hecke twist

`StandardCompletedTwistEquationStatement` specializes the supplied global pair
functional equation to a determinant twist of an actual cuspidal representation,
paired with the rank-one cuspidal realization of the constant functions. The dual
presentation uses the inverse Hecke twist of the given contragredient. For each twist
the statement supplies actual finite factors, a finite exceptional set, the two
Gamma block counts, and the equation with a constant archimedean epsilon unit.

The constant cuspidal line is self-dual by the bilinear pairing obtained from
multiplication of its scalar coordinates. The finite and compact actions fix the
line, and the real infinitesimal action is zero. The pairing takes the value one
on the two constant vectors of value one. Its invariance and nonvanishing identify
the full contragredient. The supporting existence theorem chooses the contragredient
and puts its irreducible admissible local components in the requested presentation
universe. The standard completed pair equation then applies to the twisted pair.

This is an internal specialization for the equations of the Hecke twists in
Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, proof of Proposition 6.9(ii), completed equation (6.15),
printed p. 70 (PDF p. 84).
