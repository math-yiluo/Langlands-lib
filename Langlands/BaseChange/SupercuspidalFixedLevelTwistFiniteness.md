# Supercuspidal unramified twist orbits at a fixed compact open level

`supercuspidalFixedLevelTwistFiniteness` gives a finite family of supercuspidal
representations of \(\mathrm{GL}_{n+1}(E)\) whose determinant-twist orbits cover the
supercuspidal representations with nonzero fixed vectors at the prescribed compact
open level. The field is a nonarchimedean local field, and its universe and the
complex carrier universe are independent.

Intersect the ambient level with the determinant-unit subgroup. The restriction of
each supercuspidal representation has a simple compact-coefficient summand visible
at that intersection. The compact-coefficient finiteness theorem gives finitely
many possible classes of these summands. Choose a supercuspidal representative for
each class that occurs. A common summand with an invariant complement gives a
nonzero intertwiner between the restrictions of two supercuspidals. The finite-index
norm-kernel argument and scalar action of the centre identify them up to a character
trivial on the determinant-unit subgroup. Such a character is trivial on compact
subgroups and therefore has the determinant-order description.

This is the orbit version of the fixed-level argument of Bernstein and Zelevinsky
(1976), §§3.20–3.29 and 4.14–4.16. The compact-coefficient stratum is the one used in
their proof of finiteness at a prescribed central character.
