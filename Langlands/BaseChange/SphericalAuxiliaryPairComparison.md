# Forward and dual standard factor comparison at spherical auxiliary places

For actual cuspidal presentations in a cyclic Galois extension of number
fields with compact adelic additive quotients, take a place outside the lower
forward presentation's exceptional set whose upper places all lie outside the
upper forward presentation's exceptional set. Assume the Satake power
comparison at these places with the inertia degrees. Use standard additive
finite factor choices on irreducible admissible components, trivial rank-one
second components at the chosen places, and a finite family of lower twists.
Their restrictions exhaust the local norm characters, each with multiplicity
the number of places above the lower place. The proposition compares the
forward and dual Euler products and the positive forward epsilon bases.

The dual presentations are actual contragredients, with inverse Hecke twists for the
lower family. The actual local duals carry the inverse spectra and retain the trivial
second component. Inversion of local characters preserves the norm-trivial family and
its fiber multiplicities. The same spherical norm comparison therefore applies to the
forward choices and to the choices with the two polynomials interchanged. The latter
calculation supplies equality of the original dual Euler products.

This is internal support for Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, Lemma 6.13, and the comparison of
the completed equations (6.15) and (6.16), printed p. 70 (PDF p. 84).
