# Rank-one σ-lift structure

`CuspidalSigmaLiftStructureRankOneStatement` specializes the structure
conclusion to `GL_1`. It takes irreducible admissible representations `π`
and `P` over nonarchimedean local fields `F` and `E`, an `F`-algebra
automorphism `σ` of `E`, and a σ-intertwiner for `P`. The lower
norm-twist stabilizer has order one. The rank identity, period and
product conclusion hold with the original carrier of `P`.

In rank one every group element is central, and the determinant is surjective.
Schur's lemma and the intertwining relation for a determinant twist force the
twisting character to be trivial. Thus the orbit length in the rank-one formula
is one. The given intertwiner produces an equivalence for every nonnegative
power of σ by composition. An admissible rank-one representation is
supercuspidal, and the Library's one-block equivalence identifies its normalized
orbit induction with the original representation.
