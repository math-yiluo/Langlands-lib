# Transport of rational torus Haar coordinates

Coefficient extension is a closed embedding of the ambient general
linear groups. Restriction to the rational torus gives a topological
group isomorphism onto the image subgroup. The equality of the two
ambient pushforwards then identifies the measures under this isomorphism.

The rational torus chart subtracts one at the identity. Restricting to a
compact open subgroup and translating that subgroup by a regular norm
point gives a chart in the regular norm domain. Commutativity makes
right translation Haar preserving. Passing to the open norm-domain
subtype preserves the restricted coordinate density.
