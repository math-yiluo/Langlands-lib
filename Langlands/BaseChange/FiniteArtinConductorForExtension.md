# Conductor transport for a finite abelian extension given as a field

The geometric Artin symbol is continuous for the supplied p-adic
topology. For an upper ramification group, the fixed-field extension
has trivial upper group at the same level by Herbrand's theorem. The
norm-unit cardinality formula implies that the corresponding units
are norms from the fixed field. Their images under the symbol
therefore lie in the original upper group by restriction of the
symbol. The same cardinality formula for the original extension
proves equality with that group.

Triviality of a character on these upper groups is thus equivalent to
triviality of the transported character on the corresponding unit
levels. The Library's rank-one Artin conductor formula for the upper
levels gives the conductor comparison on the supplied carrier.
