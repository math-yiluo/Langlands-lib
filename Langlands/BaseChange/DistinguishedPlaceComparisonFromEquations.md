# Distinguished-place epsilon comparison from a family of global equations

`DistinguishedPlaceComparisonFromEquationsStatement` is an internal assembly for the last step of the conductor argument in Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, proof of Proposition 6.9, printed pp. 69–70. It takes one global functional-equation package for each base twist and one for the lift. It assumes that the original and dual Euler factors agree after grouping upper places over their base places. It also assumes equality of the real Gamma counts and compatibility up to a nonzero constant of the archimedean epsilon products. The auxiliary local comparisons are stated as equalities of the positive bases of their finite epsilon monomials.

The proof multiplies the base functional equations and concatenates their Gamma blocks. Regrouping the upper Euler products along the finite fibers gives common product values. The source-independent Gamma comparison then yields equality of the two total positive epsilon bases. Grouping the finite upper bases along the same fibers and cancelling the base at each auxiliary place gives the distinguished local monomial comparison. At the distinguished place the upper fiber has one member. Its epsilon constant and those of the base twists are allowed to differ by a nonzero scalar.
