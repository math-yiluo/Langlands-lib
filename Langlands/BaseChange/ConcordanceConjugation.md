# Changing representatives in base-change concordance

For the measure association of Henniart–Lemaire, I.2.5, suppose \(x\) witnesses both the norm equality and the equality of the two measures on the extension group. When the ordinary element is changed by \(a\), change the twisted element by \(x\,a\), with \(a\) viewed in the extension group. The new cyclic norm equals the scalar extension of the new ordinary element. The two transported centralizer measures have equal pushforwards by the defining maps for the identity association witness. This follows by pushing the original measure equality forward by conjugation by \(a^{-1}\).

The proof transports both compatible quotient measures using the change of representative for twisted orbital integrals, with the identity twist for the ordinary integral. This proves the reduction of concordance to exact norm equality. The same change of variables gives ordinary orbital integral vanishing on any conjugate, and the cyclic norm identity gives invariance of norm membership. For the actual p-adic fields the scalar extension on general linear groups is continuous because the embedding over the common p-adic base is an isometry for the canonical norms.
