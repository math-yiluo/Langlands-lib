# The ambient centralizer of a block diagonal element

With pairwise coprime block characteristic polynomials, the ambient
centralizer lies in the standard Levi. Within the Levi, the
commutation equation holds exactly when it holds in each block.
Applying the continuous Levi coordinate isomorphism identifies this
centralizer with the product of the actual block centralizers.

The inverse is the block diagonal embedding. Its coordinate formula
allows the product Haar measure and its associated twisted measure
to be compared in the ambient group. This is an internal consequence
of the Library's block centralizer containment and Levi coordinates.
