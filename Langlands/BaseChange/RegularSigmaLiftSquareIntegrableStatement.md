# Square-integrability of a generic unitarizable regular σ-descent

`Langlands.BaseChange.RegularSigmaLiftSquareIntegrableStatement.{u, v, uV, uW}`
states that a generic unitarizable representation of `GL_{r+1}(F)` is
square-integrable modulo the center if it satisfies the regular σ-lift identity
with a σ-discrete series of `GL_{r+1}(E)`. This local criterion formulates the
square-integrability step of the argument for
`SigmaDiscreteRegularDescentStatement`.

## Field and representation data

The base `F : Type u` is a nonarchimedean local field of characteristic zero
with `[Field F] [ValuativeRel F] [TopologicalSpace F]
[IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F] [CharZero F]`. The field `E : Type v` is an
`F`-algebra and a topological ring with the `F`-module topology. For arbitrary
`l r : ℕ`, the datum `hσ : IsCyclicAlgebra σ l` for `σ : E ≃ₐ[F] E` describes a
cyclic extension of degree `l` with Galois group generated by `σ`. This
predicate is defined in `Langlands.BaseChange.Norm`. Its field case says that
`E` is finite of dimension `l` over `F`, that `σ` has order `l`, and that the
fixed elements are exactly the image of `F`.

The statement uses `hσ.1` for `Module.Finite F E`. It uses the
`LocalFieldTopology.generalLinear_t2Space`,
`LocalFieldTopology.generalLinear_locallyCompactSpace`, and
`LocalFieldTopology.generalLinear_totallyDisconnectedSpace` instances on
`GeneralLinear (r + 1) F`, and `generalLinear_t2Space_of_finite`,
`generalLinear_locallyCompactSpace_of_finite`, and
`generalLinear_totallyDisconnectedSpace_of_finite` on
`GeneralLinear (r + 1) E`. Here `GeneralLinear n R` is the Library's `GL_n(R)`.

The complex vector spaces `W : Type uW` and `V : Type uV` carry `ρ` on
`GL_{r+1}(E)` and `π` on `GL_{r+1}(F)`, respectively. Both representations
come with `IsAdmissible` witnesses `hρ` and `hπ` for the trace distributions.
Admissibility means smoothness and finite-dimensional fixed spaces for open
subgroups. The condition `IsSigmaDiscreteSeries σ (r + 1) ρ` from
`Langlands.BaseChange.PseudoCoefficient` says that `ρ` is admissible,
irreducible, tempered, and σ-stable and cannot be realized by unnormalized
smooth induction from a proper standard block parabolic of a smooth σ-stable
representation of its Levi. The statement allows every
`A : SigmaIntertwiner σ (r + 1) ρ` from `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedCharacter`,
an intertwining isomorphism of `ρ` onto `ρ^σ`, where `σ` acts entrywise on
matrices.

For every `ψ : UnitaryAdditiveCharacter F`, the predicate
`Whittaker.IsGeneric ψ r π` in
`Langlands.Representation.Whittaker.GeneralLinear` asserts that `π` is
irreducible and admissible and admits a nonzero Whittaker functional for the
standard upper unipotent character determined by `ψ`. The parameter `r` in
this predicate corresponds to matrices of size `r + 1`.
`UnitaryAdditiveCharacter F` in `Langlands.HarmonicAnalysis.Basic` is a
continuous nontrivial character from the additive group of `F` to `Circle`.
`IsUnitarizable π` in `Langlands.Representation.Unitary.Basic` asserts the
existence of an invariant positive definite Hermitian form.

## Proposition

For every such choice of data and every `c : ℂ`, the hypotheses
`IsSigmaDiscreteSeries σ (r + 1) ρ`, `Whittaker.IsGeneric ψ r π`,
`IsUnitarizable π`, and `IsRegularSigmaLift σ l π hπ ρ hρ A c` imply
`JacquetLanglands.IsSquareIntegrable π`.

`IsRegularSigmaLift`, imported from
`Langlands.BaseChange.SigmaDiscreteRegularDescentStatement`, requires `c ≠ 0`
and the identity \(\operatorname{tr}(\rho(\varphi)\circ A)
= c\,\operatorname{tr}(\pi(f))\) for every pair of Haar measures and every
concordant pair of Schwartz–Bruhat functions \(\varphi,f\) with respect to
those measures. The twisted function \(\varphi\) is supported on the σ-regular
set and the ordinary function \(f\) on the regular semisimple set, with
\(\det(g)\in N_{E/F}(E^\times)\) whenever \(f(g)\ne 0\).
`JacquetLanglands.IsSquareIntegrable` in
`Langlands.JacquetLanglands.EssentiallySquareIntegrable` asserts admissibility,
a unitary central character, and square-integrability of absolute matrix
coefficients for the smooth dual on the central quotient, for every Haar
measure there.

## Origin and intended proof

Henniart–Lemaire use the full lift identity and the central character
relation to obtain unitarity of the central character in II.4.8. The present
form uses the invariant Hermitian form on the irreducible `π` to give a
unitary central character. It uses the part of the character argument that
can be carried out on regular test functions with norm determinants.

1. Use Arthur–Clozel I, Lemma 2.12(i), to obtain a nonzero twisted character
   value at a σ-regular, σ-elliptic element \(\delta\). The argument uses a
   twisted pseudo-coefficient with nonzero trace and orbital integrals
   vanishing away from the σ-elliptic classes, the twisted Weyl integration
   formula, and local integrability of the twisted character.
   `TwistedCuspidalPseudoCoefficientExistenceStatement` states the
   pseudo-coefficient properties in the Library. Schur's lemma and
   linearity in the intertwiner preserve nonvanishing for every \(A\).
   Localize at \(\delta\) to obtain a σ-elliptic test \(\varphi\) with
   \(\operatorname{tr}(\rho(\varphi)\circ A)\ne 0\).
2. Apply `regularTransfer` (Henniart–Lemaire I.2.5) and localize the ordinary
   test \(f\) to the regular elliptic norm classes near the class of \(N\delta\).
   The determinant satisfies \(\det(N\delta)=N_{E/F}(\det\delta)\) and the
   norm subgroup \(N_{E/F}(E^\times)\) is open in \(F^\times\). Use a
   conjugation-invariant locally constant cutoff on the regular set
   containing the norm classes of the support of \(\varphi\). This would
   preserve concordance and ensure that all nonzero values of \(f\) are at
   regular elliptic elements with norm determinant. The
   `IsRegularSigmaLift` identity would then give \(\operatorname{tr}(\pi(f))\ne 0\).
3. Apply the elliptic character criterion, the identity-automorphism case of
   Arthur–Clozel I, Lemma 2.11. By Zelevinsky's Theorem 9.7, a generic
   irreducible representation is fully induced from essentially
   square-integrable Levi data. The vanishing of properly induced characters
   on the elliptic set is the input from Henniart–Lemaire II.4.5 used in
   II.4.8. The Library proves this generic vanishing criterion as
   `padicGenericEllipticCharacterVanishing` for `PadicField` data. The plan is
   to use `CharZeroLocalField.padicField` and `CharZeroLocalField.topologicalData`
   to apply it to `F`. A nonzero trace on \(f\) would force essential
   square-integrability of `π`.
4. Write `π` as \(|\det|^s\otimes\pi_0\) with \(\pi_0\) square-integrable
   modulo the center and \(s\in\mathbb R\). Both `π` and \(\pi_0\) have
   unitary central characters. On a central scalar \(z\), the absolute value
   of their ratio is \(|z|^{(r+1)s}\). Evaluation at a uniformizer would give
   \(s=0\) and hence square-integrability of `π`.
