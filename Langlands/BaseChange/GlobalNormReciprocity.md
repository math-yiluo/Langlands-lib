# Global norm reciprocity and its finite components

`GlobalNormReciprocityStatement` concerns a finite cyclic extension of number fields. It
supplies a continuous surjection from the lower idele class group onto the discrete Galois
group. Its kernel is the image of the idele class norm. At a finite place, restriction along
the inclusion of the units of the lower completion has kernel the image of the actual local
norm from every place above it. Its image is the corresponding decomposition subgroup.
On the inverse of a geometric local reciprocity value of a Weil element, it gives the
restriction of that element to the upper completion, included by the decomposition map.

The proof uses the arithmetic fundamental class for the relative Weil group. Its projection
to the Galois group descends through the surjective transfer because the Galois group is
abelian and the transfer kernel is the commutator subgroup. The transfer is a quotient map.
Its restriction to the embedded upper idele class group is the norm, so exactness of the
relative Weil extension identifies the kernel.

For the local assertions the proof uses the local-to-relative Weil map and its transfer
formula. Geometric Weil reciprocity is surjective onto the lower completion's units, and
the kernel of the local Weil projection to the global Galois group is the image of the upper
local Weil group. The transfer formula on that image is the inverse of the upper local
reciprocity value included into the upper idele class group, followed by the idele class
norm. The coordinate formula for that norm and injectivity of the local inclusion give the
local norm kernel. The image of the local Weil projection is the decomposition subgroup.

The class-field characters in Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, (6.2) and (6.17) are the characters
of this quotient. The construction uses the relative Weil transfer reciprocity of Artin–Tate,
Chapter XV, Theorem 1, and the local compatibility in Tate, *Number theoretic background*,
§§1.2 and 1.6.
