# The determinant of the twisted Weyl differential

The transverse derivative of twisted conjugation, using the inverse of
the affine unit as the orbit representative, is the defect of the twisted
adjoint action. A retraction of the included rational Lie algebra specifies
the complementary orbit directions. A section of the additive trace into
the upper Lie algebra supplies the derivative in the norm directions.

Extend scalars to an algebraic closure and identify the matrix spaces with
cyclic coordinates. The twisted adjoint operator then shifts the input
coordinates and applies ordinary adjoint actions as weights. Each weight
fixes the image of the rational Lie algebra. Exactness of scalar extension
identifies its image with the centralizer of the scalar-extended regular
matrix. The trace equation becomes the equation for a section of the sum
of the cyclic coordinates.

The corrected cyclic determinant theorem gives the determinant of the
ordinary adjoint defect with its torus projection. The exact norm
equation identifies the ordered product of the adjoint weights with the
adjoint action at the norm. Descending the determinant to the base
field and applying `det_ordinaryWeylDifferential` gives the nonzero
ordinary discriminant.
