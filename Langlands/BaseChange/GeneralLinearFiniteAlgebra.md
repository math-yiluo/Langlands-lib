# Topology and Haar invariance for general linear groups over finite algebras

This module supplies auxiliary results for the existence of the quotient
measures in Henniart–Lemaire (2011), §I.2.4, printed pp. 14–15. The
arguments use Mathlib's module topology and modular character with the
Library's general linear group results.

`finiteAlgebraTopology` takes a finite basis over the nonarchimedean local
field `F`. Both coordinate maps are continuous linear maps for the module
topologies, giving a homeomorphism with a finite power of `F`. It transports
Hausdorffness, local compactness and second countability. For nontrivial
Hausdorff `E`, `generalLinear_isClosedEmbedding_algebraMap` uses a continuous
linear left inverse of `algebraMap F E`. This makes coefficient extension a
closed embedding on the scalar rings, and Mathlib lifts the embedding to
matrix units.

`generalLinearResidueEquiv` applies the residue field decomposition of a
reduced Artinian ring to matrices, then to their units.
`generalLinear_haar_rightInvariant` restricts the modular character of
`GL_n(R)` to each field factor. A scalar in that factor defines a central
element of the product. Right translation by a central element is left
translation, so it preserves Haar measure and has modular character one.
The Library's `GeneralLinearUnimodularity.character_eq_one` therefore gives
the trivial character on each factor. The finite product is generated by
the individual factors, giving the trivial modular character on `GL_n(R)`
and right invariance of the given inner regular Haar measure.
