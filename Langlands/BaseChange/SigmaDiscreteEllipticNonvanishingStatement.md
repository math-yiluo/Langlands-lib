# Twisted characters of σ-discrete series are detected on the σ-elliptic set

Module `Langlands.BaseChange.SigmaDiscreteEllipticNonvanishingStatement`, declaration
`Langlands.BaseChange.SigmaDiscreteEllipticNonvanishingStatement` (level parameters `u v uW`).
This is a statement only. Nothing in the module is proved.

## Statement

Let `F` be a nonarchimedean local field of characteristic zero. Let `E` be a field that is a
cyclic `F`-algebra of degree `l` with generator `σ`, with its module topology over `F`. Let
`r : ℕ` and `n = r + 1`. Give `GL_n(E)` its Borel σ-algebra. Let `τ` be an admissible
representation of `GL_n(E)` on a complex vector space `W : Type uW` that is a σ-discrete
series representation. Let `A` be an isomorphism of `τ` onto `τ^σ`, and let `μ` be a Haar
measure on `GL_n(E)`. Then some Schwartz–Bruhat function `φ` on `GL_n(E)` is supported in the
σ-elliptic set and satisfies `tr(τ(φ) ∘ A) ≠ 0`.

In Lean, the binders are, in this order: `F : Type u` with `[Field F] [ValuativeRel F]
[TopologicalSpace F] [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F] [CharZero F]`; `E : Type v` with
`[Field E] [Algebra F E] [TopologicalSpace E] [IsTopologicalRing E] [IsModuleTopology F E]`;
`σ : E ≃ₐ[F] E`; `l r : ℕ`; `hE : IsCyclicAlgebra σ l`; then, by `haveI`/`letI`,
`Module.Finite F E` from `hE.1`, the three topological instances on `GeneralLinear (r + 1) E`
listed below, the Borel σ-algebra `borel _` and its `BorelSpace` instance; then
`W : Type uW` with `[AddCommGroup W] [Module ℂ W]`,
`τ : Representation ℂ (GeneralLinear (r + 1) E) W` and `hτ : IsAdmissible τ`. The proposition
is

`IsSigmaDiscreteSeries σ (r + 1) τ → ∀ (A : SigmaIntertwiner σ (r + 1) τ)
(μ : Measure (GeneralLinear (r + 1) E)) [μ.IsHaarMeasure],
∃ φ : SchwartzBruhat (GeneralLinear (r + 1) E),
IsSigmaEllipticSupported σ l ⇑φ ∧ twistedDistribution μ τ hτ A.toLinearMap φ ≠ 0`.

## Library notation

- `GeneralLinear n E` is `GL_n(E)`. Its topology comes from `E`. The theorems
  `generalLinear_t2Space_of_finite`, `generalLinear_locallyCompactSpace_of_finite` and
  `generalLinear_totallyDisconnectedSpace_of_finite` (module
  `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedCharacter`) make it a Hausdorff, locally compact, totally
  disconnected group when `E` is finite over `F`. They are theorems, not global instances, and
  the statement binds them with `haveI`, after `Module.Finite F E` taken from `hE.1`.
- `IsCyclicAlgebra σ l` (module `Langlands.BaseChange.Norm`): `E` is a finite reduced
  `F`-algebra of dimension `l`, `σ` has order `l`, and the fixed points of `σ` are `F`. Here `E`
  is also a field, so `E/F` is a cyclic field extension of degree `l` with Galois group
  generated by `σ`. The degree `l` is not assumed to be prime.
- `IsSigmaDiscreteSeries σ n τ` (module `Langlands.BaseChange.PseudoCoefficient`): `τ` is
  admissible, irreducible, tempered and σ-stable, and it is not isomorphic to the smooth
  induction, from the standard upper block parabolic subgroup of any block shape with at least
  two blocks, of a smooth σ-stable representation of the Levi product. This is Arthur–Clozel's
  σ-discrete representation (Chapter 1, §2.3) and Henniart–Lemaire's "série σ-discrète"
  (II.4.6, II.1.7).
- `SigmaIntertwiner σ n τ` (module `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedCharacter`): an isomorphism of
  representations from `τ` to `τ^σ = τ ∘ entrywiseAut σ n`, where `entrywiseAut σ n` applies `σ`
  to every matrix entry; that is, a linear automorphism `A` of `W` with
  `A ∘ τ(g) = τ(g^σ) ∘ A`. `A.toLinearMap` is its underlying linear map.
- `twistedDistribution μ τ hτ A.toLinearMap φ` (module
  `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedCharacter.Basic`): the twisted distribution character
  `tr(τ(φ) ∘ A)`. Here `τ(φ) = ∫ φ(g) τ(g) dμ(g)` has finite rank because `τ` is admissible
  (`hτ : IsAdmissible τ`).
- `IsSigmaEllipticSupported σ l φ` (module `Langlands.BaseChange.EllipticSupport`): every `δ`
  with `φ δ ≠ 0` is σ-elliptic, that is, associated by the norm map with a regular elliptic
  element of `GL_n(F)` (`IsSigmaElliptic`, module `Langlands.BaseChange.Norm`). Every
  σ-elliptic element is σ-regular, so `φ` is also supported in the σ-regular set
  (`IsSigmaEllipticSupported.isSigmaRegularSupported`).
- `SchwartzBruhat (GeneralLinear n E)` is the space of locally constant compactly supported
  complex functions on `GL_n(E)`.

## Design

- **Generalizes the supercuspidal statement.** The Library's
  `TwistedSupercuspidalEllipticNonvanishingStatement` (module
  `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedSupercuspidalEllipticNonvanishingStatement`) states the same
  conclusion for irreducible σ-stable supercuspidal `τ` of `GL_n(E)` with `0 < n`. The present
  statement has the same setting and conclusion, with the hypothesis "irreducible
  supercuspidal" replaced by `IsSigmaDiscreteSeries` and `n` written as `r + 1`. Neither
  statement implies the other as stated: a σ-stable supercuspidal representation need not be
  tempered (twist a unitary one by `|det|^s`, `s` real and nonzero), and a σ-discrete series
  need not be supercuspidal (for instance a σ-stable Steinberg representation).
- **`n = r + 1`.** This excludes `GL_0`, as `0 < n` does in the supercuspidal statement, and
  matches the companion statement `GenericSigmaEllipticDiscreteStatement`, whose genericity
  predicate `Whittaker.IsGeneric ψ r` is indexed by `r`.
- **Admissibility as a separate binder.** `IsSigmaDiscreteSeries` includes admissibility, but
  `twistedDistribution` takes an admissibility proof as an argument, so the statement binds
  `hτ : IsAdmissible τ` before the hypothesis. The binder adds no condition.
- **Every `A` and every Haar measure `μ`.** For irreducible `τ` the intertwiner `A` is unique up
  to a nonzero scalar (Schur's lemma), and `μ` is unique up to a positive scalar. Both scalars
  multiply the twisted trace, so the conclusion does not depend on the choices. The statement
  quantifies over both and normalizes neither.
- **Test-function form.** Henniart–Lemaire and Arthur–Clozel say that the twisted character
  function `Θ^A_τ` is not identically zero on the σ-elliptic set. The Library records values of
  that function pointwise (`HasTwistedCharacterValue`, module
  `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedCharacter.Basic`), but this statement is phrased with the
  twisted distribution character: it is nonzero on a function supported in the σ-elliptic set.
  This form needs no statement about the existence of the character function. It is also the
  form used in step 1 of the intended proof recorded in the Markdown of the Library's
  `RegularSigmaLiftSquareIntegrableStatement` (module
  `Langlands.BaseChange.RegularSigmaLiftSquareIntegrableStatement`): there a σ-elliptic test
  function with nonzero twisted trace of a σ-discrete series is compared, through a σ-lift
  identity, with an ordinary character.
- **Field `E`.** The source results are stated for a cyclic field extension.

## Provenance

This is a reformulation of Henniart–Lemaire 2011, II.5.14, Corollaire 2 (printed p. 117:
"Toute représentation essentiellement σ-discrète de G(E) est σ-elliptique"), and of
Arthur–Clozel 1989, Chapter 1, Lemma 2.12 (i) (printed p. 19: for `Π` σ-discrete,
`Θ_{Π,σ} ≠ 0` on the σ-elliptic set). Henniart–Lemaire call a σ-stable irreducible `Π`
σ-elliptic when, for some (equivalently every) isomorphism `A` of `Π` onto `Π^σ`, the character
function `Θ^A_Π` is not identically zero on the σ-elliptic set `G(E)_{σ-e}` (II.4.8,
Définition, printed p. 91). The statement here is restricted to σ-discrete series, which are
in particular essentially σ-discrete. The passage from a nonzero character function to a
nonzero value of the distribution on a test function supported in the σ-elliptic set is
derived (see the proof sketch). Henniart–Lemaire work in positive characteristic, but the
argument does not depend on the characteristic. Arthur–Clozel work in characteristic zero.

## Why it is true (sketch; not part of the Lean)

Arthur–Clozel's proof of Lemma 2.12 (printed p. 19) and Henniart–Lemaire's proof of
II.5.14, Corollaire 2 (printed p. 117), give the following route.

1. A twisted pseudo-coefficient `φ₀` of `(τ, A)` exists, and its σ-orbital integrals vanish at
   the σ-regular elements that are not σ-elliptic. In the Library this is part of the
   statement `TwistedCuspidalPseudoCoefficientExistenceStatement` (module
   `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedCuspidalPseudoCoefficientExistenceStatement`), parts (a) and
   (c); that module states it and does not prove it.
2. By the twisted Weyl integration formula (Henniart–Lemaire II.2.10 (1), printed p. 72),
   `1 = tr(τ(φ₀) ∘ A)` is a sum of integrals of `Θ^A_τ` against the σ-orbital integrals of
   `φ₀`. The terms of non-σ-elliptic tori vanish by step 1. So `Θ^A_τ` is not identically zero
   on the σ-elliptic set.
3. `Θ^A_τ` is locally integrable, locally constant on the σ-regular set, and it represents the
   twisted distribution character (Clozel 1987, as stated in the Library's
   `TwistedCharacterLocalIntegrabilityStatement`, module
   `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedCharacterLocalIntegrabilityStatement`, which that module
   states and does not prove). Take a σ-elliptic `δ₀` with `Θ^A_τ(δ₀) ≠ 0` and a compact open
   neighbourhood `U` of `δ₀` inside the open σ-elliptic set on which `Θ^A_τ` is constant.
   Then `φ = 1_U` is supported in the σ-elliptic set, and
   `tr(τ(φ) ∘ A) = Θ^A_τ(δ₀) μ(U) ≠ 0`.

## Related statements

The forward direction of Henniart–Lemaire II.5.15, Proposition 1 (printed p. 117), for
σ-discrete series, is this statement. The converse direction, for generic representations
with unitary central character, is the companion module
`Langlands.BaseChange.GenericSigmaEllipticDiscreteStatement`.
