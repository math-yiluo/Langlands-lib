# A σ-intertwiner on a supercuspidal orbit product

`sigmaOrbitIntertwiner` gives a `SigmaIntertwiner` on `sigmaOrbitProduct σ s hn P` for an irreducible supercuspidal `P` with period exactly `s`, when the product is irreducible. The result uses the actual normalized induced carrier over `E` with its supplied local-field topology. The base field supplies the module topology on `E`, and the cyclic algebra certificate gives a positive degree.

For the permutation comparison, `normalizedProductInduced_equiv_permute_of_irreducible` treats the block actions as smooth representations. The permutation matrix conjugates the two standard Levis, and the tensor permutation identifies their inducing data under this conjugation. The Jordan–Hölder comparison for associated cuspidal induction gives matching composition factors. The original induction has length one, so the matching yields an isomorphism of the induced actions.

Pullback by `σ` on the orbit product agrees with induction of the pulled-back factors by `normalizedProductInducedEntrywiseAutEquiv`. Each pulled-back factor is equivalent to the successor given by `finRotate`. With uniform blocks, this rotation leaves the block sizes fixed. Compose the permutation isomorphism with the inverses of the blockwise isomorphism and of precomposition on sections. The resulting map intertwines the actual product with its pullback by `σ`. The map uses a choice of permutation and block isomorphisms.

This is the stability part of the cyclic orbit construction in Arthur–Clozel, *Simple Algebras, Base Change, and the Advanced Theory of the Trace Formula* (1989), Chapter 1, Lemma 2.8, printed p. 17 (PDF p. 31, source `arthur-clozel-1989-base-change`, SHA-256 `c2e47ee562810367ebcacd3f1c242eda40b9e78b271668e36d99ce31001f9a19`). The supercuspidal application is Henniart–Lemaire, *Changement de base et induction automorphe pour GL_n en caractéristique non nulle* (2011), II.4.12, Remarque, printed p. 97 (PDF p. 105, source `provided-a0a5445aba86b871bcd83f08813de3234fddb463ff9bb3c9dfd60d2d2fcdae61`, SHA-256 `7af2940c75a75937c1bf9096eb329931d4245cf592e3572db0ff93ec6e76c5e0`). The Lean construction uses the associated-cuspidal comparison to implement the permutation of factors.
