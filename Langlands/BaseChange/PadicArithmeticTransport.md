# Transport of conductor and extension invariants on p-adic models

The characteristic-zero field carriers may lie in independent
universes. Mathlib's smallness of a finite module over the p-adic field
allows a common universe-zero model. The Library's algebra equivalence
of the model preserves the discrete valuation and residue cardinality
for supplied p-adic topological data.

Pullback along the unit equivalence identifies every principal-unit
level, hence the multiplicative conductor. The model also preserves the
residue degree as characterized by residue cardinalities. Its trace
pairing is the original pairing carried through the field equivalences.
The chosen uniformizer is transported with its valuation, so the same
exponent describes the trace codifferent of the model.
