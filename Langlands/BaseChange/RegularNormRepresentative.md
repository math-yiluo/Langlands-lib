# Continuous ordinary representatives of regular twisted norms

`RegularNormRepresentativeStatement` concerns a cyclic algebra \(E\)
over a topological field \(F\). The ring topology on \(E\) is its
module topology over \(F\). Fix an associated pair
\((\delta_0,\gamma_0)\) with \(\gamma_0\) regular semisimple.
There is a continuous map from the \(\sigma\)-regular locus of
\(\mathrm{GL}_n(E)\), with the subspace topology, to
\(\mathrm{GL}_n(F)\). It assigns to each point an associated
regular semisimple representative and takes \(\delta_0\) to
\(\gamma_0\).

The proof chooses an ordinary regular representative and a cyclic
basis at each point. In that basis its matrix entries are the
companion entries determined by the coefficients of the characteristic
polynomial. They are constant except in the final column, which
contains the negatives of the coefficients below the leading term.
Those coefficients are continuous functions of the twisted norm
after applying a continuous \(F\)-linear left inverse to
\(F\to E\). Conjugating all the companion matrices by the cyclic
basis at \(\gamma_0\) gives the continuous representatives and
the value at the chosen pair.

This internal construction gives a continuous choice of ordinary
representatives for the regular-pair approximation in
Henniart–Lemaire 2011, I.6.8. It uses the association convention of
that work, I.1.2.
