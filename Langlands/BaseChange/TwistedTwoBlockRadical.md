# Twisted two-block radical coordinates

In the coordinates `g = Levi(U) u(x)`, entrywise field automorphism applies the automorphism to the two Levi blocks and to the rectangle `x`. The parabolic multiplication formula then gives the twisted conjugate of `Levi(D)` as the point with Levi coordinate `V = U⁻¹ D σ(U)` and radical coordinate `σ(x) − V₀⁻¹ x V₁`.

On the rectangle, `C_U(x) = U₀ x U₁⁻¹` is the Levi action. The radical difference map at `V` is the composition of the map at `D` with `C_U` on the input and `C_σ(U)⁻¹` on the output. The Haar scaling formula for rectangular matrix multiplication and invariance of the determinant norms under the field automorphism give the same scaling for `C_U` and `C_σ(U)`. Their contributions therefore cancel, preserving the radical Jacobian under twisted Levi conjugation. This is the coordinate calculation in the twisted orbital descent of Laumon, Proposition 4.4.9.
