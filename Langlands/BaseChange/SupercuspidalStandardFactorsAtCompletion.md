# Standard Euler polynomials at a supercuspidal finite completion

`SupercuspidalStandardFactorsAtCompletionStatement` applies to the finite
factors of a cuspidal pair with the rank-one constant presentation as its second
member, using a genuine contragredient of the first member. At any finite place
where the first component is supercuspidal of rank greater than one, the forward
and dual standard Euler polynomials are both one.

The supporting theorem takes a representative of the supercuspidal class and
transports its Jacquet vanishing to the canonical p-adic object. The finite local
component of the contragredient is the smooth dual of that object. The
inverse-transpose realization of the smooth dual preserves supercuspidality.
Applying the unequal-rank standard polynomial calculation with the actual
trivial local character then proves the two identities for the supplied finite
factor choice.

This is an internal application of the supercuspidal unequal-rank L-factor
formula used in Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, proof of Proposition 6.9(ii), printed
p. 70 (PDF p. 84).
