# Integrals of concordant test functions

`Langlands.BaseChange.ConcordantIntegralEqualityStatement` expresses the weight-one
consequence of Henniart–Lemaire (2011), II.2.10, formulas (1) and (2), printed
pp. 70–72. The measures, cyclic algebra, and definition of concordance are those
of `RegularTransferStatement`. The identity applies to Schwartz–Bruhat functions
with arbitrary support.

With constant weight one on both groups, the Weyl formulas for the two integrals
have matching regular orbital integrals on the norm image. The twisted torus measure
is transported to the restriction of the ordinary torus measure to that image.
Concordance also sets the ordinary regular orbital integrals outside the image to
zero. The forward reduction uses this consequence as a premise.
