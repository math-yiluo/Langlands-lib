# Twisted Weyl orbit decomposition

Fix a cyclic algebra over a nonarchimedean local field and a representative
set in `WeylTorus F n`, the subgroups occurring as regular semisimple
centralizers. Each torus parameter is a regular point in its norm image.
Choose an exact cyclic norm lift for each parameter. The twisted Weyl map
sends a coset of the base-changed rational torus and a parameter to the
twisted conjugate of its lift by a coset representative. This descends
to the coset space because the twisted centralizer of the lift is the
base-changed rational torus.

For an associated pair of a twisted element and a rational matrix, project
both orbit-map fibers to regular points of the given torus. The two
projections have the same image. It consists of the regular torus points
associated with the twisted element. Such points belong to the torus norm
image. The cyclic norm lemma gives an ordinary conjugator to the rational
matrix and a twisted conjugator from the chosen lift to the twisted element.
The torus centralizer and the twisted centralizer show that in either
fiber a fixed torus point determines the coset. This gives a bijection
between the fibers and transfers the rational Weyl multiplicity.

Every twisted regular element has an associated ordinary regular element.
The ordinary Weyl partition then gives the unique representative torus
whose twisted orbit map contains it. This is the algebraic partition and
multiplicity for the twisted Weyl formula of Henniart–Lemaire (2011),
II.2.10, formula (1). The arbitrary exact lifts in the statement suffice for
the algebraic result. Local continuous choices and the measure calculation
are inputs for integration.
