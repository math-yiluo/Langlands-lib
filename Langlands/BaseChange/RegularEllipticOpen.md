# Openness of the regular elliptic locus

In the native topology of a nonarchimedean local field, the integer ring is compact and open. For monic polynomials of fixed degree with integral coefficients, reducibility is described by finitely many compact coefficient images. Each image is the multiplication map on two monic factor spaces of prescribed positive degrees, with all coefficients in the integer ring. Gauss's lemma for this integrally closed valuation ring identifies this test with reducibility over the local field.

Near any given polynomial, a fixed nonzero scaling of the roots puts the coefficients in the integer ring. This scaling preserves monicity, degree, and irreducibility. Characteristic-polynomial coefficients are continuous functions on the matrix group, so excluding the compact factor images gives an open neighborhood of an irreducible characteristic polynomial. In characteristic zero, irreducibility implies separability. The resulting locus is the Library's regular elliptic set.

The lemma supplies the open regular elliptic condition in the auxiliary split-place construction of Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, proof of Theorem 4.5, printed p. 43.
