# The trace-codifferent exponent and different ideals in towers

The integer rings of a finite local extension are the actual valuation
rings, and the upper one is the integral closure of the lower. The
trace dual of the unit fractional ideal consists of the elements whose
products with all upper integers have integral trace. This is the
codifferent of Serre, *Local Fields* (1979), Chapter III, §3, printed
p. 50.

The Lean proof rewrites codifferent membership using
`IsDifferentExponent`. Multiplication by the indicated power of the
uniformizer gives an upper integer exactly for elements in the
fractional ideal generated by its inverse. Inverting this fractional
ideal gives Mathlib's different ideal. The published maximal-ideal
formula for a uniformizing element computes its exponent.

For a tower, Serre's transitivity formula in Chapter III, §4, Proposition 8
identifies the upper different with the product of the relative different
and the extended lower different. The Lean proof uses Mathlib's transitivity
theorem on the three integer rings. Extending the lower maximal ideal
raises the upper maximal ideal to the relative ramification index. The
powers of the upper maximal ideal are strictly decreasing, so their
exponents satisfy the corresponding equality.
