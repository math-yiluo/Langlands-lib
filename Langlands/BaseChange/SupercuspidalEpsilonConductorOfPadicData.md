# The epsilon exponent of a supercuspidal representation against the trivial character

For an irreducible supercuspidal representation of positive rank `r + 1` over a supplied p-adic field, the theorem produces a pair epsilon factor against the trivial character of `GL₁` for any nontrivial unitary additive character `ψ` at a supplied self-dual Haar measure. Its exponent is `a + (r + 1) n(ψ)`, with `a` the finite newvector conductor and `n(ψ)` the additive conductor of the underlying local-field data. The conclusion is the `IsStandardEpsilonConductor` predicate used in the local reduction of the sigma-lift conductor identity.

Jacquet, Piatetski-Shapiro and Shalika, *Conducteur des représentations du groupe linéaire* (1981), §1.1 and Théorème (5.1), printed pp. 199 and 211, give the exponent and fixed-level assertions at additive conductor zero. Bushnell, Henniart and Kutzko, *Local Rankin–Selberg convolutions for GL(n), explicit conductor formula* (1998), introduction, printed p. 703, give the dependence of the pair exponent on the additive character.

Lean uses supercuspidal genericity and the proved `supercuspidalPadicConductorExponent` for a rescaled additive character of conductor zero. The proved pair exponent formula from both character-change laws identifies its character-independent term with the resulting natural newvector conductor, so the actual factor constructed at `ψ` has the asserted exponent. The published `PadicField.TopologicalData.eq_canonicalTopologicalData` in `GLPoleRegionTransport` identifies the supplied and canonical data on the same field. The absolute values and residue cardinalities agree definitionally, and `toLocalFieldData_additiveConductor` identifies the p-adic and all-field additive conductors.
