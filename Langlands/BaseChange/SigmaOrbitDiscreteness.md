# The unitary supercuspidal orbit product is σ-discrete

`unitarySigmaOrbitDiscreteness` proves `UnitarySigmaOrbitDiscretenessStatement`. The seed is an irreducible unitarizable supercuspidal of positive rank over a characteristic-zero nonarchimedean local field `E`. Its isomorphism class returns under `σ^i` exactly for multiples of `s`. The cyclic algebra certificate implies that `s` is positive. The conclusion is `IsSigmaDiscreteSeries` for the actual normalized `sigmaOrbitProduct`, with the supplied topology. `UnitarySigmaOrbitProduct` supplies irreducibility and temperedness, hence admissibility. `sigmaOrbitIntertwiner` supplies σ-stability.

To check the proper-Levi clause, assume an isomorphism with unnormalized smooth induction of σ-stable smooth Levi data. Irreducibility and admissibility of the induced representation give the same two properties for the Levi data by `isIrreducible_isAdmissible_of_smoothInduced`. Twisting the Levi action by the inverse half modulus expresses the same induction as normalized induction. The modulus is preserved by `σ`, so the normalized Levi data remain σ-stable. Factor this irreducible admissible Levi representation into an external tensor. The σ-equivalence of the tensor restricts to a subquotient relation in each slot, which is an isomorphism because the slot factors are irreducible.

Embed each slot factor into a normalized induction of supercuspidals. Pulling back this embedding by `σ` and comparing supercuspidal supports of the slot factor shows that its support is closed under `σ`. Tensor the embeddings and induce. Ordered regrouping gives an embedding of the whole representation into induction of the resulting flattened supercuspidal data. Uniqueness of supercuspidal support identifies the flattened multiset with the multiset of conjugates of the seed. The period makes that multiset multiplicity-free, so each orbit factor has a unique inducing block. Rotation preserves this block, and the rotation of `Fin s` is transitive. Each inducing block has positive size and contributes at least one element to the flattened support. All such elements lie in a single block, contradicting the proper-Levi hypothesis.

The proof uses the same-carrier `PadicField` construction for the published embedding, regrouping and support theorems. All induction and isomorphism tests apply to the given groups and carriers. The exclusion handles the smooth Levi data quantified by `IsSigmaDiscreteSeries` at its carrier universe.
