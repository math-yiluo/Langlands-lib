# Orbit charts for regular cyclic base change

The base field is nonarchimedean local of characteristic zero, and the cyclic
algebra has its module topology. For a twisted regular element choose an
associated regular ordinary element `γ` and twist the representative by the
association witness to obtain `δ` with norm equal to the base extension of
`γ`. The local coefficient slice furnishes a continuous unit `t(b)` given by
a polynomial in `γ`. Shrink the open patch to the separable coefficient
locus. The ordinary elements `γ * t(b)^l` are regular and have the
prescribed coefficients.

The twisted section multiplies `δ` by the base extension of `t(b)`. The
latter is fixed by the cyclic action and commutes with `δ`, since `δ`
commutes with the base extension of `γ` and the regular centralizer after
scalar extension is commutative. Multiplying the cyclic norm factors gives
the base extension of `γ * t(b)^l` as its norm, so this is a section of the
norm coefficient map. A regular matrix commuting with `γ` has the same
ordinary centralizer as `γ`, by commutativity of both centralizers. The norm
centralizer equivalence identifies each twisted stabilizer with the image of
that same ordinary centralizer under base extension.

The coefficient fibers are single twisted orbits. At a section value its
norm has a cyclic basis obtained by extending a cyclic basis of the associated
ordinary element. Comparing this section evaluated at varying coefficients
with the varying group element gives two families converging to the section
value and with equal norm characteristic polynomials. The local conjugator
theorem produces conjugators tending to one. This verifies the lift in every
identity neighborhood and gives the product homeomorphism on the whole
parameter preimage. Every element in the preimage is regular, so the chart
also gives the product description in the regular subtype.

For the ordinary chart take the base field itself as cyclic algebra with the
identity automorphism and degree one. The norm and the twisted coefficient
map then recover the ordinary element and its characteristic coefficients.
These internal chart constructions supply the local geometry for the
regular transfer proof of Henniart–Lemaire I.2.5–I.2.8.
