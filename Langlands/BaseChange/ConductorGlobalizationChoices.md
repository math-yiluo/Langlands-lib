# Local choices for the conductor globalization

`ConductorGlobalizationChoicesStatement` assumes
`CyclicFieldGlobalizationStatement` and `SupercuspidalExistenceStatement`.
Given the characteristic-zero local cyclic extension and an actual
irreducible supercuspidal representation of the lower group, it makes
the local choices for applying supercuspidal globalization.

The cyclic field globalization gives the number fields, the matched
generator, split infinite places and the distinguished tensor. The
tensor has a unique upper place and the compatible bicontinuous maps
identify the original fields with the completions. Applying the
split-place avoidance clause three times gives two split places for
the prescribed supercuspidals and a separate split auxiliary place.

The family uses the original local representation transported to the
lower distinguished completion. At every other finite place it
chooses an irreducible supercuspidal of the same rank using the local
existence theorem. This produces the representations to prescribe in
the globalization step of Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, §6.3 and proof of
Proposition 6.9(ii), printed pp. 54–57 and 69–70.
