# Irreducibility of sigma-orbit products on characteristic-zero local fields

For a finite extension of nonarchimedean local fields of characteristic
zero with the relative module topology, take an irreducible supercuspidal
representation of the upper field and an automorphism over the lower
field. The full normalized orbit product is irreducible. The proof
realizes the lower field as a p-adic field on its original carrier and
supplies the upper p-adic realization on the original upper carrier,
with the original topology and valuative relation. The equality with the
canonical p-adic realization allows use of
`supercuspidalSigmaOrbitIrreducibility`. A rank reindexing is a group
isomorphism, so an action equivalent to the reindexed product is also
irreducible.

This transports the canonical p-adic proof for the full normalized product
to the local fields of the cuspidal sigma-lift conductor formula.
