# The fundamental lemma for unramified base change of GL_{r+1}

Module `Langlands.BaseChange.FundamentalLemmaStatement`.
`Langlands.BaseChange.FundamentalLemmaStatement.{u, v}` is a proposition-valued definition
(statement 9 of the statements on Satake characters and unramified base change; statement 6
is `SatakeCharacterStatement`, statement 8 `UnitFundamentalLemmaStatement`). It states an
unproved obligation; it is neither proved nor
assumed in this module.

## Statement

The module repeats, as local instances declared for every `PadicField` over every prime,
the canonical topology, valuative relation and local-field structure of the field, and the
canonical topology, topological-group, Hausdorff, Borel measurable and Borel space
structures on `GL_n`, as in `Langlands/Representation/Unramified/HeckeOperators.lean`.
So the spherical Hecke algebras and the measures `sphericalHaar` of `F` and of `E` are the
Library's own, and the Borel structures on `GL_n(F)` and `GL_n(E)` are those that
`AreConcordant` requires.

For every prime `p` with `[Fact p.Prime]`, every `F : PadicField.{u} p`, every
`E : PadicField.{v} p`, every `F`-algebra structure on `E`
(`[Algebra F.Carrier E.Carrier]`) compatible with the two `ℚ_p`-algebra structures
(`[IsScalarTower (Padic p) F.Carrier E.Carrier]`), every `F`-algebra automorphism
`σ : E.Carrier ≃ₐ[F.Carrier] E.Carrier` and all `l r : ℕ`, assume:

1. `IsCyclicAlgebra σ l` (module `Langlands.BaseChange.Norm`): `E` is a finite
   reduced `F`-algebra of dimension `l`, `σ` has order `l`, and the elements of `E` fixed
   by `σ` are those of `F`. Since `E` is a field, this says that `E/F` is a cyclic Galois
   extension of degree `l` whose Galois group is generated by `σ`.
2. `E.canonicalTopologicalData.ramificationIndex =
   F.canonicalTopologicalData.ramificationIndex`. The Library's `ramificationIndex` of a
   `PadicField` is the valuation of `p` for its normalized valuation, i.e. its absolute
   ramification index over `ℚ_p`. Since the absolute ramification index of `E` is the
   ramification index of `E/F` times that of `F`, the hypothesis says that `E/F` is
   unramified. This is the Library's way of saying "unramified", as in
   `Langlands/Weil/Local/FiniteGaloisUnramified.lean`.

Then for every complex algebra homomorphism
`b : SphericalHeckeAlgebra E r →ₐ[ℂ] SphericalHeckeAlgebra F r` with
`IsUnramifiedBaseChangeHom F E l r b` and every `φ : SphericalHeckeAlgebra E r`,

`AreConcordant σ l (sphericalHaar F r) (sphericalHaar E r) ⇑φ ⇑(b φ)`.

Here:

- `SphericalHeckeAlgebra F r` is the Library's spherical Hecke algebra of `GL_{r+1}(F)`
  for `K = GL_{r+1}(O_F)` (compactly supported `K`-bi-invariant functions), with
  convolution for `sphericalHaar F r`, the Haar measure giving `K` mass one; `⇑φ` and
  `⇑(b φ)` are the elements viewed as functions on `GL_{r+1}(E)` and `GL_{r+1}(F)`.
- `IsUnramifiedBaseChangeHom F E l r b` (module `Langlands.BaseChange.UnramifiedHecke`):
  for every Satake parameter `s : Eigenvalues (r + 1)`, the Satake character of `F` at
  `s` composed with `b` is the Satake character of `E` at `Eigenvalues.power s l`; in
  terms of the Satake transforms, `S_F(b φ)(s) = S_E(φ)(s^l)`. This is HL's base-change
  homomorphism, defined by `S_F(b(φ))(X_1, …, X_n) = S_E(φ)(X_1^l, …, X_n^l)`.
- `AreConcordant σ l μF μE φ f` (module `Langlands.BaseChange.OrbitalIntegral`)
  is HL's "`φ` et `f` concordent" for a function `φ` on `GL_{r+1}(E)` and a function `f`
  on `GL_{r+1}(F)`, with `μF`, `μE` the Haar measures on the two groups: (a) at every
  regular semisimple `γ ∈ GL_{r+1}(F)` (`IsRegularSemisimple`: separable characteristic
  polynomial) that is not a norm (no `δ ∈ GL_{r+1}(E)` whose norm
  `N(δ) = δ · σ(δ) ⋯ σ^{l-1}(δ)` is conjugate in `GL_{r+1}(E)` to `γ`), every orbital
  integral of `f`, for every right Haar measure on the centralizer of `γ` and every
  compatible quotient measure, vanishes; (b) at every associated pair `(δ, γ)` (`N(δ)`
  conjugate in `GL_{r+1}(E)` to `γ`) with `γ` regular semisimple, the σ-orbital integral
  of `φ` at `δ` equals the orbital integral of `f` at `γ`, for all associated right Haar
  measures on the σ-centralizer of `δ` and the centralizer of `γ` and all quotient
  measures compatible with `μE` and `μF`. The paired Markdown of
  `Langlands.BaseChange.OrbitalIntegral` gives the details.

In words: for an unramified cyclic extension `E/F` of `p`-adic fields of degree `l` with
generator `σ` of its Galois group, every function `φ` in the spherical Hecke algebra of
`GL_{r+1}(E)` is concordant with its image `b(φ)` under the unramified base-change
homomorphism, for the Haar measures giving `GL_{r+1}(O_E)` and `GL_{r+1}(O_F)` volume one.

The names `IsCyclicAlgebra` and `AreConcordant` are used exactly as published.

## How this matches HL, and what is left out

- Characteristic. HL work over non-archimedean local fields of any characteristic. This
  statement is for `PadicField`s, finite extensions of `ℚ_p`, so of characteristic zero:
  this is the setting of the Library's spherical Hecke algebra and of the target of input
  (I3), the weak local base-change premise for supercuspidal classes, which needs the
  fundamental lemma for `p`-adic fields. In
  characteristic zero HL's regular elements (characteristic polynomial a product of
  pairwise distinct irreducible polynomials) are exactly the elements with separable
  characteristic polynomial, which is the Library's `IsRegularSemisimple` used by
  `AreConcordant`.
- Measures. HL fix the Haar measures giving `K` and `K_E` volume one (I.1.1 and I.3.1; in
  the proof of I.6.6 they impose this normalization again at the place `v_0` that gives back
  `E/F`, printed p. 47). This is `sphericalHaar F r` and `sphericalHaar E r`, the Haar
  measures of the Library giving `GL_{r+1}(O_F)` and `GL_{r+1}(O_E)` mass one; the
  algebra structures of the two spherical Hecke algebras are also defined with them.
- Quantification over `b`. The Library has no Satake transform, so the base-change
  homomorphism is not constructed; it is characterized by `IsUnramifiedBaseChangeHom`,
  and the statement quantifies over every `b` satisfying it. Statement 7
  (`Langlands.BaseChange.UnramifiedBaseChangeHomStatement`) asserts that exactly one `b`
  satisfies this hypothesis, so once statement 7 is proved this statement is not vacuous
  and is about HL's `b`.
- Standard elements of `E`. `IsUnramifiedBaseChangeHom` uses the standard elements and
  Satake scalars of `E` built from the uniformizer and residue cardinality `q_E` of `E`.
  For unramified `E/F` of degree `l`, a uniformizer of `F` is one of `E` and
  `q_E = q_F^l`, so these are the objects HL use in I.3.1 (see the Markdown of
  `Langlands.BaseChange.UnramifiedHecke`).
- Not included. The algebra case `E = F^l` and more generally unramified cyclic algebras
  `E = E_1 × ⋯ × E_1` (HL I.3.2, printed pp. 25-26) and the variant with functions
  transforming by an unramified central character (HL I.3.3, printed p. 26) are not part
  of this statement. Nor is strong concordance ("concordent fortement", or "concordent en
  les éléments fermés"; HL I.2.5, Définition, printed p. 17), which HL define by replacing
  "régulier" by "fermé" in both clauses of concordance: the orbital integral of `f`
  vanishes at every closed `γ ∈ G(F)` that is not a norm, and `φ` and `f` concord at every
  associated pair `(δ, γ)` with `γ` closed. Both clauses then also apply at closed
  elements that are not regular. This statement applies both clauses only at regular
  semisimple `γ`, as in `AreConcordant`.
- Relation to statement 8. Statement 8
  (`Langlands.BaseChange.UnitFundamentalLemmaStatement`) is the case `φ = 1` of this
  statement, since `b 1 = 1` for every algebra homomorphism `b`. It is stated separately
  because HL (after Kottwitz) and Arthur-Clozel prove it first, by a different argument,
  and deduce the general case from it.
