# The distinguished standard Euler polynomials

`DistinguishedStandardEulerPolynomialsStatement` concerns a completed pair
with cuspidal forward and contragredient first presentations of rank at least
two, constant rank-one second presentations, and irreducible admissible
finite components. At a chosen finite place, supply the actual JPSS standard
epsilon and forward polynomial certificates for the original first component
at the chosen additive character and Haar measure. Supply the dual polynomial
certificate for the inverse-transpose pair at the negated character with the
same measure. The two polynomials in the completed pair choice are the two
supplied JPSS polynomials.

The epsilon certificate gives a nonzero standard Whittaker functional of
the first component. The constant character gives the second one. For these
generic representations, the Langlands polynomial equals the JPSS polynomial.
The actual local component of the global contragredient agrees with the
smooth dual, which is equivalent to the inverse-transpose pullback. Changing
the additive character by minus one preserves the dual polynomial. The
same generic-pair argument identifies the polynomial in the dual equation.

The two identities let the local forward and reflected dual root separation
be used in the completed comparison at the distinguished place of
Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, proof of Proposition 6.9(ii), printed p. 70.
