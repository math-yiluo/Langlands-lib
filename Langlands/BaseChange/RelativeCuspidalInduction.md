# Normalization of relative induction from cuspidal data

Let `F` be a finite extension of `ℚ_p` with supplied `F.TopologicalData`.
`RelativeCuspidalInductionNormalizationStatement` applies to every
standard coarse Levi, each block refinement, and every complex inducing
representation on the flattened Levi. The field and inducing carrier
universes are independent.

For each block refinement, the relative parabolic inside the coarse Levi is
the product of the parabolics in the individual blocks. The proof applies the
ordered matrix-coordinate description to the block diagonal embedding of
the coarse Levi. The projection comparison identifies the relative diagonal
blocks with the coordinates of the refined Levi.

The relative half modulus in flattened Levi coordinates is the pullback of
the product of the inner half moduli. Its kernel is open and contains every
refined unipotent radical of the flattened Levi. The latter property follows
from the determinant formula for the inner moduli and the determinant-one
property of every unipotent block. Twisting by the half modulus or its inverse
preserves admissibility and leaves the Jacquet coinvariant quotient unchanged
for every proper standard refinement of the flattened Levi. This preserves
and reflects cuspidality.

The relative and refined parabolics act on the same sections after identifying
their Levi projections. The comparison with normalized induction multiplies
the inducing action by the positive half modulus. Its inverse twist reads the
original unnormalized induction as a normalized induction, giving an
equivalence of ordinary equivariant complex representations of the coarse Levi.

Bernstein and Zelevinsky, *Induced representations of reductive p-adic
groups I* (1977), §§1.7–1.8, give the half-modulus convention. Sections
2.1–2.3 use subpartitions for parabolics inside a Levi, and §2.4 gives
the cuspidality criterion of admissibility and vanishing Jacquet
coinvariants at all proper standard refinements. See printed pp. 444
