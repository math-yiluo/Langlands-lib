# Transport of sigma-lifts through field isomorphisms

`SigmaLiftFieldEquivTransportStatement` takes bicontinuous field isomorphisms
between two extensions, compatible with the scalar inclusions and the specified
cyclic automorphisms. Given a distributional sigma-lift on the target pair, its
pullback is a distributional sigma-lift on the source pair with the same constant.
It retains the same linear intertwining operator on the representation carrier.

The field maps commute with entrywise automorphisms, the ordered cyclic norm and
the scalar inclusions. They preserve and reflect norm association, and the
characteristic polynomial shows that they preserve regular semisimplicity.
Pushing forward the Haar measures on the actual centralizers preserves their
association because the two maps to the upper group commute with the field
maps. Compatibility of the quotient measures and the orbital integrals is
transported along the induced coset-space homeomorphisms. This gives concordance
of the transported tests, including vanishing at a regular non-norm. The
transport of twisted and ordinary distribution characters then gives the claimed
identity.

This is an internal transport result for the distinguished completion in
Arthur–Clozel, Chapter 1, §6.3 and the proof of Proposition 6.9(ii). It uses the
distributional definition of a sigma-lift throughout.
