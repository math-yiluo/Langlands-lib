# A common finite abelian stage for the Artin conductor comparison

A finite-order continuous character has a finite image in the roots of
unity, so its kernel is open and has finite index. The local norm-subgroup
existence theorem realizes this kernel as a finite abelian norm group.
Composing with the given abelian extension gives a common finite stage
whose norm group is contained in the character kernel.

For a supplied finite abelian tower, the geometric Artin symbol sends
the determinant norm to restriction of scalars on the Galois groups.
The proof applies the published functoriality of the arithmetic symbol
on abelianizations to the inverse unit. This square and the kernel of
the finite symbol identify the inverse image of the relative Galois
group with the actual norm subgroup of the middle field.
