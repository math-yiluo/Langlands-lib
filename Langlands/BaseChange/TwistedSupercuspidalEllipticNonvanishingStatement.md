# Twisted traces of σ-stable supercuspidals are detected on the σ-elliptic set

The module `Langlands.BaseChange.TwistedSupercuspidalEllipticNonvanishingStatement` states, as
the proposition `TwistedSupercuspidalEllipticNonvanishingStatement.{u, v, uW}`, that the twisted
distribution character `φ ↦ tr(τ(φ) ∘ A)` of a σ-stable irreducible supercuspidal
representation `τ` of `GL_n(E)`, `0 < n`, for a cyclic field extension `E/F`, is nonzero on some
Schwartz–Bruhat function supported in the σ-elliptic set. The statement is **not** proved here.

## Notation (Library and Mathlib)

- `IsCyclicAlgebra σ l` (module `Langlands.BaseChange.Norm`); here `E` is moreover a field, so
  `E/F` is a cyclic field extension of degree `l` with Galois group generated by `σ`.
- `IsSigmaElliptic σ l δ` (module `Langlands.BaseChange.Norm`): `N(δ) = δ σ(δ) ⋯ σ^{l-1}(δ)`
  is conjugate in `GL_n(E)` to a regular elliptic element of `GL_n(F)` (separable and
  irreducible characteristic polynomial). `IsSigmaEllipticSupported σ l φ` (module
  `Langlands.BaseChange.EllipticSupport`): every `δ` with `φ δ ≠ 0` is σ-elliptic.
- `GeneralLinear.IsSupercuspidal τ` (module
  `Langlands.Representation.Classification.Supercuspidal`): `τ` is admissible (first component,
  `hτ.1`) and its Jacquet coinvariants vanish along every proper block parabolic subgroup and
  its conjugates.
- `SigmaIntertwiner σ n τ`, `twistedDistribution μ τ hτ.1 A.toLinearMap φ = tr(τ(φ) ∘ A)` and
  `SchwartzBruhat` as in `TwistedTraceRegularNonvanishingStatement`.

## Statement

`TwistedSupercuspidalEllipticNonvanishingStatement.{u, v, uW}` quantifies over a field
`F : Type u` with `[ValuativeRel F] [TopologicalSpace F] [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField F]
[CharZero F]`; a field `E : Type v` with `[Algebra F E] [TopologicalSpace E]
[IsTopologicalRing E] [IsModuleTopology F E]`; `σ : E ≃ₐ[F] E`, `l n : ℕ` with
`hE : IsCyclicAlgebra σ l` and `0 < n`. The topological instances and the Borel σ-algebra on
`GL_n(E)` are introduced inside the statement as in the Library's
`TwistedCharacterRegularValuesStatement`.

The claim: for every complex vector space `W : Type uW`, every
`τ : Representation ℂ (GeneralLinear n E) W` with `hτ : GeneralLinear.IsSupercuspidal τ` and
`τ.IsIrreducible`, every `A : SigmaIntertwiner σ n τ` and every Haar measure `μ` on `GL_n(E)`,
there is `φ : SchwartzBruhat (GeneralLinear n E)` with `IsSigmaEllipticSupported σ l φ` and
`twistedDistribution μ τ hτ.1 A.toLinearMap φ ≠ 0`.

The hypothesis `0 < n` is needed: `GL_0` has no regular elliptic element (the characteristic
polynomial `1` is not irreducible), so only `φ = 0` is σ-elliptically supported.

## How it follows (sketch for the reviewer)

The modulus of the smooth central character of `τ` is trivial on the units of `E` and is a
real power of the normalized absolute value on `E^×`. Since the determinant of `z I_n` is
`z^n` and `n > 0`, choose `s ∈ ℝ` so that `τ' = τ ⊗ |det|_E^{-s}` has unitary central character.
It is then unitary and tempered, since it is supercuspidal, and it is not a subquotient of a
proper parabolic induction. It is σ-stable because `|det|_E ∘ σ = |det|_E`, and hence is
σ-discrete. The same `A` intertwines `τ'` with its σ-twist. By Schur's lemma it differs from a
normalized intertwiner by a nonzero scalar. Arthur–Clozel Lemma 2.12(i) therefore provides
some σ-elliptic `δ` with `Θ_{τ',A}(δ) ≠ 0`. Multiplication of the test function by `|det|_E^s`
in the integrated action gives `Θ_{τ,A} = |det|_E^s · Θ_{τ',A}` on the σ-regular set, so the
value at `δ` is still nonzero.

For openness, use the continuous map sending `δ` to the coefficients of the characteristic
polynomial of `N(δ)`. These coefficients lie in `F`: `σ(N(δ)) = δ⁻¹ N(δ) δ`, and `F` has the
subspace topology from `E`. By descent of the norm conjugacy class (Arthur–Clozel Lemma 1.1(i)),
the σ-elliptic locus is the preimage of the monic separable irreducible locus over `F`, which
is open by Hensel's lemma and Krasner's lemma. Proposition 2.2 gives local constancy of the
twisted character there. Choose a compact open neighbourhood `U` of `δ` within that locus on
which `Θ_{τ,A}` is constant. Its indicator `φ` satisfies the support predicate and has
`tr(τ(φ) ∘ A) = μ(U) · Θ_{τ,A}(δ) ≠ 0`, since `0 < μ(U) < ∞` for any Haar measure `μ`.

## Derived content

This is a local test-function consequence of Arthur–Clozel Lemma 2.12(i) and Proposition 2.2
in the Library's notation. It specializes σ-discreteness to supercuspidals after unramified
twisting, and allows every intertwining isomorphism and every Haar measure rather than fixing
normalizations. Henniart–Lemaire II.5.14 gives a parallel pointwise result; II.6.4 explains
the role of σ-elliptic support but is not the source of the local existence assertion. The
field case suffices for the intended application at a place with exactly one place above it,
where the completion is a field.
