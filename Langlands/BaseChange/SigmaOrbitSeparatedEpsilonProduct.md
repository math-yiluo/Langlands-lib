# Conditional epsilon-product step for a sigma-orbit

`SigmaOrbitSeparatedEpsilonProductStatement` is the local epsilon-multiplicativity step for Task 7843, under explicit block hypotheses. The representation `ρ` is equivalent to the Library's `sigmaOrbitProduct` reindexed by the actual rank equality. Over the supplied local field `E` and for a self-dual additive Haar measure, the actual orbit blocks must have full Whittaker type. Each block is supplied with a standard epsilon certificate and with standard L-polynomial certificates for the original and inverse-transpose dual pairs, at the original and inverse additive characters respectively. Here standard means paired with the trivial `GL₁` character (or its inverse-transpose pullback on the dual side). At nonzero roots of the product of the original block polynomials, the dual product must be nonzero at the reflected argument `q_E⁻¹ / x`.

The result certifies the standard epsilon factor of `ρ` with coefficient equal to the product and exponent equal to the sum of the block data. It also gives the corresponding identity of monomial values at every complex spectral parameter.

`sigmaOrbitSeparatedEpsilonProduct` applies `separatedStandardFactorProduct` to the actual entrywise pullbacks of `P₁`. The proof checks that rank substitution agrees with composition by the matrix reindexing map, then uses `normalizedProductRankCastEquiv` and invariance of `IsPairEpsilonFactor` under representation equivalence. The published `epsilonMonomial_factorProduct` with a singleton second index gives the pointwise identity. The companion modules derive the separated product theorem from JPSS (1983), Theorem (3.1)(i)–(ii), printed pp. 404–405, and an elementary root argument.

This is an internal conditional step toward Arthur–Clozel (1989), Ch. 1, Proposition 6.9(ii), not a citation of that proposition as an input. The statement does not establish the block hypotheses for a supercuspidal orbit, or the trace-character, conductor-exponent, or global comparison steps of the conductor identity.
