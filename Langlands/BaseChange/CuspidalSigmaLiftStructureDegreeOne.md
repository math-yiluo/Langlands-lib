# Supercuspidal σ-lift structure in extension degree one

Let \(E/F\) be a cyclic extension of nonarchimedean local fields of degree one with
generator \(\sigma\). Assume \(F\) has characteristic zero and \(E\) carries its
\(F\)-module topology. For \(n>0\), let \(\pi\) be an irreducible admissible
supercuspidal complex representation of \(\mathrm{GL}_n(F)\), and let \(P\) be an
irreducible admissible complex representation of \(\mathrm{GL}_n(E)\) that is a
\(\sigma\)-lift of \(\pi\) with the given intertwiner and comparison constant.
`CuspidalSigmaLiftStructureDegreeOneStatement` says that the norm-twist stabilizer of
\(\pi\) has cardinality one and that \(P\) is supercuspidal. For every natural number
\(i\), \(P\) is equivalent to its pullback by the entrywise action of \(\sigma^i\).
After rank reindexing, \(P\) is equivalent to the normalized orbit product with \(P\)
as its single block. The two fields and the two representation carriers have
independent universes.

The cyclic generator is the identity. Its fixed-field condition makes the scalar embedding
bijective, and the module topologies make its entrywise map on general linear groups a
homeomorphism. Every base element is a norm. At an associated regular pair, conjugation
reduces to exact norm equality. Association of the centralizer measures and uniqueness of
the compatible quotient measure then identify the two orbital integrals by change of variables.
Thus every upper test function and its pullback are concordant for an ambient Haar measure
and its pushforward.

Changing variables in the integrated action makes the lift identity a proportionality of
twisted distributions on the base group with the identity twist. Twisted character
separation identifies the pullback of the lift with the original representation.
Supercuspidality is invariant under the field isomorphism. Stabilizer divisibility gives
cardinality one, and the single-block normalization and rank transport give the orbit product.

The structure conclusion specializes Henniart–Lemaire, *Changement de base et induction
automorphe pour GL_n en caractéristique non nulle*, II.4.12, printed p. 96. The direct
degree-one proof uses their concordance and lift definitions in I.2.5 and II.2.7, printed
pp. 17–18 and 67. The separation step is Arthur–Clozel, *Simple Algebras, Base Change,
and the Advanced Theory of the Trace Formula*, Chapter 1, Lemma 6.3, printed p. 52.
