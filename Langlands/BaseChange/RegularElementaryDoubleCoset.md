# Double coset for a regular elementary test

`RegularElementaryDoubleCosetStatement` uses the compact open subgroup
\(I=T(\mathcal O)K_1\), the inverse image of the diagonal torus in
reduction. For a diagonal \(t\) of strictly decreasing orders,
the double coset \(ItI\) is the twisted conjugation saturation of
\(tT(\mathcal O)\) by \(I\). The field can be any canonical p-adic
field carrier and the twist any algebra automorphism over \(\mathbb Q_p\).

The proof uses the strict Iwahori filtration starting with \(I\) and
continuing through the positive principal levels. The automorphism
preserves valuation and hence each level. The coordinate ratios of
the strict diagonal send the two unipotent factors one principal level
deeper under the respective conjugations. The contracting double-coset
identity then identifies the support, using the diagonal description of
the singleton Levi. For the Labesse trace calculation, the full
spherical subgroup saturation of these weighted diagonal units is
the support of the regular elementary test in Henniart–Lemaire, I.4.1.
