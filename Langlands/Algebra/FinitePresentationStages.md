# Finite stages of a presentation over finitely generated subrings

Author: Worker 866.

This helper module writes an algebra `B` over `A` with a presentation as a
directed system of models over subrings of `A` that are finitely generated over
`ℤ`. These are the stages used to descend flatness to a finite type base. All
declarations live in `Langlands.Algebra.FinitePresentationStages`, and the
module adds no axioms.

## Provenance

- Stacks Project, Algebra, Lemma 10.127.18 (in Section 10.127, Tag 00QL): a
  finitely presented `R → S` is the directed colimit of ring maps
  `R_λ → S_λ` with `R_λ` of finite type over `ℤ`, `S_λ` of finite type over
  `R_λ`, and `S_λ ⊗_{R_λ} R_μ → S_μ` an isomorphism for `λ ≤ μ`. Stacks
  leaves the proof to the reader. This module builds that system for the
  algebra alone, without the module `M`. Stacks Algebra, Lemma 10.168.1
  uses it.
- The construction follows the usual proof. The stage `t` is indexed by a
  finite subset `t ⊆ A`. Its base ring is the `ℤ`-subalgebra of `A`
  generated by `t` and the coefficients of the relations, and its model is the
  polynomial ring over that subring modulo the same relations. The model is
  Mathlib's `Algebra.Presentation.ModelOfHasCoeffs`, so it comes with
  Mathlib's comparison `A ⊗[base] model ≃ₐ[A] B`
  (`Algebra.Presentation.tensorModelOfHasCoeffsEquiv`).
- The colimit property is not stated as a categorical colimit. It is given by
  the two element-level facts `exists_lift` and `exists_trans_eq_zero` below.
  The bases are subrings of `A`, so `A` is their directed union.
- `flat_localization_atPrime_of_isPushout` is a standard fact about base
  change and localization, stated here because the stage arguments need it.

## Declarations and Lean proofs

Throughout, `P : Algebra.Presentation A B ι σ` is a presentation of `B` over
`A` and `t, t' : Finset A`.

### Stages

`base P t` (definition): the subalgebra `Algebra.adjoin ℤ (P.coeffs ∪ t)` of
`A`. `coeffs_subset_base`, `mem_base_of_mem` and `base_mono` (monotone in `t`)
follow from `Algebra.subset_adjoin` and `Algebra.adjoin_mono`.

Instances: `P.HasCoeffs (base P t)`, because the base contains the
coefficients. If `σ` is finite, `base P t` is of finite type over `ℤ`
(`Algebra.FiniteType.adjoin_of_finite`, as `P.coeffs` is then finite) and
hence noetherian.

`model P t` (abbreviation): `P.ModelOfHasCoeffs (base P t)`, the polynomial
ring `MvPolynomial ι (base P t)` modulo the relations
`P.relationOfHasCoeffs (base P t)`, whose images in `MvPolynomial ι A` are the
relations of `P`.

`toB P t` (definition): the `base P t`-algebra map `model P t → B` induced by
`MvPolynomial.aeval P.val`. It is well defined because each relation maps to
zero in `B`. `toB_mk` computes it on residue classes.

`gen P t i` (definition): the class of the variable `X i` in the model.
`aeval_gen`: evaluating a polynomial at the generators gives its residue
class, from `MvPolynomial.comp_aeval`.

`tensorModelOfHasCoeffsEquiv_one_tmul`: Mathlib's comparison isomorphism
sends `1 ⊗ x` to `toB P t x`. Both sides agree on residue classes.

`nonempty_tensor_model`: `A ⊗[base P t] model P t ≃ₐ[A] B`, namely Mathlib's
comparison isomorphism.

Instance: if `ι` and `σ` are finite, the model is finitely presented over the
base. Mathlib provides this instance.

### Transition maps

`map_relationOfHasCoeffs`: for `t ⊆ t'`, the inclusion of bases maps the
descended relations at `t` to those at `t'`. Both have the same image in
`MvPolynomial ι A`, and `MvPolynomial.map` along an injective map is injective.

`trans P h` (definition, `h : t ⊆ t'`): the ring map
`model P t → model P t'` induced by mapping coefficients along the inclusion.
It is well defined by `map_relationOfHasCoeffs`.

`trans_mk`, `trans_algebraMap`, `trans_gen`: how `trans` acts on residue
classes, on base elements and on generators. `toB_trans` says
`toB P t' ∘ trans P h = toB P t`, using `MvPolynomial.eval₂_map`. `trans_refl`
and `trans_trans` say that the transitions form a functor on the poset of
finite subsets, using `MvPolynomial.map_id` and `MvPolynomial.map_map`.

### Colimit properties

`exists_lift`: for every finite `t₀` and every `b : B` there are `t ⊇ t₀` and
`x : model P t` with `toB P t x = b`. Write `b = aeval P.val F`, which is
possible because `aeval P.val` is surjective. Then take
`t = t₀ ∪ F.coeffs`, so that `F` has coefficients in the base.

`exists_trans_eq_zero` (assuming `σ` finite): if `toB P t x = 0`, then
`trans P h x = 0` for some `t'` with `h : t ⊆ t'`. A polynomial `F`
representing `x` lies in the kernel of `aeval P.val`, which is spanned by the
relations of `P`. So `F = ∑ c r · relation r` in `MvPolynomial ι A`.
Enlarging `t` by the coefficients of all the `c r` writes the image of `F` as
such a combination over `base P t'`, using injectivity of
`MvPolynomial.map`.

### Transitions are base changes

For `h : t ⊆ t'`, `baseAlgebra P h` is the algebra structure of `base P t'`
over `base P t` given by inclusion. `modelAlgebra P h` is that of
`model P t'` over `model P t` given by `trans P h`. `transAlg P h` is
`trans P h` as a `base P t`-algebra map, and `transAlg_apply` unfolds it.

`tensorHom P h` (definition) is the `base P t'`-algebra map
`base P t' ⊗[base P t] model P t → model P t'` given by `c ⊗ x ↦ c · trans x`.
`tensorInv P h` (definition) is the map in the other direction induced by
`X i ↦ 1 ⊗ gen P t i`. It is well defined because the relations at `t'` are
the images of those at `t` (`map_relationOfHasCoeffs`), and those vanish on
the right factor. `tensorHom_tmul` and `tensorInv_mk` compute these maps.

`tensorEquiv P h` (definition): the isomorphism
`base P t' ⊗[base P t] model P t ≃ₐ[base P t'] model P t'` built from
`tensorHom` and `tensorInv`. Both composites are the identity: check this on
the generators `gen`, and on the left factor `base P t'` of the tensor
product, where all algebra maps from `base P t'` agree. `tensorEquiv_tmul`
computes it.

`isScalarTower_model`: with these algebra structures, `base P t`,
`model P t` and `model P t'` form a scalar tower, by `trans_algebraMap`.

`isPushout`: the square of `base P t`, `model P t`, `base P t'` and
`model P t'` is an `Algebra.IsPushout`. The proof applies
`IsBaseChange.of_equiv` to `tensorEquiv`, then `Algebra.IsPushout.symm`.

### Flatness at primes along transitions

`flat_localization_atPrime_of_isPushout`: take a pushout square
`R → S`, `R → R'`, `S' = R' ⊗_R S`, and a prime `q'` of `S'`. If the
localization of `S` at `q = q' ∩ S` is flat over `R`, then `S'_{q'}` is flat
over `R'`. Proof: `T = S'` localized at the image of `S \ q` is the pushout of
`S_q` along `S → S'` (`Algebra.isLocalization_iff_isPushout`). Pasting the
squares (`Algebra.IsPushout.comp_iff`), `T = R' ⊗_R S_q`, so `T` is flat over
`R'` (`Module.Flat.isBaseChange`). `S'_{q'}` is a localization of `T`, since
`S \ q` maps into `S' \ q'` (`IsLocalization.isLocalization_of_submonoid_le`).
Hence it is flat over `T`, and over `R'` by `Module.Flat.trans`.

`flat_localization_trans`: for `h : t ⊆ t'` and a prime `q'` of `model P t'`,
if the localization of `model P t` at `q'.comap (trans P h)` is flat over
`base P t`, then `model P t'` localized at `q'` is flat over `base P t'`. This
is the previous lemma applied to `isPushout`.

`comap_trans_comap_toB`: for an ideal `Q` of `B`, its preimages in the stages
are compatible with the transitions, by `toB_trans`.
