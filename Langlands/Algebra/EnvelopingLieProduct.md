# Enveloping algebras of products of Lie algebras

This internal algebra comparison supports the finite product of complex
matrix Lie algebras in the parabolic center calculation of Harish-Chandra,
*Automorphic forms on semisimple Lie groups* (1968), Chapter I, §6,
printed pp. 19–20 (PDF pp. 29–30).

Over a commutative ring, the enveloping algebra of a product Lie algebra
is the tensor product of the two enveloping algebras. The forward map
uses the enveloping lift of the sum of the two projected generators in
the tensor factors. In the backward map the factor inclusions have
commuting images because their Lie generators have zero mutual bracket.
Commutation extends from generators to the whole algebras under sums
and products. The enveloping and tensor universal properties identify
both composites with the corresponding identities.
