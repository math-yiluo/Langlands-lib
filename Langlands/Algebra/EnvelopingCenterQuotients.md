# Quotients of centers of product enveloping algebras

The quotient lemmas preserve finite-dimensional quotients by
contraction and by extension along a finite map. They also give a
finite-dimensional quotient of a tensor product when both factor
contractions have finite-dimensional quotients. The tensor product
of the two contracted quotients surjects onto the full quotient.

The center lemmas give the actual enveloping inclusion on each factor.
For an inclusion with commuting complement, every ambient Lie
generator is the sum of an image generator and a direction that
commutes with the image. This verifies centrality on generators and
therefore on the ambient enveloping algebra.
