# Upper stages of a synchronized local algebra tower approximation

This module builds the upper rings `Tᵢ` of the synchronized tower used to prove
`Langlands.Algebra.LocalModuleTowerApproximationExistenceStatement` (Stacks 00R7, Lemma
10.128.8, the construction before the flatness argument). The input is a ring approximation
system `D` for `R → S` together with lifts of the relations of a presentation of `T` over
`S`. The output is a `LocalAlgebraTowerSystem` with lower arrow exactly `D`, and a proof that
it satisfies `LocalAlgebraTowerSystem.IsApproximation` whenever `D.IsApproximation` holds.

## Comparison with the source construction

Stacks presents `S′` (here `T`) over `R` by a single flattened polynomial quotient that
includes the relations of `S`, and localizes it over the stages of `R`. This module works
over the middle stages instead. Let `P : Pres S S T (RingEquiv.refl S)` be a presentation of
`T` over `S`; it comes from `Langlands.Algebra.LocalAlgebraTowerUpperPresentation`. The upper
stage at `i` is the localization of `Sᵢ[x₁, …, xₙ]/(lifted relations of P)` at the inverse
image of the maximal ideal of `T`. Here `Sᵢ = D.alg i`. The relations of `S` over `R` are
already built into the stages `Sᵢ`, so no flattened quotient is needed. The composite
localization condition comes from the general lemma
`LocalAlgebraTowerSystem.rt_isPrimeLocalization`
(`Langlands.Algebra.LocalAlgebraTowerPrimeLocalization`).

## Polynomials over the components (namespace `LocalAlgebraSystem`)

For a ring system `D : LocalAlgebraSystem ι R S`, transitions and cocone maps act on
polynomials coefficientwise through `MvPolynomial.map`:

- `map_algMap_map_algMap`, `map_algMap_self` and `map_algι_map_algMap` say that
  coefficientwise transitions compose, are the identity along `i ≤ i`, and are compatible
  with the coefficientwise cocone maps.
- `exists_map_algι_eq_fun`: if `D.algebraColimitMap` is surjective, a finite family of
  polynomials over `S` lifts to one component. The proof lifts the finite set of all their
  coefficients with `exists_algι_eq_fun` and then applies
  `MvPolynomial.mem_range_map_iff_coeffs_subset`.
- `exists_map_algMap_eq_zero_fun`: if `D.algebraColimitMap` is injective, a finite family of
  component polynomials whose images over `S` vanish is killed by one transition. The proof
  applies `exists_algMap_eq_zero_fun` to the coefficients on the supports.

## Data and stages (namespace `LocalAlgebraTowerApproximation`)

`RelLift D P` records, for every index `i` and every relation `f ∈ P.F`, a polynomial
`lift i f` over `D.alg i`. It has two conditions:

- (`map_lift`) the transition `i ≤ j` sends `lift i f` to `lift j f`;
- (`algι_lift`) the cocone map sends `lift i f` to `f`.

For `L : RelLift D P`, in namespace `RelLift`:

- `rel i` is the ideal generated by the `lift i f`.
- `rel_map`: the transition maps `rel i` onto `rel j`, that is, its extension is `rel j`.
- `Q i` is the finite type `D.alg i`-algebra `MvPolynomial (Fin P.n) (D.alg i) ⧸ rel i`,
  with quotient map `mkQ i`.
- `ρQ i : Q i →+* T` is induced by `P.ρ ∘ map (D.algι i)`. It kills `rel i` by `algι_lift`
  and `P.ρ_F`.
- `QMap h : Q i →+* Q j` is induced by the coefficientwise transition. It satisfies
  `ρQ j ∘ QMap h = ρQ i` and the identity and composition laws.
- `q i` is the inverse image in `Q i` of the maximal ideal of `T`. It is prime and satisfies
  `q i = (q j).comap (QMap h)`.
- `Tl i` is `Localization.AtPrime (q i)`, the upper stage.
- `topι i : Tl i →+* T` is the map induced by `ρQ i`. `TMap h` is
  `Localization.localRingHom` for `QMap h`.

The following facts are proved:

- `TMap_self` and `TMap_TMap`: the upper stages form a directed system.
- `topι_TMap`: the maps `topι` form a compatible cocone.
- `isLocalHom_topι`: each `topι i` is local. If the image of `a / s` is a unit, then `ρQ i a`
  is a unit, so `a ∉ q i`.
- `TMap_algebraMap_alg` and `topι_algebraMap_alg`: the transitions and the cocone are
  compatible with the structure maps from `D.alg i`. The second uses `P.ρ_C`.
- `isLocalHom_algebraMap`: the structure map `D.alg i → Tl i` is local. If its image of `s` is
  a unit, then `algebraMap S T (D.algι i s)` is a unit, and `s` is a unit because
  `algebraMap S T` and `D.algι i` are local.
- `essFiniteType_Tl`: `Tl i` is essentially of finite type over `D.alg i`, since it is a
  localization of a finite type algebra.

`tower` is the `LocalAlgebraTowerSystem ι R S T` whose lower arrow `rs` is `D` itself and
whose upper rings, transitions and cocone are `Tl`, `TMap` and `topι`. So the middle rings,
transitions and cocone of the tower are exactly those of `D`.

## The upper colimit

- `exists_topι_eq`: if `D.algebraColimitMap` is surjective, every `t ∈ T` comes from some
  `Tl i`. Use `P.surj` to write `t * P.ρ c = P.ρ a` with `P.ρ c` a unit. Lift `a` and `c` to
  one component. The fraction of their classes in `Tl i` then maps to `t`.
- `exists_TMap_eq_zero`: if `D.IsColimit`, an element `x` of `Tl i` with `topι i x = 0`
  vanishes at some later stage. Write `x = g / s`, so that `P.ρ (map (D.algι i) g) = 0`.
  `P.ker` gives `c` with `P.ρ c` a unit and `c * map (D.algι i) g = Σ_f μ f * f`. Lift `c` and
  the `μ f` to a component `j`, and choose `k ≥ i, j`. The polynomial
  `c̃ * g − Σ_f μ̃ f * lift k f` over `D.alg k` maps to zero over `S`, so it is killed at some
  `l ≥ k` by `exists_map_algMap_eq_zero_fun`. At stage `l` the image of `c` times the image of
  `g` lies in `rel l`. That image of `c` lies outside `q l`, so `TMap` sends `x` to `0`.
- `tower_st_isColimit`: if `D.IsColimit`, then `tower.st.IsColimit`. The base comparison of
  `tower.st` is the algebra comparison of `D`. The upper comparison is injective by
  `exists_TMap_eq_zero` and surjective by `exists_topι_eq`.

## Prime localization of the upper transitions

`isPrimeLocalization_st h` proves `tower.st.IsPrimeLocalization h` for the actual
`ringBaseChangeMap` and its induced algebra.

- `MvPolynomial (Fin P.n) (D.alg j)` is the pushout of `MvPolynomial (Fin P.n) (D.alg i)`
  along `D.algMap i j h`. This is Mathlib's instance, with `MvPolynomial.algebraMvPolynomial`.
- `rel_map` and `isPushout_quotient_of_ideal_map_eq` make `Q j` the pushout of `Q i` along
  `D.alg i → D.alg j`.
- Since `TMap h` restricts to `QMap h` on `Q i`, the adapter
  `LocalAlgebraSystem.isPrimeLocalization_of_quotient_pushout`
  (`Langlands.Algebra.TensorTransitionPrimeLocalization`) applies with the localizations at
  `(q i).primeCompl` and `(q j).primeCompl`.

## Approximation

`tower_isApproximation hD`: if `D.IsApproximation`, then `tower.IsApproximation`. The proof
supplies the five clauses as follows:

1. `D.IsApproximation` is the given hypothesis.
2. `tower_st_isColimit` gives the upper colimit.
3. `essFiniteType_Tl` gives essential finite type of the upper stages.
4. `isPrimeLocalization_st` gives the upper prime-localization clause.
5. `LocalAlgebraTowerSystem.rt_isPrimeLocalization` gives the composite clause from the
   prime-localization clause of `D` and from item 4.

## Existence of the lifts

`exists_relLift D hD P`: if `D.algebraColimitMap` is surjective, there are an index `i₀` and
a `RelLift (D.tail i₀) P`. Lift the finitely many relations of `P` to one component `i₀`
with `exists_map_algι_eq_fun`, and push the lifts to every `j ≥ i₀` along
`D.algMap i₀ j`. `D.tail i₀` is the Library restriction of `D` to the indices above `i₀`.
