# Ordered enveloping monomials of weight zero

The Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt basis is ordered in decreasing integral
adjoint weight. A central element has zero adjoint weight, so its
nonzero coordinates lie on monomials of total weight zero. A monomial
of total weight zero either begins with a positive-weight generator
or has only weight-zero letters. This gives the sum of the positive
right ideal and the weight-zero enveloping subalgebra.

This is the existence part of the algebraic projection used for the
relative enveloping-centre argument in Langlands (1979), Lemma 4,
printed p. 204 (Corvallis Part I, PDF p. 199). The registered source is
