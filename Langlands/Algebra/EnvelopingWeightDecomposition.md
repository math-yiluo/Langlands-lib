# Ordered enveloping monomials of weight zero

Let an associative algebra over a characteristic-zero field have a
finite basis of eigenvectors for the commutator action of one element.
Assume the eigenvalues are given by integer weights. Order the
Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt monomials in decreasing generator weight.
The commutator annihilates every central element, and the integer-to-field
map detects zero total weight. Each monomial in the support of a central
element therefore has total weight zero. It either begins with a
positive-weight generator or consists of weight-zero letters. Each central
element thus lies in the sum of the positive right ideal and the
weight-zero enveloping subalgebra.

This derived algebraic construction supplies representatives for the
centre-finiteness step on Levi constant-term slices. Langlands (1979),
Lemma 4, states automorphy of those slices and gives references for local
Hecke admissibility. See printed p. 204 (Corvallis Part I, PDF p. 199).
