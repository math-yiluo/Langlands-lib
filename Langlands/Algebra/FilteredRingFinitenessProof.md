# Proof of finite-type finiteness descent

`finiteTypeFinitenessDescent` proves the paired proposition
`Langlands.Algebra.FilteredRing.FiniteTypeFinitenessDescent`.
This is an internal algebraic prerequisite for the finite-morphism
clause of Brian Conrad, *Weil and Grothendieck approaches to adelic
points* (December 31, 2011), printed pp. 5–6, Theorem 3.4(2).
The registered source is `conrad-2011-weil-grothendieck-adelic-points`,
SHA-256 `fe4a9193aff4ef575665b40971f3da32f850a74d87b3f2d0fa28bba26b9cbabb`.
The categorical algebra statement and its direct integrality proof
are derived internal content, rather than a separate assertion or
proof printed in Conrad.

`exists_monic_polynomial_lift` takes an actual monic polynomial over the
colimit ring, chooses representatives for its finitely many supported
coefficients, and places them in a common stage reached by an actual arrow
from the chosen initial stage. The Mathlib monic-lift theorem produces a
monic polynomial at that stage with exactly the given colimit image.

`exists_integral_element_stage` lifts the monic relation, evaluates it in
the second diagram, and descends equality with zero using the actual
filtered colimit of underlying sets. Mapping the lifted polynomial to the
resulting stage gives the required monic relation. Naturality preserves
its coefficients and evaluation. No transition map is assumed injective.

The main theorem chooses a finite algebra-generating set at the initial
stage. Finiteness of the colimit map makes the images of all generators
integral. Applying `exists_integral_element_stage` to each generator
and forming a cocone on their finite wide span gives a common later
stage with an actual arrow from the initial stage. Naturality transports
integrality to that common stage through the commuting square of
ring maps, using `IsIntegral.map_of_comp_eq`.

Let T be the subalgebra generated by the transported finite generating
set over the source ring at that common stage. The initial generating
property, expressed as a ring-closure equality, implies that T contains
the image of the entire initial target ring. It also contains the image
of the common-stage source ring by definition of subalgebra. The actual
pushout square says that these two images generate the common-stage
target as a ring, so T is the top subalgebra.

The generators of T form a finite set of integral elements.
`Algebra.finite_adjoin_of_finite_of_isIntegral` therefore makes T a
finite module over the common-stage source ring. The equality with
the top subalgebra and its canonical linear equivalence with the target
ring give finiteness of the specified component ring map.

The proof retains arbitrary universe u, arbitrary small filtered
categories and noninjective ring maps, including zero rings. It uses
finite type only at the initial stage and actual pushout squares for
its outgoing arrows; it assumes no finite presentation or noetherian
condition. This algebraic theorem does not by itself discharge the
scheme-level relative finite-morphism descent or original-ring
specialization.
