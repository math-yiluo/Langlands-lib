# The fibrewise Čech criterion by noetherian approximation

Authors: Worker 299 and Worker 424. Worker 299 authored the proof;
Worker 424 adapted its module dependencies for separate prerequisite
publication and rechecked the complete proof. This module proves the content of
`Langlands.Algebra.SmoothEqualizerFibreCriterion` in the parameterized form
`Langlands.Algebra.CechEqualizer.mem_range_of_smooth`; the exact-target
theorem is in `Langlands.Algebra.SmoothEqualizerFibreCriterionProof`. It
uses the helper modules `Langlands.Algebra.CechEqualizer.Good`,
`Langlands.Algebra.CechEqualizer.Model` and
`Langlands.Algebra.CechEqualizer.Descent`. The argument is an internal
derivation by Worker 299 (see the statement's Markdown for the relation to
Stacks Lemma 36.32.5, Tag 0E62); it is not a formalization of the Stacks
proof, which uses perfect complexes.

## Model identifications

The construction `modelEquiv` is imported from
`Langlands.Algebra.CechEqualizer.Definitions`; the computation lemmas below
are proved in this module.

- `includeRight_comp_algebraMap`: for `ε : C₀ ≃ₐ[A] A ⊗[R₀] B₀`, the composite
  `R₀ → B₀ → A ⊗[R₀] B₀ → C₀` equals `R₀ → A → C₀`.
- `modelEquiv A₀ ε : A ⊗[A₀] (A₀ ⊗[R] B) ≃ₐ[A] C`, for a scalar tower
  `R → A₀ → A` and `ε : C ≃ₐ[A] A ⊗[R] B`, is the cancellation isomorphism
  `Algebra.TensorProduct.cancelBaseChange` followed by `ε⁻¹`.
  `modelEquiv_tmul` computes it on `a ⊗ z` as `a • ε⁻¹ (ι z)`, and
  `modelEquiv_tmul_one` shows that it sends `a ⊗ 1` to `algebraMap A C a`.

## Descent step

`mem_range_of_descent`: let `A₀ ⊆ A` (any ring map with a scalar tower) be
noetherian, `C₀ ≃ A ⊗[R₀] B₀`, `C₁ ≃ A ⊗[R₁] B₁` via `ε₀`, `ε₁`, where
`R₀ → A₀` and `R₁ → A₀` are compatible with `A`. Suppose `δ₁`, `δ₂` are the
base changes of `A₀`-algebra maps `φ₁ φ₂ : A₀ ⊗[R₀] B₀ → A₀ ⊗[R₁] B₁`
(hypotheses `hφ₁`, `hφ₂`), `A₀ ⊗[R₀] B₀` is faithfully flat over `A₀`,
`B₁` is flat over `R₁` and the fibre hypothesis holds. Then every equalized
`c` is a scalar. Proof: apply the model criterion
`mem_range_of_model` with `d = φ₁ - φ₂` (so `d 1 = 0`),
`C₀' = A₀ ⊗[R₀] B₀`, `C₁' = A₀ ⊗[R₁] B₁` (flat over `A₀` by base change),
`e₀ = modelEquiv A₀ ε₀` and `e₁ = modelEquiv A₀ ε₁`; the compatibility
`e₁ ∘ (A ⊗ d) = (δ₁ - δ₂) ∘ e₀` is checked on pure tensors with
`modelEquiv_tmul`, `hφ₁` and `hφ₂`.

## The criterion

`mem_range_of_smooth` takes instance arguments `[Algebra.Smooth A C₀]`,
`[Algebra.Smooth A C₁]` and `[Module.FaithfullyFlat A C₀]`, the fibre
hypothesis `hfib` in exactly the form of the target, and `c` with
`δ₁ c = δ₂ c`; it concludes `c ∈ Set.range (algebraMap A C₀)`. No further
assumption is made on `A`.

1. Mathlib's noetherian descent of smooth algebras,
   `Algebra.Smooth.exists_subalgebra_fg ℤ A Cᵢ`, gives finitely generated
   `ℤ`-subalgebras `Rᵢ ⊆ A`, smooth `Rᵢ`-algebras `Bᵢ` and `A`-algebra
   isomorphisms `εᵢ : Cᵢ ≃ A ⊗[Rᵢ] Bᵢ` (`i = 0, 1`).
2. The image of `Spec B₀ → Spec R₀` is open, by Chevalley's theorem for
   flat finitely presented maps
   (`PrimeSpectrum.isOpenMap_comap_of_hasGoingDown_of_finitePresentation`).
   Every prime of `A` contracts into it: lift the prime to `Spec C₀` by
   faithful flatness (`PrimeSpectrum.comap_surjective_of_faithfullyFlat`) and
   contract along `B₀ → C₀` using `includeRight_comp_algebraMap`. Lemma
   `exists_sum_eq_one_of_isOpen` then gives `s ⊆ R₀` and coefficients
   `cf : R₀ → A` with `∑ cf x * x = 1` in `A` and each `D(x)` inside the
   image.
3. Choose finitely many generators `t` of the `R₀`-algebra `B₀`, and for
   each generator `g` and `δ ∈ {δ₁, δ₂}` a finite expression
   `ε₁ (δ (ε₀⁻¹ (1 ⊗ g))) = ∑ aᵢ ⊗ bᵢ` in `A ⊗[R₁] B₁`; let `T₂` be the
   finite set of all coefficients `aᵢ`.
4. Let `A₀ ⊆ A` be the `ℤ`-subalgebra generated by the generators of `R₀`
   and `R₁`, the coefficients `cf x` (`x ∈ s`) and `T₂`. It is of finite
   type over `ℤ`, hence noetherian, and contains `R₀` and `R₁`; the inclusion
   is injective.
5. With `ι₁ : A₀ ⊗[R₁] B₁ → A ⊗[R₁] B₁` injective (`map_ofId_injective`,
   since `B₁` is flat), `exists_algHom_comp_eq` descends each `δ` to
   `φ : A₀ ⊗[R₀] B₀ → A₀ ⊗[R₁] B₁`: on generators `1 ⊗ g` the value
   `∑ aᵢ ⊗ bᵢ` lies in the range of `ι₁` by `sum_tmul_mem_range`, because all
   `aᵢ` lie in `A₀`.
6. `A₀ ⊗[R₀] B₀` is faithfully flat over `A₀` by
   `faithfullyFlat_of_sum_eq_one`, using the coefficients `cf` restricted to
   `A₀` (they lie in `A₀` by construction) and injectivity of `A₀ → A` to
   transport the identity `∑ cf x * x = 1`.
7. `mem_range_of_descent` concludes.

Smoothness is used for the descent in step 1 and, through flatness and
finite presentation of `B₀` over `R₀`, in steps 2 and 5. Faithful flatness
of `C₀` over `A` is used in step 2.
