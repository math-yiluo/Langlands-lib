# Lifting an idempotent whose adjoint is its complement

Starting from any lift \(x\) of the lower idempotent, form
\(q=(x+1-x^*)/2\). It has the prescribed reduction and satisfies
\(q^*=1-q\). The polynomial \(p=3q^2-2q^3\) has the same reduction and
satisfies \(p^*=1-p\). The identity
\[
p^2-p=(q^2-q)^2(4q^2-4q-3)
\]
gives idempotency because \(q^2-q\) belongs to the square-zero kernel.

In a symplectic module, the image of a projection with
\(p^*=1-p\) is isotropic. This is an algebraic calculation for the
stable isotropic summand step of the polarized deformation argument
in Lan's thesis revision of 14 March 2021, Proposition 2.2.4.9,
printed pp. 158–159. The algebra map and the lower projection are
the inputs to the calculation. The source is
`lan-2008-pel-thesis-revision-2021`, SHA-256
