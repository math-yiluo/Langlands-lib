# Realization of finite Brauer profiles over totally complex number fields

Over a totally complex number field, a finitely supported family of
finite Brauer invariants in the `n`-torsion subgroup with sum zero is
realized by an actual central simple algebra of degree `n`. The
invariants use the actual scalar extensions to the finite completions
with their original topology and valuation. The theorem includes the
empty profile and values whose order is a proper divisor of `n`.

The proof uses the prescribed-degree realization for the relative
class-field invariant. The canonical class-field family satisfies the
required normalization and inflation laws. The local comparison
identifies the relative value on the scalar-extended splitting chart
with the Brauer invariant for the canonical p-adic field bundle.
Equality of the canonical and original completion topologies and
valuative relations identifies this invariant with the one on the
actual completion. The same global algebra thus realizes every
prescribed finite value.

This is an internal assembly of the published invariant comparison,
relative profile realization, and finite completion data. It supplies
the central simple algebra of the Brauer-first route to Clozel's
unitary local profiles.
