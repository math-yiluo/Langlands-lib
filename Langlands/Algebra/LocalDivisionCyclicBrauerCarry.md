# The carry cocycle of the opposite division splitting chart

For each Galois element take the matrix lift indexed by its cyclic representative between zero and `d − 1`. When two lifts are composed, coefficient transport gives the lift indexed by the sum. Removing a multiple of `d` contributes that power of the central cyclic parameter to the scalar factor. The canonical splitting chart action is the conjugation of the same lifts. Thus the scalar factor set is the positive carry cocycle defining the Library's `cyclicClass`.

The chart is for the opposite division algebra because it acts by right multiplication on the left module of the embedded field. The theorem identifies the actual relative cohomology class of that chart. It supplies the sign comparison for the cyclic relations of Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 B, printed p. 110.
