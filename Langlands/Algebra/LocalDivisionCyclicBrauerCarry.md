# The carry cocycle of the opposite division splitting chart

Let \(L/K\) be a Galois subfield of degree \(d\) embedded in the central division algebra \(D/K\) of reduced degree \(d\). The unit \(j\) implements an automorphism \(\sigma\) of order \(d\), and the chosen cyclic coordinate on \(\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)\) sends \(\sigma\) to \(1\). For each Galois element \(g\), take the matrix lift \(P_g\) indexed by its cyclic representative in the range from \(0\) to \(d-1\). The canonical splitting chart action applies \(g\) to the entries of the matrix and then conjugates by \(P_g\). Thus composition of lifts uses \(P_g\,g(P_h)\). Reducing the sum of the exponents modulo \(d\) contributes the power of the central parameter \(j^d\) given by the carry. The scalar factor set is the positive carry cocycle defining the Library's `cyclicClass`.

The chart is for the opposite division algebra acting by right multiplication on the left module of the embedded field. The theorem identifies the relative cohomology class of this chart and supplies the sign comparison for the cyclic relations of Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic symmetric regions* (1976), §2 B, printed p. 110 (PDF p. 4).
