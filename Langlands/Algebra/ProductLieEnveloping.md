# Enveloping algebras of products and their centers

`prodEquiv` identifies the enveloping algebra of a binary product of Lie algebras with the algebra tensor product of the two enveloping algebras. Its forward map sends a Lie generator to the sum of the two corresponding generators in the tensor factors. For its inverse, the two Lie inclusions have mutually commuting images. Induction on enveloping words extends that commutation to their enveloping inclusions, which combine by the universal property of the tensor product. The two composites agree with the identity on the generating Lie maps.

`prod_center_le_sup` applies `TensorProductCenter.centerEquiv` to the tensor product. The preimage of a central element is a sum of tensors of central elements, and each such tensor maps to a product of images from the two centers.

For a finite product of copies of an associative algebra with its commutator Lie bracket, `singleCenter` includes each block's enveloping center into the actual product enveloping center. Commutation with a product Lie direction follows from centrality in the chosen coordinate and the vanishing bracket with the directions supported in the other coordinates. `iSup_singleCenter_range` shows that these images generate the whole center. The proof enumerates the coordinates, separates the first coordinate from the tail, and inducts using the binary center calculation. For the empty product, the enveloping algebra is generated by scalars.

These derived constructions supply the finite-product center argument for the totally complex archimedean bridge.
