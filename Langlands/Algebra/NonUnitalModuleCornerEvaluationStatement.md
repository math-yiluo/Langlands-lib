# Image and kernel statements for actual corner evaluation

This module states three properties of the actual evaluation map in David
Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, French version dated
9 July 2026, I.3.2, printed pp.21–22 / PDF pp.27–28. The governing registered
source is `renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, SHA256
`7b539996793b7c3620e72e1e389588eb42d1a8e91d6f68b2eead5c2e2ffc182c`.
The original action conventions, printed pp.5–7 / PDF pp.11–13, fixed parts
and generated category, printed p.19 / PDF p.25, and I.3.2, printed pp.20–22 /
PDF pp.26–28, were read.

The registered supplementary `renard-2010-corrections-complements`, SHA256
`fdf3c88716c294c451c1f6fad67f6b99c95dae670aae0b8ad191ab0a96e69863`,
PDF pp.1–2, was also read. It warns that the ambient ring is not a unital
right corner module and corrects the later maximal-kernel argument to use the
annihilator on the source tensor. The 2026 text governs numbering and
statements. The author's current
[book page](https://perso.pages.math.cnrs.fr/users/david.renard/) was consulted
on 1 November 2026: it links the corrected 2026 French text and separately
the 2010 errata. No separate 2026 errata link was displayed. No dedicated
Renard errata file was found in the workspace, current Library or project
`notes/errata` locations checked.

All four statement names are in
`Langlands.Algebra.NonUnitalModuleCornerEvaluationStatement`. Each is a
closed universe-polymorphic `def` with complete unapplied type `Prop`:

```lean
@EvaluationImageStatement.{uA, uM} : Prop
@EvaluationSurjectivityStatement.{uA, uM} : Prop
@EvaluationKernelAnnihilationStatement.{uA, uM} : Prop
@CornerEvaluationStatement.{uA, uM} : Prop
```

Each of the first three definitions quantifies internally over exactly

```lean
∀ (A : Type uA) [NonUnitalRing A] (e : Idempotent A)
  (M : Type uM) [AddCommGroup M] [NonUnitalModule A M], ...
```

There is no term parameter or hypothesis outside these quantifiers.
The ring universe `uA` and module universe `uM` are independent. The ring
may be associative, noncommutative and nonunital; its specified original
module action need not satisfy a unit law. Idempotentedness, nondegeneracy,
generatedness, simplicity, nonzero carriers, finite dimension and
commutativity are not premises.

The imported actual constructions are:

- `NonUnitalModuleCorner.FixedPart e M`, with exactly the inherited
  `fixedPartAddCommGroup e M` and `fixedPartModule e M`.
- `NonUnitalModuleCornerInduction.Tensor e (FixedPart e M)`, the actual
  additive balanced quotient with the original right corner multiplication.
  Write `T` for this carrier only in this explanation. Its group and
  residual original action are exactly `tensorAddCommGroup` and
  `tensorModule`; the statement definitions select all these named
  structures explicitly using `letI`. Its universe is `max uA uM`.
- `NonUnitalModuleCornerEvaluation.evaluation e M`, the published quotient
  lift of the original action, with `evaluation e M (tmul e a z) = a • z.val`.
  The map is constructed by the imported module, rather than supplied as a
  variable or chosen from maps satisfying a desired conclusion.
- `NonUnitalModuleHom.rangeSubmodule` and `kernelSubmodule` from
  `NonUnitalModuleKernels`. These are whole original stable additive
  submodule objects; their memberships are respectively `∃ t, evaluation
  e M t = m` and `evaluation e M t = 0`.
- `NonUnitalModuleIdempotentGenerated.aeM e M`, the additive subgroup closure
  of all `a • ((e : A) • m)` with its proved original-action stability, and
  `IsGeneratedByIdempotent e M`, which means every vector belongs to this
  actual submodule.
- `NonUnitalSubmodule.idempotentAnnihilator` from
  `IdempotentAnnihilatorQuotient.NamedCarrier`. On `T` it uses the named
  residual tensor action and consists of all tensors `t` such that
  `∀ a : A, ((e : A) * a) • t = 0`.

`EvaluationImageStatement` states the equality of whole original
`NonUnitalSubmodule A M` objects

```lean
NonUnitalModuleHom.rangeSubmodule (evaluation e M) = aeM e M.
```

`EvaluationSurjectivityStatement` states

```lean
Function.Surjective (evaluation e M) ↔ IsGeneratedByIdempotent e M.
```

These isolate the elementary image calculation for phi on printed p.21 /
PDF p.27 and psi on printed p.22 / PDF p.28: pure tensors evaluate to
`a • z.val` for an `e`-fixed vector `z`, and the full image is the additive
subgroup generated by these values. The source explicitly asserts
surjectivity under its simple/generated hypotheses. The exact equivalence
with generatedness and its arbitrary-ring original-action scope are
internal generalizations of that calculation.

`EvaluationKernelAnnihilationStatement` states the whole-submodule containment

```lean
NonUnitalModuleHom.kernelSubmodule (evaluation e M) ≤
  NonUnitalSubmodule.idempotentAnnihilator e T.
```

Its entire content unfolds to
`∀ A e M ... t, evaluation e M t = 0 → ∀ a : A, ((e : A) * a) • t = 0`,
with the original action on `M` and exactly `tensorModule` on `T`.
The source explicitly proves this containment on printed p.21 / PDF p.27
by balancing `e*a` across a finite sum of pure tensors. The conclusion
requires annihilation by every element of the right ideal `eA`. No
kernel-annihilation certificate is an input.

`CornerEvaluationStatement` is the closed conjunction of the three closed
statements, in image, surjectivity, kernel order, with both universe
parameters retained. It is the target for a separate exact proof. This
module supplies statements and interface checks, and claims no formal
proof of these assertions.

The reproducible `Checks` section assigns each complete unapplied
declaration to `Prop`, then checks all four at universe pairs `(0,1)` and
`(1,0)`. Its named-action checks specialize each hypothetical component
proof to a specified action `h₀` in the presence of a competing instance
`h₁`, explicitly listing the source group, source tensor action and
target action in the whole range/kernel types. The annihilator membership
check is definitionally the full `e*a` formula under that same tensor
action. A further definitional equality expands the entire closed kernel
statement to this formula. Boundary checks specialize a hypothetical
aggregate proof at the zero idempotent, zero original action,
subsingleton module carrier and subsingleton ring carrier. These checks
verify interfaces and availability of the specializations; they do not
construct an inhabitant of the closed aggregate.

Simplicity enters the source only afterward, to deduce maximal-kernel
equality. Closure of the generated category under submodules is used
later to deduce injectivity of psi. Fixed-part induction isomorphisms,
kernel equality, simple-class correspondence, generated-category closure,
categorical equivalence, adjunctions and complex-scalar comparisons
remain separate units.
