# Endomorphism reduction along a quotient

Let \(M_0\) be identified with \((R/I)\otimes_R M\). Scalar extension and
transport through the given identification define `quotientEndMap`, a rational
algebra homomorphism on endomorphism rings. Its action on the image of an
original element is the image of the original endomorphism applied to that
element. Every element of the quotient has such a representative.

If \(M\) is free, lift the images of each member of the scalar-extended basis.
The extended linear map reduces to the prescribed endomorphism. If an
endomorphism reduces to zero, it takes values in \(IM\) and sends \(IM\) into
\(I^2M\). For a square-zero ideal its square therefore vanishes. These are
the coefficient-reduction properties used to lift a projection in the
endomorphism algebra.
