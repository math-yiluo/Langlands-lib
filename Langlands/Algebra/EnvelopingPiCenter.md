# Enveloping centers of finite products

An enveloping operator from one coordinate commutes with the
Lie directions in all other coordinates. This gives the inclusion
of the corresponding center in the full enveloping center.

The quotient finiteness criterion splits off one coordinate at a
time. The center of the universal enveloping algebra of the product Lie algebra
is isomorphic to the tensor product of the two enveloping centers. The tensor product of
the factor quotients surjects onto the full quotient. The empty
product has zero Lie algebra and its enveloping algebra is the
coefficient field.

These constructions provide the passage from individual complex
places to the full archimedean center in Harish-Chandra's constant-term
argument (1968, Chapter I, §6, printed pp. 19–20, PDF pp. 29–30).
