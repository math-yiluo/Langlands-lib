# Scalar two-cocycles of actual lifts

These internal group calculations start with an equivariant injective central
scalar homomorphism and actual lifts whose multiplication defects are scalars.
Associativity and centrality force the scalar two-cocycle identity. If an actual
one-cochain trivializes that defect, rescaling each lift by the inverse scalar
produces an honest one-cocycle. The statements retain the specific lifts and
the specific trivializing cochain; no algebra-splitting assertion is assumed or
proved by this generic unit.
