# Valuation on a finite-dimensional division algebra

For a nontrivially normed field `K` with `CompleteSpace K` and
`IsUltrametricDist K`, and a division ring `D` with a central `K`-algebra
structure and `FiniteDimensional K D`, the definition
`Langlands.CentralDivision.regularNormValuation K D` is an actual valuation
`Valuation D NNReal`. Its value at `x` is

```text
‖Algebra.norm K x‖₊.
```

Here `Algebra.norm` is the determinant of left multiplication on the
original finite-dimensional `K`-vector space `D`. This is the regular
algebra norm, even when `D` is noncommutative. The construction does not
use a supplied norm on `D`, a splitting field, a separability hypothesis,
or a characteristic restriction. The base field and division ring may
inhabit independent arbitrary universes.

The normalization on the base field is

```text
regularNormValuation K D (algebraMap K D a)
  = ‖a‖₊ ^ Module.finrank K D.
```

Thus its restriction is the indicated power of the given absolute value.
No choice of a generator of a discrete value group or extraction of a
root is part of this definition.

The auxiliary results are in the same namespace:

- `norm_fieldNorm_powerBasis_gen` identifies the norm of the field norm
  of a power-basis generator with its spectral norm raised to the field
  degree.
- `norm_fieldNorm_eq_spectralNorm_pow` gives that formula for every
  element of a finite field extension `L/K`.
- `norm_fieldNorm_add_le` gives the ultrametric inequality for the
  ordinary field norm followed by the base norm.
- `regularNorm_map_field` restricts the regular norm on `D` to an actual
  embedding `ι : L →ₐ[K] D`. The restriction is
  `Algebra.norm K a ^ Module.finrank L D`, where the `L`-module on `D`
  is the one induced by left multiplication through `ι`. This theorem
  needs only fields, a finite-dimensional division algebra over `K`,
  and the displayed embedding; completeness and norm hypotheses are
  unnecessary for this restriction identity.
- `norm_regularNorm_one_add_le` and `norm_regularNorm_add_le` establish
  the ultrametric inequalities on the original division ring.
- `regularNormValuation_algebraMap` proves the displayed normalization.

The proof first uses Mathlib's spectral norm on an algebraic field
extension. Its formula in terms of the constant coefficient of the
minimal polynomial, together with the determinant formula for the norm
of a power-basis generator, proves the first identity. Passing to the
simple intermediate field generated by an arbitrary element, then
using the norm tower formula, multiplication of field degrees and
invariance of spectral norm in a tower, proves the second identity.
Monotonicity of nonnegative powers and the ultrametric inequality for
spectral norm give the third.

For the restriction identity, the proof puts the actual left
`L`-module structure on `D` using `Module.compHom D ι.toRingHom`.
Restriction of scalars of the scalar endomorphism `a • LinearMap.id`
is the original `K`-linear left multiplication by `ι a`. The
determinant-under-restriction-of-scalars formula then gives precisely
the displayed power of the ordinary field norm. This argument does
not make `D` a central `L`-algebra.

For `z : D`, the commutative subalgebra `Algebra.adjoin K {z}` is a
field by finite dimensionality and the absence of zero divisors. The
field-norm inequality applied to `1` and `z` in that field, raised to
the appropriate nonnegative integer power, proves the bound for
`1 + z` in `D`. If `x ≠ 0`, the factorization
`x + y = x * (1 + x⁻¹ * y)` then proves the general inequality by
multiplicativity and cancellation of the positive number
`‖Algebra.norm K x‖`. The case `x = 0` is immediate. The defining
valuation laws now follow from the norm's zero, one, and
multiplication laws and this inequality.

The proof route is adapted from Jean-Pierre Serre, *Local Fields*,
Chapter XII, §2, Proposition 1(b) and its proof, printed pages 182–183
(retained PDF pages 187–188; registered source
`serre-1979-local-fields`). Serre uses the reduced norm and a
normalized discrete valuation. This implementation uses the regular
determinant norm and a complete nonarchimedean absolute value; the
field generated by `x⁻¹ * y` is the same reduction. The spectral-norm
and restriction-of-scalars arguments above supply the implemented
field comparison. They apply also to inseparable extensions.

The author's *Corrections to J-P. Serre's books* (2019), PDF pages
2–3 (registered source `serre-book-corrections-2019`), was checked.
Its entries for these pages correct the Bourbaki section reference
to §17 in the 2012 edition, a valuation letter, the vector-space
dimension to `n²`, and a grammatical error. They do not alter the
triangle-inequality argument used here.

This contribution constructs the valuation and proves its stated
normalization. It does not construct a normalized discrete valuation,
identify the residue division algebra, construct an unramified cyclic
maximal subfield, prove local SK1 vanishing, or prove
`LocalKneserTitsStatement`.
