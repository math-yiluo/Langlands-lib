# Homogeneous elements of a graded ideal and the sign of an additive functional

Author: Worker 818.

This module proves a lemma of pure commutative algebra in the namespace
`Langlands.Algebra.GradedSignIdeal`. It is used by
`Langlands.GroupScheme.Rational.RootSignUnipotentInclusion` to compare the dynamic
unipotent subgroups of two cocharacters of a split torus.

## Setting

`k` is a field, `R` is a commutative `k`-algebra and `Λ` is an additive commutative
group, each in its own universe. `I` is an ideal of `R`, and `W : Λ → Submodule k I`
is a family of `k`-subspaces of `I` (labels in `Λ`). Most results assume
`DirectSum.IsInternal W` and that products add labels: `x ∈ W χ` and `y ∈ W ψ` imply
`x * y ∈ W (χ + ψ)`. Only the ideal `I` is graded. Nothing is assumed about a
grading of `R`, and `R` need not be Noetherian or a domain.

## Declarations

- `nonpositiveSpan I W ν`, for an additive map `ν : Λ →+ ℤ`, is the ideal of `R`
  generated by the underlying ring elements of the `x ∈ W χ` with `ν χ ≤ 0`.
  `mem_nonpositiveSpan` records that each such generator lies in it.
- `mem_nonpositiveSpan_of_cotangent_signs` is the main result. Let `ζ, ν : Λ →+ ℤ`.
  Suppose that for every label `χ` and every `x ∈ W χ` whose class
  `I.toCotangent x` in `I/I²` is nonzero, `0 < ν χ` implies `0 < ζ χ`. Then every
  `x ∈ W χ` with `ζ χ ≤ 0` lies in `nonpositiveSpan I W ν`.

The private helpers `square_induction`, `homogeneousProjection`,
`homogeneousProjection_same`, `homogeneousProjection_ne` and
`homogeneous_induction` are copies of the private helpers of the same names in the
published module `Langlands.Algebra.GradedIdealSupportProof`. The only change is that
their redundant `classical` tactics are dropped, since this module opens `Classical`.
Lean does not let other modules use private declarations, so they are copied here.
`homogeneousProjection I W hW χ` is the `k`-linear projection of `I` onto `W χ`
along the other components. `homogeneous_induction` proves an additive predicate on
`I` from its zero, homogeneous and addition cases. `square_induction` proves a
predicate on elements of `I²` from its truth on products of two elements of `I` and
closure under addition.

## Proof

The private theorem `mem_nonpositiveSpan_of_le` proves, by induction on `n : ℕ`,
that every `x ∈ W χ` with `ν χ ≤ n` and `ζ χ ≤ 0` lies in the nonpositive span.
The main theorem applies it with `n = (ν χ).toNat`.

For `n = 0` the element is a generator. In the step, if `ν χ ≤ 0` the element is
again a generator. Otherwise `0 < ν χ` and `ζ χ ≤ 0`, so the hypothesis,
contraposed, says that the class of `x` in `I/I²` vanishes, so `x ∈ I²`.
Write `p` for the projection onto `W χ`. Since `x ∈ W χ`, `p x = x`, and by
`square_induction` it suffices to show that `p (a * b)` lies in the span for all
`a, b ∈ I`. By `homogeneous_induction` in both factors, we may take
`a ∈ W α` and `b ∈ W β`. Then `a * b ∈ W (α + β)`, so `p (a * b)` is zero
unless `α + β = χ`, and then it equals `a * b`. If `ν α ≤ 0` or `ν β ≤ 0`, the
product lies in the span because one factor is a generator. Otherwise
`ν α, ν β ≥ 1` and `ν α + ν β = ν χ ≤ n + 1`, so both `ν α ≤ n` and `ν β ≤ n`.
Because `ζ α + ζ β = ζ χ ≤ 0`, one of `ζ α`, `ζ β` is nonpositive. The induction
hypothesis puts that factor in the span, and hence the product too.

## Provenance

This is an internal lemma written for the nested parabolic decomposition proof task
`a26bb091-8323-44b3-bb49-79a5f530d4f7`. It is the graded form of the standard
argument that a graded ideal is generated by lifts of a homogeneous basis of its
cotangent space: the hypothesis on `I/I²` controls the generators, and the degree
induction controls everything else. The induction is on the value of `ν`, so no
Noetherian, Krull-intersection or domain hypothesis is needed. Unlike
`GradedIdealSupportProof`, the lemma proves no published proposition; it is an
explicit helper. The helper code reused from that module is identified above.
