# Rigidity for bounded mixed-character algebra maps

Lean: Worker 57. Documentation and integration: Worker 872.

`Langlands.Algebra.MixedHopfPointRigidity.rigidityBound r F n`
is a modulus bound for maps into the group algebra of
`Λ = (r → ℤ) × F`, with `r` and `F` finite and `F` an additive
commutative group. It is the maximum of the mixed-configuration bound
and `2 * n + 1`. This is an internal extension of the published
free-lattice rigidity proof, intended for multiplicative-type
subgroup charts.

`injOn_mred_mbox` proves that reduction modulo `m`, retaining `F`,
is injective on the mixed box of radius `n` when `2 * n < m`.
`closure_support_eq_top` is more general: for an arbitrary additive
commutative character group `M` and a nonzero coefficient ring `C`,
if the values of an algebra map generate all of `C[M]` as a
`C`-algebra, then the supports of the images of algebra generators
generate `M` as an additive group. The proof first controls supports
of sums and products by the monoid generated by the original supports,
then applies this to every basis monomial.

`exists_mem_support_of_mred` transports nonzero coefficients across
an equality of reduced maps twisted by a reduced-character
automorphism. Injectivity of reduction on the bounded support prevents
cancellation of that coefficient.

`exists_addEquiv_of_mred_eq` applies to two algebra maps
`α, α' : A →ₐ[R] C[Λ]`. It assumes a generating family of `A`, that
both families of generator images have support in the box of radius
`n`, that each support configuration generates `Λ`, and that their
reductions differ by an automorphism of `(r → ZMod m) × F`.
For `m` at least `rigidityBound`, it produces an automorphism `T` of
`Λ` with `mapDomain T ∘ α = α'`. Although the intended applications
are Hopf points and `A` has the imported bialgebra structure, the
theorem does not require Hopf-point equations as additional
hypotheses.

The proof matches the two supports through reduction, applies the
mixed-configuration extension bound to the matching and its inverse,
and checks on the generating configurations that the resulting
endomorphisms are inverse. Reduction is injective on the bounded
supports, so equality of the reduced generator values recovers
equality of the algebra maps after twisting by `T`.

`ker_scalarExt_eq_of_mred_eq` then identifies the kernels of the two
scalar-extended maps, using invariance of that kernel under a
character-group automorphism. None of these rigidity results assumes
that `m` annihilates `F`; that separate hypothesis is needed when
descending arbitrary automorphisms to a reduced group in the
configuration module.
