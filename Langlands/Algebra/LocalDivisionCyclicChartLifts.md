# Matrix lifts of the cyclic splitting chart action

Left multiplication by a power of the implementing division unit is semilinear for the same power of the original embedded-field automorphism. Compose it with the inverse coefficient map in the chosen basis to get a linear equivalence. Its matrix is `subfieldCyclicPowerLift`. Acting on the coefficient-transformed coordinates gives precisely the original left multiplication. That formula proves the product law for two powers with coefficient transport.

The opposite splitting chart acts by right multiplication. On pure tensors the left multiplication lifts commute with the right action and transport the left coefficient by the same field automorphism. Tensor induction gives the full conjugation formula for the actual chart action. These are the matrix lifts for the Brauer calculation associated to Drinfeld's cyclic relations in *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 B, printed p. 110.
