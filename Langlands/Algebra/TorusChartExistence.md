# Existence of a torus chart around a field point

Author: Worker 1421.

This is an internal module for the chart construction of task
`45ac6e52-a4d8-4414-91f1-03c3be35223e`. It shows that every closed embedding of
a split torus `G_m^r` into `Spec A` over a field lies on a chart datum
(`TorusChartRing.Datum`) whose extension also has its weights in the box. The
notation is that of `TorusChartRing`: `W` represents homomorphisms
`μ_m^r → Spec A`, `pointOf` gives the Hopf point of a point of `W`, and `red`
is reduction `ℤ^r → (ℤ/m)^r`.

## Elementary lemmas

- `boundedSums_box_subset`: sums of at most `N` vectors of `box r n` lie in
  `box r (N n)`. The proof is by induction on `N`, using the triangle
  inequality.
- `exists_box`: every finite set of lattice vectors lies in some box.
- `exists_residueLift`: a field point `y : C → K` factors through the residue
  field of its kernel (`Ideal.ResidueField.liftₐ`).
- `exists_lift_away`, `exists_factor_away`: a map inverting `f` factors
  through `C_f` (`IsLocalization.liftAlgHom`).
- `exists_numerator`: every element of `C_f` times a power of `f` comes from
  `C`.
- `mapAlgHom_comp_mapAlgHom`: changes of coefficients compose.
- `exists_away_dvd`: `C_{f₁}` maps to `C_{f₁ w}` compatibly with `C`.
- `exists_invariant_cover`: let a finite group `Γ` act on `W` by `σ`, let
  `f ∉ p₀` for a prime `p₀`. Then there is a `Γ`-invariant `N ∉ p₀` such that
  every prime `P` not containing `N` misses some translate `σ γ f`. Proof:
  the ideal `J` generated by the translates of `f` lies in none of the primes
  `σ γ⁻¹ (p₀)`. Prime avoidance
  (`InvariantPrincipalOpen.exists_mem_forall_not_mem`) gives `g ∈ J` outside
  all of them. Take `N` to be the norm `∏ σ γ g`. It is invariant, lies
  outside `p₀` (`norm_not_mem`), and is a multiple of `g ∈ J` (`dvd_norm`).
  So a prime containing every translate of `f` contains `N`.

## Window data

`WindowDatum R A r m gen n V δ N₀` extends `Datum R A r m gen n` with two
fields, for a submodule `V ⊆ A` with coaction `δ` and a bound `N₀`:

- `weights`: the weights of `V` under `α` lie in `box r n`
  (`HopfPointWeightWindow.WeightsIn`);
- `injOn`: `red` is injective on sums of at most `N₀` vectors of `box r n`.

## Results

- `exists_point_of_hopfPoint`: every Hopf point of `μ_m^r` over `K` (in
  universe `u`) is `pointOf y` for some point `y` of `W`. Use `homEquiv` and
  `TorusChartRing.ofGroupAlgebra`.
- `exists_window_extension`: the setting is as follows.
  - `V` is a finite subcomodule with coassociative coaction `δ`, compatible
    with the comultiplication (`hι`), and containing the generators `gen i`.
  - `gen` is a bounded presentation of degree `N₀ ≥ 1`.
  - `red` is injective on `boundedSums (box r n) N₀`.
  - `α₀ : A → K[ℤ^r]` is a closed-immersion Hopf point over a field `K`, with
    weights in `box r n`.
  - `y₀` is a point of `W` with `pointOf y₀ = red ∘ α₀`.

  Then there are `f ∈ W`, a Hopf point `α : A → (W_f)[ℤ^r]` and a point `y` of
  `W_f` with the following properties:
  - the values of `α` on the generators are supported in the box;
  - `red ∘ α` is the universal point over `W_f`;
  - `α` is a closed immersion with weights in the box;
  - `y` extends `y₀`, and `y` specializes `α` to `α₀`.

  Proof:
  1. The universal point has weights in `red(box)` at the residue field of
     `ker y₀`. This holds because the weights descend along the field
     embedding `κ → K` (`weightsIn_of_mapAlgHom`,
     `rTensor_injective_of_field`) from those of `red ∘ α₀`
     (`weightsIn_mapDomain`).
  2. `HopfPointWindowExtension.exists_away_extension` gives `f₁` and the
     extension `α₁` over `W_{f₁}`.
  3. `y₀` lifts to `y₁` on `W_{f₁}`. The specialization of `α₁` along `y₁`
     equals `α₀` by uniqueness in `existsUnique_hopfPoint_extension`.
  4. At the residue field of `ker y₁` the weights of `α₁` lie in the box, by
     descent from `α₀`. `exists_away_weightsIn` gives `c₂` with `y₁ c₂ ≠ 0`
     such that the weights lie in the box over `(W_{f₁})_{c₂}`.
  5. `HopfPointClosedLocus.exists_away_surjective_of_field` gives `c₃` with
     `y₁ c₃ ≠ 0` such that `α₁` is a closed immersion over
     `(W_{f₁})_{c₃}`.
  6. Write `c₂ c₃ = w / f₁^k` and take `f := f₁ w`. Then `c₂` and `c₃` become
     units in `W_f`. Push `α₁` to `W_f` and lift `y₀` to `y` on `W_f`. All
     properties transfer by change of coefficients.
- `exists_windowDatum`: under the same hypotheses on `V`, `δ` and `gen`, every
  closed-immersion Hopf point `α₀ : A → K[ℤ^r]` over a field has the following
  data:
  - sizes `n` and `m > 0`;
  - a window datum `d`;
  - a point `y` of `W_{d.f}` with `y (d.N) ≠ 0` that specializes `d.α` to
    `α₀`.

  Proof:
  1. Choose `n` with all weights of `α₀` in `box r n` (`exists_finite_weightsIn`,
     `exists_box`).
  2. Choose `m ≥ rigidityBound r n` with `m > 2 N₀ n`. Then `red` is injective
     on `boundedSums (box r n) N₀ ⊆ box r (N₀ n)` (`injOn_red_box`).
  3. Take `y₀` with `pointOf y₀ = red ∘ α₀` and apply
     `exists_window_extension`.
  4. `exists_invariant_cover`, applied to the twisting action and the prime
     `ker y₀`, supplies `N`.
