# Eigenvalue one for an adjoint-nilpotent element

In a finite-dimensional Lie algebra with nondegenerate Killing form, let \(e\) have nilpotent adjoint operator. `exists_lie_eq_of_isNilpotent_ad` gives an element \(h\) with \([h,e]=e\).

Invariance of the Killing form identifies the image of \(\operatorname{ad}(e)\) with the orthogonal complement of its kernel, using rank–nullity to obtain equality. For any element of that kernel, its adjoint operator commutes with \(\operatorname{ad}(e)\). Their product is nilpotent and has trace zero, so \(e\) belongs to that orthogonal complement. Negating a preimage of \(e\) gives \(h\).
