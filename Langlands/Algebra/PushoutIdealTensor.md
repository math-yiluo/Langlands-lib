# Ideals in pushouts of commutative rings

Module `Langlands.Algebra.PushoutIdealTensor`, namespace
`Langlands.Algebra.PushoutIdealTensor`. Author: Worker 967 (task
`14dfbe95-4d4a-46fd-933a-182df994126c`, openness of versality for actual Picard
families). It is pure commutative algebra in one universe `u`. Everything is
proved in the file, with no hypotheses beyond those in the signatures.

The geometric use: `Γ` is the ring of sections of a flat relative scheme over an
affine open, and `Γ'` its base change along a ring map `C → S`. Then ideal
sections of `Γ'` are identified with `𝔞 ⊗[C] Γ`, and families of them over the
pieces of a finite cover with `(A₀ ⊗[C] ∏ Γ) ⊗[A₀] M`.

## `TensorMap` section

Data: commutative rings `C, S, Γ, Γ'` with algebra structures `C → S`, `C → Γ`,
`C → Γ'`, `S → Γ'`, `Γ → Γ'`, scalar towers `C → S → Γ'` and `C → Γ → Γ'`, and
Mathlib's `Algebra.IsPushout C S Γ Γ'`, so that `Γ' = S ⊗[C] Γ`.

- `idealTensorMap C Γ Γ' 𝔞 : (𝔞.restrictScalars C) ⊗[C] Γ →ₗ[C] Γ'` for an ideal
  `𝔞` of `S`. It is the composite of `LinearMap.rTensor Γ` of the inclusion
  `𝔞 → S` with Mathlib's `Algebra.IsPushout.equiv C S Γ Γ' : S ⊗[C] Γ ≃ₐ[S] Γ'`.
- `idealTensorMap_tmul`: `a ⊗ x ↦ algebraMap S Γ' a * algebraMap Γ Γ' x`.
- `idealTensorMap_injective`: injective when `Γ` is flat over `C`. This is
  flatness applied to the injective inclusion (Mathlib
  `Module.Flat.rTensor_preserves_injective_linearMap`).
- `range_idealTensorMap`: the range is
  `(Ideal.map (algebraMap S Γ') 𝔞).restrictScalars C`. Proof: Mathlib's
  `Ideal.map_includeLeft_eq` identifies the range of `rTensor` of the inclusion
  with the ideal generated by `𝔞` in `S ⊗[C] Γ`. The pushout equivalence is a ring
  equivalence sending `a ⊗ 1` to `algebraMap S Γ' a`. So it maps that ideal onto
  `Ideal.map (algebraMap S Γ') 𝔞` (`Ideal.map_map`, `Ideal.map_comap_of_equiv`).

## `Kernel` section

Data: `S, S', Γ', Γ''` with `Algebra.IsPushout S S' Γ' Γ''` and the
corresponding scalar towers.

- `ker_algebraMap_eq_map`: if `algebraMap S S'` is surjective, then
  `RingHom.ker (algebraMap Γ' Γ'') = Ideal.map (algebraMap S Γ') (RingHom.ker (algebraMap S S'))`.
  Proof: `⊇` is the scalar tower. For `⊆`, an element `x` of the kernel gives
  `1 ⊗ x = 0` in `S' ⊗[S] Γ'` by the inverse pushout equivalence. Mathlib's
  `Algebra.TensorProduct.rTensor_ker` puts `1 ⊗ x` into the ideal generated by the
  kernel of `S → S'` under `includeLeft`. Then `Algebra.TensorProduct.lid` maps
  that ideal into `Ideal.map (algebraMap S Γ') (ker)`, which gives `x`.

## `Cech` section

Data: commutative rings `C, A, A₀`, algebras `C → A`, `C → A₀`, an
`A₀`-module `M` with compatible `C`-module structure, a finite index type `ι`
with decidable equality, and two families `Γ i`, `Γ' i` of commutative rings.
Each `Γ i` is a `C`-algebra, `Γ' i` is a `Γ i`-algebra and an `A`-algebra, the
scalar towers hold, and `Algebra.IsPushout C A (Γ i) (Γ' i)` holds for every
`i`. Finally `j : M →ₗ[C] A`.

- `cechTensorMap Γ Γ' j : (A₀ ⊗[C] (∀ i, Γ i)) ⊗[A₀] M →ₗ[C] (∀ i, Γ' i)`. It is the
  composite of `TensorProduct.comm`, Mathlib's
  `TensorProduct.AlgebraTensorModule.cancelBaseChange C A₀ A₀ M (∀ i, Γ i)`,
  `TensorProduct.piRight`, and componentwise `rTensor (Γ i) j` followed by the
  pushout equivalence.
- `cechTensorMap_tmul`: `(a ⊗ x) ⊗ m ↦ (i ↦ algebraMap A (Γ' i) (j (a • m)) * algebraMap (Γ i) (Γ' i) (x i))`.
- `cechTensorMap_injective`: injective if every `Γ i` is flat over `C` and `j`
  is injective.
- `mem_range_cechTensorMap_iff 𝔞 hj y`: if the image of `j` is exactly the
  ideal `𝔞` (`hj : ∀ a, a ∈ 𝔞 ↔ ∃ m, j m = a`), then `y` is in the range iff
  `y i ∈ Ideal.map (algebraMap A (Γ' i)) 𝔞` for every `i`. Proof: `rTensor j`
  factors through the surjection of `M` onto `𝔞`, and `rTensor` of a surjection is
  surjective. Componentwise, `range_idealTensorMap` applies. For the converse,
  componentwise preimages are chosen and assembled with the inverse equivalences.
