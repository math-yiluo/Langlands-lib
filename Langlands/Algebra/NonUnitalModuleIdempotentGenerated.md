All declarations below are in
`Langlands.Algebra.NonUnitalModuleIdempotentGenerated`. The ambient
`A : Type uA` is any associative, possibly noncommutative `NonUnitalRing`,
`e : Idempotent A` is an actual idempotent, and `M : Type uM` is an additive
commutative group with its specified original `NonUnitalModule A M` action.
The ring and carrier universes are independent, as are the source, target,
and third-carrier universes of unbundled restrictions and composition laws.
No ambient identity-action law, local-unit, fullness, nonzero-idempotent,
or nontrivial-carrier hypothesis is used.

The arbitrary-ring construction and ambient functor are derived internal
generalizations of Renard's definition. For an idempotented complex algebra
with its nondegenerate original action, this is the source's underlying
additive-action definition: \(eM\) is the image, equivalently the fixed
subgroup, of the original action of \(e\), and \(AeM\) is the additive
subgroup generated by its original \(A\)-translates. Complex linear
combinations agree with this subgroup when the source's complex algebra
and recovered compatible scalar action are present: scalar multiplication
of \(a\cdot(e\cdot m)\) can be absorbed into the coefficient \(a\).
This explanatory specialization does not construct or prove a comparison
with a separately bundled complex-linear category; that comparison remains
a parent obligation.

The module imports the published original-action module and corner APIs
through `Langlands.Algebra.NonUnitalModuleCorner` and uses Mathlib's
additive subgroup closure directly. It constructs no competing module,
submodule, fixed-part, or morphism notion.

## The actual subgroup and its leastness

`actionValues e M` is the set

```text
{x | ∃ (a : A) (m : M), originalAction a (originalAction e m) = x}.
```

Here `originalAction` is the published abbreviation for the additive
endomorphism supplied by the specified original `NonUnitalModule`.
`actionValues_eq` exposes the same set as the values `a • (e • m)`.
`aeM e M : NonUnitalSubmodule A M` has underlying additive subgroup exactly
`AddSubgroup.closure (actionValues e M)`.
`aeM_toAddSubgroup` and `aeM_closure` give these closure identities.

The stability field uses additivity of each original action: its preimage
of the closure is an additive subgroup. A generator acted on by `a` is the
generator with coefficient `a * b`, by multiplicativity of the original
action. The closure universal property therefore proves stability without
assuming any lattice structure on `NonUnitalSubmodule`.
`action_mem` and `smul_mem` give membership of every generator.

`coordinate_mem` proves that `e.leftActionCoordinate m` belongs to `aeM e M`
by taking the coefficient to be `e` and using `e * e = e`.
Consequently `fixedPart_le` includes the actual published
`NonUnitalModuleCorner.fixedPart e M` in its underlying subgroup. The
published coordinate idempotence identifies this fixed subgroup with the
image \(eM\), including for a nonunital ambient ring.

The exact leastness laws are:

| Declaration | Characterization |
| --- | --- |
| `aeM_le_iff e S` | `aeM e M ≤ S` iff `fixedPart e M ≤ S.toAddSubgroup`, for an actual `NonUnitalSubmodule A M` |
| `aeM_le_stable_iff e S hS` | `(aeM e M).toAddSubgroup ≤ S` iff `fixedPart e M ≤ S`, for an additive subgroup with original-action stability `hS` |

The forward directions use `fixedPart_le`. For the reverse directions,
the original `e`-coordinate lies in the fixed subgroup, stability supplies
each generator, and `AddSubgroup.closure_le` supplies the whole closure.
Thus `aeM` is the least stable subgroup containing the actual \(eM\).

`aeMAddCommGroup e M` and `aeMModule e M` name the inherited group and
original module structures on the actual subtype. `aeMModule_smul`
exposes the full subtype formula using the named module structure:

```text
a • x = ⟨originalAction a x.val, stability proof⟩.
```

`submoduleInclusion e` is the published `NonUnitalSubmodule.subtype`
intertwiner, with value `x.val`. `submoduleInclusion_apply` and
`coe_submoduleInclusion` give its applied and complete-function formulas.
The named inherited module and explicit original structure parameters can
be used when a caller has competing actions.

## Generatedness and zero cases

`IsGeneratedByIdempotent e M : Prop` means exactly
`∀ m : M, m ∈ aeM e M`.
The following equivalent formulations are proved:

| Declaration | Equivalent condition |
| --- | --- |
| `isGenerated_iff_eq_top` | `(aeM e M).toAddSubgroup = ⊤` |
| `isGenerated_iff_coe_eq_univ` | `(aeM e M : Set M) = Set.univ` |
| `isGenerated_iff_least` | Every actual submodule containing the fixed subgroup contains every vector |
| `isGenerated_iff_stable` | Every original-action-stable additive subgroup containing the fixed subgroup contains every vector |

The first two are extensional whole-carrier characterizations. The last
two follow from the leastness laws, using `aeM` itself for their converse
directions.

`aeM_eq_bot_of_coordinate_zero` proves that if the original action of `e`
is zero on every vector, the generated subgroup is bottom: every generator
is then zero. `isGenerated_iff_zero_of_coordinate_zero` consequently says
that generatedness is equivalent to `∀ m : M, m = 0`.
`aeM_zero_idempotent` and `isGenerated_zero_idempotent_iff` specialize
these laws to the actual zero idempotent.

`aeM_zero_action` and `isGenerated_zero_action_iff` explicitly install
`NonUnitalModule.zero` and give the same conclusions for every idempotent.
They do not use an ambient inferred nonzero action. In particular, a ring
which happens to admit a unit can still act by the zero original action
on a nonzero carrier. No declaration requires `Nontrivial M`, and the zero
carrier is permitted throughout.

## Restrictions, ambient functor, and natural inclusion

For an actual `f : NonUnitalModuleHom A M N`, `map_mem e f` proves
preservation of the generated subgroup. The preimage of the target closure
under `f.toAddMonoidHom` is an additive subgroup; the intertwining law
sends a source generator to the target generator with the same coefficient.
`restrict e f` is therefore an actual original intertwiner

```text
NonUnitalModuleHom A (aeM e M) (aeM e N),
x ↦ ⟨f x.val, map_mem e f x.property⟩.
```

`restrict_apply` and `coe_restrict` expose its value and entire underlying
function. `restrict_id`, `restrict_comp`, `restrict_zero`, `restrict_add`,
and `restrict_neg` are equalities of whole intertwiners, proved by
extensionality and their subtype formulas. `restrictAddHom e` bundles
restriction as an additive homomorphism on the published pointwise Hom
groups; `coe_restrictAddHom` identifies its function with `restrict e`.

`submoduleInclusion_naturality` is the equality of whole intertwiners

```text
submoduleInclusion_N.comp (restrict e f)
  = f.comp submoduleInclusion_M.
```

At one fixed carrier universe, `functor e` is the actual additive
endofunctor of `NonUnitalModuleCat.{uA,uM} A` sending `M` to
`NonUnitalModuleCat.of A (aeM e M)` and `f` to `restrict e f`.
`functor_obj`, `functor_map`, and `functor_map_apply` give its explicit
object, whole-map, and element formulas. The last formula exposes the
underlying `toAddMonoidHom` so it also elaborates for categorical Hom aliases.
`functor_additive` uses the whole-map addition law.

`naturalInclusion e : functor e ⟶ 𝟭 _` has component
`submoduleInclusion e`, as stated in `naturalInclusion_app`.
Its naturality is the whole-map inclusion identity above. The ambient
functor has target `NonUnitalModuleCat`, with no nondegeneracy claim about
its output for arbitrary rings.

## The actual generated category and its corner functor

`generatedProperty e` is the object property on the already published
`NondegenerateModuleCat.{uA,uM} A` assigning to an object `M` the
proposition `IsGeneratedByIdempotent e M.obj`.
`GeneratedModuleCat e` is its actual `FullSubcategory`.
Thus its objects retain both the original nondegeneracy condition and
the requirement that every vector lie in the actual `aeM`.
`object_isNondegenerate` and `object_isGenerated` expose these properties.

`inclusion e` is the actual full-subcategory inclusion into
`NondegenerateModuleCat A`. `inclusion_full`, `inclusion_faithful`,
and `inclusion_additive` use the inherited full-subcategory and preadditive
instances. `inclusion_obj` states that an object is sent to `M.obj`;
`inclusion_map` states that a wrapped morphism is sent to `f.hom`.

`cornerFunctor e` is exactly

```text
inclusion e ⋙ NonUnitalModuleCorner.nondegenerateFunctor e.
```

It reuses the actual published fixed carrier, named inherited group,
and unital corner-module action. `cornerFunctor_obj` identifies its
object with
`ModuleCat.of (Idempotent.Corner e) (NonUnitalModuleCorner.FixedPart e M.obj.obj)`.
`cornerFunctor_map_hom` identifies its linear map with the published
`NonUnitalModuleCorner.restrict e f.hom.hom`; the two `.hom` projections
remove the two full-subcategory wrappers. `cornerFunctor_map_apply`
states that its ambient value on a fixed vector is the original
intertwiner's value on that vector. `cornerFunctor_additive` follows from
additivity of the two composed functors.

The intrinsic laws and functor packaging above are internal consequences
of the source definition, proved using original-action associativity,
additivity, idempotence, subgroup closure, and existing category APIs.
This unit supplies no submodule or quotient closure theorem for
`GeneratedModuleCat`, abelianity, generatedness of tensor induction,
induction/corner isomorphism, simple-class correspondence, or equivalence
criterion. These remain separate statement and proof obligations.
