# Proof of Renard C.I for a generated subalgebra

`Langlands.Renard.commutativeSubalgebraDimension.{uV}` proves the published
proposition `Langlands.Renard.CommutativeSubalgebraDimensionStatement.{uV}`
from `Langlands.Algebra.RenardCommutativeEndomorphismDimensionStatement`.
The type of the theorem is exactly that proposition, with the same arbitrary
carrier universe `uV`. No hypothesis is added, and the statement module is
imported unchanged.

## Statement proved

For every finite-dimensional complex vector space `V : Type uV`, every
natural number `l`, every family `a : Fin l → Module.End ℂ V`, and every
subalgebra `R : Subalgebra ℂ (Module.End ℂ V)`, assume
`∀ i j, Commute (a i) (a j)` and `R = Algebra.adjoin ℂ (Set.range a)`. Then

```text
(Module.finrank ℂ R : ℝ) ≤
  Real.rpow (Module.finrank ℂ V : ℝ) (2 - Real.rpow 2 (1 - (l : ℝ))).
```

This is the source-facing form of the lemma of David Renard,
*Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, French edition dated
9 July 2026, Appendix C.I, printed p.275 / one-based PDF281. There `R` is a
commutative subalgebra of `End(V)` generated by `l` elements and the
identity. The statement Markdown explains how the explicit generation
equality expresses this.

## Implemented proof

The theorem introduces `V` with its instances, `l`, `a`, `R`, the
commutation hypothesis and the generation equality
`hR : R = Algebra.adjoin ℂ (Set.range a)`. Since `R` is a local variable,
`subst hR` replaces `R` by `Algebra.adjoin ℂ (Set.range a)` throughout the
goal. The goal is then exactly the conclusion of
`Langlands.Renard.commutativeEndomorphismDimension` for `V`, `l`, `a` and
the same commutation hypothesis. That theorem, from
`Langlands.Algebra.RenardCommutativeEndomorphismDimensionProof`, is applied
directly.

The generation equality is therefore the only link between `R` and the
family. No commutativity of `R` beyond the commutation of the generators is
assumed. The first theorem covers every corner case: `l = 0`, the zero
vector space and both together. The
companion Markdown `RenardCommutativeEndomorphismDimensionProof.md`
documents the exponent comparison and the corner cases. It also documents the
reused Library theorem `Langlands.CommutingDimension.commuting_dimension` and
how its proof route differs from Renard's.
