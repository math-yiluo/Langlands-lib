# Finiteness of the parabolic projection of an enveloping centre: proof

Module `Langlands.Algebra.ParabolicCenterProjectionFiniteProof`.

`Langlands.Algebra.ParabolicCenterProjection.parabolicCenterProjectionFinite` proves
`ParabolicCenterProjectionFiniteStatement.{v, w}` (module
`Langlands.Algebra.ParabolicCenterProjectionFiniteStatement`): for a finite-dimensional complex
Lie algebra `g` with `radical = center`, an element `H` whose integral eigenvectors span `g`, an
injective Lie map `j : m → g` onto the centralizer of `H`, and an algebra map
`p : Z(U(g)) → Z(U(m))` with `z - U(j)(p z) ∈ n U(g)` for every central `z`, where `n U(g)` is
`positiveRightIdeal H`, the map `p` is finite. Both universes are kept.

## Outline

The proof follows the classical route through Harish-Chandra homomorphisms, with the
Weyl-group description of their images replaced by explicit central elements and their leading
forms.

1. **Cartan data** (`nonempty_gradingData`, modules `ReductiveCartanData`,
   `ParabolicCenterProjectionWeights`, `ReductiveGradingData`). There are an abelian subspace
   `𝔥 ∋ H`, a basis of `g` of common eigenvectors of `ad 𝔥` with linear weights `wt`, weight-zero
   basis vectors lying in `𝔥`, a nondegenerate symmetric invariant form `B`, nondegenerate on
   the centre, and `reg ∈ 𝔥` whose gradings `φ = Re (wt · reg)` vanish exactly on the zero
   weights and are positive on the positive eigenvectors of `H`.
2. **The Levi projection** (module `ParabolicCenterProjectionLevi`). The Levi basis of `m`, the
   abelian Lie subalgebra `leviCartan` of grading zero, and the centre projection
   `leviProj : Z(U(m)) → Z(U(leviCartan))` modulo the positive right ideal of `m` for `φ`. It is
   injective by the vanishing theorem for central elements in the positive right ideal (module
   `LieCenterProjectionInjective`).
3. **The Harish-Chandra map of `Z(U(g))`** (module `ParabolicCenterProjectionCoordinates`).
   `hcMap p = cartanPoly ∘ leviProj ∘ p` with values in the polynomial ring on the letters of
   grading zero (`AbelianEnvelopingPolynomial`). Composing the two congruences shows that
   `z - U(j ∘ incl)(leviProj (p z))` lies in the positive right ideal of `g` for `φ`, which
   contains `n U(g)`. Hence the coefficients of `hcMap p z` are PBW coordinates of `z`
   (`coeff_hcMap`), and `hcMap p` is injective (`hcMap_injective`).
4. **Leading forms** (modules `LieGelfandCentralElements`, `ParabolicCenterProjectionLeading`).
   For `z ∈ U_n`, `hcMap p z` has degree at most `n`, with degree-`n` part the restriction of the
   symbol of `z` to the letters of grading zero. For Gelfand's elements `tr(T^n)` of the adjoint
   representation and the form `B` this restriction is the power sum `∑ q, ℓ q ^ n` of the
   linear forms `ℓ q` dual to the weights; for a central `c ∈ g` it is the linear form of `c`.
5. **Graded finiteness** (`moduleFinite_leadForm`). The polynomial ring is finite over the
   subalgebra generated by these leading forms: each `ℓ q` is integral over it by Newton's
   identities (`isIntegral_of_powerSums`), the centre gives the remaining linear forms, and
   `𝔥` is spanned by the duals of the weights and the centre
   (`cartan_le_span_dualOf_sup_center`).
6. **Filtered finiteness** (`moduleFinite_range_hcMap`). By `moduleFinite_of_leading` the
   polynomial ring is finite over the image of `hcMap p`.
7. **Artin–Tate** (`center_isNoetherian`, `finite`). The image of `hcMap p` is a finitely
   generated algebra (Mathlib's `fg_of_fg_of_fg`), hence Noetherian, and isomorphic to
   `Z(U(g))` by injectivity. `leviHC = cartanPoly ∘ leviProj` is an injective map of
   `Z(U(g))`-modules from `Z(U(m))` into the finite module `MvPolynomial`, so `Z(U(m))` is a
   finite `Z(U(g))`-module (`Module.Finite.of_injective`).

## Declarations of this module

- `LeadIndex`, `centerBasis`, `leadDegree`, `leadForm`: the power sums `∑ q, ℓ q ^ (k+1)` for
  `k < card ι` and the restricted linear forms of a basis of the centre, with their degrees.
- `moduleFinite_leadForm`: step 5. The set of `v ∈ g` with `zeroRestrict (ξ v)` integral is a
  subspace containing the duals of the weights and the centre, hence all of `𝔥`; the variables
  are integral, and integral plus finite type gives finite (`Algebra.IsIntegral.finite`).
- `ι_mem_center`, `leadElement`, `leadElement_mem`, `homogeneousComponent_leadElement`: the
  central elements with these leading forms.
- `moduleFinite_range_hcMap`, `center_isNoetherian`, `hcMap_finite`, `finite`: steps 6 and 7.
- `parabolicCenterProjectionFinite`: the target, from `nonempty_gradingData` and `finite`.

## Relation to the sources

Harish-Chandra, *Automorphic Forms on Semisimple Lie Groups* (LNM 62, 1968), Chapter I, §6,
printed p. 19 (PDF p. 29), registered as
`provided-d0ff4732ff6b5d8ecf8562752577df639622e8aad2899ef307bc320213b534af`, obtains the
finiteness (indeed freeness of finite rank) through the Harish-Chandra isomorphisms of the two
centres with Weyl group invariants, in the normalized convention with the left ideal `𝔊n`. The
proof here does not use Weyl groups or the normalization. It works directly with the right ideal
`n U(g)` of the statement, defines the Harish-Chandra maps by the projections themselves, proves
their injectivity by a symbol argument, and replaces the description of their images by
Gelfand's central elements and their leading forms, which suffice for finiteness. Harish-Chandra,
*Two theorems on semi-simple Lie groups* (1966), Lemma 13, printed p. 95 (PDF p. 22), registered
as `provided-3315156dfd5120bb69df4005d1cb66c074532e8f8ab3cd2459c5990658370e91`, is the source of
the projection itself (there modulo `𝔊n`; the right-ideal version is its transport by the
principal antiautomorphism). The definition of reductive is Humphreys, *Introduction to Lie
Algebras and Representation Theory*, GTM 9, §19.1, printed p. 102 (PDF p. 115), registered as
`provided-e4636f4e6257fbf54e791ed1ca16e71c5ba9c10c64f8184fd7ec2e0fe6e211e5`.
