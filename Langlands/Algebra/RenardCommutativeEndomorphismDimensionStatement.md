# Renard C.I: dimension of a commutative endomorphism algebra

This module supplies two Prop-valued definitions, with arbitrary carrier
universe `uV`, for the unnumbered lemma in David Renard,
*Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, French edition dated
9 July 2026, Appendix C.I, printed pp.275–276 / one-based PDF281–282.
The statement is on printed p.275; its proof continues on p.276.

`Langlands.Renard.CommutativeEndomorphismDimensionStatement.{uV}` quantifies
over every `V : Type uV` with `AddCommGroup V`, `Module ℂ V` and
`FiniteDimensional ℂ V`, every `l : ℕ`, and every
`a : Fin l → Module.End ℂ V`. Assuming
`∀ i j, Commute (a i) (a j)`, it asserts

```text
(Module.finrank ℂ (Algebra.adjoin ℂ (Set.range a)) : ℝ) ≤
  Real.rpow (Module.finrank ℂ V : ℝ)
    (2 - Real.rpow 2 (1 - (l : ℝ))).
```

Thus, with `R = Algebra.adjoin ℂ (Set.range a)` and
`k = Module.finrank ℂ V`, the bound is precisely
`dim R ≤ k^(2 - 2^(1-l))`. Both powers are `Real.rpow`, and both
subtractions take place in `ℝ`. In particular, `1 - (l : ℝ)` is
negative when `l > 1`; no truncated natural subtraction occurs.
The dimension on the left is also explicitly cast to `ℝ`.
Finite-dimensionality of the endomorphism space and its subalgebras follows
from Mathlib's usual instances, including
`FiniteDimensional.finiteDimensional_subalgebra`.

`Langlands.Renard.CommutativeSubalgebraDimensionStatement.{uV}` additionally
quantifies over an arbitrary
`R : Subalgebra ℂ (Module.End ℂ V)`. It uses the same actual
commutation hypothesis and explicitly assumes
`R = Algebra.adjoin ℂ (Set.range a)`, then asserts the identical bound
for `Module.finrank ℂ R`.

## Correspondence with the source

The source takes a commutative complex subalgebra of `End(V)` generated by
`l` elements and the identity. `Algebra.adjoin` is the smallest complex
subalgebra containing the family; it includes scalar endomorphisms and the
identity. These are part of the unital algebra structure, and the identity
does not consume one of the `l` generators. This is the unital convention
of this particular lemma, independently of nonunital conventions elsewhere
in the book.

Starting from the Lean family, `Commute (a i) (a j)` means the actual
endomorphism compositions agree in the two orders. It implies
`(Set.range a).Pairwise Commute`: choose indices witnessing membership in
the range and apply the family hypothesis. Mathlib's
`Algebra.isMulCommutative_adjoin` then gives commutativity of the generated
subalgebra. Conversely, generators in a commutative source subalgebra commute
after inclusion in `Module.End ℂ V`; generation by those elements and the
identity means exactly the displayed equality with `Algebra.adjoin`.
Indexing a list of `l` generators by `Fin l` only changes its labels.
The all-indices commutation hypothesis includes equal indices, where
commutation holds by reflexivity, so it imposes no additional restriction.
No distinctness or minimality of the generating list is required.

The two Prop statements express the same assertion: specialize the
source-facing one to the generated algebra and the reflexive equality to
obtain the first; conversely substitute the generation equality into the
source-facing conclusion and apply the first. This correspondence is
documented here without adding a separate bridge theorem.

## Boundaries and scope

Both definitions quantify over every natural `l` and every finite dimension,
without a positivity or nontriviality assumption.
For `l = 0`, the family has empty range, its adjoin is the scalar subalgebra
`⊥`, and the real exponent is zero. The right side is therefore one,
including when `k = 0`, by `Real.rpow_zero`. On a nonzero vector space the
scalar algebra has dimension one; on the zero vector space it has dimension
zero. If `k = 0`, the whole endomorphism space and each subalgebra have
dimension zero. For positive `l` the exponent is positive, so both sides
are zero; for `l = 0` the bound is `0 ≤ 1`.
In particular, the `Real.rpow 0 0 = 1` convention handles the joint corner.
No nilpotence, diagonalizability, chosen basis, or extra faithfulness
assumption is present.
