# The right multiplication splitting chart of an embedded field

An embedded field acts on the division algebra on the left. Right multiplication by the opposite division algebra commutes with this action. In a basis for the left module, these right multiplications give a matrix representation, which extends to an algebra map from the scalar extension of the opposite division algebra. The source is simple and the two dimensions agree, so the map is a splitting chart. Its pure tensor formula keeps the original left field and right division multiplications explicit.

This chart can use the cyclic basis from the division uniformizer presentation in Drinfeld, *Coverings of p-adic Symmetric Regions* (1976), §2 B, printed p. 110. It supplies the chart needed to compare that presentation with the Library's local Brauer invariant.
