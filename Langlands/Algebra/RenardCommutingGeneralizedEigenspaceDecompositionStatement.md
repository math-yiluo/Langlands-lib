## Objects and quantified scope

`Langlands.Renard.CommutingGeneralizedEigenspaceDecompositionStatement`
is a closed proposition with arbitrary carrier universe `u`. It
quantifies over every finite-dimensional complex module `V : Type u`,
every natural tuple length `l`, and every tuple
`a : Fin l → Module.End ℂ V` whose entries commute pairwise. It imposes
no nilpotence or invertibility assumption on the original entries.

The imported declarations in `Langlands.Renard.CommutingReduction` fix
all the block data from the original tuple:

- `jointSpace a χ` is `⨅ i, (a i).maxGenEigenspace (χ i)`.
- `jointBlockIndex a` is precisely the subtype of complex tuples `χ`
  for which that intersection is not bottom.
- `blockRestriction a hcomm χ` is the restriction of the original
  evaluation action of
  `NilpotentFiltration.generatedAlgebra a = Algebra.adjoin ℂ (Set.range a)`
  to that actual subspace.
- `blockAlgebra a hcomm χ` is the unital algebra generated by the
  original restricted tuple. The published `blockAlgebra_eq_range`
  identifies it with the image of `blockRestriction`.

There is no supplied decomposition, alternate family of subspaces or
replacement algebra action.

## The six conclusions

1. `jointBlockIndex a` is finite. The entire eigenvalue-tuple space
   need not be finite; only the subtype of nonzero actual intersections
   is asserted finite.
2. The full family `jointSpace a`, indexed by all complex tuples, is
   `iSupIndep`. Thus its supported subfamily is also independent.
   Zero intersections remain in the full family and do not contribute
   to the supported family.
3. The supremum of the supported actual spaces is top. Together with
   independence, this expresses the source's direct-sum decomposition
   of `V`. It does not say that an arbitrary vector lies in a single
   block.
4. For every `Fintype` enumeration of that exact supported subtype,
   the sum of the dimensions of its actual spaces equals `finrank ℂ V`.
   This is the usual dimension consequence of the finite independent
   spanning decomposition.
5. The function sending an original algebra element `r` to the family
   `χ ↦ blockRestriction a hcomm χ.val r` on all supported blocks is
   injective. Its codomain is the dependent product of the actual
   block endomorphism algebras. Equality of all original restrictions
   therefore detects equality of original algebra elements.
6. For every `Fintype` enumeration of the same supported subtype,
   the dimension of the original generated algebra is at most the
   sum of the dimensions of the actual `blockAlgebra` images.
   The right side uses those images, rather than the full
   endomorphism algebras of the blocks. The injective restriction
   function takes values in the product of these images, giving
   this finite-dimensional comparison.

The universal `Fintype` binders make the two finite sums explicit
without choosing an enumeration in the statement's definitions.
The first conclusion guarantees that such enumerations exist.
Neither finite support, independence, spanning nor injectivity is
assumed in the target.

## Provenance and conventions

The finite supported direct-sum assertion translates the successive
characteristic-space refinement in the source's first proof paragraph.
At each refinement step one intersects the preceding space with the
next original generator's generalized eigenspace. The final actual
intersections are therefore the `jointSpace` family above. Its finite
dimension sum is an elementary consequence of that direct sum.

The source's subsequent reduction to a bound on each restricted algebra
also uses the fact that the original action is detected on the spanning
blocks. The explicit injective function and the comparison with the sum
of the image dimensions state these internal linear algebra consequences
separately; they are not additional displayed lemmas in the book.
The published proof `Langlands.CommutingDimension.commuting_dimension`
already uses this route internally, and the generic
`dimension_le_sum_restrictions` and `dimension_bound_of_decomposition`
provide related tools. They do not export the present closed statement
about the published actual `jointSpace` and `blockAlgebra` objects.

The statement retains zero-dimensional carriers, zero and redundant
generators, eigenvalue zero, and empty tuples. On a zero carrier the
supported subtype is empty, all displayed dimensions are zero, and
the restriction function is injective from the zero algebra. For an
empty tuple there is one possible eigenvalue tuple and its intersection
is top; it is supported exactly when `V` is nonzero. The algebra is
unital, so the empty tuple generates the scalar algebra. Scalars and
the identity do not count as tuple entries. Finite dimension is an
explicit hypothesis of this statement, although the imported actual
constructions have broader domains.
