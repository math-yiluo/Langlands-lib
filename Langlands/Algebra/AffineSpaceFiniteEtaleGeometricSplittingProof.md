# Geometric splitting of finite étale affine-space algebras

The internal theorem `exists_algEquiv_pi_of_isAlgClosed` proves that a finite
étale algebra over a polynomial ring over an algebraically closed
characteristic-zero field is a finite power of that polynomial ring as an
algebra over the base. This is the geometric splitting input for the
polynomial product theorem over an arbitrary characteristic-zero field.

Noetherian descent presents the given algebra as a base change of an étale
algebra over a finitely generated rational subalgebra of the polynomial
base. The coefficients of finitely many generators lie in the image of
a countable algebraically closed field. That field is the algebraic closure
of the intermediate field generated by the coefficients. Factoring the
coefficient maps through its polynomial ring gives an étale model there.
Module finiteness descends because extending a coefficient field induces
a faithfully flat map on polynomial rings.

The model field embeds in the complex numbers. Its transcendence basis over
the rationals is countable and can be embedded in a transcendence basis of the
complex numbers, which is infinite by the cardinality bound for algebraic
extensions. Evaluation at the embedded basis extends to the model field
by algebraic closedness. The complex splitting theorem applies to the base
change along this embedding. Specializing sections descends its splitting
to the model. Base change along the original coefficient embedding preserves
the finite product and gives the asserted equivalence.
