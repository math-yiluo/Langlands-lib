# Eventual residue-fibre injectivity for local approximation systems: proof

`Langlands.Algebra.localApproximationResidueInjectivity` proves the published
proposition `Langlands.Algebra.LocalApproximationResidueInjectivityStatement`.
Its complete type is exactly that proposition, with the seven independent
universe parameters `uI uA uB uN uR uS uM` in the statement's order. The
statement pair and all of its imports are unchanged. The proof uses only the
standard axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

## What is proved

The setting is a directed nonempty index preorder `ι`, local rings `R → S`, a
system `D : LocalAlgebraSystem ι R S`, a module `M` with the caller's
compatible `R`- and `S`-actions, and `E : LocalModuleSystem D M` satisfying
the six clauses of `E.IsApproximation` (Stacks 00QX). The theorem gives one
index `i₀` such that for every `j ≥ i₀` both of the following hold:

- the actual map `E.residueFiberΨ j : κ ⊗[κⱼ] (κⱼ ⊗[Rⱼ] Mⱼ) → κ ⊗[R] M` is
  injective;
- the actual map `E.residueFiberι j : κⱼ ⊗[Rⱼ] Mⱼ → κ ⊗[R] M` is injective.

Here `Rⱼ = D.base j`, `Mⱼ = E.obj j`, `κⱼ` is the residue field of `Rⱼ` and
`κ` the residue field of `R`. No hypothesis is added. The proof uses these
parts of `E.IsApproximation`:

- surjectivity of `D.baseColimitMap` and bijectivity of `E.moduleColimitMap`
  (clause 1);
- essential finite type of `D.alg i` over `D.base i`, and finiteness of
  `E.obj i` over `D.alg i`, at one fixed stage `i` (clauses 3 and 4);
- prime localization of `D.ringBaseChangeMap h` and surjectivity of
  `E.moduleBaseChangeMap h`, for transitions starting at that stage
  (clauses 5 and 6).

## Provenance and relation to the source

The argument is the eventual-injectivity paragraph of the proof of Stacks
Lemma 10.128.4 ([046Y](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/046Y)) in the
registered original `stacks-algebra-flatness-criteria-2026-10-22` (content
SHA256 `4bc1f2db6141508f6dad2132d4abc7963380cd71497ad20620c0e511b972a949`,
file `046Y.html`), together with the raw-fibre corollary at the start of the
following paragraph. The six clauses are those of Lemma 10.127.13
([00QX](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00QX), file `00QL.html`). No
source-specific errata note is registered. The current-page check
`stacks-046y-00qx-current-errata-check-2026-10-26` recorded zero comments and
unchanged statements and proofs.

The source fixes `λ` and makes three moves. The ring
`(S_λ/𝔪_λ S_λ) ⊗_{κ_λ} κ` is Noetherian, so `Ker Ψ_λ` is finitely generated.
Since `κ` and `M/𝔪M` are colimits, that kernel is generated by the kernel of
some `Ψ_{λ,λ'}`. Finally, `S_{λ'}` is a localization `W⁻¹(S_λ ⊗ R_{λ'})`, so a
unit multiple of each element of `Ker Ψ_{λ'}` comes from `Ker Ψ_λ` and is
therefore zero. The Lean proof follows the same three ideas with these changes
of presentation:

1. It works with the single tensor `κ ⊗[Rⱼ] Mⱼ` instead of
   `(Mⱼ/𝔪ⱼMⱼ) ⊗_{κⱼ} κ`. It transfers the result to the published maps only at
   the end, through the canonical cancellation isomorphism.
2. It replaces "the kernel is finitely generated and its generators die at a
   later stage" by the ascending chain condition on the kernels of the
   transitions, which is the same Noetherian input.
3. It never constructs the localization isomorphism
   `W⁻¹(M_λ/𝔪_λM_λ ⊗ κ_{λ'}) ≅ M_{λ'}/𝔪_{λ'}M_{λ'}`. It proves directly the
   one consequence the argument uses: for each element `y` there is an
   invertible `u` such that `u • y` lies in the image of the transition.

## Implemented proof

All auxiliary declarations are in the namespace
`Langlands.Algebra.ResidueInjectivityProof`. They serve only this proof and
introduce no new interface.

**Extended components.** `fieldAlgebra` makes `κ` an `Rᵢ`-algebra through
`residue R ∘ D.baseι i`. It is a local instance inside the namespace only.
`FieldFiber E i = κ ⊗[Rᵢ] Mᵢ`. The maps `fieldFiberMap E h` and
`fieldFiberι E i` are the semilinear `TensorProduct.map` of the identity of
`κ` with the published `E.baseMap h` and `E.baseι i`, so that
`a ⊗ m ↦ a ⊗ E.map i j h m` and `a ⊗ m ↦ a ⊗ E.ιM i m`. Their identity,
composition and cocone laws are `fieldFiberMap_self`, `fieldFiberMap_comp`
and `fieldFiberι_compat`.

**Colimit step.** `exists_fieldFiberMap_eq_zero` shows that if
`fieldFiberι E i x = 0` then `fieldFiberMap E h x = 0` for some `h : i ≤ j`.
This is the colimit observation of the source, with the constant field `κ`.
Let `FieldFiberLimit E` be the additive direct limit of the `κ ⊗[Rᵢ] Mᵢ`.
`limitPairing` sends `m ∈ M` and `a ∈ κ` to the class of `a ⊗ mᵢ`, where
`m = E.ιM i mᵢ`. It is defined by `AddCommGroup.DirectLimit.lift` composed
with the inverse of the bijective `E.moduleColimitMap`.
`limitPairing_balanced` checks that it is `R`-balanced. That check uses
surjectivity of `D.baseColimitMap`, directedness, and the semilinearity of
`E.baseι`. So `TensorProduct.liftAddHom` gives `limitInverse`, an additive map
`κ ⊗[R] M → FieldFiberLimit E` with
`limitInverse (fieldFiberι E i x) = of i x` (`limitInverse_fieldFiberι`).
`AddCommGroup.DirectLimit.of.zero_exact` then gives the later stage.

**Noetherian step.** `FieldFiberRing D i = Sᵢ ⊗[Rᵢ] κ` acts on
`κ ⊗[Rᵢ] Mᵢ` through the published `ResidueFiberTensor.rawModule`.
`fieldFiberRing_isNoetherianRing` gets Noetherianity from
`Algebra.EssFiniteType.baseChange`, `Algebra.EssFiniteType.isNoetherianRing`
and the tensor commutativity ring isomorphism. The module is finite because
`ResidueFiberTensor.linearComparison` identifies it with the base change
`(Sᵢ ⊗[Rᵢ] κ) ⊗[Sᵢ] Mᵢ` of the finite `Sᵢ`-module `Mᵢ`. The transitions are
semilinear over `fieldFiberRingMap` (`fieldFiberMap_smul`), so their kernels
form the submodules `transitionKernel E h`. `eventually_kernel_stable`
chooses a maximal one, at `j₀`, by `set_has_maximal_iff_noetherian`. With the
colimit step and directedness, this shows: for every `j ≥ j₀`, every
`x ∈ κ ⊗[Rᵢ] Mᵢ` with `fieldFiberι E i x = 0` has
`fieldFiberMap E (i ≤ j) x = 0`.

**Localization step.** For `i ≤ j`, `exists_unit_smul_eq` gives, for each
`y ∈ κ ⊗[Rⱼ] Mⱼ`, a unit `u` of `Sⱼ ⊗[Rⱼ] κ` and an `x` with
`u • y = fieldFiberMap E h x`. Here `u = φ(w) ⊗ 1` with `w` outside the prime
of `D.IsPrimeLocalization h`, and `φ = D.ringBaseChangeMap h`, so `φ(w)` is a
unit of `Sⱼ`. The proof is by induction on tensors:

- surjectivity of `E.moduleBaseChangeMap h` writes component elements as sums
  of `s' • E.map i j h m`;
- `IsLocalization.surj` writes `s' * φ(w) = φ(b)`;
- `smul_mem_range` shows that the image of the transition is stable under
  `φ(b) ⊗ 1`;
- sums are handled by multiplying the two units.

**Injectivity at later stages.** `fieldFiberι_injective` proves injectivity
of `fieldFiberι E j` for `j ≥ j₀`. Let `fieldFiberι E j y = 0`, and write
`u • y = fieldFiberMap E h x`. By `fieldFiberι_compat` and the semilinearity
`fieldFiberι_smul`, `fieldFiberι E i x = 0`. The Noetherian step then gives
`u • y = 0`, so `y = 0` because `u` is a unit.

**Transfer to the published maps.** `residueExtensionEquiv E j` is
`TensorProduct.AlgebraTensorModule.cancelBaseChange`, from
`κ ⊗[κⱼ] (κⱼ ⊗[Rⱼ] Mⱼ)` to `κ ⊗[Rⱼ] Mⱼ`. Here `κ` is a `κⱼ`-algebra through
`D.residueι j`, exactly as in `E.residueFiberΨ`. `residueFiberΨ_eq` checks,
on pure tensors, that `E.residueFiberΨ j` is `fieldFiberι E j` composed with
this equivalence, so `E.residueFiberΨ j` is injective for `j ≥ j₀`. Finally,
`E.residueFiberι j x = E.residueFiberΨ j (1 ⊗ x)` by the published
`residueFiberΨ_tmul`. The map `x ↦ 1 ⊗ x` is injective
(`includeResidue_injective`): it is the `rTensor` of the injective field map
`κⱼ → κ` on a `κⱼ`-vector space, which is flat. So the raw residue-fibre map is
injective at the same stages, as in the source's corollary.

The main theorem takes the fixed stage `i = Classical.arbitrary ι` and the
bound `i₀ = j₀` from the Noetherian step, and combines the two injectivity
results.
