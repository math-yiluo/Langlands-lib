All names below are in `Langlands.Algebra.NonUnitalModuleDual`. The algebra universe `uA` and module-carrier universe `uM` are independent. Inputs are a specified associative `NonUnitalRing A`, its specified `Module ℂ A`, both algebra scalar compatibility classes, `hA : IsIdempotented A`, and a specified original additive `NonUnitalModule A M` with `hM : IsNondegenerate A M`. There is no supplied complex module on `M`, no assumed unit law, and no dimension, admissibility, faithfulness, anti-involution or nontriviality hypothesis.

`FullDual hA hM` is exactly `Module.Dual ℂ M` using `NondegenerateScalars.complexModule hA hM`. `fullAction` constructs its original right action as `NonUnitalModule Aᵐᵒᵖ (FullDual hA hM)`. Its value at `a` is composition with the actual linear action `NondegenerateScalars.toLinearEndHom ℂ hA hM a.unop`. Thus linearity of the resulting functional uses the published compatibility of the original action with the recovered scalars. The action laws are proved by evaluating at `m`; associativity uses `(a * b).unop = b.unop * a.unop`, and additivity uses the original action's distributive laws and linearity of the functional. `fullAction_apply`, `fullAction_coe` and `fullAction_eq_comp` give its evaluation, entire underlying function and equality as a linear map. `full_isScalarTower` follows from the original canonical scalar compatibility and linearity of each functional; `full_smulCommClass` is pointwise. These named structures use the usual pointwise complex scalars on the full dual.

`Dual hA hM` is the actual subtype `nondegeneratePart hA.op (FullDual hA hM)` for this very action. `dualAction` names its inherited original right action. `inclusion` is the actual original intertwining subtype inclusion; `inclusion_coe` and `inclusion_apply` preserve the entire underlying functional. `dualAction_apply` evaluates its right action, and `isNondegenerate` is the published intrinsic nondegeneracy result for this subtype. `mem_dual_iff` says exactly that some idempotent `e : Idempotent A` satisfies `φ ((e : A) • m) = φ m` for every `m`. The proof transports the fixing idempotent through multiplicative opposite in each direction and uses equality/extensionality of linear maps. The equivalent opposite-ring fixing equation is the definition of this nondegenerate part.

`dualModule` constructs pointwise complex scalars on the same subtype, preserving its inherited additive group. A fixing idempotent for `φ` also fixes `c • φ`, by full-dual scalar commutation. Each module law follows by subtype extensionality from the full-dual pointwise law. `dualModule_smul_val` and `dualModule_smul_apply` give the complete scalar functional and its value. `dual_isScalarTower` and `dual_smulCommClass` restrict the full-dual compatibility laws. `canonicalModule_eq_dualModule` is equality of module structures: the scalar module recovered from this same original right action equals the inherited pointwise module. It invokes `NondegenerateScalars.module_eq_of_compatible`; only the proved tower law is needed for uniqueness. `canonical_smul_val` and `canonical_smul_apply` expose the resulting pointwise formulas under the explicitly recovered structure. `inclusionLinear` and `inclusionLinear_coe` give the complex linear inclusion for `dualModule` with the same entire underlying subtype inclusion.
