# The centre map from a triangular enveloping decomposition

`RelativeEnvelopingDecomposition` records the algebraic properties
that are needed from the triangular decomposition. The right ideal
is stable under left multiplication by the Levi algebra and has zero
intersection with its image. A central element has a unique
representative in the Levi algebra modulo this ideal. Commuting with
the Levi algebra shows that the representative is central there.
The two stability properties prove multiplicativity of this
projection.

This is the algebraic step of the relative enveloping-centre argument
used in Langlands (1979), Lemma 4, printed p. 204 (Corvallis Part I,
PDF p. 199). The registered source is
`langlands-1979-notion-automorphic-representation`, SHA-256
