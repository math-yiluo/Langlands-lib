# Cyclic determinant with a trace section

The weights on a cyclic product of copies of a vector space fix the embedded
subspace pointwise. The determinant computation completes the difference
operator with a projection from the diagonal copy of that subspace and a
section of the coordinate sum.

The proof first takes the projection from coordinate zero and takes the
section supported at the last coordinate. The correction then changes only
the last cyclic weight. The preceding weights fix the correction pointwise,
so the ordered product of the weights changes by exactly the prescribed
projection. The determinant formula for a weighted cyclic shift applies.

Changing the projection is accomplished by a shear whose image lies on the
diagonal. Changing the section uses negative partial sums of the
zero-sum difference. Subtracting the diagonal part of these partial sums
makes the shear vanish on a second application. Each shear is the identity
plus a square-zero operator and has determinant one.

The calculation extends the cyclic determinant step of Clozel, *Théorème
d’Atiyah-Bott pour les variétés p-adiques et caractères des groupes
réductifs*, §6.3, Lemma 5, printed pp. 60–61, to a corrected cyclic defect.
It uses only linear algebra over a field of arbitrary characteristic.
