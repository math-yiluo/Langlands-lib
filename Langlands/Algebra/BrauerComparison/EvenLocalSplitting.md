# Even-degree splitting of local Brauer two-torsion

`Langlands.BrauerComparison.EvenLocalSplittingStatement` says that a finite extension of even degree of a finite extension of `ℚₚ` splits a central simple algebra whose Brauer class is killed by two. The statement takes a common finite Galois splitting chart as input. It uses the published local invariant and its degree law for restriction.

The tensor-square comparison makes the relative class of the chart two-torsion. Its inflation to the common field is still two-torsion. The local invariant of its restriction multiplies by the extension degree, so the restricted invariant vanishes. Injectivity of the local invariant gives zero relative class. The scalar-extended chart computes that class and descends to a matrix chart over the extension itself.
