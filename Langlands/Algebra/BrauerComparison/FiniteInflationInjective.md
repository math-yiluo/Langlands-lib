# Injectivity of finite-stage inflation on continuous degree-two unit cohomology

A finite Galois intermediate field of a Galois extension has an open
normal fixing subgroup, and the fixed field of that subgroup is the
original intermediate field. Thus an ordinary relative class at the
intermediate field can be represented at the corresponding finite stage
of continuous cohomology.

If the class maps to zero continuously, the published finite-stage
comparison gives a refinement where its image is zero. Refinement of
open normal subgroups gives inclusion of their fixed fields. The
published refinement calculation identifies the transition on classes
with ordinary Galois inflation in this finite tower. Degree-two
inflation is injective for this tower by the published inflation and
restriction exactness theorem, so the original relative class was zero.

This gives the injective degree-two passage to the separable closure
used in J.-P. Serre, *Local Fields* (1979), Chapter X, §5, printed p. 159.
The ambient Galois extension and finite stages are arbitrary, so one can
specialize to the separable closure in any characteristic.
