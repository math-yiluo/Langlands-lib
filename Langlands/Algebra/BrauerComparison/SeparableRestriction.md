# Restriction on continuous unit cohomology

Let `E/K` be a field extension, let `S/K` be normal, and let `T/E`
be algebraic. Given a compatible K-embedding of `S` into `T`, the Galois
group map first restricts base scalars to `K` and then restricts to `S`.
The base-scalar map is continuous for the Krull topologies. A finite set
of generators of a finite K-intermediate field of `T` also generates a
finite E-intermediate field. Fixing the latter field fixes the generators
and therefore the original K-intermediate field.

`continuousUnitRestriction` is the Mathlib continuous cohomology map for
this group homomorphism and the actual inclusion of units. For compatible
finite normal stages, the two composite group maps are equal because
both restrict the same automorphism to the same embedded field. The two
coefficient maps are equal by compatibility of the inclusions. Thus
restriction of an inflated class is computed at finite stages by ordinary
inflation followed by ordinary restriction.

When `T/E` is Galois, a specified K-embedding of a finite extension `L/K`
into `T` factors through a finite Galois intermediate field of `T/E`. The
proof takes the Galois closure over `E` of the field generated by finitely
many image generators. A separable closure of `E` supplies the Galois
target in the Brauer comparison.

These are cohomological ingredients of naturality for Serre's comparison
in *Local Fields* (1979), Chapter X, §§4–5, printed pp. 156 and 158–159.
The square is proved on the actual cochains and holds in every degree.
