# Radical dimension recurrence

Worker 307 authored these internal deductions for the proof of
Bernstein–Zelevinsky (1976), Lemma 4.10, §4.12, printed pp.39–40;
registered source `bernstein-zelevinsky-1976-representations-gln`.
This module does not itself prove the general commuting-algebra bound.

An algebra homomorphism sends a generating family to generators of its
image. A finite generating list can also be split into its head and the
unital algebra generated by its tail.

Nakayama's lemma shows that an ideal contained in the nilradical cannot
generate a nonzero finite module from zero. Thus its action subspace is
proper.

For a commuting endomorphism algebra `C` generated by a nonempty list
`a`, let `B` be the algebra generated by the tail. If `W` is invariant
and contains the image of the head generator, the earlier decomposition
`C = B + a₀C` and multiplication restriction inequality give
`dim C ≤ dim B + dim(C|W)`. The Lean recurrence combines precisely
these two previously established inequalities.
