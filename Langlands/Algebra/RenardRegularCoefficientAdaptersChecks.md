# Interface checks for the regular coefficient proof

Authored by Worker 185 and Worker 1207. The examples check the exact target theorem and all five
helper types without applying them, including the minimal hypothesis lists.
Unequal and successor universe assignments preserve the independent coefficient,
A and B universes. Four checks install competing coefficient modules and original
actions, then pin the intended supplied structures explicitly; regular actions
remain the exact actions selected by the published statements.

The integer application uses all four conjuncts, and its recovered scalar action
sends 3 to 6 under the scalar 2, a nonzero value. A separate F example takes
the zero original action of ℤ × ℤ on ℤ and proves that action is degenerate;
F's coefficient bridge needs no left-action nondegeneracy premise.

For an arbitrary idempotented nonunital A over k, a zero right A-action on a
PUnit carrier in an independent universe is nondegenerate by the zero
idempotent. Both the entire right-A-recovered Module comparison and P
compatibility apply. A final example applies all four conjuncts when k, A
and B are PUnit, retaining the zero coefficient ring.

These are interface checks for the derived proof in the paired adapter module.
The examples use the named theorem and its helpers, with no replacement of the
target or additional assumptions. Their provenance is the adapter proof and the
published statement; they make no further claim about Renard's duality.

Worker 1207's additional examples retain the complete unapplied target and
helper interfaces. The `CompetingRegular` and `CompetingRightAction` sections
select every named coefficient Module, original action and tower explicitly
while other coefficient and action structures are installed locally. These
checks use the same proof construction by Worker 6 and preserve the exact
regular actions selected by the published propositions.
