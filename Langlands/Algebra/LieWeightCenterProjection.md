# The relative centre projection for an adjoint grading of a Lie algebra

Module `Langlands.Algebra.LieWeightCenterProjection`, namespace
`Langlands.Algebra.LieWeightProjection`.

This module carries out, for an arbitrary Lie algebra `L` over a field `k` of characteristic
zero, the triangular-decomposition argument of the modules
`Langlands.Algebra.EnvelopingWeightDecomposition`,
`Langlands.Algebra.EnvelopingPositiveIdealBasis` and
`Langlands.Algebra.EnvelopingPositiveWeightLie`, which treat the commutator Lie algebra of a
finite-dimensional associative algebra. The PBW basis ordered by decreasing grading,
`weightOrderedPBWBasis b d`, is `orderedMonomialBasis` of
`Langlands.Algebra.OrderedMonomialBasis` along `weightOrderedIndices d`.

## Data

A finite basis `b : Module.Basis I k L` and real gradings `d : I → ℝ`. Several results also use
an element `H` acting diagonally, `⁅H, b a⁆ = ev a • b a`, and an additive map `ψ : k →+ ℝ`
with `ψ (ev a) = d a`. For an integral grading by `H` one takes `ev a = d a` (for `k = ℂ`,
`ψ` the real part); for the real parts of the eigenvalues of a complex element, `ψ` is again
the real part.

## Results

- `positiveWeightRightIdeal b d` is the span of the products `ι(b a) * v` with `d a > 0`; it is
  a right ideal (`mul_mem_positiveWeightRightIdeal`), and
  `mul_mem_positiveWeightRightIdeal_of_span` puts `ι x * u` in it for `x` in the span of the
  positive basis vectors. `zeroWeightEnvelopingSubalgebra b d` is generated by the `ι(b a)`
  with `d a = 0`.
- `weightOrderedPBWBasis_repr_ad`: the adjoint action of `ι H` multiplies each PBW coordinate
  by the total eigenvalue of its monomial. `center_repr_sum_eq_zero`: central elements have PBW
  coordinates only on monomials of total eigenvalue zero.
- `center_mem_positiveWeight_sup_zeroWeight`: every central element lies in
  `positiveWeightRightIdeal b d ⊔ zeroWeightEnvelopingSubalgebra b d`. A monomial of total
  eigenvalue zero has total grading zero (apply `ψ`); in the decreasing order it starts with a
  positive letter or has only letters of grading zero.
- `mem_eigenSpan_of_ad`: an eigenvector of `ad H` with eigenvalue `μ` lies in the span of the
  basis vectors with `ev a = μ`. `lie_basis_mem_span`: `⁅b a, b q⁆` lies in the span of the
  basis vectors of grading `d a + d q`.
- `orderedSubindices`, `map_orderedMonomial_subindices`: ordered monomials of a Lie algebra with
  a basis mapped onto a subfamily of `b` go to ordered monomials of `L`.
- `positivePBWSpan b d` is the span of the ordered monomials having a positive letter;
  `positivePBWSpan_coord_zero`: its elements have no coordinate on monomials without positive
  letters. `positiveWeightRightIdeal_le_positivePBWSpan`: given a Lie map `j : N → L` sending a
  basis of `N` onto the positive basis vectors, the right ideal lies in `positivePBWSpan`: left
  multiplication by a positive generator only changes the positive initial word, which expands
  in the positive enveloping algebra into monomials with zero augmentation.
- `repr_envelopingMap_embDomain`: for a Lie map `j : M → L` sending a basis of `M` onto the basis
  vectors of grading zero, the PBW coordinates of `U(j) u` at exponents supported on those
  letters are the ordered PBW coordinates of `u`. `eq_zero_of_envelopingMap_mem_positivePBWSpan`
  follows: an element of `U(M)` whose image has only positive-letter coordinates is zero.
- `positiveWeightRightIdeal_stable_enveloping`: if brackets of `j(M)` with positive basis vectors
  stay in their span, the right ideal is stable under left multiplication by the image of
  `U(M)`.
- `positiveWeightLieSubalgebra`, `positiveWeightLieBasis`: the positive basis vectors span a Lie
  subalgebra (since gradings add under brackets) with the restricted basis.
- `relativeEnvelopingDecomposition_of_weight` assembles these facts into the Library's
  `Langlands.Algebra.RelativeEnvelopingDecomposition` for the enveloping map of `j`, so that
  `RelativeEnvelopingDecomposition.centerProjection` is the algebra map from the centre of
  `U(L)` to the centre of `U(M)` characterized by congruence modulo the positive right ideal.

## Provenance

Harish-Chandra, *Two theorems on semi-simple Lie groups*, Ann. of Math. 83 (1966), Lemma 13,
printed p. 95 (PDF p. 22), registered as
`provided-3315156dfd5120bb69df4005d1cb66c074532e8f8ab3cd2459c5990658370e91`, projects an element
of the enveloping algebra commuting with the grading to the enveloping algebra of the
centralizer modulo the left ideal `𝔊n` generated by the positive part. The module uses the right
ideal `n U(L)` instead, the transport of that statement by the principal antiautomorphism; with
the positive letters ordered first, the PBW argument is the same. The proofs are internal and
follow the Library modules named above.
