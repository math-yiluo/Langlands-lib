# Products of commuting rank-one relation data

Author: Worker 4348.

Module `Langlands.Algebra.RankOneRelationProducts`, namespace
`Langlands.Algebra.RankOneRelation`. Pure group theory over a field `k` and a group `H`,
extending `Algebra.RankOneRelationIdentities`.

## Definitions and results

- `Data.rootSubgroup D`: the subgroup of `H` generated by all `D.xp c` and `D.xm c`.
  `xp_mem`, `xm_mem` and `h_mem` give membership; for `h` it follows from `h_eq_word`.
- `RootsCommute D`, for a family `D : ι → Data k H`: for distinct indices `i ≠ j`, every
  root element `xp c` or `xm c` of `D i` commutes with every root element of `D j`.
- `RootsCommute.subgroups`: then the root subgroups of distinct members commute
  elementwise. This is a nested `Subgroup.closure_induction` over the generators.
- `prodHom hD : (∀ i, (D i).rootSubgroup) →* H`, for finite `ι`: Mathlib's
  `Subgroup.noncommPiCoprod`, the product of one element from each root subgroup. It is a
  group homomorphism because elements of distinct root subgroups commute.
- `prodHom_h hD u`: for units `u i`, the product of the coroot values `(D i).h (u i)` equals
  the word `(∏ xm (u⁻¹(u - 1)))⁻¹ * (∏ xp 1) * (∏ xm (u - 1)) * (∏ xp u⁻¹)⁻¹`. Each `∏` is a
  value of `prodHom`. Proof: the right side is `prodHom` of the pointwise word, by
  `map_mul` and `map_inv`, and the pointwise word is `h_eq_word`.
- `map_prodHom_of_perm hD ψ e f g hfg`: let `ψ : H →* H` be a homomorphism and `e` a
  permutation of `ι` with `ψ (f i) = g (e i)` for all `i`. Then `ψ (prodHom f) = prodHom g`.
  The proof applies `Finset.map_noncommProd` and reindexes the multiset of factors by `e`,
  using `Multiset.map_univ_val_equiv`.
