# The actual Galois action of an algebra splitting chart

The scalar-extension tensor product has its canonical coefficient action by the
actual automorphisms of the extension field. A supplied actual algebra splitting
chart transports this action to the full matrix algebra. The ring-action laws
and coefficient semilinearity follow from the actual tensor map; no descent
action or obstruction is supplied as an additional assumption.

The original algebra maps to the fixed matrix algebra by its second-factor
inclusion followed by the chart. Over a finite Galois field extension, the
published algebra descent equivalence gives the fixed algebra's exact dimension.

That homomorphism is injective by faithful scalar extension. Equality of the
finite dimensions then makes it bijective, giving an actual algebra isomorphism
from the original algebra to the fixed matrix algebra of the supplied chart.
