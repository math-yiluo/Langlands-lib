# Actual tensor-product matrix splitting charts

These internal constructions identify the extension of an actual tensor product
with the tensor product of the two extended factors, using the published actual
tensor-interchange and unit equivalences. Supplied algebra matrix charts then
give an actual Kronecker matrix chart of the tensor product. Its coefficient
Galois action is the Kronecker product of the two actual factor actions.

Actual invertible Kronecker products respect multiplication and coefficient
automorphisms. Applied to the previously constructed actual inner matrices, their
scalar multiplication defect is the product of the two actual unit defects.
These are identities of actual algebra charts and actual invertible matrices,
without a Brauer quotient or a separately assumed tensor compatibility law.

The unit also constructs transport of actual semilinear actions through actual
algebra equivalences, and hence supports arbitrary finite matrix index types.
For a `Fin n` index this action agrees exactly with the earlier splitting-chart
action, so no alternate obstruction convention is introduced.
