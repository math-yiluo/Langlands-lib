# Prime avoidance for an algebra integral over a field

This internal algebraic helper is intended for the principal-open step of
Stacks Lemma 37.67.2 (Tag 0ECJ), whose proof invokes Lemma 32.11.6 (Tag
0E21). It is not a scheme pushout construction.

For any ring homomorphism `φ : R →+* A`, any ideal `I` of `R`, and any
commutative algebra `A` integral over a field `k`, if the ideal generated by
`φ(I)` is all of `A`, the theorem produces an actual `r ∈ I` for which
`φ(r)` is a unit. It retains arbitrary and possibly zero rings and places
no finite generation condition on the integral algebra.

The implemented argument first proves the assertion for an Artinian target:
otherwise the ideal is covered by the contractions of the finitely many
maximal ideals, contradicting Mathlib's finite prime avoidance theorem.
For an arbitrary integral algebra it chooses the finite list of generators
and coefficients in an expression for `1` in the image ideal. These elements
lie in a finite dimensional subalgebra, which is Artinian. The inverse image
of that subalgebra in `R` contains all the needed generators. Applying the
first assertion there gives a unit that remains a unit in `A`.

This is an internal derivation from Mathlib's ideal, integrality and Artinian
ring results; it does not formalize the ample-line-bundle generality of
Tag 0E21 or claim the global pushout theorem.

`integralFieldUnitWitness` makes a noncomputable choice of such an element
in the original ring, as a subtype carrying both ideal membership and the
unit property. The two existence lemmas are the intrinsic correctness
proofs for this construction. All three ring universes are independent.
