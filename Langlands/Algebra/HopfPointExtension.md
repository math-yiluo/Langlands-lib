# Extending Hopf points of finite group algebras to Hopf points of lattices

Author: Worker 1421. The extension idea is due to Worker 950 (peer conversation
`58c3381ebb825a73872a6d46f365baa7`); this coefficient-level formulation and
proof are by Worker 1421.

This module proves the extension lemma used to build charts of the closed
torus subgroup functor (task `45ac6e52-a4d8-4414-91f1-03c3be35223e`). In
geometric terms it says the following. Take a homomorphism from a finite
diagonalizable group `D(M')` to an affine group `Spec A`. Suppose its
coefficients on finitely many generators of `A` lie over a finite window `s`
of `M`, and `π : M → M'` is injective on sums of at most `N` elements of `s`.
Then the homomorphism extends uniquely to a homomorphism from `D(M)` whose
coefficients on the generators lie in `s`. Here `N` bounds the degrees of the
relations and of the comultiplication of the generators.

The source for the strategy is SGA 3, Exposé XI, §3-4 (registered source
`sga3-exposes-xi-xii-xiv-gille-polo-2008`, `Expo11.pdf`). Its proof of
Theorem 4.1 approximates multiplicative subgroups by finite torsion subgroups.
This module replaces the formal algebraization used there by an elementary,
uniform statement. It is not a literal statement of the source.

## Definitions

`R` is a commutative ring, `C` a commutative `R`-algebra, `M` an additive
commutative group, `A` a commutative `R`-bialgebra.

- `groupCounit R C M : C[M] →ₐ[R] C`, sending `single χ c` to `c`.
- `diagMap R C M : C[M] →ₐ[R] C[M × M]`, `X^χ ↦ X^(χ, χ)`;
  `inlMap`, `inrMap`: the maps induced by `χ ↦ (χ, 0)` and `χ ↦ (0, χ)`.
- `pairMap f : A ⊗ A →ₐ[R] C[M × M]`, `a ⊗ b ↦ inl (f a) · inr (f b)`.
- `IsHopfPoint f`, for `f : A →ₐ[R] C[M]`: `pairMap f ∘ Δ = diagMap ∘ f` and
  `groupCounit ∘ f = algebraMap ∘ ε`. Equivalently `f` is a `C`-point of the
  homomorphism functor from `D(M)` to `Spec A`. The comparison with Worker
  950's `Langlands.FiniteBialgebraHom.IsHopfPoint` (for `P = R[M]`, under
  `C ⊗ R[M] ≅ C[M]`) is not proved here.
- `boundedSums s N`: sums of at most `N` elements of `s ⊆ M`, defined by
  recursion. `zero_mem_boundedSums`, `boundedSums_mono`,
  `subset_boundedSums` (for `1 ≤ N`) and `boundedSums_add` are its basic
  properties.
- `IsBoundedPresentation gen N`, for `gen : σ → A`: the `gen i` generate `A`
  as an `R`-algebra; the kernel of `MvPolynomial.aeval gen` is generated by
  relations of total degree at most `N`; and every `Δ (gen i)` is an
  `R`-combination of tensors `aeval gen p ⊗ aeval gen q` with `p, q` of total
  degree at most `N`.

## Results

- Support lemmas: `support_mul_subset` (support of a product lies in the sum of
  the supports), `support_algebraMap_subset`, `support_pow_subset`,
  `support_prod_pow_subset` and `support_aeval_subset`. The last says that a
  polynomial of total degree `d` evaluated at elements supported in `s` is
  supported in `boundedSums s d`. `support_inlMap_mul_inrMap_subset`: if `x`
  and `y` are supported in `S`, then `inl x · inr y` is supported in `S × S`.
- `mapDomain` lemmas: `coeff_mapDomainAlgHom`, `mapDomainAlgHom_injOn`
  (injectivity on elements supported in a set where `π` is injective, from
  `Finsupp.mapDomain_injOn`), `support_mapDomainAlgHom_subset`,
  `exists_lift_of_support_subset` (an element supported in `π '' s` lifts to
  one supported in `s`, using `Function.invFunOn`). Naturality of the counit,
  `diagMap`, `inlMap`, `inrMap` and `pairMap` under `mapDomain`
  (`groupCounit_comp_mapDomain`, `mapDomain_comp_diagMap`,
  `mapDomain_comp_inlMap`, `mapDomain_comp_inrMap`, `mapDomain_comp_pairMap`),
  and
  `IsHopfPoint.mapDomain`: Hopf points push forward along group
  homomorphisms.
- `eq_of_mapDomain_eq`: two algebra maps whose values on generators are
  supported in `s`, with the same image under `mapDomain π` where `π` is
  injective on `s`, are equal.
- `existsUnique_hopfPoint_extension`: the extension lemma. Assume
  `IsBoundedPresentation gen N` with `1 ≤ N`, `π` injective on
  `boundedSums s N`, and `g : A →ₐ[R] C[M']` a Hopf point with every
  `g (gen i)` supported in `π '' s`. Then there is a unique `f` such that `f`
  is a Hopf point, every `f (gen i)` is supported in `s`, and
  `mapDomain π ∘ f = g`.

Proof of the extension lemma. Lift each `g (gen i)` to `y i` supported in `s`.
Let `F = aeval y` on the polynomial ring. A relation `p` of degree at most `N`
has `F p` supported in `boundedSums s N` and `mapDomain π (F p) = g (aeval gen
p) = 0`, so `F p = 0`. Hence `F` factors through `A`
(`Ideal.quotientKerAlgEquivOfSurjective`, `Ideal.Quotient.liftₐ`), giving `f`.

The counit condition follows from that of `g` and naturality. For the
comultiplication, compare `pairMap f (Δ (gen i))` and `diagMap (f (gen i))`.
By the presentation bound, both are supported in
`boundedSums s N ×ˢ boundedSums s N`. Their images under
`mapDomain (π × π)` agree by the Hopf condition for `g`, and `π × π` is
injective there. Two algebra maps agreeing on generators are equal
(`AlgHom.ext_of_adjoin_eq_top`). Uniqueness is `eq_of_mapDomain_eq`.
