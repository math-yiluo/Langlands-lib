# Complete nondegenerate dual comparison interfaces

This derived internal check module imports the comparison construction supporting
Renard I.4.1. It assigns its complete unapplied additive equivalence, both original
intertwiners, inherited and canonical complex-linear equivalences, and
contravariant naturality declaration to independently specified dependent
function types. Algebra, source and target universes are distinct parameters.
The final two assignments instantiate the full canonical-linear interface in
both strict algebra/carrier universe directions. Raw and transported actions
and scalar modules are explicitly installed in the expected types.

The entire linear naturality interface is also assigned. Two further complete
original-map naturality assignments use the distinct algebra/source/target
universe triples (0,1,2) and (1,2,0).
