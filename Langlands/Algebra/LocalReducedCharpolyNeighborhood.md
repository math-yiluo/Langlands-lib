# Local splitting from nearby reduced characteristic polynomials

`Langlands.CentralSimple.ReducedCharpolySplitNeighborhood A f` says that sufficiently close monic polynomials of the same degree as `f` are separable and that any algebraic extension containing one of their roots splits `A`. The module proves this property when `f` is a separable reduced characteristic polynomial at a finite or infinite completion. This is the splitting consequence required in the coefficient approximation step of §5.4 of Martin Kneser, *Lectures on Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups*, TIFR Lectures on Mathematics 47, 1969, printed p. 69 (PDF p. 75). [TIFR publication](https://mathweb.tifr.res.in/sites/default/files/publications/ln/tifr47.pdf)

At a finite completion, the proof equips an algebraic closure with the spectral norm. The simultaneous root estimate and Krasner's lemma put an original root in the field generated by each nearby root. Thus an algebraic extension containing a nearby root also contains an original root, and the simple-root splitting theorem applies.

At an infinite completion, use its isometric embedding in the complex field. A nonsplit algebra has no root of its separable reduced characteristic polynomial in the completion. The distance from each complex root to the closed image of the completion provides a positive radius for root matching. Accordingly, a nearby root also lies outside that image. An algebraic extension that contains such a root embeds into the complex field with image containing the reals and a nonreal element, so the image is the full complex field and the algebra splits there.
