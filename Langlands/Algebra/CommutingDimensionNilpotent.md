# Nilpotent commuting endomorphisms

Worker 307 implements the nilpotent part of the proof of
Bernstein–Zelevinsky (1976), Lemma 4.10, §4.12, printed pp.39–40,
registered source `bernstein-zelevinsky-1976-representations-gln`.
The general case of commuting endomorphisms is not asserted here.

For a finite-dimensional vector space over any field, the algebra generated
by `l` commuting nilpotent endomorphisms has dimension at most
`m^(2 - 2/2^l)`, where `m` is the dimension of the space and the power is real.
The zero-generator and zero-dimensional cases are included.

The proof uses induction on the number of generators, followed by strong
induction on the vector-space dimension. The generators span an ideal `J`
in the nilradical. Nakayama's lemma makes the invariant subspace `W = JV`
proper when the space is nonzero. Writing `s = m - dim W`, the earlier
restriction argument gives `dim C ≤ s*m`. Splitting off the first generator
gives `dim C ≤ dim B + dim(C|W)`, with `B` generated by the remaining
generators. The two induction hypotheses bound these last two terms.
The real numerical induction lemma then gives the stated exponent.
