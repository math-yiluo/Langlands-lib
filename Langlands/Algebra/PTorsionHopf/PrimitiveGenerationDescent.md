# Descent of primitive algebra generators

This internal algebraic composition works over an arbitrary field k and a commutative k-bialgebra A. The coefficient field, bialgebra and scalar extension have independent universes.

First, if L is any field extension and L tensor A is generated over L by its primitive elements, then A is generated over k by its primitive elements. The primitive-defect tensor comparison identifies the primitives of L tensor A with the kernel of the scalar-extended primitive defect. Faithfully flat descent of algebra generation by a linear kernel then gives the assertion over k.

A finite-type algebra generated by primitives has a finite family of primitive generators. The proof extracts a finite subset of the generating set, then lifts it into the subtype of primitive elements. No finite-dimensionality of the whole primitive subspace is asserted.

For the geometric criterion, assume that A is cocommutative, L is algebraically closed of prime characteristic p, and L tensor A is reduced and of finite type over L. Assume also that every L-algebra map from L tensor A to L has p-th convolution power equal to the counit. The existing Verschiebung theorem then annihilates the p-th convolution power of every linear functional vanishing at 1. The existing primitive-generation theorem generates L tensor A by primitives. The first descent result brings that generation back to k. If A is of finite type over k, the finite extraction gives primitive algebra generators over k itself.

These results impose no connectedness hypothesis and do not require the original field k to be perfect, algebraically closed or infinite. Reducedness after the specified scalar extension and the actual geometric-point convolution equation remain explicit hypotheses. The module supplies no conversion from group-scheme smoothness or pointwise exponent hypotheses to these bialgebra premises.

The provenance is internal composition of `KernelGenerationDescent`, `PrimitiveBaseChange`, `Verschiebung` and `PrimitiveGeneration`; it introduces no external classification input. The finite extraction follows the finite-support argument of Worker 1589's kernel-generation descent.

`finitePrimitiveGeneratorsOfGeometricPoints` chooses such a finite set together with its original-field generation proof.
