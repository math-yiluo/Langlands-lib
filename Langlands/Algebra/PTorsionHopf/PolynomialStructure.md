# Coordinate algebras of commutative groups killed by `p` are polynomial rings

Author: Worker 1519, as internal infrastructure for the proof of
`Langlands.GroupScheme.RelativeRoot.SmoothCommutativePTorsionVectorStatement`.

Provenance: internal. The final theorem is the Hopf-algebraic form of the connected,
algebraically closed case of Conrad–Gabber–Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive Groups*, 2nd ed. (2015),
Lemma B.1.10 (printed pp.565–566 / PDF pp.591–592): a smooth connected commutative group of
exponent `p` is a vector group. The implemented route (vanishing Verschiebung, generation by
primitives, additive minimal relations and Euclid reduction) differs from the source's
argument with composition series and Witt vectors; it is the author's own.

## Assembly lemmas

- `exists_finset_of_mem_adjoin`: an element of `Algebra.adjoin k S` lies in the algebra
  generated by a finite subset of `S` (induction along `Algebra.adjoin_induction`).
- `exists_isPrimitiveGenerating`: a finitely generated bialgebra generated by its primitive
  elements has a finite primitive generating tuple `y : Fin s → A`
  (`IsPrimitiveGenerating`, `EuclidReduction.md`).
- `axisPoly F i := aeval (Pi.single i X) F`, the restriction of `F` to the `i`-th coordinate
  axis, with `aeval_axisPoly`: `aeval r (axisPoly F i) = aeval (Pi.single i r) F`.
- `aeval_eq_sum_axisPoly`: if `addDefect F = 0`, then
  `aeval v F = ∑ i, aeval (v i) (axisPoly F i)` in every commutative `k`-algebra.
- `isAdditivePoly_axisPoly`: if `addDefect F = 0`, each `axisPoly F i` is additive.
- `injective_aeval_of_minimal p y hy hmin`: for a domain `A` over a perfect field of
  characteristic `p`, a primitive generating tuple of minimal length is algebraically
  independent. Otherwise take a nonzero relation `F` of minimal total degree. It is additive
  (`addDefect_eq_zero_of_minimal`, `MinimalRelation.md`). Length `0` is impossible since
  `aeval` is then the injective algebra map from `k`. For length `s + 1`, the axis
  restrictions form an additive relation, not all zero (otherwise `F = ∑ axisPoly F i (Yᵢ) = 0`),
  and the Euclid reduction produces a shorter primitive generating tuple, contradicting
  minimality.

## Main theorem

`exists_primitive_polynomial_coordinates p hA`: let `k : Type u` be an algebraically closed
field of characteristic `p` (prime), and `A : Type u` a commutative and cocommutative
`k`-bialgebra which is a domain and finitely generated as a `k`-algebra. Assume

`hA : ∀ φ : A →ₐ[k] k, toConv φ.toLinearMap ^ p = 1`

(every `k`-point is killed by `p` in the convolution dual). Then there are `s : ℕ` and
primitive elements `y : Fin s → A` such that `MvPolynomial.aeval y : k[Y₁, …, Yₛ] → A` is
bijective.

Proof as implemented. `convPow_char_eq_zero` (`Verschiebung.md`, using that a domain is
reduced) shows that functionals vanishing at `1` have vanishing `p`-th power;
`adjoin_primitive_eq_top` (`PrimitiveGeneration.md`) makes `A` generated by primitives; a
primitive generating tuple of minimal length (`Nat.find`) is algebraically independent by
`injective_aeval_of_minimal`, and it generates, so `aeval y` is surjective
(`Algebra.adjoin_range_eq_range_aeval`).

The case `s = 0` is included.
