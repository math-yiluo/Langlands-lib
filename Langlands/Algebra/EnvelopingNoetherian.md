# Left Noetherianity of enveloping algebras

The universal enveloping algebra of a finite-dimensional Lie algebra over
a field is left Noetherian. Its degree symbols from an ordered
Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt basis send filtered products to polynomial products.
The sum of the images of the filtered pieces of a left ideal gives a
homogeneous ideal in the polynomial algebra in the basis variables.
Taking homogeneous components recovers the image in each degree.

For nested left ideals, equality of the polynomial ideals gives matching
symbols in the smaller left ideal. Subtracting a match reduces the degree,
so degree induction recovers the larger ideal from the smaller one.
The Hilbert basis theorem applied to the polynomial algebra thus gives
the ascending chain condition for left ideals in the enveloping algebra.
