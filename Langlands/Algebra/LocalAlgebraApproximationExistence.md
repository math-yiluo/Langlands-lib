# Existence of local algebra approximation systems (Stacks 00QV): construction

This module builds the approximating system used to prove
`Langlands.Algebra.LocalAlgebraApproximationExistenceStatement` (Stacks, Tag 00QV,
Lemma 10.127.11). It proves the colimit clause and both essential-finite-type clauses of
`LocalAlgebraSystem.IsApproximation`. The prime-localization clause is a hypothesis here: it
is proved in `Langlands.Algebra.LocalAlgebraApproximationTransition`, and the closing theorem
is in `Langlands.Algebra.LocalAlgebraApproximationExistenceProof`. All declarations are in the
namespace `Langlands.Algebra.LocalAlgebraApproximationExistence`.

## Comparison with the source proof

Stacks writes `S` as a localization of `R[x₁, …, xₙ]/(f₁, …, fₘ)` at a prime. It then takes the
local rings `R_λ` essentially of finite type over `ℤ` that contain the coefficients of the
`fⱼ`, and the localizations `S_λ` of `R_λ[x₁, …, xₙ]/(f₁, …, fₘ)` at the inverse images of
the maximal ideal of `S`. This module follows that construction with three changes.

1. The presentation of `S` may use any submonoid (`Langlands.Algebra.EssFinitePresentation`),
   not only a prime complement. The prime used for `S_λ` is always the inverse image of the
   maximal ideal of `S`, so the construction never needs a prime-localization presentation
   of `S`.
2. The index set is `Finset R₀`, where `R₀` is identified with `R` by a ring isomorphism `e`.
   The base ring at `i` is the concrete local subring `locSub C i` of `R₀`, where `C` is the
   finite set of coefficients of the relations. Base-colimit injectivity then comes down to
   injectivity of `Subtype.val` and `e`.
3. `R₀ = ULift.{uS} R` puts the index and every component ring in `Type (max uR uS)`, as the
   statement requires. The carriers `R` and `S` of the system are left unchanged.

## Base rings

For finite sets `C, A` of a commutative ring `R`:

- `gen C A` is the subring generated by `C ∪ A`.
- `locSub C A` is the subring of all `r` with `r * s = a`, where `a, s ∈ gen C A` and `s` is a
  unit of `R`. It is an `irreducible_def` (characterized by `mem_locSub`), so the kernel never
  unfolds it when it compares the subrings for different index sets.

Facts proved:

- `locSub_mono`: `locSub C A` is monotone in `A`.
- `mem_locSub_of_mem_left` and `mem_locSub_of_mem_right`: `C ∪ A` is contained in
  `locSub C A`.
- `isLocalHom_locSub_subtype`: the inclusion into `R` is a local homomorphism. If
  `r * s = a` and `r` is a unit, then `a` is a unit, and `r⁻¹ * a = s` shows that `r⁻¹` lies
  in `locSub C A`. Hence `isLocalRing_locSub`: `locSub C A` is local when `R` is (by
  `RingHom.domain_isLocalRing`).
- `isLocalization_locSub`: `locSub C A` is the localization of `gen C A` at the elements that
  are units of `R` (`unitsSubmonoid`).
- `finiteType_gen` and `essFiniteType_locSub`: `gen C A` is of finite type over `ℤ`. The proof
  identifies it with `Algebra.adjoin ℤ (C ∪ A)`. Therefore `locSub C A` is essentially of
  finite type over `ℤ`.

## Presentations

`Pres R₀ R S e` records a finite polynomial presentation of the `R`-algebra `S` over a ring
`R₀` identified with `R` by `e : R₀ ≃+* R`. It stores the composite map
`ρ : MvPolynomial (Fin n) R₀ →+* S` together with these properties:

- `ρ (C r) = algebraMap R S (e r)`;
- `ρ` kills the finite set `F` of relations;
- `surj`: every `s` satisfies `s * ρ t = ρ a` for some `a, t` with `ρ t` a unit;
- `ker`: if `ρ a = 0`, then `t * a ∈ Ideal.span F` for some `t` with `ρ t` a unit.

`nonempty_pres` derives such a presentation from `EssFinitePresentation R S`. Given
`R[X]/I` localized at `M` and isomorphic to `S` with `I = (F₁)`, it sets
`ρ = Φ ∘ (localization map) ∘ (quotient map) ∘ (map of coefficients by e)` and
`F = (MvPolynomial.mapEquiv e).symm '' F₁`. The `surj` and `ker` fields follow from
`IsLocalization.mk'_surjective` and `IsLocalization.map_eq_zero_iff`.

## The system attached to a presentation `P`

Indices are finite subsets `i` of `R₀`, ordered by inclusion.

- `P.coeffSet` is the finite set of coefficients of the relations `F`, and
  `P.B i := locSub P.coeffSet i`.
- `P.Poly i := MvPolynomial (Fin P.n) (P.B i)`. The injective map `P.toPoly i` sends its
  coefficients into `R₀`. `exists_toPoly_eq_of_subset`: a polynomial over `R₀` whose
  coefficients lie in `i` comes from `P.Poly i`.
- `P.rel i` is the ideal of `P.Poly i` spanned by the preimages of `F`, and
  `P.A i := P.Poly i ⧸ P.rel i`. `P.A i` is a `def` with its own `CommRing`,
  `Algebra (P.B i)` and `Algebra.FiniteType` instances. Its quotient map is `P.mkA i`, and
  `P.ρA i : P.A i →+* S` is induced by `ρ`.
- `P.q i` is the inverse image in `P.A i` of the maximal ideal of `S`, a prime ideal.
  `P.Sl i := Localization.AtPrime (P.q i)`. `P.algι i : P.Sl i →+* S` is the induced map, and
  it is local (`isLocalHom_algι`).
- Transition maps for `h : i ≤ j`:
  - `P.incl h` is the subring inclusion;
  - `P.polyMap h := MvPolynomial.map (P.incl h)`;
  - `P.AMap h` is induced on quotients by `rel_le_comap`;
  - `P.SlMap h := Localization.localRingHom (P.q i) (P.q j) (P.AMap h) (P.q_eq_comap h)`,
    with key identity `SlMap_algebraMap`.

  Identity and composition laws hold at every level (`incl_self`, `incl_incl`,
  `polyMap_self`, `polyMap_polyMap`, `AMap_self`, `AMap_AMap`, `SlMap_self`, `SlMap_SlMap`).
  The laws for `polyMap` follow from injectivity of `toPoly`, and the laws for `SlMap` from
  `IsLocalization.ringHom_ext`.
- `P.baseι i` is `e` restricted to `P.B i`. It is local because `B i → R₀` is local and `e` is
  an isomorphism.
- `isLocalHom_algebraMap`: the structure map `P.B i → P.Sl i` is local. Its composite with
  `algι` equals `algebraMap R S ∘ baseι`, and both of those maps are local.
- `P.system : LocalAlgebraSystem (Finset R₀) R S` packages these data with
  `base i = P.B i`, `alg i = P.Sl i`, `baseMap = P.incl`, `algMap = P.SlMap`,
  `baseι = P.baseι` and `algι = P.algι`. The `algebraMap` of each component is the
  composite `P.B i → P.A i → P.Sl i` (`map_comm` is `SlMap_algebraMap_base` and `ι_comm` is
  `algι_algebraMap_base`).

## Colimit clause (`system_isColimit`)

- **Base injectivity.** By `Ring.DirectLimit.lift_injective`, it suffices that every
  `baseι i` is injective, and this is injectivity of `Subtype.val` and `e`.
- **Base surjectivity.** `r : R` is the image of `e.symm r` in the base ring at the index
  `{e.symm r}`.
- **Algebra surjectivity** (`exists_algι_eq`). Write `y * ρ t = ρ a` with `ρ t` a unit. Take
  the index `i = a.coeffs ∪ t.coeffs`, lift `a` and `t` to `a'` and `t'` over `P.B i`, and
  let `x = mk' (mkA a') (mkA t')`. Then `mkA t'` lies in `(P.q i).primeCompl` because its
  image `ρ t` is a unit, and `algι x = y` follows from `IsLocalization.lift_mk'_spec` by
  cancelling the unit `ρ t`.
- **Algebra injectivity** (`exists_SlMap_eq_zero`). Write `x = mk' (mkA g) s` with
  `algι x = 0`. Then `ρ (toPoly g) = 0`. By `ker`, `t * toPoly g` lies in the span of `F`
  for some `t` with `ρ t` a unit. `exists_rel_of_mem_span` descends this to an element of
  `P.rel j` for some `j`. At `k = i ∪ j ∪ t.coeffs`, injectivity of `toPoly k` gives
  `t' * polyMap g ∈ P.rel k`, where `t'` lifts `t`. Since `mkA t'` lies outside `P.q k`,
  `IsLocalization.map_eq_zero_iff` shows `SlMap x = 0`. Hence `x` is zero in the direct
  limit (`Ring.DirectLimit.of_f`).

## Approximation and assembly

- `system_isApproximation` combines `system_isColimit`, `essFiniteType_locSub` (the base
  rings are essentially of finite type over `ℤ`) and `essFiniteType_Sl` (each `P.Sl i` is
  a localization of the finite type `P.B i`-algebra `P.A i`). The prime-localization
  clause is the hypothesis `h4`.
- `isLocalRing_ulift`: `ULift R` is local when `R` is (`IsLocalRing.of_surjective'` along
  the inverse of `ULift.ringEquiv`).
- `exists_isApproximation R S hS h4` has the conclusion of the target statement. It assumes
  the prime-localization clause for the systems of all presentations
  `P : Pres (ULift.{uS} R) R S ULift.ringEquiv`. It takes a presentation from
  `nonempty_pres` and uses the index `Finset (ULift.{uS} R)`, which lives in
  `Type (max uR uS)` and is nonempty and directed under inclusion.
