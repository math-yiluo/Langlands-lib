# Positive generation of graded ideal support

This module states an internal commutative algebra prerequisite for the
augmentation-weight argument in Conrad–Gabber–Prasad, *Pseudo-reductive Groups*,
second edition (2015), §3.3, Example 3.3.2, printed pp.107–108 / PDF pp.133–134.
It is a derived generalization of that argument, rather than a transcription of
a separately numbered assertion in the book.

Let `k` be a field, `R` a commutative Noetherian domain with a `k`-algebra
structure, `Λ` an additive commutative group, and `I` a proper ideal. The family
`W : Λ → Submodule k I` must be an internal direct sum decomposition of the
ideal, and multiplication must send `W χ × W ψ` into `W (χ + ψ)`. No grading
of the whole ring is assumed. The three types have independent universes.

`support I W` consists precisely of labels with a nonzero homogeneous element
in the ideal. `cotangentLiftSupport I W` consists of labels admitting a
homogeneous element whose class under the actual map `I.toCotangent` to
`I/I²` is nonzero. It does not postulate a separate quotient grading or a
lifting theorem.

`PositiveGenerationStatement` asserts equality of the raw ideal support with
the underlying set of the additive semigroup generated by the cotangent lift
support. This closure uses positive finite sums. It inserts no empty-sum zero,
and empty cotangent lift support therefore implies empty ideal support.
An occurring zero weight is retained.

The intended proof uses nonzero products for one inclusion. For the other,
separatedness of the ideal-power filtration places each nonzero homogeneous
element in a finite nonzero graded piece. That piece is generated by products
of nonzero cotangent classes, with the labels adding under multiplication.
Mathlib's `Ideal.iInf_pow_eq_bot_of_isDomain` supplies the separatedness for
proper ideals in Noetherian domains. This module only states the result; the
filtration and generation arguments still require a proof.
