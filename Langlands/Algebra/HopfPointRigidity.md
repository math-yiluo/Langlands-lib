# Hopf points of a lattice with bounded generator supports are determined modulo `m`

Author: Worker 1421.

This module proves that a homomorphism from a split torus `D(ℤ^r)` to an
affine group whose coefficients on fixed generators lie in a bounded box is
determined, up to an automorphism of `ℤ^r`, by its restriction to the
`m`-torsion `D((ℤ/m)^r)` for `m` large. It is the uniqueness step that makes
the torus attached to a finite subgroup well defined in the charts of task
`45ac6e52-a4d8-4414-91f1-03c3be35223e`. It is an internal lemma. The
corresponding step of SGA 3 XI §4 (registered source
`sga3-exposes-xi-xii-xiv-gille-polo-2008`) uses infinitesimal and formal
arguments instead.

Here `r` is a finite index type and `ℤ^r` means `r → ℤ`. Notation is that of `Langlands.Algebra.HopfPointExtension` and
`Langlands.Algebra.LatticeConfigurationBound`. `C` is an arbitrary commutative
`R`-algebra (no nontriviality is needed in the main theorem). `α, α' : A →ₐ[R]
C[ℤ^r]` are `R`-algebra maps from an `R`-bialgebra.

## Results

- `injOn_red_box`: reduction modulo `m` is injective on `box r n` when
  `2n < m`.
- `closure_support_eq_top` (for nontrivial `C`): if the `gen i` generate `A`
  and `α(A)` generates `C[r → ℤ]` as a `C`-algebra, then the supports of the
  `α (gen i)` generate `ℤ^r` as a group. The second condition is what a closed
  immersion gives. Every element of the `C`-subalgebra generated by the
  `α (gen i)` is supported in the additive submonoid generated by their
  supports (`Algebra.adjoin_induction`), and `single χ 1` lies in it.
- `rigidityBound r n`: the maximum of the bound of `exists_bound r n` and
  `2n + 1`.
- `exists_mem_support_of_red`: a coefficient computation. If
  `red ∘ x' = γ ∘ red ∘ x` (as `mapDomain` images) and `x` is supported in the
  box, then every `χ` in the support of `x` has some `χ'` in the support of
  `x'` with `red χ' = γ (red χ)`.
- `exists_addEquiv_of_red_eq`: let `m ≥ rigidityBound r n`. Suppose `α` and
  `α'` have all generator values supported in `box r n`, and the generator
  supports of each generate `ℤ^r`. Suppose also
  `mapDomain red ∘ α' = mapDomain γ ∘ mapDomain red ∘ α` for an automorphism
  `γ` of `(ℤ/m)^r`. Then `mapDomain T ∘ α = α'` for some automorphism `T` of
  `ℤ^r`.

Proof of the last result. Let `S` and `S'` be the sets of generator support
points of `α` and `α'`. Let `φ(χ)` be the unique box point over `γ (red χ)`.
By `exists_mem_support_of_red` it maps `S` into `S'`, and symmetrically `φ'`
(built from `γ⁻¹`) maps `S'` into `S`. The two are mutually inverse because
`red` is injective on the box. `exists_bound` gives homomorphisms `T₀`, `T₀'`
extending `φ`, `φ'`. They are mutually inverse on generating sets, hence
inverse automorphisms (`AddMonoidHom.eq_of_eqOn_dense`). Finally
`red ∘ T = γ ∘ red` on the generating set `S`, hence everywhere. So
`mapDomain T ∘ α` and `α'` have the same reduction and box-supported generator
values. The uniqueness lemma `HopfPointExtension.eq_of_mapDomain_eq` makes them
equal.
