# Lifting an invariant Lagrangian projection in the endomorphism algebra

Starting from a projection onto a stable Lagrangian, apply regular-trace
averaging. The averaged map has the same image and is the identity on
that image, hence is again a projection. Its adjoint has that image as
kernel. Averaging the projection with the complement of its adjoint
gives a projection with the same image whose adjoint is its complement.

The commutants of the original action and its reduction are star
subalgebras because the action intertwines the involutions. Regular-trace
averaging makes the map on commutants surjective. The square-zero
condition survives in the commutant, where
`exists_lift_idempotent_star_eq_one_sub` constructs the lifted projection.

For an adjoint involution on a symplectic module, the image of this
projection is a direct summand stable under the action. If \(v=px\) and
\(w=py\), their pairing is the pairing of \(x\) with
\(p^*py=(1-p)py=0\). This gives the isotropic summand once the
adjoint-compatible endomorphism algebras, action and lower projection
are supplied.
