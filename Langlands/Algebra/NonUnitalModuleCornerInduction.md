# The actual additive corner-induction tensor and functors

This file constructs the arbitrary-ring additive foundation of the functor
displayed in Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*, French
version dated 9 July 2026, I.3.2, printed p.20 / PDF p.26:
`Z ↦ A ⊗_(eAe) Z`. Its scalar ring is any associative `NonUnitalRing A`;
`e : Idempotent A` is arbitrary; and the input is an ordinary unital module
over the actual published `Idempotent.Corner e`. The ring and module carrier
universes are independent. The tensor carrier has universe `max uA uZ`.

## Retained original actions and actual quotient

All declarations are in
`Langlands.Algebra.NonUnitalModuleCornerInduction`.

`rightCornerModule e` is the named original nonunital left action of
`(Idempotent.Corner e)ᵐᵒᵖ` on the original additive group of `A`:
`op c • a = a * Idempotent.Corner.toAmbient e c`. The action laws use
distributivity, the inclusion homomorphism, and associativity.
`rightCornerModule_smul` gives the exact scalar operation;
`toEndHom_rightCornerModule` gives its whole additive endomorphism.
`regularCornerCommute` uses associativity to prove commutation with the
published named left regular action `NonUnitalModule.regular A`.
There is no right unit-law premise or conclusion: multiplication by the
corner unit is `a * e`, which need not equal `a`.

`cornerModule e Z` is precisely
`NonUnitalModule.ofModule (Idempotent.Corner e) Z`, retaining the input module's
original action and additive group. `Tensor e Z` abbreviates the published
`NonUnitalModuleTensor.Tensor (Idempotent.Corner e) A Z` with exactly these
two chosen actions. Thus it is the actual quotient of `A ⊗[ℤ] Z` by the
submodule generated by
`(a * toAmbient e c) ⊗ z - a ⊗ (c • z)`.
`tensorAddCommGroup` is the quotient's inherited group.
`tmul` is its actual pure tensor, and `tmul_balanced` proves that relation.

`tensorModule e Z` is the published
`NonUnitalBimoduleTensor.leftModule (regularCornerCommute e) Z`.
`tensorModule_def` exposes this entire structure. Its full scalar
endomorphism, exposed by `toEndHom_tensorModule`, is the actual tensor map
of left multiplication on `A` and the identity on `Z`.
`tensorModule_smul_tmul` gives
`a₀ • (a ⊗ z) = (a₀ * a) ⊗ z`.
The named structures are used as local instances only during elaboration;
the public construction takes no ambient nonunital module instance that
could replace any of these original actions.

## Whole maps and ambient additive functor

`originalMap e f` retains the full underlying additive map of the ordinary
corner-linear map `f`, under the explicit `cornerModule` adapters.
`inducedMap e f` is the original `A`-linear map constructed with the
published `leftMap`, the identity bimodule intertwiner on `A`, and
`originalMap e f`. Its complete underlying additive map is the published
tensor map `id ⊗ f`, as `inducedMap_toAddMonoidHom` states;
`inducedMap_tmul` gives `a ⊗ z ↦ a ⊗ f z`.

`induced_hom_ext` reduces equality of the whole intertwiners to the
published additive tensor extensionality theorem. It proves
`inducedMap_id`, `inducedMap_comp`, `inducedMap_zero` and `inducedMap_add`.
The composition law permits independent carrier universes for the input,
intermediate and final modules. `inducedMapAddHom` packages the actual
additive map between the pointwise Hom groups.

`ambientObject` bundles exactly `Tensor`, `tensorAddCommGroup` and
`tensorModule`; its carrier and both structure equalities are explicit.
`ambientFunctor` has type
`ModuleCat.{uZ} (Idempotent.Corner e) ⥤
NonUnitalModuleCat.{uA, max uA uZ} A`.
Its map is the whole `inducedMap e f.hom`; the full additive-map equality is
also exposed. Its additive instance and zero/addition laws use the inherited
published Hom structures.

## Intrinsic nondegeneracy and inclusion

For `hA : IsIdempotented A`, `isNondegenerate` uses a representative of
each element in the original integer tensor product. Integer-tensor induction
reduces to pure tensors and addition; zero is included as a pure tensor.
For `a ⊗ z`, a local unit for `a` fixes the pure tensor by the
proved scalar formula. For a sum, a common upper bound for the two
idempotent witnesses fixes both summands, hence their sum. This uses the
actual quotient projection and its surjectivity and assumes no general
tensor nondegeneracy theorem or subsequent corner result.

`nondegenerateObject` and the additive `nondegenerateFunctor` use exactly
the ambient objects and maps with this property attached.
`nondegenerateFunctor_comp_inclusion` proves equality of the whole composite
functor with `ambientFunctor`, without a carrier transport. Explicit object,
original-action and complete-map formulas also expose this agreement;
`inclusion_obj_isModule` and `inclusion_map` specialize it to the inclusion.

## Scope and successors

No nonzero hypothesis is imposed on `A`, `e`, or `Z`. The same construction
covers a zero idempotent, a subsingleton ring, and a subsingleton module.

Comparison of the two complex scalar structures mentioned in the original
I.3.2 passage remains an explicit successor obligation of the whole-book
parent. Additivity here does not supply that comparison or the full scalar
linearity of Renard's displayed complex functor. The fixed-part isomorphism
(I.3.2.1), its naturality, generatedness and membership in `M(A,e)`,
quotient-idempotence and its fixed-part comparison, simple-class correspondence,
and equivalence remain separate statement/proof successors. None is assumed
or claimed by this construction.
