# Finiteness of polynomial rings from leading forms

Module `Langlands.Algebra.MvPolynomialLeadingFinite`, namespace
`Langlands.Algebra.MvPolynomialLeadingFinite`.

## Degree bounds

`DegLE m p` (resp. `DegLT m p`) says that every monomial of `p` has total degree at most
(resp. less than) `m`. The lemmas `degLT_add`, `degLT_sub`, `degLT_smul`, `degLT_sum`,
`degLE_mul`, `degLT_mul_left`, `degLT_mul_right` and `degLE_of_isHomogeneous` give the closure
properties, from `MvPolynomial.support_mul` and the additivity of the degree.

## Leading forms

Let `f t : J → MvPolynomial σ k` and `n : J → ℕ`, with each `t j` homogeneous of degree `n j`
and `DegLT (n j) (f j - t j)`: `t j` is the leading form of `f j`.

- `degLE_f`, `degLE_pow_f`, `degLE_prod_f`, `degLT_pow_sub`, `degLT_prod_sub`: products of powers
  of the `f j` differ from those of the `t j` only in lower degrees.
- `isHomogeneous_aeval_monomial`, `degLT_aeval_sub`: for `Q` weighted homogeneous of weight `m`
  (variable `j` of weight `n j`), `aeval t Q` is homogeneous of degree `m` and
  `aeval f Q - aeval t Q` has degree less than `m`.
- `homogeneousComponent_aeval`: taking the degree-`m` component commutes with substituting the
  leading forms: `homogeneousComponent m (aeval t Q) = aeval t (weightedHomogeneousComponent n m Q)`.
- `homogeneousComponent_mul_monomial`: the degree-`m` component of `x * X^β`.

## Main results

- `moduleFinite_of_leading`. If `MvPolynomial σ k` is a finite module over the subalgebra
  generated by the leading forms `t j`, it is a finite module over every subalgebra `A`
  containing all `f j`.

  Proof. Choose finitely many generators over the subalgebra generated by the `t j` and let `S`
  be the finite set of monomials occurring in them; they also generate. By strong induction on
  the degree, every monomial `X^γ` of degree `N` lies in the `A`-span of `{X^β : β ∈ S}`.
  Write `X^γ = ∑ aeval t (Q β) * X^β` and take degree-`N` components: `Q β` may be replaced by
  its weighted component of weight `N - |β|`. Replacing `t` by `f` changes the right side only
  in degrees below `N` (`degLT_aeval_sub`); those terms lie in the span by induction, and the
  new main term lies in it because `aeval f (Q β) ∈ A`.

- `aeval_esymm_mem`, `isIntegral_of_powerSums`. Let `k` be a field of characteristic zero,
  `S` a commutative `k`-algebra (nontrivial, for `isIntegral_of_powerSums`), `ℓ : Q → S` a
  finite family, and `A` a `k`-subalgebra of `S` containing the power sums `∑ q, ℓ q ^ m` for
  `1 ≤ m ≤ card Q`. Then `A` contains the elementary symmetric functions of the `ℓ q`
  (Mathlib's Newton identities `MvPolynomial.mul_esymm_eq_sum`; the recurrence for the `j`-th
  elementary symmetric function is divided by the positive integer `j`, which is invertible in
  `k`), and each `ℓ q` is integral over `A`: it is a root of the monic polynomial `∏ (X - ℓ r)`,
  whose coefficients are these symmetric functions up to sign (Mathlib's
  `Multiset.prod_X_sub_C_coeff`). The field hypothesis matters: over `ℤ`, the subring
  `ℤ[x + y, x² + y²]` of `ℤ[x, y]` contains both power sums of `x, y`, but `x` is not integral
  over it.

## Provenance

Internal. `moduleFinite_of_leading` is the filtered form of the graded statement that a module
whose associated graded module is finite is finite; `isIntegral_of_powerSums` is the classical
integrality of the roots of a polynomial over the ring generated by the power sums of its roots.
