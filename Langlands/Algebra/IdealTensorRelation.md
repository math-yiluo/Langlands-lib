# Tensor relations for a finitely generated ideal

`Langlands.Algebra.IdealTensorRelation` is a helper module for the proof of
`Langlands.Algebra.TensorKernelTransitionReductionStatement` (the reduction
step in the proof of Stacks Project Lemma 10.99.14, tag **00MO**). It proves
elementary facts about an ideal \(I\) of a commutative ring \(B\) that is
generated by finitely many elements \(g_1,\dots,g_r\) (Lean: `g : Fin r → I`
with `hg : Ideal.span (Set.range fun k ↦ (g k : B)) = I`). It also shows that
two of these properties pass from a module to its localization. The results
are internal to this project. They are standard and are not taken from a
specific source statement.

## Generator form of ideal tensors

- `exists_sum_smul_of_mem_smul_top`: every element of \(I X\) has the form
  \(\sum_k g_k x_k\). Proof: induction on `I • ⊤`, using
  `Ideal.mem_span_range_iff_exists_fun` for the coefficients.
- `exists_eq_sum_tmul`: every element of \(I\otimes_B X\) has the form
  \(\sum_k g_k\otimes x_k\). Proof by tensor induction in the same way.
- `rTensor_injective_of_relations`: if every relation
  \(\sum_k g_k x_k=0\) in \(X\) has \(\sum_k g_k\otimes x_k=0\) in
  \(I\otimes_B X\), then \(I\otimes_B X\to B\otimes_B X\) is injective.
  Write a kernel element as \(\sum_k g_k\otimes x_k\) and apply
  `TensorProduct.lid` to see that it comes from a relation.
- `sum_tmul_eq_zero_of_isTrivialRelation`: a trivial relation in the sense
  of Mathlib's equational criterion (`Module.IsTrivialRelation`) has
  vanishing generator tensor. This is
  `TensorProduct.sum_tmul_eq_zero_of_vanishesTrivially`.

## Pure tensors in a quotient base change

Using `TensorProduct.quotTensorEquivQuotSMul`, which identifies
\((B/I)\otimes_B X\) with \(X/IX\):

- `exists_eq_one_tmul`: every element of \((B/I)\otimes_B X\) is
  \(1\otimes x\);
- `one_tmul_eq_zero_iff`: \(1\otimes x=0\) exactly when \(x\in IX\);
- `smul_one_tmul`: \(\bar b\cdot(1\otimes x)=1\otimes bx\).

## Localization

Let \(T\) be a \(B\)-algebra, \(W\subseteq T\) a submonoid, and
\(\psi\colon P\to N\) a \(T\)-linear map that is a localization of modules at
\(W\) (`IsLocalizedModule W ψ`). Both \(P\) and \(N\) carry compatible
\(B\)-module structures.

- `unitSMulEquiv`: multiplication by \(c\in W\) on \(N\), as a \(B\)-linear
  automorphism. It is bijective by `IsLocalizedModule.map_units`.
- `relations_of_isLocalizedModule`: assume every relation
  \(\sum_k g_k p_k=0\) in \(P\) has \(\sum_k g_k\otimes\psi(p_k)=0\) in
  \(I\otimes_B N\). Then every relation \(\sum_k g_k x_k=0\) in \(N\) has
  \(\sum_k g_k\otimes x_k=0\). Proof: clear denominators,
  \(s x_k=\psi(p_k)\) (`IsLocalizedModule.exist_integer_multiples_of_finite`).
  Then \(\psi(\sum_k g_k p_k)=0\), so \(u\sum_k g_k p_k=0\) for some
  \(u\in W\) (`IsLocalizedModule.eq_zero_iff`). The hypothesis applied to
  \(u p_k\) gives that \(us\) times \(\sum_k g_k\otimes x_k\) vanishes.
  Multiplication by \(us\) is an automorphism, so the tensor vanishes.
- `flat_of_isLocalizedModule`: if \((B/I)\otimes_B P\) is flat over \(B/I\),
  so is \((B/I)\otimes_B N\). Proof by the equational criterion
  (`Module.Flat.of_forall_isTrivialRelation`). Take a relation
  \(\sum_k f_k y_k=0\) with \(y_k=1\otimes n_k\) and lifts \(c_k\in B\) of
  the \(f_k\). Then \(\sum_k c_k n_k=\sum_l g_l m_l\) for some \(m_l\).
  Clear denominators of all \(n_k,m_l\) at once, and use `eq_zero_iff` to
  get a relation \(\sum_k f_k(1\otimes u p_k)=0\) in \((B/I)\otimes_B P\).
  Flatness of that module makes it trivial
  (`Module.Flat.isTrivialRelation_of_sum_smul_eq_zero`). The base change of
  \((us)^{-1}\circ\psi\) maps \(1\otimes u p_k\) to \(y_k\), and it maps the
  trivializing data to trivializing data in \((B/I)\otimes_B N\).
