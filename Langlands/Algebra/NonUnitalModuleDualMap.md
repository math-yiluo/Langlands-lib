Import `Langlands.Algebra.NonUnitalModuleDualMap`; its prerequisite `NonUnitalModuleDual` supplies the actual full dual, full right action, actual nondegenerate subtype and scalar comparison. Inputs retain their specified additive groups and original nonunital actions. The algebra, source, target and composition carrier universes `uA`, `uM`, `uN`, `uP` are independent. There is no supplied complex module on an input carrier: each uses precisely `NondegenerateScalars.complexModule hA hM` (respectively `hN`, `hP`). There is no assumed unit law, finite dimension, admissibility, faithfulness or nontriviality hypothesis.

`fullDual_ext` gives extensionality for the full dual after explicitly installing its chosen canonical source scalars. `dual_ext` combines it with actual subtype extensionality. These are internal proof interfaces.

For an original `f : NonUnitalModuleHom A M N`, `fullPullbackLinear hA hM hN f` is definitionally `(NondegenerateScalars.complexToLinearMap hA hM hN f).dualMap`. The published conversion proves automatic complex linearity of the original additive intertwiner. The dual map is Mathlib's actual transpose of that linear map. `fullPullbackLinear_eq_dualMap` records the exact equality, `fullPullbackLinear_coe` records the entire underlying map as composition with that precise linear map, and `fullPullbackLinear_apply` gives `φ (f m)`.

`fullPullback` bundles this same map as an original right `A`-intertwiner, encoded over `Aᵐᵒᵖ`. Its intertwining proof evaluates both sides at `m` and applies `f.map_smul`. `fullPullback_toAddMonoidHom` and `fullPullback_coe` preserve its entire additive map and function; `fullPullback_apply_eq_comp` gives equality as a functional with composition by the automatically linear `f`; `fullPullback_apply` is the evaluation formula. `fullPullback_map_smul` states the opposite-ring intertwining law.

`pullback` is exactly `fullPullback.restrictNondegeneratePart hA.op`, using the published restriction of original intertwiners. Thus the source and target are the actual nondegenerate subtypes. `pullback_coe` gives the entire subtype function, with membership provided by the actual preservation theorem. `pullback_val` preserves its entire full-dual value; `pullback_val_eq_comp` gives that value as composition of the included functional by the automatically linear map. `pullback_apply` evaluates it as `φ.val (f m)`. `inclusion_pullback` is equality of the bundled composites with the actual inclusions: including a restricted pullback equals full pullback of the included functional.

`fullPullback_id` and `pullback_id` prove the identity laws. `fullPullback_comp` and `pullback_comp` prove reversed composition: pullback of `g.comp f` is pullback of `f` composed with pullback of `g`. Their proofs use original-Hom extensionality, then functional or subtype extensionality, and reduce to evaluation. `fullPullback_zero`, `fullPullback_add`, `pullback_zero` and `pullback_add` use the published pointwise zero and addition on original `NonUnitalModuleHom`. After extensionality, they are exactly preservation of zero/addition by the functional. Each proof explicitly installs the canonical source scalar module required to apply its linear-map laws.

The chosen full-dual actions are local instances supplied by `fullAction hA hM`, `fullAction hA hN` and `fullAction hA hP`, and are retained in the types of the bundled maps. No new global action or scalar module instance is introduced. The final full unapplied interface examples check that the linear and restricted pullbacks require precisely the stated original module data with independent universes.
