## Induced generatedness with the named original action

`CornerInductionGeneratedTheorem.{uA,uZ}` quantifies independently over
`A : Type uA`, its nonunital ring structure, an actual `e : Idempotent A`,
and an ordinary **unital** module `Z : Type uZ` over
`Idempotent.Corner e`. It uses exactly the published
`NonUnitalModuleCornerInduction.Tensor e Z`, with carrier universe
`max uA uZ`. Local let-instances select exactly `tensorAddCommGroup e Z`
and `tensorModule e Z`; no additional action on that quotient is an input.
It concludes all three assertions:

1. For every `a` and `z`, `tmul e a z` equals
   `(tensorModule e Z).toDistribSMul.toSMul.smul a (tmul e (e : A) z)`.
2. Every `tmul e (e : A) z` belongs to the actual published
   `NonUnitalModuleCorner.fixedPart e (Tensor e Z)` for this residual action.
3. `NonUnitalModuleIdempotentGenerated.IsGeneratedByIdempotent e (Tensor e Z)`
   holds for precisely those named structures.

Here `Tensor` is the actual quotient of the integer tensor product
`A ⊗[ℤ] Z` by the additive balancing relations
`(a * toAmbient e c) ⊗ z = a ⊗ (c • z)`.
The right corner action on `A` is multiplication, and the residual original
left action satisfies `a₀ • (a ⊗ z) = (a₀ * a) ⊗ z`.
The actual generated subgroup `aeM e M` is the additive subgroup closure of
the values `a • ((e : A) • m)`, equipped with its proved original-action
stability. Generatedness means that every vector belongs to that closure;
it does not replace the original action by a unital action.

The intended future proof uses the balanced identity
`a ⊗ z = (a * e) ⊗ z = a • (e ⊗ z)`, the idempotence of `e`,
and additive generation of the actual tensor quotient. The first equality
uses the **corner unit acting on `Z`**. It does not assert `a * e = a`
in the ambient ring. This route is an explanation of the target, not a
proof supplied by this unit or a premise of the target.

## Descent along the actual restricted map

`IdempotentGeneratedSurjectiveDescentTheorem.{uA,uM,uN}` quantifies over
independently sized `A`, `M`, and `N`, their specified additive groups and
original `NonUnitalModule` actions, and an actual
`f : NonUnitalModuleHom A M N`. Its sole map hypothesis is surjectivity of
the whole underlying `f.toAddMonoidHom`. Its two conclusions are:

1. The whole underlying additive map of the actual published
   `NonUnitalModuleIdempotentGenerated.restrict e f` is surjective from
   `aeM e M` to `aeM e N`.
2. Generatedness of `M` implies generatedness of `N`.

The restriction is explicitly typed using the named inherited
`aeMAddCommGroup` and `aeMModule` structures on both actual subtypes.
It is the published map `x ↦ ⟨f x.val, map_mem e f x.property⟩`,
with its full original intertwining structure. The target chooses no new
map or surjectivity certificate. The proposed later proof is induction
on the target additive closure, lifting generators through surjectivity,
and then applying the same descent to every target vector.
No proof is provided here.

The assertion covers canonical quotients but does not claim closure of
generatedness under arbitrary submodules. Renard treats that closure as an
additional condition in the equivalence criterion on printed p.22 / PDF p.28.

## Factorization of the entire actual additive functor

`CornerInductionGeneratedFactorizationTheorem.{uA,uZ}` concludes, for each
`hA : IsIdempotented A` and `e : Idempotent A`, existence of

```text
G : ModuleCat.{uZ} (Idempotent.Corner e) ⥤
    NonUnitalModuleIdempotentGenerated.GeneratedModuleCat.{uA,max uA uZ} e.
```

It concludes `G.Additive` together with the whole-functor equalities

```text
G ⋙ NonUnitalModuleIdempotentGenerated.inclusion e
  = NonUnitalModuleCornerInduction.nondegenerateFunctor hA e

(G ⋙ NonUnitalModuleIdempotentGenerated.inclusion e) ⋙ nondegenerateInclusion A
  = NonUnitalModuleCornerInduction.ambientFunctor e.
```

The first inclusion forgets the generatedness property from the actual
full subcategory inside `NondegenerateModuleCat`. The actual
`Langlands.Algebra.nondegenerateInclusion` then forgets its nondegeneracy
property. The first equality retains the entire published nondegenerate
induction functor. The second retains its entire ambient functor:
at every object `Z`, it is exactly the bundled
`Tensor e Z`, `tensorAddCommGroup e Z`, and `tensorModule e Z`;
at every morphism `f`, it is exactly the complete original intertwiner
`NonUnitalModuleCornerInduction.inducedMap e f.hom`.
These are equalities of functors, so they specify the whole object structures
and maps, beyond just a formula on pure tensors.

The functor, additivity, and its generatedness and nondegeneracy
certificates are conclusions. None is a quantified input.
The published intrinsic local-unit nondegeneracy result provides the
nondegenerate adapter; local units do not enter the first two targets.
The carrier universes already agree, and no `ULift` is used.

## Aggregate, universes, and scope

`CornerInductionGeneratedStatements.{uA,uZ,uM,uN}` is the closed conjunction,
in order, of the three targets above. All ring, corner-module and original
source and target module carrier universes remain independent. There is no
carrier-size inequality. Every assertion uses the specified original action
or the explicitly named tensor action, including when competing actions exist.

No target requires a nonzero idempotent or nontrivial ring or module.
An ordinary unital module over the zero corner is necessarily subsingleton.
The descent target permits nonzero carriers with zero original action:
its restricted map is then a map between the actual zero generated subgroups,
and its generatedness implication retains its stated source hypothesis.
