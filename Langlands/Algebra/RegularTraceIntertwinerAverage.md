# Averaging to an intertwiner using the regular trace

Use the published regular trace pairing on a finite-dimensional simple
algebra over a characteristic-zero field. The products of the basis
vectors with their trace-dual vectors sum to one. Pairing the sum with
each algebra element computes the ordinary trace of left multiplication.
Nondegeneracy then identifies the sum with one.

Insert an arbitrary target element between the images of the basis and
the dual basis under the two representations. The coordinate formula
for the trace-dual basis and cyclicity of the trace give the intertwining
equation. If the element already intertwines, the unit sum gives it back.
This extends the regular-trace argument for residue representations in
`HarrisTaylorCMArtinianMatrixDeterminant` to an arbitrary element of the
target algebra.

The averaging construction commutes with the quotient map. Consequently
an arbitrary preimage of an element in the lower commutant can be averaged
to a preimage in the upper commutant.
