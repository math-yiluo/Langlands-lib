# Finite generation from a Reynolds projection

`Langlands.Algebra.SubalgebraReynoldsProjection S` packages a coefficient-linear
map into the ambient algebra, its membership in `S`, its pointwise restriction
to the identity on `S`, and its compatibility with left multiplication by
elements of `S`. The structure assumes no group action or grading.

`Langlands.Algebra.subalgebra_fg_of_graded_reynolds` is an internal helper
for the group-scheme Hilbert finite-generation proof. Its coefficient ring
`k` is commutative, and `A` is a commutative Noetherian `k`-algebra with an
internal nonnegative grading `𝒜 : ℕ → Submodule k A`. Every element of
`𝒜 0` must be a scalar.

Let `S` be a `k`-subalgebra of `A` closed under the homogeneous projections
`GradedRing.proj 𝒜 d`. The theorem assumes a `k`-linear map `P : A →ₗ[k] A`
whose values lie in `S`, which fixes `S` pointwise, and which satisfies
`P (s * a) = s * P a` whenever `s ∈ S`. Then `S.FG`: a finite set
generates `S` as a `k`-algebra. No degree preservation of `P` is assumed.
`SubalgebraReynoldsProjection.fg` gives the same conclusion using the packaged
projection.

The proof chooses a finite subset `T` of the positive-degree homogeneous
elements of `S` generating their ideal in `A`. Such a finite subset exists
by Noetherianity. Strong induction shows that every homogeneous element
`a ∈ S` of degree `d` lies in the algebra generated by `T`. For `d = 0`
it is a scalar. Otherwise write `a = ∑ fₜ t`, apply `P`, and then take
degree-`d` components. The coefficient of a generator of positive degree
`e` becomes the degree-`d-e` component of `P(fₜ)`, or zero if `e > d`.
That component belongs to `S` by the hypotheses and has degree strictly
less than `d`. The homogeneous decomposition proves the result for all
elements of `S`.

Provenance is derived internal content. This adapts the published Library
proof `Langlands.Algebra.CanonicalReynolds.invariantSubalgebra_fg`
(`Langlands/Algebra/CanonicalReynolds.lean`) to a supplied Reynolds map
and subalgebra. The classical argument is the algebraic part of Milne,
*Algebraic Groups*, corrected reprint (2022), Theorem 12.57 and Aside 12.58,
printed pp. 249–250, registered as `milne-2022-algebraic-groups`. Those
pages and the registered 2026-11-04 official errata snapshot have been read.
The group-scheme construction of `S`, `P`, and their required properties
remains a separate obligation.
