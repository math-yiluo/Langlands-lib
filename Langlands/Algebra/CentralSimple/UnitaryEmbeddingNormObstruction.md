# Determinant norms of unitary twists

`Langlands.CentralSimple.unitaryEmbeddingNormObstruction` proves `UnitaryEmbeddingNormObstructionStatement` in every universe. It takes a characteristic-zero base field \(F\), a separable quadratic extension \(E/F\), and a central simple \(E\)-algebra with an \(F\)-linear involution that restricts to conjugation on \(E\). It also takes an embedding of a field \(P\) of maximal degree, a compositum chart \(E\otimes_F K\simeq P\) for a finite field extension \(K/F\), the compatible inclusion of \(K\) in \(P\), and a symmetric unit \(g\). There is a single \(d\in F^\times\) lifting the reduced norm of \(g\). Over every commutative coefficient algebra \(R\), an equation \(g\varepsilon(a)=h\tau(h)\) with \(a\in (R\otimes_F K)^\times\) and \(h\in (R\otimes_F B)^\times\) then gives a unit of \(E\otimes_F R\) whose determinant norm is \(d\,N_{R\otimes_F K/R}(a)\).

The scalar-extended restriction formula computes the reduced norm on the embedded fixed-field tensor algebra using the ordinary determinant norm over the coefficient ring. The proof first restricts the reduced norm to the maximal subfield after base change. It identifies that tensor algebra with a scalar extension of the fixed-field tensor algebra by the compositum chart \(E\otimes_F K\simeq P\). The determinant norm commutes with this scalar extension.

For a symmetric unit of the central simple algebra, compatibility of reduced norm with the involution makes its reduced norm invariant under center conjugation. Half the trace gives a preimage in the base field. The reduced norm of a unit is a unit, which makes that preimage a field unit. In the product \(h\tau(h)\), the compatibility formula gives the product of the relative reduced norm of \(h\) with its conjugate. The determinant norm of the quadratic tensor algebra has this product as its image in the tensor algebra.

These identities are the norm calculation in Prasad and Rapinchuk, *Local-global principles for embedding of fields with involution into simple algebras with involution*, arXiv:0806.0596v5, equation (10), p. 14.
