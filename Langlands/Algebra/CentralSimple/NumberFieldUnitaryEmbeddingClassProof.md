# Global fixed-field twists with everywhere local unitary solutions

`Langlands.CentralSimple.numberFieldUnitaryEmbeddingClass` proves `NumberFieldUnitaryEmbeddingClassStatement` in every universe. Given a symmetric unit \(g\) twisting the embedding of \(P\) into \(B\), it constructs a single nonzero element of \(P^\sigma\) for which the unitary twist equation holds over every completion of the base number field \(F\).

The fixed field \(K=P^\sigma\) has index two in \(P\), and multiplication identifies \(E\otimes_F K\) with \(P\). Conjugation in \(P/K\) is \(\sigma\), and its restriction to \(E\) is the nonidentity automorphism of \(E/F\). Hence restriction between the quadratic Galois groups is injective. At each place the proof chooses a local solution with parameter one when one exists. Such a choice is possible at almost every finite place by `numberFieldUnitarySymmetricCofinite`, which represents \(g\) by a quadratic norm form in the commutative algebra generated by \(g\). A local fixed unit descends to a unit of \(F_v\otimes_F K\).

The relative reduced norm of the twist equation gives \(d N(a_v)\) as a norm from \(F_v\otimes_F E\), with the same principal scalar \(d=\operatorname{Nrd}(g)\) at every place. The prescribed tensor norm class theorem uses the Artin norm–restriction square and the Artin kernel to obtain a unit \(a\) of \(K\) in all the chosen local classes. The quadratic determinant norm becomes \(p\sigma_v(p)\) in \(F_v\otimes_F P\) under the tensor tower equivalence. At each place, replacing \(a_v\) by \(a_vp\sigma_v(p)\) replaces the witness \(h_v\) by \(\tau_v(\varepsilon_v(p))h_v\). This yields the required local equations for \(a\).

The twist identities, reduced norm condition, and Artin square follow the constructions of Prasad and Rapinchuk, *Local-global principles for embedding of fields with involution into simple algebras with involution*, arXiv:0806.0596v5, Theorem 3.2(iii), p. 11, and Theorem 4.1 proof, pp. 13–15, particularly equations (9)–(10) and the proof of Proposition 4.2. The proof here applies reciprocity directly to the local twist classes.
