# Square roots near one in finite-dimensional algebras over complete fields

Module: `Langlands.Algebra.CentralSimple.LandherrSquareRoot`.

Let `k` be a complete nontrivially normed field with `2 ≠ 0`.

- `exists_mul_self_eq_of_norm_sub_one_lt`: in a complete finite-dimensional normed
  `k`-algebra `A` there is `δ > 0` such that every `b` with `‖b - 1‖ < δ` is `s * s` for some
  `s` in the subalgebra `Algebra.adjoin k {b}`.
- `coordinateLeftMul b` is the left regular representation of a `k`-algebra `A` with basis
  `b`, as an algebra map into the continuous linear endomorphisms of the coordinate space
  `ι → k`; `coordinateLeftMul_injective` records that it is injective.
- `exists_isOpen_mul_self_eq`: for a finite-dimensional `k`-algebra `A` (no norm on `A` is
  assumed) with a basis `b`, there is an open set `U` of coordinate vectors containing the
  coordinates `b.equivFun 1` of `1` such that every `a` whose coordinates lie in `U` is the
  square of an element of `Algebra.adjoin k {a}`.

## Proofs

For `exists_mul_self_eq_of_norm_sub_one_lt`, let `c = ‖2⁻¹‖`, `ρ = 1/(4c)` and
`δ = ρ/(2c)`. The Newton-type map `φ(s) = s + 2⁻¹ (b - s²)` sends the closed set
`T = {s ∈ k[b] : ‖s - 1‖ ≤ ρ}` into itself, using
`φ(s) - 1 = 2⁻¹ ((b - 1) - (s - 1)²)`, and is a contraction with constant `1/2` there, since
elements of `k[b]` commute and `φ(s) - φ(s') = (s - s') · 2⁻¹ ((1 - s) + (1 - s'))`. The set
`T` is complete (the subalgebra is a finite-dimensional, hence closed, subspace), so the
Banach fixed point theorem (`ContractingWith.exists_fixedPoint'`) gives `s ∈ T` with
`φ(s) = s`, that is `s² = b`.

For `exists_isOpen_mul_self_eq`, apply the first result to the matrix algebra of continuous
endomorphisms of `ι → k` with its operator norm. The map sending coordinates to
`coordinateLeftMul b` of the corresponding element is linear on a finite-dimensional space,
hence continuous, and `U` is the preimage of the ball of radius `δ` around `1`. A square root
of the image of `a` lying in the subalgebra generated by that image is the image of an
element of `Algebra.adjoin k {a}` (`AlgHom.map_adjoin_singleton`), and injectivity of
`coordinateLeftMul b` transports the equation back to `A`.

## Provenance

The contraction argument gives a square root of an element close to `1` within the
subalgebra generated by that element. This is the local square-root step of situation a)
of Step 3 in the proof of Lemma 1 in Kneser, *Lectures on
Galois Cohomology of Classical Groups* (1969), §5.5, printed pp. 74–75 (PDF pp. 80–81),
where an element close to `1` is shown, by the binomial series, to be the square of an
element of the commutative algebra it generates over the completion.
