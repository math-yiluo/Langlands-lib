# Cofinite local involution products of symmetric units

`numberFieldUnitarySymmetricCofinite` proves `NumberFieldUnitarySymmetricCofiniteStatement` for the actual finite completions of a number field \(F\). A symmetric unit \(g\) of a central simple algebra \(B\) with unitary involution over \(E/F\) is a product \(h\tau(h)\) outside finitely many finite places, with \(h\) a unit of the completed algebra.

Let \(A\) be the commutative subalgebra generated by \(g\) over \(F\). Left multiplication by \(g\) is injective on \(A\) since it is invertible on \(B\). Finite dimensionality makes it surjective, so the inverse of \(g\) is also in \(A\). Every element of \(A\) is fixed by the involution, since the generator and the base field are fixed and multiplication in \(A\) is commutative.

The proof writes a nonzero conjugation-odd scalar \(\omega\) as a difference of conjugates in \(E\). Its square is the image of some nonzero \(d\in F\). The cofinite quadratic norm representation in the finite algebra \(A\) gives \(x,y\) with \(x^2-dy^2=g\) in the completed tensor of \(A\). In the completed algebra of \(B\) the element \(x+\omega y\) has involution \(x-\omega y\). The two factors commute, so their product is \(g\), and hence both are units.

The cofinite norm input is the unramified unit norm observation after Proposition 4.2 in Prasad and Rapinchuk, *Local-global principles for embedding of fields with involution into simple algebras with involution*, arXiv:0806.0596v5, p. 15.
