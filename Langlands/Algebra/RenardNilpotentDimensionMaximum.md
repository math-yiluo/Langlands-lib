Renard introduces `φ_l(k)` as the largest possible dimension of a
commutative subalgebra of `End(V)` generated by `l` nilpotent elements and
the identity, where `V` is a complex vector space of dimension `k`.
The subsequent argument uses an algebra with this largest dimension.
The present construction makes existence and attainment explicit on each
given carrier. It uses the elementary ambient dimension bound, independently
of the sharp estimate proved elsewhere in the Library.

## Actual dimension set and maximum

All carrier-dependent declarations are in `Langlands.Renard` and retain
arbitrary `V : Type u` with explicit `AddCommGroup V`, `Module ℂ V` and
`FiniteDimensional ℂ V` instances.

`nilpotentGeneratedDimensions l V : Set ℕ` consists exactly of numbers

```text
Module.finrank ℂ (Algebra.adjoin ℂ (Set.range a))
```

for tuples `a : Fin l → Module.End ℂ V` satisfying
`∀ i j, Commute (a i) (a j)` and `∀ i, IsNilpotent (a i)`.
The existential equality in the definition has this displayed dimension on
the left and the set member on the right. It concerns the actual generated
subalgebra of the endomorphisms of `V`, rather than an abstract algebra
equipped with a supplied representation or dimension bound.

The ordered tuple labels the source's listed `l` elements. It permits zero
entries, repetition and redundant generators. All-index commutation
includes equal indices, where it is automatic, and expresses the usual
pairwise commutation of the family. It implies commutation of the range;
the unital algebra generated by that range is commutative. Conversely,
generators in a commutative endomorphism subalgebra commute after inclusion.
Generation by the listed elements and the identity is precisely generation
by `Algebra.adjoin ℂ (Set.range a)`: its unital complex algebra structure
already includes the identity and all scalar endomorphisms. The identity
does not count as another tuple entry.

`nilpotentGeneratedMaxDimension l V : ℕ` is defined to be
`sSup (nilpotentGeneratedDimensions l V)`. This is the natural-number
supremum; its mathematical specification as a greatest element is proved,
not included as a premise or a property of a supplied witness.

## Intrinsic construction laws and their proofs

- `nilpotentGeneratedDimensions_nonempty` uses the actual constant zero
  tuple. Its entries commute and are nilpotent, and the dimension of its
  actual adjoin is a member of the set.
- `nilpotentGeneratedDimensions_le` bounds each member by
  `(Module.finrank ℂ V) ^ 2`. The generated algebra is a linear subspace of
  `Module.End ℂ V`, so its dimension is at most the ambient dimension.
  Mathlib's dimension formula for linear maps gives
  `dim End(V) = dim V * dim V = (dim V)^2`.
  `nilpotentGeneratedDimensions_bddAbove` packages this bound.
- `nilpotentGeneratedMaxDimension_isGreatest` uses `Nat.sSup_mem` for a
  nonempty bounded set of natural numbers and `le_csSup` for its upper-bound
  property. Thus the constructed supremum is an actual member of this
  exact set.
- `nilpotentGeneratedMaxDimension_attained` unfolds that membership to
  produce an actual tuple, its commutation and nilpotence proofs, and the
  equality between its generated-algebra dimension and the maximum.
- `finrank_adjoin_le_nilpotentGeneratedMaxDimension` inserts any admissible
  actual tuple into the dimension set and applies the greatest-element
  property. `nilpotentGeneratedMaxDimension_le` applies the ambient bound
  to the maximum's membership.

These theorems assume no greatest element, bound or attaining tuple.
The only tuple hypotheses in the universal bound are actual commutation
and nilpotence.

## Canonical specialization, corners and remaining transport

`nilpotentGeneratedPhi l k : ℕ` is the derived specialization
`nilpotentGeneratedMaxDimension l (Fin k → ℂ)`. It provides a concrete
function of two natural numbers on the canonical dimension-`k` space.

`nilpotentGeneratedMaxDimension_eq_zero_of_finrank_eq_zero` proves that
the maximum on any zero-dimensional `V` is zero, by its ambient square
bound and natural-number nonnegativity. `nilpotentGeneratedPhi_zero`
specializes this to `nilpotentGeneratedPhi l 0 = 0`.
Every definition and construction law allows `l = 0` and zero-dimensional
spaces without positivity or nontriviality assumptions. The empty tuple
generates the scalar subalgebra, which still contains the identity; on a
zero-dimensional space that identity is the zero endomorphism.

Equality of possible dimension sets under arbitrary-universe complex linear
equivalences remains a separate statement and proof obligation. In
particular, this module does not identify `nilpotentGeneratedPhi l
(Module.finrank ℂ V)` with `nilpotentGeneratedMaxDimension l V` on an
arbitrary carrier. The canonical definition alone supplies no such transport.
Monotonicity, the source ideal filtration, the floor recurrence, the final
real-power estimate and completion of Appendix C.I are also outside this
contribution.
