## Actual algebra, ideal and action

All three constructions come from
`Langlands.Algebra.RenardNilpotentIdealFiltration`. Write
`R = NilpotentFiltration.generatedAlgebra a` and
`I = NilpotentFiltration.generatorIdeal a`. Thus `R` is the unital adjoin
of the original endomorphism tuple, `I` is the ideal generated by the
tuple inside `R`, and `V_n = idealPowerSubspace a n` is the restriction
to complex scalars of the actual `R`-submodule `I^n • ⊤` of `V`.
The action always evaluates the original endomorphisms on `V`.
Scalars and the identity already belong to the unital adjoin.

For every complex complement `W` to `V_1`, the theorem proves both

```text
V_n = span_ℂ {r(w) | r ∈ I^n, w ∈ W} + V_(n+1)   for every n : ℕ,
span_R W = V.
```

The subspace `W` is used only as a complex subspace. It is never assigned
an invariance property or an `R`-module structure.

## Implemented proof of quotient generation

The helper
`NilpotentFiltration.idealPowerSubspace_eq_complement_succ` proves the
first equality directly. In fact, this helper needs neither finite
dimension, commutation nor nilpotence: the actual complement condition
and the intrinsic ideal-action laws suffice.

An element of `I^n V` is a finite sum of vectors `r(v)` with `r ∈ I^n`.
The proof uses `Submodule.smul_induction_on` to reduce membership in the
desired complex subspace to such a vector and to addition. The complement
decomposition gives `v = x + w` with `x ∈ I V` and `w ∈ W`. Hence
`r(v) = r(x) + r(w)`. The second summand belongs to the displayed complex
span. A second ideal-action induction expresses `x` as a sum of `s(y)`
with `s ∈ I`; then `r(s(y)) = (r*s)(y)` belongs to `I^(n+1) V`.
Here `Submodule.pow_succ` and `Ideal.mul_mem_mul` supply actual power
membership, including `n = 0`.

For the reverse inclusion, each displayed evaluation vector belongs to
the actual `I^n V` by `Submodule.smul_mem_smul`, and the next filtration
level lies there by the published `idealPowerSubspace_antitone` law.
Complex span closure and the supremum property finish the equality.

## Implemented proof of full generation

The main theorem derives commutativity of `R` from the original tuple
commutation, using the published
`generatedAlgebra_isMulCommutative` law. It then proves
`I ≤ (⊥ : Ideal R).radical`. Indeed, every original generator is
nilpotent in `R`: its vanishing power is obtained from the given power
equality of the original endomorphism by subtype extensionality.
The radical contains all those generators, so `Ideal.span_le` places
the actual ideal `I` in it.

The complement condition gives `W + I V = V`. At this point the proof
imports
`Langlands.CommutingDimension.span_action_of_sup_radical_eq_top` from
`Langlands.Algebra.CommutingDimensionHelpers`, authored by Worker 307.
This published helper applies Nakayama to the same finite `R`-module `V`,
the same ideal `I`, and `span_R W`, and concludes that the complex span
of all `r(w)` is `V`. Its finite-module input follows from finite
dimension over the scalar field. Each `r(w)` belongs to `span_R W`,
so this full complex span is contained in the restriction to `ℂ` of
`span_R W`. Consequently `span_R W = ⊤`, the required second conjunct.
Only the named published helper is imported; its proof is not copied.
