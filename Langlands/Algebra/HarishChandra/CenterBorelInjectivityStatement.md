# Injectivity of the center projection at a split Cartan

`CenterBorelInjectivityStatement.{v}` concerns a finite-dimensional complex
Lie algebra with a subalgebra \(H\) satisfying the Library predicate
`IsSplitCartan H`. It requires \(H\) to be abelian, the adjoint weight spaces
to span the Lie algebra and the zero weight space to equal \(H\). The root
spaces are one-dimensional and the roots are reduced. For each root \(\chi\)
there are vectors of weights \(\chi\) and \(-\chi\) with bracket \(h\) in \(H\)
such that \(\chi(h)=2\). [Definition of `IsSplitCartan`](ParabolicIndependenceStatement.md#definitions)

An element \(X \in H\) is regular here if the real part of each root
evaluated at \(X\) is nonzero. The root spaces for which this real part
is positive sum to `positiveRootSubmodule H X`. A central element of the
enveloping algebra in the right ideal generated by this subspace is zero.

Knapp and Vogan prove injectivity of the Harish–Chandra projection for
complex reductive Lie algebras in *Cohomological Induction and Unitary
Representations* (1995), Chapter IV §7, Proposition 4.89 and Theorem 4.95,
pp. 285–286, with the injectivity argument on p. 291. The Lean statement
uses the split Cartan root data above and expresses injectivity as a
zero-intersection assertion. The enveloping antipode reverses multiplication
and maps the source's left ideal to the right ideal used here. The
normalizing shift acts by an automorphism of the Cartan enveloping algebra.
