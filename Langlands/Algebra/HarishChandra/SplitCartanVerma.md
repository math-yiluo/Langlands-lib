# Mirror Verma right ideals of a split Cartan subalgebra

Module `Langlands.Algebra.HarishChandra.SplitCartanVerma`, namespace
`Langlands.Algebra.HarishChandra`.

## Definitions

- `weightForm hχ`: a function `χ : 𝔥 → ℂ` with a nonzero weight vector is linear; this is
  `χ` as a linear form.
- `IsRealRegular H X`: every root `χ` (nonzero, with nonzero weight space) has
  `Re χ(X) ≠ 0`.
- `cartanShiftSpan λ`: the right ideal spanned by the products `(ι h - λ(h)) v`, `h ∈ 𝔥`.
- `vermaIdeal H X λ = generatorRightIdeal (positiveRootSubmodule H X) ⊔ cartanShiftSpan λ`.
  Here `positiveRootSubmodule H X` is the sum of the weight spaces of the weights with positive
  real part at `X`, and `generatorRightIdeal` is the right ideal it generates. This is the
  annihilator of the highest weight vector of the mirror Verma module of highest weight `λ`
  for the Borel subalgebra determined by `X`.
- `SortedWeightBasis H X`: a basis of simultaneous eigenvectors of `ad 𝔥` with at most one
  vector of each nonzero weight, sorted by decreasing real weight at `X`, whose vectors of real
  weight zero lie in `𝔥` and have weight `0`. For `λ`, `param λ` is the family of values of `λ`
  on those vectors.

## Results

- `mul_mem_of_mem_vermaIdeal`: if `u₀` multiplies the generators of `R_X(λ)` into a right ideal
  `R`, it multiplies `R_X(λ)` into `R`.
- `IsSplitCartan.nonempty_sortedWeightBasis`: for a split Cartan subalgebra of a
  finite-dimensional Lie algebra and real-regular `X`, a sorted weight basis exists. It comes
  from `IsSplitCartan.exists_ordered_weight_basis`, using that the zero weight space is `𝔥`.
- `SortedWeightBasis.basisVermaIdeal_eq`: `basisVermaIdeal` for `param λ` is `vermaIdeal H X λ`.
  The positive parts agree by `positiveRootSubmodule_eq_span` and
  `generatorRightIdeal_span_image`. For the Cartan parts, write `h ∈ 𝔥` in the basis: its
  coordinates are supported on the weight-zero vectors (`repr_eq_zero_of_mem`), and `λ` is
  linear.

For `IsSplitCartan H`, real-regular `X` and `z` commuting with `𝔥`:

- `exists_polynomial_sub_mem_vermaIdeal`: along a line `λ₀ + t λ₁` there is a polynomial `P` with
  `z - P(t) ∈ R_X(λ₀ + t λ₁)` for all `t`. `exists_sub_mem_vermaIdeal` is the case of a single
  `λ`.
- `eq_zero_of_algebraMap_mem_vermaIdeal` and `eq_of_sub_mem_vermaIdeal`: the scalar congruent to
  `z` is unique.
- `mem_generatorRightIdeal_of_forall_mem_vermaIdeal`: if `d` commutes with `𝔥` and lies in
  `R_X(λ)` for every `λ`, write `d = p + q` with `p` in the positive right ideal and
  `q = Σ q_e · (monomial e)` supported on the weight-zero letters. Its projection for `λ` is
  `Σ q_e Π λ(b_i)^{e_i}`. Every family of values on the weight-zero vectors comes from some
  `λ` (by `Basis.constr`). So the polynomial `Σ q_e X^e` vanishes everywhere, hence is zero
  (`MvPolynomial.funext`). This gives `q = 0`, so `d` lies in the right ideal generated by
  `positiveRootSubmodule H X`.
