# Elements of an enveloping algebra commuting with a family of weight operators

Module `Langlands.Algebra.HarishChandra.RealWeightDecomposition`, namespace
`Langlands.Algebra.HarishChandra`.

## Setting

`L` is a complex Lie algebra, `H` a Lie subalgebra, and `b : Module.Basis (Fin n) ℂ L` a basis of
simultaneous eigenvectors of `ad h`, `h ∈ H`, with weights `w a : H → ℂ`:
`⁅h, b a⁆ = w a h • b a`. `orderedPBWBasisOf b` is the ordered Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt basis of
`U(L)` from `Langlands.Algebra.EnvelopingFixedWeightDecomposition`, with members the ordered
monomials `orderedPBWMonomial b e`.

## Commutators

- `commutatorMap x : U(L) →ₗ[ℂ] U(L)` is `u ↦ ι(x) u - u ι(x)`. It is a derivation
  (`commutatorMap_mul`). On generators it is the bracket (`commutatorMap_ι`). On powers and
  ordered products of eigenvectors it multiplies by the sum of the eigenvalues
  (`commutatorMap_pow`, `commutatorMap_ofFn_prod`).
- `monomialRootWeight w e = fun h => Σ i, e i * w i h` is the weight of the exponent vector `e`.
  `commutatorMap_orderedPBWMonomial` shows that the monomial of `e` is an eigenvector of
  `commutatorMap h` for `monomialRootWeight w e h`. `orderedPBWBasisOf_repr_commutatorMap` is
  the corresponding statement for coordinates. `monomialRootWeight_eq_of_mem_support`: every
  monomial in the support of a weight vector of `U(L)` has its weight.
- `repr_lie_basis`, `mem_span_of_rootEigen` and `lie_mem_span_rootWeight` are the same facts in
  `L`. The bracket of two basis vectors lies in the span of the basis vectors whose weight is
  the sum of the two weights.

## Real weights

For `X ∈ H`, `realWeight w X a = Re (w a X)`.
`realPositiveGeneratorSpan b d` is the right ideal spanned by the products `ι(b a) v` with
`d a > 0`. `realZeroGeneratorSubalgebra b d` is the subalgebra generated by the `ι(b a)` with
`d a = 0`. `realPositiveLetterSpan` and `realZeroLetterSpan` are the spans of the ordered
monomials with some letter of positive real weight, respectively with all letters of real
weight zero. These two spans meet only in `0`
(`realPositiveLetterSpan_inf_realZeroLetterSpan`).

Assume `realWeight w X` is antitone, so that letters of larger real weight come first. Then:

- `realZeroGeneratorSubalgebra_le`: the zero-weight subalgebra lies in `realZeroLetterSpan`,
  because the zero-weight basis vectors span a Lie subalgebra (`lie_mem_span_realZero`) and the
  Library's `adjoin_le_span_orderedPBWBasisOf` applies.
- `generator_mul_mem_realPositiveLetterSpan` and `realPositiveGeneratorSpan_le`: the positive
  right ideal lies in `realPositiveLetterSpan`. Split a monomial into its part with positive
  letters and the rest (`ofFn_prod_pow_add`). The product of `ι(b a)`, `d a > 0`, with the
  positive part lies in the subalgebra generated by positive letters (`lie_mem_span_realPos`).
  It is a weight vector of positive real weight, so every monomial in its support has a positive
  letter.
- `disjoint_real_of_antitone`: the positive right ideal and the zero-weight subalgebra are
  disjoint.
- `mem_sup_of_commute_of_antitone`: an element `z` with `commutatorMap h z = 0` for all `h ∈ H`
  lies in their sum. Every monomial of `z` has weight `0`. If it has a letter of positive real
  weight, its first letter has positive real weight (`ofFn_prod_pow_mem_span_of_pos_real`).
  Otherwise all its letters have real weight `0` (`weight_eq_zero_of_sum_eq_zero_real`).

This is the argument of Knapp–Vogan, *Cohomological Induction and Unitary Representations*
(1995), Lemma 4.123, with the grading by the real parts of the weights at `X`, in the mirror
form with right ideals.
