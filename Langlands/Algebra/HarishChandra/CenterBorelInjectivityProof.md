# Injectivity of the center projection at a split Cartan

`centerBorelInjectivity` proves `CenterBorelInjectivityStatement.{v}`.

Use a joint weight basis ordered so that the positive directions
come first. The positive root subspace is spanned by exactly those
basis vectors. The bracket adds weights, so the positive subspace is
closed under brackets.

To multiply a PBW monomial on the left by a positive generator,
split it into its positive prefix and the remaining factors. The
generator times the prefix is in the subalgebra generated by the
positive directions. That product expands in the PBW monomials
using only positive directions. The enveloping augmentation sends
the product to zero and detects the constant coefficient, so every
term of this expansion has a positive direction. Appending the
remaining factors preserves PBW order. By linearity, the full
right ideal of positive root directions has zero Cartan coordinates.

For each root basis vector, the split Cartan supplies an opposite
root vector whose bracket with it is a nonzero Cartan element.
Brackets with other root directions have zero Cartan component.
The theorem `cartanCenterProjectionInjectivity` then detects a
central element by its Cartan coordinates through its highest PBW
symbol, proving the zero intersection.
