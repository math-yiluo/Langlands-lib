# Facts on Levi components used for the independence of the parabolic

Module `Langlands.Algebra.HarishChandra.LeviComponentFacts`, namespace
`Langlands.Algebra.HarishChandra`.

## Linear algebra

- `trace_restrict_span_eigen`: for a basis `b` of eigenvectors of an endomorphism `T`
  (`T (b a) = c a • b a`) and the span `V` of `b` over a finite set `S` of indices, the trace of
  `T` restricted to `V` is `Σ_{a ∈ S} c a`. It is computed with the basis of `V` given by
  `Basis.span`, in which the restriction is diagonal.
- `positiveEigenSubmodule_eq_span`: let the weights of a weight basis take integer values at
  `X`. Then `positiveEigenSubmodule X`, the sum of the eigenspaces of `ad X` for the positive
  integers, is the span of the basis vectors with `Re w a(X) > 0`.
- `lie_eq_zero_iff`: `[X, x] = 0` exactly when the coordinates of `x` are supported on the
  indices with `w a(X) = 0`.
- `lie_mem_positiveEigenSubmodule`: an element commuting with `X` preserves
  `positiveEigenSubmodule X` (Jacobi identity).

## Enveloping algebras

- `commutatorMap_mem_generatorRightIdeal`: the commutator with a generator `ι y` preserves the
  right ideal `V U(𝔤)` when `ad y` preserves `V`.
- `mem_center_of_commute_ι`: an element commuting with all generators is central, because the
  generators generate `U(𝔤)` (`Langlands.Algebra.LieCasimir.enveloping_adjoin_range`).
- `mem_center_of_sub_mem_generatorRightIdeal`: **the Levi component of a central element is
  central.** Let `ι : 𝔪 → 𝔤` be injective with range `ker ad X`, `X` integral on the roots, `z`
  central, and `z - j(y) ∈ 𝔳 U(𝔤)` with `𝔳 = positiveEigenSubmodule X`. Then `y` is central.
  For `m ∈ 𝔪`, `j([m, y]) = -[ι m, z - j y]` lies in `𝔳 U(𝔤)`. It also lies in the subalgebra
  generated by the weight-zero letters of a sorted basis for `X`. These meet only in `0`
  (`disjoint_real_of_antitone`), and `j` is injective (`lift_comp_injective`). This is the
  mirror form of Knapp–Vogan, *Cohomological Induction and Unitary Representations* (1995),
  Lemma 4.123 (c).

## The shift `τ_λ`

`scalarShift λ` is the algebra endomorphism of `U(𝔪)` with `τ_λ(ι Y) = ι Y + λ(Y)`
(`scalarShift_ι`), for a linear form `λ` vanishing on brackets.

- `scalarShift_mem_center`: `τ_λ` maps central elements to central elements, since
  `ι m = τ_λ(ι m - λ(m))`.
- `eq_zero_of_mem_weightSpace`, `eq_zero_of_mem_positiveRootSubmodule`: `λ` vanishes on nonzero
  weight spaces, since `x = [h, x]/χ(h)` for `h` with `χ(h) ≠ 0`.
- `map_mem_of_mem_vermaIdeal`: an algebra map sending the generators of a mirror Verma ideal into
  a right ideal `R` sends the whole ideal into `R`.
- `scalarShift_mem_vermaIdeal`: `τ_λ` maps `R_Y(μ + λ|)` into `R_Y(μ)`.
