# Renard C.I: invariance of the nilpotent dimension maximum

This module defines a proposition using the published actual dimension-set,
maximum and canonical-carrier definitions in
`Langlands.Algebra.RenardNilpotentDimensionMaximum`. It supplies no proof
of the transport proposition.

`Langlands.Renard.NilpotentDimensionTransportStatement` asserts
two related facts. First, every complex linear equivalence between
finite-dimensional spaces in independent carrier universes preserves both
the set of dimensions of actual unital endomorphism algebras generated by
commuting nilpotent `Fin l` tuples and the maximum of that set. Second, on
every arbitrary-universe finite-dimensional complex space, this
carrier-dependent maximum equals `nilpotentGeneratedPhi` evaluated at its
complex finrank.

The definitions count the listed nilpotent generators; the identity belongs
to the unital generated algebra. No nonzero, distinctness or minimality
restriction is added. The proposition includes all natural `l`, zero spaces
and equivalences between different carrier universes. No equality of
dimension sets or maxima is assumed as an input.

The source is David Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*,
French version dated 9 July 2026, Appendix C.I, printed p.275 / one-based
PDF281, registered as
`renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, SHA256
`7b539996793b7c3620e72e1e389588eb42d1a8e91d6f68b2eead5c2e2ffc182c`.
Renard denotes the maximum by a function of the dimension of the vector
space. The explicit transport equalities are a derived justification of that
dimension-only notation and of the implementation's canonical carrier
`Fin k → ℂ`; they are not separately numbered assertions in the source.

An intended proof route is to conjugate every generator by the supplied
linear equivalence, use preservation of commutation and nilpotence, identify
the transported generated algebra with the image of the original algebra,
and use the induced algebra equivalence to preserve finrank. Apply the
inverse equivalence for the reverse inclusion of dimension sets, and then
use their actual maximum definitions. A basis indexed by the finite
finrank gives the canonical-carrier bridge. This paragraph is a proof plan;
the Lean file defines only a proposition and proves none of it.

The quantifiers carry explicit `AddCommGroup`, complex `Module` and
`FiniteDimensional` instances for each space. The first conjunct applies to
every supplied `V ≃ₗ[ℂ] W` with independent universes `u` and `v`. The
second quantifies over every `V : Type u`, while the canonical function
space lies in the universe of `ℂ`; it adds no carrier or basis restriction.
For `l = 0` the tuple is empty and its unital adjoin is the scalar algebra.
Zero-dimensional spaces are included, where the identity endomorphism is
zero. These corner conventions come from the imported definitions; this
module adds no numerical base-case theorem.

The source ideal-power filtration, monotonicity of the maximum, numerical
base cases, floor recurrence and final dimension estimate remain separate
obligations. The transport equalities above are the entire statement unit.
