# Generic flatness and density over arbitrary domains

This internal helper module by Worker 1606 supplies generic flatness and density
for the affine algebraic core of generic geometric irreducibility spreading.

The first lemma identifies scalar extension of a localization map with a
localization at the image submonoid. It uses tensor symmetry and Mathlib's
localization of the first tensor factor, followed by extension of scalars in the
localization property. The second lemma transfers flatness across the resulting
localization equivalence and flat base change.

For a finitely presented algebra over an arbitrary domain, a finite presentation
has finitely many coefficients. Mathlib's presentation core is the subalgebra
over the integers generated by these coefficients, hence a Noetherian domain.
The existing Library generic flatness theorem applies to its presentation model.
Flat base change and the presentation model equivalence then give generic
flatness over the original domain.

These are internal derived lemmas, based on the Library generic flatness proof
and Mathlib's finite presentation descent and localization APIs. The
density application is the flatness step in the registered Stacks Project
Lemma 37.27.5 (tag 0559).

The module also proves generic flatness for any finite type algebra over a
domain. Express the algebra as a quotient of a polynomial ring in finitely many
variables. Over the fraction field, Noetherianity allows finitely many elements
of the kernel to generate its localized image. Their span gives a finitely
presented algebra surjecting onto the given algebra with torsion kernel.
Localize the finitely presented algebra where it is flat. Flatness over a domain
implies torsion freeness, so the localized torsion kernel is zero. The resulting
linear equivalence transfers flatness to the original algebra.

Two intermediate lemmas implement the finite lift of ideal generators and the
transfer through a surjection with torsion kernel. The latter uses the universal
map between localized modules, its surjectivity, and scalar cancellation on the
flat source to prove its injectivity.

For a short exact sequence of modules, flatness of its localized cokernel
makes the localized sequence remain injective on the left after tensoring.
If the new scalar ring inverts the localization element, the tensor of the
original localization map is injective: it is itself a localization at a
submonoid of units. Naturality then reflects the injectivity back to the tensor
of the original sequence.

Apply this result to multiplication by a nonzero element `g` of a finite type
domain `C` over a domain `R`. The cokernel `C/(g)` is a finite type algebra,
so the generic flatness result supplies a nonzero `a` in `R`. Multiplication by
`g` is injective because `C` is a domain, and its image is exactly the kernel of
the quotient map. For every scalar algebra inverting `a`, its tensor is
injective. The field wrapper identifies this tensor map with multiplication by
`1 ⊗ g` in `K ⊗[R] C`. The element `a` precedes the quantification over fields
in an independent universe, and the proof adds no faithfulness hypothesis to
the original algebra structure.

These results are complete helper lemmas for the main spreading proof. The affine model and
nilradical reductions use them to establish irreducibility of nearby fibres.
