# Additive stable-generation comparisons for integer unitization

This proof module proves the published aggregate
`Langlands.Algebra.AdditiveUnitizationGeneration.AdditiveUnitizationGenerationStatement`
in theorem `additiveUnitizationGeneration`. Its independent universes are
`uA`, `uM`, in that order. The aggregate is the ordered conjunction of the
submodule, whole-module and Noetherian comparisons. The statement is
imported and its Lean/Markdown pair is left unchanged.

Let `A` be any nonunital ring and `M` an additive commutative group. Supply
an actual ordinary `U = Unitization ℤ A`-module structure on `M`, a
nonunital ring homomorphism `ρ : A →ₙ+* AddMonoid.End M`, and the coordinate
formula `u • m = u.fst • m + ρ u.snd m`. The integer multiple here comes
from the additive group of `M`. No scalar field, scalar tower or
scalar-linearity assumption on `ρ` is supplied. No general action or
lattice equivalence is constructed.

`fg_iff_additive_stable_generators` compares finite generation of each
actual unitized submodule `Q` with generation by a finite set among all
actual additive subgroups stable under `ρ`. In the forward direction its
proof gives each test subgroup a temporary `U`-submodule with the same
carrier. Closure under integer multiples is supplied by the additive
subgroup; the coordinate formula and stability supply unitized scalar
closure. Ordinary span minimality proves containment of `Q`. In the reverse
direction the test subgroup is the additive subgroup of the ordinary
unitized span of the proposed generators. Its stability follows by acting
with `Unitization.inr a`. Minimality and containment of the generators in
`Q` give both inclusions, hence equality with the span.

`finite_iff_additive_stable_generators` specializes the comparison to the
top submodule using `Module.finite_def`. Containment of the whole module
forces a stable subgroup to be top. `noetherian_iff_additive_stable_generators`
gives every stable additive subgroup the temporary unitized submodule with
the same carrier, which is finitely generated by Noetherianity, and applies
the submodule comparison to it. Conversely, the additive subgroup of every
actual unitized submodule is stable by acting with `Unitization.inr a`, so
the hypothesis and the submodule comparison make that submodule finitely
generated. The three closed
component theorems discharge all helper parameters, and
`additiveUnitizationGeneration` combines them in the target order. The
supplied-action section ends before the closed theorems, so the aggregate
has no additional section parameters.
