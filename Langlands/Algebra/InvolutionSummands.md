# Summands of a linear involution

For a scalar `s` with `s² = 1`, the submodule `involutionPart T s` is the
kernel of `T − s`. If `2c = 1`, the endomorphism `c(1 + sT)` has image
in this submodule and restricts to its identity. This gives a retraction
of the inclusion, so a summand of a flat module is flat. A differential
commuting with the involutions restricts to a differential of the
summands.

The projection formula also holds after scalar extension. Given an
intertwining isomorphism on the full tensor module, inclusion followed
by this isomorphism identifies the tensor of the summand with the
corresponding summand after extension. The inverse uses the projection
on the original tensor module. The formulas depend only on the two
identities for `s` and `c`.
