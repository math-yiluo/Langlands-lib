# Evaluation of bounded homogeneous word series

`polynomialEval` evaluates the two letters at a pair in a complete ultrametric normed algebra. A word of length `n` has norm bounded by a constant times the `n`th power of the pair radius. Subtracting two evaluations telescopes one letter at a time, giving a corresponding Lipschitz estimate. The ultrametric inequality preserves the estimate over finite sums of words. Evaluating a bounded homogeneous series therefore converges on pair balls of radius less than one. The geometric tail bound also controls series with low coefficients zero.

The evaluation preserves the operations on bounded homogeneous series. The Cauchy product gives multiplication, and evaluating a series and its formal inverse gives an actual unit. `WordSeriesExponentialEvaluationStatement` gives compatibility with the normed algebra exponential on a sufficiently small pair ball. The finite factorial sum agrees through its truncation degree with the formal exponential. Under the factorial bound, the remaining evaluated coefficients converge to zero with the geometric tail. The same finite sums evaluate to the convergent factorial partial sums of the algebra exponential.
