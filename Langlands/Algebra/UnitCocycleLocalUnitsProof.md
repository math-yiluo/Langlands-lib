# Proof of local unit generators for a unit cocycle along a faithfully flat algebra

Author: Worker 303.

This module proves the published proposition
`Langlands.Algebra.UnitCocycleLocalUnitsStatement` (module
`Langlands.Algebra.UnitCocycleLocalUnitsStatement`). The theorem is

```text
theorem Langlands.Algebra.unitCocycleLocalUnits : UnitCocycleLocalUnitsStatement.{u}
```

Recall the statement. Let `R → S` be faithfully flat, and let `u, v ∈ S ⊗_R S` with
`u v = 1` and `u₁₃ = u₂₃ · u₁₂` in `S ⊗_R (S ⊗_R S)`. Here `x₁₂`, `x₂₃` and `x₁₃` are the
images of `x` under `s ⊗ t ↦ s ⊗ (t ⊗ 1)`, `s ⊗ t ↦ 1 ⊗ (s ⊗ t)` and
`s ⊗ t ↦ s ⊗ (1 ⊗ t)`. Then the elements `r ∈ R` with `algebraMap r = m m'`, where
`u (m ⊗ 1) = 1 ⊗ m` and `v (m' ⊗ 1) = 1 ⊗ m'`, generate the unit ideal of `R`.

The proof implements the derivation in the statement's Markdown, which goes back to
Stacks Project, *Descent*, Proposition 3.9 (Tag 023N). It needs only the following part of
that proposition: the descended module generates `S`. That part comes directly from
flatness, so the isomorphism `S ⊗_R M_u ≅ S` is never constructed.

## Auxiliary declarations

All of them are in namespace `Langlands.Algebra`, for commutative rings `R`, `S` in one
universe and an algebra `R → S`.

- `unitCocycleDefect u : S →ₗ[R] S ⊗[R] S` is the `R`-linear map
  `m ↦ u * (m ⊗ 1) - 1 ⊗ m`. It is written as
  `LinearMap.mulLeft R u ∘ₗ includeLeft - includeRight`, and `unitCocycleDefect_apply`
  records the formula. Its kernel is the descended module
  `M_u = {m | u * (m ⊗ 1) = 1 ⊗ m}` (`mem_ker_unitCocycleDefect`).
- `lTensor_unitCocycleDefect`: base change of this map along `S` is
  `x ↦ u₂₃ * x₁₂ - x₁₃` on `S ⊗[R] S`. This is checked on pure tensors `s ⊗ t`, where both
  sides are `s ⊗ (u * (t ⊗ 1)) - s ⊗ (1 ⊗ t)`, and extended by additivity.
- `span_unitCocycle_eq_top`: if `S` is flat over `R`, `u` is a unit and
  `u₁₃ = u₂₃ · u₁₂`, then `Ideal.span {m | u * (m ⊗ 1) = 1 ⊗ m} = ⊤` in `S`.
- `unitCocycle_inv`: if `u v = 1` and `u` satisfies the cocycle identity, so does `v`.

## Proof of `span_unitCocycle_eq_top`

Let `N` be the kernel of `g := unitCocycleDefect u`. The sequence `0 → N → S → S ⊗ S`,
whose maps are the inclusion and `g`, is exact (`LinearMap.exact_subtype_ker_map`). Since
`S` is flat over `R`, applying `S ⊗_R -` keeps it exact (`Module.Flat.lTensor_exact`). So
the kernel of `id_S ⊗ g` is the image of `S ⊗_R N → S ⊗_R S`.

By `lTensor_unitCocycleDefect`, `(id_S ⊗ g)(u) = u₂₃ · u₁₂ - u₁₃`, which is `0` by the
cocycle identity. Hence `u` is the image of some `y ∈ S ⊗_R N`. Apply the multiplication
map `lmul' : S ⊗_R S → S`. By induction on `y`, the image of every element of `S ⊗_R N`
lies in the ideal of `S` generated by `N`. On a pure tensor `s ⊗ n` the image is `s · n`.
Hence `lmul' u` lies in that ideal. It is a unit, being the image of the unit `u` under a
ring homomorphism, so the ideal is `⊤` (`Ideal.eq_top_of_isUnit_mem`).

## Proof of `unitCocycle_inv`

Let `f₁₃`, `f₂₃` and `f₁₂` be the three algebra homomorphisms. Since `u v = 1`, we have
`f(u) f(v) = 1` for each of them. In the commutative ring `S ⊗ (S ⊗ S)`, the identity
`v₁₃ = v₂₃ v₁₂` is a linear combination of these three relations and of the cocycle
identity for `u`. This is checked by `linear_combination`; the underlying computation is
`v₁₃ = v₁₃ (u₂₃ v₂₃)(u₁₂ v₁₂) = v₁₃ u₁₃ v₂₃ v₁₂ = v₂₃ v₁₂`.

## Proof of the theorem

Let `M_u` and `M_v` be the two invariant sets. Then `u` and `v` are units. By
`unitCocycle_inv`, `v` also satisfies the cocycle identity, and `S` is flat over `R`. So
`span_unitCocycle_eq_top` gives `Ideal.span M_u = ⊤` and `Ideal.span M_v = ⊤` in `S`.

Let `I` be the ideal of `R` in the statement. First, `I · S = ⊤`. Indeed,
`⊤ = span M_u · span M_v = span (M_u · M_v)` (`Ideal.span_mul_span`), so it suffices to
show that every product `m m'` with `m ∈ M_u`, `m' ∈ M_v` lies in `I · S`. Multiplying the
two defining equations and using `u v = 1` gives

```text
(m m') ⊗ 1 = (u v) (m ⊗ 1)(m' ⊗ 1) = (u (m ⊗ 1)) (v (m' ⊗ 1)) = 1 ⊗ (m m').
```

Since `R → S` is faithfully flat, `Algebra.IsEffective.of_faithfullyFlat` and
`eqLocus_includeLeft_includeRight` show that the equalizer of `s ↦ s ⊗ 1` and `s ↦ 1 ⊗ s`
is the image of `R`. So `m m' = algebraMap r` for some `r ∈ R`. By construction `r` is one
of the generators of `I`, so `m m' = algebraMap r ∈ I · S`.

Finally, `Ideal.comap_map_eq_self_of_faithfullyFlat` gives
`I = (I · S) ∩ R = comap ⊤ = ⊤`.

## Hypotheses

The proof uses exactly the hypotheses of the statement. Flatness of `S` is used for exact
base change, and faithful flatness for element descent and for the contraction of extended
ideals. There are no finiteness or noetherian assumptions. No normalization `μ(u) = 1` is
needed: the argument uses only that `μ(u)` is a unit.
