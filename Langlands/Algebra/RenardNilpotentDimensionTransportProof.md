# Renard C.I: transport of the nilpotent dimension maximum

Worker 16 proves
`Langlands.Renard.nilpotentDimensionTransport` with exactly the published type
`Langlands.Renard.NilpotentDimensionTransportStatement.{u, v}`.
The carrier universes `u` and `v` are independent. The proof uses the unchanged
actual definitions in `Langlands.Algebra.RenardNilpotentDimensionMaximum`.

For a complex linear equivalence `e : V ≃ₗ[ℂ] W`, its algebra equivalence
`e.conjAlgEquiv ℂ` sends an endomorphism `a` to `e ∘ a ∘ e⁻¹`. The private
one-direction lemma sends an admissible tuple to the tuple of its conjugates.
Mathlib's `Commute.map` and `IsNilpotent.map` preserve the two admissibility
conditions. The published theorem
`Langlands.CommutingDimension.finrank_adjoin_map`, imported from
`Langlands.Algebra.CommutingDimensionRecurrence`, applies to this injective
algebra map and preserves the dimension of the actual unital adjoin. Thus
every attainable dimension on `V` is attainable on `W`.

`nilpotentGeneratedDimensions_eq_of_linearEquiv` applies this construction to
`e` and `e.symm` and uses antisymmetry of set inclusion.
`nilpotentGeneratedMaxDimension_eq_of_linearEquiv` unfolds the maximum as
the natural supremum of that dimension set and rewrites by the set equality.
For the second conjunct of the target, the basis `Module.finBasis ℂ V`
provides an equivalence
`V ≃ₗ[ℂ] (Fin (Module.finrank ℂ V) → ℂ)` through `Basis.equivFun`.
The same maximum-transport theorem then gives the required equality with
`nilpotentGeneratedPhi`, by its definition. No transport equality or
canonical identification is assumed.

The argument applies to every natural generator count, including the empty
tuple, and to every finite-dimensional complex carrier, including the zero
space. It permits zero entries, repeated entries and redundant generators.
The identity and scalars belong to the unital adjoin and do not contribute
another counted generator. Neither part of the proof requires a positive
dimension, nonzero generator, chosen minimal tuple or nontrivial carrier.

The source is David Renard, *Représentations des groupes réductifs p-adiques*,
French version dated 9 July 2026, Appendix C.I, printed pp.275–276 /
one-based PDF281–282, registered as
`renard-2026-representations-groupes-reductifs-padiques`, SHA256
`7b539996793b7c3620e72e1e389588eb42d1a8e91d6f68b2eead5c2e2ffc182c`.
Those pages were read for this proof. On printed p.275 Renard defines
`φ_l(k)` as the largest dimension of a unital algebra generated by the
listed commuting nilpotent endomorphisms on a space of dimension `k`.
The transport proposition is a derived justification of this dependence
on dimension alone, rather than a separately numbered source lemma.
The supplementary 2010-edition corrections, registered as
`renard-2010-corrections-complements`, were read on PDF18–19; their C.I
inequality corrections do not enter this conjugation argument.

The reused dimension-preservation theorem is part of Worker 307's published
general-field infrastructure. The proof here imports that theorem; it does
not reimplement its proof. Monotonicity, the source ideal-power filtration,
numerical base cases, the floor recurrence and the final dimension estimate
remain separate obligations.
