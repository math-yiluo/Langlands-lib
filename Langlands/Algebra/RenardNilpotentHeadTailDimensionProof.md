# Renard C.I: proof of the head/tail and first-level comparisons

The theorem `Langlands.Renard.nilpotentHeadTailDimension.{u}` proves exactly
the unchanged published proposition
`Langlands.Renard.NilpotentHeadTailDimensionStatement.{u}`. It quantifies over
every finite-dimensional complex vector space in an arbitrary carrier universe,
every natural `l`, and every commuting nilpotent tuple
`a : Fin (l + 1) → Module.End ℂ V`. It proves the subspace splitting and all
four dimension conclusions, including the conclusion for every complement.
The statement pair and its imports are preserved.

## Actual algebra and splitting

Write `R = generatedAlgebra a`, `B = tailAlgebra a`, `J = headIdeal a`,
`f = generator a 0` and `V_1 = idealPowerSubspace a 1`. These are the
published original-action constructions in namespace
`Langlands.Renard.NilpotentFiltration`. In particular, `B` is a subalgebra
inside `R`, and `J` is the ideal of that same algebra generated by its head.
`generatedAlgebra_isMulCommutative a hcomm` makes `R` commutative.

The private lemma `headIdealSubspace_eq_mulLeft_range` identifies the actual
complex subspace underlying `J` with the range of the complex-linear map
`t : R →ₗ[ℂ] R`, `t(c) = f * c`. The ideal's singleton-span membership
criterion expresses its elements as `c * f`; commutativity gives precisely
this range. It does not replace the ideal with an unrelated multiplication
image.

The private lemma `tail_sup_head_eq_top` considers the complex subspace
`U = B.toSubmodule ⊔ headIdealSubspace a`. It contains one. If `b,d ∈ B`
and `c,e ∈ J`, then

```text
(b + c) * (d + e) = b * d + (b * e + d * c + c * e).
```

The first term belongs to `B` and all remaining terms belong to `J` by its
ideal laws. Thus `U` is a unital subalgebra of `R`. The published
`CommutingDimension.adjoin_generators_top` and `adjoin_head_tail` show that
the head together with `B` generates all of `R`. Since both lie in `U`,
`U = ⊤`. This proves the required vector-space sum; no direct-sum assertion
is made. This subalgebra construction adapts the argument in Worker 307's
published `CommutingDimension.dimension_adjoin_insert`, using the actual ideal
directly to establish multiplicative closure.

## Dimension comparisons

The inclusion `R →ₐ[ℂ] Module.End ℂ V` is injective.
`Subalgebra.equivMapOfInjective` therefore identifies `B` linearly with its
image. The published `tailAlgebra_map` identifies that image with the actual
algebra generated by `Fin.tail a` in the original endomorphism ring.
The tail is a commuting nilpotent tuple with `l` entries.
`finrank_adjoin_le_nilpotentGeneratedMaxDimension` bounds its dimension by
the actual maximum on `V`; `nilpotentDimensionTransport.{u,0}.2` identifies
this maximum with `nilpotentGeneratedPhi l (Module.finrank ℂ V)`.

For the head comparison the proof applies the published
`CommutingDimension.multiplication_dimension_le_restriction` to `R`, `f`
and the actual `V_1`. The published `idealPowerSubspace_stable` supplies
invariance under all of `R`, and `generator_apply_mem_one` supplies
`f(V) ⊆ V_1`. For the restriction map `rho : R →ₐ[ℂ] Module.End ℂ V_1`,
commutativity gives `f * c = c * f`, so `rho(c) = 0` implies `t(c) = 0`.
The reused lemma compares these kernels and uses rank-nullity to bound
`dim(range t) ≤ dim(range rho)`. The private ideal-range equality supplies
the left side, while the published `firstLevelAlgebra_eq_range` supplies
exactly `dim(firstLevelAlgebra a)` on the right. The ambient endomorphism
algebra is not substituted for this image.

For the fourth conclusion, `firstLevelTuple_commute` and
`firstLevelTuple_isNilpotent` make the original tuple restricted to `V_1`
admissible for its actual nilpotent maximum. Applying the same maximum bound
and carrier transport gives
`dim(firstLevelAlgebra a) ≤ nilpotentGeneratedPhi (l + 1) (dim V_1)`.
There are `l` tail generators and `l + 1` restricted generators; the unital
adjoins include the identity and scalars without adding tuple entries.

Finally, `Submodule.finrank_add_le_finrank_add_finrank` applied to the
proved subspace sum gives `dim R ≤ dim B + dim J`. For every complex
subspace `W` with `IsCompl V_1 W`,
`Submodule.finrank_add_eq_of_isCompl` gives
`dim V_1 + dim W = dim V`, hence `dim V_1 = dim V - dim W` in natural
numbers. Substituting this identity into the preceding bounds proves the
fifth conclusion. This use of `W` requires no invariance.

Every dimension used here is finite: `R` embeds in the finite-dimensional
endomorphism space of `V`, `B` and `J` lie in `R`, and `V_1` lies in `V`.
The restricted endomorphism space and its generated subalgebra are likewise
finite-dimensional. The proof adds no nontriviality, distinctness, positivity
or nonzero-entry assumption. It includes zero spaces and zero, repeated or
redundant generators. For `l = 0` the tail is the empty tuple's unital scalar
algebra. On a zero space all these dimensions and the corresponding maximum
terms are zero. The same proof covers both corners without case exclusions.

The conclusion bounds each original tuple's algebra. The bound for a global
maximum using an attaining tuple and its complement, the floor recurrence,
the numerical contradiction, joint-block decomposition and the remaining
whole-book assertions are separate results and are not premises here.
