# The Lie algebra of positive adjoint weights

Let a Lie algebra over a field of characteristic zero have a basis of eigenvectors for the adjoint action of one element, with integral eigenvalues. `mem_weightSpan_of_ad` uses basis coordinates and injectivity of the integer cast to place any eigenvector in the span of the basis at its weight. The Jacobi identity adds the weights when taking brackets. The span of the positive weight vectors is therefore a Lie subalgebra, with a basis formed by restricting the ambient basis to the positive indices.

This derived construction supplies the positive basis in the triangular enveloping decomposition used for the Levi restriction in Langlands, *On the notion of an automorphic representation* (1979), Lemma 4, printed p. 204.
