# The Laurent coaction of a ℤ-grading and formal smoothness of quotients

locus of a `𝔾ₘ`-action).

## Quotients by ideals with `J ≤ J ^ 2 ⊔ N`

`formallySmooth_quotient_of_le_sq_sup`: let `R` be a commutative ring, `B` a commutative
`R`-algebra and `N ≤ J` ideals of `B` with `J ≤ J ^ 2 ⊔ N`. If `B ⧸ N` is formally smooth
over `R`, then so is `B ⧸ J`.

Proof (as implemented): given a square-zero ideal `K` of an `R`-algebra `D` and
`x : B ⧸ J →ₐ[R] D ⧸ K`, lift `x` composed with `B ⧸ N → B ⧸ J` to
`h : B ⧸ N →ₐ[R] D` by formal smoothness of `B ⧸ N`, and let `φ : B → D` be `h`
composed with the quotient map. Then `φ` kills `N`, and the image ideal `J.map φ` lies
in `K`. Mapping `J ≤ J ^ 2 ⊔ N` gives `J.map φ ≤ (J.map φ) ^ 2 ≤ K ^ 2 = ⊥`, so `φ` kills
`J` and induces the required lift `B ⧸ J →ₐ[R] D` (`Ideal.Quotient.liftₐ`).

## The Laurent coaction

Throughout, `k` is a commutative ring, `A` a commutative `k`-algebra and
`𝒜 : ℤ → Submodule k A` a grading (`GradedAlgebra 𝒜`).

- `laurentSingle 𝒜 n : 𝒜 n →+ LaurentPolynomial A` sends `a` to `a Tⁿ`
  (`AddMonoidAlgebra.single n a`; `laurentSingle_apply`). `laurentSingle_one` and
  `laurentSingle_mul` are the compatibilities with the graded unit and multiplication.
- `laurentCoaction 𝒜 : A →+* LaurentPolynomial A` is the ring map `a ↦ ∑ₙ aₙ Tⁿ`, where
  `aₙ` is the component of `a` of degree `n`. It is `DirectSum.toSemiring` of
  `laurentSingle 𝒜` after the decomposition `decomposeRingEquiv 𝒜`
  (`laurentCoaction_apply`).
- `laurentCoaction_of_mem`: for `a ∈ 𝒜 n`, `laurentCoaction 𝒜 a = a Tⁿ`.
- `coeff_laurentCoaction`: the coefficient of `Tⁿ` in `laurentCoaction 𝒜 a` is
  `gradeProj 𝒜 n a`, the component of degree `n`.

These give the three hypotheses of `formallySmooth_quotient_negIdeal`:

- `laurentCoaction_algebraMap`: scalars go to constants, `C (algebraMap k A r)`;
- `sum_coeff_laurentCoaction`: the sum of all coefficients of `laurentCoaction 𝒜 a` is
  `a`;
- `laurentCoaction_coeff`: the coefficient of `Tⁿ` is homogeneous of degree `n`, i.e.
  `laurentCoaction 𝒜` maps it to itself times `Tⁿ`.

## The negative part

- `negSpan 𝒜` is the ideal generated by the homogeneous elements of negative degree,
  `Ideal.span {a | ∃ n < 0, a ∈ 𝒜 n}`.
- `negIdeal_laurentCoaction`: the Library ideal `negIdeal (laurentCoaction 𝒜)` (generated
  by the negative-degree coefficients of the values) equals `negSpan 𝒜`.
- `formallySmooth_quotient_negSpan`: if `A` is formally smooth over `k`, then so is
  `A ⧸ negSpan 𝒜`.
