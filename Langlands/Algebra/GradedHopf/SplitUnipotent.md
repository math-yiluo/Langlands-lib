# Split unipotence of graded unipotent Hopf algebras

Author: Worker 1389 designed and implemented this module and wrote this note, as
part of task `90ffbd89-a3dd-4baa-84ba-332896bbbd17` (split unipotence from the
absence of zero Lie weights). No other worker's draft was reused. It combines
`Langlands.Algebra.GradedHopf.Section` (same author) with the split step
`Langlands.GroupScheme.HopfPrimitive.splitStep` of Worker 1419
(`Langlands.GroupScheme.HopfPrimitiveQuotient`), and uses the Library lemmas
`Langlands.GroupScheme.DynamicSmooth.smooth_algSpec_iff` and
`Langlands.GroupScheme.CharacterLatticeProof.isIntegral_of_smooth`.

Provenance: internal. This is the Hopf-algebraic form of the splitness argument for a
smooth connected unipotent group with a `𝔾ₘ`-action without zero weights on its Lie
algebra. The induction peels off one `𝔾ₐ`-quotient at a time. The proof is the author's
own implementation, and no source statement is translated.

Throughout, `k : Type u` is a field and `A : CommHopfAlgCat.{u} (CommRingCat.of k)` a
commutative Hopf algebra. `(hopfSpec (CommRingCat.of k)).obj (op A)` is its spectrum as a
group scheme over `Spec k` (Mathlib's `hopfSpec`).

## Geometric inputs

- `smooth_hopfSpec_iff`: `Spec A → Spec k` is smooth iff `A` is a smooth `k`-algebra
  (`Algebra.Smooth`). This is the Library's `smooth_algSpec_iff` for the underlying
  algebra.
- `isDomain_of_hopfSpec`: if `Spec A` is smooth and connected, `A` is a domain. Smoothness
  gives finite type, hence Noetherian. The Library's `isIntegral_of_smooth` gives an
  integral scheme, and Mathlib's `affine_isIntegral_iff` gives a domain.
- `hasSplitSeries_zero_of_forall_mem_range`: if `A` is nontrivial and every element of
  `A` is a scalar, then `HasSplitSeries 0` holds for `Spec A`, i.e.
  `SubgroupFunctor.range (𝟙 _) = ⊥`. The algebra map `k → A` is bijective, so the
  structure morphism `Spec A → Spec k` is an isomorphism. Hence any two points over a
  test scheme agree, and every point is the identity.

## Inheritance by quotients

Let `J` be an ideal of a `k`-bialgebra `A` whose scalar restriction is a coideal.

- `quot_h0`: if every degree-`0` element of the augmentation ideal of `A` lies in its
  square, then the same holds for `A ⧸ J` with the quotient family
  `quotGrade 𝒜 J (AddMonoidHom.id ℤ)`. The image of `I²` is the square of the image of
  `I`, which lies in the augmentation ideal of the quotient.
- `GradedMatrixCoefficients.quot J G`: the images `mk (M i j)` of the matrix
  coefficients of `G` are matrix coefficients for the quotient family, with the same
  weights. Their comultiplication and counit are computed by Mathlib's
  `Bialgebra.Quotient.comul_mk` and `counit_mk`. They generate the quotient because the
  quotient map is surjective. `GradedMatrixCoefficients.quot_M` records the entries.

## The theorem

`isSplitUnipotent_of_graded A 𝒜 h𝒜 h0 G hdiag hlow hSmooth hConn`. Let `𝒜 : ℤ →
Submodule k A` be a grading (`GradedAlgebra 𝒜`) with `h𝒜 : IsGradedBialgebra 𝒜`, and
`h0`: every degree-`0` element of the augmentation ideal lies in its square. Let
`G : GradedMatrixCoefficients 𝒜` (from `BigCell`) have unit diagonal (`hdiag`) and zero
entries below the diagonal (`hlow`). Suppose `Spec A` is smooth (`hSmooth`) and its
underlying space is connected (`hConn`). Then `Spec A` is split unipotent
(`RelativeRoot.IsSplitUnipotent`, the Library's finite normal `𝔾ₐ`-series).

Proof (as implemented): strong induction on the dimension `N` of the tangent space
`epsDer k A` at the counit (from `Section`).

- `A` is a domain (`isDomain_of_hopfSpec`), formally smooth and of finite type
  (`smooth_hopfSpec_iff`).
- If every element is a scalar, `hasSplitSeries_zero_of_forall_mem_range` gives a series
  of length `0`.
- Otherwise `exists_splitDatum` gives:
  - a homogeneous primitive `g` of nonzero degree `d`;
  - `s : A →ₐ[k] k[X]` with `s g = X`;
  - the strict decrease of the tangent dimension modulo any coideal containing `g`.
- Worker 1419's `splitStep A hg s hs hSmooth hConn` shows that
  `Spec (A ⧸ (g)) = Spec (HopfPrimitive.quotient A hg)` is smooth and connected, and that
  a series of length `n` for it extends to one of length `n + 1` for `Spec A`.
- The ideal `(g)` is homogeneous, as it is generated by a homogeneous element. So
  `quotGradedAlgebra` grades the quotient, `IsGradedBialgebra.quot` makes it a graded
  bialgebra, `quot_h0` gives `h0`, and `G.quot` gives unitriangular matrix coefficients.
  Its tangent dimension is smaller than `N`, so the induction hypothesis gives a series
  for the quotient, and `splitStep` extends it.
