# Existence of local base-ring approximation systems (base step of Stacks 00QV)

The module defines
`Langlands.Algebra.LocalRingApproximationExistenceStatement.{u,v} : Prop`.
It states that every commutative local ring is approximated, in the sense of
the existing `LocalAlgebraSystem.IsApproximation`, by a directed system of
local rings essentially of finite type over the integers, over the identity
algebra `R → R`. The file supplies a proposition for a later proof; it does
not prove existence.

## Source and reading

The registered original is
`stacks-algebra-flatness-criteria-2026-10-22`, content SHA256
`4bc1f2db6141508f6dad2132d4abc7963380cd71497ad20620c0e511b972a949`.
The source folder is
`Stacks Project - Algebra flatness criteria and essentially finite presentation (HTML snapshot 2026-10-22)`.

The proof of Lemma 10.127.11 (tag
[00QV](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00QV), file `00QV.html`) says:
"Write R in any way as a colimit R = colim R_λ over a directed set (Λ, ≤),
with each R_λ local and essentially of finite type over **Z**." The source
does not state this step as a separate lemma. Its construction appears in
the proofs of Lemmas 10.127.9 (tag 00QT) and 10.127.10 (tag 00QU) in
`00QL.html`. For a finite subset `A ⊂ R`, `R'_λ` is the sub-**Z**-algebra
generated by `A`, and `R_λ` is the localization of `R'_λ` at the prime
`R'_λ ∩ 𝔪`. The proof of 10.127.10 notes that all maps `R_λ → R` are
injective and that every element of `R` is eventually in the image. Remark
10.127.12 (tag 00QW, `00QL.html`) was also read. It concerns the choice of
the algebra components `S_λ` when `S ≠ R`, so it places no constraint on the
identity specialization used here.

## Hypotheses and quantifiers

The statement universally quantifies `R : Type u` together with
`CommRing R` and `IsLocalRing R`. There is no other hypothesis on `R`.

The conclusion existentially quantifies an index type `ι : Type (max u v)`,
a preorder on it, `Nonempty ι`, `IsDirectedOrder ι`, and a
`D : LocalAlgebraSystem.{max u v, max u v, max u v, u, u} ι R R`
satisfying `D.IsApproximation`. The preorder and its two properties are
witnesses, not assumptions imposed on the caller. A directed preorder
presents the source's directed set. Antisymmetry is not needed for the
transition maps or their composition laws, and nonemptiness is stated
explicitly.

The structure `LocalAlgebraSystem` and the predicate `IsApproximation` come
from the published module `Langlands.Algebra.LocalApproximationSystem` and
are imported unchanged.

## Identity specialization

The algebra structure `Algebra R R` is the canonical one, `Algebra.id R`,
found by instance search. Its algebra map is `RingHom.id R` by definition
(`Algebra.algebraMap_self`, proved by `rfl`).

`LocalAlgebraSystem ι R R` also requires an instance
`IsLocalHom (algebraMap R R)`. Instance search does not find it, because
Mathlib's `isLocalHom_id R` is stated for `RingHom.id R`. The statement
therefore installs it locally with
`haveI : IsLocalHom (algebraMap R R) := isLocalHom_id R`. The two types agree
definitionally. `haveI` is inlined during elaboration, so the elaborated
body has no `let` or `have` binder; a private `#print` check confirmed this.
`IsLocalHom` is a `Prop`-valued class, so by proof irrelevance every proof of
it is definitionally equal to this one. The choice therefore adds no
assumption and leaves no data to choose.

Since `S = R` and `algebraMap R R` is the identity, the structure's
commuting equation `ι_comm` says
`D.algι i (algebraMap (D.base i) (D.alg i) r) = D.baseι i r`.
The two cocones are separate fields that both land in `R`. The statement
does not require `D.alg i` to equal `D.base i`.

## The four conclusion clauses

The conclusion is exactly the existing `LocalAlgebraSystem.IsApproximation`
for the identity algebra.

| Clause | Lean condition and meaning |
| --- | --- |
| (1) Colimit | `D.IsColimit`: the canonical ring homomorphisms `D.baseColimitMap` and `D.algebraColimitMap` from the actual `Ring.DirectLimit` constructions to `R` are both bijective. |
| (2) Bases | `∀ i, Algebra.EssFiniteType ℤ (D.base i)`, using the canonical integer algebra structure. |
| (3) Components | `∀ i, Algebra.EssFiniteType (D.base i) (D.alg i)`, using the structure's component algebra instance. |
| (4) Transitions | `∀ i j (h : i ≤ j), D.IsPrimeLocalization h`: with the algebra structure `(D.ringBaseChangeMap h).toAlgebra`, there is an actual prime ideal `p` of `D.alg i ⊗[D.base i] D.base j` with `IsLocalization.AtPrime (D.alg j) p`. |

Here `D.ringBaseChangeMap h` sends `s ⊗ r` to
`D.algMap i j h s * algebraMap (D.base j) (D.alg j) r`, and `D.base j` is a
`D.base i`-algebra via `D.baseMap i j h`.

## Intended construction

This section describes how a later proof is expected to choose the
witnesses. It is not part of the proposition.

- The index is the finite subsets of `R` (`Finset R`), ordered by inclusion
  and lifted to `Type (max u v)`. It is nonempty and directed, since unions
  are upper bounds.
- `D.base A` is the localization of the subring of `R` generated by `A` at
  the contraction of the maximal ideal. Concretely, it is the subring of
  fractions `a / s` with `a` and `s` in the generated subring and `s` a unit
  of `R`. It is local, and the inclusion into `R` is a local homomorphism.
- `D.alg A = D.base A`, with the identity component algebra `Algebra.id`.
  `structure_local` holds by `isLocalHom_id`. Both transition maps are the
  subring inclusions, and both cocones are the inclusions into `R`.
- Clause (1) holds because every inclusion into `R` is injective and every
  `r ∈ R` lies in the stage `{r}`.
- Clause (2) holds because each base is a localization of a finitely
  generated `ℤ`-algebra.
- Clause (3) holds trivially, since every ring is of finite type over itself.
- Clause (4): for `A ⊆ B`, the map
  `D.base A ⊗[D.base A] D.base B → D.base B` is the canonical isomorphism
  `s ⊗ r ↦ s * r`. The tensor product is therefore local. A local ring is its
  own localization at its maximal ideal, so the map is the localization at
  the maximal ideal of the tensor product, which is also the pullback of the
  maximal ideal of `D.base B`.

Clause (4) holds for any choice of bases once the algebra components equal
the bases and the component algebras are the identity. In that case
`map_comm` forces the two transition maps to agree. Remark 00QW does not
apply here.

The published module `Langlands.Algebra.LocalAlgebraApproximationExistence`
already defines finite-set subrings
`LocalAlgebraApproximationExistence.gen C A` and their unit-fraction
localizations `LocalAlgebraApproximationExistence.locSub C A`. It proves
these are local, have local inclusions into `R`, and are essentially of
finite type over `ℤ`. A later proof may reuse them, for example with
`C = ∅`. That module also builds the concrete system attached to a
presentation and proves the conditional assembly
`LocalAlgebraApproximationExistence.exists_isApproximation`. That theorem
still takes the prime-localization clause for those systems as its
hypothesis `h4`. The unconditional Stacks 00QV result is
`Langlands.Algebra.localAlgebraApproximationExistence`, in the proof
companion `Langlands.Algebra.LocalAlgebraApproximationExistenceProof`. It
proves `LocalAlgebraApproximationExistenceStatement` by supplying `h4` from
`LocalAlgebraApproximationExistence.Pres.isPrimeLocalization_system` in
`Langlands.Algebra.LocalAlgebraApproximationTransition`. This proposition is
the base-ring step of the source argument and is not meant to be derived
from 00QV.

## Internal universe packaging

The proposition has exactly the universe parameters `u` and `v`. The carrier
`R` stays in `Type u`, and the system's ring universes `uR` and `uS` are both
`u`. The index, every `D.base i` and every `D.alg i` lie in `Type (max u v)`.
The auxiliary universe `v` only allows larger existential witnesses. It does
not change `R` or add an assumption on `R`.

The intended witnesses live in `Type u`: `Finset R` and subrings of `R`. A
later proof must raise them to `Type (max u v)`, for example with `ULift`.
That includes transporting the ring and local-ring structures, the maps, the
colimit comparisons, the essential finite type properties and the
prime-localization property. This statement does not implement that
transport.

## Provenance and scope

The mathematical assertion comes from the source: the colimit presentation
in the proof of 00QV, constructed as in the proofs of 00QT and 00QU. The
following are internal formalization choices, not printed in the source:

- a standalone proposition for the identity algebra;
- the reuse of the four-clause `LocalAlgebraSystem.IsApproximation` with
  `S = R`;
- the directed-preorder packaging;
- the explicit comparison maps;
- the maximum-universe witnesses.

The imported `LocalApproximationSystem` Lean/Markdown pair is unchanged.
This module adds no new ring-system structure. It includes no construction
for `S ≠ R`, no module system, no compatible-map or tower existence, and no
flatness statement.
