# A commutative subfield with the full residue field

Let `K` be a nonarchimedean local field with its supplied valuative relation and
compatible topology, and let `D` be a finite-dimensional central division
`K`-algebra. The carriers have the same arbitrary universe `u`, and the
characteristic is unrestricted. Write

```
v = localReducedNormValuation K D,
R = DivisionValuation.Residue v.
```

The residue is the actual integer-subring quotient by the strict-value ideal.
An auxiliary embedded field `L` may have an independent arbitrary universe
`uL`. All declarations are in namespace `Langlands.CentralDivision`.

## Restriction to an embedded field

For an actual `K`-algebra embedding `ι : L →ₐ[K] D`,
`localDivisionSubfieldValuation K D ι` is `v.comap ι.toRingHom`. Its value at
`x : L` is exactly `v (ι x)`, as recorded by
`localDivisionSubfieldValuation_apply`.

The map `localDivisionSubfieldIntegerMap K D ι` sends an integral `x` to the
original element `ι x`, regarded as an integral element of `D`.
The ring homomorphism `localDivisionSubfieldResidueMap K D ι` descends this
inclusion to the actual residue quotients:

```
DivisionValuation.Residue (localDivisionSubfieldValuation K D ι) →+* R.
```

The theorem `localDivisionSubfieldResidueMap_reduction` states its compatibility
with reduction of each original integral element. The theorem
`localDivisionSubfieldResidueMap_injective` proves injectivity. Neither
finite dimensionality of `L` nor surjectivity of a valuation onto its stated
value group is assumed for these restriction constructions.

The theorem `localDivisionSubfieldResidueMap_surjective` has the following
explicit additional inputs:

- A unit `a : v.integerˣ`.
- Membership of the original element `(a.val : D)` in the image of `ι`.
- The generator property that every `z : R` is zero or the reduction of
  `a.val ^ n` for some `n : ℕ`.

It proves surjectivity of the same residue homomorphism.
The definition `localDivisionSubfieldResidueEquiv` uses injectivity and this
surjectivity to construct an isomorphism of the actual residue rings.
The isomorphism's forward map is precisely the map induced by the original
inclusion, rather than an arbitrarily chosen isomorphism of finite fields.

## A field generated by a residue lift

For `a : D`, `residueGeneratorSubalgebra K D a` is the original subalgebra
`Algebra.adjoin K {a}`. The instance
`residueGeneratorSubalgebra_commutative` proves that its original multiplication
is commutative. The explicit instance-reducible definition
`residueGeneratorField K D a` gives this same subalgebra a field structure.
The theorem `residueGenerator_mem` records that `a` lies in this subalgebra.

The theorem `exists_residueGeneratorSubfield` chooses an actual unit
`a : v.integerˣ` for which the field `Algebra.adjoin K {(a.val : D)}` has
surjective residue map to `R`, with valuation restricted along its original
subalgebra inclusion. Injectivity holds by the general restriction theorem.
There is no extra premise about a generator, subfield, or its residue in this
existence theorem: the preceding residue-generator result supplies the unit.

## Implemented proofs and internal provenance

These are internal consequences of the imported actual residue and generator
constructions and Mathlib's valuation restriction, quotient universal property,
commutativity of a singly generated algebra, and finite-dimensional field
construction.

Restricting the valuation means that an element of `L` is integral precisely
when its image is integral in `D`. It also means that its value is strictly
less than one precisely when its image has that property. Thus reduction
after integral inclusion annihilates the original strict-value ideal.
`Ideal.Quotient.lift` gives the residue map. Its compatibility with reduction
holds by definition. Since its source is a division ring, this unital ring
homomorphism is injective.

For surjectivity, take a preimage `b : L` of the integral unit `a`. Zero
is in the image. Any other residue element is the reduction of `a.val ^ n`.
Then `b ^ n` is integral because its original image is that integral power.
Compatibility of the residue map with reduction and the power law for `ι`
give the desired preimage. `RingEquiv.ofBijective` packages the resulting
isomorphism.

The elements of a singleton commute pairwise, so Mathlib makes its generated
subalgebra commutative with its original ring operations. As a subalgebra of
the finite-dimensional division algebra, it is an integral domain and is
finite-dimensional over `K`. `fieldOfFiniteDimensional` extends these operations
to a field; no algebra structure making this field central in `D` is installed
or assumed. Finally, choose the actual residue-generator lift from
`exists_localDivisionResidue_generator K D`. It lies in its generated
subalgebra, so the preceding surjectivity result applies to the inclusion.

## Scope

The constructed commutative subfield has the full actual residue field of `D`.
The theorem does not compute its degree or ramification, assert separability
or cyclicity, construct a field of reduced degree, or prove local SK1 or
the local Kneser–Tits theorem.
