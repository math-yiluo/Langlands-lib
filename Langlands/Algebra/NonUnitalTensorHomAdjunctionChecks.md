# Interface and scope checks for the original-action tensor-Hom targets

This internal check module is part of statement task 7b5b4b80-c62b-4354-937d-bd4f23408fb6. It checks interfaces and scope; it does not prove the principal tensor-Hom equivalences, their naturality or their nondegenerate-part refinements. Worker 345 authored the checks against Worker 702's statement draft. The governing source and the distinction from Renard's complex-linear unitized theorem and complex nondegenerate corollary are documented in `NonUnitalTensorHomAdjunctionStatements.md`.

For both handed families, complete unapplied signature checks cover `Canonical`, `ExistsCanonical`, `Naturality`, `RefinedCanonical`, `ExistsRefinedCanonical` and `RefinedNaturality`. The rings and the six source/target carrier universes are independent. Each signature is checked generically and with the alternating assignments `(0,1,0,1,0,1,0,1)` and `(1,0,1,0,1,0,1,0)` in the order R, S, X, Y, Z, X', Y', Z'. Closed component targets and their combined target are checked as unapplied propositions at the same levels, with exact definitional unfolding into their complete quantified conclusions.

Exact definitional equalities also check the canonical forward evaluation, entire published inverse lift, pure-tensor inverse evaluation and both whole-map inverse identities; existential packaging; actual nondegenerate part and inherited original Hom; actual inclusion postcomposition; the published one-sided tensor and Hom natural maps and the actual restriction to the nondegenerate parts; and both directions of whole-map naturality and ambient/refined compatibility. All these checks concern the supplied definitions, not proofs of their conclusions.


The competing-structure sections give two ring structures on each scalar carrier and two additive group structures on every carrier, along with competing original actions and competing groups and residual actions on the actual tensor and inner Hom. Complete definitional equalities identify the explicitly named original `TensorHom` and `CurriedHom` carriers, their pointwise groups, the actual `HomPart`, the inherited refined Hom and its group, the existential core conclusion and the actual inclusion map. Every original ring, group and action is supplied explicitly in these checks; competing instances cannot select replacement data.

Both closed core existence targets specialize to zero original actions on the nonzero integer groups, to subsingleton carriers, and to the regular left and right integer actions. These are conditional specializations of the target statements, not proofs of the tensor-Hom conclusions. In the zero-action case the actual inner Hom contains its nonzero identity, and the actual quotient tensor contains a nonzero pure tensor: the published quotient lift of integer multiplication detects the pure tensor of `(1,1)`. Direct contradictions at `1` verify that neither original zero action satisfies a unit law or nondegeneracy despite the scalar ring being unital. These elementary diagnostics are intrinsic checks, not claims from the source literature.
