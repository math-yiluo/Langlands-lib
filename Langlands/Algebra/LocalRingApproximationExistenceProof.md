## The system

The construction first works with any ring equivalence `e : R₀ ≃+* R`
between commutative local rings. All helper declarations below lie in
`Langlands.Algebra.LocalRingApproximationExistence`.

The index is `Finset R₀`, ordered by inclusion. It is nonempty because
it contains the empty set, and directed because finite unions are upper
bounds. The stage `B i` is the published
`LocalAlgebraApproximationExistence.locSub ∅ i`: the subring of `R₀`
whose elements satisfy `r * s = a`, where `a` and `s` lie in the subring
generated by `i` and `s` is a unit of `R₀`. The published localization
and local-ring instances make this the local subring obtained by
inverting the elements outside the contracted maximal ideal.

In `system e`, both `base i` and `alg i` are `B i`, with the identity
component algebra `Algebra.id (B i)`. Both transition maps are the
subring inclusion `transition h`, obtained from `locSub_mono ∅ h`.
Its composite with the later subring inclusion into `R₀` is the
earlier inclusion. The published local-map instance for these subrings
and `isLocalHom_of_comp` prove `transition_local`.

Both cocones are `cocone e i`, the composite of the subring inclusion
with `e`. The equivalence is local by
`IsLocalHom.of_surjective`, and the cocone is local by composition.
The identity component map is local by `isLocalHom_id`. The identity,
composition and commuting-square laws in the system hold
definitionally for these inclusions and identity algebras.

## The four approximation clauses

`system_isColimit` proves bijectivity of the actual
`LocalAlgebraSystem.baseColimitMap`. Injectivity follows from
`Ring.DirectLimit.lift_injective`: each cocone map is the composite
of an injective subtype inclusion and the injective equivalence `e`.
For surjectivity, `r : R` is represented by `e.symm r` at the singleton
index `{e.symm r}`. The published
`mem_locSub_of_mem_right` supplies stage membership, and
`baseColimitMap_of` computes the image. The algebra diagram and cocone
are identical to the base diagram and cocone, so the same two proofs
also establish bijectivity of the actual `algebraColimitMap`.

For the base finiteness clause, `essFiniteType_locSub ∅ i` proves that
each `B i` is essentially of finite type over the integers. For the
component clause, each ring is of finite type over itself with its
identity algebra, hence essentially of finite type; Mathlib supplies
these instances.

For a transition `h : i ≤ j`, `ringBaseChangeMap_eq_lid` installs the
algebra on the later base induced by the actual `baseMap`. It proves
equality of the actual `ringBaseChangeMap h` with the ring homomorphism
underlying `Algebra.TensorProduct.lid (B i) (B j)`. The proof uses
Mathlib's tensor ring-homomorphism extensionality: on the first tensor
inclusion both maps send `s` to `transition h s * 1`; on the second,
the computation rule for `ringBaseChangeMap` and the tensor identity
rule give the same image of `r`. Consequently the actual map is
bijective.

`system_isPrimeLocalization` then installs exactly
`((system e).ringBaseChangeMap h).toAlgebra`. It chooses the prime
ideal given by the inverse image of the maximal ideal of `B j`
under that actual map. It applies the published
`Langlands.Algebra.isLocalization_atPrime_of_fractions` with this
algebra. Surjectivity gives a numerator representing every element,
with denominator `1`; injectivity gives the kernel condition, again
with denominator `1`. This proves `IsLocalization.AtPrime` for the
induced algebra required by `IsPrimeLocalization`, rather than for an
independently chosen map or algebra structure.

`system_isApproximation` assembles these four clauses.

## Universes and scope

The closing theorem takes `R₀ = ULift.{v} R` and `e = ULift.ringEquiv`.
The published `LocalAlgebraApproximationExistence.isLocalRing_ulift`
supplies its local-ring instance. The index
`Finset (ULift.{v} R)` and both component families are in
`Type (max u v)`. The ambient ring in both cocones is the original
`R : Type u`; its algebra over itself is canonical, with the
internally installed `isLocalHom_id R` proof from the target.
The theorem retains the two independent universe parameters `u` and
`v` and adds no assumptions on `R`.

This result is only the independent identity local-ring construction.
It contains no whole-system construction for a different target
algebra, no module approximation system, no compatible-map or tower
existence assertion, and no flatness conclusion.
