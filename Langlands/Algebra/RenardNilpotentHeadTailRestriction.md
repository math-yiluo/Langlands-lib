## Original objects and scope

All new declarations are in `Langlands.Renard.NilpotentFiltration`.
The carrier is an arbitrary `V : Type u` with `AddCommGroup V` and
`Module ℂ V`. The published import
`Langlands.Algebra.RenardNilpotentIdealFiltration` supplies, unchanged,
the actual algebra
`generatedAlgebra a = Algebra.adjoin ℂ (Set.range a)`, its embedded
generators, their ideal and the original-action subspace
`idealPowerSubspace a 1 = I V`.

The head and tail definitions take `a : Fin (l + 1) → Module.End ℂ V`
for any natural `l`. This is the source's nonempty list, with head
`a 0` and remaining list `Fin.tail a`. In this parameterization the source
has `l + 1` counted generators and `l` tail generators.
Thus Lean's `a 0` is the source's `a_1`, and `Fin.tail a` lists the
source's `a_2, …, a_(l+1)`.

- `tailAlgebra a : Subalgebra ℂ (generatedAlgebra a)` is
  `Algebra.adjoin ℂ (Set.range (Fin.tail (generator a)))`. It is the actual
  subalgebra `R'` inside `R`, generated by the remaining original
  endomorphisms and the identity. Scalars and the identity add no tuple
  entry.
- `headIdeal a : Ideal (generatedAlgebra a)` is
  `Ideal.span {generator a 0}`, the actual ideal `R''` generated by the
  first listed endomorphism inside `R`.
- `headIdealSubspace a` is that ideal with scalars restricted to `ℂ`,
  hence a complex subspace of the original `R`.

Mathlib's `Ideal` is a left ideal for an arbitrary ring. Under the source
hypothesis that the original tuple commutes, the published
`generatedAlgebra_isMulCommutative` supplies commutativity of `R`;
the head ideal is then the usual two-sided source ideal. The definitions
themselves need no commutation, nilpotence or finite-dimensionality
premise. This wider construction domain is internal to the formalization.

## Actual action on the first filtration subspace

For every natural tuple length `l`, including zero,
`firstLevelRestriction a` is the algebra homomorphism

`generatedAlgebra a →ₐ[ℂ] Module.End ℂ (idealPowerSubspace a 1)`.

It reuses the published generic construction
`Langlands.CommutingDimension.restrictionAlgHom` with stability supplied by
`idealPowerSubspace_stable`. It restricts each original endomorphism to
the actual invariant subspace `V_1 = I V`. No replacement carrier, action,
chosen invariant subspace or assumed action map is introduced.

`firstLevelTuple a i` is `firstLevelRestriction a (generator a i)`.
`firstLevelAlgebra a` is the actual unital adjoin of this restricted tuple
inside `Module.End ℂ (idealPowerSubspace a 1)`. Restriction here has both
domain and codomain `V_1`. It differs from restriction to a complement
`W`, whose values lie in `V` and which need not be invariant.

The constructions retain arbitrary carrier universes, zero-dimensional
spaces, empty tuples for the first-level action, single-generator lists
with empty tail, and zero, repeated or redundant entries. No positivity,
nontriviality, basis or maximizing-witness hypothesis is added. The
first filtration subspace has its inherited complex-module structure and
is finite-dimensional whenever `V` is, although finite-dimensionality is
unnecessary for the declarations in this module.

## Intrinsic laws and their implemented proofs

`tailAlgebra_map` identifies the image of the internal tail algebra under
the original inclusion with `generatedAlgebra (Fin.tail a)`. It uses
`AlgHom.map_adjoin` and the equality between the image of a range and the
range of the composite; the resulting generators are definitionally the
original tail. `generator_zero_mem_headIdeal` inserts the head into the
span of its singleton.

`firstLevelRestriction_apply` and `firstLevelTuple_apply` are reflexive
equalities: after inclusion in `V`, the restricted maps evaluate the
original endomorphisms. `firstLevelAlgebra_eq_range` reuses the published
generic laws `CommutingDimension.adjoin_generators_top` and
`CommutingDimension.adjoin_image_generators` to identify the actual
restricted adjoin with the image of the restriction homomorphism.
These helpers are imported by Lean name from
`Langlands.Algebra.CommutingDimensionRecurrence`, unchanged.

`firstLevelTuple_commute` lifts original commutation through the injective
inclusion of `R` in the original endomorphisms and maps it along the
restriction homomorphism. `firstLevelTuple_isNilpotent` similarly lifts
nilpotence by `IsNilpotent.map_iff` and maps it along restriction. Each
law assumes exactly the named property of the original tuple; no property
of a supplied restricted tuple is assumed. The generic constructions and
laws reused here belong to the published commuting-dimension
infrastructure; this pair supplies their original-object specialization.
Worker 307 authored that imported infrastructure; importing the published
lemmas does not attribute their original proofs to this pair's authors.

These compatibility laws are internal construction facts. This module
does not assert `R = R' + R''`, a dimension bound on either summand, a
comparison of `dim R''` with the restriction-image dimension, the bound on
the maximum using a complement, or the floor recurrence. Those source
assertions require separate statement and proof units. The actual
maximum, attainment, endpoint and complement statements, numerical base
cases, carrier transport, monotonicity and the rest of the whole-book
program retain their existing owners and obligations.
