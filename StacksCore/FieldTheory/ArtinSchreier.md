# Artin–Schreier extensions

Module `StacksCore.FieldTheory.ArtinSchreier`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

The file studies the Artin–Schreier polynomial `X^p − X − a` over a ring or field of characteristic `p` (a prime, supplied as `Fact p.Prime`). It proves that the polynomial is irreducible over a field when `a` is not of the form `x^p − x`, and that every Galois extension of degree `p` of a field of characteristic `p` is generated by a root of such a polynomial.

## The polynomial

- `X^p − X − C r` is monic of degree `p` (`monic_X_pow_sub_X_sub_C`, `degree_X_pow_sub_X_sub_C` and variants).
- `X_pow_sub_X_sub_C_eq_prod`: in a commutative ring of characteristic `p`, `X^p − X − (a^p − a) = ∏ i ∈ range p, (X − (a + i))`, by base change from the universal identity over `(ZMod p)[X]`. Element versions: `pow_sub_sub_pow_sub_eq_prod`, and `exists_eq_add_of_pow_sub_eq_pow_sub` (in a domain, `a^p − a = b^p − b` forces `a = b + i` with `i < p`).
- `irreducible_X_pow_sub_X_sub_C`: over a field of characteristic `p`, if `x^p ≠ x + a` for all `x`, the polynomial is irreducible. A monic proper factor is a product of some `X − (b + i)`; its degree is prime to `p`, so its second coefficient puts `b` in `F`. The helper `Associated.of_mul_prod` splits factorizations into primes.

## Minimal polynomials

- `minpoly_eq_X_pow_sub_X_sub_C`: if `b^p = b + a` and `x^p ≠ x + a` for all `x ∈ F`, the minimal polynomial of `b` over `F` is `X^p − X − C a`; the primed variant replaces the second hypothesis by `b ∉ F`.
- `exists_minpoly_eq_X_pow_sub_X_sub_C_of_apply_eq_add_one`: if `σ ∈ Gal(K/F)` satisfies `σ b = b + 1` and the fixed field of `⟨σ⟩` is `F`, the minimal polynomial of `b` is Artin–Schreier.
- `sub_one_pow_char_sub_one`: `(a − 1)^(p−1) = ∑ i ∈ range p, a^i` in characteristic `p`.

## The main theorem

- `IsGalois.exists_artinSchreier_of_finrank_eq_char`: for a finite Galois extension `K/F` of degree `p = char F`, there is `b ∈ K` with `F(b) = K` and `b^p − b ∈ F`.

Proof: pick `w` of trace `1`; the Galois group is cyclic with generator `σ`, and the trace is `∑ σ^i = (σ − 1)^(p−1)`. Then `b = (σ − 1)^(p−2) w` satisfies `σ b = b + 1`, so its minimal polynomial is Artin–Schreier of degree `p`.
