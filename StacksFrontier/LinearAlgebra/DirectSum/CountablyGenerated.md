# Countably generated decompositions of direct summands

Module `StacksFrontier.LinearAlgebra.DirectSum.CountablyGenerated`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

Over a ring R, a direct summand P of an internal direct sum of countably generated modules is itself an internal direct sum of countably generated modules.

`Submodule.exists_sSupIndep_countable_span_of_isCompl` takes an independent set of submodules of M with supremum ⊤, each spanned by a countable set, and a complement Q to P in M. It gives an independent set of submodules of P with supremum ⊤, each spanned by a countable subset of P. The construction is proved in `Stacks.DirectSum.KaplanskySummand.solution`. Lattice lemmas handle independence and complements along the chain used in the proof.

Under the projection onto P along Q, each countable generating set of a summand has countably many supporting indices. Closing sets of indices under these supports gives an exhaustive well-ordered chain of projection-stable partial sums, with countably generated increments over the supremum of the preceding stages. Intersecting with P gives a chain whose increments are generated by the projected generators. Choosing complements in that chain yields independent countably generated submodules that span P.

The theorem allows noncommutative rings. The input summand relation is `IsCompl P Q` in the submodule lattice, and the output decomposition is internal to the module carried by P. Countability applies to the generating set of each summand, while the family of summands may be uncountable.

Tag 058X is the direct-summand theorem. Tag 058U and Tag 058W describe the filtration and internal direct sum in the source argument. Tag 058Y and Tag 0590 are cited as applications. The complement lemma references SGA 4 IV 9.1.13(c). The file also cites I. Kaplansky, *Projective modules*, Ann. of Math. (2) 68 (1958), 372–377.
