# Exterior rank and injectivity of matrices

Module `StacksFrontier.LinearAlgebra.Matrix.DeterminantalRank.ExteriorRank`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

A matrix A over a commutative ring R gives a linear map from Rᵐ to Rⁿ. Its exterior powers detect the sizes of nonzero minors and give criteria for the map to be injective.

`Stacks.DeterminantalRank.exteriorRank` takes the greatest degree up to min(m, n) whose induced exterior-power map is nonzero. `Stacks.DeterminantalRank.exteriorPower_map_ne_zero_iff_exists_minor` identifies nonvanishing in degree r with the existence of a nonzero r × r minor. `Stacks.DeterminantalRank.detIdeal` is generated by the minors of size equal to the exterior rank.

Over a local ring, `Stacks.DeterminantalRank.injective_iff_rank_eq_and_annihilator_eq_bot` says that injectivity is equivalent to exterior rank m and zero annihilator of the determinantal ideal. Over a Noetherian local ring, `Stacks.DeterminantalRank.injective_iff_rank_eq_and_contains_nonZeroDivisor` replaces the annihilator condition with the ideal being the whole ring or containing a nonzerodivisor.

The wedge-basis matrix entries are the minors indexed by increasing row and column choices. At full column rank, adjugates make the maximal minors annihilate the kernel. For the converse, the McCoy argument takes the smallest minor size with a nonzero common annihilator and uses cofactors of a bordered minor to produce a nonzero kernel vector. The Noetherian reformulation uses associated primes and prime avoidance.

The bundled injectivity criterion assumes locality. Its forward implication `Stacks.DeterminantalRank.rank_eq_and_annihilator_eq_bot_of_injective` uses only nontriviality, and its reverse implication `Stacks.DeterminantalRank.injective_of_rank_eq_of_annihilator_eq_bot` works over any commutative ring. The rank definition defaults to zero over the zero ring, where even the degree-zero exterior-power map vanishes.

The definitions correspond to Tag 00MV, and the file also cites Tag 00MX for the injectivity criteria.
