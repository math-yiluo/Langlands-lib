# Determinantal rank of matrices and the ideal of minors

Module `StacksFrontier.LinearAlgebra.Matrix.DeterminantalRank.ComplexRank`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

The file defines the rank of a matrix over a commutative ring by its minors, and the ideal generated by the minors in that rank, and proves that both are unchanged by invertible multiplication and by reindexing.

A map φ : R^m → R^n is its matrix `Matrix (Fin n) (Fin m) R`. `StacksComplex.rankSet φ` is the set of sizes r for which some r × r minor is nonzero, `StacksComplex.rank φ` is its supremum (`StacksComplex.rank_eq_sSup`), and `StacksComplex.minorIdeal φ` is generated by the minors of size `rank φ`. Over a nontrivial ring the set contains 0, the empty minor having determinant 1 (`StacksComplex.zero_mem_rankSet`), so the ideal in rank zero is the whole ring. The set is bounded by both matrix dimensions (`StacksComplex.rankSet_le_n`, `StacksComplex.rankSet_le_m`), the supremum is attained (`StacksComplex.rank_mem_rankSet`), `StacksComplex.rank_le_m` bounds the rank by the number of columns, and every minor of size above the rank vanishes (`StacksComplex.minor_eq_zero_of_rank_lt`).

Two forms of the Cauchy–Binet formula, `StacksComplex.det_mul_eq_sum_det_submatrix_mul_prod` and `StacksComplex.det_mul_eq_sum_prod_mul_det_submatrix`, expand the determinant of the product of an r × n and an n × r matrix as a sum over functions Fin r → Fin n. They give the minors of A φ and of φ B as combinations of minors of φ (`StacksComplex.minor_mul_left`, `StacksComplex.minor_mul_right`), so multiplying on either side does not raise the rank.

`StacksComplex.rank_conj_eq` preserves rank under left and right multiplication when the left factor has a specified left inverse and the right factor a specified right inverse; the reverse inequality comes from multiplying back by the inverses. `StacksComplex.minorIdeal_mul_left` and `StacksComplex.minorIdeal_mul_right` give the same for the ideal of minors under one-sided multiplication. Reindexing rows and columns by bijections Fin n′ ≃ Fin n and Fin m′ ≃ Fin m changes neither the rank nor the ideal (`StacksComplex.rank_submatrix_equiv`, `StacksComplex.minorIdeal_submatrix_equiv`).

The module docstring and the empty section headings also announce complexes of finite free modules, their isomorphisms, the integers r_i, trivial complexes and invariance under isomorphism of complexes; none of these is in the file, which holds only the matrix part, under the namespace `StacksComplex`.
