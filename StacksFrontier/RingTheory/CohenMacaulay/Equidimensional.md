# Dimension lemmas for equidimensional affine algebras

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.CohenMacaulay.Equidimensional`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

The file proves two dimension lemmas, used in cutting a Cohen–Macaulay equidimensional algebra of finite type over a field down by a sequence of elements.

- `Stacks.CohenMacaulay.EquidimensionalCut.height_le_ringKrullDim_quotient_ofList_add_length` is Krull's height theorem for the ideal generated by a list f in a Noetherian ring R: a prime p containing it satisfies height p ≤ dim(R ⧸ (f)) + length f, read in `WithBot ℕ∞`. It follows from the same bound with the minimal number of generators of the ideal in place of the length, that number being at most the length.
- `Stacks.CohenMacaulay.EquidimensionalCut.localKrullDim_eq_ringKrullDim` says that for a finite-type algebra S over a field k whose spectrum is equidimensional, the topological local dimension of Spec S at every point equals dim S. By Tag 00OT the local dimension at a point is the supremum of the dimensions of the irreducible components through it, which equidimensionality makes independent of the point, and the dimension of the spectrum is the supremum of the local dimensions.

Equidimensionality is the library's `TopologicalSpace.Equidimensional`, applied to `PrimeSpectrum S`. The module docstrings present Tag 00R9, the regular-sequence criterion for such algebras, and Tag 00OV, the decomposition of the spectrum by local dimension, and announce results for them; neither is in the file.
