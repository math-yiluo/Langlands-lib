# Openness of fibrewise regularity

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.Sequence.RegularLocus`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

For a finite type algebra whose nonempty fibres are Cohen–Macaulay and equidimensional of a common dimension, regularity of a list of elements on the local rings of the fibres is open on the common zero locus of the list.

## Results

- `Stacks.RegularSequence.FibrewiseLocus.FibresCM` records the fibre hypothesis for a natural number d. At every prime p of R with nonzero fibre, it requires the fibre ring κ(p) ⊗ S over R to have Cohen–Macaulay local rings and an equidimensional spectrum of Krull dimension d.
- `fiberLocalRing` in the same namespace is S_q/pS_q for p the contraction of q. `localizationFiberPointEquiv` identifies it with the localization of the fibre at the point corresponding to q.
- `isRegular_iff_relativeDimensionAt_add_length_le` characterizes regularity of the image of a list f at a point of Spec(S/I), where I is generated by f. The relative dimension of S/I over R at that point plus the length of f must be at most d.
- `Algebra.isOpen_setOf_isRegular_fiber_of_isMaximalCohenMacaulay_of_equidimensional` gives openness in the subspace V(I) of Spec S under these hypotheses. `Stacks.RegularSequence.FibrewiseLocus.solution` proves the same statement.

The dimension comparison before and after quotienting uses the common residue-field transcendence degree. The Cohen–Macaulay criterion for a regular sequence turns the resulting dimension bound into an equivalence. Upper semicontinuity on Spec(S/I) gives openness, which transfers through the homeomorphism with V(I).

Equidimensionality is the library’s `TopologicalSpace.Equidimensional` on the fibre spectrum, paired with the dimension equality. Empty fibres have dimension ⊥, so the fibre hypothesis is restricted to nonzero fibre rings. The bound uses addition in `WithBot ℕ∞`. For list length at most d it gives the usual sublevel set at d minus that length. For greater lengths the locus is empty.

## References

Tag 00RA gives the openness statement. Tag 00QD defines relative dimension, Tag 00P1 and Tag 00P2 give the dimension comparison, Tag 00KW and Tag 00R9 concern the dimension drop for regular sequences, and Tag 00QH gives semicontinuity.
