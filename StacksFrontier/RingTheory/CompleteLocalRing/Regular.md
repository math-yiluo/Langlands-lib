# Krull dimension of power series rings over a field

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.CompleteLocalRing.Regular`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

For a field k, the file bounds the Krull dimension of the power series ring k[[x_1, …, x_n]] from both sides by n. It also indexes a generating family of a finitely generated submodule by its minimal number of generators.

- `MvPowerSeries.ringKrullDim_le` proves dim k[[x_1, …, x_n]] ≤ n. The maximal ideal is generated by the n variables, being the kernel of the constant coefficient (`MvPowerSeries.span_X_eq_ker_constantCoeff`, from an imported module), and the dimension is at most the number of generators of the maximal ideal.
- `MvPowerSeries.natCast_le_ringKrullDim` proves n ≤ dim k[[x_1, …, x_n]] by induction on n, adjoining one power series variable at a time through `MvPowerSeries.finSuccEquiv`.
- `Stacks.CompleteLocalRing.PowerSeriesRing.exists_fin_generating_family` spans a finitely generated submodule `p` with `p.spanFinrank = n` by the range of a family indexed by `Fin n`.

The two bounds are separate theorems; the file does not state their equality. Its module docstrings describe the theorem of Tag 0C0S, that a complete regular local ring with a coefficient field is a power series ring over it, with its proof and that equality; none of these is in the file.
