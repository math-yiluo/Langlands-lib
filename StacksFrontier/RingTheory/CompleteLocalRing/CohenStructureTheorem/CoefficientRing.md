# Coefficient rings and Cohen power series presentations

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.CompleteLocalRing.CohenStructureTheorem.CoefficientRing`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

A local ring R complete and separated for its maximal ideal 𝔪 has a coefficient ring. A finite generating family for 𝔪 also yields a presentation by a power series ring.

## Main results

- `IsLocalRing.exists_isCoefficientRing_and_exists_surjective_mvPowerSeries` gives a local subring complete for its maximal ideal, inducing an isomorphism on residue fields, with maximal ideal generated by the residue characteristic. If 𝔪 is finitely generated, it also gives a surjection from a power series ring in finitely many variables over a field or a Cohen ring onto R.
- `HasCoefficientRingHom` records a map from such a field or Cohen ring to R that sends nonunits into 𝔪 and is surjective onto the residue field. It specifies that the source maximal ideal is generated by the residue characteristic and that positive residue characteristic calls for a Cohen ring. `hasCoefficientRingHom` constructs this datum under the completeness hypothesis.
- `exists_surjective_eval₂Hom` works for any ideal I of a commutative ring R that is I-adically complete. A coefficient map surjective modulo I extends to a surjective power series evaluation if the variables are sent to a finite generating family of I.

Formal smoothness lifts residue field maps through square-zero steps of the adic tower, using the residue field over ℤ in characteristic zero and the Cohen ring quotients over ℤ/pⁿℤ in positive characteristic. Completeness assembles the lifts into a map whose image is complete by transport along an isomorphism or by nilpotence of its maximal ideal, and the power series map is surjective by adic completeness and surjectivity modulo the variables.

The presentation base lives in the universe of R. Its image is the embedded coefficient ring in the construction and can be a proper quotient of the Cohen ring when p is nilpotent in R.
