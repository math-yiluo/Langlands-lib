# Maximal ideals, completeness, and evaluation of power series

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.CompleteLocalRing.CohenStructureTheorem.PowerSeriesRing`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

The file develops power series results used to construct a regular power series subring of a Noetherian complete local domain. It also provides dimension and adic comparison lemmas for the target ring.

## Main results

- `maximalIdeal_mvPowerSeries` identifies the maximal ideal of a power series ring in d variables over a local ring with the sum of the extended base maximal ideal and the ideal of the variables. `maximalIdeal_powerSeries` gives the one-variable formula. If the base maximal ideal has a generating set s, `spanFinrank_maximalIdeal_mvPowerSeries_le` bounds the number of generators by the size of s plus d, and `ringKrullDim_mvPowerSeries_le` gives the same bound on dimension for a Noetherian base.
- `isAdicComplete_maximalIdeal_powerSeries` and `isAdicComplete_maximalIdeal_mvPowerSeries` give completeness for the maximal ideal over a Noetherian complete local base, in one and in finitely many variables respectively.
- `exists_eval₂Hom` extends a ring map Λ → R by sending finitely many variables to prescribed elements of I when R is I-adically complete. Its conclusion is existence of a ring homomorphism with those values and the specified values on constants.

The ideal comparison lemmas show that quotienting by a nonzerodivisor in the maximal ideal strictly drops dimension in a Noetherian local ring. They also show that a zero-dimensional quotient by a proper ideal forces that ideal to contain a positive power of the maximal ideal, and that completeness passes to such an ideal.

The maximal ideal formula splits off the constant term and uses `mem_span_range_X` for the remainder. The completeness proof uses coefficientwise limits and compares powers of the maximal ideal with the ideals generated by powers of the base maximal ideal and of the variable, then iterates the one-variable result.

The source cites Tag 00KW for the dimension drop, Tag 0319 for adic comparison, and Tag 032D for the application. The table of references below links the maximal ideal formula and the dimension bound to EGA 0 17.1.4.
