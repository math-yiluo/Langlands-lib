# Coefficient rings of a local ring

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.CompleteLocalRing.CoefficientRing`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

A coefficient ring gives a complete local subring of a local ring R with the same residue field as R.

`LocalSubring.IsCoefficientRing` is a predicate on Mathlib’s bundled local subrings Λ of R. It requires the inclusion to be local, so the maximal ideal of Λ is the contraction of the maximal ideal of R. The ring Λ must be complete and separated for powers of that ideal. The induced map on residue fields is required to be surjective and is automatically injective. The contracted ideal must equal the ideal of Λ generated by the image of the characteristic of the residue field of R.

The definition applies to any local R, with completeness required of Λ alone. The characteristic in the last condition may be zero, in which case the maximal ideal of Λ is zero.

The definition corresponds to Tag 0326.
