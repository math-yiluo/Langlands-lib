# Simple residue field extensions and directed colimits of local rings

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.Flat.LocalConstruction`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

This file constructs flat local extensions realizing simple extensions of a residue field, and the directed colimits used to assemble them.

For a local ring R with maximal ideal 𝔪 and a field extension K of its residue field k, `GoodLift R K` consists of a flat local map R → A with maximal ideal 𝔪A and a residue field embedding into K over k; `goodLift_trivial` uses R itself. By `flat_and_maximalIdeal_eq_map_comp`, the composite of two flat maps of local rings that extend the maximal ideals is flat and extends the maximal ideal.

- `exists_flat_localRing_residueField_of_transcendental` and `exists_flat_isLocalHom_residueField_equiv` realize extensions generated by one transcendental or algebraic element, respectively. They give a flat local map whose target maximal ideal is extended and whose residue field is isomorphic to the prescribed extension over the original residue field.
- `flat_directLimit_of_flat` proves flatness of the colimit of a coherent system of flat modules over a nonempty directed preorder with decidable equality.
- For a coherent system of good lifts over a nonempty directed preorder whose transition maps commute with the structure maps, `goodLift_colimit_flat_over_R` shows that the ring colimit is flat over R, and `goodLift_colimit_flat_over_base` that it is flat over every stage when the transition maps are flat.
- `goodLift_colimit_chosen_noflat` takes such a system whose transition maps are also local and respect the residue embeddings. It gives a good lift with local legs that commute with the transition maps, the structure maps and the residue embeddings and extend the maximal ideals; every element comes from a stage, and the colimit is universal among compatible families of ring maps.
- `goodLift_monogenic_step` extends a good lift h, for a given α in K, to a good lift h′ by a flat local map of carriers that extends the maximal ideal and respects the structure maps and residue embeddings, with α in the range of the residue embedding of h′.

The transcendental construction localizes a polynomial ring at the extended maximal ideal. The algebraic construction adjoins a root of a monic lift of the minimal polynomial. For colimits, the proof identifies the nonunits with the extended maximal ideal and uses commutation of tensor products with directed colimits to preserve flatness. The good-lift construction places the base, target field, carriers and indices in one universe.

The module docstring also lists a relation between good lifts, a free-ring model of colimits and upper bounds for directed systems of good lifts; none of these is in the file, whose closing section on free colimits is empty.

The table of references below relates the simple and colimit constructions to EGA 0 10.3.1.1, EGA 0 10.3.1.3, EGA 0 10.3.1.4 and SGA 3 V 4.1.2.
