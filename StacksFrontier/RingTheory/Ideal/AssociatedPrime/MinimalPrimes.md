# Associated primes in quotients by powers

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.Ideal.AssociatedPrime.MinimalPrimes`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

This file shows that a prime minimal over an associated prime plus a principal ideal appears among the associated primes of a quotient by a power of the principal ideal.

`exists_mem_associatedPrimes_quotSMulTop_pow_of_mem_minimalPrimes` takes a Noetherian commutative ring R, a finitely generated R-module M, and an element x of R. If p is associated to M and q is minimal over p + (x), then there exists a positive natural number n such that q is associated to M/xⁿM. Lean writes the quotient as `QuotSMulTop (x ^ n) M`.

Choose an element m with annihilator p. Artin–Rees gives an n > 0 with xⁿM ∩ Rm ⊆ xRm, which bounds the annihilator of the image of m between p + (xⁿ) and p + (x). Those bounds make q minimal in the support of the cyclic submodule generated by the image, so q is associated to that submodule and hence to the quotient.

The statement of Tag 0CN5 here allows any Noetherian ring and any x. The step from minimal support to associated primes uses Tag 05BV.
