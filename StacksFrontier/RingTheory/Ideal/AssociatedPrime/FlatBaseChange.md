# Associated primes after tensoring with a flat module

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.Ideal.AssociatedPrime.FlatBaseChange`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

For a map of commutative rings R → S, this file describes the associated primes over S after tensoring an R-module M with an S-module N that is flat over R. All tensor products are over R. It also gives the associated-prime inclusions for injective maps and left exact sequences over arbitrary commutative rings.

## Results

`Stacks.AssociatedPrimes.FlatBaseChange.quotSMulEquivTensorQuot` identifies N / IN with N ⊗ (R / I) by an S-linear equivalence sending the class of n to n ⊗ 1. Here IN is the S-submodule of N generated by the elements r • n for r ∈ I and n ∈ N.

`associatedPrimes.tensorProduct_of_flat` and its proof `Stacks.AssociatedPrimes.FlatBaseChange.solution` show that the associated-prime set over S of N ⊗ M contains the union of the associated-prime sets of N / 𝔭N over all associated primes 𝔭 of M. If R is Noetherian, the inclusion becomes equality. The auxiliary theorem `associatedPrimes_tensorProduct_subset_of_flat` shows that for a flat R-module P and a finite module M over a Noetherian ring R, every associated prime over R of P ⊗ M is associated to M.

With Ass = `assStacks`, `assStacks_subset_of_injective` gives Ass(X) ⊆ Ass(Y) for an injective linear map X → Y, and `assStacks_subset_union_of_exact` gives Ass(Y) ⊆ Ass(X) ∪ Ass(Z) for an exact sequence 0 → X → Y → Z.

The forward inclusion tensors the embedding R / 𝔭 → M coming from a prime annihilator. For the reverse, a tensor witnessing an associated prime comes from a finite submodule of M. A prime filtration and exactness of flat tensoring reduce to prime quotients, and contraction identifies the prime of R that indexes the corresponding summand.

For the exact-sequence results, the arbitrary-ring inclusion, and all associated primes over S, the condition is equality with an element’s annihilator, as in `assStacks`. The index set of the Noetherian equality is Mathlib’s radical-based `associatedPrimes R M`. The main equality allows arbitrary M, while the auxiliary descent theorem for P ⊗ M takes M finite. The module docstring still lists, as main results, the inclusions for a short exact sequence and the formula for a direct sum (Stacks 02M3); these are not in the file.
