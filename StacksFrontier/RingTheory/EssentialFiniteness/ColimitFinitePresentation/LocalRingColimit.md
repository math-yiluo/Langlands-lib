# Filtered local models of a local ring

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.EssentialFiniteness.ColimitFinitePresentation.LocalRingColimit`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

This file constructs every commutative local ring as a filtered colimit of local rings essentially of finite type over ℤ. It also defines the base-change condition used to study local maps by such models.

## Definitions and results

- `IsLocalizationAtPrimeOfBaseChange` is a condition on a commuting transition square at `i ≤ j`. It says that the canonical map from the tensor product of `Sᵢ` and `Rⱼ` over `Rᵢ` to `Sⱼ` presents `Sⱼ` as the localization at some prime of that tensor product.
- `tag_00QT` gives a nonempty directed preorder and a system of local rings essentially of finite type over ℤ whose direct limit is isomorphic to the given local ring `R`. Both transition maps and the resulting structure maps to `R` are local.

In the `LocalRingColimit` namespace, the index is a finite subset `s` of `R`, ordered by inclusion. The stage `Robj R s` localizes the subring `Asub R s` generated by `s` at `Pcon R s`, the contraction of the maximal ideal of `R`. Contraction makes the localized inclusions and the maps to `R` local. The latter maps are injective and give `colimEquiv`, since each colimit element comes from a stage and every `r` belongs to the stage indexed by its singleton.

The rings and the colimit index in both statements use a single universe. The proved colimit theorem concerns one local ring. The colimit presentation of a local map is discussed as background; the module docstring still lists an essential finite-presentation predicate and the local-map theorem among the file's contents, but neither is in the file.
