# A local criterion for finite normalization

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.Nagata.N1`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

A Noetherian domain has finite normalization precisely when it is normal on a nonempty principal open and has finite normalization at every maximal ideal.

`isN1_iff_exists_isIntegrallyClosed_away_and_forall_isN1_atPrime`, proved by `Stacks.Nagata.NormalLocusCriterion.solution`, states this for a Noetherian domain `R`. The conditions are that `Localization.Away f` is integrally closed for some nonzero `f` in `R`, and `Localization.AtPrime 𝔪` is N-1 for every maximal ideal `𝔪`. A private localization criterion expresses normality in terms of multiplying each integral fraction into the domain by an element of the submonoid being inverted.

In the forward direction, a common denominator for the finitely many generators of the normalization gives the normal open, and N-1 localizes. For the converse, at each maximal ideal clear denominators in generators of the local normalization to build a finite integral subalgebra of the fraction field. This subalgebra is normal at primes over the chosen maximal ideal and after inverting `f`. Openness of its normal locus and lying over produce a principal neighborhood of the maximal ideal on which the normalization is finite, and N-1 descends along the resulting cover.

The theorem uses the concrete away and prime localization models. The Noetherian hypothesis is needed for the converse, which uses both the normal open and the local finiteness conditions.

References are Tag 0333 for the equivalence, Tag 0332 for openness of the normal locus, Tag 032G and Tag 032H for localization and descent, and Tag 032F and Tag 00GV for the definitions.
