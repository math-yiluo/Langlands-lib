# Determinantal rank and depth of ideals

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.Depth.ExactnessCriterion.MinorsAndDepth`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

This file provides the matrix and depth lemmas used in the Buchsbaum–Eisenbud criterion. The determinantal ideal of a matrix is generated by minors of size equal to its rank.

## Rank and localization

- `exteriorRank_eq_rank` and `detIdeal_eq_minorIdeal` identify the two library definitions of rank and the associated ideal over a nontrivial commutative ring. The bridge is that a nonzero exterior power of the map is equivalent to a nonzero minor of that size. `exists_rank_iff` rewrites an existential rank and depth condition using these invariants.
- `rank_map_le` bounds the rank after any ring map to a nontrivial ring by the original rank. When the ranks agree, `minorIdeal_map_of_rank_map_eq` identifies the new ideal with the extension of the old one, since determinants commute with applying the ring map. At a prime, `rank_map_algebraMap_atPrime_eq` and `minorIdeal_map_algebraMap_atPrime` give both equalities if the original minor ideal contains a nonzerodivisor, which survives localization.
- `eq_zero_of_forall_algebraMap_atPrime_eq_zero` detects zero elements over a Noetherian ring by localization at its associated primes. At such a prime, `maximalIdeal_mem_associatedPrimes_atPrime` puts the localized maximal ideal among the associated primes.

## Depth

`one_le_depth_self_iff` characterizes positive I-depth of a commutative ring by I being the unit ideal or containing a nonzerodivisor. Over a Noetherian ring, prime avoidance supplies such an element when I is contained in none of the ring’s associated primes. Other lemmas give monotonicity in I, infinite depth for the unit ideal, and a regular list witnessing any natural lower bound on depth when IM ≠ M.

`succ_le_depth_of_isRegular_quotient` is one direction of the depth comparison modulo a nonzerodivisor. It assumes a Noetherian local ring, a proper ideal I and a nonzerodivisor x in I. A regular list in the image of I in R/xR gives a lower bound of its length plus one for the I-depth of R. The proof lifts the list to I and prepends x.

References include Tag 00N1, Buchsbaum and Eisenbud, *What makes a complex exact?*, Corollary 1, and EGA 0 1.7.4 for the finite intersection lemma `iInf_not_le_of_forall_not_le` in partial form.
