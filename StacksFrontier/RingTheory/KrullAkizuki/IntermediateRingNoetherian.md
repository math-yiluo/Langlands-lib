# The length bound behind Krull–Akizuki

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.KrullAkizuki.IntermediateRingNoetherian`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

Let R be a Noetherian domain of dimension at most one with fraction field K, V a finite-dimensional K-vector space and x ≠ 0 in R. This file proves that for every R-submodule M of V the R-length of M/xM is at most dimₖ V times the R-length of R/(x). Throughout, M/xM is the quotient of M by `x • ⊤`.

## Main results

- `length_quotient_smul_le_finrank_mul` is the bound for an arbitrary, not necessarily finitely generated, R-submodule M of V, under `IsNoetherianRing R` and `Ring.KrullDimLE 1 R`. `length_quot_xtop_fg_le` is the finitely generated case.
- `length_quot_xtop_eq_of_quot_finite` equates the lengths of M/xM and N/xN for a submodule N of M when multiplication by x is injective on M and M/N has finite length.
- `length_quot_xtop_pi` computes the length of the quotient by x of the free module of rank d as d times the length of R/(x); `length_quot_xtop_le_of_span` and `length_quot_xtop_span_range_le` bound a module generated by d elements in the same way.
- `length_le_of_forall_fg_submodule_le` and `length_quot_xtop_le_of_forall_fg` show that a length bound valid on every finitely generated submodule holds for the whole module.
- `exists_smul_mem_of_mem_span_fraction` and `exists_smul_le_of_le_span_fraction` clear denominators: a finitely generated R-submodule in the K-span of F has a nonzero multiple inside F.

For finitely generated N, a maximal K-linearly independent subset of a finite generating set spans a submodule F with the same K-span and at most dimₖ V generators. Clearing denominators makes N/F of finite length, so the cancellation identity reduces the bound to F. The general case follows by lifting finite chains of submodules. The finiteness of the length of R/(x) is Mathlib's `isFiniteLength_quotient_span_singleton`.

Several lemmas do not need R to be a domain. The module docstring states the Krull–Akizuki theorem itself and a section docstring outlines its proof; neither the theorem nor the finite-length and finite-generation steps it describes are in the file.

The length arguments refer to Tag 00PE, Tag 00PF and Tag 00KH. EGA IV 21.10.13 is cited for the free-module and cancellation formulas.
