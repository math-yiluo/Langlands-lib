# Cohen rings and their truncated quotients

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.CohenRing`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

Every field of positive characteristic occurs as the residue field of a Cohen ring. The file constructs such rings and proves formal smoothness of their quotients by powers of the uniformizer over the corresponding quotients of the integers.

## Main definitions and results

- `StacksIsCohenRing` is a class on commutative domains. It requires a discrete valuation ring complete for its maximal-ideal-adic topology, with maximal ideal generated by the image of a prime natural number (`StacksIsCohenRing.exists_prime_maximalIdeal_eq_span`).
- `StacksIsCohenRing.exists_residueField_ringEquiv` takes a prime `p` and a field `k` of characteristic `p`. It produces a Cohen ring `Λ` in the universe of `k`, with maximal ideal `pΛ` and a ring isomorphism from its residue field to `k`.
- `StacksIsCohenRing.formallySmooth_quotient_span_pow` assumes a Cohen ring `Λ` with `CharP (ResidueField Λ) p` and a `ZMod (p ^ n)`-algebra structure on `Λ/pⁿΛ`, supplied as an instance argument. For every natural number `n` it proves that `Λ/pⁿΛ` is formally smooth over `ZMod (p ^ n)`.

## Proof idea

The existence proof completes a flat local extension of the `p`-adic integers with residue field `k` and maximal ideal `(p)`, and uses regularity of `p` in the completion to obtain a discrete valuation ring. Formal smoothness starts with the perfect prime field at `n = 1` and proceeds by square-zero descent, using flatness by base change from the integers.

## Scope

The existence theorem gives an isomorphism of residue fields and separately pins down the uniformizer ideal. It allows imperfect `k`. In the smoothness theorem the residue characteristic forces `p` to be prime and to generate the maximal ideal. The result includes `n = 0`, where both rings are zero, and the inductive argument works already for a discrete valuation ring with this uniformizer.

The module docstrings write the class and its results without the `Stacks` prefix of their names. They also present the `p`-adic integers as a Cohen ring and announce, for Tag 0329, that there is exactly one ring map `ZMod (p ^ n) → Λ/pⁿΛ`; neither result is in this file.
