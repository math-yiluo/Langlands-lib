# The image of a prime and of the maximal ideal of its localization

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.Etale.ResidueFieldExtension`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

`Stacks.Etale.SeparableResidueAtPrime.map_algebraMap_eq_map_maximalIdeal` takes a prime `p` of a commutative ring `R` and an `R`-algebra `A` through which `R → R_p` factors, that is, with an `R_p`-algebra structure and `IsScalarTower R R_p A`. It states that the ideal of `A` generated by the image of `p` equals the ideal generated by the image of the maximal ideal of `R_p`. No unramifiedness is assumed; the case in view is `A = S_q` for a prime `q` lying over `p`. The proof rewrites with `Localization.AtPrime.map_eq_maximalIdeal`, of which the lemma is the analogue for the prime below, and composes the two maps.

The module docstrings still describe the realization of a finite separable residue field extension by an étale algebra (Tag 00UD) and the fibre ideal and residue field at a prime where a map is étale or unramified (Tags 00U4 and 00UW), with their proofs; none of these results is in the file.
