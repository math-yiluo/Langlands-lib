# Totally ramified extensions of prime-power degree

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.Kummer.Eisenstein`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

Let A be a discrete valuation ring with uniformizer π, fraction field K and positive residue characteristic p. The file constructs separable extensions of K of any degree that is a power of p, with a uniformizer satisfying two congruences.

`exists_totallyRamifiedExtension_with_uniformizer_eq` takes n > 1 and q = pʳ for some natural number r. It produces a finite separable L/K of degree q whose integral closure B is a DVR. The extension is totally ramified with index q, and a uniformizer β of B satisfies both β^q ≡ π modulo π^nB and β^q ≡ π modulo β^(nq)B, with π mapped into B.

For q ≥ 2, the construction adjoins a root of X^q − π^(n+q−1)X − π. Eisenstein's criterion gives irreducibility over K, and the nonzero derivative gives separability. The Eisenstein integral-closure criterion gives B = A[β]. Its unique maximal ideal is generated by β. The defining equation makes π a unit times β^q, so the local fundamental identity gives residue degree one. The same equation gives both congruences. For q = 1 the construction takes L = K.

The result uses the library predicate `Stacks.KummerExtension.IsTotallyRamified`, which records a unique maximal ideal above the base maximal ideal and residue degree one. The separate index equality uses `Ideal.ramificationIdx'` at those maximal ideals. The witness field L is in the same universe as K.

Reference is Tag 09EW.
