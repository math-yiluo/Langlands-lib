# The integral closure in a uniformizer root extension

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.Kummer.RingOfIntegers`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

Let A be a discrete valuation ring with fraction field K and uniformizer π, expressed by `Irreducible π`. The file computes the degree and integral closure for the extension obtained by adjoining an n-th root of π.

## The root extension and the ramification predicate

`kummerPolyA` and `kummerPoly` are Xⁿ − π over A and K. The quotient algebras are `A'` and `K1`, with chosen roots `piRootA` and `piRoot`. The subalgebra `A1` is the integral closure of A in `K1`.

The library defines `IsTotallyRamified` for a finite L/K over a local base A using the integral closure of A in L. It requires a unique maximal ideal above the maximal ideal of A with inertia degree one.

For n ≥ 1, `kummerPoly_irreducible` proves irreducibility over K and `powerBasisA'` gives the power basis of A′ over A. `finrank_K1` gives degree n for n ≥ 2. For n ≥ 2, `canonAlgEquiv` identifies A′ with A₁ over A and `integralClosure_eq_adjoin` identifies A₁ with the subalgebra generated by π′. The equivalence comes from `canonAlgHom`, which sends the polynomial root over A to the chosen root over K.

`isUnit_natCast_of_coprime_ringChar` gives a unit in any local ring from a nonzero natural number if the residue characteristic is zero or coprime to it.

Eisenstein's criterion and passage to the fraction field give irreducibility. The power bases give the degree and injectivity, and clearing denominators by a power of π lets the Eisenstein minimal polynomial control the integral elements.

The finite extension instance `instFiniteK1` takes irreducibility over K as a `Fact`. The ramification predicate uses `Ideal.inertiaDeg'` at the maximal ideal.

Tag 09EV (1) and (2) cover the degree and integral closure. Tag 09EW is cited for the related extensions of prime-power degree. The local-ring unit lemma is a special case of EGA 0 21.1.3.

The module docstring surveys the Kummer files and also announces a tame-ramification predicate and theorems that are not in this file.
