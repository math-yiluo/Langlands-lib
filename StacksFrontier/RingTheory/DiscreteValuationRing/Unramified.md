# Discrete valuation criteria, integral closures and totally ramified extensions

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.Unramified`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

The file gives criteria for discrete valuation rings, describes the integral closure in a finite separable fraction-field extension, and defines totally ramified extensions.

## Discrete valuation criteria

`of_forall_eq_unit_mul_pow` produces a discrete valuation ring with uniformizer π when π is a nonunit with all powers nonzero and each nonzero element is a unit times a power of π. `IsLocalRing.isDiscreteValuationRing_of_maximalIdeal_eq_span` applies this criterion to a Noetherian local ring whose maximal ideal is generated by a nonzerodivisor; `IsLocalRing.exists_eq_unit_mul_pow_of_maximalIdeal_eq_span` gives the factorisation it needs. Both criteria conclude with an `IsDomain` instance, `IsDiscreteValuationRing` and irreducibility of π.

## Integral closures and field extensions

For finite separable L/K with K the fraction field of A, `integralClosure_finite_dedekind_and_degree_eq_sum_ramification_inertia` shows that the integral closure B is module-finite, Noetherian and Dedekind of dimension one. It enumerates the maximal ideals over the base maximal ideal without repetition, identifies their localizations as discrete valuation rings, and gives [L : K] = ∑ eᵢ fᵢ. There is one maximal ideal if A is henselian or maximal-ideal-adically complete.

`IsTotallyRamifiedWithRespectTo` requires a unique maximal ideal above the base maximal ideal and residue degree one. Then `ramificationIdx_eq_finrank_of_totallyRamified` gives index equal to degree by the sum formula.

Henselian uniqueness uses the product decomposition of finite algebras and the domain property.

The predicate is for finite separable extensions, abbreviating the library's quotient-residue predicate. The module docstring and two empty section headings still announce unramified and tamely ramified extensions, their permanence properties and the discrete valuation criterion for formally unramified algebras, none of which the file contains.

The Stacks references are Tag 09E8 and Tag 09E9. The file also cites EGA IV 3.4.1, EGA IV 3.4.1.2 and the henselian special case of EGA IV 18.5.11.
