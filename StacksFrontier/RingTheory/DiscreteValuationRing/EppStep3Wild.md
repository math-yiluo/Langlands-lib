# Descending finite extensions from the completion in the wild case

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.EppStep3Wild`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

The file proves the descent of finite extensions from the completion that the wild case of Step 3 of Epp’s theorem needs. For a discrete valuation ring A of residue characteristic p, writing K for the fraction field of A and K̂ for that of its completion, it shows that a finite extension of K̂ is a base change of a finite extension of K.

- `exists_finite_extension_nonempty_tensorProduct_algEquiv_of_conditionB` gives K₁ finite over K with K̂ ⊗[K] K₁ isomorphic to M as a K̂-algebra, for M finite over K̂ and a uniformizer π of A. It takes `hcond`, condition (b) of the proof of Epp’s theorem. In equal characteristic p this supplies ρ in M whose pⁿ-th power is the image of π and whose minimal polynomial is X raised to pⁿ minus π, with M/K̂(ρ) separable. M lies in the universe of A, and K₁ in the universe of K.
- `kummerAlgEquivAdjoin` identifies a Kummer field with the field generated by a given element whose minimal polynomial is the Kummer polynomial. For a uniformizer π of A and q ≥ 2, `exists_algebra_isFractionRing_adjoin` uses the analogous polynomial over K̂ to identify K̂(ρ) as a fraction field of the completed Kummer ring, with its map from A compatible with A → K̂.
- `IsDiscreteValuationRing.charZero_of_ne_zero_of_charP_residueField` and `charP_of_cast_eq_zero` read the characteristic off whether (p : A) vanishes, `IsDiscreteValuationRing.charZero_adicCompletionFractionField` passes characteristic zero to K̂, and `Algebra.isSeparable_of_adjoin_pow_one` treats a root ρ already in the base.

Mixed characteristic and the exponent-one case use separable descent. In the other case the proof descends the separable part over the Kummer ring. Uniqueness of p-power roots in characteristic p gives surjectivity of the resulting tensor map, and equality of dimensions gives bijectivity.

The docstring of the main theorem speaks of a perfect residue field, but the theorem assumes no perfectness: condition (b) enters only as the hypothesis `hcond`. The module docstring announces an `example` that the file does not contain.

This is part of Step 3 of Tag 09F9. The proof uses Tag 09EJ for separable descent and Tag 09EV (3) for the Kummer ring. The reference to EGA 0 21.1.2 is partial and concerns `charP_of_cast_eq_zero`.
