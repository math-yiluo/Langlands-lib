# Formal smoothness after a finite extension of fraction fields

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.Abhyankar.FiniteFractionFieldExtension`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

This file compares normalization and tensor products for an injective local extension A → B of discrete valuation rings with fraction fields K and L and a finite extension K₁/K.

`IsDiscreteValuationRing.formallySmooth_localizations_after_finite_fraction_field_extension` takes L₁ to model the reduction of L ⊗ₖ K₁, A₁ the integral closure of A in K₁, and B₁ the integral closure of B in L₁. It assumes `hformal`, formal smoothness of A → B in the maximal-ideal adic topology on B.

- B₁ is isomorphic as a B-algebra to the reduction of B ⊗ₐ A₁, with the isomorphism compatible with the map from A₁.
- If L ⊗ₖ K₁ is reduced, there are compatible isomorphisms with the full tensor products for both L₁ and B₁.
- Each maximal ideal q of B₁ contracts to a maximal ideal of A₁. The corresponding localizations are discrete valuation rings, and their induced injective local map is formally smooth for the maximal ideal of the target.

The comparison uses normality of the reduced tensor product and its total fraction ring. For the local assertion, the special fiber calculation gives a maximal ideal generated by the image of a uniformizer downstairs. A surjection from the tensor product of residue fields gives separability, so the ramification-index-one criterion yields adic formal smoothness.

The input equivalences `eL₁`, `eA₁` and `eB₁` specify the models, with `hfieldSquare` and `hringSquare` among the map compatibility hypotheses. For the two comparisons with the full tensor products, the reducedness condition is essential. The comments cite the example of completion of a non-excellent discrete valuation ring and a purely inseparable extension of degree two, where both tensor products have nilpotents.

Stacks Project, Tag 09EQ and Tag 00PB. For the counterexample and the correction, the docstring and the note of the tag refer to a module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.Abhyankar.FiniteFractionFieldExtensionCounterexample` and to `errata.md`; neither is in this repository.

The formal smoothness of every local extension `(A₁)_{𝔪_i} → (B₁)_{𝔪_ij}` holds for every finite `K₁ / K`, as printed, and so does `B₁ = (B ⊗_A A₁)_red`. The printed equalities `L₁ = L ⊗_K K₁` and `B₁ = B ⊗_A A₁` are false in general (`exists_formallySmoothNAdic_not_isReduced_tensorProduct`); both say that `L ⊗_K K₁` is reduced, and both are proved here in exactly that case.
