# Residue-field tensor products and a DVR criterion

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.Abhyankar.ResidueFieldTensor`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

The file develops local algebra tools used in the proof of Abhyankar’s lemma. They compare residue fields after taking a tensor product and localizing, and give a criterion for recognizing a discrete valuation ring from its maximal ideal and generic localization.

`IsDiscreteValuationRing.residueTensorMap` is the canonical map from κ(D) ⊗ κ(B) over κ(A) to κ(C) for a commutative square of compatible local algebra maps from A through B and D to C. It is a κ(D)-algebra map. `residueTensorMap_surjective` proves surjectivity when κ(D) is finite over κ(A), C is a localization of B₁, and a compatible surjection from B ⊗ A₁ over A to B₁ agrees with the structure maps of B and A₁.

`separable_of_surjective_from_tensor` proves that a field quotient G of E ⊗ F over k is separable over E in the sense of `Algebra.StacksIsSeparableExtension`, provided E is finite over k and F is separable over k in the same sense.

`finite_residueField_localized_integralClosure` gives a local map and a finite residue extension at each maximal ideal of an algebra model of the integral closure in a finite extension of the fraction field. The base is a Noetherian local domain of dimension at most one. `isLocalHom_of_integral_localization` supplies the locality statement for integral algebras over a local ring.

`dvr_of_regular_principal_and_field_away` shows that a local ring is a domain and a DVR if its maximal ideal is generated by a nonzerodivisor π and inverting π gives a field.

The tensor arguments use Artinianness to make the image a field or identify a field quotient with the corresponding localization, and use geometric reducedness to obtain separability. The other proofs use quasi-finiteness to give residue finiteness and divisibility by powers of the prime π to obtain the DVR factorization.

The separability result permits transcendental F over k. The finiteness hypothesis is on E over k, and the conclusion uses the library’s `Algebra.StacksIsSeparableExtension` for general field extensions.

The module docstring introduces the whole development of Abhyankar’s lemma: none of the definitions and results it lists as main is in this file, and those on tame ramification, with the Kummer-overfield definition, are in no file of this repository. The introductory discussion cites Tag 09EQ, Tag 0BRM, Tag 0EXW, Tag 0EXY and Tag 0EXX for the surrounding development, with Tag 00PB for the need to take reductions and Tag 09EV for the Kummer models.
