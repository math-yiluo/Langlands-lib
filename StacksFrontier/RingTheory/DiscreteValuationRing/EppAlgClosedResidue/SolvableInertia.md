# Solvable inertia and reduction to a normal extension

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.EppAlgClosedResidue.SolvableInertia`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

The file finds prime-degree layers of field extensions for use in Epp’s theorem and constructs the complete discrete valuation rings attached to factors of a normalized base change.

## Results

- `isSolvable_inertia` proves solvability of the inertia group of a maximal ideal of the integral closure of a DVR A in a finite Galois extension of its fraction field. The residue characteristic of A is a prime p.
- `exists_intermediateField_prime_finrank_of_isSolvable` splits a nontrivial finite solvable Galois extension into a cyclic prime-degree bottom layer and a solvable Galois extension of strictly smaller degree above it. `exists_intermediateField_finrank_eq_of_isPurelyInseparable` finds a degree-p subextension of a nontrivial purely inseparable extension in exponential characteristic p, generated by an element whose p-th power lies in the base.
- `exists_normalClosure_data` places a finite extension M/L inside a finite normal extension N/L, with N in the universe of M. `charZero_or_charP_of_injective` shows that if a domain R maps injectively to S and maps to a ring of prime characteristic p, then S has characteristic zero or p.
- `mapIntegralClosureOfTower` is the canonical map on integral closures induced by a compatible square of commutative rings. `integralClosure_isCompleteDVR` gives the finite complete normalization in a finite fraction-field extension, with the prescribed fraction field. `integralClosure_factor_isExtension` applies this to two compatible finite extensions of the fraction fields of complete DVRs and gives a local injective map between their normalizations. `finiteFieldFactors_compatible` decomposes the reduced compositum of a finite base change into a nonempty finite product of finite extensions of L, with compatible actions of the new base.

Wild inertia is a finite p-group and the tame quotient is cyclic. A prime cyclic quotient of a nontrivial solvable Galois group gives the first layer by Galois correspondence. The normal closure is taken inside an algebraic closure of M.

The field factors may live in the maximum of the universes of L and the reduced compositum. The module docstring announces an induction over intermediate fields and the normal case; neither is in the file.
