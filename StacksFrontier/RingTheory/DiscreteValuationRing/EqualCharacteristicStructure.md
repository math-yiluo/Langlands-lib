# Power series over the residue field of a complete discrete valuation ring

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.EqualCharacteristicStructure`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

A complete discrete valuation ring R of equal characteristic is a power series ring over its own residue field, on any chosen uniformizer.

- `exists_residue_comp_eq_id` gives a ring section of the residue map for a maximal-ideal-adically complete local ring R when the residue characteristic vanishes in R. For an I-adically complete ring, `exists_powerSeries_ringHom` extends a coefficient map to power series by sending the variable to any chosen element of I.
- `exists_powerSeriesRingEquiv_of_irreducible` gives a ring isomorphism κ_R⟦X⟧ ≃+* R that sends X to a specified uniformizer π and a constant to a lift with the given residue. It assumes maximal-ideal-adic completeness and the vanishing of the residue characteristic in R.

The coefficient-ring part of the Cohen structure theorem gives the section, since the coefficient ring's maximal ideal is generated by the residue characteristic. Evaluation at π is injective because a nonzero kernel would contain a power of X (`injective_of_map_irreducible_ne_zero`, stated for any ring map from a discrete valuation ring to a reduced ring that does not kill a uniformizer), and successive approximation of coefficients gives surjectivity by Krull intersection (`surjective_of_residue_comp_eq_id`).

The condition `hchar` allows equal characteristic zero as well as equal positive characteristic. The surjectivity lemma applies to an already supplied evaluation map, using separatedness of the discrete valuation ring. The module docstring also announces the variants without a chosen uniformizer or with `ringChar R ≠ 0`, the weakly unramified pair on a common uniformizer and two lemmas on the residue characteristic; none of these is in the file.

References are Stacks Project Tag 032A, Tag 0326 and Tag 09F9, SGA 1 X 2.14, Epp's *Eliminating wild ramification*, Theorem (1.3), and Kuhlmann's *A correction to Epp's paper “Elimination of wild ramification”*.
