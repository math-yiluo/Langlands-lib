# Degrees and ramification of Artin–Schreier extensions

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.ArtinSchreier.GaloisDegree`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

The file determines the degree of a field factor of a scalar extension of a field generated by an Artin–Schreier root. It also gives ramification alternatives for finite Galois extensions of a DVR’s fraction field whose degree divides a prime.

`IsDiscreteValuationRing.artinSchreierFactorPackage_of_surjective_tensorProduct` takes a field L of prime characteristic p and an extension M/L generated by z with zᵖ − z = ξ for ξ ∈ L. It transports both the equation and generation to a field quotient F of E ⊗ M over L, given by a surjective E-algebra map. It supplies finiteness and shows that F/E either has degree one with surjective structure map or is Galois of degree p. The generation step is `adjoin_eq_top_of_surjective_tensorProduct_factor`, which holds for any simple extension.

`ramification_cases_of_isGalois_of_finrank_dvd_prime` applies to a finite Galois extension L/K with degree dividing the prime p, for K the fraction field of a DVR A. The alternatives are a trivial extension, unramified of degree p at all maximal ideals of the integral closure, total ramification of index p, or index one with a DVR integral closure and purely inseparable residue extension of degree p. `ramificationIdx_and_inertiaDeg_le_finrank` gives individual bounds by the degree for both invariants at any maximal ideal in the finite separable case.

The degree of the field factor comes from private lemmas: a field generated by an Artin–Schreier root is a separable splitting field, and its Galois group embeds into the additive prime field via σ ↦ σ(z) − z, so its degree divides p. The ramification alternatives use the product of the branch count, index and residue degree, then use the separable and inseparable degrees to analyze a residue extension of degree p.

The Galois case split applies in arbitrary characteristic. Its unramified alternative permits several maximal ideals in the integral closure. The degree bound and Galois property for an extension generated by an Artin–Schreier root, which the module docstring announces, are only private lemmas here; the public statement is the one for the field factor.

The degree formulas are cited as Tag 09E8 and Tag 09EB. The case split supplies the cases from Tag 09E3 invoked in the proofs of Tag 09F0 and Tag 09F3. The field-factor application refers to Tag 09ER.
