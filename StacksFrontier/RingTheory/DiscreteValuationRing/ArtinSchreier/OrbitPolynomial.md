# Orbit polynomials, Artin–Schreier presentations and a total ramification criterion

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.ArtinSchreier.OrbitPolynomial`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

This file supplies tools for eliminating ramification of degree p over discrete valuation rings in characteristic p. Its central object is the orbit polynomial, the product of X − v over v in V, of a finite additive subgroup V of a ring: translates of it by a suitable constant are Eisenstein at a proper ideal containing V, with Galois root fields when irreducible. It also presents Galois extensions of degree p in characteristic p in Artin–Schreier form, and gives a criterion for total ramification through powers of an ideal.

## Public results

- `exists_artinSchreier_presentation_of_charP`: for fields K → L → M with K of prime characteristic p and M/L finite Galois of degree p, there are ξ in L and z in M with z^p − z = ξ and M = L[z].
- `isTotallyRamified_of_map_le_pow_finrank`: let A be a discrete valuation ring with fraction field K, L/K finite separable, and P a maximal ideal of the integral closure of A in L. If the maximal ideal of A extends into P^[L : K], then L/K is totally ramified with respect to A (`Stacks.KummerExtension.IsTotallyRamified`). The proof bounds the ramification index of P from below and uses the fundamental identity ∑ e f = [L : K].

## Private material

- `orbitPolynomial`: invariant under translation by V; after subtracting a constant, monic of degree |V|, a product of distinct linear factors over any field into which the base embeds and which contains a root, and, when its image over a field containing the base is irreducible, with a Galois root field.
- `orbitPolynomial_sub_C_isEisensteinAt`: if V and c lie in a proper ideal I and c ∉ I², the polynomial minus c is Eisenstein at I.
- `exists_finite_translation_subgroup_in_power`: in a domain that is an algebra over a finite field F, a nonzero non-unit t gives, for every depth N and rank r, a subgroup of order |F|^r inside t^N.
- `integralClosureBaseChangeOfFinite`: for finite K₁/K, the reduced compositum (L ⊗_K K₁)_red with the integral closures of A in K₁ and of B in the compositum, as in Tag 09EM.
- Galois extensions of degree p in characteristic p are generated by an Artin–Schreier element (Tag 09DY).

## Points to watch

- In `isTotallyRamified_of_map_le_pow_finrank` the separability of L/K is not needed for the conclusion; its docstring says the hypothesis comes from the route of the proof and could be removed. The maximal ideal P is an input, not produced.
- The total ramification predicate is the library's own notion.

## References

Tag 09DY, Tag 09EM; the presentation theorem is stated under the hypotheses of Tag 09F1, in the form used by Tag 09F0.
