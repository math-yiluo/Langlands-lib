# Artin–Schreier extensions with integral constant, and the orbit extension

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.ArtinSchreier.OrbitExtension`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

This file is part of the library's treatment of eliminating ramification of degree p for extensions of discrete valuation rings in equal characteristic p. It shows that an Artin–Schreier extension whose constant is integral is weakly unramified, and it builds a totally ramified Galois extension of degree p^r generated by a root of an Eisenstein "orbit polynomial": the product of X − v over v in V, minus π.

## Main results

- `isWeaklyUnramified_of_artinSchreier_integral`: let B → C be an extension of discrete valuation rings with fraction fields L and M, where M/L is finite Galois of prime degree p, L has characteristic p, and M = L(z) with z^p − z = ξ for some ξ coming from B. Then C is weakly unramified over B.
- `exists_isGalois_totallyRamified_orbitExtension`: for a discrete valuation ring A with fraction field K of characteristic p, a uniformizer π, a depth N ≥ 1 and a rank r, there are a finite Galois extension K₁/K of degree p^r, totally ramified with respect to A, an additive subgroup V ⊆ π^N A of order p^r, and α in K₁ integral over A such that the product of α − v over v in V equals π.
- `exists_isGalois_totallyRamified_orbitExtension_forall_depth`: the same, with V shown to lie in π^n A for every n ≤ N. Its docstring explains that one extension, built once from a depth and a rank, is meant to serve several later uses at smaller depths, while the rank, which fixes the degree, must be agreed on in advance.

Private helpers give ramification index one for surjective algebras, identify a local domain dominating a prime of a Dedekind domain with its localization there, and derive weak unramifiedness of B → C from index one at the maximal ideals of the integral closure of B in M.

## Proofs

The first theorem reads the unramified alternative off `isGalois_and_ramification_cases_of_adjoin_root_artinSchreier` (the alternative M = L is excluded by the degree). For the orbit extension, V comes from `exists_finite_translation_subgroup_in_power`; the polynomial ∏ (X − v) − π is Eisenstein, hence irreducible; K₁ adjoins a root; it is Galois by `orbitPolynomial_sub_C_map_adjoinRoot_isGalois` and totally ramified by `isTotallyRamified_of_map_le_pow_finrank`, since π lies in P^(p^r).

## Points to watch

- The first theorem is only the case where ξ is integral (the file calls it the case n = 0 of Tag 09F1, clause (A) of Tag 09F0), not the full statement of either tag.
- `IsWeaklyUnramified` and `IsTotallyRamifiedWithRespectTo` are the library's own notions, defined elsewhere.
- The module docstring's weak solutions from weakly unramified extensions are not in the file.
- K₁ is constructed in the universe of K.
