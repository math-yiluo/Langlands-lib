# Elimination of ramification from Epp's theorem, part 1: statement and algebraic extension

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.EliminateRamificationSolution.AlgClosedResidueExtension`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

This is the first part of the library's derivation of elimination of ramification (Tag 09II) from Epp's theorem (Tag 09F9). For an extension A → B of discrete valuation rings, B essentially of finite type over A, Tag 09II asks for a finite K₁/K that is a solution in the sense of Tag 09EN. This part states Epp's theorem as a proposition, proves facts on prime spectra along integral maps, and builds an algebraic extension of K over which the integral closure of A is a discrete valuation ring with perfect residue field.

## Main definition

`EppTheorem`: the proposition that for every extension of discrete valuation rings A ⊂ B with fraction fields K ⊂ L satisfying `Stacks.EppTheorem.EppCondition A B p` (every element of the perfect core of κ_B is separable over κ_A) whenever p is a prime equal to the residue characteristic of A, there are a finite K₁/K and an `IntegralClosureBaseChange` that is a weak solution: every maximal ideal of the integral closure of B in (L ⊗_K K₁)_red has ramification index one over the integral closure of A in K₁.

## Main results

- `exists_algebraic_extension_isDiscreteValuationRing_integralClosure`: for a discrete valuation ring A with fraction field K, there is an algebraic E/K such that the integral closure of A in E is a discrete valuation ring with perfect residue field, whose maximal ideal is generated by that of A.
- `finite_primeSpectrum_of_isIntegral_of_isNoetherianRing`: a Noetherian integral algebra over a ring with finite spectrum has finite spectrum.
- `exists_primeSpectrum_comap_bijective`: if a ring with finite spectrum is a directed union of subrings over which it is integral, contraction of primes is eventually bijective.
- `perfectField_residueField_of_integral` and `map_under_eq_maximalIdeal_of_ramificationIdx_eq_one`: over a local ring with perfect residue field, an integral algebra has perfect residue fields at its maximal ideals; ramification index one implies that the contracted prime generates the maximal ideal of the localization.
## Points to watch

- `EppTheorem` is only a statement, proved nowhere in this file; later parts take it as a hypothesis. Its docstring names `Stacks.EppTheorem.eppTheorem_monomorphic`, in another file, as its proof. It is fixed at four universes, K₁ lies in the universe of K, `ExpChar κ_B p` is an extra argument, and its conclusion is a weak solution only.
- E/K is algebraic, in general not finite.
- The module docstring describes the whole development, including `exists_finite_extension_isSolution_of_charZero`, `exists_finite_extension_isSolution_of_perfectField` and `exists_finite_extension_isSolution_of_eppTheorem`, which are declared in other parts. It also states that clause (3) of Tag 09ET is false. Separability along directed unions and `smulLocalizationAtPrime`, which the docstrings announce for this part, are not in the file.
