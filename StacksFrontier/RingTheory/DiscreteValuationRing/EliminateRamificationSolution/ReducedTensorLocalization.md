# Reduced tensor products and localization of integral closures

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.EliminateRamificationSolution.ReducedTensorLocalization`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

The file compares reduced scalar extensions along a tower of commutative rings and describes the effect of localization and an integral change of base on integral closures.

## Results

- `reducedTensorProduct_map_injective` shows, for a field K, a commutative K-algebra L and a K-map of fields E → F, that the induced map from the reduction of L ⊗[K] E to the reduction of L ⊗[K] F is injective. `reductionMap_tensorProduct_quotient_nilradical_bijective` shows that reducing a tensor factor first leaves the final reduction isomorphic.
- `reducedTensorProductCancelBaseChange` and `reducedTensorProductBaseChangeEquiv` give the canonical equivalence between the reduction of an iterated scalar extension and that of the direct scalar extension, in the two tensor orders. For a compatible tower R → S → T and an R-algebra A, the latter identifies the reduction of T ⊗[S] Q, where Q is the reduction of A ⊗[R] S, with the reduction of A ⊗[R] T.
- `isLocalization_integralClosureMapOfScalarTower` identifies the integral closure after localizing both the base and the ambient algebra at corresponding multiplicative sets with the localization of the original integral closure. `integralClosureEquivOfIntegralBase` identifies the closures of R and of an integral R-algebra C in a common ambient algebra. `isLocalization_integralClosureMapOfIntegralBase` combines this identification with localization at a submonoid of C.
- `reducedTensorProductLocalizationMap` extends a map from the reduction of A ⊗[R] S into an S-algebra B to a map from the reduction of A ⊗[R] T to the reduction of B ⊗[S] T. `isLocalization_reducedTensorProductLocalizationMap` shows that if the original map is a localization, the new map localizes at the image of the original submonoid.

The tensor comparison uses the description of the kernel of tensoring a quotient as the ideal generated by the old kernel. The integral-closure comparisons use the localization theorem for integrality and transitivity of integral extensions.

The general scalar-extension equivalences reduce the output of the iterated tensor product. To obtain a reduced tensor product before that final reduction, `isReduced_tensorProduct_reduction_of_nilradical_map_eq` requires `hn`, the equality between the extended nilradical of the original algebra and the full nilradical after scalar extension.
