# Six characterizations of discrete valuation rings

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.TFAE`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

For an arbitrary commutative ring A, `isDiscreteValuationRing_tfae` states that the following six conditions are equivalent.

1. A is a discrete valuation ring.
2. A is a Noetherian valuation ring other than a field.
3. A is a regular local ring of Krull dimension one.
4. A is a Noetherian local domain with nonzero principal maximal ideal.
5. A is a Noetherian integrally closed local domain of Krull dimension one.
6. A is a local domain with nonzero principal maximal ideal, and for every generator π of that ideal each nonzero x has a unique expression x = uπⁿ with n a natural number and u a unit.

The proof `Stacks.DiscreteValuationRing.Criteria.solution` uses Mathlib's equivalences for Noetherian local domains with nonzero maximal ideal. In the regular case the cotangent space has dimension one, giving a principal maximal ideal. In the normal case dimension one forces the maximal ideal to be the unique nonzero prime. Cancellation and irreducibility give uniqueness of the unit–power expression.

Domain and local ring assumptions are included inside the relevant conditions. In item (6) the unique pair is quantified for each fixed generator and each nonzero element. This makes the factorization statement in the closing sentence of Tag 00PD an equivalent condition in its own right.

The comments cite Tag 00NP for regular local rings being domains and discuss alternative arguments from Tag 00P9, Tag 00IG and Tag 00IC. Tag 0BHZ and Tag 030C are cited for the formulations of regularity and normality. The EGA references are EGA 0 17.1.4, EGA II 7.1.6, EGA IV 3.4.1, EGA IV 3.4.1.2 and EGA IV 14.5.7.
