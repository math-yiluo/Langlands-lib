# Kummer presentations and transport of ramification indices

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.ExtensionLevel.RamificationTransport`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

The file supplies field and local-ring comparisons for the analysis of the level of a prime-degree extension. The comparisons identify a tensor product with a known extension and let ramification be computed in the local factors of a product.

- `PrimeKummer.presentationOfGenerator` identifies a degree-d field extension generated by x with the root algebra of X^d − a when x^d = a and d > 0. For prime d, `PrimeKummer.nonempty_algHom_of_tensor_not_isField` gives an embedding into a target field when their tensor product over the base fails to be a field.
- `nonempty_baseChange_algEquiv_of_generator_of_tensor_not_isField` gives M ⊗ₖ E ≃ L over M for a tower K ⊆ M ⊆ L in which L/M has such a presentation of prime degree p. It assumes E/K also has degree p, M ⊗ₖ E is a field, and L ⊗ₖ E is not a field.
- `exists_component_algEquiv_atMaximal` identifies a maximal localization of a finite product of local domains with one factor, compatibly with projection. The transport lemmas preserve contractions and ramification indices under isomorphisms of prime localizations over the base. The local-target version is `SplitFactors.ramificationIdx_eq_of_localComponentEquiv`.
- `ramificationIdx_eq_one_of_integral_tower` gives index one at the chosen maximal ideal of a source that is flat over an integral intermediate local ring. The comparison identifies the source localization with the local component as algebras over that component. The map from the base to the component is local. The component and the intermediate ring must have the same positive index over the base.
- `weak_indices_through_equivalence` gives index one for the composite of two maps of local rings that extend each maximal ideal to the next, followed by a ring isomorphism.

The field comparison base changes the root algebra and uses irreducibility of the prime-degree binomial in the absence of a root. Equal dimensions upgrade the embedding to an isomorphism. The index comparisons use `quotient_length_eq_of_ringEquiv` to transport lengths over the respective rings, and the tower formula gives the index-one result by cancellation. Helpers obtain flatness from an embedding into a suitable field algebra and a scalar tower from a commuting square with an injective map.
