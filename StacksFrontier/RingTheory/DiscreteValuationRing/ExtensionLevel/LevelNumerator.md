# Least level numerators and totally ramified root extensions

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.ExtensionLevel.LevelNumerator`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

This file supplies the arithmetic, residue-lifting and valuation lemmas used to lower the level of a cyclotomic quotient presentation over a discrete valuation ring.

- `extensionLevelNumerators_eq_of_associated` gives the same candidate set for associated choices of the scaling element. For a uniformizer π and positive least numerator n, `isUnit_of_isLeast_extensionLevelNumerator` makes the scaled parameter a = πⁿξ a unit. If p divides n, the residue characteristic is p, and n < pe with ζₚ − 1 associated to π raised to e, `not_isPow_residue_of_isLeast_extensionLevelNumerator` excludes a pth power residue for a under the identities `intProp f P` and `funcEq f P` of the cyclotomic quotient polynomial.
- `level_decrement_bounds` sets m = min(n, e, pe − n) for 0 < n < pe and e > 0. Then m is positive and at most n and e, and for ℓ = n/e the quotient (n − m)/e equals max(0, ℓ − 1, 2ℓ − p).
- `exists_residueField_section_quotient_pow` splits the residue map of a local truncated quotient in prime characteristic p. `ringHom_eq_on_perfectCore_of_sub_mem_nilpotent` gives agreement on the perfect core for two maps from a field to a characteristic-p ring that agree modulo a nilpotent ideal.
- `isTotallyRamified_of_dvd_ramificationIdx` applies to a finite separable extension of degree at most d if every index above the base maximal ideal is divisible by the positive integer d. Over a characteristic-zero fraction field, `exists_totallyRamified_root_uniformizer` produces an integral dth root of a uniformizer in a totally ramified extension of degree d, for d > 0. `exists_totallyRamified_root_pow_sub` gives a degree-d totally ramified extension generated by a root of yᵈ − y = a/πⁿ, with d > 1, a a unit, and positive n coprime to d.

Divisibility of the scaled parameter by π would lower the numerator, and when p divides n the cyclotomic addition law can achieve that divisibility by cancelling a pth power leading residue. For the root constructions, valuations give divisibility of the ramification indices and the degree formula forces total ramification and the claimed degree.

The ring section is for the truncated quotient. `exists_lift_residueField_section` gives a set-theoretic lift to the original ring with a ring homomorphism after reduction.
