# Uniformizer roots and separability over a complete DVR

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.EppConditionB`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

A finite extension of the fraction field of a complete discrete valuation ring with perfect residue field becomes separable over an intermediate field generated by a p-power root of a uniformizer. The file records the minimal polynomial of that root.

- `isPSpannedBy_of_isAdicComplete` proves that a complete DVR with perfect residue field of prime characteristic p and with p = 0 in the ring is p-spanned by any uniformizer π, in the sense of `IsPSpannedBy p π`.
- `exists_pow_eq_and_isSeparable_adjoin` takes a finite extension M/K of the fraction field of a complete DVR with perfect residue field of prime characteristic p. For a uniformizer π it gives n ≥ 0 and ρ ∈ M with ρ raised to pⁿ equal to π, minimal polynomial X^(pⁿ) − π over K, and M separable over K(ρ).

In equal characteristic the proof identifies the DVR with a power series ring by sending X to π. The power-series spanning result passes to fractions, and integrally closedness and irreducibility show that π has no p-th root in K. In mixed characteristic the proof takes n = 0 using separability in characteristic zero.

This is condition (b) in the completion step of Tag 09F9. The intermediate field K(ρ) is inside M. The Lean statement assumes positive residue characteristic even though the comments also discuss the characteristic-zero case.
