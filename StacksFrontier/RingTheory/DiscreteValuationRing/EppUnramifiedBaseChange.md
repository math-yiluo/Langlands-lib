# Monic lifts and the residue field of a monogenic extension

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.EppUnramifiedBaseChange`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

The file provides three steps of the residue-field enlargement in Epp’s argument: lifting monic polynomials through the residue map, describing a field generated by one element as a polynomial quotient, and computing the residue field of a monogenic extension of a discrete valuation ring.

- `exists_monic_lift_map_residue_eq` lifts a monic polynomial over a local ring’s residue field to a monic polynomial over the ring.
- For an integral element α generating a field E over K, `AdjoinRoot.algEquivOfPrimitiveElement` identifies the quotient of K[X] by the minimal polynomial of α with E as a K-algebra.
- Let A be a discrete valuation ring and P ∈ A[X], with A[X] ⧸ (P) local and A → A[X] ⧸ (P) a local map. `AdjoinRoot.aeval_residue_root_map_residue_eq_zero` says the residue of the adjoined root is a root of the reduction P̄. `AdjoinRoot.residueFieldAlgEquiv` identifies κ_A[X] ⧸ (P̄) with the residue field of A[X] ⧸ (P) as κ_A-algebras, for monic P of positive degree with irreducible reduction and A[X] ⧸ (P) a domain.

The isomorphism is evaluation at the residue of the adjoined root. It is injective because its source is a field, and surjective because both sides have dimension deg P over κ_A. No completeness of A is assumed.

The module docstring announces the perfect core as an intermediate field and the theorem realizing it by a finite unramified base change; neither is in this file.

The comments cite Epp’s *Elimination of wild ramification*, Tag 09ET, and Tag 09F9.
