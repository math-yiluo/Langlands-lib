# Local properties under completion

Module `StacksFrontier.RingTheory.AdicCompletion.PermanenceOfProperties`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

This file compares a commutative Noetherian local ring A with its completion at the maximal ideal, `AdicCompletion (maximalIdeal A) A`, for three properties: Krull dimension, regularity, and being a discrete valuation ring.

## Properties of the rings

- `ringKrullDim_adicCompletion_maximalIdeal` gives equality of the Krull dimensions of A and of its completion.
- `isRegularLocalRing_iff_adicCompletion_maximalIdeal` says that A is a regular local ring if and only if its completion is.
- `isDiscreteValuationRing_iff_adicCompletion_maximalIdeal` says that A is a discrete valuation ring if and only if its completion is.

The dimension argument uses going down for the flat map from A to its completion: the maximal ideal of the completion is generated by that of A, so the fibre adds nothing to its height, and the heights of the two maximal ideals are the Krull dimensions. Regularity, the minimal number of generators of the maximal ideal being the Krull dimension, then follows from the equality of these numbers of generators (`AdicCompletion.spanFinrank_maximalIdeal_eq`) and the dimension result. For discrete valuation rings the proof uses `isDiscreteValuationRing_tfae`, by which they are the regular local rings of dimension one, and applies both results.

Mathlib's `IsDiscreteValuationRing` takes an `IsDomain` instance, so each side of the last equivalence bundles that instance existentially. The module docstring treats ten Stacks tags, from 07NV to 0AGX; only 07NV, 07NY and 0AP1 are proved here, and the sections headed by the others are empty.
