# Separability and absolute Kähler differentials

Module `StacksFrontier.FieldTheory.KaehlerDifferential.Separable`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

For a field extension K/k, the map K ⊗[k] Ω(k/ℤ) ⟶ Ω(K/ℤ) on absolute Kähler differentials detects separability in the sense that each finitely generated intermediate field admits a separating transcendence basis.

`Algebra.isSeparableExtension_iff_injective_mapBaseChange` proves that `Algebra.StacksIsSeparableExtension k K` (written `Algebra.IsSeparableExtension` in the docstrings) is equivalent to injectivity of this map for any extension of fields. The library's predicate is defined for extensions of arbitrary transcendence degree by requiring every finitely generated intermediate extension of k to be separably generated.

For the implication from injectivity, naturality and flatness pass injectivity to intermediate fields. In positive characteristic p, the proof differentiates a normalized relation among p-th powers and inducts on the number of terms. Injectivity forces the remaining coefficients to have zero absolute differential, hence to be p-th powers in k. Taking roots then shows that p-th powers of a k-linearly independent family remain k-linearly independent, giving a separating transcendence basis by Mac Lane's criterion. In characteristic zero the proof uses the theorem for perfect base fields.

For the converse, a kernel element is written as a finite sum of tensors. Both its coefficients in K and enough data to witness its vanishing in the presentation of the differentials lie in a finitely generated intermediate field. That field is separably generated by hypothesis, hence formally smooth over k. The differential map there has a retraction, forcing the original kernel element to vanish.

The equivalence uses ℤ as the base of the differentials in every characteristic. In characteristic p this gives the differentials over the prime field in Tag 031X, and in characteristic zero both sides of the equivalence hold.

The reference for the criterion is EGA 0 20.6.3. The auxiliary implication from vanishing of an absolute differential to existence of a p-th root is also associated with EGA 0 21.4.4 and EGA 0 21.5.3 in the table of references below.
