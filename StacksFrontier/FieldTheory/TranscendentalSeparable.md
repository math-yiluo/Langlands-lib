# Separating transcendence bases and purely inseparable base change

Module `StacksFrontier.FieldTheory.TranscendentalSeparable`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

A finitely generated field extension acquires a separating transcendence basis after a finite purely inseparable change of base.

## Main results

- `exists_isPurelyInseparable_and_isSeparablyGenerated_of_essFiniteType` gives, for a finitely generated field extension K/k and a prescribed perfect extension Ω/K in a compatible tower, intermediate fields K′ inside Ω/K and k′ inside K′/k. Both K′/K and k′/k are finite and purely inseparable, and K′ is separably generated over k′. The proof is packaged as `Stacks.FieldTheory.SeparatingBaseChange.solution`.

The proof first finds a separating basis for the compositum of K with the perfect closure of k in Ω, over that perfect closure. Only finitely many elements of the closure are needed to express the basis elements and the coefficients of the minimal polynomials used for separability. Taking their union gives one finite set T, with K′ = K(T) and k′ = k(T).

The theorem uses `Algebra.EssFiniteType` for finite generation as a field and `Algebra.IsSeparablyGenerated` for separability over the field generated by a basis.

The file ends with a docstring stating Tag 030Q and comments announcing a definition of separable generation, but no declaration follows them.
