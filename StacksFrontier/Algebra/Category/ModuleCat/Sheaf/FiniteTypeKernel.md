# Kernels of surjections onto globally presented sheaves of modules

Module `StacksFrontier.Algebra.Category.ModuleCat.Sheaf.FiniteTypeKernel`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

Let `R` be a sheaf of rings on a site `(C, J)`. If `F` is a sheaf of `R`-modules of finite presentation and `θ : G ⟶ F` is a surjection from a sheaf of modules of finite type, then `Ker θ` is of finite type. The file proves this when the presentation of `F` and the generators of `G` are global, by lifting generators locally and writing down explicit generators of the kernel.

## Main results

All in `Stacks.SheafOfModules.FiniteTypeKernel`.

- `isFiniteType_kernel_of_presentation`: for `P : F.Presentation` with `P.IsFinite`, finitely many global generating sections of `G`, and an epimorphism `θ : G ⟶ F`, `kernel θ` is of finite type.
- `isColimitCmp`: given a presentation `free m --χ--> free n --φ--> F`, an epimorphism `γ : free Iγ ⟶ A`, and `α`, `β` with `α ≫ φ = γ ≫ ψ` and `β ≫ ψ = φ`, the map `ψ : A ⟶ F` is a cokernel of the comparison morphism `cmp χ γ α β`; `generatingSectionsKernel` deduces that `kernel ψ` is generated by `m ⊕ Iγ` sections.
- `sectionsOverEquiv`: global sections of the restriction to a slice are the sections over its object, `(M.over V).sections ≃ M.val.obj (op V)`.
- `exists_lift_over_of_epi`: a global section of the target of an epimorphism lifts after restriction to the members of a covering sieve.
- `epi_kernelLift_of_isColimit`: in an abelian category, if `g` is a cokernel of `f`, then `f` maps onto `kernel g`.
- `preservesLimitOverFunctor`: restriction to a slice site preserves limits of small shape, in particular kernels, as pushforward of presheaves and sheaves of modules does (`preservesLimitPushforwardPresheaf`, `preservesLimitPushforward`). With binary products in `C`, restriction also preserves colimits (`preservesColimitsOverFunctor`).

## Proof

Each lifting condition holds on a covering sieve; their finite intersection covers, and over each member the comparison morphism gives finitely many generators of the restricted kernel.

## Watch for

- Only the case of a global finite presentation of `F` and global generators of `G` is proved. Tag 01BP concerns the local notions `IsFinitePresentation` and `IsFiniteType`; the file explains that the reduction to the global case is not carried out, and what it would need.
- The main results assume, as an instance hypothesis, that every restriction functor `overFunctor R V` preserves colimits; it holds by instance when `C` has binary products (for instance the opens of a space).
- Sheafification and `WEqualsLocallyBijective` on `J` and on every slice `J.over X` are assumed (`HasSheafify`, not merely `HasWeakSheafify`).
- The module docstring also announces two results that the file does not contain.
