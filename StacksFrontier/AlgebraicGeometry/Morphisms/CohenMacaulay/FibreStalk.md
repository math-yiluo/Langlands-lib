# The local ring of a scheme-theoretic fibre

Module `StacksFrontier.AlgebraicGeometry.Morphisms.CohenMacaulay.FibreStalk`, an extract of the file of the same path of the Lean library AxiomMath/Stacks. The origin of the file and what the extract leaves out are given at the end of this page.

For a scheme morphism `f : X ⟶ Y` and a point x of X, the local ring of the fibre over f(x) at `f.asFiber x` is the quotient of the local ring of X at x by the ideal generated by the maximal ideal of the local ring of Y at f(x). No hypothesis on `f` is needed.

- `quotientStalk_bridge` gives such a ring isomorphism when the source point is written as the image `f.fiberι (f x) (f.asFiber x)` of the fibre point, together with its compatibility with the stalk map of the fibre inclusion: the class of `b` goes to the image of `b`.
- `quotientStalkEquivFiberStalk` is the isomorphism at x itself, transported along `f.fiberι (f x) (f.asFiber x) = x`.
- `quotientStalkEquivFiberStalk_mk` records that compatibility on quotient representatives at x, through the identification of the stalks at the two equal points.

The proof uses `BaseChangeStalk.pullback_stalk_isLocalization` and identifies the tensor product with the quotient. The localization map is surjective between local rings, so the localization is an isomorphism.

The module docstring also announces a bundled flat morphism with Cohen–Macaulay source, descriptions of affine fibres as spectra of fibre algebras, local Noetherianity of fibres and Cohen–Macaulayness of stalks; the file contains none of these. It cites Tag 0C0X.
